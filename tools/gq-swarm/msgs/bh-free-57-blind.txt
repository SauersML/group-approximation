Blind commutants of push families in CT_P(Z) are trivial

In Kohl's CT_P(Z), a piecewise-canonical map that commutes with a binary push family and preserves
the family's germ data is the identity. The proof counts fixed points of conjugated affine germs
and uses Haar measure. Consequence: in any copy of 2V in CT_P(Z) (any P, any symmetric power r), no
nontrivial element of the other stack's V preserves the push germ partition, and for r = 1 the
pushes must read hidden q-fibre data over every cylinder. 2V <= CT(Z) is still open.
