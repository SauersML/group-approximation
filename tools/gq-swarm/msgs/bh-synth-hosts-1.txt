Host synthesis: rank-one hosts are local, coded unit groups are residually finite

Two new lane-proof claims and one synthesis artifact:

- rank-one-cone-hosts-put-fw-groups-in-local-map-groups (with route
  rank-one-cone-hosts-fw-local-map-proof): in a full group whose cones form a
  tree and whose local maps form a self-similar groupoid, every FW subgroup
  virtually embeds in a finite power of the local-map vertex groups, and an
  infinite simple FW subgroup embeds in one of them. This recovers the
  Rover-Nekrashevych Kazhdan theorem and gives finite FW subgroups for V,
  V_{n,r}, all graph full groups and Katsura odometers.
- finitely-coded-unit-groups-act-residually-finitely: in every finitely coded
  groupoid, of any rank, unit groups act residually finitely, so simple inputs
  can only sit in coded hosts as non-unit elements.
- research/artifacts/gq-bh-synth-hosts.md: the single host machine behind the
  swarm's hosts, the four slots through which inputs enter, the five barriers
  that separate reachable inputs from hard ones, and the master host program
  (a Li-type finiteness theorem for non-LCM dissection categories, germ
  extensions of 2V, and asynchronous rank-one hosts).
