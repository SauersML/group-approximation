P2 prime b, non-amenable case: gluing kernels of free products split over the factors

For Lambda_1 * Lambda_2 acting on X, the gluing kernel is normally generated by the two factor kernels
and the kernel of F(T_1) *_(LC(X,V)) F(T_2) onto F(T). Every disjoint-support commutator of factor
elements already dies in the gluing group. So exactness over F_n, and by rank universality over the
Track A group, follows from EX(Z) (the cyclic Cantor-alphabet case) plus the dynamics-free free-product
presentation FPG, which is proved for a one-point space. The orbit relation of V times a non-amenable
Bernoulli shift is not hyperfinite, so no Brin-Thompson embedding shortcut exists there.
