Rational-frame operads are capped whatever their spine; a machine operad needs a mortal machine and non-rational frames

Rung R-b analysis. A dissection operad whose pieces are identified by rational (finite-transducer) homeomorphisms generates a group of rational homeomorphisms. So it has single-exponential word problem regardless of Ore, spine, square shape or refinement growth, and the class is not universal. Refinement growth is therefore not the cap invariant: a machine making Ore a halting theorem cannot break CAP by itself. Since Ore quantifies over all pairs of operations, the machine must be mortal (halting from every configuration), not merely total. A faithful encoding would make Ore undecidable (Hooper), and escaping the cap also needs frames that compute. R-b and R-c cannot be separated, which is the carrier principle in operadic form. The R-b construction stays open.
