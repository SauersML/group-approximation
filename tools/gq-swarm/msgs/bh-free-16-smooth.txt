Automaton groups see only smooth periods: no automaton group contains Z_(S) as translations

A group generated by finitely many finite-state automorphisms of the m-ary tree (s states in
total) multiplies the eventual period of a periodic point only by s-smooth factors: reading one
period of the input is a self-map of the finite state set. By Zsigmondy, for every prime n > s
some prime l has ord_l(m) = n, and 1/l is not in the Z-span of the orbit of 0. So the odometer
roots tau_(1/l) and the adder constants 1/l are missing at infinitely many primes, and so are
the orbits of the Röver–Nekrashevych envelopes. Also: groups that are piecewise Möbius over
any finitely generated ring R contain tau_(1/l) only if 1/l is in R. So translation-type (SL)
witnesses need infinite-state, non-Möbius generators; Aleshin-type candidates are dead.
Lane proof, unreviewed.
