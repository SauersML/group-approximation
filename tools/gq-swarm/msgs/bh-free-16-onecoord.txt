One-coordinate registers of Q admit no dilation in coordinatewise hosts (nV, SV_G, 2V_tau)

A register copy of Q living in one coordinate of a coordinatewise cellular host is normalized
as x -> dx by no element of the host, however that element permutes or mixes the other
coordinates. Invariance of the twist pi o t - d pi under the small register rotations makes it
piecewise constant and forces t to read the register through the register coordinate. Chains
of positive-shadow boxes then pull boundary values back into one finitely generated subgroup of
R, whose rational part is cyclic. The escapes left are exactly registers spread over several
coordinates and genuinely two-dimensional (odometer-type) germs. Lane proof, unreviewed.
