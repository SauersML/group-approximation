Houghton ports: abelian-cofinite actions give relative permutational Boone-Higman

Brown's Houghton group H_3 contains an element with exactly two free orbits, so every action of
Z^n with finitely many orbits extends (after doubling) to a type [A_2] action of a product of
Houghton groups. With the orbit-finite gluing lemma this proves: a finitely presented group with a
faithful action having finitely generated point stabilizers and a cofinite free abelian subgroup
satisfies relative PBH (FFWZ Conjecture 1.8). Such actions force solvable word problem and
finitely many ends per orbit. Records FFWZ Conjecture 1.8 and poses the one-dimensional Higman
embedding problem as a route to it.
