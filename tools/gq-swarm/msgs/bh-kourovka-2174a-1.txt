Kourovka 21.74(a): the reductions are blind to it; a two-transposition no-instance; psi permutes an infinite partition

All instances of the 21.74(b),(c) and 21.73 reductions permute {0(7),...,6(7)}. The element
tau_{1(2),0(4)} tau_{0(2),1(2)} permutes no nontrivial partition into residue classes, finite or
infinite. Kohl's mixer psi permutes an infinite one, so the problem depends on reading the
partition as finite. Dissipative elements are always yes-instances for infinite partitions.
21.74(a) itself stays open; the note states the missing steps both ways.
