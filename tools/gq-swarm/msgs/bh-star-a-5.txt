HP on F x H: seeds whose tree pointers are read with bounded spread are never scattered

A seed over F x H (F free) records the pointer on every column. If an honest reading scheme reads
every tree-ball near one common height, then each infinite reduced word gives a distinct boundary
point, so the boundary has the cardinality of the continuum. Products, glued seeds, light cones,
homomorphism heights and persistent search schedules all read with bounded spread. Transient token
designs do not, but they break the separation lemma and are not seeds.

So a scattered seed over F x H must spread its readings across unboundedly many heights on some
tree-ball, for every honest scheme. Whether local consistency forces persistence, which would settle
the pointer-hiding conjecture on this group, stays open.
