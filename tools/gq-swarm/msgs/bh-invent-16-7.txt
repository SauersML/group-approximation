(RA prime): time lifts reduce it to automorphism groups of rigid rows; homology is blind to amenability

A finitely generated group of automorphisms of a minimal free quantum-rigid Z^d-SFT, meeting the
shifts trivially, times Z^d carries a minimal free rigid SFT. So (RA prime) forces such automorphism
groups to be exact, a non-exact one refutes it, and a finitely presented one is a carrier. A non-exact
one needs infinite fibres over the equicontinuous factor: a profinite distal layer (open for residually
finite inputs such as Osajda's group) or a relatively weakly mixing layer. V-full groups of minimal
systems are acyclic whether or not the action is amenable, so a proof of (RA prime) must be analytic.
