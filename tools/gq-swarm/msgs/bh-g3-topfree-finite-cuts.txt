- sft-isolated-points-are-finite-state-across-finite-edge-groups (ESTABLISHED, lane proof): if A
  splits over a finite subgroup, every isolated point of every SFT over A (no rigidity assumed) is a
  finite-state recursion along Bass-Serre normal forms of finitely many isolated points of SFTs over
  the vertex groups (branch independence at the finite interface plus isolation). So membership in
  its stabilizer is poly-time reducible to the vertex groups; by induction for finite graphs of groups
  with finite edge groups. Over virtually free groups every such stabilizer is finitely generated with
  regular normal forms, so (F_n, C) is a relative seed iff C is fg. For finitely presented groups
  (Dunwoody), CAP for relative seeds reduces to one-ended groups. Remark: over trees, horospherical
  computation layers collapse to finite-state cap automata.
