Digit paths only transport the halting core mark; its negative certificate is one K_M predicate

digit-paths-only-transport-the-halting-core-mark: an E-coset of B_1 meets H_1 in at most one
P-coset gP, and by Kurosh normal form it is core for A_pm iff all syllables of g but the last lie in
P_pm and the last K_M-syllable lies in <U>P; that predicate is constant on the trace. So the digit
paths carry the core mark by transport only (free edge groups have no room for space-time diagrams,
and deterministic tree readers are sliding-block factors), and the negative certificate is the
single K_M predicate [g in <U>P]. Cosets with empty trace are the tree half of gate RR.
