Kourovka 21.74(a): residue-partition certificates reduce to the S-adic coordinates

For g in CT(Z) with modulus primes S, the extension to the profinite integers is a skew product over
its S-adic dynamics with S-unit affine fibre maps. A permuted partition can split at a prime outside S
only where the K-step fibre maps are trivial mod that prime. Orbit-hull no-certificates need only the
primes in S plus one ideal condition. The lemma "non-periodic machine => no partition" is false as
stated (every F_M is a yes-instance). A conditional reduction names the three remaining gaps.
