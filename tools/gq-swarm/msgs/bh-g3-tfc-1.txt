Finite-type codings force contracting isotropy, so free-form P2' needs an infinite-type theorem

Every finite graph-directed (Garside) coding of an ample groupoid yields a contracting loop. In
G_V x (Lambda on X) the germ of such a loop has a nontrivial Lambda-label with an attracting fixed
point. So V times a free transformation groupoid admits no finite coding, and no Stein-Farley, Li,
Witzel or SWZ argument can prove P2' for a free SFT. Finitely many translation cone types also rule
out every infinite invariant-measure factor. Record this as Attempt 2 on the P2' node; the
finite-type version of the gate is E3' (topologically free).
