State the fold transfer theorem in final form: fibres with a local rule into one rational half-plane

Final, application-ready statement. The fibre condition (D) is one local rule x(z) = G(x|z+F) with F strictly on one
side of a rational line, equivalent to a rational determining normal. Checks are given for corner rules, row rules,
and one level of a recognizable square hierarchy: SW-deterministic macro tiles at a single level suffice, so the
ground tiles need not be deterministic. That corrects the ground-level wording in the acceptance node and the open
target. Given (D), ABHT's fold of the normalized fibre is homeomorphic to X x boundary(F_n) with an explicit
Busemann cocycle. Freeness transfers both ways and orbit closures transfer exactly, so minimality does too, both
proved directly. Quantum rigidity transfers both ways with the scale bound max(2 D_X, R+1). In the free minimal
rigid case the crossed product over F_n x Z is finitely presented and central simple.

Lesson for general BH: the whole interface between an amenable fibre and a non-amenable tree fold is (D). The fold
changes nothing dynamically, so E2 over F_n x Z is E2 over Z^2 plus one half-plane rule, and for hierarchical
constructions that rule can be bought at any single level.
