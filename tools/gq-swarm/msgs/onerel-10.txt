Host condition (H) for the twisted chain lamplighter needs dynamical degrees

Finitely supported twists are circular: in lamp wreaths an evaluation twist forces the lamp to contain a (2^k,2)-chain configuration. In germ hosts, infinite 2-divisibility of the base generator forces its 2^m-th roots to move the regular fixed point along orbits of exactly 2^m points, so b carries an odometer on its degree-one fixed points and the annulus-tower model with V-pieces is impossible.
