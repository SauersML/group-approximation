all standing hypotheses of Li's Garside theorem. So its Roller-boundary full groups
are Garside isotropy groups with solvable word problem. Li's multiplicity condition
(t<d) fails at every Garside family S^{<=M}, already for the F_2 tree. N^k-gradings
with unique factorization, which supply powers, exist exactly for products of trees.
