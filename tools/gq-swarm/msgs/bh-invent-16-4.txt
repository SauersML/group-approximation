P2 prime b: type-changing transpositions lift coherently; free-product gluing reduces to the local transposition presentation

B-interleaved ladders give canonical lifts of every labelled transposition of V times a free product
action into the free-product gluing group, with (O), both splits and all conjugacy configurations
verified (full columns via the point case, under a cylinder-stabilizer hypothesis that holds for full
shifts over torsion-free groups). So FPG follows from the Quick-type local presentation of the full
group, and exactness over F_n from that presentation plus EX(Z). FPG is checked directly for PSL(2,Z)
on two points.
