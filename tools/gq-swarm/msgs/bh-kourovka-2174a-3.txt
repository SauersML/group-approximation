CT(Z) torsion problem: the Brin-Thompson transplant fails; tame equals finite order; reaching a class is undecidable

Read Belk-Bleak (2V has unsolvable torsion problem) at source. Their coding needs independent prefix
replacements on two coordinates. In CT(Z) one slope acts on every p-adic coordinate, and the rational
transfer maps that do exist move symbols between alphabets of coprime sizes. In CT(Z), tame equals
finite order. Torsion is undecidable if some finitely generated subgroup has unsolvable torsion
problem, e.g. if 2V embeds. The analog of their Theorem 6.6 (the orbit reaching a residue class) is
undecidable in CT_{3,5,7,11,13}(Z). The torsion problem itself stays open.
