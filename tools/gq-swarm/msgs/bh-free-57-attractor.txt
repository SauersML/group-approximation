Slice attractors of a spatial 2V in piecewise-affine hosts must expand a prime

In a spatial copy of 2V in CT_P(Z) (or B, G_(2,3) = R_(3)), the image T of a slice contraction has an
attractor K on which the other stack's V acts faithfully. If T has only integer slopes near K, with
a common contracted prime, then K sits over finitely many rational centres and V acts by scalings
between them. Orbits in K then grow polynomially, but q_2 maps them onto V-orbits on C, which grow
exponentially. So surviving designs need slice contractions with expanding, Conway-type pieces.
