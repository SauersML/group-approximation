Rational-base Turing machines in CT_{3}(Z) carry a potential that freezes the tape

Junk reflection works: Kari-Ollinger direction switching at the junk digit gives finite order iff the
machine is uniformly periodic. But an exact rational-base realization exists iff the states carry a
rational potential. A digit-uniqueness law (base 3/2, digits -1, 0, 1) then makes every net tape
change a function of the initial state, the current state and the head displacement. So these machines
store nothing, and the coding cannot transport Kari-Ollinger's theorem to the torsion problem.
