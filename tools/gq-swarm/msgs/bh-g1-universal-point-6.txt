Mikhailova edges transfer word-problem hardness into C but never create it

mikhailova-relative-seeds-push-down-to-bi-seeds: a relative seed for a fibre product M_Q in F x F
pushes down, condition by condition (isolation and stabilizer, orbit closure, quantum rigidity), to
a relative seed for the diagonal of Q x Q, i.e. a two-sided seed of Q whose forced point computes
WP(Q). Conversely the diagonal trick (rows forced by seeds propagated along the diagonal) turns any
seed of Q into a relative seed for the diagonal. So the Mikhailova CAP place reduces to rigid
two-sided seeds of a hard group.
