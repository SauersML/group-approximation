Telescopes and self-simulation: germ calculus, dissolution into V, and parse-shift fixed points

A telescope lets a group act on every annulus around a point through the orbit of one
endomorphism. Its germ group is (P/K_inf)*sigma. Inside a finite germ extension of V, a faithful
telescopic germ base is exactly a dynamically V-separated group, the converse of the V-separated
theorem, and a self-simulating finite germ extension of V simulates only subgroups of V.

The fixed point of the self-simulation equation theta = (s, theta, theta, ...) is unique. It is
a two-state transducer when s is in V, it is singular on the whole parse shift of
never-terminating hierarchies (a golden-mean SFT), and it carries the seed in its germ at every
point of that shift. Along one chain the host is PSPACE-capped.

Also records the OPEN multidimensional version: self-simulating hosts over one-ended parse
shifts, the group-side twin of Conjecture G2-fp.
