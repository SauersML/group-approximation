V-type full groups are acyclic over stratified-minimal systems; for full shifts acyclicity is corner-map stability

Read Li 2209.08087 sections 5-6 at source. Amplified full groups F(R x (G_V x H)) are acyclic for every
ample H (Theorem 5.17 + Theorem A + Kunneth). Restriction to closed invariant sets gives extensions
whose kernels are full groups of the open strata, so acyclicity, and with it the local transposition
presentation, holds for every transfinite filtration with minimal strata (countable subshifts, finitely
layered systems). For full shifts, acyclicity is equivalent to homology-injectivity of the corner copy
map. Li's minimality is needed exactly at pattern avoidance: fixed points cannot enter nonconstant
cylinders.
