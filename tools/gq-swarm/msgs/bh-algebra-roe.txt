Linear gate U: the oracle is free, and the gate becomes an algebraic uniform Roe embedding of R_Gamma

New claim node linear-gate-u-reduces-to-algebraic-uniform-roe-embeddings:
- (A) Effective linear Roe principle. A simple recursively presented algebra embeds unitally in a diagonal
  corner of M_n(LC(W) x| P), with W effectively closed and free, faithfully on every subsystem, iff it has ANY
  (possibly noncomputable) faithful module of bounded geometry over P. The subshift forbids local violations
  of the relations, and simplicity replaces (T3).
- (B) Linear gate U over P reduces to embedding every canonical R_Gamma in M_n(C_u[P; F_2]).
- (C) The C*-analogue holds over any non-amenable P for every exact Gamma (Kirchberg: O_2 inside C*_u(F_2)),
  and fails for non-exact Gamma over exact P.
- (D) Dichotomy: gate U is geometric iff crossed products over exact scaffolds are algebraically exact; the
  latter over F_2 would make L^x exact.

New OPEN node canonical-algebras-embed-in-algebraic-uniform-roe-algebras (the coefficient-free gate over F_2,

Lesson for general BH: once the input is simple, linear gate U has no computability content, and exactness is
its only geometric shadow. Kirchberg solves the C*-version for all exact inputs, so the invention needed is an
algebraic finite-field Kirchberg embedding, and non-exact inputs decide whether Track B escapes geometry.
