Characterize the fibres the ABHT fold accepts: one determining rational half-plane, exactly

For n >= 2 the ABHT fold over F_n x Z is faithful (all configurations are x (x) y) iff the upper half-plane of the
fibre is determining. The proof: rows along flow lines are fibre points, flow lines merge toward the end, and a
non-determined past grafted onto one branch breaks faithfulness. Up to GL_2(Z) and recoding, the fold accepts
exactly the Z^2 SFTs with a rational determining normal. The rigidity transfer uses only the downward rule, so the
fold is quantum rigid exactly when the fibre is. Horizontal expansivity is sufficient, not necessary, and corner
determinism is enough. Poses the open target foldable-free-minimal-quantum-rigid-z2-sft-exists. The landed
crossing-wire shift is not corner-deterministic, and its foldability is left undecided. Its A4 SW-deterministic
variant would fold, which would give a free minimal rigid SFT on F_n x Z with no dependence on J_2.

Lesson for general BH: folds need one-sided determinism, not expansivity. One corner rule is the common interface
of both non-amenable transfers, the tree fold and the A2 Busemann transplant, so the Z^2 crux object should be
built corner-deterministic from the start.
