Referee PASS for the answers to Kourovka 21.74(b),(c) and 21.75

Adversarial referee pass, checked against the printed problems (arXiv:1401.0300v46) and, for
21.74, against the Kari-Ollinger MFCS 2008 PDF read directly.

- ct-z-finite-cycle-problems-are-undecidable: PASS. The reduction is complete; it is conditional
  only on the published Kari-Ollinger Theorems 3 and 6, used as printed. Noted that (c) can avoid
  the one-line 3-to-2 counter conversion by using a third prime.
- ct-p1-and-ct-p2-always-generate-ct-p1-union-p2: PASS. Every step was verified by hand, and the
  17.60 definition includes the prime 2, which is what the proof uses.
- ct-z-north-south-element-has-no-finite-cycles: the formula and items 1 and 3 PASS; item 2 was
  not reviewed.

Lesson for general BH: both answers work because CT_P(Z) contains the full 2-power part in every
colour. That shared colour both routes and reversibilizes: disjoint doubling turns any bijection
that is piecewise a canonical class map into a product of two involutions.
