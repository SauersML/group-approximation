Finite-state operads are capped: bounded propagation gives a uniformly doubly exponential word problem

Answers, in the bounded-propagation case, the question posed in the saturation node. Take an operad with transformations having finite transformations, finitely many generators, Ore and a finite spine. Its category of operations is then finitely presented by the spine squares, and equality in degree N is decidable in time 2^O(N). If moves also propagate through splits as one element of a finite set P on each piece, common multiples grow linearly. The word problem is then in 2^2^O(n), uniformly over the class. Hence no family of such hosts, including the full groups of their carriers, is universal (complexity-bounded host classes). The edge-split operad violates bounded propagation for P = generators, with an explicit NP-cut example. The open case is a finite spine with an unbounded propagation alphabet: the operadic form of a not-CAP seed.
