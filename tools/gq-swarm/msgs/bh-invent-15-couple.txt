Rope readers cannot be deterministic and must couple their chains

A deterministic reader is a sliding-block factor, so it only marks residue-periodic sets;
co-deterministic automata maximize the free runs at infinity rather than removing them (fix (b)
fails). The uncoupled numeral reader is not quantum rigid (two free chains lift to a noncommuting
family). New gate (RR prime): block and scaling coupling rules that collapse the freedom to one global
bit. Lane proof for parts 1-3, sketch for part 4, not reviewed.
