One three-arc word is a basis for Thompson T's mixed identities over Cantor and circle overgroups

A single explicit mixed identity E of T (a three-arc nested commutator with a squeeze layer) forces
cyclic-order preservation on any group of homeomorphisms of the Cantor set or circle containing the
standard T. So such a group satisfies all mixed identities of T. This rules out every dynamical overgroup
as a witness against a finite basis, and hence as a source of a negative answer to BFFHZ Question 3.2 at T.
