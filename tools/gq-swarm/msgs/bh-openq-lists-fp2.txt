Basilica: every FP_2 cover contains F_2, so no FP_2 ascending base for its HNN extension; Bieri-Renz remark corrected

- New ESTABLISHED node: de Cornulier's perfect-kernel argument (Benli-Grigorchuk-de la Harpe, Prop. 5.11,
  printed for the Grigorchuk group) transfers to the Basilica group through BGdlH's contracting approximants,
  which are residually soluble because the standard cover is F_2. Every FP_2 cover of the Basilica group
  contains F_2, so the Basilica group is not of type FP_2.
- Ascending-base node: item 3 (no ascending base of type FP_2). The Sigma remark is corrected after reading
  Bieri-Renz (CMH 63, 1988) at source: it has Theorem B, not an ascending-HNN characterization for m >= 2.
- Crux node: the FP_2 route is closed, plus necessary conditions on a 2-dimensional FP_3 certificate (a
  vertex stabilizer containing B regresses to the same problem, so B must act without a fixed vertex).
