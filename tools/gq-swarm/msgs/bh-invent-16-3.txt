P2 prime b: free-product gluing is exactly the splitting of the gluing kernel

The gluing group of V times a free product action is the amalgam of the factor gluing groups over
LC(X,V) modulo label commutation on disjoint cones. So the free-product presentation FPG is equivalent
to the gluing kernel being generated by the factor kernels, and exactness forces it. FPG holds for
trivial actions. Over F_n, exactness follows from FPG at each stage plus EX(Z). Records the Boolean power
of the Bass-Serre tree as the geometric form of the open part (type-changing elements).
