E2 for plane lifts: letters are central, rigidity is clustered freedom, transport couplings cannot be minimal

In crossing-wire table lifts over Lambda_0 x Z^2, copied table wires cross inside every zone, so
all letter and table idempotents of all planes commute and are central; with central coordinates,
E2 reduces to relative rigidity at frozen (skeleton, input), which holds iff the free planes
cluster within 2D and the cluster fibre is rigid. Coupling floating identities by bijective
table-dependent transports would make the lift rigid, but the slots force a single parallel
section, an invariant factor that determines the point, so such a lift is never minimal for a
non-SFT input; the transport design rule of 3bf21d2d6 is withdrawn. Lane proof, unreviewed.
