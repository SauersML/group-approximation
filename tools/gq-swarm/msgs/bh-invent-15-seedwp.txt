Seeded groups have solvable word problem, with no rigidity

Jeandel's argument, localized to the seed cylinder: w != 1 iff the seed rules become inconsistent over
F/<<Rel,w>>, since every proper quotient pulls back to a periodic seed configuration. Recursively
presented + seed (isolated point, trivial stabilizer) implies decidable WP, and y_* is computable.
CAP reduces to the size of these contradiction certificates. Lane proof, not reviewed.
