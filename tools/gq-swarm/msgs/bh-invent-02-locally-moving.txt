Port amalgams with a locally moving factor are never faithful

If one factor of an amalgam or HNN extension of homeomorphism groups has nontrivial rigid
stabilizers of small open sets avoiding the common subgroup, the commutator [d a d^-1, b] of
two disjointly supported conjugates is a reduced word acting trivially. This settles the test of
gluing two odometer hosts V_(2,1)(<a>) along the odometer: the only same-action copies are B and
cBc, a Thompson twist is an HNN extension, and every case has a kernel. Cyclic Houghton ports
(containing FSym of a ray) glue faithfully to nothing. So Higman operations on known hosts always
need kernel removal by disjoint-support relations.
