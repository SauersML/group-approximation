Twisted shell envelopes of BS(1,2) meet the host condition except finite presentation

Put the vertices of the BS(1,2) Bass-Serre tree on the shells of a finite germ extension of V, with the vertex stabilizer acting on its shell by a degree-one zoom. The regular fixed point, degree-one character, odometer law and A cap Stab = <b> all hold by construction. So the chain group lies in B_A once one such envelope is finitely presented with fg point stabilizers; the open gate is finite presentation of the germ group generated by a non-regular implant and the shell shift, the same gate as for GL_n(Q) shells. The odometer host 2V_tau fails (H).
