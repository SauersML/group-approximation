Test cone matching on right-angled Coxeter groups: condition (C) fails for the pentagon group, and settle colour-tameness

One Slurm job (1328148, 19 s). The tree calibration passes (only the level-1 root failure). The Z^2 grid
control fails at its 4 corners at every level. The right-angled pentagon group (hyperbolic, circle
boundary) has 4 persistent nested chains of non-matching atoms per generator through every computed
level. These are at the wall endpoints, which are branch points. So (C) fails even for a cubulated
hyperbolic group, and the nucleus-tile operad is the wrong engine. The next test is configuration
insertions.

Colour-tameness holds iff every colour has an operation with inputs of that colour only. That fails
for unstabilized SFT operads, already for free groups, and holds after Cuntz stabilization. Script and
results are in research/artifacts/gq-bh-finf-hyp-cone-matching-test.md.
