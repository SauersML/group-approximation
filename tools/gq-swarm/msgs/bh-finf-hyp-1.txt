Record that the F_infinity half of the BBMZ hyperbolic host is a question about G on its own horofunction boundary

New node bbmz-host-finiteness-reduces-to-the-vertex-boundary-groupoid (lane proof, not reviewed).
For H = G*Z on its horofunction boundary, every element is finitary (by atom morphisms) off finitely
many coset pieces, each homeomorphic to the horofunction boundary of G. The groupoid homology in
degrees >= 2 equals H_k(G; C(horofunction boundary of G)). By Li, if the simple derived host is F_n,
these are finite-dimensional over Q for 2 <= k <= n. This holds for surface groups and in top degree
for closed hyperbolic manifold groups. It is open in middle degrees, including for Kazhdan Sp(n,1)
lattices.

The finite germ extension theorem of Belk-Hyde-Matucci does not apply to either resolution of the
parabolic vertex. The pieces are chopped by V, and after collapsing them to points the transport base
is not finitely generated.
