gq-k2-q: singular top matrix covered for all one-type register designs

Replace invertibility of the top matrix by injectivity of the stacked top
blocks: only finitely many occur, so harmonic functionals have bounded
denominators and [1] of infinite order is divisible by finitely many primes.
Every one-type design is top-determined, singular or not (one loop plus
dilations included). Left open: multi-type designs with a type direction that
is never a top, whose consistency conditions would need coefficients of
unbounded prime support.
