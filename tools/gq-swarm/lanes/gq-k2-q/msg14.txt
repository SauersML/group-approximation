gq-k2-q: Z_(l) in trees and free-group mapping tori; host-class survey

Z_(l), and any rank-one group divisible by infinitely many primes, fixes a
vertex or an end of every tree it acts on, so in a graph of groups it lies in
a vertex group or along an ascending ray of stabilizers. Consequently no
free-by-cyclic group, and no mapping torus of a free group endomorphism that
is injective on rational homology, contains Z_(l); in general Z_(l) forces an
element whose iterates sink through the lower central series. The artifact
surveys the literature (Rauzy; KMS through gq-infinite-primes) and the host
classes now excluded.
