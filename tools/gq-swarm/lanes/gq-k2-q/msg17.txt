gq-k2-q: faithful l-adic tree action of the Euler lamplighter; finite-state test open

G_l acts faithfully by affine maps on the Iwasawa algebra Z_l[[T]] of l-adic
measures, the Euler element acting as multiplication by 1 + l x, preserving an
l-step refinement of the (l,T)-adic filtration: an explicit faithful action on
the l-ary tree. Whether some faithful action is finite-state (equivalently,
whether G_l lies in a finitely generated self-similar group) is recorded as
open, with the natural coordinatizations failing. Also note referee-b source
confirmation and centrality attribution on the finite-residual node.
