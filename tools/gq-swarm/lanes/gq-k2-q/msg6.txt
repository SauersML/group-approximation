gq-k2-q: single-register designs cannot divide [1] at infinitely many primes

An element of infinite order in a group built from a finitely generated
module over a finitely generated commutative ring by finite extensions and
finite quotients is p-power divisible for only finitely many primes (generic
freeness). Under the Pimsner hypothesis this applies to K_0 of register
completions with one register per type, shift or reset letters and eventually
constant availability, whatever the resolvent coefficients: Q[1] needs
relations of unbounded support.
