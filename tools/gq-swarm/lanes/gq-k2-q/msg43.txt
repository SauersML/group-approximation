gq-k2-q: frame-unit symbols can vanish without a grading

Doubling the ring lamplighter along the reflection gives M_2(U_n x| Z) with the
index-one frame unit diag(u, u^-1) over a copy of U_n: its symbols {lambda, U}
cancel, [1] has infinite order and Q^x is detected in K_1 with kernel {+-1}.
So no obstruction comes from the frame unit alone; diag(u,1) still carries the
infinitely generated symbols. Calibration, not a finitely presented host.
