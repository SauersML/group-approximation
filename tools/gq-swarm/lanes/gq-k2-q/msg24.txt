gq-k2-q: one-sided inverses cannot repair the Steinberg residual

The residually finite quotient St_N(T)/St_N(T)_rf depends only on T modulo
its finite residual ideal (from steinberg-rf-forces-ring-rf), and finite
rings are Dedekind-finite, so a design with one-sided inverses of the lamps
has the same residually finite Steinberg quotient as the design with
two-sided inverses, and the same finite-normal-generation question.
