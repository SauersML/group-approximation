gq-k2-q: generation lemma and chamber-colimit presentation of SL_n(Q)

GL_n(Q) is GL_n(Z) plus one commuting dilation per prime (established,
elementary). SL_n(Q) is the colimit of squarefree diagonal lattice
stabilizers over the restricted product of Bruhat-Tits chambers (OPEN until
the two textbook imports are quoted). Artifact records the Steinberg/K_2 view
(Tate symbols of order p-1 as the uniformity defect) and what a single host
for the chain GL_n(Z[1/m!]) needs.
