Verma submodule M(-k-2) of M(k), since they share the Casimir scalar; the only
cut is the growing-length singular vector f^(k+1) v_k. Open reduction: a
finitely presented sl_2 vacuum host realizing every L(k) admits a Verma-type
module (local nilpotence of f is Pi_2, not finitely presentable), so it is not
simple, unless it bounds weights below by comparing the weight to the top
weight -- the capacity-chain two-register regime.
