Germ criterion: Nekrashevych groups of subexponential automata are fp iff the cycle germ groups are

An eventual-state cocycle into the free groupoid on loop arrows carries every stage of the lift-ideal construction into the normal closure of finitely many germ relators, so for any automaton a finitely presented V_d(G) forces finitely presented germ groups at every closed family of stable rays. For subexponential activity the cycling relators are exactly the relators of the germ groupoid at the cycle rays, giving the converse; each cycle germ group embeds in a wreath product of a strictly lower-degree automaton group with a finite group. Recovers FP2 for bounded automata, makes the spine-mover theorem an equivalence (M(P) lift-presented iff P fp), and localizes BH for subexponential automaton groups to a germ-enlargement step.
