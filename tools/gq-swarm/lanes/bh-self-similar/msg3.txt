Loop retraction: a commonly fixed letter forces a finitely presented eventual section group

If every state of an automaton fixes a letter x and V_d(G) is finitely presented, then the subgroup generated by the eventual sections at x is finitely presented: the section endomorphism at x carries each stage of the lift-ideal construction into a normal closure in that subgroup. This gives a linear-activity automaton over three letters, with the Grigorchuk group as its spine section group, whose Rover-Nekrashevych group is not finitely presented; and it is shown that a twisted-persistent automaton of subexponential activity generates a finite group, so persistent failures need exponential activity. Apply referee b's scope corrections to the lift-presented Boone-Higman node.
