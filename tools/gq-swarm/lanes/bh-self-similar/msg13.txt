Spine plus recurrent odometer mover: the test case reduces to a 2-adic Grigorchuk lamplighter

In the four-letter form of the recurrent-mover test case, Grigorchuk states act on the {0,1}-block, spine states act only after an all-zero {2,3}-prefix and the mover is the odometer on that prefix. So the group embeds in a contracting group times the restriction H' to nonempty prefixes, which lies in the product of Gamma wr Z/2^k generated by the odometer and the lamp at 0. With abelian lamps this is the ordinary lamplighter (lift-presented); with Grigorchuk lamps it is a genuinely 2-adic lamplighter, and the mover's infinite orbit of the spine ray blocks compression along finitely many rays. LP-embeddability of H' is the open question it isolates.
