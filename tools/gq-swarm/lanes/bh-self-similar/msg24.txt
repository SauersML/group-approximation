Branch substitutions are not residually finite; spine hosts reduce to fp RF overgroups of the lamp

If a group contains a level-transitive weakly branch group and an element conjugating it into its first level stabilizer, every finite quotient kills the derived subgroups of the rigid copies of the branching subgroup; so Grigorchuk's and the Bartholdi-Virag Basilica extensions are not residually finite, and no self-similar host realizes such a substitution. Germ groups at stable rays are the loop subgroups, hence fp in every lift-presented automaton group; a separated automaton group is LP-embeddable once its base lies in an fp subgroup of a lift-presented automaton group, and for spine groups every same-tree lift-presented overgroup forces this. The open input sharpens to the Grigorchuk group inside an fp residually finite group.
