Finitary extensions preserve lift-presentability; spinal extensions of lift-presented groups satisfy Boone-Higman

States whose sections at a fixed depth lie in a lift-presented sub-automaton keep the relator kernel a finitely generated lift ideal (one lift step at depth one, level regrouping for deeper). So lift-presentability reduces to automata in which every nontrivial state lies on a cycle. Combined with run compression: adding to a lift-presented base new states that loop along a letter fixed by everything, with base sections elsewhere and trivial base sections at that letter, gives a group with a lift-presented action, hence a finitely presented simple overgroup. The sharpest open test is the linear-activity automaton a = s(1,a), b = (a,b), where the base moves the loop letter.
