LP-embeddable groups are closed under products and finite extensions; only recurrent states matter

A lift-presented action of H gives one of H wr Sym(n) (diagonal repetition plus cone copies and a finitary top, a finitary extension) and of H_1 x H_2 (disjoint alphabets), so groups embedding in a group with a lift-presented action are closed under finite direct products and finite extensions, and an automaton group is one iff the group generated by its recurrent states is. Transient states that block run compression are therefore harmless; the open subexponential case narrows to recurrent states moving a ray with infinitely generated cycling relators.
