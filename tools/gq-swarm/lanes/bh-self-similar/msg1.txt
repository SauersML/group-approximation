Automaton groups with a finitely generated lift ideal satisfy Boone–Higman

Compose the lift-ideal characterization of finite presentation of V_d(G) with Zaremsky's regrouping and a cone-diagonal embedding into the simple commutator subgroup. Regrouping does not change the relator kernel or lift-closure, so every automaton group whose relator kernel is a finitely generated lift ideal embeds in a finitely presented simple group; this covers the lamplighter and affine-lamplighter automata and every automaton group freely generated by its states (whose Nekrashevych groups are therefore finitely presented). Add the open statement for all automaton groups and the overgroup reduction to it.
