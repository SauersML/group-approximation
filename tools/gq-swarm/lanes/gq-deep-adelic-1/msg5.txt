UHF-type groupoid models: AF rings omit Q; virtual Laurent trap

A finitely generated ring containing Q has no unital map to matrices over a
commutative ring, so no finitely generated ring of compactly supported
functions on an AF groupoid (the UHF tail groupoid of a Bratteli diagram)
contains Q, for any coefficients. A matrix ring over a Z-graded ring with a
degree-one unit and commutative or tracial degree-zero part has no finitely
presented E_N; so crossed products over an odometer factor fail whenever the
chain contains an indicable finite-index subgroup, perfect groups included.
Record the class verdict and the FAb remnant (e.g. SL_3(Z)).
