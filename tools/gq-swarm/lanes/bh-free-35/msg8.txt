Free SFTs without two-sided faults: surjective strip automata in every rational direction; products excluded

- no-two-sided-fault-sfts-have-rich-strips-in-all-directions: for the design target
  free-minimal-sft-without-two-sided-faults-exists, every rational direction carries a surjective strip automaton
  that is non-injective exactly at non-expansive lines. The strip subshift has no periodic point, so it is
  non-sofic, and Cyr-Kra gives it at least quadratic complexity. Products of Z-subshifts (product substitution
  tilings) always have two-sided faults, because an infinite Z-subshift has asymptotic pairs on both sides (a
  Morse-Hedlund argument). Kari-Papasoglu corner determinism qualifies only if an axis seed is forced.

self-simulation must run inside an irreversible Ledrappier-type rule, not beside it.
