Complexity is conserved along HNN stages; SV_G is conjugacy-rigid on its actor

An injective one-permutation HNN stage over a type (A) actor has its
stable letter pinned by finitely many values, so its orbit problem is
primitive recursive relative to the old one and the orbit and subgroup
distortion of the orbit-finite edges. Identity edges must be closed under
the permutation bicommutant: regular edges are, fiber-product (Mihailova)
edges never are, and over V orbit distortion is tame. What remains for a
hard actor is a regular orbit-finite edge with non-primitive-recursive
factorization distortion.

In twisted Brin-Thompson groups the germinal twist at a constant point turns
every conjugation between global twists into a conjugation in the actor, so
SV_G adds no conjugacies, and an HNN input with its base placed globally
needs a base-faithful image of itself in the actor.
