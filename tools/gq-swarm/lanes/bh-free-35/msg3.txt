HNN conservation law: the non-injective case, and the other actor constructions

Point evaluation in a one-permutation stage is primitive recursive relative
to the edge orbit distortion whether or not the stage acts faithfully. So
complexity enters such a stage only through edge distortion (injective case)
or the kernel (non-injective case, paid for by finite presentation of the
image). The other type (A) constructions on main compute their actions
coordinatewise and are conservative for that reason.
