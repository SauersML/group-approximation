Baumslag-Gersten group: highly transitive and MIF, maps onto Higman's groups

BG is acylindrically hyperbolic by Minasyan-Osin's HNN corollary (the
associated subgroup <a> of BS(1,2) is weakly malnormal), and it is
torsion-free, so Hull-Osin make it highly transitive and MIF. Main's
proper-power orbit count then leaves finite generation of point stabilizers
as the only failing clause of type (A). BG/<<t^n>> is Higman's H_n split by
C_n, which links the BG and H4 test cases and recovers Baumslag's theorem
that finite quotients of BG are cyclic. The BG node gains an Attempts entry
with a germinal-twist constraint on global-twist hosts SV_G.
