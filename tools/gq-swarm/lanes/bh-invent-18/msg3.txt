Anticipation layers: totality is isolation, and rigidity needs convergent walks

A label copied along a locally readable walk and fixed where the walk halts is forced on a seed iff
the walk halts everywhere, so a decorated seed stays isolated exactly when the computation is total,
with the same stabilizer. The layer is quantum rigid on the terminating part (a transport identity
for path projections), and two far-apart non-terminating basins in any point destroy rigidity by the
clustering lemma. States the decisive lemma CB (merge versus compute). Lane proof, not reviewed.
