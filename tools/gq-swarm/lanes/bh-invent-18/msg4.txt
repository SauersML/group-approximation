Rigid seed decorations compute only where rogue walks fill space

Brackets rigidity of anticipation decorations: coarse-dense non-halting walks suffice (the base algebra
is a quotient of Clop(Y_0), so stopped-path decompositions hold at any range), and a non-halting basin
far from another modification kills it. Periodic boundary points over Z^d (d >= 2) must be mortal. On
tree seeds of free products of finite and cyclic groups every decoration is regular (Buchi), and on the
product seed of Z^2 every rigid total decoration is finite-state. Localizes CB and gives a design rule
for the machine rung. Lane proof, not reviewed.
