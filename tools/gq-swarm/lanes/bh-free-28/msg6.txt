  T_n = (Z/d)^C(n,4), d even.

  gamma_7. So T_n meets gamma_7(C_n) trivially, and C_n embeds in
  rho_n(C_n) x C_n/gamma_7(C_n), a linear group in characteristic zero.
- Consequences: C_n is residually nilpotent and residually finite, and lies in B_A. The
