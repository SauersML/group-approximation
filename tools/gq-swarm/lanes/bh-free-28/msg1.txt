Prove F_2/[F_2'',F_2] linear, hence in B_A; reduce C_r (r >= 4) to residual finiteness

A second-order Magnus embedding x, y -> 4x4 block matrices [[1,a,c],[0,gI,b],[0,0,1]]
over Z[x^(±1),y^(±1)] is faithful on the free centre-by-metabelian group of rank 2:
the corner map is injective on F''/[F'',F] because c([e, h e h^-1]) = D (h - h^-1)
with D = a(e).b(e) != 0. So C_2 is linear in characteristic zero and lies in B_A.
For r >= 4, Gupta's 1973 theorem (abstract) reduces linearity, and hence B_A
membership, to residual finiteness of C_r. Lane proofs, not reviewed; no priority
claimed (Gupta 1969 gives faithful representations in this family).
