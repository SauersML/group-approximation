P2 prime splits: rigidity lives in LC(X,V) x| Lambda, gluing is dynamics-free

The full group of V times a free Lambda-subshift is generated by the labelled Thompson group V(Lambda)
(always fp for fp Lambda) and the Boolean-power crossed product N_X = LC(X,V) x| Lambda, meeting in
V x Lambda. N_X is fg and already sees the necessity theorem (N_X fp => X quantum rigid SFT). The
gluing group (amalgam modulo disjoint-cone commutation, finitely many relations) is self-similar with
commuting copy endomorphisms. Open halves: P2a (rigid => N_X fp) and P2b (finitely normally generated
gluing kernel), with attempts. Lane proofs, not reviewed.

Lesson for general BH: on the group side, rigidity is concentrated in the Boolean power of V over X,
the exact twin of the crossed-product ring; the rest is Thompson self-similarity. Groups lack the
linear partition-of-unity relations that let rigidity act on the universal object.
