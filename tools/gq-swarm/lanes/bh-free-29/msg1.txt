Finitely presented strongly shift-similar groups have finitely presented Houghton-like groups for every n

Answers the first sentence of Mallery-Zaremsky Question 5.13 for strongly shift-similar G, and the
finite-presentation part of its third sentence (all n, including n = 2): an explicit finite presentation of
H_n(G) from n copies of one of G, translations, one transposition identification per ray, and finitely many
mixing relations, checked by an FSym-by-germ five-lemma argument. Lane proof, not reviewed.
