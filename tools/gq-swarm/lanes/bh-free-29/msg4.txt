Odometer shift-similar group: a rigid, non-amenable test of the Houghton-like lifting

Strongly shift-similar groups are exactly the finitary preimages of index-zero germ groups normalized by
the near shift. On N as eventually-zero 2-adics the near shift is the odometer, so the preimage G_od of
K = ker chi in the odometer Nekrashevych group B is strongly shift-similar, finitely generated, contains V
and E_2, and has finitely presented Houghton-like germ groups Q_n(B) for n >= 3; K = [B,B]. B acts
genuinely on the integers and FSym(Z) x| B is finitely presented; for n >= 3 H_n(G_od) is generated by
copies of it. Open: H_3(G_od) finitely presented. Lane proofs, not reviewed.

Lesson for general BH: strongly shift-similar groups correspond bijectively to subgroups of NSym(N)
containing the near shift, so shift-similar hosts are near actions with an index-one element; fp lifts
through FSym along realizable oligomorphic germ actions (shadow of the type (A) criterion), and H_n
is a device for realizing germs on pairs of rays.
