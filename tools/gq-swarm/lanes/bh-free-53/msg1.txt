Shell germ Steinberg algebras are never simple; the algebra gate splits into A and A/J versions

Every shell envelope's germ Steinberg algebra contains the nonzero singular element
(1 - u_g) s_0 (1 - u_g) t_0, over every field, so it is not simple and the groupoid is not
Hausdorff. The envelope still embeds in the units of A/J. Record this as a correction to step 3
of the germ-algebra Boone-Higman route, and as Attempt 2 on the algebra-to-germ-group claim:
the input-letter truncation of Attempt 1 does not reach the A/J version the route needs.
