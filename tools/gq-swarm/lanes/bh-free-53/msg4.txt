Generic shells have infinitely generated singular ideals; J_p is the boundary annihilator

The local singular ideal J_p of a shell germ algebra is the annihilator of the permutation
modules on the Schreier boundary (limit stabilizers at infinity) of the germ group. Every
infinite finitely generated group has generic enumerations. For them the germ group is the
parity wreath <P, tau | [P, tau^D P tau^-D], D odd> and J_p = w(F_e) w(F_o) K[Q], which is not
finitely generated: the singular elements (1-u_g) s_0^D (1-u_h) t_0^D at odd distance D are
independent. So for generic shells, A f.p. forces A/J not f.p.; the Bieri-Strebel shift
exponent reappears inside the singular ideal.
