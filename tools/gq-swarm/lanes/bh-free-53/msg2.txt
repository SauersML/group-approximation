Shell germ algebras modulo the singular ideal are central simple; J is generated at p

For a minimal effective second-countable ample groupoid with compact Hausdorff unit space,
A/J is central simple: simplicity is the CEPSS remark after thm:simple (via Nekrashevych
Prop. 4.1), with a direct proof; the centre is computed here. So every shell envelope sits in
the units of a central simple A_nu/J. The singular ideal is generated by its part at p, which
is the annihilator in K[Q] of the shell module at infinity, so J is finitely generated iff that
annihilator is. For the zigzag shell of Z the germ group is Z^3 by Z/4 and J is finitely
generated, but not by the four-germ elements. Update the route's step-3 correction.
