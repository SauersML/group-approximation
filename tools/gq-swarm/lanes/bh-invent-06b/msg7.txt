Contracting RSGs have no scaling germs at periodic tails; the asynchronous lag bound is only partly forced

- contracting-rsgs-have-no-scaling-germs-at-periodic-tails: if an element of a contracting RSG maps an
  eventually periodic point into its own tail class, its asymptotic lag is zero (iterating a scaling
  germ yields nucleus maps with pairwise distinct slopes). Nucleus cycles whose output tail returns
  have zero weight; cycles between different tail classes are unconstrained. The example p on the
  2-shift (contraction unverified) shows why. So the asynchronous extension of the conservativity
  lemma still needs a composite-lag bound. Rotation analogue reported, not decided: the conjugate-
  odometer test family, and IET routes would contain Katok's question.
