Labelled hosts of free actions have no contracting loops; inflation hosts of substitutions are contracting RSGs

Two results on gate E3 (bh-invent-06b):
- labelled-hosts-of-free-actions-have-no-contracting-loops: in any groupoid with a continuous
  Lambda-label over an open map to X, a contracting loop is shadowed by an element of Lambda with an
  attracting fixed point. Free, invariant-measure (so amenable) and distal X admit no finitely coded
  labelled host, which covers every passive enlargement of G_V x (Lambda |x X).
- substitution-inflation-hosts-are-full-contracting-rsgs (+ proof): adjoining the inflation germs
  of a recognizable primitive aperiodic box substitution gives a full contracting RSG on the collared
  hierarchy shift, so the host is finitely presented, with no quantum rigidity and no finite type
  needed. This includes Labbe's Wang shift, whose labelled host needs the open rigidity gate.
