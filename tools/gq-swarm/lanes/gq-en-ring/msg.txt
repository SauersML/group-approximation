gq-en-ring: elementary-group route to GL_n(Q), rational-scalar obstruction

Land the matrix-ring route into gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group. An elementary
lemma embeds GL_n(Q) in PE_N(R) for every ring R containing Q (N >= n+2, every
N when R = M_2(R)). The open premise asks for one such R with PE_N(R) finitely
presented and simple for infinitely many N. A new established obstruction: a
finitely presented PE_N(R) forces the elementary rational scalars to form a
finitely generated group, so GL_N(R) = E_N(R) (the K_1 = K_2 = 0 Leavitt tensor
template) and perfect unit groups modulo centre are ruled out in characteristic 0.
