Isolate the zero-flux strand-stack obstruction and extend it to isometric enumerations

Lemma: a group without F_2 that maps to stack germs Z^S ⋊ Sym(S) with equal
offset speed at both ends of a Z-indexed stack, containing a uniform stack
shift and a non-uniform kernel element, is not finitely presented. This is
the Bieri-Strebel tameness argument in permutational form, and it tolerates
strands migrating out of the stack.

Corollary: a polyhedral piecewise-isometric enumeration of Z^2 with an
active slice whose flux character vanishes on R_0 has non-fp R_nu. So all
its MZ, Houghton-like and shell envelopes are non-fp. For coherent
successors the flux is a holonomy of the transverse jumps. The rotating
quadrant enumeration has zero holonomy and dies.

Lesson: stacks of strands are H_2-type objects inside the near group. An
amenable germ group needs every active stack to transport lines (nonzero
flux), like the n >= 3 rays of H_n. When R_nu contains F_2, Sigma-invariants
give nothing, and that is where Boone-Higman is open.
