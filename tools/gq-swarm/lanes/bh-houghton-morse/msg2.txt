Show zigzag Houghton-like envelopes are commensurable with Houghton groups

For the zigzag enumeration of Z, H_n(E_nu(Z)) has finite index in H_n(E_4),
which contains H_(4n) with finite index, because finite index passes through
the Houghton-like construction for FSym-containing strongly shift-similar
groups. So H_n(E_nu(Z)) is of type F_(4n-1) and not FP_(4n) for every n >= 1.
This realizes the rigid case left open by the refutation of the generic route,
and records why eventual periodicity cannot reach non-virtually-cyclic inputs.
