Record printed open questions near Boone-Higman from 2015-2026 papers, one answered by main

A new artifact lists the questions, problems and conjectures printed in papers on
finitely presented simple groups and their neighbours. It covers only those not
already on main's lists, each quoted from the TeX with its source line.

One is answered by composing nodes already on main. Llosa Isenrich-Schesler-Wu
(arXiv:2510.01952) Question 1.11 asks whether every finitely generated linear group
embeds in a finitely generated self-similar group. It does, and the host is even
finitely presented: the affine hosts over Z[1/m][s] and F_p[s] are finitely
presented and self-similar. No priority is claimed.

Six tractable questions get OPEN claim nodes:
- LISW Question 1.10: complex linear groups as quasi-retracts of simple groups;
- Z wr_X PSL_2(Z[1/2]) into a finitely presented self-similar group (arXiv:2609.01868);
- type F_infinity for contracting Rover-Nekrashevych groups (Skipper-Zaremsky);
- a finitely presented infinite simple group that is not two-generated (Lodha);
- derived full groups of free minimal Z^N actions (Matui);
- undistorted, type F_infinity hosts for hyperbolic groups (Zaremsky).
