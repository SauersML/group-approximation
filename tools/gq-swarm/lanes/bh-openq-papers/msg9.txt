M2 Attempt 7: the Alvarez Lopez limit needs no subsequence, so decidable groups get computable free strongly repetitive points

Lane reading of arXiv:1807.09256.
- The top-down colourings phi^N agree near the base point p for all N >= n. The top value
  at p is (chi_N(p), 0) = (0,0) because p lies in every set of type X-fraktur_N, and the
  strongly adapted colourings send those points to 0.
- The central clusters exhaust the group, so phi = lim phi^N is computable once each finite
  step is. Choosing translations for the disk maps gives strong repetitivity.
- Unread: the companion paper's construction (arXiv:2002.08662, Props 4.1-4.3).
- Modulo that, every f.g. group with decidable word problem has a computable strongly
  aperiodic, strongly repetitive point, with computable freeness radii and a computable
  central modulus.
- (M2) still needs the global modulus.

Lesson for general BH: for gate M2, freeness and computability are free for every decidable
group. The whole difficulty is the seam (global recurrence) condition.
