Subgroups of products of LR groups lie in CR when their Goursat quotient is in B_A; add BH lessons to this lane's nodes

New: product-subgroups-lie-in-cr-when-goursat-quotient-in-b-a (+ proof).
- Setting: G_1, G_2 in B_A with property (LR), such as free, limit and surface groups, and a
  subgroup H of G_1 x G_2 with finitely generated projections.
- If the Goursat quotient Q_H is in B_A, then H satisfies the coset-wreath conditions. So the
  coset wreaths, identity-edge HNN extensions and doubles over H lie in B_A.
- For finitely generated H in F_m x F_n: Q_H in B_A implies H in CR, which implies that Q_H
  has solvable word problem. Every finitely presented Q occurs, via Mihailova.
- Proof: H is the preimage of the diagonal retract of Q x Q. Pull it back along a virtual
  retraction, intersect with the retract F_1 x F_2, and return by finite-index inheritance.

Lesson for general BH: over products of free groups, identity-edge combination closures of
B_A are permutational Boone-Higman for the Goursat quotients in disguise. They cannot be
easier than PBH for finitely presented groups. The remaining difficulty of general
combination theorems lies in twisted edge maps.
