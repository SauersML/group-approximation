Prove BH for wreaths over clopen-stabilizer cosets, covering both test cases of the decidable wreath question

A new lemma handles permutational wreath products L wr_(G/H) G whose point stabilizer H
contains, with finite index, the stabilizer of a clopen set for some embedding of G in a
finitely presented full clopen-transitive Cantor host. For every L in B_A, the wreath
product lies in B_A. Each lamp is pushed onto the finite, pairwise disjoint orbit gH.U of
clopen sets, and the type (A) wreath closure for the clopen action finishes the proof.

Applications, all lane proofs and not reviewed:
- For m odd, L wr PSL_2(Z[1/2m]) over the cosets of PSL_2(Z[1/m]), which are the vertices of
  one type of the 2-adic tree. The host is V_2 of the Iwahori group, where the ball Z_2 has
  stabilizer of index 3 in PSL_2(Z[1/m]). This covers Z wr_X PSL_2(Z[1/2]) of
  arXiv:2609.01868, whose authors left it outside all known Boone-Higman methods.
- Z wr_(F_2/<a>) F_2. The proof is a ping-pong copy of F_2 in V in which <a> is the exact
  stabilizer of a clopen set.
- With bh-free-10's criterion for free permutational products, the double
  PSL_2(Z[1/2]) *_{PSL_2(Z)} PSL_2(Z[1/2]) and the centralizing HNN extensions over
  PSL_2(Z) lie in B_A. The double is finitely presented and not residually finite.

The first two are the test cases listed in pbh-closed-under-decidable-permutational-wreaths.
This lane's two earlier nodes gain the matching links: the BH form of the 2609.01868
question is now settled, and the Matui Z^N node now points to the finite-type reduction and
the Labbe test case.
