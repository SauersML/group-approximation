Obstruction: normal-germ extensions of V contain no SL_3(Z)

SL_3(Z) has property FW, so any copy in a finite germ extension G of a
Higman-Thompson group fixes a vertex of the Belk-Hyde-Matucci germ cube
complex; by Margulis a finite-index subgroup then embeds in a finite product
of germ groups. If every singular germ group contains (V)_p with finite
index, or normalizes it at a periodic point (VA, bounded-automata
Rover-Nekrashevych groups, the Corollary 2.10 regime), those germ groups are
abelian-by-(subgroups of V) and Higman's nilpotent theorem gives a
contradiction. So GL_n(Q), n >= 3, lies in no such G; germ groups of this
regime also carry no BS(1,2). Record it under Attempts of the GL_n(Q) root.
