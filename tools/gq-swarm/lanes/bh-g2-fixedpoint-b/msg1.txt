Prove that the end shift of a free group is quantum rigid over the group itself

The edge rule of the end-orientation SFT over a tree group makes the
backward arrows along any geodesic an increasing chain of idempotents, and
monotone chains commute. So the rank-one boundary shift left unchecked in
euclidean-building-boundary-shifts-are-quantum-rigid is D-rigid for every D
and field. With product stability, the boundary product over F_m x F_n is a
rigid, minimal, topologically free (not free) SFT over a one-ended
non-amenable group. Lesson: compression supplies rigidity; the G2 gap is
freeness plus coupling.
