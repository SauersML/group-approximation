Embed BS(1,m) in 3V for every odd m via SMART_m, a family of reversible machines

For m = 2r+1 >= 3, SMART_m is an 8-state reversible Turing machine on m letters whose four moves
nest by the constant-length-m substitution b -> b(dp)^r, d -> d(bq)^r, p -> (bq)^r p, q -> (dp)^r q;
m = 3 is SMART. Induced on starts of genuine level-0 moves it is an element of 2V with a brick-local
height-m renormalization, so BS(1,m) <= 3V by the existing criterion. Lane proofs, not reviewed; no
priority claimed.
