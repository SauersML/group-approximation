Validate the binary hierarchy machine for BS(1,2): exact doubling on hierarchical tapes, failure on counter-letter junk

On MSI, the radius-1 reversible design E->ER, S->RS, R->ES gives exactly doubling Y-counts with gap 12 and
phi U^2 = U phi along the zero-tape orbit. R, P and P^-1 pass exhaustively. The tower check T fails on long
b-blocks for the default fill and for every partial fill explored. The search over all fills was not
completed. Lesson: the binary wall is completeness (no periodic points on counter-letter backgrounds), not the
hierarchy.
