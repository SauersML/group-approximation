## 8. Hand design: the design over-constrains, and the unfrozen machine (SLURM job 1329218)

**Where the single-fact nogoods come from (hand trace).** Every size-1 nogood of §6 lives on one free input: `S→`
reading `b` with a nonzero right neighbour. That input is reached by exactly one chain of fixed rules. Cells are
numbered from the `a` being reset, with `x ≠ 0` to its right.

| step | where | rule | effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `S→` on `a` at 0 | `(0, A→)` | reset the `a`, move right |
| 2 | `A→` on `x` at 1 | `(x, S←)` | back left |
| 3 | `S←` on `0` at 0 | `(b, A←)` | write `b` |
| 4 | `A←` on `y ≠ 0` at −1 | `(y, S→)` | back right |
| 5 | `S→` on `b` at 0, neighbour `x ≠ 0` | free | |

In the hierarchy, step 1 is the junction J2a: an `R_R` ends at its home in an E-parent, and the cell to its right is
that parent's fresh end cell, always `0`. So the design **fixes a rule in a neighbour context the hierarchy never
visits**. The same holds for `X←` reading `0`, whose right neighbour in the hierarchy is always the peek cell, `0`.

**Principle.** With radius-1 reads, every rule may branch on its neighbour. A hierarchy constrains only the
(neighbour, input) contexts it visits; every other context is junk handling and should be free. Completing the rest
by hand means following every junk chain, which is what the solver does, so the hand design stops here.

**The unfrozen machine.**
- The contexts are those consulted by 200,000-step zero-tape orbits from the 8 non-`T` states. Those rules are fixed.
- Neighbour `0`: 10 fixed, 5 free (exactly the old free set).
- Neighbours `a` and `b`: only **4** fixed, 11 free each. So about `1.9·10^17` fills.
- Each neighbour value keeps a bijection onto the outputs the design gave its free inputs.

**Solver.** `bh_unfreeze.py`, with a z3 master (standard CDCL): exactly-one constraints both ways, nogood clauses from
consulted facts as in §6.

**Calibrations (same job).**
1. Planted: UNSAT with an empty nogood, 1 s.
2. Pinned: all newly freed contexts pinned to the design. Must reproduce §6. UNSAT, 256 fills, 1,704 clauses, 22 s.
   This also cross-checks the z3 master against the DFS master of job 1327013.
3. Soundness: random completions of 8 learned nogoods all fail again.
