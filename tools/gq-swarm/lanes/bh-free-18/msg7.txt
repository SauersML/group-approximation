Decide the free rules of the binary hierarchy machine: UNSAT; a free fourth letter stays undecided

A lazy SAT encoding of the local renormalization certificate, with clauses taken from the rule facts each failing
computation consulted, was calibrated first: a planted negative gives UNSAT, the zero-tape orbits give SAT and
consult no free rule, and learned nogoods are sound. It shows that none of the 1,728,000 completions of the
3-letter table passes (376 nogoods, some of them single rule facts). With a free marker letter, seeded by the
transferred clauses, the search timed out undecided (3,464 fills, 8,499 nogoods). Corrects the earlier claim
that no small set of free rules is contradictory.
