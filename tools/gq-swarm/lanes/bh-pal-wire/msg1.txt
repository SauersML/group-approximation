Wire the metabelian and linear Boone-Higman endpoints from the six open gaps

GroupApproximation/BooneHigmanLinear/FrontierFour.lean composes existing
endpoints of the metabelian chain and adds no new mathematics.  Four of the
ten frontier-three gap Statements are now proved outright on main (items 1,
5, 9, 10), so this module plugs them in and states

  finitelyPresentedMetabelianStatement_of_frontierFour
  finitelyGeneratedLinearStatement_of_frontierFour

from exactly items 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8.  The module is not imported by the
root; the Palomar Solution imports it directly.
