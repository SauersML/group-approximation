gq-borel-q: root needs only nontrivial maps of the simple PSL_n(Q)

PSL_n(Q) is simple (SL_n(Q) itself for odd n) and cofinal with GL_n(Q), so
the root is equivalent to a nontrivial homomorphism of PSL_n(Q) into a group
embeddable in a finitely presented simple group, for infinitely many n.
Faithfulness and kernel disjointness come for free. Adds the open target in
that form and its route into the root.
