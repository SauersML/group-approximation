Adelic complex of groups for SL_2(Q): not cocompact, no envelope on X

The SL_2(Q) action on the restricted product of Bruhat--Tits trees has one
vertex orbit per squarefree integer, and PGL_2(Q) has one edge orbit per
prime. A group acting on a connected graph with finitely many vertex and edge
orbits and finitely generated stabilizers is finitely generated, so no
cocompact model exists for any gq target. Every automorphism of the adelic
median graph acts prime by prime and moves finitely many base vertices, so no
finitely generated group of its automorphisms contains the standard SL_2(Q);
an envelope must mix primes. Record SL_2(Q) in B_A as the OPEN permanence
instance, with routes to the n = 2 claim and from the GL_n(Q) class.
