**ESTABLISHED** by the proof below (lane bh-free-25, 2026-09-18; elementary, not
reviewed; no novelty claimed. It is a Christol-flavoured Jacobian argument).

## Setting

Identify the boundary of the `p^d`-ary tree `(F_p^d)^*` with `F_p[[X]]^d`, sending a
sequence `(v_n)` to `Σ v_n X^n`. For `A ∈ GL_d(F_p[[X]])` and `m ∈ F_p[[X]]^d`, the affine
map `α(f) = Af + m` is a tree automorphism, because coefficient `n` of `Af + m` depends only
on coefficients `≤ n` of `f`. Write `D(g) = (g − g(0))/X` entrywise. For a letter
`v ∈ F_p^d` and `f = v + Xf'`,

```text
Af + m = (A(0)v + m(0)) + X·(A f' + D(A)v + D(m)),
```

so the section `α|_v` is the affine map `f' ↦ Af' + (D(A)v + D(m))`, with the same linear
part. Scalar affine actions (`d = 1`) and block-Toeplitz matrix actions (`d ≥ 2`, i.e.
2×2 or larger matrices over `F_p[[X]]`) are both of this form.

## Statement

Let `G` be a finitely generated group of such affine maps which is self-similar (every
section of every element lies in `G`).
1. There is a finite separable extension `L` of `F_p(X)` inside `F_p((X))` containing every
   entry of `A` and `m` for every `α = (A, m) ∈ G`. So `G ≤ AGL_d(L) ≤ GL_{d+1}(L)`.
2. The word problem of `G`, and of every finitely generated group that embeds in `G`, is
   decidable in polynomial time.
3. Let `U` be a finitely generated abelian group, and let `R` be a commutative
   `F_p[U]`-algebra quotient of Krull dimension at least `2`. Then `R ⋊ U` has no
   faithful action of this form in which `R` acts by translations. In particular this
   holds for the top factor `B_0` of a KMS group with `K ≥ 2` counters, whose lamp ring
   `F_p[ã_i^{±1}, (ã_i^{-1} − 1)^{-1} : i ≤ K]` has dimension `K`
   (`kms-configuration-modules-carry-self-similar-structures`, item 1 of What is known).

## Proof

1. Let `α_1, …, α_{2n}` be the generators and their inverses, and let `Z ∈ F_p[[X]]^N`
   list all entries of their `A_j` and `m_j`. For each `j` and each `v ∈ {0, e_1, …, e_d}`,
   fix a word `w_{j,v}` in the generators that equals `α_j|_v`.
   - Composition is `(A, m)(B, n) = (AB, An + m)`. So the translation part `τ(w)` of a word
     is a polynomial with `F_p`-coefficients in the entries `Z`.
   - Comparing translation parts gives `D(m_j) = τ(w_{j,0})`, and
     `D(A_j)e_i = τ(w_{j,e_i}) − τ(w_{j,0})`.
   - Hence `Z = Z(0) + X·P(Z)` for a polynomial map `P` over `F_p`.
   - Put `F(Y) = Y − Z(0) − X·P(Y)` over `K = F_p(X)`. Then `F(Z) = 0`, and the Jacobian
     `I − X·J_P(Z)` has determinant in `1 + XF_p[[X]]`, so it is nonzero.
   - Let `E = K(Z) ⊂ F_p((X))`. In `Ω_{E/K}` we get `Σ_l ∂F_i/∂Y_l(Z) dZ_l = 0`, and the
     Jacobian is invertible over `E`. So every `dZ_l = 0`, hence `Ω_{E/K} = 0`.
   - A finitely generated field extension with vanishing Kähler differentials is
     separable algebraic, so `E/K` is finite separable. Every element of `G` is a word,
     so its entries lie in `L = E`.
2. Write `L = K(θ)`. A product of `l` generators has entries in
   `F_p[X, h^{-1}][θ]/(μ_θ)` for a fixed `h`, with degrees `O(l)`. Exact arithmetic
   decides triviality in time polynomial in `l`. Subgroups reduce by substituting
   generator words, a linear blow-up.
3. Suppose `R` acts by translations through a `U`-equivariant `R ≅ M ⊆ L^d`, where `U`
   acts through its linear parts (`L` from 1). Let `S` be the `F_p`-span of the image of
   `U` in `M_d(L)`, a commutative ring.
   - `M` is an `S`-module, so `F_p[U] → R` factors through `S`, and `R` is a quotient of
     `S`.
   - Let `C` be the `L`-algebra generated by `S`. It is commutative and finite over `L`,
     so `C/rad(C)` is a finite product of finite extensions `L_i` of `L`, each of
     transcendence degree `1` over `F_p`.
   - `S ∩ rad(C)` is nil, so `S_red` embeds in `∏ S_i` with `S_i ⊆ L_i` finitely
     generated domains, each of Krull dimension at most `1`. Every prime of `S` contains
     one of the finitely many kernels, so `dim S ≤ 1`.
   - Hence `dim R ≤ 1`, a contradiction. ∎

## Consequences

- **The matrix route is closed in all dimensions `d`.** A hard KMS group, or any group
  whose word problem is not in polynomial time, embeds in no finitely generated affine
  self-similar group of this shape. For `fp-self-similar-groups-with-arbitrarily-hard-word-problem`,
  a hard example needs sections whose linear parts are not all equal. That means
  non-Toeplitz causal linear maps, with "corner" sections `A ↦ A^{(1)}`, or genuinely
  nonlinear maps.
- **Calibration.** For `d = 1` this recovers the transcendence-degree remark in
  `kms-configuration-modules-carry-self-similar-structures` (item 3). The one-counter
  factors `B_i` pass: their lamp ring maps into `F_p(X)` itself.
- By Christol's theorem (not imported) the entries are `p`-automatic. The maps are
  finite-state exactly when all entries are rational.
