**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, not reviewed). It uses two classical inputs, recalled and not
re-read, for `n ≥ 3`:
- Bass–Milnor–Serre: `SL_n(Z, 2) = E_n(Z, 2)`. The `n = 3` case is
  `sl3z-relative-elementary-equals-principal-congruence`.
- Relative elementary generation (Vaserstein–Suslin, Tits): `E_n(Z, 2)` is generated by
  `z_ij(c, t) = x_ji(c) x_ij(t) x_ji(−c)` with `t ∈ 2Z`, `c ∈ Z`. The `n = 3` case is
  `sl3-relative-elementary-generated-by-opposite-conjugates`.

Let `Γ_X` be as in `integral-affine-structure-group-is-finitely-presented`, and let
`H = {x ∈ Z_2^D : x_{d_0} even}`. Every halving direction is `Γ_X`-equivalent to `H`
(`integral-affine-splitting-types-have-finitely-many-tuple-orbits`). Put
`Q = {γ ∈ Γ_X : γ maps {H, X∖H} to itself}`.

**Claim.** `Q` is finitely generated.

## Proof

Translations map `H` to `H` or `X∖H`, so `Q = Z^(D) ⋊ Q_lin`, where `Q_lin` is the set of
pairs `(M, f)` preserving `H`. Projection `Q_lin → F` is onto: given `f`, compose with the
signed transposition matrix exchanging coordinates `d_0` and `f(d_0)`. The kernel is

    P = {M ∈ GL(Z; D) : row d_0 of M ≡ e_{d_0}^T (mod 2)}.

**`P` is generated by** `x_ab(1)` (`a ≠ d_0`, any `b ≠ a`), `x_{d_0 b}(2)` (`b ≠ d_0`), and the signs `δ_d`.
Each such element lies in `P`. Every element of `P` lies in `P_T = P ∩ GL_T(Z)` for a finite
`T ∋ d_0` with `n = |T| ≥ 3`. Let `N ≤ P_T` be generated by the listed elements supported
on `T`.
- `N` maps onto the image of `P_T` in `GL_n(F_2)`, the stabilizer of the row `e_{d_0}^T`.
  That stabilizer is generated by the images of the `x_ab(1)` with `a ≠ d_0`.
- `N` contains the level-2 kernel `Γ_GL(2) = SL_n(Z, 2) · ⟨δ_{d_0}⟩`. It suffices to show
  `z_ij(c, t) ∈ N` for `t` even.
  - If `j ≠ d_0`, both `x_ji(c)` and `x_ij(t)` are in `N`: `x_ij(t) = x_ij(1)^t` when
    `i ≠ d_0`, and `x_{d_0 j}(t) = x_{d_0 j}(2)^{t/2}`.
  - If `j = d_0`, pick `k ∉ {i, d_0}` and write `x_{i d_0}(t) = [x_ik(t), x_{k d_0}(1)]`. Then
    `z_{i d_0}(c, t) = [x_{d_0 i}(c) x_ik(t) x_{d_0 i}(−c), x_{d_0 i}(c) x_{k d_0}(1) x_{d_0 i}(−c)]`,
    and by the Steinberg commutator formulas the two entries are `x_{d_0 k}(ct) x_ik(t)` and
    `x_ki(−c) x_{k d_0}(1)`, both products of generators of `N` (`ct` is even).
- So `N ⊇ ker(P_T → GL_n(F_2))` and `N` maps onto the image, hence `N = P_T`.

**Finite generation.** Let `F_{d_0}` be the stabilizer of `d_0` in `F` (`≅ F × F`), which lies
in `Q_lin`. Its orbits on the listed generators are finite in number: ordered pairs `(a, b)`
with `a ≠ d_0` fall into finitely many `F_{d_0}`-orbits (relative positions of `a, b, d_0`),
and so do the points `b ≠ d_0` and `d`. So `P` is generated by finitely many
`Q_lin`-conjugacy classes, and `Q_lin` is generated by these, `F_{d_0}`, and lifts of the
generators of `F`. `Z^(D)` is generated by the `Q_lin`-orbits of `τ_{d_0}` and one `τ_d`
with `d ≠ d_0` (three `F_{d_0}`-orbits of points). ∎
