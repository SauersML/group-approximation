**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, elementary, not reviewed; no priority claimed).

## Setting

G_Q is the group of orientation-preserving PL homeomorphisms of R/Z with rational breakpoints and rational slopes. It
contains every Stein group T(Z[1/M], ⟨p|M⟩). Take f ∈ G_Q with rot(f) = α ∉ Q. By Denjoy's theorem (PL maps have
log-derivative of bounded variation), f = E ∘ R_α ∘ E^{-1} for a homeomorphism E.

**Hypothesis (X).** On a finite partition of the θ-circle into open intervals J_i, E(θ) = a_i + b_i k_i^θ with k_i > 0,
k_i ≠ 1, or E(θ) = a_i + b_i θ. Treat the affine pieces as the formal case k_i = 1.

## Statement

Under (X), let B = {rot g : g ∈ C_{G_Q}(f)}.
1. All k_i are equal, to some k.
2. If k = 1, contradiction: no f ∈ G_Q with irrational rotation number is PL-conjugate to a rotation.
3. If k ≠ 1, B ⊆ (L_k + Z)/Z, where L_k = {β ∈ R : k^β ∈ Q_{>0}} = (ln k)^{-1}·⊕_p Z ln p. This quotient is free abelian
   ⊕ finite cyclic. So α is divisible in B only by boundedly many n, and f has n-th roots in G_Q for only finitely many n.
   Boshernitzan's map (k = 3) is an instance.

## Proof

- **Centralizer.** The centralizer of R_α in Homeo_+ is the rotation group. So every g ∈ C_{G_Q}(f) is E R_β E^{-1} with
  β = rot g. And B ⊇ Zα is dense.
- **Step 1.** Take θ ∈ J_j with θ + β ∈ J_i on an open set. The slope of g at E(θ) is E'(θ+β)/E'(θ), which is const ·
  k_i^{θ+β} k_j^{−θ} (with E' constant on affine pieces). Since g is PL, this is locally constant in θ, so k_i = k_j. By
  density of Zα, every pair (i, j) occurs.
- **Step 2 (k = 1).**
  - For β = mα avoiding finitely many coincidences, g has breakpoints at E(θ_j) and E(θ_j − β), where θ_j are the
    breakpoints of E. The jump there is b_j/b_{j+1} ≠ 1. So these points are rational.
  - Slopes b_i/b_j of g are rational for all pairs, so b_i ∈ cQ. The values E(θ_{j+1}) − E(θ_j) = b_i ℓ_i are rational,
    and Σ ℓ_i = 1, so c ∈ Q, and every ℓ_i and b_i is rational.
  - Then E(θ_j − β) − E(θ_{j'}) = b_i(θ_j − θ_{j'} − β) ∈ Q forces β ∈ Q, contradicting α ∉ Q.
- **Step 3 (k ≠ 1).**
  - Take δ < min |J_i|. For β ∈ B with |β| < δ, some θ has θ and θ + β in the same J_i, so the slope there is
    k^β ∈ Q_{>0}. So B ∩ (−δ, δ) ⊆ L_k.
  - B is generated by its δ-small elements: write β as a sum of N elements of B close to β/N, using density.
  - L_k is free abelian by unique factorization, so (L_k + Z)/Z ≅ L_k/(L_k ∩ Z) is free abelian ⊕ finite cyclic.
  - α is irrational, so it is non-torsion there. Its divisibility is bounded by its free coordinates. A root h^n = f gives
    n·rot h = α in B.

## Scope and what is left

- **What this closes.** Every "linearizable" construction of an infinitely divisible minimal element: E piecewise
  exponential with one or several bases, piecewise affine, or mixed. Continuity of g_β in β (a one-parameter flow of PL
  maps has a piecewise-affine generating vector field, whose flow charts are exactly these) is the heuristic reason these
  are the natural shapes.
- **OPEN.** Minimal f ∈ G_Q with a singular (non-piecewise-exponential) Denjoy conjugacy, and minimal components of Stein
  V-elements that are not rotations (AIET-type). Any infinitely divisible minimal Stein element must be of one of these
  kinds.
