**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, elementary, inline; not reviewed; no novelty claimed).

## Statement

Let `R_L` be the ring of `leavitt-resolvent-ring-is-fp-simple-of-char-zero`
(generators `s_1, s_2, t_1, t_2, N, A`; relations `t_i s_j = δ_ij`, `s_1t_1 + s_2t_2 = 1`,
`N s_1 = s_1(N+1)`, `N s_2 = 0`, `A(N+1) = 1 = (N+1)A`).

1. **Two-sided division.** For every nonzero `x ∈ R_L` there are `a, b ∈ R_L` with `axb = 1`.
   More precisely there is a word `β` ending in the letter `2` such that `x s_β` is a nonzero
   element of the copy of `L_Q(1,2)` generated by `Q` and the `s_i, t_i`.
2. **Consequences** (by `leavitt-steinberg-hosts-must-live-in-the-stabilization-kernel`):
   - `PE_N(R_L)` is simple for every `N >= 3`; `(R_L, N)` has the sandwich property.
   - Every finitely presented simple quotient `St_N(R_L)/M` satisfies
     `St_N(R_L) = U_N · M`, i.e. it is a quotient of the stabilization kernel
     `U_N = ker(K_2(N,R_L) -> K_2(R_L))`.
3. **An explicit finitely generated simple group.** `PE_N(R_L)` (`N >= 3`) is a finitely
   generated simple group with solvable word problem that contains `GL_(N-2)(Q)`, via
   `g ↦ diag(g, det(g)^(-1), 1)`. It is not finitely presented
   (`ibn-failure-makes-rational-scalars-elementary`, part 3).

## Proof

*Setup.* Put `B = Q[N][(N+c)^(-1) : c >= 1]`. From the relations, for every `f ∈ B`:
- `f(N) s_1 = s_1 f(N+1)`: true for `N` by the relation, and for `(N+c)^(-1)` because
  `(N+c) s_1 = s_1 (N+1+c)` gives `(N+c)^(-1) s_1 = s_1 (N+1+c)^(-1)`;
- `f(N) s_2 = s_2 f(0)`: `N s_2 = 0`, and `(N+c) s_2 = s_2 c` gives `(N+c)^(-1) s_2 = s_2 c^(-1)`.

Here `f(j) ∈ Q` for every integer `j >= 0`, since the poles of elements of `B` lie at
`N = -c`, `c >= 1`. Hence for a word `γ = 1^j 2 w`, `f(N) s_γ = f(j) s_γ`.

By the normal form of `leavitt-nekrashevych-completion-simplicity-criterion`, part 1
(`R_L` is the completion `O_ψ` over `B`), every `x` is a finite sum
`x = Σ s_μ f_(μν) t_ν` with `f_(μν) ∈ B`. Let `L` exceed every `|ν|` occurring.

*Choice of `β`.* `R_L` is simple and nonzero, so its model representation on `Q^(X)`
(`X` the tail class of `2^∞`, as in the simplicity node) is faithful. Pick `ξ ∈ X` with
`x e_ξ ≠ 0`. Since `ξ` ends in `2^∞`, choose `m > L` with `ξ_m = 2`, and let `β` be the
prefix of `ξ` of length `m` and `ξ'` the rest. Then `s_β e_(ξ') = e_ξ`, so `x s_β ≠ 0`.

*`x s_β` lies in `L_Q(1,2)`.* For each term, `t_ν s_β = 0` unless `ν` is a prefix of `β`,
and then `t_ν s_β = s_(β_ν)` with `β = ν β_ν`, `β_ν` nonempty (as `|β| > L > |ν|`) and
ending in `2`. So `s_μ f_(μν) t_ν s_β = f_(μν)(j_ν) s_μ s_(β_ν)`, where `j_ν` is the number
of leading `1`s of `β_ν`. Summing, `x s_β` is a `Q`-linear combination of words in the `s_i`,
a nonzero element of the subring `L'` generated by `Q` and `s_1, s_2, t_1, t_2`.

*Division.* `Q` is central in `R_L` (the inverse of a central unit is central), so there
is a unital `Q`-algebra map `L_Q(1,2) -> L'`; it is onto, and injective because `L_Q(1,2)` is
simple and `L' ≠ 0`. `L_Q(1,2)` has two-sided division (Lean theorem
`exists_mul_mul_eq_one`, `GroupApproximation/Leavitt/LeavittSimplicity.lean`, over any
field). So there are `a', b' ∈ L'` with `a' (x s_β) b' = 1`; take `a = a'`, `b = s_β b'`.

*Part 2* is `leavitt-steinberg-hosts-must-live-in-the-stabilization-kernel`, parts 1 and 3,
with the Leavitt family `s_1, s_2, t_1, t_2`.

*Part 3.* `PE_N(R_L)` is generated by the images of the `x_ij(r)` with `r` in the finite
generating set of `R_L` together with `1`. Its word problem is solvable: `E_N(R_L)` has
solvable word problem (`st-n-leavitt-resolvent-word-problem-reduces-to-k2`, item 1), and a
word is trivial in `PE_N(R_L)` iff its matrix commutes with the finitely many generator
matrices, which is decidable. `SL_N(Q) <= E_N(R_L)` since `Q ⊆ R_L` unitally, and the image
of `g ↦ diag(g, det(g)^(-1), 1)` meets the centre (scalar matrices `cI`, `c ∈ Z(R_L)`) only in
`1`, because the last diagonal entry is `1`. So `GL_(N-2)(Q)` embeds in `PE_N(R_L)`.
Non-finite-presentation is the cited node, as `R_L` has a unital Leavitt pair.
