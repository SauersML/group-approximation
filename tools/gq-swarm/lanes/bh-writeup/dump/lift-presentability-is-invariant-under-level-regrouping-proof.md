Notation of `rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation`; `N = ker(F -> G)` is the same for both
actions. Write `⟨⟨R⟩⟩_1` and `⟨⟨R⟩⟩_m` for the smallest level-1 and level-`m` lift ideals containing `R` (a
level-`m` lift ideal: normal, and containing every `w` that acts trivially on `X^m` and whose level-`m` sections
lie in it). By Theorem A for each action, the Statement says: `N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_1` for a finite `R` iff
`N = ⟨⟨R'⟩⟩_m` for a finite `R'`.

## Three facts about trivially acting words

For `u in N` and `g in F`: (a) `u` acts trivially, so for every vertex `v`,
`(g u g^{-1})|_v = g|_{g^{-1}v} · u|_{g^{-1}v} · (g|_{g^{-1}v})^{-1}`, a conjugate of a section of `u`;
(b) sections of products of elements of `N` are products of their sections; (c) `N` is section-closed.

## (⇐) A level-1 lift ideal is a level-m lift ideal

Let `M` be a level-1 lift ideal, and let `w` act trivially on `X^m` with all level-`m` sections in `M`. For
`|v| = m-1`, `w|_v` has trivial root permutation and its level-1 sections are level-`m` sections of `w`, so
`w|_v in M`. Repeating for `|v| = m-2, …, 0` gives `w in M`. Hence `⟨⟨R⟩⟩_m ⊆ ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_1 ⊆ N`, and
`N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_m` forces `N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_1`.

## (⇒) The level-1 closure is caught at level m

Let `N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_1`, `R` finite, and let `R'` be the finite set of all sections of elements of `R` at levels
`0, …, m-1` (all in `N` by (c)). Put `M = ⟨⟨R'⟩⟩_m`. Build `⟨⟨R⟩⟩_1` in stages: `M_0 = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩`,
`M_{α+1} = ⟨⟨M_α ∪ L_1(M_α)⟩⟩` with `L_1(M_α)` the words with trivial root permutation and all level-1 sections
in `M_α`, and unions at limits.

**Claim.** For every `α`, every element of `M_α`, and every section of it at a level `< m`, lies in `M`.
- `α = 0`: by (a) and (b), a section at level `j < m` of an element of `⟨⟨R⟩⟩` is a product of conjugates of
  level-`j` sections of elements of `R`, which lie in `R'`.
- Successor: `M_{α+1}` is generated by conjugates of elements `u in M_α ∪ L_1(M_α)`, and by (a), (b) it suffices
  to treat the sections of such `u` at levels `j < m`. For `u in M_α` this is the induction hypothesis. Let
  `u in L_1(M_α)`. For `1 <= j < m`, the level-`j` sections of `u` are level-`(j-1)` sections of the `u|_x in M_α`,
  so they lie in `M`. For `j = 0`: `u in N` acts trivially on `X^m`, and its level-`m` sections are
  level-`(m-1)` sections of the `u|_x in M_α`, which lie in `M`; since `M` is a level-`m` lift ideal, `u in M`.
- Limits: unions.

So `N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_1 ⊆ M ⊆ N`, and `N = ⟨⟨R'⟩⟩_m`. ∎

## Corollaries

In the level-`m` action a commonly fixed word `u` is a letter fixed by every state, with section map
`σ_u(s) = s|_u`. Corollary 1 is `loop-retraction-forces-fp-eventual-section-groups` for that action, transferred
by the Statement; Corollary 2 is `commonly-fixed-letters-compress-to-faithful-actions` for that action.
