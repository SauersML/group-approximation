**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, not reviewed; no novelty claimed).

Setting: `P = A_0 * A_1`, `h_i = |A_i| - 1`, coprime `h_0, h_1`, the least-significant-first
mixed-radix enumeration `nu` of `unequal-radix-free-product-near-group-passes-both-germ-gates`,
and `R_nu <= H` as in `two-radix-odometer-host-is-f-infinity-with-fg-charge-kernel`.
Put `G_eps = < lambda(A_eps), s >`.

1. **The halves are the one-type benchmark groups.** `G_eps` lies in the subgroup
   `B_eps <= H` of elements with a table whose moduli are powers of `h_eps`. The projection
   `X -> {0,1} x Z_(h_eps)` is `B_eps`-equivariant and injective on `B_eps`, since the leaf
   formulas are affine. It identifies `B_eps` with `V_(h_eps,2)(<a>)`. Under this identification
   `lambda_x` (x in A_0) is the table `lambda^(0)_x` of `arithmetic-a5-near-group-not-fp-proof`,
   and `lambda_z` (z in A_1) is `lambda^(1)_z`, with `h = h_eps`, the same `s` and the same odometer.
2. **Each half is not finitely presented** when `h_eps >= 3`. The proof is that of
   `arithmetic-a5-near-group-not-fp-proof`, with two changes:
   - *Edge-transitivity from one factor.* The edges at vertex `(eps)` are
     `lambda(A_eps) e`, and the edges at the other root vertex are their images under `s`,
     which flips `e`.
   - *The pushing element.* Take `g = lambda_(z') s^(±1)`, which maps the root cone of the
     `eps` side rigidly, with carry 0, onto a depth-one cone `(eps, z')`. Then
     `e_n = g^n (c_x c_y^-1) g^-n` with `c_x = [lambda_x, s^(2h)]` generates `Z^infinity`, for
     three distinct digits `x, y, z'`.

   Steps 2 and 4 of that proof (locally virtually abelian stabilizers, abelian rank bound)
   apply verbatim.
3. **The halves meet in a virtually abelian group.** `G_0 ∩ G_1 <= B_0 ∩ B_1`. An element of
   `B_0 ∩ B_1` is affine on every root: by the Chinese remainder theorem each positive-depth
   `h_1`-cone meets every `h_0`-cone. Its slope is then a power of both `h_0` and `h_1`, so it is 1.
   So `G_0 ∩ G_1 <= {(eps,y) |-> (pi(eps), y + g_eps)} = Z^2 x| Z/2`.
4. **`R_nu` is not the amalgam.** The element `c_x c_y^-1` of `G_0`, supported in root 0, and
   its analogue in `G_1`, supported in root 1, commute in `R_nu`. Their commutator is a
   reduced word of length 4 in `G_0 *_(G_0∩G_1) G_1`, so the natural map from that amalgam onto
   `R_nu` is not injective. Finite presentation of `R_nu` is therefore not refuted by the
   standard fact that amalgams over finitely generated subgroups are finitely presented only
   if their factors are.
5. **`R_nu` is not a group of Bass--Serre tree automorphisms.** Identify `X` with the ends of
   the Bass--Serre tree of `A_0 * A_1`. Haar measure is the visual measure from the base edge.
   A type-preserving automorphism of this `(h_0+1, h_1+1)`-biregular tree has local slope
   `h_0^a h_1^b` with `|a - b| <= 2`. The element `(lambda_z s)^n` (`z` in `A_1`) has slope
   `h_1^-n` on a cone. So the abelian-rank mechanism of the equal-radix kill does not apply to
   `R_nu` itself.
6. **Calibration against finite index.** For `A_5 * A_6`, take `x` in `A_5` with digit 29 and
   `z` in `A_6` with digit 179. Then `lambda_x s` and `s^-1 (lambda_z s^-1) s` both fix
   `p = (0, -1/2)`, with slopes 59 and 359. The germ group of `H` at `p` is
   `<59, 359> = Z^2`, so `R_nu` realizes the full germ group of `H` there. This is consistent
   with `[H : R_nu] < infinity`, which remains OPEN.
