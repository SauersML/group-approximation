## Setting

Let `m >= 2` and `n >= 1` be coprime integers, and let `C_m = {0, …, m-1}^N`. The Katsura self-similar action for the
`1 x 1` matrices `A = (m)` and `B = (n)` (Exel-Pardo, arXiv:1409.1107, Example `KatsuraExample`, read at source in
`gq/src/kep/`) is the action of `Z` on `C_m` given by

    σ_k(j ξ) = ((kn + j) mod m) · σ_{⌊(kn+j)/m⌋}(ξ),     k ∈ Z, j ∈ {0, …, m-1}.

Write `σ = σ_1`. For `(m, n) = (2, 1)` this is the binary odometer. For `(m, n) = (2, 3)` it is the "add one in
rational base 3/2" map, with `σ(0ξ) = 1σ(ξ)` and `σ(1ξ) = 0σ²(ξ)`.

## Statement

1. **Renormalization.** `σ^{m^k}(w ξ) = w σ^{n^k}(ξ)` for every word `w` of length `k`.
2. **Odometer.** `σ` is level-transitive on the `m`-ary tree. So `σ` is conjugate, by an automorphism of that tree (an
   isometry of `C_m`), to the `m`-adic odometer `x ↦ x + 1` on `Z_m`. In particular it has infinite order.
3. **The BS relation.** On `C_m × C_m`, put `a = σ × id` and let `b(jξ, y) = (ξ, jy)` be the baker map. Then
   `b a^m b^-1 = a^n`. Both maps lie in the higher-rank-graph full group generated by the Brin-Thompson group `2V_m`
   and `a`, the analog of `2V_τ` for `(A,B) = ((m),(n))`.
4. **Only the metabelian shadow.** By item 4 of `odometer-generators-carry-no-nonsolvable-baumslag-solitar`, with
   `K = Z_m` and `n` a unit of `Z_m`, every `t` in any group containing `a` with `t a^m t^-1 = a^n` gives an
   abelian-by-cyclic `<a, t>`. So for `m, n >= 2` no choice of `t`, the baker map or any twist of it, turns the
   Katsura generator into a faithful `BS(m,n)`. For `t = b` the image is the abelian-by-cyclic quotient of `BS(m,n)`.

## Why it matters for explicit hosts

- One-vertex Katsura hosts (the natural `2V_τ`-type hosts over rational bases) cannot carry non-solvable
  Baumslag-Solitar groups through their Katsura generator. The rational base only relabels the `m`-adic odometer.
- A faithful Katsura-type model of `BS(m,n)` must see the primes of both `m` and `n` in its generator's closure. The
  Bass-Serre boundary action is one: there the self-similar graph needs two vertex types, one for each edge type. That
  route is the tree method of Bux-Llosa Isenrich-Wu (and the path-category models of Spielberg), so it is not new.
- If `m | n`, the Katsura action is trivial: `σ(jξ) = jσ^{n/m}(ξ)` fixes every level. Exel-Pardo note (right after
  their Katsura example) that the `N = 1` action "might not be faithful".
