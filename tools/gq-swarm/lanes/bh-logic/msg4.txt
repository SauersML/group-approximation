embeds in DV_F. The claim circulant-graph-wreath-products-satisfy-boone-higman is
now ESTABLISHED.

route. Unconditionally, for recursively presented groups, embeddability in some
finitely presented simple group is Sigma_3-complete, and so is embeddability in

other members have undecidable word problem. Item 5 of
boone-higman-is-equality-of-two-sigma-3-index-sets is updated.
