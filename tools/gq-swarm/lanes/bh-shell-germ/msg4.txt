Fixed-base inputs: torsion congruence and a digit-rotation seed

In a fixed-base shell enumeration over V_(h,r)(<a>), every finite subgroup C of the
input satisfies gcd(|C|, h-1) | r (calibrated on the A_5*A_5 enumeration, h = 59, r = 2),
and the level-one digit rotation v gives s v^-1 = the local odometer on digit 0.
