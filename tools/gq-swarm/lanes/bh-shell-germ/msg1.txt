Shell germ gate collapses to one clause: a finitely presented window group

The window inclusion identifies R_nu as the mapping torus of the window group W_N, so
W_N finitely presented implies R_nu, F_nu and the shell germ group Q are finitely
presented. Calibrated on the two-ended zigzag input, and the contrapositive shows the
square spiral window group W_2 of Z^2 is finitely generated metabelian but not finitely
presented.
