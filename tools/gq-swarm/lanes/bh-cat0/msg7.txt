BS classes over virtually quotient-free groups: big tree kernels, virtually K semidirect F_m

If the finite residual of G has finite index (e.g. G virtually simple), the finite
residuals of the vertex groups of any member of BS_G are one normal subgroup fixing
the tree, of finite index in every vertex group; so the member is virtually
K semidirect F_m, and virtually K x F_m when the outer action is finite. For a
lattice in three trees with a virtually simple fibre (the non-residually-finite case
BLIW cannot reach), this gives B_A when the projection to the other two factors is
discrete, and isolates the remaining case: F_m acting through a non-discrete
normalizer. Lane proof, elementary; no priority claimed.
