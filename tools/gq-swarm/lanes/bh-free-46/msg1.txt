Answer arXiv:2609.01868 Question 1.1 negatively: Z wr_X PSL_2(Z[1/2]) is not residually finite

PSL_2(Z) maps onto every finite quotient of PSL_2(Z[1/n]): the image of the unipotent
Z[1/n] is generated by the image of 1, and Z[1/n] is Euclidean, so elementary matrices
generate. Hence every finite quotient of A wr_X PSL_2(Z[1/n]) over the cosets of PSL_2(Z)
identifies all lamps, and the wreath product is not residually finite. It therefore lies in
no self-similar group, which refutes the printed question's positive form. This conflicts
with the source's Theorem B(3) and its "residually finite" citation of Cornulier. Lane
proof, elementary, not reviewed; no priority claimed for the density lemma.
