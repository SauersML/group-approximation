Record the rational-scalar obstruction for Leavitt resolvent units

scalars die in K_1; if K_1 = U^ab (purely infinite simple) and the derived unit
group D is perfect, then D/Z(D) cannot be finitely presented, since Z(D) would
be a quotient of a finitely generated Schur multiplier yet contains Q^x.
