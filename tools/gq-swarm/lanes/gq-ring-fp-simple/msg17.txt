Refute the capacity-chain presentation: a bi-infinite chain module

The finite relations of R_cap admit a module on words over a bi-infinite
c-chain with capacity 1/2 - n; X_k = [L=k](1 - sum_{i<=k}[Delta=i]) acts
as 1 there but vanishes in the concrete model, so the presented ring is not
simple, with or without a_u. Comparisons between two unbounded registers
create hidden positivity identities that finitely many relations cannot
generate. Mark the candidate refuted.
