Characteristic-zero ring envelopes: Q(t_1..t_k), representable rings, Q-bar

Q(t_1..t_k) embeds in an explicit finitely presented simple ring R_k (registers,
one transported resolvent G = N + 1 + sum (t_i - C_i) P_i, simplicity by the
Leavitt-Nekrashevych criterion). Hence every ring embeddable in
M_D(C (x) Q<x>), C a finitely generated commutative Q-algebra or field, embeds
in a finitely presented simple ring. Algebraic closures transfer through
R (x) L_Q(1,2), so every countable field of finite transcendence degree,
including Q-bar, embeds. For Q-algebras, envelopes over Z and over Q agree
(centre of R_L is Q). Open: Q(t_1, t_2, ...), and the general ring form, which
is the algebra Boone-Higman conjecture over Q. Lane proofs, not refereed.
