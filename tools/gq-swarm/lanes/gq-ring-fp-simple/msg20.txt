Prove the two-register squeeze: comparison presentations are never simple

In a register presentation (commutative registers, polynomial substitutions,
zero-indicator availability, CK and covariance), every base-closure element acts
as a constructible piecewise-rational function of the registers. Hence any
finitely many extra commutative relations true on a Zariski-dense integral model
also hold on a generic translation chain at all but finitely many levels, while
X_k = [L=k](1 - sum [Delta=i]) separates: the presented ring is not simple.
Covers the capacity chain with any added relations; dilation fibres,
noncommutative relations, non-register hosts and sparse models are outside.
