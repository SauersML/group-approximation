Semiprime PI Q-algebras embed in simple rings fp over Z; tape attempt

Every finitely generated semiprime PI Q-algebra (so every semiprime one of
GK dimension <= 1, by Small-Stafford-Warfield) embeds in M_N(C_0) with C_0 a
finitely generated commutative Q-algebra (Posner on prime quotients, bounded
PI degree), hence in a simple ring finitely presented over Z. Record the
shift-tape attempt at Q(t_1, t_2, ...): the tape over a Thompson crossed
product is finitely presented, but its values are one-pass bounded-rank
programs, which miss generic polynomials; diagonal subfields of LN
completions have transcendence degree at most dim B.
