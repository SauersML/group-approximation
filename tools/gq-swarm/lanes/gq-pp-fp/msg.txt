Prove divisible subgroups of fg projective/PL circle groups act freely

Full proof, as a gq-pp-fp artifact, of the statement drafted by gq-pp-psl2q
(pp-circle-divisible-subgroups-act-freely, not yet on main). In a group of
circle homeomorphisms that are piecewise in a projective or affine group
without divisible subgroups (every finitely generated host qualifies),
every divisible abelian subgroup acts freely, rot is injective on it, and
its normalizer centralizes it. Corollaries: such hosts and their central
lifts to R contain no Aff(Q), U_3(Q), SL_2(Q), GL_n(Q) (n >= 2), even
nonstandardly; finitely generated finitely-pieced PL or projective groups
of an interval or the line contain no (Q,+). Verdict: no one-dimensional
finitely-pieced piecewise-projective candidate reaches the
finite-presentation stage. Lane proof, not independently reviewed.
