t < 2^(n(L+2K+1)) has |δ_{g^t}(x)|_∞ > K; so limsup E_g(N)/log N ≥ 1/(2n).

times do not exist; the growth gap for Higman images is open only along
sparse times. Gives Θ(log) movement for every Baumslag–Solitar base in nV
(with the existing upper bound); fails in 2V_tau (odometer). Records attempt 2
on the gap node: the double BS chain in nV would refute the gap at powers of 2.
