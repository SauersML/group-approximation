Tree-by-complex lattices with faithful tree factor are in B_A; Amir–Lazarovich

Lane bh-testcases. A cocompact lattice in Aut(T) × Aut(Y), T a locally finite
tree and Y simply connected locally finite, lies in B_A when it acts faithfully
on T: vertex stabilizers act properly cocompactly on Y, so edge stabilizers are
finitely presented, and BLIW's tree criterion applies. Just-infiniteness
supplies faithfulness. Amir–Lazarovich's lattices Λ' (Theorem A proof, read at
source: vertex-transitive projections, just-infinite by Bader–Shalom) are
therefore in B_A, so Λ', Λ^k and Λ' × (B_A) embed in f.p. simple groups.
