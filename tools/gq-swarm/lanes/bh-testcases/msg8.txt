Higman images in brick hosts: exponent sums O((log m)^2) along 2^m

Lane bh-testcases. For any homomorphism from H4 to an isometric-section brick
group (nV, 2V_tau, affine 2V_{G_N}), the cycle of conjugations gives
E_{i+1}(2^m) <= L + 2 E_i(m); unwinding around the cycle yields
E_i(2^m) = O((log m)^2) and E_i(N) = O(log N (log log N)^2). So H4 -> 2V_tau
(and H4 -> nV) is trivial as soon as brick maps with unbounded exponent sums
must grow faster than (log m)^2 along 2^m; that growth gap is OPEN.
Lane proof, unreviewed; no priority claimed.
