Higher-rank MIF: strongly regular building elements are opposition-proximal

proximal (both parts) for every locally finite thick Euclidean building,
modulo standard facts (opposite germs, common subsectors, apartment isometries,
cone topology): opposition is open with dense slices, and strongly regular
elements contract the chambers opposite their repelling pole uniformly on
compacta. This is premise (PR2)/(D2-dynamics) of the Titz Mite–Witzel MIF
route. Also records explicit non-domestic open sets for strongly regular
elements on the nowhere-domestic node, and isolates the crux in singular
(panel-tree) directions. Lesson paragraphs included. Lane proof, unreviewed.
