Sandwiched elementary groups have only projective simple quotients

Under the sandwich property SW_N, every nonabelian simple quotient of E_N(R)
is PE_N(R/I_H) (elementary proof inline), so the rational-scalar and
full-corner budgets govern every E_N-built host. Bass's normal subgroup
theorem (recalled, not read) gives SW_N at finite stable rank; the remaining
loophole is simple rings of infinite stable rank with PE_N not simple.
Lane proof, not reviewed; no novelty claimed.
