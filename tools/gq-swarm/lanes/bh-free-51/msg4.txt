Perfect subgroups are locally trivial in abelian-germ hosts; no curve points for closed MCG

- perfect-subgroups-are-locally-trivial-in-abelian-germ-hosts (+ proof): in any
  action by maps that are locally in a base with torsion-free abelian point germs
  (V, Higman-Thompson, Brin-Thompson nV, germ extensions of these, normal-form
  boundary Thompson groups) off finite sets, a finitely generated subgroup with
  finite rational abelianization is locally trivial at all but finitely many of
  its fixed points (those lie in its generators' singular sets and are permuted
  by its centralizer); in V or nV its fixed set is clopen.
- Corollary for closed Mod(S_g), g >= 4: an equivariant injective choice of
  points p_d fixed by Stab^+(d) forces Stab^+(d), including T_d, to act trivially
  near p_d. So exploded designs with twist poles per curve are excluded in every
  such host, including nV; curve data must be fixed-point-free, at finitely many
  nonabelian singular germs, or in a host with nonabelian point germs (T_m).
