Centralizers of elements with a dense-basin attractor embed in the germ group there

A homeomorphism with an attracting fixed point of open dense basin has its centralizer
(on that point's stabilizer) embedded in the germ group at the point. In 2V, where corner
germ groups are Z^2, a diagonal north-south flow z^p x z^q with pq nonzero has centralizer
exactly Z^2. This kills the diagonal-fiber design for two-leaf graph manifolds recorded in
the automatic-germ-extensions artifact, and gives the general rule that large centralizers
need large germ groups at attractors.
