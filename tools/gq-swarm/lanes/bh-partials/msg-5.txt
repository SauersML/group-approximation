BBMZ Question 1.1: closed surface groups are contracting RSGs (Bowen–Series coding)

Completes the Bowen–Series route, as a lane proof that has not been reviewed.
- Step 4: Γ acts on the Cantorized circle, by Bowen–Series 1979 Lemma 2.4 read at source (orbit equivalence up to pairs
  lying in Π).
- Step 5: the nucleus is finite. Itineraries are directed geodesic tile rays, the level of a cut point is the time its
  ray enters the carrier of its wall, and fellow travelling matches the level defect to the Busemann shift.
So every closed hyperbolic surface group and every cocompact Fuchsian group is isomorphic to a contracting RSG. Referee
requested on the points marked (★).

Lesson for general BH: the irreducible core belongs to the coding, not to the group. Contraction is a "level = Busemann
time" statement, the same mechanism as bounded level defect for Pisot Stein groups.
