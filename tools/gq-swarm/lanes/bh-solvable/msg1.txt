Residually finite soluble groups of finite rank satisfy Boone–Higman

Pyber–Segal (Thm 1.1, Cor 1.3; read in the TeX) make every finitely generated
residually finite virtually soluble group of finite Prüfer rank linear in
characteristic zero, so the reviewed quasi-linear node puts it in B_A. Also
records the open non-residually-finite minimax case with Abels' central
quotient as calibration, and a status note on soluble classes.
