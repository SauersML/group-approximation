Levelled actions: split finitary extensions of virtually nilpotent groups are never FP_2

Corollary added to levelled-actions-make-finitary-extensions-not-fp2: for G finitely generated
virtually nilpotent and any action with an infinite orbit, FSym(X) x| G is not FP_2 over Q, since
subgroups spanning H_1 of a nilpotent group have finite index.
