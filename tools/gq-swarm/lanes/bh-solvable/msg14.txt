Split finitary criterion: the covering obstruction is vacuous for fp groups; partial-torus Abels hosts tested

For fp G acting on cosets of a fg subgroup, the scale-F nerve is simply connected, so only
line-type or homological obstructions remain. The partial-torus Abels hosts pass the stabilizer
law but keep unbounded fibre pair types, so gate (b) is unchanged; fallback is near actions or germ hosts.
