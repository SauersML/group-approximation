Reduce factorial-odometer radix-changing Aff(Q) hosts to Stein interval-exchange groups

Every rational has a finite factorial expansion, so the factorial odometer is the doubled-rationals
Cantor space. Its radix-changing value-affine homeomorphisms form the rational Stein-Thompson group,
and each finitely generated subgroup lies in a Stein group V(1; Z[1/M], <p|M>), which has a type (A)
breakpoint action. So this host embeds Aff(Q) exactly when a Stein group does (open, with a route to BH).
In a Stein group, an element with a hyperbolic periodic point has roots of only boundedly many orders,
so copies of Q or BS(1,m) need infinite-order elements without hyperbolic periodic points.
