Compute the horofunction atoms of closed surface groups as cells of wall arrangements

- New claim surface-group-atoms-are-wall-arrangement-cells, with proof
  surface-group-atoms-wall-cells-proof (lane proof, not reviewed). For pi_1(Sigma_g) with the
  regular 4g-gon generators:
  - word length counts separating tiling walls;
  - the atoms of B_n are the unbounded cells of a finite wall arrangement;
  - the horofunction boundary has no isolated points;
  - cells that are half-planes are gated.
- So every hypothesis of the Belk-Bleak-Matucci-Zaremsky theorems holds except the irreducible
  core, which remains open for surface groups.
- Lesson for general BH: gated half-plane cells are the one-ended replacement for cut vertices.
  For groups whose word metric counts separating walls, the obstruction lives only in the
  non-median part of the wall geometry.
