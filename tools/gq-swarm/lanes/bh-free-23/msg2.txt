Every free product of nontrivial hyperbolic groups is a contracting rational similarity group

This extends the Belk-Bleak-Matucci-Zaremsky G * Z theorem (arXiv:2309.06224,
thrm:FreeProductBoundary) from a Z factor to an arbitrary nontrivial hyperbolic factor.
- New claim free-products-of-hyperbolic-groups-are-contracting-rsgs, with proof route
  hyperbolic-free-product-contracting-rsg-proof. Lane proof, not reviewed.
- How it works: shadows of points just past a syllable change form one atom type, and
  translating a factor branch into such a shadow copies every atom type. So the type graph
  has an irreducible core.
- What it settles: BBMZ-hyperbolic Question 1.1 for every freely decomposable non-elementary
  hyperbolic group. The OPEN node for the question is updated, and the finite-factor node is
  marked as a special case.
