Contracting rational similarity groups are closed under finite-index overgroups, so SL_2(Z) and every virtually free group is one

New results, lane proofs, not reviewed:
- contracting-rsgs-closed-under-finite-index-overgroups: induced action on one labelled copy of
  the address space per coset.
- virtually-free-product-hyperbolic-groups-are-contracting-rsgs: every non-elementary
  virtually free group (SL_2(Z), GL_2(Z), ...) and every virtually torsion-free hyperbolic group
  with infinitely many ends is isomorphic to a contracting RSG.

With these, BBMZ-hyperbolic Question 1.1 reduces to the one-ended case. The Q1.1 node records the
reduction and a Markov-coding attempt at the one-ended case. The finite-product node is marked as
a special case.
