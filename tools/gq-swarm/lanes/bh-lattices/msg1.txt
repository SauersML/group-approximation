Show three-tree lattices with a discrete factor image satisfy permutational Boone-Higman

A discrete factor image makes that factor's kernel a lattice on the remaining
trees. The normalizer of such a lattice is discrete by a centralizer argument,
so the lattice splits virtually as kernel times a free group. For three trees
the kernel is a two-tree lattice, already in the type (A) class, so the whole
lattice is too. The open multi-tree claim is reduced to totally non-discrete
lattices whose fibres fail the normal subgroup property (Attempt 4).
