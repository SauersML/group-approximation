Prove Lemma H and put all cocompact lattices on reducible Euclidean buildings in B_A

Lemma H: an automorphism of a thick Euclidean building (or tree) fixing an open set
of chambers at infinity fixes an apartment and balls of every radius pointwise. It
needs no discreteness, so it gives effectiveness factorwise for lattices in products.
The proof: branching apartments across walls of every direction kill the
translation and diagram parts, and convexity plus a CAT(0) comparison put balls
inside shadow cones.

With product boxes as cells, complete reducibility factor by factor gives strongly
connected pieces, and the rescale, phase and stabilize machine then puts every
cocompact lattice on a finite product of thick Euclidean buildings and trees in B_A,
irreducible lattices and torsion included. Lesson: finite boundary codings multiply.
