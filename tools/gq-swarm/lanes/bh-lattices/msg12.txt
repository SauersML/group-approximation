State the finitely coded Cantor host machine in its general form

For Li boundary groupoids of finite degree categories with strongly connected
pieces, arbitrary unit groups allowed, the comparison lemma after Cuntz
stabilization needs nothing beyond UFP*, local finiteness modulo units, and the two
Cuntz loops. So whenever the stabilized full group is finitely presented, it is a
type (A) host, and all subgroups of the full group lie in B_A. The node lists the
instances on main and the limits: exponential word problem for finite unit groups,
and the central-extension obstruction. It includes a lesson for general BH.
