Replace the cited Matui comparison with a direct cylinder-refinement argument

The first version of the stabilized k-graph node cited a general comparison
theorem of Matui for purely infinite minimal groupoids. Read at source, Matui's
comparison (arXiv:1210.5800, Theorem Hopf) covers only shifts of finite type. The
comparison is now proved directly: refining cylinder decompositions spreads any
clopen set over its strongly connected piece, and the Cuntz colour then
equalizes counts. The virtually torsion-free lattice route correspondingly splits
the powered cube k-graph into strongly connected pieces, instead of using minimal
components.
