Establish: Steinberg groups of fp rings are fp in rank five

Record gq-referee-c direct proof, with the proof-gap review by gq-referee-a,
that St_n(R) is finitely presented for every finitely presented unital ring R
and every n >= 5, with an explicit presentation. This removes the
Krstic-McCool import from every rank >= 5 Steinberg route to an explicit
finitely presented overgroup of GL_n(Q).
