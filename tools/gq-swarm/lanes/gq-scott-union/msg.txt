Kill natural gluings of affine hosts for GL_n(Q) (derivative cocycle)

A finitely generated group whose generators have finitely many linear parts
(piecewise affine or piecewise projective) has all derivatives in GL_N(R) for
a finitely generated ring R, which never contains Q. So divisible subgroups
and SL_n(Q) act with trivial derivative at their fixed points, and GL_n(Q),
(Q,+), Aff(Q) and PSL_2(Q) cannot act with their natural derivatives at a
fixed point. Gluing Scott's Sc(n), Zaremsky's affine hosts or adelic V-type
groups along the chain GL_n(Z[1/m!]) while keeping the natural action
therefore never gives a finitely generated group; BHM's fixed-point-free
Q <= T-bar is not affected.
