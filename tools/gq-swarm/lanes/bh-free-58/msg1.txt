bh-free-58: Kourovka 21.74(b),(c) — finite-cycle problems in CT(Z) are undecidable

For a reversible 2-counter machine M, explicit words F_M and H_M in class
transpositions (moduli {2,3,5,7,11,13}-smooth) satisfy two equivalences:
F_M has only finite cycles iff every configuration of M is periodic, and
H_M has no finite cycles iff M has no periodic configuration. Kari–Ollinger
(MFCS 2008) Theorems 3 and 6 then settle 21.74(b) and (c) negatively.
H_M fills the garbage classes with conjugates of an explicit five-transposition
element psi that has no finite cycles and permutes no nontrivial
residue-class partition (new node).

Lane proof, not reviewed. Parts 21.74(a) and 21.73 remain open.
