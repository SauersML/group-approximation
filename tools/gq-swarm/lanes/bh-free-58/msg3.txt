bh-free-58: finite subgroups of CT(Z) are classified by stabilizer sets (Kourovka 21.73, torsion case)

Two embeddings of a finite group into CT(Z) are conjugate iff the same
subgroups occur as point stabilizers on Z. So torsion elements are conjugate
iff they have the same cycle lengths (every one is conjugate into V), and
conjugacy is decidable whenever one element has finite order. Consequence:
Salo-type reductions (finite-order yes-instances, as for 2V) cannot work in
CT(Z); any undecidability proof for 21.73 needs infinite-order yes-instances.
Includes a Salo source record. Lane proof, not reviewed.
