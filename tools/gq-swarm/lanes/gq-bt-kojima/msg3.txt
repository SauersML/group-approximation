Scaled copies of Q in nV need aperiodic-core elements

A copy of Q in nV normalized by an element acting as multiplication by a
rational a != +-1 consists of elements with zero periodic exponents, open
periodic sets and nonempty aperiodic cores (and logarithmic table length for
integer a). The odometer copy of Q in 2V has a fixed point of exponent (1,0),
so no element of any nV scales it: it cannot be the unipotent part of Aff(Q)
or GL_n(Q) in nV.
