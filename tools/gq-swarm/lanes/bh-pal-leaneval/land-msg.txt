Prove lean-eval's higman_infinite_simple from Hyde-Lodha Q_2

GroupApproximation.HydeLodha.higman_infinite_simple states the lean-eval
problem higman_infinite_simple verbatim: some n and a finite relator set in
FreeGroup (Fin n) whose presented group is simple and infinite.  The proof
takes the closed theorem finitelyPresentedInfiniteSimple_closed (Q_2) and
Mathlib's Group.IsFinitelyPresented.exists_mulEquiv_presentedGroup, and
transfers simplicity and infinitude along the isomorphism.
