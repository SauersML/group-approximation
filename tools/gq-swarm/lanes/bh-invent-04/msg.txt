Busemann transplant: free SFTs on building lattices from deterministic Z^2 tiles

Chamber-fixing elements of torsion-free building lattices have nonzero vector
Busemann translation, so a strongly aperiodic Z^r fibre carried along Busemann
coordinates makes the boundary action free. Corner-deterministic Z^r-SFTs
embedded through an open-cone basis stay coherent across the building's
branching and give SFTs over the lattice (given that the boundary shift is an
SFT); with Kari-Papasoglu tiles every simply transitive rank-2 lattice carries a
strongly aperiodic SFT. Rigidity transfer is posed and reduced to centrality of
germ idempotents.
