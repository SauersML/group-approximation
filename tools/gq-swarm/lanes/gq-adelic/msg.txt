Close adelic Q^n-deck lift hosts for GL_n(Q) (normal-Q^n lemma)

A finitely generated group with a normal subgroup isomorphic to Q^n acts on it
through some GL_n(Z[1/m]), and its quotient by it is not finitely presented.
Applied to the adelic covering A^n -> (A/Q)^n (deck group Q^n, the adelic
analogue of R -> S^1 behind Q <= T-bar): a finitely presented group of solenoid
homeomorphisms never has a finitely generated lift group, and no finitely
generated lift group carries Aff_n(Q) with the deck group as translations.
Obstruction for gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group; not independently reviewed.
