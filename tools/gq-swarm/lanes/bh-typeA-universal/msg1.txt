Reformulate permutational Boone-Higman through full clopen-transitive Cantor hosts

The canonical permutational host SV_G is already a full group of Cantor
homeomorphisms with clopen-transitive local maps, so the landed one-group
criterion runs in both directions: a group lies in B_A exactly when it embeds
in a finitely presented full clopen-transitive group of Cantor homeomorphisms.
The same brick argument makes every nV such a host, which puts the virtually
special groups, the cubulated hyperbolic groups and the additive rationals in
B_A; those three also follow from BFFHZ Remark 3.5, and the node says so.
