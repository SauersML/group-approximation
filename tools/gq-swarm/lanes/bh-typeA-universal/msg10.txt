State the interval-spherical Brown criterion as a general tool

A group acting by order-preserving maps on a highly connected union of finite
poset intervals is FP_n when vertex stabilizers are FP_n and each
homologically essential interval of degree e has an FP_{n-e} stabilizer, with
finitely many orbits of those. It is finitely presented when the complex is
simply connected, vertex stabilizers are finitely presented, cover
stabilizers are finitely generated, and the intervals with disconnected open
part come in finitely many orbits. This abstracts the arguments behind the
twisted Brin-Thompson criteria and reproves Zaremsky's theorem; the lesson is
that for Thompson-like Boone-Higman envelopes, long-edge stabilizers never
matter.
