Prove the non-faithful twisted Brin-Thompson finiteness criterion via a faithful ambient group

An abstract twisted Brin-Thompson group SV_G embeds as the S-colored subgroup
of the faithful (S+G)V_G, where G acts freely on the added orbit. Its Stein
complex inside the faithful groupoid has the right vertex stabilizers, the
wreath products of G. So the interval-filtration argument gives FP_n from
[HA_n] for every action, and a pi_1 version of the same filtration with
Brown's presentation theorem gives finite presentation from [A_2]. Together
with FFWZ Corollary 4.18 this proves the FFWZ conjecture (SV_G is F_n iff the
action is [A_n]) and re-proves Theorem B(ii), (iii) for non-faithful actions,
where the published proof has the gap recorded earlier. Lane proof, not
reviewed; the main risk is the transfer of BZ's section 5-7 arguments, flagged
in the route.
