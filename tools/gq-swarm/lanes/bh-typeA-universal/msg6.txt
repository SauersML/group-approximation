Prove the Belk-Zaremsky type F_n criterion for twisted Brin-Thompson groups

For a faithful action G on S, SV_G is of type F_n exactly when the action is
of type (A_n), and of type FP_n exactly when it is of type (HA_n). This settles
Conjecture H of Belk-Zaremsky (arXiv:2001.04579), which Fournier-Facio-Wu-
Zaremsky record as open for 3 <= n < infinity; necessity is their Corollary
4.18, imported here.

The proof filters the Stein sublevel complex by interval length. An
elementary interval is a homotopy (e-2)-sphere when every tree is a full brick
split in e colors in total, and contractible otherwise (BZ's nerve argument,
restated). So only grid intervals contribute, in degree e, with stabilizers
commensurable to products of e-or-fewer-set stabilizers, which is exactly the
(A_n) indexing. A homological Brown criterion for filtered complexes gives
FP_n, and finite presentation upgrades it to F_n. Lane proof, not reviewed.
