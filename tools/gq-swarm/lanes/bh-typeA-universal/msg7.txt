Record a gap in the non-faithful finiteness proofs of Fournier-Facio-Wu-Zaremsky

When the action has a kernel K, SV_G acts on the Stein complex of SV_(G/K)
through the quotient, so every stabilizer contains the canonical kernel SK_G.
The element [x_s, id, (k,1), x_s] with k in K lies in SK_G but not in the
image of G, because its germinal twist is not constant. So FFWZ Lemma 4.9(i)
fails for n = 1, and the non-faithful "if" directions of their Theorem B(ii),
(iii) and Corollary 4.14 are unproven, not refuted. The node lists the main
nodes that use them, for their owners to re-grade. It also caveats this lane's
BBMZ-questions node and states on the type F_n criterion node what Belk-
Zaremsky proved (oligomorphic, all finite-set stabilizers F_n) versus what they
conjectured (the graded characterization).
