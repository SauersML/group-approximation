part one). The lattices have property FA, so splitting and acylindrical methods are
unavailable, but they are colimits of finite rank-two Levi groups. Their actions are
exactly compatible families of finite-group actions (Lemma A), and a standard Baire
argument (Lemma B) reduces high transitivity to a finite free-completion statement for
the Levi amalgam, which must use non-sphericity. Also records the FFKLZ constraint:
the lattices lie in HF, so every highly transitive action has non-FP-infinity
stabilizers of finite sets of infinitely many sizes. Lane proofs, not reviewed.
