Leavitt units over fields: simple mod scalars; not f.g. over infinite k

Over every field k, L_k(1,2)^x is perfect and every normal subgroup lies in
the scalars or is everything, by the Lean root detection read without its
trivial-centre hypothesis; so L_Q(1,2)^x/Q^x is a countable simple group
containing GL_infinity(Q). Over infinite k neither the unit group nor its
scalar quotient is finitely generated, and in characteristic zero no finitely
generated subring of L_k(1,n) contains Q (reduction to a finite residue field).
