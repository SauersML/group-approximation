SteinbergFP: Kourovka 14.10(c) in the exact form of the Palomar challenge

Challenge.lean repeats, with identical bodies, the challenge's R_L (a RingQuot of ℤ⟨Fin 6⟩)
and its Steinberg presentation on symbols (i, j, r) : Fin n × Fin n × R, and proves
explicit_fp_overgroup_of_all_gl_n_q over them. It uses two isomorphisms: the RingQuot and
two-sided-ideal presentations of R_L agree, and the symbol presentation of St_n(R) agrees with
the repository's SteinbergGroup (Fin n) R.

A test file with the challenge's definitions copied verbatim closes the challenge theorem by
`exact GroupApproximation.SteinbergFP.explicit_fp_overgroup_of_all_gl_n_q` (definitional
unfolding only). Axioms: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. Green MSI build.
