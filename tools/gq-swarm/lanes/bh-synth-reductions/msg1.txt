Reduce every universal host class for decidable groups to the canonical Kazhdan groups EL_3 of Cantor crossed products

A class of groups hosts every group with solvable word problem iff it hosts
H_Gamma = EL_3(LC(2^{C(Gamma)},F_2) x| Z/2 wr C(Gamma)) for every decidable Gamma.
This gives new equivalent forms of Boone-Higman, permutational Boone-Higman,
just-infinite and isolated hosts, and the dynamical premise of the Leavitt route.
Kazhdan filter: amenable, Haagerup, residually finite and finite-Kazhdan host
classes are never universal. Synthesis artifact ranks the remaining gates.
