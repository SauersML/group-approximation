Obstruction: one-manifold groups contain no GL_n(Q), n >= 3 (Witte)

Import Witte (Proc. AMS 1994): finite-index subgroups of SL(n,Z), n >= 3, act on
the circle only through finite groups. Deduce that no group acting faithfully on
a one-manifold with finitely many components contains any copy of GL_n(Q),
n >= 3, standard or not (F, T, T-bar, Stein, Lodha-Moore, Monod H(A), circle
PL/projective groups), and that line and interval groups contain no SL_2(Q) or
GL_2(Q) (torsion of order 3). Add a scoping artifact for piecewise-projective
hosts, with a lemma that the p-adic odometer has no roots in clopen-piecewise
PGL_2(Z[1/p]) groups.
