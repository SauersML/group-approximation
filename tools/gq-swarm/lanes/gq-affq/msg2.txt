Criterion: renormalizable kV elements give BS(1,m) in (k+1)V

Suppose an infinite-order T in kV has a brick-local height-m tower
renormalization: X = A u TA u ... u T^(m-1)A, T^m A = A, and a
prefix-replacement conjugacy phi from (A, T^m) to (X, T). Then T x id is
conjugate in (k+1)V to its m-th power, giving BS(1,m). This generalizes the
odometer and baker-map computation of the bs12 node. Link it from the Aff(Q)
stepping stone. Elementary lane proof, not independently reviewed.
