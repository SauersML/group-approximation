SMART induced on genuine level-0 moves: exact tripling in 2V

Induce SMART's moving-tape map on the clopen set of genuine level-0 moves
(the leaves of the Callard-Salo move recursion). Every orbit meets the set
within 5 steps, so the first-return map is in 2V up to brick-local
conjugacy, and every level-k sweep now takes exactly 3^k steps: the +4
overhead of f(k+1) = 3f(k) + 4 is exactly the non-leaf steps. Record two
checkpoints of a candidate height-3 renormalization (a zero deleted next to
the head), which holds at the first and needs a state change at the second.
Lane proof, not independently reviewed.
