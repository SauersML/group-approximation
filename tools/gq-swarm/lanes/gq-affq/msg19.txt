Machines with exact m-fold branching for every odd m tested

A calibrated MSI search of reversible two-shape SMART-format machines finds
only branching 3 up to 4 symbols, and quintic tables with 5 symbols. The
smallest generalizes to an explicit family M_m (m odd, m symbols) with
T(L+1) = m T(L) + m + 1 exactly, verified for m <= 11, L <= 7. Landed OPEN
pending a move lemma; it is the first step toward BS(1,m) in 3V for all odd m.
