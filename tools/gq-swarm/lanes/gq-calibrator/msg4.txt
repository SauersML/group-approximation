Calibration worlds: the T3 germ-extension test needs a regime

The unrestricted question (an element conjugate to its square in some finite
germ extension of V) is trivially yes via shell envelopes, which hold every
countable group. Restrict T3 to the regular regime, cite 8fc27465a (attractors
and wandering non-V germs are undistorted and admit no BS relation), and name
the open remainder regular-germ-extensions-have-no-bs-relations.
