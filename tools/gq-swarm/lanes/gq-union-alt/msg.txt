Perfect SL_n(Q) probe: extension/product closures cannot create GL_n(Q)

The class of groups receiving only trivial homomorphisms from SL_n(Q) contains
every Q-free and every BS(1,4)-free group (residually finite, solvable, V, T,
VA, T-bar, A) and is closed under subgroups, extensions, arbitrary direct
products and free products, hence under all wreath products and finite-index
overgroups. So the permutational-class permanence theorems never produce
GL_n(Q); only directed unions, graphs of groups with nontrivial edges, or
Thompson-type completions can.
