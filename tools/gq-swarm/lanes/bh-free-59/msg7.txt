A projective thickening of SL_d(Z[1/2]) hosting Neumann's group; fp open

Gamma_P = <SL_d(Z[1/2]), 3-cycle> acting on P^{d-1}(Z/m) for odd m, d >= 3, is
finitely generated and residually finite, and contains Neumann's exact group on an affine
line. Unlike every affine thickening, it has no periodization fake: normal subgroups of
SL_d(Z[1/2]) are central or contain unipotents with fixed points (NST + CSP). Its degree
set is co-finite in the maximal one, so the co-infinite obstruction is also absent. Finite
presentability reduces to one explicit truncation and is left open; a yes answers MO
491978.
