No affine thickening host is finitely presented: periodization over a sublattice

General theorem: for any finitely generated Q <= Aff(Z[1/N]^d) containing a translation,
the residually finite group generated by Q and the 3-cycle (0, e_1, 2e_1) inside the
product of Sym((Z/m)^d) is not finitely presented. The fake periodizes the 3-cycle over
kZ[1/N]^d, which every linear part preserves; it satisfies every truncated presentation,
while the finite window groups get infinite orbits. This covers Neumann's group, the
doubling host, and the ASL_d(Z) hosts that contain Neumann's exact group. Lesson for
general BH: fp hosts of the form (locally finite) x| Q need non-linear dynamics, with no
invariant separated sublattice.

Elementary, written inline, not independently reviewed, no priority claimed.
