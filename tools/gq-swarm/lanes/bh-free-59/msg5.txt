The doubling host of Neumann's group is not finitely presented

For every K, the truncated presentation Gamma_K has a finite permutation quotient on
Z/(2^r-1) in which alpha is a product of 3-cycles along a sparse orbit of x -> 2x. All
local relations hold there, but a three-element chain of 3-cycles appears that no honest
coordinate can realize, since gcd(d-2, e+2) = 1 is arranged by CRT. So Gamma_K is never
Gamma. The same argument kills every host of the form <alpha, beta, affine maps of Z/m>
over Neumann's full degree set. The candidate node is marked refuted, and both nodes
carry a "Lesson for general BH" paragraph: the fake-action test for thickened hosts.
