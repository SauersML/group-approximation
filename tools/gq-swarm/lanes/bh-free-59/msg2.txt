Neumann's group: explicit finite windows, not finitely presented, host constraints

For Neumann's group G = <(0 1 2), x->x+1> inside the product of A_m over odd m >= 5
(MO 491978, Minasyan): the window subgroups <a_0..a_k> are exactly
A_{k+3} x prod_{m<=k+2} A_m, G is (locally finite)-by-Z, G is not finitely presented
(a finite presentation would make it an HNN extension of a finite group), and G is
effectively residually finite with linear degree bound, so the McKinsey and Rauzy
obstructions do not apply. Any host must have alternating sections of unbounded degree;
in particular no group acting on a bounded-degree rooted tree (so no self-similar group)
contains G. Minasyan's title question reduces to an fp RF group containing A_m for
infinitely many m.

Elementary, written inline, not independently reviewed, no priority claimed.
