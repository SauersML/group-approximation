Cone sliding: in V-times-groupoid full groups, disjoint-support relations reduce to bounded diameter

Cone-localized global bisections conjugate a conditioned V-element onto a translated
condition while commuting with everything on other cones. So every commutation relation
between elements with disjoint V-supports, however far apart their windows, follows from
relators of diameter at most diam F + diam F' + 1 (at most 1 for site conditions). The
finite-presentation problem for G_V x (Lambda x| X) full groups thus lives entirely in the
fibrewise relations that quantum rigidity measures.
