Roller compactifications of CAT(0) cube complexes are SFT compactifications

For a free cocompact action on a CAT(0) cube complex, the radius-2 distance-gradient SFT is exactly the
Roller compactification. Local realizability forces an ultrafilter (a one-step descent to adjacent
hyperplanes, then Helly number 2), so HL holds and the seeds are the principal ultrafilters.
Vertex-transitive actions (RAAGs, Z^n, F_n) give (IE'). Every virtually special group, hence every
cubulated hyperbolic group, virtually embeds in a RAAG. A ladder example shows the caveat for several
vertex orbits. No overgroup of an infinite Kazhdan group acts properly on a cube complex, so Kazhdan
hyperbolic groups need the general tube lemma.
