Answer Tarocchi's question: the commutator subgroup of the dendrite rearrangement group G_3 is simple

Tarocchi proved [G_n, G_n] simple for n >= 4. For n = 3 his argument reduces to showing
that the 3-cycle c at the central branch point lies in every nontrivial normal
subgroup N of [G_3, G_3]. The reduction already leaves [G_3, G_3]/N cyclic, generated
by c. Conjugating c by g = tau_2 rho_12, an element of [G_3, G_3], gives c^{-1} Y. The
error term Y is a commutator of two elements of a single rigid stabilizer, so Y lies in N.
Since the quotient is abelian, c = c^{-1} modulo N, and c lies in N.
