Record open questions near Boone-Higman found in problem lists, and answer one of them

The Oberwolfach 2018 Thompson-family problem session asked whether Higman's group
embeds in the Lodha-Moore groups or in Monod's group H(R). The piecewise-analytic
circle obstruction already on main gives a negative answer for both, recorded here as
a corollary node.

Three further questions become OPEN nodes:
- an AIM problem on finitely presented simple groups of commutator width at least 2;
- Minasyan's MathOverflow question on B. H. Neumann's group and finitely presented
  residually finite overgroups;
- the MathOverflow question on recursive presentability of E(G), reformulated as
  E(G) = (G * Z) / J_1(G), the rank-one BFFHZ quotient.

Two sibling nodes gain an "also printed" line for their AIM problem-list sources. The
artifact lists every problem list searched, what was found, and what was not reached.
