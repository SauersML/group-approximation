Obstruction: proper semisimple CAT(0) groups contain no GL_2(Q)

A group acting properly by semisimple isometries on a CAT(0) space has no
distorted cyclic subgroup (axis argument), and BS(1,2) <= GL_2(Q). This rules
out lattice-type finitely presented simple groups (Burger-Mozes, Caprace-Remy
twin building lattices, anything proper on a CAT(0) cube complex) as hosts for
gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group. Heretic artifact records the three shared
assumptions of the swarm and the fate of each denial.
