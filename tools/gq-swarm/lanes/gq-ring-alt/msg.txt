Roots force centralizer rank; wire the Leavitt unit group as GL_n(Q) host

A k-th root of a transcendental unit makes its localized centralizer a vector
space over the degree-k field K(x^(1/k)) (Eisenstein at x), so k divides the
centralizer rank. With the finite-rank bound for Thompson units this bounds
their roots in L_(F_2)(1,2)^x (the north-south unit has none), extending
Higman's root finiteness from V to the Leavitt unit group. Record the finitely
presented simple unit group as a candidate host for GL_n(Q) (route to the
root), what an embedding must supply, the commutative-subalgebra dead end, and
the obstruction (Q,+) not in U, reduced to the open finite-rank conjecture.
