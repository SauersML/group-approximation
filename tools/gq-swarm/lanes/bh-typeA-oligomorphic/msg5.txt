V-type PL groups V(l;A,P) act with type (A) on A intersect [0,l)

Hypotheses: V(l;A,P) is f.p., P is f.g., and A/IP*A is finite. The point
stabilizer is generated by one copy of V and finitely many germ elements, via
a contraction toward 0, so its finite generation is automatic. The pair orbits
come from Bieri-Strebel Theorem A. When d=1 the action is highly transitive:
the golden-ratio V_tau and its f.p. simple V_xz satisfy PBH, as do Stein
V-groups, with their f.p. cited. Lane proof, not reviewed; no novelty claimed.
