Stabilizer engines need finitely discriminable vertex groups; Reid's group is not finitely presented

- stabilizer-engines-need-finitely-discriminable-vertex-groups (ESTABLISHED, lane proof): for f.g. B
  that is not finitely discriminable (e.g. any infinite residually finite B), neither F_B nor D(F_B)
  is finitely presented. Proof: F_B is a local limit of the engines over B/N (transfer lemma); a
  finite presentation turns the approximations into homomorphisms killing N, and a double
  commutator at the emitter pulls the kernel into a fixed ball. Corollary: Reid's d = 0 group
  (MO 339541) and its derived group are not finitely presented. It also refutes
  fp-decidable-vertex-groups-have-fp-stabilizer-engines (B = Z).

Lesson for general BH: finite presentation is open in the space of marked groups, so a host
construction that sees its input only locally can be finitely presented only at inputs isolated
from their proper quotients. Uniform one-emitter engines need isolated vertex groups, and
calibrations on residually finite inputs such as Z are uninformative.
