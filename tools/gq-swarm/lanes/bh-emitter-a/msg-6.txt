Baumslag-Gersten rung: a glued seed with non-elementary forcing length and a polynomial word problem

baumslag-gersten-seed-forcing-length-is-non-elementary:
- relative seeds on BS(1,2): y_a for <a> (a sink vertex of the BS tree) and y_b for <b> (a marked axis
  with C_Z on axis cosets). Both satisfy (RS1): isolated, with stabilizer exactly the subgroup;
- the glued seed over BG = HNN(BS(1,2), <a> -> <b>) satisfies (C1). BG is in class C once y_b is rigid
  (RS2 and RS3 are open), by the relative-seed gluing theorem;
- its forcing length is at least tower(log n - 3), while WP(BG) is polynomial (Myasnikov-Ushakov-Won).
  So forcing length is not a complexity certificate.
Also corrects the rung-1b lesson in the Heisenberg node.

Lesson for general BH: distortion, even non-elementary, certifies nothing. Forcing length is a
design-dependent upper bound; the CAP invariant is the complexity of evaluating the forced labels, i.e.
the membership oracles the relative seeds must be. A real CAP test needs hard-membership subgroups.
