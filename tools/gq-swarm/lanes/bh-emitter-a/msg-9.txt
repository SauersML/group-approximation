Order seeds: order-preserving actions with monotone paths give rigid relative seeds

order-seeds-are-rigid-relative-seeds (ESTABLISHED, lane proof): for a finitely generated group acting
order-preservingly with a densely ordered orbit, a finitely generated point stabilizer and monotone
paths between orbit points, the cut at the point is a rigid relative seed (RS1-RS3, every D >= 1, every
field). Instances: the real-cut seed for (BS(1,2), <b>); groups with a dense left order whose positive
cone is finitely generated lie in class C, with the positivity oracle as forced point. It poses
Question (O): a finitely presented group with such an order and a hard word problem would refute CAP.

Lesson for general BH: relative seeds need not come from trees. Order actions with monotone connectivity
give them, with rigidity from monotone chains, so a seed certifies exactly the order's comparison
problem, and CAP becomes a question about hard comparison problems of finitely generated positive cones.
