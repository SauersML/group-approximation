Baumslag-Gersten group lies in class C: a rigid real-cut relative seed for <b> in BS(1,2)

The open relative seed for (BS(1,2), <b>) is replaced by the real-cut seed y_R: signs of the Z[1/2]
coordinate, constant along b and monotone along a, with * at 1. It is isolated with stabilizer exactly
<b>, its SFT is exactly the Dedekind cuts (orbit closure), and it is quantum rigid for every D >= 1 by
monotone chains. With (BS(1,2), <a>) a vertex-group relative seed, the gluing theorem gives BG in C.
The explicit glued seed keeps its non-elementary forcing length, while its y_R exit layers force in
logarithmic length.

Lesson for general BH: a second boundary can seed what a tree cannot. <b> fixes a non-isolated tree end,
but the real line of the affine action gives a rigid relative seed. Distortion certifies nothing:
forcing length is design-dependent, and the CAP invariant is membership complexity.
