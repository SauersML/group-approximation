SL_n(Q) cannot be split across a normal series; union criterion

Add sl-n-q-lies-in-one-factor-of-every-normal-series (+ proof): in any finite
series of normal subgroups of a group containing SL_n(Q)/C (C central), the
first term containing it has a factor that is nonabelian and contains (Q,+).
So no group with abelian-or-Q-free factors (solvable-by-Q-free, lifts with any
abelian deck group, module extensions of Q-free groups) contains GL_n(Q),
SL_n(Q), PGL_n(Q) or PSL_n(Q); this is why the Belk-Hyde-Matucci central-lift
split of (Q,+) has no analogue. Add ascending-unions-embed-along-coherent-chains
(+ proof), the union criterion, and an artifact on the pattern's scope and
what a GL_n(Q) version must supply. Self-contained proofs, not independently
reviewed.
