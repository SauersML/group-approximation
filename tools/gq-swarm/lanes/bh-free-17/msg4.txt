Show that persistent self-similar hosts build their input from their carry kernel

Let a self-similar group have a persistent retraction onto H with kernel K. The
elements of H that centralize K, restricted to those fixing the first level,
embed in K^X through their first-level carries c^x c^{-1}. The rest of H acts
faithfully on K.

So if K is finite-rank torsion-free abelian-by-free-abelian with the abelian
part characteristic, as for LISW's Z[1/N]^n x| <N>, then every abelian subgroup
of H has finite rank. Such hosts can never reach Z wr Z or the finitely
presented metabelian group Z[t^+-1,(1+t)^-1] x| Z^2, for any alphabet or
self-similar structure. This extends the rigid-ring barrier from affine hosts
to all persistent hosts.
