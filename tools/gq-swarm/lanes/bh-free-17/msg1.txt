Show minimal dihedral actions by elements of nV are free, and record SMART's time reversal

Finite-order elements of any Brin-Thompson group nV have clopen fixed sets, via
germ length changes at a fixed point. So in a minimal dihedral action realized by
elements of nV, every reflection is fixed-point free. Nekrashevych's periodic
fragmentations need a non-free minimal dihedral action, so they can never be built
from a dihedral action inside nV. For SMART, the cellwise swap 1<->2 composed with an
explicit state involution conjugates the moving-tape map to its inverse. That
reversal normalizes 2V_3 but is not in it, and all resulting reflections are free.
Lane proof, not reviewed.
