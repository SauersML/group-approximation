automorphisms that are Mobius off 0 and shell-wise Mobius near 0, with
sections at vertex 0 back in the group, yields a group meeting PGL_2(Q)
inside some PGL_2(Z[1/N]): the section condition is a recursion
M_{j+1} = rho F(M_j) rho^{-1} in one finitely generated subgroup. So the
prime-shift design z -> u_{v(z)} z cannot give Gamma_p; recorded as
