(label at one locally faithful point), so F_n/FP_n pass down to H. For the stabilizer engine
D(F_B) of Z/k * B this forces B to be finitely presented, so the simple-input engine claim is
false (S = D(F_(Z wr Z)) is decidable, simple, not fp) and its route is circular. New open
engine conjecture for fp decidable B, with a route to Boone-Higman via Clapham and doubling.
