Survey Mathlib and the repo's Lean for a Boone-Higman formalization

Records, for the Palomar planning lane, what the pinned Mathlib has
(PresentedGroup, Group.IsFinitelyPresented, IsSimpleGroup, HNN, amalgams,
Coxeter groups, general computability) and lacks (Thompson, Artin,
Baumslag-Solitar groups, word problems), the one external Lean problem found
(lean-eval higman_infinite_simple, unsolved on its page), and the repo's own BH,
Higman-Thompson and Hyde-Lodha Lean, including the closed finitely presented
infinite simple group and the metabelian Palomar skeleton.
