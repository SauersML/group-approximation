Code cube complex actions by geodesics modulo square flips, with cubes as states

For a group acting freely and cocompactly on a CAT(0) cube complex, the hyperplanes
adjacent to a vertex that separate it from a point pairwise cross and span a cube,
and a path x -> y -> z is geodesic exactly when the two such cubes at y share no
hyperplane. So geodesics modulo square flips form a finite-object category whose
states are pointed cubes, with Niblo-Reeves normal cube paths as normal forms. The
group acts on the regular part of the Roller boundary by bisections of its left
regular groupoid, via the median of o, go and g-omega. This specializes to the cube
k-graph of tree products and to reduced traces for RAAGs. It reduces the cubulated
case of Boone-Higman (BBCMP Question 1.15) to Li's finiteness conditions for this
one category, plus closedness of the regular boundary and faithfulness.
