Countable fields: no-RF obstruction for all infinite fields, char-p host facts

SL_n(K) has no finite quotients for every infinite field K, so GL_n(K) has no
residually finite overgroup in any characteristic; GL_n over an algebraic
extension of F_p lies in V (calibration: that obstruction never excludes a
finitely presented simple host); linear groups of positive characteristic
have no distorted cyclic subgroups, so the distortion obstruction O4 is
characteristic-zero specific; and for n >= 3 the Kazhdan lattice SL_3(F_p[t])
excludes V and VA for GL_n of a transcendental characteristic-p field.
