For a box groupoid, showing that the transposition group equals the full group is a
finite type calculus. Swaps preserve the type, factorization squares change it,
and refinement cancels. So only counting is needed; this is the elementary shadow
of H_0 (x) Z/2 = H_1 = 0. The lesson also records the two cautions: all the room
comes from refinement, and the check gives no relations.
