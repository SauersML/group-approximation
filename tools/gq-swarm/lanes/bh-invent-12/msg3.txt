Automorphisms of hierarchical rows and their folds are virtually abelian

New claim automorphisms-of-hierarchical-rows-are-virtually-abelian (lane proof, elementary):
- one fibre over a proximal or attractor factor sees every automorphism of a minimal row;
- minimal Z^d rows that are boundedly finite-to-one over a rotation on a dense set have virtually
  finite-by-abelian automorphism groups, also for finite-index subgroups;
- recognizable fixed-point tilings are at most |tau|-to-one over their odometer at deep points.

Consequences: the crossing-wire rows, Labbe's shift, the ABHT fold over F_n x Z and Busemann
transplants over A2-tilde lattices carry only virtually abelian time. The Lambda_1 x Z^r fibre problem
has no hierarchical solution.
