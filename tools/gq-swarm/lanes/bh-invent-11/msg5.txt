Seed full groups are oligomorphic actors whose orbit problem reads the seed; hard actors merge into SEED

For an SFT over Gamma with a seed (isolated point, trivial stabilizer, dense orbit), the
topological full group contains every finitary permutation of the seed orbit, so it acts faithfully
and highly transitively there, and conjugates of one cylinder swap reduce the two-cell patterns of
the seed configuration along words to its orbit problem. With finite presentation and one finitely
generated stabilizer this is a type (A) actor whose hardness is that of the seed configuration, not
of Gamma. A route reduces type-a-actors-with-arbitrarily-hard-word-problem to an OPEN claim of hard
evaluation seeds with finitely presented full groups; the Gamma = Z rung is empty, since isolated
points of Z-SFTs are eventually periodic.
