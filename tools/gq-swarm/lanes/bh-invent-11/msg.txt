Two new hereditary Boone-Higman separator candidates, and a kill of the naive central one

The integral Jacobi group Sp_2n(Z) ⋉ H(Z) is an integral linear Kazhdan group with
infinite cyclic centre, so "Kazhdan subgroups have finite centre" fails on finitely
presented simple groups. Its residual refinement P_Z (central elements of Kazhdan
subgroups that die in every finite quotient) holds on every rank-one cone host, and is
violated by the decidable groups Deligne's lattice and St_N(R_L). So P_Z separates
Boone-Higman iff every finitely presented simple group satisfies it; this is left open.

Likewise P_eff (every finitely generated residually finite subgroup is effectively
residually finite) is hereditary and excludes Rauzy's decidable lamplighter amalgams
L(A), read at source. It separates iff every finitely presented simple group satisfies
it; the existence side is recorded open, with constraints on any host for L(A).
