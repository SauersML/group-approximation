Evaluating an isolated SFT point on B_n costs at most exp(|B_R(n)|), where R(n) is the radius at which all admissible extensions agree. Locally forced seeds (all recorded seeds, forward-deterministic machines) have linear R, so they are capped at EXPTIME over polynomial-growth groups. Beating that needs NON-LOCAL forcing: backward signals from far-away computation zones checked near the origin. That is exactly what local-rigidity/fp is suspicious of, so this is where the SEED-vs-CAP question now sits. (S3) design rule: pin every agent by an ordered L…H…R field.
