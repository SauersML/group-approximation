Window-zero shells are exactly self-similar actions of finite defect

A self-similar action of defect m (X = m sigma-rays plus finite, sigma: X ->
X - Y intertwining rho and rho psi) is a descending window-zero shell action
for s^m for any input, with fp germ group at defect one for fp inputs; for FW
inputs every such shell action arises this way, the ascending direction never
occurs, and commuting windows reduce to it on [P,P]. The Kazhdan verdict for
these routes is one criterion, with the forced properties collected.
