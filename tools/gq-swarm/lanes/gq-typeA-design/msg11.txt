FW shell inputs at window zero (other direction) are highly transitive

If an FW input satisfies s rho(P) s^-1 <= rho(P) for an action with infinite
supports, it acts highly transitively on finitely many cofinite orbits, so it
has trivial centre and no finite normal subgroups. With the landed opposite
direction, window zero is dead in both directions for FW inputs with centre:
E_N(R_L) never passes, and St_N(R_L) needs non-central K_2.
