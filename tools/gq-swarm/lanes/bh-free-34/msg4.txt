Give Kohl's CT_P(Z) its own type (A) action on the nonnegative integers

For every finite set P of odd primes, CT_P(Z) preserves N_0 and acts on it faithfully and highly
transitively. The stabilizer of 0 is generated by a copy of CT_P(Z) (the elements supported on
the odd integers) and one contraction c_q of slope q for each q in P u {2}, so it is finitely
generated. Since CT_P(Z) is finitely presented, the action is of type (A).

So SV_{CT_P(Z)} is finitely presented and simple, and CT_P(Z) is a positive instance of Zaremsky's
Question 5.13(i), and a new row for the BFFHZ Question 3.4 census. For P empty this recovers V
acting on the tail class of 0^infinity.
