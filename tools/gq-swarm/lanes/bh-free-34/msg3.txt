Show that single-machine singular germ schedules are at most exponential

Take a singular point with annuli A_d, the annulus contraction z, and a germ h_f that runs one
fixed infinite-order homeomorphism m for f(d) steps on A_d. The germ group <z, h_f> is metabelian,
namely M semidirect <z> with M abelian and torsion-free. By Bieri-Strebel, if it is finitely
presented then it is an ascending HNN extension over a finitely generated base. So the shifts of f
satisfy a linear recurrence, and |f(d)| <= C K^d. For <V, h_f> at 0^infinity this germ group is the
host's singular germ group, so Belk-Hyde-Matucci certification forces the exponential bound.

This kills the Ackermann-schedule design for Kourovka 7.19 (Attempt 3 there). An Ackermann schedule
needs non-commuting germs.
