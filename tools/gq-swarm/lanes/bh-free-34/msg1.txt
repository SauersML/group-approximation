Confine the simple subgroups of Houghton-like groups to their germ group

Every finitely generated infinite simple subgroup of H_n(G) meets FSym trivially and embeds in
G/FSym(N) through the germ on one ray. For Mallery-Zaremsky envelopes it embeds in the near shift
group R_nu, and for free-shift enumerations every simple FA (for instance Kazhdan) subgroup
embeds in the input P. Houghton groups contain no finitely generated infinite simple subgroup.

Consequence for the Kazhdan reduction: the Houghton-like route keeps simple Kazhdan inputs, but
gains nothing from property (T). Recorded as Attempt 2 on the Kazhdan equivalence node.
