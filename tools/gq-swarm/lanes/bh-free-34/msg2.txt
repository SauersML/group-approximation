Open Kourovka 7.19 with its routes, and kill hard base points in V-like actors

Kourovka 7.19 (Cannonito) asks for an explicit finitely presented simple group whose word
problem is not primitive recursive. The node records the verbatim question and notes that its
existence form follows from the complexity root at the single bound Ackermann. It also notes
that every known explicit finitely presented simple group is primitive recursive.

Two routes. One goes through an explicit type (A) actor whose orbit problem is not primitive
recursive; that premise is OPEN. The other is a constructive Boone-Higman embedding of one explicit
C'(1/6) group G_A with a diagonal non-primitive-recursive set A.

New established lemma: in a group of Cantor homeomorphisms containing V, the stabilizer of a
non-eventually-periodic point where every element is locally a prefix replacement is an
ascending union of proper subgroups, hence not finitely generated. The same holds in nV. So
hardness cannot be placed in the base point of a type (A) action on such hosts.
