Flagged pointed-cube coding resynchronizes cubulated actions; claimed F_infinity simple hosts for all cubulated groups

Adds the flagged pointed-cube category (states may forbid adjacent walls the future will not cross): it is
Li-Garside with trivial units, its boundary is the whole Roller boundary, and every element of a free cocompact
cubulated group acts by a global bisection, repairing the resynchronization obstruction. With free loops at
every vertex (G * F_r), Cuntz stabilization, Li Corollary D and Matui simplicity this gives a claimed proof
that every virtually torsion-free cubulated group embeds in an F_infinity simple group (BBCMP Q1.15); the
claim node is OPEN pending independent review, with a referee checklist.
