Square-spiral enumeration of Z^2 is ball-rigid; pinning lesson

The square spiral is determined by its far Schreier balls from a finite seed:
successor steps are forced to be unit steps, and its four diagonal defect lines
propagate straight out of the seed. So ball-transplant flexibility cannot kill
every one-ended input; the spiral dies at the germ gate instead. This also adds
a partial answer and a Lesson for general BH paragraph to the pinning node:
finite presentation splits into a germ gate and a pinning gate.
