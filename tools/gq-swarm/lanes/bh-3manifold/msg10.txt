Extend free- and surface-by-B_A closure to kernels that are products of free and surface groups

Addendum item 5 on free-by-pbh-and-surface-by-pbh-groups-lie-in-type-a-class: every
automorphism of a finite product of nonabelian free and closed surface groups permutes
the factors, so its automorphism group is in B_A, and extensions of B_A groups by such
products stay in B_A.
