Extensions by centerless B_A kernels with virtually inner action stay in B_A

Addendum item 6 on free-by-pbh-and-surface-by-pbh-groups-lie-in-type-a-class: if Γ is
centerless and in B_A and the outer action has finite image, then Γ-by-B_A groups are in
B_A; so extensions by fundamental groups of finite-volume hyperbolic 3-manifolds (finite
Out by Mostow--Prasad) stay in B_A.
