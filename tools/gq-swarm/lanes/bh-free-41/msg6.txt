Reduce MathOverflow 412219 to a nonrecursive type [A_2] kernel

If some type [A_2] action has a nonrecursive kernel, the finitely presented
wreath product (Z/2) wr_S G has E(W) not recursively presented. The lamp at a
point and a group element satisfy the mixed identity [x c x^-1, g] exactly
when g lies in the kernel on that orbit.
