Verdict on critic gap G1: SL_n(Q) lacks property FW

Every countable group that is not finitely generated has a commensurated
subset that is not transfixed (the oriented edges of its coset tree), so
SL_n(Q), GL_n(Q), PSL_n(Q), Aff(Q), U_n(Q) and Q lack FW and FA for every
n >= 2, although for n >= 3 SL_n(Q) is an increasing union of the Kazhdan
groups SL_n(Z[1/m!]). The FW-transfer lemmas therefore kill no host class for
every embedding; level by level they exclude SFT Thompson groups (n >= 3) and
give a necessary condition on Rover-Nekrashevych vertex groups.
