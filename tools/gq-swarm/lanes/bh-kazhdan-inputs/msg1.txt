Simple Kazhdan inputs of germ extensions of V sit in one singular germ group

An infinite simple Kazhdan subgroup of a finite germ extension of V embeds
injectively in the isotropy germ group at a single singular point. When
Belk--Hyde--Matucci Theorem 2.1 certifies finite presentation, that germ group
is finitely presented. With the simple-Kazhdan reduction of Boone--Higman this
states what the germ engine must deliver. Lane proof, elementary from landed
nodes; not independently reviewed.
