Kazhdan subgroups of almost-V have no central elements in V; Deligne lattice sits in one germ group

A Kazhdan group of Cantor homeomorphisms with finite V-singular sets has no
infinite-order central element in a conjugate of V: push it to the orbit space
of an attracting basin, where it acts through V (Haagerup) and then through an
abelian group of translation numbers. For the Deligne lattice this kills the
central kernel of the FW germ map, so a finite-index subgroup embeds in one
singular germ group, with its center singular. Revealing-pair input read at
source (arXiv:1107.0672 TeX). Lane proofs, not independently reviewed.
