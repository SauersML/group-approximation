The germ method is local: fp extensions of tame bases have fp germ groups

Axiomatize the base in the almost-V germ theorem: over any finitely generated
group of homeomorphisms with finitely presented germ groups that moves points
locally (V_(d,r), nV, F, T, ...), a finitely presented extension with finite
singular sets has finitely presented germ groups at every point. Lesson for
general BH: the germ method neither creates nor destroys finite presentation
at a point, so it is a one-point problem about fp overgroups of the input.
Lane proof (the V proof, referee-a PASS, with the base axiomatized).
