Corollary 2.10-regime germ extensions of V have only finite Kazhdan subgroups

In a finite germ extension of V where every singular germ group is virtually
the V-germ group, or normalizes the V-dilation at a periodic point, every
subgroup with property (T) is finite. This generalizes the SL_3(Z) exclusion
to all Kazhdan groups: Sp_2n(Z), the Deligne lattice, Aut(F_n) and Out(F_n)
for n >= 5, and every simple Kazhdan input. Lane proof from landed nodes.
