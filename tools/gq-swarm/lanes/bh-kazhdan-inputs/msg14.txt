Finitely presented almost-V groups have finitely presented germ groups

Any finitely presented E with V <= E <= H (finite V-singular sets) has finitely
presented isotropy germ groups at every point, however its finite
presentation is proved: map the Schreier complex of the point stabilizer
into the germ groupoid of V with finitely many free arrows. Extends the shell
argument to all almost-V groups. Consequences: germ groups cannot be
compensated elsewhere; E_omega for aperiodic Grigorchuk omega is not
finitely presented. Lane proof, not independently reviewed.
