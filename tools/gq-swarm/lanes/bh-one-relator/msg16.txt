P2 prime b: the gluing kernel is relatively perfect

Lift the doubling swindle to the gluing group (mixed relations follow from
[M_0,N_1]=1 via local labels); with V-conjugate copies this gives
K = [K, Gamma~]. So the centrality lemma of P2 prime b Attempt 1 is
equivalent to K = 1, exactness of the gluing presentation.

Lesson for general BH: swindles make kernels of self-similar presentations
relatively perfect, so central-kernel tricks can only prove exactness.
