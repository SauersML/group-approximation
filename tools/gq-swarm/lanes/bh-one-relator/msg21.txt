P2 prime b: the two-point gluing test is exact; V_(2,2) is glued from an involution centralizer and a halving stabilizer

New node two-point-gluing-presentation-of-v-is-exact. V_(2,2) is the amalgam of V(Z/2), the centralizer of the copy swap, and V wr Z/2, the halving stabilizer, over V x Z/2, modulo disjoint-cone commutation. Canonical lifts of every transposition are defined, including cross lifts between comparable cones, by moves between incomparable lifts. They satisfy split relations and are natural under each generator of both pieces (a finite case analysis from disjoint-support commutation, local-label conjugation and the finite-shadow braid theorem). Strong naturality then gives Bleak-Quick's conjugacy relations, so Bleak-Quick yields a section onto the gluing group and the kernel is trivial. This is the multiplicativity lemma of the one-transposition criterion, in its simplest instance. Pointers added on the parity node and the P2 prime b node.
