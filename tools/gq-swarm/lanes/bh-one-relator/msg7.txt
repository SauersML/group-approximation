Equicontinuous square-conjugators are trivial in isometric brick hosts

In groups of brick maps of C^n with isometric sections, equicontinuity
equals bounded exponent sums, and an element conjugated to an even power of
itself by an equicontinuous homeomorphism has finite order (pro-2 sections
against an odd closure). So the 2V_tau odometer renormalizes nothing, and
BS(1,2) conjugators there have unbounded exponent sums. Parity is sharp.
