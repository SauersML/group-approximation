Notation as in the target. Maps compose right to left. `P`, `Π` and `A` denote the sets of
generators of types (P), (Π) and (A).

## Step 0. Injectivity

- A power map `ρ_m` sends the basis to `x_1^(m_1), …, x_r^(m_r), z_1, …, z_s`. These
  generate `⟨x_1^(m_1)⟩ * ⋯ * ⟨x_r^(m_r)⟩ * Z`, a free product of subgroups of the free
  factors `⟨x_i⟩` and `Z`. That free product is free on these elements, so `ρ_m` is
  injective.
- Permutations and relative automorphisms are automorphisms.
- So every `φ ∈ 𝓜` is injective.

## Step 1. Permutations to the left

For `π ∈ Π`:
- `π ρ_m π^{-1}` is again a power map (with exponents permuted);
- `π λ π^{-1}` fixes every `x_i` when `λ ∈ Aut_X(F)`, because `π` permutes the `x_i` and
  fixes `Z`.

So a word in the generators can be rewritten, moving each permutation to the left, as
`φ = π ∘ ψ` with `π ∈ Π` and `ψ` in the monoid `⟨P, A⟩`.

## Step 2. A power of φ

Let `k` be the order of `π`. Then

    φ^k = (π ψ)^k = π^k ∘ (π^{-(k-1)} ψ π^(k-1)) ∘ ⋯ ∘ (π^{-1} ψ π) ∘ ψ,

and each factor `π^{-j} ψ π^j` lies in `⟨P, A⟩` by Step 1. Since `π^k = 1`,
`φ^k ∈ ⟨P, A⟩`.

Step 1 of `power-map-free-mapping-tori-pbh-proof` shows that `⟨F, t^k⟩ ≤ F*_φ` has index
`k` and is isomorphic to `F*_(φ^k)`. That argument uses only the normal form `t^{-p} w t^q`
and the character `t ↦ 1`, `F ↦ 0`, so it holds for every injective `φ`. By part 2 of
`boone-higman-type-a-class-closed-under-finite-extensions` it suffices to show
`F*_ψ ∈ B_A` for every `ψ ∈ ⟨P, A⟩`.

## Step 3. The model

Fix `ψ = g_1 ∘ g_2 ∘ ⋯ ∘ g_n` with every `g_l ∈ P ∪ A`. Let `ρ^(1), …, ρ^(q)` be the power
maps among the `g_l`, with exponent vectors `m^(1), …, m^(q)`.

- **The linear group.** For each `i ≤ r` let `Aff_i ≤ GL_2(Q)` be generated by
  `u = [[1,1],[0,1]]` and `d_(i,j) = [[m^(j)_i, 0],[0, 1]]` for `j ≤ q`. Then
  `d_(i,j) u d_(i,j)^{-1} = [[1, m^(j)_i],[0,1]] = u^(m^(j)_i)`. Put
  `M = Aff_1 × ⋯ × Aff_r ≤ GL_(2r)(Q)`. It is finitely generated, so it lies in `B_A` by
  `char-zero-linear-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`. Write `u_i` for `u` in the
  `i`-th factor and `σ_j = (d_(1,j), …, d_(r,j)) ∈ M`. So `σ_j u_i σ_j^{-1} = u_i^(m^(j)_i)`.
- **The free product.** `Q = M * F(z_0, z_1, …, z_s)` and `P_0 = M * ⟨z_0⟩`, so
  `Q = P_0 * F(z_1, …, z_s)`.
- **The free subgroup.** `y_i = z_0^{-(i-1)} u_i z_0^(i-1)` and `Y = ⟨y_1, …, y_r⟩ ≤ P_0`.
  `Y` is free on the `y_i`, by the reduced-word argument of Step 2 of
  `power-map-free-mapping-tori-pbh-proof`: a product of nonzero powers of consecutive
  distinct `y_i` is a reduced word in `M * ⟨z_0⟩` with syllables in `M \ {1}` and
  `⟨z_0⟩ \ {1}`.
- **The copy of F.** `J = ⟨Y, z_1, …, z_s⟩ ≤ Q`. `Y` is a subgroup of the free factor `P_0`
  and `F(z_1, …, z_s)` is the other free factor, so `J = Y * F(z_1, …, z_s)`. It is free on
  `y_1, …, y_r, z_1, …, z_s`. Let `ι : F → J` be the isomorphism `x_i ↦ y_i`, `z_j ↦ z_j`.

## Step 4. Lifting the generators

- **Power maps.** For `ρ^(j)`, let `R_j` be `c_(σ_j)` on `M`, fixing `z_0, z_1, …, z_s`. It
  is the free product of an automorphism of `M` with the identity, so `R_j ∈ Aut(Q)`. Then
  `R_j(y_i) = z_0^{-(i-1)} σ_j u_i σ_j^{-1} z_0^(i-1) = y_i^(m^(j)_i)` and `R_j(z_l) = z_l`.
  Hence `R_j ∘ ι = ι ∘ ρ^(j)`, since both are homomorphisms agreeing on the basis.
- **Relative automorphisms.** For `λ ∈ Aut_X(F)`, let `Λ` be the endomorphism of `Q` that
  is the identity on `P_0` and sends `z_l ↦ ι(λ(z_l))`.
  - `Λ ∘ ι = ι ∘ λ`: on `x_i` both give `y_i`, because `λ(x_i) = x_i` and `y_i ∈ P_0`. On
    `z_l` both give `ι(λ(z_l))`.
  - Let `Λ'` be built the same way from `λ^{-1}`. Both composites `Λ Λ'` and `Λ' Λ` fix
    `P_0`, and `Λ(Λ'(z_l)) = Λ(ι(λ^{-1}(z_l))) = ι(λ λ^{-1}(z_l)) = z_l`, and symmetrically.
    So `Λ ∈ Aut(Q)`, with `Λ|_M = id`.
- **The composite.** Let `β` be the composite of these lifts in the order of
  `g_1 ∘ ⋯ ∘ g_n`. Then:
  - `β ∈ Aut(Q)` and `β ∘ ι = ι ∘ ψ`, so `β(J) = ι(ψ(F)) ⊆ J`;
  - every factor preserves `M`, acting on it as `c_(σ_j)` or as the identity, so
    `β|_M = c_σ`, where `σ ∈ M` is the product of the `σ_j` used, in order.

## Step 5. Conclusion

`Q = M * F_(s+1)` with `M` finitely generated and in `B_A`. `β` restricts on `M` to
conjugation by `σ ∈ Q`, and `J ≤ Q` has `β(J) ⊆ J`. By part (T) of
`pbh-class-closed-under-relative-holomorphs`, `J*_β ∈ B_A`. The isomorphism `ι` intertwines
`ψ` and `β|_J`, so `F*_ψ ≅ J*_β`, which lies in `B_A`. Steps 1–2 then give `F*_φ ∈ B_A`,
and `type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups` gives the envelope. `∎`
