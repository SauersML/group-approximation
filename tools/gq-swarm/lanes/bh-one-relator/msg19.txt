P2 prime b: gluing kernels over every n-generated group descend from the full shift over F_n

New node gluing-kernels-descend-from-the-free-group-full-shift. It works with full bisections, so descent to closed invariant subsets needs no freeness, and it adds descent along group quotients Lambda = Lambda~/R, which kills only the labels r|_[0] for r in R_0. So K_(Lambda,X) is an image of K_(F_n, A^(F_n)) for every subshift over A over every n-generated group. FSG(F_n, A) gives P2 prime b for all of them, and exactness for all of them is equivalent to exactness at A^(F_n). The smallest necessary test, an image of the full 2-shift over Z, is a question about Thompson's V alone: is V the amalgam of the centralizer of a fixed-point-free involution and the stabilizer of a halving it swaps, modulo disjoint-cone commutation? Attempt 5 is recorded on the P2 prime b node.
