E3': seed hosts map onto their boundary hosts with simple finitary kernel, so E3' of a seed SFT forces E3' of its boundary

New node seed-full-groups-surject-onto-boundary-full-groups. For a seed (isolated, dense, free orbit) the V-stabilized full group maps onto the full group of the boundary system. The kernel is the finitary group of the seed orbit, an increasing union of V_(2,n); it is simple, not finitely generated, and normally generated by one copy of V. So finite presentation of the seed host implies finite presentation of the boundary host. For hyperbolic seed SFTs, E3' therefore requires the V-stabilized horofunction-boundary host to be finitely presented, which runs through the branch locus studied by bh-finf-hyp. The converse is the infinite-emitter gate in seed form.
