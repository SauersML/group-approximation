Kourovka 17.61 lane pass 2: reduce the transposition-presentation gap to the Ore squares

The class-transposition presentation of CT_P(Z) has central kernel. New lane lemmas:
- Every lift of a cellwise-canonical element is a transport (Lemma T).
- Any frame lifts satisfy the permutation and naturality relations exactly.
- Assuming the Ore-category presentation of PC, the kernel is generated by the far-commutation
  and colour-exchange square defects.
Also records choice-free lifts of doubled elements.

The type F_infinity route to 17.61 was checked at source in Li 2110.04505; it is literature-only.
Bleak-Quick's P = 0 proof relies on the Cannon-Floyd-Parry presentation.

Lesson for general BH: a presentation of a full group by involutions is exactly as hard as closing
the 2-cells of its Ore category. Transport, the finite symmetric subgroups and doubled lifts reduce
it to position-independent central defects, one per colour pair.
