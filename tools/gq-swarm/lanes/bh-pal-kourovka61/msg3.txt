Pass 2 claimed that naturality holds for arbitrary lifts. That holds only for permutations fixing
the split cell; naturality defines the other lifts, consistently by the transport lemma.

With comb frames and lifts phi_0(Y_p) R_p, every far-commutation square closes exactly once the
V-core relations pi(n,p) hold. Given the Ore presentation and the P = 0 case (Bleak-Quick,
literature), the kernel of the transposition presentation of CT_P(Z) is generated by one central
colour-exchange defect per pair of colours. A {2,p,q}-cube swindle is proposed but not done.

Lesson for general BH: in a full group of a k-graph Cantor algebra, the only obstruction beyond the
Thompson V-core is one central class per colour pair. That is the shape of H_2 of the groupoid, and
the colour with two edges should kill it.
