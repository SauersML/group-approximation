Reduce finite presentation of the integral projective full groups to finite type of the unimodular dissection operad

Thumann's F-infinity theorem for operad groups applies to the operad of labelled unimodular dissections; every hypothesis but finite type is verified, and the group is identified with the operad group. The pieces of any unimodular dissection have reciprocal height products summing to one, so each piece count admits finitely many dissections in every dimension, giving cocompactness and an exact arithmetic mergeability test. In dimension 2 the medial split is a prime beyond the stellar moves, and a bounded MSI search found primes of sizes 2, 3, 6 and 9 to 15, so the canonical operad is very likely not finitely generated. The finite-presentation gate therefore becomes a Serret-type gate: a finite-type coding preserved by the group. Attempt 3 also corrects Attempt 2's test and records which arXiv ids were checked.
