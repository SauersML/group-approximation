Resolve every rational plane by edge splits, reduce Ore's condition for the edge-split operad to one rearrangement lemma, and put every perfect subgroup of the full group in the operad group

Edge-midpoint splits resolve any rational plane in a unimodular triangle cone, by a strictly decreasing crossing-complexity potential, so edge-split dissections are cofinal. Ore's condition for the edge-split operad then reduces to a local rearrangement lemma: a dissection respecting a first-split plane splits there first. That lemma is proved except for one sub-case and checked with no violations up to 7 pieces. Under Ore's condition, any operad group containing a germ-generating subgroup contains all swaps, hence the derived full group by Matui's normal-subgroup theorem, so perfect groups such as closed mapping class groups need no coding. Such groups also act with type (A) on accessible clopens without being full, and a new route derives Boone-Higman for closed mapping class groups from Ore's condition and finite type of the edge-split operad alone.
