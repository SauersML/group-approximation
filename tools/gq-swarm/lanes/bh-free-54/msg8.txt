Refute the atom-plane rearrangement lemma and split Ore's condition for the edge-split operad into synchronization and tree domination

A 17-piece edge-split dissection respects the plane x_0 = x_1 but does not split there first, because it contains the depth-five cell that descends from the simplex and not from the child it lies in. So edge-split order is strictly finer than geometric refinement, and the atom-plane route to Ore's condition is dead. In every rank, Ore's condition is equivalent to synchronization of descendants plus tree domination of descendant dissections (and to Ore against single atoms). Synchronization plus tree domination also make the edge-split group the whole rational projective full group. A reversing search found upper bounds for every tested pair and never failed.
