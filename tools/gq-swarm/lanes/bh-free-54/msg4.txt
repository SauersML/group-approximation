Show transvections are single edge-split carets, so GL_n(Z) lies in the one-generator edge-split operad group, and isolate Ore's condition as the gate

Every elementary transvection acts on the rational Cantorization by exactly one edge split on each pair of orthants, so the whole general linear group lies in the group of fractions of the operad generated by a single edge split; the infinitely many prime dissections of the full operad are not needed. The positive monoid behind that operad is strictly smaller than the nonnegative unimodular matrices (an explicit cone lies in no half of any first split), and bounded MSI tests show the iterated barycentric chain is not cofinal. So Ore's condition for the edge-split operad, stated as a covering condition on its positive monoid, is recorded as the open gate, with property (T) of SL_3(Z) explaining why no tree-like host can work.
