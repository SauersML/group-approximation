One group St_4(S) contains every GL_n(Q) (proof claimed, under review)

For any ring S containing Q and a binary Leavitt pair, the block isomorphism
St_4(M_k(Q)) = St_(4k)(Q) and the fact that scalar blocks a -> a (x) I_N multiply K_2
by N show that every torsion K_2 class of M_m(Q) dies in St_4(S). So SL_(4m)(Q), and
hence every GL_n(Q), embeds in the single group St_4(S). With S = D of the divisible
ring engine this gives one finitely presented group for Problem 2.7. OPEN pending
