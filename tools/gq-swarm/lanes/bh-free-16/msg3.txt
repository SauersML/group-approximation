Prove that atomless invariant measures of V-elements live on their periodic part

A height-cocycle argument (letters written minus letters read) shows that every atomless measure
invariant under an element of V is supported where some power is locally the identity. With
Markov-Kakutani this gives: an element of V normalizing an amenable action induces an automorphism
that is periodic on a nonempty invariant closed set, and never dilates a divisible group without a
fixed point. Consequence: in nV, SV_G and the free colour of 2V_tau, a copy of Q x| <2> whose Q acts
on a coordinate without fixed points cannot dilate through a homeomorphic base map there; with
bh-free-30's tail-map theorem, both one-coordinate shapes are dead. Adds a Lesson for general BH to
the earlier tree-coding node. Lane bh-free-16; lane proof, not reviewed.
