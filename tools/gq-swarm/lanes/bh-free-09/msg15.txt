Rigid-seed constructions pass the separable-modifications test

In the wall-chamber distance seeds, orientations along a nested wall chain switch once. So any two
modifications of one point have difference sets within r_0 + 2, which covers surface, median and
product seeds; products preserve the test. Relative-seed and Kurosh tower gluings pass on audit,
through their unique sink or end. Apartment computations for the A~2 and C~2 building seeds find
no separable freedom (a boundary field's freedom points toward its own limit), and the one
remaining configuration to settle is flagged.
