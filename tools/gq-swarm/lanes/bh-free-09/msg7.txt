Prove that deterministic finite-fibre extensions of rigid SFTs are rigid

If an SFT X over a finitely generated group projects into a quantum
rigid SFT Y, and its extra finite-fibre letter is transported
deterministically (by local maps of the base pattern) along a set of
directions generating the group as a monoid, then X is quantum rigid,
and free when Y is. The base marginal commutes by rigidity, and
transport puts each fibre operator in the algebra generated by one
fibre operator and base operators. Covers constancy, copy and
finite-group cocycle layers. Lesson: non-rigidity can only come from
genuinely free layers. Lane proof, elementary, unreviewed.
