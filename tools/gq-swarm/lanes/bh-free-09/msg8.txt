Quantum rigidity of an SFT extension is centrality of the base plus pointwise rigidity

For any SFT extension over any finitely generated group, and on spaces of any dimension, quantum
rigidity holds exactly when every quantum family has central base idempotents and each fibre is
rigid relative to its frozen base point. The proof localizes at points of the base. This removes the
finite-dimensional restriction from the Busemann transplant reduction, and shows that the centrality
statement (C) is the whole G2 content of every layered design.
