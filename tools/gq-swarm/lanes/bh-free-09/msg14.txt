Building transplants pass the separable-modifications test

Changing the chamber at infinity changes the sector germ at every vertex of every apartment that
contains both chambers, and the Busemann coordinates of those vertices fill a full coset of L. So every
skeleton modification reaches every horospherical level and meets every other modification. The
A~2 transplants therefore pass the test whenever their Z^2 fibre does, which closes the open check
left by the tree-product audit.
