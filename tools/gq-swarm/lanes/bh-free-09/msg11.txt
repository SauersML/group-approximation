Crossing-wire shifts pass the separable-modifications test

Crossing Lemma: in a crossing-wire fixed-point tiling, two modifications that change the type of a
common macrotile have intersecting difference sets. The changed input wires cross, so the crossing
tile differs in both, and the argument recurses down to single tiles. With blank corners and
side-local margins, faults leave no one-sided freedom. So the crossing-wire tiles, their one-way
variant and its transplants pass the test. The minimal version passes if every side component added
for minimality enters the crossing box.
