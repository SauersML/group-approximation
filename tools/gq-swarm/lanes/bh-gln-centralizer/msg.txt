Leavitt units commuting with a unital Leavitt copy are self-doubling

Commuting with a unital copy of L_F(1,2) is equivalent to being conjugate to
the double t+t; doubling kills Lefschetz traces and finite torsion, so no
infinite-order Thompson unit qualifies in characteristic 0 (or aperiodic over
any field), and (Q,+) in the units forces infinite centralizer rank, so the
finite-rank conjecture kills the whole finite-field Leavitt route to GL_n(Q).
