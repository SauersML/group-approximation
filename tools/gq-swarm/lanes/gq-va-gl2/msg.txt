Germ extensions of V: length functions bound BS relations (gq)

Establish regular-germ-extension-attractors-are-undistorted (lane proof, not
independently reviewed): in any finite germ extension of V the singularity
count is a length function growing linearly along wandering singular orbits;
in the regular regime (eventually periodic singular points, normal or
finite-index V-germs, V-orbit preservation) the depth-gain Lipschitz constant
is a length function growing linearly along attracting and repelling points.
So such elements are undistorted and never conjugate to proper powers, and
the divisible line of Aff(Q), SL_2(Q), GL_2(Q) or U_3(Q) must avoid both
dynamics. Record the remaining case as the open claim
regular-germ-extensions-have-no-bs-relations, with an explicit depth-gain-zero
infinite-order germ that neither function detects.
