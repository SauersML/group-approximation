Decide continuity of automatic structures; S_{1,2} modulo Birman–Hilden

An automatic structure with uniqueness is continuous exactly when each left
multiplier map has bounded lookahead (K = |Q|^2). This is decidable by a
split-cycle search in the twin graph of the multiplier automaton, and it is the
first test for Mosher's structure. Mod(S_{1,2}) is continuous automatic, given
the recalled Birman–Hilden identification with Mod(S_{0,5}). Record S_{2,1} as
the first case with no hyperelliptic shortcut, and the Raney-type theorem for
Rauzy–Veech codings as the precise missing ingredient.
