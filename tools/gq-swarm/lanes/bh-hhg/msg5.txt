BBCMP Question 1.16: low-complexity mapping class groups are continuous automatic

Record BBCMP Question 1.16 verbatim as an open root. An Artin group of finite type
modulo a central Garside power is continuous automatic: the normal forms are the
positive Deligne forms with small infimum, and left multiplication is sequential
and moves the infimum by at most one. By capping, Birman–Hilden and
commensurability, the mapping class groups of punctured spheres, the torus, the
once-punctured torus and the closed genus-two surface are continuous automatic.
Genus three and higher remain open.
