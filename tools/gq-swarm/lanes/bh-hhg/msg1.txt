Pin where the hyperbolic-groups method stops for groups with flats

A free abelian group fixing a rational point of a finite-nucleus rational
similarity group acts through germs of rank at most one. So natural
boundaries with Z^2-fixed points of faithful germs cannot be coded
rationally. The sharp host-class test is open: does Z^2 * Z, which lies in
every closed-surface mapping class group, embed in any finite-nucleus RSG?
