Closed-surface mapping class groups are self-similar only via exotic virtual endomorphisms

For genus at least three, a finite-index subgroup of Mod(S_g) acting faithfully
and self-similarly on a finitely branching rooted tree must have a section
homomorphism that is neither injective nor of finite image. The proof combines
the level-kernel recursion with Irmak rigidity and a normal core in the
extended mapping class group. So Zaremsky's self-similar route to BBMZ Problem
5.3(2) needs a homomorphism with infinite image and nontrivial kernel, which
would follow from, but is not known to require, a virtual Z quotient
(Ivanov's question).
