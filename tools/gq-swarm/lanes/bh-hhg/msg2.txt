Transport the rank-one germ lemma along orbits in finite-nucleus RSGs

In a finite-nucleus rational similarity group, a free abelian group of rank
at least two has a nontrivial element that is the identity near every point
attracted to a rational point with finite orbit. Record it as an Attempt on
the Z^2 * Z host-class question, add the Röver--Nekrashevych rational-attractor
sketch, and record the Zappa--Szép route for product-of-trees lattices as
subsumed by Bux--Llosa Isenrich--Wu.
