Once-punctured mapping class groups are continuous asynchronous automatic

Suffix uniqueness in Mosher's sense makes right multiplication a backward
finite-state process, so the inverted normal forms are continuous for the left
action and have deterministic rational multipliers. Applied to Mosher's normal
forms on the mapping class groupoid, restricted to loops and renamed as group
elements, this makes every Mod(S_{g,1}) continuous asynchronous automatic, so
it embeds in the rational group (BBCMP Corollary 1.9). This covers S_{2,1} and
every genus at least three for the asynchronous variant of BBCMP Question 1.16.
The synchronous version and closed surfaces remain open.
