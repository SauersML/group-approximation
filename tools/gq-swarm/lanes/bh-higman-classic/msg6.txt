Full closures of preimages of subgroups of Out(V) are integrally acyclic

- v-normalizer-full-closures-are-integrally-acyclic (+proof): the outer-class map makes the
  germ groupoid an extension of Q by the Cuntz groupoid O, and H_*(O) = 0 (Matui CKHomology,
  K_*(O_2) = 0). The skew product and Matui's LHS spectral sequence give H_*(G_Q) = 0. Li's
  vanishing corollary (purely infinite minimal groupoids have comparison) then shows [[Γ_Q]]
  is integrally acyclic and perfect.
- Consequence: no homological obstruction to finite presentation exists for the Q1.4 test
  family V ⋊ <t>.

Matui 0909.1624 and Li 2209.08087 were read at source (TeX). Lane proof, not reviewed.
