PBH class: free permutational products over finite stabilizers; the arbitrary-G-set wreath closure is false

- New: free-permutational-products-preserve-pbh (+ proof route). (*_X K) ⋊ W lies in
  B_A whenever Z/2 wr_X W does, in particular over W-sets with finite stabilizers.
  So B_A contains the centralizing amalgams W *_C (C × K) over finite C without any
  retraction (e.g. HNN extensions and doubles of V over finite subgroups), and there
  are criteria for general amalgams and HNN extensions over finite subgroups.
- New: pbh-wreath-closure-fails-over-undecidable-stabilizers. Mihailova's subgroup
  gives Z/2 wr_(G/C) G, G = F_2 × F_2, with unsolvable word problem. This refutes
  pbh-closed-under-permutational-wreaths-with-infinite-stabilizers as stated
  (refuted_by added).
- New OPEN node: pbh-closed-under-decidable-permutational-wreaths, the corrected
  form, with decidable stabilizers.
