Finite-edge closure of B_A: five equivalent forms, the open node, and the stabilization gate

- New: pbh-finite-edge-closure-equivalences (+ proof). The following are equivalent:
  finite amalgams; finite HNN extensions; finite-edge graphs of groups; conjugacy
  realization of finite subgroups; the involutive swap H *_(A×A) (A wr Z/2). The
  finite-quotient case is proved.
- New OPEN: pbh-closed-under-finite-edge-graphs-of-groups, with first test case
  <V, t | t c_1 t^-1 = c_2> (free involution vs involution with a fixed cone).
- New OPEN gate b-a-groups-embed-in-full-hosts-with-free-torsion, with route
  finite-edge-closure-via-free-torsion-full-hosts. Twisted Brin–Thompson actors never act
  freely. Every nontrivial finite-order element acts freely on the tautological section
  space ∏_U U, so the missing piece is a finite-presentation criterion for twisted
  Brin–Thompson groups over nontrivial Cantor bundles.

Lesson for general BH: combination closure of B_A reduces to realization moves in one
overgroup (centralizers for identity edges, conjugacy for twisted finite edges, one swap
of A × A). The finite-edge part of a Higman tower is a single stabilization problem in
full hosts.
