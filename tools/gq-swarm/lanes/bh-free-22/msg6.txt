KMS groups: faithful self-similarity must make central configurations transient

The fully marked configuration elements span an infinite central subspace, so
any faithful virtual endomorphism must push each of them out of the domain or
the center. This kills full-domain T-stable maps, including counter halving:
the coin shift is multiplication by P(a)=(a^3-2a^2+a+1)/(a(a-1)), so halving is
Frobenius-compatible and zero tests do not obstruct it, but it fixes T. It also
kills lamplighter-style division by a group-ring element, since central
configurations see only its augmentation. Lane proof, not reviewed.
