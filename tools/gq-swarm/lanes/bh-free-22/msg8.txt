Free-factor germ extensions of V fail the Belk-Hyde-Matucci SingFix gate

If the germ group at 0^infinity is <t> * H, with H preserving every cone
0^nC, then SingFix({p},{p}) is the strictly increasing colimit of the
finitely presented amalgams V_p *_(B_n) B_n H, so it is not finitely
presented. Finite annular actions also exclude germs that t normalizes or
ascends. The one-point germ route for fp RF groups thus comes back to virtual
endomorphisms. Lane proof, not reviewed.
