Commensurator-rigid groups have no faithful generalized Baumslag-Solitar members

If G -> Comm(G) is injective with finite-index image, then some finite-index
subgroup of each vertex group of any group in BS_G acts trivially on the
Bass-Serre tree: the kernel N of the commensurator map is virtually free and
finitely generated, so its centralizer in a vertex group has finite index and
fixes every vertex. So Bux-Llosa Isenrich-Wu Theorem C never applies to
Out(F_n) (n >= 4, Farb-Handel) or closed Mod(S_g) (g >= 3, Ivanov), a second
obstruction beside residual finiteness. Lane proof, not reviewed.
