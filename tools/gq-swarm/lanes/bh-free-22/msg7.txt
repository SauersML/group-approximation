Every residually finite group has free-product germs over Thompson's V

Put the finite quotients G/N_n in ping-pong position inside V, and let G act
on the n-th annulus of p = 0^infinity through G/N_n. The full closure of
<V, G> is a one-orbit finite germ extension of V whose germ group at p is
G * Z. So for finitely presented residually finite inputs, the fp germ group
condition of Belk-Hyde-Matucci Theorem 2.1 always holds. Lane proof, not
reviewed.
