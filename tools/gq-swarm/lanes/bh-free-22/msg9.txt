Finitely presented germ extensions of V are locally rigid; free-product hosts fail

If <V, x_1..x_k> with each x_i V-local off finitely many points is finitely
presented, the x_i are the only homeomorphisms satisfying its short
relations. For the ping-pong annular hosts of residually finite groups, one
deep annulus can be conjugated without changing any short relation, so
<V, G> is never finitely presented (G = Z/2 and Z included). Lesson: a BH
host must carry a finite depth-transport rule. Lane proof, not reviewed.
