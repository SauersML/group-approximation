Profinite translation groupoids times any ample groupoid give no simple hosts

The topological full group of (G acting on its completion) x H is an increasing
union of cocycle-measurability subgroups F_i, each with a normal subgroup K_i
meeting G in N_i. A finitely generated simple subgroup containing G is
therefore isomorphic to G. So a simple host for a residually finite group
cannot come from the profinite translation action plus independent expanding
dynamics. Lane proof, not reviewed.
