Strengthen FW shell window zero to the descending sign: trivial endomorphism core, and no window zero for co-Hopfian FW inputs

The FW node closes the ascending sign and the descending sign only at equality.
Exchanging the two coordinates of the commensurated graph and running the
induction along the endomorphism core shows that a descending window-zero
action of an FW input makes every element of the iterated image finitary. With
infinite supports the core is trivial, so a co-Hopfian FW input admits no
window zero at either sign. Since Boone-Higman reduces to Kazhdan inputs and
Kazhdan implies FW, the genuine-action shell route must supply a window of size
at least one, or a non-co-Hopfian input with trivial core; its known positive
family is virtually abelian, hence never Kazhdan.
