Artin Deligne boundaries: faithful and topologically free; B_3 nonsingular, rank >= 3 singular; Li (t<d) fails on Omega_infinity

- claim artin-deligne-boundary-actions-are-topologically-free (lane proof): the
  pointwise stabilizer of {a^inf, b^inf} is trivial (gcd distributes, chains have gcd 1),
  so a finite-type Artin group acts faithfully on its BBCMP boundary. This fills a gap:
  the printed proof of BBCMP Thm 2.4 shows only that generators act nontrivially.
  Irreducible gives topological freeness, and A/<Delta^c> acts faithfully and
  topologically freely on the compact quotient boundary.
- claim b3-deligne-boundary-is-nonsingular-rank-three-is-not (lane proof): the
  transducer table shows PSL_2(Z) = B_3/Z acts by local prefix replacements (empty
  singular set); commuting atoms s,t give a persistent state and a singular point
  (st)^inf in every rank >= 3 type.
- claim li-garside-criterion-fails-on-artin-monoid-omega-infinity: I_l(A^+) on
  Omega_infinity is the partial action on the positive Deligne boundary (standard for
  quasi-lattice orders); Li's (t<d) fails for B_3 even with decorations.
