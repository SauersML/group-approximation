Record Cornulier-Guyot-Pitsch Questions 1-3 on limits of isolated groups, with the structure of that class

Adds the three printed questions of arXiv:math/0511714v2 pp. 10-11 (every decidable group,
every hyperbolic group, every finitely generated solvable group a limit of isolated groups) as
OPEN nodes, read from the source. Two elementary lane lemmas: the limits of isolated groups
contain all LEF and isolated groups, are closed under finite products, marking-independent,
and characterized for finitely presented inputs by F-avoiding isolated quotients; and a relative
Kuznetsov lemma showing that no finitely generated subgroup with unsolvable word problem survives
in all nearby finitely presented quotients, which rules out the naive route to the negative
answer of Question 3 that CGP suggest. Lane proofs, not reviewed, no priority claimed.
