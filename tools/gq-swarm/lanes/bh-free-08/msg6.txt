Extend the closure principle to Brin's group A and TA through the reals with powers of two

Brin's piecewise-linear maps are self-similar near their singular points, so
their graphs are definable in the real field with a predicate for the powers of
two, whose theory is decidable (van den Dries). So the finitely presented simple
group TA has recursive mixed identities too. Record the known fp simple groups
the closure principle does not yet cover.
