Show mixed identities of homeomorphism groups depend only on the closure; decide them for dense tame 1-dimensional groups

Word maps are continuous, so a group of homeomorphisms has the same mixed
identities as its closure. Groups dense in the homeomorphisms of a Cantor space
are therefore mixed-identity-free. For dense subgroups of Homeo_+ of the interval
or circle with semialgebraic generators, non-identities are existential
sentences over the reals, so Tarski-Seidenberg decides every J_k. This covers
F, T, Stein, irrational-slope and piecewise-projective groups, and it removes all
Thompson-type fp simple groups as candidates for non-recursive mixed identities.
