Show Deligne lattices and the Torelli quotients contain H_3(Z), and record weaker nV targets

Deligne's lattice over Sp_4(Z) and every Torelli lower-central quotient
Mod(S_g)/T_g[c+1] (g >= 3, c >= 2) contain the integer Heisenberg group. So the
open statement that H_3(Z) embeds in no mV would exclude both from every
Brin-Thompson host. A new open node asks whether Kazhdan subgroups of nV have
finite center. That is weaker than finiteness of Kazhdan subgroups but already
suffices for these test cases, and a drift-invariance lemma is recorded for it.
The Kazhdan-finiteness node gets an attempt entry: a literature check finds no
result on the Haagerup property or (T) for nV (n >= 2). The Hughes local-similarity
route is dead there, since Hughes groups act properly on wall spaces and nV does
not.
