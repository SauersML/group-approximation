Embed Grigorchuk's finitely presented amenable group in a contracting Röver–Nekrashevych group

Lysenok's endomorphism of the first Grigorchuk group keeps each element as its own last
section, with first section of finite image that becomes finitary after two steps.
A general lemma embeds any such ascending HNN extension in the Röver–Nekrashevych group of
an enlarged self-similar group, using one element of Thompson's F. For Lysenok's
endomorphism the enlarged automaton is bounded, hence contracting, so BBMZ Corollary
cor:RN_BH puts Grigorchuk's group H in a finitely presented simple group, and H lies in the
permutational class. Lane proof, not reviewed; no priority claimed.
