Generalize last-section HNN embeddings: bounded criterion, Grigorchuk supergroup and Fabrykowski-Gupta

A d-ary version of the last-section embedding lemma, with a checkable criterion (finite and
eventually finitary first sections) under which the Röver-Nekrashevych host is generated by a
finite bounded automaton, hence contracting. Consequences: Boone-Higman and permutational
Boone-Higman for Bartholdi's finitely presented amenable HNN extension of the Grigorchuk
supergroup, and for the ascending HNN extension of the Fabrykowski-Gupta group. Records why
Basilica, Brunner-Sidki-Vieira and Gupta-Sidki fall outside the criterion. Lane proofs, not
reviewed; no priority claimed.
