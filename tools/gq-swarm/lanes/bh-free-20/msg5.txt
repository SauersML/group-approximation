Close a germ host class under cone-sectioned ascending HNN extensions

Define C_d: full cone-closed groups of Cantor homeomorphisms containing V_d, with singular
points in finitely many rational tail classes and F_infinity stabilizers of finite rational
sets in every cone-supported subgroup. Every bounded Rover-Nekrashevych group is in C_d;
every member embeds in a finitely presented simple group through a type (A) action; and C_d
is closed under ascending HNN extensions K*_phi (K in a member) whose endomorphism acts on the
last cone as the identity copy and whose tail group is F_infinity.

The proof reuses the one-point germ extension argument with an arbitrary member as base,
chooses a nonsingular primitive block ray (removing hypothesis H0), and returns from phi^m to
phi by a Krasner-Kaloujnine wreath with Sym(m), which a cone-closed host contains. So
iterated towers of such extensions over bounded automata groups satisfy BH and PBH. Lane
proof, not reviewed.
