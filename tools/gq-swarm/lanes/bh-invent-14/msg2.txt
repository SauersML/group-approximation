Higman envelopes are HNN extensions of (machine x input) coupled only by the graph of F -> G

Makes the Higman step explicit: a benign pair (K, L) for the relators gives P = HNN_s(H x G) with
associated subgroups <F, t^-1 F t> x 1 and the graph of pi. Under a decidably-benign premise (DB),
the envelope has decidable word and membership problems, so (D) of the envelope follows from (D) of
the input and of the machine. For the v6 seed gate, (SEED) splits into seed(G), seed(machine), and
one HNN closure along graph(pi); emitters cost one seed-coded subgroup pair. (DB) for a total
reversible decider tower is stated, not verified.

Lesson for general BH: every transfer principle splits into input, machine and one HNN along the
graph of the quotient map; CAP is decided by whether local rules can write that graph.
