Kurosh relative seeds by re-splitting; B_1 seeded modulo the input; (B_1, <t,a_i>) reduced to stable-letter pairs

A free product of factor subgroups becomes a vertex group after re-splitting V_1*...*V_r as a star
around C_1*...*C_r, so the existing gluing theorem gives the Kurosh case with no new rigidity argument.
Applied to the reordered Higman tower (Cohen, CGT 1989, sections 9.4, 9.6): K_M in C, and B_1 in C given
the input seed. New splittings B_1 = J_K *_F(a) J_Q, J_K = K_M *_F(x,t) J, J = HNN(Z_J; t centralizing
Sigma) show <t,a_i> = F(a) * <t> and reduce (B_1, <t,a_i>) to three stable-letter pairs, the first being
the locally finite digit group (Z_J, F(a)).

Lesson for general BH: relative seeds follow the subgroup s own splitting; what is left are subgroups
generated by the machine digit letters, which need gluing along a cocompact core.
