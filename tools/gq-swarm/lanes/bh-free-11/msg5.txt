Prove Kohl's factorization conjecture for RCWA(Z)

not reviewed). Every residue-class-wise affine permutation g of Z factors as
(commuting class shifts)(element of CT(Z))(commuting class reflections):
- precompose with class reflections to make every piece increasing;
- postcompose with class shifts to kill the integer shift of each piece;
- the result fixes N_0, so it lies in CT(Z) by Kourovka 17.59.

refinement.
