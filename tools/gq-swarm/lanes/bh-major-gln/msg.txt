GL_n(Q) skeleton, and roots from a Leavitt unit commuting with a Leavitt copy

An infinite-order unit of L_F(1,2) that commutes with a unital copy of
L_F(1,2) has roots of every order, by block-companion matrices over a tower
of Leavitt tuples taken inside successive relative commutants. So (Q,+)
embeds in the unit group. Over a finite field such a unit would contradict
the open finite-rank centralizer conjecture, which isolates the one question
the Leavitt route to GL_n(Q) depends on. Also adds the end-to-end skeleton
for BBMZ 5.3(5): where the primes are free (in finitely presented decidable
envelopes, through the Toeplitz resolvent), where they block (central
scalars, finite-data hosts), and the three live routes.
