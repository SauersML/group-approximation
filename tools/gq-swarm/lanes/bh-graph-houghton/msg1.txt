Reduce Boone–Higman for graph Houghton groups to finite presentation of one eventually rigid G-automorphism group

Hill–Kwak–Udall–West ask whether graph Houghton groups satisfy Boone–Higman. PB_r embeds, via a lift based at
an end, in the group P_G(r) of eventually rigid G-automorphisms of G * F(X). For G finitely generated, infinite,
simple and MIF, P_G(r) acts on finitely supported G-homomorphisms faithfully and highly transitively, with
finitely generated stabilizers, localizing BFFHZ's lemmas. Finite presentation of P_G(r) is recorded as the one
open gate, with a bounded-support presentation criterion. Lane proofs, not reviewed.
