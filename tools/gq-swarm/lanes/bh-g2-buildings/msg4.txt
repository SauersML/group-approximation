Prove the horofunction lemma: every hyperbolic group has an SFT horofunction compactification

hyperbolic-groups-have-sft-horofunction-compactifications: on any hyperbolic Cayley graph, for
k >= 13 delta + 4, every function that is k-locally a distance function is a limit of distance
functions. The defect f - d(.,q) to a local target q is non-increasing along descents and
non-decreasing along geodesics toward q. The four-point condition forces every geodesic from an
upstream point to q through the window where q fits, so the defect is constant upstream. So the
distance-gradient SFT is exactly the horofunction compactification, with an isolated dense seed orbit,
for every hyperbolic group, Kazhdan ones included. No merging is used; merging fails in general
(Webster-Winchester non-Busemann points, read at source). Rigidity stays open. Also marks HL as
proved in the reduction node. Lane proof, unreviewed.
