Wall chains prove quantum rigidity: surface groups and median Cayley graphs lie in the class C

wall-chamber-distance-sfts-are-quantum-rigid: on a chamber graph whose distance counts separating
walls, every quantum family of the distance-gradient SFT collapses to one projection per wall. A
chain of pairwise non-separated nested walls between any two walls is a local monotone chain, so
all wall projections commute. With the axioms checked for closed surface groups (standard
generators) and for groups acting simply transitively on vertices of CAT(0) cube complexes (RAAGs,
RACGs, Z^n, F_n), these groups lie in C, the first one-ended members beyond products of trees. Wall
members of C contain no infinite FW group, so no canonical Kazhdan input: (star-C) needs rigidity
without walls. Lane proof, unreviewed.
