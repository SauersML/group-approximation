Z[1/P] with P infinite lies in no f.g. linear group; localization lattice

Lane gq-infinite-primes. A congruence-filtration lemma: over a mixed-
characteristic DVR with finitely generated residue field, a matrix with
q^j-th roots for all j is torsion. Consequences: for infinite P, Z[1/P]
(hence GL_n(Z[1/P]) and GL_n(Q)) embeds in no finitely generated linear
group over any field; and Z[1/P] embeds in GL_N(Z[1/P']) only if P is
contained in P'. Lane proofs, not independently reviewed.
