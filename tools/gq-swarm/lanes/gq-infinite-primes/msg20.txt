Euler base in a triangular ring: f.p. Steinberg host with no corner obstruction

Lane gq-infinite-primes. T_l = [[D, D/Du],[0, Z]] is a finitely presented
residually finite ring whose off-diagonal corner is the Euler base M,
containing Z_(l). So M embeds in the finitely presented St_N(T_l), N >= 5,
and G_l in the f.g. RF group E_N(T_l). Z_(l) is only a square-zero bimodule,
so the corner tame-symbol obstruction for R_l does not arise. The bottleneck
reduces to residual finiteness: new OPEN claims on the finite residual of
St_N(T_l) and on K_2(N, T_l), with routes. Bottleneck Attempt 7.
