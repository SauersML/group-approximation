Degree-one corner unitaries fail the K-budget; multi-type gap closed with a positive fixed vector

Lane gq-infinite-primes. In a graded ring containing Q, a degree-one unitary on
a full corner eRe with [1] a nonzero rational multiple of [e] in K_0(R)
forces the budget to fail: either lambda -> [lambda e + 1 - e] has a
non-finitely generated kernel (then K_1 detection fails), or the Steinberg
symbols {lambda, w + 1 - e} of units of R are infinitely generated (then no
E_N(R) is finitely presented). For frame designs a fixed idempotent of the
base gives the unitary, and K_0(B) -> K_0(R) kills (1 - phi_*)K_0(B) with no
Pimsner sequence. Closes multi-type matricial bases with a nonnegative fixed
vector spanning [1_B] modulo im(1 - A), including irreducible A of spectral
radius 1. Gate Attempt 13, stated for the widened gate.
