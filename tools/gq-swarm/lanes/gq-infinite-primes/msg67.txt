the compressions along separated vacuum isometries s_1^(M_j) s_2 whose cross
terms vanish since t_2 s_1 = 0 = t_1 s_2: a simultaneous word multiplier for R_L.
With it Khanh cone and fan arguments give an (r-3)-acyclic, simply connected
frame complex X_r(R_L) for r >= 4, and GL_r/E_r = Z gives E_r-transitivity on
frames of size <= r-1. The route to (i) now needs only the characteristic-0
