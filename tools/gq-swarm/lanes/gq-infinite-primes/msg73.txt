St_N(R_L) is generated by two copies of itself (binary self-similar splitting)

Lane gq-infinite-primes. The isometric twists by s_1 and s_2 are injective with
images the e- and f-corner subgroups (R e* = R ee*), which generate St_N(R_L) by
the Cuntz relation and meet in the copy of St_(N-1) and the central K_2. Iterating
gives a binary tree of copies indexed by cylinders, a design input for window
N >= 1 shell enumerations.
