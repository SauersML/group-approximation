Unit symmetries of a sigma-simple core are graded or inessential

Lane gq-infinite-primes. For R generated by a sigma-simple core A and a unit x
normalizing it: either R = A[x^+-1; sigma] (graded, killed by the graded
dichotomy), or the kernel is generated by a central 1 + g_1 x + ... + g_m x^m
with g_m a unit; then R is free of rank m over A, A is finitely generated,
sigma^m is inner, and the gate conditions pass to A with m[1_A]. So a unit
symmetry cannot give an infinite-rank base finite presentation. Gate Attempt
11 records the constraint and leaves non-invertible shifts (register squeeze).
