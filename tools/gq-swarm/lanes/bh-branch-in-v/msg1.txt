Reduce Boone-Higman-adjacent branch-in-V question to boundary-sandwiched Schreier graphs

A finitely generated branch group embeds in Thompson's V iff some subgroup of a
boundary-point stabilizer has a context-free Schreier graph (and it can be taken
sandwiched over finite-index rigid stabilizers, by the Matte Bon-Nekrashevych-Zheng
first key step, extended here to weakly branch groups with f.g. rist(n)). With
finite germs the embedding forces an exponential-growth boundary orbit, so no
f.g. branch group generated by a bounded automaton embeds in V. Records the
reduction on the Bodart-D'Angeli-Perego-Rodaro question node.
