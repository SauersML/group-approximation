Row factors lift Z crossed products into Z² hosts: gate U decouples into 1D embedding + G2 realization

Gate U over Z² thus splits into a 1D problem (G in ((LC(Y,F_2) x Z) ⊗ L)^x for effective Y, no fp or rigidity) and G2 (rigid free minimal Z² SFT with that row factor). Lesson for general BH included.
