Finitely presented strongly shift-similar groups have finitely presented Houghton-like groups for every n

Stabilizers of finite sets in a strongly shift-similar group are copies of the group. Mixing finitely
presented highly transitive permutation groups on disjoint sets with all finitary permutations gives a
finitely presented group. Since G^n.FSym is normal in H_n(G) with quotient Z^(n-1), H_n(G) is finitely
presented whenever G is. This answers the finitely presented case of Mallery-Zaremsky Question 5.13
(first and third sentences) and settles H_n of the zigzag cyclic envelope. Lane proofs, not reviewed.
