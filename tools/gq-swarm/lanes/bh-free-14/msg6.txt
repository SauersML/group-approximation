Deligne's class survives on every linear rational projective host

No homomorphism Sp_2n(Z) -> GL_N(Z) (n >= 2) makes Deligne's class die on the
rational Cantorization S^_(N-1): every V_lambda has invariants under the
long-root SL_2 (the H-invariant weights of C[G/U] form a monoid containing all
fundamental weights), giving an orbit in no rational hyperplane with a
symplectic block in its stabilizer; then Mackey-Moore as before. Corrects my
earlier remark that k*std escapes the obstruction (two nodes amended). The
rational projective route for Deligne now needs a genuinely piecewise
embedding of Sp_2n(Z) in T_m.
