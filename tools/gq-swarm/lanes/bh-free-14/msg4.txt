Lagrangian Thompson group: Deligne's lattice inside; finiteness engines excluded

(1) Maslov Cantorization Y_Λ of the real Lagrangian Grassmannian is a Cantor
set with an equivariant surjection to Λ_n(R); the odometer Lagrangian Thompson
group V_Λ^τ (full group of O_τ × PSp_2n(Z) ⋉ Y_Λ) contains Deligne's lattice
for odd n ≥ 3, centre acting as the odometer. (2) The Lagrangian actions of
Sp_2n(Z) and of Deligne's lattice are never almost-V, so BHM Theorem 2.1 over
V cannot certify such hosts. (3) OPEN: V_Λ^τ finitely presented with simple
commutator subgroup containing Deligne's lattice; attempts recorded.
