Deligne lift: extension dies on Lagrangian Cantor covers; measure obstruction

Two lane-proof nodes. (1) Any odometer-type lift of a central Z-extension over a
compact Q-space with an invariant probability measure forces the real class to
vanish; so equicontinuous (profinite) models never lift Deligne's class, and the
Heisenberg group never lifts over Z^2. (2) Deligne's class dies on every faithful
Cantor Sp_2n(Z)-space covering the real Lagrangian Grassmannian, giving a faithful
action of Deligne's lattice on Z_2 x Y by odometer-cocycle maps (centre = +2);
finite presentation of a host (Lagrangian Thompson group) stays open.
