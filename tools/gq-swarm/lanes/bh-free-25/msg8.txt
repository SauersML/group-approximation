G2 second angle: deterministic-time lifts inherit quantum rigidity

An SFT over Lambda_1 x Lambda_2 that is deterministic in the Lambda_1 factor
(rows in an SFT Y, moved by invertible cellular automata) is quantum rigid
whenever Y is: each row algebra commutes by rigidity of Y, and the local rules
make neighbouring row algebras equal. The lift is free or minimal exactly when
the joint action on Y is, so it is never free over a free space factor
(Piantadosi, cited), and invertible linear time evolutions over trees are
shifts (trivial units in F_p[F_n]).

Lesson for general BH: deterministic (automaton, self-similar, space-time)
codings are rigid for free in the time direction and add nothing across space
walls. G2 over a product then needs a rigid Lambda_2-free row SFT with a large
automorphism group, or a genuinely coupled rule. Lane proof, not reviewed.
