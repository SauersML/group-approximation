Land the lamp-digit self-similarity criterion and the KMS self-similar module route

New claim lamp-digit-modules-give-self-similar-semidirect-products: an
F_p[Q]-module with restriction-closed lamp functions over a self-similar Q gives a
self-similar M semidirect Q on T_{X x L}, equivalently a self-similar module
structure (rho, lambda). Boundary-orbit wreaths are self-similar, and their word
problem is WP(Q) plus stabilizer membership.

New claim boundary-orbit-wreaths-over-tree-groups-are-not-fp: tree groups have
infinitely many orbits on pairs of an infinite boundary orbit, so by Cornulier
those wreaths are never finitely presented.

New OPEN claim kms-configuration-modules-carry-self-similar-structures, with
route hard-self-similar-groups-via-self-similar-kms-modules into
fp-self-similar-groups-with-arbitrarily-hard-word-problem (Attempt 4). The
one-counter factors of the KMS top are finite-state affine automaton groups for
odd p; the K-counter factor has no scalar affine realization for K >= 2.
