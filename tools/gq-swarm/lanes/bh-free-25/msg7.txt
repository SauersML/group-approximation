Affine self-similar hosts reach exactly the global-field-linear groups

A finitely generated group embeds in a finitely generated affine
self-similar group over Z_p or F_q[[pi]] iff it is linear over a global
field. The forward direction is the Jacobian/etale argument, now also in
characteristic 0 (the carries are digit constants). The converse uses a
degree-one place, S-integers closed under the digit shift, and one S-unit to
make the translation module finitely generated.

Lesson for general BH: self-similar hosts split into affine
(global-field-linear, easy WP), fibered/parameter
(positive-char-polynomial-affine-groups-are-self-similar: all char-p linear)
and non-algebraic. Hard or non-linear inputs need section equations that fail
to be etale. Lane proof, not reviewed, no novelty claimed.
