The two-counter KMS top has no self-similar affine action, whatever the corners

For K >= 2, R_K x| U_K (the KMS top factor B_0) has no faithful self-similar
action on a p^d-ary tree with lamps as translations and U acting by causal
linear maps, Toeplitz or not, finite-state or not. The corners give an
injective ring endomorphism psi; the unit lemma (u, 1-u in U forces u into a
single F_p(s_i)) makes it quasi-finite; normality gives g in R; and a
sigma-stable nonzero ideal then has all digits zero. K = 1 escapes through a
pole of g, which is the power-series shift D. Consequence recorded on the hard
self-similar node: KMS realizations must be nonlinear on digits.

Lane proof, elementary, not reviewed.
