Braid groups quasi-isometrically embed in finitely presented simple groups

Cap every hole of the n-holed disk with a one-holed torus. The framed braid group then
sits in the mapping class group of the once-punctured genus-n surface, undistorted by the
subsurface theorem. That group is a Lipschitz retract of Out(F_2n) (Hamenstadt-Hensel).
Lifting along the boundary gives an undistorted B_n in Aut(F_2n), and so in the BFFHZ
host. The same route handles mapping class groups of surfaces with boundary.

By contrast, Handel-Mosher's coindex >= 2 theorem makes the symmetric automorphism groups,
and so the loop braid groups, distorted in Aut(F_n) and in that host.
