Locally finite splittings preserve rigid SFT compactifications: BS(m,n), GBS groups, mapping tori, polycyclic groups lie in class C

A finite graph of groups with finite-index edge groups has a quantum-rigid SFT compactification once one
vertex group does: pointers toward a unique sink coset are finite labels, rigid by exact transport, edge
exclusivity and monotone chains; the sink coset runs the vertex compactification. This settles the (star-C)
test cases BS(1,2) and H_3(Z) and closes C under finite-index overgroups and mapping tori. The remaining
gate is infinite-index edge groups (relative seeds) and groups without splittings.
