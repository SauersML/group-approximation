Steinberg-algebra finite presentation is a Kakutani invariant; automorphic extensions need no rigidity but are capped

Full corners of finitely presented algebras are finitely presented, so finite presentation of the Steinberg
algebra (the rigidity gate E2 / premise E-prime) is an invariant of the groupoid up to Kakutani equivalence. In
particular quantum rigidity of Z^2 SFTs is invariant under orbit-equivalence recodings. A finitely presented
group of outer automorphisms of a groupoid with finitely presented algebra gives a finitely presented central
simple crossed product with no rigidity hypothesis, but its word problem is then bounded by the algebra's.
So rigidity and the carrier principle are one gate.
