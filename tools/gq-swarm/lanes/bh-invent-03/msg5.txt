Relative seeds glue graphs of groups into class C; graphs of free and free abelian groups are in C

A rigid relative seed for (A, C) is an SFT on A with an isolated point whose stabilizer is exactly C. They glue
along any finite graph of groups (vertex-marked and transitive), are stable under finite-index induction, and
exist for all fg subgroups of free and free abelian groups. So every graph of free or free abelian groups with
fg edge groups, including HNN extensions of free groups such as modular-machine groups, is in C. The forced
point is a membership oracle for C, so CAP reduces to rigid relative seeds for hard-membership subgroups.
