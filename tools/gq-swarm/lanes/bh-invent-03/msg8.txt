Core-marked gluing: subgroups with finite core and full core-edge stabilizers inherit rigid relative seeds

Generalizes the vertex-marked gluing (a single sink vertex) to an S-invariant core subtree with finite
quotient. Vertex pieces carry their relative seeds, core edges are read as right cosets (full edge
stabilizers), and out vertices point to the core. Rigidity comes from directed implications only: pointers
monotone toward the core, and core atoms shared across edges. Covers Kurosh subgroups of free products with
finite core; Theorem K of d13f60e6f is the star case. The residual obstruction without full edges is the
edge twist.
