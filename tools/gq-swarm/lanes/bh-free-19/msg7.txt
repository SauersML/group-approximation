Real-geometric hosts are complexity-capped: fp box-map groups specialize to algebraic data; lessons for general BH

fp-simple-real-box-map-groups-are-not-universal-hosts (lane proof, not reviewed): let G
be finitely presented and act faithfully on [0,1)^n by box-exchange maps (nV-type:
scaling, translation, coordinate permutation on half-open boxes) with arbitrary real
data. Then G specializes to algebraic data nontrivially on any finite set, and G has
solvable word problem. Its finitely generated simple subgroups embed over a real number
field and have word problem in time 2^{O(l)}. So the class of all finitely presented
simple groups of such maps, over every n and together with the one-dimensional
piecewise-projective ones, shares one recursive bound. By
complexity-bounded-host-classes-are-not-universal, it is not a universal Boone-Higman
host class.
