Refute the generic V * Z claim: V and a homeomorphism never generate their free product

For every nontrivial homeomorphism t of a Hausdorff space and any group T whose rigid
stabilizers of open sets are nonabelian, pick an open U with t(U) disjoint from U and
noncommuting a, b in T supported in U. Then [[t,a],b] = [a^{-1},b] holds in Homeo(X),
while the corresponding length-8 alternating word is nontrivial in T * <t>. So
<V,t> is never V * <t> in Homeo(Cantor), for any t, not just generically.

This refutes fp-dense-cantor-subgroups-need-not-have-fg-clopen-stabilizers (its Baire
step overlooks that syllables of V fix open sets pointwise) and reopens the remark in
permutational-boone-higman-iff-dense-cantor-actors on whether the stabilizer clause
can be dropped. Lane proof, elementary, not reviewed; no novelty claimed.
