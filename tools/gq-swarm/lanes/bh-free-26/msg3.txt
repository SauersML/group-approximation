Kill Leavitt-linear simple hosts for the C_=P-complete integral group Γ_Z

The candidate (L_{Z[1/M]}(1,2)^{⊗2})^x mod centre is never simple. For each
p ∤ M the congruence kernel contains T(c,1,d)^p = 1 + p a_c a_d^*, which is not
central, so the quotient is not simple whatever the K-theory. Over Q, scalars
lie in the perfect L_Q(1,2)^x, so no central quotient is finitely presented
(leavitt-scalar-commutators-block-fp-central-quotients).

A nontrivial f.g. simple subgroup of GL_N over a rational Leavitt tensor
algebra reduces injectively mod some prime p. It therefore has no nontrivial
square-zero unipotent, cannot hold Γ_Z in standard position, and has word
problem in ∀·Mod_pP. An abstract embedding of Γ_Z would give
C_=P ⊆ ∀·Mod_pP, which fails relative to an oracle. Affine actors over
Z[1/M] keep v_p of differences, so they are not type (A).

A C_=P-hard fp simple group must come from a non-linear construction, e.g. a
uniform direct product of the P_p via the Chinese remainder theorem.
