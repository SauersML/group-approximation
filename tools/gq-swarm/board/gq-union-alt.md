group (so RF groups, solvable groups, V, T, T-bar, A, VA by O4), and is closed under subgroups, extensions, arbitrary

wreaths, lamp wreaths and extensions never create GL_n(Q); only directed unions, graphs of groups with nontrivial
edges, and Thompson-type completions (SV_G, V_d(G), germ extensions, nV) can. First falsifiable step: the extension
clause (done: perfectness makes the composite to the quotient trivial).
