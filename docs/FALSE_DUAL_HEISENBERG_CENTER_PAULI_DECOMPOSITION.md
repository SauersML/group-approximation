# The dual Heisenberg corner is a center-valued Pauli bundle

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

Let `Gamma<G` be the fixed Kun--Thom compression pair and retain the dual
Heisenberg envelope

```text
E=H(A,T) semidirect G,
A=F_2^(G),
T=span_F2{1_(gGamma):g in G}
```

from `FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_ENVELOPE.md`.  Put

```text
p=(1-u_z)/2,
M_0=pL(H(A,T)).                                      (CPD1)
```

The negative kernel corner has a completely explicit structure.  If

```text
X=G/Gamma,
B=F_2^(X),
q:A->B,
q(a)_C=sum_(y in C)a_y,
K=ker(q),                                             (CPD2)
```

then, trace preservingly,

```text
M_0 isomorphic L(K) tensor R_X,
R_X=bar-tensor_(C in X)(M_2,tr_2).                   (CPD3)
```

The factor `R_X` is the usual qubit Pauli model of the hyperfinite CAR
factor.  The center of `M_0` is exactly `L(K)`.  After choosing one
representative in each left coset, the `G`-action on `(CPD3)` is a pure
permutation of the Pauli sites, multiplied by one explicit central
one-cocycle.

There is also an exact gauge cover.  Pull the center back along

```text
dual(A) -> dual(K).                                   (CPD4)
```

On the larger ordinary Bernoulli space `dual(A)={-1,1}^G`, the central
cocycle is an exact coboundary.  This gives a trace-preserving
`G`-equivariant embedding

```text
M_0 -> L_infinity(dual(A)) tensor R_X.                (CPD5)
```

The image is exactly the fixed algebra of a compact gauge group, and the
associated conditional expectation is `G`-equivariant.  Consequently

```text
pL(E)=M_0 crossed G
  -> (L_infinity(dual(A)) tensor R_X) crossed G       (CPD6)
```

is an expected trace-preserving inclusion.

This does not yet prove Connes embeddability.  The classical center action
is already sofic and its crossed product is Connes embeddable, but the
Pauli transport in `(CPD6)` contains the hard Kun--Thom coset action.  Thus
the sole remaining obstruction in this envelope is now identified exactly:
coherent normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt transport of the Pauli fibers over an
otherwise benign Bernoulli/central base.

## 2. The parity quotient and the radical of the pairing

For each `C in X`, let `t_C in T` be the characteristic functional of `C`.
The left cosets partition `G`, so the `t_C` are linearly independent and

```text
T isomorphic F_2^(X)=B.                               (CPD7)
```

The map `q` in `(CPD2)` is onto: if `r(C) in C` is any representative,
then

```text
q(a_(r(C)))=e_C.                                     (CPD8)
```

Moreover the evaluation pairing used in the Heisenberg multiplication is

```text
t_b(a)=sum_(C in X)b_C q(a)_C,
b in B, a in A.                                      (CPD9)
```

It follows immediately that its radical on the `A`-side is `K`, while it
has zero radical on the `T`-side.

Choose a section `r:X->G` and let

```text
j:B->A,  j(e_C)=a_(r(C)).                             (CPD10)
```

Then

```text
A=K direct-sum j(B).                                  (CPD11)
```

In the negative corner write

```text
U_k=p u_(k,0,0),                  k in K,
X_C=p u_(j(e_C),0,0),
Z_C=p u_(0,t_C,0).                                   (CPD12)
```

The `U_k` are central commuting unitaries.  The two Pauli families satisfy

```text
[X_C,X_D]=[Z_C,Z_D]=0,
Z_C X_D Z_C^*=(-1)^(delta_(C,D))X_D.                 (CPD13)
```

For a finite set `F subset X`, the algebra generated by `X_C,Z_C`,
`C in F`, is `M_(2^|F|)` with its normalized trace.  Increasing `F` through
`X` gives `R_X`.  Every twisted Fourier monomial has a unique form

```text
U_k X_b Z_t,   k in K, b,t in B,                     (CPD14)
```

up to its prescribed scalar sign.  The normalized corner trace of `(CPD14)`
is zero unless `k=b=t=0`.  Equations `(CPD11)--(CPD14)` therefore prove the
trace-preserving tensor decomposition `(CPD3)`.

They also identify the center.  A monomial commuting with every `X_C` has
`t=0`; commuting with every `Z_C` then has `b=0`.  Thus only the `U_k`
remain, and

```text
Z(M_0)=L(K).                                           (CPD15)
```

## 3. The exact center-valued Pauli cocycle

The parity map is `G`-equivariant, so `K` is a `G`-module.  Define

```text
kappa(g,C)=g j(e_C)+j(e_(gC)) in K.                  (CPD16)
```

The plus sign is also subtraction over `F_2`.  Direct expansion gives

```text
kappa(gh,C)=g kappa(h,C)+kappa(g,hC).                (CPD17)
```

Under `(CPD3)`, the action `alpha` of `G` is

```text
alpha_g(U_k)=U_(gk),
alpha_g(X_C)=U_(kappa(g,C)) X_(gC),
alpha_g(Z_C)=Z_(gC).                                  (CPD18)
```

Equation `(CPD17)` is precisely what makes `(CPD18)` an action.  Thus the
entire departure from a pure tensor permutation is the central cocycle
`kappa`.

The radical element from the envelope note is especially small in these
coordinates.  If

```text
r=t_(sGamma)+t_(gamma sGamma),                       (CPD19)
```

then

```text
p u_(v_r)=Z_(sGamma)Z_(gamma sGamma).                (CPD20)
```

It is a two-site Pauli word.  A primal lamp in the symmetric difference of
the two cosets has an `X`-component at exactly one of those sites, so the
commutator is the scalar `-1`.  Hence local trace visibility is already
exact; only global `G`-transport is missing.

## 4. The classical center action is sofic and Connes embeddable

Let

```text
Omega=dual(A)={-1,1}^G,
Y=dual(K).                                             (CPD21)
```

Restriction of characters is a `G`-equivariant continuous surjection

```text
rho:Omega->Y.                                         (CPD22)
```

Pullback along `rho` gives a trace-preserving `G`-equivariant inclusion

```text
L_infinity(Y)=L(K) -> L_infinity(Omega)=L(A).         (CPD23)
```

It follows, by preserving Fourier coefficients and the canonical trace,
that

```text
L(K) crossed G -> L(A) crossed G                     (CPD24)
```

is injective.  The algebra on the right is

```text
L((direct-sum_G C_2) semidirect G),                  (CPD25)
```

the ordinary regular wreath product algebra.  The fixed group `G` is
residually finite, hence sofic, and regular wreath products with amenable
lamps are sofic.  Therefore `(CPD25)`, and hence `(CPD24)`, is Connes
embeddable.  In Păunescu's diagonal-preserving terminology, restriction of
a Bernoulli sofic embedding also shows that the center action itself is a
sofic action.

For the fixed pair the center action is in fact essentially free.  Here is
a direct proof.  If `1!=g notin Gamma`, choose infinitely many disjoint
pairs `{x_i,y_i} subset Gamma` and put

```text
k_i=a_(x_i)+a_(y_i) in K.                             (CPD26)
```

The cosets `Gamma` and `gGamma` are disjoint, so the vectors
`(g-1)k_i` have pairwise disjoint nonempty supports and are linearly
independent.  If `1!=g in Gamma`, the infinite Kazhdan group `Gamma` is not
virtually cyclic.  Hence left translation by `<g>` has infinitely many
orbits on `Gamma`.  Pair distinct such orbits, choose one representative
from each, and use `(CPD26)` again.  The vectors `(g-1)k_i` are again
nonzero with disjoint orbit supports.  Thus

```text
dim_F2((g-1)K)=infinity                              (CPD27)
```

for every `g!=1`.  The fixed characters in `dual(K)` annihilate `(g-1)K`,
so they form a closed subgroup of infinite index and Haar measure zero.
Countability of `G` proves essential freeness.

This is a useful separation: neither the center, its invariant measure, nor
its classical orbit relation is the analytic obstruction.

## 5. Exact Bernoulli gauge trivialization

Let `x_C,z_C` denote the standard Pauli generators of `R_X` with the
relations `(CPD13)`.  For `a in A`, write

```text
hat(a)(eta)=eta(a),   eta in Omega.                   (CPD28)
```

Define a map on the generators of `M_0` by

```text
Phi(U_k)=hat(k) tensor 1,
Phi(X_C)=hat(j(e_C)) tensor x_C,
Phi(Z_C)=1 tensor z_C.                                (CPD29)
```

The relations `(CPD13)` show that `Phi` is a star homomorphism.  It is
trace preserving: a Fourier--Pauli monomial maps to

```text
hat(k+j(b)) tensor x_b z_t,                           (CPD30)
```

whose product trace is zero unless `k=b=t=0`.  Therefore `Phi` is
injective.

Let `delta` be the product action on the target:

```text
delta_g(hat(a))=hat(ga),
delta_g(x_C)=x_(gC),
delta_g(z_C)=z_(gC).                                  (CPD31)
```

Equivariance on `U_k` and `Z_C` is immediate.  On `X_C`, equations
`(CPD16)` and `(CPD29)` give

```text
Phi(alpha_g(X_C))
 =hat(kappa(g,C)+j(e_(gC))) tensor x_(gC)
 =hat(gj(e_C)) tensor x_(gC)
 =delta_g(Phi(X_C)).                                  (CPD32)
```

This proves `(CPD5)` and the crossed-product inclusion `(CPD6)`.

The image has an intrinsic description.  Put

```text
H=dual(B)={-1,1}^X.                                   (CPD33)
```

For `lambda in H`, regard `lambda composed q` as a character of `A` and
define the gauge automorphism

```text
beta_lambda(hat(a))=lambda(q(a))hat(a),
beta_lambda(x_C)=lambda_C x_C,
beta_lambda(z_C)=z_C.                                 (CPD34)
```

Every generator in `(CPD29)` is fixed.  Conversely the Fourier--Pauli
monomial `hat(a) tensor x_b z_t` is fixed by all `beta_lambda` exactly when

```text
q(a)=b.                                               (CPD35)
```

Writing `a=k+j(b)` then identifies it with the image of `(CPD14)`.  Hence

```text
Phi(M_0)=(L_infinity(Omega) tensor R_X)^H.            (CPD36)
```

The action `delta` normalizes the compact gauge action by permuting `H`.
Haar averaging in `(CPD36)` is consequently a `G`-equivariant
trace-preserving conditional expectation.  Its coefficientwise extension
makes `(CPD6)` an expected inclusion.

## 6. The hard generalized wreath algebra is already inside the corner

There is a stronger limitation than the one supplied by the gauge cover.
The dual Pauli algebra

```text
D_Z=W^*(Z_C:C in X) subset M_0.                       (CPD37)
```

is abelian, isomorphic to `L_infinity({-1,1}^X)`, and `(CPD18)` gives the
untwisted covariance

```text
alpha_g(Z_C)=Z_(gC).                                  (CPD38)
```

Therefore the canonical unitaries define a trace-preserving inclusion

```text
D_Z crossed G
 isomorphic L(T semidirect G)
 isomorphic L((direct-sum_X C_2) semidirect G)
 -> pL(E).                                            (CPD39)
```

The middle group is exactly the Kun--Thom generalized wreath product.  It
is nonsofic, and its radical witness is the two-site word `(CPD20)`.
Consequently

```text
pL(E) Connes embeddable
  ==> L((direct-sum_X C_2) semidirect G)
      Connes embeddable
  ==> FALSE.                                          (CPD40)
```

The first implication is just von Neumann subalgebra permanence.  Thus the
dual Heisenberg construction gives useful Pauli structure and a central
radical, but its tracial endpoint is not analytically cheaper than the
original generalized-wreath endpoint: the latter occurs as an expected
Fourier subalgebra of the former.

## 7. Exact limitation of the gauge cover

The pullback removes `kappa` exactly, but it does not remove the coset
transport.  Let

```text
D_X=W^*(x_C:C in X) subset R_X.                       (CPD41)
```

This is the standard diagonal algebra `L_infinity({-1,1}^X)`, it is
`G`-invariant under `(CPD31)`, and

```text
D_X crossed G
 isomorphic L((direct-sum_X C_2) semidirect G)        (CPD42)
```

embeds trace preservingly in the right side of `(CPD6)`.  The group in
`(CPD42)` is exactly the Kun--Thom generalized wreath product attached to
the nonsofic action `G action G/Gamma`; it is nonsofic.  Connes
embeddability of `(CPD42)` would itself prove the desired FALSE answer.

Thus one must not infer Connes embeddability of the gauge cover from the
ordinary Bernoulli first tensor factor.  Such an inference would amount to
proving that tensoring an arbitrary action with a free Bernoulli action
makes its crossed product embeddable, which is precisely an unavailable
action-hyperlinearity theorem.

The decomposition nevertheless removes several ambiguities.  The remaining
gate is not:

* local Pauli realization--every finite Pauli cell is exact;
* classical center approximation--`(CPD24)` is already Connes embeddable;
* the center-valued sign itself--`(CPD32)` gauges it away exactly.

It is exactly the following normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt problem:

> Build coherent finite-dimensional transport for the coset-indexed Pauli
> factors in `(CPD31)`, after allowing the regular Bernoulli gauge cover,
> while retaining the two-site word `(CPD20)`.

The operator-norm version is impossible by
`FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_OPERATOR_STERILITY.md`.  The tracial version remains
the live endpoint.  No claim of Connes embeddability of `pL(E)` is made in
this note.

## 8. Literature boundary

The soficity assertion for the classical center uses only the standard
Bernoulli/wreath construction and restriction of the resulting
diagonal-preserving embedding.  The operator-algebraic language is in:

* Liviu Păunescu, *On Sofic Actions and Equivalence Relations*,
  arXiv:1002.0605;
* Gábor Elek and Gábor Lippner, *Sofic equivalence relations*,
  arXiv:0906.3619.

Neither source proves that an arbitrary hyperfinite-factor bundle over a
sofic base has Connes-embeddable crossed product.  Applying such a statement
to `(CPD41)--(CPD42)` would already settle the present open problem, so it
cannot be used as a black-box permanence theorem here.
