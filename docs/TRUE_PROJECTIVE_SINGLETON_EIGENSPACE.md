# The positive singleton fibre is exactly one point plus a projective line

Date: 2026-08-11

## Outcome

For every odd prime `p`, the singleton cycles in the first vertex-resolution
generator of the projective HAP complex are exactly

`{one exceptional point} disjointUnion P^1(F_p)`.                 `(PSE1)`

In particular their number is exactly

`1+(p+1)=p+2`.                                                    `(PSE2)`

This proves the family formula previously observed only at the first few
levels.  It also explains why the two surviving mod-two classes have the
shape found by the exact quotient audit: one exceptional class and one class
supported on the ordinary projective-line fibre.  The theorem does **not**
by itself prove that all points of the large fibre have the same class, nor
does it provide a bounded filling of their differences.  Those are statements
about the horizontal Wall differential, not about the vertical involution.

## The fixed vertical relation

Use HAP's natural three-dimensional model
`ContractibleGcomplex("SL(3,Z)a")`.  The first degree-two generator in the
vertex-stabilizer resolution has boundary

`1+T`, where

`T=[[0,1,1],[1,0,1],[0,0,-1]]` and `T^2=1`.             `(PSE3)`

The projective coefficient action is the row action.  Over any field of odd
characteristic, the eigenspaces are

`V_+=span((1,1,1))`,
`V_-={(x,-x,z):x,z in F_p}`.                            `(PSE4)`

The integral lattice generated by `(1,1,1)`, `(1,-1,0)`, and `(0,0,1)` has
index two in `Z^3`.  Thus reduction modulo an odd prime preserves the direct
sum `F_p^3=V_+ directSum V_-`; there is no denominator collapse in this
description.

## Projective fixed-point count

A coefficient basis vector is a singleton mod-two cycle precisely when its
projective point is fixed by `T`: the two terms in `(PSE3)` then coincide and
cancel modulo two.  If a projective line `[v]` is fixed, then

`vT=lambda v`                                                     `(PSE5)`

for some nonzero scalar `lambda`.  Applying `T` twice gives
`lambda^2=1`, hence `lambda=+1` or `-1` because `p` is odd.  Consequently
the fixed projective locus is the disjoint union

`P(V_+) disjointUnion P(V_-) = P^0(F_p) disjointUnion P^1(F_p)`.   `(PSE6)`

Its cardinality is `(PSE2)`.  The six-dimensional chart used by the raw HAP
export is the symmetric-square/Veronese realization of the same projective
plane, so the count and decomposition transfer unchanged.

The exact HAP certificate is
`experiments/sl3_projective_singleton_eigenspace_exact.g`.  It checks the
boundary `(PSE3)`, both eigensublattices in `(PSE4)`, and their index two.

## Quantitative consequence and remaining gate

The growing part of the singleton set is not an unexplained collection of
coordinates: it is one ordinary projective line.  Moreover
`TRUE_PROJECTIVE_DERIVED_E1_AUDIT.md` now contains an exact four-term
chain whose boundary is the difference of two points of this line for every
odd prime.  To finish the positive two-primary decoder one still needs enough
Wall-compatible translates or additional rational templates to fill every
even subset of `P^1(F_p)` with uniformly bounded normalized Euclidean cost.
The eigenspace theorem neither assumes nor supplies that last uniformity.
