# The Pauli carrier only needs binary-algebra membership

Date: 2026-08-14

## 1. Outcome

The terminal support statement in `TRUE_PAULI_BRANCH_TRANSFER_PACKET.md` can
be weakened substantially.  Keep its projections

```text
E = P Q,                 tau(E)=1/8,
F = U* Q U,              tau(F)=1/4.                 (PCG1)
```

The earlier endpoint asked for the directed containment

```text
F <= E.                                                (PCG2)
```

It is enough to prove the unoriented statement

```text
dist_2(F, W*(E)) -> 0,                                 (PCG3)
```

where `W*(E)=span{E,1-E}` is the two-dimensional algebra generated by the
parent cut.  No identification of the correct atom and no one-sided support
inequality remain in `(PCG3)`.

## 2. Exact distance floor

Let

```text
x=tau(EF),             0<=x<=1/8.                    (PCG4)
```

The trace-preserving conditional expectation of `F` onto `W*(E)` is

```text
E_E(F)=8x E + (8/7)(1/4-x)(1-E).                    (PCG5)
```

Because conditional expectation is the Hilbert--Schmidt orthogonal
projection,

```text
||F-E_E(F)||_2^2
  = 1/4 - 8x^2 - (8/7)(1/4-x)^2.                    (PCG6)
```

Subtracting `3/28` factors the right side exactly:

```text
(PCG6) - 3/28 = (1/14)(1-8x)(1+16x)>=0.             (PCG7)
```

Therefore every such pair of projections satisfies

```text
dist_2(F,W*(E))^2 >= 3/28.                           (PCG8)
```

The bound is sharp at `x=1/8`, the largest possible overlap.  Its scalar
arithmetic is formalized source-first in
`GroupApproximation/Leavitt/PauliCarrierBinaryGap.lean` by
`three_div_twenty_eight_le_binaryResidual`.  The source was not compiled in
this iteration.

## 3. Why this is a real weakening

Containment `(PCG2)` requires recovering all of the following at once:

* the parent atom rather than its complement;
* projection-level support rather than measurability; and
* the correct orientation of the multiplication module.

Binary-algebra membership `(PCG3)` forgets all three choices.  A recovered
finite module may exchange the two parent sheets or retain a scalar mixture;
as long as it proves that `F` is asymptotically measurable by the parent
cut, the fixed trace mismatch gives `(PCG8)`.

This connects directly with
`TRUE_BINARY_EXTENSION_FIBER_MAJORITY_DECODER.md`.  For a projection whose
two branch masses over the parent atom are `m_0,m_1`, its conditional
variance is exactly the obstruction to binary measurability.  Fiberwise
majority is not required at the terminal carrier: variance tending to zero
already contradicts `(PCG8)`.

## 4. New shortest live theorem

The multiplication-to-support gate can now be replaced by:

> **Pauli binary-membership gate.**  In every trace-preserving matrix
> microstate of the atlas group, the cross-root multiplication relations and
> the raw/comb covariance force
> `||F-E_{W*(E)}(F)||_2 -> 0`.

This remains unproved.  It is strictly weaker than the former containment
gate and is not supplied by additive characters: the finite double-Pauli
model has a nonzero conditional variance.  A proof must still recover
coefficient multiplication, but it may discard the orientation and exact
support information that earlier formulations demanded.

Once the binary-membership gate is proved, `(PCG8)` is the final fixed
contradiction.  No whole-map PVM, rank selection, or directed containment is
then needed on this Pauli-carrier route.
