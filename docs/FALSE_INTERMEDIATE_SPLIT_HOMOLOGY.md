# The radical word survives in the split stabilizer preimage

Date: 2026-08-11

## 1. Outcome

For the explicit stable Laurent double, let

`pi:D=G *_Gamma G -> F=G *_E G`,
`R_0=ker(pi)`.                                         `(ISH1)`

The marked radical word

`w=i_2(h)i_1(h)^(-1)`, `h=e_12(x_1^(-1))`             `(ISH2)`

survives with infinite order in the abelianization of the split
intermediate subgroup

`H_J=pi^(-1)(J)=R_0 semidirect J`,                    `(ISH3)`

where

`J=EL_3(Z)=SL_3(Z)`                                    `(ISH4)`

is embedded on coordinates `3,4,5`.  More generally the same holds for
every subgroup `B<=E` satisfying `h notin B Gamma`.

Thus the nonamenable stabilizer does two sharply different things.

1. On the normal `R_0`-cover it constructs a diffuse `ell^2` filling of
   `w`.
2. After quotienting by the stabilizer, in the `H_J`-cover, the same word
   remains an ordinary homology class of infinite order.

Consequently no single integral filling can persist down a finite-index
tower converging to `H_J`.  The invariant transfer mode isolated in
`FALSE_ORTHOGONAL_TRANSFER_FILLING_FORMULA.md` is therefore genuinely live;
it is not another fixed-kernel artifact.

## 2. The Bass--Serre relation module

The fold-kernel computation gives an isomorphism of left `Z[F]`-modules

`(R_0)_ab ~= M`
` :=ker(Z[F/Gamma] -> Z[F/E])`.                       `(ISH5)`

Under this isomorphism,

`[w]=e_h:=delta_(hGamma)-delta_Gamma`.                 `(ISH6)`

Let `B<=E` be any subgroup.  Although the two copies of `E` are not
identified in `D`, either vertex embedding `E->D` is a homomorphic section
of `pi` over the common copy of `E` in `F`.  Fix the first one.  Hence the
restriction of `(ISH1)` over `B` splits:

`H_B:=pi^(-1)(B)=R_0 semidirect B`.                   `(ISH7)`

For a split semidirect product, abelianization contains the coinvariants of
the kernel abelianization as a direct summand:

`(H_B)_ab ~= ((R_0)_ab)_B directSum B_ab`.            `(ISH8)`

Here

`M_B=M/<bm-m:b in B,m in M>`                          `(ISH9)`

denotes coinvariants.

**Lemma 1 (split abelianization).**  Formula `(ISH8)` holds naturally, and
the image of `r in R_0` in `(H_B)_ab` is the coinvariant class of `[r]`.

**Proof.**  In `R_0 semidirect B`, the commutator subgroup is generated by
`[R_0,R_0]`, `[B,B]`, and the elements `b r b^(-1)r^(-1)`.  Quotienting by
these relations abelianizes `R_0` and `B` and imposes precisely the
coinvariant relations on `(R_0)_ab`.  The splitting `B->H_B` keeps the two
resulting factors independent.  End proof.

## 3. A visible coinvariant class

Coinvariants of a permutation module are again free permutation modules:

`(Z[F/Gamma])_B ~= Z[B\F/Gamma]`.                     `(ISH10)`

The inclusion `M->Z[F/Gamma]` induces a map

`M_B -> Z[B\F/Gamma]`.                                `(ISH11)`

No injectivity assertion about `(ISH11)` is needed.

**Theorem 2 (intermediate survival criterion).**  If

`h notin B Gamma`,                                    `(ISH12)`

then `e_h` has infinite order in `M_B`.  Consequently `w` has infinite
order in `(H_B)_ab`.

**Proof.**  The image of the coinvariant class of `e_h` under `(ISH11)` is

`delta_(B h Gamma)-delta_(B Gamma)`.                  `(ISH13)`

Condition `(ISH12)` says exactly that the two double cosets in `(ISH13)`
are distinct.  Their difference is a nonzero primitive vector in the free
abelian group `Z[B\F/Gamma]`, hence has infinite order.  Therefore its
preimage class `e_h in M_B` also has infinite order.  Lemma 1 and `(ISH6)`
give the assertion for `w`.  End proof.

If `B` centralizes `h`, then it fixes the two basis
vectors in `(ISH6)` individually.  This is the stabilizer condition used
to construct the `ell^2` filling in the normal radical cover.  Theorem 2
shows that stabilization is emphatically not annihilation in coinvariants.

## 4. The lower-right Kazhdan subgroup

Take `B=J` as in `(ISH4)`.  Since `J<=Gamma`,

`J Gamma=Gamma`.                                      `(ISH14)`

But `h notin Gamma`, so `(ISH12)` holds.  Therefore:

**Corollary 3.**  The marked word `w` has infinite order in

`H_1(H_J,Z)=(H_J)_ab`.                                `(ISH15)`

At the same time, `J` commutes with `h` and fixes `[w]` in `(R_0)_ab`.
As proved in `FALSE_NONAMENABLE_STABILIZER_FILLING_REDUCTION.md`, its
nonamenability constructs an `ell^2` filling of the lifted loop in the
normal `R_0`-cover.

This apparent contrast is consistent.  The `ell^2` filling is the infinite
translate sum

`z=sum_(s in T)xi_s c_s`.                             `(ISH16)`

It is square-summable on the `R_0`-cover.  Passing to the quotient by the
infinite group `J` sums infinitely many translates and is not a bounded
map on cellular `ell^2`.  Hence `(ISH16)` need not descend to an `ell^2`
filling on the `H_J`-cover, and ordinary homology there remains nonzero.

## 5. Finite towers approaching the split preimage

Let `L_n normal F` be finite-index subgroups and put

`H_n=pi^(-1)(J L_n)`.                                  `(ISH17)`

Assume the relative chain is separating:

`intersection_n J L_n=J`.                             `(ISH18)`

Then `intersection_n H_n=H_J`.  The finite-index radical theorem gives

`w in [H_n,H_n]`                                      `(ISH19)`

at every finite stage, while Corollary 3 gives

`w notin [H_J,H_J]`.                                  `(ISH20)`

Apply the integral filling escape theorem from
`FALSE_REAL_FILLING_ESCAPE_DICHOTOMY.md`.

**Corollary 4 (intermediate integral escape).**  Under `(ISH18)`,

`Fill_Z,2(w;H_n)->infinity`.                           `(ISH21)`

Therefore no fixed integral filling, and no uniformly bounded sequence of
integral fillings, can explain the finite-stage vanishing of `w`.

Combine this with the degree-`m_n` cover

`Y_(K_n)->Y_(H_n)`,
`K_n=pi^(-1)(L_n)`,
`m_n=|J/(J intersect L_n)|`.                          `(ISH22)`

The exact orthogonal transfer formula gives

`Fill_R(w;K_n)^2`
` =m_n^(-1)Fill_R(w;H_n)^2+epsilon_n^2`,
`epsilon_n<=C`,                                       `(ISH23)`

and the canonical discriminant radii satisfy

`rho_(K_n)(c_(K_n))^2`
` >=m_n^(-1)rho_(H_n)(c_(H_n))^2-C^2`.               `(ISH24)`

Since `(ISH21)` and the downstairs discriminant Pythagoras identity give

`Fill_Z(w;H_n)^2`
` =Fill_R(w;H_n)^2+rho_(H_n)(c_(H_n))^2 ->infinity`,  `(ISH25)`

the finite obstruction is rigorously present in the intermediate tower.
What remains unknown is its rate relative to `sqrt(m_n)` and whether the
surviving discriminant class has an unbounded marked paired ratio.

## 6. Scope and next target

Condition `(ISH18)` is a relative subgroup-separability condition.  It is
not automatic from residual finiteness of `F`.  The constant subgroup in
`(ISH4)` is in fact separable by the finite torus-quotient argument in
`FALSE_CONSTANT_STABILIZER_SEPARABILITY.md`; hence a chain satisfying
`(ISH18)` exists.  For an arbitrary residual chain the intersection is
instead the closure

`B_infinity=intersection_n J L_n`.                    `(ISH26)`

If `B_infinity<=E`, then the first vertex embedding supplies the same split
section used in Section 2, and Theorem 2 applies whenever
`h notin B_infinity Gamma`.  If `B_infinity` has a nontrivial `Q*Q`
component, a more general tower requires a separate argument controlling
the five-term transgression.  Once limiting survival of `w` is proved, one
must still make one of the two terms in `(ISH25)` grow faster than
`sqrt(m_n)` with a nonzero marked pairing.

The theorem here is standalone: the radical word is not merely nonzero in
`(R_0)_ab`; it remains an infinite-order homology class after adjoining its
Kazhdan stabilizer.  This rules out the most immediate possibility that
the orbit-average reduction had silently enlarged the fixed kernel enough
to kill the obstruction.
