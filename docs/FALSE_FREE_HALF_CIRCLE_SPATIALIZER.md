# A free half-circle spatializer inside the untwisted even-edge double

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

The parity spatializer required by the one-vertex FALSE certificate occurs
canonically inside an **untwisted** symmetric reduced amalgam over the even
edge. The construction is exact and uses no finite quotient, transversal,
or matrix calculation.

Let `P_0 subset P subset M` be finite tracial von Neumann algebras. Assume

```text
P=P_0 crossed_beta C_2,
```

where the crossed-product generator `q` is a self-adjoint unitary. Thus

```text
q a q=beta(a),       a in P_0,
q^2=1,
E_(P_0)(q)=0.                                         (FHS1)
```

Let `alpha` be the dual grading automorphism,

```text
alpha|_(P_0)=id,       alpha(q)=-q.                   (FHS2)
```

Form the reduced symmetric amalgam

```text
R=M *_P_0 M.                                          (FHS3)
```

The theorem below constructs a self-adjoint unitary `v in R` satisfying

```text
v p v=alpha(p),       p in P,                         (FHS4)
```

and proves the trace-preserving inclusion

```text
M *_P (P crossed_alpha C_2)  subset  M *_P_0 M.       (FHS5)
```

In the Kun--Thom parity application, the left side is the exact one-vertex
algebra `N_one`. Therefore Connes embeddability of one ordinary symmetric
double over the even edge already proves FALSE.

This does not yet prove that embeddability. The right side carries the
canonical reduced-amalgam trace, whereas Shulman's theorem gives MF of a
full C-star amalgam without proving that this particular trace is an MF
trace. The result nevertheless removes the *twist* from the remaining
algebraic target.

## 2. The free rotation

Write `M_1,M_2` for the two copies of `M` in `(FHS3)`, and let `q_i` be the
copy of `q` in `M_i`. Put

```text
u=q_1 q_2.                                             (FHS6)
```

**Lemma 1.** The unitary `u` commutes with `P_0`, has Haar spectral
distribution, and satisfies

```text
q_1 u q_1=u^*.                                        (FHS7)
```

### Proof

For `a in P_0`, both copies of `q` implement the same involution `beta`.
Consequently

```text
u a u^*
=q_1 q_2 a q_2 q_1
=q_1 beta(a) q_1
=a.                                                    (FHS8)
```

Every nonzero power of `u` is an alternating product of the two centered
syllables `q_1,q_2`. Reduced-amalgam freeness therefore gives

```text
E_(P_0)(u^n)=0,       n != 0.                         (FHS9)
```

Thus the scalar spectral distribution of `u` is Haar measure on the
circle. Finally,

```text
q_1 u q_1=q_2q_1=u^*.                                 (FHS10)
```

This proves the lemma. End proof.

## 3. Half-circle functional calculus

Because `u` has Haar distribution, its spectral projections at `1` and
`-1` vanish. Define in `W^*(u)`

```text
v=sgn(Im(u)).                                          (FHS11)
```

The values at the two zeroes of `Im` are irrelevant. Equivalently, `v` is
`+1` on the open upper semicircle and `-1` on the open lower semicircle.

**Lemma 2.** The element `v` is a self-adjoint unitary, commutes with
`P_0`, and

```text
q_1 v q_1=-v.                                         (FHS12)
```

### Proof

The first assertion follows from `(FHS11)` and the absence of atoms at the
two endpoints. Equation `(FHS8)` implies that every bounded Borel function
of `u` commutes with `P_0`. Conjugation by `q_1` replaces `u` by `u^*`,
and the half-circle sign changes sign under complex conjugation. Hence
`(FHS12)` follows. End proof.

Since `P` is generated by `P_0` and `q_1`, Lemma 2 proves `(FHS4)`.

## 4. The edge crossed product occurs with its canonical trace

Put

```text
B=W^*(P,v) subset R.                                  (FHS13)
```

**Lemma 3.** The assignments which fix `P` and send the canonical
crossed-product involution to `v` give a trace-preserving isomorphism

```text
B isomorphic P crossed_alpha C_2.                     (FHS14)
```

### Proof

The covariance relation follows from `(FHS4)`. It remains only to verify
the crossed-product trace. The half-circle sign has its `L^2` Fourier
series with zero constant coefficient. Every nonzero Fourier monomial
`u^n` is a reduced word containing a syllable from `M_2`, and hence

```text
E_(M_1)(u^n)=0,       n != 0.                         (FHS15)
```

Taking `L^2` limits gives `E_(M_1)(v)=0`. Since the expectation onto
`M_1` is `M_1`-bimodular,

```text
tau_R(pv)=0,       p in P.                            (FHS16)
```

Thus the covariant map from `P crossed_alpha C_2` to `B` preserves the
canonical faithful trace. It is therefore injective, and it is onto by
definition. End proof.

## 5. Freeness over the larger edge

Let

```text
C=W^*(P_1,P_2) subset R.                              (FHS17)
```

Reduced-word associativity gives

```text
C isomorphic P *_P_0 P,
W^*(M_1,C) isomorphic M_1 *_P C subset R.             (FHS18)
```

The subalgebra qualification is essential: `M_1` and `C` need not generate
the part of `M_2` outside `P_2`. For completeness, `(FHS18)` can be checked
directly. A word
alternating between `M_1 minus P` and `C minus P` expands into a sum of
words alternating between `M_1 minus P_0` and `M_2 minus P_0`. The
`P`-centering condition remains sufficient after adjacent `P` syllables
are absorbed, because the trace-preserving expectation `M_1 -> P` is
`P`-bimodular. Every resulting reduced word has zero expectation.

The algebra `B` from `(FHS13)` is a subalgebra of `C`, contains `P`, and
the expectation `C -> P` restricts to the canonical expectation `B -> P`
by Lemma 3. Hence `M_1` and `B` are free with amalgamation over `P` inside
`R`. The faithful reduced-amalgam universal property now gives

```text
W^*(M_1,B)
 isomorphic M *_P (P crossed_alpha C_2),              (FHS19)
```

which is the inclusion `(FHS5)`.

## 6. Application to the explicit parity edge

Use the notation of
`FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_GROUP_CORNER_AND_COVER.md`. Let

```text
M=pL(J),
P=pL(K),
K_0=ker(epsilon),
P_0=pL(K_0).                                          (FHS20)
```

Choose an odd lamp involution `a in K` and put `q=pu_a`. Then `K_0` is
normal of index two in `K`, `q^2=p`, and, in the unital corner `pL(K)`,

```text
P=P_0 crossed Ad(q) C_2.                              (FHS21)
```

The automorphism `alpha` in `(FHS2)` is exactly the odd-orbit parity
automorphism from the one-vertex certificate. The theorem therefore gives

```text
N_one
 subset R_even:=pL(J) *_pL(K_0) pL(J).                (FHS22)
```

The group-von-Neumann normal-form identity identifies

```text
R_even isomorphic pL(J *_K_0 J).                      (FHS23)
```

Combining `(FHS22)` with the one-vertex certificate yields:

**Corollary 4 (untwisted even-edge endpoint).** If `R_even` is Connes
embeddable, then there exists a hyperlinear nonsofic group.

The group `J *_K_0 J` is already weak/operator-norm MF by the regular-MF
symmetric-double theorem. What remains is exactly trace selection:
prove that its canonical reduced trace, or merely a trace on `(FHS22)`
retaining the scalar anticommutation, is Connes embeddable. The Borel
half-circle unitary is why the construction exists in the reduced von
Neumann amalgam; its discontinuity at `+/-1` is also why full C-star MF
permanence alone does not yet provide it in a norm-matrix model.

## 7. Scope

The theorem proves three things which were previously separate:

1. the parity twist has an exact spatializer in an untwisted symmetric
   double;
2. the one-sided twisted target embeds trace preservingly in that ordinary
   double; and
3. the only remaining obstruction is passage from Shulman's operator-norm
   MF model to a trace which supports the half-circle functional calculus.

It does **not** prove Connes embeddability of `R_even`. Claiming that from
MF of the corresponding full C-star amalgam would repeat the trace-lifting
error audited in `FALSE_SHULMAN_TRACE_AUDIT.md`.
