# FALSE lane: amenable negative sectors and their exact obstructions

Date: 2026-08-10

This note isolates the strongest trace-rounding version of the central-radical
strategy and proves two no-go results for the most natural ways of realizing
it.  It complements `FALSE_MACKEY_SECTOR.md`.

Throughout, let `J` be countable and let

`1 != z in Z(J) intersect Rad_sof(J)`.                         `(ANS1)`

## 1. An amenable negative sector would solve the main problem

Recall that a unitary representation `pi:J->U(H)` is amenable if there is a
state on `B(H)` invariant under conjugation by `pi(J)`.

**Theorem (amenable negative-sector criterion).**  Suppose that `J` has an
amenable unitary representation `pi` for which

`pi(z)=omega 1_H`, `omega in T minus {1}`.                     `(ANS2)`

Then a hyperlinear nonsofic group exists.

**Proof.**  Restrict a conjugation-invariant state on `B(H)` to
`A=C^*(pi(J))`.  The restriction `tau` is a tracial state: invariance under
the generating unitaries gives `tau(pi(g)a)=tau(a pi(g))`, first for a
generator and then on the norm-dense group algebra.  The extending central
state makes `tau` an amenable trace.

The matrix characterization of amenable traces supplies unital completely
positive maps

`phi_n:A->M_(d_n)`                                             `(ANS3)`

which are asymptotically multiplicative in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm
and recover `tau`.  Polar-correcting `phi_n(pi(g))` changes it by `o(1)` in
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm, so these maps give a homomorphism from a
quotient of `pi(J)` to a tracial matrix ultraproduct.  Because unital linear
maps preserve scalars, `(ANS2)` gives

`phi_n(pi(z))=omega 1_(d_n)`                                  `(ANS4)`

exactly.  Thus `z` survives in the hyperlinear image.  That image cannot be
sofic, since its quotient map from `J` would then have to kill every element
of `Rad_sof(J)`, including `z`.  End proof.

This criterion is the representation-theoretic core behind the tempting
Paddock/Folner route.  Unlike Paddock's finitely presented rounding lemma, it
does not require a self-adjoint-unitary presentation: amenable-trace
microstates and polar correction suffice.

## 2. The exact Mackey algebra has no amenable trace

Use the notation of `FALSE_MACKEY_SECTOR.md`.  Thus

`G=H*Z`, `C=<r>`,

and `A_Mac` is generated by the two Mackey-paired vertex representations.
Each vertex algebra is a faithful copy of `C_r^*(G)`.

**Theorem (global amenable-trace obstruction).**

`A_Mac has no amenable tracial state.`                          `(ANS5)`

**Proof.**  The group `H` is nonsofic and hence nonamenable.  Therefore
`G=H*Z` is nonamenable.  It is a Powers free-product group, so
`C_r^*(G)` has a unique tracial state, namely its canonical group trace.

Suppose `tau` were an amenable trace on `A_Mac`.  Amenability of a trace
passes to unital C-star subalgebras, so its restriction to either vertex
copy of `C_r^*(G)` would be amenable.  Uniqueness forces that restriction
to be the canonical trace.  But the canonical trace on `C_r^*(G)` is
amenable only when `G` is amenable (equivalently, the regular
representation admits a hypertrace).  This contradicts nonamenability of
`G`.  End proof.

This strengthens `(MPS18)--(MPS20)`.  The quasi-regular realization does not
merely lack a convenient approximately central sequence of density
matrices.  No choice of trace on its generated C-star algebra can satisfy
the amenable-trace hypothesis.  Any successful MF proof for `A_Mac` must
therefore use a genuinely nonamenable but Connes-embeddable trace, as happens
for the canonical trace of a nonamenable hyperlinear group.

## 3. Coamenable induction cannot detect the radical sign

There is a general obstruction, proved in full in
`FALSE_COAMENABLE_ACTION_CRITERION.md`.  If `L<J` is coamenable and
`chi:L->T` satisfies `chi(z)!=1`, then `ker(chi)` is coamenable in `J` and
omits `z`.  The amenable action on `J/ker(chi)` makes `z` fixed-point-free.
Completing its restrictions to Følner sets produces a homomorphism to a
metric ultraproduct of symmetric groups in which `z` survives.  This
contradicts `(ANS1)`.  Thus no coamenable subgroup, normal or not, has a
character detecting `z`.

In the normal case there is the following stronger algebraic conclusion.

There is a separate purely algebraic obstruction to inducing `(ANS2)` from
a character.

**Theorem (normal coamenable commutator obstruction).**  Let `L normal J`
and suppose `J/L` is amenable.  Under `(ANS1)`,

`z in [L,L]`.                                                   `(ANS6)`

**Proof.**  Let `chi:L->T` be any unitary character.  Since `L` is normal,
the induced representation `Ind_L^J(chi)` has image in the monomial group

`T^(J/L) semidirect (J/L)`.                                   `(ANS7)`

As an abstract group this is abelian-by-amenable and hence amenable, so its
image is sofic.  Centrality of `z` makes its induced operator the scalar
`chi(z)`: every conjugate character has the same value on `z`.  Since
`z in Rad_sof(J)`, the induced homomorphism must kill `z`, and consequently
`chi(z)=1`.

Thus every character of `L` kills `z`.  Circle-valued characters separate
points of every abelian group, so the image of `z` in `L_ab` is zero.  This
is `(ANS6)`.  End proof.

Consequently no coamenable character induction can supply the desired sign
sector.  Moreover, `(CAP1)--(CAP13)` in
`FALSE_COAMENABLE_ACTION_CRITERION.md` proves that every invariant-mean
permutation character is already co-sofic.  A viable amenable negative sector
therefore has to be genuinely nonpermutational, and hence nonmonomial.

## 4. Updated live boundary

The following routes are now sharply separated.

1. `A_Mac` being MF remains sufficient, but its witnessing corona trace
   must be nonamenable while remaining Connes embeddable.
2. An amenable representation satisfying `(ANS2)` would solve the problem
   directly, but it cannot arise from an amenable trace on `A_Mac`.
3. Character induction from every coamenable subgroup is impossible by the
   Følner-truncation obstruction `(CAF1)--(CAF13)`; `(ANS6)` strengthens this
   algebraically for normal kernels of amenable quotients.
4. The remaining representation-theoretic possibilities are a genuinely
   nonpermutational amenable negative sector or a direct construction of a
   nonamenable embeddable trace.

No statement in this note proves that such a sector or trace exists.
