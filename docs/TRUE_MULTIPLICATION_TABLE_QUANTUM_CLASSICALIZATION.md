# Full multiplication coherence classicalizes a quantum transport

Date: 2026-08-14

## 1. Outcome

The pairing-table data in the atlas admit nonclassical quantum symmetries,
but a **full finite group multiplication table does not**.  A theorem of
Kasprzak--Sołtan--Woronowicz says that the quantum automorphism group of every
finite quantum group is classical.  Applied to an ordinary finite group, it
turns a multiplication-coherent magic transport into a classical random
automorphism.

This gives a new exact endpoint for the missing whole-map PVM.  It is enough
to recover one magic transition matrix which respects the complete finite
multiplication table.  Its coefficients then commute automatically and
assemble into a PVM indexed by ordinary automorphisms.  No separate
``quantum symmetry is classical'' hypothesis is needed.

The theorem does **not** yet extract that magic matrix from an atlas
Hilbert--Schmidt microstate.  For quotient sizes tending to infinity, an
additional uniform approximate version is also required unless the table
relations are exactified coordinatewise.

## 2. The exact coefficient relations

Let `Q` be a finite group and let `C` be a unital C-star algebra.  A magic
unitary

```text
u=(u_(a,x))_(a,x in Q)                                (MQC1)
```

has projection entries, with every row and every column an orthogonal
partition of one.  It defines a coaction on `C(Q)` by

```text
alpha(delta_x)=sum_a delta_a tensor u_(a,x).           (MQC2)
```

Compatibility with the group coproduct

```text
Delta(delta_x)=sum_(y z=x) delta_y tensor delta_z       (MQC3)
```

is the finite family of multiplication-table identities

```text
u_(b c,x)=sum_(y z=x) u_(b,y) u_(c,z)                 (MQC4)
```

for `b,c,x in Q`.  The universal C-star algebra generated by `(MQC1)` and
`(MQC4)` is the function algebra of the quantum automorphism group of the
finite group `Q`.

Kasprzak--Sołtan--Woronowicz prove that this universal algebra is
commutative.  Therefore, in every concrete representation of `(MQC1)--(MQC4)`,

```text
[u_(a,x),u_(b,y)]=0                                   (MQC5)
```

for all four indices.  This is the point at which the full multiplication
table differs from a Hadamard or perfect-pairing table: the latter can have a
genuine noncommutative universal quantum symmetry algebra.

## 3. The whole-map PVM is explicit

Once `(MQC5)` holds, define for every set map `phi:Q->Q`

```text
P_phi=product_(x in Q) u_(phi(x),x).                  (MQC6)
```

The commuting magic-unitary relations make the nonzero `(P_phi)` pairwise
orthogonal and make their sum one.  Row orthogonality kills every
noninjective `phi`; finiteness then makes every surviving map bijective.
Equation `(MQC4)` kills every bijection which does not preserve
multiplication.  Hence

```text
P_phi!=0  =>  phi in Aut(Q).                          (MQC7)
```

Moreover

```text
u_(a,x)=sum_(phi in Aut(Q), phi(x)=a) P_phi.           (MQC8)
```

Thus `(P_phi)_(phi in Aut(Q))` is exactly the whole-response-map PVM needed
by `TRUE_EDGE_PVM_LOCALITY_CRITERION.md`.  In a tracial representation its
traces give an ordinary probability distribution on `Aut(Q)`, and `(MQC8)`
recovers every one-setting marginal from that same latent automorphism.

For four path vertices `L0--R0--L1--R1`, it is enough to obtain one such
whole-map PVM at each vertex and the already targeted edgewise commutation.
The tree-coupling theorem then supplies the common law on the four maps; no
unused-edge commutation is needed.

## 4. Fixed-table asymptotic corollary

Fix `Q`.  Suppose matrices `u^(n)_(a,x)` satisfy the magic-unitary and Hopf
relations with normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt defects tending to zero.  Passing
to the tracial matrix ultraproduct gives an exact representation of
`(MQC1)--(MQC4)`.  The theorem therefore gives

```text
||[u^(n)_(a,x),u^(n)_(b,y)]||_2 -> 0                 (MQC9)
```

for every fixed four indices.  Since the table is finite, standard finite
PVM rounding then produces an asymptotic whole-map law.  This qualitative
corollary is dimension independent in the matrix dimension.

If `|Q_n|` grows with `n`, `(MQC9)` cannot be invoked by fixing one universal
algebra.  There are two valid ways to cross that quantifier boundary:

1. exactify the full `Q_n` table at each coordinate and apply `(MQC5)`
   coordinatewise; or
2. prove a size-uniform averaged estimate converting the total Hopf defect
   into total coefficient-commutator energy.

The multiplicative-unitary proof in the cited paper is a promising source
for (2): its key identity makes two tensor-leg copies commute, and all maps
involved are unitary or trace-preserving.  The required estimate has now been
written down in `TRUE_UNIFORM_MULTIPLICATIVE_UNITARY_CLASSICALIZATION.md`.
For exact primal and dual table homomorphisms with compatibility defect
`epsilon`, the averaged coefficient commutator is bounded by
`81 epsilon^2`, uniformly in `|Q|`.  The remaining issue is construction of
those two exact coordinate homomorphisms from the atlas root packets.

## 5. Consequence for the atlas endpoint

The earlier endpoint asked for a deterministic multiplication-coherent
decoder on every character atom.  The present theorem supplies a more
operator-native sufficient condition:

> Recover, for each transport vertex, a magic transition matrix on the
> finite paired quotient which satisfies the full additive/multiplication
> Hopf table, with exact coordinate relations or a size-uniform total-error
> estimate.

Classicality and the whole-map PVM then follow automatically from
`(MQC5)--(MQC8)`.  After edgewise commutation, tree gluing and the existing
robust `1/36` paired-transport floor finish the finite contradiction.

This does not reduce to the false pairing-only shortcut.  The Walsh--Hadamard
matrix remembers only one bicharacter.  Equation `(MQC4)` remembers every
product and its compatibility with one common quantum bijection.  Recovering
that full table from the cross-root Steinberg relations is the remaining
matrix-coordinate theorem.
