# A fermionic central extension is the sharp noncommutative-fiber target

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

**Synchronization note.**  This is not a second endpoint independent of
`PERFECT_CORE_KUN_THOM_WREATH.md`.  If

```text
N_even=A_CAR crossed_product G,
```

then, for any fixed odd Majorana `C_o`,

```text
M_Cl = N_even crossed_product_(Ad C_o) C_2.
```

Indeed `C_o` normalizes `N_even`, since
`C_o u_g C_o=(C_o C_(g o))u_g` and the Clifford coefficient is even; parity
gives the reverse grading and the required conditional expectation.
Therefore `M_Cl` is Connes embeddable iff `N_even` is: one implication is
inheritance by subalgebras, and the other follows from the standard
embedding of a finite crossed product into `M_2(N_even)`.  The present note
is retained because it displays the relative-commutant wall directly, while
the perfect-core note is the authoritative group-theoretic endpoint.

Let `Gamma<G` be one of the Kun--Thom Kazhdan infranormal pairs and put

```text
X=G/Gamma.
```

There is a canonical locally finite central extension of the lamp group,
generated by a central involution `z` and involutions `c_x`, `x in X`, with

```text
c_x c_y = z c_y c_x       for x!=y.                 (FKE1)
```

The action of `G` permuting the `c_x` gives a group

```text
Wtilde=Vtilde semidirect G.                           (FKE2)
```

Its central quotient is the Kun--Thom generalized wreath group:

```text
Wtilde/<z> = (directSum_X C_2) semidirect G = W.      (FKE3)
```

Consequently `Wtilde` is nonsofic: soficity passes to quotients by finite
normal subgroups, whereas Kun--Thom prove that `W` is nonsofic.

On the other hand, the central character `z |-> -1` turns `(FKE1)` into the
Clifford relations.  The corresponding tracial von Neumann algebra is

```text
M_Cl=Cl(X) crossed_product G,                          (FKE4)
```

where `Cl(X)` is the hyperfinite infinite Clifford algebra with its canonical
trace and `G` acts by permuting the Majorana generators.  The group `Wtilde`
embeds faithfully into `U(M_Cl)` by

```text
z |-> -1,       c_x |-> the Majorana at x,
g |-> u_g.                                             (FKE5)
```

It follows that

```text
M_Cl Connes embeddable
    ==> Wtilde hyperlinear and nonsofic.               (FKE6)
```

Thus `(FKE4)` is a single, natural, genuinely matrix-valued endpoint for
the FALSE program.  It implements exactly the “noncommutative fibers” left
open by the scalar and permutation no-go theorems.  No additional
nonsoficity argument or sofic-radical quotient is required.

This note does not prove the missing CE assertion in `(FKE6)`.  It identifies
a stronger target than the commuting Bernoulli algebra: anticommutation
supplies canonical high-multiplicity Clifford blocks and retains the marked
wall with maximal trace energy.

The tempting operator-norm MF route to `(FKE6)` is closed.  Cutting a
norm-matrix representation to the central `z=-1` sector removes relative
rank blindness, but property `(T)`, stable finiteness, and the strict
compressor imply that this sector must be zero.  See
`FALSE_CLIFFORD_NORM_MF_STERILITY.md` for the self-contained proof.

## 2. The locally finite Clifford lamp group

Define `Vtilde` by the presentation

```text
z^2=1,       c_x^2=1,       [z,c_x]=1,
[c_x,c_y]=z                 for x!=y.                 (FKE7)
```

Every finitely generated subgroup is finite.  Indeed, after choosing an
order on a finite set `F subset X`, every element generated by
`z,{c_x:x in F}` has a normal form

```text
z^epsilon product_(x in F) c_x^(epsilon_x),           (FKE8)
```

so that subgroup has at most `2^(|F|+1)` elements.  The normal forms are
distinct, for example by their images in the finite Clifford algebra after
mapping `z` to `-1`.  Thus `Vtilde` is locally finite and amenable.

Every permutation of `X` preserves `(FKE7)`.  In particular the action
`G curvearrowright G/Gamma` gives the semidirect product `(FKE2)`.  Setting
`z=1` makes the `c_x` commuting involutions and proves `(FKE3)`.

## 3. The tracial Clifford representation is faithful

Let `Cl(X)` be generated by self-adjoint unitaries `C_x`, `x in X`, with

```text
C_x C_y=-C_y C_x       for x!=y.                      (FKE9)
```

The finite-subset algebras are the usual finite complex Clifford algebras;
their trace-compatible inductive limit is the hyperfinite tracial Clifford
algebra.  Permuting the generators defines trace-preserving automorphisms,
so `(FKE4)` is a finite tracial crossed product.

The assignments in `(FKE5)` obey all relations.  They are injective on the
lamp group because the Clifford monomials are linearly independent.  They
remain injective after adjoining `G`: Fourier coefficients in the crossed
product separate terms with different group coordinate.  Hence `(FKE5)`
is a faithful homomorphism.

If `(FKE4)` embeds into a tracial matrix ultraproduct, its countable unitary
subgroup `Wtilde` does as well.  This proves the hyperlinear half of `(FKE6)`.
The nonsofic half follows from `(FKE3)` and the Kun--Thom theorem.

## 4. The exact relative wall

Let `x_0=Gamma in X` and put

```text
v=C_(x_0).
```

The root is fixed by `Gamma`, so

```text
v in {u_gamma:gamma in Gamma}'.                       (FKE10)
```

For `h notin Gamma`, the points `x_0` and `h x_0` are distinct and the two
Majoranas are orthogonal in `L2(Cl(X))`.  Therefore

```text
||[v,u_h]||_2^2
 = ||v-C_(h x_0)||_2^2
 = 2.                                                  (FKE11)
```

Thus the relative-bicommutant escape is exact and has positive normalized
trace before any limiting argument.  Unlike the commuting Bernoulli model,
the conjugate root fibers anticommute; this is the new degree of freedom.

## 5. Why CE is not automatic

One cannot prove CE of `(FKE4)` by simply truncating the exponent cone.
For the finite-field pair in `FALSE_POSITIVE_CORE_IS_SOFIC.md`, suppose
finite subsets `Omega_n subset N^d minus {0}` were asymptotically invariant
under a finite positive generating set for `SL_d(Z)`.  The normalized
vectors

```text
|Omega_n|^(-1/2) 1_(Omega_n) in ell2(Z^d minus {0})
```

would be almost invariant under `SL_d(Z)`.  Property `(T)` would produce a
nonzero invariant vector.  But every nonzero orbit in `Z^d` is infinite, so
`ell2(Z^d minus {0})` has no invariant vector.  This is impossible.

There is also a specifically fermionic amplification issue.  A defect on a
fixed number of one-particle modes acts on a fixed Clifford tensor factor;
adding untouched modes does not dilute its normalized Hilbert--Schmidt
norm.  Therefore an approximate one-particle action with a bounded boundary
does not automatically give an asymptotic representation after second
quantization.

The live construction must instead use block-direct sums of matrix-valued
charts, with the bad Clifford blocks occupying vanishing normalized trace,
or establish CE of the quasi-free action by a different operator-algebraic
argument.  This is precisely the positive-density/nonliftable-holonomy gate,
now expressed in a canonical algebra rather than an ad hoc phase table.

## 6. Finite target

A successful finite model of `(FKE4)` consists of:

1. almost-representing unitaries for a fixed finite window of `G`;
2. self-adjoint unitary Clifford blocks for the required cosets;
3. approximate covariance `U_g C_x U_g^*=C_(gx)`;
4. multiplicative defect tending to zero in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt
   norm; and
5. the root statistic `(FKE11)` bounded away from zero.

Conditions 2 and 5 are exact in every finite Clifford block.  Conditions 1,
3, and 4 are the only remaining compatibility problem.  The sofic positive
core from `FALSE_POSITIVE_CORE_IS_SOFIC.md` supplies these conditions on the
entire positive semigroup sector; what remains is the non-Ore simultaneous
extension across inverse external words.
