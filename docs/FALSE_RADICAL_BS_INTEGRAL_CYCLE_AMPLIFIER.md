## 1. Outcome

The mod-`p` finite-cycle transgression in
`FALSE_RADICAL_BS_FINITE_CYCLE_TRANSGRESSION.md` is the reduction of an
integral class of much larger order.

Fix an odd prime `p`, put `q=p+1`, and let

`B=U semidirect <c> ~= BS(1,q)`, `U=Z[1/q]`,             `(ICA1)`

where `c u c^(-1)=q u`.  Let `X_M` be the transitive `B`-set on which
`U` acts trivially and `c` is one cycle of length `M`, and put

`I_M=Aug_Z(X_M)`.                                        `(ICA2)`

Then

`H_1(B,I_M) ~= Z/N_M Z`,
`N_M=(q^M-1)/(q-1)`.                                    `(ICA3)`

The explicit integral one-cycle obtained from the paired bar chain is a
generator of `(ICA3)`.  Consequently, when `M=p^k`, its `p`-primary order
is exactly `p^k`.  Its reduction modulo `p` is precisely the nonzero
augmentation transgression already found in the finite-cycle theorem.

The relevant `p`-primary character has a completely explicit clock model.  On
`C^(p^k)`, the translation generator has eigenvalues

`exp(2 pi i q^j/p^(k+1))`, `0<=j<p^k`,                   `(ICA4)`

and the stable letter cyclically permutes them.  The distinguished adjacent
pair has quotient `exp(-2 pi i/p^k)`, while the translation matrix has
normalized trace zero.  This is an exact microscopic-to-macroscopic
identity at one distinguished coordinate.  It is not an averaged
amplification statement: after translation around the orbit, the adjacent
quotients are equidistributed in a subgroup and have normalized squared
chord energy `2` for `k>=2`.

Use two exact `B`-sectors with translation matrices

`H_k^(1)=H_k`, `H_k^(2)=H_k^q`.                         `(ICA4a)`

Both use the stable-letter matrix `C_k` and satisfy the Baumslag--Solitar
relation exactly.  Their fold word is

`H_k^(2)(H_k^(1))^(-1)=H_k^p`,                         `(ICA4b)`

whose normalized squared displacement is `2` for `k>=2`.  Thus the frozen
clock block is an exact local low-defect/high-fold block.  The remaining
gate is genuinely global: extend these two sectors to approximate models
of the two copies of `G` which asymptotically agree on `Gamma`.

## 2. The LHS calculation

Index `X_M` by `Z/MZ`, writing `e_j` for its standard basis and choosing
right-module conventions

`e_j c=e_(j+1)`.                                        `(ICA5)`

The subgroup `U` acts trivially.  Since `I_M` is free abelian,

`H_1(U,I_M)=I_M tensor_Z U=I_M tensor Z[1/q]`.           `(ICA6)`

For the extension

`1->U->B->Z->1`,                                       `(ICA7)`

the only other possible total-degree-one term is

`H_1(Z,I_M)=ker(c-1:I_M->I_M)=0`.                      `(ICA8)`

Indeed the invariant vectors of the cyclic permutation on `Z^M` are
constant, and a constant integral vector in the augmentation lattice is
zero.  The homological action of `c` on `(ICA6)` combines cyclic shift on
`I_M` with conjugation on `U`.  Up to multiplication by the unit `q`, its
coinvariant operator is

`S-q`,                                                  `(ICA9)`

where `S e_j=e_(j+1)`.  The LHS spectral sequence therefore gives a
canonical isomorphism

`H_1(B,I_M) ~= coker(S-q:I_M tensor Z[1/q]
                              ->I_M tensor Z[1/q]).`    `(ICA10)`

Replacing `S-q` by `qS-1`, which corresponds to the opposite cyclic
orientation, gives the same group and only changes the displayed
generator by a unit.

## 3. Smith form on the augmentation lattice

Put `A=Z[1/q]`.  On the full permutation lattice `A^M`, the relations

`e_(j+1)=q e_j`                                         `(ICA11)`

identify the cokernel of `S-q` with

`A/(q^M-1)A`.                                          `(ICA12)`

The augmentation map intertwines `S-q` with multiplication by `1-q` on
`A`.  Since both vertical kernels vanish, the snake lemma identifies
`(ICA10)` with the kernel of

`A/(q^M-1)A -> A/(q-1)A`.                             `(ICA13)`

That kernel is the principal subgroup generated by `q-1`.  Since

`q^M-1=(q-1)N_M`,
`N_M=1+q+...+q^(M-1)`,                                 `(ICA14)`

division by `q-1` gives

`H_1(B,I_M) ~= A/N_M A`.                              `(ICA15)`

Moreover `gcd(q,N_M)=gcd(q,M)`.  In the case used below,
`M=p^k` and `q=p+1`, this gcd is one.  Localization at `q` therefore does
not alter the finite cyclic group, and `(ICA15)` becomes exactly `(ICA3)`.

## 4. The paired transgression is the integral generator

Let

`Z_p=sum_(j=1)^p[h^j|h]`
`    -[c|h]-[ch|c^(-1)]+[c^(-1)|c]`                   `(ICA16)`

be the paired bar chain from `(RB18)`, and take the coefficient `e_0`.
Because `h` acts trivially on `X_M`, its integral boundary is

`partial(e_0 Z_p)=p e_0[h]+Y_M`,                       `(ICA17)`

where

`Y_M=(e_0-e_1)([h]+[c^(-1)])+(e_(-1)-e_0)[c]`.        `(ICA18)`

The chain `Y_M` has coefficients in `I_M` and is an integral one-cycle.
Its image under the edge homomorphism from the LHS filtration to `(ICA10)`
is

`(e_0-e_1) tensor 1`.                                 `(ICA19)`

Under `(ICA11)--(ICA15)`,

`e_0-e_1=(1-q)e_0=-(q-1)e_0`.                         `(ICA20)`

After dividing the kernel in `(ICA13)` by its generator `q-1`, this is a
unit generator of `A/N_MA`.  Thus:

**Theorem 1 (integral finite-cycle transgression).**
The class `[Y_M]` generates `H_1(B,I_M)`, which is cyclic of order `N_M`
when `gcd(q,M)=1`.

Reducing `(ICA17)` modulo `p` recovers exactly the cycle `(FCT14)`.  Hence
the nonzero mod-`p` transgression in Theorem 1 of
`FALSE_RADICAL_BS_FINITE_CYCLE_TRANSGRESSION.md` is the reduction of the
integral generator `[Y_M]`, rather than an isolated order-`p` class with no
higher-depth lift.

## 5. Exact p-primary growth

Now take `M=p^k`.  The lifting-the-exponent formula for odd `p` gives

`v_p(q^M-1)=v_p(q-1)+v_p(M)=k+1`.                    `(ICA21)`

Since `v_p(q-1)=1`, equation `(ICA14)` yields

`v_p(N_M)=k`.                                         `(ICA22)`

Combining Theorem 1 with `(ICA22)` proves:

**Corollary 2 (unbounded integral order).**
For `M=p^k`, the `p`-primary component of `[Y_M]` has exact order `p^k`.
In particular the finite-cycle class has compatible lifts through every
`p`-power depth.

The statement is stronger than the mod-`p` dichotomy `p|M`: it identifies
the full growing primary tower and not only its first nonzero layer.

## 6. The explicit clock dual

Put `M=p^k` and

`omega_k=exp(2 pi i/p^(k+1))`.                         `(ICA23)`

On the basis `xi_0,...,xi_(M-1)` of `C^M`, define

`H_k xi_j=omega_k^(q^j) xi_j`,
`C_k xi_j=xi_(j-1)`,                                   `(ICA24)`

with indices modulo `M`.  Equation `(ICA21)` implies

`q^M=1 mod p^(k+1)`,                                  `(ICA25)`

so `(ICA24)` is periodic.  Directly,

`C_k H_k C_k^(-1)=H_k^q`.                             `(ICA26)`

Thus `h |-> H_k`, `c |-> C_k` is an exact unitary representation of
`BS(1,q)`.

The associated character on the augmentation lattice has coordinate
values

`lambda_k(e_j)=q^j/p^(k+1) mod Z`.                    `(ICA27)`

It annihilates `(S-q)I_M`, and on the generator `(ICA19)` it gives

`lambda_k(e_0-e_1)`
` =(1-q)/p^(k+1)=-1/p^k mod Z`.                       `(ICA28)`

Consequently it detects the exact `p^k`-primary order in Corollary 2.

Finally, for odd `p`, the element `1+p` generates the cyclic group

`1+pZ / p^(k+1)Z`,                                    `(ICA29)`

of order `p^k`.  Therefore the exponents `q^j`, `0<=j<p^k`, run once
through all residues `1+p a`, `a in Z/p^kZ`.  Equation `(ICA24)` gives

`tr_M(H_k)=0`,
`||H_k-I||_(2,normalized)^2=2`.                       `(ICA30)`

At the distinguished coordinate, the phase increment in `(ICA28)` has
chordal size

`|exp(2 pi i/p^k)-1|=O(p^(-k)).`                      `(ICA31)`

Equations `(ICA30)--(ICA31)` compare one distinguished increment with the
global displacement.  They must not be read as a normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt estimate for all translated increments.  In fact, if

`delta_j=exp(-2 pi i q^j/p^k)`,

then

`(1/p^k) sum_j |delta_j-1|^2=2`                       `(ICA32)`

for every `k>=2`; see `FALSE_RADICAL_BS_CLOCK_AVERAGE_NO_GO.md`.

Since `delta_j` is the complex conjugate of the `j`th eigenvalue of
`H_k^p`, equation `(ICA32)` is equivalently

`||H_k^p-I||_(2,normalized)^2=2`.                     `(ICA33)`

This is the macroscopic displacement of the fold between the exact local
sectors `H_k` and `H_k^q`.

## 7. What this closes and what remains

This note completes the integral one-orbit calculation requested in Section
6 of `FALSE_RADICAL_BS_FINITE_CYCLE_TRANSGRESSION.md`:

1. the integral obstruction group is cyclic and explicit;
2. the marked class is its generator;
3. its primary order on the `p^k` cycle is exactly `p^k`; and
4. the `p`-primary clock representation has one microscopic distinguished
   increment and fixed normalized translation displacement.

The exact `B` relation has zero defect, and `(ICA33)` supplies the desired
macroscopic fold displacement.  What is not supplied is an estimate for the
additional relations which identify the two copies on `Gamma` and extend
the clock through the square-zero elementary module and the `Q times L`
action.  A complete FALSE proof must therefore solve exactly the extension
problem suggested by the frozen-clock experiment:

> keep the two exact sectors `H_k,H_k^q` fixed, extend each to the finite
> square-zero relative module, and make every fixed polynomial elementary,
> `Q`-covariance, external-`L`, and amalgam relation have vanishing
> normalized defect.

The average calculation rules out counting `(ICA28)` itself as a small
orbitwise gluing error.  It does not rule out the frozen exact clock block;
instead it proves that this block already has the required local witness.
