# The projective stabilizers are not uniformly rationally 2-acyclic

Date: 2026-08-11

## Purpose

The exact projective `p=3` calculation gives

`H_2(H_3,Z) ~= C_2^3`, hence `H_2(H_3,Q)=0`,             `(PSH1)`

for the stabilizer `H_3` of a point in the selected action on
`P^2(F_3)`.  Combined with Proposition 12 of
`INTEGRAL_CARRY_DISCRIMINANT_DUALITY.md`, this removes real
stabilizer-homology denominator collapse on the first projective chart.

This note records a necessary correction of scope: `(PSH1)` cannot hold
uniformly over the projective prime family.  The obstruction is already
present in the classical cuspidal cohomology of prime-level congruence
subgroups of `SL_3(Z)`.

## Identification of the stabilizer

Let

`G=SL_3(Z)`

act on `P^2(F_p)` by reduction modulo `p`, and let `H_p` be the stabilizer of
the line generated by the first standard basis vector.  Then

`H_p={g=(g_ij) in SL_3(Z):g_21=g_31=0 mod p}`.          `(PSH2)`

This is exactly the prime-level subgroup conventionally denoted
`Gamma_0(3,p)`.  Choosing another projective point merely conjugates the
stabilizer.  Thus the homology quotient in the cover Hopf sequence for the
selected projective chart is

`K_p/Sigma_p ~= H_2(Gamma_0(3,p),Z)`.                 `(PSH3)`

## Nonvanishing at other prime levels

**Proposition 1.**  Rational 2-acyclicity fails in the projective family.
In particular,

`H_2(H_p,Q) != 0`                                    `(PSH4)`

at every prime level `p` for which
`H_cusp^3(Gamma_0(3,p),C)` is nonzero.  Published exact computations give,
among others,

`p=53,61,79,89`.                                     `(PSH5)`

**Proof.**  The locally symmetric space for `SL_3(R)` has real dimension
five.  Let `M_p` be its Borel--Serre compactification at level
`Gamma_0(3,p)`.  Cuspidal cohomology lies in interior cohomology,

`H_cusp^3(M_p,C) subset`
` im(H_c^3(M_p,C) -> H^3(M_p,C))`.                   `(PSH6)`

Therefore nonzero cuspidal `H^3` implies nonzero compactly supported
`H_c^3`.  Poincare--Lefschetz duality for this five-dimensional arithmetic
orbifold gives

`H_c^3(M_p,C) ~= H_(5-3)(M_p,C)=H_2(M_p,C)`.         `(PSH7)`

The latter is group homology `H_2(Gamma_0(3,p),C)`.  Equations
`(PSH2)` and `(PSH7)` prove `(PSH4)`.  Ash--Yasaki report nonzero
prime-level cuspidal degree-three cohomology at the four levels in `(PSH5)`
(and at further levels); this supplies the stated examples.  End proof.

The same conclusion is visible in Porat's direct prime-level computation:
his subgroup is defined by `(PSH2)`, and his cuspidal classes live in
degree three of the Borel--Serre compactification.

## Exact consequence for the carry program

Proposition 12 of `INTEGRAL_CARRY_DISCRIMINANT_DUALITY.md` says that a dual
cycle shorter than the inverse norm of every bounded spherical template is
orthogonal to the spherical lattice and hence lies in the harmonic
representative of

`H_2(H_p,R)`.                                         `(PSH8)`

At `p=3`, `(PSH1)` makes this harmonic space zero, so every nonzero dual
discriminant class has a uniform positive denominator radius on that chart.
Proposition 1 proves that this last implication cannot be repeated for all
`p`: the harmonic space is genuinely nonzero at the levels `(PSH5)`.

This does **not** imply that `Theta_p` diverges.  Nonzero rational homology
only supplies a location in which short dual cycles may occur; it does not
bound their Euclidean length, their integral covolume, or the glued range
radius in the numerator of the discriminant formula.  The surviving uniform
problem is the metric one:

1. split the primitive cycle lattice into its bounded spherical part and
   the harmonic lattice representing `H_2(H_p,Z)/torsion`;
2. prove a uniform lower bound for nonzero vectors in the dual harmonic
   lattice, or directly control their paired discriminant-radius ratios;
3. control the corresponding glued range radii, since a denominator bound
   alone never bounds `Theta_p`.

Thus the exact `p=3` result is a valuable calibration and removes one
failure mode there.  It is not evidence for uniform rational 2-acyclicity;
that proposed shortcut is now closed.
