# The negative Clifford sector cannot occur in a norm-matrix model

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

Let `Gamma<G` be a Kazhdan pair with a strict compressor `t`, so

```text
t Gamma t^(-1) < Gamma.
```

Let `X=G/Gamma`, and let

```text
E = Vtilde semidirect G
```

be either the complete or the sparse Kun--Thom Clifford extension.  Its
lamp group is generated by a central involution `z` and involutions `c_x`
with

```text
c_x c_y = z c_y c_x       (x != y).
```

Then every homomorphism

```text
Theta : E -> U(product_n M_(d_n) / directSum_n M_(d_n))
```

sends `z` to `1`.  In particular,

```text
E is not weak/operator-norm MF.
```

Thus cutting an MF model to the canonical `z=-1` corner is a valid
rank-renormalization operation, but it cannot prove MF of this group: the
same negative corner is forbidden in every norm-matrix model.

## 2. Cut to the negative central corner

Suppose `Theta(z) != 1` and put

```text
e = (1-Theta(z))/2.
```

This is a nonzero central projection.  Projections lift through the norm
matrix quotient, so after restricting to a subsequence and cutting by
nonzero lifts `e_n`, the corner is again a norm matrix quotient

```text
e A e = product_n M_(r_n) / directSum_n M_(r_n),
```

with unit `e`, and the corner representation sends `z` to `-e`.  No lower
bound on `r_n/d_n` is required.

Choose unitary lifts `U_(g,n)` for the corner images.  On

```text
K_n = L2(M_(r_n), tr_(r_n))
```

form the adjoint maps

```text
beta_n(g)(x) = U_(g,n) x U_(g,n)^*.
```

Operator-norm multiplicative defect for the lifts gives operator-norm
multiplicative defect for the adjoint maps.  Hence they define an honest
representation of `G` in the corresponding norm quotient of the
`B(K_n)`.

## 3. Property `(T)` forces the contradiction

Let `p_Gamma` be the Kazhdan projection of `Gamma`, and let

```text
P = beta(p_Gamma).
```

The inclusion `t Gamma t^(-1) <= Gamma` gives

```text
P <= beta(t) P beta(t)^*.
```

The two projections are unitarily equivalent.  A norm quotient of finite
matrix algebras is stably finite, so the inequality is equality:

```text
P = beta(t) P beta(t)^*.
```

Therefore `beta(t)` preserves the `Gamma`-fixed subspace.

Let `o=Gamma`.  The matrix vector represented by the lamp `c_o` is
`Gamma`-fixed.  Consequently the vector represented by `c_(t o)` is also
`Gamma`-fixed.  Choose

```text
gamma in Gamma with gamma t Gamma != t Gamma,
```

which exists because the compression is strict.  Fixedness gives

```text
||Theta_-(c_(gamma t Gamma))-Theta_-(c_(t Gamma))||_(2,omega)=0.
```

But the two sites are distinct, and `Theta_-(z)=-e`, so the two
self-adjoint lamp unitaries anticommute.  In the normalized corner trace,

```text
||C_x-C_y||_2^2
  = tr(2-C_x C_y-C_y C_x)
  = 2.
```

This is a contradiction.  Hence `Theta(z)=1`.

## 4. Audit of the proposed conversion theorem

The spectral projection

```text
p_n=(1-Z_n)/2
```

does eliminate relative-rank blindness: after cutting and renormalizing,
operator-norm defect dominates normalized Hilbert--Schmidt defect and the
Clifford wall has squared norm `2`.  Two independent obstructions prevent
this from finishing the FALSE program.

1. The theorem above says that a nonzero negative corner cannot occur in
   any norm-matrix representation of the Kun--Thom Clifford group.  Thus
   the desired MF hypothesis is not merely missing; it is false.
2. Even without the sterility theorem, cutting an arbitrary faithful
   norm-ultraproduct representation to one central sector need not remain
   faithful on every group element.  The Clifford wall proves that the
   corner is nontrivial, not that the corner homomorphism is injective.
   To obtain a hyperlinear nonsofic image from a nonfaithful corner, one
   would additionally need a surviving element known to lie in the full
   sofic radical.

The direct normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt endpoint remains live:

```text
Cl(X) crossed_product G is Connes embeddable.
```

The norm-MF route to that endpoint is closed.
