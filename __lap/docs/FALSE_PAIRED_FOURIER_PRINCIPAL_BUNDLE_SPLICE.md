# Exact principal-bundle completion of the paired Fourier--Q cell

Date: 2026-08-11

## 1. Outcome

The paired Fourier--`Q` cell in
`FALSE_RADICAL_BS_PAIRED_FOURIER_Q_CELL.md` can be combined with the exact
principal-bundle construction in
`FALSE_EXACT_PRINCIPAL_BUNDLE_EXTERNAL_COMPLETION.md` without returning to
the diagonal clock model.

Let `S_(0,n),S_(1,n)` be the two compressor unitaries `(PFQ6)`.  There is
a single finite quotient

`K_n=SL_d(Z/m_n Z)`                                   `(PFS1)`

and two exact representations

`Phi_(i,n):SL_d(Z)->U(H_n tensor ell^2(K_n))`,
`i=0,1`,                                               `(PFS2)`

such that, for the commuting transvections

`t=I+e_12`, `s=I+e_13`,                               `(PFS3)`

one has

`Phi_(i,n)(t)=S_(i,n) tensor L_(K_n)(tbar)`,
`Phi_(i,n)(s)=1 tensor L_(K_n)(sbar)`.                 `(PFS4)`

Both representations commute with the common left `Q_n` action.  Since
the regular `K_n` factor commutes with all coefficient operators,
`(PFQ7)--(PFQ9)` survive unchanged.  In particular,

1. the two models have identical forward action on the common positive
   additive module;
2. their two inverse compressor conjugates of the marked positive element
   are `D_n(a)` and `D_n(a)^q`;
3. the second transvection `s` fixes both marked operators; and
4. the fold is `D_n(a)^p`, with squared normalized HS norm exactly `2`.

Thus all abstract external `SL_d(Z)` relations, all coefficient/external
cross-relations, and the complete marked `t,s,c_0` slice are simultaneous
and exact in the genuinely common-positive paired model.

The only remaining gate is still the large one: extend the common positive
additive representation to the full polynomial elementary group and make
the same external matrices implement every tested Laurent monomial and
base-elementary covariance relation.  The theorem below removes the
external presentation from that gate.

## 2. A unitary principal-bundle prescription theorem

The group-valued theorem `(PBE1)` has the following direct unitary form.

**Lemma 1.**  Let `K` be a finite group, let `b in K` have order `m`, and
let `A in U(H)` satisfy `A^m=1`.  There is an exact representation

`Psi:K->U(H tensor ell^2(K))`                          `(PFS5)`

such that

`Psi(b)=A tensor L_K(b)`.                             `(PFS6)`

If a group `F` already acts on `H` and `[A,F]=1`, then `Psi(K)` commutes
with `F tensor 1`.

**Proof.**  The left `b`-orbits in `K` all have length `m`.  Choose one
point in each orbit and define

`varphi(b^j x)=A^j`, `0<=j<m`.                        `(PFS7)`

Let the block-diagonal unitary `W` be

`W(xi tensor delta_x)=varphi(x)xi tensor delta_x`.    `(PFS8)`

Put

`Psi(k)=W(1 tensor L_K(k))W^(-1)`.                    `(PFS9)`

This is an exact representation.  On the edge from `x` to `bx`, the fiber
operator is

`varphi(bx)varphi(x)^(-1)=A`,                         `(PFS10)`

which proves `(PFS6)`.  If `A` commutes with `F`, so do all values of
`varphi`, hence `W`; the last assertion follows.  End proof.

The commuting-subgroup version is equally useful.

**Lemma 2.**  Suppose `H_0<=K` acts freely on `K` by left multiplication
and `rho:H_0->U(H)` is a finite-image unitary representation.  Then there
is an exact representation `Psi` of `K` such that

`Psi(h)=rho(h) tensor L_K(h)`                         `(PFS11)`

for every `h in H_0`.

**Proof.**  On each left `H_0`-orbit choose an origin and put
`varphi(hx)=rho(h)`.  Equations `(PFS8)--(PFS10)` apply verbatim.  End
proof.

## 3. The paired compressor unitaries have finite order

It remains to justify that Lemma 2 applies to both matrices in `(PFQ6)`.
Write

`H_n=ell^2(Q_n) tensor ell^2(V_n times V_n)`.          `(PFS12)`

The Fourier wall has the form

`U_n=1 tensor U_n^fib`,                               `(PFS13)`

and the right gauge has the form

`T_n=R_(Q_n)(c_0) tensor P_n`,                        `(PFS14)`

where `P_n` is the permutation induced by

`(x,y)|->(c_0^(-1)x,q^(-1)c_0^T y)`.                 `(PFS15)`

Let `Pauli(V_n times V_n)` be the finite group generated by every
translation and every character multiplier on
`ell^2(V_n times V_n)`, together with the finite scalar group of
`p^n`-th roots of unity.  Its Schrödinger representation is irreducible.
Both `U_n^fib` and `P_n` normalize this Pauli group: finite Fourier exchange
interchanges translations and multipliers, while a linear coordinate
permutation acts contragrediently on characters.  Therefore their images
in the projective unitary group belong to the finite group

`Aut(Pauli(V_n times V_n))`.                           `(PFS16)`

It follows that every word in `U_n^fib,P_n` has finite projective order.
For such a word `Z`, some power is scalar:

`Z^r=zeta 1`.                                         `(PFS17)`

The determinants of a normalized finite Fourier transform, a coordinate
swap, and a permutation matrix are roots of unity.  Hence `det(Z)` is a
root of unity.  Taking determinants in `(PFS17)` shows that `zeta` is a
root of unity as well.  A further power of `(PFS17)` is the identity.
Thus the group generated by `U_n^fib,P_n` is torsion elementwise, and in
particular

`S_(0,n)=U_n^(-1)`,
`S_(1,n)=U_n^(-1)T_n^(-1)`                            `(PFS18)`

both have finite order.  The finite right translation in `(PFS14)` does
not change this conclusion.

Choose `m_n` to be a common multiple of the orders of the two matrices in
`(PFS18)`.  Increasing it if necessary does not affect the construction.

## 4. Exact completion of the external group

In `K_n` from `(PFS1)`, the reductions `tbar,sbar` generate

`H_(0,n)=<tbar,sbar>~=(Z/m_n Z)^2`.                   `(PFS19)`

For each `i`, define

`rho_(i,n)(tbar^a sbar^b)=S_(i,n)^a`.                 `(PFS20)`

The choice of `m_n` makes this a well-defined finite-image representation
of `H_(0,n)`.  Apply Lemma 2 and pull the resulting exact representation
of `K_n` back along reduction

`SL_d(Z)->SL_d(Z/m_n Z)`.                             `(PFS21)`

This gives `(PFS2)--(PFS4)`.

Every `S_(i,n)` commutes with the left `Q_n` action by `(PFQ18)`,
`(PFQ20)`, and `(PFQ25)`.  Hence Lemma 2 also proves all `Q_n times L`
cross-commutators exactly.  Equation `(PFQ7)` proves equality of the
forward common-positive compressor images, while `(PFQ8)--(PFQ9)` give
the marked fold.  The assignment of trivial fiber holonomy to `sbar`
shows that `s` fixes `D_n(a)` and its `q`-th power.  Multiplication by the
left coefficient operator `C_n=L_n(c_0)` then gives the full relation

`c h c^(-1)=h^q`, `c=(c_0,s)`,                        `(PFS22)`

in both marked sectors.

This proves the outcome.

## 5. Scope of the splice

This theorem does not claim that `(PFS2)` and the additive assignments
already define maps on the whole vertex group `G`.  It proves something
more precise than the earlier abstract prescribed-generator theorem: once
the paired additive/Fourier cell is fixed, every external group relation
can be satisfied **exactly**, with both compressor matrices prescribed and
with the commuting stable transvection carrying trivial fiber holonomy.

Consequently a future finite optimization should not vary any external
`SL_d` matrix.  It should freeze `(PFS2)` and vary only:

1. the remaining polynomial and Laurent tangent operators;
2. the base elementary-root operators; and
3. the intertwiners enforcing their covariance under the already exact
   external action.

Positive decay of those covariance and amalgam defects, together with
`(PFQ9)`, triggers the marked compatible-pair endpoint `(PFQ32)--(PFQ36)`.
