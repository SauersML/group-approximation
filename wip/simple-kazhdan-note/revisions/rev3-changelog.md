## A. New §2 "LEF groups"
**Statement (Corollary cor:lef):** a finitely generated group is LEF iff it is a subgroup of an infinite finitely generated simple Kazhdan LEF group.

**Reviews:**
- LEF host: PASS by sk-verify-3 part6, sk-lef-embedding-c (adversarial) and sk-verify-4 part2.
- Density patch: PASS by sk-verify-4 part4 §2 and sk-verify-7 part1.

**Opening paragraph (sk-lit-novelty-2 §3a):** "Every countable group embeds in a finitely generated simple group [Gorjuškin, Schupp], and Kionke and Schesler proved that every finitely generated residually finite group embeds in a finitely generated simple LEF group [KS, Theorem 1.2]. The next corollary adds property (T) and applies to every LEF group. Since LEF passes to subgroups, it characterizes LEF groups." The Kionke–Schesler reading matches their v1 p. 2 verbatim (Theorem 1.2: H ↪ G ↠ Q, with π∘ι injective and Q infinite, simple and LEF).

**Proof, checked paragraph by paragraph:**
1. **Overgroup.**
   - Γ ↪ ∏_ω Q_n, and the doubled regular actions are even.
   - Ore Theorem 1 makes each generator a commutator in P.
   - Δ = Δ_0 × Z is f.g., infinite and LEF, and Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ].
2. **Lamplighter.**
   - L = {x ↦ δx+f}; the action is minimal and TOPOLOGICALLY FREE (never called free).
   - Witness (W4): h ∉ W ∪ δW ∪ supp f.
   - R is generated by u_s and 1_{x(e)=1}, so f.g.; (T) from EJZ.
   - Points with trivial stabilizer in a ball are dense, so small sets exist inside every nonempty clopen set, and §1's simplicity proof applies with u_ℓ, ℓV and word length.
3. **LEF.**
   - Affine models on F_2^{Q'_n} × Q'_n, and a unital ring homomorphism R → ∏_ω M_{|E_n|}(F_2).
   - W5: the kernel on G is a normal subgroup of the simple group G missing e_12(1), so G embeds. This needs simplicity of G, not of R.
4. **Embedding.**
   - In characteristic 2, e_12(c)e_21(c⁻¹)e_12(c)·e_12(1)e_21(1)e_12(1) = diag(c,c⁻¹,1).
   - diag(a,a⁻¹,1)diag(b,b⁻¹,1)diag((ba)⁻¹,ba,1) = diag([a,b],1,1).
   - So ℓ ↦ diag(u_ℓ,1,1) is an injective homomorphism into GL_3(R) taking [L,L] into G.
   - diag(a,a⁻¹,1) is NOT used as a homomorphism, and the false double-swap lemma is not used.
