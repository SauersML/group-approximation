**OPEN.** Let `M = C(C, F_2)` with Thompson's `V` acting by `v·f = f ∘ v^(-1)`. Call
`W <= Aut(M)` *covered* if `V <= W`, `W = <V, x_1, ..., x_r>`, and for each `j` there are a
subgroup `C_j <= V` commuting with `x_j` and finitely many `m ∈ M` whose `C_j`-translates
span `M`. Then every finitely generated group with solvable word problem embeds in a
finitely presented covered `W`.

## Why it matters

By route `affine-actors-via-covered-cantor-module-amalgams` it gives
`decidable-groups-embed-in-orbit-finite-affine-actors`, hence permutational
Boone--Higman.

## What is known

- **A member.** The binary Leavitt unit group `U` is covered, with one transvection
  `x = 1 + s_(00) t_(01)` and `C_x = {c(h,k)}`: its translates of six cone indicators span
  `M` (`leavitt-cantor-module-fp-via-thompson-amalgam`, Step 1 and item 2 there).
- **Generators are pinned by finite data.** Since `x_j` commutes with `C_j`,
  `x_j·(c·m) = c·(x_j·m)`, so `x_j` is determined by the finitely many images `x_j·m`.
- **The commutant of all of `V` is `{0, id}`.** A linear map `T` commuting with `V` sends
  `χ_[0]` to a function invariant under `V_[0] × V_[1]`, i.e. `a χ_[0] + b χ_[1]`, hence
  `T χ_A = a χ_A + b χ_(C∖A)` for proper nonempty clopen `A`; comparing `T χ_[00] + T χ_[01]`
  with `T χ_[0]` gives `b = 0`. So each `C_j` must be a proper subgroup of `V`.
- **Walls.** No shared recursive word-problem bound for covered groups is known, so
  `complexity-bounded-host-classes-are-not-universal` does not apply yet.

## Attempts

1. **Diagonal cone covers stay inside the binary Leavitt unit group (2026-09-13).**
   `diagonal-cone-covered-actors-lie-in-binary-leavitt-unit-group` (ESTABLISHED, not
   independently reviewed):
   - Suppose each `x_j` commutes with a subgroup of `V` containing a diagonal cone subgroup.
     That is the shape of the Leavitt cover `c(h,k)`: one element of `V` on the tails of cones
     `[α_1..α_k]`, another on the rest.
   - Then the commutant consists of the maps `Σ λ_(ij) s_(α_i) t_(α_j) + μ P_R`, so every
     `x_j` is a Leavitt unit and `W <= U`.
   - `U` has decidable word problem, so `complexity-bounded-host-classes-are-not-universal`
     excludes some two-generated decidable input.
   - So any proof of this claim needs covering subgroups that contain no diagonal cone
     subgroup.
2. **Point-stabilizer covers leave the Leavitt algebra (hand check, not a node).** Put
   `p = 0^∞`, `q = 1^∞`, `C = Stab_V(p) ∩ Stab_V(q)`.
   - **`C` covers `M`.** Sort proper nonempty clopen sets by which of `p`, `q` they contain.
     Within one class, choose cones `[0^n]` and `[1^n]` inside the matching atoms of both sets,
     small enough that the remaining pieces are nonempty. Then move the rest by a prefix
     replacement fixing those cones. So the `C`-translates of four indicators, together with
     `1`, span `M`.
   - **A covered automorphism.** `x f = f + (f(p) + f(q))·1` commutes with `C`, and `x^2 = 1`
     because `1(p) + 1(q) = 0`.
   - **`x` is no element of `L_(F_2)(1,2)`.** Write `a = Σ s_α t_β` with `N` terms and
     `|β| <= L`. Then `a·χ_[0^n]` is supported in `∪ [α 0^(n-|β|)]`, of measure at most
     `N·2^(L-n)`, while `x χ_[0^n] = 1 + χ_[0^n]` has measure `1 - 2^(-n)`.
   - **Not decided:** whether `<V, x>` is finitely presented. Its word problem is easy (tail
     classes of `p` and `q`), so it does not bear on the complexity question.
3. **Complexity wall for the whole class: undecided (2026-09-13).**
   - **If covered groups were not complexity-bounded:** a finitely presented covered `W`
     yields the finitely presented orbit-finite affine actor `M ⋊ W` (route
     `affine-actors-via-covered-cantor-module-amalgams`), which is of type (A)
     (`orbit-finite-affine-actions-are-type-a`). So finitely presented covered groups with
     arbitrarily hard word problem would give `type-a-actors-with-arbitrarily-hard-word-problem`.
   - **If they were bounded:** one recursive word-problem bound for all covered groups would
     refute this claim through the same wall.
   - Neither direction is proved.
   - **Exact gap:** a finitely presented covered `W` whose covering subgroups contain no
     diagonal cone subgroup, with a generator outside the Leavitt algebra, containing a group
     that is not a subgroup of `U`.
