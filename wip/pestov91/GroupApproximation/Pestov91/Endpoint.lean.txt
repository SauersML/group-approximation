/-!
# Pestov Open Question 9.1: the hypothesis-free endpoint

Pestov's Open Question 9.1 (*Hyperlinear and sofic groups: a brief guide*,
arXiv:0804.3968v8, p. 21):

> Let G be an infinite simple group with Kazhdan's property (T). Can it be
> hyperlinear (sofic)?

The theorems below answer yes, in both forms, with no hypotheses.  The witness
is `EL₃(R)` for the crossed product `R = LC(X, 𝔽₂) ⋊ ℤ` of the Toeplitz subshift
`X` (blueprint `research/artifacts/pestov91-lean-blueprint-2026-09-13.md`, G8).
Its centre is trivial.

* **Kazhdan.**  `R` is finitely generated, and `EL₃` over a finitely generated
  ring has property `(T)` (Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain, proved in
  `GroupApproximation/Pestov91/Kazhdan.lean`).
* **Simple.**  `R` is a simple ring with centre `{0, 1}`, and `EL₃(R)` is simple
  (`SplitSimplicity`).
* **Infinite.**  `a ↦ x₁₂(a)` is injective.
* **LEF.**  `R` has exact local matrix models from periodic approximations of
  `X`, so `EL₃(R)` is LEF.  An LEF group is sofic and hyperlinear.

The prose of this module was written by Claude (Anthropic).
-/

namespace GroupApproximation

/-- **An infinite simple Kazhdan LEF group.**  `EL₃(LC(X, 𝔽₂) ⋊ ℤ)` for the
Toeplitz subshift `X`. -/
theorem exists_infinite_simple_kazhdan_lef :
    ∃ (E : Type) (_ : Group E), Infinite E ∧ IsSimpleGroup E ∧
      HasKazhdanPropertyT.{0, 0} E ∧ IsLEF E :=
  exists_infinite_simple_kazhdan_lef_of_elementary isFinitelyGeneratedRing_witnessRing
    isSimpleGroup_witness
    (elementaryModCentre_infinite (R := WitnessRing) (0 : Fin 3) 1 (by decide)) isLEF_witness

/-- **Pestov's Open Question 9.1, answered positively with no hypotheses.**  Some
infinite simple group with Kazhdan's property `(T)` is sofic and hyperlinear. -/
theorem exists_infinite_simple_kazhdan_sofic_hyperlinear :
    ∃ (E : Type) (_ : Group E), Infinite E ∧ IsSimpleGroup E ∧
      HasKazhdanPropertyT.{0, 0} E ∧ IsSofic E ∧ IsHyperlinear E :=
  exists_infinite_simple_kazhdan_sofic_hyperlinear_of_lef exists_infinite_simple_kazhdan_lef
