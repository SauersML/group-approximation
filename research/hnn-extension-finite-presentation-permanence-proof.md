## Direct proof, machine-checked

The statement looked like new infrastructure and is not, because of how Mathlib
*defines* the object: `HNNExtension G A B φ` is the quotient of the free product
`G ∗ Multiplicative ℤ` by the congruence generated by the pairs
`(t · a, φ a · t)`, one for every `a : A`.  Mathlib also closes
`Group.IsFinitelyPresented` under free products and under quotients by finitely
normally generated subgroups.  So the entire content is that the *infinite*
relation family may be replaced by the finitely many relators coming from a
generating set of `A`.

**The replacement.**  `relator a = t · a · (φ a · t)⁻¹` in `G ∗ ℤ`, and
`Rel φ S` is the normal closure of `relator '' S`.  In the quotient
`Q = (G ∗ ℤ) / Rel φ S`, the two maps

    conjHom : A →* Q,   a ↦ t · a · t⁻¹        phiHom : A →* Q,   a ↦ φ a

are homomorphisms — the first because conjugation is an automorphism — so
`MonoidHom.eq_of_eqOn_dense` applied to `closure S = ⊤` upgrades agreement on
`S` to agreement on all of `A`.  That is `conjHom_eq_phiHom`, and it is the only
place finite generation of `A` is used.

**The isomorphism.**  `toHNN : G ∗ ℤ →* HNNExtension G A B φ` kills every
relator (`toHNN_relator`, which is Mathlib's `HNNExtension.t_mul_of`), so it
factors as `toHNN' : Q →* HNNExtension G A B φ`.  In the other direction
`HNNExtension.lift` applies, its hypothesis being exactly the relation just
extended from `S` to `A`.  The two composites are checked on generators —
`HNNExtension.hom_ext` one way; `Coprod.hom_ext` together with
`MonoidHom.ext_mint`, which needs only the generator of `ℤ`, the other — and
`quotientEquiv` bundles them.

**The conclusion.**  `Q` is finitely presented as a quotient of the finitely
presented `G ∗ ℤ` by the normal closure of a finite set, and the isomorphism
transports that to the HNN extension.

Roughly 200 lines in total, against the 500--900 the roadmap estimated for all
of S8, and none of it specific to the Boone tower.
