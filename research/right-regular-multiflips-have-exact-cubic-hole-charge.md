Let \(G=\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\), with \(p\) odd, and use the
standard regular Iwahori endpoint

\[
X_0=\lambda_G(x),\qquad R=\lambda_G(r),\qquad T=\lambda_G(t).
\]

Let \(P\) be any projection in the right group algebra
\(\lambda_G(G)'\), and put

\[
C=1-2P,\qquad X=CX_0.                                 \tag{MFC1}
\]

Then the BS core, involution, and inversion rows are exact, while both
cubic residuals are the same operator:

\[
(XT)^3-1=C-1=-2P,
\]

\[
(XT^2R)^3-1=C-1=-2P.                                  \tag{MFC2}
\]

In particular, if \(\delta_1,\delta_2\) are their normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt norms, then

\[
\delta_1^2=\delta_2^2=4\tau(P).                       \tag{MFC3}
\]

The associated transfer is

\[
T_X=R_C T_0,                                          \tag{MFC4}
\]

and \(R_C\) commutes with \(T_0\). Since the prime endpoint transfer has
spectrum \(\{1,-1/p\}\), for every

\[
0\leq\epsilon<1-\frac1p                               \tag{MFC5}
\]

the near-top Hecke space is exactly

\[
{\cal H}_\epsilon
 =\mathbb C[G]^{\rm op}(1-P).                         \tag{MFC6}
\]

Consequently its optimal contractive coverage is

\[
c_\epsilon=1-\tau(P),                                 \tag{MFC7}
\]

and hence the coverage hole has the exact relator charge

\[
1-c_\epsilon
 =\tau(P)
 =\frac14\delta_1^2
 =\frac14\delta_2^2
 =\frac18(\delta_1^2+\delta_2^2).                     \tag{MFC8}
\]

Indeed, \(A=1-P\) lies in \({\cal H}_\epsilon\) and gives the contractive
carrier \(A^*A=1-P\). Conversely every \(A\in{\cal H}_\epsilon\) satisfies
\(AP=0\), so every sum of squares from that space vanishes on \(P\) and
has trace at most \(1-\tau(P)\).

The almost-full-frame theorem now gives

\[
\Delta^{\rm reg}_{G,B}(X)
 \leq2\sqrt{\tau(P)}
 =\delta_1=\delta_2.                                  \tag{MFC9}
\]

Thus no positive-density flip can hide behind vanishing cubic defects,
while an arbitrary operator-norm jump on a trace-small carrier is repaired
at the sharp square-root scale. This proves the desired trace-ideal
principle on all perturbations lying in the exact right-regular flip
sector. What remains open is to derive an analogous carrier charge for a
general supplied extender, whose bad near-top module need not be a right
ideal generated by one projection.
