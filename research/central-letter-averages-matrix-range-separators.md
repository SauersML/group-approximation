Let `A` be a unital C-star algebra generated by a fixed finite self-adjoint
unitary tuple `u=(u_s)_(s in S)`, and let

```text
MR_d(A,u)={ (Phi(u_s))_s : Phi:A->M_d is ucp }.                    (CLS1)
```

Suppose `X_n=(X_(n,s))_s` is a tuple of unitaries in `M_(d_n)`, and
`T_n in U(d_n)` satisfies

```text
max_s ||T_n X_(n,s)T_n^*-X_(n,s)||_2 -> 0.                        (CLS2)
```

If along a subsequence

```text
dist_2(X_n,MR_(d_n)(A,u)) >= epsilon>0,                            (CLS3)
```

then there are tuples `B_n=(B_(n,s))_s`, normalized by
`sum_s||B_(n,s)||_2^2=1`, such that

```text
Re sum_s tr_(d_n)(B_(n,s)^* X_(n,s))
 - sup_(Y in MR_(d_n)(A,u))
     Re sum_s tr_(d_n)(B_(n,s)^*Y_s) >= epsilon/2,                 (CLS4)

sum_s||T_nB_(n,s)T_n^*-B_(n,s)||_2^2 -> 0.                        (CLS5)
```

Moreover the separators may be chosen uniformly operator bounded, with the
explicit coordinate bound

```text
max_s||B_(n,s)||_op <= 8 sqrt(|S|)/epsilon^2.                      (CLS6)
```

Thus a central-letter-selected failure of ucp capture always has a
positive-gap Choi/matrix-range separator living asymptotically in the
letter's commutant.  The operator bound is essential: `(B_(n,s))` defines
an actual tuple in the tracial matrix ultraproduct, rather than a sparse
unbounded Hilbert--Schmidt witness.  Applied with `A=C^*(SL_3(Z))`,
`(CLS4)--(CLS6)` is a
necessary normal form for every counterexample to
`sl3-large-prime-hnn-matrix-range-capture`.

The conclusion is localization, not positivity: the commutant of one Haar
letter is large, and a block-diagonal separator need not vanish.  A proof of
the selected UCP seam must now use an arithmetic host relation to charge
this central separator, not another scalar average over the same letter.
