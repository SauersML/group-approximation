Literature import. Anand Natarajan and Chinmay Nirkhe, *The status of the quantum
PCP conjecture (games version)*, arXiv:2403.13084v1 (19 Mar 2024). The following
were read from the v1 PDF (pp. 1, 9--13) and the v1 TeX source on 2026-09-12:

- abstract: "(2) we explain an error in the energy amplification procedure of
  Natarajan and Vidick [NV18b] which invalidates their claim to have constructed
  a quantum games PCP for a QMA-complete problem";
- Section 4 opening (p. 9): the bug in Vidick's plane-vs-point soundness, and "it
  was discovered that the Natarajan and Vidick protocol for QMA is flawed in
  another way, so that it is now open whether QMA can be put in
  MIP*[q = O(log n), a = O(log n)] with efficient provers even if Vidick's original
  low-degree result is recovered";
- Section 4.1 (pp. 9--10): the seven-prover Steane-code template, and footnote 6
  crediting Alex Lombardi with first noticing the normalization issue;
- Section 4.2 (p. 11): eqs. (1), (2a)--(2e) and (3);
- Section 4.3 (pp. 12--13): Remark 5 (eqs. (4), (5)), Lemma 6 (eqs. (6), (7)) and
  its proof with eq. (9);
- Section 5: open problems 1--4.
