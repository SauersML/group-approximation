title: Unital UCT Kirchberg algebras are classified by scaled graded K-theory

Two unital Kirchberg algebras in the UCT class are isomorphic by a
unital `*`-isomorphism whenever there is an isomorphism of graded
K-theory carrying one unit class to the other; every such scaled graded
isomorphism is realised.  The realising isomorphism is NOT canonical and
no formula for it on generators is available -- that is exactly the
nonconstructive step this program pays for.

Reference: Phillips, *A classification theorem for nuclear purely
infinite simple C*-algebras*, https://arxiv.org/abs/funct-an/9506010
(and Kirchberg's independent proof).
