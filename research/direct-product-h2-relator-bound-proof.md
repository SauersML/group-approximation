# Apply the presentation-complex Hopf bound to K times an elementary abelian group

The presentation complex has `g` one-cells and `s` two-cells. Surjectivity
onto group homology and cellular homology give `s-g+b_1 >= b_2`. Kunneth
injects the `r(r+1)/2`-dimensional degree-two homology of `(C_2)^r`.
Bounded-width, bounded-occurrence faces supply only linearly many relators,
contradicting the bound at positive rate.
