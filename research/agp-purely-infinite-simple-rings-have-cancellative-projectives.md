**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `R` be a purely infinite simple ring, and let `V(R)*` be
the monoid of isomorphism classes of nonzero finitely generated projective right `R`-modules.
Then `V(R)*` is a group. So nonzero finitely generated projective modules that are stably
isomorphic, or equivalently have equal classes in `K_0(R)`, are isomorphic. No regularity is
assumed.
