`(Z, τ)^{*∞} ≅ (C([0,1]), τ_Leb)^{*∞}`: the infinite reduced free
products (with respect to the unique trace and Lebesgue measure) are
isomorphic.  Hirshberg--Phillips, 2026 preprint; recorded by STW as
the April 2026 addendum resolving the `k = ℓ = ∞` case of their free
isomorphism problem (`stw99-problem-xcviii-free-isomorphism`).  The
techniques crucially use the infinite free product.
