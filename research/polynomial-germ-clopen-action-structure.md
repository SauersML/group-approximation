**OPEN — submitted actor lemma pending independent review.**

For each group `B_{d,r}` of the polynomial manuscript, the action on its
countable set `Ω_r` of proper nonempty clopen subsets is faithful and
oligomorphic. The pointwise stabilizer of any finite clopen family is
a finite product of groups `B_{d,u}` on its nonempty Boolean atoms;
the setwise stabilizer is a finite extension of that product.

## Attempts

Artifact §8 proposes an orbit classification by Boolean-atom emptiness
and cone-count types modulo `b-1`. Matching emptiness patterns alone is
insufficient. The main remaining check is equality of atom restriction
groups with the entire claimed `B_{d,u}`: centered rational charts must
preserve marked points, shift `ν` by an integer, preserve polynomial
degree, and allow gluing by fullness. This is not inferred just from
the ambient group's finiteness. Once this structural lemma and the
separate `F_∞` tower theorem hold, BZ Theorem D applies to this actor.
