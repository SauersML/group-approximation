The filling `Γ/<<g^n>>` is an infinite hyperbolic quotient of the arithmetic
lattice `Γ` without nontrivial finite quotients.  So some hyperbolic group is
not residually finite, and clause (1) => (2) of the fixed-host reduction gives
such a quotient for every torsion-free cocompact `Sp(2,1)` lattice.  This covers
the lattice fixed in the target claim, which need not be `Γ`.
