title: The torus row and authenticated mixed loop kill all SL3 Steinberg affine gauges
---

For every odd prime `p`, let `St_p` be the defining-characteristic Steinberg
module of `SL_3(F_p)`.  The two universal affine Fox rows `X,Y` have zero
common kernel on `St_p`:

```text
ker(X|St_p) intersect ker(Y|St_p)=0.
```

Unlike the established pure symmetric-power cases, this is the first mixed
highest-weight test suggested by the spherical building.  The exact
flag-incidence reduction is established; injectivity of that explicit map
is the remaining open lemma.
