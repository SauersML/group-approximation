Citation import, secondary. Grigorchuk, arXiv:1111.0512, TeX source read on MSI.

- l.1084: "a nilpotent group has polynomial growth and therefore a virtually nilpotent group also
  has polynomial growth."
- l.1088–1090: "\begin{thm}\label{gromov} (Gromov 1981) If a finitely generated group $G$ has
  polynomial growth then $G$ contains a nilpotent subgroup of finite index. \end{thm}"
- l.831–833: "Milnor observed that a solvable but not polyciclic group has exponential growth.
  This, in combination with Wolf's result, led to the fact that the growth of a solvable group is
  exponential except for the case when the group is virtually nilpotent."

Gromov's and Milnor's and Wolf's original papers were not re-read.
