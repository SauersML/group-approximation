Conditional route; it fires when both required claims are established.

The second claim gives a cocompact lattice `Γ` with locally `∞`-transitive projection
closures of finite `H^(∞)`-index containing a copy of `Γ_R`. Soficity passes to subgroups,
so a nonsofic `Γ_R` makes `Γ` nonsofic.
