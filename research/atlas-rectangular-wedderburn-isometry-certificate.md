In the canonical regular-`A_8` relative-unitary model from
`leavitt-regular-atlas-hyperlinearity-criterion`, there are irreducibles
`sigma,tau` with `d_sigma<d_tau` and a fixed coefficient expression `X(U)`
from the Wedderburn compiler such that the atlas relators force

```text
||p_tau-X(U)^*X(U)||_2 <= C delta^c,                 (ARW1)
```

where `delta` is the maximum normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt defect of the fixed
canonical packet and the constants are independent of external multiplicity.

A sufficient, deliberately machine-searchable form is stronger: after
compiling the packet relator residuals to coefficient blocks, exhibit a
bounded finite ideal certificate of the form in
`fixed-coefficient-ideal-certificate-controls-hs-defect` for

```text
D(U)=p_tau-X(U)^*X(U).                               (ARW2)
```

The expression `X` may use a fixed finite matrix amplification and a fixed
finite path in the Fourier block graph, but no construction may depend on the
multiplicity `k`.

This target exploits exactly the finite/infinite multiplicity distinction.
An exact infinite-multiplicity Leavitt representation can contain an isometry
from one countably infinite coefficient corner into another even when their
finite regular multiplicities occur in the ratio `d_tau:d_sigma`.  A finite
regular multiple cannot: `rectangular-wedderburn-block-isometry-gap` gives a
fixed rank floor.

## Attempts

- **Finite Fourier path synthesis.** Compile the raw/comb and first interior
  atlas relations to the block IR and enumerate short paths between ordered
  irreducible pairs of unequal degree. Search for a Gram defect whose entries
  reduce into the coefficient ideal generated by the compiled relators.
- **Start with interior information.** Boundary-only exact models are already
  known, so a certificate using only boundary relators cannot close the live
  atlas problem. Include the canonical interior packet; collision `19243` is
  already known to be packet-forced and kills the principal affine boundary
  family.
- **Prefer degree imbalance over invariant fibers.** The new
  `pauli-compressor-equalizer-density-decays` no-go shows why iterating a fixed
  source=compressed character sector loses all density. A rectangular block
  can instead move between two fixed positive-density isotypic corners, whose
  rank ratio is protected by regular `A_8` representation theory.
- **Falsification.** Any exact finite regular-margin solution of the selected
  packet immediately refutes a proposed ideal certificate. Test candidates on
  all archived finite atlas countermodels before attempting symbolic
  elimination.
