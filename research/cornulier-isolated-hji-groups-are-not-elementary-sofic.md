**ESTABLISHED (literature import).**  Call a group *elementary sofic* if it
lies in the smallest class containing the trivial group and closed under
subgroups, direct limits, marked limits of finitely generated groups, and
extensions with amenable quotient.  Every elementary sofic group is sofic.

Let `G` be finitely presented and not residually finite.  Suppose some finite
normal subgroup `Z` makes `G/Z` hereditarily just infinite, meaning every
proper quotient of every finite-index subgroup is finite.  Then `G` is
isolated.  If moreover `G` is not amenable, `G` is not elementary sofic.

Cornulier lists some non-residually-finite lattices in simple Lie groups,
Thompson's group `T` and the Burger--Mozes simple groups as examples, and
adds "it is unknown if they are sofic".  His Question 5 asks whether any
sofic group fails to be elementary sofic.
