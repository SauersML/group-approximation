Evaluation of `1=sum a_jp_jb_j` in `M_s(F2)` and rank subadditivity give a
uniform lower bound without controlling multiplier sizes.  Conversely,
normalized-rank-zero evaluations factor the free-algebra map through the
two-matrix quotient into the rank metric matrix ultraproduct, which is stably
finite.  Hence either a finite Bezout identity or the stronger absence of a
stably finite unital quotient closes this restricted Atlas frame.
