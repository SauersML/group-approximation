If `G × Z` is of type F, then `G` is of type `F_∞` with finite cohomological
dimension (`g-times-z-type-f-iff-g-f-infinity-with-finite-cd`, the forward
direction). If every such group is of type F
(`f-infinity-groups-of-finite-cd-are-of-type-f`), then `G` is of type F, which is
the affirmative answer to Problem 1.21.
