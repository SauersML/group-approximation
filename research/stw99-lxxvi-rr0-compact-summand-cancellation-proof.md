For projection bundles, the exact strict relative theorem homotopes
the image of a common p-frame to the standard frame inside p direct-sum r
where r is nonzero. Polar transport then embeds the remaining q-frame
into r. On the clopen locus r=0, coefficient finiteness forces q=0.

For arbitrary positives, coefficient cancellation gives pointwise
comparison. The closed equality core of a source cutoff has a
continuous support projection for a. A local polar-frame argument
shows that b has continuous support and a uniform spectral gap on
the same core: its fiber support cannot properly contain an equivalent
finite projection. Projection-bundle cancellation supplies the core
embedding; strict relative extension gives the global cutoff comparison.

A detailed Cu-limit argument retains the compact summand at a common
stage. Finite-coordinate approximations of compact metrizable bases
therefore remove the dimension restriction. Ideal inclusion into the
one-point compactification gives the locally compact statement.

For image removal, all-base V surjectivity lifts the compact target
summand. Full order reflection places that projection below the source
class, where projection comparison supplies an actual orthogonal module
decomposition. Target compact cancellation removes the common summand.
