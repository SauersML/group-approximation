The separable, exact, nonnuclear, non-simple algebra

```text
C([0,1]) tensor_min C*_r(F_2)
```

is Cuntz semigroup regular but does not absorb the Jiang--Su algebra Z.

Thus Cu-regularity for the interval-field class obtained in
`stw99-lxxvi-interval-fields-over-simple-pure-fibers` is genuinely weaker
than C*-level Z-stability, even for an exact, faithfully tracial example.
