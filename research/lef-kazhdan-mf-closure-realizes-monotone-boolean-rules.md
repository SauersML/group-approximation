For every nonconstant monotone `F:{0,1}^m->{0,1}`, there is a LEF
Kazhdan group `G`, generated by torsion of orders `7,2,2`, with
pairwise commuting, disjoint normal locally finite input groups
`K_i` and an infinite simple locally finite normal output group `L`,
such that `L <= cl_MF(product_(i in I) K_i)` exactly when `F(1_I)=1`.
The total input product intersects `L` trivially.

If `F(1_I)=0`, a LEF target killing those inputs is injective on `L`.
The group has no nontrivial finite quotients. Input groups are finite
products of simple edge factors, with `1` allowed for unused variables.
