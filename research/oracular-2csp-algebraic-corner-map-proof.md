Faithfulness of the canonical group trace gives positive trace to every
nonzero algebraic projection `q`.  Under hyperlinearity, compress the
canonical matrix-ultraproduct embedding by `q` and normalize its trace.
Composing the resulting CE corner trace with the unital map
`A(B)->qQ[Gamma]q`, and then complexifying, gives a perfect CE tracial game
model.

For a canonical-profile microstate sequence the same construction is made
after passing to the ultraproduct: all group and game-algebra identities are
then exact, and the canonical moment profile gives
`tr(pi(q))=tau_Gamma(q)>0`.  Thus no finite-coordinate projection rounding is
used.  An exact perfect-side representation detecting `q` proves the
algebraic survival required by reverse Kleene.
