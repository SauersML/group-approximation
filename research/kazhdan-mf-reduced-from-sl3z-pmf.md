title: A strongly convergent sequence for SL_3(Z) embeds its reduced algebra in a matrix ultraproduct

`SL_3(Z)` is infinite and has property (T).  If finite-dimensional
representations `rho_i` converge strongly to `lambda_(SL_3(Z))`, the induced map
`C[SL_3(Z)] -> prod_i M_(N_i) / sum_i M_(N_i)` is isometric for the reduced norm
on the group algebra, hence extends to an injective *-homomorphism of
`C*_r(SL_3(Z))` (Magee--de la Salle, arXiv:2312.03220v2, discussion after
Definition 1.2).
