**ESTABLISHED by citation** (route `smith-fixed-point-theorem-for-finite-p-groups-citation`).

Let `p` be a prime, `P` a finite `p`-group and `X` a finitistic space (every open
cover has a finite-dimensional refinement), for instance a finite-dimensional
simplicial complex. If `P` acts on `X` and `X` has the mod `p` homology of a point,
then the fixed point set `X^P` also has the mod `p` homology of a point. In
particular `X^P` is nonempty.

Used here only for `X` the geometric realization of a finite-dimensional, locally
finite simplicial complex on which `P` acts simplicially. For such `X` the covering
dimension is at most the simplicial dimension, so `X` is finitistic, and the Čech
and singular theories agree. A contractible `X` has the mod `p` homology of a point.
