Assume first that `D_infinity` is finite dimensional and choose one of its
positive atoms `A`.  The action of `Q_G` permutes the finitely many atoms,
so the orbit of `A` is finite.  Since `D_0 subset D_infinity`, every atom
of `D_infinity`, and hence every translate of `A`, lies in a unique atom
of `D_0`.  This proves condition 2.

Conversely, suppose `A` satisfies condition 2 and let

```text
T={gA:g in Q_G}.
```

The union `U=union_(C in T) C` is measurable, invariant, and has positive
measure.  Ergodicity makes `U` conull.  Let `E` be the finite Boolean
algebra generated by the finite family `T`.  It is `Q_G`-invariant because
`Q_G` permutes `T`.

Every positive atom `e` of `E` has a fixed membership pattern in the sets
of `T`.  Since their union is conull, that pattern contains some
`C in T`, so

```text
e subset C subset one atom of D_0.                       (EFP1)
```

If two sets from `T` overlap on `e`, their assigned readable profiles
agree there, because distinct atoms of `D_0` are disjoint.  Hence `(EFP1)`
is unambiguous.  Every atom of `E` is profile-pure, and the atoms cover the
space, so each atom of `D_0` is a union of atoms of `E`.  Therefore

```text
D_0 subset E.
```

Invariance of `E` now gives `gD_0g^(-1) subset E` for every `g in Q_G`.
Thus `D_infinity subset E` and is finite dimensional, proving condition 1.
