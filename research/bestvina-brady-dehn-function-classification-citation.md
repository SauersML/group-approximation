- Definition 1 (v2 wording "essentially 2-reducible": "a join of two
  irreducible subgraphs, each with at least two vertices; equivalently, if it
  is neither a cone graph nor a join of three or more subgraphs");
- Definition 2, properties (D1)–(D4);
- Theorem 1: "the Dehn function of the Bestvina–Brady group BB_Γ satisfies
  δ_BB_Γ(n) ≍ n^d(Γ)";

  simply connected, then BB_Γ has cubic Dehn function";
