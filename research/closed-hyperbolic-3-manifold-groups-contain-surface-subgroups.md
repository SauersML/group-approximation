For every closed hyperbolic 3-manifold `M` there is a closed hyperbolic surface `S`
and a map `S -> M` injective on fundamental groups.

J. Kahn and V. Markovic, *Immersing almost geodesic surfaces in a closed hyperbolic
three manifold*, arXiv:0910.5501. TeX source `KM-1.tex`, fetched on MSI
2026-09-13, Theorem `thm-intro-1` (l.160--162), verbatim:

> Let $\M$ be a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold. Then we can find a closed hyperbolic
> surface $S$ and a continuous map $f:S \to \M$ such that the induced map between
> fundamental groups is injective.

Theorem `main` (l.148--151) gives the quantitative form: the surface group is a
`(1+epsilon)`-quasifuchsian subgroup of the Kleinian group, for every `epsilon > 0`.
