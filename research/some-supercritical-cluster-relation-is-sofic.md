**OPEN.** Let `G` be finitely generated and nonamenable, and `S` a finite symmetric generating set. In the
coupling of `nonsofic-groups-have-cluster-soficity-threshold-in-pc-pu`, there is `p > p_c` such that the
cluster relation `R_p` on `Omega = [0,1]^E` is sofic. Equivalently, `p_sof(G,S) > p_c`.

**What it gives.** For sofic `G` it has no content beyond soficity of Bernoulli relations. For nonsofic `G`
it gives `p_c < p_u` on `Cay(G,S)` (`nonsofic-benjamini-schramm-from-sofic-supercritical-clusters`). That
includes the binary Leavitt unit group, where the cost and `L^2`-Betti routes are empty.

## Attempts

- **Treeability.** Treeable relations are sofic (Elek--Lippner). So it would suffice that `R_p`, restricted
  to the infinite clusters, is treeable for some `p` just above `p_c`.
  - *Where it dies.* No treeing of the cluster relation itself is known in the nonuniqueness phase.
  - Gaboriau--Lyons produce free subrelations inside cluster relations. Those are not treeings of `R_p`,
    and subrelations say nothing about soficity of the whole.
- **Finite models along open paths.** A sofic model of `R_p` needs partial permutations for the moves
  along open `s`-edges, consistent with the decoration `xi` on all edges, including closed ones.
  - Word paths of a nonsoficity certificate `(F, eps_0)` are open only with probability of order `p^L`,
    and two-point connectivity decays in the nonuniqueness phase.
  - So the certificate forces nothing at small `p`. This is consistent, but it gives no construction.
  - *Where it dies.* Closed relator cycles still constrain the decorations seen from different open branches
    of one cluster, and nothing on record controls that consistency at large scale.
- **Restriction to the free part of a sofic subgroup.** If `H <= G` is sofic, cluster moves along
  `H`-labeled edges generate a sofic subrelation.
  - *Where it dies.* `R_p` is generated by all labels, and soficity is not closed under joins of
    subrelations.
- **Honest scope.** For a nonsofic `G` whose cluster relations are nonsofic at every `p > p_c`, this claim
  fails even if `p_c < p_u`. So it is a sufficient mechanism for Benjamini--Schramm on nonsofic groups, not
  an equivalent form.
