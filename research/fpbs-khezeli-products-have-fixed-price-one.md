**ESTABLISHED.** Imported from arXiv:2509.08325, by Ali Khezeli, *Products of
Infinite Countable Groups Have Fixed Price One*, Theorem
`thm:fixedpriceGeneral`. If `H` and `K` are infinite countable groups, then
every free probability-measure-preserving action of `H x K` has cost one.

This is not a percolation theorem. Status records an imported theorem, not
independent mathematical verification.
