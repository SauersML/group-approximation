Fisher, Linton, and Sánchez-Peralta, *Coherent RFRS groups*,
arXiv:2603.16763v1, Corollary 4.1, proves ten equivalent conditions for
every field `k` and every finitely generated virtually RFRS group with
`cd_k(G)<=2`.

The target records eight of those conditions. Corollary 1.6 is the
`k=Q` specialization, where the agrarian second Betti number is the usual
second L-two Betti number. Thus the field quantifier here is not inferred
from the rational statement: it is part of Corollary 4.1 itself.
