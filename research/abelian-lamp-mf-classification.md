Let `H` be countable residually finite, `L <= H` finitely generated with
property (T), `L_1 = t L t^{-1} < L`, `X = H/L_1`.  For every countable
abelian group `A`,

```text
A^(X) semidirect H  is MF   <=>   A is torsion-free.
```

**Why it matters.**  One fixed compression geometry supports both regimes:
torsion lamps collapse (binary lamps are the manuscript's mechanism),
torsion-free abelian lamps diffuse through slow character paths.  The
lamp order is the exact phase parameter of the obstruction — this is the
sharpest calibration to date of what the analytic mechanism consumes.
