**ESTABLISHED** through `rational-bs12-hull-embeds-in-a-fg-solvable-group-proof`
(elementary lane proof, not independently reviewed). The device is P. Hall's
construction of finitely generated centre-by-metabelian groups with a prescribed
countable abelian centre; no novelty is claimed.

**Statement.** Let `D_2 = Q x|_2 Z = <Q, s | s q s^-1 = 2q>`, the subgroup of
`Aff(Q) <= GL_2(Q)` generated by all translations and by `x -> 2x`. There is a
group `Ĥ` such that:
1. `Ĥ` is generated by 4 elements;
2. `Ĥ` is solvable of derived length at most 3: nilpotent of class 2, by `Z^2`;
3. `Ĥ` has solvable word problem;
4. `D_2` embeds in `Ĥ`.

**The group.**
- Put `R = Z[1/2][t, t^-1]` and `M = Z[t, t^-1] ⊆ R`.
- Let `N` be the set of triples `(a, b, c)` with `a, c ∈ R` and `b ∈ M`, under
  `(a,b,c)(a',b',c') = (a+a', b+b', c+c'+ab')`. This is the upper unitriangular
  group with entries `a, c` in `R` and `b` in `M`.
- `T(a,b,c) = (ta, t^-1 b, c)` and `Σ(a,b,c) = (2a, b, 2c)` are commuting
  automorphisms of `N`. Put `H = N x| <T, Σ>`, with `<T, Σ> ≅ Z^2`.
- Let `φ : R -> Q` be the `Z[1/2]`-linear map with `φ(t^i) = 1/|i|!`. Put
  `C = {(0,0,c) : φ(c) = 0}` and `Ĥ = H / C`.

**Where D_2 sits.** The image of `{(0,0,c)}` in `Ĥ` is `R / ker φ ≅ Q`, and `Σ`
acts on it by multiplication by 2. Together with `Σ` it generates a copy of
`D_2`.

## Consequences

- `D_2 ∈ B_A` would follow from `Ĥ ∈ B_A`, since `B_A` is closed under subgroups
  (`boone-higman-type-a-class-closed-under-finite-extensions`). That is route
  `rational-bs12-hull-via-hall-overgroup`, with open hole
  `rational-bs12-hall-overgroup-in-permutational-bh-class`.
- `Ĥ` is not residually finite, because it contains `(Q,+)` (root obstruction
  O1). So no residually finite result about finitely generated solvable groups
  applies to it.
- With `Σ` omitted, the same quotient is Hall's classical group with centre
  `≅ Q`. The dilation is what makes the `Q` non-central and brings in the
  distorted `BS(1,2)` (root obstruction O4).
