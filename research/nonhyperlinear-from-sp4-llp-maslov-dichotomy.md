The rank-reduction theorem turns a positive answer to the symplectic LLP
claim into LLP for `C^*(Sp_4(Z))`. The first alternative in the Maslov
dichotomy is then false, so its second alternative supplies a finitely
presented finite Deligne cover which is nonhyperlinear.
