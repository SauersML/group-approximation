Let `A` be a C-star algebra whose primitive ideal space `Prim(A)` is
topologically scattered: every nonempty subspace has an isolated point.  If
`A` is weakly purely infinite, then `A` is purely infinite.

No separation axiom on `Prim(A)` and no separability, nuclearity, unitality,
exactness, countability, or bound on the Cantor--Bendixson height is assumed.
The conclusion applies even when the ideal lattice has arbitrarily large
antichains.
