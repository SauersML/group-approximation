This is an alternative to `stw83-coefficient-toeplitz-three-colour-proof`.
It does not use the connector-corner claim or the Brake--Winter
normal-lifting step.

1. **The pullback.**  By the first required claim,
   `A_N={x in C(T,T_N) : q_N(x(t)) is independent of t}`, where
   `T_N=C*(S^N,K)`.
   * Given such `x`, choose `y` in `T_N` with `q_N(y)=q_N(x(t))`.  Then
     `x-y tensor 1` is a norm-continuous `K`-valued function.
   * Hence `A_N=C(T,K)+T_N tensor 1`, which is the constant-symbol pullback
     `A_T(T_N)` with `K subseteq T_N subseteq B(l^2)` separable.
2. **The quotient.**  `T_N` is unital, because `(S^N)^*S^N=1`.  `T_N/K` is
   generated by the unitary `q(S^N)`, so it is `C(sigma_ess(S^N))`.  `S^N` is
   unitarily equivalent to `N` copies of the unilateral shift, so
   `sigma_ess(S^N)=T`.  Thus `T_N/K=C(T)`, of nuclear dimension `1`.
3. **Conclusion.**  The second required claim, with `X=T`, `n=1` and `d=1`,
   gives `1<=dim_nuc(A_N)<=2`.
