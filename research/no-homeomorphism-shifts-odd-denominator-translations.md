**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, not reviewed; elementary; no priority claimed).

**Statement.** Let `Y` be a compact metrizable space and `ι: Z_ℓ → Homeo(Y)` a continuous
injective homomorphism, for example translation on one `ℓ`-adic coordinate. Let `a_0, a_1, a_2`
be `ℓ`-adic units with `a_1^2 ≠ a_0 a_2`, for example `a_k = 1/p_k` for distinct primes
`p_k ≠ ℓ`. Then no `s ∈ Homeo(Y)` satisfies `s ι(a_0) s^{-1} = ι(a_1)` and
`s ι(a_1) s^{-1} = ι(a_2)`.

**Proof.** `ι` is a closed embedding, since `Z_ℓ` is compact. The unit `a_k` generates a dense
subgroup of `Z_ℓ`, so the closure of `⟨ι(a_k)⟩` is `ι(Z_ℓ)` for each `k`. Conjugation by `s` is
a homeomorphism of `Homeo(Y)`. It maps the closure of `⟨ι(a_0)⟩` onto the closure of
`⟨ι(a_1)⟩`, so it restricts to a continuous automorphism `α` of `Z_ℓ` with `α(a_0) = a_1` and
`α(a_1) = a_2`. Every continuous endomorphism of `Z_ℓ` is multiplication by `λ = α(1)`, since `Z`
is dense. So `a_1 = λ a_0` and `a_2 = λ a_1`, hence `a_1^2 = a_0 a_2`, a contradiction. For
`a_k = 1/p_k` this reads `p_1^2 = p_0 p_2`, which is false for distinct primes. ∎

**Use.** The natural model of `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩` in a 2-dimensional Cantor host acts on `Z_2 × C`:
- the dilation is the digit move `h^{-1} ∈ 2V`, which satisfies `h^{-1}(τ_q × id)h = τ_{2q} × id`
  for every `q ∈ Z_2`;
- the translations are `τ_q × id` for `q ∈ Z_(2)`.

A finitely generated host needs all `τ_{1/p}` from finitely many generators. Neither a
denominator shift (this node) nor a multiplier shift `m_{p_k} ↦ m_{p_{k+1}}`
(`no-homeomorphism-shifts-primes-acting-as-l-adic-scalars`) exists on one coordinate. And
`τ_{1/3} ∉ W`, since its local translation part `1/3 ∉ Z[1/2]`.

## Lesson for general BH

**Prime separation.** In any compact model where a prime's arithmetic enters through a continuous
action of `Z_ℓ` or `Z_ℓ^×` on one coordinate, conjugation cannot move one prime's data to
another's. The closure of the monothetic group is rigid: its continuous automorphisms are
scalings, and the prime data are multiplicatively independent. So a finitely generated host of
`Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩`, `Aff(Q)` or `GL_n(Q)` must carry different primes on **different coordinates or
different local models**, and move them by maps that also move coordinates. This is a design
constraint for every all-primes route, including the prime-shift routes of
`gl-n-q-lies-in-prime-shift-permutation-group`.
