Use the finite subgroup `C_f^(0)` itself as the chart. Its irreducible types
are exactly the corrected child-center signs over every selector character,
even though the corresponding central idempotents are not group words. The
symmetrized reset balances this complete type vector and has a
dimension-independent approximate payment. Attach an isomorphic fresh child
chart whose central selectors are the next context words, and use an HNN edge
only to transport the now equal-dimensional types. Amalgam and HNN normal
forms preserve the marked base. This gives the four local clauses of the
target; it deliberately makes no global curvature claim for a finite graph
of such cells.
