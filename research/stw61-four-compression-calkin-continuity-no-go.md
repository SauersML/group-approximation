Let S1,S2 be orthogonal isometries on an infinite-dimensional
Hilbert space H with compact defect E=I-S1S1*-S2S2*. For any
family of self-adjoint unitaries V_t, 0<=t<=1, with V_0=I and
V_1=-I, at least one of the four compression paths

```text
[R* V_t R] in B(H)/K(H),
R in {S1,S2,(S1+S2)/sqrt(2),(S1+i S2)/sqrt(2)},
```

is not norm continuous. Strong continuity of V_t is allowed
but does not change the conclusion. All four R are obtained
from S1 by explicit fixed coefficient unitaries in
C*(1,S1,S2). No compact corrections can make all four
compressed paths norm continuous.

This rules out a uniform Calkin-norm-continuity premise for
the direct infinite-compression upgrade of the threshold
method. It does not refute the unrestricted maximal-commutator
assertion or exclude W-dependent paths, varying coefficient
representations, or other relative-index constructions.
LXI remains open.
