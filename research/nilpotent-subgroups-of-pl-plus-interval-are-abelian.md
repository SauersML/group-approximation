Let `PL_+(I)` be the group of orientation-preserving piecewise linear homeomorphisms of
`[0,1]` with finitely many breakpoints. Every nilpotent subgroup of `PL_+(I)` is abelian.
In particular the Heisenberg group, and every non-abelian torsion-free nilpotent group,
is isomorphic to no subgroup of Thompson's group `F`.

These groups are bi-orderable and have no non-abelian free subgroups. So they are
candidate witnesses for `biorderable-subgroup-of-v-without-free-subgroups-not-in-f`,
if they embed in `V`.
