title: A finite quasitraceless target containing one admissible domain refutes Problem LXXVIII
distinct_from:
  stw99-problem-lxxviii-zstable-map-to-quasitraceless-target: that asks for O-infinity-stability of every admissible map; this gives an unconditional criterion that any finite target map violates.
---

Let `A` be unital, simple, separable, nuclear, and `Z`-stable.  Let `B` be a
unital C\*-algebra with finite unit and no normalized quasitraces.  If there
is a unital \*-homomorphism `phi:A->B`, then `phi` is not
`O_infinity`-stable.  Consequently, this pair `(A,B)` is a counterexample to
Problem LXXVIII.
