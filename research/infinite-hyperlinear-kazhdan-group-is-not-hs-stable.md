If a group `G` is infinite, hyperlinear, and has property (T), then `G` is not
even locally stable with respect to unitary groups equipped with the
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt metric.

In particular it is not strictly same-dimension HS-stable.  The theorem still
does not rule out flexible HS stability, and it does not say that a stable
ambient group cannot contain a Kazhdan subgroup.  Those distinctions are
essential in applying it to subgroup-pair constructions.
