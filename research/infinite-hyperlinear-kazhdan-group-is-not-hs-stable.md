If a finitely generated group `G` is infinite, hyperlinear, and has property
(T), then `G` is not stable with respect to unitary groups equipped with the
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt metric.

This is the strict same-dimension notion.  It does not rule out flexible
Hilbert--Schmidt stability, and it does not say that a stable group cannot
contain a Kazhdan subgroup.  Those distinctions are essential in applying the
theorem to subgroup-pair constructions.
