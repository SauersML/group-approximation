Let `S` be a finite nonabelian simple group generated by two injective marked
copies

```text
i_1,i_2:A8 -> S
```

satisfying

```text
s_0=s_11=s_30=s_44=s_55=c_19243=1.                   (S6I1)
```

Then

```text
[S:i_1(A8)]=[S:i_2(A8)] >= 91,
|S| >= 91*20160 = 1834560.                            (S6I2)
```

This is CFSG/ATLAS-dependent.  Filtering the complete finite-simple order
table through `|A10|` by `20160 | |S|` gives

```text
S                  candidate index
A8                               1
PSL_3(4)                         1   (same order, nonisomorphic)
A9                               9
PSL_2(64)                       13
M22                             22
J2                              30
Sp_6(2)                         72
A10                             90.                  (S6I3)
```

The target is strictly larger than `A8`; `PSL_3(4)` cannot contain `A8`
because the two groups have equal order and are nonisomorphic.  The other
small rows are removed as follows.

* Dickson's maximal-subgroup classification for `PSL_2(64)` leaves Borel,
  dihedral, subfield, and the listed exceptional subgroups.  None contains
  `A8`: the proper subfields of `F_64` give only `PSL_2(2)`, `PSL_2(4)`, and
  `PSL_2(8)`, while the exceptional rows have order smaller than `20160`.
* The official ATLAS maximal-subgroup list of `M22` has only one maximal
  order divisible by `20160`, namely `|PSL_3(4)|=20160`; equality would again
  force the false isomorphism `A8~=PSL_3(4)`.
* Every maximal subgroup of `J2` has order at most `6048<20160`.
* In `A9` and `A10`, restriction of the natural action to any embedded `A8`
  has one natural eight-point orbit plus fixed points.  The established
  natural-orbit mixing theorem excludes the six words on generated
  components of degree at most eleven.
* Every `A8<Sp_6(2)` is the derived subgroup of an orthogonal maximal `S8`
  and belongs to one conjugacy class.  The exact six-word census over every
  relative conjugator and both marking parities has no survivor.

Thus every row in `(S6I3)` is impossible, proving `(S6I2)`.

Sources for the ambient classification are the GAP Character Table Library
complete simple-order table; Michael Giudici, *Maximal subgroups of almost
simple groups with socle PSL(2,q)*, arXiv:math/0703685; and the official
ATLAS maximal-subgroup tables for
[`M22`](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M22/) and
[`J2`](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/spor/J2/).  The `Sp_6(2)`
subgroup-class assertion is the established
`atlas-a8-index-33-to-89-is-orthogonal-sp6` classification.

Combined with `atlas-six-relator-finite-escape-is-simple-test`, this proves
that any exact finite-dimensional five-row phase escape forces a finite
simple quotient with `A8` index at least `91`.  It remains an exact-zero
restriction: an approximate normalized-HS escape need not converge inside
one fixed finite target.
