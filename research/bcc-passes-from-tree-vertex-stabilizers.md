title: "Baum–Connes with coefficients passes from vertex stabilizers to a countable group acting on a tree"
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/bc-nonhyperlinearity-integration-2026-09-20.md
---


Let P be a countable discrete group acting without inversion on a
simplicial tree. If every vertex stabilizer satisfies Baum–Connes with
coefficients, then P does too. No amenability, finite index,
quasiconvexity, or local-finiteness requirement on edge stabilizers is
added. This is the coefficient theorem; ordinary assembly by itself
has a different edge-and-vertex formulation.
