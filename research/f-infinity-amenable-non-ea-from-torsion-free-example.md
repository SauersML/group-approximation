Specialization. A torsion-free group that is amenable, not elementary amenable,
and of type `F_∞` is in particular an amenable non-EA group of type `F_∞`. The
prerequisite is strictly stronger than the target: it adds torsion-freeness,
which puts every example in one of the two cases recorded in
`there-is-torsion-free-f-infinity-amenable-non-ea-group`. Grigorchuk-type
torsion candidates fall under the target only.
