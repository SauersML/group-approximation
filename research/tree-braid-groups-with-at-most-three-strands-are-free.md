If `T` is a finite tree and `n < 4`, then `UD_nT` strong deformation retracts
onto a graph, so `B_nT` is a free group. The proof is Farley–Sabalka's
Theorem 4.3: critical cells of their discrete gradient field have dimension
`≤ min(⌊n/2⌋, #essential vertices)`.
