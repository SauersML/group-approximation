Let `H <= SL_3(Z)` have Zariski closure different from `SL_3`. Then at least
one of the following holds.

- **(V)** `H` is virtually polycyclic.
- **(F)** `H <= SO(q)(Q)` for a rational ternary quadratic form `q` that is
  indefinite over `R`, and `H` is discrete in `SO(q)(R) ≅ SO(2,1)`.
- **(P)** `H` preserves a rational line or a rational plane of `Q^3`, so it
  is conjugate in `SL_3(Z)` into a maximal parabolic subgroup.
