**Source.** H. Matui, *Topological full groups of one-sided shifts of finite type*,
J. reine angew. Math. 705 (2015), arXiv:1210.5800.

**How it was read.** The arXiv e-print TeX was fetched on MSI on 2026-09-14 into
`/scratch.global/sauer354/hl-bh-a2-boundary-hosts/src/1210.5800/main.tex`. Quotes are from
that file.

**Verbatim.**
- Abstract: "We explore the topological full group $[[G]]$ of an essentially principal
  \'etale groupoid $G$ on a Cantor set. [...] Furthermore, when $G$ is either almost finite
  or purely infinite, the commutator subgroup $D([[G]])$ is shown to be simple."
- Theorem `simple2` (TeX l.1466–1471): "Suppose that $G$ is purely infinite and minimal.
  Then any non-trivial subgroup of $[[G]]$ normalized by the commutator subgroup
  $D([[G]])$ contains $D([[G]])$. In particular, $D([[G]])$ is simple."
- Definition `pi` (TeX l.1194–1202): "A clopen set $A\subset G^{(0)}$ is said to be
  properly infinite if there exist compact open $G$-sets $U,V\subset G$ such that
  $s(U)=s(V)=A$, $r(U)\cup r(V)\subset A$ and $r(U)\cap r(V)=\emptyset$. We say that $G$ is
  purely infinite if every clopen set $A\subset G^{(0)}$ is properly infinite."
