For an approximate finite-dimensional model of the ascending HNN self-copy,
let `A` be the algebra generated by the parent tuple and `A_0` the algebra
generated by the conjugated child tuple.  Prove a modulus `omega(epsilon)->0`,
independent of matrix dimension and representation type, which either gives

```text
d_KK(A_0,A) <= omega(epsilon)
```

or directly forces the sibling Pauli generators simultaneously close to `A_0'`
and to the transported parent algebra.  Together with the exact theorem at
`compression-defect-dies-in-finite-dimensions`, this would force the sibling
commutator to collapse in every operator-norm microstate.

A normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt expander gap is not this statement.  The required
estimate is operator-norm and must survive arbitrary spectator amplification.
