For an approximate finite-dimensional model of the ascending HNN self-copy,
let `A` be the algebra generated by the parent tuple and `A_0` the algebra
generated by the conjugated child tuple.  The calibration
`bare-self-copy-mf-calibration` shows that a dimension-independent estimate of
the form `d_KK(A_0,A) <= omega(epsilon)` is not enough: its explicit matrices
have `A_0 = A` exactly while the marked sibling remains distance `1` from the
common commutant.  The surviving target must therefore directly force the
sibling Pauli generators simultaneously close to `A_0'` and to the transported
parent algebra, with a modulus independent of matrix dimension and
representation type.  Together with the exact Lean endpoint
`map_commutator_eq_one_of_commutes_compressed_units`, wired into
`compression-defect-dies-in-finite-dimensions`, this would force the sibling
commutator to collapse in every operator-norm microstate.

A normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt expander gap is not this statement.  The required
estimate is operator-norm and must survive arbitrary spectator amplification.
