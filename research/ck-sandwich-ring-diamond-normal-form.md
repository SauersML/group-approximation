Let `T` be a group, `1 != g in T`, and

    U = Z[T]<p,q> / (p(1-g)q - 1),

the sandwich ring of `ck-steinberg-marked-cover`.

1. **Basis.** `U` is a free Z-module. A basis is given by the words

       t_0 x_1 t_1 x_2 ... x_k t_k      (k >= 0, t_i in T, x_i in {p,q})

   that contain no consecutive segment `x_i t_i x_(i+1) = p g q`. Here
   `t_i = 1` means that the two letters are adjacent. In particular
   `T -> U^x` is injective and `U != 0`.
2. **Word problem.** If `T` is finitely generated with solvable word problem,
   then `U` has solvable word problem. That is, equality of ring words in the
   generators of `T` and `p, q` is decidable. Conversely, if `U` does, then so
   does `T`.
3. **Matrix groups.** If `T` is finitely generated with solvable word problem,
   then for every `n` every finitely generated subgroup of `GL_n(U)` has
   solvable word problem. In particular `EL_n(U)`, `n >= 3`, generated by the
   elementary matrices over a finite generating set of `U`, has solvable word
   problem.
