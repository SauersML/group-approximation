**ESTABLISHED SINGLE-MARK ATTACHMENT FENCE.**  Let

```text
P=<K,J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3>
```

be the finite full-Q actor packet from
`full-q-separate-actor-packet-retains-zero-cross-central-block`, with
signed corner `F` and relative word `g=J_1J_2`.  There is an exact
finite-dimensional representation `pi` such that

```text
pi(F)!=0,                     pi(FgF)=0.               (COM1)
```

Choose one of the negative center-chain roots

```text
m=x_65(q),                    q=s_1t_1.                (COM2)
```

It belongs to `K`, satisfies `mF=-F`, and is a fixed root-position Weyl
conjugate of the prescribed mark `z=x_13(q)`.  In particular `pi(m)` is a
nonidentity involution with a nonzero negative eigenspace.

Now adjoin the genuine common-opposite binary Morita return

```text
S_i=x_(6,k_i)(s_i),        T_i=x_(k_i,5)(t_i),
C_i=[S_i,T_i]=x_65(q_i),   C_0C_1=x_65(1)=N,
D=x_56(1),
U_i=[D,S_i],               V_i=[T_i,D],
R_i=[U_i,V_i]=x_56(q_i),   R_0R_1=D,
NDN=DND,                                             (COM3)
```

where `q_i=s_it_i`, `k_0=9`, and `k_1=10`.  Identify its marked child
`C_1` literally with `m=x_65(q)`.  Equations `(COM3)`
use both uncancelled binary branches, completeness, one common reverse
parent, both native returns, and the parent Whitehead braid.  They are
ordinary consequences of the full binary-Leavitt Steinberg presentation
and lie outside the finite packet `P`.

Even this union has an exact finite-dimensional unitary model retaining
`(COM1)`.  The reason is that the common-opposite table has a finite model
in

```text
B=GL_3(F_2) times GL_3(F_2)
```

whose marked child `C_1` is a nonidentity involution.  After adding trivial
summands and amplifying the `P`- and `B`-representations, their restrictions
to the shared group `<m>=<C_1>~=C_2` are unitarily identical.  Put the two
representations on that common finite-dimensional Hilbert space and
identify the two marked involutions.  The universal property of the
amalgamated presentation then realizes every `P` row and every row in
`(COM3)` exactly.

The extra trivial `P` summands do not change the zero type: since `F` is the
idempotent of a nontrivial character, it is zero in the trivial
representation.  Hence the amplified union still satisfies

```text
F!=0,                         FJ_1J_2F=0.              (COM4)
```

Thus a genuine full-Leavitt relation outside `P`, even the smallest one
placing both branches on one shared opposite-root parent, does not exclude
the zero-cross type when it meets `P` only through the marked root.  A live
relation must share at least one additional typed occurrence with the
full-Q packet: for example an actual source/target Hecke arm, one native
Whitehead factor, or a paid-sheet detector whose compression to `F` is
forced.  Merely adding more finite marked cells through `z` is closed under
the same amplification-and-amalgamation escape.

This is a scoped finite-dimensional model of the union of the two displayed
ordinary-word tables, not a representation of the whole Steinberg group.
No Property `(T)`, canonical-trace substitution, local computation, or
literature theorem is used.
