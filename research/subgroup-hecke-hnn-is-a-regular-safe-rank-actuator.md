**ESTABLISHED ALGEBRAIC ACTUATOR.**  Let a base group `B` contain finite
subgroups `K,H` and an injective homomorphism `phi:K->H`.  Form the HNN
extension

```text
G=<B,u | u^(-1) k u=phi(k), k in K>.                  (SHA1)
```

Put the subgroup Reynolds projections

```text
p=e_H=|H|^(-1)sum_(h in H)h,
q=e_K=|K|^(-1)sum_(k in K)k.                           (SHA2)
```

Then the ordinary HNN relations give the exact one-sided corner identity

```text
q u p=u p.                                             (SHA3)
```

Hence every finite-dimensional representation of `B` which extends through
the edge satisfies

```text
dim Fix(H) <= dim Fix(K).                              (SHA4)
```

After normalized-HS exactification of the two fixed finite subgroup tables,
the statement has a sharp approximate form.  If
`epsilon_k=||U^*pi(k)U-pi(phi(k))||_2`, then

```text
max(0,tau(p)-tau(q))
 <= ||(1-q)Up||_2^2
 <= (|K|^(-1)sum_k epsilon_k)^2
 <= |K|^(-1)sum_k epsilon_k^2.                         (SHA5)
```

Thus any typewise reverse fixed-space gap is paid by ordinary relator energy
with constant one, independently of ambient dimension.

This actuator passes the regular firewall.  Britton's lemma embeds `B` in
`G`, and in the canonical trace

```text
tau_G(p)=1/|H| <= 1/|K|=tau_G(q),                      (SHA6)
```

exactly because `phi` is injective.  Multiple such HNN letters share one
exact completion and still embed `B`.  More strongly, any separated unitary
representation of `B` can be induced to the multiple HNN extension, so a
properly infinite/nontracial completeness representation can retain the
original mark.

## Exact Schur interface

If one constructs finite surrogate groups `H_f,K_f` on the adjoint packet
carrier such that

```text
Fix(H_f)=Ran(R_A),   Fix(K_f)=Ran(R_B),
K_f embeds in H_f,                                      (SHA7)
```

then `(SHA5)` immediately converts the forbidden Schur rank gap
`rank(R_A)>rank(R_B)` into the required dimension-free group-relator floor.
This is a concrete finite representation-theory target; no proper-isometry
or trace-return estimate remains after `(SHA7)`.

The native pair does not satisfy it.  Its packet groups obey `A_f<=B_f`, so
the available injection gives the harmless direction

```text
source R_B -> target R_A.
```

The paid direction would require an injection `B_f->A_f`, impossible when
the predicate gate strictly enlarges the finite group.  Padding `A_f` by a
dummy finite factor fixes abstract order but also adds representation data;
unless that factor is payload-coupled, its regular companion types restore a
stationary flow.  Constructing surrogates satisfying `(SHA7)` on allowed and
forbidden sectors is therefore the precise remaining compiler.

## Literal involution spectral projections do not suffice

Let `a,b` be nontrivial involutions and

```text
p=(1+epsilon a)/2,   q=(1+delta b)/2.
```

If `qup=up` is a universal identity in the faithful complex group algebra,
put `c=u^(-1)bu`.  Comparing the support of

```text
delta c(1+epsilon a)=1+epsilon a                       (SHA8)
```

shows that either `c=1` and the conjugated target is the whole space, or
`c=a`, `delta=epsilon`, and the two spectral projections are equal.  There
is no proper third case.  Therefore the useful connector is the additive
subgroup Reynolds projection `(SHA2)` (or a separately wordized finite-type
corner), not two bare named involution half-projections.

## Attempts

- The V4 character example already exhibits a reverse type gap, but an HNN
  conjugacy only enforces equality and finite companion characters restore
  the regular flow.
- Subgroup averages give genuine one-sided inclusion and the exact estimate
  `(SHA5)` without contradicting `(SHA6)`.
- Applying the construction directly to `A_f<=B_f` gives the wrong direction.
- Enlarging the source group by a trivially acting factor destroys common
  completeness unless its action is coupled to the predicate; that coupling
  is the remaining nontransport incidence, not free order padding.
