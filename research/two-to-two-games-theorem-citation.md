Literature import, credited in full.

Subhash Khot, Dor Minzer, Muli Safra, *Pseudorandom Sets in Grassmann Graph
have Near-Perfect Expansion*, Electronic Colloquium on Computational Complexity,
Report No. 6 (2018). Conference version: FOCS 2018.

Verified from the ECCC PDF, page 2, on 2026-09-12:

* Definition 1.3 defines `2-to-2 Game[F_2^l]`, with constraints
  `T_ij x_i + T'_ij x_j in {b_ij, b'_ij}` for invertible `T_ij, T'_ij`.
* **Theorem 1.4.** "For every constant `eps > 0`, there exists a sufficiently
  large integer `l = l(eps)` such that Gap 2-to-2`[F_2^l](1-eps, eps)` is
  NP-hard."
* The text states that Theorem 1.4 holds "thanks to the main result of this
  work and the previous works", references [12, 3, 4] and [2]. The abstract
  says the paper "completes the proof of the 2-to-2 Games Conjecture (albeit
  with imperfect completeness)".

Nothing here reproves the theorem.
