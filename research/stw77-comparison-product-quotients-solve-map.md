Let `(B_lambda)_(lambda in Lambda)` be any family of nonzero unital simple
C*-algebras such that every `W(B_lambda)` is almost unperforated, and put

```text
P=product_(lambda in Lambda) B_lambda.
```

If `1_P` is properly infinite, then `P` is strongly purely infinite.  More
strongly, for every closed two-sided ideal `J` with `P/J` nonzero,

```text
P/J is strongly purely infinite.                              (1)
```

Consequently, if `A` is separable and exact, every nuclear homomorphism

```text
phi:A->P/J
```

is strongly `O_infinity`-stable.  In particular, every unital
finite-nuclear-dimensional map into `P/J` satisfies the conclusion of STW
LXXVII.  For this target class the problem's hypothesis on every nonzero
positive range element can be replaced by the single ambient assumption that
`1_P` is properly infinite.

The index set may be uncountable, the quotient may have no coordinate
description, and no separability, nuclearity, exactness, or finite ideal
lattice is assumed for `P`, `J`, or `P/J`.
