Balanced `b`-fold copying multiplies source dimension, target dimension,
kernel dimension, and squared unnormalized return norm by the same factor
`b`.  Square padding increases source and target dimensions equally and
therefore leaves the excess unchanged while increasing the denominator.
Taking the corresponding ratios proves monotonicity of deficiency density;
normalizing the direct-sum Hilbert--Schmidt norm proves conservation of the
return error for equal copies.
