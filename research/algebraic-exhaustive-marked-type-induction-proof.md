title: Induce a rejected selector character first to the finite packet and then to the ambient group
target: algebraic-exhaustive-marked-type-detectors-violate-induction
requires: []
---

Choose a rejected marked character `chi` of the selector subgroup.  A marked
irreducible `lambda` containing it occurs in `Ind_A^H chi`; equivalently
`q_lambda p_bad q_lambda` is nonzero.  Induce `lambda` from `H` to the
ambient group.  The identity-coset copy retains this nonzero finite-factor
operator.  Every genuine group relator is the identity on the induced
representation, however, so the left side of any exact typewise relator
domination estimate is zero.  This contradicts a positive lower bound.

Balanced SMC rows do not evade the argument: their external multiplicity
operators cancel algebraically, and Schur factorization works on the
infinite induced multiplicity just as it does in matrices.
