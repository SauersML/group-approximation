## Setting

This follows Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir, Bull. Math. Sci. 7 (2017), §4.2, pp. 338--341.
- `Š` is the semigroup of `S(MM_k)` without the Minsky relations.
- `W` is the set of nonzero elements of `Š` containing a q-letter, and `W_0 = psi(W)` in
  `S(MM_k)`.
- `T_1` is the elementary abelian `p`-group with basis `z(i, w)`, where
  `i in {1,2,3}^k` and `w in W ∪ W_0`.
- `M <= Aut(T_1)` is the group generated by the automorphisms assigned to the letters
  `L_1 ∪ L_2`.
- For `w in W`, `|w|_a` is the total exponent of the a-letters in the normal form
  `q_i a_1^(m_1) ... a_k^(m_k) A_1^(beta_1) ... A_k^(beta_k)`. It is well defined by KMS
  Lemma 3.15(b).

## Statement

1. The KMS homomorphism `gamma : G(MM_k) -> T_1 x| M` is an isomorphism, and it carries
   `T` onto `T_1`.
2. For `n >= 0` let `U_n` be the span of the `z(i, w)` with `w in W_0`, or with `w in W`
   and `|w|_a >= n`. Each `U_n` is `M`-invariant, and `U_(n+1)` is a proper subspace of
   `U_n`.
3. So `M` has infinitely many orbits on `T_1`, and the affine action of
   `G(MM_k) ≅ T_1 x| M` on `T_1` is not of type (A).

## Meaning

This settles Attempt 2 of `research/artifacts/solve-bh-apapa-2026-09-13.md`, which was
recorded there as "not decided". The obvious translation placement of the hard
examples of `fp-rf-apapa-groups-satisfy-boone-higman` is not a type (A) actor.

Any affine actor containing `G(MM_k)` needs a larger module or a larger complement.
- **Finitely many element orders** are a necessary condition on the module (item 4 of
  `orbit-finite-affine-actions-are-type-a`).
- **Finitely many orbits** would force finitely many submodules, since each orbit spans
  its own cyclic submodule, and the cyclic submodules then determine every submodule.
