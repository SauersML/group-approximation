`w!=1`.  Suppose every sufficiently accurate canonical normalized-HS
microstate which keeps `w` separated produces projections `E_n` and constants
`c,delta>0` such that

```text
liminf tr_ad(E_n)>=c,
||(Ad(u_n(h))-1)E_n||_2 ->0             (h in tQt^(-1)),
liminf ||(Ad(u_n(q_0))-1)E_n||_2>=delta (some fixed q_0 in Q).
```

Then `G` is non-hyperlinear.  Indeed canonical microstates separate every
nonidentity word, while
`tracial-superoperator-self-copy-has-no-macroscopic-excess` makes the last
two lines incompatible.
