**THEOREM.**  Let `Gamma^` be one of the five simple Titz Mite--Witzel
CAT(0) Kazhdan groups.  Exactly one of the following holds:

```text
(a) Gamma^ is not hyperlinear — an explicit finitely presented
    torsion-free SIMPLE non-hyperlinear group, closing the goal; or
(b) Gamma^ is hyperlinear, and then C^*(Gamma^) fails the LLP (while it
    fails LP unconditionally), Gamma^ is not weakly ucp-stable, and any
    eventual LLP proof for any group in the class would separate LLP
    from LP for group C*-algebras.                               (TWD1)
```

In case (b) the LLP failure is of a kind not covered by any published
mechanism: `Gamma^` is simple (so the Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma
finite-dimensional realizability condition is vacuous — scalar-image
projective representations only), torsion-free (so no char-p module
pair exists), normalizes no distorted `Z^k`
(`twin-lattices-exclude-known-llp-fences` part (b)), and has property A
subgroups (part (d)), so neither known LLP-failure family embeds.  The
candidate is therefore strictly sharper than the Kac--Moody one on every
axis on which they differ: uniform, torsion-free, explicit small
presentations (15 generators, 24 short relations at q = 2), LP-status
decided, and the same closed refutation surface.
