Citation import, not a reproof. Robert Spahn and Matthew C. B. Zaremsky, *The
BNSR-invariants of the Stein group F_{2,3}*, arXiv:2012.05000. From `main.tex` fetched on
MSI:

- l.163 (definition): "The \emph{Stein group} $F_{2,3}$ is the subgroup of
  $\Homeo([0,1])$ consisting of all orientation-preserving piecewise linear
  homeomorphisms with breakpoints in $\Z[\frac{1}{6}]$ and slopes in the multiplicative
  group $\langle 2,3\rangle_{\Q^\times}=\{2^p3^q\mid p,q\in\Z\}\le \Q^\times$."
- l.168: "An important result Stein proved in \cite{stein92} is that $F_{2,3}$ is of type
  $\F_\infty$. Recall that a group is of \emph{type $F_n$} if it admits a classifying
  space with compact $n$-skeleton, ... type $\F_2$ is equivalent to finite
  presentability."
- Bibliography, l.564–567: "Melanie Stein. Groups of piecewise linear homeomorphisms.
  Trans. Amer. Math. Soc., 332(2):477--514, 1992."

Hyde--Skipper--Zaremsky, arXiv:2605.20564, l.188 give the same definition.
