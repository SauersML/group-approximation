ESTABLISHED (route `amenable-symbol-subgroups-keep-the-jacobson-head-proof`).
This is an obstruction for [[property-t-free-jacobson-head-collapse]] (HC).
It does not decide HC.

Notation: `J = F_2<S,T | TS=1>`, `Q = 1-ST`, `I = span{S^a Q T^b}`, and
`sigma : J -> J/I = F_2[z,z^-1]` is the symbol map. Here `x_13(Q)` is the
head, and `N_n = ker(EL_n(J) -> SL_n(F_2[z,z^-1]))`.

**Theorem A (amenable symbol, head kept).** Let `H <= GL_n(J)` be a
countable subgroup whose symbol image `sigma(H)` is amenable. Then:

1. `H` is amenable.
2. `H` has an injective norm-corona representation.
3. Every nontrivial involution of `H`, including the head whenever
   `x_13(Q)` lies in `H`, is at operator-norm distance exactly 2 from
   the identity.

**Corollary A1 (the P22 pigeonhole has no MF shadow).** Let `H0` be the
group generated by the w12 bus certificate (p22-double-parabolic-is-not-lef, not yet landed):
`U = M_2(J)`, together with `g = diag([[S,Q],[0,T]],1_2)`, `e = x_12(1)`,
`x_34(1)` and `x_43(S)`. Then `H0` is amenable and contains the head. The w12
pigeonhole runs inside `H0` (with `A = {x_13(r)x_14(r') : r,r' in F_2[S]}`:
`gAg^-1 <= A`, `e` centralizes `A`), so
every homomorphism from `H0` to a finite group kills the head, and `H0`
is not residually finite. Even so, `H0` is MF with the head kept. So the conjugation-pigeonhole
relations (`Ad g` a proper self-map of the right-orbit group `A`, `e`
centralizing `A`, and `[e, g^-1 u g] = x_13(Q)`) cannot force head
collapse in norm-corona representations. This answers the w12 spark
"does the pigeonhole have an MF shadow?" with no.

**Theorem B (Borel amalgams over the symbol kernel).** Let `B_n^+` and
`B_n^-` be the preimages in `EL_n(J)` of the upper and lower triangular
subgroups of `SL_n(F_2[z,z^-1])`. The head lies in `N_n`, and `N_n` is
normal in both `B_n^+` and `B_n^-`. By Schafhauser (Theorem 1.3 of
arXiv:2306.02498) the amalgam `Lambda_n = B_n^+ *_{N_n} B_n^-` is MF. It
contains the head as an element of the amalgamated subgroup and surjects
onto `EL_n(J)` for every `n >= 2`.

The same construction works inside `P22(J) = M_2(J) semidirect
(EL_2(J) x EL_2(J))`. With `C0 = U semidirect (N_2 x N_2)`,
the amalgam `(U semidirect (B^+ x B^+)) *_{C0} (U semidirect (B^- x B^-))`
is MF, keeps the head, and surjects onto `P22(J)`.

**The invariant, and where every member dies.** The invariant is the
symbol image. Take any argument for HC, or for a P22-local version of it,
that only uses relations holding in a group `H` with amenable symbol image.
Every such argument dies at the step that derives `pi(x_13(Q)) = 1`,
because Theorem A gives a faithful model of `H` in which the head sits at
distance 2. The same applies to any argument that only uses the relations
of the upper Borel preimage and the lower Borel preimage separately,
glued along the symbol kernel: Theorem B gives a faithful model of
`Lambda_n` or of the P22 amalgam.

So a norm-corona kill of the head must use a relation in the kernel of
`Lambda_n -> EL_n(J)`, a genuinely Borel-mixing relation. This rules out:

* every rank or conjugation pigeonhole on a certificate with triangular
  symbol, including the w6 locally finite certificate and the w12 P22
  certificate;
* every argument that treats the upper and lower unipotent packets as
  separately given groups.

The Steinberg commutator `[x_12(a), x_21(b)]`, the Weyl elements and
the Kazhdan-type spectral gap are exactly such mixing relations. That is
consistent with the known (T)-based proof of the head statement.

**What it does not do.** It does not show that `P22(J)`, `H_mix =
U semidirect (EL_2(J) x <x_34(1), x_43(S)>)` or `EL_5(J)` is MF, and it
does not refute HC. Quotients of MF groups need not be MF.
