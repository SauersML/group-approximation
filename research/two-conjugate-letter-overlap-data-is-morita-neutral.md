**ESTABLISHED TWO-LETTER TENSOR ESCAPE.**  Let `A` be generated by a fixed
unitary tuple `u=(u_s)_(s in S)`, and let `(X,B)` be an `m`-dimensional
matrix-range test tuple with gap

```text
Delta_m(B;X)=Re sum_s tr_m(B_s^*X_s)-h_m(B).             (TLM1)
```

For every finite word window `Omega` and every sufficiently large `k`,
there are finite-dimensional unitaries `T_k,H_k` and

```text
U_k=H_kT_kH_k^*
```

such that:

1. `T_k` and `U_k` have order exactly `k` and uniform `k`-point spectra;
2. every nonidentity word from `Omega` in `T_k,U_k` has normalized trace
   zero, exactly as in the canonical trace of `C_k*C_k`;
3. after tensoring by `C^m`, both letters commute with every operator on
   the multiplicity factor; and
4. for

```text
X~_s=I tensor X_s,          B~_s=I tensor B_s,
```

one has

```text
Delta(B~;X~)=Delta_m(B;X).                                (TLM2)
```

Consequently the spectral multiplicity blocks of `T_k` and
`U_k=H_kT_kH_k^*`, their joint canonical finite-window moments, and the
fact that both centralize a common actor do not yield any two-letter
support-function inequality.  An arbitrary Choi separator survives on the
common multiplicity factor.

Apply this with the actor equal to a matrix model of

```text
C=Lambda cap hLambda h^(-1).
```

The local subgroup generated by the two letters and `C` has the same
finite-window trace law as `C direct_product (Z*Z)`: the common actor may
even be canonical while the separator remains untouched.  Hence the local
Iwahori overlap and canonical pair moments cannot prove
`sl3-hnn-central-dual-gap-vanishes`.

This is a scoped no-go, not a counterexample to the full HNN claim.  In the
tensor model `H_k` acts trivially on the multiplicity factor.  It therefore
does not realize the required denominator conjugation of the two lattice
vertices outside `C`.  The exact surviving arithmetic datum is the action
of `phi(h)` on `Lambda\C` (equivalently the compatibility of the two full
Iwahori endpoint representations), not the two letter block systems or
their common overlap alone.
