Take `L = Out(F_n)` itself, a subgroup of finite index 1. This is the converse of
`out-fm-aut-embedding-via-virtual-embedding`; the two routes record that the
Boone--Higman premise `out-free-groups-virtually-embed-in-aut-free-groups` is
equivalent, rank by rank, to the first question of Zaremsky Problem 3.10 having a
positive answer. The resulting cycle is intended; do not delete either route.
