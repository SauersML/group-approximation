**ESTABLISHED SHORT-CERTIFICATE FENCE.**  In the full signed source put

```text
F=Q(1+r)(1+l)/4,              g=J_1J_2,
h=FgF,                         A=A_1=x_47(s_1t_0).     (A1C1)
```

The root position of `A` is disjoint from those of `r,l`, and the exact
common-source calculation gives `AQ=QA`.  Hence

```text
AF=FA.                                                (A1C2)
```

Since `A` is an involution, `u=FAF` is a unitary in the unital corner

```text
C=F C[Delta] F,               1_C=F.                  (A1C3)
```

It follows immediately that

```text
F(AgA)F=u h u^*,
Ideal_C(h, uhu^*)=Ideal_C(h).                         (A1C4)
```

More generally every finite family obtained from `h` by left and right
multiplication with polynomials in `u` lies in the same two-sided ideal.
Thus no positive identity assembled only from `A`-conjugates of `h` can
prove that `h` has full central support: such an identity would already be
an identity in the old ideal generated by `h`.

The common `A_1` suffix in the two Hecke words has the same exact
limitation.  Write

```text
u_1=B_2A,                 u_2=B_3A_2A,
v_1=B_2,                  v_2=B_3A_2.                 (A1C5)
```

Then `(A1C2)` gives

```text
u_iF=v_iF A,                                        (A1C6)
(u_iF)(u_iF)^*=v_iFv_i^*,
(u_1F)^*(u_2F)=u (Fv_1^*v_2F) u.                     (A1C7)
```

Consequently the two self-Gram range projections are completely independent
of the common tail, and the mixed source Gram is only internally conjugate
to the tail-free one.  Its kernel, rank, spectral mass, and central support
are unchanged.  The common suffix is therefore not a short positive
certificate for `FgF`.

The paid sheet commutator

```text
K_A=[A,H]=x_43((s_1t_0)h_coeff)                       (A1C8)
```

does leave the old packet algebra and is globally nontrivial.  But
normalization `(A1C2)`, global marked-root rank, and even zero `H`-leakage
still do not make it positive on `F`: the exact four-dimensional model in
`row2-source-factor-detects-paid-pauli-sheet` has

```text
F(K_A-1)F=0,
K_A!=1,                   z=V K_A V^(-1),             (A1C9)
```

after taking `F=Q` in that scoped compression table.  Hence `(A1C8)` also
cannot replace a mixed source-incidence relation.

This sharply locates the new positive `A_1` signal.  The canonical
full-support calculation cannot be explained by `A_1`-conjugates of `h`,
by the common right tail in `u_1,u_2`, or by global nontriviality of
`[A_1,H]`.  Any literal positive certificate must use a relation in which
`A_1` meets an actor that moves the `F`-corner, most economically

```text
[B_2,A_1]=X_1,
J_1=X_1Y_1X_1,
(J_1B_2A_1)^7=1,                                     (A1C10)
```

and must prove that the resulting off-corner words cover `F`.  Equivalently,
one must exhibit a finite family `w_j` containing such mixed actors and an
ordinary-word-derived bound

```text
F <= C_0 sum_j (h w_j F)^*(h w_j F)                  (A1C11)
```

or the left-right ideal analogue.  Formula `(A1C11)` is not proved here;
the claim proves that every version with all `w_j` inside the `A_1` corner
is tautological.

This is a scoped obstruction to the shortest proposed certificate, not a
countermodel to the complete canonical `A_1` extension and not a
representation of the whole Steinberg group.  No Property `(T)`, quotient
rank computation, canonical trace, local computation, or literature theorem
is used.
