**ESTABLISHED (proof in `rover-nekrashevych-characters-are-sum-invariant-proof`; unreviewed).**

Let `G <= Aut(T_d)` be self-similar and faithful, with `d >= 2`, `X = {1,...,d}`,
`C = X^N`. For `g in G` and a cone `uC`, let `g_(u)` act by `u w -> u g(w)` on `uC` and
as the identity elsewhere. It lies in `V_d(G)`.

1. For `χ in Hom(G,R)`, the function `(ψχ)(g) = Σ_{x in X} χ(g|_x)` is again a homomorphism.
2. Fix a nonempty word `u`. The map `Φ -> (g -> Φ(g_(u)))` is a linear isomorphism
   `Hom(V_d(G), R) -> {χ in Hom(G,R) : ψχ = χ}`, and it does not depend on `u`. The inverse
   sends `χ` to `Φ_χ(h) = Σ_i χ(g_i)` for any table `h(u_i w) = v_i g_i(w)` of `h`.
3. Every real character vanishes on `V_d`.

**Consequences.**

- `S(V_d(G))` is nonempty iff `ψ` has eigenvalue 1 on `Hom(G,R)`. Its dimension is that of
  the eigenspace.
- The sphere is empty when `G` is generated by torsion elements, for example the
  Grigorchuk group, where Röver's group has only finite abelian quotients.
- Binary odometer `a = σ(1, a)`: `ψχ(a) = χ(1) + χ(a) = χ(a)`, so `Hom(V_2(Z), R) = R`.
  `Φ_χ` counts the total `a`-exponent of a table.
- This agrees with item (R) of `rover-nekrashevych-finite-presentation-criteria`:
  regrouping into `m` blocks multiplies `ψ` by `m`. So characters survive iff `1/m` is an
  eigenvalue of the section-sum matrix `A`.
- This is the real part of Nekrashevych's abelianization formula
  (arXiv:1312.5654, Theorem 4.8, as recorded in item (Ab)).
