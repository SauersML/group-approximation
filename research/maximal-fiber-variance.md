title: Exact joint covariance can retain maximal fiber variance
invalidates: [additive-fourier-refinement]

For every proper finite binary inclusion `α : F → G`, represent `G`
diagonally on `ℓ²(Ĝ)`, restrict to `F`, take the compressor to be the
identity: joint `α×α` covariance and every finite-depth path identity are
exact, yet a branch bit balanced on each restriction fiber has
`‖B − E_{A_F}(B)‖₂² = 1/4`, the maximal binary conditional variance. Works
along a whole chain; deeper windows do not remove the fresh Bernoulli
directions. Forces the mixed-relator-energy route relator-energy-variance-bound.
