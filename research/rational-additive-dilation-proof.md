title: Rational additive unitary eigencharacters are constant
target: rational-unitary-additive-dilation-is-trivial

The additive law makes all matrices commute, so simultaneous unitary
diagonalization reduces the problem to homomorphisms
`chi:(Q,+)->S^1` represented by rational functions on an interval.  The
functional equation and rationality make `chi` continuous at a regular point,
hence `chi(t)=exp(i alpha t)`.  A nonconstant exponential cannot agree with a
rational function on an open interval, so `alpha=0`.  Every eigencharacter is
one and the original homomorphism is trivial.
