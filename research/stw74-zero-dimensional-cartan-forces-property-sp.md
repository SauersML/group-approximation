Let `A` be a separable unital C-star algebra admitting a Cartan subalgebra

```text
D ~= C(X)
```

whose spectrum `X` is zero-dimensional.  Then `A` has property (SP): every
nonzero hereditary C-star subalgebra of `A` contains a nonzero projection.

Consequently, suppose in addition that `A` is simple and every nonzero
projection in `A` is infinite.  Then `A` is purely infinite.  Thus Problem
LXXIV has a positive answer within the class of separable unital algebras
admitting a zero-dimensional Cartan subalgebra.

No nuclearity, exactness, amenability of the Weyl groupoid, real rank,
stable rank, comparison, or finite-dimensionality hypothesis is imposed.
