Notation as in the target.

**(SC1).** Synchrony and perfection give `sum_a <P_(x,a) Q_(x,a) xi, xi> = 1`.
Since `P_(x,a)` and `Q_(x,a)` commute,

```text
1 = sum_a <Q_(x,a) xi, P_(x,a) xi>
  <= sum_a ||Q_(x,a) xi|| ||P_(x,a) xi||
  <= (sum_a ||Q_(x,a) xi||^2)^(1/2) (sum_a ||P_(x,a) xi||^2)^(1/2) = 1.
```

Equality in the second inequality forces `||Q_(x,a) xi|| = ||P_(x,a) xi||`
for every `a`. Equality in each Cauchy--Schwarz term then forces
`Q_(x,a) xi = P_(x,a) xi`.

**Reversal.** Let `B_0` be the star-algebra generated by the `Q_(y,b)`. For a
word `w = P_1 ... P_n` in the `P`'s, put `w^flat = Q_n ... Q_1`, where `Q_i`
is the `Q` with the same label as `P_i`. Induction on `n` with `(SC1)` and
`[A_0,B_0]=0` gives

```text
w xi = P_1...P_(n-1) Q_n xi = Q_n P_1...P_(n-1) xi = ... = w^flat xi.   (SCP1)
```

**(SC2).** For words `u,w`, `(SCP1)` applied to `w^*` gives
`w^* xi = (w^flat)^* xi`. Hence

```text
<u w xi, xi> = <u w^flat xi, xi> = <w^flat u xi, xi>
             = <u xi, (w^flat)^* xi> = <u xi, w^* xi> = <w u xi, xi>.
```

**(SC3).** Let `V(a,b|x,y)=0`. Perfection gives `<P_(x,a) Q_(y,b) xi, xi> = 0`.
The product of these commuting projections is a projection, so
`P_(x,a) Q_(y,b) xi = 0`, and by `(SC1)`, `P_(x,a) P_(y,b) xi = 0`. For a word `w`
in `A_0`, `(SCP1)` and commutation give

```text
P_(x,a) P_(y,b) w xi = P_(x,a) P_(y,b) w^flat xi = w^flat P_(x,a) P_(y,b) xi = 0.
```

Hence `P_(x,a) P_(y,b) e = 0`.

The subspace `e H` is `A_0`-invariant, so `e in A_0'`. The kernel of the
normal star-homomorphism `A_0'' -> A_0'' e`, `t -> t e`, is `A_0''(1-z)`, where
`z` is the central support of `e` in `A_0''`. So `t e = 0` implies `t z = 0`.
Therefore `P_(x,a) P_(y,b) z = 0`. The elements `P_(x,a) z` form
projection-valued measures summing to `z`, so
`e_(x,a) -> P_(x,a) z` defines a unital star-homomorphism
`A(G) -> A_0'' z subset z M z`.

**(SC4).** Given a unital `pi : A(G) -> zMz` with `M` on `L^2(M,tau)`, fix an
answer `a_0`. Put `P_(x,a) = pi(e_(x,a)) + delta_(a,a_0)(1-z)`, which is a
projection-valued measure in `M`. Put `Q_(y,b) = J P_(y,b) J` in `M'` and
`xi = z Omega/||z Omega||`. Since `J t J (s Omega) = s t^* Omega`,

```text
<P_(x,a) Q_(y,b) xi, xi> = tau(z P_(x,a) z P_(y,b))/tau(z)
                         = tau(pi(e_(x,a)) pi(e_(y,b)))/tau(z).
```

This vanishes when `V(a,b|x,y)=0`, and the entries sum to 1. So the
correlation is perfect and synchronous.
