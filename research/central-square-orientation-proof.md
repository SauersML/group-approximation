# Square the raw holonomy lift and compare its real and skew parts

For unitary `W`, `W^*(W^2-I)=W-W^*` and
`W^*(W^2+I)=W+W^*`, proving `(CSO4)--(CSO5)`. Exact signs give `(CSO3)`.
The square transforms by a minus sign under `W->iW`, so tying it to the
payload involution removes the complementary-selector symmetry while staying
inside the multiplicity algebra.
