Let `A` be a simple separable unital nuclear non-elementary C-star algebra.
Assume that the extreme boundary of `T(A)` is nonempty, compact, and has
finite covering dimension.  If `A` has strict comparison of positive
elements, then

```text
A is Z-stable.
```

Thus STW Problem XVIII has a positive answer on every compact
finite-dimensional extremal-trace-boundary stratum.  In particular it has a
positive answer for finitely many extremal traces and for a unique trace.
