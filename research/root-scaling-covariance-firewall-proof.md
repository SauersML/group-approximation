Set `Z=1` and `U_j=lambda_j` in dimension one.  Every involution and
commutation relation holds, and both sides of `(RSC1)` are `1` regardless of
`epsilon_j`.  Formula `(RSC3)` is then `1`, whereas `S_Z=0`, contradicting
`(RSC4)` for every `c>0` at zero relator energy.  Sending an independent
central mark to `-1` and every generator of a Heisenberg packet to `1`
preserves all the additional relations.
