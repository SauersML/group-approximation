Let `G` be `F`-linear sofic with `char F = 0`, and `w` nonsingular. Put `L` = an
algebraic closure of `F`, also of characteristic 0.
`amplified-algebraic-roots-in-characteristic-zero` gives `ARR(L)`, and
`linear-sofic-kl-from-amplified-algebraic-roots` with `F <= L` gives injectivity
of `G --> G_w` for countable `G`.

For uncountable `G`, reduce to the countable subgroup generated by a
witnessing identity and the coefficients, exactly as in
`kl-for-linear-sofic-via-amplified-algebraic-roots`.
