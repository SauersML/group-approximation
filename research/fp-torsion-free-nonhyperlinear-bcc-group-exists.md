There is a finitely presented torsion-free group P with cd_Z(P)<=3
which is nonhyperlinear and satisfies Baum–Connes with coefficients.
The witness is the finite Ollivier–Wise double over the preimage of
the native Leavitt subgroup diag(EL_3(R),1)<=EL_4(R)≅R^x.

The positive assembly proof is independent of Liu–Thom normalization;
nonhyperlinearity depends on that named analytic input. Finite
presentation of R^x is the separate Khanh import. Consequently this
witness cannot refute ordinary Baum–Connes. For this P, the canonical
K₀ trace range is ℤ and C*ᵣ(P) has no scalar projections other than 0
and 1. This does not compute all matrix K-classes.
