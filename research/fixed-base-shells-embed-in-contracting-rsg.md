Let P be infinite and finitely generated, with shell envelope E_nu.
Suppose that for some fixed h>=2,r>=2 each actual shell generator
permutation rho_g, and its inverse, has a near class in the faithful
near action of V_(h,r)(Z_odometer) on N. Equivalently, outside
finitely many exceptions its branches are

    i+r*u+r*h^k*t -> j+r*v+r*h^l*(t+c),

with source and target forest-prefix partitions and integer c.

Then E_nu embeds faithfully in H_(h,r), the full contracting RSG
from the terminated-digit nucleus. In particular the WHOLE shell
envelope E_nu embeds in a finitely presented simple group.

This uses a new Cantor action obtained by replacing each eventually
zero limit point by a Cantor fiber. It is not a conjugacy of the
original shell action and does not prove E_nu itself finitely
presented. The fixed-base hypothesis is not available for arbitrary
decidable inputs.
