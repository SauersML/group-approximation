Let A be finite, G residually finite, X any G-set, and
W=(direct_sum_X A) semidirect G. Every H<=W with finite intersection
with direct_sum_X A is residually finite. In particular every
word-hyperbolic subgroup of W is residually finite, as is every
torsion-free subgroup. No quasiconvexity or finite generation of H is
needed for the finite-intersection assertion; A need not be abelian.

The finite-support proof was supplied by the user and checked directly
during the second Problem 141 campaign. It applies to the supplied Liu
wreath because its actor is residually finite, as checked in the artifact.
It is an obstruction to extracting a counterexample as a subgroup of that
wreath, not a proof of residual finiteness for all hyperbolic groups.
