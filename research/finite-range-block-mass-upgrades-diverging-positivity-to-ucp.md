**ESTABLISHED.** Let `S` be a finite-dimensional operator system and let

```text
phi_n:S -> M_(d_n)
```

be unital `k_n`-positive maps.  Put `B_n=C^*(phi_n(S))` and write its
concrete finite-dimensional decomposition as

```text
B_n = direct_sum_j (M_(r_(n,j)) tensor I_(m_(n,j))).       (FRB1)
```

Let `z_n` be the central support of the summands with `r_(n,j)>k_n`, and
put

```text
rho_n=tr_(d_n)(z_n)
     =(1/d_n) sum_(r_(n,j)>k_n) r_(n,j)m_(n,j).             (FRB2)
```

There are ucp maps `psi_n:S->M_(d_n)` such that, for every contraction
`x in S`,

```text
||psi_n(x)-phi_n(x)||_(2,d_n) <= 2 sqrt(rho_n).             (FRB3)
```

Consequently, if `rho_n->_omega 0`, then `(psi_n)` and `(phi_n)` define the
same map into the tracial matrix ultraproduct.  Applied to
`maslov-central-regular-lifts-have-diverging-positive-order`, vanishing
high-degree range mass proves
`maslov-sector-tracial-generator-ucp-liftability`.

The criterion is invariant under common amplification: replacing every
range block by `M_r tensor I_(mL)` changes neither `r` nor its normalized
trace weight.  Thus it survives the amplification fence which destroys
criteria involving `k_n/d_n`.

Equivalently, failure of the desired Maslov lift forces a positive amount of
coordinate trace to lie in irreducible blocks of the algebra generated by
the **fixed root/Weyl images** whose degrees exceed `k_n`.  An escaping Choi
witness on a trace-negligible multiplicity corner is not enough.
