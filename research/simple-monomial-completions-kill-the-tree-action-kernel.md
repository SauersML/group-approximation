**ESTABLISHED.** Let `P` be a group with a wreath recursion
`g |-> (π_g; g|_1, ..., g|_d)`, not necessarily faithful. Let
`psi(g) = Σ_x E_(π_g(x), x)(g|_x)` be the induced recursion on `K[P]`, `O_psi`
its completion, and `N` the kernel of the induced action of `P` on the rooted
tree `X^*`. Then the ideal of `O_psi` generated by `{g - 1 : g in N}` is proper.

Hence, if `O_psi` is simple:
- `P -> O_psi^x` factors through the residually finite group `P/N`;
- every subgroup of `P` with no nontrivial finite quotient, in particular every
  infinite simple subgroup, maps to `1`.

## Consequence for completion hosts

- **The route of `nekrashevych-completions-contain-their-coefficients` needs
  non-monomial recursions.** A Higman overgroup `P` of an infinite simple input
  `S`, with any group-type recursion, gives a simple completion only if `S`
  dies in it.
- **What survives.** Units that act by prefix exchange, such as `V <= V_d(P)`,
  are unaffected. That is the Rover--Nekrashevych shape, whose only open input is
  `decidable-groups-embed-in-rover-nekrashevych-hosts`.
- **The one open mechanism.** New cases can come only from recursions `psi` whose
  matrices `psi(s)` for `s in S` are not monomial. For example, some entry of
  some `psi^n(s) - 1` could be a unit.
