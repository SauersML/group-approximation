Full write-up: artifact §2 (Theorem 1). Here `x_i` is column `i` of `X`, and `y_i` is row `i` of `Y`.
- **(IBN-m) for `L_K(m,n)`.** The import gives `V(L_K(m,n)) = ⟨x | mx = nx⟩` with `x = [1]`. In that monoid
  `ix = jx` iff `i = j` or `i, j ≥ m` with `i ≡ j (mod n − m)`, since the congruence on `ℕ` generated by `m ~ n` is
  already additive. `[R^i] = ix`, and `P ↦ Hom_R(P,R)` transfers the statement between left and right modules.
- **Existence.** An isomorphism `R^M ≅ R^N` with `M ≠ N` forces `M, N ≥ m`. This gives the second bullet.
- **Complement of a column.** `R^N = X(⊕_i e_i R) = ⊕_i x_i R`. Since `y_i x_i = 1`, the map `r ↦ x_i r` is injective,
  so `x_i R ≅ R`, and `x_i R` has complement `⊕_{j≠i} x_j R ≅ R^{M−1}`.
- **Complement after completion.** If `x_i = g e_1` with `g ∈ GL_N(R)`, then `g(e_2R ⊕ … ⊕ e_NR) ≅ R^{N−1}` is also a
  complement of `x_i R`.
- **Contradiction.** Complements of one direct summand are isomorphic, both being `≅ R^N/x_iR`. So `R^{M−1} ≅ R^{N−1}`
  with `M−1 ≠ N−1` and `min(M−1, N−1) = m−1 < m`, which contradicts (IBN-m).
- **Columns of `Y`.** They are the columns of the inverse isomorphism `R^N → R^M`; exchange `M` and `N`.
- **No free coordinate.** A column equal to a standard basis vector is completable (take `g` a permutation matrix). ∎

Model tests (artifact §2):
- `L_K(1,2)` at `N = 1`: the isometries `s_i` are not units. Correct.
- `L_K(2,3)` at `N = 3 > m`: silent, and free coordinates do exist. Correct.
- `L_K(3,4)` at `N = 3`: all four columns are non-completable.
