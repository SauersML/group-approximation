`Gamma_D = SL_1(O_D)` is an infinite cocompact lattice in `SL_3(R)`, so it has
property (T).  Strong convergence of finite-dimensional representations to
`lambda_(Gamma_D)` makes the map from `C[Gamma_D]` to the norm matrix corona
isometric for the reduced norm, so `C*_r(Gamma_D)` is MF.
