**ESTABLISHED KILL.**  Let `R=L_(F_2)(1,2)`, let `R_0` be its degree-zero
core (the increasing union of the matrix rings `M_(2^m)(F_2)` spanned by the
`s_alpha t_beta` with `|alpha|=|beta|=m`), and let

```text
Delta=St_20(R),        L=X_12(R_0)={x_12(a): a in R_0}.
```

`L` is a locally finite elementary abelian 2-group; fix any increasing
exhaustion `L=union_k F_k` by finite subgroups.

Let `(V_n)` be an operator-norm asymptotic representation of `Delta` on
`C^(d_n)`, and let `Theta:Delta->U(Q)` be the induced homomorphism into the
norm matrix corona.  As in the manuscript transport theorem, let
`sigma~(g)=[Ad V_n(g)]` in the adjoint corona
`B=prod_n B(M_(d_n)(C),||.||_2)/direct_sum`, and put

```text
P_k=(1/|F_k|) sum_(l in F_k) sigma~(l)  in B,          (WAV1)
```

a decreasing sequence of projections.  Then the following are equivalent.

1. `(P_k)` is Cauchy in the norm of `B`.
2. `(P_k)` is eventually constant.
3. `Theta` is trivial.

Consequently every Property-`(T)`-free replacement of the Kazhdan projection
`e_L` by a norm limit of finite-window averaging projections on this root
subgroup, in the manuscript argument
`U^*PU<=P ==> U^*PU=P` (stable finiteness) `==>` transport `==>`
Hilbert--Schmidt invisibility, has a hypothesis that is verifiable only in
representations where the conclusion (indeed operator-norm triviality) is
already reached.  The transport step and stable finiteness are never used.

**Invariant and failing step.**  If `P_k=P_K` for `k>=K`, then along every
ultrafilter the image `Theta_omega(L)` is contained in the `{+-1}`
combinations of the at most `|F_K|` joint spectral atoms of `Theta_omega(F_K)`,
so `|Theta_omega(L)|<=2^|F_K|`.  A finite-image kernel on `L` contains
`x_12(I)` for an additive subgroup `I` of finite index in `R_0`; the ideal
generated by `I` contains `1`, so the normal closure is all of `Delta`.  Every
member of the class therefore dies at the existence of the norm limit: in a
nontrivial representation the window projections are never eventually
constant, so for every `K` some later difference `P_K-P_k` has norm one.  Only the
level-shifted inequalities `U^*P_(k')U<=P_k` with `k'>k` remain, and they are
compatible with stable finiteness.

For groups `L` with Property `(T)`, `e_L` exists in `C*_max(L)` for all
representations, whatever the size of `C*(pi(L))`.  That size is the
invariant that separates the two cases.

No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, literature import beyond the established
exact-lift theorem, or computation is used.
