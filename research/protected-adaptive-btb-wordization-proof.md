Add the finitely many relations `(PBT1)`.  A countable-coordinate witness
with two infinite `B`-eigenspaces makes every carrier and range
`B`-invariant, while the partial bijections themselves need not intertwine
`B`.  This proves consistency and preserves nonvanishing of every named
source.  The new equations have coefficients in `Z`, so the existing
characteristic-three base change and spare-index context-absorption proof
apply verbatim.

The split finite packet action fixes `a_(sigma,i)` under `K_i`; therefore it
fixes its additive root.  Exact order three and the fact that `K_i` is a
two-group prove that `(PBT4)` is the displayed direct product.  This is the
injective edge group needed in `(PBT5)`.  Iterated Bass--Serre normal form
then preserves every old and new vertex group.

Because every new vertex factor is the direct product of `K_i` with the
binary-tetrahedral packet, it centralizes `J` and `B`.  The initial
projection `(PBT6)` therefore already reduces the carrier menu consisting
of the shared BCS tuple and the attached BTB packets.  The Pauli mate `C`
is deliberately absent from that hull menu.  Injectivity of the old Pauli packet and of the fresh `ell` factor
gives its canonical trace `1/8`.  Centrality of `J,ell` throughout the
iterated amalgam gives `(PBT7)` and the `1/4` capacity bound.  Finally, when
all source roots are trivial, the compatible maps which retain `K_i` and
kill each binary-tetrahedral component prove extension of the perfect
marked model.
