Let `B_EL=<Lambda,tau_0,tau_1>` be the two-compressor diagonal subgroup of
`EL_20(R)`, with

```text
Lambda=EL_5(D),
tau_i Lambda tau_i^(-1)=Psi_i(Lambda),
Lambda=Psi_0(Lambda) x Psi_1(Lambda).
```

Every finite-dimensional unitary representation of `B_EL` kills `Lambda`,
and hence kills `z=x_13(s_1t_1)`.

This does not imply the MF statement: matrix dimension may grow while the
finite-dimensional algebras and their conjugate child subalgebras vary.
