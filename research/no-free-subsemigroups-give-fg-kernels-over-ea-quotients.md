**Statement.** Let `G` be a finitely generated group with no free subsemigroup on two
generators, and `N ◁ G` with `G/N` elementary amenable. Then `N` is finitely generated.

Special case (Rosset 1976): if `G` is finitely generated of subexponential growth and `G/N` is
solvable, `N` is finitely generated.

**Consequence here.** A finitely generated group of intermediate growth that maps onto `Z` is
`B ⋊ Z` with `B` finitely generated of subexponential growth. So maps onto `Z` give no
obstruction specific to finite presentation.
