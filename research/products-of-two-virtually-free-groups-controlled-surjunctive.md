**OPEN.** Let `p, q >= 3` and `Lambda = C_2^(*p) x C_2^(*q)`. Then `Lambda` is measured-controlled
surjunctive in the sense of `measured-controlled-surjunctivity-is-envelope-invariant`.

In detail, let `X` be a compact metrizable zero-dimensional `Lambda`-space carrying an invariant
Borel probability of full support. Then no `X`-controlled automaton has all fibre maps injective
and no fibre map surjective.

**Weakest form used.** The route `product-tree-lattices-surjunctive-via-envelope-transfer`
uses only homogeneous bases `X = L/Gamma`, with `L = Aut(T_p) x Aut(T_q)`, `Gamma` a cocompact
lattice and Haar probability on `X`.

**Commensurability.** Part 3 of the envelope theorem, with a discrete envelope, makes the claim
equivalent for all groups commensurable with `Lambda`.

**How it can fail independently.** A strict controlled automaton over some measured base, for
example over `L/Gamma` with `Gamma` residually finite, need not come from any strict cellular
automaton on a lattice. So a counterexample here refutes nothing about Gottschalk's conjecture.

## Attempts

- 2026-09-17 (swarm-0917-w5-gs-decomp-new). **What a proof must spend.**
  - *The invariant measure.* `Lambda` is nonamenable, so by
    `compressible-base-controlled-automata-are-strict` it carries strict controlled automata over a
    compressible base. The swindle `T(z) = s(z) z` has `T(X)` a proper clopen of the same measure,
    which is impossible under an invariant measure of full support.
  - *The relative sofic-entropy route.* It needs sofic models of the base action. For all bases,
    that is `Lambda ∈ 𝒮`, commensurably `F_2 x F_2 ∈ 𝒮` (`f2xf2-in-paunescu-class`). That already
    makes every cocompact lattice of `L` sofic (`product-tree-lattices-in-paunescu-class-iff-f2xf2`),
    and then Gromov–Weiss applies directly, so this method adds nothing for the route. A gain
    requires a Garden-of-Eden argument that does not build finite models of the base.
- **Finite equivariant models of homogeneous bases are only the finite `L`-sets** (proved here).
  - *Statement.* Let `Lambda' <= Lambda` have finite index, and let `E` be a `Lambda'`-invariant
    clopen subset of `L/Gamma`. Then `E` is a union of fibres of `L/Gamma -> L/O` for some open
    finite-index subgroup `O` containing `Gamma`.
  - *Proof.*
    - Let `E~` be the preimage of `E` in `L`, and `D''` a compact open fundamental domain for
      `Lambda'`.
    - `E~ ∩ D''` is compact open, so it is a finite union of left cosets of a compact open
      subgroup `V`.
    - Hence `E~ = Lambda' (E~ ∩ D'')` satisfies `E~ V = E~`, and also `E~ Gamma = E~`.
    - The subgroup `O` generated by `V` and `Gamma` is open, and `L/O` is discrete and a
      continuous image of the compact space `L/Gamma`, hence finite. `E~ O = E~`.
  - *Consequence.* Lawton-type counting on `Lambda'`-periodic points, carried out equivariantly over
    the base, only sees the finite `L`-set `L/O`. There the controlled rule is not constant, so
    residual finiteness of `Lambda` cannot be used by periodizing the base.
- **Where it stops.** No Garden-of-Eden mechanism is known over a measured base that uses neither
  amenability nor sofic models of the base action. The next test is the special case `Gamma = Lambda`
  (base `L/Lambda`), where every strict automaton would have to be intrinsically controlled.
