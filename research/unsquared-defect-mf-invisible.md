`u = [tct^{-1}, v_1] in Rad_MF(E)`: every corona representation of the
literal group kills the unsquared defect, not only its square `w`.

Consequently `Rad_MF(E)` strictly contains `{1, w}`, answering the
manuscript's open question about exactness of the sign radical negatively:
the sign is only the first element of a larger universal MF kernel, and
`Rad_MF(E) = q^{-1}(Rad_MF(E/<w>))` pushes the computation entirely into
the marked quotient.
