title: Standard Burnside Cayley graphs admit rank-uniform expanding local sparsifiers with logarithmic routes
root: true

Theorem 1. Fix odd n>=1003. For every m>=2 and epsilon in (0,1), a finite-radius FIID subgraph of the original standard Cayley graph is connected, has half expected degree <=2-2/n+epsilon, expansion >=h_n/2 independent of m and epsilon, and coding and generator-routing radii <=C_n(1+log(m/epsilon)). Retained types beyond the first two have total intensity <=epsilon.
