**OPEN.** For every finitely generated infinite simple group `G` with property (T), and every
finite alphabet `A` with `|A| >= 2`,

    h^Rok_G(A^G, uniform product measure) = log |A|.

By `rokhlin-maximality-via-fg-simple-kazhdan-hosts`, this implies
`bernoulli-rokhlin-entropy-maximal-for-every-group`, and the converse is specialization. So INF
may be attacked on a simple Kazhdan host, and any deficit that exists already occurs on one.

## Attempts

- **Container reduction (2026-09-12).** Proposition 5 of the artifact: every countable group embeds
  in such a host (`countable-group-embeds-in-fg-simple-kazhdan-group`), and maximality passes to
  subgroups (`bernoulli-rokhlin-maximality-passes-to-subgroups`). This is an exact reduction. It
  proves nothing about any host.
- **Localization is vacuous on nonsofic hosts.** A simple nonsofic group has full sofic radical. So
  `sofic-radical-localizes-bernoulli-deficit-witnesses` requires only that the subgroup generated by
  a witness be nonsofic, while the counting proof of maximality needs that subgroup to be sofic.
- **Finite models, restriction, co-induction.** Every known lower bound runs through sofic entropy.
  Restriction to a subgroup and co-induction bound the Rokhlin entropy of the host only from above
  (`leavitt-unit-group-has-positive-rokhlin-entropy-action`).
- **Dichotomy.** Suppose a host contains a copy of its own square and finite subgroups of unbounded
  order. Then `h^Rok_sup` is `0` or `infinity`
  (`rokhlin-supremum-dichotomy-with-centralized-self-copies`), so INF for that host is equivalent
  to the existence of one free action of positive entropy. No existence mechanism is known.
- **Property (T) and spectral invariants.** The Koopman representation of a uniform Bernoulli shift
  is a countable multiple of the regular representation on the orthocomplement of the constants,
  whatever the base. So spectral gap, strong ergodicity and every other Koopman invariant are blind
  to the base entropy and cannot detect a deficit. No property (T) mechanism for a lower bound is
  known (`fg-simple-kazhdan-groups-are-surjunctive`, Attempts).
