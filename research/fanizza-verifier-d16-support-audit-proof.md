# Diagonalize the verifier flag and compare its selector with the signal support

On the `X=+1` flag line each block of `(FDC1)` is the identity, and on the
`X=-1` line it is `1-2L_j`, proving `(FDC2)`.  Substitute these two blocks
into the exact D16 commutator formula and use `L_j^2=L_j` to obtain `(FDC3)`.
The verifier carrier instead uses the `Z=+1` flag line, so it is transverse
and not a reducing D16 selector corner.  Finally the four-character truth
table proves that the overlap reflection `(FDC8)` is not a word in the two
commuting recurrence signs.  These computations respectively isolate the
support and wordization obstructions.
