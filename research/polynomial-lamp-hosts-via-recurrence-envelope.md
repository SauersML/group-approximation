Take the finitely many Newton profiles `C(n,0),…,C(n,d)` on the
integers. Their positive and negative tails satisfy constant-coefficient
recurrences, since a sufficiently high finite difference vanishes.
Their translates span exactly the integer-valued polynomial module
`P_d`. The source action is precisely the grading action defining
`W_(m,q,d)`. The prerequisite therefore supplies the same simple
`F_∞` host with two finite-order generators claimed by the target.
This alternate conditional route bypasses the older polynomial
degree induction; it does not mark either theorem established.
