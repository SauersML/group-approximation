Direct proof, given in the target under `## Proof`. By the identity principle,
the pieces of products and inverses of piecewise-`L` maps with pieces in `Δ`
lie in `Δ`. A map that is locally in `B` off a closed nowhere dense set
therefore has its pieces in `Δ`. Corollary (b) then uses Mal'cev: `Δ` is a
finitely generated linear group, so it is residually finite and has no
nontrivial divisible subgroup.

Lane proof (lane gq-pp-higher), not independently reviewed. This route only
records the proof already written in the target, so that the graph computes the
claim's stated status; it adds no mathematics.
