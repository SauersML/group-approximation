**Established by citation.**  Each of the four items is a numbered statement of
arXiv:2607.13762 read off directly: Proposition 2.1 for the counting law,
Theorem 3.7 for the kernel law, Lemma 5.3(1) for pin homogeneity and the
linearity of `V_k`, Section 4.1 for the definition of `V_k`, and Remark 4.10 for
the two residual conditions of the germ step together with the verified range
`2->3`, `3->4`, `4->5`.

The scope of the import is deliberately narrow.  Remark 4.10's further assertion
that unseen coordinates are free at every level is left out of this claim; the
audit records why (it is the one step of the downstream argument that is neither
proved in the source nor supplied by the dossier that builds on it).
