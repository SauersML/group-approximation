Imports Theorem 2 of V. Guba, *Systems of equations over the group ring of Thompson's group
F*, arXiv:2201.02308v1 (Comm. Algebra 50:12 (2022) 5171–5183). The survey
arXiv:2305.07113v4 restates it as Theorem 3.18. The statement is quoted in the claim.

Read by lane `thompson-f-amenability` on 2026-09-12 from the PDFs of both papers, extracted
on MSI. The proof of Theorem 2 (§3) and of its auxiliary Theorem 3 were read:
- Theorem 3 is proved by induction on the number of homogeneous components, using
  `w v = v phi^d(w)` when every letter of `w` exceeds every letter of `v`;
- Theorem 2 reduces `b` to `K[M_1]` and puts `v = sum_k x_0^k v_k`.
