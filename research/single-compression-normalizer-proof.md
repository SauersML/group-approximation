# Compare two equal-trace projections after compressed unitarity

Set `p=w^*qw`. The equation `(qwq)^*(qwq)=q` is `qpq=q`, hence `q<=p`.
Since `p` is conjugate to `q`, finite faithful trace forces equality. The
free-product norm calculation in `(SC2)` is
`tau(qw^*qwq)/tau(q)=tau(q)^2/tau(q)=1/2`.
