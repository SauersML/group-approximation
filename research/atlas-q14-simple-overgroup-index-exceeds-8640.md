Let `S` be a finite simple group generated by two marked `A8` charts satisfying
the Atlas packet, collision `19243`, and `q14`.  Then

```text
[S:A8] > 8640.                                        (OSI8641)
```

Indeed the established finite-simple sieve gives `[S:A8]>=8640`, with equality
possible only for `S~=O8+(2)`.  The ten marked screens exhaust all chart pairs
in that equality group, and every one already fails packet plus collision
`19243`.  Thus equality is impossible, independently of `q14`.
