**ESTABLISHED** (route `leavitt-cone-cover-kernel-dense-compact-model-proof`; unreviewed).

**Setting.** As in `leavitt-cone-cover-kernel-is-free-and-gamma-perfect`: `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, `q = s_1t_1`,
`Delta = St_20(R) = GL_20(R)`, `z = x_13(q)`, `Delta_+-` the subgroups generated by root elements with entries in the
Cuntz cone `R_+ = <s_1, s_2, q>` and its mirror `R_- = <t_1, t_2, q>`, `Delta_0 = Delta_+ ∩ Delta_-`,
`Gamma = Delta_+ *_(Delta_0) Delta_-`, `p : Gamma -> Delta`, `K = ker p`. By
`leavitt-cuntz-cone-halves-have-rf-amalgam-cover`, `Gamma` is residually finite and `K` is the normal closure of
finitely many relators `k_1, ..., k_r`, among them `kappa = [x_12(s_1), x_23(t_1)] z^(-1)`.

**Statement.**
1. **Compact density.** For every homomorphism `rho : Gamma -> G` into a Hausdorff compact group,
   `closure rho(K) = closure rho(Gamma)`.
2. **Finite density.** For every finite quotient `pi : Gamma -> F`, `pi(K) = F`. Equivalently `K N = Gamma` for every
   finite-index normal subgroup `N` of `Gamma`: `K` is a proper dense normal subgroup of the residually finite group
   `Gamma` in its profinite topology, and `K` surjects onto the profinite completion of `Gamma`.
3. **Exact finite models are trivial.** A finite-dimensional unitary representation of `Gamma` (or a homomorphism to a
   finite group) that kills `k_1, ..., k_r` is trivial.
4. **Head as a profinite consequence.** `z ∉ K`, yet for every finite-index normal `N`, `z ∈ K N`: in every finite
   quotient of `Gamma` the head is a product of conjugates of the images of `k_1^(±1), ..., k_r^(±1)`.
5. **Finite-dimensional coefficients carry nothing.** Every finite-dimensional unitary `Delta`-module `M` is trivial,
   and for it `H^1(Delta; M) = H^1(Gamma; M) = H^2(Delta; M) = 0` and `Hom_Delta(K^ab, M) = 0`. This is the
   finite-dimensional case left open under "What is and is not claimed" in
   `leavitt-cone-cover-kernel-free-gamma-perfect-proof`: both pieces
   `M^(Delta_0)/(M^(Delta_+) + M^(Delta_-))` and `ker(H^2(Delta; M) -> H^2(Gamma; M))` vanish.

**Class killed.** Exact finite-model detection of the K-essential step of `agent-leavitt-not-bcs-negative-root-corner`
and of the MF cover test of `leavitt-cuntz-cone-halves-have-rf-amalgam-cover` (4a).
- *Members.* Any argument that tries to measure how far a finite or compact model of `Gamma` is from factoring through
  `Delta` by an exact invariant of the image of `K`, or of the finitely many relators `k_i`. Examples: the index or
  order of `pi(K)` in finite quotients, counts of finite quotients of `Gamma` in which the `k_i` vanish, profinite or
  pro-`p` separation of `z` from `K`, compact-group-valued (Bohr) charges on `K`, and first-order charges in
  finite-dimensional coefficient modules (linearization of `K^ab`).
- *Invariant.* `Delta` is infinite, simple and finitely generated. So it has no nontrivial maximally almost periodic
  quotient: Peter–Weyl and Malcev turn any nontrivial compact image into a nontrivial finite quotient of `Delta`.
- *Death step.* Passing to the quotient by `closure rho(K)`. It is a compact image of `Delta`, hence trivial. So the
  image of `K` fills the model, and every exact invariant of it equals the same invariant of the whole model. In
  finite-dimensional coefficient modules the step is (5): the module is trivial and all charges vanish.
- *Survivors.* Only asymptotic models: sequences of finite-dimensional unitary or MF representations of `Gamma` in
  which the `k_i` are small but nonzero, measured in normalized Hilbert–Schmidt or operator norm. In such a sequence
  the images of `K` are dense, yet the relators are approximately trivial. Deciding whether the head survives there is
  exactly the hyperlinearity or MF question for `Delta`, which this node does not touch. Noncompact targets (the
  regular representation, where `leavitt-cone-cover-kernel-is-free-and-gamma-perfect` (5) exhibits a nonzero charge)
  also survive.
