Apply expander Poincare in each equality cloud and choose one existing
occurrence reflection `Q_i` for every logical coordinate.  Averaged over all
tested ports, the squared movement to these representatives is `O(epsilon)`.
Fixed face width and bounded port congestion let one telescope the parity
words and pair commutators, giving

```text
 average parity defect of (Q_i) = O(epsilon),
 L^(-2)sum_(i,j)||[Q_i,Q_j]||_2^2 = O(epsilon).           (BTOP1)
```

The second line is equivalently the quantitative assignment supplied by
`pair-sum-faces-control-shared-commutator-energy`.  The algebra generated by
the representatives is a subalgebra of the occurrence algebra, hence still
has irreducible type at most `D` after its own central decomposition.

Apply `bounded-type-noncentral-code-sectors-round-uniformly` to `(BTOP1)`.
It produces one commuting code tuple, and therefore one joint codeword PVM,
at cost `Omega_D(epsilon)`.  Assign its coordinate marginals to every cloud.
Triangle inequality with the representative-selection cost proves `(BTO1)`.
Finally, a bounded binary face PVM is a fixed multilinear expression in its
commuting marginal spectral projections, so
`binary-face-pvm-is-determined-by-coordinate-reflections` reconstructs every
face with no length- or dimension-dependent loss.

For `(BTO2)`, run the argument on the central type-at-most-`D` projection.
On its complement assign the all-identity codeword to every occurrence.
The latter costs at most four times its central trace, while concavity of the
bounded-type modulus absorbs the former compression's trace weight.
