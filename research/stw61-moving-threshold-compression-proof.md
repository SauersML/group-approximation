Fix the coefficient representation and represent z by a strongly
continuous path of spectral sign unitaries V_a running from +1
to -1. Uniform norm approximation by free-product polynomials
proves strong-* continuity of pi_a(W) for every fixed unitary W.

After replacing p by its complement if necessary, let P=rho(p)
have finite nonzero rank. Compress pi_a(W)* V_a pi_a(W) to the
fixed finite-dimensional space PH. This self-adjoint matrix path
is norm continuous and has endpoints +I and -I. An eigenvalue
vanishes somewhere, producing a unit vector in the conjugate
projection range which V_a sends into its orthogonal complement.
The commutator therefore has norm at least one, and the universal
upper bound gives equality.

The Toeplitz extension makes one side of each nontrivial
classical Toeplitz projection finite rank. Faithful one-isometry
inclusion and full unital free-product inclusion transport that
case into T2. The ordinary binary Fock representation sends
the full T2 defect to a rank-one projection, yielding the
unrestricted defect-projection consequence separately.
