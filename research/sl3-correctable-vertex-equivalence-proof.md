The standard dictionary gives a canonical-character normalized-HS
microstate sequence exactly when the countable group `D` is hyperlinear.  If
there is no such sequence, the universally quantified correctable-vertex
property is vacuous.  If there is such a sequence, the second prerequisite
gives a fixed positive lower bound on both vertex distances to the exact
finite-dimensional representation variety, so the correctable-vertex
property fails.  This proves the equivalence.
