**ESTABLISHED C-STAR QUOTIENT FIREWALL.**  The rank-three free group `F_3`
is finitely presented, residually finite, and operator-MF.  Its full group
C-star algebra is residually finite-dimensional and hence stably finite.
Nevertheless there is a surjective unital star homomorphism

```text
C*(F_3) ->> O_2,                                             (PIQ1)
```

and `O_2` is not directly finite: for a canonical Cuntz isometry `S_0`,

```text
S_0^* S_0=1,                  S_0 S_0^*!=1.                  (PIQ2)
```

The quotient `(PIQ1)` is genuinely generated by the three unitary images of
the free generators.  A direct Julia-operator construction gives those three
unitaries; no appeal to an unspecified infinite unitary generating set is
needed.

Consequently the following proposed Property-`(T)`-free route is invalid:

```text
construct a unitary-generated, non-stably-finite C-star quotient of C*(Gamma)
=> Gamma is not operator-MF.                                (PIQ3)
```

It fails already for the finitely presented MF group `F_3`, even though the
source of `(PIQ1)` is itself stably finite.  Thus a complex Cuntz/Leavitt
completion of a quotient of the binary-Leavitt group algebra would not prove
the desired group theorem.

A viable stable-finiteness bridge must do strictly more: it must embed the
one-sided inverse in a C-star subalgebra or hereditary corner retained by
**every group-faithful norm-corona representation**, or prove directly that
one specified group-faithful representation retains the relevant projection
and arrows.  Quotient evaluation only chooses one C-star norm and may kill a
linear ideal while remaining injective on every individual group element.
For the present Leavitt target, the required retention statement is exactly
the semantic content of a same-atom decoder such as
`binary-leavitt-atomic-morita-return`; stable finiteness of the ambient matrix
corona supplies the contradiction only after that retention is established.

No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan projection, stability theorem, or Lean input is
used.  The sole standard external input is Choi's residual
finite-dimensionality theorem for `C*(F_n)`, already used elsewhere in this
graph.
