**Source.** G. Robertson, T. Steger, *Affine buildings, tiling systems and higher rank
Cuntz–Krieger algebras*, J. reine angew. Math. 513 (1999), 115–144, arXiv:1302.5593.

**How it was read.** The arXiv e-print TeX was fetched on MSI on 2026-09-14 into
`/scratch.global/sauer354/hl-bh-a2-boundary-hosts/src/1302.5593/main.tex`. Quotes are from
that file.

**Verbatim.**
- TeX l.182–185: "Let $\G$ be a group of type rotating automorphisms of $\cB$ which acts
  freely on the vertex set with finitely many orbits. There is a natural action of $\G$ on
  the boundary $\Omega$ of $\cB$, and we can form the universal crossed product algebra
  $C(\Om)\rtimes \G$. This algebra is isomorphic to an algebra of the form $\cA$ obtained
  by the preceding construction."
- (H2), TeX l.248–250: "Consider the directed graph which has a vertex for each $a \in A$
  and a directed edge from $a$ to $b$ for each $i$ such that $M_i(b,a) =1$. This graph is
  irreducible."
- Proposition `M_1M_2`: "Suppose that $\G$~acts freely and with finitely many orbits on the
  vertices of~$\cB$. Then the matrices $M_1$, $M_2$ of the previous section satisfy
  conditions {\rm(H0)},{\rm(H1)}, and {\rm(H3)}."
- TeX l.1941–1946: "We prove that conditions (H0), (H1), and (H3) are satisfied so long as
  $\G$~acts freely and with finitely many orbits on the vertices of~$\cB$. Moreover, if
  $\cB$ is the building of $G=\PGL_3(\KK)$, where $\KK$ is a nonarchimedean local field of
  characteristic zero and $\G$ is a lattice in $\PGL_3(\KK)$ we prove that (H2) holds as
  well."
- Theorem `main2`: "The map $\phi$ is an isomorphism from $\cA$ onto $C(\Om)\rtimes \G$."
  From its proof: "If $p \in \oW$ then $\Ind _{\Omega(p)}=\phi(s_{\ow,\ow})$, where
  $\ow=\oa(p)$ and so Lemma~\ref{c2} shows that the range of $\phi$ contains $C(\Om)$."
- Theorem `H(3)`: "Let $\cB$ be the building of $G=\SL_3(\KK)$, where $\KK$ is a local field
  of characteristic zero. Let $\G$ be a lattice in $\SL_3(\KK)$ which acts freely on the
  vertices of $\cB$ with finitely many orbits. [...] Then for each $i=1,2$, the directed
  graph with vertices $a \in A$ and directed edges $(a,b)$ whenever $M_i(b,a) =1$ is
  irreducible." Corollary `H(3)+` extends this to lattices in $\PGL_3(\KK)$ acting freely on
  the vertices.
