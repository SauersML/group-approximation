For `A_n=C_n times B_n`, the two maps

```text
s |-> (lambda_n(s),1),
s |-> (lambda_n(s),beta_n(tau(s)))
```

have equal kernels by `(RTS1)`.  Their restrictions in the left regular
representation of the finite image are therefore exactly unitarily conjugate.
If `(A,rho,tr)` is the given regular-MF realization of `Gamma`, work first in

```text
product_n (A tensor M_|G_n|) / direct-sum_n (A tensor M_|G_n|).
```

The classes of `rho(g) tensor L_n(lambda_n(g),beta_n(q))` form an exact vertex
representation, and the classes of `1 tensor W_n` exactly conjugate the two
edges.  The ultralimit of `tr tensor normalized-trace` is the regular character:
`tr(rho(g))` kills `g!=1`, and eventual separation by `beta_n` kills `q!=1`
when `g=1`.

Every separable subalgebra of this reduced product is MF.  For finitely many
noncommutative polynomials, choose one sufficiently late coordinate for each
polynomial's quotient limsup and take the direct sum of those coordinate
tuples.  Its norm gives every required lower estimate, while lateness gives
all upper estimates.  Use MF of that finite direct sum of
`A tensor M_|G_n|` algebras; diagonalization gives a matrix-corona embedding.  Apply this to the
separable algebra generated by the vertex image and edge conjugator.  Now
Ueda's universal-HNN corner construction and Shulman's compatible-corona
criterion apply.  Finally use the reduced von Neumann HNN normal form for the
regular ultralimit trace to separate every Britton-reduced word.
