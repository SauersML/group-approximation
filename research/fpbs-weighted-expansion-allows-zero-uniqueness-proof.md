Section 4 gives a direct construction. The upward-minus-downward degree
is at least three fifths of the total degree. Each sufficiently high
layer pair fails connectivity with probability at most 6n exp(-pn/4),
which is summable for n=2^k. Borel--Cantelli yields a connected cofinite
tail. Sending one unit from every vertex to the unique degree-two vertex
excludes any unimodular root law. This proof uses no percolation theorem
about Cayley graphs and supplies no counterexample to Benjamini--Schramm.
