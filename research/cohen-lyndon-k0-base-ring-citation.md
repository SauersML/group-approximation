Jaikin-Zapirain, Linton, and Sánchez-Peralta, *Group pairs, coherence and
Farrell--Jones Conjecture for K_0*, arXiv:2510.23518v2, state immediately
before Theorem 1.7 that the theorem implies the target claim.

Theorem 1.7 proves the underlying surjection for a Cohen--Lyndon group pair:
if each stabilizer is self-normalizing, `R` is regular,
`cd_R(G)<infinity`, and `R[G]` is coherent, then

```text
K_0(R[G]) -> K_0(R[G/<G_x : x in X>])
```

is surjective. Applied to a torsion-free Cohen--Lyndon presentation with
free ambient group, this gives the target isomorphism; injectivity also
follows from the augmentation retraction.
