Put

```text
(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3)=(i,j,ell,k),
type(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3)=(triv,nontriv,nontriv,triv),
P(c_a)=c_(a+1).
```

For indices modulo four define

```text
X_(a,s)=x_(c_(a+2),c_a)(t_s),
Y_(a,r)=x_(c_a,c_(a+1))(s_r),
D_a=x_(c_(a+2),c_(a+1))(1).
```

Thus each row satisfies

```text
[X_(a,s),Y_(a,r)]=D_a^(delta_(sr)).                     (QPF1)
```

On the nine joint characters of `C=<J,Z>`, the exact support table is

```text
a       X_(a,s)        Y_(a,r)        D_a
0       complete       complete       diagonal
1       complete       diagonal       complete
2       complete       complete       diagonal
3       complete       diagonal       complete.         (QPF2)
```

Here `complete` means equality of the `h=J^2Z` character, with three
target atoms and squared norm `1/27` on each compatible block.
`Diagonal` means literal equality of the two `C` characters, with one
block of squared norm `1/9`. Consequently every incidence matrix in
`(QPF2)` is doubly stochastic and the uniform atom vector `(1/9)^9`
solves every scalar rank and Hall constraint from these rows.

The first relation absent from the original finite-vertex shadow is already

```text
[P Y_(0,r) P^(-1),J]=1.                                 (QPF3)
```

It changes the base complete graph into the translated diagonal graph.
Nevertheless `(QPF3)` has an exact residually finite cover. Let

```text
K_1=<C,P>,                 C'=P^(-1)CP,
A=<Y_(0,0),Y_(0,1)> isomorphic to C_2^2.
```

Then `C intersect C'=<h>`, and

```text
Gamma_Y=K_1 *_(C') (C' times A)                         (QPF4)
```

maps onto the concrete subgroup generated by `C,P,Y_(0,0),Y_(0,1)`.
It is a finite-edge amalgam of finite groups, hence virtually free and
residually finite. Its canonical trace has all nine `C`-atom weights
`1/9`, gives the complete `1/27` table for `Y_(0,r)`, and gives the
diagonal `1/9` table for `P Y_(0,r)P^(-1)`. Finite quotients preserve
these finitely many tables exactly.

Thus the first central-output-to-noncentral-input phase flip is not the
Hilbert--Schmidt obstruction. The full cycle becomes nonsofic only after
the negative-letter family `X_(a,s)` is coupled across at least two actor
translates. The next exact object is the smallest mixed `X`/`Y`
cross-translate relation not contained in `Gamma_Y` or in one 32-element
delta packet.
