An atomic-center type-III von Neumann algebra is the direct product of its
type-III factor central summands.  Every coordinate has approximate
uniqueness by the arbitrary-factor theorem.  Closure of Jiang--Su embedding
uniqueness under arbitrary direct products then supplies one product unitary
for each finite set and tolerance, proving the claim.
