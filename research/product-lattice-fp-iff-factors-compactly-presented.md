**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`product-lattice-fp-iff-factors-compactly-presented-citation`).

Let `G_1`, `G_2` be compactly generated locally compact groups and `Γ` a
cocompact lattice in `G_1 × G_2`. Then:

1. `Γ` is finitely generated;
2. `Γ` is finitely presented **if and only if** both `G_1` and `G_2` are compactly
   presented.

No irreducibility or density hypothesis is needed. For a Bader–Shalom lattice this
turns finite presentability of the host into a local property of each factor
separately, with no reference to `Γ`.
