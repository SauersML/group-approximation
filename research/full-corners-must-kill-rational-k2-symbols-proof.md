**Inputs beyond the landed nodes (trust surface).**
- **(M) Full corners.** For a full idempotent `e`, the corner inclusion `i: eRe -> R` induces an
  isomorphism `K_2(eRe) ≅ K_2(R)` (Morita invariance of `K_*`). On units it is
  `x ↦ x + (1 - e)`, and it sends Steinberg symbols to Steinberg symbols:
  `i_*{a, b} = {a + (1-e), b + (1-e)}`. Standard; cited from memory.
- **(T) Tate and Matsumoto.** `K_2(Q)` is torsion (Tate) and is generated by the symbols
  `{λ, μ}` (Matsumoto). The first is quoted in `matricial-divisibility-kills-rational-k2-symbols`;
  the second is used in the same way in `infinitely-generated-symbols-block-fp-elementary-groups`.

**`PE_N` finitely presented implies `E_N` finitely presented.**
- `E_N(R)` is perfect, so it is finitely generated
  (`rational-scalars-obstruct-fp-elementary-hosts`, part 1).
- `Z(E_N(R))` is finitely generated (part 2 there).
- `fg-central-extension-of-fp-group-is-fp` then gives finite presentation of `E_N(R)`.

**Proof.** For `λ ∈ Q^x` put `d_e(λ) = λe + (1 - e)`.
- `d_e(λ)` is a unit with inverse `d_e(λ^-1)`, and `d_e(λ)`, `d_e(μ)` commute.
- By `infinitely-generated-symbols-block-fp-elementary-groups`, part 1, the symbols
  `{d_e(λ), d_e(μ)}` lie in the finitely generated group `Sym(R)`. So they generate a finitely
  generated subgroup `S ⊆ K_2(R)`.
- By (M), `{d_e(λ), d_e(μ)} = i_*{λe, μe}`.
- By (T), the image `I_e` of `K_2(Q)` in `K_2(eRe)` is generated by the `{λe, μe}`. So
  `i_*(I_e) = S`.
- `i_*` is injective by (M), so `I_e ≅ S` is finitely generated.
- `I_e` is torsion by (T), so it is finite. ∎
