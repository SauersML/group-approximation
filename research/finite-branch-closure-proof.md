title: Use finite-dimensional injectivity to make the branch surjective

An injective endomorphism of a finite-dimensional vector space is
surjective.  Thus every `v in V` is `T(w)` for some `w`, and `(FBC1)` gives

```text
eta(v)=eta(T(w))=0.
```

Therefore `eta=0`.
