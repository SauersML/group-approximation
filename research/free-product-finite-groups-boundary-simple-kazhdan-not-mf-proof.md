Details are in `research/artifacts/un-boundary-families-2026-09-13-part1.md`, §5. We check the hypotheses of the two
required claims.

- **Cantor.** `Ω` is closed in a product of finite sets. After a letter of `A \ 1` there are `|B| - 1 >= 2` choices,
  so every cylinder is uncountable.
- **Action.** Multiply `γ = y_1 ... y_k` (normal form) against `ω` and reduce at the junction. At most `k` letters are
  consumed and one more is modified. Each `γ` is a homeomorphism with inverse `γ^(-1)`.
- **(H-gen).** `Γ` is generated by the finite set `(A \ 1) ∪ (B \ 1)`, and the one-letter cylinders `C(x)` generate:
  `C(x_1 ... x_m) = (x_1 ... x_(m-1)) · C(x_m)`, and cylinders separate points.
- **Minimal.** Given `C(w)` and `ω`, insert one or two letters `y` between `w` and `ω` so that `w y ω` is reduced.
  Then `(wy)·ω ∈ C(w)`.
- **Topologically free.** Suppose `γ != 1` of length `k` fixes pointwise a cylinder, which we may extend to `C(w')`
  with `|w'| >= k + 2`. For a tail `ω''` that is not eventually periodic, `γ·(w'ω'') = (γw')ω''`, where `γw'` is a
  reduced word ending in the last letter of `w'`. Equality with `w'ω''` forces `|γw'| = |w'|`, hence `γw' = w'`, so
  `γ = 1`.
- **North–south.** Take `g = ab` with `a ∈ A \ 1` and `b ∈ B \ 1`, `ξ^+ = abab...` and `ξ^- = b^(-1)a^(-1)b^(-1)...`.
  A compact `K` avoiding `ξ^-` misses some `C((b^(-1)a^(-1))^M)`, so `g^n K ⊆ C((ab)^(n-M-1))`. `g^(-1)` is symmetric.

Now `north-south-minimal-cantor-action-is-paradoxical` gives (H-par), and
`paradoxical-cantor-actions-give-simple-kazhdan-not-mf` gives the statement.
