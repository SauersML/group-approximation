Assume `S ≤ \hat Σ ≅ T^2` is closed, one-dimensional, and has
`dim^c_Rok(\hat α|_S) = d < ∞` on `A = C(T^3) ⋊ Σ`.

1. **The fixed-point algebra.** Averaging over `S` sends `f u_γ` to
   `1_{γ ∈ H} f u_γ`, where `H = S^⊥`. Since `\hat S ≅ Σ/H` has rank one,
   `H ≅ Z` is generated by a unit `h ≠ ±1`. Thus
   `A^S = C(T^3) ⋊ H = C*(Z^3 ⋊_h Z)`.
2. **Its decomposition rank.** The unit `h` of the totally real cubic field
   has a conjugate of absolute value greater than one. So `Z^3 ⋊_h Z` is not
   virtually nilpotent.
   - By Eckhardt, arXiv:1309.2205, Theorem 3.3, its group C*-algebra is not
     strongly quasidiagonal.
   - By Kirchberg--Winter, Theorem 5.3, finite decomposition rank would imply
     strong quasidiagonality.
   - Hence `dr(A^S) = ∞`.
3. **The transfer.** `S` is a compact group of covering dimension one.
   Gardella--Hirshberg--Santiago, arXiv:1709.00222, Theorem 3.17(4), gives
   `dr(A^S) = dr(A ⋊ S) ≤ (d+1)(dr(A)+1) - 1`. Therefore `dr(A) = ∞`, which
   is the target.

The implication is valid, but its hypothesis is refuted by
`berend-dual-circles-have-infinite-rokhlin-dimension`. So this route is
dead. Reviving the transfer would require a bound for `dr(A ⋊ S)` in terms of
`dr(A)` that does not rest on Rokhlin dimension of the dual action.
