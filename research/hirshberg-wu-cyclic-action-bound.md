title: Integer and finite-group actions on finite-dimensional compact spaces have uniform bounds
---

Let `X` be a compact metrizable space of covering dimension `r<infinity`.

1. Every action of `Z` on `X` satisfies

   ```text
   dim_nuc(C(X) rtimes Z) <= 2r^2+6r+4.                 (HWC1)
   ```

2. Every action of a finite group `F` on `X` satisfies

   ```text
   dim_nuc(C(X) rtimes F) <= r.                         (HWC2)
   ```

In particular, the same bound `2r^2+6r+4` works for an action of any cyclic
group, independently of its order, while arbitrary finite groups satisfy the
sharper bound `(HWC2)`.
