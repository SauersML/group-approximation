Citation import, not a reproof.

J. M. Belk, C. Bleak and F. Matucci, *Embedding right-angled Artin groups into
Brin-Thompson groups*, arXiv:1602.08635v1 (27 Feb 2016). Lane
z2-19-hyperbolic-nv read pages 1–4 of the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-13. Quoted
verbatim:

- **Theorem 1.1.** "For any simple graph Γ, there exists an n ≥ 1 so that the
  right-angled Artin group A_Γ embeds isomorphically into nV." The text that
  follows gives `n = |V| + |E^c|`, where `E^c` is the set of complementary
  edges.
- **Theorem 1.2.** "For any simple graph Γ, there exists an n ≥ 1 so that the
  standard restricted wreath product nV ≀ A_Γ ≅ (⊕_{A_Γ} nV) ⋊ A_Γ embeds
  isomorphically into nV." This rests on Bleak–Salazar-Díaz, Proposition 3.6.
- **Corollary 1.3.** "For any finite graph Γ there is a natural number n so
  that every finite extension of A_Γ embeds into nV."
- **Text after Corollary 1.3.** "Note also that any group that virtually embeds
  into nV embeds isomorphically into nV." The source derives this from the
  demonstrative embedding of finite groups into V (Bleak–Salazar-Díaz, Lemma
  3.3) and the wreath product argument. It adds: "In particular, any group that
  virtually embeds into a right-angled Artin group embeds isomorphically into
  some nV."
- **Corollary 1.5.** "For any virtually special group G, there exists an n ≥ 1
  so that G embeds isomorphically into nV." Its list includes all finitely
  generated Coxeter groups, many word hyperbolic groups including all one-relator
  groups with torsion, all limit groups, and many 3-manifold groups.
- **Corollary 1.4.** Surface groups and graph braid groups embed in some nV.

In the source "simple graph" means finite simple graph: §2 takes a finite vertex
set. The lane did not re-read the ping-pong proof in §§2–4. This route asserts
only that the source says what the claim records.
