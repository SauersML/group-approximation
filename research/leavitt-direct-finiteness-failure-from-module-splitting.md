**INVALIDATED 2026-08-17 by `leavitt-evaluation-does-not-split-as-module-map`.**
The first prerequisite is false: `A = F_2[R^x]` is prime
(`leavitt-unit-group-algebra-is-prime`), and a section would make `ker(pi)`
annihilate the nonzero ideal generated by `sigma(1_R)`.  The construction
below is still correct — a complement really would produce `ba = 1 != ab` —
but there is no complement, so this route can never fire, and `R` is not
projective as a left `A`-module.

## Why sufficient

Write `A = F_2[R^x]`, `pi : A ->> R` for the evaluation map of the second
prerequisite, `K = ker(pi)`.  A left `A`-linear `sigma : R -> A` with
`pi . sigma = id_R` gives a decomposition of left `A`-modules

    A = sigma(R) (+) K,

with `pi` identifying `sigma(R)` with `R`.

On `R`, put `f(r) = r s_0` and `g(r) = r t_0`.  Both are left `A`-linear for
the module structure through `pi`, since scalars act on the left.  They
satisfy `f(g(r)) = r t_0 s_0 = r`, so `f . g = id_R`, while `g . f != id_R`:
`g(f(1)) = s_0 t_0 != 1`, since `s_0 t_0 = 1` would give `s_1 t_1 = 0` and
then `0 = t_1 (s_1 t_1) s_1 = (t_1 s_1)^2 = 1`.

Extend by the identity across the complement:

    F = f (+) id_K,        H = g (+) id_K.

These are left `A`-module endomorphisms of the left regular module `A`, with
`F . H = id_A` and `H . F != id_A`.  Every endomorphism of the left regular
module is right multiplication by its value at `1`, so with `a = F(1)` and
`b = H(1)` this reads

    b a = 1,      a b != 1

in `A`, which is the target.

## What the route consumes

Only a **module** splitting.  Multiplicativity of `sigma` is never used, which
is essential: a ring section of `pi` is impossible, because the `F_2`
augmentation forbids a unital binary Leavitt family in any `F_2[G]` — this is
the scalar half of `augmentation-blocks-leavitt-family-proof`, as recorded in
`leavitt-corner-idempotent-unital-surjection`.  The whole content of the
splitting hypothesis is the distance between *direct summand* and *subring*.

This route stays in the whole algebra and so needs no corner; it is
independent of `leavitt-unit-algebra-not-directly-finite-by-corner-padding`
and of the radical hypothesis `leavitt-corner-kernel-is-jacobson-radical`,
which reaches the target by making a quotient argument legal instead of by
producing elements.
