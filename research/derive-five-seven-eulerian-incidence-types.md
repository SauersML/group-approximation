The equations `z beta=z` and `beta z=z` say exactly that every nonbase left
and right `H`-coset meets the support evenly and each base coset meets it
oddly.  The base cell contains one or three terms.  Removing it leaves an
Eulerian bipartite multigraph with four or six edges.  Its cycle partitions
are respectively `4` or `2+2`, and `6`, `4+2`, or `2+2+2`.  Parallel edges
can occur only in a normalizer cell, because two distinct elements in one
left-right cell have quotient in both `H` and its conjugate.
