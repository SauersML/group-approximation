Let `U=L_(F_2)(1,2)^x`.  Let

```text
Q=F_r * G_1 * ... * G_k,                                (VFR1)
```

where `r,k` are finite and every `G_i` is finite.  There is an explicit
prefix-table embedding of `Q` in `U` and a nonzero Leavitt corner whose
`Q`-orbit is wandering.  Consequently `U` contains an internal restricted
wreath product

```text
W_Q=U^(Q) semidirectProduct Q=U wr Q<=U.                 (VFR2)
```

The group `W_Q` is finitely generated, non-MF, and

```text
Rad_MF(W_Q)=U^(Q),               R_MF(W_Q)~=Q.           (VFR3)
```

Its radical is normally generated by one fixed involution in the identity
coordinate.  Precomposition with the quotient `pi_Q:W_Q->Q` gives natural
bijections

```text
Hom(Q,M)~=Hom(W_Q,M)                                     (VFR4)
```

for every MF group `M`, and likewise for finite groups, residually finite
groups, compact Hausdorff groups, and finite-dimensional linear groups over
every field.

For every normal subgroup `N` of `W_Q`,

```text
cl_MF^(W_Q)(N)=pi_Q^(-1)(cl_MF^Q(pi_Q(N))),              (VFR5)

W_Q/N is MF
 iff U^(Q)<=N and Q/pi_Q(N) is MF.                       (VFR6)
```

Thus inverse image along `pi_Q` identifies the lattice of MF-closed normal
subgroups of `Q` with that of `W_Q`.  In particular, every MF-visible
quotient and every MF-semantic consequence of `W_Q` is exactly the
corresponding one for the prescribed free-product actor.
