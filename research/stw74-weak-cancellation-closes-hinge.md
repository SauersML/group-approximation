title: Weak cancellation of the Cuntz semigroup closes Problem LXXIV
distinct_from:

Let `A` be simple and unital, and suppose every nonzero projection in `A` is
infinite.  If `Cu(A)` has weak cancellation,

```text
a+c << b+c  implies  a<<b,
```

then every nonzero element of `Cu(A)` equals `[1_A]`.  Consequently `A` has
property (SP) and is purely infinite.

Only cancellation in pairs arising from a finite-unit-root identity is used.
The abstract truncated-soft-interval countermodel fails weak cancellation
exactly at those pairs.
