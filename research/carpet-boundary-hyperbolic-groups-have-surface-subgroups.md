Let `G` be a hyperbolic group whose Gromov boundary is homeomorphic to the Sierpinski carpet.

Then `G` contains a quasiconvex subgroup isomorphic to `pi_1(S)`, for a closed orientable surface
`S` of genus at least `2`.

No torsion-freeness is assumed. The subgroup has finite index in the stabilizer of a peripheral
circle of the boundary.
