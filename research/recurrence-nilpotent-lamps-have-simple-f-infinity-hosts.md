For `m,q≥2`, let `f₁,…,f_s:ℤ→ℤ` have eventually rational
constant-coefficient recurrent tails `f_i(n)` and `f_i(−n−1)`,
independently. Let `𝔽` be the additive span of their translates and
`1`. Then

`UT_m(ℤ[1/q])^(ℤ)⋊(𝔽⋊ℤ)`

embeds in a simple group of type `F_∞` generated by two finite-order
elements. The shift moves lamps one position; profile `f` acts on lamp
`n` by the grading dilation `δ_q^(f(n))`. The sum is restricted.
Finite exceptions in the profiles are allowed and must be detected
faithfully. This statement imposes no finite-rank condition on the
global translate module.

## Attempts

Sections 5–6 of the artifact construct independent arithmetic finite-root
bases, one forward observation lattice containing both tails and constant
isotropy, and a two-end shell embedding. A subgroup need not be normal
for its own left translations to preserve its complement. The faithful
shell test compares `u` and `uh`, avoiding the loss of finite-support
profiles. The finite-forward-lattice, arithmetic-realization and
compact-core SingFix claims remain open. BHM Theorem 2.1 and the BZ
simple-host/generation theorems are explicit classical inputs to the
conditional route, not Lean results.
