Import of three statements from M. Kapovich and B. Kleiner, *Hyperbolic groups with
low-dimensional boundary*, Ann. Sci. Ecole Norm. Sup. (4) 33 (2000) 647--669. Source: the
authors' PDF `www.math.ucdavis.edu/~kapovich/EPR/KK2000.pdf`, fetched 2026-09-16 and read
through a text extraction of its compressed streams. The extraction drops ligatures and some
spaces; the quotes below restore them and change nothing else.

1. **Theorem 4**, verbatim: "Let G be a hyperbolic group which does not split over a finite
   or virtually cyclic subgroup, and suppose ∂∞G is 1-dimensional. Then one of the following
   holds (see Section 2 for definitions): (1) ∂∞G is a Menger curve; (2) ∂∞G is a Sierpinski
   carpet; (3) ∂∞G is homeomorphic to S^1 and G maps onto a Schwartz triangle group with
   finite kernel."
2. **Theorem 8**, verbatim: "Let G be a hyperbolic group with boundary homeomorphic to the
   Sierpinski carpet S. Then: (1) there are finitely many G-orbits of peripheral circles in S;
   (2) the stabilizer of each peripheral circle C is a quasi-convex subgroup G whose boundary
   is C." In (2), "subgroup G" is the source's wording for a subgroup of `G`.
3. **Peripheral subgroups**, the sentence ending Section 4 (the proof of Theorem 8), verbatim:
   "From now on we will refer to stabilizers of peripheral circles as peripheral subgroups.
   By [24,17,43] each peripheral subgroup is, modulo a finite normal subgroup, a cocompact
   Fuchsian group in Isom(H^2)." The bibliography entries are [24] D. Gabai, *Convergence
   groups are Fuchsian groups*, Ann. Math. 136 (1992); [17] A. Casson and D. Jungreis,
   *Convergence groups and Seifert fibered 3-manifolds*, Invent. Math. 118 (1994); [43]
   P. Tukia, *Homeomorphic conjugates of Fuchsian groups*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 391 (1988).

No torsion-freeness is assumed in any of the three statements. The proof of Theorem 4 opens
with, verbatim: "The fact that G does not split over a finite group implies [40] that G is
one-ended, and ∂∞G is connected." Here [40] is J. Stallings, *On torsion-free groups with
infinitely many ends*, Ann. Math. 88 (1968).

As used here, "does not split" means "admits no nontrivial splitting as an amalgamated free
product or HNN extension".
