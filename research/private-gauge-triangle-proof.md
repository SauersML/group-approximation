For each nonempty allowed support `S`, choose a transitive
hyperoctahedral packet whose tautological type has joint spectrum exactly
`S`, equal atom multiplicities, and the common marked sign.  After countable
amplification, any exact context representation has infinite multiplicity
on every atom of its actual support, so it extends to the corresponding menu
type; unused menu factors act trivially.

Duplicate each gauge share on every graph half-edge.  On its private Pauli
factor represent source shares in the `Z` chart and target shares in the `X`
chart, keeping their product equal to the original logical word.  Hadamard
reset on that factor implements the edge covariance, and different edge
resets commute.

For triangle resets `W_DZ,W_ZR,W_DR`, set

```text
U_DZ=W_DZ W_DR,  U_ZR=W_ZR,
U_DR=W_ZR W_DZ W_DR.
```

Extra reset actions lie on factors invisible to the corresponding edge
group, so each `U` is a valid intertwiner and `U_ZR U_DZ=U_DR` exactly.
The original logical products and native mark are unchanged, proving the
exact HALT extension.
