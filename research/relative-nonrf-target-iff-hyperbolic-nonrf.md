  non-rf-relatively-hyperbolic-with-rf-peripherals: that is the relative existential target; this proves its exact equivalence to the ordinary hyperbolic counterexample problem.
  non-residually-finite-hyperbolic-group: that asks for the ordinary counterexample; this records both reductions without deciding whether either counterexample exists.

The following existential assertions are equivalent.

1. There is a group hyperbolic relative to finitely many finitely generated
   residually finite peripheral subgroups that is not residually finite.
2. There is a word-hyperbolic group that is not residually finite.

The reverse implication remains true after requiring the relative example to
have one nonempty, infinite, proper peripheral subgroup: from a hyperbolic
counterexample `H`, one may take `H * Z` relative to the free factor `Z`.
