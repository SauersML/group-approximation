title: Every countable residually finite group has uniquely ergodic Toeplitz subshifts almost 1-1 over any totally disconnected metric compactification (Cecchi-Bernales–Cortez–Gómez)
---

**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Let `G` be a countable residually finite group and `←G` a totally
disconnected metric compactification of `G`, with `G` acting by left multiplication. Then there are
a uniquely ergodic Toeplitz `G`-subshift `(X, σ, G)` and an almost 1-1 factor map `π : X -> ←G`, and
`π` is a measure conjugacy with the Haar measure.
