Let `G` be hyperbolic relative to finitely many conjugacy classes of
virtually polycyclic subgroups. Assume either that `G` is one-ended or
that `G` is virtually torsion-free. Then, for every automorphism
`Phi in Aut(G)`, the suspension

```text
G_Phi = G semidirectProduct_Phi Z
```

belongs to `AC(VNil)`.

Consequently, for each `X in {A,K,L}`, the group `G_Phi` satisfies the
fibred Farrell--Jones conjecture in `X`-theory with respect to the family
of virtually cyclic subgroups.
