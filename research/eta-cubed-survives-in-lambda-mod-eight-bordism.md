Let BSpin_8 -> BSpin be the homotopy fibre of lambda mod 8, where
2·lambda = p_1. Write Omega^8_* for bordism of closed manifolds with a
BSpin_8-structure on the stable tangent bundle. Framings induce such
structures, so there is a unit map pi_*^s -> Omega^8_*.

The image of eta^3 = 12 nu in Omega^8_3 is nonzero. More precisely, the
kernel of pi_3^s -> Omega^8_3 lies in {0, 8 nu, 16 nu}.

Contrast: Omega^Spin_3 = 0 kills eta^3, and the same argument with lambda
reduced mod 2 or mod 4 detects nothing at eta^3.

Complete argument. Not independently reviewed.
