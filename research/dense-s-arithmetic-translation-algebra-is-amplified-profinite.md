**THEOREM (established here; proof in
`dense-s-arithmetic-translation-algebra-proof`).**  Let `A = SL_n(Z[1/p])`
act on `L = SL_n(Q_p)` (Haar measure) by left translation, through the dense
inclusion, and let `C = SL_n(Z)` act on `K = SL_n(Z_p)` by left translation.
Put `N = L^infinity(K) rtimes C`, the profinite crossed product (a
Connes-embeddable II_1 factor).  Then

```text
L^infinity(L) rtimes A   =   N (x) B(l^2),                          (DT1)
```

a II_infinity factor; in particular it is Connes embeddable in the II_infinity
sense (every finite corner embeds in `R^omega`).  Concretely, the orbit
relation `R_A` of `A` on `L` restricted to any right coset `Kg` is the
`C`-translation relation on that coset, and `K` meets every `A`-orbit.

**Corollary.**  For any cocompact torsion-free lattice `Lambda' < L`, the
commuting right action of `Lambda'` on `L` gives

```text
L^infinity(L) rtimes (A x Lambda')  =  (N (x) B(l^2)) rtimes Lambda'
                                    =  (L^infinity(L/Lambda') rtimes A) (x) B(l^2 Lambda'),   (DT2)
```

so the homogeneous-quotient crossed product `M = L^infinity(L/Lambda') rtimes A`
of `homogeneous-quotient-ce-refutes-arithmetic-commutant-collapse` is a
II_1 corner of the crossed product of a Connes-embeddable II_infinity factor
by the residually finite lattice `Lambda'`.  Its Connes embeddability is
therefore an instance of the crossed-product permanence question for a
hyperlinear group acting on an embeddable factor, with the action given
explicitly: `Lambda'` permutes the right cosets `Kg` freely with `r` orbits
and acts on each `N`-corner by the right-translation automorphisms of `K`.
The other natural corner, over `K`, is the relation on `(K, Haar)`
generated by left `C`-translations and the partial maps
`x |-> a x l^-1` (`a in A`, `l in Lambda'`, wherever defined), i.e. by
`C`-translations together with `Lambda'`-conjugation germs on the profinite
group `K`.
