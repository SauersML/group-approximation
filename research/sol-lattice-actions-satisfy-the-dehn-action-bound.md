**Statement.** Let `M ∈ GL_2(ℤ)` have no eigenvalue of modulus 1, and let
`G = ℤ² ⋊_M ℤ`. Let `G` act cellularly, cocompactly and without inversions on
a simply connected simplicial complex `X` with `δ_X(n) < ∞`, finitely presented
vertex stabilizers and finitely generated edge stabilizers (setting (S) of
`zaremsky-1-17-dehn-function-from-cocompact-action-data`). Put
`dist_X(n) = max { dist^G_{G_σ}(n) : dim σ ≤ 2 }`. Then

```text
δ_G(n) ≼ δ_X(n) · max_v δ_{G_v}( dist_X‾( δ_X(n) ) ).          (UB)
```

Here `≼` is the relation of Llosa Isenrich–Weis (arXiv:2608.07191v1, §2.1):
`f(n) ≤ C g(Cn + C) + Cn + C`. The statement uses **normalization (N)**: every
Dehn function `δ` is replaced by `max{δ(n), n}`, and `dist_X(n)` by
`max{dist_X(n), n}`. This matches the convention already used in
`cocompact-action-data-do-not-determine-dehn-function` (every Dehn function
satisfies `n ≼ δ`). When `det M = −1` the group is only virtually a lattice in
Sol; "Sol lattice" in the title is used loosely for the whole family.

Some normalization of this kind is needed for (UB) to be non-degenerate at all:

- **Dehn functions.** `ℤ²` is an HNN extension of `ℤ`, whose presentation
  `⟨a | ⟩` has Dehn function `0`, so without it Theorem B of Llosa Isenrich–Weis
  would give `δ_{ℤ²} ≼ 0`.
- **Distortion.** A free cocompact action has `dist_X ≡ 0`, so without it the
  right-hand side of (UB) vanishes.

**Proof outline** (full proof in the artifact of
`sol-lattice-dehn-action-bound-proof`). `δ_G ≼ e^n` by pushing `t`-letters
through fiber words. A subgroup meeting `ℤ²` nontrivially lies in `ℤ²` or has
finite index. This gives three cases:

1. **Some vertex stabilizer has finite index.** Its Dehn function is `≃ δ_G`.
2. **Some vertex stabilizer is a nontrivial subgroup of `ℤ²`.** Fiber elements
   are exponentially distorted, `ℓ_G(z^m) = O(log m)`, so
   `dist_X(n) ≽ e^n`.
3. **Otherwise.** Every vertex stabilizer is trivial or cyclic, generated by an
   element acting on `ℝ²` as `p ↦ M^k p + w` with `k ≠ 0`, which has a unique
   fixed point.
   - This yields an equivariant map `X^(0) → ℝ²` for an area-preserving affine
     action.
   - The signed-area 1-cochain bounds `δ_X` from below by the area of orbit
     polygons, up to a linear error.
   - The orbit polygon of `[t^n a t^{−n}, t^{−n} a t^n]` has area
     `det(M^n e_1, M^{−n} e_1)`, of size `|λ|^{2n}`, whatever the base point.
     So `δ_X ≽ e^n`.

**Scope.** This is a calibration for the open claims
`dehn-bound-from-action-data-without-finite-index-faces` and
`some-action-violates-dehn-bound-without-finite-index-faces`. It does not
establish either, and it gives no route into the root.
