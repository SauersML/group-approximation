The joint spectral projections of the finitely many central involutions
`t_c` are central and sum to one.  On a profile sector, the three allowed
ONE3 words show directly that fixing `t_c=1` leaves the singleton `00`, while
fixing `t_c=0` leaves the affine pair `10,01`.  This gives `(CTO2)` clause by
clause.  A nonzero tracial carrier meets some profile sector nontrivially;
normalize that corner and apply the established finite LCS OR compiler.
