title: A finitely generated group quasi-isometric to a simply connected geodesic space is finitely presented
---

Let G be a finitely generated group quasi-isometric to a simply connected
geodesic metric space X. Then G is finitely presented.

Properness of X is not used. This is the case n = 2 of the geodesic reading
of Zaremsky Problem 1.3
(`zaremsky-1-03-qi-to-connected-space-implies-type-fn`). There the answer is
yes with no uniformity assumption, because only edges of a coarse loop have
to be realized in X, and geodesics do that.
