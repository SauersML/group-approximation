Let `F_G` be the finitely generated ambient strategy group and let `Q_G`
be its subgroup generated by the unreadable variables and the central
involution `J`.  If `mu` is a Z-aligned IRS strategy and `H` is a
`mu`-random subgroup, then for every readable bit `r` and `g in Q_G`
exactly one of

```text
g r g^(-1) in H,        J g r g^(-1) in H               (BRP1)
```

holds almost surely.  Define the readable configuration `Theta(H)` by
recording this choice for every pair `(g,r)`.  Conjugation makes
`Theta` equivariant.  The pullbacks of its finite cylinder algebras are
exactly the Boolean algebras generated by the projections

```text
pi(g) D_0 pi(g)^*,      g in Q_G.
```

Consequently

```text
dim D_infinity <= B
iff the law nu=Theta_*mu is concentrated on at most B configurations.
                                                               (BRP2)
```

Choose nested finite word sets `W_n` exhausting `F_G`, enlarged so that
they contain all conjugates in `(BRP1)` for the first `n` unreadable
words.  Let `Theta_n` be the corresponding finite readable pattern.  A
probability measure `nu` on the compact configuration space is concentrated
on at most `B` points if and only if every marginal `(Theta_n)_*mu` has
at most `B` positive atoms.  The forward implication is immediate.  For
the converse, if `nu` had `B+1` distinct points in its measure support,
finitely many coordinates would separate them into `B+1` disjoint
positive-measure cylinders, contradicting the marginal bound.

Now start with the random-pseudo-subgroup polytope used for
`zpc-irs-value-computable-upper-hierarchy` at level `n`.  Its rational
variables give the probabilities of every membership pattern
`H intersect W_n`.  Hence the probability of each value of `Theta_n` is
a rational linear form in those variables.  To impose support size at most
`B`, enumerate every subset `C` of at most `B` readable patterns and
set the probabilities of all patterns outside `C` equal to zero.  For
fixed `C` this is a rational face of the pseudo-IRS polytope.  There are
only finitely many choices of `C), so maximizing the rational game
functional over their union is an effective finite list of linear programs.
Let its largest optimum be `alpha_(n,B)(G)`.

Restriction from `W_(n+1)` to `W_n` preserves the support bound, so these
optima decrease.  Any bounded-factor ZPC-IRS strategy is feasible at every
level, giving the lower inequality in `(BRH1)`.  Conversely, take
near-maximizers along a subsequence on which the decreasing optima converge.
The same projective compactness argument as in Manzoor's pseudo-subgroup
hierarchy gives an actual IRS limit satisfying every ZPC cylinder equation.
The finite-pattern support conditions persist at every level; the preceding
separation argument and `(BRP2)` give `dim D_infinity<=B`.  Continuity of
the finite-coordinate payoff identifies its value with the limit of the
optima.

Finally, the set of IRS laws satisfying the ZPC equations and `(BRP2)` is
closed: probability measures supported on at most `B` points form a closed
subset of the probability measures on the compact readable-configuration
space.  It is therefore compact, and the continuous game functional attains
`omega_ZIRS^B(G)`.
