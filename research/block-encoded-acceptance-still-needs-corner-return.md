title: Fresh control qubits block-encode the acceptance compressor, but its return is still one Hecke corner moment
---

Fresh controls encode a fixed product of acceptance projections as one
corner of one unitary exactly, even for noncommuting tests.  This removes
the additive naming problem, but arbitrary representations still require a
selected controller orientation and a mixed source-to-corner return moment.

Thus block encoding reduces the backend to one corner-return scalar plus
source saturation; ordinary HNN or finite-wreath relations do not prove
either scalar.
