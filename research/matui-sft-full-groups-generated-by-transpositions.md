**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`matui-sft-full-groups-generated-by-transpositions-citation`). H. Matui, *Topological full
groups of one-sided shifts of finite type*, arXiv:1210.5800, read from the TeX source on MSI on 2026-09-13.
- **Remark `HigThomp`:** "This identification yields an isomorphism between the Higman-Thompson group $V_{n,1}$ and the
  topological full group $[[G]]$", where `G` is the groupoid of the full shift over `n` symbols. The identification is
  Nekrashevych's `V_(n,1) ⊆ U(O_n)` (Prop 9.6 of the paper cited there as Ne04JOP).
- **Transpositions** (§ on the kernel of the index map): "We call $\alpha\in[[G|Y]]$ a transposition if there exists a
  clopen set $A\subset Y$ such that $A\cap\alpha(A)=\emptyset$, $\supp(\alpha)=A\cup\alpha(A)$ and $\alpha^2=1$."
- **Lemma `Tgenerate`:** "Suppose that $(X,\sigma)$ is topologically mixing. Then $[[G|Y]]_0$ is generated by the set
  $\mathcal{T}$ of transpositions."
- **§6.7:** "One has $H_0(G_{n,r})\cong\Coker(\id-M^t)\cong\Z_{n-1}$ and $H_1(G_{n,r})\cong\Ker(\id-M^t)=0$ ... From
  $H_1(G_{n,r})=0$, we obtain $[[G_{n,r}]]=[[G_{n,r}]]_0$ ... $[[G_{n,r}]]/D([[G_{n,r}]])\cong\Z_{n-1}\otimes\Z_2$,
  which is $0$ if $n$ is even and $\Z_2$ if $n$ is odd."

**Consequence used downstream.** The full shift is topologically mixing, so `V_(n,1) = [[G_(n,1)]]` is generated by
transpositions.
