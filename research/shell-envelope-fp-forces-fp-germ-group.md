**ESTABLISHED** by `shell-envelope-fp-forces-fp-germ-group-proof` (written proof,
not independently reviewed, no novelty claimed).

Let P be infinite and generated by a finite set S, and let nu: P -> N be any
enumeration. Use the shell construction of
`shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`, with p = 0^infinity,
E_nu = <V, hat(P)>, and Q = (E_nu)_p = <germs of rho(P), tau>.

If E_nu is finitely presented, then Q is finitely presented. More precisely,
fix a finite generating set Y of V and a finite presentation
E_nu = <Y u hat(S) | R>. Then there is a finite set W_R of words in tau and S,
read off from R and finitely many values of nu, such that

    Q = <tau, S | W_R>.

Consequently, by `shell-germ-group-has-index-two-fiber-product` and
`shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate`:

    E_nu finitely presented
      ==> F_nu = R_nu x_Z R_nu finitely presented
      ==> R_nu finitely presented, and R_nu passes the ascending HNN gate.

So these germ tests are necessary for `decidable-inputs-have-fp-shell-envelopes`
itself, not only for the stronger three-stabilizer hypothesis.

The converse is not asserted. Neither is finite presentation of A_1 or of the
two-point stabilizers.

Mechanism. Map the Schreier 2-complex of St(p) into the groupoid obtained from
the germ groupoid of V on Omega by adjoining free loops at p for the hat letters.
Relator cells give only finitely many loops at p. The neighbourhood kernel dies
because each of its elements commutes with a V element supported near p that
moves p off the finitely many points where its word applies a hat letter at p.
