**OPEN.** Notation of Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir, arXiv:1204.6506v5, §4.1, read in
the TeX (`gq/src/kms/KMS-322.tex`, lines 922–1075). There `G(M) = T ⋊ Q`:
- `T` is the normal closure of the configuration letters `x_u`, abelian of exponent `p`
  (Lemma lT);
- `Q = ⟨H_1 ∪ H_2⟩` is the metabelian top (Lemma lH).

**Claim.** For every recursive time bound there is a machine `M` such that `G(M)` is
harder than that bound, as in `kms-arbitrarily-hard-fp-rf-groups`, and also:
- (i) `Q` acts faithfully and self-similarly on some `T_X`;
- (ii) `T`, as an `F_p[Q]`-module, carries a self-similar module structure `(ρ, λ)` over
  that action, in the sense of item 2 of
  `lamp-digit-modules-give-self-similar-semidirect-products`.

By that node, (i) and (ii) make `G(M)` a finitely presented self-similar group on
`T_{X×F_p}`, with the given hard word problem (route
`hard-self-similar-groups-via-self-similar-kms-modules`).

## What is known (lane bh-free-25, 2026-09-18; lane proofs, not reviewed)

1. **The top is a product of Baumslag-type groups.** Killing `T` leaves only the
   relations G1–G4 (G5–G8 all involve `x`-letters). By G2, `Q ≅ B_1 × ⋯ × B_K × B_0` with:
   - `B_i = ⟨a_i, a_i', A_i⟩` for `i ≥ 1`. By G3 and Lemma lBRG with `f(t) = t − 1`, the
     lamp module is `F_p[a^{±1}, (a^{-1} − 1)^{-1}]`, with `a'` acting as `(a^{-1} − 1)^{-1}`.
     The composite `R → ⟨⟨A⟩⟩ → R`, `1 ↦ A ↦ 1`, is the identity, so the lamp module is
     exactly this ring.
   - `B_0 = ⟨ã_i, ã_i', A_0 : i ≤ K⟩` has the K-variable lamp ring
     `F_p[ã_i^{±1}, (ã_i^{-1} − 1)^{-1} : i ≤ K]`.
2. **The one-variable factors are automaton groups, for odd `p`.** Take `c ∈ F_p \ {0,1}`
   and let `B_i` act on `F_p[[X]]` (the `p`-ary tree of `X`-adic digits):
   - `a` acts as multiplication by `u = c + X`, `a'` as multiplication by `u/(1 − u)`,
     and `A` as translation by `1`.
   - The lamp ring maps isomorphically onto `F_p[X]_S`, where `S` is generated by
     `c + X` and `1 − c − X`. This ring is closed under `f ↦ (f − f(0))/X`.
   - Multiplication by a unit `w` has sections `h ↦ w h + (element of the lamp ring)`.
   - Every element involved is a rational power series, so it has finitely many tails.
   - So `B_i` is a faithful finite-state self-similar group. KMS fix an arbitrary prime
     `p`; their proofs were not re-checked for odd `p` here.
3. **`B_0` has no scalar realization of this shape when `K ≥ 2`.**
   - A finitely generated subring of `F_p[[X]]` closed under `f ↦ (f − f(0))/X` has
     generators satisfying `g = g(0) + X·P(g)` for a polynomial system `P`. Its
     Jacobian at `X = 0` is the identity, so every element is algebraic over `F_p(X)`,
     and the ring has transcendence degree at most 1.
   - The lamp ring of `B_0` has transcendence degree `K`.
   - So `B_0` needs a matrix-affine or genuinely nonlinear realization. None is
     constructed here.
4. **Necessary shape of `λ`.**
   - `λ` is `Q`-invariant, so it factors through the coinvariants `T/[T, Q]`.
   - `x_{uA_i} = [x_u, A_i]`, so the images of all `x_{uA_i}` vanish, and G6 and G8
     become trivial.
   - Hence `T/[T, Q]` is spanned by the images of `x_{q_0}, …, x_{q_N}`, and `λ` is
     determined by `N + 1` values.
   - All separation must come from the maps `ρ_x`.
5. **Naive Cartier maps do not supply `ρ`.** See the Attempts on
   `fp-self-similar-groups-with-arbitrarily-hard-word-problem` from this lane:
   - the plain Cartier projection breaks the `Add` relations;
   - binomial counter encodings force every counter step to be `0`.
   So `ρ` must act by the polynomial operators `P_i` or by odometer-type carries.

## What would decide it

- Either a matrix or nonlinear self-similar realization of `B_0` for `K = 2`, together
  with explicit maps `ρ_x` on the `x_{q_j}`-generated module satisfying (S1);
- or an obstruction, for example a `Q`-invariant property of self-similar module
  structures that the machine relations G8 violate.
