The ambient classification proves that no finite simple group contains
`A8` at an index from 991 through 2199.  Its only arithmetically possible
interior candidate is `L3(9)`, excluded by the exact projective 2-rank
argument.  Therefore every augmented finite-simple escape has index at least
2200.

At equality the same classification gives `S=HS`, one conjugacy class of
`S8`-derived chart subgroups, and ambient realization of the graph
automorphism.  This yields exactly the equality description in `(OSI2200)`
without claiming the remaining marked relations are solvable.
