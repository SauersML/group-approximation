Crisp's injective maps (quoted in Paris, arXiv:0711.2372, §6) embed every
spherical-type Artin group in a direct product of Artin groups of types
`A_n`, `D_n` and `E_k` (k = 6,7,8).

- **Types A and D:** these factors satisfy the permutational Boone--Higman
  property by BFFHZ Theorem A and Corollary B, since embedding in a twisted
  Brin--Thompson group passes to subgroups.
- **Products:** PBH is closed under direct products (BFFHZ and [Zarb], as
  recorded in the frontier artifact).
- **E factors:** they are the hypothesis.

Hence the product, and every subgroup of it, satisfies PBH. The target is a
conditional statement, so no Boone--Higman root is established by this route.
