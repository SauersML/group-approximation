A C\*-simple group has trivial amenable radical
(Breuillard--Kalantar--Kennedy--Ozawa: C\*-simplicity is equivalent
to freeness of the Furstenberg boundary action and forces the
amenable radical trivial — the easy direction: a nontrivial amenable
normal subgroup obstructs simplicity of the reduced algebra).  A
nontrivial C\*-simple linear group therefore satisfies the hypotheses
of the required Vigdorovich import (nontrivial linear, trivial
amenable radical), so its reduced algebra is selfless; Robert's
theorem converts selflessness into strict comparison with unique
quasitrace.
