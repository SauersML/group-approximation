Let `z_0,z_1` be commuting central involutions and let `z=z_0 z_1`.  In every
representation on which both children act as `-I`, the parent acts as `+I`.
Equivalently, an untwisted wreath recursion which sends a marked central lift
to `(z,z)` cannot reproduce the `-1` sector through binary refinement.

Therefore a self-similar negative sign tape must contain a nontrivial phase:
a central extension cocycle, a projective lift, or an explicit extra central
factor.  The raw equation `z_n=z_(n+1) z'_(n+1)` is not a valid marked-sign
completeness law.
