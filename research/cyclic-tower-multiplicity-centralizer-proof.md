identify `R` with `M_m(A)`. Across-colour roots and the spare-colour
commutator prove `EL_3(R)=EL_(3m)(A)` under flattening. Refinement sends

over `F_2`; every amplified elementary root is a product of elementary
roots. Nonzero block positions and block entries prove injectivity.
