The matrix-unit rule `E_rsE_tu=delta_(s,t)E_ru` proves every equation in
`(PAO2)`.  It also gives `Ca=C` and `C^2=C`, while `aC=q`; the standard
three-elementary block calculation therefore makes `P` an involution.

Embed each `4 by 4` elementary matrix over `M_3(F_2)` as a `12 by 12`
binary matrix.  Multiplying the `(1,4)` blocks of `P` and `R` gives
`P^2=R^2=1` and `(PR)^3=1`; their off-diagonal blocks differ because
`a!=A_j`, so `P!=R`.  Root-coordinate faithfulness and `q!=0` give
`x_14(q)!=1`.  This proves `(PAO5)--(PAO6)` by explicit finite matrix
arithmetic.

Finally the subgroup of `GL_12(F_2)` generated by the finitely many cell
matrices is finite.  A nonidentity left regular translation has trace zero,
which proves `(PAO7)`.
