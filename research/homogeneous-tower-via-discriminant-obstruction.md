**Dead.**  The plan was to choose a diagonal tower with
`Delta(E_j)^(floor(N_j/2)) != 0` at every stage, so that
`full-square-zero-forces-discriminant-power-vanishing` excludes a full
square-zero section at each stage.  Along any tower of multiplicity form with
unbounded rank that power vanishes at all large stages, by
`discriminant-obstruction-dies-in-multiplicity-towers`, which invalidates this
route.
