**Problem XLIX of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(after Connes--Jones, who separated Cartans in a II₁ factor by
relative property Γ).  Does a unital simple separable nuclear
C\*-algebra exist containing Cartan subalgebras both with and without
relative uniform property Γ?

## Attempts

* STW note the dynamical-characterisation conjecture (Problem XXXIV's
  optimistic form) predicts NO for Cartans from free minimal group
  actions: relative Γ = SBP would be forced by `Z`-stability of the
  ambient algebra, making relative Γ an invariant of the algebra, not
  the Cartan.  So a positive example must either use non-dynamical
  Cartans (Li-type twisted-limit Cartans, whose relative Γ nobody has
  computed) or refute the XXXIV conjecture along the way — this
  problem is a designated pressure point on XXXIV.
* Sharp candidate: the Giol--Kerr algebra `A` (if
  `giol-kerr-crossed-products-have-uniform-gamma` holds).  Its
  dynamical Cartan fails relative Γ (mean dimension > 0 = SBP failure,
  Lindenstrauss--Weiss/KLTV), while `A` would have absolute uniform
  Γ; a SECOND Cartan with relative Γ inside the same `A` (e.g. from a
  mean-dimension-zero model of the same completion, Problem XXXV
  territory) would answer XLIX affirmatively.  So XXXV + XX +
  a Cartan-transport step is a live route to a positive answer —
  recorded as the concrete plan, pinned on realizing the second
  Cartan.
