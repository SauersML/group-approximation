**ESTABLISHED PREFIX COMPRESSOR.**  In Baumslag's torsion metabelian group

```text
Lambda_2=<a,b,c | a^2=1, [b,c]=1, [a^b,a]=1, a^c=a^b a>,
```

use `x^y=y^(-1)xy` and put

```text
B_n=b^n a b^(-n).                                    (BBC1)
```

Adjoin one unitary letter `v` and only the two centralizer rows

```text
[v,c]=1,                     [v,B_(-1)]=1.            (BBC2)
```

Then

```text
[v,B_(-r)]=1                         (r>=1).           (BBC3)
```

Consequently the translated words

```text
v_n=b^n v b^(-n)
```

satisfy the entire first-hit prefix table

```text
[v_n,B_k]=1                           (k<n).           (BBC4)
```

To see this without an infinite induction, write the lamp module additively
over `F_2`, let `T` denote conjugation by `b`, and note that conjugation by
`c` is `1+T`.  Lucas' parity identity gives, for `r>=1`,

```text
B_(-r)
 = product_(j subset_bits (r-1)) (B_(-1))^(c^j).      (BBC5)
```

Indeed the right side is

```text
T sum_(j subset_bits (r-1))(1+T)^j
 =T(1+(1+T))^(r-1)=T^r
```

on the cyclic lamp generator.  There are at most `r` factors in `(BBC5)`.
Rows `(BBC2)` therefore make `v` commute with every factor, proving `(BBC3)`;
conjugating by `b^n` proves `(BBC4)`.

The normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt authentication cost is polynomial.  The word
on the right of `(BBC5)` has length `O(r^2)`.  Baumslag's quadratic Dehn
bound fills `(BBC5)` with `O(r^4)` base relators, and moving `v` through its
at most `r` conjugate factors uses `O(r^2)` conjugates of `(BBC2)`.  Thus a
unitary approximate representation of the finite enlarged presentation obeys

```text
||[rho(v_n),rho(B_k)]-1||_2
 <= C(n-k+1)^4 sqrt(E(rho))             (k<n),        (BBC6)
```

with `C` independent of matrix dimension.  This is enough for every fixed
first-hit depth and, unlike a list of rows, is generated by one finite
template.

This theorem does not couple `v_n` to a forbidden Reynolds payload and does
not prove carrier persistence.  It closes the infinite address-prefix
commutation problem only; `baumslag-address-geometric-stationary-profile`
shows that this alone still admits the regular geometric first-hit flow.
