**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Ω_U` be Labbé's 19-tile Wang shift and `q` a prime power. Then
`LC(Ω_U, F_q) ⋊ Z^2` embeds unitally in an algebraic ultraproduct of matrix algebras over fields iff there is a
finite-index `Λ ≤ Z^2` such that `𝒯_(1,Λ)(Ω_U, F_q)` has a nonzero finite-dimensional module.

`𝒯_(1,Λ)(Ω_U, F_q)` is generated by idempotents `e_a(t)` (`a ∈ U`, `t ∈ Z^2/Λ`) with the following relations:
- the `e_a(t)` at one site form a partition of unity;
- generators at sites whose difference has a representative in `B_2` commute;
- `prod_(h ∈ B_1) e_(π(h))(t + h) = 0` for every `3×3` pattern `π` that is not globally legal for `Ω_U`.

**Hypotheses verified** (artifact §6, Theorem C; §7).
- `Ω_U` is free, minimal, nearest-neighbour.
- Labbé's `ω = αβγ` is a 2-dimensional morphism on `L(Ω_U)` with `ω(L(Ω_U)) ⊆ L(Ω_U)`. This is imported, and
  re-checked exactly from Labbé's figure source: shapes agree on all colour-matching dominoes, and seams are valid.
- `ω^2` has every letter image of shape `>= (2,2)` (exact computation). Alternatively `ω^8`, using only `M^7 > 0` and
  one `(2,2)` letter image.

**Consequence.** Row 7 becomes one question at scale `1`. A single module decides it positively. A proof that no
`Λ` admits one decides it negatively. Every reviewed firewall still applies to such a module: it is contextual,
both axis periods are `>= 6`, and narrow strips are noncommutative.
