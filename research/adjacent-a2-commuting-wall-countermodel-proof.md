Adjacent `A_2` positive systems intersect in two commuting roots, with the
simple and central roles exchanged.  In the regular representation of the
finite Heisenberg group, restriction to the subgroup generated by its simple
root `y` and center `z` is a multiple of the regular representation of
`C_p^2`.  Hence a unitary can swap `lambda(y)` and `lambda(z)`.  Conjugating
the whole packet by that unitary gives a second exact regular packet sharing
both wall matrices literally with the required role exchange.

The packet algebras remain different because the same shared matrix is
central in one and noncentral in the other.  Tensoring the implementer with
a two-bit swap supplies the independent four-atom carrier.  Orthogonal
projection onto the first regular group algebra gives the cross-Gram formula
for the off-wall generator and proves that all pairwise wall moments are
silent on the remaining positive mixed defect.
