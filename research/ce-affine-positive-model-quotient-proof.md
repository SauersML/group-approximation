The given CE model induces rho:Gamma->U(M) for a CE tracial M, with
rho(J)=omega(J)I. Its image H is a countable hyperlinear quotient in which
J retains exact order n. The artifact proves this via the characters
((1+tau(rho(h)))/2)^m, which converge to the canonical trace of H.

The normalized canonical J-character corner of L(H) is CE and pulls back
to a new CE model of the original corner. On any finite abelian context
A, its spectral support is the affine coset of characters factoring
through the image of A and agreeing with omega on its intersection with
the central cyclic subgroup. An affine decoder sends this to an affine
source support. Universal CE-exactness makes it allowed and supplies
consistent shared source variables; the source algebra remains CE as a
subalgebra of the quotient corner. The artifact details every step,
including possibly noninjective context maps.
