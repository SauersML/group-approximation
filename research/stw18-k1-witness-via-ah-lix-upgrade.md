**DEAD**: invalidated by `simple-ah-strict-comparison-is-k1-injective`.

The proposal: keep the LIX inductive systems (homogeneous blocks
`Gamma(X_i, End E_i)` with twisted pullback-plus-point-evaluation maps) and
tune the base spaces, line-bundle twists and weights so the limit has strict
comparison while the unit-level Euler-degree obstruction persists.

Any such limit is a unital simple AH algebra. Toms makes a unital simple AH
algebra with strict comparison Z-stable, and Jiang makes it K1-injective. So
the obstruction cannot persist. The same argument covers every AH design,
not only the LIX shape.
