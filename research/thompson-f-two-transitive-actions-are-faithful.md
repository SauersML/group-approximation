title: Every 2-transitive action of Thompson's group F on at least three points is faithful, with [F,F] transitive
---

Let `F` act on a set `Ω` with `|Ω| ≥ 3`, transitively on ordered pairs of
distinct points. Then:

1. the action is faithful;
2. `Ω` is infinite;
3. `[F,F]` acts transitively on `Ω`.

So Problem 2.20 is the question whether `F` has any 2-transitive action on at
least three points.
