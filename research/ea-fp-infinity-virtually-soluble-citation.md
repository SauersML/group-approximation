Citation import, secondary.

**Source.** S. St. John-Green, *Cohomological Finiteness Properties of Groups*, PhD
thesis, University of Southampton (October 2014), arXiv:1410.4363v1 (16 October 2014),
§6.2.7 "Elementary amenable groups" (PDF page 133). PDF text extracted with pypdf and
read on 2026-09-16. Verbatim: "If G is an elementary amenable group of type FP∞ then G
is virtually soluble [KMPN09, p.4], in particular Bredon duality over Z of dimension hG."
The bibliography entry: "[KMPN09] P. H. Kropholler, C. Martínez-Pérez, and
B. E. A. Nucinkis, Cohomological finiteness conditions for elementary amenable groups,
J. Reine Angew. Math. 637 (2009), 49–62."

**Background, recorded by other lanes.** `elementary-amenable-f-infinity-groups-are-type-vf-citation`
quotes Kropholler–Martínez-Pérez (arXiv:1805.10052v2): Kropholler's work "led to a proof
that elementary amenable groups of type FP_∞ over Z are constructible". It also records
the zbMATH review Zbl 1202.20055 of the 2009 paper. Neither passage states virtual
solubility in those words, so neither is counted as corroboration here.
