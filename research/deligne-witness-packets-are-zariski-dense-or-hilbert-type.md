**Setting.** As in `deligne-schur-witness-needs-maslov-carrying-packet`:
`Gamma = Sp_4(Z)`, and `alpha = e_3` is Deligne's multiplier. A witness is a
nonsingular `w in Gamma * <t>` with `alpha_H(K_2(Gamma, w)) != 0`.

**Claim.** Let `w` be a witness, `B_c <= Gamma` the subgroup generated by its
coefficients, and `H` the identity component of the Zariski closure of `B_c`
in `Sp_4`. Then:

1. `H = Sp_4`, or `H` is a `Q`-simple form of `SL_2 x SL_2` acting on
   `W_1 + W_2`, with the invariant planes defined over a real quadratic field
   and `H(R) = SL_2(R) x SL_2(R)` (Hilbert type, `SL_1(D)` over `O_K`).
2. `B_c` is not virtually a torsion-free Fuchsian group, whatever `H` is.

**Classification of the dead cases.**

| case | what `H` is | where it dies |
|---|---|---|
| C0 | non-reductive | the fixed space of the unipotent radical is rational (P2) |
| C1 | a torus | amenable |
| C2a | `A1` of type `[2,1,1]` | its fixed plane is rational (P2) |
| C2b | principal `A1` of type `[4]` | `SU(2)`: finite; `SL_2(R)`: virtually Fuchsian |
| C2c | `A1` of type `[2,2]`, `H = H'T` | `T` R-split: real Lagrangian `W (x) u_+` (P3); `T` compact or trivial: finite-by-Fuchsian or finite |
| C2d | `A1 x A1`, planes rational | P2 |
| C2d | `A1 x A1`, `K` imaginary quadratic | `H(R) = SL_2(C)`, simply connected (P4) |
| C2d | `A1 x A1`, `K` real quadratic, at least one compact factor | finite or virtually Fuchsian |

Types `G2`, `A2` and `C2` other than `Sp_4`, and `A1 x A1` acting on
`V1 (x) V1`, admit no faithful 4-dimensional symplectic action.

**Changed belief.** The explicit maximal order `Lambda` of `D = (-1, 3)_Q`
gives the packet `Lambda^1 <= Sp_4(Z)`. It has torsion, `H_2(-; Q) = Q`,
winding 2 and compact commutant, and it survives P1--P4, Howie and
Forester--Rourke. It is killed here.
