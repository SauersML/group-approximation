Let `P` be a separable pure C-star algebra such that

```text
P tensor_min P
```

is pure.  Let `(A_i)_(i in I)` and `(B_j)_(j in J)` be finite or countable
families of separable C-star algebras such that every member of either family
is either isomorphic to `P` or is Z-stable.  Then

```text
A=direct_sum_(i in I) A_i,       B=direct_sum_(j in J) B_j
```

are pure and so is `A tensor_min B`.

In particular one may take `P=C*_r(F_2)`.  If `N` is any unital separable
nonexact C-star algebra and

```text
R=N tensor_min Z,       A=C*_r(F_2) direct_sum R,
```

then `A` is separable, unital, nonsimple, nonexact, pure, and non-Z-stable,
while `A tensor_min A` is pure.  Thus this positive Problem XCV class can
have both factors simultaneously nonexact and non-Z-stable.
