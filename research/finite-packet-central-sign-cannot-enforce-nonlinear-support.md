Let `K` be finite, let the central involution `J` belong to an abelian
selector subgroup `A`, and fix any character `chi` of `A` with `chi(J)=-1`.
Some irreducible representation of `K` has central sign `J=-I` and contains
the `A`-weight `chi`.  Consequently the union of selector spectra over the
whole marked sector is every character with `J=-1`; neither enlarging the
finite packet nor gauge-doubling can enforce a proper nonlinear truth set
there.

The result rules out a common central sign as a threshold-free Fanizza
carrier.  It does not rule out a selected irreducible-type projection or a
genuinely infinite matrix-only actuator.
