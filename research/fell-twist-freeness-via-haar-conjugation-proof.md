The body: pre-composing the twist with a relative Haar unitary is free to do
(commutes with L, preserves the edge relation); the amalgamated Voiculescu
theorem then gives freeness for a genuine B_n-Haar twist, proving the central
(phi=0) case and re-deriving the central-HNN closure; for phi != 0 the twist's
B_n-valued distribution is pinned by the edge relation, so the remaining
estimate is amalgamated strong freeness of a cocycle-twisted shift.
