Write `q:G->A` for the quotient map and put

```text
r=dim_Q(Q tensor_Z A).
```

The group `G` is amenable because locally finite and abelian groups are
amenable and amenability is closed under extensions.

## The finite-stage group theory

Let `H subset G` be finitely generated.  By `(KF)`, `F=H intersection L` is
finite, and restriction of `q` gives

```text
1 -> F -> H -> q(H) -> 1,                                (2)
```

where `q(H)` is finitely generated abelian.  For completeness, (2) makes `H`
virtually abelian.  Pass to the finite-index kernel of the conjugation map
`H->Aut(F)`, and call it `H_0`.  Its kernel under `q` is
`F_0=H_0 intersection F`, which is finite and central in `H_0`; because
`q(H_0)` is abelian, `[H_0,H_0] subset F_0`.  Choose lifts
`x_1,...,x_d` of generators of `q(H_0)`, and let `m` annihilate `F_0`.
The elements `x_i^m` commute because

```text
[x_i^m,x_j^m]=[x_i,x_j]^(m^2)=1.
```

They generate an abelian subgroup of finite index.  Finite kernels do not
change Hirsch length, so

```text
h(H)=rank_Q(q(H))<=r.                                    (3)
```

## The two ranks

The map `G_ab->A` induced by `q` is onto.  Its kernel is the image of `L`,
hence is torsion because `L` is locally finite.  Tensoring with `Q` therefore
gives

```text
rank_Q(G_ab)=rank_Q(A)=r.                                (4)
```

If `r<infinity`, (2)--(3) show that `G` is locally virtually abelian with
local Hirsch rank at most `r`.  The imported exact theorem of
Chan--Lippert--Moutzouris--Weld for finitely generated virtually abelian
groups, together with the previously proved subgroup-exhaustion theorem, is
packaged as `stw84-locally-virtually-abelian-uniform-rank`; it gives

```text
dim_nuc(C*(G))<=r.
```

Equation (4) and `stw84-abelianization-rank-lower-bound` give the reverse
inequality.  If `r=infinity`, that same lower bound already forces infinite
nuclear dimension.  This proves the claim in every rank.

## Semidirect-product criterion and a nontrivial example

Suppose `G=L rtimes_alpha A` satisfies `(FOH)`.  If `H` is generated by
finitely many pairs `(l_i,a_i)`, put `B=<a_i>` and let `K` be the finite group
generated by the `B`-orbits of the finitely many `l_i`.  Multiplication in the
semidirect product shows `H intersection L subset K`, proving `(KF)`.

For a concrete non-direct example, let `X` be the disjoint union of one
cycle of every positive integer length, let

```text
L=direct_sum_X Z/2,
```

and let the generator of `Z` rotate every cycle.  Every finite subset of `L`
meets only finitely many cycles, so `(FOH)` holds and

```text
dim_nuc(C*(L rtimes Z))=1.
```

The action has infinite order.  The group is not virtually abelian: for every
`n!=0`, the fixed subgroup of the `n`-th power of the rotation has infinite
index in `L`, whereas an abelian finite-index subgroup would force such a
power to fix a finite-index subgroup of `L` pointwise.

The new input in this route is the extension-theoretic reduction (2)--(4) and
the criterion `(FOH)`.  The finite-stage nuclear-dimension computation and
inductive-limit permanence are imported through the two named prerequisite
claims; no crossed-product nuclear-dimension theorem is assumed.
