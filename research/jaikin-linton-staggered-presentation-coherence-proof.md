Use Andrei Jaikin-Zapirain and Marco Linton, *On the coherence of one-relator
groups and their group algebras*, Annals of Mathematics (2) 201 (2025),
909--959, doi:10.4007/annals.2025.201.3.4, Theorem 1.5, proved in Section 6.3
from Theorems 6.4--6.6.

A finite staggered presentation complex is bireducible. Theorem 6.6 proves
that Magnus subgroups of fundamental groups of finite bireducible complexes
have the finitely generated intersection property. The Karrass--Solitar
graph-of-groups criterion quoted as Theorem 6.5 then lets Theorem 6.4 induct on
the number of two-cells: a reduction splits the group as an amalgam of coherent
smaller vertex groups over such a Magnus subgroup. Hence the fundamental
group of every finite bireducible complex is coherent.

For an infinite staggered presentation, the Freiheitssatz writes the group as
a bi-infinite staggered amalgam. Every finitely generated subgroup is
contained in a finite consecutive window, and that window is the fundamental
group of a finite bireducible complex. Theorem 6.4 makes the window coherent,
so the subgroup is finitely presented. Thus the whole group is coherent.
QED
