Residual finiteness supplies finite quotients separating an exhausting
sequence of finite word sets.  Their left regular characters vanish exactly
on those sets.  Tensor products preserve normalized-HS edge and commutator
norms, while the trace of every normal-form evaluation factorizes and hence
becomes canonical.
