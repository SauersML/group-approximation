Source: Sam P. Fisher and Andrew Ng, *Outer automorphism groups and the
Atiyah Conjecture*, arXiv:2606.19606v1 (2026).

Theorem 1.5 states that for every field `K`, both `Out(A_Gamma)` and
`Mod(Sigma_(g,b,p))` contain torsion-free finite-index subgroups whose
`K`-group algebras embed into division rings. Corollary 4.8 gives the same
conclusion for handlebody mapping class groups. A subring of a division ring
has no nonzero zero divisors, yielding the stated domain consequence.

This route does not promote the positive-characteristic conclusion to a
Strong Atiyah statement: the source presents division-ring embeddability as
the algebraic all-characteristic analogue, while Strong Atiyah is stated over
`C`.
