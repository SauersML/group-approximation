title: One-relator products of coherent locally indicable groups are coherent
distinct_from:
  one-relator-groups-are-coherent: that is the ordinary one-relator case; here both factors may be arbitrary coherent locally indicable groups.
  groups-with-staggered-presentations-are-coherent: that is formulated for staggered presentations, not factor-relative one-relator products.
---

Let `A` and `B` be locally indicable coherent groups and let
`w in A*B` not be conjugate to an element of either factor. Then

```text
(A*B)/normal_closure(w)
```

is coherent.

The statement includes the proper-power case.
