title: Vanishing Kadison--Kastler distance identifies the two norm ultraproducts

Let `omega` be a free ultrafilter and, for every `n`, let `A_n,B_n` be
C*-subalgebras of one ambient C*-algebra `C_n`.  If

```text
lim_(n->omega) d_KK(A_n,B_n)=0,
```

then their canonical images in the ambient norm ultraproduct are literally
equal:

```text
prod_omega A_n = prod_omega B_n  subset prod_omega C_n.
```

Thus a hypothetical sequence of XCIX counterexamples at distances tending to
zero has one common limit algebra.  The discrepancy between the two sides can
only remain at the coordinate level.
