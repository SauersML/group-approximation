A computation from two classical theorems; no new result is claimed.

* **Stability.** `R` is noetherian of Krull dimension 3. By van der Kallen's
  injective stability for K2 (Weibel, *The K-book*, Remark III.5.5.2), the map
  `K2(r,R) -> K2(R)` is an isomorphism for `r >= 3 + 3 = 6`. By definition
  `K2(r,R)` is the kernel of `St_r(R) -> E_r(R)`.
* **Fundamental theorem.** For a regular ring `A` (Weibel, Theorem V.6.3),
  `K2(A[t^(+-1)]) = K2(A) (+) K1(A).{-,t}` and
  `K1(A[t^(+-1)]) = K1(A) (+) K0(A)`, where the second summand of `K1` is
  generated by the unit `t`. Start from `K0(F_2) = Z`, `K1(F_2) = 0` and
  `K2(F_2) = 0`:
  * `R_1 = F_2[x_1^(+-1)]`: `K1 = Z x_1`, `K2 = 0`;
  * `R_2 = R_1[x_2^(+-1)]`: `K1 = Z x_1 (+) Z x_2`, `K2 = Z {x_1,x_2}`;
  * `R = R_2[x_3^(+-1)]`: `K2 = Z {x_1,x_2} (+) Z {x_1,x_3} (+) Z {x_2,x_3}`.
* **Symbols at rank six.** The rank-three symbol convention defines each
  `c(x_i,x_j)` inside `St_6(R)`. Stabilization sends it to the stable symbol,
  so under the isomorphism above the three elements generate `K2(6,R)`.
  Killing three generators of a normal subgroup presents the quotient.
