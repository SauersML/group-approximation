**Tools.**
- **Ultraproducts.**  A tracial ultraproduct `prod_W N_i` is the algebra of
  norm-bounded sequences modulo `||.||_2`-null sequences along `W`, with
  `tau_W([x_i]) = lim_W tau(x_i)`.  An element of norm at most 1 has a
  representative made of contractions, as in step 2 of
  `group-factor-local-universality-ultraproduct-proof`.
- **Lemma M** (moments determine the embedding) is proved on route
  `locally-universal-group-factor-nonhyperlinear-proof`.  Suppose `a_i`
  generate `N`, and contractions `b_i` in a tracial `M` satisfy
  `sigma(p(b)) = tau(p(a))` for all *-polynomials `p`.  Then `a_i -> b_i`
  extends to a unital trace-preserving *-isomorphism `N -> W*(b_i)`.
- **2-norm facts.**
  - (i) `C[H]` is `||.||_2`-dense in `L(H)`, because `y -> y delta_e` is
    `||.||_2`-isometric into `l^2(H)`, and `sum_(g in S) (y delta_e)(g) lambda(g)`
    for finite `S` approximates it.
  - (ii) `||xy||_2 <= ||x|| ||y||_2` and `||yx||_2 <= ||y||_2 ||x||`.

**Item 1.**
Enumerate `Gamma = {g_0 = e, g_1, ...}` and `H = {h_0, h_1, ...}` (repetitions
allowed).  Put `E_n = {g_0..g_n}`, `S_n = E_n u E_n E_n` and `A_n = {h_0..h_n}`.
Let `Sigma_n` have unknowns `v_t` for `t in S_n` and constants from `A_n`:

```text
v_x v_y = v_(xy)     for x, y in E_n;
v_s != v_t           for s != t in S_n;
v_x a = a v_x        for x in E_n, a in A_n.
```

`Sigma_n` is solved in `H x Gamma`, which contains `H = H x {e}`, by
`v_t = (e, t)`.  Since `H` is existentially closed, there is
`psi_n: S_n -> H` that is injective, multiplicative on `E_n x E_n`, and whose
values on `E_n` commute with `A_n`.  Also `psi_n(e) = e`, from
`psi_n(e)^2 = psi_n(e)`.

Put `u_n(g) = lambda(psi_n(g))` for `g in E_n`, `u_n(g) = 1` otherwise, and
`u(g) = [(u_n(g))_n]`, a unitary in `L(H)^omega`.

- **(a) Homomorphism.**  For `g, g'` in `Gamma`, all large `n` have
  `g, g', gg'` in `E_n`, and then `u_n(g) u_n(g') = u_n(gg')`.  Cofinite sets
  lie in `omega`, so `u` is a homomorphism, `u(e) = 1` and
  `u(g)* = u(g^(-1))`.
- **(b) Trace.**  For `g != e` and `g in E_n`, `psi_n(g) != psi_n(e) = e`, so
  `tau(u_n(g)) = 0`.  Hence `tau_omega(u(g)) = delta_(g,e)`.
- **(c) Commutation.**  For `h = h_m` and `n >= m`, `u_n(g)` commutes with
  `lambda(h)`.  So `u(g)` commutes with `C[H]`.  For `y in L(H)` and `y_0 in C[H]`,
  fact (ii) gives
  `||[u(g), y]||_2 = ||[u(g), y - y_0]||_2 <= 2 ||y - y_0||_2`,
  and this is small by fact (i).  So `u(g) in L(H)' ∩ L(H)^omega`.
- **(d) Embedding.**  A *-monomial in the `u(g_i)` equals `u(w)` for the
  corresponding product `w in Gamma`, by (a), and has trace `delta_(w,e)` by
  (b).  That is also the trace of `lambda(w)` in `L(Gamma)`.  So the unitaries
  `u(g_i)` have the moments of the generators `lambda(g_i)`, and Lemma M embeds
  `L(Gamma)` onto `W*(u(g_i))`.  This lies in the von Neumann algebra
  `L(H)' ∩ L(H)^omega`.

**Item 2.**
- *Infinite.*  For `Gamma = Z`, `psi_n(S_n)` has `|S_n|` elements, and this is
  unbounded.
- *ICC.*  Let `h != e`, and `C` a finite set of conjugates of `h`.
  - Consider the system in one unknown `x`: `x h x^(-1) != c` for `c in C`.
  - It has a solution in `H * <t>`: `t h t^(-1)` is a reduced word of length 3,
    so by the normal form theorem for free products it is not in `H`.
  - So it has a solution in `H`, i.e. a conjugate of `h` outside `C`.
  - So conjugacy classes are infinite, and `L(H)` is a II_1 factor by the
    Murray–von Neumann ICC criterion.
- *Noncommutative central sequences.*  Apply item 1 to `Gamma = F_2`.
- *Property Gamma.*  Apply item 1 to `Gamma = Z` with generator `g`.  Put
  `u = u_n(g)` for a suitable `n`:
  - by (b), `tau(u_n(g)) = 0` for all large `n`;
  - by (c), `lim_omega ||[u_n(g), y_j]||_2 = 0` for each `j`.

  The set of `n` meeting all `k + 1` conditions lies in `omega`, so it is
  nonempty.

**Item 3.**
Let `N in GF`, embedded in `prod_V L(Gamma_i)`.  `N` is separable, so it is
generated by contractions `a_1, a_2, ...`.  Fix `k`, and let `P_k` be the
*-monomials of degree at most `k` in `k` variables.
1. Lift the images of `a_1..a_k` to contraction representatives `(b_(j,i))_i`.
   The set of `i` with `|tau(m(b_(.,i))) - tau(m(a))| < 1/(2k)` for all
   `m in P_k` lies in `V`.  Pick one such `i_k`.
2. By item 1, `L(Gamma_(i_k))` embeds in `L(H)^omega`.  Lift the images of
   `b_(1,i_k)..b_(k,i_k)` to contraction sequences in `L(H)`.  As in step 1,
   some index gives contractions `d^(k)_1..d^(k)_k` in `L(H)` with
   `|tau(m(d^(k))) - tau(m(a))| < 1/k` for all `m in P_k`.  Put
   `d^(k)_j = 1` for `j > k`.
3. In `L(H)^omega` put `x_j = [(d^(k)_j)_k]`.  Take a *-polynomial `p` in
   `x_1..x_J` of degree at most `D`.  For `k >= max(J, D)` its monomials lie
   in `P_k`, so `tau_omega(p(x)) = tau(p(a))`.  Lemma M embeds `N` onto
   `W*(x_j)`.

**Item 4.**
- (c) ⇒ (b): countable existentially closed groups exist
  (`countable-groups-embed-in-countable-existentially-closed-groups`).
- (b) ⇒ (a): `H` is countable.
- (a) ⇒ (c): let `L(U)` be locally universal, and `N` separable tracial.  `N`
  embeds in an ultrapower of `L(U)`, which is an ultraproduct of group factors,
  so `N in GF`.  By item 3, `N` embeds in `L(H)^omega`.

**Item 5.**
- By `hyperlinear-iff-group-factor-embeds-in-hyperfinite-ultrapower`, `L(H)`
  is Connes-embeddable iff the countable group `H` is hyperlinear.
- By `local-approximation-properties-are-decided-by-ec-groups`, with `M` =
  hyperlinear, that holds iff every group is hyperlinear.
