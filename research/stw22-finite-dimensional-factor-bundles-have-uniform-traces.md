Let `K` be a compact Hausdorff space of finite covering dimension, and let
`(M,E)` be a factorial tracially complete C-star algebra over `K`. If
the uniform `2`-norm Hilbert `C(K)`-module completion is countably
generated, then every bounded positive trace on every fibre gap vanishes and

```text
T(M)={a |-> integral_K tau_x(a_x) dmu(x):
      mu a Radon probability measure on K}.             (AFD-5)
```

Thus every trace on `M` is uniform-`2`-norm continuous. Matrix sizes,
diffuse II1 isomorphism classes, and matrix/diffuse fibre type may vary
arbitrarily. There is no fixed upper bound on the finite dimension and no
assumption of a common ambient factor, local triviality, hyperfiniteness,
property Gamma, or metrizability of `K`.

Countable generation is automatic for uniform tracial completions of
separable C-star algebras. The theorem makes no assertion for
infinite-covering-dimensional bases or for non-countably-generated bundles.
