Fix a faithful representation `P <= GL_d(k)`.  A packet is a pair `(S,K)`
where `K <= L` is finite and `S subset X` is finite, nonempty,
`K`-invariant, and the action `K -> Sym(S)` is faithful.  Put

```text
W_(S,K)=P^S rtimes K.                                  (1)
```

Such sets `S` always exist.  For each `h in K minus {e}`, choose
`x_h in X` moved by `h`, and take the union of the finitely many `K`-orbits
of the `x_h`; add one arbitrary point if `K` is trivial.

## Directed exhaustion

Given two packets, local finiteness makes the subgroup generated by their
two finite top groups finite.  Enlarge the union of their coordinate sets by
its orbit under this subgroup and by a finite faithful witness set for its
action.  The result is a packet containing both.  Conversely, a finite set
of elements of `W` involves finitely many top-group elements and finitely
many lamp coordinates.  The generated top subgroup is finite, and closing
the lamp support under it and adjoining a faithful witness set puts the
given elements in one packet.  Therefore

```text
W=union_(S,K) W_(S,K)                                  (2)
```

as an upward directed union.

## Linear packets with trivial amenable radical

The group in (1) embeds faithfully in `GL_(d|S|)(k)`: `P^S` acts block
diagonally and `K` permutes the blocks.  Faithfulness of the `K`-action on
`S` is exactly what prevents a nontrivial permutation part from disappearing
in this representation.

Write `N=P^S` and `G=N rtimes K`.  The amenable radical of `N` is trivial.
Indeed, every coordinate image of an amenable normal subgroup of `N` is an
amenable normal subgroup of `P`, hence is trivial.  If `R` is the amenable
radical of `G`, then `R intersect N={e}`.  For `r in R` and `n in N`,

```text
[r,n] in R intersect N,
```

so `R` centralizes `N`.  Write `r=(a,h)`.  If `h` moves `s in S`, conjugating
a nontrivial lamp supported only at `s` by `r` moves its support to `hs`;
coordinatewise conjugation by `a` cannot erase or return that support.  This
contradicts centrality.  Hence `h` fixes `S` pointwise, so faithfulness gives
`h=e`; then `r in R intersect N` and `r=e`.  Thus

```text
Rad_am(W_(S,K))={e}.                                   (3)
```

Vigdorovich's linear-group theorem makes every packet selfless.  The
inclusions in (2) induce the canonical trace-preserving inclusions of reduced
group C-star algebras, so Robert's directed-union permanence makes
`C*_r(W)` selfless.  Robert's regularity theorem then gives stable rank one
and strict comparison.

The proof differs essentially from the finitary-action argument.  There is
no finite union of global supports for `K`; instead, finite faithfulness
witnesses precisely the part of the action needed to kill the finite
amenable radical of each packet.
