let `L` be the integral left ideal of `Z[G]` generated by all left translates


cokernel has free rank `20160-20040=120`.  Exact mod-two closure gives
`rank_F2(L mod 2)=20005`, so precisely

```text
20040-20005 = 35
```

nonzero Smith factors are even.

```text

```

valuation is one, and vanishes on factors divisible by four.  Therefore all


characteristic-two atlas countermodel and is not classified here.
