title: A discrete Kazhdan group has only finitely many bounded-degree irreducible unitary types
---

Let `Gamma` be a discrete group with property `(T)`.  For every integer
`K>=1`, the set

```text
Irr_<=K(Gamma)={pi irreducible unitary: dim(pi)<=K}/equivalence
```

is finite.

This is P. S. Wang's finite-representation-type theorem: property `(T)`
isolates every finite-dimensional irreducible point of the unitary dual and,
more strongly, there are only finitely many equivalence classes in each
fixed dimension.  The bounded-degree statement follows by taking the finite
union over dimensions `1,...,K`.

The finiteness is qualitative; no effective bound on its cardinality is
asserted.  It is enough to turn positive Hilbert mass in bounded-degree
types into positive normalized adjoint commutant density by a finite
Cauchy--Schwarz estimate.
