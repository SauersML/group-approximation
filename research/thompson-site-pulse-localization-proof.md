The group `L` is finite.  The rational-tail action of `V` is transitive, has
finitely generated point stabilizer, and has two pair orbits.  The same
permutational-wreath finite-presentation theorem used in the Thompson--
Clifford tape makes `(directSum_X L_x) semidirect V` finitely presented.
Finitely many centrality relations for one local `J` identify all its
conjugates.  The quotient does not kill `J`: if `pi(J)=-I`, represent `L_x`
on `(H_pi tensor H_pi)_x`, implement `s_x` by tensor swap, take the infinite
tensor product, and let `V` permute its factors.  Every local `J` acts as the
same scalar `-I`.

Different lamp sites commute, proving `(TSP4)`.  Since `s_n` commutes with
every factor of `C_n`, it commutes with `C_n`.  Moreover

```text
B_n s_n P_n=s_n A_nP_n=-s_nP_n.
```

Together with `P_n<=C_n`, this proves `(TSP7)`.  The identities

```text
C_n=C_(n+1)+X_n
```

give orthogonality and the trace budget.

For the approximate statement, fix `n`.  Each same-site relation is a
conjugate of one local-table relator.  Each off-site commutator is obtained
from the single off-diagonal pair-orbit relation; the standard prefix-table
addresses and stabilizer normal forms used in the Thompson tape have
polynomial derivation area in `n`.  Therefore all involution, commutation,
and covariance defects among

```text
B_0,...,B_n,A_n,s_n
```

are at most `p_0(n)sqrt(E)`.

Do not exactify the abelian prefix independently of the current swap.  Instead
take the finite subgroup

```text
K_n=<B_0,...,B_(n-1)> times <A_n,B_n,s_n>,
```

where the second factor is the finite dihedral swap group generated by two
commuting involutions exchanged by `s_n`.  Every element has a normal form of
length `O(n)`.  Reordering and cancelling two normal forms uses `O(n^2)` of
the preceding site relations, so evaluating the chosen normal forms gives an
all-pairs `p_1(n)sqrt(E)`-representation of the **whole** group `K_n`.
Flexible finite-group exactification therefore produces simultaneously
commuting involutions `hat B_k`, a current source involution `hat A_n`, and a
unitary involution `hat s_n` satisfying

```text
hat s_n hat A_n hat s_n=hat B_n,
[hat s_n,hat B_k]=1                  (k<n),
```

with total correction `p_2(n)sqrt(E)`.  Common-overlap alignment with the
fixed local `H` packet may then be performed on this already exact `K_n`
table; it does not independently move the prefix or swap.

Put `hat C_n=product_(k<n)(I+hat B_k)/2` and
`hat X_n=hat C_n(I-hat B_n)/2`.  For a corrected `P_n` satisfying `(TSP6)`,

```text
||(I-hat C_n)hat s_nP_n||_2
 <=||[hat C_n,hat s_n]||_2
 <=(1/2)sum_(k<n)||[hat B_k,hat s_n]||_2,               (1)
```

while

```text
||(I-(I-hat B_n)/2)hat s_nP_n||_2
 <=(1/2)||hat B_nhat s_n-hat s_nhat A_n||_2.            (2)
```

Both right sides are polynomial times `sqrt(E)`.  Since `hat C_n` commutes
with `hat B_n`, adding `(1),(2)` and squaring proves `(TSP8)` after enlarging
the polynomial.
