**Dead.** Xin Ma, *Fiberwise amenability of ample étale groupoids*, arXiv:2110.11548v1:
- **Theorem 7.5:** a second countable minimal groupoid that admits a Følner sequence has a sofic
  topological full group.
- **Corollary D(1):** the same conclusion for fiberwise amenable minimal groupoids with a unit of
  trivial isotropy.

The Cuntz groupoid `G_2` is second countable and minimal, and `V <= [[G_2]]`. So a Følner
sequence for `G_2`, or fiberwise amenability of `G_2`, would make `V` sofic.

`cuntz-groupoid-admits-no-folner-sequence` shows `G_2` has neither. The obstruction is the
uniform boundary of Cantor orbits, which the theorem's spatial approximations cannot avoid.
