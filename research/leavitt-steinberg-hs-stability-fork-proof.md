On the stable branch, a hyperlinear model of `U` would correct to genuine
finite-dimensional representations, all of which are trivial, contradicting
trace separation.  On the unstable branch, a non-correctable asymptotic
representation remains nontrivial in a tracial matrix ultraproduct.  Its
kernel is a proper normal subgroup of quasisimple `U`, hence central; the
image is hyperlinear, while the central-cover theorem makes it nonsofic.

The cited exact-atlas criterion gives an equivalent finite-dimensional
formulation: exact representations of a fixed virtually free atlas group
make the finite normal generators of the quotient kernel tend to `1` while
one word stays a fixed normalized-HS distance from `1`.
