For one forbidden flip square, `(SPF3)` gives anticommuting packet
implementers.  Tensor them with one anticommuting Pauli pair on `C^2`; the two
commutator signs cancel, giving an exact representation of the commuting
stable letters.  For `n` independent squares, tensor these cancellation
qubits.  The multiplicity space is `C^(2^n)`, all cross-site operators commute,
and the packet cocycle matrix is the direct sum of `n` symplectic blocks.

Tensor with an arbitrary spectator `C^L`.  This changes no relation and gives
`m=2^nL`, proving `(ISR1)`--`(ISR2)`.  These models are exactly the finite
tensor windows of the infinite-site Bernoulli construction.  Since every
fixed window has zero defect in some member and arbitrary spectator
amplification, no dimension-independent inference from the listed fixed-scale
properties can force strict supercritical rank.
