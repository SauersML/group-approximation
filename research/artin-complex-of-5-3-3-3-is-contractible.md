Let `Λ = [5,3,3,3]`, the path `s_1 −5− s_2 −3− s_3 −3− s_4 −3− s_5`. Then the Artin complex `Δ_Λ`
is contractible.

This is the case Huang lists as exceptional (arXiv:2405.12068, Conjecture `conj:exceptional`),
together with `F̃_4`, `Ẽ_6`, `Ẽ_7`, `Ẽ_8` and `[3,5,3]`.

## Attempts

- **Haettel's criterion along the path order fails.** At a vertex of type `ŝ_3`, the upper set is
  the `A_2` complex on `{s_4, s_5}`, a bipartite graph of girth 6. An embedded hexagon is a
  non-flag triple. The reversed order fails symmetrically, and the relation is a partial order
  only for the two path orders. Recorded on `artin-complexes-of-5333-and-533-11-are-contractible`.
