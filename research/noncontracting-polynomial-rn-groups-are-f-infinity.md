**OPEN — proposed consequence, not a general finiteness theorem or priority claim.**

For every `m≥2,q≥2,p∤q,d≥1`, the finitely generated self-similar
group `G_d=<UT_m(Z[1/q])⋊<δ_q>,ρ_1,…,ρ_d>` in the artifact has a
noncontracting displayed tree action, and every finite-root RN group
`V_{b,r}(G_d)` has type `F_∞`.

## Attempts

The proposed proof identifies these RN groups with `B_{d,r}`, then
uses the separate polynomial finiteness tower. Its noncontraction test
uses the infinitely many sections `δ_q^kρ_1`. The recursion/full-group
identification and the tower are both unresolved claims. This does not
infer `F_∞` for the self-similar input itself, does not address all
noncontracting inputs, and does not settle the existing general
contracting RN question.
