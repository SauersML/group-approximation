For `E=F/[F,N]`, quotient permanence gives property `(T)` of `E`.  The image
`A=N/[F,N]` is central, and the third isomorphism theorem identifies `E/A`
with the finite group `F/N`; hence `A` has finite index in `E`.  Finite-index
subgroups of property-`(T)` groups have property `(T)`, so `A` has property
`(T)`.  Since `A` is abelian, its Pontryagin dual has no nontrivial sequence
of characters converging to the trivial character; equivalently, the trivial
character is isolated.  A compact dual with an isolated point is discrete
and therefore finite.  Thus `A` is finite, and the finite extension `E` is
finite.  An injective self-map of `E` cannot have proper image.
