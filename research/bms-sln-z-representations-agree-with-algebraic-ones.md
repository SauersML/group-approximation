**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `Γ` be a finite-index subgroup of `SL_3(Z)`, and let
`f : Γ → GL_m(Q)` be a homomorphism. Then there is a homomorphism of algebraic groups
`F : SL_3 → GL_m`, defined over `Q`, that coincides with `f` on a subgroup of finite index of `Γ`.
In particular, `f` sends every unipotent element of that subgroup to a unipotent matrix.

This is Bass--Milnor--Serre, Publ. Math. IHÉS 33 (1967), Theorem 16.2. The hypothesis (16.1) holds for
`SL_3` over `Q`: strong approximation holds because `SL_3(Z) → SL_3(Z/N)` is onto, and the congruence
kernel is trivial by Corollary 4.3(b) of the same paper. Verbatim text in the `-citation` route.
