Import. R. Pavlov, S. Schmieding, *On the structure of generic subshifts*, arXiv:2203.15159v1 [math.DS], 29 Mar 2022. Pages 1–4 were read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv PDF fetched by lane sk-free-2.
- The abstract: "generic subshifts in both T̄′ and T̄T̄′ are zero entropy, minimal, uniquely ergodic, and have word complexity which realizes any possible subexponential growth rate along a subsequence. In addition, a generic subshift in T̄′ is a regular Toeplitz subshift which is strongly orbit equivalent to the universal odometer."
- Theorem 1.3 (p. 4) is quoted verbatim in the claim. Its item (1) cites Theorem 5.15 of the paper, and item (3) cites Corollary 5.32.
- p. 3: "we define T′ to be the set of infinite transitive subshifts in S′. (We note that for transitive subshifts, being isolated is equivalent to being finite.)"
- p. 4: "It turns out that T′ is not closed, and hence not complete as a metric space, so we consider its closure T̄′."
- Lemma 2.3, as stated on p. 3: the subshifts contained in a nonempty SFT form a clopen subset of `S`.

Not read: §5 (the proofs of Theorem 5.15 and Corollary 5.32), and the journal version.
