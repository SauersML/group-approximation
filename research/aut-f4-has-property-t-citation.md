Citation import. Read on 2026-09-12 from PDFs downloaded from arXiv and extracted on MSI:

* M. Nitsche, *Computer proofs for Property (T), and SDP duality*,
  arXiv:2009.05134v3 (26 December 2022). Read the abstract, Section 1, Section 6
  (including Theorem 6.1), and references [9], [11] and [14].
* M. Kaluba, D. Kielak, P. W. Nowak, *On property (T) for Aut(F_n) and SL_n(Z)*,
  arXiv:1812.03456v2; Ann. of Math. (2) 193 (2021). Read the introduction (the
  cases n = 2 and 3; n = 4 open as of that paper) and Theorem 5.9.
* M. Kaluba, P. W. Nowak, N. Ozawa, *Aut(F_5) has property (T)*,
  arXiv:1712.07167v2; Math. Ann. 375 (2019). Read Theorem 1 and Corollary 2.

The Out(F_4) statement follows because property (T) passes to quotients. That is
the same step Kaluba–Nowak–Ozawa take from Theorem 1 to Corollary 2 for n = 5.

Not reproved here. The witness (Zenodo record 7065231) and the interval-arithmetic
check were not rerun. The quoted wording is collected in the linked artifact.
