**Established by citation.** Artem Dudko and Konstantin Medynets, *Finite factor representations of
Higman--Thompson groups*, Groups Geom. Dyn. 8 (2014), no. 2, 375--389, arXiv:1212.1230. Read from the
arXiv PDF, 2026-09-12.

- p. 10: "Observe that the commutator subgroup of F_(n,r) is a simple group and the abelianization of
  F_(n,r) is isomorphic to Z^n [1, Section 4]."
- **Corollary 3.3**, p. 11, verbatim: "(1) The group F'_(n,r) has no proper characters. (2) If chi is an
  indecomposable character of F_(n,r), then chi is either regular or chi(g) = rho([g]), where [g] is the
  image of g in the abelianization of F_(n,r) and rho : Z^n -> T is a group homomorphism."

Thompson's `F` is `F_(2,1)` (their Definition 3.1: piecewise linear homeomorphisms of `[0,r]` with
breakpoints in `Z[1/n]` and slopes powers of `n`). The passage from indecomposable characters to all
characters is the Choquet decomposition of the character simplex.
