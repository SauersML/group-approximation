Let

```text
0 -> I -> A -> Q -> 0                                      (QI1)
```

be a C*-extension.  Suppose every bounded 2-quasitrace on `I` is zero and
every bounded 2-quasitrace on `Q` is a trace.  Then every bounded
2-quasitrace on `A` is a trace.

Thus the property “all bounded 2-quasitraces are traces” is extension-stable
whenever the ideal is bounded-quasitrace-invisible.  No exactness, splitting,
or separability assumption is needed.
