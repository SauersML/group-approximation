The representations `pi_p = l^2_0(SL_3(F_p))` of `Gamma_D = SL_1(O_D)` are
finite-dimensional and unitary.  Strong convergence `pi_p -> lambda_(Gamma_D)`
along primes is the definition of purely matricial field (Magee--de la Salle,
arXiv:2312.03220, Definition 1.2) applied to this sequence.
