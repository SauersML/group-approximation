## Setting

For a subgroup `S` of the multiplicative group `R_{>0}`, let `PL_S(R)` be the group of
increasing homeomorphisms of `R` that are piecewise linear with a locally finite set of
breakpoints and all slopes in `S`. Breakpoints and intercepts are unrestricted. Write
`PL_2(R) = PL_{2^Z}(R)`. It contains Thompson's `F` (extended by the identity),
Belk--Hyde--Matucci's `T-bar`, Brin's `A` (homeomorphisms of `R` agreeing with `T-bar`
near both ends), and the commensurator of `<z>`, `z(x) = x + 1`.

## Statement

Let `S <= R_{>0}` be free abelian of finite rank `r`. Let `D <= (Q,+)` be **non-cyclic**,
let `M <= Q^x_{>0}` satisfy `qD = D` for all `q in M`, and let `H = D x| M`, with `M`
acting on `D` by multiplication. If `H` embeds in `PL_S(R)`, then `M` embeds in `S`.
In particular `rank M <= r`.

## Corollaries

1. **`PL_2(R)`** (`r = 1`) contains no copy of `Z[1/6] x| <2,3>`. So it contains none
   of `Aff(Q)`, the Borel group `B_2(Q)`, and `GL_n(Q)`, `SL_n(Q)`, `PGL_n(Q)`, `PSL_n(Q)`
   for any `n >= 2`. Each contains `Q x| M` with `M = Q^x_{>0}` or its subgroup of
   squares, which has infinite rank. The same holds in every subgroup of `PL_2(R)`:
   `F`, `T-bar`, Brin's `A`, and the commensurator of `<z>`. No finite generation is
   assumed and no distortion theorem is used.
2. **Finitely generated PL groups.** Let `Gamma` be a group of increasing PL
   homeomorphisms of `R` (locally finite breakpoints), generated by finitely many
   elements, each with finitely many distinct slopes. Then `Gamma` contains none of the
   groups in 1. Examples: Stein--Thompson groups, Bieri--Strebel groups
   `G(I; A, P)` with `P` finitely generated, and every finitely generated subgroup of
   `PL_2(R)`. The generators' slopes generate a finitely generated `S` by
   `fg-groups-have-fg-derivative-images`, and all slopes of `Gamma` lie in it.
3. **Sharpness.** Rank 1 is attained even when the dilation is not a slope. For every
   integer `q >= 2`, `BS(1,q) = Z[1/q] x| <q>` embeds in `PL_2(R)`, with `z` as the
   generator of `Z[1/q]`.

## Consequence for the root

This kills spark S1 of `research/artifacts/gq-gq-lit-q-embeddings.md` §7 (the
commensurator of `<z>` in `PL_2(R)`) for `Aff(Q)` and `GL_2(Q)` in every form. More
generally no PL group of the line with finitely generated slopes hosts
`gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group`, through any embedding.

- **Where the proof uses PL.** It needs the one-sided germ at a common fixed point
  to be a slope in `S`, and `S` to have no nontrivial element with roots of unboundedly
  many orders.
- **Where it breaks.** Both fail for piecewise-projective germs, whose derivatives
  at a fixed point range over the divisible group `R_{>0}`. They also fail for actions
  on higher-dimensional spaces such as `nV`. Rank-`>= 2` dilations over a divisible
  line must come from there.
