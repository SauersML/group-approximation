Assume canonical global RCC (KRT1). We first remove the trace restriction.
Let rho:A->U(Q) be an exact representation in a Connes-embeddable finite
tracial algebra, and let x in Q commute with rho(C). Since A is
hyperlinear, L(A) is Connes embeddable. Finite tensor products of
Connes-embeddable algebras are Connes embeddable: finite moment models
tensor, and their traces multiply. Thus the algebra generated by

    rho(a) tensor lambda_A(a),   a in A,   and x tensor 1

embeds trace-preservingly in a tracial matrix ultraproduct. Its actor
trace is canonical, since tau(lambda_A(a))=0 for a!=1. Applying (KRT1)
shows that x tensor 1 commutes with every rho(a) tensor lambda_A(a),
and hence x commutes with rho(A). Therefore RCC holds for all exact
actors in Connes-embeddable ambient algebras, with arbitrary actor trace.

Apply the conjugation representation of C to L^2(Q). Its invariant
subspace is L^2(rho(C)' cap Q), and its orthogonal projection on a
bounded x is the conditional expectation E_C'(x). The Kazhdan constant
gives

    ||x-E_C'(x)||_2^2
       <= kappa^(-2) sum_(s in S)||x rho(s)-rho(s)x||_2^2.

By the preceding RCC statement E_C'(x) commutes with rho(h). Thus
||x rho(h)-rho(h)x||_2<=2||x-E_C'(x)||_2. Squaring proves (KRT2) with
no error and coefficient K=4/kappa^2 for every exact CE actor.

For finite matrix assignments and contractions x, define eta_o(delta)
to be the supremum of the positive part of

    ||x rho(h)-rho(h)x||_2^2
       -K sum_(s in S)||x rho(s)-rho(s)x||_2^2

over all dimensions and assignments with defining-relator defect at
most delta. This is a nonnegative nondecreasing function bounded by 4,
and the exact inequality gives eta_o(0)=0. If eta_o(delta) did not tend
to zero, some delta_j->0 and such assignments would have a positive
excess bounded below by a fixed epsilon>0. Their tracial matrix
ultraproduct gives an exact actor rho of the finitely presented group
A and a contraction x. The algebra they generate is Connes embeddable.
The norms and finite sum in the excess pass to the ultralimit, violating
the exact inequality just proved. Hence eta_o(delta)->0, proving
(KRT1)=>(KRT2) with the prescribed K.

Given (KRT2), replace the actor assignment by

    rho'(a)=rho(a) tensor conjugate(rho(a))

and its contraction by the unitary U'=U tensor conjugate(U). Complex
conjugation is taken in a fixed matrix basis. Every evaluated word
tensors in the same way, and a relator has normalized HS defect at most
2delta(rho), by the two-factor telescoping identity. For every unitary W,

    ||W tensor conjugate(W)-I||_2^2=2-2|tr(W)|^2.

The ordinary commutator norm between U' and rho'(a) equals the norm of
their group commutator minus I; the latter group commutator is
W_a tensor conjugate(W_a). Applying (KRT2) therefore gives twice the
left side of (KRT3), twice each subgroup energy, and error at most
eta_o(2delta). Divide by two. This proves (KRT2)=>(KRT3) with (KRT5),
without invoking perfectness or an infinite-dimensional superoperator
algebra.

Finally assume (KRT3), and let pi:A->U(M) be a canonical representation
in a tracial matrix ultraproduct. Choose U in pi(C)' cap M unitary.
Unitary representatives of the actor generators and U give finite
assignments with relator defect tending to zero along the ultrafilter.
Apply (KRT3) to the doubled actor and auxiliary unitary

    pi'(a)=pi(a) direct_sum pi(a),   U'=U direct_sum I,

using their corresponding finite matrix representatives. Subgroup
energies and the defect modulus vanish in the ultralimit. If
W=U pi(h) U^* pi(h)^*, the conclusion is

    |(tau(W)+1)/2|=1.

Since |tau(W)|<=1, equality in the triangle inequality forces tau(W)=1.
Faithfulness of the trace gives ||W-I||_2^2=2-2 Re tau(W)=0, so U
commutes with pi(h). Since A=<C,h>, it commutes with pi(A). A von
Neumann algebra is linearly spanned by its unitaries; hence the entire
commutant of pi(C) lies in that of pi(A). The reverse containment is
automatic. This proves (KRT3)=>(KRT1), establishing equivalence of the
first three assertions.

To prove (KRT3)=>(KRT4), abbreviate the target energy by e, the sum of
subgroup energies by q, and the defining-relator defect by delta. Thus
0<=e<=1 and e<=Kq+eta_p(delta). Fix K'>K and put

    r=K/K',   t=(1-r)/2,   0<t<1/2.

Choose L>=1 with eta_p(1/L)<t^2. Suppose for contradiction that one
assignment has e>K'q+L delta. Necessarily e>0, q<e/K', and
delta<e/L<=1/L. If e>t, then

    e-Kq >= (1-r)e=2te>2t^2,

contradicting e-Kq<=eta_p(delta)<=eta_p(1/L)<t^2.

Otherwise 0<e<=t. Let k=ceil(t/e), so t<=ke<=2t<=1. Tensor both the
actor assignment and U diagonally k times. Every group word tensors,
and the resulting energies and relator defect satisfy

    e'=1-(1-e)^k,
    q'<=kq,
    delta'<=k delta.

The subgroup bound follows from 1-(1-z)^k<=kz for each projective
subgroup energy z. The defect bound follows by telescoping k tensor
factors for each defining relator. Moreover,

    e'>=1-exp(-ke)>=ke-(ke)^2/2>=ke(1-t).

Consequently

    e'-Kq'>=ke(1-t-r)=ke t>=t^2,
    delta'<ke/L<=2t/L<=1/L.

Applying (KRT3) to the amplified assignment contradicts
eta_p(delta')<=eta_p(1/L)<t^2. This proves (KRT4) with the choice
(KRT6). Both cases used only the assumed violation, so the conclusion
holds for every defect value, not just for sufficiently small defects.

For completeness (KRT4) also directly recovers (KRT3) at the exact K.
For each integer m>=1 choose a coefficient L_m valid at slope K+1/m.
Since q<=|S| for every assignment, all these estimates give

    e<=Kq+|S|/m+L_m delta.

Taking the infimum over m defines

    eta_p^*(delta)=inf_(m>=1)(|S|/m+L_m delta).

This is finite, nonnegative, nondecreasing, equals zero at delta=0,
and tends to zero there: first fix m large and then send delta to zero.
Thus e<=Kq+eta_p^*(delta), proving (KRT3). No argument here supplies a
linear defect modulus at coefficient exactly K.
