The commutator word changes by at most twice the actor-coordinate error,
giving `(BNC5)`.  Kazhdan projection and finite-dimensional arithmetic
co-density give `(BNC6)--(BNC7)`.  The canonical trace bound forces the
opposite lower bound `||[T,H]-I||_2>=sqrt(2(1-delta_B))`.  Solving the two
bounds for the comparison distance and taking the infimum proves `(BNC3)`.
