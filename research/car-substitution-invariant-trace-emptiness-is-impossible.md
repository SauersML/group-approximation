Suppose a proposed twisted binary substitution descends to a unital
endomorphism of a CAR/Clifford sign-sector algebra and preserves its marked
sign.  Then the proposed endpoint

```text
there is no transport-invariant sign-alive trace                     (CIT1)
```

is false.  The general Cesaro theorem supplies such a trace from any
sign-alive trace.  More sharply, the CAR algebra has a unique tracial state,
so its composition with any unital endomorphism is again a tracial state and
hence equals the original trace.

Therefore an Arf or Pfaffian charge cannot prove non-hyperlinearity by making
the invariant marked trace face empty.  A stable-letter route could survive
only through a specifically **finite-dimensional approximate** obstruction
that coexists with this exact invariant trace and is stable under tensor
amplification.  No such obstruction is supplied by invariant-state
emptiness.
