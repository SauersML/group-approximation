Let `Omega` be the `12144` conjugates of a fixed `A~=A8<M24`.  Sending a
conjugate to its unique fixed natural point defines an `A`-equivariant map

```text
f: Omega -> {1,...,24}.                              (M24D-1)
```

Every fiber has size `506`: it is precisely the unique maximal `A8` class in
the corresponding `M23` point stabilizer.  Since `A` has natural orbit sizes
`1+8+15`, the three inverse images have sizes

```text
506,       8*506=4048,       15*506=7590.            (M24D-2)
```

In the first fiber, the exact `M23` double-coset computation gives the four
`A`-subdegrees

```text
1, 15, 210, 280.                                    (M24D-3)
```

Thus the existing M23 packet theorem absorbs all `506` same-fixed-point
conjugates.  Every still-unknown M24 double cell lies over the eight- or
fifteen-point natural orbit, a total of `11638` conjugates.  No claim is made
about their further suborbit decomposition.
