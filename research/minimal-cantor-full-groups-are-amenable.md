**ESTABLISHED (import).** For every minimal homeomorphism `T` of the Cantor set, the topological full group `[[T]]` is
amenable (K. Juschenko and N. Monod, Ann. of Math. 178 (2013), Theorem A).

With Matui's results (`derived-full-group-of-cantor-minimal-system-is-simple`, `derived-full-group-fg-iff-minimal-subshift`),
`[[T]]'` is an infinite, finitely generated, simple, amenable group for every infinite minimal subshift. This is their
Corollary B.
