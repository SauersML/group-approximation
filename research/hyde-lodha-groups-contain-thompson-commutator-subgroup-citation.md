**Citation import.** Definitions 2.3 and 3.2, Lemma 2.4 and Theorem 1.3 of arXiv:1807.06478v2 were read
from the PDF (pages 3–9) on 2026-09-13.
- Definition 3.2 records `λ : H → 𝒦 ≤ G_ρ` as an isomorphism.
- Lemma 2.4 gives `F' = H' ≤ H`.

**Derivation.**
- `λ` restricted to `F'` embeds `F'` in `G_ρ`.
- For disjoint standard dyadic intervals `I_1, …, I_n ⊂ (0,1)`, the nontrivial subgroups
  `F_{I_k} ≤ F'` have pairwise disjoint supports.
- By artifact Lemma A they generate `Z^n`. So `cd F' = ∞`, and `cd` of every overgroup is infinite.
