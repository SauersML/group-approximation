`Sofic/LevelShiftObstruction` (formalized). A permutation shifting a graded
set up one level is never compatible on the top level, missing at least
`|S_m|` points; for level growth `k ≥ 2` the top level carries more than
`(k−1)/k` of the mass uniformly in the height. Fatal against exponential
amplification. Calibration: the count applies to `BS(1,2)` (`k = 2`) where
the wreath product IS sofic via Følner sets — the obstruction is in the
architecture, not the group.
