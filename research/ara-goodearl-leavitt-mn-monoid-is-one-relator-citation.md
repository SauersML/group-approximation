P. Ara and K. R. Goodearl, *Leavitt path algebras of separated graphs*, arXiv:1004.4979. Read from the arXiv TeX source
(`sepgraph.25mar11.tex`) on MSI, 2026-09-13.

Definition, introduction, verbatim: "Leavitt introduced in \cite{lea} a class of algebras $L_K(m,n)$ (in current
notation), for $1\le m\le n$, over an arbitrary field $K$, which have a universal isomorphism between the free modules
of ranks $m$ and $n$."

Proposition `keyexample`, verbatim: "Let $m\le n$ be positive integers, and define $E(m,n)$, $C(m,n)$, $A_{m,n}$ as
above. … (2) The monoids $\mathcal V(L_K(m,n))$, $\mathcal V(A_{m,n})$, $\mathcal V(vA_{m,n}v)$, and
$\mathcal V(wA_{m,n}w)$ are all of the form $\langle x\mid mx=nx\rangle$, where the generator $x$ corresponds to the
classes $[1]$, $[w]$, $[\alpha_1\alpha^*_1]$, $[w]$ in the four respective cases."

The TeX writes `\mon{...}` for the monoid of finitely generated projective modules. The paper works with left modules
("the left $R$-modules $Rf_{11}$ and $Rg_{11}$" in item (3)). Numbering is taken from the TeX labels, not a compiled
PDF.
