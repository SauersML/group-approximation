For `u=V+(1-q)`, the standard Steinberg words map under the elementary
quotient to `diag(u,u^-1)`, so one fixed word names the Hecke transport
exactly.  Separately, in a signed-permutation wreath representation, a base
tuple acts as `sum_i e_i tensor l_i`, giving one ordinary monomial element
for the same controlled sum.

Neither fact makes an arbitrary unitary representation a star-representation
of the coefficient ring.  Root tables may be realized on additive-character
labels without placing `q`, the analytic source `Q`, and the row coefficient
on one multiplicity reservoir.

The monomial model makes the obstruction explicit.  For the natural
signed-permutation type in degree at least three, the support of its central
idempotent generates the whole packet.  A base tuple preserving that
primitive carrier must have all coordinates equal, hence acts only as a
uniform multiplicity unitary and cannot implement block escape.  A
nonconstant tuple implements the desired Hecke control but has positive
source deficit in the regular packet.  Therefore exact naming does not
authenticate the capacity Gram.
