The canonical trace of `Gamma` restricts faithfully to the embedded finite
packet, hence gives `P_f` a fixed positive Plancherel mass.  Hyperlinear
microstates converge on every packet word and fixed-group exactification
preserves this mass up to `o(1)`.  Substituting `(LCN1)--(LCN2)` into the
five-fourths projection-surplus inequality forces that positive mass to tend
to zero.  This contradiction proves `(LCN4)` and the claim.
