Section 5 of the artifact proves the estimate directly. The row-one
and column-one root groups generate SL_d(F_2). In each, all nonzero
characters have the same multiplicity under the Levi action. There
are 2^(d-1)-1 nonzero characters, of which 2^(d-2) are nontrivial on
the chosen transvection. The row and column transvections are
conjugate. The codimension of the intersection of their fixed spaces
is at most the sum of their codimensions, giving the displayed bound.
