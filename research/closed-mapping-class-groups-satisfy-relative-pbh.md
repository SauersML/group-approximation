**OPEN** (posed by lane bh-invent-02, 2026-09-19).

It is implied by Boone–Higman for `Mod(S_g)` (FFWZ Theorem C,
`boone-higman-implies-relative-permutational-bh`), and hence by R1.

Route `closed-mcg-relative-pbh-via-dehn-thurston-lattice-port` gets it from one finiteness
gate. That gate uses a translation port adjoined on the lattice `ML_Z`, not one taken from
the group: by `mapping-class-ports-are-dehn-thurston-translations-not-twists`, no abelian
subgroup of `Mod(S_g)` can be a port.
