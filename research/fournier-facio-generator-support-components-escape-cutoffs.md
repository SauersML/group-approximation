**ESTABLISHED MINIMAL-OVERLAP FENCE.**  Fix a finite symmetric generating
set `S` of the Fournier--Facio group and a charged-basis near-representation
`pi`.  Suppose that for each `s in S` there is a comparison unitary `B_s`
with

```text
phi_mu((pi(s)-B_s)^*(pi(s)-B_s))=0.                  (FSC1)
```

On the basis `X`, form the undirected support graph generated by the matrix
coefficients of the finitely many `B_s` and `B_s^*`: join `x` to `y` when
one of those coefficients from `x` to `y` is nonzero.  Let `C(x)` be the
connected component of `x`, with size in `N union {infinity}`.  Then

```text
mu({x: |C(x)|<=m})=0                    for every m.  (FSC2)
```

The comparison frames may have arbitrary finite bandwidth, arbitrary
overlap pattern, and genuinely noncommuting matrix coefficients.  No block
partition is assumed in advance.

Indeed each support component is reducing for every generator `B_s`.
The charged near laws and `(FSC1)` propagate through a word in `S`, so every
group element is charge-null close to a unitary preserving the same
components.  Hence the support components are the canonical common block
system modulo the charge null ideal.  If the union of components of size at
most `m` had positive charge, restriction and normalization would produce a
uniformly bounded block witness, contradicting
`fournier-facio-bounded-block-near-witness-impossible`.

Consequently a surviving finite-overlap construction has only two options:
positive charge on genuinely infinite support components, or a purely
finitely additive escape through finite component sizes tending to infinity.
In particular locally finite generator supports do not help when their
finite-component size distribution is tight.
