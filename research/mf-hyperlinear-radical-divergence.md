For the literal group `E`, its central involution `w` belongs to the
operator-norm MF residual but not to the hyperlinear residual.  Thus

```text
Rad_MF(E) is not contained in Rad_hyp(E).
```

The same group and same word exhibit the operator-norm/tracial phase
transition; no change of candidate or presentation is involved.
