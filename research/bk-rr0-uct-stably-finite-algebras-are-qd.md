**OPEN.**  Let `E` be separable, nuclear, stably finite and of real rank zero,
with every quotient satisfying the UCT.  Then `E` is quasidiagonal.

Status check (bounded): Moutzouris' list of known cases includes real rank zero
AH algebras crossed by `Z`, but no general real rank zero statement.  Nothing
further was found in Brown--Dadarlat Section 4 or Schafhauser's introduction.

## Attempts

- **Projection-generated radicals: dead.**
  `bk-uct-counterexample-radical-not-projection-generated`: a counterexample's
  radical `R = R_qd(E)` contains no full projection.  So `R` is not generated by
  finitely many projections, and `E` has infinitely many ideals.
- **Boundary-free corner traces: dead.**
  `bk-radical-traces-vanishing-on-boundary-are-zero`: every positive
  homomorphism `K_0(R) -> R` vanishing on `H = ∂(K_1(E/R))` is zero.
- **Where it dies: non-archimedean exhaustions.**  So a counterexample needs
  `(K_0(R) ⊗ Q)/(H ⊗ Q)` with no nonzero positive functional.  AF algebras with
  lexicographically chained `K_0` show this ordered shape occurs, although AF
  radicals have the K_0-embedding property and give no counterexample.  The
  trace step only sees one archimedean class at a time.  The missing tool is a
  relative Lemma B: kill the whole boundary in `R/J` when the trace is faithful
  on `I_u/J` for a projection-generated `I_u` and infinite on `R/I_u`.
  Equivalently, a transfinite filtration of `R` by archimedean classes with one
  Brown--Dadarlat gluing step per class.  None of the read tools does this.
