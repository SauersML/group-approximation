Let `p` be prime, and let `Ax=b` be a linear system over `Z_p` with no
classical solution.  Let `Gamma=Gamma_p(A,b)` be its solution group, with
generators `x_j` and a central `J`, subject to:

- `x_j^p=J^p=1`;
- `x_j x_k=x_k x_j` whenever `x_j` and `x_k` occur in a common equation;
- `prod_j x_j^{A_ij}=J^{b_i}` for every equation `i`.

**Claim.** For every homomorphism `phi` from `Gamma` onto a finite nilpotent
group of nilpotency class `< p`, `phi(J)=1`.

Consequences:

1. **Finite-dimensional solutions.** If `pi` is a finite-dimensional operator
   solution with `pi(J)=omega I`, `omega != 1`, then the group generated by
   the operators `pi(x_j)` is not nilpotent of class `< p`.
   - Generalized Pauli (Weyl-Heisenberg) groups and their tensor powers have
     class 2, so for `p>=3` they never give a nonclassical solution.  This
     recovers and extends the Qassim-Wallman no-go for any LCS over `Z_p`.
   - For `p=2`, the claim says only that abelian images are useless, and
     Pauli (class 2) certificates exist.
2. **Pro-nilpotent search starts at class p.** A `p`-quotient search for such
   solutions must go to class at least `p`.
   `z3-heawood-incidence-system-has-a-finite-dimensional-solution` shows that
   class `p=3` is attained for the Heawood incidence system.
