**OPEN.**  Let `p` be prime and `G = Z/p`.  If `D` is a separable nuclear
`G`-C\*-algebra with `Res^G_1 D ≃_KK 0` and `D ⋊ G ≃_KK 0`, is
`D ≃_(KK^G) 0`?

**Equivalent forms (lane ex-uct-detection-phantom, 2026-09-13, unreviewed).**
* **Literature.** By `zp-detection-equals-meyer-nadareishvili-sufficiency`, this
  is the prime-order case of a question Meyer–Nadareishvili
  (arXiv:2406.11787v2) state they cannot decide. Their converse asks: does
  `Res A ∈ B` and `A ⋊ G ∈ B` force `A ∈ B^G`? It is equivalent for all
  separable algebras and for nuclear ones. So this claim is open in the
  literature. Also `UCT_G ⟺ UCT ∧ Det(nuclear)`.
* **Dynamics.** By `zp-restriction-trivial-splits-cellular-plus-phantom`, this
  claim holds iff every `(E, α)` with `Res E ≃_KK 0` has `[α_ω] = 1` in
  `KK^G(E, E)`.
  - Every such `E` splits canonically as `eE ⊕ (1 − e)E`, with
    `e = N([α_ω])/p`. The first summand is a retract of `τ(E ⋊ G)`, the second
    an equivariant phantom.
  - An equivariant phantom is exactly an object on which
    `(1 − χ)(1 − [α_ω])` is invertible.

**Why it matters.**  Let `α : G ↷ O_2` be any action.
`bootstrap-zp-actions-realize-every-cyclotomic-k-module` gives `A ∈ B^G` and
`x ∈ KK^G(A, (O_2, α))` inducing an isomorphism of Köhler invariants, with cone
`D`.  Then `Res D ≃ 0`.  If `O_2 ⋊_α G` satisfies the UCT, `x ⋊ G` is a
K-theory isomorphism between UCT algebras, hence a KK-equivalence, and
`D ⋊ G ≃ 0`.  So this claim gives:

* for actions on `O_2`, the crossed product satisfies the UCT iff the action
  lies in the equivariant bootstrap class;
* with Barlak--Li Theorem 4.16, the UCT at `p` iff every outer strongly
  approximately inner `Z/p`-action on `O_2` is in `B^G`;
* any counterexample to this claim that is not already a non-UCT crossed
  product is a UCT-invisible equivariant phantom.

## Attempts

* **Adjunction composites.**  `D` is two-sidedly orthogonal to trivial-action
  algebras, by Green--Julg and its dual.  It is also orthogonal to induced
  algebras, by induction–restriction.  So every map out of or into such an
  object vanishes.  But `id_D` need not factor through a single one: the
  unit–counit composite `D → τ(D ⋊ G) → D` of the Green--Julg adjunction is a
  norm-type element.  On `C` it is the regular representation `ρ`.  On `C(G)`
  it is `Σ_g [translation_g]`.  On the Köhler component `K_*(A ⋊ G)` it acts as
  `N(s_1)`, which is `0` on every algebra with contractible restriction
  (`zp-algebras-with-contractible-restriction-are-cyclotomic`).  Dies: the
  composite is zero exactly where it would be needed.
* **Isotropy separation.**  For commutative `C(X)`, the fixed set and its free
  complement put the algebra in the localizing subcategory generated by trivial
  and induced algebras, which would kill `D`.  A noncommutative outer action has
  no fixed-point stratum, and nothing places `(O_2, α)` in that subcategory.
  Dies at the absence of isotropy strata.
* **Duality.**  The hypotheses are self-dual under Baaj--Skandalis:
  `(D ⋊ G, dual)` satisfies them again.  No asymmetry to exploit.
* **Rokhlin special case (settled, lane ex-uct-detection-prove, 2026-09-13,
  unreviewed).**  The earlier sketch through Izumi's uniqueness theorem is
  replaced by `outer-actions-on-o2-kk-g-contractible-iff-rokhlin`:
  - Rokhlin actions on unital `O_2`-absorbing algebras absorb `id_(O_2)`
    (Hirshberg--Winter Theorem 3.3, Szabó arXiv:1509.08380 Corollary 3.8), so
    they are KK^G-contractible;
  - every `D` here is KK^G-equivalent to a pointwise outer action on unital
    `O_2` (split `O_2`-unitization, Gabe--Szabó Theorem 3.13);
  - a pointwise outer action on `O_2` is contractible iff it is Rokhlin
    (Gabe--Szabó Corollary 6.4(ii)).

  So this claim is **Rokhlin rigidity**: every outer `Z/p`-action on `O_2` whose
  crossed product is KK-contractible has the Rokhlin property.  It holds when
  the action or its dual is Rokhlin, in particular for outer strongly
  approximately inner actions.  The open core is outer actions on `O_2` with
  contractible crossed product that are neither Rokhlin nor approximately
  representable.  Even on unital `O_2` it is open whether every outer
  `Z_2`-action is strongly approximately inner (Barlak--Li II,
  Remark 4.14(2)).
* **Triangulated form (lane ex-uct-detection-prove).**  By
  `zp-detection-iff-bootstrap-sufficiency-and-kk-g-generation`, this claim says
  that `KK^G_nuc` is generated by trivial-action and induced algebras.  A
  single generation argument would have to place an outer action on `O_2`, which
  has no fixed-point strata, in that subcategory.
* **Dynamical criteria for the open core (lane ex-uct-detection-prove,
  2026-09-13, unreviewed).**  Let `α : Z/p ↷ O_2` be outer with
  `O_2 ⋊_α Z/p ≃_KK 0`.
  - *Equivalent to strong approximate innerness.*  Route
    `zp-detection-from-strong-approximate-innerness`, with the peer route
    `outer-zp-actions-on-o2-strongly-approx-inner-via-detection`, makes this
    claim equivalent to `outer-zp-actions-on-o2-are-strongly-approximately-inner`.
  - *Central sequences.*  By
    `o2-rokhlin-iff-unit-vanishes-in-fixed-central-sequences`, `α` is Rokhlin
    iff `[1] = 0` in `K_0((A_∞ ∩ O_2')^α)`.  That algebra is always properly
    infinite, and its `K_0` carries a dual action through eigen-isometries.
  - *Half-flip.*  By `o2-contractible-crossed-product-inner-half-flip-mcduff`,
    every such `α` has approximately G-inner half-flip, and `α^(⊗∞)` is the
    Rokhlin model.  So a phantom is an action with inner half-flip that does
    not embed equivariantly into its own central sequences.
  - *Rokhlin dimension.*  A phantom has infinite Rokhlin dimension with
    commuting towers (item 5 of
    `outer-actions-on-o2-kk-g-contractible-iff-rokhlin`, Szabó
    arXiv:1804.04411 Corollary B).
  - *Where every attempt dies.*  Each criterion asks for an equivariant object
    in the central sequence algebra: a fixed `O_2`, a trivial unit class, a
    McDuff embedding, or a coboundary for the cocycle `v*α_g(v)` of an
    approximately inner implementation of `α_ω`.  The hypothesis
    `O_2 ⋊_α G ≃ 0` controls K-theory of separable algebras only.  No argument
    was found that transfers it to the central sequence algebra.
* **Adversarial survey (lane ex-uct-detection-phantom, 2026-09-13).**
  - **Setting.** By `zp-restriction-trivial-splits-cellular-plus-phantom`, a
    phantom needs an object `E` with `Res E ≃ 0` and `θ_E = [α_ω] ≠ 1`.
    Realized on `O_2`, it has contractible crossed product and is neither
    Rokhlin nor approximately representable, even in the sequence sense (item 4
    of `outer-actions-on-o2-kk-g-contractible-iff-rokhlin`). So there is no
    "KL-phantom" of approximately representable type.
  - **Trivial-action coefficients cannot create one.** `θ` is monoidal, so
    `θ_(τB ⊗ X) = θ_X`. Tensoring any algebra, UCT or not, into a cell object
    keeps `θ = 1`. This applies to `1_T ⊗ τB` and to all of `B^G` (item 5
    there).
  - **Self-tensoring kills phantoms at `p = 2` (proved).** Suppose
    `ψ : E ≅ E ⊗ E` is an equivariant isomorphism, `Res E ≃ 0` and
    `E ⋊ Z/2 ≃ 0`. Naturality gives `ψ θ_E ψ^(−1) = θ_E ⊗ θ_E = (−1)(−1) = 1`.
    A phantom has `θ = −1`, so `2 = 0` on `E`, and `2` is invertible, so
    `E ≃ 0`. No phantom at `p = 2` is equivariantly self-absorbing under `⊗`.
  - **Unit maps vanish.** If `D` is a unital phantom and `F` any unital
    `G`-algebra, then `f ↦ f ⊗ 1_D` is `0` in `KK^G(F, F ⊗ D)`. Indeed
    `(1 ⊗ θ_D) ∘ ι = ι`, and `1 − 1 ⊗ θ_D` is invertible.
  - **Norms (functoriality now proved; lane ex2-uct-norm-functor,
    2026-09-13, unreviewed).** `N(A) = A^(⊗p)` with the cyclic shift is a
    non-additive functor `KK → KK^G` (`kk-tensor-power-norm-functor-to-kk-zp`).
    The proof averages Kasparov partitions of unity over the shift, not the
    operators. A KK-null-homotopy of `id_A` norms to an equivariant one, so
    `A ≃_KK 0` gives `N(A) ≃_(KK^G) 0`. The cylinder filtration then carries
    KK-equivalences to `KK^G`-equivalences, and localization gives the functor.
    - `N(B) ⊆ B^G`.
    - `N(O_2) ≃ 0`, so the shift on `O_2^(⊗p)`, in particular the flip on
      `O_2 ⊗ O_2`, is Rokhlin (`o2-tensor-power-shift-is-rokhlin`).
    - Norms of KK-contractible algebras are never phantoms. The only phantom
      candidate from norms is the Tate part `1_T ⊗ N(A)` of a non-bootstrap
      `A` (`tate-norm-shift-is-kkg-trivial`, open).

    The direct Cuntz-trick proof of `N(O_2) ≃ 0` still fails as recorded: the
    orthogonal sum `Σ_w Ad(S_w)` is an endomorphism of `N(O_2)`, not the
    identity.
  - **Non-nuclear direction (reduced, open).** Take `C*_r(Γ^p)` with the
    permutation action for a torsion-free Kazhdan hyperbolic `Γ`. The
    equivariant diagonal has a source with `θ = 1`, so the phantom part of
    `1_T ⊗ N(C*_r Γ)` is that of the diagonal's cone. Its crossed product is
    the subgroup inclusion `ΔΓ × G ≤ Γ ≀ G`. Baum–Connes with coefficients for
    `Γ ≀ G` makes that crossed product K-invisible (sketch). What remains is a
    KK-question on the `(1 − γ)`-parts
    (`kazhdan-group-algebra-tate-diagonal-is-kkg-equivalence`, open; artifact
    `research/artifacts/uct-kk-norm-functor-2026-09-13-part3.md`).
* **Dual-action transfer (lane ex2-uct-dual-action-transfer, 2026-09-13,
  unreviewed).**
  - *Two commuting actions.*  By
    `o2-central-sequence-k-theory-bi-cyclotomic-transfer`, for outer `α` on
    `O_2` the group `M_* = K_*((A_∞ ∩ A')^α)` carries two commuting actions:
    - `φ` of `Ĝ`, through eigen-isometries;
    - `ψ` of `G`, through invariant isometries implementing `α_g`.  These are
      the dual action's eigen-isometries.
  - *Transfer.*  Corner transfer through the `O_2`-absorbing algebras
    `A_∞ ∩ A'` and `B_∞ ∩ B'` gives `N_φ = 0` always, and `N_ψ = 0` when
    `O_2 ⋊ G ≃ 0`.
  - *Baaj–Skandalis.*  This sidesteps the self-duality in the "Duality" bullet
    above.  Both hypotheses become commuting norm identities on one self-dual
    group; duality swaps the identities and fixes `[1]`.
  - *Unit class.*  `φ_λ ψ_h [1] = [1]` iff `α_h` is inner, sequentially, by
    `λ`-eigen unitaries.  Transfer alone gives: strong approximate innerness
    implies `p[1] = 0`.
  - *p = 2* (`z2-o2-actions-anti-inner-and-o3-rokhlin-criterion`).  Here
    `φ = ψ = −1`.  So every such action is approximately inner by
    anti-invariant unitaries.  Detection at `2` says `2[1] = 0`, equivalently a
    unital `O_3` in the fixed central sequences.
  - *Dies at non-formal input.*
    - The free module over `Λ = Z[ζ_p] ⊗ Z[ζ_p]` satisfies every transfer
      relation, with `1 ≠ 0`.
    - The algebras with known vanishing K-theory here are `A_∞ ∩ A'`,
      `B_∞ ∩ B'` and `B_∞ ∩ A'`.  They yield only the two isotropic relations.
    - Open inputs: removing the sign at `p = 2`, and the `p − 1` anisotropic
      components of `M_*[1/p]` for odd `p`.
* **Cohomology through Gabe–Szabó Theorem 5.14 (lane
  ex2-uct-unit-class-cohomology, 2026-09-13, unreviewed).**
  - *Tool.*  By `gabe-szabo-asymptotic-coboundary-kk-criterion`, a cocycle of
    an outer finite group action on a unital Kirchberg algebra is a coboundary
    iff its unit class `KK^G(ι_B, w)` is trivial.
  - *Every non-central obstruction vanishes.*  By
    `zp-o2-contractible-crossed-product-only-coboundaries`, an outer `α` on
    `O_2` with contractible crossed product has:
    - only coboundaries;
    - exact eigen-unitaries and exact Rokhlin towers in `O_2`;
    - a unital equivariant copy of every separable exact unital `G`-algebra,
      including the Rokhlin model and `(O_2, α)` itself (Theorem 5.6).
  - *The cocycle is a character.*  By
    `zp-o2-generator-kk-class-is-twisted-asymptotic-innerness`, `E` splits
    into Galois components with `θ = χ^k`, `k ∈ F_p^×`.
    - Each component is an outer action whose generator is asymptotically
      inner along unitary paths `u_t` with `α_g(u_t) ≈ χ^k(g) u_t`.
    - The implementing cocycle `v^* α_g(v)` in `F_∞(O_2)` is the scalar
      `χ^k`.
    - Detection is "`χ^k`-twisted asymptotic innerness implies untwisted", at
      `p = 2` "odd implies even".  This matches the anti-inner sequences of the
      `p = 2` transfer node.
  - *Meeting the transfer.*  The path gives a unitary `w` with
    `α_g(w) = χ̄^k(g) w` and `w^* a w = α_ω(a)`.  So item 3 of
    `o2-central-sequence-k-theory-bi-cyclotomic-transfer` gives
    `(1 − x^(−k) y)[1] = 0` on component `k`.
    - `Λ·[1]` is a quotient of `Λ/(y − x^k) ≅ Z[ζ_p]`, on which `x = ζ` and
      `y = ζ^k`.
    - The component is Rokhlin iff that quotient is `0`.
  - *Submodel reduction.*  Theorem 5.14 on a separable invariant unital
    Kirchberg submodel `S ⊆ F_∞(O_2)` with outer restricted action decides
    the Rokhlin property exactly when `(1 − ψ)·KK^G(ι_S) = 0` for some `ψ ≠ 1`
    (item 6 of the coboundary node).  So the route needs one
    contractible-crossed-product subsystem inside `F_∞(O_2)`.
  - *Dies at the step from non-central to central.*
    - Any subsystem satisfying the condition gives a central eigen-unitary,
      hence the Rokhlin property.  The condition is equivalent to the
      conclusion.
    - Theorems 5.6, 5.8 and 5.14 construct and compare maps into separable
      Kirchberg algebras, so they place contractible subsystems in `O_2`.
    - Heuristically, a central copy of `S` is a unital equivariant `A ⊗ S → A`
      that is asymptotically the identity on `A`.  Its KK^G-shadow
      `x ∘ (1 ⊗ ι_S) = 1_E` with `(1 − ψ) ι_S = 0` would force
      `(1 − ψ)·1_E = 0`.  So the step carries the whole content of detection.
    - The subsystems known to embed centrally in every outer action are
      generated by eigen-isometries.  Making one of them unitary is the unit
      class problem again.

A positive answer is a noncommutative isotropy-separation principle.  A negative
answer needs an equivariant phantom, which by the fence node no K-theoretic
invariant can exhibit.
