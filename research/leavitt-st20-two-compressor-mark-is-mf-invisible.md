**OPEN.**  Let `D` be the binary Cantor diagonal and let `Lambda_Delta` be
the subgroup of `St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))` generated by the rank-five diagonal
root packet.  Fix the two explicit Whitehead words whose elementary images
are the complementary compressors, and put

```text
B_Delta=<Lambda_Delta,tau_0,tau_1>,       z=x_13(s_1t_1).
```

Prove that every homomorphism from `B_Delta` to a norm matrix corona kills
`z`; equivalently `z in Rad_MF(B_Delta)`.

The claim concerns the literal chosen Steinberg words.  Current compressor
covariance is verified after projection to the elementary group, not
upstairs modulo the Steinberg kernel, so no unproved lift of that covariance
is included in the statement.

## Attempts

Downstairs, every exact finite-dimensional representation kills the mark,
but `leavitt-two-compressor-covariance-triangle-has-marked-models` shows that
the two covariance laws, commuting children, and the obvious order-three
relative compressor all admit marked finite-window permutation models.  The
next attack is to find a further literal kernel relation of the concrete
two-compressor subgroup and then verify its chosen Steinberg lift.
