**REFUTED.**  The proposed statement was the following.  Let `D` be the
binary Cantor diagonal and let `Lambda_Delta` be the subgroup of
`St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))` generated by the rank-five diagonal
root packet.  Fix the two explicit Whitehead words whose elementary images
are the complementary compressors, and put

```text
B_Delta=<Lambda_Delta,tau_0,tau_1>,       z=x_13(s_1t_1).
```

Every homomorphism from `B_Delta` to a norm matrix corona was claimed to kill
`z`; equivalently `z in Rad_MF(B_Delta)`.

The claim is false.  The elementary projection maps `B_Delta` onto the exact
two-compressor core

```text
C_lc({0,1}^N,GL_5(F_2)) semidirect (C_2*C_3),
```

which is LEF and has trivial MF radical by
`leavitt-two-compressor-core-is-lef-and-marked-operator-mf`.  Pulling that
corona embedding back along the projection retains `z`; see
`leavitt-st20-two-compressor-mark-is-mf-visible`.  No covariance identity
for chosen Steinberg lifts is needed.

## Earlier attempts

Downstairs, every exact finite-dimensional representation kills the mark,
but `leavitt-two-compressor-covariance-triangle-has-marked-models` shows that
the two covariance laws, commuting children, and the obvious order-three
relative compressor all admit marked finite-window permutation models.  The
further literal kernel relations do exist, including the modular relations
isolated in `leavitt-two-compressor-core-has-modular-normal-form`, but finite
graph-of-finite-groups windows realize all of them while retaining the mark.
Thus the two-compressor-only endpoint cannot prove the full
Property-`(T)`-free radical theorem.
