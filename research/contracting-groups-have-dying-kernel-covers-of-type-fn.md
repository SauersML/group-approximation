**OPEN.** Let `G ≤ Aut(T_d)` be finitely generated, contracting and self-similar. For every `n`,
there is a group `B` of type `F_n` with a wreath recursion `ψ : B → B ≀ S_d` such that:
- the faithful quotient of `B` is `G`, with the given recursion;
- the kernel is `∪_j K_j`, i.e. `B` has **dying kernel**.

**Why it matters.** By `wreath-recursion-thompson-groups-inherit-fn` it implies
`contracting-rover-nekrashevych-groups-are-f-infinity`. One cover per `n` suffices, since each
`V_{d,r}(G)` is `V_{d,r}(B_n, ψ_n)` for every such cover.

## What is known

- **`n = 2` always.** Nekrashevych's cover `⟨N | all relations of length <= 3 among nucleus
  elements⟩` is finitely presented and has dying kernel (arXiv:1312.5654, Props.
  `pr:lengththree` and `pr:kernelcontracting`). This re-proves finite presentation of `V_d(G)`.
- **`G` of type `F_n`.** Take `B = G`, the Skipper–Zaremsky case.
- **Iterated monodromy groups of expanding coverings of compact aspherical spaces.** Take
  `B = π_1(M)`, as in `hyperbolic-pcf-rational-nekrashevych-groups-are-f-infinity`.
- **Grigorchuk group.** `B = C_2 * (C_2 × C_2)`, which is virtually free.

## An obstruction to torsion-free covers (lane lemma, elementary)

**Claim.** Suppose some `g ∈ G` of finite order `k` fixes a ray `ξ ∈ X^ω` and has `g|_{ξ_{≤j}} ≠
1` for all `j`. Then no **torsion-free** group is a dying-kernel cover of `G`.

**Proof.**
- Let `b` lift `g`. Every prefix `v` of `ξ` is fixed by `b`, so `(b^k)|_v = (b|_v)^k`.
- `b|_v` maps to `g|_v ≠ 1`, so `b|_v ≠ 1`. In a torsion-free `B` this gives `(b|_v)^k ≠ 1`.
- So `b^k` lies in `K`, since it acts as `g^k = 1`, but it lies in no `K_j`. `∎`

**Example.** In the Grigorchuk group, `b` fixes `1^ω` with sections `c, d, b, c, ...` along it. So
free covers fail, and torsion is forced. Correspondingly Nekrashevych's cover is `C_2 * (C_2 × C_2)`.

**Lesson.** Covers must contain every finite germ that recurs along a ray. So the natural
candidates are groups generated by finite subgroups: free products and graphs of finite groups, or
complexes of groups.

## Candidate route (not proved)

Nekrashevych's cover `U(N)` is the universal group of the partial multiplication on the nucleus.
- If that partial multiplication is a Stallings pregroup, then `U(N)` is virtually free, hence
  `F_∞` (Rimlinger; recalled, not re-read).
- More generally, if the presentation 2-complex of `U(N)` (triangles for the length-3 relations)
  is aspherical, or carries a CAT(0) complex-of-groups structure, then `U(N)` has type `F_∞`. This
  looks like the "non-positively curved universal contracting cover" condition in Bartholdi
  arXiv:1107.5339 (abstract only; not re-read).
- A test to run first: the nucleus of the basilica group. Its length-3 relations appear to be
  definitional, which would make `U(N)` free of rank 2, consistent with `π_1` of the twice
  punctured plane. This is not checked here.
