Citation import, secondary.

**Sources read on 2026-09-16** (PDF text extracted with pypdf).

- A. Engel and C. Wulff, *Coronas for properly combable spaces*, arXiv:1711.06836v3
  (13 October 2021), §3.6 "Automatic groups", p. 28, verbatim: "If G is finitely generated and automatic,
  then by [ECH+92, Theorem 2.5.1] we can (and will) assume that the automatic structure
  of it has the uniqueness property." Here [ECH+92] is *Word Processing in Groups*.
- S. Rees, *The development of the theory of automatic groups*, arXiv:2205.14911v1
  (30 May 2022), p. 9, verbatim: "Given an automatic (or biautomatic) structure (L, k)
  for a group G, it is straightforward (using well known properties of regular
  languages, such as the “Pumping lemma” [46]) to modify the structure and achieve a new
  automatic structure with particular properties. For instance we can achieve a
  structure in which every element of G has a unique representative (a structure with
  uniqueness)".

**Not read.** The primary source, ECHLPT, *Word Processing in Groups* (Jones and
Bartlett, 1992), Theorem 2.5.1. Rees defines automatic structures through the fellow
traveller property. The artifact uses the equivalent multiplier-language formulation of
Romankov, arXiv:1511.00223v1, §6. The equivalence of the two formulations is standard
(ECHLPT Chapter 2); this lane did not re-read it.
