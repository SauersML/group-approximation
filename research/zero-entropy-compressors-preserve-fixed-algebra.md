## Setup

Let a countable group `G` act by measure-preserving Borel automorphisms on a standard probability space `(Z, kappa)`. There is no assumption of freeness, ergodicity, soficity or Connes embeddability. For `H <= G` write

```text
F_H = { E Borel : kappa(hE triangle E) = 0 for all h in H }
```

for the complete sigma-algebra of `H`-invariant sets. For `s in G`, `h(s, P)` is the Kolmogorov--Sinai entropy of `s` relative to a finite partition `P`, and `h_kappa(s) = sup_P h(s, P)`.

## Statements

**(ZE1) Monotone entropy lemma.** Let `T` be an invertible p.m.p. automorphism and `F` a complete sub-sigma-algebra with `T^-1 F <= F`. Suppose `E in F` is not `T^-1 F`-measurable mod null. Then

```text
h(T, {E, E^c}) >= integral phi( E(1_E | T^-1 F) ) d kappa  >  0,
phi(x) = -x log x - (1-x) log(1-x).
```

**(ZE2) Compressors move fixed algebras inward.** If `s Gamma s^-1 <= Gamma`, then `s F_Gamma = F_(s Gamma s^-1)`, which contains `F_Gamma`. Equivalently, `s^-1 F_Gamma <= F_Gamma`.

**(ZE3) Theorem.** Let `Gamma <= G` and `S <= G` with `s Gamma s^-1 <= Gamma` for every `s in S`. Suppose that for every `s in S` and every `E in F_Gamma`, `h(s, {E, E^c}) = 0`. In other words `F_Gamma` lies inside the Pinsker algebra of `s`, which holds in particular when `h_kappa(s) = 0`. Then:

- `s F_Gamma = F_Gamma` for all `s in S`;
- `F_Gamma` is invariant under `H = <Gamma, S>`;
- `F_Gamma = F_(<<Gamma>>_H)`, where `<<Gamma>>_H` is the normal closure of `Gamma` in `H`.

Quantitatively, for every `s in S` and every `E in F_Gamma`, `h_kappa(s)` is at least `integral phi(E(1_E | F_(s^-1 Gamma s)))`.

**(ZE4) Kun--Thom Theorem E pair.** Let `R = F_q[x_1^(±1),...,x_d^(±1)]`, `R_+ = F_q[x_1,...,x_d]`, `r, d >= 3`, `Gamma = EL_r(R_+)`, `G = EL_r(R) x| SL_d(Z)` and `N = EL_r(R)`. Let `s_0 in SL_d(Z)` be the element whose monomial substitution acts on exponent vectors by the elementary matrix `e_12(1)`. Take any p.m.p. action of `G` with `h_kappa(s_0) = 0`. Then

```text
F_Gamma = F_N    is G-invariant.
```

More generally, the same conclusion holds when each of the `d(d-1)` positive elementary substitutions `s_ij` has `F_Gamma` inside its Pinsker algebra.

**(ZE5) Coset-lamp consequence.** Let `W = (directSum_(G/Gamma) F_2) x| G` be the Kun--Thom wreath, and `m_a = delta_Gamma + delta_(a Gamma)` with `a in N - Gamma`. These seeds lie in `Rad_sof(W) = K_even`. Suppose `m_a notin Rad_hyp(W)`. Then there is a shift-invariant probability measure `mu` on `F_2^(G/Gamma)`, with Connes-embeddable crossed product, in which some positive elementary substitution `s_ij` has positive entropy on some `Gamma`-invariant set. In particular `h_mu(s_0) > 0`.

## What this kills and what it leaves

**Class kill.** Every Connes-embeddable certificate of `m_a notin Rad_hyp(W)` fails at the step "a moves a Gamma-invariant set" when it is built from a zero-entropy action. The same holds for every other pair generated by compressors. Zero-entropy actions include compact and profinite actions, distal actions, rigid actions, zero-entropy Gaussian actions, and actions through unipotent substitutions with zero-entropy homogeneous dynamics. Such an approach might show that the Kun--Thom seed survives in some tracial ultraproduct, or that `W` is hyperlinear (`hyperlinear-wreath-model`). The invariant that kills it is the one-sided sigma-algebra `s^-1 F_Gamma <= F_Gamma`. It forces `h(s) >= H(P | s^-1 F_Gamma)`, and that bound is strictly positive exactly when the fixed algebra moves.

**Decomposition toward nonhyperlinearity.** By `coset-lamp-seed-radical-is-ce-fixed-algebra-rigidity`, `W` is nonhyperlinear as soon as `m_a in Rad_hyp(W)` for one `a in N - Gamma`. After (ZE4), that requirement reduces to one statement that can fail on its own:

> **(PE)** No p.m.p. action of `G` with Connes-embeddable crossed product has a `Gamma`-invariant set on which `s_0` (or another `s_ij`) has positive entropy and which `N` moves.

The positive-entropy information sits in the `s`-invariant factor `vee_n F_(s^n Gamma s^-n)`, generated by the increasing fixed algebras of the shrinking subgroups `s^n Gamma s^-n`. On that factor `F_Gamma` is a one-sided generator: `s^-1 F_Gamma <= F_Gamma` and `vee_n s^n F_Gamma` is the whole factor. A proof of (PE), or of the Hilbert--Schmidt Theorem C (`kt-centralizer-normalization-hs`), therefore has to use information that grows strictly along the compressor. Every argument that reads only zero-entropy invariants of the action (spectral discreteness, rigidity sequences, distality) is already exhausted by (ZE3).
