A one-relator group `<A | r>` with `A` finite is finitely generated. It has
solvable word problem by item 2 of
`magnus-moldavanskii-hierarchy-with-decidable-edges` (take `A_0 = ∅`). So
`boone-higman-conjecture` applies to it and gives an embedding in a finitely
presented simple group. `∎`
