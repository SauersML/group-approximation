The harmonic device, a function with values `1/m` on clopen pieces, is
the one of `harmonic-cuntz-ring-is-purely-infinite-simple` and
`leavitt-resolvent-ring-is-fp-simple-of-char-zero`. What is new is the host: a tracial crossed
product instead of a purely infinite one.

## Setting

- `(X, σ)` is an infinite minimal subshift over a finite alphabet `𝒜`. It has no periodic points.
- `A = C(X) ⋊_σ Z` is the crossed product C*-algebra. Its unitary `u` implements `σ`:
  `u g u^* = g ∘ σ^(-1)` for `g ∈ C(X)`.
- Fix `x_∞ ∈ X` and pairwise disjoint nonempty clopen sets `C_1, C_2, ...` that accumulate only at
  `x_∞`: every neighbourhood of `x_∞` contains all but finitely many `C_m`, and `x_∞ ∉ ∪ C_m`.
  For example, take `C_m = U_(k_m) ∖ U_(k_(m+1))`, where `U_k` are the central cylinders around
  `x_∞` and `k_m` is a subsequence along which these differences are nonempty.
- The **harmonic function** is `f = Σ_m (1/m) χ_(C_m)`, with `f = 0` off `∪ C_m`.
  - It is continuous: on the neighbourhood of `x_∞` that omits `C_1, ..., C_(m-1)`, `f ≤ 1/m`.
  - It is locally constant off `x_∞`.
- `R_h` is the subring of `A` generated by `u`, `u^(-1)`, the letter cylinders
  `χ_[a] = χ{x : x_0 = a}` (`a ∈ 𝒜`), and `f`.

## Statement

1. **Finite generation.** `R_h` is generated by `|𝒜| + 3` elements, and it contains `Q`.
2. **Structure.** `R_h = ⊕_(j ∈ Z) B u^j` is the skew Laurent ring `B[u^(±1); σ]`.
   - `B ⊆ C(X, Q)` is the commutative ring generated by `LC(X, Z)` and the translates `f ∘ σ^k`.
   - `B` contains `Q` and `LC(X, Q)`.
3. **Simplicity.** `R_h` is simple, and its centre is `Q`.
4. **Rational scalars.** For every `N >= 3`,
   `Λ_N(R_h) = {λ ∈ Q^x : λ I_N ∈ E_N(R_h)} ⊆ {±1}`. Precisely, the Fuglede–Kadison
   determinant gives a homomorphism `GL_N(R_h) -> R_(>0)`. It is trivial on `E_N(R_h)` and sends
   `λ I_N` to `|λ|`.
5. **Host data.** These follow from `q-algebra-elementary-quotients-contain-gl-n-q`:
   - `Z(E_N(R_h)) ⊆ {±I}` (its part 2);
   - `PE_N(R_h)` is a finitely generated group;
   - `GL_n(Q)` embeds in `PE_N(R_h)` for `N >= n+2` (its part 4).

## Consequence for the ring route

- **The centre clause can be met.** Condition 3 of
  `q-algebra-with-fp-simple-projective-elementary-group` (parts 2–3 of
  `rational-scalars-obstruct-fp-elementary-hosts`) holds for a finitely generated simple ring. So
  the scalar obstruction does not exclude finitely generated simple ring hosts as such. It
  excludes the purely infinite ones.
- **This ring still fails at K_2.** `laurent-rational-symbols-block-fp-elementary-groups` shows
  that the same detected scalars produce the Steinberg symbols `{λ, u}` in `K_2(R_h)`. So neither
  `E_N(R_h)` nor `PE_N(R_h)` is finitely presented.
- **What a host needs next.** The rational-scalar tension in `K_1` and `K_2`, and the refined
  target, are in `research/artifacts/gq-deep-adelic-1-k-budget.md`.
- **Not claimed:**
  - finite presentation of `R_h` as a ring;
  - simplicity of `PE_N(R_h)`, which needs a normal-subgroup theorem for `E_N` over `R_h`.
