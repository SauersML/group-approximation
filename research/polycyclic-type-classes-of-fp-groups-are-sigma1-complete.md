**ESTABLISHED** by `polycyclic-type-recognition-sigma1-complete-proof`.

For finite presentation codes each of the following sets is `Sigma^0_1`-complete:

```text
ABELIAN_fp,  NILPOTENT_(<=c)_fp  (each c >= 1),  NILPOTENT_fp,
POLYCYCLIC_fp,  FINITE_fp,  TRIVIAL_fp.                               (PT1)
```

**Upper bounds.**
- *Bounded nilpotency.* A group generated by `X` is nilpotent of class at most
  `c` iff all left-normed commutators of weight `c + 1` in `X` vanish. That is a
  finite packet of word equalities, and `c = 1` is the abelian case.
- *Unbounded nilpotency.* `NILPOTENT_fp` is the union over `c` of these uniformly
  c.e. sets.
- *Polycyclicity.* It is recognized by the Tietze search for an honest polycyclic
  presentation, per Baumslag--Cannonito--Miller.
- *Finiteness.* Coset enumeration halts on finite groups.

**Lower bounds.** All six properties hold for the trivial group and fail for every
overgroup of `F_2`. So the Adian--Rabin family used in
`amenability-of-finite-presentations-arithmetical-position`, which is trivial on
`w = 1` and contains `F_2` on `w != 1`, reduces a `Sigma^0_1`-complete word
problem to each of them.

**Contrast.** On recursive presentations bounded nilpotency is already
`Pi^0_2`-complete. A finite presentation certifies these laws with finitely many
derivations. This collapse is the pattern that
`metabelianity-of-finite-presentations-is-recursively-enumerable` conjectures for
the metabelian law. Through the certificate screen of
`amenability-of-finite-presentations-is-pi1-hard`, none of these classes can be
the positive branch of a switch whose locus is not `Sigma^0_1`.

No novelty is claimed. These are classical facts assembled as ledger cells.
