title: For second countable Hausdorff etale groupoids, Cc(G) injects into the full and reduced groupoid C*-algebras
---

Let `G` be a second countable, locally compact, Hausdorff étale groupoid. The canonical homomorphisms
`C_c(G) -> C*(G)` and `C_c(G) -> C*_r(G)` are injective.
