With the notation and common Koopman operator of Sections 1-3, the finite
cluster spectral measures satisfy ||mu_q-mu_c||_TV<=K(q), where
K(q)=(1+sqrt(1-theta(q)))sqrt(2delta(q)-theta(q)) tends to zero.
Consequently sup_n rho^(-n)|f_n(q)-a_n(c)|<=K(q). This concerns finite
connectivity f_n, not full connectivity a_n above criticality.
