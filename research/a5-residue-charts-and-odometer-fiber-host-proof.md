Write h=59 and identify the integer n=2*q+i with root i in
{0,1} and q in the h-adic Cantor set. The arithmetic construction
gives each lambda_g on source residues n=d+2*h*k by

    lambda_g(n)=a_d*k+b_d,
    a_d in {2*h^2,2*h,2},

apart from finitely many exceptions. Write d=i+2*u, with 0<=u<h.
If a_d=2*h^l, l in {0,1,2}, choose uniquely j in {0,1},
0<=v<h^l, and c in Z such that

    b_d=j+2*v+2*h^l*c.

The branch becomes

    i+2*u+2*h*k -> j+2*v+2*h^l*(k+c).

This is exactly a forest-prefix branch with source root i and
one digit u, target root j and l-digit prefix v, and suffix
odometer label a^c. The image progressions partition a cofinite
subset of N by bijectivity of lambda_g, so their h-adic cylinders
are disjoint and cover the two-root Cantor set. Thus these branches
form an element of B, with the same near action. The same applies
to inverses. No finite exceptional values need to be assigned to
the h-adic action: it realizes the near class, not the original
permutation at those exceptions.

The shift s is the B element

    (0,q)->(1,q),       (1,q)->(0,q+1).

The faithful near-action model for B now identifies R_nu with the
subgroup of B generated by these elements. Because chi is exactly
near index, this embedding intertwines eta and chi, and chi(s)=1.

## A finitely presented ambient group for all germs

B is finitely presented and its character kernel is finitely
generated by the required index claim. Also chi splits by s, so
B=ker(chi) semidirect <s>. The finitely generated kernel case of
[Martinez-Perez, Proposition 3.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1302.2745v2)
therefore makes the untwisted fiber product F_B=B x_Z B finitely
presented. This application requires finite generation, not finite
presentation, of ker(chi).

The index-preserving embedding R_nu->B induces an embedding
F_nu=R_nu x_Z R_nu -> F_B. To include the odd germ coset, work
in (B times B) semidirect <omega>, with omega exchanging factors,
and put tau_B=(s,1)*omega. Then

    tau_B^2=(s,s),
    tau_B*(b,c)*tau_B^-1=(s*c*s^-1,b).

This conjugation preserves equality of the two characters, and
tau_B^2 belongs to F_B. Thus D=<F_B,tau_B> is the union of F_B
and its distinct coset F_B*tau_B, distinguished by the permutation
coordinate in the wreath product. A finite-index overgroup of a
finitely presented group is finitely presented, so D is.

The formulas match the established index-two description of Q.
They define its embedding: it is injective on Q_0=F_nu, and an
odd-coset element cannot map to the identity because its omega
coordinate is nontrivial. This completes the claimed ambient model.

Nothing here proves that R_nu or Q equals or has finite index in
its ambient group. In particular their finite presentation cannot
be inferred from this construction. E_nu is a group of different
Cantor homeomorphisms with a nontrivial neighborhood kernel in its
stabilizer; replacing it by the germ quotient would discard that
kernel and is not an embedding of E_nu.
