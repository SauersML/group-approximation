Let `phi:A->B` be a unital homomorphism.  Suppose that for some `n>=2`:

1. there is a unital homomorphism
   `M_n -> A_infinity intersect A'`;
2. there is a unital C-star subalgebra
   `C subset B intersect phi(A)'` for which the unit of `M_n(C)` is properly
   infinite.

Then `phi` is O-infinity-stable.  Thus a central matrix block in the domain
folds a properly infinite finite amplification in the ordinary relative
commutant back to the unamplified sequence relative commutant.
