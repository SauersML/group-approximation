**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `Y` be the corrected shared BCS and role-packet tuple,
let `B_f` be the projection of `role-packed-bcs-gap-is-one-idempotent`, and
let `Q` be a finite-dimensional projection reducing `Y`.  At zero structural
defect assume

```text
tau(R B_f)>=(beta/m)tau(R)                              (AFS1)
```

for every projection `R<=Q` reducing `Y`.  Then the smallest `Y`-reducing
projection containing `QB_f` is exactly `Q`.

Equivalently, `QB_f` has full central/reducing support in the finite
dimensional star-algebra generated by `Y` on `QH`.  This does not say that
`QB_f=Q`; it says that no nonzero reducing summand of `Q` can avoid the
forbidden aggregate.

There is a quantitative approximate version.  Suppose `(AFS1)` is replaced
by

```text
tau(R B_f)>=(beta/m)tau(R)-C_f sqrt(E)                  (AFS2)
```

for every corrected reducing `R<=Q`.  Let `T<=Q` be reducing and let `D` be
a projection or positive contraction satisfying

```text
T D=0,                 ||D-QB_f||_2<=delta.             (AFS3)
```

Then

```text
tau(T)<=(m/beta)(delta^2+C_f sqrt(E)).                  (AFS4)
```

In particular, if `Q_D` is the reducing hull of `D` inside `Q`, then

```text
tau(Q-Q_D)<=(m/beta)(delta^2+C_f sqrt(E)).              (AFS5)
```

Thus a fixed-word transport which carries the role-packed aggregate
equivariantly into another copy of the same shared BCS/role module does not
install a small successor piece.  Its reducing hull fills that entire target
module, up to the presentation error.

This is a useful restriction on
`finite-depth-role-packed-aggregate-escape`.  A successful overlap-loss word
must change the shared-algebra incidence of the transported payload.  It
cannot merely move each forbidden assignment character to the corresponding
character in a fresh seed half and then rely on the reducing hull for
renewal.
