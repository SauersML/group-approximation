Construct a finite two-dimensional complex of packet groups whose vertex
charts are the three conjugate `R_i`, whose context-private variables obey the
Mastel--Slofstra overlap condition, and whose incidence hypergraph satisfies a
Slofstra--Zhang `(4,4)` or `(3,6)` curvature condition.  The desired theorem
has two parts:

1. exact minimal pictures protect the marked phase while the chart returns to
   a fresh selector context; and
2. a quantitative picture-stability estimate converts any positive
   forbidden-carrier mismatch into the transport energy already bounded
   below by `(RLP1)`.

If both parts hold, the two-dimensional cells evade the stationary
representation-ring flows of finite graphs of finite groups and supply
`s3-radical-chart-returns-to-fresh-context`.

## Attempts

- The exact half is modeled directly on Slofstra--Zhang and is plausible once
  packet-cell reductions are specified.  The soundness half is open: their
  theorem has no normalized-HS stability modulus, so Euler curvature alone
  cannot yet charge approximate transport defects.
- Plain Bass--Serre gluing remains excluded.  The point of this attack is to
  make the return genuinely two-dimensional, with curvature carried by
  relator cells rather than by restriction equations on a graph.
