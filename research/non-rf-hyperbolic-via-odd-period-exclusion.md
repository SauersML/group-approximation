If the parity claim were true, choose any odd prime `ell>=m_0`.  No member of
`Sigma` could divide `ell`, and the period-spectrum criterion would produce a
non-residually-finite word-hyperbolic `G_ell`.

This implication is valid, but the route is dead: the exact
`PSL_2(11)` certificate proves that its parity premise is false.  It must not
be used as evidence that Gromov's problem is solved.
