**OPEN ONE-ROW GROUPIFICATION TARGET.**  Fix the robust shared BCS, its
role-packed forbidden projection `B_f`, and the corrected Pauli quarter
carrier `Q_0`.  Construct one finite ordinary-group extension, a fixed depth
`N`, and words

```text
U_0,...,U_(N-1)                                         (RPA1)
```

which preserve the marked `J=-1` sector.  In every sufficiently accurate
canonical normalized-HS matrix model recursively put

```text
A_n=Q_n B_f,
D_n=the corrected marked-sector range of U_n A_n U_n^*,
Q_(n+1)=the reducing hull for the corrected shared BCS and role tuple
        generated by Q_n join D_n.                     (RPA2)
```

Require only, along this reached chain,

```text
tau(Q_n D_n)<=(1-eta)tau(A_n)+C sqrt(E),               (RPA3)
```

for fixed `eta>0`, `C<infinity` independent of matrix dimension.  The same
presentation must extend the perfect tracial BCS model with `J=-1`.  There
`B_f=0`, so every condition in `(RPA2)--(RPA3)` is vacuous.

By `reducing-hull-carry-forward-after-aggregate-escape`, no separately
decoded successor idempotent, transported context copy, common
multiplicity gauge, or coefficient-root orientation is required.  The hull
automatically satisfies

```text
tau(Q_(n+1))
 >=(1+eta beta/m)tau(Q_n)-C' sqrt(E).                  (RPA4)
```

Thus a fixed depth with `(1+eta beta/m)^N>2` is enough.

The unresolved content is exactly `(RPA3)`: ordinary group relations must
make the actual aggregate payload lose a fixed fraction of overlap with its
current, representation-dependent reducing hull.  Independent tags,
finite-character packing, or trace-functorial HNN transport cannot prove
this row, by the canonical packing ceiling.  Unlike the Steinberg unary
decoder, however, there is no second carry-forward gate after `(RPA3)`.

Nor can one feed `B_f` directly into the otherwise useful two-grading
zero-compression cell.  `role-packed-forbidden-reflection-is-not-a-group-word`
proves from canonical trace that `1-2B_f` is not a literal group element
while the role and context packets embed.  The live row must therefore use
multi-piece corner support, alter those embeddings in the load-bearing
extension, or authenticate the aggregate only after compression to the
reached carrier.

The minimal two-edge repair is also closed.
`two-edge-holonomy-erases-role-packed-payload` shows that two parallel
intertwiners of full corrected shared-algebra copies compose to a normalizer.
If they implement the same edge map and preserve the reached carrier, their
holonomy fixes `Q_nB_f` and gives zero overlap loss.  If the edge maps differ,
the reducing hull of the returned aggregate is the entire translated
carrier, so the quantitative payload mass disappears and only finite orbit
growth remains.  Thus a successful cross-edge row must be partial on the
shared algebra in a way authenticated specifically on `Q_nB_f`; full-algebra
two-edge holonomy cannot prove `(RPA3)`.

Nor can one use a single HNN letter and list one Reynolds conjugacy for each
summand of `B_f`.  By
`one-letter-reynolds-rows-globalize-structurally`, all assignment characters
in a context live on the same finite context subgroup, and conjugation by one
letter must agree on subgroup overlaps.  The rows therefore extend to the
group generated by every shared variable and the common role diagonal; after
transverse role covariance they are a full structural intertwiner and again
return the whole translated carrier.  Omitting that covariance leaves the
spin multiplicity gauge free.  The missing partial row cannot be ordinary
subgroup conjugacy by one common transporter.

The smallest additive coefficient repair is also fenced.  The three-root
Whitehead word

```text
W_p=x_12(B_f)x_21(-B_f)x_12(B_f)
```

does conjugate `diag(B_f,0)` exactly to `diag(0,B_f)` in a genuine
coefficient representation.  Thus multi-piece recombination solves the
algebraic corner geometry with only two coordinates.  But
`split-steinberg-conditional-swap-has-root-kernel-countermodel` proves that
every split Steinberg compiler for this word retracts onto the external
BCS/role packet by killing all roots.  Pulling back the finite uniform
assignment model keeps `QB_f` positive and the mark separated while making
`W_p=1`, so the overlap loss is zero at zero relator defect.  Further finite
2-by-2 or 3-by-3 sum/product charts remain in the killed root normal subgroup.
The surviving syntax must therefore contain one load-bearing
root-to-payload relation which destroys that retraction and is nevertheless
vacuous in the perfect `B_f=0` model.

A finite cyclic or Clifford actor cannot be that row when it meets the outer
system only through protected central signs.
`finite-cyclic-actor-root-kernel-dichotomy`
already gives the quarter-turn its exact conditional grading law
`W_p z W_p^(-1)=W_p^2z`.  Killing the root then factors the finite actor by
its distinguished cyclic generator.  If the quotient loses the perfect seed
character, completeness fails; if it retains that character, it yields an
exact finite marked positive-`QB_f` model with `W_p=1`.  The live row must
therefore couple to noncentral payload structure and distinguish its perfect
restriction from the uniform positive restriction.

A fresh HNN letter does not manufacture that noncentral coupling.
`load-bearing-hnn-substitution-is-an-ordinary-packet-detector` shows that a
row `tW_pt^(-1)=gV`, with `V` another root word, becomes exactly the literal
packet relator `g=1` after killing the roots.  Homogeneous power/conjugacy rows
leave the root-kernel countermodel unchanged.  A nonhomogeneous row can work
only if `g` itself is trivial in the perfect trace and has a
dimension-uniform defect on every positive-`B_f` matrix model.  That is the
original nonlinear-predicate groupification gate, now isolated without any
HNN bookkeeping.

Nor can one compare two Reynolds edges only through their relative selector
holonomy.  `two-reynolds-edges-absorb-selector-holonomy-at-one-range` takes
one HNN implementer `U` and the two words `U_0=Up_a`, `U_1=Uq_a`.  They have
relative word `p_aq_a`, whose square and commutator equal the marked sign on
the forbidden atom, but they transport every Reynolds source to exactly the
same final projection.  The finite uniform-assignment/Pauli model realizes
this with positive forbidden mass and zero defect.  The missing comparison
must therefore read a noncentral target-support observable; powers,
commutators, and Reynolds averages of the external holonomy cannot force
escape.

The smallest such target observable gives only a one-use actuator.
`smallest-target-sign-split-is-regularly-absorbed` proves first that a fixed
target contraction cannot distinguish equal path ranges except by at most
twice their projection distance.  Splitting the ranges in the minimal
`C_2^2 rtimes S_3` packet produces two orthogonal target character atoms and
an exact squared HS mismatch `tau(F)/2` on payload `F`.  But the regular
packet contains the whole three-atom orbit at zero defect and its hull
saturates at trace `3/4`; fresh copies prepay source density `4^(-N)`.  Thus
the next support observable must use a nonstationary label, not another
finite target character orbit.

The smallest infinite-orbit repair is stationary for the same quantitative
reason.  `selector-baumslag-orbits-overlap-or-prepay` keeps the exact odd
selector square on a positive forbidden atom and tensors it with the
finitely presented Baumslag address host.  Its unbounded finite-field
quotients make raw translated lamp halves overlap by `tau(A)/4`; the
first-hit projections are orthogonal but have traces
`2^(-(n+1))tau(A)`.  Thus infinite naming plus the selector phase does not
produce an unprepaid branch.  A successful aggregate escape row must rule out
this exact active multiplicity profile.
