**ESTABLISHED** (route `jacobson-corner-compressor-hulls-carry-fredholm-index-proof`).

**Setting.**
- `F` a finite field, `J = J_F = F<S,T | TS = 1>`, `I = (1 - ST)` the finitary ideal, and
  `rho : GL_N(J) -> GL_N(F[x,x^-1])` the symbol map (reduction mod `I`).
- `V = F^(N) (x) F^N`, the standard faithful `M_N(J)`-module, split by coordinates as
  `V = V_1 + V_2` with `V_1` the first `m` coordinates, `3 <= m <= N`.
- A **corner subgroup** is a subgroup `Gamma = Gamma_1 + 1_(N-m)` with
  `SL_fin(V_1) <= Gamma_1` and `Gamma_1` normal in `GL_m(J)`, or a `GL_N(J)`-conjugate of one.
  Examples: `E_m(J) + 1` (Kazhdan for `m >= 3`), `GL_m(J) + 1`, and every `GL_m(J)`-normal
  subgroup of `E_m(J)` containing the finitary special linear group.

**Theorem (FI).** Let `Gamma` be a standard corner subgroup and `g in GL_N(J)` with
`g Gamma g^-1 <= Gamma`. Then:
1. `g V_1 <= V_1` and `g V_2 >= V_2`, and `c(g) = dim_F V_1 / g V_1` is finite.
2. `c(g) = 0` if and only if `g Gamma g^-1 = Gamma`.
3. For every subgroup `H <= GL_N(J)`, every element of the group `D_H` generated by the
   compressors of `Gamma` in `H` commensurates `V_1`, and the relative index
   `ind(h) = [V_1 : h V_1]` is a homomorphism `D_H -> Z` with `ind(g) = c(g)` on compressors.

**Corollary (FI-normal).** If `Gamma <= G <= GL_N(J)` and every homomorphism from `D_G` to `Z`
is trivial (for instance `Gamma` infranormal in `G` and `G` Kazhdan, or `G^ab` torsion), then
every compressor of `Gamma` in `G` normalizes it. In particular a corner subgroup that is
infranormal in a Kazhdan subgroup `G` of `GL_N(J)` is normal in `G`.

**Corollary (FI-DD).** For every `H <= GL_N(J_F)`, including `EL_n(J)`, `GL_n(J)` and all
Toeplitz-supported corner groups, no pair `Gamma <= G <= H` with `Gamma` a corner subgroup
and `G` Kazhdan has a nontrivial rigid compression defect. If `Gamma` is normal in `G`, then
`g z g^-1` centralizes `g Gamma g^-1 = Gamma`. So neither `DD(sofic)` nor `DD(hyperlinear)`
can certify nonsoficity or nonhyperlinearity of a subgroup of `GL_N(J_F)` through a corner pair.

## Obstruction (class killed)

**Class.** Every rigid-compression-defect certificate (`DD(sofic)`,
`DD(hyperlinear)`, and the ultraproduct normalization forms equivalent to them by
`rigid-compression-defect-normalization-dichotomy`) that tries to show a subgroup of
`GL_N(J_F)` is nonsofic or nonhyperlinear using a corner pair.

**Invariant.** The relative Fredholm index `[V_1 : h V_1]` of the moved module
`V_1 = span (gamma - 1)V` of the corner. Compressors commensurate it, it is additive on the
compressor hull, and it equals the codimension of the compressed corner.

**Death step.** The hypothesis that the compressor hull `G` is Kazhdan. A strict compressor has
index `c(g) > 0`, and a Kazhdan group has no nonzero homomorphism to `Z`. So every compressor
normalizes the corner, and the defect vanishes. The corner compressor `u = [[S, Q], [0, T]]` of
the closing remark in `compression-criterion-inert-on-modular-linear-extensions-proof` has
`ind(u) = m`, the codimension of `S V_1`. That remark saw its top-block determinant; FI shows
that every compressor hull of every corner has the same obstruction.

## What survives

- Kazhdan subgroups `Gamma` of `GL_N(J_F)` that are not corners, whose moved module is not
  commensurated by their compressors. For example, `Gamma` whose symbol image is itself strictly
  compressed in `GL_N(F[x,x^-1])`. No such pair inside `GL_N(J_F)` is recorded in this graph.
  Kun--Thom's compressors are monomial substitutions in several variables, while the only
  `F`-algebra automorphisms of `F[x,x^-1]` are `x -> a x^(+-1)`, so the symbol ring offers no
  analogue of them. An embedded Kun--Thom pair would have to arise from a non-corner subgroup.
- Pairs whose ambient `H` is not inside `GL_N(J_F)`, e.g. the binary Leavitt unit group, where
  `V_1` has no Fredholm structure. This is consistent with `openai-leavitt-unit-nonsofic`.

## Scope

- Nothing is claimed about soficity or hyperlinearity of any group. FI removes a certificate.
- `F` finite is used only for local finiteness of the kernel elsewhere in the class. The proof of
  FI works over any field.
