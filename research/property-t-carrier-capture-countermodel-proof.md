The generator `w` has infinite order, so its spectral distribution in the
canonical group trace is Haar measure on the circle and
`tau(1_E(lambda(w)))=m_T(E)=beta`. The subgroup `K` centralizes `w`, while
the `C_2` generator sends `lambda(w)` to `lambda(w)^*`. Inversion invariance
of `E` therefore makes `p` fixed by all of `H`.

The infinite dihedral group is residually finite, direct products of
residually finite groups are residually finite, and `K` was chosen residually
finite. Hence `G=D_infinity times K` is residually finite and therefore
hyperlinear. This exact canonical model has all the advertised positive
density and Kazhdan data while its carrier distance from the full fixed
algebra is zero.
