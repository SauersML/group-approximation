title: If Y is a factor of X, the LEF growth of EL_3(LC(Y,F_2)⋊Z) is at most that of EL_3(LC(X,F_2)⋊Z), up to a linear rescaling of the radius
---

**Statement.** Let π: X → Y be a factor map of infinite minimal subshifts. Then G_Y = EL_3(LC(Y,F_2)⋊Z) embeds in G_X through f ↦ f∘π and u ↦ u. The embedding distorts word length by at most a constant K, so

`L_Y(r) ≤ L_X(Kr)` for all r.

Consequences:
- conjugate subshifts have ≃-equivalent LEF growth;
- lower bounds such as L ≽ exp(r²) pass from a factor to every extension.
