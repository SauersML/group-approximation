title: Torsion-free finite-index subgroups of hierarchically hyperbolic groups have contractible Rips complexes
distinct_from:

---

Let `H` be a hierarchically hyperbolic group and `G ≤ H` a torsion-free subgroup of finite
index. Then there is a finite generating set `T` of `G` such that the Rips complex
`R_1(G, T)`, the clique complex of `Cay(G, T)`, is contractible.

Examples: torsion-free finite-index subgroups of mapping class groups of surfaces,
right-angled Artin groups, compact special groups. For mapping class groups this gives
groups of type F with contractible Rips complexes, which are neither CAT(0) nor, as far as
is known, Helly.
