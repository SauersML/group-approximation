For a fixed finite relational template `A`, commutativity gadgets in each
standard quantum model are characterized by classicality of the corresponding
quantum polymorphisms, and arity `|A|^2` suffices.  Robust gadgets are
characterized by stable commutativity of the weighted polymorphism algebra at
the same bounded arity.  In the `qa` and `qc` regimes, the exact gadget
condition is equivalent to the corresponding robust condition; a `qa` gadget
also gives a robust finite-dimensional `q` gadget.

The exact model-dependent statements are imported by
`quantum-polymorphism-commutativity-gadget-citation`.
