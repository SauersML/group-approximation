**ESTABLISHED** by `lo-syllable-relators-chen-lodha-derivation` (literature
derivation: Chen–Lodha's theorems are imported, not reproved). Not independently
reviewed; no novelty claimed. Chen–Lodha state the weaker hypothesis for their
spectral gap theorem (l.313), and the deduction below is their proof of Theorem B
run with that hypothesis.

**Statement.** Let `A` and `B` be arbitrary groups, `G = A * B`, and let
`w = a_1 b_1 ... a_n b_n` be cyclically reduced, `n >= 1`, `a_i ∈ A∖{1}`,
`b_i ∈ B∖{1}`. Suppose the syllable subgroups `A' = <a_1,...,a_n>` and
`B' = <b_1,...,b_n>` are left-orderable. Then the natural maps
`A -> G/<<w>>` and `B -> G/<<w>>` are injective. In particular `w` does not
normally generate `G`.

**Remarks.**
- No hypothesis is placed on `A` or `B`: they may contain torsion, or be
  non-orderable, as long as the finitely generated subgroups spanned by the
  letters of `w` are orderable.
- For torsion-free `A`, `B`, this contains Chen–Lodha Theorem B (orderable
  factors have orderable subgroups).
- The consequence for normal generators is
  `normal-generators-of-free-products-have-non-lo-syllables`.
- Kirby's 1995 Problem 5.7 (Freedman) asks whether one of the two maps is
  injective when `A` is torsion-free and `B` is nontrivial
  (`kirby-freedman-one-factor-injects-when-a-is-torsion-free`). This claim gives
  both maps for relators with orderable syllable subgroups.
