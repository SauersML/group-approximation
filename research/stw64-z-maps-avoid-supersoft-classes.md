Let A satisfy the hypotheses of stw64-supersoft-calculus, and let
f:Cu(Z)->Cu(A) be a unit-preserving Cu-morphism. Write f(t) for the image
of the soft real class t, not the compact integer class when t is integral.

If f(t)=alpha(t*[1_A]-hat) for even one finite t>0, then rc(A)=0.
Consequently, if rc(A)>0, then for every finite t>0,

    f(t) <= alpha(t*[1_A]-hat)
    and f(t) != alpha(t*[1_A]-hat).

This excludes the proposed canonical supersoft family as a second
unit-preserving Cu-morphism in a positive-radius Villadsen--Toms target.
It does not construct two actual morphisms and does not resolve STW LXIV
or the Jiang--Su uniqueness problem LXIII.
