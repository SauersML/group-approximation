This is a citation import, not a reproof.

- **Primary:** J.-C. Schlage-Puchta, *A p-group with positive rank gradient*,
  arXiv:1105.1631 (J. Group Theory 2012), Theorem 2.
- **Read from:** M. Ershov, *Golod-Shafarevich groups: a survey*, IJAC 22(5)
  (2012), Section 9, Theorem 9.1 (i)-(ii), page 34, from the author's revised
  PDF on 2026-09-11.

The survey proves (ii) by reducing to a normal subgroup of index `p` and citing
[SP, Theorem 2].
