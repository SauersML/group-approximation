The conjugation calculation in the prerequisite says that `x_ij(r)` changes
the `j`th coordinate character by

```text
phi(r)(a)=chi_i(ra).                                         (SRG2)
```

Because the coefficient ring has characteristic two, `phi` is a homomorphism
from the additive elementary abelian group of `L` to its character group.
Its kernel is exactly the right ideal `K` in `(SRG1)`.  Hence the orbit map

```text
L/K -> U_ij chi,        r+K |-> x_ij(r) chi                  (SRG3)
```

is a bijection, and `x_ij(s)` acts through `(SRG3)` by translation by
`s+K`.  The prerequisite proves that `L/K` is infinite.

Let `S` be any finite symmetric subset of `U_ij`, and let `V` be the subgroup
of `L/K` generated by its translation labels.  Since `L/K` is an elementary
abelian `2`-group and `S` is finite, `V` is finite.  The `S`-Schreier
components of `L/K` are the finite cosets of `V`.

Let `M_S` be the normalized Schreier operator on `ell^2(L/K)`.  The indicator
of any one coset of `V` is a nonzero finitely supported vector fixed by every
translation in `S`, and hence by `M_S`.  Since `M_S` is an average of
unitaries, `||M_S||<=1`; the fixed vector gives the reverse inequality.
Therefore

```text
||M_S||=1,                                                   (SRG4)
```

which is precisely zero finite-set Schreier gap.
