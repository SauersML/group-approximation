title: There are uncountably many finitely generated residually finite groups up to isomorphism (Chong–Wise)
---

There are uncountably many pairwise nonisomorphic finitely generated residually finite groups: the doubles
`F_2 *_H F_2` of Chong and Wise, varying the infinitely generated subgroup `H`. Literature import; see the
`-citation` route. Grigorchuk's groups give another uncountable family (recalled only).
