ESTABLISHED.  Let `U_1,...,U_k` be commuting diagonal unitaries on
`C^N`.  Suppose their joint eigenvalue labels are all distinct, so their
joint spectral algebra is the full position algebra `D_N`.  If
`N >= 2^(k+1)`, there is a self-adjoint unitary `S` such that

```text
dist_2(S,D_N) >= 1/sqrt(2),                              (BAR1)
sum_(i=1)^k ||[S,U_i]||_2^2
 <= 16 pi^2 k (2/N)^(2/k).                              (BAR2)
```

Here `||.||_2` is normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  If the joint labels
are not distinct, the commutant of the register words already contains a
nontrivial within-label matrix block, so those words cannot recover the
position algebra at all.

Thus no fixed number of commuting group-word register tests has a
dimension-independent local-to-global constant for block diagonalization.
The inverse correction constant grows at least on the order of `N^(1/k)`.

The statement does not exclude a number of generators growing with `N`, nor
does it claim that a nonabelian expander representation has a position basis
generated by group words.  The latter would require algebraic spectral
idempotents or another groupification mechanism, which is a separate hole.
