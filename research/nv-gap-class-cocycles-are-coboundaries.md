**ESTABLISHED** by `nv-gap-class-cocycles-are-coboundaries-proof`.

**Setting.** `n ≥ 2`, `C = {0,1}^ω`, `G = nV` acting on `C^n`. A *brick* is
`B = [w_1] × … × [w_n]`, with canonical chart `ι_B(z_1,…,z_n) = (w_1z_1,…,w_nz_n)`. A *brick
partition* is a finite partition `P` of `C^n` into bricks. For `P`, a coordinate `j`, and
`h = (h_B)_{B∈P} ∈ V^P`, let `h^{P,j} ∈ G` act on each `B ∈ P` by `ι_B ∘ h_B^{(j)} ∘ ι_B^{-1}`,
where `h_B^{(j)}` acts by `h_B` on coordinate `j` only. Then `A_P^{(j)} = {h^{P,j}} ≅ V^{|P|}` is
a subgroup of `G`: the *brick-local copies of V in coordinate j*. For the one-brick partition,
`A^{(j)} = V_{(j)}`, the coordinate copy.

**Definition (gap class).** A unitary representation `ρ` of `G` is in the *gap class* if, for
every brick partition `P` and every coordinate `j`, the restriction `ρ|_{A_P^{(j)}}` has no almost
invariant vectors, that is, there are a finite `F ⊆ A_P^{(j)}` and `c > 0` with
`c‖r‖ ≤ Σ_{s∈F} ‖r − ρ(s) r‖` for all `r`.

**Theorem.** If `ρ` is in the gap class, then every 1-cocycle `b: G → ρ` is a coboundary:
`H^1(nV, ρ) = 0`. Equivalently, every affine isometric action of `nV` with linear part `ρ`
has a fixed point.

**Corollary (extractable representations).** Suppose `ρ` has a *measure extraction*
`r ↦ μ_r ∈ M_+(C^n)` with `μ_r(C^n) = ‖r‖²`, `μ_{ρ(g) r} = g_* μ_r` for all `g ∈ G`, and
`|μ_r(E) − μ_s(E)| ≤ ‖r − s‖(‖r‖ + ‖s‖)` for Borel `E`. Then `ρ` is in the gap class, with
`F` the `3|P|` brick-local copies of the triple `h_1,h_2,h_3` and `c = 1/10`. So `H^1 = 0` for:
- chart modules `ℓ²(X; H)` (any invariant set `X` of charts, any twisting `π`);
- `ℓ²(G/L) ⊗ π` for every `L` fixing a Borel probability measure on `C^n`: every amenable `L`,
  point stabilizers, rigid stabilizers, and `L = 1` (so `H^1(nV, ℓ²(nV)) = 0` and the first
  `ℓ²`-Betti number of `nV` vanishes);
- Koopman modules `L²(Ω, μ; H)` over any `G`-space with an equivariant map to `Prob(C^n)`,
  including `L²(C^n, λ)`;
- and every representation weakly contained in a direct sum of these (the gap inequality with
  fixed `F, c` is linear in functions of positive type).

**Consequences for the goal.**
- *Class-kill.* A proper cocycle of `nV` (Haagerup), a cocycle proper modulo `T_n` (R1 of
  `brin-thompson-nv-a-t-menable-via-proper-modulo-triangular`), and a cocycle witnessing the
  failure of property (T) (`brin-thompson-groups-nv-lack-property-t`, through Delorme–Guichardet)
  all need a representation outside the gap class: for some brick partition `P` and coordinate
  `j`, the brick-local copies `A_P^{(j)} ≅ V^{|P|}` must have almost invariant vectors.
  - **Invariant:** spectral gap of the brick-local coordinate copies of `V`.
  - **Step where every member dies:** the conjugation step. `g H_D g^{-1} = H_R` for the domain
    and range brick partitions of `g`, where `H_P = A_P^{(1)} ⋯ A_P^{(n)}` is the brick-product
    subgroup. Once `b` vanishes on all `H_P`, `b(g)` is `H_R`-invariant, hence `0`.
- *The bounded-generation heuristic was not the obstruction.* The target of
  `nv-extractable-cocycles-bounded-on-centralizers` expected gap-class cocycles to be unbounded,
  because the baker's powers are not boundedly generated by `V^n` and a finite set. That is
  true (each factor from a normalizer of a bounded-depth local gap set moves boundedly many
  bits between coordinates), but it is irrelevant: the subgroups `H_P` are weakly normal in
  the sense that every `g` conjugates some `H_D` onto some `H_R`, and the vanishing propagates
  through that.
- *Calibration (`n = 1`).* For `V` the theorem is false (Farley's chart cocycle is proper and
  its module is extractable). The proof dies at Step 2: a brick-local copy of `V` in the only
  coordinate commutes with no gap set. So the argument uses the product structure `n ≥ 2`,
  which the calibration table of the goal names as the one property a positive proof must use.
