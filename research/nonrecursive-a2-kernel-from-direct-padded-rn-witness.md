Take `E` acting on its proper nonempty boundary clopens, as in items 3–4
of the prerequisite. The acting group is finitely presented, its point
stabilizers are finitely generated, and there are finitely many ordered-pair
orbits. Its kernel is exactly the nonrecursive subgroup `M`. These are
precisely the target's assertions. This conditional projection does not
replace the target's pre-existing, separate derivations.
