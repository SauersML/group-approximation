**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`zelmanov-standard-isomorphisms-linear-groups-with-half-citation`).
E. I. Zel'manov, *Isomorphisms of linear groups over an associative ring*, Sibirsk. Mat. Zh. 26 (1985) no. 4,
49–67; English translation Siberian Math. J. 26 (1985) 515–530, doi:10.1007/BF00971299. Read from the scanned
translation (pp. 515–529), fetched from Springer on MSI on 2026-09-13.

Standing assumptions of §3 (p. 528): "We start with two associative rings R, S with 1/2; the group G lies either
between E_n(R) and GL_n(R) or between EO_n(R, π) and O_n(R, π), n ≥ 3; φ: G → S* is a monomorphism such that
φ(G) ⊇ φ(Σ)_S. We shall assume that the ring S is generated by the set φ(G)." Here φ(Σ)_S is the orthogonal
closure (p. 520): the subgroup of S* generated by the involutions 1 − 2e over the Boolean closure of the idempotents
(1 − φ(σ_i))/2, with Σ = Σ_1 = {diag[±1, ..., ±1] ∈ E_n(R) | det σ = 1} (p. 523).

- Corollary 1 (p. 528): "Assume that E_n(R) ⊆ G ⊆ GL_n(R). Then there exist 1) a decomposition of the ring S into a
  direct sum of ideals S = S_0 ⊕ S_1 ⊕ S', 2) an epimorphism φ_0: R_n → S_0, 3) an antiepimorphism φ_1: R_n → S_1,
  such that for every element g ∈ E_n(R) we have φ(g) = φ_0(g) + φ_1(g^{-1}) + f, where f is the identity of the ideal
  S'."
- Corollary 4 (p. 529): "If S = A_m is the matrix ring of order m ≥ 2 over a ring A ∋ 1/2, φ(G) = GL_m(A) = S* then
  R_n = I_0 ⊕ I_1, S = S_0 ⊕ S_1, φ_0: I_0 → S_0 an isomorphism, φ_1: I_1 → S_1 an antiisomorphism, and for every
  element g = g_0 + g_1 ∈ E_n(R), g_i ∈ I_i, i = 1, 2 we have φ(g) = φ_0(g_0) + φ_1(g_1^{-1})."
- Introduction (p. 515): "the isomorphisms φ: GL_n(R) → GL_m(S), φ: O_n(R, π) → O_m(S, π'), where n ≥ 3, m ≥ 2;
  R, S ∋ 1/2, are standard on the elementary subgroup."

Scope:
- 1/2 must be invertible in both rings, so characteristic 2 is excluded.
- The groups are between E_n and GL_n. Quotients E_n(R)/Z by the centre are not covered.
- When φ(G) is only E_n(S), the closure condition φ(G) ⊇ φ(Σ)_S has to be checked separately.
