If a discrete group `G` has Ozawa's PHP property, then `C*_r(G)` with its
canonical trace is completely selfless, hence selfless.  The PHP property is
closed under finite direct products and holds for acylindrically hyperbolic
groups with trivial finite radical.

**Established (literature).**  These are Theorem 14, Section 8, and
Proposition 15 of Narutaka Ozawa, *Proximality and selflessness for group
C*-algebras*, arXiv:2508.07938.  They are imported here, not reproved.
