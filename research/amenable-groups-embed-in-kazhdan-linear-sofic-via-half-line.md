This route replaces the swap-double step of `amenable-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-linear-sofic-proof`. That map
`γ ↦ (γ, γ^(-1))` is not a homomorphism for non-abelian `Γ` (`wreath-products-keep-the-abelianization-of-the-base`).

Let `Γ` be finitely generated and amenable, and let `q` be a prime power.

1. **Overgroup.** Let `Δ = C(Γ)` be the half-line overgroup. It is finitely generated, infinite and amenable, and
   `δ_0 : Γ → Δ` is injective with `δ_0(Γ) ≤ [Δ,Δ]` (items 1–4 of the required overgroup claim).
2. **System.** `Δ` is countably infinite, so Gao–Jackson–Seward give a free minimal subflow `X ⊆ A^Δ` over a finite
   alphabet. The transformation groupoid is ample, Hausdorff, principal and minimal, with compact infinite unit space.
3. **Ring.** `R = LC(X, F_q) ⋊ Δ` is its Steinberg algebra. It is simple with centre `F_q`. As a ring it is generated by
   the letter indicators, `u_δ` for `δ` in a finite generating set of `Δ`, and a primitive element of `F_q`: translates
   of letter indicators and their products give every cylinder indicator.
4. **(T), infinite, simple.** `EL_3(R)` is finitely generated and Kazhdan. It is infinite because `e_12(LC(X,F_q))` is
   infinite. `S = EL_3(R)/Z` is simple, with `Z` consisting of scalar matrices over `F_q^×`.
5. **Linear soficity.** `Δ` is amenable and acts minimally, so `R` has the faithful Foelner rank model, and `S` is
   `F_q`-linear sofic by the projective transfer claim.
6. **Embedding.** By item 3 of `perfect-groups-embed-in-crossed-product-elementary-groups`, `[Δ,Δ] → EL_3(R)`,
   `δ ↦ diag(u_δ, 1, 1)`, is an injective homomorphism. Composing with `δ_0` embeds `Γ` in `EL_3(R)`. The composite stays
   injective modulo `Z`, because `diag(u_δ, 1, 1)` with `u_δ ≠ 1` is not a scalar matrix. For `n > 3`, pad with ones.

**Model tests.**
- `Γ` finitely presented, solvable and not residually finite: the envelope contains a non-LEF subgroup, so it is not LEF,
  and only linear soficity is asserted. This is consistent with `gap-sofic-approximations-are-local-embeddings`.
- `Γ = F_2`: `C(F_2)` is not amenable, and step 5 fails, which is the boundary of the route.
- `Γ` perfect: `δ_0` can be replaced by the identity map of `Γ`, recovering
  `amenable-perfect-groups-embed-in-kazhdan-linear-sofic-proof`.
