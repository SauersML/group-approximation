Let `alpha` be a homeomorphism of a compact metrisable space `X`.  The
following are equivalent:

1. `C(X) rtimes_alpha Z` is AF-embeddable;
2. it is quasidiagonal;
3. it is stably finite;
4. there is no open `U subset X` for which
   `alpha(U) proper_subset U`.
