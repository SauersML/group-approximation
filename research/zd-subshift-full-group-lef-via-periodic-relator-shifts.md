**ESTABLISHED.**

**Setting.** `Omega ⊆ A^{Z^d}` is a subshift on which `Z^d` acts freely. `Gamma ≤ [[Z^d ~ Omega]]` is generated by a finite symmetric `S`.
- Write `g(x) = sigma^{c_g(x)} x`, with `(sigma^v x)_w = x_{w+v}`.
- Fix `R` such that, for `s ∈ S`, `|c_s| <= R` and `c_s(x)` depends only on `x|B(R)`.

**Extension.** An extension of radius `R' >= R` is a family of maps `ĉ_s : A^{B(R')} -> B(R) ∩ Z^d` that agree with `c_s` on patterns of points of `Omega`.
- Put `ŝ(z) = sigma^{ĉ_s(z|B(R'))} z` on `A^{Z^d}`.
- For a word `w`, put `ŵ(z) = sigma^{ĉ_w(z)} z`.

**Relator shift.** `Z_n` is the set of all `z` with `ĉ_w(sigma^v z) = 0` for all `v ∈ Z^d` and all words `w` of length `<= 2n` that are trivial in `Gamma`. It is an SFT containing `Omega`.

**Criterion.** Suppose that for every `n` there are an extension of radius `R'_n` and a periodic `y_n ∈ Z_n` with period lattice `L_n` such that:
- (H1) every pattern of radius `2nR'_n` occurring in `Omega` occurs in `y_n`;
- (H2) `L_n` has no nonzero vector of norm `<= 2nR`.

Then `Gamma` is LEF. The permutations `ŝ` of the finite orbit of `y_n` generate a finite group with exactly the relators of `Gamma` of length `<= 2n`.

**Minimal case.** If `Omega` is minimal, (H1) and (H2) follow from a single legal patch in `y_n`, of a radius depending only on `n` and `R'_n`.

**Proof.** The proof is `zd-full-group-lef-relator-shift-criterion-proof`. The artifact, Section 1, has the same argument.
- The relators of length 2 make each `ŝ` a permutation of the orbit.
- Trivial short words have zero extended cocycle on `Z_n`.
- A nontrivial short word moves some legal patch by a nonzero vector. By (H1) that patch occurs in `y_n`, and by (H2) the vector is not a period.

No novelty is claimed beyond the formulation. It is the spatial form of the Grigorchuk--Medynets and Cornulier arguments, with the periodic configuration allowed to contain forbidden patterns.
