title: Stable weak cancellation and finite Z-stabilization prevent compact Cu collapse

Let `A` have stable weak cancellation: whenever projections `p,q` in
`A tensor K` generate the same ideal `J` and have the same class in `K_0(J)`,
they are Murray--von Neumann equivalent.  Assume also that `A tensor_min Z`
is stably finite.  Then

```text
Cu(A) -> Cu(A tensor_min Z)
```

is injective on compact elements represented by projections.

In particular, no stably finite pure algebra satisfying stable weak
cancellation can realize the finite-projection compact counterexample route.
Pureness is not needed for the injectivity statement itself.
