For triangle faces with carrier incidence
`(P,Q)`, `(Q,R)`, `(R,P)`, every carrier occurs once with each sign if and
only if the three face orientations have the same normal/inverse chirality.
Otherwise nonabelian elimination retains a carrier holonomy and does not
produce a coefficient-only boundary.
