Let `F=C_2*C_2=<a,b | a^2=b^2=1>` and let `pi:F->U(C^2)` be

```text
pi(a)=diag(1,-1),              pi(b)=[[0,1],[1,0]].       (SRC1)
```

For the rank-one projection `P=diag(1,0)`, the compression

```text
Phi:C^*(F)->B(P C^2),          Phi(x)=P pi(x) P           (SRC2)
```

is ucp and satisfies `Phi(a)=P`.  Thus the relator unitary `a` belongs to
the multiplicative domain of `Phi`.  Nevertheless `Phi` does **not** factor
through the quotient imposing `a=1`:

```text
Phi(bab)=P pi(bab) P=-P,
q(bab)=q(b^2)=1.                                      (SRC3)
```

Equivalently,

```text
Phi(b(a-1)b)=-2P != 0,                                (SRC4)
```

so the two-sided ideal normally generated by `a-1` is not killed.  The
multiplicative-domain identities control `Phi(ax)` and `Phi(xa)`, but they
cannot be iterated across an arbitrary sandwich `x(a-1)y` because neither
`xa` nor `ay` need remain in the multiplicative domain.

More generally, let `pi:F(S)->U(H)`, let `P` be a projection, and suppose a
family of relator words `r` obeys

```text
P pi(r) P=lambda_r P,       |lambda_r|=1.               (SRC5)
```

Then `P H` reduces each `pi(r)` and `pi(r)|_(P H)=lambda_r`, but this only
gives the relations on the single compressed fibre.  Compression descends
through the normal group quotient once the same scalar relation holds for
every conjugate on that fibre:

```text
P pi(g r g^-1) P=lambda_r P       for every g in F(S).  (SRC6)
```

Indeed `(SRC6)` says every conjugate relator fixes `P H` with the prescribed
phase.  For `K=closure(span pi(F(S))P H)`, normality then gives

```text
pi(r) pi(g)xi
 =pi(g) pi(g^-1 r g)xi
 =lambda_r pi(g)xi,
```

so all quotient relators act correctly on the cyclic hull `K`, producing a
representation of the quotient.  Conversely, any descended compression has
the conjugate relations `(SRC6)`.

For readable-control freezing, `(SRC5)` is exactly what perfect acceptance
gives on one readable atom.  Condition `(SRC6)` asks for the selected frozen
relation on every unreadable translate of that atom.  Remote readable-bit
motion changes the selected relation on those translates, so `(SRC6)` is
precisely the orbit-coherence/invariant-fibre resource that the compression
shortcut was supposed to avoid.
