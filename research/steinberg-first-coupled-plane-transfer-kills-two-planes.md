This route is invalidated by
`steinberg-coupled-plane-transfer-has-marginal-absorber`.  For
every `p>=7`, an explicit nonzero function on the `b=-4,-4/5,0` planes has
zero point and plane marginals, lies locally in `im(1-q)`, and satisfies the
entire first coupled transfer.  A valid second-plane argument must add
pointwise `X` information or couple further `b`-planes.
