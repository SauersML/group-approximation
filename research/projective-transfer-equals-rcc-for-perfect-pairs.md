If `C<=A=<C,h>` and both groups are perfect, then in every tracial matrix
ultraproduct, projective centralization of `C` transferring to `h` is
equivalent to ordinary relative-commutant collapse
`pi(C)'=pi(A)'`.  Projectivization removes no obstruction for such a pair;
its benefit can only be quantitative before the zero-defect limit.
