By `closed-3-manifold-groups-hhg-classification` (the "if" direction of
Hagen–Russell–Sisto–Spriano Theorem 3.3), `π_1 M` is a hierarchically hyperbolic group. By
`hierarchically-hyperbolic-groups-have-contractible-rips`, it has a finite
generating set `T` with `P_1(π_1 M, T)` contractible. Torsion, which occurs when a prime
factor is spherical, is allowed by that claim. ∎
