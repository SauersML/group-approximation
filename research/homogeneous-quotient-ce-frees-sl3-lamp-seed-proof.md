Full proof: Section 4 of the artifact, including Remarks D3 and D4.

**Generation.** From `h e_ij(x) h^(-1) = e_ij((h_i/h_j) x)` with `(h_1,h_2,h_3) = (2,1,1/2)`, conjugating
`e_ij(1)` by powers of `h^(+-1)` and taking integer powers gives every `e_ij(y)`, `y in Z[1/2]`, inside `<C,h>`.
`Z[1/2]` is Euclidean, so `SL_3(Z[1/2])` is generated by elementary matrices (row reduction, then Whitehead's lemma).
Hence `<C,h> = A`.

**(D1).**

1. By clauses 2 and 3 of `homogeneous-quotient-ce-refutes-arithmetic-commutant-collapse`, with `n = 3`, `p = 2` and
   `S = {h}`, a `K`-orbit `P <= Y` is `C`-invariant and `mu(P triangle hP) > 0`.
2. Self-contained check. `P` is compact and open. If `mu(P triangle hP) = 0`, then full support of `mu` gives
   `hP = P`. The stabilizer of `P` is then an open, hence closed, subgroup containing `<C,h> = A`. `A` is dense in
   `SL_3(Q_2)`, because the closure contains every `e_ij(x)`, `x in Q_2`. So the stabilizer is everything, and
   `P = Y`, contradicting `r >= 2`.
3. Apply (C1) of `coset-lamp-seed-radical-is-ce-fixed-algebra-rigidity` with `(Z, kappa) = (Y, mu)`, `E = P` and
   `a = h`. The crossed product is Connes embeddable by `(SH1)`, so `m_0 notin Rad_hyp(L rtimes A)`. Then (C3), with
   `t = delta_C`, gives the `L_0` statement.

**(D2).** Apply `central-d8-mark-radical-equals-seed-radical`, direction 2, to (D1).

**(D3).** Lift the images of the finitely many generators under the ultraproduct homomorphism of (D2) to unitary
tuples. Windowed relator defects tend to `0` along the ultrafilter, and `||w_J - I||_2` tends to
`||-1 - 1||_2 = 2`. Finitely many such conditions hold together on an ultrafilter-large set. This is the negation of
the finite-window clause of `hyperlinear-radical-presentation-compactness`.

**Contrapositive.** A `C`-invariant set not fixed by `A = <C,h>` is moved by `h`. So (C1), (C3) and Lemma A show that
a Connes-embeddable action with `L^infinity(Z)^C != L^infinity(Z)^A` puts `J` outside the radical. `(SH1)` supplies
such an action.
