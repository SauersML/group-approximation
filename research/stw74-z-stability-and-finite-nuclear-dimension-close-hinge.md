Let `A` be a simple unital Z-stable C*-algebra such that every nonzero
projection in `A` is infinite.  Then `A` is purely infinite.

Consequently, every separable simple unital C*-algebra of finite nuclear
dimension which satisfies the same projection hypothesis is purely infinite.
Equivalently, any separable counterexample to Problem LXXIV must satisfy

```text
A is not Z-stable,                 dim_nuc(A)=infinity.
```

The non-Z-stability conclusion does not require separability.
