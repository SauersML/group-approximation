For every countable group `C` there is a finitely generated, infinite, simple
group with property (T) that contains a copy of `C`.

Construction (Section 1 of the artifact):
1. Put `C` inside a two-generator infinite simple group `C'` (Schupp).
2. Take `H * C'`, with `H` a torsion-free hyperbolic Kazhdan group, and apply
   Hull's small-cancellation theorem twice:
   - first so that the generators of `C'` land in the image of `H`;
   - then so that the generators of `H` land in the normal closure of `C'`.
   Because `C'` is simple, injectivity on `C'` is one finite condition, which
   the theorem's ball clause supplies.
3. The result `Q` is a quotient of `H`, hence finitely generated and Kazhdan,
   and is normally generated by any nontrivial element of `C'`.
4. A maximal proper normal subgroup of `Q` misses `C'`, and the quotient by it
   is the required simple Kazhdan group.

No novelty is claimed. The container is not claimed finitely presented.
