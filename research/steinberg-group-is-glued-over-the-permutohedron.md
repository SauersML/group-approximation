**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `R` be an algebra over a finite field, `n>=3`, and
for each total order `pi` of `{0,...,n-1}` let

```text
U_pi = < x_ij(a) : i <_pi j, a in R >  <=  St_n(R),                    (SPG1)
```

the unipotent radical of the ordering `pi`; each `U_pi` is a locally finite
`p`-group, and for orderings `pi, pi'` the intersection `U_pi cap U_pi'` is
the subgroup generated by the roots positive in both.  Then

```text
St_n(R) = colim_(pi in S_n) U_pi,                                        (SPG2)
```

the colimit over the diagram of all `U_pi` and their pairwise
intersections; equivalently, `St_n(R)` is the fundamental group of the
complex of groups on the permutohedron of `S_n` whose vertex groups are
the `U_pi`, whose edge groups (adjacent transpositions) are the
intersections `U_pi cap U_(s pi)` of index-`|R|`-type, and whose two-cells
are the squares (commuting adjacent transpositions) and the hexagons
(braid relations of every rank-two subsystem `A_2`).

Consequently a corona representation of `St_n(R)`, at all large
coordinates and for any radius, is a family of exact unitary
representations `rho_pi` of the finite pieces `U_pi^((r))` agreeing up to
`o(1)` along every edge, and it comes from an exact finite-dimensional
representation of `St_n(R)` (which kills the compression defect) exactly
when the compatibility unitaries can be gauged to have trivial holonomy
around every hexagon; holonomy around squares is always trivializable.
The whole Property-`(T)`-free question is therefore the holonomy of a
single rank-two configuration: six locally finite Heisenberg-type groups
`U_pi(R)`, `pi in S_3`, glued cyclically along their abelian root pairs,
in the trace-vanishing regime forced by
`degree-zero-leavitt-core-characters-are-dirac-haar-mixtures`.

No Property `(T)`, spectral gap, or literature input is used.
