## Statement

Let `Λ <= Aut(T_p)` contain the rational Iwahori group `Γ_p` of
`pgl2-q-in-rover-nekrashevych-group-of-rational-iwahori`, acting on
`Z_p = ∂T_p` by Möbius maps.

- **(a) Not an automaton group.** `Λ` is not generated by finite-state
  automorphisms. In particular no group generated by finitely many finite
  automata contains `Γ_p`.
- **(b) Not metabelian**, if `Λ` is finitely generated.
- **(c) Not linear** over any field, if `Λ` is finitely generated.
- **(d) No ring constructions.** A finitely generated `Λ` is not any of the
  following.
  - **(d1)** A group of continuous affine maps `z ↦ A(z) + b` of `Z_p`, where
    `A` is a continuous additive automorphism.
  - **(d2)** A group of affine maps `x ↦ ax + b`, or of Möbius maps
    `x ↦ (ax+b)(cx+d)^{-1}`, with coefficients in a ring `R`, commutative or
    not, acting on the `π`-adic tree of `R` identified with `T_p`.
    - Here `π ∈ R` is not a left zero-divisor, `πR = Rπ`, `|R/πR| = p`, and the
      identification sends the cone `r + π^kR̂` to a level-`k` cone.
    - The **commutativity lemma** in the proof shows that the `π`-adic completion
      `R̂` of such an `R` is a commutative complete discrete valuation ring with
      residue field `F_p`. So a noncommutative `R` gives nothing beyond a
      commutative one.

## Where this leaves `rational-iwahori-group-lies-in-fp-self-similar-group`

A witness `Λ` must be a finitely presented self-similar group with all of
these properties:
- it has a generator that is not finite-state;
- it is non-linear and non-metabelian;
- its action on `Z_p` is not by affine or Möbius maps over any ring whose adic
  tree is `T_p`.

The coordinator's suggested families (automaton groups, affine or Möbius groups
over finitely generated noncommutative rings, and Zaremsky-type affine hosts)
are all excluded in this form.
