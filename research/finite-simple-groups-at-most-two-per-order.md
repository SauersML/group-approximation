title: At most two finite simple groups share an order
---

**ESTABLISHED** by literature import `finite-simple-groups-order-coincidence-citation`.

For every integer `n` there are at most two isomorphism types of finite simple
groups of order `n`. The only coincidences are:
- `A_8 ≅ A_3(2)` and `A_2(4)`, of order 20160;
- `B_n(q)` and `C_n(q)`, for `q` odd and `n > 2`.

The statement rests on the classification of finite simple groups.
