Let `A` be a unital simple separable exact stably finite non-elementary
C-star algebra, and assume that the extreme boundary of `T(A)` is nonempty
and finite.  Then for every strictly positive lower-semicontinuous affine
function

```text
f:QT(A) -> (0,infinity]
```

there is `a in (A tensor K)_+` such that

```text
d_tau(a)=f(tau)                 for every tau in QT(A).
```

Exactness is used only to identify quasitraces with traces.  No stable-rank,
strict-comparison, nuclearity, or Jiang--Su-stability hypothesis is used.
