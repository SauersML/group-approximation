**ESTABLISHED** (literature import, `gupta-sidki-3-group-center-out-presentation-citation`).

For the 2-generated infinite Gupta–Sidki 3-group `𝔊𝔖` (the GGS group with defining vector
`(1, -1)` on the ternary tree):
1. `𝔊𝔖` has trivial center;
2. `Aut(𝔊𝔖) = Inn(𝔊𝔖)·(V D)` with `V` an elementary abelian 3-group and `D` elementary abelian of
   order four normalizing `V`; so `Out(𝔊𝔖)` is a quotient of `V ⋊ D` and every finitely generated
   subgroup of `Out(𝔊𝔖)` is finite;
3. `𝔊𝔖` is a just-infinite branch group that is not finitely presented.
