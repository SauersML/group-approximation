**Dead.** If some infranormal Kazhdan pair inside `V` had a nontrivial rigid
defect, `sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects` would make `V`
nonsofic. The premise is refuted, and the route is invalidated, by
`thompson-v-has-no-rigid-compression-defect`: `V` is a-T-menable, so its
Kazhdan subgroups are finite and its rigid defects vanish. A proof that `V`
is nonsofic needs a mechanism without property (T).
