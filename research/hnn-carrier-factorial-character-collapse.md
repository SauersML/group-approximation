Let `n >= 3`, `Gamma = SL_n(Z[1/p])`, `Lambda = SL_n(Z)`,
`H = < Gamma, t | [t, Lambda] = 1 >`.  Claim: every factorial
hyperlinear character `tau` of `H` with `tau|_Gamma = delta_e`
satisfies

```text
tau(t^(-1) gamma^(-1) t gamma) = 1  for all gamma in Gamma,      (FC1)
```

equivalently `pi_tau(t)` is central in the II_1 factor
`N = pi_tau(H)''`.

By `rcc-carrier-character-face-compression` (whose center hypotheses
are trivial for odd `n`) this claim is EQUIVALENT to the collapse
`(RC3)` of `sl3-pair-relative-commutant-route`, hence reaches the goal
through `non-hyperlinear-from-sl3-relative-commutant-collapse`.  The
gain over the operator form is the shape of the enemy: one separable
II_1 factor `N` generated by the irreducible property (T) inclusion
`L(Lambda) subseteq L(Gamma)` together with a single unitary
`k = pi_tau(t)` commuting with `L(Lambda)`.

  pins the enemy character's restriction to every amenable subgroup

  The tower/outlier sector split collapses at depth, so this claim is

- **Tree deformations pass through the enemy (2026-08-21, negative

- **Charmenability of the carrier is FALSE (2026-08-21): the
  coset-Bernoulli character refutes it.**  Let `W = (direct_sum_
  (Gamma/Lambda) C_2) rtimes Gamma` be the coset wreath group and
  `q : H -> W` the surjection `gamma -> gamma`, `t -> x_Lambda` (the
  base lamp; it commutes with `Lambda`, and the lamps are its
  `Gamma`-translates, so `q` is onto).  `W` is icc, so its regular
  character `delta_e^W` is extremal, and the pullback `tau = delta_e^W
  compose q` is an extremal character of `H` with `tau|_Gamma =
  delta_e` whose GNS factor contains `L(Gamma)`, hence is
  NON-amenable.  It does not vanish off the center: for `gamma notin
  Lambda`, the word `(t gamma t gamma^(-1))^2` is nontrivial in `H`
  (Britton: no pinch, since `gamma notin Lambda` separates the
  `t`-letters), while `tau` of it is `tau_W(x_Lambda^2 x_(gamma
  Lambda)^2) = 1`.  So `H` has an explicit extremal character that is
  neither supported on the amenable radical nor amenable-GNS: the
  BBHP-type dichotomy ("charmenability of the relative HNN carrier")
  proposed above as a virgin target cannot hold, independently of
  any hyperlinearity question, and the same construction with other
  bases gives infinitely many such characters.  What survives of
  that bullet is only its negative half: no dichotomy can eliminate
  intermediate enemy shapes, and the collapse `(RC3)` must be
  attacked through the outlier sector and the coset wreath group
  directly (`nonhyperlinear-coset-wreath-from-p3-collapse`).
