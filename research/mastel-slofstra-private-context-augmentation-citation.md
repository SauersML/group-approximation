In arXiv:2404.00926v2, Definition 6.5 defines classical homomorphisms of BCS
algebras.  Lemma 6.6 proves that they are `1`-homomorphisms for the weighted
defect.  Corollary 6.7 applies this in both directions when contexts are
enlarged without changing pairwise intersections and every old satisfying
assignment extends.  The paragraph following the corollary states the
two-way defect preservation.  Theorem 7.5 gives the separate polynomial
bound for subdivision.
