Let `B` be a separable unital simple C\*-algebra in which every nonzero
projection is infinite, and let `H` be a hereditary C\*-subalgebra of `B`
such that `M_2(H)` contains a nonzero projection.  Then `M_2(H)!=0`, so
`H!=0`.  By the required claim `B` has property (SP), so the nonzero
hereditary C\*-subalgebra `H` contains a nonzero projection.

Together with `stw74-separable-two-level-descent-forces-sp` this makes the
new hole equivalent to `stw74-hypothesis-forces-property-sp` (a two-route
cycle; neither route establishes anything on its own).
