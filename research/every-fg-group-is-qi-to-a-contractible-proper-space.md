title: Every finitely generated group is quasi-isometric to a contractible proper metric space
---

Let G be a finitely generated group. There is a contractible proper metric
space (T, d) quasi-isometric to G. So the metric-only reading of Zaremsky
Problem 1.3 has answer no for every n ≥ 2: take any finitely generated group
that is not of type F_n.

The metric d is not a length metric on T. Under the geodesic reading this
construction says nothing
(`zaremsky-1-03-qi-to-connected-space-implies-type-fn`).
