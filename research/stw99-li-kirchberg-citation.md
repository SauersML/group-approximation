**Established by citation.**  Gabe--Szabo, *The dynamical
Kirchberg--Phillips theorem* (Theorem B and its consequences; STW
arXiv:2506.10902 record precisely this): outer actions of countable
discrete amenable groups on stable Kirchberg algebras are classified
by equivariant `KK`, and for a strongly self-absorbing Kirchberg
target the invariant of a strongly outer action of a torsion-free
amenable group is unique, giving cocycle conjugacy of all such
actions; STW state the extension to amenable actions of exact
Haagerup groups.
