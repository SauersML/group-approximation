The trace bound in case 1 is the sum of the `r` coordinate traces in
`(CCF1)`.  For fixed radius, every rooted ball in the `q`-cycle agrees with
the rooted ball in `Z` once `q>2r`; normalized orbit averages therefore have
the same moments as the bilateral-shift tracial model in the limit.  This is
case 2.

Any word which distinguishes the two rooted structures must traverse the
wrap and hence has clock length at least `q`.  Lemma 1.1-style word
telescoping gives the `q Def` bound, while localization at the unique wrap
has trace `c/q`.  These are the two alternatives in case 3.
