distinct_from:
  trace-conditioned-reverse-kleene-collapse: that allows canonical-moment side conditions, which tensor powers need not preserve; this is an unconditional cutoff and obtains a strong finite-dimensional invisibility consequence by tensor amplification.
  fd-invisibility-of-compression: that concerns the fixed compression mark and finite-dimensional representations of one compressor group; this applies to any presented group and mark satisfying one normalized-HS cutoff.
---

Let `Gamma=<S|R>` be finitely presented and `w in Gamma`.  Suppose there are
`delta>0` and `alpha<sqrt(2)` such that every finite-dimensional unitary tuple
with relator defect below `delta` satisfies

```text
||w(U)-I||_2 <= alpha.                                  (RKF1)
```

Then every exact finite-dimensional unitary representation of `Gamma` kills
`w`.  More generally every homomorphism from `Gamma` into a tracial matrix
ultraproduct kills `w`.  Consequently an unconditioned reverse-Kleene
groupifier is impossible if the NONHALT presentation has even one exact
finite-dimensional representation detecting its mark.
