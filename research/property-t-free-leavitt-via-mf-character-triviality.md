The marked root `z=x_13(s_1t_1)` is a nonidentity involution and normally
generates the rank-twenty Steinberg group.  The torsion-normal-generator
criterion therefore identifies full MF radical with triviality of every MF
character.  The first prerequisite supplies exactly that open character
statement, so the criterion gives the desired radical equality.
