Let P be countably infinite and choose a bijection nu:P->N, where
N={0,1,...}. Transfer its left regular action to the even integers,
fixing the odd integers; call the resulting permutation rho_g for
g in P. On C={0,1}^N set p=0^infinity and C_n=0^n*1*C, and define

    hat(g)(p)=p,
    hat(g)(0^n*1*z)=0^(rho_g(n))*1*z.

Then g |-> hat(g) is a faithful homomorphism into Homeo(C), and

    E_nu=<V,hat(P)>

is a finite germ extension of V, with singular set Omega=V*p.
It preserves every V orbit, and Omega is countable and dense. If
P is finitely generated then E_nu is finitely generated.

The isotropy germ group (E_nu)_p identifies with the subgroup of
the near permutation group of N generated by the germs of rho(P)
and the cofinite shift tau:n->n+1. Under this identification (V)_p
is <tau>. Every nonidentity element of P remains nonidentity as
a germ. No finite-state or finite-presentation assertion is made.
