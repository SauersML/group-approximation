Let `R` be a countable nontrivial unital ring containing elements
`s_0,s_1,t_0,t_1` with

```text
t_i s_j=delta_ij,
s_0 t_0+s_1 t_1=1.
```

Let `n>=16`.  If both `E_4(R)` and `E_n(R)` have property `(T)`, then

```text
Rad_MF(E_n(R))=E_n(R).
```

Thus the full-radical conclusion is a theorem of explicit algebraic
self-similarity plus the two Kazhdan hypotheses; it is not special to one
coefficient ring.  The load-bearing ring input is the displayed Leavitt
family, which supplies actual one-sided inverses for the compression and
saturation words.
