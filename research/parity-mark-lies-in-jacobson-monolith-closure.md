**ESTABLISHED** (route `parity-mark-lies-in-jacobson-monolith-closure-proof`).

**Setting.** `H=H(alpha,chi)` is the parity-headed compressor of
`parity-headed-compressor-kills-mark-in-exact-matrix-models`: base `Lambda`, injective `alpha`, nonzero
`chi:Lambda->C_2` with `chi alpha=0`, a central involution `J` of `K=(Lambda x <J>) semidirect <q>`, and
`t g t^-1=alpha(g)`. `H_T` is the Toeplitz instance of `toeplitz-parity-compressor-candidate`:
`Lambda=EL_4(T) x C_2`, `alpha(g,eps)=(beta(g)c^eps,0)`, `c=s I_4`. Put
`L=GL_fin(N x [4],F_2)=ker(EL_4(T)->EL_4(F_2[z,z^-1]))` and `e=(1,1) in Lambda`.

**PML1 (general identity).** For every `p in Lambda` with `chi(p)=1`,

```text
J in <<alpha(p)>>_H.                                          (PML1)
```

**PML2 (Toeplitz instance).** `c=alpha(e)` lies in `L`, and

```text
J in <<x>>_(H_T)    for every 1 != x in L.                    (PML2)
```

**PML3 (faithfulness).** Every homomorphism `pi:H_T->G`, into any group, with `pi(J)!=1` is injective on
`Lambda`. Consequently:

* if `EL_4(T)` is not hyperlinear, every homomorphism `H_T->U(R^omega)` kills `J`, that is,
  `parity-compressor-hs-return` holds;
* a single homomorphism `H_T->U(prod_omega M_(d_n))` with `pi(J)!=1` proves `EL_4(T)` hyperlinear.

So `nonhyperlinear(EL_4(T)) => parity-compressor-hs-return => nonhyperlinear(H_T)`. The compressor carries
information beyond the Jacobson endpoint only on the branch where `EL_4(T)` is hyperlinear. There every
`J`-seeing ultraproduct model is a faithful embedding of `Lambda` that fails to extend over the parity cell.

**PML4 (the mark sector is orthogonal to the invariant mass).** Let `tau` be any character of `H_T`, with GNS
representation `pi_tau` and cyclic vector `xi`. Let `e_inf` be the projection onto the `L`-invariant vectors,
and put `P_-=(1-pi_tau(J))/2`. Then

```text
P_- e_inf = 0,          tau(1-e_inf) >= tau((1-J)/2).         (PML4)
```

The same holds for every group `G` generated by letters comparable on `L`, in the sense of FSS6 of
`finitary-sign-sector-has-no-kazhdan-low-density`, that contains `EL_r(T)`, `r>=3`, and has a central-in-base
involution `J_G in <<L>>_G`.

**PML5 (no Kazhdan low density on the parity sector).** Let `phi_n` be normalized-HS microstates of `H_T`, with
`tr o phi_n -> tau`. Let `Z_n=1_(Re<0)(phi_n(J))`, fix `0<b<kappa`, and let `P_n=1_[0,b](A_n)` be the old
low-commutator projection of FSS1. Then

```text
limsup_n ||R_(Z_n) P_n||_2^2 = limsup_n ||L_(Z_n) P_n||_2^2 = 0.   (PML5)
```

So every sequence of projections `W_n<=P_n` approximately supported, on one side, on the sector `J=-1` has
`tr_ad(W_n)->0`, in every microstate sequence and whatever the mass `tau((1-J)/2)`.

**Impact on the density program.**
* `parity-twist-copies-the-old-commutant-into-the-excess` copies ranges into the compressed corner `J=-1`. By
  PML5, any low-commutator range placed there has density tending to zero.
* So a quantitative placement of the form `(PCD1)` inside the `J=-1` sector can hold only vacuously, when no
  positive-mass microstates exist, which is the HS return itself.
* This closes the escape "packet centre identified with `J_H`" listed in the scope of
  `finitary-sign-sector-has-no-kazhdan-low-density`, for every design keeping PML4's hypotheses.
* The step where every such design dies is the placement step. The invariant is the central `<<L>>`-fixed
  projection `e_inf`: it carries all of FSS1's low-density budget, and the parity sector is orthogonal to it.

**Scope.**
* A design escapes PML2 only if no odd `p` has `alpha(p) in <<L>>`. For the Toeplitz head-tail compressor this
  needs a `c` with nontrivial Laurent symbol. Any `c=s I_4` with `s` in the unitization `F_2+F` of the finitary
  ideal has symbol `I`. This covers every depth and every head swap of `toeplitz-parity-compressor-candidate`.
* Nothing here decides `parity-compressor-hs-return` on the branch where `EL_4(T)` is hyperlinear.
