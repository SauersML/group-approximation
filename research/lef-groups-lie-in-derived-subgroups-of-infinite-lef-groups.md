Let `Γ` be LEF and generated by a finite set `S`. Then there are an infinite LEF group `Δ` generated by `|S|+1`
elements and an injective homomorphism `Γ → Δ` with image in `[Δ,Δ]`, mapping each `s ∈ S` to a single commutator.

**Construction.**
- Take finite models `φ_n : B_n → Q_n` on word balls, `m_n = n|Q_n|`, and `Λ_n = Q_n^(Z/m_n) ⋊ Z/m_n` with
  `(t f t^(-1))(i) = f(i-1)`.
- In `Λ_n` the constant function with value `q` is the commutator `[f,t]` with `f(i) = q^i`.
- In the algebraic ultraproduct `∏_ω Λ_n`, `Γ` sits as the constant functions with values `φ_n(γ)`, each `s` equals
  `[f_s, t]`, and `Δ = ⟨t, f_s : s ∈ S⟩`. It is infinite because `t` has infinite order.

**Application.** The note-level proof of the LEF characterization, route
`lef-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-lef-groups-wreath-proof`. The proposal is in
`research/artifacts/sk-lef-proof-compress-proposal-2026-09-13.md`.

**Firewalls.**
- The double swap `γ ↦ (γ,γ^(-1))` is not used.
- `diag(a,a^(-1),1)` is not used as a homomorphism.
- The copy of `Γ` is the diagonal of constant functions, which is a homomorphism. The witnesses `f_s` are only
  elements, not images of a homomorphism.
