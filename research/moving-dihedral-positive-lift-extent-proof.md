Let `rho` be any unitary representation of `H_N` and put

```text
U=rho(q_N(w)).
```

Since `U^N=1`, its spectrum is contained in the `N`th roots of unity.
Functional calculus applied to (DPL1) gives eigenvalues

```text
c_N+cos(2 pi k/N),        0<=k<N.                      (DPP1)
```

For odd `N`, the minimum of the cosine samples is

```text
-cos(pi/N),
```

attained at the two roots nearest `-1`. Hence the minimum in (DPP1) is

```text
c_N-cos(pi/N)=mu_N>0,                                  (DPP2)
```

which proves (DPL2) in every representation.

On the other hand, `W` has the one-dimensional representation

```text
s |-> 1,       t |-> -1.
```

It sends `w=st` to `-1`, and therefore sends `p_N` to

```text
c_N-1=-mu_N<0.                                         (DPP3)
```

So `p_N` is not positive in the full group C-star algebra of `W`.

## The injectivity-radius lower bound

The Cayley graph of `C2*C2` with generators `s,t` is the bi-infinite
line: reduced words alternate the two involutions. The quotient by
`(st)^N=1` is the dihedral group of order `2N`, whose Cayley graph with
the same generators is the cycle of length `2N`. The quotient map from
the line to the cycle is injective on the open word ball

```text
{g in W: word_length(g)<N}.                            (DPP4)
```

Equivalently, distinct reduced words of length less than `N` remain
distinct in `H_N`.

Suppose an algebraic lift `P_N` had extent less than `N`. Because
`p_N` has extent two and `N>=3`, the difference `P_N-p_N` is supported
in the ball (DPP4). Group elements are a linear basis of a group algebra,
and their images from this ball are still distinct. Thus

```text
q_N(P_N-p_N)=0       implies       P_N-p_N=0.          (DPP5)
```

But (DPP3) says that `p_N` is not positive. Therefore no positive lift
has extent less than `N`, proving (DPL3).

## A finite positive lift exists

The positive square root

```text
r_N=q_N(p_N)^(1/2)
```

lies in the cyclic rotation algebra generated by `q_N(w)`. Because `N`
is odd, Fourier interpolation gives a unique expression

```text
r_N=sum_(k=-(N-1)/2)^((N-1)/2) alpha_k q_N(w)^k.       (DPP6)
```

Lift it termwise to

```text
a_N=sum_k alpha_k u_w^k in C[W].
```

The reduced word `w^k` has length `2|k|`, so

```text
extent(a_N)<=N-1.                                      (DPP7)
```

Now set

```text
P_N=a_N* a_N.
```

This is positive in `C*(W)`, its quotient image is
`r_N* r_N=q_N(p_N)`, and

```text
extent(P_N)<=2 extent(a_N)<=2N-2.                      (DPP8)
```

This proves (DPL4). Finally, the Taylor expansion

```text
1-cos x asymptotic to x^2/2
```

at `x=pi/N` gives (DPL5). The family therefore isolates a linear
relation-ideal lifting cost even though the input extent stays equal to
two and positive lifts exist at every coordinate.
