Let M = S^{n_1} x ... x S^{n_5}, and let u_j in C(M, M_2) be pulled back from
the j-th factor through maps S^{n_j} -> U(2). Put
f = [u_5, [u_4, [u_3, [u_2, u_1]]]]. Let G be any tangential structure under
which the open top cell of M carries its framing structure. Then
[M, f]_red = 0 in Omega-tilde^G_{dim M}(U(2)).

The stable image of the collapsed map is eta^a·y^b·(leaf images) with
a + b = 4. Here y is the stable image of <omega, omega> in pi_3^s. Every
such product is 0. At fourfold commutators (a + b = 3), eta^3 survives, and
the lambda-mod-8 tower detects it
(simple-ah-algebra-with-class-four-component-group). So rank-two trapping is
sharp at class four for these seeds. Class five by trapping needs protected
rank at least three, non-spherical seeds with a non-spin detector, or a
different detector.
