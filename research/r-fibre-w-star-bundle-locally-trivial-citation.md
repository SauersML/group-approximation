**Established by citation.**  S. Evington and U. Pennig, *Locally trivial
W*-bundles*, arXiv:1601.05964v2 (Internat. J. Math. 2016), read from the PDF
text on MSI.

- Definition 2.10: "We say that a W*-bundle M over X is locally trivial if every
  x ∈ X has a closed neighbourhood Y such that M_Y is isomorphic to a trivial
  bundle over Y."
- Theorem 4.10: "A locally trivial W*-bundle with all fibres isomorphic to the
  hyperfinite II_1 factor R is trivial."
- Abstract: "The proof uses the contractibility of the automorphism group Aut(R)
  shown by Popa and Takesaki. There is no restriction on the covering dimension
  of X."
