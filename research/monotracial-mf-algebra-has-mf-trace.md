Let `A` be a separable unital MF C-star algebra with exactly one tracial
state `tau`.  Then `tau` is an MF trace in Shulman's sense.

Equivalently, `A` has operator-norm asymptotic matrix coordinates whose
normalized traces converge pointwise to `tau`.  No nuclearity, exactness,
quasidiagonality, or positivity of the coordinate maps is required.
