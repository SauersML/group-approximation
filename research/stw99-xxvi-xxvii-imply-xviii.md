Let `A` be unital simple separable nuclear non-elementary with strict
comparison; the purely infinite case is Kirchberg's, so assume stably
finite.  Part (1) of Problem XXVII upgrades strict comparison to
pureness; STW Proposition `prop:cu-regular` ((5) ⟹ (2)) turns pureness
into `Cu(A) ≅ Cu(A ⊗ Z)`; Problem XXVI then yields `A ⊗ Z ≅ A`.  The
nonunital case reduces by Brown stabilization/hereditary permanence of
all three properties.  Only part (1) of XXVII is used.
