**ESTABLISHED (written deduction).** Proof in `fpbs-near-optimal-morse-matchings-have-non-tight-heights-proof`.

**Setting.**
- `Gamma` is an infinite group generated by a finite set `S` with `1 ∉ S`.
- `G = Cay(Gamma, S ∪ S^-1)` is the simple Cayley graph. It is `d`-regular with `d = |S ∪ S^-1|`.
- `h` is the unnormalized edge Cheeger constant of `G`, as in `fpbs-quantitative-routing-tail-bound`. It is positive
  iff `Gamma` is nonamenable.
- `a` is any essentially free p.m.p. action, `R` is a finite list of trivial words of maximal length `k >= 2`, and `M`
  is a Morse matching for `<S | R>` in the sense (M1)–(M3) of `fpbs-bernoulli-morse-collapse-criterion`.
- `nu` is edge measure on `X x S`, so `mu(M) = nu(E_M)`.
- For `e` in `E_M`, `ht(e)` is the height of its recovery tree `T(e)`: `1` if `e` has no children, and otherwise
  `1 + max ht(child)`. Write `E_M^{>H}` for the removed edges of height greater than `H`, with `E_M^{>0} = E_M`.

**Theorem.**
1. **(Height to modulus.)** If `ht(e) <= H`, the endpoints of `e` are joined by a path of length at most `(k-1)^H` in
   the retained graphing `Phi' = Phi_S \ E_M`.
2. **(Height tail inequality.)** For every `H >= 0`,

   ```text
   2 nu(E_M^{>H}) >= h - d (|S| - 1 - mu(M)) |B_G((k-1)^H)|.
   ```

3. **(Bounded height.)** If every recovery tree has height at most `H`, then
   `mu(M) <= |S| - 1 - h / (d |B_G((k-1)^H)|)`.

**Reading.** When `beta_1^(2)(Gamma) = 0`, the quantity `eta(M) = |S| - 1 - mu(M)` is the Morse deficit of `M`, the
amount by which `M` falls short of `z(Phi_S)`. This covers infinite Kazhdan groups, although the theorem does not use
that fact. For such `Gamma` nonamenable:

- **Fixed lists.** Let `M_j` be matchings over one finite list `R` with `eta(M_j) -> 0`, which is what (Q1) or (Q2)
  would supply. Then `liminf_j nu(E_{M_j}^{>H}) >= h/2` for every `H`. At least mass `h/2` of removed edges has
  recovery heights that escape to infinity.
- **Full presentations, depth one.** Theorem B realises `lim_L m(P_L)` with depth-one matchings over `P_L`. A
  depth-one matching `M` over `P_L` with `eta(M) < h/d` needs `|B_G(L-1)| >= h / (d eta(M))`, so
  `L >= log_d(h / (d eta(M)))`.
- **Target.** `fpbs-morse-deficit-vanishes-for-full-presentations` holds iff there are matchings over `P_{L_j}` with
  `eta -> 0`. Every such family must have `nu(E^{>H}) >= h/2 - (d/2) eta_j |B_G((L_j-1)^H)|` for all `H`.

**What is killed.**
- Every bounded-height Morse construction, whatever its relators, position rule or action. This includes finite-radius
  block rules whose collapse recursion has bounded depth.
- Every family of near-optimal matchings whose height distribution is tight.
- At each fixed pair `(k, H)`, the bound in item 3 is an explicit rational lower bound on the deficit of every
  certificate, given a rational lower bound on `h`. So a search over finite certificates must let heights grow.

**What survives.** Constructions whose recovery heights have a non-tight tail carrying mass at least `h/2`. The
geometric-stopping matchings in the product calibrations of the collapse-criterion artifact are of this kind, and so
they meet the inequality.

**Calibration.**
- For amenable `Gamma`, `h = 0` and the inequality is empty. This is consistent with `m = 0` for `Z^2`.
- For a free basis `S` of `F_n`, a closed path in the tree `G` crosses every edge an even number of times, so (M1)
  never holds, `M` is empty and `mu(M) = 0`. Here `h = 2n - 2` and `d = 2n`, so the right side is at most
  `2n - 2 - 2n(n-1) <= 0`. The inequality is empty, as it must be when `beta_1 > 0` carries the whole defect.
- For `F_2 x Z`, which has fixed price one, the bound still applies. It is not an obstruction to fixed price. It is an
  obstruction to reaching fixed price with tight heights.
