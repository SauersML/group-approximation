Let `(G,S)` be a colorable hierarchically hyperbolic group, and let
`S_max` be the unique nesting-maximal domain. Assume that for every
proper domain `U` nested in `S_max`, the stabilizer `Stab_G(U)`
satisfies the K- and L-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjectures with respect
to the family of virtually cyclic subgroups. Then `G` satisfies those
K- and L-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjectures.

No torsion-free hypothesis is imposed. This node does not assert the
A-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjecture.
