**OPEN.** Let `X, Y` be infinite minimal subshifts over finite alphabets and `G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ Z)`.
Does `L(G_X) ≅ L(G_Y)` imply `G_X ≅ G_Y`? This is the last question of revision 4.1 of
`simple_kazhdan_sofic_group.tex` (main 37551fd939, section Questions), option O1 of sk-questions-section.

The easy direction holds: `G_X ≅ G_Y` gives `L(G_X) ≅ L(G_Y)`.

## Attempts
- **Separability counting** (`separable-factors-hold-countably-many-subshift-groups`, reviewed PASS). The proof of
  Ozawa's Theorem 2 shows that an isomorphism class of the factors `L(G_X)` meets only countably many `X`.
  Dies at exactness: it gives countable fibres of `X ↦ L(G_X)`, not injectivity up to group isomorphism.
- **Character rigidity** (`subshift-elementary-groups-are-character-rigid`, reviewed PASS twice). `L(G_X)` is the
  only II_1 factor generated by a unitary representation of `G_X`. Dies at identification: it constrains the
  representations of one group, while an isomorphism `L(G_X) ≅ L(G_Y)` need not carry group unitaries to group
  unitaries.
- **Recover the locally constant diagonal inside the factor.** The group-level route needs the image of
  `GL_3(LC(X,F_2))` (open node `subshift-el3-isomorphisms-recognize-diagonals-up-to-normalizer`). That subgroup is
  locally finite, so it generates a hyperfinite subalgebra of `L(G_X)`, and nothing recorded on main characterizes
  this subalgebra intrinsically. Not attempted beyond this observation.
- **Deformation/rigidity (Popa).** Named as the needed tool in `separable-factors-hold-countably-many-subshift-groups`.
  Not attempted.
