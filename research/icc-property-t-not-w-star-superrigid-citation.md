Citation import, not a reproof.  Theorem A and the construction were read from
the LaTeX source of Shuoxing Zhou, *ICC Property (T) Groups without
W\*-Superrigidity*, supplied 2026-08-19; the source carries no arXiv number, so
cite it by author and title until one exists.  The ingestion note
`research/artifacts/connes-rigidity-counterexample-2026-08-19.md` records which
steps were re-derived here (the linearity and cocycle identity of `ell`, the
shear identity `J_q(z) = calR(qz) - calR(z)`, Haar preservation, and the
nonsplitness of `E_ell`) and which were read but not audited (the relative
property (T) estimate and ICC).

The parallel OpenAI Astra family — countably many pairwise nonisomorphic,
mutually commensurable ICC Kazhdan groups with a common group factor,
distinguished by torsion in the abelian kernel — is a second, independent
witness for the same claim, announced 2026-08-01.  This route does not depend
on which of the two came first: the claim is an existence statement and either
source establishes it.

This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what this graph records it
as saying.  It asserts nothing about `Gamma_1` and `Gamma_2` beyond the four
listed properties — in particular nothing about their soficity or
hyperlinearity, which the ingestion note explains is left open on purpose.
