Citation import, with a trust surface worth stating precisely, plus one
elementary half that is not imported.

**Imported.**  The 1993 original (Comm. Algebra 21, 2555--2575) was **not**
read.  The statement was confirmed on 2026-08-17 from the surrounding
literature only: the standard summaries record that Klyachko proved
injectivity of `G -> <G, t | w>` for `G` torsion-free and `w` of exponent sum
one, that the proof rests on his "funny property of the sphere" for cell
subdivisions of `S^2` with at least one 1-cell, and that this yields the
Kervaire conjecture for torsion-free groups; Fenn--Rourke, *Klyachko's
methods and the solution of equations over torsion-free groups*, is the usual
exposition.  Note this is a different paper from the Klyachko reference on
`weak-kervaire-scope-citation` (arXiv:math/0409146), which concerns the weak
conjecture's equivalence with unimodular coefficient injectivity.

**Not imported.**  The scope half of the target — that the instrument
survives at `|m| >= 2` over torsion-free groups — needs no citation: it is
the observation that Klyachko's hypothesis is `deg_t(w) = ±1` together with
`weak-kervaire-transfers-only-at-unimodular-degree`, which records that the
hyperlinear theorem being contradicted is the nonsingular form and that a
counterexample at any nonzero degree suffices.

The surface is therefore one classical, thirty-year-old, universally cited
theorem taken at second hand.  That is weaker provenance than this graph
normally accepts, and it is accepted here because nothing depends on the
claim in the direction that could break: it is used only to close off part of
a falsification route, so an error in it could at worst reopen an instrument,
never validate a conclusion.  Anyone hanging a load-bearing consequence on
this node should read the original first.
