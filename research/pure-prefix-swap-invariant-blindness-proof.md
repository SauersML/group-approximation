Use the balanced involution and the two common-carrier packet completions in
`(PSI2)--(PSI4)`.  They have identical determinant, spectrum, power traces,
and separate packet ranks but distinct Gram defects.  Rank--nullity gives
`(PSI5)` independently of the involution, and determinant multiplicativity
gives `(PSI7)`.  These calculations prove that all listed pure invariants
are blind and that selector-sensitive information first enters through the
mixed moment `(PSI8)`.
