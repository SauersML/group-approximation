**ESTABLISHED.**  In

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,          Q=1-ST,
E=EL_5(J),                  w=x_13(Q),
```

put, for `i>=0`,

```text
X_i=x_12(Q T^i),            Z_i=x_23(S^i Q).            (JCT1)
```

Then

```text
[X_i,Z_j]=w^[i=j],
[X_i,X_j]=[Z_i,Z_j]=1,                                (JCT2)
```

and `w` is a central involution in the subgroup generated by these words.
For every `N`, the finite packet

```text
C_N=<w,X_0,Z_0,...,X_(N-1),Z_(N-1)>                    (JCT3)
```

is the extraspecial two-group with `N` Pauli pairs.  In every unitary
representation, its nonzero `w=-1` sector is a multiple of the unique
`2^N`-dimensional Clifford representation.  Hence the rank of that sector is
divisible by `2^N`.

All the coefficients in `(JCT1)` are finitary matrix units.  Thus

```text
C_infinity=union_N C_N
  <= L=ker(EL_5(J)->EL_5(F_2[z,z^(-1)]))
  <= K,                                                    (JCT4)
```

where `K` is the amenable monomial-symbol preimage of
`binary-jacobson-monomial-symbol-preimage-is-marked-mf`.  Its faithful
operator-MF model retains `w`.  Consequently the entire native Clifford
tower, together with every balanced Laurent translation, Weyl permutation,
and monomial-symbol transport in `K`, is compatible with a surviving head in
a norm matrix corona.

This gives both a resource and a sharp fence for the Property-`(T)`-free
selector program:

* target packet fullness of any prescribed finite degree is already
  word-visible on the head sector;
* increasing Clifford depth, even without a fixed bound, does not collapse
  the head, because the CAR-like direct limit has a marked MF realization;
* the first load-bearing common return must use the raw constant root outside
  `K` (equivalently, the nonmonomial elementary symbol seam), not merely a
  deeper packet or another monomial transport.

In particular the divisibility of the head rank by every **fixed** `2^N`
does not imply its coordinatewise vanishing: a norm-corona model may let the
matrix size and the exactified packet depth grow together.
