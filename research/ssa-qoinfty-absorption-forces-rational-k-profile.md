**ESTABLISHED.** Put `B=Q tensor O_infinity`. Let `D` be strongly
self-absorbing. If `B` is `D`-stable -- in particular, if the equivalent
embeddability conclusion in STW Problem XIV(2) holds for `D` -- then

```text
K_0(D) tensor Q  ~=  Q,       [1_D] tensor 1 |-> 1,
K_1(D) tensor Q  =   0.                                      (RKP)
```

Thus a strongly self-absorbing `D` not isomorphic to `O_2` with any other
rational K-profile would be an immediate counterexample to Problem XIV(2).
Condition `(RKP)` is necessary only; it supplies no embedding.

Here `[1_(O_infinity)]=1` in `K_0(O_infinity)=Z`, so
`[1_(Q tensor O_infinity)]=1` in `Q`; a zero unit class would contradict the
first-factor K-isomorphism for the strongly self-absorbing `O_infinity`.
