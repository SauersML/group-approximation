Let H<=E<=Gamma with [E:H] infinite. If H has finite bi-index
in Gamma, then for every gamma in Gamma the group

    K_gamma=E intersect gamma^-1 H gamma

has finitely many orbits on E/H. In particular K_gamma is infinite,
and E intersect gamma^-1 E gamma is infinite for every gamma.

Consequently no proper almost malnormal embedding of E can supply
such a host. Applied to a shell stabilizer, a proper malnormal
Higman embedding necessarily gives infinitely many pair orbits
in the induced Gamma/H action.

This excludes that particular induced action and retained stabilizer.
It excludes neither other actions of Gamma nor other embeddings
or enlarged stabilizers.
