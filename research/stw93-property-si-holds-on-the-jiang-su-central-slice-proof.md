The trace and tracial `2`-norm of `1⊗z` are those of `z`, so the hypotheses
for `ι(e),ι(f)` are exactly the property-(SI) hypotheses for `e,f` in
`F(𝒵)`.  Matui--Sato property (SI) for the nuclear algebra `𝒵` supplies
`t∈F(𝒵)` with `t*t=e` and `ft=t`.  Taking `s=ι(t)` proves the
claim.
