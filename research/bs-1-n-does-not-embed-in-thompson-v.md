For `n ≥ 2`, `V` has no nontrivial element with `n^j`-th roots for all `j`. So `Z[1/n]`,
and hence `BS(1,n) ⊃ Z[1/n]`, is isomorphic to no subgroup of Thompson's group `V`.
Neither is a witness for `biorderable-subgroup-of-v-without-free-subgroups-not-in-f`.
