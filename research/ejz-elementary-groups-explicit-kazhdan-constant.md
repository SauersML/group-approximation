**Import.** Let `R` be an associative ring with `1`, generated as a ring by
`x_0 = 1, x_1, ..., x_d`, and let `n >= 3`. Put

```text
Σ = { e_ij(x_m) : i ≠ j in {1,...,n}, 0 <= m <= d }.
```

Then

```text
κ(EL_n(R), Σ) >= 1 / ( 8 (12√(2d) + 2√(3n) + 36√2) ).
```

Here `κ(G,S)` is the Kazhdan constant: the infimum, over unitary representations
without nonzero invariant vectors and nonzero vectors `v`, of `max_{s∈S} ||sv − v|| / ||v||`.

The bound depends only on `n` and `d`, not on `R`. So it holds uniformly for every ring
generated by `d` elements besides `1` (every quotient of `Z<x_1,...,x_d>`) and for its
induced generating set. For `n = 3`, `d = 4` it is larger than `1/727`.
