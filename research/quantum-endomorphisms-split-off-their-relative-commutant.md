**ESTABLISHED** (route `quantum-endomorphisms-split-off-their-relative-commutant-proof`).

**Setting.** Let `G` be a group and `d >= 2`.
- `A = (x)_G M_d` is the quasi-local C*-algebra, with local algebras `A_F` for finite `F` and shift `alpha_g(A_h) = A_(gh)`.
- A *quantum automaton* is a unital *-endomorphism `Phi` of `A` with `alpha_g Phi = Phi alpha_g` for all `g`, and
  `Phi(A_e) <= A_N` for a finite `N` containing `e`. Then `Phi(A_h) <= A_(hN)`, and `Phi` is injective because `A`
  is simple.

**Statement.** Put `D = Phi(A)' n A`.
1. **Splitting.** For finite `L`, `Phi(A_(G \ L))' n A = Phi(A_L) (x) D`. Globally, multiplication gives
   `A = Phi(A) (x) D`. So `D` is simple, unital, `alpha`-invariant, has a unique trace, and satisfies `A (x) D = A`.
2. **Local defect.** `A_e <= Phi(A_(N^-1)) (x) (D n A_(N^-1 N))`. The *defect algebra* `F` is the unital
   *-algebra generated by the slice coefficients of `A_e` in this decomposition. It is a finite-dimensional
   C*-subalgebra of `D n A_(N^-1 N)` and does not depend on the choice of matrix units.
3. **Criterion.** The following are equivalent: `Phi` is onto; `D = C`; `F = C`.
4. **Index dichotomy.** `Phi` extends to a normal endomorphism of the hyperfinite II_1 factor `R`, with
   `R = Phi(R) (x) Q` and `Q = Phi(R)' n R` a factor. If `G` is infinite, `Q` is either `C` or infinite dimensional.
   So `[R : Phi(R)]` is `1` or `infinity`, and `Phi_* = id` on `K_0(A) = Z[1/d]`.

**Consequence (obstruction).** Every approach to the quantum conjecture through a numerical index or K-theoretic
invariant of `Phi` dies at the same step, the claim that a strict `Phi` has a finite invariant greater than 1.
- The invariants here are the relative commutant `D` and its von Neumann closure `Q`.
- For a strict `Phi` on an infinite group, `D` is an infinite-dimensional simple monotracial algebra with
  `A (x) D = A`, the Jones index is infinite, and the trace and `K_0` classes do not move.
- This kills the "index quantization" item of playbook lane (a). The surviving invariant is the *local* defect
  `F`, a nonzero finite-dimensional algebra whose translates are packed into `D`. That is what
  `sofic-groups-are-quantum-surjunctive` counts.
