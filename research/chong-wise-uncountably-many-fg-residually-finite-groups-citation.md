H. K. Chong and D. T. Wise, *An Uncountable Family of Finitely Generated Residually Finite Groups*,
arXiv:2207.00410; J. Group Theory, doi:10.1515/jgth-2021-0094. The DOI is from the publisher landing page; volume and
pages were not checked. Abstract, quoted verbatim from the arXiv TeX source (extracted on MSI, 2026-09-13):

> We study a family of finitely generated residually finite groups. These groups are doubles $F_2*_H F_2$ of a
> rank-$2$ free group $F_2$ along an infinitely generated subgroup $H$. Varying $H$ yields uncountably many groups up
> to isomorphism.
