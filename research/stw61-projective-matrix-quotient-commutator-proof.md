Lift the coefficient projection to actual matrix projections and
block-sum each coordinate with a sufficiently late coordinate
detecting its missing side. Retaining the original coordinate makes
the induced quotient map isometric. Constant functions embed this
matrix quotient into the corresponding product of projective-space
matrix-function algebras modulo norm-null sequences.

Send z to the tautological unitaries i(2e_j-1). Each individual
quotient unitary Psi(W) has genuine continuous coordinate unitary
lifts after polar correction. The preceding finite projective
argument gives commutator norm exactly one at every coordinate.
The quotient norm is their limsup, proving the desired lower bound;
the universal unitary-projection commutator bound gives equality.
