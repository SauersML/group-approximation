The target contains the proof.  Frobenius induction sends an extension to
an invariant rank-`d` projection whose three diagonal compressions are
`1/3`.  Conversely `sqrt(3)` times identity-coset evaluation is a unitary
`B`-intertwiner on every invariant rank-`d` projection with that flat
compression.  With only approximate edge agreement, the three Schreier
cocycle errors give the fixed-constant commutator estimate `(IQR9)`.
Compression and the two-projection polar lemma are inverse up to the usual
rank-paid flexible convention, proving the quantitative equivalence.
