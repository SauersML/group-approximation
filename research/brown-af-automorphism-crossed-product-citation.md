This is the AF-coefficient case proved by Nathanial P. Brown,
*AF Embeddability of Crossed Products of AF Algebras by the Integers*,
Journal of Functional Analysis 160 (1998), 150--175,
DOI `10.1006/jfan.1998.3339`.  Brown characterizes AF-embeddability by the
ordered-`K_0` incompressibility condition and proves its equivalence with
stable finiteness.  AF-embeddability implies quasidiagonality, while a unital
quasidiagonal algebra is stably finite, giving the displayed three-way
formulation.  This is also the Brown theorem recorded immediately after
Problem XI and in the statement of Problem XII in the supplied STW source.
