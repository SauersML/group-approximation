Complete proof in the artifact, §3 (Theorem A) and §4 (Corollary B, Bernoulli case).

Weak containment is used in the Kechris form printed in Abért–Weiss, arXiv:1103.1063v2, §1: "we say that f
weakly contains g (f⪰g) if for all Borel subsets Y₁,…,Yₙ∈𝒞, finite sets S⊆Γ and ε>0 there exist Borel subsets
X₁,…,Xₙ∈ℬ such that |μ(fᵧXᵢ∩Xⱼ)−ν(gᵧYᵢ∩Yⱼ)|<ε (1≤i,j≤n,γ∈S)." This is a definition, not an imported theorem.

1. **(1 ⇒ 2).** Code each `Φ_n` equivariantly into the compact space `Y = [0,1]^Γ × {0,1}^{Γ×Γ}` by the base
   coordinate along the orbit and the label indicators `ω(γ,g) = 1[γx ∈ A^n_g]`. Take a weak* limit `ν`.
   - The Bernoulli marginal is fixed, so `a' = (Y,ν)` is free.
   - Portmanteau on an algebra of continuity cylinders gives `a' ≺ b`.
   - The clopen sets `D_g = {ω(e,g)=1}` form a symmetric graphing `Ψ`. Fatou gives `c(Ψ) ≤ C(b)`.
   - Under (T), bounded-length route events with labels in a finite set are clopen, so they pass to the limit
     with measure at least `1−η`. Hence `Ψ` generates `R_{a'}`.
   - Abért–Weiss gives `b ≺ a'`. Kechris monotonicity in both directions gives `C(a') = C(b)`, so `Ψ` attains the
     cost.
2. **(2 ⇒ 3).** Proposition 2.23 makes `Ψ` a treeing. Together with `b ≺ a'` from Abért–Weiss, `a'` is a free
   treeable action weakly equivalent to `b`.
3. **(3 ⇒ fixed price and 2).** Kechris gives `C(a') = C(b)`. Theorem 2.24 gives `c(Ψ) = C(a')`. Corollary 2.26 gives
   `C(a') = C_*(Γ)`. Bernoulli maximality gives `C(b) = C^*(Γ)`.
4. **(2 ⇒ 1).** Transfer `Ψ` into `b` with the weak-containment definition.
   - Use the atoms of the finite partition generated by translates of `D_g`, over growing radii `M_n` and
     label sets `F_n`.
   - The transferred label sets are `B_g ∩ g^{-1}B_{g^{-1}}`. Add `s`-edges on the set where no short route exists.
   - The error bookkeeping in the artifact shows the resulting graphings generate `R_b`, have cost at most
     `C(b) + (1+2|S|)η_n`, and satisfy (T).
5. **Corollary B.** If `Γ` is non-treeable, (3) fails, so (1) fails. Adams–Spatzier gives this for infinite
   Kazhdan groups. The second sentence of Corollary 2.26 gives it for nonamenable groups with a cost-one free
   action.
