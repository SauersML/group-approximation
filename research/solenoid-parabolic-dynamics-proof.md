The upper dyadic unipotents form the additive group `Z[1/2]`, and
conjugation by `diag(2,1/2)` is multiplication by four.  Pontryagin duality
therefore identifies the abelian spectral parameter with the two-adic
solenoid and the stable-letter action with its times-four automorphism.
Finite orbits are exactly torsion characters of odd order.  The expanding
automorphism also has many diffuse invariant measures, while periodic orbit
measures are weak-star dense; in particular the established odd-root
Wasserstein vacuity shows that scalar atomic approximation supplies no
operator repair.  Thus any far-sector rigidity must use the additional
noncommuting `SL_2` symmetry, exactly as claimed.
