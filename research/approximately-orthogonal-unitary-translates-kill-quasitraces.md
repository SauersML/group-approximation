**ESTABLISHED (derivation `approximately-orthogonal-translates-quasitrace-proof`).**

**Statement.** Let `A` be unital, `σ` a bounded 2-quasitrace on `A`, and
`a ∈ A_+` with `‖a‖ ≤ 1`. Suppose `u_1, …, u_m ∈ A` are unitaries with
`‖u_i a u_i* · u_j a u_j*‖ ≤ δ` for `i ≠ j`. Then

    σ(a) ≤ σ(1)/m + (m + 1) δ^{1/4} σ(1).

**Consequences.**
- If such families exist for every `m` and `δ`, then `a` is null for every bounded
  2-quasitrace, and also for every trace.
- The quasitrace-null positive elements are the positive part of the closed ideal
  `∩_σ N_σ`. So a closed ideal generated by such displaceable elements is invisible
  to bounded quasitraces.

**Use.** For Ozawa's `SL(3,Z)` block corona, every quasitrace on the block algebra
is a trace iff no quasitrace charges the trace kernel `K`. This criterion settles
that whenever `K` is generated by elements with approximately orthogonal
`Γ`-translates.

Proof: artifact Proposition 4.3. The only inputs are the 2-quasitrace axioms, and
the continuity estimate `|σ(x) − σ(y)| ≤ ‖x − y‖ σ(1)` in unital algebras.
