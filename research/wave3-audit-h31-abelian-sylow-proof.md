The subgroup generated by `b,c` in `H_31` is the specified vertex
group of order `625`. Its image in a finite target with abelian
Sylow `5` subgroups is therefore abelian. Thus `[b,c]=1` in the
image; `[a,c]=1` is already a defining relator.

Adding `[b,c]=1` to the presentation leaves precisely the presentation
of `Q` on `a,b`, the order-five relation for `c`, and commutation
of `c` with both generators of `Q`. The resulting quotient is
`Q x C_5`, with no additional mixed relations.

Since `Q` is nonabelian simple, perfect, and centerless, the only
normal subgroups of `Q x C_5` are `1`, `Q`, `C_5`, and the whole
group. For example a normal subgroup with full projection to `Q`
contains `Q x 1` by commutation with `Q x 1`; one with trivial
projection lies in `1 x C_5`. Thus there are exactly the four
displayed quotients. Each has abelian Sylow `5`, and each is indeed
a quotient of `H_31`.

For `PSL_2(q)`, in characteristic five a Sylow five subgroup is the
additive unipotent group of the field. In other characteristics it
is cyclic: the five part of the group order lies wholly in one of
`q-1` and `q+1`, and is realized by a split or nonsplit cyclic torus.
This proves the last assertion without a subgroup-classification
theorem. All four possible images have order prime to seven.
