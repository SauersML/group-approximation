**ESTABLISHED BY PRIMARY SOURCES.**  Let

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>.
```

Then

```text
bsr(J)=2,                     K_2(J)=0,                 (JSI1)
K_2(5,J)=ker(St_5(J)->E_5(J))=0.                        (JSI2)
```

Consequently the canonical Steinberg map is an isomorphism

```text
St_5(J) ~= E_5(J)=EL_5(J).                              (JSI3)
```

Thus the Jacobson head-root endpoint is exactly the operator-MF
classification of one explicit elementary group; it is not strengthened by
passing to a nontrivial Steinberg cover.
