Let `G` be surjunctive. The second prerequisite makes `G x K` surjunctive for every sofic `K`, and the first then
makes `G` quantitatively surjunctive.

Conversely the target gives both prerequisites.
- **The second.** This is `sofic-factor-products-via-quantitative-surjunctivity` (Theorem Q).
- **The first.** `K = 1` makes its hypothesis imply surjunctivity.

So the target is equivalent to the two prerequisites together. Proposition 4.1 of the artifact.
