Both of the following closure statements fail:

1. Hyperlinearity of the two factors implies hyperlinearity of their
   semidirect product. A counterexample has a locally finite abelian
   residually finite normal subgroup and a residually finite quotient.
2. Hyperlinearity of two groups and their amalgamated subgroup implies
   hyperlinearity of their amalgamated free product. A counterexample is
   a symmetric double with both vertices and the edge residually finite.

The witnesses are respectively the binary Kun--Thom wreath W_(2,3,3)
and the double D_6. No assertion of amenability of the amalgamated subgroup
is made. Amenable-quotient extension theorems are not contradicted.

These are elementary permanence consequences of Jihao Liu's analytic
input and Andreas Thom's conditional construction for the Kun--Thom
pairs, not separate independent proofs of nonhyperlinearity.
