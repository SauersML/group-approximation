**ESTABLISHED FIREWALL.**  Let `P` be the two-qubit extraspecial Pauli
group with nontrivial central involution `J`, and put

```text
q=(1-J)/2,
e=q(1+Z_1)/2,
f=q(1+Z_1)(1+Z_2)/4.                                  (RFR1)
```

In the canonical trace of any group containing `P` injectively,

```text
tau(e)=1/4,                    tau(f)=1/8.              (RFR2)
```

Consequently no compression of a group word, fixed group-algebra corner
unitary, or tracially functorial finite-packet construction can satisfy

```text
U e U^*=f                                               (RFR3)
```

while the marked Pauli packet remains embedded.  Here `U` is a unitary in the
marked corner `qL(Gamma)q`, whose unit is `q`.  More generally, every
functorial estimate

```text
||UeU^*-f||_2 <= omega(def),       omega(0)=0           (RFR4)
```

fails at zero defect in the left regular representation.

This applies directly to the proposed finite-router repair of the first
Leavitt coarse-to-fine cell.  A selector-correlated direct-power router and
a balanced truth word can synthesize the nonlinear fine reflection

```text
R_f=q-2f                                                (RFR5)
```

on the selected marked finite type without an external multiplicity action.
The coarse reflection `R_e=q-2e` is already a Pauli word.  If the router word
could then be coupled functorially to the existing prefix partial swap
`J_x` so that

```text
R_f J_x R_e J_x^*=q                                    (RFR6)
```

on that same marked carrier, with `J_xq=qJ_x`, `(RFR6)` would be equivalent
to `(RFR3)`.
The canonical trace would give `1/4=1/8`, a contradiction.  Thus the local
finite truth-table compiler does not close the global active-module
exposure gate: its desired weld to the Leavitt actuator must be a
finite-matrix-only selection statement, not another exact covariance or
balanced-word identity.

The same proof is robust in the only direction needed for a firewall.  For
projections,

```text
||UeU^*-f||_2^2 >= |tau(e)-tau(f)|=1/8.                (RFR7)
```

Hence no dimension-independent modulus derived by operations valid in all
finite tracial representations can make the proposed returned selector
defect vanish.  Any successful recurrence must expose finite multiplicity
coordinates unavailable in the regular representation, or charge the
failure of `(RFR6)` as boundary mass.
