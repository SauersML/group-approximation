**ESTABLISHED.**  Let

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,       Gamma_T=St_5(J)~=EL_5(J),
Q=1-ST,                  w_T=x_13(Q).
```

Interpret `(JPI1)` over one fixed finite presentation of `Gamma_T`, with the
active `EL_3(J)` cycle retained as a named subgroup package and with the four
rank-five target words evaluated in that same presentation.  With the
quantifiers currently written in
`binary-jacobson-raw-cycle-selects-common-pi-intertwiner`, the existence of
the typed deterministic selector and a modulus `omega(epsilon)->0` is
equivalent to

```text
every norm-matrix-corona homomorphism Gamma_T -> U(Q)
sends w_T to I.                                           (JPTE1)
```

The forward implication is the valid sparse standard-polynomial
contradiction recorded in `binary-jacobson-opnorm-pi-selector-proof`.  For
the reverse implication, `(JPTE1)` first gives a uniform presentation gap:
below some `gamma>0`, no finite-dimensional presentation tuple has a
nonzero rounded `w_T=-1` cut.  Consequently there is no sequence to which
the selector's small-defect, nonzero-cut obligation applies.  Any fixed
syntactically permitted raw-cycle compression/polar operation therefore
satisfies the asserted sequential semantics vacuously.

Occurrence typing does not prevent this collapse.  It restricts the
conclusion demanded on a qualifying sequence, but `(JPTE1)` makes the class
of qualifying sequences empty.  Thus the current selector is not an
independent intermediate theorem, even after its ambient rank is repaired.
A noncircular replacement must prescribe an actual mixed `S/T` occurrence
formula and a numerical defect estimate on a separately certified
nonvacuous partial-presentation family.

The rank-five qualification is essential.  The earlier rank-three wording
was not a presentation of the needed interface: `x_14(Q)` and `x_43(1)` do
not belong to the embedded upper-left `EL_3(J)`.  The implication from a
rank-three selector to `(JPTE1)` would still be useful, but the converse
cannot be obtained by restricting `(JPTE1)`, because arbitrary approximate
representations of a subgroup need not extend to `Gamma_T`.
