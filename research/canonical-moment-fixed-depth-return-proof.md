Expand `P^*P-A` as `(CMF1)`.  Canonical trace is one on identity words and
zero on nonidentity words.  Bound the former by finite van Kampen
telescoping, the latter by the finite canonical separation window, and sum
the coefficient masses as in `(CMF4)--(CMF6)`.  The orthonormal-basis
identity `(CMF7)` is the same expansion applied to `(w-a)^*(w-a)`.
