This route imports the results; it does not reprove them. Source: the PDF of arXiv:1609.05086v1
(U. Haagerup and K. K. Olesen), read on 2026-09-13.

- Theorem 2.2 (Thurston): "There exists a homeomorphism φ of R ∪ {∞} onto R/Z = S¹ such that the
  map Φ: PPSL(2,Z) → T given by g ↦ φ ∘ g ∘ φ^{-1} is an isomorphism." The version used is
  Imbert's (their reference [12, Theorem 1.1]). Definition 2.1 defines PPSL(2,Z).
- Proposition 2.4: `Λ = Φ(PSL(2,Z)) ≅ Z_2 * Z_3`, freely generated by `D² = CA = Φ(a)` and
  `C = Φ(b)`.
- Proposition 2.5: "An element f ∈ PSL(2,Z) is uniquely determined by its value on two distinct
  points in Q ∪ {∞}." Remark 2.6: φ restricts to a bijection from `Q ∪ {∞}` to
  `[0,1) ∩ Z[1/2]`.
- Proof of Theorem 3.4: "it suffices to prove that {g ∈ Λ : gf = fg} is amenable, for all
  f ∈ V ∖ {e}".
  - For `f ∈ Λ ∖ {e}` the centralizer is cyclic (their reference [17], Theorems 2.3.3 and 2.3.5).
  - For `f ∈ T ∖ Λ`, the centralizing elements permute the at least two breakpoints of
    `φ^{-1} f φ`, so they form a finite group.
  - For `f ∈ V ∖ T`, they permute the at least two dyadic discontinuity points of f, so again they
    form a finite group.
