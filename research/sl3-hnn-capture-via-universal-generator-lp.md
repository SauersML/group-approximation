**INVALIDATED PROPOSAL.**  If the canonical finite-dimensional generator
operator system `E_S` had the lifting property, then the ucp map induced by
the HNN lattice embedding into the tracial matrix quotient would have ucp
coordinate lifts on `E_S`; their generator values would give `(HMR1)`.

But `sl3-generator-system-has-no-universal-lp` proves that `E_S` cannot have
LP: because it contains enough generating unitaries, LP would force LLP of
`C^*(SL_3(Z))`, contrary to Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma.  The selected HNN claim
therefore cannot be proved by a quotient-independent operator-system
lifting theorem.  It must use both the tracial matrix quotient and the fact
that the map extends to the fixed arithmetic HNN presentation.
