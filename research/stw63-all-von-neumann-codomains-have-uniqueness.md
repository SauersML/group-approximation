title: Every von Neumann codomain has unique Jiang--Su embeddings

Let `M` be an arbitrary von Neumann algebra.  Then every two unital
embeddings

```text
phi,psi:Z->M
```

are approximately unitarily equivalent in norm.  There is no restriction on
the type, center, cardinality, predual, or Hilbert space on which `M` acts.

The conclusion is approximate rather than asymptotic: assembling arbitrary
products gives uniform implementing unitaries for each finite set and
tolerance, but does not by itself give coordinate paths with uniform
convergence in the product norm.
