Use the circulant form `(DPC2)`.  The three displayed radical vectors are
independent and the leading six-by-six minor has determinant one, so the
quotient form is nondegenerate of dimension six and its Heisenberg group has
`J!=1`.  Identity `(DPC7)` makes the child substitution preserve the form;
its kernel before quotient is the all-ones vector, which lies in the radical,
so it is invertible afterward.  The shift and substitution obey `(DPC8)`.
Their finite automorphism semidirect product satisfies every checksum word,
while `f(2)=1` gives the surviving commutator `(DPC9)`.
