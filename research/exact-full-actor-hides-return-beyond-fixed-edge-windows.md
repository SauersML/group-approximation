Fix the Kun--Thom pair `Gamma<G`, a strict compressor
`t Gamma t^(-1)<Gamma`, and

```text
h=t^(-1) gamma t notin Gamma.                           (FEW1)
```

For every finite `F subset Gamma` and finite
`S subset G\{1}` there is an exact finite-dimensional representation

```text
rho:G->U(ell^2(Q))                                     (FEW2)
```

such that

```text
tr(rho(s))=0                         for s in S,
<rho(h),rho(f)>_2=0                  for f in F,        (FEW3)
rho(h) in C*(rho(Gamma)).                              (FEW4)
```

Thus the full actor relations, the strict compressor, and the literal word
`h=t^(-1)gamma t` all hold exactly, while the return is orthogonal to every
edge word named by the fixed packet.  Its absorption into the edge algebra
is witnessed by a quotient-dependent element

```text
gamma_(F,S) in Gamma\F,
rho(h)=rho(gamma_(F,S)).                                (FEW5)
```

Exhausting `G\{1}` and `Gamma` gives exact full-actor representations with
canonical limiting character for which the return remains orthogonal to the
entire edge window at stage `n`, although it belongs to the complete edge
algebra at every stage.

Consequently no fixed mixed actor/return packet which detects edge absorption
only through finitely many edge-word coefficients can distinguish the exact
full actor from a vanished relative return.  A positive Certificate C packet
must authenticate the **complete moving edge algebra** by a projection,
commutant, or positive-density lamp datum; bounded word-span tests cannot do
so, even after imposing every actor relation exactly.
