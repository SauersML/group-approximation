Below twice the rank, the Stiefel stable-range lemma in the monotracial
prerequisite makes pi_0 G(E) -> K^1(X) an isomorphism.

At exactly twice the rank, the gauge/section sequence of the circle-sphere
prerequisite runs for any finite complex. All unit sections are homotopic.
The quotient K^1(X) is abelian. The kernel is the image of H^(2r)(X;Z)
under the boundary map, and it is central, because a gauge unitary fixing
the unit section preserves relative Euler numbers. So every triple
commutator vanishes.

Products of groups of class at most two have class at most two. The limit
component group is the direct limit of the stage groups (polar approximation
and finite-stage path detection), and such direct limits keep class at most
two.
