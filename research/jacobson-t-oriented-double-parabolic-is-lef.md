**ESTABLISHED.** Let `F` be a finite field, `J = F<S,T | TS=1>`, `Q = 1 - ST`.
Write `F[S]` and `F[T]` for the (commutative) subrings of `J` generated by `S`
and by `T`. Elements of `GL_(k+l)(J)` are written in `k + l` block form, and

```text
(g, X, B) = [[g, X], [0, B]],     (g, X, B)(g', X', B') = (g g', g X' + X B', B B').
```

## Statement

**(A) T-oriented double parabolic.** For `l >= 1` the group

```text
P^T_l = { (g, X, B) : g in EL_2(J), X in M_(2 x l)(J), B in EL_l(F[T]) }  <=  EL_(2+l)(J)
```

is LEF, hence sofic, hyperlinear and MF with `Rad_MF = 1`. The head
`x_13(Q) = (1, Q E_11, 1)` has nontrivial image in every model below. For
`l = 2` this is `M_2(J) x| (EL_2(J) x EL_2(F[T]))` inside the double
parabolic `P_(2,2)(J)` of `EL_4(J)`.

**(B) Both Levis polynomial.** For `k, l >= 1` the group

```text
P^(S,T)_(k,l) = { (g, X, B) : g in EL_k(F[S]), X in M_(k x l)(J), B in EL_l(F[T]) }  <=  EL_(k+l)(J)
```

is LEF, and its head `x_(1,k+1)(Q)` has nontrivial image in every model below.

**(C) Mirror.** The automorphism `alpha(x) = w ((x^tau)^t)^(-1) w` of
`GL_4(J)`, with `tau` the anti-automorphism `S <-> T` of `J` and `w` the block
swap, sends `x_ij(a)` to `x_(pi j, pi i)(a^tau)` in characteristic 2, where
`pi = (13)(24)`. It fixes the head, because `Q^tau = Q`. It maps
`M_2(J) x| (EL_2(F[S]) x EL_2(J))` onto `P^T_2`. So this mirror group is LEF
and keeps the head too.

## Consequences for the head collapse

**(D) The trap needs x_43(S), and only that and x_34(1) on the right.**
- Step 5 of `jacobson-double-parabolic-two-step-trap-proof` goes through with
  the trap space `Z = F[M(S)] v` in place of `𝓜 v`.
  - `Z` is `Lambda(S)`-invariant by Step 4 and (R1) for `S`.
  - `Lambda(T) Lambda(S) = 1` on `Z` by (R2).
  - So `Lambda(S)|_Z` is bijective, and `Lambda(S) Lambda(T) v = v`.
- So the right generators `x_34(1), x_43(S)` suffice. The left needs
  `x_21(1), x_12(S), x_12(T)`.
- In particular every two-step model of the spark group
  `H_mix = M_2(J) x| (EL_2(J) x <x_34(1), x_43(S)>)` kills the head.
- The models of (A) are two-step for the Levi-stable flag `W_M ⊕ 0`, and they
  keep the head. So no trap exists with right coefficients in `F[T]`.
- The linear algebra says the same thing. Replace the S-side hypothesis
  `Lambda(S) v = M(S) v` by the T-side one `Lambda(T) v = M(T) v` with `M(T)`
  commuting. Then (R2) no longer forces (R3). Counterexample on `F^2`:
  `v = e_1`, `Lambda(S) e_1 = e_2`, `Lambda(T) e_2 = e_1`,
  `Lambda(T) e_1 = 0`, `M(T) = 0`.
- By (C), the mirror trap needs the left generators `x_21(1), x_12(T)` and the
  right generators `x_34(1), x_43(T), x_43(S)`.

**(E) Class kill.** Consider any proof that every norm-corona, MF, sofic or
LEF-type representation kills `x_13(Q)`, which uses only relations holding
inside `P^T_l`, `P^(S,T)_(k,l)` or the mirror group, together with asymptotic
multiplicativity there.
- **Where it dies.** At the step concluding `pi(x_13(Q)) = 1`. The models
  `Psi` of the route are exact on balls and send the head to a nonidentity
  matrix. Composing with regular representations gives MF and corona
  representations in which the head is at distance `sqrt 2` from `1`.
- **Invariant.** The orientation of the right-Levi coefficient ring relative
  to the evaluation window `span(e_0, ..., e_(d-1))` of `V_+`.
  - `T` preserves the window and acts on it by the nilpotent shift `N_d`.
    So right multiplication by `F[T]` is exactly `ev_d`-equivariant.
  - `S` moves the window by one. This is the only obstruction, and it is
    where the control computation fails.
- **What the class includes.** Transport through Kazhdan subgroups of the
  Levis: `SL_l(F[T]) = EL_l(F[T])` for `l >= 3` (`F[T]` is Euclidean), and
  `EL_k(F[S])` for `k >= 3`. Also every argument using both three-index
  commutator families across the two Levis, as long as all right roots have
  coefficients in `F[T]`.
- **Remark (imported, not load-bearing).** Ershov and Jaikin-Zapirain,
  *Property (T) for noncommutative universal lattices*, Invent. Math. 179
  (2010), main theorem, paraphrased and not checked verbatim here:
  `EL_n(R)` has property (T) for `n >= 3` and every finitely generated
  associative ring `R`. So a Kazhdan
  subgroup, even a Kazhdan Levi on both sides for `k, l >= 3`, does not by
  itself force head collapse. A T-free proof needs to combine the
  wrong-oriented roots: `x_43(S)` with `x_12(S)`, `x_12(T)`, `x_21(1)`, or
  the mirror set.
