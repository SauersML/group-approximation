For `|R|>=2`, distinct assignments differ in some coordinate, so the joint
spectral projection

```text
e_a=product_(j=1)^k (1+a_j d_j)/2
```

is the rank-one projection onto `C delta_a`. Products of a permutation
matrix carrying `a` to `b` with `e_a` give all matrix units. The algebra
generated by `K_R` is therefore `End(C^R)`, proving irreducibility. The joint
eigenvalue string at `delta_a` is exactly `a`, so the spectrum is `R`.

For the singleton construction, the two-dimensional tensor representation is
irreducible and every coordinate is the required scalar. Apply
`nontrivial-finite-type-corner-kills-scalar-characters` to either irreducible
type to obtain the final corner statements.
