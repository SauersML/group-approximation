**ESTABLISHED CANONICAL PRINCIPAL-ANGLE LAW.**  Put

```text
A=SL_3(Z[1/2]),              C=SL_3(Z),
E=<A,s | s^2=1, [s,C]=1>,   h=diag(2,1,1/2).
```

For any hypothetical canonical-character normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt
microstate sequence of `E`, round the matrix assigned to `s` by `o(1)` to an
exact self-adjoint involution `S_n`, and set

```text
P_n=(I-S_n)/2,
Q_n=H_n P_n H_n^*,          H_n=rho_n(h),
V_n=(I-2Q_n)(I-2P_n)=H_nS_nH_n^*S_n,
K_n=Q_nP_nQ_n.                                             (ASG1)
```

Then the empirical spectral measures satisfy

```text
mu_(V_n)  => Haar_T,
mu_(K_n)  => (1/2)delta_0+(1/2)Arcsine_[0,1].             (ASG2)
```

In particular,

```text
tr(P_nQ_n)->1/4,
tr(P_nQ_nP_nQ_n)->3/16,
tr(K_n-K_n^2)->1/16,
||[P_n,Q_n]||_2^2->1/8.                                  (ASG3)
```

For every moving sequence `epsilon_n->0`, one also has

```text
tr(1_[1-epsilon_n,1](K_n))->0.                           (ASG4)
```

Thus the automatic Gram `P_nQ_n` has no fixed-density near-isometric
singular sector in the actually needed canonical adjacent-vertex-double
class.  Canonicality does not leave its idempotence defect uncontrolled: it
forces the defect to the positive value `1/16`.  Any arithmetic argument
forcing that defect to zero is therefore already a finite-coordinate
contradiction to the second canonical fold moment, not a consequence of the
canonical spectral data.
