## 1. The support is one finite-subgroup augmentation projection

Work in the finite-dimensional algebra `C*(H)`, with `H` as in `(NCC2)`.
For a finite subgroup `R<=H`, the averaging projection `p_R=1-e_R` acts in
every representation as the projection onto the `R`-invariant vectors.
Since every summand in `(NCC1)` is positive,

```text
ker(h)=intersection_(j=0)^(N-1) ker(e_(alpha^j(F)))
      =intersection_(j=0)^(N-1) ran(p_(alpha^j(F))).   (NCC8)
```

The last space consists of the vectors fixed by every generating subgroup
`alpha^j(F)`, hence exactly the vectors fixed by `H`.  It is the range of
`p_H`.  Therefore the support of `h` is `1-p_H=e_H`, proving `(NCC3)`.

This also explains the collapsing spectral gap from the preceding package:
the support itself is simple, but the smallest positive eigenvalue of the
average measuring escape from the common fixed space need not be uniform in
the finite representation theory of `H`.

## 2. The generated ideal records normal closure

Inside `C*(H)`, the ideal generated by `e_H` is the augmentation ideal.  It
is generated equivalently by

```text
{1-lambda_h:h in H},                                  (NCC9)
```

because `(1-lambda_h)p_H=0`, while `e_H` belongs to the span of the same
differences.  In `C*(G)`, conjugating `(NCC9)` and multiplying differences
shows that the ideal generated by `e_H` is generated by
`1-lambda_m` for `m` in the normal closure `M`.

The universal group quotient `G->G/M` induces a surjection on full group
C-star algebras, and its kernel is precisely the ideal generated by those
relations.  This proves `(NCC4)` and identifies the ideal generated by
`s(h)` with `Q_M`.  Since `M<=K`, this ideal lies in `J`.

Because `s(h) in J`, compressing either `J` or `C*(G)` gives the same corner,
which proves `(NCC5)`.  By construction the projection is full in `Q_M`.
Full corners of separable C-star algebras are stably isomorphic to their
ambient ideals, and nuclear dimension is stable-isomorphism invariant.  This
proves `(NCC6)`.

## 3. Alternating shift

The finitary alternating group on an infinite set is simple.  Every
nontrivial `H<=K` therefore has normal closure `K` already inside `K`, and
the normal closure inside `G` is still `K` because `K` is normal in `G`.
Thus `Q_M=J`, proving `(NCC7)`.

The carriers used for a nonzero algebraic augmentation coefficient are
nontrivial: the finite subgroup `F` must contain a nonidentity group element.
Hence the full-corner conclusion applies at every nonzero stage relevant to
local approximation.
