title: Finite relators repair a finitely presented cover to embed a prescribed finitely presented subgroup and preserve a normal generator

Suppose P is finitely presented, pi:P->Q is surjective, and j:H->Q is
injective with H finitely presented. Suppose Q is normally generated by
j(h) for some h in H.

Then a quotient Gamma of P by finitely many additional relators has:

1. a surjection rho:Gamma->Q;
2. an embedding i:H->Gamma satisfying rho i=j;
3. Gamma normally generated by i(h).

If P has property (T), so does Gamma. With fixed finite presentations,
at most the number of relators of H plus the number of generators of P
additional relators suffice.

This makes no claim that rho has trivial, central, or finitely normally
generated kernel. Finite normal generation of ker(P->Gamma) is a different
statement from finite normal generation of ker(Gamma->Q).
