Let `X` be a finite graph and let `D` be a simple separable
stable-rank-one stably projectionless C*-algebra. Point evaluation is a
natural isomorphism

```text
Cu(C(X,D)) -> Lsc(X,Cu(D)).
```

No restriction on `K_1(D)` is imposed.

Consequently, if `D` is simple, separable, stably finite, pure and stably
projectionless, then `C(X,D)` is pure and Cuntz semigroup regular for every
finite graph `X`. Equivalently, the actual first-factor map induces an
isomorphism

```text
Cu(C(X,D)) -> Cu(C(X,D) tensor_min Z).
```

The finite-graph hypothesis is used to neutralize only finitely many chord
mismatches. Stable projectionlessness provides arbitrarily small spectrum
in every nonzero cut fiber. The theorem does not assert the same formula for
arbitrary one-dimensional compacta or for coefficients with nonzero
projections.
