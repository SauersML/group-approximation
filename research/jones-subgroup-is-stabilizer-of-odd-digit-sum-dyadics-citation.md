**Source.** G. Golan and M. Sapir, *On Jones' subgroup of R. Thompson group F*, arXiv:1501.00724
(v1 4 Jan 2015, revised 16 Apr 2015); J. Algebra 470 (2017) 122–159.

**How it was read.** The arXiv PDF was fetched on 2026-09-14 and its text extracted locally with
`pdftotext -layout`. The quotes are from the introduction (extracted lines 134–140) and §4.3 (line 747).
The journal version was not compared.

**Verbatim.**
- "Theorem 2. Jones' subgroup \vec F is the stabilizer of the set of dyadic fractions from the unit
  interval [0, 1] with odd sums of digits, under the standard action of F on the interval [0, 1]."
- "Corollary 3. Jones' subgroup \vec F coincides with its commensurator in F."
- Abstract: "we prove that the subgroup \vec F is generated by x0x1, x1x2, x2x3 (where xi, i=0,1,2,... are
  the standard generators of F) and is isomorphic to F_3".

**Secondary confirmation.** Aiello–Brothier–Conti (arXiv:1901.10597, Theorem 4.2) restate Theorem 2, with
`S` defined as the `t = .a_1⋯a_n1` for which the number of `i ≤ n` with `a_i = 1` is even, i.e. an odd total
digit sum.
