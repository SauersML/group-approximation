Section 1 imports the published critical-finiteness theorem explicitly.
Conditional on C_c(o)=K, its boundary labels are independent uniforms on
(c,1). The cluster stays K precisely if all remain above q. Summing over
K, followed by dominated convergence and the bound min(tB,1), proves all
claims. This is a written deduction using that published theorem, not
formal verification or a new proof of critical finiteness.
