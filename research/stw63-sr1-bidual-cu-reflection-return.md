Let `B` be a unital stable-rank-one C-star algebra.  If the canonical
inclusion `i:B->B**` induces an injective map

```text
Cu(i):Cu(B)->Cu(B**),                                   (1)
```

then every two unital embeddings `Z->B` are approximately unitarily
equivalent.

More sharply, if `phi,psi:Z->B` are a counterexample with `B` of stable rank
one, then `Cu(i)` identifies two distinct elements lying in the two image
subsemigroups `Cu(phi)(Cu(Z))` and `Cu(psi)(Cu(Z))`.  Thus noninjectivity is
forced on actual Jiang--Su image data, not merely somewhere abstract in
`Cu(B)`.
