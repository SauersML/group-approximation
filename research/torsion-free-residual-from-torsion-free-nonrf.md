The finite residual of a torsion-free group is a torsion-free subgroup, and it
is nontrivial when the group is not residually finite.  This is half of an
intended equivalence cycle with `torsion-free-nonrf-from-torsion-free-residual`;
do not delete either half to silence the cycle warning.
