OPEN.  Item **S5b** of the roadmap in `Computability/BooneGroupBase`.  Inside
the tower `G'_M` of a modular machine,

    T = T' cap G     and     T_M = T'_M cap G,

where `T'` and `T'_M` are the subgroups generated by `T` respectively `T_M`
together with all the stable letters.

## What remains, precisely

The base-group half is done: [[boone-halting-index-good-parameter-set]]
exhibits the source and target sides at each quadruple as the two images of one
parameter set.  What is left is purely HNN transport -- rewriting those two
images along the associated isomorphism `emb c 0 M^2 1 . (emb a b M M)^{-1}`,
which is well defined because `emb_injective` holds -- and then iterating
[[hnn-good-subgroup-lemma]] once per stable letter, each stage's conclusion
supplying the next stage's goodness hypothesis.

Estimated cost: about 120 new lines once the good-subgroup lemma is available.
No new mathematics is expected here; the work is that Mathlib's HNN extension
carries one stable letter, so the statement has to be assembled stage by stage
rather than proved once.
