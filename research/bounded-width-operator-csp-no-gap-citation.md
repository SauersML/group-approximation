Source: A. A. Bulatov and S. Zhivny, *Satisfiability of commutative vs.
non-commutative CSPs*, arXiv:2404.11709. The v4 abstract states that tractable
CSPs of bounded width have no satisfiability gaps of any kind, after defining
the comparison between ordinary and operator satisfiability over finite
domains. This is exactly the imported statement; no stronger classification
claim is used here.
