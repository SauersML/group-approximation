title: An infinite torsion group of type F_infinity is a finitely presented infinite torsion group

A group of type F∞ is of type F_n for every `n`, in particular of type F_2. A
group is of type F_2 exactly when it is finitely presented: a `K(Q,1)` with
finite 2-skeleton gives a finite presentation from its 1- and 2-cells, and a
finite presentation complex extends to a `K(Q,1)` by attaching cells of
dimension at least 3. So an infinite torsion group of type F∞ is a finitely
presented infinite torsion group.
