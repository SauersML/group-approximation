Use the exact shared face representation to obtain a representation of the
homogeneous LCS group.  Its assumed normalized Kazhdan gap applies to the
conjugation representation on `L_2(M)`.  The face-versus-coordinate Laplacian
comparison then gives the uniform adjoint syndrome gap.
