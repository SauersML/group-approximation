Fence A (an infinite RF amenable quotient defeats the uniform gap via Folner
deformations) and fence B (LERF defeats co-density via separability) are each
preserved by free products, amalgams over finite subgroups, products with
amenable groups, and finite extensions; the complete published roster of
non-amenable HS-stable groups is checked to hit A or B, and weak ucp-stability
(implied by LLP) is inconsumable by the HNN mechanism.  Hence no host exists
in the current literature.
