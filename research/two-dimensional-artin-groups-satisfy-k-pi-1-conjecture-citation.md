Citation import, not a reproof. Primary source: R. Charney and M. W. Davis, J. Amer. Math.
Soc. (1995), 597–627. The primary source was NOT read; the statement is taken from secondary
sources read on 2026-09-13 from their arXiv LaTeX sources.

- R. Boyd, *An introduction to the geometric and combinatorial group theory of Artin groups*,
  arXiv:2601.08658 (January 2026): "An Artin group is said to be 2-dimensional if all finite
  type T⊆S satisfy that |T|≤2 (type A_1 or I_2(p)). In particular this means that the
  Salvetti complex and the Deligne complex are 2-dimensional. For these groups the K(π,1)
  conjecture was proved by Charney and Davis [CharneyDavis1995]".
- J. Huang and P. Przytycki, arXiv:2509.06914, introduction: "The dimension ≤2 case of
  Theorem [main intro] was established in 1995 [CharneyDavis]."
- G. Paolini and M. Salvetti, arXiv:1907.11795, introduction: "Besides Euclidean cases, the
  conjecture was proved for Artin groups of dimension ≤ 2 and for those of FC type
  [hendriks1985hyperplane, charney1995k]."

Large type: if `m_st ≥ 3` for all `s ≠ t` and `|S| ≥ 3`, then every triple has
`1/m_rs + 1/m_st + 1/m_rt ≤ 1` and so generates an infinite Coxeter group; hence the
dimension is at most 2.
