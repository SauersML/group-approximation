**ESTABLISHED (proposed; unreviewed).**  Derivation: [[monomial-origin-level-subpairs-rank-three-cone-proof]].
It is an obstruction for the nonsofic side of [[rank-three-finite-twisted-kazhdan-cone-is-sofic]].

## Setting

`R_+ = F_q[x_1..x_d]`, `R = F_q[x_1^(±1)..x_d^(±1)]`, `Gamma = SL_3(R_+)`, `G = EL_3(R) x| SL_d(Z)`,
`psi = ev_0 : Gamma -> F = PSL_3(F_q)`.  A Kun-Thom certificate for `T_3` is a `B ≤ G` with
`C = B ∩ Gamma` such that `C_F(psi(C)) ≠ 1`, `B` and `C` are Kazhdan, and `C` is infranormal and not normal in
`B` ([[divisor-level-subpairs-cannot-certify-rank-three-twisted-cone]], (B2) of
[[block-subpairs-make-twisted-kazhdan-cones-nonsofic]], [[kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic]]).

For `k ≥ 1` let `U_k = < e_ij(x^m) : i ≠ j, m ∈ N^d, |m| ≥ k >`.  It lies in every level `Gamma(m_0^k)`.  For
`H ≤ SL_d(Z)` put

```text
S = H · { m ∈ N^d : |m| ≥ k },   P = the additive semigroup generated by S,
K0 = cone(H · R_+^d)  (not closed),   K = closure of K0,   B_(H,k) = < U_k , H > ≤ G .
```

## Theorem

**(M0) Structure.**  `B_(H,k) = Ũ x| H` with `Ũ = < e_ij(x^m) : i ≠ j, m ∈ P >`, and
`Ũ ⊆ 1 + M_3(F_q[P])`, where `F_q[P]` is the span of the monomials `x^m`, `m ∈ P`.  So `C = Ũ ∩ Gamma`.

**(M1) Salience is forced, for every origin-level B.**  Let `B ≤ G` be any subgroup containing `U_k`, and
`H_B = B ∩ SL_d(Z)`.  If `cone(H_B · R_+^d)` contains a line, then `e_ij(1) ∈ C` for all `i ≠ j` and
`psi(C) = F`.  This sharpens (D5) of the hypersurface node, which assumed that the cone is all of `R^d`.

**(M2) The origin image is all or nothing.**  For `B = B_(H,k)`: if `K0` contains a line, `psi(C) = F`.  If
`K0` is salient, `0 ∉ P` and `psi(C) = 1`, so `C_F(psi(C)) = F` and the centraliser condition holds in its
strongest form.

**(M3) Infinite H with pointed closure: B is not Kazhdan.**  Suppose `K0` is salient, `K` is pointed and `H`
is infinite.  Then the set `X` of irreducible elements of `P` is infinite and `H`-invariant, and `B` maps onto
the permutational wreath product `F_q wr_X H`, which is not Kazhdan.

**(M4) Finite H: C is profinitely closed, so not a certificate.**  Suppose `K0` is salient and `H` is finite.
Then `C` is closed in the profinite topology of `B`.  By
[[infranormal-subgroups-have-normal-profinite-closure]], if `C` is infranormal in `B` it is normal.

**(M5) Consequence.**  A Kun-Thom certificate of the form `B_(H,k)` needs an infinite `H ≤ SL_d(Z)`
(Kazhdan, since it is a quotient of `B`) whose cone `K0 ⊇ R_+^d` is salient while its closure `K` has a nonzero
lineality space `L = K ∩ -K`.  Every other `H` fails at an explicit step: (M2) kills condition (i), (M3) kills
property (T) of `B`, and (M4) kills non-normal infranormality.

## The PSD candidate

`H = Sym^2 SL_3(Z)` acting on `Sym^2 Z^3 ≅ Z^6` in the basis `E_ii`, `(e_i + e_j)(e_i + e_j)^T` preserves the
PSD cone, which is closed and pointed and contains the orthant.  So `K ⊆ PSD` is pointed and `H` is infinite,
and (M3) applies: `B_(H,k)` is not Kazhdan, for every `k`.  The same holds for every infinite `H` preserving a
closed pointed cone that contains the orthant, for instance any symmetric-cone automorphism lattice.

## What is left

The residual geometry of (M5) is not excluded by these arguments.  The natural test case: take
`O = Z[√2]` and `H = Sym^2 SL_3(O)` on `Sym^2 O^3 ≅ Z^12`, in the basis `ω E_ii`,
`ω (e_i + e_j)(e_i + e_j)^T` with `ω ∈ {1, √2}`.  All basis vectors have nonzero PSD image `v_+` under the real
embedding `√2 ↦ √2`, so `K0` lies in `Q = { v : v_+ PSD }` and is salient.  `H` is Kazhdan (a lattice in
`SL_3(R)^2`), and `psi(C) = 1` by (M2).  `Q` has the irrational lineality space `V_-` of the other embedding.
If `K` is pointed, (M3) applies and `B` is not Kazhdan.  If `K` contains a line, which density of `H` in
`SL_3(R)^2` suggests, this is the residual case: whether `Ũ` and `C` are Kazhdan there, and whether the
compressors of `C` generate `B`, is not settled.  Subgroups `B ⊇ U_k`
that are not generated by `U_k` and monomial elements are not covered either; for them only (M1) is proved.
