**ESTABLISHED (written deduction).** Proof in `fpbs-letter-saturated-morse-matchings-die-on-bernoulli-proof`.

**Setting.**
- `Gamma` is generated by a finite set `S` and acts on the Bernoulli shift, or on any free factor of it.
- `R = (w_1, ..., w_m)` is any finite list of trivial words, and `M` is any Morse matching for `R` ((M1)-(M3) of
  `fpbs-bernoulli-morse-collapse-criterion`). Letters and positions are arbitrary Borel choices.
- For a type `r` and a letter `s` with `s^2 != 1`:
  - `k_rs >= 2` is the number of occurrences of `s` in `w_r`, and their bases `h_a` are pairwise distinct;
  - `Lambda_rs = <h_a h_b^{-1}>`, and `rho_rs` is its Kesten radius;
  - `mu(r -> s)` is the mass of type-`r` cells matched to `s`-edges.

**Theorem A (letter-share cap).**

```text
mu(r -> s)  <=  1 - (k_rs - 1)(1 - rho_rs) / (6 k_rs^2).
```

- *Corollary B.* A letter-pure matching has `mu(M) <= sum_r (1 - c_{r,sigma(r)})`.
- *Corollary C.* At a torsion-free one-relator `{w}`, every family reaching the ceiling `1` keeps each letter's share
  `<= 1 - c_s`. For `abaBabcdcDcd`, such a family must spread over at least two letters.

**Proposition D (barrier).** Let `Sigma` be the inequality system the per-letter sublevel method produces: height
monotonicity, sublevel almost-invariance plus the Kesten gap, the thick-level count, and the Cheeger height tail. For
two or more letters with equal shares, `Sigma` is satisfied at zero deficit by the lockstep profile
`u_j^s = lambda^j / |T|`. This holds for every `lambda >= (k - 1)/(k - 1 + k|T|)` (sharp), and with
`lambda -> 1` for the tail.

- *Where it dies:* Lemma 1'. The straddling mass of letter `s` is bounded only by `delta` plus the other letters'
  removed mass above the level, which is the same size as the quantity it has to control.
- This is a statement about the method. It does not construct a matching.

**Class killed.** Every Morse construction at any list that needs some cell type to be almost entirely matched to one
letter used `>= 3` times with nonamenable transport. This includes "main letter plus corrections" schemes and
dual-forest or Borel-peeling transplants with helper matches.
- *Invariant:* `1 - rho(Lambda_rs, Q_rs)`.
- *Step where it dies:* (M3), through the top property.

**Survivors, sharpened.**
- Over growing lists `P_L` the cap is vacuous, because the per-type share `z/m_L` goes to `0`. The target
  `fpbs-morse-deficit-vanishes-for-full-presentations` is untouched there.
- At a fixed list, a ceiling-reaching family must be:
  - unsaturated (Theorem A);
  - height-non-tight (`fpbs-near-optimal-morse-matchings-have-non-tight-heights`);
  - jointly balanced across letters in a way `Sigma` cannot see. This part is OPEN, and the test case is
    `abaBabcdcDcd`.
