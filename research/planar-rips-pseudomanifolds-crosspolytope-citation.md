Citation import. M. Adamaszek, F. Frick and A. Vakili, "On homotopy types of
Euclidean Rips complexes", arXiv:1602.04131v1 (12 Feb 2016), p. 3; published
in Discrete Comput. Geom. 58 (2017) 526–542 (bibliographic data from
OpenAlex). Read from the PDF on 2026-09-13:

> Theorem IV. Suppose that `X ⊆ R^2` is a finite subset, `d ≥ 2` and that
> `R(X)` contains a `d`-dimensional normal pseudomanifold `K` as an induced
> subcomplex. Then `K` is isomorphic to the boundary of the
> `(d+1)`-crosspolytope.

The authors state that they address the question raised in Chambers–de
Silva–Erickson–Ghrist [2, 6.(1)], "of whether the only significant examples
of homology in dimensions `d ≥ 2` of a planar Rips complex can be generated by
'local' point configurations", without resolving it completely. Their Rips
complex uses `diam(Y) < r`, and Remark 1.4 transfers finite-set results to
`≤ r`.
