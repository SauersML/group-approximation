**ESTABLISHED CONJUGACY-CLOCK NO-GO.**  Let `K=B_7`.  A one-clock actor
which retains `K` and whose finite state is entirely conjugacy data has the
relative presentation

```text
Gamma_(alpha)=<K,t | tkt^(-1)=alpha(k), k in K>,       (BCC1)
```

for some `alpha in Aut(K)`, possibly together with one consistent return

```text
t^N=z,              z in K.                            (BCC2)
```

Finite flag registers and finite controller states only replace `K` by a
larger finite packet and do not change the argument.

Without `(BCC2)`, `Gamma_(alpha)=K semidirect_alpha Z`.  Since `Aut(K)` is
finite, `alpha` has finite order `r`; the subgroup generated by `t^r` is
central, infinite cyclic, and has finite index.  Hence `Gamma_(alpha)` is
virtually cyclic and residually finite.

With `(BCC2)`, consistency and injectivity of `K` require

```text
alpha^N=Ad(z),             alpha(z)=z.                 (BCC3)
```

Every word then has a normal form `kt^j`, `k in K`, `0<=j<N`.  Thus the
actor is finite of order at most `N|K|` and is again residually finite.
More generally, collect every monomial one-clock relator to `k t^n=1`.
The subgroup of clock exponents generated by the relators is either zero or
`dZ`.  In the second case a Bezout combination, collected again through
`alpha`, yields one relation `t^d=z`; the remaining relators become
relations in `K`.  If those relations do not already hold, they collapse
part of `K` and fail the required B7 retention.

It follows from
`b7-faithful-two-cell-retains-or3-prefix-defect` that every such actor has
canonical finite-quotient models in which all clock relators are exact and

```text
||p_rho q_3[B,q_2]q_1P||_2^2=1/92160.                 (BCC4)
```

Therefore no dimension-independent HS payment of the oriented prefix can
come from a finite-state controller which only makes its clock normalize the
B7 packet.  This includes finite automorphism clocks, conjugacy counters,
finite-order return phases, and any finite flag enlargement of them.
