For every integer d>=2 there exists a simple separable unital nuclear
stably finite infinite-dimensional AH algebra A_d with

    K1(A_d)=0,   U(A_d)/U0(A_d) ~= Z/d.

The initial building block is C(S2 times CP^(d-1), M_d). A generator
is obtained by composing the characteristic unitary S^(2d) -> U(d)
with the degree-one top-cell collapse of S2 times CP^(d-1).
Its first matrix stabilization is null-homotopic.

The twisted tower doubles ranks and adds tautological-line evaluation
blocks. Every finite-stage component group is Z/d, and every connecting
map induces an isomorphism. This strengthens the LIX counterexample
without asserting a second named open-problem solution or realization
of arbitrary noncyclic finite groups.

The complete proof has undergone internal mathematical review;
it has not been externally peer reviewed or formally verified.

The subsequent theorem `stw59-exact-finite-abelian-unitary-component-groups`
uses equal-dimension joined bases and independently attaining global
Euler classes to realize every finite abelian group in one simple
algebra. It retains this cyclic calculation as its armwise input.
