title: "Assouad's theorem (Naor–Neiman form): snowflakes of doubling metric spaces embed bi-Lipschitz in some R^N"
---

For every `K > 0` and `ε ∈ (0, 1/2)` there are `N = N(K)` and
`D = D(K, ε)` with the following property. Every separable metric space
`(X, d)` with doubling constant at most `K` has a map `ψ : X → R^N` and a
scale `s > 0` such that, for all `x, x' ∈ X`,
`s·d(x,x')^{1−ε} ≤ |ψ(x) − ψ(x')|_2 ≤ D·s·d(x,x')^{1−ε}`.
