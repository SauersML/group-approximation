**1. Links do not see the gluing.** The link at `v_i` is the coset graph of the images of the two edge
groups in `A_i`. Precomposing an embedding with an automorphism of the edge group does not change its
image. So:
- `Γ_1 = Γ_2 = Γ_{SL_2(k)}(u(k), v(k))`;
- `Γ_3 = Γ_s = Γ_{SL_2(F)}(u(k), v(sk))`.

**2. The link at `A_3`.** Put `σ = σ_2(Γ_s)`.
- For `q = 8, 16`: `σ < 1 - 2/q` and `Γ_s` is connected, by `sl2-subfield-root-links-codistance-q8-q16`.
- For `q >= 32`: the same holds by `sl2-subfield-root-links-codistance-large-q`.

**3. (K1).** `SL_2(2^n)` is simple for `2^n >= 4` (standard).
- `<u(k), v(k)> = SL_2(k)`, because `Γ_1` is connected
  (`sl2-opposite-root-subgroups-codistance-inverse-sqrt-q` (a)).
- `<u(k), v(sk)> = SL_2(F)`, by Step 2.
- The edge groups have order `q >= 8`, the face group is trivial, and no edge monomorphism is surjective.

**4. (K2).** The half-girths are:
- `r_1 = r_2 = 3`, by `sl2-opposite-root-subgroups-codistance-inverse-sqrt-q` (a);
- `r_3 >= 5`, by `sl2-subfield-root-pair-girth-eight` (b).

Since `1/3 + 1/3 + 1/5 < 1`, `triangle-of-groups-half-girth-structure` (i), (iii), (v) gives
developability, injectivity of the vertex groups, infiniteness and non-elementary hyperbolicity.

**5. (K3).** The colimit is generated by the vertex groups, and each vertex group is generated by its edge
groups. So `Λ` is generated by the three finite subgroups `E_12, E_13, E_23`. Fix a unitary
representation `V` of `Λ`, and apply `coset-graph-singular-values-bound-fixed-space-angles` to
`A_i -> Λ`:
- `cos ∢(V^{E_12}, V^{E_13}) <= σ_2(Γ_1) = q^{-1/2}`;
- `cos ∢(V^{E_12}, V^{E_23}) <= σ_2(Γ_2) = q^{-1/2}`;
- `cos ∢(V^{E_13}, V^{E_23}) <= σ`.

Take `ε = q^{-1/2}` for the first two pairs and `ε = σ` for the third. The leading principal minors of
the matrix with `1` on the diagonal and `-ε` off it are:
- `1`;
- `1 - 1/q > 0`;
- `1 - 2/q - σ^2 - 2σ/q = (1 - 2/q - σ)(1 + σ) > 0`.

So the matrix is positive definite (Sylvester), and `kassabov-subspace-angle-criterion` gives property
(T). ∎
