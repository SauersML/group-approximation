Let `R=L_(F_2)(1,2)`, put

```text
D=span_(F_2){s_wt_w},       Lambda=EL_5(D),
u=x_ij(s_0),                v=x_ij(t_0),
G=<Lambda,u,v>,             z=x_13(1-s_0t_0)=x_13(s_1t_1).
```

Then `G` is not LEF.  More precisely, there is a finitely presented group
`Gamma_T`, a homomorphism `pi:Gamma_T->G`, and a word `w_T in Gamma_T` such
that

```text
pi(w_T)=z!=1,
phi(w_T)=1 for every homomorphism phi:Gamma_T->F with F finite.       (FNL1)
```

Consequently one fixed finite multiplication table in `G` containing `z`
has no injective partial homomorphism to a finite group.

The coefficient subring generated by `D,s_0,t_0` is the algebraic
one-branch Toeplitz ring.  If `q=1-s_0t_0` and `A=qDq`, its compact ideal is

```text
K=directSum_(m,n>=0) s_0^m A t_0^n ~= M_fin(A),
```

and the symbol quotient is `F_2[zeta,zeta^(-1)]`.  Thus

```text
G=EL_5(<D,s_0,t_0>)
```

is a locally-finite-by-residually-finite Toeplitz elementary group.  This
structure does not imply LEF; `(FNL1)` gives the explicit obstruction.

A single new occurrence beyond the completed Whitehead face already
suffices: expose one raw factor.  With the constant Weyl swap `P in Lambda`
and

```text
W_0=u (P v P^(-1)) u,
```

the root involution identity gives

```text
P v P^(-1)=u W_0 u.
```

Hence `<Lambda,W_0,u>=G`; the complementary Whitehead `W_1` is unnecessary
for the non-LEF conclusion.  In particular the infinite-order monomial
`W_0P` found by the prefix-shift fence is only a symptom.  The load-bearing
step is exposing one factor of `W_0`, which reconstructs both one-sided
inverse coefficient roots and therefore the finite Toeplitz presentation.

No operator-MF conclusion is asserted.  An operator-norm microstate of `G`
precomposes to an almost representation of `Gamma_T`, whereas `(FNL1)`
only controls exact representations.  Making the same marked word small in
all such microstates is exactly the root-to-square coefficient-decoding gap:
after a square decoder the equations `t_0s_0=1` and
`1-s_0t_0=q` kill the normalized trace of `q`, while rectangular Morita
models evade that deduction.

## Finite-truncation audit

There is no marked coefficient-ring periodization of this packet.  A finite
path truncation retains the endpoint defect `q` only by violating
`t_0s_0=1` at its far endpoint.  A cyclic truncation makes `s_0` invertible,
so `t_0s_0=1` forces `s_0t_0=1` and kills `q`.  The finitely presented cover
packages this direct-finiteness dichotomy into the fixed multiplication
table in `(FNL1)`, so increasing the path or cycle length cannot restore
marked LEF.  This does not settle operator MF, where approximate group
relations need not decode to a finite coefficient ring.
