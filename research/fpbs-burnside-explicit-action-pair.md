title: A Bernoulli shift and its product with a cyclic quotient have costs c_n and 1+(c_n-1)/n
---

Section 5. For Gamma=B(2,n), pi(a)=1 and pi(b)=0 modulo n, let alpha be Bernoulli and beta(g)(omega,j)=(g omega,j+pi(g)). Both are free ergodic. Cost(alpha)=c_n and Cost(beta)=1+(c_n-1)/n. They are NOT asserted to have different costs, because c_n>1 is unproved.
