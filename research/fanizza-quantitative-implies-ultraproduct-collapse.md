The HALT representation is identical. On a NONHALT instance, represent any
homomorphism to a tracial matrix ultraproduct by coordinate tuples. Their
finite presentation defects tend to zero. The moduli `(NSG1)--(NSG2)` produce
BCS strategies whose errors tend to zero and identify the coordinate image of
`d_m` with the distinguished Fanizza involution. Native NONHALT soundness makes
that involution converge to `I`, hence the ultraproduct homomorphism kills
`d_m`.

This route records only logical strength: the qualitative target may admit an
exact ultraproduct proof even when no effective coordinatewise modulus is
available.
