# Citation import

Not a reproof. `requires: []` asserts only that the cited statements say what the claim says.
Source: C. Ciobotaru, *A unified proof of the Howe--Moore property*, arXiv:1403.0223, Definition 2.6
(p. 4) and Theorem 1.1 (p. 2), read from the PDF text on 2026-09-12 and transcribed in
`research/artifacts/nonsofic-action-mixing-transfer-2026-09-12.md`, Section 1.

The mixing consequence in the claim is the Howe--Moore property applied to the Koopman representation
on `L²_0` of an ergodic action. That representation is continuous and has no nonzero invariant
vectors, so its coefficients are `C_0`. A discrete subgroup meets every compact set in a finite set,
so the coefficients tend to `0` along the subgroup.
