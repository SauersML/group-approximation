Let `k` be any field and let `G` be finitely generated and virtually RFRS,
with

`cd_k(G) <= 2`.

The following are equivalent:

1. `G` is coherent;
2. `k[G]` is coherent;
3. `G` is homologically coherent over `k`;
4. `H_2(H;k)` is finite-dimensional for every finitely generated
   subgroup `H<=G`;
5. the second `D_{k[H]}`-Betti number is finite for every finitely
   generated `H<=G`;
6. the second `D_{k[G]}`-Betti number of `G` vanishes;
7. `G` is virtually free-by-cyclic;
8. some finite-index subgroup of `G` is the fundamental group of a
   two-complex with nonpositive immersions.

The free kernel in item 7 is not required to be finitely generated.
For `k=Q`, item 6 is the usual condition `b_2^(2)(G)=0`.
