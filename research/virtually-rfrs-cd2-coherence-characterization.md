title: Coherence of virtually RFRS groups in dimension two has equivalent analytic and structural tests
distinct_from:
  one-relator-groups-are-coherent: that is a presentation-class theorem; this is an if-and-only-if characterization for finitely generated virtually RFRS groups.
---

Let `G` be finitely generated and virtually RFRS, with
`cd_Q(G)<=2`. The following are equivalent:

1. `G` is coherent;
2. `Q[G]` is coherent;
3. `G` is homologically coherent over `Q`;
4. `b_2^(2)(G)=0`;
5. `G` is virtually free-by-cyclic;
6. some finite-index subgroup of `G` is the fundamental group of a
   two-complex with nonpositive immersions.

The free kernel in item 5 is not required to be finitely generated.
