Let U=(A,B,C) in U(d)^3, and suppose each of the eleven standard GHB7
relators r(U) commutes exactly with all three generators. Let
delta=max_r ||r(U)-I||_(2,d) and delta_0=2 sin(pi/2401). Then there is
a genuine pi:GHB2(7)->U(d) with, for each generator s,

    ||pi(s)-U_s||_(2,d)^2
       <= (pi/14)^2 ||U_s^7-I||_(2,d)^2
          +(4/delta_0^2) sum over eight commutator relators r
                                      ||r(U)-I||_(2,d)^2.

Hence the maximum generator error is at most
sqrt((pi/14)^2+32/delta_0^2) delta. No smallness restriction, expansion
assumption, dimension inflation or block-number bound is needed.

The doubled version only requires exact centrality of the plus
relators, vanishing plus-relator defects, and vanishing copy-equality defects. The latter may be
noncentral; the plus rounding and triangle inequality suffice.

**Boundary.** Exact centrality refers to the algebra generated by all
three generators. Being block scalar is insufficient if the generators
permute the blocks. Approximate centrality is not covered. The open
scalar-expander and one-edge problems remain open.
