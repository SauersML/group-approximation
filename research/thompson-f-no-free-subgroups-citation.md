Imports two classical facts:
- Brin–Squier (Invent. Math. 79 (1985)): no non-abelian free subgroups in groups
  of piecewise-linear homeomorphisms of the line.
- Cannon–Floyd–Parry (Enseign. Math. 42 (1996), §4): `F` is not elementary
  amenable.

Both are standard. Lane `thompson-f-amenability` recorded them on 2026-09-12
without re-reading the PDFs.
