Pass to the factor-preserving subgroup `Γ_0` (finite index; `B_A` passes to
finite-index overgroups).

**Step 1 (product cells).** In each factor fix a special vertex type and bh-groupoid's
model tiles and boxes (Step 0 of `euclidean-building-lattices-lie-in-permutational-bh-class`;
for trees, oriented edges and segments).
- A cell is a tuple of factor tiles, and a box of multi-shape `(m^1, ..., m^m)` is a
  tuple of factor boxes.
- Composition is factorwise, so bh-groupoid's composition lemma, applied in each
  factor, gives (UFP*) and finite alignment for product boxes.
- Sectors of `X` toward points of `∏ Ch(∂X_i)` are products of factor sectors.

**Step 2 (orbit category).** Define `ℭ_(Γ_0)` as in Step T1 of
`tree-lattice-with-torsion-via-zappa-szep-kgraphs` and Step 1 of the Euclidean node:
- objects are `Γ_0`-orbits of cells;
- morphisms are `(p, γ)`, with `p` a box from the representative cell and
  `γ c_(t(p)) = t(p)`;
- units are the finite cell stabilizers.
The result is a finite, cancellative, locally finite degree category with finite
unit groups. Its Li groupoid is the `Γ_0`-twisted tail equivalence on product
sectors. `Γ_0` acts by cylinder exchanges, because parallel product sectors share
product subsectors (Step 2 of the Euclidean node, factorwise).
- **Faithful and effective.** By the consequence of
  `automorphisms-fixing-open-boundary-sets-fix-large-balls`, no nontrivial element
  fixes an open set of `∏ Ch(∂X_i)`. So the groupoid is effective and `Γ_0` embeds in
  its full group.

**Step 3 (strongly connected pieces, factor by factor).** Fix a factor `i` and a cell
`α` in the other factors, with stabilizer `H_α`. `H_α` acts on `X_i` with finite
stabilizers and finitely many orbits, by the argument of Lemma P in
`product-of-trees-faithful-factor-pbh-via-bass-serre-induction`, which uses only
proper cocompact actions on products of locally finite complexes.
- **One factor.** Moves in factor `i` alone, with `α` fixed, are governed by the
  factor-`i` transition counts for `H_α`. By complete reducibility from counting
  (Step 3 of the Euclidean node for building factors; Hashimoto graphs, as in Step T4
  of the torsion route, for tree factors), reachability under these moves is
  symmetric.
- **Finite kernels.** The kernel `N` of `H_α` on `X_i` lies in every tile stabilizer,
  so it rescales all the weights `1/|K_a|` by one factor and leaves the Perron
  vectors unchanged. The count needs only finite stabilizers, finitely many
  `H_α`-classes of tiles, and successor counts that depend only on phase (confirmed
  by bh-groupoid, 09-18).
- **All factors.** Every morphism factors into single-factor, single-colour steps
  (UFP*). So reachability between objects is generated by symmetric relations,
  hence an equivalence relation, and the classes are strongly connected pieces with
  no morphisms between them.
- **Loops.** Each class of each factor-`i` count matrix has spectral radius greater
  than 1 (thickness), so rescaling by a large common multiple `n` of all periods gives
  at least two loops of every colour at every object. The rescaled category still
  splits into strongly connected pieces, by the symmetry argument of Step 1 of
  `virtually-torsion-free-tree-lattices-pbh-via-kgraphs`.

**Step 4 (phases, finiteness, type (A)).**
- **Phases.** The Busemann lag of product sectors is a vector in `Z^(Σ rank_i)`, and
  the phase construction of Step 5 of `tree-lattice-kgraph-power-phase-proof` embeds
  the full group into that of the rescaled groupoid.
- **Finiteness.** Li's Theorem `thm:deg` gives type `F_∞` for the rescaled category
  times the Cuntz graph on all object boundaries: two loops everywhere, (F) from right
  cancellation, finite unit groups.
- **Type (A).** `degree-category-full-groups-stabilized-have-type-a-actions` then
  gives a type (A) action, so the host lies in `B_A`. Hence `Γ_0` and `Γ` lie in
  `B_A`.
