Let `A` be a C*-algebra, let `p,q` be projections in `A tensor K`, and let
`J` be the closed two-sided ideal of `A tensor K` generated by `p` and `q`.
If

```text
[p] != [q] in K_0(J),
```

then

```text
[p tensor 1_Z] != [q tensor 1_Z]
    in Cu(A tensor_min Z).
```

Consequently, any compact-class counterexample to Problem LXXVI must use two
projections that have equal `K_0` class already in their generated ideal.
Ambient `K_0(A)` equality alone is insufficient.
