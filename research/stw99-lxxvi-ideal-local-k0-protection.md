title: A projection K0 difference survives Z-stabilization when the generated tensor ideal is stably finite

Let `A` be a C*-algebra, let `p,q` be projections in `A tensor K`, and let
`J` be the closed two-sided ideal of `A tensor K` generated by `p` and `q`.
Assume that `J tensor_min Z` is stably finite.  If

```text
[p] != [q] in K_0(J),
```

then

```text
[p tensor 1_Z] != [q tensor 1_Z]
    in Cu(A tensor_min Z).
```

Consequently, a compact-class counterexample whose Z-stabilized generated
ideal is stably finite must use two projections that have equal `K_0` class
already in their generated ideal.  Without stable finiteness this conclusion
is not justified: Cuntz-equivalent infinite projections need not be
Murray--von Neumann equivalent and need not have equal `K_0` classes.
