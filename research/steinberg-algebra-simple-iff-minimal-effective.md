title: The Steinberg algebra of a Hausdorff ample groupoid over a field is simple iff the groupoid is effective and minimal
---

**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `𝒢` be a Hausdorff ample groupoid and `k` a field. Then the Steinberg algebra
`A_k(𝒢) = k𝒢` is simple if and only if `𝒢` is effective and minimal.

Sources: B. Steinberg, arXiv:1408.6014, Corollary `simplehaus`, following Theorem `simple` (numbered Corollary 3.6 in
the PDF, per `minimal-subshift-algebra-is-simple-lef-ring`); L. O. Clark and C. Edie-Michell, arXiv:1403.4684,
Corollary 4.6, a ring version. Brown–Clark–Farthing–Sims, arXiv:1204.3127, Theorem 4.1, is the complex case.
