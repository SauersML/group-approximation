**ESTABLISHED.**  For Deligne's connected triple cover

```text
1 -> C_3=<z> -> E_3 -> Sp_4(Z) -> 1
```

one has the unconditional dichotomy

```text
Rad_MF(E_3) in {1,C_3}.                                (DMD1)
```

Moreover the following are equivalent:

```text
Rad_MF(E_3)=C_3;
z belongs to Rad_MF(E_3);
E_3 is not MF.                                         (DMD2)
```

Here `C_3` is the order-three covering kernel.  It should not be called the
full center without qualification: the full center of this triple cover has
order six.
