For each degree-three length-fifteen unary profile

```text
(1,3,11),       (1,5,9),       (1,7,7),
```

the cyclic first/last-corner overlap cannot transport an identity source
holonomy to a fixed nonidentity target `h`.  The statement holds for
arbitrary coefficient units and every cancellation branch of the long source
corner.

On the central-palindrome nonidentity branch, the same three copy
retractions force the source and target holonomies to be conjugate in the
coefficient group.  Thus unequal-order Leavitt holonomies cannot escape by
increasing the residual valences from length thirteen to length fifteen.
