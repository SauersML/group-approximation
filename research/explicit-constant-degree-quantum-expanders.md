title: Explicit constant-degree quantum expanders exist with a uniform Hilbert-Schmidt spectral gap
---

There is an explicit infinite family of dimensions `N` and fixed constants
`D` and `lambda<1` with unitaries

```text
U_1^(N),...,U_D^(N) in U(N)
```

such that the channel

```text
Phi_N(X)=(1/D) sum_j U_j^(N) X U_j^(N)*
```

fixes the scalar matrices and has Hilbert--Schmidt operator norm at most
`lambda` on the traceless subspace. The family is explicit in the usual
quantum-circuit sense.

Ben-Aroya--Schwartz--Ta-Shma construct such a family by quantum squaring,
tensoring, and a quantum zig-zag product. This existence theorem does not say
that the `U_j^(N)` are generated by finitely many group words uniformly in
`N`; that stronger self-similar syntax is a separate target.
