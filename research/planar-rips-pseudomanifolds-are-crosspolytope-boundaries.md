If `X ⊆ R^2` is finite, `d ≥ 2`, and `R(X)` contains a `d`-dimensional normal
pseudomanifold `K` as an induced subcomplex, then `K` is isomorphic to the
boundary of the `(d+1)`-dimensional crosspolytope.

Context for Zaremsky Problem 4.2: the "local" sources of higher homology in
planar Rips complexes that are pseudomanifolds are exactly the crosspolytope
spheres, as for a regular `2(d+1)`-gon at a scale just below its diameter.
