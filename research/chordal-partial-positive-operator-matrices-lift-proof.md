Import Paulsen--Rodman Lemma 3.4.  A chordal graph admits a perfect
elimination ordering.  Starting at a simplicial vertex, its already specified
neighbors form a clique, so the positive clique corner lifts through the
quotient; induction removes the vertex and preserves partial positivity.
