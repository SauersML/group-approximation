Use the actor basis `(2,4,5,6,7,8,9)`.  The signed subgroup `L_0` is the
order-`8192` binary matrix group generated by the directed transvections

```text
6->5, 5->4, 4->2, 8->2, 9->4,
9->2, 9->7, 5->7, 6->7, 6->8.                         (1)
```

Its character is negative on the first three arrows and positive on the
last seven.  The incoming-row matrices of `v_1=B_2A_1` and `v_2=B_3A_2`
are obtained from `v_i=J_it_i`; conjugating the ten generators in `(1)`
gives, in the same order,

```text
v_1: h3, h2 v, h1 d, d, f c, k, c, v, w,
     h3 h2 h3 h2 s,

v_2: h3 s, h2 f, h1, h2 h1 h2 h1 d k,
     f, k, c, c v, w, s.                               (2)
```

Here `h3,h2,h1` are the first three generators and
`d,f,k,c,v,w,s` are the last seven.  The normal forms in `(2)` remain in
`L_0`; their character signs are respectively the original signs in
`(1)`.  The seven-coordinate action is faithful on `L_0` because both the
collected root group and its displayed binary matrix image have order
`8192`.  Hence `(2)` proves in the actual finite root group, not just a
quotient, that

```text
v_i(L_0,lambda_0)v_i^(-1)=(L_0,lambda_0).              (3)
```

Now `t_i=J_iv_i` and `(3)` immediately give `(LWB2)`.  Conjugating that
identity by `t_i^k` proves both full-Gram identities `(LWB4)`.  Finally

```text
t_i(t_i^kJ_it_i^(-k))
 =(t_i^(k+1)J_it_i^(-(k+1)))t_i
```

is `(LWB5)` as an exact word identity.

The reservoir typing in `(LWB6)` follows because the bridge contains the
same literal native Whitehead occurrence whose relative multiplicity
involution defines the stage gauge.  Hence the two occurrences of each
mixed square carry the same adjacent gauges and cannot be inserted as the
ungauged hypotheses `(SGL2)--(SGL3)`.
