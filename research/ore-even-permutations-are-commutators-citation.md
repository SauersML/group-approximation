Literature import, not a reproof. The scan (JSTOR stable URL http://www.jstor.org/stable/2032506) was read page by
page on 2026-09-13.
- p. 307, introduction: "In the following it is shown that in the finite symmetric group Σ_n all elements of the
  alternating group are commutators; one can extend this and show that when n ≧ 5 all elements in the alternating
  group are commutators of elements in A_n."
- p. 308: "THEOREM 1. Every element in the alternating group A_n is a commutator of Σ_n."
  - The proof rests on a lemma: T is a commutator iff T = V·V′ with V, V′ conformal.
  - It writes an odd cycle (1,…,2i+1) as a product of two cycles of order i+1, and a pair of even cycles as a
    product of two cycles of order i+j+1.
- p. 313: "THEOREM 7. When n ≧ 5 every element in the alternating group A_n is a commutator of elements in A_n."
  Ore omits the details.

DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-1951-0040298-4. The claim uses Theorem 1 only.
