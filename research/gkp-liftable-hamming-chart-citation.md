Definition 2.1 gives the density-one finitary chart formulation.
Proposition 2.9 proves it equivalent to an equivariant isometric embedding
of the discrete action into the universal sofic action on liftable maps from
the Loeb carrier to `N`.  Pairwise discrete distance one is exactly
`mu{ell_x != ell_y}=1`; equivariance is exactly the first identity in
`(LHC2)`.  This proves the stated characterization.
