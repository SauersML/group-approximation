**ESTABLISHED.**  Put `p=s_0t_0`, `q=s_1t_1` in the binary Leavitt
algebra and let

```text
j=s_0t_1+s_1t_0.                                        (WSC1)
```

Thus `j^2=1`, `jpj=q`, and `jqj=p`.  For every invertible matrix `g` over
the active corner define

```text
c_0(g)=q+s_0 g t_0,
c_1(g)=j c_0(g) j=p+s_1 g t_1.                          (WSC2)
```

The two terms commute, and their product is the unital diagonal
amplification

```text
c_0(g)c_1(g)=s_0 g t_0+s_1 g t_1=:Delta(g).             (WSC3)
```

Under the depth-one chart `Phi_1` this says

```text
Phi_1(c_0(g))=diag(g,1),
Phi_1(c_1(g))=diag(1,g),
Phi_1(Delta(g))=diag(g,g).                              (WSC4)
```

Consequently one Whitehead conjugacy, one conjugation by the fixed cylinder
swap, and one multiplication do give a bounded word for `Delta(g)`.  This is
the strongest immediate ``copy the missing corner'' use of the existing
Whitehead cell.  It does **not** identify the pre-amplification and
post-amplification multiplicity reservoirs.

Indeed, let `E` be any nonzero finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space,
let `F=E direct_sum E`, and let `s_i:E->F`, `t_i=s_i^*:F->E` be the
coordinate maps.  The Whitehead matrices

```text
U=[[s_0,s_1t_0],[0,t_1]] : E direct_sum F -> F direct_sum E,
V=[[t_0,0],[s_0t_1,s_1]] : F direct_sum E -> E direct_sum F             (WSC5)
```

are mutually inverse unitaries.  For every `g in U(E)`,

```text
U (g direct_sum 1_F) V=c_0(g) direct_sum 1_E.           (WSC6)
```

Conjugating the first target block by `j` and multiplying gives exactly
`Delta(g) direct_sum 1_E`.  Thus all of `(WSC1)--(WSC6)` have an exact
finite-dimensional model: one copy of `g` on the source object becomes two
copies on the target object, while stabilization changes the ordered carrier
from `E direct_sum F` to `F direct_sum E`.  No error or leakage term detects
this exchange.

The obstruction remains visible after adjoining the exact Pauli endpoint.
Take `E=C^2 tensor C^m`.  On `F=C^2_(branch) tensor C^2 tensor C^m`, the
two completed source Pauli words are

```text
Delta(X)=I_(branch) tensor X tensor I_m,
Delta(Z)=I_(branch) tensor Z tensor I_m,                (WSC7)
```

while the prefix matrix units supply the commuting branch pair

```text
X_(branch) tensor I_2 tensor I_m,
Z_(branch) tensor I_2 tensor I_m.                       (WSC8)
```

These four operators generate the full two-qubit factor on `F`.  Extended
by the identity on the stabilizing `E` summand, their standard polynomial
has normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm

```text
8 sqrt(dim(F)/(dim(F)+dim(E)))=8 sqrt(2/3).             (WSC9)
```

The source algebra before the Whitehead move is only one `M_2` label factor
on `E` (and scalars on `F`), hence satisfies `s_4=0`.  Therefore the fixed
PI gap and every canonical Whitehead/swap identity coexist exactly in this
finite model.  What is missing is still a relation whose finite-matrix
decoding confines `(WSC7)--(WSC8)` to that **same pre-existing factor**.

The calculation is also a bounded-depth no-go: any fixed finite chain of
these canonical cells has an exact model on
`E, E direct_sum E, ..., E^(direct sum 2^N)`.  Iteration merely changes the
rectangular label/multiplicity bookkeeping.  A successful stabilized Morita
cell must add a non-typed return that is not generated by Whitehead corner
compression, branch permutation, and multiplication alone.
