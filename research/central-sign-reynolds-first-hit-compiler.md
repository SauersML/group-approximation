**OPEN REGULAR-SAFE COMPILER.**  Starting from the fixed robust non-CE BCS,
use `central-sign-bcs-atoms-are-subgroup-reynolds-projections` and finitely
many subgroup-Hecke HNN edges to construct a finite presentation with the
following properties.

For one fixed depth `N`, every sufficiently accurate canonical finite matrix
model produces active carrier masses `q_n` and pairwise summable first-hit
exit masses `r_n` satisfying the hypotheses of
`branching-plus-first-hit-exits-forces-hs-floor`, with

```text
q_0>=q_*>0,                 r_n>=eta q_n-error,
```

where `q_*,eta>0` and all error constants are dimension-independent.  By
`central-sign-reynolds-atoms-have-fixed-canonical-density`, `q_*` is already
the Plancherel mass of one embedded context atom and does not come from the
game gap.  The robust non-CE BCS input must instead be used in deriving a
finite-matrix-only exit/selection inequality which removes the regular
companion flow.  Merely lower-bounding scalar rejection mass makes no
progress on this gate.

The construction must also have an exact infinite tracial completion.  In
that completion the regular-trace companion sectors required by

```text
tau(e_H)=1/|H|,       tau(e_K)=1/|K|,
```

may flow down the tail without satisfying the finite-matrix exit lower bound.
This clause is load-bearing: an exact trace-functorial first-hit inequality
would contradict `exact-first-hit-ladder-kills-regular-carrier`, while a
finite cycle admits stationary companion-type flows.  The missing theorem is
therefore precisely a finite-matrix-only removal of the regular compensation,
not another subgroup-average identity.

The local rank orientation is now solved.
`central-sign-assignment-atom-is-index-two-reynolds-surplus` proves


and the HNN actuator turns that exact index-two difference into an exit
projection of trace `tr(A_a)`.  The compiler no longer needs surrogate
finite groups or reverse order padding for one forbidden atom.  Its remaining

the two-child/BCS recurrence to keep one active copy from shrinking.  The
canonical regular model must still be allowed to send its companion flow

Local atom transport and finite target orthogonality are also solved.
`index-two-reynolds-atoms-have-orthogonal-hnn-transports`
conjugates both levels of the marked index-two pair, so their Reynolds
difference moves exactly; distinct characters of one elementary-abelian

It does not supply active persistence: literal finite-pair covariance is a

Only one type is needed at a given level.  By

The index-two edge has a stronger local interpretation which is useful for

## Attempts

- **One generic HNN edge dies on regular orientation; the central-sign atom
  is the exceptional aligned case.**  Injectivity `K->H` forces
  `1/|H|<=1/|K|`.  For `H_a<<H_a,J>` this is exactly the useful direction and
  the difference is the forbidden atom.  It supplies one exit, but its
  positive regular trace prevents stationary repetition across arbitrarily
  many orthogonal layers.
- **A finite edge cycle is stationary.**  The companion multiplicities admit
  a nonnegative stationary flow around the cycle; repeating subgroup tables
  does not consume trace.
- **A trace-functorial infinite tail is impossible.**  If its exit lower bound
  held in every finite tracial algebra, applying it to `L(Gamma)` would kill
  the initial carrier by `exact-first-hit-ladder-kills-regular-carrier`.
- **The Baumslag register compresses the whole prefix table, but not
  persistence.**
  `baumslag-binomial-prefix-centralizer`
  derives every translated commutator with earlier lamps from the two rows
  `[v,c]=[v,B_(-1)]=1`, with polynomial normalized-HS cost.  Thus infinite
  address naming and prefix preservation are not the open obstruction.
  `baumslag-address-geometric-stationary-profile` evaluates the
  same rows in unbounded finite-field affine quotients: the first `m` lamps
  are independent and `tau(C_n)=2^(-n)`, so a positive structural BCS payload
  simply decays geometrically.  FHC3 holds while FHC1 fails at zero relator
  defect.  A payload-dependent active-child/proper-corner row must exclude
  this exact stationary profile.
- **Fixed density is not the missing step.**
  `central-sign-reynolds-atoms-have-fixed-canonical-density` gives
  `tau(e_(H_a)Q_-)=2^(-(k+1))` in every canonical microstate.  Therefore the
  live use of the robust non-CE BCS gap must be operator-valued: after
  finite-dimensional packet exactification it must select or charge the
  companion multiplicity in a way unavailable in the exact infinite model.
  Feed that matrix-only selection through the established relative-Pauli
  active/exit split and place the selected companions in the disjoint dyadic
  E5 degree bands.  The first-hit ledger and presentation-error summation are
  already established.
