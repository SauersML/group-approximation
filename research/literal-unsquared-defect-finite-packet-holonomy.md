For every norm-matrix-corona homomorphism from `H=E/<w>`, the image of

```text
q(u),  u=[tct^-1,v_1],
```

is trivial.

More precisely, exactify the selected packet `K~=(C_2)^8`, polar-correct its
`AGL_3(F_2)` covariance, and let `r_a` be the rank of the displacement of the
marked lamp by a generator `a` of the Kazhdan base.  With `W=sum_a r_a`, the
packet spectral decomposition gives exact unnormalized Hilbert--Schmidt
energy `4W`.  The one-sided
compression returns the Kazhdan primitive through the same authenticated
packet carrier and makes that energy `o(W)`.  On a fixed tail the finite
rational certificate is

```text
W >= 0,   4W <= 2W,   therefore W=0.
```

With a nonnegative slack coordinate this is the exact rational relation
`4W+S=2W`, hence `2W+S=0`.

Thus all moved-lamp character types vanish, in particular the type detected
by `v_1`.  The common-carrier return is essential: the character table and
the finite permutation holonomy alone still admit the positive regular
profile.
