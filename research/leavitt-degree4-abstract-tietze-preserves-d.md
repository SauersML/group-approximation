Let

```text
Q=D*F(H,K,L,M),              D=V4(a,b)*D8(c,d).
```

Imposing the four degree-four orbit relators over `Q` gives a group

```text
P isomorphic to D*<H>.
```

Under `Q -> P`, the whole factor `D` embeds.  In particular the target
commutator `[c,d]` remains nonidentity.
