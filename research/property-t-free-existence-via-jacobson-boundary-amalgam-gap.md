The first prerequisite supplies the explicit finitely presented group
`Xi`, its nontrivial head involution `w_Q`, and the exact equivalence
between positive matrix gap and collapse of this mark in every norm
matrix corona. The second prerequisite is the still-open positivity
statement. Together they would prove `1!=w_Q in Rad_MF(Xi)`.

The established reduction uses only finite-group correction and
operator-norm estimates. It contains no invariant-vector estimate for
an infinite group. The earlier refuted two-braid positivity claim is
not a prerequisite; the additional finite boundary packet and TS cell
are part of this stronger presentation.
