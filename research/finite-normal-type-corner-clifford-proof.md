Let `D=z_rho C[K] z_rho`.  The invariant irreducible type makes `D` a full
matrix algebra and every `Gamma/K`-homogeneous fibre a rank-one
`D`-bimodule.  Innerness of automorphisms of `D` supplies fibre generators
centralizing `D`; their products differ from the generator in the product
fibre by a scalar cocycle.  Orthogonality of distinct cosets computes the
canonical trace as matrix trace tensor twisted regular trace.  Since the
obstruction comes from the finite group `K`, its class is torsion and can be
represented by a finite-root-valued cocycle.  This proves the claim; full
details are recorded in
`research/artifacts/finite-type-corner-clifford-collapse-2026-08-21.md`.
