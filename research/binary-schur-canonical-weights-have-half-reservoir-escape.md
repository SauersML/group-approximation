Fix one forbidden selector sector of the binary Schur embedding.  Write

```text
I_x=M_(2D)(k),
M_2(C_x)=M_(2D)(k) direct_sum M_(2D)(k),
phi_x(a)=(a,a).                                         (BCW1)
```

Let `V_x` denote the corresponding complex full-packet irreducible of
dimension `2D`; the two simple target blocks restrict along `phi_x` to two
copies of this same packet type.  For every even multiplicity `m`, there are
exact complex unitary packet models on one Hilbert space `H` such that:

1. the parent `I_x`-module is `m` copies of its natural irreducible;
2. the target two-child module has multiplicities `(m/2,m/2)`;
3. restriction along `phi_x` is unitarily identical to the parent module;
4. the two target central signs have normalized weights exactly `1/2`; and
5. either child is a half-trace reducing subspace whose inclusion into `H`
   and projection back give an exact rectangular return with an unpaid
   complementary half.

Consequently every normalized character moment of every old-packet word is
identical before and after the binary head, while the complete child-center
type vector is the canonical balanced vector.  No inequality using only
these canonical traces or finite packet type weights can distinguish the
same-reservoir return from the half-reservoir escape, let alone charge one
branch by HS relator energy.

The construction applies sectorwise and survives direct sum with the
canonical weights of all forbidden selectors (clear denominators and make
all multiplicities even).  Thus reset-free canonical packet recovery does
not close `binary-schur-toeplitz-one-branch-hs-payment`.  A successful return
cell must authenticate a **multiplicity coordinate across the branch**--for
example a source-full Gram/support scalar, a raw phase-sensitive holonomy,
or an incidence relation not determined by the packet character.  The
canonical type ray itself is exactly stationary under the rectangular split.
