title: "Thompson's T contains a copy of PSL(2,Z) whose elements are determined by two dyadic values and whose centralizers of nontrivial elements of V are amenable"
---

There is a homeomorphism φ of `R ∪ {∞}` onto `R/Z` that conjugates PPSL(2,Z) onto Thompson's
group T (Thurston; Imbert). Here PPSL(2,Z) is the group of homeomorphisms that are piecewise in
PSL(2,Z) with finitely many rational breakpoints. Put `Λ := φ PSL(2,Z) φ^{-1} ≤ T`, so
`Λ ≅ Z_2 * Z_3`. Then:

1. φ maps `Q ∪ {∞}` bijectively onto the dyadic points `Z[1/2]/Z` of the circle, and an element of
   PSL(2,Z) is determined by its values on two distinct points of `Q ∪ {∞}`. So an element of Λ is
   determined by its values on two distinct dyadic points.
2. For every `f ∈ V ∖ {e}`, the centralizer `{g ∈ Λ : gf = fg}` is amenable. It is cyclic when
   `f ∈ Λ` and finite when `f ∉ Λ`.
