Choose `U^(n)` with `E_(d_n)(U^(n))<1/n`.  Their coordinate classes are
unitaries in the tracial matrix ultraproduct; every polynomial residual is
zero in normalized `2`-norm and every target trace is its prescribed
ultralimit.  Conversely, choose unitary representatives of an exact
ultraproduct tuple.  For each `epsilon>0`, simultaneous satisfaction of the
finitely many residual and trace bounds is an ultrafilter-large, hence
nonempty, set of coordinates.  This proves both implications and the
contrapositive uniform gap.
