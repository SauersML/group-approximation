Suppose `phi:A->B` is a counterexample to STW Problem LXXVIII.  Then there
are finitely many elements `b_1,...,b_m in B` such that

```text
B_0=C*(phi(A),b_1,...,b_m)
```

is separable and has no normalized 2-quasitrace, and the corestriction
`phi_0:A->B_0` is still not O-infinity-stable.  More quantitatively, for
some finite `F subset A`,

```text
kappa_(phi_0)(F) >= kappa_phi(F)>0,
```

where `kappa` is the exact central-isometry defect.  Hence LXXVIII has a
counterexample if and only if it has one whose codomain is separable and is
generated by the image together with finitely many elements.
