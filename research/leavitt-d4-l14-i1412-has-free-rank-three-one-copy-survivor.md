The inverse `1;412` class of the degree-four profile
`---++-+-++++++` has an explicit minimal 15-block noncrossing carrier
scheme whose coefficient quotient is the free group

```text
F(P,Q,R).
```

In that quotient the raw inverse boundary is exactly

```text
(Q P^-1)_3.
```

It is a nontrivial word lying in one coefficient copy.  Thus this inverse
class is not fenced by coefficient free-product normal form: unlike the
normal `0;511` class, it contains a genuine free-coefficient survivor.
This is an algebraic carrier/boundary result only; no normalized-HS or
non-hyperlinearity conclusion is asserted here.
