Let `X` be a countable discrete bounded-geometry metric space with property A,
let `A=C*_u(X)`, and let `G` be a finite subset of the ghost ideal of `A`.
For every `epsilon>0`, there are a finite subset `F subset X` and c.p.c. maps

```text
A --psi--> M_F --phi--> A,      M_F=B(ell^2(F)),
```

such that `phi` is a star-homomorphism (hence c.p.c. order zero) and

```text
norm(phi psi(g)-g)<epsilon       for every g in G.
```

The maps are the literal compression and inclusion associated to
`p_F=chi_F`.  Consequently, no finite-packet lower-bound argument whose test
packet consists only of ghosts can force even nuclear dimension one for a
property-A uniform Roe algebra.  Ghost classes also vanish in the uniform Roe
corona.  This does not exclude an obstruction involving ghosts jointly with
non-ghost ambient elements or a genuinely global uniformity condition.
