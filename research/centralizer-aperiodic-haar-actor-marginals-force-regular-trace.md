Let `H` act by automorphisms on a countable discrete abelian group `A`, and
assume

```text
|C_H(g) . a| = infinity                              (CAM1)
```

for every `g != e` and `a != 0`. Let `(Q,tau)` be a finite tracial von
Neumann algebra containing exact unitary families

```text
V_a V_b=V_(a+b),       U_g U_h=U_(gh),
U_g V_a U_g^*=V_(g.a).                               (CAM2)
```

Suppose only the two marginal trace laws are known:

```text
tau(V_a)=1_(a=0),             tau(U_g)=1_(g=e).        (CAM3)
```

Then all mixed coefficients are forced:

```text
tau(V_a U_g)=1_((a,g)=(0,e)).                         (CAM4)
```

Consequently `(a,g) |-> V_a U_g` extends to a trace-preserving normal
embedding

```text
L(A rtimes H) -> Q,                                   (CAM5)
```

with range `W^*(V_a,U_g)`. In particular, if a fixed non-Connes-embeddable
tracial algebra embeds in that generated range, then `A rtimes H` is
non-hyperlinear.

This gives a concrete reduced packet for the native arithmetic-actor branch.
One no longer has to verify infinitely many lamp--actor cross moments. It is
enough to construct exact covariance, Haar Fourier trace on the abelian lamp
group, regular trace on the actor, and the algebraic aperiodicity `(CAM1)`.
Every individual condition in `(CAM3)` is a finite Fourier/word moment.
For a torsion-free actor there is a simpler purely module-theoretic sufficient
condition:

```text
ker(g^n-1:A->A)={0}       for every g != e and n>=1.   (CAM6)
```

Indeed `<g><=C_H(g)`, and `(CAM6)` says exactly that its orbit of every
nonzero lamp is infinite.

There is an explicit `SL_3(Z)`-native testing ground. Take the torsion-free
finite-index principal congruence subgroup `H=Gamma(3)<SL_3(Z)`. For the
regular module `A=direct_sum_H F_p`, `(CAM1)` holds: `C_H(g)` contains the
infinite cyclic group generated by `g`, and no nonzero finitely supported
lamp is periodic under a nontrivial translation. Thus on this packet the two
marginals alone authenticate the full wreath-product trace. The regular
module itself is barren (its semidirect product is sofic); its role is to
show that the mixed-trace gate is genuinely discharged. A successful module
must retain `(CAM1)` while also carrying the nonsplit, non-homoclinically-dense
relations already required by
`algebraic-compact-action-envelope-for-non-ce-relation`.
