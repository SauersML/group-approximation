**OPEN.**  For each fixed context packet `H_c` and every
`lambda in Irr_-(H_c)`, construct a finite menu of ordinary group words whose
finite-dimensional compressed defects satisfy `(EMT2)` in
`exhaustive-marked-type-energy-summation`, with one computable
dimension-independent modulus, while the perfect commuting model extends
exactly and keeps the common central mark nontrivial.

The construction must be genuinely finite-dimensional-only.  In particular
it must not imply the operator inequality `(IEM2)` of
`algebraic-exhaustive-marked-type-detectors-violate-induction`, because the
induced infinite representation is an exact countermodel to every such
algebraic inequality.

A sufficient target is a typewise trace-cyclic or proper-corner return:

```text
tr(Y_lambda X_lambda)-tr(X_lambda Y_lambda)
  = c_lambda tr(F_c Q_lambda)+o(1),                   (FMEA1)
```

where the left side is exactly zero only in finite matrices and the decoded
block maps arise from a fixed ordinary-word actuator.  Since there are only
finitely many marked types, any positive type constants can then be minimized
and `exhaustive-marked-type-energy-summation` removes all alignment loss.

The strongest surviving concrete variant is
`type-filtered-proper-corner-return-actuator`.  It replaces relator energy by
a Fourier-filtered wandering moment.  This avoids the induction firewall:
the finite projection `q_lambda F_c` is only an analytic linear combination,
while the canonical tests remain ordinary nonidentity words.

There is now a second, explicitly global sufficient interface.
`global-marked-type-cycle-selector-decoder` lets one actuator permute the
finite set of marked context types.  Analytic coarse-half and fine-quarter
selectors are linked along the permutation cycles; cyclic trace pressure
charges their total carrier mass despite arbitrary type multiplicities, and
orthogonality of the isotypic projections packs every compressed row back
into one ordinary word menu.  Its two remaining obligations are precisely a
matrix-only domination of the summed compressed rows and global coverage of
the shared BCS forbidden mass.  No per-type preferred carrier is selected.

## Attempts

- **Repeat the balanced SMC row on every marked type.**  The multiplicity
  cancellation is algebraic on infinite induced representations and is
  ruled out by `algebraic-exhaustive-marked-type-detectors-violate-induction`.
- **Use one scalar word or finitely many scalar words.**  Their common zero
  selector set is affine and cannot encode a nonlinear predicate.
- **Select one preferred type.**  This returns to the orthogonal-carrier
  rotation problem and is not exhaustive coverage.
- **Quotient away all rejected types.**  Induction recreates every character
  of the surviving selector subgroup and the required packet semantics are
  lost.
