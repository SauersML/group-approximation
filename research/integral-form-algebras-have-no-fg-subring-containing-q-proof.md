Direct proof. Lane proof, not independently reviewed.

Let `B` be a `Z`-basis of `A`. Since `A` is a ring and the elements of `B` span it, every
product `b b'` with `b, b' ∈ B` is a `Z`-combination of `B`. So `A ⊗ K` is a
`K`-algebra with basis `B` and integral structure constants, and `M_n(A ⊗ K)` has basis
`e_ij ⊗ b` with integral structure constants.

1. **Generators lie in a finitely generated coefficient ring.** Let `g_1, ..., g_r` generate a
   subring `S` of `M_n(A ⊗ K)`. Let `D ⊆ K` be the subring generated by the finitely many
   coefficients of the `g_i` in the basis `e_ij ⊗ b`. Then `M_n(A ⊗ D)` is a subring (the
   structure constants are integral) that contains every `g_i`, so `S ⊆ M_n(A ⊗ D)`. As a
   `D`-module, `M_n(A ⊗ D)` is free with basis `e_ij ⊗ b`.
2. **Some prime is not a unit of `D`.** If `char K = p > 0`, then `p = 0` in `S`, and `S`
   contains no copy of `Q`. Let `char K = 0`. `D` is a finitely generated commutative
   ring and a domain. If every prime were a unit of `D`, then `Q ⊆ D`. A maximal ideal `m`
   of `D` would then give a field `D/m`, finitely generated as a ring. By the
   Nullstellensatz for finitely generated `Z`-algebras, such a field is finite. But
   `Q -> D/m` is injective, because `Q` is a field. That is a contradiction. So there is a
   prime `p` that is not a unit of `D`.
3. **Krull intersection.** `D` is a Noetherian domain and `pD` is a proper ideal, so
   `⋂_k p^k D = 0` by the Krull intersection theorem. Hence `⋂_k p^k F = 0` for every free
   `D`-module `F`, in particular for `F = M_n(A ⊗ D)`, coefficientwise.
4. **A copy of `Q` forces an element divisible by every `p^k`.** Suppose `ι: Q -> S` is an
   injective ring homomorphism, unital or not. Put `e = ι(1)`; it is a nonzero idempotent.
   For every `k`, `e = p^k ι(p^(-k))`, so `e ∈ p^k S ⊆ p^k F`. By step 3, `e = 0`. That is
   a contradiction.

So `S` contains no subring isomorphic to `Q`. ∎

**Remark.** The argument uses only that the finitely generated coefficient ring `D` has
a non-invertible prime `p` with `⋂ p^k D = 0`, and that the ambient ring is free over
`D`. By this claim, the finitely presented ring of
`q-embeds-in-a-corner-of-a-fp-toeplitz-ring`, which contains `Q`, is a finitely generated
subring of no such `M_n(A ⊗ K)`. Its resolvent generator `(N+1)^(-1)` carries the
denominator of every positive integer.
