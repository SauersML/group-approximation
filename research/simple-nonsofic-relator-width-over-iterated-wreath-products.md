**ESTABLISHED** through `deep-wreath-relator-width-from-commutator-width`. Unreviewed.

Let `G = <X | R>` be an infinite finitely presented simple group that is not sofic. For a finite group
`Q` and `a in Q^X`, `w_Q(a)` is the least `K` such that every generator `a_x` is a product of at most `K`
`Q`-conjugates of relator values `r(a)^(+-1)`.

**Claim.** For every `m` there is `K(G, m)` such that `w_Q(a) <= K(G, m)` for every iterated permutational
wreath product `Q = T_1 wr T_2 wr ... wr T_d` of transitive groups `T_i <= Sym(m_i)` with `m_i <= m`, every
depth `d`, and every `a in Q^X`.

**Stronger form proved.** The bound holds for every finitely presented group with no nontrivial finite
quotient, sofic or not, with one fixed balanced template of length
`sum_r |n_(x,r)| + 2|R| (|Phi_m| + m - 1 + 6m (m! + m + 2))`. Here `Phi_m` is the free group on `X` of the
variety generated by `Sym(m)`.

**Payoff.**
- **No weak models in deep wreath towers.** For the binary Leavitt unit group, and every finitely
  presented simple group, towers of bounded degree host no weak models with any bi-invariant lengths,
  at any depth, abelian and solvable layers included.
- **Where a counterexample would have to live.** The deep wreath half of the room on
  `fg-simple-weakly-sofic-groups-are-sofic` is closed for full towers. A counterexample to that rigidity
  claim needs one of:
  - finite targets that are not full towers of bounded degree, such as subgroups of towers (with
    conjugators only from the subgroup);
  - towers with unbounded vertex degrees under non-symmetric tops;
  - linear soficity over finite fields.

**How it was proved.**
- **Template theorem.** `relator-width-over-towers-of-bounded-commutator-width` gives the bound over any
  tower class of bounded degree whose derived subgroups have bounded commutator width. Its ingredients:
  - a balanced template, recursive on coordinates fixed by the root tuple;
  - a relatively free top block;
  - spanning-tree dipoles on each orbit of the root tuple;
  - nested commutators for one defect per orbit.
- **Commutator width.** `transitive-wreath-towers-bounded-commutator-width` proves that bound, `3(m! + m + 2)`,
  for all transitive towers of degree `<= m`. The tools are a base lemma for `[(u; 1), (q; beta)]` and a
  three-for-two absorption at two adjacent coordinates.
- **Special cases proved first.**
  - simple towers, through the classification (`relator-width-bounded-over-simple-iterated-wreath-products`);
  - full-cycle towers (`full-cycle-wreath-towers-bounded-commutator-width`);
  - bounded bases under symmetric tops (`simple-nonsofic-width-over-bounded-base-symmetric-wreath`);
  - nilpotent-by-(locally finite variety) groups (`relator-width-over-nilpotent-by-locally-finite-groups`).

## Attempts (history)

- **Naive level-by-level correction.** Lifting the top width and correcting each layer separately
  grows linearly in depth. The growth is avoided by the recursive balanced template, and by commutator
  width inherited with ratio `2/3`.
- **Lengths that see only supports.** Monotone subadditive functions of supports on all tree levels
  collapse by Lemma 1.2 of `simple-group-support-and-semisimple-rank-models-collapse-proof`, level by level.
- **Lower-bound tool for refutations elsewhere.** `l(g) = log|g^Q|` is a bi-invariant pseudo-length, so
  `w_Q(a) >= log|a_x^Q| / max_r log|r(a)^Q|`.
