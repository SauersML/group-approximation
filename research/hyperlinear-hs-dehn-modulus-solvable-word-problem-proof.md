The trivial-word side is the usual enumeration of consequences of the
relators.  The nontrivial-word side searches the dense computable family
of Cayley transforms of rational skew-Hermitian tuples for a tuple whose
relator defect is below a rational `t` while the marked word is farther
from the identity than the computable modulus `Phi(|w|,t)`; both
quantities are exact rationals.  Length control forbids a trivial word from
ever being found; hyperlinearity, together with `Phi(|w|,.)->0`, forces a
nontrivial word to be found.  Theorem 2.1 of the artifact.
