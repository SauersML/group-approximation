Literature import, not a reproof. Source: C. Anantharaman-Delaroche, *Amenability and
exactness for dynamical systems and their C\*-algebras*, arXiv:math/0005014v2. The source
was downloaded from `https://arxiv.org/e-print/math/0005014v2` on 2026-09-17, and the
statements were read in `Edinburgh.tex`:

- the definition of a transformation group and `P(G)` (lines 124-145);
- Definition 2.1 (lines 150-160);
- the notation `C^*(X⋊G)`, `C^*_r(X⋊G)` (lines 510-516);
- Theorem 3.4 (lines 587-600).

Every item in the target node is copied from these lines, with only the macros expanded.
The "Consequence used downstream" paragraph there only specializes the theorem to
`X = Ω` compact, `G = Γ` discrete and `A = C(Ω)`.
