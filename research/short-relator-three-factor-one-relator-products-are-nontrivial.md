independently reviewed.

**Statement** (Chinyere–Howie, Theorem 1.3). Let `A`, `B`, `C` be nontrivial groups and
`w ∈ A * B * C` a relator of free-product length at most eight. Then
`(A * B * C)/<<w>>` is nontrivial. Equivalently, no element of free-product length at
most eight normally generates a free product of three nontrivial groups.

**Consequence for Gordon's conjecture.** Any counterexample to
`gordon-free-products-of-three-groups-have-normal-rank-above-one` with three factors
needs a relator of free-product length at least nine. For `A_5 * A_5 * A_5`, see
`a5-cubed-relators-of-length-at-most-nine-are-not-normal-generators`.

**Trust surface.** The proof in the source uses its Theorem 5.3 (a proper power `r^n`
with `2 ≤ ℓ(r) ≤ 6`, `n ≥ 2`, has order `n` in the one-relator product of two groups),
whose proof the authors omit as "straightforward but lengthy" and attribute to Chapter 4
of Chinyere's thesis. The thesis was not read.
