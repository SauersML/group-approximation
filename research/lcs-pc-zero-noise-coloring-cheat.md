Let G be the three-colouring game of K4, with uniform ordered vertex
pairs, and let (B,pi) be its TV Definition 3.2 projection BCS. Put
P=G(B,pi). Then

    omega_q(G)<=11/12,
    omega_q(P)<=sqrt(23/24)<1,
    lim_(u->infinity) omega_q(P^tensor-u)=0,
    omega_classical(L^0(u,B,pi))=1 for every u>=1.

Here L^0 is precisely TV Definition 4.1 with the mask fixed to one.
The scalar strategy uses all three colours at each endpoint and a fixed
three-element derangement matching at each edge, then takes their products
over repetitions. It is compatible with every raw query identification.

Consequently the unchanged zero-noise test has no generic decoder with
a positive lower bound on source winning probability at test value one,
uniformly over sources and repetition counts. The inverse-degree selector
loss is exactly 3^{-u} on this global strategy.

This does not refute source-specific perfect-completeness reductions or
the perfect quantum completeness RE-hardness target.
