Dead: the attempt derives summable size-weighted projection norms from the general geometry of an invariant finite-cluster process, using only nonamenability of the ambient graph, equivariance and almost sure finiteness of clusters. A factor of independent labels on T_4 square Z has all three properties, infinite susceptibility, and divergent dyadic norm sum (fpbs-fiid-finite-clusters-do-not-imply-dyadic-summability). Large clusters align along the amenable direction. Any proof must use a Bernoulli-specific input; the estimate itself remains open.
