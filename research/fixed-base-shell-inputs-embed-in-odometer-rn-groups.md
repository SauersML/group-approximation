**ESTABLISHED** through `fixed-base-shell-input-odometer-rn-embedding-proof`
(elementary; not independently reviewed; no novelty claimed).

**Setting.**
- `P` is infinite and generated by a finite set `S`. `nu : P -> N` is an
  enumeration, and `rho_g` are its shell generator permutations
  (`shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`).
- Fix `h >= 2` and `r >= 1`. Let `B = V_(h,r)(<a>)`, with `a` the h-adic
  odometer, acting faithfully on `N` by near permutations as in
  `odometer-prefix-groups-have-fg-index-kernels`.
- Suppose the near class of `rho_g` lies in the image of `B` for every `g` in
  `S`. This is the fixed-base hypothesis of
  `fixed-base-shells-embed-in-contracting-rsg`.

**Statement.**
1. `P` embeds in `B`.
2. Every subgroup of `P` with property FW is virtually free abelian of finite
   rank. Every subgroup of `P` with property (T) is finite.
3. If `P` contains an infinite subgroup with property (T), no enumeration of `P`
   satisfies the fixed-base hypothesis, for any `h` and `r`. This applies to
   Deligne's lattice `Gamma~` (`deligne-universal-cover-lattice-is-non-rf-kazhdan`)
   and to every input containing it.

**Consequence for the shell programme.** The terminated-digit overgroup of
`fixed-base-shells-embed-in-contracting-rsg` gives finitely presented simple
overgroups of shell envelopes of some inputs that are not virtually cyclic, such
as `A_5 * A_5`. Every input it reaches already lies in `V_(h,r)(<a>)`, and it never
reaches an input with an infinite Kazhdan subgroup.

**Scope.**
- **One base only.** Residue-affine regular actions whose slopes and moduli
  involve several primes are not covered. Their markings form a product of trees,
  and the finiteness of short markings in the Kazhdan proof does not transfer, as
  for Brin--Thompson groups. Those arithmetic enumerations of `Gamma~` are not
  excluded.
- Nothing is said about finite presentation of any `E_nu`, or about enumerations
  that do not meet the fixed-base hypothesis.
