**ESTABLISHED (import)** through `golod-shafarevich-deficiency-growth-citation`.

Let `Γ = ⟨X | R⟩` be a finite presentation, `p` a prime, and
`d_p(Γ) = dim_{F_p} Γ/[Γ,Γ]Γ^p = dim_{F_p} H₁(Γ; F_p)`.
1. If `d_p(Γ) > 1` and `|R| < d_p(Γ)²/4 − d_p(Γ) + |X|`, then `Γ` is Golod–Shafarevich with respect
   to `p`, meaning that its pro-`p` completion is a Golod–Shafarevich pro-`p` group.
2. Golod–Shafarevich abstract groups have uniformly exponential growth, in particular exponential
   growth with respect to every finite generating set.
