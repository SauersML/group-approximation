For the Brin–Dehornoy braided Thompson group `bV`, `H_n(bV; Q) = 0` for all
`n ≥ 1`. Consequently every `H_n(bV; Z)` with `n ≥ 1` is a torsion group.
`H_1(bV; Z) = 0` by `braided-thompson-group-bv-is-perfect`.

This is the rational case of Zaremsky Problem 2.9
(`zaremsky-2-09-braided-v-acyclic`). It is not an answer to the problem: the
problem asks for integral acyclicity.

the three artifact parts. Status: unreviewed as of 2026-09-13.
