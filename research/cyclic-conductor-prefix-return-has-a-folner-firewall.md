Consider a family of exact cyclic clock sectors with coordinate projections

```text
e_0,...,e_(q-1),
S e_j S^*=e_(j+1 mod q),
tr(e_j)=c/q,                                         (CCF1)
```

inside a carrier of trace `c>0`.  A proposed finite-prefix Julia return
based only on bounded-radius clock relations faces the following trilemma.

1. **Distinguished prefix.**  A construction supported on `r=o(q)` named
   clock sites has trace at most `cr/q=o(1)`.  It cannot return a
   positive-density mark.
2. **Orbit average.**  Translating a fixed radius-`r` cell around all `q`
   sites restores constant mass, but every normalized trace calculation is
   the average of one bounded local pattern.  As `q->infinity` these
   patterns have the same tracial limit as the bilateral shift action of
   `Z`; equivalently the cyclic models embed in the hyperfinite crossed
   product/clock ultraproduct.  Any scalar return proved from this bounded
   local average is therefore tracially functorial and meets the regular
   firewall.
3. **Wrap detector.**  The only datum distinguishing the finite cycle from
   the bilateral shift is the global wrap.  A literal product following it
   has length at least `q`; direct HS telescoping costs `q Def`.  A single
   wrap edge has mass `c/q`.  Turning it into a constant normalized charge
   requires a separate constant-sound PCP/expander or square-function
   theorem; cyclic first-hit bookkeeping alone does not provide one.

The statement is stable under using a finite number of ancilla colors or a
fixed Julia colligation at each site: these only enlarge the bounded local
alphabet and do not change the Følner boundary/volume calculation.

Hence an unbounded **amenable cyclic** conductor does not close
`julia-authenticated-high-spectrum-rank-selector`.  The user's recursive
error-spreading idea requires genuinely non-Følner authentication: a
constant-gap global verifier, a nonamenable expanding actor tied to the same
mark, or another mechanism which makes the global wrap syndrome extensive.
This claim does not rule out any of those mechanisms.
