**ESTABLISHED (imported, with an elementary step).** Let `S` be finite and `R` a finite
set of cyclically reduced words in `S^(+-1)`, none a proper power, such that the
presentation `<S | R>` satisfies the classical `C'(1/6)` condition. Then:
1. the presentation complex of `<S | R>` is aspherical;
2. `G = <S | R>` is torsion-free;
3. `G` is word-hyperbolic.

*Source.* D. Gruber, *Groups with graphical C(6) and C(7) small cancellation
presentations*, arXiv:1210.0178, TeX fetched on MSI. Quotations in
`cprime-one-sixth-hyperbolic-torsion-free-citation`.
