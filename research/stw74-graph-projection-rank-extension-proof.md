Let `q in M_k(D(X;r))` be a projection.  Since `X` is connected, the rank of
the continuous projection field

```text
x |-> q(x) in M_(kN)
```

is one constant integer `d`.  At a vertex there is a projection
`a_i in M_k(C)` with

```text
q(v_i)=a_i tensor e_i.
```

Writing `alpha_i=rank(a_i)`, a nonzero `q` therefore gives

```text
d=alpha_i r_i,                 1<=alpha_i<=k             (all i).   (GL1)
```

Thus `d` is a positive common multiple of all the `r_i`, so `d>=L`.  For an
index at which `r_i` is minimal, `(GL1)` yields

```text
k>=alpha_i=d/r_i>=L/min_j r_j=m_0.                       (GL2)
```

Conversely suppose `k>=m_0`.  At each vertex choose `a_i in M_k(C)` of rank
`L/r_i`.  Then every vertex projection `a_i tensor e_i` has the same rank
`L` in `M_(kN)`.  Any two equal-rank projections in a full matrix algebra
are unitarily conjugate, and the unitary group is path connected.  On each
closed edge of `X`, choose a unitary path joining its two already prescribed
vertex projections.  The resulting projection paths agree at common
vertices, so they glue to a continuous projection field on the whole graph.
It belongs to `M_k(D(X;r))` and is nonzero.  This proves `(GL)`.

At level one, `(GL1)` forces every `alpha_i=1`, so a nonzero projection exists
exactly when all `r_i` are equal.  Hence unequal vertex ranks make `D(X;r)`
projectionless.

For a first-level projection `P`, the coefficient algebra `C_P` is a C-star
subalgebra of the `N`-subhomogeneous algebra `D(X;r)`, hence is
subhomogeneous and remains projectionless.  Its defining coefficients
generate all matrix entries of the ideal generated by `P`; consequently
`P` is full in `M_(m_0)(C_P)`.  The full corner
`P M_(m_0)(C_P) P` is unital and Morita equivalent to `C_P`, so
`Prim(C_P)` is compact.  Finally, a projection in `M_j(C_P)` for `j<m_0`
would be one in `M_j(D(X;r))`, contradicting `(GL)`.  The coefficient
algebra therefore retains the exact graph threshold.
