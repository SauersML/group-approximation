For each modular vertex, the three-projection formula identifies cuspidal
cohomology with the kernel of parabolic restriction and identifies its
orthogonal complement with the positive singular subspace of `P_u|K`.
The congruence triple-angle gap therefore gives a right inverse of parabolic
restriction of norm at most `1/(2c)`, uniformly in the coefficient module.
The opposite vertex is conjugate, so the same constant, up to a fixed
generator-norm equivalence, applies to both groups in
`iwahori-parabolic-boundary-splitting-is-uniform`.  The edge is used only as
the common target of the two restrictions and needs the total fixed-complex
Hodge norm comparison, not its own interior/Eisenstein lift.
