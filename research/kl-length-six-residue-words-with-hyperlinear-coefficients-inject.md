Let `G` be any group and `w` a word of one of the fourteen normalized residue
shapes of `kl-length-six-coincidence-saturated-torsion-residue`:
- `+++++-`: `(1,1,1,1)`, `(1,1,1,g)`, `(1,1,g,1)`, `(1,g,1,1)`, `(g,1,1,1)`,
  `(1,1,g,g)`, `(1,g,1,g)`, `(1,g,g,1)`;
- `++++--` (N-label `1`): `(1,1,g)`, `(g,1,1)`, `(1,g,g)`, `(g,g,1)`, `(g,1,g)`,
  `(g,g,g)`.

Here `g != 1` is the label, and `y` and `x` are the loop coefficients at `t^-`
and `t^+`. Put `A = <g, x, y> <= G`.

**(a) Hyperlinear coefficients.** If `A` is hyperlinear, then
`G -> (G * <t>)/<<w>>` is injective. More generally, any word of variable length
six whose coefficients generate a hyperlinear subgroup injects its coefficient
group, whether or not it is normalized.

**(b) Involution loops.** Suppose `x^2 = y^2 = 1` and `g in <x, y>`. Then
`A = <x, y>` is a quotient of the infinite dihedral group, hence hyperlinear,
and `w` injects. This holds for all fourteen shapes, every such `g`, and does
not depend on whether `x` and `y` commute or on the order of `x y`.

**(c) Finite or amenable loop groups.** For loops of any orders, if `g in <x, y>`
and `<x, y>` is finite or amenable, then `w` injects.

## Consequences for the involution residue

With both loops involutions, the case of the Leavitt search, combine (b) with
`kl-three-equal-residue-shapes-inject-for-every-loop-order`. That node leaves
open, for the four three-equal `+++++-` shapes, only the relations
`g = x` or `g = y` (the named loop), `g = y x`, and `g x g^(-1) = y`. The first
two families lie in `<x, y>`, and so does the third whenever `g in <x, y>`.

| Shapes | Open with involution loops, after this node |
|---|---|
| `(1,1,1,1)` | none (`kl-two-coefficient-residue-word-with-involution-loops-injects`) |
| `(1,1,1,g)`, `(1,1,g,1)`, `(1,g,1,1)`, `(g,1,1,1)` | only `g x g^(-1) = y` with `g` outside `<x, y>` |
| `(1,1,g,g)`, `(1,g,1,g)`, `(1,g,g,1)` | only `g` outside `<x, y>` |
| all six `++++--` shapes | only `g` outside `<x, y>` |

So a length-six Kervaire--Laudenbach violation with involution loops needs:
- a label `g` outside the dihedral group `<x, y>`;
- `A = <g, x, y>` not hyperlinear, and by `kervaire-laudenbach-holds-for-mf`
  not MF either;
- for the three-equal shapes, in addition `y = g x g^(-1)`, so `A = <x, g>` is
  generated by an involution and one further element.

**What this does not reach.** Every group generated by an involution and one
further element is a candidate `A` in the conjugate-relation case, and this
node says nothing when `A` is not known to be hyperlinear. The substitution
`t -> s g^(-1)` carries `(1,1,1,g)` with `y = g x g^(-1)` to
`(s g^(-1))^3 s^2 x s^(-1) x`, whose loops are both `x`; renormalizing by
`s -> r g` returns the original word, so it is not a reduction.
