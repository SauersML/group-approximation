A nontrivial finite-dimensional unitary representation of the infinite
simple finitely generated group is injective.  Malcev's theorem would then
make the group residually finite, impossible for an infinite simple group.
Thus every finite-dimensional group representation is trivial.  Hulanicki's
criterion says that the trivial representation is weakly contained in the
regular representation exactly for amenable groups, so it does not descend
to the reduced algebra here.  Hyperrigidity identifies boundary
representations of the Harris system with representations of its amplified
reduced C-star envelope, completing the claim.
