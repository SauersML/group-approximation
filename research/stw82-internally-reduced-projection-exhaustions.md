Let `A` be a C-star algebra and let `p in M(A)` be a projection.  Suppose
there are increasing projections `Q_n in M(A)` such that

```text
Q_n -> 1 strictly,       [Q_n,p]=0,       pQ_n in A.     (IRE1)
```

Then

```text
dim_nuc(A+C p)=dim_nuc(A).                               (IRE2)
```

The condition is an exact block-diagonal condition, not a quasicentrality
condition.  Put `A_n=Q_n A Q_n`.  The internal compression `pQ_n` lies in
`A_n`, while the complementary tail

```text
r_n=p(1-Q_n)
```

annihilates `A_n` on both sides.  Consequently the algebra generated by
`A_n` and `p` is `A_n direct_sum C r_n` (with the scalar summand omitted
when `r_n=0`).  These algebras are nested because

```text
r_n=r_(n+1)+p(Q_(n+1)-Q_n),
```

and the second term belongs to `A_(n+1)`.  Their union is dense in
`A+C p`.  Hereditary-subalgebra and inductive-limit permanence give the
upper bound in `(IRE2)`, while the ideal inclusion `A ideal A+C p` gives
the reverse bound.

For a concrete module form, let `B` be unital, decompose a separable
infinite-dimensional Hilbert space as a finite-dimensional block sum

```text
H=direct-sum_(j>=1) H_j,
```

and choose arbitrary projections `e_j in M_(dim H_j)(B)`.  The strict
block sum

```text
p=direct-sum_(j>=1)e_j in M(B tensor K(H))
```

satisfies `(IRE1)` for the cumulative block projections.  Hence

```text
dim_nuc((B tensor K(H))+C p)=dim_nuc(B).                 (IRE3)
```

This is strictly larger than constant coefficient form.  Already for
`B=C`, taking one-dimensional blocks and alternating `e_j=1,0,1,0,...`
gives an infinite-rank, infinite-corank diagonal projection in `B(H)`.  It
is not unitarily equivalent to either constant coefficient projection
`0` or `1`, while `(IRE3)` says that `K(H)+C p` has nuclear dimension zero.
