Artifact Sections 1–4.

1. **Simplicity (Lemma 2).** Take a nonzero `x` of minimal support in an ideal and shift its support to contain
   `e`. For `c ∈ K`, `cx - xc = sum a_g (c - σ_g(c)) u_g` has smaller support, so it vanishes. Faithfulness forces
   `x ∈ K^x`. With `rank-condition-rings-have-weakly-finite-images` (the only nonzero image of a simple ring is
   itself) the rank condition equals weak (= stable) finiteness.
2. **Stably finite implies the rank condition (Lemma 1).** Pad `AB = I_d` to square matrices; the zero rows of
   `B'` contradict `B'A' = I_d`.
3. **Locality (Lemma 4).** A matrix identity lives in `K*H`, where `H` is generated by the supports.
4. **Finite index (Lemma 5).** `K*G` is a free left `K*N`-module on right coset representatives. Right
   multiplication gives an injective unital homomorphism `K*G -> M_n(K*N) -> M_n(S)`.
5. **Division rings (Theorem 6).** Item 2 of `fsp-graph-of-rings-and-linnell-extension-imports` (Jaikin-Zapirain,
   Selecta Math. 27 (2021), Thm 1.1, verbatim in its citation route) gives a Hughes-free division ring for
   `K*N` over residually torsion-free nilpotent, locally indicable amenable and free-by-cyclic `N`. Item 6 gives
   one for graphs of such groups. Matrices over a division ring are directly finite.
6. **Boundary (Lemma 8).** A finite-index subgroup of an infinite finitely generated Kazhdan group is Kazhdan
   (Bekka–de la Harpe–Valette, Thm 1.7.1), hence has finite abelianization and is not locally indicable
   (`atiyah-kazhdan-edge-blocks-routes-proof`).
