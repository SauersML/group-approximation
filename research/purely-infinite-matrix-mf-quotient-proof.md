Write E = EL_n(R), C = [G,G], and N for the stated MF-hom kernel.

1. Pure infiniteness supplies s,t with ts=1 and e=1-st nonzero
   (`exists_unitProperIsometry`). Simplicity supplies a finite identity
   sum a_i e b_i=1. The prerequisite therefore says that every homomorphism
   from E to an MF group is trivial. Restricting any G -> M to E gives E <= N.
   It also proves the intrinsic full MF radical of E, which is stronger than
   the assertion about restrictions of homomorphisms from G.

2. Every nonzero element of R sandwiches to 1
   (`exists_sandwich_of_isPurelyInfiniteSimpleRing`). Rank-n Gaussian
   elimination over such rings writes every A in G as P diag_l(u) Q, with
   P,Q in E. Diagonal conjugation preserves E, so E is normal. Thus G/E is
   generated by the image of the homomorphism u -> diag_l(u). Whitehead's
   two-coordinate identity places diag_l([u,v]) in E for every pair of units,
   so this image is abelian. Hence C <= E. The complete elimination,
   conjugation, and Whitehead proofs are the three KOne artifacts above;
   `RankNElimination.commutator_le_elementaryGroup` assembles them. A second
   coordinate is exactly why n >= 2 is required.

3. G is countable, so its abelianization is countable abelian and MF.
   The latter assertion is proved internally by residual finiteness of
   finitely generated abelian groups, followed by the LEF-to-MF theorem.
   Abelianization is consequently one of the homomorphisms defining N,
   and its kernel is C. Thus N <= C.

The three inclusions give C = E = N and transfer E's intrinsic full MF
radical to C. The quotient isomorphism G/N -> G/C identifies the quotient
with a countable abelian MF group. Every G -> M kills N=C and therefore
descends to G/C; surjectivity of the quotient map makes the descent unique.

The argument uses neither the AGP unit K1 theorem nor the two named AGP
steps. It does use the full-defect prerequisite and its property-(T) proof.
It supplies no property-(T)-free result, rank-one classification, or K1
identification.
