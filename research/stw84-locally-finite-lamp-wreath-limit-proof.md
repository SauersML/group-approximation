## The exact finite-stage parameter

The imported analytic input is Eckhardt--Wu,
[*Nuclear dimension and virtually polycyclic
groups*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2025.110768), Theorem 5.14.  For every
finite-dimensional C*-algebra `A` and every finitely generated virtually
nilpotent group `V`, their Bernoulli-shift estimate is

```text
dim_nuc(A^(tensor V) rtimes V) <= 2 * 9^d(V),           (2)
```

where `d(V)` is the degree of polynomial growth of `V`.  Crucially, the
right side is independent of the number and sizes of the matrix summands of
`A`.

For a finite group `F`, the finite-coordinate identifications

```text
C*(direct_product_E F) = tensor_E C*(F)
```

are compatible as the finite set `E subset V` grows.  Since `C*(F)` is
finite dimensional and hence nuclear, the resulting group algebra of
`direct_sum_V F` is the minimal infinite tensor product `C*(F)^(tensor V)`.
The shift actions agree under this identification.  As `V` and the lamp base
are amenable, full and reduced crossed products agree, and therefore

```text
C*(F wr V) = C*(F)^(tensor V) rtimes V.                (3)
```

This argument uses no commutativity of `F`: `C*(F)` may be any multimatrix
algebra arising from a finite nonabelian group.  Equations (2) and (3) give
the bound `2*9^d(V)` uniformly over all finite lamp groups `F`.  No
Hirsch-length conversion is needed for the primary theorem.

## Simultaneous exhaustion

Enumerate `L` and `H`.  Let `L_n` be the subgroup generated by the first `n`
elements of `L`, enlarging indices if necessary, and define `H_n` similarly.
Local finiteness makes every `L_n` finite.  Every `H_n` has polynomial growth
and `d(H_n)<=D`; Gromov,
[*Groups of polynomial growth and expanding
maps*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02698687), makes `H_n` virtually nilpotent,
exactly as required in (2).  Inside `L wr H`, put

```text
G_n=L_n wr H_n=(direct_sum_(H_n) L_n) rtimes H_n.
```

Extension by zero on the lamp coordinates gives compatible inclusions
`G_n subset G_(n+1)`.  Given `(f,h) in L wr H`, the support of `f` and its
image are both finite.  A sufficiently large single index `n` therefore
contains `h` and `supp(f)` in `H_n`, and contains `f(supp(f))` in `L_n`.
Thus `(f,h) in G_n`, proving the literal group equality

```text
L wr H = union_n G_n.                                  (4)
```

Equations (2) and (3) give

```text
dim_nuc(C*(G_n)) <= 2 * 9^d(H_n) <= 2 * 9^D            (5)
```

for every `n`.  Each `H_n` is virtually nilpotent and hence amenable, so their
directed union `H` is amenable.  The base `direct_sum_H L` is locally finite:
a finite set uses finitely many coordinates, and in each coordinate its
values generate a finite subgroup of `L`.  Hence `L wr H`, an extension of
an amenable group by an amenable group, is amenable.

For completeness, restriction of the left regular representation to any
subgroup is a direct sum of copies of that subgroup's regular
representation.  Thus every map `C*_r(G_n)->C*_r(L wr H)` is isometric, and
the union of its images is dense by (4).  Amenability identifies all full and
reduced group algebras, so (4) is an injective C*-inductive limit.  Now (5)
and `stw84-directed-union-uniform-bound` prove (1).

If `D=0`, every finitely generated subgroup of `H` has growth degree zero and
hence is finite; thus `H` is locally finite.  The class of
locally finite groups is closed under direct sums and extensions: a finitely
generated subgroup has finite image in the locally finite quotient, and its
finite-index intersection with the locally finite kernel is finitely
generated and hence finite.  Therefore `L wr H` is locally finite and its
group C*-algebra is AF, giving nuclear dimension zero.

## Recover the Hirsch-length version

Polynomial-growth degree and Hirsch length are unchanged on passing to
finite-index subgroups and after discarding finite torsion in a finitely
generated nilpotent group.  If `a_i` are the dimensions of the successive
homogeneous pieces of the rational Malcev Lie algebra, the Bass--Guivarc'h
formula (see Bass,
[*The degree of polynomial growth of finitely generated nilpotent
groups*](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-25.4.603)) says

```text
d(V)=sum_i i*a_i,              h(V)=sum_i a_i.          (6)
```

For nilpotency class `c`, every degree from `1` through `c` is nonzero, so
`c<=h(V)`.  Therefore

```text
h(V) <= d(V) <= c*h(V) <= h(V)^2.                     (7)
```

The finite case has `d(V)=h(V)=0`.  Thus a uniform local Hirsch bound `r`
implies `D<=r^2`, and (1) yields (2) from the claim.  Conversely, (7) shows
that a uniform degree bound implies a uniform Hirsch bound.  The two
boundedness conditions therefore define the same acting-group class, but
`D` is the exact parameter in the imported analytic estimate.

Finally take `L=direct_sum_N A_5` and `H=Q`.  Every nontrivial finitely
generated actor subgroup is infinite cyclic and has growth degree one, so
`D=1` and (1) gives `18`.  Restricting lamps to any order-two
subgroup of `L` and the actor to `Z subset Q` embeds `(Z/2) wr Z`.  This
subgroup is finitely generated but is not virtually polycyclic: its base
`direct_sum_Z Z/2` is not finitely generated, whereas every subgroup of a
virtually polycyclic group is finitely generated.  Hence `L wr Q` is not
locally virtually polycyclic, and its infinite nonabelian lamp group shows
that the theorem is strictly stronger than the finite-lamp result it
replaces.
