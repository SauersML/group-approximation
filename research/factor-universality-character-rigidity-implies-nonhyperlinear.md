Suppose a countable group `H` has both properties below.

1. Every separable tracial von Neumann algebra embeds trace-preservingly in
   a finite factor `Q=pi(H)''` generated by a representation of `H`.
2. Every extremal character of `H` is either the regular character or the
   character of a finite-dimensional factor representation.

Then `H` is nonhyperlinear.  This implication is sound, but it does not
combine with the Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana theorem: their source class is
acylindrically hyperbolic, and such groups fail property 2 by their proper
infinite ICC quotients.
