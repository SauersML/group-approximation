First verify that the displayed actor is a genuine unit.  With
`Q=1-ST`, the relations

```text
Q^2=Q,          QS=0,          TQ=0,          TS=1
```

give

```text
[[S,Q],[0,T]] [[T,0],[Q,S]] = I_2
 = [[T,0],[Q,S]] [[S,Q],[0,T]].                         (BAQ4)
```

Thus `a` is invertible.  The established balanced-cycle generation theorem
also places it in `EL_3(J)`.

The finite scalar group `K` contains, in the same three-coordinate block,

```text
x_12(1),             x_23(1),             x_31(1).
```

The balanced-cycle theorem now gives

```text
<a,x_12(1),x_23(1),x_31(1)>=EL_3(J).                    (BAQ5)
```

Hence `<K,a>` contains every `J)-labelled elementary root in that
three-coordinate block.  Scalar permutation matrices belong to `K`.
Conjugating by them moves a fixed ordered pair of the block to any ordered
pair among the 28 coordinates, without changing its coefficient in
characteristic two.  Therefore `<K,a>` contains every `x_ij(r)) with
`r in J` and `i!=j`, so it contains `EL_28(J)`.  The reverse
containment is immediate from `K<=EL_28(J)` and `a in EL_28(J)`.  This
proves `(BAQ2)`.

The injective coefficient map `J->L_(F_2)(1,2)` and the outer 27-leaf
module isomorphism carry this equality to the literal Leavitt host.  By the
one-chart theorem, the scalar group `K` there is generated by the
transitive qutrit packet and the matched roots `e_12(s_0),e_21(t_0)`.
Thus no abstract finite packet was substituted for the concrete qutrit cell.

Apply the symbol map in `(BAQ3)).  It sends the upper-left block of `a`
to `diag(z,z^(-1))`, which has infinite order over the Laurent polynomial
ring.  Hence `a` itself has infinite order.  The same symbol map gives the
rank-28 exact sequence

```text
1 -> GL_fin(N x {1,...,28},F_2)
  -> EL_28(J)
  -> EL_28(F_2[z,z^(-1)]) -> 1.                         (BAQ6)
```

The left term is the infinite simple locally finite finitary group, and
`x_ij(Q)` is a nonidentity element of it.

Finally let `phi:EL_28(J)->L` have finite image.  Restrict `phi` to a
five-coordinate copy of `EL_5(J)` containing a chosen head root.  The
finite-quotient invisibility theorem kills that root.  Scalar permutation
conjugacy then kills every `x_ij(Q)`.  Since such a root is nontrivial,
`EL_28(J)` is not residually finite.  Every finitely generated virtually
free group is residually finite, so `<K,a>` cannot be a finite amalgam
fallback.  The argument makes no claim about Hilbert--Schmidt microstates;
that is precisely the boundary recorded in the claim.
