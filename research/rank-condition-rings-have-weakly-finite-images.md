**ESTABLISHED** (artifact Section 3, Lemma C.1 and Theorem C). For a unital ring `A`, these are
equivalent:
1. `A` satisfies the rank condition (no `A^(n+1)` is a retract of `A^n`);
2. `A` has a nonzero weakly finite homomorphic image.

When they hold, a maximal ideal `J` among those with `A/J` satisfying the rank condition gives `W = A/J`
with these properties:
- `W` is nonzero and weakly finite;
- every idempotent matrix of class `0` in `K_0(W)` is zero;
- every state on `(K_0(W), [W])` is strictly positive on nonzero idempotent matrices.

**Key lemma.** Killing the ideal generated by the entries of idempotent matrices of class `0` (or of
value `0` under a state) preserves the rank condition.
- Suppose a failure `YX = I + UẼV` holds modulo that ideal.
- Then `[[X],[ẼV]]` and `[Y, -UẼ]` exhibit `A^(n+1)` as a retract of `A^n ⊕ ẼA^R`.
- That contradicts the rank condition, since `[Ẽ] = 0`.

This may be known (P. Malcolmson's work on weakly finite images; not read locally, unverified).

**Consequence.** By `anti-central-state-obstruction-equals-stable-finiteness` and
`twisted-leavitt-corner-fullness-equals-absence-of-k0-states`, the ternary swap corner is full iff
`eps_- F_3[G_3]` has no nonzero weakly finite image. That is `anti-central-rank-condition-gives-weakly-finite-image`
and `ternary-anti-central-summand-has-no-weakly-finite-image`.
