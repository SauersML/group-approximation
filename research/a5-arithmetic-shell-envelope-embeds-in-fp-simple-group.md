Let E_nu be the specific bijective-base-59 shell envelope of
P=A_5*A_5. Then E_nu embeds in the full contracting RSG H_(59,4)
of the terminated-digit construction, and hence in a finitely
presented simple group.

This embeds the whole envelope, including its neighborhood kernels,
not only its input P or its isotropy germ quotient Q. The question
whether E_nu itself is finitely presented remains OPEN. This is
one restricted positive family and does not settle Boone--Higman.
