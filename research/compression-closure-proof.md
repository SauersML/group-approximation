## Why sufficient

Soundness of `Delta_comp`: the normal-Kazhdan family lies in the radical
by `normal-kazhdan-defect-non-mf` (formalized as the intrinsic
normal-Kazhdan radical theorems); the projection family lies in the
radical by `compression-torsion-collapse` for group-element finite-order
witnesses and by `zero-dimensional-spectral-collapse` for the general
zero-dimensional case; and a subgroup generated by subgroups of
`Rad_MF(H)` lies in `Rad_MF(H)`.

Given soundness for every countable group, each transfinite stage is a
sound-rule iterate in the sense of `sound-rule-transfinite-closure`
(formalized as `IsSoundIterate` in
`GroupApproximation/Sofic/MFRelationClosure.lean`): the successor stage
pulls back a subgroup of `Rad_MF(H/N_alpha)`, hence is a subgroup of the
MF closure of `N_alpha`, and unions are the `sSup` constructor.  The
three displayed conclusions are exactly the soundness, completeness, and
saturation clauses of that claim.
