**Data.** Fix a finite symmetric generating set `S` of `G`. For each `s in S`
and `x in X`, record the letter `s(x)` and a word `sigma(s,x) in S*` with
`s|_x = sigma(s,x)` in `G`. Put `c0 = max |sigma(s,x)|`, at least `1`.
`V_d(G)` is generated by the finitely many Higman--Thompson generators of
`V_d` and by `S` acting at the root. Any other finite generating set changes
only the constant `c` below.

**Representation.** An element is a finite list of triples `(u, v, w)` with
`u, v in X*` and `w in S*`, where `{u_i}` and `{v_i}` are complete prefix
codes and `f(u_i zeta) = v_i w_i(zeta)`.

**Refinement.** Replace `(u, v, w)` by the `d` triples
`(u x, v w(x), w|_x)`, `x in X`.
- **Letter image.** `w(x)` is computed letter by letter from the table.
- **Section word.** For `w = s_1...s_m`, with `y_m = x` and
  `y_j = s_(j+1)(y_(j+1))`, the section word is
  `sigma(s_1,y_1)...sigma(s_m,y_m)`, of length at most `c0*m`.
  Time `O(m)`.
- **Range refinement.** Refining at a range letter `y` uses the domain letter
  `w^(-1)(y)`, which is computable because `S` is symmetric.

**Composition.** To compose `f` after `h`, refine the range code of `h` and the
domain code of `f` to a common refinement, then match `(u, v, w)` in `h` with
`(v, q, w')` in `f` into `(u, q, w'w)`.

**Bounds.** Each generator has a representation of depth at most a constant
`D`, with decorations of length at most `1`. Inductively, after composing `l`
generators:
- every code word has length at most `D*l`, so there are at most `d^(D*l)`
  triples;
- each one-letter refinement multiplies a decoration's length by at most
  `c0`, and each composition adds the lengths of two decorations. So all
  decorations have length at most `(c0+1)^(D*l) * l`.

The whole list is built in time `2^(c1*l)`.

**Identity test.** `f = id` iff `u_i = v_i` and `w_i = 1` in `G` for every
triple.
- **Codes agree.** If `f = id`, then for each `i` the cylinder `[u_i]` equals
  the image of `[u_i]`, which is `[v_i]`, because `w_i` is a surjective
  automorphism of `X^omega`. So `u_i = v_i`.
- **Decorations trivial.** Then `w_i(zeta) = zeta` for all `zeta`. A tree
  automorphism fixing every end fixes every vertex, and `G <= Aut(T_d)` acts
  faithfully. So `w_i = 1` in `G`.
- **Converse.** Immediate.

This takes at most `d^(D*l)` word-problem calls in `G`, on words of length at
most `2^(c1*l)`, which gives `(RN1)` for a suitable `c`.

**Subgroups.** A finitely generated `K <= V_d(G)`, generated by elements of
length at most `L` over the generators of `V_d(G)`, reduces to `V_d(G)` by
substitution: length `L*l`, time `O(L*l)`. That changes only `c`.

**The two directions of the payoff.**
- **Upward.** A word over `G` is a word over `V_d(G)`. If `G <= [V_d(G),V_d(G)]`
  or `G` lies in any finitely presented simple overgroup, the same substitution
  reduces `WP(G)` to that host's word problem.
- **Downward.** If `WP(G)` is in `F(g)`, then `(RN1)` puts every finitely
  generated subgroup of `V_d(G)` in
  `F(n -> 2^n (g(2^n) + 2^n))`. Here `c` is absorbed by monotonicity:
  `2^(c l) <= 2^(n)` once `n = c*l`.

`∎`
