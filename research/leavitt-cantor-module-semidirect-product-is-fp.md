**ESTABLISHED (2026-09-13)** through `leavitt-cantor-module-fp-via-thompson-amalgam`
(not independently reviewed). Let `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)` with generators `s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1`
(`t_i s_j = δ_ij`, `s_0 t_0 + s_1 t_1 = 1`). Let `M = C(C, F_2)`, the locally constant
`F_2`-valued functions on `C = {0,1}^N`, with `R` acting by
`(s_i f)(a w) = δ_(a,i) f(w)` and `(t_i f)(w) = f(i w)`. Let `U = R^×` act on `M`
through this representation. Then `M ⋊ U` is finitely presented.

## Routes

- `leavitt-cantor-module-fp-via-thompson-amalgam`:
  - `M ⋊ V` is the centralizer in `V` of the sheet-swapping involution. So it is of type
    `F_∞` by Martínez-Pérez–Matucci–Nucinkis Corollary 4.11
    (`cantor-module-thompson-v-product-is-finitely-presented`).
  - `U` is generated by `V` and the transvection `x = 1 + s_(00) t_(01)`
    (`thompson-steinberg-presentation-of-leavitt-unit-group`).
  - The amalgam `(M ⋊ V) *_V U` modulo six relators `x χ_w x^(-1) = x·χ_w` is `M ⋊ U`.
    - The subgroup of `V` acting by one element on both cones `00`, `01` and by another on
      the cone `1` commutes with `x`.
    - It carries the six cones onto every generator of `M`. So the relators make `M` normal.
    - The quotient by `M` is `U`, and `M` injects.

## Consequence

With `orbit-finite-affine-actions-are-type-a`, the route
`leavitt-pbh-via-cantor-module-affine-actor` gives `binary-leavitt-unit-group-satisfies-pbh`.

## What is known around it

- `U` is finitely presented (`leavitt-unit-group-finitely-presented`); it is a retract of
  `M ⋊ U`.
- `M` is generated as a `U`-group by one element `χ = χ_[0]`, because the copy of
  Thompson's `V` inside `U` (`leavitt-cylinder-swaps-generate-thompson-in-el`) acts on
  indicators of clopen sets by precomposition and is transitive on proper nonempty
  clopen sets, and indicators of clopens span `M`.
