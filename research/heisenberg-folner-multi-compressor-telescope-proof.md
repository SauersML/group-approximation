Index copies of an arbitrary coefficient representation by a finite right
Følner set of the endomorphism semigroup and let each compressor act by the
partial inverse right translation, completed arbitrarily on boundary blocks.
Covariance is exact in the interior and costs at most twice the square root
of the boundary fraction.  Products of compressor permutations agree on the
common prefix interior, proving all fixed semigroup relations asymptotically.
For `u_13,u_32`, use the Heisenberg normal form and the boxes `(AMC8)`; direct
coordinate counting gives `(AMC9)` and hence the joint `O(L^(-1/2))` rate.
