**Theorem C (split torsion symmetric bound).** Let a countable amenable `G` act
on a compact metrizable `X`. Let `L_1, ..., L_s` be line bundles on `X` with
`x_j = c_1(L_j) ∈ Ȟ^2(X; Z)` and `x_j^2 = 0`. Suppose that for every `(G_0, δ)`
there are a `(G_0, δ)`-invariant `F` and an `r` such that the elementary
symmetric polynomial `e_{sr}((T_g^* x_j)_{g ∈ F, j <= s})` is nonzero in
`Ȟ^{2sr}(X; Z)` and `2sr/|F| > d - δ`. Then `rc(C(X) ⋊ G) >= m`, where `m` is
the greatest integer `< d/2`.

No rational coefficients are needed. Nonvanishing may be checked mod `p`.

**Theorem D.** Let `K_p = Π_p / A` be a quotient of the Pontryagin surface. It
has `dim = dim_{Z/p} = 2`, `dim_Q <= 1`, and an integral class `x` with
`x̄ != 0` mod `p` and `x ⊗ Q = 0`. For the minimal subshift
`X = X(K_p^s, ρ, η) ⊂ (K_p^s)^Z` of `rational-witnesses-miss-torsion-mean-dimension`,
with density `ρ_inf`:

* `mdim(X) = 2sρ_inf`;
* `mcid(σ|X; Q) <= mdim/2`, so both Hirshberg--Phillips rational bounds give at
  most `mdim/4`;
* `mdim/2 - 1 <= ⌈sρ_inf⌉ - 1 <= rc(C(X) ⋊ Z) <= mdim/2`, the upper bound by Niu.

So on the systems where the rational obstruction applies, Phillips--Toms holds
up to an additive `1`. The obstruction is exactly the kernel of the Chern
character, and it does not obstruct the conjecture.

**Mechanism.** Hirshberg--Phillips use `Q` only to realise a class `η` as
`c(E) = 1 + η`. Here we take `E = ⊕_j L_j`, which gives
`c(⊕_g T_g^* E) = Π_{g,j}(1 + T_g^* x_j)`. Because `x_j^2 = 0`, its inverse is
`Π(1 - T_g^* x_j)`. The top component of that inverse is `± e_{sr}`. By
Künneth over `Z/p`, `e_{sr}` restricts to `x̄^{× sr} != 0` on the unconstrained
coordinates.
