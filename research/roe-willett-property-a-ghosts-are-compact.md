title: Property A makes every uniform Roe ghost compact
---

Let `X` be a bounded-geometry discrete metric space.  If `X` has property A,
then every ghost operator in `C*_u(X)` is compact.  Equivalently, the ghost
ideal of `C*_u(X)` is exactly `K(ell^2(X))`.

This is an established theorem, imported through
`roe-willett-ghostbusting-citation`.
