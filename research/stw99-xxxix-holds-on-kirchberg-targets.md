Every action (no outerness assumed) of a countable discrete amenable
group on a Kirchberg algebra is equivariantly `O_∞`-stable, hence
equivariantly `Z`-stable.  So STW Problem XXXIX holds on purely
infinite targets, and — via `stw99-xxxix-implies-xxxviii`'s
mechanism — every such crossed product is `Z`-stable.  The
conjecture's open content is exactly the stably finite side, where
the equivariant-glueing gate (gates atlas, gate 3) sits.
