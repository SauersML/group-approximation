title: Finitely generated groups of polynomial growth are virtually nilpotent, and solvable groups grow polynomially or exponentially
---

**Statement.**
1. (Gromov) A finitely generated group of polynomial growth has a nilpotent subgroup of finite
   index; conversely virtually nilpotent groups have polynomial growth.
2. (Milnor–Wolf) A finitely generated solvable group has exponential growth unless it is
   virtually nilpotent.
