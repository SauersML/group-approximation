**OPEN.** Let

```text
Q=L_(F_2)(1,2)^x
```

and let `(H,phi,pi)` be any purely singular state-form near representation
with regular character as in `binary-leavitt-hyperlinear-iff-singular-near`.
Set

```text
A=C^*(pi(Q)),
tau=phi|_A,
I_tau={a in A:tau(a^*a)=0}.
```

By `invariant-free-near-state-is-amenable-regular-cover`,

```text
A/I_tau ~= C^*_r(Q).                                    (BLL1)
```

Prove that the quotient map in `(BLL1)` is locally liftable: for every
finite-dimensional operator system `E subset C^*_r(Q)` there is a u.c.p.
map

```text
s_E:E -> A,          q o s_E=id_E.                      (BLL2)
```

This is strictly weaker than making `pi` genuine or proving normalized-HS
stability. It permits all multiplicative defects to remain in `I_tau`; it
asks only that finite operator systems of the regular quotient can be lifted
through the particular singular cover generated by the near witness.

A positive proof would rule out the witness entirely: `tau` is amenable
upstairs, and local liftability would make the regular trace amenable
downstairs. Since `Q` is nonsofic and every amenable group is sofic, `Q` is
nonamenable, so its reduced regular trace cannot be amenable.
