Convention: `Rad_MF(Gamma)` is the intersection of the kernels of all
homomorphisms from `Gamma` to MF groups. Equivalently, it is the set of
elements that every operator-norm asymptotic unitary representation sends
asymptotically to `1`.

**Push-forward lemma.** Let `phi : Gamma -> Lambda` be any homomorphism.
Then `phi(Rad_MF(Gamma)) <= Rad_MF(Lambda)`.

*Proof.* Take `psi : Lambda -> M` with `M` MF. Then `psi o phi` is a
homomorphism to an MF group, so it kills `Rad_MF(Gamma)`. Intersect over
`psi`.

This lemma has two special cases:
- for a surjection, it is the image statement;
- for an inclusion `Gamma <= Lambda`, it gives `Rad_MF(Gamma) <= Rad_MF(Lambda)`.

**(a)** If `R_0 <= R_1`, the quotient `q : Gamma_(R_0) ->> Gamma_(R_1)`
sends `zhat` to `zhat`. The lemma gives `zhat in Rad_MF(Gamma_(R_1))`.

The canonical map `Gamma_(R_0) ->> Delta` sends `zhat` to `z`. So a
certificate gives `z in Rad_MF(Delta)`.

For the converse, suppose `Rad_MF(Delta) = Delta`. Then
`full-mf-radical-has-fp-cover-same-generators` gives finitely many relators
`r_1, ..., r_s` in `ker pi` with full radical on the same generators, so
`{r_i}` is in `Cert`. This applies in our case by item 2 of
`leavitt-st20-t-free-holes-hold-via-kazhdan`, which states
`Rad_MF(Delta) = Delta`.

(Only the full-radical case of the converse is needed below. That is the
case of `Delta`.)

**(b), Kazhdan cofinality.** Item 1 of
`leavitt-st20-t-free-holes-hold-via-kazhdan` gives `Delta ~= R^x`.
`binary-leavitt-unit-group-is-simple` gives `R^x = GL_3(R) = EL_3(R)`.
`R` is a finite-type `ZMod 2`-algebra, generated by `s_1, s_2, t_1, t_2`.
So `elementary-group-property-t-over-free-algebras` makes `Delta` Kazhdan.

Let `mu` be uniform on `S` and `Delta_mu` its Laplacian. By
`property-t-laplacian-sos-certificate` there are `kappa > 0` and
`xi_1, ..., xi_r` in `R[Delta]` (real coefficients) with

```text
Delta_mu^2 - kappa Delta_mu = sum_j xi_j* xi_j .
```

Lift each group element in the support of each `xi_j` to a word in `F(S)`.
This gives `xihat_j` in `R[F(S)]`. Form `Delta_mu` in `R[F(S)]` by the same
formula in the letters of `S`, and put

```text
a = Delta_mu^2 - kappa Delta_mu - sum_j xihat_j* xihat_j   in R[F(S)] .
```

Then `pi(a) = 0` and `a` has finite support `A`. For each `g` in `pi(A)`
fix `w_g` in `A` with `pi(w_g) = g`. Put

```text
R_T = { w_(pi(w))^-1 w : w in A }   (finite, contained in ker pi).
```

For each `g`, the coefficients of `a` over the fibre `A cap pi^-1(g)` sum
to `0`, because `pi(a) = 0`. So

```text
a = sum_(w in A) a_w (w - w_(pi(w))) .
```

Each difference `w - w_(pi(w))` vanishes in `R[Gamma_(R_0)]` as soon as
`R_T <= R_0`. So the certificate identity holds verbatim in
`R[Gamma_(R_0)]`, for the push-forward of `mu`, which is again finitely
supported, symmetric and generating.

The converse half of `property-t-laplacian-sos-certificate` makes
`Gamma_(R_0)` Kazhdan. In particular, for any certificate `R_c`, the
certificate `R_c u R_T` is Kazhdan, by (a).

**(b), non-Kazhdan certificates.** Over `S' = S u {t}` with `pi'(t) = 1`,
a set `R_0` contained in `ker pi` lies in `ker pi'` and presents
`Gamma_(R_0) * <t>`.

The inclusion `Gamma_(R_0) <= Gamma_(R_0) * Z` and the lemma give
`zhat in Rad_MF(Gamma_(R_0) * Z)`. The free product surjects onto `Z`, so it
is not Kazhdan.

**(c)** This follows from (b). The certificates `Gamma_(R_0) * Z` realise
every property stable under the operation `Gamma -> Gamma * Z`. The
certificates `Gamma_(R_0 u R_T)` realise every property of Kazhdan quotients
of `F(S)`.

In each case, existence depends only on `Cert` being nonempty, which is the
(T) theorem.

**(d)** Take `phi : Gamma -> Delta` with `phi(Rad_MF(Gamma)) != 1`.
- By the lemma, `Rad_MF(Delta)` contains `phi(Rad_MF(Gamma)) != 1`.
- `Rad_MF(Delta)` is normal, and `Delta` is simple by item 1 of
  `leavitt-st20-t-free-holes-hold-via-kazhdan`.
- So `Rad_MF(Delta) = Delta`.
- `Rad_MF(Gamma) != 1` says that `Gamma` is not MF.

The equivalence in the definition of Kazhdan-blind is standard: property (T)
passes to quotients, so the image of a Kazhdan group is a Kazhdan subgroup.
Haagerup groups are Kazhdan-blind by the second fact recalled in
`thompson-v-has-haagerup-property`.

**(e)** This restates (d). The absence claim is a record of this graph, not
of the literature. No node here certifies a non-MF group without an infinite
Kazhdan subgroup.

No computation is needed. Every step is a finite algebraic manipulation or a
cited theorem.
