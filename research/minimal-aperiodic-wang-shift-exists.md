**ESTABLISHED (import).** There is a set `U` of 19 Wang tiles whose Wang shift `Omega_U ⊆ U^{Z^2}` has three properties:
- it is nonempty and aperiodic, meaning no tiling has a nontrivial period;
- it is minimal as a `Z^2`-subshift;
- it is a nearest-neighbour subshift of finite type, being defined by colour matching on contiguous edges.

Consequently `Z^2` acts freely and minimally on the Cantor set `Omega_U`.

The source is J. Labbé, *A self-similar aperiodic set of 19 Wang tiles*, arXiv:1802.03265, Theorem 1: "The Wang shift `Omega_U` is self-similar, aperiodic and minimal." The TeX source was read on MSI. The conventions are in the artifact, Section 1.
