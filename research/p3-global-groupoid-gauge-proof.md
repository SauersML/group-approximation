Record the scalar factor set of every composable pair of native chart
intertwiners.  Associativity of three operator products is exactly the
groupoid two-cocycle identity `(FGA2)`, so there is no residual associator.
Construct the inverse twisted left-regular action on the finite arrow fibres.
Its multiplier is `alpha^(-1)` by direct basis calculation.  Tensoring the
two projective functors cancels their multipliers on every composable pair,
and therefore on every word and every chart loop.  The preceding two-chart
naturality theorem identifies the native isotropy classes needed to regard
all arrows as one projective groupoid system.
