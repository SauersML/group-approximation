**ESTABLISHED PROTECTED WORDIZATION.**  Fix the finite adaptive depth and
choose characteristic three as in
`odd-characteristic-adaptive-tree-wordization`.  Let `B` be the independent
Pauli seed involution.  Add to the finite coefficient presentation

```text
[B,q_sigma]=0,             [B,d_(sigma,i)]=0            (PBT1)
```

for every named node and edge.  The shared BCS letters, and hence every
assignment projection `P_i`, already commute with `B`.  Use the split finite
packet action on the Steinberg group: an external packet unit `g` acts by
`x_rs(r) |-> x_rs(grg^(-1))`.  This is a finite semidirect-product
presentation, and its projection onto the external packet proves that the
packet remains injected.  Inductively
`q_(sigma i)=q_sigma+d_(sigma,i)` commutes with `B`, so every source

```text
a_(sigma,i)=q_sigma P_i,
Z_(sigma,i)=x_13(a_(sigma,i))                           (PBT2)
```

is fixed by conjugation by `B`.  Do **not** impose
`[B,s_(sigma,i)]=0`: the Murray--von Neumann arrow is allowed to carry a
source from one `B`-sector to the other, while its source and range
projections remain `B`-reducing.

For a forbidden type `i`, let `H_i` be its finite Reynolds two-group and put

```text
K_i=<H_i,J,B>.                                          (PBT3)
```

This is a finite two-subgroup of the injected context/Pauli packet.  The
coefficient identities defining the `H_i`-atom, centrality of `J`, and
`(PBT1)` make the corresponding inner coefficient actions fix
`a_(sigma,i)`.  Hence the split external packet action makes
`Z_(sigma,i)` commute with `K_i`.  Every named `Z_(sigma,i)`
has exact order three.  Consequently

```text
K_i times <Z_(sigma,i)> times <ell>                     (PBT4)
```

is an injected direct product: the order-three factor has trivial
intersection with the finite two-group, and `ell` is a fresh independent
central involution.

For each named source attach a binary-tetrahedral factor by the finite
amalgam

```text
K_i times <Z_(sigma,i)> times <ell>
        --> K_i times (Q8 semidirect <y_(sigma,i)>),
y_(sigma,i)=Z_(sigma,i),                               (PBT5)
```

where the center of `Q8` is `ell`.  Bass--Serre normal form at every step
embeds the preceding group, the new finite factor, and all previously
attached factors.  In particular all attached packets centralize both `J`
and `B`; including `J` in `(PBT3)` is necessary, since protecting only `B`
would not preserve centrality of the marked sector across the amalgam.

The added coefficient relations are consistent in every odd
characteristic.  On a countably generated space split the basis into
infinite `B=+1` and `B=-1` pieces.  Recursively choose every `q_sigma` and
`d_(sigma,i)` to be diagonal for this split, with `d_(sigma,i)` orthogonal
to `q_sigma`, and use unrestricted `0,1` partial bijections for
`s_(sigma,i),sbar_(sigma,i)`.  A finite tree needs only finitely many
infinite coordinate reservoirs; sibling ranges may also overlap because no
cross-sibling orthogonality is imposed.  Thus each prescribed source can be
kept nonzero, and the exact-order proof survives the characteristic-three
base change.  The relations `(PBT1)` are finite integral polynomial
relations, so rank-five context absorption still gives a finite ordinary
group presentation.

The protected presentation has the intended canonical accounting.  The
subgroup generated by `J,B,ell` is `C_2^3`, and in the canonical trace

```text
e_0=(1-J)/2 (1+B)/2 (1-ell)/2,
tau(e_0)=1/8.                                           (PBT6)
```

The shared BCS tuple and every attached BTB packet commute with `e_0`.
The Pauli mate `C`, which exchanges the two `B`-halves, is used to calibrate
the seed and is not one of the generators in the carrier-reducing hull.
Hence the common reducing hull used at initialization is exactly `e_0`, not
the full common negative sector.  Every transport word commutes with the
central signs `J,ell`, and every structural packet reduces their joint
negative sector

```text
c=(1-J)/2 (1-ell)/2,             tau(c)=1/4.            (PBT7)
```

Thus every subsequently reached carrier remains below `c`.  It need not
remain in the `B=+1` half: the unprotected arrow letters may switch
`B`-sectors.  This is precisely why `(PBT6)` supplies a factor-two budget
rather than an impossible fixed `1/8` cap.

The perfect tracial model also extends.  All forbidden source roots map to
one; retain the old action of each `K_i`, send `ell` and the
binary-tetrahedral part of every new vertex factor to one, and use the
universal property of the iterated amalgams.  The old marked involution
remains nontrivial.

This closes the protected-commutation and finite-wordization prerequisite
of `canonical-btb-finite-depth-escape-tree`.  It does not prove the overlap
loss `(CBR3)`: after initialization a packet hull may enlarge either
`B`-sector, and no relation above forces the conditioned E5 fiber to land
outside its current analytic carrier.
