The synchronization theorem makes every raw-packet commutant unitary
asymptotically commute with the transported carrier.  The established Haar
witness produces one such unitary with squared commutator energy at least
`3/8-o(1)`.  The two statements are incompatible, excluding a hyperlinear
microstate sequence for the atlas group.
