Let `G` be a word-hyperbolic group acting properly and cocompactly by isometries
on a CAT(0) cube complex `X`. Then `G` has a finite-index subgroup `F` such that
`X/F` is a special cube complex. In particular `G` is virtually special, and so
by Haglund–Wise it virtually embeds in a finitely generated right-angled Artin
group.
