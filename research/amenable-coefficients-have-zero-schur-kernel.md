Let `w in Q * <t>` be nonsingular, with `Q` countable, and let `B` be the
subgroup generated by its coefficients. If every twisted group von Neumann
algebra `L_omega(B)` embeds in a tracial ultraproduct of matrix algebras,
then `K_2(Q, w) = 0`. In particular this holds when `B` is amenable.

**Consequences.**

* A Schur kill needs a non-amenable coefficient subgroup, and one with a
  non-Connes-embeddable twisted group algebra.
* The killed class must be invisible in the second homology of every finite
  quotient of the coefficient subgroup.
* For `sp4-schur-kernel-meets-the-deligne-triple-class`, the coefficient
  subgroup `B <= Sp_4(Z)` must be non-amenable, and the restricted
  multiplier's twisted algebra `L_(alpha|B)(B)` must be non-Connes-embeddable.
  Products like `SL_2(Z) x SL_2(Z)`, whose Schur multiplier is seen by a
  finite abelian quotient, are excluded.
