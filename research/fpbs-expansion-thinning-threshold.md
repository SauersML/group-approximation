title: Positive edge expansion bounds the independent bond threshold by one over one plus expansion

Section 9, Lemma 4. Every infinite connected locally finite graph of edge expansion h>0 satisfies p_c<=1/(1+h). This concerns independent percolation on the specified graph and does not assert a uniqueness threshold gap.
