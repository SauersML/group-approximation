If all six internal coefficient identities are visible in `Q`, the short
atlas theorem excludes the disk.  Otherwise the complete long-residue
classification leaves only internal lengths `(2,4,4,4,4,11)` with outer
length one.  The odd-core theorem says no planar cubic ribbon map has that
designated face data.  These cases are exhaustive, so no area-ten kernel
disk exists.
