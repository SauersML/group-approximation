**Literature input.**  G. Elek and E. Szabó, *On sofic groups*, J. Group
Theory 9 (2006), 161--171; arXiv:math/0305352v1 (25 May 2003).

> **Theorem 1.** The class of sofic groups is closed under the following
> constructions:
>
> 1. direct products, subgroups, inverse limits, direct limits,
> 2. free products,
> 3. certain extensions: if `N ◁ G`, `N` is sofic and `G/N` is amenable
>    then `G` is also sofic.

Soficity here is the paper's Definition 1.2 (existence of
`(F, epsilon)`-quasi-actions on finite sets for every finite `F` and
`epsilon`), which is the permutation definition used throughout this graph.
Item 1 covers arbitrary direct products (the proof projects onto a finite
subproduct injective on `F ∪ {1}`).

Several nodes already quote clause 3 inline, for example
`centralizing-hnn-over-conjugate-basis-is-always-sofic`. This node gives the
theorem a claim of its own so that routes can require it.
