The upper root subgroup generated by `u` is the additive group `F_p`.
Conjugation by `h` sends `u(x)` to `u(4x)`, and the projective order of `h`
is exactly `m_p=ord_p(4)`: `h^k` is scalar precisely when `4^k=1`.
The diagonal and unipotent subgroups intersect trivially in `PSL_2(F_p)`,
which proves `|H_p|=p m_p`.

For odd `p`,

```text
|PSL_2(F_p)|=p(p^2-1)/2.
```

This gives the equality in `(CIB2)`. Since `m_p<=p-1`, its index is at least
`(p+1)/2`. The dimension formula for finite-group induction gives `(CIB4)`.

The even-Weil fixed-line compression supplies, for unbounded prime levels,
an exact global `Q_p` atom, a codimension-one near-extension after deleting
its fixed line, and a one-line restoration. The cubic residuals have
absolute constant Frobenius energy by the two residue-class calculations
already summarized in `bs14-flexible-cross-packet-boundary-reconciliation`.
Comparing the one-line native restoration with `(CIB4)` proves the claimed
unbounded inefficiency.
