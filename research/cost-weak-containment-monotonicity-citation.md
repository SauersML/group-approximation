Published dependency: Kechris, Corollary 10.14 of reference [7] of Abért–Weiss arXiv:1103.1063v2,
quoted in their §1 for finitely generated groups and free actions. The groupoid-cost form is
Abért–Weiss Theorem 9. If `b ≺ a` for free p.m.p. actions of a finitely generated group, then
`C(a) ≤ C(b)`. Constancy on weak-equivalence classes follows by applying it in both directions.

This route imports the result; it is not an independent derivation. Earlier text attributed the
monotonicity to Abért–Weiss Theorem 9 and omitted finite generation; it was corrected after
gk-vf-positive's trust note (artifact Section 5).
