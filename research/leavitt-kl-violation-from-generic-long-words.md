The brute-force hope: once the length-five fence is passed, sample long
nonsingular words with varied Leavitt coefficients and look for a collapse,
on the theory that more variable occurrences give more room for a
coefficient-killing identity.

Dead: `kl-small-cancellation-words-inject-every-coefficient-group`.  From
ten variable occurrences on, a word whose cyclic syllable sequence has no
exact repetition of about a sixth of its length satisfies relative `C'(1/6)`
and injects every coefficient group, the Leavitt units included.  Length adds
room only for nearly periodic words.
