**REFUTED ROUTE.** Use the first two mixed-direction St3 rows with
nonconstant coefficients,

```text
[x_13(e),x_32(e)]=x_12(ee),
[x_13(e),x_32(f)]=x_12(ef).
```

Keeping the two right-hand-side products as nuisance monomials makes the
quotient rank vanish: arbitrary fine maps are absorbed by the independently
typed products `ee,ef`. See
`atlas-first-composable-mixed-pair-has-zero-quotient-rank`.
