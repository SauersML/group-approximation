For every action of a countable discrete amenable group on a Kirchberg
algebra, the (reduced = full) crossed product is `Z`-stable — indeed
`O_∞`-stable.  So the purely infinite companion of STW Problem
XXXVIII is closed; with `stw99-xxxix-holds-on-kirchberg-targets` and
`stw99-li-holds-for-kirchberg-targets` and the Gabe--Szabo
classification (`stw99-liii-holds-on-kirchberg-targets`), the entire
noncommutative-dynamics cluster XXXVIII/XXXIX/LI/LIII displays the
same shape: purely infinite half solved, stably finite half = the
equivariant glueing gate.
