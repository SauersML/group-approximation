Citation import, not a reproof. Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX source of
E. Godelle and L. Paris, arXiv:1007.1365, Math. Z. 272 (2012). Parts read: the introduction.
Not read: the proofs in Section 2.

Verbatim from the introduction:

- "Theorem 2.2. The natural embedding Ω(Γ_T) ↪ Ω(Γ) admits a retraction
  π_T : Ω(Γ) → Ω(Γ_T)."
- "a straightforward consequence of the existence of this retraction is the following
  result, which, curiously, was unknown before: if T ⊂ S and Γ is of type K(π,1), then
  Γ_T is also of type K(π,1) (see Corollary 2.4)."

Here `Ω(Γ)` is the simplicial complex of Charney–Davis and Salvetti, which the introduction
says "has the same homotopy type as E = E_Γ".

The corollary is also used, cited as [godelle2012k, Corollary 2.4], in the proof of
Corollary 7.3 of J. Huang, arXiv:2305.16847.
