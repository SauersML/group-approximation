title: Bounded exponent and unbounded finite subgroup orders imply fixed price one
root: true

Theorem 7.1 consequence. Every countably infinite bounded-exponent group with finite subgroups of arbitrarily large cardinality has cost one for every essentially free pmp action. The finite subgroups need not be nested or normal.
