title: A joint central-sequence Jiang--Su bridge forces pairwise approximate uniqueness
---

Let `B` be unital, let `phi,psi:Z->B` be unital embeddings, and write

```text
B_infinity = ell_infinity(N,B) / c_0(N,B).
```

Regard `phi(Z)` and `psi(Z)` as constant sequences.  If there is a unital
homomorphism

```text
rho:Z -> B_infinity intersect phi(Z)' intersect psi(Z)',
```

then `phi` and `psi` are approximately unitarily equivalent in `B`.

This is a pairwise absorption criterion.  It does not assume that `rho(Z)`
commutes with all of `B`, nor that `B` is `Z`-stable.
