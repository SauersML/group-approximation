## 1. Stabilizers and their reductions

- **Vertex stabilizers.** The vertex `(a,b)` is the class of the lattice `diag(t^a,t^b,1)·O^3`,
  `O = F_q[[1/t]]`. So `Γ_(a,b) = Γ ∩ diag(t^(a_i)) SL_3(O) diag(t^(a_i))^(-1)`: the entry `g_ij`
  lies in `t^(a_i - a_j) O ∩ F_q[t]`, the polynomials of degree `<= a_i - a_j`.
- **Cell stabilizers.** The action is type-preserving, so a cell is fixed pointwise by its
  stabilizer, which is the intersection of the vertex stabilizers. Its entry bounds are
  `d_ij(σ) = min_(v∈σ) (a_i(v) - a_j(v))`.
- **Shape.** Since `a >= b >= 0` on `Q`, `d_ij >= 0` for `i < j`. For `i > j`, `d_ij >= 0` only when
  `a_i = a_j` on all of `σ`, and then `d_ij = d_ji = 0`. So `Γ_σ` is block upper triangular with
  Levi blocks over `F_q` and upper entries of degree `<= d_ij`.
- **Reduction.** `SL_3(A)` is generated by elementary matrices because `A` is finite, so
  `Γ -> SL_3(A)` is onto. The image `π(Γ_σ)` is the set of block upper triangular matrices in
  `SL_3(A)` with Levi entries in `F_q` and upper entries in `V_(d_ij)`.
  - Every such matrix lifts: lift the upper entries to polynomials of degree `<= d_ij` and keep the
    constants.
  - The determinant is the product of the Levi determinants, which lies in `F_q ⊂ A`, so the lift
    has determinant 1.
- **Order.** `|π(Γ_σ)| = |S(Π_blocks GL_(n_k)(F_q))| · Π q^(min(d_ij+1, D))`, the product running
  over the upper block entries.

## 2. The quotient complex

- By (S) and §1, the cells of `X` are the `Γ`-translates of cells of `Q`, with stabilizers `Γ_σ`.
- `Γ(f)` is normal in `Γ` with `Γ/Γ(f) = SL_3(A)`. So the `Γ(f)`-orbits of cells over `σ` are
  `Γ(f)\Γ/Γ_σ ≅ SL_3(A)/π(Γ_σ)`, and there are `N(σ)` of them.
- **Rational comparison.** `X` is contractible and `Γ(f)` acts with finite stabilizers, fixing
  cells pointwise.
  - In the equivariant cohomology spectral sequence, `E_1^(p,q) = Π_(σ∈Σ_p) H^q(Γ(f)_σ;R)`
    vanishes for `q > 0`.
  - The row `q = 0` is the cellular cochain complex of `Y` with trivial orientation coefficients.
  - So `H^*(Γ(f);R) = H^*(Y;R)` (Brown, *Cohomology of Groups*, Ch. VII; chapter-level pointer, not
    re-read).
- **Integral classes.** With `Z` coefficients the same row gives an edge map
  `H^2(Y;Z) -> H^2(Γ(f);Z)`, compatible with extension of scalars. Since `Y` is homotopy
  equivalent to a finite complex (§3), `H^2(Y;Z) ⊗ R -> H^2(Y;R)` is onto. So integral classes with
  nonzero real image exist whenever `b_2 > 0`.

## 3. Collapsing the strips

**Setup.**
- `D = deg f` and `m(d) = min(d+1, D)`. A cell's image is determined by the triple
  `(d_12, d_13, d_23)` and the Levi flags:
  - `L_12`: `a = b` on the whole cell;
  - `L_23`: `b = 0` on the whole cell.
- For a face `ε ⊂ τ`, `π(Γ_τ) ⊆ π(Γ_ε)`. They are equal exactly when the flags agree and the
  `m`-values agree on the non-Levi entries.
- **Collapse principle.** Suppose `ε` has no coface except `τ` in the current subcomplex, and the
  two images are equal. Then each lift of `ε` in `Y` lies in exactly one lift of `τ`, so all lifts
  collapse simultaneously.

**The strip.** Fix `R >= max(1, 2D-2)`. Write `u_b = (R,b)` and `w_b = (R+1,b)`. The new cells of
`Q_(R+1)` are:
- vertices `w_b` for `0 <= b <= R+1`;
- edges `ε_b = w_b w_(b+1)`, `f_b = u_b w_b` and `e_b = u_b w_(b+1)`, for `0 <= b <= R`;
- triangles `τ_b = {u_b, w_b, w_(b+1)}` for `0 <= b <= R`, and `σ_b = {u_b, u_(b+1), w_(b+1)}`
  for `0 <= b <= R-1`.

**Step 1: `τ_b` through `ε_b`.**
- `ε_b` lies only in `τ_b`.
- Triples: `τ_b` gives `(R-b, R, b)` and `ε_b` gives `(R-b, R+1, b)`. No flags hold for either.
- The images are equal since `m(R) = m(R+1)`, i.e. `R >= D-1`.

**Step 2: `σ_b`.** Now `e_b` and `f_(b+1)` each lie only in `σ_b`.
- Triples: `σ_b` gives `(R-b-1, R, b)`, `e_b` gives `(R-b, R, b)`, and `f_(b+1)` gives
  `(R-b-1, R, b+1)`. No flags hold.
- For `b <= R-D`, collapse through `e_b`: `m(R-b) = m(R-b-1)`.
- For `b > R-D`, collapse through `f_(b+1)`: here `b >= D-1` because `R >= 2D-2`, so
  `m(b) = m(b+1)`.

**Step 3: the new vertices.** The remaining new edges are `f_0..f_(R-D+1)` and `e_(R-D+1)..e_R`.
Each `w_b` lies on exactly one of them: `f_b` if `b <= R-D+1`, and `e_(b-1)` otherwise.
- **`w_b` through `f_b`.** Triples: `(R-b, R, b)` for `f_b` and `(R+1-b, R+1, b)` for `w_b`. Flag
  `L_23` holds for both exactly when `b = 0`, and `L_12` for neither. The images are equal since
  `R-b >= D-1`.
- **`w_b` through `e_(b-1)`.** Triples: `(R+1-b, R, b-1)` for `e_(b-1)` and `(R+1-b, R+1, b)` for
  `w_b`. Flag `L_12` holds for both exactly when `b = R+1`, and `L_23` for neither. The images are
  equal since `b-1 >= R-D+1 >= D-1`.

**Conclusion.** `Y_(R+1)` collapses onto `Y_R`, and `Y` deformation retracts onto
`Y_(R_0)`. Therefore `χ(Y) = χ(Y_(R_0)) = 1 - b_1 + b_2`, which gives the formula.
