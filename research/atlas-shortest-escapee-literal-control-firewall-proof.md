# Exact word expansion and the literal-control parabolic

All matrix strings below are row-major hexadecimal encodings of `4 x 4`
matrices over `F_2`.  Direct expansion of the three rank-five dictionary
words in `st3_135_11` gives

```text
x_13(1) = (1,8461)(2,8621)(1,8461)(2,8621),
x_35(1) = (2,8461),
x_15(1) = (1,8461).
```

Substitution into

```text
[x_13(1),x_35(1)] x_15(1)^(-1)
```

and free-product reduction gives

```text
(1,8461)(2,8621)(1,8461)(2,8241)(1,8461)
(2,8621)(1,8461)(2,8461)(1,8461),                    (SLP1)
```

which is `(SLC1)`.  Write these three matrices as `a,b,c`.  Reading their
entries gives

```text
a = I+E_21,        b = I+E_12,        c swaps e_1 and e_2
```

(indices start at zero).  Thus `a^2=b^2=c^2=1`, and direct multiplication in
the displayed `2 x 2` block gives

```text
c=aba,             (ab)^3=1.                          (SLP2)
```

There are five first-chart occurrences, all equal to `a`, so `p_1=a^5=a`.
The second projection is

```text
b c b a = b(aba)ba = (ba)^3=1.                       (SLP3)
```

The first occurrence of `b_2` has four first-chart letters to its right,
`c_2` has three, the second `b_2` has two, and `a_2` has one.  These are all
the inversions.  Grouping equal pairs gives multiplicities `6,3,1`, proving
`(SLC2)`.

For unitaries `A,B`, right multiplication by the unitary `BA` shows

```text
||ABA^*B^*-1||_2 = ||AB-BA||_2.                       (SLP4)
```

The emitted control `orth_15_35_11` is exactly `[a_1,a_2]`; `(SLP4)` proves
`(SLC3)`.

It remains to identify what the other literal controls with first entry `a`
can generate.  Their exact expansions are

```text
orth_15_34_11 : [a_1,(8431)_2],
orth_15_35_11 : [a_1,(8461)_2],
orth_15_43_11 : [a_1,(8423)_2],
orth_15_45_11 : [a_1,(8425)_2].                      (SLP5)
```

On the last three coordinates these four matrices are respectively

```text
I+E_23,       I+E_21,       I+E_32,       I+E_31.
```

The first and third generate the lower-right `GL_2(F_2)`, while the second
and fourth generate its normal translation module `F_2^2`.  Hence they
generate `F_2^2 : GL_2(F_2)`, of order `4*6=24`.  Equivalently this is the
stabilizer in `GL_3(F_2)` of the nonzero covector `e_1^*`, namely the
parabolic `P` in the claim.  The second row of `b=8621` is `0110`, and that
of `c=8241` is `0010`, rather than the required `0100`; consequently

```text
b notin P,             c notin P.                    (SLP6)
```

Finally

```text
[A,B_x B_y]=[A,B_x]B_y+B_x[A,B_y]
```

for the additive commutator, so unitary invariance and the triangle
inequality give `(SLC4)`.  Iterating it reaches precisely words in the
subgroup generated by the available second-chart letters, which is `P`.
By `(SLP6)` it cannot reach the `b`- or `c`-terms in `(SLC2)`.  This proves
the stated literal-control firewall and nothing stronger; longer controls
may carry operator information not present in `(SLP5)`.
