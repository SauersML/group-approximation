Let `φ` be a free minimal action of `Z^N` on a Cantor space `X`, with `N ≥ 1`. `Z^N` is infinite, so it has at least four
elements.

**Item 1 (⇒).** Suppose `D([[φ]])` is finitely generated. It contains every 3-cycle, being generated by commutators and
containing `[t_1, t_2]` for any two clopen exchanges (Step 3 of
`free-action-fg-alternating-full-groups-force-expansivity-proof`). By item 1 of
`free-action-fg-alternating-full-groups-force-expansivity`, `φ` is conjugate to a subshift `X_P ⊆ P^{Z^N}`.
Conjugacy preserves freeness and minimality, so `X_P` is a free minimal subshift.

**Item 1 (⇐).** If `φ` is conjugate to a free minimal subshift, then `D([[φ]])` is finitely generated by
`minimal-z2-subshift-derived-full-groups-are-fg-simple` (Chornyi–Juschenko–Nekrashevych, stated there for all `Z^d`).
Conjugate systems have isomorphic full groups.

**Item 2.** A finitely presented group is finitely generated. So by item 1, `φ` is conjugate to a free minimal subshift
`X_P`, and `D([[X_P]]) ≅ D([[φ]])` is finitely presented. By `zd-derived-full-group-fp-forces-finite-type`, `X_P` is of
finite type.

**Item 3.**
- `N = 1`: every nonempty `Z`-SFT contains a periodic point, so a minimal `Z`-SFT is a single finite orbit. An infinite
  minimal `Z`-subshift is therefore never of finite type, and item 2 excludes finite presentation.
- `N ≥ 2`: the equivalence is items 1 and 2 read in both directions. A free minimal `Z^N`-SFT is in particular a free
  minimal action. ∎
