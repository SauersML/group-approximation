title: Every atomic type-I von Neumann algebra has unique Jiang--Su embeddings
---

Let `M` be an arbitrary atomic type-I von Neumann algebra.  Thus

```text
M = product_(j in J) B(H_j)
```

with no countability restriction on `J` or the Hilbert spaces.  Then every
two unital embeddings `Z->M` are approximately unitarily equivalent.
