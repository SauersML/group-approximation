**ESTABLISHED** (literature import, `fp-covers-of-grigorchuk-groups-are-large-citation`).

A group is *large* if it has a finite-index subgroup with a non-abelian free quotient; large
groups contain non-abelian free subgroups. With `𝔊` the first Grigorchuk group, `𝔅` the
Basilica group, and `G_ω` (`ω ∈ Ω₊`, sequences in `{0,1,2}^ℕ` that are not eventually constant)
Grigorchuk's groups of intermediate growth:

1. every finitely presented cover of `𝔊` is large;
2. every finitely presented cover of `𝔅` has non-abelian free subgroups;
3. for `ω ∈ Ω₊`, every finitely presented cover of `G_ω` is large;
4. if `H` is a finitely generated cover of `𝔊`, `𝔅`, or some `G_ω` with `ω ∈ Ω₊`, every finitely
   presented cover of `H` is large.

A *cover* of `Q` is a group with a quotient isomorphic to `Q`.
