**ESTABLISHED FUNCTORIAL PROPER-CORNER FIREWALL.**  Let `M` be a finite
matrix algebra with normalized trace, let `E` be a projection, and let
`D=DE` be a contraction.  Put

```text
H=supp(D^*D)<=E,             F=E-H.                    (OPC1)
```

For every unitary `U` and `X=FUE`,

```text
||D||_2^2 <=tau(H)<=||X^*X-E||_2^2.                   (OPC2)
```

The first inequality uses `0<=D^*D<=H`.  For the second, the support of
`X^*X` is Murray--von Neumann equivalent to the support of `XX^*`, which is
below `F`.  Hence its trace is at most `tau(F)=tau(E)-tau(H)`.  The kernel of
`X^*X` inside `E` therefore has trace at least `tau(H)`, and on that kernel
`X^*X-E=-E`.  This argument works verbatim in every finite tracial von
Neumann algebra.

For the exact B7 OR3 witness,

```text
E=P=e_0+e_1,
D=q_3[B_(x_31),q_2]q_1P=|e_4><e_0|,
H=e_0,                    F=e_1.                       (OPC3)
```

The identity unitary attains equality throughout `(OPC2)`.  Thus the
natural seven-dimensional squared-HS floor is `1/7`, and in the canonical
`p_rho` corner it is exactly

```text
||p_rho D||_2^2=1/92160.                               (OPC4)
```

Consequently a source-fullness decoder

```text
||X^*X-E||_2^2 <= C E_actor+o(1)                       (OPC5)
```

would give the desired dimension-independent payment of the oriented OR3
prefix with the same constant.  No second return or determinant estimate is
needed.

## Why a fixed Leavitt or block actor cannot supply OPC5

Suppose `E,H,F,U` are obtained from fixed group words by a construction
available functorially in every finite tracial representation: finite packet
exactification, matrix amplification, group-algebra arithmetic, spectral
support, polar decomposition, or conditional expectation.  If exact actor
relators imply

```text
(FUE)^*(FUE)=E,                                        (OPC6)
```

apply the same construction to the left regular representation.  Equation
`(OPC2)` forces `tau(H)=0`; faithfulness gives `H=0` and hence `D=0` there.
When these are fixed group-algebra blocks, faithfulness makes the vanishing
algebraic.  A stabilized Leavitt pair `ts=1`, `st=1-H` gives the same
conclusion by trace cyclicity, whether it is written directly or hidden in a
Halmos block unitary.

This is incompatible with retaining the B7 packet, where `(OPC4)` is
positive.  More generally, applying such a fixed return to every member of
the exhaustive Fano profile frame is incompatible with the supplied
full-support exact completion: some profile has positive sequential mass,
and universal raw covariance on that source is forbidden by
`full-support-factor-refutes-exact-exhaustive-typed-cap-chain`.

Therefore the smallest non-automorphic actor has a sharp dichotomy:

1. if its source-fullness is a fixed proper-isometry, Leavitt, Julia, or
   block identity, it collapses the positive prefix source in the regular
   representation and loses exact completeness;
2. if source-fullness is not a tracially functorial consequence, `(OPC2)`
   supplies the complete HS modulus, but a genuinely finite-matrix
   authentication of `(OPC5)` remains necessary.

The live interface is no longer the choice of source or target.  They are
canonically `supp(D^*D)` and its complement in `P`.  It is exactly a
matrix-coordinate rule making the compressed Gram source-full in matrices
without asserting `(OPC6)` in the regular finite factor.
