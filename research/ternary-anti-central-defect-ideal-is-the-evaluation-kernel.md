**OPEN.** The proof is on paper (artifact Theorem 2.1), held OPEN until `w4-vf-linear-b` re-derives it.

Let `R = L_(F_3)(1,2)`, `G = R^x = EL_3(R)`, `S_- = eps_- F_3[G]`, `D = N_23 N_12`, and let `pi : S_- -> R` be the
evaluation. Then `S_- D S_- = ker pi`, so `S_-/S_- D S_- ≅ R`.

**Consequence** (artifact Corollary 2.2).
- For every quotient `W` of `S_-`, `W/WDW` is `0` or `R`.
- Case 2 of Lemma 5.2 of `research/artifacts/matrix-state-deficit-item-two-2026-09-12.md` says exactly that `pi` factors
  through `W`.
- Then `W/WDW = R` fails the rank condition because `R ≅ R^2`, with no state argument.
