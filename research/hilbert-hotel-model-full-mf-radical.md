For the binary Leavitt algebra `R=L_(F_2)(1,2)`, put

```text
H=EL_16(R).
```

Then `H` is infinite, finitely generated, simple, ICC, Kazhdan, and

```text
Rad_MF(H)=H.
```

Every homomorphism from `H` to an MF group is trivial.

Consequently `H` is an explicit simple group whose entire group structure is
invisible to norm-matrix-corona representations.
