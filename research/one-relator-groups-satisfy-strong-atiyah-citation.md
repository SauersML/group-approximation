Lueck approximation conjectures for one-relator groups*, arXiv:1810.12135v4
(Math. Ann. 376 (2020)). It was read verbatim from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-12,
pages 2-3, together with the printed derivation from Theorem 1.1, Wise's theorem
and Brodskii's theorem. The claim node quotes all three and lists the references
