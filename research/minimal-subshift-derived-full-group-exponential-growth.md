title: "Matui: for an infinite minimal subshift, every subgroup of [[φ]] containing [[φ]]′ contains a lamplighter group, so [[φ]]′ has exponential growth"

**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `(X, φ)` be an infinite minimal subshift over `Z`. Every subgroup of `[[φ]]` containing `[[φ]]′` contains a subgroup isomorphic to the lamplighter group `(Z/2Z) ≀ Z`. So it contains a free semigroup on two generators, and `[[φ]]′` has exponential growth.

Source: Matui (Cornulier's reference [Ma2]), as stated and proved in Y. Cornulier, Séminaire Bourbaki 1064, arXiv:2002.09342, §`sgl`.
