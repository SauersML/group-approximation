# The rank-normalized coboundary step

This route records Steps 3--5 of `notes/COMPRESSION_COLLAPSE_MF_RADICAL.md`
after the only missing input in those steps, exact commuting torsion lifting,
has been supplied by [[corona-commuting-torsion-lifts]].

Let `Lambda` be the property-(T) compression subgroup and let
`v_(lambda,n)` be the commuting exact `m`-torsion lifts furnished by that
claim.  For orbit indices define

```text
d_n(lambda,mu) = rank(v_(lambda,n)-v_(mu,n)).
```

Exact finite spectrum gives a dimension-free constant

```text
eta_m = min_{i != j} |zeta^i-zeta^j| > 0
```

such that

```text
eta_m^2 d_n(lambda,mu)
  <= Tr |v_(lambda,n)-v_(mu,n)|^2
  <= 4 d_n(lambda,mu).                                          (1)
```

Covariance in the corona has operator-norm defect tending to zero.  Since the
`v`'s are exact finite-spectrum unitaries, disagreement projections that are
at operator distance `<1` have the same rank.  Therefore, for each fixed
`g,lambda,mu`,

```text
d_n(g lambda,g mu)=d_n(lambda,mu)
```

eventually.

## 1. Normalize by a finite Kazhdan generating set

Property (T) gives a finite symmetric generating set `S`.  Put

```text
k_n = sum_(a in S) d_n(1,a).
```

If `lambda` has `S`-word length `r`, repeated triangle inequality and the
eventual invariance above give

```text
d_n(1,lambda) <= r k_n                                            (2)
```

eventually.  If the desired collapse fails for some `lambda_0`, exact
finite-spectrum rigidity makes `d_n(1,lambda_0)>=1` infinitely often, hence
`k_n>=1` on an infinite subsequence.  Take a nonprincipal ultrafilter
concentrated on that subsequence.

## 2. The rescaled adjoint cocycle

Form the Hilbert ultraproduct of the matrix spaces with squared norm

```text
||x||_n^2 = Tr(x^*x)/k_n.
```

The adjoint maps `Ad(U_(g,n))` are unitary for this norm and their
operator-norm multiplicative defects still tend to zero, so they induce a
genuine unitary representation `sigma` in the ultraproduct.

Set

```text
c_n(lambda)=v_(lambda,n)-v_(1,n),
c(lambda)=[c_n(lambda)].
```

Equation (1) and (2) give

```text
||c_n(lambda)||_n^2 <= 4r,
```

so `c(lambda)` is well-defined.  Covariance gives the cocycle identity

```text
c(a lambda)=c(a)+sigma(a)c(lambda).
```

Indeed the coordinate defect has operator norm tending to zero and rank at
most a constant multiple of `d_n(1,lambda)=O(k_n)`; hence its rescaled
Hilbert--Schmidt mass is `O(||defect||_op^2)` and vanishes in the ultraproduct.

The cocycle is nonzero: summing (1) over `a in S` gives

```text
sum_(a in S) ||c(a)||^2 >= eta_m^2 lim_omega(k_n/k_n)=eta_m^2.
```

On the compressed copy `H=s Lambda s^-1`, the exact-lift rigidity coming from
the corona equality makes `v_(h,n)=v_(1,n)` eventually for each fixed `h`, so

```text
c|_H = 0.                                                        (3)
```

## 3. Delorme--Guichardet

Restrict `sigma` to `Lambda` (and, if desired, to the closed real Hilbert
subspace generated by the cocycle orbit).  Property (T), through the
repository's kernel-checked Delorme fixed-point theorem, makes every
1-cocycle a coboundary.  Thus there is `w` with

```text
c(lambda)=sigma(lambda)w-w.
```

Equation (3) says `sigma(h)w=w` for every `h in H`: the primitive is
`H`-fixed.  This is precisely the output called the "Kazhdan coboundary
collapse" by the consuming proof; the subsequent scaled Mautner/transport
step belongs to the next stage of `compression-torsion-collapse-assembly`,
not to this claim.

Thus [[kazhdan-coboundary-collapse]] follows from the now-established exact
commuting torsion lift theorem, with the remaining ingredients already
formalized in the repository.
