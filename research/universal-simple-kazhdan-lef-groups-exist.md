title: One infinite finitely generated simple Kazhdan LEF group contains every recursively presented finitely generated residually finite group
---

(a) For every countable set `𝒞` of countable residually finite groups, one infinite, finitely generated, simple
Kazhdan LEF group `U` contains a copy of every member of `𝒞`.

(b) In particular, one such `U` contains every recursively presented finitely generated residually finite group. That
includes every finitely presented residually finite group and every finitely generated linear group.

The countability hypothesis is sharp: `no-finitely-generated-group-contains-all-fg-rf-groups`. Established through
the `-proof` route, which inherits the review status of `countable-rf-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-lef-groups`.
