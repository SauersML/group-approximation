- TeX l.2126 (Theorem `thm:k-graphs`), under the standing assumptions `P = Z_{>=0}^k`,
  `S_P = {(z_j) ∈ P : 0 <= z_j <= 1}` and "Assume that $\fC$ is finite": "Suppose that for
  all $\mfv \in \fC^0$ and $1 \leq j \leq k$, we have $\# \mfv \bbd^{-1}(\epsilon_j) \mfv

  \coprod_{\mfv \in \fC^0} Y_{\mfv}$, where $Y_{\mfv} = \emptyset$ or $Y_{\mfv} = X(\mfv;

  Then $\bmF((I_l \ltimes X)_Y^Y)$ is of type ${\rm F}_{\infty}$."
