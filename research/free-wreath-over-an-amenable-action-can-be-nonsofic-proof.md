Notation as in the target. Put `W = ( *_(x in X) Z_x ) x| H`, write `z_x` for
the generator of `Z_x`, and let `x_0 = K`. By
[[centralizer-hnn-is-free-generalized-wreath]] `W ~= < H, v | [v, K] = 1 >`
with `v = z_(x_0)`.

## Step 1: the orbit subgroup

`O = G_0 x_0` is isomorphic to `G_0/Gamma_0`, as a `G_0`-set, because
`G_0 ∩ K = Gamma_0` (proof of
[[amenable-action-of-a-sofic-group-need-not-be-sofic]], (A4)). The subgroup
of `*_(x in X) Z_x` generated by the factors `Z_x` with `x in O` is their
free product `*_(x in O) Z_x`. A subfamily of free factors generates its own
free product: apply the retraction that kills the other factors. `G_0`
permutes `O`, so it normalizes `*_(x in O) Z_x`. Its intersection with `H` is
trivial. Hence

```text
W_O := < G_0, z_x : x in O >  =  ( *_(x in O) Z_x ) x| G_0
                            ~=  Z wr^*_(G/Gamma) G
                            ~=  < G, v | [v, gamma] = 1, gamma in Gamma >,
```

where the last isomorphism is [[centralizer-hnn-is-free-generalized-wreath]]
for the inclusion `Gamma <= G`. `W_O` is generated by `G_0` and `v` alone,
since `z_(g x_0) = g v g^-1`.

## Step 2: the double inside the centralizing HNN

`< G, v | [v, Gamma] = 1 >` is the amalgam `G *_Gamma (Gamma x <v>)`. The map

```text
D = G *_Gamma G  -->  < G, v G v^-1 >,     a |-> a  (first copy),   b |-> v b v^-1  (second copy)
```

is well defined, because `v` centralizes `Gamma`. It is injective. A
nontrivial reduced word of `D` alternates `a_1 b_1 a_2 b_2 ...` with the inner
letters outside `Gamma`. Its image `a_1 v b_1 v^-1 a_2 v b_2 v^-1 ...`
alternates letters of `G \ Gamma` with the letters `v^(±1)`, which lie in
`(Gamma x <v>) \ Gamma`. So the image is a reduced word of the amalgam and is
nontrivial by the normal form theorem. The same holds for words that begin
or end with a letter in `Gamma`, after absorbing that letter into its
neighbour.

## Step 3: conclusion

By [[kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic]] (Kun--Thom Theorem A, v3; checked
against page 2 of the v3 PDF on 2026-09-13) `G *_Gamma G` is not sofic.
It embeds in `W_O <= W`, and subgroups of sofic groups are sofic
([[elek-szabo-sofic-permanence]] (1)). So `W` is not sofic. `∎`

## Remark: other lamps

The same argument works for any nontrivial lamp group `L` in place of `Z`.
Take `l in L \ {1}` in place of `v` in Step 2. The orbit subgroup is
`( *_O L ) x| G_0 ~= G *_Gamma (Gamma x L)`, which contains `G *_Gamma G`.
This remark uses the identification of the free wreath product with lamps `L`
as an amalgam. The graph records that identification only for `L = Z`, so
the remark is not part of the established statement.
