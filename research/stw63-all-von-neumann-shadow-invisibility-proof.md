The compositions `pi phi` and `pi psi` are unital star homomorphisms from the
simple algebra `Z`, hence injective.  They are two embeddings into the
separably acting von Neumann algebra `M`, so the all-type von Neumann theorem
gives the required implementing unitaries in `M`.

The theorem gives no reason for those unitaries to belong to `pi(B)`.  The
remaining LXIII obstruction is therefore C-star-algebraic unitary lifting,
not a discrepancy visible in a separably acting von Neumann completion.
