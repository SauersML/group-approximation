Every proper subgroup of the finite group `M24` lies in a maximal subgroup.
Lagrange removes every ATLAS maximal row except the four orders displayed in
`(M24C-1)--(M24C-2)`.  The socle/quotient argument following `(M24C-2)`
removes the fourth.  This proves the three-carrier fork without assuming an
ambient conjugacy or fusion computation.
