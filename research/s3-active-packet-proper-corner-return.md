For the six-generator S3 radical atlas, let `A` be the analytically defined
sum of every allowed `K=+1,J=-1` endpoint type and the nonexit half of the
one-jump `K=-1,J=-1` type.  Construct one finitely presented group cell such
that:

1. the source and target finite packet tables exactify with fixed normalized-
   HS constants;
2. the weighted source isotypic mass of `A` differs from the next Fanizza
   carrier mass by at most `C sqrt(E)`;
3. the complementary contextual failure blocks enter the already named
   first-hit exits and are not silently restored by another packet type;
4. the cell imposes no commutation between distinct original BCS contexts;
   and
5. an exact representation separating the global mark extends across the
   cell, with properly infinite multiplicity allowed.

The cell cannot be an ordinary graph of finite groups: its Plancherel flow is
stationary.  Nor can it name the three-atom active complement by one scalar
involution.  It must use a genuine one-sided corner, projective two-cell, or
equivalent infinite-order transport whose strictness is finite-dimensional-
only.

This is the residual placement theorem after the endpoint atlas, local
failure payment, private-gauge renewal, and reducing-corner estimates have
all been separated out.

## Attempts

- An ordinary finite amalgam or HNN edge is insufficient: the regular
  Plancherel multiplicity vector gives a positive stationary flow, and an HNN
  isomorphism preserves the source multiplicities instead of removing the
  failure summand.
- Naming the analytic complement by an involution is impossible by the
  affine-selector barrier: on the `(K,b)` character square it is the union of
  three atoms.
- The active route is a Toeplitz/proper-corner placement.  Properly infinite
  multiplicity is legitimate for the exact marked witness, but a separate
  finite-matrix HS inequality must still be proved; no universal tracial
  corner inequality may be assumed.
