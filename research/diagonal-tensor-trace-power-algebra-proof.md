The tensor product trace gives `(DTP1)` immediately, and faithfulness of the
product trace identifies its GNS algebra with `(DTP3)`.  Strictness is already
visible for a finite group `G` with a nontrivial irreducible representation
`pi:G->U(d)`, `d>=2`, generating `M_d(C)`.  The diagonal algebra generated by
`pi(g)^(tensor n)` is contained in the image of the finite-dimensional group
algebra `C[G]`, so its vector-space dimension is at most `|G|`.  The full
tensor algebra has dimension `d^(2n)`, and is therefore strictly larger for
all sufficiently large `n`.

Finally, scalar powers tend to zero exactly in the open unit disk.  A
unit-modulus value different from one disproves the claimed raw limit; Cesaro
summation gives the stated scalar repair but only replaces one diagonal
algebra by a finite direct sum of them.  None of these identities supplies
non-CE inheritance from the full tensor product.
