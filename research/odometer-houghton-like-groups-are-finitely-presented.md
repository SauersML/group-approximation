**OPEN.**

**Statement.** Let `G_od <= Sym(N)` be the strongly shift-similar odometer group of
`odometer-kernel-preimage-is-strongly-shift-similar`, the full preimage of `K = ker chi` for
`B = V_(2,1)(<a>)`. Then `H_3(G_od)` is finitely presented.

**Why it matters.**
- It would be a finitely presented shift-similar group that is not amenable, since `H_3(G_od)` contains
  `FSym ⋊ V`. No such group is recorded on main or in the source. That would answer Mallery–Zaremsky Question `quest:examples_fin_props` ("What sorts of finitely presented
  groups are shift-similar, beyond `E_n` and `H_n`?") with non-amenable examples.
- By `houghton-like-fp-spectrum-is-closed-under-multiplication`, every `H_(3m)(G_od)` would then be finitely
  presented as well.
- It is the first test of the Houghton-like lifting in the rigid regime left open by
  `free-shift-houghton-like-envelopes-are-not-fp`, for an input that is not virtually abelian.
- It is not aimed at new Boone–Higman inputs: its building blocks `V` and `B` already embed in finitely presented
  simple groups (scope note of `thompson-v-binary-shell-action-passes-both-germ-gates`).

**Known.**
- **Germ gate passes.** `H_3(G_od)/FSym ≅ Q_3(B)`, and this group is finitely presented
  (`odometer-kernel-preimage-is-strongly-shift-similar` item 4).
- **No local-stability kill.** The near shift `s̄ = ā` lies in the finitely presented germ group `B`, so the
  hypothesis of `free-shift-houghton-like-envelopes-are-not-fp` fails. The generators from `V` displace points linearly, so
  `fp-bounded-displacement-e2-envelopes-are-eventually-periodic` does not apply.
- **Built from finitely presented pieces.** `H_3(G_od)` is generated by three copies `eps_12`, `eps_13`, `eps_23`
  of the finitely presented group `L_od = FSym(Z) ⋊ B`, one on each pair of rays
  (`fsym-by-odometer-group-on-the-integers-is-fp` item 3). Each copy contains a genuine copy `B_ij` of `B`.
- **Stronger, harder.** If `G_od` is finitely presented, the statement follows for every `n`
  (`fp-strongly-shift-similar-groups-have-fp-houghton-like-groups`). That route needs `K = [B,B]` finitely
  presented, which is not known. `K` is finitely generated, and `B = K ⋊ <a>`.

## Attempts

1. **Gluing presentation (bh-free-29, 2026-09-18).** *Reduced, not finished.*
   - Generators: those of the three copies of `L_od`.
   - Relations:
     - the three finite presentations;
     - the identification of each ray's finitary symmetric group across the two copies containing that ray;
     - one lift of each relator of a finite presentation of `Q_3(B)` in the `Δ_ij` generators, each corrected by
       a finitary word.
   - The presented group maps onto `H_3(G_od)`, and modulo transpositions its quotient is `Q_3(B)`.
   - What remains, as in Step 2 of `houghton-like-explicit-presentation-from-fp-g-proof` (the
     explicit-presentation route to `fp-strongly-shift-similar-groups-have-fp-houghton-like-groups`), is to show
     that the transpositions generate a normal copy of `FSym`. That is, conjugating a transposition by a generator
     of `B_12` must give a transposition, from finitely many relations.
   - This holds automatically when the transposition lies inside rays 1 and 2 (inside `L_od`). The open cases are
     transpositions `(x y)` with `x` on ray 1 and `y` on ray 3. `B_12` fixes `y` but moves `x` across rays 1
     and 2, so its orbits on such pairs are infinite in number.
   - In that route, the analogous cases were closed with the translations relation
     `t g t^-1 = theta(g)`. Here `t B_12 t^-1` agrees with a copy of `B` only up to a correction supported on
     ray 2 with germ in `K`. That correction is not a product of the pieces in any evident uniform way.
2. **P. Hall.** *Circular.* The only normal subgroup with a visible finitely presented quotient is the preimage of
   `K^3`, which is `G_od^3 · FSym`. Its finite presentation needs `G_od` finitely presented.
3. **Split the one-ended germs.** *Open subquestion.* If the near action of `K` on `N` lifted to a genuine action
   on `N ⊔ F`, with `F` finite, then `G_od ≅ FSym(N ⊔ F) ⋊ K`. (Adding finitely many points does not change the
   isomorphism type, by `strongly-shift-similar-point-stabilizers-are-copies-of-the-group`.) The lemma of
   `fsym-by-odometer-group-on-the-integers-is-fp`
   would then reduce `G_od`, and so every `H_n(G_od)`, to `K` finitely presented plus f.g. pair stabilizers. The
   analogous lift exists for `QV -> V` after adding one point (Nucinkis–St. John-Green's `Q~V`, arXiv:1412.7715).
   Whether it exists for `K` is not known.

**Lesson for general BH.**
- The Houghton-like construction is a device for realizing germs. Pairing two rays turns index-zero germ pairs
  `(b, cbc)` into a genuine action on `Z`, where finite presentation lifts by the lemma of
  `fsym-by-odometer-group-on-the-integers-is-fp`.
- For any host of this shape, what remains is one gluing theorem: a group generated by finitely many finitely
  presented realizable pieces that share finitary symmetric groups should be finitely presented. That is the same
  step as Brown's proof for Houghton groups.
- A general gluing theorem would lift finite presentation from germs to `H_3(G)` for every germ group
  `B ∋ s̄` that acts genuinely on `Z` with oligomorphic data. It would bypass the refuted generic lifting, and
  isolate exactly what the refutation's local stability destroys: a genuine two-ended realization.
