**ESTABLISHED (literature import)** by `gupta-torsion-element-survives-modulo-gamma-seven-citation`.

Let `F = F(f_1, ..., f_4)`, and let `u_6 ∈ F''` be the word of C. K. Gupta displayed in
Gupta–Levin (1986), §2, item (i): a product of 12 commutators
`[[a, b], [c, d, e, g]]` of weight 6 in the generators. Then:
1. `u_6^2 ∈ [F'', F]`, but `u_6 ∉ [F'', F]`;
2. `u_6 ∉ [F'', F] · γ_7(F)`.

So in `C_4 = F/[F'', F]`, the image of `u_6` has order 2 and lies outside `γ_7(C_4)`.
