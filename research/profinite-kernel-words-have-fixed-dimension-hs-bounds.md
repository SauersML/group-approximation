**Statement.** Let `P = <S | R>` be a finite presentation and `w in F(S)`. In normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt norm put `delta_R(U) = max_(r in R) ||r(U) - I||_2`, and let `C_n(R,w) in [0, infinity]` be the least
constant with `||w(U) - I||_2 <= C_n delta_R(U)` for all `U in U(n)^S`.

1. **Ideal membership.** Let `B_0 = Z[x_(s,ij), y_s]`, and substitute `X_s` for `s` and `y_s adj(X_s)` for `s^-1`. Let
   `J_n(R)` be generated by `y_s det X_s - 1` and the entries of `r(X) - I`. If `w` is profinitely trivial in `P`, then
   every entry of `w(X) - I` lies in `J_n(R)`.
2. **Linear bound.** Consequently `||w(U) - I||_2 <= |R| n^2 L_n delta_R(U)` on all of `U(n)^S`, where `L_n` is the
   coefficient `l^1`-size of an integer certificate. So `C_n(R,w) < infinity`. The exponent is one, the bound is global, and
   scalar phases are kept.
3. **Converse.** `C_n(R,w) < infinity` for every `n` if and only if `w` is profinitely trivial in `P`.
4. **Monotone.** `C_n <= C_(n+1)`, so `sup_n C_n = lim_n C_n`.
5. **Mass form.** If `mu_D(U)` is the proportion of the dimension in irreducible summands of degree at most `D`, then
   `||w(U) - I||_2^2 <= |R| C_D^2 delta_R(U)^2 + 4 (1 - mu_D(U))` in every dimension. So witnesses to a failure of
   uniformity need irreducible degree tending to infinity with a fixed share of the dimension.
6. **Projective form.** `1 - |tr w(U)|^2 <= C_(n^2)^2 max_r (1 - |tr r(U)|^2)`, via `U ⊗ conj(U)`.
