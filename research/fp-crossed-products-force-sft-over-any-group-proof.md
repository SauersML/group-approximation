This is Theorem A of the artifact (Sections 0–2).

1. **Presentation.** `B_X = LC(X,k) ⋊ P` is generated by `e_a` and `u_s`, since every cylinder is
   a product of `u_h e_a u_h^(-1) = χ[x(h) = a]`. Finite presentation does not depend on the
   generating set, so there are finitely many relators `r_j` in letters `E_a, U_s` whose monomials
   have length `<= n`.
2. **Monomials.** In any crossed product `LC(Z,k) ⋊ P`, a monomial `W` of length `<= n` equals
   `c_W u_(g_W)`. Here `g_W` is the product of its `U`-letters, and `c_W(z) = ∏ χ[z(p_i) = a_i]`,
   with `p_i` the product of the `U`-letters to the left of the `i`-th `E`-letter. So `c_W` is a
   fixed function `φ_W` of `z|_(B_n)`.
3. **The model.** Let `Y = {y ∈ A^P : every n-window of y lies in L_(B_n)(X)}`. For each relator
   and each `g ∈ B_n`, the zero coefficient of `u_g` in `B_X` says that `Σ_([g_W]=g) λ_W φ_W`
   vanishes on `L_(B_n)(X)`. Every `y ∈ Y` has its windows in that language, so the relators vanish
   in `B_Y`. This gives `ψ : B_X -> B_Y` with `e_a -> e_a` and `u_h -> u_h`.
4. **Descent.** If a pattern `π` on finite `F` does not occur in `X`, then `∏_(h∈F) u_h e_(π(h)) u_h^(-1) = 0`
   in `B_X`. Its image is the cylinder of `π` in `LC(Y,k)`, so `π` occurs in no `y ∈ Y`. Hence
   `Y ⊆ X`, and since `X ⊆ Y`, `X = Y` is an SFT. `∎`
