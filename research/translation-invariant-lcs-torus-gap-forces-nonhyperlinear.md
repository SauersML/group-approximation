**Setting.** A *translation-invariant binary linear rule* \(R\) on \(\mathbb Z^2\) is
a finite set \(S\) of site types and a finite set of contexts. Each context is a
finite set \(c\) of pairs \((u,s)\in\mathbb Z^2\times S\) together with a parity
\(b_c\in\mathbb F_2\); its translates are \(c+t\), \(t\in\mathbb Z^2\). Let
\(\rho\) bound the \(\ell^\infty\)-diameter of every context.

- \(\mathrm{Sol}_R\) is generated by \(J\) and \(x_{t,s}\) (\(t\in\mathbb Z^2\),
  \(s\in S\)) with relations: \(J^2=x_{t,s}^2=1\); \(J\) central; for every context
  \(c\) and every \(t\), the generators occurring in \(c+t\) commute pairwise and
  their product is \(J^{b_c}\).
- \(\Gamma_R=\mathrm{Sol}_R\rtimes\mathbb Z^2\), with \(\mathbb Z^2\) translating
  indices and fixing \(J\).
- \(\mathrm{Sol}_{R,n}\) is the torus system on \(T_n=\mathbb Z^2/n\mathbb Z^2\).
- An *\(\varepsilon\)-model* of a finite family of these relators is a list of
  exact involutions in \(U(d)\), one per generator, with \(J\mapsto -I_d\) and every
  relator of the family evaluated within normalized Hilbert--Schmidt distance
  \(\varepsilon\) of \(I_d\).

**Claim.**

1. \(\Gamma_R\) is finitely presented, and \(\mathrm{Sol}_R\le\Gamma_R\).
2. **Window models.** If \(J\ne1\) in \(\mathrm{Sol}_R\) and \(\mathrm{Sol}_R\) is
   hyperlinear, then for every finite window \(W\subset\mathbb Z^2\) and every
   \(\delta>0\) the relators of \(\mathrm{Sol}_R\) supported in \(W\) have a
   \(\delta\)-model.
3. **Periodization.** If the relators supported in \(W_m=\{0,\dots,m-1\}^2\) have a
   \(\delta\)-model and \(m\ge 2\rho+1\), then for every multiple \(n\) of \(m\) the
   torus system \(\mathrm{Sol}_{R,n}\) has an \(\varepsilon\)-model with
   \(\varepsilon^2\le\delta^2+16\rho/m\).
4. **Corollary.** Suppose \(J\ne1\) in \(\mathrm{Sol}_R\), and there are
   \(\varepsilon>0\) and \(n_0\) such that no \(\mathrm{Sol}_{R,n}\) with
   \(n\ge n_0\) has an \(\varepsilon\)-model. Then \(\Gamma_R\) is a finitely
   presented nonhyperlinear group.
5. **Screen.** If \(J\ne1\) in \(\mathrm{Sol}_R\), every van Kampen derivation of
   \(J=1\) in \(\mathrm{Sol}_{R,n}\) has area at least \(n/(2\rho)\). Exact triviality
   of \(J\) on tori therefore certifies at most a gap \(\varepsilon\ge 4\rho/n\to0\).
   A uniform gap as in (4) cannot come from exact derivations. It needs an analytic
   rigidity input.
