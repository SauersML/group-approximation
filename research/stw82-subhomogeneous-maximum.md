title: Arbitrary subhomogeneous-by-subhomogeneous extensions obey the nuclear-dimension maximum formula
---

Let

```text
0 -> I -> E -> D -> 0
```

be an extension with `I` and `D` subhomogeneous.  No separability hypothesis
is imposed.  Then

```text
dim_nuc(E) = max(dim_nuc(I), dim_nuc(D)).
```

This extends the separable subhomogeneous case of an Huef--Williams,
[arXiv:2412.10241v4, Lemma 7.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10241), and in
particular settles all arbitrary commutative extensions.
