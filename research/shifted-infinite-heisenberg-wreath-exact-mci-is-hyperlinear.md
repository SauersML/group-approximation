There is a finitely generated recursively presented sofic group `W`, an
infinite property-`(T)` subgroup `H<=W`, and an infinite-order word `w` such
that:

1. every nonzero power of `w` has an infinite `H`-conjugacy orbit;
2. every finite-dimensional unitary representation of `W` kills `w`;
3. `W` is hyperlinear, so its canonical regular representation detects `w`
   and has the strict carrier-energy floor.

Take the infinite Heisenberg tower with commuting Weyl pairs
`[x_i,y_i]=z`, adjoin the index shift, and use that amenable group as the
lamp group in a restricted wreath product with an infinite sofic
property-`(T)` group. On a finite-dimensional `z`-eigenspace where `z` has
order `q`, the first `N` Weyl pairs force dimension divisible by `q^N` for
every `N`; hence `q=1`.

Thus exact finite-dimensional cyclic invariance, even for an infinite-order
transverse carrier, supplies no dimension-uniform normalized-HS modulus.
Higman embedding gives a finitely presented overgroup preserving the exact
collapse and orbit geometry, but not known hyperlinearity of that overgroup.
