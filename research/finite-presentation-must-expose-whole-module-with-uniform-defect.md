OPEN.  For a concrete finitely presented semidirect/module host

```text
Gamma=M rtimes A,
```

with `M` an elementary abelian two-group normally generated by finitely many
seed lamps, prove that every matrix tuple of presentation defect `delta`
canonically yields a map

```text
phi_U:M -> U(d)
```

such that, with a modulus independent of the virtual lamp names,

```text
sup_(m,n in M) ||phi_U(m+n)-phi_U(m)phi_U(n)||_2
    <= omega(delta),                                         (FME1)
```

the seed values remain close to their fixed group words, and the finite
actor generating set transports `phi_U` with uniform squared-HS energy.

Once `(FME1)` is proved,
`all-abelian-uniform-hs-rounding-gives-global-pvm` gives one exact
same-dimensional finite-support PVM for the whole module.  The actor
transport then feeds `one-seed-infinite-character-expansion-collapses-mark`.

## Attempts

- **Choose one word for every module element.**  A multiplication identity
  between remote words has van Kampen area depending on their addresses, so
  the direct estimate is `A_(m,n) delta`, not one uniform modulus.
- **Round every finite module window separately.**  Uniform abelian
  stability rounds each completed all-pairs table, but a finite presentation
  has not supplied those tables with a window-independent defect, and the
  corrected PVMs need not be compatible as the window grows.
- **Use only pairwise lamp commutators.**  Pairwise almost commutation is not
  itself the all-pairs approximate multiplication law `(FME1)` for chosen
  products of arbitrarily many lamps; naïve telescoping grows with support.
