If `(A,tau)` is a selfless C*-probability space and `tau` is tracial, then
`A` is simple, has stable rank one and strict comparison by `tau`, and `tau`
is the unique tracial state and the unique normalized 2-quasitrace on `A`.

**Established (literature).**  This is Robert, *Selfless C*-algebras*,
arXiv:2309.14188v3, Theorem 3.1.  It is imported here, not reproved.
