**Established by citation.**  Cornel Pasnicu and Mikael Rordam,
[*Purely infinite C*-algebras of real rank
zero*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0606378), J. Reine Angew. Math. 613
(2007), 51--73, Proposition 2.14, prove that for C*-algebras with the ideal
property the weak, ordinary, and strong forms of pure infiniteness coincide.
In particular, ordinary pure infiniteness plus the ideal property implies
strong pure infiniteness.

Their Proposition 2.11 proves that, for a separable purely infinite
C*-algebra, the ideal property is equivalent to the primitive ideal space
having a basis of compact-open sets.  The topological reformulation in the
claim uses exactly this equivalence; it does not assert the equivalence for
arbitrary non-purely-infinite algebras.
