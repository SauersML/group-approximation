Use the notation and fixed robust gap

```text
E_base+q>=beta                                           (PAV1)
```

from `constant-repetition-v4-needs-only-disjoint-tags`.  Repeat every context
packet a fixed number `L` of times and split every synchronized V4 failure
projection into its five radical-atom occurrences.  The total source mass is

```text
s=(5/4)Lq.                                               (PAV2)
```

Suppose a joint decoder supplies partial isometries `T_i` into one common
ambient amplification of capacity `M_0` and, for every pair `i<j`, a unitary
`R_ij` with opposite source phases as in `(PAC1)`, such that

```text
sum_(i<j)(||D_(i,ij)||_2^2+||D_(j,ij)||_2^2)
 <= C_pair E_rel.                                       (PAV3)
```

Then `pairwise-antiphase-covariance-controls-cross-gram` and
`gram-branching-capacity-with-overlap`, with target projection the ambient
identity, give

```text
M_0 >= (5/4)Lq-C_pair E_rel.                            (PAV4)
```

Choose the fixed repetition count

```text
L>4M_0/(5beta).                                         (PAV5)
```

For `E=E_base+E_rel`, `(PAV1)` and `(PAV4)` imply the dimension-independent
floor

```text
E >= ((5/4)L beta-M_0)/((5/4)L+C_pair)>0.              (PAV6)
```

Unlike a common finite tag group, this criterion has no character-packing
ceiling: the separating observable is allowed to depend on the unordered
pair.  It remains a fixed finite presentation because `L` and the BCS are
fixed before the group is defined.  The unresolved issue is groupification
with exact tracial completeness, not the Hilbert--Schmidt accounting.
