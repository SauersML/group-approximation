**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Γ_R = <a,b,c,x,y,z | involutions, axax, ayay, azbz, bxbx, bycy, cxcz>`
act on `T_h × T_v` (`radu-bmw-lattice-embeds-in-titz-witzel-kernel`). Put `δ = xz`, let
`Λ_v = ker(Γ_R → Aut(T_h))`, `Q = Γ_R/Λ_v`, `W = V/Λ_v ≅ PGL_2(Z)` with `V = <x,y,z>`, and
`G_2 = Γ_R/[Λ_v, Λ_v]` (`radu-lattice-is-limit-of-nilpotent-by-arithmetic-groups`). Let
`M = Λ_v^ab`, and let `m_0 ∈ M` be the class of `δ^2`, so that the mark `(xz)^4` maps to `2m_0`.

1. **Sign character.** In `Γ_R`,

   ```text
   a δ^2 a = δ^2,   b δ^2 b = δ^2,   c δ^2 c = δ^(−2),   x δ^2 x = z δ^2 z = δ^(−2).
   ```

2. **The xz-slab.** Let `L ⊂ T_v` be the line through the base vertex whose edges are labelled
   `x, z`. The stabilizer of `T_h × L` in `Γ_R` is `H_Γ = <a,b,c,x,z>`. It acts simply
   transitively on the vertices of `T_h × L`, and

   ```text
   H_Γ = <a,b,c,x,z | a^2, b^2, c^2, x^2, z^2, axax, bxbx, azbz, cxcz>,
   H_Γ ∩ Λ_v = <δ^2>,      H_Q := H_Γ/<δ^2> = image of H_Γ in Q.
   ```

   `ψ' : H_Γ → {±1}`, `a, b ↦ +1` and `c, x, z ↦ −1`, is a homomorphism, and
   `h δ^2 h^(−1) = δ^(2ψ'(h))` for every `h ∈ H_Γ`. `ψ'` descends to `H_Q`.

3. **Signed permutation module.** `Q = W·H_Q` and `W ∩ H_Q = D_2 := <x,z>`. The stabilizer of
   `{±m_0}` in `Q` is exactly `H_Q`, and

   ```text
   Z[Q]·m_0  ≅  Ind_(H_Q)^Q Z_(ψ'),
   ```

   which is free abelian on `Q/H_Q ≅ W/D_2`, the set of 4-cycles `(xz)^2` of
   `Y = Λ_v\T_v = Cay(W; x,y,z)`.

4. **Parity lemma.** Let `π : G_2 → F` be a finite quotient and `N ⊆ Q` the image of `ker π`.
   If some `h ∈ H_Q ∩ N` has `ψ'(h) = −1`, then `π((xz)^4) = 1`. Consequently, if `ker ψ'` is
   not closed in the topology that the profinite topology of `Q` induces on `H_Q`, then
   `(xz)^4` lies in the finite residual of `G_2`, by a mechanism that uses only the sign
   character and the abelian kernel.

**Reading.** The mark of Radu's lattice is twice a generator of a *signed* permutation module
over the infinite-index, virtually free subgroup `H_Q` of the arithmetic lattice `Q`. The sign is
`ψ'`, and `ψ'` is not the restriction of any character of `Q`: `ψ'(b) ≠ ψ'(c)`, while
`c = yby` is conjugate to `b` in `Q`. So exact abelian chain-lamp models over a finite quotient
`Q/N` see the mark only if `H_Q ∩ N ⊆ ker ψ'`. A near-exact model needs approximately
`ψ'`-balanced `H_Q`-slices, and these are the "odd `c`-cycles" of the parity screen in
`radu-nilpotent-by-arithmetic-quotients-are-sofic`, with the correct sign on `x` and `z`.
