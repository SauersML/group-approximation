The BS(1,4) skeleton gives linear length control for the parabolic direction
(`bs14-strict-hs-dehn-separation`); extending it to the averaged congruence
multiplication defect (`far-sector-length-control-extension-gap`) promotes a
prime-level microstate to an averaged almost-representation of
`PSL_2(F_p)`, which de la Salle Thm 1.4 exactifies with linear
dimension-free constants.  Property `(tau)` plus co-density then place it
near a congruence representation.  To close the whole far sector one must
add either the corresponding composite-level quotient presentation or a
proof that prime levels alone supply the needed periodic approximation.
