title: Kernels of maps onto Z from groups of intermediate growth are finitely generated of intermediate growth
---

**Statement.** Let `G` be a finitely generated group of intermediate growth and `pi: G -> Z`
surjective. Then `N = ker pi` is finitely generated, has intermediate growth, and is not virtually
nilpotent. The same holds for every finite-index subgroup of `G` mapping onto `Z`.

No finite presentation is assumed. Proof: `intermediate-growth-z-quotient-kernels-proof`.
