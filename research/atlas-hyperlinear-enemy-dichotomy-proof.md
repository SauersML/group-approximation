Suppose `Lambda` admits a nontrivial finite-dimensional unitary
representation `pi`.  Its image `L=pi(Lambda)` is a finitely generated linear
group.  Choose `x in L`, `x != 1`.  By Malcev residual finiteness there is a
finite quotient of `L` in which `x` remains nontrivial.  Composing all maps
gives a nontrivial finite quotient of `Gamma_6`.

The established finite-simple reduction now supplies a nonabelian finite
simple group `S`, generated by two marked injected `A8` charts and satisfying
all six literal relators.  The strict index theorem gives
`[S:A8]>48960`.  If no nontrivial finite-dimensional representation of
`Lambda` exists, `Lambda` is minimally almost periodic by definition.  These
alternatives exhaust the possibilities.

For the character statement, let a nontrivial factorial CE character have
GNS factor `M`.  If `M` is finite-dimensional, its GNS group image is a
nontrivial finite-dimensional unitary representation and the preceding
argument applies.  An infinite-dimensional finite factor is type II1, giving
the other asserted possibility.  No approximation-to-exactification or
finite-quotient search is used beyond the already established finite branch.
