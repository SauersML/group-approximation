title: For every m >= 1 the subgroup of SL_3(Z) generated by the elementary matrices e_ij(m) has finite index

For every integer `m >= 1`, the subgroup `E(m) <= SL_3(Z)` generated by the
six elementary matrices `e_ij(m) = I + m E_ij` (`i != j`) has finite index in
`SL_3(Z)`.

This is a literature statement for `SL_n(Z)`, `n >= 3`. It is recalled as
J. Tits, *Systèmes générateurs de groupes de congruence*, C. R. Acad. Sci.
Paris (1976), which rests on the congruence subgroup property of `SL_n(Z)`,
`n >= 3` (Bass–Milnor–Serre; Mennicke).
