title: Every proper normal subgroup of braided V lies in the pure braided kernel of bV -> V

Let `pi: V_br -> V` be the natural quotient of the Brin–Dehornoy braided Thompson
group onto Thompson's group `V`, and let `P_br = ker pi`. Then every proper normal
subgroup of `V_br` is contained in `P_br`.

This is Zaremsky's Corollary 2.8 (arXiv:1403.8132v2, Groups Geom. Dyn.). Since `V`
is simple, an equivalent form is: any normal subgroup of `V_br` either contains
`P_br` or is contained in it.
