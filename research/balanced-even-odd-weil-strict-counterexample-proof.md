Let (W_p) be the Schrodinger Weil representation of
(SL_2(F_p)).  For (p=1pmod 4), reflection of the Schrodinger
coordinate splits it into irreducible parity summands

```text
W_p=E_p direct-sum O_p,
dim(E_p)=(p+1)/2=d_p+1,       dim(O_p)=(p-1)/2=d_p.    (BWO1)
```

The central element (-I) acts by (+1) on (E_p) and by (-1) on
(O_p).  Pull both representations back along reduction
(Gammalongrightarrow SL_2(F_p)).

## The exact vertex pair

For (p=5pmod 8), take the torsion-normalized compressed even-Weil
packet from
`exact-torsion-frame-has-rank-one-strict-counterpackets`.  Write it as

```text
F=<X,A,B | X^2=A^3=B^3=1> -> U(d_p).                  (BWO2)
```

The two exact modular vertices are generated by ((X,A)) and ((X,B)).
With

```text
T=XA,        R=A^2 X A^2 B,                            (BWO3)
```

the two remaining words are

```text
u=(XR)^2,        v=RTR^(-1)T^(-4).                    (BWO4)
```

Their normalized Hilbert--Schmidt defects are (O(p^{-1/2})).
The fixed Tietze transformations in
`iwahori-square-free-bs14-presentation` identify these two word
residuals with the original Iwahori edge mismatch, with only universal
word-length loss.  Lift this central-trivial pair to the two copies of
(SL_2(Z)), and direct-sum both vertices with the two exactly compatible
restrictions of (O_p).  This gives (pi_{p,+},pi_{p,-}) on dimension
(2d_p).  The odd block contributes zero edge error, so

```text
def(pi_p)=O(p^(-1/2)).                                  (BWO5)
```

## The SL2 trace is regular

The Weil character formula is

```text
|Tr(W_p(g))|^2=|ker(g-I)|.                              (BWO6)
```

For the standard normalization with (p=1pmod4),

```text
chi_(E_p)(g)=(chi_(W_p)(g)+chi_(W_p)(-g))/2,
chi_(O_p)(g)=(chi_(W_p)(g)-chi_(W_p)(-g))/2.           (BWO7)
```

If the class of (g) is nontrivial in (PSL_2(F_p)), neither (g) nor
(-g) is the identity.  Hence each kernel in ((BWO6)) has cardinality
at most (p), and

```text
|chi_(E_p)(g)|, |chi_(O_p)(g)| <= sqrt(p).             (BWO8)
```

For every fixed (gammainGammasetminus{I,-I}), reduction of
(gamma) is not (I) or (-I) for all but finitely many odd primes:
after clearing a power of two from its entries, infinitely many such
congruences would force the rational matrix itself to be scalar.
Therefore both normalized characters in ((BWO8)) tend to zero.

Deleting the one line from (E_p), polar rounding, and the two-triangle
torsion normalization move every fixed generator by (O(p^{-1/2})) in
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  Fixed-word telescoping preserves the
same trace limit.  On the central element the two (d_p)-dimensional
blocks cancel exactly:

```text
tr_(2d_p)(pi_p(-I))=(d_p-d_p)/(2d_p)=0.                (BWO9)
```

Thus the exact ultraproduct homomorphism defined by ((BWO5)) has
character (delta_e) on (SL_2(Z[1/2])), not merely on its central
quotient.

## Central-sector dimension lock

Suppose, toward contradiction, that exact representations

```text
sigma_p:Gamma->U(2d_p)                                 (BWO10)
```

approach (pi_p) on a fixed generating set.  Put

```text
Z_p=I_(C_p) direct-sum (-I_(O_p)),
P_0=(1+Z_p)/2,       Q_0=(1-Z_p)/2,
P=(1+sigma_p(-I))/2, Q=(1-sigma_p(-I))/2.              (BWO11)
```

The element (-I) is a fixed word, so
(|P-P_0|_{2,2d_p}=o(1)).  In unnormalized Frobenius norm this gives

```text
Tr(PP_0)=d_p-o(d_p),       Tr(QQ_0)=d_p-o(d_p).         (BWO12)
```

The ranges of (P,Q) reduce (sigma_p); write
(m_p=operatorname{rank}P).

Fix a ((T;FD)) Kazhdan pair for
(PSL_2(Z[1/2])).  First suppose (m_ple d_p).  Let

```text
J_p:E_p -> C_p direct-sum O_p                          (BWO13)
```

be the partial isometry which identifies the compressed (d_p)-space
with the positive input block and kills the deleted even fixed line.  Put
(V_p=PJ_p:E_p	o Pmathbb C^{2d_p}).  By ((BWO12)),

```text
||V_p||_F^2=d_p-o(d_p).                                (BWO14)
```

For every element (s) of the fixed Kazhdan set,

```text
||sigma_p(s)V_p-V_pE_p(s)||_F=o(sqrt(d_p))+O(1).       (BWO15)
```

Indeed the first term is the assumed normalized-HS closeness multiplied
by (sqrt{2d_p}).  The (O(1)) term is the one-line compression,
polar, and torsion-normalization error: its normalized size is
(O(p^{-1/2})) on a space of dimension comparable to (p).
Consequently the defect in ((BWO15)), divided by (|V_p|_F), tends
to zero.

Both (E_p) and the (P)-sector have central sign (+1), so their
conjugation representation on
(operatorname{Hom}(E_p,Pmathbb C^{2d_p})) factors through the central
quotient.  Property ((T;FD)) now produces a nonzero exact intertwiner
from (E_p) into the positive sector of (sigma_p).  Since (E_p) is
irreducible, that intertwiner is injective, forcing

```text
m_p>=dim(E_p)=d_p+1,                                   (BWO16)
```

a contradiction.

The other integer case is (m_pge d_p+1), so
(operatorname{rank}Q=2d_p-m_ple d_p-1).  Let
(J_p^-:O_p	o C_poplus O_p) be the exact inclusion of the odd block and
put (V_p^-=QJ_p^-).  Equations ((BWO12)) and exactness of the odd
input give

```text
||V_p^-||_F^2=d_p-o(d_p),
||sigma_p(s)V_p^--V_p^-O_p(s)||_F=o(sqrt(d_p)).        (BWO17)
```

Both representations have central sign (-1), so their Hom
representation again factors through (PSL_2).  Property ((T;FD))
produces a nonzero exact intertwiner from irreducible (O_p) into the
negative sector.  It must be injective, contradicting

```text
rank(Q)<=d_p-1<dim(O_p)=d_p.                           (BWO18)
```

The two cases exhaust the integer (m_p).  Hence no exact sequence
((BWO10)) approaches (pi_p), which gives the uniform subsequential
constant (c) in ((RSC2)).

## Congruence exactification contradiction

If a same-dimensional congruence pair (ho_p) has edge defect
(eta_p), `congruence-repair-bypasses-koopman-cycle-firewalls`
gives a literally compatible pair (widetildeho_p) in the same

```text
d_2(rho_p,rho~_p)<=K eta_p.                            (BWO19)
```

The compatible pair is an exact representation of (Gamma).
Triangle inequality with ((RSC2)) proves

```text
d_2(pi_p,rho_p)>=c-K eta_p,                            (BWO20)
```

after absorbing the fixed coordinate-change constants into (K).
This proves ((RSC3)) and refutes strict regular congruence
exactification.

(E_poplus O_p) in dimension (2d_p+1), at generalized distance
(O(p^{-1/2})).  Thus the proof leaves the flexible generalized-metric
question completely open.
