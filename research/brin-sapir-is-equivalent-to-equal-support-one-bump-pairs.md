**Setting.** `F` acts on `[0,1]` (left actions, `(ab)(x) = a(b(x))`). Fix the one-bump
element `f = x_0^{-1} ∈ F` (`f(x) = 2x` on `[0,1/4]`, `x + 1/4` on `[1/4,1/2]`,
`(x+1)/2` on `[1/2,1]`), so `f(x) > x` on `(0,1)`, and the dyadic interval
`J = [1/8, 3/16]`, with `f(1/8) = 1/4 > 3/16`. Put `J_m = f^m(J)` (`m ∈ ℤ`): pairwise
disjoint dyadic intervals, and `B_m ≤ F` the subgroup of elements supported in `J_m`.

**Statement.** For every finitely generated `H ≤ F` there is `h ∈ F` supported in
`J_{a_1} ∪ … ∪ J_{a_k}` (finitely many blocks) such that, with `g = f h`:

1. `g` is one-bump with support `(0,1)`, like `f`;
2. `G = ⟨f, g⟩ = ⟨f, h⟩ = N ⋊ ⟨f⟩`, where `N` is the germ-trivial kernel of `G` on
   `(0,1)` and `N ≤ ⊕_{m∈ℤ} B_m` has every block projection isomorphic to `H`;
3. `G` is elementary amenable iff `H` is;
4. `G` contains a copy of `F` iff `H` does.

Consequently the following are equivalent:

- (BS) every subgroup of `F` is elementary amenable or contains `F`
  (`every-subgroup-of-thompson-f-is-ea-or-contains-f`);
- (BS₁) for every `g ∈ F` that is one-bump with support `(0,1)`, the group `⟨x_0, g⟩`
  is elementary amenable or contains `F`;
- (BS₂) for every pair `f, g ∈ F` of one-bump elements with the same support, `⟨f, g⟩`
  is elementary amenable or contains `F`;
- (BS₃) for all `u, v ∈ F` supported in `J = [1/8, 3/16]`, the group `⟨x_0, u x_0 v⟩`
  is elementary amenable or contains `F`;
- (BS₄) for all `v ∈ F` supported in `J`, the group `⟨x_0, w x_0 v⟩` is elementary
  amenable or contains `F`. Here `w = φ x_0^{-1} φ^{-1}` for one fixed dyadic PL
  homeomorphism `φ: [0,1] → J`, extended by the identity.

So the conjecture is a statement about one explicit family of 2-generated groups,
parametrized by `v ∈ F_J ≅ F`.

In (BS₃), `⟨x_0, u x_0 v⟩ = ⟨f, h⟩` with `h = v^{-1}·f u^{-1} f^{-1}` supported in the
two adjacent blocks `J_0 ∪ J_1`. The width is sharp: if `h` is supported in the single
block `J_0`, then `⟨f, h⟩ ≅ ⟨h⟩ ≀ ℤ = ℤ ≀ ℤ` (or `ℤ` when `h = 1`).

The same holds verbatim in `PL_o(I)` (arbitrary breakpoints and slopes), with `F`
replaced by `PL_o(I)` as the ambient group.

**Why it matters for Zaremsky Problem 2.3.** This is a class-killing statement for
one line of attack. The graph recorded "two one-bump generators with equal support"
(`every-subgroup-of-thompson-f-is-ea-or-contains-f`, Attempts) as the open sub-case
left by `two-one-bump-pl-maps-unequal-supports-ea-or-contain-f`. It is not a
sub-case: it is the whole conjecture, even with one generator fixed. In the other
direction, a counterexample (`some-subgroup-of-thompson-f-is-neither-ea-nor-contains-f`)
may be assumed to be `⟨x_0, g⟩` with `g` one-bump on `(0,1)` and `x_0^{-1}g`
block-diagonal: this is the first entry in the biography of a minimal counterexample.
Every argument that uses only the positions of supports of two generators (Brin's
one-sided criterion, Kim–Koberda–Lodha overlap) is exhausted at this point. The
remaining information sits in the block group `N`, a `ℤ`-shift-generated subdirect
subgroup of `⊕_ℤ H`.
