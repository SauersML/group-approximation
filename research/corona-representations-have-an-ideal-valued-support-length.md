Let `rho:G->U(Q_d)` be a group homomorphism.  Define

```text
Supp_rho(g)=the closed ideal of Q_d generated by rho(g)-1.
```

Then `Supp_rho` is an ideal-valued invariant length:

```text
Supp(1)=0,
Supp(g^(-1))=Supp(g),
Supp(hgh^(-1))=Supp(g),
Supp(gh)<=Supp(g) join Supp(h),
Supp([g,h])<=Supp(g) meet Supp(h).                     (IS1)
```

For every closed ideal `J normal Q_d`,

```text
N_J={g in G:Supp_rho(g)<=J}
```

is exactly the kernel of the induced homomorphism

```text
G -> U(Q_d/J).
```

Thus the ideal lattice of the corona gives a canonical increasing filtration
of `G` by normal subgroups.  In particular, elements with disjoint support
ideals commute in the corona.

If `g` has finite order, let `p_g` be the spectral projection of `rho(g)` on
the complement of the eigenvalue `1`.  Then

```text
Supp_rho(g)=I(p_g).                                    (IS2)
```

When `p_g` has coordinate ranks `r_n`, the complete principal-ideal footprint
of `g` is therefore the rank-growth class of `(r_n)` under eventual
big-`O` domination.  This refines the binary distinction between visibility
and invisibility into a continuum-valued support geometry.
