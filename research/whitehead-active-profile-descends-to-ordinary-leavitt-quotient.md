The active coefficient quotient preserves the nonzero coarse and fine
Leavitt corners.  In odd characteristic the image of the signed Whitehead
word has nonidentity square and fourth power one, hence exact order four.
It therefore remains an injective edge in the quotient amalgam
`D *_(C_4) Q`.

If `Q` is hyperlinear, finite-edge amalgam permanence gives canonical
microstates of that quotient graft.  Pull them through the quotient of the
full affine graft.  Every full occurrence relator has vanishing defect while
the embedded finite packet keeps its nontrivial Clifford mark and regular
type densities.  Tensoring a fixed exact positive outer payload fiber turns
these into compressed active-gauge countermodels.  Thus a uniform active
floor would force `Q` itself to be nonhyperlinear.
