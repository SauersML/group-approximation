**ESTABLISHED (proved on paper, unreviewed).** For every field `K`, `β_n^(2)(L_K(1,2)^x) = 0`
for all `n >= 0`. For `char K = p > 0`, this group is nonsofic, since it contains
`L_(F_p)(1,2)^x`.
