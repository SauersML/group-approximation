Citation import. Original source: E. W. Chambers, V. de Silva, J. Erickson and
R. Ghrist, "Vietoris–Rips complexes of planar point sets", Discrete Comput.
Geom. 44 (2010). The statement was read on 2026-09-13 from its restatement in
M. Adamaszek, F. Frick and A. Vakili, "On homotopy types of Euclidean Rips
complexes", arXiv:1602.04131v1 (12 Feb 2016), p. 2, Theorem 1.3, which cites
the original as [2]:

> Theorem 1.3 ([2]). Let `X ⊆ R^n` be a finite subset. The projection map
> `p: R(X) → S(X)` (a) is a `π_0`-isomorphism for all `n`, (b) is a
> `π_1`-isomorphism when `n = 2`, (c) may fail to be `π_1`-surjective for each
> `n ≥ 4`.

The same page says: "Part (b) of Theorem 1.3 implies that for `X ⊆ R^2` the
group `π_1(R(X))` is free. To our best knowledge this is the only known
non-trivial piece of information about the homotopy types of Rips complexes
of subsets of Euclidean spaces." AFV use the strict convention
`diam(Y) < r` and note (Remark 1.4, p. 3) that for finite `X` the results
hold in both conventions. The original paper was not opened here; the import
relies on AFV's restatement.
