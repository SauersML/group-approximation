Every proper subgroup of `G2(4)` lies in a maximal subgroup.  Lagrange and
the official maximal-subgroup orders leave exactly `(G24A8-1)`.  The `J2`
maximal list and the central-extension argument in the claim remove those
two rows, respectively.  Hence no `A8` exists in `G2(4)`.
