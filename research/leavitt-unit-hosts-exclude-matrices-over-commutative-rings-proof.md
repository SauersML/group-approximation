**Proof.** Artifact Section 2.

1. **Finite generation.** Both groups are generated by `V` and one transvection, and `V` is finitely generated.
2. **Malcev over commutative rings** (statement level).
   - A finitely generated `Γ ≤ GL_n(C)` lies in `GL_n(C_0)`, with `C_0` finitely generated.
   - `C_0` is residually finite. For `a ≠ 0`, take a maximal ideal `m ⊇ ann(a)`. Krull's intersection theorem gives
     `a ∉ m^k` for some `k`, and `C_0/m^k` is finite.
   - Reduction modulo `m^k` separates `γ ≠ 1`.
3. **Binary case.** `ρ(G)` is `1` or `≅ G`. An infinite simple group is not residually finite, so `ρ` is trivial.
4. **Ternary case.**
   - Every normal subgroup lies in `{±1}` or equals `G_3`, by simplicity of `G_3/{±1}` and perfectness.
   - So the nontrivial quotients are `G_3` and `G_3/{±1}`. Both are infinite with no proper finite-index normal
     subgroup.
   - So `ρ` is trivial, and `ρ(z) = 1 ≠ -1` in characteristic 3.
