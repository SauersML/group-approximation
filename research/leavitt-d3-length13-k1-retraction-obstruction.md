five-valent source holonomy to be the identity and the seven-valent target
holonomy to be `h=[c,d]`.  For arbitrary coefficient units, including
identity and arbitrarily long Leavitt compounds, the cyclic corner equations
have no solution whenever `h != 1`.

The four exhaustive source-corner cancellation branches reduce to two unit
systems.  Projecting their common-carrier equality to the three free
coefficient copies forces `h=1` in either system.  Thus no enlargement of a
finite coefficient library can realize this topology.
