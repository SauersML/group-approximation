Notation is as in the target. "CE" means Connes embeddable. All tracial
algebras are finite von Neumann algebras with a faithful normal tracial
state. Throughout, `||.||_2` is the trace norm.

## 0. Standard facts used

- **(F0) Microstates.** A tracial algebra generated by a sequence of
  elements is CE exactly when every finite family of moments of finitely
  many generators can be approximated within any `epsilon` by matrices of
  the same operator-norm bounds. Consequences:
  - subalgebras of CE algebras are CE;
  - finite tensor products of CE algebras are CE;
  - a separable subalgebra of an ultraproduct `prod_omega M_N` of CE
    algebras is CE, by diagonalizing matricial approximations of
    `M_N`-moments that are themselves close to the limit moments.
  In particular, a CE separable algebra embeds trace-preservingly into a
  tracial matrix ultraproduct.
- **(F1) Weyl identification.** Let `V` be a real vector space with a
  positive semidefinite form `Q`. Let `(N,tau)` be tracial, with unitaries
  `W(v)` (`v in V`) such that

  ```text
  W(v)W(w) = W(v+w),        tau(W(v)) = exp(-Q(v)/2).                  (W)
  ```

  Then the von Neumann algebra `W*(W(V))` is trace-preservingly isomorphic
  to `L^infinity` of the centered Gaussian process `(Y_v)` with
  `E[Y_v^2] = Q(v)`, through `W(v) -> exp(i Y_v)`.

  *Proof.* Products of generators are generators on both sides. So the
  linear map `sum c_j W(v_j) -> sum c_j exp(i Y_(v_j))` preserves
  `tau(y* z)`, since both equal `sum conj(c_j) c_k exp(-Q(v_k - v_j)/2)`.
  It is therefore well defined, isometric and a `*`-homomorphism on the
  spans. These spans are weakly dense in the two algebras: on the Gaussian
  side the variables `exp(i t Y_v)` generate the sigma-algebra of the
  process. So the map extends to a trace-preserving isomorphism.
- **(F2) Crossed products.** If moreover unitaries `u_a in N` (`a in A`)
  form a representation with `u_a W(v) u_a* = W(a.v)` for a linear action
  of `A` preserving `Q`, and `tau(y u_a) = 0` for all `y in W*(W(V))` and
  `a != e`, then `W*(W(V), u_A)` is isomorphic to the Gaussian crossed
  product `L^infinity(Omega) rtimes A`. The algebraic crossed product maps
  onto the covariant pair with the same trace, and the trace is faithful.

The Gaussian space of a covariance `psi` is the case
`V = V_A = R^(A)` (finitely supported real functions),
`Q_psi(sum c_a [a]) = sum_(a,b) c_a c_b psi(a^-1 b)`, and
`a.[b] = [ab]`. This form is positive semidefinite because `psi` is
positive-definite, and it is `A`-invariant.

## 1. Proof of (Z1)

*Convexity.* Let `psi_1, psi_2 in CEcov`, `t in [0,1]`, and
`M_j = L^infinity(Omega_j) rtimes A`. The diagonal crossed product
`(L^infinity(Omega_1) (x) L^infinity(Omega_2)) rtimes A` maps into
`M_1 (x) M_2` by `(y_1 (x) y_2) u_a -> y_1 u_a (x) y_2 u_a`. The images of
`u_a` form a representation implementing the diagonal action, and

```text
tau((y_1 (x) y_2)(u_a (x) u_a)) = tau(y_1 u_a) tau(y_2 u_a) = 0     (a != e),
```

so this is a trace-preserving embedding (F2). By (F0) it is CE. Inside it
put `Y_a = sqrt(t) Y^1_a + sqrt(1-t) Y^2_a`. This is a Gaussian process with
covariance `t psi_1 + (1-t) psi_2`, translated by the diagonal action. By
(F1) and (F2), `W*(exp(i Y_v), u_A)` is the Gaussian crossed product of
`t psi_1 + (1-t) psi_2`, and it is CE as a subalgebra.

*Closure.* Let `psi_n in CEcov` converge pointwise to `psi`. Then `psi` is
a covariance. Let `N = prod_omega M_n` with `M_n` the crossed product of
`psi_n`. For `v in V_A` put `W(v) = (exp(i Y^(n)_v))_n` and `u_a = (u_a)_n`.
The relations `W(v)W(w) = W(v+w)` and `u_a W(v) u_a* = W(a.v)` hold
coordinatewise. Since `Q_(psi_n)(v)` is a finite sum of values of
`psi_n`,

```text
tau(W(v)) = lim_omega exp(-Q_(psi_n)(v)/2) = exp(-Q_psi(v)/2).
```

For `y` in the span of the `W(v)` and `a != e`, `tau(y u_a)` is the
`omega`-limit of zeros. By Kaplansky density this extends to all of
`W*(W(V_A))`. So (F1) and (F2) identify `W*(W(V_A), u_A)` with the
Gaussian crossed product of `psi`. It is countably generated inside an
ultraproduct of CE algebras, hence CE by (F0).

## 2. Proof of (Z2): tensor central limit

Replacing `B` by the `A`-invariant separable subalgebra generated by the
translates of `x` keeps `B rtimes A` CE, so assume `B` is separable. Put
`M = B rtimes A` and `M_N = ((x)^N B) rtimes A` for the diagonal action. As
in Section 1, `M_N` embeds trace-preservingly into `(x)^N M` by
`(y_1 (x) ... (x) y_N) u_a -> y_1 u_a (x) ... (x) y_N u_a`, so `M_N` is CE.

The function `psi_x` is real, because `tau(yz) = tau(zy)` is the conjugate
of `tau((yz)*)` for self-adjoint `y, z`. It is positive-definite, and
`psi_x(c) = ||x||_2^2 = 1` on `C`. So it is a covariance. For
`v = sum c_a [a] in V_A` put `x_v = sum c_a alpha_a(x)`, which is self-adjoint,
bounded and of trace zero, with `||x_v||_2^2 = Q_(psi_x)(v)` and
`alpha_a(x_v) = x_(a.v)`. Define

```text
W_N(v) = exp(i x_v / sqrt N) (x) ... (x) exp(i x_v / sqrt N)  in  (x)^N B.
```

The tensor legs commute, so this equals `exp(i N^(-1/2) sum_k x_v^(k))`.
Moreover `u_a W_N(v) u_a* = W_N(a.v)`.

*Two expansions.* Let `p, q` be self-adjoint with `||p||, ||q|| <= epsilon <= 1`.
Taylor expansion gives

```text
exp(ip) = 1 + ip - p^2/2 + R_1,                      ||R_1|| <= epsilon^3,
exp(-i(p+q)) exp(ip) exp(iq) = 1 + (qp - pq)/2 + R_2,  ||R_2|| <= c epsilon^3,
```

with `c` absolute. In the second product the first-order terms cancel,
and the second-order terms are
`-(p+q)^2/2 - p^2/2 - q^2/2 + (p+q)p + (p+q)q - pq = (qp - pq)/2`.
Commutators have trace zero. With `p = x_v/sqrt N` and `q = x_w/sqrt N`,
and `tau(x_v) = 0`:

```text
tau(exp(i x_v/sqrt N)) = 1 - Q(v)/(2N) + O(N^(-3/2)),
tau(r_N) = 1 + O(N^(-3/2)),    r_N = exp(-i x_(v+w)/sqrt N) exp(i x_v/sqrt N) exp(i x_w/sqrt N).
```

Hence

```text
tau(W_N(v)) = tau(exp(i x_v/sqrt N))^N -> exp(-Q(v)/2),
||W_N(v)W_N(w) - W_N(v+w)||_2^2 = 2 - 2 Re tau(r_N)^N -> 0.
```

The second line uses `W_N(v+w)* W_N(v) W_N(w) = r_N^((x)N)` and
`||U - 1||_2^2 = 2 - 2 Re tau(U)` for unitaries.

*Limit.* In `prod_omega M_N`, the unitaries `W(v) = (W_N(v))_N` and the
constant `u_a` satisfy (W) with `Q = Q_(psi_x)`, the covariance relation,
and `tau(y u_a) = 0` for `a != e`. The last holds because it holds for
`y in (x)^N B` in each `M_N`, then passes to spans and Kaplansky limits.
By (F1) and (F2), `W*(W(V_A), u_A)` is the Gaussian crossed product of
`psi_x`, and it is CE by (F0). So `psi_x in CEcov`.

## 3. Proof of (Z3): a nontrivial CE covariance is a leak

Let `psi in CEcov` with `psi != 1`, and let
`N = L^infinity(Omega_psi) rtimes A`. It is separable and CE. By (F0) there
is a trace-preserving embedding of `N` into a tracial matrix ultraproduct
`Q`. Put `pi(a) = u_a`. Since `tau(u_a) = 0` for `a != e`, `pi` extends to a
canonical trace-preserving embedding `pi : L(A) -> Q`. Put
`U = exp(i Y_e) in N`.

- *`U` is `C`-central.* For `c in C`, `u_c U u_c* = exp(i Y_c)`, and
  `E|Y_c - Y_e|^2 = 2 - 2 psi(c) = 0`. So `U in pi(L(C))' cap Q`.
- *`U` is not `A`-central.* Choose `a` with `psi(a) != 1`. Gaussian
  integration by parts gives, for centered jointly Gaussian variables,
  `E[Y_w exp(i Y_v)] = i E[Y_v Y_w] exp(-E[Y_v^2]/2)`. So

  ```text
  E[Y_e exp(i Y_a)] = i psi(a) e^(-1/2),      E[Y_e exp(i Y_e)] = i e^(-1/2).
  ```

  These differ, so `u_a U u_a* = exp(i Y_a) != U`, and
  `U notin pi(L(A))' cap Q`.

Thus condition 2 of `arithmetic-hnn-nonhyperlinearity-equals-global-collapse`
(`n = 3`, `p = 2`) fails. By that established equivalence, the HNN group
`A *_C (C x Z)` and the double `A *_C A` are hyperlinear. The same node,
together with `rf-perfect-pair-transfer-equals-regular-rcc` (as recorded in
the statement of `projective-trace-square-transfer-for-sl3-pair`), gives
`(PTS1)` the same truth value as these conditions. So `(PTS1)` fails.

## 4. Proof of (Z4)

*If `(PTS1)` holds*, then by (Z3) no covariance other than 1 lies in
`CEcov`. Since `1 in CEcov`, `CEcov = {1}`.

*If `(PTS1)` fails*, the HNN group is hyperlinear. Condition 3 of
`binary-free-and-ordinary-coset-wreaths-share-hyperlinearity` then says
that `L^infinity(R^(A/C), gamma^(A/C)) rtimes A` is CE. With independent
standard Gaussians `Z_p` (`p in A/C`) and `Y_a = Z_(aC)`, this is exactly
the Gaussian crossed product of `1_C`. So `1_C in CEcov`.

Let `eta in ell^2_R(A/C)` be a finitely supported unit vector that is
`C`-invariant, and put `Y^eta_a = sum_p eta(p) Z_(ap)`. Then

```text
E[Y^eta_a Y^eta_b] = sum_p eta(p) eta(b^-1 a p) = <lambda(a^-1 b) eta, eta> =: psi_eta(a^-1 b),
```

and `psi_eta` is a covariance, because `eta` is `C`-invariant. The
variables `exp(i Y^eta_v)` together with `u_A` generate, by (F1) and (F2),
the Gaussian crossed product of `psi_eta` inside that of `1_C`. So
`psi_eta in CEcov`.

The `C`-orbits on `A/C` are finite, since `C` is commensurated. So every
`C`-invariant unit vector is an `ell^2`-limit of finitely supported
`C`-invariant unit vectors. The corresponding coefficients converge
pointwise. By (Z1), `CEcov` contains the closed convex hull `P_lambda`.
By (Z2) it also contains every `psi_x`.

The two alternatives are exclusive, because `1_C in P_lambda` and
`1_C != 1` (`C` is proper in `A`). The first alternative is equivalent to
`(PTS1)` by the first paragraph and (Z3); the second is equivalent to its
failure by the second paragraph.

*Single tests.* Let `psi_0 in P_lambda \ {1}`.
- If its crossed product is CE, (Z3) makes `(PTS1)` fail.
- If it is not CE, then `CEcov` does not contain `P_lambda`, so
  `CEcov = {1}` and `(PTS1)` holds.

## 5. Remarks (not used above)

- `C\A/C = K\G_2/K` and `A/C = G_2/K`. So a covariance is the restriction
  of a unique `K`-biinvariant locally constant positive-definite function
  on `G_2`. Positive-definiteness is checked on finite sets `g_j`, choosing
  `a_j in g_j K`. By Godement, covariances are therefore normalized
  measures on the positive-definite spherical dual of `(G_2, K)`.
  Presumably `P_lambda` corresponds to measures on the tempered part; this
  would need a Plancherel/temperedness import and is not claimed.
- Non-tempered covariances, for example the `K`-orbit-indicator covariance
  of `L^infinity(G_2/Lambda')` from
  `homogeneous-quotient-ce-refutes-arithmetic-commutant-collapse`, remain
  one-directional firewalls. The question of whether `CEcov != {1}` puts
  them in `CEcov` is open.
