Let `Q` be torsion-free and admit a presentation satisfying the
Cohen--Lyndon property. For every commutative regular ring `R`, inclusion
induces an isomorphism

```text
K_0(R) -> K_0(R[Q]).
```

This is the paper's degree-zero base-ring formulation of Farrell--Jones; it
is not the coefficient-rich Full Farrell--Jones conjecture.
