ESTABLISHED.  Let `M` be a modular machine with modulus `M >= 2`, `K = < x,
y, t | [x,y] = 1 > = Z^2 * Z`, `t(a,b) = x^-a y^-b t y^b x^a`, and let

```text
K_M = < K, r_i (i in I), l_j (j in J) |
        r_i^-1 g r_i = phi_i(g)  (g in G^(MM)_(a_i b_i)),
        l_j^-1 g l_j = psi_j(g)  (g in G^(MM)_(a_j b_j)) >,
G_M = < K_M, k | [k, <t, r_i, l_j>] = 1 >                            (MM1)
```

be Simpson's groups (`G'_M` and `(G'_M)'`; Definition 6 and Theorem 8 of his
2005 note; `BooneGroupTower`/`BooneGroupGoodness` in this repository), where
`phi_i : G^(MM)_(ab) -> G^(M^2,1)_(c0)` sends `t(a,b) -> t(c,0)`, `x^M ->
x^(M^2)`, `y^M -> y`, and `psi_j : G^(MM)_(ab) -> G^(1,M^2)_(0c)` sends
`t(a,b) -> t(0,c)`, `x^M -> x`, `y^M -> y^(M^2)`.  Then `K_M` and `G_M` are
regularly operator-MF realized, hence MF.  In particular the finitely
presented group with unsolvable word problem of Simpson's Theorem 8 is MF.

## Proof

*Enlarged base.*  Let `BS_x = < x, m_x | m_x x m_x^-1 = x^M >`, `BS_y = < y,
m_y | m_y y m_y^-1 = y^M >`, and

```text
B_0 = < t > * (BS_x x BS_y).                                         (MM2)
```

`K <= B_0` since `Z^2 = <x,y> <= BS_x x BS_y`.  Put `Theta_0 = id_<t> *
Ad(m_x m_y^-1)`, an automorphism of `B_0`; it satisfies `Theta_0(x^M) =
x^(M^2)`, `Theta_0(y^M) = y`, `Theta_0(t(a,b)) = x^(-Ma) y^(-b/M) t y^(b/M)
x^(Ma)`, with `y^(b/M) = m_y^-1 y^b m_y`.  With `h = y^(b/M) x^(Ma)` and `g =
x^-c h` (an element of the abelian group `Z[1/M]^2 <= BS_x x BS_y`, so `Ad(g)`
fixes `x, y`), `Ad(g) o Theta_0` restricts to `phi_i` on the three generators
of `G^(MM)_(a_i b_i)`, which are free (Simpson, Lemma 5), so

```text
phi_i = (Ad(g_i) o Theta_0)|,     psi_j = (Ad(g_j') o Theta_0^-1)|,   (MM3)
```

the second by the symmetric computation with `x` and `y` exchanged.

*Central tower.*  By [[asymmetric-hnn-embeds-in-central-hnn-of-inner-envelope]]
(multiple letters, same Britton argument), `K_M <= < B_0 x| F, z_i, z_j |
[z_i, G^(MM)_(a_i b_i)] = [z_j, G^(MM)_(a_j b_j)] = 1 >` where `F` is free
on letters `theta_i, theta_j` acting through `Ad(g_i) Theta_0` and `Ad(g_j')
Theta_0^-1`, and `r_i = z_i theta_i`, `l_j = z_j theta_j`.  Replacing
`theta_i` by `theta'_i = g_i^-1 theta_i` (acting by `Theta_0`) and
`theta_j` by `theta'_j = g_j'^-1 theta_j` (acting by `Theta_0^-1`), and
fixing one letter `theta = theta'_(i_0)`, every `w = theta'_i theta^-1` and
`w' = theta'_j theta` centralizes `B_0`.  Hence

```text
B_0 x| F = < B_0 x|_(Theta_0) <theta>, w, w' | [w, B_0] = [w', B_0] = 1 >,   (MM4)
```

a multiple central HNN extension of the mapping torus `B_0 x|_(Theta_0) Z`
over `B_0`.

*The mapping torus is an amalgam of amenable groups.*  In `B_0 x|_(Theta_0)
<theta>`, put `theta'' = (m_x m_y^-1)^-1 theta`, which centralizes `BS_x x
BS_y`, while `theta` centralizes `t`.  Therefore

```text
B_0 x|_(Theta_0) Z = ( (BS_x x BS_y) x <theta''> ) *_<theta> ( <t> x <theta> ),   (MM5)
```

the amalgam of `BS(1,M)^2 x Z` and `Z^2` over the infinite cyclic subgroup
generated by `theta = m_x m_y^-1 theta''` on the left and `theta` on the
right.  Both factors are amenable and residually finite.  By Shulman's
Theorem 19 (`C*` of an amalgam of amenable groups is MF) the full group
C-star algebra of `(MM5)` is MF; moreover `(MM5)` is residually finite: the
amalgamated subgroup lies in the retract `<m_x> x <m_y> x <theta''> ~= Z^3`
of the left factor and is a direct factor of the right one, so it is closed
on both sides, and choosing quotients `BS(1,M) -> (Z[1/M]/nZ[1/M]) x| Z/k`
with `M^k = 1 (mod n)` together with `Z -> Z/k` gives images of `theta` of
the same order `k` on both sides; the amalgam of the two finite quotients
over `Z/k` is virtually free, and Britton-reduced words stay reduced.  Hence
`(MM5)` is regularly operator-MF realized.

*Climbing.*  [[regular-mf-central-hnn-closure]] applied to `(MM4)`, then to
each `z_i, z_j`, then to Simpson's `k` (central over `<t, r_i, l_j>`, a
subgroup of the enlarged tower) gives regular realizations at every stage;
`K_M` and `G_M` are subgroups of the corresponding stages (central HNN
extensions of a subgroup embed in those of the overgroup over the same
subgroup, by Britton), and subgroups of MF groups are MF.

## Why the Turing-machine version does not follow

In Rotman's Turing-machine Boone group the instruction twists act on the
free factor of state letters by different transvections, which are not
equal modulo inner automorphisms; this is
[[boone-base-has-corona-inner-instruction-automorphisms]].  The modular
machine stores its state in the residue classes of `(alpha, beta)` and has no
state letters, which is exactly why every twist is `Theta_0^(+-1)` up to
conjugation.
