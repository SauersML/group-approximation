Sections 1--2 put all orthogonal vertex and marker idempotents into
the same elementary root position. Their span gives the finite
elementary abelian subgroup. The product root is nontrivial exactly
at its selected vertices and its selected or incident edges, so the
normal-lattice theorem gives its exact normal closure and intersection
formula. Complete binary codes provide the short words. Cycle closure
supplies the one extra reflection relator. Unions of finite incidence
coordinates give the bounded subfamily of arbitrary input relations.
