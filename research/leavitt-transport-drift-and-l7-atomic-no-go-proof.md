## Drift-rank lower bound

Let the original equation have degree `d>0`.  Reidemeister--Schreier
rewriting in the index-`d` kernel gives `d` translated relators, and every
one has stable-letter exponent one in `z=t^d`.  The exponent-zero sublattice
of their relation lattice therefore has rank at most `d-1`: it is generated
by differences of translated relators.

Transport of a two-generator law requires two independent zero-drift
identifications, one for each generator.  Reserving the common affine drift
therefore forces

```text
d-1 >= 2,  hence d >= 3.                              (DT1)
```

This is a lower bound for the direct relation-transport architecture, not a
claim about every possible KL picture.  At even `t`-length, degree has the
same parity as length, so the corresponding bound is `d>=4`.

## The minimal odd case

The first odd possibility is length seven, degree three.  Up to cyclic
rotation its sign sequence is one of

```text
+++++--,   ++++-+-,   +++-++-.                       (DT2)
```

Use the natural atomic compiler alphabet

```text
a,e,q,c=x_13(s_0),p^(+-1),r^(+-1)=(qp)^(+-1),1.
```

At every cyclic sign change require one of the nontrivial involutions
`a,e,q,c`.  Require full support in the exact sense that `a,e,p` occur and
either `q` or `r` occurs; `c` is generated by `a,p`, while `q=rp^(-1)`.

For every assignment and each translate having a single `z`, solve that
relator for `z` and reduce the other two in the free product of the three
coefficient copies.  A direct two-generator transport would make both
residual relators two-block identifications involving the same pair of
copies.  The exact counts are

```text
sign type       assignments       direct transports
+++++--             242040                 0
++++-+-               45120                 0
+++-++-               45120                 0
total                332280                 0.        (DT3)
```

The artifact performs only symbolic Reidemeister--Schreier rewriting and
free reduction.  Thus `(DT3)` is independent of finite quotients or a chosen
Leavitt representation.

This fences the literal atomic slot encoder, not composite coefficient
packets.  The next viable target is degree four at length eight, where four
translated relators leave three zero-drift differences and composite packets
may encode the two transports while absorbing the remaining compatibility
relation.
