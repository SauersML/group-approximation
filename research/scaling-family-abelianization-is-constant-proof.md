Write all generators additively in the abelianization.  The three linear
torsion relators give

```text
3x=0,   3y=0,   2z=0.
```

The relator `(xz)^3=1` also gives `3x+3z=0`, hence `3z=0`; together
with `2z=0` this gives `z=0`.  The two relators

```text
(x^(-1) z x y)^2=1,       (y^(-1) z y x)^2=1
```

then give `2y=0` and `2x=0`.  Combining these with `3y=0` and `3x=0`
gives `x=y=0`.

The three cyclic permutation relations involving `x` identify
`v_1=v_2=v_3=:v`.  Abelianizing

```text
y v_2 y^(-1)=v_2^(-1)v_3
```

now gives `v=-v+v`, hence `v=0`.  Thus every generator of the affine
base dies in the abelianization.  This computation uses only base relators
and is independent of `m`; the relations
`t v_i t^(-1)=v_i^m` add nothing after `v_i=0`.

The distinguished word `w_m` is a commutator, so it is already zero in an
abelian quotient.  Consequently all eight relators asserting centrality of
`w_m` abelianize to tautologies.  The only remaining relation is `2c=0`,
and no relation involves a nonzero multiple of `t`.

Therefore `E_m^ab` is generated by `t,c` subject only to `2c=0`.  To see
that there is no hidden quotient, send all six base generators to zero,
`t` to `(1,0)` and `c` to `(0,1)` in `Z direct_sum C_2`; every displayed
relator is satisfied.  This proves

```text
E_m^ab ~= Z direct_sum C_2
```

for every `m>=2`.
