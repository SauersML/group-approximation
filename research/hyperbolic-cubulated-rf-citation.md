**Citation.** These are quoted statements, not re-derived. The same chain is consumed
by `cprime-one-sixth-presentations-are-hyperlinear`.
* I. Agol, *The virtual Haken conjecture*, Doc. Math. 18 (2013), Theorem 1.1:
  hyperbolic groups acting properly and cocompactly on CAT(0) cube complexes are
  virtually special.
* F. Haglund and D. T. Wise, *Special cube complexes*, GAFA 17 (2008): virtually special
  groups virtually embed in right-angled Artin groups, so they are linear over `Z`
  and residually finite.
* D. T. Wise, *Cubulating small cancellation groups*, GAFA 14 (2004): finite classical
  `C'(1/6)` and `C'(1/4)–T(4)` presentations act properly cocompactly on CAT(0) cube
  complexes.
