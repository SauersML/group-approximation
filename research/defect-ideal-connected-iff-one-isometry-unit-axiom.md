Let `A_T = T_2 *_C T_2`, `e = iota_1(1 − s_1s_1^* − s_2s_2^*)`, `J` the closed ideal generated by `e`,
and `B_J = J + iota_1(T_2)`, as in `toeplitz-defect-ideal-unitization-k1-injective`. Let `S ∈ B_J` be an
isometry such that `1 − SS^*` is properly infinite and full in `B_J`. For example `S = iota_1(s_2)`
works: `1 − SS^* ≥ iota_1(s_1s_1^*) ~ 1`.

Then for every `u = 1 + x ∈ U(1 + J)`:
1. `C_S(u) = 1 + SxS^*` lies in `U_0(J~)`;
2. `U(J~)` is path-connected iff `u ~_h 1 + SxS^*` in `U(J~)` for every such `u`.

The earlier form of this equivalence assumed the halving homotopy (`o2-free-circle-halving-homotopy`).
It appears in the quotient-criterion Attempts of `toeplitz-defect-ideal-unitization-k1-injective`,
in the LXI root, and in `k1-defect-ideal-z-stable-quotients-2026-09-13.md` §4. That hypothesis is not
needed.

So the open claim `toeplitz-defect-ideal-unitization-k1-injective` is exactly the unit axiom for one
isometry, applied to unitaries in `1 + J`.
