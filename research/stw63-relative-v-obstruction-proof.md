The first prerequisite says that equivalence of `p` and `q` in `D` is
equivalent to approximate unitary equivalence of `phi` and `psi`.  The second
says that their images are always equivalent in `E`.  Hence any failure of
LXIII is exactly a failure of (1) to reflect equivalence on this distinguished
pair.  Equality in `V(E)` but inequality in `V(D)` is the same assertion in
projection-monoid language.
