Notation as in the claim. All algebras are finite-dimensional C*-algebras and all subalgebras are unital
*-subalgebras. `k_min(B)` is the least dimension of an irreducible representation of `B`.

**Standard facts.**
- (T1) Irreducible representations of `B_1 (x) ... (x) B_r` are exactly `W_1 (x) ... (x) W_r` with `W_i` irreducible
  for `B_i`.
- (T2) Burnside: an algebra acting irreducibly on `C^n` acts as all of `M_n`.
- (T3) Unital *-homomorphisms out of `M_d` or out of `A` are injective, because these algebras are simple. The image of
  a C*-homomorphism is closed.

## Step 0. Supports

For a region `R`, a site `k in R` and `x in A_R = A_k (x) A_(R \ k)`, the slice span `sl_k(x)` is the span of all
`(id_k (x) omega)(x)`, `omega in A_(R \ k)^*`. Writing `x = sum_i s_i (x) r_i` with the `r_i` linearly independent,
`sl_k(x) = span{s_i}`. `sl_k(x^*) = sl_k(x)^*`, and `sl_k(1) = C1`.

Fix `h`. `S(h,k)` is the *-algebra generated by `sl_k(x)` for `x in Phi(A_h) <= A_(hN)`, `k in hN`. It is unital and
self-adjoint. Equivariance gives `Phi(A_h) = alpha_h Phi(A_e)`, hence `S(h, hm) = alpha_h S(e, m)` under the
canonical identification of site algebras. So `c_m := k_min(S(e,m)) = k_min(S(h,hm))` for all `h`.

**Lemma 1 (image inside the tensor product of supports).** `Phi(A_e) <= (x)_(m in N) S(e,m)`.

*Proof.* For a subspace `W <= A_m`, choose any linear projection `P_W` of `A_m` onto `W`. If `sl_m(x) <= W`, then
`(P_W (x) id)(x) = x`. For `x in Phi(A_e)` apply this at every `m in N` in turn. `x` is fixed by
`(x)_m P_(S(e,m))`, whose range is `(x)_m S(e,m)`. □

**Lemma 2 (one-site overlaps commute).** Let `U, V` be disjoint finite regions not containing `k`. Let
`x in A_U (x) A_k` and `y in A_k (x) A_V` commute in `A_(U u {k} u V)`. Then `sl_k(x)` and `sl_k(y)` commute. Hence if
`X <= A_U (x) A_k` and `Y <= A_k (x) A_V` commute elementwise, the algebras generated by their `k`-slices commute.

*Proof.* Write `x = sum_i a_i (x) b_i` with `(a_i)` independent in `A_U`, and `y = sum_j c_j (x) e_j` with `(e_j)`
independent in `A_V`. Then `xy - yx = sum_(i,j) a_i (x) [b_i, c_j] (x) e_j = 0`. The `a_i (x) - (x) e_j` are
independent, so `[b_i, c_j] = 0` for all `i, j`. Commutation of generating sets passes to the generated algebras. □

**Lemma 3 (Sidon gives (CS)).** If `|N cap gN| <= 1` for all `g != e`, then (CS) holds.

*Proof.* Let `h != h'` with `k in hN cap h'N`. Then `hN cap h'N = h (N cap h^-1 h' N) = {k}`. Put `U = hN \ k` and
`V = h'N \ k`, which are disjoint. `Phi(A_h) <= A_U (x) A_k` and `Phi(A_h') <= A_k (x) A_V` commute, because
`A_h, A_h'` commute and `Phi` is a homomorphism. Lemma 2 applies. □

## Step 1. Two inequalities

**(a) Lower bound.** `prod_(m in N) c_m >= d`.

*Proof.* For each `m`, pick an irreducible representation `W_m` of `S(e,m)` of dimension `c_m`.
- `W = (x)_m W_m` is a unital representation of `(x)_m S(e,m)`.
- By Lemma 1 it restricts to a unital representation of `Phi(A_e)`.
- By T3, `Phi(A_e) = M_d`, and every nonzero unital representation of `M_d` has dimension a positive multiple of `d`.
□

**(b) Upper bound under (CS).** `prod_(m in N) c_m <= d`.

*Proof.* Fix a site `k`. The elements `h` with `k in hN` are the `|N|` distinct elements `h_m = k m^-1`, `m in N`.
Put `B_m = S(h_m, k) <= A_k = M_d`, so that `k_min(B_m) = c_m`.
- Under (CS) the `B_m` pairwise commute, so the multiplication map `mu: (x)_m B_m -> M_d` is a unital
  *-homomorphism. Through `mu`, `C^d` is a unital representation of `(x)_m B_m`.
- Take any irreducible subrepresentation `W <= C^d`. By T1, `W = (x)_m W_m` with each `W_m` irreducible, so
  `dim W >= prod_m c_m`.
- Since `dim W <= d`, the bound follows. □

## Step 2. Equality forces the slot splitting

By (a) and (b), `prod_m c_m = d`. Fix a site `k` with `B_m` as in (b).

1. **`C^d` is irreducible.** Every irreducible subrepresentation `W` of `C^d` has `d >= dim W >= prod c_m = d`. So
   `C^d` is irreducible for `(x)_m B_m`, and `C^d = (x)_m W_m` with `dim W_m = c_m`.
2. **Each `B_m` is a matrix algebra.** Through this decomposition `B_m` acts as `pi_m (x) 1`, with `pi_m` its action
   on `W_m`.
   - `B_m <= M_d` acts faithfully, so `pi_m` is injective.
   - `pi_m` is irreducible, so by T2 `pi_m(B_m) = End(W_m)`.
   - Hence `B_m = M_(c_m)`.
3. **The site splits.** By T2 `mu` is onto, and `dim (x)_m B_m = prod c_m^2 = d^2`. So `mu` is a *-isomorphism and
   `A_k = (x)_(m in N) S(k m^-1, k)`.
4. **The image is the full tensor product of supports.** Take `k = e`, so `S(e,m) = alpha_m S(m^-1, e)` is also
   `M_(c_m)`. By Lemma 1, `Phi(A_e) <= (x)_m S(e,m)`, and the right side has dimension `prod c_m^2 = d^2`. By T3,
   `dim Phi(A_e) = d^2`, so `Phi(A_e) = (x)_m S(e,m)`. Translating, `Phi(A_h) = (x)_m S(h, hm)`.

## Step 3. Surjectivity and inverse

- `S(h, hm) <= Phi(A_h) <= Phi(A)` for every `h, m`, as the tensor slot `S(h,hm) (x) 1`.
- By Step 2.3, each `A_k` is generated by the `S(k m^-1, k) <= Phi(A)`. So `Phi(A)` contains every local algebra and
  is dense.
- By T3 `Phi(A)` is closed, so `Phi(A) = A`.
- `Phi^-1(A_k) = (x)_m Phi^-1(S(k m^-1, k))`, and `Phi^-1(S(k m^-1, k)) <= A_(k m^-1)`. So `Phi^-1(A_k) <= A_(k N^-1)`.
□

## Checks of the consequences in the claim

- **Two-point sets in torsion-free groups.** For `N = {x, y}`, a nontrivial coincidence of quotients needs
  `x y^-1 = y x^-1`, i.e. `(x y^-1)^2 = e`, which is impossible in a torsion-free group unless `x = y`. So two-point
  sets are left Sidon.
- **`{1, a, b}` in `F_2`.** The six quotients `a, b, a^-1, b^-1, a b^-1, b a^-1` are distinct, so the set is left
  Sidon.
- **Prime `d = p`.** `prod c_m = p` forces a single `c_m = p`.
- **Tree calibration.** `alpha(X_v) = X_c X_c'` and `alpha(Z_v) = Z_c^h Z_c'^h` have slices `X_c` and `Z_c^h`, which
  generate `M_p` at `c` (and likewise at `c'`).
  - Each vertex has one parent, so (b) at `c` reads `p <= p`.
  - (a) at `v` reads `p^2 >= p`.
  - Step 2 needs the two products to run over the same index set. That is exactly what a group supplies and the
    end-stabilizer tree does not.

## Numerical sanity checks

These are not load-bearing. See `experiments/quantum-sidon-supports-2026-09-17/sidon_supports-output.txt`.
- **E1.** Split wiring over `Z` with `d = 6 = 2 . 3` and random site unitaries:
  - supports are `M_2`, `M_3` and `C`, with `k_min` product `6`;
  - the site-1 supports of `Phi(A_0)` and `Phi(A_1)` commute and generate `M_6`;
  - `Phi(A_0)` equals the tensor product of its supports.
- **E2 (teeth).** The cluster entangler has two-site overlaps. Its site supports `M_2` and `{1, Z}` do not commute,
  so Lemma 2 genuinely needs one-site overlaps.
- **E3.** For `B = {x (+) lambda 1_5}` in `M_10` and its commutant, `dim/centre` gives `169 > 100`, while `k_min` gives
  `1 <= 10`. This shows why the invariant is `k_min`.
- **E4.** A block-diagonal support `x (+) u x u^*` is abstractly `M_2`, and (a) holds with equality `4 >= 4`.
