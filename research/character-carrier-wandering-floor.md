Let `K<=Gamma` be finite, let `chi:K->S^1` be one dimensional, and put

```text
q=|K|^(-1) sum_(k in K) conjugate(chi(k)) k,
beta=tau_Gamma(q)=1/|K|.
```

Let `w in Gamma` have infinite order and let `V=qVq` be any
self-adjoint unitary in the `q` corner (`V^2=q`).  Then in the regular
representation

```text
||q w q-V||_2 >= (1-1/sqrt(2))/sqrt(|K|).              (FCF1)
```

Consequently every finite-dimensional tuple with sufficiently accurate
canonical profile on the fixed support of `q,w,V` has the same lower bound
up to `o(1)`, after the fixed packet has been exactified.  This conclusion
does not assume residual finiteness or a Bass--Serre presentation.

Applied to a one-dimensional-character realization of the Fanizza
clock/flag carrier, `(FCF1)` gives a sharp escape alternative to `(FCW1)`:
canonical finite models retain a dimension-independent compression error.
Thus a successful coherent-verifier promotion must itself exclude all such
canonical profiles.  The verifier reflection and positive packet density
do not supply that exclusion; it is already the nonhyperlinear inference.
