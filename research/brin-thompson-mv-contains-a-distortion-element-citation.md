Citation import. No proof is re-derived here.

A. Callard and V. Salo, *Distortion element in the automorphism group of a
full shift*, arXiv:2208.00685v2 (arXiv stamp 31 Oct 2022; title page dated
November 1, 2022). Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 44 (2024) 1757–1817; the arXiv
abstract page records a later revision on 15 May 2023. Lane z2-19-glnz-q-2v read
the v2 PDF on 2026-09-13, pages 1, 3 and 5. The quotations below are from
v2; the numbering in the journal version was not checked.

- **Abstract (p. 1):** "We obtain also that groups of Turing machines and the
  higher-dimensional Brin-Thompson groups mV admit distortion elements; in
  particular, 2V (unlike V) does not admit a proper action on a CAT(0) cube
  complex."
- **p. 5, before Theorem E:** "All groups of Turing machines in turn embed in the
  higher-dimensional Brin-Thompson mV for m ≥ 2 introduced by Brin [11], and we
  obtain the following."
- **Theorem E (p. 5):** "The Brin-Thompson group mV contains a distortion
  element; indeed there is an element f such that |f^n| = O(log^4 n)."
- **Theorem D (p. 5):** "Let n ≥ 2, k ≥ 1. Then the group of Turing machines
  RTM(n,k) contains a distortion element; indeed there is a finitely-generated
  subgroup G = ⟨F⟩ and an element f such that |f^n|_F = O(log^4 n)."
- **Corollary 1.2 (p. 5):** "The Brin-Thompson group mV does not act properly on
  a CAT(0) cube complex for m ≥ 2."

On p. 2 the paper defines a distortion element as one of infinite order whose
word norm |g^n| grows sublinearly.
