If `R` is quasidiagonal then every finitely generated unital
`B ⊂ R` is quasidiagonal with a faithful quasidiagonal trace
(restriction of `τ_R`), and the asymptotically norm-multiplicative
u.c.p. models assemble into a unital \*-homomorphism
`B → ∏_ω M_{k_n} / ⊕ M_{k_n}`, which is injective because the trace
is recovered in the limit and is faithful on `B`.  That is the MF
embedding.
