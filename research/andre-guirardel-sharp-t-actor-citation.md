The abstract and Theorem 1.1 of André--Guirardel state the existence of an
infinite two-generated simple sharply two-transitive property-`(T)` group.
Theorem 1.4 gives the version with four conjugacy classes.  The title,
abstract, and theorem consistently assert finite generation, not finite
presentation.  The construction described in Sections 4--5 passes through
increasing constructions and iterative small-cancellation quotients.  No
finite-presentation theorem or finite-generation theorem for the point
stabilizer is stated.  The audit therefore imports `(AGA1)` and no stronger
finiteness conclusion.
