**ESTABLISHED** through `leavitt-chen-module-line-action-proof` (not independently
reviewed; no novelty is claimed).

Let `p` be a prime, `K = F_p`, `R = L_K(1,2)` with generators `s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1`
(`t_i s_j = δ_ij`, `s_0 t_0 + s_1 t_1 = 1`), and `U = R^×`.
- Let `D ⊂ {0,1}^N` be the tail class of `0^∞`, i.e. the eventually-zero sequences.
- Let `W = K[D]` be the vector space with basis `δ_ξ`, `ξ ∈ D`, and let `R` act by
  `s_i δ_ξ = δ_(iξ)` and `t_i δ_(jξ) = δ_ij δ_ξ`.
- Put `G = U / K^×`, and let `P(W)` be the set of lines of `W`.

Then:
1. `G` acts faithfully on `P(W)`;
2. the action is 2-transitive;
3. the stabilizer in `U` of the vector `δ = δ_(0^∞)` is generated by four pieces:
   - the copy `P_0 + s_1 U t_1` of `U`, where `P_0 = s_0 t_0`;
   - the transvection `1 + s_0 t_1`;
   - the Thompson element `g = s_00 t_0 + s_01 t_10 + s_1 t_11`;
   - the element `w_0 = 1 + s_10 t_0 − s_10 t_00`.

   So it is finitely generated. The stabilizer of the line `[δ]` in `G` is
   isomorphic to it, hence also finitely generated.

**Mechanism.**
- **Transitivity:** every finite configuration of points of `D` sits in pairwise
  incomparable cones of one length, where matrix units realize all of `GL_m(K)`.
- **Stabilizer:**
  - Germs at `0^∞` give a column vector over `K[s, s^(-1)]`.
  - The column is unimodular, and all entries away from `0^∞` are divisible by `s − 1`.
  - Two elementary operations reduce it to `e_(0^∞)` by the Euclidean algorithm, one
    from the tail-fixing subgroup and one a conjugate of `w_0`.
  - What remains fixes a cone `0^k` pointwise, and those units are conjugates by `g`
    of an extension of `U` by `(R, +)`. Since `R` is spanned by `U`, that extension
    is generated by `U` and one transvection.
