Dead.  Recorded so the obstruction is visible.  The proposal was to take the
known non-MF groups `H = EL_12(L_{F_2}(1,2))` (simple, Kazhdan) or the
torsion-free finitely presented Kazhdan group `Q` and embed them in `U(R)`.
Both are finitely generated with property (T), and neither is residually
finite (`H` is infinite simple; every nontrivial quotient of `Q` is non-MF,
so `Q` has no finite quotients).  By
`kazhdan-groups-in-hyperfinite-unitary-group-are-rf` a finitely generated
Kazhdan subgroup of `U(R)` is residually finite, so neither embeds.

Invalidated by `kazhdan-groups-in-hyperfinite-unitary-group-are-rf`; the
`requires: []` is the house pattern for a recorded dead route and is
inert only because that obstruction is established.
