Let `A` be a C-star algebra whose lattice of closed two-sided ideals is
finite.  If `A` is weakly purely infinite, then `A` is purely infinite.

No separability, nuclearity, unitality, exactness, stable-rank, real-rank, or
Hausdorff-spectrum assumption is imposed.  Equivalently, Problem LXXII has a
positive answer for weakly purely infinite C-star algebras with finite ideal
lattice.
