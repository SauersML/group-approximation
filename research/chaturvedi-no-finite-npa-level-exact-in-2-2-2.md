In the `(2,2,2)` Bell scenario, for every level `k` of the standard NPA
hierarchy the relaxation `Q_k` strictly contains the quantum set `Q`:

```text
Q_k \ Q  is nonempty  for every finite k.
```

The separation is exhibited along a symmetric doubly tilted CHSH functional, on
which the quantum maximum and the level-`k` bound approach the critical
parameter at different rates, with the obstruction traced to the Motzkin
polynomial being nonnegative but not a sum of squares.  Nonquantum behaviours
accumulate at deterministic limit points.  This is the minimal scenario, where
the earlier finite-exactness results for plain CHSH and for the non-critical
tilted variants had left the question open.

Source: Anubhav Chaturvedi, *No Finite NPA Level Characterizes the Complete
Quantum Set in the Simplest Bell Scenario*, arXiv:2607.14569.
