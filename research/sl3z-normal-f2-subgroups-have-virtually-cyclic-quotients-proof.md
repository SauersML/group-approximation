Full proof: artifact, Sections 3–4.

**Classical imports** (recalled, not re-read).

- (I3) Nielsen: `Aut(F(a, b))` is generated by `σ: (a, b) -> (b, a)`,
  `ι: (a, b) -> (a^-1, b)` and `τ: (a, b) -> (ab, b)`; also
  `Out(F_2) ≅ GL_2(Z)`.
- (I4) `GL_2(Z)` is virtually free, e.g. via the torsion-free congruence
  subgroup `Γ(3)`.
- (I5) Nielsen–Schreier; a solvable free group is trivial or `Z`.
- (I6) P. Hall: finite-index overgroups of finitely presented groups are
  finitely presented.

**Lemma N.** For `α in Aut(F(a, b))` and `c = [a, b]`, `α(c)` is conjugate to
`c` or `c^-1`.

- The set of such `α` is closed under products and inverses.
- It contains the generators: `σ(c) = c^-1`, `ι(c) = a^-1 c^-1 a`, `τ(c) = c`.

**Proof of the claim.** Let `N = <a, b>` and `c = [a, b]`, which has infinite
order.

1. *`C_G(N)` is finite.* Otherwise `<N, C_G(N)>` would be polycyclic by
   `sl3z-products-of-infinite-subgroups-are-polycyclic`, yet it contains `F_2`.
2. *Monodromy kernel.* If `g` acts on `N` as conjugation by `n in N`, then
   `n^-1 g in C_G(N)`. So the kernel of `G/N -> Out(N)` is
   `C_G(N) N/N ≅ C_G(N)`, which is finite because `Z(F_2) = 1`.
3. *Orbit of size at most two.* By Lemma N, `G` maps `[c]_N` into
   `{[c]_N, [c^-1]_N}`. So `G^+ = Stab_G([c]_N)` has index at most 2, and by
   `sl3z-normal-subgroup-class-stabilizers-are-polycyclic`,
   `G^+/N ≅ C_G(c)/C_N(c)` is polycyclic.
4. *Image in `GL_2(Z)`.* Let `Q` be the image of `G/N` and `Q^+` the image of
   `G^+/N`. With a free `Φ <= GL_2(Z)` of finite index, `Q^+ ∩ Φ` has finite
   index in `Q` and is solvable and free, hence trivial or `Z`. So `Q` is
   finite or virtually `Z`.
5. *Lift.* `G/N` is an extension of the finite kernel `K` by `Q`. If `Q` is
   infinite, take the preimage `P` of an infinite cyclic finite-index subgroup
   and `t in P` mapping to a generator. Then `P = K<t>`, and `<t>` has index at
   most `|K|` in `P`. This gives (2).
6. *Infinite-order monodromy.* If `gN` has infinite order and its monodromy
   has order `e`, then `g^e N in K` is torsion, a contradiction. This gives (3).
7. *Finite presentation.* Either `N` has finite index in `G`, or `N x| <t>`
   has finite index with presentation
   `<a, b, t | t a t^-1 = φ(a), t b t^-1 = φ(b)>`. P. Hall gives (4).

Sanity check: the commutator subgroup of `SL_2(Z) <= SL_3(Z)` is free of rank
2 with index 12, so the quotient is finite, consistent with (2).

Experiment: `experiments/sl3z-normal-f2-obstruction-2026-09-16/lemma_n_check.py`
checks the generator images of Lemma N at the word level (output in
`lemma_n_check.out`). It confirms `σ(c) ~ c^-1`, `ι(c) ~ c^-1` and `τ(c) = c`,
that `c` is not conjugate to `c^-1`, and that 2000 random products pass. The
generator check is the proof content; the random products only sanity-check
the bookkeeping.
