## Statement

Let `F ∈ B_A` (for example free of finite rank), `φ : F → Q` a homomorphism, and
`P = P_φ = {(g, h) ∈ F × F : φ(g) = φ(h)}`.

1. **Positive.** If `Q ∈ B_A`, then `P ∈ CR(F × F)`
   (`pbh-coset-wreath-subgroups-closure-properties`). So, for all `A, K ∈ B_A`, each of the
   following lies in `B_A`:
   - `A ≀_{(F×F)/P} (F × F)`;
   - `(F × F) *_P (P × K)`;
   - the double `(F × F) *_P (F × F)`.
2. **Negative.** Suppose `F` is finitely generated, `φ` is onto, and `Q` has unsolvable word
   problem. Then `Z/2 ≀_{(F×F)/P} (F × F)` has unsolvable word problem, so `P ∉ CR(F × F)`.

**Finite presentation.** If `F` is free of rank `k ≥ 2` and `Q = ⟨F | r_1, …, r_s⟩` is finitely
presented, then `P` is generated by the diagonal `{(g,g)}` and `(r_j, 1)`, so it is finitely
generated (Mihailova; Baumslag–Roseblade). The HNN extension `(F × F) *_P (P × Z)` and the
double are then finitely presented. So item 1 gives finitely presented groups in `B_A`
built over Mihailova subgroups: one for every finitely presented `Q ∈ B_A` (for example
Thompson's `V`, or any finitely presented simple group with a type (A) action).

**Reading.**
- Item 1 is the positive half of `pbh-wreath-closure-fails-over-undecidable-stabilizers`: the
  same Mihailova construction is harmless as soon as the quotient is in `B_A`.
- Under the permutational Boone–Higman conjecture, `P ∈ CR(F × F)` iff `Q` has solvable word
  problem, for finitely generated `F` and `Q`.
