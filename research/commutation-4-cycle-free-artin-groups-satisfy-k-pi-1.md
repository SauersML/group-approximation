Let `Λ` be the Coxeter diagram of `A_Λ` (vertices `S`, an edge `st` labelled `m_st` whenever
`m_st ≥ 3`, including `∞`). Let `Λ^c` be the complement graph: `st` is an edge of `Λ^c` exactly
when `m_st = 2`. If `Λ^c` has no embedded 4-cycle, then `A_Λ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture.

If `A_Λ` has dimension at most 3 (every `T ⊆ S` generating a finite Coxeter group has
`|T| ≤ 3`), then `Λ^c` has no embedded 4-cycle, so `A_Λ` satisfies the conjecture.
