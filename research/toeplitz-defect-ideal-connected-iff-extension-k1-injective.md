Let `A_T = T_2 *_C T_2` with inclusions `iota_1, iota_2`, `e = iota_1(1 - s_1s_1^* - s_2s_2^*)`,
`J` the closed ideal generated by `e`, and `B_J = J + iota_1(T_2)`. Then:

1. `B_J` is a unital, properly infinite C\*-subalgebra of `A_T`, and `B_J/J ≅ O_2`.
2. `U(J~)` is path-connected iff `B_J` is K1-injective. Here `K_*(B_J) ≅ K_*(J) = (Z, 0)`.
3. STW Problem LXI implies that `U(J~)` is path-connected.
4. LXI holds iff both the halving homotopy (`o2-free-circle-halving-homotopy`) holds and `U(J~)` is
   connected. A unitary of `J~` outside `U_0(J~)` refutes LXI.
5. `M(J)` contains no unital copy of `O_2`, because J has the unital quotient O_3 with `[1] ≠ 0` in
   `K_0(O_3)`. So `o2-multiplier-extension-k1-injective-iff-unitization` does not apply to J, and the
   quotient form `o2-quotient-extension-ideal-k1-injective-iff-algebra` is needed.

The same lemma gives a one-ideal form of LXI. Let `I_f = ker(A_T -> O_2)` be the kernel of the fold of the
two Cuntz quotients. By Blanchard arXiv:0804.4624v13, Proposition 4.2, LXI holds iff `U(I_f~)` is
connected (artifact, Theorem 7).
