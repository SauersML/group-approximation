Right multiplication of the conjugator by `S_3` conjugates the central class
sum `k` trivially.  Left multiplication by the `C_3` normalizer pulls through
the invariant idempotent `e`.  If the conjugator itself normalizes `C_3`, it
pulls entirely through and leaves `eke=0`.
