```text
pi(z)=pi(z_0)pi(z_1)=(-I)(-I)=I.
```

The same calculation applies to the two coordinates of a binary wreath
recursion.  Hence no untwisted homomorphic replication can preserve the
negative central character, and a cocycle phase is logically necessary.
