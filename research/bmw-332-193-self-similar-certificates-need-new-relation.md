**ESTABLISHED (hand proof over exact finite computations).** Proof:
`bmw-332-193-self-similar-certificates-proof`.

**Setting.**
- `K` is one of the four invariant-subtree automaton groups on the ternary tree: `#332` on the V or
  H side, or `#193` on the V or H side. They come from `selfrep.build` and are tabulated in `aut.py`
  (`checkaut.log`).
- Each `K` is generated by four involutions: three "D-letters", which generate an elementary
  abelian group and have sections among themselves, and a letter `τ` acting as `(01)(τ,τ,τ)`.
- `A` is the abstract group:
  - `A = V_4 * C_2` for 332V, where `a_2 = a_0a_1`;
  - `A = C_2^3 *_{⟨c⟩} S_3` for the other three, where `c` is the product of the three D-letters and
    `S_3 = ⟨τ, c⟩`.
- `π : A → K` sends letters to letters.
- `G_1 = φ_0(St_K(0))` is the section group at the vertex `0`. The *missing letter* `ℓ` is `τ` for
  `#332` (letter 3) and the D-letter `1` for `#193`. It is the only letter not visibly in `G_1`
  (`amalg12_*.log`).

**Theorem.** For each of the four:
1. **Agreement to length 12.** `π` is a well-defined surjection. Every cyclically reduced word of
   length `≤ 12` that is trivial in `K` is already trivial in `A`: 603 such words for 332V and 242
   for each of the others. So `ker π` contains no nontrivial cyclically reduced word of length `≤ 12`.
2. **The recursion lives on A.** The sections of every defining relator of `A` are trivial in `A`.
   Hence `St_A(0) := π^{-1}(St_K(0))` carries a section homomorphism `φ_0^A : St_A(0) → A` with
   `π ∘ φ_0^A = φ_0 ∘ π`.
3. **A non-congruence index-3 obstruction.**
   - There is a surjection `ρ : A → S_3` with `φ_0^A(St_A(0)) ≤ M := ρ^{-1}(Stab(p))`, which has
     index 3, while `ρ(ℓ)` moves `p`.
   - For 332H, `ρ` does not factor through the level quotient `K_n` for any `n ≤ 5`.
   - `M` is the only proper subgroup of `A_12` of index `≤ 9` containing `G_1`.
4. **Consequences.**
   - (a) `ℓ ∈ G_1`, or equivalently `K` is self-replicating at a level-1 vertex, holds exactly only
     if `ker π ⊄ ker ρ`. That requires a relation of `K` of length `≥ 13` that fails in `A`. In
     particular it forces `K ≇ A`.
   - (b) The same holds for:
     - any branching certificate `N × N × 1 ≤ ψ(N)` with `N ≠ 1`, which forces `K` not to be
       virtually free;
     - any closure `G_j^g ≤ G_k` (`j < k`) of the section chain `G_{k+1} = φ_0(St_{G_k}(0))`. In `A`
       these subgroups have index exactly `3^k` for `k ≤ 6` (332H) or `k ≤ 5` (the others), so no
       closure with `k` in that range can hold in `A`.
   - (c) So the planned exact certificate is **false if `K ≅ A`**. In that case, however, `K` is
     infinite and `Γ` is irreducible. The dichotomy is:

     > either `K ≅ A`, and then `Γ` is irreducible but no self-similar certificate exists; or `K` has
     > a relation outside `A` (necessarily of length `≥ 13`).

**What this settles.**
- The step "express the missing letter as a word in `G_1` and verify with `trivial()`" cannot
  succeed with words whose verification uses only relations of length `≤ 12`.
- Nor can it succeed at all if `K ≅ A`, which is exactly the case in which `Γ` would be irreducible
  for the simplest reason.
- The observation of the earlier lane that `τ ∈ G_1` modulo levels 5–6 is explained by item 3: `ρ`
  is not a congruence quotient, so modular membership says nothing exact.
- The strongest step this leaves is to prove `K ≅ A`. For 332V this is reduced to an orbit condition
  on the dual transducer in `bmw-40-40-332-irreducible-if-dual-orbit-parity`.
