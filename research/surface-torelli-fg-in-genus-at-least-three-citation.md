Citation import, taken from a secondary source; Johnson's original paper was not
reached on 2026-09-13. Source read from the arXiv PDF, page 2: M. Kassabov,
A. Putman, *Equivariant group presentations and the second homology group of the
Torelli group*, Math. Ann. 376 (2020), arXiv:1807.01338v3, §1, verbatim:
"However, a remarkable theorem of Johnson [J2] says that I_g^b is finitely generated
for g ≥ 3." Their surfaces have `b ∈ {0, 1}` boundary components (footnote 1 and
the paragraph "Torelli group").

The same page records the open problem this graph tracks, verbatim: "However, it is
not known whether I_g^b is finitely presentable for g ≥ 3."

Cubic-size generating sets: A. Putman, *Small generating sets for the Torelli group*,
arXiv:1106.3294 (per its arXiv abstract).
