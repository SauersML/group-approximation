title: "For g >= 4 the second integral homology of the surface Torelli group is finitely generated"
---

For `g >= 4` and a surface with `b` boundary components and `p` punctures,
`b + p <= 1`, the abelian group `H_2(I^b_{g,p}; Z)` is finitely generated
(Gaifullin, arXiv:2606.13517, Theorem A at `k = 2`). This is a June 2026 preprint,
unrefereed as of 2026-09-13. The import is
`surface-torelli-second-homology-finitely-generated-citation`.

Earlier and weaker statements:

- finite generation as a `Z[Sp_{2g}(Z)]`-module for `g >= 3`, `b ∈ {0,1}`
  (Kassabov–Putman, Math. Ann. 376 (2020), Theorem A);
- finite dimensionality of `H_2(I^b_{g,p}; Q)` for `g >= 5`, algebraic for `g >= 6`
  (Minahan–Putman, arXiv:2504.00211, Theorem B).

Role in Zaremsky Problem 3.1: it removes the degree-2 homological obstruction to
`surface-torelli-groups-fp-for-large-genus` for every `g >= 4`. So a negative
answer (`surface-torelli-not-fp-for-infinitely-many-genera`) needs an obstruction
finer than finite generation of `H_2`. Genus 3 is not covered.
