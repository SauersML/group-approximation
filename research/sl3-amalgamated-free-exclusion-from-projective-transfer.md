In the ultraproduct, `(PTS1)` with vanishing subgroup energy says every
unitary of `L(C)' cap M` has a scalar commutator with `u_h`: `[k, u_h] = z 1`
with `|z| = 1`, so `|tau([k, u_h])| = 1`.  For an amalgamated-free Haar
unitary `k` the commutator `k u_h k^* u_h^*` is an alternating centred
word and has trace `0`.  The two are incompatible, so no such `k` exists.
