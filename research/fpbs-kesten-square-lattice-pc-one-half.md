**ESTABLISHED (imported).** For Bernoulli bond percolation on the
nearest-neighbour square lattice `Z^2`, `p_c(Z^2) = 1/2` (Kesten, 1980).
Since `p_c` is monotone under subgraphs, `p_c(G) <= 1/2` for every graph `G`
that contains a copy of `Z^2`, for example `Z^d` with `d >= 2`.

Status records an imported theorem, not independent mathematical verification.
