## Statement

Let a group `Γ` act by homeomorphisms on a Hausdorff space `X`, and let
`S ⊆ X` be a `Γ`-invariant subset such that

1. `Stab_Γ(s)` is finitely generated for every `s ∈ S`, and
2. `Γ` has finitely many orbits on two-element subsets of `S`.

Faithfulness and finite presentation are not needed. Let `x ∈ S` be an
accumulation point of its own orbit `Γx`. Put `G_x = Stab_Γ(x)` and let
`N_x ⊴ G_x` be the elements that are the identity on some neighbourhood of
`x`. Then the germ group `G_x / N_x` is **finitely generated and nontrivial**.

So in every type (A) action (Zaremsky's condition (A),
`type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups`) by homeomorphisms, every
point of `S` that accumulates on its orbit has a nontrivial finitely generated
germ group.

## Consequences

- **Where the difficulty goes.** Let `H ≤ Γ` act quasi-analytically at `x`,
  i.e. an element of `H` fixing `x` is determined by its germ at `x`. Then
  `Stab_H(x)` embeds in `G_x/N_x`. So the germ group at `x` must be a finitely
  generated group containing `Stab_H(x)`. For `H = PGL_m(Q)` acting on
  `RP^(m-1)` (or on a blow-up of it) and `x ∈ P^(m-1)(Q)`, the germ group must
  be a finitely generated group containing the parabolic `P(Q)`, which contains
  the divisible group `(Q^(m-1), +)`. By obstruction O1 of
  `gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group` that germ group is not residually finite.
  See `pgl-q-projective-type-a-extension`.
- **Generic orbits never work.** If every element of `Γ` fixing `x` is the
  identity near `x`, as for points with trivial stabilizer in a
  piecewise-analytic host, then the orbit of `x` carries no action with 1 and 2.
  This is why type (A) applications use orbits of singular or periodic points.
  One example is the orbit of `0^ω` for Röver--Nekrashevych groups in
  Belk--Bleak--Matucci--Zaremsky's contracting case (survey arXiv:2306.16356v3,
  proof of Corollary 4.16).
- **Calibration.** Thompson's `V` on the orbit of `0^ω`: the germ group of
  `Stab_V(0^ω)` is infinite cyclic, generated by the germ of `0w ↦ 00w`. It is
  nontrivial and finitely generated, as the lemma requires. The stabilizer
  itself is finitely generated (survey, proof of Corollary 4.16, with trivial
  coefficient group). At a point `x` that is not eventually periodic, an element
  of `V` fixing `x` is the identity near `x`, because `uy = vy` with
  `|u| < |v|` forces `y`, and hence `x`, to be eventually periodic. There the
  stabilizer is the strictly increasing union of the pointwise stabilizers of
  the cones around `x`, so it is not finitely generated.
