False: `SL₃(ℤ)` is C*-simple (Bekka--Cowling--de la Harpe, Publ. IHÉS 80
(1994)) and, being a higher-rank lattice with property (T), is not
acylindrically hyperbolic.  Whether `SL₃(ℤ)` has rapid decay is itself open
(Valette's conjecture).

Kept as a dead premise so that `agkp-selfless-instances-of-xc` displays what
AGKP leaves of Problem XC: the C*-simple groups that are not acylindrically
hyperbolic, or lack rapid decay.
