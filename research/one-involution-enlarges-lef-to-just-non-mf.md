There are three-torsion-generated Kazhdan groups `H<G`, with `H` LEF
and maximal proper, `G` just non-MF, and `G=<H,a>` for an explicit
involution `a`. Their common infinite simple locally finite subgroup
is the full MF radical of `G` and the kernel of the map from `H` to
the MF reflection of `G`. The subgroup `H` normally generates `G`.

More generally every finite directed-seam group with common endpoints
is generated by its canonical LEF diagonal subgroup and one involution
whose endpoint coordinates are pairwise distinct.
