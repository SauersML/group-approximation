Fisher, Linton, and Sánchez-Peralta, *Coherent RFRS groups*,
arXiv:2603.16763v1, Theorem 1.7 (Theorem 4.2), proves the Euler-characteristic
criterion. Corollary 4.3 computes the L-two Betti numbers of every coherent
infinite two-dimensional Coxeter group and concludes that it is virtually
free-by-cyclic. The converse follows in the same paper from the dimension-two
virtually RFRS characterization, since Coxeter groups are virtually RFRS.

The source states that the criterion confirms Conjectures 3.4 and 4.7 and
solves Problem 4.6 of Jankiewicz--Wise.
