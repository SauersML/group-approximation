**ESTABLISHED** by `relator-width-bounded-by-almost-simple-quotients-proof`. It relies on the import
`nikolov-segal-generation-by-conjugates`, which uses the classification of finite simple groups.

Let `G = <X | R>` be finitely presented with no nontrivial finite quotient. `G` is perfect, so fix integers
`n_(x,r)` with `e_x = sum_r n_(x,r) e_r` in `Z^X`, and put `N_0 = max_x sum_r |n_(x,r)|`. For a finite group `Q` and
`a in Q^X` with `Q = <a>`, define:
- `w(a)`: the least `k` such that every `a_x` is a product of `k` `Q`-conjugates of elements `r(a)^(+-1)`;
- `nu(a)`: the least `n` such that `Q` is generated by `n` `Q`-conjugates of elements `r(a)^(+-1)`;
- `nu_0(a)`: the least `n` such that `n` such conjugates generate `Q` modulo `Q_0`.

Here `Q_0` is the intersection of the normal subgroups with almost simple quotient. Let `F_0(r)` and `F_1(r)` be the
maxima of the Nikolov--Segal functions `f_0(r, d)` and `f_1(r, d)` over `d <= |X|`.

- **(a) Width is generation by relator conjugates.** `nu(a) <= |X| w(a)` and `w(a) <= N_0 + 4 nu F_1(2 nu)`, with
  `nu = nu(a)`.
- **(b) Reduction to `Q/Q_0`.** `nu(a) <= r F_0(r)` with `r = 2 (nu_0(a) + |R|)`. Also `nu_0(a) <= |X| w(abar)`, where
  `abar` is the image of `a` in `Q/Q_0`.
- **(c) Bounded almost simple quotients.** For every `M` there is `Phi(G, M)` such that `w(a) <= Phi(G, M)` whenever
  every almost simple quotient of `<a>` has order at most `M`.
- **(d) No almost simple quotients.** If `<a>` has no almost simple quotient, for example if it is soluble, then
  `nu_0(a) = 0` and `w(a) <= N_0 + 4 nu_s F_1(2 nu_s)` with `nu_s = 2|R| F_0(2|R|)`.

For a general finite `Q` and `a in Q^X`, the width with conjugators in `Q` is at most `w(a)` computed in `<a>`.

**Consequences.**
- **The bounded-factor claim holds.** If every composition factor of `Q` has order `<= m`, the almost simple quotients
  of `<a>` have order at most the largest `|Aut(S)|` with `S` simple non-abelian of order `<= m`. So (c) settles
  `simple-nonsofic-width-over-bounded-composition-factor-groups` for every finitely presented group without finite
  quotients. Neither simplicity nor nonsoficity is used.
- **Soluble targets.** The width is uniformly bounded over all finite soluble groups: any primes, derived length and
  Fitting height.
- **Where the room is.** By (a) and (b), `w(a)` is bounded by a function of `G` and `w(abar)`. `Q/Q_0` embeds in the
  product of the almost simple quotients of `Q`. So any family of finite targets with unbounded relator width
  can be replaced by its quotients `Q/Q_0`. These are subdirect products of almost simple groups whose socles are
  alternating or of Lie type of unbounded order, and the width stays unbounded.
- **Supersedes** the tower, nilpotent-by-locally-finite and abelian-by-nilpotent width bounds, as statements. Those
  proofs stay useful because they avoid the classification and give explicit constants.

**Credit.** The argument is a short application of Nikolov--Segal, Theorems 1.1 and 1.2, and it is **not new in
substance**. Nikolov, Schneider and Thom (`nst-simple-weakly-sofic-groups-are-psl-approximable`, arXiv:1703.06092)
proved in their Theorem 1 that no non-trivial finitely generated perfect group is approximable by finite solvable
groups. That is item (d) for soluble targets in length form, and more, since finite presentation is not needed. Their
Theorem 4 reduces approximation by all finite groups to `PSL_n(q)`-approximation of a quotient, with the same generation
tools. This node records the width form with explicit dependence on `nu`, `nu_0` and `Q/Q_0`. The graph had no node
applying either paper before 2026-09-13. The literature check was bounded to the arXiv abstracts and theorem
statements of those two papers. **Unreviewed.**
