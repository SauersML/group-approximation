A representation-universal scalar loop value is a central packet element.
The selector-character argument forces its truth table to be affine.  If the
loop value instead depends on the packet simple through a Schur multiplier,
factor every stable letter into a fixed packet intertwiner and an arbitrary
multiplicity unitary.  The latter realizes the inverse finite cocycle after a
fixed amplification, exactly as in the conjugate-absorber construction.  The
explicit binary Schur cell gives the sharp parity example and its vanishing
HS remainder estimate.
