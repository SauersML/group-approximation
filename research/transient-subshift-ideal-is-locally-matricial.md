For the cycle core `Y subset X`, the ideal
`LC_c(X\Y,F_q) semidirect Z` is locally matricial. Every finite subset
lies in a finite product of full matrix algebras over `F_q`, with a
local unit. Over `F_2`, the elementary and general linear restriction
kernels coincide for ranks at least two and are locally finite.
