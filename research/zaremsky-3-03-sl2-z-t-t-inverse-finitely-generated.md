M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Some open problems* (version of July 12, 2026,
https://zaremsky.github.io/open_problems.pdf), Section 3 ("Matrix groups, braid
groups, etc"), Problem 3, verbatim: "Is SL2(Z[t, t−1]) finitely generated?"

This claim is the question. It is established only through an answer route:
- **Yes**: `zaremsky-3-03-by-yes` requires `sl2-laurent-integers-is-finitely-generated`.
- **No**: `zaremsky-3-03-by-no` requires `sl2-laurent-integers-is-not-finitely-generated`.
Never write a `requires: []` route into this claim.

**Reading.** `R = Z[t,t^{-1}]`, with units `±t^k`. `SL_2(R)` is the group of 2×2
matrices over `R` with determinant 1, and the question is whether it is finitely
generated as an abstract group. `GL_2(R)` is `SL_2(R)` extended by `R^× ≅ Z × Z/2`
(via `diag(u,1)`), so a yes answer for `SL_2(R)` gives `GL_2(R)`. The converse is not
automatic, because `SL_2(R)` is the kernel of a map from `GL_2(R)` onto an infinite
group.

**Known context** (checked from zbMATH records 2026-09-13; the papers themselves not
re-read):
- `GL_2(R)` is not finitely presentable. More generally, for a commutative ring `A` and
  an indicable group `Q`, every subgroup of `GL_2(AQ)` containing `E_2(AQ)` is not
  finitely presentable (S. Krstić and J. McCool, Invent. Math. 129 (1997) 595–606,
  Zbl 0880.20031). So `SL_2(R)` and `E_2(R)` are not finitely presented. There is a
  geometric proof for `SL_2(R)` by K.-U. Bux and K. Wortman, Algebr. Geom. Topol. 6
  (2006) 839–852, Zbl 1128.20037.
- `H_2(SL_2(R); Z)` is not finitely generated (K. P. Knudson, Algebr. Geom. Topol. 8
  (2008) 2253–2261, Zbl 1167.20026). The zbMATH review of that paper says it is
  unknown whether `SL_2(R)` is generated by elementary matrices. `H^2` of
  `SL_2(J[t,t^{-1}])` with `Q` coefficients is infinite-dimensional for integral
  domains `J` (S. Cobb, J. Algebra (2016) 181–196, Zbl 1350.20034).
- For `R = Z[x]` without the inverse: `SL_2(Z[x])` modulo the subgroup generated by
  unipotents has free quotients of every finite rank (F. Grunewald, J. Mennicke,
  L. Vaserstein, Israel J. Math. 86 (1994) 157–193, Zbl 0805.20042).
- For `n ≥ 3`, `SL_n` over these rings is generated by elementary matrices (Suslin;
  Grunewald–Mennicke–Vaserstein 1991 for Laurent rings over local PIDs), so the
  question is a rank-2 phenomenon.

**Proved in this region** (unreviewed):
- `elementary-sl2-laurent-integers-is-finitely-generated`: `E_2(R)` is generated by
  five matrices. So `SL_2(R) = E_2(R)` would answer yes.
- `sl2-laurent-integers-splits-over-gamma0-t`:
  `SL_2(R) = SL_2(Z[t]) *_{Gamma_0(t)} D SL_2(Z[t]) D^{-1}` with `D = diag(1,t)`.
- `sl2-laurent-integers-fg-iff-gamma0-t-in-fg-subgroup`: `SL_2(R)` is finitely
  generated iff `SL_2(Z[t]) ⊂ <E_2(R) ∪ F>` for a finite set `F`. `SL_2(R) = E_2(R)`
  iff `SL_2(Z[t]) ⊂ E_2(R)`.

## Attempts

- 2026-09-13 (lane z3-03-sl2-laurent): the open status is confirmed within the zbMATH
  records above; no answer was found. The attack runs through the amalgam: decide
  which elements of the level-`t` subgroup `Gamma_0(t)` of `SL_2(Z[t])` are products of
  Laurent elementary matrices. See the Attempts sections of the two answer claims.
- 2026-09-13, later: the NO side is reduced to one condition. With `U_2` the subgroup
  generated by unipotents,
  `SL_2(R)/U_2(R) ≅ SL_2(Z[t])/(U_2(R) ∩ SL_2(Z[t]))`
  (`sl2-laurent-unipotent-quotient-is-polynomial-quotient`). If the intersection is
  exactly `U_2(Z[t])` (implied by the open claim
  `polynomial-unipotent-subgroup-fricke-compatible`), Grunewald–Mennicke–Vaserstein's
  free quotients answer NO (route `sl2-laurent-integers-not-fg-via-fricke-compatibility`).
  The one-dimensional analogue of that condition is false, so a NO answer needs a
  genuinely two-dimensional invariant.
