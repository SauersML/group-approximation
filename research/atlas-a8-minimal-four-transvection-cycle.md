Inside `A8 ~= GL_4(F2)`, let

```text
tij=I+E_(i,j).
```

The four directed-cycle transvections

```text
T={t01,t12,t23,t30}
```

generate all of `GL_4(F2)`, and the collision-19243 transvection is exactly
`t23`.  Their directed Cayley diameter is `17`.  Each of `t01,t12,t30` is a
single conjugate of `t23`; shortest conjugators in the four-letter alphabet
have length eight.

Moreover **no three transvections can generate `GL_4(F2)`**.  A transvection
has the form

```text
x |-> x+phi(x)v,      phi(v)=0,
```

and fixes the hyperplane `ker(phi)` pointwise.  Three such hyperplanes in the
four-dimensional vector space over `F2` have an intersection of dimension at
least one, so any group generated by three transvections fixes a common
nonzero vector.  The full `GL_4(F2)` action has no such fixed vector.

Thus a collision-centered conjugacy-star compiler needs at least three leaves
if its output opcodes are required to be transvections generating the whole
chart, and this four-cycle attains that lower bound exactly.
