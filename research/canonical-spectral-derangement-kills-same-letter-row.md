Let `a` be an ordinary group element of finite order `m>=2`.  In every
canonical-trace matrix model exact on `<a>`, its spectral projections

```text
p_j=(1/m) sum_(k=0)^(m-1) zeta^(-jk)a^k,
              j in Z/m,                               (CSD1)
```

have asymptotic trace `1/m`.  Dimension counting between `p_j` and a
unitarily transported copy can therefore force at most

```text
tr(p_j meet t p_j t^*) >= max(2/m-1,0)=0.             (CSD2)
```

The zero bound is sharp simultaneously for every type.  On
`C^m tensor C^N`, let `a` be diagonal with eigenvalues
`1,zeta,...,zeta^(m-1)`, each with multiplicity `N`, and let `T` cyclically
shift the `m` spectral summands.  Then

```text
T p_j T^*=p_(j+1),
p_j T p_j=0                         (all j).            (CSD3)
```

Moreover

```text
||(aT-Ta)p_j||_2^2
 =|zeta-1|^2 tr(p_j).                                (CSD4)
```

Thus the entire canonical type mass may be deranged with a fixed
same-letter-row defect and with no nonzero type-preserving subcarrier.
For the Fanizza involution case `m=2`, the two half-spaces are exchanged and
the defect squared is exactly `4 tr(p_j)`.

This countermodel tensors with any independent exact finite edge-packet
transporter.  Consequently finite packet relations, canonical marginal type
multiplicities, and the lower bound `tr(p_j)=1/m` do not imply the compressed
row `(FSC1)` in `fanizza-same-letter-compressed-row`.  A successful compiler
must contain a mixed relation or matrix-only gap coupling the transported
edge coordinate to the **same** Fanizza observable.  Merely obtaining more
copies or a larger union of spectral types does not help: the cyclic shift
deranges all types at once.
