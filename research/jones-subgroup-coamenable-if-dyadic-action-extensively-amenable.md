**ESTABLISHED.** Suppose the action of `F` on the dyadic rationals `D ⊂ (0,1)` is extensively amenable.
Then the homogeneous space `F/\vec F` of Jones' oriented subgroup carries an `F`-invariant mean, i.e.
`1_F ≺ λ_(F/\vec F)`.

**Contrapositive.** If `\vec F` is not co-amenable in `F`, then `F ↷ D` is not extensively amenable, so `F` is
not amenable. The chain is
`F amenable ⇒ F ↷ D extensively amenable ⇒ \vec F co-amenable in F`.

Proof route: `jones-subgroup-coamenable-from-extensive-amenability-proof`.
