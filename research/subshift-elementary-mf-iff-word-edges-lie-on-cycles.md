For any nonempty finite-alphabet subshift `X`, finite field `F_q` and
fixed `n>=4`, put `R_X=LC(X,F_q) semidirect Z`. Then

`EL_n(R_X) is MF iff EL_n(R_X) is LEF iff R_X is directly finite`
`iff every edge of every prefix-suffix word graph lies on a directed cycle`.

These groups are finitely generated and Kazhdan. In the positive case,
`GL_m(R_X)` is LEF for every `m>=1`, including the rank-three elementary
subgroup. In the negative case, a failing finite graph constructs a
nonzero cylinder idempotent `e=1_(U minus T(U))` and a nontrivial
`e_ij(e) in Rad_MF(EL_n(R_X))`.

For binary `X` over `F_2`, the rank-four group is generated by the earlier
triple in three coordinates and the constant swap `s_34`, with orders
`7,2,2,2`. Its MF and LEF parameter loci coincide and are closed. The
binary subshift forbidding `10` gives an explicit non-MF example, with
defect `e=1_(x_(-1)=0,x_0=1)`. The negative direction at rank three is
not asserted.
