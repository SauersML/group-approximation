title: The zero-divisor conjecture gives the positive-characteristic idempotent conjecture

requires: [kaplansky-zero-divisor-conjecture]
---

Let `e` be an idempotent of `K[G]`, `G` torsion-free, `char K = p`. Then
`e(1 - e) = e - e^2 = 0`. If `K[G]` has no zero divisors, `e = 0` or `1 - e = 0`.
