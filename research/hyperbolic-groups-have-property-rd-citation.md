Published theorem: Jolissaint, *Rapidly decreasing functions in reduced
C\*-algebras of groups*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 317 (1990) 167–196, and
de la Harpe, *Groupes hyperboliques, algèbres d'opérateurs et un théorème de
Jolissaint*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris Sér. I 307 (1988) 771–774.

Chatterji's survey (arXiv:1604.06387), read from its LaTeX source, states the
definition verbatim: "A finitely generated group $G$ has *the Rapid Decay
property* ... if there are constants $C$ and $D$ such that for any $R\in\N$ and
any $f\in\cg$ such that $f$ is supported on elements shorter than $R$, the
following inequality holds: $\|f\|_*\leq CR^D\|f\|_2$". Its list of examples
includes "Hyperbolic groups \cite{Jolissaint}, \cite{Harpe}", and it records
that "The extension to Gromov hyperbolic groups is due to de la Harpe".
