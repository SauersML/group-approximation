For the degree-four length-twelve orbit profile `(1,3,3,5)`, all twelve
balanced rank-nine all-same-chirality triangle systems fall into two classes,
according as the five-corner face is partitioned cyclically as `(1,3,1)` or
`(2,2,1)`.  In every exact solution of the carrier equations, the resulting
coefficient boundary is either the identity or has reduced free-product
length greater than one.  Hence none kills a nontrivial element of a
coefficient copy, for arbitrary coefficient units.
