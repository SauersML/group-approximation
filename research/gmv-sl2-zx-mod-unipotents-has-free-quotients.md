title: "SL_2(Z[x]) modulo its unipotent subgroup has free quotients of every finite rank"
---

Let `U_2(Z[x])` be the subgroup of `SL_2(Z[x])` generated by unipotent matrices. Then
for every `r ≥ 1` the group `SL_2(Z[x]) / U_2(Z[x])` surjects onto a free group of rank
`r`. In particular `SL_2(Z[x])/U_2(Z[x])` is not finitely generated.

Source: F. Grunewald, J. Mennicke, L. Vaserstein, "On the groups SL_2(Z[x]) and
SL_2(k[x,y])", Israel J. Math. 86 (1994) 157–193, Zbl 0805.20042. The zbMATH review
states the result in these words: "The authors prove that SL_2(R)/U_2(R) has a free
quotient of any finite rank", for `R = Z[x]` or `k[x,y]` with `k` a finite field. The
proof specializes `x` to algebraic integers of imaginary quadratic fields, where the
image is "almost surjective", and uses free quotients of Bianchi groups.
