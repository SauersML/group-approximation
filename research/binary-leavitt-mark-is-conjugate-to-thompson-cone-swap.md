**ESTABLISHED** (route `binary-leavitt-mark-is-conjugate-to-thompson-cone-swap-proof`;
pure algebra, no property (T)).

Let `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`. Let `a`, `b` be incomparable finite binary words, and put

```text
tau   = 1 + s_a t_b,         h = 1 + s_b t_a,
sigma = 1 + e_a + e_b + s_a t_b + s_b t_a     (the cylinder swap of a and b, in V).
```

1. **S_3.** `tau^2 = h^2 = 1` and `h tau h = tau h tau = sigma`. So
   `<tau, h> ~= S_3 = GL_2(F_2)`, and in it the transvection `tau` is
   conjugate to the permutation `sigma`.
2. **Two copies of V.** With `a = 00`, `b = 01`,
   `R^x = <V, 1 + s_00 t_01> = <V, V^h>`. So the unit group is generated by two
   conjugate copies of the Haagerup group `V`.
3. **The mark.** Let `iota` be the chain of isomorphisms
   `St_20(R) ~= GL_20(R) ~= R^x`. It sends `z = x_13(s_1 t_1)` to a monomial
   transvection `1 + s_alpha t_beta` with `alpha`, `beta` incomparable. Hence `z`
   is conjugate in `Delta = St_20(R)` to the preimage of a cone transposition of `V`.
4. **Consequence.** `Delta` is simple. So for every normal subgroup `N` of
   `Delta`, including `N = Rad_MF(Delta)`, the following are equivalent:
   - `N = Delta`;
   - `z` lies in `N`;
   - one cone transposition of the embedded `V` lies in `N`;
   - the embedded `V` lies in `N`.

## Why it matters

The goal and the hole `thompson-v-not-mf` ask the same question about one
element, a cone transposition `sigma`:
- the goal asks whether `sigma` dies in every corona representation of
  `Delta = <V, V^h>`;
- the hole asks whether `sigma` dies in every corona representation of `V`.

There is no intermediate group to aim at. Any subgroup that contains `V` and a
single monomial transvection, or `V` and `V^h`, is already the Kazhdan group
`Delta`. So a (T)-free proof must either work inside `V`, where every Kazhdan
subgroup is finite (`thompson-v-has-no-rigid-compression-defect`), or use
relations that genuinely glue `V` to `V^h`. Every relation of the second kind
lives in a group that has (T).
