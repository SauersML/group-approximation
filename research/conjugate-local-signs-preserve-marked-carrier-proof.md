Word evaluation commutes with multiplication and inversion. Hence the first
identity in `(CLS1)` is exact. Normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm is invariant
under unitary conjugation, which gives the second identity.

The Borel functional calculus is equivariant under unitary conjugation, so it
gives `(CLS2)`. Taking normalized traces proves equality of all carrier
densities. Notice that none of these statements uses an approximate defining
relation: the conjugacy is built into the spelling of `J_n` itself.
