## Setting

- `S` is a unital ring with an involution `a ↦ a^*`. A **`*`-Leavitt pair** is
  `x_1, x_2 ∈ S` with `x_i^* x_j = δ_ij` and `x_1 x_1^* + x_2 x_2^* = 1`. Integers are
  central and fixed by `*`.
- `J_m = [[0, I_m], [-I_m, 0]]`, `Sp_2m(S) = {h ∈ GL_2m(S) : h^* J_m h = J_m}`, with `h^*`
  the conjugate transpose. `H(φ) = diag(φ, (φ^*)^-1)` embeds `GL_m(S)` in `Sp_2m(S)`.
- For `m ≥ g`, `A_m : Sp_2g(Z) → Sp_2m(S)` is base change followed by the standard
  stabilization: `[[α, β], [γ, δ]] ↦ [[α ⊕ I, β ⊕ 0], [γ ⊕ 0, δ ⊕ I]]`.
- `Q_0(m) = ⟨A_m(Sp_2g(Z)), H(E_m(S))⟩ ≤ Sp_2m(S)`. Any reasonable elementary symplectic
  group `Ep_2m(S)` contains `Q_0(m)`, because `Sp_2g(Z)` is generated by symplectic
  elementary matrices (standard, not re-read).

## Statement

Let `g ≥ 3` and `m ≥ 3g`, and let `Q` be any subgroup of `Sp_2m(S)` containing `Q_0(m)`.

1. **Swindle.** `(A_m)_* : H_2(Sp_2g(Z); Z) → H_2(Q; Z)` is zero.
2. **Centres die in every central extension.** Let `G' → Sp_2g(Z)` be a perfect central
   extension with kernel `K`. Let `π : E → Q` be a central extension, and let `Φ : G' → E`
   satisfy `π Φ = A_m p`, where `p : G' → Sp_2g(Z)`. Then `Φ(K) = 1`.
3. **Deligne's lattice.** Take `g ≥ 4` and `G' = Γ~_g`, the preimage of `Sp_2g(Z)` in the
   universal cover of `Sp_2g(R)`. It is the universal central extension
   (`deligne-lattice-is-universal-central-extension-for-g-ge-4`), so it is perfect. So no lift of the base-change map `Γ~_g → Q` to any central
   extension of `Q` is injective: the whole centre `Z` dies.
   - This covers `StSp_2m(S) → Ep_2m(S)` whenever that extension is central. That is
     Lavrenov's theorem for commutative `S`, and is open for `R_L`.
   - **Lower ranks, `g ≤ m' < 3g`.** Let `Φ'` be a lift into `E'` over `Q' ≤ Sp_2m'(S)`.
     Suppose there is a homomorphism `σ : E' → E`, with `E` central over `Q`, that covers
     stabilization. Then `Φ'` sends the centre into `ker σ`. For Steinberg groups this is
     the unstable stabilization kernel.
4. **`R_L`.** The ring `R_L` of `leavitt-resolvent-ring-is-fp-and-contains-q` has an
   involution with `s_i^* = t_i`, `t_i^* = s_i`, `N^* = N` and `A^* = A`, and `(s_1, s_2)`
   is a `*`-Leavitt pair. So items 1–3 apply to `S = R_L`. The route "`Γ~_g = StSp_2g(Z)`
   into `StSp_2m(R_L)` by base change" is dead for `m ≥ 3g`. Below that rank it survives
   only through a nontrivial unstable symplectic stabilization kernel, the symplectic
   counterpart of the open linear `U_r` question in
   `leavitt-refinement-identifies-unstable-k2-levels`.
5. **Linear base change.** The same proof, with `E_3n(S)` and no involution, shows that
   `a ↦ a ⊕ I_2n` kills `H_2` of any perfect subgroup of `SL_n(Z)` in `E_3n(S)`, for any
   `S` with a Leavitt pair. Without a Leavitt pair, the factorization
   `Sp_2g(Z) ≤ SL_2g(Z)` already kills `2Z` of the centre, because `H_2(SL_2g(Z)) = Z/2`
   for `2g ≥ 5` (Funar–Pitsch, quoted in the Deligne node).

## What this means for the Deligne test case

- Deligne's lattice can't reach `St_N(R_L)`, `StSp_2m(R_L)` or any other Steinberg-type
  host over a ring with a `*`-Leavitt pair through a ring map from `Z`. It can't reach
  them through a representation of `Sp_2g(Z)` factoring through such a map either. In
  every central extension the centre is killed.
- So the Deligne case does **not** reduce to the GL_n(Q) instance
  `steinberg-resolvent-shell-envelope-is-finitely-presented` by base change.
- A Steinberg host over a Leavitt ring has to carry the centre as a genuine non-`K_2`
  element, for instance a central unit. That is the generic algebra-host framework of
  `decidable-group-algebras-have-fp-central-simple-hosts` and
  `simple-inputs-have-fp-elementary-leavitt-tensor-hosts`, which covers every input and
  gives the Deligne case no advantage.
- A ring that keeps the Maslov class must either lack a `*`-compatible Leavitt pair, or
  put the centre where the hyperbolic swindle does not reach.
  - A real point `S → R` keeps it, through the topological universal cover. Such a point
    is incompatible with any Leavitt pair, because `R` has IBN.
  - Whether a ring with a Leavitt pair can carry an involution with no `*`-compatible pair
    and a surviving signature-type class is not examined here.

## Scope

- Only lifts over the **base-change** map `A_m p` are constrained. Other embeddings of
  `Γ~_g` into `E` are untouched, for example ones in which the image of the centre does not
  lie in `ker π`.
- Non-central extensions of `Q` are not covered. Centrality of symplectic `K_2` over
  noncommutative rings such as `R_L` is not known here.
