Let `A` be separable and exact, let `B` be nonzero, unital, separable, and
nuclear, and let

```text
phi:A->B
```

be a unital nuclear homomorphism.  Suppose `B` has a continuous increasing
filtration `(I_alpha)_(alpha<=lambda)` by closed ideals, indexed by an
arbitrary ordinal, such that `I_0=0`, `I_lambda=B`, the filtration is
continuous at limits, and every nonzero successor layer

```text
I_(alpha+1)/I_alpha
```

is algebraically simple with almost-unperforated `W`.

If `1_B` is properly infinite, then `B` is `O_infinity`-stable and `phi` is
strongly `O_infinity`-stable.  In particular, the conclusion holds under the
hypotheses of Problem LXXVII: requiring every nonzero positive element of
`phi(A)` to be properly infinite already makes `1_B=phi(1_A)` properly
infinite.  Thus the finite-nuclear-dimension version of LXXVII is true for
every target in this arbitrary-ordinal regular nuclear class.
