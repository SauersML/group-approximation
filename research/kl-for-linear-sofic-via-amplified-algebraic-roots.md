Let `G` be `F`-linear sofic and `w` nonsingular. Put `L` = an algebraic
closure of `F`. `amplified-algebraic-roots-for-nonsingular-equations` gives
`ARR(L)`, and `linear-sofic-kl-from-amplified-algebraic-roots` with `F <= L`
gives injectivity of `G --> G_w` when `G` is countable.

For uncountable `G`: a failure is witnessed by a finite identity
`a = prod_j u_j w^{e_j} u_j^{-1}` in `G * <t>` with `a != 1`. So it already
fails for the countable subgroup `G_0` generated by `a`, the coefficients of
`w`, and the coefficients occurring in the `u_j`. `G_0` is `F`-linear sofic,
by restricting the representation, so the countable case applies.
