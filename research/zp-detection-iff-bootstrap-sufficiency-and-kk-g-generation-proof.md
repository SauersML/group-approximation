**Sources (read 2026-09-13 from ghostscript text extractions on MSI).**
* Meyer--Nadareishvili, arXiv:2406.11787 (MN24).  §2.1: "If G/H is discrete,
  then Ind is left adjoint to Res"; for finite `G` also right adjoint.
  Proof of Theorem 3.1: for a family of triangulated functors with
  coproduct-preserving left adjoints and kernel `I` on morphisms, there are
  enough `I`-projective objects ([16, Proposition 55] = Meyer--Nest,
  *Homological algebra in bivariant K-theory ... I*).  The localizing
  subcategory generated by them and the `I`-contractible objects form a
  complementary pair ([13, Theorem 3.16] = Meyer, *... II*, Tbil. Math. J. 1
  (2008)).  The cellular approximations are built from the objects `T^k(A)`
  by a phantom castle ([13, §3]).  Corollary 3.3: `B^G` is generated by
  `C(G/H)` for cyclic `H`, here `C` and `C(G)`.
* Meyer--Nadareishvili, arXiv:2604.12529v2, Theorems 2.9 and 2.10 (Köhler's
  range and UCT), quoted in `bootstrap-zp-actions-realize-every-cyclotomic-k-module-proof`.
* Gabe--Szabó, arXiv:2205.04933.  Theorem 3.13: an amenable action on a
  separable nuclear C\*-algebra embeds, inducing a KK^G-equivalence, into an
  amenable isometrically shift-absorbing action on a stable Kirchberg
  algebra.  Proposition 3.15: for countable discrete `G`, an action on a
  Kirchberg algebra is isometrically shift-absorbing iff pointwise outer.
  Corollary 6.4(i): for compact second-countable `G` and isometrically
  shift-absorbing actions on stable Kirchberg algebras, "every invertible
  element x ∈ KK^G(α, β) lifts to a conjugacy".

**Standard inputs, not re-read.**
* (GJ) Green--Julg with coefficients: `KK^G(τA, D) ≅ KK(A, D ⋊ G)`, so `τ` is
  left adjoint to `- ⋊ G`, and both preserve countable direct sums.
* (N) Nuclear objects form a localizing subcategory.  For nuclear `X`, `Y`,
  a class in `KK^G_1(X, Y)` is an equivariantly semisplit extension of `X` by
  `Y ⊗ K(H_G)`, whose middle algebra is nuclear.  Semisplit extensions give
  exact triangles (Meyer--Nest, *The Baum--Connes conjecture via localisation
  of categories*, §2).  So cones of morphisms between nuclear objects are
  nuclear.  Countable direct sums are nuclear, and a homotopy colimit is the
  cone of a morphism between direct sums.
* (KP) A stable Kirchberg algebra that is KK-contractible is `O_2 ⊗ K`
  (Kirchberg--Phillips, KK form, no UCT).
* Every action of a finite group is amenable.

**(L) ⟹ (D).**  Fix nuclear `D` with `Res D ≃ 0` and `D ⋊ G ≃ 0`.  The class
of `X` with `KK^G_*(X, D) = 0` is localizing, since
`KK^G(⊕ X_n, D) = ∏ KK^G(X_n, D)`.  It contains `Ind A`, because
`KK^G(Ind A, D) ≅ KK(A, Res D) = 0`.  It contains `τA` by (GJ).  By (L) it
contains `D`, so `id_D = 0`.

**(D) ⟹ (L).**  Apply the MN24 Theorem 3.1 argument to the functor
`F = (Res, - ⋊ G) : KK^G → KK × KK`, whose left adjoint is
`(A, B) ↦ Ind A ⊕ τB`.  Its `I`-projectives generate `⟨Ind, τ⟩`, and its
`I`-contractible objects are exactly the `N` with `Res N ≃ 0` and
`N ⋊ G ≃ 0`, since `KK(A, N ⋊ G) = 0` for all `A` means `N ⋊ G ≃ 0`.  So each
`A` sits in an exact triangle `P → A → N → ΣP` with `P ∈ ⟨Ind, τ⟩`.  Here `P`
is built from `T^k(A)` with `T = Ind Res ⊕ τ(- ⋊ G)`, by cones and one
homotopy colimit.  For nuclear `A` every `T^k(A)` is nuclear, so `P` and `N`
are nuclear by (N).  (D) gives `N ≃ 0`, so `A ≅ P`.

**(S) ⟹ (D).**  `Res D ≃ 0` and `D ⋊ G ≃ 0` lie in `B`, so (S) puts `D` in
`B^G`.  By item 1 of `bootstrap-zp-actions-realize-every-cyclotomic-k-module`,
`U(D) = Q(0, 0, M)` with `M = K_*(D ⋊ G) = 0`, so `U(D) = 0`.  Theorem 2.10,
with `D ∈ B^G` in the first variable, gives `KK^G(D, D) = 0`.

**(D) ⟹ (S).**  Let `A` be nuclear with `Res A ∈ B` and `A ⋊ G ∈ B`.
1. `U(A)` is exact (Theorem 2.9, (2) ⟹ (3)), so `U(A) = U(A')` for some
   `A' ∈ B^G` (Theorem 2.9, (3) ⟹ (1)).
2. Theorem 2.10 lifts the identity of `U(A)` to `x ∈ KK^G(A', A)`.  Let `D` be
   its cone, a nuclear object by (N).  The long exact sequences give
   `U(D) = 0`.
3. Components 0 and 1 of `U` are `K_*(Res -)` and `K_*(- ⋊ G)` (index
   bookkeeping in the proof of the fence node).  So `K_*(Res D) = 0` and
   `K_*(D ⋊ G) = 0`.
4. `Res` and `- ⋊ G` are triangulated and preserve countable sums.  They send
   the generators `C` and `C(G)` of `B^G` into `B`: `Res C(G) = C^p`,
   `C ⋊ G = C*(G) = C^p` and `C(G) ⋊ G = M_p`.  So `Res A'` and `A' ⋊ G` lie
   in `B`.  Then `Res D` is a cone between objects of `B`, hence lies in `B`,
   and has zero K-theory, so `Res D ≃ 0`.  Likewise `D ⋊ G ≃ 0`.
5. (D) gives `D ≃ 0`, so `x` is invertible and `A ≅ A' ∈ B^G`.

**(D) ⟹ (K).**  Let `δ, δ'` be pointwise outer on `O_2 ⊗ K` with contractible
crossed products.  Both are nuclear with contractible restriction, so (D)
makes both KK^G-contractible.  Then `0 ∈ KK^G(δ, δ')` is invertible.
Proposition 3.15 makes both isometrically shift-absorbing, and Corollary
6.4(i) lifts `0` to a conjugacy.

**(K) ⟹ (D).**  Let `D` be nuclear with `Res D ≃ 0` and `D ⋊ G ≃ 0`.
1. Theorem 3.13 gives an isometrically shift-absorbing `β` on a stable
   Kirchberg algebra `B` with `(B, β) ≃_(KK^G) D`.  By Proposition 3.15, `β` is
   pointwise outer.
2. `B ≃_KK Res D ≃ 0`, so `B ≅ O_2 ⊗ K` by (KP).  Descent is a functor
   `KK^G → KK`, so `B ⋊_β G ≃_KK D ⋊ G ≃ 0`.
3. Theorem 3.13 applied to `τO_2` gives a pointwise outer `β_0` on
   `O_2 ⊗ K`.  It is KK^G-equivalent to `τO_2`, which is contractible because
   `τ : KK → KK^G` is a functor and `O_2 ≃_KK 0`.  Its crossed product is
   KK-equivalent to `O_2 ⊗ C*(G) ≃ 0`.
4. (K) makes `β` conjugate to `β_0`, so `D ≃ (B, β) ≃ (O_2 ⊗ K, β_0) ≃ 0`.
