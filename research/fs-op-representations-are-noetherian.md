title: Finitely generated representations of FS^op over a noetherian ring are noetherian
---

Let `FS` be the category of nonempty finite sets and surjective functions. Let
`k` be a left-noetherian ring. Every finitely generated functor `M: FS^op -> k-Mod`
is noetherian: every ascending chain of subfunctors of `M` stabilizes. Here `M` is
finitely generated if it is a quotient of a finite direct sum of principal
projectives `k[Hom(x, -)]`, equivalently generated by finitely many elements of
the `M(x)`.

This is Sam–Snowden, Corollary 8.1.3 of arXiv:1409.1670.
