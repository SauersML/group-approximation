Let `J` be an infinite set of cardinality `lambda`, and let `d_j>=1` be
finite for every `j in J`.  Every quotient set of

```text
product_(j in J) U(d_j)
```

has cardinality at most

```text
continuum^lambda.                                         (WID1)
```

Consequently a group of cardinality strictly greater than
`continuum^lambda` cannot embed in any `J`-indexed quotient of finite unitary
groups, including any filter or norm-null matrix quotient on that index set.

Thus the cardinality of the coordinate set is a genuine approximation
resource outside the countable-group setting.
