**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).**

**Setting.**
- `X` is an infinite minimal subshift, `R = LC(X,F_q)⋊_T Z`, and `G = EL_3(R)`.
- `φ_ℓ : R → M_{m_ℓ}(F_q)` are the periodic-word models of the note: `P δ_t = δ_{t+1}`,
  `D_ℓ(f)δ_t = f(T^t y_ℓ)δ_t` and `φ_ℓ(Σ_j f_j u^j) = Σ_j D_ℓ(f_j)P^j`, where `y_ℓ` has the same words of length
  `2ℓ+1` as `X`. They are extended entrywise to `G`.
- `ρ_ℓ` is ANY unitary representation of `GL_{3m_ℓ}(F_q)`.
- `Λ ≅ SL_{3k}(F_q[t^{±1}])` is the copy in `G` constructed in `subshift-elementary-groups-contain-laurent-sl-n-lattices`
  (a tower of height `k` over `V`, and the first-return unit `u_V`).

**Claim.** There are finite commutative rings `A_ℓ` and genuine homomorphisms `Ψ_ℓ : Λ → GL_{3m_ℓ}(F_q)` factoring
through `SL_{3k}(A_ℓ)`, such that for every finite `F ⊆ Λ`, `φ_ℓ = Ψ_ℓ` on `F` for all large `ℓ`. Here `A_ℓ` is
generated by a permutation matrix `Q_ℓ`, and `Λ → SL_{3k}(A_ℓ)` is induced by `t ↦ Q_ℓ`.

**Consequence.**
- If `ρ_ℓ∘φ_ℓ → λ_G` strongly, then the genuine representations `ρ_ℓ∘Ψ_ℓ` of `Λ` satisfy
  `‖ρ_ℓΨ_ℓ(z)‖ → ‖λ_Λ(z)‖` for every `z ∈ C[Λ]`.
- In words, `SL_n(F_q[t^{±1}])` would be purely matricial field through congruence representations, for every `n`.
- So a no-outlier theorem for the models is at least that strong. The heuristic "cuspidal or Steinberg-type
  irreducibles of the finite models" in `directly-finite-subshift-el-n-reduced-cstar-is-mf` is a statement about
  congruence representations of positive-characteristic higher-rank lattices.
