Let `G` be any group and `w in G * <t>` with `deg_t(w) != 0`. Write
`ell_t(w)` for the variable length, the sum of the absolute values of the
`t`-exponents. If `ell_t(w) <= 5`, then `G -> (G * <t>)/<<w>>` is injective.

The sources are the same three theorems already imported for the Leavitt
specialization:
- length three: Howie (1983);
- length four: Edjvet--Howie (1991);
- length five: Evangelidou (2007).
