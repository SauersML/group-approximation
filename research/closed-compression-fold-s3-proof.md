Equations `(CCF2)--(CCF3)` and the controlled Pauli factors are the explicit
twelve-dimensional model in `one-arm-range-sum-three-character-proof`.
They prove every range-sum, inverse, cross-zero, fixed-arm, and transported
output row, with a rank-eight `J=-1` carrier.

It remains only to extend the two compression-preimage rows.  On an
independent label factor take the literal matrices `T_i,K_i,J_i` from
`(CPW1)--(CPW3)`.  Put identity on the reservoir for `T_i`, and tensor both
`K_i` and `J_i` with the corresponding `W_i` from `(CCF3)`.  Then

```text
(T_i tensor 1)(K_i tensor W_i)(T_i^(-1) tensor 1)
  =J_i tensor W_i,
```

so both nonnormal compression identities and all their full-Gram equations
are exact.  This is precisely the `A=B=1`, `D_K=D_J=W_i` specialization of
`(CPW4)`.

Finally the diagonal character group generated by `J,C_0,C_1` is normalized
by `W_0,W_1`, and the displayed transpositions generate `S_3`.  Projection
to this permutation quotient sends every root-output and fold sign to one
and the two branch chart edges to `W_0,W_1`.  Therefore a telescoping outer
word has trivial quotient, while a one-step character transport has a
transposition quotient.  This proves the asserted finite exact fence.
