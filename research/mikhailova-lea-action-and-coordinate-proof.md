## LEA packet transfer

Fix finite `F_0 subset F_m x F_m`, finite `E subset H`, and
`epsilon>0`.  Choose word representatives for the two coordinates of
every element of `F_0`.  Let `T subset H` be a finite set containing:

- `1`, `E`, and the inverses needed below;
- the values under `q` of every prefix of every chosen word;
- every intermediate product used to compute
  `q(u)h q(v)^(-1)` for `(u,v) in F_0` and `h in E`;
- the corresponding entries needed whenever that transformed point lies
  in the requested orbit packet.

By the LEA hypothesis there are an amenable group `K` and an injective
partial homomorphism

```text
theta:T->K.                                             (MLP3)
```

Replacing `K` by the subgroup generated by the finite image if necessary,
we may take it countable.  Define `p:F_m->K` on a free basis by

```text
p(x_i)=theta(q(x_i))
```

and extend by freeness.  The prefix closure of `T` and partial
multiplicativity in `(MLP3)` give

```text
p(w)=theta(q(w))                                       (MLP4)
```

for every word needed by `F_0`.  The product closure gives, throughout
the requested packet,

```text
theta(q(u)h q(v)^(-1))
   =p(u)theta(h)p(v)^(-1).                             (MLP5)
```

The group `K x K` is amenable, so every one of its set actions is sofic
by GKP Theorem 2.17.  Apply this to its left-right action on `K` and
pull the action back along the homomorphism

```text
p x p:F_m x F_m->K x K
```

using GKP Proposition 2.15(1).  Take a finitary orbit approximation for
the finite subset `theta(E) subset K`.  Precomposing each chart with
the injection `theta|E` preserves injectivity, and `(MLP5)` supplies
exactly the covariance equations for the original action on every
requested entry.  This proves GKP soficity of the action.

## A global H-valued root coordinate forces amenability

Let `S` be a finite generating set of `H` and assume `(MLQ3)`.  Write

```text
mu_n=(z_n)_*(uniform measure on A_n).
```

For fixed `s in S`, sample `a` uniformly from `A_n`.  Since
`L_(s,n)` is a permutation,

```text
z_n(L_(s,n)a) has law mu_n,
s z_n(a) has law s mu_n.
```

Equation `(MLQ3)` couples these two random variables so that they disagree
with probability tending to zero.  The coupling inequality therefore gives

```text
||mu_n-s mu_n||_TV -> 0                                (MLP6)
```

for every `s in S`.  A weak-star cluster point of the associated states
on `ell^infinity(H)` is a left-invariant mean.  Hence `H` is amenable.

The GKP definition avoids this conclusion because its maps
`pi_a:E->B` use an arbitrary auxiliary color set and vary with `a`;
they need not arise by restricting one map `z_n:A_n->H`.  Thus
`(MLP6)` closes the global-coordinate and Følner-coinduction ansatz, not
the general one-color code.
