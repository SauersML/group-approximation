Collect the finitely many commuting support signs into their finite
`F_2`-span `V`.  Their prescribed finite normalizer actions generate a finite
subgroup of `GL(V)`, so the affine semidirect product `(FAR1)` realizes every
product and conjugacy row.  Regular Fourier orthogonality gives `(FAR4)` and
the uniform `2^(-r)` law.  The parent-product and connector equations are
therefore exact while the purported nested cuts miss each other by one
quarter.  Tensoring with the protected outer packet scales this mismatch by
the selected fiber trace and leaves all affine relators exact.
