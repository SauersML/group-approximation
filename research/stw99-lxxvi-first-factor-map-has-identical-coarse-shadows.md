For every separable C*-algebra `A`, the first-factor embedding

```text
iota_A:A -> A tensor_min Z
```

induces

1. an isomorphism of ideal lattices, sending `I` to `I tensor_min Z`;
2. isomorphisms on `K_0` and `K_1`; and
3. an affine homeomorphism between the cones of densely defined lower
   semicontinuous 2-quasitraces.

Therefore a negative answer to Problem LXXVI cannot be detected by the
ideal lattice, ordinary K-theory, or the quasitrace cone.  It must be a
genuinely finer Cuntz-semigroup defect: compact cancellation/clutching, a
failure of rank realization, or noncompact gluing invisible to all three
coarse shadows.
