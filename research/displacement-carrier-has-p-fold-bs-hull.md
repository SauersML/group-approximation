**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `p` range through odd primes, put

```text
G_p=PSL_2(F_p),
B_p=<r,s>,                 D_p=<r,x>,                  (DBH1)
```

where `r s r^(-1)=s^4`, `xrx=r^(-1)`, and `x` is the Weyl involution in the
standard Iwahori quotient.  In the left regular endpoint on `l2(G_p)`, let

```text
Y_p=lambda_p(x),
q_p=|D_p|^(-1/2) 1_(D_p),
Q_p=q_p q_p^*,
C_p=1-2Q_p,
X_p=C_pY_p.                                             (DBH2)
```

Then:

1. `q_p` is fixed by both `lambda_p(r)` and `Y_p`.  Hence `C_p` commutes
   with those two unitaries, and

   ```text
   X_p=X_p^*=X_p^(-1),
   X_p lambda_p(r) X_p=lambda_p(r)^(-1).                (DBH3)
   ```

   Thus the raw BS core, involution, and inversion rows are exact.
2. The displacement from the exact endpoint is

   ```text
   X_p-Y_p=-2Q_p,                                       (DBH4)
   ```

   so `Q_p` is literally the spectral projection of `|X_p-Y_p|` at the
   nonzero singular value `2`.
3. Both cubic defects have rank at most three and Frobenius norm at most
   six.  Therefore their total normalized energy is

   ```text
   O(1/|G_p|)->0.                                       (DBH5)
   ```
4. Every reducing carrier for the exact BS core which contains `q_p` has
   rank at least

   ```text
   [B_p:B_p intersect D_p]=p.                           (DBH6)
   ```

Indeed the `B_p`-cyclic hull of `q_p` has the orthonormal basis of normalized
coset indicators

```text
{ |D_p|^(-1/2)1_(bD_p):b in B_p/(B_p intersect D_p)}.  (DBH7)
```

Here `B_p` is the upper triangular subgroup generated by the unipotent and
the fourth-power torus, while `B_p intersect D_p=<r>`; adjoining the
unipotent gives index `p`.

Consequently there is no dimension-independent exact/reducing
invariantization theorem of the form

```text
rank(Hull_B(Q))<=C rank(Q)                              (DBH8)
```

even when `Q` is the canonical high-displacement projection from an exact
endpoint, all noncubic rows are exact, and cubic energy tends to zero.
Nor can the hull rank be bounded linearly by cubic Frobenius energy: in
unnormalized units the energy is `O(1)` while the mandatory hull rank is
`p`.

This does not refute qualitative flexible repair.  Since
`p/|G_p|->0`, the entire legal hull still has vanishing density and may be
discarded at `o(1)` normalized-HS cost.  It proves that the legal operation
necessarily loses a moving packet-length factor.  Support-changing surgery
must therefore use the slow packet cutoff/long-tail discard, or a new
arithmetic charge; direct comparable-rank invariantization is false.
