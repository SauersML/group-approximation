**Step 1: one good coordinate.**
- Let `Ψ = [ψ_k]_omega : R -> prod_omega M_(d_k)(F_k)` be the embedding, and let `F ⊆ R` be a finite window.
- The window is described by finitely many atomic facts: `a + b = c`, `ab = c`, `a = 0`, `a = 1`, and
  `a ≠ b`, for elements of `F`.
- `Ψ` is a unital injective ring homomorphism. So each fact holds for `Ψ`, and hence for `ψ_k` at
  `omega`-many `k` (Łoś for finitely many atomic formulas and their negations).
- A finite intersection of sets in `omega` is in `omega`, so some single `k` satisfies all of them. Fix it,
  and put `φ = ψ_k|_F : F -> M_d(F_k)` with `d = d_k`.
- Then `φ` preserves the window's sums, products, `0` and `1`, and it is injective on `F`.

**Step 2: a finite ring.**
- Let `A ⊆ F_k` be the subring generated by the finitely many entries of the matrices `φ(a)`, `a ∈ F`.
  `A` is a finitely generated commutative ring, and `φ` lands in `M_d(A)`.
- For each pair `a ≠ b` in `F`, pick a nonzero entry `c_(ab) ∈ A` of `φ(a) − φ(b)`.
- Finitely generated commutative rings are residually finite (recalled, not imported: Orzech–Ribes;
  equivalently, maximal ideals of finitely generated rings have finite residue fields, and Krull's
  intersection theorem applies in these Noetherian rings). So there are finite-index ideals `I_(ab)` with
  `c_(ab) ∉ I_(ab)`.
- Put `I = ∩ I_(ab)`, a finite-index ideal, and let `π : M_d(A) -> M_d(A/I)` be reduction.
- `π ∘ φ` preserves the window's sums, products, `0` and `1`, since `π` is a unital ring homomorphism.
- It is injective on `F`, because each `c_(ab)` survives modulo `I`.
- `M_d(A/I)` is a finite unital ring. So `R` is LEF.

**Model tests.**
- **`R = F_q[t]`** embeds in `prod_omega F_(q^n)` through evaluations at points of growing degree, and it is
  LEF through its finite quotients. Consistent.
- **`R = A_1(Q)`,** the Weyl algebra, is exactly matricial (`weyl-algebra-is-exactly-matricial`), so it is
  LEF.
  - Partial models exist in `M_p(F_p)` for large `p`.
  - No contradiction with `lef-simple-rings-are-not-finitely-presented`: `A_1(Q)` contains `Q`, so it is not
    a finitely generated ring.
- **`R = L_k(1,2)`** is not directly finite. Finite rings are directly finite, and a finite inverse witness
  `ts = 1 ≠ st` transfers exactly. So the Leavitt algebra is not LEF, hence not exactly matricial.
  Consistent with `leavitt-algebra-has-no-unital-rank-model`.
