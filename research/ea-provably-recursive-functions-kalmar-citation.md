Imports one classical theorem: a recursive function is provably recursive in `IΔ0+Exp`
(equivalently in `EA`) if and only if it is Kalmár elementary. It is quoted verbatim in the
claim from P. Henk and F. Pakhomov, arXiv:1602.01822v2, §2.1, pp. 8–9, together with their
definition of `f↓` and the conservativity of `IΔ0+Exp` over `EA` (p. 7). Lane
`swarm-0917-w10-w10-f-pull` fetched the PDF on 2026-09-18.

Only the direction "provably recursive ⟹ elementary" is used. The tower bound on elementary
functions is proved in the claim by induction on the quoted inductive definition.

Scope: the original sources of the characterization (Parsons, Mints, Takeuti, and Henk–Pakhomov's
reference [20] for the definition of the class) were not re-read.
