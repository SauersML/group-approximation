The prerequisite identifies the recursive finite packets and their
shared row parabolic. The countermodel itself is given by explicit
finite-group formulas. For `V=F_2^5`, set `E=V direct-sum F_2` and
use the alternating bilinear map
`M((v,t),(w,s))=s v+t w` with quadratic refinement
`Q(v,t)=(1+t)v`. Its polarization identity makes

```text
(a,b,z)(a',b',z')=(a+a',b+b',z+z'+M(b,a'))
```

an associative group law and makes the maps

```text
h(a,b,z)=(b,a,z+M(a,b)),
u(a,b,z)=(a+b,b,z+Q(b))
```

involutive automorphisms whose product has order three. They commute
with the simultaneous linear action on `V`. Taking the resulting
finite semidirect product supplies the claimed `h,u,C_0` data.

The row elements are `a_v=X(v,0)` and the residual element is
`r=X(0,1)`, where `X(e)=(e,0,0)`. Since `Q(v,1)=0`, every affine
product cell is exact. Since `Q(v,0)=v`, its individual boundary
defect is the nonidentity central element `Z(v)=(0,0,v)` whenever
`v!=0`. This verifies both claims directly, with no approximation
or dimension estimate.

The subgroup generated by the row elements, `C_0`, and `r` is
the row parabolic of `GL_6(F_2)` times a group of order two.
It embeds in `GL_6(F_2) x GL_3(F_2)` by placing `r` at a
transvection of the second factor. Repeated regular actions of
these two finite groups on the same number of points restrict to
isomorphic free actions of their common subgroup. Identifying those
restrictions and generating a finite permutation group keeps both
factor representations faithful. This adds the whole exact enlarged
packet while retaining each nonidentity defect `Z(v)`.

The construction does not identify the auxiliary `u` with the
specified mixed word in the residual tail roots and does not verify
the original-tail first braid. Those omissions are part of the exact
scope of the claim, rather than additional premises for Theta.
