Let `B` have full MF radical and be normally generated by `d`, put
`D=<d>`, and let

```text
pi:C_d(Q)=B *_D (Q x D) -> Q,
K=ker(pi)=normalClosure(d).
```

Assume every quotient of `Q` is MF.  Then for every normal subgroup
`N normal C_d(Q)`,

```text
cl_MF(N)=N K=N normalClosure(d),                       (HMQ1)
```

and therefore

```text
C_d(Q)/N is MF  iff  K<=N  iff  d in N.               (HMQ2)
```

Thus the entire MF semantic closure operator is the principal closure
obtained by adjoining one fixed word.  The MF-closed normal subgroups are
exactly the interval `[K,C_d(Q)]`, and the maps

```text
N |-> pi(N),                J |-> pi^(-1)(J)
```

identify that interval with the complete normal-subgroup lattice of `Q`.

Every countable amenable group satisfies the hypothesis: its quotients are
amenable and hence MF.  Consequently the one-word classification `(HMQ2)`
holds with an arbitrary amenable visible quotient, not only a cyclic one.
