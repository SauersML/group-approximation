Let `D` be unital and suppose that there is a unital embedding `Z->D`.  If
`alpha in pi_k(U(n))` is stably zero, then its scalar image in

```text
pi_k(U(M_n(D)))
```

is zero.  Consequently, no such `D` can be the coefficient in the surviving
unstable-clutching witness for Problem LXXVI.
