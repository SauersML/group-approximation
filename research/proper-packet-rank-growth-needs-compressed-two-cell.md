Consider a finite atlas of finite packet groups with arbitrary injective
proper edge-group attachments.  Vertex and edge restriction multiplicities
alone cannot force rank accumulation along a path or cycle.  Positive
integer multiples of the vertex regular representations match on every edge
simultaneously, including on cycles, by
`finite-packet-graph-atlases-have-a-regular-stationary-flow`.

Consequently, a finite-base complex intended to distinguish finite from
infinite multiplicity must contain a two-cell which constrains the **chosen
edge intertwiners** on their multiplicity spaces.  After exactifying the
finite packet charts, that two-cell must have one of two forms:

1. all decoded source and target carriers have equal normalized rank; then
   its transports are square holonomies and the cell supplies no
   finite-dimensional rank pressure by itself;
2. some decoded cyclic products approximate carriers of unequal normalized
   rank; then finite trace cyclicity charges the gap, exactly the
   trace-cyclic-return-cell interface.

Thus proper finite-group attachments plus relative small cancellation do
not constitute a new multiplicity backend.  The only surviving rank-growing
variant reduces to constructing one compressed two-cell with a dimension-
independent unequal-carrier trace estimate.  This is the same local TCRC
actuator gap already isolated in the Schur/Leavitt route.
