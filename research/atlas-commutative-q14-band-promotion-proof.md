All residual entries lie in the PID `F2[t,t^-1]`.  If their gcd is one,
Bezout and rank subadditivity give a uniform gap.  Otherwise low residual
rank makes the gcd polynomial small.  Rank stability of the finite algebra
`F2[t,t^-1]/(g)` corrects the parameter to an exact root of the gcd, where
every residual entry vanishes and the exact finite-quotient theorem applies.
