For the revised degree-four KL word, no connected relative picture with at
most six relator vertices can have all inner coefficient regions trivial and
a nonidentity outer boundary lying wholly in any one of the four coefficient
copies.  Thus every picture witnessing failure of coefficient injectivity has
area at least eight.

This statement is exact for the full Leavitt coefficient group and is not
limited to the intended boundary `[c_0,d_0]`.
