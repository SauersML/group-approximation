Choose `L` divisible by all vertex-group orders and assign
`(L/|G_v|) lambda_(G_v)` to vertex `v`.  The restriction of the regular
representation of `G_v` to an injected copy of `H_e` is
`[G_v:H_e] lambda_(H_e)`.  Multiplication by `L/|G_v|` leaves exactly
`(L/|H_e|) lambda_(H_e)` at either endpoint.  Hence every edge restriction
equation holds simultaneously.

Restricting a regular representation further to an embedded elementary
abelian selector subgroup gives a positive multiple of its regular
representation, so every selector character has positive mass.  Evaluating
any proposed positive forbidden-mass lower bound on this stationary vector
gives zero on its mismatch side and a positive number on its forbidden side.
