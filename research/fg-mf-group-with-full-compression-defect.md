## Statement

Notation follows `non_mf_groups_exist.tex`. For a subgroup `L` of `G`,

```text
D_G(L) = << [u c u^-1, l] : u in G, u L u^-1 <= L, c in C_G(L), l in L >>_G .
```

**Theorem.** There is a finitely generated group `G` with an element `a'`
such that the following hold.

1. `G` embeds in the unitary group of a norm matrix corona, so `G` is MF.
   In particular `Rad_MF(G)=1` and `G` is not trivial.
2. `L=<a'>` is infinite cyclic, and some `t'` in `G` satisfies
   `t' L t'^-1 = <a'^2>`, a proper subgroup of `L`.
3. `D_G(L)=G`.
4. `G` is generated by finitely many involutions, and all of them are
   conjugate in `G` to one involution `e` in `D_G(L)`.

Hence, for every class `P` of pairs that contains `(G,<a'>)`, the
Kazhdan-free full-defect collapse (FDC_P) of
`thompson-commutator-full-defect-bars-t-free-collapse` is **false**. No
amenability hypothesis is needed. The manuscript's one-sided compression
criterion (Theorem `thm:compression-criterion`) assumes property (T) for
`L`. Here `L=Z`, so the criterion does not apply and there is no
contradiction. Whether such a `G` can itself have property (T) is left
open.

## Consequences

The following properties are automatic for any pair with `D_G(L)=G`,
using `intrinsic-compression-defect-functoriality-and-saturation`,
`compression-defects-die-in-rigid-targets` and
`locally-residually-finite-targets-kill-compression-defects`:

- the group is perfect;
- `L` normally generates the group;
- there is no nontrivial finite, abelian or linear quotient;
- there is no nontrivial locally residually finite quotient, since `L` is
  finitely generated.

So none of these properties, and none of the following properties of
`(G,L)`, can drive a Kazhdan-free proof that full defect forces
`Rad_MF=G`:

- finite generation of the ambient group;
- `L` infinite cyclic, torsion-free, finitely presented and amenable;
- strict one-sided compression.

This removes "finite generation of the ambient group" from the survivor
list of `thompson-commutator-full-defect-bars-t-free-collapse`,
unconditionally.

## Class killed

The class killed is every Kazhdan-free argument that deduces
`Rad_MF(G)=G` from `D_G(L)=G`, finite generation of `G`, and hypotheses
on `L` satisfied by `Z`.

- The invariant is the corona symmetry group `G`.
- The step where each member fails is the passage from full defect to
  "every corona representation of G is trivial". Here the identity
  representation of `G` is a nontrivial corona representation.

The surviving hypotheses a Kazhdan-free route must use are:

- finite presentability of `G`;
- torsion-freeness of `G`;
- simplicity of `G`;
- property (T), or another spectral input, for `L` or for its compression
  hull (the manuscript's hypothesis);
- a spectral input on `G`;
- the specific ring data of `Delta=St_20(L_{F_2}(1,2))`.
