Let `W=F_2^(X)` and let `G` act oligomorphically with finitely generated
finite-tuple stabilizers.  The finitary special linear group is generated by
transvections `e_xy`; its square, Steinberg-triangle, and disjoint-support
relations involve bounded index tuples.  There are finitely many `G`-orbits
of those tuples.  Taking orbit representatives, stabilizer generators, and
one relator per orbit gives a finite presentation of
`SL_fin(W) semidirect G`.

Adjoin `J,X_v,Z_phi` with the additive and pairing Pauli relations.  These
also have bounded arity and finitely many orbits on the two-sorted
`W disjoint_union W^circ`, so the same compression gives a finite
presentation of the Pauli semidirect product.

On `ell^2(W)`, translations `X_v` and phases `Z_phi` give an exact model with
`J=-I`.  Choosing `N` dual vector/covector pairs embeds `E_N` for every `N`
with that same `J`.  A finite-dimensional `J=-1` sector would have dimension
divisible by `2^N` for all `N`, impossible; hence all finite-dimensional
representations kill `J`, while the displayed infinite representation does
not.  With an amenable oligomorphic actor the whole extension is amenable,
showing why the result alone is not nonhyperlinearity.
