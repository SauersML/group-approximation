`w4-vf-linear-b`).

Let `R = L_(F_3)(1,2)`, `G = R^x`, `z = -1`, `S_- = eps_- F_3[G]`, `u = [eps_-]` and `H = E x| V`.
- For a finite subgroup `K` containing `z` and an idempotent matrix `p` over `eps_- F_3[K]`, put
  `trl(p) = 2 dim_(F_3)(p F_3[K]^n) / |K|`.
- For `L <= G`, let `H_fin(L)` be the subgroup of `K_0(S_-)` generated by the classes of such `p` with `K <= L`.
- Let `s_3 = 2 t_3`, where `t_3` is the additive lifted trace.

1. *Well defined.* `trl` extends to a homomorphism `H_fin(G) -> Q`, the restriction of `s_3`. So no equality
   `sum n_i [p_i] = 0` among finite-subgroup projectives has `sum n_i trl(p_i) != 0`.
2. *The whole invariant.* `s_3 : K_0(S_-) -> Z_3` has `s_3(u) = 1` and equals `trl` on every finite subgroup of `G`.
   In particular it is Haar measure on clopens of `M_-` and `1/2` on each semidihedral block. It also satisfies
   `s_3 o F_* = s_3/2` and `s_3 o alpha_* = s_3`, for the halving map and the transpose-inverse automorphism.
3. *Extension.* For every subgroup `L` with `u in L <= H_fin(G)`, `trl|_L` extends to a state on `(K_0(S_-), u)` iff
   `trl >= 0` on `L ∩ K_0^+`.
4. *All states.* `(K_0(S_-), u)` admits a state iff `trl >= 0` on `H_fin(H) ∩ K_0^+`.
5. *The corner.* `ternary-anti-invariant-swap-corner-is-full` holds iff there are finite sums `P`, `Q` of projectives
   induced from finite subgroups of `H`, a projective `X` and `r >= 0` with `P (+) S_-^r ~= Q (+) X (+) S_-^r` and
   `trl(P) < trl(Q)`. A witness `c e_- b = eps_-` supplies `P = e_- S_-`, `Q = eps_- S_-` and `X = h S_-`, where
   `h = e_- - (e_- b)(c e_-)`, and `s_3([h]) = -1/2`.

Parts 4 and 5 use `anti-central-k0-states-equal-lifted-trace-on-signed-thompson`, which is not yet verified.

The condition in part 4 is the open claim `anti-central-lifted-trace-is-relatively-positive`. Its truth refutes the
swap corner, and its failure is the swap corner.
