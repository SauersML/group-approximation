title: A monotone FIID partition model realizes an immediate sampling-radius jump and the matching peak exponent

Section 7. Merge all vertices with iid labels <=t into a single class, leaving other vertices singleton. Then a_n(t)=t^2+(1-t^2)r_n. The logarithmic derivative has maximum sqrt((1-r_n)/r_n), at t=sqrt(r_n/(1-r_n)), for r_n<1/2. Its exponential rate is (-log rho)/2. Classes are not graph-connected, so this is not Bernoulli percolation and does not refute Benjamini-Schramm.
