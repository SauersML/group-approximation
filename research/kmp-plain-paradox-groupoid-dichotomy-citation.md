Read from the arXiv PDF of 2502.17190v3 (28 Jul 2026) on 2026-09-13.

* Theorem 6.5 (p. 37): for simple `C*_r(G, L)`, conditions (2) "C*_r(G, L) is
  stably finite", (5) "S_B(G) admits a nontrivial state" and (9) "there is a
  nontrivial regular G-invariant Borel measure on X" are equivalent.
* Corollary 6.8 (p. 38): "Let (G, L) be a twisted groupoid with minimal and
  topologically free G. Let B be an inverse semigroup basis consisting of
  precompact, sigma-compact subsets that trivialise the twist L. Assume that
  there is no f in C*_r(G, L)_+ \ {0} for which {x in X : E(f)(x) != 0} is
  meagre. Assume that the type semigroup S_B(G) has plain paradoxes. Then
  C*_r(G, L) is simple and either purely infinite or stably finite."
* Remark 6.6: for ample `G` and compact open bisections, Theorem 6.5 holds
  with `S(G)`.
* Definition 2.18 and Example 2.22 as quoted in the claim.

The Cantor consequence in the claim body is a direct specialization: the
transformation groupoid is Hausdorff and ample, and a nonzero continuous
function on a Baire space has non-meagre support.
