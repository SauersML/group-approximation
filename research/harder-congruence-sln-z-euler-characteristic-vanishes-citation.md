Literature import. The source is J. Bajpai, G. Harder, I. Horozov, M. Moya Giusti, *Boundary
and Eisenstein cohomology of SL_3(Z)*, arXiv:1812.03734v2 [math.NT] (23 Mar 2020). The PDF
was fetched from arxiv.org on 2026-09-18 (swarm-0917-w11) and text-extracted.

Definition, Section 5, page 17, eq. (16). Formula rendered in plain text:

> The homological Euler characteristic χh of a group Γ with coefficients in a representation
> V is defined by (16) χh(Γ, V) = Σ_{i=0}^∞ (−1)^i dim H^i(Γ, V).

The statement, Section 5.1, page 17:

> For any torsion free arithmetic subgroup Γ ⊂ SLn(R) we have the Gauss-Bonnet formula
> χh(Γ\X) = ∫_{Γ\X} ωGB where ωGB is the Gauss-Bonnet-Chern differential form and
> X = SLn(R)/SO(n, R), see [10]. This differential form is zero if n > 2 and therefore for
> any torsion free congruence subgroup Γ ⊂ SLn(Z), χh(Γ\X) = 0. In particular, by the
> definition of orbifold Euler characteristic given by (17), this implies that χ(SL3(Z)) = 0.

Reference [10] of the source is G. Harder, *A Gauss-Bonnet formula for discrete arithmetically
defined groups*, Ann. Sci. École Norm. Sup. (4) 4 (1971), 409--455. It was not fetched.
