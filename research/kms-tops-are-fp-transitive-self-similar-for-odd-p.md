## Setting

- `M` is a Minsky machine with `K >= 1` glasses. `G(M)` is the group of
  Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir (arXiv:1204.6506v5, §4.1), built with a
  prime `p`. KMS allow any prime ("Also fix a prime $p$ (say, $p=2$)").
- `Q = <L_1 ∪ L_2>` is the top: the subgroup generated by the `A`-letters and
  the `a`-letters. By KMS Lemma `lH` (relations G2),
  `Q = ∏_{i=1}^K B_i × B_0`, with `B_i = <a_i, a_i', A_i>` and
  `B_0 = <ã_i, ã_i', A_0 : i ≤ K>`.
- For `d >= 1` and `g ∈ F_p[x]`, `KS_d(g)` denotes the group of
  Kochloukova--Sidki Theorem D (arXiv:1710.04745; Geom. Dedicata 204 (2020)
  241--264):
  - `Ã_d = ⊗_{i≤d} F_p[x_i^{±1}, 1/g(x_i)]`;
  - `Q̃ = Z^{2d} = <x_i, y_i>`, where `x_i` acts by multiplication by `x_i`
    and `y_i` by multiplication by `g(x_i)`;
  - `KS_d(g) = Ã_d ⋊ Q̃`.

## Statement

Let `p` be odd.

1. **Identification.** `B_0 ≅ KS_K(1 + x)` and `B_i ≅ KS_1(1 + x)` for
   `1 ≤ i ≤ K`. The isomorphism sends
   - `A_0 ↦ 1 ∈ Ã_K`;
   - `(ã_i')^{-1} ↦ x_i`;
   - `ã_i^{-1} ↦ y_i`.

   In words, relation G4 says exactly that `y_i` acts as `1 + x_i`. In the
   coordinates `s_i = ã_i` of `two-counter-kms-top-has-no-affine-self-similar-action`,
   this is the Möbius change of variable `x_i = s_i^{-1} − 1`. It maps
   `R_K = F_p[s_i^{±1}, (1 − s_i)^{-1}]` onto `F_p[x_i^{±1}, (1 + x_i)^{-1}]`,
   and `U_K = <s_i, 1 − s_i>` onto `<x_i, 1 + x_i>`.
2. **Self-similarity.** `Q ≅ KS_1(1+x)^K × KS_K(1+x)` is transitive
   self-similar. It has a simple virtual endomorphism of index `p^{3(K+1)}`,
   the product of the Kochloukova--Sidki maps.
3. **Finite presentation.** `Q` is finitely presented, since each factor is
   (KS Lemma 7.4 with `g = 1 + x`).
4. **Characteristic-p Kochloukova--Luiz fails.** Take `K = 2`. Then `B_0` has
   an abelian normal subgroup `A = R_2`, a cyclic `F_p Q̃`-module, and
   `B_0 = A ⋊ Z^4`, with the following properties:
   - `A` is a domain of Krull dimension 2;
   - no nontrivial element of `Z^4` is algebraic over `F_p` in `Frac(A)`;
   - `B_0` is finitely presented;
   - `B_0` is self-similar.

   So the Main Theorem of Kochloukova--Luiz (arXiv:2509.05798) has no
   analogue with `Z` replaced by `F_p`. Kochloukova--Luiz themselves record
   that `Z`-torsion-freeness "is important", citing Kochloukova--Sidki. The new
   point is that the counterexample is the KMS top itself. It is the
   reduction mod `p` of their own non-self-similar Section 8, Example 2,
   `Z[x^{±1}, 1/(x+1)] ⋊ <x, x+1>`, which is `KS_1(1+x)` lifted to
   characteristic 0.

## Consequences for the crux

- **KD2_p test 1 dies.** `two-counter-kms-groups-are-self-similar`, Suggested
  first test 1, asks whether the Kochloukova--Luiz argument transfers to
  characteristic `p` and kills metabelian tops. It does not. The top of every
  KMS group with `p` odd is self-similar.
- **KD2_p test 2 is answered positively.** That test asks for a
  characteristic-`p` self-similar module of Krull dimension at least 2 that
  carries the BR-conjoint letter `x ↦ x(a − 1)`. `KS_K(1+x)` is exactly such
  a module, with the conjoint relation built in.
- **The affine kill is sharp.** `two-counter-kms-top-has-no-affine-self-similar-action`
  forbids affine realizations for `K ≥ 2`. The Kochloukova--Sidki realization
  moves counters by odometer carries: `x_1^p ∈ Q̃_0` is sent to `x_2`, and the
  transversal of `Q̃_0` is `{x_1^j y_1^k : 0 ≤ j, k < p}`. So it is not affine,
  as that node predicts.
- **Localization.** Any proof of KD2_p for odd `p` may use a top that is
  already self-similar and finitely presented. Any obstruction to KD2_p for odd
  `p` must come from the configuration layer `T`, from relations G5--G8, and
  from how `T` is glued to `Q`. It cannot come from `Q` alone. The known
  constraints on `T` are `kms-configuration-modules-have-trivial-coinvariants`
  and `kms-self-similarity-must-make-central-configurations-transient`.
- **`p = 2` is not covered.** Over `F_2` the three punctures `0, 1, ∞` of `R_K`
  can only be placed at `0, ∞, 1`. The only available `g` is then `x + 1 = x − 1`,
  which Theorem D excludes: `A_0 S^{-1} = A S^{-1}`, so the Dantas--Sidki map
  has no domain. Whether `B_0` is self-similar for `p = 2` is left open.
