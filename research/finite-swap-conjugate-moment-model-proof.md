The subgroup generated by finitely many distinct lamp involutions is their
free product by `(DSW1)`.  A finite free product of finite groups is
virtually free, hence residually finite.  For each word in the prescribed
finite set choose a finite quotient in which it survives, and take the
product of those quotients.  The left regular representation of the product
quotient has normalized trace zero on each surviving nonidentity element.
Expanding each requested monomial in the projections `(1+s_x)/2` reduces its
trace to this finite list of group-word traces.
