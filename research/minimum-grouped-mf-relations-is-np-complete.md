The graph-certified grouped-relation problem is NP-complete even for
LEF Kazhdan sources with three torsion generators, independent commuting
involution inputs and output, and a return network made of internally
disjoint paths of length two. Inputs contain the graph and edge-group
description; expanded words can be supplied in polynomial size.
Every ordinary input quotient keeps the output nontrivial.
