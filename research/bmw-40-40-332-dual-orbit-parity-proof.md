The only computation used is the orbit count in `dual332V.log`. Everything else is proved here.

## 0. Conventions

- Automorphisms of `X* = {0,1,2}*` act on the left, with `s(xv) = s(x) s|_x(v)`.
- Composition is `(gh)(v) = g(h(v))`. Hence `(gh)|_x = g|_{h(x)} h|_x` and `g|_{xy} = (g|_x)|_y`.
- `aut.py` stores `K(332V)` as `out[a][x]` and `sec[a][x]`, in this convention, and `checkaut.log`
  confirms it is the output of `selfrep.build`:
  - `a_0 = (a_1,a_1,a_1)`
  - `a_1 = (12)(a_2,a_0,a_0)`
  - `a_2 = (12)(a_0,a_2,a_2)`
  - `τ = (01)(τ,τ,τ)`
- `D* = {a_0,a_1,a_2}`. Two facts are read off the table:
  - **(F1)** every section of a letter of `D*` is again in `D*`, never `1`;
  - **(F2)** the root permutation of `a_0` is trivial, and that of `a_1` and of `a_2` is `(12)`.
- A = V_4 * C_2 with `V_4 = {1,a_0,a_1,a_2}` and `C_2 = ⟨τ⟩`. The map `π : A → K` exists by
  `bmw-332-193-self-similar-certificates-need-new-relation` §1. It is also immediate here: the letters
  are involutions, and `a_0a_1 = a_2` holds in `K`.

## 1. Sections of alternating words (duality)

For `n ≥ 1` and `u = (u_1,…,u_n) ∈ (D*)^n`, let `w_u` be the word `τ u_n τ u_{n−1} ⋯ τ u_1`, read as
an element of `K`. So `u_1` is the letter that acts first.

**Lemma 1.** For `x ∈ X`, define `x_1 = x` and `x_{i+1} = τ(u_i(x_i))`. Then:
- `w_u(x) = x_{n+1}`;
- `w_u|_x = w_{u'}`, where `u' = (u_1|_{x_1}, …, u_n|_{x_n}) ∈ (D*)^n`.

*Proof.* Apply `(gh)|_x = g|_{h(x)}h|_x` letter by letter from the right. The letter `u_i` meets the
point `x_i`: its image is `u_i(x_i)`, and it contributes the section `u_i|_{x_i}`. The following `τ`
sends that image to `τ(u_i(x_i)) = x_{i+1}` and contributes the section `τ`. So
`w_u|_x = τ u_n|_{x_n} ⋯ τ u_1|_{x_1}`, and every `u_i|_{x_i}` lies in `D*` by (F1). ∎

**The dual transducer.**
- `B` has states `b_x` for `x ∈ X`, and input and output alphabet `D*`.
- In state `b_x`, reading `d` outputs `d|_x` and moves to state `b_{τ(d(x))}`.
- Lemma 1 says exactly that `w_u|_x = w_{b_x(u)}`, where `b_x` reads `u_1` first.
- By induction on `|v|`, for `v = y_1⋯y_k`: `w_u|_v = w_{β_v(u)}`, where `β_v = b_{y_k} ∘ ⋯ ∘ b_{y_1}`.

`dual.py` builds `B` literally from this rule: `B_out[x][d] = sec[d][x]` and
`B_sec[x][d] = τ(out[d][x])`. It prints:
- `b_0 = (a_0 a_1 a_2)(b_1,b_1,b_1)`
- `b_1 = (a_0 a_1)(b_0,b_2,b_2)`
- `b_2 = (a_0 a_1)(b_2,b_0,b_0)`

Here the output permutations are on `D*`, and the sections are listed in the order `a_0,a_1,a_2`.

**Each b_x is a bijection of (D*)^n.** Each state's output map `d ↦ d|_x` is a permutation of `D*`,
namely `(a_0a_1a_2)` or `(a_0a_1)`. A transducer whose every state has bijective output is invertible,
so it permutes each level.

**Semigroup orbit = group orbit.** The `β_v` form the semigroup generated by the `b_x`, acting on the
finite set `(D*)^n` by permutations. A semigroup of permutations of a finite set is a group. So
`{β_v(u) : v ∈ X*}` is exactly the `⟨B⟩`-orbit `O(u)` of `u`.

**Lemma 2 (triviality test).** `w_u = 1` in `K` iff every `u' ∈ O(u)` gives `w_{u'}` a trivial root
permutation.

*Proof.* A tree automorphism `g` is trivial iff `g|_v` has trivial root permutation for every
`v ∈ X*`. Indeed `g(y_1⋯y_k) = g(y_1) g|_{y_1}(y_2) ⋯ g|_{y_1⋯y_{k−1}}(y_k)`. Now apply §1:
`{w_u|_v} = {w_{u'} : u' ∈ O(u)}`. ∎

## 2. The parity of a_0

**Lemma 3.** The root permutation of `w_u` has sign `(−1)^{#{i : u_i = a_0}}`.

*Proof.*
- The root permutation of a product is the product of the root permutations of the letters.
- `τ` contributes `(01)`, which is odd. There are `n` of them.
- By (F2), `a_0` contributes the identity and `a_1, a_2` contribute `(12)`, which is odd.
- So the sign is `(−1)^{n + #non-a_0} = (−1)^{2·#non-a_0 + #a_0} = (−1)^{#a_0}`. ∎

**Corollary 4.** If `O(u)` contains a sequence with an odd number of `a_0`, then `w_u ≠ 1` in `K`.
(Lemmas 2 and 3: that section has an odd, hence nontrivial, root permutation.)

## 3. Normal form in V_4 * C_2

**Lemma 5.** Every `1 ≠ g ∈ A` is conjugate in `A` to one of the following:
- (i) a nontrivial element of `V_4` or of `C_2`;
- (ii) `τ d_n ⋯ τ d_1` with `n ≥ 1` and all `d_i ∈ D*`.

Moreover, a word in the letters of length `L` gives, in case (ii), `2n ≤ L`.

*Proof.* This is the standard normal form in a free product:
- Multiply adjacent letters from the same factor. This merges `D`-letters in `V_4` and cancels
  `ττ`, and gives a reduced alternating word of length at most `L`.
- Then conjugate away a first and a last syllable from the same factor. This merges or cancels them
  and does not increase the length.
- The result is cyclically reduced. It has length 1 (case (i)), or even length `2n` with alternating
  factors, which is case (ii) after a rotation. ∎

**The factor elements are nontrivial in K.**
- `τ`, `a_1` and `a_2` have nontrivial root permutation.
- `a_0 = (a_1,a_1,a_1)` is nontrivial because `a_1` is.

## 4. Proof of the theorem

**Item 1** of the claim is Lemmas 2 and 3. Our `w_u` with `u = δ(w)` is the node's word `w`.

**Item 2.**
- Let `1 ≠ g ∈ A`. By Lemma 5, `π(g)` is conjugate in `K` to one of two things:
  - a nontrivial factor element, which is `≠ 1` by §3;
  - some `w_u`, `u ∈ (D*)^n`, which is `≠ 1` by Corollary 4 and the hypothesis.
- So `ker π = 1`, and `π` is an isomorphism.
- `A` is infinite: for example `τa_1` has infinite order, since its powers are reduced alternating
  words.
- So `K` is infinite, and `Γ` is irreducible by Burger–Mozes Prop. 1.2, as recorded in
  `bmw-44-sat-survivor-parity-kernels-not-left-orderable`.
- The dichotomy there then reduces, for `#332`, to left-orderability of `Γ^+`.
- If `⟨B⟩` is level-transitive, then `O(u) = (D*)^n` contains `(a_0,a_1,…,a_1)`, which has one `a_0`.
  So level-transitivity implies the hypothesis.

**Item 3.**
- `dual332V.log` shows that the orbit of one point of `(D*)^n` has `3^n` elements for `n = 1,…,8`.
  So `⟨B⟩` is transitive on those levels, and the hypothesis holds for `n ≤ 8`.
- Let `1 ≠ g ∈ ker π`. By Lemma 5 and §3, `g` is conjugate to a case-(ii) word with `n ≥ 9`, whose
  cyclically reduced length is `2n ≥ 18`.
- Suppose a word of length `≤ 17` were trivial in `K` but not in `A`. By Lemma 5 it would give such an
  element with `2n ≤ 17`, which is impossible.
- So on words of length `≤ 17`, triviality in `K` and in `A` coincide. ∎

## Remark (why the open step is not immediate)

- Level-transitivity of `⟨B⟩` would follow from `B` being self-replicating and transitive on level 1.
- The claim node records that `B` meets the same non-congruence index-3 obstruction as `K`. That is
  checked modulo its relations of length `≤ 8` (`bobs_8_2.log`).
- So the orbit condition has to be proved by another invariant.
