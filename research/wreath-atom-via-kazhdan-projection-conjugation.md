**Dead.** The proposal was to take a lamp-averaging element and conjugate it
by the Kazhdan projection of `Gamma`, so that eigenvalue atoms isolate the
`Gamma`-invariant part where compression acts. That projection is `0` in
`L(W)` and is not an integral element
(`kazhdan-projections-vanish-in-regular-group-algebra`). Kazhdan rigidity
enters the trace picture only as spectral gaps of integral Laplacians.
