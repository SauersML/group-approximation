Let `Λ` be one of the four diagrams. `W_Λ` is infinite, and each `Λ − {s}` is spherical (see
the target), so `A_{Λ−{s}}` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture by
`spherical-artin-groups-satisfy-k-pi-1-conjecture`. With `Δ_Λ` contractible, the criterion
`artin-k-pi-1-from-contractible-artin-complex` gives the conjecture for `A_Λ`.
