**ESTABLISHED.**  Put

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,       Q=1-ST,       P=ST,
E=EL_5(J).
```

For an ordered pair of coordinate rows `(i,j)`, let `a_ij` be the identity
off those rows and let its `(i,j)` block be

```text
a=[[S,Q],[0,T]],          a^(-1)=[[T,0],[Q,S]].          (JTC1)
```

On the first three rows, with coefficient columns written `(r_1,r_2,r_3)^t`,
the three cyclic shifts act by

```text
a_12:(r_1,r_2,r_3) |-> (S r_1+Q r_2, T r_2, r_3),
a_23:(r_1,r_2,r_3) |-> (r_1, S r_2+Q r_3, T r_3),
a_31:(r_1,r_2,r_3) |-> (T r_1, r_2, Q r_1+S r_3).       (JTC2)
```

Their cyclic product is not an oriented one-sided shift.  It is the
head-controlled swap

```text
c=a_31 a_23 a_12
 =[[1,0,0],[0,P,Q],[0,Q,P]],            c^2=1.          (JTC3)
```

Let `w_23` be the Weyl swap of rows `2,3`, put

```text
u_2=x_24(1),       z_2=x_24(Q),
u_3=x_34(1),       z_3=x_34(Q).
```

Then

```text
c u_2 c^(-1)=x_24(P)x_34(Q)=u_2 z_2 z_3,
c u_3 c^(-1)=u_3 z_2 z_3,
c z_2 c^(-1)=z_3,                 c z_3 c^(-1)=z_2,    (JTC4)
w_23 u_2 w_23^(-1)=u_3,           w_23 z_2 w_23^(-1)=z_3.
```

Consequently

```text
H=<c,w_23,u_2,z_2>
```

is finite: its elementary abelian normal subgroup
`<u_2,u_3,z_2,z_3>` has rank four and its quotient is generated by the two
commuting involutions induced by `c,w_23`.  The head root `z_2` is nontrivial
in `H`, so the literal controlled-swap packet has an exact finite-dimensional
regular model in which the head survives.

The stronger subsystem retaining the three shifts separately is still
operator-MF.  Let

```text
A=<a_12,a_23,a_31,w_23>,
G_triangle=<A,u_2,z_2> <= E.                            (JTC5)
```

Each generator of `A` lies in the determinant-one monomial-symbol preimage
`K` of `binary-jacobson-monomial-symbol-preimage-is-marked-mf`; hence `A` is
amenable.  The common-target column group

```text
U_4={x_14(r_1)x_24(r_2)x_34(r_3):r_i in J}
```

is abelian and normalized by `A`.  Thus `G_triangle cap U_4` is abelian and
`G_triangle/(G_triangle cap U_4)` is a quotient of `A`.  Therefore
`G_triangle` is amenable.  By `amenable-implies-operator-mf` it has a faithful
norm-matrix-corona model, and that model separates `z_2` from `1`.

Hence a three-coordinate cycle of balanced triangular shifts, Weyl row
permutations, one raw constant root in a fixed target column, and the head
root does **not** yield an oriented Hall deficit or a square Toeplitz decoder.
The next occurrence must leave this common-column amenable semidirect product:
it must either couple different target columns or use a larger interaction
with the monomial-symbol preimage than these balanced shifts and Weyl swaps.
