For the integral marked extension B_c0, z^(7^a) belongs to
gamma_(5+6a)(B_c0) for every a >= 0. Consequently an image of z of
order at least 49 in a nilpotent quotient requires class at least eleven.
This gives no upper bound on the finite orders of z.
