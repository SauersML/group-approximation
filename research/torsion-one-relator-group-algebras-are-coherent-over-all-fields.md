Let `G` be a one-relator group with nontrivial torsion and let `K` be any
field, of arbitrary characteristic.  Then `K[G]` is coherent.

This is an all-fields theorem for the torsion branch only.  It makes no
positive-characteristic claim for torsion-free one-relator groups.
