Write

```text
B=BS(1,4)=<R,S | RSR^(-1)=S^4>,
D=D_infinity=<R,X | X^2=1, XRX=R^(-1)>.
```

The displayed passive-core presentation is the amalgam

```text
K=B *_(<R>) D.
```

The standard one-relator complex of `B` is aspherical.  Its rational
abelianization is generated by `R`, because the relator gives `3[S]=0`;
Euler characteristic zero then gives

```text
H_1(B;Q)=Q<R>,             H_2(B;Q)=0.
```

The infinite dihedral group is virtually free and has finite abelianization,
so `H_1(D;Q)=H_2(D;Q)=0`.  In the Mayer--Vietoris sequence the edge map

```text
H_1(<R>;Q) -> H_1(B;Q) (+) H_1(D;Q)
```

is `r |-> (r,0)`, hence is an isomorphism.  Exactness proves

```text
H_1(K;Q)=H_2(K;Q)=0.                                  (1)
```

For completeness, compute the quotient rather than importing another open
node.  The Bruhat--Tits edge decomposition is

```text
G=PSL_2(Z[1/2])
 = PSL_2(Z) *_(Gamma_0(2)) PSL_2(Z),
PSL_2(Z)=C_2*C_3,              Gamma_0(2)=C_2*Z.
```

Thus both vertex groups have zero positive-degree rational homology, while
the edge has `H_1(-;Q)=Q`.  Mayer--Vietoris gives

```text
H_2(G;Q)=Q,                 H_1(G;Q)=0.                 (2)
```

The long exact sequence of the mapping cone of `Bq:BK->BG` contains

```text
H_2(K;Q) -> H_2(G;Q) -> H_2(Cone(Bq);Q) -> H_1(K;Q).
```

Substituting `(1)` and `(2)` identifies the middle arrow with an isomorphism
`Q ~= H_2(Cone(Bq);Q)`, as asserted.  No injectivity assumption on `q` is
used.
