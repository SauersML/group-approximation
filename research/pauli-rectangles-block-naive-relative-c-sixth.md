Collapse every X-equality cloud to the factor `H_X` and every Z-equality
cloud to `H_Z`, and give each signed X--Z pair edge a letter in the free
factor generated by `P_0 union P_1`.  A rectangle of four pair incidences
then has a contour of free-product syllable length at most eight:

```text
p H_Z p^-1 H_X p H_Z p^-1 H_X.                                (PR8-1)
```

The complete Pauli commutation table, and its ordinary occurrence-cloud
degree reduction, contains such rectangles.  Hence its natural relative
graphical presentation cannot satisfy the minimum-length-nine hypothesis of
`relative-c-sixth-protects-sign-double-cosets`.

This does not refute the occurrence route.  It shows exactly what a terminal
construction must add: either a finite **base address group** in which all
bounded Pauli rectangle templates already hold while the two sign double
cosets remain separated, followed by relative small cancellation only for
long contours; or a dimension-preserving gadget which lengthens those
relative rectangles.  An arbitrary graph lift is not such a gadget because
its sheet number can absorb the dimension lower bound.
