**ESTABLISHED.** Each group below is infinite, finitely generated, nonamenable
and torsion, and each of its amenable subgroups is finite.

1. **Ol'shanskii's torsion Tarski monster `T`.** This is the infinite torsion
   group with all proper subgroups cyclic, from A. Yu. Ol'shanskii, *An infinite
   group with subgroups of prime order*, Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR Ser. Mat. 44
   (1980).
2. **The free Burnside group `B(m,n)`**, for `m >= 2` and odd `n >= 1003`.

The route proves this from imported statements. The nonamenability in 1 and 2
and the subgroup theorem of Ivanov and Atabekyan are imports and were not
independently verified.
