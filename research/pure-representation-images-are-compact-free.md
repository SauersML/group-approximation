title: Every representation image of a pure C-star algebra is disjoint from the compact operators
---

Let `A` be pure and let `rho:A -> B(H)` be an arbitrary representation, not
necessarily faithful, nondegenerate, or irreducible.  Then

```text
rho(A) intersect K(H) = {0}.                          (R1)
```

Thus purity rules out compact operators in all representation images, not
only in irreducible images.
