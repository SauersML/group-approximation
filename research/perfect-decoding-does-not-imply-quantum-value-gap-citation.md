Slofstra, arXiv:1703.08618, constructs a linear-system nonlocal game with a
perfect strategy in `C_qa` but no perfect strategy in `C_q` or `C_qs`.
Because `C_qa` is the closure of finite-dimensional quantum correlations, the
finite-dimensional quantum value of this game is one while no perfect
finite-dimensional strategy exists.  This is exactly the counterexample to the
inference recorded in the target claim.
