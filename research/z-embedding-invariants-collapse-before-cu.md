title: Unital Jiang--Su embeddings agree on KK, traces, and ideals before Cuntz data
---

Let `B` be unital and let `phi,psi:Z->B` be unital embeddings.  Then:

1. `[phi]=[psi]` in `KK(Z,B)`, and hence the induced maps on ordinary and
   coefficient K-theory agree;
2. `tau phi=tau psi` for every bounded trace `tau` on `B` (and likewise for
   normalized bounded 2-quasitraces);
3. every nonzero positive element of either image is full in `B`, so the two
   maps induce the same map from the ideal lattice of `Z` to that of `B`.

Thus a counterexample to STW LXIII cannot be separated by these invariants.
This statement does **not** assert that `Cu(phi)=Cu(psi)`.
