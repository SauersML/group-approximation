The proof is Section 2 of the artifact. It uses only Sylvester axioms.

- **Lemma 2.1.** At a periodic point of nonzero slope, a cylinder `[u]` with `x^r[u]` strictly inside `[u]` gives
  the wandering annulus. Shrinking `u` separates the `r` orbit pieces.
- **Setup 2.2.**
  - Conjugating one clopen `p`-cycle along the orbit gives independent disjoint lamps.
  - After a scalar extension by restriction of scalars, isotypic idempotents give a Boolean homomorphism from
    clopens of `mu_p^Z`, with rank `a [1 in S] + (1 - a) Haar(S)`.
  - `[x] e_S [x]^(-1) = e_(T S)`.
- **Theorem 2.3.**
  - Normalize `q(0) != 0`, and take first-return Kakutani towers over a cylinder of measure `p^(-l)` with heights at
    most `R`.
  - Covariance gives `c_0 e_(level i) = e_(level i) q([x]) - sum_(k>=1) c_k [x]^k e_(level i-k)`.
  - By induction every level idempotent lies in the left ideal generated by `q([x])` and the bottom `d` levels,
    so `1` lies in that ideal plus `e_(Z_R)`.
  - (S6) gives `rk q([x]) >= 1 - d p^(-l) - a - mu(Z_R)`, and letting `R`, then `l`, tend to infinity gives the
    bound.
