Build one finite group-relation gadget which, uniformly for every machine
control table, has all of the following properties in normalized-HS
microstates:

1. a fixed finite CPU exactifies on a positive-density `pi`-isotypic carrier;
2. its commutant multiplicity `M_k(C)` supports a finitely compiled simulation
   of the countable Amitsur--Levitzki instruction sequence;
3. the forced zero at instruction `k` activates a finite control flag;
4. the flag propagates to a fixed marked word with error at most
   `C Def(U)`, where `C` is independent of `k`, the matrix dimension, and the
   simulated runtime;
5. loss of carrier mass, failure of synchronization, or movement into
   incompatible multiplicity fibers pays a fixed normalized-HS defect.

The point of clause 2 is finite presentation: merely adjoining all inverses
`p_n^(-1)` gives a countably presented algebra.  The point of clause 4 is PCP
soundness: ordinary Boone/van Kampen propagation and direct evaluation of
`p_k` both have dimension- or runtime-dependent loss.  This claim is the
concentrated unresolved groupification step.

## Attempts

- **Raw Boone/S-machine simulation fails quantitatively.**  A computation of
  length `T` gives a derivation with error on the order of `T Def(U)`.  The
  approximator can always choose defect much smaller than `1/T`.
- **Direct evaluation of the dimension polynomial also fails
  quantitatively.**  The degree and coefficient mass of `p_k` grow with the
  unknown multiplicity `k`, so telescoping its evaluation does not give the
  universal constant in clause 4.
- **Conjugate terminal flag is only the last step.**  It makes the norm of the
  final conjugate equal to the norm of one base relator, but does not control
  the semantic comparison between the marked word and that conjugate.
- **Active route.**  Compile the constant-gap checks behind
  `kleene-mipco-self-destroying-game` through finite BCS/LCS control sectors,
  then return the terminal flag to the marked word.  The new
  `odd-selector-priority-decoding` lemma shows that overlapping active control
  sectors need no penalty: every odd selector atom can be assigned losslessly
  to one active branch.  The unproved quantitative points are now
  cross-context consistency and runtime-independent semantic transport from
  the triggered control to the fixed mark; either may still use
  `fixed-holonomy-on-overlap-pays-relator-energy` when a genuine incompatible
  carrier is present.
- **Same-model capacity formulation.**  The intended dimension oracle must
  force a contradiction *inside the candidate `d`-dimensional tuple*, not
  merely prove that a later, more accurate tuple needs dimension `F(d)`.
  This is isolated as `same-model-supercritical-clifford-trigger`: the trigger
  must robustly materialize a Clifford system whose certified minimum
  dimension is greater than `d` on the current positive-density carrier.
  Slofstra's established subexponential profile theorem supplies the closest
  quantitative template, but not this diagonal step.
