For a Kun--Thom Theorem E pair Gamma<=G, write N=EL_r(R) and
D=G *_Gamma G, with vertex maps i_0,i_1. If rho_0,rho_1:G->U(d)
are exact homomorphisms with finite images and agree on Gamma, then
rho_0(n)=rho_1(n) for every n in N.

In particular n=e_12(x_1^(-1)) belongs to N\Gamma, and the reduced
word w=i_0(n)i_1(n)^(-1) is nonidentity in D. Its image in every
such glued model is exactly I. Hence its normalized trace is 1,
not the canonical trace 0. This persists regardless of increasing
matrix dimensions or canonical convergence of the two vertex marginals.

This is a finite-model obstruction independent of Jihao Liu's analytic
theorem. It isolates why residual vertex approximations plus exact edge
agreement cannot construct the required mixed traces. It does not address
all infinite-image finite-dimensional representations or approximate
vertex representations.
