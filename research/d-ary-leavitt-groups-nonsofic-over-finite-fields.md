ESTABLISHED.  `Leavitt/AryEndpoints`: let `k` be a finite field and `A` a
nontrivial countable `k`-algebra carrying a `d`-ary Leavitt family, `d >= 2`.
Then `A^x` is nonsofic (`units_not_isSofic`), every `GL_{m+1}(A)` is nonsofic
(`gl_not_isSofic`), and every `EL_{m+1}(A)` with `m >= 1` is nonsofic
(`elementary_not_isSofic`).  Specialized to the universal algebra
`L_k(1,d)` this is `aryLeavitt_units_not_isSofic`,
`aryLeavitt_gl_not_isSofic`, `aryLeavitt_elementary_not_isSofic` and the
headline `aryLeavitt_EL4_not_isSofic`.

## Why finite fields, exactly

The restriction is the reach of the formalized property-`(T)` input: rank three
by the free-algebra theorem over a finite field, rank four by the Leavitt rank
equivalence.  The integral case `EL_4(L_Z(1,2))` is **not** formalized anywhere
in the development, and this is why -- not neglect, but a missing `(T)` theorem
over `Z`.

## The corner route, which is what makes it generic

A `d`-ary family places a *binary* family in the corner at
`e = p_0 + ... + p_{d-2}` (`AryCorner`), and the `k`-subalgebra generated by
the four binary family elements is finite type, countable and nontrivial -- so
finite generation costs nothing.  The rank-four pipeline
(`Leavitt/FamilyRankFour`) is stated uniformly in the pair (coefficient ring,
binary family), so it applies to that core; the corner-extension homomorphisms
then transport the nonsofic subgroups back into `A^x`, `GL_r(A)` and `EL_r(A)`,
injectively.

Note what is *not* asserted: the accompanying finite-generation, infiniteness
and property-`(T)` clauses of Theorem A are not restated here, and these
statements are about the universal algebras built in the development rather
than about a presented `L_k(1,d)`.
