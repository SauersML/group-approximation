Let `(A,phi)` be a selfless C-star probability space.  Then `A` is simple,
and the following structural alternatives apply:

* if `phi` is tracial, then `A` has stable rank one and strict comparison of
  positive elements by `phi`, and `phi` is the unique normalized
  `2`-quasitracial state;
* if `phi` is nontracial, then `A` is purely infinite.

In particular every selfless C-star algebra is pure.  The formerly untreated
nonfaithful-state case is included: if `phi` is nonfaithful, then `A` is
purely infinite and simple.
