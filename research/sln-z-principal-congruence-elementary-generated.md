ESTABLISHED (literature import).  Let `n >= 3` and `N >= 1`.  Then
`ker(SL_n(Z) -> SL_n(Z/NZ))` equals the normal subgroup of `SL_n(Z)` generated by the
matrices `I + t e_ij` with `i != j` and `t in NZ`.

This is Bass--Milnor--Serre, Publ. Math. IHÉS 33 (1967), Corollary 4.3 a) and b), with
their notation from §4 (p. 94).  The verbatim text is in the `-citation` route.
