**Established by citation.** Chun Guang Li and Zhuang Niu, *Stable rank of
C(X) ⋊ Gamma*, [arXiv:2008.03361v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.03361),
Corollary 7.14, read from the PDF text on MSI on 2026-09-11: "Let (X, Gamma) be a
free and minimal dynamical system with the (URP), and denote by
A = C(X) ⋊ Gamma. Then A ~= A tensor Z if, and only if, A has strict comparison
of positive elements. (In other words, A satisfies the Toms-Winter
conjecture). In particular, the statement holds for Gamma = Z^d."

The proof shows `A` is tracially 0-divisible and uses Niu's radius-of-comparison
estimates. STW v2 (arXiv:2506.10902), in its discussion of Problem XVIII,
lists this class as one where the conjecture "is known in full
generality", citing [270, Corollary 7.14] (Li--Niu), [317] (Niu, Canad. J.
Math. 76 (2024), 1240--1266), and [1] (Alboiu--Lutley, Münster J. Math. 15
(2022), 167--220) for `d = 1`.
