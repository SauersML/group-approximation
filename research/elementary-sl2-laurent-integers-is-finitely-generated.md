title: "E_2(Z[t,t^-1]) is generated by five elementary-type matrices"
---

Let `R = Z[t,t^{-1}]` and let `E_2(R)` be the subgroup of `SL_2(R)` generated by the
elementary matrices `E_12(r) = [[1,r],[0,1]]` and `E_21(r) = [[1,0],[r,1]]`, `r ∈ R`.
Then `E_2(R)` is generated by

`E_12(1), E_12(t), E_21(1), E_21(t), diag(t, t^{-1})`.

So `SL_2(R)` is finitely generated as soon as it is generated by `E_2(R)` together
with finitely many further matrices; in particular if `SL_2(R) = E_2(R)`.
