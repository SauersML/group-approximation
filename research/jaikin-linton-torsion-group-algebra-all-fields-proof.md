Jaikin-Zapirain and Linton, *On the coherence of one-relator groups and their
group algebras*, Annals of Mathematics (2) 201 (2025), 909--959,
doi:10.4007/annals.2025.201.3.4, state immediately before Theorem 1.6 that a
one-relator group with torsion is virtually free-by-cyclic and that `K[G]` is
coherent for any field `K`.  The proof of Theorem 1.1(2) repeats this branch
and cites Henneke--López-Álvarez, Proposition 2.9, or their own Theorem 3.4.

Thus the characteristic-zero restriction needed for the Hughes-free
division-ring argument in the torsion-free branch is absent here.  For every
field `K`, the group algebra of every one-relator group with nontrivial
torsion is coherent.  QED
