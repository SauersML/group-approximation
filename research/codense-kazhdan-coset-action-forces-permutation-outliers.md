Let `C<A` be proper.  Fix a finite symmetric generating set `S_C` of `C`
with Kazhdan constant `kappa>0`, and choose `h in A\C`.  Assume

```text
q(C)=q(A)                                                (CKO1)
```

for every homomorphism `q:A->Q` to a finite group.  Equivalently, in every
exact action of `A` on a finite set, the permutation groups generated by `C`
and by `A` are equal.

Then the transitive action

```text
A curvearrowright X=A/C                                 (CKO2)
```

has a uniform gap against every exact finite action in the orbit-atlas
sense of Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell.

More precisely, put

```text
F=S_C union {h},
E={C,h^(-1)C}.                                          (CKO3)
```

Let `sigma:A->Sym(V)` be an exact finite action.  Suppose there are a finite
set `B`, a set `V_0 subset V` with

```text
|V_0|>(1-epsilon)|V|,                                   (CKO4)
```

and injections `pi_v:E->B`, one for every `v in V_0`, satisfying the orbit
covariance

```text
pi_(sigma(g)v)(x)=pi_v(g^(-1)x)                         (CKO5)
```

whenever both sides are named (`v,sigma(g)v in V_0`, `g in F`, and
`x,g^(-1)x in E`).  Then

```text
epsilon >= epsilon_0 := kappa^2/(2 kappa^2+8).          (CKO6)
```

Thus no sequence of exact finite `A`-actions can approximate `(CKO2)` with
orbit-atlas error tending to zero.

## Proof

For `v in V_0` define

```text
f(v)=pi_v(C) in B,                                      (CKO7)
```

and extend `f` arbitrarily to all of `V`.  Regard

```text
xi(v)=e_(f(v))                                          (CKO8)
```

as a unit vector in the normalized Hilbert space
`l2(V;l2(B))`.  The finite action acts by translation in the `V` coordinate.

For `c in S_C`, the base coset is fixed by `c^(-1)`.  Therefore `(CKO5)`
gives

```text
f(sigma(c)v)=f(v)                                       (CKO9)
```

unless `v` or `sigma(c)v` lies outside `V_0`.  The exceptional fraction is
at most `2 epsilon`, and two distinct one-hot vectors have squared distance
two.  Hence

```text
||sigma(c)xi-xi||_2^2 <= 4 epsilon.                     (CKO10)
```

Let `Q_C` be the orthogonal projection onto the `C`-invariant vectors.
The Kazhdan inequality gives

```text
||xi-Q_C xi||_2 <= 2 sqrt(epsilon)/kappa.               (CKO11)
```

By `(CKO1)`, `sigma(C)=sigma(A)`, so the `C`-fixed space is the `A`-fixed
space.  In particular `Q_C xi` is fixed by `sigma(h)`.  Consequently

```text
||sigma(h)xi-xi||_2
 <= 2||xi-Q_C xi||_2
 <= 4 sqrt(epsilon)/kappa.                              (CKO12)
```

It follows that

```text
Pr_v[f(sigma(h)v)!=f(v)] <= 8 epsilon/kappa^2.          (CKO13)
```

On the other hand, if `v,sigma(h)v in V_0`, covariance at `g=h,x=C`
and injectivity of `pi_v` give

```text
f(sigma(h)v)
 =pi_v(h^(-1)C)
 !=pi_v(C)
 =f(v).                                                 (CKO14)
```

This holds on a fraction at least `1-2 epsilon` of `V`.  Combining
`(CKO13)--(CKO14)` gives

```text
1-2 epsilon <= 8 epsilon/kappa^2,                       (CKO15)
```

which is `(CKO6)`.

The same proof tolerates an additional Hamming error `delta` in the named
permutations: replace `epsilon` by `epsilon+O_F(delta)`.  Hence an orbit-
atlas approximation of `(CKO2)` cannot be asymptotically corrected, on the
same set or after an `o(|V|)` flexible enlargement, to exact finite actions
of `A`.

## The arithmetic coset is a Bruhat--Tits vertex orbit

For the live pair set

```text
A=SL_3(Z[1/2]),
C=SL_3(Z),
G=SL_3(Q_2),
K=SL_3(Z_2).                                            (CKO16)
```

The dyadic rationals are dense in `Q_2`, elementary matrices generate
`SL_3(Q_2)`, and therefore `A` is dense in `G`.  Since `K` is open, `A`
meets every coset in `G/K`.  Moreover

```text
A cap K=C                                                (CKO17)
```

because a dyadic rational integral at the 2-adic place is an integer.
Thus

```text
A/C  ~=  G/K.                                           (CKO18)
```

The right side is the type-zero/special vertex orbit in the affine
Bruhat--Tits building of `SL_3(Q_2)`.  The element
`h=diag(2,1,1/2)` is a genuine building translation and moves the base
vertex.

This identification explains why ordinary finite quotients do not settle
the action.  Finite quotients of the building (for example arithmetic
quotients by cocompact lattices) retain the local unlabeled Hecke geometry,
but the left `A`-action on `G/K` does not canonically descend through a left
lattice quotient unless that lattice is normalized by `A`.  Conversely,
finite quotient actions of `A` retain the generator labels, but `(CKO1)`
collapses the `C`-orbit and the `A`-orbit to the same finite orbit.  Theorem
`(CKO6)` is the quantitative form of this incompatibility.

## Consequence for the CE falsification gate

Let

```text
W=(direct_sum_(A/C) Z/2) semidirect A.                  (CKO19)
```

If the set action `(CKO2)` is sofic, Gao--Kunnawalkam
Elayavalli--Patchell, Theorems 3.6 and 3.8, imply respectively that `W` is
sofic and that `L(W)` is Connes embeddable.  The base lamp at `C` then gives
the exact relative-commutant countermodel.

The theorem above shows that such a sofic action, if it exists, cannot be
produced by residual finite actions, congruence quotients, or any family of
approximate permutation actions which is flexibly correctable to exact
actions.  It must use a **uniform permutation outlier** of the arithmetic
presentation: the orbit atlas survives while the underlying approximate
`A`-action remains a fixed Hamming distance from every exact finite action.

This is a genuine narrowing, not a decision of the gate.  Infinite
hyperlinear/property-`(T)` groups are known not to be uniformly stable in
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt or Hamming metrics, so generic stability cannot
exclude the required outlier.  The remaining question is whether the
specific building-vertex atlas `(CKO18)` occurs in that unstable sector.

## Claim boundary

Established here:

- the explicit uniform lower bound `(CKO6)` for exact finite actions;
- exclusion of all asymptotically exactifiable orbit atlases;
- the concrete building identification `(CKO18)`;
- the precise location of any producer countermodel in the far permutation-
  instability sector.

Not established here:

- that `A curvearrowright A/C` is or is not a sofic action;
- that `W` is or is not hyperlinear;
- the unitary relative-commutant collapse needed for the non-hyperlinear HNN
  group.

## Literature

Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell, *Soficity for group actions on sets
and applications*, Research in the Mathematical Sciences 12 (2025), Article
48, gives the orbit-atlas definition and the generalized-wreath permanence
theorems.  Their positive coset theorem covers locally finite stabilizers
and they explicitly leave arbitrary actions of sofic groups open; it does
not cover the Kazhdan stabilizer `SL_3(Z)`.

Kun--Thom, *Inapproximability of actions and Kazhdan's property (T)*,
arXiv:1901.03963 (updated 2026), develops the complementary finite-group-of-
clusters mechanism for almost automorphisms of expander approximations.
The one-hot proof above is the exact-action endpoint of that philosophy;
extending it to the non-exact cluster sector is the remaining plausible
attack on this coset action.
