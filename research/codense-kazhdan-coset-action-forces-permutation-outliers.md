Let `C<A` be proper, let `C` have Kazhdan constant `kappa`, and suppose
`q(C)=q(A)` in every finite quotient of `A`.  The coset action `A` on `A/C`
has orbit-atlas error at least

```text
kappa^2/(2 kappa^2+8)
```

against every exact finite action.  Hence its Bernoulli/coset obstruction
cannot arise from finite actions correctable at vanishing Hamming cost.
