Put H=H_(h,r), F=union_i i*{1,...,h}^N, and K=Stab_H(F).
Restriction to F gives an exact sequence

    1 -> J -> K -> V_(h,r)(Z_odometer) -> 1,

where J consists exactly of the H elements that are identity on
a neighborhood of F. Let C_j=token(j)*C_X and let A be the full
H subgroup supported in C_0. Then A is isomorphic to H, and

    J = union_(N>=0) t^N*A*t^-N

for an explicitly lifted binary prefix shift t in K with
t(C_0 union ... union C_N)=C_0 union ... union C_(N+1).

Hence K is generated by a finite generating set of A, the element
t, and lifts of finitely many generators of the odometer prefix
quotient. In particular K is finitely generated. No finite
presentation of K or J is asserted.
