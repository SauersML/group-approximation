In the Bass--Serre tree of `A*_C(C x Z)`, the stabilizers of two adjacent
vertices are `A` and `tAt^(-1)` and their intersection is the edge
stabilizer `C`.  Equivalently, Britton reduction preserves every reduced
word in `A*_C tAt^(-1)`.  Hence the subgroup they generate is their
amalgamated free product `A*_C A`.
