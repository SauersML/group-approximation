The restricted wreath product `Z ≀ Z^2` is isomorphic to no subgroup of Thompson's group
`V`.

Imported from N. Corwin's 2013 PhD thesis at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, which
confirms a conjecture of Bleak and Salazar-Díaz. The result is restated as a theorem in
the Burillo–Cleary–Röver survey (arXiv:1402.3860v2). See route
`wreath-z-by-z2-does-not-embed-in-thompson-v-citation`.

**Consequence for the reposed Problem 2.15.** `Z ≀ Z^2` is bi-orderable, metabelian and
not a subgroup of `F`, but it is not a witness for
`biorderable-subgroup-of-v-without-free-subgroups-not-in-f`, because it is not a subgroup
of `V`. This claim refutes `wreath-z-by-z2-embeds-in-thompson-v`, and that kills route
`biorderable-v-subgroup-not-in-f-via-wreath-z-by-z2`.
