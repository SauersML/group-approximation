Let `G` be a discrete word-hyperbolic group. For every finite group `F`,
the regular wreath product

```text
G wr F = G^F semidirectProduct F
```

satisfies the Baum--Connes conjecture with coefficients. Equivalently,
`G` satisfies the Baum--Connes conjecture with coefficients with finite
wreath products.

Torsion in `G` is allowed. The finite-wreath conclusion is strictly
stronger as a formulation than Baum--Connes with coefficients for `G`
alone; no general finite-extension permanence theorem makes it redundant.
