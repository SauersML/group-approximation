In the relative free product, every term of `P_x` has reduced form
`Gamma_0 x Gamma_0 x^-1 Gamma_0`, and similarly for `P_y`.  Multiplication
by `A` or `B` changes only the adjacent base syllables.  Thus the linear,
linear, bilinear, and defect terms occupy distinct relative components and
cannot cancel.  A solution must identify some of these components through a
relation not present in the relative free product.
