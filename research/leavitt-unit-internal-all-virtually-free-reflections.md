Let `U=L_(F_2)(1,2)^x`.  For every countable virtually free group `Q`, there
is a prefix-table embedding `Q<=U` and a nonzero Leavitt corner `p` such that

```text
q p q^(-1),                     q in Q,                  (AVF1)
```

are pairwise orthogonal.  Hence `U` contains the internal restricted wreath
product

```text
W_Q=U^(Q) semidirectProduct Q=U wr Q<=U.                (AVF2)
```

Its complete MF reflection is `Q`:

```text
Rad_MF(W_Q)=U^(Q),                  R_MF(W_Q)~=Q,        (AVF3)
Hom(Q,M)~=Hom(W_Q,M)                                      (AVF4)
```

naturally for every MF group `M`.  The radical in `(AVF3)` is normally
generated by one fixed involution in the identity coordinate.  The same Hom
factorization holds for finite groups, residually finite groups, compact
Hausdorff groups, and finite-dimensional linear groups over every field.

For every normal subgroup `N` of `W_Q`,

```text
cl_MF^(W_Q)(N)=pi_Q^(-1)(cl_MF^Q(pi_Q(N))),             (AVF5)

W_Q/N is MF
 iff U^(Q)<=N and Q/pi_Q(N) is MF.                      (AVF6)
```

Thus the MF-closed normal-subgroup lattices of `W_Q` and `Q` are canonically
isomorphic.  If `Q` is finitely generated, then `W_Q` is finitely generated.
Nonisomorphic actors give nonisomorphic internal groups because universal MF
reflection is an isomorphism invariant.

This has an exact dynamical maximality statement.  For a finitely generated
group `Q`, the following are equivalent:

1. `Q` is virtually free;
2. `Q` has a prefix-table embedding in Thompson's group `V<=U` with a
   nonempty clopen set whose distinct translates are pairwise disjoint;
3. the internal construction `(AVF2)` is obtained from a wandering
   prefix-table corner;
4. the word problem of `Q` is context-free.

Consequently the finitely generated virtually free groups are precisely the
MF reflections realized by the prefix-dynamical form of the internal
wandering-corner compiler.  Equivalently, these internal MF reflections are
classified exactly by the context-free group languages.
