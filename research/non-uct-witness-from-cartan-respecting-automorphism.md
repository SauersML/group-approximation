**Dead.**  The natural finite-order automorphisms of `O_2` that this repository
can write down combinatorially are gauge automorphisms `λ_u` with `u` in
Thompson's group `V` (prefix-code permutations, as in the Leavitt region) and
flips of `O_2 ⊗ O_2 ≅ O_2`.  One could hope some order-`p` element gives a
non-UCT `O_2 ⋊ Z_p`, the Kirchberg test case.

Invalidated by `cartan-respecting-o2-automorphisms-give-uct-crossed-products`:
both families leave a Cartan subalgebra invariant, so the crossed products
satisfy the UCT by Barlak--Li Proposition 1.6.
