title: Global packet covariance neither switches off at HALT nor controls a compressed leakage source
---

An unconditional conjugacy relator controls a full context tuple but, after
compression to the analytic leakage source, leaves exactly the unknown
commutator of that source with the shared BCS word.

Requiring global packet extension can also fail exact HALT completeness.
The required cell is therefore the capacity-gated mixed Hecke covariance,
not ordinary global conjugacy.
