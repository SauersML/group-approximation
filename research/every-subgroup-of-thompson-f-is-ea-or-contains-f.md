Every subgroup `H` of Richard Thompson's group `F` is elementary amenable, or has a
subgroup isomorphic to `F`. This is the affirmative answer to Zaremsky Problem 2.3
(`zaremsky-2-03-brin-sapir-conjecture`).

By closure of elementary amenable groups under directed unions, it is enough to
prove it for finitely generated `H`.

## Attempts

- **Transfinite induction on a bounded EA-class (dead as a uniform bound).** Brin
  (arXiv:math/0501085, Question 2) asked whether `ω²+1` is the largest elementary
  class of an elementary amenable subgroup of `PL_o(I)`. Bleak–Brin–Moore
  (arXiv:1711.10998, Theorem 1) realize EA-class `α+2` for every `α < ε_0` among
  finitely generated subgroups of `F`. Any induction must therefore run over
  ordinals at least `ε_0`; no fixed finite-depth or `ω²`-depth structure theorem
  can prove the conjecture.
- **Germ-kernel recursion (2026-09-13, z2-03-brin-sapir, in progress).** Reduce to
  the compactly supported kernels of the slope-germ maps at the ends of each
  orbital, where Brin's 1999 criterion forces the two germ kernels to coincide for
  `F`-free groups, and try to show that `F`-freeness makes the recursion well
  founded. Where it stands: the reduction and the balanced-germ property are
  elementary; the well-foundedness is the open crux.
- **Fast families.** Bleak–Brin–Kassabov–Moore–Zaremsky (arXiv:1701.08321) decide
  the isomorphism type of groups generated by fast families from finite dynamical
  data. Extending that to all finitely generated subgroups of `F` would need a
  normal form for arbitrary generating sets, which is not available.
