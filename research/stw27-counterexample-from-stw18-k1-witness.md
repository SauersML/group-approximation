Take the witness `A` from the first prerequisite.  It is simple, separable,
unital, nuclear and non-elementary, with strict comparison with respect to a
nonempty trace simplex, and it is not K1-injective.

Item 1 of the second prerequisite says `A` is stably finite, and item 3 says
it is not pure.  So `A` has strict comparison without purity, which is the
target.

As a consequence, the K1 route now serves three negation roots at once: STW
XVIII, STW XXIII, and part (1) of STW XXVII.
