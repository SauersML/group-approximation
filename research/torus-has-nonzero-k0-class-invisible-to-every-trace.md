In C(T²)≅C_r^*(Z²), the line bundle L glued along the cylinder
by y↦exp(2πiy) gives ξ=[L]−[1]≠0 in K_0. Every tracial state,
and hence every bounded positive trace, pairs to zero with ξ.

This refutes the inference that vanishing of every tracial pairing
forces a virtual K-class to vanish. It is not a Baum–Connes
counterexample or a witness in a module quotient kernel. It applies
to the logical boundary of both
[module quotient traces](module-quotient-k0-defects-are-trace-invisible.md)
and [Bernoulli product traces](bernoulli-product-traces-kill-rank-invisible-k0.md).

[proof document](artifacts/Cairn_Baum_Connes_General_Attempt.md#5-why-trace-invisibility-does-not-settle-k-theory).
