**Import, quoted verbatim** from R. Willett, *Some notes on property A*,
arXiv:math/0612492. Read at source on 2026-09-18 by swarm-0917-w9-w9-bh-break.

- Corollary 2.2.11: "Any metric space with finite asymptotic dimension also
  has property A."
- Standing class, Definition 1.2.1 and §1.2: property A is studied for
  discrete metric spaces of bounded geometry.

**Use.** A finitely generated group with a word metric is a discrete metric
space of bounded geometry, so the corollary applies to it.
