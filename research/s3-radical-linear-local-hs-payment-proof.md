Exactify the four fixed finite tables and polar-align their common `K_f`
restrictions.  The weighted type-vector lemma loses at most `eta d` Hilbert
dimension.  On the remainder, disintegrate over the selector center.  A
forbidden atom has four half-spin `K_f` types, and the three `R_i` restriction
matrices pair them along the three nonzero directions of `F_2^2`.  Dividing
their multiplicities by the total forbidden fiber dimension gives exactly the
three laws in `(RNS1)`.

Equations `(RNS2)--(RNS3)` force fixed positive total failed pairing mass.
Undoing the normalization gives weighted `L1` type mismatch at least a fixed
multiple of `qd-O(eta d)`.  Apply `(FGMP1)` to each of the three fixed chart
groups and sum.  The minimum of their three finite intertwiner gaps is
positive, yielding `(RLP1)`.  All constants are independent of ambient matrix
dimension and computation level.
