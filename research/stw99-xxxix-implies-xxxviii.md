Cocycle conjugate actions have isomorphic (reduced) crossed products,
and for the trivial second tensor factor
`(A ⊗ Z) ⋊_{α⊗1} G ≅ (A ⋊_α G) ⊗ Z`.  So under Szabo's conjecture
every action as in Problem XXXVIII has
`A ⋊ G ≅ (A ⋊ G) ⊗ Z`.  Outerness and stable finiteness are not even
needed for this direction.
