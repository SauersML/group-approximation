ESTABLISHED.  Let `A_1,...,A_r in M_n(Z)` have nonzero determinant and form
the multiple ascending-HNN presentation

```text
G_A=<Z^n,t_1,...,t_r |
      t_i v t_i^(-1)=A_i v       (v in Z^n, 1<=i<=r)>.  (AMH1)
```

For every nonzero `y in Z^n`, there is a finite quotient of `G_A` in which
`y` remains nonzero.

Choose a prime `p` which divides neither any `det(A_i)` nor every coordinate
of `y`.  Then each reduction

```text
bar A_i in GL_n(F_p)                                    (AMH2)
```

is invertible and `bar y!=0`.  Let `L` be the finite subgroup of
`GL_n(F_p)` generated by the `bar A_i`.  The assignments

```text
v |-> bar v in F_p^n,          t_i |-> bar A_i          (AMH3)
```

define a homomorphism

```text
G_A -> F_p^n rtimes L.                                  (AMH4)
```

Every relation in `(AMH1)` holds by the defining affine action, the target
is finite, and `(AMH3)` sends `y` to `bar y!=0`.

Consequently no finite family of proper linear endomorphisms of a
finite-rank abelian packet can supply the non-fusion marked-type recurrence
left by `kazhdan-type-blocks-pack-a-square-function`.  However expanding its
integer dynamics looks, one common finite congruence turns every branch into
an invertible permutation and gives an exact zero-defect marked reservoir.
This includes multiple binary/dyadic branches and remains true when the
stable letters themselves generate a nonamenable group.

The proper-endomorphism escape from
`rf-packets-have-finite-marked-automorphism-orbits` must therefore be
genuinely nonabelian or infinite-rank, and it must prevent all branches from
descending simultaneously to any finite quotient which detects the mark.
Otherwise the proposed first-hit type layers are again finite periodic
orbits rather than an extensive seam.
