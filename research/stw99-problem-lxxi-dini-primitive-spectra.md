**Problem LXXI of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(Kirchberg--Rordam GAFA Question 1.1).  Is every Dini topological
space the primitive ideal space of (1) a separable nuclear
C\*-algebra, (2) a separable C\*-algebra?

## Attempts

* Dini-ness (every point a countable intersection of opens, plus the
  sober `T_0` locally-compact-ish axioms KR isolate) is necessary for
  separable algebras; KR realized large classes (spaces built from
  their `O_2`-absorbing bundle machinery).  Two structural remarks
  recorded: the nuclear and general cases (1),(2) are equivalent iff
  primitive-spectrum realization can always be pushed through an
  exact-to-nuclear replacement — and `O_2 ⊗ K`-fibred realizations
  make (1) plausible without touching classification: the obstruction
  candidates all live in the interplay of infinite descending chains
  of primes with second countability (the known non-examples of
  non-Dini spaces fail exactly countable intersection).  This node is
  recorded as a wiring anchor for LXVII/LXIX's wild-ideal-lattice
  frontier; no new attack is claimed.
