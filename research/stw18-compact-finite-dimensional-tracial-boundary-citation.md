Toms--White--Winter, *Z-stability and finite dimensional tracial
boundaries*, International Mathematics Research Notices 2015, no. 10,
2702--2727, prove precisely the following implication for a simple
separable unital nuclear non-elementary C-star algebra whose tracial state
space has nonempty compact extreme boundary of finite covering dimension:

```text
strict comparison  implies  Z-stability.
```

Their theorem constructs uniformly tracially large order-zero maps from
matrix algebras into the central sequence algebra and applies the
Matui--Sato absorption criterion.  The adjectives and boundary condition
are exactly those in the target claim, so the published theorem establishes
it directly.
