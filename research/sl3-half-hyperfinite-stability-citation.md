Peterson--Thom, arXiv:1303.4007, supplies the character classification for
`SL_3(Z[1/2])`.  The regular character is nonamenable and the remaining
extremal characters are finite-dimensional; finite direct sums approximate
their barycenters.  Dogon--Vigdorovich's general amenable-character
criterion, imported by the required node, is exactly the conclusion.
