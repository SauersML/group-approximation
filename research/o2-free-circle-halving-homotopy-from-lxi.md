`U = O_2 *_C C(T)` is unital and properly infinite, so LXI makes it K1-injective. In `K_1(U)`,
`[s_1 z s_1^* + s_2s_2^*] = [Θ(diag(z,1))] = [z]`, where `Θ` is implemented by the unitary row `(s_1,s_2)`.
So `z^*(s_1 z s_1^* + s_2 s_2^*)` is K1-trivial, hence in `U_0(U)`, and `z ~_h s_1 z s_1^* + s_2s_2^*`.
