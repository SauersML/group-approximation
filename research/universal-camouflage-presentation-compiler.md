Fix a group `B` with full MF radical, normally generated by `d`, and put
`D=<d>`.  For a nonempty finite or countable set `X`, let `F_X` be the free
group on `X` and define the single source

```text
U_X=C_d(F_X)=B *_D (F_X x D).                         (UPC1)
```

Let `R normal F_X`, put `Q=F_X/R`, and let `s:F_X->U_X` be the canonical
section.  Then

```text
U_X / normalClosure_(U_X)(s(R)) ~= C_d(Q),             (UPC2)
```

and imposing the one additional fixed relation `d=1` gives

```text
U_X / normalClosure(s(R),d) ~= Q.                      (UPC3)
```

If `Q` is MF, the intermediate quotient in `(UPC2)` is non-MF and has

```text
Rad_MF(C_d(Q))=normalClosure(d),
Hom(C_d(Q),M) ~= Hom(Q,M)                              (UPC4)
```

naturally for every MF group `M`.  Thus the same word `d` is the final MF
switch for every MF presentation on `X`.

The assignment

```text
R |-> normalClosure_(U_X)(s(R))                        (UPC5)
```

is an order embedding of the complete lattice of normal subgroups of `F_X`
into the normal-subgroup lattice of `U_X`, preserves arbitrary joins, and
has

```text
normalClosure(s(R)) intersect s(F_X)=s(R).             (UPC6)
```

The quotient map in `(UPC2)` has an exact split kernel.  If `R!=1`, then

```text
ker(U_X->C_d(Q)) ~= F_infinity semidirectProduct R.     (UPC7)
```

Here the `F_infinity` is the kernel of the noninjective fold from the star
indexed by `F_X` to the star indexed by `Q`, and the displayed copy of `R`
comes from the visible free-group section.

Consequently:

1. for every positive integer `n`, one fixed finitely generated non-MF
   group `U_n` compiles every `n`-generated MF group;
2. if `B` is finitely presented, one fixed finitely presented `U_n`
   compiles every finitely presented `n`-generated MF group by adding its
   finite list of relators, and every compiled group is finitely presented;
3. one fixed countable group `U_infinity` compiles every countable MF group;
4. the quotient interval above `normalClosure(d)` is canonically the normal-
   subgroup lattice of `F_X`, and its MF quotients are exactly the MF groups
   generated by `X`.
