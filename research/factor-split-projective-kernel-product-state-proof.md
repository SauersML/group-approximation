For each `(a,b)` in the scalar projective kernel, move the right multiplier
across the scalar equation.  It then lies in `L(M) intersect R(M)=C`, forcing
both player components to be scalar.  The product of the two restricted
source states satisfies every kernel character equation.  Approximate each
one-player state by finite-dimensional vector states and tensor them; this
puts a state of the face in the finite-dimensional weak-star closure.
