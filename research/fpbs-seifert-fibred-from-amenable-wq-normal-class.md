For a closed Seifert-fibred `M` with hyperbolic base orbifold `B`, the regular
fibre generates an infinite cyclic normal subgroup `Z <= pi_1(M)` with quotient
`pi_1^orb(B)`, a non-elementary Fuchsian group. So `pi_1(M)` is nonamenable and
finitely generated, and `Z` is infinite, amenable and normal, hence wq-normal.
The premise then applies to every Cayley graph of `pi_1(M)`.
