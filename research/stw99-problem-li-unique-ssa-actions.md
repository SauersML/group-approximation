**Problem LI of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(Izumi ICM Conjecture 2; Szabo CMP19 Conjecture A).  For `D` strongly
self-absorbing and `G` countable discrete torsion-free amenable, are
all strongly outer actions `G ↷ D` cocycle conjugate?  Torsion is
genuinely obstructed (Izumi, Duke Thm 4.8).

Known: `D` Kirchberg — YES (Gabe--Szabo dynamical Kirchberg--Phillips,
even for amenable actions of exact Haagerup groups); stably finite
side: Kishimoto (`Z` on UHF) through Szabo CMP19 Thm C (the class of
torsion-free elementary amenable groups built from `1` by directed
unions and extensions by `Z`), no UCT needed.

## Attempts

* The gap on the stably finite side is exactly the gap between
  Szabo's induction class and all torsion-free amenable groups — the
  same elementary-amenable-vs-amenable frontier as Problem XXXII's
  known-groups list; the first open test group is again intermediate
  growth (Grigorchuk), where no Rokhlin-dimension or extension
  induction applies.
* A stably finite dynamical Kirchberg--Phillips theorem
  (`stw99-problem-liii-stably-finite-dynamical-kp`) would reduce LI
  to showing the equivariant invariant of a strongly outer action of
  torsion-free amenable `G` on `D` is unique — for `D = Z` this is an
  equivariant-KK collapse computation (`KK^G(Z ⊗ −)` via Baum--Connes
  with the Higson--Kasparov γ-element for a-T-menable `G`), plausible
  machinery already in Szabo's January 2026 equivariant KK-uniqueness
  toolkit; recorded as the expected post-LIII path.
