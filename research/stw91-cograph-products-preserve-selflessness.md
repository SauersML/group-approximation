Let `Gamma` be a nonempty countable simple graph with no induced path on four vertices,
and let `(G_v)_(v in V(Gamma))` be countable discrete groups.  Assume that
every

```text
(C*_r(G_v),tau_v)
```

is exact and selfless.  Then the reduced group C-star algebra of the graph
product

```text
G_Gamma = graph_product_(Gamma) G_v
```

is exact and selfless.  In particular `G_Gamma` is C-star-simple.

For a finite graph this covers precisely the cograph recursion generated by
the two operations

```text
disjoint union  <->  group free product,
graph join      <->  group direct product.
```

For countable `Gamma`, no global finite decomposition tree is required: the
hypothesis is equivalent to every finite induced subgraph being a cograph,
and the full graph product is the directed union of those finite stages.
