**Sources.**
- V. O. Ferreira, J. Z. Gonçalves, J. Sánchez, *Free symmetric group algebras in division rings generated by
  poly-orderable groups*, arXiv:1206.7013v1 (29 Jun 2012). The PDF was downloaded from arXiv on 2026-09-17 and
  its text was extracted and read.
- S. P. Fisher, P. Sánchez-Peralta, *Division rings for group algebras of virtually compact special groups and
  3-manifold groups*, arXiv:2303.08165v4. The PDF was downloaded from arXiv on 2026-09-17 and its text was
  extracted and read. It is the same source as [[fsp-graph-of-rings-and-linnell-extension-citation]].
- The original extension result is I. Hughes, *Division rings of fractions for group rings II*, Comm. Pure
  Appl. Math. 25 (1972), 127–131.

**Definition and item 2**, arXiv:1206.7013, §2, verbatim: "We recall that a locally indicable group Γ is said to
be Hughes-free embeddable if every crossed product F ∗ Γ of Γ over a division ring F has a Hughes-free division
ring of fractions. For example, torsion-free nilpotent groups, or more generally orderable groups, are Hughes-free
embeddable. It was proved in [7] that extensions of Hughes-free embeddable groups are Hughes-free embeddable."
Here [7] is Hughes 1972.

**Item 1**, arXiv:1206.7013, Lemma 3.2, verbatim: "Let G be a locally indicable group with a normal subgroup N
such that G/N is locally indicable. Let K be a division ring and let K ∗ G be a crossed product. If K ∗ N has a
Hughes-free division ring of fractions D and G/N is Hughes-free embeddable, then K ∗ G has a Hughes-free division
ring of fractions. More precisely, regarding K ∗ G = (K ∗ N) ∗ (G/N) ↪ D ∗ (G/N), if E is a Hughes-free division
ring of fractions of D ∗ (G/N), then E is a Hughes-free division ring of fractions of K ∗ G." The paper says it is
extracted from Hughes 1972 and from Sánchez's thesis (UAB, 2008).

**Item 3**, arXiv:2303.08165v4, Lemma 7.6, verbatim: "Let G be a locally indicable group which splits as a graph
of groups G_Γ = (G_v, G_e; Γ). If D_{k∗G} exists, then U(DG_Γ) ≅ D_{k∗G} as kG-rings, where DG_Γ is the graph of
division rings (D_{kG_v}, D_{kG_e}; Γ)." The proof compares the Sylvester rank functions of the two fields:
universality of `U(DG_Γ)` gives one inequality, and maximality of the Hughes-free rank function (their
Proposition 7.4) gives the other. The same page says that a positive answer to Question 3.13 "is equivalent to the
existence of a Hughes-free division ring".

**Item 4**, arXiv:2303.08165v4, Theorem 7.8, verbatim: "Let G be a torsion-free one-relator group and let k be a
division ring such that kG embeds into a Hughes-free division ring D_{kG}. Then k_G ≅ D_{kG} as kG-division
rings." Here `k_G` is the Lewin–Lewin ring (Construction 7.9). Remark 7.10 adds that the argument extends to
crossed products.

**Item 5**, arXiv:2303.08165v4, Corollary 7.11, the paragraph after Definition 7.12, and Question 7.13. The
paragraph reads: "By the previous remarks, it is settled in the affirmative in characteristic 0, and only the
primitivity rank 2 case remains in characteristic p > 0. Question 7.13. Is the Lewin–Lewin construction always
Hughes-free?"
