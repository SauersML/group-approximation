Source: Ulrich Bunke, Daniel Kasprowski, and Christoph Winges,
*On the Farrell--Jones conjecture for localising invariants*,
Advances in Mathematics 489 (2026), 110788,
doi:10.1016/j.aim.2026.110788, Theorem 1.2 and Example 1.9.

Theorem 1.2 states exactly that for every `E1`-ring spectrum `R`, the
displayed virtually-cyclic assembly map is an equivalence when `G` is
hyperbolic, virtually solvable, a subgroup of `GL_n(Q)`, or acts
properly and cocompactly by isometries on a finite-dimensional CAT(0)
space.

Example 1.9 identifies the sphere-spectrum instance with the
nonconnective A-theory functor, so the A-theory specialization in the
claim is part of the theorem's formal setup, not an analogy.

The paper explicitly says that its methods are insufficient for the
L-theoretic version. The claim therefore imports no L-theory, no
arbitrary left-exact coefficient-category statement, and no finite-wreath
upgrade beyond Theorem 1.2. QED
