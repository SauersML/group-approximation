title: For minimal etale groupoids with compact unit space, a faithful trace, stable finiteness, the coboundary condition and complete non-paradoxicality of the reduced algebra coincide
---

Let `G` be an étale groupoid with compact unit space. Consider:

1. `C*_r(G)` admits a faithful tracial state.
2. `C*_r(G)` is stably finite.
3. `G` satisfies the coboundary condition.
4. `G` is completely non-paradoxical.

Always 1 ⇒ 2 ⇒ 3 ⇒ 4. If `G` is minimal, all four are equivalent.
