In a degree-three length-eleven cyclic cover with orbit valences `(1,5,5)`,
the two nonpivot relators have a common signed-corner pattern

```text
(-,+,-,+,+).
```

Let the unary pivot be `H z=1`, so `z=H^(-1)`.  For either residual orbit,
the five coefficient corners

```text
(H^(-1), H X, H^(-1) A, H Y, H B)
```

reduce the post-pivot relator exactly to `X A Y B`.  Using coefficient arcs
`A,B` in the first orbit and `C,D` in the second gives the minimal HNN
holonomy

```text
Y^(-1)(A^(-1)C)Y = B D^(-1).
```

Consequently choosing `A^(-1)C=Omega(a,r)=1` and
`B D^(-1)=Omega(c,p)=h!=1` kills `h`.  Thus `(1,5,5)`, not `(1,1,9)`, is the
first length-eleven topology which passes the exact abstract post-pivot test.
