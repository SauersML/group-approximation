For each fixed group Gamma, the following assertions are equivalent:

1. The group algebra F_2[Gamma] is directly finite.
2. Every injective scalar Boolean cellular automaton
   F(x)=x+A(xR_t x) on Gamma x C_3 is surjective, where t generates
   the central C_3 and A is any scalar F_2-linear cellular automaton.

The sufficiency direction uses the scalar direct-finiteness assumption
only. It does not assume stable direct finiteness. The norm subclass
proves necessity through an explicit reversible conjugacy to arbitrary
scalar linear dynamics on Gamma.

This equivalence leaves both assertions unresolved for arbitrary Gamma.
It does not prove Gottschalk's conjecture or Kaplansky's conjecture.
The arbitrary-feedback assertion here concerns C_3; the companion
odd-cycle conjugacy concerns the specified norm subclass.
