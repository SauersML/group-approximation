**ESTABLISHED CROSS-BRANCH RETURN FENCE.**  Continue the common-opposite
aggregate Morita table with the literal constant Whitehead

```text
H=w_(9,10)(1).                                           (PMC1)
```

Thus `H` swaps the two private middle coordinates `9,10`, fixes the parent
coordinates `7,8`, and commutes with the aggregate parents

```text
N=x_78(1),                 D=x_87(1).                    (PMC2)
```

Conjugating the literal positive and returned arms and then taking the
opposite branch gives

```text
P^+=[HS_0H^(-1),T_1]=x_78(s_0t_1),
R^+=[HS_1H^(-1),T_0]=x_78(s_1t_0),
P^-=[HU_0H^(-1),V_1]=x_87(s_0t_1),
R^-=[HU_1H^(-1),V_0]=x_87(s_1t_0).                     (PMC3)
```

These are genuine cross-branch occurrences made from the same named arms
as the aggregate fold and its common reverse return.  In particular, they
do not lie in either private `GL_3(F_2)` factor of the old model.  With

```text
p=s_0t_1,        r=s_1t_0,        pr=q_0,       rp=q_1,
```

the two cross partial Whiteheads are

```text
K_0=P^+R^-P^+=W_78(p,r),
K_1=R^+P^-R^+=W_78(r,p).                               (PMC4)
```

They satisfy

```text
K_0^2=K_1^2=1,       [K_0,K_1]=1,
K_0K_1=w_78(p+r)=:T,
TNT^(-1)=D,          TDT^(-1)=N.                       (PMC5)
```

Thus the private-middle swap really destroys the block-diagonal
`GL_3(F_2) times GL_3(F_2)` factorization: its commutators are off-diagonal
branch arrows, and their product exchanges the positive and returned
aggregate parents.

The stronger table still has an exact finite marked model.  On the six
vertices

```text
(7_0,9,8_0,7_1,10,8_1),                               (PMC6)
```

write `e_ab=I+E_ab` in `GL_6(F_2)`.  Put the branch-zero packet on the
triangle `(7_0,9,8_0)`, the branch-one packet on
`(7_1,10,8_1)`, exactly as in the two old `GL_3` factors, and let `H` be the
permutation matrix interchanging only `9` and `10`.  The four commutators
in `(PMC3)` are then

```text
P^+=e_(7_0,8_1),      R^+=e_(7_1,8_0),
P^-=e_(8_0,7_1),      R^-=e_(8_1,7_0).                 (PMC7)
```

Consequently `K_0` swaps `7_0,8_1`, `K_1` swaps `7_1,8_0`, and their
product exchanges `N` with `D`.  Every original aggregate-return row,
every equation `(PMC1)--(PMC5)`, and the matching and crossed child rows
hold exactly, while

```text
C_1=e_(7_1,8_1) != 1.                                  (PMC8)
```

The left regular representation of the finite subgroup of `GL_6(F_2)`
generated by these matrices is therefore a zero-defect marked model.  The
private-middle swap is the first literal continuation that mixes the two
returned packets rather than merely adding another common parent, but it
only fuses the two private `GL_3` charts into one finite six-coordinate
chart.  A live continuation must leave this finite cross-return closure;
reusing `H`, its four cross commutators, or their partial Whitehead products
cannot establish Atomic Morita Return.

This is a scoped occurrence-table model, not a finite representation of the
full binary Leavitt Steinberg group.  No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input,
stability theorem, trace hypothesis, literature theorem, or local
computation is used.
