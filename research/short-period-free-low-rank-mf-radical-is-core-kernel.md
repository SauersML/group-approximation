Over `F_2`, if `T^j` has no fixed points for `1<=j<k` and `nk>=4`,
the MF radical of `GL_n(R_X)` is exactly its locally finite kernel
of restriction to the cycle core. Its maximal MF quotient is the LEF
restriction image. For `n>=2`, the same holds for `EL_n(R_X)`, with
quotient exactly `EL_n(R_Y)`. In particular this covers rank two without
fixed points and units without periods one, two or three.
