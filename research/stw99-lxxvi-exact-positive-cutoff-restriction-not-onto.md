There exist positive elements b=(c-1/2)_+ and a=(c-1/4)_+ in the
CAR algebra A, with [b]<=[a]<[1_A], such that restriction of actual
right Hilbert module embeddings

```text
Emb(closure(aA), A) -> Emb(closure(bA), A)
```

is not onto. Both cutoff spectra accumulate at zero. The same example
works in A tensor K with the target fixed to the projection module
1_A(A tensor K).

The coefficient is simple, stably finite, real rank zero, stable
rank one, and Z-stable; every nonzero hereditary subalgebra of its
stabilization is K-stable. Thus hereditary K-stability and strict
comparison against the target do not imply exact restriction lifting
along arbitrary positive cutoffs.

This does not refute density, approximate lifting, weak contractibility
of a single embedding space, or the established RR0 selection theorem
using a chosen tower of projection submodules. It does not provide a
counterexample to STW LXXVI. The explicit proof is internally reviewed.
