The simple-order classification proves there is no ambient finite simple
group containing `A8` at an index from 507 through 989.  At equality 990 it
forces `S=A11`, and every chart subgroup is `Alt(X)` on an eight-element
support in the natural eleven-point action.

The natural-support theorem is label-independent.  Its packet argument
forces the two eight-point supports to coincide.  The chart subgroups are
then equal, and its classical-collapse step shows collision 19243 is
nonidentity for every remaining relative marking.  Thus no index-990 pair
can satisfy the augmented relations or generate `A11`, proving `(OSI991)`.
