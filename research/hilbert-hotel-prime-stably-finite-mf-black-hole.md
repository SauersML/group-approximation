Let

```text
H=EL_16(L_(F_2)(1,2)),    A=C*_r(H).
```

Then `A` is separable, unital, infinite-dimensional, stably finite, prime,
and primitive.  At the same time every star homomorphism from `A` to an MF
C-star algebra is zero.  Consequently:

1. every nonzero closed two-sided ideal of `A` is essential;
2. every nonzero quotient of `A` is non-MF;
3. every nonzero quotient of `A` is non-quasidiagonal;
4. `A` has a faithful irreducible representation, but no nonzero
   finite-dimensional representation and no nonzero MF representation.

Thus primitivity and stable finiteness coexist with total failure of every
matricial quotient model, not merely with failure of one faithful MF
embedding.
