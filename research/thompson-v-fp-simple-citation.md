**Established by citation.**  Cannon--Floyd--Parry, *Introductory notes on
Richard Thompson's groups*, Enseign. Math. 42 (1996), record Thompson's
finite presentation of `V` and its simplicity.  `V` contains every finite
symmetric group, so it is infinite.  The same citation already underlies
`thompson-v-not-lef`.
