Samuel J. Harris, *Synchronous games with *-isomorphic game algebras*,
Quantum Information and Computation 22 (2022), Theorem 3.3, constructs the
`nk`-input, `nk`-output bisynchronous game and proves the game-algebra
*-isomorphism.  Corollary 3.7 applies it to a synchronous `qc`/`qa`
separation.  Because the algebra is preserved rather than merely its value,
nonzero representations into a finite von Neumann algebra or `R^omega`
transport in both directions.
