**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `R` be a unital ring and `p = p^2 in R`.  Give the
corner `B = pRp` its own unit `p`.  For every `n >= 1` the map

```text
iota_p : GL_n(B) --> GL_n(R),      U |--> U + (1-p)I_n            (CU1)
```

is an injective group homomorphism, and

```text
iota_p(p I_n + a E_ij) = I_n + a E_ij        (a in B, i != j).    (CU2)
```

Consequently `iota_p` restricts to an injective homomorphism

```text
EL_n(B) --> EL_n(R)                                              (CU3)
```

whose image is generated by the ambient elementary matrices with entries
in `B`.  In particular `e_12(p)` is the `iota_p`-image of the unit-entry
elementary matrix of `EL_n(B)`, and `e_12(p) != 1` whenever `p != 0`.

No fullness, no proper infiniteness and no exhaustion `p R p = R` is
assumed, and `p` need not be central.
