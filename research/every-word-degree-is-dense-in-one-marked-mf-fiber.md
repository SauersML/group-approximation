(0,1) minus the rotation cuts. Every word-problem degree is dense relative
to its image. Thus every finite relation neighborhood realized by the
family meets every degree stratum, with unchanged marked MF quotient
and unchanged MF consequence operator. This is not density in all
Kazhdan groups or injectivity of unmarked isomorphism types.
