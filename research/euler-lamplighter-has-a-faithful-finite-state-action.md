**OPEN (2026-09-18, lane gq-k2-q).** For some prime `l`, the group `G_l` of
`z-localized-embeds-in-fg-rf-soluble-group` has a faithful action on some regular rooted tree `T_d` in which every
element is finite-state.

## Why it matters

- **Equivalent form.** A faithful finite-state action is the same as an embedding of `G_l` in an *automaton group*, a
  group generated by a finite set of tree automorphisms closed under taking sections.
  - One direction: the finitely many states of the images of `f_0, x, β` generate such a group.
  - The other: every element of an automaton group is finite-state.
  - "Finitely generated self-similar" is not enough, because elements of such groups need not be finite-state
    (gq-referee-a, H1).
  - Automaton groups act faithfully on a rooted tree, so they are residually finite.
- **Payoff.** If that automaton group can be chosen finitely presented, `z-localized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group`
  follows. Finite presentation of the state closure is a further, separate question.

## What is known

- **Faithful tree actions exist** (`euler-lamplighter-acts-faithfully-on-the-l-ary-tree`): on `Z_l[[T]]`, read as
  `l`-adic measures on `Z_l`, `β` acts as multiplication by the function `1 + lx`.
- **In that action `β` is probably not finite-state.** This is a heuristic, not a proof, and it concerns the
  natural coordinates only, not every action. As corrected by gq-referee-a (H3):
  - *Cylinder-value coordinates.* On `a + l^m Z_l`, `1 + lx = (1 + la)(1 + l^(m+1)x'/(1 + la))`. So `β` multiplies
    the digit stream of the coordinate `(a, m)` by the integer `1 + la`. Infinitely many such streams are
    interleaved, and the pending carries are unbounded. Cylinders are coordinates, not vertices of the tree, so
    this does not by itself count sections.
  - *Monomial digits.* The graded action `e_a ↦ e_a + (n−a) e_(a+1)` is periodic mod `l` in `(n, a)`. Any
    obstruction there sits in the carries of the integer factors `b` in `D(l^a T^b)`, not in the graded part. A
    proof, or a finite-state recoding, has to act on those carries.
- **State counts are not an obstruction by themselves.**
  - Let `q` have no prime factor `<= d`, and let `l ∤ q`, so that `f_0/q ∈ M`. Then `g ↦ g^q` is bijective on every level quotient of `Aut(T_d)`, whose orders have only prime
    factors `<= d`. So in any finite-state action on `T_d` the `q`-th root of `f_0` is the unique one in `Aut(T_d)`.
    For example `d = 3`, `q = 4` fails the hypothesis (gq-referee-a, H2).
  - For odometer-type actions such roots have about `q` states. A finitely generated finite-state group does contain
    elements of unbounded state count, so this gives no contradiction.
- **Literature.** arXiv:2405.16678 shows that `A ≀ G` is a subgroup of an automata group whenever `G` is and `A` is a
  finitely generated abelian group. It covers `Z ≀ Z ≅ H`, but not `G_l`. The base `M = Z_(l)[Z]` is not finitely
  generated, and `β` lies outside any restricted wreath product over `Z`.

## Attempts

1. **Natural coordinates (gq-k2-q, 2026-09-18).** Two coordinatizations were tried: monomial digits of `Z_l[[T]]`,
   and cylinder values of measures. In both, the Euler element `β` carries the coordinate-dependent multipliers
   `1 + lj`, which appear to give infinitely many sections. An action that makes `β` finite-state has to encode the
   arithmetic progression `j ↦ 1 + lj` by a transducer on the index, as the lamplighter `Z/p ≀ Z ⊂ Aut(F_p[[t]])`
   encodes translation.
