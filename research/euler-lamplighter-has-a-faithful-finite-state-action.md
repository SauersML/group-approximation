**OPEN (2026-09-18, lane gq-k2-q).** For some prime `l`, the group `G_l` of
`z-localized-embeds-in-fg-rf-soluble-group` has a faithful action on some regular rooted tree `T_d` in which every
element is finite-state.

## Why it matters

- **Equivalent form.** A faithful finite-state action is the same as an embedding of `G_l` in a finitely generated
  self-similar group: the one generated by the finitely many states of the images of `f_0, x, β`. Self-similar
  groups are residually finite.
- **Payoff.** If that self-similar group can be chosen finitely presented, `z-localized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group`
  follows. Finite presentation of the state closure is a further, separate question.

## What is known

- **Faithful tree actions exist** (`euler-lamplighter-acts-faithfully-on-the-l-ary-tree`): on `Z_l[[T]]`, read as
  `l`-adic measures on `Z_l`, `β` acts as multiplication by the function `1 + lx`.
- **In that action `β` is probably not finite-state.** On the cylinder `a + l^m Z_l`, `β` acts on the restricted
  measure as the scalar `1 + la` times a correction `1 + l^(m+1)x'/(1 + la)`. The scalars `1 + la` are pairwise
  distinct for different `a`, which suggests infinitely many distinct sections. This argues against the natural
  coordinates only, not against every action. It is not written as a proof.
- **State counts are not an obstruction by themselves.** In any finite-state action, the `q`-th roots of `f_0` for
  `q > d` are the unique roots in `Aut(T_d)`. For odometer-type actions they have about `q` states. A finitely
  generated finite-state group does contain elements of unbounded state count, so this gives no contradiction.
- **Literature.** arXiv:2405.16678 shows that `A ≀ G` is a subgroup of an automata group whenever `G` is and `A` is a
  finitely generated abelian group. It covers `Z ≀ Z ≅ H`, but not `G_l`. The base `M = Z_(l)[Z]` is not finitely
  generated, and `β` lies outside any restricted wreath product over `Z`.

## Attempts

1. **Natural coordinates (gq-k2-q, 2026-09-18).** Two coordinatizations were tried: monomial digits of `Z_l[[T]]`,
   and cylinder values of measures. In both, the Euler element `β` carries the coordinate-dependent multipliers
   `1 + lj`, which appear to give infinitely many sections. An action that makes `β` finite-state has to encode the
   arithmetic progression `j ↦ 1 + lj` by a transducer on the index, as the lamplighter `Z/p ≀ Z ⊂ Aut(F_p[[t]])`
   encodes translation.
