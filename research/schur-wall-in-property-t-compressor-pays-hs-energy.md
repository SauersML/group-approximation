In the setting of `property-t-hs-positive-density-commutant-no-growth`, suppose
a locally exactified Schur packet of baseline dimension `D_0` and violation
count `K` supplies a projection `W` on adjoint space satisfying

```text
W<=Q,     W perpendicular P,
tr_ad(W) >= (3/(4D_0^2K))q^2-C_0 delta,                         (SPW1)
```

where `q` is its forbidden Hilbert mass. Then

```text
q^2 <= C_1 delta                                                (SPW2)
```

for a fixed constant `C_1`. Hence positive forbidden mass cannot be hidden in
a growing matrix dimension once the packet Reynolds difference is realized
as a compressor wall.
