A prime unital properly infinite `B` is in particular unital and properly infinite, so LXI makes it
K1-injective. Then every `v ∈ U(B)` with `[v] = 0` in `K_1(B)` lies in `U_0(B)`, whatever its behaviour in
the quotients of `B`.
