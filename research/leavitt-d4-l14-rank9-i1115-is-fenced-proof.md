For the start-2, size-`151` representative the carrier equations are

```text
H^-1B0=E3^-1H^-1, H^-1C0=A1^-1H^-1,
H^-1B1=E2^-1H.
```

In branch I, `B0=1` and `H=h_2`, `h=pd`.  The last equation gives either
`h=1,p=v,d=v^-1`, with `K=1`, or a nontrivial involution `h=p` with
`v=d=1`; the latter boundary has unique copy-3 and copy-1 syllables and is
not one-copy.  Branch II forces

```text
H=1, c=z^-1, d=v^-1, p=zv,
```

and direct reduction gives `K=1`.  Branch III forces
`H=1,c=z,d=1,p=z^-1`, again with `K=1`.

In branch IV the first equation has two maximal families.  One has
`g10=g11=g12=f=1,g13=y^-1`; the other has
`g10=g11=g12=g13=y=1` and initially leaves `f` free.  The last carrier
equation forces respectively `a=1,p=y` or `f=a=p=1`, and both boundaries
are the identity.

In branch V the first equation gives `g10=g11=g12=g13=1,H=f_2`.
The last equation gives either `f=1,a=v^-1,p=v`, with `K=1`, or

```text
v=a=1, p=f, f^2=1.
```

In the latter case the boundary has a unique nontrivial copy-1 syllable.  A
one-copy reduction would require both adjacent sides to vanish; its right
side forces `x=u=1`, after which its left side is
`f_3f_2f_0f_2`, nontrivial for `f!=1`.  Hence this case is also fenced.
