Pass to the faithful GNS image of one non-CE BCS trace, tensor with the Haar
circle algebra, and reduce-free-product with `C*_r(F_2)`.  The two free-group
generators and the circle generator satisfy Avitzour's trace-orthogonality
hypotheses, so the free product is simple with unique trace.  Its trace is
non-CE because its restriction to the original GNS image is the original
non-CE trace.  Finite generation is preserved by the tensor product and by
taking the C-star algebra generated by the two free-product factors.
