**OPEN.** There are a finitely presented infinite simple unital ring `R` and `n >= 3` such that
`S = EL_n(R) / Z(EL_n(R))` is finitely presented, simple and sofic.

Such an `S` is infinite, since it contains the image of `e_12(R)`. It is Kazhdan, because `R` is
finitely generated (`elementary-groups-over-fg-rings-have-property-t`).

**Three gates.**
- **(a) The ring.** `R` finitely presented, simple, and with no halvable idempotent in any `M_d(R)`.
  The last condition is necessary by `halvable-corner-makes-elementary-groups-nonsofic`.
- **(b) The group.** Krstić--McCool (JPAA 141 (1999), Theorem 3, as quoted in
  `research/artifacts/khanh-2609-08428-leavitt-gl-verified-2026-09-11.md`) makes `St_n(R)`
  finitely presented for `n >= 4`. So `S` is finitely presented iff the kernel of `St_n(R) -> S` is
  finitely normally generated. Simplicity of `S` needs a root-detection argument adapted to `R`.
- **(c) Soficity without LEF.** By `lef-simple-rings-are-not-finitely-presented`, the models of `R`
  must be approximate.

## Attempts

- **Rings with a halvable corner: dead.**
  - This removes `L_k(1,n)` and every algebra with a unital Cuntz family: graph algebras with such
    a family, Nekrashevych algebras.
  - It removes every purely infinite simple algebra over a finite field
    (`purely-infinite-simple-algebras-have-nonsofic-el-groups`).
  - This is exactly what separates the brief's model test `L_(F_2)(1,2)` from a sofic candidate:
    the unit `1` is halvable.
- **LEF coefficient rings: dead for (a).** An infinite simple LEF ring is not finitely presented.
  In particular the Pestov 9.1 ring `LC(X, F_q) ⋊ Z` is infinitely presented
  (`minimal-subshift-crossed-product-is-not-finitely-presented`).
- **Weyl algebras: dead as posed.**
  - `A_1(K)` is simple only in characteristic zero. There `K ⊇ Q` is not a finitely generated ring,
    and a subring generated by finitely many elements lies in `A_1(S_0)` for a finitely generated
    subring `S_0 ⊊ K`. So `A_1(K)` is not finitely generated as a ring, and Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain
    does not apply.
  - `A_1(Z)` has the ideals `p A_1(Z)`, and `A_1(F_p)` has central `x^p`.
- **Rank models give linear soficity, not soficity.**
  - If `R` is simple with a unital homomorphism into a rank ultraproduct `M` over a field `k`, the
    homomorphism is injective. So `GL_n(R)` embeds in `M_n(M)^x` and `EL_n(R)` is `k`-linear sofic.
  - Upgrading to soficity is Arzhantseva--Păunescu Question 8.5 at this group, which is open.
  - Every natural finite set on which `GL_N(F_q)` acts (vectors, subspaces, flags) turns a rank
    defect `r` into a Hamming defect `1 - q^(-O(r))`. So almost multiplicativity in rank does not
    transfer.
- **Standard soficity certificates: dead.**
  - A finitely presented infinite simple group is not LEA (`hyperlinear-fp-infinite-simple-group`,
    Attempts).
  - By (T) and simplicity, `S` lies in a vertex group of any amalgam or HNN extension it embeds in,
    in the base or the acting group of a wreath product, and in the kernel of an amenable extension.
    So no permanence construction certifies it.
  - It has no sofic embedding with ergodic centralizer (`kazhdan-ergodic-sofic-centralizer-forces-lef`).
  - It has no realization in an amenable orbit full group
    (`kazhdan-subgroups-of-amenable-orbit-full-groups-are-rf`).
- **Live candidate (analysis in progress, unreviewed).** `R_U = LC(Omega_U, F_q) ⋊ Z^2` for Labbé's
  minimal aperiodic Wang shift (`minimal-aperiodic-wang-shift-exists`).
  - Known: it is finitely generated, it is simple (free minimal action), it has a unital rank model
    from Følner truncation, and so it has no halvable corner.
  - Open: whether it is finitely presented. Peer Theorem A
    (`research/artifacts/fp-simple-amenable-z2-sft-2026-09-12.md`) removes the periodic-model
    obstruction.
  - Gate (a) for this ring is exactly quantum rigidity of `Ω_U`
    (`sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`, open instance
    `labbe-wang-shift-crossed-product-is-finitely-presented`).
  - Positive-entropy SFTs are excluded (`positive-entropy-sft-crossed-products-are-not-fp`).
  - Zero entropy does not exclude finite presentation: Ledrappier's shift is rigid
    (`ledrappier-crossed-product-is-finitely-presented`), though with a fixed point.
  - Also open: the Steinberg kernel gate (b) and soficity (c).
