Kakariadis--Leimbach--Todorov--van Suijlekom, *Operator systems and positive
extensions over discrete groups*, arXiv:2603.29958v1 (2026), Theorem 5.11 and
the example following it, identify `UOS(Delta)` as the coproduct of the two
one-axis sums-of-squares systems for

```text
Delta={(0,0),(+-1,0),(0,+-1)} subset Z^2.
```

They compute its minimal C-star cover/C-star envelope as `C^*(F_2)`.  The
concrete Fourier system generated by the same coordinate unitaries lies in
`C^*(Z^2)` and has C-star envelope `C^*(Z^2)`.  A unital complete order
isomorphism preserves the C-star envelope, so the two operator systems cannot
be completely order isomorphic.  The paper states the equivalent failure of
the complete factorisation and complete extension properties.  This is the
claimed counterexample.
