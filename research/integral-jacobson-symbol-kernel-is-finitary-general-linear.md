**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `J = Z<s,t | ts = 1>`, `e = 1 - st`,
`f_(ij) = s^i e t^j`, `I = JeJ = M_fin(N_0, Z)`, `S = Z[z,z^-1] = J/I`.
Write `GL_fin(Z)` for the finitary automorphisms of the free module
`Z^((n x N_0))` on the internal shift basis (finite integral matrices
extended by the identity) and `SL_fin(Z)` for its determinant-one
subgroup, the finitary determinant of a finite-rank perturbation of the
identity being well defined and conjugation invariant.  For every
`n >= 2`:

```text
E_n(J, I)               = SL_fin(Z),                                   (SK1)
ker( EL_n(J) -> EL_n(S) ) = GL_fin(Z),                                 (SK2)
eta = [U, diag(-1,1)] = diag(1 - 2e, 1) in EL_2(J),  det_fin(eta) = -1, (SK3)
```

where `U = [[s, e],[0, t]]` is the manuscript's compression-cell
compressor (`lem:ring-compression-cell` in `non_mf_groups_exist.tex`).
Consequently `T_n = EL_n(J)/SL_fin(Z)` is a central extension

```text
1 -> C_2 -> T_n -> EL_n(S) -> 1,                                       (SK4)
```

with `C_2` generated by the class of `eta`, and the stabilization maps
`T_n -> T_(n+1)` are injective.

**What is new relative to the archive.**  The order-two quotient ring's
symbol sequence (`order-two-universal-ring-has-finitary-symbol-sequence`)
has kernel `GL_fin(F_2) = SL_fin(F_2)`; over the integers the kernel is
larger than the relative elementary subgroup by exactly one sign, and
that sign is realized by an elementary matrix of the original ring, not
by an element adjoined from `GL`.  Everything downstream
(`integral-jacobson-sign-quotient-is-residually-finite`,
`universal-torsion-defect-rings-have-exact-mf-radical`) turns on tracking
this `C_2`.
