Expand each squared projection distance and sum.  Since
`tau(E_iA_i)<=tau(E_i)`, the total overlap can cancel at most the total PVM
mass `s`; this proves `(DRM2)`.  Substitution of the canonical half- and
quarter-traces gives `(DRM3)--(DRM4)`.

Tensor separation realizes zero internal relation and first-exit energy
without changing any trace in `(DRM2)`, proving that those terms alone
cannot repair the capacity mismatch.
