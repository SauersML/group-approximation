**ESTABLISHED** through `partial-burnside-golod-shafarevich-proof`.

**Statement.** Let `m >= 2`, `R >= 1` and `N >= 2`. Suppose a prime power
`q = p^a` (with `a >= 1`) divides `N` and `q >= 4R + 5`. Then:
1. The presentation `⟨x_1, ..., x_m | h^N : h in F_m, 1 <= |h| <= R⟩` of the
   partial Burnside group `Π_R(m,N)` satisfies the Golod--Shafarevich condition
   at `p`, with `τ = 3/(2m)`.
2. The pro-p completion of `Π_R(m,N)` is infinite.
3. For every `C` there is a finite `p`-group `Q` generated by `m` elements whose
   words of length `<= R` all satisfy `h^N = 1`, with `|Q| > C`. So `LRB(R, C)`
   of `local-restricted-burnside-iff-partial-burnside-finite-quotients` fails for
   every `C`.

**Consequences.**
- A radius `R` witnessing `LRB(m,N)` satisfies `R > (q - 5)/4` for every prime
  power `q` dividing `N`. For prime `N`, every radius `R <= (N - 5)/4` fails.
- The radius `R_nil(m,N)` of `finite-nilpotent-groups-satisfy-local-restricted-burnside`
  satisfies `R_nil(m,N) > (q - 5)/4` for every prime power `q` dividing `N`.

No novelty is claimed; the proof is a direct application of the
Golod--Shafarevich criterion.
