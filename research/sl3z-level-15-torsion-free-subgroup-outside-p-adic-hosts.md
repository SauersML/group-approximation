**ESTABLISHED** by [[sl3z-level-15-subgroup-profinite-torsion-proof]]. Not
independently reviewed. No novelty is claimed.

**The group.**
- Let `P_5 <= SL_3(F_5)` be generated by `diag(2,1,3)`, `diag(1,2,3)` and
  `[[0,1,0],[1,0,0],[0,0,4]]`. It has order 32 and is a Sylow 2-subgroup.
- For `M` in `P_5`, let `chi(M)` be 1 if the top-left `2x2` block of `M` has
  non-square determinant mod 5, and 0 otherwise.
- Let `t = diag(-1,-1,1)` mod 3, and
  `S = {(t^chi(M), M) : M in P_5} <= SL_3(F_3) x SL_3(F_5)`.
- Put `H = {g in SL_3(Z) : (g mod 3, g mod 5) in S}` and `N = H cap Gamma(3)`.

**Statements.**
1. `H` is torsion-free of index `65286000 = 2^4 3^4 5^3 13 31` in `SL_3(Z)`. It is a
   lattice in `SL_3(R)` with property (T).
2. `N` has index 2 in `H` and satisfies Strong Atiyah over `C`. So
   `2 rk_(N(H))(A) in Z` for every matrix `A` over `C[H]`.
3. `H` is not isomorphic to a subgroup of any torsion-free profinite group. In
   particular it lies in no torsion-free compact `p`-adic analytic group, for any
   `p` and any embedding.
4. `H` is not locally indicable, not in Linnell's class `C`, and not virtually
   special, since all of its finite-index subgroups have finite abelianization.
5. Strong Atiyah for `SL_3(Z)` with denominator 24 implies Strong Atiyah for `H`,
   because `24` divides `[SL_3(Z):H]`.
6. Let `h` in `H` have `h = t mod 3`, and let `sigma` be conjugation by `h` on
   `D_C(N)`. `H` satisfies Strong Atiyah over `C` if and only if no `a` in `D_C(N)`
   satisfies `sigma(a) a = h^2`
   (`index-two-strong-atiyah-is-a-twisted-norm-equation`).

**What it is for.**
- A torsion-free group where Strong Atiyah is open by every recorded method and
  one factor of 2 is missing.
- A group element `a = g` in `N` never solves item 6: `sigma(g) g = h^2` means
  `(g h^-1)^2 = 1`, which is torsion. Any solution must be a genuinely
  non-group element of the Linnell division ring of `N`.

**Checked on MSI** (`experiments/strong-atiyah-sl3z/level15_torsion_free_subgroup_check.py`,
python3.12, seconds):
- `|P_5| = |S| = 32`, and `S` is closed under multiplication;
- `S` projects onto `{1, t}` mod 3;
- no pair in `S` has both coordinates involutions, and over `t` the second
  coordinates have orders 4 and 8;
- none of the 6527 nontrivial torsion elements of `SL_3(Z)` with entries in
  `[-2, 2]` lies in `H`.
