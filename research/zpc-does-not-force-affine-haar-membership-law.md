Z alignment, edge commutation, and perfect acceptance do not imply that the
stabilizer-membership name process is affine Haar.

Indeed, let

```text
X={1,2,3} x F_2
```

with uniform measure, and define commuting involutions

```text
J(i,t)=(i,t+1),
u(i,t)=(i,t+epsilon_i),       (epsilon_1,epsilon_2,epsilon_3)=(0,1,1).
                                                        (ZAH1)
```

Then `J` is free, `u` commutes with `J`, and `u x` is always either `x` or
`Jx`.  Thus `u` is a valid Z-aligned readable variable.  All required
same-question and edge commutators hold because the action generated by
`J,u` is abelian.  In particular this is a perfect finite ZPC permutation
strategy for the one-readable-variable tailored game whose verifier always
accepts.

For the stabilizer `H_x`, put `b_u(x)=1_(u in H_x)`.  The involution `u`
fixes exactly the first signed pair, so

```text
Pr[b_u=1]=1/3,
E[(-1)^b_u]=1-2/3=1/3.                                 (ZAH2)
```

This Fourier moment has modulus neither zero nor one.  By
`affine-haar-process-fourier-modulus-compiler`, the full membership-name law
is not Haar measure on any affine `F_2`-subshift.

Consequently no argument using only the ZPC structural axioms, even together
with perfect acceptance, can prove the affine-Haar Fourier identities for
the existential strategy in `perfect-zpc-irs-quantum-gap-game`.  A surviving
route must make a special strategy selection/construction and certify all of
its joint parity moments; the ZPC label alone supplies none of that
certification.
