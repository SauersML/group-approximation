Let B and C be the finite graph algebras with adjacency matrices
`[[2,2],[1,3]]` and `[[2,1,2],[1,2,2],[1,1,3]]`, respectively.
Let A=B tensor B. The four specified edge-phase circle actions on
C, transported through a single fixed unital isomorphism C -> A,
and the two specified tensor-factor circle actions on A form a
Z-basis of pi_1(Aut(A),id).

With the scaled K-theory marking in the artifact their invariant
matrix is equation (15), of determinant 1. A has the same scaled
graded K-theory as the irrational rotation algebra. All formulas
for the edge actions, row unitaries and tensor actions are given
in Sections 2--6. The fixed classification isomorphism need not
have a formula on generators; the statement makes that choice
explicit and does not assert six formulas on A_theta itself.
