Let `H` be Higman's group with the splittings `H = G_1 *_(F_ac) G_2 = G_3 *_(F_bd) G_4` of
`higman-group-splits-as-amalgams-over-free-subgroups`. Let `S <= H` be finitely generated and nonsofic.
Then, for each splitting, `S` fixes no vertex and no end of the Bass--Serre tree, and some edge stabilizer
`S cap g F g^(-1)` is a nonabelian free group (`F = F_ac`, resp. `F_bd`). In particular `S` lies in no
conjugate of `<a,b,c>`, `<b,c,d>`, `<c,d,a>` or `<d,a,b>`.

**For strict automata.** By `strict-composites-have-strict-factors` and `sofic-groups-are-surjunctive`, the
memory of a strict factor of a strict automaton on `A^H` generates such an `S`. Every automaton whose memory
lies in a conjugate of the subgroup generated by three consecutive generators is surjunctive.
