**ESTABLISHED** (literature import).

**Source.** M. Chaudkhari, *Confined subgroups of Thompson's group F and its embeddings into
wobbling groups*, arXiv:1809.05146v2 (4 Oct 2018), §2.1, which attributes the facts to
Cannon–Floyd–Parry (Enseign. Math. 42 (1996)) and Burillo's notes.

**Verbatim.**
- (F1) "Its commutator subgroup $F'$ is a simple group which coincides with the subgroup of all
  elements with support contained in the unit interval $(0,1$). The quotient $F/F'$ is isomorphic
  to $\mathbb{Z}^2$, and a subrgoup $H<F$ is normal if and only if it contains the commutator
  subgroup of $F$."
- (F2) "If $a<b$ are dyadic rationals, then $F[a,b]$ is isomorphic to $F$. Furthermore, its
  commutant is exactly the subgroup $F(a,b)$."
- (F3) "If $a<b<c<d$ are dyadic rationals then group generated by elements of $F[a,c]$ and
  $F[b,d]$ contains $F[a,d]$"
- (F4) From the proof of Theorem 3.1: "action of $F'$ is transitive on ordered n-tuples of dyadic
  rationals for any $n$".

**Reading notes.**
- `F[a,b]` is the group of elements of `F` fixing `[0,1] ∖ [a,b]` pointwise, and `F(a,b)` the
  elements whose support lies in `(a,b)`.
- "Support" is the closure of the moved set. This is the only reading consistent with (F1)–(F2):
  under the open-moved-set reading, (F2) would make `F[a,b] ≅ F` perfect, against `F/F′ ≅ Z²` in (F1).
  So `F(a,b)` is the set of `g` that are the identity near `a` and near `b`, supported in `[a,b]`.
- "Ordered n-tuples" in (F4) means increasing tuples in `(0,1)`.
- The last clause of (F1) is misstated as printed, because the trivial subgroup is normal. It is
  not used downstream. Only simplicity of `F′`, the description of `F′`, and (F2)–(F4) are used.
