Derivation: Sections 2 and 3 of the artifact. `LSI` contains the sofic groups and is closed under O1--O7, so it
contains `S*`. No stage induction is needed.

- **Sofic groups.** Their finitely generated subgroups are sofic and are their own sofic quotients.
- **O1, O2.** The invariant is a condition on finitely generated subgroups.
- **Extensions (Lemma 2.3).** Let `N` be normal in `E`, with `N` and `E/N` in `LSI`, and let `H <= E` be finitely
  generated and nontrivial.
  - If `H` maps nontrivially to `E/N`, the image is a nontrivial finitely generated subgroup of `E/N`, and its sofic
    quotient is a quotient of `H`.
  - Otherwise `H <= N`.
- **O4, O6, O7.** The kernels are residually finite (detection A1), a graph product of LEF groups (Ciobanu--Holt--Rees),
  and residually finite. All are sofic, so they lie in `LSI`, and Lemma 2.3 applies.
- **O5 (Lemma 2.6).** The kernel of a fold that is injective on vertex groups acts freely on the Bass--Serre tree, so it
  is free. The image lies in a group of `S*`, and Lemma 2.3 applies.
- **O3 (Lemmas 2.4, 2.5).** Let `H = <s_1, ..., s_k>` be nontrivial and locally embeddable into `LSI`.
  - Then `H` embeds into an ultraproduct `prod H_i / U`, where `H_i` is the subgroup generated by the images of the
    `s_j`, and `H_i` is nontrivial for `U`-almost every `i`.
  - Choose nontrivial sofic quotients `psi_i : H_i -> Q_i`. Some fixed generator `s_j` has a nontrivial image for
    `U`-almost every `i`, so `psi = prod psi_i / U` is nontrivial on `H`.
  - `psi(H)` is finitely generated and locally embeddable into sofic groups, so it is sofic.

**Verification tier.** Proposed-established; no referee yet. The external imports are:
- sofic groups are closed under local embeddings;
- residually finite groups are sofic;
- Ciobanu--Holt--Rees for O6, which a kernel-in-`LSI` form of O6 would avoid;
- Serre's theorem that groups acting freely on trees are free.
