Almost surely, the critical-cluster quotient of a nonamenable d-regular
locally finite Cayley graph has infinitely many mass-one vertices of
degree d, with parallel edges retained. Giving each quotient edge intensity
ell>0 yields psi_ell(1)<=d ell and sum_k 1/psi_ell(2^k)>=1/(d ell).
Thus the profile hypothesis sum_k 1/psi_ell(2^k)<1/625 from
Hartarsky--Severo--Teixeira, arXiv:2609.07768v1, cannot hold for
t=1-exp(-ell)<=1-exp(-625/d).

This does not obstruct percolation: the actual quotient has p_c=0.
It obstructs applying that sufficient criterion directly near zero.
It proves neither uniqueness nor nonuniqueness there.
