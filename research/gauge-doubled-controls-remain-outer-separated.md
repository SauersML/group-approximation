title: Gauge doubling does not put the two logical-share controls in one outer class
distinct_from:
  gauge-doubling-gives-selector-flip-symmetries: that constructs the two balanced shares and proves that their product retains the arbitrary logical involution; this computes the outer classes of the two corresponding controlled gates.
  packet-dressed-shared-implementer-requires-one-outer-class: that gives the general outer-class obstruction; this verifies the obstruction explicitly for the tempting gauge-doubled relative-Pauli cell.
---

Let `P` be the class-two two-group generated by involutions

```text
J,A,B,X,P_t,Q_t
```

with `J` central,

```text
[A,B]=1,       [X,A]=[X,B]=J,       [P_t,Q_t]=J,       (GOC1)
```

and all other named cross-commutators trivial.  Put

```text
r=AB.
```

Then `r` is a central involution independent of `J`.  In the relative-Pauli
interpretation, `A` and `B` are the two balanced gauge shares and `r` is the
original arbitrary logical involution.

There are two controlled-Z automorphisms of `P`:

```text
alpha_A(X)=X Q_t,       alpha_A(P_t)=A P_t,
alpha_B(X)=X Q_t,       alpha_B(P_t)=B P_t,             (GOC2)
```

with all omitted generators fixed.  The relations in `(GOC1)` show directly
that these assignments preserve every commutator and square relation, so they
are involutive automorphisms.  Their relative automorphism is

```text
delta=alpha_B alpha_A^(-1),
delta(P_t)=r P_t,       delta(g)=g for every other generator.   (GOC3)
```

The automorphism `delta` is outer.  Indeed `P` has nilpotency class two and

```text
[P,P]=<J>.
```

Consequently every inner automorphism sends every `g in P` to either `g` or
`Jg`.  Equation `(GOC3)` sends `P_t` to `rP_t`, and `r` is independent of
`J`; hence `delta` cannot be inner.  Therefore

```text
[alpha_A] != [alpha_B] in Out(P).                       (GOC4)
```

By `packet-dressed-shared-implementer-requires-one-outer-class`, stable
letters implementing `alpha_A` and `alpha_B` cannot be packet dressings of
one common stable letter while `P` remains embedded.  Thus the attractive
gauge-doubling idea

```text
logical r=AB
  + balanced controls A,B
  + one shared multiplicity implementer
```

fails exactly at the last step.  It can be rescued only on representations
where the logical radical collapses to `r in {1,J}`, which destroys the
arbitrary logical BCS variable.  The obstruction is algebraic and does not
come from an HS estimate or from an insufficient amplification.
