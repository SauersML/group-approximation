title: Gauge doubling does not put the two logical-share controls in one outer class
---

The controlled automorphisms attached to the two balanced gauge shares
differ by an outer automorphism multiplying one Pauli generator by the
independent logical product.  They therefore cannot be dressings of one
common implementer while the logical variable remains embedded.
