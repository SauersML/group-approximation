## 1. Exact source interfaces

Lin's Definition 6.14 and Theorem 6.15 give an effective finite
synchronous game from a program with an unconditional infinite loop such
that

```text
omega_co(G_loop)=1,                    omega_*(G_loop)<=1/2. (1)
```

The paragraph after Theorem 6.15 explicitly permits this known nonhalting
input. The perfect-regime game-graph reduction of Mancinska--Spaas--Spirig--
Vernooij, Theorem 3.1 and Remark 3.2, gives one fixed independent-set game
`I` with

```text
omega_co(I)=1,                         omega_*(I)<1.       (2)
```

The proof of their theorem is written with PVMs in a tracial von Neumann
algebra, so perfect commuting completeness is preserved. Remark 3.2 gives
the value-one equivalence needed to preserve strict tensor-product
soundness.

Let `A_I` be the universal perfect synchronous algebra of `I`. Its tracial
state space is nonempty. A character of `A_I` would be a deterministic
perfect strategy, contradicting `(2)`. The stronger facts that `A_I` has no
matrix representation and no Connes-embeddable trace are established in
`perfect-qc-separation-yields-finite-bcs-with-no-matrix-model`.

Next use the existing audited fixed-language route on this Lin-derived BCS.
Culf--Mastel Example 4.13 identifies positive ONE3 as the Boolean relation

```text
{001,010,100},
```

and Theorem 4.14 covers every Boolean NP-complete language. Their Lemma 3.10
is the trace-dependent assignment-to-context comparison used by the
repository's moment-exact audit. The resulting fixed positive-ONE3 system
`B_13` has a perfect trace and no perfect Connes-embeddable trace. This last
sentence uses the already audited node
`non-ce-bcs-has-positive-one3-weighted-form`; it is not attributed to
Theorem 4.14 alone.

## 2. A finite perfect face with no character is uniformly nonabelian

We use the following elementary lemma. Let `A` be a unital C-star algebra
generated by a finite family of PVMs `(p_(x,a))`. Suppose its trace space
`T(A)` is nonempty and `A` has no character. Put

```text
J={(x,a)},                 N=|J|,
K(tau)=N^(-2) sum_(i,j in J) tau([p_i,p_j]^*[p_i,p_j]). (3)
```

Then `K(tau)>0` for every trace.

Indeed, if `K(tau)=0`, every summand in `(3)` is zero. Pass to the tracial
GNS quotient. Each commutator is zero there, so the finitely many PVM
atoms generate a finite-dimensional abelian algebra. Its joint atoms are

```text
z_s=product_x p_(x,s(x)).                               (4)
```

Zero atoms may be discarded, and the remaining atoms sum to one. At least
one has positive trace. Evaluation on that atom sends every PVM to a
zero-one PVM and respects all exact relations, hence gives a character of
`A`, a contradiction.

The tracial state space of a unital C-star algebra is weak-star compact.
The function `K` is a finite sum of evaluations of fixed positive
elements and is continuous. Therefore

```text
kappa_A=min_(tau in T(A)) K(tau)>0.                     (5)
```

For `A_I`, a hypothetical character contradicts `omega_*(I)<1`, so `(5)`
gives `kappa_I`. For the exact positive-ONE3 algebra, a character is a
classical satisfying assignment and hence a one-dimensional
Connes-embeddable perfect trace, contradicting the defining property of
`B_13`. This gives `kappa_13`.

Notice what was and was not used. Non-Connes-embeddability is needed only
to exclude a character of `B_13`; the uniform floor itself follows from
finite generation and compactness. No effective numerical value for
`kappa_I` or `kappa_13` is claimed.

## 3. The exact three-corner identity

Let `p,q` be projections in a tracial von Neumann algebra and put

```text
X=p q (1-p).                                             (6)
```

Since

```text
[p,q]=X-X^*,                 X^2=(X^*)^2=0,
```

traciality gives

```text
||[p,q]||_2^2=||X||_2^2+||X^*||_2^2=2||X||_2^2.         (7)
```

If `p=p_(x,a)` belongs to an `m`-outcome PVM, then

```text
1-p=sum_(a'!=a) p_(x,a').                               (8)
```

The summands `p q p_(x,a')` in `X` are Hilbert--Schmidt orthogonal: for
`a'!=a''`, cyclicity moves the orthogonal product
`p_(x,a'')p_(x,a')` together. Hence

```text
sum_(a'!=a) ||p_(x,a) q p_(x,a')||_2^2
  =(1/2)||[p_(x,a),q]||_2^2.                            (9)
```

Sum `(9)` over all ordered atom pairs `p,q`. There are
`R=N^2(m-1)` ordered triples. With the uniform probability measure on
that row,

```text
(1/R) sum_(x,a,y,b,a'!=a)
  ||p_(x,a)p_(y,b)p_(x,a')||_2^2
 =K(tau)/(2(m-1)).                                      (10)
```

Equation `(10)` proves `(LQ5)` for the literal Lin atoms. In particular
the fixed aggregate row, rather than a representation-dependent chosen
entry, is the canonical object.

## 4. The qutrit specialization has exactly 27 words

In `B_13`, each positive-ONE3 clause gives a three-outcome PVM
`(p_(c,0),p_(c,1),p_(c,2))`. Let

```text
omega=exp(2 pi i/3),
u_c=p_(c,0)+omega p_(c,1)+omega^2 p_(c,2).              (11)
```

Then `u_c^3=1`, and finite Fourier inversion gives

```text
p_(c,a)=(1/3)sum_(r=0)^2 omega^(-ar)u_c^r.              (12)
```

Apply `(10)` with `m=3`. If `N_13=3|Clauses(B_13)|`, the row has
`R_13=2N_13^2` entries and

```text
(1/R_13) sum_(c,a,d,b,a'!=a)
 ||p_(c,a)p_(d,b)p_(c,a')||_2^2
 =K_13(tau)/4
 >=kappa_13/4.                                          (13)
```

Substituting `(12)` three times gives, without approximation,

```text
p_(c,a)p_(d,b)p_(c,a')
 =(1/27)sum_(r,s,t=0)^2
   omega^(-ar-bs-a't)u_c^r u_d^s u_c^t.                (14)
```

Thus every entry is in the image of the complex group algebra of the
group generated by the order-three clause clocks and uses at most 27
ordinary words. Coincident words only shorten the support.

The two end atoms in `(14)` belong to the same clock. Consequently

```text
u_c D=omega^a D,                 D u_c=omega^(a')D.      (15)
```

Since the row only uses `a'!=a`, every nonzero selected entry has distinct
left and right qutrit Fourier characters. This is the promised exact
off-diagonal typing.

## 5. A positive initial spectral projection

Let `gamma` denote the normalized row lower bound: either

```text
gamma=kappa_I/(2(m-1))
```

for the literal Lin row, or

```text
gamma=kappa_13/4
```

for the qutrit row. Some row entry `D=p q p'` satisfies

```text
tau(D^*D)>=gamma.                                       (16)
```

The selected entry may depend on the trace. Since `D` is a contraction
with right support below `p'`,

```text
0<=D^*D<=p'<=1.                                         (17)
```

Put `R=1_[gamma/2,1](D^*D)` in the tracial GNS von Neumann algebra.
Off `R`, the operator is at most `gamma/2`; on `R`, it is at most one.
Therefore

```text
gamma
 <=tau(D^*D)
 <=(gamma/2)(1-tau(R))+tau(R).                          (18)
```

Rearranging gives

```text
tau(R)>=gamma/(2-gamma),
D^*D R>=(gamma/2)R,                    R<=p'.            (19)
```

The inequality `gamma<=1` follows already from `(13)`, since it is an
average of squared norms of contractions. Thus the denominator in `(19)`
is positive.

## 6. Normalized robustness around the exact face

The exact result has a qualitative robust neighborhood which is useful for
ruling out asymptotically abelian compilers. Let `F_13` be the free product
of one copy of `C^3` for each clause, so the local clause PVMs are exact.
Choose any fixed finite relation menu `(r_l)` presenting the remaining
`B_13` identifications in tracial GNS quotients, and positive weights
`alpha_l`. Define

```text
Def(tau)=sum_l alpha_l tau(r_l^*r_l),
W=sum_l alpha_l,                   d(tau)=Def(tau)/W.    (20)
```

The division by `W` is essential; no unproved assumption that the original
weights sum to one is made. The zero set of `d` is the exact trace face.
Both `d` and `K_13` are continuous on the compact trace space of `F_13`.
The compact set

```text
{tau: K_13(tau)<=kappa_13/2}
```

is disjoint from `{d=0}`. Hence `d` has a strictly positive minimum
`epsilon_13` on that set. It follows that

```text
d(tau)<epsilon_13  =>  K_13(tau)>kappa_13/2
                       and qutrit row average >kappa_13/8. (21)
```

This constant is source-dependent and non-effective. The conclusion is
nevertheless uniform over matrix dimension and over all tracial models of
the fixed relation menu.

## 7. Consequence and boundary

Equations `(5)`, `(13)`, and `(21)` give a broad impossibility theorem:
no exact or asymptotically exact realization of the Lin-derived
positive-ONE3 face can put every clause atom into one global abelian
algebra. This includes a single diagonal qutrit packet, a diagonal
multiplicity algebra, and any router family whose decoded cross-context
commutator energy tends to zero.

The positive alternative is equally precise. Every perfect trace carries
the fixed finite row `(14)` with normalized energy at least
`kappa_13/4`; some entry has the initial support `(19)`. Thus a perfect
zero-noise compiler must preserve genuinely nonabelian cross-context
transport.

This is not yet the missing groupification theorem. The row is a complex
linear combination, the selected entry and spectral cut depend on the
trace, and no ordinary raw `S/T` relation has been shown to force the
right zero-compression in finite approximations while retaining `(19)` in
the perfect trace. The common Naimark transport is additionally an inner
gauge by `common-naimark-context-transport-is-an-inner-full-c-gauge`.
No nonhyperlinear group is claimed.
