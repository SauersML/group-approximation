Let P be countably infinite, let nu be a shell enumeration, and put
Q=(E_nu)_p=<rho(P),tau>, H=<tau>. The following are equivalent:

1. The normal core intersection_(q in Q) q*H*q^-1 is nontrivial.
2. Some C=<tau^m>, m>=1, is normal in Q.
3. For some m>=1, every g in P satisfies
   rho_g(n+m)=rho_g(n)+m for all sufficiently large n (with the
   threshold allowed to depend on g).

When these hold, C is central in Q. Such an enumeration exists if
and only if P is virtually cyclic. In particular, for every enumeration
of an infinite non-virtually-cyclic P the prefix germ has trivial
normal core in Q.

This classifies a repair of the normal-germ criterion by a finite-index
subgroup of H. It does not assert that a trivial normal core prevents
finite presentation of the shell group or of its singularity stabilizers.
