Details are in `research/artifacts/un-boundary-families-2026-09-13-part2.md`, §3.

1. **Cuntz groupoid.** `L_k(1, m) = A_k(𝒢_m)` by `leavitt-path-algebras-are-steinberg-algebras`. Here `𝒢_m` is the
   Hausdorff ample groupoid on `Ω_m = {1..m}^ℕ` with arrows `(αz, |α| - |β|, βz)`.
   - Minimal: `(αy, |α|, y)` moves `y` into `Z(α)`.
   - Effective: on a basic bisection `Z(α, β)` with `|α| != |β|`, the isotropy points satisfy `αz = βz`, which
     determines `z` uniquely. With `|α| = |β|` and `α != β` there are no isotropy points.
2. **Products.** `𝒢_m × 𝒢_n` is Hausdorff ample, with compact infinite unit space `Ω_m × Ω_n`.
   - Orbits are products, so it is minimal.
   - An open set inside the isotropy contains basic `U × V ⊆ Iso × Iso` with `U` and `V` open, so `U` and `V` consist
     of units. So it is effective.
3. **Tensor product.** `f ⊗ g ↦ ((γ, η) ↦ f(γ)g(η))` is an isomorphism `A(𝒢_m) ⊗ A(𝒢_n) ≅ A(𝒢_m × 𝒢_n)`.
   - Multiplicative: convolution factorizes.
   - Surjective: compact open sets of the product are finite disjoint unions of products of compact open sets.
   - Injective: evaluate a relation `Σ f_i ⊗ g_i ↦ 0` with the `f_i` linearly independent at each `η`.
4. **Finitely generated with a full defect.** `R_(m,n)` is generated by the `2m + 2n` Leavitt generators. The copy
   `L(1,m) ⊗ 1` gives `s = s_1 ⊗ 1` and `t = t_1 ⊗ 1` with `ts = 1` and `(t_2 ⊗ 1)(1 - st)(s_2 ⊗ 1) = 1`.
5. `paradoxical-steinberg-elementary-simple-kazhdan-not-mf` gives the statement.
