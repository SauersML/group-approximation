Robert, *Selfless C*-algebras*, arXiv:2309.14188v3, Theorem 4.2.  The source
allows the selfless first factor to be nonseparable.  The second factor is
required to be separable and its state must have faithful GNS representation.
The conclusion is selflessness of the reduced free product.  This is a
primary-source import, not an attribution-free reproof.
