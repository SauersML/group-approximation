Source: Shintaro Nishikawa and Nansen Petrosyan,
*Dehn fillings, equivariant homology, and the Baum--Connes conjecture*,
arXiv:2509.15070v2, Corollary G (Corollary 6.5).

Corollary 6.5 states directly that every lattice in a simple Lie group of
real rank one with finitely many connected components satisfies the
Baum--Connes conjecture with coefficients with finite wreath products.

The uniform case is covered by hyperbolicity. In the nonuniform case the
lattice is relatively hyperbolic with respect to its cusp groups; the
peripheral input and Theorem 6.2 give the result. The source explicitly
notes that this reaches nonuniform lattices in `Sp(n,1)`, a case not
previously covered by ordinary Baum--Connes. QED
