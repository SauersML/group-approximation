Fix the no-CE BCS and let `beta>0` be the self-adjoint-contraction
energy bound derived in the artifact from its established robust
unitary matrix gap. Let `G` be countable, `K<=G` finite and
nontrivial, and `q=1-|K|^-1 sum_(k in K) k`.

If self-adjoint contractions `a_x in q C*_max(G) q` satisfy
`E_B(a)<=c q` for some `c<beta`, then `K<=Rad_MF(G)`.
Moreover `G` is nonhyperlinear. No normality or centrality is assumed.

Finite-support sum-of-squares identities can certify this strict
inequality and the contraction bounds. An exact unital map of the
BCS algebra into the actual maximal C-star corner also supplies
finite-support tuples with sufficiently small energy by norm density.
Neither direction asserts that a single strict inequality yields an
exact algebraic BCS map, or that such an inequality has been constructed.
