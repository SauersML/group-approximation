Modus ponens. `jones-subgroup-coamenable-iff-thompson-f-amenable` is ESTABLISHED. The remaining prerequisite is
the Aiello–Brothier–Conti question: does the quasi-regular representation `λ_(F/\vec F)`, i.e. the Jones
chromatic representation at `Q = 2` restricted to the cyclic subrepresentation of the vacuum, weakly contain
`1_F`?

This opens the Temperley–Lieb–Jones and planar-algebra toolkit (vacuum-vector almost invariance, Jones'
unitary representations) as an attack on amenability of `F`. It does not escape the known obstacles, since
the prerequisite is equivalent to the target.
