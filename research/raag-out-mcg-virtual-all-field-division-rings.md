Let `K` be any field. For every finite-graph RAAG `A_Gamma` and every
compact surface `Sigma`, there are torsion-free finite-index subgroups

`G <= Out(A_Gamma)` and `H <= Mod(Sigma)`

such that `K[G]` and `K[H]` embed in division rings. The same conclusion
holds for a torsion-free finite-index subgroup of every handlebody mapping
class group `Mod(V)`.

In particular all these subgroup algebras are domains. The subgroup is
allowed to depend on `K`; no claim is made that a torsion-containing
ambient group algebra is a domain.
