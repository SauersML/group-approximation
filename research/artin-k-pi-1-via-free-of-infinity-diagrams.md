Let `Γ` be a finite Coxeter graph. Every free-of-infinity subset `X ⊆ S` gives a
free-of-infinity Coxeter graph `Γ_X`, which is of type K(π,1) by
`free-of-infinity-artin-groups-satisfy-k-pi-1`. By
`artin-k-pi-1-reduces-to-free-of-infinity-parabolics`, `Γ` is of type K(π,1).
