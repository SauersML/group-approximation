Forward, restrict a hypothetical trace-preserving embedding of `L(G)` into a
tracial matrix ultraproduct to the nonzero normalized corner and then to the
embedded tracial algebra.  Reverse, for a nonhyperlinear `G` take
`M=L(G)`, `p=1`, and the identity map.  These are exactly the two implications
written in the target claim.
