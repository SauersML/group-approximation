title: Every generating set of an infinite finitely generated Burnside subgroup has a local expanding sparsifier
---

Theorem 2. For any infinite finitely generated Gamma<=B(m,n), odd n>=1003, and finite symmetric generating set S, choose noncommuting s,t in S. There is an original-edge FIID connected spanning graph with half-degree <=(1-1/ord(s))+(1-1/ord(t))+epsilon, expansion >=h_H/2>0, and logarithmic coding and routing radii. Constants depend on Gamma,S,n, not epsilon.
