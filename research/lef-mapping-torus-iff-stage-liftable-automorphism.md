**ESTABLISHED.** Proof: `lef-mapping-torus-iff-stage-liftable-automorphism-proof`.

## Setting

- `K` is generated by a finite set `S`, and `F = F(S)` is the free group on `S`.
- `φ ∈ Aut(K)`, and `G = K ⋊_φ Z` is marked by `S ∪ {t}`, where `t s t^-1 = φ(s)`.
- For each `s ∈ S`, fix words `w_s, v_s ∈ F` representing `φ(s)` and `φ^-1(s)`.
- A sequence of marked groups `(H_i, S)` means epimorphisms `π_i : F -> H_i`. It *converges to
  `(K, S)`* if, for every `u ∈ F`, eventually `π_i(u) = 1` exactly when `u = 1` in `K`.
- An automorphism `α_i ∈ Aut(H_i)` is a *stage lift of `φ`* if `α_i(π_i(s)) = π_i(w_s)` for
  every `s ∈ S`.

## Theorem

The following are equivalent.

- **(a)** `G` is LEF.
- **(b)** Some sequence of **finite** marked groups `(F_i, S)` converges to `(K, S)`, and every
  `F_i` carries a stage lift `α_i` of `φ`.
- **(c)** Some sequence of finitely generated **residually finite** marked groups `(H_i, S)`
  converges to `(K, S)`, and every `H_i` carries a stage lift `α_i` of `φ`.

When they hold, `G` is operator MF (`lef-implies-operator-mf`), so `Rad_MF(G) = 1`, and (LK3)
of `torsion-free-lef-kazhdan-kernel-with-exact-mapping-torus-radical` fails whenever `K ≠ 1`.

No hypothesis on `K` beyond finite generation is used. Property (T), torsion and finite
presentation play no role. The outer orders of the stage lifts `α_i` may be unbounded.

## What dies

**Invariant:** *stage-liftability* of `φ`, meaning that `φ` is a limit of automorphisms of
residually finite approximants of `K`. **Death step:** each stage torus `H_i ⋊_{α_i} Z` is
residually finite, and LEF is closed under marked limits. So the whole torus is LEF, hence MF.

- **Every LEF construction of the torus is of this form**, by (a) ⇒ (b). For every finitely
  generated kernel `K ≠ 1`, (LK3) implies

  ```text
  (NSL)  φ is not a marked limit of automorphisms of finite approximants of K,
  ```

  and (NSL) is exactly "G is not LEF". (LK3) needs more: G must not even be MF. The items (A), (B) and (C) of
  `quotient-permuting-and-ambient-inner-mapping-tori-are-lef` are three sufficient conditions
  for the failure of (NSL).
- **Mother-group limits.** Let `K` be a marked limit of residually finite (for instance finite) quotients `M/N_i` of a mother `M` (for
  example the alternating-mother limits of `torsion-free-kazhdan-alternating-mother-limit-gate`).
  Then any `φ` that is induced stagewise dies, even when the stage automorphisms `α_i ∈ Aut(M/N_i)`
  are not induced by one automorphism of `M` and do not permute the `N_i`. This is more than (B).
- **Hyperbolic-stage limits.** Let `K` be a direct limit of residually finite hyperbolic stages,
  as for the monster of `hyperbolic-rf-gives-torsion-free-lef-quotientless-kazhdan` under
  `(RF_Γ)`. Then any `φ` that lifts to automorphisms of infinitely many stages dies. See
  `tarski-monster-tori-evade-compression-and-stage-lifts`.
- **Elementary kernels.** Take `K = EL_n(R)` with a ring automorphism `σ` of an exactly matricial
  ring. If `EL_n(σ)` is a limit of automorphisms of finite approximants of `EL_n(R)`, such as
  groups `EL_n(M_N(F_q))`, it dies. The space-time criterion of
  `commuting-subshift-automorphisms-die-when-space-time-is-rf` is one sufficient condition.

## What survives

A witness of (LK1)-(LK3) with a LEF kernel needs an automorphism *born in the limit*: no
convergent sequence of finite (equivalently, residually finite) approximants of `K` carries
automorphisms converging to `φ`. The theorem says nothing about MF models that are not
permutation models, so it does not prove (EKL1).
