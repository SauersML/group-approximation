**Established by citation.**  Gabe--Szabo, *The dynamical
Kirchberg--Phillips theorem*, Theorem B (and Theorem F beyond discrete
groups), exactly as quoted by STW arXiv:2506.10902: for countable
discrete amenable `G` and outer actions `α, β` on stable Kirchberg
algebras, `α` and `β` are cocycle conjugate iff they are `KK^G`-
equivalent.
