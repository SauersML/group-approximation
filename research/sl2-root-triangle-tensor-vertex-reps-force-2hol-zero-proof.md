Notation as in the target. For the root groups we use:
- the data of `kazhdan-hyperbolic-sl2-root-triangles`;
- from `sl2-root-triangle-char2-frobenius-spectrum-transport`, the Frobenius expansion `ρ(p(y)) = Π_c (1 + y^{2^c} X_c^p)`.

In that expansion the `X_c^p` pairwise commute, square to zero, and are **unique**: any product
`Π_c (1 + y^{2^c} Y_c)` with commuting square-zero `Y_c` that equals `ρ(p(y))` for all `y ∈ k` has `Y_c = X_c^p`.
We also use (d) of that claim: `S_1 + hol = S_1`, and likewise for `S_2`, `S_3`.

In `End(K^2)`, `E_12 E_21 = E_11` and `E_21 E_12 = E_22`. So `E_12, E_21` generate `End(K^2)`,
`[E_21, E_12] = E_22 - E_11 ≠ 0`, the commutant of `E_21` is `span{1, E_21}`, and that of `E_12` is `span{1, E_12}`.

**Step 0. The model at `A_1`.** By (T), after conjugating `ρ`, `M = ⊗_{c ∈ T_1} K^2` and
`ρ(u(y)) = ⊗_c (1 + y^{2^c} E_12)`, `ρ(v(y)) = ⊗_c (1 + y^{2^c} E_21)`. Condition (P) is invariant under conjugation.
Put `y_c = E_12` and `x_c = E_21` in slot `c` (identity elsewhere). Then
`ρ(u(y)) = Π_{c ∈ T_1}(1 + y^{2^c} y_c)`, with commuting square-zero factors. By uniqueness:
- `X_c^{u} = y_c` and `X_c^{v} = x_c` at `A_1` for `c ∈ T_1`, and both are `0` for `c ∉ T_1`;
- `S_1 = T_1`.

Let `B_c ⊂ End(M)` be `End(K^2)` in slot `c`, the algebra generated by `x_c, y_c`.

**Step 1. Exact transport along a semilinear edge.** Let `e = (i,j)` with `φ_e(x) = λ x^{2^a}`, and let `p, p'` be its
parametrizations at `i, j`. In `Λ`, `ρ(p'(y)) = ρ(p(ψ_e(y)))` for `y ∈ k`
(route of `sl2-root-triangle-char2-frobenius-spectrum-transport`, Step 5), with `ψ_e(y) = μ y^{2^{-a}}` and
`μ = λ^{-2^{-a}} ∈ k^*`. So

```text
ρ(p'(y)) = Π_c (1 + μ^{2^c} y^{2^{c-a}} X_c^p) = Π_{c'} (1 + y^{2^{c'}} μ^{2^{c'+a}} X_{c'+a}^p).
```

The factors commute and square to zero. By uniqueness, `X^{p'}_{c-a} = μ^{2^c} X^p_c`: a nonzero multiple.
Applied to the three edges (`u` and `v` at `A_1, A_2`; `u` and `v(s·)` at `A_3`):
- **(e12)** `X^{u}_{c - a_12}` at `A_2` is a nonzero multiple of `y_c`;
- **(e13)** `X^{u}_{c - a_13}` at `A_3` is a nonzero multiple of `x_c`;
- **(e23)** `X^{v(s·)}_{d - a_23}` at `A_3` is a nonzero multiple of `z_d := X^{v}_d` at `A_2`.

So `S_2 = T_1 - a_12` and `S_3 = T_1 - a_13 = S_2 - a_23`.

**Step 2. The commutation pattern of `z_d`.** Fix `d ∈ S_2`. Put `c_1 = d + a_12` and
`c_2 = d + a_13 - a_23 = c_1 - hol`. Both lie in `T_1`, since `d - a_23 ∈ S_3 = T_1 - a_13`.
- By (e12) and (P) at `A_2`, for `c ∈ T_1`, `y_c` and `z_d` commute iff `c - a_12 ≠ d`, i.e. iff `c ≠ c_1`.
- By (e13), (e23) and (P) at `A_3`, for `c ∈ T_1`, `x_c` and `z_d` commute iff `c - a_13 ≠ d - a_23`, i.e. iff `c ≠ c_2`.

**Step 3. The commutant (uses `hol ≠ 0`).** Now `c_1 ≠ c_2`.
- `z_d` commutes with `x_c, y_c` for every `c ∈ T_1 \ {c_1, c_2}`, hence with those `B_c`. The commutant of
  `⊗_{c ≠ c_1, c_2} B_c = ⊗_{c ≠ c_1,c_2} End(K^2)` in `End(M)` is `B_{c_1} ⊗ B_{c_2}`.
- Also `z_d` commutes with `x_{c_1}` (since `c_1 ≠ c_2`) and with `y_{c_2}` (since `c_2 ≠ c_1`).

*Lemma.* In `A ⊗ B`, the commutant of `{a ⊗ 1, 1 ⊗ b}` is `C_A(a) ⊗ C_B(b)`. Proof: write `z = Σ_j a_j ⊗ b_j`
with `(b_j)` linearly independent. Then `[z, a ⊗ 1] = Σ_j [a_j, a] ⊗ b_j = 0` forces every `a_j ∈ C_A(a)`.
Symmetrically, expanding in a basis of `C_A(a)` gives `b_j ∈ C_B(b)`.

Hence `z_d ∈ span{1, x_{c_1}} ⊗ span{1, y_{c_2}}`:

```text
z_d = α + β x_{c_1} + γ y_{c_2} + δ x_{c_1} y_{c_2}.
```

- The three non-scalar terms commute and square to zero. In characteristic 2, `z_d^2 = α^2`, and `z_d^2 = 0`, so `α = 0`.
- `[z_d, y_{c_1}] = (β + δ y_{c_2}) [x_{c_1}, y_{c_1}]`. The factors sit in distinct slots, so this is nonzero iff
  `(β, δ) ≠ (0, 0)`. By Step 2 it is nonzero.
- `[z_d, x_{c_2}] = (γ + δ x_{c_1}) [y_{c_2}, x_{c_2}]`. It is nonzero iff `(γ, δ) ≠ (0, 0)`, and by Step 2 it is nonzero.

`experiments/sl2-root-triangle-frobenius-holonomy-2026-09-16/tensor_gluing.py`, part (1). It is exact linear
algebra over `F_2`, and the rank does not change under field extension. The proof above does not depend on it.

**Step 4. Contradiction (uses `2·hol ≠ 0`).** `S_2 + hol = S_2`, so `d' = d + hol ∈ S_2`. For `d'`, Step 2 gives
`c_1' = c_1 + hol` and `c_2' = c_1`. So by Step 3:

```text
z_d  = x_{c_1} A + γ y_{c_1 - hol},       A = β + δ y_{c_1 - hol},   (β, δ) ≠ 0,
z_d' = y_{c_1} B + β' x_{c_1 + hol},      B = γ' + δ' x_{c_1 + hol}, (γ', δ') ≠ 0.
```

Since `hol ≠ 0` and `2·hol ≠ 0`, the slots `c_1 - hol`, `c_1`, `c_1 + hol` are distinct. So:
- `γ y_{c_1-hol}` commutes with `z_d'`, and `β' x_{c_1+hol}` commutes with `z_d`;
- `A` and `B` commute with each other and with `x_{c_1}, y_{c_1}`.

Therefore

```text
[z_d, z_d'] = [x_{c_1} A, y_{c_1} B] = [x_{c_1}, y_{c_1}] · A · B.
```

This is a product of nonzero operators in three distinct tensor slots, so it is nonzero. But `z_d` and `z_d'` are
coefficient operators of one parametrization, so they commute. This contradiction proves the claim.

nonzero entries are exactly `β γ', β δ', δ γ', δ δ'`, which vanish together iff `(β, δ) = 0` or `(γ', δ') = 0`.

**Step 5. When (P) holds.**
- **At `A_2`, `ρ|_{A_2} ≅ ⊕_j L(T_j)`.** In each summand, `X_c^u` and `X_d^v` are `E_12` in slot `c` and `E_21` in
  slot `d` (or `0`).
  - For `c ≠ d` they commute in every summand.
  - For `c = d ∈ S_2`, some summand has `c ∈ T_j`, and there `[E_21, E_12] ≠ 0` in slot `c`.
- **At `A_3`, summands `⊗_{t ∈ T} V^{(t)}` of `SL_2(F)` with `t ↦ t mod f` injective on `T`.** For `y ∈ k`,
  `u(y)` acts in slot `t` as `1 + y^{2^{t mod f}} E_12`, and `v(sy)` as `1 + s^{2^t} y^{2^{t mod f}} E_21` with
  `s^{2^t} ≠ 0`. So `X^u_{t mod f} = E_12` and `X^{v(s·)}_{t mod f} = s^{2^t} E_21` in slot `t`, and the same argument applies.

**Step 6. Steinberg-type restrictions, and the count.**
- `St = L(Z/f)` satisfies (T) at `A_1` and (P) at `A_2`.
- `⊗_{t ∈ T_3} V^{(t)}`, with `T_3` a transversal of `Z/2f -> Z/f`, satisfies (P) at `A_3`.

`hol = 0` number `f^2 (q-1)^3 = 3087`, since `hol = 0` determines `a_13`. That leaves `6174`.
