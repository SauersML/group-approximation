**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Γ` be generated by a finite set `S`, and let `C(Γ) = ⟨σ, h_s : s ∈ S⟩` be its
half-line overgroup (`groups-embed-in-derived-subgroup-of-half-line-overgroup`).
1. If `Γ` is LEF, then `C(Γ)` is LEF. Conversely, `C(Γ)` LEF forces `Γ` LEF, since `Γ ≅ δ_0(Γ) ≤ C(Γ)`.
2. `WP(C(Γ)) ≡_T WP(Γ)`.

**The finite models** (item 1). For radius `r`:
- take a finite model `ψ : B_(2r)(Γ) → Q`, and put `M = 3r` and `H = Q^({-M,…,M})`;
- let `θ` be the level shift, an isomorphism from `{v : v_(M-1) = v_M}` onto `{v : v_(-M) = e}`;
- let `t` be a bijection of `H` with `t(ax) = θ(a) t(x)`, which exists by choosing transversals;
- let `τ(x,i) = (t(x), i+1)` on `H × Z/5r`.

Then `fσ^k ↦ λ((ψ f(m))_m) τ^k` is injective and multiplicative on the `r`-ball, with `λ` left multiplication in the
first coordinate.

**Consequence.** Every f.g. LEF group `Γ` lies in `[Δ, Δ]` for the f.g. infinite LEF group `Δ = C(Γ)`, and
`WP(Δ) ≡_T WP(Γ)`. That is the overgroup step of `lef-wp-groups-have-decidable-simple-kazhdan-lef-hosts`. It also
replaces the ultraproduct overgroup of `rf-groups-lie-in-derived-subgroups-of-rf-groups` (2) in the LEF envelope.
