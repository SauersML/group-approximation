title: Every atomic-center type-III von Neumann algebra has unique Jiang--Su embeddings
distinct_from:
  stw63-type3-separable-predual-vn-uniqueness: that permits diffuse center but assumes separable predual; this permits arbitrary cardinality but assumes atomic center.
---

Let `M` be a type-III von Neumann algebra whose center is atomic.  Equivalently,

```text
M = product_(j in J) M_j
```

for an arbitrary family of type-III factors, with no countability restriction
on `J` or on the factors.  Then every two unital embeddings `Z->M` are norm
approximately unitarily equivalent.
