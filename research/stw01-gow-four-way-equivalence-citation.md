Alec Gow, *On the Quasitrace Problem and a Characterization of W*-algebras*,
arXiv:2601.04431v2, Theorem A (Theorem 4.11), proves exactly the four
equivalences in the target.

This route establishes the equivalence theorem only. It does not establish
any of its four equivalent open assertions and therefore must not be used as
an empty-requirement route to STW Problem I.
