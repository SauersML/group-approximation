title: Some decidable finitely presented residually finite groups admit no rational Cantor embedding

---

There exists a finitely presented, residually finite group G, solvable
of class 3 and with decidable word problem, which does not embed in
the rational homeomorphism group on any finite alphabet of size at
least two.

Thus finite-state Cantor actions cannot be a universal realization
method for the Boone--Higman conjecture, even after restricting the
inputs to finitely presented residually finite groups. This rules
out arbitrary abstract embeddings too, not just computably chosen
embeddings. It does not say that G lacks a finitely presented simple
envelope with a different sort of action.
