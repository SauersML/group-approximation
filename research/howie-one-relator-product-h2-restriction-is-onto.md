Let `G = (*_lambda G_lambda)/<<R^n>>`, where:

- each `G_lambda` is locally indicable;
- `R` is cyclically reduced of length at least 2 and is not a proper power in
  `*_lambda G_lambda`;
- `n >= 1`.

Let `C = <R> <= G`, and let `M` be a `ZG`-module. Then the restriction map

    H^k(G; M) -> H^k(C; M) x prod_lambda H^k(G_lambda; M)

is an isomorphism for `k > 2` and an epimorphism for `k = 2`.

This is J. Howie, *Cohomology of one-relator products of locally indicable
groups*, J. London Math. Soc. 30 (1984), 419--430, Theorem 3. It is imported
second hand; see the citation route.
