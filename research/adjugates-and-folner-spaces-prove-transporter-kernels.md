The full source has dimension 2m^2 on an m-dimensional tail space;
the target over an n-dimensional output space has dimension n(n-1).
For a regular amenable coefficient group, a finite support set with
expansion less than sqrt(2) makes the difference positive. The
Følner characterization supplies such a set for the fixed finite
coefficient support.

For Laurent coefficients, represent the two-sector tensor map by
a two-by-two matrix M over the polynomial ring in two independent
sets of Laurent variables. The exterior quotient kills precisely
the polynomials fixed by exchanging the variable sets, including
diagonal terms in characteristic two. If delta=det(M) is nonzero,
tau(delta) adj(M)e_1 is a nonzero source whose image is
delta tau(delta)e_1. This image is fixed by the exchange. The
singular determinant cases have explicit nonzero matrix kernels.
