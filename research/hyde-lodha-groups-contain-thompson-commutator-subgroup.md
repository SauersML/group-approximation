**ESTABLISHED (literature import plus a one-line consequence).**

J. Hyde and Y. Lodha, *Finitely generated infinite simple groups of homeomorphisms of the real line*,
arXiv:1807.06478v2, Invent. Math. (2019), doi:10.1007/s00222-019-00880-7.
- **Definition 2.3 and Lemma 2.4.** `H = ⟨F', ν_1⟩ ≤ F` is generated by `ν_1, ν_2, ν_3`, and
  `H' = F'` is simple.
- **Definition 3.2.** For a labelling `ρ` of `(1/2)Z`,
  `G_ρ = ⟨ζ_1, ζ_2, ζ_3, χ_1, χ_2, χ_3⟩ ≤ Homeo+(R)`. Its subgroups `𝒦 = ⟨ζ_1, ζ_2, ζ_3⟩` and
  `ℒ = ⟨χ_1, χ_2, χ_3⟩` are isomorphic to `H` via `λ : H → 𝒦` and `π : H → ℒ`. The element `λ(f)`
  restricts to `f`, or to `ι f ι`, on every interval `[n, n+1]`.
- **Theorem 1.3.** For every quasi-periodic labelling `ρ`, `G_ρ` is a finitely generated simple subgroup
  of `Homeo+(R)`.

**Consequence.**
- `λ(F') ≅ F'` lies in `G_ρ`.
- `F'` contains `Z^n` for every `n`: nontrivial elements supported in `n` disjoint dyadic intervals commute
  and are independent (`finite-cd-line-groups-bound-disjointly-supported-families`).
- So `cd G_ρ = ∞`, and every group containing some `G_ρ` has infinite cohomological dimension. None is of
  type F.
- The supports of the elements `λ(f)` meet every unit interval. As
  `fg-simple-line-groups-have-no-one-sided-supports` predicts, `G_ρ` has no nontrivial rigid stabilizer on
  a bounded interval. Its infinite dimension comes from disjoint non-interval supports, not from
  micro-support.

**Scope.** The groups `G_ρ` of §3 only. Not checked:
- the generalized family `G_{ρ,α}` of §6;
- the finitely presented type-F∞ groups of arXiv:2302.04805. The owner lane z1-22-lo-simple-bm read
  that paper's Proposition 1.4, which gives infinite geometric dimension.
