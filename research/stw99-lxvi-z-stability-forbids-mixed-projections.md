Let `B` be a simple unital Z-stable C*-algebra.  Then exactly one of the
following projection regimes occurs:

1. `B` is stably finite;
2. every nonzero projection in `B` is properly infinite.

In particular, a simple unital Z-stable algebra cannot contain both a
nonzero finite projection and an infinite projection.  Consequently no
solution of STW Problem LXVI is Z-stable.

If an LXVI witness is separable, then it has infinite nuclear dimension.
More generally, a separable simple unital finite-nuclear-dimension algebra
cannot contain both a nonzero finite projection and an infinite projection.
