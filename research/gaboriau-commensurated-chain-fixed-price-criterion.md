**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let a countable group `Γ` be the increasing union of
infinite subgroups `Γ_n`, where `Γ_1` has fixed price one (for example, `Γ_1` is amenable).
Suppose each `Γ_(n+1)` is generated by `Γ_n` and elements `γ in Γ` with
`γ^(-1) Γ_n γ ∩ Γ_n` infinite. Then `Γ` has fixed price one: every free p.m.p. action of `Γ`
has cost one.
