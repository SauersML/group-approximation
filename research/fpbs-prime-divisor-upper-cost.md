title: The least prime divisor improves every-action Burnside cost to two minus two over that prime

Theorem 5. For odd n>=1003 and its smallest prime divisor p, every free p.m.p. action of B(m,n), m>=2, has cost at most 2-2/p. The same holds for each infinite finitely generated subgroup. In particular 3|n gives the bound 4/3. This is an upper bound, not an exact action cost or fixed-price assertion.
