On a face character atom, `I-product P_i` is one exactly when at least one
coordinate projection vanishes, while the average of `I-P_i` is at most one.
This proves `(UCK2)`.  Sum over faces, use bounded occurrence to compare with
the normalized coordinate Laplacian, and apply the assumed conjugation-
Kazhdan inequality.  The invariant space is the common commutant.
