**OPEN.**  Put

```text
L1 = SL_2(F_2[t,1/t]),   C1 = SL_2(F_2[t]),   X1 = L1/C1,
```

the action on one type of vertices of the 3-regular Bruhat--Tits tree of
`SL_2(F_2((t)))`.  The claim is that `L1 action X1` is sofic (GKP
Definition 2.1(5)).

## Why this pair

- **Co-dense, so no exact atlas.**  For `h=diag(t,1/t)`,
  `h e_12(p) h^(-1) = e_12(t^2 p)`.  In any finite quotient `h` has finite
  order `k`, so `h^(-1) = h^(k-1)` and the image of `C1` contains
  `e_12(t^(-2)p)`, hence `e_12(F_2[t,1/t])`, and likewise `e_21`.  Since
  `F_2[t,1/t]` is Euclidean, `SL_2 = E_2` and `C1` is co-dense in every
  finite quotient.  So `C1` is not separable and every sofic model is
  non-exact.
- **Escapes the finite-presentation stop.**  Over `F_2` units are trivial,
  so Nagao (`polynomial-gl2-over-a-field-is-a-nontrivial-amalgam`) gives
  `C1 = S_3 *_(Z/2) V` with `S_3=SL_2(F_2)` and `V=U(F_2[t])` the additive
  group of `F_2[t]`, which is not finitely generated.  Item 5 of
  `sofic-coset-actions-pass-to-marked-colimits` therefore does not exclude
  exact-model colimits, unlike the rank-two pair.
- **Necessary for rank two.**  The corner orbit of the base vertex of the
  `SL_3` building is `X1` (route
  `function-field-rank-one-vertex-action-from-rank-two`).

## Structure used by the attempts

- `L1 = C1 *_(B0) C1'` (Bass--Serre for the edge-transitive action on the
  tree), with `C1' = theta(C1)`, `B0 = C1 ∩ C1'` the Iwahori subgroup, and
  `theta` conjugation by `[[0,1],[t,0]]`, an involution of `L1` swapping
  `C1` and `C1'`.
- `B0` is generated by `V` and `V' = theta(V) = U^-(t F_2[t])`.
- Eliminating `C1'` gives
  `L1 = < C1, s | s u_(t^2 q) s^(-1) = u_q (q in F_2[t]), (s w)^2 = 1, (s u_t w)^3 = 1 >`
  with `s = diag(1/t,t)`, `w=[[0,1],[1,0]]`, `u_p=e_12(p)`.  So `L1` is the
  quotient of `S_3 *_(Z/2) B` by two relators, where
  `B = F_2[t,1/t] ⋊ <s> ≅ (Z/2)^2 wr Z` is the Laurent Borel subgroup.

## Attempts

- **Separable approximants (current route).**
  `function-field-rank-one-vertex-action-via-separable-approximants`
  presents `(L1,C1)` as the directed colimit of the amalgams
  `Gamma_n = C1 *_(B0,n) C1'` over `B0,n = <V_n, V'_n>` (degree-truncated
  root groups) and retracts each window onto the finitely generated
  amalgam `Delta_(n,m) = C1,m *_(B0,n) C1',m` of two virtually free groups.
  It needs `sl2-laurent-f2-amalgam-approximants-separate-the-vertex`.
- **Where it can die.**  An exact-intersection normal subgroup of
  `C1,m` must respect the partial conjugacies `u_q ~ u_(t^2 q)` that
  `s = w'w` induces inside `Delta_(n,m)` for small degrees.  No obstruction
  is known.  For `n=0` the amalgamated subgroup is `Z/2` and everything is
  virtually free.
- **Stabilizer or host amenable pieces.**  `V` and `B` are amenable, and
  `L1 action L1/V` is sofic (locally finite stabilizer, GKP Theorem 2.14),
  but no permanence theorem passes from the stabilizers `S_3` and `V` to
  the join `C1 = <S_3,V>`.
