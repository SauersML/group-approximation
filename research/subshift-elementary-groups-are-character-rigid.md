**OPEN.** Let `X` be an infinite minimal subshift and `G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ Z)`. Conjecture: every extreme character of `G_X` is `1` or the regular character `δ_e`.

Consequences, if it holds:
- `L(G_X)` is the only II_1 factor generated by a unitary representation of `G_X`;
- `IRS(G_X) = {b δ_G + (1−b) δ_{1}}`, by `research/artifacts/leavitt-irs-rigidity-from-character-simplex-2026-09-08.md` §1.

The opposite behavior holds for the commutator subgroup of the topological full group of `X`. Its extreme characters include `γ ↦ ∏ μ_i(Fix γ)` (Dudko–Medynets, arXiv:2602.16885, Thm 1.1).

Known:
- finite-dimensional unitary representations of `G_X` are trivial (Mal'cev, simplicity);
- `subshift-elementary-root-continuous-characters-are-trivial`: a character tending to 1 on root elements `e_12(e_P)` with `sup_μ μ(P) → 0` is trivial.

So the conjecture is equivalent to automatic continuity: a non-regular extreme character must tend to 1 on small root elements.

## Attempts

- **Dudko–Medynets automatic continuity (Thm 4.2 of arXiv:2602.16885).** Several steps transfer:
  - positions are orbit points, and the derived full group acts through `a ↦ aI_3 ∈ G`;
  - germ projections `R_Z` are well defined and commute for distinct `Z`;
  - `T_Q` is central, because `G` is generated by elements supported off any given point.

  It dies at DM (4.3)–(4.10). Their endgame needs every group element to permute positions, but a general matrix spreads the germ at `y` over `{T^j y : |j| ≤ w}`. A new endgame is needed.
- **Near-constancy on finite simple subgroups.** By Gluck's bound, `χ` is within `8/2^k` of a constant at the non-central elements of any copy of `SL_n(F_{2^k})` in `G`. It dies at two points:
  - controlling the remainders of Kakutani–Rokhlin block divisions is exactly the continuity hypothesis, since `K^0(X,T)` is not divisible in general;
  - a positive trivial-representation weight gives no `G`-invariant vector, because `G` is not locally finite.

  Next idea: an angle or (T) argument between the fixed spaces of two finite subgroups that contain the generators.
- **Kirillov Fourier trick on `U ≅ R^2`.** The spectral measure on `R̂^2` is `EL_2(R)`-invariant. Left ideals `I` give invariant subgroups `I^2`, so invariance does not force `δ_0` plus Haar.
- **Lattice restriction.** `EL_3(F_2[u^{±1}]) ⊆ G` is a higher-rank S-arithmetic lattice, and charmenability (Bader–Boutonnet–Houdayer–Peterson) would restrict `χ` there. Congruence characters would still have to be excluded from outside the lattice. Not attempted.
- **Along the note's LEF models.** Limits of normalized irreducible characters of `GL_{3N}(F_2)` give only convex combinations of `1` and `δ_e`. This constrains only characters that arise as such limits.
