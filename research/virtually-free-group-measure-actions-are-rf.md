**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Γ` be a countable group with a free subgroup of finite index (rank `<= ∞`),
e.g. any finitely generated virtually free group, `SL_2(Z)`, `PSL_2(Z)`, free products of finite groups. Every
continuous `Γ`-action on a Cantor space admitting an invariant Borel probability measure of full support is
residually finite in the sense of Kerr–Nowak. In particular every minimal such action with an invariant measure
is RF.

Every group containing `Z^2` fails this (`z2-containing-groups-have-non-rf-measure-actions`).
