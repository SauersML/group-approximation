**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `G` be a group and let `(H,phi,pi)` be an invariant
state-form near representation.  Put

```text
A=C^*(pi(G)),                 tau=phi|_A,
d(g,h)=pi(g)pi(h)-pi(gh),
I_tau={a in A:tau(a^*a)=0}.                              (SNG1)
```

Let `(K_phi,sigma,Omega)` be the GNS representation of `phi` on `B(H)` and
let

```text
K_A=closure(sigma(A)Omega) subset K_phi.                 (SNG2)
```

Then `K_A` is invariant under every `sigma(pi(g))`, and

```text
rho(g)=sigma(pi(g))|_(K_A)                               (SNG3)
```

is a **genuine** unitary representation of `G`.  More precisely,

```text
(K_A,rho,Omega) ~= L^2(A/I_tau,bar tau),                 (SNG4)
```

and, when the induced character is the regular character, `(SNG4)` is the
left regular representation of `G`.

However every multiplicative defect, and every finite two-sided translate
of such a defect, is the zero vector on this sector:

```text
sigma(a d(g,h) b)Omega=0                 (a,b in A).     (SNG5)
```

Consequently the closed `A`-bimodule generated by the near defects inside
`K_A` is zero.  In particular no nonzero Hilbert-space quasi-cocycle can be
obtained by taking finite sums or `L^2(tau)` limits of left/right translates
of the multiplicative defects.  The representation required to invoke
property `(TT)/T` exists canonically only after passing to the sector where
the proposed curvature vectors have disappeared.

On the full GNS space `K_phi`, left multiplication by the same unitaries need
not be a representation.  Its group-law defect is `sigma(d(g,h))`, and the
near-state hypothesis gives only

```text
sigma(d(g,h))Omega=0.                                   (SNG6)
```

It does not give

```text
sigma(d(g,h))sigma(T)Omega=0            (T in B(H)),     (SNG7)
```

because the left null space of a singular state need not be a two-sided
ideal of `B(H)`.  Thus the complementary singular directions can retain the
defect, but there the coefficient action is not a genuine representation and
property `(TT)/T` does not apply.

For the binary Leavitt unit group this closes the most direct
singular-state-to-quasi-cocycle proposal.  Its established `(TT)/T` can be
used only after a new theorem propagates `(SNG6)` to a nonzero invariant
Hilbert sector on which the group law is genuine while retaining some
nonzero defect-derived vector.  Trace-kernel quotienting alone does exactly
the opposite: it makes the group law genuine by killing every such vector.
