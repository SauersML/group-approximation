Let `A` be a finitely generated group and `C=<c_1,...,c_m><A`.  Let
`pi_FD^0` be the direct sum, over all finite-dimensional unitary
representations `rho` of `A`, of the restrictions of `rho` to

```text
H_rho^0=(H_rho^A)^perp.
```

Assume that there is `kappa>0` such that

```text
kappa ||xi||^2 <= sum_i ||rho(c_i)xi-xi||^2            (PSG1)
```

for every finite-dimensional `rho` and every `xi in H_rho^0`.  Let `M` be
a finite tracial von Neumann algebra, let `pi:A->U(M)` be a representation,
and let `P_A` be the orthogonal projection of `L^2(M)` onto the
`Ad pi(A)`-fixed vectors.  For a selected unitary `v in U(M)` put

```text
eta=(1-P_A)v,
K_v=closure span{Ad pi(a)eta:a in A}.                  (PSG2)
```

If the representation on `K_v` is weakly contained in `pi_FD^0`, then

```text
kappa ||(1-P_A)v||_2^2
 <= sum_i ||[v,pi(c_i)]-1||_2^2.                       (PSG3)
```

Consequently, for every `a in A`,

```text
||[v,pi(a)]-1||_2
 <= 2 kappa^(-1/2)
      (sum_i ||[v,pi(c_i)]-1||_2^2)^(1/2).             (PSG4)
```

In particular, a `C`-central selected unitary whose cyclic module is
finite-dimensionally/profinitely continuous is already `A`-central.

For the explicit thin pair

```text
C=<a,b> < A=SL_3(Z)
```

of `explicit-sl3-thin-codense-tau-pair`, hypothesis `(PSG1)` holds by
profinite co-density and Bourgain--Varju expansion.  This is a strictly
smaller sufficient endpoint for thin relative-commutant transfer than weak
ucp-stability: non-tempered congruence summands are allowed, and only the
one cyclic module generated by the proposed stable letter is tested.
