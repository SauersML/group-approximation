Let `(A, n, k)` be given by `free-algebra-elementary-group-is-flexibly-hs-stable`, so that
`E_n(A<x_1..x_k>)` is flexibly HS stable, with `n >= 3` and `k >= 4`. Put `q = 2` if `A = Z`, and
`q = p` if `A = F_p`.

Take `I` generated by:
- `q`, if `A = Z`;
- the Leavitt relations `x_(2+i) x_j - δ_ij` and `x_1 x_3 + x_2 x_4 - 1`;
- the extra generators `x_5, ..., x_k`.

Then `S/I = L_(F_q)(1,2)` is simple and infinite, and `I` is finitely generated. By
`universal-lattice-flexible-stability-forces-nonhyperlinear-group`, item 3 and Variants, the group
`E_n(L_(F_q)(1,2))` is not hyperlinear. It is finitely generated, being a quotient of
`E_n(A<x_1..x_k>)`, so it is a finitely generated non-hyperlinear group.

**Independent failure points.**
- The first prerequisite is established.
- The second fails at `(F_p, n)` as soon as `SL_n(F_p[t])` is shown not to be flexibly HS stable.
  That group is a retract (see its Attempts), and it is the target of the Kun--Thom instability
  routes under `el-r-polynomial-vertex-not-flexibly-hs-stable`.
- It fails at `(Z, n)` as soon as `SL_n(Z)` or `E_n(Z[t])` is shown not to be flexibly HS stable.
- It fails at `(A, n, k)` if some finitely generated ideal `J` of `S = A<x_1..x_k>` has
  `E_n(S)/E_n(S,J)` hyperlinear and not flexibly stable
  (`relative-elementary-quotients-inherit-flexible-hs-stability`).
