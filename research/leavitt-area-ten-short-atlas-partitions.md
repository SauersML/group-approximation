An area-ten trivalent spherical picture has `E=15`, `F=7`, and total face
length `30`.  If its six internal coefficient regions are trivial in

```text
Q=V_4(h_0,h_2)*V_4(k_0,k_1)*F(h_1,k_2),
```

then all seven face lengths are even.  The sole possible digon-free
partition is `(4,4,4,4,4,4,6)`, but no planar cubic map realizes it.
Consequently every such picture contains a gate digon.

Contracting one digon gives an eight-vertex bipartite cubic core.  Restoring
one edge in the complete five-core/equivalently 33-rotation atlas gives
exactly eleven face partitions:

```text
(2,2,2,2,2,10,10)  (2,2,2,2,4,8,10)
(2,2,2,2,6,6,10)   (2,2,2,2,6,8,8)
(2,2,2,4,4,6,10)   (2,2,2,4,4,8,8)
(2,2,2,4,6,6,8)    (2,2,4,4,4,4,10)
(2,2,4,4,4,6,8)    (2,2,4,4,6,6,6)
(2,4,4,4,4,6,6).                                      (ATP1)
```

These are the complete short-atlas area-ten structural targets.
