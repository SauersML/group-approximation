Source: Matthew Gentry Durham, Yair Minsky, and Alessandro Sisto,
*Asymptotically CAT(0) spaces, Z-structures, and the Farrell--Jones
Conjecture*, arXiv:2504.17048v1, Theorem C and Corollary 8.2.

Theorem 8.1 constructs a finitely `F`-amenable action of `G` on a
compact Euclidean retract. Here `F` consists exactly of virtually cyclic
subgroups and subgroups of stabilizers of non-maximal HHS domains.
Corollary 8.2 then applies the Bartels--Bestvina inheritance theorem:
if all those domain stabilizers satisfy Farrell--Jones, then `G` does.

The paper uses "Farrell--Jones" for the K- and L-theoretic conjectures on
group rings. The target deliberately records no A-theoretic conclusion.
QED
