Simultaneously diagonalize the commuting adjoint involutions in one check.
Their product-one relation permits only even-parity sign strings.  On every
such string, a negative sign at coordinate `i` forces a negative sign at some
other coordinate.  Integrate this pointwise indicator inequality against the
spectral measure of `v`.  For `v=Q_j`, the displacement by `Ad(Q_i)` is the
commutator with `Q_i`.
