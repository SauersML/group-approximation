After extending scalars to F_4, split the natural binary boundary
module into the two field-character sectors. The source vectors
k_+(xi,eta)=(u_+(xi)+v_+(xi)) wedge u_+(eta) are fixed by f_0 and
nonzero for nonzero arguments. The target form
J_+(xi,eta)=P_0(v_+(xi) wedge u_+(eta)) is symmetric and alternating.

Write the first coordinate's plus-to-plus block of g as R. The
other field coordinate is fixed. First-coordinate wedges and mixed
central-character terms vanish after P_0, leaving exactly

    P_0[g] k_+(xi,eta)=J_+(xi,R eta).

If R is nonzero, choose eta with R eta nonzero and xi=R eta.
If R is zero, choose any nonzero xi,eta. Either choice gives a
nonzero source kernel. The subgroup's boundary representation and
conditional expectation handle unrestricted proposed left inverses.
The artifact checks every projector identity and provides explicit
binary certificates for the displayed prefix actors.
