**ESTABLISHED** by `normal-generators-non-lo-syllables-proof` (elementary, given
`lo-syllable-relators-keep-free-factors`). Not independently reviewed; no novelty
claimed.

**Terminology.** In a free product `G = A * B`, a *cyclically reduced word of
length `2m`* is `x = a_1 b_1 ... a_m b_m` with `m >= 1`, `a_i ∈ A∖{1}`,
`b_i ∈ B∖{1}`. Its *syllable subgroups* are `A'(x) = <a_1,...,a_m> <= A` and
`B'(x) = <b_1,...,b_m> <= B`.

**Statement.** Let `A` and `B` be groups and suppose `G = A * B = <<w>>`. Let
`p: A -> Ā` and `q: B -> B̄` be onto with `Ā ≠ 1` and `B̄ ≠ 1`, and put
`w̄ = (p * q)(w) ∈ Ā * B̄`. Then:
1. `w̄` is not conjugate into `Ā` or `B̄`;
2. every cyclically reduced conjugate of `w̄` has length `2m >= 2`;
3. for every such conjugate `x`, at least one of `A'(x)`, `B'(x)` is not
   left-orderable.

The case `p = id`, `q = id` applies to `w` itself whenever `A` and `B` are
nontrivial.

**Consequences.**
- **Clause (b) of `normal-rank-one-free-products-need-cyclic-abelianization`,
  for two factors.** If `Ā` and `B̄` are left-orderable, every syllable subgroup
  is left-orderable, which contradicts item 3. So at most one factor has a
  nontrivial left-orderable quotient.
- **Kirby Problem 66** (`torsion-free-free-products-have-normal-rank-above-one`).
  Let `A` and `B` be nontrivial and torsion-free with `A * B = <<w>>`. Take any
  cyclically reduced conjugate `x = a_1 b_1 ... a_m b_m` of `w`.
  - **K1.** One of `A'(x)`, `B'(x)` is a non-left-orderable torsion-free group
    generated by `m` nontrivial elements. It is not cyclic, since an infinite
    cyclic group is left-orderable. So `m >= 2`, and the relator has syllable
    length at least 4.
  - **K2.** The factor containing that syllable subgroup is not left-orderable,
    since a subgroup of a left-orderable group is left-orderable.
  - **K3 (bounded syllable rank).** Suppose every subgroup of `A` and of `B`
    generated by at most `N` elements is left-orderable. Then no relator of
    syllable length at most `2N` normally generates `A * B`.
  - **K4 (quotient-stable form).** The same holds after passing to any
    nontrivial quotients `Ā`, `B̄`: the images of the syllables, after cyclic
    reduction, still generate a non-left-orderable subgroup on one side.
- **Sharpness of K1 as a test.** Torsion-free non-left-orderable groups on two
  generators exist, so the test cannot force `m >= 3`. This is only a remark
  (for example the Promislow group), and no node is required for it.

**What is not claimed.** Item 3 does not say which side is non-orderable, and
it says nothing when one syllable subgroup is orderable and the other is not.
That mixed case contains Klyachko's `B = Z`, exponent sum ±1 situation and is
open in general.
