**Established by citation.**  Theorem 5.5 of Fanizza--Kroell--Mehta--
Paddock--Rochette--Slofstra--Zhao gives the embedding, extension of tracial
states, and the bound `2^16 M^2 ell^2 Lambda`.  The flat-BCS lemma in the same
section gives the intermediate bound `9 M^2 ell^2 Lambda`.  The subsequent
single-conjugacy embedding lemma and its remark give cost `1750` and the
counts 42 variables and 31 linear constraints of arity at most three.

Source: Marco Fanizza et al., *The NPA hierarchy does not always attain the
commuting operator value*, arXiv:2510.04943v4, Section 5.1.
