# Change the X swap to the Z basis and compute its corner deficit

Conjugating the gauged X-basis swap by the scalar Hadamard matrix gives
`(TDS2)`. Matrix multiplication gives `p_0BAp_0=TU`. The functional-calculus
identity `T^*T=I-S^2` gives `(TDS4)`. Faithfulness makes corner unitarity
equivalent to `S=0`, which is precisely the one-path degeneration `(TDS6)`.
