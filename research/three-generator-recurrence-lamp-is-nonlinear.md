For `q,a≥2`, in scalar lamps `ℤ[1/q]^(ℤ)` let `A` be the unit
lamp at zero, `X` the shift, and `T` the automorphism scaling the lamp
at `n` by `q^(a^|n|)`. The group `G_(q,a)=⟨A,X,T⟩` is exactly
`ℤ[1/q]^(ℤ)⋊(M_a⋊ℤ)`, with `M_a` generated by the translates
of `f(n)=a^|n|`. It is torsion-free, has derived length exactly three,
and admits no faithful finite-dimensional representation over any field.
The global profile module `M_a` has infinite rational rank even though
each endpoint recurrence lattice has finite rank.

## Attempts

Artifact Section 7 supplies the proposed complete algebra calculation.
`TAT⁻¹=A^q` and conjugates give all denominators and lamps. The
nonzero commutator of `[X,T]` with `[T,A]` witnesses derived length
three. In a putative linear representation, conjugacy of `A,A^q`
makes a power of `A` unipotent. Positive characteristic contradicts
infinite order; characteristic zero uses `log(A^h)` and infinitely
many eigenvalues `q^(a^n)` for `Ad(T)`. The defect identity
`(aσ²−(a²+1)σ+a)f=(a²−1)δ_(-1)` proves infinite global rank.
These exact calculations are submitted for review as a substantive
open lemma; finite parameter checks do not certify them in all fields
or all dimensions. No novelty claim or complete-proof route is added.
