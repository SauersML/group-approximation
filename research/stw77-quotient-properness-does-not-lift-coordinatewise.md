title: Proper image in a bounded-operator product quotient need not make a chosen lift coordinatewise proper
distinct_from:
  stw77-products-of-bounded-operator-targets-solve-map: that proves the product theorem from coordinatewise essentiality; this shows why quotient properness alone does not supply that premise for a lift.
  stw77-hypotheses-and-stability-descend-to-quotients: stability still descends from a good lifted map; the present claim concerns failure of the lifting inference.
---

There are a quotient of a countable product of bounded-operator algebras and
a unital homomorphism into the quotient whose every nonzero positive image
element is properly infinite, together with a unital homomorphic lift for
which every coordinate map has a nonzero finite-rank positive image.

Thus the coordinatewise proof for products cannot be applied to an arbitrary
lift merely from proper infiniteness in the quotient. This does not give an
STW77 counterexample: the quotient map in the example is O-infinity-stable.
