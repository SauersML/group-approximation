The central idempotents `A,1-A` split `S`. The support relations annihilate
every branch generator on the inactive factor, while coefficient centrality
makes the active factor the tensor product of `AS` with the universal binary
Leavitt algebra. The two explicit mutually inverse maps in `(CMT1)` verify
the decomposition. Tensoring the established affine Wedderburn decomposition
`AS~=M_3(k) direct_sum M_3(k)` with `L_2(k)` gives `(CMT2)`.
