Milhøj–Rørdam, arXiv:2309.17412v2, Theorem 2.3 and the paragraph after it, on
2026-09-11.  Their convention is that "quasitrace" means normalized
2-quasitrace.  The cited proof combines Blackadar–Rørdam's state extension on
preordered semigroups (J. Algebra 152 (1992)) with Blackadar–Handelman,
