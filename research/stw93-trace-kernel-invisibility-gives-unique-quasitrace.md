A quasitrace vanishing on `I` descends to a normalized quasitrace on `D/I`.
The quotient has only its II₁-factor quasitrace, so every normalized
quasitrace on `D` equals the pullback of that quasitrace, namely the limit
trace.  This proves uniqueness once the open ideal-annihilation requirement is
established.
