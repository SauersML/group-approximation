**ESTABLISHED** (literature import, `grigorchuk-group-center-and-out-citation`).

For the first Grigorchuk group `𝔊`:
1. the center of `𝔊` is trivial;
2. `Out(𝔊)` is an elementary abelian 2-group (of infinite rank).

In particular every finitely generated subgroup of `Out(𝔊)` is finite.
