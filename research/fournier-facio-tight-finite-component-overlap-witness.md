For a finite generating set, choose genuinely nonmonomial finite-band
comparison unitaries.  Allow their supports to overlap, but require the
connected components of the joint support graph to be finite with a tight
size distribution under the invariant charge.  Put all remaining errors on
a charge-null reservoir.

This architecture is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-generator-support-components-escape-cutoffs`.  The
support components are already the minimal common reducing blocks for every
comparison word, and every bounded component-size stratum must have charge
zero.  A surviving overlap frame needs positive charge on infinite
components or a dimension-at-infinity finitely additive charge.
