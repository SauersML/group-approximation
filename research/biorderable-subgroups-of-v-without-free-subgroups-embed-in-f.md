title: Every bi-orderable subgroup of Thompson's group V with no non-abelian free subgroup is isomorphic to a subgroup of F
---

For every subgroup `G` of Thompson's group `V` such that `G` is bi-orderable and
contains no non-abelian free subgroup, there is an injective homomorphism
`G → F`.

This is the affirmative answer to
`zaremsky-2-15b-bi-orderable-subgroups-of-v-embed-in-f`.

## Attempts

- 2026-09-13 (z2-15b-biorderable). Necessary conditions a subgroup of `F`
  satisfies, which such a `G` would then have to satisfy: no non-abelian
  nilpotent subgroup, no `BS(1,n)` with `n ≥ 2`, no hyperbolic `Z^2 ⋊_A Z`, and
  no `Z ≀ Z^2`. The first three should also fail in `V` for germ reasons at
  attracting periodic points; `Z ≀ Z^2` inside `V` is the first test case.
  No embedding theorem is proved yet, even for solvable `G` or for subgroups of
  `T`.
