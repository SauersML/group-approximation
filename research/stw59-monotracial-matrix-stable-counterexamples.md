For each nonzero finite abelian group G, the constructed simple unital
nuclear stably finite AH algebra A_G has stable rank exactly two,
a unique trace tau, and satisfies

    U(A_G)/U0(A_G) = G,    K1(A_G)=0,
    pi_k U(M_m(A_G)) = K_(k+1)(A_G)  for every m>=2 and k>=0.

The displayed homotopy identifications are the canonical stable maps.
For the rank sequence r_i=R 2^i, the trace pairing has range
(1/R) Z[1/2]. Every M_m(A_G), m>=2, is K-stable. Consequently
A_G is a full unital corner of a unital simple K-stable algebra even
though A_G is not K1-injective.

This strengthens LIX, without resolving an additional named problem
or computing the higher unstable kernels at matrix size one.
