* K.-U. Bux, C. Llosa Isenrich, X. Wu, *On the Boone--Higman Conjecture for
  groups acting on locally finite trees*, arXiv:2408.05673v2, HTML rendering
  (https://arxiv.org/html/2408.05673v2). Read: the statements of Theorem B,
