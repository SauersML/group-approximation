title: Every finitely presented group embeds in the derived subgroup of a finitely presented group
---

Every finitely presented group H embeds in [T,T] for some finitely
presented group T. This is Chatterji--Kassabov Lemma 6; the canonical
route gives its finite amalgam construction explicitly.
