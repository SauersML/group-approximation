Choose real `x_s` such that

```text
a_r=sum_s exponent_s(r)x_s,
A=max_r|a_r|>0.
```

For any input defect `delta_j->0`, choose positive `t_j->0` with
`delta_j/t_j->0`, for example `t_j=sqrt(delta_j)+1/j`. Put
`W_j(s)=exp(i t_j x_s)U_j(s)`. Since scalar factors commute with all
matrices, the defining residual is exactly

```text
W_j(r)-I=(exp(i t_j a_r)-1)I
           +exp(i t_j a_r)(U_j(r)-I).                   (SPP1)
```

If `Delta_j=max_r||W_j(r)-I||_2`, the triangle and reverse triangle
inequalities give

```text
|Delta_j-max_r|exp(i t_j a_r)-1|| <= delta_j,
Delta_j/t_j -> A.
```

Hence `Delta_j` is positive for all sufficiently large `j`, tends to
zero, and `(SPP1)` implies

```text
max_r ||(W_j(r)-I)/Delta_j-i(a_r/A)I||_2 -> 0.          (SPP2)
```

These residuals are uniformly square-integrable. To see this directly,
if `X_j` is 2-norm close to `Y_j` with `||Y_j||_op<=C`, the best
2-norm approximation to `X_j` in the operator-norm ball of radius `C`
is its singular-value truncation there. On singular values greater
than `2C`, the singular value is at most twice its excess over `C`.
Thus

```text
||X_j 1_(|X_j|>2C)||_2 <= 2||X_j-Y_j||_2.
```

One may enlarge `C` to a positive constant. Applying this to `(SPP2)`
proves the claimed uniform integrability.

The generator displacement satisfies

```text
max_s ||W_j(s)-U_j(s)||_2 <= t_j max_s |x_s| -> 0.      (SPP3)
```

Any exact correction of `W_j` consequently corrects `U_j` by the
triangle inequality. The same reasoning works after flexible padding or
restriction: compression cannot increase unnormalized HS norm, and the
normalizing dimensions have ratio tending to one. Conversely unrestricted
stability applies to its uniformly integrable subclass. Fixed-word traces
are also unchanged asymptotically, by word telescoping and
`|tr_d(X)|<=||X||_2`.

For the finite-abelianization assertion, abelianizing the presentation
gives `Z^S` modulo the subgroup generated by the exponent-sum vectors of
`R`. If the quotient is finite, those vectors span `Q^S`; when `S` is
nonempty at least one is nonzero.

For an arbitrary presentation, use `P^+=<S,z|R,z=1>`. Given the original
tuple, retain every old generator and put `W_j(z)=exp(i t_j)I`, choosing
`t_j` as above. The new defect is

```text
Delta_j=max(delta_j,|exp(i t_j)-1|),
Delta_j/t_j -> 1.
```

Each old defining residual divided by `Delta_j` tends to zero in
2-norm. The residual for `z=1` divided by `Delta_j` tends to `iI`.
The preceding bounded-approximation argument proves uniform
integrability. Exact correction for `P^+` restricts to exact correction
for `P`, with the same dimension budget.

For the reverse implication, start with any approximate tuple for
`P^+`. Unrestricted stability for `P` corrects its old generators.
Extend the correcting representation by sending `z` to the identity.
The additional generator has error `||W_j(z)-I||_2->0`, including after
the same flexible padding. Thus unrestricted stability for `P` implies
unrestricted, and hence uniformly integrable input, stability for `P^+`.
This proves the general equivalence without any assumption on the old
relators' exponent sums.
