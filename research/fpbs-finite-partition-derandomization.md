Section 2. For disjoint finite orbit-coordinate sets P_i in an essentially free action, and q,eta>0, one can select a measurable A with mu(A)+mu(D(A)) <= q+product_i(1-q^|P_i|)+eta. No extension of the action or infinite independent labeling is used.
