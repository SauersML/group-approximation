From `dp=pd`, both `up` and `udp` have initial projection `p`, and

```text
(udp)(udp)^*=udpd^*u^*=upu^*.
```

The relative product is

```text
(up)^*(udp)=pu^*udp=pdp.
```

Squaring gives the central-square formula.  The approximate assertion is
the standard polar correction of two paths which use the same corrected
outgoing section.
