The inputs are the ancillary files `census52.json` (the 52 supports) and
`box_b4_syllable_intersect.json` (the 155-element box) of arXiv:2608.00103v1.
The group model is `(s, t)(s', t') = (ss', t + s t')` with
`a = ((1,-1,-1),(1,1,0))` and `b = ((-1,1,-1),(0,1,1))`, and
`promislow-radius4-census.py` asserts both relators.

**Step 1: seven pairs.** Mode `orbits` checks that the 52 supports are
21-element subsets of `B(4)`. Of the 16 substitutions sending `a` and `b` to
generators, exactly 8 satisfy both relators and are injective on `B(4)`:
`a -> a^(+-1), b -> b^(+-1)` and `a -> b^(+-1), b -> a^(+-1)`. Each is an
automorphism of `P`. They split the 52 supports into 7 orbits, of sizes
`8, 8, 8, 8, 8, 8, 4`. An automorphism `phi` sends a unit `u` with inverse
`v` to `phi(u)` with inverse `phi(v)`. So it identifies the units on `(S, T)`
with the units on `(phi S, phi T)` over every coefficient ring, and one
representative per orbit suffices. For each representative, `T` comes from
solving `u_0 v = 1` over `F_2` with `v` supported in the box. The solution is
unique there, and the script checks it by convolution in `F_2[P]`.

**Step 2: the case split.** Write `S = (g_0, ..., g_20)` in the script's
order, `u = sum u_i g_i` and `v = sum v_j h_j` with `h_j in T`. Suppose
`u v = 1` and `u` is not `lambda g`. Let `i < j` be the first two indices
with `u_i, u_j != 0`. Scale `u` by `u_i^(-1)` and `v` by `u_i`, and put
`w = u_j^(-1)`. Then `(u, v, w)` is a `K`-point of the ideal `I_ij`
generated by the 121 coefficients of `u v - 1`, the `u_k` with `k < i` or
`i < k < j`, `u_i - 1` and `u_j w - 1`. Conversely, every point of `I_ij` is
a nontrivial unit on the pair.

Mode `cases k QQ 1800 inv` computes a basis of each of the 210 ideals `I_ij`
with Singular `slimgb` over `Q`. For all seven representatives, every case
except `(0,1)` gives a basis containing a nonzero constant, and no case
reaches the time limit. A nonzero constant in `I_ij` over `Q` rules out
points over every field of characteristic zero.

**Step 3: case `(0,1)`.** `promislow-radius4-census-points.py` substitutes
`u_0 = 1`. For each representative, `I_01` is zero-dimensional of length 16.
Let `cp` be the characteristic polynomial of multiplication by a random
linear form `L` on `Q[u, v, w]/I_01`. Its roots are the values of `L` at the
points of `I_01` over `Qbar`, each counted with its local length. For every
representative, `cp` has degree 16, is squarefree, has no real root, is a
product of four irreducible quartics over `Q` and splits into linear factors
over `Q(zeta_8)`. So `I_01` has 16 distinct points and `L` separates them.
Also `1, L, ..., L^15` is a basis of `Q[u, v, w]/I_01`, so each coordinate is
a polynomial in `L` with rational coefficients. Every point is defined over
`Q(zeta_8)`.

**Step 4: the points, checked with the group law.**
`promislow-radius4-census-verify.py` recomputes the product table and the
trivial pairs from the group law and compares them with `census_pairs.json`.
It writes each coordinate as a polynomial in `L`, evaluates it at the 16 roots
of `cp` in `Q(zeta_8)`, and multiplies each resulting `u` and `v` out in
`Q(zeta_8)[P]`. All 16 points give `u v = 1` with `u_1 != 0`. So every field
containing `zeta_8` has a nontrivial unit on the pair. For `j = 3, 5, 7`, the
automorphism `zeta_8 -> zeta_8^j` moves some coordinate of each point, so the
coordinates of each point generate `Q(zeta_8)`.

Let `u` be a nontrivial unit on the pair over `K`. By Step 2, its normalized
point lies in `I_01` and has coordinates in `K`. By Steps 3 and 4 it is one
of the 16 points, so `K` contains `Q(zeta_8)`. Over `Qbar` the nontrivial
units up to scaling are these 16 points. QED

**Structure of the 16 points.** Not needed for the proof.
`promislow-radius4-census-twists.py` checks that the abelianization map
`P -> Z/4 + Z/4` is additive on all products of two elements of `S u T`. A
character `chi: P -> mu_4` then sends a unit `u = sum u_g g` on the pair to
the unit `sum chi(g) u_g g` on the same pair. For each representative, the 16
points are the 16 pairwise distinct twists of one point, and that point has
all its coefficients in `mu_8`. So every nontrivial unit on a census pair is,
up to scaling, a twist of one unit with coefficients in `mu_8`. The Galois
automorphism `zeta_8 -> zeta_8^5` acts on the points as the twist by the sign
character `g -> (-1)^(A(g) + B(g))`.

**Trust surface.** `slimgb` over `Q` is exact. The emptiness of the other 209
cases per representative rests on Singular finding a constant in the basis,
with no cofactor certificate. The length 16 and `cp` rest on the basis of
Step 3. The 16 points are checked by multiplication in `Q(zeta_8)[P]`,
independently of any Groebner basis. The list of 52 supports and its
completeness come from Tabei's census. The orbits and the inverse supports
are recomputed here.
