Literature derivation. Source: L. Chen and Y. Lodha, *The Wiegold problem and
free products of left-orderable groups*, arXiv:2510.26073 v2, file `wiegold.tex`,
read on MSI (`~/wiegold-lane/cl/wiegold.tex`) on 2026-09-13. TeX macros are
stripped from the quotes. Their input theorems are imported, not reproved.

**Pins.**
- **l.286:** "A group admits a left-order if and only if it admits a right-order."
- **l.313:** "If we express w as a cyclically reduced word a_1b_1⋯a_nb_n, Theorem
  [spectral gap theorem] holds under the weaker assumption that the finitely
  generated subgroups A′=⟨a_1,…,a_n⟩ and B′=⟨b_1,…,b_n⟩ are right-orderable."
- **l.685–690, Theorem `thm: spectral gap from stacking`:** "For any free product
  G=A⋆B and a cyclically reduced word w=a_1b_1…a_nb_n∈A⋆B with n∈Z_+,
  a_i∈A∖{id} and b_i∈B∖{id}. If there is a relative stacking of w in G′=A′⋆B′,
  where A′ (resp. B′) is the subgroup generated by a_1,…,a_n (resp. b_1,…,b_n),
  then for any boundary-incompressible w-admissible surface S in G, we have
  −χ^−(S)≥deg(S)."
- **l.931–938, Theorem `thm:StackingsRF`:** "Let A,B be countable right-orderable
  groups and G=A⋆B. Consider a cyclically reduced word w=a_1b_1…a_nb_n∈G,
  a_i∈A∖{id}, b_i∈B∖{id}. Assume that w is not a proper power. Then there exists
  an action σ:G→Homeo^+(R) and some x∈R such that Ω(w,x) is stable." A stable
  trajectory is by definition a relative stacking (Definition `def: relative
  stacking`, l.330–345).
- **Example `example: equations in groups` (l.448–463).** For `a ∈ A∖{id}` and `w`
  not conjugate into `A` or `B`, `a ∈ <<w>>` exactly when
  `a = (g_1 w^{n_1} g_1^{-1})⋯(g_k w^{n_k} g_k^{-1})` with `n_i ≠ 0`. Each such
  equation gives a `w`-admissible surface: a sphere with `k+1` boundary
  components, of degree `Σ|n_i|`, which is boundary-incompressible when `k` is
  minimal.
- **l.1456–1466:** the proof of Theorem B from the spectral gap theorem, which the
  steps below repeat.

**Standard fact used** (conjugacy theorem for free products, Lyndon–Schupp
Ch. IV; not re-read here). Two cyclically reduced elements of length at least 2
are conjugate only if one is a cyclic permutation of the other.

**Step 1 (reduce to a relator that is not a proper power).** Let `u` be a root of
`w` that is not a proper power, `w = u^k`, `k >= 1`.
- Choose a cyclically reduced conjugate `v` of `u`. It has length at least 2,
  because `w` is not conjugate into a factor. Then `v^k` is cyclically reduced:
  `v` begins and ends in different factors, so no cancellation happens at the
  seams.
- `v^k` is conjugate to `w`, so by the standard fact `w` is a cyclic permutation
  of `v^k`. So the letters of `w` are the letters of `v` up to rotation, and
  `A'(v) = A'`, `B'(v) = B'`. Both are left-orderable.
- `<<w>> = <<v^k>> ⊆ <<v>>`, so injectivity for `v` implies injectivity for `w`.
- `v` is not a proper power in `G` (a root of `v` conjugates to a root of `u`).
  So it is not a proper power in `A'(v) * B'(v)` either.

Rename `v` as `w`, which is now cyclically reduced of length `2n >= 2` and not a
proper power.

**Step 2 (relative stacking in `G' = A' * B'`).** `A'` and `B'` are finitely
generated, hence countable, and they are right-orderable by l.286. `w` is a
cyclically reduced word in `A' * B'` with letters `a_i ∈ A'∖{id}`,
`b_i ∈ B'∖{id}`, and it is not a proper power there. Theorem `thm:StackingsRF`
gives a relative stacking of `w` in `G'`.

**Step 3 (spectral gap for surfaces in `G`).** By Theorem `thm: spectral gap from
stacking`, every boundary-incompressible `w`-admissible surface `S` in `G`
satisfies `−χ^−(S) >= deg(S)`.

**Step 4 (injectivity of `A`).** Suppose `a ∈ A∖{id}` lies in `<<w>>`. Take an
equation as in the Example with `k >= 1` minimal. The corresponding surface `S`
is a connected sphere with `k+1 >= 2` boundary components. So it is not a disk
or a sphere, `−χ^−(S) = −χ(S) = k−1`, and `deg(S) = Σ|n_i| >= k`. By step 3,
`k−1 >= deg(S) >= k`, a contradiction. Hence `A -> G/<<w>>` is injective.

**Step 5 (injectivity of `B`).** The hypotheses are symmetric in `A` and `B`. The
rotation `b_1 a_2 b_2 ⋯ a_n b_n a_1` is a cyclically reduced conjugate with the
same syllable subgroups, and Steps 2–4 apply with the roles exchanged.

**Step 6 (normal generation).** `A` and `B` are nontrivial because `n >= 1`, so
`G/<<w>>` contains a nontrivial subgroup. ∎

**Trust surface.**
- Theorems `thm: spectral gap from stacking` and `thm:StackingsRF` (§5–§6 of the
  preprint) are imported.
- The claim that Example `example: equations in groups` produces
  boundary-incompressible surfaces at minimal `k` is imported.
- The conjugacy theorem for free products is standard and was not re-read.
