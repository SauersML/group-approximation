**ESTABLISHED.** Let `psi : B -> M_d(B)` be a unital homomorphism and `O_psi`
the completion of `nekrashevych-completions-contain-their-coefficients`. Let `I`
be an ideal of `B` with `psi^-1(M_d(I)) = I`. Then the ideal `J` of `O_psi`
generated by `I` satisfies `J ∩ B = I`. In particular, if `0 != I != B`, then
`O_psi` is not simple.

## Consequences

- **Simplicity needs data specific to `B`.** For `psi(b) = b I_d`, every ideal
  is saturated and invariant, so `B ⊗ L_d` is simple only when `B` is. Any
  recursion defined naturally in `B` descends to quotients `B/I` and produces
  such ideals.
- **What a simple completion must do.** The saturated invariant closure of
  every nonzero ideal of `B` must be `B`. For an infinite simple input
  `S <= B^x`, the closure of `{s - 1}` must reach `1`.
