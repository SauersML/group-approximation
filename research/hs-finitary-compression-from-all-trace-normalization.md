## Attribution

Jihao Liu, [*Nonhyperlinear groups exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf) (2026-09-20), Theorems 6.7 and 7.4. This node records or conditionally uses Liu's result; it does not claim that analytic contribution for Cairn. Andreas Thom has priority for the conditional implication from the universal centralizer theorem to normalization; Liu explicitly credits it.

Specialize the all-trace theorem to the Theorem E pair and its two positive compressors. The recorded finitary equivalence identifies normalization of the unitary centralizer in every ultraproduct with the target's dimension-uniform inequality. Algebra normalization implies unitary-centralizer normalization. Negating the finite-window inequality would give an exact quotient homomorphism and a commuting unitary whose transported image does not commute, contradicting the premise. No trace restriction may be inserted into this argument.
