Paddock--Slofstra Lemma 5.7 identifies tracial satisfiability of a linear
system with nontriviality of the central solution-group involution `J`, and
`R^U`-satisfiability with nontriviality of `J` in approximate representations.
Their Proposition 5.8 then proves in both directions that a tracial/non-`R^U`
linear-system gap exists exactly when a finitely presented nonhyperlinear group
exists.  The cited artifact records the source location and the reduction.
