For an involution acting on `G/H`, every nonfixed orbit is a two-cycle and
contributes one negative eigenvector, proving `(HCQ1)`.  In the regular module
a nonidentity involution has no fixed points, giving `(HCQ2)`.  If a finite
edge identifies `H` with `L`, conjugation transports the subgroup averages
`p_H` and `p_L`; normalized trace is conjugation invariant and `|H|=|L|`, so
all matched character-idempotent ranks agree.  Therefore an untyped coset cut
can display quarter rank only representation-dependently, while every
authenticated finite-edge transport preserves the selector rank.  These are
the two exhaustive branches claimed in the target.
