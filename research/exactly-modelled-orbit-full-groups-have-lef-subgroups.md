**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).**

**Setting.**
- `Λ` is a countable group acting essentially freely by measure-preserving automorphisms of `(X, μ)`.
- `𝒜` is a countable `Λ`-invariant algebra of measurable sets.
- `W_𝒜(Λ ↷ X)` is the group of Borel bijections `g(x) = c_g(x)·x` with `c_g : X -> Λ` of finite range and level sets in
  `𝒜`.

**Exact models.** For every finite symmetric `F ∋ e` in `Λ`, finite subalgebra `𝒜_0 ⊆ 𝒜` and `ε > 0`, there are:
- a finite set `E` and `σ : F·F -> Sym(E)`, exactly multiplicative on `F × F`, with `|Fix σ(λ)| <= ε|E|` for `λ ≠ e`;
- a Boolean algebra homomorphism `β` on the algebra generated by `F·𝒜_0`, with `β(λA) = σ(λ)β(A)` exactly, and
  `|β(A)|/|E|` within `ε` of `μ(A)` on `𝒜_0`.

**Theorem.** If `Λ ↷ X` has exact models, every finitely generated `Γ ≤ W_𝒜(Λ ↷ X)` with `μ{gx ≠ x} > 0` for `g ≠ 1` is
LEF.

**Instances with exact models.**
1. Profinite actions `Λ ↷ lim Λ/Λ_k` of residually finite groups. For their topological full groups the stronger
   conclusion, residual finiteness, is `equicontinuous-action-full-group-fg-subgroups-are-rf`.
2. Bernoulli shifts `A^Λ` of LEF groups, modelled on `A^Q × Q` over finite partial models `Q` of balls of `Λ`, with
   cylinder sets.
3. The dual actions of `G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ Z)` on the Pontryagin dual of `LC(X × {1,2,3}, F_2)`, through its finite
   linear models `SL_(3N)(F_2)` acting on `F_2^(3N)`.

**Consequence.** Orbit full groups make groups sofic beyond LEF only through actions whose finite models are
approximately, not exactly, equivariant. With `kazhdan-subgroups-of-amenable-orbit-full-groups-are-rf`, a non-LEF
Kazhdan group obtained this way needs a non-amenable, non-LEF sofic acting group, for example a Bernoulli shift of
`BS(2,3) × SL_3(Z)`. This is target T1 of the artifact for `sofic-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-sofic-groups`.

**Model test.**
- **Odometer `Z`-actions:** exact models, and their full groups are residually finite, so LEF.
- **Bernoulli shift of `BS(2,3)`:** `BS(2,3)` itself lies in the full group and is not LEF, so this action has no exact
  models. That is consistent: exact models would make `BS(2,3)` LEF.
- **Essential freeness is used.** The injectivity step reads `{c_g ≠ e}` as the moved set.
