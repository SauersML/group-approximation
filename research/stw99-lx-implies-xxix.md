STW (footnote after Problem `q:K1SurjectRR0`, attributed to the
referee): if a simple unital infinite RR0 algebra `A` contains a
finite projection `p`, then `pAp` is unital simple RR0 and finite, so
by Ara--Goodearl--O'Meara--Pardo (Thm 7.6, using separativity = stable
weak cancellation, supplied by part (1) of the premise applied to the
stabilization) `pAp` is STABLY finite; but
`pAp ⊗ K ≅ A ⊗ K` by simplicity (Brown), which contains an infinite
projection — contradiction.  So under LX(1), every simple RR0 algebra
is stably finite or has only infinite projections, i.e. is purely
infinite (infinite projection in every hereditary subalgebra via RR0).
