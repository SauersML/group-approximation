Let `Q` be as in `sofic-tf-hyperbolic-kazhdan-group-with-full-mf-radical`. It is nontrivial,
torsion-free and Kazhdan. It is finitely presented, being word-hyperbolic. It is sofic, and it
satisfies `Rad_MF(Q) = Q`.

By part 2 of `finite-outer-order-radical-witnesses-cross-alekseev-thom`, `(Q × Z, Q × 0)` is a
witness of `(TFZ1)`: `Q × Z` is finitely presented, torsion-free and sofic, and
`Rad_MF(Q × Z) = Q × 0` with quotient `Z`. The direct verification of every clause except soficity
is Part 3 of `non-mf-tf-hyperbolic-gives-fp-full-radical-kazhdan-group-proof`.

**Preferred strengthening.** The kernel `Q × 0` is finitely presented. It is normally generated by
one element whenever `Q` is, which holds for every `Q` produced by
`non-mf-tf-hyperbolic-gives-fp-full-radical-kazhdan-group`, part (b).

**Region.** The witness lies in region (AT) of FO-AT, and the automorphism is the identity. So this
route is a pure Alekseev--Thom 6.1 instance, like the defect-core and Titz--Witzel routes. The
difference is that its only open input is about word-hyperbolic groups.
