## 4. Full-(C) covariance

Let (C) be the abelian Pauli torus whose selected character atoms are
the (e_a), and write (chi_a) for the character of (e_a).  For
(cin C),

[
 ce_b=chi_b(c)e_b,qquad e_ac=chi_a(c)e_a.            	ag{11}

[

 B_{y,b;x,a}c=chi_a(c)B_{y,b;x,a}.                    	ag{12}

Equivalently,

[
 cB_{y,b;x,a}c^{-1}
 =chi_b(c)overline{chi_a(c)}B_{y,b;x,a}.             	ag{13}

The routers may be completely nonnormal relative to (C); the two
outer Fourier atoms make (12)--(13) automatic.  This is the required
full-(C), rather than merely (h)-typed, covariance.

The coefficient is also genuinely off-diagonal in occurrence space.
Its middle term is

[

After conjugating the left and right ends back to their context
coordinates, (14) is exactly the cross-context product in (10).  It is
not an element of the diagonal algebra generated by one occurrence PVM
unless the two context tables already reduce together.

## 7. Exact remaining raw-word gate

The present theorem reaches group-algebra zero compression, not yet an
ordinary relator.  Each (e_a) is a fixed finite Fourier polynomial,
so (8) is a fixed finite linear combination of ordinary words.  But the
condition (B=0) is not itself a group equation.

A successful Leavitt continuation must use the **same** middle
occurrence (14).  More precisely, it must provide a bounded ordinary
word package in which:

1. a positive and a negative raw Leavitt letter remain uncancelled;
2. the outer source and range coefficients remain the full-(C) atoms
   (e_a,e_b);
3. ordinary relator defect controls (|B|_2), or controls the
   polar on the spectral corner (28); and
4. the unused complement of (e_r) stays free enough for the exact
   dilation (17)--(22).

Multiplying (8) by a raw unitary from a commuting tensor factor does not
help: it cancels from (B^*B) and is a removable multiplicity gauge.
Likewise replacing (14) by independent per-check routers destroys the
one common decoded strategy.

The shared-multiplicity commutator theorem shows how to cancel a common
finite-packet gauge after a selected type is available.  Equations
(8)--(31) now supply that type and its exact Gram state.  What remains is
strictly the one-sided ordinary-word return of (14) through the raw
(S/T) actor.  No further qutrit robustness or occurrence-rank lemma is
missing.
