distinct_from:

---

Suppose there are a simple unital infinite C-star algebra `B` and a unital
embedding

```text
phi:M_(2^infinity) -> B
```

whose range contains a nonzero finite projection, as requested in STW
Problem LXVI.  Then there are an admissible domain and target for Problem
LXXVIII for which the map is not `O_infinity`-stable.

More precisely, one can take the domain to be the CAR algebra and the target
to be a simple unital finite C-star algebra with no normalized quasitraces.
Consequently:

```text
a positive answer to LXVI  ==>  a negative answer to LXXVIII.
```

Equivalently, a positive answer to LXXVIII would force a negative answer to
LXVI.
