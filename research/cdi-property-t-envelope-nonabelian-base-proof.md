In the proof of Theorem A(1) of Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana
([arXiv:2205.07442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07442)), the authors set

```text
M_tilde = (M * L(F_2)) tensor R
```

and use Proposition 3.8 to obtain a cocycle action `(beta,w)` of a countable
group `C` on `M_tilde^C`.  The target is explicitly

```text
Q := M_tilde^C rtimes_(beta,w) C.
```

It contains `M`, and a representation of a property-`(T)` group generates
`Q`, but `M_tilde^C` is a nonabelian tensor-product factor.  No compact
abelian group, Haar base, or Fourier identification with a semidirect-product
group factor occurs.  Remark 1.1 separately says that embedding universality
of the family `{L(G)}` is open, confirming that "generated by a group
representation" in Theorem A is not being asserted to mean "is a group von
Neumann algebra with its canonical trace."  The relation version likewise
provides a relation-factor inclusion, not an algebraic-action realization.
