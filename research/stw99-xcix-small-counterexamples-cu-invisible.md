title: Any XCIX counterexample below radius 1/6422957 is invisible to scaled Cu

If `A` is Z-stable, `B` is not Z-stable, and

```text
d(A,B) < 1/6422957,
```

then `(Cu(A),Sigma(A))` and `(Cu(B),Sigma(B))` are nevertheless isomorphic.
Thus neither Cu, its scale, nor any property invariant under scaled-Cu
isomorphism can witness such a counterexample.

This is an obstruction to a proposed counterexample mechanism, not an
existence assertion.
