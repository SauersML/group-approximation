title: Apply the uniform Eckhardt--Wu bound along a subgroup exhaustion

Enumerate `G` and let `G_n` be generated by its first `n` elements.  Each
`G_n` is virtually polycyclic with `h(G_n)<=r`.  In particular every `G_n` is
amenable, and amenability passes to their directed union `G`.

Eckhardt--Wu, [*Nuclear dimension and virtually polycyclic
groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07223), Theorem A (Theorems 5.4 and 5.9),
give a finite nuclear-dimension bound for `C*(H)` which is uniform among
virtually polycyclic groups of the same Hirsch length.  Taking the maximum of
their finitely many bounds for Hirsch lengths `0,1,...,r` gives a finite
constant `N(r)` with

```text
dim_nuc(C*(G_n))<=N(r)       for every n.
```

Now `stw84-directed-union-uniform-bound` gives
`dim_nuc(C*(G))<=N(r)`.  The argument uses neither finite generation nor a
global finite-index polycyclic subgroup.
