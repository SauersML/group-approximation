Uniform property Γ of a C\*-algebra with compact nonempty trace space
is a property of its uniform tracial completion as a tracially
complete algebra (CCEGSTW: `A` has uniform Γ iff
`(\bar A^{T(A)}, T(A))` has property Γ, since the uniform tracial
ultrapowers agree).  Problem XX (established: Vaccaro) gives uniform Γ
for the Villadsen first-type algebras, so their completions have Γ; if
Problem XXXV identifies the Giol--Kerr completions with such
completions, the Giol--Kerr algebras inherit uniform Γ.
