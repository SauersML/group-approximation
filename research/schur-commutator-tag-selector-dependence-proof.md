In a class-two Schur--Clifford packet every basic commutator of noncentral
packet generators belongs to the elementary-abelian central group generated
by `J,z_1,...,z_k`.  Commutator bilinearity modulo that center therefore puts
the commutator of any two fixed packet words in the same group.  Since all
central generators are involutions, this gives the unique form `(SCT1)` after
discarding zero exponents.

Conjugate `(SCT1)` by `u` and apply `(SCT2)`:

```text
T_1
 =uK_a u^*
 =u(J^c product_i z_i^(r_i))u^*
 =J^c product_i (z'_i)^(r_i).
```

This is `(SCT3)`.  Thus the target subgroup generated by the transported
selectors and `T_1` has the same rank as the selector subgroup; it is not the
direct product of that subgroup with a new tag bit.  The two alternatives in
the claim simply record whether the selector generators are or are not part
of the transported edge subgroup.
