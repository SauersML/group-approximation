Let `rho_2(g)=rho_4(g) tensor I_2` be the repeated natural representation of
`A8=GL4(2)` on `F2^8`.  In row-byte little-endian hexadecimal convention put

```text
U = 401004080301c030.                                  (M2E1)
```

The two marked charts `rho_2` and `g -> U^-1 rho_2(g) U` satisfy every H6 and
H18 directed packet context exactly, and collision `19243` evaluates to the
identity.  But for the hub transvection `b=t23`,

```text
h=(U^-1 rho_2(b) U) rho_2(b)
 =8040a05058440201
```

has exact order four.  In particular the two marked chart maps differ.
Relation q14 is nonidentity on the witness, with value
`8040201048040201`.

Let `G` be the finite subgroup of `GL8(2)` generated by `U` and the first
chart, and pass to `Reg(G)`, using `lambda(U^-1)` as the common relative frame.
Restriction to every packet A4 is a direct sum of regular A4 representations,
so this is an exact-regular context network with `eta=0`; the literal mixed
context representations allow every graph stable letter to be the identity.
Collision defect is zero, while the hub opcode is the regular unitary
`lambda(h)` for `h!=1`.
Therefore

```text
||lambda(h)-1||_2=sqrt(2).                             (M2E2)
```

The right side of `(A4-STAR-2)` is zero and its left side is `sqrt(2)`.
Hence no finite constant `C_col` exists for the stated packet-plus-collision
interface.  The failure is finite-dimensional and CE-sensitive escape is not
needed.

The same frame also moves both raw-S3 generators `t21,t12` by nonidentity
order-four opcodes.  Therefore its regularization refutes the stronger
high-level ceiling `atlas-a4-holonomy-coherence-gap`; that calculation is
recorded separately in
`atlas-multiplicity-two-refutes-holonomy-coherence-gap`.
