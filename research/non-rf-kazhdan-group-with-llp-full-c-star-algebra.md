OPEN CLAIM.  There is a finitely generated group `Gamma` with Kazhdan's
property (T), not residually finite, such that `C^*(Gamma)` has
Kirchberg's local lifting property.

By `llp-non-rf-kazhdan-group-is-non-hyperlinear` such a `Gamma` is not
hyperlinear, so this claim alone reaches the goal
(`non-hyperlinear-from-llp-non-rf-kazhdan`).  Its negation is equally
interesting: it says every non-residually-finite Kazhdan group fails LLP,
a sweeping extension of Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma.

## Attempts

- **Fences (Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma arXiv:2006.01874, abstract level).**
  `C^*(SL_n(Z))`, `n >= 3`, and `C^*(Z^2 rtimes SL_2(Z))` fail LLP; the
  (stronger) lifting property LP fails for every Kazhdan `Gamma` with
  `H^2(Gamma, R) != 0` or `H^2(Gamma, Z Gamma) != 0`, for every Kazhdan group
  admitting a p.m.p. action with non-vanishing second real cohomology, and
  for every non-finitely-presented Kazhdan group.  Since LLP passes to
  subgroups (`llp-non-rf-kazhdan-group-is-non-hyperlinear`), a candidate
  must avoid all these as subgroups; in particular Olshanskii-type
  infinitely presented quotients are fenced at the LP level and their LLP
  is open only because LLP is weaker than LP.
- **Flagged candidates: simple Kac--Moody lattices**
  (`simple-kazhdan-kac-moody-lattices-exist`, source-verified Caprace--Remy
  corollary: 2-spherical generalized Cartan matrix, `q > 1764^n` gives
  finitely presented simple Kazhdan `Lambda/Z(Lambda)`; explicit instance
  the `(2,4,6)` matrix `(KM2)`).  Compact-hyperbolic types have no affine
  sub-Kac--Moody subgroups, so the obvious `Z^2 rtimes SL_2(Z)`-type
  relative-(T) subgroups are absent; `H^2(Gamma, R)` for these lattices is
  the first thing to compute (if nonzero, LP fails and only LLP survives).
  By `kac-moody-lattice-is-non-hyperlinear-or-llp-failure` each of them is
  a forced witness: non-hyperlinear, or an LLP failure of a new kind.
  These groups are generated by finitely many finite root subgroups, so
  `C^*(Gamma)` is a quotient of the free product of finite-dimensional
  algebras `C^*(*_i U_(alpha_i))`, which HAS LLP (Pisier: LLP is preserved
  by free products); LLP is not preserved by quotients in general, so the
  question is whether the Kac--Moody relations destroy it.
- **Thom's groups are on the wrong side.**  Thom's hyperlinear Kazhdan
  groups without the factorization property (Groups Geom. Dyn. 4 (2010))
  are non-RF Kazhdan and hyperlinear, hence by the theorem their full
  C*-algebras FAIL LLP: every such example is a new LLP counterexample,
  and the construction principle behind them (central extensions /
  hyperlinear profiles) is a source of fences, not candidates.
- **No known positive LLP theorem reaches a Kazhdan group.**  LLP is known
  for nuclear algebras, `C^*(F)` (Kirchberg), free products and amalgams
  over finite-dimensional subalgebras, and is inherited by quotients only
  under lifting of the kernel; every one of these mechanisms produces
  groups without (T).  Any positive answer needs a new LLP permanence
  statement for quotients of free products of finite groups, i.e. a
  C*-algebraic input, not a stability input -- which is the point of this
  node.
