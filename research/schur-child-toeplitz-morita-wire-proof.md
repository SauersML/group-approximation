On an active sector, the full Schur matrix has one extra hyperbolic plane.
Thus `T=S tensor C^2`, the old packet `A_f` acts as `End(S) tensor I_2`, and
the two new generators act as a Pauli `P,Q` on `C^2`. After adding external
multiplicity `M`, the double-commutant theorem gives

```text
Comm(A_f)=M_2 tensor End(M).
```

Adjoining only `P` cuts the relative factor to `Comm(P)=MASA_P`; adjoining
only `Q` gives `MASA_Q`; adjoining both cuts it to the scalars. This is
`(STM2)`. The two Pauli MASAs generate `M_2`, proving `(STM3)`.

The Toeplitz packet embedding puts the entire `Q` child in
`EL_r(sigma(T))` and leaves the transverse `P` generator in the finite head.
The tail-plus-swap theorem says the unital ring generated by `sigma(T)` and
one fixed involutive head swap is all of `T`. Hence the missing head
coefficient, and therefore the transverse child chart, is a fixed word in the
tail and one actuator. This realizes the exact return on the relative
`M_2` multiplicity coordinate without identifying nonisomorphic packet
subgroups by conjugacy.
