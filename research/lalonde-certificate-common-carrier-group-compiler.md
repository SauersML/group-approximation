Construct a finite marked group presentation `(Gamma_L,J)` and a finite
word realization of Lalonde's effect constraints with both properties:

1. the explicit perfect local-dimension-six **vector-state** strategy
   produces an exact representation in which `J != 1`; and
2. every separated canonical normalized-HS microstate for `(Gamma_L,J)`
   produces, on one common carrier, a finite-matrix **tracial** tuple whose
   algebra residual and losing energy tend to zero.

Property 2 contradicts
`lalonde-certificate-gives-dimension-free-tracial-gap`; property 1 prevents
the presentation from killing its own mark.

The unresolved content is substantial.  Lalonde's effects are not
already literal group words, the perfect strategy is nontracial, and local
finite packets must be synchronized on one canonical carrier across the
whole incidence graph.  A construction must preserve the vector-state
completeness witness while exposing canonical group microstates to the
tracial certificate.  Merely replacing the perfect strategy by a
maximally entangled one is impossible by Lalonde's theorem itself.
