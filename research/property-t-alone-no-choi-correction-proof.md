Hyperlinearity supplies a faithful homomorphism of Thom's group C-star
algebra into a tracial matrix ultraproduct.  Ucp coordinate lifts on the
canonical generators would, by the approximate multiplicative-domain
calculation in `brown-ucp-generator-lift-closes-maslov-gap`, make its
canonical trace amenable.  That is exactly the factorization property, which
Thom's example lacks.
