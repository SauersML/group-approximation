**Problem XCVI, general part, of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White.**  Must every
unital separable nowhere-scattered C*-algebra be generated by one operator?

The answer is **no**.  The Elliott--Li--Niu example is simple, unital, and
infinite-dimensional, hence has no nonzero quotient containing a minimal
projection.  It is therefore nowhere scattered, while it is not singly
generated.
