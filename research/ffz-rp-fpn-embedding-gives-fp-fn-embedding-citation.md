Literature import, not a reproof. The statement was read on 2026-09-13 from
the arXiv e-print source of arXiv:2607.21727v1 (`rope_trick.tex`, SHA-256
recorded in the artifact): Theorem A (`\label{thm:FP_to_F}`), with its proof
and the Lemma 2.1 it relies on ("If a finitely presented group G is of type
FP_n, then it is of type F_n."). The artifact quotes both, together with
Questions 1.3–1.4, Definition 1.5, Lemma 1.6, Theorem B and Lemmas 3.2–3.4.

The proof in the source uses: the Higman embedding theorem and a universal
finitely presented group [Hig61]; [GMSW01, Lemma 3.1] to recognize an ascending
HNN extension of a subgroup; [Bie81, Proposition 2.12] (an ascending HNN
extension of a group of type `FP_n` is of type `FP_n`); and Lemma 2.1, proved
in the source by attaching finitely many orbits of cells, in the style of the
Eilenberg–Ganea argument.

This route asserts only that the source proves the statement as recorded.
