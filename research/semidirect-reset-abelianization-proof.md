The splitting gives the quotient homomorphism `(SRI1)`. Since `C_2` is
abelian, `chi([u,v])=0` for all `u,v in R`. But `chi(sh)=1` for every `h in
H`. Thus `[u,v]=sh` is impossible, including for elements in an arbitrary
subgroup of `R`.
