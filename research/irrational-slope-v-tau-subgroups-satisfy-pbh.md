Let `τ = (√5 − 1)/2` and let `V_τ` be the irrational-slope Thompson group of
Burillo--Nucinkis--Reeves (arXiv:2006.02401). Then `V_τ` admits an action of type (A),
namely its action on `Z[τ] ∩ [0,1)` in the right-continuous model. Hence every subgroup
of `V_τ` satisfies permutational Boone--Higman, has solvable word problem, and embeds in
a finitely presented simple group. This covers `F_τ ≤ T_τ ≤ V_τ` and the index-two
simple subgroups `T_{xz}` and `V_{xz}` (arXiv:2006.02401, l.319 "The group \txz\ is
simple" and l.429–430 "The group $\vxz$ is a simple group").

**Relevance to Question 3.4.** `T_τ` and `T_{xz}` act on the circle, the family where
BFFHZ point to groups that are not MIF (arXiv:2503.21882v2, l.246). Once `V_τ` is
finitely presented, `T_{xz}` and `V_{xz}` are finitely presented simple groups, and the
claim answers Question 3.4 positively for them without MIF.
