**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, not reviewed; elementary). No priority claimed.

Let `D` be the dyadic rationals in `(0,1)`, `F` Thompson's group acting on `D`, and
`GL(Z; D)` the finitary integral matrices indexed by `D`. Put

    Γ_X = (Z^(D) ⋊ GL(Z; D)) ⋊ F,

acting on `X = Z_2^D` by `x ↦ Mx + b` composed with the coordinate permutation by `f ∈ F`.
These are exactly the homeomorphisms of `X` that are globally of the form
"`Z[1/2]`-affine on finitely many coordinates, then permute coordinates by `F`". If
`M ∈ GL_T(Z[1/2])` maps `Z_2^T` onto itself then `M ∈ GL_T(Z_2) ∩ GL_T(Z[1/2]) = GL_T(Z)`,
and a translation `b ∈ Z[1/2]^T` preserving `Z_2^T` lies in `Z_2 ∩ Z[1/2] = Z`.

**Claim.** `Γ_X` is finitely presented.

## Proof

Fix `d_0 < d_1` in `D`. Generators:
- the generators of `F`;
- `x = x_{d_0 d_1}(1)` and `x' = x_{d_1 d_0}(1)` (one per `F`-orbit of ordered pairs);
- `δ = diag(−1 at d_0)`;
- `τ =` translation by `e_{d_0}`.

Relations, each imposed for one representative of each of the finitely many `F`-orbits
of ordered tuples of at most four distinct points of `D`. `F` has `k!` orbits on ordered
`k`-tuples (`finitary-steinberg-extensions-by-oligomorphic-actions-are-fp`), and the
pointwise stabilizers of points and pairs are `F^2` and `F^3`, which are finitely generated.
1. The relators of `F`, and commutation of `x, x'` with generators of the stabilizer of
   `(d_0, d_1)`, and of `δ, τ` with generators of the stabilizer of `d_0`. These make
   `x_ab(1)`, `δ_a` and `τ_a` well-defined `F`-conjugates for all `a ≠ b ∈ D`.
2. The Steinberg relations among the `x_ab(1)`, and the `K_2(Z)` relation
   `(x_12 x_21^{-1} x_12)^4 = 1` on two indices. With 1 this presents `E(Z; D) ⋊ F`, by that node.
3. `δ_a^2 = 1`, `[δ_a, δ_b] = 1`, `δ_a x_bc δ_a = x_bc^{ε}` with `ε = −1` iff exactly one of
   `b, c` equals `a`, and `δ_a δ_b = h_ab(−1)` (a fixed Steinberg word). Since
   `GL_n(Z) = SL_n(Z) ⋊ ⟨δ_1⟩` with `δ_a = δ_1 h_{1a}(−1)`, relations 2–3 present
   `GL(Z; D) ⋊ F` by Tietze moves on each finite index set, and hence on their union.
4. `[τ_a, τ_b] = 1`; `x_ab τ_b x_ab^{-1} = τ_b τ_a`; `[x_ab, τ_c] = 1` for `c ≠ b`;
   `δ_a τ_a δ_a = τ_a^{-1}`; `[δ_a, τ_c] = 1` for `c ≠ a`. These are the action of the
   generators of `GL(Z; D)` on the free abelian basis `τ_c` of `Z^(D)`. So with 1–3 they
   present the semidirect product `Z^(D) ⋊ (GL(Z; D) ⋊ F)`.

All relations involve at most four indices, so finitely many suffice. The group presented
maps onto `Γ_X`, and the inverse map is defined on the normal subgroup generated by
`x, x', δ, τ` exactly as in the proof of `finitary-steinberg-extensions-by-oligomorphic-actions-are-fp`. ∎
