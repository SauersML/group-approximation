For `(ALB1)`, cyclicity of trace and `X_i^2=X_i` give

```text
||E X_i||_2^2=tr_d(X_i E^*E X_i)=tr_d(E^*E X_i).
```

Sum and use `P<=I`. Replacing `E` by `U_iEU_i^*` and conjugating inside the
trace gives `(ALB2)`. The rank-one choice in `(ALB3)` proves sharp failure and
also proves the conditional bound `(ALB4)`.

In the one-compressor generalized wreath, the root-coordinate relation says
that its lamp is fixed by `Gamma`; conjugating by `t` says the lamp at
`t Gamma` is fixed by `t Gamma t^(-1)`. A `gamma` outside that strict subgroup
moves it to a distinct coordinate. Distinct fair Bernoulli lamp projections
have trace `1/2` and overlap `1/4`, so their squared `2`-distance is
`1/2+1/2-2(1/4)=1/2`. Canonical permutation microstates preserve these fixed
moments and give `(ALB5)`.

For `(ALB6)`, compute on the matrix-unit basis. `L_X` has rank
`rank(X)d`, and the Frobenius norm of `L_A` divided by `d^2` is the Frobenius
norm of `A` divided by `d`. The covariance identity is direct composition.
Finally `C_R` has normalized trace `|tr_d(R)|^2`, whence the equality in
`(ALB7)`; the inequality follows from
`1-|z|^2<=2(1-Re z)` for `|z|<=1`.

The positive-density Kazhdan theorem counts the dimension of a spectral
subspace in the adjoint Hilbert space. It does not count the matrix rank of
one vector belonging to that Hilbert space. This proves the amplifier fence.
