Starting from the common-scale affine transport of
`s3-affine-hnn-transports-the-active-vector`, add one finitely presented
two-cell or infinite-order compressor whose action is on the **external
multiplicity coordinates** of the three active atoms.  Prove:

1. in finite-dimensional normalized-HS microstates, the transported
   three-coordinate active vector differs from the next Fanizza carrier by
   at most `C sqrt(E)`;
2. the `K=-1,b=-1` half remains in the already named first-hit exit and is
   not restored through a stationary complementary type;
3. the cell visibly fails on the positive regular stationary flow of every
   ordinary finite graph of packet charts; and
4. the marked base embeds, while an exact completeness representation may
   use properly infinite multiplicity.

The two-cell need not recognize the nonlinear projection `A`.  Its three
source coordinates are the affine atoms `(SEA4)`, all at one spin scale, and
ordinary HNN transport already supplies their local comparison.  The only
open content is nonstationarity on their multiplicity spaces.

Positive density is no longer an additional unknown.
`s3-active-vector-carries-positive-density-multiplicity-algebra` gives

```text
tr_ad(W)>=tau(A)^2/3-C sqrt(E)                         (STH1)
```

for the direct sum `W` of the three endpoint character-block algebras.  The
missing two-cell only has to make the compressor action factor through the
endpoint algebra on this explicit `W`, placing it in the relevant
low-commutator/excess space.  It must fail on the tensor-decoupled model by
an ordinary-word coefficient-covariance relation.

Acceptable implementations include a Toeplitz proper corner, a projective
matrix-valued holonomy, or a property-`(T)` coefficient action.  A scalar
Schur multiplier or another finite restriction edge is insufficient.

## Attempts

- Ordinary HNN transport of the three affine coordinates is now solved, but
  has the positive regular stationary flow and supplies no strictness.
- A scalar projective phase is insufficient: it acts only on the chart
  label and is blind to the external multiplicity algebra.  The next attack
  should insert the three transported coordinates into the explicit
  Toeplitz head matrix units and ask the head swap to act nontrivially on
  their common multiplicity block.
