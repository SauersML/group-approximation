The coefficient is the zero-one function `phi=1_H`.  Every tensor power
replaces it by the pointwise power `phi^k=phi`, and every orbit Gram entry by
the same zero-one equality test.  Direct sums and trivial spectator tensors
also preserve the normalized distinguished-vector coefficients.  None of
these operations introduces a finite carrier, coordinate functions on that
carrier, or pointwise covariance of coordinate functions.  Hence scalar
amplification produces no datum beyond `(TPC1)`, as claimed.
