Notation as in the claim; `o = Gamma_hat` in `X = G_hat/Gamma_hat`, and `e_x`
is the lamp at `x`.

**1. Torsion-free.**  `G` is torsion-free
(`hyperbolic-kazhdan-cover-of-leavitt-unit-group`), so `G_hat` is.  The kernel
`L = direct_sum_X Z` is torsion-free.  An element of finite order in `W_hat`
maps to an element of finite order in `G_hat`, hence to `1`.  So it lies in
`L`, and is trivial.

**2. Finitely generated.**  `G_hat` is Kazhdan, hence finitely generated.  It
acts transitively on `X`, so `L` is generated as a `G_hat`-module by `e_o`.  So
`W_hat` is generated by `e_o` and generators of `G_hat`.

**3. Rigid defect.**

- `Gamma_hat` and `G_hat` are Kazhdan, and `Gamma_hat` is infranormal and not
  normal in `G_hat`, with the strict compressor `u_hat`
  (`hyperbolic-kazhdan-group-has-infranormal-kazhdan-subgroup`).
- Pick `gamma in Gamma_hat` outside `u_hat Gamma_hat u_hat^-1`.
- `z = e_o` centralizes `Gamma_hat`, since `Gamma_hat` fixes `o`.
- `[u_hat z u_hat^-1, gamma] = e_(u_hat o) - e_(gamma u_hat o)` in additive
  notation.
- This is nonzero: `gamma u_hat Gamma_hat = u_hat Gamma_hat` would put `gamma`
  in `u_hat Gamma_hat u_hat^-1`.

**4. Not sofic.**  `W_hat` is countable and contains the pair
`Gamma_hat <= G_hat` with a nontrivial rigid defect.  So it is not sofic by
`sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects`.  ∎
