Let `H` be the subgroup generated by the `z_ij(t, c)`. Then `H` contains every
`e_ij(t)` with `t ∈ J`, and `H <= E_3(A, J)`. It suffices to show
`x z x^-1 ∈ H` for `x = e_hk(b)` and `z = z_ij(t, c)`. Let `l` be the third
index. The identities used are the Steinberg relations and the conjugation
formulas `(C1) e_ki(s) e_ij(t) e_ki(-s) = e_kj(st) e_ij(t)` and
`(C2) e_jk(s) e_ij(t) e_jk(-s) = e_ik(-ts) e_ij(t)`.

1. **`(h,k) ∈ {(j,i), (j,l), (l,i)}`.** `x` commutes with `e_ji(c)`, and
   `x e_ij(t) x^-1` is `z_ij(t, b)` or a product of level-`J` elementary
   matrices. Conjugating those by `e_ji(c)` stays in `H`.
2. **`(h,k) = (i,l)` or `(l,j)`.** `x z x^-1` is `z` conjugated by `e_jl(-cb)`
   or by `e_li(bc)`, which is case 1.
3. **`(h,k) = (i,j)`.**
   - `z = [e_jl(ct) e_il(t), e_li(-c) e_lj(1)]`, from
     `e_ij(t) = [e_il(t), e_lj(1)]`.
   - Conjugating by `x` gives `[X, Y]` with `X = e_jl(ct) e_il(t(1+bc))` in
     level `J` and `Y = e_li(-c) e_lj(1+cb)`.
   - `[X, Y] = X · Y X^-1 Y^-1`. Conjugating column-`l` level-`J` matrices by
     the row-`l` matrix `Y` produces level-`J` elementary products and the
     generators `z_il(s, r)`, plus a conjugate of `z_jl(s, r)` handled by
     case 1.

The full computation is Lemma A of the artifact.
