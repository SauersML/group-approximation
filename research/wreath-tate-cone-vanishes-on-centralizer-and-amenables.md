**ESTABLISHED (derivation; unreviewed).**
- **Setting.** `Γ` is torsion-free hyperbolic, `p` prime, `W = Γ ≀ Z/p`, `H = ΔΓ × <σ>`, and
  `Y = cone([ℓ²(W/H)]) ⊗ q^*1_T`, as in `tate-diagonal-cone-is-weakly-contractible-wreath-object`.
- **Statement.** `Res^W_K Y ≃ 0` in `KK^K` for:
  - every finite `K`;
  - every `K ⊆ Γ^p`;
  - every amenable `K`;
  - every `K` contained in a conjugate of `H`.
- **Consequence.** `KK^W(Ind_K^W B, Y) = 0` for all such `K`.
- **Mechanism.** For `w ∉ H`, the stabilizer `H ∩ C_W(wσw^(−1))` is torsion-free and virtually
  cyclic. This is because centralizers of nontrivial elements of `Γ` are cyclic. On it the inflated
  Tate object vanishes, by Meyer–Nest Thm. 8.5.
- **Generation form.** If `q^*1_T` lies in the localizing subcategory generated by objects induced
  from these subgroups, then `Y ≃ 0` and the Tate diagonal of `C*_r Γ` is a `KK^(Z/p)`-equivalence.

Proof: `wreath-tate-cone-vanishes-on-centralizer-and-amenables-proof`.
