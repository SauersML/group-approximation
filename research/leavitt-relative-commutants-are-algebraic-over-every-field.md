Let `k` be a field and `L = L_k(1,2)`, generated by `s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1` with `t_i s_j = δ_ij` and
`s_0 t_0 + s_1 t_1 = 1`.

**Claim.**
1. *(Field-uniform rank bound.)* `L` contains no two commuting elements `a, b` with `P(a, b) ≠ 0` for every nonzero
   `P ∈ k[x, y]`. Equivalently `k[x, y]` has no injective algebra map into `L`, unital or not.
2. *(Relative commutants.)* For every unital endomorphism `rho` of `L`, every `z ∈ C_L(rho(L))` is algebraic over `k`.
   Moreover `L ⊗ C_L(rho(L))` embeds unitally in `L`.
3. *(Finite fields.)* If `k` is finite, every unit of `C_L(rho(L))` has finite order.

Part 1 is the statement of the w18 node `leavitt-algebras-contain-no-two-variable-polynomial-ring`, which is not
yet in this checkout. The route re-proves it in full, so this claim depends on no unlanded node.

**Consequences.**
- Over `F_2`, the relative commutant of `rho_w` is `{ z : z = w phi(z) w^(-1) }`. So no unit of infinite order is
  conjugate to its diagonal copy (`diagonal-copy-nonconjugacy-from-algebraic-relative-commutants`, which closes
  `leavitt-units-are-not-conjugate-to-diagonal-copies`).
- `infinite-order-leavitt-unit-conjugate-to-its-diagonal-copy` is false, and its equivalent form (b), that
  `L ⊗ F_2[t^±1]` embeds unitally in `L`, is false over every field. `L ⊗ k[t^±1]` contains the polynomial ring
  `k[s_0 ⊗ 1, 1 ⊗ t]`.
- So the premise of the route `rationals-in-leavitt-units-via-diagonal-copy` is false, and that route can never
  fire. The target `rationals-embed-in-binary-leavitt-unit-group` stays OPEN: `F_2[Q]` has Krull dimension one,
  and part 1 does not see it.
- *Weyl algebra in characteristic p (closes the "Open in characteristic p" note of
  `weyl-algebra-blocks-a-universal-leavitt-host`).* If `char k = p > 0`, the first Weyl algebra `A_1(k)` is not
  embeddable in `L_k(1,2)`, unitally or not. Its centre is `k[x^p, ∂^p]`, a polynomial ring in two variables,
  since `A_1(k)` is free of rank `p^2` over it.
- *Bilich--Hazrat--Nam, arXiv:2512.09241v2, Question 2.8 (as quoted in
  `no-unital-map-from-leavitt-tensor-square-to-leavitt-over-f2`).* Let `E` and `F` be graphs, each with a cycle.
  Then `L_k(E) ⊗ L_k(F)` does not embed in `L_k(1,2)`, unitally or not. By
  `countable-graph-leavitt-path-algebras-embed-in-binary-leavitt`, it therefore embeds in no Leavitt path algebra
  of a countable graph. The reason: a cycle `c` has linearly independent powers `c^m`, because they are distinct
  paths, so `k[c ⊗ 1, 1 ⊗ c']` is a polynomial ring in two variables. So Kumjian--Pask algebras of 2-graphs do not
  all embed in `L_k(1,2)`, and the product 2-graph `E × F` is a counterexample for any such `E` and `F`. The case
  `E = F = R_2` is Ara--Cortiñas Question 5.7, answered negatively over every field.

**Scope.** Part 2 does not bound `dim C_L(rho(L))`. An infinite-dimensional algebraic commutant, for example one
containing a copy of `M_(2^∞)(k)`, is not excluded.
