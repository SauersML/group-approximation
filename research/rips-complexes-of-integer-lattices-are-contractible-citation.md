Citation import, not a reproof. Žiga Virk, *Contractibility of the Rips
complexes of Integer lattices via local domination*, arXiv:2405.09134 (v1
15 May 2024, v4 26 July 2025).

- Abstract: "We prove that for each positive integer n, the Rips complexes of
  the n-dimensional integer lattice in the d_1 metric (i.e., the Manhattan
  metric, also called the natural word metric in the Cayley graph) are
  contractible at scales above n^2(2n-1), with the bounds arising from the
  Jung's constants."
- Theorem 5.1 (v4): "For each `r ≥ n^2(2n−1)` we have
  `Rips((Z^n, d_1), r) ≃ •`."
- Convention: the closed Rips complex, where a finite `σ ⊆ X` is a simplex iff
  `Diam(σ) ≤ r`.
- The paper also proves that scale `r ≥ n` suffices for `n = 1, 2, 3`
  (Theorems 3.2 and 5.2).
