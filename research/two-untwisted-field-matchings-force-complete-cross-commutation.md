ESTABLISHED.  Let `F=F_(2^n)`, and choose `alpha in F` with
`F_2(alpha)=F`.  Suppose `X,Z:F->G` are homomorphisms from the additive
group of `F` to an arbitrary group `G`.  If

```text
[X(b),Z(b)]=1,                 [X(alpha b),Z(b)]=1             (UFM1)
```

for every `b in F`, then

```text
[X(a),Z(b)]=1                                                    (UFM2)
```

for every `a,b in F`.  Consequently the abstract group generated by two
additive copies of `F` subject to `(UFM1)` is exactly `F x F`.  In
particular, no higher commutator, weak-commutativity, or exterior-square
residue survives, and the conclusion holds in every unitary representation
without a monomial, multiplicity-free, or finite-dimensional hypothesis.

There is also a quantitative fixed-rank statement.  For exact additive
unitary representations in a tracial matrix algebra, put

```text
d(a,b)=||X(a)Z(b)-Z(b)X(a)||_2,
D(r)=(E_t d(rt,t)^2)^(1/2).                                  (UFM3)
```

There is an explicit ratio-circuit weight `kappa:F->N`, generated from
`1,alpha` by addition and parallel sum, such that

```text
D(r) <= kappa(r) max(D(1),D(alpha)).                           (UFM4)
```

One may take

```text
max_r kappa(r) <= K_n := 8 n 3^(ceil(log_2 n)).                (UFM5)
```

Hence

```text
(E_(a,b) d(a,b)^2)^(1/2)
  <= K_n max(D(1),D(alpha)).                                  (UFM6)
```

The same estimate transfers through any same-Hilbert BLR decoder.  More
precisely, suppose arbitrary unitary tables `X_0,Z_0` admit exact additive
tables `X,Z` with

```text
eta_X=(E_a ||X_0(a)-X(a)||_2^2)^(1/2),
eta_Z=(E_b ||Z_0(b)-Z(b)||_2^2)^(1/2),                         (UFM7)
```

and let `epsilon` be the larger RMS commutator defect of `X_0,Z_0` on the
two matchings.  Then their complete RMS commutator defect is at most

```text
K_n (epsilon+2 eta_X+2 eta_Z) + 2 eta_X+2 eta_Z.               (UFM8)
```

Finally, the untwisted theorem closes the **exact** arbitrary-multiplicity
version of the two-matching Weyl sampler.  If exact additive `U,W` obey

```text
U(b)W(b)=(-1)^beta(b,b) W(b)U(b),
U(alpha b)W(b)=(-1)^beta(alpha b,b) W(b)U(alpha b)             (UFM9)
```

for a nondegenerate binary pairing `beta`, tensor `U,W` with a canonical
Weyl pair having the same signs.  The two signs cancel, `(UFM1)` applies,
and untensoring gives the complete Weyl table

```text
U(a)W(b)=(-1)^beta(a,b) W(b)U(a).                             (UFM10)
```

Every finite-dimensional such representation therefore has dimension a
positive multiple of `2^n`.

## Scope

The bound `(UFM5)` grows polynomially in `n`; it is not the
dimension-independent normalized-HS threshold required by
`bounded-degree-entangled-agreement-pauli-metapixel`.  The surviving problem
is now sharply quantitative: improve the ratio-circuit propagation to a
rank-independent average estimate, or construct approximate additive tables
showing that no such estimate follows from the two matchings alone.
