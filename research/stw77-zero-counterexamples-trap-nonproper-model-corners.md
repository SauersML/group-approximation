Let `phi:A->B` satisfy the hypotheses of STW LXXVII, have nuclear dimension
zero, and fail to be O-infinity-stable.  Then there are a finite set
`F subset phi(A)` and `epsilon>0` such that every unital finite-dimensional
subalgebra

```text
D=direct_sum_j M_(n_j) subset B
```

which approximates every element of `F` within `epsilon` has a minimal
projection in at least one block which is not properly infinite in `B`.

Thus a zero-dimensional counterexample cannot merely admit badly chosen
finite-dimensional models: one finite image packet forces a nonproper support
in every sufficiently accurate model.  This is the uniform finite-junk-corner
normal form for the remaining zero-colour obstruction.
