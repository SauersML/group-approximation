The partner citation realizes a torsion-free cocompact lattice `Γ < Sp(2,1)` as
a non-elementary hyperbolic group.  Being torsion-free, `Γ` has trivial finite
radical, so it is an admissible host.  Given a non-residually-finite hyperbolic
group, clause (1) => (2) of the fixed-host reduction gives an infinite
hyperbolic quotient of `Γ` without nontrivial finite quotients.

`kazhdan-quotientless-hyperbolic-from-sp21-quotient`; do not delete a true
implication to silence the cycle warning.
