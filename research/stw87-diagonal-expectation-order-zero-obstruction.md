If `X` contains two distinct points, there is a *-homomorphism
`phi:C^2->C*_u(X)` such that, for the canonical expectation
`E_X:C*_u(X)->ell^infinity(X)`, one has

```text
inf_theta max_(i=1,2)
  norm(theta(e_i)-(E_X phi)(e_i)) = 1/2,                 (D)
```

where the infimum is over all c.p.c. order-zero maps
`theta:C^2->ell^infinity(X)` and `e_1,e_2` are the two minimal projections.

Thus diagonal averaging not only destroys order zero: its defect cannot be
repaired by an arbitrarily small perturbation through diagonal order-zero
maps, already on one two-point matrix corner.
