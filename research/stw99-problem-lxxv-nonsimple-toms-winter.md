**Problem LXXV of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(cf. APTV Question B).  For separable nuclear nowhere scattered `A`,
are finite nuclear dimension, `Z`-stability, and `Cu`-regularity
(`Cu(A) ≅ Cu(A ⊗ Z)` via the first-factor map) equivalent?

## Attempts

* Known arcs: `Z`-stable ⟹ Cu-regular (Rordam machinery, no
  simplicity needed); finite nuclear dimension ⟹ (m,m')-pure ⟹ pure
  nonsimply (Robert--Tikuisis + APTV24's nonsimple dimension
  reduction); pure ⟹ Cu-regular is OPEN nonsimply — that is the
  peer-covered Problem LXXVI (`stw99-problem-lxxvi-pure-implies-cu-regular`).
  The two load-bearing arcs are Cu-regular ⟹ `Z`-stable (the
  nonsimple Winter question — needs a nonsimple (SI)/CPoU, i.e. the
  glueing frontier again, now over the ideal lattice AND the trace
  space simultaneously) and `Z`-stable ⟹ finite nuclear dimension
  (the nonsimple structure theorem: known for finitely many ideals
  by CETWW-style arguments layer-by-layer, open for infinite
  lattices, where nuclear dimension must be bounded uniformly across
  infinitely many subquotients — the honest candidate counterexample
  is a `Z`-stable algebra over an infinite ideal chain with
  subquotient dimensions forced upward, but Z-stability of every
  subquotient caps each layer at the known finite bound, so a
  counterexample must make the EXTENSIONS expensive; Problem LXXXII
  (dimnuc of extensions, peer-covered) is exactly the sub-question).
  Recorded: LXXV decomposes into LXXVI + nonsimple-Winter + LXXXII;
  all three are in the graph.
