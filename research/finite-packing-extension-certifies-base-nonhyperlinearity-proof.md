Assume `Gamma` were hyperlinear.  By
`finite-packing-preserves-hyperlinearity`, every
finite-overgroup amalgam `Gamma_hat` built along the finite context subgroups
would then be hyperlinear.  This contradicts the hypothesis that the packing
argument proves the same `Gamma_hat` nonhyperlinear, and proves `(FPB1)`.
