Define `Z_x` by either side of `(CBP1)`.  In a context `c`, conjugating all
the diagonal matrices `(RBH2)` by the same `theta_c` preserves their joint
spectral projections.  Those projections are indexed only by assignments in
`R_c`; hence the context commutation and predicate relations hold exactly.
The common definition in `(CBP1)` makes the same `Z_x` occur in every context.
This is a unital map to `M_D(C)`, and normalized matrix trace is CE.

The spanning-tree reformulation is ordinary gauge theory on a finite graph:
use vertex gauges to make all tree-edge coordinate identifications standard.
A family of vertex gauges satisfying `(CBP1)` exists exactly when every
remaining chord transport preserves the corresponding already-identified
sign operator.  Its failure is the residual cycle holonomy.
