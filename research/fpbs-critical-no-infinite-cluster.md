title: Critical percolation on a nonamenable quasi-transitive graph has no infinite cluster
---

**ESTABLISHED (imported).** Let `G` be a connected, locally finite, nonamenable
quasi-transitive graph. Then at the critical parameter `p_c`, Bernoulli bond
percolation has no infinite cluster almost surely: `N_infinity(p_c) = 0`.

Due to Benjamini, Lyons, Peres and Schramm, *Group-invariant percolation on
graphs*, GAFA 1999 (Theorem 1.1), for unimodular nonamenable transitive graphs,
and generalized by Hutchcroft to every quasi-transitive graph of exponential
growth (*Percolation on nonunimodular transitive graphs is nonunimodular*-line
work, read via Choi--Seo arXiv:2508.08932 Section 2, "[Hut16, Theorem 1.2]").

The statement is read from the survey of the Preliminaries of Choi--Seo
arXiv:2508.08932v2 (page 3): "for non-amenable quasi-transitive graphs,
`N_infinity(p_c) = 0` almost surely."

Status records an imported theorem, not independent mathematical verification.
