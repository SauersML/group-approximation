For every genuine finite-dimensional representation in the compressor lane,
the compressed subgroup and the ambient elementary group have the same
image.  Therefore no two irreducible blocks can agree on the compressed
image while carrying different central characters.  The proposed twisted
central-character obstruction is empty, even though the relative `K_1`
central pieces themselves may be nontrivial.
