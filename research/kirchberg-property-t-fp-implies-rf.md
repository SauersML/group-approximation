title: A property (T) group has Kirchberg's factorization property if and only if it is residually finite
---
Let `G` be a discrete group with Kazhdan's property (T). Then `G` has Kirchberg's
factorization property if and only if `G` is residually finite.

The factorization property (Kirchberg): the representation `λ ⊗ ρ` of `G × G` on
`ℓ²(G)` factors through the minimal tensor product `C*(G) ⊗_min C*(G)`. Residually
amenable groups have it, and every group with it is hyperlinear; for finitely
generated groups the class lies strictly between residually amenable and hyperlinear
groups. Under property (T) it collapses to residual finiteness.
