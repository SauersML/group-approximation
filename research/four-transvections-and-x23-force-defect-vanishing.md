**OPEN.** Let `G = L_(F_3)(1,2)^x`, `z = -1`, `T_(σ,κ) = 1 + s_σ t_κ`,
`S4 = {T_(0,1), T_(1,0), T_(1,00), T_(00,1)}` and `Γ_5 = <S4, x_23(1)>` with `x_23(1) = 1 + s_10 t_11`. Then `z` and
`x_12(1) = 1 + s_0 t_10` lie in `Γ_5`. The claim: every homomorphism `ρ : Γ_5 -> W^x` into the units of a weakly
finite `F_3`-algebra with `ρ(z) = -1` has `(ρ(x_23(1)) - 1)(ρ(x_12(1)) - 1) = 0`.

**Why it is a candidate input.** With `weakly-finite-leavitt-representations-killing-defect-are-trivial` it gives
`ternary-anti-central-summand-has-no-weakly-finite-image` (route `summand-obstruction-from-four-transvection-defect`),
which refutes Gottschalk on `G`.

**What is already firewalled (artifact Sections 1 to 4).**
- `Γ_5` has weakly finite models with `z -> -1`, the symbol and a character
  (`four-transvection-units-have-a-weakly-finite-symbol-image`). So only the defect form is live.
- Every algebra-level model of `Γ_5`, every tensor power of one, and every model through
  `Γ_5 -> SL_2(F_3[ζ, ζ^(-1)])` sends `x_23(1)` to `1`, so none of them refutes this claim.
- No conjugate of `Γ_5` lies in a depth-monotone unit group (`four-transvection-set-escapes-depth-monotone-units`).

**What refutes it.**
- soficity of `Γ_5` (artifact Theorem 4.1);
- a finite quotient of `Γ_5` separating the eight elements `z^e x_23(1)^a x_12(1)^b` with `e, a, b in {0, 1}`;
- any weakly finite model of `Γ_5` with `z -> -1` and `D != 0`.

## Attempts

    and `s_0 t_1`, so it sends `x_23(1)` to `1`.
  - **Soficity.** `<S4>` is locally finite by linear and not amenable, with image `SL_2(F_3[ζ, ζ^(-1)])`. Soficity,
    LEF and residual finiteness of `Γ_5` are not decided, and no splitting as a graph of locally finite groups is known.
  - **No proof and no model found.**
