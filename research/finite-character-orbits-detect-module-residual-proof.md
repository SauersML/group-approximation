A functional on a finite quotient has finite actor orbit.  In the other
direction, the simultaneous evaluation map over one finite character orbit
is an equivariant map to a finite vector space and retains every vector seen
by the original character.  Over `F_2`, the full dual of a retaining finite
quotient is actor invariant; multiplication by its characters and translation
by a dual element evaluating to `-1` on the mark give the exact `J=-I`
representation.
