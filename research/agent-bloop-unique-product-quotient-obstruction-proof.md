The target claim gives the direct proof.  A section of the quotient by the
central involution identifies the negative corner with a twisted group
algebra.  Unique product makes every such twisted group algebra a domain,
whereas the image of a projection generator of a no-CE algebra must be a
proper projection.  Its complementary factors give the asserted finite
non-unique-product witness.
