title: In a 2-transitive action of Thompson's group F, no rigid stabilizer of an interval fixes a point
distinct_from:
  thompson-f-two-transitive-actions-satisfy-grid-condition: that claim constrains how orbits of the two halves of a point stabilizer in (0,1) intersect; this claim says every point stabilizer in the abstract set misses every rigid stabilizer group of compactly supported elements
---

Let `F` act on a set `Ω`, `|Ω| ≥ 3`, transitively on ordered pairs of distinct
points. For a nonempty open interval `U ⊂ (0,1)`, let `F_U^c` be the elements
of `F` whose support is a compact subset of `U`. Then `F_U^c` fixes no point of
`Ω`.

Equivalently, no point stabilizer `F_ω` contains any `F_U^c`. So any
2-transitive `F`-action must look like the actions `F/H` for subgroups `H`
that contain no rigid-stabilizer group, for example stabilizers of dense sets
or of partitions of an orbit (the candidates of Le Boudec–Matte Bon,
arXiv:1906.05744, p. 23). It excludes actions on orbits of closed subsets of
`(0,1)` with nonempty complement, which includes the order-preserving actions
on orbits in `(0,1)`.
