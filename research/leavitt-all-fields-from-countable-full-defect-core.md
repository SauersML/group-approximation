For any field k, let S be the unital subring of L_k(1,2) generated by its four standard Leavitt generators s,t,a,b. This S is countable even if k is uncountable, is nonzero, and has ts=1 with full defect e=1-st because bea=1. The prerequisite implies S^x is nonhyperlinear. Its inclusion into L_k(1,2)^x is injective: an inverse already in S is the same inverse in the larger ring. Heredity of hyperlinearity to subgroups proves the target.

Unlike the earlier finite-factorization route, this needs no GL=EL input or enlarged coefficient subring. The full-defect theorem is the Cairn algebraic application of Jihao Liu's [Theorem 1.2/6.7](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf) and Andreas Thom's [conditional normalization theorem](https://andreasthom.github.io/pdf/nonhyperlinear.pdf). Their analytic authorship is retained.
