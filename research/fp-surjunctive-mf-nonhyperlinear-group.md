The exact group D_6 in
[[fp-surjunctive-nonhyperlinear-group-exists]], with q=2,r=6,d=3 and the
two polynomial inclusions, is finitely presented, surjunctive, MF in the
abstract-group operator-norm sense, and not hyperlinear. In particular a
finitely presented MF nonhyperlinear group exists.

The nonhyperlinearity input is Jihao Liu's analytic theorem followed by
Andreas Thom's conditional mechanism; the pair is Kun-Thom's. Tatiana
Shulman's amalgamation theorem gives the MF half through the repository's
group-embedding argument. Finite presentation and surjunctivity use the
separate structural inputs already recorded for this exact D_6.
