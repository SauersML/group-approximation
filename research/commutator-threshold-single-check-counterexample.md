title: Mix the 110 and 101 character transitions in two Pauli blocks
target: commutator-threshold-sets-need-not-be-stopping-sets

---

On one two-dimensional block take diagonal parity signs whose endpoint ratio
is `110`, and on another take ratio `101`.  The Pauli X swap anticommutes with
exactly the coordinates selected by each ratio.  Equal block weights make
coordinate one anticommute on all mass and coordinates two and three on half
the mass, giving squared commutator magnitudes `(4,2,2)`.  The parity relation
is exact on each block.
