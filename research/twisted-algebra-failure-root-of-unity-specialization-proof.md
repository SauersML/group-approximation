Section numbers refer to the artifact. Standard facts are in Section 1:
- the Nullstellensatz for finitely generated `Z`-algebras;
- Baer injectivity of the divisible group `F̄_p^x`;
- cocycle normalization.

1. **Residue map** (Section 2). Let `S` be generated by the entries' coefficients, the cocycle values on the
   finite set `P` of support pairs and their inverses, and `c^{-1}`, where `c` is a nonzero coefficient of
   `BA - I`. Choose `ρ: S -> F` onto a finite field.
2. **Root-of-unity cocycle** (Section 2). Extend `ρ|Λ_0` to `χ: Λ -> F̄_p^x` and put `β = χ ∘ α`. Then
   `ÃB̃ = I_n` and `B̃Ã ≠ I_n` in `M_n(F̄_p^β[G])`, since `β = ρ ∘ α` on `P`.
3. **Untwisting** (Section 3). Take `E = M ×_β G` with `q: F̄_p[E] -> F̄_p^β[G]`, and let `e` be the character
   idempotent of the finite cyclic `p'`-group `M_1`. Then `e(ÂB̂ - I) = 0` and `q(e(B̂Â - I)) ≠ 0`, and
   `A* = eÂ + (1-e)I`, `B* = eB̂ + (1-e)I` is a one-sided pair.
4. **Descent** (Section 4) to `F_q` and to the finitely generated `E_0`, a central extension of `G_0`.
5. **Corollary** (Section 5).
   - `stable-finiteness-failure-refutes-surjunctivity` shows `E_0` is not surjunctive.
   - The Seward import, `INF ⇒ RBS ⇒ GOT` per group, gives `¬INF(E_0)`.
   - Its `(∀G POS) ⇒ (∀G INF)` then refutes `∀G POS`.
