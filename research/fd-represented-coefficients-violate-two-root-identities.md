Let `S` be a unital ring with a unital ring homomorphism `S -> M_n(F_p)` for some `n >= 1`.
Then `EL_3(S)` has a rank model into a rank ultraproduct over `F_p` with

```text
N_23 N_12 != 0,        N_ab = sigma(x_ab(1)) - 1 .
```

The model is the left regular representation of the finite image of `EL_3(S)` in
`GL_(3n)(F_p)`, placed in the constant rank ultraproduct. `N_23 N_12` is left multiplication by
`h_23 h_12 - h_23 - h_12 + 1`, a nonzero group-algebra element, since the four group elements
are distinct.

**Consequence.**
* **Relations alone don't suffice.** Relations holding in `EL_3(R)` among finitely many elements
  `x_ij(a_s)` hold in `EL_3(S)`, where `S` is the subring generated by the `a_s`. So they can't
  force `N_23 N_12 = 0` when `S` has a finite-dimensional representation over `F_p`.
* **The Toeplitz subring isn't enough.** The subring `<s_0, t_0 | t_0 s_0 = 1>` of
  `L_(F_2)(1,2)` maps onto `F_2`.
* **What a proof must use instead:** coefficients generating a subring with no nonzero
  finite-dimensional representation, for example a unital Leavitt family; or the no-rank-model
  hypothesis in a way that is not a relation.
