**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed; items 1–2 are computed 2-adically).** Notation of
`radu-delta-squared-orbit-is-signed-permutation-module` and
`radu-horizontal-quotient-is-arithmetic-lattice`: `Q` acts on `T_h × T'`, `p ∈ T'` is the unique
vertex fixed by `<xz, xyx> ≅ S_3`, and `e' = {p, q}` is the edge at `p` inverted by `x`.

1. **Edge stabilizer.** `a, b, c` fix `p` and `q`, and `x, z` swap them. So

   ```text
   H_Q = <a,b,c,x,z> = Stab_Q(e'),       Q/H_Q ≅ E(T'),
   ```

   and the bijection `Q/H_Q ≅ W/D_2` matches the 4-cycles `(xz)^2` of `Cay(PGL_2(Z); x,y,z)`
   with the edges of the second tree.
2. **The character is a local sign.** Let `N(e')` be the six vertices at distance at most 1 from
   `e'`. For `h ∈ Stab_Q(e')` let `sgn_6(h)` be the sign of the permutation of `N(e')` induced by `h`.
   Then

   ```text
   ψ' = sgn_6 = or_(e') · sgn_4   on Stab_Q(e'),
   ```

   where `or_(e')(h) = −1` iff `h` inverts `e'`, and `sgn_4` is the sign on the four outer vertices.
   On generators: `c` swaps the two outer neighbours of `p` and fixes those of `q`
   (`sgn_4 = −1`, `or = +1`). `x` and `z` invert `e'` (`or = −1`, `sgn_4 = +1`). `a` and `b` fix
   `N(e')` pointwise.
3. **Module.** By item 3 of `radu-delta-squared-orbit-is-signed-permutation-module`,

   ```text
   Z[Q]·[(xz)^2]  ≅  Ind_(Stab_Q(e'))^Q Z_(sgn_6)  =:  C_1^sgn(T'),
   ```

   the *local-sign edge module* of the second tree. It is free abelian on `E(T')`, and `g ∈ Q`
   sends the basis vector of `e` to `±` that of `ge`. The sign is the parity of how `g` maps
   `N(e)` to `N(ge)`, relative to fixed reference labellings. The mark `(xz)^4` is twice a basis
   vector.

**Reading.**
* Radu's mark lives in a signed permutation module over the action of the arithmetic lattice `Q`
  on the edges of its *second* tree. Edge stabilizers there are the cocompact virtually free
  lattices `Stab_Q(e') ⊂ PGL_2(Q_2)`.
* `sgn_6` is not the orientation character: `c` fixes `e'` pointwise yet flips the sign.
  So `C_1^sgn(T')` is not the cellular chain module `C_1(T')`. The extra sign `sgn_4` is what
  gives `c δ^2 c = δ^(−2)`, and the parity screens pin on it.
* Abelian lamp models of the mark are microstates for the signed edge action of `Q` on `T'`. By
  strong approximation, edge stabilizers of `T'` are dense in `Q` at every tested congruence
  level (`research/artifacts/radu-slab-character-screen-output-2026-09-13.txt`). So finite
  quotients of `Q` cannot approximate this action, and models must be perturbative. Unlike the
  Kazhdan Hecke pairs, the stabilizer is virtually free, so perturbation is not excluded.
