**ESTABLISHED.**  In

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,             Q=1-ST,
E=EL_3(J),                     F=EL_3(F_2[Q]),
h=[[Q,S,0],[T,0,0],[0,0,1]],
```

let `Gamma_F -> E` be the complete one-step finite-type envelope map of
`binary-jacobson-one-step-finite-type-envelope-is-marked-mf`.  Write

```text
u=x_12(1),       v=x_23(1),       g=x_31(1),
r=v [g,u] v=x_23(1)x_32(1)x_23(1)=w_23.                 (JB1)
```

Thus `r` is a literal word using the complete directed constant three-cycle.
Then

```text
h^2=r^2=1,                  (h r)^3=1 in E.             (JB2)
```

The word `(h r)^3` is a nontrivial cyclically reduced word in `Gamma_F`.
Its three balanced `h`-syllables have the least possible positive number
among elements of `ker(Gamma_F -> E)`, by the minimum-length theorem in
`binary-jacobson-first-mixed-kernel-is-three-syllable`.  Equivalently, the
literal cross-block braid

```text
h r h = r h r                                               (JB3)
```

is another shortest relation beyond the complete finite overlap.

This occurrence equality is nevertheless not the missing PI selector.  For
every fixed `N`, let `C_N` be the `N`-pair finitary Clifford packet containing
the head `w=x_13(Q)`.  The literal subgroup

```text
H_N=<h,r,C_N> <= E                                         (JB4)
```

is finite and retains `w!=1`.  Hence it has an exact faithful finite-dimensional
unitary model containing the affine braid, the nonzero head cut and the
complete degree-`2^N` Clifford packet.

Consequently a common degree-two-to-degree-four PI return cannot be a formal
consequence of `(JB3)` and any fixed finitary packet.  Although the braid
authenticates two literal word paths, both are unitaries in one finite `S_3`
actor and preserve packet degree.  A load-bearing Jacobson occurrence must
combine a shortest relator with another independent raw incidence whose orbit
is not contained in a fixed finite part of the finitary kernel, or must use a
joint compression not forced by the affine braid alone.
