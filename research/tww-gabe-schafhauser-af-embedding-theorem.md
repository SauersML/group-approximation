**Theorem 11 of STW.**  Let `A` be a separable exact C\*-algebra satisfying the
UCT.  Then `A` embeds into a unital simple AF algebra if and only if `A` has a
faithful amenable trace.  (Tikuisis--White--Winter, Ann. of Math. 185 (2017);
Gabe, J. Funct. Anal. 272 (2017); Schafhauser, Ann. of Math. 191 (2020) and
Duke Math. J. 169 (2020).)  The stronger form in which the AF embedding
extends the given trace is what Schafhauser proves.

**Established (literature).**  It is the UCT case of Problems VIII and IX(2),
and STW footnote 26 records that its UCT-free form would settle Problem X for
exact algebras.
