title: The long-root carrier is common but not a decoder
invalidates: [long-root-carrier-decoder]

The long root commutes with both outer packets and the middle root, so its
nontrivial spectral cut is an exact common positive-density carrier — and
the finite `UT₄(F₂)` regular model realizes this carrier and the full
nested table WITHOUT a classical decoder. Carrier existence alone cannot be
the finite-window decoder; finite-window-decoder must use growing extension-fiber
covariance, not merely the finite four-root packet.
