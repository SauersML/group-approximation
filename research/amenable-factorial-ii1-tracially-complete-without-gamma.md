**Statement (unreviewed).** There is a tracially complete C*-algebra `(M, X)` with the following
properties, in the terms of CCEGSTW Definitions 3.4, 3.8, 3.13 and 4.1:
- amenable;
- type II_1;
- factorial;
- without property Γ in the sense of Definition 5.19.

So CCEGSTW Question 1.5 has a negative answer.

**Routes.**
- `q15-negative-via-toms`: the completion of Toms's simple AH algebra without uniform Γ, through
  CCEGSTW Proposition 5.20. The designated traces form the Poulsen simplex, and the credit is
  Toms's.
- `q15-negative-via-bauer-pauli`: the Pauli tower completion. The designated traces form a Bauer
  simplex, and the failure is maximal: no projection of constant trace `1/2` exists in the reduced
  power.

**Status note.** CCEGSTW v6 (7 Aug 2026) still lists Question 1.5 as open and does not cite Toms's
arXiv:2606.12188. The first route shows that the answer was already negative once Toms's Theorem 5
is combined with their own Proposition 5.20.
