Take the forward lattice generated by both families of tail profiles and
`1`; it is finite-rank free abelian with injective shift and contains
the entire scalar depth-zero isotropy. The arithmetic prerequisite gives
the two-species annular realization and the faithful restricted lamp
embedding. The compact-core theorem gives finite-germ actors of type
`F_∞` for every finite root number, together with the finite-subset
clopen stabilizers and oligomorphic action. Apply the explicitly cited
BZ finiteness and finite-order generation theorem to obtain the target
simple host. None of the three unresolved prerequisites is discharged
by this route or by the earlier proposed polynomial tower.
