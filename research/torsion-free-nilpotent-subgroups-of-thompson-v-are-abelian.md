Every torsion-free nilpotent subgroup of Thompson's group `V` is abelian. So the Heisenberg
group, and every non-abelian torsion-free nilpotent group, is isomorphic to no subgroup of
`V`. None of them is a witness for
`biorderable-subgroup-of-v-without-free-subgroups-not-in-f`.
