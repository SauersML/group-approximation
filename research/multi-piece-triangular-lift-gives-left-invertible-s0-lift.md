Artifact Lemma 4.1.
- `b a = 1 + sum X_ij`, with `b = sum E_i[g_i^(-1)]`.
- An acyclic defect digraph makes `sum X_ij` strictly triangular in Peirce form, so `b a` is a unit.
- `(b a)^(-1) b` is a left inverse of `a`, and `pi(a) = s0`, which is not invertible.
