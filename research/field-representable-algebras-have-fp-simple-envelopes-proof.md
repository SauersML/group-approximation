Lane proof (bh-algebra, 2026-09-18). Steps 2–4 are the ring content of
`char-p-polynomial-embedding-via-noether-normalization` (and its characteristic-zero twin),
which is stated there for `F_p` and for groups; every step uses only that the base is a
field, so it is repeated here for an arbitrary field `K`. Stacks tags as cited in that route.

Let `A ⊆ M_N(F ⊗_K K<x>)` be generated by finitely many matrices `a_1, ..., a_g`.

1. **Finitely generated coefficients.** Each entry of each `a_i` is a finite sum of
   `c ⊗ w` with `c ∈ F`, `w` a word. Let `K_0 ⊆ F` be the subfield generated over `K` by the
   finitely many `c` that occur. `K_0` is a finitely generated field extension of `K`, so
   it has a transcendence basis `t_1, ..., t_k` and `d = [K_0 : K(t)] < ∞`. Then
   `A ⊆ M_N(K_0 ⊗ K<x>)`, since `K_0 ⊗ K<x> → F ⊗ K<x>` is injective (tensor over a field).
2. **Restriction of scalars.** A `K(t)`-basis of `K_0` gives an injective unital
   `K(t)`-algebra map `K_0 → M_d(K(t))` (regular representation), hence an injective map
   `M_N(K_0 ⊗ K<x>) → M_(Nd)(K(t) ⊗ K<x>)`.
3. **One denominator.** The images of `a_1, ..., a_g` involve finitely many elements of
   `K(t)`; let `f ∈ K[t] \ 0` be a common denominator and `B = K[t][1/f] = K[t,u]/(uf - 1)`.
   Then `A` lands in `M_(Nd)(B ⊗ K<x>)`. (If `k = 0`, `K_0` is finite over `K` and
   `B = K`; skip to step 5.)
4. **`B` is free over a polynomial ring.** Exactly as in steps 3–5 of
   `char-p-polynomial-embedding-via-noether-normalization`, with `F_p` replaced by `K`:
   - Nagata's substitution `s_i = t_i - u^(D^i)` (with `D` larger than every exponent of
     `f`) makes `u` integral over `P = K[s_1, ..., s_k]` with leading coefficient a unit of
     `K`, so `B = P[u]` is a finite `P`-module and `P → B` is injective (transcendence
     degree `k`).
   - `B` is a localization of a polynomial ring over a field, hence regular, hence
     Cohen–Macaulay; `P` is regular and normal; going down and the fibre dimension count
     give miracle flatness, so `B` is finite projective over `P`.
   - Quillen–Suslin over the field `K` (a PID) makes `B ≅ P^r`.
   - Multiplication on a `P`-basis gives an injective unital `K`-algebra map
     `B → M_r(P)`, hence `B ⊗ K<x> → M_r(P ⊗ K<x>)` injective (tensoring over the field `K`
     preserves injectivity).
5. **Conclusion.** Composing, `A` embeds in `M_n(K<x_1..x_m> ⊗_K K[s_1..s_k])` with
   `n = N d r`. By `polynomially-representable-algebras-have-fp-simple-envelopes`, every
   subalgebra of that algebra embeds in the finitely presented simple `K`-algebra
   `M_n(L_2^{⊗(k+1)})`. ∎

**Special case 2** of the claim composes step 1 with Anan'in's theorem (cited, not read at
source): a finitely generated right noetherian PI algebra embeds in `M_N(F)` for some field
`F ⊇ K`, and a finitely generated commutative algebra is noetherian and PI.
