Literature import. Source: M. G. Benli, R. Grigorchuk, P. de la Harpe, *Amenable groups without
finitely presented amenable covers*, arXiv:1206.2072 (Bull. Math. Sci. 3 (2013)); TeX source
`BGHS+3may13.tex` fetched on MSI, 2026-09-13. Verbatim pins are in
`research/artifacts/hl-intermediate-growth-pins-2026-09-13.md`.

- Item 1: Theorem 1.6 (TeX l.386–392), "Any finitely presented cover of the group $\mathfrak G$
  is large." BGdlH attribute it to [GrHa--01]; the Grigorchuk survey arXiv:1111.0512 (l.683–685)
  credits the observation to de la Harpe and Grigorchuk.
- Largeness and free subgroups: l.394–396, "Recall that a group is large if it contains a
  subgroup of finite index that has non-abelian free quotients. Note that large groups have
  non-abelian free subgroups."
- Item 2: Theorem 1.8 (Erschler), "Any finitely presented cover of the Basilica group
  $\mathfrak B$ has non-abelian free subgroups."
- Item 3: Theorem 1.10, "For $\omega \in \Omega_+$, the group $G_\omega$ is of intermediate
  growth, and any finitely presented cover of $G_\omega$ is large." (l.511–512 define `Ω₊` as the
  complement of the eventually constant sequences.)
- Item 4: Corollary 1.11, "Let $H$ be a finitely generated cover of one of $\mathfrak G$ ...,
  $\mathfrak B$ ..., or $G_\omega$, $\omega \in \Omega_+$ .... Any finitely presented cover of $H$
  is large."

The Basilica clause of Corollary 1.11 is quoted as printed; this route asserts only what the
cited paper states.
