**Setting.** `R` is a countable pmp Borel equivalence relation on a standard probability space `(X, mu)`.
`L(R)` is its von Neumann algebra with the trace from `mu`. Q1 is
`hyperlinear-group-actions-have-ce-covariant-models`. "Q1 fails at `Lambda`" means `Lambda` is hyperlinear and
some pmp action of `Lambda` has a non-CE crossed product (the group-by-group form of
`free-action-covariant-models-contain-the-crossed-product`, item 3).

**Definition (free host).** A *free host* of `R` with actor `Lambda` consists of:
* a countable group `Lambda` with an essentially free action on a standard measure space `(Z, zeta)` that preserves a
  sigma-finite measure `zeta`;
* a Borel set `A ⊆ Z` with `0 < zeta(A) < infinity`, and a Borel subrelation `S ⊆ R_Lambda(Z)|A`;
* a Borel map `r : A -> X` pushing `zeta_A = zeta(.|A)` to `mu`, which for a.e. `a` maps the class `[a]_S`
  bijectively onto the class `[r(a)]_R`.

The host is *finite* if `zeta(Z) < infinity`.

## Statement

1. **Host transfer.** If `R` has a free host, then `L(R)` embeds trace-preservingly in the corner
   `1_A (L^infinity(Z) ⋊ Lambda) 1_A` with trace `tau/zeta(A)`.
   * For a finite host: if `L(R)` is not CE, then `L^infinity(Z) ⋊ Lambda` is not CE. So `Lambda` is
     non-hyperlinear, or Q1 fails at `Lambda`.
2. **Splittings give finite hosts.** Suppose `Lambda` has a pmp action on `(X, mu)`, not necessarily free, and
   `c : R -> Lambda` is a Borel cocycle with `c(x, x') · x = x'` for a.e. `(x, x')` in `R`. Then `R` has a finite
   free host with actor `Lambda` (if `R` has infinite classes on a set of positive measure; otherwise `L(R)` is type
   I and CE).
3. **Finite stabilizers give crossed-product corners.** Suppose `R` is ergodic and generated by a pmp action of
   `Lambda` with finite stabilizers a.e. Then `L(R)` is, up to scaling the trace, a corner of `L^infinity(X) ⋊ Lambda`.
   * So if `L(R)` is not CE, then `Lambda` is non-hyperlinear, or Q1 fails at `Lambda`.
4. **Exact equivalence.** The following are equivalent:
   * (a) a countable non-hyperlinear group exists, or Q1 fails at some group;
   * (b) some pmp relation with a non-CE factor has a finite free host;
   * (c) some ergodic pmp relation with a non-CE factor is generated by an essentially free pmp action.
5. **Obstruction for actors with CE crossed products.** Let `R` be any relation with `L(R)` not CE. This includes
   the Manzoor relation `R_mu` of `non-ce-relation-is-not-treeable` (3) and the property-(T) relation of
   `non-ce-property-t-equivalence-relation-exists`. Let `Lambda` be amenable-by-(finitely generated virtually free),
   or any countable free group. Then:
   * `R` has no finite free host with actor `Lambda`;
   * `R` has no splitting cocycle into any pmp action of `Lambda`;
   * `R` is not generated by any pmp action of `Lambda` with finite stabilizers;
   * if `Lambda` is amenable or free, `R` has no free host with actor `Lambda` even with `zeta` infinite.

## Consequences

* **Non-treeability is a special case.** A treeing of `R` yields a splitting cocycle into `F_infinity`, acting on `X` by
  automorphisms that extend the treeing maps. So item 5 recovers `non-ce-relation-is-not-treeable` (1). It also
  recovers `free-actor-extensions-cannot-host-nonce-relation` for class-bijective placements.
* **Regenerating the Manzoor relation.** `R_mu` is generated by an action of a free group with infinite stabilizers
  (`manzoor-isotropy-has-no-positive-finite-type-corner`). Item 3 shows that any action of any group that generates
  the same relation with finite stabilizers has an actor that is non-hyperlinear or violates Q1. So "can the known
  non-CE relation be regenerated with finite isotropy?" is a measured orbit-equivalence question, and a yes answer
  settles the disjunction (a) of item 4 with no approximation theory at all.
* **Infinite measure is not a loophole for the known Q1 groups.** Item 5, last bullet, excludes a placement of
  `R_mu` as a class-bijective factor of a restriction of an infinite-measure free action of an amenable or free group.
  For other actors the infinite-measure case is not covered by Q1, and item 1 then gives only a non-CE corner of a
  semifinite crossed product.
* **Decomposition.** Under Q1, a finite free host of `R_mu` with any actor names a non-hyperlinear group. This is the
  route `nonhyperlinear-via-q1-and-free-host-of-nonce-relation`, whose placement prerequisite is
  `nonce-relation-admits-free-host`.
