First let `D=J/I` be a continuous-trace subquotient of `A`.  Nuclear
dimension does not increase on quotients or ideals, so

```text
dim_nuc(D) <= dim_nuc(A).
```

Winter--Zacharias identify the left side with `dim(Prim(D))`.  Taking the
supremum over `D` proves `ctdim(A)<=dim_nuc(A)`.

Assume next that `A` has the LXXXI shadow property, and fix a
continuous-trace subquotient `D=J/I`.  Regard `D` as an ideal of `A/I`.
For every `x in Prim(D)`, local triviality of its compact-operator bundle
gives an open neighborhood `U` and a rank-one corner

```text
C_0(U)e ~= C_0(U).
```

This corner is hereditary in an ideal of `D`, hence hereditary in `A/I`.
The shadow property gives `dim(U)<=1`.  Since `A` is separable,
`Prim(D)` is second countable and locality of covering dimension yields
`dim(Prim(D))<=1`.  Thus `ctdim(A)<=1`.

Conversely, assume `ctdim(A)<=1`.  Let `C` be a commutative hereditary
subalgebra of `A/I`, and let `L` be the ideal of `A/I` generated by `C`.
Then `C` is a full hereditary subalgebra of `L`.  Hence `L` is strongly
Morita equivalent to the commutative algebra `C`; continuous trace and
primitive spectrum are Morita invariant.  Therefore `L` is a
continuous-trace subquotient of `A` and

```text
Prim(L) ~= Prim(C).
```

The definition of `ctdim` now gives `dim(Prim(C))<=1`, which is precisely
the LXXXI shadow property.

**Trust boundary.**  The proof uses standard Morita invariance of continuous
trace and primitive spectra, locality of covering dimension for
second-countable locally compact Hausdorff spaces, and the cited
Winter--Zacharias computation.  No extension maximum formula is used.
