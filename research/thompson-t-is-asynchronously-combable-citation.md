Citation import, not a reproof. Read from the arXiv API record and the arXiv HTML
rendering on 2026-09-17.

L. Funar and C. Kapoudjian, *The braided Ptolemy-Thompson group T^* is
asynchronously combable*, arXiv:math/0602490, Comment. Math. Helv. 86 (2011),
707–768.

- Abstract, verbatim: "Our main result here is that T^* (and T) is asynchronously
  combable. The method of proof is inspired by Lee Mosher's proof of automaticity of
  mapping class groups."
- Introduction, verbatim (HTML rendering, through a summarizing fetch): "Corollary
  1.2. The Thompson group T is asynchronously combable."

The precise form of the asynchronous fellow traveller property used, with or
without a departure function, was not re-read. The claim uses only the name of the
property.
