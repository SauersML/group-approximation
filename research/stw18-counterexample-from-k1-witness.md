Let `A` be the algebra supplied by the first prerequisite. It is simple,
separable, nuclear and non-elementary with strict comparison, and it is not
K1-injective. If `A` were Z-stable, the second prerequisite would make
`U(A)/U_0(A) -> K_1(A)` injective. So `A` is not Z-stable, which is the
target. Square.
