title: Diagonalize LLP lifts of a tracial matrix-ultraproduct embedding
target: llp-makes-hyperlinear-traces-amenable
requires: []
---

Represent a hyperlinear trace by a trace-preserving homomorphism into a
tracial matrix ultraproduct.  LLP lifts its restriction to every finite
operator system to the bounded matrix product.  In the quotient the lift is
multiplicative, so its coordinate multiplicative errors vanish in normalized
two-norm along the ultrafilter, and its coordinate traces converge to the
given trace.  Extend the coordinate ucp maps from each operator system and
diagonalize over increasing finite sets.  The resulting ucp matrix maps are
the amenable-trace approximation.
