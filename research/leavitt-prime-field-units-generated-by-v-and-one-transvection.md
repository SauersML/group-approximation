**ESTABLISHED** (route `leavitt-units-v-and-one-transvection-generation-proof`; artifact Section 1; verification
requested from `w4-vf-gate`).

For every field `K`, `L_K(1,2)^x` is generated by Thompson's `V` (the prefix-replacement units) together with the
transvections `T_(00,01)(λ) = 1 + λ s_00 t_01`, `λ in K`. Over a prime field `F_p`, one transvection is enough:

`L_(F_p)(1,2)^x = ⟨V, 1 + s_00 t_01⟩`.

In particular `G_3 = L_(F_3)(1,2)^x = ⟨V, x⟩` with `x = 1 + s_00 t_01` of order 3, and
`L_(F_2)(1,2)^x = ⟨V, 1 + s_00 t_01⟩` with an involution.

**Proposition (artifact 1.3).** Take transvections `T_(σ_i,κ_i)(1)` whose `2k` cylinders are pairwise disjoint
and do not cover `X`. Over `F_3` every nonidentity element of the elementary abelian group they generate is
conjugate in `G_3` to `x`.

**Why it matters for the minus-one gate.** `w5-c3-signed-thompson` reduced the gate on `E ⋊ V` to the rank gate of
`V`. By this claim, everything `G_3` adds beyond `V` is generated by one element. A proof of
`sylvester-rank-functions-on-ternary-leavitt-units-kill-minus-one` that does not settle `V` must therefore use
relations involving `x` beyond the ones already covered by
`anti-central-sofic-data-cannot-force-defect-submultiplicativity`:
- `V`-equivariance, i.e. single conjugations;
- splitting and same-code Steinberg relations, which live in finite subgroups `SL_n(F_3)` over one prefix code;
- commutation of transvections on disjoint cylinders.

The first configurations outside all of these are pairs `T_(σ,κ)`, `T_(κ,ρ)` with `ρ` comparable to `σ`, such as
`x_12(s_0) x_21(1)`. These have infinite order.
