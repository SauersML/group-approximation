Write the positive Heisenberg monoid generated by `t,s,r` in normal form

```text
p=r^c s^b t^a.
```

The relation `t s=r s t` gives

```text
(r^c s^b t^a)t=r^c s^b t^(a+1),
(r^c s^b t^a)r=r^(c+1)s^b t^a,
(r^c s^b t^a)s=r^(c+a)s^(b+1)t^a.                      (KAP1)
```

Consequently

```text
F_L={r^c s^b t^a:0<=a,b<L, 0<=c<L^2}                  (KAP2)
```

is a right Folner sequence.  Right multiplication by `t` loses the face
`a=L-1`; right multiplication by `r` loses `c=L^2-1`; and right
multiplication by `s` loses `b=L-1` together with at most the top `a`
values of the `c` coordinate.  Every relevant boundary ratio is
`O(1/L)`.

Use the finite quotient from the nested-wall theorem,

```text
R_L=F_q[x_1,x_2]/(x_1^L,x_2^2),                         (KAP3)
```

killing `x_3,...,x_d`, and let `lambda_L` be the left regular
representation of `B_L=EL_r(R_L)`.  The substitutions induced by `s` and
`r` fix `x_1,x_2` and only multiply `x_3` by `x_2` and `x_1`, respectively.
Because `x_3` and the later variables vanish in `(KAP3)`, for
`p=r^c s^b t^a` one has

```text
q_L(alpha_p(Gamma))=H_a=EL_r(F_q[x_1,x_1^a x_2]),
q_L(alpha_p(gamma))=e_(ab)(x_1^a x_2).                  (KAP4)
```

In particular all coefficient data depend only on the `t`-depth `a`.

For `a>=1`, choose the diagonal sign involution

```text
D_a in lambda_L(H_a)'
```

from `kt-nested-compressor-flat-tower-wall-proof`, so that

```text
||lambda_L(e_(ab)(x_1^(a-1)x_2))
   -D_a^*lambda_L(e_(ab)(x_1^(a-1)x_2))D_a||_2^2>=2.    (KAP5)
```

Put `D_0=1`.  On

```text
K_L=direct_sum_(p in F_L) ell^2(B_L)
```

define

```text
Pi_L(delta)|_(K_p)=lambda_L(q_L(alpha_p(delta))),
D_L|_(K_(r^c s^b t^a))=D_a.                             (KAP6)
```

Equation `(KAP4)` gives `[D_L,Pi_L(Gamma)]=0` exactly.

For `u in {t,s,r}`, let `T_u` be the block permutation which is the identity
map `K_(pu)->K_p` whenever both indices lie in `F_L`, completed arbitrarily
on the boundary.  The standard Folner-telescope calculation and `(KAP1)`
give `(KAH4)` and `(KAH2)` with defect `O(L^(-1/2))`.

Now leave `T_s,T_r` unchanged and set

```text
T_(0,t)=T_t,                    T_(1,t)=T_t D_L.          (KAP7)
```

Since `D_L` commutes with `Pi_L(Gamma)`, the modified `t` has exactly the
same covariance defect.  More importantly, right multiplication by `s` and
by `r` preserves the exponent `a` in `(KAP1)`.  Hence, off the same Folner
boundary,

```text
T_s^*D_LT_s=D_L,                T_r^*D_LT_r=D_L.          (KAP8)
```

Moving `D_L` through the two sides of `t s=r s t`, and through
`t r=r t`, therefore leaves the base block-permutation identities unchanged.
The only new errors are supported on the `O(1/L)` boundary.  This proves the
Steinberg estimates for the second map.

Finally, every source block of positive `t`-depth has its predecessor inside
`F_L`; these blocks occupy fraction `1-1/L`.  On a depth-`a` source block,
the two returned `h` operators are the two sides of `(KAP5)`.  Averaging
over `F_L` proves `(KAH5)`.  No assertion about generators outside this
rank-two Heisenberg packet is used or obtained.
