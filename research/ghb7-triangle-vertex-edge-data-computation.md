1. **Vertex groups as matrix groups.** By item 5 of `cckw-ghb2-kms-group-structure`, `ψ` is injective
   on `⟨a,b⟩`, `⟨c,b⟩`, `⟨c,a⟩`. So the subgroups of `SL_4(F_7)` generated by `ψ(a), ψ(b)`, by
   `ψ(c), ψ(b)` and by `ψ(c), ψ(a)` are isomorphic to `X_0, X_1, X_2`, with the edge generators
   matching.
2. **Computation.** `experiments/ghb7-edge-matching/vertex_edge_data.py` (md5
   `c7538da9d3f073f4d1090149ee89939d`) was run on MSI with `/usr/bin/python3.12`, exit code 0. It uses
   only exact arithmetic mod 7:
   - closure under right multiplication by the generators gives the group;
   - the derived subgroup is the closure of the generator commutators, repeatedly closed under
     conjugation by the generators and their inverses until stable;
   - the abelianization order is `|X|/|[X,X]|`, and the 49 products `x^i y^j` are checked to lie in 49
     distinct cosets;
   - conjugacy classes are enumerated orbit by orbit;
   - centralizer orders `|C_X(x^j)|` are counted over all elements.
3. **Output.** `experiments/ghb7-edge-matching/vertex_edge_data.json` records the table in the claim.
4. **Consequences.**
   - Items 1 and 2 of the claim follow from the table.
   - Degrees of irreducibles divide `|X|` and are powers of 7, the number of irreducibles equals the
     number of classes, and `Σ χ(1)^2 = |X|`. With 49 linear characters, the other `k − 49`
     irreducibles have degrees in `{7, 49}`. Degree 49 is impossible because `49 + 49^2 > 2401`, so
     all have degree 7: `(343 − 49)/49 = 6` and `(2401 − 49)/49 = 48`, matching `k − 49 = 6` and `48`.
   - Item 3 is column orthogonality `Σ_χ |χ(g)|^2 = |C_X(g)|`, with each linear character
     contributing 1.
