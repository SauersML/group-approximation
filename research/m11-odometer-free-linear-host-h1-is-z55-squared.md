**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Proof: `m11-odometer-free-linear-host-h1-is-z55-squared-proof`.
The proof is by hand. The script decides the word problem exactly and checks every identity it
uses: the order of `u`, the infinite-order certificate, the mass identities, the shape of
stable words, and a relation census.

**Setting.** The notation is that of `tits-hosts-for-transitive-local-actions` item 6:
`X = {1, …, 11}`, `M_11 = Stab_{M_12}(12)`, `α = (1, …, 11)`, `β` of order 5, and
`b = (b, α, β, 1, …, 1)`, with right actions. `R_0 = ⟨M_11, b⟩` is fully self-replicating. Put

- `c = (c, bα, 1, …, 1)`, with trivial root permutation;
- `u = c b^{-1} = (u, b, β^{-1}, 1, …, 1)`;
- `R'' = ⟨M_11, b, c⟩ = ⟨M_11, b, u⟩`.

No state of `R''` is an odometer. Every state has trivial root except the finitary ones and
the products `bπ`.

**Theorem.**

1. **Candidate.**
   - `R''` is fully self-replicating with root image `M_11`, so `R'' ≅ R'' ≀_X M_11`.
     The reason is that `c@1 = c · ((bα)@2)^{-1}`, and `(bα)@2 ∈ R_0`.
   - `R''` is generated by a finite automaton with states `c, bα, b, u, α, β, β^{-1}, 1`.
     Its activity is linear, so it is amenable (Amir–Angel–Virág; cited).
   - `bα` has infinite order. The certificate: the map `π ↦ ρ`, with `(bπ)^L|_1 = bρ` and
     `L` the length of the `π`-cycle through `1`, sends `α ↦ αβ ↦ β ↦ αβ`. The lengths on
     the 2-cycle are `5` and `5`, and their product `25 > 1` is impossible for elements of
     finite order.
   - So `c` has infinite order, with `c^m|_1 = c^m` and `c^m|_2 = (bα)^m`. Hence `R''` is
     **not contracting**, and Bartholdi's theorem does not apply.
2. **Torsion generators.** `b` and `u` both have order exactly `55`. For `b`:
   `b^{55} = (b^{55}, 1, …, 1)`, which is trivial. The same argument works for `u`.
3. **Homology.** `H_1(R''; Z) ≅ Z/55 ⊕ Z/55`, with basis `[b]`, `[u]`. The two coordinate
   characters are the exponent sums `e_u` and `e_b` of a word in `M_11 ∪ {b, u}^{±1}`, read
   mod `55`. Along the way, `H_1(R_0; Z) ≅ Z/55` via `e_b`. So `b_1(R'') = 0`, and
   `H_1(R''; k) = 0` for every field `k` whose characteristic does not divide
   `|M_11| = 2^4 · 3^2 · 5 · 11`.
4. **Where `R''` stands against the known obstructions.** Every one of the following
   passes:
   - `b_1 ≤ 1`, and more strongly `H_1(R''; k) = 0` for `char k ∤ |M_11|`. This covers
     item 3(d) of `fully-self-replicating-fp-infinity-groups-are-acyclic`.
   - `R''^{ab}` is finite, with primes `5, 11` dividing `|M_11|`. This is the `FP_{d−1}`
     condition there, and it is also degree 1 of the `FP_∞` condition 3(c).
   - The odometer obstruction (item 5 of `m11-torsion-linear-host-has-b1-one`) does not
     apply, because `R''` has no odometer state.
   - Contracting obstructions are out, since `R''` is not contracting.
   - Characters, Σ-invariants, L² invariants and congruence quotients are blind, as for
     every such group.

   `R''` is the first non-contracting fully self-replicating `M_11` group on the graph for
   which no homological obstruction to type `FP_∞` is known in degree 1.
5. **Tits host.** By `tits-hosts-for-transitive-local-actions`,
   `Γ⁺ = (R'' ≀ M_12) *_{R''×R''} (R'' ≀ M_12)` is a finitely generated simple group. It is
   finitely presented, or of type `F_∞`, if and only if `R''` is.

**What is left for the Tits route of `boone-higman-conjecture`.**

- The degree-1 test is passed, and the next test is `H_2`. Item 3(a) of the acyclicity node
  says that if `R''` is of type `FP_{20}`, then `H_2(R''; Q) = 0`. A computation of `H_2(R''; Q)`, for example
  from the relators of the L-presented cover over `⟨b, u⟩` and `M_11`, is the next check.
- Finite presentability of `R''` is open. The candidate relators are
  `u^{55}`, `b^{55}`, the `M_11` relators, and commutation of `R''` elements with disjoint
  supports, closed under the section substitution.

**Calibration.**

- The upper bound `H_1(R'') ≤ (Z/55)^2` holds for any group generated by a perfect group and
  two elements of order `55`, so the content is the lower bound. The descent produces
  exactly the torsion of the stable letters. On `R_0`, whose only stable cycles are powers
  of `b`, it gives `Z/55`.
- The script's census of short relations (item 6) finds no relation that violates the two
  characters.
- The old candidate `c = (c, a, 1, …)`, which has an odometer, gives `b_1 = 2` by
  `m11-linear-activity-group-b1-two-not-finitely-presented`. The same stable-cycle method
  gives `0` here, because the only `b`- or `u`-exponent carried along stable cycles is a
  multiple of `55`.
- Section-exponent screen (item 6 of `m11-torsion-linear-host-has-b1-one`): in the basis
  `b, u` the matrix is `[[1, 0], [1, 1]]`, with `det(t − μ) = (t − 1)^2`. The screen
  allows `b_1 ≤ 2`, so the finer descent is what gives the exact value `0`.
