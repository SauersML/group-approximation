**ESTABLISHED (import).**

**Setting.** `G` is a minimal effective étale groupoid over a Cantor space `X`, `F(G)` its topological full
group and `A(G)` Nekrashevych's alternating full group. Let `H ≤ F(G)`.

**Theorem (Matte Bon).** The following are equivalent:
1. `H` is confined by `A(G)`: some finite `P ⊆ F(G) \ {1}` meets every `A(G)`-conjugate of `H`;
2. there is a unique finite `Q ⊆ X`, possibly empty, with `St^0_{A(G)}(Q) ≤ H ≤ St_{F(G)}(Q)`.

Here `St^0` consists of the elements fixing a neighbourhood of `Q` pointwise, and `St` of the elements
stabilising `Q` setwise. Moreover `|Q| <= |P| - 1`.
