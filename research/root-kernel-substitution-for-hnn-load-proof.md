Quotient by the normal closure of all roots.  The conditional Whitehead word
and every auxiliary root word vanish, leaving only the packet word `g` in the
HNN row.  For the metric form, insert and subtract `tW_pt^(-1)` and `gV`, use
unitary invariance of normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm, and apply the triangle
inequality to obtain `(HRS3)`.
