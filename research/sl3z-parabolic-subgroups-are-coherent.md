Let `P_1` be the stabilizer in `SL_3(Z)` of the line `Q e_1`, and `P_2` the
stabilizer of the plane `Q e_1 + Q e_2`. Then:

- a subgroup of `SL_3(Z)` preserving a rational line (or rational plane) of
  `Q^3` is conjugate in `SL_3(Z)` into `P_1` (or `P_2`);
- `P_1` and `P_2` are coherent;
- so every subgroup preserving a rational line or plane is coherent.

This includes the Borel subgroup and the affine groups `Z^2 ⋊ F` with
`F <= GL_2(Z)`, such as `Z^2 ⋊ F_2`.
