If a finite simple group `S` is generated by two marked `A8` charts satisfying
the Atlas packet, collision `19243`, and `q14`, then

```text
[S:A8] > 11880.                                      (OSI11881)
```

The complete interval after `O8-(2)` has only the equality candidate `A12`.
Every `A8<A12` is a natural eight-point support subgroup, and the Atlas packet
plus collision excludes every pair of such subgroups in every alternating
group.
