title: Transposing Bergman-chart pairs is not algebraic inversion

In the faithful partial-bijection chart of `L_F2(1,2)`, swapping every pair
in the support of a unit computes an adjoint operation, not its inverse as a
ring unit.  Exact searches that use this swap as a generic `invu` operation
are unsound unless every enumerated unit is a genuine permutation unit.
