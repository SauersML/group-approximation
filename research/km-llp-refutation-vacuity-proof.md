(1) A finite-dimensional projective unitary representation of
`Lambda` has image in `PU(n) < GL_(n^2)`, a finitely generated
linear group; by Malcev it is residually finite, and an infinite
finitely generated simple residually finite group is impossible (a
nontrivial finite quotient's kernel is a proper normal subgroup), so
the image -- a quotient of the simple `Lambda` -- is trivial.  Hence
no sequence of cocycles satisfies conditions (1) and (3) of ISW
Theorem A simultaneously.  (2) is
`km-group-ring-h2-vanishes-for-the-lattices` plus the verbatim
statement of Corollary D (it concludes "does not have the LP").
(3) The paper states verbatim that removing condition (3) yields
failure of the LP (not the LLP), and Corollaries D and E are stated
for the LP; source quotations in the
isw-ddjmo-cr-cohomology-sources artifact, 2026-08-23 re-read
section.  (4) is the cited fence node.  No other LLP-refutation
mechanism exists in the recorded literature audit.
