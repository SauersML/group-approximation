**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `H <= Γ` have finite index and let `α : Γ ↷ X` be a continuous action on a
perfect compact metrizable space. If `α|_H` is residually finite in the sense of Kerr–Nowak, then so is `α`.

The finite model is the induced finite `Γ`-set `Γ ×_H E_H`, mapped into `X` by `(t,z) ↦ α(t)z` over a
transversal `T`. Together with restriction this makes residual finiteness of an action depend only on its
restriction to any finite-index subgroup.
