Let `A,B` be C*-subalgebras of the same `B(H)`, suppose `A` is Z-stable, and
assume that at least one of `A` and `B` is separable and nuclear.  If

```text
d(A,B) < 1/420000,
```

then `B` is Z-stable.  In particular, this gives a positive target-side
class for Problem XCIX: `A` may be nonnuclear provided that the nearby
algebra `B` is separable and nuclear.  No separability assumption on `H` is
needed.
