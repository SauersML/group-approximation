**ESTABLISHED (class obstruction), NO PROPERTY `(T)`.**

**Setup: the radical calculus.**  An assignment is a map `R` sending each
countable group `H` to a subset `R(H) <= H`.  The rules below are Horn
closure rules on assignments, and `Rad_MF` satisfies every one of them.

- **(F) forward functoriality.**  `f(R(G)) <= R(H)` for every homomorphism
  `f`.  This includes normality of `R(H)`, the case of inner automorphisms.
- **(S) saturation.**  If `d in R(E)` and `f(d)` normally generates `Q`, then
  `R(Q) = Q` (`mf-radical-saturation-along-any-homomorphism`).
- **(X) closure of the full class.**  The class `{H : R(H) = H}` is closed
  under extensions, directed colimits, free products and amalgams
  (`full-mf-radical-groups-form-an-extension-colimit-class`).
- **(D) radical-kernel descent.**  If `N` is normal in `H` and `N <= R(H)`,
  then the preimage of `R(H/N)` is `R(H)`.
- **(I) finite-index trace.**  For `E <= H` of finite index,
  `R(E) = E intersect R(H)` (`corona-finite-index-radical-heredity`).
- **(C) torsion conclusions.**  This covers any rule whose conclusion is
  `K <= R(H)` for a subgroup `K` generated by elements of finite order.
  Examples: `nonhyperlinear-finite-augmentation-corner-kills-mf`, the
  involution-generated Leavitt absorbers, and the torsion-corner theorems.
- **Input (A).**  `R(St_20(L)) = St_20(L)`, where `L = L_(F_2)(1,2)`.  This is
  endpoint 2, `property-t-free-leavitt-full-mf-radical`.

**Theorem.**

(a) The assignment `Rad*(H) = Rad_MF(H) intersect Phi(H)` satisfies (F), (S),
(X), (D), (I) and (A).  It is trivial on every torsion-free group.  Here
`Phi(H)` is the largest subgroup of `H` lying in the class `Cl` of groups with
no nontrivial finite quotient and no nontrivial torsion-free quotient.

(b) The assignment `Rad#(H) = Rad_MF(H) intersect T(H)` satisfies (F), (S),
(X), (D), (C) and (A).  It is trivial on every torsion-free group.  Here
`T(H)` is the kernel of the universal torsion-free quotient of `H`.
`Rad#` violates only (I).

(c) **Small-kernel kill.**  Let `G` be superperfect (`H_1 G = H_2 G = 0`),
with no nontrivial finite quotient and with a nontrivial element of finite
order.  `G = St_20(L)` is an example.  Let `N` be any group with `Out(N)`
residually finite, for example `Z^k`, `F_n` or a surface group.  Then every
extension `1 -> N -> Et -> G -> 1` contains a copy of `G`, so it has torsion.

**Consequences.**

1. By (a), any derivation of `1 != x in R(T)` for a torsion-free `T` from (A)
   must use a rule outside {F, S, X, D, I}.
2. By (b), any such derivation must also use a rule outside {F, S, X, D, C}.
3. So a derivation inside the calculus must use both a torsion conclusion (C)
   and the finite-index trace (I).  This gate is realized exactly by
   `virtually-torsion-free-infinite-mf-radical-gives-seed`.
4. Otherwise the derivation leaves the calculus.  It must either descend
   across a kernel not known to be in the radical, the gate used by
   `full-mf-radical-abelianized-cover-is-not-mf`, or supply an independent
   analytic non-MF theorem for a torsion-free group.
5. By (c), any torsion-free cover of the Leavitt collapse through the descent
   gate has a kernel `N` with `Out(N)` not residually finite.  In particular
   the kernel is not finitely generated abelian, not free of finite rank and
   not a surface group.  The abelianized-cover kernel `R/[R,R]`, an
   infinitely generated free abelian group, is consistent with this.

**Invariant and death step.**  In (a) the invariant is `Phi`, the largest
`Cl`-subgroup, and every radical-calculus route from (A) dies at the first
torsion-free group.  In (b) the invariant is the torsion-free residual `T`,
and the step that dies is the trace to a torsion-free finite-index subgroup.
For example `T(D_inf) = D_inf` while `T(Z) = 1`.  A genuine violation of (I)
by `Rad#` is exhibited in the proof route.  It uses the swap wreath
`(T_0 x T_0) x| Z/2` over any torsion-free non-MF `T_0`.
