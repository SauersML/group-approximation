## 1. Rectangular covariance has a complete normal form

Let `S:H_S-->H_T` be an isometry, put `T=S^*), and define

```text
Q=I_(H_T)-ST.                                            (OLJ6)
```

Then `TS=I_(H_S)`, `QS=0`, and `TQ=0).  Let
`pi_S,pi_T` be unitary representations of an arbitrary group
`Gamma` satisfying

```text
pi_T(g)S=S pi_S(g)                         for all g in Gamma. (OLJ7)
```

Applying `(OLJ7)` to `g^(-1)` and taking adjoints gives

```text
T pi_T(g)=pi_S(g)T.                                      (OLJ8)
```

Consequently

```text
pi_T(g)ST=S pi_S(g)T=ST pi_T(g).                        (OLJ9)
```

Thus `Q` commutes with `pi_T(Gamma)).  The range `SH_S` and
the cokernel `QH_T` both reduce `pi_T), and the unitary
`S:H_S-->SH_S` identifies the first restriction with `pi_S).
If

```text
alpha=pi_T restricted to QH_T,
```

then

```text
pi_T is unitarily equivalent to alpha direct-sum pi_S.  (OLJ10)
```

Conversely, for every pair of representations `alpha,pi_S), their
direct sum with `S` the inclusion of the second summand satisfies
`(OLJ7)`.  Therefore `alpha` is not merely unknown; it is a free
parameter in the exact solution set of all rectangular covariance rows.

## 2. Literal source words remain in the complement corner

Equation `(OLJ7)` multiplies along words.  For every
`w in Gamma`,

```text
pi_T(w)S=S pi_S(w),               T pi_T(w)=pi_S(w)T.    (OLJ11)
```

Multiplying by `Q` gives

```text
Q pi_T(w)S=0,                     T pi_T(w)Q=0.           (OLJ12)
```

There is a useful algebraic strengthening.  On
`H_T direct-sum H_S`, let

```text
mathcal Q=diag(Q,0).
```

The projection `mathcal Q` commutes with every diagonal operator
`diag(pi_T(w),pi_S(w))` and annihilates both off-diagonal corners
`S` and `T`.  Hence `mathcal Q` is central in the linking
algebra generated by the diagonal Lin words and the rectangular edge.
Every word in that linking algebra is block diagonal between

```text
QH_T             and             (I-Q)H_T direct-sum H_S. (OLJ13)
```

This proves that inserting a Lin word literally as
`S pi_S(w)=pi_T(w)S` does not improve target incidence.  The final
qutrit compression by `Q` kills it exactly, before any singular-value
estimate or polar decomposition is considered.

Commuting full-`C` head coefficients do not change this conclusion.
They preserve `Q` and may be inserted anywhere in `(OLJ11)`;
the mixed corner in `(OLJ12)` remains zero.  To reach `QH_T`, one
must adjoin an operator outside this linking algebra.

## 3. The reflection packet is a functorial D8 gauge

Define operators on `H_T direct-sum H_S` by

```text
a=[ Q  S ],                    z=[ I   0 ].
  [ T  0 ]                       [ 0  -I ]
```

Using `Q^2=Q`, `QS=TQ=0`, `ST=I-Q), and `TS=I), direct
multiplication gives

```text
a^2=z^2=I,                         (az)^4=I.              (OLJ14)
```

Put

```text
D_g=diag(pi_T(g),pi_S(g)).
```

Equations `(OLJ7)--(OLJ9)` give

```text
[a,D_g]=[z,D_g]=1.                                      (OLJ15)
```

The top-right entry of `D_g a` is the literal decorated edge

```text
pi_T(g)S=S pi_S(g).                                     (OLJ16)
```

If `g^2=1), as for a Lin binary observable, then

```text
(D_g a)^2=I.                                            (OLJ17)
```

More generally, all products of decorated reflections reduce to the
corresponding products of the diagonal representation and the fixed
reflection `a).  The generated image is a quotient of

```text
pi(Gamma) times D_8.                                    (OLJ18)
```

This construction is functorial in the completely arbitrary pair
`(alpha,pi_S)`.  Thus involutory group words with literal Lin entries
in the off-diagonal still do not compare the cokernel representation
`alpha` with the source representation.

## 4. Exact qutrit ranks and the free character

Use the regular 288-dimensional qutrit head and its right-regular target
projection `Q=R(Ee_b)`.  The rank calculation is

```text
rank(Q)=16,                      rank(I-Q)=272.           (OLJ19)
```

After an arbitrary common amplification, write `chi_alpha` for the
normalized character of the representation on `QH_T` and `chi_S`
for that on the complement.  Equation `(OLJ10)` gives the exact
character identity

```text
chi_T(g)
 =(16/288)chi_alpha(g)+(272/288)chi_S(g)
 =(1/18)chi_alpha(g)+(17/18)chi_S(g).                    (OLJ20)
```

The tensor absorber is the special choice in which `alpha` is another
copy of the same source representation.  At the opposite extreme one
may take `alpha` trivial, making the qutrit cokernel occurrence-free.
Both choices, and every intermediate representation of the correct
dimension, satisfy all equations through `(OLJ18)` exactly.

The second choice changes `chi_T(g)` by a visible one-eighteenth
summand.  This is not itself a contradiction: none of the rectangular
rows prescribes the target character.  In an approximate regular model,
one would need an additional argument showing simultaneously that

```text
chi_T(g) is approximately 0,
chi_S(g) is controlled,
chi_alpha(g) is forced by the finite target tuple.       (OLJ21)
```

No one of these conclusions implies the other two.

## 5. The relation which is genuinely missing

Let `W` be a proposed equality router with final projection `Q).
Rank equality and full-`C` typing of `W` do not constrain
`alpha).  A non-Morita-neutral row must instead impose covariance on
the cokernel itself, for example

```text
alpha(g)W=W rho(g)                                      (OLJ22)
```

for an independently named source tuple `rho), with `W^*W` already
authenticated before `W` is chosen.  Equivalently, the mixed return
must fail to commute with `mathcal Q` in `(OLJ13)`.

If the intended finite target makes `alpha` trivial or degree four
while `rho` is the degree-two Lin tuple, `(OLJ22)` is exactly the
new information.  It is not a consequence of putting `rho(g)` in
the `S/T` off-diagonal, because `(OLJ12)` says that edge never
reaches `Q).

Therefore the literal off-diagonal construction closes as a no-go.  The
live object is a left-right equality router with an explicit Lin
covariance row on its rank-sixteen target, together with a quantitative
polar modulus.  No nonhyperlinearity conclusion is asserted here.
