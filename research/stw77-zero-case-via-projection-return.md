## Why sufficient

For a zero-dimensional LXXVII map, projection return supplies precisely the
finite-dimensional models required by the established central-splitting
criterion, with `C=phi(A)`.  The criterion gives a unital copy of
`O_infinity` in the relative central sequence algebra, which is the desired
O-infinity-stability.
