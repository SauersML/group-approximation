Citation import. Not reproved here.

**Source.** J. P. Bell, "A generalization of Cobham's theorem for regular sequences", Séminaire Lotharingien de
Combinatoire 54A (2006), Article B54Ap (zbMATH 5660479 lists it as Sémin. Lothar. Comb. 54A, B54Ap (2005)).
Earlier main text cited it as "55 (2005/07), Art. B55f"; that volume and article number are wrong.

**How it was read.** Fetched on 2026-09-18 from the SLC mirror
`https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/s54Abell.pdf`, via curl on MSI (the emis.de mirror returns 403),
and read locally. SHA-256 of the local copy:
`d17dbbb889bb67bae820814b79595ffbb87a612cd1a2fdb1c253ecde448cf005` (209535 bytes; the file renders 6 pages and
stops inside §3, so only §§1–2 and the start of §3 were read). Theorem 1.4, Theorem 1.5 and Definitions 1.2–1.3
are on printed pages 2–3; they are quoted in the target node, verbatim up to typesetting. The proofs (§§3–4) were
not read. They are the published author's, and nothing on main re-proves them.
