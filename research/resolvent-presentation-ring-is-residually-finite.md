**ESTABLISHED (2026-09-18)** through `resolvent-presentation-ring-is-residually-finite-proof`, for every
prime `l`. Lane proof. Citation and hypothesis review PASS by gq-referee-b (report in
`artifacts`); proof-gap reviews by gq-referee-a and gq-referee-c are pending. It uses the path normal form
`resolvent-ring-has-path-normal-form`, and density of alternating words in `F_l[Z * C_m]` together with
Gruenberg's theorem.

**Statement.** For some prime `l` (in fact every `l`), the ring
`R_l = Z<u, x, x', B, e | x x' = x' x = 1, x u = (u+l) x, B(u+1) = (u+1)B = 1,
u e = e u = 0, e^2 = e>` is residually finite: every nonzero element survives in some
finite quotient ring.

**Consequences.** `R_l` is a finitely presented residually finite ring with decidable word problem.
Its corner `e R_l e` contains `Z_(l)` unitally. `E_N(R_l) <= GL_N(R_l)` is a finitely generated
residually finite group containing `Z_(l)` for `N >= 3`. What remains for
`z-localized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group` is the kernel of `St_N(R_l) -> E_N(R_l)`, see
`resolvent-steinberg-rf-residual-is-finitely-normally-generated` and `resolvent-elementary-group-is-fp`.

**Why it matters.** It is necessary for `resolvent-steinberg-group-is-residually-finite`,
by `steinberg-rf-forces-ring-rf`. If it holds, then:
- `R_l` has decidable word problem: equalities are recursively enumerable, and
  nonvanishing is witnessed by finite quotients;
- `R_l` is a finitely presented residually finite ring containing `Z_(l)` in a
  corner;
- `E_N(R_l) ⊆ GL_N(R_l)` is residually finite, and it is finitely presented exactly
  when the kernel of `St_N(R_l) -> E_N(R_l)` is finitely normally generated.

**Evidence recorded before the proof (kept for the record).**
1. **The shift part is residually finite.** Let `D ⊆ R_l` be generated by `u, x, x', B`.
   - `R_l / (e) ≅ D`, so `D ∩ (e) = 0`.
   - `D ≅ A[x^(±1); σ]` is the skew Laurent ring over
     `A = Z[u][(u + 1 + kl)^(-1) : k in Z]`, with `σ(u) = u + l`.
   - The modules `⊕_(j in Z/N) (Z/l^r) f_j`, with `l^(r-1) | N`, `u f_j = l j f_j` and
     `x` the shift, separate `D`. A nonzero `Σ_k a_k x^k` has some rational function
     `a_k != 0`, which is nonzero at `u = l m` for all but finitely many integers `m`,
     and nonzero modulo `l^r` for large `r`.
2. **A large family of finite quotients.** Take `r >= 1`, `N` with `l^(r-1) | N`,
   `d >= 1`, `g in GL_d(Z/l^r)` and an idempotent `E in M_d(Z/l^r)`. On
   `⊕_(j in Z/N) (Z/l^r)^d` let:
   - `u` act on copy `j` by `l j`;
   - `x` map copy `j` to copy `j-1` by the identity, and copy `0` to copy `N-1` by `g`;
   - `B = (u+1)^(-1)`;
   - `e = E` on copy 0 and 0 elsewhere.

   All eight relations hold; the wrap-around step uses `l N ≡ 0 (mod l^r)`. In this
   module `e x^k e` is `E g^(k/N) E` if `N | k`, and 0 otherwise. The modules
   `M_r` of `R̄_l` are the case `d = 1`, `g = 1`, `N = l^(r-1)`.
3. **Torsion of the corner generators is detected.** In `R_l`, `e x^k e` has additive
   order exactly `l^(v_l(k)+1)`.
   - The upper bound: `x^k u = (u+kl) x^k` gives `kl · e x^k e = 0`, and `k/l^(v_l(k))`
     is invertible on the corner.
   - The lower bound: the component `r = v_l(k)+1` of `R̄_l`.

**How the proof went.** Both expected ingredients held:
- the normal form is `resolvent-ring-has-path-normal-form`, proved through universal properties with no
  Diamond Lemma;
- density holds for words in `F_l[Z * C_m]`, with `m = 2` for `l` odd and `m = 3` for `l = 2`.
