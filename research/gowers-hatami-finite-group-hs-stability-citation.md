Established by W. T. Gowers and O. Hatami, *Inverse and stability theorems
for approximate representations of finite groups*, arXiv:1510.04085, in the
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt stability theorem; the displayed formulation and
constants are also quoted as Theorem 3.2 of Slofstra,
arXiv:1806.05267.  The target records exactly the all-pairs form used here.
