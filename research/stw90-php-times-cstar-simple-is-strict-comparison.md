Let `P` be a countable discrete group with Ozawa's PHP property and let `H`
be any countable discrete C*-simple group.  Then

```text
C*_r(P x H)
```

is selfless and therefore has strict comparison with respect to its canonical
trace.  No exactness assumption on `H` is required.

In particular, `C*_r(F_2 x H)` has strict comparison for every C*-simple
group `H`, including every hypothetical nonexact case still outside current
solutions of Problem XC.
