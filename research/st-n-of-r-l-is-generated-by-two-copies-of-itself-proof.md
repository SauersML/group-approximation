**Item 1.**
- `R e* = R ee*`: `y e* = y e* (e e*)` since `e* e = 1`, and `x ee* = (x e) e*`. Likewise `e R = ee* R`.
- `Σ^(e)` fixes the `X_ij(a)` with `i, j < N`, and sends `X_iN(a) -> X_iN(a e*)` and `X_Nj(a) -> X_Nj(e a)`. As `a` ranges
  over `R_L`, these range over all the generators of `H_(ee*)`.
- The image of a homomorphism is generated by the images of the generators, so `Σ^(e)(P) = H_(ee*)`.
- The same argument with `f` gives `Σ^(f)(P) = H_(ff*)`.

**Item 2.**
- `X_iN(a) = X_iN(a ee*) X_iN(a ff*)` and `X_Nj(b) = X_Nj(ee* b) X_Nj(ff* b)`, by additivity and `ee* + ff* = 1`.
- So every generator of `P` lies in `<H_(ee*), H_(ff*)>`.
- **Properness.** `H_(ee*)` is proper (`steinberg-groups-of-r-l-are-not-co-hopfian`, item 1). `H_(ff*) = Σ^(W)(H_(ee*))` is
  proper as well, where `W = ef* + fe*`.

**Item 3.**
- Both corner subgroups contain the `X_ij(a)` with `i, j < N`.
- `K_2(N, R_L)` consists of the symbols `{λ, N+1}_N` on coordinates `1, 2`
  (`leavitt-resolvent-k2-stability-holds`, item 2). So it lies in both.

**Item 4.**
- `Σ^(g) Σ^(h) = Σ^(gh)` for isometries (`leavitt-stabilization-kernels-are-one-step`, item 3). So words of length `k`
  in `e, f` give the twists `Σ^(s_w)`, `|w| = k`, with images `H_(s_w s_w*)` by the argument of item 1.
- `s_w s_w* = s_(w1) s_(w1)* + s_(w2) s_(w2)*` splits every coefficient as in item 2. So `H_(s_w s_w*)` is generated by
  its two children.
