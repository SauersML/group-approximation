Let `Λ` be a Coxeter diagram with vertex set `S`: a finite simplicial graph whose edges `st`
carry labels `m_st ∈ {3, 4, …, ∞}`, with `m_st = 2` for non-edges. For `s ∈ S` let `A_ŝ` be
the standard parabolic subgroup generated by `S − {s}`.

The *Artin complex* `Δ_Λ` is the simplicial complex whose vertices are the left cosets
`g A_ŝ` (`g ∈ A_Λ`, `s ∈ S`), a set of vertices spanning a simplex exactly when the cosets
have non-empty common intersection. A vertex `g A_ŝ` has *type* `ŝ`.

**Criterion.** Suppose `W_Λ` is infinite and `Δ_Λ` is contractible. If `A_{Λ − {s}}` satisfies
the K(π,1) conjecture for every `s ∈ S`, then `A_Λ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture.

Literature import through `artin-k-pi-1-from-contractible-artin-complex-citation`.
For an almost spherical diagram (non-spherical, with every `Λ − {s}` spherical) the second
hypothesis holds by Deligne, so the K(π,1) conjecture for `A_Λ` follows from contractibility
of `Δ_Λ` alone.
