**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let Γ be a countably infinite group and let `f` be a free p.m.p. action of Γ. Then `f` weakly contains every Bernoulli action `κ^Γ` of Γ, where κ is any probability space and `κ^Γ` carries the product measure and the shift.

Source: M. Abért and B. Weiss, *Bernoulli actions are weakly contained in any free action*, arXiv:1103.1063v2, Theorem 1. Verbatim text: [[abert-weiss-free-actions-weakly-contain-bernoulli-citation]].
