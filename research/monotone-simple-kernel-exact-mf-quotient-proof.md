Section 5 gives the argument. The dichotomy supplies the nontrivial
transition-cylinder root `e_12(e)` in the MF radical at rank at least
four. It lies in the endpoint kernel `L`, whose simplicity forces its
normal closure to be all of `L`. Hence `L` is contained in the radical.
The endpoint quotient is residually finite and therefore MF, giving the
reverse containment. The quotient universal property follows immediately.
The preceding dichotomy also supplies the four torsion generators and
property (T) in rank four.
