For every countably infinite P and enumeration nu, the derived
subgroup E_nu' is nontrivial and simple. The abelianization of E_nu
is a quotient of P_ab. In particular, if P is perfect then E_nu
is simple for every enumeration nu.

When P is also finitely generated, this is a finitely generated
simple group containing P. Finite presentation is not asserted.
For a perfect input, a non-finitely-presented E_nu cannot be repaired
by a nontrivial proper quotient: simplicity leaves only E_nu itself
and the trivial quotient.
