**ESTABLISHED.** Imported from arXiv:1310.6289, by A. Minasyan and D. Osin,
*Acylindrical hyperbolicity of groups acting on trees*. A subgroup `C` of `G` is
**weakly malnormal** if some `g in G` has `|C^g ∩ C|` finite.

1. (Corollary `cor:amalg-intr`) Let `G = A *_C B` with `A != C != B`, and let
   `C` be weakly malnormal in `G`. Then `G` is either virtually cyclic or
   acylindrically hyperbolic.
2. (Corollary `cor:HNN-intr`) Let `G` be an HNN extension of `A` with
   associated subgroups `C` and `D`. Suppose `C != A != D` and `C` is weakly
   malnormal in `G`. Then `G` is acylindrically hyperbolic.
