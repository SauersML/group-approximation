Give T^3 x S^3 the BSpin_8-structure of its Lie framing. Let

    c_4(s_1, s_2, s_3, z) = [alpha(s_3), [alpha(s_2), [alpha(s_1), omega(z)]]]

with alpha(s) = diag(s, 1) and omega: SU(2) ⊂ U(2). Then
[T^3 x S^3, c_4]_red is nonzero in Omega-tilde^8_6(SU(2)) ≅ Omega^8_3.

Complete argument. Not independently reviewed.
