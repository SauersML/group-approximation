Average the cocycle identity over its second variable.  Translation
invariance of the finite sum gives `(FWT3)`, so every cocycle is the
derivative of global conjugation.  If the cocycle vanishes on `Q`, its
primitive centralizes `rho(Q)`, proving the relative statement.  For an
approximate cocycle the same average leaves error at most `epsilon`.
Actor-average the primitive; its displacement is at most `eta+epsilon`, and
changing primitives costs twice that displacement, yielding `(FWT8)`.
The regular commutant has real skew dimension `|H|k^2`, which gives `(FWT4)`.
