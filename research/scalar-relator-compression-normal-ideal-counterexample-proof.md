With the matrices in `(SRC1)`, `P pi(a)P=P`, while

```text
pi(bab)=pi(b)pi(a)pi(b)=-pi(a).
```

Hence `P pi(bab)P=-P`.  In the group quotient by the normal closure of
`a`, however, `bab=b^2=1`.  This proves `(SRC3)` and `(SRC4)`.

Since compression of a representation is ucp and its compression of the
unitary `a` is the unit of `B(P C^2)`, Schwarz equality puts `a` in the
multiplicative domain.  The counterexample therefore isolates the exact
logical error: multiplicative-domain identities do not permit simultaneous
multiplication on both sides by elements outside that domain.

For the final assertion, `(SRC6)` makes each conjugate relator reduce `P H`
and act there by its scalar phase.  The displayed cyclic-hull calculation
then makes every original relator act with that phase on the invariant
subspace generated by `P H`; restricting `pi` to this subspace factors
through the quotient.  The converse follows by evaluating every conjugate
relation in a quotient representation.
