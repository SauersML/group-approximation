**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G`, `Gamma = pi(P) <= G` and `S` be as in
`fournier-facio-torsion-free-skeleton`: `Gamma` is Kazhdan, hence finitely generated by a
finite set `F_Gamma`, and `pi(S) <= Gamma` is a copy of a finitely presented infinite simple
group. Let `sigma : Gamma -> U(prod_U M_n)` be a homomorphism with coordinates `sigma_n`.

If (H1) holds at `sigma`, i.e. after `o(n)` padding there are genuine representations
`pi_n : Gamma -> U(n')` with `n'/n -> 1` and `max_(s in F_Gamma) ||sigma_n(s) - pi_n(s)||_2 -> 0`
along `U`, then `sigma(pi(S)) = {1}`.

Consequently (H1) fails at every model that is nontrivial on `pi(S)`. That includes the
restriction to `Gamma` of every trace-preserving `sigma : G -> U(prod_U M_n)`, since such a
model has `tau(sigma(s)) = 0` for `s != 1`.

**Reading.**
- **Architecture.** The Hilbert--Schmidt rigidity architecture that assumes vertex rounding
  cannot touch this group: `hs-rounding-and-actor-gap-force-ccr`, the unit-type chain behind
  `theorem-e-vertex-rounds-to-unit-type-representations`, and every excess or aspect-ratio
  estimate conditioned on (H1) are void at all faithful models. A non-hyperlinearity proof for
  it must work with approximate vertex coordinates throughout.
- **Scope of (H1).** Read literally over every infranormal Kazhdan pair,
  `hs-stable-vertex-rounding-for-every-model` implies that `G` has no trace-preserving model,
  i.e. that `G` is not hyperlinear. This parallels the existing scope note for the Leavitt pair.
- **The two named witnesses have opposite stability profiles.**
  - A Kun--Thom coset wreath can be hyperlinear only through flexible instability of the
    residually finite vertex `EL_r(F_q[x_1..x_d])` at actor-extendable models
    (`kt-hyperlinear-coset-wreath-forces-nonroundable-vertex`), a theorem of a kind not known
    for any Kazhdan group.
  - For Fournier--Facio the vertex instability is automatic. The positive branch meets no
    rounding obstruction. Its gates are the modelling gates of `fournier-facio-group-hyperlinear`:
    a hyperlinear model of the finitely presented simple factor and the binary-tree slot
    geometry.
