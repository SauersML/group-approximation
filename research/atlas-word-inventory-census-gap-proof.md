The six-relator and raw/comb artifacts were audited field by field.  The
former contain literal relation words but no common-corner block map to the
radical-quotient arrows.  The latter contain two operator names but no
relation row involving those names.  The mixed-collision JSON contains only
summary counts, while its generator keeps collision normal forms in a local
variable and emits neither those forms nor bounded fillings in the six
relators.

The missing type map cannot be supplied by an arbitrary identification of
equal-dimensional blocks.  `separated-channel-identities-retain-rectangular-escape`
shows that separated carrier data and coefficient transports retain the
rectangular escape, while the two-compressor centralizer calculation leaves
the full multiplicity holonomy.  Hence a legitimate row must declare common
source and target projectors and obtain its multiplicity path from the same
literal equation.  Since no current artifact gives those declarations or
defines `W,R0,R1,L0,L1` as compressed word blocks, `[C D]` is not yet a
well-defined output of the repository data.  The acquisition artifact lists
the finite fields which make it well-defined without assuming the desired
holonomy collapse.

Moreover a universal algebraic choice of the common cut would pass unchanged
to the exact Leavitt tracial model and would force the forbidden pairing
collapse there.  The missing declaration is therefore not merely another
word projector: it must include a finite-matrix-only positive-mass selection
and its uniform HS estimate.  This prevents the census schema from silently
crossing the finite-multiplicity category boundary.
