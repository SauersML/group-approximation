**ESTABLISHED** through `z-localized-embeds-in-fg-rf-soluble-group-proof`. Lane proof,
not independently reviewed. No priority is claimed.

**Construction (the Euler lamplighter).** Let `l` be a prime and
`M = Z_(l)[y, y^(-1)]`, with basis `f_j = y^j`. Let `x` be multiplication by
`y^(-1)`, so `x f_j = f_(j-1)`. Let `β = 1 + l · y d/dy`, so `β f_j = (1 + l j) f_j`,
an automorphism because `1 + lj` is a unit of `Z_(l)`. Put
`H = <x, β> <= Aut_(Z_(l))(M)` and `G_l = M ⋊ H`.

**Statement.**
1. `H ≅ Z ≀ Z`. The conjugates `β_k = x^(-k) β x^k = diag(1 + l(j - k))_j`,
   `k in Z`, are a basis of a free abelian group, and `x` shifts them.
2. `G_l` is generated by `f_0, x, β`, and `Z_(l) f_0 ⊆ M ⊆ G_l`. So
   `(Z_(l),+) <= G_l`.
3. `G_l` is soluble of derived length at most 3 (abelian by `Z ≀ Z`), and at least 3
   by `fg-metabelian-groups-have-roots-at-finitely-many-primes`.
4. `G_l` is residually finite, through the finite quotients
   `(⊕_(j in Z/N) (Z/l^r) f_j) ⋊ H̄` with `l^(r-1) | N`.
5. `G_l` has solvable word problem and is effectively residually finite: from a word
   `w != 1` one computes a finite quotient in which `w` survives.

**Reading for the bottleneck.** The obstructions this lane found in the literature to
embedding a finitely generated group in a finitely presented residually finite group
are two. Such subgroups have solvable word problem, and they are effectively
residually finite (Rauzy, arXiv:2002.02540v2, whose counterexample fails the second).
`G_l` passes both, so neither rules out `z-localized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group`.

An affirmative answer to the effective-RF form of the Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir
question (arXiv:1204.6506, §1.1.7), left open by Rauzy, would settle the bottleneck
through `G_l`. That form asks whether every finitely generated, effectively residually
finite group with solvable word problem embeds in a finitely presented residually
finite group. Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir ask the unrestricted question. Rauzy refuted only
the unrestricted form. The lane found no source settling the restricted form, on a
bounded search.
