Take the premise triangle `T` with finite simple vertex groups `A_v` and a quotientless
colimit. Let `T°` be its core, with `B_v` the subgroup of `A_v` generated by the two edge
groups at `v`.

`T°` has trivial face group, nontrivial edge groups and generated vertex groups. By (U1)
it has the same half-girths as `T`, so it is hyperbolic. By (U4) `Λ(T°)` has no finite
quotient injective on every `B_v`. This is (U5) (b) => (a) of
`simple-vertex-triangles-are-universal-for-triangle-vtf`.
