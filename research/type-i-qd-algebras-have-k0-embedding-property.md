**OPEN.**  Let `A` be a separable quasidiagonal type I C\*-algebra, and
`G ⊆ K_0(A)` a subgroup with `G ∩ K_0^+(A) = {0}`.  Then there is an injective
\*-homomorphism `ρ : A → C` into a quasidiagonal algebra with `ρ_*(G) = 0`
(Brown--Dadarlat Definition 4.4).

By Theorem A of the artifact this is equivalent to
`bk-type-i-stably-finite-algebras-are-qd`.  As a statement about all such `A`
at once, it is also equivalent to its restriction to cyclic `G = Zx`
(artifact §3, (b) ⇔ (e)).  For a single algebra only the implication from all
`G` to cyclic `G` is claimed.

Known instances:
- `A ⊗ K ≅ C_0(X) ⊗ K`, asserted by Brown--Dadarlat without proof;
- AF algebras, by Spielberg (not fetched);
- `A` locally approximated by Moutzouris' class `Y`;
- by the reductions in artifact §6.1, stable isomorphism and unitization.

## Attempts

- **Rank functions on CCR algebras (partial).**  Artifact §6.2, Lemma 4,
  sketched.  Let `A` be CCR and `x ∈ K_0(A)`.
  - If `r_x(π) = π_*(x) ∈ Z` takes both signs on `Â`, direct sums of
    compressions of irreducible representations kill `x`.  The semigroup
    generated by the values is a group.
  - The same holds if `r_x` vanishes on a dense set.
  - Brown--Dadarlat Theorem 4.12 then gives a killing embedding.
- **Where it dies.**
  - Take `C(S²) ⊕ C` and `x = (β, 1)`.  Then `x` is singular and `r_x ≥ 0`, with
    a non-dense zero set.  Every norming sum of compressions sends `x` to a
    positive integer.  Killing `x` needs twisted almost-representations of
    `C(S²)` with negative Bott index, which no rank-function argument sees.
  - For CCR algebras with nontrivial Dixmier--Douady class no source of such
    twisted maps is known.  That case was not analysed.
- **Toeplitz points.**  Outside the CCR part, compressions of irreducible
  representations are not almost multiplicative: the shift gives defect `1`.
  The Toeplitz pair algebra (`toeplitz-pair-algebra-is-qd-with-non-qd-irreps`)
  shows that the killing maps must then come from reducible representations.
- **Through extensions (circular).**  A singular `G ⊆ K_0(A)` need not come from
  an ideal, and its image in a quotient need not be singular.  No permanence of
  the embedding property under extensions is known.
