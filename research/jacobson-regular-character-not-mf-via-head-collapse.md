A regular-trace model of `EL_5(J)=St_5(J)` has `||sigma_k(g)-1||_2^2 -> 2`
for every `g!=1`.  It therefore defines a norm-corona homomorphism that
retains the head `x_13(1-ST)`.  A property-`(T)`-free proof that every such
homomorphism kills the head excludes the regular character with the same
provenance.
