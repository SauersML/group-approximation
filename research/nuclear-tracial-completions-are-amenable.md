Let `A` be a unital nuclear C*-algebra with `T(A) ≠ ∅`, and let `(M, X)` be its uniform tracial
completion, with `X` the induced copy of `T(A)`.

Then `(M, X)` is amenable in the sense of Carrión–Castillejos–Evington–Gabe–Schafhauser–Tikuisis–White
(CCEGSTW), Definition 4.1:
- `id_M` is tracially nuclear;
- that is, there are finite-dimensional algebras `F_λ` and c.p. maps `M → F_λ → M` whose composites
  converge to `id_M` in the point-`‖·‖_{2,X}` topology.
