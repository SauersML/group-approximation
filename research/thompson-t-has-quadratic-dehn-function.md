**Established (literature import).** The Dehn function of Thompson's group T is
quadratic: `δ_T(n) ≃ n^2`. More generally, the source's abstract states that
all Higman–Thompson groups `T_n` have quadratic Dehn function.

Earlier upper bounds were `n^7` (Guba 2000) and quintic (K. Wang, Z. Zheng and
J. Zhang, 2015, as recorded in the introduction of Lorrain, arXiv:2608.03813v2).

This is the T part of Zaremsky Problem 2.5
(`zaremsky-2-05-dehn-functions-of-thompson-t-and-v`), which the list's own
update already reports.
