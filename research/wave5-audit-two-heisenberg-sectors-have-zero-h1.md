section 4, take the two F8 representations of central characters
(0,6,0) and (1,0,0), with the symplectic radical acting trivially.

`H^1(K7;V)=0`.

This is a bounded two-sector computation, not a vanishing theorem
for the whole class-two cover or all mixed-prime finite quotients.
