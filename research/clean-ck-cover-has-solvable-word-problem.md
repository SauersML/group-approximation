**OPEN.** Let the marking be clean, as in
`clean-ck-cover-confines-poison-to-covering-kernel`: `S <= H <= [T,T]`, with `H`
and `T` finitely presented with solvable word problem, `g = s`, and
`U = Z[T]<p,q>/(p(1-g)q-1)`. Then the finitely presented marked Steinberg cover
`Gamma ->> EL_4(U)` of `ck-steinberg-marked-cover` has solvable word problem.

**Reduction.** By (b) and (c) of the confinement node, the membership `w in K`
is decidable. So the claim is equivalent to: triviality is decidable for
words of `Gamma` lying in `K = ker(Gamma -> EL_4(U))`. Since `Gamma` is finitely
presented, triviality is r.e. So what is missing is a co-r.e. certificate of
nontriviality for kernel words.

**Why it matters.**
- If it holds, then `Gamma` has no poisoned elements. A clean envelope can then
  be refuted only by a structural obstruction, not a computability one.
- A proof would likely go through a normal form for `K_2(4,U)` of the
  Diamond-lemma ring. For instance, it would follow if `K` were central and
  finitely generated, or if `K_2(4,U)` were computable symbol by symbol.

**Sufficient input (sketch).** Suppose `K` is abelian and generated by
explicitly known words `k_1, ..., k_r`, with a known relation lattice
`Lambda <= Z^r`. Then the claim holds. Given a word `w`, decide `w in K` by
(b). If `w in K`, search, using the r.e. word problem of the finite
presentation, for an exponent vector `a` with `w = k^a`. Then `w = 1` iff
`a in Lambda`.

## Attempts

1. **Centrality of unstable `K_2` (swarm-0917-w4-pull-bh-3).** *Stalls at the
   first step.*
   - The plan was to show that `K_2(4,U)` is central in `St_4(U)`. Then `K`
     would be central in `Gamma`, and one would look for explicit symbol
     generators.
   - The known centrality theorems for rank-`n` Steinberg groups (van der
     Kallen, Tulenbaev) need quasi-finite or stable-rank hypotheses. `U`
     contains the free sandwich `p, q` and is neither quasi-finite nor of
     small stable rank.
   - Even with `K` central, it need not be finitely generated, and its
     relation lattice is not known to be computable.
2. **Diamond normal form for the Steinberg group.** *Deferred.* A rewriting
   system on the symbols `x_ij(u)`, with `u` in Diamond normal form, has
   critical pairs coming from the commutator relations. These were not
   computed.
