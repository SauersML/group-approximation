Slofstra--Zhang, arXiv:2412.10305v2, Theorem 1.3 states the order-`p`
conclusion under exactly the non-zero-divisor and `(4,4)` or `(3,6)`
degree/girth hypotheses.  Definition 2.1 and Lemma 2.2 identify collapse of
`J^k` with a closed picture of phase `k`.  Lemma 2.5 supplies the reduced
vertex-degree and label properties, Lemmas 2.6--2.7 transfer incidence girth
to face size, and Lemma 2.8 is the Euler obstruction used in the theorem's
proof.
