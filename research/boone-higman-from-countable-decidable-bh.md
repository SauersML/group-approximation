title: Finitely generated decidable groups are countable decidable groups
target: boone-higman-conjecture
requires: [countable-decidable-groups-embed-in-fp-simple-groups]
---

A finitely generated group with solvable word problem, with its finite generating
set as the enumeration, is a countable group with a solvable word problem in the
sense of `countable-decidable-groups-embed-in-fp-simple-groups`. So that claim
embeds it in a finitely presented simple group. ∎
