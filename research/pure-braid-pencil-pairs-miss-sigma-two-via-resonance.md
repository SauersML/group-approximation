`P_n` is the fundamental group of the braid arrangement complement in `C^n`
(`l = n`), and `2 <= n - 1`. The upper bound gives
`Sigma^2(P_n) ⊆ Sigma^2(M, Z) ⊆ S(R^2(P_n; R))^c`. The subspaces
`L_{U_1} + L_{U_2}` are defined over `Q` by linear equations in the `a_{ij}`, so
their real points are real 4-dimensional subspaces inside `R^2(P_n; R)`. Every
nonzero character in them is therefore outside `Sigma^2(P_n)`.
