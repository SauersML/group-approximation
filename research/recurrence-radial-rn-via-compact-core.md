The first two prerequisites construct the scalar and matrix forward
observation groups of type `F_∞` with injective shift and full base
isotropy. The arithmetic prerequisite verifies all hypotheses of the
compact-core theorem, which gives type `F_∞` for the resulting
finite-germ group for every root number. The final prerequisite
identifies that group with `V_(b,r)(K)` and supplies noncontraction
of its displayed state action. This uses no assertion that `K`
itself is `F_∞` and no previously proposed polynomial-tower theorem.
The listed mathematical prerequisites remain unresolved.
