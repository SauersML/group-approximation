For a single-seam group with infinite simple Kazhdan endpoint groups,
Rad_MF(H)=Rad_am(H)=Rad_lf(H)=L, the simple locally finite seam kernel.
In particular L is the largest locally finite normal subgroup as well
as the largest amenable normal subgroup. This is a theorem about this
family, not a general criterion for MF groups or all dynamical rings.
