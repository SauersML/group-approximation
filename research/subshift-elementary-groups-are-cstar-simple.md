title: The simple Kazhdan LEF groups EL_n(LC(X,F_2)⋊Z) are C*-simple
---

**OPEN.** Let `X` be an infinite minimal subshift, `n ≥ 3` and `G = EL_n(LC(X,F_2) ⋊_T Z)`. Is `C*_r(G)` simple?

**Known.**
- `C*_r(G)` has a unique tracial state (`subshift-elementary-group-reduced-cstar-has-unique-trace`).
- For countable groups, unique trace is strictly weaker than C*-simplicity: Le Boudec 2017 constructs groups with trivial
  amenable radical that are not C*-simple (recalled, not re-read).
- Criterion (Kennedy; Le Boudec–Matte Bon; now read at source, `cstar-simple-iff-amenable-subgroups-not-confined`): a
  countable group is C*-simple iff it has no nontrivial amenable confined subgroup. Here `H` is confined if some finite
  `P ⊆ G \ {1}` meets every conjugate `gHg^{-1}`.

**Progress (sk-cstar-simple, 2026-09-13, unreviewed).** None of the natural amenable test subgroups is confined:
- root subgroups `e_ij(R)`, for every field and `n ≥ 3` (`elementary-root-subgroups-over-crossed-products-are-not-confined`);
- `GL_n(LC(X,F_2))`, containing the locally finite union `EL_n(LC(X,F_2))` (`locally-constant-gl-n-subgroup-is-not-confined`);
- the images of `[[T]]'` and `[[T]]` (`topological-full-group-images-in-el3-are-not-confined`);
- for `n = 3`, the half-orbit cut stabilizer `S_y`, and with it the locally finite Kakutani–Rokhlin tower unit groups `L_y`
  (`cut-stabilizer-subgroups-of-el3-are-not-confined`).

The mechanism is the same throughout: conjugators with large `u`-exponents (`e_31(u^N)`, Whitehead diagonals
`diag(u^{N_1},u^{N_2},u^{N_3})`) push any finite set out of the subgroup. The one delicate case is diagonal elements, which
the cut stabilizer removes first by an elementary conjugator chosen through Neumann's covering lemma.

**Why it matters.** A yes makes `C*_r(G_X)` a simple unital C*-algebra with unique trace, generated by an infinite simple
Kazhdan LEF group. It separates these groups from the non-C*-simple simple groups, and it frames the open MF question for
`C*_r(G_X)` (sk-cstar-mf).

## Attempts
1. **Commutator lemma** (`confined-subgroup-commutator-lemma`, Le Boudec–Matte Bon).
   - What it needs: a faithful permutation action with rich rigid stabilizers and displacement configurations.
   - Where it dies: the natural actions of `G` are linear, on orbit modules `⊕_{t∈Z} F_2^3`. There the pointwise fixator of
     the complement of a proper set of vectors is trivial as soon as that complement spans, so the lemma does not start. No
     micro-supported compact `G`-space is known.
2. **Rigid supports.**
   - Setup: the subgroups `G_U` over clopen `U` satisfy `C_G(G_U) = G_{X∖U}`
     (`subshift-elementary-group-rigid-supports-are-mutual-centralizers`). Each `G_U` contains `EL_3` of a corner algebra, hence
     nonabelian free subgroups.
   - Plan: a confined `H` meets conjugates of a finite `P`. An analogue of displacement configurations, with supports in `X`
     playing the role of the `Ω_σ`, would put commutators `[h,x]`, `x ∈ G_U`, inside `H`, and so give `H` nonamenable pieces of
     some `G_U`.
   - Where it dies: matrices over `R` move mass along orbits without acting pointwise on `X`, so conditions (ii)–(iii) of a
     displacement configuration have no direct meaning yet.
3. **Amenable subgroups to test first.**
   - The root subgroups, the locally constant `GL_n`, `[[T]]'` and the Kakutani–Rokhlin tower unit groups are all done
     (Progress above).
   - Showing test subgroups are not confined is necessary, not sufficient. Kennedy's criterion quantifies over all amenable
     subgroups.
4. **Transfer from subgroups.**
   - `EL_3(F_2[u^{±1}]) ≤ G` is a linear group with trivial amenable radical, hence C*-simple (BKKO, recalled).
   - C*-simplicity does not pass to overgroups.
   - Directed unions (`cstar-simple-groups-products-directed-unions`) do not apply, since `G` is finitely generated.
5. **Character and IRS rigidity** (`subshift-elementary-groups-are-character-rigid`, `subshift-elementary-groups-have-trivial-irs`,
   unreviewed).
   - These classify invariant probability measures on `Sub(G)`.
   - Kennedy's criterion concerns uniformly recurrent subgroups, i.e. minimal closed invariant subsets of `Sub(G)`. For a
     nonamenable group these need not carry invariant measures, so trivial IRS does not rule out an amenable URS.
   - No implication is known in either direction for these groups.
6. **Idempotent displacement (next target, not done).** A general proof that no amenable subgroup is confined needs a
   commutator lemma for `G` in which idempotents play the role of subsets.
   - Rigid subgroups would be `G_E = G ∩ (I + E M_3(R) E)`, orthogonal idempotents would replace disjoint sets, and displacement
     would be `gEg^{-1} ⊥ E`.
   - Why it might work: fibrewise elements such as `e_12(1)` move no clopen set but displace rank-one idempotents over small
     clopen sets.
   - What it would give: for a tower corner `E` of rank at least 3, `G_E` contains `EL_3` of a first-return crossed product,
     which is infinite, simple and nonamenable. A transferred lemma would then put a nonamenable subgroup inside every
     confined `H`.
   - Missing: the combinatorial displacement conditions (i)–(iii) for idempotents, and a replacement for "every nontrivial
     conjugacy class of `R_G(Ω_σ)` has more than `r` elements".
