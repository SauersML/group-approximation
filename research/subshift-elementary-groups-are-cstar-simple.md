title: The simple Kazhdan LEF groups EL_n(LC(X,F_2)⋊Z) are C*-simple

**OPEN.** Let `X` be an infinite minimal subshift, `n ≥ 3` and `G = EL_n(LC(X,F_2) ⋊_T Z)`. Is `C*_r(G)` simple?

**Known.**
- `C*_r(G)` has a unique tracial state (`subshift-elementary-group-reduced-cstar-has-unique-trace`).
- For countable groups, unique trace is strictly weaker than C*-simplicity: Le Boudec 2017 constructs groups with trivial amenable radical that are not C*-simple (recalled, not re-read).
- Criterion (Kennedy; Le Boudec–Matte Bon; recalled, not re-read): a countable group is C*-simple iff it has no nontrivial amenable confined subgroup. Here `H` is confined if some finite `P ⊆ G \ {1}` meets every conjugate `gHg^{-1}`.

**Why it matters.** A yes makes `C*_r(G_X)` a simple unital C*-algebra with unique trace, generated by an infinite simple Kazhdan LEF group. It separates these groups from the non-C*-simple simple groups, and it frames the open MF question for `C*_r(G_X)` (sk-cstar-mf).

## Attempts
1. **Commutator lemma** (`confined-subgroup-commutator-lemma`, Le Boudec–Matte Bon).
   - What it needs: a faithful permutation action with rich rigid stabilizers and displacement configurations.
   - Where it dies: the natural actions of `G` are linear, on orbit modules `⊕_{t∈Z} F_2^3`. There the pointwise fixator of the complement of a proper set of vectors is trivial as soon as that complement spans, so the lemma does not start. No micro-supported compact `G`-space is known.
2. **Rigid supports.**
   - Setup: the subgroups `G_U` over clopen `U` satisfy `C_G(G_U) = G_{X∖U}` (`subshift-elementary-group-rigid-supports-are-mutual-centralizers`). Each `G_U` contains `EL_3` of a corner algebra, hence nonabelian free subgroups.
   - Plan: a confined `H` meets conjugates of a finite `P`. An analogue of displacement configurations, with supports in `X` playing the role of the `Ω_σ`, would put commutators `[h,x]`, `x ∈ G_U`, inside `H`, and so give `H` nonamenable pieces of some `G_U`.
   - Where it dies: matrices over `R` move mass along orbits without acting pointwise on `X`, so conditions (ii)–(iii) of a displacement configuration have no direct meaning yet.
3. **Amenable subgroups to test first.** Showing that each of these is not confined is a necessary first step; none is done.
   - the root subgroups `e_ij(R)` (abelian);
   - the locally finite union `⋃_k EL_3(A_k)` over a refining Kakutani–Rokhlin sequence (the algebraic AF subalgebra);
   - `[[T]]'` embedded through `a ↦ diag(a,1,1)` (`topological-full-group-embeds-in-subshift-elementary-group`).
4. **Transfer from subgroups.**
   - `EL_3(F_2[u^{±1}]) ≤ G` is a linear group with trivial amenable radical, hence C*-simple (BKKO, recalled).
   - C*-simplicity does not pass to overgroups.
   - Directed unions (`cstar-simple-groups-products-directed-unions`) do not apply, since `G` is finitely generated.
