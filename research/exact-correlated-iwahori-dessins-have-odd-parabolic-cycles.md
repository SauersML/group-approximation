Let `(u_0,e_0)` and `(u_1,e_1)` be finite permutation actions of
`B=Z*C_2`, each extending to a permutation action of
`C=C_2*C_3`.  If, after a point relabelling,

```text
(u_0,e_0)=alpha(u_1,e_1),
```

for the Iwahori Nielsen involution `alpha`, then every cycle of `u_0` and
every cycle of `u_1` has odd length.

Consequently the exactly correlated modular dessin locus lies entirely in
the odd-parabolic sector.  More generally, for a sequence with uniformly
bounded parabolic cycle lengths, asymptotically exact Nielsen matching
forces the proportion of points in even parabolic cycles to tend to zero.
This does not exclude long even cycles: changing a vanishing number of
arrows can merge or split macroscopic cycles, so no unbounded-cycle Hamming
conclusion is asserted.
