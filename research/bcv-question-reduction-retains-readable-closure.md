Let `S` be a finite-dimensional signed-permutation ZPC strategy for a
tailored game `G`, and let `QR_k(S)` be the honest strategy used in
Bowen--Chapman--Vidick Question Reduction, acting on

```text
ell^2(F_2^k) tensor H.
```

Write `D_infinity(S)` and `D_infinity(QR_k(S))` for the Boolean
algebras generated by the readable spectral projections and all of their
structural-unreadable conjugates.  Restrict the source algebra to vertices
in the support of the question distribution.  Under the natural
block-diagonal representation,

```text
ell^infinity(F_2^k) tensor D_infinity(S)
    subseteq D_infinity(QR_k(S)).                         (QRR1)
```

Consequently, if the source algebra has `b` atoms, then the honest
Question Reduction algebra has at least `2^k b` atoms.  In particular,
finite verifier control and the syntactic compression fixed point do not
make the canonical readable orbit periodic with a size independent of the
strategy being compressed.
