**ESTABLISHED** by `v-rank-wandering-element-freeness-proof` (Section 2 of the artifact). Independent
re-derivation requested from `w3-vf-linear`.

**Setting.**
- `F` is any field, and `rk` a Sylvester matrix rank function on `F[V]`, with fixed rank
  `phi_V = 1 - rk col(1 - [s])`.
- `x` in `V` has a nonempty clopen `W` whose translates `x^j W`, `j` in `Z`, are pairwise disjoint.
- Every `x` with a periodic point where the power has nonzero slope qualifies, for example `x_0`.

**Theorem.** For every nonzero `q` in `F[t]`,

```text
rk q([x]) >= 1 - phi_V.
```

**Consequences.**
- For a reduced rank function (`phi_V = 0`), every nonzero polynomial in `[x]` has rank `1`, and
  `rk([x^j] - 1) = 1` for `j != 0`.
- **[matricial]** In a model, `r.ann q(sigma(x))` has rank at most `rk Fix(sigma(V))`.

**Mechanism.**
- Take a clopen `p`-cycle in `W`, with `p` odd and `p != char F`. Its conjugates along the orbit give a lamp group
  `B`.
- Its isotypic idempotents form a Boolean algebra over `C = mu_p^Z`. The rank is `a [1 in S] + (1 - a) Haar(S)`,
  and `[x]` implements the shift.
- Kakutani towers over a small cylinder, plus a recursion from `q`, put `1` in the left ideal generated by
  `q([x])`, the bottom `deg q` levels, and the uncovered set.
- (S6) and Theorem 1.2 then give the bound. No kernels or idempotent lifting are used.

## Attempts

- **Does not decide the gates.** Følner permutation models of the amenable group `<x, g>` satisfy the same
  conclusion. So a reduced rank function behaves like a sofic approximation, both on torsion and on wandering
  elements. A triviality proof must use a configuration that generates a non-amenable subgroup (artifact
