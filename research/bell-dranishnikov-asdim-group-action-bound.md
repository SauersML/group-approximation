title: A finitely generated group acting isometrically on a space of finite asymptotic dimension with finite-asdim coarse stabilizers has finite asymptotic dimension (Bell-Dranishnikov)
---

**ESTABLISHED (import).**

**Statement.** Let `Γ` be a finitely generated group acting by isometries on a
metric space `X`, and fix `x0 ∈ X`. Put
`W_R(x0) = {γ ∈ Γ : d(γ x0, x0) ≤ R}`, with the word metric of `Γ`. If
`asdim X ≤ k` and `asdim W_R(x0) ≤ n` for all `R`, then `asdim Γ ≤ n + k`.

**Source.** G. Bell and A. Dranishnikov, *A Hurewicz-type theorem for asymptotic
dimension and applications to geometric group theory*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc.
358 (2006), arXiv:math/0407431, Theorem 2.
