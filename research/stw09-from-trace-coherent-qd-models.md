The trace-coherent criterion constructs a homomorphism from `A` into the AF
limit and a target trace extending the given `tau`.  Since `tau` is faithful,
the homomorphism has zero kernel.  Thus it is an AF embedding, in fact one
with the stronger trace-extension conclusion.
