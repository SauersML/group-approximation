**ESTABLISHED.**  Use the notation and constant `K>=1` (enlarging it if
necessary) of
`leakage-small-polar-compressions-obey-projective-transfer`, and put
`r=|S_A|`.  Suppose a polar compression of an exact arithmetic
representation and a unitary `U` satisfy

```text
E_h >= gamma,
sum_(c in S_C) E_c <= gamma/(2K).                       (PSC1)
```

Then some named generator `s in S_A`, with
`T_s=Ppi(s)P`, has a spectral projection

```text
B_s=1_[0,sqrt(1-theta)](|T_s|),
theta=gamma/(4Kr),                                      (PSC2)
```

of normalized corner trace at least `theta`:

```text
tr_PH(B_s)>=theta.                                      (PSC3)
```

Thus every fixed-gap projective-transfer enemy in the exact-dilation/polar
class loses a fixed positive amount of singular overlap on a fixed positive
fraction of one generator's corner.  The remaining regime is not merely
“positive codimension”: it contains a macroscopic block on which the raw
compression is quantitatively nonisometric before polar completion.

This is a decomposition, not a contradiction.  Relations among the polar
unitaries may conceivably repair or hide that block.  A proof of full
projective transfer must charge this bad singular mass to presentation
defect or decode arithmetic incidence across it; a countermodel must exploit
exactly such a block while retaining canonical character and vanishing
relator defect.
