Notation as in the target: `A = SL_n(Z[1/p]) < L = SL_n(Q_p)`,
`C = SL_n(Z) < K = SL_n(Z_p)`, `Lambda'` torsion-free cocompact in `L`,
`X = L/Lambda'`, `mu` the `L`-invariant probability, `r = |K \ L/Lambda'|`.

**Step 0 (density).**  `C` is dense in `K` and `A` is dense in `L`: the
root subgroups `e_ij(Z[1/p])` are dense in `e_ij(Q_p)`, which generate
`SL_n(Q_p)`, and `e_ij(Z)` is dense in `e_ij(Z_p)`, which generate
`SL_n(Z_p)` (strong approximation).  `L` has no proper open subgroup of
finite index: such a subgroup would meet each root subgroup `(Q_p, +)` in a
finite-index, hence (divisibility) full, subgroup, so it contains all root
subgroups.  Consequently every finite-index subgroup of `A` is still dense
in `L`.

**Step 1 (K-orbits).**  Torsion-freeness gives `K cap x Lambda' x^-1 = 1`
for every `x`, so each `K`-orbit `K x Lambda'/Lambda'` is `K`-equivariantly
isomorphic to `K` with Haar measure and has `mu`-measure
`Haar(K)/Haar(L/Lambda')`.  Since `K` is open and `Lambda'` cocompact, there
are finitely many orbits, `r` of them, and `r . Haar(K) = covol(Lambda')`;
a finite-index subgroup of `Lambda'` multiplies `covol` by the index, so
`r >= 2` is achievable.  Fix the orbit decomposition `X = P_1 sqcup ... sqcup P_r`.

**Step 2 (ergodicity, freeness, trace).**  An `A`-invariant vector of the
Koopman representation of `L` on `L^2(X)` is `L`-invariant, because `A` is
dense and the representation is strongly continuous; `L` is transitive on
`X`, so the vector is constant.  Thus `A` acts ergodically, and by Step 0 so
does every finite-index subgroup of `A`.  For `a != e` the set
`{x = g Lambda' : a x = x} = {g : g^-1 a g in Lambda'}` is a countable union
over `l in Lambda'` of sets `{g : g^-1 a g = l}`, each empty or a coset of
the proper Zariski-closed centralizer `Z_L(a)` (for central `a` it is empty
since `Lambda'` is torsion-free), hence `mu`-null; the action is essentially
free.  The canonical trace `tau(sum f_a u_a) = integral f_e dmu` gives
`tau(u_a) = 0` for `a != e`.  An essentially free ergodic p.m.p. action has a
II_1-factor crossed product.

**Step 3 (C-invariant functions).**  `C < K` is dense and the `K`-action on
`L^2(X)` is continuous, so `L^2(X)^C = L^2(X)^K`, the functions constant on
the `r` orbits `P_j`.  Hence `L^infinity(X)^C = span{1_(P_j)}` has dimension
`r >= 2`, and `U = 1 - 2 . 1_(P_1)` is a nonconstant symmetry with
`u_c U u_c^* = U o c^-1 = U` for all `c in C`.

**Step 4 (non-scalar commutator).**  Let `A_U = {a in A : a . U in T . U}`
where `(a . U)(x) = U(a^-1 x)`.  This is a subgroup of `A` (if
`a . U = lambda_a U` and `b . U = lambda_b U` then
`(ab) . U = a . (lambda_b U) = lambda_b lambda_a U`), and `a |-> lambda_a` is a
homomorphism `A_U -> T`.  Because `U` takes only the values `+-1`,
`lambda_a in {+-1}`, so `A_0 = ker(lambda)` has index at most `2` in `A_U`.
Suppose, for contradiction, that `W_s = U . (s . U)^*` is a scalar for every
`s in S`; then `S subset A_U`, and `C subset A_U` by Step 3, so `A_U = A`
and `A_0` has index at most `2` in `A`.  By Step 2, `A_0` acts ergodically
on `X`, and `U` is `A_0`-invariant, so `U` is constant -- contradicting
Step 3.  Hence some `s in S` has non-scalar `W_s in U(L^infinity(X))`, and a
unitary function has `|integral W_s dmu| = 1` only if it is a.e. constant, so
`|tau(W_s)| < 1`.  For `n = 3`, `A = <C, h>` with `h = diag(p,1,1/p)`:
`h^(-k)` conjugates `e_13(1)` to `e_13(p^(-2k))`, `e_12(1)` to
`e_12(p^-k)`, `e_23(1)` to `e_23(p^-k)`, and symmetrically for the lower
root subgroups, and together with `e_13(p^(-2k-1)) = [e_12(p^-k), e_23(p^(-k-1))]`
these give all elementary generators of `A`.  For `n = 2`,
`SL_2(Z[1/p])` is generated by `SL_2(Z)` and `e_21(1/p)`: in Serre's amalgam
`SL_2(Z) *_D g SL_2(Z) g^-1`, `g = diag(p,1)`, the second factor is
`g <e_12(1), e_21(1)> g^-1 = <e_12(p), e_21(1/p)>` and `e_12(p) in SL_2(Z)`.

**Step 5 (consequence).**  A trace-preserving embedding `theta` of `M` into
a tracial matrix ultraproduct preserves the trace of `theta pi(a)` (so
`theta pi` is regular-trace), the identities `[theta(U), theta pi(c)] = 0`,
and the value `tau(theta(U) theta pi(s) theta(U)^* theta pi(s)^*) = tau(W_s)`
of modulus `< 1`.  So `theta(U) in (theta pi)(C)' setminus (theta pi)(A)'`,
which is the negation of each relative-commutant-collapse statement named in
the target.  For `(NSL1)`: if `k` is a unitary with
`||E_(pi(A)' cap M)(k)||_2 = 1 = ||k||_2`, then `k = E(k)`, so `(NSL1)`
asserts `pi(C)' cap M subset pi(A)' cap M`, which `theta(U)` violates.  For
`(PTS1)`: choose unitary lifts `U_k in U(d_k)` of `theta(U)` (unitaries lift
along tracial ultraproducts of matrix algebras) and let `rho_k` be a
representative sequence of `theta pi` on the finite generating set of the
fixed presentation; the presentation defect of `rho_k` tends to `0`, and
`|tr[U_k, rho_k(x)]| -> |tau(theta(U) theta pi(x) theta(U)^* theta pi(x)^*)|`,
which is `1` for `x in S_C` and `|tau(W_h)| < 1` for `x = h`.

**Step 6 (structure).**  `(HQ4)`: as a `C`-space, `X` is the disjoint union
of the `r` copies of `K` from Step 1, each with `C` acting by left
translation through the dense inclusion, so `L^infinity(X) rtimes C` is the
direct sum of `r` copies of `L^infinity(K) rtimes C`.  With `K_m` the
principal congruence subgroups of `K`, `L^infinity(K) = union_m l^infinity(K/K_m)`,
and `l^infinity(K/K_m) rtimes C = M_([K:K_m]) (x) L(C cap K_m)` since `C` acts
transitively on `K/K_m` with stabiliser the congruence subgroup `C cap K_m`;
each is Connes embeddable (`C cap K_m` is residually finite), and so is the
increasing union.  `(HQ5)`: the crossed product of an amalgamated free
product of groups is the amalgamated free product of the crossed products
over the crossed product by the amalgam (`L^infinity(X) rtimes (C *_D C')
= (L^infinity(X) rtimes C) *_(L^infinity(X) rtimes D) (L^infinity(X) rtimes C')`,
the reduced amalgamated free product with respect to the canonical
trace-preserving expectation onto `L^infinity(X) rtimes D`, since alternating
words from the kernels of the two expectations stay free in the crossed
product exactly as reduced words do in `C *_D C'`); `C' = g C g^-1 < g K g^-1` is
dense in that maximal compact subgroup, so its crossed product is profinite
by the same argument as `(HQ4)`.  The stable identification with
`(N (x) B(l^2)) rtimes Lambda'` is `dense-s-arithmetic-translation-algebra-is-amplified-profinite`.
