**ESTABLISHED** through `leavitt-square-chen-line-action-proof` (not independently
reviewed; no novelty is claimed).

Let `p` be a prime, `K = F_p`, `L = L_K(1,2)`, and `R = L ⊗_K L`, `U = R^×`,
`G = U / K^×`.
- Let `D ⊂ {0,1}^N` be the tail class of `0^∞` and `W = K[D]` the Chen module, with
  `s_i δ_ξ = δ_(iξ)` and `t_i δ_(jξ) = δ_ij δ_ξ`.
- `R` acts on `W ⊗ W = K[D × D]` factorwise. Put `b = (0^∞, 0^∞)` and `δ = δ_b`.

Then:
1. `G` acts faithfully on the set of lines of `W ⊗ W`;
2. the action is 2-transitive;
3. the stabilizer `H = Stab_U(δ)` of the vector `δ` is generated by the four pieces
   - the corner copy `{P + X c Y : c ∈ U}` of `U`, where `P = s_0 s'_0 t'_0 t_0`,
     `X = s_1 t_1 + s_0 s'_1 t_0` and `Y = s_1 t_1 + s_0 t'_1 t_0`,
   - the transvection `1 + s_0 s'_0 Y`,
   - the product Thompson element `g ⊗ g`, with `g = s_00 t_0 + s_01 t_10 + s_1 t_11`,
   - the two nilpotent transvections `w_1 = 1 + (s_10 t_0 − s_10 t_00) ⊗ 1` and
     `w_2 = 1 + 1 ⊗ (s_10 t_0 − s_10 t_00)`,

   so it is finitely generated. The stabilizer of the line `[δ]` in `G` is isomorphic to
   `H`, and every line stabilizer is conjugate to it.

Here primed letters denote the second tensor factor.

**Mechanism.**
- **Transitivity:** product matrix units `s_p t_q ⊗ s_p' t_q'` realize `GL_m(K)` on the
  span of any finite set of points.
- **Stabilizer:** germs at `b` give a unimodular column over `F_p[s_1^±, s_2^±]` whose
  entries away from `b` lie in `I = (s_1 − 1, s_2 − 1)`. Elementary moves among rows away
  from `b`, from them into `b`, and `I`-multiples of `b` into the others, drive it to
  `e_b` in four steps. No generation theorem for `SL_n` over Laurent rings is needed.
