**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).**

**Statement.** Let `X ⊆ A^(Z^2)` be a free minimal subshift of finite type, and let `k` be a field with
`char k ∉ {3, 5}`. If `D([[Z^2 ~ X]])` is finitely presented, then `X` is `D`-quantum rigid over
`k` for all large `D`. By `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`, `LC(X, k) ⋊ Z^2` is then finitely
presented.

**Consequences.**
- **Labbé.** Finite presentation of `D([[Omega_U]])` gives
  `labbe-wang-shift-crossed-product-is-finitely-presented` over every `F_q` with `q` prime to `15` (route `labbe-crossed-product-fp-via-group-fp`). With `labbe-shift-is-wall-rigid`, it would
  force the idempotent commutator ideal `J_D` to vanish.
- **Refutation mechanism.** A noncommuting operator family of the tiles at every scale, over such a
  field, refutes finite presentation of the group.
- **Scope.** The converse (ring finite presentation implying group finite presentation) is open.

**Mechanism.**
1. Rule 3-cycles map to units of the local tiling algebra (part 1). An operator family therefore
   carries a representation of the group, in which every 3-cycle word is a product of factors
   `1 + E ⊗ (γ - 1)`.
2. Induction on commutation distance. Two 3-cycles whose site triples share one site, with coefficients
   reaching distance `d + 1`, satisfy `[a, b]^3 = 1`, and the same holds for the complementary set of
   compatible patterns.
3. In the free algebra on two idempotents, `PQ - QP` lies in the ideal generated by those two relation
   sets. This is an exact 17-term certificate with denominators divisible only by `3` and `5`,
   `experiments/labbe-relation-climbing/idealcert.py`.
4. So commutation propagates one step. The relation `W(q, e) = 1` for illegal `q` propagates legality.
