**ESTABLISHED.** Let `q` be a prime power, `r,d>=3`,
`R_+=F_q[x_1,...,x_d]`, `R=F_q[x_1^(+-1),...,x_d^(+-1)]`, `E=EL_r(R)`,
`Gamma=EL_r(R_+)`, `G=E semidirect SL_d(Z)` with `L e_ij(f) L^(-1)=e_ij(beta_L f)`.
Let `K_Gamma` be the least subgroup `Gamma<=K<=G` with `L(K)<=L(G)` scalar
relatively embeddable (`relative-embedding-is-commutant-separation`; `G` is
residually finite, hence hyperlinear). So `W_K=(direct_sum_(G/K) Z/2) rtimes G`
is hyperlinear exactly when `K_Gamma<=K`.

For a subgroup `H<=GL_r(R)` let its **entry ring** `D(H)` be the
`R_+`-span of all matrix entries of all elements of `H`.

**Theorem.**

- (a) `K_Gamma<=E`, unconditionally.
- (b) `g K_Gamma g^(-1)<=K_Gamma` for every compressor `g in P_Gamma`, in
  particular for every `L in SL_d(N)`.
- (c) If `Gamma<=H<=E` and `LHL^(-1)<=H` for all `L in SL_d(N)`, then `D(H)` is a
  subring and `D(H) in {R_+,R}`. Moreover `D(H)=R_+` iff `H=Gamma`.
- (d) If `E` is not contained in `K_Gamma`, i.e. the floor claim
  `kt-pair-hyperlinear-floor-below-normal-closure` holds, then
  `K_Gamma cap U_kl(R)=U_kl(R_+)` for all `k != l`, and no torus
  `diag(x^m,x^(-m),1,...,1)` with `m != 0` lies in `K_Gamma`. More generally
  `K_Gamma` contains no `EL_r(S)` with `S != R_+`.

**Trichotomy.** Exactly one of the following holds.

1. `K_Gamma=E`: (CCR) holds for every compressor in every canonical CE actor
   model, every
   intermediate wreath missing `E` is non-hyperlinear, and `W_Gamma` is
   non-hyperlinear.
2. `K_Gamma=Gamma`: `W_Gamma` is hyperlinear (`hyperlinear-wreath-model`).
3. `K_Gamma` is a **root-invisible Laurent overgroup**:
   `Gamma<K_Gamma<E`, `D(K_Gamma)=R`, `K_Gamma` is `SL_d(N)`-compressed, and it
   contains no non-polynomial root and no nontrivial torus. Then `W_(K_Gamma)`
   is a hyperlinear nonsofic group, and `W_Gamma` is non-hyperlinear.

**Consequences.**

- Any hyperlinear intermediate wreath `W_Delta` with `E` not contained in
  `Delta` gives `K_Gamma<=Delta cap E`. Overgroups `Delta` outside `E`, such as
  `<Gamma,t>` for `t in SL_d(N)`, carry no information beyond `Delta cap E`.
- Goal decomposition for `non-hyperlinear-group` through `W_Gamma`: it suffices
  to force **one** element of `E \ Gamma` into `K_Gamma`. Such an element
  cannot be a root or torus unless alternative 1 holds, and it immediately
  makes the entry ring all of `R`.
- The cheap refutation of (AC) by a hyperlinear intermediate wreath is exactly
  alternative 2 or 3. Alternative 3 is a hyperlinear nonsofic group whose floor
  sees all Laurent entries but none of the Laurent roots.

## Attempts

* **Alternative 3 is empty (w6-121).** A root-invisible Laurent overgroup `H` is
  `SL_d(N)`-compressed, lies between `Γ = SL_r(R_+)` and `E`, and meets `U_12(R)` in `U_12(R_+)`,
  so `H = Γ` by `kt-compressor-stable-kernel-overgroups-are-polynomial`, contradicting
  `D(H) = R`. The trichotomy is a dichotomy `K_Γ ∈ {Γ, E}`; model by model the saturation is
  `Γ` or contains `E` (`kt-gamma-saturation-is-gamma-or-contains-e`). The weak and strong
  targets of the Decomposition paragraph coincide.
