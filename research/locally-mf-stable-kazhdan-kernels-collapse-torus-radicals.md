**ESTABLISHED.** Proof: `locally-mf-stable-kazhdan-kernels-collapse-torus-radicals-proof`. It is short.
It imports Theorem A and Corollary A of `finite-image-exact-kazhdan-torus-models-reduce-to-stage-lifts`,
Shalom's openness of (T), Mal'cev's theorem and `lef-implies-operator-mf`.

## Setting

- `K` is generated by a finite symmetric set `S`, `F = F(S)`, `φ ∈ Aut(K)`, `G = K ⋊_φ Z` with
  `t s t^-1 = φ(s)`. `G` is **finitely presented**.
- `w_s, v_s, ŵ, R_0, κ, r_s, R_Γ` are as in `finite-image-exact-kazhdan-torus-models-reduce-to-stage-lifts`.
  So `K~ = <S | R_0>` is Kazhdan with constant `κ` for `S`, and `R_Γ = R_0 ∪ ŵ(R_0) ∪ {r_s}`.
- A finite set `R` of relators of `K` is **G-complete** if `R ⊇ R_Γ` and

  ```text
  G = < S, t | R, t s t^-1 = w_s (s ∈ S) >.                        (GC)
  ```

  G-complete sets exist, and every finite set of relators of `K` containing one is G-complete
  (proof, step 1). Put `P_R = <S | R>`. It is a finitely presented Kazhdan cover of `K`.
- `Hom_n(P_R) ⊆ U(n)^S` is the set of `b` with `r(b) = 1` for all `r ∈ R`. For `b ∈ U(n)^S` put
  `dist_R(b) = min { max_s ‖b(s) − c(s)‖ : c ∈ Hom_n(P_R) }` (operator norm; a minimum by compactness).
- A **covariant model** is a homomorphism `π : G -> U(Q)`, `Q = ∏ M_(n_i) / ⊕ M_(n_i)`, with unitary
  lifts `π(s) = [U_i(s)]`, `π(t) = [T_i]`.
- `K` is **locally MF-stable** if for every finite set `R` of relators of `K` and every sequence
  `b_i ∈ U(n_i)^S` with `‖r(b_i) − 1‖ -> 0` for **every** relator `r` of `K`, `dist_R(b_i) -> 0`.
  (Enlarging `R` only shrinks `Hom_n(P_R)`, so it is enough to ask this for cofinally many `R`. For
  finitely presented `K` it is Dadarlat's point-norm matricial stability of `K`.)
- `Rad_fin(G)` is the finite residual, `Rad_fd(G)` the intersection of kernels of finite-dimensional
  unitary representations.

## Statement

Assume throughout that `K` is Kazhdan and `G = K ⋊_φ Z` is finitely presented.

**Theorem D (near-exact models are genuine).** Let `R` be G-complete, `π` a covariant model and
`k ∈ K`. Suppose there are an infinite set `J` of indices and `δ > 0` with

```text
‖U_i(k) − 1‖ ≥ δ  and  dist_R(U_i|_S) -> 0      (i ∈ J).
```

Then for all large `i ∈ J` there is a genuine unitary representation `ρ_i : G -> U(d_i)`,
`d_i ≤ n_i`, with `‖ρ_i(k) − 1‖ ≥ δ/2`. Hence `k ∉ Rad_fd(G) = Rad_fin(G)`.

**Corollary D1 (radical collapse).** If `K` is locally MF-stable, then

```text
Rad_MF(G) = Rad_LEF(G) = Rad_fd(G) = Rad_fin(G).
```

**Corollary D2 (no torus, no (T)).** A finitely generated MF group that is locally MF-stable is LEF.

**Corollary D3 (witnesses).** If `K` is nontrivial, Kazhdan, locally MF-stable and has no nontrivial
finite quotient, then `Rad_MF(K ⋊_φ Z) = K` for **every** `φ ∈ Aut(K)` whose mapping torus is finitely
presented.

**Corollary D4 (the survivor invariant).** Let `Rad_fin(G) = K`. This is necessary for
`Rad_MF(G) = K`, and it holds exactly when `K` has no nontrivial finite quotient. Let `π` be a
covariant model and `k ∈ K` with `π(k) ≠ 1`. For `δ > 0` put `J_δ = {i : ‖U_i(k) − 1‖ ≥ δ}`. Then for
every G-complete `R` and every `δ` with `J_δ` infinite,

```text
σ_R(π, k; δ) := liminf_(i ∈ J_δ) dist_R(U_i|_S) > 0.                (SD)
```

`σ_R` does not depend on the choice of lifts, since two lifts differ by a null sequence. It is the
**stability defect** of the model at `k`.

## What this kills

**The class.** Every construction of an MF model of a torus with `Rad_fin(G) = K` that works by
rounding.
- Its kernel lifts are, up to `o(1)` in operator norm and along the indices where `k` is seen, exact
  representations of some finitely presented cover `<S | R>` of `K` with `R` G-complete.
- The exact representations may have any image, finite or infinite. They may be dense subgroups of
  compact semisimple groups, Galois conjugates of arithmetic lattices, Weil or cuspidal
  representations over growing fields, or representations of hyperbolic or Shalom covers.
- The cover may carry any character fields, and no spectral gap is assumed.
- The wraps `T_i` are arbitrary.

This contains the survivor (S1) of `finite-image-exact-kazhdan-torus-models-reduce-to-stage-lifts`
whenever its exactness set is G-complete, and every near-exact perturbation of such a model.

**The invariant.** The stability defect `dist_R(U_i|_S)`: the operator-norm distance from the kernel
lifts to the compact real-algebraic set `Hom_(n_i)(P_R)`.

**Where every member dies.** At the stage where `dist_R` plus the wrap defect drops below `κ`:
1. Theorem A replaces the rounded lifts `c_i` and the wrap `T_i` by the multiplicity-free `c♭_i` and
   an **exact** wrap `V_i`, without changing any operator norm on `C[F]`.
2. The pair `(c♭_i, V_i)` then satisfies `R` and `t s t^-1 = w_s` exactly.
3. Because `R` is G-complete, `(c♭_i, V_i)` is a genuine representation of `G` itself. It is not only
   one of the larger group `G_Γ`.
4. By Mal'cev its image is residually finite. So `k` survives in a finite quotient of `G`, which is
   impossible when `Rad_fin(G) = K`.

**Refinement of the w19 survivor list.** For a witness, (S1) and (S2) of the finite-image node merge
into one class:
- **(S\*)** For every G-complete `R`, the kernel lifts stay a definite operator-norm distance
  `σ_R > 0` from exact representations of `P_R`, along every subsequence where `π(k) ≠ 1` is seen.
- The (S1) models that survive are genuine representations of `G_Γ` that are exact on `R_Γ`. On them
  the finitely many relators of a G-complete `R \ R_Γ` tend to `1` in norm, while the models stay
  `σ_R` away from `Hom(P_R)`. This is an instability of the finitely presented pair
  `(P_(R_Γ), P_R)`, not a question about the infinitely presented `K`.

## Consequence for the goal

Corollary D3 gives the route `exact-mf-radical-over-z-via-locally-mf-stable-kernel`. Its one open
hole is `torsion-free-sofic-quotientless-locally-mf-stable-kazhdan-kernel`. That hole weakens (P1)
of `automorphic-sofic-kazhdan-kernel-with-full-mf-radical`: the hypothesis `Rad_MF(K) = K` is replaced
by two conditions that can fail separately:
- (Q) `K` has no nontrivial finite quotient. This is necessary for every witness, by
  `mapping-torus-mf-radical-lies-in-finite-residual`.
- (LS) `K` is locally MF-stable. This is not necessary.

`Rad_MF(K) = K` implies both, since then every sequence `b_i` as in the definition has
`b_i(s) -> 1` and the trivial representation rounds it. The converse fails: finite groups satisfy
(LS) but not (Q). The new branch is also open to MF kernels. By Corollary D2 these are then LEF, so
a solution with an MF kernel would also refute (EKL1) of `exact-kazhdan-radical-kernel-cannot-be-lef`,
provided `K` is Kazhdan, quotientless, LEF and locally MF-stable.

## Scope

- The theorem needs `G` to be finitely presented only to make G-complete sets exist. It needs `K`
  Kazhdan only to invoke Theorem A.
- Local MF-stability is never used at two different covers. A single G-complete `R` suffices, both
  for Corollary D1 and for Corollary D3.
- Nothing here decides whether any infinite Kazhdan group without finite quotients is locally
  MF-stable while being MF. That is the MF half of the hole.
