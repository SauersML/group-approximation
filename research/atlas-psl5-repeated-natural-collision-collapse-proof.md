The exact MeatAxe decomposition in every marked five-dimensional slice is

```text
W|A = U direct_sum U direct_sum 1,       dim(U)=2,
End_A(W) = M2(F2) x F2.                                  (PSL5M-2)
```

Consequently

```text
End_A(W^m) = M_(2m)(F2) x M_m(F2).
```

Fix any computed basis `E0,...,E4` of `End_A(W)`.  Every A-central operator
on `W^m` has the unique form

```text
C = sum_i E_i tensor X_i,        X_i in M_m(F2).          (PSL5M-3)
```

Put `R=F2<x0,...,x4>`, the free associative algebra: no commutativity of the
future matrices `X_i` is imposed.  Form the `5 x 5` matrix
`C_R=sum E_i x_i` and the scalar matrices `T,S` of the marked natural
module.  Let `I` be the two-sided ideal generated by every matrix entry of

```text
C_R^2-I,        (C_R T)^3-I,
T C_R S C_R T^-1 C_R S T C_R-I.                         (PSL5M-4)
```

For any `m` and any tuple `(X_i)` satisfying `(PSL5M-1)`, evaluation
`R -> M_m(F2)` kills `I`; conversely `(PSL5M-4)` is exactly the block-entry
expansion of `(PSL5M-1)`.  Thus this one coefficient algebra represents the
collision problem at every multiplicity.

GBNP 1.1.0 computes a strong two-sided Groebner basis.  In isotypic matrix
unit coordinates `p11,p12,p21,p22,q`, the relations `C^2=1` and
`(CT)^3=1` first reduce to

```text
p21+p12+p11+1,                  p22+p11,
q+1,                            p12*p11+p11*p12,
p12^2+p11*p12+p11^2+p12+1.                         (PSL5M-5)
```

The remaining collision entries have exactly three nonzero normal forms:

```text
p11+1,             p12,             p12+p11+1.           (PSL5M-6)
```

Therefore the full strong basis is

```text
p11+1,             p12,             p21,
p22+1,              q+1.                                  (PSL5M-7)
```

So `C_R=I` in `R/I`, and every matrix evaluation has `C=I`.

Finally, the orbit audit is complete rather than tied to this convenient
chart.  The two marked Q1920 classes, two conjugacy classes of embeddings of
their central quotient in `PSL5(2)`, and the four right cosets

```text
Aut(Q/Z(Q)) / Aut_PSL5(2)(Q/Z(Q))
```

give sixteen marked slices.  The verifier constructs a fresh centralizer
basis in each.  Their relation counts range from `33` to `63`; all sixteen
strong bases have five elements, and all five coordinate differences from
the identity have zero normal form.  Hence `(PSL5M-7)` is invariant under
every marked orbit, proving the claim.
