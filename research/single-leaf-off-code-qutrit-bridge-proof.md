For each `d in D`, choose `f_d in F` with `f_d(d_0)=d` and set

```text
h_d=f_d h f_d^(-1).                                    (SOB3)
```

`Sym(D)` here means the standard prefix-permutation copy:
`f(d xi)=f(d) xi` for every leaf `d` and every infinite tail `xi`.  Hence
the stabilizer in `F` of `d_0` acts identically, point by point, on the
whole cylinder `[d_0]`.

It follows that `(SOB3)` does not depend on the choice of `f_d`.  Indeed,
two choices differ on the right by an element of that leaf stabilizer, which
commutes with every element supported in `[d_0]`.

The support of `h_d` is contained in `[d]`.  Distinct code cylinders
are disjoint, so

```text
[h_d,h_e]=1                         for d!=e.            (SOB4)
```

The cyclic factors are independent.  If

```text
product_(d in D) h_d^(n_d)=1,
```

then restriction to `[d]` gives `h_d^(n_d)=1` for each `d`.
Thus `n_d=0 mod m`, with the evident interpretation when `m=infinity`.
Consequently

```text
N=<h_d:d in D> isomorphic to C_m^D.                    (SOB5)
```

For `f in F`, direct conjugation gives

```text
f h_d f^(-1)=h_(f d),                                  (SOB6)
```

so `N` is normal in `Gamma=<F,h>` and `F` acts by the stated
coordinate permutations.  Every word in `F union {h}` rewrites into
`N F`.

Finally `N intersect F={1}`.  Every element of `N` preserves each
cylinder `[d]` setwise.  If such an element also lies in `F`, its leaf
permutation is trivial; by faithfulness of the standard `Sym(D)` action it
is the identity on every `[d]`, hence is the identity element.
Equations `(SOB5)--(SOB6)` therefore prove the internal semidirect product

```text
Gamma=N semidirect F isomorphic to C_m^D semidirect F.
```

If `m<infinity`, this group is finite.  If `m=infinity`, the subgroup
`N isomorphic to Z^D` has finite index `|F|`; hence `Gamma` is
virtually free abelian.  In both cases it is amenable and hyperlinear.

The conjugate qutrit packet `hFh^(-1)` belongs to `Gamma`.  More
importantly, allowing an overlap coefficient to be a sum of many words does
not leave `C[Gamma]`: addition changes the group-ring element, not its
support group.  Thus any finite family of exact projection and marginal
equalities built from the two packets in `(SOB2)` has cumulative support
subgroup contained in the amenable group `Gamma`.

If that family completed a unital algebraic realization of the fixed no-CE
BCS in a nonzero corner, finite-support localization would say that its
cumulative support subgroup is nonhyperlinear.  Every subgroup of the
amenable `Gamma` is amenable and hyperlinear, a contradiction.  This
argument never replaces the mixed overlap by a conjugacy arrow or a Morita
equivalence; it excludes arbitrary additive coefficients in the whole
one-actor group algebra.

The cylinder-swap embedding of `V` into the binary Leavitt elementary
group is injective, and the Thompson splitting into the Steinberg group is
injective as well.  Applying either injection to the internal semidirect
product above preserves `(SOB1)` exactly.  Therefore an off-code element
which is only the image or lift of one localized Thompson actor cannot become
load-bearing in those larger hosts.
