For each n ≥ 2, the premise in rank n−1 gives (B_{n−1}). The direction
(B_{n−1}) ⇒ (A_n) of `shehper-z-conjecture-iff-one-stabilization-ac` then gives the
conjecture on n generators.

The case n = 1 is immediate: the only relator is w, which equals x_1^{±1} because
it presents the trivial group, and it is AC-trivial after (AC2).
