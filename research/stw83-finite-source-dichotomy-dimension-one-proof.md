This is Theorem B of the July 2026 revision of Faurot--Schafhauser,
[Nuclear dimension of graph C*-algebras with Condition (K)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15073).
Their proof first splits the sources connecting to no cycle into a finite-dimensional
direct summand.  In the remaining graph, the ideal generated by sources is a
stable AF algebra, the quotient is separable, nuclear, and O-infinity-stable, and
the extension is full precisely because every remaining source connects to every
cycle.  Evington's full-extension theorem then gives nuclear dimension one.  If
there is no cycle, the finite graph algebra is finite-dimensional.

This route uses more than the generic fact that nuclear dimension is finite under
extensions: stability of the ideal and fullness of the Busby map are the two
properties that prevent the extension estimate from adding a color.
