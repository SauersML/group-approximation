## Why sufficient

This is the converse half of Proposition 6 in the artifact, Section 6.

Let `G` be amenable and automatic. Automatic groups are finitely generated by definition.

1. By `amenable-automatic-groups-are-elementary-amenable`, `G` is elementary amenable.
2. By `automatic-groups-are-of-type-f-infinity`, `G` is of type `F_∞` and hence of type
   `FP_∞` over `Z`. The claim records the passage from `F_∞` to `FP_∞`.
3. By `elementary-amenable-fp-infinity-groups-virtually-soluble`, `G` has a soluble
   subgroup `H` of finite index.
4. `H` is finitely generated, being of finite index in a finitely generated group. By
   `finite-index-subgroups-of-automatic-groups-are-automatic`, `H` is automatic.
5. By `soluble-automatic-groups-are-virtually-abelian`, `H` has an abelian subgroup `B` of
   finite index in `H`.
6. Then `[G : B] = [G : H][H : B] < ∞`, so `G` is virtually abelian.

The converse implications, (H) ⇒ (S) and (H) ⇒ (EA), are the routes
`soluble-automatic-va-from-amenable-case` and
`amenable-automatic-ea-from-virtually-abelian-case`. So the target is equivalent to the
conjunction of the two open prerequisites, given the three imports.
