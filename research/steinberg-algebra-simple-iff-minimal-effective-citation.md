Imported from B. Steinberg, *Simplicity, primitivity and semiprimitivity of étale groupoid algebras with applications
to inverse semigroup algebras*, arXiv:1408.6014. Quoted verbatim from the arXiv TeX source (fetched on MSI,
2026-09-13, `main` lines 317–330):

> \begin{Thm}\label{simple} Let $\mathscr G$ be an ample groupoid and $\Bbbk$ a field. If $\Bbbk \mathscr G$ is
> simple, then $\mathscr G$ is effective and minimal. The converse holds if $\mathscr G$ is Hausdorff. \end{Thm}

> \begin{Cor}\label{simplehaus} Let $\mathscr G$ be a Hausdorff ample groupoid and $\Bbbk$ a field. Then
> $\Bbbk \mathscr G$ is simple if and only if $\mathscr G$ is effective and minimal. \end{Cor}

Here `k𝒢` is the Steinberg algebra `A_k(𝒢)`. The same corollary, read from the PDF (Corollary 3.6, p. 10), is quoted in
`minimal-subshift-algebra-is-simple-lef-ring`.
