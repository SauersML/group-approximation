Every character is `χ_c` for a hyperplane-affine `c` (affine claim). Then
`[χ] ∈ Σ^m(B_nΓ)` iff some admissible representative has `(m−1)`-connected
ascending matching complexes at every configuration: the sufficient
direction is established, and the converse is the exactness claim. Finally,
the closed connectivity description turns this into a criterion stated in
`Γ`, `n` and the values of `c`. Artifact part 2, §3.
