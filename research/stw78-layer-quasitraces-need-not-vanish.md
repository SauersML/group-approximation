title: Ambient quasitracelessness need not pass to nonunital ideal layers

There is a unital separable nuclear C-star algebra `E` with no normalized
2-quasitrace and with exactly one nonzero proper ideal `K` such that

```text
0 -> K -> E -> O_n -> 0       (n>=2),                  (1)
```

where both simple subquotients have almost-unperforated Cuntz semigroups,
but `K` is stably finite and carries a nonzero densely finite lower
semicontinuous trace.

Consequently, even for nuclear targets with finite ideal lattice,
quasitracelessness of the unital target plus almost unperforation of every
simple layer does not force the layers, or the target, to be strongly purely
infinite.  A nonsimple extension theorem must separately exclude unbounded
quasitraces on nonunital layers.
