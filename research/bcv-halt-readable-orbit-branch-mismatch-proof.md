Bowen--Chapman--Vidick's HALT completeness strategy acts by signed
permutations on a finite signed set.  The group generated by all readable and
unreadable strategy observables therefore has finite image.  Its conjugation
orbit on the finite Boolean algebra generated by the readable observables is
finite as well.

This is not the strategy used by
`perfect-zpc-irs-quantum-gap-game`.  That diagonal argument selects a machine
`M_0` on the NONHALT branch: TailoredMIP soundness gives

```text
omega*(G_(M_0)) < 1/2,
```

while compactness of the ZPC-IRS strategy space supplies a value-one
measurable strategy.  The pseudo-IRS hierarchy retains fixed cylinder
equations but contains neither a named finite translate algebra nor a uniform
bound on its orbit size.  Thus the finite-image conclusion from the explicit
HALT construction has no premise on the branch that supplies the gap witness.

The distinction is already visible inside the explicit HALT packet.  In the
question-reduction strategy the readable `SamZ` atoms and unreadable Pauli-X
translations satisfy

```text
E_z = |z><z| tensor I,
(X^alpha tensor I) E_z (X^alpha tensor I)^* = E_(z+alpha).
```

For nonzero `alpha`, `E_z E_(z+alpha)=0`.  Hence any projection below one
`SamZ` atom and invariant under every unreadable Pauli-X translation is zero.
Finite readable orbit therefore does not even imply a nonzero invariant atom
on the HALT branch, and BCV supplies no such atom or finite-orbit bound for
the compactness-selected NONHALT strategy.  This proves precisely the stated
branch-mismatch audit; it does not decide whether a specially chosen gap IRS
has additional orbitwise structure.
