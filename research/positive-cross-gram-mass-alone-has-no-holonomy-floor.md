For every fixed `0<gamma<1`, exact packet intertwiners may have common
source and target with cross-Gram mass `gamma` while retaining an arbitrary
relative commutant gauge and a fixed orthogonal residual component.  Hence a
positive overlap scalar alone supplies neither near alignment nor a marked
holonomy floor; a mixed phase/type relation must inspect it.

The transport theorem is compatible with this no-go: it removes the common
corner extraction step only after the missing packet has been shown to act
covariantly on the same cross-Gram. The arbitrary gauge here is exactly what
that extra mixed hypothesis must inspect.
