Let `G` be a countable group, `|A| = q >= 2`, and `(k, E, F, psi)` a configuration as in
`bernoulli-rokhlin-deficit-has-a-finitary-witness`:

    Phi = (1/k) [ H(psi(x|_E)) + H( x(1) | (psi((x(fe))_(e in E)))_(f in F) ) ].

Put `P = FE ∪ {1}`, `D = { p^-1 p' : p != p' in P }` and `K = <E ∪ F>`.

**Theorem.** If `Phi < log q`, then `D` contains an element of `Rad(K)`, the sofic radical of `K`
as defined in `sofic-radical-localizes-garden-of-eden-windows`. Since `Rad(K) <= Rad(G)`, `D` also
contains a nontrivial element of `Rad(G)`.

The proof runs the Gromov--Weiss count along almost-multiplicative permutation models of `K`.
Suppose no difference in `D` is invisible. A disjoint union followed by a product amplification
gives models in which all but a vanishing fraction of points carry an injective chart of `P` with
the right products. On those points the configuration has the same law as in `G`. Counting the
entropy of the model shift then gives `log q <= Phi + o(1)`. The full proof is Section 1 of the
artifact.

Consequences (Section 1 of the artifact):

* **Sofic groups.** With `Rad(K) = 1` no witness exists, so every sofic group is Rokhlin-maximal at
  every `q`. This recovers, without sofic entropy, maximal Rokhlin entropy of uniform Bernoulli
  shifts over sofic groups.
* **Deficits prove nonsoficity.** A deficit over `G` makes the subgroup generated by some witness
  nonsofic. For example, a deficit over Thompson's `V` would prove `V` nonsofic.
* **Sofic quotients.** `Rad(G)` lies in the kernel of every homomorphism to a sofic group. Over a
  Kun--Thom wreath `(Z/2) wr_(G/Gamma) G` with `G` residually finite, every witness has two points
  in one coset of the lamp subgroup, and they differ by an invisible lamp configuration.
* **Simple nonsofic hosts.** There `Rad = G`, so the theorem requires only that the subgroup `K` be
  nonsofic.
