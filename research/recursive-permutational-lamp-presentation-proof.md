Use presentation `(RPL1)`.  Its relators are recursively enumerable.  Every
conjugate of `c` is an involution, stabilizer words identify precisely the
conjugates naming the same point, and the all-word commutators make distinct
site lamps commute.  The resulting normal closure is therefore exactly
`direct_sum_X C_2`, with the prescribed `A`-action.  Transitivity supplies
finite generation.  For a conjugacy-class action the stabilizer is a
centralizer and proof enumeration for `[h,i]=1` recursively enumerates it.
The finite D8-seed extension preserves recursive presentability.
