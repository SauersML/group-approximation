Let `B` be any unital simple separable nuclear non-elementary stably
finite AH algebra without uniform property Γ — Toms's counterexample to
STW Problem XIX (`toms-ah-counterexample-to-uniform-gamma`) is one.
Then `B` fails strict comparison; equivalently (since all ranks almost
occur in `B`) `B` is not pure, and `Cu(B) ≇ Cu(B ⊗ Z)`.

Consequences: Toms's refutation of XIX cannot be leveraged against
Problem XVIII (Toms--Winter) or Problem XXVI (Winter's question) —
their hypotheses provably fail on every AH algebra without uniform Γ.
Any strict-comparison counterexample to uniform Γ, which is what a
refutation of XVIII by this route would need, must be non-AH or must
evade the argument below at the almost-occurrence step.
