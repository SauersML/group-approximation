**Step 1 (multiplicities).**  Each `q_k` is a finite orthogonal sum of the
projections `s_j p_(K_j) s_j^* ~ p_(K_j)`, so `q_k ~ p(m_k)` where `m_k(K)`
counts the `j in B_k` with `K_j=K`.  By item 3 of
`rordam-line-bundle-sums-are-k-theoretically-rigid`, (H1) gives
`2^n | m_k(K)` for all `K`.  A set occurring among the `K_j` occurs in some
block with positive multiplicity divisible by `2^n`, hence at least `2^n`
times in total.

**Step 2 (disjoint groups).**  Let `R=2^n` and choose an integer
`M >= 1` with `(R-s)M > |C|`.  Partition infinitely many of the distinct
sets from (H2) into pairwise disjoint groups `G_1,G_2,...` of `M` sets.
For each `K` in a group choose `R` indices `j` with `K_j=K` (Step 1).
Since each `K` belongs to at most one group, the chosen index sets `J_G`
are pairwise disjoint, with `|J_G|=RM`.

**Step 3 (a trivial line in each group).**  Put
`r_G=sum_(j in J_G) s_j p_(K_j) s_j^* in A`.  Its range bundle is isomorphic
to the pullback of `E_G=directSum_(j in J_G) zeta_(K_j)` along the coordinate
projection `Z -> (S^2)^U`, where `U` is the union of the sets in `G` and
`|U| <= |C|+sM < RM`.  So `E_G` has complex rank `r=RM` over a finite CW
complex of real dimension `2|U| <= 2r-2`.  By standard obstruction theory
(the unit-sphere bundle has fibre `S^(2r-1)`, which is `(2r-2)`-connected,
so a section extends over all cells of dimension at most `2r-1`), `E_G`
has a nowhere-zero section.  Pulling it back and normalizing gives a
continuous unit-vector field `xi_G` with `xi_G(z)` in the range of `r_G(z)`.
Thus `f_G = |xi_G><xi_G|` is a projection in `A` with `f_G <= r_G`.

**Step 4 (summing the lines to the unit).**  Enumerate the groups as
`G(1),G(2),...` and let `e_k` be the standard basis of `l^2`.  Put
`w_k = |xi_(G(k))><e_k| in A`, so `w_k^* w_k = 1 tensor e_kk` and
`w_k w_k^* = f_(G(k))`.  For `k != l`, `w_k^* w_l = 0` because the ranges of
`r_(G(k))` and `r_(G(l))` are orthogonal, and `w_k w_l^* = 0` because
`e_k` is orthogonal to `e_l`.  For `a in A`
and a finite set `S` of indices,

```text
||sum_(k in S) w_k a||^2  = ||a^* P_S a||,     P_S = sum_(k in S) 1 tensor e_kk,
||sum_(k in S) a w_k||^2  = ||sum_(k in S) a f_(G(k)) a^*|| <= ||a T_S a^*||,
```

where `T_S` is the sum of `s_j s_j^*` over the indices in the union of the
`J_(G(k))`, `k in S`, using `f_G <= r_G <= sum_(j in J_G) s_j s_j^*`.  Since
`sum_k (1 tensor e_kk) a` and `sum_j s_j s_j^* a^*` converge in norm, and
since the finite sets `J_G` are pairwise disjoint (so for `min S` large every
index in `T_S` exceeds any prescribed bound, and
`||a T_S a^*|| <= ||(1-sum_(j<=j_0) s_j s_j^*) a^*||^2`), both right-hand
sides tend to zero as `min S -> infinity` (only finitely many of the
pairwise disjoint finite sets `J_G` meet `{1,...,j_0}`).  The partial sums
`V_S` satisfy `V_S^* V_S = P_S`, so they have norm at most one, and they are
strictly Cauchy; since `M(A)` is strictly complete,
`V=sum_k w_k` converges strictly in `M(A)`, and strict continuity of
multiplication on bounded sets gives
`V^*V = sum_k 1 tensor e_kk = 1` and `VV^* = sum_k f_(G(k)) <= Q`.  Thus
`1 ~ VV^* <= Q`.

**Step 5 (conclusions).**  Since `VV^* <= Q`, `VV^* = VV^* Q`, so
`1 = V^*V V^*V = V^*(VV^*)Q V` lies in the ideal generated by `Q`; thus `Q`
is full.  (The positivity `VV^* <= Q` in Step 4 holds because
`Q - sum_(k<=l) f_(G(k)) >= Q - sum_(k<=l) r_(G(k)) >= 0` for every `l`, and
strict limits of positive elements are positive.)  The matrix `W=[s_1 s_2]` satisfies
`W^*W = 1 directSum 1` and `WW^* <= 1`, so `1 directSum 1 <~ 1` in `M(A)`.
Therefore `Q directSum Q <= 1 directSum 1 <~ 1 ~ VV^* <= Q`, so `Q` is
properly infinite, and `g <= 1 <~ Q`.

**Step 6 (transported singleton seeds).**  Induct on the length of `h`.
For the empty history take `C_h` empty and `u_i=a_i`.  Suppose
`h({a_i}) = C union {u_i}` for `i` in a cofinite set `I`, with distinct
`u_i` outside `C`, and apply a branch `beta`.  Only finitely many `i` have
`u_i in E_beta` (the `u_i` are distinct) or `nu_beta(u_i) in I_beta`
(`nu_beta` is injective and `I_beta` finite).  For the remaining cofinitely
many `i`,

```text
alpha_beta(C union {u_i}) = C' union {nu_beta(u_i)},     C' = nu_beta(C \ E_beta) union I_beta,
```

and `nu_beta(u_i)` are pairwise distinct and lie outside `C'` by
injectivity, `u_i notin C` and `nu_beta(u_i) notin I_beta`.  So infinitely
many distinct transported sets satisfy `|K \ C_h| <= 1`, which is (H2) with
`s=1<2^n` for every `n>=1`.
