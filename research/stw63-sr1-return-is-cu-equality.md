Let `B` be any unital C-star algebra of stable rank one, with no simplicity,
exactness, nuclearity or separability assumption.  For unital embeddings
`phi,psi:Z->B`,

```text
phi and psi are approximately unitarily equivalent
        if and only if
Cu(phi)=Cu(psi).                                          (1)
```

Thus stable rank one returns the implementing unitaries precisely after the
remaining nonstable `Cu` datum has been shown equal.  Bidual approximate
unitary equivalence alone does not supply that equality.
