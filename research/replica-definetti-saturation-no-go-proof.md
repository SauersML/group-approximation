Let `pi` act on `H`, let `HS(H)` carry left multiplication
`L_pi(g)X=pi(g)X`, and put `xi=I/sqrt(d)`. Then
`<xi,L_pi(g)xi>=chi(g)`. After conjugate doubling, one block has coefficient
`|chi(g)|^2`. On `m` blocks the vector

```text
(xi tensor bar(xi))^{tensor m}
```

is a pure tensor across the replica split and its coefficient is exactly
`|chi(g)|^{2m}`. Hence separability/product structure across replicas is
present before any de Finetti theorem is invoked. Such a theorem cannot, by
that conclusion alone, say anything about whether the within-block
input/output/path variables encode a permutation assignment.
