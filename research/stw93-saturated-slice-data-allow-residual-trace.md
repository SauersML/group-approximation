There is a unital C*-algebra `D_0` with a normalized trace `tau_0`, a
trace-kernel ideal `I_0`, and a unital Jiang--Su central-sequence slice
`F(Z)->D_0` such that all of the following analogues of the established
XCIII structure hold:

1. `D_0/I_0` is the II_1-factor quotient of `F(Z)`;
2. the canonical slice exhausts that quotient;
3. every unital homomorphism `F(Z)->D_0` is automatically
   `tau_0`-preserving;
4. the ideal `K_0^sat` generated by the trace-zero parts of all such slices
   is annihilated by every normalized `2`-quasitrace on `D_0`;
5. quotienting by `K_0^sat` preserves the normalized quasitrace space; and
6. `D_0` is pure.

Nevertheless

```text
I_0/K_0^sat
```

is itself a II_1 factor and carries a nonzero bounded trace.  Thus factor
quotient structure, slice surjectivity, automatic trace preservation, and
saturation over every Jiang--Su central slice do not by themselves prove
that the actual residual XCIII ideal is bounded-trace-free.
