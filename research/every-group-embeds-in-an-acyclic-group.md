Every group `H` embeds in a group `Y` with `H_n(Y; Z) = 0` for all `n >= 1`.
One can take `Y` to be the direct limit of the binate tower of `H`, which is a
pseudo-mitotic (binate) group. If `H` is countable, this `Y` is countable.

Citation import, with the countability remark proved in the route. Trust
surface: the acyclicity theorem is Varadarajan's Theorem 1.7 (independently
Berrick), quoted here from the account in Fournier-Facio--Löh--Moraschini,
arXiv:2111.04305. The primary sources were not read.
