**ESTABLISHED (2026-09-13)** through `kassabov-g0-not-fp-proof`, an elementary
proof (not independently reviewed).

**Setup.** This is arXiv:math/0602446, §3.1, checked against the source; the
construction is credited there to Kassabov's unbounded-rank expanders paper.
- Let `p` be a prime, and let `R` be the subring of `prod_(n >= 3) M_n(F_p)`
  generated by `1`, `a`, `a^(-1)`, `b`, `c`, where
  `a_n = e_(1,2) + e_(2,3) + ... + e_(n,1)`, `b_n = e_(1,2)`, `c_n = e_(2,1)`.
- Put `G_0 = EL_3(R) <= prod_n SL_(3n)(F_p)`. It is generated by the ten
  elements `e_(i,j)(1)` and `e_(1,2)(x)` with `x in {a, a^(-1), b, c}`.
- `G_0` contains `(+)_n SL_(3n)(F_p)` (their Proposition `g0`).

**Statement.** For every prime `p`, `G_0` is not finitely presented.

**Mechanism.** Take a field `K` containing `F_p` and some `λ ≠ 0, 1`. In `M_n(K)`,
weight one edge of the cycle `a_n` by `λ`, far from positions `1` and `2`. The
resulting marked groups satisfy every relation of `G_0` of length at most
`(n-6)/2`. In them `a^n = λ`, which breaks a relation valid in `G_0`:
`e_(1,2)((q a^n q)^2 - q a^n q) = 1` with `q = bc`. A finite presentation
would make them quotients of `G_0` for large `n`.

**Scope.**
- The proof uses only two things: `R` is generated by the cycle and the two
  matrix units, and factors of unbounded size occur. So it applies verbatim to
  - the ring generated by the same elements in `prod_(n in S) M_n(F_p)`, for
    any infinite set `S` of integers `>= 3`;
  - `EL_d` in place of `EL_3`, for every `d >= 3`.
- Residual finiteness and the direct sum are not used.
- **Literature status.** Not checked. No search was run and no novelty is
  claimed.

**Consequence.** `G_0` has elements of every prime order `l`:
- for `l = p`, take `e_(1,2)(1)`;
- otherwise, let `k` be the order of `p` mod `l` and `g in F_(p^k)^x` an
  element of order `l`, acting on `F_p^k`. Then `diag(g, g^(-1))` lies in
  `SL_(2k)(F_p) <= SL_(3n)(F_p)` once `3n >= 2k`.

Still, `G_0` cannot witness `fp-rf-group-with-unbounded-prime-torsion`. Both
starting points of the Kassabov--Nikolov construction are now excluded:
- the alternating frames, through H_2 (`kassabov-nikolov-alternating-frames-are-not-fp`);
- `G_0`, here.

That target stays OPEN.
