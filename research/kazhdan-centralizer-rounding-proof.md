`research/artifacts/wreath-permutation-collapse-2026-08-22.md`
(Theorem R): almost-invariance of the permutation graph under the
diagonal action, (T)-projection onto orbital indicators with the exact
identity `||1_G - sum a_O 1_O||^2 = sum |O| a_O (1 - a_O)`, the
row-sum identity `sum a_O d_O = 1` per orbit, and the integer count
forcing a single degree-one orbital, which is the graph of an
equivariant bijection.
