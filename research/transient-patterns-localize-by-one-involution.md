For any finite field and full clopen return section C, every finite
subset F of the transient ideal I admits a unit w in 1+I with w^2=1
and wFw^-1 contained in 1_C I 1_C. Over F2 this simultaneously localizes
any finite collection of core-kernel matrices, including finite subgroups.
For matrix rank at least two the scalar conjugator lies in EL_n(R).
