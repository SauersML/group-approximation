Let `Λ` be one of the four Coxeter diagrams on 5 vertices:

- the paths `[5,3,3,3]`, `[4,3,3,5]`, `[5,3,3,5]` (consecutive edge labels);
- the tree `[5,3,3^{1,1}]`: a path `s_1 −5− s_2 −3− s_3` with two further vertices `s_4, s_5`
  joined to `s_3` by edges of label 3.

Then `A_Λ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture.

**What these diagrams are.** In each case `W_Λ` is infinite and every `Λ − {s}` is spherical,
so `Λ` is almost spherical and non-affine. Checking vertex by vertex: removing an end of a
path gives `H_4`, `A_4` or `B_4`; removing an interior vertex gives a product of spherical
dihedral, `A_3`, `B_3` or `H_3` pieces. For the tree, removing `s_1` gives `D_4`, removing `s_4`
or `s_5` gives `H_4`, removing `s_2` gives `A_1 × A_3`, and removing `s_3` gives
`I_2(5) × A_1 × A_1`. So `W_Λ` is generated by the reflections of a compact simplex in `H^4`
(Lannér). The fifth compact hyperbolic 4-simplex group, the cyclic diagram `(3,3,3,3,4)`, is
covered by J. Huang, arXiv:2305.16847.

Since every proper standard parabolic subgroup is spherical, the conjecture for `A_Λ` follows
from contractibility of `Δ_Λ` (route
`compact-hyperbolic-4-simplex-artin-k-pi-1-via-contractibility`). Contractibility splits into
two cases (`compact-hyperbolic-4-simplex-complexes-contractible-by-cases`).

## Attempts

- **Why no known theorem applies** (checked 2026-09-13 against the sources imported in this
  region):
  - these diagrams have dimension 4, and in `Λ^c` the vertices `s_1, s_4, s_2, s_5` form an
    embedded 4-cycle, so `commutation-4-cycle-free-artin-groups-satisfy-k-pi-1` does not apply;
  - they are not spherical, affine, of rank 3, complete bipartite or locally reducible;
  - they have no edge label at least 6 and are not stars, so Huang's tree theorems do not apply;
  - they have no cycle, so Huang's cycle theorems do not apply;
  - they have no affine subdiagram, so Proposition `prop:reduction` of arXiv:2405.12068 does not
    apply;
  - arXiv:2405.12068 names `[5,3,3,3]` among its exceptional almost spherical types (Conjecture
    `conj:exceptional`).
  - The lecture notes arXiv:2607.24659 (July 2026) and the survey arXiv:2601.08658 (January 2026)
    list no result covering these groups.
  - Bounds on the search: no MathSciNet or zbMATH search, and the arXiv API query failed.
- **`[4,3,3,5]` and `[5,3,3,5]` reduced to one statement** (lane z1-05-artin-kpi1, unreviewed).
  Haettel's criterion along the path order, the argument Huang–Przytycki use for the rank-4
  paths `434, 435, 535`, needs only the `B_n` and `H_3` flag theorems plus
  `h4-deligne-complex-upward-flag-toward-5-edge`. See
  `artin-complexes-4335-5335-contractible-via-haettel-criterion`.
- **`[5,3,3,3]` and the branched tree.** The criterion fails at the `A_2` residue of `[5,3,3,3]`,
  and the tree has no total order; see `artin-complexes-of-5333-and-533-11-are-contractible`.
