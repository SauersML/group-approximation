Retain the order-`8192` signed source pair `(L_0,lambda_0)`, the marked
involution

```text
z=x_13(q),                  P_z=(1-z)/2,               (RDC1)
```

and the detector words from
`paid-t00-carrier-has-signed-hecke-reynolds-dichotomy`.  Put

```text
epsilon(g)=(1-lambda_0(g))/2 in F_2,
r_g=z^epsilon(g) g.                                    (RDC2)
```

Then

```text
<z,L_0>=<z> directProduct L_0                         (RDC3)
```

and the map

```text
theta:L_0 -> <z,L_0>,          theta(g)=r_g            (RDC4)
```

is an injective homomorphism.  Its image `R_0` is the graph of the sign
character, has order `8192`, and is the kernel of the character

```text
mu(z)=-1,                  mu|_(L_0)=lambda_0.          (RDC5)
```

In particular

```text
r_g=1  iff  g=1.                                        (RDC6)
```

The signed Reynolds projection is simply the ordinary `R_0`-fixed
projection after cutting by the mark.  If

```text
Q=|L_0|^(-1) sum_(g in L_0) lambda_0(g)g,
e_(R_0)=|R_0|^(-1) sum_(r in R_0)r,                    (RDC7)
```

then

```text
QP_z=e_(R_0)P_z.                                       (RDC8)
```

Thus the detector branch of the Reynolds dichotomy is not a disguised
finite menu of defining relators.  It measures failure to lie in the fixed
space of a genuine marked complement subgroup.

This obstruction is sharp already in the left regular representation of
the full group.  All presentation relators vanish there, whereas for every
`g!=1`,

```text
||lambda(r_g)-I||_op=2,
||(lambda(r_g)-I)P_z||_op=2.                           (RDC9)
```

Consequently no fixed-area telescoping estimate can make every detector
small from the ordinary relator defects.  The useful surviving branch of
`paid-t00-carrier-has-signed-hecke-reynolds-dichotomy` is therefore the
source-compressed `T/Y` discrepancy (or a new relation coupling the paid
carrier to `R_0`), not an attempt to declare the detector menu null.

This does not refute the open Whitehead occurrence seam.  A detector may
still be small on a specially selected model-dependent paid subcorner, and
the source-compressed mismatch may still yield the desired native row after
an additional incidence argument.  The claim only removes relator
telescoping as the mechanism for doing so.
