Use the two-bit tag bridge at level `n` to carry the endpoint exit atom into
the distinguished first-hit tag atom and all three complementary endpoint
atoms into its survivor.  The tag telescope prevents the exit from returning
at later levels.  Attach that survivor to the next independent occurrence
through `tagged-s3-survivor-installs-next-recurrent-carrier`; its packet
centrality then supplies the shared-word reducing estimate.

The construction never names the three-atom union by one scalar word.  It
uses the four minimal projections of an exact elementary-abelian table and
forms the survivor analytically as the complement of the distinguished tag
atom.
