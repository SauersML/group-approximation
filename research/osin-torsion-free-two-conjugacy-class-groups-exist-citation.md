Citation import, secondary. The statement was read on 2026-09-13 in von Puttkamer–Wu,
arXiv:1607.03790v2, page 2, Remark (a), which attributes it to Osin, Ann. of Math. 172 (2010)
1–39. Barnea–Camina–Ershov–Lewis, arXiv:2210.15746v3, page 3, also attribute to Osin's main
theorem the existence of infinite 2-generated groups with exactly `n` conjugacy classes, for
each `n >= 2`. Osin's paper was not re-read.
