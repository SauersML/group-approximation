**OPEN.**  Notation as in `function-field-rank-one-vertex-action-is-sofic`:
`w=[[0,1],[1,0]]`, `u_p = e_12(p)`, `v'_p = e_21(p)`, `S_3 = <w,u_1>`,

```text
V_m   = { u_p : deg p <= m },            C1,m  = <S_3, V_m> = S_3 *_(Z/2) V_m,
V'_n  = { v'_(tq) : deg q <= n-1 },      B0,n  = <V_n, V'_n>,
theta = conjugation by [[0,1],[t,0]],    C1',m = theta(C1,m).
```

For `n >= 1` and `m >= n+1`, `B0,n` lies in `C1,m ∩ C1',m`.  Form the
abstract amalgam

```text
Delta_(n,m) = C1,m *_(B0,n) C1',m .
```

The claim: `C1,m` is closed in the profinite topology of `Delta_(n,m)`.

Both factors are virtually free (amalgams of finite groups) and `B0,n` is a
finitely generated subgroup of each.  Explicitly, for `n=1`, `m=2`,
`Delta` is generated by `w, u_1, u_t, u_(t^2), w'` with `S_3` relations on
`<w,u_1>`, `V_2 = <u_1,u_t,u_(t^2)>` elementary abelian, `w'` an
involution, and

```text
(w' v)^3 = 1,  [v, w' u_1 w'] = 1,  [v, w' u_t w'] = 1,   v = w u_t w,
```

together with the analogous commutation of `u_(t^2)`'s partners at larger
`m`.

## Attempts

- **n = 0.**  `B0,0 = Z/2`, so `Delta_(0,m)` is an amalgam of virtually free
  groups over a finite group, hence virtually free and LERF.  The claim
  holds.  The colimit needs every `n`.
- **Compatible normal subgroups.**  It suffices to find, for each finite
  set of letters outside `B0,n`, normal subgroups `N <| C1,m` and
  `N' <| C1',m` of finite index with `N ∩ B0,n = N' ∩ B0,n` that keep those
  letters outside `B0,n N` and `B0,n N'`.  Then `Delta` maps onto an
  amalgam of finite groups where normal forms survive.  Obstacle: inside
  `Delta`, `s = w'w` satisfies `s u_(t^2 q) s^(-1) = u_q` for small degrees,
  so the common intersection must be saturated under these partial
  conjugacies.  Retractions `C1 -> C1,m` (kill `u_(t^k)`, `k>m`) fix
  `B0,n` but do not by themselves produce the finite quotients.
- **Twisted double.**  `theta` maps `C1,m` to `C1',m` but not `B0,n` to
  itself (`theta(B0,n) = <V'_(n+1), V_(n-1)>`), so the double-retraction
  argument for untwisted doubles does not apply.
