## Statement

Let `Λ` satisfy hypothesis (D) of `pp-circle-divisible-subgroups-act-freely`,
and let `Γ ≤ PP_Λ(S^1)`. Let `Γ~ ≤ Homeo_+(R)` be the group of all lifts of
elements of `Γ` to the universal cover. It is a central extension of `Γ` by the
integer translations.

**(a) Lifts.** Let `D ≤ Γ~` be divisible abelian. The translation number
`τ : D -> R` is an injective homomorphism, and `N_{Γ~}(D) = C_{Γ~}(D)`. If
`A ≤ Γ~` is amenable and `D ≤ [A, A]`, then `D = 1`.

**(b) Excluded subgroups.** Let `H` be a group containing a divisible abelian
subgroup `D ≠ 1` such that either

- some element of `H` normalizes `D` and acts nontrivially on it by conjugation,
  or
- `D ≤ [A, A]` for some amenable `A ≤ H`.

Then `H` embeds in neither `Γ` nor `Γ~`. The following groups are of this kind.

- `GL_n(Q)`, `SL_n(Q)`, `PGL_n(Q)` and `PSL_n(Q)` for every `n >= 2`.
- `Aff(Q) = Q x| Q^x`, `Aff_+(Q)`, and the Borel group `B_2(Q)`.
- `Q x|_r Z` for every `r ∈ Q^x` with `r ≠ 1`.
- The Heisenberg group `U_3(Q)`, and `U_n(Q)` for `n >= 3`.

**(c) Hosts covered.** By the finite-generation remark of
`pp-circle-divisible-subgroups-act-freely`, (b) applies to every finitely
generated `Γ` of piecewise `PSL_2(R)` circle homeomorphisms, or of PL circle
homeomorphisms, with finitely many breakpoints per element, and to the lifts of
these groups. Examples:

- Thompson's `T`, in its PL or its piecewise-`PSL_2(Z)` model, and the
  Ghys--Sergiescu group `T-bar` of its lifts. `F` is covered by gluing the ends
  of `[0,1]`.
- Lodha's finitely presented simple group `S` (arXiv:1710.06220), generated by
  `T` and one piecewise projective homeomorphism.
- The Lodha--Moore groups, and every finitely generated subgroup of Monod's
  groups `H(A)` of piecewise `PSL_2(A)` homeomorphisms of `RP^1`. They fix
  `∞`, and they are covered on `RP^1`.
- Stein--Thompson groups, and PL groups with irrational slopes such as Cleary's
  golden-ratio groups.
- Every group whose pieces lie in one fixed `PSL_2(Z[1/N])` or
  `Aff(Z[1/N])`, even if it is not finitely generated.

The descriptions of these hosts are standard; the primary sources were not re-read.
The extra generator of Lodha's `S` has pieces in `PSL_2(R)` (integer matrices of
determinant 2), not in `PSL_2(Z)`; case (P) allows this.

## Sharpness

`T-bar` contains `(Q,+)` (Belk--Hyde--Matucci, *Embedding Q into a finitely
presented group*, Bull. AMS 59 (2022)). So divisibility itself is not excluded,
only divisibility with a nontrivial normalizer action or inside the derived
subgroup of an amenable subgroup.

Every element of `T` has rational rotation number (Ghys--Sergiescu). So `τ` maps
`T-bar` into `Q`, and by (a) every divisible abelian subgroup of `T-bar` is
trivial or isomorphic to `(Q,+)`. In particular `T-bar` contains no `Q^2`, and
the normalizer of each copy of `Q` centralizes it.

## Consequence for the root

For `gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group`, the one-dimensional piecewise-projective
family is dead as a host, for every `n >= 2` and for the stepping stones
`Aff(Q)`, `B_2(Q)`, `U_3(Q)`, `SL_2(Q)`. That family consists of the circle and
line groups with finitely many breakpoints, and their `T-bar`-style lifts. This
holds although these hosts can contain `BS(1,2)` and `(Q,+)`.

Hosts this does not address:

- higher-dimensional piecewise-projective groups on `RP^(n-1)`, `n >= 3`;
- groups with infinitely many breakpoints per element, such as Brin's `A`
  (O4 covers `A`, since `A ≤ VA`);
- Cantor-set hosts: germ extensions of `V`, twisted Brin--Thompson groups and
  `nV`.

Such circle groups can still serve as germ groups or building blocks inside a
Cantor-set host, as `T-bar` and `A` do inside `VA`.
