Let `C<A` be finitely generated, with `C` nonamenable, let `M` be a
finite tracial von Neumann algebra, and let

```text
pi:A->U(M)
```

be a representation.  Write `alpha=Ad pi` on `H=L^2(M)`, let `P_A`
be the orthogonal projection onto `H^A`, and fix `U in U(M)`.  Put

```text
eta=(1-P_A)U,
K_U=closure span{alpha(a)eta:a in A}.                    (TSV1)
```

Assume that the selected cyclic representation satisfies

```text
alpha|_(K_U) weakly contained in lambda_A.              (TSV2)
```

For a finite symmetric generating set `S_C` there is a constant
`kappa_C>0`, depending only on `(C,S_C)`, such that

```text
kappa_C ||(1-P_A)U||_2^2
 <= sum_(s in S_C)||[U,pi(s)]-1||_2^2.                  (TSV3)
```

Consequently, for every `a in A`,

```text
||[U,pi(a)]-1||_2
 <= 2 kappa_C^(-1/2)
      (sum_(s in S_C)||[U,pi(s)]-1||_2^2)^(1/2).        (TSV4)
```

In particular a `C`-central selected unitary satisfying `(TSV2)` is
`A`-central.  Applied to

```text
C=SL_3(Z) < A=SL_3(Z[1/p]),
```

this is the HNN-specific selected-vector form of relative-commutant
collapse.  It is strictly narrower in quantifiers than weak ucp-stability:
one neither corrects the lattice microstate nor controls the whole
conjugation representation, but only asks for temperedness of the cyclic
module generated by the proposed stable-letter witness.

The lemma does not by itself close the uniform-outlier sector.  Matrix
ultraproduct origin is not known to imply `(TSV2)`.  Rather, it gives a
sharp spectral localization: every arithmetic commutant leak has a
nonzero selected cyclic component which is **not** weakly contained in
`lambda_A`.  In the regular HNN enemy this component is the
quasi-regular representation `lambda_(A/C)`, and its failure of
temperedness is forced by nonamenability of `C` (Hulanicki's criterion).
One cannot replace the selected-module hypothesis by temperedness of the
whole adjoint complement: `canonical-congruence-adjoint-complement-is-not-tempered`
constructs non-tempered finite-quotient Koopman summands even for exact
regular congruence embeddings.  Those summands have no `C`-fixed mean-zero
vector when `C` is profinitely dense, so they do not refute the present
selected-vector statement.
Thus a denominator/Weyl proof which only establishes discrete temperedness
of the selected orbit would already suffice, while a proof using the full
weak-ucp correction theorem is stronger than necessary.
