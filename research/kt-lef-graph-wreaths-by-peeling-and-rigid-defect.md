Theorem E supplies residual finiteness of `G`, property (T) for `Gamma`
and `G`, and infranormality without normality. Residual finiteness makes
the actor surjunctive, and the LEF graph-wreath theorem makes `W_Lambda`
surjunctive.

If every compressor normalized `Gamma`, the group generated by them would
normalize it, a contradiction. Choose a strict compressor `t` and
`gamma in Gamma` outside `t Gamma t^-1`. The two-site commutator in
Section 6 of the artifact is nontrivial by a vertex retraction, while its
root lamp centralizes `Gamma`. The rigid-defect theorem excludes soficity.

Property (T) makes `G` finitely generated. If `A` is finitely generated,
generators for the root lamp and `G` generate the full graph wreath because
the actor is transitive on the vertices.
