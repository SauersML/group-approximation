**Standard inputs used without a node.**
- A 3-deformation (finite sequence of elementary expansions and collapses of
  dimension at most 3) is a simple homotopy equivalence, hence a homotopy
  equivalence.
- Hyperlinearity and MF pass to subgroups.
- Van Kampen's theorem for a wedge.
- The moves (AC1)--(AC5) on a presentation are realized by 3-deformations of
  its presentation complex. This is recalled (for example from the
  Hog-Angeloni--Metzler survey on two-dimensional homotopy), not pinned, so
  here is the sketch.
  - (AC2) reverses the orientation of a 2-cell and leaves the complex unchanged.
  - (AC3) and (AC1) replace an attaching loop by a loop homotopic to it in the
    complex minus that 2-cell. Gluing a 3-cell along the mapping cylinder of the
    homotopy gives a 3-complex that collapses onto both 2-complexes.
  - (AC4) and (AC5) add or remove a loop `x` with a 2-cell attached along `x`,
    which is an elementary expansion or collapse.
  - The trivial presentation has a presentation complex that collapses to a point.

**Notation** as in the target claim: `G != 1`, `K` the presentation complex of
`<X | R>` with `|X| = |R| = n`, `G_w = 1`, `L` the presentation complex of
`P_w = <X, t | R, W>`, `e_W` the 2-cell along `W`.

**Item 1.**
1. By item 2 of `deficiency-zero-kervaire-failure-yields-whitehead-counterexample`:
   - `deg_t(w) = +-1`;
   - `L` is a finite contractible 2-complex;
   - `pi_2(K) != 0`;
   - `G` has torsion and is neither hyperlinear nor MF.
2. `L - e_W` has one 0-cell, the 1-cells `x_1, ..., x_n, t` and the 2-cells
   `r_1, ..., r_n`. No relator of `R` involves `t`, so `L - e_W = K v S^1`,
   with the circle `t`.
   - By van Kampen, `pi_1(L - e_W) = G * Z`.
   - The retraction `rho: K v S^1 -> K` collapsing the circle satisfies
     `rho o iota = id_K`, so `pi_2(K) -> pi_2(K v S^1)` is injective, and
     `pi_2(L - e_W) != 0`.
3. If `P_w` is stably AC-trivial, the sketch above shows that `L` 3-deforms to a
   point. So the hypothesis of item 1 always gives a 3-deformation of `L` to a
   point.
4. By `howie-point-deformable-complex-minus-cell-deforms-to-lot`, applied to `L`
   and `e = e_W`, the subcomplex `L - e_W` 3-deforms to a LOT complex `K(Gamma)`.
5. Take any `Gamma` with `K(Gamma)` 3-deformation equivalent to `L - e_W`. The
   homotopy equivalence gives:
   - `G(Gamma) = pi_1(K(Gamma)) = G * Z`, which is (a);
   - `pi_2(K(Gamma)) = pi_2(L - e_W) != 0`, which is (c).
6. `G` embeds in `G * Z`. So `G(Gamma)` contains torsion, and it is neither
   hyperlinear nor MF, because those properties pass to subgroups. This is (b).

**Item 2.**
1. In `G(Gamma) = < V | iota(e) lambda(e) tau(e)^-1 lambda(e)^-1 >`, every
   relator gives `tau(e) = lambda(e)^-1 iota(e) lambda(e)`. The tree is
   connected, so all vertex generators are conjugate, and `G(Gamma) = <<x_1>>`.
2. Abelianizing identifies `iota(e)` with `tau(e)` along every edge of the
   tree, so `H_1(G(Gamma)) = Z`, generated by the image of `x_1`.
3. Let `phi: G(Gamma) -> H * <s>` be an isomorphism with `H != 1`, and put
   `w = phi(x_1)`.
   - Then `<<w>> = H * <s>`, so `(H * <s>)/<<w>> = 1`.
   - `H_1(H * <s>) = H_1(H) + Z` is isomorphic to `Z`, so `H_1(H) = 0`.
   - `w` maps to a generator of `H_1 = Z`, so its exponent sum in `s` is `+-1`.

**Consequence.** Under `stable-andrews-curtis-conjecture`, a balanced failure
makes `P_w` stably AC-trivial. Item 1 then gives a LOT group with torsion that
is neither hyperlinear nor MF, contradicting any of (i)--(iii). It is a free
product `G * Z` with `G != 1`, contradicting (iv). The case `m < n` is item 1
of the bridge theorem.
