For every finite abelian group G there is a simple separable unital
nuclear stably finite infinite-dimensional AH algebra A_G with

    K1(A_G)=0,   U(A_G)/U0(A_G) ~= G.

For nonzero G the construction supplies commuting unitaries representing
its cyclic generators, each null-homotopic after its first matrix
stabilization. The trivial group is supplied by an infinite-dimensional
UHF algebra.

This strengthens the LIX counterexample. It does not assert realization
of nonabelian groups or an additional named-problem solution.
