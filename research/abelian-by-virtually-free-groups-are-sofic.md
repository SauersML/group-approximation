If `A` is a countable abelian group and `Lambda` is finitely generated and
virtually free, then every semidirect product `A semidirect Lambda` is sofic.

Thus an algebraic-envelope construction with a virtually free acting group
cannot yield a non-hyperlinear group merely by making the abelian module more
complicated or non-residually finite.
