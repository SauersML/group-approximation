title: Bernoulli actions attain the maximal free-action cost of a finitely generated group
---

For every infinite finitely generated group Gamma, every free p.m.p. action has cost at least one, and the supremum of these costs is attained by a nontrivial Bernoulli action. Graphing cost for a simple undirected graphing is half mean degree. These are imported published facts, not new claims of priority.
