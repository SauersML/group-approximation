Integrality gives `integral log max(|Q|,delta) d mu_k >= mu_k(Z) log delta`.
Pass to the weak limit, use `limsup mu_k(Z) <= mu(Z)` for the closed zero set
`Z`, and let `delta` decrease to 0. Full proof in Section 2 of the artifact.
