For `n ∈ {0, 1, 2}`, every group of type `FP_n` over `ℤ` acts freely and
cellularly, with finitely many orbits of cells, on an `(n−1)`-acyclic CW
complex. Together with `type-fhn-groups-are-of-type-fpn`, types `FP_n` and
`FH_n` coincide for `n ≤ 2`, so a negative answer to Zaremsky Problem 1.4
must occur at some `n ≥ 3`.

For `n = 2` (Theorem B of the artifact): `H_1` of a Cayley graph is the
relation module, which is finitely generated by `FP_2`. Attach finitely many
`G`-orbits of 2-cells along closed loops representing module generators. The
resulting 2-complex is connected with `H_1 = 0`. It is usually not simply
connected; its fundamental group is the perfect kernel of the presented
group onto `G`.
