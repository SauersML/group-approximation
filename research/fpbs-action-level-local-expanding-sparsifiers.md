Section 7. Each free p.m.p. action in Theorem 1 or 2 has a generating graphing using restrictions of its original generators, with the same cost bound, deterministic logarithmic replacement paths, and pointwise expansion on each free orbit. This does not assert iid labels, a Bernoulli extension, or locality relative to a preassigned labeling of that action.
