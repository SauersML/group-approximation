For `Gamma=Theta_shift`, its normal finitary subgroup `L`, every
`g in Gamma`, and every finite subgroup `F<=L`, there is `v in L`
such that

```text
v f v^(-1)=g f g^(-1)       (f in F).
```

For finitely many actors and inspected words, the implementers
may be chosen consistently on one common finite core packet,
including all intermediate conjugates and inverse actions. They
and the inspected core lie in a larger finite general linear
packet whose regular complex representation realizes all these
covariance equations exactly and is faithful on every inspected
core group algebra.

An actor word representing the identity need only become an
element centralizing the inspected core. It need not become the
identity. Thus this does not produce finite models of all the
candidate's mixed multiplication relations or an MF embedding.
It does not assert extension of every preselected representation
of a smaller core packet.
