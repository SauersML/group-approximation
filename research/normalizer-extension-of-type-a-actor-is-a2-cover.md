**ESTABLISHED** by `normalizer-extension-a2-cover-proof`.

Let `Γ <= Sym(Ω)` be a finitely presented permutation group with finitely many
orbits on `Ω x Ω` and finitely generated point stabilizers. Let
`Q <= N_(Sym(Ω))(Γ)` be generated by `q_1, ..., q_m`, and let `φ : F_m -> Q`
send the basis to the `q_i`. Let `F_m` act on `Γ` by `θ_w(γ) = φ(w) γ φ(w)^-1`,
and put

```text
G~ = Γ x|_θ F_m,        (γ, w) . x = γ φ(w) x    (x in Ω).
```

Then:
1. this is an action, and it is of type `[A_2]` in the sense of
   Fournier-Facio--Wu--Zaremsky, arXiv:2603.24687: `G~` is finitely presented,
   point stabilizers are finitely generated, and there are finitely many orbits
   on `Ω x Ω`;
2. its image is `ΓQ <= Sym(Ω)`, and its kernel is
   `{(φ(w)^-1, w) : φ(w) in Γ}`, isomorphic to `φ^-1(Q ∩ Γ)`;
3. if `Q/(Q ∩ Γ)` is not finitely presented, the action image `ΓQ` is not
   finitely presented.

The hypotheses on `Γ` are those of a type (A) action without faithfulness, and
faithfulness of `Γ` plays no role.
