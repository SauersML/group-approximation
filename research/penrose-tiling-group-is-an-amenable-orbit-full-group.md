**ESTABLISHED.**

**Setting** (Chornyi--Juschenko--Nekrashevych, arXiv:1602.04255, section
"Penrose tiling group").
- `zeta = e^(2 pi i/5)` and `P = (1 - zeta) Z[zeta] ≅ Z^4`, the group generated by
  the side vectors of a regular pentagon.
- `L` is the set of lines `i zeta^j R + w` with `w ∈ P`, and `C~` is the plane
  `C` with every line of `L` doubled. It is locally compact and totally
  disconnected; polygons with sides on lines of `L` form a basis; `P` acts on
  `C~` lifting translations of `C`.
- `w_1 = zeta^2 - zeta^3`, `w_2 = 5(1 - zeta^2 - zeta^3 + zeta^4)`,
  `Lat = <w_1, w_2>`, `X = C~ / Lat`, and `Lambda = P / Lat`.
- `𝒫` is the topological full group of Penrose tilings: homeomorphisms of the
  space of pointed Penrose tilings locally equal to moving the marked vertex
  inside a patch.

**Statement.**
1. **(ChJN)** `Lambda ≅ Z^2 ⊕ Z/5Z`, `X` is a Cantor set, and `𝒫` is isomorphic to
   the topological full group `[[Lambda ~ X]]`. The derived subgroup `D(𝒫)` is
   simple and finitely generated.
2. The action of `Lambda` on `X` is free, and it preserves a Borel probability
   measure `mu` of full support: normalized Lebesgue measure on the torus `C/Lat`,
   lifted to `X`.
3. Hence `𝒫 ≤ W(Lambda ~ X)`, and every subgroup of `𝒫` is sofic by
   `amenable-orbit-full-group-subgroups-are-sofic`. In particular `D(𝒫)` is a
   finitely generated infinite simple sofic group.

**Status of the neighbours.** ChJN state that amenability of `𝒫` is open. Its
finite presentation is `penrose-derived-full-group-is-finitely-presented`, open.
No novelty is claimed for item 3; soficity of full groups of amenable orbit
relations is folklore.
