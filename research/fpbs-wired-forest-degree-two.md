title: An invariant connected infinite spanning graph admits a wired forest of expected degree two with at most two ends per component
---

For an invariant random connected spanning subgraph H of a locally finite Cayley graph, its wired uniform spanning forest F is jointly invariant, has only infinite components, and has expected root degree two. Each F-component has at most two ends almost surely. This uses unimodularity and the standard degree-two characterization.
