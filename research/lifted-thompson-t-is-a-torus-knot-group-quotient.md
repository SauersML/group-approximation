**ESTABLISHED (written proof; not Lean-verified).**  Proof route:
`lifted-thompson-t-is-a-torus-knot-group-quotient-proof`.

Let `K = K(3,4) = <x, y | x^4 = y^3>`.  This is the fundamental group of the
complement of the `(3,4)` torus knot.  Put `c = x^4 = y^3`, which is central
in `K`.  Let `T̄` be the lift of Thompson's `T` to `R`, and let `z(x) = x + 1`.

1. **(Surjection.)**  There are `a, b in T̄` with `a^4 = b^3 = z` that
   generate `T̄`.  So `phi : K -> T̄`, `x -> a`, `y -> b`, is a surjective
   homomorphism with `phi(c) = z`.  Its kernel is the normal closure of
   `(yx)^5 c^(-3)` and of the two commutator relators of
   `funar-sergiescu-lifted-thompson-t-is-t311`.
2. **(Transport.)**  Let `Q` be a norm matrix corona and `rho : T̄ -> U(Q)` a
   homomorphism.  Then `sigma = rho o phi` is a corona representation of `K`
   with

   ```text
   sigma(K)' ∩ Q  =  rho(T̄)' ∩ Q    and    sigma(c) = rho(z).
   ```

   So `lifted-thompson-t-centre-has-commutant-eigencorners` is exactly
   `torus-knot-group-k34-has-central-eigencorners` restricted to those
   corona representations of `K` that kill `ker(phi)`.
3. **(Corona form of the relations.)**  A corona representation of `K` is
   the same thing as a pair of unitaries `(X, Y)` in `Q` with `X^4 = Y^3`.
   Equivalently, it is a sequence of pairs of unitary matrices
   `(X_n, Y_n)` in `U(k_n)` with `||X_n^4 - Y_n^3|| -> 0`.  The
   representations of `T̄` are those for which, in addition, the three
   further relators tend to `1`.

## What this changes

- **The Thompson-specific part of (EC) is gone.**  Only one statement is
  left, `torus-knot-group-k34-has-central-eigencorners`.  It is about one
  finitely presented group, with no dilation and no piecewise-dyadic
  structure, and it can fail on its own.  It implies (EC) (route
  `lifted-thompson-t-eigencorners-via-torus-knot-cover`).  In turn it is
  implied by one concrete matrix-perturbation statement,
  `torus-knot-group-k34-is-matricially-stable`, which is: unitaries with
  `A^4 ≈ B^3` are close to unitaries with `A'^4 = B'^3`, uniformly in the
  dimension.  See `matricially-stable-groups-have-central-eigencorners`.
- **The flagship splits.**  Combining this with
  `lifted-thompson-t-not-mf-via-central-eigencorners`, the statement
  `lifted-thompson-t-is-not-mf` follows from two statements that can each
  fail on their own:
  - `thompson-t-has-full-mf-radical`, which is about Thompson's `T`;
  - `torus-knot-group-k34-is-matricially-stable`, which is about the knot
    group and not about Thompson's groups.
- **The same reduction applies to every quotient.**  Let `G` be any perfect
  quotient of `K` in which the image `c-bar` of `c` is central and
  `G/<c-bar>` has full radical.  If `K` is matricially stable, then `G`
  has full radical, by item 1 of
  `central-eigencorners-die-over-full-radical-quotients`.
