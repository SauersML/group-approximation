Citation import, not a reproof. James Hyde, Rachel Skipper and Matthew C. B.
Zaremsky, *Action graphs, semiconjugacy, and non-embedding in Thompson's
group V*, arXiv:2605.20564 (v1 19 May 2026, v2 29 May 2026), Corollary D:
"The Stein group `F_{2,3}` does not embed in `V`."

The paper defines `F_{2,3}` as in the target claim. The abstract describes
the mechanism in three steps:

1. every action graph of a finitely generated subgroup of `V` acting on an
   orbit in Cantor space is quasi-isometric to a tree;
2. for a broad class of groups of homeomorphisms of the real line, `F` among
   them, any action on Cantor space coming from an embedding into `V` is
   semiconjugate to the standard action on the line;
3. many such groups therefore cannot embed into `V`, in particular `F_{2,3}`,
   which answers a question of the third author.

Read from the arXiv abstract page and the HTML rendering of v2 on 2026-09-13.
The proof was not re-derived here.
