A second, self-contained derivation of Farley's theorem. By
`thompson-v-standard-cylinder-charts-are-commensurated`, `V` acts on the set of
groupoid charts and commensurates the standard charts `A`, with
`|gA Δ A| = 2c(g)`, twice the caret count. `g ↦ ‖1_{gA} - 1_A‖²_{ℓ²}` is
conditionally negative definite and proper, so `V` has the Haagerup property.
The consequence that Kazhdan subgroups of `V` are finite follows as in the
citation route.
