Try to turn amenability of the charged near-state into an increasing
exhaustion of every generator support component by finite equivalence
relations.  Then apply
`fournier-facio-generator-support-components-escape-cutoffs` to one finite
stage and conclude that no infinite support component can carry positive
charge.

This inference is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-hyperfinite-support-exhaustion-misses-cutoff`.  A finite
subrelation is not an actual reducing support component.  In the explicit
singular bilateral-shift calibration, uniformly finite nested relations
exhaust the unique infinite support component, but every finite stage cuts a
set of strictly positive charge.  Those boundary charges tend to zero
without ever entering the charged null ideal.  A valid continuation needs a
new uniform theorem producing one fixed null-boundary decomposition, not
mere hyperfinite approximation.
