Direct proof, machine-checked.  `all_groups_isHyperlinear_iff_quotient_closed`
runs `FreeGroup.lift (id : H -> H)` onto `H` and applies
`isHyperlinear_of_isSofic (isSofic_freeGroup H)`;
`exists_not_isHyperlinear_iff_exists_hyperlinear_quotient` is the same map
read contrapositively; `exists_not_isHyperlinear_iff_exists_fg` is
`isHyperlinear_of_local` applied to the subgroup generated by a finite test
set.  No external input, no soficity hypothesis on the target.

The idempotent restatement in the claim body is a change of notation on the
same theorem: `tau_N` is the regular character of `F_r/N` pulled back, and a
matrix microstate sequence for `tau_N` is literally a hyperlinear model of
`F_r/N`.
