Citation import, not a reproof. Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX sources.

1. J. Huang and P. Przytycki, arXiv:2509.06914, Theorem `thm:tripleBn` [haettel2021lattices,
   Prop 6.6], verbatim: "Let Λ=s_1⋯s_n be the Coxeter diagram of type B_n with
   m_{s_{n−1}s_n}=4, and total order ŝ_1<⋯<ŝ_n. Then the induced relation < on Δ_Λ^0 from
   Definition [def:order] is a partial order that is weakly graded, bowtie free and upward
   flag".
2. J. Huang, arXiv:2405.12068, Theorem `thm:triple`, verbatim: "Let A_S be the Artin group of
   type B_n. Let S={s_1,s_2,…,s_n} such that s_i and s_{i+1} are adjacent in the Coxeter
   diagram and m_{s_{n−1},s_n}=4. We put a total order on S by s_1<s_2<…<s_n. Let the vertex set
   V of Δ_S be endowed with the relation < induced from this partial order on S. Then (V,≤) is
   an upward flag poset." It is introduced as: "For the upward flag part of Conjecture
   [compareB], the case when A_S=A_{S'} is type B_n follows from the work of Haettel
   [haettel2021lattices, Proposition 6.6]."

Primary source not read: T. Haettel, *Lattices, injective metrics and the K(π,1) conjecture*,
Proposition 6.6.

Reversing the total order on types replaces the poset by its opposite, which exchanges upward
and downward flagness.
