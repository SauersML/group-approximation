Let B=B_c0 be the canonical central Z-extension of K7, with generator z.

1. No reduction c0 mod m with m>1 is obtained by pulling back a central
   pushout of the stable Steinberg extension along a CCKW matrix map
   K7->E(R), for any associative unital F_7-algebra R.
2. If f:B->F is onto a finite group and F/<f(z)> is isomorphic to
   Sp_4(F_(7^r)) for r>=1, then f(z)=1.

These exclusions concern the specified extension mechanism and the
specified full quotient groups. They do not bound central orders over
all finite images or eliminate arbitrary central extensions of matrix
image subgroups.
