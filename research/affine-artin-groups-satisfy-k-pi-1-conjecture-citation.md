Citation import, not a reproof. Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX source of
G. Paolini and M. Salvetti, *Proof of the K(π,1) conjecture for affine Artin groups*,
arXiv:1907.11795, Invent. Math. 224 (2021), no. 2, 487–572 (bibliographic data from the
bibliography of arXiv:2607.24659). Parts read: the abstract and the introduction.

Verbatim:

- Abstract: "We prove the K(π,1) conjecture for affine Artin groups: the complexified
  complement of an affine reflection arrangement is a classifying space."
- Introduction, Main Theorem [thm:conjecture]: "The K(π,1) conjecture holds for all affine
  Artin groups."
- Introduction: "So our paper completes the list of affine Artin groups with the case D̃_n and
  with all the remaining exceptional cases." Earlier cases: Ã_n and C̃_n (Okonek, Math. Z. 168
  (1979)), B̃_n (Callegaro–Moroni–Salvetti, J. Eur. Math. Soc. 12 (2010)), G̃_2 (Charney–Davis).
- Introduction, consequences: affine Artin groups "have a classifying space with a finite
  number of cells (see [salvetti1994homotopy])".
