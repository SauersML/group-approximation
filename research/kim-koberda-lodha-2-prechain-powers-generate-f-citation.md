Citation import, not a reproof. Setting 1.1, the definition of chain group and
Theorem 1.1 of S.-H. Kim, T. Koberda and Y. Lodha, *Chain groups of homeomorphisms
of the interval*, arXiv:1610.04099v3, were read verbatim from pages 1–2 of the arXiv
PDF on 2026-09-13. The two-generator form recorded in the claim is the case `n = 2`
of Theorem 1.1, after replacing a generator by its inverse when needed so that it
moves points to the right, which does not change the generated group or the support.
This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what the claim records.
