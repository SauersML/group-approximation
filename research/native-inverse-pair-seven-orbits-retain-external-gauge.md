**ESTABLISHED EXACT MIXED RELATIONS AND SCOPED GAUGE FENCE.**  For

```text
T=J_1B_2A_1,
u=x_75(t_0),
v=x_56(s_0),
a=x_97(1),
```

the direct Whitehead interaction and Singer orbit are

```text
J_1uJ_1=x_75(s_1t_(01))x_85(e_(00)),
(T^j u)^7=x_75(s_1t_(01))       (1<=j<=6).            (NIG1)
```

Returning the latter word through `v,a` gives only

```text
[a,[(T^j u)^7,v]]=x_96(q_1),                          (NIG2)
```

and `x_96(q_1)` fails the full signed-source normalizer test.  Thus the
literal `u` occurrence does not itself close the native gauge on `Q`.

The more symmetric adjacent inverse pair

```text
p=x_97(t_0),                  q=x_76(s_0)
```

does return the actual external normalizer:

```text
[p,q]=x_96(1).                                        (NIG3)
```

It also satisfies four exact odd Singer norm identities: the forward and
reverse products of the seven conjugates `T^k p T^(-k)`, and likewise of
`T^k q T^(-k)`, are all one.  These are genuine new non-Boolean/native
occurrence relations.

They do not yield `x_96(1)^7=1` by collection.  The complete cross table
has seven diagonal and twenty-one off-diagonal nonzero entries, and every
nonzero entry is the same element `x_96(1)`, not a positive signed-source
root.  Hence collection gives only

```text
x_96(1)^(7+21)=x_96(1)^28=1,                          (NIG4)
```

the tautological involution relation.

They still do not identify the returned sign with the native sign.  There
is an exact `224`-dimensional Pauli--Singer moving-chart model in which
`T^7=1`, all four odd norms hold, `[p,q]` is a nontrivial returned
reflection `E`, and `[T,E]=1`, while a chosen native transition carries an
independent commuting reflection `D` with

```text
D != E,                    DE != 1.                   (NIG5)
```

The odd norms constrain the cyclic product of seven stage gauges; they do
not put an individual nonnormal conjugate on one `T`-reducing chart.  A
same-carrier lock therefore still needs additional typing of an
intermediate conjugate, not merely `(NIG1)--(NIG4)`.

No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, trace hypothesis, literature theorem,
local computation, or Lean compilation is used.
