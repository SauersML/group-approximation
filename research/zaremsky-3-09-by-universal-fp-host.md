Both parts of Problem 3.9 are answered by these two claims:
- the classification of self-similar braid groups answers "Are braid groups
  self-similar?";
- the universal embedding theorem answers the second question negatively in its
  strongest reading: no finitely presented residually finite example exists.

The universal embedding theorem would also give `fp-residually-finite-boone-higman`,
through Zaremsky's embedding of finitely presented self-similar groups into
finitely presented simple groups. This is one of the two admissible routes into
the root. The other is `zaremsky-3-09-by-fp-rf-example`.
