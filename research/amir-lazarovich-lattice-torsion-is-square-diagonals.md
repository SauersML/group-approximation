**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, elementary, not reviewed; no priority claimed). It is a reduction of
the open question in Amir–Lazarovich (AL), arXiv:2605.09493, §Discussion (artifact §A).

## Setting

- `d >= 3`, `L = O_d` the Odd graph, and `X = X_L` its right-angled Davis complex.
- `Λ'` is the group of an interlacing pair, AL Prop. "the lattice Lambda":
  `⟨Z ∪ V(L) | z², D², [D,D'] ({D,D'} ∈ E(L)), z·D·δ_D(z)·ζ_z(D)⟩`.
  It acts simply transitively on the vertices of `T_c × X`, by product automorphisms.
- `Λ'_col` is the subgroup whose two components preserve the proper vertex 2-colourings of `T_c`
  and of `X`. It has index at most 4, and it contains AL's `Λ'^+`: `Aut^+` is generated by
  halfspace stabilizers, and AL, l.812, note that these preserve the colouring.
- `Λ` is the finite residual of `Λ'`. When `Λ'` is as in AL's Theorem A, `Λ` is simple and of finite
  index, so it lies in every finite-index subgroup, in particular in `Λ'_col`.

## Statement

1. Every nontrivial finite-order element of `Λ'_col` is `Λ'`-conjugate to `DD'` for some edge
   `{D, D'}` of `O_d`. Each such `DD'` is an involution in `Λ'_col`.
2. For a normal subgroup `Δ ⊴ Λ'` contained in `Λ'_col`: `Δ` is torsion-free iff `DD' ∉ Δ` for every
   edge `{D, D'}` of `O_d`.
3. For AL's simple lattice `Λ`: `Λ` is torsion-free iff every `DD'` has nontrivial image in the finite
   group `Λ'/Λ`, equivalently in some finite quotient of `Λ'`, equivalently iff `Λ'` is virtually
   torsion-free.
   - In that case `Λ` is a finitely presented simple group of cohomological dimension 3, by AL's
     remark that torsion-free uniform lattices in `T_c × X_{O_d}` have cd 3. That would answer
     Kourovka 21.46 and AL's Question positively.
   - Since `Λ ⊆ [M, M]` for every finite-index `M ≤ Λ'` (`Λ` is perfect and contained in `M`), a
     sufficient test is that the class of `DD'` is nonzero in `H_1(M; Z)` for some finite-index
     `M ∋ DD'`.

## Proof of item 1

- **Fixed cell.** Let `g ∈ Λ'_col` have finite order, `g ≠ 1`, and write `g = (g_T, g_X)`.
  - `g` fixes a point `p = (p_T, p_X)` of the CAT(0) space `T_c × X` (Bruhat–Tits fixed-point lemma).
  - Let `σ_T ∋ p_T` and `σ_X ∋ p_X` be the open cells containing them. Then `g_T` stabilizes `σ_T`
    and `g_X` stabilizes `σ_X`.
- **Tree factor.** If `σ_T` is an edge, `g_T` preserves the colouring, so it fixes both endpoints.
  In either case `g_T` fixes a vertex `t`, and `g` fixes `(t, p_X)`.
- **Davis factor.** `O_d` has no triangles: three pairwise disjoint `(d−1)`-subsets of `[2d−1]` need
  `3d − 3 <= 2d − 1`, i.e. `d <= 2`. So `X` has no 3-cubes, and `σ_X` is a vertex, an edge or a square.
  - **Vertex.** `g` fixes the vertex `(t, σ_X)`. Since the action on vertices is free, `g = 1`.
  - **Edge.** `g_X` preserves the colouring, so it fixes both endpoints, and again `g = 1`.
  - **Square `Q`.** `g_X` permutes the four corners, fixes none, and preserves the colouring. So it
    preserves both diagonals and swaps each. It is therefore the central symmetry, and `g` maps the
    vertex `(t, v)` to the opposite corner `(t, v'')`.
- **Conjugation.** Choose `h ∈ Λ'` with `h x_0 = (t, v)`, where `x_0` is the base vertex. Then
  `h^{-1}gh` maps `x_0` to the opposite corner of the X-square `h^{-1}({t} × Q)` at `x_0`.
  - The X-squares at `x_0` correspond to the edges `{D, D'}` of the link `O_d`. The relators
    `[D, D']` give them, with corners `x_0, Dx_0, D'x_0, DD'x_0`.
  - So `h^{-1}gh` and `DD'` both send `x_0` to `DD'x_0`. By freeness on vertices, `h^{-1}gh = DD'`.
- **The diagonals.** `(DD')² = D D' D D' = 1` since `D, D'` commute and are involutions, and
  `DD' ≠ 1`. `DD'` fixes the T-coordinate and moves the X-coordinate by distance 2, so it
  preserves both colourings.

## Proof of items 2 and 3

- **Item 2.** Conjugates of `DD'` lie in `Δ` iff `DD'` does, because `Δ` is normal.
- **Item 3, the finite quotient.** `Λ` is the intersection of all finite-index subgroups of `Λ'`.
  So `DD' ∉ Λ` iff some finite quotient of `Λ'` detects `DD'`. Since `[Λ' : Λ] < ∞`, `Λ'/Λ` is one
  such quotient, and it detects every element outside `Λ`.
- **Item 3, virtual torsion-freeness.** `Λ` lies in every finite-index subgroup. So `Λ'` is virtually
  torsion-free iff `Λ` is torsion-free.

## Scope

- This reduces AL's torsion question to a question about finitely many involutions, one `Λ'`-orbit
  type per edge of `O_d`.
- It does not decide it. Deciding it requires AL's explicit interlacing data (their §"Construction of
  a lattice containing a BMW"), or a torsion-free redesign of the interlacing pair with fixed-point-free
  generator pairings.
