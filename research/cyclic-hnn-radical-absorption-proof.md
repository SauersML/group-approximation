Sections 4--5 give the full proof. The kernel is generated by E_i=s^i E
s^-i, and its relations include r_(i+1)=d_i. An MF map out of the kernel
kills every B_i, hence every d_i, hence every r_(i+1), and therefore each
E_(i+1). It is trivial. The quotient Z separates everything outside the
kernel, even by finite quotients. Killing d kills B, then r, then E;
this computes the normal closure. Applying the same argument to the image
of N in any quotient proves the entire quotient-lattice formula.
