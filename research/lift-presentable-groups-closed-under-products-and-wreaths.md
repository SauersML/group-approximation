## Definitions

- A finite-state action (`S <= Aut(A^*)` finite, closed under sections and inverses) is **lift-presented** if
  its relator kernel is a finitely generated lift ideal (equivalently `V_{|A|}(⟨S⟩)` is finitely presented).
- A group is **LP-embeddable** if it is isomorphic to a subgroup of a group that has a lift-presented
  action. Every finitely generated LP-embeddable group embeds in a finitely presented simple group
  (`lift-presented-automaton-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`).

## Statement

1. **Wreaths with finite groups.** If `H` has a lift-presented action, so does `H ≀ Sym(n)` (for the action of
   `Sym(n)` on `{1,…,n}`).
2. **Products.** If `H_1`, `H_2` have lift-presented actions, so does `H_1 × H_2`.
3. **Consequently** LP-embeddable groups are closed under finite direct products and finite extensions: if
   `H ≤ Γ` has index `n` and `H` is LP-embeddable, then `Γ ≤ H ≀ Sym(n)` (Kaloujnine–Krasner) is LP-embeddable.
4. **Recurrent part.** Let `S` be finite and closed under sections and inverses, let `S^{(k)}` be the set of
   level-`k` sections of states, and `S^{(∞)} = S^{(k)}` for `k >= k_0` (the sequence decreases and
   stabilizes). Then `⟨S⟩ ≤ ⟨S^{(∞)}⟩ ≀ Sym(A^{k_0})`. So `⟨S⟩` is LP-embeddable iff `⟨S^{(∞)}⟩` is:
   transient states never matter.

## Proof

**1.** Let `H = ⟨S⟩` act on `A^*` lift-presentedly, and put `B = {1,…,n} × A`. Define states on `B^*`:
- `Δ(s)`, for `s in S`: `(i,a)w ↦ (i, s(a)) Δ(s|_a)(w)` (the action repeated over the first coordinate);
- `ι_j(s)`, for `s in S`: `(j,a)w ↦ (j, s(a)) Δ(s|_a)(w)`, and `(i,a)w ↦ (i,a)w` for `i ≠ j`;
- `π in Sym(n)`: `(i,a)w ↦ (π(i), a)w`.
`S' = Δ(S) ∪ ι(S) ∪ Sym(n) ∪ {1}` is closed under sections and inverses, and every level-1 section of every
state lies in `Δ(S) ∪ {1}`. The action of `Δ(S)` is the letter repetition of the action of `S`, which has the
same relator kernel and the same lift ideals (§1 of `lift-presented-automaton-groups-satisfy-boone-higman-proof`),
so it is lift-presented. By `finitary-extensions-preserve-lift-presentability` (`k = 1`), `S'` is
lift-presented. Finally `ι_j(h) = Δ(h)` on the cone of the letters `(j,·)` and the identity elsewhere, so
`h ↦ ι_j(h)` is injective, the `ι_j(H)` have disjoint supports, `π ι_j(h) π^{-1} = ι_{π(j)}(h)`, and `Sym(n)`
meets `∏ ι_j(H)` trivially. Hence `⟨ι_1(H),…,ι_n(H), Sym(n)⟩ ≅ H ≀ Sym(n)` inside `⟨S'⟩`.

**2.** Let `S_i` act on `A_i^*` (`i = 1, 2`) and put `B = A_1 ⊔ A_2`. Let `s in S_1` act on `B^*` as before on
letters of `A_1` (with sections extended in the same way) and fix letters of `A_2` with trivial section, and
symmetrically for `S_2`. Each `S_i` then acts only on the maximal `A_i`-prefix of a word, so the two groups
commute, meet trivially, and act faithfully: the group is `H_1 × H_2`. Let `R = R_1 ∪ R_2 ∪ {[s,t] : s in S_1,
t in S_2}` and `M = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_lift` in `F(S_1 ∪ S_2)`.
- `M ∩ F(S_i)` is a lift ideal of `F(S_i)` for the original action: sections at letters of the other alphabet
  are empty. It contains `R_i`, so it contains `N_i`.
- A relator `w` equals `π_1(w) π_2(w)` modulo `⟨⟨[S_1,S_2]⟩⟩`, where `π_i` deletes the other letters, and
  `π_i(w) in N_i`. So `w in M`, the relator kernel is `M`, and the action is lift-presented.

**3.** Subgroups of LP-embeddable groups are LP-embeddable by definition. Apply 2 to products, and 1 together
with Kaloujnine–Krasner (`Γ ↪ H ≀ Sym(Γ/H)` for any subgroup `H` of finite index) to finite extensions.

**4.** Sections of products are products of sections, so for `g in ⟨S⟩` and `|v| = k_0`, the section `g|_v` is a
product of elements of `S^{(k_0)} = S^{(∞)}`. Hence `g ↦ ((g|_v)_{v in A^{k_0}}; g|_{A^{k_0}})` embeds `⟨S⟩` in
`⟨S^{(∞)}⟩ ≀ Sym(A^{k_0})`. Conversely `⟨S^{(∞)}⟩ ≤ ⟨S⟩`. Apply 3. ∎

## Consequence for the open subexponential case

In `polynomial-automata-reduce-to-cycling-relators`, the open case was cycling relators along a periodic ray
whose letter some state moves, so that compression is blocked. By 4, only recurrent states matter. If every
state moving the ray letter is transient, pass to `⟨S^{(∞)}⟩`, where the letter may be fixed by every state and
compression applies. Example: adding to the spine-Grigorchuk automaton the finitary state `m = (0 2)` blocks
compression, but `m` is transient, so the group stays LP-embeddable
(`spine-grigorchuk-automaton-group-has-a-bounded-action`). What remains open is a **recurrent** state moving a
ray that carries infinitely generated cycling relators.
