Let `R` be a nonzero unital ring and `m >= 4`.  Write

```text
sigma = P_((12)(34)) = the permutation matrix of (1 2)(3 4), padded by I_(m-4).
```

Then `sigma` lies in `EL_m(R)`, has order exactly two, and normally generates
`EL_m(R)`.

Its elementary membership is explicit: with
`w_ij = e_ij(1) e_ji(-1) e_ij(1)`,

```text
sigma = w_12 w_34 w_13^2.
```

Its normal generation is two commutators:

```text
[e_13(r), sigma] = e_13(r) e_24(-r),
[e_12(1), [e_13(r), sigma]] = e_14(-r).
```

So every elementary root is a product of at most four conjugates of `sigma`.
No characteristic hypothesis enters: every coefficient is `+-1` or the single
arbitrary element `r`, and `2` may be zero or a zero divisor.

Consequently, in `universal-seed-group-collapses-elementary-groups`, the seed
group `B = EL_4(C)` is itself normally generated by one involution, and for
every nonzero countable unital ring `R` with a properly infinite unit and
every `n >= 2` the image of that involution normally generates `EL_n(R)`.
Where `EL_n(R)` is nontrivial the image is nontrivial, so it too has order
exactly two: **the universal obstruction is carried by a single involution.**

The rank bound is forced.  In rank three there is no double transposition,
and the single transposition has determinant `-1`, so it lies in `EL_3(R)`
only when `2 = 0`; that case is
`char-two-basis-swap-normally-generates-elementary-group`.  The zero ring is
excluded because there `sigma = 1`.

**Formalization: not formalized.**  The rank-two ingredient is on main —
`GroupApproximation/Leavitt/MatrixDiagonalization.lean`,
`exists_elementary_signedSwap` — and the rank-four assembly is three disjoint
instances of it, but neither the assembly nor the two commutator identities is
in Lean.  This is an arithmetic strengthening of an established neighbour, not
a new analytic input, so nothing downstream depends on formalizing it first.
