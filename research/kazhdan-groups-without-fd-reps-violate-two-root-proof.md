Artifact Section 2.

1. **Phases.** On the orbit closure of `z(n) = nu_2(n) mod 2`, the phase mod `2^j` is readable
   from a window of length `2^(j+1)` (Lemma 2.1). This gives clopen partitions cyclically permuted
   by the shift, hence unital `M_(2^j)(F_2)` inside `A_X`.
2. **Property (T).** `A_X` is generated by `u`, `u^-1` and one cylinder function, so it is of finite
   type, and `elementary-group-property-t-over-free-algebras` applies.
3. **No finite-dimensional representations.** `SL_N(F_2)` is simple, and contains `(Z/3)^floor(N/2)`
   and a large elementary abelian 2-group. So its nontrivial representations have degree at least
   `floor(N/2)` (Lemma 2.2). Hence any representation of `H` kills
   `x_12(1)` in `SL_(3·2^j)(F_2)`, and normal generation finishes (Proposition 2.3).
4. **Rank model.** `2^J`-periodic approximations of `z` give representations of the full-shift
   crossed product, in which the kernel onto `A_X` has bounded rank (Lemma 2.4).
   `rank-modelled-coefficients-violate-two-root-identities` gives the violating tensor-square model.
5. **Commutant and corner.** Coordinate truncations commute with diagonals and almost commute with
   the cyclic shift, so the commutant is diffuse. On the full corner `e = chi_(C^1_0)`, the
   subgroup `EL_3((1-e) A_X (1-e))` fixes the image of `Y (x) X + X (x) Y` pointwise, while
   `x_12(e)` does not (Theorem 2.5).
