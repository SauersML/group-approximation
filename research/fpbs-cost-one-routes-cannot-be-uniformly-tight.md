title: Nonamenability prevents uniformly tight routes in near-unit-degree connected invariant subgraphs
root: true
---

Let G be a nonamenable locally finite Cayley graph. If invariant random connected spanning subgraphs H_n have half expected root degree tending to one, then it is impossible that d_Hn(o,s) is uniformly tight in n for every generator s. In particular, all these expectations cannot be uniformly bounded.
