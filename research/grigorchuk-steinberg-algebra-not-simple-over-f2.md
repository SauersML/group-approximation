**ESTABLISHED (import; see `grigorchuk-steinberg-algebra-not-simple-over-f2-citation`).** Let `G` be the Grigorchuk
group with its self-similar action `(G, X)`, and `𝒢` the associated groupoid, which is not Hausdorff.

1. For every field `K` of characteristic zero, `A_K(𝒢)` is simple, and the C*-algebra `O_(G,X)` is simple.
2. `A_(Z_2)(𝒢)` is not simple.

**Firewall for the UN program.** Over `F_2` the reviewed theorem `steinberg-elementary-groups-are-simple-mod-centre`
does not apply: the groupoid is non-Hausdorff and the ring is not simple. In characteristic 0 the ring is simple, but
over an infinite field it is not finitely generated, so Ershov–Jaikin-Zapirain gives no (T). The Grigorchuk group
therefore gives no simple Kazhdan elementary group in this framework over `F_2`.
