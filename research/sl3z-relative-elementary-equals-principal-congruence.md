For every nonzero ideal `q = mZ`:

- `SL_3(Z) = E_3(Z)`;
- `E_3(Z, q) = SL_3(Z, q) = ker(SL_3(Z) -> SL_3(Z/q))`.

Here `E_3(Z, q)` is the normal subgroup of `E_3(Z)` generated by the
`e_ij(t)` with `t ∈ q`.
