**Citation.** Read 2026-09-17 from the arXiv PDF of P.-E. Caprace, *Finite and
infinite quotients of discrete and indiscrete groups*, arXiv:1709.05949. The
page numbers below are the printed ones.

* p. 27, definition: "a locally compact group is called just-non-compact if it is
  not compact and if all its proper Hausdorff quotients are compact. It is
  hereditarily just-non-compact if every finite index open subgroup is
  just-non-compact."
* p. 27: "The following theorem is a slight strengthening of the original
  formulation of Bader–Shalom in [BS06], taking advantage of recent results from
  [CLB] concerning the case where the number of factors of the ambient product
  group is at least 3."
* p. 27, Theorem 4.21 (U. Bader and Y. Shalom): "Let n≥2 and for each
  i = 1,...,n, let Gi be a non-discrete compactly generated locally compact group
  which is just-non-compact and contains no abelian normal subgroup other than the
  identity subgroup. Let Γ < G1×···×Gn be a cocompact lattice such that for all j,
  the projection Γ→Gj has dense image. If n>3, assume in addition that for all j,
  the projection Γ→∏_{i≠j}Gi has non-discrete image. Then Γ is just-infinite. If
  in addition Gi is hereditarily just-non-compact for all i, then Γ is hereditarily
  just-infinite."
* p. 27, from the proof: "Therefore, in all cases, we may invoke [CLB, Theorem H].
  This ensures that for all j, the projection pj : Γ→Gj is injective."
* p. 28: "The condition excluding abelian normal subgroups in Gi in Theorem 4.21 is
  necessary" (example `(Z×Z)⋊C2` in `Isom(R)×Isom(R)`). "Similarly, the condition
  of compact generation is also necessary." (Example: `PSL2` over the algebraic
  closure of `F_p`, extended by its Galois group, times `Z`.)
* p. 28: "Theorem 4.21 also holds for some non-uniform lattices under a technical
  hypothesis called integrability, see [BS06]."
* p. 36: "All those groups are linear, hence residually finite. There is currently
  no known example of a lattice in a product of more than two non-linear locally
  compact groups satisfying the hypotheses of the Bader–Shalom Normal Subgroup
  Theorem 4.21."
* p. 35, Theorem 4.30 (A. Le Boudec): "There is a finitely generated infinite
  simple group Γ acting edge-transitively on the regular tree T of degree 20, and
  whose local action at every vertex is isomorphic to the permutational wreath
  product Alt(4)≀Alt(5)." The survey adds that it "is not finitely presented (see
  [LB16, Proposition 5.4])".

[BS06] = U. Bader, Y. Shalom, *Factor and normal subgroup theorems for lattices in
products of groups*, Invent. Math. 163 (2006) 415–454. Not re-read.
