**ESTABLISHED (obstruction, operator-algebras family).**

**Setting.** Let `K` be a countable group and let `(L, t, c)` be a Kazhdan
compression core in `K`:

- `L <= K` has property (T);
- `t L t^-1 <= L`;
- `[c, L] = 1`;
- the defect survives: `[t c t^-1, l_0] != 1` for some `l_0 in L`.

The **compression semigroup** of `L` is `P_L = {x in K : x L x^-1 <= L}`, and
the **compressor hull** is the subgroup it generates, `Hull_K(L) = <P_L>`.

**Theorem.** If `K` is sofic, then:

- **(H1)** every Kazhdan subgroup `G <= K` in which `L` is infranormal
  normalizes `C_K(L)`, so `t notin G`;
- **(H2)** in particular `Hull_K(L)` is not Kazhdan. The same holds for every
  intermediate subgroup `L u {t} <= G <= Hull_K(L)` generated by `P_L ∩ G`,
  for example `<L, t, c>`;
- **(H3)** if `K` itself is Kazhdan, then `L` is not infranormal in `K`. So
  `Hull_K(L)` is a proper non-Kazhdan subgroup of `K`, and `K` is not
  generated by elements that compress `L`.

**Corollary (the arithmetic router).** Take the source `R = E * F_2` (repo
`E * Z`), `P = Gamma(3) ltimes Z^3`, `w = [t c t^-1, p_0]`, and any quotient
`q : R ->> Q` with `Q` sofic and Kazhdan and `q(w) != 1`. This covers every
output of `(SPR1)`, of `(ADS1)`, and every `K` of `(TFC1)` built from this
core. Put `Gamma = q(P)`. Then:

1. `Gamma` is not infranormal in `Q`, and `Q != Hull_Q(Gamma)`.
2. `q(E) = <q(P), q(t), q(c)>` lies in `Hull_Q(Gamma)` and is not Kazhdan.
3. `Q = <q(E), q(s)>` for the free letter(s) `s`, so by 1 some free letter
   has `q(s) notin Hull_Q(Gamma)`. More generally, if `Q = <x_1, ..., x_k>`
   (for the Hull router, the two images of the Kazhdan partner `H`), then some
   `x_i` does not compress `Gamma`.

**Kill.** Consider any router design that routes the defect into a Kazhdan
subgroup generated by compressors of the core. Examples:

- dropping the free factor and Kazhdanizing `E` directly, so that `Q = q(E)`;
- sending the free letters, or the partner generators, into `C_Q(q(P))`,
  into `N_Q(q(P))`, or into the compression semigroup;
- any construction in which `q(P)` is infranormal inside a Kazhdan subgroup
  that contains `q(t)`.

Every such design produces a sofic group with a nontrivial rigid defect. It
therefore dies at the soficity clause of `(SPR1)`. The invariant is property
(T) of the compressor hull `Hull_Q(q(P))`, and the step where it dies is
Kun--Thom's normalization (Theorem B / Theorem 4.1) through
`sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects`. The only sofic outputs left
are those in which the compression rigidity of the arithmetic core is
**strictly non-Kazhdan**. There the compressor hull is a proper non-Kazhdan
subgroup of the Kazhdan group `Q`, and the Kazhdan generators of `Q` sit
outside it.

**Sharpness.** The Kazhdan hypothesis in (H2) cannot be removed. The literal
group `E` is sofic, `P` is infranormal in `E = Hull_E(P)`, and the defect
survives (Sharpness section of `sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects`).
So `E` is exactly a non-Kazhdan hull, and it passes the obstruction.

**What this does not show.** It does not refute `(SPR1)`. The obstruction
applies only to Kazhdan hulls, and nothing in Hull's small-cancellation
construction forces the free letters or the partner generators to compress
`q(P)`. It is a necessary non-rigidity prerequisite, not a sufficient one.
