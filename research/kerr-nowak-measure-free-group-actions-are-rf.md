**ESTABLISHED by citation (secondary source).** Let `F_r` (`r ∈ N ∪ {∞}`) act minimally on the Cantor
set `X` and admit an `F_r`-invariant Borel probability measure. Then the action is residually finite
in the sense of Kerr–Nowak.

The statement is quoted from Ma's restatement. The primary paper was not read; see the citation route.
