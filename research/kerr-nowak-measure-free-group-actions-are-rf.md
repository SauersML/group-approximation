**ESTABLISHED by citation (primary source confirmed at review).** Let `F_r` (`r ∈ N ∪ {∞}`) act minimally on the Cantor
set `X` and admit an `F_r`-invariant Borel probability measure. Then the action is residually finite
in the sense of Kerr–Nowak.

The statement was first quoted from Ma's restatement. At review it was confirmed verbatim in the primary paper, Kerr–Nowak Theorem 5.2 (Ergod. Th. Dynam. Sys. 32 (2012), p. 1604). See the citation route.
