Schur factorization gives `(CHR3)--(CHR5)`.  On a forbidden predicate simple,
take the known anticommuting packet implementers.  Send every root subgroup
generator to the identity.  Root additivity, Steinberg commutators, the
wordized coefficient rows, and their covariance relations then all hold
exactly.  Put an inverse Pauli pair on a two-dimensional multiplicity factor;
its projective phase cancels the forbidden packet phase, so the global stable
letters commute.

This is the binary instance of the conjugate-absorber construction for finite
projective sector groupoids.  It preserves the forbidden selector type but
has zero named-root support, proving `(CHR6)`.  The exact coefficient-module
rank contradiction becomes available only after a separate theorem identifies
the physical root support with the BCS payload carrier.
