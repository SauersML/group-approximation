OPEN.  Put `C=SL_3(Z)`, `A=SL_3(Z[1/2])`, and `B=L(C) subset L(A)`.
There is no trace-preserving embedding `pi:L(A)->M` into a tracial matrix
ultraproduct and no Haar unitary `k in pi(B)' intersect M` such that

1. `W^*(pi(B),k)` and `pi(L(A))` are free with amalgamation over `pi(B)`;
2. for some (equivalently every) representing approximate actor
   `(X_n,H_n)` of `pi`, the lattice tuples satisfy

   ```text
   liminf_U inf_(sigma:A->U(d_n))
     max_(s in S)||X_(n,s)-sigma(s)||_2 > 0.             (AOR1)
   ```

Here `S` is any fixed finite generating set of `C`.  Changing `S` changes
the positive constant but not the property, by fixed-word telescoping.

By `sl3-amalgamated-free-enemies-are-ambient-extension-outliers`, condition
`(AOR1)` is automatic for every hypothetical HNN enemy.  Thus this is the
exact residual leaf after the unbalanced-torus extraction.  A proof may use
the full `B`-valued alternating-word law; it does not need to prove scalar
commutators for arbitrary auxiliary unitaries, as projective transfer does.

The outlier condition alone is not contradictory: it is a separation from
the exact ambient representation variety, not a matrix-order obstruction.
The missing argument must couple that separation to the common actor which
simultaneously transports the free conjugate-letter frame.  Scalar actor
moments or the single coefficient `1_C` cannot do this, because the same
joint law exists exactly in the abstract HNN group factor.
