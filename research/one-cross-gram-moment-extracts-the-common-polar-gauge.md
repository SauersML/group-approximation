Two exact packet intertwiners with common source and target and cross-Gram
square within `Delta` of full source mass admit a common relative commutant
unitary `V` with errors `sqrt(Delta)` and `sqrt(2Delta)`.  One overlap scalar
therefore suffices after the corners are identified.  The theorem does not
force that scalar to be large.
