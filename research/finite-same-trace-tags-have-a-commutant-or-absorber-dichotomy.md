Let `A` be a finite-dimensional C-star algebra, let
`pi_S,pi_T` be finite-dimensional unital representations, and let
`S:H_S->H_T` be an isometry satisfying

```text
pi_T(a)S=S pi_S(a)                    for every a in A.
```

Put `T=S^*` and `Q=I-ST`. Then

```text
Q in pi_T(A)'.                                             (STA1)
```

Consequently a literal finite-packet authentication `Q=pi_T(p)`, with
`p` a projection of `A`, forces `pi_T(p)` to be central in the
represented packet algebra. On every represented simple block, `p`
must therefore have rank zero or full rank. A noncentral packet atom
cannot be the cokernel of a fully packet-covariant rectangular
occurrence.

The alternative is exactly absorbable. If the authentication asks only

```text
rank(Q)=rank(pi_T(p)),
```

or adjoins an otherwise unconstrained router `W` with
`WQW^*=pi_T(p)`, then such a `W` exists whenever the two ranks agree.
If equality of the traces of two packet projections is required in every
finite-dimensional representation of `A`, their ranks agree in every
simple block and the packet algebra itself already contains a partial
isometry between them. Thus pure finite same-trace data gives the
dichotomy

```text
full covariance + literal atom:       incompatible unless central,
trace/equivalence only:               exact finite absorber.           (STA2)
```

The value `alpha=1/18` has a literal qutrit absorber. Let
`B=<H> times C` be the abelian order-eighteen head torus and let

```text
q_b=E e_b,                    E=(1-H)/2.
```

In the regular representation of `B), `q_b` is a minimal central
character projection. Take `H_T=ell^2(B)`, take `H_S` to be the sum
of the other seventeen character lines, and let `S` be the inclusion.
Then

```text
TS=I,              ST=I-q_b,              rank(q_b)/18=1/18,            (STA3)
```

with exact `B)-covariance. After adjoining the target/source grading
`z`, the block actor

```text
a = [ Q  S ]
    [ T  0 ]
```

is an involution, `(az)^4=1`, and its target compression is
`p_T a p_T=Q=q_b`. The image generated by `B,a,z` is finite (a
quotient of `B times D_8`). Hence even a literally authenticated
qutrit `17->18` occurrence has an exact finite model with positive
head.

For the full finite subgroup

```text
F_0=<C,H,C_1,W>,                       |F_0|=288,
```

the same atom has regular rank sixteen. On `ell^2(F_0)`, let
`Q=R(q_b)` be right convolution and let the carrier be the left regular
representation. Then `Q` commutes with the whole carrier, and the same
construction gives

```text
dim(H_T)=288,       dim(H_S)=272,       rank(Q)/288=1/18.                (STA4)
```

The actor and grading again generate only a finite extension of the left
regular image. By contrast, the natural left-convolution atom
`L(q_b)` is noncentral: in the three-dimensional induced irreducible
blocks of `F_0`, it has rank one. Therefore it cannot equal a
full-`F_0)-covariant cokernel. It has the same rank as `R(q_b)`, so
an unconstrained equality router still exists; that router necessarily
fails to intertwine the full left action.

Thus neither the qutrit value `1/18` nor a finite same-trace packet
exposes the rectangular multiplicity cokernel. A viable authentication
must use one mixed nonfinite relation which couples the equality router
to a raw `S/T` occurrence without demanding the impossible full-packet
covariance.
