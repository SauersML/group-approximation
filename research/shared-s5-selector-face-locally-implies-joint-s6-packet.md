**INVALIDATED PROPOSAL.**  Enlarge the three complement selectors and common
pivot to the full point-stabilizer `S_5`.  Exactify each contextual `S_6`
chart relative to that same face, then use the selector centralizer to assert
that their two missing transpositions coincide and hence that both charts
come from one joint `S_6` representation.

`shared-s5-selector-face-has-multiplicity-rotation-gauge` gives a
six-dimensional exact countermodel.  The natural permutation representation
restricted to the point stabilizer has two trivial summands.  Rotating them
commutes with the entire shared `S_5` action but changes the missing
transposition.  Both arms therefore commute with the same selector `S_4`,
and every relation on the shared face and nonshared collision remains exact,
without a joint extension.  A live selector-packet proof needs one further
relation coupling the two extension multiplicity spaces; subgroup
centralizers alone are insufficient.
