Let a countable discrete amenable group `Gamma` act freely and minimally on a
compact Hausdorff space `X` with the uniform Rokhlin property, and put
`A = C(X) ⋊ Gamma`. Then `A ~= A tensor Z` if and only if `A` has strict
comparison of positive elements. In particular this holds for every free
minimal `Z^d`-action.

So Problem XVIII has a positive answer on this class. By Li--Niu's
remark, every metrizable Choquet simplex already occurs as the trace simplex of
some `C(X) ⋊ Z`.
