This is Lemma 1, Theorem 2 and Corollary 3 of the artifact.

1. **Presentation.** `R = LC(X,k) ⋊ P` is generated by `e_a` and `u_s`. Finite presentation does not
   depend on the generating set, so there are finitely many relators in letters `E_a, U_s^(±1)` whose
   monomials have length `<= ℓ`.
2. **Regular modules.** For `x ∈ X`, `ρ_x(f) δ_z = f(z·x) δ_z` and `ρ_x(u_p) δ_z = δ_(pz)` make `k[P]` an
   `R`-module, and `ρ_x(e_a)` reads the site `z^(-1)`.
3. **Wall lemma.** Suppose `T_1, T_2 ∈ X` and `P = I ⊔ M ⊔ O` with `d(I,O) > 2ℓ`, `T_1 = T_2` on `M`, and
   differences at `t_1 ∈ I` and at `t_2 ∈ O`. Put `D_a(t) = diag([T_1(t) = a], [T_2(t) = a])` and
   `S = [[1,0],[1,1]]`. On `k[P] ⊗ k^2`, let `E_a(δ_z ⊗ v) = δ_z ⊗ E_a(z^(-1)) v`, where
   `E_a(t) = D_a(t)` for `t ∈ I ∪ M` and `E_a(t) = S D_a(t) S^(-1)` for `t ∈ O`. On `M`, `D_a` is scalar,
   so both formulas agree there. Let `U_s` translate.
   - A monomial of length `<= ℓ` applied to `δ_z ⊗ v` visits sites in `z^(-1) B_ℓ`, which misses `I` or
     `O`. So in the basis `(w_1, w_2)`, respectively `(S w_1, S w_2)`, every relator acts as
     `ρ_(T_1)(r) ⊕ ρ_(T_2)(r) = 0`. So the space is an `R`-module.
   - In `R`, `e_a` commutes with `u_p e_b u_p^(-1)`. On the module this says `E_a(z^(-1))` commutes with
     `E_b(z^(-1) p)`, so `E_a(t_1)` commutes with `E_b(t_2)`.
   - For `a = T_1(t_1)` and `b = T_1(t_2)`, these are the projections along `k w_2` onto `k w_1` and onto
     `k(w_1 + w_2)`, which do not commute. Contradiction.
4. **SFT.** By `fp-crossed-products-force-sft-over-any-group`, `X` is an SFT of some radius `r`.
5. **Splice.** Let `T_1 != T_2` be homoclinic with `D_0 = Δ(T_1,T_2)` and `K = N_(2r)(D_0)`.
   - By minimality, `T_1|_K` recurs on a syndetic, hence infinite, set of translates. Pick `g` with
     `T_1(gq) = T_1(q)` for `q ∈ K` and `d(K, gK) > 2ℓ + 4r`.
   - Let `T_3` copy `T_2` on `D_0` and on `g D_0`, via `T_3(gq) = T_2(q)`, and `T_1` elsewhere. Every
     `r`-window of `T_3` is a window of `T_2` (near `D_0`), of a translate of `T_2` (near `g D_0`), or of
     `T_1`. So `T_3 ∈ X`.
   - `T_1` and `T_3` agree off `D_0 ∪ g D_0` and differ on both, with `d(D_0, g D_0) > 2ℓ`. This
     contradicts Step 3.
6. **Shell filling.** If `x = y` on `N_r(F) \ F`, let `z` be `y` on `F` and `x` elsewhere. Its windows
   meeting `F` lie in `N_r(F)`, where `z = y`, so `z ∈ X` and `Δ(x,z) ⊆ F`. By Step 5, `z = x`. `∎`
