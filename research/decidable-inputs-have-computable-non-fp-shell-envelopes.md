Given an infinite finitely generated P with a word-problem algorithm
and any consistent finite prescription for nu, one can compute an
extension nu such that E_nu contains P faithfully, is finitely
generated, has decidable word problem, and is NOT finitely presented.
If P is perfect, this E_nu is also simple.

This gives computable bad enumerations for finite presentation of
the WHOLE envelope, not only of its singularity stabilizers. For
virtually cyclic P these coexist, in every finite-prescription
neighborhood, with enumerations giving finitely presented shell
envelopes. Computability and a finite initial enumeration cannot
by themselves certify success of the universal presentation route.
