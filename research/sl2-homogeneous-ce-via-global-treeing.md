Try to tree the orbit relation of

```text
SL_2(Z[1/2]) action SL_2(Q_2)/Lambda'
```

using the Bruhat--Tits Bass--Serre decomposition.  If this worked, the
relation algebra would be an `L^infinity(X)`-amalgamated free product of
hyperfinite one-edge relation algebras, and Brown--Dykema--Jung permanence
would make it Connes embeddable.

This route is dead.  `sl2-homogeneous-action-is-not-treeable` proves that
the essentially free relation has positive second L2-Betti number, whereas
every treeable relation has zero L2-Betti numbers above degree one.  The
Bass--Serre decomposition instead amalgamates over the nonamenable
finite-index relation generated by the Iwahori subgroup `D`; it is relative
tree structure, not a global treeing over the Cartan.

The refutation does not decide Connes embeddability: Brown--Dykema--Jung is a
sufficient permanence theorem, not a converse.  The non-Cartan common-base
embedding of `free-lattice-ce-is-joint-ultrapower-spatialization` remains
open.
