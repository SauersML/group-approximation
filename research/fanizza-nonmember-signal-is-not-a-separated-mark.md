For `m notin L`, `(FTB1)` applies in particular at `epsilon=0`.  Therefore
every perfect BCS strategy in the scope of the theorem has

```text
phi(D)=0.                                               (FNM1)
```

So the distinguished projection `D` cannot simultaneously be used, on the
same nonmember side of the reduction, as the positive-trace carrier whose
separation starts a hyperlinearity contradiction.  The cited theorem also
does not construct a finitely presented group or prove that a named group word
is nontrivial.

Any groupification route using the Fanizza recurrence must add a separate
completeness interface: a genuine word `w!=1`, for example a Boone nonhalting
word or a self-referential mark, whose matrix separation feeds the initial
rank-one carrier while the Fanizza computation drives that carrier toward
zero.  Treating `D` itself as that mark silently reverses the two alternatives
of the `L`-family and is invalid.
