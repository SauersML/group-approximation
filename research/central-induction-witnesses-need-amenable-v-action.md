**ESTABLISHED** by `central-induction-witnesses-need-amenable-v-action-proof` (unreviewed).

**Setting.**
- `C = {0,1}^N` and `A = C(C,Z)`.
- `G = A ⋊ V` with `(v·f)(x) = f(v^(-1)x)`.
- `z = 1_C` is central in `G`.
- `p : G → V` is the quotient map.

**Theorem.** Let `H ≤ G` be co-amenable, so that `G/H` carries a `G`-invariant mean.
1. If `p(H) = V`, then `H ⊇ nA` for some integer `n ≥ 1`.
2. If `z ∈ H` and the image of `z` in `H^ab` has infinite order, then `p(H)` is a proper
   co-amenable subgroup of `V`. Hence `thompson-v-has-a-fixed-point-free-amenable-action` holds, and
   by `thompson-v-sofic-from-fixed-point-free-amenable-action` `V` is sofic.

**Special cases.** None of the following is co-amenable in `G`:
- `V` itself;
- `Z·z × V`;
- any twisted complement `{(c(v) + kz, v)}` from a cocycle `c : V → A/Z·z`.

**What this kills.** It kills the refutation route recorded under
`cantor-integer-maps-central-direction-has-relative-t`, Attempts, 2026-09-17 (w6b). That route takes a
co-amenable `H ∋ z` and a character `ψ` of `H` with `ψ(z) ≠ 0`, induces `e^(2πitψ)`, and uses Fell
continuity. Here is the class and where it dies:
- *Class.* Every induction witness `Ind_H^G χ_t` with `H` co-amenable, `z ∈ H`, and characters `χ_t` of
  `H` with `χ_t → 1` and `χ_t(z) ≠ 1`. The last two conditions force `z` to have infinite order in
  `H^ab`.
- *Invariant.* The constancy partition `P(f)`, the coarsest cone partition on which `f` is constant.
  It is quasi-equivariant under the affine `V`-action on `A/Z·z`, and it grows to `2^d` cones on
  depth-`d` checkerboards.
- *Where every member dies.*
  - If `p(H) = V`, the `V`-submodule `H ∩ A` is either inside `Z·z` or contains `nA`.
  - In the first case the transfer theorem puts positive mean on partitions with a bounded number of
    cones, and checkerboard translates are disjoint, so no invariant mean exists.
  - In the second case `z` has order dividing `n` in `H^ab`, because `A` is generated by the elements
    `v·f − f`.
  - So the route survives only through a proper co-amenable `p(H) < V`. That is the open problem
    `thompson-v-has-a-fixed-point-free-amenable-action`, and a solution would make `V` sofic.
  - The candidate `p(H)` must moreover avoid every null stabilizer family of
    `thompson-v-amenable-actions-are-free-off-fixed-points` and
    `thompson-v-finitary-structure-stabilizers-are-not-co-amenable`.

**Consequence for the goal.** A proof that `nV` is a-T-menable must refute
`cantor-integer-maps-central-direction-has-relative-t`, because `G ≤ 2V ≤ nV`. By this claim, it cannot
do so by inducing characters from a co-amenable subgroup unless it also produces a fixed-point-free
amenable action of `V`. The remaining witnesses are non-induced ones, such as the long-range random
scale laws left open by the w6b class-kill.

**Not proved.** This says nothing about witnesses induced from higher-dimensional representations of
co-amenable subgroups, or about non-induced witnesses.
