title: A zero-dimensional tensor factor with a character preserves nuclear dimension exactly
---

Let `A` and `B` be C*-algebras.  Suppose

```text
dim_nuc(A)=0
```

and `A` admits a nonzero character `chi:A->C`.  Then

```text
dim_nuc(A tensor_min B)=dim_nuc(B),
```

including when the common value is infinite.

The character hypothesis is the exact source of the lower bound: without a
one-dimensional quotient, tensoring by a zero-dimensional algebra can erase
parts of the primitive spectrum and need not admit this quotient argument.
