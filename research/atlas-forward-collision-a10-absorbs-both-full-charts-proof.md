Let `Q=A10` be the exact finite image from
`atlas-forward-collision-has-faithful-a10-quotient`.  Its labeled local
subgroups include faithful copies

```text
F~=C3 x S3,       |F|=18,
K~=S3,            |K|=6.                              (FC1)
```

The first full atlas chart `A8^(0)` contains the same labeled `F`; the second
chart `A8^(1)` contains the same labeled collision subgroup `K`.  On a set of
size

```text
|A10|=1814400=90|A8|,                                 (FC2)
```

compare the left regular action of `A10` with ninety copies of the regular
action of each `A8`.  Restriction of a regular action to a subgroup is a
multiple of that subgroup's regular action, and exact index arithmetic gives

```text
Reg(A10)|_F       =100800 Reg(F)
                  =(90 Reg(A8^(0)))|_F,

Reg(A10)|_K       =302400 Reg(K)
                  =(90 Reg(A8^(1)))|_K.               (FC3)
```

Thus there are bijections of the underlying 1814400-point sets intertwining
the **labeled** `F` actions and, independently, the labeled `K` actions.
Conjugate the two ninety-fold A8 permutation actions by these bijections and
let `P` be the subgroup of `S_(1814400)` generated by them and the regular
A10 action.  Each vertex action remains faithful.  Since the common subgroup
elements agree literally, every relation already holding in the A10 carrier
-- collision, the forward edge, and its adjacent core edge -- still holds.
All internal relations of both complete A8 charts hold as well.

Equivalently, before passing to the finite permutation image the vertex
diagram is the tree of finite groups

```text
A8^(0) --F-- A10 --K-- A8^(1).                        (FC4)
```

The next distinct cross-chart A4 context is qualitatively different.  Adding
an A4 vertex along its endpoint cyclic subgroups `C2` and `C3` in the two
chart vertices closes a cycle in `(FC4)`.  Bass--Serre normal form would retain a
stable-letter holonomy around that cycle; the literal atlas pair-cube sets
that holonomy to the identity.  Hence regular restriction matching alone no
longer proves vertex injectivity.  This identifies, without asserting a gap,
the first exact double-coset/centralizer seam after the full-chart extension.

The artifact checks every multiplicity in `(FC2)--(FC3)` exactly and was run
through the MSI wrapper.
