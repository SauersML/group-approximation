**ESTABLISHED (elementary).**

**Twisted lemma.** Let `T <= Sym(m)` and `H` be finite groups, `W = H wr T = H^m ⋊ T`, with
`(f; p)(g; q) = (f p(g); pq)`. Let `beta in T` be an `m`-cycle, `alpha in T`, and put
`gamma = alpha beta alpha^-1`. Let `f in H^m`, and let `F` be the product of the coordinates of `f` taken
along the cycle of `gamma^-1`, from any starting point. If `F` is a commutator in `H`, then `(f; [alpha, beta])`
is a commutator in `W`.

**Inheritance.**
- *Hypotheses.* Suppose `T` contains an `m`-cycle `zeta` with `[T, <<zeta>>] = [T, T]`, where `<<zeta>>`
  is the normal closure. Let `c_T` be the least `c` such that every element of `[T, T]` is a product of
  `c` commutators `[alpha, beta]` with `beta` conjugate to `zeta`. Then `c_T <= |T|`.
- *Conclusion.* If every element of `[H, H]` is a product of `c_H` commutators of `H`, then every element
  of `[W, W]` is a product of `max(c_T, c_H)` commutators of `W`.

**Towers.** Let `W = T_1 wr ... wr T_d` be an iterated permutational wreath product, where each vertex
group `T_i <= Sym(m_i)` contains an `m_i`-cycle `zeta_i` with `[T_i, <<zeta_i>>] = [T_i, T_i]`. Then every
element of `[W, W]` is a product of at most `max_i c_(T_i) <= max_i |T_i|` commutators, for every depth
`d`.

**Examples of admissible vertex groups.**
- *Every `T` normally generated by a full cycle:* `C_m` acting regularly, `Sym(m)`, and `Alt(m)` for odd
  `m`.
- *Dihedral groups.* `D_m` on `m` points: the rotation normally generates `C_m`, and
  `[D_m, C_m] = [D_m, D_m]`.

So solvable towers such as `C_2 wr C_3 wr C_2 wr C_3 wr ...`, Sylow towers `C_p wr C_p wr ...`, and mixed
towers such as `C_2 wr Alt(5) wr Sym(4) wr ...` have uniformly bounded commutator width on their derived
subgroups. Many of them need unboundedly many generators: `W^ab` contains `prod_i T_i^ab`.
