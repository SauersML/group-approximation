**Problem LXVII of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**
Classify unital separable nuclear `Z`-stable algebras with exactly one
proper nonzero ideal by ideal-related `KK` plus tracial data.

## Attempts

* The purely infinite case is done (Kirchberg's ideal-related
  classification / Gabe--Ruiz for extensions of Kirchberg algebras);
  the extension problem proper: `0 → I → A → Q → 0` with `I, Q`
  classifiable, and the algebra determined by the classes of `I`, `Q`
  plus the Busby class in an ideal-equivariant `KK(Q, I)` together
  with the trace-pasting datum (which traces of `I` extend, an
  `Aff`-cone extension).  The genuinely open half is stably finite:
  there the Busby invariant must be paired with tracial data that has
  NO purely `KK` home — the same trace/KK entanglement as Conjecture
  D (LVII), now relative.  Recorded reduction: LXVII for stably
  finite extensions = relative Conjecture D for the corner inclusions
  `I ◁ A`; the full-extension uniqueness technology (Gabe--Ruiz
  corona lifting + Szabo's KK-uniqueness) makes existence again the
  missing half.
