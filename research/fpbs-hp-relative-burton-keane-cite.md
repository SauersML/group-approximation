Citation import, not a reproof. T. Hutchcroft and M. Pan, arXiv:2409.12283v1,
Theorem 1.7: if `G` is a Cayley graph of a finitely generated group `Gamma` and
`H <= Gamma` is amenable, then for each `p in [0,1]` the percolation `G_p` has at
most one H-infinite cluster almost surely, and in particular
`p_c(H;G) = p_u(H;G)`. Read from the PDF on 2026-09-11. This route asserts only
that the cited theorem says what the target records.
