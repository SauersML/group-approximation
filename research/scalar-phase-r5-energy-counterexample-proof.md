Because `b` is a presentation generator and the relator set is finite,
the scalar tuple `(SPR1)` has defect at most `L|t|`.  Normalized HS norm of a
scalar is its absolute value.  All word evaluations commute with `U_t(b)`,
so every proposed conjugate is `z`; direct evaluation gives
`(SPR5)--(SPR6)`.
