Let G be infinite countable torsion-free, p prime, and W=C_p wr G.
Then, as additive subgroups of ℝ,

```text
τ_W(K_0(C*_r W))=τ_G(K_0(C*_r G))+ℤ[1/p].
```

This equality assumes no Baum–Connes theorem for either group.
The finite-subgroup trace ring Λ_W is ℤ[1/p]. If ordinary host
assembly is surjective, the displayed range equals Λ_W, so canonical
trace cannot detect a new wreath-product obstruction beyond the host.
The unresolved virtual K_0 and odd K-theory remainders are not removed.
