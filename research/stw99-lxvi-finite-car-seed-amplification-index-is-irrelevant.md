title: The amplification index of a finite CAR seed is irrelevant
---

The following existential statements are equivalent.

1. There is a simple unital finite but non-stably-finite C-star algebra that
   contains a unital copy of the CAR algebra.
2. There is a simple unital C-star algebra `C` with finite unit and a unital
   CAR embedding such that `M_2(C)` has infinite unit.

Thus the unresolved construction in Problem LXVI does not need to control
the first unstable matrix size.  Instability at any finite matrix level can
be converted, without tensoring by the CAR algebra, into instability of the
double of another finite CAR-containing simple algebra.
