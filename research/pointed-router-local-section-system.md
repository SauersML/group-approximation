**OPEN.**  Fix the distinguished perfect commuting trace of the finite
non-RU independent-set instance used by
`source-specific-pointed-rank-router-face`, together with its row-clock PVM
packet.  There is a finite cover by source-selected packet charts such that,
for every forbidden pair `f`, each chart carries exact local role words
`R_(f,a),S_(f,a)` satisfying on that chart

```text
R_(f,a)=diag(1-2P_f,1-2Q_f),
S_(f,a)=diag((1-2P_f)(1-2Q_f),1).                    (PRL1)
```

The same local formulas are robust for canonical-trace matrix microstates:
after the already available row-PVM rounding, their Hilbert--Schmidt error is
bounded by a dimension-independent constant times the fixed local packet
relator defect plus `o(1)`.

On a nonempty overlap of two charts `a,b`, the two local realizations differ
only by a finite-dimensional **source gauge** in the authenticated role
multiplicity/selector algebra.  In particular the discrepancy can be recorded
by a finite list of integral rank coordinates and mixed trace coordinates
after clearing the fixed denominators of the source packet.

No global compatibility is asserted.  The point is to separate the algebraic
problem into local exact sections and a finite gluing problem.  Tensor closure
does not refute `(PRL1)`: the claim never promises that the local sections
exist for an arbitrary abstract pair `PQ=0`, only on charts cut out inside the
one distinguished source packet.
