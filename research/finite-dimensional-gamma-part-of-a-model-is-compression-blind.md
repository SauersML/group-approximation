title: In any unitary model of a compressed pair the finite-dimensional part of the subgroup action on the standard space is a central projection of the model and is blind to the compression, without property (T)
distinct_from:
  amenable-kazhdan-models-absorb-compression: that needs the whole subgroup algebra atomic, obtained from property (T) plus amenability, and concludes absorption of the decompressed subgroup on all of the model; this needs no hypothesis at all, isolates the finite-dimensional part of the subgroup action as a corner, and concludes absorption only on that corner.
  bounded-rank-corners-excluded: that excludes bounded-rank mark-detecting corners in a relative commutant over a Kazhdan source; this identifies a canonical corner, the closure of all finite-dimensional subrepresentations, and shows the compression-detecting elements of any faithful model live in its complement.

Let `Gamma <= G`, let `t in G` satisfy `t Gamma t^(-1) <= Gamma`, and let
`pi : G -> U(N)` be any homomorphism into a von Neumann algebra `(N, tau)`
with faithful normal tracial state.  Let `Gamma` act on the standard space
`H = L^2(N)` by left multiplication `L_gamma xi = pi(gamma) xi`, and let

```text
K_Gamma = closure of the sum of all finite-dimensional L_Gamma-invariant subspaces of H
```

be the finite-dimensional part of this action.  Write `e = e_(K_Gamma)`.

**Theorem** (no hypothesis on `Gamma`, `G`, `pi` or the character).

1. `e in N`, and `e` commutes with `pi(Gamma)`, with `pi(t)` for every
   compressor `t`, and with every element of the centralizer
   `pi(Gamma)' cap N`.  Hence `e` is central in the von Neumann algebra
   generated by `pi(Gamma)`, the compressors, and `pi(Gamma)' cap N`.
2. `K_Gamma = K_(t^(-1) Gamma t)`: the finite-dimensional part of the action
   of the larger group `t^(-1) Gamma t` coincides with that of `Gamma`.
3. The corner `e N e` carries the compressed model `pi(.) e`, in which the
   subgroup algebra `pi(Gamma)'' e` is atomic and, by
   `amenable-kazhdan-models-absorb-compression` (its atomic case, which uses no
   property (T)), `pi(t^(-1) Gamma t) e <= pi(Gamma)'' e`.

**Consequence for the Kun--Thom objects.**  For a homomorphism `Phi` of the
coset wreath `W`, the free-lamp group `E = G *_Gamma (Gamma x K)` or the
double `D` into `U(N)`, the projection `e` computed from `Phi|_G` commutes
with the lamps too (they lie in `Phi(Gamma)' cap N`), so `e in Phi(W)' cap N`
is a central projection of the whole model, and on the corner `e` every
compression commutator `[h, a_Gamma]`, `h in t^(-1) Gamma t - Gamma`, is
trivial.  If `Phi` is faithful these commutators are therefore detected only
on the complementary corner `1 - e`, where the action of `Gamma` on
`L^2((1-e) N (1-e))` has **no finite-dimensional subrepresentation at all**.
This sharpens consequence 3 of `amenable-kazhdan-models-absorb-compression`:
not only must the subgroup algebra be non-amenable, the compression-detecting
part of the model must be free of finite-dimensional `Gamma`-subrepresentations
of its standard space.

**What it does not say.**  Nothing about the corner `1 - e`, which is where
every live route lives; and nothing at finite level: coordinatewise-exact
finite models of a compressor do exist
(`amenable-multi-compressors-have-a-joint-hs-telescope`), because the mass
conservation behind this theorem is exact only where the invariant subspace
is a genuine finite-trace projection, not up to a Folner boundary.
