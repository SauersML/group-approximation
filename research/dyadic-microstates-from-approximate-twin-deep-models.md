`approximate-twin-deep-vertex-models-exist` supplies `(TD_(R,ε))` for every `R >= 2` and `ε > 0`.
Part (a) of `twin-deep-vertex-models-give-homogeneous-microstates` turns that hypothesis into
topological microstates `(HV1)` for the action of `PSL_2(Z[1/2])` on `PSL_2(Q_2)/Λ`, for every
torsion-free cocompact lattice `Λ`. That is exactly the target claim.
