**Problem LIII of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(their Challenge): classify strongly outer (cocycle) actions of
countable discrete amenable `G` on unital simple separable nuclear
stably finite `Z`-stable algebras up to cocycle conjugacy, by
equivariant `K`/`KK`, traces and group cohomology — the stably finite
counterpart of the Gabe--Szabo dynamical Kirchberg--Phillips theorem
(cocycle conjugacy ⟺ `KK^G`-equivalence, for outer actions on stable
Kirchberg algebras).

## Attempts

* The Gabe--Szabo proof runs on `O_∞`-absorption (isometric shift
  absorption), unavailable when crossed products are stably finite; a
  finite version must replace it by tracial technology, and every
  ingredient now has an identified analogue EXCEPT one: equivariant
  existence.  Uniqueness: Szabo's equivariant KK-uniqueness (Jan
  2026) + Ocneanu at the von Neumann level (strong outerness) +
  equivariant Z-stability (XXXIX where known).  Existence: an
  equivariant version of the trace-KK compatibility existence theorem
  behind Conjecture D — which is exactly Problem LVII made
  equivariant.  So the challenge factors: LVII(equivariant) +
  XXXIX + Szabo-uniqueness ⟹ LIII in outline; recorded so progress
  on LVII/XXXIX moves this node.  The cohomological component enters
  through cocycle actions (untwisting obstructions, Problem LVI).
