Tensor products of the one-leg central isotypic projections are orthogonal,
sum to one, commute with every diagonal operator
`lambda_Q(g)^(tensor t)`, and have product normalized ranks.  Conjugating by
the tensor power of an automorphism implementer permutes each label, proving
equivariance and the independent product-Plancherel law.

For a centerless nonabelian simple `K`, every nontrivial irreducible is
faithful.  If a tensor product of its matrices is the identity, every factor
is scalar; faithfulness and trivial center then force the group element to be
the identity as soon as one factor is nontrivial.  This gives the displayed
good-mass formula coordinate by coordinate.  For `A5`, its irreducible
dimensions `1,3,3,4,5` give collision probability `29/100`; independence
raises it to the `t`-th power.
