The structural carrier from `atlas-s288-carrier-extends-through-first-core-cycle`
extends through both remaining edges of the first `K_(2,2)` component.  The
right finite vertex is the canonical `GL_3(F_2) x C_2` packet block, and the
common labeled subgroup is `S_4 x C_2`.  Seven left regular actions and twelve
right regular actions have identical restriction to this subgroup, giving a
faithful finite carrier on 4032 points.

In this finite completion the third distinct edge does not merely add another
Coxeter generator: choosing the canonical rank-three block simultaneously
closes the fourth edge.  This is an existence statement for the chosen finite
completion, not a claim that the fourth cube follows abstractly from the first
three.
