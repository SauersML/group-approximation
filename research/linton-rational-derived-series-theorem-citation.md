Citation import, not a reproof.  Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2(2) of
arXiv:2407.09272v2 were read from the arXiv record on 2026-08-17 and quoted
verbatim in the artifact, including the sentence bounding the finite stage `n`
by the length of the relator.

Nothing beyond those two statements, and the corollary's remark that
`G/G_Q^(ω) = F/<<r>>` is itself residually rationally solvable, is imported.
The reading was checked against the abstract, which states the same content in
prose: "the intersection of the rational derived series of a one-relator group
is rationally perfect and is normally generated by a single element.  As a
corollary, we characterise precisely when a one-relator group is residually
rationally solvable."
