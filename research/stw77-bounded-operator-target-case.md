title: Properly infinite-image maps into B(H) are O-infinity-stable
distinct_from:
  stw77-af-range-maps-are-oinfty-stable: that theorem assumes an AF range in an arbitrary target and uses internal matrix-unit transport; this theorem allows an arbitrary separable range in B(H) and uses essential-representation absorption.
---

Let `H` be a separable infinite-dimensional Hilbert space, let `A` be
separable and unital, and let

```text
phi:A->B(H)
```

be unital.  If every nonzero positive element of `phi(A)` is properly
infinite in `B(H)`, then `phi` is O-infinity-stable.

Neither exactness of `A` nor finite nuclear dimension of `phi` is needed.
The centralizing mechanism is absorption of essential representations, not
proper infiniteness of finite-dimensional model supports.
