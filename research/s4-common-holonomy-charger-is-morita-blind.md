# The s4 common-corner separator cannot charge one commuting multiplicity holonomy

ESTABLISHED.  Let `P_1,...,P_4` be unitaries in a finite packet algebra and
let `W` be a unitary commuting with that algebra.  For arbitrary integers
`n_i`, put

```text
T_i=P_i W^(n_i).                                         (SCH1)
```

Every monomial in the alternating standard polynomial has the same
multiplicity factor.  Hence, exactly,

```text
s4(T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4)
 =s4(P_1,P_2,P_3,P_4) W^(n_1+n_2+n_3+n_4),              (SCH2)
```

and normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm gives

```text
||s4(T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4)||_2=||s4(P_1,P_2,P_3,P_4)||_2.    (SCH3)
```

The same formula holds with `W^(n_i)` replaced by any four commuting
unitaries in the abelian algebra generated by `W`.  Thus every direct mixed
word obtained by appending powers of the surviving D16/Hadamard common block
to the four packet paths is exactly blind to that block, not merely blind
after amplification.

There is also a sharp same-corner dichotomy.  If relations really put the
four returned operators in `M_2 tensor 1` on the selected corner, then
Amitsur--Levitzki makes `s4=0`; imposing the two independent Pauli pairs makes
`s4=8ABCD`, so the exact completeness model is already impossible at
`W=1`.  If instead the presentation only authenticates one finite
two-dimensional packet type, Schur decomposition gives

```text
H_q = C^2 tensor H_mult,                                 (SCH4)
```

and it does not put returned words in `M_2 tensor 1`.  On the single common
carrier

```text
H_q=C^2 tensor C^4 tensor K,                             (SCH5)
```

let the authenticated packet act on `C^2`, put the two independent Pauli
pairs on `C^4`, and put the common holonomy `W` on `K`.  This is an exact
same-carrier model (no disjoint-summand split), the finite packet and its
selector survive, and the Pauli value of `s4` lives entirely in the
multiplicity algebra.  There is no degree-two polynomial identity on
`M_2 tensor B(C^4 tensor K)`.

Consequently the `s4` separator cannot serve as an external charger for the
one common D16 multiplicity word through a direct commuting mixed-word
coupling.  To use the separator one must additionally authenticate the
non-Morita statement that the returned tuple lies in the scalar copy
`M_2 tensor 1`; that is precisely the common-corner/multiplicity-locking gate,
not a consequence of a fixed packet type.
