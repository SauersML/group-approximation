Let `R = F_2[x_1^(+-1), x_2^(+-1), x_3^(+-1)]` and `r >= 6`. Then

```text
ker( St_r(R) -> E_r(R) ) = K2(r,R) ~= K2(R) ~= Z^3,
```

and the Steinberg symbols `c(x_1,x_2)`, `c(x_1,x_3)`, `c(x_2,x_3)` generate
it as a group. Here `c(u,v) = [H_12(u), H_13(v)]` is the convention (MSP1) of
`stable-laurent-k2-has-minimal-three-symbol-packet`. Hence

```text
St_6(R) / << c(x_1,x_2), c(x_1,x_3), c(x_2,x_3) >> ~= E_6(R).
```

This is the kernel control consumed by
`fp-surjunctive-nonsofic-double-by-stable-k2`, the finite presentation step
for the finitely presented surjunctive nonsofic double.
