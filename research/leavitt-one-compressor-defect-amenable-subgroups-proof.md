All identities below are in `H=EL_12(R)` with the manuscript's `tau`,
`c=x_34(1)`, `l=x_12(1)`; the corner conjugation `(AOC1)` and the moved-mark
identity `tau c tau^-1=x_01(q)x_34(1)` are the manuscript's equations
`(eq:corner-conjugation)` and `(eq:moved-mark)`, kernel-checked in
`RankTwelveConfiguration.lean` (`tau_compresses_corner`,
`tau_c_commutator_ell`).

## Step 1: local finiteness

`R_0=union_k B_k` with `B_k~=M_(2^k)(F_2)` and `B_k<=B_(k+1)` via
`s_ut_v=sum_b s_(ub)t_(vb)`, so `R_0` is a locally finite ring and

```text
L_0=EL_3(R_0)=union_k EL_3(B_k)=union_k SL_(3*2^k)(F_2)                (1)
```

is locally finite.  For `P=U_3(R) semidirect T_0`,
`T_0=(R_0^x)^3 cap EL_3(R)`: a finitely generated subgroup is generated by
finitely many unipotents `x_ij(a_1),...,x_ij(a_m)` and finitely many
diagonal matrices with entries in one finite group `GL_(2^k)(F_2)^3`.  The `F_2`-span of the orbit of the
`a_i` under the finite set of maps `a -> g a h^-1`, `g,h in GL_(2^k)(F_2)`,
is finite-dimensional, so by
`finite-positive-root-fragments-have-marked-regular-models` the unipotent
part generated is finite, and a finite group extended by a finite group is
finite.  Hence `P` is locally finite.

## Step 2: the compressor preserves both subgroups

`Psi(x_ij(a))=x_ij(s_0at_0)` and `s_0B_kt_0<=B_(k+1)`, so `Psi(L_0)<=L_0`.
`Psi` is multiplicative and unital on `M_3(R)`, sends unitriangular matrices
to unitriangular matrices (diagonal `q+s_0t_0=1`, strict part
`s_0a_ijt_0`), and sends `diag(u_i)` to `diag(q+s_0u_it_0)` with
`q+s_0u_it_0 in R_0^x` (inverse `q+s_0u_i^-1t_0`).  Conjugation by `tau`
preserves `EL_3(R)` because it sends each elementary generator to an
elementary generator, so `Psi(T_0)<=T_0` and `Psi(P)<=P`.

## Step 3: the moved centralizer commutes with the compressed subgroup

`tau c tau^-1=c x_01(q)` and `c` commutes with every matrix supported in
positions `0,1,2`.  It remains to check `[x_01(q),Psi(g)]=1` for `g` in a
generating set of `B`.  Write `b=s_0at_0`.  The Steinberg rows give

```text
[x_01(q),x_12(b)]=x_02(qb)=1,      qb=s_1t_1s_0at_0=0,
[x_20(b),x_01(q)]=x_21(bq)=1,      bq=s_0at_0s_1t_1=0,
[x_01(q),x_0j(b)]=[x_01(q),x_21(b)]=[x_01(q),x_01(b)]=1                 (2)
```

by disjoint or coinciding positions, and for the opposite root, in the
`(0,1)` block,

```text
(1+qE_01)(1+bE_10)=1+qE_01+bE_10+qbE_00,
(1+bE_10)(1+qE_01)=1+qE_01+bE_10+bqE_11,                               (3)
```

which agree because `qb=bq=0`.  For the torus,
`diag(v_0,v_1,v_2)x_01(q)diag(v)^-1=x_01(v_0qv_1^-1)` with
`v_i=q+s_0u_it_0`, and `v_0q=q=qv_1^-1` since `t_0q=0=qs_0`, so the
conjugate is `x_01(q)` again.  Every generator of `Psi(L_0)` and of
`Psi(P)` is covered, so `x_01(q)` centralizes `Psi(B)`.

## Step 4: the HNN presentation and amenability

Let `B x <c>` be the direct product (valid: `c notin L` and `c` centralizes
`L`).  By Steps 2 and 3,

```text
phi(g c^e)=Psi(g) x_01(q)^e c^e                                          (4)
```

is a homomorphism `B x <c> -> B x <c>`; it is injective because the
`c`-exponent is preserved and `Psi` is injective (`t_0Psi(A)s_0=A`).  The
relations `tau b tau^-1=phi(b)`, `b in B x <c>`, hold in `H` by `(AOC1)` and
`(eq:moved-mark)`, so `Lambda_B=<B,tau,c>` is a quotient of the ascending
HNN extension `E=(B x Z/2)*_phi`.  Since `phi` is injective,

```text
E=(union_(n>=0) t^-n (B x Z/2) t^n) semidirect Z,                        (5)
```

an increasing union of copies of a locally finite group, extended by `Z`.
So `E` and its quotient `Lambda_B` are elementary amenable.

## Step 5: MF and the surviving defect

By `amenable-implies-operator-mf`, `Lambda_B` is operator MF, i.e. its MF
radical is trivial.  The defect `d=[tau c tau^-1,l]=[x_01(q),x_12(1)]
=x_02(q)` is a word in `tau,c,l in Lambda_B`, and `x_02(q)!=1` in `H`
because `t_1qs_1=1`.  Hence `d notin Rad_MF(Lambda_B)` and some
norm-matrix-corona homomorphism of `Lambda_B` separates `d`.  This holds
for `B=L_0` and for `B=P`, giving `(AOC6)`.

## Step 6: maximality remark

`w=x_01(1)x_10(1)x_01(1) in SL_3(F_2)<=L_0` satisfies
`w x_01(a) w^-1=x_10(a)` for all `a in R`, so `<L_0,P>` contains every root
subgroup of `L` and equals `L=EL_3(R)`.  Thus the two amenable subgroups
are separated by exactly the Weyl-mixed nonzero-degree relations, and their
join is the Property-`(T)` group of the manuscript.
