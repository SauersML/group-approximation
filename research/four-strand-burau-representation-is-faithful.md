The Burau homomorphism `B_4 -> GL_4(Z[t, t^{-1}])` (equivalently its reduced
3-dimensional version) is injective. This is the Yes answer to Zaremsky
Problem 3.5 (`zaremsky-3-05-four-strand-burau-faithful`).
