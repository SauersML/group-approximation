**Setting.** Let `H` be any group. Call `B in M_k(Z[H])` **cubic without
inversions** if:
- it is self-adjoint with nonnegative integer coefficients;
- every row has coefficient sum `3`;
- no diagonal entry has a nonzero coefficient at `e` or at an involution.

Then `r_B` is the adjacency operator of a 3-regular multigraph on `H x [k]`,
with `H` acting freely on vertices and edges. For `L >= 2`, the `L`-subdivision
`A_L in M_(k(3L-1)/2)(Z[H])` replaces each edge orbit by an orbit of paths of
`L` edges. It is nonnegative, and it has the same component stabilizers as `B`.

**Chebyshev data.**
- `U_m(x/2)` is the monic Chebyshev polynomial.
- `lambda_j = 2cos(pi j/L)` for `j = 1..L-1`.
- `p_L(x) = U_L(x/2) - 2U_(L-2)(x/2)`, which lies in `Z[x]` and satisfies
  `p_L(lambda_j) = 3(-1)^j`.

**The claim.**
1. **Norm.**
   `rho(T_(L-1,L-1,L-1)) <= ||r_(A_L)|| <= 3/sqrt 2 + 3 * 2^(-(L+1)/2)`.
2. **Spectral transfer.**
   `mu_(A_L) = (k/2) sum_j delta_(lambda_j) + p_L^* mu_B`, where `p_L^* mu_B`
   puts the mass of `t` on the `L` roots of `p_L(x) = t`, with multiplicity.
3. **Violation transfer.** For nonzero `R in Z[t]` and `Q = R o p_L`,
   `integral_{Q!=0} log|Q| d mu_(A_L)` equals
   `(k/2) sum_(j : R(3(-1)^j) != 0) log|R(3(-1)^j)| + L integral_{R!=0} log|R| d mu_B`.
   If `||r_B|| < 3` and `B` violates Serre with `R`, then `A_L` violates Serre
   with `((t^2-9) R^m) o p_L` for all large `m`.
4. **Every group appears.** If `H` is generated by `d` elements, the hub-cycle
   operator `B_H in M_(2d)(Z[H])` is cubic without inversions with connected
   graph. So for every `eps > 0` some nonnegative integral matrix over `H` has
   norm below `3/sqrt 2 + eps` and a connected graph with stabilizer `H`.
5. **Partial converse.** If `A_L` violates Serre with `Q`, and `mu_B` has no
   atom on `p_L(Z(Q))`, then `B` violates Serre with the resultant
   `Res_lambda(p_L(lambda) - t, Q(lambda))`.

**Consequence.** Suppose that for some `eps > 0`, over a group `H`, all
nonnegative self-adjoint integral matrices of norm below `3/sqrt 2 + eps` obey
Serre. Then so do all cubic operators without inversions of norm below `3`
over `H`.

**Remark, outside this claim.** `||r_(B_H)|| < 3` when `H` is nonamenable. The
graph of `B_H` is quasi-isometric to a Cayley graph of `H`, so it has positive
Cheeger constant, and `fpbs-mohar-adjacency-norm-cheeger-bound` applies. Theorem
C does not say that cubic operators capture the determinant conjecture; that
reduction is open (artifact, Section 6.4).
