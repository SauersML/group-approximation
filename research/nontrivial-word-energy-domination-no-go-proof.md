Use the permutation tuple and unit vector given by the prerequisite.
The quadratic form of $(w(U)-I)^*(w(U)-I)$ at that vector is $2$.
Every term $(r(U)-I)^*(r(U)-I)$ has quadratic form zero there.
The alleged operator-order inequality would imply $2\leq0$.

No claim is made about the full operator norms of the relation errors
in these permutation tuples; that distinction is essential.
