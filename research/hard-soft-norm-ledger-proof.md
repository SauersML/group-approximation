Write the Newton recurrence in the residual row norm before squaring.
The linear term is `theta sqrt(E)` and the two-odd-factor term is
`Ct sqrt(E)`, so their coefficients add as in `(HSN2)`.  Compare the
independent squared metric reset cost `E/t^2` only after this amplitude
calculation.  A fixed small threshold, or any power strictly below the RMS
exponent, satisfies both requirements.
