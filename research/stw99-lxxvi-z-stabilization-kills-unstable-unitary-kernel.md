Let `D` be a unital C*-algebra, let `n>=1`, and let

```text
alpha in pi_k(U(n))
```

have zero image in the stable unitary group `pi_k(U(infinity))`.  Then the
scalar-extension image of `alpha` in

```text
pi_k(U(M_n(D tensor_min Z)))
```

is zero.

In particular, the second homotopy condition formerly imposed in the
`S^6` clutching route is automatic: the class
`alpha in pi_5(U(2))=Z/2` always dies after tensoring any coefficient algebra
with `Z`.
