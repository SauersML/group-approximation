**Established by citation.** Francesc Perera and Mikael Rordam,
[*AF-embeddings into C*-algebras of real rank
zero*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0310340), state in the application recorded
in their abstract that, for a unital real-rank-zero C-star algebra `B` and a
natural number `N`, a unital star-homomorphism from a finite direct sum of
matrix algebras, every summand having size at least `N`, exists if and only
if `B` has no representation of dimension less than `N`.

The claim is exactly this theorem, with “representation” understood as a
nonzero star-representation. No injectivity of the unital homomorphism is
needed below. A summand killed by the homomorphism contributes the zero
Cuntz class to both sides of the block calculation and may simply be
discarded; the restriction to the surviving summands remains unital.
