## Why sufficient

**The linear model.**  Let `V = Q^((N_0))` with basis `v_0, v_1, ...`,
and let `s v_j = v_(j+1)`, `t v_0 = 0`, `t v_j = v_(j-1)` for `j > 0`.
Then `ts = 1`, and by the second prerequisite the induced map
`J_Z -> End_Q(V)` is injective (its monomial basis `s^i t^j` acts
faithfully; the integral and rational cases have the same proof).  So
`GL_n(J_Z)` acts faithfully on `V^n`, and any `Delta <= GL_n(J_Z)` is a
faithful linear group over `k = Q`.

**The finite-rank kernel.**  `e = 1 - st` is the projection onto `Q v_0`,
`f_(ij) = s^i e t^j` sends `v_j` to `v_i`, and since `te = es = 0` a
reduced monomial times `e` times a reduced monomial is `0` or a matrix
unit; so the ideal `I = J_Z e J_Z` is `M_fin(N_0, Z)`, the finite-rank
integral matrices.  Setting `e = 0` makes `s, t` mutually inverse, so
`J_Z / I = Z[z, z^-1]` and the symbol map
`sigma : GL_n(J_Z) -> GL_n(Z[z,z^-1])` has kernel consisting of matrices
`g` with `g - I` of finite rank on `V^n`.  Put `N = Delta cap ker sigma`,
normal in `Delta`, with `a - I` of finite rank for each `a in N`.

**The locally residually finite quotient.**  `Delta / N` embeds in
`GL_n(Z[z,z^-1])`, which is residually finite: for a nonidentity matrix
choose a nonzero Laurent entry of its difference from `I`, clear the
power of `z`, reduce modulo a prime keeping that polynomial nonzero, and
reduce modulo an irreducible over the finite field that is neither `z`
nor a divisor of it (infinitely many exist); the finite-field matrix
group detects the matrix.  Subgroups of residually finite groups are
residually finite, so `Delta / N` is locally residually finite.

**Conclusion.**  The first prerequisite applies to `Delta <= GL_Q(V^n)`,
`N`, any finitely generated `L <= Delta`, and any finitely generated
`K <| Delta` with `K <= D_Delta(L)`: since `char Q = 0`, `K` is free
abelian of finite rank.  A Kazhdan group is finitely generated, and a
free abelian Kazhdan group is trivial; a finitely generated free abelian
group with finite abelianization is trivial.  This is `(IJ1)` and `(IJ2)`.

**The homomorphic-encoding clause** is formal: for `f : P -> GL_n(J_Z)`,
`f` carries compressors, centralizers, and elements of `L_0` to the
corresponding objects for `f(L_0)` in `f(P)`, so
`f(D_P(L_0)) <= D_(f(P))(f(L_0))`, and `f(K_0)` is a finitely generated
normal Kazhdan subgroup of `f(P)` inside that defect.

**The sharpness examples** in the claim are the manuscript's identities
for `prop:torsion-defect-ring`, which are ring identities in
`Z<s,t | ts = 1>` and hold verbatim over `J_Z`; the only place the
coefficient ring enters is the order of `z = D(1 + f_12)`, infinite over
`Z` and `p` over `F_p`.  `SL_3(Z)` is generated by its elementary
matrices, which are the `e_(ij)(e)` restricted to the three
`v_0`-coordinates.
