Use a common charged block system with unbounded finite block dimensions,
but require its dimension distribution to be tight: bounded block sizes
carry charge arbitrarily close to one.  Put arbitrary matrix-valued near
cocycles inside the blocks and allow charge-null noncompact errors.

This architecture is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-block-dimension-escapes-every-charge-cutoff`.  In fact every
bounded block-size cutoff must have charge zero, not merely charge bounded
away from one.  Any surviving common-block construction needs a purely
finitely-additive charge concentrated at dimension infinity.
