A group `G` of type F is of type `F_∞`, since a finite `K(G,1)` has finite
skeleta, and has `cd G ≤ dim K(G,1) < ∞`. If none of its Rips complexes (any finite
generating set, any scale) is contractible
(`some-type-f-group-has-no-contractible-rips-complex`), then `G` witnesses the
target. Both claims use the weak reading: some generating set, some scale.
