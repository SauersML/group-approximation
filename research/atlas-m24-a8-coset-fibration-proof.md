The first input supplies the unique fixed point and the `1+8+15` action of
`A`, making `(M24D-1)` equivariant.  A fiber is the set of `A8` subgroups in
one point stabilizer `M23`; its size is the known index `506`.  This proves
`(M24D-2)`.  The second input gives the four subdegrees `(M24D-3)`, whose sum
is `506`, and proves packet collapse throughout that fiber.
