**ESTABLISHED** by `substitution-inflation-hosts-contracting-rsg-proof`. This is a lane proof (bh-invent-06b,
2026-09-18), elementary given the BBMZ theorems quoted there from TeX and the recognizability of the
substitution. It has not been reviewed, and no priority is claimed. Anderson–Putnam collaring and the carry
calculus of self-similar tilings are classical. What is new is reading the result as a contracting RSG and what
that means for gates E2 and E3.

## Setting

- `d >= 1`. `ω` is a **box morphism** on a finite alphabet `A`: each letter `a` goes to a pattern `ω(a)` on a box
  `R(a) = ∏_i [0, s_i(a))`. The images of the letters of every `y ∈ X_ω` fit on a product grid, and `ω(y)` is the
  resulting configuration with the image of `y(0)` at `R(y(0))`. This is the d-dimensional morphism of Labbé
  (arXiv:1802.03265); for `d = 1` it is an ordinary substitution.
- **(H1)** `ω` is primitive. `X_ω` is its substitutive subshift: `x ∈ X_ω` iff every finite pattern of `x`
  occurs in some `ω^n(a)`.
- **(H2)** `X_ω` is aperiodic, so `Z^d` acts freely by `(t_v x)(z) = x(z + v)`.
- **(H3)** `ω` is recognizable. Every `x ∈ X_ω` is `x = ι_p(y) := S^p ω(y)` for a unique pair `(y, p)` with
  `y ∈ X_ω` and `p ∈ R(y(0))`. Here `(S^p w)(z) = w(z + p)`.
- **(H4)** every side length `s_i(a)` is at least 2.

`𝒢⁺` is the groupoid of germs generated by the translations `t_v` and the partial homeomorphisms `ι_p`. Its full
group is `F(𝒢⁺) = [[⟨t_v, ι_p⟩ | X_ω]]`.

## Statement

1. **Coding.** Let `Γ` be the *collared hierarchy graph*:
   - its nodes are the legal patterns on `[-1, 1]^d`;
   - an edge `(π, p, π′)` runs from `π` to `π′` when `p ∈ R(π′(0))` and `π` is the pattern of `ω(π′)` on
     `p + [-1, 1]^d`.

   Then `X_ω` is homeomorphic to the edge shift `Σ_Γ`. `Σ_Γ` is irreducible, and each `ι_p`, restricted to a
   cylinder of fixed collared type, is a canonical similarity (prepend one edge).
2. **Contracting RSG.** Under this homeomorphism `F(𝒢⁺)` is a **full contracting RSG** in `R_Γ`. It contains
   `V_Γ`, the inflation germs and `Z^d`. Its nucleus is contained in a finite set: the identities, and the one-level
   translation maps `y ↦ ι_q(t_c y)` with `c ∈ {-1, 0, 1}^d`, with their common output prefix stripped. These maps
   are the carries across fault lines.
3. **Consequences.**
   - `F(𝒢⁺)` is finitely presented (BBMZ `thrm:fin_pres`).
   - It embeds in a finitely presented simple group (`contracting-rsgs-embed-in-fp-simple-groups`).
   - It is a type (A) actor.
   - It contains the free translation action of `Z^d` on `X_ω`.
4. **Contrast with the labelled host.**
   - `G_V × (Z^d ⋉ X_ω)` has no finite coding (`free-sft-hosts-admit-no-finite-type-coding`, 4a).
   - If `X_ω` is not of finite type, `F(G_V × (Z^d ⋉ X_ω))` is not finitely presented
     (`fp-v-times-subshift-full-groups-force-quantum-rigidity`, part 2). For `d = 1` this holds for every such
     `ω`.
   - The inflation host is finitely presented in every case, and its finiteness uses no quantum rigidity.
   - The contracting loops of `𝒢⁺` are the inflations at the fixed tilings of the powers of `ω`. Their germs have
     no `Z^d`-label, as `labelled-hosts-of-free-actions-have-no-contracting-loops` requires.

## Instances

- **Labbé's 19-tile Wang shift `Ω_U`**, with the power `ω^8`:
  - (H1)–(H3) come from `labbe-substitution-is-primitive-and-recognizable`, and recognizability passes to powers
    by composing the unique decompositions;
  - (H4) is Step 2 of `research/artifacts/un-labbe-ring-substitution-complexity-2026-09-13.md`:
    `shape(ω^8(a)) >= (2, 2)` for every letter.

  `Ω_U` is a minimal free `Z²`-SFT. Its labelled host `F(G_V × (Z² ⋉ Ω_U))` is finitely presented only if `Ω_U`
  is quantum rigid, which is open (gate E2 on the Z² calibration). **Its inflation host is finitely presented
  unconditionally.**
- **`d = 1`.** Every primitive aperiodic substitution, raised to a power so that all lengths are at least 2:
  Thue–Morse, and Fibonacci² (`0 ↦ 010`, `1 ↦ 01`). Recognizability is Mossé's theorem (classical, not re-read).
  None of these subshifts is of finite type, so the labelled host is never finitely presented.
- **Constant-shape `k^d` block substitutions** (`k >= 2`) that are primitive, aperiodic and recognizable.

## Lesson for general BH

The two hard gates of Track A, rigidity (E2) and the infinite-type finiteness of free P2′ (E3), are both
artifacts of the **labelled** host `F(G_V × (Λ ⋉ X))`.
- **One family of unlabelled germs removes both.** Adjoining the self-similarity of `X` (its inflation) makes
  the translations a contracting self-similar action over the hierarchy shift. The fault lines, where rigidity
  and quantum families live, become finitely many nucleus maps: translate by `c ∈ {-1,0,1}^d` with one level of
  lookahead.
- **This is the mechanism Conjecture SS needs,** with inflation in place of telescopes.
- **The limit.** Contracting carries need the inflation to intertwine the translations with an **expanding**
  endomorphism of `Λ`, here `v ↦ (box grid)`. An injective expanding endomorphism with finite-index image
  forces polynomial growth:

  `|B(2r)| <= [Λ : φ(Λ)] · |B(r + C)|`

  so `Λ` is virtually nilpotent (Gromov).

So over the non-amenable `Λ` of Track A, amenable `Z^d`-directions can be finitely coded by inflation with no
rigidity. The non-amenable directions must be compressed either by `Λ` itself (with isotropy, gate E3′) or by
unlabelled germs that intertwine no endomorphism. This is the precise remaining shape of the problem.
