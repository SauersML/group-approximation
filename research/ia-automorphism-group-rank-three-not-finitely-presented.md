title: "The IA-automorphism group IA_3 of F_3 is not finitely presented"
---

`IA_3 = ker(Aut(F_3) -> GL_3(Z))` is not finitely presented. It follows from
`outer-torelli-rank-three-not-finitely-presented` and the rank-by-rank equivalence
`ia-n-fp-iff-outer-torelli-fp`; see the route
`ia-rank-three-not-fp-via-outer-torelli`.

Role in Zaremsky Problem 3.1: the Aut family also needs "large enough" ranks. The
open ranks are `n >= 4` (Ershov, arXiv:2601.01377, abstract).
