`pi(S) = J_1 <= Gamma` and `pi|_S` is injective by simplicity
(`fournier-facio-torsion-free-skeleton`).  Subgroups of hyperlinear groups
are hyperlinear.  The prerequisite concerns the smaller group `S` and is
strictly stronger than the target, so this is permanence, not a restatement.
