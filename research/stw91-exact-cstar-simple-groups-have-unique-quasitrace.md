If `G` is an exact C*-simple discrete group, then the canonical trace is the
unique normalized 2-quasitrace on `C*_r(G)`.

Consequently, an exact C*-simple counterexample to selflessness cannot be
detected by producing a second normalized 2-quasitrace.  Within Robert's
known necessary regularity package, one must instead detect failure of
stable rank one or strict comparison (or find an obstruction not implied by
that package).  Any counterexample detected by extra quasitraces must be
nonexact.
