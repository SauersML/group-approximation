**ESTABLISHED** (proof route `universal-lattice-stability-nonhyperlinear-proof`).

Let `S` be a finitely generated unital ring, `n >= 3`, and `I` a finitely generated two-sided ideal
such that `S/I` is a simple ring and infinite. Suppose `E_n(S)` is flexibly Hilbert--Schmidt stable
(Dogon--Vigdorovich). Then:

1. Every finite-dimensional unitary representation of `Q_I = E_n(S)/E_n(S,I)` is trivial.
2. Every asymptotic representation `σ_k` of `Q_I` is asymptotically trivial:
   `||σ_k(x) - 1||_2 -> 0` for every `x`.
3. Every nontrivial quotient of `Q_I` is not hyperlinear. In particular, the infinite group
   `E_n(S/I)` is not hyperlinear.

**Instance: the noncommutative universal lattice.** Let `S = Z<x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4>`, and let `I` be
generated by the six elements

```text
2,   x_(2+i) x_j - δ_ij  (i, j in {1,2}),   x_1 x_3 + x_2 x_4 - 1.
```

Then `S/I = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, which is simple (`leavitt-algebras-l-k-1-n-are-simple`) and infinite.
Hence:

```text
EL_3(Z<x_1..x_4>) flexibly HS-stable   ==>   EL_3(L_(F_2)(1,2)) is a non-hyperlinear group.
```

- The same holds for `F_p<x_1..x_4>` for every prime `p`, and for free algebras of any rank at
  least 4, with the extra generators sent to 0.
- `EL_3(Z<x_1..x_4>)` is a residually finite Kazhdan group:
  - it is residually finite because `Z<x_1..x_4>` is a residually finite ring;
  - it is Kazhdan by `ershov-jaikin-kassabov-steinberg-groups-have-property-t`.

**Readings.**
- **A dichotomy for free-algebra lattices.** Either a non-hyperlinear group exists, or for every
  `n >= 3` and `k >= 4` the residually finite Kazhdan group `E_n(Z<x_1..x_k>)` is **not** flexibly
  HS stable. The same holds with `F_p` in place of `Z`.
  - So proving flexible stability of any one of these groups settles `non-hyperlinear-group`.
  - A proof of their instability that does not construct a non-hyperlinear group can only use
    hyperlinear targets.
- **The cone-half route is one instance.** The wave-22 reduction of the Leavitt Steinberg route (side
  branch commit `9d1e3f7f0a`) derives non-hyperlinearity of `E_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))` from flexible
  rounding on `E_20(F_2 Q)`, where `F_2 Q` is the path algebra of the two-vertex quiver with a loop
  at each vertex and an arrow each way.
  - That is the case `S = F_2 Q`, `I = ker(F_2 Q ->> L_(F_2)(1,2))` of this claim.
  - Here the cover can be any finitely generated ring with such a quotient; the free algebra is the
    universal choice.
- **Scalar cocycles see nothing at the stable level.** By Gersten, `K_2(F_p<X>) = K_2(F_p) = 0`. So
  the stable elementary group over a free algebra is centrally closed: `H_2(E(F_p<X>); Z) = K_2 = 0`.
  - The Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma and Dogon engine behind `rf-kazhdan-group-not-flexibly-hs-stable`
    needs a non-torsion class in `H^2`. It therefore has no data on the stable free-algebra group.
  - This is a remark, not a proof: homological stability at `n = 3` is not checked here. If it holds,
    then in a world where every group is hyperlinear, the instability of `EL_n(F_p<x_1..x_4>)` is
    invisible to every scalar-cocycle construction.
