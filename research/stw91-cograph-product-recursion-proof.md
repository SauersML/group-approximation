## Finite cographs

Proceed by induction on the number of vertices.  A one-vertex graph gives
one of the assumed vertex groups.  Every finite cograph with at least two
vertices admits a nontrivial decomposition as either a disjoint union

```text
Gamma=Gamma_1 disjoint_union Gamma_2
```

or a join

```text
Gamma=Gamma_1 join Gamma_2,
```

with both induced subgraphs again cographs.

In the disjoint-union case, the graph-product normal form gives

```text
G_Gamma = G_(Gamma_1) * G_(Gamma_2),
```

and the canonical tracial reduced algebra is the reduced free product of the
two factor algebras.  By
`stw91-reduced-free-products-preserve-selflessness`, it is selfless.
Exactness is preserved by group free products (equivalently here by reduced
free products of exact C-star algebras), so the induction also retains the
exactness needed at later join nodes.

In the join case, every vertex group on one side commutes with every vertex
group on the other, and graph-product normal form gives

```text
G_Gamma = G_(Gamma_1) times G_(Gamma_2).
```

Therefore

```text
C*_r(G_Gamma)
 = C*_r(G_(Gamma_1)) tensor_min C*_r(G_(Gamma_2))
```

with the product canonical trace.  Both factors are selfless by induction,
and both are exact.  The ordinary-selfless, exact-second-factor clause of
`complete-selflessness-tensor-permanence` makes their tensor product
selfless.  Minimal tensor products preserve exactness.  This completes the
finite induction.

## Countable P4-free graphs

Let `F` range over the finite vertex subsets of `Gamma`.  The induced graph
`Gamma|F` is a finite cograph because `Gamma` has no induced `P_4`.  The
normal-form theorem for graph products embeds `G_(Gamma|F)` as the subgroup
generated by the vertices in `F`.  These subgroups form an upward directed
family and every graph-product word uses only finitely many vertices, so

```text
G_Gamma = union_(F finite) G_(Gamma|F).
```

Every finite-stage reduced algebra is selfless by the first part.
`stw91-directed-unions-of-selfless-groups` now proves selflessness of the
full reduced algebra.  Exactness also passes through this directed union,
so the stated exactness conclusion survives.  Finally Robert's simplicity
theorem for selfless tracial algebras makes `G_Gamma` C-star-simple.
