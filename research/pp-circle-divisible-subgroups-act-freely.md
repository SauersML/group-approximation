## Setting

`S^1` is oriented and `Homeo_+(S^1)` is its orientation-preserving group. Let
`Λ` be one of the following.

- **(P)** A subgroup of `PSL_2(R)`, acting on `S^1 = RP^1`.
- **(L)** A subgroup of `Aff_+(R) = {x -> ax + b : a > 0}` containing
  `x -> x + 1`, acting on the universal cover `R` of `S^1 = R/Z`.

A homeomorphism `g ∈ Homeo_+(S^1)` is **Λ-piecewise** if there is a finite set
`B_g ⊂ S^1` with the following property. On the closure of each component of
`S^1 \ B_g`, `g` agrees with an element of `Λ` in case (P). In case (L), a lift
of `g` to `R` agrees with an element of `Λ` on each lift of such a closed arc.
The Λ-piecewise homeomorphisms form a group `PP_Λ(S^1)`.

**Hypothesis (D).** `Λ` has no nontrivial divisible abelian subgroup.

(D) holds for every finitely generated `Λ`. Such a `Λ` is linear, because
`PSL_2(R)` embeds in `GL_3(R)` by the adjoint representation and `Aff(R)` in
`GL_2(R)`. So it is residually finite (Mal'cev). A residually finite group has no
nontrivial divisible subgroup, because a divisible group has no nontrivial finite
quotient. (D) also holds for `PSL_2(Z[1/N])`, `PSL_2(O[1/N])` with `O` a ring of
algebraic integers, and `Aff_+(R) ∩ Aff(Z[1/N])`.

**Finitely generated hosts.** Let `Γ ≤ PP_{PSL_2(R)}(S^1)` or
`Γ ≤ PP_{Aff_+(R)}(S^1)` be generated by `γ_1, ..., γ_k`, and let `Λ_0` be the
group generated by the pieces of the `γ_i`, together with `x -> x + 1` in case
(L). On a small arc a piece of a product is the product of pieces, so every
element of `Γ` is `Λ_0`-piecewise. `Λ_0` is finitely generated, so it satisfies
(D). So the statement applies to **every finitely generated group of piecewise
`PSL_2(R)` circle homeomorphisms, and every finitely generated group of PL
circle homeomorphisms**, each with finitely many breakpoints per element.

## Statement

Let `Λ` satisfy (D), let `Γ ≤ PP_Λ(S^1)`, and let `D ≤ Γ` be a divisible abelian
subgroup.

1. **Freeness.** Every nontrivial element of `D` acts on `S^1` without fixed
   points.
2. **Rotation number.** The rotation number `rot : D -> R/Z` is an injective
   homomorphism.
3. **Normalizers.** `N_Γ(D) = C_Γ(D)`: an element of `Γ` normalizing `D` acts
   trivially on it by conjugation.
4. **Amenable subgroups.** Let `A ≤ Γ` be amenable, for instance solvable. If
   `D ≤ [A, A]`, then `D = 1`.
5. **Rational rotation numbers.** If every element of `Γ` has rational rotation
   number, then `D` embeds in `Q/Z`. In particular `D` is a torsion group and
   `Γ` contains no subgroup isomorphic to `(Q,+)`.

Case (5) applies, for example, to Thompson's group `T` (Ghys--Sergiescu), and to
PL circle homeomorphisms with rational breakpoints whose slopes are powers of a
fixed integer. For the latter, Calegari arXiv:math/0609573 (abstract) proves that
the rotation numbers are rational and says the proof "generalizes to PL
homeomorphism of the circle with rational break points and derivatives which are
powers of some fixed integer".

## Sharpness and scope

- **Divisible subgroups do occur.** They act freely. The image in `T` of the
  copy of `(Q,+)` in `T-bar` is a nontrivial divisible abelian subgroup of `T`
  (Belk--Hyde--Matucci, *Embedding Q into a finitely presented group*, Bull.
  AMS 59 (2022)). Item 1 is about fixed points, not about existence.
- **BS-type relations are allowed.** `BS(1,2)` embeds in `PSL_2(R)` and in
  Lodha's finitely presented simple group `S`
  (`piecewise-analytic-circle-square-conjugate-has-parabolic-ends`). So hosts
  of this type can have exponentially distorted cyclic subgroups, and the
  distortion obstruction O4 of `gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group` does not
  apply to them. Item 3 forbids only a divisible `D` carrying a nontrivial
  normalizer action.
- **Where the hypotheses are used.** (D) is used only for the germ group at one
  common fixed point of `D`, and finitely many breakpoints per element only to
  get finitely many gaps. (D) cannot be dropped: `PSL_2(R)` itself contains
  `Aff_+(Q)`, whose translation subgroup is divisible and has fixed points.
- **Not covered.** Infinitely many breakpoints, where (D) fails for the
  germ groups; higher-dimensional piecewise-projective groups; and Cantor-set
  hosts.

## Consequence

`pp-circle-and-lift-hosts-miss-gl-n-q`: neither these circle groups nor their
lifts to `R` contain `GL_n(Q)`, `SL_n(Q)`, `PSL_n(Q)` (`n >= 2`), `Aff(Q)`,
`B_2(Q)` or `U_3(Q)`.
