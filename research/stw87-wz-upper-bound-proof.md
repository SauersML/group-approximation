This is Winter--Zacharias, arXiv:0903.4914, Theorem 8.5.  An asymptotic-dimension
cover with `n+1` colors is converted into a completely positive approximation
whose return map is the sum of `n+1` order-zero maps.  Their statement applies
to discrete bounded-geometry metric spaces and gives exactly the displayed
inequality.
