Citation import. D. Farley and L. Sabalka, *Discrete Morse theory and graph
braid groups*, arXiv:math/0410539, Algebr. Geom. Topol. 5 (2005). Corollary
4.2, verbatim, credited there to Ghrist [14]: "If Γ is a radial tree – i.e.
has exactly one essential vertex, v – then BnΓ is free of rank
Σ_{i=2}^{d(v)−1} ( n+d(v)−2 choose n−1 ) − ( n+d(v)−i−1 choose n−1 )."

Read from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-13. Independent check through Gal's Euler
characteristic formula at `d = 3, 4` and `n = 2, 3`: artifact part 3.
