**ESTABLISHED QUANTIFIER FIREWALL.**  The current statement of
`binary-leavitt-native-two-scale-cross-gram-positive` begins with a
canonical-trace binary-Leavitt Steinberg microstate.  In particular, for
each fixed nonidentity packet word `g`, it may use

```text
tr(U_n(g)) -> 0.                                           (CMP1)
```

By contrast, `torsion-normal-generator-has-full-support-corona-core` starts
with an arbitrary homomorphism to a norm matrix corona.  Reblocking a
surviving finite-order mark produces operator-norm asymptotic
representations with a positive-density marked spectral projection, but it
does not change their normalized character into the canonical group
character.  Operator-norm defect implies normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt defect,
yet it gives no estimate of the moments in `(CMP1)`.

This distinction is already visible on every fixed finite packet.  An exact
finite-group representation can concentrate on one irreducible type, while
the canonical trace has the regular Plancherel multiplicities.  Amplifying,
compressing to an invariant active summand, or passing to a subsequence
preserves the chosen normalized character; none of those operations makes it
regular.  For the center-chain subgroup this means that the canonical value

```text
tau(e_chi)=1/64                                            (CMP2)
```

and the ensuing Hecke support fractions are not consequences of small
operator-norm relator defect on an arbitrary active core.

Therefore the route
`two-scale-cross-gram-implies-shared-gauge-compiler` has a missing premise.
Its input proves the cross-Gram estimate only for canonical-trace
microstates, whereas its target compiler is consumed by
`property-t-free-leavitt-via-two-shared-gauge-rows` on every surviving
operator-norm corona profile.  The formal implication

```text
operator-norm defect -> normalized-HS defect
```

does not repair the absent trace-moment hypothesis.

Closing the canonical native cross-Gram claim can still serve the separate
non-hyperlinear lane.  To recover the Property-`(T)`-free MF route one needs
either an operator-norm native cross-Gram theorem quantified over arbitrary
active trace profiles, or a new intrinsic argument forcing the relevant
finite-packet profile from the surviving mark.  No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan
projection, or literature theorem is used in this scope audit.
