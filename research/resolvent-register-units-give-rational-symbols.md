**OPEN (2026-09-18, lane gq-k2-q).** Computation done by hand under hypothesis (P) below, which is not proved for
these rings.

## Setting

A *register completion* `R` is generated by:
- a coefficient ring `B` of functions of registers on a set of states (`N`, `L`, `Δ`, ...), including resolvents
  such as `(N+1)^(-1)`;
- letters `s_x, t_x` with Cuntz–Pimsner relations and substitutions `σ_x`, as in
  `leavitt-resolvent-ring-is-fp-simple-of-char-zero` (`R_L`) and
  `capacity-chain-resolvent-ring-has-rational-k0` (`R_cap`).

Let `B⁺` be the `σ`-closure of `B`. Its units include the *register units* `N + c` (and `L + c`, `Δ − j`, ...),
corrected at the finitely many states where they vanish. For each tail of the state space, let `T` be the lattice
of these unit classes modulo locally constant units, and let `D = 1 − [X]` be the induced map on `T`.

**(P)** `coker(1 − [X] : K_n(B⁺) -> K_n(B⁺))` injects into `K_n(R)` for `n = 1, 2`. This is the Pimsner sequence
that ABC Theorem 3.6 (`abc-twisted-laurent-k-theory-fibration`) gives for a corner skew Laurent ring whose Nil
terms vanish.

## Statement

Assume (P), and that tame residues identify the tail part of `K_2(B⁺)` with `Q^x ⊗ T`, compatibly with `[X]`.
If `coker(D) ≠ 0`, then:
- the symbols `{λ, w}`, with `λ ∈ Q^x` and `w` a register unit, generate a subgroup of `K_2(R)` with quotient
  `Q^x ⊗ coker(D)`, which is not finitely generated;
- hence `E_N(R)` is not finitely presented for any `N >= 3`, by `infinitely-generated-symbols-block-fp-elementary-groups`.

## Computed instances (artifact §2)

- **`R_L`.** `D[N+c] = [N+c] − [N+c+1]` for `c >= 1`, so `coker(D) ≅ Z`. `R_L` is already excluded as a host by
  its elementary scalars.
- **`R_cap`, variant without `a_u`.**
  - `coker(D) ≅ Z^(Z) ⊕ Z`, free on the classes `[N+c]_v` for `c ∈ Z` and on `[Δ]` at infinity.
  - The `(v, u-corner)` block alone has `det(1 − [X]) = −t^(−1)`, a unit in `Z[t^(±1)]`, where `t` shifts `c`.
  - What leaves the free part is the infinite chain of row ends `Δ = j`: `c` sends row `j` to row `j+1`, so
    `D r_j = r_j − r_(j+1)` consumes one relation per row and gives none back.

## Design criterion

`coker(D) = 0` is needed.
- For finitely many tail types this means that `1 − [X]_T(t)` is invertible over `Z[t^(±1)]`. A
  register-preserving loop letter at each register type, together with one shift, makes the diagonal a unit.
- Infinite families of tail types, such as rows, must chain in the direction that terminates. For example, a
  capacity letter that sends row `j` to row `j−1`, with row `0` fed from another type, leaves no free class.
