**Import, quoted verbatim** from Guentner–Tessera–Yu, *Discrete groups with finite decomposition complexity*, Groups Geom. Dyn. 7 (2013) 377–402, doi 10.4171/GGD/186, p. 378. The quotations were read at source by sk-exact-gx and recorded in `research/artifacts/sk-exact-gx-2026-09-13-part1.md` §2.
- "The collection of countable groups having finite decomposition complexity contains all countable subgroups of GL(n,R), where R is any commutative ring, all countable subgroups of an almost connected Lie group, all hyperbolic groups and all elementary amenable groups."
- "Countable FDC groups have Property A."

**Use.** For a finitely generated group, Property A is equivalent to exactness (Guentner–Kaminker; Ozawa). `F_q[t^{±1}]`, and finite products of copies of it, are commutative, and `M_p(S) ⊆ M_{np}`-blocks turn `GL_n(M_p(S))` into `GL_{np}(S)`. So finitely generated subgroups of `GL_n(M_p(F_q[t^{±1}]) × M_{p′}(F_q[t^{±1}]))` are exact.
