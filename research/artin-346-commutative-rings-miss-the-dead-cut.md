title: "No commutative coefficient ring, and no finite abelian twist, detects the dead character (1,-1,-1) of A(3,4,6)"
---

Let `A = A(3,4,6)`, `χ = (1, -1, -1)` on `(a, b, c)`, with `l(ab) = 4`, `l(ac) = 6`,
`l(bc) = 3`.

1. The only vertex partition whose cross edges are all dead, with a live vertex on
   each side, is `{a} | {b, c}`: any other partition crosses the odd edge `bc`.
2. For every commutative ring `R` and every homomorphism `ρ : A -> R^×` with
   `1 + ρ(ab) = 0` and `1 + ρ(ac) + ρ(ac)^2 = 0`, we have `R = 0`. Indeed `b` and `c` are
   conjugate (`(bc) b (bc)^{-1} = c`), so `ρ(b) = ρ(c)` and `z := ρ(ab) = ρ(ac)`; then
   `1 = (1 + z + z^2) - z(1 + z) = 0`.
3. So the hypothesis of Escartín-Ferrer's Theorem 4.4 (arXiv:2501.08692v3) fails for
   every finite abelian `G`. A character `μ : G -> C^×` would need `μ(φ(ab)) = -1` and
   `μ(φ(ab)) = μ(φ(ac))` to be a nontrivial cube root of unity. Equivalently, the ideal
   generated by `1 + g` and `1 + g + g^2`, `g = φ(ab)`, is the unit ideal, and their
   Lemma 4.1 gives finite-dimensional `H_1(ker ψ; F)`.

So finite abelian twists cannot prove the Σ¹-conjecture in general, even in circuit
rank 1, where Almeida–Kochloukova proved it by other means.
`artin-346-dead-character-outside-sigma1-by-rank-two-rep` detects this character
with a non-commutative ring.
