Let `G` be a countable nonamenable group and let

```text
alpha:G acts on A
```

be an amenable action on a unital separable nuclear C-star algebra with
`T(A)` nonempty.  There is no nonempty weak-star compact `G`-invariant
subset `K` of `T(A)` for which the restricted family

```text
{alpha_g^*:K->K : g in G}
```

is equicontinuous.

Consequently the trace dynamics of every proposed witness for STW Problem
XL have all of the following properties:

1. no trace has a finite orbit;
2. no nonempty invariant compact trace subsystem factors continuously
   through a compact group;
3. every nonempty invariant compact trace subsystem contains a uniform
   failure of equicontinuity.

Thus replacing the finite-extremal-boundary obstruction merely by an
infinite compact rotation or a profinite trace permutation cannot work.
The trace action must carry genuinely non-equicontinuous boundary-type
dynamics.
