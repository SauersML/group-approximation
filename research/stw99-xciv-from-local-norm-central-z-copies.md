Apply the recursive construction in
`stw99-xciv-local-norm-central-z-equivalence-proof` with `B=R`.  Starting from
the C\*-algebra generated by an arbitrary norm-separable subset, adjoin one
approximately central copy of `Z` at each stage and include all earlier
ranges in later finite tests.  Their diagonal defines a unital map into the
norm central sequence algebra of the resulting separable hull, so the
strongly-self-absorbing absorption criterion makes that hull `Z`-stable.
