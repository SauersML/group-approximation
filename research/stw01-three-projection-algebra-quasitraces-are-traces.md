Every bounded 2-quasitrace on `P_3 = C^2 * C^2 * C^2`, the universal unital
C*-algebra of three projections (equivalently `C*(Z_2 * Z_2 * Z_2)`), is a trace.

This statement is equivalent to STW Problem I. The implication into the root is
`stw01-problem-i-via-three-projection-algebra`, and its proof is in
`research/artifacts/stw01-three-projection-universal-test-2026-09-12.md`.

The number of projections is sharp. The two-projection algebra `C*(D_infinity)` is
nuclear, so Haagerup's theorem makes all its quasitraces traces. Hence **Kaplansky's
quasitrace problem is exactly the three-projection problem**: any counterexample
can be taken to be a C*-algebra generated by three projections.

The proof also isolates where non-additivity must appear. In a type `II_1`
AW*-factor the quasitrace is linear iff `tau(y + q) = tau(y) + tau(q)` for every
finite-spectrum `y >= 0` and every projection `q`.

## Attempts

- **Exactness (Haagerup).** Dead: `P_3` is the full algebra of the virtually free
  group `Z_2 * Z_2 * Z_2`, which contains `F_2`, so `P_3` is not exact.
- **Two projections.** Dead as a host, because the algebra is nuclear. So the
  counterexample needs the third projection `q` to meet the dilation pair `p, p'`
  in a non-exact configuration.
- **Haagerup property or amenable actions of `Z_2 * Z_2 * Z_2`.** Dead: these give
  exactness only of the reduced algebra (the group is exact), not of the full
  algebra `P_3`. The universal test sits on the full side, as for `C*(F_2)`.
- **Reduce to projections of equal trace, or to a free triple.** Not attempted to
  a conclusion. The dilation in the proof forces `p' = [[A, S], [S, P - A]]`,
  whose trace is `tau(P)`, but `q` stays arbitrary. No normalisation of `q`
  preserving the defect is known.
