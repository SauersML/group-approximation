Let `D` be a finite maximal binary prefix code, let
`F<=Sym(D)<=V` be a finite subgroup acting transitively on `D), and
fix one leaf `d_0 in D`.  Let `h` be any nonidentity element of
Thompson's group supported inside the cylinder `[d_0]`; thus `h` is an
off-code refinement actor, not a permutation of the leaves of `D`.
If `m=ord(h)`, with `C_infinity=Z`, then

```text
<F,h> isomorphic to C_m^D semidirect F,                 (SOB1)
```

where `F` permutes the `|D|` cyclic coordinates through its action on
the leaves.

In particular `<F,h>` is finite when `h` is torsion and virtually
free abelian when `h` has infinite order.  It is always amenable and
hyperlinear.

Apply this to the order-`3^5` extraspecial subgroup on the 27-cylinder
code in `thompson-27-cylinder-qutrit-cell-is-fd-invisible`.  The two
distinct packets

```text
F,                         h F h^(-1)                  (SOB2)
```

and every projection, corner coefficient, or overlap word formed from them
lie in `C[<F,h>]`.  Therefore **no** proposed bridge supported by these
words can complete a no-CE algebraic atlas, even if the bridge is a genuine
additive multi-path equality rather than conjugacy, flat Morita transport, or
a same-code permutation.  Finite-support localization would make
`<F,h>` nonhyperlinear, contradicting `(SOB1)`.

The same no-go holds after the cylinder-swap embedding into
`EL_4(L_(F_2)(1,2))` and after the injective Thompson lift into
`St_n(L_(F_2)(1,2))`, `n>=5`: the generated subgroup is still an
isomorphic copy of `(SOB1)`.

Thus the first load-bearing off-code candidate cannot use only one localized
prefix actor.  It must use at least two tail actors whose one-leaf subgroup
has already left the cyclic/amenable basin, or one genuinely cross-leaf
support word which is not contained in the finite wreath packet above.  In a
Steinberg host, using merely the lift of a localized Thompson element is
equally insufficient; a candidate must use a genuinely non-Thompson
Steinberg root or a mixed coefficient return.
