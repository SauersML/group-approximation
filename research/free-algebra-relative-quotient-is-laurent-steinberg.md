**ESTABLISHED** (proof route `free-algebra-relative-quotient-is-laurent-steinberg-proof`).

Let `A` be `Z` or `F_p`, `k >= 3`, `S = A<x_1, ..., x_k>`, and `n >= 3`. Let `q` be a power of `p`
when `A = F_p`, and any prime power `q = p^f` when `A = Z`. Fix `θ` with `F_q = F_p(θ)`, and let
`f ∈ Z[x]` be a monic lift of its minimal polynomial over `F_p`. Let `I` be the two-sided ideal
generated by

```text
x_1 x_2 - 1,  x_2 x_1 - 1,  x_1 x_3 - x_3 x_1,  x_2 x_3 - x_3 x_2,  f(x_3),
x_j (4 <= j <= k),  and p when A = Z.
```

Then `S/I ≅ F_q[t,t^-1]` via `x_1 -> t`, `x_2 -> t^-1`, `x_3 -> θ`. Moreover

```text
Q_I = E_n(S) / E_n(S,I)  ≅  St_n(F_q[t,t^-1]).
```

In words, the image of `K_2(n,S)` in `St_n(S/I)` is trivial.

**Use.** With `relative-elementary-quotients-inherit-flexible-hs-stability`, flexible HS stability of
`E_n(S)` implies flexible HS stability of `St_n(F_q[t,t^-1])`. That group is not residually finite
for `q >= 3` (`laurent-steinberg-groups-are-deligne-type`).
