## Setting

- `G = F_n ⋊_φ ⟨y⟩` with `y u y⁻¹ = φ(u)`, and `ε: G → Z` the exponent of `y`.
- A marked graph `Γ` with `π_1 Γ = F_n` and a topological representative `f: Γ → Γ` of `φ`.
  - `M_f` has one vertical 1-cell per vertex of `Γ`, the horizontal 1-cells of `Γ`, and one 2-cell `σ_e`
    per edge `e`, with boundary `e`, vertical, `f(e)`, vertical.
- `H ≤ G` is f.g. with `ε(H) ≠ 0`, and `N = H ∩ F_n`.
- For (C2): `n = 4`, `F_4 = ⟨p,q,r,s⟩`, `φ: p→q→r→s→rrSqPRs`, and `H = ⟨a,…,e | u0, u1⟩` with
  `χ(H) = −2`, as in `c2-fibred-realizations-reduce-to-a-primitive-class`.
- A **free-factor level splitting (FFLS)** of `H` is a finite graph of groups `𝔾` on a graph `Δ`, with a
  morphism to `G`, such that:
  - each vertex group `V_v` is a f.g. subgroup of `N`;
  - each edge `j` carries an element `t_j ∈ G` with `ε(t_j) = 1`, and `E_j ≤ V_{ι j}` with
    `t_j E_j t_j⁻¹ ≤ V_{τ j}`;
  - the induced map `π_1 𝔾 → G` is injective with image `H`;
  - `{E_j}` is a free factor system of `F_n` up to conjugacy in `F_n`. Trivial `E_j` are allowed.

## Statement

**Theorem A.** For every marked graph `Γ` and every representative `f`, these are equivalent:
- `M_f` carries a 2-cell-injective combinatorial map from a finite 2-dim `K(H,1)` inducing `H ≤ G`.
  1-cells may fold.
- `H` has an FFLS.

Moreover, if `H` has an FFLS, then the rose on any basis adapted to its free factor system realizes it.

**Corollaries.**
- (A1) Realizability of `H` in the mapping tori of `φ` depends on neither `Γ` nor `f`. If no rose
  realizes `H`, then no marked graph does.
- (A2) Kurosh. In an FFLS each `E_j` is a free factor of `V_{ι j}`, and `t_j E_j t_j⁻¹` is a free factor
  of `V_{τ j}`.
- (A3) Rank budget. `Σ_v rk V_v = R_E + 1 − χ(H) − b_1(Δ)`, where `R_E = Σ rk E_j ≤ n` and
  `b_1(Δ) ≥ 1`. For (C2): `Σ rk V_v = R_E + 3 − b_1(Δ) ≤ 6`.
- (A4) A fixed rose on a basis `𝔅` realizes `H` iff `H` has an FFLS whose edge groups are, up to
  conjugacy, `π_1` of pairwise edge-disjoint immersed subgraphs of that rose.
  - The w16 gluing-rank test is a necessary condition for this.
  - The 7 stalled `w2 = a` bases of w16 (S5) are the instances of (A4) for those `𝔅`.
- (A5) The necessity half contains (R1)/(P_free). The class `c_H` is carried by the free factor
  generated by the edge groups.

**Computation for (C2)** (`experiments/c2-level-splitting-2026-09-17`).
- (C1) With `a` eliminated through `u0`, `H = ⟨b,c,d,e | r⟩` is one-relator, where
  `r = eDDEdd·bCB·eDDEdd·bDcBd`. `ε = (b,c,d,e) ↦ (3,0,1,1)`. Both relators and `a` are checked in `G`.
- (C2) Whitehead search over the one-relator presentations, with stable letter `d`.
  - The Moldavanskii level width (the edge-group rank of the induced one-vertex HNN splitting) starts
    at 4. It falls to 3 after one move and stays at 3 through 104233 states (depth 6, beam).
  - For each of the 48 width-3 presentations reachable at depth 2 with length at most 22, the edge group
    `A ≤ F_4` has rank 3 and is not a free factor. Its minimal Whitehead core has 8 edges, not 3.
- (C3) The width-3 splitting `r' = eDDEdd·CB·eDDEdd·bDcd` (`c' = cB`) has these edges and edge groups:
  - `e_{-2} = pQ`, `e_{-1} = qR`, `e_0 = rS`, `c'_2 = SrpQsRRsq`;
  - edge group `A = ⟨e_{-2}, e_{-1}, c'_2⟩`.

  Here `⟨e_{-2},e_{-1}⟩` and `⟨e_{-2},e_{-1},e_0⟩` are free factors and `c'_2` is primitive. Even so,
  neither `{⟨e_{-2},e_{-1}⟩, ⟨c'_2⟩}` nor `{⟨e_{-2},e_{-1},e_0⟩, ⟨c'_2⟩}` is a free factor system.
  So the natural two-edge refinement of this splitting is not an FFLS either.

## Consequences for the lane

- The w17 step "prove the gluing-rank bound for every rose and marked graph that passes T0" is
  equivalent, by Theorem A, to: **(C2) has no FFLS.** This is a strict reduction. It replaces the
  unbounded family of complexes with one splitting question inside `H`, bounded by (A3).
- The 7 stalled bases need no low-index search. Each is decided by (A4). A negative answer for all FFLS
  settles all 7 at once, together with every other rose and marked graph.
- The class of one-vertex, one-edge FFLS coming from width-3 level rewritings is excluded by (C2).
  Twisted-Alexander and `L²` bounds cannot exclude FFLS in general, because genuine rank-3 HNN splittings
  of `H` over `N`-subgroups exist. Any obstruction must use free-factor-ness in `F_4`.
- Slide complexes (`c2-slide-route-passes-every-relation-module-test`) are not mapping tori of `φ`, so
  Theorem A does not decide them. The analogue of Theorem A for them is the next open item.
