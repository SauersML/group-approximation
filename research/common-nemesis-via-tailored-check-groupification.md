Take the finite family `E_c`.  Clause 1 of the transducer is common HALT
completeness.  For a marked canonical CE base type, clauses 2--3 say that
extension through every `E_c` would give one CE strategy satisfying every
tailored-game check.  This contradicts `(TCN1)`.  Hence at least one gadget
is a nemesis for every marked extension type, exactly as required by the
compiler.
