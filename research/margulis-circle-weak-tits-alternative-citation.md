Imports one classical theorem.
- G. Margulis, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris Sér. I Math. 331 (2000) 669–674: a subgroup of
  `Homeo_+(S^1)` either preserves a probability measure on `S^1` or contains a free subgroup on
  two generators.
- The statement is quoted verbatim from A. Navas, *Groups of circle diffeomorphisms*,
  arXiv:math/0607481, `navas.tex` l.5575–5578 (theorem `margulis`), with the attribution at
  l.5567–5569 and the bibliography entry at l.18858–18859. Lane `swarm-0917-w9-w9-z-pull`
  fetched the source on 2026-09-17.

Scope: the theorem is stated for every subgroup, with no countability or regularity
hypothesis. The lane did not re-read Margulis's original note or Ghys's proof as presented
by Navas.
