**ESTABLISHED PRIVATE-ARM A3 FENCE.**  Retain the literal coefficients and
root words

```text
alpha=t_00,       beta=s_00t_01,       gamma=s_01,
A=x_(7,10)(alpha), B=x_(10,11)(beta),
C=x_(11,8)(gamma), G=x_(10,8)(gamma),                 (APW1)
```

from `depth-two-a3-return-is-gauged-external-root`.  Their genuine opposite
coefficients are

```text
alpha^-=s_00,       beta^-=s_01t_00,       gamma^-=t_01. (APW2)
```

They satisfy the partial-inverse rows

```text
alpha alpha^-=1,          alpha^- alpha=e_00,
beta beta^-=e_00,         beta^- beta=e_01,
gamma^- gamma=1,          gamma gamma^-=e_01.          (APW3)
```

Attach the actual reverse-root occurrences and partial Whitehead words

```text
A^-=x_(10,7)(alpha^-),       W_A=A A^- A,
B^-=x_(11,10)(beta^-),       W_B=B B^- B,
G^-=x_(8,10)(gamma^-),       W_G=G G^- G.              (APW4)
```

Thus each arm occurs inside its opposite-root word before either nested A3
commutator is collapsed to `x_78(1)`.  Include the ordinary incidence-zero
rows among these named roots and retain the A3 table

```text
[[A,B],C]=P,              [A,[B,C]]=P,
[A,G]=1,                  P=x_78(1),
H=[C,G^-],                [H,G]=C.                    (APW5)
```

This complete displayed occurrence packet has an exact finite model.  In
`GL_6(F_2)` put

```text
A=x_12(1), B=x_23(1), C=x_36(1), P=x_16(1),
G=x_56(1),
A^-=x_21(1), B^-=x_32(1), G^-=x_65(1).                (APW6)
```

The chain `1->2->3->6` gives the standard `A_3` unitriangular path, while
`G=x_56` retains the cross-zero shortcut without specializing it to the
identity.  Moreover `H=x_35`, so the nonzero interaction of the `G`
opposite with the `C` arm and its return to `C` are retained rather than
killed.  Direct multiplication gives `(APW5)` and all required nonincident
commutations.  For every `X in {A,B,G}`,

```text
W_X^2=1,        W_X X W_X^(-1)=X^-,
W_X X^- W_X^(-1)=X.                                   (APW7)
```

The same model may include the output opposite root `P^-=x_61(1)` and its
Whitehead `W_P=P P^- P`; its analogous rows are exact.  Nevertheless

```text
P=x_16(1)!=1.                                          (APW8)
```

Hence the left regular representation of the finite subgroup generated by
the packet keeps every displayed relation exact while

```text
||lambda(P)-1||_2=sqrt(2).                             (APW9)
```

This is an occurrence model, not a finite coefficient-algebra
specialization: `(APW3)` together with `alpha beta gamma=1` and
`alpha gamma=0` cannot hold nontrivially in a finite-dimensional unital
coefficient algebra.  The finite escape instead separates the marked A3
path and the zero shortcut into compatible finite root-role blocks while
sharing every named arm with its own opposite cell.

Consequently an individual private-arm Whitehead adds no forced reservoir
identity.  Its rank-two packet can close finitely before the total returned
word is restricted to the signed Hecke carrier, where the only established
equation remains

```text
E'=D E D^(-1).                                         (APW10)
```

A successful refinement must make one opposite cell interact
nontrivially with another A3 channel on the returned Hecke carrier, rather
than attach separate partial inverses to individual arms.  No Property
`(T)`, Kazhdan input, trace hypothesis, literature theorem, local
computation, local build, or Lean compilation is used.
