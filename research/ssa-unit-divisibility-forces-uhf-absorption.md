Let `D` be a strongly self-absorbing C\*-algebra and `p` a prime with
`[1_D] ∈ p·K_0(D)`.  Then `D ≅ D ⊗ M_{p^∞}`.  Consequently if
`[1_D]` is divisible by every prime in a set `J`, then
`D ≅ D ⊗ M_{J^∞}`, and if divisible by all primes, `D ≅ D ⊗ Q`.

Two auxiliary facts proved en route, both via the tensor-shift
endomorphism of `D ≅ D^{⊗∞}`:

* (tail-centrality) every unital subalgebra `B ⊆ D` admits unital
  copies in `D_ω ∩ D'`;
* (central upgrade) for ANY strongly self-absorbing `E`, the
  block-shift defines a unital *-homomorphism `E_ω → E_ω ∩ E'`, so a
  unital embedding `D → E_ω` yields one into `E_ω ∩ E'`; hence
  `E ≅ E ⊗ D` whenever `D` unitally embeds into `E_ω`.
