title: The subdivided Rips complex of a hyperbolic group is a finite model for proper actions
---

Let `G` be `δ`-hyperbolic with respect to a finite symmetric generating set `S`, let
`d >= 32δ + 20`, and let `P` be the second barycentric subdivision of the Rips complex
`P_d(G,S)`. Then `P` is a finite `G`-CW model for the classifying space
`\underline{E}G` for proper actions:

- `P` has finitely many `G`-orbits of cells, and a cell mapped to itself is fixed
  pointwise;
- all stabilizers are finite;
- `P^H` is contractible, in particular nonempty, for every finite subgroup `H`.

Consequences used in the graph: a hyperbolic group has a finite-dimensional
contractible proper cocompact `G`-CW complex in which every finite subgroup fixes a
vertex, so it has finitely many conjugacy classes of finite subgroups, and the
orders of its finite subgroups are bounded. This is the input to
`chern-quillen-class-detects-all-prime-order-torsion` for hyperbolic groups.
