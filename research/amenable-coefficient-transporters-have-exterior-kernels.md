For g=diag(a,b) with a,b in GL_2(F_2[Lambda]), acting on the regular
coefficient module and with the constant GL_2(F_4) chart, amenability
of Lambda forces a nonzero kernel of P_0[g]f_0 on the complete
exterior-square source. Thus the reduced left-inverse identity is
impossible even with unrestricted ambient group-algebra support.

More quantitatively, if an m-dimensional tail space has all eight
character-block images in an n-dimensional space, the kernel dimension
is at least 2m^2-n(n-1). No invariant finite-dimensional tail space
is required. For Laurent coefficients in any finite number of
variables, the artifact gives an explicit finite tensor certificate.
