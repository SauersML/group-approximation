Let `A` be finitely generated and virtually free, and let `M` be the dual
module of a finite-type finite-field linear subshift over `A`.  For every
`0!=m_0 in M` there is a finite `A`-orbit character `chi in M^` with

```text
chi(m_0)!=1.                                             (VFSM1)
```

In the binary case, the Pauli-seed extension at `m_0` consequently has an
exact finite-dimensional representation with `J=-I`.

Thus the most direct way to defeat actor word holonomy does not close
`infinite-character-actor-word-energy-interface`.  A free actor gives exact
reduced-word conjugacy names, and a virtually free actor retains finite-state
normal forms, but every finite-type linear module over such an actor is
residually finite.  Its self-similar relations therefore create a finite
spectral packet retaining the mark rather than an aperiodic infinite
character backend.

Any successful finite-presentation actor/module construction must instead
put its marked seed in the module finite residual `(FCRD1)`.  In particular,
ordinary residually finite algebraic self-similarity cannot turn restriction
collisions into the desired dimension-independent word energy.
