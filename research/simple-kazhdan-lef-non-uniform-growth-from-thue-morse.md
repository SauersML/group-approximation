The Thue–Morse substitution `0↦01, 1↦10` is primitive, of constant length 2, and its fixed point is not periodic. So `substitution-subshift-elementary-groups-non-uniform-growth` applies. `G_X` is infinite, finitely generated, simple, Kazhdan and LEF, and has non-uniform exponential growth.
