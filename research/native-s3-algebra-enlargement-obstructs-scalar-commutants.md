Use the native rank-three subgroup `L` of the binary Leavitt group
`H`, its compressor `u`, and the two fourth-coordinate involutions

```text
k_+=diag(1,1,1,1+a t^3),
k_-=diag(1,1,1,1+s^3 b).
```

They generate a fixed `S_3` inside the complementary subgroup `K`.
Moreover `H=<L,u<k_+,k_->u^(-1)>`.

Suppose a full canonical matrix microstate sequence of `H` admits
unital finite-dimensional algebras `A_n` satisfying these three conditions:

1. The sum, over a fixed finite generating list of `L`, of the Haar
   averages of squared commutators with `U(A_n)` tends to zero.
2. Both fixed matrices representing `k_+,k_-` have normalized-HS
   distance tending to zero from `A_n`.
3. The Haar average over `v in U(A_n)` of
   `||v-E_(U_n A_n U_n*)(v)||_2^2` tends to zero.

Then the full external `H`-commutant in its tracial matrix ultraproduct
contains a self-adjoint element with trace zero, operator norm at most
one, and normalized-HS norm at least `1/8`. In particular that commutant
is not scalar. Centrality in the full commutant is not asserted.

This uses no approximation of the whole native heat map. Constructing
the stated enlargement remains open. The algebra generated by an exactified
canonical `S_3` alone has forward Haar leakage at least `1/2-o(1)`;
finite-group repair by itself cannot meet condition 3.

A separate global-rounding argument would need to supply a canonical
sequence with scalar full commutant. For such a sequence, the stated
finite-observable enlargement conditions suffice for contradiction.
This claim neither proves that selection nor constructs the enlargement;
no nonhyperlinearity conclusion is asserted. This is a written proof,
not newly Lean-verified.
