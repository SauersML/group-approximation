For every `lambda in Lambda`, the literal two-compressor core satisfies

```text
tau_0 lambda tau_0^(-1)
 = (tau_0^2 lambda tau_0^(-2))
   (tau_1 tau_0 lambda tau_0^(-1) tau_1^(-1)).         (DTS1)
```

This is the depth-two identity
`Psi_0(lambda)=Psi_0^2(lambda) Psi_1Psi_0(lambda)`.  The canonical marked
model on `H x H` used for the one-level covariance table violates `(DTS1)`
for every nontrivial involution `lambda`; its coordinatewise amplifications
do not repair the defect.

Thus growing the finite diagonal stage in that model cannot approximate the
literal group.  The next MF construction or obstruction must incorporate
recursive child splitting, not only both depth-one children.
