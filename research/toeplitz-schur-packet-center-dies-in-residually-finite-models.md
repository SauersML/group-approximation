**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `T=F_2<x,y | yx=1>`, `f=1+xy`, `e=xy`,
`e_1=xfy=sigma(f)`, and let

```text
iota:B_f -> EL_r(T),        B_f=<P,Q,Z>,   Z=<J,z_1,...,z_k>,
```

be the scaled unitriangular embedding of the Schur--Clifford packet from
`schur-child-crosses-toeplitz-compressor-proof` (every `q`- and
`z`-coordinate is scaled by `e_1`).  Put `I=TfT` and let `L<=EL_r(T)` be the
subgroup generated by the root elements `x_ij(u)`, `i!=j`, `u in I`.  Then:

1. `L` is isomorphic to the finitary group `SL_fin({1,...,r} x N, F_2)`,
   which is infinite and simple;
2. `iota(Q) union iota(Z) <= L`; in particular the packet central sign
   `iota(J)` lies in `L`;
3. every homomorphism `pi` from any group containing `EL_r(T)` to a
   residually finite group (in particular every finite-dimensional
   representation factoring through a finite quotient, and every
   representation factoring through the symbol quotient
   `EL_r(F_2[z,z^(-1)])`) satisfies

   ```text
   pi(iota(Q))=pi(iota(Z))=1.                          (TPC1)
   ```

Consequently, in every such model the packet image is the abelian quotient
`B_f/<Q,Z> = P`, the `J=-1` sector `H_-` of `iota(B_f)` is zero, and the
forbidden Schur mass `q` of `predicate-rank-jump-forces-adjoint-reynolds-gap`
is `0`.  Also `pi(z(1-a))=0` for every forbidden packet projection `z`
supported on the `J=-1` sector.

**Consequence for the recorded fences.**  The finite-quotient countermodels
recorded against `parity-compressor-old-commutant-has-positive-density`
all assume a residually finite base, or take finite quotients of the base
that separate a prescribed finite packet: `finite-index-covariance-retains-coefficient-residual`,
`high-type-irreducibles-retain-finite-packet-mass`,
`rf-hnn-telescope-retains-coefficient-residual`,
`rf-amenable-two-cell-retains-coefficient`,
`profinite-free-compressors-retain-coefficient-residual`, and
`finite-normalizer-covariance-density-no-go`.  For the actual Toeplitz base
`EL_r(T)` these hypotheses fail (`L` lies in the finite residual).  Pushed
through the largest residually finite quotient instead, the countermodels
have `q=0`, and `(PCD1)` says nothing about microstates with `q=0`.  So none
of those fences, as stated, refutes a coefficient cell for the Toeplitz
packet embedding.  The repair, conditional on hyperlinearity of the base,
is `hyperlinear-base-amenable-cells-retain-coefficients`.
