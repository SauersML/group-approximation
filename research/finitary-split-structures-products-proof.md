Section 1 of the artifact. Over `X_1 ⊔ X_2` put `N_S = N_1,S_1 x N_2,S_2` with product projections and
embeddings. Axiom (D) holds because products of residually finite groups are residually finite. Detection
(A1) applies each factor's detection to the nontrivial coordinates. Finite support (A2) takes
`L(a,b) = L_1(a) ⊔ L_2(b)`, and a translate misses `S∖U` iff each coordinate misses its part.
Stabilizer invariance (A3) holds because the pointwise stabilizer of `S_1 ⊔ S_2` is the product of the
pointwise stabilizers. The surjunctivity equivalence is then Theorem 0.
