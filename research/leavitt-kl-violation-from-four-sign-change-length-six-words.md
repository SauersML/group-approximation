The two length-six sign classes `+++-+-` and `++-++-` were left unexamined by
`length-five-kl-fence-for-leavitt-equations`.  The hope was to find a violation
among them using full-support Leavitt coefficients, including the window words
`a_1 t b_1 t^(-1) a_2 t b_2 t^(-1) a_3 t b_3 t`.

Dead generically: `kl-length-six-four-sign-change-words-inject`.  Over every
coefficient group such a word injects unless the quotient of its two same-sign
coefficients has finite order or one sign-change coefficient lies in a cyclic
group generated by that quotient.  Only those coincidence families remain.
