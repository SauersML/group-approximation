Let `A` be an actor as in `tarski-lamp-nonconstant-sector-has-amenable-stabilizers`.
If `A` is sofic, then `defect-controlled-lamp-character-transport` is **false**:
no constants `delta_0, C, C_s` independent of the matrix dimension satisfy
`(DLT1)` and `(DLT2)`.

Consequently the atomic Schreier endpoint is not a property-(T)-free shortcut.
Its hypothesis carries a **non-soficity obligation about the actor itself**,
and the amenability of proper subgroups, which is the only structural input the
gap mechanism uses, is not an obstruction to soficity -- it is the hypothesis
under which the known permanence theorems make the candidate sofic.

The statement is a genuine restriction rather than a triviality: it says the
hole is falsifiable, and it names the one input any proof must consume.
Nothing here decides the soficity of any Tarski monster, which is open, so no
route is invalidated.
