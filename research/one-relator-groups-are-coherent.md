For every one-relator group `G`, every finitely generated subgroup of `G` is
finitely presented.  There is no torsion, hyperbolicity, residual-finiteness,
or primitivity-rank hypothesis.

This settles Baumslag's 1974 coherence conjecture for one-relator groups.  It
does not settle [[one-relator-groups-sofic]]: coherence is a subgroup finiteness
property, not an approximation by finite symmetric groups.
