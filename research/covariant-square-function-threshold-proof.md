Equation `(CSF4)` is the spectral coarea identity in an eigenbasis of `H`;
Cauchy--Schwarz bounds it by the Hilbert--Schmidt covariance defect.  Average
over the threshold interval and combine with Markov's trace bound.  The
cosine--sine decomposition then rounds each unitary to the block-diagonal
unitary group of the same common projection with squared cost controlled by
its commutator with that projection.
