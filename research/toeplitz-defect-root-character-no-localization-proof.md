The faithful unilateral-shift model shows every `e_n` is nonzero. Their
pairwise products vanish, so a finite linear dependence multiplied by the
idempotent of any nonzero coefficient has only that coefficient left; hence
the family is linearly independent. Define an `F_p`-linear functional to be
one on this basis and extend it to an additive basis of `R_f`. Exponentiation
to `mu_p` gives the asserted additive character. No positivity or
multiplicativity condition is present on a root character, so ring
orthogonality imposes no restriction on these simultaneous values.
