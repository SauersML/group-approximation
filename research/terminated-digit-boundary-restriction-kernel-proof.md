Write T={1,...,h}^N. On T the nucleus states restrict to id,a,
and prefix(h)*a^-1. A sufficiently deep table for an element of
K therefore restricts to a table in B=V_(h,r)(<a>) on F. This
defines the restriction homomorphism K->B.

## Binary prefix maps available in the host

The maps on C_X

    A_0(i*z)=(i+1)*z for i<r-1,
    A_0((r-1)*z)=0*I(z),       A_1(z)=0*#*z

are rational injections onto complementary clopen images. They
implement binary prefixing on the dense gap-token space. A binary
prefix table lifts by composing these injections and their inverses
on the appropriate clopen domains. Its recurrent local actions lie
in the nucleus: fixed positive carries reduce to I or identity,
and inverse carries reduce to D or identity. This is the binary-V
part of the previously written digit-lifting proof and is independent
of any input shell permutation.

In particular, any two nonempty finite unions of first-gap token
cones can be matched by the restriction of such a lifted V element.
Indeed their binary counterparts are proper nonempty clopen subsets
of C, and a binary prefix element maps one onto the other.

## Surjectivity of restriction

Take a table in B with integer powers of a on its leaves. Replace
each a^c by the partial terminated-digit translation I^c. For c<0
its domain omits only finitely many terminated tails; for c>=0 its
image omits only finitely many. The finite boundary prefix partition
also misses only finitely many short terminated first-gap words.
The result is a rational bijection between clopen neighborhoods
of F, with the prescribed boundary action and recurrent states in N.
Both omitted complements are finite unions of token cones C_j.

If necessary remove a further matched pair of terminated token
cones from the partial map so that both complements are nonempty.
Such a pair exists in any chart; the map there retains the suffix
after the first #. Match the two complements using a restriction
of a lifted binary V element, as above. Gluing gives a homeomorphism
of C_X whose recurrent local actions still belong to N. It lies
in K and realizes the original B table. Restriction is onto.

## Its kernel is compactly supported off F

Suppose k in K fixes F pointwise. At sufficiently deep input cones
alpha*C_Y along F, its local state lies in {id_Y,I,D}. The output
prefix beta has no #, since the boundary image lies in F.

If the state is I, equality of boundary images forces alpha=beta
because I(T)=T. Pointwise fixing would then make a the identity,
which it is not. If the state is D, equality of images forces
alpha=beta*h. Pointwise fixing would make D(z)=h*z on T, whereas
D(z)=h*a^-1(z), again impossible. The remaining state is id_Y,
with alpha=beta, so k is identity on the whole input cone.

Finitely many such cones cover F. Thus k is identity on a
neighborhood of F. The converse is immediate, giving the asserted
description of J. The support of an element of J is compact in
the union of the token cones, hence lies in D_N=C_0 union ...
union C_N for some N.

## Finite generation

Let t be the lift of the binary V table

    0 -> 00,       10 -> 01,       11 -> 1.

On F it cycles the r roots, applying a at the wrap, so it preserves
F. Directly on the token space, t(C_j)=C_(j+1) for j>=1 and
t(C_0)=C_0 union C_1. Thus t(D_N)=D_(N+1).

The full H subgroup A supported in C_0 is isomorphic to H by the
canonical prefix identification C_0=token(0)*C_X. It is finitely
generated because H is finitely presented. Conjugation gives the
full supported subgroup on D_N as t^N*A*t^-N. Their union is J.

The quotient B is finitely generated by the required odometer
prefix theorem. Adjoin lifts of a finite generating set of B to
A and t. Any element of K differs from a word in those lifts by
an element of J, which the conjugates of A already generate.
This proves finite generation of K without assuming its kernel
is finitely generated or its extension splits.
