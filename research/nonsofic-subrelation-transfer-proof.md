# Proof

Suppose `G ↷ X` is sofic. We derive that `H` is sofic.

1. **The host relation is sofic.** `G ↷ X` is essentially free, so by Păunescu
   Proposition 1.15 (*"Let α be a free action. Then E_α is a sofic equivalence
   relation if and only if α is a sofic action."*; verbatim in
   `research/artifacts/free-nonsofic-action-sources-verified-2026-09-11.md`)
   the orbit relation `R_G` is sofic.
2. **Restriction.** By Cordeiro, arXiv:1608.05608v2, Proposition 3.2(a)
   (*"If R is sofic and A ⊆ X is any (non-null) subset, then R|_A is sofic."*,
   with `R|_A` carrying `μ_A`; verbatim in
   `research/artifacts/paunescu-class-measure-equivalence-2026-09-12.md` §0),
   `R := R_G|_A` is sofic on `(A,μ_A)`.
3. **Subrelation.** Let `S := R_H ⊆ R`. The Feldman--Moore algebra `M(S)` is
   the von Neumann subalgebra of `M(R)` generated by `A_0 = L^∞(A,μ_A)` and the
   unitaries `u_φ`, `φ ∈ [S] ⊆ [R]`. The inclusion preserves the trace, and
   `A_0` is a Cartan subalgebra of both. A unitary of `M(S)` normalizing `A_0`
   is a unitary of `M(R)` normalizing `A_0`, so
   `𝒩_(M(S))(A_0) ⊆ 𝒩_(M(R))(A_0)`. Take an embedding
   `Θ : M(R) → Π_(k→ω) M_(n_k)` with `Θ(A_0) ⊂ Π D_(n_k)` and
   `Θ(𝒩_(M(R))(A_0)) ⊂ 𝒰(Θ(A_0))·Π P_(n_k)` (Păunescu Definition 1.12). Its
   restriction to `M(S)` satisfies both conditions, so `S` is sofic.
4. **The seed action is sofic.** `H ↷ A` is essentially free and its orbit
   relation `S` is sofic, so Proposition 1.15 again makes `H ↷ A` sofic.
5. **The seed group is sofic.** A sofic action gives a trace-preserving
   embedding `Θ : L^∞(A) ⋊ H → Π_(k→ω) M_(n_k)` with
   `Θ(u_h) = (σ_k(h))_(k→ω)` for permutation matrices `σ_k(h)`
   (Definition 1.4). Multiplicativity gives
   `||σ_k(g)σ_k(h) − σ_k(gh)||_2 → 0` along `ω`, which is normalized Hamming
   distance tending to `0`. The canonical trace has `τ(u_h) = 0` for `h ≠ e`,
   so the proportion of fixed points of `σ_k(h)` tends to `0` along `ω`. For
   each finite `F ⊆ H` and `ε > 0` some index `k` gives an `(F,ε)`-sofic
   approximation, so `H` is sofic.

So if `H` is not sofic, `G ↷ X` is not sofic, and `G ∉ 𝒮`. ∎

Step 5 is the fact recorded on `hyperlinear-nonsofic-free-action` as
"a sofic action makes the acting group sofic" (Kun--Thom p. 4).
