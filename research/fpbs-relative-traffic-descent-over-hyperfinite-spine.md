Let H be an aperiodic hyperfinite subrelation of the base relation and lift it orbitwise to the source. The lift is again aperiodic hyperfinite, because a factor map of free actions is orbitwise bijective and carries finite-class approximants to approximants with the same class sizes, so both copies have cost one. Allow routing plans to use lifted H-steps free of charge and count traffic only on the added group-labelled maps. All the descent and variational statements survive with the relative budgets: C(Y) - 1 = inf B_H and C(X) - 1 = inf (B_H + J_pi,H). For a nonamenable Bernoulli target such a spine exists: take the infinite cyclic subrelation of one free generator of the Gaboriau-Lyons free F_2 subrelation; for amenable groups fixed price one is a standard input. No small-cancellation presentation is needed.

Status records a written deduction resting on imported relative-cost and Gaboriau-Lyons theorems, not independent validation.
