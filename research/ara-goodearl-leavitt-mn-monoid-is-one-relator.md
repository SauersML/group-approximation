title: The monoid of finitely generated projectives of the Leavitt algebra L_K(m,n) is ⟨x | mx = nx⟩ with x = [1]
---

For positive integers `m ≤ n` and a field `K`, the Leavitt algebra `L_K(m, n)` has
`V(L_K(m,n)) ≅ ⟨x | mx = nx⟩`, with the generator `x` corresponding to the class `[1]` of the free module of rank one.
Here `L_K(m,n)` is the algebra with a universal isomorphism between the free modules of ranks `m` and `n`.

**Consequence used on main.** In `⟨x | mx = nx⟩`, `ix = jx` iff `i = j` or `i, j ≥ m` with `i ≡ j (mod n − m)`. So
`L_K(m,n)^i ≅ L_K(m,n)^j` exactly in those cases, for left modules, and by duality for right modules.
