Since K is the normal closure of c, K_ab is generated by the
Q-conjugates of its class v. These generators have order dividing
five, making K_ab an F5[Q]-module. The relation [a,c] makes
(a-1)v=0. The relation [b,c,b] makes (b-1)^2v=0, up to an
invertible power of b and a sign. The other two mixed b,c relators
have at least two c entries and vanish automatically when K is
abelian. Conversely the semidirect product of the cyclic module
with Q satisfies the full H31 presentation, so this is an exact
module presentation.

The a-fixed relation first gives the permutation module on the
2976 cosets q<a> of Q. The remaining relation and its Q-translates
give the rows with coefficients 1,-2,1 at q<a>,qb<a>,qb^2<a>,
respectively, for every q in Q. The script verifies the pinned
CCKW matrices, their orders, all three mixed a,b relators, and
the group order14880 before constructing these rows.

Both rank algorithms compute rank2975 exactly modulo five. The
independent sparse algorithm uses only invertible row operations,
retiring each nonzero pivot row and eliminating its pivot column
from every remaining row. All rows vanish after2975 distinct
pivot columns. Also every input row has coefficient sum zero,
providing the explicit one-dimensional constant quotient. Thus
the quotient module has dimension exactly one. Its augmentation
onto the trivial module is an isomorphism, proving the asserted
trivial Q action and K_ab=C5.
