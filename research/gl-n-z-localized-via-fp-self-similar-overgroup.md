Let `S` be a finitely presented self-similar group containing `Aff_n(Z_(l))`, for
the prime `l` given by the first input. Zaremsky's Theorem 1.1
(`fp-self-similar-groups-embed-in-fp-simple-groups`) embeds `S` in a finitely
presented simple group. So does its subgroup `GL_n(Z_(l)) <= Aff_n(Z_(l))`. ∎
