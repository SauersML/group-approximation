Let `K` be a field of characteristic zero, let `A,B` be locally
indicable groups, and let `w in A*B` be neither conjugate into a factor nor
a proper power. Put

```text
G=(A*B)/normal_closure(w).
```

If `K[A]` and `K[B]` are coherent rings, then `K[G]` is coherent.
