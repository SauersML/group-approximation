Direct proof.  For uniform `x` on the unit sphere,
`E_x ⟨Mx, Mx⟩ = (1/d) tr(M^*M) = (1/d)‖M‖_F^2`, since
`E_x[x x^*] = I/d`.  Apply with `M = U - V` and expand `‖U-V‖_F^2` through
`Re tau(U^*V)` for the stated corollaries.  No external input.
