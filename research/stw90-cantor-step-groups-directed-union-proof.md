Realize `X={0,1}^N`.  Let `G_n` consist of the functions which are constant
on every cylinder determined by the first `n` coordinates.  Then

```text
G_n ~= P^(2^n),
```

and the inclusion `G_n->G_(n+1)` duplicates each coordinate on its two child
cylinders.  Every locally constant function has finite image and clopen
fibres; compactness and the cylinder basis give one common depth at which all
fibres are unions of cylinders.  Hence

```text
LC(X,P)=union_n G_n.                                  (1)
```

There are only countably many cylinder-labelled finite tuples, so this group
is countable.

PHP is closed under finite direct products.  Thus every `G_n` has PHP, and
Ozawa's theorem makes `C*_r(G_n)` completely selfless, hence selfless.
Equation (1) and reduced group-algebra directed-union permanence make
`C*_r(LC(X,P))` selfless.  Robert's tracial regularity theorem gives
C\*-simplicity, stable rank one, strict comparison, and uniqueness of the
normalized `2`-quasitrace.

We next prove nonlinearity without classifying representations.  Choose
pairwise disjoint nonempty clopen sets `U_1,U_2,...` in `X`.  Functions which
equal an element of `P` on `U_j` and the identity off `U_j` give commuting
subgroups `H_j~=P` and embed

```text
direct_sum_j P <= LC(X,P).                             (2)
```

The nontrivial PHP group `P` is C\*-simple by Ozawa's theorem, and therefore
is nonabelian.  If (2) had a faithful representation in `GL_d(k)`, let `R_n`
be the unital subalgebra of `M_d(k)` generated by the first `n` images
`H_1,...,H_n`.  If `R_(n+1)=R_n`, then the algebra generated by `H_(n+1)`
would lie in both `R_n` and its commutant, hence in the commutative center of
`R_n`, contradicting nonabelianness.  The resulting infinite strictly
increasing chain of vector subspaces in `M_d(k)` is impossible.  Thus the
subgroup in (2), and hence `LC(X,P)`, is nonlinear over every field.

Finally, every element has a non-virtually-cyclic centralizer.  This is clear
for the identity.  If `f` is nontrivial, there are a nonempty clopen
`U subset X` and `g!=e` such that `f|U=g`.  Split `U` into infinitely many
pairwise disjoint nonempty clopen sets `V_j`.  The functions equal to `g` on
`V_j` and to the identity elsewhere generate a copy of

```text
direct_sum_j <g>
```

inside the centralizer of `f`; this group is not virtually cyclic.  A
non-virtually-cyclic acylindrically hyperbolic group has a loxodromic element,
whose centralizer is virtually cyclic.  Therefore `LC(X,P)` is not
acylindrically hyperbolic.

If `P` is exact, so is every finite power `G_n`.  The embeddings of reduced
group algebras in (1) form an injective inductive system with limit
`C*_r(LC(X,P))`; exactness passes to this limit.  The specialization `P=F_2`
is exact and has PHP.

**Trust boundary.**  Imported inputs are finite-product permanence of PHP,
Ozawa's complete-selflessness theorem, Robert's directed-union and tracial
regularity theorems, exactness permanence for injective C\*-inductive limits,
and the virtually-cyclic-centralizer theorem for loxodromic elements.  The
Cantor-stage exhaustion, nonlinear subgroup obstruction, and elementwise
centralizer calculation are proved above.
