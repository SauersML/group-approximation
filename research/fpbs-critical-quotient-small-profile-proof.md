Choose infinitely many original vertices at pairwise distance at least
three. Their incident edge sets are disjoint, and each is entirely closed
at c with probability (1-c)^d>0. Almost surely infinitely many therefore
give singleton quotient cells. Each has exactly d boundary edges counted
with multiplicity. The singleton candidate bounds psi_ell(1) from above;
its reciprocal is the first term of the profile sum. Section 2 gives
the full argument and the exact sprinkling parameter conversion.
