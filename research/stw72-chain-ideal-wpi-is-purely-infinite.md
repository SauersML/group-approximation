Let `A` be a C-star algebra whose closed two-sided ideals are linearly
ordered by inclusion.  If `A` is weakly purely infinite, then `A` is purely
infinite.

Thus STW Problem LXXII has a positive answer throughout the chain-ideal
class, with no separability, nuclearity, unitality, stable-rank, real-rank,
or dimension hypothesis.
