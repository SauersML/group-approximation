Citation import, read from the scanned journal PDF on 2026-09-13.

K. S. Brown and R. Geoghegan, *An infinite-dimensional torsion-free FP_∞ group*,
Invent. Math. 77 (1984), 367–381. The group F is defined there (p. 367) by the
presentation `<x_0, x_1, x_2, … | x_i^{-1} x_n x_i = x_{n+1} for i < n>`, which is
Richard Thompson's group F.

- p. 379, verbatim: "Theorem 7.2. H^n(F, ZF)=0 for all n."
- p. 379, Remarks, verbatim: "1. F seems to be the first FP_∞ group known to have
  H*(F, ZF)=0."
- p. 368, verbatim: "In fact, the cellular chain complex P of the universal cover
  of Z is a finite type free resolution of Z over ZF."
- p. 367, verbatim: "We also obtain homology and cohomology calculations of F,
  including the result that H*(F,ZF)=0."

The proof of Theorem 7.2 uses the ascending HNN structure of F over a copy
`F_1 ≅ F` and a monomorphism theorem from the authors' paper *Cohomology with free
coefficients of the fundamental group of a graph of groups* (Comment. Math. Helv.).

This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what the claim records.
