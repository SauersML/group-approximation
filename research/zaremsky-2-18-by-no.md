Problem 2.18 asks whether `F` admits a cobounded isometric action on the hyperbolic plane.
Its source, Question 11.4 of arXiv:2406.12982, asks for actions on "(a space
quasi-isometric to) the hyperbolic plane". Both readings are answered negatively by the two
required claims: the literal one for `H^2` itself, the source one for geodesic spaces
quasi-isometric to `H^2`. The route fires only when both are established.
