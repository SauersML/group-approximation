**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Fix `n >= 2` and `d > 1/3`. Let
`G = <S | R>` with `|S| = n` and `R` a random collection of `(2n−1)^{ld}`
cyclically reduced relators of length `l`, each chosen uniformly and
independently. With probability tending to 1 as `l → ∞` through multiples of 3,
`G` has property (T).

Sources:

- M. Kotowski and M. Kotowski, *Random groups and property (T): Żuk's theorem
  revisited*, arXiv:1106.2242v2, **Theorem B**, with the proof in Section 3.
  The authors write "Actually we have covered only the case of l divisible by
  3"; other lengths are only sketched, so they are not imported.
- Independent proof: C. Druțu and J. M. Mackay, arXiv:1607.04130v3, **Theorem
  12.6** and Corollary 12.7, for any fixed `k >= 2` and `d > 1/3` (and at
  `d = 1/3` with a polynomial correction).
