**ESTABLISHED (literature import).**  Let `A` be a unital C\*-algebra, let
`U_0(A)` be the connected component of the identity in the unitary group
`U(A)`, and let `DU_0(A)` be the abstract commutator subgroup of `U_0(A)`
(no closure is taken).

**Theorem A of Chand--Robert.**  If `A` is simple and unital, then
`DU_0(A)` is simple modulo its center.

Source: Abhinav Chand and Leonel Robert, *Simplicity, bounded normal
generation, and automatic continuity of groups of unitaries*,
arXiv:2108.09796.  The simplicity is abstract: the proof takes an arbitrary
normal subgroup of `DU_0(A)/Z(DU_0(A))` that is not trivial and shows that
it is everything.  No topology on the normal subgroup is assumed.
