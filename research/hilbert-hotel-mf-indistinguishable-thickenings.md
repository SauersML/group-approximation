Let `A` be any full-MF-radical group, and let

```text
1 -> N -> E --pi--> B -> 1
```

be a countable group extension in which `N` is normally generated by the
image of a homomorphism `A -> E`.  Then, for every MF group `M`, pullback
along `pi` is a natural bijection

```text
Hom(B,M)  ~=  Hom(E,M).                            (T1)
```

The same holds for every compact Hausdorff target and every countable linear
target.  Consequently `pi` induces isomorphisms of:

```text
universal MF-visible quotients,
Bohr compactifications,
profinite completions,
finite-dimensional complex representation categories.
```

Taking `A` to be either Hilbert-hotel full-radical group gives explicit
thickenings with this property.  In particular, for every countable `B`, the
projection

```text
EL_16(L_(F_2)(1,2)) x B  ->  B
```

is invisible to every MF-valued representation functor even though its
kernel is infinite, finitely generated, Kazhdan, and equal to its own MF
radical.

If the finitely presented Hilbert-hotel cover and a finitely presented base
are used, both groups in the comparison are finitely presented.  Thus there
are finitely presented nonisomorphic groups which no homomorphism to any MF
group can distinguish beyond the prescribed base.
