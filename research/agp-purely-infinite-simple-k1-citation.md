## Why sufficient

Literature import.  `arXiv:math/0111066`, Theorem 2.4, read 2026-09-07 and
quoted verbatim in the target.  The hypotheses of the theorem as printed are
that `R` is a purely infinite simple ring; regularity appears in the paper's
title and in its later sections but not in this statement.

Theorem 4.2 of the same paper, also read, states that `V_(1,n)` is purely
infinite simple with `K_0(V_(1,n)) = Z/(n-1)Z` for every `n >= 2`, which is
what places the binary Leavitt algebra under Theorem 2.4.
