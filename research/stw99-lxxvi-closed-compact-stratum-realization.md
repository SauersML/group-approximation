Let D be separable, simple, stably finite, pure, and real rank zero,
and let X be finite-dimensional compact metrizable. Let b be positive
in C(X,(D tensor Z) tensor K). Suppose there is a closed Y in X such
that [b|Y] is compact in Cu(C(Y,D tensor Z)) and every fiber b(x)
outside Y has soft or zero Cuntz class.

Then [b] belongs to the image of the actual first-factor map
Cu(C(X,D))->Cu(C(X,D tensor Z)). The compact bundle need only extend
to a neighborhood of Y; its attachment to the soft complement is
constructed through strict hereditary embeddings on closed collars.

The theorem includes zero fibers on Y. It does not assert realization
for arbitrary nonclosed compact fiber loci or for closed loci whose
restrictions are not compact classes. STW LXXVI remains open.
