For a unital C*-algebra A, stabilization and Bott periodicity give a
homomorphism pi_1(U(A),1)->K_0(A) taking the scalar loop of winding n
to n[1_A]. The fundamental group of a topological group is abelian.

For the irrational rotation algebra, the Pimsner--Voiculescu sequence
gives 0->Z[1]->K_0(A_theta)->Z->0, so there is a group homomorphism
epsilon:K_0(A_theta)->Z with epsilon([1])=1. The unital inclusion into
A_theta tensor O_infinity preserves and identifies the K-theory and
unit class.

These are literature inputs and elementary consequences, not newly
solved open problems. The artifact specifies the Bott sign and proves
the splitting of the displayed exact sequence.
