title: Interval fields over simple stably finite pure fibers are Cu-regular

Let `D` be a simple separable stably finite pure C*-algebra.  Then the
non-simple C*-algebra

```text
A = C([0,1],D)
```

is Cuntz semigroup regular: the first-factor map induces an isomorphism

```text
Cu(C([0,1],D))
    -> Cu(C([0,1],D) tensor_min Z).
```

This conclusion does not require `D` itself to be Z-stable.  It gives a
genuinely non-simple positive class for Problem LXXVI by a concrete Cu
calculation rather than by absorption of Z at the C*-level.
