The diagonal and Fourier PVMs define a representation of the free product
`C^R*C^R`.  Equation `(NRS2)` produces every matrix unit, so the image and
GNS closure are `M_n(C)`.  Faithfulness of normalized matrix trace makes all
atoms positive.  Thus the extreme local support is exactly `R`, unsafe
whenever `R` is nonaffine.
