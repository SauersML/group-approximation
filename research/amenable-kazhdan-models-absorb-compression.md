Let `Gamma <= G` and let `t in G` satisfy `t Gamma t^(-1) <= Gamma`.  Let
`pi : G -> U(N)` be a group homomorphism into a von Neumann algebra `(N, tau)`
with faithful normal tracial state -- no faithfulness of `pi`, no condition on
the character -- and put `P = pi(Gamma)''`.

**Theorem.**  If `P` is atomic (`P ~= (+)_i M_(n_i)(C)`), then

```text
pi(t) P pi(t)^* = P,   hence   pi(t^(-1) Gamma t) <= P,                (AKM1)
```

and inductively `pi(<t^(-k) Gamma t^k : k >= 0>) <= P` for every compressor.
By `kazhdan-generated-amenable-algebra-is-atomic`, `P` is atomic as soon as
`Gamma` has property (T) and `P` is amenable -- e.g. whenever `pi` takes values
in the hyperfinite factor `R`, or whenever `pi|_Gamma` is a direct sum of
finite-dimensional representations.

**Consequences for the Kun--Thom pair** `Gamma = EL_r(F_q[x_1..x_d]) < G`,
where strict compressors exist (`t Gamma t^(-1)` a proper subgroup, so some
`h in t^(-1) Gamma t` lies outside `Gamma`):

1. *Coset wreath.*  Every homomorphism `Phi` of `W = (+_(G/Gamma) C_2) x| G`
   into the unitary group of a tracial von Neumann algebra with
   `Phi(Gamma)''` amenable is non-injective: the base lamp `a_Gamma` commutes
   with `Gamma`, hence with `P`, hence with `Phi(h)`; but
   `h a_Gamma h^(-1) = a_(h Gamma) != a_Gamma` since `h Gamma != Gamma`.
2. *Free lamps and the double.*  The same for `E = G *_Gamma (Gamma x K)`,
   `K != 1` (`[h, k] != 1` in `E` by Britton's lemma, while `Phi(k)` commutes
   with `P`), and for the double `D = G *_Gamma G`: a faithful `Phi` on `D`
   with amenable `Phi(Gamma)''` induces to a faithful representation of
   `E = D x| C_2` on `M_2(N)` whose `Gamma`-algebra is `P (x) 1`, contradicting
   the previous sentence.
3. *Design constraint.*  With `abstract-unitary-embedding-upgrades-to-hyperlinear`
   this says: **any hyperlinear model of `W`, `E` or `D` -- any unitary
   embedding whatsoever, canonical character or not -- represents the Kazhdan
   subgroup by a non-amenable algebra.**  The Kazhdan part of a witness can
   never be a direct sum of finite-dimensional representations; the
   abstract-embedding freedom cannot be spent on making it one.
4. *Amenable-base amalgamation is closed.*  Brown--Dykema--Jung permanence and
   Popa's relative free independence both need an amenable base; for a
   compressed Kazhdan pair the only amenable bases containing the
   `Gamma`-algebra already contain `pi(t^(-1) Gamma t)`, so a freely adjoined
   `K` commutes with more than `Gamma` and the amalgam is never faithful.  The
   route `kt-double-hyperlinear-via-hyperfinite-edge-model` records the attempt
   and is killed by this claim.
5. *Partial answer to the normalization question.*  Since `Ad pi(t)` maps `P`
   onto itself, it maps `P' cap N` onto itself: the `Gamma`-centralizer of any
   model of `G` with amenable `Gamma`-algebra IS normalized by every
   compressor, and by `G` when `G` is generated by compressors of `Gamma`
   together with `Gamma` (infranormality).  The open content of
   `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs` is exactly the non-amenable case, which
   for the canonical character is `L(Gamma)`.

**What is not claimed.**  Nothing about representations in which
`pi(Gamma)''` is non-amenable (diffuse) -- that is where the sofic-radical and
relative-commutant lanes live, and where the Hilbert--Schmidt analogue of
Kun--Thom's cell counting has no atoms to count.
