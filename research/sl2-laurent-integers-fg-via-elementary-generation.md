If `SL_2(Z[t,t^{-1}]) = E_2(Z[t,t^{-1}])` (`sl2-laurent-integers-equals-elementary-subgroup`),
then the five generators of `E_2` from
`elementary-sl2-laurent-integers-is-finitely-generated` generate `SL_2(Z[t,t^{-1}])`.
