The Fourier decomposition
`L^2(B tensor_bar L(Z))=direct_sum_n L^2(B)t^n` diagonalizes the Poisson
map with eigenvalues `r^|n|`.  Truncation in `n` proves right-`B` module
compactness, while the fixed `n=0` summand proves failure of scalar
compactness.  Every nonzero central corner contains the expected copy of
the nonamenable factor `B`, and Morita invariance of amenability passes the
obstruction to every nonzero corner; the amenable tensor leg proves
relative amenability.  These observations establish all five assertions in
the target.
