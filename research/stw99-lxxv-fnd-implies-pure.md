Every separable C\*-algebra of finite nuclear dimension is
`(m, m')`-pure for suitable `(m, m')`, and every `(m, m')`-pure
C\*-algebra — simple or not — is pure.  So finite nuclear dimension
implies pureness with no simplicity hypothesis, and the nonsimple
Toms--Winter cycle's (i) ⟹ (iii)-direction is known up to the
pure-versus-Cu-regular gap (Problem LXXVI).
