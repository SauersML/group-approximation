Under `(RC3)` every unitary of `L(C)' cap M` commutes with `L(A)`.  A
unitary `k` as in the target satisfies `tau(k u_h k^* u_h^*) = 0` for
`h = diag(2,1,1/2) notin C` by amalgamated freeness (the word alternates
between `W^*(B,k) (-) B` and `N (-) B`), whereas commuting with `u_h` would
give trace `1`.  So no such `k` exists.
