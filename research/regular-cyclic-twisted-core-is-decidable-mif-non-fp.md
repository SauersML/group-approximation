Let Omega=Z with Z acting by translations, and let

    T=Omega V_Z

be the associated twisted Brin--Thompson group. Then T is infinite,
finitely generated, simple, and mixed identity-free (MIF). It has solvable
word problem and hence a computable presentation on finitely many
generators. It is not finitely presented.

The infinite coordinate set and its regular action are part of this
definition. This is not Thompson's circle group conventionally denoted T.
