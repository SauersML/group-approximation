For anticommuting Pauli involutions `X,Z`, every unitary `V` is within
`||[V,X]||_2+||[V,Z]||_2` of a unitary in their common commutant.  Thus two
mixed return words lock an exposed relative gauge into the smaller
multiplicity reservoir with dimension-free constants.  One commutator test
is insufficient.
