Let `A` be a separable type-I C-star algebra satisfying the hereditary
commutative-shadow hypothesis of Problem LXXXI.  Then its Brown--Pedersen
topological dimension satisfies

```text
topdim(A) <= 1.
```

Consequently, a separable type-I counterexample to Problem LXXXI would have
to satisfy

```text
topdim(A) <= 1 < dim_nuc(A) < infinity.
```

It would therefore separate nuclear dimension from topological dimension
inside the type-I category; high-dimensional type-I strata cannot provide a
counterexample.
