**ESTABLISHED.**  Put

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,       Q=1-ST,       E=EL_5(J).
```

Let `I={1,2,3}` and `Omega={4,5}`.  Let `A` be any subgroup of `E`
supported on the `I x I` block and contained in the determinant-one
monomial-symbol preimage `K` of
`binary-jacobson-monomial-symbol-preimage-is-marked-mf`.  In particular `A`
may contain every balanced triangular shift `a_ij`, `i,j in I`, and every
Weyl permutation of the active rows.  Let

```text
B=<x_45(1),x_54(1)> = EL_2(F_2)
```

on the target block, and put

```text
N={ product_(i in I, alpha in Omega) x_(i alpha)(r_(i alpha))
      : r_(i alpha) in J }.
```

Then `N` is abelian, isomorphic to the additive group of `M_(3 x 2)(J)`,
and `A x B` normalizes `N`.  Consequently every literal occurrence package
built from

```text
A,
x_(i4)(r), x_(i5)(r)       (i in I, r in J),
x_45(1), x_54(1)
```

lies in the amenable semidirect product

```text
N semidirect (A x B).                                      (J2T1)
```

In particular the subgroup generated by the three cyclic balanced shifts,
active-row Weyl words, raw constant roots in **both** target columns, and
the internal target-cycle roots `x_45(1),x_54(1)` is amenable.  It contains
the nontrivial head root `x_24(Q)`.  By `amenable-implies-operator-mf` it has
a faithful norm-matrix-corona representation retaining that head root.

Thus the first two-target extension of the controlled-swap package produces
neither an oriented Hall deficit nor a same-square Toeplitz decoder.  A
successful occurrence package must leave this upper block parabolic.  It
must contain a reverse root from a target row back to an active column, a
raw nonmonomial root internal to the active block, or other data not contained
in `(J2T1)`.
