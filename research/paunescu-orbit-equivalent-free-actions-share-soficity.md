**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `α` and `β` be essentially free p.m.p. actions of
countable groups, on standard probability spaces, that are orbit equivalent: there is a
measure-preserving isomorphism of the spaces carrying almost every `α`-orbit onto a `β`-orbit. If
`α` is sofic (Păunescu Definition 1.4), then `β` is sofic, and likewise for hyperlinear (Definition 1.3).

L. Păunescu, *On sofic actions and equivalence relations*, arXiv:1002.0605v5, verbatim:

> **Theorem 1.5.** Let α and β be two free orbit equivalent actions. If α is hyperlinear (sofic) then
> also β is hyperlinear (sofic).

The proof identifies both crossed products with the Feldman--Moore algebra of the common orbit
relation, by an isomorphism that is the identity on `L^∞(X)`. Each `v_s` of the second group is then
`Σ_g p_g u_g` with projections `p_g` summing to `1`, which Lemma 1.6 turns into a permutation.
