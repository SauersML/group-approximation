Artifact Sections 2 and 3 give the proof. A chosen Steinberg lift splits
the pulled-back central extension and every pushout, while primitive
evaluation makes c0 mod m nonzero for m>1. Alternatively any difference
between a central detector and the chosen lift is a homomorphism from
B to its abelian central image; it kills z because z lies in [B,B].

For the finite-field assertion the prerequisite gives a surjection
H_2(F/<f(z)>;Z)-><f(z)>. Steinberg's Lectures on Chevalley Groups,
Section 7, Corollary 2 after Theorem 10, states that Sp_4(F_q) is
centrally closed for q>4. Its multiplier is therefore zero at q=7^r.
The primary PDF, precise page reference, and source scope are supplied
in the artifact. This last multiplier statement is a literature import.
