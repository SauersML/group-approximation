(equivariantly `O_∞`-stable), and cocycle conjugate actions have
isomorphic crossed products with
`(A ⊗ Z) ⋊_{α⊗1} G ≅ (A ⋊_α G) ⊗ Z` — the same trivial-factor
commutation as in `stw99-xxxix-implies-xxxviii`, now with an
