## Setting

- **Groups.** `Γ <= Aut(T_2)` is self-similar and contains the odometer `T_1`. Points of `Z_2` are `y`, and `ι(y) = y`.
- **The adder.** On `Z_2 × Z_2`, coded as the 4-ary tree of digit pairs, `A(x, y) = (x + y, y)` (2-adic addition).
  For a function `ψ : Z_2 → Z_2`, put `F_ψ(x, y) = (x + ψ(y), y)`.
- **The orbit span.** `M(Γ)` is the `Z`-span of the functions `g : Z_2 → Z_2`, `g ∈ Γ`. It is a `Z[Γ]`-module under
  precomposition, and it is generated by `ι`.
- **The group.** `G_Γ = ⟨A, id × Γ⟩ <= Aut(T_4)`.

## Theorem

1. **Structure.**
   - `G_Γ = {(x, y) ↦ (x + ψ(y), g(y)) : ψ ∈ M(Γ), g ∈ Γ} ≅ M(Γ) ⋊ Γ`.
   - `G_Γ` is self-similar on `T_4`.
   - `T_c × id ∈ G_Γ` if and only if the constant function `c` lies in `M(Γ)`.
2. **Host.** Let `Z_(2) · 1 ⊆ M(Γ)`, meaning every odd-denominator constant is a `Z`-combination of elements of `Γ`
   viewed as functions. Then the natural `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩` lies in `2V_(G_Γ)` over the 4-ary product 2-graph.
   - `Q` acts by 2-adic addition on the `x`-digits, with carries upward.
   - `2` acts by the pair shift `u ∈ 2V`.
   - If `G_Γ` is moreover of type `F_n`, then Li and Matui, used as in the criterion node, give an `F_n` simple
     group containing `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩`. The groupoid conditions (pseudo-freeness and the rest) are needed there. For
     `G_Γ`, pseudo-freeness holds iff `Γ` is pseudo-free and no nonzero element of `M(Γ)` vanishes on a cylinder.
3. **Necessary.**
   - `Γ` is not abelian. Otherwise `G_Γ` is finitely generated metabelian, and contains no `Z_(2)` by
     `fg-metabelian-groups-have-roots-at-finitely-many-primes`.
   - `Γ` is not finitely generated piecewise-affine over one `Z[1/N]`. Otherwise `M(Γ)` consists of piecewise-affine
     functions over `Z[1/N]`, and its constants lie in `Z[1/N]`.
4. **The bottleneck.** Any finitely presented coefficient group `H` (this `G_Γ`, or the `H` of the criterion node)
   that puts the natural `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩` into `2V_H` is a finitely presented residually finite group containing `Z_(2)`.
   - So it is a witness for `z-localized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group` at `l = 2`, hence for (SL)
     (`z-semilocalized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group`).
   - The Röver–Nekrashevych product route to `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩` is therefore never easier than (SL).
   - It asks for the smallest such object: an abelian `Z_(2)` of translations. The one-dimensional
     Röver–Nekrashevych route through Möbius maps at `∞` needs upper and lower unipotent copies of `Z_(2)`, i.e. the
     rational Iwahori group of `rational-iwahori-group-lies-in-fp-self-similar-group`.

## What this changes

- **For builders.** The target becomes: find a finitely generated self-similar `Γ` whose orbit functions have
  odd constants in their `Z`-span. `Γ` must be non-abelian and non-affine.
  - The divisibility then lives in the abelian normal subgroup `M(Γ)`, not in `Γ`.
  - This matches the (SL) exclusion list: divisibility cannot come from linear, metabelian or bounded-valence
    pieces, and must come from an infinite-index ascending mechanism. Here that mechanism is the cyclic
    `Z[Γ]`-module `M(Γ)` over a non-Noetherian group ring.
- **Pass status.** No such `Γ` was found in this pass. For the maps tried by hand, the constants in `M(Γ)` were only
  those forced by affine pieces. Those maps were translations, multiplications by odd `p` and XOR masks, in any
  combination (see the proof's remark).

## Lesson for general BH

**Adders turn multiplicative divisibility into additive span.**
- Adjoining `(x, y) ↦ (x + y, y)` to a group acting on `y` makes every `Z`-combination of orbit functions a fibered
  translation of `x`.
- An element with roots of every odd order is then the same thing as an odd constant in the orbit span.
- This is the general mechanism behind "carry the arithmetic in an abelian normal subgroup of a crossed-product-like
  host". The span `Z[Γ]·ι` is the group-level shadow of the crossed product `LC(X) ⋊ Γ` of the master route.

The bottleneck is the same on every route. `GL_n(Q)` needs `Z_(S)` inside a finitely presented residually finite
group, whether through BLIW trees, self-similar product hosts, or adders. A cyclic module over a non-Noetherian
group ring of an automaton group is the only place left where such a `Z_(S)` could appear.

## Referee (bh-ref-kourovka-b, 2026-09-18): PASS

**Verdict: PASS.** I checked `adder-hosts-linearize-odd-odometer-roots-proof` line by line. Lane
bh-free-16's adder construction is a neat device.
- **Step 1.** The composition law `(ψ,g)(ψ',g') = (ψ' + ψ∘g', gg')` is right. The conjugate
  `(0,g)^{-1}(ι,1)(0,g) = (g,1)` gives `G_Γ = M ⋊ Γ`, and `ι∘T_1 − ι = 1` puts `Z ⊆ M`.
- **Step 2.** Writing `ψ(y_0 + 2y') = e + 2ψ'(y')` with `ψ' = Σ n_g g|_{y_0} ∈ M` is correct. The
  section is `F_{ψ' + (x_0+e−j)/2}`, and it lies in `G_Γ`.
- **Step 3.** The translation by `c ∈ Z_(2)` touches only nonnegative positions and equals `F_c`.
  Conjugating by the shift gives `c/2^k`, so the copy of `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩` is correct.
  - **Pseudo-freeness.** Fixing a vertex `(w, v)` of length `k` forces `ψ ≡ 0 mod 2^k` on the
    cylinder, and a trivial section forces `ψ = 0` there. So the stated condition is exactly the
    right one.
  - **Relies on.** The framework of `natural-q-rtimes-2-in-2v-h-iff-odd-odometer-roots`
    (2V_H over the product 2-graph), which I did not re-referee.
- **Step 4.** The metabelian obstruction uses the cited node. The piecewise-affine obstruction is
  correct, and it does not need finite generation.
- **Step 5.** Faithful action on a rooted tree implies residually finite, so the reduction to (SL)
  at `l = 2` is correct.
