Choose a finite quotient

```text
q:G->F                                                     (PAP1)
```

which separates the prescribed actor words and every pair of group elements
in the support of `1-a`.  Put `Q=q(Gamma)` and replace `F` by the subgroup
generated by `Q,q(A),q(B)`.  Compression gives

```text
q(A)Qq(A)^(-1)<=Q,       q(B)Qq(B)^(-1)<=Q.            (PAP2)
```

Conjugation is injective and `Q` is finite, so both inclusions are
equalities.  Hence `Q` is normal in `F`.  Since `q(r) in Q` and `q(L)`
normalizes `Q`,

```text
q(h)=q(L)q(r)q(L)^(-1) in Q.                           (PAP3)
```

Let `X=F/Q` and form the finite binary generalized wreath

```text
W=(direct_sum_X C_2) semidirect F.                     (PAP4)
```

Write `ell_Q` for the lamp at the base coset.  It commutes with `Q`, so it
satisfies every row `(PAM2)`.  Equation `(PAP3)` gives

```text
q(h) ell_Q q(h)^(-1)=ell_Q.                            (PAP5)
```

Consequently the last factor in `(PAM3)` is exactly zero.

Tensor the left regular representation of `W` with the left regular
representation of the finite payload packet.  Evaluate `z` in the second
factor.  The packet trace is `tau_packet(z)>0`.  On the first factor,
restriction to `Gamma` is a multiple of the regular representation of `Q`.
Support separation in `(PAP1)` makes the regular matrices indexed by the
support of `1-a` orthogonal.  Therefore

```text
||z(1-a)||_2^2
 =tau_packet(z) sum_g |coefficient_g(1-a)|^2
 =tau_packet(z)||lambda_Gamma(1-a)||_2^2,              (PAP6)
```

proving `(PAM5)`.  Actor-word separation in `(PAP1)` gives canonical regular
trace on the requested actor window.  For the bounded-type approximation,
the regular representation kills `p_<=K`; maximal-norm approximation gives
`||lambda_Gamma(a)||_op<=epsilon`, and the reverse triangle inequality in
`L2` gives `(PAM6)`.

Finally, if the chosen exact perfect payload trace kills `z`, tensor it with
the canonical infinite generalized wreath.  Then `(PAM3)` is zero from its
left factor while the two coordinate lamps are distinct, proving the stated
consistency and completing the double-vacuity audit.
