title: Radius 1/420000 preserves Z-stability when the Z-stable algebra is nuclear

Let `A,B` be C*-subalgebras of the same `B(H)`.  If `A` is separable,
nuclear, and Z-stable, and

```text
d(A,B) < 1/420000,
```

then `B` is Z-stable.  No separability assumption on `H` is needed.

This is an unconditional positive answer to Problem XCIX on the nuclear
subclass.  It uses abstract isomorphism, not a claim that the two concrete
representations are unitarily conjugate at this radius.
