**Established by citation.**  Pieter Spaas, *Hilbert--Schmidt stability for
graph products*, arXiv:2603.02058 (2026), proves Hilbert--Schmidt stability
for graph products of abelian groups over chordal graphs; the paper states the
right-angled Artin group case as an immediate consequence.

The result is imported here because it supplies same-dimension exactification
for the commuting root charts proposed in the Deligne--Maslov `C2` compiler.
