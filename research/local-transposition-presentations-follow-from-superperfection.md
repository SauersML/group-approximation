## Setting

- `Λ` is a countable group and `X` a compact zero-dimensional `Λ`-space.
- `𝔅` is a basis of compact open subsets of `X` that is closed under finite intersections and
  `Λ`-translates, and in which complements are finite unions. For a subshift, the cylinders `Z(F, p) ∩ X`
  qualify.
- `T = G_V × (Λ ⋉ X)`. `F = F(T)` is its group of full compact open bisections, which is the
  homeomorphism reading when `T` is effective.
- A **piece** is `[u] × P` with `P ∈ 𝔅`.
- A basic transposition `[α|β]_λ` swaps disjoint pieces `α = [u] × P` and `β = [u'] × λP` by
  `(uw, x) ↔ (u'w, λx)`.
- **`P_LTP`** is the group presented by all basic transpositions, subject to:
  - (O): each generator is an involution;
  - (C): Quick's conjugacy relations, whenever the images of both pieces are pieces;
  - (S_C): splitting in the `C`-direction;
  - (S_X): splitting along any finite partition of `P` into members of `𝔅`.
- `ε : P_LTP → F` is the natural map. It is onto, because `F` is generated by transpositions
  (`v-times-ample-full-groups-are-generated-by-transpositions`, valid for full bisections of any ample
  groupoid), and each transposition refines into basic ones.
- An element `g ∈ F` is **canonical on a piece** `π` if it maps `π` by one germ type
  `(vw, x) ↦ (v'w, μx)` onto a piece.

## Statement

1. **Transport lemma.** Let `h ∈ P_LTP` and `g = ε(h)`, and let `t = [E|F]_λ` with `g` canonical on `E`
   and on `F`. Then `h t h^-1 = [gE | gF]_(λ')` in `P_LTP`, where `λ'` is the transported label.
2. **Central kernel.** `ker ε` is central in `P_LTP`.
3. **Perfectness.** `P_LTP` is perfect, and so is `F`.
4. **LTP from superperfection.**
   - If `H_2(F; Z) = 0`, then `ε` is an isomorphism, so LTP holds.
   - In general, `ker ε` is a quotient of `H_2(F; Z)`.
   - `H_2(F) = 0` whenever `T` is minimal: then `F` is integrally acyclic. The inputs are:
     - Li, arXiv 2209.08087, Corollary D. For `G` ample and minimal with comparison, with no isolated
       units: `H_*(G) = 0` in all degrees forces `F(G)` to be integrally acyclic.
     - `T` is purely infinite minimal when `Λ ↷ X` is minimal, so it has comparison.
     - `H_*(T) = 0`, by Matui's Künneth formula for groupoid homology (recalled, not re-read) and
       `H_*(G_V) = 0` (the Cuntz groupoid of `O_2`; Li, p. 5).
5. **Consequences.**
   - **The master route's object.** For the (★′) SFT `X` over `Λ₀`, which is minimal and topologically
     free, `F(G_V × (Λ₀ ⋉ X))` has the local transposition presentation.
   - **The claim of the parent node.** `v-times-full-groups-have-local-transposition-presentations`
     holds for every minimal `X`, with (S_X) over finite partitions.
   - **Free products.** By `free-product-gluing-reduces-to-local-transposition-presentation`, FPG holds
     for every torsion-free free product acting minimally with hypothesis (H) of that node. For the full
     shift `A^(F_n)`, FPG ⇐ `H_2(F(T_(F_n, A^(F_n)))) = 0`.

## Proof

**Regular refinement.** Let `w = s_1 ⋯ s_r` be a word in generators and `π` a piece. Some partition of `π`
into pieces has every cell **regular along `w`**: each successive image under `s_r`, then `s_(r−1)s_r`, and
so on, is a piece that is contained in, or disjoint from, each piece of the next letter.
- Refine for `s_r`. Cones in `C` are comparable or disjoint, and `𝔅` is closed under intersections and
  complements as finite unions.
- Push forward by the canonical map of `s_r`, which sends sub-pieces to sub-pieces bijectively.
  Refine for `s_(r−1)`, and pull back. There are finitely many steps.

**1.** Write `h = s_1 ⋯ s_r` with each `s_j` a generator; they are involutions by (O).
- *Split.* Choose a uniform refinement `{κ_E(u)}_u` of `E`, and let `κ_F(u)` be the `t`-images, so that
  `κ_E(u)` corresponds to `κ_F(u)`. Take it fine enough that every `κ_E(u)` and `κ_F(u)` is regular along
  `w`. Iterated (S_C) and (S_X) give `t = ∏_u [κ_E(u) | κ_F(u)]_λ` in `P_LTP`.
- *Transport.* Conjugate each factor by `s_r`, then by `s_(r−1)`, and so on. By regularity, each step is
  an instance of (C), and it produces the basic transposition between the image pieces, with the
  transported label.
- *Reassemble.* After all `r` steps the pieces are `g κ_E(u)` and `g κ_F(u)`. Since `g` is canonical on `E`
  and on `F`, these are the uniform sub-pieces `κ_(gE)(u)` and `κ_(gF)(u)`, and all carry the same
  label `λ'`. So (S_C) and (S_X) reassemble the product into `[gE|gF]_(λ')`.

**2.** Let `k ∈ ker ε`. Then `ε(k) = id` is canonical on every piece, with trivial germ. By 1, `k` commutes
with every generator.

**3.**
- Let `t = [α|β]_λ`, with split halves `t_0 = [α0|β0]_λ` and `t_1 = [α1|β1]_λ`.
- Some `g ∈ F` maps `α → α0` and `β → β0` canonically. Over `pr(α) ∪ pr(β)` take the prefix maps
  `[u] → [u0]` and `[u'] → [u'0]` fibrewise (where the projections overlap, `u ⊥ u'`, because the pieces
  are disjoint), and complete them by prefix bijections of the complements.
- Lift `g` to `h`. Then `h t h^-1 = t_0` by 1, and likewise `t` is conjugate to `t_1`.
- In the abelianization, (S_C) gives `[t] = [t_0] + [t_1] = 2[t]`, so `[t] = 0`.
- The same argument in `F` shows that `F` is perfect.

**4.**
- *Kernel.* `ε` is a central extension of `F` with perfect total group, by 2 and 3. So it is a quotient of
  the universal central extension, whose kernel is `H_2(F)`.
- *Vanishing.* If `H_2(F) = 0`, then `F` is superperfect, so `ε` splits by a homomorphism `σ`. Hence
  `P_LTP = σ(F) · ker ε`, and `P_LTP = [P_LTP, P_LTP] ⊆ σ(F)`, so `ker ε = 1`.
- *Purely infinite minimal.* Let `U` and `W ⊇ [v] × Q` be nonempty compact open sets. By minimality,
  finitely many translates `λ_j Q` cover `X`. Cut `U` into pieces, each carried into `[v] × Q` by one
  `λ_j^-1` and a distinct prefix extension of `v`. This gives a compact open bisection `σ` with
  `s(σ) = U` and `r(σ) ⊆ W`. ∎

## What remains

**For the gluing presentation.** Exactness `K = 1` of the gluing group `Γ~ = M *_I N / ⟨⟨[M_0, N_1]⟩⟩` is
not given by this node. What this node shows is that the central-kernel mechanism is the right one, and
the analogue in `Γ~` would finish the job.
- *Lemma T in Γ~.* Suppose the canonical lifts `L[α|β]_λ ∈ Γ~` satisfy:
  - (S_C) and (S_X), and
  - one-step transport under the generators of `M` and `N`: `s L(t) s^-1 = L(s·t)` when the pieces of
    `t` are regular for `s`.
- `Γ~` is generated by lifts: the conditioned `V`-elements `(ww')^[P]` are label-1 lifts inside `N`; `Λ` and the
  full-column transpositions lie in `M`; and identities among these hold inside `M` or `N`, which embed in `Γ~`.
- So the proof of 1–2 runs verbatim in `Γ~`: every kernel element commutes with every lift, and `K` is central
  in `Γ~`.
- Since `K = [K, Γ~]` (`v-times-sft-gluing-kernel-is-relatively-perfect`), this gives `K = 1` directly,
  with no `H_2` input.

This lift statement belongs to bh-p2b-exact. It needs no (C) with overlapping supports, only one-step
transport by the generators of the two pieces.

**For full shifts.** LTP holds iff the central extension `ε` is trivial, and that holds if
`H_2(F(G_V × (Λ ⋉ A^Λ))) = 0`.
- Li's unconditional amplified theorem gives acyclicity of `F(R × T)`.
- Passing from `F(R × T)` back to `F(T)` needs homological stability for the corner maps without
  minimality (Li's Morita invariance assumes minimality).

That step is open, and it is the full-shift residue of this lane.

## Lesson for general BH

**Local presentations of V-type full groups are free once the group is superperfect.** In any Quick-type
local presentation, every lift is a transport. A kernel element acts trivially, so it commutes with every
generator, and the kernel is central. Topological full groups of `G_V ×` anything are perfect, and in the
minimal case Li's machinery makes them acyclic. So the infinite local presentation holds automatically
for every system the master route uses: no Stein-complex bookkeeping and no case analysis of overlapping
supports.

The same mechanism, transport implies central implies trivial, closes the gluing presentation once lifts
transport one step at a time inside `Γ~`. There the relative perfectness `K = [K, Γ~]` replaces `H_2`. The
hard part of P2′b is therefore no longer overlapping (C): it is one-step transport of lifts in the gluing
group.

## Referee (bh-ref-ffwz, 2026-09-18): PASS, with one repairable nit

- **Items 1–2.** Correct.
  - Regular refinement along a word makes every one-letter conjugation an instance of (C).
  - The transported labels are the germs of `ε(h)`, which are constant on `E` and on `F`, so
    (S_C) and (S_X) reassemble.
  - A kernel element has trivial germs everywhere, so it commutes with every generator.
- **Item 3 (nit).** The conjugator `g` mapping `α → α0` and `β → β0` fibrewise needs nonempty
  complements on both sides in each fibre. This fails, for example, for `u = ∅`, or for
  `u = 0`, `u' = 1` over `P ∩ λP`. Repair: first split `t` by (S_C) until `|u|, |u'| ≥ 2`.
  Then both complements are nonempty clopens of `C`, hence prefix-equivalent. Also
  `[t] = Σ [split factors]`, so it suffices to kill the long-cone generators.
- **Item 4.**
  - A perfect central extension is a quotient of the universal one, and superperfection makes
    it split. Both steps are correct.
  - **Li, arXiv:2209.08087, checked at source (TeX).** The vanishing corollary
    (`introcor:Vanish`, Corollaries `cor:HVanish` and `cor:Acyclic`) is stated for ample
    groupoids with locally compact Hausdorff unit space without isolated points, minimal, with
    comparison: if `H_*(G) = 0` for all `* ≥ 0`, then `F(G)` is integrally acyclic and
    `F(G) = D(G)`.
    - Effectiveness is **not** assumed.
    - `F(G)` is the group of global compact open bisections, which matches this node.
  - The pure-infiniteness argument for comparison is correct.
  - `H_*(G_V) = 0` for the `O_2` groupoid is correct.
  - Matui's Künneth formula (Proc. LMS 104 (2012), Thm 2.4) was not re-read. Its standard
    statement gives `H_*(T) = 0` from `H_*(G_V) = 0`.
- **"What remains".** The gluing-presentation question has since been settled. `K = 1` is
  proved in `v-times-gluing-presentations-are-exact` (22a3770d9, refereed), and independently
  in `research/artifacts/gq-bh-one-relator-independent-proof-of-gluing-exactness.md`
  (9752ecd5d). The Lemma T suggested here is realized there by the auxiliary-cone lifts.
