title: An ascending HNN extension along a non-surjective injective endomorphism contains a free subsemigroup of rank two
---

**Statement.** Let `H` be a group, `phi: H -> H` injective but not surjective, and
`G = <H, t | t h t^{-1} = phi(h), h in H>`. For any `a in H \ phi(H)`, the elements `t` and
`a t` generate a free subsemigroup of rank 2. In particular, if `H` is finitely generated, `G`
has exponential growth.

Elementary; no novelty claimed. Proof: `ascending-hnn-non-surjective-free-subsemigroup-proof`.
