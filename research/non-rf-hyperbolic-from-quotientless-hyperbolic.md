title: An infinite hyperbolic group without finite quotients is not residually finite
target: non-residually-finite-hyperbolic-group
requires:
  - hyperbolic-group-without-finite-quotients
---

Every nonidentity element of an infinite group with no nontrivial finite
quotient lies in its finite residual, so the group is not residually finite.
Part of an intended equivalence cycle (see
`quotientless-hyperbolic-via-kapovich-wise`).
