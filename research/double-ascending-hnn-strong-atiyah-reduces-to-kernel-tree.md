**ESTABLISHED** by [[double-ascending-hnn-atiyah-kernel-tree-proof]].

**Setting.** Let `K` be a subfield of `C`, `Gamma` a torsion-free group, `S` a
nonempty set, and `phi_s: Gamma -> Gamma` an injective endomorphism for each
`s in S`. Put

```text
E = < Gamma, t_s (s in S) | t_s gamma t_s^-1 = phi_s(gamma) for gamma in Gamma, s in S >,
pi: E -> F(S),   pi(Gamma) = 1,   pi(t_s) = s,       N = ker pi.
```

For a reduced word `w` in `S`, write `t_w` for the same word in the letters
`t_s`, and `Gamma_w = t_w Gamma t_w^-1`.

1. **Splitting.** `E` is torsion-free, `pi` restricts to an isomorphism
   `<t_s : s in S> -> F(S)`, so `E = N semidirect F(S)`, and `N` is the normal
   closure of `Gamma`.
2. **Kernel tree.** `N` is the colimit of the tree of subgroups indexed by the
   Cayley tree of `F(S)`. Vertex `w` carries `Gamma_w`. The edge joining `w` and
   `w s^-1` carries `Gamma_w`, which is the whole vertex group at `w` and sits
   inside `Gamma_(w s^-1)` as `t_(w s^-1) phi_s(Gamma) t_(w s^-1)^-1`.
3. **Reduction.** The following are equivalent:
   - `E` satisfies Strong Atiyah over `K`;
   - `N` does;
   - for every finite subtree `Y` of the Cayley tree, the subgroup `N_Y`
     generated by the `Gamma_w`, `w in Y`, does.
4. **Growth by leaves.** Let `Y` be a finite subtree and `w in Y`.
   - Adding the child `ws` does not change `N_Y`.
   - Adding the parent `l = w s^-1` gives `N_(Y + l) = N_Y *_(Gamma_w) Gamma_l`.
     Here `Gamma_w ≅ Gamma` sits in `Gamma_l ≅ Gamma` as the compressed image
     `phi_s(Gamma)`.
5. **Consequence.** Suppose `Gamma` satisfies Strong Atiyah over `K`. Then `E`
   satisfies it provided the following permanence holds: whenever `A` satisfies
   Strong Atiyah over `K` and contains a copy `C` of `Gamma`, the amalgam
   `A *_C B` with `B ≅ Gamma` and `C` embedded in `B` as some `phi_s(Gamma)`
   satisfies Strong Atiyah over `K`.

## What it changes

* **Free stable letters are harmless.** `one-compressor-support-atiyah-reduces-to-base`
  said approximation-free permanence gives out at non-Ore two-letter supports.
  For the free multiple ascending HNN extension that boundary moves. The free
  quotient is absorbed by `strong-atiyah-passes-to-locally-indicable-extensions`.
  The only remaining input is amalgamation over compressed copies of `Gamma`.
* **Kazhdan base.** If `Gamma` is infinite with property (T), every edge group
  `Gamma_w` is infinite and Kazhdan. The permanence in item 5 is then an
  instance of `strong-atiyah-kazhdan-edge-amalgam-permanence`, applied once for
  each parent leaf.
* **First nontrivial amalgam.** A vertex `w` with two parents `w s^-1` and
  `w s'^-1` in `Y` gives `Gamma *_Gamma Gamma`, embedded by `phi_s` and
  `phi_(s')`. This is the kernel amalgam `P^(u_1) *_P P^(u_2)` of Section 3(e)
  of `research/artifacts/fournier-facio-one-compressor-reduction-2026-09-11.md`.
* **Scope.** It applies to the pre-quotient double HNN extension of the
  Fournier--Facio construction, and to any subgroup of a host generated by
  `Gamma` and compressors satisfying no relations beyond (1). It does not apply
  to the Fournier--Facio group itself. That group has property (T), so it has
  no infinite locally indicable quotient, and Strong Atiyah does not pass to
  quotients.

## Model tests

* **A trivial model fails the hypothesis.** A finite nontrivial `Gamma` has only
  automorphisms as injective endomorphisms. Then `E = Gamma semidirect F(S)`, the
  tree collapses to `N = Gamma`, and the averaging projection of `Gamma` has
  trace `1/|Gamma|`. Torsion-freeness is exactly what fails.
* **A real object with a Kazhdan base.** Let `Gamma = Gamma(3) <= SL_3(Z)`, with
  each `phi_s` conjugation by an element of `SL_3(Z)`. These are automorphisms,
  so the tree collapses to `N = Gamma(3)`, and `E = Gamma(3) semidirect F(S)`
  satisfies Strong Atiyah over `C` by items 1 and 3. Strict compressions are what
  make the amalgams of item 4 nontrivial.
