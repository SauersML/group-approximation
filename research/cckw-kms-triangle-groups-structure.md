**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** P.-E. Caprace, M. Conder, M. Kaluba, S. Witzel, *Hyperbolic
generalized triangle groups, property (T) and finite simple quotients*, arXiv:2011.09276 (CCKW).

Conventions (CCKW §7.1): `[x,y] = x^{-1}y^{-1}xy`, and `[x_1,…,x_n] = [[…[x_1,x_2],…],x_n]`.

1. **Theorem 3.1** (called "well known"; for (i) and (v) CCKW cite Bridson–Haefliger II.12.28).
   Let `G(T)` be a triangle of groups with trivial face group, and let `G` be its fundamental
   group, the direct limit of the vertex and edge groups. Assume that each vertex group `X_i` is
   generated by the images of its two edge groups. Let `Γ_i` be the coset graph of `X_i` with
   respect to these images, and `r_i` half its girth. If `1/r_0 + 1/r_1 + 1/r_2 ≤ 1`:
   - **(i)** `G` acts isometrically, by simplicial automorphisms, on a CAT(0) simplicial complex
     `Y(T)` of dimension 2. The action has a 2-simplex `(v_0, v_1, v_2)` as strict fundamental
     domain, isometric to a euclidean or hyperbolic triangle with angles `(π/r_0, π/r_1, π/r_2)`.
     The link of `Y(T)` at `v_i` is isomorphic to `Γ_i`, and the stabilizer `G_{v_i}` is
     isomorphic to `X_i`.
   - **(iv)** If some homomorphism from `G` to a finite group is injective on each `G_{v_i}`,
     then `G` is virtually torsion-free.
   - **(v)** If `1/r_0 + 1/r_1 + 1/r_2 < 1`, then `G` is non-elementary hyperbolic, and `Y(T)`
     carries a `G`-invariant CAT(−1) metric.
2. **The KMS families (§7.2).** For every odd prime `p`, CCKW attach a presentation to ten rank-3
   Dynkin diagrams. The six of non-euclidean half girth type are:
   - `G_{HC_2^{(1)}}(p) = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b], [b,c,b], [b,c,c], [a,c,a], [a,c,c,a], [a,c,c,c]>`;
   - `G_{HB_2^{(2)}}(p) = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b], [c,b,c], [c,b,b,c], [c,b,b,b], [c,a,c], [c,a,a,c], [c,a,a,a]>`;
   - `G_{HC_2^{(2)}}(p) = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b], [b,c,b], [b,c,c,b], [b,c,c,c], [a,c,a], [a,c,c,a], [a,c,c,c]>`;
   - `G_{HBC_2^{(2)}}(p) = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b], [b,c,b], [b,c,c,b], [b,c,c,c], [c,a,c], [c,a,a,c], [c,a,a,a]>`;
   - `G_{HB_2^{(3)}}(p) = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b,a], [a,b,b,b], [b,c,b], [b,c,c,b], [b,c,c,c], [a,c,a], [a,c,c,a], [a,c,c,c]>`;
   - `G_{HBC_2^{(3)}}(p) = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b,a], [a,b,b,b], [b,c,b], [b,c,c,b], [b,c,c,c], [c,a,c], [c,a,a,c], [c,a,a,a]>`.
   Each is the fundamental group of a triangle of groups with cyclic edge groups of order `p` and
   vertex groups `U_3(p)` or `U_4(p)`. CCKW: "In view of Theorem 3.1 and Proposition 7.1, the half
   girth type of G(T) is (r_0, r_1, r_2)."
3. **Proposition 7.6** assigns the half girth types: `(3,3,4)` for `G_{HC_2^{(1)}}(p)`; `(3,4,4)`
   for `G_{HC_2^{(2)}}(p)`, `G_{HB_2^{(2)}}(p)`, `G_{HBC_2^{(2)}}(p)`; `(4,4,4)` for
   `G_{HB_2^{(3)}}(p)` and `G_{HBC_2^{(3)}}(p)`.
4. **Theorem 7.7**, verbatim in part: "Let G be a KMS group of half girth type (r_0,r_1,r_2) over
   F_p. Then G is acylindrically hyperbolic, and if 1/r_0 + 1/r_1 + 1/r_2 < 1, then G is
   hyperbolic." Items (iii) and (iv): half girth types `(3,3,4)` and `(3,4,4)` give property (T)
   for all `p ≥ 7`.
5. **Corollary 7.8**, verbatim: "For every prime p ≥ 7, the groups G_HC2(1)(p), G_HC2(2)(p),
   G_HB2(2)(p) and G_HBC2(2)(p) are infinite hyperbolic groups with property (T). For every prime
   p ≥ 11, the groups G_HB2(3)(p), G_HBC2(3)(p) and G̃_HBC2(3)(p) are infinite hyperbolic groups
   with property (T)." (Sub- and superscripts flattened by the text extraction.)
6. **Theorem 1.3**: "For each odd prime p, the groups G_HC2(1)(p), G_HB2(2)(p), and
   G̃_HBC2(3)(p) are infinite hyperbolic."

**Reading of (i) used downstream.** As in `cckw-ghb2-kms-group-structure` item 3, the stabilizer of
`v_i` is the vertex group `X_i ≤ G` itself (the development of `G(T)`), with the link at `v_i`
identified with `Γ_i`.
