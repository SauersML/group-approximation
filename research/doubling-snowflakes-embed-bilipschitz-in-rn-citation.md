Citation import, not a reproof. A. Naor, O. Neiman, *Assouad's theorem with
dimension independent of the snowflaking*, arXiv:1012.2307 (Rev. Mat.
Iberoam. 28 (2012)). Abstract, read on 2026-09-18, verbatim:

"It is shown that for every K>0 and ε∈(0,1/2) there exist N=N(K)∈N and
D=D(K,ε)∈(1,∞) with the following properties. For every separable metric
space (X,d) with doubling constant at most K, the metric space (X,d^{1−ε})
admits a bi-Lipschitz embedding into R^N with distortion at most D."

"Distortion at most `D`" is read in the standard sense: after rescaling by
some `s > 0`, the map expands distances by at most `D` and contracts them by
at most `1`. Only existence of some finite `N` and `D` is used on this graph,
which is Assouad's original 1983 theorem.
