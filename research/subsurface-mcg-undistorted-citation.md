Citation import. Read on 2026-09-18 from the arXiv PDF, text extracted with `pdftotext`:
K. Rafi and S. Schleimer, *Covers and the curve complex*, arXiv:math/0701719v2, published in
Geom. Topol. 13 (2009) 2141--2162.
- Remark 8.3 is on p. 14. It credits Masur--Minsky, *Geometry of the complex of curves II*
  (GAFA 10 (2000)), Theorems 7.1, 6.10 and 6.12, and Hamenstädt, arXiv:math/0511349, Theorem B and
  Corollary 4.6.
- The definition of cleanly embedded is in Section 3, p. 5.

The same paper proves Theorem 8.1 (an orbifold cover induces a quasi-isometric embedding of mapping
class groups) and Corollary 8.8 (the spherical braid group on `2g+2` strands quasi-isometrically embeds
in the genus-`g` mapping class group). The main node does not use them.
