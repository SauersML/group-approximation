An exact representation with `J=omega` proves `J` has order three.  The
three central Fourier idempotents in `C[<J>]` are therefore nonzero and each
has canonical trace `1/3`.  If `Gamma` were hyperlinear, its canonical group
factor would be CE.  CE passes to nonzero corners, and on `e_omega` the
central element acts as `omega I`.  The normalized corner trace contradicts
the assumed absence of such a CE trace.
