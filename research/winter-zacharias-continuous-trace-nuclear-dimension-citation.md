Wilhelm Winter and Joachim Zacharias,
[*The nuclear dimension of C-star algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4914),
Advances in Mathematics 224 (2010), Corollary 2.10.  The source proves that
for every separable continuous-trace algebra `D`, both its decomposition rank
and nuclear dimension equal the covering dimension of its spectrum.  This
theorem is imported with credit and is not reproved here.
