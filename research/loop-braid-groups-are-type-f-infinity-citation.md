Citation import, not a reproof. Two published statements were read verbatim
from the arXiv PDFs on 2026-09-13.

- M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Symmetric automorphisms of free groups, BNSR-invariants,
  and finiteness properties*, arXiv:1607.03043v1:
  - p. 9: "The groups ΣAut_n and PΣAut_n are of type F∞ (this can be seen for
    example after work of Collins [Col89])".
  - Footnote 4: "Actually, PΣAut_n is even of 'type F', meaning it has a compact
    classifying space".
  - p. 10: "In [Col89], Collins found a contractible simplicial complex ΣK_n on
    which the 'Outer' versions of ΣAut_n and PΣAut_n act properly and
    cocompactly, described by symmetric marked cactus graphs. We will use the
    obvious analog of this complex for our groups."
- S. Witzel and M. C. B. Zaremsky, arXiv:1405.5491v3, p. 55: "The group LB_n is
  known to be of type F∞, for instance it acts properly cocompactly on the
  contractible space of marked cactus graphs [Col89]."

[Col89] is D. J. Collins, *Cohomological dimension and symmetric automorphisms
of a free group*, Comment. Math. Helv. 64 (1989) 44–61. Its theorem numbers were
not checked. A reviewer who wants the primary source should read Collins 1989,
or McCullough–Miller, *Symmetric automorphisms of free products*, Mem. AMS 582
(1996).

The deduction used downstream is Brown's criterion: a group acting properly and
cocompactly on a contractible complex is of type F∞.
