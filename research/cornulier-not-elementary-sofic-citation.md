**Citation.**  Y. Cornulier, *A sofic group away from amenable groups*,
arXiv:0906.3374 (Math. Ann. 2011), read from the PDF on 2026-09-11.

* Pages 2--3 define elementary sofic groups through the four stability
  properties and state Question 5.
* Page 3 gives Lemma 7: a non-amenable finitely generated group all of whose
  finite-index subgroups are isolated and whose amenable quotients are finite
  is not elementary sofic.  It also gives Proposition 8, quoted verbatim in
  the artifact.
* Page 4 contains the remark on Burger--Mozes groups.

The proof of Proposition 8 uses finite discriminability.  An element `x` in
every finite-index subgroup, together with `Z - {1}`, is a discriminating
subset.  Lemma 7 then follows by transfinite induction on the constructive
hierarchy `C_alpha`.
