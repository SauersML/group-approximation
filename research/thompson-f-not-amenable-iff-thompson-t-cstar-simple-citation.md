Citation import, not a reproof. Both statements were read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX
sources, fetched and extracted on MSI.

**A. Le Boudec and N. Matte Bon, arXiv:1605.01651v3** (`subgroupdynamics-arxiv-v3.tex`,
lines 234–241), verbatim up to macros:

> The following statements about Thompson's groups F and T are equivalent:
> (i) The group F is non-amenable; (ii) The group F is C*-simple; (iii) The group T is
> C*-simple.

The theorem is labelled "see also Theorem [thm-T-set-points-stab]", and the ar5iv render
numbers it Theorem 1.6. The authors add (line 243): "while we prove these properties to be
equivalent, we do not elucidate whether these are true or false." Line 232 records a stronger
form: "the existence of at least one overgroup of F inside Homeo(S^1) which is not
C*-simple, would imply that F is amenable".

**U. Haagerup and K. K. Olesen, arXiv:1609.05086** (`main.tex`, lines 810–813), verbatim up to
macros:

> If C*_r(T) is simple, then F is non-amenable.

They note (lines 126–131) that Breuillard–Kalantar–Kennedy–Ozawa re-obtained this result, and
that Le Boudec–Matte Bon re-proved it and showed the converse. The proof (lines 814–891) shows
that the quasi-regular representation `π` on the dyadic orbit is weakly contained in the
regular representation when `F` is amenable. It exhibits commuting `a, b` whose values
`(a(x), b(x))` and `(ab(x), x)` "agree, up to a permutation", so `π(a + b − ab − e) = 0`.

**Scope.** Only the theorem statements and the Haagerup–Olesen proof outline were read. The
Le Boudec–Matte Bon proof was not re-read. Hypotheses: none beyond the definitions of `F`,
`T` and C*-simplicity (simplicity of the reduced group C*-algebra).
