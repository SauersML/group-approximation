**ESTABLISHED FORMAL COUNTEREXAMPLE.**  There are finite-dimensional unital
C-star algebras `A,B`, a ucp complete-order embedding `i:A->B`, and a ucp
retraction `r:B->A` such that

```text
r i=id_A,                                                   (SBR1)
```

but one unitary `u` in a finite unitary generating set of `A` has

```text
d=1-i(u)^*i(u)>0,              r(d)=0.                     (SBR2)
```

Moreover `d` lies in the C-star algebra generated by `i(A)`, and the
restriction of `r` to that generated algebra is itself a surjective
star-homomorphism whose nonzero kernel is the boundary summand containing
`d`.  Thus none of the following formal data forces a split ucp retraction to
be faithful on the ideal generated by multiplicative defects:

1. a finite matrix amplification as the ambient algebra;
2. a complete-order embedding with a ucp inverse on its range;
3. exact splitting `r i=id`;
4. multiplicativity of `r` on `C^*(i(A))`.

Consequently the positive-kernel escape in
`odd-maslov-ucp-retract-must-hide-generator-defect` is formally viable.
Ruling it out for the odd Maslov fibre requires special information about
that fibre and the even target; it cannot follow from generic
operator-system retract or C-star-envelope theory.

The proof is `two-by-two-state-summand-retract-proof`.
