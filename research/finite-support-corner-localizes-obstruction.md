Let `B` be a finitely generated unital star algebra having no
Connes-embeddable tracial state.  Let `Gamma` be any group, let

```text
0 != q=q^*=q^2 in C[Gamma],
```

and suppose that there is a unital star homomorphism

```text
Phi:B -> q C[Gamma] q,             Phi(1_B)=q.             (FSL1)
```

Choose a finite star-generating set `Y` of `B`, and let `Lambda` be the
subgroup of `Gamma` generated by the union of the finite group supports of
`q` and of all `Phi(y)`, `y in Y`.  Then

```text
q in C[Lambda],       Phi(B) subset q C[Lambda] q,          (FSL2)
```

and `Lambda` is finitely generated and nonhyperlinear.  In particular, the
finite presentation of a larger ambient group and all generators outside
these supports play no role in the obstruction.

This has a sharp consequence for signed-Hecke compilers.  A construction
cannot first realize the no-CE BCS atlas inside a support group known to be
hyperlinear and hope that a later ambient extension, Higman wrapper, or
unrelated rigid block will promote it.  The finite set of words actually
occurring in the atlas already generates a nonhyperlinear group.  Thus a
completed block-escaping cycle is not merely a compatibility gadget: among
the finitely many chord-support extensions, some first cumulative support
subgroup must leave the hyperlinear class.

For example, free phase labels, finite sheet transports, and finite
Bass--Serre packets remain impossible even when they are placed inside an
arbitrary nonhyperlinear-looking ambient presentation, as long as the
supports of `q` and of every atlas projection stay in their hyperlinear
subgroup.  To evade this statement one must put an actually load-bearing
nonhyperlinear relation into the finite support of the overlap equations;
ambient decoration cannot supply it.

This support-localization theorem should not be confused with
`finite-support-one-sided-stabilizers-are-torsion`.  The latter is an
elementary obstruction to equations of the form `aq=bq` with literal group
elements: the quotient `b^(-1)a` must permute the finite support of `q` and is
therefore torsion.  Together they say that a rowwise Hecke-CRT construction
must put genuinely multi-piece, load-bearing group-algebra coefficients into
the overlap equations; neither ambient decoration nor literal translations
can supply the missing interpolation.
