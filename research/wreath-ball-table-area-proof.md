Each true radius-`R` multiplication cell has length at most `3R`, so the
HS--Dehn estimate gives `(WBT2)`.  Pointwise finiteness of the Dehn function
allows the standard diagonal choice of `R_n`, proving `(WBT3)` without any
computability assumption.  For a finite quotient cell, its lift is merely in
the quotient kernel; when nontrivial in `G` it has no filling, proving the
sharp separation from `(WBT2)`.
