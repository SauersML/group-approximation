Choose a purely singular charged basis and, for every actor, a
phase-permutation unitary which agrees with the proposed actor in charged
column norm.  Permit arbitrary noncompact mixing, arbitrary propagation,
and order-one operator norm on the complementary charge-null directions.

This architecture is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-charge-null-monomial-perturbations-impossible`.  The state
null ideal and invariance remove all such mixing and return an essentially
free monomial near representation, already impossible by nonsoficity.
