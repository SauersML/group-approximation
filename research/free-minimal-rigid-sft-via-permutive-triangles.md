- **The shift.** By the first claim there is a free minimal SFT that is triangle-permutive in some recoding.
  Quantum rigidity is a conjugacy invariant: it is equivalent to finite presentation of the crossed product,
  which depends only on the system.
- **Rigidity.** The second claim makes that recoding `D`-quantum rigid for every `D >= 1`.
- **Conclusion.** The shift is a quantum rigid free minimal `Z^2`-SFT.

The second claim is established and reviewed; the first is open.
