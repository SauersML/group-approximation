One step in the target is ours rather than Dadarlat's: for large n the
product `∏[πn(āi), πn(b̄i)]` lies within 2 of `1`, so Lemma 2.1 identifies
the winding number with κ. This holds because `∏[āi, b̄i] = 1` in Γ,
`πn(1) = 1`, and πn is asymptotically multiplicative, so πn of a finite
product of group elements and inverses is asymptotically the product of the
πn-values and their adjoints. The det is 1 because the product consists of
commutators.
