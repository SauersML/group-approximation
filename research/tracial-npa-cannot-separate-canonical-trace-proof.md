For each cutoff, set the moment of a word to its canonical trace.  Positivity
of the moment matrix is positivity of `tau_Gamma(q^*q)` for truncated
polynomials `q`; cyclicity and the relator constraints hold in the group von
Neumann algebra.  Hence this is a primal feasible point at every level.

Weak duality makes every sound dual lower bound valid on that point.  A dual
functional negative on the canonical trace would contradict weak duality, so
it cannot come from the standard tracial hierarchy.  The argument is
dimension-free and needs no convergence theorem.
