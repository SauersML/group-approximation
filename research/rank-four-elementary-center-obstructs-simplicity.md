For every unital associative ring L with 2*1_L != 0, the subgroup
{I_4,-I_4} is a nontrivial proper central subgroup of EL_4(L).
Consequently EL_4(L) is not simple, even if L is simple.

This applies to every unital ring containing an integral group ring.
It invalidates the step in the original Boone--Higman ring route claiming
that ring simplicity and the normal-generator calculation make EL_4(L)
simple. A normal generator can generate a group with nontrivial center.

The statement does not refute a route through a projective quotient. Such a
route must actually prove simplicity and finite presentation of that
quotient; these are not provided by the exhibited central involution.
