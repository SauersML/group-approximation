## Proof

**Reduction to monomials.**  `TS = 1` lets every word in `S,T` be
rewritten as `S^i T^j` with `i,j >= 0`, so those monomials span `J_C`.

**Faithfulness and independence together.**  Suppose the finite sum
`a = sum_(i,j) c_(ij) S^i T^j` acts as zero on `C^((N))`.  Fix a diagonal
offset `h in Z`.  For `l >= max(0,-h)`, the coefficient of the output
basis vector `v_(l+h)` in `a v_l` is

```text
sum_(max(0,-h) <= j <= l) c_(j+h, j).                              (1)
```

Every such partial sum vanishes.  Taking consecutive differences, with
the empty initial partial sum equal to zero, gives `c_(j+h,j) = 0` for
every admissible `j`.  Varying `h` over all offsets gives `a = 0`.  So
the representation is faithful, and since a spanning set with no nonzero
relation is a basis, the monomials form a `C`-basis.

**Matrix units.**  `QS = 0` and `TQ = 0` because `Q = 1 - ST` and
`TS = 1`.  Hence

```text
Q_(ij)Q_(kl) = S^i Q T^j S^k Q T^l = delta_(jk) S^i Q T^l,
```

the off-diagonal cases dying on `QS = 0` or `TQ = 0` after cancelling
`T^jS^k` down to a one-sided power.  In the faithful representation
`Q_(ij)` is the matrix unit `E_(ij)`, so the span of the `Q_(ij)` is the
set of finite-support matrices, an ideal `F_C = M_fin(N,C)`.

**Symbol sequence.**  Killing `F_C` kills `Q`, making `S` and `T`
mutually inverse, so the quotient is generated by an invertible element
`z` with no further relation, by the basis statement.  This is `(JSF2)`.

**Coefficient extraction.**  Both sides of `(JSF3)` act on the basis in
the same way: `Q` is the projection onto `Cv_0`, `T^i` moves `v_i` to
`v_0`, and `S^j` moves `v_0` to `v_j`.  So `Q T^i a S^j Q` acts as
multiplication by the `(i,j)` coefficient of `a` on `Cv_0` and as zero
elsewhere, which is the action of `cQ`.  Faithfulness upgrades the
equality of actions to an equality in `J_C`.
