For the corner map, Leavitt cancellation gives

```text
alpha_i(r)alpha_i(q)
 =s_i r(t_i s_i)q t_i=s_i rq t_i,
t_i alpha_i(r)s_i=r.                                    (HSP1)
```

So it is multiplicative and injective.  Applied to elementary matrices,
the formula `g |-> I+alpha_i(g-I)` is a homomorphism and is injective by
the second identity in `(HSP1)`.  Its value on the common root is
`x_13(e_i)`.

Since `A=e_i+e_(1-i)` and the two coefficients are orthogonal, root
additivity gives

```text
x_13(A)=x_13(e_i)x_13(e_(1-i)).                         (HSP2)
```

Thus identifying `x_13(e_i)` with `J` kills the complementary root.  Weyl
root permutations first propagate this equality to the same coefficient in
every root.  The Steinberg relation

```text
[x_ab(r),x_bc(q)]=x_ac(rq)                              (HSP3)

then kills every coefficient in the two-sided ideal generated by
`e_(1-i)`.  Equation `(HSD5)` places `A` in that ideal, proving `J=1`.
Britton's lemma cannot rescue the mark: it embeds the base only when the HNN
map is used without the extra incompatible fixed-sign quotient.

For the diagonal map, orthogonality gives

```text
delta(r)delta(q)
 =sum_(i,j)s_i r(t_i s_j)q t_j
 =sum_i s_i rq t_i=delta(rq),                           (HSP4)
```

and `delta(A)=e_0+e_1=A`.  Applying `t_i(-)s_i` to `(HSP4)` recovers `r`,
so `delta` is injective.  Iterating `(HSP4)` proves `(HSD7)`.  The same word
appears on both sides of every summand; no iteration produces `u!=v`.

Finally,

```text
beta_(u,v)(r)beta_(u,v)(q)
 =s_u r(t_v s_u)q t_v=0                                (HSP5)
```

for incomparable `u,v`, while taking `r=q=A` makes
`beta_(u,v)(rq)=s_u t_v!=0`.  This proves that the cross-corner transport
needed for the dual-prefix table is not a ring homomorphism.

An ascending HNN presentation over a finitely generated group needs only
relations on its finite generating set, and conjugating a base relator costs
no additional 2-cells.  Therefore uniform area is not the failed step.  The
failed step is earlier and algebraic: no one canonical Leavitt endomorphism
has all three required properties

```text
fix J,       retain J!=1,       address every prefix cross-corner. (HSP6)
```
