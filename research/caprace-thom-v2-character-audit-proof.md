Corollary 5.3 of arXiv:2606.27993v2 classifies invariant random subgroups.
Remark 5.4 then states that absence of nontrivial characters merely "seems
plausible" and explains that the proposed application of
Creutz--Peterson requires square integrability of a projected lattice, which
has not been checked.  The v2 source is explicitly a complete rewrite of the
earlier sole-author version.  Hence the removed v1 abstract cannot be used as
a current character-rigidity theorem.
