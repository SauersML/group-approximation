Restrict any norm-corona homomorphism of `Delta` to the literal subgroup
`B_Delta`.  The first prerequisite kills `z` on that restriction.  Since the
same element normally generates `Delta`, the original homomorphism is
trivial.  Thus the MF radical is the whole Steinberg group.
