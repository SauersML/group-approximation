J. Castillejos, S. Evington, A. Tikuisis, S. White, *Uniform property Gamma*,
arXiv:1912.04207 (Int. Math. Res. Not. 2022).  Theorem A, read from the arXiv
PDF text on 2026-09-12 (introduction, page 3): the Toms--Winter conjecture
holds among separable, simple, unital, nuclear, non-elementary C*-algebras with
uniform property Gamma.  The implications from Z-stability to strict comparison
and to finite nuclear dimension are unconditional in this class; the new
content is strict comparison plus uniform Gamma implies Z-stability.
