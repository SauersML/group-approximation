For a finitely generated group `G`:

- if `G` has solvable word problem, `boone-higman-conjecture` embeds `G` in a
  finitely presented simple group;
- if `G` embeds in a finitely presented simple group, then
  `subgroups-of-fp-simple-groups-have-solvable-wp` says `G` has solvable word
  problem.

Together these are the "iff" of Problem 2.14, so its answer is yes.
