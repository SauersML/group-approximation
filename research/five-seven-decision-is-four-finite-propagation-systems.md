After `C_3` Fourier compression and multiplication by corner units, the
unique unresolved `(5,7)` profile consists of exactly four systems:

1. `(e+Q_y)Q_u=e`, for `Q_v=e[v]e` (the three-base-term four-cycle);
2. a factor `e+Q_y` against a partner consisting of a nonzero `F_4` scalar,
   one four-cycle `aQ_ub`, and one normalizer monomial (the `4+2` type);
3. the same factor against a nonzero base scalar plus one alternating
   six-edge incidence operator (the 6-cycle type);
4. a three-monomial by four-monomial inverse equation in the fixed `F_4`
   crossed product of the projective semilinear normalizer quotient.

There are no other support or coefficient geometries.  In item 1 the desired
reverse product is `Q_u(e+Q_y)=e`; the forward product says that the
equal-degree `C_3`-coset incidence operator `Q_u` has a finite-propagation
left inverse.  The other three systems are its multi-transport analogues.
Finite support and equality of local fiber dimensions alone do not turn
injectivity into surjectivity on an arbitrary nonamenable coset graph.  A
soficity or amenability theorem for each support subgroup would finish the
reverse products, but no such theorem is presently available here.
