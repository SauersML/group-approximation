Source: P. Ara and K. R. Goodearl, *Tame and wild refinement monoids*,
arXiv:1405.7582v2 (13 Oct 2014).  Read from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-12.

Verbatim, Definition 2.1: "Let M be a refinement monoid. We say that M is tame
in case M is an inductive limit for some inductive system of finitely
generated refinement monoids, and that M is wild otherwise."

Verbatim, Theorem 2.3: "Every tame refinement monoid is separative,
unperforated, and satisfies the Riesz interpolation property."  Proof in the
source: the properties pass to inductive limits, and hold for finitely
generated refinement monoids by Brookfield's results.

Examples 2.2 (items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6) list the tame classes quoted in the claim;
item 5 says the graph monoids "have refinement ... and we prove below that
they are tame (Theorem 3.1)", and item 6 attributes tameness of primely
generated conical refinement monoids to Ara--Pardo, Theorem 0.1.  Page 6,
after Theorem 2.3, records embeddings of arbitrary monoids into refinement
monoids as order-embeddings, so non-separative monoids give wild refinement
monoids.  Page 1: "If R is a (von Neumann) regular ring or a C\*-algebra with
real rank zero (more generally, an exchange ring), then V(R) is a refinement
monoid."
