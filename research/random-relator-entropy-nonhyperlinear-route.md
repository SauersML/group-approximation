The established finite-moment sparsification theorem removes the old
all-dimensions metric-entropy gate: for a fixed finite check menu, every
normalized-HS quantity used by the microstate test factors through one compact
finite trace-profile space with a dimension-independent finite net.

`robust-locally-satisfiable-relator-code` supplies a finite check menu with a
constant unitary/tracial soundness fraction, while random sparse submenus retain
a prescribed nontrivial short group ball often enough to beat the finite net
union bound.  Thus some sparse presentation preserves the test ball yet admits
no separated sufficiently accurate normalized-HS microstate in any matrix
size.  `explicit-random-relator-witness-extraction` then names one concrete
successful submenu and records its finite group-theoretic and quantitative
certificate, satisfying the root's word **explicit**.

This lane uses no property-(T), HS-stability-to-exact-representation theorem,
commutant reconstruction, Leavitt/Pauli/PVM decoder, Cartan recovery, soficity
input, or nonlocal game as the final witness.  The live mathematical gate is
now a finite PCP/BCS/LCS-style groupification problem rather than an ambient
`U(n)^k` entropy problem.
