The open entropy/return theorem supplies a finite random-relator model whose
summable return bound is exactly the hypothesis of
`random-relator-net-killing-criterion`.  The positive-probability preservation
event keeps a fixed short free ball distinct, while the complementary union
bound rules out an accurate normalized-HS model of that finite ball in every
matrix dimension.  `explicit-random-relator-witness-extraction` then names one
concrete relator tuple and carries the quantitative certificate required by the
root's word **explicit**.

This lane has no property-(T), no HS-stability-to-exact-representation step, no
commutant reconstruction, no Leavitt/Pauli/PVM decoder, no soficity input and no
nonlocal game.  Its live gates are the representation-stratum entropy theorem
and effective extraction of one fixed finite presentation.
