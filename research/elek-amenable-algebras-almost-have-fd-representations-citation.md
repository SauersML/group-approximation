Read verbatim from the arXiv TeX sources on MSI (`e-print` tarballs, files `aracikk.tex` and `rowen.tex`).

- **G. Elek, "On algebras that almost have finite dimensional representations", arXiv:math/0311376.**
  - (E1) is the Proposition immediately following the definition of amenability in the "Examples" section.
  - (E2) is Proposition 4.1, "RR(A) is an ideal".
  - (E3) is Theorem 2, "If A is a simple algebra almost having finite dimensional representations then it is
    stably finite."
- **G. Elek, "The amenability of affine algebras", arXiv:math/0203261**, J. Algebra 264 (2003) 469–478.
  - (E4) is Proposition 4.1, "Any affine algebra of subexponential growth is amenable."
  - (E5) is Theorem 2, stated there for "affine algebras `R` without zero-divisors".

The quoted definitions and statements appear in §1 of the artifact.
