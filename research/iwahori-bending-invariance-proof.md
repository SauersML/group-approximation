The first-vertex commutant fixes the common edge pointwise.  Conjugation by
such a unitary therefore changes the second vertex while leaving its edge
restriction unchanged, which is the standard bending operation for an
amalgamated free product.  The square-root and central-word identities are
just the three edge-generator equalities written in the `X,Y` coordinates.
