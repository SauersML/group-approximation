Citation import. M. Adamaszek, F. Frick and A. Vakili, "On homotopy types of
Euclidean Rips complexes", arXiv:1602.04131v1, pp. 2–3; Discrete Comput. Geom.
58 (2017) 526–542. Read from the PDF on 2026-09-13:

> Theorem II. For every finite simplicial complex `K` that is PL-embeddable
> in `R^n` there exists a finite subset `X ⊆ R^n` with a homotopy equivalence
> `R(X) ≃ K`.

> Theorem III. For every finitely presented group `G` there exists a finite
> subset `X ⊆ R^4` such that `π_1(R(X)) = G`.

The claim's remark that `RP^2` gives a non-wedge Rips complex in `R^4` uses
only Theorem II and the standard fact that `RP^2` embeds PL in `R^4`.
