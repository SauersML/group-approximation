Ozawa, *Proximality and selflessness for group C-star-algebras*,
[arXiv:2508.07938v7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07938), Theorem 11, states
that the tensor product of separable completely selfless C-star-probability
spaces is completely selfless.

For discrete groups, the canonical identification

```text
C*_r(G x H) = C*_r(G) tensor_min C*_r(H)
```

preserves the canonical traces, giving the stated group consequence.
