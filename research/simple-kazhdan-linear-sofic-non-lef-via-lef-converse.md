This route is a repair (lane sk-subgroups, 2026-09-13) of `simple-kazhdan-linear-sofic-non-lef-via-abels-prufer-subflow`.
- That route's step 6 used `diag(u_γ, u_γ^(-1), 1)` as a homomorphism on the nonabelian `Γ_p`, which fails
  (`whitehead-diagonal-map-is-not-multiplicative`).
- Steps 1–5 below are that route's, unchanged (artifact `un-sofic-not-lef` Section 4). Steps 6–7 are new.

1. **The group and the subflow.**
   - `Γ_p` is finitely presented and amenable.
   - It has a central `c_p ≠ e` that every finite-dimensional unitary representation kills. Regular representations of
     finite quotients kill it, so `Γ_p` is not residually finite.
   - So `Γ_p` is not LEF, by `finitely-presented-lef-groups-are-residually-finite`.
   - Gao–Jackson–Seward give a free minimal Cantor subflow `X ⊆ 2^(Γ_p)`.
2. **The ring.** `R = LC(X,F_q) ⋊ Γ_p` is countable. It is generated by `1_([x_e = 1])`, a generator of `F_q`, and the
   `u_s`. So `EL_N(R)`, `N >= 3`, is finitely generated.
3. **Simplicity.**
   - `R` is the Steinberg algebra of a Hausdorff ample principal minimal groupoid with compact infinite unit space.
   - So `R` is simple, and `S_N` is infinite and simple with scalar centre.
4. **(T).** Ershov–Jaikin-Zapirain applies to the finitely generated ring `R`, and (T) passes to the quotient.
5. **Linear soficity.**
   - Theorem F gives a unital rank model over `F_q`.
   - `R` is simple with centre `F_q`, so the adjoint theorem makes `S_N` `F_q`-linear sofic.
6. **Not LEF, `N >= 4`.** Suppose `EL_N(R)` is LEF.
   - Then `R` is a LEF ring (`lef-elementary-groups-force-lef-rings`).
   - `R` is countable and simple, so it embeds unitally in a matrix ultraproduct over finite fields
     (`simple-lef-rings-are-exactly-matricial`).
   - So `GL_1(R) = R^×` is LEF (`exactly-matricial-rings-have-lef-general-linear-groups` with `N = 1`).
   - `γ ↦ u_γ` is an injective homomorphism `Γ_p -> R^×`: injective because the representation `Σ_γ f_γ u_γ` is unique.
   - Subgroups of LEF groups are LEF, so `Γ_p` is LEF, contradicting step 1.
7. **Trivial centre over `F_2`.** Take `q = 2`.
   - A central element of `EL_N(R)` commutes with every `e_ij(1)`, so it is `cI` with `c ∈ R`.
   - Commuting with every `e_ij(r)` gives `c ∈ Z(R)^×`, and `Z(R) = F_2` (`minimal-effective-steinberg-algebras-have-scalar-centre`).
   - So `c = 1`, `S_N = EL_N(R)`, and `S_N` is not LEF for `N >= 4`.
