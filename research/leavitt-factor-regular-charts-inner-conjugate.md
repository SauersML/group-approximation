title: The two regular A8 chart embeddings are internally conjugate in the Leavitt group factor
---

Let `Q = L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` and let `K_1,K_2 <= Q` be the two `A_8` chart
subgroups in `leavitt-regular-atlas-hyperlinearity-criterion`, with their
fixed label-preserving identifications with `A_8`.  In the group von Neumann
algebra `L(Q)` with its canonical trace, there is a unitary `W in L(Q)` such
that

```text
W lambda(k_1(h)) W^* = lambda(k_2(h))
```

for every `h in A_8`.

The point is that the exact regular tracial model does not merely contain the
two chart algebras abstractly: after choosing the prescribed chart labels it
contains an **internal relative chart unitary**.  This is a boundary condition
on every proposed universal coefficient identity in the relative-unitary
variable.
