**EXACT SYNTACTIC COMPILER AND TRACE/SOFICITY FIREWALL; NO
NONHYPERLINEAR GROUP IS CLAIMED.**

For a unital ring \(A\) and \(n\geq3\), write

\[
 x_{ij}(a)=I+aE_{ij}.
\]

The elementary identities

\[
\begin{aligned}
 x_{ij}(a)x_{ij}(b)&=x_{ij}(a+b),\\
 [x_{ij}(a),x_{jk}(b)]&=x_{ik}(ab)\quad(i,j,k\ {\rm distinct}),\\
 x_{ij}(a)=x_{ij}(b)&\Longleftrightarrow a=b
\end{aligned}
\tag{SUT1}
\]

show that a finite table of Hecke sums, products, and equalities can be
encoded by finitely many ordinary group words.  If \(p=p^*=p^2\), the
Whitehead word

\[
 W_p=x_{12}(p)x_{21}(-p)x_{12}(p)
 =\begin{pmatrix}1-p&p\\-p&1-p\end{pmatrix}
\tag{SUT2}
\]

satisfies

\[
 W_p^*=W_p^{-1},\qquad
 W_p^2=\operatorname{diag}(1-2p,1-2p).
\tag{SUT3}
\]

Thus the involution \(1-2p\) also has an exact group-matrix wordization.

This algebraic success does **not** transport a non-Connes-embeddable trace.

First, if \(\pi:A\to M\) is a unital star representation into a complex
finite von Neumann algebra and \(\pi(p)\neq0\), then

\[
 I+\pi(p)E_{12}
\tag{SUT4}
\]

is not unitary.  Hence the coefficient representation which carries the
desired BCS trace is not a unitary representation of the elementary-root
group.  The composite word \(W_p\) is unitary, but its elementary factors
are not.  Passing to characteristic \(\ell\) makes the roots have finite
order, but a nonzero complex unital star algebra admits no unital
characteristic-\(\ell\) coefficient representation.

Second, every split ordinary-group presentation made from external context
groups and elementary roots has a root-killing retraction whenever all
mixed relators are homogeneous in the root normal subgroup:

\[
 \varepsilon(\text{external generator})=\text{itself},
 \qquad
 \varepsilon(x_{ij}(a))=1.
\tag{SUT5}
\]

Steinberg addition, multiplication commutators, Weyl conjugacy, covariance,
and equations \(x_{ij}(a)=x_{ij}(b)\) all become identities after (SUT5).
Consequently the presentation retains every external bad sector with all
coefficient tests trivial.  In particular, adjoining the words (SUT1) for
the Heisenberg predicate projections from
central-block-hecke-predicates-force-affine-group-spectra
does not authenticate those projections in an arbitrary unitary
representation.

Third, pure finite-coefficient unitization is already on the hyperlinear
side.  Let \(K\) be finite and let \(\mathcal O\) be a finitely generated
subring of a number field containing the coefficients of the finite
signed-Hecke menu.  Left regular multiplication embeds

\[
 \mathcal O[K]\hookrightarrow M_{|K|}(\mathcal O).
\tag{SUT6}
\]

Therefore every finitely generated subgroup of
\(\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathcal O[K])\), including the group generated by the
finite packet, the roots in (SUT1), and the Whitehead words in (SUT2), is a
finitely generated characteristic-zero linear group.  It is residually
finite and hence hyperlinear.  The same conclusion holds for finite graphs
of such local groups glued over finite subgroups, and more generally for
the already audited amenable-edge hyperlinear amalgams.

It follows that no such local matrix group can have a positive canonical
finite-subgroup block corner carrying a unital copy of a BCS algebra with
no Connes-embeddable tracial state: normalized canonical corner trace would
pull back to a Connes-embeddable trace on the BCS algebra.

The boundary is sharp.  A positive Steinberg reduction must add a
load-bearing relation which destroys (SUT5) and, at the same time, proves
that the chosen non-CE coefficient trace extends to a **unitary** group
trace.  Identifying more root words, or replacing \(x_{12}(p)\) by the
unitary composite \(W_p\), does neither.  Equivalently, the missing theorem
is not finite ring wordization; it is canonical-trace reflection of the
coefficient algebra through the common root kernel.
