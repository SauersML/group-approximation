Direct proof (2026-09-13, lane `bh-solvable-general`). Not independently reviewed.
`B_A` is closed under subgroups by definition.

## (a)

- **Lamps.** `(Z/m)^r` is in `B_A`.
  - For `m = 0` it is `Z^r`, the diagonal subgroup of `GL_r(Q)` generated by the
    matrices `diag(1, ..., 2, ..., 1)`.
  - For `m >= 1` it is finite. Its left regular representation by permutation
    matrices embeds it in `GL_(m^r)(Q)`.

  Both are finitely generated linear groups in characteristic zero
  (`char-zero-linear-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`).
- **Top group.** By hypothesis, `G = F/N ∈ B_A`.
- **Wreath product.** `G` acts on `X = G` by left translation, with trivial point
  stabilizers. By `permutational-boone-higman-closed-under-wreath-products`, the
  restricted regular wreath product `(Z/m)^r wr G` is in `B_A`.
- **Embedding.** By `magnus-embedding-mod-m-into-wreath-product`, `F/[N,N]N^m`
  embeds in `(Z/m)^r wr G`, so it is in `B_A`.

## (b)

Induct on `d`. For `d = 0`, `F/N_0` is trivial.

- `N_k = [N_(k-1), N_(k-1)] N_(k-1)^(m_k)` is generated by values of words on
  `N_(k-1)`. So it is invariant under every automorphism of `N_(k-1)` and in
  particular characteristic in `N_(k-1)`.
- By induction `N_(k-1)` is normal in `F`, so `N_k` is normal in `F`.
- (a) with `N = N_(k-1)` and `m = m_k` gives `F/N_k ∈ B_A` from `F/N_(k-1) ∈ B_A`.

## (c)

For a variety `U` and a group `H`, `U(H)` is the verbal subgroup: the subgroup
generated by all values on `H` of the laws of `U`.
- It is monotone: `K ≤ H` implies `U(K) ≤ U(H)`.
- Homomorphisms map `U(H)` into `U` of the image.
- For `U = A_m`, the laws are `[x, y]` and `x^m`, so `A_m(H) = [H,H]H^m`.

**Membership.** Induct on `d`; the case `d = 0` is trivial.
- `F/N_(k-1)` lies in `A_(m_(k-1)) ... A_(m_1)`.
- `N_(k-1)/N_k = N_(k-1)/[N_(k-1),N_(k-1)]N_(k-1)^(m_k)` is abelian of exponent
  dividing `m_k`.
- So `F/N_k` lies in `A_(m_k) A_(m_(k-1)) ... A_(m_1)`.

**Freeness.** Let `E` be in `A_(m_d) ... A_(m_1)` and let `f : F -> E` be a
homomorphism. We show `f(N_d) = 1`, again by induction on `d`.
- Choose `K` normal in `E`, with `K ∈ A_(m_d)` and `E/K ∈ A_(m_(d-1)) ... A_(m_1)`.
- Apply the induction hypothesis to `F -> E/K`. This gives `f(N_(d-1)) ≤ K`.
- So `f(N_d) = f(A_(m_d)(N_(d-1))) ≤ A_(m_d)(f(N_(d-1))) ≤ A_(m_d)(K) = 1`.

So `F/N_d` is free of rank `r` in that variety, on the images of `x_1, ..., x_r`.

## Consequence

By `type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups` (Zaremsky, Corollary B), every
subgroup of `F/N_d` embeds in a finitely presented simple group and, if finitely
generated, has solvable word problem. `∎`
