For q>=2, N>=3, t=e_12(1) in E_N(Z/q), and every complex unitary
representation sigma,

    dim((Fix sigma(E_N(Z/q)))^perp)<=4 rank(sigma(t)-I).

In R_q=Z<s,t | ts=1, q(1-st)=0>, the defect ideal is M_fin(Z/q).
Its finitary determinant-one group A=E_fin(Z/q), viewed on four
copies of the shift module, embeds as a normal locally finite
subgroup of E_4(R_q). The root e_13(1-st) is a standard transvection
of A, of order q. These assertions require no full-idempotent
hypothesis and no description of the symbol quotient.
