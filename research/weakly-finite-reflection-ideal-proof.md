Artifact Section 1, Theorem 1.

1. **Quotient is weakly finite.** If `AB ≡ I_n` modulo `J_omega`, the finitely many entries of `AB - I_n`
   lie in some `J_k`, so the entries of `BA - I_n` lie in `J_(k+1)`.
2. **Universality.** By induction on `k`, a homomorphism `phi` into a weakly finite ring kills `J_k`: a
   `J_k`-pair maps to an exact pair `phi(A) phi(B) = I_n`, so `phi(B) phi(A) = I_n`, and `phi` kills the entries
   of `BA - I_n`.
3. **Detection.** By 1 and 2, a nonzero weakly finite image exists iff `A / J_omega != 0`. Membership
   `1 ∈ J_k` unwinds into finitely many generators at finitely many stages.
4. **Stage one.** `J_1` is generated by defects of exact pairs. If it is zero, then `Phi(0) = 0`, and every
   stage vanishes. QED
