**ESTABLISHED OBSTRUCTION.**  Let `(M,tau)` be a finite tracial von Neumann
algebra, let `A` be the abelian algebra generated by the readable controls,
and let `B` be the algebra generated by the unreadable observables.  On
`L^2(M,tau)` write

```text
L(x) xi = x xi,                 R(y) xi = xi y.
```

The left and right actions commute, so replacing a readable projection
`p in A` by `R(p)` makes it commute with every left unreadable observable,
and for the trace vector `Omega=1`,

```text
<L(x)R(p)Omega,Omega> = tau(xp).                       (SFR1)
```

Every acceptance expression containing one separated readable cut therefore
has the same scalar expectation after the switch.  That much is valid.

It does not produce the two commuting player algebras a commuting-operator
strategy requires.  In the original tracial strategy the two player algebras
are generated by left and right copies of the *same* observables; after the
proposed switch the natural mixed player algebras are

```text
M_A = W*(L(B),R(A)),             M_B = W*(R(B),L(A)).  (SFR2)
```

These commute if and only if `A` commutes with `B` in `M`.  Their cross
commutators include

```text
[L(b),L(a)] = L([b,a]),          [R(a),R(b)] = R([b,a])
```

for `a in A`, `b in B`, and faithfulness of `L` and `R` gives the reverse
implication.  So the side switch is a valid two-player construction exactly
when the readable controls already commute with every unreadable observable
-- which is the global-invariance/frozen-fibre hypothesis the shortcut was
meant to supply.

Using `R(A)` for both players does not help: it still fails to commute with
the right copy of `B`.  Switching both unreadable copies to the left has the
symmetric failure.  Tensoring an independent copy restores commutation but
replaces the correlated quantity `tau(xp)` in `(SFR1)` by a product of
marginals, so it does not preserve perfect controlled acceptance.

The obstruction occurs already in `M_2(C)`, it is visible in the canonical
vector state alone, and it is exactly the remote-control obstruction in the
finite magic-square selector.  The three calculations are in
`standard-form-right-copy-no-freezing-proof`, and are replayed exactly by
`experiments/standard_form_right_copy_no_freezing.py`.

## Consequence for the live gate

Standard form supplies a correlation-preserving **one-side** commutant copy;
it does not broadcast the readable algebra to a globally commuting
two-player system.  The route `zpc-readable-control-freezing-to-lcs`
therefore still needs one of its existing substantive inputs:

- a positive readable atom invariant under the unreadable action;
- an orbitwise constant frozen equation menu; or
- a different compiler which pays for remote atom motion.

This rules out a shortcut.  It does not prove that a nonhyperlinear group
exists, and it says nothing about whether that claim's remaining inputs hold.
