**Setting.** `G` is any group. `tau: (T^d)^G -> (T^d)^G` is injective, continuous and
equivariant, with degree `D in M_d(Z[G])`. Lemmas A and B of
`research/artifacts/lef-torus-small-fibre-2026-09-18.md` give finite `W, F ⊆ G`: `W` a
modulus set for `tau^-1`, and `F` the memory of a close approximation `mu'` of
`ev_1 o tau` with the same degree data.

Let `Gamma_(W,F)` be generated by disjoint copies of `W` and `F`, with the relators
`w f f'^-1 w'^-1` (whenever `wf = w'f'` in `G`) and `w f` (whenever `wf = 1`). Let
`N_(W,F)` be the kernel of `Gamma_(W,F) -> <W ∪ F> ≤ G`. `N` is *co-sofic* when finite
`Gamma`-sets exist whose fixed-point proportions tend to `1_N` pointwise.

**Claim.**
1. **(Theorem E.)** If `N_(W,F)` is co-sofic, then `xi -> xi D` is injective on
   `Q[G]^d`, so `tau` is surjective. `G` need not be sofic.
2. **(Stable covers.)** If `<W ∪ F>` is sofic and `Gamma_(W,F)` is permutation stable,
   then `N_(W,F)` is co-sofic. Examples of stable covers are finite, free, f.g. abelian
   and polycyclic-by-finite groups.
3. **(Square models, Proposition B.)** A finite set with permutations for `W ⊔ F` in
   which every vertex carries the pull-back certificate is exactly a finite
   `Gamma_(W,F)`-set. Moreover, `N_(W,F)` is co-sofic for every finite pair `(W, F)`
   if and only if `G` is LEF.

**Obstruction (class kill).** Item 3 bounds every method that certifies all vertices
of a finite model and uses nothing of `tau` beyond `(W, F)`. Such methods include:
- dropping the surplus outputs;
- rewriting the defect rows;
- adding registers;
- per-vertex moduli;
- choosing a special sofic approximation.

Uniformly over automata, all of them prove C2 exactly for LEF groups.
- **Invariant.** Exact satisfaction of `R_(W,F)` at every vertex.
- **Dying step.** The well-definedness of the pulled-back configuration
  `x_(wf) = u_(s(f)^-1 s(w)^-1 v)` at a vertex where a relator fails.

Beyond LEF, a proof must either use which `(W, F)` occur for automata of non-regular
degree, or tolerate defects, as in
`local-rectangular-small-fibre-maps-lose-proportional-rank`.
