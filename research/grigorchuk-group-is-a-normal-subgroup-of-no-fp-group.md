**Statement.** No finitely presented group has a normal subgroup isomorphic to the first
Grigorchuk group `𝔊`.

The first Grigorchuk group does embed in finitely presented groups (for instance the ascending
HNN extension `grigorchuk-ascending-hnn-extension-is-finitely-presented`), but never as a normal
subgroup. The fact was communicated by M. Sapir, as reported in Benli, arXiv:1112.1764, Remark 2
(no proof given there). This node supplies a proof; no priority is claimed. Proof:
`grigorchuk-group-not-normal-in-fp-via-out-proof`.
