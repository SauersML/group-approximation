Use `(GMC2)` to telescope each literal seventh-power relation through its
seven moving cuts.  The stagewise typing in `(GMC3)` keeps one multiplicity
involution throughout the cycle, so odd order forces both row gauges to the
identity.  On the shared positive initial cut the actual native transports
are therefore asymptotic to the two common-source Hecke maps.  Their
cross-Gram has fixed positive mass, and the mixed Pauli commutators are
preserved.  This gives the arbitrary-profile EL20 native cross-Gram without
forming the impossible common reducing Hecke subcut.
