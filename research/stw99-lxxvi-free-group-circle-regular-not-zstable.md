The separable, exact, nonnuclear, non-simple algebra

```text
C(T) tensor_min C*_r(F_2)
```

is pure and Cuntz semigroup regular, but it does not absorb the Jiang--Su
algebra `Z`.

Thus the compact `K_1` monodromy in a circle field does not force
Cu-irregularity, and Cu-regularity remains strictly weaker than Z-stability
in this non-simple exact class.
