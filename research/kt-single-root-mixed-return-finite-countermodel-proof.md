Fix `N>=3` and put `R_N=F_q[(Z/NZ)^3]`.  Reduction of Laurent
exponents modulo `N` is equivariant for
`SL_3(Z)->SL_3(Z/NZ)`.  It therefore gives a finite quotient of the pinned
actor

```text
theta_N:G -> K_N <= EL_r(R_N) rtimes SL_3(Z/NZ).
```

Write `R=theta_N(r)` and `H=theta_N(h)`.  In the `(1,2)` root subgroup their
coefficients are respectively the distinct group-algebra basis vectors
`x^(1,0,0)` and `x^(-1,-1,-1)`.  Moreover

```text
<R>={e_12(k x^(1,0,0)): k in F_p},
```

so `H` is not in `<R>`.  The same conclusion holds after replacing `K_N` by
the image of `theta_N`.

Let `X_N=K_N/<R>` and form the finite generalized wreath product

```text
W_N=(direct_sum_(X_N) C_2) rtimes K_N.
```

Let `a` be the lamp at the base coset `<R>`.  Then `[a,R]=1`, while
`b=HaH^(-1)` is the distinct lamp at `H<R>`.  In the left regular
representation of `W_N` all actor and covariance relations are exact.  The
two commuting independent involutions have trace-zero product, hence, for
`P=(1+a)/2` and `Q=(1+b)/2`,

```text
||P-Q||_2^2=tr(P)+tr(Q)-2tr(PQ)=1/2.
```

Finally let `F` be any finite actor window.  Residual finiteness of the pinned
actor `G` supplies a finite quotient separating every nonidentity member of
`F`.  Replace `theta_N` by its diagonal product with that quotient and repeat
the construction using the cyclic subgroup generated by the image of `r`.
The first coordinate still proves that the image of `h` is outside that
cyclic subgroup, while the second makes every prescribed nonidentity actor
word have zero regular trace.  This proves both the exact countermodel and
the finite-window trace assertion.
