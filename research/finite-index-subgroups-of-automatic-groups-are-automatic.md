title: A subgroup of finite index in an automatic group is automatic
---

If `G` is automatic and `H ≤ G` has finite index, then `H` is automatic. (`H` is
finitely generated, being of finite index in a finitely generated group.)

ECHLPT, *Word Processing in Groups* (1992), as summarised by Rees. Citation route:
`finite-index-automatic-subgroups-citation`.
