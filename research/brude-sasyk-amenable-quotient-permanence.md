Source: Javier Brude and Roman Sasyk, *Metric approximations of unrestricted
wreath products when the acting group is amenable*, Communications in Algebra
50 (2022), no. 3, 949--961,
doi:10.1080/00927872.2021.1976790, arXiv:2004.05735v4.

Corollary 1.2 is exactly the target statement. Its proof is equivalent to
Theorem 1.1 plus the Kaloujnine--Krasner embedding. Theorem 1.1 says that, for
amenable `A`, the unrestricted wreath product

```text
N wrwr A = (product_A N) semidirect_product A
```

inherits weak soficity, soficity, linear soficity, or hyperlinearity from
`N`, respectively. Kaloujnine--Krasner embeds every extension of `N` by
`A` into this unrestricted wreath product. Each of the four classes passes
to subgroups, so the extension inherits the property.

For linear soficity the field is fixed throughout the corresponding clause.
No finite generation, splitting, residual finiteness, or hypothesis on the
extension action is imposed.

Status: peer-reviewed and published. The final arXiv version and published
statement were checked 2026-08-31.
