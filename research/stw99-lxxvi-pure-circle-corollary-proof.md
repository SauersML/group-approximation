Let `D` be simple, separable, stably finite, and pure.  Lin's theorem gives
stable rank one, while STW Proposition 23 says that the actual first-factor
map `Cu(D)->Cu(D tensor Z)` is an isomorphism.  The hypotheses of
`stw99-lxxvi-circle-formation-preserves-cu-regularity` are therefore
satisfied, and its conclusion is exactly Cu-regularity of `C(T,D)`.
