## The ring

- `Γ = SL_3(Z)`, `Γ(d)` the principal congruence subgroups, `Γ_n = Γ((n+1)!)`.
- `K = lim Γ/Γ_n`, the congruence odometer; `Γ` acts freely and minimally by left multiplication.
- **The Toeplitz subshift.** Cecchi Bernales–Cortez–Gómez, *Invariant measures of Toeplitz subshifts on
  non-amenable groups*, arXiv:2305.09835v2. Standing hypotheses, as quoted from the abstract: "Let G be
  a countable residually finite group ... and let G← be a totally disconnected metric compactification
  of G equipped with the action of G by left multiplication." Theorem 1.3, as quoted from the arXiv HTML:
  > "For every integer r>1 there exists a Toeplitz G-subshift X⊆{1,…,r}^G with at least r ergodic
  > probability measures ν₁,…,νᵣ, and whose maximal equicontinuous factor is G←."

  Here `G←` is the inverse limit of `G/Γ_n` for finite-index normal subgroups with trivial
  intersection, and each `(X, σ, G, ν_i)` is measure conjugate to `G←` with Haar measure.

  Apply it with `G = Γ` and the chain above. This gives a minimal subshift `X ⊆ A^Γ`, a factor map
  `π : X -> K`, and an ergodic invariant probability `ν` with `(X, ν) ≅ (K, Haar)`.
- **Generators.** `R_T ⊆ C(X, Q) ⋊_alg Γ` is generated by:
  - `u_s^(±1)` for a finite generating set `S` of `Γ`;
  - the letter cylinders `χ_[a]`, `a ∈ A`;
  - the harmonic function `f = f_K ∘ π` of `harmonic-elimination-puts-q-in-odometer-crossed-products`.

## Statement

1. `R_T` is finitely generated, `LC(X, Z) ⊆ R_T`, and `Q ⊆ R_T`.
2. `R_T` is simple, and its centre is `Q`.
3. **(K1).** `ker(Q^x -> K_1(R_T)) ⊆ {±1}` and `Λ_N(R_T) = {λ : λI_N ∈ E_N(R_T)} ⊆ {±1}` for all
   `N`. So `Z(E_N(R_T)) ⊆ {±I}` and `GL_n(Q)` embeds in `PE_N(R_T)` for `N >= n+2`
   (`q-algebra-elementary-quotients-contain-gl-n-q`).
4. **Matricial divisibility.** For every `d >= 1`, `R_T ≅ M_(n_d)(S_d)` with `n_d = [Γ : Γ(d)]`, and
   `d | n_d`. Hence `K_2(Q) -> K_2(R_T)` is `0` (`matricial-divisibility-kills-rational-k2-symbols`).
5. **No Laurent trap on blocks.** Every `Γ(d)` has finite abelianization (Lee–Szczarba, in
   `sl3-congruence-transfer-kills-abelianization`). So `matrix-corner-laurent-rings-block-fp-elementary-groups`
   does not apply to any block corner.
6. **Block units (part of R2).** Let `C` be a block of level `d >= 2`, i.e. the preimage in `X` of the
   closure of a coset of `Γ(d)`, and let `γ ∈ Γ(d)`. Then `v = χ_C u_γ + (1 - χ_C)` has
   `[v] = ε·[χ_(C')]` for a block `C' ⊆ C` of level `d^2` and a sign class `ε ∈ K_1(Z) = {±1}`, so
   `[v]` is either `0` or the class of `1 - 2χ_(C')`. Moreover `{λ, v} = 0` in `K_2(R_T)` for every
   `λ ∈ Q^x`. The same holds
   for every `u_γ`, `γ ∈ Γ`, since `Γ` is perfect, and for all products of these units.

## Position

This is the first finitely generated simple ring on main that contains `Q`, detects `Q^x` in `K_1` with
finite kernel, kills `K_2(Q)`, and has no Laurent-type unit on its blocks. That is the example
`matricial-divisibility-kills-rational-k2-symbols` records as missing. Still open:
- the remaining parts of both K-budget clauses (`sl3-toeplitz-harmonic-ring-meets-the-uhf-k-budget`);
- finite presentation;
- simplicity of `PE_N(R_T)`.
