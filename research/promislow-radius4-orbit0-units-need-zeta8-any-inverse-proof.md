Put `S = S_0` and `T = T_0`. The scripts read `census_pairs.json`, written by
mode `orbits` of `promislow-radius4-census.py`, where `S` is representative 0
and `T` is its inverse support over `F_2`. This route is the route of
`promislow-radius4-orbit1-units-need-zeta8-any-inverse-proof` run on
representative 0; only the evidence in Step 2 differs.

**Step 0: one representative.** Each `phi in Phi` is an automorphism of `P`
(Step 1 of `promislow-radius4-support-pairs-need-zeta8-proof`). If `u` is a
nontrivial unit of `K[P]` supported in `phi(S)`, then `phi^(-1)(u)` is a
nontrivial unit supported in `S`, with inverse `phi^(-1)(u^(-1))`. So it is
enough to treat `S`.

**Step 1: the box.** Let `u in K[P]` be a unit with `supp(u) in S`. By
`promislow-inverse-has-equal-dihedral-syllable-length`,
`L_i^m(u^(-1)) = L_i^m(u) <= max_(h in S) l_i^m(h)` for `i = 0, 1, 2` and
every `m`. Mode `bounds` of `promislow-radius4-census-box.py` (job 597558)
computes the set `T'` of elements satisfying these conditions for
`-40 <= m <= 40`, with the same range assertion as in the orbit-1 route. For
representative 0 its output reads `|box(S)| = 77`, `T in box True` and
`S^-1 in box True`. So `supp(u^(-1)) in T'` with `|T'| = 77` and `T in T'`.

**Step 2: the case split on `(S, T')`.** This is Step 2 of
`promislow-radius4-support-pairs-need-zeta8-proof`, with `v` supported in
`T'`. For a nontrivial unit `u` with inverse `v`, let `i < j` be the first two
indices with `u_i, u_j != 0`, scale so `u_i = 1`, and put `w = u_j^(-1)`. Then
`(u, v, w)` is a `K`-point of the ideal `I_ij` generated by the coefficients
of `u v - 1` on the 338 cells of `S T'`, the `u_k` with `k < i` or
`i < k < j`, `u_i - 1` and `u_j w - 1`.

So all 210 cases are decided, and every case except `(0,1)` rules out points
over every field of characteristic zero. Hence `u_0 != 0` and `u_1 != 0`, and
the normalized point lies in `I_01`.

**Step 3: case `(0,1)` on `(S, T')`.**
`promislow-radius4-census-box-verify.py` (job 599042, task 0) recomputes
`I_01` over `Q` in `u_1, ..., u_20`, `v_h` for `h in T'`, and `w`. The ideal is
zero-dimensional of length 16, and the characteristic polynomial of a random
linear form on the quotient is squarefree of degree 16, so `I_01` has 16
distinct points over `Qbar`. All 16 roots lie in `Q(zeta_8)`. The script
evaluates the coordinates at them, multiplies each `u` and `v` out in
`Q(zeta_8)[P]` with the group law, and finds `u v = 1` with `v` vanishing off
`T` for all 16.

**Step 4: conclusion.** Let `u in K[P]` be a nontrivial unit with
`supp(u) in S`, and embed `K` in an algebraic closure. By Steps 1 and 2 its
normalized point is a point of `I_01` on `(S, T')`. By Step 3 it is one of 16
points, and each has `v` supported in `T`. So `supp(u^(-1)) in T`, and `u` is
a nontrivial unit on the pair `(S, T)`. By
`promislow-radius4-support-pairs-need-zeta8`, `K` contains a primitive eighth
root of unity. QED

**Trust surface.** `slimgb` over `Q` is exact. The emptiness of the other 209
cases on `(S, T')` rests on Singular finding a constant in the basis, with no
cofactor certificate; for `(2,5)` that basis came from the rerun in job
689435. The length 16 and the characteristic polynomial rest on the basis in
Step 3. The 16 points are checked by multiplication in `Q(zeta_8)[P]`,
independently of any Groebner basis. Step 1 imports Theorem 3.1 of
Craven--Pappas through `promislow-inverse-has-equal-dihedral-syllable-length`.
The support `S` comes from Tabei's census through `census_pairs.json`.
