**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Proof: `m11-balanced-loop-host-h1-is-z55-proof`. The proof is
by hand. The script checks the four local lemmas on all 22408 reduced words of length at
most 5, and that `b` has order exactly `55`.

**Setting.** As in `m11-balanced-loop-host-has-finite-h1`: `X = {1, …, 11}`,
`M_11 = ⟨α, β⟩`, `b = (b, α, β, 1, …, 1)`,
`C = (1, 1, 1, C, bα, C^{−1}, 1, …, 1)` and `R° = ⟨M_11, b, C⟩`.

**Theorem.**

1. **`H_1(R°; Z) ≅ Z/55`**, generated by `[b]`, with `[C] = [b]` and `[M_11] = 0`. The
   isomorphism is induced by `χ = e_b + e_C` mod `55`, the total exponent of `b` and `C`.
   This answers the order question left open in `m11-balanced-loop-host-has-finite-h1`.
2. **`FP_2` screen.** `H_1(R°; Z)` is odd and cyclic, so `R°` passes item 3 of
   `fully-self-replicating-fp2-groups-have-odd-cyclic-h1`.
3. **Degree 2 is silent.** For every field `k`, the degree-2 part of Nakaoka's
   decomposition `H_2(R°; k) ≅ H_2(R° ≀_X M_11; k)` reads `H_2(R°; k) ≅ H_2(R°; k)`, with
   no other summand. So no argument based only on the homology recursion
   `R° ≅ R° ≀ M_11` can bound `dim H_2(R°; k)` or refute `FP_2`.
4. **Degree 3 is not silent.** For `p ∈ {5, 11}`, `H_3(R°; F_p)` is infinite-dimensional.
   This is a concrete form of "not `FP_3`", which is already known from
   `self-wreathing-groups-are-not-fp-infinity`.
5. **Tits host.** The simple host `(R° ≀ M_12) *_{R°×R°} (R° ≀ M_12)` has abelianization
   `0`, since it is simple. It is finitely presented iff `R°` is, by
   `tits-hosts-for-transitive-local-actions`. That remains **open**.

**What this settles on the step "is `R°` `FP_2`?"** It is not settled. The step is
blocked on homological grounds, by item 3. Every obstruction on the graph that has been
used against fully self-replicating `M_11`-groups is either:

- an `H_1` condition, which `R°` meets by item 1 (odd cyclic `H_1`, `b_1 = 0`, loop
  character silent);
- a loop-group condition, which `R°` meets by `m11-balanced-loop-host-has-finite-h1`
  item 4;
- or contraction, which `R°` lacks.

The degree-2 recursion adds nothing, by item 3. So deciding `FP_2` needs one of the
following:

- an explicit (L-)presentation of `R°` and a proof that finitely many of its iterated
  relators suffice, or that they do not;
- a non-homological invariant that can see relators produced at every level by the
  exponential activity of `C`. Its sections spread over `{4, 6}^n`.

The obvious candidates each fail for a stated reason:

- degree-2 homology, by item 3;
- the ray-loop and loop-character tests, by `m11-balanced-loop-host-has-finite-h1`;
- Bartholdi's contraction theorem, because `R°` is not contracting.
