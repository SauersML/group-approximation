**OPEN.** Let `U = L_(F_2)(1,2)^x`. Every free ergodic p.m.p. action of `U` has Rokhlin entropy `0`.

`positive-rokhlin-entropy-makes-leavitt-units-surjunctive` and
`seward-per-group-rokhlin-entropy-of-bernoulli-shifts` give three equivalent forms:
- `h^Rok_sup(U) = 0`;
- `h^Rok_U(A^U, uniform) = 0` for every finite alphabet `A`;
- some uniform Bernoulli shift over `U` has Rokhlin entropy below `log |A|`.

The last form reduces to one finite configuration (`bernoulli-rokhlin-deficit-has-a-finitary-witness`):
finite sets `E, F` in `U` and a function `psi` with
`H(psi(x|_E)) + H(x(1) | F-translates of psi) < k log q`. Its incidence pattern is decidable
through the word problem of `U`. The inequality is a strict comparison of finite sums of
logarithms over the product measure on `P = FE ∪ {1}`, which interval arithmetic verifies. So a
deficit certificate is finite and checkable.

**Payoff.**
- It answers negatively, for a specific group, Seward's question whether every countable group has
  a free action of positive Rokhlin entropy.
- It refutes `leavitt-unit-group-has-positive-rokhlin-entropy-action`,
  `bernoulli-rokhlin-maximal-on-fg-simple-kazhdan-groups` and
  `bernoulli-rokhlin-entropy-maximal-for-every-group`.
- It is weaker than a counterexample to Gottschalk's conjecture on `U`.
  `leavitt-zero-rokhlin-supremum-from-strict-automaton` derives it from
  `leavitt-unit-group-nonsurjunctive`, and no converse is known. A measurable construction that
  decodes almost everywhere is enough here.

## Attempts

- **From a strict automaton.** This is the route above. It inherits every obstacle of the
  topological lanes.
- **Localization.** `U` has full sofic radical (`leavitt-unit-group-has-only-trivial-sofic-morphisms`).
  So `sofic-radical-localizes-bernoulli-deficit-witnesses` requires only that the subgroup
  generated by a witness be nonsofic. A witness inside a sofic subgroup is impossible. A witness
  inside Thompson's `V` would prove `V` nonsofic.
- **Linear witnesses.** For `k = 1` and prime `q`, a nonzero linear `psi` is uniform, so every
  witness is nonlinear.
- **Spectral invariants.** The Koopman representation of a uniform Bernoulli shift does not depend
  on the base. So spectral gap, strong ergodicity and property (T) cannot detect a deficit.
- **Doubling structures.** A measurable 2-to-1 compression that lives on a sofic subgroup cannot
  give a witness, by the localization. A compression has to run through a nonsoficity
  configuration of `U`, such as the Kazhdan compression `u L u^-1 < L` behind
  `openai-leavitt-unit-nonsofic`. No construction is known.
