**ESTABLISHED (reduction).**  Let `J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>`, `Q=1-ST`,
`G_n=EL_n(J)` and `L_n=GL_fin(N x {1,...,n},F_2)` as in
`binary-jacobson-mark-dichotomy-holds-from-rank-two`.  Let
`R=L_(F_2)(1,2)`, `q=s_1t_1`, and let `T_2` be the unital subalgebra of `R`
generated by `s_2,t_2`.

1. **(TJ1)** `S |-> s_2, T |-> t_2` defines a ring isomorphism
   `iota:J -> T_2` with `iota(Q)=1-s_2t_2=q`.  Hence
   `EL_20(T_2)` is isomorphic to `G_20`, and `x_13(q)` corresponds to
   `x_13(Q)`, which lies in `L_20`.
2. **(TJ2)** For every `n>=2` and every nonidentity `w in L_n`:

   ```text
   some homomorphism from G_n to a hyperlinear group retains w
     iff  G_n is hyperlinear
     iff  w notin Rad_hyp(G_n).
   ```

   So `Rad_hyp(G_n)` is either `1` or contains `L_n`.
3. **(TJ3)** Suppose a diagram `LNC3` of
   `agent-leavitt-not-bcs-negative-root-corner` has support `X` with
   `pi(X) <= EL_20(T_2)`.  Then `G_20=EL_20(J)` is nonhyperlinear.
   Equivalently, if `EL_20(J)` is hyperlinear, then every `LNC3` diagram has
   some support element `g` with `pi(g) notin EL_20(T_2)`.

**Consequence for the target.**  The target's "next falsifiable step"
asks whether `E_20(T_2)` has a hyperlinear quotient or model in which
`x_13(1-s_2t_2)` survives.  By `(TJ1)`--`(TJ2)`, that question is literally
"is `EL_20(J)` hyperlinear?".  The only hyperlinear quotient that can
retain the mark is the group itself.  So an obstruction to the elementary
Toeplitz survivor class at the level of models is exactly a hyperlinearity
theorem for `EL_20(J)`.  `EL_20(J)` is Kazhdan and not LEF, and its
soficity is open (`binary-jacobson-el3-is-sofic` and its firewalls).  Going
the other way, a Toeplitz-supported construction would prove `EL_20(J)`
nonhyperlinear.

**Scope.**  This decides neither side.  It does not cover supports with
`pi(X)` in `GL_20(T_2)` but outside `EL_20(T_2)`.  It does not cover
supports that use `s_1,t_1` beyond `q`.
