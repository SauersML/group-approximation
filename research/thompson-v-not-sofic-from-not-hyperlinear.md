Every sofic group is hyperlinear (`sofic-implies-hyperlinear`). If `V` is not
hyperlinear, it is therefore not sofic.

The converse direction is not available: a hyperlinear but nonsofic `V`
would answer Pestov's Question 3.4 (`hyperlinear-nonsofic-from-thompson-v`).
