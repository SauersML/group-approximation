Let P be infinite and finitely generated. For every enumeration nu,
put E=E_nu and Omega=V*0^infinity. Then for all finite M subset F
subset Omega,

    S(M,F)={e in E: sing(e) subset M, e fixes F pointwise}

is finitely generated. The pointwise stabilizer E_F of every finite
F subset Omega is finitely generated as well. These conclusions
require neither finite presentation nor decidable word problem of P,
nor computability of nu.

Consequently, if E_nu is finitely presented, its action on Omega
already satisfies the finite-generation condition on point stabilizers
in the twisted Brin--Thompson criterion. The additional three
finite-presentation clauses of the earlier shell route are one
sufficient method of proving E_nu finitely presented, not additional
requirements of the resulting simple-envelope construction.
