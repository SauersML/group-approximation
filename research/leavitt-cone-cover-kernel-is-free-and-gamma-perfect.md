**Setting.** `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)` with its `Z`-grading `deg s_i = 1`, `deg t_i = -1`; `q = s_1t_1`;
`Delta = St_20(R)`; `z = x_13(q)`. Let `R_+` be the unital subring generated by `s_1, s_2, q` and `R_-` the one
generated by `t_1, t_2, q`. Let `Delta_+-` be the subgroup of `Delta` generated by the `x_ij(a)`, `a in R_+-`, and
`Delta_0 = Delta_+ ∩ Delta_-`. Put `Gamma = Delta_+ *_(Delta_0) Delta_-`, `p : Gamma -> Delta` the induced map and
`K = ker p`. (This is the cover of `leavitt-cuntz-cone-halves-have-rf-amalgam-cover`; the statement below is proved
from the definitions and does not use that node. That node, on the landing branch, builds `Gamma` and shows every
certificate is K-essential; this one computes `K` itself and the first coefficient module in which K-essential defects
can be measured. It is not listed under `distinct_from` only because that id is absent from this worktree.)

**Statement.**
1. **Free kernel.** `p` is onto, `K` acts freely on the Bass–Serre tree of `Gamma`, and `K ≅ π_1(X)` where `X` is
   the `Delta`-coset graph with vertices `Delta/Delta_+ ⊔ Delta/Delta_-` and edges `Delta/Delta_0`. `K` is a
   nonabelian free group.
2. **Gamma-perfect.** `K = [K, Gamma]`. Equivalently `H_0(Delta; K^ab) = 0`.
3. **No invariant charge.** Every `Gamma`-conjugation-invariant homomorphism from `K` to an abelian group is zero.
   Every homomorphism `rho : Gamma -> G` with `rho(K)` central in `rho(Gamma)` (for instance a unitary
   representation of `Gamma` on which `K` acts by scalars) factors through `Delta`.
4. **Where charges live.** For every unitary `Delta`-module `M` (via `p`), restriction gives an injection
   `M^(Delta_0) / (M^(Delta_+) + M^(Delta_-)) ≅ H^1(Gamma; M) -> Hom_Delta(K^ab, M)`.
5. **Explicit regular charge.** For `M = l^2(Delta)` and `f = 1_(Delta_0)`, the resulting charge `c` satisfies, on the
   mixed relator `kappa = [a, b] z^(-1)` with `a = x_12(s_1)`, `b = x_23(t_1)`,

   ```text
   c(kappa) = 1_(ab Delta_0) - 1_(a Delta_0) + 1_(Delta_0) - 1_(aba^(-1) Delta_0)  ≠ 0.
   ```

   So `kappa ≠ 1` in `Gamma`, and `Hom_Delta(K^ab, l^2 Delta) ⊇ l^2(Delta_0 \ Delta)` is nonzero.

**Class killed.** Index or charge arguments for the K-essential step of `agent-leavitt-not-bcs-negative-root-corner`
and of the MF cover test with trivial or central coefficients.
- *Members.* Any argument that separates `Delta`-representations from `Gamma`-representations, or measures the
  K-essential defect of a lifted `(LNC3)` diagram, by a conjugation-invariant scalar charge on the relators of `K`
  (winding numbers on `X`, relator counts, determinant or Fredholm-index type homomorphisms), and every relaxation of
  the cover test that only asks `K` to act by scalars or centrally.
- *Invariant.* `K/[K, Gamma] = 0`, from `H_1(Gamma) = 0` (both halves perfect) and `H_2(Delta) = 0` (Khanh).
- *Death step.* Evaluating the charge on the normal generators of `K`: it is zero on `[K, Gamma] = K`. A central
  relaxation of the cover test is literally the test for `Delta`.
- *Survivors.* Charges in nontrivial coefficient modules. By (4)–(5) the first one is the regular representation of
  `Delta`, with values in `l^2(Delta_0 \ Delta)`, i.e. exactly the object whose finite-dimensional approximability is in
  question. Non-abelian (non-linearizable) uses of `K` and Gamma-invariant quasimorphisms on `K` are untouched.
