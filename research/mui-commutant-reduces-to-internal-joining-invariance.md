## Setting

As in `planted-lifts-defeat-hilbert-level-gaussian-soficity`. `Gamma < G`
is the Theorem E pair (or any infranormal non-normal Kazhdan pair), `N` is
the normal closure of `Gamma`. `s = (s_n)`, `s_n : G -> Sym(Y_n)`, is a lift
of a trace-faithful sofic `sigma`. `W_n = Y_n × Y_n`, `psi_n(g) = s_n(g) ×
s_n(g)`, `H = prod_U l^2(W_n, counting/|Y_n|)`, `H^eq(s)` the asymptotically
equivariant part, `H^eq_MUI` its marginally uniformly integrable part.

An **internal pair measure** is a sequence `lambda = (lambda_n)` of
probability measures on `W_n`. It is

- *MUI* if both marginals `(p_1)_* lambda_n`, `(p_2)_* lambda_n` are
  uniformly absolutely continuous with respect to the uniform measure on
  `Y_n`, along `U`;
- *internally `g`-invariant* if `|| psi_n(g)_* lambda_n - lambda_n ||_TV -> 0`
  along `U`;
- *internally equivariant* if
  `|| psi_n(gh)_* lambda_n - psi_n(g)_* psi_n(h)_* lambda_n ||_TV -> 0`.

Its **standard part** `st(lambda)` is the measure on `X × X`, where
`X = Loeb(Y_n)` with the Loeb action of `G`, defined on the product
sigma-algebra by `st(lambda)(A × B) = lim_U lambda_n(A_n × B_n)`. For an MUI
internal measure it is a coupling of two measures absolutely continuous with
respect to `mu_X`. If `lambda` is internally `Gamma`-invariant, then
`st(lambda)` is `Gamma`-invariant.

## Theorem

**(1) Reduction to internal measures.** For every sofic `sigma` the
following are equivalent.

- (MUI-C) Every `Gamma`-invariant vector of `H^eq_MUI` is `N`-invariant.
- (MUI-J) Every MUI, internally equivariant, internally `Gamma`-invariant
  pair measure is internally `N`-invariant.

The map is `xi -> lambda_n = |xi_n|^2 / ||xi_n||^2`, after the reduction
`xi -> (Re xi)_+, (Re xi)_-, (Im xi)_+, (Im xi)_-`: entrywise Lipschitz
functions vanishing at `0` commute with every `psi_n(g)`, preserve MUI,
`H^eq` and `Gamma`-invariance, and recover `xi`. For nonnegative vectors,
Hilbert distance is Hellinger distance, which is equivalent to total
variation.

**(2) The standard-part shadow is false, at the genuine models.** Let `Q_n`
be finite quotients of `G` exhausting it, `K = {0,1}`, `Y_n = K^(Q_n)`, and
let `G` act on `Y_n` by exact left shifts. This is a sofic representation
(trace-faithful and injective) that is **coordinatewise genuine**. For it:

- (a) (MUI-J) and (MUI-C) hold, and in fact every `Gamma`-invariant vector of
  `H` is `N`-invariant, with no MUI hypothesis.
- (b) The Loeb action `X` contains the Bernoulli shift `K^G` as a factor. The
  relatively independent self-joining `lambda_Gamma` of `X` over the
  sigma-algebra `F_Gamma` generated by the Bernoulli coordinates indexed by
  `Gamma` has both marginals equal to `mu_X`. It is `Gamma`-invariant and is
  **not** `N`-invariant: for `h in G`, `lambda_Gamma(x_h = y_h) = 1` if
  `h in Gamma` and `= 1/2` otherwise.
- (c) Consequently `lambda_Gamma` has **no** internal lift: there is no MUI
  internally `Gamma`-invariant internal pair measure `lambda` with
  `st(lambda) = lambda_Gamma`. Likewise the conditional expectation
  `E_(F_Gamma)` is a `Gamma`-equivariant, non-`N`-equivariant Markov
  projection on `L^2(X)`.

## What is killed, and the exact failing step

**Killed:** every argument for (MUI-C), (MUI-K) or CCR that passes from a
`Gamma`-invariant MUI vector (or commutant element) to its standard-part
data on the Loeb space and then applies ergodic theory there:

- the self-joining `st(|xi|^2)` of `X`, or the Markov operator it defines on
  `L^2(X)`;
- "Theorem C for joinings" (`Gamma`-invariant self-joinings of a sofic
  `G`-action are `N`-invariant);
- "Theorem C for factors" (`Gamma`-invariant sub-sigma-algebras of a sofic
  action are `N`-invariant);
- "Theorem C for Koopman intertwiners" (`Gamma`-equivariant bounded
  operators on `L^2(X)` are `N`-equivariant).

All four are false at the coordinatewise genuine Bernoulli models of (2),
where the true statement holds. The failing step is the inference

> `lambda` internally `Gamma`-invariant `⟹` only `st(lambda)` matters,

which loses exactly the internal total-variation invariance that (a) uses.
The planted lifts killed the non-MUI part of `H^eq`. This node kills the
external part of the MUI part. What survives is the purely internal
statement (MUI-J).

**Calibration.** Any proof of (MUI-J) must give the right answer on the
Bernoulli models: there it is true, but its external shadow is false. So the
proof must use internal TV-invariance of `lambda_n` in a way that fails for
couplings that are only asymptotically invariant after taking the standard
part. (1) also shows that (MUI-C) cannot be reduced to nonnegative vectors
inside `L^2(M)`: `|x|` (entrywise) of a unitary need not be bounded (the
Fourier matrix gives `J/sqrt|Y|`). So the positive reduction is a
MUI-level move, and CCR itself does not become a question about nonnegative
matrices.
