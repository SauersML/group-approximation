Citation import. Read from source on 2026-09-11:

* M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Embedding finitely presented self-similar groups into
  finitely presented simple groups*, arXiv:2405.09722 (v2, HTML), published
  in Bull. London Math. Soc. 2025, doi:10.1112/blms.70022. The definition of
  self-similarity ("its image in `S_d wr_d Aut(T_d)` ... is contained in
  `S_d wr_d G`"), Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.2 were read from the HTML text;
  the abstract states that the simple groups are commutator subgroups of
  Rover--Nekrashevych groups.
* J. Belk, C. Bleak, F. Matucci, M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Progress around the
  Boone--Higman conjecture* (Glasgow eprint 359114), Theorem 5.1(12) and
  Problem 5.3(12), read from the PDF pages 16--18.

Not reproved here; no Lean counterpart. The quoted wording is collected in
the linked artifact.
