Let `A` be simple separable nuclear stably finite.  In the unital
case, stable finiteness gives a quasitrace (Blackadar--Handelman),
nuclearity makes it a trace (Haagerup), and simplicity makes it
faithful; Problem IX(1) then gives quasidiagonality of `A`.  In the
non-unital case, pass to a hereditary unital corner `pAp` (simple
algebras have full projections... when they exist; otherwise use a
densely defined lower semicontinuous trace, faithful by simplicity,
and IX(1)'s standard reduction to units via hereditary subalgebras)
— quasidiagonality is inherited from full hereditary subalgebras by
Brown-type stability, since `A ⊗ K ≅ pAp ⊗ K`.
