**Import.** Read at source by sk-exact-banded on 2026-09-14, in the published PDF (Documenta Math. 4 (1999), pp. 513–514):
- **Abstract, p. 513, verbatim:** "It is shown that the class of exact groups, as defined in a previous paper, is closed under various operations, such as passing to a closed subgroup and taking extensions."
- **Introduction, p. 514, verbatim:** "What we are able to show is that the class of exact groups is closed under various operations such as passing to closed subgroups and taking extensions."
- **Introduction, p. 514, on the method:** "We shall use Green's notion of a twisted action of a group G on a C*-algebra [Gr] to prove that exactness is preserved on taking extensions."

The definition (p. 513) is that `G` is C*-exact if the reduced crossed product functor `A ↦ A ⋊_{α,r} G` preserves short exact sequences. The theorem number inside the paper was not recorded; the abstract carries the statement.

**Use.** A locally finite group is amenable, hence exact. So an extension of a locally finite group by an exact group is exact.
