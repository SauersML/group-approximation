title: A uniform singular-value gap in a full square-zero section forces Dixmier-Douady torsion
---

Let `A` be a continuous-trace C-star algebra over a connected compact
Hausdorff space `X`, and write `x_t` for the image of `x in A` in the compact
operator fiber over `t`.  Suppose `x` is full and `x^2=0`, and put

```text
m=min_{t in X} ||x_t|| > 0.
```

If some `lambda in (0,m)` is separated uniformly from every fiber spectrum
`spectrum(|x_t|)`, then `A` contains orthogonal Murray--von Neumann equivalent
full finite-rank projections.  In particular, the Dixmier--Douady class of
`A` is torsion.
