The defining relations of the relative Leavitt envelope are homogeneous for
the integer grading assigning degrees `1,-1,0` to `s_i,t_i,S`.  Thus a finite
linear relation among pure `s`-prefixes splits into separate relations at
each length.  At a fixed length `n`, left multiplication by `t_alpha` and
`(LHP2)` recover the coefficient of `s_alpha`, proving it is zero.  This
proves independence of the whole family.  Extend the function which is one
on every prefix to a linear functional on the underlying `F_p`-vector space
and exponentiate it to obtain `(LPB2)`.  The `t` statement is symmetric.
