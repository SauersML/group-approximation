If hyperlinear groups `Delta` and `Gamma` are joined as
`Delta semidirect Gamma` by a sofic hyperlinear action, then the semidirect
product is hyperlinear.  In particular, functorial set- and graph-halo products
with hyperlinear finite pieces remain hyperlinear under a sofic action on the
site structure.
