Let `l` and `N >= 3` be as in `rf-resolvent-elementary-group-is-fp`. Then
`E_N(R̄_l)` is finitely presented by that input. It is residually finite, and contains
`e_12(θ(Z_(l))) ≅ Z_(l)`, by `sl-n-z-localized-embeds-in-fg-rf-elementary-group`. ∎
