For every sequence of canonical regular-`A8` two-chart matrix models whose
full atlas defect tends to zero, each of the thirty pair-cubes in
`atlas-shortest-a4-triangle-packet` flexibly exactifies to an exact `A4`
representation containing a regular `A4` summand of relative dimension
`1-o(1)`.

Since the packet has exactly thirty members, the sum of all exceptional local
codimensions is still `o(d_n)`.  Thus, after discarding a vanishing total
matrix rank, the local representation type on **every** shortest A4 triangle
is fixed: it is regular.  Any surviving nonhyperlinear obstruction is therefore
a compatibility/holonomy problem among regular A4 multiplicity spaces, not a
local exotic representation problem.
