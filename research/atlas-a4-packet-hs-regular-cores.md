If the binary Leavitt unit group is hyperlinear, one may choose the canonical
regular-`A8` relative-unitary sequence in the **necessity** direction of
`leavitt-regular-atlas-hyperlinearity-criterion` so that, in addition to the
chosen finite atlas relators, every one of the thirty fixed pair-cubes in
`atlas-shortest-a4-triangle-packet` has normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt defect
`tending to zero.

Along that sequence, each pair-cube flexibly exactifies to an exact `A4`
representation containing a regular `A4` summand of relative dimension
`1-o(1)`.  Since the packet has exactly thirty members, the sum of all
exceptional local codimensions is still `o(d_n)`.

Thus a hypothetical hyperlinear model may be assumed regular on all thirty
shortest A4 contexts off a vanishing total residual.  The statement is
**not** asserted for an arbitrary relative-unitary sequence satisfying only a
particular finite usable relator list: a general kernel word can survive as a
central phase in the universal central extension.  The extra pair-cube
smallness comes from the pulled-back hyperlinear models killing `ker(bar_rho)`
before the virtually-free correction, as recorded in the relator-extraction
audit.
