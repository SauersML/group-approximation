## Attribution

Jihao Liu, [*Nonhyperlinear groups exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf) (2026-09-20), Theorem 1.3 / 7.4. This node records or conditionally uses Liu's result; it does not claim that analytic contribution for Cairn. Andreas Thom has priority for the conditional implication from the universal centralizer theorem to normalization; Liu explicitly credits it.

Let H be a countable Kazhdan group, Lambda<=H, and H=<Lambda,t_1,...,t_s> with finitely many compressors t_i Lambda t_i^(-1)<=Lambda. For a homomorphism pi:H->U(M) into a specified tracial matrix ultraproduct, suppose D=pi(Lambda)' cap M=[A_n]_omega is internal. Then pi(H) normalizes D. Moreover there are unital abelian B_n<=Z(A_n) with Z(D) cap pi(H)'=[B_n]_omega.

This is Liu Theorem 7.4. Lambda need not itself have property (T) for this CONDITIONAL statement. The ambient group must. The general consequence of universal centralizer internality has priority in Thom's Theorem 1.2, as Liu explicitly credits. The cited audit distinguishes Thom's assumptions from this formulation.
