Let G = Gr_k(H^{k+2d}) with k, d >= 1. Let zeta be the tautological
quaternionic k-plane bundle, viewed as complex of rank 2k, and put
q_1 = c_2(zeta). Put Q = d·zeta and W = Hom(Q, C^2) ≅ 2d·zeta*. Then:

- G is closed and 3-connected, and dim_R G = rank_R W = 8kd;
- <c_{2k}(zeta)^{2d}, [G]> = ±1;
- TG − W_R is spin, and lambda(TG) − lambda(W_R) = k·q_1 in
  H^4(G; Z) ≅ Z·q_1.

So for 8 | k, TG − W_R carries a BSpin_8-structure. For 24 | k, lambda of
the difference is divisible by 24.

Contrast: for Toms's Gr_k(C^{k+2d}) with k even, the defect is
(1 − k/2)·c_1^2 + k·c_2. That is 2·c_2 at k = 2, and it is never 0 mod 8.

Complete argument. Not independently reviewed.
