Direction `2 => 1` of `thompson-f-sofic-iff-relator-system-unstable`. It is the implication
Arzhantseva--Păunescu note after their Open problem (arXiv:1410.2626v2, p. 3). In a separating
almost-homomorphism the commutator `[a,b]` is far from the identity. Stability puts the generators near
exact solutions, which commute, and then the commutator is near the identity.
