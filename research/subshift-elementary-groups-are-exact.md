**OPEN.** Let X ⊆ A^Z be an infinite minimal subshift and G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ Z).

Claim: G_X is exact, that is, it has property A, equivalently C*_r(G_X) is an exact C*-algebra.

- A yes separates the Z-family from the lamplighter hosts on the C*-side: `some-simple-kazhdan-lef-groups-are-not-exact`.
- A no would put a non-exact subgroup inside GL_m(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ Z).

Search bounds: web searches for exactness or property A of topological full groups, wobbling groups and Thompson's V returned nothing decisive (2026-09-13). The literature status of exactness for these nearby ample groups is unknown to this lane.

## Attempts
- **The algebraic action is essentially free p.m.p., so it cannot witness exactness.**
  - Fix y ∈ X. Through the orbit representation at y, G_X acts on the compact group K = F_2^{Z×3} by continuous automorphisms (finite propagation), preserving Haar measure.
  - **Essentially free.** If g ≠ I, a nonzero row (r_1,r_2,r_3) of g − I is a nontrivial local F_2-linear form. By minimality its coefficient pattern recurs at infinitely many separated sites, where it reads disjoint independent Haar bits. So Fix(g) is null.
  - **Ergodic.** Every nonzero character in F_2^{(Z×3)} has an infinite orbit; for example e_21(u^k) moves δ_{n,1} to distinct characters for distinct k.
  - An essentially free ergodic p.m.p. action of a non-amenable group is not topologically amenable. The point 0 is fixed by G_X, and orbit closures of generic points appear to reach 0 (sketch: tower copies of SL_d(F_2) push mass out of windows). So exactness needs a boundary-type space, not K.
- **Cellular automata viewpoint, unresolved.** The action on X × (F_2^3)^Z commutes with the diagonal shift. So G_X ≤ Aut(X × (F_2^3)^Z, σ), a group of automorphisms of a positive-entropy subshift without periodic points. Exactness of f.g. subgroups of such automorphism groups is not known to this lane.
- **Permutation models are impossible; linearity is essential.**
  - Claim: an infinite countable Kazhdan group has no faithful bounded-displacement action on Z.
  - Infinite orbits have linear-growth, hence amenable, Schreier graphs, contradicting (T).
  - On a finite orbit O the Schreier graph is an ε-expander, while the median cut of O ⊂ Z is crossed by at most 2C|S| edges. So |O| is bounded, and a f.g. group acting faithfully with uniformly bounded orbits is finite.
  - So wobbling-group arguments (Juschenko–Monod, Juschenko–de la Salle) do not apply to G_X directly.
- **Wobbling group / coarse groupoid, dies at isotropy.** For subgroups of W(Z), βZ ⋊ Γ maps to the amenable coarse groupoid of Z. The kernel is the germ-isotropy bundle Γ^0_ω = {γ : Fix(γ) ∈ ω}, which can be non-amenable. The linear analog has non-amenable stabilizers of vectors and of lattices.
- **Compact orbit space, dies at fixed points.** X × F_2^{Z×3} has the fixed zero section. Every finitely supported vector is fixed by copies of EL_3 over corners, which are non-amenable.
- **Tate-space lattices, dies.** The stabilizer of the standard half-lattice contains EL_3(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ N) ⊇ SL_3(F_2[u]), which is non-amenable. So the lattice space has non-amenable stabilizers.
- **Finite asymptotic dimension, dies (sketch).** [[T]]′ ≤ G_X contains ⊕_N Z, generated by commuting first-return maps to disjoint clopen sets. So asdim G_X = ∞, and the Bell–Dranishnikov route is closed.
- **Sofic-approximation criteria give nothing.** Alekseev–Finn-Sell (Groups Geom. Dyn. 13 (2019), arXiv:1608.02242) derive amenability, a-T-menability or (T) of a group from coarse properties of its sofic approximations. The SL_{3N}(F_2) models are expanders, so property A of the approximations fails and gives no information on exactness.
- **Negative side, unresolved.**
  - A non-exact subgroup of GL_m(LC(X,F_2)⋊Z) must admit banded finite representations (sk-lef-embedding-d, for f.p. subgroups).
  - It must also have no distorted infinite-order elements (`sk-sl3z-bandwidth`).
  - Neither forbids Osajda-type growing-girth relator graphs, and no embedding of an Osajda group is known.
- **Two-ended skew lattices (sk-exact-gx, 2026-09-13), open at the last step.**
  - G_X acts on pairs of finitely generated B[[u]]- and B[[u^{-1}]]-lattices in B((u))^3 × B((u^{-1}))^3, with B = LC(X,F_2).
  - Every pair stabilizer has width ≤ 2n, so it is locally finite: `subshift-el3-two-ended-lattice-stabilizers-locally-finite`, through `bounded-width-subgroups-of-subshift-gl-are-locally-finite`.
  - This repairs the single-end Tate attempt above, whose stabilizer contains SL_3(F_2[u]).
  - Dies (for now) at the base: the lattice spaces have infinite residue ring B twisted by α, so no finite asymptotic dimension. The scaling lattices with a(Tx) ≤ a(x)+1 give unboundedly many independent directions.
  - Guentner–Tessera–Yu permanence (GGD 7 (2013) p. 378: subgroups, extensions, amalgams, HNN, direct unions; commutative-ring linear groups) does not reach G_X, which is finitely generated, simple and Kazhdan over a non-PI ring.
  - Artifact `research/artifacts/sk-exact-gx-2026-09-13-part1.md` §2.
- **Cut space G_X/S_y, reformulation only.** S_y is locally finite (PASS), but an amenable action on β(G_X/S_y) is as hard as exactness itself: for F_2[u^{±1}] the cut space is the group modulo a finite subgroup.
- **Linear wobbling reformulation, open.** G_X ≤ GL^{band}(Z×3;F_2), the units of the algebraic uniform Roe algebra of Z over F_2. Exactness of finitely generated subgroups of GL^{band}(Z×k;F_q) would settle G_X. Strang's block-diagonal factorization gives the same "two locally finite groups at bounded depth" structure as W(Z), so it decides nothing. Unlike W(Z), this unit group contains infinite simple Kazhdan groups.
  - **Made exact (sk-exact-banded, 2026-09-14):** `banded-matrix-groups-are-subshift-crossed-product-groups`. GL^{band}(Z×m;F_q) = GL_m(LC(βZ,F_q) ⋊ Z), and its finitely generated subgroups are exactly the faithful orbit-representation images of finitely generated subgroups of GL_m(LC(Y,F_q) ⋊ Z) over transitive subshifts Y.
- **Transfer across ranks and induced systems (sk-exact-banded, 2026-09-14).** `subshift-el-n-exactness-is-kakutani-invariant`:
  - EL_n(M_k(S)) = EL_{nk}(S); the k-tower system gives G_{X^{(k)}} ≅ EL_{3k}(R_X); and EL_n(R_{X_V}) ≤ EL_n(R_X) ≤ EL_{nk}(R_{X_V}) for induced systems.
  - So exactness in every rank is a Kakutani-equivalence invariant, and "every G_X is exact" is equivalent to "every EL_n(R_X) is exact". A proof may pass to induced systems with long towers; a counterexample may be sought in any rank over any minimal X.
  - Literature bound (two web searches, one fetch): no exactness or property A theorem found for finitely generated subgroups of W(Z), or for unit groups of algebraic uniform Roe algebras over finite fields.
- **Reduction to the limit subsystem (sk-exact-banded, 2026-09-14).** `subshift-gl-exactness-reduces-to-limit-subsystem`:
  - If every orbit of a subshift Y outside a closed invariant Z has both limit sets in Z, a finitely generated subgroup of GL_m(LC(Y,F_2)⋊Z) is exact whenever its image over Z is. The congruence kernel is locally finite because the complement ideal is ultramatricial, and exact groups are closed under extensions (Kirchberg–Wassermann).
  - So filtrations ending in periodic orbits give exact groups (Guentner–Tessera–Yu), including every banded matrix group over F_2 with coefficients eventually periodic in both directions.
  - The open question is concentrated in minimal aperiodic subsystems, where it is this node in rank m.
- **Weakly embedded expanders through tower copies, dies.** The matrix units of tower copies GL_{3h}(F_2) involve level indicators of return time ≥ h, which depend on ≳ h/2 coordinates for Sturmian X. So every non-constant generator has G_X-length ≳ h/2, and homomorphic tower copies are not uniformly Lipschitz.
- **Sup-metric copies through full-group cocycles, dies.** Commuting first-return maps on k disjoint pieces need 2^k masks, and balls grow at most exponentially, so some masks cost ≳ k/C. The induced metric is not uniformly ℓ^∞ on Z^k.
