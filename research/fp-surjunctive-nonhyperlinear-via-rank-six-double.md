## Attribution

The negative-family premise uses Jihao Liu, [*Nonhyperlinear groups
exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf)
(2026-09-20), Theorems 1.2/6.7 and 1.3/7.4, and Andreas Thom's
conditional normalization/double construction. Kun and Thom supply
the pairs. The structural finite-presentation and surjunctivity
application is earlier Cairn/OpenAI work, with its classical imports
made explicit in the artifact. This is a downstream synthesis, not
a new proof of Liu's analytic input.

## Why sufficient

Fix q=2,r=6,d=3 throughout. For the three-variable Laurent ring R,
St_6(R) is finitely presented and the FULL kernel onto EL_6(R) is
generated by the three symbols from the kernel prerequisite. Adding
their three relators therefore finitely presents EL_6(R). The group
SL_3(Z) is finitely presented, so its specified semidirect product G_6
is finitely presented. The polynomial subgroup Gamma_6 is finitely
generated by elementary roots with coefficients 1,x_1,x_2,x_3.
Identifying those generators in two finite presentations of G_6 gives
a finite presentation of D_6=G_6 *_(Gamma_6) G_6.

The surjunctive-double family applies to this exact Theorem E pair,
so D_6 is surjunctive. The q=2 nonhyperlinear-double family applies
with the SAME r=6,d=3, action and edge maps, so D_6 is not hyperlinear.
Thus this one group witnesses the target. Neither the old bare
existence of a finitely presented surjunctive nonsofic group nor Liu's
fixed rank-three wreath statement alone is used for this identification.
