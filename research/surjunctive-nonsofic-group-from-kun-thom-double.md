This is a second, independent witness.

* **Not sofic.** `kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic` makes `G *_Gamma G` not
  sofic for the Theorem E pair.
* **Surjunctive.** `kun-thom-group-doubles-are-surjunctive` makes the same
  double surjunctive.
* **Finitely generated.** The double is generated by two copies of the
  finitely generated group `G`.

This route does not use the wreath product route.
