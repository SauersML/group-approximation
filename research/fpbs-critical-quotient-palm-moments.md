For the critical quotient, put kappa=E[1/|C_pc(o)|] and tilt the rooted
law by 1/(kappa |C_pc(o)|). The resulting marked quotient is unimodular.
Under this law E_dagger m=1/kappa and h/kappa<=E_dagger B<=d/kappa, but
E_dagger m^2=E_dagger B^2=infinity. Consequently the additional
degree-biased root law has infinite mean degree. Sections 2-3 prove this
from mass transport and the explicitly imported infinite critical
susceptibility theorem. No independent branching description is claimed.
