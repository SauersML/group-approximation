title: No abelian group of isometries of the hyperbolic plane acts coboundedly
---

Let `A` be an abelian group acting on the real hyperbolic plane `H^2` by isometries
(through any homomorphism to `Isom(H^2)`). Then no orbit of `A` is coarsely dense.

Equivalently: the image of `A` in `Isom(H^2)` either fixes a point, or lies (up to
index two) in the one-parameter group of translations along a geodesic, or lies (up to
index two) in the one-parameter parabolic group fixing a boundary point; in each case
the orbits stay on a circle, an equidistant curve or a horocycle.
