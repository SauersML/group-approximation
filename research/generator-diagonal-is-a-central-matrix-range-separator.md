Let `S` have cardinality `m`, let `A` be a unital C-star algebra generated by
a unitary tuple `u=(u_s)_(s in S)`, and put

```text
MR_d=MR_d(A,u).
```

For any unitary tuple `X=(X_s) in U(d)^S`, define

```text
D=dist_(2,S)(X,MR_d),       B_s=X_s/sqrt(m).                    (GDS1)
```

Then

```text
sum_s||B_s||_2^2=1,          max_s||B_s||_op=1/sqrt(m),         (GDS2)
```

and the matrix-range separation gap satisfies

```text
Re sum_s tr_d(B_s^*X_s)-h_d(B) >= D^2/(2sqrt(m)).               (GDS3)
```

If `T in U(d)`, the centrality defect is inherited exactly:

```text
sum_s||T B_s T^*-B_s||_2^2
 =m^(-1)sum_s||T X_s T^*-X_s||_2^2.                            (GDS4)
```

In particular, for the arithmetic HNN packet, every positive primal
distance of the lattice tuple from its ucp matrix range produces a uniformly
operator-bounded stable-letter-central separator without Hahn--Banach,
truncation, or averaging.  The separator belongs to the fixed generator-word
span itself.

## Proof

Every coordinate of `Y in MR_d` is a contraction.  Therefore

```text
sum_s||X_s-Y_s||_2^2
 =m+sum_s||Y_s||_2^2-2 Re sum_s tr_d(X_s^*Y_s)
 <=2(m-Re sum_s tr_d(X_s^*Y_s)).                               (GDS5)
```

Take the infimum on the left and the supremum of the pairing on the right.
Since

```text
Re sum_s tr_d(B_s^*X_s)=sqrt(m),
h_d(B)=m^(-1/2)sup_(Y in MR_d)Re sum_s tr_d(X_s^*Y_s),
```

equation `(GDS3)` follows.  Equations `(GDS2)` and `(GDS4)` are immediate.

## Consequence for the finite-window attack

Canonical trace orthogonality of nonidentity group words cannot by itself
charge this separator: its source pairing is

```text
sum_s tr_d(B_s^*X_s)=sqrt(m),
```

coming entirely from the identity words `X_s^*X_s`.  Thus a finite mixed
Britton window can prove `sl3-hnn-finite-window-central-dual-gap` only by
forcing the support function upward -- equivalently, by manufacturing a ucp
matrix-range point close to `X`.  It cannot work by showing that every
fixed-word separator is trace-orthogonal to the lattice tuple.  This does not
refute FDG; it refutes that proposed proof mechanism and isolates its primal
content.
