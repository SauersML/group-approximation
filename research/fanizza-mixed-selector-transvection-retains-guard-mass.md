Let `K,Z,S` be involutions with `K` central in their subgroup and impose

```text
S Z S^(-1)=K Z.                                         (MST6)
```

With `H=(1-K)/2`, relation `(MST6)` only balances the two `Z` eigenspaces
inside `H`; it does not force `H=0`.  There is an exact finite-dimensional
model with

```text
tr(H)=1/8,
K|_H=-I,       Z|_H=sigma_z tensor I,
S|_H=sigma_x tensor I.                                  (MST7)
```

The complement is a trivial positive-character block of seven times the
guarded dimension.  Hence zero mixed-relation defect coexists with the
canonical positive D8 holonomy density.

Therefore the minimal non-split selector--machine transvection cannot supply
a proper-return trace inequality.  The return cell must act
non-automorphically after compression, comparing unequal carrier ranks or
using a one-sided map, rather than merely imposing a finite Clifford
covariance table on the guard sector.
