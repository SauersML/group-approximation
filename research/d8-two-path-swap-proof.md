# Cycle Klein characters and compute the two-path block matrix

Apply `phi` to `(DTP1)` to obtain `(DTP2)`. The two summands of `(DTP3)` are
mutually adjoint partial isometries with source/range projections `p_0,p_1`,
giving `(DTP4)`. Restriction to their multiplicity spaces gives `(DTP5)`,
which a block-diagonal gauge conjugates to the standard swap.
