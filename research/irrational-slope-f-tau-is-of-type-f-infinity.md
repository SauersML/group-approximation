**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `τ = (√5 − 1)/2`. The group
`F_τ = G([0,1]; Z[τ], <τ>)` is of type `F_∞`, in particular finitely presented. It
consists of the increasing piecewise-linear homeomorphisms of `[0,1]` with breakpoints in
`Z[τ]` and slopes powers of `τ`.
