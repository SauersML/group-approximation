Notation is from `zp-loop-braid-forbidden-ring-push-reduction-2026-09-17.md` §0–§2.
- `P = PLB_n = PΣAut_n` acts on `X_n = L_n(LB_*)` with strict fundamental domain `Q ≅ OM(K_n)`.
- A vertex is `p[τ] = p P_τ`. The cabling subgroup `P_(a,b)` is generated by three families:
  - `A_ij`, for `i, j ∉ {a, b}`;
  - `θ_k : x_k ↦ (x_a x_b)x_k(x_a x_b)^{-1}`;
  - `ζ_q : x_a, x_b ↦ x_q(·)x_q^{-1}`.
- By the Link Lemma, `lk(b0) ≅ X_{n−1}(F ∪ {f})`, where f is the fused ring. Its c-avoiding part
  is `L_c ≅ X_{n−1}(F ∪ {f, c})`.

**Claim.** Let `n ≥ 7`, let `v, w, c` be distinct free rings, let `b0 = [(v,w)]`, let `ε = ±1`,
and put `I = lk(b0) ∩ lk(A_vc^{ε} b0)` in `X_n(F)`.
1. No vertex of I has a type meeting c. Hence `I ⊆ L_c`.
2. If there are further free rings `a, d`, then `A_ca[(a,d)] ∈ L_c ∖ I`. Hence `I ⊊ L_c`.

Consequences:
- The one-ring push (w8 §7), and the `A_av` repair (w11 §0.5), do **not** have a forbidden-ring
  family member as their mutual link, in the natural candidate form `I = L_c`.
- The non-membership in item 2 cannot be seen by the abelian invariant e or by D4. It is seen by
  Fox 3-colorings.
