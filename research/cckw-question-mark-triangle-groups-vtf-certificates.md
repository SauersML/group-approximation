The full account is in `research/artifacts/cckw-vtf-question-mark-certificates-2026-09-14.md`.

1. **Presentations.** The relators come from the arXiv source files `presentations_3_3_4.tex`,
   `presentations_3_4_4.tex` and `presentations_4_4_4.tex`, converted to GAP words by
   `experiments/hyperbolic-triangle-search/cckw_convert.py`. The md5 sums are in artifact §2, and the
   converted files are `experiments/cckw-vtf-certificates/cckw{334,344,444}_q.g`.
2. **The triangle of groups.** `G` is the colimit of the triangle whose vertex groups are
   `X = <x,y | R_xy>`, with edge groups `<a>, <b>, <c>` of order 3 and trivial face group. Each vertex
   group is generated by its two edge groups.
   - GAP computes `|X| = 3n/2` for the relevant labels, which matches the edge-regular action (`|V|=` in
     `summary.txt`; `experiments/hyperbolic-triangle-search/cckw_vertex.out`).
   - The half-girths `(r_0, r_1, r_2)` are of type (3,3,4), (3,4,4) or (4,4,4), with `Σ 1/r_i < 1`.
3. **Certificate.** For each group, a file in `experiments/cckw-vtf-certificates/certs/` gives
   permutations `a, b, c` of degree `d`. They come from a LINS normal subgroup (`cert_vtf.g`), or from
   epimorphisms onto CCKW's listed simple quotients concatenated on disjoint points (`cert_product.g`).
4. **Homomorphism.** `verify_vtf_cert.py` uses no GAP. It evaluates every relator on `a, b, c` and gets
   the identity, so `a, b, c` define `ψ: G -> S_d`.
5. **Injective on vertex groups.** The script computes `|<a,b>|, |<b,c>|, |<c,a>|` by closure and gets
   `3n_0/2, 3n_1/2, 3n_2/2`. Each image is a quotient of the vertex group of the same order, so `ψ` is
   injective on it.
6. **Girths.** The script computes the girths `2r_i` of the coset graphs of the images, which are
   isomorphic to the vertex groups, and checks `Σ 1/r_i < 1`.
7. **Conclusion.** By (iv) of `triangle-of-groups-half-girth-structure`, `ker ψ` is torsion-free of finite
   index. By its (v), `G` is non-elementary hyperbolic.

**Controls** (artifact §4).
- The VTF `Yes` group `G_0^{14,24,40}` certifies through `A_7`.
- The same certificate with `c` overwritten by `b` is rejected.
- LINS finds the known `A_7` quotients of three `Yes` groups.
