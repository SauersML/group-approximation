Let `A` be a commutative ring and `q` an ideal. The subgroup
`F(q) = <e_ij(t) : t ∈ q, i != j> <= SL_3(A)` contains `E_3(A, q^2)`, the
normal subgroup of `E_3(A)` generated by the `e_ij(s)` with `s ∈ q^2`.

This is the `SL_3` case of a statement attributed to Tits (1976; recalled,
not read).
