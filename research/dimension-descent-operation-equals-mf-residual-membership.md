**ESTABLISHED** (route `dimension-descent-operation-equivalence-proof`).
No property (T), Kazhdan projection or spectral gap is used.

**Setting.** `G` is a countable group generated by `s_1,...,s_k`, with
presentation `F_k -> G`. `z` is a word whose image in `G` is an involution.
For a finite set `R` of relators of `G` and `epsilon > 0`, set

```text
delta_R(U) = max_(r in R) ||r(U) - I||_op,         U in U(d)^k,
Vis(R, eps) = { U in U(d)^k, d >= 1 : delta_R(U) < eps, ||z(U) - I|| >= 1 },
delta_*(R)  = inf { delta_R(U) : d >= 1, U in U(d)^k, ||z(U) - I|| >= 1 }.
```

`Op(R, eps)` is the hypothesis of `dimension-descent-criterion-for-non-mf`:
every `U in Vis(R, eps)` of dimension `d` has some `V in Vis(R, eps)` of
dimension `d' < d`.

**(E1) Operation equals emptiness.** `Op(R, eps)` holds iff `Vis(R, eps)` is
empty. Equivalently, `Op(R, eps)` holds iff `eps <= delta_*(R)`. No map
satisfying the descent property has a nonempty domain.

**(E2) Emptiness equals MF-residual membership.**
`z in Res_MF(G)` iff there are a finite set `R` of relators and `eps > 0`
with `Vis(R, eps)` empty. Here `Res_MF(G)` is the intersection of the kernels
of all homomorphisms `G -> U(prod_n M_(d_n) / (+)_n M_(d_n))`.

**(E3) Consequence for the route.** Take `G = H`, the binary Leavitt unit
group of `binary-leavitt-dimension-descent-operation`.
- By (E1) and (E2), the hole `binary-leavitt-dimension-descent-operation`
  holds for some `(R, eps_0)` iff `z in Res_MF(H)`.
- Both directions are elementary: well-ordering of dimensions and one
  ultraproduct. So the equivalence also holds between Kazhdan-free proofs.
- The route `leavitt-full-radical-via-dimension-descent` therefore reduces
  its target to itself. Its hole is not a last missing piece in any sense
  stronger than "the goal".
- Unrestricted, the hole is true, vacuously, as soon as `z in Res_MF(H)` is
  known by any proof, including a Kazhdan one. It is purely a provenance hole.

**(E4) What this kills.** The class of "descent operation" constructions is
the intended shape: detect a finite matrix factor, pass to a multiplicity
space, rebuild a tuple.
- **Invariant.** `delta_*(R)`.
- **Where every member dies.** Any candidate formula that acts on some actual
  visible model at threshold `eps` certifies `eps > delta_*(R)`. At that
  threshold no descent operation exists. So a candidate is either never
  applied, or it is refuted by the minimal-dimension element of its domain.
- **No numerical test is possible.** At an admissible threshold there is no
  model to test a candidate descent formula on.
- **Loss rates.** A construction with loss `delta(V) <= f(delta(U))`, for
  `f(t) < t` on `(0, eps)`, gives no more than `Vis(R, eps) = empty` (see the
  proof). The admissibility accounting of
  `quadratic-descent-loss-is-admissible-linear-is-not` is subsumed.
- **What a real decomposition must supply instead.** A lower bound
  `delta_*(R) > 0` from the relations, with no candidate model to work on.
  That is `uniform-finite-mf-obstruction` for `H`, made Kazhdan-free.

**Not affected.** Descent arguments whose output is allowed to leave
`Vis(R, eps)` (to a different group, relator set or threshold) are not
covered by (E1). Nor are operations that raise the dimension and lower the
defect: these are stability or correction statements and need a separate
limit argument.
