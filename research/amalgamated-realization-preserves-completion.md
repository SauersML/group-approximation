Let `P` be the specific affine `A_5` cyclic-HNN group of
`sofic-cyclic-mf-quotient-with-full-radical`, with its displayed stable
letter `s` and split epimorphism `chi:P->Z`. Put `N=ker chi`.
The reference to this concrete seed is essential for the acyclicity
and Bass-Serre calculations below; those do not follow merely from a
split cyclic quotient and an MF-invisible kernel.
Let `A` be a finitely presented sofic group and let `a in A` have infinite
order.  Amalgamate the infinite cyclic subgroups generated by `s` and `a`:

```text
G_A = P *_(<s> = <a>) A,
q : G_A ->> A,   identity on A,   p -> a^(chi(p)) on P.
```

Then `G_A` is finitely presented, sofic, contains `P`, and is not MF, and:

1. composition with `q` is a bijection from homomorphisms `A -> M` to
   homomorphisms `G_A -> M` for every MF group `M`; so
   `Rad_MF(G_A) = q^(-1)(Rad_MF(A))`, and `A` is the **largest MF quotient**
   of `G_A` whenever `A` is itself MF;
2. `ker q ~= *_(A/<a>) N` is nontrivial, perfect, rationally acyclic, and
   equal to its own MF radical;
3. `q` induces an isomorphism of profinite completions, and
   `H_j(G_A; q^* M) ~= H_j(A; M)` and `H^j(A; M) ~= H^j(G_A; q^* M)` for
   every `QA`-module `M` and every `j`;
4. `G_A` is acylindrically hyperbolic with trivial finite radical, so
   `C^*_r(G_A)` is simple with a unique tracial state, has stable rank one,
   and is stably finite but not MF.

Taking `A = Z^m` and `a` a basis vector gives a finitely presented sofic
non-MF group with largest MF quotient `Z^m`, profinite completion `Zhat^m`,
and the rational cohomology ring of the `m`-torus.

The attached proof route supplies the argument from that concrete seed.
The manuscript supplies the seed's presentation and established properties;
the additional amalgamation and rational-acyclicity arguments are in the
route itself.
