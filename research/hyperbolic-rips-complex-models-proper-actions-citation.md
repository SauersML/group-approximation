D. Meintrup and T. Schick, *A model for the universal space for proper actions of a
hyperbolic group*, New York J. Math. **8** (2002), 1–7; arXiv:math/0209163v3. Read from
the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-12.

**Theorem 1**, verbatim: "Let `G` be a `δ`-hyperbolic group relative to a set of
generators `S`, `P_d(G,S)` its Rips complex, with `d ≥ 32δ + 20`, and `P` its second
barycentric subdivision. Then `P` is a finite `G`-CW-model for the universal space
`\underline{E}G` of proper actions, i.e. `P` consists of finitely many `G`-equivariant
cells, `P` has only finite stabilizers and `P^H` is contractible for any finite
subgroup `H ⊂ G`."

Their introduction defines a `G`-CW complex as a CW complex with a cellular action such
that "whenever a cell is mapped on itself by some `g ∈ G`, the restriction of the
action of `g` to this cell is the identity map", and the Rips complex `P_d(G,S)` as the
simplicial complex whose `k`-simplices are the `(k+1)`-tuples of pairwise distinct
elements with pairwise word distance at most `d` (Definition 3). The abstract records
the corollary that a hyperbolic group has only finitely many conjugacy classes of
finite subgroups.
