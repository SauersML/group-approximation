**ESTABLISHED (conditional on the imports behind
`leavitt-square-unit-group-is-fp-simple-and-acyclic`).** Let `K` be a field of
characteristic two with transcendence degree at most two over `F_2`, and let
`n >= 1`. Every finitely generated subgroup of `GL_n(K)` embeds in the finitely
presented simple group `(L ⊗ L)^x`, with `L = L_(F_2)(1,2)`. So it satisfies the
Boone--Higman conjecture.

**Examples.**
- Finitely generated subgroups of `GL_n(F_q(t_1, t_2))` for `q` a power of
  two.
- `SL_n(F_q[t_1, t_2])` for `n >= 3`, finitely generated by Suslin's
  `SL_n = E_n`.
- The lamplighter `Z/2 wr Z^2 <= GL_2(F_2[t_1^±, t_2^±])`.
- Finite products of such groups, through block-diagonal matrices.

The survey of Belk--Bleak--Matucci--Zaremsky, arXiv:2306.16356v3, lists no
positive-characteristic linear class. Zaremsky arXiv:2405.09722 settles
`GL_n(Q)` in characteristic zero. The novelty search was bounded (2026-09-12).
