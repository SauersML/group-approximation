Take the dual action of `Q^times` on `Q-hat`, add the countable invariant
translation subgroup generated by rational-phase points, and form the finite
crossed product.  Character calculus gives `(SHA1)`.  The affine actor is
abelian-by-abelian, so its crossed product is amenable and Connes embeddable.
