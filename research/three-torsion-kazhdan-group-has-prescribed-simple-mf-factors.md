For any two infinite minimal binary subshifts `Y_i`, put
`Q_i=EL_6(LC(Y_i,F_2) semidirect Z)`. There is a Kazhdan group `H`
generated by three elements of orders `7,2,2` whose maximal MF
quotient is exactly `Q_- x Q_+`, with radical
`L ~= SL_infinity(F_2)` under the ordinary identity-block inclusions.
The group `L` is infinite, simple and locally finite.

Each `Q_i` is infinite simple Kazhdan LEF and has a generating triple
of orders `7,2,2`, through binary letter doubling. The group `H` has
no nontrivial finite quotient: `Res_fin(H)=H` while `Rad_MF(H)=L<H`.
Thus `H` is non-MF with a nontrivial LEF maximal MF quotient. If an input
is the universal-odometer binary system, its factor contains every
countable locally finite group.
