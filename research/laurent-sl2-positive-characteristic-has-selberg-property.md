**Statement.** Let `p` be prime, `R = F_p[t, t^{-1}]` and `E = SL_2(R)`. Fix a finite generating
set `S` of `E`. There is `c > 0` such that, for every nonzero ideal `J ⊂ R`, the Cayley graph of
the finite group `E/E(J)` with respect to the image of `S` has Cheeger constant at least `c`. Here
`E(J) = ker(E → SL_2(R/J))`.

`R` is a Euclidean domain, being a localization of `F_p[t]`. So `SL_2(R) = EL_2(R)`, and `E` is
finitely generated by `elementary-sl2-laurent-integers-is-finitely-generated`, via reduction mod `p`.
The statement does not depend on `S`.

**Why it should hold.** `E` is an irreducible `S`-arithmetic lattice in
`SL_2(F_p((t))) × SL_2(F_p((t^{-1})))`, for the two places `t = 0` and `t = ∞`. The expected proof
replaces Selberg's `3/16` theorem by Drinfeld's Ramanujan–Petersson theorem for `GL_2` over function
fields. Lubotzky states this in prose, not as a numbered theorem.

- Source: A. Lubotzky, *Expander graphs in pure and applied mathematics*, Bull. AMS 49 (2012),
  arXiv:1105.2389, §2.4, after Corollary 2.13. Checked 2026-09-19 against the arXiv PDF. The text
  reads: "Analogous results for arithmetic groups in positive characteristic such as `SL2(Fp[t])`
  or `SL2(Fp[t,t−1])` (when this time a result of Drinfeld replaces the Theorem of Selberg) can
  make, for a fixed p, the family `{SL2(Fpα) | α∈N}` into a family of expanders."
- The same survey, in §2.5, says that the Selberg property for general arithmetic groups "has not
  been completed yet for the positive characteristic case". The `SL_2` case over function fields is
  the Drinfeld case, which is not affected by this remark.

**Gap.** The quoted sentence names only the prime-level quotients `SL_2(F_{p^α})` explicitly. The
statement needs all nonzero ideals `J`, including prime powers. A verbatim theorem is still
needed, with the full congruence family and the ring `F_p[t,t^{-1}]` (two places). Candidate
sources, not yet checked:
- Lubotzky, *Discrete Groups, Expanding Graphs and Invariant Measures* (1994), the chapters on the
  Ramanujan conjecture and on property (τ);
- Lubotzky–Zuk, *On property (τ)*, the chapter on the Selberg property;
- Morgenstern's function-field Ramanujan graphs (1994).
