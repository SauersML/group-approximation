**Step 1 (the HNN group is a star amalgam by the shift).**  Let
`N_0 = ker(G -> Z)`, `t -> 1`.  It is generated by the conjugates
`A_j = t^j A t^-j`, `j in Z`, which pairwise intersect in `C` (the letter
`t` centralises `C`), and Britton normal form for `A *_C (C x <t>)` shows
that reduced words `a_(j_1) a_(j_2) ... a_(j_m)` with `a_(j_i) in A_(j_i) - C`
and consecutive indices distinct are nontrivial.  Hence `N_0` is the
amalgamated free product of the family `(A_j)_(j in Z)` over the single
shared subgroup `C` — the star amalgam `*_C^(j in Z) A` — and
`G = N_0 rtimes <t>` with `t` acting by the index shift.

**Step 2 (Gao).**  The group factor of a star amalgam is the tracial
amalgamated free product of the group factors over the shared subgroup
factor: `L(N_0) = *_(L(C))^(j in Z) L(A)`.  Gao, arXiv:2012.07940,
Theorem 1.1 (with Corollary 2.1), states that if `B subset N` is `RE/N_1`
then the amalgamated free product of countably many copies of `N` over `B`
tracially embeds into an ultrapower of `R (x) N_1`.  With `N_1` Connes
embeddable, `R (x) N_1` and its ultrapowers are Connes embeddable, so
`L(N_0)` is Connes embeddable and `N_0` is hyperlinear.

**Step 3 (hyperlinear-by-`Z` is hyperlinear).**  Let `N_0` be hyperlinear
and `G = N_0 rtimes_alpha Z`.  Fix finite `F_0 subset G`, `epsilon > 0`;
write its elements as `n t^m` and let `F = {-L, ..., L}` be a Folner
interval for the finitely many exponents `m` that occur, and `S subset N_0`
the finite set of all `alpha_f^(-1)(n)` with `f in F` and `n t^m in F_0`.
Take a microstate `sigma : N_0 -> U(d)` that is `epsilon`-multiplicative
on `S S` with `|tr sigma(n)| < epsilon` for `n in S - {e}`.  On
`C^d (x) l^2(F)` define

```text
rho(n)(v (x) delta_f) = sigma(alpha_f^(-1)(n)) v (x) delta_f,
rho(t)(v (x) delta_f) = v (x) delta_(f+1)   (f+1 in F; any unitary completion on the boundary).
```

Then `rho(t) rho(n) rho(t)^* = rho(alpha_t(n))` exactly on interior
fibres, `rho` is `epsilon`-multiplicative on `F_0` up to the boundary mass
`O(1/L)`, and `tr rho(n t^m) = 0` for `m != 0` while
`|tr rho(n)| <= max_f |tr sigma(alpha_f^(-1)(n))| < epsilon` for `n != e`.
Letting `epsilon -> 0`, `L -> infinity` gives regular-trace microstates of
`G`.  This is the Elek--Szabo construction for sofic-by-amenable
extensions (Elek--Szabo, *On sofic groups*, J. Group Theory 9 (2006),
Theorem 1) with permutations replaced by unitaries; for general amenable
quotients replace `F` by a Folner set and `t^m` by a section.

**Step 4 (consequence).**  `G` hyperlinear means `L(G)` embeds in a
matrix ultraproduct; restricting to `L(A)` and applying
`hnn-hyperlinearity-is-amalgamated-free-haar-unitary` gives the
amalgamated-free Haar unitary in that model.

**Microstate form.**  Given `(CF1)`, the map `pi = prod_omega pi_k` is a
trace-preserving embedding `L(A) -> prod_omega M_(d_k)` (regular trace),
the `B_k` are finite-dimensional hence hyperfinite, `M_(d_k)` is QWEP, and
the commuting square `E_(prod B_k) o pi = pi o E_(L(C))` holds on the group
unitaries by `(CF1)` (`E(pi(u_c)) = pi(u_c)` for `c in C`,
`E(pi(u_a)) = 0` for `a notin C`), hence on their weakly dense span and,
both sides being normal, on `L(A)`.  So `L(C) subset L(A)` is `RE/C`.
