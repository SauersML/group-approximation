Complete proof: artifact Section 4 (Theorem N).

1. **The group and the subflow.** `Γ_p` is finitely presented and amenable, and has a central `c_p ≠ e` killed by every
   finite-dimensional unitary representation. Regular representations of finite quotients kill it, so `Γ_p` is not residually
   finite. By the finitely-presented-LEF claim, `Γ_p` is not LEF. Gao–Jackson–Seward give a free minimal Cantor subflow `X`.
2. **The ring.** `R = LC(X,F_q) ⋊ Γ_p` is generated by `1_([x_e = 1])`, a generator of `F_q` and the `u_s`. So `EL_N(R)`,
   `N >= 3`, is finitely generated.
3. **Simplicity.** `R` is the Steinberg algebra of a Hausdorff ample, principal, minimal groupoid with compact infinite unit
   space. So `S_N` is infinite and simple, with scalar centre.
4. **(T).** Ershov–Jaikin-Zapirain for the finitely generated ring `R`, passed to the quotient.
5. **Linear soficity.** Theorem F gives a unital rank model over `F_q`. `R` is simple with centre `F_q`, so the adjoint
   theorem makes `S_N` `F_q`-linear sofic.
6. **Not LEF.** Whitehead embeds `Γ_p` in `S_N`.
