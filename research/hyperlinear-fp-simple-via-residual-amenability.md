Show that a non-amenable finitely presented infinite simple group is
residually amenable, or LEA, and conclude soficity and hence hyperlinearity.

Dead, invalidated by `fp-simple-groups-escape-soft-hyperlinear-permanence`:
either property forces the group to be amenable.
