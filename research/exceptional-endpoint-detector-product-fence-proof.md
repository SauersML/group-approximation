Use the actor coordinate order `(2,4,5,6,7,8,9)`.  Exact conjugation by
the ten displayed generators of `L_0` gives

```text
Cl(g_A)={vw,uw},        Cl(g_B)={uv},
Cl(g_C)={uvw,w},                                      (1)
```

where `u,v,w` are the three commuting involutions in `(EDP2)`.  Hence the
subgroup generated by the three full conjugacy classes is exactly
`H_exc=<u,v,w>` of order eight.  The signed enumeration gives character
zero, namely sign `+1`, on all eight elements.  The artifact closes all
`8192` elements of `L_0`, performs the conjugations, and checks these
statements directly.

For completeness, if `G` is the constant actor matrix of `g` and
`Y_m=1+b_mE_(k_m,3)`, then `g` fixes coordinate `3` and

```text
gY_mg^(-1)
 = product_(s in actor) x_(s,3)(G_(s,k_m)b_m).         (2)
```

All factors in `(2)` commute.  Multiplication by `Y_m^(-1)=Y_m` cancels
the diagonal `s=k_m` factor, so `[g,Y_m]` contains precisely the
off-diagonal column-`k_m` factors.  Commuting this product with
`A_(r,m)` kills every factor except `x_(r,3)(b_m)`, and prefix cancellation
`a_mb_m=q` proves `(EDP4)`.

The matrices `u,v,w` have endpoint support only at

```text
u: E_(2,6),       v: E_(2,9),
w: E_(4,6)+E_(4,9).                                  (3)
```

Among the three native middle columns `(7,8,9)`, equation `(3)` therefore
meets only column `9`.  Taking all eight products gives exactly `(EDP5)`
and proves the fixed-source adjacent-depth fence.
