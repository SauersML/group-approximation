On `M_d` with conjugation action, the fixed spaces of the two vertex
representations are precisely their commutants.  Hence the desired transfer
is `dist_2(v,M^(C,-))<=f(delta_B)` for unitary
`v in M^(C,+)`.  At zero edge defect the data represent the amalgam, and
finite-dimensional co-density makes both spaces equal to `M^A`.

If no uniform modulus exists, choose dimensions with defect tending to zero
and distance bounded below.  Their tracial/Hilbert ultraproduct is an exact
representation of the amalgam with a nonzero vector fixed by the first
vertex group but not by the whole group.  This is an `H^0` failure.  A
degree-one restriction gap on cocycles does not exclude it, and abstract
infinite-dimensional examples already occur in `ell^2(A/C)`.  Thus only a
theorem using the finite matrix-coordinate origin can close the transfer.
