A linear constraint on encoded variables is a product over contexts of
`+-1`-valued functions of the context assignment; honest satisfaction on
all consistent tuples makes each such function constant on the fibres of
the shared variables, and an odd product of a constant is that constant.
Within a context the solutions of the long-code linear relations with
`a_(-1) = J` are the characters of the function group with odd support.
