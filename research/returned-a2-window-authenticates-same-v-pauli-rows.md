Dead route.  Let `V=U_0^*U_1` be the literal order-three relative word in
the native `A_2` occurrence orbit.  Adjoin the returned opposite-root
comparisons and the packet-local Pauli relations on the selected source
carrier.  The hoped inference was that bounded normal-closure consequences
of this finite window would give

```text
[V,X]=o(1),                 [V,Z]=o(1),                (RAP1)
```

for the two source Pauli generators on one positive common cut.  Then
`relative-gauge-invariance-transfers-cross-orthogonality` would transfer the
target cross-zero estimate back to the source and close the remaining
relative-gauge term in `separate-native-quarter-carrier-recovery`.

`returned-a2-window-leaves-same-cut-pauli-commutators-free` refutes this
inference by an exact finite semidirect-product model.  The same literal
order-three word cycles three packet copies and satisfies the full returned
window, while an invariant trace-`1/8` cut carries a Pauli pair whose two
commutators in `(RAP1)` both have squared normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm
`2`.  Hence neither commutator is a normal-closure consequence, bounded or
otherwise, of the presently named rows.

This does not refute the endpoint.  It proves that the next source row must
be genuinely mixed: it must identify the returned Pauli occurrence with its
source occurrence (or charge the failure on a finite-coordinate boundary),
not merely place packet-local Pauli relations beside the existing relative
orbit and return words.
