Let `K<=A_8 x A_8` be generated by the fixed pairs `(a_s,b_s)` in `(APN1)`.
The canonical atlas model sits in a finite tracial group-algebra model in which
each finite chart has its regular normalized character: every nonidentity
chart element has trace zero.  By hypothesis the exact relative implementer
satisfies all paired equations in `(APN1)`.

Therefore `regular-tracial-paired-relations-force-kernel-balance` applies and
gives

```text
ker(p_1:K->A_8)=ker(p_2:K->A_8).                       (1)
```

The same theorem then says that the two finite regular pullback
`K`-representations are unitarily isomorphic at every common external
multiplicity.  Choose an exact unitary intertwiner `V`.

By `paired-finite-group-intertwiner-laplacian`, the zero-energy space of
`(APN1)` is precisely the full `K`-intertwiner space, so it contains `V`.
Every spectral low-energy space contains the zero-energy space and hence
contains `V` as well.  For every subspace `X`, its image under that matrix
space contains `V(X)`, so its dimension is at least `dim X`.  No shrunk
subspace exists at any threshold.

Thus `(ASI3)` cannot come from a quadratic form of the restricted paired type.
