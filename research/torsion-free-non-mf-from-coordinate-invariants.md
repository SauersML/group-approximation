## Why sufficient

Let `Gtilde` be the torsion-free finitely presented group of
`infinite-order-central-mark-group`, with nontrivial central mark `z` in the
normal closure of the compression defect inside `H_0`, and let `Theta` be
any corona representation with models `U_n`.

If `Theta(z) = 1` there is nothing to prove, so assume `Theta` detects the
mark, which is the hypothesis `kazhdan-invariant-subspace-generates`
carries.  On the coordinate ultraproduct,
`coordinate-ultraproduct-transport` clause 3 says `tilde U(z)` acts as the
identity on `ran P`, the `iota(Gamma)`-invariant subspace.  Since `z` is central, `tilde U(z)`
commutes with `tilde U(H)`, so the set of vectors it fixes is a closed
`tilde U(H)`-invariant subspace; containing `ran P`, it contains the
`tilde U(H)`-invariant subspace generated by `ran P`, which by
`kazhdan-invariant-subspace-generates` is everything.  So `tilde U(z) = 1`,
and by clause 1 the coordinate ultraproduct carries the corona operator
norm, so `Theta(z) = 1`.  As `z != 1`, no corona representation of `Gtilde`
is injective and `Gtilde` is not MF, while being torsion-free and finitely
presented.

This route is unusual in the cluster in that its analytic prerequisite makes
no reference to the mark and no spectral hypothesis: it asks only that one
canonical subspace be generating.  The price is that the subspace may be
zero, which is where all the difficulty now sits.
