Citation: Gao–Jackson–Seward, arXiv:1201.0513, Theorem `INTRO PERFDENSE` (`chapter01.tex` l.156–158), quoted verbatim in the
claim. With `k = 2` and `U = 2^G`, the theorem gives a minimal hyper aperiodic point. Its orbit closure is minimal by the
definition of a minimal point, and free by the definition of hyper aperiodicity.
