The full derivation is in artifact part 2, §§2–4. Summary of the steps:

1. **Construction.**
   - `Σ_0 = {s,t,w}`, `E_m = {e < m : ¬H(e,m−1)}` with `H(e,m)` = "`M_e^S` halts within `m` steps".
   - `τ_m(c) = s^{k_m}·π_m(c)·W_m·t` with `k_m = 2 + S(m)`, and `W_m = w s t` followed by the carriers.
   - Payloads have constant length, contain no `s`, and are injective. `π_m(x_e) = x_e w^{p−1}` and `π_m(y_e) = y_e w^{p−1}` while `e ∈ E_{m+1} ∩ E_m`; the other payloads are words over `{w,t}`.
   - `B_{m+1}(c) = B_m(τ_m(c))`, and `X` is the subshift generated by all blocks.
2. **Recognizability.** Runs of at least two letters `s` occur only as markers. By induction, boundaries and letters are local.
3. **Minimality and aperiodicity.** `W_m` contains every letter. A period would be divisible by every `L_m`.
4. **Degree of the language.** Words of length `≤ L_m` are the subwords of `B_{m+2}(s)`, so `L(X) ≤_T S`. Conversely `L(X)` decodes `k_m` (the letter pattern `t s s s`) and `Σ_{m+1}` level by level, so `S ≤_T L(X)`.
5. **Counts.** `d_m(c) = #x_e − #y_e` over level-`(e+1)` sub-blocks.
   - While `e` runs: `d_m(x_e) = 1`, `d_m(y_e) = −1`, `d_m(other) = 0`, and `d_m(W_m) = 0`.
   - Once `e` halts: `d ≡ 0` from the retirement level on.
6. **Halting ⇒ coboundary.** The within-block count `g` is continuous, and `f_e = g∘T − g`.
7. **Not halting ⇒ not a coboundary.** A transfer function of radius `r < L_m` is constant at level-`(m+1)` boundaries, which all have the window `(end of B_m(t))(start of B_m(s))`. So every level-`(m+1)` block sums to 0, but `d_{m+1}(x_e) = 1`.
8. **Degrees.**
   - `WP(G_X) ≡_T L(X) ≡_T S` by `subshift-elementary-group-word-problem-degree`.
   - `S′ ≤_m {e : e_13(1_{A_e}) ∼ e_13(1_{B_e})}` by the transvection criterion. The words are computable from `S ≤_T WP ≤_T CP`, so `S′ ≤_T CP`.
   - `CP ≤_T L(X)′ = S′` by the conjugator search.
