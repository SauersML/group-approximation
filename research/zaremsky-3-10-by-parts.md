Problem 3.10 asks three things: whether every `Out(F_m)` embeds in some
`Aut(F_n)`, and whether every closed-surface `MCG(S_g)` embeds in some
`Out(F_n)`, respectively in some `Aut(F_n)` ("and/or"). Deciding each of them,
in either direction, answers the problem. This is the only admissible route into
the root.
