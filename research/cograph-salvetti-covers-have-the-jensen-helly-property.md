Let `Γ` be a finite P4-free graph (a cograph) with clique number `k ≥ 1`.
Then the Salvetti cover `X_Γ`, with the l^1 metric on cubes and vertex set
`A_Γ`, has the Jensen–Helly property of dimension `k` from
`jensen-helly-graphs-have-contractible-rips-complexes`:

- Jensen barycentres (J);
- Helly number `k+1` for balls (H);
- rounding within `k/2` (R).
