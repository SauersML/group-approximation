title: An elementary amenable group of type FP_infinity is virtually soluble

If `G` is elementary amenable and of type `FP_∞` over `Z`, then `G` has a soluble
subgroup of finite index.

Kropholler–Martínez-Pérez–Nucinkis, *Cohomological finiteness conditions for elementary
amenable groups*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 637 (2009), 49–62, p. 4 of the preprint, as
cited by St. John-Green. Citation route: `ea-fp-infinity-virtually-soluble-citation`.
