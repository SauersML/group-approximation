This is a citation import, not a reproof.

**Source.** S. Gelaki, D. Nikshych, *Nilpotent fusion categories*, Adv. Math. 217 (2008), no. 3,
1053--1071, arXiv:math/0610726v2. The PDF was fetched on 2026-09-20 and its text extracted with `pypdf`.
The quotations below are verbatim from that extraction. Page numbers are those of the arXiv PDF.

**Section 6.2, page 15 (definition of the centralizer and criterion (18)).**

> In [Mu] M. Müger introduced the centralizer K′ of K, which is a full tensor subcategory of C. In the
> case when C is pseudounitary and modular, K′ is the fusion category generated by all simple objects
> X_i of C such that (18) s_ij = d_i d_j for all simple X_j ∈ K. If (18) holds we will say that X_i and
> X_j centralize each other.

**Page 16.**

> Recall that C_pt denotes the maximal pointed fusion subcategory of C; i.e., the subcategory generated
> by all invertible objects of C.
> Corollary 6.9. Let C be a pseudounitary modular category. Then C_ad = (C_pt)′.

**Section 6.2, page 15 (spherical structure).**

> From now on we will assume that C is a pseudounitary modular category. By [ENO, Proposition 8.23],
> there is a canonical spherical structure on C with respect to which the categorical dimensions of
> objects coincide with their Frobenius-Perron dimensions.

The target's (GN1) is (18), and (GN2) is Corollary 6.9. (GN3) is derived in the target from these two.

**Trust surface.**
- The proofs were not re-checked. Corollary 6.9 rests on Theorem 6.8 and Müger's double centralizer
  theorem.
- The S-matrix convention of the source is not restated there. The target explains why (GN3) does not
  depend on that convention.
