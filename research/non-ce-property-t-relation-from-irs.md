**Established by citation.**  Manzoor arXiv:2502.06697v2, Theorem 1.1 and the
joint proof of Theorems 1.0--1.1, give this transfer.  Choose the
non-co-hyperlinear IRS ergodic by extremal separation.  Its GNS algebra embeds
in the relation algebra of an ergodic p.m.p. action realizing the IRS.
Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana, cited there as Theorem D, embed that relation algebra in
one coming from a property-`(T)` ergodic relation.  The embedded non-CE
subalgebra forbids Connes embeddability of the target factor.

This route deliberately ends at a relation factor.  It does not identify the
factor as `L(Gamma)` for a discrete group and therefore does not feed
[[non-hyperlinear-group]].
