The direct-sum vector in `(SDH1)` has norm one, and its matrix coefficient
sees only the first summand, proving `(SDH2)`.  The difference
`beta'_n(g)-1` is supported on a block of dimension `dim(K_n)` and has
operator norm at most two.  Normalizing the Hilbert--Schmidt norm by the
total dimension gives `(SDH3)`.  Direct sums preserve asymptotic
multiplicativity, so the construction stays inside the same class of
finite-dimensional HS models.
