title: One exact factor preserves nowhere scatteredness under separable minimal tensor products
---

Let `A` and `B` be separable, nowhere scattered C-star algebras.  If at least
one of `A` and `B` is exact, then

```text
A tensor_min B
```

is nowhere scattered.

In particular, if `A` and `B` are separable pure C-star algebras and one is
exact, no irreducible representation of their minimal tensor product can be
GCR or have image containing a nonzero compact operator.  This is a
permanence theorem for nowhere scatteredness, not a proof that the tensor
product is pure.
