Asserted while testing whether the connectivity operator could be treated as the inverse of a graph Laplacian, which would have made an expansion-based proof of critical L2 boundedness plausible.

## Attempts

The session tested the sign property directly and disproved it: on the four-cycle at p=1/2 the exact inverse has a strictly positive off-diagonal entry. Withdrawn in the same response in which it was proposed. See fpbs-inverse-connectivity-matrix-not-laplacian.
