Let `rho:E_n(R)->U(Q_d)` be a norm-corona representation.

For every finite additive subgroup `A<=(R,+)` and root position `(i,j)`, the
finite Fourier projections

```text
P_(A,chi)=|A|^(-1) sum_(a in A) conjugate(chi(a)) rho(x_ij(a))
```

define an exact `D_d^+`-valued measure

```text
mu_rho(A,chi)=Dim_Q(P_(A,chi)).
```

These measures refine exactly under inclusions of finite additive subgroups.

More generally, choose finite additive subgroups `A,B,C` with `AB<=C` and
three distinct root indices.  The group generated by

```text
x_ij(A), x_jk(B), x_ik(C)
```

is finite and two-step nilpotent, with multiplication recorded by

```text
[x_ij(a),x_jk(b)]=x_ik(ab).
```

Every irreducible representation of this finite Heisenberg window therefore
has a well-defined multiplicity rank germ in `D_d^+`.  Restriction between
nested windows gives integral branching maps.  Hence a corona representation
determines a compatible inverse system encoding finite addition and finite
multiplication simultaneously.
