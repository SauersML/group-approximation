Failure of pure infiniteness, together with the exact property-(SP)
equivalence, supplies a nonzero hereditary subalgebra `D` with no nonzero
projections.  Since `A` is simple, `D` is full.  It may be chosen generated by
one positive element, hence sigma-unital.  Brown's stabilization theorem for
the full hereditary inclusion gives

```text
D tensor K isomorphic to A tensor K.
```

In particular the stabilization contains a projection corresponding to
`1_A`.  The stabilized-projection theorem says that every such nonzero
projection is properly infinite.  This proves all three assertions while
showing exactly why the obstruction disappears after stabilization.
