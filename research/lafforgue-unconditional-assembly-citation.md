Published theorem: V. Lafforgue, *K-théorie bivariante pour les algèbres de Banach et
conjecture de Baum–Connes*, Invent. Math. 149 (2002) 1–95.

Read from Lafforgue's own ICM 2002 survey of that paper (arXiv:math/0304342), extracted on
MSI:
- Theorem 1.6.2: "For any group G in the classes a′), b) or c′), and for any unconditional
  completion A(G) of C_c(G), μ_A : K_*^G(EG) → K_*(A(G)) is an isomorphism."
- The class statement before Theorem 1.5.3: a′) and c′) "respectively contain all closed
  subgroups of reductive Lie groups, and all hyperbolic groups".

Cross-check: Gomez Aparicio–Julg–Valette (arXiv:1905.10081), §6.1.2, puts real
semisimple Lie groups, p-adic reductive groups and their closed subgroups in Lafforgue's
class C′, and says the unconditional-completion assembly map is an isomorphism for them.
