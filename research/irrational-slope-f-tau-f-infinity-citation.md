Citation import, not a reproof. José Burillo, Brita Nucinkis and Lawrence Reeves, *An
irrational-slope Thompson's group*, arXiv:1806.00108, `ftaurevised3.tex` fetched on MSI,
l.137:

> "Let $\tau$ be the small golden ratio $\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}=0.6180339887...$, which is a
> zero of the polynomial $X^2+X-1$. We will consider the ring $\zz[\tau]$ of elements of
> the type $a+b\tau$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers. Observe that $\tau=(1+\tau)^{-1}$ is
> a unit of this ring, hence we can consider the group
> $G([0,1];\zz[\tau],\left<\tau\right>)$ of piecewise linear orientation preserving
> homeomorphisms of $[0,1]$ having breakpoints in the ring $\zz[\tau]$ and slopes in the
> subgroup $\langle \tau \rangle$ of units of this ring. ... Following Cleary,
> \cite{clearyirr}, we denote this group \ft. Cleary proved that the group is of type
> $F_\infty$, so in particular, it is finitely presented, however no explicit finite
> presentation was given \cite{clearyirr}."

The cited paper is Sean Cleary, *Regular subdivision in Z[(1+√5)/2]*, Illinois J. Math.
44(3):453--464, 2000 (bibliography of arXiv:2006.02401, `ttau.bbl`). The same paper gives
an explicit finite presentation of `F_τ` (l.83, "We construct presentations, both finite
and infinite").

**Trust surface.** Cleary's paper was not read. The statement is quoted from
Burillo--Nucinkis--Reeves.
