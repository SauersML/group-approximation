Lemma 6.1. For G=<s_1,...,s_d>, H_i=<s_i,g>, ell=ord(g), C(alpha)-1 <= sum_i(C(alpha|H_i)-1)+(d-1)/ell, with 1/infinity=0. The proof uses a finite cyclic transversal and correct normalized induction, or the aperiodic relative bound. No group-cost minimum, action substitution, or finite-index multiplicativity is used.
