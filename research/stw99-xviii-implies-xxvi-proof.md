Assume the positive statement of Problem XVIII.  Let `A` satisfy the
hypotheses of Problem XXVI.  The isomorphism
`Cu(A) ~= Cu(A tensor Z)` makes `A` pure by the standard Cuntz-semigroup
regularity equivalences, hence `A` has strict comparison.  The assumed
Toms--Winter statement therefore makes `A` Z-stable.  This proves the
conditional implication as a theorem; it is recorded as a claim rather than
as a dependency edge so that the converse reduction through Problem XXVII
does not create a circular build graph.
