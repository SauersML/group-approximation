Notation as in `toeplitz-defect-ideal-unitization-k1-injective`: `A_T = T_2 * T_2`, `e = iota_1(p_3)` with
`p_3 = 1 - s_1s_1^* - s_2s_2^*`, and `J = <e>`.

1. **Quotient.** Dividing a full free product by the ideal generated by an ideal of one factor gives the
   free product of the quotients. Here `iota_1(T_2)` modulo the ideal generated by `p_3`, which is
   `K ⊆ T_2`, is `O_2`, so `A_T/J ≅ O_2 *_C T_2`.
2. **The quotient is K1-injective.** `O_2 * T_2` contains `O_2` unitally, so it is properly infinite with
   `[1] = 0`. By `trivial-unit-class-lxi-iff-universal-halving-homotopy`, the halving homotopy
   `o2-free-circle-halving-homotopy` makes every such algebra K1-injective.
3. **Extension.** If `J~` is K1-injective (`toeplitz-defect-ideal-unitization-k1-injective`), then
   `k1-injectivity-extends-over-properly-infinite-quotients` makes `A_T` K1-injective.
4. **Universality.** Blanchard arXiv:0804.4624v13, Proposition 4.2, (i) => (v), read from the PDF:
   "`T_2 *_C T_2` is K_1-injective" implies "Any unital properly infinite C\*-algebra A is K_1-injective".
   The proof goes through BRR Proposition 5.1 (iii).

**K-theory of J.** Germain's six-term sequence for full free products of nuclear algebras gives:
- `K_*(O_2 * T_2) = (0, 0)`, because `[1] -> (0, [1])` is injective into `K_0(O_2) ⊕ K_0(T_2) = 0 ⊕ Z`.
- `K_*(A_T) = (Z, 0)` (Blanchard, Lemma 4.4).

The six-term sequence of `0 -> J -> A_T -> O_2 * T_2 -> 0` then gives `K_1(J) = 0` and `K_0(J) ≅ Z`. So
K1-injectivity of `J~` means that `U(J~)` is connected.

Since LXI implies the halving homotopy, a counterexample to LXI is either a counterexample to the halving
homotopy (the `[1] = 0` case) or a non-null unitary in `J~`. Whether LXI implies K1-injectivity of `J~` is
not known.
