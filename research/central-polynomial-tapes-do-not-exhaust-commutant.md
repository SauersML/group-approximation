ESTABLISHED NO-GO.  Let a finite packet act on its selected carrier as

```text
 rho(F) tensor I_k  on C^r tensor C^k.                 (FCP1)
```

Suppose finitely many authenticated tape words `T_1,...,T_s` centralize the
packet and generate a full factor `M_q(C)` on part of the multiplicity
coordinate.  Fixed central polynomials and standard identities can certify
the simple degree `q` of this **named** algebra.  They cannot certify

```text
 alg(T_1,...,T_s)=Comm(rho(F))=M_k(C).                 (FCP2)
```

Indeed amplify the entire representation by an identity spectator of size
`l>1`.  The named tape algebra becomes

```text
 M_q(C) tensor I_l,                                   (FCP3)
```

with the same PI degree and the same values of every fixed central
polynomial, while the actual packet commutant becomes

```text
 M_k(C) tensor M_l(C)=M_(kl)(C).                      (FCP4)
```

Thus `(FCP2)` fails after amplification even though every group relation and
every normalized word statistic is unchanged.

More generally, a finite tower of named commuting tapes does not repair the
problem.  At each stage the amplified named words are the old words tensored
with `I_l`; the new spectator factor remains in the ambient commutant and is
not generated by those words.  Group relations can assert that named tapes
lie in a commutant.  Equality with the **entire** commutant quantifies over
all ambient matrices and is not a group-relation property.

Consequently PI degree, a fixed central polynomial, the center of a named
factor, and its abstract simple-block count detect only the authenticated
word algebra, never anonymous spectator multiplicity.  The Amitsur--Levitzki
dimension oracle remains correct when granted arbitrary variables in the
full commutant; transporting that grant into finitely many group words is
exactly the missing matrix-coordinate compiler.

This does not exclude a conductor/type observable intrinsic to a named
group element.  Such a conductor is amplification invariant and can label a
genuine representation type, but it is not raw matrix dimension; an
unbounded family still needs canonical tightness or a non-Folner return to
prevent mass escaping to later conductors.
