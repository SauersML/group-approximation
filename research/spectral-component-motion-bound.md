title: Compression moves a commuting-orbit witness only within connected spectral components
distinct_from:
  zero-dimensional-spectral-collapse: That claim is the qualitative endpoint at component diameter zero; this one is the quantitative bound for arbitrary spectrum, and it explains exactly which witnesses can escape.
---

In the setting of `zero-dimensional-spectral-collapse` but with arbitrary
spectrum: for every `gamma in L`,

```text
‖ pi(gamma) v pi(gamma)^* - v ‖ <= sup { diam(C) : C a connected
                                          component of sigma(v) }.
```

More precisely, at every character of the commutative algebra generated by
`v` and one conjugate, the two scalar values lie in the same connected
component of `sigma(v)`.

**Consequences.**  A compression witness that genuinely survives an
`L`-translate in operator norm must carry a nontrivial connected spectral
arc.  The slow walls of `corona-commutant-growth-witness` and
`infinite-cyclic-compression-lamp-mf` have exactly that property — their
phases fill an arc — so this bound is the sharp conceptual border between
the collapsing (discrete-spectrum) and surviving (connected-spectrum)
regimes.
