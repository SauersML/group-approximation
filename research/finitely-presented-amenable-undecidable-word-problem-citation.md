Kharlampovich, *A finitely presented solvable group with unsolvable word
problem*, constructs a finitely presented solvable group of derived length
three with unsolvable word problem.  The Russian original is in
`Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR Ser. Mat. 45 (1981), no. 4, 852--873`; the English
translation is `Math. USSR-Izv. 19 (1982), no. 1, 151--169`.

The Math-Net primary record and abstract were checked on 2026-08-24.  Its
abstract states exactly the existence of a finitely presented solvable group
of length three with unsolvable word problem.  Solvable groups are amenable,
and [[amenable-implies-operator-mf]] supplies the operator-MF conclusion under
Cairn's convention.
