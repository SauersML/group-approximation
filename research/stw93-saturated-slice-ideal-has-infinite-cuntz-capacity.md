For the XCIII central sequence algebra, retain the notation

```text
D=(C*_r(F_2) tensor Z)_omega intersect (C*_r(F_2) tensor Z)',
K_Z^sat=Ideal_D({theta(E):theta:F(Z)->D unital}),
E=J_(tau_Z) intersect F(Z).
```

Then, for every `a in (K_Z^sat)_+` and every `n>=1`,

```text
n[a]<=[1_D] in Cu(D).                                  (1)
```

Thus every nonzero positive element of `K_Z^sat`, not merely every nonzero
spectral cut, has infinite central Cuntz capacity.

The proof uses two established actual-XCIII inputs.  Every normalized
`2`-quasitrace on `D` annihilates the saturated ideal, so all quasitracial
dimension functions vanish on `a`.  The algebra `D` is pure and hence has
strict comparison.  Comparing `a` separately into arbitrarily many
orthogonal full plateau elements in one canonical unital copy of `Z` gives
`(1)`.

Consequently any finite-capacity spectral cut witnessing an extra ordinary
trace must remain nonzero in the residual ideal

```text
(J_tau intersect D)/K_Z^sat.
```

This does not prove that the residual ideal is zero or quasitrace-null and
does not solve XCIII.
