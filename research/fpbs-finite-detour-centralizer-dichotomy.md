Lemma 6.1. For finite K of size t and a outside K in exponent N, either k disjoint first-return power sets exist or some w outside K satisfies |K intersect C_G(w)|>=t/((k-1)(N-1)). Its relative extension costs at most (k-1)(N-1)/t. The second case requires k>=2.
