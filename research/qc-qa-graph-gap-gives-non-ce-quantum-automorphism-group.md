**ESTABLISHED** by the written proof in
`qc-qa-graph-gap-gives-non-ce-quantum-automorphism-group-proof`.  It has not
been reviewed by an ex-verify lane.

Let `X, Y` be finite simple graphs.  `A(Iso(X,Y))` is the universal unital
*-algebra generated by a magic unitary `V=(v_(xa))` with `A_X V = V A_Y`
(BCEHPSW Remark 4.3).  Suppose

```text
X ≅_qc Y        (A(Iso(X,Y)) != 0, equivalently it has a tracial state),
X not ≅_qa Y    (no unital *-homomorphism A(Iso(X,Y)) -> R^U).       (QG1)
```

Replace `(X,Y)` by `(X^c,Y^c)` if `X` is disconnected.  This does not change
the algebra, and then both graphs are connected.  Put `Z = X ⊔ Y`, let
`G = Qut(Z)`, and let `U=(u_(cd))` be the magic unitary of `O(G)`.  Then:

1. `P = sum_(b in Y) u_(xb)` does not depend on `x in X`.  It is a nonzero
   central projection of `O(G)`, and its Haar trace is `h(P) = 1/2`.
2. `v_(xa) |-> u_(xa)` is a unital *-homomorphism `A(Iso(X,Y)) -> P O(G)`,
   and `2 h` restricted to it is a tracial state.
3. The Haar von Neumann algebra `L^infinity(G) = pi_h(O(G))''` is not Connes
   embeddable.  Equivalently, the discrete dual of the Kac quantum group
   `Qut(Z)` is not hyperlinear.

For isomorphism games the trace-selection problem of `mipstar-equals-re`
therefore disappears: every tracial state that the gap needs is a corner of a
Haar state.  A pair as in `(QG1)` is
`qc-isomorphic-not-qa-isomorphic-graph-pair-exists`.  No such pair is known.
A finitely presented nonhyperlinear group would supply one through
linear-system graphs.  A bounded literature search (artifact §6) found no
non-Connes-embeddable Kac quantum group.
