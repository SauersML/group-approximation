## Direct proof, machine-checked

Four steps, none of which needs the algebra to be universal or presented.

1. **Corner.**  `AryCorner`: the corner of `A` at `e = p_0 + ... + p_{d-2}`
   carries a unital *binary* Leavitt family, and the corner inherits
   countability and nontriviality.

2. **Finite-type core.**  `LeavittFamily.coreSubalgebra` is the `k`-subalgebra
   generated by the four elements of that binary family; it is finite type by
   construction (`coreSubalgebra_finiteType`), nontrivial
   (`coreSubalgebra_nontrivial`), and carries the family (`coreFamily`).  So the
   finite-type hypothesis of the pipeline costs nothing.

3. **Pipeline.**  `FamilyRankFour` makes the unit group, every `GL_{m+1}` and
   every `EL_{m+1}` with `m >= 1` over that core nonsofic -- via property `(T)`
   at ranks three and four over a finite field and the non-LEF corner witness.

4. **Extension.**  The corner-extension homomorphisms `cornerUnitsExtend` and
   `cornerElementaryExtend`, together with `elementaryGroupMap_injective` and
   `Units.map_injective` along the subalgebra inclusion, are injective, and
   `isSofic_of_injective` runs the conclusion backwards: soficity upstairs would
   force soficity of the core's groups.  `Matrix.scalar` embeds `A^x` in
   `GL_{m+1}(A)`, which is how the `GL` statement reduces to the unit one.
