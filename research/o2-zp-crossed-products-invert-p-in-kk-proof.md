1. `O_2` is separable, unital and KK-contractible.  Any two unital embeddings of
   `O_2` into `O_2` are approximately unitarily equivalent, and
   `id ⊕ id ≅ id` through `s_1, s_2`, so `[id] = 2[id]`.  Hence
   `KK(O_2, O_2) = 0`, and the same holds for `O_2 ⊗ M_p`.
2. `C = O_2 ⋊_α Z_p` is separable and unital.  The dual action `α̂` of
   `Ẑ_p ≅ Z_p` is generated by `β` with `β^p = id`.
3. Takai duality for the finite abelian group `Z_p` gives
   `C ⋊_β Z_p = (O_2 ⋊_α Z_p) ⋊_(α̂) Ẑ_p ≅ O_2 ⊗ M_p`.  Concretely, the algebra
   acts on `ℓ²(Z_p) ⊗ O_2`: `a ∈ O_2` as `diag(α_h^(−1)(a))`, the group as
   translations, the dual group as multiplications.  These generate
   `M_p(O_2)`.  So `C ⋊_β Z_p` is KK-contractible by step 1.
4. `order-p-automorphism-kk-cyclotomic-structure` applies to `(C, β)` and
   gives every bullet of the claim, including the contractibility of
   `C ⋊_β Z`.

The consequences listed in the claim are commentary.  Only the three bullets and
the module statement are established here.  Consequence 1 explicitly stops
before the Milnor-sequence step, and consequence 3 is labelled heuristic.
