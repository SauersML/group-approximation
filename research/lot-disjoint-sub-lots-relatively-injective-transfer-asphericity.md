Let `P` be a reduced labeled oriented tree with maximal proper sub-LOTs
`T_1..T_n`, pairwise disjoint and each aspherical. Collapse each `T_i` to a chosen
vertex `t_i`, relabeling edge labels from `T_i` by `t_i`. If the resulting quotient
LOT is injective, then `P` is aspherical.
