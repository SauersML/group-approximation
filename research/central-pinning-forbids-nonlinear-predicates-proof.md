Uniqueness of the `J=-1` irreducible makes the `(1-J)/2` summand of the
group algebra a single full matrix algebra of dimension `|H|/2`, and
character orthogonality then forces `chi_pi` to vanish off `{1,J}`.
Restricting a character supported on `{1,J}` to an abelian subgroup gives
every character of the subgroup (compatible with `J`) the same
multiplicity.  Section 4 of the artifact.
