title: Locally equivalent graphings transfer small separator costs with a loss independent of component size

For locally equivalent graphings of maximum degree d, fractional
k-separation numbers agree. The integral separation number on the
second graphing is at most t log_2(8d/t), where t is the separation
number on the first, in the conventions of Lovasz. In particular
the bound tends to zero with t independently of k. The result is
Theorems 11--12 and Corollary 13 of arXiv:1709.03179v2.
