Let `G` be of type F, with a finite `K(G,1)` `Y`. The skeleta of `Y` are finite,
so `G` is of type `F_∞`. Cellular chains of the universal cover give a free
resolution of length `dim Y`, so `cd G ≤ dim Y < ∞`. By
`f-infinity-finite-cd-groups-have-contractible-rips`, `G` has a contractible Rips
complex. The cycle with `f-infinity-fcd-contractible-rips-from-1-21-and-4-07` is
intended.
