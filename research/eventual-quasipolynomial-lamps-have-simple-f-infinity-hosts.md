**OPEN — conditional manuscript extension; no independent certification or priority claim.**

For `m≥2,q≥2,d≥0,h≥1`, let `E_{d,h}` be the additive group of integer
sequences which on each residue class modulo `h` agree eventually at
each end with an integer-valued polynomial of degree at most `d`, with
arbitrary finitely many exceptions. Then the finitely generated group
`UT_m(Z[1/q])^(Z)⋊(E_{d,h}⋊Z)`, with the coordinatewise scaling and
shift action, embeds in a simple group of type `F_∞` generated by two
finite-order elements.

## Attempts

The proposed proof first embeds eventual polynomial profiles in the
two-root host, then handles residue classes by a finite product and
restores unrestricted shifts through a finite extension of index `h`.
The actor construction and the stronger per-shell faithfulness test are
unresolved in the separate extension lemma. The tower and clopen actor
claims remain unresolved as well. No common fixed host for unbounded
degree or unbounded period is asserted. See artifact §10.
