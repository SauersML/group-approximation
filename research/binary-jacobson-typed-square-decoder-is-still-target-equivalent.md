Let `Gamma_T`, `w_T` and `(JSD1)` be as in
`binary-jacobson-head-root-has-opnorm-square-decoder`.  With the quantifiers
currently written there, the existence of the fixed typed decoder and
modulus is equivalent to

```text
every norm-matrix-corona homomorphism Gamma_T -> U(Q)
sends w_T to I.                                          (JTE1)
```

The forward implication is the valid square-matrix contradiction already
recorded in `binary-jacobson-opnorm-square-decoder-proof`.  The reverse
implication is not constructive Jacobson incidence: `(JTE1)` first supplies
a uniform no-head presentation gap, and below that gap the decoder's
nonzero-cut antecedent is empty.  Above the gap a fixed bounded-error
spectral expression satisfies the requested modulus.

Consequently, occurrence typing, determinism and an unspecified modulus
`omega(epsilon)->0` do not make the current decoder an independent
intermediate problem.  A noncircular replacement must prescribe and verify
an actual `S/T`-bearing occurrence formula with a numerical defect estimate,
or impose a separately proved nonvacuity/calibration condition.  The
implication from such a decoder to head invisibility remains correct, but it
cannot serve as a reduction while the decoder assertion itself is
goal-equivalent.
