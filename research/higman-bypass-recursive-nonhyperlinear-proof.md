Let `G` be finitely generated, recursively presented, and nonhyperlinear.
Higman's embedding theorem gives an injective homomorphism `G -> H` into a
finitely presented group `H`. If `H` were hyperlinear, then restricting each
finite-set matrix approximation of `H` to the embedded copy of any prescribed
finite subset of `G` would prove `G` hyperlinear. This contradicts the
hypothesis, so `H` is finitely presented and nonhyperlinear.

The argument uses only injectivity and subgroup heredity. It does not ask the
embedding to preserve the actor, lamp decomposition, property `(T)`, or a
specific presentation geometry.
