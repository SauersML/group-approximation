**ESTABLISHED** by `lie-closure-trichotomy-without-finite-quotients-proof` (written proof, unreviewed).
The proof is an audited transfer of the two V routes: every lemma of those routes that mentions `G0`
or `V` is re-proved from hypotheses (H1)-(H2) below, and the rest are group-free.

## Setting

- `Gamma0` is a finitely generated group and `R` a finite subset of `Gamma0`. Put `Q = Gamma0 / <<R>>`.
- For a finite-dimensional unitary representation `rho` of `Gamma0`, the **defect** is
  `D(rho) = max_(r in R) ||rho(r) - 1||` (operator norm).
- For `N >= 1`, `delta_N = inf { D(rho) : rho nontrivial unitary, dim rho <= N }`, with `inf {} = +infinity`.
  Put `delta_0 = +infinity`. So `delta_N` is nonincreasing in `N`. It equals the infimum over
  nontrivial **irreducibles** of dimension `<= N`, because the defect of an orthogonal sum is the maximum
  over the summands.
- `gap(Gamma0, R) = inf_N delta_N`.
- Put `eta_0 = min(1/2, delta_1)`. Here `delta_1` is the least defect of a nontrivial character of
  `Gamma0`, or `+infinity` if `Gamma0` is perfect.

Hypotheses:

- **(H1) Fixed dimensions are gapped.** `delta_N > 0` for every `N`.
- **(H2) No finite quotients.** Every homomorphism from `Q` to a finite group is trivial.

For a representation `sigma` let `K_sigma` be the closure of `sigma(Gamma0)`, `l_sigma` its Lie
algebra, and `N_sigma = dim_R [l_sigma, l_sigma]`. For a compact simple Lie algebra `h`, `Inn(h)` is the
group generated by the `exp(ad Y)`. A homomorphism `tau : Gamma0 -> Inn(h)` is measured in an
`Inn(h)`-invariant inner product, with `D(tau) = max_r ||tau(r) - 1||`.

## Statement

Assume (H1) and (H2). Let `sigma` be a nontrivial irreducible unitary representation of `Gamma0`.

1. **(Imprimitive models.)** If `sigma(Gamma0)` permutes the summands of an orthogonal decomposition
   `C^n = E_1 (+) ... (+) E_k` into nonzero subspaces, and the permutation action is nontrivial, then
   `D(sigma) >= sqrt 2`. In particular every representation induced from a proper subgroup of finite
   index has defect `>= sqrt 2`. If `D(sigma) < sqrt 2`, then `sigma` restricted to `K_sigma^0` is
   isotypic, and `Z(K_sigma^0)` acts by scalars.
2. **(Abelian identity component.)** If `K_sigma^0` is abelian, then `D(sigma) >= eta_0`. This covers
   every representation with finite image, or with image finite modulo scalars, or with virtually
   abelian image.
3. **(Adjoint transfer.)** `D(sigma) >= min(eta_0, delta_(N_sigma) / 2)`.
4. **(Fixed-group amplification.)** Let `M` be a compact Lie group, `phi : Gamma0 -> M` a homomorphism
   and `pi` a finite-dimensional unitary representation of `M` with `pi o phi` nontrivial. Then
   `D(pi o phi) >= min(eta_0, delta_(dim M) / 2)`. This holds for every `pi`, in every dimension.
5. **(Density in one simple adjoint group.)** Suppose `D(sigma) < eta_0`. Then `m = [l_sigma, l_sigma]`
   is a nonzero orthogonal sum of simple ideals `h_1, ..., h_r`, each `Ad(K_sigma)`-invariant. For each
   `a`, `tau_a = Ad(sigma(.))|_(h_a)` is a nontrivial homomorphism `Gamma0 -> Inn(h_a)` with **dense
   image**, `D(tau_a) <= 2 D(sigma)`, and `delta_(dim h_a) <= 2 D(sigma)`.
6. **(Converse.)** If there are compact simple `h_j` and nontrivial homomorphisms
   `tau_j : Gamma0 -> Inn(h_j)`, orthogonal for invariant inner products, with `D(tau_j) -> 0`, then
   `gap(Gamma0, R) = 0`. Density is not needed.

**Corollary (classical trichotomy).** For `X` in `{SU, SO, Sp}` let `X(k)` be `su(k)`, `so(k)` or
`sp(k)`, over the `k` for which it is simple, and define

```text
Gap_(Gamma0,R)(X):  exists eps > 0  such that for every k and every homomorphism
                    tau : Gamma0 -> Inn(X(k)) with dense image,   D(tau) >= eps.
```

Then, under (H1) and (H2),

```text
gap(Gamma0, R) > 0   <=>   Gap_(Gamma0,R)(SU)  and  Gap_(Gamma0,R)(SO)  and  Gap_(Gamma0,R)(Sp).
```

The corollary uses the classification of compact simple Lie algebras, and only its finiteness of
exceptional types. Items 1 to 6 do not use it.

## Why it matters

- **One engine, three holes.** The V engine of waves 17 and 18 does not depend on the amalgam. Its only
  inputs are (H1), (H2) and the number `delta_1`. So every "not MF" reduction of this shape, over a
  group with positive fixed-dimension gaps, splits into the same three classical gaps.
- **Invariant and death step.** The invariant is `N_sigma`. In every model class where it stays bounded,
  the argument fails at the same step. The adjoint representation on `[l_sigma, l_sigma]_C` is a
  nontrivial representation of dimension `N_sigma` with defect at most `2 D`, and `delta_(N_sigma)`
  gaps it. The model classes killed this way, uniformly in dimension, include:
  - finite quotients;
  - imprimitive and induced representations;
  - virtually abelian images;
  - pullbacks from one fixed compact group, such as its tensor powers, Schur functors and
    highest-weight towers.
- **What survives.** Only dense homomorphisms into `PSU(k)`, `SO(k)/{+-1}` (or `SO(k)`) and
  `Sp(k)/{+-1}` with `k -> infinity`.
