Under `(KSB1)`, the distinct projective basis lines form a cancellative
subsemigroup of `U(M)/T`.  Every line squares to the identity line, so the
subsemigroup is a group of exponent two and therefore abelian.  Choosing
representatives gives a scalar two-cocycle.  Orthonormality identifies the
trace with the twisted regular trace, while completeness identifies the
whole von Neumann algebra with the twisted group factor.  Twisted group von
Neumann algebras of amenable groups are injective, hence hyperfinite in the
separable finite case.
