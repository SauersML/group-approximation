The central sequence algebra `(C*_r(F₂) ⊗ 𝒵)_ω ∩ (C*_r(F₂) ⊗ 𝒵)'` has a unique
quasitrace, namely the limit trace.  Equivalent to STW Problem XCIII.

## Attempts

The natural quotient by the trace-kernel ideal is already a II₁ factor and has
a unique quasitrace.  Thus it remains only to prove that every quasitrace on the
central sequence algebra annihilates the kernel.  Exactness of
`C*_r(F₂) ⊗ 𝒵` does not by itself give exactness of its sequence algebra, so
Haagerup's theorem cannot simply be applied there.  The tensor-factor central
sequences are quasitracially rigid, but this gives no control on mixed or
free-factor trace-kernel sequences.
