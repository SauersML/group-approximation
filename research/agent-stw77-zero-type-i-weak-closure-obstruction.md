There is a unital nuclear-dimension-zero inclusion `phi:A->B` such that
every nonzero positive element of `phi(A)` is properly infinite in `B`, but
the following two weak-closure reductions both fail.

1. The von Neumann algebra generated by a representation of `B` need not be
   type I.
2. The von Neumann algebra generated only by the represented range may be
   finite, so the proper-image hypothesis need not survive after replacing
   the ambient algebra by that smaller weak closure.

Thus `stw77-type-i-von-neumann-targets-solve-map` cannot close
`stw77-nucdim-zero-case` merely by using either the bidual or the weak
closure of the range in an arbitrary chosen representation.  (A faithful
irreducible representation can instead have type-I closure; the theorem
then applies there, but still gives no descent.)  A valid general bridge
must additionally return the amplified finite-dimensional models or central
isometries to `B` in operator norm.
