OPEN.  For `C=SL_3(Z)<A=SL_3(Z[1/2])`, there is no trace-preserving
embedding `pi:L(A)->M` into a tracial matrix ultraproduct which is a uniform
ambient-extension outlier and carries projections

```text
q_(r,j)^x       (r>=1, 0<=j<2^r, x in A/C)
```

satisfying `(DPT2)--(DPT5)` of
`free-hnn-outlier-has-dyadic-a2-pvm-tower`, with the algebras generated at
distinct cosets freely independent in the canonical lamp trace.

The quantification over every depth is essential.  At any fixed depth and
on any fixed finite coset set, finite free-lamp moment models and Morita
tensoring realize the same scalar data.  A proof must use the common actor
to propagate the nested atoms through an unbounded family of rank-two
loops, or find a dimension-free obstruction to that propagation.  Merely
reapplying one balanced-involution fold estimate discards exactly the
coherence retained here.
