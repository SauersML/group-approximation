Tatiana Shulman, *Homotopy lifting, asymptotic homomorphisms, and
traces*, arXiv:2508.00125v5, Theorem 28 and Corollary 32, proves exactly
the target statement. Taken together, those results cover the two
alternatives that `A` is exact or `B` is exact.

The paper works with separable C-star algebras and uses the same notion of
homotopy domination displayed in the target. No UCT, nuclearity,
faithfulness, or unitality hypothesis is added.
