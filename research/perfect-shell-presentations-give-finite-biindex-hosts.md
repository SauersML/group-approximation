If the required OPEN presentation hypothesis holds, choose its
enumeration and put Gamma=E_nu. The action on Omega is doubly
transitive since it contains V. Thus the point stabilizer H has
exactly two double cosets in Gamma: the diagonal and the orbit
of distinct pairs. This proves the new host hypothesis conditional
on the older one, without asserting the older hypothesis.
