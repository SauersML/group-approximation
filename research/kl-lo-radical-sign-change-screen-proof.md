The notation is that of the target claim.

**Step 0 (change of variable).** For `y ∈ G` let `φ_y` be the endomorphism of
`G * <t>` that fixes `G` and sends `t ↦ t y`.
- It is an automorphism, with inverse `t ↦ t y^(-1)`.
- `φ_y(<<w>>) = <<φ_y(w)>>`, so `φ_y` induces an isomorphism
  `(G * <t>)/<<w>> -> (G * <t>)/<<φ_y(w)>>` that is the identity on `G`.
- Hence `G` injects for `w` iff it injects for `φ_y(w)`.

**Step 1 (coefficients after the shift).** Substitute `t = t y` and
`t^(-1) = y^(-1) t^(-1)` letter by letter in
`w = c_1 t^(e_1) ... c_N t^(e_N)`. The coefficient `c'_j` standing between the
letters `t^(e_(j-1))` and `t^(e_j)` of `φ_y(w)` depends on the pair of signs:

| `(e_(j-1), e_j)` | `c'_j`             |
|------------------|--------------------|
| `(+1, +1)`       | `y c_j`            |
| `(+1, -1)`       | `y c_j y^(-1)`     |
| `(-1, +1)`       | `c_j`              |
| `(-1, -1)`       | `c_j y^(-1)`       |

- **Sign-change coefficients** are conjugates of the old ones. `R(G)` is
  normal, so by (a) they lie outside `R(G)`; in particular they are `≠ 1`.
- **Same-sign coefficients** map to `ȳ π(c_j)` or `π(c_j) ȳ^(-1)` in `Q`, where
  `ȳ = π(y)`. They lie outside `R(G)` iff `ȳ ∉ S`, where `S` is the finite set
  of all `π(c_j)^(-1)` over `(+,+)` positions and all `π(c_j)` over `(-,-)`
  positions.

**Step 2 (choice of `y`).**
- A nontrivial right-orderable group is torsion-free, hence infinite. So by
  (b) there is `ȳ ∈ Q ∖ S`; let `y` be any lift.
- Then every `c'_j ≠ 1`, so `φ_y(w)` is cyclically reduced of syllable length
  `2N`, with syllables `c'_j ∈ G ∖ {1}` and `t^(±1)`.
- Every `c'_j` lies outside `R(G)`.

**Step 3 (the conditions of the stacking theorem).** Let `A'` be the subgroup
generated by the `c'_j`. Put `p = π|A': A' -> Q`, which is right-orderable, and
`q = id` on `<t>`.
- **(L)** holds by Step 2.
- **(P).** The image of `φ_y(w)` in `Q * <t>` equals `φ_ȳ(π(w))`, where `φ_ȳ` is
  the automorphism `t ↦ t ȳ` of `Q * <t>`. Automorphisms carry proper powers to
  proper powers and back, so (c) gives (P).

`orderable-quotient-stackings-inject-the-kernel` (Kervaire–Laudenbach form, with
an arbitrary orderable `p`, followed by the pushout from `A'` to `G`) gives that
`G -> (G * <t>)/<<φ_y(w)>>` is injective. Step 0 transfers this to `w`.

**Localization remark.** Every homomorphism from `G` to a right-orderable group
restricts to one from `A`, so `R(A) ⊆ R(G) ∩ A`. Running Steps 0–3 over `A` and
then applying `kl-failure-localizes-to-the-coefficient-subgroup` gives the
sharper form stated in the target.

**Where the method stops.** The shift `t ↦ t y` conjugates sign-change
coefficients, and conjugation cannot move an element out of a normal subgroup.
The substitutions `t ↦ g t^(±1) h` (`g, h ∈ G`) act on sign-change
coefficients by conjugation and inversion, possibly combined with a cyclic
rotation. So none of them removes case 1. By the sharpness part of
`orderable-quotient-stackings-inject-the-kernel`, no relative stacking of the
image of `w` under any such substitution exists in case 1. Removing case 1
needs a different invariant.
