Section S1 of the artifact. `H_2(Q * <t>) = H_2(Q)`. In the five-term
sequence of `1 -> R -> Q * <t> -> X -> 1`, `R/[R, Q * <t>]` is cyclic on
the class of `w`, whose image in `H_1(Q) (+) Z` has second coordinate
`deg_t(w) != 0`. That class has infinite order, so the map to `H_1` is
injective and `H_2(Q) -> H_2(X)` is onto. The certificate form is Hopf's
formula for the two presentations, using
`[F', R_Y <<w~>>] = [F', R_Y][F', <<w~>>]`.
