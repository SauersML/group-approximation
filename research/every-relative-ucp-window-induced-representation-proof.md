A relative positive-definite kernel gives a finite-dimensional minimal
Stinespring triple `Theta(y)=V_0^* sigma(y)V_0`. Its unitary values on
`B` put `C*(B)` in the multiplicative domain. In each irreducible
`G`-block, minimality makes the multiplicity components of `V_0`
linearly independent in `Hom_B(H_beta,H_pi|_B)`. Hence the multiplicity
of `pi` in `sigma` is no larger than that Hom-space dimension.
Frobenius reciprocity identifies the latter with the multiplicity of
`pi` in `Ind_B^G(beta)`. Thus `sigma` embeds equivariantly into the
single induced representation and its coefficient kernel has the asserted
form.
