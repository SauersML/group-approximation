title: Arbitrary products of sigma-finite type-III factors have unique Jiang--Su embeddings
distinct_from:
  stw63-type3-separable-predual-vn-uniqueness: that permits diffuse center but assumes separable predual; this permits arbitrary product cardinality and nonseparable factor preduals but requires each factor to be sigma-finite.
---

Let

```text
M = product_(j in J) M_j
```

for an arbitrary family of sigma-finite type-III factors.  There is no
countability restriction on `J`, and the factors need not have separable
predual.  Then every two unital embeddings `Z->M` are norm approximately
unitarily equivalent.
