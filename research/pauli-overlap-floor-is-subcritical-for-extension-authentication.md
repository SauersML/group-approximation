The Pauli capacity calculation guarantees one ordered cross-Gram square of
mass at least `5/64` and hence a spectral subcorner of trace at least `5/123`
with singular-value floor `sqrt(5/128)`.  These are positive-density and
positive-injectivity statements, but neither says that the cross-Gram square
is close to the full source projection.

At every fixed non-full overlap, the exact dilution model of
`positive-cross-gram-mass-alone-has-no-holonomy-floor` retains an arbitrary
relative commutant unitary.  Therefore the square-root polar estimate in
`one-cross-gram-moment-extracts-the-common-polar-gauge` cannot be invoked from
the Pauli floor.  Restricting to the canonical intersection projection of
`transported-coefficient-corners-share-flat-quarter-carrier` does not repair
the inference: that projection is a common **range carrier**, while the
missing `S4` authentication concerns labelled reduced source blocks of one
hard/comb transport.

Consequently the compulsory overlap, its positive spectral corner, and the
canonical common quarter carrier do not by themselves isolate the extension
triple inside the fivefold `S3` operator channel.  A mixed word must inspect
the relative phase/type data, or a transverse subgroup fingerprint such as
`a4-s3-hybrid-fingerprint-diagonalizes-s4-extension-triple` must couple to the
same transport.
