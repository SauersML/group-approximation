title: Real-rank-zero algebras without small representations contain arbitrarily large unital matrix blocks
---

Let `B` be a unital C-star algebra of real rank zero and let `N` be a
positive integer.  The following are equivalent:

1. `B` has no nonzero representation on a Hilbert space of dimension less
   than `N`;
2. there are integers `r>=1` and `m_1,...,m_r>=N` and a unital
   star-homomorphism

```text
M_{m_1} direct-sum ... direct-sum M_{m_r} --> B.
```

Only the forward implication is used in the STW Problem XXVII application.
