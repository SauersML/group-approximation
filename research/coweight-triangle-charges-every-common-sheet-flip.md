Fix a prime `p` and put

```text
d_12=diag(p,p^(-1),1),
d_23=diag(1,p,p^(-1)),
d_31=diag(p^(-1),1,p).                                  (CTA1)
```

These are three `Lambda=SL_3(Z)`-conjugate denominator directions and

```text
d_12 d_23 d_31=1.                                       (CTA2)
```

Here `d_31=h^(-1)` for `h=diag(p,1,p^(-1))`, so `(CTA2)` is the
coweight form of the unbalanced torus closure relation in
`two-torus-closures-detect-denominator-multiplicity`.

Let the group generated by them act on a finite probability space `Omega`,
and let `ell:Omega->Z/2` be any label.  Define the failure-to-flip rate

```text
delta_r=Pr[ell(d_r omega)=ell(omega)]
       for r in {12,23,31}.                              (CTA3)
```

Then

```text
delta_12+delta_23+delta_31 >= 1,                         (CTA4)
max(delta_12,delta_23,delta_31) >= 1/3.                  (CTA5)
```

The constant is sharp.  On one copy of `Z/2`, let one direction act
trivially and let the other two act by the flip; the three commuting
permutations have product one and their failure vector is `(1,0,0)`.
Take the disjoint union of the three cyclic variants.  Each `delta_r` is
then `1/3`.  In particular no exact finite atlas can make all three
coweight directions reverse one common two-sheet label.

There is also a dimension-free matrix form.  Let `P` be a projection in a
finite tracial von Neumann algebra and let `U_12,U_23,U_31` be unitaries.
Put

```text
epsilon_r=||U_r P U_r^*-(1-P)||_2,
eta=||U_12 U_23 U_31-1||_2.                             (CTA6)
```

Then

```text
epsilon_12+epsilon_23+epsilon_31+2 eta >= 1.             (CTA7)
```

In particular, when the triangle relation is exact,

```text
max_r epsilon_r >= 1/3,
sum_r epsilon_r^2 >= 1/3.                               (CTA8)
```

The constants do not depend on matrix dimension, projection rank, a
congruence level, or a Kazhdan constant.

Moreover the obstruction occurs exactly at `(CTA2)`.  Away from `p`, the
construction of `two-denominator-edges-retain-the-exact-two-sheet-atlas`
can assign a sheet flip to each of the three directions while satisfying
all three integral overlap-conjugacy tables and all pairwise commutation
relations.  The evaluated word `d_12 d_23 d_31` is then the nontrivial sheet
flip.  Imposing `(CTA2)` destroys the model on every point.

This closes the literal common-Boolean-sheet enemy with a dimension-free
constant.  It does not yet prove `sl3-hnn-central-dual-gap-vanishes`: an
arbitrary matrix or sofic outlier atlas must first be shown to expose one
common Boolean sheet that the three directions approximately reverse.
The affine countermodel in
`three-parahoric-weyl-triangle-has-affine-gauge-countermodel` proves that
separate parahoric projections do not supply that extraction.
