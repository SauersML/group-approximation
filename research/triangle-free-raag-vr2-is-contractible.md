title: The scale-2 Vietoris–Rips complex of a triangle-free RAAG with standard generators is contractible
---

Let `Γ` be a finite triangle-free simplicial graph. Then `VR_2(A_Γ)`, the
Vietoris–Rips complex at scale 2 for the standard word metric, is
contractible.

Imported from Hulbert–Zaremsky, arXiv:2608.25614, Theorem B: route
`triangle-free-raag-vr2-is-contractible-citation`. The same paper predicts
that `VR_t(A_Γ)` is contractible for all `t ≥ n`, where `n` is the maximal
clique size of `Γ`.
