**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `G` be a finitely generated group that is a limit of
left-orderable amenable groups in the space of marked group presentations. Then

  `β₁^(2)(G) ≤ β₁(G) − 1 ≤ nrk(G) − 1`.
