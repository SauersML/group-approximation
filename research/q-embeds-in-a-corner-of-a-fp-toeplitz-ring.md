**ESTABLISHED** through `q-embeds-in-a-corner-of-a-fp-toeplitz-ring-proof` (lane proof,
not independently reviewed). No priority is claimed: the ring is a Toeplitz ring with a
number operator and its resolvent, and the computation is elementary.

**Statement.** Let

    R_T = Z< S, T, N, A | TS = 1,  NS = S(N+1),  N(1 - ST) = 0,  A(N+1) = 1 >,

a finitely presented unital ring (4 generators, 4 relations), and put `E = 1 - ST`. Then
`E` is a nonzero idempotent, and there is an injective unital ring homomorphism
`Q -> E R_T E` with `1/m ↦ E T^(m-1) A S^(m-1) E` for `m >= 1`.

**Concrete model.** On `V = Q^(N)` with basis `f_0, f_1, ...`, the relations hold for
- `S f_k = f_(k+1)`, `T f_k = f_(k-1)` and `T f_0 = 0` (the Toeplitz shifts);
- `N f_k = k f_k` (the number operator);
- `A f_k = f_k/(k+1)` (its resolvent at `-1`).

`E` acts as the projection onto `Q f_0`.

**Why it matters.**
- It is an explicit finitely presented ring containing `Q` (for Belyaev's Higman-type
  theorem for rings, see `fp-simple-ring-of-characteristic-zero-exists`). By
  `integral-form-algebras-have-no-fg-subring-containing-q`, such a ring cannot be found
  inside any combinatorial Leavitt, group or Steinberg algebra.
- It gives `GL_n(Q) <= E_(n+1)(R_T)` (`gl-n-q-embeds-in-e-n-plus-1-of-a-fp-ring`).
- **Limits.** `R_T` is not simple. In the concrete model, the ideal generated by `E` is
  the ring `J` of finitary matrices over `Q`. `J` is simple as a ring and has zero
  annihilator, so every nonzero ideal of the model meets `J`, hence contains it and
  contains `E`. So no simple quotient of the model contains `Q`. The mechanism needs a
  vacuum vector (`N f_0 = 0`), and a vacuum brings finite-rank elements with it. See the
  Attempts of `fp-simple-ring-of-characteristic-zero-exists`.
