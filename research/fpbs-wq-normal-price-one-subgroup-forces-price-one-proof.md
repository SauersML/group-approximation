Complete proof. The successor step is the argument of Theorem 2.45 in Gaboriau's
lecture notes on measure equivalence, cost and L2-Betti numbers
(`https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/ME-Cost-L2-Lectures/ME-Cost-L2-lectures.pdf`).
There the chain is indexed by `N` and each step adds one element. Here the chain
is the transfinite hull, and each step adds countably many elements.

**Facts used from the notes** (text extracted on MSI, 2026-09-12):
- Exercise 1.17 (i)=>(iii): if almost every class of a standard p.m.p.
  equivalence relation is infinite, then for every `eps > 0` there is a
  complete section of measure at most `eps`.
- Proposition 2.15, see also Corollary 2.35: if `C(R) < 1`, then `R` has a
  non-null set of finite classes.
- Theorem 2.20 (Ornstein--Weiss): for an infinite amenable group acting with
  almost all orbits infinite, the orbit relation is generated by a free
  `Z`-action and has a treeing of cost one.

**Setup.**
- Let `Gamma` act freely on a standard probability space `(X, mu)`, preserving
  `mu`, after discarding an invariant null set.
- For `L <= Gamma`, write `R_L` for the orbit relation of the restricted
  action. It is free, so for infinite `L` almost every `R_L`-class is infinite.
- By `fpbs-wq-normal-hull-is-almost-malnormal` item 4, the hull of `H` is
  `Gamma`.
  - Its chain `(W_beta)_(beta <= alpha)` has countable length `alpha`, with
    `W_0 = H` and `W_alpha = Gamma`.
  - `W_(beta+1)` is generated by `W_beta` and the set `S_beta` of `g` with
    `g W_beta g^(-1) ∩ W_beta` infinite.
  - Unions are taken at limits.
- `S_beta` increases with `beta`. Enumerate the countable set `∪_beta S_beta`
  as `g_1, g_2, ...`, and let `beta_k` be the least `beta` with
  `g_k in S_beta`.
- Put `Sigma_k = g_k^(-1) W_(beta_k) g_k ∩ W_(beta_k)`. This is the conjugate by
  `g_k^(-1)` of `g_k W_(beta_k) g_k^(-1) ∩ W_(beta_k)`, so it is infinite.

**Graphing.** Fix `eps > 0`.
- `H` has fixed price one and acts freely, so `C(R_H) = 1`. Choose a graphing
  `Phi_0` of `R_H` of cost at most `1 + eps/2`.
- By Exercise 1.17 choose a Borel complete section `A_k` for `R_(Sigma_k)` with
  `mu(A_k) <= eps/2^(k+1)`. Let `phi_k` be the restriction of `g_k` to `A_k`.
- Let `Phi = Phi_0 ∪ {phi_k : k >= 1}`, and let `R_Phi` be the relation it
  generates. Then `Phi ⊆ R_Gamma` and the cost of `Phi` is at most `1 + eps`.

**Absorption.** Call `g in Gamma` *absorbed* if `gx in [x]_(R_Phi)` for almost
every `x`.
- The exceptional set of an absorbed element is null, and so is its saturation
  under the countable group `Gamma`. So `g` is absorbed on a `Gamma`-invariant
  conull set.
- On the intersection of these sets for countably many absorbed elements, every
  word in them and their inverses is absorbed. For inverses: if `g^(-1)x` lies
  in the invariant set, then `x = g(g^(-1)x) in [g^(-1)x]_(R_Phi)`.
- So the absorbed elements form a subgroup, and a subgroup generated by
  countably many absorbed elements is absorbed.

**Induction: every `W_beta` is absorbed.**
- **`beta = 0`.** `Phi_0` generates `R_H`.
- **Limit `beta`.** `W_beta` is the union of the countably many `W_gamma`,
  `gamma < beta`.
- **Successor.** Assume `W_beta` is absorbed, on a `Gamma`-invariant conull set
  `X_beta`. Let `g_k in S_beta`, so `beta_k <= beta`.
  - Both `Sigma_k` and `g_k Sigma_k g_k^(-1)` lie in `W_(beta_k) <= W_beta`.
  - Let `M_k` be the null set of points whose `Sigma_k`-orbit misses `A_k`.
    Take `x in X_beta` outside `Gamma M_k`, and choose `sigma in Sigma_k` with
    `sigma x in A_k`.
  - `sigma x in [x]_(R_Phi)`, since `sigma in W_beta` and `x in X_beta`.
  - `g_k sigma x = phi_k(sigma x) in [sigma x]_(R_Phi)`.
  - `g_k x = (g_k sigma^(-1) g_k^(-1))(g_k sigma x)`, with
    `g_k sigma^(-1) g_k^(-1) in W_beta` and `g_k sigma x in X_beta`. So
    `g_k x in [g_k sigma x]_(R_Phi)`.
  - So `g_k x in [x]_(R_Phi)` for almost every `x`: `g_k` is absorbed. By the
    absorption bullets, `W_(beta+1) = <W_beta, S_beta>` is absorbed.

**Conclusion.**
- `W_alpha = Gamma` is absorbed and `Phi ⊆ R_Gamma`, so `Phi` generates
  `R_Gamma` up to a null set, and `C(R_Gamma) <= 1 + eps` for every `eps > 0`.
- Every `R_Gamma`-class is infinite, so `C(R_Gamma) >= 1` by Proposition 2.15.
- The action was arbitrary, so `Gamma` has fixed price one.
- For the consequence, Theorem 2.20 gives every free action of an infinite
  amenable group cost one.
