Section 4: finite-component connectivity along stationary lazy quotient
walk is a positive spectral moment sequence supported on [0,rho]. Under
almost-sure uniqueness, full connectivity adds the atom theta_nu(t)^2
at 1 and another measure on [0,rho]. The density bound compares theta_nu
with the original theta and gives theta_nu(t)->0 as t decreases to zero.
The environment gap comparison therefore supplies no contradiction to
uniqueness at all positive sprinkling parameters by itself.
