**Items 1--3 ESTABLISHED; item 4 is an implication from (CSL).** Proof in
`mixed-degree-units-lift-non-positive-fixed-classes-proof`. Refereed: gq-referee-a PASS on items 1--3, item 4 a
correct implication from (CSL) (report 1a50e4465, N1--N4 applied); gq-referee-b PASS, citation lens (report f9605d67e):
the exact sequence part of (CSL) is derivable, see Status. No priority is claimed.

**Setting.** `R` is simple and generated by `B` and an isometric frame, graded as in
`isometric-frames-over-rank-one-bases-fail-the-k-budget`, item 4. `B = B_1 × ... × B_m` is a product of `U_Q`-type
rings. `A` is the matrix of `φ_* = [t (·) s]` on `K_0(B) = Q^m`, and `R_0` is the degree-zero part.

**Statement.**
1. **Core and shift.** `R_0` is ultramatricial over `Q`, so it has cancellation, and `K_0(R_0) ⊗ Q = Q^m` when `A` is
   invertible, with level-`0` coordinates. A degree-`1` partial isometry `w` with domain `w' w = D` and range
   `w w' = E` in `R_0` has `[E] = A^-1 [D]`.
2. **Corner skew Laurent form.** Suppose some idempotent class `c = A^-1 [1_B]` lies in `[0, [1_B]]`. Then there is a
   degree-`1` isometry `t_+` with `t_- t_+ = 1` and `t_+ t_- = g`, `[g] = c`. So
   `R = R_0[t_+, t_-; α]` with `α = t_+ (·) t_-`, the corner skew Laurent ring of
   Ara--González-Barroso--Goodearl--Pardo.
3. **Mixed-degree units.** Let `v != 0` with `A v = v`, and suppose `A` is invertible. For small rational `ε > 0`
   there is a unit `U = w_+ + w_- + w_0` of `R` with:
   - `w_+` of degree `1`, `w_-` of degree `-1` and `w_0` of degree `0`;
   - pairwise orthogonal domains summing to `1` and pairwise orthogonal ranges summing to `1`;
   - `[dom w_+] - [ran w_-] = ε v` in `K_0(R_0)`.
   `λ in Q^x` commutes with `U`, so the `{λ, U}` are Steinberg symbols of commuting units of `R`.
4. **Detection, assuming (CSL).** In the setting of item 2, assume (CSL):
   - there is a long exact sequence
     `K_n(R_0) --(1 - α_*)--> K_n(R_0) --> K_n(R) --∂--> K_(n-1)(R_0)`, that is, the `NK` terms of Ara--Brustenga--Cortiñas
     Theorem 3.6 vanish for this corner isomorphism;
   - `∂` is `K_*(Q)`-linear;
   - on units that are sums of homogeneous partial isometries of degrees `1, -1, 0`,
     `∂[U] = [dom w_+] - [ran w_-]`.
   Then `∂{λ, U} = λ ⊗ ε v in K_1(R_0) = Q^x ⊗ K_0(R_0)`. These classes generate an infinitely generated group, so
   no `E_N(R)`, `N >= 3`, is finitely presented. With item 7(a) of `degree-one-corner-unitaries-fail-the-k-budget`,
   every graded frame design over a multi-type matricial base with invertible `A` and a corner isometry fails the
   K-budget.

## The example of Attempt 13

`A = [[2,1],[2,3]]`, `[1_B] = (1,1)`, `v = (1,-1)`.
- `A^-1 (1,1) = (1/2, 0)`, so item 2 applies.
- For item 3: `dom w_+` has class `ε(2,2)` and range `ε(1,0)`; `dom w_-` has class `ε(0,1)` and range `ε(1,3)`. So
  `[dom w_+] - [ran w_-] = ε(1,-1)`, and both totals are `ε(2,3)`.
- If (CSL) holds, this design fails the budget. So it is not a loophole unless (CSL) fails for it.

## Status of (CSL)

- **The exact sequence: derivable** (gq-referee-b, f9605d67e). Ara--Brustenga--Cortiñas, arXiv:0903.0056v2, Theorem
  3.6 gives the fibration for a corner isomorphism with `R ⊗ A` H'-unital, and the proof of their Proposition 7.5
  kills the `NK` terms. In that proof, `B = α^-1 L_0` and `NK(B ⊕ k, φ)_± = 0` by Lemma 7.2 once `B ⊕ k` is regular
  supercoherent. Here `B = α^-1(R_0)` is a union of finite-dimensional semisimple algebras. So `B ⊕ Q` is
  ultramatricial, hence regular supercoherent (flat colimits; Gersten, Prop. 1.6, as cited by ABC, not read), and
  `B` has local units, hence is H'-unital. So the sequence of item 4 holds unconditionally for these rings.
- **Still assumed.** `K_*(Q)`-linearity of `∂` and the index formula `∂[U] = [dom w_+] - [ran w_-]`. Neither is stated in
  ABC Theorem 3.6 or 7.1--7.7. Ara--Brustenga, ABC's reference [2], may contain them; it was not reached. These two
  are the remaining content of (CSL). They are also the inputs behind trust surface T1 of
  `laurent-rational-symbols-block-fp-elementary-groups`.
- The corner skew Laurent rings are Ara--González-Barroso--Goodearl--Pardo, *Fractional skew monoid rings*,
  J. Algebra 278 (2004), 104--126 (ABC's [3]; not read).
