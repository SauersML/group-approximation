Literature import. J. McKee and C. Smyth, *Integer symmetric matrices of small
spectral radius and small Mahler measure*, arXiv:0907.0371 (July 2009, by
identifier).
- **What was read.** The TeX source was fetched from arXiv and read on
  2026-09-16: the abstract, Section 1 (Theorem `T:spectral radius`, Table 1 and
  the remark on `2.019800887...`), the definitions section, and Section 7
  ("Charged signed graphs of small spectral radius").
- **Not checked.** A journal version, recalled as IMRN 2012, is unverified.

**The proof in the source.**
- It finds all minimal noncyclotomic integer symmetric matrices (Theorem
  `T:main`). Only the section headings of Sections 5 and 6 were seen: the proof
  of `T:main`, with subsections on entries of modulus at least `3` and `2`, and
  the list of minimal noncyclotomic matrices.
- It grows the ones of radius below `2.019` by adjoining vertices of charge in
  `{-1, 0, 1}` and degree at most `4` (Section 7, read).

The statement is quoted in the claim.
