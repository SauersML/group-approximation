Fix `a in (K_Z^sat)_+`.  By
`stw93-all-z-central-slices-generate-quasitrace-null-ideal`, every normalized
`2`-quasitrace `q` on `D` satisfies

```text
q(a)=0.
```

The square-null space of `q` is a hereditary ideal.  It therefore contains
every positive root `a^(1/m)`, and hence

```text
d_q(a)=lim_(m->infinity) q(a^(1/m))=0                  (2)
```

for every normalized `q`.

Fix `n`.  In a canonical unital central copy `rho:Z->D`, choose pairwise
orthogonal nonzero positive contractions

```text
g_1,...,g_n.
```

Every normalized quasitrace `q` on `D` restricts along `rho` to the unique
normalized trace of `Z`.  That trace is faithful, so

```text
d_q(g_i)>=q(g_i)>0                                     (3)
```

for all `q` and every `i`.  Moreover each `g_i` is full in `D`: a nonzero
positive element is full in the simple algebra `Z`, and `rho` is unital, so
the ambient ideal generated by `g_i` contains `rho(1_Z)=1_D`.

By `stw93-central-sequence-purity-from-z-stability`, `D` is pure and in
particular has strict comparison by normalized `2`-quasitraces.  Equations
`(2)` and `(3)`, together with fullness of `g_i`, give

```text
a Cuntz-subequivalent to g_i                            (4)
```

for each `i`.  Add `(4)` in `Cu(D)`.  Orthogonality of the `g_i` gives

```text
n[a]<=[g_1]+...+[g_n]=[g_1+...+g_n]<=[1_D],            (5)
```

because the orthogonal sum of positive contractions is again a positive
contraction.  Since `n` was arbitrary, `(5)` proves infinite capacity.

The passage from slice-wise annihilation to `(2)` is what removes the
apparent cross-embedding alignment problem: strict comparison uses only the
common zero dimension functions, so all target plateaus may be chosen in a
single fixed copy of `Z`.
