**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed)** by [[rokhlin-entropy-quotient-formula-proof]].

Let `W` be countably infinite, `N ◁ W` finite with `n = |N|`, and `Q = W/N`. Let `W ↷ (X, μ)` be free, ergodic
and p.m.p., and let `X/N` be the `Q`-factor of `N`-invariant sets. Then `X/N` is free and ergodic, and

    n h^Rok_W(X) <= h^Rok_Q(X/N) <= n h^Rok_W(X) + r log n

whenever `Q` is generated by `r` elements (the second inequality needs `Q` finitely generated).

**Proof shape** (Section 2 of the artifact).
- **Lower bound for `Q`.** The torsor `X -> X/N` has zero relative Rokhlin entropy, by markers in `X/N` that reveal
  the fibre coordinate. The stabilizer index is `n`, so Alpeev–Seward Theorem 1.11(i) bounds the outer entropy of
  `X/N` by `(1/n) h^Rok_Q(X/N)`. Sub-additivity combines the two.
- **Upper bound for `Q`.** Fix a Borel transversal `M` of the `N`-orbits. From a `W`-generator `α` of `X`, record at
  each fibre the `N`-tuple of `α`-labels of the fibre, read from its `M`-point. This costs at most `n H(α)`, by
  concavity. Also record, for each generator of `Q`, which element of `N` aligns the lifted translate of the
  `M`-point with the next `M`-point. This costs `log n` per generator. The alignment cocycle then gives every
  `α`-label of the `M`-point's orbit, so the recorded partition generates `X/N`.

**Remarks.**
- For finite-index subgroups the analogous formula is open over nonamenable groups (Seward, arXiv:1602.06680v2).
  Across a finite kernel it holds up to a bounded error.
- The freeness hypothesis is needed. A `Q`-action pulled back to `W` has the same Rokhlin entropy over both groups.
- **Consequences:** `rokhlin-maximality-descends-along-finite-normal-quotients`, and the ascent analysis on
  `rokhlin-maximality-ascends-amenable-normal-extensions`.
