## Setting

`W(Z)` is the group of bijections of `Z` with bounded displacement. For finite `Q ⊂ W(Z)` put
`r(Q) = max_{f∈Q} sup_n |f(n) − n|`. A *cut* is a half-integer `c`. A finite set `b ⊂ Z`
*crosses* `c` if `b_< = b ∩ (−∞, c)` and `b_> = b ∩ (c, ∞)` are both nonempty.

A finitely generated group `Γ` with finite generating set `S` has *property (τ)* if there is `κ > 0`
such that for every finite `Γ`-set `F` and every `w ∈ ℓ²_0(F)`, meaning `Σ w = 0`,
`max_{s∈S} ‖s·w − w‖ ≥ κ‖w‖`. This is the uniform gap over all finite quotients (Lubotzky's (τ)
for the family of all finite-index subgroups).

## Statement

Let `Γ ≤ W(Z)` be finitely generated by `S`, with `r = r(S)`.

1. **(Bounded crossing.)** Let `H ⊴ Γ` with `(Γ, H)` relative (T). Then there is `K < ∞` such that
   every `H`-orbit is finite, and for every cut `c`,
   `Σ_{b crossing c} |b_<|·|b_>| / |b| ≤ K`,
   summed over the `H`-orbits `b`. In particular at most `2K` `H`-orbits cross any cut, and each has
   `min(|b_<|, |b_>|) ≤ 2K`.
2. **(Property (τ) bounds finite orbits.)** If `Γ` has property (τ) with constant `κ` for `S`, then
   every finite `Γ`-orbit in `Z` has at most `16 r / κ²` points.
3. **(Class kill.)** If `Γ` is infinite and has property (τ), then there is a finite-index normal
   `Γ_0 ⊴ Γ` that fixes every point lying in a finite `Γ`-orbit. `Γ_0` acts faithfully on the
   nonempty union `Z_∞` of the infinite `Γ`-orbits. For every `x ∈ Z_∞` the stabiliser `Γ_x` has
   infinite index and is co-amenable in `Γ`. If `Γ` is non-amenable, then `Γ_x` is non-amenable.
   So: **an infinite finitely generated group with property (τ) and no infinite-index co-amenable
   subgroup embeds neither in `W(Z)` nor in the full-shift topological full group `[[X]]`.**
4. **(Reduction for the candidate, conditional.)** Let `R = F_p[t, t^{-1}]`, `Λ = SL_2(R)` and
   `Γ_p = Λ ⋉ R²` (or with `EL_2(R)` in place of `Λ`). Assume that `Λ` has property (τ). This is
   recalled from Serre's congruence subgroup property for `SL_2` of an `S`-integer ring with
   `|S| = 2`, together with the congruence (τ) coming from Drinfeld's Ramanujan–Petersson theorem
   over function fields; neither is checked verbatim here. Then `Γ_p` has (τ), and an embedding
   `Γ_p ↪ W(Z)` gives a subgroup `Λ_x ≤ Λ` that is:
   - of infinite index,
   - co-amenable in `Λ`,
   - non-amenable, and
   - the stabiliser of a finite-codimension `F_p`-subspace `V ⊂ R²` (namely `V = (R²)_x`, the
     `R²`-stabiliser of a point, which has finite index in `R²` by item 1).

## Role

- **New prerequisite on the refutation route.** An embedding of `Γ_p` into `[[X]] ≤ W(Z)` refutes
  `full-shift-topological-full-group-is-a-t-menable`, and through it
  `brin-thompson-groups-nv-are-a-t-menable`. Modulo the recalled (τ), that embedding needs **(P)**:
  `SL_2(F_p[t,t^{-1}])` has a non-amenable, infinite-index, co-amenable subgroup that fixes a
  finite-codimension subspace of `R²`. (P) is a statement about one well-studied lattice in
  `SL_2(F_p((t))) × SL_2(F_p((t^{-1})))`, and it can fail on its own. If (P) fails, the flagship
  candidate `Γ_p` is dead for `[[X]]` and `W(Z)`.
- **Class kill.** Item 3 kills, as a subgroup of `W(Z)` or `[[X]]`, every infinite finitely
  generated (τ)-group all of whose co-amenable subgroups have finite index. It is the step "finite
  orbits have bounded size", followed by "infinite orbits give co-amenable stabilisers", where every
  member dies. Kazhdan groups are the special case already known.
- **Shape of any witness.** Item 1 says that, inside `W(Z)`, the orbits of a normal relative-(T)
  subgroup form a family of finite blocks with bounded crossing multiplicity at every scale. This
  is stronger than the window-fraction bound of
  `wobbling-normal-relative-t-subgroups-are-locally-finite`.
