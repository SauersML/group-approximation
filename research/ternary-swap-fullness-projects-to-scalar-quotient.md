Multiply `c e b = 1` by the central idempotent `2(1 + [z])` and apply
`2(1 + [z]) F_3[G] ~= F_3[PG]`. This gives `cbar ebar bbar = 1`. So the
unit-group equation is at least as strong as the scalar-quotient equation, and
only the latter is needed for a counterexample.
