**ESTABLISHED (literature).** Route: `residually-finite-non-exact-groups-exist-citation`.

There exist finitely generated residually finite groups whose reduced C*-algebras are not exact, equivalently without Yu's property A. The examples are defined by infinite graphical small cancellation presentations, and their Cayley graphs contain D-regular graphs of growing girth isometrically.
