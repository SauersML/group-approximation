**ESTABLISHED (literature).** Route: `residually-finite-non-exact-groups-exist-citation`.

There exist finitely generated residually finite groups whose reduced C*-algebras are not exact, equivalently groups without Yu's property A.
- The examples are defined by infinite graphical small cancellation presentations.
- Their Cayley graphs contain D-regular graphs of growing girth isometrically.
- Non-exactness comes from girth alone. The relator graphs are not expanders (`osajda-rf-relator-covers-are-not-expanders`), so no failure of Baum--Connes with coefficients is known for these groups.
