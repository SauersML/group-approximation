**ESTABLISHED (proof in `commuting-living-generators-give-sigma-one-proof`; unreviewed).**

Let `Γ` be generated by a finite set `S`, and let `χ: Γ -> R` be a nonzero homomorphism.
Call `s in S` *living* when `χ(s) != 0`, and let `L` be the set of living generators.
Suppose:

1. the elements of `L` pairwise commute, and
2. every `s in S` commutes with at least one element of `L`.

Then `[χ] in Σ^1(Γ)`.

This is a special case of the commuting-generator criteria for `Σ^1`: the
living-subgraph criterion of Meier–VanWyk for right-angled Artin groups and the
lemma Koban–McCammond–Meinert use for pure braid groups. Those sources were not
re-read here, so the attribution is unverified and the proof below is self-contained.

Uses: `rover-nekrashevych-groups-have-full-bns-invariant`. The criterion needs no
relations beyond commutation, so it also applies to Thompson-like groups whose
characters are realized by small-support elements.
