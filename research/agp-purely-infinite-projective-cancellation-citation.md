P. Ara, K. R. Goodearl, E. Pardo, *K_0 of purely infinite simple regular rings*,
arXiv:math/0111066v1. Read from PDF text on MSI, 2026-09-12. Section 2 defines
"V(R)∗ := V(R) \ {0}", the nonzero isomorphism classes of finitely generated projective right
R-modules.

> **Proposition 2.2.** If R is a purely infinite simple ring, then V(R)∗ is a group. In particular,
> any nonzero finitely generated projective R-modules which are stably isomorphic must be isomorphic.

The definition (Section 1, verbatim): "A simple ring R is said to be purely infinite if every nonzero
right ideal of R contains an infinite idempotent". Proposition 2.2 as printed assumes no regularity.
This is also how `agp-purely-infinite-simple-k1-citation` reads Theorem 2.4 of the same paper.
