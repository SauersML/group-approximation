**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** The Stein group `F_{2,3}` is of type `F_∞`. It is the
group of increasing piecewise-linear homeomorphisms of `[0,1]` with breakpoints in
`Z[1/6]` and slopes in `2^Z 3^Z`. In particular it is finitely presented.
