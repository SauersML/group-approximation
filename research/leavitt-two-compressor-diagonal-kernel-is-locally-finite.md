Let `D` be the binary Cantor diagonal and let

```text
K=GL_5(D)^4 <= GL_20(R)
```

be the four-block diagonal ultramatricial group.  Both complementary
compressor matrices `tau_0,tau_1` normalize `K`.  Consequently, in

```text
B_EL=<Lambda,tau_0,tau_1>,             Lambda=EL_5(D)
```

the normal closure of `Lambda` is contained in `K` and is locally finite.
More generally `B_EL intersect K` is a locally finite normal subgroup, and
the quotient is generated by the two compressor cosets; their relative
coset has order dividing three.

This is only a structural reduction.  Soficity or MF is not inferred from
an amenable locally finite kernel and an unspecified quotient, and the
claim supplies no Steinberg lift control.
