Literature import. The statement of `strong-atiyah-passes-to-locally-indicable-extensions`
is Proposition 6.5 of Jaikin-Zapirain and Lopez-Alvarez, *The strong Atiyah and
Lueck approximation conjectures for one-relator groups*, arXiv:1810.12135v4
(Math. Ann. 376 (2020)). It was read verbatim from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-12,
pages 31-32. The claim node quotes the statement, the credit to Henneke and
Kielak, the printed proof, and the parts not re-derived. The proof is the
paper's; nothing here reproves it.
