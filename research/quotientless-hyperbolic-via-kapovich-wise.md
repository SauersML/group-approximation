If some hyperbolic group is not residually finite, then not every hyperbolic
group is residually finite, so by the Kapovich--Wise theorem some nontrivial
hyperbolic group has no nontrivial finite quotient; being nontrivial, it is
infinite.

This route and `non-rf-hyperbolic-from-quotientless-hyperbolic` form an
intended equivalence cycle; do not delete either to silence the cycle warning.
