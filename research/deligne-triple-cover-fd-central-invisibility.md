Let `E_3` be the inverse image of `Sp_4(Z)` in the connected three-fold
cover of `Sp_4(R)`.  Then

```text
1 -> <z> = Z/3 -> E_3 -> Sp_4(Z) -> 1,                  (DTC1)
```

`z!=1`, and every finite-dimensional unitary representation
`rho:E_3->U(d)` satisfies

```text
rho(z)=1.                                                (DTC2)
```

The exact invisibility is much stronger than mere non-residual finiteness and
is the classical arithmetic input for the Maslov-sector route.  It leaves a
single metric question: can an almost representation keep positive HS mass in
a nontrivial central character sector even though no exact finite-dimensional
representation can do so?
