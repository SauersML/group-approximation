For finite question and answer sets,

```text
closure_l1(C_qc^Tr(X,A)) = C_qc(X,A).
```

This permits arbitrary correlation-level approximation of a commuting
strategy by a tracially embeddable standard-form strategy.  It does not give
an exact tracially embeddable realization and does not preserve exact word
relations, a marked element, a fixed support projection, or normal closure.
