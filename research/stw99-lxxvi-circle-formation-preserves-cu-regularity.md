Let `D` be a simple separable C*-algebra of stable rank one.  If the
first-factor map

```text
Cu(D) -> Cu(D tensor_min Z)
```

is an isomorphism, then the first-factor map for the circle algebra is also
an isomorphism:

```text
Cu(C(T,D)) -> Cu(C(T,D) tensor_min Z).
```

Thus adjoining one circle variable preserves Cuntz semigroup regularity in
the simple stable-rank-one class.  No nuclearity or C*-level Z-absorption is
assumed.
