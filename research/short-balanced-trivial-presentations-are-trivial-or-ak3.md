Every balanced two-generator presentation of the trivial group with total relator
length ≤ 12 is AC-trivial, as proved by Miasnikov and Myasnikov.

Every such presentation of total length 13 is AC-trivial or AC-equivalent to
AK(3) = ⟨x, y | x^3 = y^4, xyx = yxy⟩, which itself has total length 13 (Havas
and Ramsay).

Consequently AK(3) is the unique minimal potential counterexample to
`andrews-curtis-conjecture` in rank 2.
