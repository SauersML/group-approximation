Corollary 6.3. For bounded exponent and C(alpha)>1 every infinite subgroup restriction has at least C(alpha), and an infinite pair subgroup exists. For a d-generated group of unbounded exponent with C(alpha)=1+delta>1, some pair restriction has cost >=1+delta/(2d), by choosing a common element of order >=2(d-1)/delta.
