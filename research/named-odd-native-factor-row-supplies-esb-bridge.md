Dead route.  The current ordinary packet contains several relations with
odd incidence in one of `J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3`: the Whitehead definitions, the
commutator factorizations of the `B` arms, and the endpoint Coxeter rows.
The hoped inference was that the newly positive B2/B3 quarter source would
place one such relation on the full Hecke carrier and turn its odd factor
into the ungauged bridge `(ESB2)`.

`named-odd-native-factor-rows-have-no-esb-common-carrier` audits every
currently authenticated odd row.  The `J_i` rows move the Hecke chart and
retain the native gauge; the odd commutator definitions of `B_i` use arms
which have no common-Hecke-carrier typing; and the endpoint odd rows use a
partner which may carry the whole source out of itself.  The only literal
operators already typed on `Q` are the B2/B3 Pauli sign flips, and no
authenticated odd row containing them has all its other factors typed on
that same carrier.

Thus odd syntax and positive support are both present but not on one
occurrence.  A live route needs a new mixed relation with its complete
source/range Gram, not another parity identity in separately moving charts.
