Sections 4--5 contain the two implications and the full elementary
correction proof. A zero infimum gives an exact corona homomorphism
whose marked distance is two. Conversely a surviving corona mark lifts,
on a subsequence, to marked exact finite packets with overlap-central
involutions and braid defects tending to zero. No faithfulness of a
mark-retaining homomorphism from the joint quotient is assumed.
