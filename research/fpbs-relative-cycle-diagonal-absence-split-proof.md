Section 1. The diagonal of K_x is the fibre integral of
rho_H(e)-||P_Z(Phi_y)delta_e||^2. When e is absent from Phi_y, the
closed cycle span of Phi_y vanishes at coordinate e, so the subtracted
term is zero. When e is present, Z(Phi_y) is contained in Z(H), so it is
at most rho_H(e). Splitting the integral over presence gives the identity.
