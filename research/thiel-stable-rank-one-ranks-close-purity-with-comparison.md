title: Stable rank one realizes all ranks and closes purity in the presence of strict comparison
distinct_from:
  stw99-problem-xxiii-rank-problem: that asks for rank realization without stable rank one; this imports Thiel's established stable-rank-one theorem and records its strict-comparison consequence.
---

Let `A` be a unital simple separable stably finite non-elementary C-star
algebra of stable rank one.  Every strictly positive lower semicontinuous
affine function on `QT(A)` is the rank function of a positive element of
`A tensor K`.

If `A` also has strict comparison, then `A` is pure.  Indeed rank realization
and strict comparison give almost divisibility of `Cu(A)`, while strict
comparison gives almost unperforation.
