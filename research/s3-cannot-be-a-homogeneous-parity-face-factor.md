Suppose a homogeneous binary LCS face system labels every generator by an
involution of `S_3` and is intended to present `S_3` as the subgroup generated
by those labels.  Every nonidentity involution of `S_3` is a transposition,
and two distinct transpositions do not commute.  Hence every permitted face
contains nonidentity K-labels of at most one transposition type.  Its product-
one relation says only that this same transposition occurs an even number of
times.

Replace the three transposition labels by abstract involutions `t_1,t_2,t_3`
with no relations between distinct types.  Every face relation remains true
in

```text
C_2 * C_2 * C_2.                                         (S3F1)
```

Therefore the universal homogeneous face group maps onto the free product
generated by the transposition types which occur.  If at least two types
occur—as required to generate `S_3`—this quotient is infinite.  The face
relations cannot force the Coxeter relation `(t_i t_j)^3=1`, so the universal
group is not `S_3` and cannot be `S_3 x F_2^r`.

Auxiliary LCS variables do not help when their K-labels are also involutions
of `S_3`: the same commuting-face argument applies to every auxiliary.
Thus the smallest fixed nonabelian packet fails structurally, independently
of code rate, distance, or occurrence repetition.
