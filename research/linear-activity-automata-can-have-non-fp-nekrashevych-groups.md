**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof below, from `loop-retraction-forces-fp-eventual-section-groups`; not yet refereed;
no priority claimed).

## The automaton

`X = {0,1,2}`. Write `u = π(u_0, u_1, u_2)` for the automorphism with root permutation `π` and sections `u_i`.
- **Grigorchuk part:** `a = (0 1)(1,1,1)`, `b = (a, c, 1)`, `c = (a, d, 1)`, `d = (1, b, 1)`.
- **Spine part:** for `γ in Γ = ⟨a,b,c,d⟩`, `ŝ_γ = (γ, 1, ŝ_γ)`, i.e. `ŝ_γ` acts as `γ` on every cone
  `2^k 0 X^*`, fixes every cone `2^k 1 X^*`, and fixes `2^ω`. The spine generators are `ŝ_a, ŝ_b, ŝ_c, ŝ_d`.

`S = {1, a, b, c, d, ŝ_a, ŝ_b, ŝ_c, ŝ_d}` is closed under sections, and under inverses (all are involutions).
Let `G = ⟨S⟩ <= Aut({0,1,2}^*)`.

## Statement

1. `G` is generated by a finite automaton of **linear activity**: the number of level-`n` vertices where a
   state has a nontrivial section is `O(n)`.
2. `G` is not contracting.
3. **`V_3(G)` is not finitely presented** (given the necessity half of Theorem A).

## Proof

- **`Γ` is the Grigorchuk group.** On `{0,1}^*` the generators act as the standard ones. Every generator fixes
  `2` with trivial section there, so every element of `Γ` acts on `u2w` (`u in {0,1}^*`) as `g(u)2w`. So the
  action on `X^*` is determined by the faithful standard action on `{0,1}^*`, and `Γ` is the Grigorchuk group,
  which is not finitely presented (`grigorchuk-group-is-just-infinite-torsion-not-fp`).
- **The spine copy.** `γ ↦ ŝ_γ` is an injective homomorphism `Γ -> Aut(X^*)`: componentwise on the recursion,
  and `ŝ_γ` acts as `γ` on the cone `0X^*`. So `⟨ŝ_a, …, ŝ_d⟩ ≅ Γ`.
- **(3).** Every state fixes the letter `2`. The section map at `2` sends `a,b,c,d` to `1` and each `ŝ_γ` to
  itself, so `p = 1`, `S_∞ = {1, ŝ_a, …, ŝ_d}`, and the eventual section group is `G_∞ = ⟨ŝ_a,…,ŝ_d⟩ ≅ Γ`,
  which is not finitely presented. By `loop-retraction-forces-fp-eventual-section-groups`, `V_3(G)` is not
  finitely presented.
- **(1).** `a` has activity `0` below the root. `b, c, d` are bounded: at level `n` their only nontrivial
  sections are along `1^n` and at one child of it, so at most `2`. `ŝ_γ` has nontrivial sections only at
  `2^n` and inside the cones `2^k 0`, where they are sections of `γ`; so at most `1 + Σ_{k<n} 2 = O(n)`.
- **(2).** `ŝ_γ|_{2^n} = ŝ_γ` for every `n` and each of the infinitely many distinct `ŝ_γ`, `γ in Γ`. A
  nucleus would have to contain all of them, so no finite nucleus exists.

## Scope

- This concerns this action only. Whether `G` satisfies Boone–Higman is not decided here: it may have another
  finite-state action meeting the hypothesis of `lift-presented-automaton-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`.
- So finite presentation of `V_d(G)` holds for bounded activity (Nekrashevych FP2) and fails already at
  linear activity. Any polynomial-activity approach to `automaton-groups-satisfy-boone-higman` through
  Nekrashevych groups must allow changing the action.
