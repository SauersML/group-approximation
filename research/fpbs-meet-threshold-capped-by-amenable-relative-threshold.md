**ESTABLISHED.** Route `fpbs-meet-threshold-capped-by-amenable-relative-threshold-proof`.

Let `G = Cay(Gamma,S)`, and use the notation of
`fpbs-replica-meet-threshold-sandwich`: `M_k(o)` is the meet of the clusters of
`o` in `k` independent Bernoulli(`p`) configurations, and
`p_cap^(k) = sup{p : |M_k(o)| < infinity a.s.}`. Let `p_c(H;G)` be
Hutchcroft–Pan's relative threshold (`fpbs-hp-relative-burton-keane`).

1. **Amenable cap.** For every infinite amenable `H <= Gamma` and every `k`,
   `p_cap^(k)(G) <= p_c(H;G)`. More precisely, for `p > p_c(H;G)`,
   `P_p^(k)(|M_k(o)| = infinity) >= P_p(o in U)^k > 0`, where `U` is the
   unique `H`-infinite cluster.
2. **Coset cap.** For every infinite `H` generated by `S ∩ H`, with
   `Y = Cay(H, S ∩ H) ⊆ G`, `p_cap^(k)(G) <= p_u(Y)` for every `k`, and
   `p_c(H;G) <= p_c(Y)`.
3. **Necessary condition for [cap_fin].** If some `p > p_c(G)` and `k` give
   `|M_k(o)| < infinity` a.s. (that is, `fpbs-replica-meet-threshold-gap-universal`
   holds on `G`), then:
   - `p_c(G) < p_c(H;G)` for every infinite amenable `H <= Gamma`, or
     equivalently `E_q|K_o ∩ H| < infinity` for some `q > p_c(G)`;
   - `p_c(G) < p_u(Cay(H, S∩H))` for every infinite `H` generated by `S ∩ H`.

Proof: Sections 0 and 1 and Corollary C of the artifact. The key tool is the
invariant-finite-set lemma, applied to `W = U_1 ∩ ... ∩ U_k ∩ H`, whose law is
`H`-invariant.
