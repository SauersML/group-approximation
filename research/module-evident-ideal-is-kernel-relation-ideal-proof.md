Fourier duality sends w−1 to f_w(χ)=χ(w)−1 with common zero set Z.
On the support of h∈C_c(X\Z), a finite sum F=Σ|f_w|² is positive;
division by F with a cutoff writes h in their coefficient ideal.
Density yields C_0(X\Z). The ideal it generates in C(X)⋊_rG is the
closed span of Σ f_g u_g with all f_g in that coefficient ideal,
which is exactly the embedded reduced crossed product. No exactness
of G is used.

This is a written deduction integrated from the supplied attempt, not
an independent referee report or a Lean proof.
