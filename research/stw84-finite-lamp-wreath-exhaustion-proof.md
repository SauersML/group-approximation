## The uniform finite-stage estimate

The imported analytic input is Eckhardt--Wu,
[*Nuclear dimension and virtually polycyclic
groups*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2025.110768), Theorem 5.14.  If `A` is
finite dimensional and `V` is finitely generated and virtually nilpotent,
then for the Bernoulli shift

```text
dim_nuc(A^(tensor V) rtimes V) <= 2 * 9^d(V),           (2)
```

where `d(V)` is the degree of polynomial growth of `V`.  Taking `A=C*(K)`
identifies the crossed product in (2) with `C*(K wr V)`.

We next turn (2) into a Hirsch-length bound.  Polynomial-growth degree and
Hirsch length are unchanged on passing to finite-index subgroups and after
discarding the finite torsion in a finitely generated nilpotent group.  For a
torsion-free nilpotent finite-index model, let `a_i` be the dimensions of the
successive homogeneous pieces of its rational Malcev Lie algebra.  The
Bass--Guivarc'h formula (see Bass,
[*The degree of polynomial growth of finitely generated nilpotent
groups*](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-25.4.603)) gives

```text
d(V)=sum_i i*a_i,              h(V)=sum_i a_i.          (3)
```

If the nilpotency class is `c`, every degree from `1` through `c` has a
nonzero homogeneous piece.  Hence `c<=h(V)`, and (3) gives

```text
d(V) <= c * sum_i a_i <= h(V)^2.                       (4)
```

For a finite `V`, both sides before the square in (4) are zero.  Combining
(2) and (4), every finitely generated virtually nilpotent `V` of Hirsch
length at most `r` satisfies

```text
dim_nuc(C*(K wr V)) <= 2 * 9^(r^2).                    (5)
```

The use of (4) is essential: stagewise finiteness alone would not supply the
uniform bound required by an inductive limit.

## Exhaust the whole wreath product

Enumerate `H`, and let `H_n` be the subgroup generated by its first `n`
elements.  Put `G_n=K wr H_n`, using lamps supported on `H_n`.  These are
naturally increasing subgroups of `K wr H`.  Every element of a restricted
wreath product consists of one acting coordinate and a finitely supported
lamp function.  A sufficiently large `H_n` contains that coordinate and the
finite lamp support, so

```text
K wr H = union_n G_n.                                  (6)
```

Each `H_n` is virtually nilpotent with `h(H_n)<=r`; hence (5) bounds all the
stage algebras uniformly.  The groups `H_n` are amenable, the direct-sum lamp
kernel is locally finite, and therefore every `G_n` and their union are
amenable.  Reduced subgroup algebras embed isometrically, amenability
identifies full and reduced group algebras, and (6) becomes an injective
inductive limit of group C*-algebras.  Applying
`stw84-directed-union-uniform-bound` proves (1).

## The class is genuinely new

Take `H=(Q,+)`.  Every finitely generated subgroup of `Q` is cyclic, so the
uniform local Hirsch length is one and (1) gives the bound `18`.  On the
other hand, if `K` is nontrivial, restricting the lamps and acting coordinate
to a copy of `Z subset Q` embeds `K wr Z` in `K wr Q`.  The former is finitely
generated, but its base `direct_sum_Z K` is not finitely generated.  Since
every subgroup of a virtually polycyclic group is finitely generated,
`K wr Z` is not virtually polycyclic.  Consequently `K wr Q` is not locally
virtually polycyclic, so the earlier local-polycyclic theorem cannot produce
this example.
