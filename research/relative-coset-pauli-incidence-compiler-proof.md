Put

```text
Omega_X=A/H_X,                 Omega_Z=A/H_Z.                    (1)
```

Define an `A`-invariant binary matrix `B` on `Omega_X x Omega_Z` by

```text
B(gH_X,hH_Z)=b
  if g^-1 h lies in H_X P_b H_Z,
B=0 on all still unspecified pairs.                              (2)
```

This is well defined by `(RCI2)` and is invariant under simultaneous left
translation.  Let `N_B` be the class-two binary Clifford group generated by
involutions `X_omega,Z_nu,J`, with `J` central and

```text
[X_omega,X_omega']=1,       [Z_nu,Z_nu']=1,
[X_omega,Z_nu]=J^(B(omega,nu)).                                  (3)
```

The usual twisted group-algebra/Pauli representation of this possibly
infinite binary symplectic space sends `J` to `-I`, so `J!=1`.  Left
translation preserves `B`, hence `A` acts on `N_B`.  In `N_B semidirect A`,
send

```text
x -> X_(H_X),          z -> Z_(H_Z).                             (4)
```

Equations `(1)--(3)` verify every relator `(RCI3)` and prove exact marked
completeness.

Any additional bounded local product/linearity templates allowed in clause
4 are checked here as explicit identities in `N_B` and added as finitely many
prototype relators.  This does not change the argument, but their validity
is a hypothesis rather than a consequence of pairwise commutator signs.

For a matrix tuple, attach to an X occurrence at address `g` the conjugate
`g x g^-1`, and similarly use `h z h^-1` for a Z occurrence.  Every equality
or pair word in `C_N` is literally a conjugate of one of the finite relators
`(RCI3)`.  Therefore normalized-HS error is bounded by one fixed multiple of
the presentation defect, independent of the address length and `N`.
Fixed involution/central-sector rounding gives the strategy extraction in
the hypothesis of `occurrence-local-dimension-games-force-mark-collapse`.
Since the robust dimensions tend to infinity, that theorem collapses the
marked finite-matrix sector and gives the stated nonhyperlinearity conclusion.
