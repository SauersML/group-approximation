The third prerequisite supplies a finitely presented group `G = E/<<w>>`
which is not MF.  Present it as `G = F/R` with `F` free of finite rank and
form the free abelianized cover `Ecover = F/[R,R]`.

By the second prerequisite `Ecover` is torsion-free, generated by the rank of
`F`, and in particular countable.  By the contrapositive of the first
prerequisite, `G` not MF forces `Ecover` not MF.  So `Ecover` is a
torsion-free countable non-MF group, which is the target.

**What this route does and does not use.**  It uses no Property `(T)`, no
Kazhdan subgroup, no small-cancellation input and no new analytic mechanism:
the group theory is the elementary cover construction and the analysis is
entirely inside the first prerequisite.  It does not give finite
presentability -- `Ecover` is finitely generated only -- and by
`torsion-is-a-finite-datum-torsion-freeness-is-not` that gap cannot be closed
by truncating relators; the target node's own reduction through
`torsion-free-higman-embedding` is the intended way up.

The whole content sits in `free-abelianized-cover-reflects-mf`, which is open
and whose recorded attempts explain why the archive's general "covers do not
work" verdict does not decide it.
