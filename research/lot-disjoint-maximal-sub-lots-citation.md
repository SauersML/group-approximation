Citation import. J. Harlander and S. Rosebrock, arXiv:1212.1943v6 (`src.tex`, md5
`635eef20d92bf6906f45a53ca270460c`), fetched on MSI 2026-09-13.

- l.555: "From each subtree `T_i` choose a vertex `t_i` and collapse each `T_i` in
  `P` to `t_i` to obtain a quotient tree `P'` of `P`. If an edge in `P'` is labeled
  with a vertex `t_i' ≠ t_i` from `T_i`, then relabel that edge with `t_i`. ... We
  say that `P` is injective relative to `T` if `P'` is injective."
- l.817--821, Theorem `sgene` (1): "Let `P` be a reduced labeled oriented tree and
  let `{T_1, ..., T_n}` be the set of maximal proper sub-LOTs. Let
  `T = T_1 ∪ ... ∪ T_n`. Assume each `T_i` is aspherical. If the `T_i` are
  pairwise disjoint and `P` is injective relative to `T`, then `P` is aspherical."
- l.102--122: sub-LOTs (connected, at least one edge, labels inside) and reduced
  LOTs.
