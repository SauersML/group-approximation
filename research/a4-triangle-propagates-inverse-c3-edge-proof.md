The group

```text
<x,y | x^3,y^2,(xy)^3>
```

is the orientation-preserving `(3,2,3)` spherical triangle group, hence is
isomorphic to `A4`.  Choose the standard realization with `x` a three-cycle
and `y` a double transposition.  Direct permutation multiplication shows
`x^-1 y` is a three-cycle.  Since the presentation maps isomorphically onto
this realization, `(x^-1y)^3` is a consequence of the three displayed
relators, proving the claim in every target group.
