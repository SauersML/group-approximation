Citation import. Read on 2026-09-13 from two secondary statements; Alonso's
primary papers were not re-read.

- M. C. B. Zaremsky, arXiv:2305.15176v2, p. 4, Citation 1.2: "[Alo90, Alo96] Let
  G and H be finitely presented groups, viewed as metric spaces via word metrics
  coming from finite generating sets. Suppose H is a quasi-retract of G. Then
  δ_H ≼ δ_G." Definition 1.1 there defines quasi-retracts through
  `(C,D)`-Lipschitz maps `r: X -> Y`, `ι: Y -> X` with `d_Y(r ∘ ι(y), y) <= D`.
- C. Llosa Isenrich, E. Schesler, X. Wu, arXiv:2510.01952v1, p. 12 (PDF):
  - Theorem 4.1 [Alo94, Theorem 8]: "Let G and Q be finitely generated groups such
    that Q is a quasi-retract of G with respect to word metrics corresponding to
    some finite generating sets of G and Q. Then (1) if G is of type F_n, so is Q,
    (2) if G is of type FP_n(R) for some unital commutative ring R ≠ 0, so is Q."
  - Theorem 4.2 ([Alo90],[AWP99, Corollary 5]): "Let G and Q be groups of type
    F_{n+1} such that Q is a quasi-retract of G with respect to word metrics
    corresponding to some finite generating sets. Then δ_Q^{(k)} ≼ δ_G^{(k)} for
    1 ≤ k ≤ n."
  - On the same page their `≼` is `f_1(n) ≤ K f_2(Kn) + Kn + K`.
