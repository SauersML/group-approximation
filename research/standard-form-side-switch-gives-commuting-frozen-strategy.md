REFUTED.  The proposed shortcut represents the finite tracial algebra `M` of
a perfect ZPC-IRS strategy on `L^2(M,tau)`, keeps the unreadable observables
in the left action, moves the readable algebra `A` to the commuting right
action, and reads off a perfect commuting-operator strategy for the frozen
linear system on the trace vector.

The correlation half of that argument is correct: `R(A)` commutes with
`L(B)` and the one-vector expectations `tau(xp)` are unchanged.  The
strategy half fails.  The two mixed player algebras `W*(L(B),R(A))` and
`W*(R(B),L(A))` commute only when `A` already commutes with `B`, which is
the frozen-fibre hypothesis the switch was supposed to prove.  Independently,
the mixed vector state is not tracial, and a relator that is scalar on the
selected vector need not act as that scalar on its right ideal.  The exact
counterexamples are in
`standard-form-right-copy-does-not-freeze-zpc-controls`.
