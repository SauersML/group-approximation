Citation import, not a reproof. Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX source of J. Huang,
*Cycles in spherical Deligne complexes and application to K(π,1)-conjecture for Artin
groups*, arXiv:2405.12068 (v3, January 2025; no journal reference listed). Parts read: the
introduction and the statements of the higher-dimensional section. Not read: the proofs.

Verbatim:

- Theorem (= Theorem `thm:complete bipartite`): "Let A_S be an Artin group such that its
  Coxeter diagram is complete bipartite. Then A_S satisfies the K(π,1)-conjecture."
- "In each dimension ≥ 5, there are infinitely many free-of-infinity Artin groups satisfying
  the assumption of Theorem [bipartite] whose K(π,1)-conjecture were not previously known.
  The edge labels of the diagrams in Theorem [bipartite] is allowed to be arbitrary."
- "When the Coxeter diagram is the star K_{1,n} and all edges are labeled by 3, the only
  previously known cases of K(π,1)-conjecture were K_{1,1} (type A_2) and K_{1,2} (type A_3)
  ..., K_{1,3} (type D_4) ... and K_{1,4} (type D̃_4) recently in [paolini2021proof]."
- The paper states its results "using Coxeter diagrams": nodes `S`, and `s_i, s_j` "joined an
  edge labeled by m_ij if m_ij ≥ 3".
