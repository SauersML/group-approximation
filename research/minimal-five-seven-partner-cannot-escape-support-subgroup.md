Let `k=F_2`, `G` be any group, and suppose

```text
alpha beta=1!=beta alpha,
|supp(alpha)|=5,       |supp(beta)|=7.
```

Put `K=<supp(alpha)>`.  Then

```text
supp(beta) subset K.
```

In particular, for the rectangle branch of a minimal Leavitt corner witness,
the entire `(5,7)` pair lies in the two-generator subgroup `<C_3,x>`; for the
normalizer branch it lies in the subgroup generated by `C_3` and at most two
normalizer representatives.  Terms outside these subgroups cannot serve as
kernel corrections.
