**OPEN.** Let `W = P x| G` be the Kun--Thom coset wreath, `P = F_2[G/Gamma]`, and `K_even` its
sofic radical of fibre-even lamps. There is a `G`-submodule `U <= K_even` such that
`H_U = W/U` carries an injective, non-surjective cellular automaton on some finite-alphabet full
shift.

**Payoff.** This refutes `gottschalk-surjunctivity-conjecture` and shows that surjunctivity does
not pass to quotients even across an abelian kernel inside the sofic radical.

**Why this family.** `W` is surjunctive, and all of its nonsoficity sits in `K_even`. Quotienting
by part of `K_even` keeps the group nonsofic (`kun-thom-lamp-quotients-have-lamp-sofic-radical`),
while the quotient module has no lamp sites for the peeling proof to use.

## Attempts

- **Closed submodules are dead** (`residually-permutational-lamp-extensions-are-surjunctive`).
  If `U` equals its permutation closure, `W/U` embeds locally into permutational wreaths with
  binary lamps over `G`, which are surjunctive. That covers every intermediate coset wreath
  `W_K` with `Gamma <= K <= N`, every intersection of kernels of permutation maps, and every
  submodule generated by one fibre pair. A candidate needs `U != U-bar`: a lamp relation that
  every `phi_c(e_(g Gamma)) = g c`, with `c` finitely supported and `Gamma`-fixed, kills only as a
  consequence of killing `U`.
- **The decoder must read `U`** (`strict-lamp-quotient-realizations-need-lamp-hinges`). Every
  lift of a strict realization to `W` loses forward sufficiency, and the missing coincidence is a
  nonzero fibre-even lamp configuration of `U`. Designs whose forward coincidences all hold in
  `W` are dead.
- **Linear front, not yet tried.**
  - Over odd `l`, `F_l[H_U] = C(U^perp, F_l) x| G` by the Fourier transform of the elementary
    abelian lamps, where `U^perp <= F_2^(G/Gamma)` is the annihilator subshift. Its Haar measure
    is `G`-invariant, so honest clopen compressions die. A witness would need mod-`l`
    cancellation.
  - Over `F_2`, the all-field proof of `kun-thom-wreath-stably-finite` detects elements through
    marked lamp sites, which a quotient module lacks. Stable finiteness of `F_2[H_U]` for
    non-closed `U` is open.
- **Finitely many lamps suffice** (artifact Proposition 5.1). A strict pair over `W/U` transfers
  to `W/U_0` for the submodule `U_0 <= U` generated by the finitely many lamp hinges of its lifts.
  So `U` may be taken finitely generated, and every finitely generated submodule of a candidate
  must be non-closed somewhere.
- **Finite coset spaces are blind** (Lemma 5.2). `Gamma` is profinitely dense in `N`, so every map
  into a finite permutation module kills `K_even`. Closure is decided only by infinite `G`-sets.
- **Hecke correspondences** (Lemma 5.3). Every permutation map is a sum of
  `T_(Gamma_0, L) : e_(g Gamma) -> sum_(gamma in Gamma/Gamma_0) e_(g gamma L)`, with `Gamma_0` of finite
  index in `Gamma` and `L >= Gamma_0`. Overgroups `L >= Gamma` give only permutation kernels. So a
  non-closed `U` must be invisible to the correspondences through finite-index subgroups of
  `Gamma` whose overgroups do not contain `Gamma`.
- **Where it stops.** No non-closed submodule of `K_even` has been exhibited, and no design is
  known.
