The premise names H=L_(F_2)(1,2)^x and asserts it is nonhyperlinear,
which is an explicit witness for the root. Its stronger structural
properties are not needed for this implication.

**Attribution.** This is a downstream Cairn application of Jihao Liu,
[*Nonhyperlinear groups exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf),
Theorem 1.2/6.7, via the recorded Leavitt heat argument. The analytic
result remains Liu's; the route does not import it unconditionally.
