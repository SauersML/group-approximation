**ESTABLISHED (obstruction / necessary gate).**  Put

```text
R=L_(F_2)(1,2),   Delta=St_20(R),   pi:Delta->E_20(R)<=GL_20(R),
q=s_1t_1,          z=x_13(q),        P_z=(1-z)/2.          (CG1)
```

Suppose `Phi:A(B_loop)->P_z C[Delta] P_z` is a unital star homomorphism
(the diagram `LNC3` of `agent-leavitt-not-bcs-negative-root-corner`).  Let
`X` be the finite set of group elements occurring with nonzero coefficient
in the images of the BCS generators, and let

```text
S_X = unital F_2-subalgebra of R generated by q and all matrix
      entries of pi(g), pi(g)^(-1), for g in X.                  (CG2)
```

Then

```text
q in Rad_rep(S_X) := intersection of ker(rho) over all unital ring
      homomorphisms rho:S_X->M_k(K), K any field, k>=1.          (CG3)
```

More generally (the hyperlinear shadow gate): for every group homomorphism
`h` from `Gamma_X=<X,z> <= Delta` to a hyperlinear group, `h(z)=1`.

**Consequences (classes that die).**  A diagram `LNC3` cannot have support
`X` in any of the following situations, at any support size and any complex
coefficients:

1. `S_X` is finite-dimensional, commutative, or more generally satisfies a
   polynomial identity (PI).  In particular: all degree-zero-core
   coefficients, all bounded-prefix degree-zero inventories, and every
   family whose entries commute.
2. `S_X` is contained in a subalgebra of `R` with a unital finite-dimensional
   representation nonzero on `q`.  Example: the one-sided Toeplitz range
   algebra `F_2<s_1,t_1>` (`t_1s_1=1`, `q=s_1t_1` is its range projection):
   the character `s_1,t_1 |-> 1` sends `q` to `1`.  So no diagram whose
   coefficients are words in `s_1,t_1` only exists.
3. `Gamma_X` has a hyperlinear quotient (for instance finite, residually
   finite, amenable, or linear) in which `z` survives.  This contains the
   nested spin reservoir of `agent-leavitt-local-spin-corner`, where
   `Gamma_X` is itself a finite extraspecial group.

**What survives, and the sharpness of the gate.**  Every construction must
use coefficients whose fd representations all kill the mark.  Typical
mechanism: a Dedekind defect.  If `S_X` contains `s_2,t_2`, then in any
fd representation `t_2s_2=1` forces `s_2t_2=1`, hence `q=1-s_2t_2 |-> 0`;
so `F_2<s_2,t_2>` (the complementary Toeplitz algebra, whose defect is
exactly `q`) and `R` itself pass the gate, as does the unital hull
`qRq+F_2(1-q)`.  The gate is therefore a genuine filter, not a
no-go for the hole.  The first natural survivor class, complementary
Toeplitz coefficients, has `Gamma_X` locally-finite-by-linear with `z` in
the locally finite congruence kernel; whether such groups can carry the
diagram is recorded as open under the target's `## Attempts`.
