**ESTABLISHED.**  Suppose countably many finite auxiliary gadgets have exact
tracial models whose restrictions agree trace-preservingly on successive
finite-dimensional packet wires.  Then they have one exact tracial model
simultaneously, obtained by the countable reduced amalgamated free product of
`countable-tracial-amalgam-extension`.  Every original mark retains its trace,
and no unintended relation is imposed between auxiliaries from different
free legs.

If all gadgets already act on one countably amplified Hilbert representation
and agree on the base generators, the conclusion is even formal: the union
of their assignments represents the union presentation because there are no
cross-gadget relators.

Hence common HALT completeness need not obstruct bespoke enumeration when
the gadgets are designed over compatible finite wires or one Fock
amplification.  What amalgamation cannot do is create local NONHALT
nonextendability.  In particular it does not groupify the Culf--Mastel
nonaffine projection predicates.
