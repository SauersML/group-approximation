Fix `n`. The first premise embeds `B_n` in `GL_m(F)` for a number field `F`.
`B_n` is finitely generated, so the second premise gives a proper isometric
action of `B_n` on a proper, complete, finite-dimensional CAT(0) space. The
space is a product of symmetric spaces and Bruhat–Tits buildings, each
building with an `R` factor. Properness is in the Bridson–Haefliger sense
used by the target claim.
