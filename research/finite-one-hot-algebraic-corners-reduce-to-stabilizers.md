Let `Q` be a finite set, let `p` be prime, and put

```text
K=F_p^Q.
```

Suppose a countable group `Lambda` acts on `K` by the coordinate-permutation
automorphisms coming from an action on `Q`.  Let

```text
Y={e_q:q in Q} subset K
```

be the one-hot locus and let `z=1_Y in L^infinity(K)`.  Decompose `Q` into
`Lambda`-orbits `O`, choose `q_O in O`, and write

```text
H_O=Stab_Lambda(q_O).
```

Then, with the canonical normalized traces,

```text
z (L^infinity(K) rtimes Lambda) z
   ~= direct_sum_O M_|O|(L(H_O)).                       (FOH1)
```

(The trace on a summand has the scalar weight inherited from Haar measure on
`K`; this does not affect Connes embeddability.)  Consequently, if a unital
star algebra `B` having tracial states but no Connes-embeddable tracial state
embeds trace-preservingly into the one-hot corner in `(FOH1)`, then at least
one point stabilizer `H_O` is already nonhyperlinear.

Thus the elementary finite truth-table/Fourier trick does not manufacture a
trace-promotion bypass.  It stores each truth-table state as a basis vector,
but all noncommutative cycle holonomy is carried by the isotropy group of that
state.  If the resulting corner contains the fixed no-CE BCS relation algebra,
one of those isotropy groups already solves the nonhyperlinear-group problem.

There is no positive-corner limit obtained by assigning an independent
one-hot register to every translate.  More precisely, for an infinite set
`I`, finite sets `Q_i`, and

```text
K_I=product_(i in I) F_p^(Q_i),
Y_I=product_(i in I) {e_q:q in Q_i},
```

Haar measure satisfies

```text
m(Y_I)=product_(i in I) |Q_i|/p^(|Q_i|).                (FOH2)
```

For nonempty `Q_i` every factor is at most `1/2`, so `m(Y_I)=0` for every
infinite family of nonempty registers.  The formal infinite one-hot selector is then the zero
projection in `L^infinity(K_I)`.  A direct one-hot compiler therefore has an
exact dichotomy: finitely many registers give the stabilizer reduction
`(FOH1)`, while infinitely many independent registers lose the positive
trace corner required for trace promotion.
