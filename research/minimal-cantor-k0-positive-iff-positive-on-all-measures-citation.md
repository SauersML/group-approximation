Citation import, read verbatim from the TeX source of Durand–Perrin, *Dimension Groups and Dynamical Systems*
(Cambridge 2022), arXiv:2007.15721. Quotes are in artifact part 2, (I2)–(I3).
- `chapter5.tex` l.832–836, label `theoremDimensionGroup`, credited to Herman, Putnam and Skau: "For any minimal
  Cantor system $(X,T)$ [...] the ordered group $K^0(X,T)$ is a simple dimension group."
- `chapter4.tex` §"Invariant measures and states", label `propositionKerov`: for a recurrent system,
  `μ ↦ α_μ = ∫ · dμ` is a bijection onto the states of `K^0(X,T)`.
- `chapter3.tex` l.480–490, label `propositionEffros` (Effros 1981, Corollary 4.2; Effros–Handelman–Shen 1980): "If
  $\G=(G,G^+,u)$ is an unperforated simple unital group, then $G^+=\{g\in G\mid p(g)>0\mbox{ for every }p\in S(\G)\}\cup \{0\}$."

Minimal systems are recurrent, and dimension groups are unperforated. The primary papers (Herman–Putnam–Skau,
Internat. J. Math. 3 (1992); Effros, CBMS 46 (1981)) were not opened.
