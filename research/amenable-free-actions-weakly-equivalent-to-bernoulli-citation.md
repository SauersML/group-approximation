`/scratch.global/sauer354/ex/ex2-rokhlin-liftable-positive/bk1611.txt`.

P. J. Burton and A. S. Kechris, *Weak containment of measure preserving group actions*, arXiv:1611.07921
(version dated April 17, 2019), Section 3, the paragraph immediately after Theorem 3.6 ([T-D1, Corollary 1.6]):

> "When the group Γ is amenable, there is only one free weak equivalence class, i.e., all free actions are
> weakly equivalent to sΓ, and moreover a ≼ sΓ for any action a ∈ A(Γ, X, µ), see [K, page 91]."

In the source, `sΓ` is the shift action of `Γ` on `[0,1]^Γ` with the product measure, `A(Γ, X, µ)` is the space
of measure preserving actions of `Γ` on a non-atomic standard probability space, and `≼` is weak containment.
Reference [K] is A. S. Kechris, *Global aspects of ergodic group actions*, AMS 2010. The book was not read here;
the statement is used as Burton--Kechris state it.

The groups in the source are countable (discrete) and infinite in this context, so the import is for countably
infinite amenable `Γ`.
