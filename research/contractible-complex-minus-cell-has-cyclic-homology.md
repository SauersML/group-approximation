**ESTABLISHED** through `contractible-complex-minus-cell-homology-proof`.

Let L be a finite connected contractible 2-dimensional CW complex, and let
K ⊂ L be the subcomplex obtained by removing one open 2-cell e. Then:
1. χ(K) = 0;
2. H₁(K; Z) ≅ Z and H₂(K; Z) = 0;
3. π₁(K) is normally generated by the attaching loop of e, so it has weight one,
   and it is infinite.

Call such K a **one-cell complement** of L.
