**ESTABLISHED** through `leavitt-surjective-k2-stability-implies-injective-proof`. Lane proof, not independently
reviewed. Elementary given `leavitt-stabilization-kernels-are-one-step`. No priority is claimed.

**Setting.** `R` is a unital ring with a Leavitt pair `e, f`, and `N >= 3`. `Σ^(g)` and `D_N` are as in
`leavitt-stabilization-kernels-are-one-step`, where `j_N = D_N ∘ Σ^(e)` and `U_N = ker j_N = ker Σ^(e)`.
- For an idempotent `p`, the **`p`-corner subgroup** `H_p ⊆ St_N(R)` is generated by the `X_ij(a)` with `i, j < N`,
  the `X_iN(a)` with `a in Rp`, and the `X_Nj(b)` with `b in pR`.
- The explicit element `u = e e e* + f e* f* + e f f* f*` is a unit, with inverse `u*`.

**Statement.**
1. **Local unitary agreement.** `u` restricts to `e` on `ee*R`, that is `u e e* = e e e*`. Hence
   `Σ^(e) = Σ^(u)` on `H_(ee*)`. `Σ^(u)` is an automorphism, so `Σ^(e)` is injective on `H_(ee*)`, and
   `U_N ∩ H_(ee*) = 1`. The same holds for `H_(ff*)`, with the roles of `e` and `f` exchanged.
2. **Surjective stability implies injective stability.** If `j_N` maps `K_2(N, R)` onto `K_2(N+1, R)`, then
   `Σ^(e)(K_2(N,R)) = K_2(N,R)`. So `K_2(N,R) ⊆ Σ^(e)(St_N(R)) ⊆ H_(ee*)`, and `U_N = 1`.
3. **For `R_L`** (`e = s_1`, `f = s_2`). Let `S = {{λ, N+1}_N : λ in Q^x}` be the symbol section of
   `leavitt-resolvent-stable-k2-class-is-computable`. Then:
   - `Σ^(e)(K_2(N, R_L)) = S` and `K_2(N, R_L) ∩ H_(ee*) = S`;
   - the following are equivalent: (i) `U_N = 1`; surjective stability `j_N(K_2(N)) = K_2(N+1)`; and
     `K_2(N, R_L) ⊆ H_(ee*)`. The last says every element of `K_2(N)` is a word whose last-column coefficients lie
     in `R s_1 t_1` and last-row coefficients in `s_1 t_1 R`.
4. **Shape of a counterexample.** A nontrivial element of `U_N` is a Steinberg word that cannot be rewritten with
   its last-coordinate coefficients inside the `e`-corner, nor inside the `f`-corner. It must use both halves of
   the last coordinate.

## Reading

Gate 1 is now a support question: can every relation word in `K_2(N, R_L)` be pushed into one Leavitt half of the
last coordinate? The standard surjective-stability arguments clear the last row and column by stable range, and
`R_L` has infinite stable range. So neither direction is decided.
