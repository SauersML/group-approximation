**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, elementary; not reviewed; folklore-level, no priority claimed).

## Statement

Let `Y ⊆ A^{Λ_2}` be a subshift over a finitely generated group `Λ_2`, with language `L(Y)`, the set
of globally admissible finite patterns. Let `H = ⟨Φ_1, …, Φ_k⟩ ≤ Aut(Y)` be generated by
shift-commuting homeomorphisms whose local rules and inverse local rules have radius `≤ R`.

1. **Word problem below the language.** A word `w` of length `n` in the `Φ_i^{±1}` is trivial in `H`
   iff `F_w(p) = p(1)` for every `p ∈ L(Y)` with support the ball `B_{nR}`, where `F_w` is the
   composed local rule. So the word problem of `H` is decidable relative to `L(Y)`. For `Λ_2 = Z^d`,
   put `β(m) = |B_m| = O(m^d)`. Then it runs in time `|A|^{β(nR)} · (β(nR) n + L_Y(β(nR)))`, up to
   constants, where `L_Y(m)` is the cost of deciding admissibility of patterns of size `m`.
2. **Time lifts.** In the setting of `deterministic-time-lifts-inherit-quantum-rigidity`, with
   `Λ = Λ_1 × Λ_2` and `Φ : Λ_1 → Aut(Y)` faithful, the word problem of `Λ_1` obeys the bound of 1.
   So does the word problem of every group `G ≤ Λ_1`, up to a linear-time substitution.
3. **No uniform space SFT.** Fix `d`, and let `𝒴` be any family of subshifts over `Z^d` whose
   languages are uniformly in `F(g)` for one recursive non-decreasing `g` (notation of
   `uniform-wp-bound-excludes-bh-universal-targets`). Then some finitely presented group with
   solvable word problem embeds in no group of automorphisms of a member of `𝒴`. In particular it
   embeds in no `Λ_1` of a time lift over `𝒴`.

## Proof

1. By Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon, `Φ_w` has a local rule of radius `≤ nR`. The rule is obtained by applying
   the generators' rules in turn to shrinking balls, and this is valid on admissible patterns, since
   `Y` is invariant. `Φ_w = id` on `Y` iff its local rule returns the centre on every admissible
   pattern of radius `nR`. Enumerate the `|A|^{β(nR)}` patterns on `B_{nR}`, discard those not in
   `L(Y)`, and evaluate the rest.
2. Item 1 applied to `Φ(Λ_1) ≅ Λ_1`, together with the substitution `G → Λ_1`.
3. Put `g'(n) = 2^{n^d} (n^{d+1} + g(n^d))`. By 1, every finitely generated group of automorphisms
   of a member of `𝒴` has word problem in `F(g')`. The constants depend on the radius, the alphabet
   and the generating set, which `F(g')` allows, but `g'` is the same for all of them. Finitely
   generated subgroups inherit the bound by substitution. By
   `uniform-wp-bound-excludes-bh-universal-targets`, some finitely presented residually finite group
   with decidable word problem embeds in none of these groups. ∎

## What it changes

In master-route design rule 8, the second factor of `Λ_0 × Z^2` plays time: G2 over `Λ_0 × Z^2` is
reduced to a rigid free minimal `Z^2`-SFT `Y` with `Λ_0 ≤ Aut(Y)` acting jointly freely and minimally
(`research/artifacts/gq-bh-synthesis-master-route.md`, §2). This node adds a complexity constraint to
that reduction. `WP(Λ_0)`, and so the input's word problem, is primitive recursive relative to `L(Y)`.
So `Y` must be programmed with the input's complexity. Its language must be at least as hard as the
input's word problem, up to the bound in 1. No rigid `Y`, and no family of `Y`s with uniformly bounded
languages, serves all inputs.

Track A through rule 8 therefore meets Track B's requirement: a quantum-rigid free minimal `Z^2`-SFT
with a **programmable language**. The hard minimal free `Z^2`-SFTs of
`minimal-free-z2-sfts-have-arbitrarily-hard-row-languages` show that such languages exist. The open
part is combining them with rigidity and a rich automorphism group.

## Lesson for general BH

Automorphisms of a subshift see only its language. So whenever an input acts on a space SFT by
cellular automata, the input's complexity must already be written into that SFT's language. In the
time-lift form of the master route, "complexity sits in `Λ_0`" and "complexity sits in `L(X)`" are
the same requirement. The G2 object must therefore be a uniform family `Y_P` of rigid minimal
`Z^2`-SFTs, programmable by the input `P`, not one universal tiling. This is the same shape as every
other input-dependent host on main (`twisted-brin-thompson-wp-equals-actor-orbit-problem`,
`hnn-stages-add-no-complexity-beyond-edge-distortion`): complexity is carried, never created, and
the carrier must be chosen after the input.
