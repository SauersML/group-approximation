Let `B` be unital and `phi,psi:Z->B` unital embeddings, and form `D->E` as
in `stw63-relative-v-obstruction`.  If

```text
K_0(D) -> K_0(E) is injective                              (2)
```

and projections cancel in matrix algebras over `D`, then `phi` and `psi`
are approximately unitarily equivalent in `B`.

In particular, the cancellation hypothesis holds if `D` has stable rank
one.  Notice that this is stable rank one of the pair-dependent relative
commutant `D`, not stable rank one of `B`; no permanence implication from
`B` to `D` is asserted.
