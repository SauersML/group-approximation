Distinct from `el20-native-cross-gram-gives-full-mf-radical`: that route
applies the compiler to the original reblocked arbitrary-profile model and so
needs `(EAP1)`.  This route first replaces the model by conjugate tensor powers
and needs the estimate only on canonical operator-norm microstates.  It is also
distinct from `property-t-free-el20-collapse-via-regular-character`, which
needs a routeless proof that `delta_e` is not an MF character.  This route
supplies such an exclusion for EL20 through the native cross-Gram compiler.

Let `G=EL_20(R)`, `R=L_(F_2)(1,2)`, and suppose `Rad_MF(G)!=G`.  `G` is
simple (`binary-leavitt-elementary-group-is-simple`), nonabelian, and hence
perfect.  It is finitely generated by the root elements over the finite
generating set `1,s_0,s_1,t_0,t_1` of `R`, and `z=e_13(s_1t_1)` is a nonidentity
involution.

Apply `surviving-mf-mark-diracizes-to-canonical-opnorm-profile` with `C=1`.
It gives operator-norm asymptotic representations `W_n` with
`tr W_n -> delta_e`.  Every fixed finite packet has Plancherel multiplicities,
and `z` has negative carrier of normalized trace `1/2+o(1)`.

On these models `binary-leavitt-el20-canonical-opnorm-native-cross-gram`
provides `(CAP1)` and the two mixed Pauli commutator estimates.  The steps from
there on are the same as in `el20-native-cross-gram-gives-full-mf-radical`:

- `two-transport-cross-gram-has-a-fixed-mass-reducing-corner` cuts a fixed-mass
  approximately reducing source corner. Fixed finite-packet exactification
  makes it reducing.
- `two-mixed-pauli-commutators-lock-the-relative-reservoir-gauge` moves the
  relative polar unitary into the common multiplicity reservoir.
- The two selector rows force one gauge fraction toward both `1/2` and `5/6`.
  This contradicts `two-shared-gauge-selector-rows-have-an-affine-rank-obstruction`,
  which gives a row defect of at least `1/3-o(1)` on a positive-density carrier,
  while `W_n` has vanishing relator defects.

Hence no such `W_n` exists, and so `Rad_MF(G)=G`, which is `(EMF1)`.  The canonical
trace profile is a consequence of the surviving mark, not a hypothesis.  No
Property `(T)`, Kazhdan projection, or literature theorem is used.
