OPEN.  For the finite menu of gauge-doubled Fanizza Schur/mixed-CNOT hard
frames, construct a finitely presented occurrence model with Thompson `V`
such that:

1. each level's private hard involution `A_x` is fixed by the point stabilizer
   of its site and the finite menu of roles gives only finitely many site
   orbits;
2. the invariant logical products of the two gauge shares still realize the
   original shared Fanizza BCS words and native signal;
3. no identification of private shares at distinct sites forces two original
   logical variables from noncommon contexts to commute; and
4. the exact gauge-covariant marked representation is a site tensor model to
   which `point-stabilizer-local-hard-share-compiles-bridge-orbit` applies.

This is the residual incidence/coherence problem after the analytic bridge,
the exact spectral matching, and the abstract finite-orbit syntax have been
separated.  It must be proved on the actual gauge-doubled Fanizza menu, not by
declaring all logical variables site-local.

## Attempts

- Putting the entire shared logical tuple into commuting lamp factors is
  invalid: it globalizes commutation across contexts.
- Keeping the logical product global while making only one oriented private
  gauge share local is compatible with the exact gauge symmetry, but a finite
  presentation must still express every local product relation without
  identifying the other share across sites.
- Occurrence-specific copies plus direct consistency tests avoid literal
  identification, but then one needs a fixed quantitative consistency decoder;
  this is the local shadow of `finite-orbit-occurrence-pauli-compiler`.
