Let a finite abelian selector subgroup centralize a finite edge group.  If one
irreducible target type contains the entire induced edge type, then that target
contains every selector character compatible on the intersection.  A saturated
selector-invariant finite edge therefore cannot encode a proper nonlinear
allowed set.
