Allcock Theorems 1.4(ii) and 1.1 finitely present `St_r(R)` for every
`r>=3`, because the Laurent ring is generated by finitely many units and
type `A_(r-1)` is spherical without an `A_1` component.  Hence
`G_tilde=St_r(R) semidirect SL_d(Z)` is finitely presented.  Steinberg
addition and commutator relations finitely generate its polynomial-root
subgroup `Gamma_tilde`.

Positive compressors send `Gamma_tilde` into itself and, together with it,
generate `G_tilde`.  In every finite image the compressor inclusions become
equalities, so the image of `Gamma_tilde` is normal.  The marked root
`h_tilde` is a compressor conjugate of a polynomial root, hence its finite
image belongs to the image of `Gamma_tilde`.

Put the images in `G` of every cover relator prefix, polynomial subgroup
generator and the marked lift into one actor table.  Scalar Clifford
curvature projects this table to symplectic maps satisfying every cover
relator, so it defines a homomorphism `G_tilde->Sp(K)`.  The tested covariance
makes the base lamp label fixed by the polynomial subgroup and sends it by
the marked lift to the neighbouring label.  Finite-image collapse says that
the marked lift also fixes the base label.  The two labels coincide,
contradicting their prescribed alternating pairing `1`.
