Let `K` be countable amenable, let `Lambda -> Aut(K)`, and suppose
`D <= L(K)` is a `Lambda`-invariant Cartan subalgebra.  The Feldman--Moore
relation `S_D` of the Cartan pair `D <= L(K)` is hyperfinite, and the
`Lambda`-action normalizes `S_D`.

Consequently, a base-equivariant embedding of a pmp `Lambda`-action
`L^infinity(X) <= D` into this group-basis model can only be built through a
`Lambda`-normalized hyperfinite relation envelope on the target measure
space.  If the embedding identifies the full Cartan, the original action
must act by automorphisms of one ergodic hyperfinite relation.  Thus a
Bratteli/locally-finite construction does not merely need finite-dimensional
matrix approximants: it must install a coherent normalized hyperfinite
relation.  Non-Cartan embeddings remain outside this obstruction.
