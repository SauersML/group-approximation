**Established by citation.**  Sources, checked on 2026-08-22 against the
arXiv HTML renderings:

- Mehta, Slofstra, Zhao, *Positivity is undecidable in tensor products of
  free algebras*, arXiv:2312.05617v2, Section 4: the presentation of `G_L`
  (relations (0)--(3), with relation (2) the addressed commutator schedule
  `(MGR2)`), Lemma 4.6 (finite generation), the presentation of `A_L(m)` by
  relations (R0)--(R6) with (R6) the guarded product `(MGR1)`, Remark 4.9
  (relator norms and `m`-independence), and the proof of Lemma 4.10 (the
  halting representation, with the projections `(MGR3)` and the
  verification of (R6) cell by cell, including the terminal cell where
  `X_(m,n) Z_(m,n) X_(m,n) Z_(m,n) = 1` and the factor `1 + J` kills
  `P_n = (1 - J)/2`).
- Fanizza, Kroell, Mehta, Paddock, Rochette, Slofstra, Zhao, *The NPA
  hierarchy does not always attain the commuting operator value*,
  arXiv:2510.04943v4, Section 5: the relation groups `(R~2)--(R~6)` of the
  nested BCS, with `(R~5) = R_nest(C_17)`, `C_17 : O_D = O_P AND O_Q`, and
  `(R~6) = R_nest(C_18)` for the six-context Boolean constraint whose
  algebraic form is `(MGR1)`; Proposition 5.13 and its proof, which repeats
  the Lemma 4.10 representation with the additional relations.
