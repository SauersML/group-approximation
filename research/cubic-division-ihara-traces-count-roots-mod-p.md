title: For a free pair in SL_1(O_D), the prime-level Ihara traces of the degenerate principal series count roots of reduced characteristic polynomials mod p

ESTABLISHED (unreviewed; elementary given the cited nodes).  Let `D`, `O_D` and
`Gamma_D` be as in `cubic-division-congruence-representations-converge-strongly`.
Let `A, B in Gamma_D` generate a free group `H`, and put `Z = A + A^-1 + B + B^-1`.
Let `P_H` be the finite set of primes outside which `H -> SL_3(F_p)` is onto
(`cubic-division-congruence-sc-descends-to-thin-subgroups`, part 1).  For
`w in Gamma_D` put

```text
f_w(x)   = x^3 - Trd(w) x^2 + Trd(w^-1) x - 1  in Z[x]    (reduced characteristic polynomial)
Delta(w) = f_w(1) = det(1 - w) = Trd(w^-1) - Trd(w)       in Z
```

and let `CR_n` be the cyclically reduced words of length `n` in `A, B`, with
`|CR_n| = 3^n + 2 + (-1)^n`.

1. **Words are cubic units.**  For `w != 1`, `f_w` is irreducible over `Q`,
   `E_w = Q(w) <= D` is a cubic field, `w` is a unit of norm 1 in it, and
   `Delta(w) != 0`.
2. **Projective plane.**  Let `rho_p = l^2_0(P^2(F_p))`, the unipotent representation of
   dimension `p^2 + p`.  For `p` not in `P_H` and `w in H`,
   `chi_(rho_p)(w) = #Fix_(P^2(F_p))(w mod p) - 1 = sum_(lambda in F_p) (p^(d_lambda) - 1)/(p - 1) - 1`,
   where `d_lambda = dim ker(w - lambda mod p)`.  If `p` does not divide `disc(f_w)`, this is
   `r_p(w) - 1`, where `r_p(w)` is the number of roots of `f_w` mod `p`.  It is also the
   Frobenius trace at `p` of the 2-dimensional Artin representation `rho_(E_w)`: the Galois
   permutation representation on the roots of `f_w`, minus the trivial one.  So
   `L(s, rho_(E_w)) = zeta_(E_w)(s) / zeta(s)`.
3. **Every degenerate principal series at once.**  Let `sigma_p = l^2_0(F_p^3 minus 0)`, of
   dimension `p^3 - 2`.  It contains `rho_p` and every `Ind_P^G(chi o alpha)`, `chi != 1`.  For
   `w in H`, `chi_(sigma_p)(w) = p^(d_1(w)) - 2`.  Here `d_1(w) >= 1` iff `p | Delta(w)`, and
   `d_1(w) >= 2` iff `w mod p` is a transvection or `1`.  With
   `N_p(n) = #{w in CR_n : p | Delta(w)}`, `U_p(n) = #{w in CR_n : rank(w - 1 mod p) <= 1}` and
   `I_p(n) = #{w in CR_n : w = 1 mod p}`,
   `T_(sigma_p)(n) = (p-1) N_p(n) + (p^2-p) U_p(n) + (p^3-p^2) I_p(n) - |CR_n|`.
4. **Reformulations.**  These follow from `free-group-ihara-bass-outlier-trace-criterion` with
   `k = 2`, `q = 3`.
   - (a) If `limsup_p ||rho_p(Z)|| <= 2 sqrt 3`, then for every `delta > 0`, all large `p` and all `n`:
     `|sum_(w in CR_n) (#Fix_(P^2)(w mod p) - 1)| <= 2 (p^2 + p)((sqrt 3 + delta)^n + 1)`.
     Conversely, suppose there are `C`, `delta_p -> 0` and even `n_p` with `n_p / log p -> infinity`
     such that the sum at `n_p` is `<= p^C (sqrt 3 + delta_p)^(n_p)`.  Then `limsup ||rho_p(Z)|| <= 2 sqrt 3`.
   - (b) If `limsup_p ||sigma_p(Z)|| <= 2 sqrt 3`, then for every `delta > 0`, all large `p` and all `n`:
     `|(p-1) N_p(n) + (p^2-p) U_p(n) + (p^3-p^2) I_p(n) - 3^n| <= 2 p^3 ((sqrt 3 + delta)^n + 1)`.
     Conversely, suppose that at even `n_p` with `n_p / log p -> infinity` the left side, taken without
     absolute values, is `<= p^C (sqrt 3 + delta_p)^(n_p)`.  Then `limsup ||sigma_p(Z)|| <= 2 sqrt 3`.

   So no outlier in the degenerate principal series is exactly an optimal level of
   distribution: along cyclically reduced words, the integers `Trd(w^-1) - Trd(w)` must be
   divisible by `p` with frequency `1/(p-1)`, corrected by the unipotent terms, up to an
   error of relative size `(sqrt 3 + o(1))^n / 3^n` at lengths `n >> log p`.  The P^2
   sector is a Chebotarev statement about the cubic fields generated by long words.
5. **Vertical statistics at fixed length** (import: the Chebotarev density theorem).  Fix
   `n`.  For all but finitely many `p`, every `w in CR_n` is regular semisimple mod `p` and
   `T_(rho_p)(n) = sum_(w in CR_n) tr rho_(E_w)(Frob_p)`.  Over primes, in natural density,
   `T_(rho_p)(n)` has mean `0` and mean square
   `V_n = sum_E m_E c_n(E)^2`.  Here `E` runs over isomorphism classes of cubic fields,
   `c_n(E) = #{w in CR_n : E_w = E}`, and `m_E = 1` for `S_3` fields and `2` for cyclic ones.
   Rotating or inverting a word preserves `E_w`, so `V_n >= n #{primitive w in CR_n} = (1 + o(1)) n 3^n`.
   The fluctuations over primes are therefore at least of square-root size `3^(n/2)`, exactly the
   tempered scale in part 4.  Field coincidences between non-conjugate words would enlarge them.
