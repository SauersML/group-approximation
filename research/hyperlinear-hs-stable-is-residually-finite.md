Let `G` be finitely presented, hyperlinear, and strictly same-dimension stable
for normalized Hilbert--Schmidt approximate representations.  Then `G` is
residually finite.

Equivalently, if a finitely presented same-dimension HS-stable group is not
residually finite, it is already an explicit nonhyperlinear group.  In
particular, every noncircular hyperlinear candidate for the ambient group in
`hs-stable-finite-bi-index-kazhdan-pair-exists` must be residually finite.
