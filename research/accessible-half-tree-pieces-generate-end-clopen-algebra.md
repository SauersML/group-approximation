**OPEN** (split out 2026-09-17 as the unproved step of
`accessible-end-euler-measure-proof`, after referee lenses 1 and 2 refuted
its coarse-connectivity step 2 when a vertex group is infinite).

Let `P` be finitely generated with finite generating set `S` and Cayley graph
`Gamma` (edges `{y, gy}`, `g` in `S`). Suppose `P` is the fundamental group of
a finite reduced graph of groups with finite edge groups and vertex groups
having at most one end, and that `P` has infinitely many ends. Let `P` act on
the right on the Bass-Serre tree `T`, subdivide one edge orbit, and let `v0`
be a midpoint, so `G0 = Stab(v0)` is finite. Put `pi(y) = v0.y`, and for an
oriented edge `e` put `P_e = pi^-1(T_e)`, where `T_e` is the half-tree `e`
points into.

**Claim.** Every subset `U` of `P` with finite coboundary in `Gamma` lies in
the Boolean algebra generated by the sets `P_e` and the finite subsets of `P`.

When every vertex group is finite, `T` is locally finite and steps 2(a) and 3
of the route go through. The open case is when some vertex group is infinite.

## Attempts

- **2026-09-17: route `accessible-end-euler-measure-proof`, steps 2-3
  (refuted).** Step 2(a) asserts that orbit vertices at tree distance at most
  `2R0+1` differ by one of finitely many `G_(v0)`-orbit types, and step 2(b)
  asserts that `N_v = pi^-1(B_T(v,R1))` is a finite union of orbits
  `y_i G_v`. Both need `T` to be locally finite, and both fail when a vertex
  group is infinite. For example, take `Z^2*Z^2*Z^2` with graph `A-B-C`,
  trivial edge groups, the `A-B` edge subdivided and `v = C`: `N_v` then
  contains `BC`, which has infinitely many right `C`-orbits and is not
  coarsely one-ended. The full referee reasons are recorded verbatim in
  `accessible-non-virtually-cyclic-groups-carry-end-euler-measures`. The
  referees expect the statement can be repaired, for example by choosing `v0`
  and `R1` more carefully or by proving that half-spaces are coarsely connected
  some other way.
