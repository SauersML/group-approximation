Jaikin-Zapirain and Linton, *On the coherence of one-relator groups and
their group algebras*, Annals of Mathematics 201 (2025), 909--959,
DOI 10.4007/annals.2025.201.3.4, Theorem 3.4, states exactly that if `K`
is a field and `G` is an ascending HNN extension of a free group, then
`K[G]` is coherent.

No characteristic, proper-power, virtual-specialness, or hyperbolicity
hypothesis occurs in Theorem 3.4.
