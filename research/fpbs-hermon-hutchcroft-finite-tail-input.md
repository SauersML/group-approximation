Section 3 imports Jonathan Hermon and Tom Hutchcroft, arXiv:1904.10448v3,
Theorem 1.1. Its positive exponential decay rate for the number of edges
incident to a finite cluster also bounds its vertex volume in this
bounded-degree transitive setting. Summing the tail gives finite truncated
susceptibility. This is an identified published input, not a new proof.
