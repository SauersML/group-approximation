Let D be separable, simple, stably finite, pure, and real rank zero,
and let X be second-countable locally compact Hausdorff with finite
covering dimension. For positive a,b in C0(X,D tensor K), global
Cuntz comparison [a]<=[b] is equivalent to fiberwise comparison and
the following condition: for every nonzero projection class v and
every compact K contained in {x:[a(x)]=v=[b(x)]}, the support
projections of a and b over K are Murray--von Neumann equivalent.

The fixed-class supports are continuous. Equivalence on every compact
subset of a stratum is required; no single equivalence over a whole
noncompact stratum is assumed. The criterion determines comparison
of actual classes and does not itself realize arbitrary prescribed data.
