Direct proof, written in the claim body: the three-generator value is
`(F_oo x F_oo) x| Z` and embeds in `F_2 x F_2`; the two-generator value is an
HNN extension of `F_2` by the restriction of the swap automorphism to the
normal closure of one generator, and a `sigma`-invariant (characteristic)
filtration of `F_2` produces finite HNN quotients in which every
Britton-reduced word stays reduced.
