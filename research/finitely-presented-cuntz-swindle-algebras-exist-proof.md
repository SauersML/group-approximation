Lane proof (bh-algebra, 2026-09-19). Notation as in the claim. Write `I = ⟨R ∪ σ̃(R) ∪ Q ∪ σ̃(Q)⟩`
and `I_0 = ⟨Q ∪ U⟩ ⊆ I`.

**Step 1: σ̃² ≡ Ad(u) σ̃ modulo `I_0` on all of `F`.**
- The map `f ↦ u σ̃(f) ū` is a unital homomorphism `F -> F/I_0`. It is multiplicative because
  `u σ̃(f) ū · u σ̃(g) ū ≡ u σ̃(fg) ū` using `ūu ≡ 1`, and it is unital because `u ū ≡ 1`.
- The map `f ↦ σ̃²(f)` is also a unital homomorphism.
- On each letter `x` the two maps agree modulo `Q`.
- A homomorphism out of a free algebra is determined by its values on the letters, so
  `σ̃²(f) − u σ̃(f) ū ∈ I_0` for every `f ∈ F`.

(For `x = s_i, s_i*, u, ū` the element of `Q` is already `0` in `F`. The nontrivial ones are
`x = t_i, t_i*, v, v̄`, and they say `ū v` commutes with `u t_i ū`, with `u t_i* ū`, with `u v ū` and
with `u v̄ ū`.)

**Step 2: σ̃(I) ⊆ I.** Since `σ̃` is a homomorphism, it suffices to check the generators of `I`.
- For `r ∈ R ∪ Q`, `σ̃(r) ∈ I` by definition.
- For `σ̃(r)` with `r ∈ R ∪ Q`, Step 1 gives `σ̃(σ̃(r)) ≡ u σ̃(r) ū` modulo `I_0 ⊆ I`, and
  `u σ̃(r) ū ∈ I`.

So `σ̃` induces a unital endomorphism `σ` of `A_sw = F/I`. Only finitely many relators were used:
`|R| = 5 + 4 + 24` and `|Q| = 12`.

**Step 3: the swindle identity (claim part 1).**
- The set `S = {a ∈ A_sw : σ(a) s_1 = s_1 a and σ(a) s_2 = s_2 σ(a)}` contains `1`.
- It is closed under sums and products. For products, `σ(ab) s_1 = σ(a) s_1 b = s_1 ab` and
  `σ(ab) s_2 = σ(a) s_2 σ(b) = s_2 σ(ab)`.
- By (W) it contains every letter, so `S = A_sw`.
- Multiplying by `1 = s_1 s_1* + s_2 s_2*` on the right gives `σ(a) = s_1 a s_1* + s_2 σ(a) s_2*`.

**Step 4: σ² = Ad(u) σ (claim part 2).** Both sides are unital homomorphisms of `A_sw`, since `u` is a
unit by (U), and they agree on the letters by `Q`.

**Step 5: nonvanishing (claim part 3).**
- *Space and Cuntz pair.* Let `X = N × N` and `V = K^(X)` with basis `e_x`. Fix a bijection
  `f : X -> N`. Put `i_1(y) = (0, f(y))` and `i_2(m, n) = (m+1, n)`, which are injections with
  disjoint images covering `X`. Let `s_j e_x = e_(i_j(x))`, and let `s_j*` send `e_(i_l(x))` to
  `δ_jl e_x`. These satisfy the Cuntz relations in `E = End_K(V)`.
- *Decomposition.* Put `w_n = s_2^n s_1`. The range of `w_n` is spanned by the basis `{n} × N`, so
  `V = ⊕_n w_n V` on the nose, with `w_n* w_m = δ_nm`.
- *The swindle.* For `a ∈ E` define `σ(a) = Σ_n w_n a w_n*`. The sum converges pointwise, since each
  basis vector lies in one summand. This is a unital homomorphism. Also `σ(a) s_1 = w_0 a = s_1 a`.
  Since `w_0* s_2 = 0` and `w_n* s_2 = w_(n−1)*`, we get
  `σ(a) s_2 = Σ_(n≥1) w_n a w_(n−1)* = s_2 σ(a)`.
- *The unit.* The operators `w_n w_m`, for `(n, m) ∈ N²`, are left-invertible with
  `(w_n w_m)* (w_(n') w_(m')) = δ δ`. Their ranges `w_n w_m V` form a direct-sum decomposition of
  `V`, because `V = ⊕_n w_n V` and `w_n V = ⊕_m w_n w_m V`. Fix a bijection `π : N -> N²` and put
  `u = Σ_k w_(π(k)) w_k*`. This is invertible with inverse `Σ_k w_k w_(π(k))*`, and
  `u σ(a) u^(−1) = Σ_k w_(π(k)) a w_(π(k))* = σ²(a)`.
- *The representation.* Send `s ↦ s`, `t_i ↦ σ(s_i)`, `t_i* ↦ σ(s_i*)`, `u ↦ u`, `v ↦ σ(u)`, and
  `ū, v̄` to the inverses. This defines `ρ : F -> E` with `ρ σ̃ = σ ρ` on the letters, and hence on
  `F`. For instance `ρ σ̃(t_i) = u σ(s_i) u^(−1) = σ²(s_i) = σ ρ(t_i)`. Checking the relators:
  - `ρ` kills (C) and (U);
  - it kills (W), because the swindle holds on all of `E`;
  - it kills `σ̃(R)`, since `ρ σ̃(r) = σ ρ(r) = 0`;
  - it kills `Q` and `σ̃(Q)`, because `σ² = Ad(u) σ` on all of `E`.
- So `ρ` factors through `A_sw`, and `1 ↦ 1 ≠ 0`.

**Step 6: universality and reduction (claim parts 4, 5).**
- *Universality.* The argument of Step 5 applies verbatim to any `(A', s', σ', u')` with
  `σ'² = Ad(u') σ'`. It uses only the swindle identity on `A'`, the invertibility of `u'` and of
  `σ'(u')`, and `σ'² = Ad(u') σ'`. The map is unique because `t_i ↦ σ'(s'_i)` and `v ↦ σ'(u')` are
  forced.
- *Reduction.* If `M` is `σ`-invariant, `σ` descends to `A_sw/M` and the swindle identity survives.
  If `M` is generated by finitely many elements, lifting them to `F` adds finitely many relators.
  Kernels of intertwining maps are `σ`-invariant. ∎

So `K_q(D ⊗ A_sw) = 0` for every `D` and `q`, and `HH_0(A_sw) = 0`. What `A_sw` lacks for the
