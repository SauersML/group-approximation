Conjugation by an odd permutation of eight letters induces an order-two
outer automorphism `sigma` of `A8`.  The permutation

```text
W delta_g=delta_(sigma(g))
```

is real, self-adjoint, and satisfies `(OIT1)`.  Its spectral projections are
orthogonal and sum to the identity.  Hence `(OIT2)` is unitary:

```text
U^*U=P_plus tensor I + P_minus tensor I=I.            (OITP1)
```

Let `C=I tensor V_minus^*`.  This belongs to the commutant of the
untransported regular chart, so

```text
(UC)(lambda(g) tensor I)(UC)^*
 =U(lambda(g) tensor I)U^*.                           (OITP2)
```

Thus `U` and `UC` define exactly the same second chart, not merely charts
with equal defects.  Expanding `UC` proves `(OIT4)`.

In the standard `delta_g` basis, every block of `P_plus` and `P_minus` is a
scalar.  Consequently every block of `U'` is of the form

```text
a I + b Z,       a,b in C,                            (OITP3)
```

and the star algebra generated by all blocks is contained in `C*(Z)`.
Because `Z` is unitary, `C*(Z)` is commutative.  In finite dimension it is a
direct sum of scalar algebras with arbitrary multiplicities, so all of its
coefficient weight lies in internal matrix size one.  Formula `(BMG2)` of
`leavitt-regular-atlas-block-monomial-gap`, with `R=1`, proves `(OIT5)`.

The argument also explains why apparent raw full generation is spurious.
Although two unrelated unitaries `V_plus,V_minus` can generate `M_r(C)`, the
right multiplication in `(OITP2)` is invisible to every group word and
replaces them by `Z,I`.  Hence raw coefficient generation before quotienting
by the chart commutant is not a gauge-invariant complexity witness.
