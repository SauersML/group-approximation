`J` have negative carrier `P=(1-J)/2` of normalized trace `mu`.  Suppose a
decoded exact module PVM on `PH` has actor energy `E_act`, seed defect
`eta_seed`, and Schreier gap `gamma>0`.  Then

```text
||J-I||_2^2=4mu<=8E_act/gamma+eta_seed^2.              (ICR1)
```



the hyperlinear radical.  For the central `D_8` seed construction, this
already makes the underlying semidirect-product base nonhyperlinear.

Conversely, canonical microstates of a hyperlinear base have `mu->1/2`, so
every seed-preserving decoded PVM obeys

```text

```

There is therefore no independent compactness argument that can bypass the
uniform whole-module covariance gate.
