Let

```text
F = H_X * H_Z * F(P_0 disjoint_union P_1),                     (RC6-1)
```

where the members of the two finite sets `P_0,P_1` are distinct free
generators of the last factor.  Let `R` be a symmetrized set of cyclically
reduced free-product words satisfying free-product `C'(1/6)`, and assume

```text
min_{r in R} |r|_* >= 8,                                       (RC6-2)
```

where `|.|_*` is free-product syllable length.  In

```text
G = F / <<R>>                                                   (RC6-3)
```

the natural copies of `H_X,H_Z` embed, and

```text
H_X P_0 H_Z  intersect  H_X P_1 H_Z = empty.                  (RC6-4)
```

Thus a relative graphical-small-cancellation incidence construction does
have the exact algebraic protection needed by the coset-Clifford compiler,
provided all genuinely new contours have relative length at least eight.
Ordinary word length is the wrong metric for this statement.
