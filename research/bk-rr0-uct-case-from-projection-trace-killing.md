Let `E` be separable, nuclear, stably finite and of real rank zero, with every
quotient UCT, and suppose `E` is not quasidiagonal.

1. `R = R_qd(E)` is nonzero and `E/R` is quasidiagonal, by
   `stw07-separating-qd-quotients-force-quasidiagonality`.
2. `R` is separable, nuclear and of real rank zero (it is an ideal).  Every
   quotient `R/J` satisfies the UCT by two-out-of-three in
   `0 -> R/J -> E/J -> E/R -> 0`.
3. `H = ∂(K_1(E/R))` is singular in `K_0(R)`, by stable finiteness (step 2 of
   Proposition B in the artifact).  Every projection over `R` carries a
   normalised trace annihilating `H` on its finiteness ideal, by
   `stably-finite-projections-carry-annihilating-traces`.
4. `rr0-projection-traces-give-qd-killing` gives a faithful `φ : R -> D`, `D`
   quasidiagonal, with `φ_*(H) = 0`.
5. Push the extension out along `φ` into the hereditary subalgebra generated by
   `φ(R)` (quasidiagonal), passing to a separable subextension containing the
   image of `E`.
   - The new extension has quotient `E/R` and zero boundary, so it is
     quasidiagonal by Brown–Dadarlat Theorem 3.4 in
     `brown-dadarlat-qd-extensions-and-k0-hahn-banach`.
   - `E` embeds in it, because `φ` is injective on `R`.
   - This is the gluing step of Theorem 1 in
     `research/artifacts/bk-radical-projection-trace-step-2026-09-13.md`.

So `E` is quasidiagonal, a contradiction.
