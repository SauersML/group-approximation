In the left regular representation of the order-`p^3` Heisenberg group, every
nonidentity order-`p` element has `p^2` cycles of length `p`.  A unitary can
therefore identify the matrix of the central generator in one regular copy
with the matrix of a simple generator in a conjugated regular copy.  The
shared matrix is central in the first packet algebra and noncentral in the
second, proving the algebras are different despite exact regular trace and
identical shared-root spectral data.

Tensor with a four-dimensional two-bit algebra.  Swap the bits together with
the packet-conjugating unitary.  The two half projections are transported
into each other, commute, and have overlap `1/4`, while the shared root stays
literal.  Finally, conditional expectation of a second noncommuting generator
onto the first packet algebra gives the exact mixed scalar which distinguishes
this rotation and whose vanishing forces the two faithful packet algebras to
coincide.
