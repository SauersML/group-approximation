Fix the exact q=2 pair at arbitrary r,d>=3. Theorem E in the Kun-Thom
prerequisite makes G countable and residually finite. The symmetric-double
prerequisite applies to this G and this actual polynomial subgroup Gamma,
without any subgroup-separability assumption, and makes D operator-norm
MF. The final prerequisite, using Jihao Liu's analytic theorem and Andreas
Thom's conditional mechanism, makes this same D nonhyperlinear. This proves
both assertions simultaneously, not by identifying unrelated existential
witnesses. Shulman supplies an MF completion containing D faithfully; she
does not assert that the canonical trace on that completion is MF.
