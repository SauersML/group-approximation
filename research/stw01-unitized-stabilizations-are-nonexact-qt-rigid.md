Let `D` be a nonzero separable nonexact C*-algebra and put

```text
A=(D tensor K)~.                                         (US1)
```

Then `A` is separable, unital, and nonexact.  Every bounded 2-quasitrace on
`A` is a nonnegative scalar multiple of the quotient character

```text
pi:A -> A/(D tensor K) ~= C.                             (US2)
```

In particular every bounded 2-quasitrace on `A` is a trace, and `A` has a
nonzero trace.  Thus quasitrace linearity holds for a concrete parametric
class beyond exact C*-algebras and not merely because the algebras are
quasitraceless.
