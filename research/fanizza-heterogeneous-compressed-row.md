**OPEN MINIMAL WANDERING-PROMOTION ROW.**  Construct a finitely presented
Fanizza/non-CE base `B`, finite associated subgroups `A,C`, a stable letter
`t` with `tAt^(-1)=C`, base words `a,b` with `b notin A`, `a notin C`, and a
fixed packet projection `p in C[B]` such that:

1. `[b,p]=0` and canonical packet exactification gives
   `tau(p_U)>=alpha-o(1)` for one fixed `alpha>0`;
2. every canonical-trace matrix microstate satisfies

   ```text
   ||a(U)t(U)p_U-t(U)p_Ub(U)||_2=o(1);                (FHC1)
   ```

3. the exact marked/non-CE representation needed to prove the base and
   packet are noncollapsed extends to the HNN presentation.

By `hnn-compressed-row-is-unnamed-fixed-carrier`, the heterogeneous Britton
word

```text
u=a t b^(-1)t^(-1)
```

has infinite order and `(FHC1)` makes the moving projection `t p_U t^*` an
approximate fixed space of density `alpha`.  The Fejer endpoint then proves
non-hyperlinearity.  No verifier reflection, packet-return equality, polar
part, spectral threshold, boundary-cylinder identification, or Cesaro
amplifier remains.

## Attempts

- **Existing context-local Fanizza rows do not imply `(FHC1)`.**  The exact
  finite Bass--Serre countermodel in
  `context-local-fanizza-exits-do-not-control-global-leakage` keeps every
  local bridge relator exact while a private exit has a fixed commutator with
  a shared word outside its context.  The same model violates the required
  heterogeneous row unless one adds a genuinely mixed source/exit input.
- **The dihedral row is analytically exact but syntactically collapses the
  candidate.**  `two-letter-dihedral-swap-wordizes-one-gram-row` controls a
  private involution `a` against a shared involution `b` on its spin carrier
  by imposing the ordinary word `ajbj` as a defining relator.  For
  involutions this is precisely `a j b^(-1)j^(-1)`, the heterogeneous
  Britton word which the present route needs to remain infinite.  Therefore
  the established two-letter cell cannot simply be reused: it proves the
  row by declaring the marked word equal to one.
- **Deleting that relator reverses the useful inequality.**  If all other
  rows of a finite-gap BCS are small on one positive-mass Gram operator,
  `deleting-one-gap-row-has-the-wrong-orientation` gives a fixed *lower*
  bound on the deleted private/shared row.  The game gap cannot semantically
  recover `(FHC1)`; it makes its defect macroscopic.  Any separate theorem
  forcing the deleted row small would already contradict the gap and close
  the matrix-microstate problem directly.
- **Spectral intersection has exactly zero guaranteed mass.**  When `a=b`
  is a Fanizza involution, canonical exactification gives two sign spaces of
  density `1/2`.  By
  `canonical-spectral-derangement-kills-same-letter-row`, an exact transporter
  may swap those halves.  Thus the equal-letter specialization cannot be
  recovered by a majority or spectral-intersection argument.
- **What remains.**  The non-CE/matrix-only source must infer one
  private-to-shared rectangular row while the corresponding four-letter word
  is *not* a relator.  This is strictly smaller than global leakage
  localization `(NCL13)`, but it cannot be a universal tracial block
  identity or a fixed finite-packet covariance table.
- **The row is analytically minimal but not logically weaker than the
  conclusion.**  `heterogeneous-britton-row-has-regular-energy-floor` gives
  the exact canonical value

  ```text
  ||a t p-t p b||_(2,L(Gamma))^2=2 tau_Gamma(p)>0.
  ```

  Therefore `(FHC1)` fails along every canonical microstate sequence if one
  exists.  Any redundant-check or decoded-row mechanism proving `(FHC1)`
  must already be a finite-matrix-only theorem which excludes canonical
  microstates; an ordinary tracial consequence of finitely many group
  relators is impossible because the regular representation is an exact
  countermodel.  Thus the node packages the whole remaining
  non-hyperlinearity compiler into one row, but does not provide a strictly
  easier intermediate soundness statement.
