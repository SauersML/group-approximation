Immediate: if every torsion-free group of type F_∞ with infinite cohomological
dimension contains `F`, the group Problem 2.8 asks for does not exist, which
settles the problem negatively. This is one of the two admissible answer routes
into the root; the other is `zaremsky-2-08-by-construction`.
