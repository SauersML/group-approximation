**ESTABLISHED (artifact Section 6).** Let `A in M_{m x n}(Z[G])` and let `H`
be the subgroup generated by the group elements in its support. If
`det_N(G)(A) < 1`, then `H` is not sofic.

The trace of `N(G)` restricts to the trace of `N(H)`, so spectral measures and
Fuglede--Kadison determinants of `A` agree in `H` and `G`. Apply the sofic
theorem to `H`.

For the test groups this means:
- **Kun--Thom wreath `B x| G`.** Matrices over the locally finite base or
  over the residually finite `G` obey the conjecture, so a violation must mix
  lamps with group letters.
- **Leavitt unit group.** Matrices over locally finite or free subgroups obey
  it. A violating support must generate a nonsofic subgroup, such as the
  nine-leaf elementary group.
