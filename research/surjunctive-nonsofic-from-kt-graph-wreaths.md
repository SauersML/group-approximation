Take `A = Z/2Z` and any invariant graph on the Kun--Thom coset space,
for example the empty graph. The prerequisite gives surjunctivity,
nonsoficity, and finite generation of this group, which is exactly the
root claim. This records a class of witnesses extending the two existing
endpoint constructions; it is not a new resolution of the already
established existential root.
