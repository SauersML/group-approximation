This is Theorem 1.5 of Jaikin-Zapirain, Linton, and Sánchez-Peralta,
*Group pairs, coherence and Farrell--Jones Conjecture for K_0*,
arXiv:2510.23518v2.

Its hypotheses are exactly: `char(K)=0`; `A,B` locally indicable;
`w` neither conjugate into a factor nor a proper power; and
`K[A],K[B]` coherent. The proper-power extension is explicitly left
conjectural.
