**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** H. Bass, J. Milnor, J.-P. Serre, *Solution of the congruence subgroup problem for
SL_n (n ≥ 3) and Sp_2n (n ≥ 2)*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 33 (1967), 59–137, doi:10.1007/BF02684586.

Let `A` be a Dedekind ring of arithmetic type, defined by a finite set `S_∞` of primes of a global field `k`, and let
`n ≥ 3`. Write `E_n(A)` for the subgroup generated by elementary matrices, `E_n(A,q)` for the normal subgroup of
`E_n(A)` generated by the elementary matrices with entry in the ideal `q`, and
`SL_n(A,q) = ker(SL_n(A) → SL_n(A/q))`.
- **a)** `SL_n(A) = E_n(A)`, and it is finitely generated and perfect.
- **b)** If `A` is not totally imaginary, then `SL_n(A,q) = E_n(A,q)` for every ideal `q`.
- **Totally imaginary** means that `S_∞` consists of complex primes, i.e. `k` is a totally imaginary number field and `A`
  is its ring of algebraic integers.

So every ring of `S`-integers of a global function field satisfies b). Instance used in the graph: `A = F_q[t,t^{-1}]`,
`k = F_q(t)`, `S_∞ = {t = 0, t = ∞}`.
