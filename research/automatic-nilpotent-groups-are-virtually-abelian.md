  amenable-automatic-groups-are-virtually-abelian: that is the open statement for all amenable automatic groups; this is the classical nilpotent case, established by import
  soluble-automatic-groups-are-virtually-abelian: that is Thurston's open conjecture for soluble automatic groups; this is the proved nilpotent case
  polycyclic-automatic-groups-are-virtually-abelian: that is Harkins' polycyclic case, which needs Lie group lattices and higher isoperimetric functions; this is the earlier nilpotent case, proved through language growth

If `G` is nilpotent and automatic, then `G` has an abelian subgroup of finite index.

ECHLPT, *Word Processing in Groups* (1992), as stated in Rees's survey. Citation route:
