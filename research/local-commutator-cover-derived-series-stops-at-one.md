**ESTABLISHED (written proof; not Lean-verified).**

## Setting

Use the notation of `abelianized-cover-commutator-is-finitely-generated-perfect`.

- `G = F/R = <x_1,...,x_r | r_1,...,r_s>` is a finite presentation of a
  nontrivial group with full MF radical.  By
  `full-mf-radical-has-fp-cover-same-generators` this covers the instance
  used by the torsion-free route.  `G` is then perfect.
- `E = F/[R,R]` and `T = E'`.
- `S_L` is the ball of radius `L` in `F`.
- The local-commutator covers are
  ```text
  N_L = << [u,v] : u, v in R cap S_L >>,      Gamma_L = F/N_L.
  ```
  Here `N_L` increases with `L`, the union of the `N_L` is `[R,R]`, and every
  `Gamma_L` surjects onto `E`.

## Statement

There are an integer `L_1` and words `t_1, ..., t_d` in `[F,F]`, with
`d <= r + s + r^2`, such that for every `L >= L_1` the following hold.

1. **Finite generation.**  The commutator subgroup `Gamma_L'` is generated
   by the images of `t_1, ..., t_d`.
2. **Perfectness.**  `Gamma_L'` is perfect.  So the derived series stops at
   step one:
   ```text
   Gamma_L^(k) = Gamma_L'  for every k >= 1.
   ```
3. **Solvable images are abelian.**  Let `phi : Gamma_L -> H` be any
   homomorphism.  Then `phi(Gamma_L)'` is a perfect group generated by at
   most `d` elements.  If `H` is solvable, the image `phi(Gamma_L)` is
   abelian and is a quotient of `Z^r`.
4. **Nonabelian finite quotients are non-solvable.**  Let `P` be a
   nonabelian finite quotient of `Gamma_L`.  Then `P'` is a nontrivial
   perfect group generated by at most `d` elements.  In particular `P` has a
   nonabelian simple composition factor.  Together with the diameter lemma
   of `abelianized-cover-mf-radical-is-union-of-local-cover-radicals`, `P`
   also has diameter at least `(L - c + 1)/2`, where `c` is the maximal
   relator length.

## What this kills

`(DF)` is finitary.  By
`abelianized-cover-mf-radical-is-union-of-local-cover-radicals`, it holds
iff some word of `[F,F] \ [R,R]` lies in `Rad_MF(Gamma_L)` for one `L`.  Its
exact-permutation shadow is a word of that kind lying in the finite residual
of some `Gamma_L`.  Past `L_1`, the statement above removes a whole class of
test objects in both directions.

- **Solvable models are blind.**  Every homomorphism from `Gamma_L` to a
  solvable group factors through `Z^r`.  This covers:
  - finite solvable groups, `p`-groups and nilpotent groups;
  - metabelian Magnus-type and Fox-calculus models such as
    `(Z/N)^n wr Q` with `Q` abelian;
  - lamplighters over abelian bases, and polycyclic groups.

  Such a model detects no word of `[F,F]`.  It can neither exhibit a word
  that survives in `E` but dies in every finite quotient of `Gamma_L`, nor
  separate such a word.  So finite separation of `Gamma_L`, if it happens at
  all, happens only through quotients with nonabelian simple sections.
- **Finite witnesses are large and non-solvable.**  A nonabelian finite
  quotient of `Gamma_L` has a nonabelian simple section, a `d`-generated
  perfect derived subgroup, and diameter at least `(L-c+1)/2`.

## What this does not do

- It proves nothing about corona homomorphisms of `Gamma_L`.  Those have
  non-solvable targets, so `(DF)` stays open.
- It does not decide whether `E` is LEF.  That would need control of the
  finite quotients of `Gamma_L` that have nonabelian simple sections.
