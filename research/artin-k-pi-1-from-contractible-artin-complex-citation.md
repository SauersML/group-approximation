Citation import, not a reproof. Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX sources.

1. J. Huang, *Labeled four cycles and the K(π,1)-conjecture for Artin groups*,
   arXiv:2305.16847 (Invent. Math., 2024), Section 2.1, verbatim:
   "Theorem [Theorem 3.1 of godelle2012k]. Suppose S is not spherical. If the associated
   Artin complex Δ_Γ is contractible and each group in {A_ŝ}_{s∈S} satisfies the
   K(π,1)-conjecture, then A_S satisfies the K(π,1)-conjecture."
   The proof there identifies the Godelle–Paris complex `Φ(Γ, 𝒮)`, for `𝒮` the family of
   proper standard parabolic subgroups, with the barycentric subdivision of `Δ_Γ`.
2. J. Huang and P. Przytycki, *353-combinatorial curvature and the 3-dimensional K(π,1)
   conjecture*, arXiv:2509.06914, Theorem 2.x (label `thm:Kpi1`), verbatim:
   "Let Λ be non-spherical with Δ_Λ contractible. If A_{Λ'} satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture
   for all subdiagrams Λ' induced on all but one vertex of Λ, then A_Λ satisfies the
   K(π,1) conjecture." It is attributed to [huang2023labeled, Thm 2.2], "which is a
   reformulation of [godelle2012k, Thm 3.1]".

The definition of the Artin complex in the target is Definition 1.x of arXiv:2509.06914
(label `def:Artin0`), and the flagness of `Δ_Λ` is [godelle2012k, Prop 4.5].
