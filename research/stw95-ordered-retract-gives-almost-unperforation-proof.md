As in the divisibility argument, `S` absorbs `Cu(𝒵)` and is almost
unperforated.  If `(n+1)x<=ny` in `T`, additivity and order preservation give

```text
(n+1)sigma(x) <= n sigma(y)
```

in `S`.  Hence `sigma(x)<=sigma(y)`.  Applying `theta` and using
`theta sigma=id_T` gives `x<=y`.  Only the ordered-monoid structure of
`sigma` is used.
