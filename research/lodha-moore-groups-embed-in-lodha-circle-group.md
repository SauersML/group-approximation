## Setting

Work in Lodha's Cantor model (arXiv:1710.06220v2, §§2.2–2.4), with right actions on `2^N`.
- `x` and `y` are his two primitive maps.
- For `σ ∈ 2^{<N}`, the maps `x_σ` and `y_σ` act as `x` and `y` on the cone `σ2^N` and as the
  identity elsewhere. By convention `x_∅ = x` and `y_∅ = y`.
- Let `𝒢 = ⟨x_σ, y_σ : σ ∈ 2^{<N}⟩ ≤ Homeo(2^N)`. It is Thompson's `F` (generated by the `x_σ`)
  together with the percolating elements at every address, including the constant ones
  `0^k, 1^k` and `∅`.
- Let `S = ⟨x, x_1, p_0, y_10 y_110^{-1}⟩` be Lodha's circle group in this model
  (`lodha-circle-group-is-finitely-presented-and-simple`, part 3).

## Statement

1. **Lodha–Moore.** The Lodha–Moore group `G_0 = ⟨x, x_1, y_10⟩` (Lodha p. 27: "G_0 is generated
   by x, x_1, y_10") embeds in `S`. This is Lodha's Lemma 5.2 ("G_0 < S", p. 26).
2. **All percolating elements.** The map `ψ(h) = h_0 · (f h f)_1` is an injective homomorphism
   `𝒢 → S`. Here `f` is the digit flip `0 ↔ 1` on `2^N`, and `h_σ` denotes `h` transplanted to
   the cone `σ`.
3. **Consequences.**
   - Every subgroup of `𝒢`, in particular `G_0`, embeds in the finitely presented simple group
     `S`, so it satisfies the Boone--Higman conjecture.
   - The larger Lodha–Moore group `G = ⟨F, c, d_1, d_2⟩` on the real line (Lodha p. 3, from
     Lodha–Moore [23]) is isomorphic to `⟨x, x_1, y_10, y_1, y_0⟩ ≤ 𝒢`, with `c ↔ y_10`,
     `d_1 ↔ y_1` and `d_2 ↔ y_0^{-1}` (proof route, part 4). So `G` embeds in `S` as well.
4. **Permutational form (conditional).** By `lodha-circle-group-satisfies-pbh`, which is a lane
   proof not independently reviewed, every subgroup of `S` satisfies permutational
   Boone--Higman. So do `G_0`, `G` and every subgroup of `𝒢`.

## Scope and credit

- **Part 1 is in the literature.** Lodha states `G_0 < S` explicitly, so Boone--Higman for the
  Lodha--Moore group `G_0` is immediate from arXiv:1710.06220. The node records it because the
  graph's Boone--Higman subgraph did not have it. The existing `lodha-circle-group-satisfies-pbh`
  left the containment unclaimed.
- **Part 2 is new here as far as this lane knows.** Lodha's embedding `φ` of Lemma 5.2 compensates
  the `y`-exponent sum with a power of `y_110`. That needs every percolating address to be
  non-constant. The flip double instead pairs each `y_σ` with `y_{1σ̃}^{-1}`, and so reaches
  percolating elements at every address. No literature search beyond Lodha's paper was done.
- `BB(1,2) < S` is Lodha's Corollary 5.3, and it is not used here.
