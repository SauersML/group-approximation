Let G be countable torsion-free, p prime, W=C_p wr G, and
A=C_0((widehat C_p)^G\{0})⋊_rG. With K_0^inv(A)=⋂_x ker r_x,

```text
ker μ_(W,j) ≅ ker μ_(G,j),                  j=0,1,
coker μ_(W,0) ≅ coker μ_(G,0) ⊕ K_0^inv(A),
coker μ_(W,1) ≅ coker μ_(G,1) ⊕ K_1(A).
```

For a positive ordinary host the wreath question is exactly whether
both extra groups vanish. A nonzero extra summand would refute ordinary
surjectivity without needing to decide the host. Neither a nonzero
class nor general vanishing is established here.
