title: Amenability of finitely generated groups is invariant under quasi-isometry
---

**ESTABLISHED** through `amenability-is-a-quasi-isometry-invariant-proof`.

Let `G` and `H` be finitely generated groups with word metrics. If `G` and `H` are
quasi-isometric and `H` is amenable, then `G` is amenable.

The proof transfers thick-boundary Følner sets through a quasi-isometry by taking
preimages; the only input is Følner's criterion.

**Use in the graph.** Combined with `thompson-t-is-not-amenable`, it gives the two
amenability routes for Zaremsky Problem 2.4(c): an amenable F is not
quasi-isometric to T (`thompson-f-not-qi-to-t-if-f-is-amenable`), and an F
quasi-isometric to T is not amenable (`thompson-f-nonamenable-if-qi-to-thompson-t`).
