**ESTABLISHED.**  Put

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,                 Q=1-ST
```

and work in the displayed four-coordinate block of `EL_5(J)`.  Define

```text
w=x_13(Q),        v=x_13(SQ),        d=x_13(S^2T),
X=x_12(Q),        u=x_21(T),
C=x_14(Q),        l=x_41(T),

P=<w,v,d,X,u,C,l>.                                        (JFD1)
```

Then `P` is finite and contains the nontrivial head mark `w`.  More
precisely, put

```text
A_13=span_F2{Q,SQ,S^2T},
A_23=A_43=span_F2{Q,ST},
c_T=I+QT E_11=[X,u]=[C,l],

N=<x_13(A_13),x_23(A_23),x_43(A_43),c_T>.                (JFD2)
```

The three root subgroups in `(JFD2)` commute, their product has elementary
abelian rank at most seven, and `c_T` is an involution normalizing it.  Hence

```text
|N| <= 2^8.                                               (JFD3)
```

The subgroup `N` is normal in `P`.  Modulo `N`, the four remaining generators
`X,u,C,l` are commuting involutions, so

```text
P/N is a quotient of C_2^4,             |P| <= 2^12.      (JFD4)
```

Thus the entire literal word package defining the raw source signs
`w,v,d`, both balanced target pairs and their common center has an exact
faithful finite-dimensional model with `w` visible.  Neither the center fold
nor its two named leakage rows can produce an MF contradiction from these
uncompressed group relations alone.  A load-bearing continuation must use
the representation-dependent compression onto the raw range and an
additional occurrence that authenticates how the T-roots act there; merely
closing the displayed conjugation and commutator table stays inside this
finite marked envelope.
