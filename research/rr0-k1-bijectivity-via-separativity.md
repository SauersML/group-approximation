Let `A` be a unital C\*-algebra of real rank zero.  By
`rr0-c-star-algebras-are-separative`, `A` is separative.  By part 1 of
`agor-separative-rr0-algebras-are-k1-bijective` (AGOR Theorem 3.1), the natural
map `U(A)/U(A)° -> K_1(A)` is an isomorphism.

The converse direction is not a route: K1-bijectivity is only known to reduce
separativity to prime algebras
(`rr0-separativity-from-prime-case-and-k1-bijectivity`).
