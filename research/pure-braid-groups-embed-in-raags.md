**OPEN.** For every `m`, `PB_m` is isomorphic to a subgroup of `A_Γ` for some finite
graph `Γ`. `m ≤ 3` holds, since `PB_3 ≅ F_2 × Z`.

**Relation to a known open problem.** An embedding in a right-angled Artin group
puts `PB_m` in a special cube complex group. In general that complex is noncompact
and the subgroup is not quasiconvex, so the statement is weaker than virtual
(compact) specialness of braid groups. It is not claimed to be equivalent. Agol's
2014 ICM question and Wu--Ye, arXiv:2608.13978 (abstract only), record virtual
specialness of braid groups as open. The standard necessary conditions for
subgroups of right-angled Artin groups all hold for `PB_m`:
- bi-orderability and residual torsion-free nilpotence (Falk--Randell; not re-read);
- the RFRS property (Wu--Ye 2026, abstract).

So no obstruction is known. No literature search beyond two arXiv abstract
queries was made on whether the statement itself is known or refuted.
