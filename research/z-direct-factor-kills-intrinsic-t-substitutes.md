**ESTABLISHED (written proof; not Lean-verified), except item (E), which
rests on a cited external theorem and is labelled as such.**

Use the manuscript defect

```text
D_G(L) = << [ucu^-1, l] : uLu^-1 <= L, c in C_G(L), l in L >>_G
```

of `non_mf_groups_exist.tex` (eq. `intrinsic-defect`).  For a property
`P` of finitely generated groups consider the statement

```text
(CC_P)  For every countable G, every finitely generated L <= G with P,
        and every operator-norm asymptotic representation (V_n) of G:
        (i)  ||V_n(d)-1||_2 -> 0 for every d in D_G(L), and
        (ii) every normal subgroup of G inside D_G(L) that has (T) dies
             in every MF image of G.
```

For `P=(T)` this is Corollary `cor:defect-hs` together with
Theorem `thm:compression-criterion` of the manuscript.

**Theorem.** Let `K` be any countable MF group (finitely generated if a
finitely generated `L` is wanted).  Then `(CC_P)` is false for every `P`
with `P(Z x K)`.  Explicitly, with `G_0=<a,t,c>` the MF group of
`finite-order-central-compression-defect-survives-mf`, put

```text
G = G_0 x K,   L = <a> x K,   u=(t,1),   c'=(c,1),   l=(a,1).
```

Then `uLu^-1=<a^2> x K < L`, `c' in C_G(L)`,
`d'=[uc'u^-1,l]=(d,1)`, and in the tensor operator-norm model
`V_n(g,k)=V^0_n(g) (x) W_n(k)` one has `V_n(d') -> -I` in operator norm.
So `||V_n(d')-1||_op -> 2` and `||V_n(d')-1||_2 -> 2`, which contradicts
(i).  The central subgroup `{1,d'}` is normal in `G`, finite, hence
Kazhdan, lies in `D_G(L)`, and embeds in the MF group `G`, which contradicts (ii).

**Killed substitutes.** Choose `K`:

* (A) `K=F_2`.  `L=Z x F_2` is finitely presented, nonamenable,
  residually finite, linear, exact, and Haagerup.  This kills nonamenability,
  residual finiteness, linearity, exactness, and any C*-exactness or
  approximation property of `L`.
* (B) `K=SL_3(Z)`.  `L=Z x SL_3(Z)` is not Haagerup.  It contains the infinite
  normal Kazhdan subgroup `1 x SL_3(Z)`, so the pair
  `(L, 1 x SL_3(Z))` has relative (T).  This kills "contains an
  infinite normal Kazhdan subgroup", "relative (T) for an infinite normal
  subgroup", and "not a-T-menable".
* (C) For every `K`: the compressor `u` acts as the identity on the factor
  `K`.  So any substitute expressed as a property of a direct factor of `L`
  that is complementary to the compressed direction is killed, whatever
  that factor is.
* (E) *(cited, not verified in-graph.)* `L(Z x K)=L(Z) (x) L(K)` contains
  the diffuse abelian subalgebra `N=L(Z) (x) 1`.  It is central, hence
  regular.  Hayes, *1-bounded entropy and regularity problems in von
  Neumann algebras* (IMRN 2018), shows that `h(N:M)` does not increase when
  `N` is replaced by the algebra generated by its normalizer, and that
  `h(N:M)<=h(N)=0` for diffuse hyperfinite `N`.  Hence `h(L(L))<=0`.  This kills
  1-bounded entropy zero (strong 1-boundedness type smallness obtained from
  a regular diffuse hyperfinite subalgebra) and property Gamma of `L(L)`
  coming from the central `L(Z)`.

**Surviving invariant.** Any subgroup-intrinsic replacement of `(T)` in
`(CC_P)` must be **false for every group `Z x K`**, that is, it must fail as
soon as `L` has an infinite cyclic direct factor.  Property `(T)` has this
feature, since `Z x K` surjects onto `Z`.  So do non-inner-amenability,
the ICC property, and trivial amenable radical, and this theorem does not
touch them.  Properties that pass to overgroups `L <= L'` with `L'` having
a direct factor `Z` cannot help, and the obstruction does not care which
group sits in the uncompressed factor.

## Impact on the Property-(T)-free endpoint

For `binary-leavitt-atomic-morita-return` and
`property-t-free-manuscript-results`, a Property-`(T)`-free transport must
use one of the following:

1. a property of the compressed subgroup that fails for all `Z x K`, such as
   ICC, no amenable radical, or non-inner-amenability.  None of these is
   known to yield `(MSG)` or a same-class inequality `U^*PU<=P`;
2. data of the ambient group or of the model, such as simplicity of
   `EL_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))`, which rules out the tensor splitting used here,
   or Leavitt coefficient structure;
3. a condition on the pair `(u,L)` that the cyclic compression `t<a>t^-1=<a^2>`
   violates.

In particular, transplanting 1-bounded entropy or regularity properties of
`L(L)` in place of `(T)` dies at the subgroup-intrinsic step.  This does not
prove `(BLAMR)`, which stays OPEN.
