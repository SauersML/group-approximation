title: Two opposite return Fourier modes are a relative tangent with full base hull

Fix an odd `m>=3` and a simple fourth-power orbit of length `m`; for example
take a primitive orbit in the roots of unity of order `4^m-1`.  On its
packet choose an eigenbasis `e_0,...,e_(m-1)` for the base unitary `S` so
that

```text
R e_j=e_(j-1),                 S e_j=lambda^(4^j)e_j.  (FRT1)
```

The eigenvalues in `(FRT1)` are distinct.  Let the inversion reflection be

```text
X e_j=e_(-j).                                             (FRT2)
```

After reversing the cycle orientation if necessary, `(FRT1)--(FRT2)` give
the exact BS relation and `XRX=R^(-1)`.

Let

```text
f_k=m^(-1/2) sum_j omega^(-kj)e_j,
P_k=|f_k><f_k|,              omega=exp(2 pi i/m).       (FRT3)
```

Then `R f_k=omega^(-k) f_k` and `X f_k=f_(-k)`.  For any
`k not=0` put

```text
h=i(P_k-P_(-k)).                                         (FRT4)
```

This `h` is anti-Hermitian, commutes with `R`, and satisfies `XhX=-h`.
It is therefore an exact tangent to `(RTF3)`.  Since

```text
|h|=P_k+P_(-k),                                         (FRT5)
```

its nonzero spectral support has rank two.

Every coefficient of `f_k` in the `S`-eigenbasis is nonzero.  Because the
`m` eigenvalues of `S` are distinct, Lagrange interpolation gives every
coordinate projection `|e_j><e_j|` as a polynomial in `S`.  Hence the
`S`-cyclic subspace generated by the single vector `f_k` already contains
all `e_j` and is the whole packet.  A fortiori the `(R,S)`-invariant hull of
`P_k+P_(-k)` has dimension `m`.

Taking `m -> infinity` proves that a rank-two correction cut can have
arbitrarily large, indeed full, invariant-hull amplification.  The same
construction may be repeated with arbitrary multiplicity and embedded as a
vanishing-density summand of a regular microstate, so regular trace does not
remove the local firewall.
