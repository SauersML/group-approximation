## Statement

1. **Sufficiency for all finitely generated self-similar groups.**
   - **Setting.** Let `G ≤ Aut(X^*)` be generated by a finite set `S` whose word-level sections `s|_x` are words in
     `S^{±1}` (a finite wreath recursion; every finitely generated self-similar group has one). Let `F` be free on
     `S` and `N = ker(F -> G)`.
   - **Conclusion.** If `N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_lift` for a finite `R ⊆ N`, then `V_d(G)` is finitely presented. The
     presentation is the one of the finite-state theorem, with `(S)` read as
     `L(s) = h_s · ∏_x L_{0x}(s|_x)` for the words `s|_x`.
   - **No section closure needed.** `R` need not be section-closed. That hypothesis in the appended section of
     `decidable-fg-self-similar-groups-embed-in-fp-self-similar` (354df8d20d) was stronger than necessary.
2. **Tests on infinite-state groups.**
   - (a) **`Z` via the virtual endomorphism `φ(2k) = 3k`.** This is a faithful self-similar action on the binary
     tree. The sections of `t^n` have exponents about `3n/2`, so there are infinitely many states. Since `N = 1`,
     `V_2(Z, φ)` is finitely presented. This agrees with Scott's FP1.
   - (b) **A naive infinite-state lamplighter is not faithful.** Try `L = Z/2 ≀ Z` with the virtual endomorphism
     `φ(t^2) = t^3`, `φ(e_(2j)) = e_(3j)`, `φ(e_(2j+1)) = e_(3j+1)` on `H = ⟨t^2, lamps⟩`.
     - **Why it fails.** The lamp subgroup is normal in `L`, lies in `H`, and is `φ`-invariant. So it lies in the
       `φ`-core and acts trivially: the action factors through `Z`.
     - **What a faithful version needs.** Lamps must lie outside the first-level stabilizer, as in the finite-state
       lamplighter automaton `a = σ(a,b)`, `b = (a,b)` (`rover-nekrashevych-group-of-lamplighter-automaton-is-fp`),
       where `N = K_∞`.
     - **The open test.** A faithful infinite-state action of a non-finitely-presented group, together with a
       verdict on finite lift generation, is still needed.

## Proof of 1

The steps of `rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation-proof` used for sufficiency are §§0, 1a, 2 and 3.
None of them uses that `S` is closed under sections.
- **The group `V̂`.** It is still finitely presented. Each `(S)` relation is one relation, and `L_{0x}(W)` for a word
  `W` is `A_{0x} L(W) A_{0x}^(-1)`.
- **Surjectivity (Nekrashevych's Lemma 5.12)** uses only that `S` generates `G`.
- **(2a) Word splitting.** The induction over letters, inverse letters and products works with word-valued
  sections.
- **(2b)** uses no finiteness.
- **(2c).** An element of `G` that acts as an element of `V_d` has all sections trivial at some level. That uses only
  faithfulness.
- **§3** is `N ⊆ M_𝔑` by (2b), then (2c).
- **Where finite-state enters.** Only in the necessity half (§4), through the finite section closure of step (1c).
∎

## Lesson for general BH

**Finite lift generation, not finite state, is the finite-presentation currency of Röver–Nekrashevych hosts.** So
infinite-state hosts, which hard word problems require, are not excluded by the finite-presentation step.
- **The coupling to watch.** It sits between state growth and relator growth under sections. In linear or affine
  families (virtual endomorphisms of abelian-by-cyclic groups) the two are driven by the same map, so
  non-contraction of relators and infinitely many states tend to come together. This is a heuristic, not a theorem.
- **The same tension as before.** It mirrors the one the seed branch hit between domain types and hardness.
- **The design principle it suggests.** Let states grow along directions that carry no relators, such as free
  factors or free generators, and keep every relator inside a contracting part.
- **The next test.** A faithful self-similar `A * B`, or an HNN extension, with `A` contracting (carrying all
  relators) and `B` free with infinite-state sections.
