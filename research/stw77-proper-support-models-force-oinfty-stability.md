Let `C subset B` be a separable unital C*-subalgebra.  Suppose that for every
finite `F subset C` and `epsilon>0` there is a unital finite-dimensional
subalgebra `D subset B` such that

1. every element of `F` is within `epsilon` of `D`;
2. one, equivalently every, minimal projection in each simple summand of `D`
   is properly infinite in `B`.

Then the inclusion `C->B` is O-infinity-stable.  Consequently STW LXXVII is
positive for every nuclear-dimension-zero map whose one-sided local
finite-dimensional models can be chosen with properly infinite minimal
supports.
