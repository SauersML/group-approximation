The target fixes a residually finite base G and the permutation action on
X=G/Gamma. Its lamp group is A=C_2, which is finite and hence residually
finite. Therefore rf-lamp-permutational-wreaths-satisfy-stable-finiteness
applies to W=A wr_X G for every field k and every matrix size.

No normality, co-amenability, or approximation hypothesis on Gamma is
needed for this conclusion. The nonsoficity theorem is not an input to the
finiteness proof; it identifies why this application is of interest.
The route establishes the all-field fixed-family target only. It supplies
no implication to full nonlinear surjunctivity of W or to finiteness for
arbitrary groups.
