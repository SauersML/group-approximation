title: Hierarchically hyperbolic groups act properly and cocompactly on coarsely injective spaces
---

Every hierarchically hyperbolic group, including the mapping class group of a surface,
acts properly and cocompactly by isometries on a coarsely injective metric space: the
hierarchically hyperbolic space itself, with a new automorphism-invariant metric
quasi-isometric to the old one. Every Helly group is also coarsely injective.
