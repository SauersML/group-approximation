We first compute all information supplied by unit-zero annihilation.  First
fix `ell=ell^* in L` and two diagonal coordinates, possibly in different
matrix summands of `B`.  The self-adjoint map which sends

```text
e_ii |-> rho(ell),       e_jj |-> -rho(ell)                (1)
```

and every other matrix unit to zero has value zero at the unit.  Equation
`(DCA1)` applied to `(1)` gives `(DCA3)` for self-adjoint `ell`; applying it
to the real and imaginary parts gives the
identity for every `ell in L`.

Similarly, for `i!=j`, use the two self-adjoint unit-zero maps supported on
`e_ij,e_ji`, with values respectively

```text
rho(ell),rho(ell)^*       and       i rho(ell),-i rho(ell)^*.
```

Their annihilation kills the real and imaginary parts of the off-diagonal
Choi coefficient on `rho(ell)`.  This proves the stated off-diagonal
identity.  Taking `ell=x^*x` gives `(DCA4)`.  Positivity of `omega` makes each
`lambda_i` positive, but the calculation contains no equation comparing the
common nonnegative number in `(DCA4)` with zero.

We now show that this missing inequality cannot be recovered from the rest
of the packet formalism.  The RFD triangle algebra has a finite-dimensional
irreducible representation `rho:A->M_d`, `d>=2`, which does not factor through
`C^*(Lambda)`.  One way to choose it is to use the irreducible full-ideal
packets of `native-relator-soft-modes-persist-in-simple-ideal-blocks`, taking
the finite almost-invariance test large enough to exclude the finitely many
one-dimensional characters of the virtually free group `G_0`.  Since the
target is simple and `rho(I)!=0`,

```text
rho(I)=M_d.                                                 (2)
```

The algebraic ideal generated by the finitely many native kernel relators is
dense in `I`, so its image is a dense linear subspace of the finite-dimensional
space `M_d`, hence is all of `M_d`.  Choose `z` in that algebraic ideal with
`rho(z)=I_d`, replace it by

```text
y=(z+z^*)/2,
```

and obtain `y=y^*`, `rho(y)=I_d`.  Both `y` and `y^2=y^*y` are finite algebraic
native-relator coefficients.  For

```text
L=span_C{y,y^2}                                             (3)
```

we have `rho(L)=C I_d`.  More generally every finite product enlargement
formed from this `y` still has scalar image under `rho`, so adding further
square terms does not alter the counterexample below.

Choose a nontrivial projection `p` in one finite face algebra and orthonormal
vectors `xi,eta in C^d`.  Put `P_xi=|xi><xi|`, `P_eta=|eta><eta|`, and define
a unital self-adjoint linear map `T:E->M_d` by prescribing

```text
T(1)=I_d,
T(p)=2P_xi-P_eta+(1/2)(I_d-P_xi-P_eta),                    (4)
```

then extending self-adjoint linearly across a real complement of
`span_R{1,p}`.

This `T` is the packet image of a legitimate quotient section.  Indeed start
with any unital self-adjoint linear section `t_0:E->A`.  Because `(2)` makes
`rho:I->M_d` surjective, the finite-dimensional unit-zero self-adjoint map
`T-rho o t_0` has a unit-zero self-adjoint linear lift `d:E->I`.  Then

```text
t=t_0+d,       q t=id_E,       rho o t=T.                  (5)
```

Any unit-zero correction `v:E->L` has

```text
rho(v(p))=c I_d                                               (6)
```

for a real scalar `c`.  If `T+rho o v` were ucp, its value at `p` would be an
effect.  On the `xi` line this requires

```text
0<=2+c<=1,       hence -2<=c<=-1,
```

whereas on the `eta` line it requires

```text
0<=-1+c<=1,      hence 1<=c<=2.                            (7)
```

This contradiction proves `(DCA6)`.

Finally define

```text
Omega(phi)=<phi(p)xi,xi>+<phi(1-p)eta,eta>.                (8)
```

It is nonnegative on every cp map because `p,1-p>=0`.  Equations `(6)` and
`v(1)=0` give

```text
Omega(rho o v)=c-c=0.                                     (9)
```

Formula `(4)` gives `Omega(T)=2+2=4`.  For every ucp `theta`, both terms in
`(8)` are at most one.  Conversely, on `span{1,p}` choose an effect which is
one on `xi` and zero on `eta`; the resulting ucp map extends to `E` by
Arveson's extension theorem.  Thus the upper bound two is attained and the
affine gap is exactly two.  The two
diagonal densities in `(8)` are `P_xi,P_eta`, while `rho(y)=I_d`; this proves
`(DCA8)` and completes the native-packet counterexample.
