Let `B` be Toms's algebra. The second prerequisite makes it unital, simple,
separable, nuclear, non-elementary and stably finite. Nuclearity gives
exactness, so Haagerup's theorem identifies `QT(B)` with `T(B)`.

The first prerequisite supplies a strictly positive continuous affine
`f: T(B) -> (0, infinity)` that is not the rank of any positive element of
`B tensor K`. A continuous affine function is lower semicontinuous. So `f` is
an unrealized lower semicontinuous affine map `QT(B) -> (0, infinity]`, which
is the target. Square.
