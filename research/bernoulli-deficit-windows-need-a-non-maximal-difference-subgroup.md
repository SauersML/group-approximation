Let `G` be a countable group, `|A| = q >= 2`, and `(k, E, F, psi)` a configuration as in
`bernoulli-rokhlin-deficit-has-a-finitary-witness`, with value `Phi`. Put `H = <E E^-1>`.

**Theorem.** If `H` is finite or Rokhlin-maximal at `q`, then `Phi >= log q`.

So every deficit witness has `<E E^-1>` infinite and not Rokhlin-maximal, hence nonsofic, and this
does not depend on `F`.

**Proof sketch** (Section 1 of the artifact):
- codewords whose reads are not connected to the special site through overlapping reads are
  independent of it and can be dropped;
- the remaining codewords `C` lie in one left coset `e_0^-1 H`, because overlapping reads differ by
  an element of `E E^-1`, so their reads lie in `e_0^-1 H E`;
- conjugation by `e_0` carries the configuration, with its coincidences and special site, into `H`
  with window `E e_0^-1 ⊂ H`, without changing `Phi`;
- maximality of `H` then gives `Phi >= log q`, and for finite `H` a direct count over `H` does.

**Corollaries.**
- No configuration with `|E| <= 2` is a witness over any group, since `<E E^-1>` is cyclic.
- No window contained in a coset of a Rokhlin-maximal subgroup is a witness.
- Every witness has `|E| >= 3`, and when `|E| = 3` its difference subgroup is two-generated and not
  Rokhlin-maximal.
