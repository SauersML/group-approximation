For `g>=3`, the Siegel Levi of `Sp_(2g)(Z)` contains `SL_g(Z)`. The full
group C-star algebra of that subgroup fails LLP by Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma.
Since LLP passes from a full group C-star algebra to the full group C-star
algebra of a subgroup, `C^*(Sp_(2g)(Z))` cannot have LLP. Thus any positive
case among `g>=2` is `g=2`.
