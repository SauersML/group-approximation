**ESTABLISHED LOCALLY FINITE CORE FENCE.**  In the binary Leavitt algebra

```text
R=L_(F_2)(1,2)
```

put, for binary words `u,v` of the same length `n`,

```text
e_(u,v)=s_u t_v.                                      (DLC1)
```

Prefix cancellation and the iterated range sum give

```text
e_(u,v)e_(a,b)=delta_(v,a)e_(u,b),
sum_(|u|=n)e_(u,u)=1.                                 (DLC2)
```

The `4^n` elements in `(DLC1)` are linearly independent: multiplying a
putative linear relation on the left by `t_a` and on the right by `s_b`
recovers its `(a,b)` coefficient.  Consequently

```text
B_n=span_(F_2){e_(u,v):|u|=|v|=n}
    ~=M_(2^n)(F_2).                                   (DLC3)
```

If `m<n`, then

```text
e_(u,v)=sum_(|w|=n-m)e_(uw,vw),                       (DLC4)
```

so `B_m subset B_n`.  Hence the gauge-degree-zero core

```text
R_0=union_(n>=0) B_n                                  (DLC5)
```

is locally finite: every finite coefficient inventory in `R_0` lies in
one finite matrix ring `B_n`.

This strictly crosses the earlier Boolean boundary.  At depth one,

```text
q_0=s_0t_0,  q_1=s_1t_1,  p=s_0t_1,  r=s_1t_0        (DLC6)
```

are the four matrix units of `B_1~=M_2(F_2)`.  Thus the aggregate identity

```text
pr+rp=q_0+q_1=1                                       (DLC7)
```

and every other ordinary identity formed from any fixed finite balanced
prefix window remain inside a finite coefficient ring.

More precisely, let `T` be any collection of genuine ordinary Steinberg
word identities whose root coefficients lie in one finite subset of
`R_0`, at arbitrary positive or opposite root positions and with arbitrary
constant Weyl words.  Choose `n` containing all those coefficients.  The
canonical map

```text
St_20(R) -> E_20(R)                                   (DLC8)
```

sends the whole inventory into the finite group `E_20(B_n)`, so every
identity in `T` has one simultaneous exact finite model.  The marked root

```text
z=x_13(q_1)                                           (DLC9)
```

survives because `q_1` is a nonzero diagonal matrix unit (or, at depth
`n`, the nonzero sum of the diagonal units indexed by words beginning in
`1`).  The left regular representation of `E_20(B_n)` therefore gives a
finite-dimensional exact unitary model with a nonzero `(-1)` spectral
projection for `z`.

It follows from the Atomic Leavitt Gap that no finite ordinary-word menu
confined to the degree-zero core can imply
`binary-leavitt-atomic-morita-return` with a modulus tending to zero.  In
particular, an "equivalent non-Boolean prefix matrix unit" is not enough
when it is balanced: `s_0t_1`, `s_1t_0`, arbitrary same-depth cylinder
matrix units, their opposite-root Whiteheads, and all their mixed
commutators still have finite exact marked models.

The live occurrence boundary is therefore sharper.  A successful bounded
prototype must expose a coefficient of nonzero Leavitt gauge degree, such
as an uncancelled `s_i` or `t_i` or an unequal-length prefix `s_u t_v`, and
must couple that occurrence to the prescribed marked atom.  Alternatively,
an adaptive construction must use genuinely unbounded depth rather than a
fixed finite degree-zero window.  This claim does not fence such
occurrences and does not refute global Atomic Morita Return.

No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, stability theorem, trace hypothesis,
literature theorem, local computation, or Lean compilation is used.
