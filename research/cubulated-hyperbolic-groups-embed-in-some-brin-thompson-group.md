**ESTABLISHED** (`cubulated-hyperbolic-in-brin-thompson-proof`).

If `G` is word-hyperbolic and acts properly and cocompactly on a CAT(0) cube
complex, then `G` embeds in nV for some n.

Examples: finite-volume hyperbolic 3-manifold groups, finite `C'(1/6)`
small-cancellation groups, hyperbolic Coxeter groups, and random groups at
density below 1/6 in Gromov's density model. For the last class the cubulation
comes from Ollivier–Wise. This lane has not re-checked that source, and the
proof route does not use it.

This is the known part of Zaremsky Problem 2.19(a)
(`zaremsky-2-19a-every-hyperbolic-group-in-some-nv-resolved`). It says nothing
about hyperbolic groups with property (T), which virtually embed in no
right-angled Artin group.
