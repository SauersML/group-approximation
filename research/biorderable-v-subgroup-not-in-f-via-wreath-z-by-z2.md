`Z ≀ Z^2` is bi-orderable (lexicographic order, with the base ordered by the sign at the
largest differing index for a translation-invariant order on `Z^2`; artifact part 2) and
metabelian, so it has no non-abelian free subgroup. The two required claims say that it
embeds in `V` and not in `F`. Together they give the witness.
