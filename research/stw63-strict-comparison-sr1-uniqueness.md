Let `B` be a unital C-star algebra of stable rank one.  Assume that
the normalized 2-quasitrace space `QT_1(B)` is nonempty and that `B` has
strict comparison by these quasitraces: whenever positive elements `x,y` in
`B tensor K` satisfy

```text
d_tau(x)<d_tau(y)       for every tau in QT_1(B),
```

then `x` is Cuntz subequivalent to `y`.  If `B` admits a unital embedding of
`Z`, then all unital embeddings `Z->B` are approximately unitarily
equivalent.

Neither simplicity nor uniqueness of the 2-quasitrace is assumed.  The
explicit nonemptiness hypothesis avoids treating strict comparison as a
vacuous condition in a target with no normalized quasitraces.
