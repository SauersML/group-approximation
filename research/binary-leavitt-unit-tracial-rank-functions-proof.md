**Part 1.**
- `φ = τ ∘ π` restricted to `G` is a normalized positive-definite class function. By
  `binary-leavitt-full-character-simplex`, `φ = α 1 + (1-α) δ_1`.
- Range projections of `π(A)` lie in `M_m(N)`, `N = π(C[G])''`, and `τ` is faithful on `N`. So
  `(N, τ)` is the GNS algebra of `φ`, and `rk_π` depends only on `φ`.
- For `0 < α < 1`, the GNS representation of `φ` is the cyclic subrepresentation of
  `triv ⊕ λ` on `C ⊕ ℓ²G` generated by `(√α, √(1-α) δ_1)`.
  - `G` is infinite, so `ℓ²G` has no invariant vector, and `triv` and `λ` are disjoint.
  - Hence the von Neumann algebra they generate is `C ⊕ L(G)`, the vector is cyclic, and the
    trace is `α τ_C ⊕ (1-α) τ_G`.
- Range projections in a direct sum are computed componentwise. This gives
  `rk_π = α rk_ε + (1-α) rk_vN`. The cases `α in {0,1}` are the two summands alone.

**Part 2.**
- The `σ_n` are unitaries, asymptotically multiplicative in `||.||_2`, with `||X||_op <= 1`.
  So words in the `σ_n(g)` are `||.||_2`-close to `σ_n` of the product, and
  `σ_n(g^(-1)) - σ_n(g)^*` tends to `0`. Using `|tr X - tr Y| <= ||X - Y||_2`, the limit `φ` is
  a normalized positive-definite class function, so Part 1's formula applies to it.
- For a matrix `A`, `σ_n(A)^* σ_n(A)` and `σ_n(A^* A)` are `||.||_2`-close and uniformly
  bounded by `||A||_1²`. Their moments under `Tr ⊗ tr` converge to `(Tr ⊗ φ)((A^*A)^j)`.
- So the spectral measures `μ_n` converge weakly to the spectral measure `μ` of `A^*A` in
  `(C ⊕ L(G), α ⊕ (1-α))`, which is `α μ_(ε(A^*A)) + (1-α) μ_(λ(A^*A))`.
- Portmanteau: `lim_ω μ_n((s², ∞)) = μ((s², ∞))` whenever `μ({s²}) = 0`, which excludes
  countably many `s`. Letting `s ↓ 0` gives `μ((0, ∞)) = α rk_ε(A) + (1-α) rk_vN(A)`.
- The closed set `{0}` gives `limsup_ω μ_n({0}) <= μ({0})`, the kernel bound.
