**Inputs read at source** (arXiv:0904.2423v4, PDF pp. 1--7):
- §1.3: `Γ^{Z[t]}` "is a union of an ascending chain of extensions of centralizers",
  each step `Γ_{i+1} = <Γ_i, t_i | [C_{Γ_i}(u_i), t_i] = 1>`, and "every exponential
  extension `H` of `G` is also a subgroup of an iterated extension of centralizers".
- Theorem C: for `Γ ∈ 𝒢`, a finitely generated `Γ`-group is `Γ`-universally equivalent
  to `Γ` iff it embeds in `Γ^{Z[t]}`.
- Theorem B and the paragraph after it (quoting [9], [1], [4], and Groves's theorem
  that groups in `𝒢` are equationally Noetherian, §1.4): for finitely generated
  `Γ`-groups, universal equivalence with constants, `Γ`-discrimination, and being
  a coordinate group of an irreducible algebraic set over `Γ` are equivalent.

**Step 1.** A finitely generated subgroup `H` of `Γ^{Z[t]}` lies in a finite stage
`Γ_k` of the ascending chain (finitely many generators, each in some stage).

**Step 2.** Each `Γ_i` is finitely generated and in `B_A`, by induction. `Γ_0 = Γ`.
The presentation `<Γ_i, t_i | [C_{Γ_i}(u_i), t_i] = 1>` is the amalgam
`Γ_i *_C (C × <t_i>)`, `C = C_{Γ_i}(u_i)` (same generators and relations). So
`pbh-class-closed-under-centralizer-amalgams` with `M = Γ_i`, `F = {u_i}`, `P = Z`
gives `Γ_{i+1} ∈ B_A`, and it is generated by `Γ_i` and `t_i`.

**Step 3.** `H ≤ Γ_k ∈ B_A`, and `B_A` is closed under subgroups. Items 1--3 of the
claim are finitely generated subgroups of `Γ^{Z[t]}` by the quoted theorems.

**Hyperbolic case.** A non-abelian torsion-free hyperbolic group is finitely
generated, in `𝒢` (hyperbolic relative to the empty family; Osin's convention, not
re-read), and in `B_A` by `hyperbolic-groups-lie-in-the-permutational-class`.

**Scope.** "Limit group over `Γ`" is used in the with-constants sense (the group
contains `Γ`). Limit groups over `Γ` in Sela's sense without constants are covered
whenever they embed in a finitely generated `Γ`-group discriminated by `Γ`; that
embedding is not proved here.
