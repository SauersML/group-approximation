## Statement

Let `n >= 2` and `C = C_n = {0, ..., n-1}^N`, and let `V_(n,1)` be the Higman--Thompson group of
prefix replacements of `C`. Let `A` be a finite abelian group and `M_A` the group of locally
constant maps `C -> A` under pointwise addition, with `V_(n,1)` acting by `v·f = f ∘ v^(-1)`.
Then `M_A ⋊ V_(n,1)` is of type `F_∞`; in particular it is finitely presented.

## Use

For `A = (F_q, +)` the group `M_A` is the Cantor module `C(C_n, F_q)` of the Leavitt algebra
`L_(F_q)(1,n)`, where `(s_i f)(a w) = δ_(a,i) f(w)` and `(t_i f)(w) = f(i w)`. The copy
`Σ s_(a_i) t_(b_i)` of `V_(n,1)` in the unit group acts on it by precomposition as above. So
this is the finitely presented sub-extension that `amalgamated-module-extensions-are-fp` needs
for any unit group of `L_(F_q)(1,n)` generated by `V_(n,1)` and finitely many covered units.
See `prime-field-leavitt-pbh-via-covered-module-amalgam`.

**Remark.** `V_(n,1)` has only finitely many orbits on `M_A`: split cones until corresponding
level sets of two functions have equally many cones, then match cones. For `n >= 3` a split adds
`n - 1` cones, so the orbit also records the cone counts modulo `n - 1`. Step 6 of
`prime-field-leavitt-pbh-via-covered-module-amalgam` writes this out for `n = 2`.
