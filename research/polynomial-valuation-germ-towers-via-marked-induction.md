and every finite marked set. The second gives each successor case from
the entire preceding family. Ordinary induction supplies every finite
degree, with setwise stabilizers obtained by finite extension. Both
substantive prerequisites are OPEN; this route certifies no new theorem
without them. The explicit classical inputs are SZ, BHM, and the standard
