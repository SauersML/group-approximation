In the degree-four `(1,3,3,3)` topology with sign profile
`(-,-,-,+,+,+,+,+,+,+)`, making either adjacent pair of residuals trivial
forces all four unary-corner projections to agree.  Every free-copy
retraction of the third residual is then trivial.  Thus the third residual
cannot be a one-copy nonidentity target.
