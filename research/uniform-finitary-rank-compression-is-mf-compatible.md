Let `A=GL_fin(Z,F_2)` act on the direct-sum vector space with basis
`(e_i)_(i in Z)`, let `t e_i=e_(i+1)`, and put

```text
G=A semidirect <t>,
H=GL_fin({0,1,2,...},F_2),
c=I+E_(-2,-1),  b=I+E_(0,1),  a=I+E_(-1,1).
```

Then all the following hold.

1. `G` is three-generated and LEF, and it has an injective norm-matrix-corona
   representation retaining the involution `a`.
2. `A` is a simple locally finite normal subgroup of `G`.
3. `t H t^(-1)<H`, `c` centralizes `H`, and
   `[t c t^(-1),b]=a!=1`.
4. If `D_G(H)` is the normal subgroup generated by all compression-centralizer
   commutators for `H`, then `D_G(H)=A`.
5. The standard finite interval exhaustion `A_j`, for `j>=2`, contains `a`
   and satisfies, in every finite-dimensional complex unitary representation,

   ```text
   codim Fix sigma(A_j) <= 4 rank(sigma(a)-I).
   ```

Thus local finiteness, simplicity, normality, the exact compression-defect
identity, and the uniform active-rank estimate do not by themselves force MF
collapse. This leaves the stated property-T-conditional collapse theorem
intact. It identifies why the rank and cutoff inputs alone do not settle
`property-t-free-jacobson-head-collapse`: a replacement proof must use further
relations absent from this model.

The proof is written mathematics; no new Lean verification is asserted.
