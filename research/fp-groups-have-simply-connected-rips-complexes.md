title: A finitely presented group has simply connected Rips complexes at every scale past half its longest relator
---

Let `G = <S | R>` be a finite presentation, with `S` finite and symmetric, and
let `L = max(2, longest relator length)`. Then the Rips complex `R_t(G,S)` of
`(G, d_S)` is simply connected for every real `t >= L/2`.

Every finite generating set of a finitely presented group carries a finite
presentation, so a finitely presented group has, for **every** finite
generating set, simply connected Rips complexes at all large scales. This is
the case `n = 2` of Zaremsky Problem 4.4, recorded in the list as known.
