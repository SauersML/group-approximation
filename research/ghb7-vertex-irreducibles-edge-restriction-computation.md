1. **Vertex groups as matrix groups.** By item 5 of `cckw-ghb2-kms-group-structure`, the quotient
   `ψ : G → SL_4(F_7)` is injective on the three vertex groups, so the subgroups generated by the
   images of the edge generators are the vertex groups, with the edge generators matching.
2. **The abelian normal subgroup.** For a vertex group `X` and an edge generator `x` with the other
   edge generator `y`, the script sets `A = C_X(x)` and checks, by exhaustive exact multiplication
   mod 7:
   - `[X : A] = 7`;
   - `A` is abelian;
   - `A` is normal and `y ∉ A`.

   This was done for `(X_0, a, b)`, `(X_0, b, a)`, `(X_1, c, b)`, `(X_2, c, a)`. It gives
   `|A| = 49` for `X_0` and `|A| = 343` at `c` for `X_1, X_2`.
3. **Irreducibles (standard Clifford–Mackey theory for an abelian normal subgroup of prime index).**
   - `X/A` is cyclic of order 7, generated by the image of `y`.
   - A character `ψ` of `A` fixed by `y` extends to 7 linear characters of `X`.
   - A character with a `y`-orbit of size 7 induces an irreducible `Ind_A^X ψ` of degree 7, and
     characters in the same orbit induce the same irreducible.
   - The script identifies `A ≅ (Z/7)^r` through a computed basis, writes conjugation by `y` as an
     `r × r` matrix over `F_7`, and runs through all characters and their orbits.
4. **Completeness.** In every case the number of irreducibles found equals the number of conjugacy
   classes computed by exhaustive orbit enumeration (55, 55, 97, 97), and the sum of squared degrees
   equals `|X|`. So the list is complete.
5. **Edge restrictions.**
   - **At `x`.** `x ∈ A` is central in `A`, and its eigenvalues on `Ind_A^X ψ` are
     `ψ^(y^t)(x)`, `t = 0..6`, one for each coset of `A`. The script records their multiplicity
     vector, giving the patterns in the claim.
   - **At `y`.** `y` permutes the 7 cosets of `A` cyclically, so `χ(y^j) = 0` for `j ≠ 0` and `y`
     is regular in every degree-7 irreducible.
   - Linear characters have one eigenvalue each and contribute to the linear column.
6. **Cross-check.** For `j = 1..6` the script compares `Σ_χ |χ(x^j)|^2`, computed from the recorded
   multiplicities, with `|C_X(x^j)|`, counted directly. They agree in all four cases.
7. **Run.** `experiments/ghb7-edge-matching/edge_restrictions.py` (md5
   `97aa929d16d4b5fa7c49677101b187a1`) ran on MSI with `/usr/bin/python3.12`, exit code 0. Its output
   is `experiments/ghb7-edge-matching/edge_restrictions.json`.
8. **Edge `a` in `X_2` and edge `b` in `X_1`.** Those are the `y`-edges in the runs `(X_1, c, b)` and
   `(X_2, c, a)`, so step 5 makes them regular in every degree-7 irreducible. That agrees with
   `ghb7-triangle-vertex-groups-split-abelianizations`, where `|C(b)| = |C(a)| = 49 = |X^ab|` forces
   all nonlinear characters to vanish there.
