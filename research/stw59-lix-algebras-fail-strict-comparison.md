Neither the order-two algebra of `stw59-simple-ah-non-k1-injective` nor any
algebra `A_G` of `stw59-exact-finite-abelian-unitary-component-groups` has
strict comparison of positive elements. The factorial and arbitrary cyclic
families are special cases of the finite-abelian family.

The algebras are unital, simple, AH and not K1-injective, which strict
comparison would forbid. For the monotracial families a second proof runs
through Toms--White--Winter: unique trace plus strict comparison already
forces Z-stability, and Jiang then forces K1-injectivity.

So the LIX mechanism, as constructed, lives strictly outside the hypotheses of
Problem XVIII.
