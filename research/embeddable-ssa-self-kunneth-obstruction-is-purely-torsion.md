**ESTABLISHED.** Let `D` be strongly self-absorbing and suppose
`Q tensor O_infinity` is `D`-stable, equivalently that `D` has the
embeddability in STW Problem XIV(2). For the external product

```text
alpha:K_*(D) tensor K_*(D) -> K_*(D tensor D),
```

the rationalized map `alpha tensor Q` is an isomorphism. Consequently

```text
ker(alpha) is a torsion direct summand of K_*(D) tensor K_*(D),
K_1(D) is a torsion group,
K_0(D) tensor Q ~= Q.
```

Thus every possible failure of the self-Kunneth sequence for an
XIV(2)-embeddable SSA algebra is purely integral torsion: either torsion in
the kernel of the external product or the algebraic self-`Tor` term. No
rational invariant can close or refute the remaining gate.
