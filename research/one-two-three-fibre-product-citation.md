Citation import.  Baumslag--Bridson--Miller--Short (2000).  Not reproved
here and no Lean counterpart exists.

The statement was checked 2026-08-24 against the subdirect-product
literature, which restates it constantly and in a stable form; the primary
paper was not read, so the risk carried is a misremembered hypothesis rather
than a misattributed theorem.  The hypothesis most at risk of being
misquoted is `F_3` on the quotient, and it is the one the downstream route
is most sensitive to: weakening it to `F_2` would make the criterion vastly
easier to satisfy and is exactly the strengthening the subdirect-product
literature records as open in higher degrees (the `n`-`(n+1)`-`(n+2)`
conjecture).
