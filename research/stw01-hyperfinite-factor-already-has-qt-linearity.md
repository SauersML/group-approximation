Every bounded 2-quasitrace on the hyperfinite `II_1` factor `R` is a
nonnegative scalar multiple of its unique tracial state.  In particular
STW Problem I is already affirmative for `R`, independently of the open
quasidiagonality question in STW Problem X(2).

The standard hyperfinite sequence does not bridge the two problems: it is
dense in 2-norm, whereas the local quasitrace theorem and quasidiagonality
use operator-norm control.
