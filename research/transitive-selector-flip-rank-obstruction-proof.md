# Propagate the zero overlap rank around the selector orbit

Unitary conjugacy preserves matrix rank and tracial weight.  A transitive
actor on the four joint atoms therefore proves `(TSF3)`.  Native Leavitt
orthogonality sets the overlap atom to zero, so every atom in its orbit is
zero; full transitivity kills the parent.  Restricting to the sibling swap
keeps the allowed atoms in one orbit but leaves the overlap in a singleton
orbit, permitting its independent multiplicity Pauli absorber.  This proves
the claimed dichotomy.
