Construct a finite group-word mechanism such that the packet exit emitted at
level `n`, after analytic compression by its forbidden selector atom, is
trivial on every E5 coefficient root below the pulse's top degree and
negative on its top hard root, up to polynomial normalized-HS loss.
Equivalently, construct prefix carriers `C_(n+1)<=C_n` which approximately
reduce the original BCS words, contain the active packet copy, and whose
difference contains the emitted exit.  Either form makes the first-hit layers
summable and defeats the persistent character of
`triangular-e5-pulses-admit-persistent-tail-character`.

## Attempts

- Strictly increasing degree and a unique leading coefficient are
  insufficient: a recursive tail character detects every pulse, and finite
  ring quotients realize this through every fixed depth.
- Imposing all moving kernels as normal relations kills the hard form, by
  `normal-closure-of-moving-kernels-kills-the-hard-form`.
- The live options are a genuinely ascending/noninvertible root shift or a
  prefix projection generated by finitely many commuting covariance words
  and preserved by the two-child recurrence.
