Let `Γ` be a LOT with vertex generators `x_1, ..., x_n`, and let `φ : H * <s> → G(Γ)` be an isomorphism
with `H ≠ 1`.

1. **`H` is perfect.** Every relator `i λ t^-1 λ^-1` identifies `t` with a conjugate of `i`, and `Γ` is
   connected. So all `x_j` are conjugate, `H_1(G(Γ)) = Z` is generated by the class of `x_1`, and
   `G(Γ) / <<x_1>> = 1`. Also `H_1(H * <s>) = H_1(H) ⊕ Z`. A finitely generated abelian group `A` with
   `A ⊕ Z ≅ Z` is trivial (compare ranks and torsion), so `H_1(H) = 0`.
2. **A degree-one killing word.** Let `w = φ^-1(x_1)` and let `p : H * <s> → <s> ≅ Z` kill `H`. Since
   `H_1(H) = 0`, `p` induces an isomorphism `H_1(H * <s>) → Z`. The class of `w` generates `H_1`, so
   `p(w) = s^{±1}`, i.e. `deg_s(w) = ±1`. Moreover `(H * <s>) / <<w>> ≅ G(Γ) / <<x_1>> = 1`.
3. **Torsion.** If `H` were torsion-free, `klyachko-kl-holds-for-torsion-free` would make
   `H → (H * <s>) / <<w>>` injective. That group is trivial, which contradicts `H ≠ 1`. So `H` has a
   nontrivial element of finite order.
4. **The remaining assertions.**
   - A locally indicable group is torsion-free.
   - A finite aspherical 2-complex has a torsion-free fundamental group: the group has cohomological
     dimension at most `2`, and a nontrivial finite cyclic subgroup has infinite cohomological dimension.
