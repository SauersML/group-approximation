The normal closures of `y` and `t` have free bases consisting of their
`x_1`- and `x_2`-conjugates.  Hence the kernel of the exponent-difference map
is generated by those two normal closures and `(x_1,x_2)`, exactly the image
of `B3(1)`.

For a finite-index normal `J` in this kernel `H`, conjugation by a lift of
the generator of `P/H=Z` has a finite orbit on subgroups of index `[H:J]`,
because `H` is finitely generated.  Intersect that orbit to obtain a
finite-index `P`-normal subgroup `M<=J`.  The cyclic action on the finite
group `H/M` has finite order `d`; imposing that the cyclic lift have order
`d` gives a finite quotient of `P` whose kernel intersects `H` in exactly
`M`.  The construction is effective from a finite quotient table.
