**OPEN.** A drafted proof is recorded as an attempt (see Attempts). It is not established
because a referee lens refuted the statement as written: the cut-and-paste sentence of item 3 and
(C3) claim arbitrary re-gluing, which is false.

Let `G` be generated by a finite symmetric set `S`, with Kazhdan
constant `kappa > 0` in the convention

```text
dist(v, H^G) <= kappa^(-1) max_(s in S) ||pi(s)v - v||                          (KP)
```

for every unitary representation `pi`. Let `sigma : S -> Sym(n)` be arbitrary, extended to the
free group `F(S)`. The *path from `x` labelled `w = s_1...s_k`* visits
`x, sigma(s_k)x, sigma(s_(k-1)s_k)x, ..., sigma(w)x`. A set `A` of points is **consistent** if
every path that starts in `A`, stays in `A`, and is labelled by a word trivial in `G` ends at its
starting point. Put `eta = |[n] \ A|/n`.

1. **No finite quotients.** If `G` has no nontrivial finite quotient and `A` is consistent, then

   ```text
   d_H(sigma(s), 1) <= (2 + 2/kappa^2) eta            for every s in S.        (CP1)
   ```

2. **General `G`.** If `A` is consistent, there is a finite `G`-set `X''` containing `[n]`, with
   `|X''| <= (1 + 4 eta/kappa^2) n`, whose action `rho` satisfies

   ```text
   |{x in [n] : rho(s)x != sigma(s)x}| <= (2 + 4/kappa^2) eta n    for every s in S.   (CP2)
   ```

3. **Source of consistent sets.** If `Z` is any `G`-set (finite or infinite) and `iota : A -> Z`
   is injective with `iota(sigma(s)x) = s . iota(x)` whenever `x, sigma(s)x in A`, then `A` is
   consistent. So (CP1) and (CP2) apply to every model obtained by cutting a genuine `G`-set, such
   as a coset space `G/H`, a building or a boundary, to `(1-eta)n` points and re-gluing the rest
   arbitrarily.

**Consequences.**

* **(C1) Residual finiteness.** If `G` has sofic approximations `sigma_j` carrying consistent sets
  with `eta_j -> 0`, then `G` is residually finite. For `g != 1` written as a word `w`, the
  genuine actions `rho_j` of item 2 agree with `sigma_j(w)` off `|w|(2+4/kappa^2) eta_j n_j`
  points of `[n_j]`, and `sigma_j(w)` moves almost all points, so `rho_j(g) != 1` in the finite
  group `Sym(X''_j)`.
* **(C2) Consistency deficit.** For `G` with no finite quotients, every consistent set of every
  model has `eta >= c_kappa m`, where `m = max_s d_H(sigma(s),1)` and
  `c_kappa = kappa^2/(2 kappa^2 + 2)`. Any model with a mark (`m` bounded below) has a consistency
  deficit of the same order.
* **(C3) The kill.** For a Kazhdan group with no finite quotients, such as the Titz Mite--Witzel
  kernel `K` of `titz-witzel-kernel-sofic`, no construction of almost actions that agrees with a
  cut-and-paste of any genuine `K`-set on `(1-eta)n` points can move a generator on more than
  `(2 + 2/kappa^2) eta n` points. The invariant is the partial action on the agreement set, and
  every such construction dies at its globalization (step 2 of the proof). It applies to
  every sparse re-gluing of a genuine action, finite or infinite, and to all partial actions of
  `K` restricted from infinite `K`-sets.

**Calibration.**

* The constant depends only on `kappa`. There is no dependence on relator lengths, word lengths
  or the index of any subgroup.
* Property (T) is necessary in item 1. Let `G` be an infinite finitely generated simple amenable
  group (for instance a derived topological full group of a minimal subshift, Juschenko--Monod),
  so it has no finite quotients. Take a Folner set `F` with `|sF \ F| <= eta |F|`, let `sigma(s)`
  be left multiplication on `F ∩ s^(-1)F`, completed to a permutation of `F` arbitrarily, and put
  `A = F ∩ ∩_s s^(-1)F`. By item 3 (with `Z = G`), `A` is consistent with density `>= 1 - |S| eta`,
  yet every generator `s != 1` moves every point of `A`. So (CP1) fails without (T).
* The necessity of the constant's growth in `1/kappa` is not tested here.
* Consistency uses trivial words of every length. For finitely presented `G` the local-to-global
  passage is `kazhdan-sofic-models-carry-long-scale-monodromy`.

**Scope.** Globalization of partial actions (Abadie; Kellendonk--Lawson; Exel) and the Kazhdan
projection onto finite orbits are standard. The quantitative combination for permutation models
was not found in the sources read, and no novelty is claimed.
