**PROPOSED ESTABLISHED.** A written deduction; see the route `fpbs-cost-is-limit-of-cayley-subgraphing-costs-proof`.

**Statement.** Let `Gamma` be an infinite group generated by a finite set `S_0`, and `a` an essentially free pmp
action. For a finite generating set `S ⊆ Gamma \ {1}` let `C_sub^a(S)` be the infimum of costs of Borel subgraphings
of the Cayley graphing `Phi_S^a` that generate `R_a`, and let `B_R` be the `S_0`-ball of radius `R` minus `1`.

- **(E)** `C(a) = lim_R C_sub^a(B_R) = inf_S C_sub^a(S)`, and the sequence is nonincreasing. Concretely, any
  generating graphing `Psi` of finite cost becomes, after truncating to finitely many labels `F` and adding back the
  `S_0`-edges not yet connected, a generating subgraphing of `Phi_(S_0 ∪ F)^a` of cost `< c(Psi) + eps`.
- **(F)** For the Bernoulli shift `b`,
  `lim_R lim_L m_(B_R)(P_L) = lim_R lim_L A_(B_R)(P_L) = C(b) - 1 - beta_1^(2)(Gamma)`, with `m` the Morse deficit
  of `fpbs-bernoulli-morse-collapse-criterion` and `A` the attainment defect of
  `fpbs-morse-mass-bounded-by-relator-boundary-rank`.
- **(G)** For a fixed finite generating set `S`, `lim_L m_S(P_L) = 0` iff `C(b) = 1 + beta_1^(2)(Gamma)` and
  `(Q3)(S)`: `C_sub(S) = C(b)`. The property `(Q3)(S)` is upward closed in `S`.

**Reading.** The Morse reformulation of the cost–Betti target loses nothing once the generating set may grow. The
open premise `fpbs-morse-deficit-vanishes-for-full-presentations` exceeds the target only by subgraphing attainment at
small generating sets, which no route to tail compactness needs.
