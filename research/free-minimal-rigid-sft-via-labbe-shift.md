- **Free and minimal.** Labbé's `Ω_U` is a minimal aperiodic Wang shift. It is a nearest-neighbour
  SFT on which `Z^2` acts freely (second claim).
- **Rigid.** If `LC(Ω_U, F_q) ⋊ Z^2` is finitely presented (first claim), the third claim makes
  `Ω_U` `D`-quantum rigid for some `D`.
