Let a countable Haagerup group `Lambda` act trace-preservingly on an abelian
finite von Neumann algebra `A`, and put `M=A rtimes Lambda`.  No property-`(T)`
`II_1` factor admits a trace-preserving embedding into `M`.

In particular, an algebraic compact-abelian crossed product containing the
property-`(T)` non-Connes-embeddable factor of
`non-ce-property-t-equivalence-relation-exists` must use a **non-Haagerup**
acting group.  This conclusion does not assume that the embedding respects
the Cartan algebra, normalizers, or the original relation action.
