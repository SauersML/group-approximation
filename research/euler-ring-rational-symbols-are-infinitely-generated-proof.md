**Textbook inputs (not re-read).**
- (TS) The tame symbol of a discrete valuation `v` with residue field `κ` is `∂_v{a,b} = (−1)^(v(a)v(b)) a^(v(b)) b^(−v(a)) mod v`
  (Milnor, *Introduction to algebraic K-theory*, §11). Each `z ∈ K_2(Q(u))` has `∂_v z ≠ 1` for finitely many `v`.
- (UFD) A regular local ring is a UFD (Auslander–Buchsbaum).
- (L) In a one-dimensional noetherian local domain `B`, `ℓ(B/gh) = ℓ(B/g) + ℓ(B/h)` for nonzero `g, h`.

**Reciprocity lemma.** Let `O` be a two-dimensional regular local ring with fraction field `K`. For a height-one
prime `𝔭 = (π)` define `ord_𝔭 : κ(𝔭)^x -> Z` by `ḡ ↦ ℓ(O/(π, g))` for `g ∈ O ∖ (π)`, extended multiplicatively. This
is well defined by (L). Then `Σ_𝔭 ord_𝔭(∂_𝔭 z) = 0` for every `z ∈ K_2(K)`.
*Proof.*
- It suffices to treat `z = {a,b}`. By (UFD), write `a = ε ∏ π^(α_π)` and `b = η ∏ π^(β_π)` with `ε, η` units.
- By (TS), `∂_π{a,b} = ± a'^(β_π) / b'^(α_π)`, where `a' = a π^(−α_π)` and `b' = b π^(−β_π)` are prime to `π`.
- Additivity (L) and `ℓ(O/(π, unit)) = 0` give `ord_π(∂_π{a,b}) = Σ_(ρ≠π) (β_π α_ρ − α_π β_ρ) ℓ(O/(π,ρ))`.
- Summing over `π`, the terms for `(π,ρ)` and `(ρ,π)` cancel, because `ℓ(O/(π,ρ)) = ℓ(O/(ρ,π))`. ∎

**Part 1.** Fix a prime `p ≠ l`.
- *The shift.* `σ` extends to `Q(u)` and sends `v_(π_k)` to `v_(π_(k+1))`, inducing the identity on the residue field
  `Q`. So `∂_(k+1)(σz) = ∂_k(z)`, and `I_p(σz) = I_p(z) + T_p(z)` with `T_p(z) = Σ_k v_p(∂_k z)`.
- *Where the lines meet the fibre.* For `z ∈ K_2(A)`, `∂_𝔭 z = 1` at every height-one prime `𝔭` of `Z[u]` other than
  the `(π_k)`, because such `𝔭` misses the multiplicative set, so `z` comes from `K_2(A_𝔭)` with `A_𝔭 = Z[u]_𝔭` a DVR. `K_2` of a local ring is
  generated by Steinberg symbols of units (Dennis–Stein, LNM 342, §10(a)(i), p. 252, read at the source; see
  `resolvent-steinberg-rf-residual-is-infinitely-normally-generated`), and `∂_𝔭` of a symbol of units is `1`.
- Mod `p`, `π_k ≡ u + 1 + kl`. Since `p ∤ l`, the line `π_k` meets the fibre over `p` in exactly one point
  `x = (p, u + 1 + kl)`. The line is regular there, `Z[u]/π_k ≅ Z`, so `ord` at `x` is `v_p`.
- *Applying reciprocity.* Apply the lemma at each such `x`, with `O = Z[u]_x`. It gives `Σ_(k : π_k ∈ x) v_p(∂_k z) = 0`.
  Summing over the points `x` of the fibre gives `T_p(z) = 0`. ∎

**Part 2.** By (TS), `∂_0 c_d = π_d^(−1)|_(u=−1) = 1/(dl)` and `∂_d c_d = π_0|_(u=−1−dl) = −dl`. All other residues
are trivial. So `I_p(c_d) = 0·v_p(1/(dl)) + d·v_p(−dl) = d v_p(d)`, since `p ≠ l`.

**Part 3.**
- By part 1, `I = (I_p)_(p≠l) : K_2(A) -> ⊕_(p≠l) Z` factors through `coker(1 − σ_*)`. By part 2, `I(c_q) = q e_q`,
  so the classes of the `c_q` are independent.
- `A` is regular noetherian, hence regular supercoherent, and `σ` is an automorphism. By
  `abc-twisted-laurent-k-theory-fibration` there is an exact sequence
  `K_2(A) --(1−σ_*)--> K_2(A) -> K_2(D) -> K_1(A)`, so `coker(1 − σ_*)` injects into `K_2(D)`.
  - The convention `σ` versus `σ^(−1)` does not change the cokernel.
- The unital maps `D × Z -> T_l`, `(d, n) ↦ diag(d, n)`, and `T_l -> D × Z` (kill `M`) compose to the identity, so
  `K_2(D)` is a direct summand of `K_2(T_l)`. Likewise `D ⊆ R_l -> R_l/(e) ≅ D` (`resolvent-ring-has-path-normal-form`,
  item 1) makes `K_2(D)` a summand of `K_2(R_l)`.

**Part 4.**
- `π_0` and `π_q` are commuting units of `D`, of `T_l` via `diag(·, 1)`, and of `R_l`. Their rank-`N` symbols lie in
  `K_2(N, ·)` and have the stable images of part 3.
- The symbol lemma (`infinitely-generated-symbols-block-fp-elementary-groups`, part 1) gives non-finite-presentation
  of each `E_N`.
- `K_2(N, T_l)` contains elements whose stable images generate a free group of infinite rank, so it is not finitely
  generated. ∎
