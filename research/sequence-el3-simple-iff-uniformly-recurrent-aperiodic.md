**Statement.**
- Let A be a finite set and x ∈ A^Z. Let R_x ⊆ End_{F_2}(F_2[Z]) be generated by the shift σ (σδ_n = δ_{n+1}) and the maps D_a δ_n = [x_n = a]δ_n.
- Let n ≥ 3 and G = EL_n(R_x).
  1. G is infinite.
  2. G/Z(G) is simple iff x is uniformly recurrent and not periodic.
  3. In that case Z(G) = 1 over F_2, and G is finitely generated, has property (T), and is LEF, so it is sofic and hyperlinear.

**Reading for the note.** "Uniformly recurrent" means that every word occurring in x occurs in every sufficiently long window of x. So the headline of simple_kazhdan_sofic_group.tex becomes: *for every uniformly recurrent non-periodic sequence x, EL_3 of the ring generated by the shift and multiplication by the letters of x is an infinite simple Kazhdan LEF group*. Both hypotheses are necessary:
- a constant or periodic x gives SL_{3N}(F_2[t,t^{-1}]);
- a non-recurrent x gives an ideal.
