**ESTABLISHED.** The proof is `gersten-pairs-lie-in-no-locally-finite-tree-host-proof`.

**Definition.** A *Gersten pair* in a group `Γ` is a pair `(a, t)` of elements
such that `a` has infinite order and `b := t a t^-1` satisfies
`b a b^-1 = a^2`. Equivalently, it is a homomorphism from the Baumslag--Gersten
group `BG = < a, t | (t a t^-1) a (t a t^-1)^-1 = a^2 >` to `Γ` under which `a`
has infinite order. `BG` itself contains one, because `<a, b>` is a copy of
`BS(1,2)`, in which `a` has infinite order.

**Statement.**

1. **One tree.** Let `Γ` act by automorphisms on a locally finite tree `T`, and
   suppose the kernel of the action is a torsion group (for example trivial).
   Then `Γ` contains no Gersten pair.
2. **Products.** The same holds when `Γ` acts on finitely many locally finite
   trees `T_1, ..., T_m` at once (for example factor by factor on their product)
   and the common kernel is a torsion group.
3. **Hosts excluded.** No group containing a Gersten pair is a subgroup of:
   - **(E1)** a group acting faithfully on a locally finite tree, the hypothesis
     of Theorem B of Bux--Llosa Isenrich--Wu;
   - **(E2)** the fundamental group of a finite graph of groups with finite-index
     edge inclusions acting faithfully on its (locally finite) Bass--Serre tree,
     the hypothesis of their Theorem 10.5, of
     `finite-index-edge-hnn-embeds-in-fp-simple-group`, and of the faithful
     enlargement used in their Theorem 11.1;
   - **(E3)** the automorphism group of a locally finite rooted tree, which
     contains every self-similar group;
   - **(E4')** a group acting factor by factor on a finite product of locally
     finite trees with finite (or torsion) kernel, which covers the
     factor-preserving lattices in products of trees.
4. **Consequence for the HNN permanence.** Take the input
   `A = BS(1,2) = < a, b | b a b^-1 = a^2 >`, `C_1 = <a>`, `C_2 = <b>`,
   `phi(a) = b` of `bh-embeddability-survives-decidable-edge-hnn`. It meets
   every hypothesis: `A` embeds in a finitely presented simple group by
   `baumslag-solitar-and-free-by-cyclic-satisfy-boone-higman`, and membership in
   `<a>` and `<b>` is decidable in the faithful affine representation
   `a = (x -> x+1)`, `b = (x -> 2x)` of `A` on `Z[1/2]`. Its HNN extension is
   `A*_phi = BG`. So no group in (E1)--(E4') contains `A*_phi`. More generally,
   every input in which some `a in C_1` of infinite order satisfies
   `phi(a) a phi(a)^-1 = a^2` in `A` is excluded. The same input is a Magnus
   step, so the obstruction applies to `bh-embeddability-survives-magnus-subgroup-hnn`,
   to `baumslag-gersten-group-satisfies-boone-higman` and to the Magnus route of
   `one-relator-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`.

## The invariant and where every engine dies

For an elliptic element `x` of a locally finite tree and a vertex `w` it fixes,
let `P(x)` be the set of primes dividing the order of `x` on some finite ball
`B_R(w)`. It does not depend on `w`, and it is a conjugacy invariant. In a
Gersten pair, `a` and `b` are conjugate, so `P(a) = P(b)`. On a ball fixed by
both, `b` acts on the cyclic group generated by the image of `a` by squaring.
So the smallest prime `p` in `P(a)` has `ord_p(2) > 1`, and every prime factor
of `ord_p(2)` is smaller than `p` and lies in `P(b) = P(a)`. That contradicts
the choice of `p`, so `P(a)` is empty and `a` acts trivially.

Every engine dies at the same step, placing the input inside the host: there
the base `BS(1,2)` is elliptic, and the invariant forces `a` into the kernel.
Enlarging the input first, as in Theorem 11.1, does not help, because every
enlargement is again a host of type (E1) or (E2). This settles the question left
open in item 3 of `baumslag-gersten-base-is-elliptic-in-every-tree-action`: no
Theorem 11.1 host, and no faithful locally finite tree host at all, contains `BG`.

**Relation to the equicontinuity claim.** Item 1 can also be derived from
`baumslag-gersten-equicontinuous-generator-acts-trivially`. Apply it to the end
compactification of `T`, which is compact, metrizable and zero-dimensional. The
powers of the elliptic `a` lie in a compact vertex stabilizer, so they act
equicontinuously. The direct proof here stays on finite balls. The new content is
the ellipticity input for unrooted trees and the consequences for the engines and
the HNN permanence.

## What this changes

- **Attempt 7 of `bh-embeddability-survives-decidable-edge-hnn`** needed an
  infinite non-residually-finite FA subgroup such as the Kac--Moody lattice
  `S_q`. This obstruction needs neither FA nor a failure of residual finiteness
  in the base. The base `BS(1,2)` is residually finite, linear, metabelian and
  one-relator, and the edges are infinite cyclic. So the tree and self-similar
  engines cannot prove the permanence even for cyclic edges over a linear
  base, and the one-relator route is affected, which Attempt 7 had left open.
- **Free bases survive.** If `A` is free, no input has such an `a`: in a free
  group `b a b^-1 = a^2` forces `a = 1`. Moreover every Gersten pair in `F*_phi`
  would place `<a, b>` in a conjugate of `F` by
  `baumslag-gersten-base-is-elliptic-in-every-tree-action`. So
  `oriented-artin-bh-via-free-group-hnn-permanence` is untouched.
- **What survives in general.** Type (A) actors and twisted Brin--Thompson
  groups, whose actors need not be profinite, and hosts in which `a` is not
  equicontinuous (compare `baumslag-gersten-equicontinuous-generator-acts-trivially`).
