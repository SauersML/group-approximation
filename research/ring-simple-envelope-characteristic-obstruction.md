title: A nonzero unital ring has an abstract simple unital envelope exactly in prime characteristic or with torsion-free additive group
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/boone-higman-ring-route-repair-2026-09-08.md

Let R be a nonzero unital associative ring. There is an injective unital
map from R into a nonzero simple unital ring if and only if either R has
prime characteristic or its additive group is torsion-free.

No finite-generation, finite-presentation, or effectiveness conclusion for
the envelope is asserted.

In particular the finite, decidable, finitely presented ring Z/6Z has no
such envelope. This refutes the unrestricted statement of
[[finite-fullness-simulation-theorem-for-rings]] before the question of
finite presentation arises. Requiring characteristic zero alone does not
fix it: Z x F_2 has characteristic zero and nonzero additive torsion.

The group rings Z[H] used for Boone--Higman have torsion-free additive
group, so these counterexamples do not refute a construction tailored to
those group rings. Their abstract simple envelopes exist; controlling a
finite presentation remains unresolved.
