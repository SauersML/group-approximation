**INVALIDATED PROPOSAL.**  The proposal was to prove weak ucp-stability of
`SL_3(Z)` using only structure special to regular-trace hyperlinear
approximations, while allowing arbitrary nonseparating asymptotic
representations to remain uncorrectable.

That distinction does not exist for this lattice.
`residual-finite-regularization-removes-the-weak-ucp-quantifier` tensors any
asymptotic representation with exact regular representations of separating
finite congruence quotients.  The tensor product is hyperlinear.  Any ucp
correction of it recovers a ucp correction of the original tuple through
the invariant maximally entangled vector, contractively in normalized HS
norm.  Property `(T)` then turns that correction into a flexible genuine
representation.

Thus a positive proof may still use the regular tensor packet during its
argument, but it must in fact imply full flexible HS-stability.  The weak-ucp
input quantifier does not make the `SL_3(Z)` problem smaller.
