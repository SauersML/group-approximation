Artifact Section 3.  Take a faithful model `psi` with values `a_n`, and
quasidiagonal models `sigma^+`, `sigma^-` realizing `tau_+`, `tau_-`.  By the
value formula `k_n(omega_n(x^)+o(1))` their values `b_n>0>c_n` eventually.
Form `|c_n| psi_n + a_n sigma^-_n` when `a_n>0`, `b_n psi_n + |a_n| sigma^+_n`
when `a_n<0`, and `psi_n` when `a_n=0`.  Direct sums preserve the
multiplicativity defect and the norm lower bound, and the total value is
`0`.
