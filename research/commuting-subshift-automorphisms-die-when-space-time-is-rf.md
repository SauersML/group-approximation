**ESTABLISHED** (proof: `commuting-subshift-automorphisms-space-time-rf-proof`).

**Setting.**
- `A` is a finite alphabet and `X ⊆ A^Z` is an infinite minimal subshift, with
  shift `(Tx)_m = x_(m+1)`.
- `ψ ∈ Aut(X,T)` is a homeomorphism commuting with `T`.
- By Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon, `ψ` is the restriction of a sliding block code
  `F : A^Z -> A^Z`, `F(y)_m = f(y_(m-ρ), ..., y_(m+ρ))`.
- The *space-time subshift* is
  `Y_ψ = {Φ(x) : x ∈ X} ⊆ A^(Z^2)`, where `Φ(x)(m,n) = (ψ^n x)_m`.
  It is conjugate, via `Φ` and `g ↦ -g`, to the `Z^2`-action on `X` generated by `T` and `ψ`.

**Theorem 1 (reduction to one block code).** The following are equivalent.
- (a) The `Z^2`-action `⟨T,ψ⟩` on `X` is Kerr–Nowak residually finite.
- (b) `Y_ψ` is window-periodic, i.e. window-LEF.
- (c) Condition `(*)_F`: for every `M ≥ 1` there are `p ≥ 1` and a `p`-periodic `y ∈ A^Z` such that, for every
  `j ≥ 0`, every length-`M` subword of `F^j(y)` lies in `L_M(X)`.

Moreover, `(c)` for one block code of `ψ` implies it for every block code of `ψ`.

**Theorem 2 (class kill).** Assume `(a)–(c)`. Let `q` be a prime power, `S = LC(X,F_q) ⋊_T Z` and
`σ_ψ(f u^n) = (f ∘ ψ^(-1)) u^n`. Let `σ ∈ Aut(S)` satisfy
`σ^m = Ad(w) ∘ σ_ψ^k` on `S`, for some `m ≥ 1`, `k ∈ Z` and `w ∈ S^×`. Then `(G2)` of
`simple-ring-with-centralizer-compressing-automorphism` fails for `(S, σ)`, for every `R` and `z`.

**Theorem 3 (the kill reaches genuine survivors).**
- Let `X_1` and `X_2` be infinite minimal subshifts such that `X = X_1 × X_2` (over `A_1 × A_2`) is minimal.
  Then `ψ = T_1 × id` has infinite order modulo `⟨T⟩` and satisfies `(*)`.
- Such pairs exist. One example is the period-doubling subshift with a Sturmian subshift of irrational slope.
- So Theorem 2 kills some automorphisms whose spatial class modulo `[[T]]` has infinite order. These belong to
  the first survivor class left by `subshift-diagonal-automorphisms-need-infinite-spatial-class`, which is
  therefore not killed by spatial class alone but is killed here whenever `(*)` holds.

**Theorem 4 (every candidate counterexample is a Z^2 subshift).**
- Let `Y'` be a free minimal `Z^2`-subshift, and let `v ∈ Z^2` be a primitive vector that is an expansive
  direction of `Y'` and acts minimally on `Y'`.
- Then there are an infinite minimal `Z`-subshift `X` and a `ψ ∈ Aut(X,T)` of infinite order modulo `⟨T⟩` such
  that `Y_ψ` is conjugate to `Y'`, after an automorphism of `Z^2`.
- Hence the hypothesis of Theorem 2 holds for every infinite-order `ψ` on every infinite minimal subshift iff:
  every free minimal `Z^2`-subshift with a rational direction that is both expansive and minimal is Kerr–Nowak
  residually finite.
- Every minimal subsystem of a `Z^2` SFT without periodic points fails to be residually finite.
- The period-doubling and Sturmian facts in Theorem 3 are recalled standard facts, not proved here. Theorems 1,
  2 and 4 use only nodes of this graph.

**Consequence for the gate.**
- The commuting-automorphism survivor of `simple-ring-with-centralizer-compressing-automorphism` splits into
  two prerequisites that can fail independently:
  - a non-RF `Y_ψ`, which is the open `aperiodic-sft-minimal-subsystem-with-expansive-minimal-direction`
    when `Y_ψ` sits inside an aperiodic SFT;
  - for such `ψ`, `(G2)` itself.
- A witness of the first kind escapes every permutation-model argument. It is not yet known to escape
  non-permutation matricial models. That is the quantum-to-classical gap of
  `labbe-tiles-admit-periodic-quantum-tilings-at-every-scale`.
- `minimal-z2-directions-forbid-eigenvalues-vanishing-on-them` rules out the standard aperiodic SFTs as sources
  of such a `Y_ψ`.
