Sections 1 and 2 prove the radial first-discovery property, force a whole
radius-m half-product ball before the first crossing, and count its
vertices. Finite priority sublevel sets give completion. Section 2 also
identifies the full finite-transcript cylinder probability, including
open answers, to prevent interpreting the deterministic bound as an
unjustified Bernoulli expectation bound.
