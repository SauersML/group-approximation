ESTABLISHED.  Let `G` be a countable nonsofic group whose reduced C-star
algebra is MF, and let `N = Rad_hyp(G)` be the set of elements which are not
tracially visible.  Then `N` is an amenable normal subgroup of `G`, `G/N` is
hyperlinear, and exactly one of the following holds:

```text
(a) G/N is not sofic:  G/N is a hyperlinear nonsofic group (Pestov 3.4, negative);
(b) G/N is sofic:      G is a nonsofic extension of the amenable group N by a sofic group.
```

**On alternative (b).**  Extensions of an amenable normal subgroup by a sofic
quotient can be nonsofic in general: the Kun--Thom coset wreath
`(Z/2)^(G/Gamma) rtimes G` is one, with residually finite quotient.  That group
is not operator MF (the commuting-lamp row of
`nonsofic-mechanism-mf-classification`), so it does not realize (b) with MF
reduced algebra.  Nothing recorded here excludes (b) for a reduced-MF group.
