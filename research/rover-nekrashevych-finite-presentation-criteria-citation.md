## Zaremsky

M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Embedding finitely presented self-similar groups into finitely presented simple groups*, arXiv:2405.09722v2 (21 Jan 2025).

- **Citation 2.3 (FP1).** "[Sco84a, Theorem 2] For any self-similar G <= Aut(T_d), if G is finitely presented then so is V_d(G)."
  - [Sco84a] is E. A. Scott, *A construction which can be used to produce finitely presented infinite simple groups*, J. Algebra 90(2) (1984), 294--322.
- **Citation 2.4 (S).** "[Nek18, Theorem 4.7] For any self-similar G <= Aut(T_d), the commutator subgroup [V_d(G),V_d(G)] is simple."
- **The text after Citation 2.4 (FP3).** "even if V_d(G) is finitely presented, its commutator subgroup might not be. Of course if the abelianization of V_d(G) is finite, then the commutator subgroup has finite index and is thus finitely presented."
- **Section 3, proof of Theorem 1.1 (Ab and R).**
  - The abelianization procedure, "described for example in [Nek18, Theorem 4.8]".
  - The recursion `g -> sigma^m(g_1,...,g_d, ..., g_1,...,g_d)` on `T_(md)`.
  - The choice of an even `m` such that `1/m` is not an eigenvalue of `A`.
  - The finish through Proposition 2.5 (`F wr [V_d(G),V_d(G)]` embeds in `[V_d(G),V_d(G)]`) and Kaloujnine--Krasner.
- **Introduction (type F_n).** "the proof of Theorem 1.1 also shows that every self-similar group of type F_n embeds in a simple group of type F_n".
  - The paper cites Skipper--Witzel--Zaremsky, *Simple groups separated by finiteness properties*, Invent. Math. 215(2) (2019), 713--740, for simple groups of arbitrary finiteness length.
  - That paper was not read.

## Nekrashevych

V. Nekrashevych, *Finitely presented groups associated with expanding maps*, arXiv:1312.5654. This is [Nek18], in *Geometric and cohomological group theory*, LMS Lecture Note Ser. 444 (2018), 115--171.

- **Theorem 5.9 (FP2).** "If G is a contracting self-similar group, then the group V_G is finitely presented."
- **Section 5.3.** "for any contracting group G, the group generated by the nucleus N is self-similar contracting, and V_<N> = V_G."
- **Theorem 4.7.** "The commutator subgroup of V_G is simple."

Used at statement level. Not reproved here.
