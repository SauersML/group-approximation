Let D_o=(sum_B b_AB)^2/sum_B b_AB^2 for the open cluster A of o.
Its initial Bernoulli expectation derivative is 143 on T_13 and -29/5
on T_4 square K_10. Both are nonamenable 13-regular Cayley graphs and
have identical initial rooted mass and contact data. Thus D_o is not
universally nondecreasing. This does not disprove a near-critical
averaged criterion or Benjamini--Schramm.
