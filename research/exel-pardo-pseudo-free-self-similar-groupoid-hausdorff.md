**ESTABLISHED (import; see `exel-pardo-pseudo-free-self-similar-groupoid-hausdorff-citation`).** Let `(G, E, φ)` satisfy
Exel–Pardo's standing hypothesis: `G` a countable discrete group, `E` a finite graph with no sources, an action of `G`
on `E` with a one-cocycle `φ`. Let `𝒢_(G,E)` be the associated groupoid.

1. `𝒢_(G,E)` is Hausdorff iff every `g ∈ G` has at most finitely many minimal strongly fixed paths.
2. If `(G, E, φ)` is pseudo free (`g e = e` and `φ(g, e) = 1` imply `g = 1`), then no path of nonzero length is
   strongly fixed by a nontrivial `g`.
3. `𝒢_(G,E)` is minimal iff `E` is weakly `G`-transitive.

**Consequence.** Pseudo-free self-similar actions have Hausdorff groupoids. With one vertex (the Nekrashevych case)
they are also minimal.
