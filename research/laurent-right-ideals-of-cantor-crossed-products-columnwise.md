**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `(X,T)` be a minimal homeomorphism of the Cantor set, `F` a field, `R = LC(X,F) ⋊_T Z`. Let `V = F^{(Z)}` carry the orbit representation at a point `x`: `fδ_t = f(T^tx)δ_t`, `uδ_t = δ_{t+1}`.

For every nonzero `q ∈ F[u^{±1}]` and every `z ∈ R`: `z ∈ qR` if and only if `zδ_t ∈ qV` for every `t`. Here `V ≅ F[u^{±1}]` by `δ_t ↦ u^t`, and `qV` is the ideal generated by `q`.

**Consequence.** With `q = u^αq_0`, `q_0(0) ≠ 0`, and the residue map `ρ_q : V → F[u]/(q_0)`, `δ_t ↦ ū^t`, membership reads `ρ_q ∘ z = 0`.
