`T` is generated by `x,y`, so for `r>=3` the group `EL_r(T)` is generated by
the finitely many root elements `x_ij(1), x_ij(x), x_ij(y)`, via the
commutator identity `[x_ij(u),x_jk(v)]=x_ik(uv)` for distinct `i,j,k`.  So
branch 1 gives a finitely generated non-hyperlinear group.  Branches are
exhaustive.

**Branch 2, amenable holonomy.**  Apply `hyperlinear-base-amenable-cells-retain-coefficients`
with the window containing the packet, the base relators used by the cell,
and the support of `z(1-a)`.  `(HBA2)` makes the packet trace canonical, so
the forbidden mass tends to `q_0`.  `(HBA3)` retains the regular norm of
`z(1-a)`, and `(HBA4)` together with the relation clause makes the cell
energy tend to zero.  The regular floor `(1-epsilon)sqrt(q_0)` holds because
an infinite property-`(T)` group has no finite-dimensional subrepresentation
in `lambda`, so `lambda(p_<=K)=0` and
`||lambda(z)(1-lambda(a))||_2 >= ||lambda(z)||_2-||lambda(z)(lambda(p)-lambda(a))||_2 >= (1-epsilon)sqrt(q_0)`.
A factor `C_2` is absorbed by tensoring with its regular representation.

**Branch 2, finite index.**  A finite-index overgroup `G>=Lambda` contains
the normal core `N`, which has finite index in `G` and is a subgroup of the
hyperlinear `Lambda`.  An extension of a hyperlinear normal subgroup by a
finite (amenable) quotient is hyperlinear (Arzhantseva--Berlai--Finn-Sell--Glebsky,
arXiv:1802.04688, Corollary 1.1, as imported in
`ascending-hnn-cannot-create-nonhyperlinearity`).  Canonical microstates of
`G` have vanishing relator energy, canonical packet traces, and retain
`||lambda_G(z(1-a))||_2=||lambda_Lambda(z(1-a))||_2`, since the restriction
of `lambda_G` to `Lambda` is a multiple of `lambda_Lambda`.

**Head-blindness of the earlier fences.**  This is
`toeplitz-schur-packet-center-dies-in-residually-finite-models`: the
finite-quotient countermodels require separating the packet, which lies
partly in the infinite simple finitary subgroup `L` of the finite residual.

**One-endomorphism self-similarity.**  If `Lambda` is hyperlinear and
`alpha` is injective, the ascending HNN extension `H` is hyperlinear.
Restricting its microstates to `Lambda` and to the stable letter gives
canonical base microstates `rho` and unitaries `U` with
`U rho(g) U^* ~ rho(alpha g)` on windows.  The several-compressor statement
in the gate paragraph is not claimed; it is recorded as open.
