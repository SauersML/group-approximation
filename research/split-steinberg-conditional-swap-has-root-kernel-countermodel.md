**ESTABLISHED SHARP SPLIT-COMPILER NO-GO.**  Let `K_str` be the external
group generated by the shared structural BCS tuple and the finite role
packet.  The orthogonal aggregate is the finite-support rational group-ring
idempotent

```text
p=B_f=sum_i R_i P_i in C[K_str],   p^2=p=p^*.           (SCW1)
```

The smallest additive Steinberg recombination actually has the desired
corner geometry.  In two stabilized coordinates put

```text
x_12(p)=[[1,p],[0,1]],       x_21(-p)=[[1,0],[-p,1]],
W_p=x_12(p)x_21(-p)x_12(p)
   =[[1-p,p],[-p,1-p]].                                 (SCW2)
```

Then

```text
W_p^*=W_p^(-1),       W_p^2=diag(1-2p,1-2p),
W_p diag(p,0) W_p^*=diag(0,p).                          (SCW3)
```

Thus a genuine coefficient representation gives complete rather than merely
fractional escape.  Moreover orthogonality of the summands in `(SCW1)` makes

```text
x_12(p)=product_i x_12(R_iP_i),                         (SCW4)
```

so this is already the minimal multi-piece, non-literal-reflection use of
Steinberg addition.  A third coordinate or further multiplication triangles

epsilon(k)=k,                 epsilon(x_rs(a))=1.        (SCW5)
