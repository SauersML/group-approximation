title: Every infinite simple group has infinite conjugacy classes
---
Every infinite simple group `G` is ICC: each nontrivial conjugacy class is infinite.
See `infinite-simple-groups-are-icc-proof`. So its group von Neumann algebra `L(G)`
is a II_1 factor.
