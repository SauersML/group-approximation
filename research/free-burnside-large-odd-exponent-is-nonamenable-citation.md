Literature import, not a derivation.

Pestov, *Hyperlinear and sofic groups: a brief guide*, arXiv:0804.3968v8,
§9, p. 22, checked against the text on 2026-09-13:

> The free Burnside group of exponent n is the quotient of the free group Fm
> on m ≥ 2 generators by the normal subgroup generated by the n-th powers of
> elements of Fm. As shown by Adian [2], free Burnside groups of sufficiently
> large odd exponent are non-amenable.

Adian's monograph itself was not re-read. Infinitude follows because every
finite group is amenable.
