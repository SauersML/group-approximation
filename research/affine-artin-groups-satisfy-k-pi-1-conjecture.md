(types `Ã_n, B̃_n, C̃_n, D̃_n, Ẽ_6, Ẽ_7, Ẽ_8, F̃_4, G̃_2`), then the orbit configuration space of
`W_Γ` is a `K(A_Γ, 1)`. In particular affine Artin groups are torsion-free and have finite
classifying spaces.
