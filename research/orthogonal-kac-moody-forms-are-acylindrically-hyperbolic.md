Caprace--Hume prove that orthogonal forms of Kac--Moody groups over arbitrary
fields are acylindrically hyperbolic.  More precisely, their building-action
criterion applies to a group acting on an irreducible non-spherical
non-affine building when a chamber has finite stabilizer and its orbit
contains an apartment, and they verify that criterion for the orthogonal
forms.

This supplies legitimate source groups for Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana Theorem
A(1).  It does not identify an orthogonal form with the centre-free split
Caprace--Remy twin-building lattices in
`simple-kazhdan-kac-moody-lattices-exist`, and it proves no classification of
characters.  Consequently the shared phrase “Kac--Moody group” cannot splice
the CDI source property to character rigidity of another family.

Primary source: P.-E. Caprace and D. Hume,
[*Orthogonal forms of Kac--Moody groups are acylindrically hyperbolic*](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/aif.2998/),
abstract and main building-action criterion.
