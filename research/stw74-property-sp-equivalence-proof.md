If `A` is purely infinite, every nonzero hereditary subalgebra contains an
infinite projection, so `A` has property (SP).

Conversely, suppose `A` has property (SP), and let `D` be a nonzero hereditary
subalgebra.  Choose a nonzero projection `p in D`.  The hypothesis of Problem
LXXIV makes `p` infinite, and
`stw74-simple-infinite-projections-are-properly-infinite` strengthens it to a
properly infinite projection.  Thus every nonzero hereditary subalgebra of
the simple algebra `A` contains an infinite projection.  The hereditary
criterion for pure infiniteness now gives that `A` is purely infinite.
