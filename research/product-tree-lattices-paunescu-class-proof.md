# Proof

Artifact Section 4.1 and 4.2.

1. **A reducible lattice.** Let `Δ_p = C_2 * ... * C_2` (`p` factors), generated by
   involutions `s_1, ..., s_p`. Its Cayley graph for these generators is the
   `p`-regular tree: each vertex has `p` neighbours, and a reduced word has no
   backtracking, so the graph has no cycles. So `Δ_p` acts simply transitively on
   the vertices of `T_p`. The product `Δ_p × Δ_q` is discrete in `L` with trivial
   vertex stabilizers and one vertex orbit, so it is a cocompact lattice.
2. **Commensurable with `F_2 × F_2`.** The kernel `K_p` of `Δ_p → (C_2)^p`,
   `s_i ↦ e_i`, has index `2^p`. An element of finite order in a free product is
   conjugate into a factor, and every conjugate of `s_i` maps to `e_i ≠ 0`, so
   `K_p` is torsion free, hence free by Kurosh's theorem, of rank at least `2`
   because `Δ_p` is not virtually cyclic. Every free group `F_a` of rank `a >= 2`
   is isomorphic to a subgroup of index `a − 1` in `F_2`. So `Δ_p × Δ_q` and
   `F_2 × F_2` share the finite-index subgroup `K_p × K_q`.
3. **Couple.** Any two lattices of the unimodular lcsc group `L` are measure
   equivalent through `(L, Haar)` with `(γ, λ)·ω = γωλ^(-1)`. By
   `paunescu-class-is-measure-equivalence-invariant`, `Γ ∈ 𝒮` iff
   `Δ_p × Δ_q ∈ 𝒮`.
4. **Commensurability.** By `sofic-action-class-closed-under-commensurability`
   (subgroups, and finite-index overgroups), `Δ_p × Δ_q ∈ 𝒮` iff
   `K_p × K_q ∈ 𝒮` iff `F_2 × F_2 ∈ 𝒮`.

**Consequence 1.** A group with a sofic p.m.p. action is sofic (the images of the
`u_g` form a sofic representation, Kun--Thom p. 4). The fundamental group of a
one-vertex complete square complex whose horizontal and vertical links have `p`
and `q` vertices acts simply transitively on the vertices of `T_p × T_q`, so it is
a cocompact lattice in such an `L`.

**Consequence 2.** `F_2 × F_2` has the Haagerup property, subgroups of Haagerup
groups are Haagerup, and infinite Kazhdan groups are not Haagerup.
