There is a Bernoulli base X for Gamma=F_r x C_M, a free finite extension
Y->X, and a connected generating source treeing Phi inside the lifted
conditional-support graphing H, such that determinantal deletion with
the relative operator K leaves infinitely many connected components
almost surely. Here K=P_Z(H) is already a projection and has trace
c(H)-c(Phi). The source cost is exactly 1+(r-1)/M.

Sections 2 and 3 give the construction and a successful alternative
using a Borel representative of each finite cyclic block. The latter
shows that this is an obstruction to the specified determinantal law,
not to every rounding algorithm or to Fixed Price. No claim is made
that the disconnected law requires a positive repair cost.
