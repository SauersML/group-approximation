Let `G` be a countable group and let `N` be an infinite normal subgroup of infinite index with `cost(N) < ∞`. Then `cost(G) = 1`, where `cost` is the infimum over free p.m.p. actions. This is an imported published result (Gaboriau 2002, Theorem 3.4, in the finite-cost form). It gives no statement about every action.
