For the retraction `H_31 -> Q=PSL_2(31)` that sends `c` to `1`,
let `K` denote the kernel. Then

```
K/[K,K] = C_5,
```

generated by the class of c, with trivial conjugation action of Q.
Equivalently the cyclic F5[Q]-module generated by v subject to
`(a-1)v=0` and `(b-1)^2v=0` has dimension one.

The exact relation matrix has 14880 rows and 2976 columns over F5.
Flint rank computation and an independent standard-library sparse
elimination both give rank 2975. The latter certificate records
2975 distinct invertible pivots and eliminates all rows. This is
a computational proof of this specified finite module, not a
classification of every finite quotient of H31.
