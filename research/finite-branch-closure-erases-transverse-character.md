Let `V` be a finite-dimensional vector space over `F_p`, let `T:V->V` be
injective with `T(V) subset V`, and let `eta in V^*` satisfy

```text
eta composed_with T = 0.                                  (FBC1)
```

Then `eta=0`.

For the Leavitt branch `a |-> a t_0`, the transverse shear character lies in
the kernel of dual pullback.  Hence no nonzero finite coefficient packet can
both be closed under that branch and retain the transverse direction that
creates the exact Heisenberg multiplicity doubling.  The infinite coefficient
reservoir in that proof is essential, not an artifact of presentation.
