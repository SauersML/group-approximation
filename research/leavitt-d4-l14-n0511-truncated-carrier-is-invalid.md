The reductions formerly recorded as
`leavitt-d4-l14-n0511-branch-i-needs-nontrivial-carrier` and
`leavitt-d4-l14-n0511-five-branches-share-one-core` are invalid and have
been removed.  They read the carrier without its copy-zero and copy-three
endpoint syllables.

In the notation

```text
a=g1, i=g10, j=g11, k=g12,
```

the correct branch-I carrier is

```text
H=j_0 k_1 a^-1_2 i_3,
```

not `k_1 a^-1_2`.  Consequently the claimed periodic eighteen-syllable
boundary and its six identity strata do not follow.  The independently
verified nineteen-syllable cancellation-tree analysis of branch I remains
valid; branches II--V must be derived from the full four-copy carrier.
