Every `H_beta` in the fixed-slope seam family, with intercept outside
the rotation-boundary orbit, is Hopfian and is not finitely presented.
This includes the members with decidable word problem. Nearby distinct
intercepts agree on any prescribed finite list of marked relations,
but cannot be marked quotients of one another because both are non-MF
and every proper quotient is MF.
