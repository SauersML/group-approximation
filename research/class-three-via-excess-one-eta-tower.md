The first prerequisite gives unitaries u_1, u_2, v at stage zero with a
nonzero triple commutator. If the second prerequisite holds, that class
stays nonzero at every stage of a simple dense-tail twisted tower.

The component group of the limit is the direct limit of the stage groups,
and a class that is nonzero at every stage is nonzero in the limit. So the
triple commutator lies outside U0 of the simple limit.
