`nV` is a subgroup of itself and is infinite. If every subgroup of `nV` with
property (T) is finite (`kazhdan-subgroups-of-brin-thompson-groups-are-finite`),
then `nV` does not have property (T). This route is weaker than the route from
a-T-menability, since the finiteness claim is itself implied by
a-T-menability. It is recorded because the finiteness claim might be proved
without a proper action.
