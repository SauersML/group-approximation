## Why sufficient

Choose `ts = 1 ≠ st` and let `S` be the unital subring generated by `s, t`,
which is finitely generated, so `L = EL_3(S)` has property (T)
(Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain, Theorem 1.1; literature input).  By
[[one-sided-inverse-elementary-centralizer-defect]] the compressor
`u ∈ EL_4(S) ≤ EL_n(R)` satisfies `u L u⁻¹ ≤ L`, and every off-diagonal
entry of a compressed matrix has the form `sat`, annihilated on the left by
`e = 1 - st ≠ 0`, while `e_{12}(1)` has an entry `1` with `e · 1 ≠ 0`; so the
compression is strict.  `EL_n(R)` is countable.  Apply
[[maximal-group-cstar-infinite-under-strict-compression]] to `EL_n(R)`, `L`,
and `u`.
