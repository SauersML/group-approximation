Sources, read from the arXiv LaTeX sources on MSI on 2026-09-12:

- D. R. Farkas and P. A. Linnell, *Congruence subgroups and the Atiyah
  conjecture*, arXiv:math/0511747v2, Theorems 1.1 (division ring
  `Qbar[G] <= D(G) <= U(G)` for torsion-free `G` containing `CS(u,d,p)` as a
  normal subgroup of finite index), 1.2 and 1.3 (`k[CS(u,d,p)]` is a domain in
  characteristic `p` and `0`).
- S. P. Fisher and A. Ng, arXiv:2606.19606v1, Section 2, Theorem
  `thm:p-adic-atiyah` (torsion-free compact `p`-adic analytic groups satisfy
  Strong Atiyah over `C`, via Farkas--Linnell and Jaikin-Zapirain's base-change
  theorem for sofic groups), and the remark that Strong Atiyah passes to
  subgroups of torsion-free groups.

The passage from `CS(u,d,p)` to subgroups and to torsion-free open subgroups of
`SL_d(Z_p)` is written in the target node.
