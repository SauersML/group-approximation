The notation is that of the target claim.

**1. π is a ring homomorphism.**
- On LC(X,F_2), π(f) = diag(f(T^n x))_n is multiplicative and additive.
- σπ(f)σ^{-1} δ_n = σπ(f)δ_{n-1} = f(T^{n-1}x)δ_n = π(f∘T^{-1})δ_n.
- So σπ(f)σ^{-1} = π(ufu^{-1}), with σ invertible.
- Define π(Σ_j f_j u^j) = Σ_j π(f_j)σ^j. This is well defined, since R is a free left LC(X,F_2)-module on the u^j.
- It is multiplicative: (fu^i)(gu^j) = f(g∘T^{-i})u^{i+j} maps to π(f)σ^iπ(g)σ^{-i}σ^{i+j} = π(f)σ^iπ(g)σ^j.

**2. The image is R_x.**
- π(u^{±1}) = σ^{±1}.
- π(e_a) δ_n = [(T^n x)_0 = a] δ_n = [x_n = a] δ_n = D_a δ_n, where e_a = e_{[x_0=a]}.
- R is generated as a ring by u, u^{-1} and the e_a: the translates u^{-t} e_a u^t = e_{[x_t = a]} and their products give every cylinder indicator, and the cylinder indicators span LC(X,F_2). So π(R) is the ring generated by σ^{±1} and the D_a, which is R_x.

**3. π is injective.**
- Let r = Σ_j f_j u^j ≠ 0, and pick j_0 with f_{j_0} ≠ 0.
- Choose a nonempty clopen set W ⊆ X with f_{j_0} = 1 on W. Since the orbit of x is dense in X, there is m ∈ Z with T^{m+j_0}x ∈ W.
- Then π(r)δ_m = Σ_j f_j(T^{m+j}x) δ_{m+j}.
- The vectors δ_{m+j} for distinct j are distinct basis vectors, and the coefficient of δ_{m+j_0} is f_{j_0}(T^{m+j_0}x) = 1.
- So π(r)δ_m ≠ 0.

**4. Consequences.**
- X is the set of y ∈ A^Z all of whose finite words occur in x, so R ≅ R_x depends only on L(x).
- EL_n and GL_n are functorial under ring isomorphisms, so EL_n(R_x) ≅ EL_n(R).

**5. Model tests.**
- x periodic with primitive period word w of length N:
  - X is one orbit of N points, and R ≅ M_N(F_2[t,t^{-1}]) with t = u^N. So R_x ≅ M_N(F_2[t,t^{-1}]).
  - On V, the operator σ^N commutes with every D_a, and σ^N ≠ 1, consistent with t.
  - So EL_3(R_x) = SL_{3N}(F_2[t,t^{-1}]): infinite, residually finite, not simple. The right answer.
- x the Fibonacci word:
  - X is the Fibonacci subshift, and R_x is the note's ring.
  - Here R_x is generated by σ^{±1} and D_1 alone, since D_0 = 1 − D_1.
- Trivial model x constant:
  - R_x = F_2[σ^{±1}] and G_x = SL_3(F_2[t,t^{-1}]). Not simple, correctly.
