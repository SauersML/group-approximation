**Source.** M. Steenbock, *Rips-Segev torsion-free groups without the unique
product property*, J. Algebra (2015), doi:10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.05.004,
arXiv:1307.0981. TeX source fetched from arXiv and read on 2026-09-13.

**Verbatim (introduction, theorem `gh`):** "The Rips-Segev torsion-free groups
without the unique product property are Gromov hyperbolic."

**Verbatim (abstract):** "Our graphical small cancellation theorem then provides
first examples of Gromov hyperbolic groups without the unique product property."

The proof there shows the Rips--Segev presentations satisfy the graphical
metric small cancellation condition with respect to the free product length,
over a free product of torsion-free groups, and proves hyperbolicity for such
presentations when the factors are hyperbolic and the graph family is finite.
It was not re-derived here.
