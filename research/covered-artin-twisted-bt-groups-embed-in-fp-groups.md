Let `A = A_Γ` be an Artin group satisfying the hypothesis of
`artin-wp-from-covered-maximal-free-of-infinity-subgraphs`. That is, every maximal
free-of-infinity subset of the vertex set splits, along labels 2, into pieces that are:
- right-angled;
- spherical;
- irreducible euclidean;
- of type FC;
- of large type;
- of dimension at most 2;
- of rank three;
- of cyclic type;
- with no `x, y, z` satisfying `m_xy = 3`, `m_xz = 2` and `m_yz ∈ {2, 3, 4}`.

**Statement.** The twisted Brin–Thompson group `AV_A`, for the translation action, embeds
in a finitely presented group.

See route `covered-artin-twisted-bt-groups-embed-in-fp-groups-proof`.

This extends the partial yes answer to Zaremsky Problem 3.8. What it leaves of the problem
is the word problem of `every-free-of-infinity-artin-group-has-solvable-wp` for connected
Dynkin diagrams outside the classes above.
