**OPEN.** Assume every infinite finitely generated group has fixed price.
Let `Gamma` be a countably infinite group that is not generated by finitely
many amenable subgroups. Prove that `Gamma` has fixed price.

Equivalently, `Gamma` is not of the form `<A_1, ..., A_m, S>` with the `A_i`
amenable and `S` finite. In particular, `Gamma` is not finitely generated and
not amenable.

**What is known about a counterexample.** Under the hypothesis, a countably
infinite group without fixed price:
- lies in this class (`fpbs-fixed-price-amenably-generated-from-fg`);
- is not locally finite, has `C(Gamma) < infinity`, and has shape (A) (no
  fixed pseudocost) or shape (B) (a free action of infinite cost). This is
  Theorem G of `research/artifacts/fpbs/docs/fixed-price-countable-passage.md`.

## Attempts

**Amalgamation descent reaches exactly the complementary class (September
16, 2026).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-amalgam-descent-amenably-generated-2026-09-16.md`.
- Its Theorem 1 descends fixed price from `G = Gamma *_A B` to `Gamma` when
  `A` is infinite amenable and both factors have only finite-cost free
  actions.
- Its Proposition 4 proves that if `Gamma *_A B` is finitely generated, then
  `Gamma = <A, S>` for a finite `S`. So one descent step from a finitely
  generated group reaches only groups generated by finitely many amenable
  subgroups, and this claim is exactly what that method leaves.
- Not analyzed, and possible ways further:
  - longer towers where only the top group is finitely generated (Proposition
    4 does not propagate downward, since amenable subgroups of an amalgam need
    not be conjugate into a factor);
  - HNN extensions and finite graphs of groups with amenable edge groups
    (Gaboriau's lecture notes, Cor 2.32, gives the HNN cost formula);
  - supergroups in which `Gamma` is normal rather than a factor.

**A test group (September 16, 2026).** Artifact §5.1 builds
`Gamma_mal = lim (F_2 -> F_2 -> ...)` along `a -> a`, `b -> b a b^-2`.
- It is countable, locally free and not finitely generated.
- The stage subgroups are malnormal, by a Stallings-graph certificate.
- All its amenable subgroups are cyclic, so it lies in this class.
- It has no normal, commensurated or chain-base finitely generated infinite
  subgroup, so Proposition F of the passage artifact does not apply.
- `c* = 2`.

The finitely generated subgroups of `Gamma_mal` are free, so the hypothesis
holds for them unconditionally. So any argument that applies the hypothesis
only to subgroups of `Gamma_mal` would prove the unconditional statement
"`Gamma_mal` has fixed price" outright. An argument applying it to a
finitely generated supergroup, as the amenably generated case does, is not
limited in this way. Neither artifact decides the implication for
`Gamma_mal`. The natural finitely generated supergroup
`<a, b, t | t a t^-1 = a, t b t^-1 = b a b^-2>` contains it as a normal
subgroup of infinite index (not re-derived) and is an HNN extension over a
nonamenable subgroup. So neither Theorem 1 nor the HNN formula over amenable
subgroups applies.

**Locally free groups: shape (A) is impossible (September 17, 2026).**
Artifact `research/artifacts/fpbs-locally-free-fixed-pseudocost-2026-09-17.md`.
Two results are unconditional.
- `locally-free-groups-first-l2-betti-equals-c-star-minus-one` (ESTABLISHED):
  for every countable locally free `Γ`, `beta_1^(2)(Γ) = c*(Γ) − 1`.
  - The minimal-rank exhaustion has compressed stages. These are
    `L²`-independent by Jaikin-Zapirain, arXiv:2403.09515, so the colimit
    loses no `L²`-homology.
- `fpbs-locally-free-free-action-costs-are-c-star-or-infinite` (ESTABLISHED):
  every free action has cost `c*` or `∞` and pseudocost at most `c*`. If any
  free cost is finite, then `C(Γ) = c*` and all pseudocosts equal `c*`.

For locally free groups, the chain `1 + beta_1 ≤ C(Γ) ≤ PC(s_Γ) ≤ c*` of
Theorem G collapses. So:
- (L) holds;
- shape (A) cannot occur;
- a locally free counterexample is exactly a group with free actions of costs
  `c*` and `∞` (shape (B)).

For `Γ_mal`, every free action has cost 2 or ∞.

This does not decide the claim, even for `Γ_mal`. The remaining statement is
`fpbs-locally-free-free-actions-have-finite-cost` (OPEN). It feeds
`fpbs-locally-free-groups-have-fixed-price` (OPEN) through route
`fpbs-locally-free-fixed-price-from-finite-cost`.

The method is specific to locally free groups. It needs that a minimal-rank
finitely generated supergroup exists and that its stages are
`L²`-independent. For other groups in this class, no analogue of Theorem L
(`beta_1 = c* − 1`) is available, and shape (A) is not excluded.

**Exhaustion.** See the attempts on
`fpbs-fixed-price-countable-from-finitely-generated`: exhausting `Gamma` by
finitely generated subgroups bounds pseudocost from above only, and intermediate
relations between subgroup orbit relations are not controlled by the
hypothesis.

**Longer towers and graphs of groups: what tower descent can reach (September
17, 2026, swarm-0917-w2-fp-pull-3, reframing).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-amenable-edge-tower-reach-2026-09-17.md`. This bounds
every descent of the Theorem 1 kind along a finite tower. In such a tower
`K_0 = Gamma`, and each `K_j` is the fundamental group of a finite graph of
groups with amenable edges, with `K_{j-1}` as a vertex group and `K_n`
finitely generated. The bound holds whatever descent step is used.
- **Theorem A (established, `fpbs-amenable-edge-towers-reach-only-class-t`).**
  `Gamma` lies in `T`, the class of groups killed by finitely many iterated
  normal closures of amenable subgroups. `T` is closed under quotients, and
  infinite free products of nontrivial groups are not in `T`.
  - So no tower of any height reaches a group that surjects onto `F_inf`, for
    example any extension `N -> Gamma ->> F_inf`.
  - The step where every such attempt dies is the bottom of the quotient
    chain: infinitely many free factors survive.
- **Theorem B (established in the artifact).** It extends Proposition 4 to
  HNN extensions and finite graphs of groups. If the fundamental group is
  finitely generated, every vertex group is generated by its incident edge
  images together with a finite set, so it lies in `AG`.
- **Theorem C (artifact §3).** Suppose the vertex group `Gamma` satisfies:
  - **(FH):** for all incident edge images `A`, `A'`, the set
    `{x : A ∩ x A' x^-1 ≠ 1}` lies in a finitely generated subgroup.

  Then fundamental group in `AG` implies `Gamma in AG`.
  - The proof sorts amenable subgroups of the fundamental group: those fixing
    a vertex, those fixing an end (the letters along the fixed ray lie in
    the hull), and those preserving a line.
  - It is complete modulo the classical tree trichotomy for amenable groups
    (Pays–Valette 1991), cited without a verbatim quotation.
  - `Gamma_mal` satisfies (FH): its centralisers are cyclic and its maximal
    cyclic subgroups are malnormal. **So no tower of height `<= 2` reaches
    `Gamma_mal`.**
- **Where it stops.**
  - `Gamma_mal in T_2`, since `Gamma_mal/<<a>>` is cyclic. So Theorem A does
    not exclude it.
  - At height 3, (FH) can fail at the vertex `K_1 = Gamma_mal *_{<w>} B_1`,
    because an edge group `A_2 <= B_1` can have a commensurator in `B_1` that
    is not finitely generated. The Theorem C induction therefore does not
    propagate.
  - Whether a height-3 tower reaches `Gamma_mal` is open.

**Contact repair from subgroup orbit relations dies across almost malnormal
subgroups (September 17, 2026).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-finite-contact-obstruction-2026-09-17.md`.
- **Result (ESTABLISHED,
  `fpbs-malnormal-subgroup-relations-finite-contacts`).** Take any
  free action, any subgroup `Lambda` and any finite-cost graphing `Phi`. The
  number of `Phi`-edges between the classes `Lambda x` and `Lambda gamma x`
  has integral at most `2 |Lambda ∩ gamma^{-1} Lambda gamma| c(Phi)`.
- **Consequence for the criterion.** In the infinite-contact criterion
  `fpbs-infinite-contact-zero-relative-cost`, take `S` inside
  `E_{a|Lambda}`. The class graph then stays inside
  `<Q(Lambda)>`-orbits, where `Q(Lambda)` is the set of `gamma` with
  `Lambda ∩ gamma^{-1} Lambda gamma` infinite. So the criterion can apply
  only when `W_1(Lambda) = <Q(Lambda)> = Gamma`. This forces `Lambda` to be
  wq-normal, and it holds for commensurated anchors, which is the
  Proposition F regime.
- **`Gamma_mal`.** Its stages are malnormal. So every subgroup inside a stage
  (every finitely generated subgroup, and the stepwise pairs
  `(E_{H_{n+1}}, E_{H_n})`) fails the criterion for every action and every
  graphing.
- **What is killed.** This kills repairing `E_a` from a subgroup orbit relation
  by infinite contacts in this class, whenever the anchor lies in a proper
  almost malnormal subgroup. Only subrelations not contained in any such
  subgroup orbit relation remain. It is not a lower bound on relative cost:
  the `L²` relative cokernel bound is 0 for the stage pairs.
- **Structural consequence.** For a free action of `Gamma_mal`, every
  finite-cost graphing has almost surely finitely many edges between any two
  distinct classes of any stage orbit relation.
- **Cross-check of `beta_1^(2)(Gamma_mal) = 1`.** Lemma
  `fpbs-homogeneous-group-ring-symbols-l2-injective` (ESTABLISHED) says that
  `1 - T`, with `T` homogeneous of nonzero height, is `ℓ²`-injective with
  dense range. The proof uses a circle action and Kaplansky's trace formula.
  The stage Fox matrix of `Gamma_mal` is triangular with diagonal
  `1, 1 - b a b^{-1} - b a b^{-2}`, as recomputed by
  `experiments/fpbs-locally-free-malnormal-union-2026-09-17/fox_homogeneity.py`.
  So the stage maps are injective on `H_1(-; N(Gamma_mal))`. Lück's colimit
  theorem then gives `beta_1^(2) = 1`, independently of Linnell's theorem and
  Jaikin-Zapirain's `L²`-independence. With Gaboriau's inequality and
  Tucker-Drob Prop 6.7(1), every free cost is 2 or ∞, and the claim for
  `Gamma_mal` is the finiteness of free costs.

**Shape (A) is a statement about finitely generated groups (September 17,
2026, b-t-nonamgen, quantifier shift).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-sandwich-cost-quantifier-shift-2026-09-17.md`.
- **Localization (ESTABLISHED,
  `fpbs-pseudocost-localizes-to-fg-sandwich-costs`).**
  - Tucker-Drob Lemma 6.2 localizes sandwich relations
    `E_{a|<F>} ⊆ E ⊆ E_a` into finitely generated subgroups.
  - Cor 6.19 reads pseudocost off them.
  - `s_Gamma|Delta ≅ s_Delta` by regrouping coordinates along cosets.

  Together these give, for every countable `Gamma` and every action `a`,
  `PC(a) = sup_F inf_{Delta f.g. ⊇ F} p^Delta_F(a|Delta)` and
  `PC(s_Gamma) = sup_F inf_Delta p^Delta_F(s_Delta)`. The Tucker-Drob
  statements are imported as `tucker-drob-sandwich-sets-open-at-free-actions`
  (ESTABLISHED).
- **Quantifier shift.** Consider the OPEN finitely generated statement
  `fpbs-relative-sandwich-cost-bernoulli-lower-bound` (RBL):
  `p^Delta_F(s_Delta) ≤ p^Delta_F(a)` for every finitely generated `Delta`,
  every finite `F ⊆ Delta` and every free `a`.
  - RBL gives fixed pseudocost for every countably infinite group, with no
    hypothesis H (route `fpbs-fixed-pseudocost-from-relative-sandwich-bound`
    to the OPEN `fpbs-countable-groups-have-fixed-pseudocost`). So shape (A) of
    Theorem G is excluded everywhere by a statement about finitely generated
    groups.
  - H is the instance where `F` generates `Delta`, so RBL implies H. The
    passage artifact's remark that shape (A) is invisible to subgroup
    arguments is true for H, but not for RBL.
- **New decomposition of the flagship.** Route
  `fpbs-fixed-price-from-fixed-pseudocost-and-finite-costs` proves
  `fpbs-fixed-price-universal` from fixed pseudocost and the OPEN
  `fpbs-finite-cost-groups-free-actions-have-finite-cost` (U_fin), bypassing
  this claim and the countable passage.
  - The two premises fail independently: they are shapes (A) and (B).
  - The flagship implies U_fin, and it implies fixed pseudocost at groups of
    finite cost.
- **Consequence for this claim.** Under RBL, this claim is equivalent to U_fin
  for groups not generated by finitely many amenable subgroups. That is shape
  (B) alone, a free-action instance of Tucker-Drob Q 7.6, which is not reduced
  to finitely generated groups. Quotient and retract descents toward U_fin are
  void, because quotients of amenably generated groups are amenably generated.
- **Open.**
  - Whether H, or the flagship, implies RBL.
  - Any case of RBL beyond finite or amenable `Delta`. The natural first case
    is `F_2` with `F` generating a free subgroup of rank 2 and infinite index.
**Calibration: malnormal exhaustion is blind to cost (September 17, 2026,
b-p-fp3, calibrator).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-malnormal-exhaustion-calibration-2026-09-17.md`.
Node `fpbs-malnormal-exhaustion-blind-to-cost` (ESTABLISHED).
- **World W1.** `W1 = ker(F_2 -> Z)` shares every lattice and extension
  feature used for `Gamma_mal`:
  - it is locally free;
  - every finitely generated subgroup lies in a malnormal stage, so it has no
    chain, no finitely generated wq-normal anchor and the same finite-contact
    hypothesis;
  - it is normal in a finitely generated group with quotient `Z`, with a
    shift;
  - under H its finitely generated subgroups give nothing new.

  Yet every free action of `W1` has cost infinity.
- **What is killed.** Every method for `Gamma_mal` whose group-specific inputs
  are only these features, together with H, is dead as one class. Such a
  method would refute H via `W1`. The dying step is any step that turns
  malnormal exhaustion into an upper bound on cost. The invariant it misses is
  `c*`.
- **World W2.** `W2 = Gamma_inf` (T5) has `c* = 2` and fixed price 2, but its
  stages are not malnormal.
- **Separating datum.** For `H_n <= H_m` of rank 2 with `<H_n, t> = H_m`, the
  map `H_n * <t> -> H_m` has nontrivial kernel. Every nontrivial kernel element
  has at least 3 `t`-letters, by malnormality, and 3 is attained by
  `t a t^{-2} b_{m-1}^{-1}` with `t = b_m`. In `W1`, at free-factor stage
  pairs of rank one more, the corresponding map is injective, so there are no
  detours and the floor is 1.
- **Consequence.** A proof must consume these forced relations. Among the
  recorded families only detour packing (for example the open long-generator
  packing growth) and the `L^2`/Fox computation do, and the latter gives only
  lower bounds. Calibration test for new proposals: return infinite cost on
  `W1`, and do not rely on a chain as in `W2`. The claim stays OPEN.

**Extension certificates are circular for finite cost (September 17, 2026,
swarm-0917-w4-fp-nonamenably-gen, inverter).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-extension-certificates-carry-no-cost-information-2026-09-17.md`.
- **Result (`fpbs-extension-routes-to-finite-cost-are-circular`).** Let
  `C(Gamma) < infinity` and let `b` be free. The minimum cost over free
  extensions of `b` is exactly `C(Gamma)`, attained by `b x a_0`. So (U)
  is equivalent to descent of finiteness along product projections.
  Shape (B) holds iff `b x a_0 -> b` has cost `C(Gamma)` over an
  infinite-cost base.
- **What is killed.** This is every route that certifies a bound on the
  cost of an extension `e -> b` and then descends to `b`:
  - products or joinings with auxiliary or Bernoulli actions (`a_0` can be
    taken mixing);
  - coinduction through a finitely generated overgroup, then restriction;
  - measure-equivalence transfer that keeps `b` as a factor.

  The invariant is the cost of a free extension, which is constant at its
  minimum. The dying step is descent along non-atomic fibres. Uniform
  finite fibres do descend.
- **`Gamma_mal`.** Coinduction to `M = F_2 *_phi`, which is finitely
  generated and so has fixed price under the hypothesis, needs two
  descents:
  - (i) from `M`-cost to `Gamma_mal`-cost, which fails for finiteness in
    general (`F_inf x Z` has cost 1);
  - (ii) from the product of twists `(x)_k b o T^k` to `b`, which is (U).
- **Where this leaves the claim.** Both known ways of certifying (U) are
  now closed at `Gamma_mal`. Extension certificates die here, and
  base-measurable repair anchored in almost malnormal subgroup orbit
  relations dies by the finite-contact obstruction. A proof must build a
  graphing on `b` itself, by repair from subrelations that do not lie in
  any almost malnormal subgroup orbit relation. The claim stays OPEN.

**Detour packing has a positive floor at malnormal free stages (September 17,
2026, swarm-0917-w5-fp-nonamenably-gen, inverter).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-malnormal-coset-cycles-linear-perimeter-2026-09-17.md`.
- **Result (ESTABLISHED, `fpbs-malnormal-coset-cycles-linear-perimeter`).**
  Let `H <= F` be finitely generated and malnormal, and `t` outside `H`. A
  simple coset cycle with `k` new edges has perimeter at most
  `B (4k-3) max(D, |t|)`. This closes the linear-length step left open by
  `fpbs-malnormal-coset-cycles-bounded-syllables`.
  - **Proof.** The union tree of the polygon suppresses to at most `4k-3`
    arcs. Each arc has at least two covering sides, so its length is `< D` or
    `<= |t|`. Distinct cosets through an edge have distinct `D`-ball traces,
    so multiplicities are bounded.
  - **Consequences.** The packing growth `h` is finite, and every packing
    certificate (small-cancellation-family (2.1), arbitrary detour words) is
    at least `δ(F,H,t) > 0`.
- **`Gamma_mal`.** The floor is one constant for all pairs `(H_m, H_n)` with
  generator `b_m`, or any generator in `H_n b_m^{±1} H_n`. So stage-sum and
  subsequence packing repairs are dead in every growth regime, not only the
  bounded one. The invariant is the linear perimeter constant. The dying step
  is the divergence step (2.3)–(2.5).
- **Decomposition left.** The only single-generator packing route is long
  generators `t_j`, with `|t_j| -> infinity`, whose floors tend to zero. It is
  recorded as OPEN `fpbs-mal-long-generator-packing-growth`. If it holds,
  every free cost of `Gamma_mal` is finite, hence 2. The claim stays OPEN.

**Shifted stages: relative cost is 0 or infinity, uniformly (September 17,
2026, swarm-0917-w5-pull-fp-2, inverter).** Artifact
`research/artifacts/fpbs-shifted-stage-relative-cost-zero-or-infinite-2026-09-17.md`.
- **Result (ESTABLISHED, `fpbs-shifted-stage-relative-cost-zero-or-infinite`).**
  Let `theta` be an automorphism with `theta(H_n) = H_{n+1}`, and let `a` be a
  theta-invariant free action, for example any Bernoulli shift. Then
  `relC(E_a; E_{a|H_n})` does not depend on `n`, and it is `0` if
  `C(a) < infinity` and `infinity` otherwise.
  - **Proof.** Transport the pairs by `T`, then truncate a finite-cost graphing
    by labels.
  - **For `Gamma_mal`.** The map `b_k -> b_{k+1}` is such a `theta`, so the
    Bernoulli shift has `C = 2` iff `relC(E_beta; E_{beta|H_1}) = 0`.
- **Decomposition left.** Shape (B) at the Bernoulli shift reduces to one
  positive lower bound at one stage, recorded as OPEN
  `fpbs-mal-bernoulli-single-stage-floor`. If it holds, it answers Gaboriau
  Q 2.66 and the second part of Q 2.65, and it refutes
  `fpbs-locally-free-free-actions-have-finite-cost`. The "slowly decaying
  relative cost" regime does not exist for theta-invariant actions.
- **Dead ends tried here, none written as nodes.**
  - **`C*(Gamma_mal) < infinity` by treeability.** No construction: the free
    `R`-tree limits of stage Cayley trees make `a` elliptic, because the
    length of `b_1` in `H_m`, divided by the length of `a`, grows like
    `4^(m-1)`.
  - **Gaboriau's Th. 2.56 applied to `1 -> Gamma_mal -> M -> Z -> 1`.** No
    obstruction: `C*(M) = 1` already follows from rank gradient, since normal
    finite-index `K` with `psi(K) = mZ` have `rank(K) <= 2 + [M:K]/m`.
  - **Linnell ranks.** `H_1(H_n, D) -> H_1(H_{n+1}, D)` is an isomorphism,
    because the Fox derivative `∂w/∂b_{n+1}` of `w = b_{n+1} a b_{n+1}^{-2}` is
    nonzero, hence invertible in `D`. This agrees with the
    `L²`-independence used by Theorem L. So every relative `l2` obstruction
    vanishes.

  The claim stays OPEN.

- **Relative fixed price decomposition (swarm-0917-w6-w6-fp-last1, September 17, 2026; artifact `research/artifacts/fpbs-relative-fixed-price-decomposition-2026-09-17.md`).**
  - **ESTABLISHED lemma** `fpbs-relative-stage-costs-bound-cost-by-stage-liminf`.
    Along a finitely generated subgroup chain, suppose
    `relC(R_m;R_n) <= (C(R_m) - C(R_n))^+`. Then `C(E_a) <= liminf C(R_n)`.
    The proof is self-contained and uses no fixed price input.
  - **OPEN prerequisite** `fpbs-relative-fixed-price-fg-pairs` (RFP). For an
    infinite finitely generated `K <= L` and a free action of `L`, the relative
    cost is at most the positive part of the cost difference. It holds for:
    - amenable `K` (Ershov–Golan–Sapir Lemma 5.7);
    - cost-1 `L`;
    - normal, commensurated or finite-index `K`;
    - free factors.

    The content is in finite-contact (malnormal) pairs.
  - **Under H and RFP**, every free action of every countable group that is
    not locally finite has `C(a) <= c*`. So shape (B) of Theorem G occurs only
    with `c* = infinity`. The target splits into three prerequisites, each of
    which can fail on its own:
    1. RFP;
    2. fixed pseudocost, the shape (A) half;
    3. `C(Gamma) < infinity => c* < infinity` under H.
  - **Locally free groups.** The free-pair case
    `fpbs-relative-fixed-price-free-pairs` gives
    `fpbs-locally-free-free-actions-have-finite-cost` through the new route
    `fpbs-locally-free-finite-cost-from-relative-free-pairs`. This is
    unconditional.
  - **Γ_mal.** At the pair `(F_2, <a, bab^-2>)`, RFP_free at the Bernoulli
    stage action implies `C(beta) = 2`, and the single-stage floor refutes
    RFP_free.
  - **Dead derivations of RFP from H:**
    - the double `L *_K L`;
    - the HNN extension centralizing `K`, which is circular;
    - relative rank gradient, which vanishes.

  The claim stays OPEN.
