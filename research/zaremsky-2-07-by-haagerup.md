If every `nV` (`n >= 2`) has the Haagerup property, the Haagerup part is
answered "yes" for all `n`, and the (T) part is answered "no" for all `n`,
because an infinite a-T-menable group does not have property (T). Both parts of
Zaremsky Problem 2.7 are then decided.
