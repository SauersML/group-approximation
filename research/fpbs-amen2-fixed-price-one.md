Class theorem: bounded exponent is handled by the new subgroup-transfer proof and Suchkova-Shunkov; unbounded exponent for finitely generated groups is published; countable exhaustion uses relC(R_Hj;R_H1)=0 and summable additions. Does NOT settle the universal Fixed Price problem.
