For each finite alphabet `A`: every group is `A`-surjunctive if and only if
every finitely generated infinite simple group with property (T) is
`A`-surjunctive.

"Nonsofic" may be added to the class, because sofic groups are surjunctive.

This is an exact reduction, not a proof of either side. The open restriction is
`fg-simple-kazhdan-groups-are-surjunctive`.
