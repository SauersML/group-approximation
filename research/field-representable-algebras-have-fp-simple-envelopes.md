**ESTABLISHED** through `field-representable-algebras-have-fp-simple-envelopes-proof`
(lane proof, bh-algebra, not reviewed). No priority is claimed: the ingredients are
standard and the reduction is the ring content of the repository's Noether-normalization
routes for linear groups.

**Statement.** Let `K` be any field, `F ⊇ K` any (commutative) field, and `N, m >= 1`. Every
finitely generated `K`-subalgebra `A` of `M_N(F ⊗_K K<x_1, ..., x_m>)` embeds in a finitely
presented simple `K`-algebra, namely `M_n(L_2^{⊗(k+1)})` for suitable `n, k`, where
`L_2 = L_K(1,2)` and `k` is the transcendence degree over `K` of the subfield generated by the
coefficients of generators of `A`.

**Special cases.**
1. Every finitely generated subalgebra of `M_N(F)`, `F` any field extension of `K`: e.g.
   finitely generated subalgebras of `M_N(F_2(t))`, of `M_N` of any function field or number
   field, and of `M_N(F)` for `F` algebraically closed.
2. *(Citation-dependent, not read at source.)* By Anan'in's representability theorem
   (A. Z. Anan'in, Arch. Math. 59 (1992) 1–5: a finitely generated right noetherian PI
   algebra over a field embeds in `M_N(F)` for a field `F ⊇ K`), every finitely generated
   right noetherian PI `K`-algebra, in particular every finitely generated commutative
   `K`-algebra (with nilpotents allowed), embeds in a finitely presented simple `K`-algebra.

**Relation to the root.** This is Attempt 7 of `algebra-boone-higman-conjecture`: the algebra
Boone–Higman conjecture holds for all representable inputs over every field. It does not
touch the complexity benchmark: every covered input embeds in one of the hosts
`M_n(L_2^{⊗(k+1)})`, which share an exponential-time word-problem bound, so monomial inputs
`K<x,y>/(x y^n x : n ∈ X)` with hard decidable `X` remain the test case.

**Group corollary (not new).** Every subgroup of `GL_N(F)` lies in the unit group of a
finitely presented simple `K`-algebra. Boone–Higman for finitely generated linear groups is
already on main (`finitely-generated-linear-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`); this only records
the algebraic host.
