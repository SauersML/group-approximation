**Upper bound.**  Let `mu` be the uniform measure on the multiset `S = X u
X^-1`; it is symmetric and its support generates `G`.  With `P` the
associated Markov operator on `l^2(G)`, `p_{2n}(e) = <P^{2n} delta_e,
delta_e> = N_{2n} / |S|^{2n}`, and `p_{2n}(e) <= ||P||^{2n} = rho^{2n}`,
while `p_{2n}(e)^{1/2n} -> rho` (Kesten, *Symmetric random walks on
groups*, Trans. AMS 92 (1959)).  If `rho = 1` then for every `k` some `n`
has `p_{2n}(e)^{1/2n} >= 1 - 1/k`; conversely if such `n` exist for every
`k` then `rho >= 1 - 1/k` for every `k`, so `rho = 1`.  Kesten's theorem
identifies `rho = 1` with amenability.  This is (AM2).  The predicate
`N_{2n} >= T` is recursively enumerable in `(G, n, T)`: it holds iff there
are `T` distinct words of length `2n` each with a finite derivation from
the relators, and the relators are enumerated from the presentation
index.  A universal quantifier over `k` of a `Sigma^0_1` predicate is
`Pi^0_2`.  For a countably generated presentation, `G` is amenable iff
every subgroup generated by an initial segment `X_m` of the alphabet is
amenable (amenability is local), and the trivial-word counts over
`S_m = X_m u X_m^-1` are counts of trivial words in `G`, so the same form
with an extra universal quantifier over `m` applies.

For the other two predicates: `<u, v>` is free of rank two iff every
nontrivial reduced word in `u^{+-1}, v^{+-1}` is nontrivial in `G`, a
`Pi^0_1` condition (the complement enumerates a trivial one), so
`CONTAINS-F2` is `Sigma^0_2`; metabelian is `Pi^0_2` because "every
double commutator of words is trivial" is a universal quantifier over a
`Sigma^0_1` matrix.

**Hardness.**  `TOT = {e : W_e = N}` is `Pi^0_2`-complete.  The map
`e |-> G_{J_e}` with `J_e = {j + 1 : j in W_e}` is computable (emit
`[a, t^{j+1} a t^{-j-1}]` when `j` is enumerated), and by (SR2)
`G_{J_e}` is amenable iff metabelian iff `J_e = Z_{>0}` iff `e in TOT`,
while it contains `F_2` iff `e notin TOT`.  Hence `TOT <=_m AMENABLE_rec2`,
`TOT <=_m METABELIAN_rec2`, and the complement of `TOT`, which is
`Sigma^0_2`-complete, many-one reduces to `CONTAINS-F2_rec2` and to
`NONAMENABLE_rec2`.  Padding with trivial generators gives any fixed larger
number of generators, and the enumerated-presentation statement follows
because two-generator presentations are enumerated presentations.
