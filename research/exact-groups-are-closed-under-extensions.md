**ESTABLISHED (import).**

**Statement.** Let `N` be a closed normal subgroup of a locally compact group `G`. If `N` and `G/N` are C*-exact, then `G` is C*-exact, and closed subgroups of C*-exact groups are C*-exact. For countable discrete groups, C*-exactness is exactness of `C*_r(G)`, and for finitely generated groups it is equivalent to Property A (Guentner–Kaminker; Ozawa). Amenable groups are exact.

**Source.** E. Kirchberg and S. Wassermann, *Permanence properties of C*-exact groups*, Documenta Math. 4 (1999), 513–558.
