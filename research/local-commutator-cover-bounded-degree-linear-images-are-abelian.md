**ESTABLISHED (written proof; not Lean-verified).**

## Setting

Use the notation of `local-commutator-cover-derived-series-stops-at-one`.

- `G = F/R = <x_1,...,x_r | r_1,...,r_s>` is a finite presentation of a
  nontrivial group with full MF radical.
- `E = F/[R,R]` and `T = E'`.
- `N_L = << [u,v] : u, v in R cap S_L >>` and `Gamma_L = F/N_L`.
- `L_1` and `t_1, ..., t_d` in `[F,F]` are as in that claim.  For
  `L >= L_1` the images of the `t_j` generate `Gamma_L'`, and `Gamma_L'` is
  perfect.

Rings are commutative with `1`.  For a finite simple group `S`, let `m(S)`
be the least degree of a nontrivial representation of `S` over any field.

## Statement

For every `n >= 1` there is an integer `L(n) >= L_1` such that the
following hold for every `L >= L(n)`.

1. **Commutator subgroup.**  Every homomorphism `Gamma_L' -> GL_n(A)`, for
   every commutative ring `A`, is trivial.
2. **Whole cover.**  Every homomorphism `Gamma_L -> GL_n(A)` has abelian
   image and factors through `Gamma_L^ab = Z^r`.  Since `Gamma_L' ->> T != 1`,
   `Gamma_L` has no faithful representation of degree `n` over any
   commutative ring.
3. **Finite quotients.**  Let `P` be a finite quotient of `Gamma_L`.  Then
   no nontrivial quotient of `P'` has a nontrivial homomorphism to any
   `GL_n(A)`.  In particular every simple quotient `S` of `P'` is
   nonabelian and has `m(S) > n`.
4. **Minimal nonabelian quotients.**  Let `P` be a nonabelian finite
   quotient of `Gamma_L` all of whose proper quotients are abelian.  Then:
   - `P' = S^k` is the unique minimal normal subgroup of `P`, with `S`
     nonabelian simple and `m(S) > n`;
   - `C_P(P') = 1`, so `P <= Aut(S) wr Sym(k)`;
   - `P` permutes the `k` factors of `S^k` through an abelian group acting
     regularly.

   Every nonabelian finite quotient of `Gamma_L` maps onto such a `P`.  So
   its commutator subgroup maps onto `S^k` with `m(S) > n`.
5. **Fixed-alphabet wreath models.**  Let `A_0` be a finite group with
   `|A_0| <= n`, let `B` be a finite abelian group, and let `X` be a finite
   `B`-set.  Every homomorphism `Gamma_L -> A_0 wr_X B` has abelian image.

Item 1 holds with the same `L(n)` for every ring, of every characteristic,
at once.  Items 1 and 2 cover unitary representations, since
`U(n) <= GL_n(C)`.  Item 2 in degree `n^2`, that is for `L >= L(n^2)`,
also covers projective representations of degree `n` over a field.  This
is because `PGL_n(K)` acts faithfully on `M_n(K)` by conjugation, and so
embeds in `GL_(n^2)(K)`.

## What this kills

By `abelianized-cover-mf-radical-is-union-of-local-cover-radicals`, `E` is
LEF iff no word of `[F,F] \ [R,R]` lies in the finite residual of any
`Gamma_L`.  So the exact-permutation shadow of `(DF)` is a question about
the finite quotients of the `Gamma_L` for large `L`.

**Invariant.**  The ideal of the trivial point in the representation ring
of `Gamma_L'` in degree `n`.  It is generated by the `n^2 d` entries of
`Y_j - 1`, for the generic matrices `Y_j` attached to the `t_j`.

**Step where every member dies.**  These finitely many generators lie in the
relation ideal `I_L` of `Gamma_L'` once `L >= L(n)`.  After that, every
degree-`n` model of `Gamma_L'` over every ring is the trivial point.

**Members killed, in both directions.**  None of the following can separate
a word of `[F,F] \ [R,R]` in `Gamma_L`, nor certify that such a word lies in
the finite residual.
- Congruence-type quotients `SL_k(Z/N)` and `Sp_(2k)(Z/N)` with `k` bounded.
- `PSL_2(q)`-families and, more generally, any family of finite groups of
  Lie type of bounded rank.  Each is linear of bounded degree through the
  adjoint representation of the adjoint group.
- Alternating quotients `Alt(k)` with `k <= n+1`.
- Bounded-dimensional unitary models.
- Fixed-alphabet cellular-automaton and lamp models `A_0 wr_X B` over
  abelian `B`, with `B` of any size.

This subsumes the solvable-blindness of
`local-commutator-cover-derived-series-stops-at-one` only for solvable
targets of bounded linear degree.  The two results are complementary:
- that one handles unbounded solvable groups with one threshold `L_1`;
- this one handles non-solvable groups of bounded degree, with threshold
  `L(n)`.

## What survives

A finite witness for or against LEF of `E` at level `L` has a
minimal nonabelian quotient `P <= Aut(S) wr Sym(k)` whose socle factor `S`
has `m(S) > n` for every `n` with `L(n) <= L`.

**Interpretation (imported, not part of the established statement).**  By
the classification of finite simple groups, and the lower bounds for
`m(S)` of Landazuri--Seitz and of Wagner, `S` must therefore be:
- an alternating group of large degree, or
- a classical group of large rank,

as `L -> infinity`.  Up to finitely many sporadic exceptions, these are the
only families with unbounded `m(S)`.

The exact-permutation side of `(DF)` now lives entirely in high-rank
and high-degree simple sections.

## What this does not do

- It says nothing about corona or approximate homomorphisms of unbounded
  dimension.  `(DF)` stays OPEN.
- It does not decide whether `E` is LEF.  That would need control of
  quotients of `Gamma_L` whose simple sections are alternating groups of
  unbounded degree, or classical groups of unbounded rank.
- No explicit bound on `L(n)` is given.  It is the level at which
  finitely many ideal-membership certificates first appear, and the proof
  bounds it neither in terms of `n` nor in terms of the presentation of `G`.
