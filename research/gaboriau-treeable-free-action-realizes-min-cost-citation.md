Citation import from D. Gaboriau, *Around the orbit equivalence theory, measure equivalence, cost and ℓ2
Betti numbers*, lecture notes dated October 3, 2025,
https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/ME-Cost-L2-Lectures/ME-Cost-L2-lectures.pdf, Section 2.3,
pages 12–13.

- **Definition 2.18.** "A group is said to be 1. treeable if it admits a free p.m.p. action that is treeable; otherwise, it is non treeable;"
- **Theorem 2.24 (Cost and treeings, Gaboriau [Gab00]).** "If Ψ is a treeing of a p.m.p. equivalence relation R then C(Ψ)=C(R)."
- **Corollary 2.26 (Min-cost, treeability and anti-treeability).** "If Γ admits a free p.m.p. treeable action, then this action realizes the infimum C∗(Γ). In particular, if a non-amenable Γ admits a cost=1 free p.m.p. action, then Γ is non treeable."

des relations d'équivalence et des groupes*, Invent. Math. 139 (2000). Nothing is re-proved here.
