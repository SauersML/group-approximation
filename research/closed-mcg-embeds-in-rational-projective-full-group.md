## Setting

For `m ≥ 1` let `S^m = (R^{m+1} ∖ 0)/R_{>0}` and `Γ_m = GL_{m+1}(Z)`, acting on
`S^m` through its linear action.

- A *rational half-sphere* is `H_ℓ = {[x] : ℓ(x) > 0}` for a nonzero integral
  linear form `ℓ`.
- `B_m` is the Boolean algebra of regular open subsets of `S^m` generated by the
  `H_ℓ`, with join `int cl(u ∪ v)`, meet `u ∩ v` and complement `int(S^m ∖ u)`.
  Its elements are the *rational polyhedral regular open sets*.
- `Ŝ_m` is the Stone space of `B_m`, and `Γ_m` acts on it by homeomorphisms.
- `𝒯_m = [[Γ_m ⋉ Ŝ_m]]` is the topological full group: all homeomorphisms of
  `Ŝ_m` that agree, on each piece of some finite clopen partition, with an
  element of `Γ_m`.
- `Δ = int cone(e_0, …, e_m)` is the standard unimodular simplex, and `Δ` also
  denotes its clopen in `Ŝ_m`.

## Statement

1. **The host.**
   - `Ŝ_m` is a Cantor space, and the action of `Γ_m` on it is topologically free
     and minimal. So `Γ_m ⋉ Ŝ_m` is its own germ groupoid; it is Hausdorff and
     effective.
   - Every nonempty clopen of `Ŝ_m` is a finite disjoint union of translates
     `gΔ`, `g ∈ Γ_m`, and `Δ = g'Δ ⊔ g''Δ`.
   - Hence `𝒯_m` is *full* and *clopen transitive*: any two nonempty clopen sets
     are joined by a `𝒯_m`-local map, as in hypotheses 1 and 2 of
     `fp-clopen-transitive-full-cantor-groups-have-type-a-actions`.
   - `Γ_m ≤ 𝒯_m`, so for `m ≥ 2` the host contains the Kazhdan group
     `SL_{m+1}(Z)`.
2. **The embedding.** Let `g ≥ 2` and `m = 6g − 7`.
   - The action of `Mod(S_g)` on `PML(S_g) ≅ S^m` lifts functorially to an
     action on the rational Cantorization `Ŝ(PML)` by elements of the full group
     of integral projective germs.
   - That full group is isomorphic to `𝒯_m`.
   - For `g ≥ 3` the resulting map `Mod(S_g) → 𝒯_{6g−7}` is injective.
   - For `g = 2` its kernel is the hyperelliptic involution, so every
     torsion-free finite-index subgroup embeds.
3. **Calibration, genus one.**
   - `m = 1`: `SL_2(Z)` acts faithfully on `S^1` (`−I` is the antipodal map), so
     `SL_2(Z) ≤ 𝒯_1`.
   - `𝒯_1` contains `V ≅ V_{2,4}`, acting by prefix replacement on the four
     mediant (Farey) trees of the unimodular quadrant cones.

## Consequence

Boone–Higman for every closed-surface mapping class group of genus `g ≥ 3` now
reduces to a single, surface-independent finiteness statement:
`rational-projective-full-groups-are-finitely-presented` for `m = 6g − 7`. The
route is `closed-mcg-bh-via-rational-projective-full-group`.

In the language of `train-track-trace-groupoid-hosts-closed-mcg`:
- the Serret gate G1 and the regularization gate G2 are not needed for this
  host;
- clopen transitivity G3 is proved here;
- only the finite-presentation gate G4 remains, in the form of the named claim
  above.

## Trust surface

These are recalled from the standard literature and were not re-read at source
in this lane:
- the piecewise integral linear (PIL) structure of `ML(S_g)` given by complete
  train-track charts, with integral points exactly the integral multicurves in
  every chart (Thurston; Penner–Harer);
- `PML(S_g) ≅ S^{6g−7}`;
- the faithfulness of `Mod(S_g)` on `PML` for `g ≥ 3`, and the hyperelliptic
  kernel for `g = 2`;
- the existence of unimodular refinements of rational fans in every dimension
  (toric resolution of singularities).
