## Setting

- `C = {0,1}^ω`, `n >= 1`, and `nV` is Brin's group of homeomorphisms of `C^n`.
  Each is given by finitely many brick prefix replacements
  `(u_1 y_1, ..., u_n y_n) -> (u'_1 y_1, ..., u'_n y_n)`.
- The **slope** of `g` at `x` is `s(g,x) = (|u'_j| - |u_j|)_j ∈ Z^n`, computed
  on any table brick containing `x`. It is well defined and locally constant,
  and it is a cocycle: `s(gh,x) = s(g,hx) + s(h,x)`. This is Step 0 of
  `brin-thompson-exponent-cocycle-proof`.
- For `K <= nV`, the **slope lattice** `L(K) <= Z^n` is the subgroup generated by
  all `s(g,x)` with `g ∈ K` and `x ∈ C^n`. If `K` is generated by a set `S`, then
  `L(K)` is generated by the slopes of the elements of `S`.
- A **sign-definite block decomposition** of `L(K)` consists of the following
  data, such that `L(K) <= Z v^(1) ⊕ ... ⊕ Z v^(k)`:
  - a partition `{1,...,n} = Z_0 ⊔ P_1 ⊔ ... ⊔ P_k`;
  - vectors `v^(i) ∈ Z^n` supported on `P_i` whose entries on `P_i` are all
    strictly positive.

  The multiplier of `v^(i)` may be any integer, of either sign, and may depend
  on the element and the point.

## Statement

Let `K <= nV` be finitely generated, and suppose `L(K)` has a sign-definite
block decomposition with `k` blocks.

1. **Block coding.** `K` is isomorphic to a subgroup of `kV`, through a
   homeomorphism that carries brick prefix replacements to brick prefix
   replacements. For `k = 0`, meaning all slopes vanish, `K` is finite.
2. **One sign-definite line.** Suppose `L(K) <= Z v` for some `v ∈ Z^n` whose
   nonzero entries all have the same sign. Then `K` is isomorphic to a subgroup
   of Thompson's group `V`. Consequently:
   - `K` has the Haagerup property (`thompson-v-has-haagerup-property`);
   - every subgroup of `K` with property (T) is finite;
   - every subgroup of `K` with property FW is finite
     (`fw-subgroups-of-eventually-similar-groups-virtually-embed`, part 2).

## Consequences

- **Shape of an infinite Kazhdan subgroup of nV.** Let `Q <= nV` be infinite
  with property (T). Then `Q` is finitely generated, and `L(Q)` lies on no
  sign-definite line. So `L(Q)` has one of two shapes:
  - **rank at least two** (Robertson–Steger type,
    `rank-two-prefix-replacement-groups-contain-infinite-kazhdan`);
  - **rank one, spanned by a mixed-sign vector** (baker type): some coordinate
    is expanded while another is contracted, in fixed proportion.
- **Target hole.** Take `Q` to be a torsion-free hyperbolic Kazhdan group
  (`torsion-free-hyperbolic-kazhdan-partner-exists`). Any embedding
  `Q -> nV` that proves `every-hyperbolic-group-embeds-in-some-brin-thompson-group`
  for `Q` must produce a slope lattice of one of those two shapes. Every
  construction whose slopes are proportional to one sign-definite scaling
  vector dies at the recoding step, because it lands in `V`. Such constructions
  include:
  - diagonal self-similar codings;
  - codings by a one-dimensional rational similarity structure, run in several
    coordinates with a common expansion rate;
  - codings that use extra coordinates only as bounded finite labels.
- **Unimodular remark.** Suppose instead that every slope of `Q` has coordinate
  sum `0`, which includes the mixed-sign line `Z(a,-a)`. Then brick prefix
  replacements preserve the product Lebesgue measure. By part 1 of
  `kazhdan-subgroups-of-nv-finite-iff-invariant-sets-carry-measures`
  (unreviewed), `U_Q` is then open, invariant and conull. So an infinite `Q` of
  this shape carries all its non-locally-finite dynamics on a closed, nowhere
  dense, Lebesgue-null invariant set.
- **Not reached.** Nothing here excludes infinite Kazhdan subgroups of rank at
  least two, or of a mixed-sign line.
  - Write the line as `v = (a_+, -a_-)`, with positive block sum `A` and negative
    block sum `B`.
  - When `A = B`, the ambient orbits are Diestel–Leader graphs `DL(2^A, 2^A)`,
    which are amenable. A Kazhdan Schreier graph on such an orbit is only a
    bounded-distance subgraph and need not be amenable.
  - When `A ≠ B`, the ambient orbits are non-amenable.

## Scope

- The embedding needs no property (T) and no finite presentation.
- The slope lattice depends on the embedding and is not a conjugacy invariant
  in `nV`. The obstruction is therefore stated for all embeddings at once: an
  infinite Kazhdan group admits no embedding into any `nV` whose slope lattice
  lies on a sign-definite line.
- Not Lean-verified. No novelty is claimed beyond the packaging; block recoding
  of products of shifts is standard in symbolic dynamics.
