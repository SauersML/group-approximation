**ESTABLISHED COMPLEMENTARY-COMPRESSION FENCE.**  Put

```text
p=s_0t_0,                  q=s_1t_1,                  p+q=1,
```

and let `t=bar t_i` be either constant binary Singer actor on its three active
coordinates.  Thus `t^7=1`; for row one the active coordinates are
`(4,7,8)`, and for row two they are `(5,8,9)`.  Apply the two complementary
Leavitt compression endomorphisms entrywise to this constant matrix:

```text
c_0=qI+s_0tt_0=qI+pt,
c_1=pI+s_1tt_1=pI+qt.                                (CCS1)
```

Both are ordinary elementary words: apply the corresponding endomorphism
to any fixed elementary spelling of `t`.  Orthogonality of `p,q` and the
fact that constant binary matrices commute with their scalar copies give

```text
c_0c_1=t,      [c_0,c_1]=[c_0,t]=[c_1,t]=1,
c_0^7=c_1^7=1.                                           (CCS2)
```

For arbitrary integers `a,b`, put `h=c_0^ac_1^b`.  Then

```text
h=pt^a+qt^b.                                             (CCS3)
```

If `h` carries the constant signed Hecke subgroup to the next Singer
subgroup,

```text
hL_0h^(-1)=tL_0t^(-1),                                  (CCS4)
```

then necessarily

```text
a=b=1 mod 7,                  h=t.                      (CCS5)
```

For every `d in L_0`, `(CCS3)` gives

```text
h d h^(-1)
 =p t^a d t^(-a)+q t^b d t^(-b).                      (CCS6)
```

Every element of the target in `(CCS4)` is a constant binary matrix.
Separating `(CCS6)` on the two nonzero orthogonal idempotents `p,q` therefore
shows that both `t^a d t^(-a)` and `t^b d t^(-b)` belong to
`tL_0t^(-1)`.  Cardinality gives

```text
t^aL_0t^(-a)=t^bL_0t^(-b)=tL_0t^(-1).                (CCS7)
```

For row one, the common fixed-vector space of `L_0` is
`<e_2,e_7>`.  The Singer actor fixes `e_2` and acts freely on the seven
nonzero vectors of its active `(4,7,8)` block.  Equality `(CCS7)` therefore
forces `t^ae_7=t e_7` and `t^be_7=t e_7`, hence `a=b=1 mod 7`.
For row two use the common fixed-covector space
`<e_6^*,e_9^*>`: `t` fixes `e_6^*` and acts freely on the nonzero covectors
of its active `(5,8,9)` block.  Equality of the spaces
`<e_6^*,e_9^*t^(-a)>` and `<e_6^*,e_9^*t^(-1)>` gives the same conclusion.
This proves `(CCS5)`.

Consequently complementary compression does not provide an independent
parallel edge for the moving-cut compiler.  An asymmetric power leaves a
literal `p/q` split in the conjugated Hecke root, while the unique uniform
power is the original Singer actor and carries its actor/multiplicity
gauge.  In particular, splitting the head into its two canonical Leavitt
branches and recombining them cannot produce the ungauged bridge `(ESB2)`.

This is a group-level exact-support fence.  It does not exclude a
model-dependent proper subcut on which an asymmetric word happens to have
positive overlap, nor any mixed word outside `<c_0,c_1>`.  No Property
`(T)`, trace assumption, computation, or literature input is used.
