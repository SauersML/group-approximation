**ESTABLISHED** by `left-right-group-strategies-intersection-proof`. No
novelty is claimed. This is Mackey double-coset bookkeeping together with
`ce-standard-form-strategies-are-quantum-approximable`.

Let `Lambda` be a countable group and `S_A, T <= Lambda` subgroups. Take
positive operator-valued measures `{P_(x,a)}_a` in `lambda(S_A)''` and
`{Q_(y,b)}_b` in `rho(T)''` on `l^2(Lambda)`, where
`(rho(t) zeta)(w) = zeta(w t)`, and a unit vector `xi in l^2(Lambda)`. If

```text
I_gamma = S_A cap gamma T gamma^(-1)   is hyperlinear for every gamma in Lambda,   (LRI1)
```

then `p(a,b|x,y) = <P_(x,a) Q_(y,b) xi, xi>` lies in `C_qa`.

In particular, if every `I_gamma` is amenable (for instance trivial), every
such correlation is quantum approximable, whatever `Lambda`, `S_A` and `T`
are.

**Obstruction reading.** Consider any route that tries to certify
non-hyperlinearity of `Lambda` by an explicit Bell violation. In such a route
- Alice's measurements are built from one subgroup acting on the left, for example spectral projections of torsion elements of `S_A`;
- Bob's are built from another subgroup acting on the right;
- the vector is arbitrary.

Such a route must make some intersection `S_A cap gamma T gamma^(-1)`
non-hyperlinear. The separation is carried entirely by the standard form of
that intersection subgroup. Every member of this class dies at the
double-coset decomposition in `(LRP2)` of the proof when the intersections
are hyperlinear. Heterogeneous designs in which Alice and Bob "live in
different factors" of an amalgam, a graph product or an HNN extension, and
meet only in amenable edge groups, therefore produce only `C_qa`
correlations.
