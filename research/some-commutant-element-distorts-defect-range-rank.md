**OPEN.** There are `kappa > 0`, a clopen set `U` with nonempty complement, a frame triple `Theta` with
union a proper subset of `U`, and `n >= 1`, such that every nontrivial fixed-point-free characteristic-two
rank model `sigma` of `R^x = L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` admits `Z` in `M_n(C_U)` with

```text
| rk(D_Theta Z) - delta(sigma) rk(Z) |  >=  kappa · delta(sigma) .
```

Here `C_U` is the commutant of `sigma(G_U)`, and `G_U` is the group of units supported on `U`.

**Payoff** (route `leavitt-rank-triviality-via-commutant-rank-distortion`): with
`near-minimal-defect-ranges-are-rank-faithful-for-the-commutant` at `eps < kappa`, no nontrivial model
exists, so `R^x` is not `F_2`-linear sofic.

**Implied by** `leavitt-disjoint-cylinder-defects-strictly-submultiplicative` (route
`commutant-distortion-from-strict-defect-submultiplicativity`), with `kappa = (1 - theta) c_0`.

## Attempts

- **Unitriangular commutant elements (w4-sub-halving, 2026-09-12): dead.** For `Z` in the group algebra
  of the unitriangular cylinder groups supported off `U`, and `T` unitriangular, the regular model of
  that locally finite group has `rk(TZ) = rk(T) rk(Z)` exactly. So no such `Z` can witness the
  distortion from relations and conjugacy rank equalities of that group alone (artifact Section 3;
  Proposition 5.2 of `research/artifacts/rank-four-orthogonality-check-2026-09-12.md`).
- **Open inputs.** `sigma` of opposite roots, torus units or `V`-elements supported off `U`, and central
  idempotents of the algebra generated by `sigma(R^x)`. Theorem 2.1 constrains all of them, but no
  distortion mechanism is known.
- **Commuting witnesses from any rank-modelled subgroup off `U` (w5-commutant-distort, 2026-09-12): dead as
  inputs.** `commuting-subgroup-witnesses-cannot-distort-defect-ranges` (artifact
  `research/artifacts/commutant-distortion-product-firewall-2026-09-12.md`, Theorem 2.1). Tensor the regular
  direct-limit model of a locally finite `L <= G_U` with any rank model of a subgroup `Lambda <= G_(U^c)`. That
  gives a nontrivial fixed-point-free model of `L x Lambda` with `rk(D_Theta Z) = delta rk(Z)` exactly for every
  matrix `Z` over `sigma(F_2[Lambda])`.
  - **Covered:** the infinite-order escapes `b = iota_A(w_23) iota_(A1)(w_12)` and `x^A_12(s_0) x^A_21(1)` placed
    off `U`; opposite roots and torus units off `U`; every sofic, amenable or linear sofic `Lambda`.
  - **Not covered:** `V`-subgroups not known to be rank-modelled; central idempotents of the algebra generated by
    `sigma(R^x)`; identities using straddling units as factors together with elements of `G_U`; global inputs.
  - **Reformulation** (artifact Lemma 1.1): distortion is an invariant summand whose normalized defect differs
    from `delta`, which is w4-sub-weyl's summand test.
