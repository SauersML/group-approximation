title: Bounded generator displacement in a regular enumeration forces linear word growth

Let P be generated by a finite symmetric set S, let B_r be its word ball
of radius r, and let nu:P->N be injective. For r>=1 define

    D_nu(r)=max{|nu(sp)-nu(p)| : s in S, p in B_(r-1)}.

Then

    |B_r| <= 2r*D_nu(r)+1.

In particular, if every generator has a uniform displacement bound over
all P, then P has at most linear word growth. For the standard two
generators of Z^2 and their inverses,

    D_nu(r) >= r+1.

Thus every enumeration of Z^2 has unbounded generator jumps. Allowing
nonperiodic permutations with bounded displacement still cannot realize
its regular action. This is a restriction on the regular enumeration,
not a claim about all subgroups of a bounded-displacement permutation
group.
