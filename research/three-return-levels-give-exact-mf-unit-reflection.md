If a compact metrizable zero-dimensional homeomorphism has no periods
below k and nk>=3, GL_n of its crossed product over any finite field
is MF iff LEF iff the ring is directly finite. Over F2 its exact MF
radical is the full locally finite core restriction kernel.

Thus the unit-group theorem requires no points of periods one or two,
and includes period three. Aperiodic systems need no matrix amplification.
The elementary-group budget remains nk>=4 with n>=2.
