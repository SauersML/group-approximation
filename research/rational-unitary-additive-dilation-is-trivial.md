Any homomorphism from a nonzero rational interval in `(Q,+)` to a fixed
finite-dimensional unitary group whose matrix entries are rational functions
of the parameter is trivial.  A fixed-size rational unitary block therefore
cannot groupify characteristic-zero root addition.
