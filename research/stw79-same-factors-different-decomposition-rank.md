Fix irrational `theta` and let `U` be the CAR algebra. There are unital
separable nuclear nowhere scattered algebras `E_0,E_1`, each with exactly
one nonzero proper ideal, fitting essential extensions with the same
identified endpoints

```text
0 -> K tensor U -> E_i -> A_theta tensor U -> 0.
```

Both endpoint algebras have finite decomposition rank and are strongly
quasidiagonal. Nevertheless

```text
dr(E_0)=1,                dr(E_1)=infinity,
dim_nuc(E_0)=1,           1 <= dim_nuc(E_1) <= 2.
```

Both tracial state spaces are singletons, and both traces vanish on their
nonzero ideal. The index boundary is zero for `E_0` and is
`(x,y) |-> -x` from `Z[1/2]^2` to `Z[1/2]` for `E_1`.

Thus quasidiagonality of the simple composition factors, even together
with their full isomorphism types, the ideal chain and the tracial state
simplex, does not characterize finite decomposition rank in Problem
LXXIX. This does not refute a condition on **all** subquotients, which
would include `E_i` itself. The construction uses classical extensions;
its role here is to correct the factorwise proposal and locate the
extension information that it omitted.
