title: The LXXVII hypotheses and O-infinity-stability descend to unital codomain quotients
---

Let `phi:A->B` satisfy the hypotheses of STW LXXVII and let `q:B->B/J` be a
unital quotient.  Then `q phi` has finite nuclear dimension, and every
nonzero positive element of `(q phi)(A)` is properly infinite in `B/J`.
Moreover, if `phi` is O-infinity-stable then `q phi` is
O-infinity-stable.
