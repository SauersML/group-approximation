Given `G` exact nonamenable, (CMA_G) supplies `X` with commuting actions of
`G` (amenable) and `Gamma` (`Z` or locally finite, free minimal, `dim X` finite).
The established criterion makes `alpha : G acts on C(X) x Gamma` an amenable
action on a unital stably finite classifiable algebra.  This is one direction
only: an XL witness need not be a crossed product of this form.
