Over any finite field, if the chain-recurrent core consists of fixed
points, every Kazhdan subgroup of GL_2(R_X) is finite. If every core
point has period at most two, every Kazhdan subgroup of R_X^x is finite.
Thus their intrinsic Kazhdan compression defects all vanish. This is
a limitation of the compression criterion, not an MF assertion.
