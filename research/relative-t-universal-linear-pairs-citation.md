Literature import, checked 2026-08-20 against Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain--
Kassabov, *Property (T) for groups graded by root systems*,
arXiv:1102.0031, especially the discussion and theorem asserting that for a
finitely generated associative unital ring `R`,

```text
(EL_2(R) semidirect R^2, R^2)
```

has relative property `(T)`. The paper attributes the commutative case to
Shalom and the general case to Kassabov and uses it as the local relative-gap
input for its root-system argument.

Only existence of a finite relative Kazhdan set and positive constant is
imported. The source is not claimed to provide the Heisenberg central
extension, compatible Schrodinger--Weil projective representations, or a
finitely presented self-similar binary congruence tower needed by the present
application.
