title: Positive subgroup expansion supplies exponentially many short disjoint torsion detours

Section 3. In a group with abelian centralizers of size at most n, for a nonamenable two-generator H and a outside H, k detours c_i=a b_i with disjoint nonidentity power supports can be chosen with |b_i|_H <= ceil(log(nk)/log(1+h_H/4)). This is conditional on the stated structural hypotheses, not a universal group assertion.
