title: A countable group with an infinite wq-normal subgroup of fixed price one has fixed price one
distinct_from:
  fpbs-normal-subgroup-bounded-cost-fixed-price-one: that is the open fixed price statement for an infinite normal subgroup whose actions have uniformly bounded cost; this assumes the subgroup has fixed price one and weakens normality to wq-normality.
  fpbs-bounded-exponent-two-groups-fixed-price: that transfers cost one upward from any infinite subgroup of a bounded-exponent group; this needs no exponent bound but requires the subgroup to be wq-normal and of fixed price one.
---

**ESTABLISHED.** Let `Gamma` be a countable group and `H <= Gamma` an infinite
wq-normal subgroup. If `H` has fixed price one, then `Gamma` has fixed price
one: every essentially free p.m.p. action of `Gamma` has cost one.

**Consequence.** Infinite amenable groups have fixed price one (Ornstein--Weiss).
So every countable group with an infinite amenable wq-normal subgroup has fixed
price one. In particular Lyons' theorem
`fpbs-non-fixed-price-one-has-nonuniqueness` applies to no group in the class of
`fpbs-amenable-wq-normal-class-nonuniqueness`. Every group it covers lies in the
class of `fpbs-no-amenable-wq-normal-class-nonuniqueness`.
