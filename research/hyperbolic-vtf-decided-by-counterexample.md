`hyperbolic-groups-virtually-torsion-free` asks to **decide** whether every
word-hyperbolic group is virtually torsion-free.  One word-hyperbolic group
that is not virtually torsion-free decides it negatively.

The positive answer has its own route,
`hyperbolic-virtual-torsionfree-via-finite-residual`, through
`hyperbolic-finite-residual-is-torsion-free`.  Before this route existed the
root had only that positive route, so the graph could not record the negative
outcome at all.

By `hyperbolic-rf-question-equals-vtf-question`, this negative answer is
available exactly when `non-residually-finite-hyperbolic-group` is established.
