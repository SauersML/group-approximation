title: A nontrivial word has no universal relation-energy domination

  - notes/PROPERTY_T_FREE_FINITE_BALL_LIMIT_2026-09-07.md

For a finite generating presentation $G=\langle S\mid R\rangle$, a
finite $R_0\subseteq R$, and a word $w\ne1$ in $G$, no $C\geq0$
makes the operator-order inequality

$$
(w(U)-I)^*(w(U)-I)
\leq C\sum_{r\in R_0}(r(U)-I)^*(r(U)-I)
$$

valid for all finite-dimensional unitary tuples $U$.

This excludes precisely the displayed universal positive-operator
estimate. It does not exclude an inequality between scalar operator
norms or a proof using additional hypotheses. In particular, it does
not refute the existence of a property-(T)-free non-MF proof.
