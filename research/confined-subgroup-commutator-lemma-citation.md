**Source.** A. Le Boudec, N. Matte Bon, *Confined subgroups and high
transitivity*, arXiv:2012.03997v2 (November 19, 2021). The PDF was read on
2026-09-13, pp. 1–4 and 8–10. On p. 10 the authors write: "The following is
Theorem 3.17 from [LBMB20]", where [LBMB20] is their arXiv:2006.08677, *A
commutator lemma for confined subgroups and applications to groups acting on
rooted trees*. The original was not re-read; the restatement is by the same
authors.

Verbatim, p. 10: "**Theorem 3.5** (Commutator lemma for confined subgroups).
Let `H, G ≤ Sym(Ω)` be two subgroups such that `H` is confined by `G`, and let
`P` be a confining subset for `(H,G)`, and `r = |P|`. Assume that
`{Ω_σ}_{σ∈P}` is a displacement configuration for `P` such that for all
`σ ∈ P` the group `R_G(Ω_σ)` is non-trivial, and every non-trivial conjugacy
class in `R_G(Ω_σ)` has cardinality `> r`. Then there exists `ρ ∈ P` such that
`H` contains a non-trivial subgroup `N ≤ R_G(Ω_ρ)` whose normalizer in
`R_G(Ω_ρ)` has index at most `r`." Same page: "`R_G(Σ)` the pointwise fixator
of the complement of `Σ`, and call it the rigid stabilizer of `Σ`."

Verbatim, p. 9, **Definition 3.2**: "Let `P` be a finite set of non-trivial
elements of `Sym(Ω)`, and let `{Ω_σ}_{σ∈P}` be a family of non-empty subsets of
`Ω` indexed by `P`. We say that `{Ω_σ}_{σ∈P}` is a displacement configuration
for `P` if the following hold: i) for all `σ, ρ ∈ P`, either `Ω_σ = Ω_ρ`, or
`Ω_σ` and `Ω_ρ` are disjoint; ii) for all `σ, ρ ∈ P`, either `σ` fixes `Ω_ρ`
pointwise, or `σ(Ω_ρ)` is disjoint from `⋃_{α∈P} Ω_α`; iii) for all `σ ∈ P`,
`σ(Ω_σ)` is disjoint from `⋃_{α∈P} Ω_α` and also from `⋃_{α∈P} σ^{-1}(Ω_α)`."

Verbatim, p. 9, proof of Lemma 3.3: "Lemma 4.1 in [LBMB20] states that if `X`
is a Hausdorff space without isolated points, and if `σ_1, …, σ_q` are
homeomorphisms of `X` such that `σ_i^2` is non-trivial, then there is a
displacement configuration for `{σ_1, …, σ_q}` consisting of open subsets."

Verbatim, p. 1: "Equivalently, `H` is a confined subgroup if there exists a
finite subset `P` of non-trivial elements of `G` such that `gHg^{-1} ∩ P` is
not empty for every `g ∈ G`."

This route asserts only that the cited statements say what the target
records.
