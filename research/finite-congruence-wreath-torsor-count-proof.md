Character orthogonality applied to `d delta_e` gives `(FCW2)`, proving that
an exact regular finite packet is a multiple of its left regular
representation.  Lamp Bernoulli moments alone make all joint character
spaces equidimensional; their choices form `(FCW3)`, one global unitary
orbit.  After diagonalizing the lamps, the remaining exact actor blocks are
finite-group representation data modulo the block-diagonal gauge.  General
finite-group representations are classified by integer irreducible
multiplicities, giving `(FCW4)`.  In the regular case `|H|` divides `d`, so
`r<=|H|<=d` and `(FCW5)` follows.
