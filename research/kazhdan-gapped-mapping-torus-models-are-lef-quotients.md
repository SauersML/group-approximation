**ESTABLISHED.** Proof: `kazhdan-gapped-mapping-torus-models-are-lef-quotients-proof`. It is
elementary, apart from the imported openness of property (T) (Shalom) and the established
stage-lift criterion for LEF mapping tori.

## Setting

- `K` is a Kazhdan group generated by a finite set `S`. `F = F(S)` is the free group on `S`.
- `φ ∈ Aut(K)` and `G = K ⋊_φ Z`, where `t s t^-1 = φ(s)`.
- For each `s ∈ S`, fix words `w_s, v_s ∈ F` representing `φ(s)` and `φ^-1(s)`. Let
  `ŵ, v̂ : F -> F` be the endomorphisms `s ↦ w_s` and `s ↦ v_s`.
- By Shalom (`fpbs-shalom-property-t-is-open`), there is a finite set `R_0` of relators of `K`
  such that `K~ = <S | R_0>` has property (T). Fix a Kazhdan constant `κ > 0` of `K~` for `S`.
- Put `r_s = ŵ(v_s)^-1 s`, a relator of `K`, and let `R_Γ = R_0 ∪ ŵ(R_0) ∪ {r_s : s ∈ S}`. This
  is a finite set of relators of `K`.
- `Q = ∏ M_n / ⊕ M_n` is the norm corona, and `[x_i]` is the class of a bounded sequence.

A homomorphism `U : F -> U(n)` is a *model* if `U(r) = 1` for every `r ∈ R_Γ`. It is
*`c`-gapped* if `‖U(g) − 1‖ ≥ c` whenever `g ∈ F` and `U(g) ≠ 1`.

## Statement

**(SR1) Support rigidity of wraps. This holds in every dimension, for every image and every wrap.**
Let `U : F -> U(n)` be a model, and let `T ∈ U(n)` be any unitary with

```text
δ = max_(s in S) ‖ T U(s) T* − U(w_s) ‖ < κ.
```

Then:

- `U` and `U ∘ ŵ` are representations of `K~` with the same irreducible constituents;
- `ker U = ker (U ∘ ŵ)` in `F`;
- `α(U(x)) = U(ŵ(x))` well defines an automorphism `α` of the group `M = U(F)`, with
  `α(U(s)) = U(w_s)`.

`M` may be infinite. No block structure of `U` is assumed, and none of `T`.

**(SR2) Gapped corona models factor through LEF quotients.** Let `π : G -> U(Q)` be a
homomorphism. Suppose that, for some `c > 0` and some `c`-gapped homomorphisms
`U_i : F -> U(n_i)`, we have `π(s) = [U_i(s)]` for all `s ∈ S`. The unitary `π(t)` is arbitrary.
If `π(k) ≠ 1` for some `k ∈ K`, then there is a normal subgroup `N ⊴ K` with:

- `φ(N) = N` and `k ∉ N`;
- `(K/N) ⋊_φ̄ Z` is LEF, hence operator MF.

So `k ∉ Rad_MF(G)`.

**(SR3) Equivalence.** For `k ∈ K`, the following are equivalent:

- some homomorphism `G -> U(Q)` that is gapped on `K` (in the sense of (SR2)) is nontrivial on `k`;
- `k` survives in some LEF quotient `(K/N) ⋊ Z` of `G` with `N ⊴ K`.

Hence, on `K`, the *gapped MF residual* of `G` equals its *LEF residual*.

**(SR4) Witnesses.** Suppose `Rad_MF(G) = K`. This covers every witness of (LK3) in
`torsion-free-lef-kazhdan-kernel-with-exact-mapping-torus-radical`, and every Kazhdan-kernel
witness of `torsion-free-sofic-exact-mf-radical-over-z`. Then every homomorphism `G -> U(Q)`
that is gapped on `K` is trivial on `K`, whatever it does on `t`.

In finite dimensions, this becomes a statement about models. Suppose some model `U` admits a
unitary `T` of defect `< κ`. Then `s ↦ U(s)`, `t ↦ τ` satisfies `t s t^-1 = w_s` *exactly* in the
group `M ⋊_α Z`, whatever `T` was. If moreover `U` kills every relator of `K` and `M` is finite,
this is a homomorphism of `G` onto a finite group. For a witness, `K` has no nontrivial finite
quotient (`mapping-torus-mf-radical-lies-in-finite-residual`), so such a `U` is trivial.

## Examples of gapped models

The following classes are `c`-gapped, and so is every finite direct sum of them.

| Model | Gap `c` |
|---|---|
| Permutation matrices (any finite `F`-set; includes `ℓ²(Π/Λ)` with `Λ` not normal, and Gassmann pairs) | `√3` |
| Regular representations `λ_Π ∘ θ` of finite groups | `√3` |
| Representations of finite groups with rational character | `1` |
| Any representation whose image has exponent dividing `N` | `2 sin(π/N)` |

The last row is `finite-order-unitary-has-uniform-opnorm-gap`.

## What this kills

**The class.** Every operator-norm corona model of `G` whose kernel part is a gapped
representation. This includes:

- every permutation model of `K` (actions of `F` on finite sets), with an *arbitrary* unitary,
  not necessarily a permutation, implementing `φ`;
- every sum of regular representations of finite approximants, as in any LEF sequence of `K`;
- every rational-character or bounded-exponent model.

At the level of corona models, the class contains the whole regular-block class of
`kazhdan-regular-block-periodizations-of-mapping-tori-are-finite`, and no finite presentation of
`G` is needed. The only survivor listed there that it does not contain is blocks with infinite
image. It also contains two of that
node's three survivors: permutation blocks `ℓ²(Π/Λ)`, including Gassmann pairs where kernel
rigidity (RB1) genuinely fails, and non-block-monomial wraps `T`.

**The invariant.** The *irreducible support* of the kernel representation. The Hilbert--Schmidt
space `HS(H_σ, H)` carries the unitary representation `X ↦ U(ŵ g) X σ(g)*` of `K~`. The vector
`T J`, where `J` embeds a constituent `σ`, is `δ`-almost invariant for it, in normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt norm. Property (T), with the dimension-free constant `κ`, then produces a
nonzero intertwiner. So the support is carried *exactly* onto itself by `ŵ`. Kernels of
finite-dimensional unitary representations are determined by their supports, so `ŵ` preserves
the kernel exactly.

**Where every member dies.** At the wrap. The exact kernel equality turns `ŵ` into an
automorphism of every stage image. The gap makes every relator of `K` exact at late stages.
The stage images then converge to a nontrivial quotient `L = K/N` of `K`, carrying stage lifts
of `φ̄`. By `lef-mapping-torus-iff-stage-liftable-automorphism`, `L ⋊ Z` is LEF.

For a witness (`Rad_MF(G) = K`), this contradicts `k ∈ Rad_MF(G)`. So:

```text
no gapped model of K is phi-covariant in Q, by any unitary whatsoever.
```

For LEF `K`, this is sharp. Take any LEF sequence `(F_i, S) -> (K, S)`. It gives a faithful gapped
embedding `π_0 : K -> U(Q)`, `π_0(k) = [λ_(F_i)(k)]`. For a witness, `π_0 ∘ φ` is never unitarily
equivalent to `π_0` in `Q`. This is the operator-norm content of (NSL).

## What survives

Any homomorphism `G -> U(Q)` that is nontrivial on `K`, and so any proof that such a `G` is
MF, needs a kernel part `π|_K` with **no gapped lift**. Its lifts `U_i` must fall under one of
two cases:

- they are not exact representations of any finitely presented cover of `K`, and the relators
  of `K` hold only approximately;
- they are exact, but their images contain nontrivial elements arbitrarily close to `1`: finite
  images with irrational character values and growing orders (for example Weil or cuspidal
  representations of `EL_n` over growing finite fields, with irrational characters), or infinite
  images.

Both escapes are genuinely outside the argument. In the first, the relators of `K` are never
exact at any stage, so no finite presentation of `K~` sees them. In the second, the relators are
exact, and the wrap exploits spectral clustering of the images near `1`, which the gap forbids.

**Consequence for (L2) and (EKL1).** Let `K` be LEF and `Rad_MF(G) = K`. By (SR3), `G` then has
no LEF quotient `(K/N) ⋊ Z` with `N ≠ K`. This is (NSL) strengthened from `K` to every
`φ`-invariant quotient of `K`:

```text
(NSL+)  for every phi-invariant N < K with N != K,
        phi-bar is not a marked limit of stage automorphisms of finite approximants of K/N.
```

(LK3) splits into two prerequisites, and each can fail on its own:

- **(LK3-gap)**, which is equivalent to (NSL+). It is algebraic and concerns stage lifts only.
- **(LK3-ng)**: no *non-gapped* homomorphism `G -> U(Q)` is nontrivial on `K`.

A proof of (EKL1) must therefore do one of two things. It can derive a `φ`-invariant proper
quotient with stage lifts from the LEF structure of `K` and the exact radical, which is purely
combinatorial. Otherwise it must build a non-gapped covariant model: an operator-norm model whose
kernel images cluster spectrally at `1`. No argument confined to permutation, regular or
rational models can prove (EKL1) except through (NSL+).
