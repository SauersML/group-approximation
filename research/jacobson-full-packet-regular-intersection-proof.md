In the faithful literal actions, a far shifted matrix preserving
the original levels has no entries mixing its middle coordinate
with its first and third. Over the binary field its remaining
one-dimensional block is the identity, leaving exactly `K_P`.
Removing this common far factor leaves the local `C_0`. Since
each finite packet embeds faithfully in the literal model, their
abstract intersection is also the specified shared subgroup.

The two indices needed for the regular restrictions are
`|GL_6|/|GL_5|=2016` and `|GL_3|/|GL_2|=28`. Therefore the
regular `B` module and 2016 regular `C` modules both restrict
to 56,448 regular intersection modules. A unitary intertwiner
identifies them. Restricting these regular characters to any
finite subgroup gives the stated fixed dimensions. Squaring the
absolute character gives the same result for the conjugation
representation. Mixed words outside either finite packet remain
additional conditions not imposed by this construction.
