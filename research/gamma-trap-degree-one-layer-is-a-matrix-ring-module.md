## Setting

- `J = F_2<S,T | TS=1>`. As an algebra, `J` is the monoid algebra of the
  bicyclic monoid, with basis the monomials `S^i T^j`.
- `P = P_{2,2}(J)` and head `h = x_13(1+ST)`, as in
  `double-parabolic-head-dies-on-commuting-right-orbits`.
- Seven generators: `as = x_12(S)`, `at = x_12(T)`, `c = x_21(1)`,
  `b1 = x_34(1)`, `bs = x_43(S)`, `bt = x_43(T)`, `u = x_13(1)`.
- For elements `g, x` put `d_g(x) = x g x g`.
- `Gamma_1 = Gamma_trap + EXTRA_RELS + (C1),(C2)` at right-orbit depth `<= 1`.
  This is the candidate named in the w10 entry of
  `property-t-free-jacobson-head-collapse`. Its word lists are in `words.py`.

## Theorem (degree-one rigidity)

Let `G` be any group generated by involutions `as, at, c, b1, bs, bt, u`
satisfying the following.

- (LR) each of `as, at, c` commutes with each of `b1, bs, bt`.
- (R1) `d_as d_c u = d_bs d_b1 u`.
- (R2) `d_at d_c u = d_bt d_b1 u`.
- (R3) `d_at d_c d_as d_c u = u`.
- (U) `as, at, bs, bt` each commute with `u`.

Let `N = <<u>>`, let `W = N/[N,N]`, and let `v` be the image of `u` in `W`.
Then the following hold.

1. `W` is an `F_2`-vector space on which `G/N` acts by conjugation, and it is
   generated by `v` as an `F_2[G/N]`-module.
2. Let each of the six Levi generators act on `M_2(J)` as its image in `P`
   acts by conjugation on the block `n = {x_13, x_14, x_23, x_24}` of the
   unipotent radical. There is a
   surjective linear map `phi : M_2(J) -> W` that commutes with each of the six
   Levi generators and sends `E_13(1)` to `v`. Explicitly,
   `phi(E_13(a)) = x_13(a)`, `phi(E_23(a)) = (1+c) x_13(a)`,
   `phi(E_14(a)) = (1+b1) x_13(a)`, `phi(E_24(a)) = (1+c)(1+b1) x_13(a)`,
   where `x_13(a)` is defined in the route.
3. `ker phi = M_2(I)` for a two-sided ideal `I` of `J`. So `W ≅ M_2(J/I)`,
   with the left Levi acting by left multiplication by `1 + S E_12`,
   `1 + T E_12`, `1 + E_21`, and the right Levi acting by right multiplication
   by `1 + E_12`, `1 + S E_21`, `1 + T E_21` (block indices `3,4 -> 1,2`).
4. If `W` is finite, then `J/I ≅ F_2[x]/(p)` with `p(0) != 0`, `S -> x`,
   `T -> x^{-1}`. In particular `1 + ST` lies in `I`. So `h` maps into
   `[N,N]`, and `dim W = 4 deg p`.
5. For `G = P` the map `phi` is an isomorphism (`I = 0`).

Only (LR), (R1)-(R3), (U) and the involution relations are used. In
particular the unipotent commutations `UU`, the relations (C1),(C2), and the
relations `[as,at] = [bs,bt] = 1` are not used. The five hypotheses are among
the relators of `Gamma_1`, and (U) is
`X_as_u, X_at_u, X_bs_u, X_bt_u` of `EXTRA_RELS`.

## Corollaries

1. **Every finite quotient of `Gamma_1` has a ring degree-one layer.** Let
   `H` be a finite quotient of `Gamma_1`, or more generally any quotient with
   finite `<<u>>^ab`. Then its degree-one layer is exactly
   `M_2(F_2[x]/(p))` with `S -> x`, `v = E_13(1)`, for a single polynomial
   `p` with `p(0) = 1`, and `G/N` acts on it through that ring action. The same
   holds for any group that is the target of a map from a ball of `P`
   containing the sixteen relators and the involution relators, provided the map is multiplicative on that
   ball; this covers LEF witnesses.
2. **The ring-module scans are complete at degree one.** The class-two scan
   `c2scan.py` of the w11 pull entry, and `c3scan.py` here, range over the
   Levi modules `M_2(F_2[x]/(p))` with `S -> a`. The case `a = x` is included
   for every `p` with `2 <= deg p <= 5`, at depth 0 and at depth 1
   (`scan_depth1.txt`, 44 polynomials). The scan skips `a = 1`, so the case
   `p = x + 1` was run separately here with `c2scan.test`: the head dies at
   depths 0 and 1 for 4 seeds. By Corollary 1 these are all the
   degree-one layers of dimension `<= 20` that any finite quotient of
   `Gamma_1` can have. There is no non-ring Levi module to search.
3. **What a head-keeping finite quotient of `Gamma_1` must look like.** Since
   `h` lies in `[N,N]`, a head-keeping finite quotient differs from the
   scanned family only above degree one. The possibilities are:
   - a class-two layer whose cocycle is not captured by the
     `G0`-equivariant tensor model (for example a non-split extension, or a
     cocycle `c_u` outside the sampled ones);
   - a polynomial `p` of degree `>= 6`;
   - nilpotency class `>= 3` with a different cocycle, or a normal closure
     `<<u>>` that is not nilpotent.

## Where the argument stops

The theorem constrains only `N/[N,N]`. Two gaps remain before the class-two
scans become a proof that `Gamma_1` has no head-keeping class-two quotient
over a given `p`.

- *Universality of the tensor model.* `N_W = W + W (x) W` with cocycle
  `w w'^T`, cut down by the `G0`-span of the relator differences, is universal
  only among class-two extensions whose `u`-lift is fixed up to the gauge
  `c_u -> c_u + l(v)`, with `l` `G0`-equivariant (see `tensor_model.py` of
  the w10 artifacts). The scans sample `c_u` at random with 2 or 3 seeds per
  `(p, a)`, and do not enumerate the gauge classes.
- *Infinitely many `p`.* The scans cover `deg p <= 5` completely and
  `(x+1)^k` for `k <= 10`.

## Evidence (bounded computation; not part of the established statement)

The files are in `experiments/p22-class-three-depth-one-2026-09-17/`.

- **Class three (`c3scan.py`).** `c3scan.py` is the truncated tensor-algebra
  model `F_2 + V + V^2 + V^3`. `K` is the `G0`-span of the exact relator
  differences together with `e (x) k_2 + k_2 (x) e`, for `k_2` in the
  class-two relator span; this makes the class-two layer of `K` central.
  - Self-tests: associativity, inverses and the conjugation formula
    `(1+y)(1+k)(1+y)^{-1} = 1 + k + y_1 k_2 + k_2 y_1`.
  - Over `p = (x+1)^4`, the depth-0 class-two survivor persists in class
    three.
  - Adding (C) at depth 1 kills the head in class three as well (3 seeds).
    So a third layer does not revive the head.
- **Local rings (class two, via `c2scan.py`).** For `p = (x+1)^k`,
  `k = 6..10`, 2 seeds each, (C) at depth 1 kills the head. The `C/K`
  dimensions range from 762 to 1581. So the depth-0 survivors do not climb to
  higher local rings once depth-1 (C) is imposed.
- **Propagation (`propagate.py`).** For `p = (x+1)^4`:
  - depth-0 (C) follows from `Gamma_trap + EXTRA_RELS`;
  - depth-1 (C) forces (C) at depths 2, 3 and 4, so the depth-one kill is the
    orbit trap of `double-parabolic-head-dies-on-commuting-right-orbits` acting
    through the class-two layer;
  - without depth-1 (C), the numbers of (C) differences outside `K` at depths
    0..4 are `[0, 2, 2, 4, 0]`.
- **Non-ring Levi images (`levi_search.py`).** This script computes the
  largest degree-one layer `W_max = F_2[G0] / F_2[G0]{rho_1..rho_9}` for
  random Levi images `G0 = Lbar x Rbar` in `S_m`.
  - `S_4`: `W = 0` in all 299 trials.
  - `S_5`: 397 trials, of which 379 give `W = 0` and 18 exceed the order
    cap. No nonzero layer was found.
  - Sanity check with the ring action of `SL_2(F_4)`: `dim W_max = 8`,
    matching `M_2(F_4)`, as the theorem predicts.

  The theorem explains these results: a nonzero layer forces `G0` to act
  through `M_2(F_2[x]/(p))`.
