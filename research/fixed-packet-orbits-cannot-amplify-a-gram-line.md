A finite packet or finite gauge orbit of one Gram vector spans only bounded
dimension, hence has normalized density `O(d^-2)` in `L^2(M_d)`.  More
strongly, for any fixed finite collection of packet twists there are Gram
unitaries with an exact invariant vector but whose every twisted fixed space
has normalized dimension at most `1/d`.

Thus fixed packet covariantization cannot promote the PAZ invariant Gram
line to the positive-density projection required by graph return.  A
surviving construction must supply a genuinely capacity-selected mixed
corner or another finite-matrix density theorem.
