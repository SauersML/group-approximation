Goldbring--Sinclair, arXiv:2509.15086v1, Theorem 5.4 proves the qc-full
obstruction to simultaneous computable presentations with computable factor
embeddings.  Their Corollary 5.5 applies it to the standard presentations of
`C^*(F_n x F_n)`, using qc-fullness and computability of the standard
presentation of `C^*(F_n)`.  Corollary 5.7 deduces that any ucp lift of a
fixed quotient from `C^*(F_infinity)` cannot be computable.  The source
explicitly keeps existence of such a lift, equivalently LP, open.
