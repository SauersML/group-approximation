For countable groups `G,H`,

```text
Rad_MF(G x H)=Rad_MF(G) x Rad_MF(H),
R_MF(G x H)=R_MF(G) x R_MF(H).                        (MP1)
```

For a countable family of countable groups `(G_i)` whose restricted direct
sum is countable,

```text
Rad_MF(directSum_i G_i)=directSum_i Rad_MF(G_i),
R_MF(directSum_i G_i)=directSum_i R_MF(G_i).           (MP2)
```

In particular finite products and countable restricted sums of MF groups are
MF.  More generally, every countable group which is residually MF is MF, so
the class of countable MF groups is closed under countable subdirect products
and arbitrary intersections of MF-closed normal subgroups.
