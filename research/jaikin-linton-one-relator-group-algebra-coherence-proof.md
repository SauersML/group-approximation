Use Andrei Jaikin-Zapirain and Marco Linton, *On the coherence of one-relator
groups and their group algebras*, Annals of Mathematics (2) 201 (2025),
909--959, doi:10.4007/annals.2025.201.3.4, Theorem 1.1(2).

For the torsion-free branch, a one-relator presentation is a finite reducible
two-complex without proper powers.  Theorem 1.6 of the same paper says that if
`G` is the fundamental group of such a complex and `K` has characteristic
zero, then `K[G]` is coherent.  Its proof uses the Hughes-free division ring
supplied by the strong Atiyah theorem and the weak-dimension bound established
in Theorem 1.7.

The paper treats the torsion branch separately through the virtually
free-by-cyclic structure and combines the two branches in Theorem 1.1(2).
Therefore `K[G]` is coherent for every one-relator `G` and every
characteristic-zero field `K`.  QED
