**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `S` be a finitely generated, infinite, simple unital
ring, let `sigma in Aut(S)` act entrywise on `EL_3(S)`, and put

```text
E = EL_3(S) semidirectProduct_sigma Z,     K = EL_3(S) x {0}.
```

Suppose there are a finitely generated unital subring `R <= S` and a unit
`z` of `S` such that

```text
(CC1) EL_3(S) is MF;
(CC2) sigma(R) <= R;
(CC3) z lies in the subgroup [S^x, S^x] generated by commutators of units;
(CC4) z commutes with every element of R, and z does not commute with
      some element of sigma^{-1}(R).
```

Then `Q = EL_3(S)` and `psi = sigma` witness
`mf-kazhdan-quotientless-mapping-torus-exact-radical`:
`Q` is countable, nontrivial, MF and Kazhdan, it has no nontrivial finite
quotient, and `Rad_MF(E) = K`.

The squaring special case: a unit `w` with `sigma(w) = w^2`, the subring
`R` generated by `w^2, w^{-2}`, and `z in [S^x,S^x]` with
`[z,w^2] = 0 != [z,w]`.
