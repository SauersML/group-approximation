## Step 1: linearize

By (b) of `char-p-untwisted-self-similar-affine-groups-are-linear` there is an
injective homomorphism `Ψ: A x| Γ -> GL_N(F_p[[t]])`, with `N = (r+1)d`.

## Step 2: a finitely generated coefficient algebra

Let `H <= A x| Γ` be generated by a finite set `B`. Let `S <= F_p[[t]]` be the
`F_p`-subalgebra generated by the entries of `Ψ(b)` and `Ψ(b)^(-1)`, for `b in B`.

- `S` is a finitely generated commutative `F_p`-algebra.
- Every element of `H` is a product of elements `b^(±1)`, so `Ψ(H) <= GL_N(S)`.
- `GL_N(S) = Aut_S(S^N)` embeds in `Aff_S(S^N)` by `w -> (0, w)`.

So `Ψ(H)` is a finitely generated subgroup of `Aff_S(S^N)`, and `S^N` is a finitely
generated `S`-module.

## Step 3: transfer the bound

`Ψ` is injective. So a word over `B` is trivial in `H` iff the corresponding word
over `Ψ(B)` is trivial in `Ψ(H)`.

Apply parts 1 and 2 of `commutative-algebra-affine-groups-have-polynomial-word-problem`
with `q = p`, `R = S` and module `S^N`. This gives conclusions 1 and 2.

That node's algorithm uses finite data attached to `(Ψ(H), Ψ(B))`: a presentation
of `S`, lifts of the generators, and a Gröbner basis. Such data exists, so the
algorithm exists. Nothing here asks that it be computable from `(A, ν, Γ)`.

## Step 4: exclude the KMS group

Suppose the KMS group `G` embeds in some `A x| Γ` as above. `G` is finitely
presented, hence finitely generated. Steps 1 and 2 then embed `G` in `Aff_S(S^N)`,
where `S` is a finitely generated commutative algebra over the finite field `F_p`
and `S^N` is a finitely generated `S`-module. This contradicts
`kms-groups-avoid-commutative-algebra-affine-hosts`, which proves conclusion 3. `∎`
