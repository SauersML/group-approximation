Let `A_Gamma` be the right-angled Artin group on a finite simplicial graph,
and let `Sigma_(g,b,p)` be a compact surface, allowing boundary components
and marked points. There are torsion-free finite-index subgroups of

`Out(A_Gamma)` and `Mod(Sigma_(g,b,p))`

which satisfy the Strong Atiyah Conjecture over `C`. Consequently both
ambient groups satisfy the Weak Atiyah Conjecture over `C`.

This includes `Out(F_n)`, since a finitely generated free group is a RAAG.
