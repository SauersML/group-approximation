**REFUTED.**  The exact Walsh--Hadamard models have equal left and right
`D_d`-dimensions `d->infinity`, full carrier, vanishing edge expectation,
and an exact exterior-conjugate expectation test.  They pay no relation
defect.  Divergent correspondence degree is therefore compatible with finite
matrices even without hiding on a vanishing physical-trace sector.
