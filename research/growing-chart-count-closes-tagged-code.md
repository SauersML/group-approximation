**INVALIDATED GROWING-CHART ROUTE.**  Let the number of internal normalized
charts grow with the tagged addresses while keeping the total presentation
area of each edge discrepancy uniformly bounded.

`uniform-area-bounds-essential-chart-count` shows that a diagram of area at
most `B` has at most `B` nonempty chart subdiagrams.  Zero-cell chart pieces
carry only free reductions and can be collapsed.  Hence a genuinely growing
number of independent fusion charts is incompatible with the uniform-area
hypothesis `(CCC2)`; only the lengths of cell-free conjugating trees can
grow.  Those trees do not supply new mark-fusion relations.
