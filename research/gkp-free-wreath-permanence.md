**ESTABLISHED LITERATURE INPUT.**  Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell,
*Soficity for group actions on sets and applications*, Research in the
Mathematical Sciences 12 (2025), 48, Theorem 3.7: if `K` and `A` are sofic
groups and `A curvearrowright X` is a sofic action, then

```text
K wr^*_(A curvearrowright X) A
  := ( *_(x in X) K_x ) rtimes A                              (GFW1)
```

is sofic.
