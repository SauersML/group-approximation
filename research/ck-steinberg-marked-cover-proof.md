The integral group ring Z[T] is a finitely presented unital ring with
torsion-free additive group. Its rational group algebra and an amplified
regular representation supply the sandwich witness for the nonzero
element 1-g, as in Chatterji--Kassabov Lemma 4. The universal-sandwich
lemma makes U finitely presented and keeps Z[T] embedded.

For a unit u, diag(u,u^-1) is the elementary word w_ij(u)w_ij(-1),
where w_ij(u)=e_ij(u)e_ji(-u^-1)e_ij(u). Hence the commutator of
diag(a,a^-1,1,1) and diag(b,1,b^-1,1) is the elementary matrix
diag([a,b],1,1,1). Products show that [T,T], and therefore H, embeds
diagonally in Q.

Q is normally generated by d(g)=diag(g,1,1,1):
[e_12(1),d(g)]=e_12(1-g), and the normal closure of this root contains
every root with parameter in the two-sided ideal generated by 1-g,
using elementary index changes and the Steinberg commutator identities.
That ideal is U because p(1-g)q=1.

Now P=St_4(U) is finitely presented with property (T) and maps onto Q,
by steinberg-finite-presentation-and-kazhdan-theorem. Apply
finite-marking-normal-generator-cover to H->Q and g. Its Gamma has
all the required properties. This argument never identifies P with Q
and never assumes the unstable Steinberg kernel is central.
