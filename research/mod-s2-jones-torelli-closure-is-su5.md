**Setting.** As in `mod-s2-jones-rectangular-separating-twist-spectrum`: `M = Mod(S_2)`, `K` its Torelli
group, `t_s = (s_1 s_2)^6` a separating twist, `V` the 5-dimensional `TL_6(delta)`-module with Gram form
`B`, `A = exp(i(pi/2 + x))`, `delta = 2 cos 2x`, `|x| < pi/8`, and `rho = rho_(x,beta)` one of the 10 honest
unitary representations `s_i -> beta (A + A^(-1) e_i)`. By (J2) there, `rho(t_s)` has eigenvalues
`zeta w^(-4)` (once) and `zeta w` (four times), `w = exp(-24 i x/5)`, `zeta = zeta(x,beta) in mu_5`.
Put `G = cl rho(M)`, `H = cl rho(K)`, `S = cl[H,H]` (closures in `U(V,B)`), and for a unit scalar `lambda`
put `eps_lambda = ||rho(t_s) - lambda I||`.

**Statement.**

1. **(JC1) Closures.** Suppose `0 < |x| < pi/8` and `x/pi` is irrational. This holds, for example, for every
   rational `delta` in `(sqrt 2, 2)`, by Niven's theorem. Then for all 10 rescalings `beta`:
   ```text
   SU(V,B) <= G ,     H = S = SU(V,B) .
   ```
   The Lie-algebra input is exact and holds on the whole unitary arc. The depth `>= 2` brackets of
   `e_1, ..., e_5` span `sl(V)` for every `delta` with
   ```text
   P(delta) = 2^17 delta^18 (delta-1)^5 (delta+1)^5 (delta^2-2)^6 (delta^2+2)  !=  0 ,
   ```
   and `P` has no root in `(sqrt 2, 2]`.
2. **(JC2) Fifth-root case (a), with eps to 0.** Here `zeta I in S` and `H/S = 1`. For the fifth-root
   analogue of the tensor-word length of `mod-s2-near-scalar-torelli-closure-dichotomy`,
   `D_zeta(rho) = min{a + b : a - b not 0 mod 5, V_ab^S != 0}`, we have `D_zeta = infinity`. Meanwhile
   ```text
   eps_zeta = max( 2|sin(48x/5)| , 2|sin(12x/5)| )  ->  0   as x -> 0 .
   ```
   All five `zeta in mu_5` occur, including primitive ones. The twisted representation
   `tau = chi_zeta-bar (x) rho`, where `chi_zeta` is the character of `M` with `chi_zeta(t_s) = zeta`,
   satisfies `||tau(t_s) - I|| = eps_zeta` and `cl[H_tau, H_tau] = SU(V,B)`, which contains `mu_5 I`.
   This answers the question of residual (R-a) (need of swarm-0917-w16-w16-deligne-last1) in the
   fifth-root analogue: *yes*. Honest infinite-image representations of `M` give compact groups that are
   topologically generated by one `M`-conjugacy class of `eps`-near-identity unitaries
   `zeta-bar rho(t_y)`, with `eps -> 0`, and whose closed derived subgroup contains the far scalar
   `zeta I`.
3. **(JC3) Closure-functorial Torelli-abelianizations: optimal value, and a class kill.** For an honest
   `rho` and a unit scalar `lambda`, put
   ```text
   Lambda*_lambda(rho) = inf { ||pi(rho(t_s)) - lambda I|| : pi != 0 a continuous f.d. unitary
                                representation of G = cl rho(M) with pi(H) abelian } .
   ```
   - (i) Every representation of `M` of the form `pi o rho` is honest and Torelli-abelian. These include
     the `sigma` of (CD3), and anything else built from `rho` that extends continuously to `G`: tensor
     words, Schur functors, sub- and quotient representations, isotypic projections, conjugates and direct
     sums. Each has distance at least `Lambda*` from `lambda I`.
   - (ii) Exact value: `Lambda*_lambda(rho) = inf_chi max_(g in G) |chi(g rho(t_s) g^-1) - lambda|`, where
     `chi` runs over the characters of `H/S`.
   - (iii) (CD3) gives `c_ab <= Lambda*_omega(rho) <= D(rho) eps_omega` outside case (a).
   - (iv) *On Jones, for all x as in (JC1):* `Lambda*_lambda(rho) = |1 - lambda|`. At `lambda = zeta` a
     primitive fifth root, this is `>= 2 sin(pi/5) = 1.1755...`, while `eps_zeta -> 0`.
   - (v) *Kill.* There is no function `f` with `f(0+) = 0` such that
     `Lambda*_lambda(rho) <= f(||rho(t_s) - lambda I||)` for all honest `rho` and all `lambda` in `mu_15`.
     So an argument for `mod-s2-near-scalar-reps-reduce-to-torelli-abelian` that builds the
     Torelli-abelian approximant out of `cl rho(M)` must use an input that separates `omega` from `mu_5`.
     Examples of such inputs are (DTC2)/(ST5), or the census arithmetic of
     `mod-s2-torelli-abelian-census-limit-is-positive`.
     - The invariant is `Lambda*`.
     - Each target-blind member dies at the step "a closure representation `pi` with `pi(H)` abelian lies
       within `f(eps)` of the approached scalar".
     - At the fifth roots the reduction is nevertheless *true*: `zeta in Z_ab` exactly, via `chi_zeta`.
       The witness is a character that does not depend on `rho`, and no closure-functorial construction
       produces it.
4. **(JC4) Absorption and the missing omega-absorber.**
   - (i) Let `sigma` be honest and Torelli-abelian with `||sigma(t_s) - lambda I|| <= e`. Let `tau` be
     honest with `||tau(t_s) - I|| <= eta` and `mu I in cl[H_tau, H_tau]`. Then `sigma (x) tau` is honest,
     `||(sigma (x) tau)(t_s) - lambda I|| <= e + eta`, and `mu I in cl[H, H]` for `sigma (x) tau`.
     Consequently, if a sequence of `omega`-absorbers exists (`tau_j` with `eta_j -> 0` and
     `omega I in cl[H_(tau_j), H_(tau_j)]`), then `1/3 in Z_ab` forces case (a) at `omega` with `eps -> 0`.
     So a proof that (R-a) is empty for small `eps` would then imply (TAP).
   - (ii) No continuous representation `pi` of `G` in the setting of (JC1) is in case (a) at `omega`, and
     none is an `omega`-absorber. Every scalar in `cl[pi(H), pi(H)] = pi(SU(V,B))` is a fifth root of
     unity. Absorbers must therefore come from outside the tensor category of a single Jones rectangular
     family.

**What this changes.**
- *Residual (R-a) is not a closure or Lie-theoretic impossibility.* Its fifth-root analogue is realized by
  honest infinite-image representations with `eps -> 0` (JC2). So the "no" asked for at `omega` can only
  come from 3-specific arithmetic.
- *Target-blind closure-functorial reductions are dead* (JC3 v), with the optimal loss identified as
  `Lambda*`. The surviving shapes for the reduction at `omega` are:
  - (1) exclude case (a) at `omega` for small `eps`, by an argument that fails at `mu_5`;
  - (2) produce the Torelli-abelian witness independently of `rho`, as at `mu_5`. That is, prove (TAP) or
    its negation directly, which is the census route.
- *Discharges a hypothesis.* The unread hypothesis "the closure of `rho_x(M)` contains `SU(5)`" of the
  subrepresentation sketch in `mod-s2-jones-rectangular-separating-twist-spectrum` is now proved (JC1) for
  `x/pi` irrational. The remaining steps of that sketch are unchanged and still unclaimed.
