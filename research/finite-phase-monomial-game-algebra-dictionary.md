Fix `s<infinity`, a primitive `s`th root `zeta`, and matrices
`A,B in M_n(C)`.  The bisynchronous `s`-monomial isomorphism game has game
algebra *-isomorphic to the universal algebra generated by an order-`s`
quantum-reflection matrix `u` satisfying

```text
uA=Bu.
```

For an entry `u_(ij)`, the finite Fourier projections

```text
a_(ij)^(p)=(1/s) sum_(r=0)^(s-1) zeta^(-rp) u_(ij)^r
```

assemble into a faithful `sn by sn` sudoku magic unitary.  Thus the phase
labels are exact projection-valued game coordinates, not a classical
permutation approximation.

For Butson matrices `H,H'` of order `s`, the paper's block construction
specializes this dictionary to the Hadamard-equivalence game.  Its game
algebra is the linking algebra `A_(H_n^(s+))(H,H')`; perfect commuting play
is equivalent to `s`-quantum equivalence, and perfect quantum-approximate
play is equivalent to a nonzero homomorphism of that linking algebra into
the hyperfinite ultrapower `R^omega`.
