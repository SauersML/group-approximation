Let `A` be a separable unital C-star algebra.  If every primitive quotient
`A/P` carries a faithful MF tracial state, then `A` is MF.  If `A` is
nuclear, it is therefore quasidiagonal.

In particular, a separable unital nuclear C-star algebra is quasidiagonal
whenever every primitive quotient carries a faithful tracial state and
satisfies the UCT.  Thus Problem VII has a positive answer for this
faithfully tracial fibrewise-UCT class, without assuming that `A` has finitely
many ideals or that the primitive traces fit together continuously.
