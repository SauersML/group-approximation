The finite-unit-root theorem says that every nonzero `x` satisfies the premise
of (3).  Consequently (2) and (3) are equivalent.

Assume (2), and let `D` be a nonzero hereditary subalgebra of `A`.  Choose
`0!=a` in `D_+`.  There is a nonzero projection `p` in `A tensor K` with
`[p]<=[a]`.  Since a projection Cuntz below a positive element is
Murray--von Neumann equivalent to a projection in its hereditary subalgebra,
there is such a projection in

```text
Her_(A tensor K)(a tensor e_11)
    = Her_A(a) tensor e_11.
```

It therefore corresponds to a nonzero projection in `Her_A(a) subset D`.
Thus `A` has property (SP).

Conversely, property (SP) passes to matrix algebras and to `A tensor K`.
Given `0!=x=[b]` in `Cu(A)`, choose `epsilon>0` with
`(b-epsilon)_+!=0`.  Property (SP) supplies a nonzero projection `q` in the
hereditary subalgebra generated by this cutdown.  Then

```text
0 != [q] << [b] = x.
```

Thus every nonzero class dominates a nonzero projection class, proving (1)
implies (2) and completing the equivalence.
