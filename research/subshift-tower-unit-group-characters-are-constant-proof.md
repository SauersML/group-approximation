The full proof is in the artifact, §1–§3. Outline:

1. **Matrix units.** `E^C_{ab} = e_{T^aC}u^{a−b}` satisfy `E^C_{ab}E^{C'}_{a'b'} = δ_{CC'}δ_{ba'}E^C_{ab'}`.
   - Nesting gives `E^C_{ab} = Σ_{passages (C',c)} E^{C'}_{c+a,c+b}`, so `A(𝒫_m) ⊆ A(𝒫_n)` is unital and block diagonal.
   - `GL_3(A(𝒫_n)) ⊆ G`: the transvections between coordinates `(p,a)` and `(q,b)` with `p ≠ q` are `e_pq(E^C_{ab})`, and those with `p = q` are their commutators.
2. **Growth of passages.**
   - `min h_C → ∞`, because `T` has no periodic points and `B_n ↓ {y}`.
   - Every orbit segment of length `L` meets a base `C_0` of `𝒫_m` (minimality plus compactness). So each tower `C'` of `𝒫_n` passes through `C_0` at least `⌊h_{C'}/L⌋` times.
   - If `h_{C_0} ≠ 1`, the component of `h ∈ GL_3(A(𝒫_m))` in `GL_{3h_{C'}}(F_2)` has support at least `⌊h_{C'}/L⌋`.
3. **Generation.**
   - `e_ij(e_D) ∈ L_y` for clopen `D`.
   - Write `u = u e_{X∖T^{-1}B_n} + u e_{T^{-1}B_n}`. The first summand is `Σ E^C_{a+1,a} ∈ A(𝒫_n)`. The second lies in `A(𝒫'_m)` once `B_n ∩ B'_m = ∅`, because it avoids the top levels of `𝒫'_m`. The same works for `u^{-1}`.
   - `R` is generated by `LC` and `u^{±1}`, so the elementary identities give `G = ⟨L_y, L_{y'}⟩`.
4. **Bound.** Take `x ∈ GL_N(F_2)` with `supp(x) ≥ αN`.
   - Class size: `dim C(x) ≤ (N − supp x)N` (Jordan forms: `Σ(μ'_k)² ≤ μ'_1·Σμ'_k`), and `|GL_N(F_2)| ≥ 2^{N²}/4`. So `log|x^G|/log|G| ≥ α/2` for `N² ≥ 4/α`.
   - Degree: a nontrivial irreducible representation is faithful, and `GL_N(F_2)` contains an elementary abelian group of order `2^{⌊N²/4⌋}`. So the degree is at least `⌊N²/4⌋`.
   - Larsen–Tiep then gives `|χ(x)|/χ(1) ≤ ⌊N²/4⌋^{−cα/2}`.
5. **Constancy.**
   - `χ|_{L_n}` is a convex combination of normalized irreducible characters of `∏_C GL_{3h_C}(F_2)`. Each nontrivial one is at most the bound of step 4 at `h ∈ L_m ∖ 1`, with `α = 1/(4L)`.
   - So `|χ(h) − c_n| → 0`, where `c_n = τ(p_{L_n})` is the trivial weight. The weights decrease to `c`.
