Assume hyperlinearity and take the canonical microstates supplied by the
Leavitt--atlas criterion.  The fixed-PI claim, instantiated at packet degree
two, produces on one positive carrier a source `s4` evaluation tending to
zero, a transverse four-Pauli evaluation bounded below, and a closed common
transport making the two evaluations asymptotically equal.  This is an
immediate contradiction.

External multiplicity is harmless because both standard-polynomial norms
tensor with the spectator identity.  In particular this route does not use
`common-corner-mutual-bicommutant-extraction` or any scalar joint-commutant
gap; the sole open matrix-coordinate compiler is the common-intertwiner
bridge beneath the fixed-PI claim.
