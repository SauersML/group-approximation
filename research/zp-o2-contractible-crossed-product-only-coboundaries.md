**ESTABLISHED (derivation from Gabe--Szabó Theorems 5.6 and 5.14 and Izumi's
Theorem 4.6; standard inputs flagged in the proof).**  Let `G` be a finite
group, `β : G ↷ B` a pointwise outer action on a unital Kirchberg algebra, and
`ι_B : C → B` the unit.  For a `β`-cocycle `w` put
`w^♯ = KK^G(ι_B, w) ∈ KK^G(C, B)`.

1. **Coboundaries.**  `w` is a coboundary, `w_g = v β_g(v)^*` with one unitary
   `v ∈ B`, iff `w^♯ = KK^G(ι_B)`.
2. **Genuine embeddings.**  Let `(A, α)` be a separable exact unital
   `G`-algebra and `x ∈ KK^G(A, B)`.  Then `x` is the class of a unital
   equivariant \*-homomorphism `A → B` iff `[1_A]_0 ⊗ x = [1_B]_0` in `K_0(B)`
   and `KK^G(ι_A) ⊗ x = KK^G(ι_B)` in `KK^G(C, B)`.
3. **Contractible crossed product.**  Let `B = O_2`, `E = (O_2, β)`, and
   `O_2 ⋊_β G ≃_KK 0`.  Then `KK^G(C, E) ≅ K_0(O_2 ⋊_β G) = 0`, and:
   - every `β`-cocycle is a coboundary, so every cocycle perturbation `β^w`
     is conjugate to `β`;
   - for abelian `G` and every `ψ ∈ Ĝ`, `O_2` contains a unitary `v_ψ` with
     `β_g(v_ψ) = ψ(g) v_ψ` exactly;
   - **universality:** every `x ∈ KK^G(A, E)`, for `(A, α)` separable exact
     unital, is the class of a unital equivariant embedding `A → O_2`.  In
     particular every separable exact unital `G`-algebra embeds unitally and
     equivariantly into `(O_2, β)`;
   - every element of `KK^G(E, E)`, idempotents included, is the class of a
     unital equivariant endomorphism of `(O_2, β)`.
4. **Non-central Rokhlin towers and universality.**  For a pointwise outer
   `β : G ↷ O_2` the following are equivalent:
   - (i) `KK^G(ι_(O_2)) = 0`.  Under Green--Julg this says the averaging
     projection `|G|^(−1) Σ_g u_g` is `0` in `K_0(O_2 ⋊_β G)`;
   - (ii) there are projections `e_g ∈ O_2` with `Σ_g e_g = 1` and
     `β_h(e_g) = e_(hg)`;
   - (iii) every separable exact unital `G`-algebra admits a unital equivariant
     embedding into `(O_2, β)`.
5. **Central eigen-unitaries.**  Let `G = Z/p`, `α : G ↷ O_2` outer with
   `O_2 ⋊_α G ≅ O_2`, and `ψ ∈ Ĝ` nontrivial.  Then `α` has the Rokhlin
   property iff `F_∞(O_2) = A_∞ ∩ O_2'` contains a unitary `w` with
   `α_∞,g(w) = ψ(g) w`.
6. **Submodel criterion.**  With `α`, `ψ` as in item 5, `α` has the Rokhlin
   property iff there is a unital equivariant \*-homomorphism
   `(S, γ) → (F_∞(O_2), α_∞)`, where `γ` is a pointwise outer action on a
   unital Kirchberg algebra with `(1 − ψ)·KK^G(ι_S) = 0` in `KK^G(C, S)`.
   For example, any such `S` with `S ⋊_γ G ≃_KK 0` will do.

**Consequence for the detection step.**  Consider an outer
`α : Z/p ↷ O_2` with contractible crossed product.  It is Rokhlin iff the unit
class vanishes in `K_0(F^α)`.

The non-central version of every obstruction vanishes:
- the cohomology of `α` with coefficients in `U(O_2)` is trivial (item 3);
- `O_2` contains exact eigen-unitaries of every eigenvalue and exact Rokhlin
  towers (items 3, 4);
- `(O_2, α)` contains unital equivariant copies of the Rokhlin model, of
  itself, and of every separable exact unital `G`-algebra (item 3).

Theorem 5.14 applied to a separable invariant submodel `S` of the central
sequence algebra decides detection for `α` exactly when that submodel
satisfies `(1 − ψ)·KK^G(ι_S) = 0` (item 6).  So the cohomology route reduces
to one step: **move a single contractible-crossed-product subsystem from
`O_2` into `F_∞(O_2)`.**  The subsystems known to embed centrally for every
outer action are generated by eigen-isometries (Szabó arXiv:1509.08380,
Theorem 5.15 with Theorem 3.7).  Turning an eigen-isometry into an
eigen-unitary is again the unit-class problem of
`o2-rokhlin-iff-unit-vanishes-in-fixed-central-sequences`.

**Model tests.**
* **Outerness is needed in items 3 and 4.**  The trivial action on `O_2` has
  crossed product `O_2 ⊗ C^(|G|) ≃ 0`.  A nontrivial homomorphism
  `G → U(O_2)` is a cocycle, but not a coboundary, since `v β_g(v)^* = 1`.
  There is no Rokhlin tower, because `β` fixes every projection.
* **The Rokhlin model** `id ⊗ μ_G` satisfies everything.  All its cocycles
  are coboundaries, as Gabe--Szabó Remark 6.5 notes for Rokhlin actions.
* **Contractibility is needed in item 3.**  Gabe--Szabó Remark 6.5, citing
  Barlak--Li [3, Corollary 5.5], exhibits outer `Z/2`-actions on `O_2` that are
  cocycle conjugate but not conjugate.  So some outer `Z/2`-actions on `O_2`
  carry non-coboundary cocycles.  By item 3 their crossed products are not
  contractible.

No priority is claimed.  Items 1 and 2 are immediate from Gabe--Szabó and are
close to their Remark 6.5.  Their Theorem G (dynamical `O_2`-embedding
theorem) gives, for an amenable action on a separable exact algebra and an
isometrically shift-absorbing `β` on a Kirchberg algebra, "a proper cocycle
embedding (A, α) → (B ⊗ O_2, β ⊗ id_(O_2))".  Items 3 and 4 give unital,
genuinely equivariant embeddings into `β` itself.  The action is not assumed
to absorb `id_(O_2)`, and for a phantom it does not.
