The proof is Section 1 of the artifact. It uses only Sylvester axioms; the one **[matricial]** corollary is
marked there.

- **Section 0 (Sylvester consequences).**
  - Unit invariance, subadditivity, and additivity on orthogonal idempotents.
  - **Column submodularity:** `rk col(Y_1 cup Y_2) + rk col(Y_0) <= rk col(Y_1) + rk col(Y_2)` for `Y_0 <= Y_1 cap Y_2`.
    Block operations turn `diag(col Y_1, col Y_2)` into `[[col(Y_1 cup Y_2), *], [0, col Y_0]]`.
  - **Left-ideal invariance:** `rk col` depends only on the left ideal the list generates.
  - For finite `P` with `|P|` invertible, `phi_P = rk(e_P)`.
- **Lemma 1.1.** `V = <V_(X \ [00]), V_(X \ [11])>`.
  - Clopen transpositions generate `V`, and they fragment into small pieces.
  - A piece meeting both `[00]` and `[11]` is `r r' r`, routed through a clopen in `[01]`.
  - Each rigid stabilizer is generated by finitely many clopen `p`-cycles.
- **Theorem 1.2.**
  - **Upper bound:** `phi_V <= a`, from tree groups of unbounded order.
  - **Lower bound:** each generator `s` cuts the tail rank `rk(e_(T_k))` by `(1 - a) p^(-k)(1 - 1/p)`, through the
    clopen-cycle subgroup `<s> x T_k`. Three rounds of column submodularity, through `T_k x T'_k`, give
    `phi_V >= a - (m + m' + 3) p^(-k)`.
- **Corollary 1.3.** The dichotomy with `simple-group-rank-functions-are-augmentation-or-detecting`, and the
  reduced torsion law. The matricial compression uses block-triangular idempotent ranks and perfection of `V`.
