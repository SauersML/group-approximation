The proof is Section 1 of the artifact.

- **Lemma 1.1.**
  - `V = <V_U, V_W>` with `U = X \ [00]` and `W = X \ [11]`. Clopen transpositions generate `V`, since `V` is
    simple.
  - Fragment each transposition into small pieces. A piece that meets both `[00]` and `[11]` equals `r r' r`,
    with `r` in `V_W` and `r'` in `V_U`, routed through a clopen `D <= [01]`.
  - `V_U` and `V_W` are isomorphic to `V`, and each is generated by finitely many clopen `p`-cycles.
- **Theorem 1.2.**
  - **Upper bound:** `rk(f) <= a`, from tree groups of unbounded order.
  - **Lower bound, step 1:** with a tail `T_k` of `k` disjoint `p`-cycles in `[00]`, each generator `s` in `S_U`
    gives the clopen-cycle subgroup `<s> x T_k`, so `Fix(s)` cuts `e_(T_k) M` by rank
    `(1 - a) p^(-k) (1 - 1/p)`.
  - **Step 2:** the iterated modular law gives `rk(Fix(S_U) cap e_(T_k) M) >= a - m p^(-k)`, and the same holds
    for `S_W` with a tail `T'_k` in `[11]`.
  - **Step 3:** `T_k x T'_k` is a clopen-cycle subgroup of fixed rank `a + (1 - a) p^(-2k)`. Two more modular
    steps put an ideal of rank `>= a - (m + m' + 3) p^(-k)` inside `Fix(S_U cup S_W) = Fix(V)`.
- **Corollary 1.3.** Faithfulness gives the rigid-stabilizer equality. The corner compression is block
  triangular, and idempotent block-triangular elements have rank `rk(f) + rk(e'_P)`, so the torsion constant
  of the compressed model is `0`. Perfection of `V` makes the compressed model nontrivial.
