Full derivation in the artifact, §N3. Let `N = <u, v>` be the image of `Z^2` and
`p = inf supp(N)`. Then `p` is the left endpoint of a bump of `u` or `v`, is fixed by `N`,
and is fixed by `t`, since `t` normalizes `N`. The homomorphism `σ(w) = log w'(p+)` on `N`
satisfies `σ∘A = σ`, so it vanishes on the finite-index subgroup `(A − I) Z^2`. `R` is
torsion-free, so `σ = 0`. But the generator whose bump starts at `p` has slope `≠ 1` there.
