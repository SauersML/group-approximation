Notation is that of the claim. Bishop–Schesler's definitions (§3, `lem:branch-group-arg`) were read
at source on MSI. Write `w_x = 0`, `w_y = 100`, `w_z = 101`, `w_o = 1100`, `w_p = 1101`,
`w_q = 1110`, `w_r = 1111`, and `c_n(a) = w_r d` for `a ∈ Q_n ∖ {o}`, `d ∈ D_n`.

**Item 1.**
- *Φ is a homeomorphism.* `C_n = {c_n(a)}` is a complete prefix code, and complete prefix codes
  concatenate to a homeomorphism `∏ X_n → {0,1}^N` that maps cylinders to cylinders.
- *Rooted elements.* An element of `B_0` permutes `X_1`, so its image is the prefix permutation of
  `C_1`, an element of `V`.
- *Spinal elements.* By Bishop–Schesler's recursion, `h̃^[ℓ]` fixes `X_(ℓ+1)` and has these
  sections:
  - at `x_(ℓ+1)`: `h̃^[ℓ+1]`;
  - at `y_(ℓ+1)`: rooted `φ_(ℓ+2)(g)`;
  - at `z_(ℓ+1)`: rooted `ψ_(ℓ+2)(σ)`;
  - everywhere else: the identity.
- *Reading off ρ_ℓ(h).* Under `Φ` the vertex `x_1⋯x_ℓ` becomes the cylinder `0^ℓ C`. Its non-`x`
  children fill `A_ℓ = 0^ℓ 1 C`, with `y_(ℓ+1)` at `0^ℓ 1 00 C` and `z_(ℓ+1)` at `0^ℓ 1 01 C`. So
  `ĥ = Φ h̃^[0] Φ^(-1)` acts on `A_ℓ ≅ C` (drop `0^ℓ 1`) as follows:
  - on `Y = 00C`: by `00 c_(ℓ+2)(a) u ↦ 00 c_(ℓ+2)(φ_(ℓ+2)(g)a) u`;
  - on `Z = 01C`: by the same rule with `ψ_(ℓ+2)(σ)`;
  - elsewhere: the identity.
- *Result.* This is `ρ_ℓ(h) ∈ V`. It is a homomorphism in `h`, because the two pieces have
  disjoint supports. `ĥ` fixes `p` and is V-local off `p`. So `ΦΓΦ^(-1) = ⟨Φ B_0 Φ^(-1), Ĥ⟩ ≤ Γ_ρ`.

**Item 2.**
- *The scaffold is level-independent.* `ψ_(ℓ+2)(σ)` permutes `{x,y,z,o,p,q}_(ℓ+2)`, whose
  codewords do not depend on `ℓ`, and fixes every `c(a)` with `a ≠ o`. So
  `ρ_ℓ((1,σ)) = e(σ) ∈ V`, supported in `Z` and independent of `ℓ`. Hence `t^(-1) σ̂_p t = σ̂_p`,
  using the conventions of `annular-free-zoom-window-equations-proof`: `τ^(-δ) ĥ τ^δ` acts on
  `A_n` by `ρ_(n+δ)(h)`.
- *The germ map is injective.* `ĥ_p = 1` iff `ρ_ℓ(h) = 1` for all large `ℓ`, iff
  `φ_(ℓ+2)(g) = 1` and `ψ_(ℓ+2)(σ) = 1` for all large `ℓ`. Now `φ_m(g) = 1` iff `λ_m(g) = 1`,
  because an identity permutation is even. That happens iff `g ∈ N_m`, since `N_m` is normal and
  `λ_m` is the left action on `G/N_m`. And `∩ N_m = 1`, while `ψ_m` is faithful.
- *The germ group.* The germ group at `p` is generated by `Ĥ_p` and the germs of `V_p`, which form
  `⟨t⟩ ≅ Z` because `p = 0^∞`. The `Y`- and `Z`-supported parts commute, and conjugating by `t`
  preserves the `Y`/`Z` splitting of each annulus. So `(Γ_ρ)_p = ⟨Ĝ_p, t⟩ × Â_p`, where the
  factor `Â_p` is detected on `Z` and `⟨Ĝ_p, t⟩` acts trivially there.

**Item 3.** This is item 2 of `annular-hosts-with-free-zoom-germs-are-not-finitely-presented`,
with the central factor carried along.
1. The kernel of `F(ĝ, σ̂, t) → (Γ_ρ)_p` is the normal closure of four sets:
   - the words `t^(-δ) v t^δ`, where `v` is a word in the `ĝ` with `v_p = 1`;
   - the commutators `[σ̂, ĝ_j]`;
   - the commutators `[σ̂, t]`;
   - the relators of `A`.
   All of these lie in the `t`-exponent-zero subgroup `K`, the free product of the shifted
   copies.
2. Let `Γ_ρ` have relators of length at most `R_0`. Take the window words from item 1 of the
   window lemma with `R = R_0`, and write each as a product of `K`-conjugates of finitely many
   shifted generators of that kernel. Let `Δ` bound the shifts and `N_0` the depths beyond which
   the `v` used satisfy `ρ_m(v) = 1`.
3. Choose `L ≥ max(2B + 2, N_0 + Δ + B + 1)` and a generator `g_i` with `ρ_L(g_i) ≠ 1`. One
   exists because `G` is infinite, so some generator lies outside `N_(L+2)` for every `L`. Choose
   `κ ∈ V` supported in `Y` and not commuting with `ρ_L(g_i)`. It exists because the restriction
   of `V` to a cylinder has trivial centre.
4. Put `ρ'_L(g) = κ ρ_L(g) κ^(-1)` on `G` and keep every other datum, including all the `A`-data.
   `ρ'_L` is still a homomorphism on `H`, since `κ` commutes with the `Z`-supported `e(σ)`. The
   evaluation `ev_k(·; ρ')` is a homomorphism on `K`, and it kills each kernel generator used:
   - `t^(-δ) v t^δ`: it maps to `κ`-conjugates of trivial elements, since the depth is at least
     `N_0`;
   - `[σ̂, t]`: it maps to `e(σ) e(σ)^(-1) = 1`;
   - `[σ̂, ĝ]`: it maps to 1, because `Y` and `Z` are disjoint;
   - the relators of `A`: they map to 1, since `e` is a homomorphism.
5. So every relation of length at most `R_0` holds for `ρ'` (window lemma, item 1). Local rigidity
   (item 1 of `annular-free-product-germ-hosts-are-not-finitely-presented`) then forces `ĝ'_i = ĝ_i`,
   but `ρ'_L(g_i) ≠ ρ_L(g_i)`. This is a contradiction.

**Item 4.**
- *Splitting a window word.* Write each window word `u_j` as `u_j^Y · u_j^Z`, the ordered products
  of its `G`-letters and of its `A`-letters. They can be separated because `Y`- and `Z`-supported
  elements commute.
- *Both parts are trivial.* `ev_n(u_j^Z) = e(product of the A-letters)` does not depend on `n`, and
  `u_j` has trivial germ. So both parts vanish at all large depths.
- *The perturbed data satisfy the short relations.* Take `ρ'` that changes only the `G`-data, is a
  homomorphism into `V_Y` at each depth, agrees up to depth `2B + 1`, and satisfies every recurrence
  `ev_n(u_j^Y; ρ') = 1`. By the window lemma it satisfies every relation of length at most `R_0`.
- *Conclusion.* Local rigidity gives `ρ' = ρ`.

**Item 5.**
- *ψ is an injective homomorphism.* Conjugation by `t^δ` is an automorphism of the germ group, and
  `g ↦ ĝ_p` is injective (item 2).
- *Uniformity.* Let `s_1, …, s_k` generate `G_1`, which is finitely generated since it has finite
  index in `G`. The germ equality `t^(-δ) ŝ_i t^δ = ψ(s_i)^` means `ρ_(m+δ)(s_i) = ρ_m(ψ(s_i))` for
  all `m ≥ m_i`. Put `m_0 = max m_i`. For `m ≥ m_0` the homomorphisms `ρ_(m+δ)|_(G_1)` and
  `ρ_m ∘ ψ` agree on generators, hence on `G_1`.
- *The chain.* For `g ∈ G_1` and `m ≥ m_0`, `φ_(m+δ+2)(g)` and `φ_(m+2)(ψ(g))` are the same element
  of `V`, so one is trivial iff the other is. By item 2 this says `g ∈ N_(m+δ+2)` iff
  `ψ(g) ∈ N_(m+2)`, i.e. `N_(m+δ+2) ∩ G_1 = ψ^(-1)(N_(m+2))`. Shifting `m` by 2 gives the claim.
- *Codes play no role.* The codes `D_n` enter only through "the same element of `V`". Triviality
  of the prefix permutation of a complete code does not depend on the code.
