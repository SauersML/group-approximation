Let `pi : G -> R^x` be the cover, with Kazhdan kernel `N`. Let
`Gamma <= G_L` be the Leavitt pair, with compressors `u, v`, commuting factor
`J`, and `j, gamma` witnessing its defect. Put

```text
Gamma_hat = pi^-1(Gamma),     G_hat = pi^-1(G_L).
```

- **Property (T).** Each of `Gamma_hat` and `G_hat` is an extension of a
  Kazhdan group by the Kazhdan group `N`.
- **Infranormal.** Lifts `u_hat`, `v_hat` satisfy
  `u_hat Gamma_hat u_hat^-1 = pi^-1(u Gamma u^-1) <= Gamma_hat`. Elements of
  `N <= Gamma_hat` compress `Gamma_hat` trivially. The semigroup generated by
  `Gamma_hat`, `u_hat` and `v_hat` maps onto the generating set of `G_L` and
  contains `N`, so it generates `G_hat`.
- **Strict and not normal.** The image of `Gamma_hat` is `Gamma`, which is not
  normal in `G_L`. Pulling back preserves strictness of the inclusion
  `u Gamma u^-1 <= Gamma`.
- **Absolute defect trivial.** `N` is infinite, so `Gamma_hat` is an infinite
  Kazhdan subgroup of `G`, hence non-elementary. By
  `hyperbolic-compression-centralizer-rigidity`, every compressor normalizes
  `C_G(Gamma_hat)`, and so does `G_hat`, which the compressors generate as a
  group. Therefore `g z g^-1` centralizes `Gamma_hat`.
- **Relative defect nontrivial.** Let `z` be any lift of `j`. For
  `gamma' in Gamma_hat`, `pi([z, gamma']) = [j, pi(gamma')] = 1`, so
  `[z, Gamma_hat] <= N`. For a lift `gamma_hat` of `gamma`,
  `pi([u_hat z u_hat^-1, gamma_hat]) = [u j u^-1, gamma] != 1`, so the
  commutator is not in `N`. ∎
