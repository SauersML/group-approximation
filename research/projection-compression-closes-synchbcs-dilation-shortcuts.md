# Exact dilation does not weaken the group-corner gate

Let `A_I` denote the finite SynchBCS algebra in the target claim.  It is
generated by finitely many question-outcome projections, subject to the
question PVM relations and the finitely many forbidden-overlap relations.
The source theorem gives two facts which will be used below:

1. `A_I` has a tracial state; and
2. every tracial state on `A_I` is non-Connes-embeddable.

No chosen source trace and no faithfulness assumption on a map out of
`A_I` will be needed.

## 1. A sharp compression of a projection is reducing

Let `M` be a unital star-algebra represented on a Hilbert space, and let
`p,P in M` be projections.  Put `e=pPp in pMp`.  If `e` is a projection,
then

~~~text
e-e^2
 =pPp-pPpPp
 =pP(1-p)Pp
 =((1-p)Pp)^* ((1-p)Pp).                    (1)
~~~

The left side is zero.  Hence `(1-p)Pp=0`.  Taking adjoints gives
`pP(1-p)=0`, and therefore

~~~text
[p,P]=0.                                                   (2)
~~~

Equivalently, `P` is block diagonal for
`pH direct_sum (1-p)H`.  This elementary identity is the exact sharpness
wall: a Naimark compression can be genuinely nonreducing only while its
compressed effects are not projections.

Apply this separately to ambient Naimark projections `P_(q,a)`.  If every
compressed effect

~~~text
e_(q,a)=pP_(q,a)p
~~~

is one of the sharp SynchBCS outcome projections, then `p` commutes with
every `P_(q,a)`.  If the `e_(q,a)` satisfy the PVM and forbidden-overlap
relations, the universal property gives a unital star-homomorphism

~~~text
Phi:A_I -> pMp,    Phi(e_(q,a))=pP_(q,a)p.                (3)
~~~

Thus an exact Naimark realization is already a literal reducing-corner
realization.

## 2. The full Stinespring multiplicative-domain lemma

The same conclusion does not depend on projection generators.  Let
`pi:C->M` be a unital star-representation of a unital C-star-algebra and
define the compression

~~~text
Psi(a)=p pi(a) p.
~~~

Suppose that `Psi` is a unital star-homomorphism on a C-star-subalgebra
`D subset C`.  For `a in D`, multiplicativity gives

~~~text
0
 =Psi(a^*a)-Psi(a)^*Psi(a)
 =p pi(a)^*(1-p)pi(a)p
 =((1-p)pi(a)p)^*((1-p)pi(a)p),              (4)
~~~

so `(1-p)pi(a)p=0`.  The other Schwarz defect is

~~~text
0
 =Psi(aa^*)-Psi(a)Psi(a)^*
 =p pi(a)(1-p)pi(a)^*p,                      (5)
~~~

and hence `(1-p)pi(a)^*p=0`.  Taking adjoints in the last equality gives
`p pi(a)(1-p)=0`.  Consequently

~~~text
[p,pi(a)]=0    for every a in D.                           (6)
~~~

It follows that `Psi(D)=p pi(D)p` is not merely a multiplicative
compression: it is the restriction of `pi(D)` to a reducing summand.

In particular, a Stinespring or matrix-unit dilation whose compression is
exactly multiplicative on all of `A_I` gives (3).  Exact matrix-unit
relations only within one question context do not meet this hypothesis;
they yield reducing blocks for that context alone and say nothing about
the simultaneous reduction of the other contexts.

## 3. Application to a finite-phase Pauli crossed product

Let

~~~text
B=L_omega(K) crossed_product_beta G
~~~

be the finite tracial Pauli-basis crossed product from the target claim,
let `n<infinity`, and let `0!=p in M_n(B)` be a projection.  Set
`M=M_n(B)` in Sections 1 or 2.

Every one of the proposed exact constructions has now produced a unital
star-homomorphism

~~~text
Phi:A_I -> pM_n(B)p.                                      (7)
~~~

This statement is stronger than the requested embedding gate: `Phi` need
not be injective.  Let `tau_B` be the canonical faithful trace and put

~~~text
tau_p(x)
 =(tr_n tensor tau_B)(x)/(tr_n tensor tau_B)(p).           (8)
~~~

Then `sigma=tau_p circle Phi` is a tracial state on `A_I`.  Faithfulness of
`tau_p` shows that the null ideal of `sigma` is exactly `ker(Phi)`:
for `a in A_I`,

~~~text
sigma(a^*a)=0  iff  Phi(a)=0.                              (9)
~~~

Therefore the GNS von Neumann algebra of `(A_I,sigma)` is trace
preservingly isomorphic to

~~~text
W^*(Phi(A_I)) subset pM_n(B)p.                            (10)
~~~

By the defining property of `A_I`, this von Neumann algebra is non-CE.
Connes embeddability passes to finite amplifications, nonzero corners, and
tracial von Neumann subalgebras.  Hence (10) implies

~~~text
B is non-Connes-embeddable.                               (11)
~~~

This proves in particular that no CE Pauli crossed product can contain an
exact amplified Naimark, Stinespring, matrix-unit, or direct corner model
of these SynchBCS generators.

## 4. The finite-phase basis compiler finishes the group step

For completeness, the projective Pauli words and actor unitaries form an
orthonormal projective group basis of `B`.  The calculation in
`finite-phase-basis-crossed-product-and-circularity-proof` gives

~~~text
B isomorphic to L_Omega(H),
H=K semidirect G,                                         (12)
~~~

where `Omega` is a scalar two-cocycle.  Finite phase means that its range
lies in some finite root group `mu_m`.  The honest countable group

~~~text
Gamma_hat=mu_m times_Omega H                              (13)
~~~

is a finite central extension of `H`.  The central-character projection
`p_chi in L(Gamma_hat)` has trace `1/m` and

~~~text
p_chi L(Gamma_hat) p_chi isomorphic to B.                 (14)
~~~

If `Gamma_hat` were hyperlinear, `L(Gamma_hat)` and its positive corner
(14) would be CE, contrary to (11).  Hence any construction in (7)
produces a countable nonhyperlinear group.  This is why exact dilation is
not an intermediate shortcut: it is already a solution of the root
problem.

## 5. What the inverse-semigroup proposal can and cannot do

For every inverse semigroup `S`, its idempotents `E(S)` form a commuting
semilattice.  In every star-representation of the inverse-semigroup
algebra, those idempotents are commuting projections.  Therefore the
natural globalization proposal in which every question-outcome atom is
an idempotent of one `E(S)`, or a finite sum of orthogonal such
idempotents, makes all outcome projections commute globally.

The image of `A_I` would then be a nonzero unital commutative C-star
algebra.  Composing its quotient map with any character of that
commutative algebra gives a unital one-dimensional representation of
`A_I`.  This contradicts the fact that `A_I` has no unital
finite-dimensional representation.  Thus one common idempotent
semilattice cannot encode this source.

This does not rule out separate inverse semigroups for separate question
contexts.  It explains their exact residue.  Their idempotent atoms can be
matched locally, but transition partial isometries around a cycle carry a
holonomy which need not be trivial or scalar.  Making all those local
pieces act inside one Pauli crossed product while preserving every source
product is precisely the construction of (7).

## 6. Why local matrix-unit refinement stops at holonomy

For one question context, its outcome algebra is a finite-dimensional
commutative algebra, so it has an exact atomic projection model in a
finite Pauli or signed-Hecke block.  On a selected finite list of context
overlaps, a tracial model supplies finite overlap distributions.  At the
level of a rational approximation to those distributions, finite
amplification clears denominators and matches the corresponding
multiplicities.

This is useful local data, but it does not choose compatible transition
unitaries.  Edgewise choices are unique only modulo the commutants of the
matched blocks.  Products of those gauges around context cycles are the
remaining holonomies.  Finite amplification enlarges those commutants and
does not force the cycle products to be trivial.

If a common ambient matrix-unit refinement did make every source product
exact after compression by `p`, Section 2 would force `p` to reduce the
generated source algebra and would give (7).  Hence neither rational
multiplicity matching nor finite amplification supplies the missing
global coherence automatically.

## 7. Exact boundary

The following implications are unconditional:

~~~text
exact sharp Naimark compression
       or exact multiplicative Stinespring compression
    => unital SynchBCS corner map (7)
    => non-CE Pauli crossed product
    => countable nonhyperlinear finite central extension. (15)
~~~

What remains open is the first construction in (15) for the pinned finite
SynchBCS source.  One must either build (7) directly in a genuinely
non-CE finite-phase Pauli crossed product, or realize the nonlinear source
products by new non-Cartan group-algebra elements.  The standard dilation
theorems, one common inverse-semigroup semilattice, and local matrix-unit
refinement do not supply that global multiplication.  No nonhyperlinear
group is constructed here.
