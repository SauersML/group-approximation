One countable separating family has exact common stabilizer `C`, so
the stabilizer theorem supplies scalar relative embeddability. Models
for different outside elements combine by weighted direct sums.

Conversely scalar relative embeddability gives a canonical CE free
binary coset-wreath model. Its base-lamp projection commutes exactly
with `C` and has nonzero commutator with every outside actor word.

For the least scalar-RE overgroup, choose one projection/model
witness for each element excluded by the universal stabilizer
intersection. There are only countably many actor elements, so the
stabilizer theorem makes that intersection scalar-RE. A canonical
binary lamp for any scalar-RE intermediate subgroup proves that the
intersection is contained in it. Section 3 of the artifact supplies
the quantifier and empty-family details.
