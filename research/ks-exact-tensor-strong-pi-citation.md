**Established by citation.**  Eberhard Kirchberg and Adam Sierakowski,
[*Filling families and strong pure
infiniteness*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.08519), Theorem 1.3 (and the
detailed tensor-product argument in Section 6), prove exactly that the
minimal tensor product of a C*-algebra and an exact C*-algebra is strongly
purely infinite when at least one of the two factors is strongly purely
infinite.  The theorem explicitly notes that it does not matter which factor
is the strongly purely infinite one.

This node imports only that theorem.  It does not import a converse, and it
does not omit the requirement that at least one factor be exact.  The exact
factor may or may not be the factor carrying strong pure infiniteness.
