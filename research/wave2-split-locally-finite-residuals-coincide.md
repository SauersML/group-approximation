Let E=L semidirect G, with L locally finite and G residually finite.
Then the intersection of kernels of all homomorphisms from E to
word-hyperbolic groups equals Res_fin(E). Every such homomorphism kills
the entire finite residual of E, not just one compression witness.

The split hypothesis is retained. This does NOT imply that all
word-hyperbolic quotients of E are residually finite: a quotient can
acquire a new finite-quotient obstruction. It rules out transporting
existing finite-residual elements to nontrivial hyperbolic images.
