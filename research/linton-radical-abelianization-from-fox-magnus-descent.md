Restrict the exact mod-`k` Magnus embedding of `G/R'` to its normal subgroup
`R/R'`.  The image is the cyclic `ZH`-submodule generated by `D(r)` modulo
`k`, and purity of `D(N/N')` in `(ZH)^d` identifies that submodule with

    ZH/kZH ~= (Z/kZ)[H].

Since `R/R'` is the abelianization of `R`, this recovers the target as an
internal layer of the whole-group quotient.  The original Lyndon proof route
remains the shorter independent derivation; this route records that the new
Fox--Magnus theorem genuinely subsumes it.
