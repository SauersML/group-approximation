First quotient by cyclic rotation and inversion and reject adjacent inverse
letters, leaving `1682` and `58826` representatives in lengths five and
seven.

Expand the two bridge labels:

```text
h_1=q_0p_1,       k_2=p_0e_1.                            (OFP1)
```

Collect maximal syllables in `G_0` and `G_1`.  Inside either factor use only
the exact relations

```text
a^2=e^2=q^2=1,   [a,e]=[a,q]=[e,q]=1,   pp^(-1)=1.       (OFP2)
```

The first six relations hold because `a,e` occupy one abelian root subgroup
and the two roots forming `q` are disjoint from it.  Thus
`<a,e,q>` is the elementary abelian group generated by the three displayed
nonzero root packets.  Repeatedly delete trivial `(OFP2)` blocks and merge
newly adjacent syllables in the same free factor.

For every remaining syllable the artifact supplies a basis vector in the
standard infinite-binary-word Leavitt module which the corresponding
`20 x 20` elementary/permutation matrix word moves.  This is an exact
nonidentity certificate.  After the `(OFP2)` simplifier the MSI audit reports

```text
length 5: 1682 orbit representatives, 0 survivors;
length 7: 58826 orbit representatives, 0 survivors.      (OFP3)
```

Every expanded word therefore has a nonempty alternating free-product
normal form whose syllables are nonidentity, so no word is trivial.

Before exact simplification the only unwitnessed syllables were

```text
(ae)^2, (aq)^2, (qa)^2,
e(aq)^2e, p(aq)^2p^(-1), q(ae)^2q, e(qa)^2e.
```

All are literally consequences of `(OFP2)`; after deleting them, no
exception remains.  This also makes the finite audit transparent rather
than relying on a probabilistic word test.
