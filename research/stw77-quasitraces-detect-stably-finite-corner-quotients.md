title: Quasitraces on a unital corner are exactly stably finite quotient witnesses

Let `C` be a nonzero unital C*-algebra.  The following are equivalent.

1. `C` admits a normalized `2`-quasitrace.
2. `C` has a nonzero stably finite quotient.

Every normalized `2`-quasitrace on `C` descends through its null ideal to a
faithful normalized `2`-quasitrace on a nonzero stably finite quotient.  If
`C` is exact, the two conditions are also equivalent to the existence of a
tracial state on `C`.

Thus every nonzero projection corner has an ordinary state, but it has a
normalized quasitracial witness precisely when some nonzero quotient of the
corner is stably finite.  Mere failure of the corner unit to be properly
infinite does not imply either condition.
