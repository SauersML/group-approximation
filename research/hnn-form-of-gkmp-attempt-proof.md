The body carries the graded attempt.  Its content as a route: the HNN edge
is MF iff the regular model `pi` of `L` is unitarily equivalent in the matrix
ultraproduct to its shear `pi . v` by `v = rho_C . phi`; Fell absorption gives
this at each finite quotient of the residually finite `C`, and the remaining
open lemma is uniformity of the conjugators along the residual chain.
