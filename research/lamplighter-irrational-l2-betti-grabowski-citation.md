- Ł. Grabowski, *Irrational l2-invariants arising from the lamplighter group*,
  arXiv:1009.0229v4. Abstract: "Finally we show that the l2-Betti number of a matrix over the
  integral group ring of the group Z_p ≀ Z, where p is a natural number greater than 1, can be
  irrational." **Theorem 2**: "There is a matrix T with entries in the group ring Z[Z_p ≀ Z]
  such that dim_vN ker T = [an explicit series in (p−1)/p]". The introduction defines an
  "l2-Betti number arising from Γ" as the von Neumann dimension of the kernel of a matrix over
  `Z[Γ]`.
- Ł. Grabowski, *On Turing dynamical systems and the Atiyah problem*, arXiv:1004.2030v4.
  **Theorem 1.1**: "The set of l2-Betti numbers arising from the group (Z/2Z ≀ Z)^3 contains
  1/64 − 1/8 Σ_(k=1)^∞ 1/2^(k²+4k+6), which is an irrational number." Here the definition uses
  `M_k(QG)`.
