L. O. Clark, R. Exel, E. Pardo, A. Sims and C. Starling, *Simplicity of algebras associated to non-Hausdorff
groupoids*, arXiv:1806.04362. Quoted from the arXiv TeX source, fetched to MSI on 2026-09-13.

- Theorem grigsimple (l.2282): "Let $G$ be the Grigorchuk group, let $(G,X)$ be its self-similar action, and let $\ggx$
  be the associated groupoid. Then (1) For any field $\KK$ of characteristic zero, $A_\KK(\ggx)$ is simple, and (2)
  $\ogx$ is simple."
- Corollary grigZ2notsimple (l.2432): "Let $G$ be the Grigorchuk group, let $(G,X)$ be its self-similar action, and let
  $\ggx$ be the associated groupoid. Then $\s_{\Z_2}(\ggx)$ is nonzero, and hence the Steinberg algebra
  $A_{\Z_{2}}(\ggx)$ is not simple."
- The same paper, just after the theorem, says "simplicity can fail when $\KK$ has nonzero characteristic, see
  Corollary~\ref{cor:grigZ2notsimple}". Its Lemma compactopennotregularopen exhibits a compact open set that is not
  regular open.
