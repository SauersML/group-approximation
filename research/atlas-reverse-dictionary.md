**Theorem (machine-verified exact computation).**  Each of the twelve
adjacent-transvection atlas generators of `P̄ = A₈ * A₈ ↠ Q` is an
explicit word in the hundred rank-five Steinberg generators
`x_kl(b) = 1 + E_k b E_l^*` of the join code — and the expressions are
remarkably short: **eight of the twelve are single generators**

```text
x_51(1), x_15(1), x_12(1), x_21(1), x_53(1), x_35(1), x_34(1), x_43(1),
```

and the remaining four are products of exactly two (e.g. the first
chart's `p₁`-transvection is `x_35(E)·x_45(F)`).  All twelve verified
exactly by binary Leavitt arithmetic.

**Consequences.**

1. **Surjectivity.**  In any quotient `G = P̄/⟨⟨T⟩⟩` where the translated
   Steinberg relators die, the comparison map `ψ : St₅(L) → G` hits every
   atlas generator, hence is surjective.  This closes a genuine gap in
   the naive translation program: `G` is generated by the two charts, not
   a priori by the images of the `x_kl(b)`, and centrality transport
   through `ψ` needs surjectivity.
2. **A literature dependency removed.**  Generation of `Q` by the
   rank-five Steinberg generators would otherwise be cited from the
   rank-five analogue of the recorded rank-three generation theorem; here
   it is proved in-repo: the twelve atlas generators generate `Q` by
   construction, and each is an explicit `x`-word.
3. Together with `atlas-rank-five-dictionary`, the two charts and the
   rank-five root system now translate into each other explicitly in both
   directions — the atlas and the Steinberg presentation are one
   presentation away from interchangeable.
