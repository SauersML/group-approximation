---

Extract from any admissible microstate a common classical mixture of whole
equivariant self-maps on ONE source-sized paired label space, against which
the Leavitt multiplication table can be tested. This is the gate the
`1/36` (or `2−√3`) endpoint consumes.

The rectangular escape (rectangular-escape) shows source-only measurability plus
equivariance is insufficient: deterministic fully-equivariant maps evade the
`1/36` theorem by enlarging their codomains.
