## Statement

Let `tr = d^(-1) Tr` on `M_d(C)`. Let `U_1, U_2, ...` be independent Haar-distributed unitaries in `M_d(C)`. Let
`(W_i^d)_(i in I)` be deterministic matrices in `M_d(C)` whose joint distribution has a limit: for every
noncommutative monomial `q`, `tr q(W^d)` converges as `d -> infinity`.

- **(a) Limit and freeness.** For every noncommutative polynomial `Q`, `E tr Q(W^d, U_1, U_1^*, ...)` converges. The
  limit functional `phi` makes the subalgebras generated by `{W_i}`, by `{u_1, u_1^*}`, by `{u_2, u_2^*}`, ... free.
- **(b) Concentration.** For each word `W_1 U_(i_1)^(e_1) ... W_n U_(i_n)^(e_n)` with `e_j = +-1` and each
  `epsilon > 0`, the probability that its normalized trace differs from the corresponding value of `phi` by at least
  `epsilon` is `O(d^(-2))`. In particular it converges in probability.

A standard remark, not part of the import: `E tr U^j = 0` for `j != 0`, by invariance of Haar measure under
`U -> e^(i theta) U`. Hence `phi(u^j) = 0` for `j != 0`, and each `u_s` is a Haar unitary in the limit.
