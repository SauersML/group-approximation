Citation import, secondary. Three independent secondary sources are quoted below. The
first gives `F_∞` directly. The other two give `FP_∞`, which is what the routes of this
lane use.

- **`F_∞`.** M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Bestvina–Brady discrete Morse theory and Vietoris–Rips
  complexes*, arXiv:1812.10976v3, §6.1. The remark is recorded verbatim in
  `contracting-combings-give-contractible-rips-complexes-citation`, which was read by
  another lane on 2026-09-13 and re-extracted on 2026-09-16: "groups admitting bounded
  combings include the important family of automatic groups (in this case the function
  φ can be taken to be φ(n) = Cn+D for constants C and D), so this implies automatic
  groups are of type F_∞." This lane did not re-read Zaremsky.
- **`FP_∞`, read by this lane on 2026-09-16.** M. Brittenham, S. Hermiller and
  A. Johnson, *Homology and closure properties of autostackable groups*,
  arXiv:1506.00071v1 (30 May 2015), p. 3, verbatim: "A group G has homological type FP_n
  if there is a partial projective resolution of length n, by finitely generated
  ZG-modules, of the module Z (with trivial G action). In the case that G has type FP_n
  for all n ∈ N, then G is said to be of type FP_∞. Alonso [1] has shown that all groups
  that admit a bounded combing, including all automatic groups, have type FP_∞." Here
  [1] is J. M. Alonso, *Combings of groups*, in Algorithms and Classification in
  Combinatorial Group Theory (Berkeley, 1989), MSRI Publ. 23, Springer, 1992, 165–178.
- **`FP_∞`, read by this lane on 2026-09-16.** A. Engel and C. Wulff, *Coronas for
  properly combable spaces*, arXiv:1711.06836v3 (13 October 2021). On p. 28, §3.6, for a
  finitely generated automatic group with uniqueness: "We then conclude from [ECH+92,
  Lemma 2.3.2] that G is combable by a combing which is quasi-geodesic". They explain
  that their combings are what "Alonso [Alo92] calls a bounded combing". On p. 50:
  "Recall that a group G is called of type FP∞ if there exists a (possibly infinite)
  projective resolution of Z over ZG by finitely generated modules. [...] Now if G is
  combable, it is of type FP∞ (Alonso [Alo92, Theorem 2])".
- **Finite presentability.** S. Rees, arXiv:2205.14911v1 (30 May 2022), p. 10,
  Proposition 2.1: "Every automatic group is finitely presented, with a quadratic upper
  bound on the Dehn function, and hence soluble word problem."

**Not read.** Alonso's paper. The passage from `FP_∞` plus finite presentability to
`F_∞` is Wall's theorem, from memory; Zaremsky's remark states `F_∞` directly. The
`FP_∞` consequence recorded in the claim needs only the `F_∞` statement and cellular
chains, or either `FP_∞` quote on its own.
