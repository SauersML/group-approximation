Let `Gamma` be a countable hyperlinear group with finite center
`Z = Z(Gamma)` and `Gamma/Z` ICC, let `Lambda <= Gamma` be a proper
subgroup with `Z subseteq Lambda`, and let

```text
H = < Gamma, t | [t, Lambda] = 1 >
```

be the HNN carrier.  Write `(RCC)` for: in every tracial matrix
ultraproduct `M` and every trace-preserving regular-trace representation
`pi : Gamma -> U(M)`, `pi(Lambda)' cap M = pi(Gamma)' cap M`.  For a
character `chi` of `Z` let `delta_chi` denote the trace of `Gamma` with
`delta_chi(z) = chi(z)` on `Z` and `0` elsewhere (so
`delta_e = (1/|Z|) sum_chi delta_chi`), and set

```text
E_chi = { tau in T(H) : tau hyperlinear, tau|_Gamma = delta_chi }.
```

Then:

1. **Ambient elimination.**  `(RCC)` holds if and only if every
   `tau in E_chi`, for every `chi`, satisfies

   ```text
   tau(t^(-1) gamma^(-1) t gamma) = 1  for all gamma in Gamma,   (CF1)
   ```

   equivalently `pi_tau(t)` is central in `pi_tau(H)''`.

2. **Face structure.**  Each `E_chi` is a weak*-compact face of the
   trace simplex `T(H)`; its extreme points are factorial characters
   of `H`.

3. **Factorial reduction.**  Consequently `(RCC)` holds if and only if
   every EXTREMAL (= factorial) hyperlinear character `tau` of `H` with
   `tau|_Gamma = delta_chi` satisfies `(CF1)`.

The instances consumed downstream are
`(Gamma, Lambda) = (SL_n(Z[1/p]), SL_n(Z))`: for `n = 2`,
`Z = {+-1}` and there are two faces; for odd `n` (in particular the
(T)-side pair `n = 3`, `p = 2` of
`sl3-pair-relative-commutant-route`), `Z` is trivial, `delta_chi =
delta_e`, and the statement has a single face.  In every instance
`Gamma` is finitely generated and residually finite, hence hyperlinear.

The point of the compression: after it, no matrix ultraproduct and no
representation variety appears anywhere -- the collapse is a statement
about the characters of one explicit countable group `H`, the GNS
algebra of each remaining enemy is a II_1 FACTOR generated by the
image of `Gamma` and one unitary commuting with the image of `Lambda`,
and Popa deformation/rigidity, Peterson-style character rigidity and
charmenability apply to it verbatim rather than by analogy.
