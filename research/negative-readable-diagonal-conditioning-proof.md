Represent the stabilizer-character GNS algebra inside the orbit-relation
algebra of the pmp action and write `M_f` for multiplication by `f`. For
a readable involution `r`, put `A_r={x:rx=x}`. Z alignment, commutation
with `J`, and freeness of `J` give

```text
pi(r)=M_(1_(A_r))+M_(1_(A_r^c))pi(J),
q_-(1+pi(r))/2=q_-M_(1_(A_r)).                          (RID1)
```

Every `A_r` is `J`-invariant. Conjugation by any unreadable word sends
`q_-M_(1_A)` to `q_-M_(1_(gA))`. Hence

```text
D_infinity subset A_-={q_-M_f:f in L^infinity(X)^J}.    (RID2)
```

The map `Theta(f)=q_-M_f` is a unital star homomorphism from
`L^infinity(X)^J` to the corner with unit `q_-`. It is injective. Indeed
the canonical relation trace and essential freeness of `J` give

```text
||Theta(f)||_(2,tau)^2
 =tau(q_-M_(|f|^2))
 =(1/2) integral_X |f|^2 dmu.                           (RID3)
```

Therefore every projection in `A_-` has a unique form `q_-M_(1_U)`
modulo null sets for a `J`-invariant measurable event `U`. Applying this
to `z` gives `(RIP2)`, and `(RID3)` gives
`mu(U)=2tau(z)>0`.

For `g in Q_G`, covariance and `(RIP1)` imply

```text
Theta(1_(gU))=pi(g)zpi(g)^*=z=Theta(1_U).
```

Injectivity makes `gU=U` modulo null sets. Thus `U` is invariant under
all unreadable generators and under `J`. If `r` is readable, `(RID1)`
says `rx` is either `x` or `Jx` almost everywhere. Since `U` is
`J`-invariant,

```text
1_U(rx)=1_U(x)                                          (RID4)
```

almost everywhere. Thus `U` is invariant under every readable generator
as well. Because the acting group is countable, changing `U` on a null set
yields a representative invariant under the full action.

Let `mu_U=mu(U)^(-1)mu|_U` and restrict the pmp action to `U`. Its
stabilizer pushforward is an IRS; no measurability of `U` with respect to
the stabilizer map is required. The involution `J` remains free. In the
relation algebra of the restricted action its negative projection is

```text
q_-^U=1_Uq_-=z.                                         (RID5)
```

Moreover

```text
N=W*(q_-pi(g):g in Gamma),                              (RID6)
```

because the unreadable generators lie in `Q_G` and `(RID1)` gives
`q_-pi(r)=2E_r-q_-` for every readable generator. Since `z` is
invariant, the negative algebra generated by the restricted action is

```text
W*(zpi(g):g in Gamma)=zN.                               (RID7)
```

For `x in zN`, its normalized compressed trace is

```text
tau_z(x)=tau(x)/tau(z)=2tau(x)/mu(U),                   (RID8)
```

which is exactly the normalized negative-corner trace of the restricted
relation algebra. Hence `(RID7)` is, by uniqueness of the tracial GNS
construction, the negative-corner GNS algebra of the conditioned stabilizer
IRS.

The readable positive spectral projections in the restricted negative
corner are `zE_r`, so

```text
D_0^U=zD_0,
D_infinity^U
 =W*(zpi(g)D_0pi(g)^*z:g in Q_G)
 =zD_infinity.                                          (RID9)
```

Thus finite dimensionality of `zD_infinity` transfers exactly. Every
losing projection and every ZPC relation was already zero in the faithful
original GNS algebra, so its compression by `z` is zero; the conditioned
strategy remains perfect.

For the itinerary projection,
`profile-itinerary-meet-extracts-finite-readable-summand` gives
`z in D_infinity`, `z!=0`, `Q_G`-invariance, and finite-dimensional
`zD_infinity`. Applying the preceding construction gives the claimed
finite-readable ZPC-IRS witness. If the original pmp action is ergodic,
`(RID4)` and positive measure force `U` conull, so already `z=q_-`
and the uncompressed `D_infinity` is finite dimensional.
