ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).  `SL_3(Z)` is purely matricial field
(`sl3z-purely-matricial-field`) iff `P = Z^3 x| SL_3(Z)` is.  The same holds
with "PFF", and with "`C*_r` is MF".  `P` has property (T), and it is the
index-2 subgroup of the integral maximal parabolic `P_(3,1)(Z)` of `SL_4(Z)`.
That parabolic contains every group used in Steps 2 and 3 of the
Magee--de la Salle proof that `SL_4(Z)` is not PMF
(`mdls-obstruction-is-affine-mechanism-plus-level-matching`).

So the `d = 3` question can be asked inside the part of `SL_4(Z)` that carries
the `d = 4` obstruction.  What `P` lacks is the level matching supplied by the
rest of `SL_4(Z)`.
