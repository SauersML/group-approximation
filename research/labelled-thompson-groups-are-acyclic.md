title: For every discrete group G the labelled Thompson group V(G) = C(Cantor, G) ⋊ V is integrally acyclic
---

For every discrete group `G`, the labelled Thompson group `V(G)` of Palmer–Wu
is acyclic: `H_k(V(G); Z) = 0` for `k ≥ 1`. Its elements are G-tables whose
expansion duplicates labels, so `V(G) ≅ C(C, G) ⋊ V`, with `C(C, G)` the locally
constant maps from the Cantor set to `G`.

Used by `braided-thompson-group-bv-is-rationally-acyclic` (Step B), with
`G = Q^j`.
