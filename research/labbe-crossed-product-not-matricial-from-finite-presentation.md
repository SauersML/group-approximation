`Ω_U` is an SFT with no periodic point. By `matricial-aperiodic-sft-rings-are-not-quantum-rigid`, if
`LC(Ω_U, F_q) ⋊ Z^2` were exactly matricial it would not be finitely presented. So finite presentation of the crossed
product (`labbe-wang-shift-crossed-product-is-finitely-presented`) forbids every exact matricial model.
