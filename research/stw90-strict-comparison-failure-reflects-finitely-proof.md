The finitely generated subgroups of `G`, ordered by inclusion, form an upward
directed family: two finite generating sets are contained in the subgroup
generated by their union.  Their union is `G`.  If every finitely generated
subgroup `H` had canonical strict comparison, then
`stw90-directed-unions-of-strict-comparison-groups` would give canonical
strict comparison for `G`.  Taking the contrapositive proves the claim.

No simplicity passes to the reflecting subgroup, so no stronger reduction is
asserted.
