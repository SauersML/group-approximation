**Statement.** Let `K` be a field of characteristic `p > 0` and let
`Γ <= GL_N(K)` be finitely generated. Then there are integers `k >= 0`, `M >= 1`
and an injective homomorphism

```text
Γ  ->  GL_M(F_p[s_1, ..., s_k]).
```

**The numbers.**
- `k` is the transcendence degree over `F_p` of the field `K_0` generated by the
  entries of the generators and their inverses.
- `M = N d r`, where `d = [K_0 : F_p(t_1..t_k)]` for a transcendence basis `t`, and
  `r` is the rank of the finite free module of the proof.

**Example.** `Z/p wr Z` embeds in `GL_2(F_p[t^±1])`, and `F_p[t^±1] = F_p[s]·1 ⊕ F_p[s]·t`
with `s = t + t^-1`, exactly as in the characteristic-zero example. So it embeds in
`GL_4(F_p[s])`.

**Consequence.** With `positive-char-polynomial-affine-groups-are-self-similar`,
this gives `char-p-linear-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`.
