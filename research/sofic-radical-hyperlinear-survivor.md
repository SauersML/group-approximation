distinct_from:
  literal-clifford-ce-model: The literal mark has a hyperlinear detector but is also visible in a sofic group, so it is not in the sofic radical required here.

---

Find an explicit countable group `H`, a nonidentity element
`x in Rad_sof(H)`, and one homomorphism from `H` to a hyperlinear group which
does not kill `x`.

The Kun--Thom compression constructions provide the sofic-radical side.  The
open task is to make the same defect survive a tracial matrix model.  The
literal Clifford sign cannot be used unchanged: literal-clifford-ce-model
also supplies a sofic detector of that sign.
