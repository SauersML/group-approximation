**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Thompson's group `F` contains no non-abelian free
subgroup, and `F` is not elementary amenable.

Sources:
- M. G. Brin and C. C. Squier, *Groups of piecewise linear homeomorphisms of the
  real line*, Invent. Math. 79 (1985) 485–498: groups of piecewise-linear
  homeomorphisms of the line, `F` among them, contain no non-abelian free
  subgroups.
- J. W. Cannon, W. J. Floyd and W. R. Parry, *Introductory notes on Richard
  Thompson's groups*, Enseign. Math. (2) 42 (1996) 215–256, §4: both facts, with
  proofs.

**Consequence.** `F` falls under neither classical criterion: von Neumann's
(free subgroup ⟹ non-amenable) and Chou's closure of elementary amenable groups
(⟹ amenable). The amenability question for `F` is the named problem behind the
roots `thompson-f-is-amenable` and `thompson-f-is-not-amenable`.
