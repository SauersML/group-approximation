Unit preservation and the integer bounds on soft real classes give
f(t)-hat=t*u-hat. A Cu-morphism sends these soft classes to soft classes:
given v<<f(t), choose r<t with v<=f(r), then k with (k+1)r<=kt.
Thus (k+1)v<=k*f(t). The supersoft majorant theorem gives f(t)<=s_t.

If f(t_0)=s_(t_0), additivity and the absorption identity imply
f(t_0+r)=s_(t_0+r) for every r>0. Preservation of compact containment
then gives s_(t_0+1)<<s_(t_0+1+delta) for every delta>0.
The gap formula gives rc(A)<=delta for every delta, hence rc(A)=0.
