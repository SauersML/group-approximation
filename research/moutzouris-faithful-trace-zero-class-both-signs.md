**Established (literature).**  Moutzouris, arXiv:2112.03224v3.

- (Proposition 4.3)  Let `A` be separable, unital, nuclear and UCT; let
  `tau in T(A)` be faithful; and let `x in K_0(A)` be non-torsion with
  `tau^(x)=0`.  Then there are faithful unital \*-homomorphisms
  `phi_i: A -> B_i` into unital AF algebras with `(phi_1)_*(x)>0`,
  `(phi_2)_*(x)<0` and `(phi_3)_*(x)=0`.
- (Theorem 4.1, from Schafhauser, Cor. 5.4)  The lifting tool.  Let `A` be
  unital, separable, nuclear and UCT, and `B` a `Q`-stable AF algebra with
  unique trace.  A positive unital homomorphism `sigma: K_0(A) -> K_0(B)` with
  `tau_A^ = tau_B^ o sigma`, for a faithful `tau_A in T(A)`, is induced by a
  unital faithful \*-homomorphism.
