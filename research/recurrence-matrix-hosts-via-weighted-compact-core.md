Choose the common forward recurrence lattice for the finitely many
endpoint coefficients and exponents. The weighted matrix theorem gives
`L=E×𝒰` of type `F_∞` with injective shift and the entire constant
matrix isotropy. The arithmetic data theorem supplies its faithful
radial realization, finite-root bases and the faithful shell embedding
of the global profile lamp group, including finite exceptions. The
compact-core theorem gives `F_∞` actors with the required clopen
action. The classical BZ finiteness and finite-order generation result
produces the simple host. All these prerequisites remain open; no
unrestricted product closure or polynomial-tower axiom is used.
