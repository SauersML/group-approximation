## Step 1: `c` is a continuous cocycle, and so is `c^{(n)}`

The multiplication rule quoted above gives `c((x,m,y)(y,m',y')) = m + m' = c + c`. `c` is
continuous because it is constant, equal to `|μ| - |ν|`, on each basic set `U_{μ,ν}`, and
these form a basis. A product of cocycles on a product groupoid, composed with the
projections, is a cocycle; `c^{(n)}` is continuous because the sets
`U_{μ_1,ν_1} × … × U_{μ_n,ν_n}` form a basis of `G_2^n`. This is item 1.

## Step 2: the kernel is tail equivalence

`c(x, m, y) = 0` means `m = 0`, i.e. there are `k, l` with `k = l` and `σ^k x = σ^l y`,
that is `σ^k x = σ^k y` for some `k`: the two sequences agree from coordinate `k` on.
So `ker c = R_2 = ⋃_k R_k` with `R_k = {(x,0,y) : x_i = y_i for all i ≥ k}`. A product
kernel is the product of the kernels, so `ker c^{(n)} = R_2^n`. This is item 2.

## Step 3: `R_k` is elementary, and `R_2^n` is AF

Fix `k`. As a subset of `G_2`, `R_k = ⋃_{|μ| = |ν| = k} U_{μ,ν}`, a finite union of basic
compact open sets, so `R_k` is compact and open, and it contains the unit space. It is
principal: `(x,0,y) ∈ R_k` is determined by its source and range. Its orbits are the
`2^k` sequences agreeing with a given one after coordinate `k`, so they are finite of
constant size `2^k`. A compact open principal étale groupoid with finite orbits and
Cantor unit space is elementary in the usual sense (equivalently: `C*(R_k) ≅
C(C) ⊗ M_{2^k}` is finite dimensional over its diagonal).

`R_k ⊆ R_{k+1}` and `⋃_k R_k = R_2`, so `R_2` is AF. Products: `R_k^n` is again compact
open, principal, with finite orbits of size `2^{kn}`, hence elementary; `R_k^n ⊆
R_{k+1}^n`; and `⋃_k R_k^n = (⋃_k R_k)^n = R_2^n` because any single element of `R_2^n` has
`n` coordinates, each with its own agreement level, and the maximum of `n` levels is
finite. So `R_2^n` is AF. This is item 3.

(Consistency check on the C\*-side: `C*(R_2) = M_{2^∞}` is the UHF core of `O_2`, the
fixed point algebra of the gauge action generated by `c`, and `C*(R_2^n) = M_{2^∞}^{⊗n}`
is the fixed point algebra of the `T^n`-gauge action generated by `c^{(n)}`.)

## Step 4: the remaining properties, and `nV ≤ [[G_2^n]]`

`G_2` is ample, Hausdorff, second countable, minimal, effective and purely infinite
(Matui, quoted in `amenable-purely-infinite-groupoid-kazhdan-host-proof`: Lemma
`SFT>pim`, "For any non-empty clopen set $Y\subset X$, the étale groupoid $G|Y$ is purely
infinite and minimal"; essential principality likewise). Products preserve all of these,
as recorded in `product-two-graph-kp-elementary-simple-kazhdan-not-mf-proof` (minimality
and effectiveness) and in Step 5 of `amenable-purely-infinite-groupoid-kazhdan-host-proof`
(pure infiniteness of a product with `G_2`).

The inclusion `nV ≤ [[G_2^n]]` is the standing fact of
`brin-thompson-groups-nv-are-a-t-menable`: a brick map replacing the brick
`C_{ν_1} × … × C_{ν_n}` by `C_{μ_1} × … × C_{μ_n}` is `π_U` for the compact open
bisection `U = U_{μ_1,ν_1} × … × U_{μ_n,ν_n}`, and a brick map is a finite disjoint union
of these. This is item 4.
