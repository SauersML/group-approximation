Write the trivial word as a freely reduced product of `Area(w)` conjugates
of relators.  Each factor is at normalized-HS distance exactly
`||r(U)-I||_2<=Def_R(U)` from the identity, and the distance of a product
of unitaries from the identity is subadditive.  Section 1 of the artifact.
