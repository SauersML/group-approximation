The following are equivalent.

1. Every bounded quasitrace on every C*-algebra is a trace.
2. Every normalized `2`-quasitrace on every separable unital simple stably
   finite non-elementary C*-algebra is a trace.

Equivalently, a negative answer to STW Problem I always has a separable,
unital, simple, stably finite witness.  Such a witness is necessarily
nonexact by Haagerup's theorem.
