**ESTABLISHED CAPACITY FIREWALL.**  Let `C_1,...,C_r` be pairwise disjoint
affine character cylinders in a finite elementary-abelian address space
`F_2^D`, with codimensions `c_i`.  Normalized counting gives

```text
sum_i 2^(-c_i)<=1.                                    (IAC1)
```

If a selected payload projection `A` is coupled only to this independent
address algebra, a transport authenticated by `c_i` address bits can place
at most the fraction `2^(-c_i)` of the ambient address multiplicity in
`C_i`.  Hence the sum of the traces of all pairwise disjoint authenticated
destinations is at most the trace of the unsplit payload carrier.  Adding
`s` further independent Boolean bits creates `2^s` times as many cylinders
and divides every cylinder trace by `2^s`; it never creates a trace surplus.

The same statement holds for an arbitrary finite address algebra: if
`R_i` are pairwise orthogonal address projections, then

```text
sum_i tau(A tensor R_i)
 =tau(A) sum_i tau(R_i)<=tau(A).                       (IAC2)
```

Thus an independent lamplighter, finite Reynolds packet, or character
register can authenticate names and orthogonality, but cannot prove the
strict growth required by `(FAT1)` or `(SAE1)`.  A successful row must make
the placement itself depend on the forbidden shared-context payload (or
charge failure of that dependence to relator energy).  This is a capacity
no-go, not a moving-address problem.
