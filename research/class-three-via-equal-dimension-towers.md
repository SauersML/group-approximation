Dead. The hope was that stacking more Samelson commutators on
equal-dimension twisted towers would produce a nonzero triple commutator.

equal-dimension-ah-component-groups-class-two kills it. In that regime the
kernel of the K1 map is a quotient of a top-cell group and is central, and
K1 is abelian, so every triple commutator vanishes at every stage and in
the limit.
