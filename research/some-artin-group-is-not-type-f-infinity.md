title: Some finitely generated Artin group is not of type F_infinity
---

There is a finite Coxeter graph `Γ` whose Artin group `A_Γ` has no `K(A_Γ, 1)` with
finitely many cells in each dimension.

This is the negative answer to Zaremsky Problem 1.5
(`zaremsky-1-05-artin-groups-are-type-f-infinity`).

## Attempts

- No candidate is known. A counterexample violates the K(π,1) conjecture, since the
  conjecture gives a finite `K(π,1)`. By the Godelle–Paris reduction (arXiv:1007.1365)
  it then has a free-of-infinity parabolic subgroup that violates the conjecture. Taking
  the preprint arXiv:2509.06914 at face value, that parabolic subgroup has dimension at
  least 4, and it lies outside the affine, rank-three and FC classes.
- An obstruction would have to detect an infinitely generated homotopy group of the
  Salvetti complex (or of the Deligne complex) through a finiteness invariant such as
  `H_k(A_Γ; Z A_Γ)`. No mechanism of this kind is recorded.
