Gao--Junge--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell--Robert,
arXiv:2607.20361, the cp-map permanence theorem in Section 5 and its
scalar-state tensor corollary in Section 7.  Their theorem says that tensoring
a selfless cp map with an approximately selfadjoint inner cp map preserves
selflessness when the second algebra is exact, and removes exactness when the
first map is completely selfless.  For a simple uniquely tracial algebra,
Haagerup--Zsidó's Dixmier theorem makes the scalar trace map approximately
selfadjoint inner, exactly as checked in the paper's scalar corollary.

All of this is a literature import; no new tensor-permanence proof is claimed.
