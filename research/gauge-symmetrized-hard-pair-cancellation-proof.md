For every packet representation `S`, pullback by the automorphism `alpha`
does not change its commutant algebra.  Since

```text
S_(alpha(k))=alpha^*S_k,       V_-=alpha^*V_+,
```

we have

```text
C_-(alpha(k))
 =End_B(alpha^*S_k tensor alpha^*V_+)
 =End_B(alpha^*(S_k tensor V_+))
 isomorphic C_+(k).                                      (1)
```

On the center, `alpha` swaps `J,z_e` and fixes `z_f`, so its fixed subgroup
is generated by `Jz_e,z_f`.  In bit notation both `101` and `011` evaluate
negatively on `Jz_e` and on `z_f`; hence their restrictions to this subgroup
coincide.  This is the precise logical/private quotient used by the gauge
interpretation.

Put first `k=h` and then `k=h'`.  Because `alpha` is involutive, `(1)` gives
both identities in `(GHC3)`.  Taking `m` copies and reordering the two direct
summands proves `(GHC4)`.

An isomorphism between these concrete finite-dimensional `*`-subalgebras is
implemented after matching their Wedderburn blocks by a unitary on the
underlying direct-sum Hilbert space.  That unitary conjugates the associated
orthogonal Reynolds projections, so their overlap equals their common rank.

Finally `gauge-doubling-gives-selector-flip-symmetries` constructs the exact
gauge-orbit covariant representation as a direct sum over the private flip
orbit.  On a two-element orbit its two orientations have equal multiplicity,
which is exactly the hypothesis of `(GHC4)`.  Hence conditioning only on the
flip-invariant logical product cannot retain the oriented scalar gap.
