Choose a nonzero coordinate `y_j` and a prime not dividing

```text
y_j product_i det(A_i).
```

Then `V=F_p^n` contains the nonzero reduction of `y`, and every reduced
matrix `alpha_i` lies in `GL(V)`.  Let `L=<alpha_1,...,alpha_r>`, a finite
subgroup of `GL(V)`.  In the affine group with multiplication
`(v,a)(w,b)=(v+aw,ab)`, define

```text
v |-> (bar v,1),             t_i |-> (0,alpha_i).
```

Direct multiplication gives

```text
(0,alpha_i)(bar v,1)(0,alpha_i)^(-1)
  =(alpha_i bar v,1)=(bar(A_i v),1),
```

so every defining HNN relation holds.  The finite target `V rtimes L` is
generated by these images, and the marked base element maps to
`(bar y,1)!=1`.
