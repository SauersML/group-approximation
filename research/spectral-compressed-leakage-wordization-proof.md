Projection calculus and traciality give

```text
||(1-C)UG||_2^2
 =tau(GU^*(1-C)UG)
 =tau(G)-tau(GU^*CU).
```

Substitute the two finite Fourier formulas `(SCM1)` and distribute the finite
sums.  This gives `(SCM3)` exactly in every finite tracial algebra.
