Let `A=C*_r(F₂)⊗𝒵`, let `τ` be its unique trace, put
`D=A_ω∩A'`, and let `I=J_τ∩D`.  Then

```text
D/I ≅ (L(F₂) ̅⊗ R)^ω ∩ (L(F₂) ̅⊗ R)',
```

and this quotient is a II₁ factor.  In particular it has a unique normalized
quasitrace.
