**ESTABLISHED (imported).** Let `G` be a torsion-free word-hyperbolic group and
`a in G` nontrivial. Then there is a homogeneous quasimorphism `h` on `G` with
`h(a) = 1`.

**Consequence.** If moreover `a in [G, G]`, then `scl_G(a) > 0`: `h` is not a
homomorphism (homomorphisms to `R` vanish on `[G, G]`), so `D(h) > 0`, and Bavard
duality gives `scl(a) >= 1/(2 D(h))`.

*Source.* D. Calegari and K. Fujiwara, *Stable commutator length in word-hyperbolic
groups*, arXiv:math/0611889, TeX `wordstable0.28.tex` fetched on MSI. Quotations in
`calegari-fujiwara-gap-theorem-citation`.
