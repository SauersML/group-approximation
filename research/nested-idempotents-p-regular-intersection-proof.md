## Why sufficient

- **Coset components.** Write `e = sum_t h_t eps_t` over left cosets of `C` in `H_1`, with
  `eps_t in M_n(F_p[C])`. The product `ef = f` splits over the disjoint cosets `h_t H_2`, so
  `eps_1 f = f` and `eps_t f = 0` for `t != 1` (artifact Lemma 1).
- **An idempotent between them.** `M_n(F_p[C])` is semisimple because `p ∤ |C|`, so the left
  annihilator of `f` in it is generated by an idempotent `1 - π`. Then `π f = f` and `e π = π`.
- **Two comparisons.** `π e` is an idempotent below `e` equivalent to `π`, and `f π` is an
  idempotent below `π` equivalent to `f`. The idempotents `e`, `π`, `π e` lie in
  `M_n(F_p[H_1])`, and `π`, `f`, `f π` lie in `M_n(F_p[H_2])`. So the lifted trace formula
  LFT1 gives `t_p(e) >= t_p(π) >= t_p(f)`, with equality only if `e = π e` and `π = f π`.
- **Conclusion.** Equal traces force both equalities, so `e = π e = f π e = f e = f`.
