Orthogonality of the initial projections gives `(OCS2)` immediately.
Choose one block on which `W-I` has norm two per unit vector and direct-sum it
with an arbitrarily large identity spectator.  Its conditional syndrome
stays maximal, while its normalized trace and global squared-HS contribution
tend to zero.  Thus only a separate same-reservoir return can upgrade the
mass-weighted sum to a root mark collapse.
