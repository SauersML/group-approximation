title: One binary transvection bounds the active dimension of every finite special linear representation by four times its moving rank

For d>=3, K=SL_d(F_2), a=e_12(1), and every finite-dimensional
complex unitary representation sigma of K,

    dim((Fix sigma(K))^perp)
      <= (4-2^(3-d)) rank(sigma(a)-I)
      <= 4 rank(sigma(a)-I).

The estimate includes arbitrary trivial and reducible summands.
Its constant is uniform over the standard exhaustion of SL_fin(F_2).
