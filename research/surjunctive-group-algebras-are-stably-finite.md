title: Surjunctive groups have stably finite group algebras over every field
distinct_from:
  stable-finiteness-failure-refutes-surjunctivity: "That gives the finite-field contrapositive by duality; this includes every field and imports Phung's all-field theorem."
---

For every surjunctive group H, every field k, and every n>=1, matrices
A,B in M_n(k[H]) satisfying AB=I also satisfy BA=I.

Surjunctive has its ordinary finite-alphabet meaning: every injective
cellular automaton on a full finite-alphabet H-shift is surjective.
Neither algebraic surjunctivity nor hyperlinearity is assumed.

This is Xuan Kien Phung's Theorem B in
[*A geometric generalization of Kaplansky's direct finiteness conjecture*](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.07930).
It is a literature input, not a new consequence of Jihao Liu's work.
