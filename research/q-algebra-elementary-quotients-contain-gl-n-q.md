## Statement

Let `R` be a nonzero unital ring that receives a unital ring homomorphism
`Q -> R` (equivalently, every nonzero integer is invertible in `R`). For
`N >= 2` write `E_N(R)` for the subgroup of `GL_N(R)` generated by the
elementary matrices `e_ij(r)`, `i != j`, and `PE_N(R) = E_N(R)/Z(E_N(R))`.

1. **Rational scalars are central.** `Q` lies in the centre `Z(R)`.
2. **The centre is scalar.** The centralizer of `E_N(R)` in `GL_N(R)` is
   `{dI : d ∈ Z(R)^x}`. So `Z(E_N(R)) = E_N(R) ∩ Z(R)^x I`.
3. **PSL_N(Q) embeds.** The entrywise inclusion `SL_N(Q) = E_N(Q) -> E_N(R)`
   induces an injective homomorphism `PSL_N(Q) -> PE_N(R)`.
4. **GL_n(Q) embeds.** For `N >= n+2`, `g ↦ [diag(g, det(g)^-1, I_(N-n-1))]`
   is an injective homomorphism `GL_n(Q) -> PE_N(R)`.
5. **Matrix rings.** For `N >= 2` and `k >= 1`, `E_N(M_k(R)) = E_(Nk)(R)` as
   subgroups of `GL_N(M_k(R)) = GL_(Nk)(R)`. Hence if `R ≅ M_2(R)` as unital
   rings, then `PE_N(R) ≅ PE_(2^j N)(R)` for all `j >= 0`, and `GL_n(Q)` embeds
   in `PE_N(R)` for **every** `n >= 1` and every `N >= 2`.

## Use

This is the embedding half of the matrix-ring route
`gl-n-q-via-projective-elementary-hosts`. The whole remaining burden is the
host itself: a ring `R ⊇ Q` with `PE_N(R)` finitely presented and simple for
infinitely many `N` (`q-algebra-with-fp-simple-projective-elementary-group`).
By part 5, a ring with `R ≅ M_2(R)` needs this for one `N` only.

`K_2(Q)` and the Tate symbols play no role in the embedding (compare
`research/artifacts/gq-gq-k2-q-uniformity.md`, N4). They only enter through the
finite presentation of the host.
