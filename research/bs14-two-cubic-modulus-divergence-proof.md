Use the compressed even-Weil tuple.  Polar rounding preserves the exact
involution and exact inversion of the exact BS implementer, so only the two
cubic words have normalized-HS defect `O(p^(-1/2))`.  Property `(T;FD)` gives
a level-independent positive distance from every exact representation in
dimension `(p-1)/2`, even when the comparison core moves.  Dividing gives a
`sqrt(p)` lower bound for any linear strict repair constant and the analogous
`p^(theta/2)` bound for a Holder exponent `theta`.
