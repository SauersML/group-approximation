Let `mu` be an IRS of a countable group `Gamma`.  Put

```text
V=directSum_Gamma C_2.
```

For `H<=Gamma`, define the equivariant coset-folding map

```text
T_H:V -> directSum_(Gamma/H) C_2,
T_H(e_x)=e_(xH).                                         (RCQ1)
```

Let

```text
N_mu={c in V:T_H(c)=0 for mu-almost every H},
B_mu=V/N_mu,
G_mu=B_mu semidirect Gamma.                              (RCQ2)
```

This is a concrete countable abelian-by-`Gamma` group.  In the measurable
random-coset wreath bundle of
`random-coset-lamp-correlations-realize-every-irs-character`, the lamp fields
and the constant actor fields give a faithful group representation

```text
rho_mu:G_mu -> U(M_mu).                                  (RCQ3)
```

Its trace is

```text
tau_mu(rho_mu([c],g))
 =0                                      if g!=e,
 =mu({H:T_H(c)=0})                       if g=e.          (RCQ4)
```

The following are equivalent.

1. The trace in `(RCQ4)` is the canonical regular trace of `G_mu`.
2. Every finite parity-collapse event `{H:T_H(c)=0}` has probability zero or
   one.
3. The IRS membership character

   ```text
   chi_mu(g)=mu({H:g in H})
   ```

   is zero-one valued.
4. `mu=delta_K` for a normal subgroup `K normal Gamma`.

The key two-lamp identity is

```text
{H:T_H(e_e+e_g)=0}={H:g in H}.                           (RCQ5)
```

Thus any genuinely random IRS coefficient `0<chi_mu(g)<1` appears as the
nonzero trace of a nonidentity element of `B_mu`.  Quotienting by all
almost-sure lamp relations does not remove the noncanonical trace.

This gives a terminal audit of the most direct infinite Fourier-mixing
semidirect product.  If the Manzoor IRS is nonatomic, `(RCQ4)` is not the
canonical trace and the representation does not identify `M_mu` with
`L(G_mu)` or place the IRS GNS algebra in that group factor.  Moreover its
native actor is free, so `G_mu` is sofic by
`abelian-by-virtually-free-groups-are-sofic`; its canonical group factor is
Connes embeddable and cannot contain the non-CE IRS algebra by any other
placement.

If `(RCQ4)` is canonical, the IRS is already `delta_K`.  Replacing the actor
by `Gamma/K` gives the ordinary coset-lamp group

```text
(directSum_(Gamma/K) C_2) semidirect (Gamma/K),          (RCQ6)
```

whose group factor contains the canonical subgroup factor `L(Gamma/K)`, the
IRS GNS algebra.  For a non-cohyperlinear IRS this makes `Gamma/K`
non-hyperlinear.  Hence the construction closes exactly in the previously
terminal normal-Dirac case and supplies no intermediate algebraic envelope.

The obstruction concerns this canonical lamp-field quotient, not arbitrary
non-Cartan Fourier mixtures in a different non-Haagerup actor crossed
product.  Those remain the live algebraic-envelope route.
