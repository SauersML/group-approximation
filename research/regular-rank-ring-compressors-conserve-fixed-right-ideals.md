Let `U` be a unital von Neumann regular ring with a faithful Sylvester matrix rank function `rk`,
`Gamma = <S>` finitely generated, `G` generated by elements of `P_Gamma = { g : g Gamma g^-1 <= Gamma }`,
and `sigma : G -> U^x` a homomorphism.

1. `Fix_r(sigma(Gamma)) = eU` for an idempotent `e`, and
   `rk((1-e)x) <= sum_(s in S) rk((sigma(s)-1)x)` for every `x` in `U`.
2. `sigma(G)` preserves `eU`, and `<<Gamma>>_G` fixes it pointwise, so
   `Fix_r(sigma(Gamma)) = Fix_r(sigma(<<Gamma>>_G))`.

**Consequence.** `leavitt-rank-models-kazhdan-fixed-ideals-are-global` holds in degree `(1,0)` for
homomorphisms of `L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` into every faithful regular rank ring (artifact Corollary 2.4). The
class is closed under corners and metric ultraproducts (artifact Lemma 2.1), so this is the setting of
the defect chain for abstract targets.

**Scope.** Degrees `(a,b) != (1,0)` are not covered: they need a tensor product with multiplicative
rank.
