## Setting

- `R` is a ring with `t s = 1`, and `q = 1 - s t`. The main case is
  `R = J = F<S,T | TS = 1>`, where `x_13(Q)` is the head.
- `P = P_{2,2}(R) <= EL_4(R)` is generated by the Levi roots
  `x_12, x_21, x_34, x_43` and the unipotent roots `x_13, x_14, x_23, x_24`.
- `B_0 ⊂ P` is the finite set of the elements `x_ij(a)` with `ij` among these
  roots and `a in {0, ±1, ±s, ±t, ±st, ±q}`, together with all their products
  of length at most 4.
- A **root-preserving partial model** on vector spaces `V_1, ..., V_4` over
  any field is a map `pi : B_0 -> GL(V_1 ⊕ V_2 ⊕ V_3 ⊕ V_4)` that is
  multiplicative whenever `x, y, xy in B_0`, and satisfies
  `pi(x_ij(a)) = 1 + phi_ij(a) e_ij` with `phi_ij(a) : V_j -> V_i`.
  No dimension bound, topology or filtration is assumed. Exact
  representations of `P` of this form are included.

Put

```text
m = phi_13(1) : V_3 -> V_1,    L(a) = phi_12(a) phi_21(1) : V_1 -> V_1,    Y = range(m) ⊂ V_1.
```

## Theorem

1. **Cokernel form of the head.** `L(s) Y ⊂ Y`, `L(t) Y ⊂ Y`, and
   `L(t) L(s) = id` on `Y`. Moreover

   ```text
   pi(x_13(q)) = 1   <=>   L(s) Y = Y,
   ```

   so the head survives in `pi` exactly when `L(s)|_Y` is a non-surjective
   injection.
2. **Index bound.** Suppose `L(s)` is Fredholm on `V_1` in the algebraic
   sense (finite-dimensional kernel and cokernel), and `Y` has finite
   codimension. Then `ind L(s) <= 0`. If moreover the head survives, then
   `ind L(s) <= -1`.
3. **Finite dimensions.** If `Y` is finite-dimensional, the head dies. This
   recovers the known pigeonhole kill of finite-dimensional root-preserving
   models, from the commutator relations of the four root triples
   `(1,2,3)`, `(2,1,3)`, `(1,3,4)`, `(1,4,3)` and `ts = 1`.
4. **Sink ends do not exist.** Let `V_1 = V_3 = K^((k <= M))`, a
   left-infinite half-line. Suppose `m - 1` has finite rank and `L(s)` agrees with the
   shift `e_k -> e_(k+1)` on all but finitely many basis vectors. This is the
   shape that the Laurent symbol of the identity forces at a mirror end.
   Then no root-preserving partial model with this `V_1` exists, whether or
   not it keeps the head.

## Consequence for seam gluing

- The EL_2 construction `binary-jacobson-el2-is-lef` glues a source end
  (`V_+`, where `S` has index `-1`) to a mirror end (`V_-^M`, where the lift
  `theta(S) = S'` has index `+1`), and both ends are root-preserving.
- For `P_{2,2}(J)` the far end must again be root-preserving with symbol
  `sigma' o theta = sigma`. At that end `m = theta(1)_- = 1 + finite rank` and
  `L(S) = theta(x_12(S)) theta(x_21(1))` restricts to the shift of symbol `z`.
- Part 4 then says no such far end exists for any target ring `J'` and any
  lift `theta`. This holds even though the head is only needed at the near
  end.
- **Obstruction invariant:** the Fredholm index of the S-root `L(S)` on
  `range(phi_13(1))`. The near end needs index `<= -1`, and a mirror end
  would supply `+1`.
- **Death step:** the right-Levi relations `[x_13(1), x_34(1)] = x_14(1)` and
  `[x_14(1), x_43(a)] = x_13(a)` give `L(a) m = m R(a)`, which forces `Y` to
  be `L(S)`-stable. The one-sided parabolic lacks exactly these relations.
