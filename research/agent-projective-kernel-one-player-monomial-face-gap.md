**OPEN.**  For the chosen extreme non-CE monomial/game trace, let `G_A,G_B`
be its finitely presented one-player question groups and let

```text
K_A={a:L(u_a) is scalar},       L(u_a)=alpha(a)I,
K_B={b:R(v_b) is scalar},       R(v_b)=beta(b)I.        (APO1)
```

Prove that at least one of

```text
F_(K_A,alpha)^tr intersect T_CE(G_A),
F_(K_B,beta)^tr intersect T_CE(G_B)                    (APO2)
```

is empty.

This is sufficient for a finitely presented nonhyperlinear group by
`agent-projective-kernel-arbitrary-phase-face-compiler`.  Conversely,
`agent-projective-kernel-factor-face-is-playerwise` shows that every
two-player projective-kernel CE gap of the factorial representation implies
`(APO2)`.  Thus the two-player linking syntax does not weaken this missing
theorem.

Non-Connes-embeddability of the selected trace alone does not prove `(APO2)`.
The missing source-specific input must show that every CE trace sharing all
scalar word relations of one player also satisfies enough of the monomial
losing ideal to contradict the original gap.  Equivalently, it must prevent
the unrelated CE quotient traces exhibited by the general raw-kernel
counterexamples.

## Attempts

- Taking the raw GNS projective kernel and invoking non-CE fails by
  `non-ce-trace-does-not-promote-to-subgroup-character-gap`: mixing any
  non-CE trace with a small regular component can make the projective kernel
  trivial while preserving non-CE.
- Hahn--Banach separates the chosen trace from CE traces by a general
  selfadjoint group-algebra element, not by scalar word equations.  No
  polarization step presently places that separator in the binomial ideal
  generated by `(APO1)`.
- On one commuting Boolean context, all scalar word equations recover only
  the affine hull of the trace support.  Thus contextwise extraction loses
  precisely the nonlinear forbidden projections; any positive proof must
  use source-specific cross-context monomial relations rather than repeat
  the local projective-kernel calculation.
- `agent-pk-affine-ghost-survives-quotient` strengthens
  that observation at the canonical trace.  Every forbidden assignment in
  the affine hull survives with equal positive mass in the twisted regular
  trace of the **full** projective quotient.  Hence the known perfect-BCS
  non-CE gap cannot be applied to that canonical trace.  Showing that it is
  non-CE would already be a new global group-factor theorem.
- The factorial left-right split does not add such relations.  It proves the
  reduction to `(APO2)` but supplies no reason for either player face to be
  CE-empty.
