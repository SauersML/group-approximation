**ESTABLISHED.**  Put `Gamma=SL_3(Z)`.  For primes `p` let `rho_p` be the
augmentation representation of the action of `SL_3(F_p)` on the projective
plane `P^2(F_p)`, pulled back along reduction modulo `p`.  Its dimension is

```text
d_p=p^2+p -> infinity.                                  (NRT1)
```

Then all of the following hold simultaneously.

1. Every `rho_p` is an exact representation and is irreducible.
2. Its normalized character converges pointwise to the regular character of
   `Gamma`.
3. The coefficient C-star algebra generated by `rho_p(Gamma)` is the single
   simple block `M_(d_p)(C)`.  Hence its trace-weighted block-degree
   distribution is

   ```text
   mu_p=delta_(d_p),                                     (NRT2)
   ```

   so for every fixed `D`, `mu_p({m>D})=1` eventually.  The degree tail is
   maximally non-tight.
4. For one fixed Kazhdan pair `(S,kappa)` of `Gamma`, conjugation has the
   dimension-independent gap

   ```text
   max_(s in S)||rho_p(s)x rho_p(s)^*-x||_2
      >= kappa ||x||_2                                  (NRT3)
   ```

   for every trace-zero `x in M_(d_p)`.
5. `Gamma` is a higher-rank property-`(T)` character-rigid lattice.

Thus exact relator flatness, convergence to the canonical regular trace,
property `(T)`, character rigidity, and even a uniform adjoint spectral gap
do **not**, separately or together, imply tightness of the coefficient-block
degrees.  The same examples are already exact, so hyperfinite HS stability
merely corrects them to themselves.

For the compact-hyperbolic Kac--Moody candidate, the additional fact that
every exact finite-dimensional representation is trivial removes these exact
examples but supplies no quantitative estimate on growing approximate
blocks.  Excluding the remaining one-block high-degree tuples while retaining
regular trace separation is exactly regular-branch normalized-HS stability,
and hence is already the terminal non-hyperlinearity problem rather than a
consequence of the four inputs above.  The proof is
`projective-plane-regular-tail-no-go-proof`.
