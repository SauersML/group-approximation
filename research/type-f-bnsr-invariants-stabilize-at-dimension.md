Let `G` be a group with a finite classifying space of dimension `n`. Then
`Σ^n(G) = Σ^∞(G)`, so `Σ^m(G) = Σ^n(G)` for every `m ≥ n`. In particular
`Σ^n(G) = Σ^{n+1}(G)`, so no group of type F answers Zaremsky Problem 1.18 in
the affirmative.
