For every competitor `c` in `(GOI4)`, apply the already established induced
isometry and projection-rounding argument to the hybrid tuple `U_c`.  Its
intertwining energy is the last summand of `(GOI4)`, while the first two
summands pay the prior movement of the core.  The triangle inequality then
gives `(GOI6)`, so the uniform bound `(GOI5)` implies energy-paid flexible
repair.  On the cyclic counterfamily the untwisted core makes the hybrid
tuple exact and gives `(GOI8)--(GOI9)`.  The abelianization calculation
shows that this accounts for every asymptotically small scalar congruence
character, while leaving matrix-valued core deformations in the infimum.
