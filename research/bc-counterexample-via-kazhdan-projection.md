**Dead.** The proposed witness is the K_0-class of the Kazhdan projection of
an infinite Kazhdan subgroup of `G`, taken to lie outside the assembly image.
Here `G` is a residually finite Kun--Thom Theorem E group, or `L^x` with an
elementary Kazhdan subgroup.

The projection lives in `C*_max G` and maps to `0` in `C*_r G`
(`subgroup-average-projections-give-no-reduced-bc-obstruction`, part 1). So
it bears only on the maximal assembly map, which the conjecture does not
assert.

Literature context, not imported: box spaces of residually finite Kazhdan
groups break the coarse assembly map (Higson; Higson--Lafforgue--Skandalis).
That is a statement about the coarse metric space, not about the group.
