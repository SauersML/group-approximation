The GNS closure of `A` at its unique trace is
`M=L(F₂)̅⊗R`.  Kirchberg--Rørdam, arXiv:1209.5311, Theorem 3.3,
prove without nuclearity that the canonical map
`A_ω∩A' → M^ω∩M'` is onto; its kernel is exactly `J_τ∩D`.
Dixmier--Lance, Proposition 19, prove that `L(F₂)̅⊗R` is super McDuff
(also Fang--Ge--Li, Theorem 4.7).  By definition its central sequence algebra
is a II₁ factor.  The Murray--von Neumann uniqueness theorem for the
quasitrace of a finite factor gives the last assertion.
