The prerequisite gives a simple unital C*-algebra A and unitaries
u_1, u_2, u_3, v with [u_3, [u_2, [u_1, v]]] not in U_0(A). That is the root
statement verbatim.

The blind spot of spin trapping is bypassed in two ways. The detector needs a
tangential structure only on the difference TX − W_R, and only with lambda
trivialized mod 8. That structure's bordism sees eta cubed. Quaternionic
Grassmannians Gr_8(H^{8+2d}) supply the steps, since their defect is 8·q_1.
