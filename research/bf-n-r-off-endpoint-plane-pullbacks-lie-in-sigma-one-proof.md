Artifact part 3, Lemma 8 and Theorem 9.

- `[psi]` lies in `Sigma^2(F_{n,1}) ⊆ Sigma^1` by Zaremsky's Theorem A (some
  `c_i != 0`). It transports to `F_{n,r}` by the root isomorphism recorded in
  `bf-n-r-sigma-m-holes-of-f-n-r-pull-back`.
- The kernel is generated by the windings. By the orbit classification of leaf
  pairs, each winding is an `F_{n,r}`-conjugate of one representative per type.
  Two-leaf windings are products of the others after cabling.
- A representative commutes with every element supported in one of its positive
  gaps. The element that moves a split from child 1 to child 2 of a standard
  interval of residue `q` takes the value `e_q - 2e_{q+1} + e_{q+2}` under `psi`.
  These values are not all zero off the endpoint plane, so the conjugator can be
  corrected to have `psi >= 0`.
- The extension lemma: follow a `psi >= 0` path in `F_{n,r}`, then each
  conjugate `h e h^{-1}` along a word with nonnegative prefixes. `chi` does not
  change on `N`.
