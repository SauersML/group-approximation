The lattice `Γ < Sp(2,1)` has property (T) (partner citation), and property (T)
passes to quotients.  So an infinite hyperbolic quotient of `Γ` without
nontrivial finite quotients is an infinite hyperbolic Kazhdan group without
nontrivial finite quotients.
