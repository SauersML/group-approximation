**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `F` be a free group, `r in F` a
cyclically reduced word and `G = F/<<r>>`. Every proper nonempty subword of
`r` is nontrivial in `G`. The word `r` may be a proper power.

Applied to a cyclic permutation of `r`, which defines the same group, the
statement also covers every proper nonempty cyclic subword. Equivalently, the
closed path in the Cayley graph of `G` that reads `r` visits `|r|` distinct
vertices.
