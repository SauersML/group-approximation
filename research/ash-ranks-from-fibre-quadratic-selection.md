Take an injective unital subhomogeneous presentation of the given ASH
algebra, as explained in Section 2 of the artifact. The second prerequisite
provides the finite-stage moment selections. The first identifies their
existence with rank density in the simple limit. Nuclearity identifies
quasitraces and traces, so this is precisely STW XXIV.

The unresolved work is the positive contraction selection in each later
subhomogeneous algebra, including its attaching equations. Selecting
matrices independently at each irreducible representation does not meet
that prerequisite.
