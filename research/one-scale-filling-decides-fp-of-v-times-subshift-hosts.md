## 1. One-scale criterion

**Lemma.** Let `Y_0 ⊆ Y_1 ⊆ 𝔛` be `F`-invariant, `F`-cocompact subcomplexes with `Y_0` connected.
If `π_1(Y_0) → π_1(Y_1)` is trivial, then `F` is finitely presented. Conversely, if `F` is
finitely presented, every such `Y_0` has such a `Y_1`.

*Proof.*
- Replace `Y_1` by its component containing `Y_0`. It is `F`-invariant.
- Let `Π_i = π_1(EF ×_F Y_i)`. Since `Y_i` is connected, `1 → π_1(Y_i) → Π_i → F → 1` is exact.
  `Π_i` is the fundamental group of a finite complex of finite groups, so it is finitely presented.
- The inclusion gives `Π_0 → Π_1` over `F`. It kills `π_1(Y_0)`, so it factors through
  `Π_0/π_1(Y_0) = F`. This gives a section `s : F → Π_1`, so `F` is a retract of the finitely
  presented group `Π_1`, hence finitely presented.
- Conversely, if `F` is finitely presented, `π_1(Y_0) = ker(Π_0 → F)` is normally generated by
  finitely many `F`-orbits of loops. Each of them dies in the contractible `𝔛`, inside some
  cocompact subcomplex, and `Y_1` can be taken to contain those finitely many. `∎`

**Corollary.** Such a `Y_0` exists inside one domain level `𝒫^(R_0)`, because `F` is finitely
generated: take the `F`-translates of a base vertex together with paths from it to its translates
by the generators. Fix this `Y_0`. Then:

> `F` is finitely presented iff the loops of that single level die at some larger level
> `𝒫^(R_1)`.

The descending links give no obstruction, by the horizontal node. So the essential form of
(A_N) needs checking at **one** scale only.

## 2. Finite shadow bases

A **finite shadow basis** for `Λ ↷ X` consists of the following data.
- Finitely many clopen tiles `Y_j`, whose translates form a basis of `X`.
- For each tile, finitely many splits into translates of tiles. These satisfy:
  - square filling: any two iterated splittings of a tile have a common refinement over each;
  - a finite spine of minimal common refinements.
- The only canonical maps between tile translates are elements of `Λ`. So the automorphism group
  of `Y_j` is `Λ_{Y_j} = {λ : λY_j = Y_j}`.

If every `Λ_{Y_j}` is `F_∞^+`, then `F` is a Thumann dissection-operad group, and hence `F_∞`. The
colours are the tiles, the transformations are the `Λ_{Y_j}`, and the splits are the given
splits together with the C-split. The proof of `cocompact-tree-end-shift-hosts-are-f-infinity`
applies word for word; only the tile family changes. With `F_∞` rather than `F_∞^+` stabilizers,
use the unreviewed weakening `thumann-operad-groups-need-only-f-infinity-transformations`.

Known instances:
- tree end shifts (half-trees);
- products of trees and Burger–Mozes-type lattices (products of half-trees; trivial or finite
  stabilizers), which Li's k-graph and Cuntz-stabilized Garside theorems also cover;
- `Ã₂` lattice boundaries (chamber shadows), conditional as in their node.

Every instance is a compression coding over a non-amenable group. Over an amenable group, an
invariant measure of full support excludes a finite shadow basis (P2′ Attempt 1).

## 3. Which inputs this reaches

- **Directly.** Every group with a cocompact, strongly faithful action on a locally finite
  leafless tree whose edge stabilizers are `F_∞^+`, together with all its subgroups. Equivalently:
  a finite graph of groups with finite-index edge inclusions, vertex groups commensurable with one
  `F_∞^+` group (in practice, virtually polycyclic), and a strongly faithful action.
- **After an upgrade.** A faithful HNN extension over finite-index subgroups becomes strongly
  faithful after the Baumslag–Solitar twist on `A × Z` (`faithful-finite-index-hnn-upgrades-to-half-tree-free`).
  `A × Z` stays `F_∞^+` when `A` is.
  - The same twist for graphs with several vertices is plausible, but not written out.
- **Not reached here: relative seeds.** Relative-seed gluings (`relative-seeds-glue-graphs-of-groups-into-class-c`)
  have locally infinite trees. They would fit Part 2 only under two further conditions:
  - each seed compactification has a finite shadow basis;
  - the `F_∞` weakening is used, since free edge groups are not `F_∞^+`.

  Neither is checked. This is the natural next widening. Its first test is HNN extensions of free
  groups over finitely generated subgroups of infinite index; their Boone–Higman status has not
  been checked here.

## 4. What it settles: no new Boone–Higman case (credit)

Every input reached in Part 3 already satisfies BH, and indeed PBH, by earlier work.
- **Bux–Llosa Isenrich–Wu**, arXiv:2408.05673. Their results are imported in
  `bliw-locally-finite-tree-actions-embed-in-fp-simple-groups` and
  `baumslag-solitar-and-free-by-cyclic-satisfy-boone-higman`:
  - Theorem A: all Baumslag–Solitar groups, free-by-cyclic groups, Leary–Minasyan groups and
    Euclidean triangle Artin groups;
  - Theorem B: faithful cocompact actions with finitely presented edge stabilizers;
  - Theorem 10.5: simple groups of type `F_n`;
  - Theorem C: every `K ∈ BS_G` once one member acts faithfully. With `BS(2,3)` faithful, this
    covers every generalized Baumslag–Solitar group.
- **Type (A).** `bs-class-with-faithful-member-lies-in-type-a-class` already puts these classes
  in the permutational class.
- **Topological full groups.** For the tree end shifts, `F_∞` of the V-stabilized full group is
  plausibly a case of X. Li's Zappa–Szép finiteness theorem (arXiv:2110.04505, `thm:ZS`, applied
  to Exel–Pardo self-similar graphs). The Katsura–Exel–Pardo groupoids of Baumslag–Solitar type
  are the unstabilized versions (Exel–Pardo; Nyland–Ortega).
- **What is new here.** A Thumann-operad proof. The multi-vertex case with `F_∞^+` edge
  stabilizers as the hypothesis. And the E3′ / (A_N) reading.
- **Priority correction.** These are not the first `F_∞` or finitely presented type (A) hosts of
  the Track A shape. Earlier ones:
  - the trivial-stabilizer tree scaffold `v-times-tree-boundary-full-groups-are-f-infinity`
    (2914b739e);
  - the non-ascending HNN end shifts `nonascending-hnn-end-shifts-satisfy-e3-prime`
    (1908f110d, lane bh-e3prime, through Li).

  `cocompact-tree-end-shift-hosts-are-f-infinity` extends the latter to graphs of groups.

## 5. The crossing-wire case: what self-similarity can and cannot do

Take the crossing-wire fixed-point shift `Ω` (`crossing-wire-fixed-point-tile-sets-are-quantum-rigid`)
with zoom `q`. Assume macrotile decompositions are unique, so `Ω = ⊔_{i ∈ [0,q)^2} (i + S(Ω))`.
The groupoid is then self-similar, `𝒢 ≅ 𝒢 × Pair(q²)`. This gives an isomorphism `Θ` of `F`
that shrinks domain radii from `R` to about `R/q + c`, with an inverse that enlarges them by at
most `qR + c'` (sketch).

- **Propagation (sketch).** A filling at one scale gives filling at every scale, with a filling
  radius linear in the loop's radius: contract with `Θ^k`, fill, then expand back.
  - This is quantitative information only.
  - By Part 1 the one-scale filling is already equivalent to finite presentation, without any
    self-similarity.
- **Self-similarity cannot create the filling (sketch).** Write `F = Π/K` at a scale `R_*` fixed by
  `Θ`, with `Π` finitely presented and `θ` the lift of `Θ`.
  - Every kernel element dies at a scale that `θ^k` shrinks to a fixed `R_b`. So
    `K = ⋃_k θ^{-k}(K_b)` with `K_b = ker(Π → Π_{R_b})`.
  - This is the contraction-saturated presentation of contracting self-similar groups. Such
    groups are often infinitely presented (for example Grigorchuk's group, which has an
    L-presentation).
  - Finite presentation holds iff `K_b`, enlarged by finitely many relators, is `θ`-saturated. That
    is a genuine one-scale statement about infinitely many loops.
- **So the open step is the nonlinear, one-scale form of quantum rigidity.** Show that every loop
  of the cocompact level `𝒫^(R_*)` dies at a fixed larger level. Quantum rigidity, proved for
  these tiles, is exactly its linear shadow. The ring side proves the linear version; a
  group-side proof must upgrade it from commuting operators to null-homotopies of cone-partition
  loops.

## Lesson for general BH

Finite presentation of a Cuntz-stabilized subshift host is a **one-scale** question: fix one
cocompact domain level, and ask whether its loops die at a larger one. That is a retract
argument.

- A finite shadow basis answers it for free. That is compression over a non-amenable group, with
  infinite isotropy allowed as transformations.
- Over amenable groups, no finite shadow basis exists.
- Self-similarity does not supply the answer. It only propagates a one-scale filling across
  scales, and presentation-wise it lands in the same place as contracting self-similar groups.

So the E3′/P2′ frontier is exactly the nonlinear upgrade of quantum rigidity at one scale. The
tree hosts are genuine and `F_∞`, but every input they reach already satisfies BH (Bux–Llosa
Isenrich–Wu). New BH cases must come from inputs outside locally finite tree actions:
relative-seed gluings, or amenable-scaffold (Z²-type) rigid hosts.
