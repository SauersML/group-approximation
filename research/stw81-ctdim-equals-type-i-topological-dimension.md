For every separable type-I C-star algebra `A`,

```text
ctdim(A) = topdim(A),
```

including the value `infinity`.  Thus `ctdim` extends Brown--Pedersen
topological dimension from type-I algebras to all separable C-star algebras
by testing their continuous-trace subquotients.
