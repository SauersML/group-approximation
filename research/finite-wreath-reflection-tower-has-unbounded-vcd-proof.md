Start with

```text
K_0=U wr F_2<=U,                 Q_0=F_2.
```

The internal free-reflection theorem gives the quotient `pi_0:K_0->Q_0`,
all target-factorization statements, and a radical normally generated by one
involution.  Inductively put

```text
K_(n+1)=K_n wr C_2<=U,
Q_(n+1)=Q_n wr C_2,
```

using the finite-wreath lift.  Residual finiteness is preserved by finite
direct products and finite extensions, so every `Q_n` is residually finite
and therefore MF.  Equations `(FWL5)--(FWL7)` give `(FWT2)` and `(FWT4)` at
every stage, and the transitive `C_2` actor preserves single normal
generation of the radical.  The same induction for the other target classes
gives the simultaneous residual statement and `(FWT5)`.

For the dimension calculation, `Q_n` contains a finite-index torsion-free
subgroup

```text
H_n~=F_2^(2^n).                                         (FWTP1)
```

This is clear at `n=0`.  If it holds at stage `n`, then `H_n^2` has finite
index in `Q_n^2`, hence in `Q_n^2 semidirectProduct C_2=Q_(n+1)`, and

```text
H_n^2~=F_2^(2^(n+1)).
```

The cohomological dimension of a direct product of `m` nonabelian free groups
is `m`.  Therefore `(FWTP1)` gives `vcd(Q_n)=2^n`, proving `(FWT3)`.

Universal MF reflection is invariant under isomorphism, so the distinct
virtual cohomological dimensions make the `K_n` pairwise nonisomorphic.  An
infinite virtually free group has virtual cohomological dimension one, and
Muller--Schupp characterize finitely generated context-free groups as
virtually free.  Hence `Q_n` is neither virtually free nor context-free for
`n>=1`.
