**Established by citation.**  Szabo, *Equivariant Kirchberg--Phillips-
type absorption for amenable group actions* (CMP; Theorem 3.4), as
recorded verbatim by STW arXiv:2506.10902: "the equivariant
`O_∞`-stability theorem of Szabo implies that all (cocycle) actions
of countable discrete amenable groups on Kirchberg algebras are
equivariantly `O_∞`-stable (and hence equivariantly `Z`-stable)";
Szabo's MJM Corollary 3.6 extends this to amenable actions of
non-amenable groups.
