Citation import, not a reproof. Source read on 2026-09-17: *Smith theory of group
actions*, Encyclopedia of Mathematics
(https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Smith_theory_of_group_actions).

- Setting, verbatim: "Let X be such a finitistic space and let P be a finite p-group
  acting on it". The same article defines finitistic spaces as those "such that
  every covering has a finite-dimensional refinement", and says "The most important
  examples are compact spaces and finite-dimensional spaces."
- Theorem a), verbatim: "If X has the mod p homology of a point, then the fixed-point
  set X^P also has the mod p homology of a point; in particular, it is non-empty."
- Attribution in the article: "first obtained by P.A. Smith around 1940". Primary
  sources it lists: P. A. Smith, *Transformations of finite period* (1938), *II*
  (1939), and *Fixed point theorems for periodic transformations* (1941). The primary
  sources were not read here.

The textbook form for finite-dimensional complexes is K. S. Brown, *Cohomology of
Groups*, Chapter VII (from memory, not re-read). The claim uses only the special case
of a simplicial action on a finite-dimensional contractible simplicial complex.
