## Why sufficient

`common-quotient-onto-normal-subgroup` already establishes the hard half: in
Hull's machinery the reservoir of small-cancellation target words may be
confined to any prescribed infinite normal subgroup of a factor, at no cost,
with torsion-freeness and finite presentability preserved and prescribed finite
sets protected.  Apply it with `G_1 = A`, `G_2 = B` and `D = <<u>>^B`, which is
infinite because `B` is torsion-free and `u != 1`, and which has trivial finite
radical for the same reason.  That yields clauses 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 directly.

The remaining clause is two-generation, and it is an observation inside the
proof already cited rather than a new small-cancellation estimate.

**Stage one.**  Choose `f_1, f_2 in A` and `h_1, h_2 in D` by Hull's Corollary
5.7, and promote the four cyclic subgroups to the hyperbolically embedded
family in `F = A * B` by Lemma 3.5.  Theorem 7.1 applied to `F` with suitable
subgroup `S_D = <h_1, h_2>`, prescribing a finite generating set of `A` and
protecting a ball containing `F_A cup F_B`, gives `gamma : F ->> F'` with

```text
gamma(A) <= gamma(S_D) <= gamma(D) <= gamma(B),   hence   F' = gamma(B).
```

**Stage two.**  Hull's published proof of Corollary 7.4 uses Lemma 4.4 to keep
`{<gamma(f_1)>, <gamma(f_2)>}` hyperbolically embedded in `F'` and then calls
the whole image `gamma(A)` suitable.  Call the smaller subgroup
`S_A = <gamma(f_1), gamma(f_2)>` suitable instead: `<gamma(f_1)>` is
hyperbolically embedded and proper in `S_A` because the two cyclic subgroups
remain non-commensurable, so Lemma 5.8 applies verbatim.  `F'` is finitely
generated by the display above; apply Theorem 7.1 a second time with suitable
subgroup `S_A`, prescribing a finite generating set of `F'`.  Every generator
of `F'` lands in `eta(S_A)`, so

```text
Q = eta(S_A) = <eta gamma(f_1), eta gamma(f_2)>.
```

Both maps are onto — `A` because `f_1, f_2 in A`, `B` because `F' = gamma(B)` —
and chaining `Q = eta gamma(A) <= eta gamma(D) <= Q` gives clause 6 again.
Torsion is Theorem 7.1(e) at each of the two stages, and finite presentability
holds because each stage adds one relator per prescribed element and the
finitely generated case never invokes the direct limit of Corollary 7.3.

## Why this is a genuine step and not a restatement

The prerequisite is silent about the number of generators of `Q` and about
single-element reservoirs; it can hold with `Q` needing arbitrarily many
generators, since nothing in its statement constrains which suitable subgroup
the second stage is handed.  The step that fails or succeeds here is exactly
the substitution of `S_A` for `gamma(A)`, and it is the substitution the repo
has already audited adversarially in the Fournier--Facio instance
(`defect-normally-generates-torsion-free-quotient`, addendum 2026-08-15, and
the external multi-agent audit recorded on GitHub issue #1), where the same
mechanism produces `Q = q(<h_1, h_2>)`.  What is generalized is which two
elements the final suitable subgroup is built from, not any estimate.
