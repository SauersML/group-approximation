title: Finite-lamp wreath products over uniformly bounded locally virtually nilpotent groups have finite nuclear dimension
---

Let `K` be a finite group and let `H` be a countable group such that every
finitely generated subgroup of `H` is virtually nilpotent.  Suppose that

```text
sup{h(V): V subset H finitely generated}=r<infinity.
```

For the restricted wreath product

```text
K wr H = (direct_sum_H K) rtimes H
```

one has

```text
dim_nuc(C*(K wr H)) <= 2 * 9^(r^2) < infinity.          (1)
```

In particular, for every nontrivial finite `K`, the group `K wr Q` has
finite-dimensional group C*-algebra (with bound `18`) although it is not
locally virtually polycyclic.  Thus (1) is not a consequence of the
locally-virtually-polycyclic exhaustion theorem.
