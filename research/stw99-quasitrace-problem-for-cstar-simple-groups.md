For every countable discrete C\*-simple group `G`, the unique tracial state of
`C*_r(G)` (Breuillard--Kalantar--Kennedy--Ozawa, Publ. IHÉS 126 (2017)) is its
unique bounded quasitrace.

Open in general (STW, footnote 152, attributed to Hannes Thiel).  Follows from
Problem I, and from strict comparison for `C*_r(G)` with respect to its trace
(`stw99-xc-implies-quasitrace-for-cstar-simple`).  For exact `G` Haagerup's
theorem gives it directly.
