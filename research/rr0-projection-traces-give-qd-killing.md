**OPEN.**  Let `A` be separable, nuclear and of real rank zero, with every
quotient satisfying the UCT, and let `G ⊆ K_0(A)` be singular.  Suppose every
nonzero projection `P ∈ M_∞(A)` carries a lower semicontinuous trace `σ_P` with
`σ_P(P) = 1` that annihilates `G` on its finiteness ideal (in the sense of
`af-killing-embeddings-force-projection-annihilating-traces`).  Then some faithful
*-homomorphism of `A` into a quasidiagonal algebra kills `G`.

Role: the route `bk-rr0-uct-case-from-projection-trace-killing` derives
`bk-rr0-uct-stably-finite-algebras-are-qd` from this claim.

## Attempts

- **Necessity, and why this is the right hypothesis.**  For AF targets the trace
  condition is necessary (Theorem A of
  `research/artifacts/bk-af-killing-projection-traces-2026-09-13.md`).  In every
  stably finite exact extension it holds automatically for the boundary
  (`stably-finite-projections-carry-annihilating-traces`).  The single-trace
  uniqueness regime of relative Lemma B is empty here
  (`relative-lemma-b-uniqueness-excludes-af-killing`).
- **Known cases.**  For AF sources the claim holds, even with an AF target:
  - the quotient `(G/S) ⊗ Q` with isolated cone extends to a total order (artifact
    §4, Levi recalled);
  - Elliott realises the resulting positive map;
  - property SP gives faithfulness.

  When every ideal contains a full projection it holds by
  `bk-projection-generated-ideals-with-uct-quotients-force-qd`.
- **One trace at a time: where it dies.**
  - *Setup.*  Take `P` in an approximate unit of projections so large that a
    finitely generated `G_0 ⊆ G` lies in `im K_0(I_P)` (Moutzouris Prop 3.3 reduces
    to such `G_0`).
  - *Lemma B step.*  `σ_P` is faithful on `I_P/N_P`, where `N_P` is its kernel
    ideal.  Lemma B kills the image of `ι_P^{-1}(G_0)` there, and this image
    contains `∂(K_1(A/I_P))`.
  - *Pushout.*  A Brown–Dadarlat pushout then makes `A/N_P` embed in a
    quasidiagonal algebra killing `G_0`, **provided `A/I_P` is quasidiagonal**.
  - *Obstruction 1.*  For the radical `R` of a counterexample, `R/I_P` is an ideal
    of `E/I_P`, which is not quasidiagonal because `I_P ⊊ R`.
  - *Obstruction 2.*  Even granting that, the countably many kernels `N_P` have
    zero intersection, so a direct product of the pieces is faithful.  But `K_0`
    of a product need not see the killing: the pushouts need not have
    cancellation, and one needs Moutzouris' asymptotic form (Prop 3.2) with
    uniform sizes.
- **Classification route (not attempted).**  Realising the totally ordered `K_0`
  map by an embedding into a non-simple AF algebra, with prescribed traces along a
  chain of ideals, is the non-simple codomain form of Schafhauser Theorem D.
  Schafhauser (arXiv:1807.07381v2, p. 4, read) defers it to Carrión–Gabe–
  Schafhauser–Tikuisis–White.  Whether their later work covers it was not checked.
