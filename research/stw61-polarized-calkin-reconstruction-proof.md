The coefficient unitary W_U=sum S_a U_ab S_b*+E, for the
identity, swap, real rotation, and imaginary rotation matrices
U, carries S1 to the four stated isometries. The two rotated
compressions polarize to recover S1* V_t S2 and its adjoint
from the diagonal compression data.

If all four compression classes were norm continuous, every
matrix corner would be norm continuous in the Calkin algebra.
The compact defect then reconstructs [V_t] as the sum of the
four fixed-isometry corner products. This would be a norm-
continuous self-adjoint-unitary path from +1 to -1, equivalently
a norm-continuous projection path from 1 to 0. The identity
projection is isolated among projections, so such a path
cannot exist. Compact corrections do not alter the quotient
classes and therefore cannot remove the obstruction.
