**ESTABLISHED** (proof in the artifact, Section 3). Let `S` be a unital algebra over a prime field
`F_p` with `s, t ∈ S`, `ts = 1 != st`, and put `e = 1 − st`. Every homomorphism from `EL_3(S)`,
or from any group containing it, into a residually finite group (in particular any finite group)
kills `x_12(λ e)` for all `λ ∈ F_p`.

**Why.**
* The elements `s^a e t^b` are nonzero matrix units, so the root elements `x_ij(λ s^a e t^b)`
  generate a copy of `SL_fin(F_p)`, which is infinite and simple.
* A residually finite group contains no infinite simple subgroup.

**Consequence.** Left-regular rank models of finite images of `EL_3(S)`, and all stagewise limits of
them, satisfy `σ(x_12(1 − st)) = 1` once `S` contains the Toeplitz pair. So the finite-image models
of `fd-represented-coefficients-violate-two-root-identities` never assemble into a nontrivial rank
model of `EL_3(L_(F_2)(1,2))`.
