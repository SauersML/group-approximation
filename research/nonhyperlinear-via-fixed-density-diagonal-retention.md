Take the finitely presented quotient `Q=G/H_tau`.  Along the supplied
recurrence sequence, the diagonal powers converge to the canonical quotient
character while retaining the non-CE `tau` summand with weight at least
`beta`.  The domination theorem identifies the `tau`-GNS algebra with a
central summand of `L(Q)`.  Hence `L(Q)` is non-CE and `Q` is the required
explicit non-hyperlinear group.
