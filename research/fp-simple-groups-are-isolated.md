- **Finitely discriminable** (Proposition--Definition, lines 519–533). Among
  the equivalent conditions: "There exists a finite discriminating subset in
  G: this is a finite subset F ⊂ G−{1} such that any non-trivial normal
  subgroup of G contains at least one element of F."
- **Isolated** (Proposition, lines 554–557): "A group G is isolated if and
  only if it is both finitely presentable and finitely discriminable."

**Statement.** A finitely presented simple group is isolated.

**Novelty.** None. CGP say so directly (line 1207): "Obvious examples of
isolated groups are finitely presented simple groups." The node exists so the
routes of `every-decidable-group-embeds-in-an-isolated-group` can cite it.
