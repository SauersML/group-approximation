**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `(M,tau)` be a finite tracial von Neumann algebra.  Let
`p,q_1,...,q_r` be projections and suppose the prescribed incidence pieces
`r_i` are projections satisfying

```text
p q_i=r_i.                                               (PCM1)
```

Put

```text
delta_i=tau(1-q_i),             m=meet_i r_i.           (PCM2)
```

Then

```text
p q_i=q_i p=r_i,                r_i<=p,                 (PCM3)
tau(p-r_i)<=delta_i,                                    (PCM4)
tau(m)>=tau(p)-sum_i delta_i
       >=max_i tau(r_i)-sum_i delta_i.                  (PCM5)
```

In particular, if one prescribed negative incidence has trace larger than
the total mask deficit, every projection CRT solution forces a nonzero
literal common negative subspace.  This conclusion needs neither the tight
defect cubic nor finite-dimensionality.

For two incidences there is a sharper cross-deficit form:

```text
tau(r_2)-tau(r_1 meet r_2)<=delta_1,
tau(r_1)-tau(r_1 meet r_2)<=delta_2.                    (PCM6)
```

Thus two generic half-rank projections with zero meet require total mask
deficit at least one.  Canonical L2 closeness alone is insufficient: a small
generic rotation can have zero literal excess intersection even though its
L2 displacement is small.

## Sharp noncommuting two-context cell

The bound is attained.  In `M_d(C)`, choose a projection `p` of rank `N>=2`
and two distinct nonorthogonal rank-one projections `u_1,u_2<=p`.  Put

```text
r_i=p-u_i,                 q_i=1-u_i,
A_i=1-2r_i,                B=1-2p.                     (PCM7)
```

Then `q_1,q_2` do not commute, both have deficit `1/d`, and

```text
(B-A_i)q_i=0,              (1-A_i)q_i/2=r_i.           (PCM8)
```

Moreover `r_1 meet r_2=p-(u_1 join u_2)`, so both inequalities in `(PCM6)`
and `(PCM5)` are equalities.  Taking, for example, the lines generated by
`e_1` and `(3e_1+4e_2)/5` makes every coefficient rational.  The same
construction with finitely many rank-one `u_i` gives an `r`-incidence cell
with total deficit `r/d` and common core

```text
p-join_i u_i.                                           (PCM9)
```

It can therefore be placed in a sufficiently large full matrix summand of a
finite group algebra, including the affine Frobenius simple block.

This cell identifies, rather than solves, the global BCS gate.  It works by
making every context marginal for a shared variable a finite-rank
perturbation of the same projection `p`.  Requiring all context PVMs and all
variables to admit such cells with total canonical deficit below the fixed
BCS gap is already the forbidden finite row-mask certificate.  Hence local
noncommuting CRT interpolation is scalable; the missing construction is the
simultaneous correlation of the fixed nonaffine context PVMs.  A Bass--Serre
or finite-packet realization cannot supply that global correlation.
