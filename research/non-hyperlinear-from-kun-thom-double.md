Specialize the negative double family to r=d=3. The explicitly defined
amalgamated group is a witness for the root.

**Attribution.** This conditional consequence uses Jihao Liu,
[*Nonhyperlinear groups exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf),
Theorem 1.2/6.7, through Thom's conditional normalization and the Kun–Thom
pair, together with the recorded wreath/double equivalence. No independent
discovery or unconditional import of Liu's analytic theorem is claimed.
