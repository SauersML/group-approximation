**ESTABLISHED** by `locally-moving-groups-first-l2-betti-proof` (lane proof, bh-free-13, 2026-09-18; not
independently reviewed). In one case the proof cites Peterson–Thom (arXiv:0708.4327, Lemma `alter` and
Theorem `main1`, read at source); the other case is elementary. **Priority.** Special cases are known:
Bader–Furman–Sauer (arXiv:1206.4793, abstract) give "a uniform proof for the vanishing of L2-Betti numbers of
Thompson's groups F and T". No priority is claimed for the general statement.

## Statement

Let `X` be a Hausdorff space without isolated points and `G ≤ Homeo(X)`. For an open `U ⊆ X` let
`G_U = {g ∈ G : g = id on X ∖ U}`. Assume
- **(LM1)** `G_U ≠ 1` for every nonempty open `U`;
- **(LM2)** `G` is generated by the groups `G_V` for open `V` whose complement has nonempty interior.

Then:
1. `β_1^(2)(G) = 0`.
2. If `G_U` is nonamenable for every nonempty open `U`, then moreover `H^1(G, ℓ^2 G) = 0` (unreduced).
3. (LM2) holds whenever `G` is simple and (LM1) holds. The family `{G_V}` is permuted by conjugation, since
   `g G_V g^(-1) = G_(gV)`, so it generates a nontrivial normal subgroup.

## Examples

Every group below is simple, satisfies (LM1) for its standard action, and so has `β_1^(2) = 0`:
- Thompson's `F'`, `T` and `V`, and the Brin–Thompson groups `nV`;
- the Higman–Thompson commutator subgroups, and the Röver–Nekrashevych commutator subgroups;
- twisted Brin–Thompson groups `SV_G` and `TA`, `VA` (arXiv:2407.03149);
- Lodha's `S` and the Lodha–Moore commutator subgroup `G_0'`;
- the Hyde–Lodha groups.

(LM1) is checked in each case by exhibiting a copy of `F'` or `V` supported in any given cone or arc. This is
standard and not re-derived here.

## Lesson for general BH

The absorption lemma of the proof is a general tool for locally moving hosts. Any subgroup that contains one
rigid stabilizer `G_U`, and that absorbs every element conjugating it to itself with infinite overlap, is
already the whole group. So in every dynamical Boone–Higman host (`V`, `nV`, twisted Brin–Thompson, germ
extensions, circle groups), one rigid stabilizer is wq-normal. Cocycle, L² and cost invariants of the host are
determined by that one piece and vanish. Two consequences:
- An obstruction to BH that is to survive passage to dynamical hosts cannot be of L²/cocycle type.
- Conversely, to verify a property of a locally moving host that propagates along infinite overlaps
  (vanishing cocycles, rigidity, and similar), it is enough to verify it on one rigid stabilizer.

## Why it matters here

With `simple-groups-on-trees-with-fg-edge-groups-have-b1-zero`, this closes the L²-rank route of
`fp-infinite-simple-group-not-two-generated` (Attempt 2) on every known family of finitely presented simple
groups except the Kac–Moody lattices:
- locally moving groups (this node);
- lattices in products of trees (that node);
- Kazhdan groups.

A finitely presented simple group with `β_1^(2) > 1` would have to be of a new kind: not locally moving, with
no fixed-point-free tree action with a finitely generated edge group, and without (T).
