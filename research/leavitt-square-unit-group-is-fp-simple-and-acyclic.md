**ESTABLISHED (conditional on the imported criteria of Khanh arXiv:2609.08428v1
and on Ara--Cortiñas arXiv:1108.0352v3).** Let `L = L_(F_2)(1,2)`.
- The group `(L ⊗ L)^x` is finitely presented, simple, infinite and integrally
  acyclic.
- For every `r >= 3`, `St_r(L ⊗ L) -> GL_r(L ⊗ L)` is an isomorphism.

It is a second finitely presented simple group of Leavitt type. Its point is
the Boone--Higman class in `char-two-surface-linear-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`:
`L ⊗ L` contains `F_2[t_1, t_2][1/h]` for every nonzero `h`.

Scope: whether `(L ⊗ L)^x` is isomorphic to `L^x` is not settled.
