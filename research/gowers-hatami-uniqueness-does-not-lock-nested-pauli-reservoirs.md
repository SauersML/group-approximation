title: Gowers--Hatami uniqueness synchronizes fixed-level Pauli representations but not nested multiplicity reservoirs
---

Fixed-level approximate uniqueness aligns two copies of the same Pauli
packet on most of the space.  It cannot choose the next Pauli factor inside
the old packet's multiplicity algebra.

The exact nested Pauli tower realizes label doubling and multiplicity
halving with zero defect and arbitrary child-factor gauge.  A mixed return
cycle, not stronger same-level uniqueness, is required to lock reservoirs.
