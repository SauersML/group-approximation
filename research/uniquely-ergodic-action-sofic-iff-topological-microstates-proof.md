Full proof: Section 3 of `research/artifacts/sl3z-cocompact-quotient-microstates-2026-09-13.md`. Soficity
is tested by Lemma LC, proved in Section 2 of `research/artifacts/nonsofic-action-mixing-transfer-2026-09-12.md`
inside the proof of `sofic-action-with-sofic-ergodic-components-is-sofic`.

0. **Partitions.** Use the partitions `P_m` generated by finite covers by `μ`-continuity balls of radius
   `1/m`. Then `mesh(P_m) <= 2/m`, and all atoms and their translates are continuity sets.
1. **Sofic ⇒ microstates.** Take a model of level `m` and error `ε/(|P_m|^2+1)`. Send each point of
   `Y_p` to a point `x_p ∈ p`. The word `u_s u_t u_(st)^*` gives the first bound. The word
   `1_q u_s 1_p u_s^*` has trace `μ(q ∩ sp)`. A pair with `d(s x_p, x_q) >= ε` has `sp ∩ q = ∅` once the
   mesh is small, so its model fraction is below the error. Summing over pairs gives the second bound.
2. **Equidistribution.** For microstates `(V_k, σ_k, y_k)` with `F_k ↑ Γ` and `ε_k → 0`, put
   `ν_k = (y_k)_* unif`. Because `σ_k(s)` is a bijection,
   `|∫ f∘s dν_k − ∫ f dν_k| <= ω_f(ε_k) + 2||f|| ε_k`. So limit points are invariant, and `ν_k → μ`.
3. **Models.** On `V_k × W_k`, with `τ_k` a sofic approximation of `Γ`, act by `σ_k × τ_k` and partition
   by `y_k^(−1)(p) × W_k`. A word's trace factors as `tr(w_V)·tr(S_w(τ_k))`. If the group part is
   nontrivial, the second factor tends to `0`. If it is trivial, iterating the microstate bounds along
   the `<= m` letters shows that, outside density `2mε_k`, the path closes and its marks lie within
   `η_k → 0` of the translates of `y_k(v)`. So `tr(w_V) = ν_k(B_w) + o(1)`, using Portmanteau for the
   closed `η`-neighbourhoods of the null boundaries. This tends to `μ(B_w)`. Lemma LC concludes. ∎
