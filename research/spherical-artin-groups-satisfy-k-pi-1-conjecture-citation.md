Citation import, not a reproof. Primary source: P. Deligne, *Les immeubles des groupes de
tresses généralisés*, Invent. Math. 17 (1972), no. 4, 273–302 (bibliographic data from the
bibliography of arXiv:2607.24659). The primary source was NOT read; the statement is taken
from three independent secondary sources, read on 2026-09-13 from their arXiv LaTeX sources.

- G. Paolini and M. Salvetti, arXiv:1907.11795, introduction: "The conjecture was proved for
  spherical Artin groups (i.e. if W is finite) by Deligne [deligne1972immeubles], after being
  proved by Fox and Neuwirth in the case A_n [fox1962braid] and by Brieskorn in the cases
  C_n, D_n, G_2, F_4, and I_2(p) [brieskorn1973groupes]."
- E. Godelle and L. Paris, arXiv:1007.1365, introduction: "Spherical type Artin-Tits groups
  are torsion free, they have (fast) solutions to the word problem ..., and they are of type
  K(π,1) (see [Delig1])."
- G. Paolini, *The K(π,1) conjecture for Artin groups of spherical type*, arXiv:2607.24659
  (lecture notes, July 2026), whose abstract says the notes give a proof of the conjecture in
  the spherical case, drawing on a special case of Deligne's 1972 theorem.
