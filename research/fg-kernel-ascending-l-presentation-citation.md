Literature import. Source: M. G. Benli, *Indicable Groups and Endomorphic Presentations*,
arXiv:1112.1764 (TeX `GMJ-11-0168.tex`, fetched on MSI 2026-09-13).

- Abstract (l.37–40), verbatim: "We prove that if $H$ is a finitely generated normal subgroup of a
  finitely presented group $G$ with $G/H$ cyclic, then $H$ has ascending finite endomorphic
  presentation. It follows that any finitely presented indicable group without free semigroups has
  the structure of a semidirect product $H \rtimes \field{Z}$ where $H$ has finite ascending
  endomorphic presentation."
- Remark 1 (l.491–494): "In our special case (i.e. a presentation for $H$ is obtained via theorem
  1), the endomorphisms of the L-presentation of $H$ actually induce automorphism of $H$ and $H$
  embeds into $G$ as a normal subgroup."

This route asserts only what the cited paper states; the proof of Theorem 1 (TeX l.213) was not
re-derived.
