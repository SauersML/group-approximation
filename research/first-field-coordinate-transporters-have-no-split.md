title: An arbitrary binary-linear transporter confined to the first field coordinate has no reduced projective split

Use K=GL_2(F_4) and the reduced projectors f_0,P_0 of the half-corner
construction. Encode its two field coordinates as four binary roots.
If g acts by diag(a,I_2), where a is any invertible binary-linear
operator on the first field coordinate's boundary space, then

    L P_0[g]f_0=f_0

has no solution in the group algebra of <K,g> or any larger ambient
group. In particular this covers every such matrix over L_2(F_2).

The theorem concerns the literal single transporter. It does not
cover arbitrary finite-chart coefficients placed around g. The
artifact also excludes a block-diagonal actor whenever either block
preserves or reverses field characters, and gives a fully mixed
opposite-compressor control. General mixed actors remain open.
