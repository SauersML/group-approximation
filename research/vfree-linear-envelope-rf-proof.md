Pontryagin duality presents `A` as a finite-rank free `F_p[G]`-module modulo
the finitely generated module of local constraints.  Restrict to a finite-index
normal free subgroup `F`.  The residual-finiteness theorem for finitely
presented `F_p[F]`-modules separates every nonzero element by a finite module
quotient.  Intersect the finitely many conjugates of its kernel to make it
`G`-invariant without losing the chosen element.  Hence `A` is residually
finite as a `G`-module.  Separate `(a,g)` in `A semidirect G` either through a
finite quotient of `G` when `g!=1`, or through a finite `G`-module quotient
when `g=1`.  Fourier duality identifies the Haar crossed product with the
resulting group von Neumann algebra.
