The two gate corners at each relator vertex give the usual nonbranching
square corridors; commuting `V_4` squares allow the two gate colors to cross
without creating an endpoint.  An internal endpoint can occur only at a
coefficient region whose cyclic corner label is an actual identity not
fillable by the short group.  The prerequisite says no such word exists in
any length.  Replacing the region by its short-group van Kampen filling
continues the corridor, contradicting maximality.  Hence only the outer
boundary or a closed corridor remains.
