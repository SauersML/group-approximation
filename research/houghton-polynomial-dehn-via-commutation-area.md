By `houghton-dehn-polynomially-equivalent-to-commutation-area`,
`δ_(H_n)(L) <= C L^3 (A(CL) + 1)`. If `A(ℓ) ≼ ℓ^k`, as asserted by
`houghton-transposition-commutation-area-is-polynomial`, then
`δ_(H_n)(x) ≼ x^(k+3)`.
