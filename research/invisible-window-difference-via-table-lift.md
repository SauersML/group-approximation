The generators map onto their group elements, and the imposed relators make the
coincidence patterns on `S x M` and `Omega x M` exact in the table group.
Transporting assignments along these bijections preserves `sigma tau = id` and
the Garden of Eden. The table group is countable, so the localization theorem
gives an invisible difference of two window generators. It is nontrivial
because it maps to a nontrivial element of `G`. Section 5 of the artifact.
