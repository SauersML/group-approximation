**ESTABLISHED.**  The metaplectic double cover `E_2` of `Sp_4(Z)` is
residually finite, and some finite quotient detects its central involution.
Consequently the nontrivial central-character summand of a finite-dimensional
representation of that quotient gives an exact projective representation of
`Sp_4(Z)` whose multiplier is the Maslov half-class.

The proof is `metaplectic-exact-finite-model-proof`.
