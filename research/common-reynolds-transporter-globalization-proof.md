All forbidden assignments in one context are characters of the same finite
context subgroup.  A common stable letter acts by one conjugation map, so its
specified restrictions agree on overlaps and extend to the subgroup generated
by all context groups and the common role diagonal.  Transverse role
covariance extends this to the full structural algebra, where forbidden
support saturation turns the return into whole-carrier transport.  Without
that covariance, the role multiplicity gauge remains free.
