OPEN.  Build a family of product-socle packets from an almost simple group
`X` with socle `T`, a proper subgroup `K<T`, and its normalizer `U=N_X(K)`,
so that `K` is maximal among proper `U`-invariant subgroups of `T`.  At
multiplicity scale `k`, the packet must expose `T^k`, `K^k`, and a transitive
coordinate actor by fixed presentation words whose number does not grow with
the ambient matrix dimension.

For every sufficiently accurate microstate, decode the transported
reservoir support as an approximate intermediate object

```text
K^k <= E <= T^k                                           (SND1)
```

which is approximately invariant under the normalizer and coordinate actor.
Prove a dimension-independent perturbation/dichotomy:

```text
E is close to K^k    or    E is close to T^k.             (SND2)
```

Maximal `U`-invariance gives the coordinatewise alternative, and actor
transitivity must force the same choice in every coordinate.  Crucially,
`k` is the Leavitt multiplicity reservoir itself (or a word-visible
commutant/socle support canonically equivalent to it); it is not a fixed
finite tag tensored with an anonymous spectator.

The algebraic template is Li--Yang, *Products of nonconjugate maximal
subgroups and solvability* ([arXiv:2608.19478](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19478)).
Their almost-simple normalizer choice and product-socle lifting explain the
binary pattern, but do not supply the normalized-HS robust decoding in
`(SND1)--(SND2)`.  That stability statement is the new project theorem.

## Attempts

**Coordinatewise exactification.**  Exactify the fixed local `T,U,K` tables
on each detected socle coordinate, use maximal `U`-invariance to obtain the
local `K`/`T` alternative, then average the choice projection under the
transitive coordinate actor.  The attack currently stops before the first
step: ordinary finite-group exactification does not canonically name the
growing coordinate decomposition or prevent it from rotating into the
spectator multiplicity.  A successful packet must make that scalable support
word-visible before normalizer averaging is applied.

## Fixed-packet firewall and kill test

Holding `T,U,K` and the number of socle coordinates fixed leaves an arbitrary
spectator factor and falls exactly under
`fixed-packet-normalizers-cannot-supply-the-matrix-only-pi-lock`.  Such a
model is not partial progress on this claim.  The first exact test is to add
the proposed packet to `rectangular-escape`; if label enlargement can still
be absorbed by an unused multiplicity factor while all normalizer words stay
exact, the design is refuted.

The dichotomy alone also does not close the route.  Its `T^k` branch is a
genuine transverse enlargement and must be excluded by a matrix-only
detector, such as the fixed-degree PI fingerprint, before the `K^k` branch
can be read as same-reservoir closure.
