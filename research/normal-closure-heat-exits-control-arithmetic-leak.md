Let `A=SL_3(Z[1/p])`, `C=SL_3(Z)`, and let `x_N,q` be as in
`arithmetic-leak-has-fd-null-laplacian-witness`.  Since `A` is perfect and
is generated by `C` and the denominator `h`, the cyclic quotient
`A/normal_closure(C)` is trivial.  Fix once and for all an expression

```text
h=product_(j=1..r) a_j c_j a_j^(-1),        c_j in C.   (NHE1)
```

For every exact unitary representation `alpha` of `A`, every `C`-fixed
vector `xi`, and every `N>=1`,

```text
||(alpha(h)-1)xi||
 <= [2/(1-q^N)] sum_(j=1..r)
      ||(1-alpha(a_j x_N a_j^(-1)))xi||.                (NHE2)
```

To prove this, let `P` project onto the `C`-fixed vectors.  Property `(T)`
and spectral calculus give

```text
||(1-P)zeta||
 <= (1-q^N)^(-1)||(1-alpha(x_N))zeta||.                 (NHE3)
```

Telescope `(NHE1)`.  Since `(alpha(c_j)-1)P=0`, each conjugate factor costs
at most `2||(1-P)alpha(a_j^(-1))xi||`; inserting `(NHE3)` and conjugating
the heat filter gives `(NHE2)`.

The bound isolates the missing arithmetic datum.  Transported filters are
tautologically exact on their transported vectors, while `(NHE2)` needs the
**cross** exits of those filters on the original selected vector.  Finite
spherical averages do not control them.  A successful proof must authenticate
root/denominator incidence forcing these finitely many cross exits to vanish;
the canonical HNN leak shows that abstract group relations alone cannot do
so.
