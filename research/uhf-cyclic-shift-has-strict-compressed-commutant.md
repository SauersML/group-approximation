**ESTABLISHED.**  Put

```text
Q=(product_(n>=1) M_(2^n)(C))/(directSum_norm M_(2^n)(C)) . (UCS1)
```

Embed the algebraic union of the UHF tensor stages in `Q` by

```text
x in M_(2^k) |-> [x tensor I_(2^(n-k))]_(n>=k).        (UCS2)
```

Let `L` be the locally finite group generated by the single-site Pauli
operators in this union.  Its complex linear span is norm dense in the
`2^infinity` UHF algebra.

For each `n`, let `c_n` cyclically permute the `n` tensor factors, with the
orientation

```text
Ad(c_n)(X at site i)=X at site i+1 mod n,              (UCS3)
```

and put `c=[c_n] in Q`.  On every fixed tensor word the wraparound disappears
for all sufficiently large `n`; hence

```text
c L c^* = sigma(L) < L,                               (UCS4)
```

where `sigma` is the one-sided shift and the inclusion is proper.

Let `B=L' intersect Q`.  Taking commutants in `(UCS4)` and conjugating gives

```text
c^* B c <= B.                                         (UCS5)
```

The inclusion is strict.  Let

```text
v_n=Z at the last tensor site,
v=[v_n].                                               (UCS6)
```

Every fixed element of `L` is supported in a bounded initial tensor interval,
so `[v,l]=0` in `Q` for every `l in L`; thus `v in B`.  On the other hand
`c_n^*(X at site 1)c_n=X at site n`, which anticommutes with `v_n`.  Hence

```text
v notin (c^* L c)' intersect Q = c^* B c,              (UCS7)
```

and therefore

```text
c^*(L' intersect Q)c < L' intersect Q.                (UCS8)
```

Thus one-sided compression does not generically normalize a norm-corona
relative commutant, even for a locally finite tensor-Pauli source whose span
is a UHF algebra.  Any Property-`(T)`-free Leavitt argument using such
normalization must prove it from additional coefficient-sensitive relations;
compression and finite tensor approximation alone give the opposite
behavior.

This is an operator-norm statement.  No trace, Property `(T)`, stability
theorem, or literature input is used.
