title: A finite simple seed with infinite double has no central two-by-two matrix sequence

  stw99-lxvi-car-stabilization-destroys-finite-seed: that concerns a tensor-product CAR stabilization; this rules out even an asymptotically central sequence of unital two-by-two matrix algebras inside the seed itself.
  stw99-lxvi-finite-seed-has-no-quasitrace: that is a tracial obstruction; this is a central-sequence obstruction requiring no quasitrace.
---

Let `A` be a simple unital C\*-algebra.  If there is a unital embedding

```text
M_2 -> A_infinity cap A'
```

and the unit of `M_2(A)` is infinite, then the unit of `A` is infinite.

Consequently, a simple unital finite algebra whose two-by-two amplification is
infinite has no unital copy of `M_2` in its central sequence algebra.  In
particular, in any solution of
`stw99-lxvi-finite-car-corner-with-infinite-double`, the tail matrix algebras of
the unital CAR copy cannot become asymptotically central in the ambient
algebra.
