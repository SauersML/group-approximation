Written deduction in Section 2 of the linked note. Its inputs are:
* weak containment of actions passes to their Koopman representations on
  `L^2_0` (a short argument via simple functions, given in the note);
* the tensor basis decomposition of `L^2_0` of a Bernoulli shift;
* Hulanicki--Reiter amenability through weak containment of the trivial
  representation in the regular representation.

This is not a formal verification.
