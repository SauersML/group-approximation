SSA algebras are separable and nuclear, so Problem II gives them the
UCT; the identification with the known list is then the
Tikuisis--White-era classification of UCT strongly self-absorbing
algebras (finite: `Z` and UHF∞-type by TWW quasidiagonality plus
classification; infinite: Kirchberg--Phillips).
