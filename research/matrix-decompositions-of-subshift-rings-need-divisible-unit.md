**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed; sk-growth-gx-sturmian).** Let `X` be a Cantor set with a homeomorphism `T`, `q` a prime power and `R_X = LC(X,F_q) ⋊_T Z`. If `M_k(R_X) ≅ M_N(A)` as rings for a unital ring `A`, then `k[χ_X] = N·c` for some `c ∈ K^0 := C(X,Z)/(1−T_*)C(X,Z)`. Consequently, for every `T`-invariant Borel probability `μ`, `k/N ∈ μ(K^0)`, where `μ(K^0)` is the subgroup of `R` generated by the measures of clopen sets. If `K^0` is torsion-free (true for minimal `T`, by Herman–Putnam–Skau; not needed below), `[χ_X]` is divisible by `N/gcd(N,k)` in `K^0`.

**Consequences.**
- For a Sturmian subshift `X_α` (`α` irrational), `M_3(R_X)` is not a matrix ring `M_N(A)` with `N` not dividing 3. In particular `EL_3(R_X)` has no ring-level decomposition `EL_{3·2^n}(A)`, so the dyadic matrix engine behind `self-similar-ring-elementary-groups-non-uniform-growth` cannot start. This relies on the recalled computation `K^0(X_α) ≅ Z + αZ` with unit 1 (not re-read at source); the proof only uses that 1 is not 2-divisible, which already follows from unique ergodicity and clopen measures in `Z + αZ`.
- Every decomposition `M_3(R_X) ≅ End(P^N ⊕ Q)` with `P` repeated `N = 2^n·m` times leaves a nonzero remainder summand `Q` whenever `[χ_X]` is not `2^n`-divisible.
