Literature import. Read on MSI on 2026-09-13 from the author PDF of B. Bekka,
P. de la Harpe, A. Valette, *Kazhdan's Property (T)*, New Mathematical Monographs 11,
Cambridge University Press 2008. The text extraction is
`/scratch.global/sauer354/bh-rn/bdlhv.txt`, Chapter 1, book page 72.

> Theorem 1.7.11 Let G be a locally compact group and C a closed subgroup contained in
> the centre of G. Assume that G/C has Property (T) and that G/[G,G] is compact. Then G
> has Property (T).

From the same page:

> Remark 1.7.12 (i) The assumptions on G/C and G/[G,G] in the previous theorem are
> necessary, by Theorem 1.3.4 and Corollary 1.3.6.

The discrete form in the claim uses only that a discrete group is compact exactly when it
is finite, and that every subgroup of a discrete group is closed. The source proves the
theorem by contradiction through the unitary dual. The proof is not reproduced here.
