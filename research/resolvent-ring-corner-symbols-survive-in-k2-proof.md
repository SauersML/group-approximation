All path notation is that of `resolvent-ring-has-path-normal-form`, whose items 1–3 are used.

**J is an ideal.** Multiplication concatenates the middle words, `[a|K|b]·[a'|K'|b'] = [a|K, b+a', K'|b']`, and
`x^c`, `f(u)` act only on the end letters. So a product with a factor having nonempty `K` again has nonempty
middle word. Also `(e)` is the direct sum of the `G_K [a|K|b]`, so `J ∩ (Z_(l)-span of the [a||b]) = 0` and
`J ∩ D = 0`.

**The quotient.** Modulo `J`:
- `[a||b]·[a'||b'] = [a||b']` if `b + a' = 0`, and `0` otherwise, because a nonzero `b + a'` becomes a middle
  letter.
- With `G_∅ = Z_(l)`, this gives `(e)/J ≅ M = M_Z^fin(Z_(l))` via `[a||b] ↦ E_(a,−b)`.
- The ring `R_l/J = D ⊕ M` is a split extension. `D` is a subring meeting `(e)` in `0` (item 1), and
  `R_l/J -> (R_l/J)/M ≅ R_l/(e) ≅ D`.
- `θ(c) = c·[0||0] ↦ c E_(0,0)`.

**K_2.**
- `M` is the filtered union of the unital rings `M_S(Z_(l))`, for finite `S ⊂ Z`, so it has local units and is
  Tor-unital. Suslin excision (as quoted in `harmonic-coefficient-rings-are-k-equivalent-to-lc-proof`) applies.
- Hence the split surjection `R_l/J -> D` gives `K_2(R_l/J) ≅ K_2(D) ⊕ K_2(M)`.
- `K_2(M) = colim_S K_2(M_S(Z_(l))) ≅ K_2(Z_(l))` by Morita invariance, and the corner `E_(0,0)` induces this
  isomorphism.
- The ring map `R_l -> R_l/J` composed with `θ` is that corner. So `K_2(Z_(l)) -> K_2(R_l) -> K_2(R_l/J)` is the
  split inclusion, and `θ_*` is injective.

**Corner symbols.** `St_N(θ)` sends `K_2(N, Z_(l))` into `K_2(N, R_l)`, since `A ↦ A + (1−e)I_N` embeds
`GL_N(e R_l e)` in `GL_N(R_l)` compatibly with `x_ij(a) ↦ e_ij(a)`. Stabilization commutes with `St(θ)`, so the
stable class of `c(λ,μ)` is `θ_*{λ,μ}`.

**Infinite generation.** Compose with `Z_(l) ⊂ Q`.
- For an odd prime `q ≠ l`, `q` and a primitive root `g_q` modulo `q` (prime to `l`) are units of `Z_(l)`.
- The tame symbol gives `∂_q{q, g_q} = g_q^(−1) mod q ≠ 1`, from the formula
  `∂_v{a,b} = (−1)^(v(a)v(b)) a^(v(b)) / b^(v(a))` (Milnor, *Introduction to algebraic K-theory*, §11; textbook).
- The group generated by these symbols is torsion and has nonzero components at infinitely many `q`, so it is
  infinite and not finitely generated. The same holds before mapping to `Q`. ∎
