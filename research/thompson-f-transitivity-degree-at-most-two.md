**ESTABLISHED (import).**

Imported from A. Le Boudec and N. Matte Bon, *Triple transitivity and non-free
actions in dimension one*, arXiv:1906.05744v3. For `G ≤ Homeo^+(R)`, write
`G^0` for the compactly supported elements of `G`, and `G_x^-`, `G_x^+` for the
subgroups of the stabilizer `G_x` supported in `(-∞,x]` and `[x,+∞)`.

- **Proposition 5.1.** Suppose `G ≤ Homeo^+(R)` acts on `R` with no global
  fixed points and `G^0 ≠ 1`, and `G` acts faithfully and 2-transitively on
  `Ω`. Then at least one of the following holds:
  1. `G^0` is regular on `Ω`;
  2. for every `x ∈ R` and all orbits `O^-` of `G_x^-` and `O^+` of `G_x^+`
     in `Ω`, `|O^- ∩ O^+| ≤ 1`.
- **Proposition 5.2.** Under the same hypotheses on the action on `R`, the
  transitivity degree of `G` is at most 2.
- **Corollary 5.3.** The transitivity degree of Thompson's group `F` is at most
  2.

`F` satisfies the hypotheses through `(0,1) ≅ R`: it has no global fixed point
in `(0,1)`, and `F^0 = [F,F] ≠ 1`.
