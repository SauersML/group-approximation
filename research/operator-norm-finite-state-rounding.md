Prove that every sufficiently accurate finite-dimensional operator-norm model
of a fixed finite presentation of `I_X semidirect V` either violates a fixed
root relator by a uniform amount, or admits a finite exact joint-character
support for the even lamp module on which the actor covariance is exact up to a
controlled correction.

The exact detector `thompson-even-lamp-fp-finite-state-detector` would then
erase the whole corrected model.  The theorem must be dimension-independent
and cannot use normalized-HS density: one corrupted spectral state must remain
visible in operator norm.
