**Established (literature).**  J. Gabe, *Traceless AF embeddings and unsuspended
E-theory*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 30 (2020), arXiv:1804.08095v2, read from the PDF
on 2026-09-12.

1. (Theorem A)  For a separable exact C\*-algebra `A` the following are
   equivalent: (i) `A` embeds into Rørdam's purely infinite ASH-algebra
   `A_[0,1]`; (ii) `A` embeds into `C_0((0,1], O_2)`; (iii) `A` embeds into a
   zero-homotopic C\*-algebra; (iv) `Prim A` has no non-empty, compact, open
   subsets.
2. (Corollary B)  If `A` is separable and exact and `Prim A` has no non-empty
   compact open subsets, then `A` is AF embeddable, in particular quasidiagonal.
3. (Corollary D)  The Blackadar--Kirchberg problem has an affirmative answer for
   all traceless C\*-algebras, and for all C\*-algebras whose primitive ideal
   space has no non-empty, compact, open subsets.  Such algebras are always
   stably projectionless, hence stably finite.
4. (Remark 1)  Compact ideals correspond to compact open subsets of `Prim A`.
5. (Corollary E)  For separable nuclear `A`, `[[A, B⊗K]]` is a group for every
   separable `B`, equivalently the suspension map `[[A, B⊗K]] -> E(A,B)` is an
   isomorphism for every separable `B`, iff `Prim A` has no non-empty compact
   open subsets.
