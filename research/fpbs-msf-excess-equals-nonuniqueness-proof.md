Response 47, Section 4. The event identity P(e in F minus W) = integral over p of P(the endpoints of e are separated into different infinite clusters at level p) is the computation inside the proof of Proposition 3.6 of Lyons-Peres-Schramm, Minimal spanning forests, arXiv:math/0412263; the same proposition gives expected wired root degree two, and Theorem 3.12 gives F = W almost surely if and only if p_c = p_u on a Cayley graph. Summing over the edges at the root converts the degree excess into the integral. This route imports those published results and records the arithmetic; it is not independent verification.
