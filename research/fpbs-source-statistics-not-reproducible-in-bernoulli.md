For nonamenable finitely generated Gamma let b be a free Bernoulli action and let alpha be b times the two-point trivial action. Then alpha is free and has an invariant set of measure 1/2. Approximating that label process in b would give sets A_n with mu(A_n)->1/2 and mu(A_n symmetric difference sA_n)->0, contradicting strong ergodicity of the nonamenable Bernoulli action. A transport theorem must therefore preserve the achievable cost only, not the source correlations.
