Apply `central-letter-averages-matrix-range-separators`.  Its uniform
operator bound allows passage to an ultraproduct tuple `B`; `(CLS5)` gives
`B_s in T' cap M`.  The scalar ucp map

```text
a |-> tau_C(a) I_(d_n),                                           (LMP1)
```

where `tau_C` is the regular trace of `C`, belongs to the matrix range and
sends every `u_s`, `s in S`, to zero.  Hence the support function in
`(CLS4)` is nonnegative, and `(CLS4)` implies

```text
Re sum_s tau(B_s^*X_s)>=epsilon/2.                                (LMP2)
```

The subgroup generated by `C` and `t` in the HNN extension is the direct
product `C x <t>`.  Canonical trace therefore gives

```text
tau(T^k X_s)=0       (k in Z, s in S).                             (LMP3)
```

Trigonometric polynomials are `L^2`-dense in `W^*(T)`, so `(LMP3)` implies
`E_(W^*(T))(X_s)=0`, or equivalently

```text
tau(E_T(B_s)^*X_s)=0.                                             (LMP4)
```

Subtract `(LMP4)` from `(LMP2)` and apply Cauchy--Schwarz.  Since each
`X_s` is unitary, the tuple `X=(X_s)` has norm `sqrt(|S|)`, giving exactly
the lower bound `(LMC1)`.
