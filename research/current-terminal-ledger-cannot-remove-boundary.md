ESTABLISHED FIREWALL.  The
common terminal pinching ledger inserts `O(D)` into parity and complete-pair
energy.  Generated-adjoint rounding on a block of gap `lambda_0` divides
that energy by `lambda_0`.  Summing the blocks gives `(TDE2)`.  Therefore the
available estimates are circular and a new boundary-free or jointly
absorbable estimate is genuinely required.

## Attempts

Reading this calculation as a proof that terminal diagonal code error
vanishes reverses its logic: the upper bound contains the desired terminal
displacement divided by a vanishing gap.  It is a no-go for that proof
schedule, not a route establishing the missing estimate.
