Section 1. The nested finite-cycle spaces give an orthogonal projection
Q=P_H-P_Phi. Both generating graphings have the same group Betti number,
so the required identity gives Tr_Y(Q)=c(H)-c(Phi). Freeness identifies
the orbit edge spaces across the factor. Integrating Q's matrix
coefficients against the equivariant disintegration gives a positive
contraction bounded by P_H; Fubini on the finite incident-edge trace
preserves its trace. This proves the stated operator assertion only.
