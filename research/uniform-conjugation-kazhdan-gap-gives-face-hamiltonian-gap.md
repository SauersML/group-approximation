Let `G_H` be generated by the `L` coordinate involutions, with face width at
most `q`, and suppose every coordinate occurs in between `d_min` and `d_max`
faces.  Assume the normalized conjugation-Kazhdan inequality

```text
(1/L)sum_i ||Ad(pi(x_i))v-v||_2^2
 >= kappa dist(v,Inv(Ad pi))^2                             (UCK1)
```

for every finite-dimensional unitary representation `pi`, with `kappa>0`
uniform in `L`.

For a face `c`, write `P_i=(I+Ad(pi(x_i)))/2`.  The `P_i` commute inside the
face and its pinching is `Phi_c=product_(i in c)P_i`.  On their common
spectral decomposition,

```text
I-Phi_c >= (1/q)sum_(i in c)(I-P_i).                       (UCK2)
```

Averaging `(UCK2)` over the `m=Theta(L)` faces and using the occurrence
bounds gives

```text
(1/m)sum_c <v,(I-Phi_c)v>
 >= c(q,d_min,d_max) kappa dist(v,N')^2.                   (UCK3)
```

This is `repeated-ldpc-adjoint-face-hamiltonian-gap`, with a uniform constant.
Thus uniform property (T) for the exact face groups, quantitatively with the
normalized coordinate sets, is sufficient.

This implication starts only after an exact representation of `G_H` exists;
property (T) does not correct an approximate multiplication table.  It
therefore cannot replace
`repeated-ldpc-shared-face-marginal-synchronization` without a separate HS-
stability theorem.
