For `2n` Majorana modes, label Hermitian Majorana strings by
`v in F_2^(2n)`.  Their commutation sign is the alternating form

```text
omega=I^c,
mu(v)mu(w)=(-1)^(v^T omega w)mu(w)mu(v),                 (MCT1)
```

and fermion parity is `p(v)=v^T v`.  Conjugation by a Majorana Clifford
induces a binary symplectic transformation.  It preserves parity exactly
when the induced transformation is binary orthogonal:

```text
S^T S=I.                                                 (MCT2)
```

Every such transformation fixes the all-ones parity vector `j`.  The
orthogonal action is generated by even Householder reflections

```text
h_a(v)=v+(a^T v)a,       p(a)=0,                         (MCT3)
```

and `h_a` is implemented by the honest braid unitary

```text
B(a)=exp(i pi mu(a)/4)=2^(-1/2)(I+i mu(a)).              (MCT4)
```

Apart from the separately treated full-parity reflection, the source proves
that weight-two and weight-four braid words generate every
parity-preserving Clifford action, with a polynomial word algorithm.

On one fixed parity sector, the effective orthogonal action is the ordinary
symplectic action on

```text
F_2^(2n,+)/<j> ~= F_2^(2n-2).                            (MCT5)
```

Consequently its restricted frame potential equals that of the ordinary
Clifford group on `n-1` qubits.  For this program, `(MCT2)--(MCT4)` provide a
finite honest-word compiler for orthogonal Majorana frame transports, while
`(MCT5)` is a scope fence: parity-preserving Clifford transport does not
create extra orbit capacity beyond the associated symplectic quotient.

This imported result does **not** produce the contextual non-character
decoder required by the V4 five-fourths route.  In particular it does not
alter the bound in `clifford-radical-antiphase-routing-has-character-capacity`.
