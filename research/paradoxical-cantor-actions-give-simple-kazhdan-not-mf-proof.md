Full details are in `research/artifacts/un-boundary-families-2026-09-13-part1.md`, §§1–3.

1. **Groupoid.** By `crossed-products-are-transformation-steinberg-algebras`, `R ≅ A_(F_q)(X ⋊ Γ)`, where `X ⋊ Γ` is a
   Hausdorff ample groupoid with compact infinite unit space `X`.
   - It is minimal, since every orbit is dense.
   - It is effective. An open subset of the isotropy meets `{g} × X` in `{g} × W_g`, with `W_g` open and contained in
     `Fix(g)`. Topological freeness gives `W_g = ∅` for `g != e`.
2. **Finite generation (Lemma F).** Let `R_0` be generated by `u_s^(±1)` for generators `s`, by `χ_P` for the finite
   family `𝒫`, and by a generator of `F_q`.
   - The clopens `U` with `χ_U ∈ R_0` form a Boolean algebra containing every translate `gP`, since
     `χ_(gP) = u_g χ_P u_g^(-1)`.
   - These translates separate points. By compactness (twice), every clopen is a finite union of finite
     intersections of such sets and their complements.
   - So `R_0 ⊇ LC(X, F_q)`, and `R_0 = R`.
3. **Simple and Kazhdan.** `steinberg-elementary-groups-are-simple-mod-centre` applies to `A_(F_q)(X ⋊ Γ)`, which is
   finitely generated over the finite field `F_q`. Normal subgroups of `EL_N(R)` are central or everything,
   `Z ⊆ F_q^× I_N`, and `S_N` is an infinite finitely generated simple group with (T).
4. **Full defect (Lemma P).** Use `u_g χ_A = χ_(gA) u_g`.
   - `ts = Σ_(i,i') u_(g_i)^(-1) χ_(g_i A_i) χ_(g_(i') A_(i')) u_(g_(i'))`. The cross terms vanish because the images
     are disjoint, so `ts = Σ_i χ_(A_i) = 1`.
   - `st = χ_E`, where `E = ⊔_i g_i A_i`.
   - For each `j`, `χ_(B_j) u_(h_j)^(-1) χ_(X \ E) u_(h_j) χ_(B_j) = χ_(B_j ∩ h_j^(-1)(X \ E)) = χ_(B_j)`, since
     `h_j B_j ⊆ X \ E`. Summing over `j` gives `R(1 - st)R = R`.
5. **No MF quotient.** `full-defect-ring-non-mf-at-rank-two` makes every homomorphism from `EL_n(R)`, `n >= 2`, to an
   MF group trivial. A homomorphism from `S_N` to an MF group composes with the quotient map, so it is trivial.
