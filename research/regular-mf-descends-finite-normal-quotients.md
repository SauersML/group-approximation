Let `G` be a countable group, let `F normal G` be finite, and suppose the
regular character `delta_G` is an MF character.  Then the regular character
of `G/F` is an MF character.

This is a finite-kernel permanence theorem for the **trace-controlled** MF
model.  It does not say that MF groups are quotient closed: an arbitrary
faithful norm-corona representation need not carry a trace from which the
finite-kernel averaging corner has positive asymptotic rank.
