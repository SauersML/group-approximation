Lane proof (bh-higman-classic), elementary, not independently reviewed. No priority claimed.

## Statement

Let `k` be a nonzero commutative ring, `T` a group, `1 != g in T`, and

    U_k = k[T]<p,q> / (p(1-g)q - 1).

For `j >= 1` put `w_j = p q p^j q` (all intervening group letters trivial). For `A ⊆ {1,2,3,...}`
let `I_A` be the two-sided ideal generated by `{w_j : j in A}`.

1. `U_k` is a free `k`-module on the words `t_0 x_1 t_1 ... x_m t_m` (`t_i in T`, `x_i in {p,q}`)
   with no segment `x_i t_i x_(i+1) = p g q`.
2. `I_A` is the `k`-span of the basis words of the form `u w_j v` (plain concatenation, merging the
   flanking group letters) with `j in A`.
3. `w_j in I_A` iff `j in A`, and `1 notin I_A`.

So `A |-> I_A` is an injective, inclusion-preserving map from subsets of `N` to proper two-sided
ideals: `U_k` has `2^aleph_0` proper two-sided ideals and infinite strictly ascending chains
`I_{1} ⊊ I_{1,2} ⊊ I_{1,2,3} ⊊ ...`.

## Consequence

By item (B) of `sandwich-ring-envelopes-have-infinite-core-bi-index`, for every `n >= 2` and every
group `Gamma` mapping onto `EL_n(U_k)`, no subgroup `S` meeting every proper normal subgroup of
`Gamma` trivially has finite bi-index `|S \ Gamma / S|`. That node proves this for `k = Z` only
(its item (U)). With this claim the class kill of Attempt 9 on
`ck-envelope-simple-core-has-finite-bi-index` holds for sandwich rings over every coefficient ring,
including `Q[T]` and `F_p[T]`, where the integer congruence chain collapses.

## Scope

- Nothing here concerns the original Chatterji--Kassabov subring `L ⊆ End(W)`, which is a quotient of
  `U_Z`; ideal chains need not survive quotients.
- Nothing here addresses the maximal-kernel route `ck-envelope-has-a-finitely-normally-generated-maximal-kernel`.
