This is exactly Theorem 3.7 of Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell,
*Soficity for group actions on sets and applications*, Research in the
Mathematical Sciences 12 (2025), 48.  Their hypotheses are soficity of the
lamp group, actor group, and actor action on the index set; their conclusion
is soficity of the free generalized wreath product `(GFW1)`.
