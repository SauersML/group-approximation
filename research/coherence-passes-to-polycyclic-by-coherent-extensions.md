title: An extension of a coherent group by a polycyclic-by-finite normal subgroup is coherent
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/zp-sl3z-coherence-non-dense-2026-09-13-part1.md
---

If `1 -> N -> G -> Q -> 1` is exact, `N` is polycyclic-by-finite and `Q`
is coherent, then `G` is coherent.

Consequences used by the Problem 3.4 region: every `Z^n ⋊ Q` with `Q`
coherent is coherent, for instance `Z^2 ⋊ F_2` and the maximal parabolic
subgroups of `SL_3(Z)`.
