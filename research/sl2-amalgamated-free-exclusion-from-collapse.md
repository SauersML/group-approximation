Under `(RCC)` every unitary of `L(C)' cap M` commutes with `L(A)`; an
amalgamated-free `k` has `tau(k u_h k^* u_h^*) = 0` for `h = diag(2, 1/2)`
(alternating centred word), while commuting with `u_h` would give trace
`1`.  So no such `k` exists.
