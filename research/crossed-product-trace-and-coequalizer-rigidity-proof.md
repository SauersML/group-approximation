Let `alpha:Gamma->Aut(A_B)` preserve `tau). On the algebraic crossed
product write its monomials as `a u_g). The reduced conditional
expectation is

```text
E(sum_g a_g u_g)=a_e.                                   (CTR1)
```

It is unital, completely positive, and faithful. The canonical copy of
`A_B` in the reduced crossed product is unital and injective.

Define `tau_r=tau after E`. For two monomials, both cyclic orders have
zero `tau_r)-value unless `h=g^(-1)`. In that case,

```text
tau_r((a u_g)(b u_(g^(-1))))
 =tau(a alpha_g(b))
 =tau(alpha_g(b)a)
 =tau(b alpha_(g^(-1))(a))
 =tau_r((b u_(g^(-1)))(a u_g)).                         (CTR2)
```

The middle equalities use traciality and `alpha)-invariance. Density
proves that `tau_r` is a tracial state, and (CTR1) gives

```text
tau_r(i(a))=tau(a).                                     (CTR3)
```

If `tau` is faithful and `tau_r(x* x)=0`, faithfulness of `tau)
gives `E(x* x)=0), and faithfulness of `E` gives `x=0`. Thus
`tau_r` is faithful.

Connes embeddability of a trace passes to its restriction along a unital
star-homomorphism. Therefore CE of `tau_r` would imply CE of `tau),
contrary to the hypothesis. Likewise, if `A_B` has no unital
star-homomorphism to `R^U`, a map `B_r->R^U` would contradict this
after composition with `i`.

For the promised property-`(T)` example, take the trivial action of

```text
Gamma=SL_3(Z).
```

This group is finitely presented, nonamenable, and has property `(T)`.
The reduced crossed product is the minimal tensor product in (PTE4).
The canonical group unitaries satisfy the relators of `Gamma` and
commute with the copy of `A_B`. This is a finite abstract-group
interface. It is not an LCS presentation: the original forbidden spectral
projection relations of `A_B` remain relations in the first tensor
factor.

We now prove the quotient statement in its general form. For a tracial
state `rho` on a unital C-star algebra `C`, put

```text
N_rho={x in C:rho(x* x)=0}.                             (CTR4)
```

The trace inequality shows that `N_rho` is a closed two-sided ideal; it
is the kernel of the trace-GNS representation. Let `q:C->D` be a
unital surjection and suppose `rho=sigma after q`. Then

```text
ker(q) subseteq N_rho.                                  (CTR5)
```

Indeed, `q(x)=0` implies
`rho(x* x)=sigma(q(x)*q(x))=0`. If `rho` is faithful, (CTR5) says
that `q` is injective and hence an isomorphism.

A C-star coequalizer of unital arrows `f,g:C_0->C` is the quotient of
`C` by the closed ideal generated by

```text
{f(a)-g(a):a in C_0}.                                  (CTR6)
```

If a faithful trace descends through this quotient, (CTR5) makes the
ideal zero. Hence `f(a)=g(a)` for every `a`: the coequalizer imposed no
new relation. Applying this with `C=B_r` proves the reduced assertion.
In particular a trace-preserving quotient `B_r->A_L` to a finite LCS
solution algebra forces an isomorphism `B_r=A_L`; it cannot be a
linearizing quotient.

Finally let

```text
Lambda:B_u=A_B crossed-product_(alpha,max) Gamma -> B_r
```

be the canonical full-to-reduced quotient and set
`tau_u=tau_r after Lambda`. Faithfulness of `tau_r` gives

```text
N_(tau_u)=ker(Lambda).                                  (CTR7)
```

If `q:B_u->D` is surjective and `tau_u=sigma after q`, then directly
from the two trace formulas,

```text
q^(-1)(N_sigma)=N_(tau_u).                              (CTR8)
```

The isomorphism theorem and (CTR7)-(CTR8) now give

```text
D/N_sigma  is isomorphic to  B_u/N_(tau_u)
          is isomorphic to  B_r.                       (CTR9)
```

Thus a full crossed-product quotient can discard only trace-null
universal relations; its represented trace algebra is unchanged. This
also explains the precise boundary of the property-`(T)) factor and
equivalence-relation envelope results: an embedding into a rigid tracial
algebra supplies an ambient trace, but it does not present that faithful
GNS algebra as a finite LCS solution algebra.

If one instead finds a unital arrow from the envelope into an LCS algebra
and extends `tau_r` across it, then the composite from `A_B` is exactly
the terminal trace-extending arrow already isolated in
`trace-extending-bcs-to-lcs-maps-are-already-terminal`. The crossed
product has solved the easy extension half, not the groupification half.
