There are finite products Y_k of complex projective spaces, twisted
tautological bundles H_k, and dense-tail twisted evaluation maps. With
X_k = T^2 x S^3 x Y_k and E_k = 1^2 + H_k, so dim X_k = 2·rank E_k + 1, the
class w + 1_(H_k) of the eta-squared triple commutator is nontrivial in
pi_0 G(E_k) at every stage, and the connecting maps preserve that
nontriviality.

## Attempts

- *Restriction to a point of Y_k.* Dead: it stabilizes w into
  pi_5 U(2+m) = Z, which kills the torsion class. Detection must see the
  twisting.
- *Gauge sequence at excess one.* The class lies in the image of pi_1 of
  the section space. That group has a secondary coordinate
  H^(2r+1)(X; pi_(2r+2) S^(2r+1)) = Z/2, where eta squared lives. What is
  missing is that twisted tautological blocks keep this coordinate out of
  the image of the unstable group pi_1 G(E+1). A Sq^2/Wu-type invariant
  analogous to the mod-2 top Chern class used for LIX is the natural
  candidate. Not yet computed.
