There is a property-`(T)` group `Gamma`, a finite generating set `S`, dimensions
`d_n -> infinity`, and irreducible unitary tuples

```text
V_n=(V_(s,n))_(s in S) in U(d_n)^S
```

with the following simultaneous properties.

1. Their joint commutant is scalar, so the exact irreducible decomposition of
   the generator algebra consists of one full-trace block.
2. For every fixed finite family of words in the generators whose images are
   trivial in `Gamma`, the corresponding word defects of `V_n` tend to zero
   in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.
3. The scalar-block adjoint Poincare constant tends to zero:

   ```text
   inf_(x=x*, tau(x)=0, ||x||_2=1)
     sum_(s in S) ||[V_(s,n),x]||_2^2 -> 0.
   ```

Hence one cannot prove `hs-one-level-expander-block-decomposition` by taking
the exact irreducible decomposition of the matrices generated by an almost
representation and discarding irreducible blocks with large finite-table
relator defect.  The failure can occupy the unique block of trace one while
all fixed relator defects vanish.
