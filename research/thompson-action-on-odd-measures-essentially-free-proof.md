Section 3 of the artifact (Proposition 3.1).

* `g mu = mu` iff `mu` vanishes on `D_g = {f o g + f : f in C(X, F_2)}`.
* The annihilator of a subspace of dimension `d` has Haar measure `2^(-d)`, and `0` when `d` is infinite.
* `g != 1` moves a point, so some cylinder `C` has `C cap g^-1 C = empty`. The disjoint subcylinders
  `C_j` of `C` give linearly independent vectors `1_(C_j) + 1_(g^-1 C_j)`, so `dim D_g` is infinite.
