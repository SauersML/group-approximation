The top-right column of `M_f` is `e_t`. Hence the packet relations give

```text
[p_i,q_(N+1)]=J^((e_t)_i),
```

and in particular `(RSC1)`. Thus raw `q_(N+1)` exchanges the two
`p_t`-eigenspaces and cannot belong to the commutant of the full matrix algebra
generated by `A_f`.

On selector sector `x`, multiply it by old `q` generators with exponent vector
`v`. Its commutator vector against the old `p` generators becomes
`U_f(x)v+e_t`. Since `U_f(x)` is invertible, the unique correction is
`v(x)=U_f(x)^(-1)e_t`. This gives the sectorwise `Q_x` of `(SCP4)`, which
commutes with `A_f`. Summing against the orthogonal selector idempotents gives
the uniform group-algebra involution `Q_f` in `(RSC2)`.

The child-square theorem identifies its two signs, not those of raw
`q_(N+1)`, with the transverse child MASA. Unless every coordinate of `v(x)`
is implemented by one affine selector word, the idempotent sum cannot be
replaced by a group element. Therefore raw-`q` spectral balance does not
control the child-simple type vector.
