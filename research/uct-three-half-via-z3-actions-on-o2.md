Barlak--Li II Theorem 4.16 at `p = 3`, implication (iii) ⟹ (i): if every outer
strongly approximately inner `Z_3`-action on `O_2` leaves a Cartan subalgebra
invariant, then every separable nuclear algebra KK-equivalent to its
`M_(3^∞)`-stabilization satisfies the UCT.  In particular `A ⊗ M_(3^∞)` does for
every separable nuclear `A`.  This is the twin of
`uct-two-half-via-z2-actions-on-o2`.
