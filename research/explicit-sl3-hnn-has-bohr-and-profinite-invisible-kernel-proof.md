In every finite-dimensional representation, the image of `<a,b>` equals the
image of `SL_3(Z)`.  The stable letter therefore centralizes the entire
lattice image, giving unique factorization through `SL_3(Z) x Z`.  The same
argument for finite targets identifies profinite completions; the universal
property for compact unitary images identifies Bohr compactifications.
Britton supplies the explicit nontrivial kernel element `[t,r]`.
