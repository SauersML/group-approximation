Direct proof, given in the target: an element of order `p` in the image must move
a subspace of dimension at least `p^(k−1)(p−1)/[K:Q]` for every `k`, because it
is a `p^(k−1)`-th power of an element of order `p^k`. A finitary matrix moves only
a finite-dimensional subspace.
