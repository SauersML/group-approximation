Let `mu` be an invariant random subgroup of a countable group `G`.  For
independent `K_1,K_2,...` of law `mu`, let `mu_n` be the law of

```text
K^(n)=K_1 intersection ... intersection K_n.
```

Put

```text
H={g in G: Prob(g in K_1)=1}.
```

Then `K^(n)` decreases almost surely to `H`, and hence

```text
mu_n -> delta_H.                                       (IIR1)
```

If for one `beta>0` and every `n` there are probability measures `nu_n`
with

```text
mu_n=beta_n mu+(1-beta_n)nu_n,   beta_n>=beta,          (IIR2)
```

then `mu=delta_H`.  Thus the most literal self-similar IRS proposal--keep a
fixed-weight copy of the original IRS while repeated intersections polarize
its membership character--cannot preserve any genuinely random IRS.
