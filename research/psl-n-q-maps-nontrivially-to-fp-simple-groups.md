**OPEN.** For infinitely many `n >= 2` there are a finitely presented simple
group `S` and a nontrivial homomorphism `PSL_n(Q) -> S`. It is enough to map
`PSL_n(Q)` nontrivially into any group that embeds in a finitely presented
simple group, and for odd `n` one may use `SL_n(Q) = PSL_n(Q)`.

By `gl-n-q-root-needs-only-nontrivial-psl-maps` this is equivalent to
`gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group`; route `gl-n-q-root-via-nontrivial-psl-maps`
records the direction into the root. It is the form to aim a construction at
when faithfulness is the awkward part, for example after passing to a quotient.
