A single-site configuration must change the output somewhere, and
`f(e_m) - f(0)` is the linear coefficient `a_m`; relabeling invariance pairs
`a_m` with `a_(hm)`. An injective automaton permutes the constant configurations,
so `f(1,...,1) - f(0,...,0)`, the parity of the number of nonconstant
monomials, is `1`. Relabeling by `h` pairs off the non-invariant monomials and
fixes no variable. Section 3 of the artifact.
