**ESTABLISHED COPRIME-TRACE LEMMA.**  Let `Gamma` contain an injective
finite `2`-subgroup `H`, let `chi` be a one-dimensional character of `H`,
and let `z in Gamma` have exact odd prime order `p`.  Put

```text
e_chi=|H|^(-1) sum_(h in H) conjugate(chi(h)) h,
P_(z,1)=p^(-1) sum_(k=0)^(p-1) z^k,
S_z=1-P_(z,1).                                        (CRI1)
```

Then the canonical group trace satisfies

```text
tau(e_chi P_(z,1))=p^(-1)tau(e_chi),
tau(e_chi S_z)=(1-p^(-1))tau(e_chi).                  (CRI2)
```

No commutation or normalization relation between `H` and `<z>` is needed.
Indeed, a term `h z^k` contributes to the canonical trace only when
`h=z^(-k)`.  But

```text
H intersect <z>={1}                                  (CRI3)
```

by coprimality, so only `(h,k)=(1,0)` contributes.  Since
`tau(e_chi)=1/|H|`, this proves `(CRI2)`.  Although `e_chi` and `S_z` need
not commute, positivity is not an issue:
`tau(e_chi S_z)=tau(e_chi S_z e_chi)>=0`.

The statement is dimension-independently robust along canonical
normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt microstates.  Exactify the fixed finite
`H`-packet and the fixed cyclic `p`-packet, form the two finite Fourier
polynomials in `(CRI1)`, and use the canonical moment conditions for the
finite list

```text
{h z^k : h in H, 0<=k<p}.                             (CRI4)
```

Every nonidentity moment tends to zero, while fixed-packet correction and
finite-word telescoping contribute `O(sqrt(E_pres))`.  Consequently

```text
tr_d(e_chi(U) S_z(U))
 =(1-p^(-1))tr_d(e_chi(U))+o(1),                      (CRI5)
```

with constants independent of matrix dimension.

This applies verbatim when a source is a **literal prefix word carrier**.
If commuting involutions `B_0,...,B_(n-1)` are independent from an injected
BCS context packet and

```text
Q_n=product_(j<n)(1+B_j)/2,
E=Q_n Q_- P_(c,a),                                   (CRI6)
```

then `E` is one character idempotent of the finite two-group generated by
the prefix signs, `J`, and the context involutions.  Hence `(CRI2)` gives
the payload-to-root estimate with the sharp constant `1-1/p`, provided the
named root has exact order `p`.

The restriction to literal character carriers is load-bearing.  If `Q` is
instead an analytically chosen reducing hull, a decoded coefficient corner,
or an arbitrary projection commuting with the BCS tuple, then `QP_(c,a)`
need not be a fixed group-algebra Fourier polynomial.  Canonical moments do
not control its correlation with `P_(z,1)`; it can in principle concentrate
inside the trivial root sector.  Thus `(CRI5)` closes root detection at the
initial Pauli/BCS Reynolds seed and at every genuinely literal finite prefix
atom, but it does not prove the adaptive-carrier inequality in
`bcs-atom-detects-its-e5-central-root`.

There is also no first-hit amplification hidden in `(CRI6)`.  Independent
positive prefixes have canonical mass `2^(-n)`, and
`companion-free-prefix-return-has-exponential-regular-capacity` proves that
lossless algebraic return through them cannot reach the required
supercritical threshold.  The coprime calculation removes the local
payload-to-root seam on that architecture; the remaining obstruction is
the matrix-only carrier incidence/capacity seam.
