If no dimension-free transfer modulus existed, the established reduction
would produce a tracial matrix ultraproduct with exact unitaries `X,Y` and a
positive-density projection `P` satisfying `(MSX1)` but not `(MSX2)`.  The
square-root exclusion forbids precisely that configuration.  Sequential
compactness then gives the required modulus.
