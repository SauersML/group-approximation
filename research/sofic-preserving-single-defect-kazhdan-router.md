Let `R` be the torsion-free finitely presented arithmetic free-product source
and `1!=sigma in R` the Britton-certified compression defect used in
`defect-saturation-from-arithmetic-source`.  Construct a surjection

```text
q:R->>Q
```

such that

```text
Q is nontrivial, finitely presented, torsion-free, sofic and property (T),
Q = <<q(sigma)>>^Q.                                    (SPR1)
```

Equivalently, strengthen the existing single-element Hull router only at its
positive approximation output.  Residual finiteness is impossible here:
the downstream normal-Kazhdan theorem makes `Rad_MF(Q)=Q`, whereas every
nontrivial residually finite group is MF.  The required output must therefore
be genuinely sofic but non-MF.

## Attempts

1. **Ordinary Hull--Osin routing.**  It gives every algebraic clause in
   `(SPR1)` but has no finite-model clause.  Soficity cannot be pushed through
   the resulting quotient map.
2. **Finite-quotient routing.**  Requiring the marked relators to survive in
   a cofinal family of finite quotients would make `Q` residually finite and
   contradict the forced full MF radical, so exact finite quotients are the
   wrong model.
3. **Finite-window permutation routing.**  The viable target is weaker and
   genuinely sofic: for each finite word window and tolerance, choose the
   long Hull relators together with a finite permutation model in which the
   routed generator identities hold off a small exceptional set, while the
   protected defect moves a fixed fraction of points.  A diagonal choice
   must preserve torsion-freeness and normal generation at the exact group
   level.  No theorem in the repository currently synchronizes these two
   choices; that synchronization is the precise frontier.
4. **Host-geometry soficity (embed the output in a known geometric group).**
   Dead at the survival step (swarm-0917, `defect-survival-embeds-ascending-affine-core`).
   A torsion-free `Q` with `q(sigma) != 1` is faithful on
   `(Gamma(3) ltimes Z[1/2]^3) x| Z` and on `P x <c>`. So `Q` contains the
   non-finitely-generated solvable group `Z[1/2]^3` and `BS(1,2)`. By the Solvable
   Subgroup Theorem it lies in no CAT(0)-cocompact group, so it is not in a
   Titz--Witzel or Kac--Moody lattice or in a cocompactly cubulated group.
   It lies in no hyperbolic group either.  The invariant is the forced
   non-finitely-generated abelian subgroup.
5. **Window-interleaved routing, as a finitely presented target.**  Dead by a quantifier
   shift (swarm-0917).  Suppose the relators are chosen stage by stage along with the window
   models, with kernels `N_1 <= N_2 <= ...`.  Then `N = union N_k` is the kernel of a finitely
   presented `Q` only if it is finitely normally generated.  Its generators then lie in one
   `N_(k_0)`, so `N = N_(k_0)`.  Every window after `k_0` must therefore model one fixed
   one-shot quotient.  Attempt 3's synchronization is then exactly the soficity of a single
   admissible Hull quotient, which is `arithmetic-defect-saturated-kazhdan-group-is-sofic`
   for that choice of relators, and interleaving gives no leverage.  If the chain does not
   stabilize, the limit `R/union N_k` stays torsion-free, because `x^r in N_k` gives
   `x in N_k`.  From a finite stage on it is Kazhdan and normally generated by the defect,
   but it is not finitely presented.  So it leaves `(SPR1)` and can only feed a
   mapping-torus route (`fp-mapping-torus-iff-finite-automorphic-presentation`).
   The remaining positive content is a *uniform-in-window* sofic approximation
   theorem for one fixed small-cancellation quotient.
6. **Rigidifying routers (compressor-generated Kazhdan outputs).** Dead at the soficity
   clause (swarm-0917 wave 5, `sofic-defect-cores-have-non-kazhdan-compressor-hull`).
   Let `Gamma = q(P)`, and let `Hull_Q(Gamma)` be the subgroup generated by
   `{x : x Gamma x^-1 <= Gamma}`. `Gamma` is infranormal in its hull and
   `q(E) <= Hull_Q(Gamma)`. So `sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects` gives the
   following for every `(SPR1)` output:
   - `Gamma` is not infranormal in `Q`;
   - `Hull_Q(Gamma)` and `q(E)` are proper non-Kazhdan subgroups;
   - some generator of `Q` (a free letter, and some partner image) does not compress `Gamma`.

   This kills every design that makes the core infranormal inside a Kazhdan subgroup
   containing `q(t)`: Kazhdanizing `E` itself, or sending free letters into `C_Q(q(P))`
   or `N_Q(q(P))`. The invariant is property (T) of the compressor hull. It is not a
   refutation, because Hull's construction never forces the partner generators to compress
   `q(P)`. The surviving positive target now carries a necessary non-rigidity clause: the
   compressor hull of the core must be a proper non-Kazhdan subgroup of the Kazhdan group
   `Q`, and the Kazhdan generators of `Q` must lie outside it.
7. **Compression-exact models and cover lifts (swarm-0917, stability-approximation).** Dead at
   the finite-image step (`compression-exact-models-kill-defect-generated-groups`). Take any
   `(SPR1)` output and any approximation over a generating set that contains the core letters
   `p_i, t, c` of `P x <c>` and the HNN letter. Suppose that at infinitely many scales the
   compressor `rho(t)` maps the finite group generated by the `rho(p_i)` into itself, and either
   `rho(c)` centralizes those generators exactly, or `rho` is an exact `P`-action and `rho(c)`
   centralizes only approximately (`P = Gamma(3) ltimes Z^3` is Kazhdan).
   - *The kill.* By cardinality the inclusion is an equality. So `rho(sigma) = 1` exactly,
     and `Q = <<q(sigma)>>` forces every generator to act almost trivially. The linear and
     unitary versions work the same way, by dimension.
   - *What dies.*
     - sofic approximations pulled back from finite actions of `R`, or of any cover in which
       the core relations hold, whether residually finite or not (`delta_(ker q)` is never
       co-sofic, since `sigma` lies in every finite residual);
     - the genuine partner-lift route;
     - congruence models with `t` acting as the dilation mod odd `m`;
     - every model in which `t` acts by an automorphism of a finite image of `P`;
     - hyperlinear models pulled back from finite-dimensional representations of covers.
   - *Calibration.* Property (T) together with the absence of finite quotients is not enough
     to kill cover lifts: the LEF simple Kazhdan group has co-sofic kernels in a finitely
     presented Kazhdan cover. The defect normal generation in `(SPR1)` is what kills them.
   - *What survives.* The exceptional set of Attempt 3 must meet the compression step at every
     scale: the permutation `rho(t)` must fail to normalize the permutation group generated by
     the `P`-letters. Soficity of `E` shows such models exist for the source.
   - *Status.* This is a class kill, not a refutation, and the hole stays OPEN.
   - *Reduction recorded en route.* `(SPR1)` implies `(ADS1)` for the same quotient. Conversely,
     `arithmetic-defect-saturated-sofic-via-sofic-router` gives the other direction up to the
     generation count. So this router carries essentially all the difficulty of its parent,
     and is at least as hard as Alekseev--Thom Open Problem 6.1
     (`fp-sofic-kazhdan-group-not-residually-finite`).
