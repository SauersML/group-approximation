**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `Gamma` be finitely generated with finite symmetric generating set `S`, and let
`c_inv(S)` be half the infimum of the expected root degree of `Gamma`-invariant random connected spanning subgraphs of
`Cay(Gamma, S)`.

1. **Kazhdan groups (Hutchcroft–Pete).** If `Gamma` is Kazhdan, then `c_inv(S) = 1` for every `S`. Their
   construction is not a factor of iid.
2. **Amenable groups (Beringer–Pete–Timár).** If `Gamma` is amenable, then `c_inv(S) = 1` for every `S`.
3. **Open question (Beringer–Pete–Timár).** It is recorded as not known in general whether `c_inv(S)` equals
   `cost(Gamma)`.
