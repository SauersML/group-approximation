Let `(A_n)_(n>=1)` be unital C\*-algebras such that

1. `1_(A_n)` is finite;
2. there is a unital embedding `M_(2^n) -> A_n`; and
3. the unit of `M_2(A_n)` is infinite.

Then

```text
Q = (prod_n A_n)/(directSum_n A_n)
```

has finite unit, admits a unital embedding of the CAR algebra, and has
infinite two-by-two amplification.

Thus the separate finite-stage Rordam-type constructions can be assembled
without tensor stabilization.  The unresolved step toward Problem LXVI in
this route is to retain these three properties while forcing simplicity.
