**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** For every natural number `d` there is `f = f(d)` such that, in every
finite group `G` generated by `d` elements, every element of the derived subgroup `[G, G]` is a product of
at most `f` commutators.

This is the simple-commutator case `w = [x_1, x_2]` of Theorem 1.2 of Nikolov--Segal, *On finitely
generated profinite groups, I: strong completeness and uniform bounds* (arXiv:math/0604399v1; published
in Ann. of Math. 165 (2007)). A `w`-value there is `w(g_1, g_2)^(+-1)`, and the inverse of a commutator
is a commutator.
