distinct_from:
  overgroup-room-does-not-supply-a-root: that says a faithful unitary representation with a root is a restatement of the conjecture at one group; this computes exactly which coefficient traces the conjecture supplies roots for, namely the closed convex hull of quotient indicators, and so separates the tracial root theorem from the conjecture.
  kl-failure-localizes-to-the-coefficient-subgroup: that reduces the conjecture to the coefficient subgroup; this is a statement about traces on a fixed coefficient group.

**Setting.**  A *character* of a group `H` is a function `psi : H -> C` that
is positive definite, constant on conjugacy classes, and has `psi(1) = 1`.
For a normal subgroup `K` of `Gamma` put `1_K(g) = 1` if `g in K` and `0`
otherwise.

**Statement.**  Let `Gamma` be a countable group and `w in Gamma * <t>` with
`deg_t(w) = m != 0`.  For `K` normal in `Gamma` let `w_K` be the image of
`w` in `(Gamma/K) * <t>`.  Suppose `Gamma/K -> (Gamma/K)_(w_K)` is injective
for every normal subgroup `K`.  Let `chi` be a character of `Gamma` in the
closure, for pointwise convergence, of the convex hull of `{ 1_K }`.  Then:

1. `chi` is the restriction of a character `psi` of `Gamma_w`;
2. equivalently, the tracial von Neumann algebra `L_chi(Gamma)` generated by
   the GNS representation of `chi` solves `w`, with coefficients `pi_chi(g)`,
   in a trace-preserving extension. This is the conclusion of
   `tracial-algebras-solve-nonsingular-equations-in-extensions` for these
   coefficients.

**Converse at the regular trace.**  If the regular character `1_{1}` of
`Gamma` extends to a character `psi` of `Gamma_w`, then
`Gamma -> Gamma_w` is injective. Indeed, if `g != 1` maps to `1`, then
`psi(g) = psi(1) = 1`, but `1_{1}(g) = 0`.

**Consequence.**  `kervaire-laudenbach-nonsingular-conjecture` holds if and only
if, for every countable `Gamma` and every nonsingular `w`, every character in
the closed convex hull of the quotient indicators of `Gamma` extends to
`Gamma_w`.
- The forward direction is the statement, applied to all quotients.
- The reverse direction is the converse at `K = 1`, together with
  `kl-failure-localizes-to-the-coefficient-subgroup` for uncountable groups.

All these characters are real and nonnegative. So the tracial root theorem is
a priori stronger than the conjecture only at characters outside this hull,
and by `connes-embeddable-tracial-algebras-solve-nonsingular-equations` only
at those whose GNS algebra is not Connes-embeddable.

**Proof.**  Route `kl-quotient-indicator-trace-extension-proof`. It is
elementary, and no novelty is claimed.
