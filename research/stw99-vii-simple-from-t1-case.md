If `A` is simple, separable, nuclear and stably finite, `Prim(A)` is a single
point, which is `T_1`.  So `bk-t1-primitive-spectrum-case` makes `A`
quasidiagonal.  Together with `bk-t1-spectrum-from-simple-case` this makes the
simple case of Problem VII and its `T_1` case equivalent.
