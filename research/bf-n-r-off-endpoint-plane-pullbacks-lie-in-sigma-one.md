For `n >= 3`, `r >= 1` and `psi` in `Hom(F_{n,r}, R)` not in the span of the
endpoint characters `chi_0, chi_1`, `[psi o pi]` lies in `Sigma^1(bF_{n,r})`.
For `n = 2` the span is everything, so the claim is vacuous.

The method is an extension lemma: `[psi o pi]` is in `Sigma^1` whenever `[psi]` is
in `Sigma^1(F_{n,r})` and the kernel is generated by `psi`-nonnegative
conjugates of finitely many elements. The winding generators centralize elements
supported in their gaps, and off the endpoint plane `psi` is unbounded on those.
