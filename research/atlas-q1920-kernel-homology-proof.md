Let `N` be the normal closure of `V` in the reduced universal seam `P`.
The forced bridge gives the nonidentity element `v v^c=d d^c` in the normal
Klein four of the collision `S4`.  Normality of `N` therefore puts the
labeled collision involution `c` in `N`; the relation `<c,z>=S3` then puts
`z` in `N`.  Conversely, the canonical map which kills `V,c,z` has image
`Q/V=A5`, and its kernel is normally generated by those elements.  Hence it
has kernel exactly `N`.

The marked local presentation retracts from `P` by sending `c,z` to one.
The certified split structure `Q=V semidirect A5` makes its `A5` complement
an embedded complement to `N`, proving `P=N semidirect A5` for both
markings.

The GAP verifier constructs the exact six-generator presentation and the
canonical epimorphism to `A5`, checks that its image has order sixty, and
computes the integral invariants of its kernel:

```text
N_ab = C2^9.
```

It independently forms the cellular chain complex of the connected
sixty-sheeted presentation cover over `F2`.  There are `6*60` one-cells and
sixty vertices, so `dim ker(d1)=360-60+1=301`.  Exact row reduction gives
`rank im(d2)=292`, hence `dim H1=9`, agreeing with the integral computation.

Appending the explicit lift rows before row reduction gives dimensions five
for both `V` and `V^c`, five for their join, zero for the image of `z`, and
four for the `A5`-orbit span of `c`; the latter has zero intersection with
`V`.  These ranks prove the direct-sum decomposition in the claim.  The
stdlib audit binds the GAP source to the archived output by SHA-256 and
recomputes every dimension identity for both markings.
