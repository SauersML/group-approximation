Let D be separable, simple, stably finite, pure, and real rank zero.
For every second-countable locally compact Hausdorff space X, the
actual first-factor map induces an isomorphism

```text
Cu(C_0(X,D)) -> Cu(C_0(X,D tensor Z)).
```

There is no base-dimension, nuclearity, K1-vanishing, or coefficient
Z-stability assumption. These fields are pure by the known purity
theorem and ideal permanence.

The proof constructs, over finite-dimensional compact bases, a source
class between each target positive cutoff and its parent class. It
retains the compact projection-bundle data at both ends of this
sandwich while gluing overlapping ranges inside a largest active
hereditary module. Increasing suprema give surjectivity; Cu continuity
and ideal permanence give all the stated bases.

The theorem is internally reviewed. The general STW LXXVI problem
for arbitrary pure algebras remains open.
