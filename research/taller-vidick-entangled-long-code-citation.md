**Established by citation.**  Aviv Taller and Thomas Vidick,
*Approximating the quantum value of an LCS game is RE-hard*,
arXiv:2507.22444v2 (2026).

Proposition 4.1 is `(TV2)`, Lemma 4.2 is `(TV3)`, and Theorem 5.2 with its
proof gives `(TV1)` and answer length three.  Remark 5.3 passes from the
synchronous statement to unrestricted quantum value because the output is a
projection game.  The introduction states both the generic BCS-algebra to
LCS-algebra obstruction and the consequence that perfect completeness would
imply a nonhyperlinear group.
