For `n ≥ 2`, `BS(1,n) = <a, t | t a t^{-1} = a^n>` is isomorphic to no subgroup of
`PL_+(I)`, hence to no subgroup of Thompson's group `F`. `BS(1,n)` is bi-orderable
(`Z[1/n] ⋊ Z`, with `t` acting by multiplication by `n`) and solvable.
