ESTABLISHED (definition and formal-interface audit; literature statement checked
against arXiv:2603.24502v5).

The formal compiler asks for

```text
IsOperatorMF (Carrier input) -> IsOperatorMF (Carrier output).
```

Here `IsOperatorMF` is the bare group-theoretic MF predicate: a faithful
homomorphism into the unitary group of a cofinite norm-matrix corona.  It does
not include normalized-trace convergence or convergence to reduced group
C-star norms.

By contrast, the property called `MF` in Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--
Manzoor--Patchell has all three approximation clauses:

1. asymptotic multiplicativity in operator norm;
2. normalized traces converging to the regular character; and
3. operator norms of group-ring polynomials converging to their reduced
   group-C-star norms.

The closest existing formal predicate is therefore `IsTraceRegularMF`, not
`IsOperatorMF`.  The repository proves only the downgrade

```text
IsTraceRegularMF G -> IsOperatorMF G
```

for countable `G`; it has no reverse implication.  The representation
variants now also expose the honest structural chain

```text
IsPPF -> IsPFF -> IsTracePMF -> IsTraceRegularMF -> IsOperatorMF.
```

The new `IsTracePMF -> IsTraceRegularMF` step merely forgets exact
multiplicativity while retaining the same trace and reduced-norm limits.  It
does not construct any approximation.  In particular, the current formal
positive switch branch proves no `IsTracePMF`, `IsPFF`, or
`IsTraceRegularMF` statement for its base or for any compiler stage.

This last assertion is a complete declaration-name inventory, not an
inference from filenames.  Searching all Lean sources for the three
predicates and their implication method names yields only:

* the definitions in `Sofic/MFDefinitions.lean` and
  `Sofic/MFRepresentationVariants.lean`;
* eliminations from trace-regular MF to weaker predicates;
* the structural implications `IsPPF.isPFF`, `IsPFF.isTracePMF`, and
  `IsTracePMF.isTraceRegularMF`; and
* negative obstruction fields and contrapositives.

There is no theorem with a concrete group as the conclusion of
`IsTraceRegularMF`, `IsTracePMF`, or `IsPFF`, and none of these predicates
occurs in the Higman compiler files.  Thus the formal implication chain has
no positive source to consume on the compiler branch.

## Consequence for the injected compiler tower

Even before the named Nielsen edges, `InjectedCompilerTower.FreeLetterTower`
starts from `ConjugatedDetector.FreeLetterExtension K`, namely the free
product of `K` with the infinite cyclic group generated by `q`.  There is no
formal forward free-product permanence theorem for `IsOperatorMF` in the
repository.

After that initial free-letter edge, the unresolved positive obligations are
strictly stronger than the earlier operator-MF list:

1. supply trace-regular MF for the input and preserve it through the `q`
   free-letter edge;
2. preserve trace-regular MF through every noncentral Nielsen `tau_j` HNN
   edge;
3. preserve trace-regular MF through the `d` free-subgroup HNN edge;
4. preserve trace-regular MF through the `sigma` free-subgroup HNN edge; and
5. prove that the transported detector subgroup is separable in the
   completed inner tower.

Only after 1--5 does GKMP Theorem 1.1 apply to the outer central detector,
with its added factor `Z`.  Its conclusion then downgrades to formal
`IsOperatorMF`.  None of the `ExplicitFreeEdge.Data` fields supplies matrix
models, joint strong convergence, conditional expectations, or profinite
separability: the data record only two injective free-group evaluations and
the induced HNN edge isomorphism.

This route also cannot prove the compiler's displayed `MFPermanence` from its
present bare `IsOperatorMF` premise.  It would require either strengthening
that premise to trace-regular MF (and discharging all stagewise obligations)
or a different theorem that genuinely starts from bare operator MF.

## The central-HNN literature does not remove the mismatch

There is no Lean theorem in this repository that directly preserves
`IsOperatorMF` under the outer central HNN extension.  The node
[[regular-mf-central-hnn-closure]] is a research argument with the additional
hypothesis of a faithful MF-algebra realization whose trace is the regular
character.  Bare `IsOperatorMF` does not provide that datum.

Shulman's central-HNN theorem (arXiv:2603.13564v2, Theorem 25) is a theorem
about the universal C-star HNN extension of an MF C-star algebra.  Applying it
to the abstract group still requires a faithful group embedding into that
C-star HNN algebra; the regular-character/Britton argument is exactly the
extra bridge used by the research node above.  No such bridge is formalized
for the compiler tower.

Nor do the two later HNN papers give bare-MF permanence.  arXiv:2604.06982
proves selflessness statements for reduced C-star HNN extensions, not MF
closure.  arXiv:2604.14106 transports strong convergence only from already
jointly compatible base, subgroup-expectation, and edge-isomorphism data.
Those inputs do not follow from `IsOperatorMF` or `ExplicitFreeEdge.Data`.

Thus no generic HNN MF permanence claim is justified edge by edge, and GKMP
is useful only after a still-missing trace-regular inner-tower construction
and transported-subgroup separability proof.
