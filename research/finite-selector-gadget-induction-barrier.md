title: Finite group gadgets cannot prune characters of a commuting marked selector subgroup
---

Every character of a finite abelian selector subgroup compatible with the
central mark occurs inside a marked representation of any finite overgroup.
Hence finite group relations cannot retain selected joint characters while
deleting other mark-compatible ones; spectral pruning needs a corner/trace
condition or must leave the finite commuting-subgroup setting.
