Section 1 of the linked note. The finite-cost normal subgroup theorem gives, for every `ε > 0`, a free action of cost below `1 + ε`. A descending normal chain with trivial intersection is Farber, so Abert--Nikolov Theorem 1 makes the profinite boundary action free of cost `1 + RG`. Taking `ε < RG` separates the two actions. This is dependency bookkeeping for a written deduction.
