- Item 4:

  - `[[T]]'` embeds by `topological-full-group-embeds-in-subshift-elementary-group`.
- Item 5: `subshift-elementary-groups-are-not-uniformly-simple`.
- Item 6: `subshift-elementary-group-reduced-cstar-has-unique-trace`.
