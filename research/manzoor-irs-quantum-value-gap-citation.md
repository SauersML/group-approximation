**Established by citation.**  Aareyan Manzoor, *There is an equivalence
relation whose von Neumann algebra is not Connes embeddable*,
arXiv:2502.06697v2, Theorem 3.15, proves `(MIG1)`.  The decreasing hierarchy is
Theorem 3.6 and the TailoredMIP input is Theorem 3.14.

The paper's diagonalization dovetails the upper IRS hierarchy with increasing
finite-dimensional quantum lower bounds.  A halting machine yields a perfect
finite permutation strategy, while a nonhalting machine yields quantum value
below `1/2`.  Universal absence of an IRS/quantum gap would therefore make one
of the two searches halt correctly on every input, contradicting undecidability.
