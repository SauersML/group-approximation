Culf--Mastel, arXiv:2410.21223v2, Definition 4.11 identifies value-one
instances with zero infimal finite-dimensional tracial defect and notes that
finite-dimensional traces may equivalently be replaced by CE traces.
Theorem 4.14 and Corollary 4.16 give a constant `s<1` and an RE-complete
perfect-versus-`s` promise family for the assignment algebra of 3-colouring,
or more generally the language of all 2-CSPs over a fixed alphabet containing
the empty constraint.  Corollary 4.18 expands the succinct output to a finite
instance by a total exponential-time reduction while retaining a constant
gap.  Lemma 6.3 and Corollary 6.4 record oracularizability of the perfect
completeness strategy.  Taking a tracial matrix ultraproduct of a
zero-defect sequence makes completeness exact.
