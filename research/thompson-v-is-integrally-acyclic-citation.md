Literature import. M. Szymik and N. Wahl, "The homology of the Higman–Thompson
groups", Invent. Math. 216 (2019), 445–518, arXiv:1411.5035. The abstract,
read verbatim from arXiv on 2026-09-13, says: "We prove that Thompson's group
$V$ is acyclic, answering a 1992 question of Brown in the positive. More
generally, we identify the homology of the Higman-Thompson groups $V_{n,r}$
with the homology of the zeroth component of the infinite loop space of the
mod $n-1$ Moore spectrum."

The theorem number inside the paper was not checked; the statement above is
the authors' own abstract.
