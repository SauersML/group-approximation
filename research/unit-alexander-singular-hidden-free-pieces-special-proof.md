## 1. Part (A): the kernel is perfect

`N` is generated by the `a_j`, `j in Z`, subject to the shifted relators `W_j`, where `W_j` is
`W_0` with every index raised by `j`. Abelianizing gives
`H_1(N; Z) = Z[t^(+-1)] / (Delta)`, as a module over `Z[t^(+-1)]` with `t` acting by the shift. If
`Delta = +-t^k`, this is `0`, so `N` is perfect.

The exponent sum of `a` in `w` is `sum eps_i = Delta(1) = +-1`, and the exponent sum of `t` is `0`.
So `G^ab = Z^2 / <(+-1, 0)> = Z`, generated by the image of `t`. The map `G -> G/N = Z` factors
through `G/G'`, and a surjection `Z -> Z` is an isomorphism. So `G' = N`. Since `N` is perfect,
`G'' = N' = N`, and by induction `G^(k) = N` for all `k >= 1`. Hence
`G^(omega) = N`, which contains `B_0`, since every `a_i` lies in `N`.

- A solvable quotient `Q` of derived length `d` kills `G^(d) = N`, and so it kills `B_0`.
- If `rho(N)` is residually solvable, then `rho(N)` is perfect and
  `intersect_k rho(N)^(k) = rho(N)`, so `rho(N) = 1`.
- If a finite quotient `Q` of `G` is nontrivial on `B_0`, then the image of `N` is a nontrivial
  perfect subgroup, so `Q` is not solvable.

This is obstruction (1) in its strongest form. No solvable, residually solvable or residually-`p`
alphabet in the (AA) or (AA_G) dictionary can separate any nontrivial element of `B_0` from `1`.

## 2. Part (B): enumeration and minimality

`gen_singular.py LMAX NMAX` enumerates cyclic syllable sequences `(h_k, e_k)` with these
properties:
- heights `0..NMAX`, with both `0` and `NMAX` used and no two consecutive heights equal;
- exponents `1 <= |e| <= 3`;
- exactly one level sum nonzero, and equal to `+-1` (a unit `Delta`);
- both extremes unpeelable in the census sense (`census.peelable` false);
- at least one extreme with level sum zero.

It reduces modulo reflection `h -> NMAX-h`, reversal with inversion, sign change and rotation. It
then runs the landed `census.cert_word` with budget 4000. The runs `19 1`, `19 2` and `19 3` give
182 candidates, all CERT; 52 candidates, 4 CERT and 48 OPEN; and 0 candidates.

**Completeness at length at most 19.** Take a singular unpeelable extreme with level sum 0. It has
at least 3 syllables, so at least 4 letters. The other extreme is unpeelable, so it has at least 2
syllables. If its sum is `+-1`, those syllables have unequal absolute exponents and use at least 3
letters. If it is singular too, it uses at least 4.

As a closed walk in the levels, the word visits level 0 in at least 3 separate stops and level `n`
in at least 2. That needs at least `2n + 6` letters `t^(+-1)`. For `n >= 3` the length is at least
`12 + 7 = 19`. At `n = 3`, the walk `0 -> 3 -> 2 -> 3 -> 0` stops at level 2 with a nonzero syllable
there. That level has sum 0, so it needs a second syllable, and the length exceeds 19; the `n = 3`
run confirms that there are none. For `n >= 4` the length is at least `14 + 7 = 21`.

A syllable with `|e| >= 4` at a level of sum 0 forces at least 8 letters there. At the level of sum
`+-1` it forces at least 7. Either way the length is at least `10 + 11 > 19`. So the bound
`|e| <= 3` loses nothing at length at most 19. At length at most 16 the class is empty, consistent
with the landed length-16 residue having no singular extreme.

## 3. Part (C): transporting the certificate to a hidden free basis

**Tracked Whitehead minimisation (`linfree.py`).** Whitehead minimisation of the cyclic word
`W_0 in F_3 = F(a_0,a_1,a_2)` applies Whitehead automorphisms `(a, S)` until no length drop is
possible. It records the composite automorphism `phi` by the images `phi(a_i)`, reduced freely but
not cyclically. For the 16 relators the minimum has length 1: `phi(W_0)` is conjugate to `c^(+-1)`
for a basis letter `c`. The script checks this again by substitution (`assert` in `run`).

Let `pi : F_3 -> F_2` delete `c`. Then `phi` induces
`B_0 = F_3/<<W_0>> -> F_3/<<c>> = F_2`, an isomorphism with `a_i -> pi(phi(a_i))`. Write
`s = t^(-1)`. Then

    G = < K, s | s a s^(-1) = psi(a), a in A >,  K = F_2,
    A = < pi phi(a_0), pi phi(a_1) >,  B = < pi phi(a_1), pi phi(a_2) >,
    psi(pi phi(a_0)) = pi phi(a_1),  psi(pi phi(a_1)) = pi phi(a_2).

This is the input format of `lintongraph.certify(2, Agens, Bgens)`, whose convention is
`t a t^(-1) = psi(a)`. `certify` recomputes (C0): `A` and `B` are free of rank 2 on the displayed
generators, as the Freiheitssatz predicts.

**The landed soundness proof applies verbatim.** [[linton-graph-certificate-soundness-proof]] uses
only the following facts:
- Step 1: `K` is free; `A` and `B` are finitely generated of rank 2, hence strongly inert
  ([[rank-two-free-subgroups-are-strongly-inert]]); and `W_0` is primitive, so the Magnus splitting
  is a one-relator hierarchy of length one ending in the free group `K`.
- Steps 2–6: statements about the triple `(K, A, B, psi)`, which are invariant under isomorphism
  of triples.

The hypothesis "`a_1` occurs once" enters only as one way to see that `W_0` is primitive. Here
primitivity comes from `phi`. So (C0)–(C3) passing implies that `G` has no Baumslag–Solitar
subgroup and that the length-one hierarchy is quasi-convex. By Linton's Theorems 6.14 and 7.1, as
imported in [[linton-z-stable-one-relator-hierarchy-theorem]], `G` is hyperbolic and virtually
special.

All 16 verdicts are `PASS`, with `hloops = tloops = 0` and no alternating cycle (`linfree19.out`).
None is `PASS_HLOOP`.

**Consequences.**
- A virtually compact special group is linear over `Z` (Wise, as used in
  [[quasiconvex-hierarchy-piece-nonsingular-extremes-sofic-proof]]). So `G` is residually finite,
  hence sofic.
- In the quasi-convex hierarchy, `K = B_0` is quasi-convex in `G`. `A = F_-` and `B = F_+` are
  finitely generated subgroups of the free group `K`, hence quasi-convex in `K`, hence in `G`.
- By Haglund–Wise separability of quasi-convex subgroups, again as in
  [[quasiconvex-hierarchy-piece-nonsingular-extremes-sofic-proof]], `F_-` and `F_+` are closed in
  the profinite topology of `G`.
- So the finite quotients of `G` separate finite subsets of `B_0 \ F_-` from `F_-`, likewise for
  `F_+`, and finite subsets of `B_0 \ {1}` from `1`. This is the witness family of the (AA_G)
  dictionary.
- By Section 1, each of these witnesses restricts to a non-solvable finite group.
- A relation `g^(-1) c g = c^k` with `|k| >= 2` and `c != 1` would give a subgroup `BS(1,k)`. So
  obstruction (2) misses.

**Calibration (`calib16.py`).**
- The tracked route was re-run on every `VERDICT` line of the landed `lg_words16.out`, using the
  same Magnus rewriting as `lintongraph.py words`.
- All 70 landed `PASS` verdicts come back `PASS`. The verdict is basis independent, as it should be.
- Of the 141 landed `NONFREE` lines:
  - 105 are genuinely non-free;
  - 34 are hidden free and `PASS`;
  - 1 is `PASS_HLOOP`, which is not certified;
  - 1 has an alternating cycle in the over-approximating graph, which is not certified either way.
  These groups were already residually finite by virtual fibring, so nothing conflicts.

## 4. Part (D): the certificate one level down

`mmcert.py` takes a letter `s in {x,y,z}` of exponent sum 0 in `W_0` and rewrites `W_0` in the
letters `u_i = s^(-i) u s^i`, for `u` in the other two letters. Normalizing the minimum level to 0,
the rewritten relator is `R`. Standard Magnus–Moldavanskii gives `B_0 = V *_psi` with

    V = < u_i : m_u <= i <= M_u | R >,  A = < u_i : i < M_u >,  B = < u_i : i > m_u >,
    psi(u_i) = u_(i+1),

and this is the first step of a one-relator hierarchy of the presentation complex of `B_0`.

If `R` is primitive, `V` is free, and the tracked basis of Section 3 transports `(V, A, B, psi)` to
a free group of rank `r - 1`. When `A` and `B` have rank 1 or 2, they are strongly inert, and the
argument of Section 3 goes through with `K = V`: Steps 2–6 of the soundness proof never use the rank
of `K`.

A `PASS` therefore gives two things:
- the hierarchy of the complex of `B_0` is Z-stable;
- `B_0` contains no Baumslag–Solitar subgroup.

That is hypothesis (QC) of [[quasiconvex-hierarchy-piece-nonsingular-extremes-sofic]]. Its proof
then gives that `B_0` is hyperbolic and virtually special and that `F_-` and `F_+` are separable.
24 of the 32 non-free pieces `PASS` (`mmcert19.out`). All of them have `eps_0 = 0`, so (N) fails,
and it is the only hypothesis of that theorem that fails.

For the example `AAtATatatAATTattaTT`, rewriting along `y` shows the structure by hand:
`B_0 = <x,z,y | y^(-1) x y = x^2 z^(-1) x^(-1) z^2>`. Here `x^2 z^(-1) x^(-1) z^2` has `z`-exponent
sum 1, so no power of it is conjugate to a nonzero power of `x`. It is not a proper power. So the
edge groups are non-conjugate maximal cyclic subgroups, and there is no Baumslag–Solitar subgroup.

The 8 unresolved relators give, at the second level, either edge groups of rank 3 or a non-primitive
`R` for every choice of `s`.
