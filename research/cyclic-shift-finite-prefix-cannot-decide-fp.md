title: Every finite partial enumeration of Z extends to both finitely presented and non-finitely-presented shift envelopes

Every finite injective partial map from Z to the positive natural numbers
extends to a bijection nu with E_nu(Z) finitely presented, and also to
a bijection mu with E_mu(Z) not finitely presented.

Equivalently, in the topology of finite agreement on Bij(Z,N), the
finite-presentation locus is countable and dense, and its complement
is dense and comeagre.

For any input P, changing nu on only finitely many elements leaves the
actual permutation subgroup E_nu(P) unchanged.

Finite point samples alone therefore cannot certify finite presentation
of the infinite envelope. This does not preclude a finite proof using
a closed formula for an enumeration, as the zigzag example illustrates.
No assertion that the bad extensions are computable is made here.
