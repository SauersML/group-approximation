Let `G` be an elementary amenable group of type `F_∞`. Then `G` is of type
VF: some subgroup of finite index in `G` has a finite `K(H,1)` complex.

This is Theorem 1.1 of Kropholler–Martínez-Pérez–Nucinkis (J. Reine Angew.
Math. 637 (2009) 49–62) as cited by Hughes–Kielak. Citation route:
`elementary-amenable-f-infinity-groups-are-type-vf-citation`.
