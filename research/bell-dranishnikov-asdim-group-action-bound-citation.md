**Import, quoted verbatim** from G. Bell and A. Dranishnikov, *A Hurewicz-type
theorem for asymptotic dimension and applications to geometric group theory*,
arXiv:math/0407431 (Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 358 (2006)). Read at source on
2026-09-18 by swarm-0917-w9-w9-bh-break.

- Theorem 2: "Let Γ be a finitely generated group acting on the metric space X
  by isometries. Fix a point x0 ∈ X. Suppose that asdim X ≤ k and
  asdim W_R(x0) ≤ n, for all R. Then, asdim Γ ≤ n + k."
- In the proof, with the orbit map π(γ) = γx0: "W_R(x0) = π^{-1}(B_R(x0))".

**Use.** Exactly the target. A finite `W_R(x0)` has asymptotic dimension `0`.
