For `Λ = [4,3,3,5]` and `Λ = [5,3,3,5]` (paths `s_1 ⋯ s_5` with these consecutive labels), the
Artin complex `Δ_Λ` is contractible.

By `artin-k-pi-1-from-contractible-artin-complex`, `A_Λ` then satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture,
since every `Λ − {s}` is spherical.

`h4-deligne-complex-upward-flag-toward-5-edge`, through the route
