**Source.** D. Osajda, *Residually finite non-exact groups*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 28 (2018), no. 2, 509–517, doi 10.1007/s00039-018-0432-4. Crossref confirms the container, volume, issue and pages. arXiv:1703.03791; the source `rfneg_arXiv.tex` was fetched on MSI 2026-09-13.

**Verbatim.**
- Definition, l.119–120: "A finitely generated group is \emph{non-exact} if its reduced $C^{\ast}$--algebra is non-exact. Equivalently, it has no Guoliang Yu's property A".
- Main Theorem, l.125–127: "There exist finitely generated residually finite non-exact groups defined by infinite graphical small cancellation presentations."
- l.128: "This answers one of few questions from the Open Problems chapter of the Brown-Ozawa book \cite[Problem 10.4.6]{BrownOzawa-book}."
- l.424–425: "the Cayley graph $\mr{Cay}(G,S)$ contains isometrically embedded copies of all the graphs $\hth{i}$ … That is, $\mr{Cay}(G,S)$ contains a sequence of $D$--regular graphs of growing girth".
