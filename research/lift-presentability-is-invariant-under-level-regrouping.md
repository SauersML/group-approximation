## Statement

Let `G = ⟨S⟩ <= Aut(X^*)` be finite-state (`S` closed under sections and inverses), `m >= 1`, and give `G` the
level-`m` action on `(X^m)^*` by the same states (root permutation on `X^m`, sections at level `m`). Then
`V_{|X|}(G)` is finitely presented if and only if `V_{|X|^m}(G)` is.

## Corollaries

Call `u in X^m` **commonly fixed** if `s(u) = u` for every `s in S`.
1. **Obstruction at a word.** If `u` is commonly fixed and the eventual section group at `u` (generated by
   `σ_u^p(S)`, `σ_u(s) = s|_u`, `σ_u^p` idempotent on `S`) is not finitely presented, then `V_{|X|}(G)` is not
   finitely presented. (Apply `loop-retraction-forces-fp-eventual-section-groups` to the level-`m` action, in
   which `u` is a commonly fixed letter, and transfer by the Statement.)
2. **Compression at a word.** If `u` is commonly fixed, then `G` acts faithfully, by the same states, on
   `((X^m ∖ {u}) × {0,…,p+q-1})^*` (apply `commonly-fixed-letters-compress-to-faithful-actions` to the level-`m`
   action).
3. **Search space.** Combined with letter repetition and compression, whether some action of `G` built from
   `S` is lift-presented may be tested after any regrouping, without changing the answer for the regrouped
   action itself.
