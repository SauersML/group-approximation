Let `R_BCL` be the finitely generated characteristic-`p` associative ring
with the full named corner rows `(BCL0)--(BCL2)`, and let `G_BCL` be its
finite rank-five Steinberg wordization.  Raising the Steinberg rank closes
finite generation and ordinary Kazhdan rigidity, but does not import a
known higher-cohomology or normalized-HS theorem.

Precisely:

1. Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain applies to every finitely generated unital
   associative ring and proves property `(T)` for `St_n(R_BCL)`, `n>=3`.
   The finite root-relator quotient used in the BCL wordization retains
   property `(T)`.  If the named compressor unit is merely a coefficient
   unit represented by its usual Steinberg diagonal/Weyl word (or a Tietze
   alias for that word), it does not change this conclusion.
2. That theorem is degree one.  None of the audited universal-lattice
   theorems proves

   ```text
   H^2(St_n(R_BCL),pi)=0                              (B2K1)
   ```

   for all unitary `pi`, at any rank.  The available higher-Kazhdan results
   concern arithmetic lattices or groups acting on suitable high-rank
   buildings.  The arbitrary noncommutative quotient `R_BCL`, with its BCS
   idempotents and free compressor relations, satisfies no such hypothesis.
   Moreover higher Kazhdan vanishing is not inherited by arbitrary
   quotients, so it cannot be descended formally from a hypothetical free
   universal source.
3. Even granting `(B2K1)`, the de Chiffre--Glebsky--Lubotzky--Thom theorem
   corrects approximate representations in the **unnormalized** Frobenius
   norm.  It does not correct normalized Hilbert--Schmidt microstates.  For
   a rank-one projection `e_d`,

   ```text
   ||2e_d||_(2,d)=2/sqrt(d) -> 0,
   ||2e_d||_F=2.                                      (B2K2)
   ```

   Thus a fixed-rank relator error disappears in the hyperlinear metric but
   never enters a Frobenius stability basin.  Equivalently, normalized HS is
   not submultiplicative as a matrix-algebra norm: `e_d^2=e_d` while
   `||e_d||_(2,d)=d^(-1/2)`.  This is exactly the hypothesis used by the
   cohomological splitting mechanism.
4. If instead the compressor is adjoined as an external ascending-HNN
   stable letter, rather than represented by a coefficient unit inside the
   Steinberg group, neither the property-`(T)` theorem nor any audited
   2-Kazhdan/Frobenius permanence theorem applies to the enlarged group.
   In the bare mapping-torus factor the exponent map of the stable letter
   even gives a quotient onto `Z`, so ordinary property `(T)` is lost.

The marked-root payoff is therefore exact but conditional on a genuinely
new metric theorem.  If `w=x_12(P_f)` is nontrivial in `G_BCL` and `G_BCL`
is uniformly flexibly stable in **normalized** Hilbert--Schmidt norm, then
`agent-free-compressor-full-corner-root-is-fd-invisible` and
`hs-stability-plus-fd-residual-forces-nonhyperlinearity` imply that `G_BCL`
is nonhyperlinear.  Without a separate proof that `w!=1`, stability only
proves the marked-root kill estimate and is not by itself a witness.

Consequently the current exact failure is twofold and independent:

```text
no imported 2-Kazhdan theorem for St_n(R_BCL),
and 2-Kazhdan Frobenius stability has the wrong normalization. (B2K3)
```

Finite presentation, ordinary property `(T)`, and exact finite-dimensional
invisibility of the payload root are not the missing hypotheses.
