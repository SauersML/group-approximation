**Setting.** Let `p` be prime, `R = F_p[t, t^{-1}]`, `M = R²` (column vectors), `E = EL_2(R)` (the
subgroup of `SL_2(R)` generated by elementary matrices) and `Γ_p = E ⋉ M`. Let `W(Z)` be the
group of bijections of `Z` with bounded displacement `d(g) = sup_n |g(n) − n|`. Suppose `Γ_p` acts
faithfully on `Z` with every element in `W(Z)`. For `x ∈ Z` put `K_x = Stab_M(x)` and
`Q_x = {e(K_x) : e ∈ E} `, the `E`-orbit of `K_x` among subgroups of `M`. For a nonzero ideal
`J ⊂ R`, let `E(J) = E ∩ ker(SL_2(R) → SL_2(R/J))`.

**Statement.** There is `B` such that the following hold.

1. *(Bounded kernels.)* `|Mx| = [M : K_x] ≤ 2B − 1` for every `x`.
2. *(Equivariance.)* `K_{gx} = e(K_x)` for `g = (e, m) ∈ Γ_p`. So `x ↦ K_x` maps each
   `Γ_p`-orbit `Y` onto one `E`-orbit `Q_x`, equivariantly.
3. *(Finite orbits, congruence.)* Every `Q_x` is finite. For each `x` there is a nonzero ideal
   `I_x` with `I_x M ⊆ K` for every `K ∈ Q_x`. Hence `E(I_x)` fixes every point of `Q_x`, and the
   `E`-set `Q_x` is a quotient of the finite congruence quotient `E/E(I_x)`.
4. *(Unbounded levels.)* For every nonzero ideal `J` some `x` has `JM ⊄ K_x`, and
   `sup_x |Q_x| = ∞`.
5. *(Non-expansion.)* Let `S ⊂ Γ_p` be finite and `D_S = Σ_{s∈S} d(s)`. For every `x` and every
   `1 ≤ k ≤ |Q_x|` there is `A ⊂ Q_x` with `|A| = k` and `Σ_{s∈S} |sA \ A| ≤ 2D_S`. In particular
   the Schreier graph of `E` on `Q_x`, with respect to the image of `S`, has Cheeger constant at
   most `2D_S / ⌊|Q_x|/2⌋`, which tends to `0` along any `x` with `|Q_x| → ∞`.

The same holds for any embedding `Γ_p ≤ [[X]]`, `X` the full shift, via `[[X]] ≤ W(Z)`.

**Consequence.** The exclusion of `Γ_p` from `W(Z)` and from `[[X]]` is reduced to property (τ)
for `E` with respect to the congruence subgroups `E(J)`. See
`burger-laurent-pair-is-not-in-the-wobbling-group`.

**Note.** Item 3 kills the scenario in which `W(Z)` realizes the base through an infinite
`E`-orbit of point kernels. It is unconditional. The stabilizer of such a kernel is virtually
abelian, and the layout sets of item 5 make it co-amenable, which would force `E` to be amenable.
