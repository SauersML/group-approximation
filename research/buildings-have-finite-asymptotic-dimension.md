**ESTABLISHED (import).**

**Statement.** Let `(W, S)` be a finitely generated Coxeter system and `C` a
building of type `(W, S)` with `W`-distance `δ_C`. The chamber set with the
gallery distance `ℓ∘δ_C` has finite asymptotic dimension. No thickness or local
finiteness is assumed.

**Source.** J. Dymara and T. Schick, *Buildings have finite asymptotic
dimension*, Russ. J. Math. Phys. 16 (2009), 409–412, arXiv:math/0703199v2,
Theorem 1.
