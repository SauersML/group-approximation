**Nonsofic.** `hyperbolic-kazhdan-group-has-infranormal-kazhdan-subgroup` gives Kazhdan groups
`Gamma_hat <= G_hat`, with `Gamma_hat` infranormal and not normal in `G_hat`. That is exactly the
hypothesis of Kun--Thom Theorem A as recorded in `kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic`: an
infranormal, non-normal subgroup, with both groups Kazhdan. Its conclusion makes
`D = G_hat *_(Gamma_hat) G_hat` not sofic.

**Finitely generated and torsion-free.**
- `G_hat` is Kazhdan, hence finitely generated, so `D` is generated by two copies of a finite
  generating set.
- `G` is torsion-free (`hyperbolic-kazhdan-cover-of-leavitt-unit-group`), so the vertex groups
  are torsion-free.
- A finite subgroup of an amalgam fixes a vertex of the Bass--Serre tree, hence is conjugate into
  a vertex group, hence is trivial.

**Finite cohomological dimension.**
- A torsion-free word-hyperbolic group has finite cohomological dimension. Its Rips complex
  `P_d(G)` is contractible for `d` large, finite-dimensional and locally finite, and `G` acts on
  it properly and cocompactly. When `G` is torsion-free that action is free, so the quotient is a
  finite `K(G,1)`. This is standard (Rips; Bridson--Haefliger, Part III.Gamma); the exact
  statement number was not re-read here.
- Put `c = cd G`. Subgroups have smaller or equal cohomological dimension, because `ZG` is free
  over the group ring of any subgroup. So `cd G_hat, cd Gamma_hat <= c`.
- The Mayer--Vietoris sequence of the amalgam, for any `D`-module `M`, reads

  ```text
  H^n(G_hat;M) + H^n(G_hat;M) -> H^n(Gamma_hat;M) -> H^(n+1)(D;M) -> H^(n+1)(G_hat;M) + H^(n+1)(G_hat;M).
  ```

  For `n + 1 > c + 1`, both ends vanish, so `H^(n+1)(D;M) = 0`. Hence `cd D <= c + 1`.
- Consequently `D` contains no `Z^n` for `n > c + 1`, since `cd Z^n = n`.

**Model test of the hypothesis.** Take the normal pair `1 <= SL_3(Z)`: both groups are Kazhdan,
but `1` is normal. The double over it is `SL_3(Z) * SL_3(Z)`, a free product of residually finite
groups, hence sofic. So the non-normality clause of Theorem A is essential. It holds here because
the image `Gamma` of `Gamma_hat` is not normal in the Leavitt group `G_L`. `QED`
