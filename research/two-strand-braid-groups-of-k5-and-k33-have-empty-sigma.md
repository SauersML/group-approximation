`UD_2K_5` is a closed nonorientable surface with Euler characteristic `−5`,
i.e. of genus 7: 10 vertices, 30 edges, 15 squares, and every vertex link is
a 6-cycle. `UD_2K_{3,3}` is a closed nonorientable surface with Euler
characteristic `−3`, i.e. of genus 5: 15 vertices, 36 edges, 18 squares,
with vertex links 6-cycles or 4-cycles. For `n = 2` both graphs satisfy
Abrams' subdivision condition, so `B_2K_5 ≅ π_1(N_7)` and
`B_2K_{3,3} ≅ π_1(N_5)`. In a closed surface group with negative Euler
characteristic, nontrivial finitely generated normal subgroups have finite
index. So no nonzero character has finitely generated kernel, `Σ¹ = ∅`, and
hence `Σ^m = ∅` for all `m`.
