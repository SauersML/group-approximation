**OPEN.** This is the smallest open case of `two-patch-ternary-rules-reduce-to-linear-strictness` left by the
free-memory census.

**Setting.**
- **Automaton.** `A = F_3`, `G` a group, and `1, a, b` distinct in `G`.
  `mu = lambda + t_p [y = p] + t_q [y = q]`, where `lambda = c_1 y_1 + c_2 y_a + c_3 y_b + c_0`, `p != q` share a
  symbol, and the weights are nonzero. `tau(x)(g) = mu(x(g), x(ga), x(gb))`.
- **Pair-distinct.** Each coordinate has exactly one blind pair, and the three pairs are distinct.
- **Unbalanced.** The value counts of `mu` are not `9, 9, 9`.

**Claim.** If `c_1 + c_2 a + c_3 b` is a unit in `F_3[G]`, then `tau` is not injective.

**Why this case is smallest.** See artifact Sections 5–6.
- **Census.** There are 855 raw rigid unbalanced two-patch rules, and 126 of them collapse on constants.
  - Classes `I_3` and `I_1` (504 rules) have two-site collisions over free memory.
  - Class `I_0` (195 rules) allows collisions only on three or more sites, which were not searched.
  - This class (30 rules) is the only one with no finite collision over free memory.
- **Rules in this class.** 42 raw rules, 30 of them with an injective diagonal. All have coefficient class
  `(1, 1, -1)`, and in each one every coordinate has exactly one blind pair.

## Attempts

- **Amenable and sofic placements: done.** Balance rules the rules out. An injective automaton there is
  bijective and preserves the uniform Bernoulli measure, and the rule is unbalanced.
- **Constants** (artifact Corollary 5.2). A rule whose diagonal `v -> mu(v, v, v)` is not injective is not injective
  over any group. This removes 12 of the 42 raw pair-distinct rules.
- **No bounded changes on large-girth placements** (artifact Section 6).
  - **Lifting.** If the ball of radius `R` of `F(a, b)` injects into `<a, b>`, a collision with changed sites
    within `R - 2` of each other lifts to a finite collision over free memory. By
    `pair-distinct-ternary-rules-pre-injective-on-free-memory`, no such collision exists.
  - **Scale of the unit.** A unit `1 + a - b` whose inverse is supported on words of length at most `r` forces a
    relation of length at most `2r + 2`. So collisions must use relations at that scale.
- **Host filter** (artifact Lemma 5.1 and Corollary 5.3). These, the lifting bound, the unit scale bound and the
  equivalence of class `II` with the hypothesis of `pair-distinct-ternary-rules-pre-injective-on-free-memory`
  were re-derived by w7-vf-nonlinear (verification artifact Section 1). The census counts were not re-counted.
  - **Descent.** Injectivity descends to marked quotients.
  - **For the seed.** In every amenable quotient of a counterexample host, `a` maps to `1`. Every other
    coincidence pattern restricts `mu'` to an unbalanced rule.
  - **With the unit.** The abelianization of `<a, b>` is cyclic of odd order, generated by the image of `b`.
    There `1 + a - b` becomes `2 - b`, which is not a unit over `Z` or over even cyclic groups.
- **Seed.** `mu' = x_1 + x_a - x_b + [(0,1,0)] - [(1,2,2)]`:
  - counts `10, 7, 10`;
  - blind pairs `{0,1}`, `{1,2}` and `{0,2}` at `1, a, b`;
  - identity diagonal, and shared symbol `1`.
- **Where it stops.** There is no collision for `mu'` over a nonsofic group in which `1 + a - b` is a unit and `a`
  dies in every amenable quotient, and no obstruction either. Unused:
  - the constant-background residues of `distinct-symbol-patch-collision-2026-09-12.md`, Proposition 5, for `mu'`;
  - delta-preimage changes at the scale of the inverse's support.
