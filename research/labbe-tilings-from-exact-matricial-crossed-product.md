`Ω_U` is an SFT with no periodic point. Item 1 of `matricial-aperiodic-sft-rings-are-not-quantum-rigid` turns an exact
matricial embedding of `LC(Ω_U, F_q) ⋊ Z^2` into periodic quantum tilings at every scale `D`, on finite-dimensional
spaces over finite fields. That is the statement of `labbe-tiles-admit-periodic-quantum-tilings-at-every-scale` for
Labbé's 19 tiles. The tiles are the letters of `Ω_U`, and the tile idempotents at each site satisfy (Q1)–(Q3).
