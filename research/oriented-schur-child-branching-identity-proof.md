If `f(x)=1`, the relative factor of `T_x` is the unique two-dimensional Pauli
module. Restricting it to the subgroup generated by `q_(N+1)` decomposes it
into its `+1` and `-1` eigenlines. Tensoring with the baseline spin module
gives two inequivalent `B_f^Q` simples, each once. If `f(x)=0`, the relative
algebra is abelian and a full-packet simple has already chosen one character;
restriction to the `Q` child therefore remains one simple. This proves
`(OCB1)--(OCB2)`.

Split the sum for `a_Q` into active and inactive sectors:

```text
a_Q=(D/d)(2 sum_active m_B+sum_inactive m_B)=2a_B-b.
```

Rearrangement gives `(OCB3)`.
