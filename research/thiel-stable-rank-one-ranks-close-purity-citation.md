Hannes Thiel, *Ranks of operators in simple C-star-algebras with stable rank
one*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 377 (2020), 37--76, Theorem
8.11, realizes every strictly positive lower semicontinuous affine rank for
unital simple separable stably finite non-elementary algebras of stable rank
one.

Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, *Nuclear C-star-algebras: 99 problems*,
Proposition `prop:cu-regular`, identifies strict comparison together with
rank functions almost occurring with pureness.  Exact rank realization is
stronger than almost occurrence.  Equivalently, strict comparison supplies
almost unperforation and, together with the realized fractional rank
functions, supplies almost divisibility.
