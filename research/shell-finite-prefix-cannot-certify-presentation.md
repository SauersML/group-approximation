Let P be infinite and virtually cyclic. Every finite injective partial
map P->N extends to an enumeration with E_nu and all its finite
singularity stabilizers finitely presented. It also extends to an
enumeration for which neither E_nu nor A_1 admits a recursive
presentation, even as an abstract group.

For any infinite finitely generated P, changing an enumeration at
only finitely many inputs leaves the actual subgroup E_nu<=Homeo(C)
unchanged, and thus leaves each specified S(M,M') unchanged.

For a virtually cyclic input the finite-presentation locus for E_nu
is countable and dense in the topology of finite agreement on
Bij(P,N). The same holds for A_1, while the non-recursively-presentable
locus for each is dense and comeagre. Finite point samples cannot
certify the presentation properties. A proof based on a global
enumeration formula is not excluded.
