Use the finite-rank normalized trace states and estimate `(DFC2)` in the claim.
A weak-star cluster point is central for the generating unitaries, hence for
the represented twisted C-star algebra.  Its pullback to the full twisted
algebra is an amenable tracial state, contradicting the required no-amenable-
trace theorem.
