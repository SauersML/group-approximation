Fix a maximal `M~=S8<HS` and its derived subgroup `H=M'~=A8`, with one
marked Atlas chart on `H`.  If a conjugator `g in HS` gives a second marked
chart satisfying the packet and collision 19243, then

```text
g notin M,       H^g != H,       M^g != M.           (HSC1)
```

Indeed `N_HS(H)=M`.  For `g in M`, the second chart is merely another
automorphism marking of the same `A8`; all inner and graph markings occur
because `M/H~=Out(A8)`.  The established classical-collapse theorem excludes
packet plus collision for every such marking.

Thus an `HS` equality screen may discard the identity `M`--`M` double coset
before any computation.  The only unresolved positions cross two distinct
maximal `S8` subgroups; there is still just one marking parity because the
normalizer realizes `Out(A8)`.
