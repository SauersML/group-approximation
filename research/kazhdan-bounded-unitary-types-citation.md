P. S. Wang, *On isolated points in the dual spaces of locally compact
groups*, Math. Ann. 218 (1975), Theorem 2.6, proves that a discrete Kazhdan
group has only finitely many inequivalent irreducible unitary
representations of each prescribed finite dimension.

A modern quantitative treatment explicitly recalls this consequence after
Theorem 1.3: M. Doucha, M. Malicki and A. Valette, *Property (T),
finite-dimensional representations, and generic representations*, J. Group
Theory 23 (2020), [arXiv:1711.04584](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04584).
Taking the union of the finitely many sets for dimensions at most `K` proves
the claim.
