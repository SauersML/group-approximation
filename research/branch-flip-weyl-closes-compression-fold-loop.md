**INVALIDATED BRANCH-FLIP COMPLETION.**  Adjoin the literal Leavitt unit
`u=s_0t_1+s_1t_0` and its opposite-root Weyl word.  Its conjugation swaps
both individual branch arms, including the complementary arms not fixed by
the one-arm range-sum table.  This is the smallest actual EL20 relation
whose full action is not contained in the old character-only `S_3` model.

`binary-branch-flip-weyl-has-s3-c2-model` extends that marked model by one
qubit swap.  The new Weyl relation, both compression preimages, and the
range-sum fold are all exact, while the marked carrier survives.  The actor
enlarges from the character `S_3` to the finite quotient `S_3 x C_2`; it
does not force the required bridge gauge to be the identity.
