title: Essential representations have central Cuntz isometries despite scalar ordinary commutant
distinct_from:
  stw99-lxxviii-finite-target-obstruction: that supplies a genuine obstruction from finiteness of the target; this rules out the false obstruction based only on a small ordinary relative commutant.
---

Let `A` be a separable unital C\*-algebra and let
`pi:A->B(H)` be a faithful unital essential representation on a separable
infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.  Then

```text
B(H)_infinity cap pi(A)'
```

contains a unital copy of `O_2`, and hence a unital copy of `O_infinity`.

In particular, if `A` is infinite-dimensional and simple, every faithful
irreducible representation is essential and its scalar ordinary commutant
does not obstruct `O_infinity`-stability of the map into `B(H)`.
