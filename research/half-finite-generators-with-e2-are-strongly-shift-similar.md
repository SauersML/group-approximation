Work in `Sym(N)` with `N = {1, 2, 3, ...}`.
- The **slide** `sl` sends each odd number to the next odd number, each even
  number other than 2 to the previous even number, and 2 to 1.
- The **flip** is `(1 2)(3 4)(5 6)...`.
- A permutation has **half-finite support** if it fixes all sufficiently large
  even numbers, or all sufficiently large odd numbers.

Then:
1. `E_2`, the group of eventually 2-periodic permutations, is generated by `sl`,
   the flip and `(1 2)`.
2. If `Gamma <= Sym(N)` is generated by permutations with half-finite support,
   then `G = <Gamma, E_2>` is strongly shift-similar.

In particular, for any `gamma_1, ..., gamma_k` with half-finite support, the
group `<gamma_1, ..., gamma_k, sl, flip, (1 2)>` is finitely generated and
strongly shift-similar. Finite presentation is not claimed and does not follow.
