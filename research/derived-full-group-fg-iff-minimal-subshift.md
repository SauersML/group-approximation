**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `(X, φ)` be a Cantor minimal system.
- The commutator subgroup `D([[φ]]_0)` is finitely generated if and only if `(X, φ)` is conjugate to a minimal subshift
  (H. Matui, Internat. J. Math. 17 (2006), Theorem 5.4).
- `D([[φ]]_0) = D([[φ]])`, the commutator subgroup of the whole topological full group (remark after Theorem 4.8).
