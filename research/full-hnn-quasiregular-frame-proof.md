The HNN relation makes `(HQR1)` independent of the representative of
`aC`.  Its pair coefficient is the canonical trace of

```text
(a t a^(-1))^(-1)(b t b^(-1))
 =a t^(-1)(a^(-1)b)t b^(-1).                          (HQP1)
```

If `a^(-1)b in C`, the middle pinch reduces `(HQP1)` to the identity.  If
`a^(-1)b notin C`, `(HQP1)` is Britton reduced and nonidentity.  The full
canonical character therefore gives `(HQR2)`.  Conjugating `(HQR1)` by
`pi(g)` gives the quasiregular action, and taking `a=e,b=g` gives `(HQR3)`.

The same normal-form argument identifies the subgroup generated by the
conjugates of `t` with the free product of copies of `Z` indexed by `A/C`;
hence every nonempty reduced lamp word has canonical trace zero.  Finally,
the trace of the commutator in `(HQR4)` is one of the two orientations of
the coefficient in `(HQR3)`, so it vanishes at `h notin C`.
