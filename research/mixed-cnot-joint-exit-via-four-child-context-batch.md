Replace the one-exit/two-return interpretation of the three endpoint failures
by three exits in three separate recurrence grandchildren.  Their total mass
is at most `3q`, so the fourth grandchild preserves the active carrier.  Their
rank-one lower sum is a fixed fraction of `q`, and the E5 first-hit layers
have finite total capacity.

This route never constructs the nonlinear projection `I-QF(I-b)/2` as a
returned word.  Its only remaining content is the finite word-level block
placement in `s3-three-context-failure-block-exit-compiler`.
