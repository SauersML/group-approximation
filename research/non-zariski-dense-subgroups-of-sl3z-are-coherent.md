title: Every subgroup of SL_3(Z) that is not Zariski-dense in SL_3 is coherent
artifacts:

If `H <= SL_3(Z)` has Zariski closure different from `SL_3`, then every
finitely generated subgroup of `H` is finitely presented.

So a finitely generated, not finitely presented subgroup of `SL_3(Z)`, a
witness against Serre's coherence question (Zaremsky Problem 3.4), must be
Zariski-dense. It must also have infinite index, since `SL_3(Z)` is finitely
presented. This claim decides nothing about Zariski-dense thin subgroups.
