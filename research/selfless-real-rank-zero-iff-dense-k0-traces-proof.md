**Literature input.** Dykema--Rørdam, *Projections in free product C\*–algebras, II*, arXiv:math/9911009v1
(Math. Z. 234 (2000) 103--113), read from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-12.
- **Definitions, p. 3.** `a ≲ b` means `||a - x_n b x_n^*|| → 0` for some `x_n ∈ A`, and
  `D_φ(a) = lim_(ε→0+) φ(f_ε(a))`.
- **Theorem 1.5, verbatim.** "Let A be a unital C\*-algebra, let Q be a compact convex subset of the state
  space of A, let Π be a subset of of the set of projections in A, and let 𝔄 be a dense \*-subalgebra of A,
  which is closed under continuous function calculus (on its normal elements). Assume that each state in Q
  is faithful on 𝔄, and that the following comparison properties hold for all positive elements a ∈ 𝔄 and
  for all projections p ∈ Π: (α) if D_φ(a) < φ(p) for all φ ∈ Q, then a ≲ p, (β) if φ(p) < D_φ(a) for all
  φ ∈ Q, then p ≲ a, and (γ) the subset of Aff(Q) induced by Π is dense in the interval [0,1] of Aff(Q).
  It follows that (i) if sr(A) = 1, then RR(A) = 0, ..."

**Item 1.** Take `Q = {τ}`, `Π` = all projections of `A`, and `𝔄 = A`.
- *Faithfulness.* `τ` is faithful.
- *The dimension function.* For a trace, `D_τ(a) = μ_a((0,∞)) = lim_n τ(a^(1/n)) = d_τ(a)`.
- *(α) and (β).* These are strict comparison (`robert-tracial-selfless-regularity`). Cuntz subequivalence in
  `A ⊗ K` between elements of the corner `A` descends to `A`: if `r_n b r_n^* → a` with `a, b ∈ A = eAe`,
  then `(e r_n e) b (e r_n e)^* = e r_n b r_n^* e → a`.
- *(γ).* With `Q` a point, `Aff(Q) = R` with its usual order and topology. By
  `selfless-projection-traces-equal-k0-trace-image` the projection traces are `τ_*(K_0(A)) ∩ [0,1]`, which
  is dense in `[0,1]` when `τ_*(K_0(A))` is dense.
- *Stable rank.* `sr(A) = 1` by Robert's Theorem 3.1(ii).

Theorem 1.5(i) gives `RR(A) = 0`.

**Item 2 (squeeze).** Let `M` be the von Neumann algebra generated by `A` in the GNS representation of `τ`,
with `τ` its faithful normal trace. Let `F(c) = μ_x((-∞, c))`, continuous by atomlessness and strictly
increasing on the support interval. Fix `c` in the interior of the support and `δ > 0`.
- *Perturb.* `RR(A) = 0` gives an invertible self-adjoint `y ∈ A` with `||y - (x - c)|| < δ' < δ`. Put
  `p = χ_(-∞,0)(y)`, a projection of `A` since `0 ∉ spec(y)`.
- *Lower bound.* Let `P_- = χ_(-∞,-δ](x - c) ∈ M`. A unit vector `ξ ∈ ran P_- ∩ ran(1 - p)` would satisfy
  `0 <= <yξ,ξ> <= <(x - c)ξ,ξ> + δ' <= -δ + δ' < 0`. So `P_- ∧ (1 - p) = 0`.
- *Kaplansky's formula.* In a finite von Neumann algebra `τ(e ∨ f) + τ(e ∧ f) = τ(e) + τ(f)` (standard;
  Kadison--Ringrose 6.1.7, not re-read). It gives `τ(P_-) + τ(1 - p) <= 1`, that is
  `F(c - δ) <= τ(P_-) <= τ(p)`.
- *Upper bound.* Symmetrically, with `P_+ = χ_[δ,∞)(x - c)`, `P_+ ∧ p = 0`, so
  `τ(p) <= 1 - τ(P_+) <= F(c + δ)`.

Letting `δ → 0` and varying `c` shows that the projection traces are dense in `[0,1]`. Every projection
trace lies in `τ_*(K_0(A))`, which is a group containing `Z`, so it is dense in `R`.

**The group element.** For `g` of infinite order, `C*(λ(g)) ≅ C(T)` with `τ` restricting to Haar measure.
So `μ_x` is the push-forward of Haar measure under `z ↦ z + z̄ = 2 Re z`: the arcsine law on `[-2,2]`,
atomless with support `[-2,2]`. A nontrivial torsion-free group has such a `g`.
