Section 4 proves each infinite Kazhdan endpoint nonamenable by its regular
representation. Simplicity then kills both coordinate images of any
amenable normal subgroup. It must lie in L, which is already locally
finite and amenable. Every locally finite normal subgroup is amenable,
so the same argument gives its maximality, together with the input MF
radical equality.
