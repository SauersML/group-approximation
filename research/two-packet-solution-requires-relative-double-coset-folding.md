Let `Gamma_0` contain the supports of `A`, `B`, `Delta`, and the constant
`S_3`.  If the packet conjugators `x,y` are free relative to `Gamma_0`, then

```text
A P_y + P_x B + P_x P_y != Delta.
```

Indeed the four displayed components have reduced relative syllable lengths
`2,2,4,0`, and the two length-two components begin in different free letters.
Coset projection separates them.  Hence every solution of the minimal
two-packet equation requires two outside-normalizer double cosets satisfying
a nontrivial folding relation over `Gamma_0`; generic independent outside
letters cannot solve it.
