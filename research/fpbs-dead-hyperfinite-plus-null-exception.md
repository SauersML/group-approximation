Dead: the attempt keeps the hyperfinite construction and concentrates all nonhyperfinite behaviour on sets whose measure tends to zero. Extending the hyperfinite restriction by singletons and charging the two deleted endpoints gives average defect at least epsilon_n(1-n/k)-2delta, so vanishing mean errors force the exceptional measure to stay above epsilon_n/2 (fpbs-cap-robust-barrier). A usable construction must be nonhyperfinite on a uniformly positive measure.
