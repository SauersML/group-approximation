**Source.** H. Matui, *Some remarks on topological full groups of Cantor minimal systems*, Internat. J. Math. 17 (2006),
no. 2, 231–251, doi:10.1142/S0129167X06003448 (Crossref, checked 2026-09-13); arXiv:math/0404117v3.

**How it was read.** The arXiv PDF was fetched on MSI, copied locally, and pages 1–4 and 10–16 were read. The journal
version was not compared.

**Verbatim.**
- "Theorem 5.4. Let (X, φ) be a Cantor minimal system. The commutator subgroup D([[φ]]_0) is finitely generated if and
  only if (X, φ) is conjugate to a minimal subshift."
- After Theorem 4.8: "Note that sgn(φγφ^{-1}) is equal to sgn(γ) for all γ ∈ D([[φ]]_0). It follows that the commutator
  subgroup D([[φ]]_0) coincides with D([[φ]])."
- Definition 2.7: "I is a surjective homomorphism from [[φ]] to Z. We call the homomorphism I the index map and denote its
  kernel by [[φ]]_0."
