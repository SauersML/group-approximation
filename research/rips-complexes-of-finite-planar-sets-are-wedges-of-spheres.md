title: "Every connected Rips complex of a finite subset of the plane is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of spheres"
---

For every finite `X ⊆ R^2` with the Euclidean metric and every `r > 0`, if
the Rips complex `R_r(X)` is connected, then it is homotopy equivalent to a
finite wedge of spheres of dimensions `≥ 1` (a point when the wedge is empty).

Its negation is `some-finite-planar-set-has-a-non-wedge-rips-complex`.

## Attempts

- Same state as `rips-complexes-of-planar-sets-are-wedges-of-spheres`: known
  for finite subsets of a circle (Adamaszek), with a free fundamental group in
  general (Chambers–de Silva–Erickson–Ghrist); no mechanism for higher
  homotopy is known. The negative attacks are recorded on the negation.
