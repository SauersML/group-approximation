Let `U in U(20160k)` be a regular Atlas relative unitary.  Let
`m_epsilon(U),m_sigma(U)` be the two nontrivial collision-channel masses, let
`t(U)` be any normalized rank or normalized Hilbert--Schmidt mass assigned to
one fixed finite menu of typed admissible incidences, and let `Delta(U)` be
any fixed normalized packet-defect statistic.  Assume all three statistics
commute with external amplification:

```text
(m_epsilon,m_sigma,t,Delta)(U tensor I_r)
 =(m_epsilon,m_sigma,t,Delta)(U).                     (FRA1)
```

Add any fixed finite family of homogeneous congruence or divisibility
conditions on the integral ranks of the word-visible `A8/S4/S3` blocks.
Suppose a proposed arithmetic transfer proves, on dimensions satisfying
those conditions,

```text
t(U) >= c_epsilon m_epsilon(U)-A Delta(U)-B/d,
t(U) >= c_sigma   m_sigma(U)  -A Delta(U)-B/d,        (FRA2)
```

with fixed nonnegative constants and `d=20160k`.  Then the terms `B/d` and
all arithmetic hypotheses can be deleted: `(FRA2)` holds for **every**
finite regular Atlas matrix with `B/d` replaced by zero.

Indeed let `r_0` be a common multiple of the exponents of the finite residue
groups and of the required divisors.  For arbitrary `U`, every amplification
`U tensor I_(r_0 ell)` satisfies the arithmetic conditions.  Integral ranks
scale by `r_0 ell`, while `(FRA1)` preserves all normalized quantities.
Apply `(FRA2)` in dimension `r_0 ell d` and let `ell` tend to infinity.  This
gives

```text
t(U) >= c_epsilon m_epsilon(U)-A Delta(U),
t(U) >= c_sigma   m_sigma(U)  -A Delta(U).            (FRA3)
```

The same proof applies to a branchwise statement: any threshold deciding the
sign or standard branch from amplification-invariant normalized data is
unchanged by `U -> U tensor I_r`.

Consequently the collision dichotomy would turn `(FRA2)` into exactly the
same positive-density incidence bound as `(FRA3)`.  Along a vanishing packet
sequence its limiting floor would be

```text
min(c_epsilon/2048,c_sigma/1536),                     (FRA4)
```

with the corresponding direct-collision constants `1/512,1/384`.  No part
of that floor comes from integrality, divisibility, parity, determinant
residue, or bounded integer rounding.

Thus fixed arithmetic is not the finite-matrix-only selection field missing
from `atlas-typed-hard-interior-comb-row-census`.  A successful proof must
establish an **extensive** mixed-rank or singular-direction inequality before
normalization.  Since the universal tracial six-row charging route is already
refuted by the exact Leavitt factor, the rank input must also be specifically
matricial: it cannot be merely a Sylvester/Murray--von Neumann dimension
inequality valid in every finite factor.  Possible survivors must use a
dimension-activated family, a global matrix identity whose complexity grows
with dimension, or another non-factorializable mixed-coordinate operation.
