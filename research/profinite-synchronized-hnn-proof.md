Apply the finite left-regular representation to every quotient in `(PSH1)`.
The restrictions along `theta_0` and `theta_1` are multiples of regular
representations of isomorphic finite images and hence are exactly unitarily
conjugate.  Their product-corona classes give a regular embedding of `A` and
an inner conjugacy of the two edge C-star subalgebras.

Ueda identifies the universal C-star HNN extension with a full corner of an
amalgam of `M_2(D)` and `M_2(B)`.  The coordinate conjugating unitary gives
compatible embeddings of these two algebras into one matrix corona, so
Shulman's Theorem 20 makes the amalgam, and therefore its corner, MF.
Finally use the ultralimit of normalized finite regular traces and the reduced
von Neumann HNN normal form to separate every nonidentity Britton-reduced
group word.
