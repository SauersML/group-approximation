Let `Λ = s_1 ⋯ s_n` be the Coxeter diagram of type `B_n` with `m_{s_{n−1} s_n} = 4`, ordered
`ŝ_1 < ⋯ < ŝ_n`. Then the induced relation `<` on the vertices of the Artin complex `Δ_Λ` is a
partial order that is weakly graded, bowtie free and upward flag.

Equivalently, with the reversed order, which starts at the end labelled 4, the poset is
bowtie free and downward flag.

Literature import through `b-n-deligne-complex-upward-flag-toward-4-edge-citation`. This is the
`B_n` case of Conjecture `conj:compareB` of arXiv:2405.12068.
