**Problem LXXXVIII of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(Blackadar).  (1) Is every separable C\*-algebra an inductive limit of
semiprojective algebras?  (2) Is every separable NUCLEAR algebra a
limit of semiprojective nuclear algebras?

## Attempts

* Known positives: nuclear + suspension-like cases via Enders'
  characterizations; 1-NCCW limits by definition; UCT Kirchberg
  algebras (ranges built from semiprojective models).  The recorded
  structural remark: the question behaves like a C*-analogue of
  "every compact metric space is an inverse limit of ANRs" — TRUE
  topologically, and the C*-obstruction candidates are all
  K-theoretic: semiprojective algebras have finitely generated
  K-theory in each ideal-filtration layer, which limits accommodate,
  so no invariant-level obstruction exists; the difficulty is purely
  perturbative (lifting relations).  A counterexample would need a
  separable algebra whose every finite presentation is "infinitely
  unstable" — no such phenomenon is known even at the level of
  examples, which is why Blackadar's question has survived 40 years
  with neither answer; flagged as a problem where the negative
  direction has no known candidate mechanism at all.
