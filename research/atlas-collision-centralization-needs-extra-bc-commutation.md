Put `y=t^-1` and `b=s t` in the forward-collision presentation.  If one adds
the extra relation `[b,c]=1`, then collision `q=1` forces `[c,y]=1`.  Indeed,
with `a=bc` and `x=a y b`, the collision word becomes `y^-1 x^2`; hence
`x^2=y`.  The involution relation `a^2=1` then gives `b x b=x^-1`, and
therefore `c=x^3`, central in `<x,b>`.

This does not apply to the literal candidate.  The graph-of-groups
presentation `F *_A C *_B K` contains no relation between `b` and `c` before
collision.  The commutation `[b,c]=1` was an additional feature of the
S288/S4032 finite carrier, not a packet or forward relator.  Moreover the
known exact `GL5(2)` model of all fourteen core words plus collision has
nontrivial collision coefficient, so those relators cannot imply the proposed
collapse.  Thus the calculation is a sharp explanation of why the central-c
carrier cannot be retuned, not a KL proof.
