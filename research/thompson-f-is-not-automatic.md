**OPEN.** No finite generating set of Thompson's group `F` carries an automatic
structure in the sense of Epstein et al., *Word Processing in Groups* (1992).

## Attempts

- **Classical consequences of automaticity.** Dead as obstructions. Automatic
  groups are finitely presented, have quadratic Dehn functions and are of type
  `F_∞`. `F` has all three: Guba (arXiv:math/0211395) and Brown–Geoghegan
  (1984).
- **Near-geodesic languages.** Hauze (arXiv:1801.01965) excludes languages over
  `{x0^±1, x1^±1}` with bounded additive excess over geodesic length. Automatic
  structures with uniqueness consist of multiplicative quasigeodesics (standard
  pumping argument, recorded in `zaremsky-2-02-thompson-f-automatic`), so
  extending Hauze to bounded multiplicative excess over every generating set is
  equivalent to this claim. That is a restatement, not a reduction, and no
  route is written for it.
- **Infinite dimensionality.** `F` is torsion-free and of infinite cohomological
  dimension, so no Rips complex of `F` is contractible. A theorem that
  torsion-free automatic groups have finite cohomological dimension, or that
  automatic groups (or amenable automatic groups, Zaremsky Problem 4.1) have
  contractible Rips complexes, would give a negative answer (the second only
  together with `thompson-f-is-amenable`). No such theorem is known to this
  lane. These routes are added when their prerequisite claims are on main.
