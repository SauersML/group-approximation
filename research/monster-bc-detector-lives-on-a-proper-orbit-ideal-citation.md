Direct literature import; the Finn-Sell paper was read in full from the arXiv text on
2026-09-13. Theorem numbers and statements are quoted.

- **FS14:** M. Finn-Sell, *On the Baum--Connes conjecture for Gromov monster groups*,
  arXiv:1401.6841v2 (21 Sep 2014).
  - Theorem 3.6: for a Gromov monster `Γ` with coarsely embedded large-girth expander `X`, the
    coarse groupoid `G(X)` is Morita equivalent to `Ω_(βX) ⋊ Γ`.
  - Theorem 3.7: "the Baum--Connes conjecture for `Γ` with coefficients in `C_0(Y_(βX))` fails to
    be a surjection, but is an injection." (`Y_(βX) = Ω_(βX)`.)
  - Theorem 3.8 and its proof: `Γ` is not K-exact; the ladder of Rieffel long exact sequences
    with vertical isomorphisms exhibits
    `0 -> C_0(Ω_X) ⋊_r Γ -> C_0(Ω_(βX)) ⋊_r Γ -> C_0(Ω_(∂βX)) ⋊_r Γ -> 0` non-exact in the
    middle, with `C_0(Ω_X) ⋊_r Γ ≅ K` (the compacts, bottom-left `K_0(K)` in the displayed
    ladder).
  - Definition 4.1, Proposition 4.2: a large-girth bounded-degree expander is a-T-menable at
    infinity (its coarse boundary groupoid `G(X)|_(∂βX)` is a-T-menable).
  - Theorem 4.3: `Ω_(∂βX) ⋊ Γ` is a-T-menable.
  - Corollary 4.4: "the Baum--Connes conjecture for `Γ` with coefficients in any
    `(Ω_(∂βX) ⋊ Γ)`-C*-algebra is an isomorphism."
- **HLS02:** N. Higson, V. Lafforgue, G. Skandalis, *Counterexamples to the Baum--Connes
  conjecture*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 12 (2002), no. 2, 330--354 (the original ladder and
  non-surjectivity).
- **WY12:** R. Willett, G. Yu, *Higher index theory for certain expanders and Gromov monster
  groups, I*, Adv. Math. 229 (2012), no. 3, 1380--1416 (injectivity, and the ghost-class
  non-surjectivity via the maximal Roe algebra).
- **FSW14:** M. Finn-Sell, N. Wright, *Spaces of graphs, boundary groupoids and the coarse
  Baum--Connes conjecture*, Adv. Math. 259 (2014), 306--338 (a-T-menability at infinity of
  large-girth expanders).
- **Tu99:** J.-L. Tu, *La conjecture de Baum--Connes pour les feuilletages moyennables*,
  K-Theory 17 (1999), no. 3, 215--264 (Baum--Connes for a-T-menable groupoids).
- **KS02:** M. Khoshkam, G. Skandalis, *Regular representation of groupoid C*-algebras and
  applications to inverse semigroups*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 546 (2002), 47--72 (the cocycle
  Morita-equivalence theorem, Finn-Sell Theorem 2.6).
