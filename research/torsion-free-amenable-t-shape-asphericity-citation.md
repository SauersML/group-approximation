Citation import. The TeX sources were fetched on MSI on 2026-09-13; the
verbatim passages and line numbers are in the artifact.

- **Item 2.** M. Forester and C. Rourke, *Diagrams and the second homotopy
  group*, arXiv:math/0306088, Main Theorem (`pi-two.tex` l.174–186): if
  `L = K cup e^1 cup e^2`, `pi_1(K)` is torsion-free and the `t`-shape of `w`
  is amenable, then `pi_2(L) = Z pi_1(L) (x)_(Z pi_1 K) pi_2(K)`. The same
  paper says `K` need not be 2-dimensional (l.202–203). The shape definitions
  and the exponent-sum-one remark are l.367–385. With `K = K(G,1)`,
  `pi_2(K) = 0`, so `pi_2(L) = 0`.
- **Item 1.** Forester--Rourke, l.193–195: "under conditions (1) and (2),
  Fenn and Rourke have solved the adjunction problem, and proved that
  `pi_1(K) -> pi_1(L)` is injective". This is second hand for the
  Fenn--Rourke 1996 paper, which was not read. For exponent sum `±1` it is also
  part (a) of the Main Theorem of Forester--Rourke, *The adjunction problem
  and a theorem of Serre*, arXiv:math/0412274 (`multi4.tex` l.98–106), taking
  one layer with the `1 x 1` exponent matrix `(±1)`.
- **Item 3.** W. A. Bogley, M. Edjvet and G. Williams, arXiv:1809.03460,
  Lemma `Covering` (l.87–88): for a relative two-complex `(Y, X)` with
  `pi_1 X -> pi_1 Y` injective and `pi_2 Y = 0`, `Y` is aspherical iff `X` is.
  Apply it to `(L, K)` with items 1 and 2. Their Lemma `KG1` (l.99–100) turns
  this into asphericity of the relative presentation.

Trust surface: Klyachko's and Fenn--Rourke's original papers were not read.
Items 2 and 3 are first hand, and so is item 1 at exponent sum `±1`.
