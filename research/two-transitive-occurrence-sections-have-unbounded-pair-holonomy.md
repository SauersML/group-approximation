If a finitely generated group acts two-transitively on an infinite set, no
choice of two global role sections and simultaneous pair transporters can
keep both endpoint stabilizer holonomies in fixed finite sets.  In
particular their ambient word lengths are jointly unbounded.

Thus finitely many tuple orbits do not make all consistency instances have
constant normalized-HS cost: converting a contextual pair transporter into
the strategy's fixed question words introduces an unbounded stabilizer
cocycle.  A viable compiler needs either dimension-free stabilizer
covariance or a bounded-neighbor incidence structure.
