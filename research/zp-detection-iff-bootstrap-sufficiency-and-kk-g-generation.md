**ESTABLISHED (derivation from cited theorems; a few standard triangulated
inputs are flagged in the proof as not re-read).**  Let `p` be prime,
`G = Z/p`, `B ⊆ KK` the bootstrap class and `B^G ⊆ KK^G` the equivariant
bootstrap class.  Call an object of `KK^G` *nuclear* if it is
KK^G-equivalent to a separable nuclear `G`-C\*-algebra.  The following are
equivalent.

* **(D) Detection.**  Every nuclear `D` with `Res D ≃_KK 0` and
  `D ⋊ G ≃_KK 0` is KK^G-contractible.  This is
  `zp-restriction-and-crossed-product-detect-kk-g-contractibility`.
* **(L) Noncommutative isotropy separation.**  Every nuclear object of `KK^G`
  lies in the localizing subcategory generated by the induced algebras
  `Ind A = C(G, A)` and the trivial-action algebras `τA`, for separable `A`.
* **(S) Sufficiency at `Z/p`.**  A nuclear `G`-algebra `A` with `Res A ∈ B`
  and `A ⋊ G ∈ B` belongs to `B^G`.
* **(K) Dynamical uniqueness.**  Any two pointwise outer actions of `G` on
  `O_2 ⊗ K` whose crossed products are KK-contractible are conjugate.

**Literature status (read 2026-09-13).**  Meyer--Nadareishvili, *A universal
coefficient theorem for actions of finite groups on C\*-algebras*,
arXiv:2406.11787, §3, text before Corollary 3.5: "The following corollary
relates certain conditions that are clearly necessary for A ∈ KK^G to belong
to the equivariant bootstrap class. We do not know whether they are also
sufficient."  The conditions are that `A ⋊ H` lies in `B` for all cyclic
subgroups `H ⊆ G`.  For `G = Z/p` the cyclic subgroups are `1` and `G`, so the
question is exactly (S).  Detection is therefore the prime-order case of a
question the authors of the finite-group equivariant UCT record as open.
The check was bounded: that paper was read around §3.  The later
Meyer--Nadareishvili paper arXiv:2604.12529v2 was searched only in a
letter-scrambled text extraction, for "whether", "necessary", "sufficient"
and "question", and no match concerned this question.

**What the equivalences say.**
* A counterexample to detection is a nonzero nuclear object right-orthogonal
  to every trivial-action algebra and every induced algebra, by (L).  It is
  also a counterexample to the Meyer--Nadareishvili sufficiency question, by
  (S).
* Detection is not a consequence of the UCT.  If every separable nuclear
  C\*-algebra satisfied the UCT, (S) would say that every nuclear object of
  `KK^G` lies in `B^G`.  That is the equivariant bootstrap problem for `Z/p`,
  a separate problem.
* By (K), detection is a statement about outer dynamics alone.  Theorem 3.13
  of Gabe--Szabó supplies one contractible model on `O_2 ⊗ K`; detection says
  it is the only pointwise outer action there with contractible crossed
  product.

The Arano--Kubota theorem (Meyer--Nadareishvili, Theorem 2.1: `A ≃ 0` once
`Res^G_H A ≃ 0` for all cyclic `H`) is empty for `G = Z/p`, because `G` is
itself cyclic.  So (L) is genuinely a different generation statement from
their Theorem 3.1.
