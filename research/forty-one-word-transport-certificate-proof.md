Exact prefix reduction gives 37 support words of total coefficient mass
`11/4` for `F` and four support words of total coefficient mass `1` for `G`.
If every support word is within `eta` of its selected returned algebra, the
triangle inequality gives total carrier distance at most `(15/4)eta`.
The D8 recovery floor is `1/8`, so `(15/4)eta >= 1/8`, equivalently
`eta >= 1/30`.  Keeping individual errors gives the stated weighted form.
