Let `G` be the fundamental group of a hyperbolic triangle of finite simple groups with
no nontrivial finite quotient. `G` is word-hyperbolic. Its vertex groups are nontrivial
finite subgroups, and they die in the only finite quotient, the trivial one. By
`simple-vertex-groups-vtf-iff-some-finite-quotient`, `G` is not virtually
torsion-free. In fact this needs only the last clause of that claim: the whole of `G`
is its own finite residual.
