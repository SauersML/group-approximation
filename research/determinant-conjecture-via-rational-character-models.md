**Dead.** Invalidated by `kun-thom-wreath-is-not-rational-character-approximable`.

The idea: `rational-character-approximable-groups-obey-determinant`
proves the conjecture for every rational-character approximable group. That
class contains the sofic groups, and it could have reached nonsofic groups
through non-permutation rational representations of finite groups.

It cannot cover every group. The Kun--Thom wreath admits no approximation by
finite subgroups of unitary groups in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt, and
rational-character models are such approximations. This is the analogue, one
class up, of `determinant-conjecture-via-soficity-of-every-group`.
