Artifact Sections 3,5,6,7 give the proof. Every class-three image is
generated by exponent-seven vertex groups and itself has exponent
seven by power collection. In its F7 Lie algebra the three corner
logs are iU_j-i^2 V_j/2+i^3 C_j, with C_j derived.

Their BCH product R(i) has degree at most six; the marked equations
give R(0)=0 and R(i)=-Z for all six nonzero i. Thus R(i)=-Z i^6.
The degree-two coefficient forces sum V_j into gamma2. The degree-four
coefficient modulo gamma3 forces sum_[j<k][V_j,V_k] into gamma3.
These imply that each pair bracket of V's is in gamma3, so every
triple bracket of V's vanishes. Those triple brackets are the only
possible degree-six terms of BCH; hence Z=0.

The class-two special case is the degree-four power-sum argument
in Section5. If c0 mod7 inflated from K7^ab, its finite central
extension by C7 would give a class-two image detecting z, contrary
to the theorem. Naturality puts all cup products and Bocksteins
of degree-one classes in that same inflation image.
