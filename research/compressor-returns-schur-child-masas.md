Construct a finite presentation with a one-sided Kazhdan compressor and the
finitely many Schur packet diamonds such that:

1. it has an exact finite-tracial representation realizing the fixed non-CE
   BCS trace and a separated mark;
2. after finite-packet exactification in every finite-dimensional microstate,
   a positive-density forbidden carrier yields one centered child module
   `range(E_P-E_B)`;
3. compressor covariance returns that module to the same fixed packet scale
   as `range(E_Q-E_B)`, with squared transport distance bounded by a
   dimension-independent, bounded-overlap sum of named relator energies; and
4. the return relation is false in the tensor-decoupled packet/compressor
   model.

On forbidden sectors the source and target modules are the orthogonal
`Z tensor M_m` and `X tensor M_m` spaces of `(SPS5)`, so a low-energy return
contradicts their positive density.  On allowed sectors the two binary child
refinements have a common joint character decomposition and must not be
charged.

## Attempts

- A finite overgroup cannot perform the selector-dependent return: regular
  normalization gives a nonlinear phase one-half wrong mass.
- Bare commutation with the uncompressed/compressed Kazhdan images admits the
  separate tensor-factor countermodel.
- The active construction should use a two-dimensional HNN/complex-of-groups
  cell whose two edges are the actual child inclusions in `(SPS1)`.  The
  analytic endpoint is now `(SPS4)`; what remains is a finite word-level cell
  with runtime- and multiplicity-independent covariance loss.
- The local two-cell is now explicit:
  `schur-packet-flips-realize-predicate-cocycle` gives two commuting packet
  automorphisms whose implementers anticommute exactly on forbidden simples.
  The live route `schur-flip-amplification-returns-child-masas` leaves only
  `compressor-amplifies-schur-flip-cocycle-rank`: recursively create enough
  independent copies of this fixed cell with uniform HS loss.
