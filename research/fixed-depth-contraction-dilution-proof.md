The Poisson measure at `rho` has analytic moments `rho^n`.  Apply
Caratheodory in `R^(2N)` to its first `N` complex moments, diagonalize a
finite unitary at the resulting unit-circle atoms, and take the rank-one
projection onto the square-root weight vector.  Its compressed powers are
the prescribed moments exactly.
