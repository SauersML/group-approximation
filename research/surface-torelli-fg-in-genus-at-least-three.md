title: "The Torelli group of the genus g surface with at most one boundary component is finitely generated for g >= 3"
---

For `g >= 3` and `b ∈ {0, 1}`, the Torelli group `I_g^b` is finitely generated
(Johnson, 1983). Putman later gave generating sets whose size grows cubically in
`g` (arXiv:1106.3294). The import is
`surface-torelli-fg-in-genus-at-least-three-citation`.

Role in Zaremsky Problem 3.1: in the open genera `g >= 3` the question is about
relations only. In genus 2 finite generation fails
(`genus-two-torelli-group-infinitely-generated`).
