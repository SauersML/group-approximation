Take finitely many finite Pauli packets and their finite Clifford semidirect
products.  Glue them only by injective finite-subgroup amalgams and HNN stable
letters implementing packet automorphisms or subgroup isomorphisms.  Then the
resulting presentation is the fundamental group of a finite graph of finite
groups.

Consequently it has a finite-dimensional representation whose restriction to
every vertex is a positive integer multiple of that vertex's regular
representation and whose edge restrictions agree exactly.  Every local
marked packet type, including every unwanted multiplicity-twist extension of
a Clifford implementer, occurs with positive mass in this representation.

In particular, no dimension-independent inequality of the form

```text
covariance mismatch >= c (controlled forbidden mass),       c>0, (CFR1)
```

and no absorbing constant-depth contraction can follow from the covariance
relations and their finite restriction tables alone.  The mismatch side is
zero on the regular stationary flow while the controlled forbidden mass is
positive.

Pauli completion therefore solves the ordinary-group realization of a
controlled block **as covariance**, but a successful nested reset must add a
relation not represented by a one-dimensional graph of finite groups.  It
must constrain the edge intertwiners on their multiplicity spaces through a
two-cell/holonomy word, a property-`(T)` adjoint wall, or equivalent global
contextual operator data.
