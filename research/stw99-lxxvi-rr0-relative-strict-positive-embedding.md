Let D be separable, simple, stably finite, pure, and real rank zero;
put B=D tensor K. Let X be finite-dimensional compact metrizable,
Y closed in X, and c,d in C(X,B)_+ be contractions. Suppose
[c(x)]<=[d(x)] everywhere, with strict inequality off Y whenever c(x)
is nonzero. Then every norm-continuous z_Y:Y -> B satisfying

```text
z_Y* z_Y=c|Y,   z_Y(y) in closure(d(y)B)
```

extends to z in C(X,B) with z*z=c and zz* in Her(d).

In particular, the precise relative cutoff lemma holds: prescribed
embeddings of (a-epsilon/2)_+ on Y extend, after multiplication on Y
by sqrt((a-epsilon)_+/(a-epsilon/2)_+), to an embedding of
(a-epsilon)_+ over X whenever [a(x)]<[b(x)] off Y at nonzero source
fibers.

Uniform local connectivity is proved only for strict embedding spaces
and singleton prescribed boundary values. It is not asserted for the
unrestricted family of equal-class positive-source embedding spaces.
The theorem concerns comparison and exact extension; mixed target Cu
realization remains separate.
