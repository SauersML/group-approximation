**Statement.** Assume hypothesis (T) of `one-hole-toeplitz-subshifts-have-linear-cost-towers`, with p_{k+1}/p_k ≤ ρ. Then for r ≥ 100|A| + 1920ρ|A| + 480,

`L_X(r) ≥ 2^{18((r − 100|A|)/(1920ρ|A| + 480) − 1)²}`,

where L_X is the LEF growth of EL_3(LC(X,F_2)⋊Z).

For the period-doubling subshift: L_X(r) ≥ 2^{18((r − 200)/8160 − 1)²}.
