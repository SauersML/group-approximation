**Established by citation.** Chun Guang Li and Zhuang Niu, *Stable rank of
C(X) ⋊ Gamma*, [arXiv:2008.03361v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.03361)
(15 July 2023). Read from the PDF text on MSI, 2026-09-11:

- Theorem 7.8: a free minimal `Gamma`-action with (URP) and (COS) gives a
  crossed product of stable rank one.
- Corollary 7.9: "Let Z^d act freely and minimally on a compact Hausdorff
  space X. Then tsr(C(X) ⋊ Z^d) = 1." The introduction records that any
  free and minimal `Z^d`-action has (URP) and (COS).
- Corollary 7.11(3), for free minimal `Z^d`: "The canonical map
  U(A)/U_0(A) -> K_1(A) is an isomorphism." The introduction states that
  these consequences hold for every free minimal action with (URP) and (COS).

The K1 conclusion is also Rieffel's Theorem 10.12 applied to stable rank one
(`stable-rank-one-unital-algebras-are-k1-bijective`).
