The words `w_k = [α, α^(g_1^(-k))]` have length `4k + 4` and are null-homotopic.
So `δ_(H_n)(4k+4) >= Area(w_k)`. If `Area(w_k)` is not bounded by any polynomial
in `k`, then `δ_(H_n)` is not bounded by any polynomial.
