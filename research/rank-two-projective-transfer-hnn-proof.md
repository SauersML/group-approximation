**Hypotheses of the general theorem.**

*Finite presentation.*  By Ihara's theorem (Serre, *Trees*, Chapter II),
`SL_2(Z[1/p])` is the amalgamated free product of two copies of
`SL_2(Z)` over the Hecke congruence subgroup `Gamma_0(p)`.  `SL_2(Z)` is
finitely presented (it is `Z/4 *_(Z/2) Z/6`), and `Gamma_0(p)` has index
`p+1` in it, so it is finitely generated.  An amalgamated free product of
two finitely presented groups over a finitely generated subgroup is
finitely presented: take both presentations and add one identification
relation for each generator of the edge group.  Hence `A` has a finite
presentation, and the statement may fix any one of them.

(Referee addition, 2026-09-16: the book was not fetched, so here is the
Bass--Serre derivation of the amalgam from standard tree facts.
`G=SL_2(Q_p)` acts without inversion on the Bruhat--Tits tree `T` of
`Q_p^2`, with one edge `e=[v_0,v_1]` as fundamental domain, where
`v_0=[Z_p^2]` and `v_1=[Z_p direct_sum pZ_p]`; the stabilizers are open.
`Z[1/p]` is dense in `Q_p`, so the elementary subgroups of `A` are dense in
those of `G`, and `A` is dense in `G`.  For any vertex or edge `x` and any
`g in G`, the open coset `g Stab_G(x)` meets `A`, so `A x=G x`; thus `e` is
also a fundamental domain for `A`.  The stabilizers in `A` are
`A cap SL_2(Z_p)=SL_2(Z)` (since `Z[1/p] cap Z_p=Z`), its conjugate
`d SL_2(Z) d^-1` by `d=diag(1,p)` (conjugation by `d` preserves `A`), and
their intersection `{c equiv 0 mod p}=Gamma_0(p)`.  The structure theorem
for groups acting on trees with an edge as fundamental domain gives
`A=SL_2(Z) *_(Gamma_0(p)) d SL_2(Z) d^-1`.)

*Residual finiteness, hence hyperlinearity.*  Let `g in A` with `g!=1`.
Write `g-I=p^(-m)B` with `m>=0` and `B` a nonzero integer matrix, and pick
a prime `l!=p` not dividing some nonzero entry of `B`.  Since `p` is
invertible modulo `l`, reduction modulo `l` is a homomorphism
`A->SL_2(F_l)`, and it sends `g` to `I+p^(-m)B!=I`.  Given finitely many
nontrivial `g_1,...,g_n`, the product of the corresponding reductions is
a homomorphism `q_n` to a finite group separating all of them.  The left
regular representation of the finite quotient composed with `q_n` is an
honest unitary representation of `A`, hence a zero-defect assignment of
any presentation, and its normalized trace vanishes at `g_1,...,g_n`.
Enumerating `A\{1}` yields the microstates used in Step 1 of
`adjoint-regular-rcc-projective-transfer-proof`; in particular `A` is
hyperlinear.

*Generation.*  By `smith-prime-localization-maximality-and-word-transfer`
`(SPL1)`, for `n=2`, every `h in A\C` satisfies `<C,h>=A`, which is
`(AR0)`.

**Chaining.**  `adjoint-regular-rcc-gives-projective-transfer` now applies
to `A=<X|R>`, `S` and `h`: assertions 2, 3 and 4 of the present claim are
`(AR1)`, `(AR3)` and `(AR2)` there, once assertion 2 is matched with
`(AR1)`.

That match is standard.  A canonical trace-preserving embedding
`pi:L(A)->Q` restricts to a unitary representation of `A` with
`tau(pi(g))=0` for `g!=1`.  Conversely a unitary representation of `A` in
`Q` with this trace generates a von Neumann subalgebra whose trace agrees
with the canonical trace on the group algebra, so by uniqueness of the GNS
construction it extends to a normal trace-preserving embedding of `L(A)`.
In either direction, an element of `Q` commutes with `pi(C)` if and only
if it commutes with the von Neumann algebra `pi(L(C))` generated by it
(commutation passes to `*`-algebraic combinations and weak limits), and
likewise for `A`.  Hence `(AR1)` is assertion 2.

By `arithmetic-hnn-nonhyperlinearity-equals-global-collapse` with `n=2`,
assertion 2 is equivalent to assertion 1.

**The qualitative modulus.**  Assertion 4 gives assertion 5 with `K=L` and
`eta(delta)=L delta`.  Conversely, under assertion 5, every sequence with
`delta(rho_n)->0` and `q(rho_n,U_n)->0` has
`e_h(rho_n,U_n)<=K q(rho_n,U_n)+eta(delta(rho_n))->0`, so there is no
sequence as in `(PL3)` of
`projective-transfer-linear-bound-equals-no-ultraproduct-leak`; that
claim then gives `(PL2)`, which is assertion 4.

**The twisted-leak remark.**  If assignments with `delta->0` carry unitaries
whose source commutators approach scalars (for example a nontrivial
character of `C`) while `e_h` stays bounded below, assertion 5 fails,
hence assertion 1 fails, so `H` is hyperlinear.  The explicit untwisted
regular-trace leak is `(Ad(U_n tensor I))_omega` from Steps 1--3 of the
general route.  In the ultraproduct the limiting source scalars
`[u,pi(c)]=lambda(c)` are multiplicative in `c`, since
`u pi(c c') u^*=lambda(c)lambda(c')pi(c c')`, so they form a character of
`C`, of order dividing `12`.  The argument never uses this order bound;
only `q->0` matters.

No assertion of the claim is decided: every step is an equivalence.
