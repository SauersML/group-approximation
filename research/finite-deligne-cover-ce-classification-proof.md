The central `Z/q` Fourier idempotents split the group von Neumann algebra of
the `q`-fold Deligne cover into the `q` scalar-twisted Maslov algebras at
parameters `j/q`. Hyperlinearity is equivalent to Connes embeddability of
all finite summands. Since the CE parameters form a subgroup, membership of
`1/q` is equivalent to membership of all `j/q`. An accumulating sequence of
CE parameters forces the whole circle; conversely hyperlinear covers of
unbounded order contribute torsion parameters of unbounded order and hence
an accumulation at zero. If the CE subgroup is finite, a prime outside its
order gives a nonhyperlinear finite cover.
