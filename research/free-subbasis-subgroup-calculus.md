ESTABLISHED, for an arbitrary free group and an arbitrary index set.  Write
`basisSubgroup S` for the subgroup of `FreeGroup i` generated by the basis
elements indexed by `S`.  Then

* `of_mem_basisSubgroup_iff` -- `FreeGroup.of a` lies in `basisSubgroup S`
  exactly when `a` lies in `S`;
* `basisSubgroup_inf` -- `basisSubgroup S` meet `basisSubgroup T` equals
  `basisSubgroup (S cap T)`;
* `map_basisSubgroup` and `range_indexMap` -- how such subgroups transport
  along a relabelling of the index set.

All of it runs off one construction, the retraction `retract S` fixing the
basis elements in `S` and killing the rest.  Nothing in it is specific to the
Novikov--Boone chain, and nothing in it is conditional.

## Why these three and no others

They are exactly the free-group facts the Aanderaa--Cohen construction
consumes, and each is consumed at a named place.  Membership is the last line
of Simpson's Theorem 8, where `t(a,b) in T_M` has to be turned back into
`(a,b) in H_M`.  Intersection is what reduces the good-subgroup verifications
-- Simpson's Definition 2 applied to `T` and to `T_M` -- to statements about
index sets, which is the reduction that makes [[boone-halting-index-good-parameter-set]]
free of HNN theory.  Transport is how the associated isomorphisms of Simpson's
Definition 6 act on basis indices.

The alternative to having these is the Kurosh subgroup theorem, which Mathlib
does not have; avoiding it is why the base group is modelled as a split
extension rather than presented (see [[boone-base-group-and-lemma-five]]).
