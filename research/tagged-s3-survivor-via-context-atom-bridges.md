There are finitely many predicate occurrences at fixed machine index.  For
each one, take a free Thompson orbit of copies of its complete commuting
context tuple.  At level zero identify that tuple with the retained original
BCS words.  Attach the fixed Schur/S3 packet and use the context-atom bridge
to send every active endpoint atom into the same Boolean atom of the next
context copy, below the fresh survivor cut.  Send the complementary endpoint
atom into the first-hit tag.

The child packet used at the next step is fresh and context-local.  Its
endpoint is not the previous tag.  Consequently the next bridge may commute
with all previous tags while transporting the new endpoint to the next fresh
tag.  This is the separation that the invalid free-occurrence HNN route was
missing.

The pointwise oriented identities give `(BFE1)--(BFE2)`.  The context-atom
bridge places the retained active copy in `q_(n+1)` and gives the polynomial
placement error.  The packet payment/source certificate gives `(BFE3)`, and
the two-bit prefix telescope gives `(BFE4)`.  Since the context menu and its
arities are finite, the sum of all local exactification and bridge losses is
bounded by a computable polynomial in the level times `sqrt(E)`.

Different original contexts receive separate ladders.  They are amalgamated
only at their level-zero retained logical words, so the construction does not
add cross-context commutators.  Countable amplification and spectral matching
extend every perfect marked context model across all ladder bridges.
