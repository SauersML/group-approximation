title: "Only countably many finitely generated groups have r.e. or co-r.e. word problem"
---

Up to isomorphism there are only countably many finitely generated groups
whose word problem is recursively enumerable or co-recursively enumerable.
This does not depend on the finite generating set. Consequently, in any
uncountable family of pairwise non-isomorphic finitely generated groups, all
but countably many members have word problem that is neither r.e. nor co-r.e.

For simple groups the r.e. case is already special: by Kuznetsov, a finitely
generated simple group with r.e. word problem (a recursively presented one)
has solvable word problem.
