Let `P=C direct_sum C` with its average trace and let `N=C1`.  Then `N<P`
properly.  Every pair of elements of `P` commutes.  If self-adjoint unitaries
`v,w` anticommuted, commutativity and anticommutation would imply
`vw=-vw`, hence `2vw=0`; this is impossible because `vw` is a unitary.
Therefore this proper finite tracial inclusion contains no Majorana pair.
