The four copy retractions of the degree-four shared-triangle boundary admit
an exact order-sixteen solution with images `(w^8,1,1,1)`, where `w^8!=1`.
Nevertheless its full free-product normal form has sixteen blocks, so it is
not the one-copy word `w^8`.  Hence solving all retraction equations does not
close the shared-carrier triangle.
