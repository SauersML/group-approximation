For the specified bijective-base P=A_5*A_5 enumeration, put
B=V_(59,2)(<a>), with the odometer character chi from the index
claim. The near group R_nu embeds in B, preserving its index
character and sending the shift s to the map exchanging the two
roots with an odometer carry on the second root.

Let F_B=B x_Z B using chi on both factors. It is finitely presented.
In B wr C_2, let omega exchange the factors and put tau_B=(s,1)*omega.
Then D=<F_B,tau_B> contains F_B with index two and is finitely
presented. The full shell germ group Q embeds in D, compatibly with
Q_0=R_nu x_Z R_nu and tau->tau_B.

These are embeddings of the NEAR group and the GERM quotient.
Finite presentation is not inherited by their subgroups R_nu or Q.
No embedding of the whole Cantor shell envelope E_nu into D, nor
a finite presentation of E_nu, follows from these statements.
