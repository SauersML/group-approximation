On the fiber `ell^2(Gamma/H)`, the coefficient of the base vector is
`<delta_H,g delta_H>=1_(gH=H)=1_(g in H)`.  Integrating proves `(IMC2)`.
The coefficient is real, so the cyclic real span supplies the stated real
orthogonal representation if desired.

Covariance gives
`U_g C U_g^*=c(pi_mu(g)xi)`.  The Clifford two-point formula `(IMC3)` then
proves `(IMC5)`.  Equation `(IMC6)` follows from the same covariance.
Products `V_gV_h` contain four generally noncancelling Majoranas, whereas
`V_(gh)` contains two, so the assignment is not a representation.  The
canonical crossed-product expectation kills every nonidentity Fourier
coefficient, proving the regular-trace assertion for `U_g` and its fixed
unitary conjugate.
