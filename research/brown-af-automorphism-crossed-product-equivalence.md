Let `B` be a unital separable AF algebra and let `beta` be an automorphism of
`B`.  The following are equivalent:

1. `B rtimes_beta Z` is AF-embeddable;
2. `B rtimes_beta Z` is quasidiagonal;
3. `B rtimes_beta Z` is stably finite.
