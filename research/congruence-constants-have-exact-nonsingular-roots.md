title: Nonsingular equations with constants in a p-adic principal congruence subgroup have exact roots when p does not divide the exponent sum, so simultaneously unipotent tuples satisfy the characteristic-zero root premise at amplification one
distinct_from:
  amplified-algebraic-roots-in-characteristic-zero: that asks for approximate amplified roots for every tuple over every algebraically closed field of characteristic 0; this gives exact roots at amplification 1 only for tuples that embed p-adically near the identity, which include every simultaneously unipotent tuple.
  kervaire-laudenbach-holds-for-hyperlinear: that solves equations with constants in a compact unitary group by a degree argument; this solves them with constants in a pro-p congruence group by successive approximation, which reaches tuples that are not unitarizable.

**ESTABLISHED** through `congruence-constants-have-exact-nonsingular-roots-proof`
(successive approximation and the weak Nullstellensatz; unreviewed, no novelty
claimed).

**Setup.** Fix a prime `p`, a finite extension `K` of `Q_p` with valuation ring
`O`, uniformizer `pi` and residue field `k = O/pi O`. For `j >= 1` put
`Gamma_j = 1 + pi^j M_n(O)`. For `w` in `F_k * <x>` write `w(T ; a)` for its
value at `x = T` and constants `a = (a_1, ..., a_k)`, and `m` for the exponent
sum of `w` in `x`.

**Theorem A (p-adic).** If `a_1, ..., a_k` lie in `Gamma_1` and `p` does not
divide `m`, then there is exactly one `T` in `Gamma_1` with `w(T ; a) = 1`.

**Theorem B (characteristic 0).** Let `L` be an algebraically closed field of
characteristic `0`, let `a` be in `GL_n(L)^k`, and let `F` be the subfield of
`L` generated by the entries of the `a_j`. Suppose that there are a prime `p`
not dividing `m`, a finite extension `K` of `Q_p`, a field embedding
`iota : F --> K`, and `g` in `GL_n(K)` with `g iota(a_j) g^{-1}` in `Gamma_1`
for every `j`. Then `w(T ; a) = 1` has a solution `T` in `GL_n(L)`.

**Corollary C (unipotent constants).** If `a_1, ..., a_k` in `GL_n(L)` are
upper unitriangular in a common basis, then every `w` with `m != 0` has an
exact root in `GL_n(L)`. So these tuples satisfy the conclusion of
`amplified-algebraic-roots-in-characteristic-zero` with `r = 1` and
`epsilon = 0`.

**Corollary D (triangular constants).** Let `a_1, ..., a_k` in `GL_n(L)` be
upper triangular in a common basis, and fix `m != 0`. Suppose there are a prime
`p` not dividing `m`, a finite extension `K` of `Q_p` and a field embedding
`iota` of the field generated by the entries into `K` with
`|iota(d) - 1|_p < 1` for every diagonal entry `d` of every `a_j`. Then every
`w` with exponent sum `m` has an exact root in `GL_n(L)`. Examples: the affine
tuple `diag(z, 1)`, `1 + E_12` with `z` transcendental (all `m`), and
`diag(3, 1)`, `1 + E_12` (odd `m`, with `p = 2`).

**What this does not reach.** The hypothesis of Theorem B puts the group
generated by `a` inside a pro-`p` group, so all its torsion has `p`-power
order and all eigenvalues of its elements are `p`-adically close to `1`. It
therefore misses, for example:
- `diag(2, 1)`, `1 + E_12` (the affine tuple of `BS(1,2)`), since `2 - 1` is a
  unit at every prime;
- `1 + E_12`, `1 + E_21`, which generate `SL_2(Z)`, whose torsion has orders
  `3` and `4`.

For these, `amplified-algebraic-roots-in-characteristic-zero` stays open.

**Contrast with characteristic `p`.** `amplified-roots-fail-in-odd-characteristic`
has constants of order `p` over a field of characteristic `p`, where `m = 1` is
invertible but `(s - 1)^p = s^p - 1`. Here the constants live in
characteristic `0` and only their reductions have characteristic `p`. The
successive approximation needs `m` to be a unit in `O`, not in `L`.
