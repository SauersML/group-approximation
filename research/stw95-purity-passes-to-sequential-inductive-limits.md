Let

```text
A_1 -> A_2 -> ...
```

be any sequential inductive system of C*-algebras, with no injectivity
assumption on the connecting maps.  If every `A_n` is pure, then its
inductive limit is pure.

Equivalently, sequential inductive limits in the category `Cu` preserve
simultaneous almost unperforation and almost divisibility.
