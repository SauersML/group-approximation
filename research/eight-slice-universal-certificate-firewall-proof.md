For the packet projections, canonical trace gives
`tau(S)-tau(C)=tau(P_f)/16>0`.  For every group word `W`, positivity and
unitary invariance imply

```text
Theta_W=tau(SW^*CWS)<=tau(C),
tau(S)-Theta_W>=tau(P_f)/16.                            (1)
```

In the regular representation every defining relator has zero defect.
Thus an inequality bounding the left side of `(1)` by any finite multiple
of relator energy fails immediately.  A cyclic sum-of-squares identity
modulo the relator ideal would give exactly such a universally tracial
inequality.  Likewise a finite-level NPA dual certificate is valid in every
operator model of the displayed relations, including the regular one, and
is therefore impossible.  The argument does not exclude an inequality
valid only in matrices or their tracial ultraproducts.
