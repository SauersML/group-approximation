The quotient `E / <w>` of the literal finitely presented non-MF group by its
marked central involution is MF in the norm-matrix-corona sense.

## Ideas

**What the group is.**  By `literal-lamp-kernel-clifford-block-amalgam`,

```text
E/<w> = ( *_{i in I} (Z/2)^8 ) semidirect V ,
```

a free product of elementary abelian blocks over the block set `I`, which is
`V`-isomorphic to the site set `X`.  Collapsing each block to a single
elementary abelian lamp group over `X` — equivalently, making all sites
commute rather than only those in a common block — gives `W/<zeta>` of
`commuting-lamp-quotient-structure`.  So `E/<w>` surjects onto `W/<zeta>`,
and `w` is not in that kernel.

**Position relative to the companion claim.**  This claim carries two
burdens: the one analyzed in `commuting-lamp-quotient-mf`, and the free-product
structure of the lamp kernel, which is not locally finite — it contains
infinite dihedral subgroups generated by two same-level lamps in different
blocks, so the finite residual computed for `W/<zeta>` has here an
infinite-order analogue.  MF does not pass to quotients
(`mf-quotient-nonclosure`), so this claim does not formally imply the
companion one; but a single negative answer to `corona-commutant-no-growth`
refutes both.

**New route out.**  `literal-mark-quotient-detects-defect`: because `u` is
not in `{1,w}`, this claim implies `unsquared-defect-mf-visible` on its own,
independently of `commuting-lamp-quotient-mf`.  That makes
`unsquared-defect-mf-visible` the common consequence of both open quotient
claims and the right place to look for a refutation.

**Routes that are closed.**  `marked-quotients-not-lef` applies verbatim to
`E/<w>`: the compression and centralizer relators survive in the quotient and
`u` does not die there, so no exact finite model separates it, and `E/<w>` is
neither residually finite nor LEF.  As for the companion claim, amenability
is unavailable and no `Z`-extension permanence theorem can help
(`mf-not-closed-under-integer-extensions`).  Note especially that `literal-telescope-core-lef` makes `E/<w>` a
`Z`-extension of an LEF, hence MF, core — but so is `E` itself, and `E` is
not MF.  So "the core is already MF, only the stable letter remains" is not
a partial proof of this claim: by `mf-not-closed-under-integer-extensions`
the stable-letter step is the entire difficulty, and it is exactly where the
two known negative answers live.

**What is now proved about this group.**
`literal-mark-quotient-finite-residual`: every finite quotient identifies all
lamps of a common telescope level, the finite residual is exactly the
level-collapse kernel `R`, and `(E/<w>)/R = (*_{n in Z} Z/2) semidirect V` is
residually finite.  `literal-mark-quotient-corona-blindness`: every corona
representation is trace-blind on `R` and on nothing else.  So this claim is
now the same shape as its companion — separate a trace-invisible level
collapse in operator norm — with `R` non-locally-finite here and elementary
abelian there.

**Central-extension criterion.**  `central-extension-twisted-sector-criterion`
imports the unused criterion of `notes/NOTEPAD.md:39199-39232`.  Applied to
`(E,<w>)` it restates Theorem A as emptiness of the twisted sector over this
group, and imposes no constraint on the present claim.  It should not be
mistaken for an obstruction.

**Side effect on the stability question.**  `marked-quotients-not-lef`
resolves the first disjunct of the trichotomy recorded in
`literal-mark-quotient-flexibly-stable`: `E/<w>` is not residually finite, so
soficity of `E` plus the Gohla--Thom theorem gives no information about its
permutation stability.

**Order of work.**  Work on `commuting-lamp-quotient-mf` first.  It is the
same analytic problem with the group-theoretic unknowns removed, and a
positive answer there is a prerequisite in spirit — a faithful corona model
of `E/<w>` restricts to one of every subgroup and pushes no information
through the unknown kernel, so nothing about `E/<w>` is easier.

**A second, non-exact-model route.**  `literal-mark-quotient-via-shulman-amalgam`:
Shulman's Theorem 20 (`shulman-amalgam-mf-criterion`, source-verified) makes
`A *_C B` MF exactly when the two vertex algebras admit corona embeddings
agreeing on `C`.  Applied to `V *_{B_1} ((Z/2)^8 sd B_1)` this asks for
`amalgam-edge-compatible-corona-embeddings`.  That datum implies commutant
growth as well, so the two routes meet at the same hinge; it also costs more
than the group claim needs, since Theorem 20 requires both vertex algebras to
be MF and both contain a property-(T) group.

**The hinge is exact.**  `marked-quotient-mf-forces-commutant-growth`: MF of
this group forces operator-norm commutant growth at the compressor, witnessed
by a single lamp.  So `corona-commutant-no-growth` decides this claim,
`commuting-lamp-quotient-mf` and `kun-thom-wreath-weak-mf` together.

**Best next unit.**  `corona-commutant-no-growth`, then, if that fails, the
edge-compatibility datum of the amalgam route.

**RESOLVED NEGATIVELY (2026-08-15).**  The negation is established:
`literal-mark-quotient-not-mf` — the image of `d = tct^{-1}` is an
involutive compression witness inside the level-one `(Z/2)^8` block of
the block-amalgam structure, so the involutive collapse places the image
of `u` in the radical.  Consequently `u in Rad_MF(E)`
(`unsquared-defect-mf-invisible`) and `Rad_MF(E) > {1, w}`.  This claim
can never be established; retained as the record of the question.
