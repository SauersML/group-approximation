For the explicit q=2 Kun--Thom double `D = G *_Gamma G`:

* `kt-double-reduced-cstar-is-mf` makes `C*_r(D)` MF;
* `kt-q2-double-has-trivial-amenable-radical` makes `R_a(D)` trivial, hence
  finite;
* `kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic` makes `D` nonsofic.
