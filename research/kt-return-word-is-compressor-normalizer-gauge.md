**REFUTED.**  One fixed `delta in Gamma` with
`h^(-1)delta h notin Gamma` gives a normalized-HS witness.  The conjugate of
`phi(delta)` by the literal return has edge expectation tending to zero,
whereas conjugation by every edge normalizer produces a unitary inside the
edge algebra.  Consequently the literal return, and its unitary polar
correction when the return expectation vanishes, stay at distance at least
`1/2-o(1)` from the entire edge normalizer.
