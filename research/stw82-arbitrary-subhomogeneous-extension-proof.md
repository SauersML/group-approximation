Write

```text
n = max(dim_nuc(I), dim_nuc(D)).
```

If `n` is infinite, ideal and quotient monotonicity immediately gives the
claimed equality.  Suppose `n<infinity`.

First note that the middle algebra is subhomogeneous, without any
separability assumption.  If `I` is `r`-subhomogeneous and `D` is
`s`-subhomogeneous, take an irreducible representation `pi:E->B(H)`.  If
`pi(I)=0`, then `pi` factors irreducibly through `D`, so `dim(H)<=s`.  If
`pi(I)` is nonzero, then `pi|I` is irreducible: the closed space
`closure(pi(I)H)` is a nonzero invariant subspace and hence all of `H`, and
the ideal approximate-unit argument shows that every operator commuting
with `pi(I)` commutes with `pi(E)`.  Thus `dim(H)<=r`.  Hence `E` is
`max(r,s)`-subhomogeneous.  Subhomogeneity also passes to C*-subalgebras
(equivalently, use the degree-`2r` standard polynomial identity).

Fix a finite set `Omega` in `E` and a positive tolerance.  Apply the exact
separable-hull construction proved in
`stw82-counterexamples-reflect-to-separable-extensions`.  It gives a
separable exact subextension

```text
0 -> I_0 -> E_0 -> D_0 -> 0
```

containing `Omega`, with

```text
dim_nuc(I_0), dim_nuc(D_0) <= n.
```

Because subhomogeneity passes to C*-subalgebras, `I_0` and `D_0` are
subhomogeneous.  The separable subhomogeneous extension theorem gives

```text
dim_nuc(E_0)
  = max(dim_nuc(I_0), dim_nuc(D_0))
  <= n.
```

Therefore `Omega` has an `n`-coloured nuclear-dimension approximation inside
`E_0`, hence inside `E`.  Since `Omega` and the tolerance were arbitrary,
`dim_nuc(E)<=n`.  Endpoint monotonicity supplies the reverse inequality.

For commutative endpoints one can additionally see directly that `E` is
commutative: the left-multiplication images of two elements of `E` commute
in the commutative multiplier algebra `M(I)`, so their commutator lies in
`I` and annihilates `I`; multiplying it by its adjoint forces it to vanish.
Thus the commutative case is the degree-one instance of the theorem.

### Stress checks

* The exactness condition `I_0=E_0 intersect I` is essential.  Merely taking
  a separable algebra generated by `Omega` does not provide it; inequality
  `(1)` in the reflection proof is precisely what closes the asymptotic
  kernel.
* The argument never asserts nuclear dimension is monotone for arbitrary
  C*-subalgebras.  The endpoint dimension bounds come from the dedicated
  nuclear-dimension hull lemma, while subhomogeneity alone passes to
  subalgebras.
* No dimension is silently assumed finite: the infinite-endpoint case is
  disposed of before the finite-colour construction.
* No metric or countability property is imposed on the original primitive
  ideal spaces.  Metrizability enters only after reflection, where the
  separable theorem legitimately applies.
