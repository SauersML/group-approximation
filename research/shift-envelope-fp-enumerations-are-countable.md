title: For any fixed infinite finitely generated input, only countably many enumerations produce finitely presented shift envelopes
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/boone-higman-zigzag-shift-compiler-2026-09-08.md
---

Fix an infinite finitely generated group P. Among all bijections nu:P->N,
only countably many can give a finitely presented E_nu(P).

More generally, each fixed countable abstract group is isomorphic to
E_nu(P) for at most countably many nu. Consequently varying nu for
this one fixed P gives continuum many isomorphism classes of envelopes.

This is a statement about all bijections, including noncomputable ones.
It does not show that a computable good enumeration cannot be found:
the collection of computable enumerations is itself countable. It does
not refute the existential infinite-input compiler.
