Conjugate the first Heisenberg representation and its intertwiner system by
the signed Weyl chart map.  Stone--von Neumann uniqueness says the resulting
intertwiners differ from the chosen second-chart intertwiners by scalars
`b(g)`.  Multiplying two such identities gives `(TWC2)`, so the restricted
factor sets differ by `delta b`.  Opposite-root duality inverts the factor
set, while the metaplectic factor set is `mu_2`-valued in cohomology, hence
unchanged by inversion.  Rephase by `b` and use one inverse twisted regular
representation to cancel both systems.  Formula `(TWC4)` separately proves
that without overlap identification even unrelated finite factor sets admit
a joint tensor-product gauge.
