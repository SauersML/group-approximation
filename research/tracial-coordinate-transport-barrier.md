Arbitrary tracial coordinates do not support the full one-sided-compression
architecture.  There are two independent failures.

1. **Transport may already fail.**  In standard form the fixed-space
   projection belongs to the algebra generated by left and right
   multiplication, but that algebra need not be finite.  For an infinite
   factor it is `B(L^2 M)`.  Constant coordinates `C*_r(E)` give an explicit
   exact counterexample to arbitrary faithfully-traced-coordinate transport.
2. **Tracial corner renormalization has an uncontrolled cost.**  Even when
   transport is available in matrix coordinates, passing from a global
   normalized 2-norm to a corner of trace `delta` costs `delta^(-1/2)`.
   The negative spectral sector of a mark may have vanishing density.
3. **The desired tracial conclusion is false in general.**  The literal mark
   lies in the operator-norm MF residual but outside the hyperlinear residual,
   so no universal tracial analogue can kill it in every tracial model.

The corrected positive boundary is `left-right-finite-coordinate-obstruction`:
operator-norm models over faithfully traced coordinates whose left-right
algebras are finite.
