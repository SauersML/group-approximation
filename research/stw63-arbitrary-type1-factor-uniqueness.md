Let `H` be an arbitrary Hilbert space.  Then every two unital embeddings

```text
phi,psi:Z->B(H)
```

are approximately unitarily equivalent in norm.  No separability or cardinal
bound is imposed on `H`.

For finite-dimensional `H` the statement is vacuous, since the simple
infinite-dimensional algebra `Z` has no unital map to `B(H)`.
