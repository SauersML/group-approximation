Put `K = Gamma_1^2` and `G = K x Z`.  The proof route
`titz-witzel-exact-kazhdan-mf-radical-over-z-proof` takes exactly this
product for any simple Titz Mite--Witzel kernel.  It gives:

* `G` finitely presented and torsion-free;
* `K` finitely presented, infinite, simple and Kazhdan;
* `Rad_MF(G) = K` and `G/K = Z`.

If `K` is sofic, so is `G`, as a direct product of sofic groups.  This is
`(TFZ1)`.  It includes the preferred strengthening: `K` is finitely
presented and normally generated by any nonidentity element.

This route is the only place where the Titz--Witzel region feeds the goal,
and it waits entirely on `titz-witzel-kernel-sofic`.
