Notation as in the target. `R = F_q[t,t^-1]`.

**Step 1 (the ring).** In `S/I`, `x_2` is a two-sided inverse of `x_1`, and `x_3` commutes with
`x_1` and `x_2`. The generators `x_j`, `j >= 4`, vanish. So `S/I` is commutative and generated by
`x_1^(±1)` and `x_3`, and it is a quotient of `F_p[x_3]/(f)[x_1, x_1^-1] = F_q[t,t^-1]`. The map
`S -> R` given in the target kills `I` and induces a surjection `S/I -> R`. The composite
`R -> S/I -> R` is the identity on generators, so `S/I ≅ R`. `I` is finitely generated by
construction.

**Step 2 (the quotient).** Let `π : St_n(S) -> St_n(R)` be the induced map.
- Comparing presentations, `ker π` is the normal closure of `{x_ij(a) : a ∈ I}`.
- `E_n(S) = St_n(S)/K_2(n,S)`, and `E_n(S,I)` is the image of that normal closure.
- Hence `Q_I = St_n(S) / (K_2(n,S) · ker π) = St_n(R) / π(K_2(n,S))`.
- `π(K_2(n,S)) ⊆ K_2(n,R)`, since its image in `SL_n(R)` is the image of the trivial matrix group.

**Step 3 (the image dies).** Consider the commutative square of ring maps `S -> R -> F_q(t)` and
the maps of Steinberg groups they induce, together with stabilization `K_2(n,-) -> K_2(-)`, which is
natural in ring homomorphisms. For `y ∈ K_2(n,S)`, the image of `π(y)` in `K_2(F_q(t))` equals the
image of `[y] ∈ K_2(S)` under `K_2(S) -> K_2(F_q(t))`.
- By `gersten-free-algebra-k-theory` (`Z` and `F_p` are noetherian regular), `[y] = ι(z)` for some
  `z ∈ K_2(A)`, where `ι : A -> S` is the inclusion.
- The composite `A -> S -> F_q(t)` factors through `A -> F_p`. By
  `quillen-k-theory-of-finite-fields`, `K_2(F_p) = 0`. So `[y]` maps to `0` in `K_2(F_q(t))`.
- The map `K_2(n,R) -> K_2(n,F_q(t))` is injective, by
  `laurent-unstable-k2-splits-by-tame-symbol` item 2 with `k = F_q`.
- The map `K_2(n,F_q(t)) -> K_2(F_q(t))` is injective, by item 3 of the same claim.
- So `π(y) = 1`.

Hence `π(K_2(n,S)) = 1` and `Q_I ≅ St_n(R)`. ∎

**Remark.** Only `x_1, x_2, x_3` are used, so the claim holds for every `k >= 3`. For `q = p`,
`x_3` is unnecessary and `k >= 2` suffices. The target claim
`free-algebra-elementary-group-is-flexibly-hs-stable` takes `k >= 4`, which is covered.
