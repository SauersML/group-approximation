The corrected aggregate inequality `(IAG1)` is false. More precisely, for
arbitrarily long fourth-power orbit lengths `L` there are exact square-free
Iwahori endpoints `(X,R,T)` and one-parameter perturbations

```text
z_epsilon=exp(i epsilon a),       a=a^* in {T}',
X_epsilon=z_epsilon X z_epsilon^(-1)                  (LKC1)
```

such that

```text
X_epsilon^2=1,          (X_epsilon T)^3=1             (LKC2)
```

exactly, while, with `g_L=|1-exp(2 pi i/L)|`,

```text
||(X_epsilon R)^2-1||_2<=2 epsilon g_L,
||(X_epsilon T^2R)^3-1||_2<=3 epsilon g_L.            (LKC3)
```

Let `e_epsilon` be the Weyl residual, conditionally projected to
`Z={T}'`, centered, and stripped of the `psi=Ad(R^(-1))` fixed algebra in
the convention of `(IAG1)`. If `P_t` are its dyadic Koopman bands, then
for every sufficiently large odd `L` and all sufficiently small positive
`epsilon` (the threshold may depend on `L`),

```text
sum_t t^(-2)||P_t e_epsilon||_2^2 >= epsilon^2/32.     (LKC4)
```

The total squared presentation-row defect is at most
`13 epsilon^2 g_L^2`. Since `g_L<=2 pi/L`, no dimension-free constant can
satisfy `(IAG1)`.

The examples may be taken in left regular representations of the finite
reductions generated by the standard matrices in
`PSL_2(Z/(4^L-1)Z)`. Along odd `L -> infinity`, choose
`epsilon_L -> 0` below the finite-dimensional differentiability threshold.
The exact quotient characters converge to the regular character of
`PSL_2(Z[1/2])`, and `(LKC1)` moves each fixed word by `O(epsilon_L)`.
Thus the perturbed tuples also have regular limiting character. This is
not a nonregular packet or a compressed Weil boundary effect.

## Why endpoint-energy minimization does not repair the estimate

Coboundary solutions form the right coset

```text
c U({R,T}').                                            (LKC5)
```

Distance minimization merely picks a canonical representative of this
coset. At the tangent of `(LKC1)`, the minimum inverse-Koopman preimage of
the regular projected Weyl residual has norm at least
`1-(4^L-1)^(-1/2)`, whereas every presentation row has derivative
`O(g_L)`. Hence an energy-minimizing preimage is not `O(delta)`.

This also refutes
`iwahori-weyl-coboundary-admits-row-tame-target`. If that selector held,
its selected target and the input would both have `O(delta)` involution and
first-cubic rows. The established exact pairwise identity would make the
selected gauge `O(delta)=O(epsilon g_L)`. Linearizing its coboundary
equation as `epsilon -> 0` would give an inverse preimage of norm `O(g_L)`,
contradicting the lower bound above for large `L`.

## Surviving formulation

The failure is a genuine endpoint-conjugation symmetry. A viable Iwahori
estimate must either minimize over simultaneous conjugation of the whole
first modular vertex, measure the transverse distance to that conjugation
orbit, or add a coupling that fixes the relative placement of the two
vertices. No inequality depending only on the five frozen square-free row
values can charge this low Koopman mode linearly.
