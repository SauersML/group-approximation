Literature import. Peterson--Thom, arXiv:1303.4007 (J. reine angew. Math.
716 (2016)), theorem labelled `secondmain` in the arXiv source, together
with the definition of "induced from a normal subgroup" in the introduction.
Both are transcribed verbatim in the target. The proof uses the finite-index
lemma for `<U_alpha, U_alpha^t>` (Carter--Keller--Paige, Morris) and the
dichotomy "finite quotient or weakly mixing" of the proposition before the
theorem. Neither is re-derived here.
