**ESTABLISHED.**  Retain the controlled-Clifford notation

```text
p_s=e z_+,                 p_t=f(z_++z_-).             (SHG1)
```

Let `h` be a separate character-preserving HNN transporter, so that in an
exact representation

```text
h p_s h^*=p_t,                                             (SHG2)
```

and let `W` be any unitary, including the evaluated native signed Whitehead
word.  The only natural same-source cross-Gram is

```text
G_W=p_s h^* W p_s.                                        (SHG3)
```

It satisfies the exact identity

```text
G_W^*G_W=p_s W^*p_tWp_s,
tau(p_s)-tau(G_W^*G_W)=||(1-p_t)Wp_s||_2^2.              (SHG4)
```

Consequently the polar part of `G_W` cannot manufacture any missing source
mass.  Source saturation of this cross-Gram is **equivalent** to the typed
support incidence for `W`.  Quantitatively,

```text
||(1-p_t)Wp_s||_2^2<=epsilon
 iff tau(G_W^*G_W)>=tau(p_s)-epsilon.                    (SHG5)
```

The same statement is dimension-free under approximate HNN covariance.  If

```text
||h p_s h^*-p_t||_2<=delta_H,
```

then

```text
|tau(G_W^*G_W)-tau(p_sW^*p_tWp_s)|<=delta_H.            (SHG6)
```

Thus attaching the already available balanced HNN edge and polarizing its
cross-Gram does not prove `(OUF8)`: the desired estimate is exactly the mass
which must already be present before the polar decomposition.

This is sharp even after adjoining the odd row.  On the balanced marked
module

```text
H=S tensor C^2,
t=C tensor diag(1,-1),       r=Z_1 tensor 1,             (SHG7)
```

the restrictions of the two subgroups

```text
H_s=<J,Z_1,tZ_2>,            H_t=<J,Z_1,Z_2>             (SHG8)
```

are characterwise equivalent under the isomorphism from
`primitive-clifford-balanced-atoms-have-hnn-obstruction`.  Indeed both
restrictions contain each of the four characters with `J=-1` exactly twice.
Hence there is a unitary `h` satisfying all three HNN conjugacy rows and
`(SHG2)`.

At the same time there is a unitary `W` satisfying

```text
t=WrW^*,                   p_tWp_s=0.                   (SHG9)
```

To see this, `p_s` has dimension `2`.  The negative eigenspace of `t` has
dimension `4`, and its part orthogonal to `p_t` is

```text
(1-f)S tensor span(e_-),
```

of dimension `3`.  Map `p_s` isometrically into that subspace, extend to a
unitary from the negative eigenspace of `r` onto the negative eigenspace of
`t`, and then extend on the positive eigenspaces.  One may choose an
order-four permutation: in the binary basis `|b_1 b_2 o>` numbered
`0,...,7`, with `o=0,1` the two orientations, take

```text
W=(4 1 0 2)(6 3 7 5).                                  (SHG9a)
```

It sends the `r=-1` set to the `t=-1` set and sends the basis of `p_s`,
`{4,6}`, to `{1,3}`, which is disjoint from the basis `{6,7}` of `p_t`.
This gives `(SHG9)`.
For this exact marked model,

```text
G_W=0,
||(1-p_t)Wp_s||_2^2=tau(p_s)>0.                         (SHG10)
```

Therefore the finite subgroup matching, its HNN letter, the balanced
controlled packet, and the odd selector row admit a simultaneous exact
finite-dimensional model with zero HNN/Whitehead cross-Gram.  The actual
native signed Whitehead **word** can also be installed without changing this
conclusion.  Let `B` be the finite block-transvection group of one typed
rectangular coefficient chart over `F_p`, `p>=5`, containing

```text
w_x=E_12(x)E_21(-y)E_12(x),       yx=e, xy=f.           (SHG11)
```

On the exchanged `e`/`f` blocks, `w_x^2=-1`, and it is the identity on the
unused complement, so `w_x` has order `4`.  Replace the model `(SHG7)--(SHG9)`
by the direct sum of the four character twists

```text
W, iW, -W, -iW.                                         (SHG12)
```

All packet, HNN, odd-row, and zero-overlap identities survive, while the
restriction of `(SHG12)` to `<W>~=C_4` is `8` copies of the regular
representation.  Choose the HNN implementer and the original `W` as signed
permutation matrices.  The finite monomial image generated by the packet,
that implementer, and `(SHG12)` will be denoted by `D`.  The regular
representation of `B` restricts to
`|B|/4` copies of the regular `C_4` representation.  After amplifying the
first model by `|B|` and the second by `32`, the two restrictions agree.
The universal property of

```text
D *_(<W>=<w_x>) B                                      (SHG13)
```

therefore gives one exact finite-dimensional marked representation in which
the letter `W` is literally the evaluated native word `(SHG11)`, every
relation of that finite typed coefficient chart is exact, and `G_W=0`.

This does not represent the **full** affine-Leavitt Steinberg presentation;
that would contradict its established exact finite-dimensional marked-root
collapse.  It proves the precise local firewall: even literal reuse of the
native Whitehead occurrence, the separate HNN edge, and finite
Gram/polar extraction leave zero overlap.  Some additional full-presentation
coefficient-occurrence relation must prove typed overlap directly; the
balanced transporter supplies no surplus from which to extract it.
