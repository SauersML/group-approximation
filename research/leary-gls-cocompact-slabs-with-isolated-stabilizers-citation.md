Citation import, not a reproof. Read 2026-09-13 from the arXiv TeX source
of arXiv:1512.06609v4 (`main.tex`, fetched on MSI). Theorem numbers are not
recorded; the statements are named by content.

- **Existence theorem for `X_L^{(S)}`:** "The function `f^{(S)}:= f∘b=l̃∘c`
  is a Morse function on `X^{(S)}`"; "The links of vertices of `X^{(S)}` are
  either `𝕊(L)` or `𝕊(L̃)`, depending on their height"; "The ascending and
  descending links of vertices of `X^{(S)}` are either `L` or `L̃` depending
  on their height" (`L` if `f^{(S)}(v) ∈ S`, `L̃` otherwise); "The group
  `G_L(S)` is by definition the group of deck transformations of the branched
  covering `c:X_L^{(S)}→X_L/BB_L`."
- **Branched covering theorem:** "There is a CAT(0) cubical complex `X̄` and a
  regular branched covering map `c:X̄→X`, with branching locus contained in
  `V`".
- **Connectivity of level sets, proof:** "Since `X^{(S)}` is contractible".
- **Corollary on stabilizers:** "The cellular action of the group `G_L(S)`
  on `X_L^{(S)}` is free, except that each vertex whose height is not in `S`
  is stabilized by a subgroup isomorphic to `π_1(L)`."
- **Theorem on `FP_n` (general `S`), proof:** "`G` acts cocompactly on each
  `X(m)`", where `X(m)` is the slab of heights in `[−m−1/2, m+1/2]`.
- **Proposition for finite `S`, proof:** "The closure of each such ball is
  `G_v`-equivariantly isomorphic to the mapping cylinder `M(𝕊(L̃)→∗)`".
  After replacement, "By construction, `G` acts freely on `Y`, and the
  quotient `Y/G` is obtained from the finite complex `X(m)/G` by replacing
  finitely many copies of `M(𝕊(L)→∗)` by copies of `M(𝕊(L)→L)`". Hence
  `G_v` acts freely on `𝕊(L̃)` with quotient `𝕊(L)`. The ball structure is
  local at a branch vertex and is the same for every `S`.
- **Morse lemma (after Bestvina–Brady):** "Up to homotopy `Y_{[a,d]}` is
  obtained from `Y_{[b,c]}` by coning off a copy of `Lk↓_Y(v)` for each
  vertex `v` with `g(v)∈(c,d]` and coning off a copy of `Lk↑_Y(v)` for each
  vertex `v` with `g(v)∈[a,b)`."
