Section 9. The explicit surjective endomorphism Phi of the torsion-free construction has nontrivial kernel. Every kernel element acts trivially on the pullback of any action along Phi, so the pullback is never essentially free and cannot be one member of a pair of free actions with different costs. Non-Hopficity of the group is therefore not a source of a Fixed Price counterexample.
