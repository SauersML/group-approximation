For every stable classifiable C\*-algebra `A` (simple separable
nuclear `Z`-stable, UCT, stable) there are a classifiable C\*-algebra
`C` and an action `Z ↷ C` with

```text
A ≅ C ⋊ Z .
```

So the stable half of STW Problem XLVI is closed: the open content of
the decomposition problem lives entirely in the unital case, where
`[1]`-positioning obstructions (Pimsner--Voiculescu) are genuine.
