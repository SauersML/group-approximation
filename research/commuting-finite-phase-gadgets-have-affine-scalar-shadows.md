Fix a prime `p`.  Suppose a finite gadget has commuting order-`p` interface
and auxiliary elements and only ordinary word equations.  Its scalar
solutions are an affine subset of a finite `F_p`-space, and existentially
forgetting auxiliaries again gives an affine subset of the visible
coordinates.

Consequently such a gadget cannot exactly realize either the Boolean
three-way one-hot relation or the qutrit inequality relation.  In
particular, a projector synchronizer which preserves arbitrary freedom on
the complementary qutrit eigenspaces cannot arise solely from commuting
finite-order interface letters and scalar word equations.  A successful
gadget must use genuinely noncommutative tracial/dimensional structure.
