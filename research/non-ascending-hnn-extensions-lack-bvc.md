title: An HNN extension whose associated subgroups both have index at least two in the base does not have property BVC
---

Let `H` be a group, `theta: A -> B` an isomorphism between subgroups of `H` with
`[H : A] >= 2` and `[H : B] >= 2`. Then the HNN extension `H*_theta = <H, t | t x t^{-1} = theta(x), x in A>`
does not have property BVC.

So an HNN extension with property BVC is ascending: `A = H` or `B = H`.

Source: von Puttkamer–Wu, arXiv:1607.03790v2, Lemma 2.4 (page 8).
