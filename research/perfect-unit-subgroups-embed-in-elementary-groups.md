title: A perfect subgroup of the unit group of any ring embeds in every elementary group E_n(R) with n >= 2
---

**ESTABLISHED.** Let `R` be a unital associative ring and let `P <= R^x` be a
perfect subgroup, `P = [P, P]`. For every `n >= 2` the map

    u |-> diag(u, 1, ..., 1)  in GL_n(R)

restricts to an injective group homomorphism `P -> E_n(R)`, where `E_n(R)` is
the subgroup generated by the elementary matrices `e_ij(r)`, `i != j`.

No hypothesis on `R` is used: no commutativity, stable rank, simplicity or
`K`-theory. This is the point of the claim. It lets an embedding into a unit
group be upgraded to an embedding into the elementary group without assuming
`K_1(R) = 0`, as long as the input group is perfect, for instance infinite
simple.
