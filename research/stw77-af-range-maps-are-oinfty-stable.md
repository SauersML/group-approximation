title: Properly infinite AF-range maps are O-infinity-stable
---

Let `phi:A->B` be a unital homomorphism into a unital C-star algebra.  Suppose
the range `C=phi(A)` is a separable AF algebra and every nonzero positive
element of `C` is properly infinite in `B`.  Then `phi` is
O-infinity-stable.

No nuclear-dimension or exactness hypothesis is needed for this AF-range
case.
