Let `M_-` be the space of finitely additive `F_2`-valued measures on the clopens of Cantor space
`X` with `mu(X) = 1`, with normalized Haar measure `lambda`, as in
`signed-thompson-algebra-is-odd-measure-crossed-product` and
`odd-measure-space-has-no-honest-thompson-compression`. Let `P_n` be the partition of `X` into the
`2^n` cylinders of length `n`.

1. `U_n = {mu : mu restricted to P_n has weight at least 3}` is clopen and contains no Dirac measure.
   So `1_(U_n)` is an idempotent in the kernel of the Leavitt evaluation.
2. `lambda(U_n) = 1 - 2^(n + 1 - 2^n)`, which is `0, 1/2, 15/16` for `n = 1, 2, 3` and tends to `1`.
3. The Dirac measures `delta(X)` form the closed set `intersection_n (M_- \ U_n)`, and it is
   `lambda`-null.

So the kernel's mass sits at finite depth: half of all mass is kernel at depth two. What sits at
infinite depth is the null set of Dirac measures, which is the spectrum of the quotient
`L_(F_3)(1,2)`.

Every element of a group algebra is finitely supported. So a support no-go can never say "a
witness needs infinite support" without proving that no witness exists; its provable form is a
condition on the subgroup generated by the support. Section 11 of the artifact.
