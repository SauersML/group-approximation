Let `V_n` be an input game satisfying the hypotheses of
Bowen--Chapman--Vidick Answer Reduction, let `S` be a perfect
finite-dimensional signed-permutation ZPC strategy for `V_n`, and let

```text
AR_n(S)
```

be the particular perfect strategy obtained by following BCV's published
perfect-completeness construction through restriction-free padding,
purification, double cover, oracularization, and equations `(192)--(193)`
of Proposition 5.80. Then there is a unital Boolean-algebra embedding

```text
D_infinity(S) -> D_infinity(AR_n(S)).                    (HAR1)
```

Here both full readable translate algebras are generated by readable spectral
projections and all conjugations by structural unreadable words.
Consequently,

```text
dim D_infinity(AR_n(S)) >= dim D_infinity(S).            (HAR2)
```

This statement remains about BCV's explicit forward completeness strategy.
The separate exact theorem
`bcv-perfect-answer-reduction-decodes-double-cover` handles arbitrary
attained perfect targets: it universally returns `DoubleCover(V_n)` and
returns `V_n` itself under synchronization or bipartiteness. The present
claim is stronger only in identifying the given source strategy as a literal
left inverse of its own chosen forward construction.
