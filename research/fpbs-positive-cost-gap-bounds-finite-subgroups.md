title: Positive upper-cost excess quantitatively bounds finite subgroup orders in bounded exponent

Equation 7.4. If delta=C*(G)-1>0 for a countably infinite exponent-N group, each finite subgroup of order h>=3 satisfies delta^N h<=(N-1)log h. Thus finite subgroup orders are uniformly bounded. The converse is not asserted.
