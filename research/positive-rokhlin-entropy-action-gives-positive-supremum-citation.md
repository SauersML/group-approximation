Citation import from B. Seward, arXiv:1501.03367v4. The statements were read from the PDF text, extracted on MSI on 2026-09-12.

* **Corollary 7.7.** "The following are equivalent: (i) hRok_sup(G) > 0; (ii) there is a free ergodic p.m.p. action with 0 < hRok(X;µ) < ∞; (iii) there is a free ergodic p.m.p. action with hRok(X;µ) = ∞."
* **Proof of Theorem 7.6, first paragraph.** Take a free ergodic action with positive Rokhlin entropy and an increasing sequence of finite partitions, and choose the factors free using Theorem 2.6. By Theorem 7.3 some factor has positive Rokhlin entropy, and that entropy is at most the partition's Shannon entropy. So `h^Rok_sup(G) > 0`.
