Expand the regauged packet as

```text
WYW^*=sum_(i,j)y_(ij)E_(ij)h_i h_j^(-1).
```

Choose one root in each connected component of the off-diagonal coefficient
graph.  Replacing every nonroot basis element `h_i` by
`h_i h_root^(-1)` is a Nielsen basis change.  Edge differences generate all
and only these nonroot basis elements: containment in one direction is
immediate, and a path to the component root telescopes in the other.  Hence
the phase support is generated by a subset of a free basis and has rank
`D-c`.

Apply `free-factor-phase-graph-hnn-is-residually-finite` to a termwise HNN
transport between two endpoint packets.  The finite packet and old commuting
phase data are its auxiliary residually finite factor, so a proper
nonamenable sparse edge of this standard form is still residually finite.
