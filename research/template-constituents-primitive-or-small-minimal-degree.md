**ESTABLISHED (lane sk-trace-template, 2026-09-13, unreviewed).** Proof: `template-constituents-primitive-or-small-minimal-degree-proof`.

**Setting.** A finite-group hyperlinear (or sofic) model of a finitely presented infinite simple group `S = ⟨X | R⟩`, as on
`fp-simple-models-concentrate-on-template-constituents`, with `Q_k = ⟨φ_k(X)⟩`. This costs nothing: restricting `ρ_k` to the
subgroup generated by the generator images keeps the model.

**Definitions.**
- For a nontrivial permutation group `P` on a finite set `Δ`, `μ(P)` is the least number of points moved by a nonidentity
  element, and `μ̂(P) = μ(P)/|Δ|`.
- A *primitive section* of a transitive action of `Q` on `Ω` with `|Ω| ≥ 2` is the action of `Q` on the blocks of a
  `Q`-invariant partition with at least 2 blocks on which `Q` acts primitively. When the action is primitive, `Ω` itself is
  one. Every transitive action on at least 2 points has a primitive section.

**Theorem.** Fix `θ < 1`.
- **(U) Unitary.** Let `σ ∈ T_k(θ,D)` be a template constituent. Either `σ` is a primitive irreducible representation (not
  induced from a proper subgroup), or `σ = Ind_H^{Q_k} τ` for a proper subgroup `H`. In the second case, for every primitive
  section `𝓑` of `Q_k` acting on `Q_k/H`:
  - some relator acts nontrivially on `𝓑`;
  - every relator moves fewer than `(1−θ)|𝓑|` blocks;
  - so `μ̂(Q_k^𝓑) < 1 − θ` and `|𝓑| > 2/(1−θ)`.
- **(P) Permutation.** Every primitive section `𝓑` of `Q_k` acting on a template orbit has the same three properties.

**Corollary (relative minimal-degree gap).** Suppose `c > 0`, and every primitive section of every transitive `Q_k`-set has
`μ̂ ≥ c`.
- (P) `S` has no sofic model through these actions.
- (U) In every finite-group hyperlinear model through these groups, the template constituents with `θ > 1 − c` are primitive
  irreducible representations. So along `ω` they carry weight at least `η²/4`.

**Model tests.**
- `Sym(n)` on `n` points: primitive with `μ̂ = 2/n → 0`, so it is allowed. Sofic models live there.
- `GL_N(F_2)` on nonzero vectors: primitive, since it is 2-transitive. A nonidentity element fixes at most `2^{N−1} − 1` of the
  `2^N − 1` points, so `μ̂ > 1/2`, and it is excluded for `θ ≥ 1/2`. This agrees with `gap-sofic-approximations-are-local-embeddings`.
- Wreath products `H ≀ Sym(b)` acting imprimitively have the section `Sym(b)` on blocks, with `μ̂ = 2/b`. They are allowed.

**Unconditional reading.** With `θ_k → 1` along `ω`, as in Corollary 1 of the template claim, every template orbit and every
induced template constituent yields primitive permutation groups of degree `→ ∞` whose relative minimal degree tends to `0`.
Identifying those groups, and the primitive branch (U), is the open claim
`fp-simple-finite-group-models-reduce-to-alternating-constituents`.
