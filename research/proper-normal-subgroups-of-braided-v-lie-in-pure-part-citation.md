Citation import, not a reproof. Read from the PDF of arXiv:1403.8132v2 (M. C. B.
Zaremsky, *On normal subgroups of the braided Thompson groups*, dated 31 Jan 2018,
Groups Geom. Dyn.), page 1:

- Theorem 2.1: "Let N be a normal subgroup of F_br. Then either N ≤ P_br or else
  [F_br, F_br] ≤ N."
- Corollary 2.8: "Any proper normal subgroup of V_br is contained in P_br."

There `P_br` is "the kernel of the map V_br ↠ V, and is also the kernel of
F_br ↠ F", and `V_br` is the braided Thompson group introduced independently by
Brin and Dehornoy.
