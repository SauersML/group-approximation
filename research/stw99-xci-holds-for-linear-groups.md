Every C\*-simple linear group (over any field of characteristic zero,
in Vigdorovich's generality) has a selfless reduced group
C\*-algebra; in particular `C*_r(G)` has strict comparison and a
unique quasitrace.  So STW Problems XCI and XC both hold on the class
of linear groups — together with the acylindrically-hyperbolic
rapid-decay cell (AGKP) this makes the open content of XC/XCI the
non-linear, non-acyl-hyperbolic C*-simple groups (e.g. Thompson-type
and iterated-monodromy examples).
