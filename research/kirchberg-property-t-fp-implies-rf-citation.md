E. Kirchberg, *Discrete groups with Kazhdan's property T and factorization property
are residually finite*, Mathematische Annalen **299** (1994), 551--563,
doi:10.1007/BF01459798.

**Verified 2026-09-13 (sk-operator-algebras).**
- Crossref (`api.crossref.org/works/10.1007/BF01459798`): vol. 299, pp. 551-563, 1994.
- The page header of the Springer PDF reads "Math. Ann. 299, 551-563 (1994)".
- The earlier range 551--564 and issue 3 (EUDML) are superseded. Crossref lists issue 1.

From p. 552, verbatim:
- "Theorem 1.1 A discrete group with property (F) and property T is residually finite."
- "Corollary 1.2 For a discrete group G with property T, the following properties are
  equivalent:
  (i) G has property (F).
  (ii) There exists a faithful unitary representation from G into the hyperfinite II_1-factor.
  (iii) There exists a faithful group homomorphism from G into a locally compact group H with nuclear universal group C*-algebra C*(H).
  (iv) G is residually finite.
  (v) There exists a faithful group homomorphism from G into an almost connected locally compact group."
- "The implications (iv)⇒(v)⇒(iii)⇒(i) and (iv)⇒(ii)⇒(i) are true in full generality."

The target (under property (T), the factorization property holds iff G is residually finite) is Theorem 1.1
together with (iv)⇒(ii)⇒(i).

Thom (arXiv:0810.2180) points out an error only in the implication (v)⇒(i) of Corollary 1.2. The target does not use it.
