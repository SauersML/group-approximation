E. Kirchberg, *Discrete groups with Kazhdan's property T and factorization property
are residually finite*, Mathematische Annalen **299** (1994), no. 3, 551--564.

The paper's title is its theorem: a discrete group with property (T) has the
factorization property exactly when it is residually finite. Verified against the
Springer/EUDML record (EUDML doc 165222; Math. Ann. 299(3), pp. 551--564). The DOI
(reconstructed as 10.1007/BF01459798) is NOT yet confirmed against Crossref; volume,
issue and pages are confirmed and suffice for the citation.
