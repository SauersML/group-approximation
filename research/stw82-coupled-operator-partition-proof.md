For each `v`, apply
`stw82-quasicentral-cutdowns-repair-to-banded-order-zero` to `phi_v` and its
`d`-term partition.  Since `V` is finite, choose one `delta` below the
minimum of the finitely many stability constants.  This gives cpc order-zero
maps `psi_(v,k)` in the stated hereditary algebras with

```text
norm(phi_v-sum_k psi_(v,k))<epsilon.
```

Fix `k`.  If `v!=w`, condition (C3) and positivity imply

```text
c_(v,k)^(1/2)c_(w,k)^(1/2)=0.
```

Consequently the two hereditary algebras generated by `c_(v,k)` and
`c_(w,k)` annihilate one another in both orders.  Values of `psi_(v,k)` and
`psi_(w,k)` are therefore orthogonal.  Orthogonality within each summand is
preserved because `psi_(v,k)` is order zero.

The finite sum `Psi_k` is completely positive.  Its value at the unit is a
sum of pairwise orthogonal positive contractions, so

```text
norm(Psi_k(1))=max_v norm(psi_(v,k)(1))<=1.
```

Hence `Psi_k` is contractive and order zero.  Repeating this for every `k`
proves the coupled merger.
