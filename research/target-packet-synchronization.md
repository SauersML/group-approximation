title: Commuting target packets synchronize transition PVMs
---

For one common source projection `P` and two target PVMs `E, F`, the
transition POVMs `P Eᵢ P` and `P Fⱼ P` satisfy a cardinality-free total
commutator bound: its square root is at most
`√(target commutator energy) + √(E leakage/2) + √(F leakage/2)`. Individual
de la Salle rounding preserves vanishing total commutator energy without an
outcome-count loss. The multi-edge analytic gate closes whenever the
raw/four-root packet routes the three required pairs to commuting target
root packets on common source carriers.
