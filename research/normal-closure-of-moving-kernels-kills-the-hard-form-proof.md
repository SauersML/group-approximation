The fixed core contributes `e_01,e_23`.  Formula `(MVK4)` places every moving
line in

```text
Sym^2=span{e_02,e_03+e_12,e_13}.
```

The first three Veronese points are independent over `F_2(x)`: their
determinant is the characteristic-two Vandermonde product
`(r_0+r_1)(r_0+r_2)(r_1+r_2)`, nonzero because the projective slopes are
pairwise distinct.  Equivalently, the exact polynomial Gaussian audit reports
cumulative ranks `3,4,5` at levels `0,1,2`.  Hence the moving lines span all
of `Sym^2`, proving `(NCM1)`.  Taking the coordinate annihilator gives
`(NCM2)`.  The transverse pairing `(HTK2)` excludes `h` from that line.

In a group, every conjugate of an identity relation is again an identity.
Therefore a stable letter acting linearly by `T` forces the complete
`F_2[x]`-submodule generated by the orbit relations, which is `W`; the dual
form conclusion follows.
