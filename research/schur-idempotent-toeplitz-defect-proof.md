Because the predicate has both allowed and forbidden characters, the central
idempotents `P_f` and `1-P_f` are nonzero. On a countable direct sum of the
regular `k[B_f]`-module, their ranges are both countably infinite-dimensional.
An arbitrary vector-space isomorphism from the whole module onto the second
range, extended by zero in the reverse direction on the first range, gives
the operators `X,Y` in `(STD3)`. The universal property of `(STD2)` then
proves that the packet algebra remains embedded.

Every finite ring is directly finite: if `yx=1`, left multiplication by `x`
is injective on the finite underlying set, hence surjective, and its left
inverse is also its right inverse. Therefore `xy=1` and the image of
`P_f=1-xy` is zero. The same last implication is the definition of direct
finiteness for an arbitrary target. The presentation consists of a finite
presentation of the fixed finite-dimensional algebra `k[B_f]`, two new
generators, and two relations.
