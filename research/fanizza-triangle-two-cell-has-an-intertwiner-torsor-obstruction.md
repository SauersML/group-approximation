title: The Fanizza triangle two-cell is developable exactly when three intertwiner torsors close
---

The triangular two-cell extends fixed vertex representations exactly when
the direct-edge intertwiner torsor meets the product of the other two edge
torsors.  Individual nonemptiness, even after countable amplification, does
not imply this cycle condition.

For the half-edge-refined Fanizza packet, incidence-private gauge factors
give an explicit exact closure.  This settles HALT completeness of the
two-cell while leaving Gram mass and finite-dimensional soundness untouched.
