ESTABLISHED.  Let `W=F2^s`, `K=F2^k`, and identify the Atlas
multiplicity space with

```text
M=W tensor K.                                         (FWB1)
```

Fix `q` and let `T0 in GL_q(End(M))` be a chart change such that every
coefficient product of `T0` and its inverse belongs to a fixed-width
amplified algebra:

```text
(T0)_(ia) (T0^(-1))_(bj) in End(W) tensor I_K.        (FWB2)
```

If `T in GL_q(End(M))` satisfies

```text
r=rank(T-T0),                                         (FWB3)
```

write `C_T` for the algebra generated by the `q^4` products
`T_(ia)(T^(-1))_(bj)`.  Then every subspace `U <= M` satisfies

```text
dim(C_T U) <= s^2 (dim U + 2 q^4 r).                  (FWB4)
```

Consequently, for fixed `q,s`, neither a rank-one bridge nor any
`o(k)`-rank perturbation of a fixed-width amplified phase can make an
`o(k)`-dimensional seed cyclic.  In the multiplicity-two Atlas escape
(`q=4,s=2`), cyclicity forces

```text
dim U + 512 r >= k/2.                                 (FWB5)
```

Thus perturbing a direct amplification of the stored `M_2` phase by one or
finitely many outer products cannot produce the growing-width enemy in
`atlas-left-ideal-rank-nonwandering`, even though every fixed Atlas word
residual changes by only `O(r)` rank.

### Proof

Put `S=T^(-1)` and `S0=T0^(-1)`.  Multiplication by invertible maps gives

```text
rank(S-S0)=rank(S(T0-T)S0)=r.                         (FWB6)
```

Hence every block difference of `T-T0` and `S-S0` has rank at most `r`.
For each coefficient generator

```text
B_(iabj)=T_(ia) S_(bj)
```

and its base value `A_(iabj)=(T0)_(ia)(S0)_(bj)`, write

```text
B_(iabj)=A_(iabj)+F_(iabj).
```

The identity

```text
F_(iabj)=(T_(ia)-(T0)_(ia))S_(bj)
          +(T0)_(ia)(S_(bj)-(S0)_(bj))
```

shows

```text
rank F_(iabj) <= 2r.                                  (FWB7)
```

For a subspace `H <= W tensor K`, let `p(H) <= K` be the span of all its
`W`-coordinate projections.  Then

```text
dim p(H) <= s dim H,       H <= W tensor p(H).         (FWB8)
```

Define

```text
Z=p(U)+sum_(i,a,b,j) p(im F_(iabj)) <= K.             (FWB9)
```

Every base generator `A_(iabj)` preserves `W tensor Z` by `(FWB2)`, while
every error generator maps all of `M` into
`im F_(iabj) <= W tensor Z`.  Therefore `W tensor Z` is invariant under
all `B_(iabj)` and contains `U`, so it contains `C_T U`.  Finally,

```text
dim Z <= s dim U + s sum rank F_(iabj)
      <= s(dim U+2q^4r),
```

and multiplication by `dim W=s` proves `(FWB4)`.

The argument also explains why one generic outer product is insufficient:
arbitrarily long words may repeatedly use it, but every use lands in the
same fixed passive-coordinate span.  Word length cannot manufacture new
multiplicity directions outside the images of the finitely many perturbed
coefficient generators.
