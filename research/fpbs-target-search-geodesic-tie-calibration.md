Section 3: on the all-open T_4 x Z graph, endpoints separated by ell in
both coordinates require at least ell(ell+1)/2-1 fresh queries for beta=1
with insertion-order ties, despite distance 2ell. On any connected
locally finite all-open graph, beta>1 instead gives at most 2D fresh
queries for endpoint distance D. These calibrations at p=1 say nothing
by themselves about expected query cost at a near-critical p<1.
