Write `q=1-p`.  Multiplication by multiplier projections preserves `A` and
all of its ideals, so every displayed corner is a C-star corner or a closed
ternary corner as appropriate.

## 1. The off-diagonal corner is exactly the overlap module

For `a in A`, the element `paq` belongs to `L` because `pa in L`, and it
belongs to `R` because `aq in R`.  Hence

```text
pAq subset I.
```

The reverse inclusion after cornering is tautological, so `X=pIq`.

Now

```text
closure(XX*)=closure(pAqAp)=pRp.
```

Since `B=pIp` is contained in `pRp`, while `XX* subset I`, both inclusions
give `B=closure(XX*)`.  Interchanging `p,q` gives
`C=closure(X*X)`.  This proves `(OLC1)` and makes `X` a full imprimitivity
bimodule between `B` and `C`.  Its linking algebra is precisely the Peirce
decomposition `(OLC2)` of `I`.

## 2. The three larger linking algebras

The `p`-corner of `L=closure(ApA)` is all of `P=pAp`, its `q`-corner is

```text
qLq=closure(qApAq)=closure(X*X)=C,
```

and its off-diagonal corner is `X`.  This is the first matrix in `(OLC3)`.
The second follows symmetrically for `R`; adding the two ideals, which equal
`A`, gives the third.

Because `p notin A`, the corner `P` is nonunital with multiplier unit `p`.
Indeed, if `e in P` were a unit for `P`, then `p-e` would annihilate
`pAp`; in particular `(p-e)A(p-e)=0`.  Essentiality of `A` in `M(A)`
would force `p-e=0`, contradicting `p notin A`.
In the Peirce decomposition, adding the scalar projection `p` changes only
the upper-left corner from `P` to its minimal unitization `P+C p`.  This
proves `(OLC4)`.

Compressing the Chinese-remainder quotient gives

```text
p(A/I)p = P/B,       q(A/I)q=Q/C.
```

The first is the surviving `p`-corner after killing `R`, and the second the
surviving `q`-corner after killing `L`, proving `(OLC5)`.

## 3. Why a linking frame does not finish the proof

Every finite row `v=(xi_1,...,xi_m)` over `X` has

```text
vv*=sum_j xi_j xi_j* in B,
v*v in M_m(C).
```

Thus all compact operators generated by such rows remain in the overlap
corners.  If `e in B` is a positive contraction and `pi:P->P/B` is the
quotient map, then `pi(eye)=0`; therefore

```text
||y-eye|| >= ||pi(y-eye)||=||pi(y)||.
```

This is `(OLC7)`.  The same calculation holds in `Q/C`.

Consequently a Kasparov-frame or linking-unitary construction can reuse
colours for the overlap ideal, but no choice of such a frame controls a
nonzero residual diagonal class.  Any complete proof must add a genuinely
relative step that coordinates approximations of `P/B` and `Q/C` with the
same overlap colours; simply stabilizing the full linking core does not
provide it.
