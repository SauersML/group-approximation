**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Notation of `radu-horizontal-quotient-is-quaternionic-s-arithmetic`:
`Q = ρ(Γ_R) <= N(O)/F^×`, discrete in `PB^×(F_𝔮) × PB^×(F_(𝔮')) ≅ PGL_2(Q_2) × PGL_2(Q_2)`,
acting on `T_h × T'`.

1. **Second tree.** On `T'`, the group `<ρ(xz), ρ(xyx)> ≅ S_3` fixes exactly one vertex `p`
   within distance 12 of the standard vertex. It permutes the three edges at `p` as `S_3`, and
   `ρ(x)` and `ρ(z)` invert one of them. So `ρ(V)` is transitive on the vertices of `T'`.
2. **Vertex group.** `ρ(V) ≅ S_3 *_(C_2) D_2 ≅ PGL_2(Z)`, the Coxeter group
   `<x,y,z | x^2,y^2,z^2,(xz)^2,(xyz)^3>`, acting on `T'` as on its Bass–Serre tree. So
   `Λ_v = ker ρ` is the normal closure of `(xz)^2` and `(xyz)^3` in `V`, and the horizontal
   automaton group `V/Λ_v` is `PGL_2(Z)` exactly. This proves the relation `(xyz)^3`, which
   `radu-horizontal-projection-kills-delta-squared` observed only through level 10.
3. **Lattice.** `Q` is transitive on the vertices of `T_h × T'`, with vertex stabilizer
   `ρ(V) ∩ Stab(p) ≅ S_3`. So `Q` is a cocompact irreducible lattice in
   `PGL_2(Q_2) × PGL_2(Q_2)`, of finite index in the `S`-arithmetic group `N(O)/F^×`. Its Euler
   characteristic is `χ(Q) = −χ(PGL_2(Z))/2 = 1/24`.
4. **The horizontal lattice is bounded on `T'`.** `ρ(a), ρ(b), ρ(c)` are involutions, and
   `tr^2/det` of `ab`, `bc`, `ca` is `1 + w/2`, of `𝔮'`-valuation 2. So all of them are
   elliptic on `T'`. By Serre's lemma (*Trees* I.6.5) `ρ(A)` fixes a point `p_A` of `T'`,
   a vertex or an edge midpoint. `gA ↦ ρ(g)p_A` is then a `Γ_R`-equivariant Lipschitz map
   `T_v → T'` collapsing `Λ_v`.

**Picture.** Radu's non-residually-finite lattice
`Γ_R <= Aut(T_3) × Aut(T_3)` is obtained from the arithmetic lattice
`Q <= PGL_2(Q_2) × PGL_2(Q_2)` of the totally definite quaternion algebra over `Q(√17)`,
whose vertex stabilizers are `S_3`, by a construction in two steps:
* replace the second tree `T'` by the Cayley graph of the vertex group `PGL_2(Z)` with
  respect to `x, y, z`;
* pass to its universal cover `T_v`.

`Γ_R` is the group of lifts of `Q`. Every finite quotient of `Q` is a finite quotient of
`Γ_R`, and the whole finite residual, with the mark `(xz)^4`, lies in the free kernel.

exactly when `[N(O)/F^× : O^1_S/±1] = 4`.
