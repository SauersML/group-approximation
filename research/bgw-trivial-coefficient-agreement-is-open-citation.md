**Source.** A. Buss, S. Echterhoff, R. Willett, *The minimal exact crossed
product*, arXiv:1804.02725v3 (2020), Appendix A "Erratum", pp. 38--40.
Also published as *Erratum to: "The minimal exact crossed product"* (EMS Press,
Documenta Math.). The arXiv PDF was downloaded on MSI, and pages 36--41 were
read as page images.

**Verbatim, p. 39.** "The second, and most severe, problem in the paper comes
from Lemma 4.3, which was an important step in our proof of Proposition 4.4.
Proposition 4.4 says that the smallest exact crossed-product ⋊_ε(µ) which
dominates a Morita compatible functor ⋊_µ with the ideal property is
automatically Morita compatible. We do not know if Proposition 4.4 is correct
or not, but Lemma 4.3 is definitely false."

**Verbatim, pp. 39--40.** "Therefore Proposition 4.4 together with
Corollaries 4.5, 4.6, 4.7, and 4.8 are still open. In particular it is still
open, whether the group algebra C*_{ε_M}(G) = C ⋊_{ε_M} G for the smallest
Morita compatible exact crossed-product functor ⋊_{ε_M} coincides with the
reduced group algebra C*_r(G)."

**The withdrawn claim, main text** (ghostscript text extraction on MSI, after
Corollary 4.7). "Note that this answers [2, Questions 8.5, (iv) and (v)].
Indeed, question (iv) asks whether C ⋊_E G equals C*_r(G), and Proposition 2.6
and Corollary 4.6 imply that this is always true." Here [2] is
Baum--Guentner--Willett, *Expanders, exact crossed products, and the
Baum-Connes conjecture*, Ann. K-theory 1 (2015). Corollary 4.6 identifies
`⋊_(E(r))` with the functor `⋊_E` of [2, Theorem 3.13].

**Lemma A.1, verbatim, p. 38.** "Suppose that ⋊_µ has the ideal property. Then
every short exact sequence of G-algebras 0 → I → A → C → 0 descends to a short
exact sequence 0 → I ⋊_µ G → A ⋊_µ G → C ⋊_µ G → 0." The erratum attributes it
to Narutaka Ozawa, communicated by Yosuke Kubota. It observes that the reduced
crossed product satisfies the ideal property.
