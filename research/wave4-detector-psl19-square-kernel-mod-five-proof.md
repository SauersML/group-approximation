Write `k=F_5`, `Q=PSL_2(19)` and `R=k[Q]`. Take the explicit subgroup
`A` of order twelve generated by the Hurwitz matrices in
[[wave4-detector-psl19-borel-a4-sector-proof]]. Its averaging idempotent
`e=|A|^-1 sum(A)` gives the285-dimensional projective permutation
module `P=Re=k[Q/A]`.

**Generator certificate.** Start with the285 indicator vectors of
left cosets of `A` in `Q`, viewed in the3420-dimensional group algebra.
Close their row span under right multiplication by the generating
matrices `x12,x13`. At each stage exact row reduction over `k` and
adjoining the two translated bases give ranks
`285,853,1969,3012,3420`. Thus `ReR=R`. Equivalently, copies of `P`
surject onto the regular module `R`; since `R` is projective, `R` is
a direct summand of a finite direct sum of copies of `P`.
It follows by tensoring that `k[Q²]` is a direct summand of copies
of `P⊗P`.

**Verified direct summands.** GAP proposes a decomposition of `P`
into eight invariant summands with dimensions
`60,45,20,20,20,20,45,55`. The stored JSON contains their bases in
`k^285` and their four induced generator matrices for
`x12,x13,x23,y`, where `y=u^-1 x23 u`. The independent Python
checker verifies that the concatenated basis has rank285 and that
for every one of the32 matrices the exact intertwining equation
holds against the original coset permutation. These verifications
prove a direct-sum decomposition; no reliance on GAP's claim of
indecomposability or on probabilistic classification is necessary.

For each of the64 tensor summands `V=P_i⊗P_j`, let `d=dim V`.
It is projective over `k[Q²]`; restriction to any embedded vertex
`Q` or edge `C_5` is projective. In particular all positive-degree
vertex and edge cohomology vanishes. The equivariant spectral
sequence for the contractible triangle development computes
`H¹(Λ;V)` by the invariant cellular cochain complex

`⊕_v V^(A_v) → ⊕_e V^(C_e) → V`.

Let `E12,E13,E23` denote the three edge fixed subspaces in `V`.
Each has dimension `d/5`, since restriction is free over `k[C_5]`.
The vertex fixed subspaces are the three pairwise intersections,
as the adjacent edge generators generate that vertex copy of `Q`.
The last cochain map sums the edge inclusions with signs, and the
kernel of the first map is `V^Λ=V^(Q²)`. Therefore

`dim H¹(Λ;V) = sum_(a<b) rank(Ea+Eb) - rank(E12+E13+E23)
               - 3d/5 + dim V^(Q²)`.

**Exact invariant bases and ranks.** For each generator on a summand
of dimension `a`, the rank of `(g-I)^4` is checked to be `a/5`.
Independent rows of that fourth power select standard vectors whose
five-element cyclic strings form a basis; this is checked by rank
`a`. If two such cyclic strings are `(v_k)` and `(w_k)`, the five
vectors `sum_k v_k⊗w_(k+s)` for `s=0,...,4` give the fixed space in
that25-dimensional tensor block. This constructs full edge fixed
bases without a dense `d×d` generator matrix. The edge generator
pairs are `(x12,x12),(x13,x13),(x23,y)`.

The stored permutation matrices act on rows by the opposite group
convention. Inverting all group actions gives right modules with
the usual product convention and leaves every fixed subspace and
rank in this computation unchanged. Thus this convention has no
effect on the cohomology calculation.

All64 rank computations give zero in the displayed formula. The
maximum `d` is3600. Hence `H¹(Λ;P⊗P)=0`; the direct-summand generator
certificate implies `H¹(Λ;k[Q²])=0`. Finite-index Shapiro identifies
this with `H¹(M;k)`. Duality with coefficients in the field gives
`H_1(M;k)=0` as claimed. Since `M` is torsion-free, its finite
quotient of the triangle development has10,260 vertices,7,017,840
edges and11,696,400 faces. Its Euler characteristic is4,688,820;
hence `dim H_2(M;k)=4,688,819`.

**Consequences and scope.** A nontrivial finite five-group has a
nontrivial quotient `C_5`. Thus an image of `M` which is a five-group
must be trivial. For a nonzero twist, the established restriction
class in `H²(M;k)` is nonzero. The low-degree cohomology sequence of
`1→C_5→M_c→M→1` contains

`0 → H¹(M;k) → H¹(M_c;k) → Hom(C_5,k) → H²(M;k)`.

The last map sends the identity to that nonzero extension class and
is injective. Hence `H¹(M_c;k)=0` as well, so `M_c` has no nontrivial
finite five-group image. Other-prime abelianizations and mixed-prime
solvable refinements are not excluded by this proof.
