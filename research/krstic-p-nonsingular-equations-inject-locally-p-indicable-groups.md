Let `p` be a prime and `G` a locally `p`-indicable group: every nontrivial
finitely generated subgroup of `G` maps onto `Z/p`. Let `w in G * <t>` with
`p` not dividing `deg_t(w)`. Then

    G  -->  (G * <t>)/<<w>>

is injective. This is Krstic's theorem (Invent. Math. 81 (1985), 373--378) for
the one-equation system `w = 1`.

## Consequences proved in the route

1. **Extensions.** If `H <| G`, `H` is locally `p`-indicable, and `G/H` is
   locally `p`-indicable, then so is `G`. In particular the class contains:
   - every finite `p`-group;
   - every residually finite-`p` group;
   - every extension of a locally indicable group by a finite `p`-group.
   These can carry `p`-torsion that is not central.
2. **Screen for violations.** If `(G, w)` violates
   `kervaire-laudenbach-nonsingular-conjecture` at degree `m`, then the
   finitely generated subgroup `A` generated by the coefficients of `w` is
   locally `p`-indicable for no prime `p` not dividing `m`. At `m = ±1` it is
   locally `p`-indicable for no prime at all: for every `p`, some nontrivial
   finitely generated subgroup of `A` has no quotient `Z/p`.

The screen is consistent with `kl-counterexample-can-be-two-generator-simple`,
since a nonabelian simple group has no `Z/p` quotient.
