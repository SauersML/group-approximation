- **Primary source.** W. Krieger, *On the subsystems of topological Markov chains*, Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 2 (1982), no. 2, 195–202, doi:10.1017/S0143385700001516. Title, journal, volume, issue, year and pages were checked against Crossref (2026-09-14); the paper itself was not opened.
- **Secondary statement, read at source (web fetch of the arXiv HTML, 2026-09-14).** arXiv:2606.25475, *A Krieger Embedding Theorem for Near Markov Sofic Shifts*, introduction, "Theorem 1 (W. Krieger)", quoted verbatim in the claim.
- **Where the hypotheses are used.** In the aperiodic zero-entropy case the periodic-point condition is vacuous and the strict entropy inequality holds against the full 2-shift, which is a mixing SFT.
