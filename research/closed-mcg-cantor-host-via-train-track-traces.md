By `train-track-trace-groupoid-hosts-closed-mcg`, the full group `D` of the trace
groupoid is finitely presented, full and clopen transitive on the Cantor space
`Ω`, and a finite-index subgroup `L ≤ Mod(S_g)` embeds in `D`. Those are exactly
the three hypotheses and the embedding asked for by
`closed-mcg-virtually-embeds-in-fp-full-cantor-group`.
