Let K be any field of characteristic 19, S=PSL2(F19), and
V=Sym^4(K^2) with its natural S-action. Then

    H^1(S,End_K(V))=0,
    Ext^1_(K[S])(V,V)=0.

Every finite-dimensional K[S]-module all of whose composition factors
are isomorphic to V is consequently a direct sum of copies of V.

This is specialized standard modular representation theory, with a
complete elementary proof. It removes a possible nonsemisimple escape
from a stable-Sym4 detector exclusion, but does not control mixed
composition factors or solve hyperbolic residual finiteness.
