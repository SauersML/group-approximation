title: "If a group acts properly and cocompactly by isometries on a CAT(0) space, every solvable subgroup is finitely generated and virtually abelian"
---

Let `Γ` act properly and cocompactly by isometries on a CAT(0) space `X`. Then
every solvable subgroup of `Γ` is finitely generated and virtually abelian
(the Solvable Subgroup Theorem, Gromoll–Wolf, Yau; Bridson–Haefliger, *Metric
Spaces of Non-Positive Curvature*, Part II, Chapter 7). The import is
`cat0-group-solvable-subgroups-are-virtually-abelian-citation`.

Use in the graph: a group that contains a Heisenberg group (for example any
finite-index subgroup of `GL_n(Z)`, `n >= 3`, or of `Sp_{2g}(Z)`, `g >= 3`) admits
no proper cocompact isometric action on a CAT(0) space.
