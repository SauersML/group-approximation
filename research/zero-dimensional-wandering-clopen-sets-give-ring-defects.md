**ESTABLISHED** (route `zero-dimensional-wandering-clopen-defects-proof`).

Let `X` be a compact zero-dimensional space, `T` a homeomorphism, `k` a field, and
`R = C(X,k) ⋊ T Z` the algebraic crossed product, written `sum_j f_j u^j` with
`u f u^-1 = f . T^-1`. Let `U` be clopen with `T(U) <= U`. Then

```text
s = u chi_U,     t = chi_U u^-1,     p = chi_U,        ts = p,     p - st = chi_(U \ T(U)),
```

so `(s,t)` is a one-sided pair of the corner `pRp`, and
`(s + 1 - p,\ t + 1 - p)` is a one-sided pair of `R` itself with defect `chi_(U \ T(U))`.

Consequently the Hilbert-hotel radical `hh(R)` contains the ideal generated by `chi_W` for every
wandering collar `W = U \ T(U)` of a clopen trapping region. Points outside the chain-recurrent core
are exactly the points that a clopen trapping region separates from their own future, so this is the
"hard" half of `zero-dimensional-hilbert-hotel-radical-is-core-ideal`, modulo the standard fact that
a compact zero-dimensional system has a basis of clopen trapping regions around each attractor.

**Calibration.** For `X = Z u {-inf, +inf}`, the two-point compactification with `T(n) = n+1`, take
`U = {+inf} u Z_(>=0)`. Then `T(U) = {+inf} u Z_(>=1)` and the defect is `chi_({0})`, whose ideal
is the finitary ideal `M_fin(Z,k)`; the quotient is `C({-inf,+inf},k) ⋊ Z = k[z,z^-1] x k[z,z^-1]`,
which is directly finite. So `hh(R)` is exactly the core-vanishing ideal here, and the printed
chain-core formula for the MF radical is the corresponding instance of
`mf-radical-sandwiched-by-hilbert-hotel-radical`. The same computation on the one-sided shift is the
Toeplitz-Jacobson algebra `k<s,t | ts=1>` of the manuscript.
