Because `z` and `u` are commuting involutions, reduce any word to
`z^a u^b`, with `a,b in F_2`.  The four joint characters give the table

```text
             z=+1             z=-1
T              1                u
z^a u^b        u^b          (-1)^a u^b.
```

Equality in the left column for both `u=+1` and `u=-1` forces `b=0`; equality
in the right column would then require one constant sign to equal both signs
of `u`.  This proves the word obstruction.

If a finite auxiliary group is adjoined, restriction to the Boolean subgroup
generated by the visible control and payload has the induced-character
extensions of `finite-group-selector-induction-barrier`.  Thus exhibiting the
desired block in one representation cannot establish the required
all-representations law.
