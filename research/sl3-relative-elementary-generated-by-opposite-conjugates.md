Let `A` be a commutative ring, `J` an ideal, and `E_3(A, J)` the normal
subgroup of `E_3(A) = <e_ij(A)>` generated by the `e_ij(t)` with `t ∈ J`.
Then `E_3(A, J)` is generated as a group by the elements

`z_ij(t, c) = e_ji(c) e_ij(t) e_ji(-c)` with `t ∈ J`, `c ∈ A`, `i != j`.

This is the `n = 3` case of the generation lemma usually attributed to
Vaserstein–Suslin and to Tits (1976; recalled, not read). The proof route is
self-contained.
