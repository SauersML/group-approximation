## Proof

Let `eta:C->Z` be the unital inclusion.  The Jiang--Su algebra is
KK-equivalent to `C`, with `[eta]` a KK-equivalence.  Pullback along `eta`
therefore gives an isomorphism

```text
KK(Z,B) -> KK(C,B).
```

Both `[phi]` and `[psi]` map to the class of the same unital inclusion
`C->B`.  Hence `[phi]=[psi]`.  Naturality of K-theory, including K-theory
with coefficients, gives the first conclusion.

If `tau` is a bounded trace on `B`, then `tau phi` and `tau psi` are traces on
`Z`, have the same value `tau(1_B)` at the unit, and `Z` has a unique tracial
state.  They are therefore both `tau(1_B) tau_Z`.  The same argument applies
to a bounded normalized 2-quasitrace because its restriction to the nuclear
algebra `Z` is a trace.

Finally, take nonzero `a in Z_+`.  Simplicity of `Z` says that the closed
two-sided ideal generated by `a` is all of `Z`; consequently finite sums
`sum_i x_i a y_i` approximate `1_Z`.  Applying either embedding shows that
`1_B` belongs to the closed ideal of `B` generated by the image of `a`.
That image is full.  Since the only ideals of `Z` are `0` and `Z`, the ideal
maps agree.
