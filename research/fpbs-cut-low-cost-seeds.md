**Statement.** Theorem 7.1 of the cut-repair note: let Gamma be a countable **nonamenable** group and
consider its atomless-base Bernoulli action. There are nested subrelations (S_t) for 0<=t<=1 with

    C(S_t) = 1+t,   S_t ergodic,
    C(S_t restricted to Y, normalized) = 1 + t/mu(Y) for every Y of positive measure,
    relC(S_t;S_s) = t-s for 0<=s<=t<=1,
    intersection_{t>0} S_t = S_0 modulo null sets, with S_0 hyperfinite.

So for t>0 the relation S_t is nonhyperfinite on every positive-measure restriction even though its
cost decreases to one. Nonamenability is a genuine hypothesis and not decoration: it is what supplies
the free ergodic F_2 subrelation the construction restricts.

**Proof location.** `research/artifacts/fpbs/cut-repair.md`, Section 7. Take an atomless uniform
coordinate h, put A_t = {h<=t}, and let S_t be generated by U on X together with V restricted to A_t,
where U,V are free generators of the F_2 treeing. The graphing is a subtreeing, so treeing minimality
identifies its cost as exactly 1+mu(A_t), rather than merely bounding it.

**Imported inputs.** Gaboriau-Lyons, *A Measurable-Group-Theoretic Solution to von Neumann's Problem*,
arXiv:0711.1643v2, Theorem 1, for a free ergodic F_2 orbit subrelation inside the Bernoulli relation of
any countable nonamenable group. Miller-Tserunyan, *Edge sliding and ergodic hyperfinite
decomposition*, arXiv:1704.06019v2, Theorem 1.3, to realize the same treeable subrelation with an
ergodic free generator; the note is careful that these are full-group transformations and not elements
of Gamma. Gaboriau, arXiv:1011.2294v1, for treeing minimality and cost two of a free F_2 treeing. All
three identifiers resolve with matching titles and authors.

**Finite-replay evidence.** `research/artifacts/fpbs/cut-repair-checks.json` via
`scripts/replay_cut_repair.py`: status PASS, 56,149 exact assertions, including 1,940 distinct support
families, all connected simple labelled graphs on up to five vertices, and 14,944 exhaustive colorings
over 277 finite subrelations. `cut-repair-audit.json` lists the infinite measurable-selection limit,
the Gaboriau-Lyons theorem, the Miller-Tserunyan theorem and treeing minimality as not tested by the
finite checks.

**Evidence level.** Written deduction from three verified published inputs, with a passing finite replay
of the combinatorial accounting only; the three substantive steps are imports. Not refereed, not
formally verified. The seeds are real objects, which is the point: they show a cost-one-approaching
family that stays nowhere hyperfinite, so cheapness does not by itself buy hyperfiniteness.

Written deduction or explicitly imported theorem; not independent mathematical validation.
