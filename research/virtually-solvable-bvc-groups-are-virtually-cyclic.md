Virtually solvable groups have property BVC if and only if they are virtually cyclic.

Source: von Puttkamer–Wu, arXiv:1607.03790v2, Proposition 1.12 (page 5). It combines their
Lemma 1.11 (BVC passes to finite-index subgroups, citing Kochloukova–Martínez-Pérez–Nucinkis
5.6) with J. R. J. Groves and J. S. Wilson, *Soluble groups with a finiteness condition arising
from Bredon cohomology*, Bull. London Math. Soc. 45 (2013) 89–92.
