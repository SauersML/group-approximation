Every elementary amenable group `G` of finite Hirsch length has a locally finite normal subgroup
`N` such that `G/N` is virtually solvable.

Source: J. A. Hillman and P. A. Linnell, *Elementary amenable groups of finite Hirsch length are
locally-finite by virtually-solvable*, J. Austral. Math. Soc. Ser. A 52 (1992), no. 2, 237–241.
