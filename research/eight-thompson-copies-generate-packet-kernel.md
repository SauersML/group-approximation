Sections 1--3 authenticate the native V embedding and show that setting
c=1 gives a split map `Q->C_2^3`. Move all b letters to the right:
its kernel is generated by `b_x c b_x^-1`. Since c lies in V, the
eight conjugates of V generate that kernel. They are perfect and
have no nontrivial finite-dimensional unitary representations, which
gives the stated kernel and radical identities.

Sections 5--7 prove the explicit finite-extension microstate construction,
the Bass--Serre fixed-vertex obstruction to the stated finite amalgam,
and the induced finite-wreath equivalence. None constructs compatible
microstates of the perfect kernel.
