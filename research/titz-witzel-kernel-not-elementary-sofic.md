**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `K` be any of the five simple Titz Mite--Witzel
finite residuals, and let `barGamma_1^2` be the index-two overgroup of
`Gamma_1^2`.  Each of them is isolated and not elementary sofic.

Hence none of the following can produce a sofic approximation of `K`:
subgroup inclusion, direct limits, marked limits of finitely generated
groups, extensions with amenable quotient, or any finite combination.  A
proof of `titz-witzel-kernel-sofic` would exhibit a finitely presented,
torsion-free, simple Kazhdan CAT(0) group that is sofic but not elementary
sofic, a positive answer to Cornulier's Question 5.
