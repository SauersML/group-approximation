**Direct proof** (lane proof, elementary, not reviewed; no priority claimed). Its only input is
the construction of `E_nu`. It strengthens `shell-germ-algebra-fp-forces-bounded-germ-presentation`
from "boundedly many input letters" to a finite presentation. The unbounded shift exponents found
there sit in columns whose entries are pure powers of `τ`, and those relations are free
consequences.

## Setting

`K` is any field. `P`, `S` (finite, symmetric), `nu`, `rho`, `hat(g)`, `E_nu = <V, hat(P)>` and
`p = 0^∞` are as in `shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`. `G_nu` is the germ
groupoid of `E_nu` on `C`, and `A = A_K(G_nu)` is its Steinberg algebra. `Q` is the isotropy of
`G_nu` at `p`, `τ ∈ Q` is the germ of `z ↦ 0z`, and `r_g ∈ Q` is the germ of `hat(g)`, so
`Q = <τ, r_g : g ∈ S>` (same node). `F = F(τ, S)` is the free group, and `π : F -> Q` is the
natural map.

`s_i` is the indicator of the compact open bisection of germs of `z ↦ iz` (`i = 0, 1`), `t_i`
is the indicator of the inverse bisection, and `u_g = 1_(hat(g))` for `g ∈ S`.

**Theorem.** If `A` is finitely presented as a `K`-algebra, then `Q` is finitely presented.

## Step 0: the generators

`A` is spanned by the indicators `1_([e, U])` of germ bisections of `e ∈ E_nu` over cylinders
`U = [w]`, because every compact open bisection is a finite disjoint union of such.
- Here `1_([e,[w]]) = 1_([e]) s_w t_w`, with `s_w = s_(w_1) ⋯ s_(w_k)` and `t_w = t_(w_k) ⋯ t_(w_1)`.
- `1_([e])` is a product of the `1_([v])` (`v ∈ V`) and the `u_g^(±1)`, and `u_g^(-1) = u_(g^(-1))`.
- Each `v ∈ V` is a prefix replacement `u_j z ↦ u'_j z` over a partition, so
  `1_([v]) = Σ_j s_(u'_j) t_(u_j)`.

So `A` is generated by `s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1` and `u_g` (`g ∈ S`). If `A` is finitely presented,
it has a presentation on these generators with finitely many relators `r_1, ..., r_k`: finite
presentation does not depend on the finite generating set.

## Step 1: the fibre model

Let `Ω = V·p = E_nu·p`, the eventually-zero sequences. For `x = u 0^∞ ∈ Ω`, with `u` empty or
ending in `1`, let `γ_x` be the germ at `p` of `0^(|u|) z ↦ u z`, so `γ_p = 1`.
- The set `G_p` of germs with source `p` is `⊔_x γ_x Q`.
- So `K[G_p]` is a free right `K[Q]`-module on `{γ_x}`.
- `A` acts on it on the left, by convolution, commuting with the right `Q`-action.

Write `a · γ_x = Σ_y γ_y a_(yx)` with `a_(yx) ∈ K[Q]`, and `(ab)_(yx) = Σ_z a_(yz) b_(zx)`. For the
indicator of a bisection `B`, `1_B · γ_x = β γ_x` for the unique `β ∈ B` with source `x`, or `0`.
So the column `x` of `1_B` is the single entry `γ_y^(-1) β γ_x` in row `y = r(β)`, or empty. The
computation in step 2 of `shell-germ-algebra-fp-forces-bounded-germ-presentation`, which uses only
the formula for `hat(g)`, gives:
- `s_0`, `s_1` have entry `τ`, and `t_0`, `t_1` have entry `τ^(-1)`, wherever they are defined;
- `u_g` at `x = 0^n 1 w' 0^∞` has entry `τ^(rho_g(n) - n)`;
- `u_g` at `p` has entry `r_g` and fixes `p`; `s_0`, `t_0` fix `p`; `s_1 p = 1 0^∞`; `t_1 p` is undefined.

**Consequence (the key point).** Every entry of every generator is a power of `τ`, except the
`(p,p)` entries of the `u_g`.

## Step 2: words, and the finite singular ball

For a monomial `m = a_1 a_2 ⋯ a_j` in the generators and a column `x`, set `z_j = x` and
`z_(i-1) = a_i z_i` while defined. If the path is defined, `m · γ_x = γ_(z_0) w_m(x)` with
`w_m(x) = e_1 e_2 ⋯ e_j`, where `e_i` is the entry of `a_i` at `z_i`. Read the same product as
a word `W_m(x) ∈ F`: write `τ^c` for a `τ`-power entry, and `g` for the entry `r_g`. Then
`π(W_m(x)) = w_m(x)`.

Let `X_0 ⊆ Ω` be the set of columns `x` such that, for some monomial `m` of some relator `r_i`,
the path of `m` from `x` visits `p` at some `z_i` with `i >= 1`.
- **`X_0` is finite.** Each generator is a partial injection of `Ω`. So `x` is determined by the
  monomial and the step: `x = (a_(i+1) ⋯ a_j)^(-1) p`. Hence `|X_0|` is at most the total
  length of the relators.
- **Outside `X_0`, words are powers of `τ`.** For `x ∉ X_0` no step of any relator monomial is a
  `u_g` applied at `p`. So every `W_m(x)` is a product of `τ`-powers, and freely equal to
  `τ^(N_m(x))`.
- **`τ` has infinite order in `Q`.** `τ^c` is the germ of `z ↦ 0^c z`, which is not the identity
  near `p` for `c ≠ 0`.

## Step 3: a finite relator set

Fix a relator `r = Σ_m c_m m` and columns `x, y`. Its `(y,x)` entry is
`Σ_(m : z_0 = y) c_m w_m(x) = 0` in `K[Q]`, because `r = 0` in `A` and the action is a
representation. Grouping the monomials by the value `w_m(x) ∈ Q`, the coefficients in each
group sum to `0`.

Let `R_0 ⊆ F` consist of the words `W_m(x) W_(m')(x)^(-1)`, over:
- `x ∈ X_0`;
- relators `r_i`;
- pairs of monomials of `r_i` whose paths from `x` are defined, end at the same row, and have
  `w_m(x) = w_(m')(x)`.

`R_0` is finite, and `π(R_0) = 1`. Put `Q_0 = F / <<R_0>>`, with `π_0 : Q_0 -> Q`.

**The model.** Give each generator the monomial column-finite `Ω × Ω` matrix over `K[Q_0]` with
the same positions, and with entries `τ^c` or `g` read in `Q_0`. Column-finite matrices form a
ring, so each relator gives a matrix, whose `(y,x)` entry is `Σ c_m [W_m(x)]` in `K[Q_0]`.
- For `x ∈ X_0`, words in the same group are equal in `Q_0` by `R_0`, so the entry is `0`.
- For `x ∉ X_0`, the group of `w_m(x)` is determined by `N_m(x)`, since `τ` has infinite
  order. The words in one group are all freely equal to the same `τ^N`, so the entry is again
  `0`.

So the generator matrices satisfy the relators, and define a unital homomorphism
`ψ : A -> M_Ω(K[Q_0])`. Unitality holds because the identity is `s_0 t_0 + s_1 t_1`, a
consequence of the relators whose model is the identity matrix.

## Step 4: every relation of `Q` holds in `Q_0`

Let `W ∈ F` with `π(W) = 1`. Let `m_W` be the monomial obtained by `τ ↦ s_0`, `τ^(-1) ↦ t_0` and
`g ↦ u_g`.
- **In the model.** Each letter fixes `p` with `(p,p)` entry equal to that letter. So the
  `p`-column of `ψ(m_W)` is the single entry `[W] ∈ Q_0` at `(p,p)`.
- **In `A`.** `m_W = 1_B` for the product bisection `B`. The germ of `B` at `p` is `π(W) = 1`,
  so `B` contains the unit at `p`. In an étale groupoid, even a non-Hausdorff one, the unit
  space is open, so `B` contains all units over some cylinder `[0^n]`. Hence
  `m_W e_n = e_n` with `e_n = 1_([0^n]) = s_0^n t_0^n`.
- **Comparing.** The `p`-column of `ψ(e_n)` is `τ^n τ^(-n) = 1` at `(p,p)`. Applying `ψ` and
  reading the `(p,p)` entry gives `[W] = 1` in `K[Q_0]`, so `[W] = 1` in `Q_0`.

So `ker π ⊆ <<R_0>>`, and the reverse inclusion is `π(R_0) = 1`. Hence `Q = <τ, S | R_0>` is
finitely presented. `∎`

## Why Attempt 1 stopped at bounded input length

Attempt 1 put into `Q'` every relation of input length at most `2ℓ`, from every column. The
columns in which step 2 of `shell-germ-algebra-fp-forces-bounded-germ-presentation` produces the
exponents `rho_g(n) - n` are the columns at shell `n`, far from `p`. There every entry is a
power of `τ`, so these relations are consequences of the free group alone. Only the finitely
many columns whose relator paths pass through `p` contribute relations.

## Consequences

1. **The algebra gate, as written, contains the germ half of the group gate.** Finite
   presentation of `A_nu` gives finite presentation of `Q_nu`. By
   `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate`, it therefore gives finite presentation of `R_nu`
   together with the ascending HNN condition, and by
   `accessible-shell-inputs-admit-no-shift-normalizing-enumeration` the gate needs a window
   `N >= 1` for every finitely presented input that is not virtually cyclic. This is the
   same condition that finite presentation of `E_nu` imposes
   (`shell-envelope-fp-forces-fp-germ-group`). The escape recorded in Attempt 1, an algebra
   gate free of the ascending-HNN clause, does not exist for `A_nu`.
2. **Generic enumerations.** For a generic `nu`, `Q_nu ≅ Q_gen = <P, τ | [P, τ^D P τ^(-D)], D odd>`
   by `generic-shells-have-infinitely-generated-singular-ideals`. `Q_gen` is not finitely
   presented: see `generic-shell-germ-algebras-are-not-finitely-presented`. So `A_nu` is not
   finitely presented for any generic enumeration of any infinite finitely generated input.
   The conditional "if `A_nu` is finitely presented then `A_nu/J` is not" of that node
   therefore has a false hypothesis there.
3. **What is left of the algebra route.** Its step 4 asks for finite presentation of `A_nu/J`,
   and proposes to obtain it from finite presentation of `A_nu` plus finite generation of `J`.
   That decomposition now requires `Q_nu` finitely presented. The only remaining way through
   the algebra is `A_nu/J` finitely presented while `A_nu` is not. For that version the same
   argument gives a finitely presented local algebra `K[Q]/J_p` instead of a finitely
   presented group: `shell-germ-quotient-algebra-fp-forces-fp-local-algebra`.
