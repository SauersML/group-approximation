# Proof

Full text: Section 3 of the artifact.

A coupling of `G` and `H` is a standard σ-finite `(Ω, m)` with commuting, free,
`m`-preserving actions of `G` and `H` having finite-measure fundamental domains
`D_G`, `D_H`. Groups coupled to a finite group are finite, and finite groups are
in 𝒮, so let `G`, `H` be infinite.

**Twisting.** Let `H ↷ (Z, ζ)` be arbitrary and `B = ({0,1}, fair)^(G×H)`. On
`Ω_Z = Ω × Z × B` let `g·(ω, z, b) = (gω, z, (g,1)b)` and
`h·(ω, z, b) = (hω, hz, (1,h)b)`.

1. These commute, preserve the measure, are free, and have fundamental domains
   `D_G^Z = D_G × Z × B` and `D_H^Z = D_H × Z × B`.
2. `H ↷ Y_Z = Ω_Z/G` and `G ↷ X_Z = Ω_Z/H` are essentially free: `h[x] = [x]` in
   `Y_Z` forces `(g^(-1), h)` to fix `x` for some `g`, and the `G × H` Bernoulli
   shift on the `B`-coordinate is essentially free.
3. `Ω_Z → Z` is `G`-invariant and `H`-equivariant, so `Z` is a factor of `Y_Z`.
4. With `E` the orbit relation of `G × H` and `W = D_G^Z ∪ D_H^Z`:
   `R(H ↷ Y_Z) ≅ E|_(D_G^Z)` and `R(G ↷ X_Z) ≅ E|_(D_H^Z)`, and both domains meet
   almost every class of `E|_W`.

**Transfer.** Since `G ∈ 𝒮`, `G ↷ X_Z` is a sofic free action. By
`sofic-free-actions-are-soe-invariant`, applied through `E|_W` (amplify from
`D_H^Z`, restrict to `D_G^Z`), the free action `H ↷ Y_Z` is sofic. Restricting its
sofic embedding to the von Neumann algebra generated by `L^∞(Z)` and the `u_h`
gives a sofic embedding of `L^∞(Z) ⋊ H`: the base still lands in the diagonal and
each `u_h` is still a permutation (Lemma A of
`sofic-action-class-commensurability-proof`). As `Z` was arbitrary, `H ∈ 𝒮`.

**Consequence 2 (trees).** `C_2^(*p)` acts simply transitively on the vertices of
`T_p`, so `C_2^(*p) × C_2^(*q)` is a cocompact lattice in `Aut(T_p) × Aut(T_q)`.
Its torsion-free finite-index subgroup `ker(C_2^(*p) → C_2^p) × ker(...)` is a
product of free groups (Kurosh), commensurable with `F_2 × F_2`. Lattices of one
group are coupled by Haar measure, and 𝒮 is closed under commensurability
(`sofic-action-class-closed-under-commensurability`).
