Artifact Section 4 gives the five entry calculations. A finite
monogenic F_7-algebra is a product of truncated polynomial rings over
finite fields: factor its defining polynomial, apply Chinese remainders,
and lift each residue field by the unique roots of X^q-X through its
nilpotent ideal. Hesselholt--Madsen's even relative K-group vanishing
for truncated polynomials over perfect positive-characteristic fields,
together with K_2 of finite fields being zero and the split residue
map, gives K_2=0 for every factor and hence for the product.

The precise external theorem and primary author link are supplied in
the artifact. This route imports that published K-theory theorem;
the matrix gauge and algebra decomposition are proved directly.
