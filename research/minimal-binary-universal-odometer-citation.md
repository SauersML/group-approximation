Su Gao and Ruiwen Li, [Subshifts of Finite Symbolic Rank,
Theorem 8.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.09352v2#S8), realize every infinite
odometer as the maximal equicontinuous factor of a minimal subshift of
symbolic rank two. Their displayed construction is over `{0,1}`.
The odometer `lim_j Z/j!Z` is infinite, so this gives exactly the target.
The statement and construction were checked in the primary source on
2026-09-13; Section 1 of the artifact records the specialization.
