Let `Q=A*B*F(h,k)`, where `A` and `B` are the two gate `V_4` groups, and
put

```text
P=<Q,z | h_0 z h z h_2 z^(-1),
          k_0 z k_1 z k z^(-1)>.
```

Then the canonical map `Q -> P` is injective.
