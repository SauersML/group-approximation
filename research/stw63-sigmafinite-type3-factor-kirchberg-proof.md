First verify the C-star-algebraic target hypothesis.  In a sigma-finite
type-III factor every two nonzero projections are Murray--von Neumann
equivalent.  If `0!=a in M_+`, choose `epsilon>0` such that the spectral
projection

```text
p=1_[epsilon,infinity)(a)
```

is nonzero, and choose `v in M` with `v*v=1` and `vv*=p`.  Functional
calculus on the spectral corner gives `y=a^(-1/2)p v in M`, and

```text
y* a y=1.                                                   (1)
```

Thus every nonzero positive element is full, so `M` is simple as a C-star
algebra.  Moreover `w=a^(1/2)y` is an isometry and `ww*` is an infinite
projection in the hereditary algebra generated by `a`.  Hence `M` is a
unital simple purely infinite C-star algebra.

The precise imported uniqueness result is Hua--White Theorem A.2
(Kirchberg's classification theorem): if `A` is separable, unital, and exact,
`B` is unital simple purely infinite, and two unital injective nuclear maps
`A->B` have equal `KK_nuc` class, then they are asymptotically unitarily
equivalent.  The theorem places no separability hypothesis on `B`.

Apply it with `A=Z` and `B=M`.  The maps are injective and nuclear because
`Z` is simple and nuclear.  The established invariant-collapse theorem gives
the same class in `KK(Z,M)`.  Since `Z` satisfies the UCT, the canonical map
from nuclear KK to ordinary KK is an isomorphism, so their `KK_nuc` classes
also agree.  Hua--White Theorem A.2 now gives the required continuous path of
unitaries in `M`.

The sigma-finite hypothesis is used exactly in the equivalence of nonzero
projections.  The argument does not import the stronger separable-predual
hypothesis of Hua--White Theorem A.1.  For a non-sigma-finite factor,
nonzero projections can have different cardinal dimension and generate
proper norm ideals, so this proof does not establish simplicity or pure
infiniteness of the factor as a C-star algebra.
