## Why sufficient

* **Part 1 (artifact Lemma 2.1).** `omega` is generated as a left ideal by the `g_i - 1`, since
  `gh - 1 = g(h-1) + (g-1)`. If `rho(X) = 0`, every `1 - [h]` has rank `0`, and the first part of
  `simple-group-rank-functions-are-augmentation-or-detecting` makes `rho` the augmentation rank. Lemma 1.1 of
  `sylvester-rank-functions-descend-to-quotients-and-compressions` gives `rho_omega`.
* **Part 2 (Corollary 2.3).** `h -> h - 1` is a homomorphism `G -> omega/omega^2` with spanning image, so a perfect
  `G` has `omega = omega^2`. The idempotent-ideal clause of the ideal-quotient lemma gives
  `rho_omega(X Z) = rho_omega(Z)`, so `rho_omega(X) = 1`, and stacking gives the displacement bound.
* **Part 3 (Proposition 3.1).**
  - The two-sided ideal generated by `omega_Gamma` contains `n - 1` for every `n` in the normal closure, so
    `X = Y [X_S h_1; ...; X_S h_m]` for finitely many right translates `h_j`.
  - For `h` in `P_Gamma`, `X_(hSh^-1) = Y_h X_S = (h (x) I) X_S h^-1` has the rank of `X_S`, and the Frobenius
    inequality turns this into `psi(X_S Z) = psi(X_S h^-1 Z)` for all `Z`. The invariant `h` form a group, which
    is `G`.
  - Hence `psi(Z) = psi(X Z) <= sum_j psi(X_S h_j Z) = m psi(X_S Z)`.
