**OPEN — proposed multiscaling envelope family; independent review pending; not Lean certified; no priority claim.**

Let `S` be a nonempty finite set of primes, `m≥2`, `d≥0`, and `h≥1`.
Let `E_{d,h}` be the additive group of integer profiles which, on each
residue class modulo `h`, are eventually integer-valued polynomials of
degree at most `d` independently at each end, with arbitrary finite
exceptions. The proposed source is

`UT_m(Z[S^-1])^(Z) ⋊ (E_{d,h}^S ⋊ Z)`.

The integer factor shifts lamp positions. A vector profile `(f_s)` acts
at position `n` by `∏_{s∈S}δ_s^{f_s(n)}`. This source, and also its
subfamily obtained by replacing `E_{d,h}^S` by the global polynomial
profiles `P_d^S`, embeds in a simple group of type `F_∞` generated by
two elements of finite order.

The conclusion also holds for every finite product of these source
groups, allowing different finite parameter choices in different
factors, and every finite extension of such a product. More generally,
it holds for an overgroup containing such a product as a finite-index
subgroup, by passing to the normal core. All parameters are finite and
fixed for each instance; no single common host for unbounded degrees,
periods, or prime sets is asserted.

## Attempts

The new uniform multiscaling tower/actor claim is the missing finiteness
gate. Conditional on that claim, the extension lemma proposes the
two-root vector shell embedding, detects each twist by multiplicative
independence of the primes, and handles finite exceptions by the
per-shell coordinate comparison. Residue classes reduce fixed-period
profiles to finite products. Kaloujnine–Krasner and block actions give
finite extensions of the resulting actors; their finite-subset
stabilizers must retain type `F_∞` before BZ applies.

These embedding and extension assertions, as well as the uniform tower,
remain OPEN. The conditional route records both prerequisites. The
construction for finite-index overgroups uses the actor embedding of
the normal core, not an unsupported closure assertion for arbitrary
simple groups. See artifact §§2 and 10 for the classical sources and
the full proposed proof mechanisms.
