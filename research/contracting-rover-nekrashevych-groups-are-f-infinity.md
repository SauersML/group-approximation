**OPEN** as printed; partially settled on main. R. Skipper, M. C. B. Zaremsky,
*Almost-automorphisms of trees, cloning systems and finiteness properties*,
arXiv:1709.06524v2 (TeX main.tex l.1365–1367):

> Is V_d(G) of type F_∞ for every finitely generated, contracting self-similar G?

(The companion question at l.1371, whether some finitely generated self-similar `G`
has `V_d(G)` not of type `F_∞`, is recorded there as solved by Skipper–Witzel–Zaremsky.)

**Status on main.** `rover-nekrashevych-polynomial-activity-tower-sigma-full`
(lane proof) covers contracting `G` of polynomial activity growth. Nekrashevych
proved `V_d(G)` finitely presented for contracting `G`; the higher finiteness
properties for general contracting `G` are what this node asks. It is the
Röver–Nekrashevych analogue of BBMZ-hyperbolic Question 1.2 ("Do full, contracting
RSGs have type F∞?", recorded in `research/artifacts/gq-bh-bh-lit-survey.md`).
Before attacking it, check Belk–Hyde–Matucci arXiv:2407.03149 (finite germ
extensions give `F_n` criteria) for a direct answer.

## Attempts

1. **Status at source, and a reduction to covers (2026-09-18, lane `bh-free-06`).** *Reduced;
   claim stays OPEN.*
   - **Status at source.**
     - Nekrashevych asks the question for `V_f` in arXiv:1312.5654 (TeX l.171–175) and proves
       finite presentation (Thm `th:finitepresentation`, l.1948).
     - Skipper–Zaremsky (their TeX l.1355–1369) record Bartholdi–Geoghegan's unpublished 2014
       claim.
     - Belk–Hyde–Matucci arXiv:2407.03149 prove `F_∞` for bounded automata. Their footnote says
       the Bartholdi–Geoghegan approach "has not yielded a complete proof".
     - Li arXiv:2110.04505 gives `F_n` criteria that need faithful invertibles: condition (F).
     - Perego–Tarocchi arXiv:2412.04138 still list Nekrashevych's IMG question as open.
   - **New transfer theorem** `wreath-recursion-thompson-groups-inherit-fn`.
     - For any group `B` and any wreath recursion `ψ`, injective or not, `V_{d,r}(B, ψ)` has type
       `F_n` whenever `B` has.
     - It equals `V_{d,r}(G)` exactly when the kernel of `B → G` dies under splitting.
     - The method is an EI cloning category with a calculus of fractions, and Quillen's Theorem A.
       Descending links are polyhedral joins over `d`-matching complexes with fibers `⊔BK_1`.
   - **Consequences.**
     - Finite presentation for every contracting `G`, re-proved.
     - `F_∞` for Röver's group, re-proved.
     - `F_∞` for `V_f` of every hyperbolic PCF rational map and every expanding covering of a
       compact aspherical space: `hyperbolic-pcf-rational-nekrashevych-groups-are-f-infinity`.
   - **What is left.** The whole question is now
     `contracting-groups-have-dying-kernel-covers-of-type-fn`.
     - Torsion-free covers are excluded whenever a torsion element recurs along a ray, as in the
       Grigorchuk group.
     - So the candidates are covers generated by finite groups.

2. **What the bounded-RSG machinery reaches (2026-09-19, lane `bh-sz-vdg`).** *Scoping; the claim stays OPEN.*
   - See `level-set-route-reaches-only-bounded-rover-nekrashevych-groups`.
   - **Zero defect.** Every element of `V_{d,r}(G)` has zero defect, because Röver–Nekrashevych groups have surjective nucleus.
   - **When the machinery applies.** `V_{d,r}(G)` is a bounded RSG exactly when the non-identity nucleus graph has no path between distinct cycles and no strongly connected component other than a single cycle. That is the bounded-automata case, which Belk–Hyde–Matucci already proved (arXiv:2407.03149, abstract read at source).
   - **So nothing new here.** The level-set, torsion-defect and defect-free theorems add nothing for this question.
   - **Singular-set trichotomy.** It is finite, countable or continuum, according as the activity is bounded, polynomial or exponential.
   - **Status by case.**
     - The polynomial case rests on the unreviewed `rover-nekrashevych-polynomial-activity-tower-sigma-full`.
     - The exponential case is open, except for dying-kernel covers from expanding coverings of compact aspherical spaces.
   - **Most concrete next case.** Sub-hyperbolic PCF rational maps, which need an orbifold version of Nekrashevych's `pr:expandingpi1`.

3. **All post-critically finite rational maps (2026-09-19, lane `bh-sz-vdg`).** *New case; the claim stays OPEN.*
   - See `pcf-rational-nekrashevych-groups-are-f-infinity`. It is OPEN (proof claimed, under review by bh-ref-g), and rests on the transfer theorem, which bh-ref-q12 is reviewing.
   - For every PCF rational map, `V_{d,r}(IMG f)` has type `F_∞`. This includes sub-hyperbolic maps with postcritical points in the Julia set.
   - **The cover.** It is the orbifold fundamental group of Thurston's orbifold.
   - **Why the kernel dies.** Short orbifold loops are elliptic by a displacement lemma, and elliptic elements map injectively to `IMG f`, since cone orders equal Thurston's `ν`.
   - **Credits.** Bounded automata are BHM's. The (P) row is BHM Remark 2.14 made complete, and uses Sidki's circuit theorem.
   - **What is left of case (E).** Contracting groups not of dynamical origin. They need a geometric dying-kernel cover.

4. **Geometric form of the rest of case (E) (2026-09-19, lane `bh-sz-vdg`).** *Reformulation; the claim stays OPEN.*
   - See `contracting-groups-have-aspherical-expanding-models`.
   - An expanding self-covering of a compact orbi-complex with contractible universal cover, whose finite local groups inject into `G`, gives `F_∞` by the same displacement argument.
   - The limit orbispace itself fails as a model whenever it is not semi-locally simply connected. Examples are the basilica Julia set and the Sierpiński gasket.
   - Hence the open ingredient is an aspherical thickening of the limit orbispace.
