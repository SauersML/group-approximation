**OPEN** as printed; partially settled on main. R. Skipper, M. C. B. Zaremsky,
*Almost-automorphisms of trees, cloning systems and finiteness properties*,
arXiv:1709.06524v2 (TeX main.tex l.1365–1367):

> Is V_d(G) of type F_∞ for every finitely generated, contracting self-similar G?

(The companion question at l.1371, whether some finitely generated self-similar `G`
has `V_d(G)` not of type `F_∞`, is recorded there as solved by Skipper–Witzel–Zaremsky.)

**Status on main.** `rover-nekrashevych-polynomial-activity-tower-sigma-full`
(lane proof) covers contracting `G` of polynomial activity growth. Nekrashevych
proved `V_d(G)` finitely presented for contracting `G`; the higher finiteness
properties for general contracting `G` are what this node asks. It is the
Röver–Nekrashevych analogue of BBMZ-hyperbolic Question 1.2 ("Do full, contracting
RSGs have type F∞?", recorded in `research/artifacts/gq-bh-bh-lit-survey.md`).
Before attacking it, check Belk–Hyde–Matucci arXiv:2407.03149 (finite germ
extensions give `F_n` criteria) for a direct answer.
