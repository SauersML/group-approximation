title: Ascending chains of V-invariant subgroups of each lower central quotient of P_br stabilize
---

Let `P_br = ker(V_br -> V)` and `gr_c := gamma_c(P_br)/gamma_{c+1}(P_br)` for `c >= 1`.
Conjugation in `V_br` induces an action of `V` on `gr_c`, since `P_br` acts trivially.
For each `c`, every ascending chain of `V`-invariant subgroups of `gr_c` stabilizes.

The invariant subgroups of `gr_c` embed, preserving strict inclusions, into the
subrepresentations of the finitely generated `FS^op`-representation
`n ↦ gamma_c(PB_n)/gamma_{c+1}(PB_n)`, whose structure maps come from cabling strands.
