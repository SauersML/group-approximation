Let `(X,T)` be a compact zero-dimensional system with compatible onto
cyclic factors of orders `N` and `m=3rN`, where `r,N >= 1`. Put
`R=LC(X,F_2) semidirect Z`, `A=qRq` for the zero cell `q` at level `m`.
Embed `GL_(3N)(F_2)` into `EL_3(R)` through the level-`N` matrix units
`E_ab=1_(pi_N^{-1}(a)) u^(a-b)`.

The centralizer of this entire finite linear group in `EL_3(R)` contains
an injective copy of `EL_3(A)^r semidirect Sym(r)`, with the natural
permutation action on the factors. No minimality or property (T) is
assumed in this matrix identity.
