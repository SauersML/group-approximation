Let `B`, `d`, `D`, and

```text
K_X=*_(D,x in X) B_x
```

be as in the universal-outer-symmetry theorem, with `B!=D` and `X`
countably infinite.  Every self-map `f:X->X` induces an endomorphism `Phi_f`
of `K_X` which sends `B_x` identically onto `B_(f(x))` and fixes `D`
pointwise.  The assignment

```text
End_Set(X) -> End_Group(K_X),   f |-> Phi_f             (UEM1)
```

is an injective monoid homomorphism, and it detects the three basic map
classes exactly:

```text
Phi_f is injective   iff f is injective,
Phi_f is surjective  iff f is surjective,
Phi_f is invertible  iff f is bijective.               (UEM2)
```

The same group is countably self-absorbing over its distinguished cyclic
subgroup.  For every nonempty countable set `I`,

```text
*_(D,i in I) K_X^(i) ~= K_X.                           (UEM3)
```

Consequently `End(K_X)` contains every countable monoid.  The group `K_X`
is both non-Hopfian and non-co-Hopfian, while remaining countable,
centerless, perfect, singly normally generated by an involution, and of full
MF radical.

For an equivalence relation `E` on `X`, let `N_E` be the normal closure of

```text
b_x b_y^(-1)     (x E y, b in B).
```

Then

```text
K_X/N_E ~= K_(X/E).                                    (UEM4)
```

For a self-map `f`, one has

```text
ker(Phi_f)=N_(ker f),       im(Phi_f)=K_(f(X)).         (UEM5)
```

Every noninjective self-map has the same abstract kernel:

```text
f noninjective  ==>  ker(Phi_f) ~= F_infinity.          (UEM5a)
```

The map `E |-> N_E` is an order embedding of the partition lattice of `X`
into the normal-subgroup lattice of `K_X` and preserves arbitrary joins.  If
`X` is countably infinite, continuum many distinct `N_E` satisfy

```text
K_X/N_E ~= K_X,
```

and every one of these quotient maps splits.

Every nonzero `N_E` is the free group of countably infinite rank.  In
particular, the continuum family satisfies

```text
N_E ~= F_infinity,
K_infinity ~= F_infinity semidirectProduct K_infinity. (UEM6)
```

Thus one full-MF-radical group has continuum many distinct normal copies of
`F_infinity` giving split self-quotients.  In particular, the MF radical is
not hereditary even to normal subgroups: each nonzero `N_E` is MF and has
trivial MF radical, while `K_infinity` has full MF radical.
