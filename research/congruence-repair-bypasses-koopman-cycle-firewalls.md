Let `(pi_(n,+),pi_(n,-))` be exact same-dimensional representations of the
two modular vertices, each factoring through an arbitrary congruence
quotient, and suppose their Iwahori mismatch tends to zero.  Then they are
`o(1)`-close in normalized HS to a literally compatible pair, uniformly in
all odd levels, dyadic depths, CRT labels, and dimensions.

This is the direct consequence of
`mixed-crt-iwahori-repair-is-labeled-pure-dyadic-rounding` and
`odd-label-stabilization-has-uniform-repair`: the dyadic theorem repairs
every pure label, while odd-label stabilization balances the label fibers
without enlarging the dyadic constant.

Consequently none of the long fourth-power cycles used in
`bs14-periodic-koopman-modes-refute-skeleton-regularity`, including the
nonlinear two-jump phase packet of
`two-jump-cycle-refutes-functional-calculus-gauge-bound`, is a surviving
obstruction once the two exact vertices lie in congruence quotients.  The
repair theorem is global in normalized HS and does not invoke a Taylor
remainder, an operator-norm basin, or a cyclewise logarithm.

Therefore any countersequence to Iwahori repair, or any application of
`weyl-defect-koopman-spectral-regularity` which is still genuinely needed,
must fail congruence capture: after localization it must contain modular-
vertex packets which remain a positive normalized-HS distance from every
congruence pair.  The remaining dynamical theorem is thus not about the
periodic congruence cycles themselves.  It is equivalent in role to the
relative arithmetic preprocessing problem
`regular-iwahori-relative-congruence-exactification` on the regular branch.
