For the reduced projectors f_0,P_0, K=GL_2(F_4), and the binary
Leavitt actor

    g=diag(a,b), a=[[1,s_0],[t_0,0]], b=[[1,s_1],[t_1,0]],

every d in F_2[K] gives a nonzero full-source kernel for
P_0[g]d f_0 in the natural boundary exterior square. Thus
L P_0[g]d f_0=f_0 has no group-ring solution, including with
arbitrary ambient support for L.

The proof reduces all right coefficients to four cases and
uses exact natural-module kernels to construct exterior source
kernels. It requires no bounded-support assumption on d.

The separate left-coefficient theorem and this right-coefficient
theorem do not settle simultaneous arbitrary coefficients in
P_0 c[g]d f_0 or sums of such terms. Kaplansky's conjecture
remains unresolved by these exclusions.
