Sections 0–3 of the artifact.

* **Item 1.** Borel–Cantelli makes a proper closed image `mu`-null, so `nu` and
  the graph joining are singular to the product measures. The malleable
  deformation is a transformation of the measure algebra of `mu x mu` and does
  not act on singular measures.
* **Item 2.** The prerequisite supplies the strict coset injection for every
  base space. The coordinatewise rotation deformation of `[0,1]^(G/H)` makes the
  generalized Bernoulli shift s-malleable. It is weakly mixing on `G` because
  `G/H` is one infinite orbit. The orbit-equivalence and W*-superrigidity
  conclusions are statements up to conjugacy, and `tau` is a conjugacy onto
  `nu`.
* **Item 3.** Over each coset `g<s>`, the edges `{g, gs}` with `g` in a finite
  window form a forest. So the XOR map is onto on finite windows and pushes
  uniform to uniform, and its kernel is the configurations constant on the
  cosets of `<s>`.
