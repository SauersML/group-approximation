**ESTABLISHED (swarm-0917 w12, agent-verified, no referee)** by route
`p22-double-parabolic-is-not-lef-proof`. No property (T), spectral gap,
trace or Steinberg presentation is used. Both artifacts exit 0: an exact
check over `J = F_2<S,T|TS=1>`, and a signed check over `J_Z = Z<S,T|TS=1>`.

**Setting.**
* `R` is a ring with elements `t s = 1`, and `q = 1 - st`.
* `P = P_{2,2}(R) = M_2(R) x| (EL_2(R) x EL_2(R))` inside `EL_4(R)`, with
  `n(Y) = [[1,Y],[0,1]]` and `diag(A,B) n(Y) diag(A,B)^{-1} = n(A Y B^{-1})`.
* The head is `h = x_13(q)`. For `R = J` this is the Jacobson head `x_13(Q)`.

**Certificate.** Take five elements of `P`:
* `u = x_13(1)`;
* `g = x_12(s) x_21(-t) x_12(s) x_21(1) x_12(-1) x_21(1) = diag(u2, 1)`,
  where `u2 = [[s,-q],[0,t]]` and `u2^{-1} = [[t,0],[-q,s]]`
  (over `F_2` this is `wK` with `w` the Whitehead involution);
* `e = x_12(1)`;
* `r1 = x_34(-1)`;
* `r2 = x_43(-s) x_34(-1)`.

They satisfy:
* **(P1)** `[g,r1] = [g,r2] = 1`, since the Levi blocks commute.
* **(P2)** `g u g^{-1} = x_13(s) = (r2 u r2^{-1})(r1 u r1^{-1})^{-1}`.
* **(P3)** `[e,u] = [e,r1] = [e,r2] = 1`.
* **(H)** `g^{-1} u g = x_13(t) x_23(-q)`, and
  `[e, g^{-1} u g] = x_13(-q) = h^{-1}`.

**Theorem.** Let `Gamma_pig = <g,e,u,r1,r2 | P1, P2, P3>` (6 relators).
* (a) In every group `G` and every homomorphism `Gamma_pig -> G` for which
  the subgroup `A` generated by the `<r1,r2>`-conjugates of `u` is finite,
  the word `[e, g^{-1}ug]` maps to `1`.
* (b) The same holds when `G = GL_n(k)` for any field `k` and any `n`, with
  `A` arbitrary. The proof uses the Zariski closure of `A` in place of `A`.
* (c) Hence every finite quotient of `P_{2,2}(R)`, and every
  finite-dimensional linear representation of it over any field, kills
  `h = x_13(1-st)`. When `st != 1`, `P_{2,2}(R)` is not LEF, not residually
  finite, and not linear over any field. `Gamma_pig` is a finitely presented
  group that is not residually finite.

**The mechanism in one line.** In `P`, `A = n(E_11 V)`, where `V` is the
additive span of the orbit `(1,0).B^{-1}`, `B` in `<r1,r2>`, inside the row
space `R^2`. On `A`, `Ad g` acts as left multiplication by `s`. That map is
injective, since `ts = 1`, and not onto, since `(1,0)` is not in `sR^2`. In
a finite, or Noetherian, image an injective self-map of `A` is onto. So `g^{-1}ug` falls back into `A`, which `e`
centralizes. This is the Baumslag-Solitar mechanism, with the one-sided
inverse pair `(s,t)` in place of `a -> a^2`.

## Consequences

1. **The w9/w12 open question is settled.** "Is `P_{2,2}(J) <= EL_4(J)`
   LEF?" has the answer no. The parabolic threshold is therefore:
   * `P_{2,1}(J)` is LEF (`jacobson-rank-three-parabolics-are-lef`);
   * `P_{2,2}(J)` is not.
2. **A new proof for `EL_4`.** `EL_4(J)`, and so every `EL_n(J)` with
   `n >= 4`, is not LEF. This proof uses only (P1)-(P3), not the finite
   presentation of `St_4(J)`. It also covers `EL_4(R)` for every ring that
   is not directly finite.
3. **The `Gamma_trap`/(C) programme is moot.** It
   (`double-parabolic-head-dies-on-commuting-right-orbits`,
   `gamma-trap-degree-one-layer-is-a-matrix-ring-module`) aimed at non-LEF
   through (C)-propagation. That goal is now reached without (C).

## Class kill

**Class.** Head-keeping models `sigma` of `P_{2,2}(J)` in which the orbit
group `A` has a "co-Hopfian" hull. This includes:
* finite quotients;
* LEF partial models on the ball containing (P1)-(P3) and (H);
* finite-dimensional unitary or linear representations, over any field;
* more generally, any `sigma` for which `Ad sigma(g)` restricted to a
  closed hull of `sigma(A)` that `sigma(e)` centralizes is onto.

**Invariant.** The endomorphism `Ad sigma(g)` of the hull of `sigma(A)`.
Relation (P2) forces this map into the hull.

**Step where every member dies.** Pigeonhole, or equality of dimension and
component count for the Zariski closure, makes the endomorphism onto. Then
`sigma(h) = 1`.

## Where it stops: the MF gap

This is a decomposition. It does not close the hole.

**The necessary condition.** Let `sigma` be any unitary representation that
keeps `h`, and let `C*(sigma(A)) = C(Y_sigma)`, which is abelian because `A`
lies in the abelian radical. Then `sigma(g)^{-1} sigma(u) sigma(g)` is not in
`C(Y_sigma)`. So `Ad sigma(g)` is a proper unital endomorphism of
`C(Y_sigma)`, and its dual `f_sigma : Y_sigma ->> Y_sigma` is surjective and
not injective. In `lambda_P` the space `Y` is the Pontryagin dual of `V`, and `f`
is the dual of left multiplication by `s` on `V`.

**What this means for (HC).** Every head survivor has this form. In
particular, by `p22-head-survival-forces-regular-weak-containment`, any MF
model weakly containing `lambda_P` must be a non-LEF MF model whose Levi
unitary compresses an abelian subalgebra properly into itself.

**No finiteness obstruction applies.** Proper endomorphisms implemented by
unitaries do occur in quasidiagonal algebras. An example is `Ad t` on
`C*(a)` in `C*(BS(1,2))`.

**The remaining P22-local question, sharpened.** Is there an MF model of
`P_{2,2}(J)` realizing `f_sigma` non-injectively? Equivalently, is `P` MF?

**Toy calibration (open).** Consider the amalgam
`BS(1,2) *_<a> (<a> x <e>)`, where `e` centralizes `a` but not `t^{-1}at`.
It carries the same mechanism with no ring structure.
* If its head `[e, t^{-1}at]` dies in every MF model, the pigeonhole has an
  MF shadow.
* If it survives, any MF proof of (HC) must use the Levi relations of
  `P_{2,2}(J)` beyond (P1)-(P3).
