The first claim gives a finitely presented, infinite, simple, sofic group
`S = EL_n(R) / Z(EL_n(R))`. The second makes `S` hyperlinear.

This route meets the root's preferred strengthening "sofic". It adds property (T). It does not
meet "torsion-free": `EL_n(R)` contains the elementary abelian root subgroups `e_12(R)` when `R`
has positive characteristic.
