**OPEN TYPEWISE GROUPIFICATION GATE.**  For the fixed robust non-CE BCS and
its context-local Schur packets, construct a finite recursive presentation
whose level-`n` matrix decoding supplies the objects in `(PCS1)--(PCS4)` of
`partitioned-child-surplus-collision-supplies-contraction`, with the following
additional properties.

1. The source family is a fixed finite menu of the genuine corrected Schur
   child types.  It satisfies

   ```text
   sum_i tr(C_(n,i))>=a_n+v_n-Cp(n)sqrt(E),            (TSP1)
   ```

   where `v_n` is measured on the original shared noncommuting BCS tuple,
   not on independent context copies.
2. The target family is a pairwise orthogonal partition below the next
   recursive-tail carrier, and fixed-table alignment plus ordinary addressed
   relations gives

   ```text
   sum_i ||(1-D_(n+1,i))U_(n,i)C_(n,i)||_2^2
      <=Cp(n)sqrt(E).                                  (TSP2)
   ```
3. The level-zero carrier has a fixed marked floor, all addressed derivation
   costs are polynomial, and the presentation has an exact infinite
   representation in which the marked word survives.  In that completion
   the BCS violation surplus is zero, so no trace-functorial strict
   contraction is asserted.

The local finite-type ingredients already exist:
`groupify-schur-child-center-chart` exposes the complete corrected child type
table without naming its nonlinear center;
`translation-symmetrized-schur-child-reset` balances that table;
`hnn-selector-chart-transport-preserves-mark-and-types` transports it; and
`free-cone-shift-finitely-wordizes-recursive-clifford-tails` supplies the
addressed recursive tail.  The remaining operation is narrower than the old
aggregate wordization: install those finitely many transported types as
orthogonal target pieces of one next carrier while retaining the shared BCS
surplus estimate `(TSP1)`.

At the Reynolds-atom level, the transport/orthogonality half is now explicit:
`index-two-reynolds-atoms-have-orthogonal-hnn-transports`
moves an index-two Reynolds difference by ordinary pair covariance and
places distinct differences in orthogonal elementary-abelian character
atoms.  What it cannot do—and what remains load-bearing here—is select a
supercritical active family by literal Bass--Serre relations.  That selection
must still consume the original shared non-CE tuple and fail in the regular
representation.

The orthogonality must be a finite-matrix consequence of the recurrent
packet/tail incidence, not an exact exhaustive partition with positive
surplus in the regular representation.  The latter would violate the regular
firewall for the same reason as `exact-first-hit-ladder-kills-regular-carrier`.
The one-scale analogue of this firewall and its genuinely global shared-BCS
requirement are recorded by `shared-bcs-eight-slice-symmetric-return`; a
contextwise finite-tag partition is not a solution here either.

The remaining datum is decomposed as
`shared-bcs-matrix-only-supercritical-atom-selection`.  Once that claim
selects the active family from the common contextual tuple, the established
index-two pair router and partitioned collision theorem provide the rest of
this compiler; see
`typewise-tail-partition-via-matrix-only-atom-selection`.
