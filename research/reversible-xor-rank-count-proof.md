Section 6 of the artifact proves both inverse identities by substitution.
With alpha_j=(-1)^(a_j), beta_j=(-1)^(b_j), set m_k=T^k alpha_0 for every
integer k. The one-site module is generated by beta_0,m_0,m_1.
Because S=T^(-1), the depth-n module consists exactly of the beta_0 orbit
and the m_k orbits for -n<=k<=n+1.

Each m_k has a unique alpha occurrence. Anchoring it at zero distinguishes
the beta supports for all integers k, including zero and negative k.
The beta orbit contains no alpha occurrence. These are 2n+3 distinct
orbits of tensor-basis vectors. Their stabilizers in Z are trivial, so
each contributes exactly one von Neumann dimension. This proves the
formula for every n, independently of the finite replay depth.
