**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G = <X | R>` be a finitely presented group with no nontrivial finite
quotient, for example an infinite finitely presented simple group. For a finite group `Q` and
`a in Q^X`, `w_Q(a)` is the least `K` such that every generator `a_x` is a product of at most `K`
`Q`-conjugates of relator values `r(a)^(+-1)`. It is finite by (E1) of
`simple-group-relator-width-in-finite-simple-targets-proof`.

**Theorem.** Let `V` be a locally finite variety of groups, so the free group `F_V(X)` of `V` on `X` is
finite. There is `K = K(G, V)` such that `w_Q(a) <= K` for every finite group `Q`, every `a in Q^X`,
and every nilpotent normal subgroup `P` of `Q` with `Q/P` in `V`. The nilpotency class of `P` is
unrestricted.

**Special cases.**
- **Bounded Fitting quotient.** For each `N`, the width is bounded over all finite `Q` with
  `|Q / F(Q)| <= N`. Take `V` generated by the finitely many groups of order `<= N`.
- **Deep unipotent towers over a bounded top.** For fixed `n` and `q`, the width is bounded over
  `GL_n(F_q[t]/(t^k))`, `GL_n(Z/p^k)` and their subgroups, uniformly in `k`. The congruence kernel
  is a `p`-group of index at most `|GL_n(q)|`.
- **Bounded order and bounded type.** Take `P = 1`: the width is bounded over any locally finite
  variety. Examples are the groups of exponent dividing `e` (by Zelmanov's positive solution of the
  restricted Burnside problem; theorem number not re-checked, and not used elsewhere here), and
  the groups `T^n . B` with `T`, `B` of bounded order.
- **Nilpotent targets.** With `V` trivial this is `perfect-group-relator-width-over-nilpotent-groups`
  for groups without finite quotients.

**Consequence for weak models.** If `G` is also simple, every sequence of weak models of `G` with
any bi-invariant lengths must eventually leave each such class. So its targets `Q_n` satisfy
`|Q_n / F(Q_n)| -> infinity`, and the quotients `Q_n / F(Q_n)` leave every locally finite
variety. For the binary Leavitt unit group this kills weak models in congruence towers over a
bounded residue group. That was the "deep unipotent structure" left open on
`binary-leavitt-unit-group-is-weakly-sofic`, in the case of a bounded top.

**Mechanism.**
- The tuple's image in `Q/P` is one of finitely many marked groups, since it is a quotient of
  `F_V(X)`.
- For each of them, the kernel `F_b` of `F(X) -> Q/P` is a free group of finite index. `G` has no
  finite quotient, so `N F_b = F`. `G` is perfect, so `N ∩ F_b` maps onto the abelianization of
  `F_b`.
- The nilpotent theorem, run inside `F_b` with finitely many products of relator conjugates as
  its relators, gives one template for every class.

**Where the room is now.** In finite groups whose Fitting quotients are unbounded and outside
every locally finite variety: iterated wreath products of nonabelian simple groups (trivial
Fitting subgroup, unbounded exponent), symmetric and classical tops over nilpotent kernels, and
products of those.
