For every infinite minimal binary subshift `Y` and positive integer `k`,
repeat each letter `k` times and take the union of the `k` shift phases,
denoted `Z_k(Y)`. It is an infinite minimal binary subshift and

`LC(Z_k(Y),F_2) semidirect Z ~= M_k(LC(Y,F_2) semidirect Z)`.

Consequently `EL_(3k)(LC(Y,F_2) semidirect Z)` is itself a binary Pestov
group: infinite, simple, Kazhdan, LEF, and generated by three elements
of orders `7,2,2`. No isomorphism with the original rank-three group is
asserted.
