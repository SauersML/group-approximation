Write `X = G/Gamma`, a transitive `G`-set with point stabilizer `Gamma`.  Fix
`e in FIN`.

## 1. A `Z/2`-wreath is a retract of the target

By [[machine-center-vanishes-exactly-on-inf]], `Phi_e != 1` on FIN, and `Phi_e`
is elementary abelian of exponent 2, i.e. an `F_2`-vector space.  Pick
`0 != phi in Phi_e`, extend `{phi}` to an `F_2`-basis, and let
`rho : Phi_e -> Z/2` be the coordinate functional with `rho(phi) = 1`.  Then
`rho` is a surjective group homomorphism split by `Z/2 ~= <phi> ↪ Phi_e`.

`G` permutes the coordinates of `bigoplus_X Phi_e` and acts trivially on the
coefficient group `Phi_e` (it is a separate direct factor of `T_e`), so the
coordinatewise map `bigoplus_X rho` is `G`-equivariant.  Extending by the
identity on `G` gives a split surjection of wreath products

```text
r_1 : Phi_e wr_X G  -->>  (Z/2) wr_X G,
```

a retraction.  Composing with the projection `T_e = G_e x (Phi_e wr_X G) ->>
Phi_e wr_X G` shows `(Z/2) wr_X G` is a retract of `T_e`, and also a retract of
the wreath `Phi_e wr_X G`.

## 2. The `Z/2`-wreath is not finitely presented

Cornulier's finite-presentation theorem for permutational wreath products
(Y. de Cornulier, *Finitely presented wreath products and double coset
decompositions*, arXiv:math/0509090, Theorem 1.1 / 2.11): for `H != 1`,
`H wr_X G = (bigoplus_X H) x| G` with `G` transitive on `X` is finitely
presented **iff** (i) `H` and `G` are finitely presented, (ii) point
stabilizers are finitely generated, and (iii) `G` acts on `X x X` with finitely
many orbits.  For transitive `X = G/Gamma`, condition (iii) is exactly
`|Gamma \ G / Gamma| < infinity`.

By [[strict-one-sided-compression-forces-infinite-bi-index]] (SCI5), an
infranormal non-normal subgroup always has `|Gamma \ G / Gamma| = infinity`,
and the Theorem E pair is infranormal and not normal
([[kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath]]).  Condition (iii) therefore fails, so
`(Z/2) wr_X G` is not finitely presented.  (This is condition-independent of
`H`: infinite double cosets alone defeat (iii) for every nontrivial lamp.)

## 3. Retract-closure

Finite presentability passes to retracts: if `r : G' ->> H` is a retraction of
a finitely presented group `G' = <S | R>` (`S`, `R` finite) onto `H <= G'`,
then `H` is finitely presented.  Indeed put `w_s = s * (r(s))^(-1) in ker r`
for `s in S` and `N = <<w_s : s in S>>`.  The composite `G' -> G'/N` and the
retraction identify `G'/N ~= H` (each `s` equals `r(s)` mod `N`, and `r`
factors through `G'/N` with the inclusion of `H` as a two-sided inverse), so
`ker r = N` is normally generated by the finite set `{w_s}` and
`H = <S | R, w_s>` is finitely presented.

If `T_e` (or `Phi_e wr_X G`) were finitely presented, Step 1 would make its
retract `(Z/2) wr_X G` finitely presented, contradicting Step 2.  Hence neither
is finitely presented.  QED

## Remark

The retract in Step 1 needs only that `Phi_e` is a nonzero `F_2`-vector space
on which the actor `G` acts trivially; it uses nothing about the machine beyond
`Phi_e != 1` on FIN.  The whole argument is therefore the general fact that a
finite-lamp permutational wreath over an infranormal non-normal Kazhdan pair,
and any group retracting onto it, is not finitely presented.
