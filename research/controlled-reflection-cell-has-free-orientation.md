title: A Pauli controlled-reflection cell has a free multiplicity orientation in the marked spin sector
---

The minimal Pauli controller cell has the intended controlled reflection as
an exact model, but the marked spin type permits an arbitrary commuting
multiplicity involution in front of it.  A character twist reverses this
orientation while fixing all Pauli and system data.

Thus spin selection and finite wreath relations do not universally orient
the acceptance corner; one additional Hecke orientation moment or an
authenticated selected type is necessary.
