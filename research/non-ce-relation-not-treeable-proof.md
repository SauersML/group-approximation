**Treeable implies Connes embeddable.**  Let `{phi_i}_(i in I)` be a
treeing of `R`, `I` countable, and let `R_i subset R` be the subrelation
generated by the single partial isomorphism `phi_i`.  Each `R_i` is
hyperfinite: its classes are the orbits of one partial map, linearly
ordered chains, so `R_i` is a subrelation of a `Z`-action relation.  The
treeing condition says precisely that `R` is the free product
`*_i R_i` of equivalence relations in Gaboriau's sense (no nontrivial
reduced cycle of partial isomorphisms returns to the diagonal;
Gaboriau, *Cout des relations d'equivalence et des groupes*, Invent.
Math. 139 (2000), Sect. IV).  For a free product of relations the von
Neumann algebra is the tracial amalgamated free product over the common
Cartan subalgebra,

```text
L(R_1 * R_2)  =  L(R_1) *_(L^infinity(X)) L(R_2),
```

because an alternating product of elements of `L(R_(i_k)) (-) L^infinity(X)`
is supported on pairs `(x, y)` joined by a reduced cycle-free path of
length `>= 1`, hence has zero conditional expectation onto the diagonal
(this is the standard identification used, e.g., in Ioana--Peterson--Popa,
*Amalgamated free products of weakly rigid factors and calculation of
their symmetry groups*, Acta Math. 200 (2008), Sect. 5, in the converse
direction).  Each `L(R_i)` is hyperfinite, and `L^infinity(X)` is
hyperfinite, so for every finite `J subset I` the algebra
`L(*_(i in J) R_i) = *_(L^infinity(X), i in J) L(R_i)` is Connes
embeddable by Brown--Dykema--Jung, arXiv:math/0609080, Corollary 4.5
(tracial amalgamated free products of Connes-embeddable algebras over a
hyperfinite subalgebra are Connes embeddable).  `L(R)` is the increasing
union of these algebras over finite `J`, and an increasing union of
Connes-embeddable algebras is Connes embeddable.  Hence `L(R)` is Connes
embeddable, contradicting the hypothesis; this is (1).

**(2).**  The Cayley graphing of an essentially free p.m.p. action of a
free group is a treeing, so its orbit relation is treeable; restrictions
to positive-measure subsets and subrelations of treeable relations are
treeable (Jackson--Kechris--Louveau, *Countable Borel equivalence
relations*, J. Math. Logic 2 (2002), Prop. 3.3).  So such relations
have Connes-embeddable von Neumann algebras, and `R` is not among them.

**(3).**  By `non-cohyperlinear-irs-exists` (Manzoor, arXiv:2502.06697,
Theorem 1.1) the GNS algebra of the IRS trace `tau_mu` is not Connes
embeddable, and it embeds trace-preservingly into `L(R_mu)` as the von
Neumann algebra generated by the unitaries `v_g`, `g in F`, of the full
group implementing the action (their traces are `nu(Fix g) = tau_mu(g)`).
Connes embeddability passes to subalgebras, so `L(R_mu)` is not Connes
embeddable and (1) applies.  The property-(T) relation of
`non-ce-property-t-equivalence-relation-exists` is non-CE by that claim;
it is also non-treeable directly, since a treeable relation has the
Haagerup property and a property-(T) relation does not.

**Consequences.**  If `L(R_mu) subset L^infinity(Y) rtimes Lambda` for an
essentially free p.m.p. action, then `L^infinity(Y) rtimes Lambda = L(R_Lambda)`
is non-CE, so `R_Lambda` is non-treeable by (1); in particular `Lambda` is
not acting as a free group does, and the action is not a sofic action
(Elek--Lippner, *Sofic equivalence relations*, JFA 258 (2010): sofic
relations -- including treeable relations, profinite actions and
Bernoulli shifts of sofic groups -- have Connes-embeddable von Neumann
algebras; item (1) above is a reproof of the treeable case via
Brown--Dykema--Jung).  An earlier version concluded "`Lambda` itself is
non-hyperlinear because `L(Lambda) subset L(R_Lambda)`"; that is
backwards (CE passes to subalgebras, not up from them) and is withdrawn
(2026-08-21 audit).
