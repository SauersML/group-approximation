Choose a purely singular probability charge on an atomic basis.  For each
actor start with an arbitrary phase-twisted permutation unitary and perturb
it by a compact operator, with no uniform rank bound, in an attempt to break
the exact Cartan/permutation decoding while retaining the same singular
state moments.

This architecture is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-compact-monomial-near-witness-impossible`.  The singular
charged diagonal state annihilates the compact ideal, and compact operators
are pointwise negligible on a charged orthonormal basis.  Removing all
compact perturbations recovers a forbidden essentially free monomial near
representation.
