title: Deep torsion fillings of the periodic-Higman torus are hyperbolic and retain a
---

For the group `M_phi` in
`periodic-higman-mapping-torus-is-word-hyperbolic`, set

```text
G_m=M_phi/<<t^m>>.
```

There is an integer `m_0` such that, for every `m>=m_0`, `G_m` is
word-hyperbolic and the image of `a` in `G_m` is nontrivial.
