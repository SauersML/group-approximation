**Established (literature).**  N. P. Brown and M. Dadarlat, *Extensions of
quasidiagonal C\*-algebras and K-theory*, arXiv:math/0008182, read from the PDF
on 2026-09-12.

1. (Proposition 2.5)  Let `0 -> I -> E -> B -> 0` be exact with Busby invariant
   `γ`.  If `I` and `B` are quasidiagonal, `B` is separable, `I` is σ-unital,
   either `I` or `B` is nuclear, and `[γ^s] = 0 ∈ Ext(K⊗B, K⊗I)`, then `E` is
   quasidiagonal.  Here `γ^s` is the Busby invariant of the stabilised sequence
   (Definition 2.4), and `E ≅ E(γ) ↪ E(γ^s)`.
2. (Definition 3.1 and the remarks after it)  An approximately unital embedding
   `I ↪ J` induces `Q(I) ↪ Q(J)`.  So a Busby invariant `γ : B -> Q(I)` induces
   `η : B -> Q(J)` with `E(γ) ↪ E(η)`.  For any embedding `I ↪ J'`, the
   hereditary subalgebra of `J'` generated by `I` receives `I` approximately
   unitally.
3. (Proposition 4.2)  Let `0 -> SI -> E -> B -> 0` be exact, where `I` is
   σ-unital and `B` is separable, quasidiagonal and nuclear.  Then `E` is
   quasidiagonal.
4. (Proposition 4.1, from Spielberg's Lemma 1.5)  If `I` and `B` are stably
   finite, `E` is stably finite iff `∂(K_1(B)) ∩ K_0^+(I) = {0}`.
5. (Proposition 4.6)  A separable quasidiagonal algebra has the QD extension
   property (Definition 4.3, UCT quotients) iff it has the K_0-embedding property
   (Definition 4.4).

`Ext(K⊗B, K⊗I)` is the semigroup of Busby invariants modulo `γ ⊕ τ ~ γ̃ ⊕ τ̃`
with `τ, τ̃` trivial (p. 5).
