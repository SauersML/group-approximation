title: "The Torelli group of Out(F_n) is finitely generated for every n"
---

For every `n >= 1`, `T_n = ker(Out(F_n) -> GL_n(Z))` is finitely generated (Magnus;
Nielsen for `n <= 3`). Since `IA_n` is an extension of `T_n` by the finitely
generated group `Inn(F_n)` (`ia-n-fp-iff-outer-torelli-fp-proof`, Step 1), `IA_n`
is finitely generated too. The import is
`outer-torelli-groups-finitely-generated-citation`.

Role in Zaremsky Problem 3.1: finite generation, the first step toward finite
presentability, holds in every rank, so the question is genuinely about relations.
