**OPEN.** The group `Gamma_U` of `positive-rokhlin-entropy-on-one-host-proves-gottschalk` admits a free ergodic p.m.p. action with positive Rokhlin entropy.

## Attempts

* **Sofic entropy.** `Gamma_U` contains nonsofic groups, and every known lower bound for Rokhlin entropy runs through sofic entropy.
* **Summands.** Positive entropy for actions of individual summands does not transfer to `Gamma_U`. Restriction to a subgroup bounds the Rokhlin entropy of the whole group only from above.
* **Scope.** The claim is weaker than Seward's hypothesis that every countably infinite group has a positive-entropy free ergodic action. It is not known to follow from Gottschalk's conjecture.
