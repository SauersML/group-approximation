Citation import, not a reproof.

* H. Kesten, *The critical probability of bond percolation on the square
  lattice equals 1/2*, Comm. Math. Phys. 74 (1980), 41–59.
* Recorded in R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and Networks*
  (Cambridge, 2016), Section 5.1, page 140, verbatim: "Generally, pc(G) is
  extremely difficult to calculate. Clearly pc(Z) = 1. After long efforts, it
  was shown that pc(Z2) = 1/2 (Kesten, 1980)." Read from the PDF on
  2026-09-19.

The monotonicity `p_c(G') <= p_c(G)` for a subgraph `G ⊆ G'` is the trivial
coupling: an infinite open cluster of `G` is contained in one of `G'`.
