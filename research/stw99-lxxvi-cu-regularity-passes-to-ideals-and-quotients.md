title: Cuntz semigroup regularity passes to ideals and quotients

Let `A` be a separable C*-algebra and suppose that its first-factor map

```text
Cu(A) -> Cu(A tensor_min Z)
```

is an isomorphism.  For every closed ideal `I` in `A`, both `I` and `A/I`
are Cuntz semigroup regular.  Explicitly, the first-factor maps

```text
Cu(I)   -> Cu(I tensor_min Z),
Cu(A/I) -> Cu((A/I) tensor_min Z)
```

are isomorphisms.

Consequently Cuntz semigroup regularity also passes to hereditary
subalgebras and is invariant under stable isomorphism.  In particular, a
counterexample to STW Problem LXXVI cannot occur as an ideal, a quotient, or
a hereditary subalgebra of a Cu-regular separable algebra.
