For every finitely generated group `G` of polynomial growth
(= virtually nilpotent), the group C\*-algebra `C*(G)` has finite
decomposition rank.  So one direction of the Eckhardt--Wu conjecture
(STW Problem LXXXVI) is a theorem, and the conjecture's open content
is exactly the converse: no finitely generated elementary amenable
group outside polynomial growth has finite decomposition rank — the
Berend-frontier examples `Z^3 ⋊ Z^2` recorded on this lane being the
decisive test family (strongly quasidiagonal, finite nuclear
dimension, decomposition rank unknown).
