On the two selected equal-rank blocks,

```text
rho(m)=chi_0(m) I=chi_1(m) I,       m in T.
```

Therefore the block exchange `S` commutes with the entire finite-dimensional
algebra generated by the tested evaluations.  Moving every occurrence of
`S` and `S^*` past those scalar coefficients reduces any mixed word to a
scalar coefficient times a power of `S`.  Replacing the labels of the two
blocks, while keeping the same matrices, changes no such word.

Nevertheless `S` sends each atom to the other one.  With normalized trace
and two equal blocks,

```text
sum_(i=0,1) ||S P_(chi_i) S^*-P_(chi_i)||_2^2=2.
```

The same construction works after any fixed matrix amplification by
exchanging the whole amplified blocks.  This proves the stated blindness
for all finite conjugation-balanced mixed-word square functions generated
by the tested module algebra.
