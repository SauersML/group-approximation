Literature import from I. J. Leary, *Uncountably many groups of type FP*,
arXiv:1512.06609v4 (Proc. London Math. Soc. 2018). For a finite connected
flag complex `L` and `S ⊆ ℤ`:

1. `G_L(S)` is the deck group of a regular branched covering
   `c : X_L^{(S)} → X_L/BB_L`. Here `X_L^{(S)}` is a CAT(0), hence
   contractible, cube complex, and `f^{(S)}` is a `G_L(S)`-invariant Morse
   function on it.
2. The action is free except that each vertex of height not in `S` has
   stabilizer isomorphic to `π_1(L)`.
3. Vertex links are `𝕊(L)` at heights in `S` and `𝕊(L̃)` at heights not in
   `S`. Ascending and descending links are `L` at heights in `S` and `L̃` at
   heights not in `S`.
4. `G_L(S)` acts cocompactly on each slab `X(m)`, the preimage under
   `f^{(S)}` of `[−m−1/2, m+1/2]`.
5. The closed ball of radius `1/4` around a vertex `v` with nontrivial
   stabilizer is `G_v`-equivariantly the cone `M(𝕊(L̃) → ∗)`. `G_v` acts
   freely on `𝕊(L̃)` with quotient `𝕊(L)`.
6. The Bestvina–Brady Morse lemma: enlarging a slab cones off copies of the
   descending links of new top vertices and the ascending links of new bottom
   vertices.
