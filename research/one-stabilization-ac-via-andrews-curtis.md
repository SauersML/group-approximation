Let S = ⟨y_1, …, y_m | s_1, …, s_m⟩ present the trivial group. If S is AC-trivial,
apply the same AC moves to the relators s_i of ⟨x, y_1, …, y_m | x, s_1, …, s_m⟩, never
touching the relator x. This reaches ⟨x, y | x, y_1, …, y_m⟩, which is trivial.
