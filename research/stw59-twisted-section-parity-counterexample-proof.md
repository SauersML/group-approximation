Theorem 1 of the artifact proves the target.

Lemmas 2 and 3 prove the finite bundle obstruction. An isomorphism
F direct-sum H ≅ C2 direct-sum H gives a unitary automorphism g of
C3 direct-sum H carrying a constant section to the sphere section.
Its mapping torus then has odd top Chern number by explicit transverse
zero count. Every such mapping torus has even top Chern number:
Kunneth writes its virtual difference as ch(delta)=z ch(beta);
line-monomial generators for K0 of the projective product and Newton's
identity produce coefficients divisible by (|b|+1)(|b|+2).
Torsion-free integral cohomology makes the divisibility integral.

Corollary 4 converts this failure of bundle cancellation to a non-null
unitary with trivial stable class. Sections 4 and 5 construct the
unital injective tower and prove simplicity by dense point evaluations,
as well as the stated structural properties. Lemma 6 identifies each
image unitary up to homotopy with the finite obstruction. Lemma 7 proves
that a limit null-homotopy would occur at a finite stage, including
explicit polar-decomposition and logarithm bounds.
