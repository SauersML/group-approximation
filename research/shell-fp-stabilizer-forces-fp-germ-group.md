For every countably infinite P and enumeration nu in the shell construction,
put p=0^infinity, Q=(E_nu)_p, and

    A_1={e in E_nu: sing(e) subset {p}, e(p)=p}.

The germ homomorphism A_1->Q is onto. Its kernel K is the nontrivial
simple group of elements of V equal to the identity near p. In particular,
any nonidentity member of K normally generates K in A_1. Consequently

    A_1 finitely presented  ==>  Q finitely presented.

This is a necessary condition for the universal shell-stabilizer route.
The reverse implication is not asserted. K is not finitely generated,
so finite presentation of the germ quotient alone does not discharge the
presentation problem for A_1.
