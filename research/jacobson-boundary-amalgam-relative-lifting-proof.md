The literal model verifies nontriviality of the head mark and all
specified relations. Compatible copies of the two regular finite-factor
actions provide at least one admissible pair of matrix representations.

A sequence of such pairs with vanishing mixed errors gives a norm
corona homomorphism. Its marked involution remains at distance two.
Conversely lift a marked corona homomorphism on both finite factors,
correct the finite multiplication tables in the original dimensions,
and average the intertwiner between their restrictions to the common
finite subgroup. Its polar unitary aligns the entire second factor
with the unchanged first factor. All corrections tend to zero and all
words use one common corrected matrix for `h`. Select the infinitely
many coordinates where the marked exact involution is nonidentity.
This proves the infimum is zero, establishing the claimed equivalence.
