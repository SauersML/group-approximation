**ESTABLISHED SCOPED NO-GO.**  Use the archived affine frame of
`atlas-affine-acute-angle-forces-two-point-k-orbit`, and take the singular
threshold below the least nonzero singular value of the Reynolds residual.
Write

```text
P=supp|U-E_K(U)|.
```

The five boundary residuals vanish.  The collision residual is

```text
R_c=lambda(g)-1=-2Q,
```

where `g` is a nonidentity involution and `Q` is its negative spectral
projection.  Hence

```text
|R_c|=2Q,        V_c=-Q,        Q'_c=V_c Q V_c^*=Q.    (APC1)
```

In particular, the nonunital supported star-algebra generated by the
collision high projection and its polar partial isometry is just `C Q`.
Every finite family of maps obtained by duplicating, relabelling, or
postprocessing pure collision-polar slots therefore factors through the
single map

```text
QP:PH -> QH.                                           (APC2)
```

Its common kernel contains `Ran(P) intersect Ker(Q)`.

The translation module of the affine frame is elementary abelian.  If the
raw `K~=S_3` orbit has size `n=3`, its two distinct nonzero orbit differences
are linearly independent, so the subgroup `L` generated by all differences
has order at least `4`.  If `n=6`, its five distinct nonzero differences do
not fit in a two-dimensional `F_2`-space, so `|L|>=8`.  The exact Reynolds
rank computation gives

```text
rank(P)/d = 1-1/|L| >= 3/4  (n=3),
rank(P)/d = 1-1/|L| >= 7/8  (n=6),                    (APC3)
```

whereas `rank(Q)=d/2`.  Therefore

```text
dim(Ran(P) intersect Ker(Q))
 >= rank(P)-d/2
 >= d/4  (n=3),
 >= 3d/8 (n=6).                                      (APC4)
```

Thus no finite number of copies of the initial/final collision polar slots,
and no bounded postprocessing of those copies, is jointly injective on the
source support.

More generally, for maps `Phi_(r,j):PH->Q_rH`, their common kernel is zero
exactly when the direct-sum map into `direct_sum_(r,j) Q_rH` is injective.
Consequently a bounded-congestion assertion with at most `M` copies per
residual gives only the tautological rank condition

```text
rank(P) <= M sum_r rank(Q_r).                          (APC5)
```

Without specified transports this is just the desired singular-counting
inequality in another form.  A surviving bounded-multiplicity mechanism must
introduce genuinely different **external pretransports**
`Q_r W_(r,j) P`.  Maps of the form `B_(r,j) Q_r P` remain trapped behind the
kernel in `(APC4)` and cannot close `(SCI3)`.
