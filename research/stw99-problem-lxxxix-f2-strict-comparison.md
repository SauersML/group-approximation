**Problem LXXXIX of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**
Does `C*_r(F_2)` have strict comparison?

**ESTABLISHED** (STW Addendum 25.1): AGKP prove `C*_r(F_n)` (`n ≥ 2`)
is selfless, hence has strict comparison with respect to its unique
trace, which is its unique quasitrace; route
`stw99-lxxxix-via-agkp`.  Downstream uses already in the literature:
Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Schafhauser distinguish the ultrapowers of
`C*_r(F_n)` for different `n` (`K_1` of the ultrapower = ultrapower of
`K_1`, via abstract classification unlocked by comparison).
