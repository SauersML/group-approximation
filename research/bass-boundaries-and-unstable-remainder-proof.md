Set `B=k[x_3,...,x_d]`.  Homotopy invariance gives

```text
K2(B)=0,       K1(B)=k^*,       K0(B)=Z.                    (BUR1)
```

Apply the fundamental theorem successively to `x_1` and `x_2`.  The two
tame boundary maps and their composite split the result as

```text
K2(B[x_1^(+-1),x_2^(+-1)])
 ~= K2(B) (+) K1(B)_(x_1) (+) K1(B)_(x_2) (+) K0(B)_(x_1x_2).  (BUR2)
```

Up to harmless inversions caused by the choice of boundary convention,

```text
c(a,x_1) maps to a in the first K1 summand,
c(a,x_2) maps to a in the second K1 summand,
c(x_1,x_2) maps to 1 in the K0 summand.                     (BUR3)
```

Since `a` generates `k^*=C_(q-1)`, equations (BUR1)--(BUR3) prove (MSP3).
The stable Steinberg kernel is central.  Hence normal generation of that
kernel is ordinary abelian generation, and quotienting by the three words
gives (MSP4).

This packet is minimal when `m=q-1>1`.  Choose a prime `ell` dividing `m`.
Then

```text
(Z (+) C_m (+) C_m)/ell(Z (+) C_m (+) C_m) ~= F_ell^3.      (BUR4)
```

Thus at least three generators, and hence at least three central normal
relators, are necessary.  When `m=1`, (MSP3) is just `Z`, generated by
`c_0`.

For a noetherian ring of dimension at most `d`, classical injective
stability gives

```text
K2(r,R) -> K2(R) is an isomorphism for r>=d+3.               (BUR5)
```

The words in (MSP1) already use only three matrix indices.  Therefore they
exist in `St_r(R)`, and (BUR5) turns (MSP3) into a generating set for the
whole finite-rank kernel.  This proves (MSP5).  It also makes that kernel
central: a commutator with it maps trivially after stabilization and
injectivity in (BUR5) forces the commutator to be trivial.

For arbitrary `r>=3`, the same three words lie in `U_r`, and their images
generate stable `K2(R)`.  The kernel calculation in (MSP6) is immediate.
Moreover, if `u in U_r`, choose `c in C_r` with the same stable image.
Then `u c^(-1)` lies in `ker(U_r->K2(R))` and represents the same coset in
`U_r/C_r`.  Hence

```text
ker(U_r->K2(R)) ->> U_r/C_r.                                (BUR6)
```

This proves the exact unstable qualification.

Finally let `phi=barPsi:Q_2->St(R)`, and let `N` be the normal closure of
chosen lifts of the three symbols.  Since `phi(N)=K2(R)`, there is a
surjection

```text
Q_2/N ->> St(R)/K2(R)=E_inf(R).                             (BUR7)
```

If `y` lies in the kernel of (BUR7), choose `n in N` with
`phi(n)=phi(y)`.  Then `y n^(-1) in ker(phi)` and it represents the same
element modulo `N`.  Thus `ker(phi)` surjects onto the kernel of (BUR7).
This is exactly the cone-presentation remainder asserted in the claim and
explains why killing the computed `K2` packet does not establish
injectivity of (BUR7).
