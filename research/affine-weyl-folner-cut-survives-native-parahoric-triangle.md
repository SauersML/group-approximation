# The six shifts generate an amenable affine-Weyl action

Let `L=P^vee` be the `A_2` coweight lattice and let `W=S_3`.  For

```text
lambda=(1,0,-1),
V=W lambda,                                             (AWF1)
```

the subgroup generated by the six translations in `V` is the coroot lattice
`Q^vee`, of finite index in `P^vee`.  Consequently the six denominator
translations and the two simple Weyl reflections generate

```text
Gamma_ap=Q^vee semidirect W,                            (AWF2)
```

the affine Weyl group of type `A_2` (and a finite-index subgroup of the
extended affine Weyl group `P^vee semidirect W`).  In particular it is
virtually `Z^2` and amenable.  Adding all forty-two labels over each of the
six directions, with arbitrary finite-fiber permutations, does not change
this conclusion; this is the skew-product situation of
`six-parahoric-finite-fiber-folds-remain-amenable`.

There is an explicit joint finite-model escape at every prescribed density
`0<alpha<1`.  On the finite torus

```text
T_M=Q^vee/MQ^vee,                                      (AWF3)
```

let the translations and `W` act by their exact permutations.  Choose a
`W`-invariant hexagonal ball `B_M` in the centered torus, with radius chosen
so that

```text
|B_M|/|T_M| -> alpha.                                  (AWF4)
```

Such radii exist because the normalized lattice-point count of centered
hexagons converges uniformly to the continuous area profile; changing the
radius by one changes the count by `O(M)`.  If `E_M` is multiplication by
`1_(B_M)`, then

```text
tau(E_M)->alpha,
w E_M w^*=E_M                         (w in W),
||t_v E_M t_v^*-E_M||_2^2=O(1/M)      (v in V).         (AWF5)
```

The last equality is the normalized cardinality of the symmetric
difference, which is contained in a bounded-width neighborhood of the
hexagon boundary.  Thus all six shifts and both Weyl generators admit one
joint almost-invariant cutoff.  In particular `(AWF5)` applies to
`alpha=1/42`; the six directions do not upgrade the one-denominator Rohlin
escape to a spectral gap.

# Adding the native triangle still does not authenticate the cutoff

The first native relation outside the affine apartment action is the signed
parahoric corrector triangle

```text
Z_D=Z_(aD) Z_(bD),                                     (AWF6)
```

together with its `C`-translates and the `D`-invariance relations.  On an
authenticated common carrier, this relation is extremely strong:
`actual-parahoric-triangle-kills-abelian-correctors` kills every abelian
gauge, and `native-parahoric-triangle-kills-all-corrector-gauges` kills even
a nonabelian one.

It nevertheless has no coercivity on a conductor cutoff which has not been
identified as the support of those occurrences.  Indeed, tensor the exact
permutation model on `T_M` with any finite label space and put

```text
Z_x=I                  for every forty-two-point chart label x.            (AWF7)
```

All covariance, `D`-invariance, translated-triangle, inverse, and local
holonomy relations for the `Z_x` hold exactly.  Put the projection
`E_M tensor I` in the same ambient abelian algebra.  It has trace tending to
`1/42`, its joint affine-Weyl covariance energy tends to zero by `(AWF5)`,
and every native-triangle energy is zero.  Hence no dimension-independent
inequality of the form

```text
c tau(E)
 <= sum_(v in V)||t_vEt_v^*-E||_2^2
    + WeylEnergy(E) + triangleEnergy(Z)                 (AWF8)
```

can hold with `c>0` for this relation class.

This is not an arithmetic-group microstate: it deliberately omits the raw
denominator-section incidence which must identify a decoded occurrence with
the cutoff's source and range.  It is a sharp endpoint fence.  The native
triangle destroys all gauges **after** common-carrier authentication, but it
does not perform that authentication.  The smallest remaining datum must be
a mixed support relation making `E(Z-I)` (or the source/range of the two
triangle factors) the literal raw branch compression.  Once such supported
factors exist, `partial-isometry-translate-cancel-charges-first-exit` gives
the required dimension-free payment; without them, `(AWF3)--(AWF7)` is a
joint Folner escape.
