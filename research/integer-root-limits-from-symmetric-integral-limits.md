A symmetric integer matrix of norm at most `C` is an integer matrix with real
spectrum in `[-C, C]`. So approximants for
`integral-group-ring-spectra-are-integral-limits` are approximants for
`integral-group-ring-spectra-are-integer-root-limits`. The converse needs totally
real Galois orbits realized by symmetric integer matrices with negligible extra
spectrum. That is not settled here.
