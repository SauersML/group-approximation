For the critical quotient of a fixed nonamenable d-regular Cayley graph,
degree bias its unimodular Palm root law to nu. The environment operator
K that reroots across a uniformly chosen quotient boundary edge has
Dirichlet gap at least 1-r, where r is the original nonlazy regular
walk norm. Equivalently (I+K)/2 has mean-zero spectrum in [0,(1+r)/2].
Sections 1-3 prove the energy identity by mass transport and compare
variances using B/m<=d. This is an annealed environment operator claim,
not a statement about the quenched walk on one realization.
