**ESTABLISHED** through `ascending-hnn-quotients-affiliated-h1-proof`, from Lück's mapping-torus
theorem (`lueck-mapping-tori-and-homology-invariance`, item 3, in degree 2) and the regularity of
`U(G)`.

`U(G)` is the algebra of operators affiliated to the group von Neumann algebra of `G`, and
`H_1(G; U(G)) = Tor_1^{Z[G]}(U(G), Z)`.

1. **Main statement (any group).** Let `G` be a group, `K ≤ G` a finitely generated subgroup, and
   `t ∈ G` such that
   - `G` is generated by `K` and `t`,
   - `t K t⁻¹ ⊆ K`, and
   - some homomorphism `χ : G → Z` has `χ(K) = 0` and `χ(t) = 1`.

   Then `H_1(G; U(G)) = 0`. Every ascending HNN extension `K *_φ` with finitely generated base,
   and every quotient of one to which the canonical character `K ↦ 0`, `t ↦ 1` descends, is of
   this form. No isomorphism `G ≅ K *_φ` is needed. In particular this holds for `N ⋊ Z` with `N`
   finitely generated.
2. **Σ¹ form.** If `G` is finitely generated and its BNS invariant `Σ¹(G)` is non-empty, then
   `H_1(G; U(G)) = 0`.

**Why it matters.** For non-`FP_2` groups, `H_1(G; U(G)) = 0` is strictly stronger than
`b_1^(2)(G) = 0`. For a finitely generated amenable group of cd 2 it is exactly type `FP_2`
(`amenable-fp-iff-affiliated-top-homology-vanishes`, item 4). The w3-112 attempt on
`amenable-groups-of-finite-cd-are-virtually-solvable` identified `H_1(K *_φ; U)` with the
Mayer--Vietoris cokernel `coker(1 − tφ_*)` and recorded that "nothing makes this cokernel vanish".
Item 1 shows that it always vanishes when the base is finitely generated.

**Calibration.**
- `G = K × Z` with `K` finitely generated: `t − 1` is a non-zero-divisor of `N(G)`, hence a unit of
  `U(G)`, so `H_*(G; U(G)) = 0` by a direct cone argument. This agrees with item 1 for arbitrary
  `K`, including `K = Z ≀ Z`.
- `BS(1,m) = ⟨a, t | t a t⁻¹ = a^m⟩` with `K = ⟨a⟩`: item 1 gives `H_1 = 0`, as it must for a
  group of type `F`, which is `L²`-acyclic because it is amenable and infinite.
