**ESTABLISHED AUDIT.**  For

```text
R=L_(F_2)(1,2),       Delta=St_20(R),       q=s_1t_1,
z=x_13(q),
```

the following components of the proposed Property-`(T)`-free proof are
unconditional and already established in the Cairn graph.

1. `q!=0`, the marked root `z` is a nontrivial involution, and `z` normally
   generates `Delta` by the two displayed ordinary Steinberg commutators.
2. If a norm-corona representation keeps `z` nontrivial, finite-order
   rounding and normal-generation reblocking give an equivalent active model
   in which the negative spectral support of `z` has a fixed positive density.
3. The commuting double-commutator word gives a named common marked carrier
   `F` with at least one quarter of that active mass.
4. Three same-center Leavitt coefficient factorizations recover on `F` a
   three-qubit Pauli factor `M_8(C)` with one multiplicity reservoir.
5. The two literal adjacent-prefix Whitehead words act as
   `SWAP_(1,2) tensor V_1` and `SWAP_(2,3) tensor V_2`; their Coxeter braid
   makes the reservoir involutions `V_1,V_2` unitarily conjugate.
6. Removing the fixed swaps inside the recovered matrix factor exposes both
   gauges as finite-coordinate analytic operators.  Fixed label
   multiplication therefore constructs analytic candidates

   ```text
   C_(1/4) tensor V_1,        C_(1/8) tensor V_2.
   ```

7. The independent affine rank theorem says that **if** the actual native
   rows with source fractions `1/2,1/4` are authenticated as those candidates
   on one nonzero reducing carrier, their common gauge fraction is forced to
   be both `1/2` and `5/6`.  Quantitatively their defect sum is at least `1/3`,
   their squared defect sum is at least `1/13`, and one defect is at least
   `1/5`.
8. Operator-norm exactification of the one fixed packet is dimension-free.
   None of the preceding statements invokes Property `(T)`, a Kazhdan
   projection, spectral-gap transport, an asymptotic-commutant theorem, or a
   literature input.

The proposal is **not** an unconditional MF-collapse proof because its
selector groupification paragraph asserts one additional occurrence identity:
that the actual depth-one and depth-two selector rows inherit precisely the
Whitehead gauges `V_1,V_2`.  The established Whitehead words have label
fractions `1/4,1/4`, not `1/4,1/8`.  Multiplying by an analytic rank-one
three-qubit reflection creates the desired second candidate but does not make
that reflection, or the corresponding covariance row, an ordinary group-word
occurrence.

The full self-Gram of two unitaries reducing `F` is tautological and does not
authenticate the missing row.  The support-return menu also does not repair
the type: its literal normalizers are quarter swaps, its common chart
intersection retains only two Pauli pairs, and the named Hecke reflections
are analytic group-algebra elements rather than typed source reflections.
The strengthened literal audit reaches the same boundary: both native
Whitehead factors, their two-sided prefix refinement, the adjacent braid, and
both order-seven Hecke heads have one exact marked `GL_7(F_2)` model.  The two
actors are equal-type adjacent swaps there.  Thus neither literal factor
typing nor seventh-power anchoring supplies the missing half/quarter source
returns or the quarter/eighth controlled-target occurrence.
For the operator-MF quantifier, the missing input is sharper than the
canonical Hecke rank calculation: row one's conjugacy word is already
literal, but its half-source fraction is known only on the canonical
center-chain character type.  Arbitrary norm-corona active profiles need not
contain that type.  A free marked `S_3` type has operator-norm leakage one on
every nonzero adaptive subcut, and an irreducible recentered row-two type has
quarter-source conjugacy defect two.  Hence positive active density does not
regularize the packet profile or validate the asserted source ranks.
The first external and mixed repairs are now fenced as well.  Every word in
the authenticated local closure fixes `z`.  A constant Weyl dressing can
move it to a disjoint root and make the Gram full, but it moves the whole
Pauli chart and retains a scalar `-1` gauge even after both charts are placed
in one six-pair factor.  Independently, the literal marked action
`qa_2=a_2`, its constant-Whitehead return, and two factor-by-factor `A_2`
paths all admit one exact marked `GL_8(F_2)` model.  A dual-Heisenberg
spectator can also make the dressed outputs share a trivial gauge while the
two raw affine gauges retain fractions `1/2` and `5/6`.  Consequently neither
root-valued dressing, finite-factor enlargement, nor spectator Gram payment
authenticates the two raw selector rows.
One full-presentation coefficient payment is nevertheless now available.
The discrepancy root `x_78(t_00+s_0t_00)` has an explicit four-conjugate
normal return to `z`; its negative projection occupies a fixed positive part
of the marked carrier and the two coefficient arms differ there by norm two.
This rules out the earlier exact `GL_8` collision.  It still does not type a
selector source: a marked `GL_10(F_2)` packet keeps the paid discrepancy on
transverse root-position vertices, and a second `GL_10` packet realizes the
fine-idempotent root and both Morita absorption rows with half, rather than
quarter, negative spectral rank.  The remaining seam is therefore a
spectral incidence between the paid carrier and the signed Hecke source, or
a fixed ordinary word which charges escape from that source.
Thus the statement called `finite covariance groupification and the reservoir
gauge` in the proposal is valid as a conditional representation-theoretic
lemma, but the assertion that the native Leavitt words satisfy its covariance
hypothesis is exactly the open
`same-center-whitehead-native-selector-subcorner-attachment` claim.

Once that occurrence claim is supplied, the affine theorem, active density,
and normal generation do give full MF radical.  Until then, the unconditional
result is the safe prefix above, not
`Rad_MF(Delta)=Delta`.  The retained manuscript consequences are likewise
already algebraic consequences of full collapse, but they cannot be asserted
unconditionally before this one analytic input is proved.
