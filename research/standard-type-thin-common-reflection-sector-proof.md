In the permutation representation of `S_n`, the transposition `(1 2)` has
negative eigenspace `C(e_1-e_2)` and fixes the hyperplane `z_1=z_2`.  The
negative line lies in the sum-zero standard module.  Intersecting the fixed
hyperplane with the sum-zero module gives dimension `n-2`, so these are the
complete `-1` and `+1` eigenspaces on the `(n-1)`-dimensional irreducible
standard module.  Fourier compression by its primitive central idempotent
turns `(1-s)/2` and `(1+s)/2` into the two spectral projections.  Normalized
corner trace is rank divided by `n-1`, giving the asserted values.
