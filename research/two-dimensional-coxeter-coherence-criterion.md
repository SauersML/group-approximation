Let `G` be a two-dimensional Coxeter group. Then `G` is coherent if and
only if

`chi(H) <= 0`

for every infinite Coxeter subgroup `H` generated by a subset of the defining
Coxeter generators.

If `G` is infinite, these conditions are also equivalent to `G` being
virtually free-by-cyclic. When they hold,

`b_1^(2)(G) = -chi(G)`

and `b_n^(2)(G)=0` for every `n != 1`.
