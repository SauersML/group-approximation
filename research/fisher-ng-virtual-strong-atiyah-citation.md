Source: Sam P. Fisher and Andrew Ng, *Outer automorphism groups and the
Atiyah Conjecture*, arXiv:2606.19606v1 (2026), Theorem 1.3.

The theorem states exactly that `Out(A_Gamma)` and
`Mod(Sigma_(g,b,p))` have torsion-free finite-index subgroups satisfying
the Strong Atiyah Conjecture over `C`, and concludes the Weak Atiyah
Conjecture for the ambient groups.

For the RAAG on `n` vertices, the source may take the kernel of reduction

`Out(A_Gamma) -> GL_n(F_p)`

for an odd prime `p`. Finite-index von Neumann rank scaling then places the
ambient ranks in a discrete subgroup of `Q`; this is the Weak Atiyah
conclusion, not an assertion that Strong Atiyah passes to finite-index
overgroups.
