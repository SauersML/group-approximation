Within each exponent-seven class-two vertex, BCH gives
log(u^i v^(-i^2/2) w^k)=iU-i^2 V/2+(k-i^3/4)[U,V].
Thus the established cubic coefficient sigma*i^3/12 is obtained
by k=(sigma+3)*i^3/12. Reducing these vertex exponents modulo
seven gives coefficients 3 for V, and 6 or 5 for W at the two
nonabelian vertices. The first vertex has W=1.

The exact marked face equations are C_i=z^-1. Eliminating z by
C_1^-1 gives precisely the stated five equalities and six
centrality relations. The thirteen vertex relations present the
known C7^2 and two Heisenberg vertex kernels exactly. No global
nilpotency, exponent-seven, or BCH hypothesis is imposed on the
resulting group or its targets.
