**ESTABLISHED** (literature import, `gap-theorems-residually-p-and-nilpotent-citation`).

Let `G` be a finitely generated group that is residually nilpotent (for example residually finite-`p`).
If `γ_G(n) ≺ e^{√n}`, then `G` has polynomial growth.
