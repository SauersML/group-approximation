**Established by citation.**  Slofstra, arXiv:1806.05267, Lemma 3.1, proves
`(RCB1)` by applying Gowers--Hatami stability to obtain an exact
representation in dimension `m` with `d<=m<=2d`.  Retention of `J` makes the
`-1` eigenspace of the exactified central involution nonzero, and that
eigenspace is a representation of the rank-`n` Clifford algebra, whose
irreducible dimensions are `2^floor(n/2)`.

Slofstra's Lemma 3.4 separately shows that a presentation-level
`epsilon`-representation of `C(n)` gives only an `(n+1)^2 epsilon`-homomorphism
on the whole finite group.  This is the stated normalization fence.
