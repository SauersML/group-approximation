Let `A` be a simple separable unital nuclear non-elementary C-star algebra
with a unique tracial state, stable rank one, and real rank zero.  Then

```text
A is Z-stable
    if and only if
K_0(A) is weakly unperforated.                                  (RR0-K)
```

In particular, Problem XXV has a positive answer for the subclass with real
rank zero and weakly unperforated ordered `K_0`.  Conversely, every real-rank-
zero counterexample to Problem XXV must have perforated `K_0`; within real
rank zero this is the exact remaining obstruction, rather than merely a
necessary condition obtained from examples.
