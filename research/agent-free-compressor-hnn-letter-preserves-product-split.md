**ESTABLISHED HNN FIREWALL.**  Let `F` be a finite-rank nonabelian free
group, let `alpha:F->F` be injective, and put

```text
T_alpha=<F,t | t f t^(-1)=alpha(f), f in F>.           (HPS1)
```

Fix `1!=r in F` and an odd prime `p`.  There are exact finite-dimensional
unitary representations of the rank-five Steinberg wordization in which all
of the following hold with zero defect:

```text
P^2=P,                     [P,F]=[P,t]=0,
(r-1)P=0,
t f t^(-1)=alpha(f),       f in a fixed basis of F,    (HPS2)
```

but the payload root `Z=x_13(P)` has spectral density `1-1/p`, while the
diagonal word representing `r` has arbitrarily small near-identity spectral
density.  Consequently every projection `e_n` with

```text
||(D_(r,n)-1)e_n||_2->0
```

has `tr(e_n)->0`, even though the full HNN letter preserves the nontrivial
spectral carrier of `Z` and implements every self-copy conjugacy relation
exactly.

Thus the obvious nonlocal strengthening of the one-row compiler,

```text
conditional kernel rows + [P,t]=0 + the actual HNN relations,
```

still admits the same product-factor escape.  The stable letter acts on the
independent mapping-torus factor and is invisible to the payload root factor.
A surviving coupling must force the `P`-corner to carry a prescribed
nonstationary parent/child packet type; mentioning the actual HNN letter, or
making it commute with `P`, is not by itself non-retractive.
