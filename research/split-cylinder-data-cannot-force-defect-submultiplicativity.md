**OPEN** (proved on paper, artifact Section 2; held OPEN until `w4-vf-gate` re-derives it).

**Statement.** Let `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, let `G <= R^x` contain `D_8 = <x_12(1), x_23(1)>`, and let `A_1, ..., A_k` be
pairwise incomparable words. Put `tau_AB = S[A]T[B] + S[B]T[A] + 1 - e_A - e_B`.
1. `tau_AB` is an involution in `V` with `tau_AB iota_A(g) tau_AB = iota_B(g)`. It changes depth when `|A| != |B|`.
2. The group `Gamma_k(G)` generated by the `iota_(A_i)(G)` and the `tau_(A_i A_j)` is isomorphic to `G wr S_k`.
3. Let `rho` be a characteristic-two matricial rank model of `G`, with defect `delta_rho` and fixed rank `f_rho`. Then
   `Gamma_k(G)` has a rank model `sigma` that is `rho` in slot `i` on `iota_(A_i)(G)` and flips slots on the swaps.
   It has `rk(D_(A_(i_1)) ... D_(A_(i_m))) = delta_rho^m` for distinct indices, and fixed rank at most `f_rho`.

**Consequence.** Suppose a relation-only argument derives `theta < 1` from relations holding in `Gamma_2(G)`. Then
every fixed-point-free model of `G` has `delta_rho = 0`.
* For sofic `G` this is false, by `sofic-subgroups-carry-independent-cylinder-defects`.
* For `G = R^x` it is the gate, through `leavitt-rank-model-defect-gap-on-fixed-point-free-quotients`.

So swap letters between cylinder copies, at any depths, give no route shorter than the gate.

**Scope.** Not covered are letters that do not carry each cylinder onto a cylinder by one prefix replacement, such as
`x_0` or the right-child product `b`. Over `F_3` the tensor model sends `iota_A(-1) iota_B(-1)` to `+1`, so it is
anti-central only when `[A] cup [B]` is proper.
