**Established by citation.**  For `Z`-stable `A`, the first-factor
embedding `A → A ⊗ Z` is approximately unitarily equivalent to an
isomorphism (`Z`-stability plus an intertwining, Rordam/Toms--Winter
standard), and approximately unitarily equivalent *-homomorphisms
induce the same map on `Cu`; hence `Cu(A) → Cu(A ⊗ Z)` is an
isomorphism.  Recorded by STW around Proposition `prop:cu-regular`
((1) ⟸ `Z`-stability) and in Rordam's IJM paper.
