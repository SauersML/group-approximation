Citation import, secondary, and the only source is a survey report of an unpublished
thesis. A referee should weigh it accordingly.

**Source.** S. Rees, *The development of the theory of automatic groups*,
arXiv:2205.14911v1 (30 May 2022), p. 14. PDF text extracted with pypdf and read on
2026-09-16. Verbatim, directly after the nilpotent case: "It was conjectured by Thurston
that the same result must hold for an automatic soluble group. That conjecture remains
open, but it was proved for automatic polycylic groups in [39], using an embedding of a
finite index subgroup of a polycyclic group of exponential growth as a lattice in an
appropriate Lie group, where [26, Theorem 10.3.5] about higher dimensional isoperimetric
functions could be applied". The "same result" is "an automatic nilpotent group must be
virtually abelian". The bibliography entry: "[39] A. Harkins, Combing lattices of soluble
Lie groups, PhD thesis, University of Newcastle, 2001." Here [26] is ECHLPT.

**Reading.** The quoted sentence says that Thurston's conjecture, "automatic soluble ⇒
virtually abelian", was proved for polycyclic groups. This is the statement of the claim.
The mechanism Rees describes covers polycyclic groups of exponential growth. The
polynomial-growth polycyclic groups are virtually nilpotent and are covered by the
nilpotent case (`automatic-nilpotent-groups-are-virtually-abelian`, with
`finite-index-subgroups-of-automatic-groups-are-automatic`).
