Immediate. If every group of type `FP_n` acts freely and cocompactly on an
`(n−1)`-acyclic complex, then it acts properly and cocompactly on one. Since
`FH_n ⇒ FP_n` always holds, the two types are equivalent for every `n`. This
is one of exactly two admissible routes into the root; the other is
`zaremsky-1-04-by-no`.
