The six ray transfers generate the zero-sum translation lattice.
Conjugating head transpositions by them gives every adjacent edge
transposition along the rays, so together with head links they generate
the entire finitary symmetric kernel. The quotient is virtually
abelian, proving amenability and finite generation of the base `H`.

Uniform translation on the bilateral rays conjugates `H` into itself
and fixes the old head in its image. The increasing union of the
backward conjugates of `H` is amenable and normal in `Gamma`; its
quotient is infinite cyclic, detected by the sum of tail translations.
Thus `Gamma` is countable amenable and MF by the prerequisite.

In an exact finite-dimensional representation, the algebra spanned by
the image of `H` is carried into itself by stable-letter conjugation.
Finite dimension makes this inclusion equality. The head lies in that
algebra and centralizes it, so its perfect group maps into an abelian
center and dies. The faithful literal head action and the MF embedding
retain the marked involution. The artifact proves the finite splitting
and records the precise primary amenable-to-MF citation.
