For each `x`, map `B_x` identically to `B_(f(x))`.  These maps agree on the
common subgroup `D`, so the universal property of the generalized amalgam
gives `Phi_f`.  The identities

```text
Phi_id=id,       Phi_(f after g)=Phi_f after Phi_g
```

prove that `(UEM1)` is a monoid homomorphism.  If `f!=g`, choose `x` with
`f(x)!=g(x)` and `b in B\D`.  The elements `Phi_f(b_x)` and `Phi_g(b_x)` lie
in distinct distinguished factors and are unequal by amalgam normal form.
Thus `(UEM1)` is injective.

If `f` is injective, relabeling identifies `K_X` with the subamalgam on
`f(X)`, and normal form embeds that subamalgam in `K_X`; hence `Phi_f` is
injective.  If `f` is not injective, choose distinct `x,y` with
`f(x)=f(y)` and `b in B\D`.  The reduced word `b_x b_y^(-1)` is nontrivial
but lies in the kernel of `Phi_f`.

If `f` is surjective, the image of `Phi_f` contains every distinguished
factor and is therefore all of `K_X`.  If `f` is not surjective, its image
lies in the proper subamalgam supported on `f(X)`; an element of
`B_y\D` for `y notin f(X)` is outside that image by normal form.  This proves
`(UEM2)`.

Every countable monoid embeds in the full transformation monoid of a
countable set by its left regular action: evaluation at the identity makes
that action faithful.  Transport the action to a subset of `X` and fix the
complement.  Finally, an injective non-surjective shift of `X` gives a proper
injective endomorphism of `K_X`, while any surjective non-injective self-map
of `X` gives a proper surjective endomorphism.  Hence `K_X` is neither
co-Hopfian nor Hopfian.

For `(UEM3)`, expand each copy of `K_X` as the generalized amalgam of copies
of `B` indexed by `X`.  Associativity of the amalgamated coproduct flattens
the left side to copies of `B` indexed by `I x X`.  Since `I` is nonempty
countable and `X` is countably infinite, a bijection `I x X ~= X` relabels
this generalized amalgam as `K_X`.

For `(UEM4)`, quotienting by `N_E` identifies exactly the copies of `B`
whose indices lie in the same `E`-class.  The universal property leaves one
copy for each class, with all copies of `D` still identified, which is
`K_(X/E)`.  Factoring a self-map through its set-theoretic fiber quotient,
a bijection onto its image, and the inclusion of its image gives `(UEM5)`.

If `E<=F`, then `N_E<=N_F`.  Conversely, if `x E y` but `x` and `y` are in
different `F`-classes, choose `b in B\D`; the word `b_x b_y^(-1)` lies in
`N_E` but remains a nontrivial reduced word in `K_(X/F)`.  Thus the
assignment reflects order.  Taking the normal closure of combined
equal-factor relations corresponds to the equivalence relation generated by
their union, so arbitrary joins are preserved.

There are continuum many partitions of `N` into countably many nonempty
blocks.  Identify `N` with `N x {0,1,2}`; a binary sequence independently
merges or separates `(n,0)` and `(n,1)`, while every `(n,2)` remains a
singleton and keeps the quotient infinite.  Distinct sequences give distinct
kernels.  By `(UEM4)` and `(UEM3)`, every quotient is isomorphic to `K_X`.
Finally, the quotient map of countable sets has a section; functoriality
supplies a right inverse to the corresponding group quotient, so every
self-quotient splits.

The kernel `N_E` acts freely on the Bass--Serre tree of `K_X`.  Indeed, a
vertex stabilizer is a conjugate of some `B_x`, and `Phi_q` is injective on
every such conjugate.  Hence `N_E` intersects every vertex stabilizer
trivially, and therefore also every edge stabilizer.  A group acting freely
on a tree is free.

The continuum family may be chosen with infinite free rank.  Identify `X`
with `N x {0,1,2,3}`.  Always merge `(n,0)` with `(n,1)`, and independently
merge or separate `(n,2)` and `(n,3)` according to a binary sequence.  Let

```text
P=B_0 *_D B_1 -> B
```

be the two-factor fold.  Its kernel `F` is a nontrivial free group: it acts
freely on the Bass--Serre tree, and `b_0 b_1^(-1)` is a nonidentity kernel
element for `b in B\D`.  Choose `u in F` with nonzero image under some
homomorphism `lambda:F->Z`.

Place a copy `u_n` of `u` on the permanently merged pair
`{(n,0),(n,1)}`.  There is a retraction from `K_X` to that pair amalgam which
is the identity on its two factors and sends every other factor identically
to the first one.  On `N_E`, its image lies in `F`; it sends `u_n` to `u`
and every `u_m`, `m!=n`, to the identity.  Composing with `lambda` gives
homomorphisms `N_E->Z` which prove that the classes of the `u_n` are linearly
independent in the abelianization of `N_E`.  Hence the free rank of `N_E` is
infinite.  Since `N_E` is countable, `N_E~=F_infinity`.  Combining this with
the split quotient gives `(UEM6)`.  Free groups are MF, so the final radical
comparison follows.
