Settle the open branch with an invariant — index, entropy, or undiscovered —
that is conjugation-invariant and strictly monotone on the compression.
Dead: the Bernoulli crossed product realizes `uNu* ⊊ N` with `u`
implementing a properly outer automorphism, so no such invariant exists
(trace-blindness-sharpness). The obstruction, if there is one, can only be stability.
