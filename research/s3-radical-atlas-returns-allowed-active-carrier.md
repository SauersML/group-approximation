Attach the one-rank-jump coordinate of a Schur--Clifford predicate packet to
the `K` coordinate of the six-generator atlas.  Use its three `S_3` charts to
transport the endpoint words in `(SRA6)` through the `00,01,11` rank-one
contexts.  Prove that:

1. every `K=+1,J=-1` radical character, including all three nonzero
   characters, has an exact zero-energy return channel;
2. on the analytically compressed forbidden `K=-1` child, the contextual
   failure complement is the active half up to fixed relator energy; and
3. the returned active projection approximately reduces the original shared
   BCS words with dimension-independent loss.

The finite algebraic prerequisite is now established.  What remains is the
sparse endpoint/commutator payment after amalgamating the relative pair;
the six-element symmetry alone does not supply that analytic estimate.

## Attempts

- Two independent CNOT shears on a common relative endpoint fail before the
  analytic stage: exhaustive quadratic-form search gives no invariant marked
  rank-four form.  Pair permutations are the surviving three-chart geometry.
- Merely averaging the three `S_3` charts returns allowed radical characters
  but also averages the forbidden child; without a signed endpoint failure
  estimate it produces no HS payment.  The next search must retain the
  `00,01,11` orientation rather than use the Reynolds average.
