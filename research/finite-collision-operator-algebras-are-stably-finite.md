Let G be any group and n >= 1 a fixed finite integer. On X = G^n let
C_n be the unital complex algebra of functions generated by the
indicators

    1_{x_i^(-1) x_j = s},     1 <= i,j <= n, s in G.

Put K = G^n. Use coordinatewise right translation and the action

    alpha_h(f)(x) = f(xh).

Then the algebraic crossed product

    D_n = C_n semidirect_alpha K

is stably finite: for every finite m and A,B in M_m(D_n),

    BA = I_m implies AB = I_m.                         (FCO1)

No amenability, soficity, residual finiteness, finite-generation, or
torsion hypothesis on G is required. Coefficients here are complex;
the claim does not assert the analogous result in positive
characteristic or resolve Kaplansky's general conjecture.

The natural representation on finitely supported functions on G^n is
faithful:

    (f u_h xi)(x) = f(x) xi(xh).

It extends to bounded operators on l^2(G^n) and respects the involution

    (f u_h)^* = alpha_{h^(-1)}(conjugate(f)) u_{h^(-1)}.

In particular real collision indicators and coordinate shifts are
closed under formal transpose. Thus an operator in D_n that maps basis
tuples injectively to basis tuples satisfies T^*T = I and, by (FCO1),
TT^* = I. The same implication holds at every matrix size and in every
idempotent corner, using the corner identity.

For infinite G, C_n is the direct sum of its components indexed by
partitions of {1,...,n}. A partition with k blocks specifies k freely
chosen block roots and finitely specified relative offsets inside
each block. Products join partitions. Assigning the polynomial weight
z^k to each offset indicator gives an invariant functional and hence
a polynomial-valued trace on D_n. For a nonzero matrix idempotent, its
components with maximal block count are themselves idempotents. Their
finite-matrix identifications over C[G^k] make the leading trace
coefficient strictly positive. This proves (FCO1). For finite G, D_n
is finite-dimensional.

The argument uses neither a permanence assertion for sums of locally
stably finite ideals nor an unsplit-extension assertion. Its positivity
test applies to idempotents through the leading partition degree; it
does not claim that the polynomial functional is an ordinary finite
positive measure on the whole coefficient algebra.

Finite-height orbit stratification alone would not justify the result.
The attached audit gives a two-end compactification whose crossed
product contains a strict piecewise shift. The specific collision
partition decomposition and its leading-idempotent trace are essential
to the proof here. The audit also records the exact hypotheses of
nearby groupoid-algebra finiteness results, without a novelty claim.
