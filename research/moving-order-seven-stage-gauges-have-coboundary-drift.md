**ESTABLISHED MOVING-CHART FENCE.**  Let `Gamma=GL_3(F_2)` be the exact
native packet generated by the three factors `(J,B,A)` from
`native-whitehead-hecke-head-has-order-seven-anchor`, and put `t=JBA`, so
`t^7=1`.  There is an exact finite-dimensional unitary packet model with
seven moving projections

```text
E_0,E_1,...,E_6,E_7=E_0,
T E_k=E_(k+1),                 T=pi(t),                 (MGC1)
```

and hence zero transition leakage at every stage, for which the stage
charts can report

```text
M tensor D_0, M tensor D_1, ..., M tensor D_6,          (MGC2)
```

with arbitrary commuting involutions `D_k` satisfying only

```text
D_6 D_5 ... D_0=1.                                     (MGC3)
```

In particular, with any nontrivial involution `V`, one may take

```text
(D_0,D_1,D_2,...,D_6)=(V,V,1,...,1),                   (MGC4)
```

or use different charts on the same cuts and the same exact operator to
make every `D_k=1`.  The initial chart is identical in both descriptions;
only the six intermediate charts change.

Thus neither the literal relation `T^7=1`, all seven zero-boundary orbit
cuts, nor separate occurrence typing at each stage gives the equality
`D_0=...=D_6`.  The only chart-invariant datum is the conjugacy class of the
cyclic holonomy product, which the seventh-power relation already makes
trivial.  A proof of `growing-multicut-el20-order-seven-inheritance` must
therefore provide a **cross-stage gauge lock** from additional EL20 words:
one fixed family of coefficient intertwiners joining consecutive stage
charts and forcing their transition functions to centralize and equal the
same named involution.  Merely retyping each stage up to an allowed packet
label relabeling is insufficient.

The obstruction persists even when the stage gauges are required to be
transported conjugates of one named involution.  Let `U` cyclically shift
seven reservoir coordinates and let `D` be negative on exactly two of
them.  Then

```text
U^7=1,       D^2=1,       D!=1,
D_k=U^kDU^(-k),       D_6D_5...D_0=1.                 (MGC5)
```

Every coordinate encounters exactly two negative signs in the product.
Thus the moving telescope naturally gives the conjugacy norm
`D_6...D_0`, not `D^7`.  Odd order kills `D` only after the missing
coefficient intertwiners also prove `[U,D]=0`, or equivalently identify all
seven gauges in one fixed Hecke reservoir frame.

This is a scoped finite-packet countermodel, not an exact finite-dimensional
model of the whole marked EL20 group and hence not a refutation of the
Property-`(T)`-free non-MF target.  It shows exactly what the full-group
relations still have to add.  No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, canonical
trace, or literature theorem is used.

The fence persists on the actual seven-pair marked Heisenberg actor packet.
There is a finite-dimensional exact representation in which `z=-1`, the
signed Hecke source `Q=e_(L_0,lambda_0)` is nonzero, both actual EL20 block
heads `T_1,T_2` have order seven, and the orbit cuts
`T_i^kQT_i^(-k)` have zero boundary.  Amplifying once by a trivial
two-dimensional spectator makes the chart changes above commute with every
named packet label.  Thus positivity of the marked source, both literal
order-seven block actions, and all currently named finite Heisenberg/actor
relations still do not make the phrase “the same stage gauge” intrinsic.
The missing cross-stage lock must use EL20 coefficient relations outside
that finite actor packet.
