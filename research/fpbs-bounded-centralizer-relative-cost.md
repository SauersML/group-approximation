Section 3. Under the explicit centralizer hypothesis, for every infinite H<=G and every free G-action, rel-C(R_G;R_H)=0 and C(R_G)<=C(R_H). Consequently C^*(G)<=C^*(H). This does not say C(R_G)=1.
