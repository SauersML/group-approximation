For every extension of separable C-star algebras

```text
0 -> I -> E -> D -> 0,
```

the continuous-trace shadow invariant from
`stw81-continuous-trace-shadow-invariant` satisfies

```text
ctdim(E) = max(ctdim(I),ctdim(D)).
```

Thus the canonical type-I lower bound detected by Problem LXXXI glues with
no dimension loss.  Any failure of the nuclear-dimension maximum formula
must come from noncommutative approximation colors, not from an undetected
increase in continuous-trace subquotient dimension.
