title: Nuclear-dimension-zero unital maps have one-sided local finite-dimensional range models
---

Let `phi:A->B` be a nuclear-dimension-zero unital homomorphism, with `A`
separable and `B` unital.  For every finite `F subset A` and `epsilon>0`
there is a unital finite-dimensional C-star subalgebra `D subset B` such
that every element of `phi(F)` is within `epsilon` of `D`.

This is only the near inclusion `phi(F) subset_epsilon D`; it does not say
that the unit ball or the minimal projections of `D` are close to `phi(A)`.
