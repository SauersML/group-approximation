Let `(N,tau)` be a finite factor with separable predual, let `m>=2`, and
let `a` be a positive contraction with

```text
m tau(supp(a))<=1/2.                                   (AF5-1)
```

Then the Hilbert-sum weighted-copy fibre `F_(N,m)(a)` has the universal
four-sphere modulus

```text
rho_AF,4(epsilon)=min(epsilon,1)^3/216000000.          (AF5-2)
```

Every such fibre is four-connected. Together with the lower-degree
theorems, the half-slack family is uniformly equi-`LC^4`, independently
of factor type, matrix size, II1 isomorphism class, copy number, and
weight.
