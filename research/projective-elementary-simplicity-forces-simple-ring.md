title: A nontrivial simple projective elementary group in rank at least three forces its coefficient ring to be simple

  - research/artifacts/boone-higman-projective-finiteness-gate-2026-09-08.md

Let R be a nonzero unital associative ring and n>=3. If

    EL_n(R)/Z(EL_n(R))

is a nontrivial simple group, then R is a simple ring.

No finite generation or finite presentation is assumed. This is a
necessary condition only: no converse asserting projective simplicity
over every simple ring is included.
