**ESTABLISHED RELATIVE-INTERFACE ZERO TYPE.**  In the exact binary
eight-summand chart put

```text
K=<L_0,r,l>,                 F=Q(1+r)(1+l)/4,
g=J_1J_2,                    H=<K,g>,
A=F C[H] F,                  h=FgF.                    (RHC1)
```

Here `F` is the signed character idempotent of the finite pattern group
`K`.  The exact twisted double-coset calculation gives

```text
|K|=32768,              [H:K]=1344,
dim_C(A)=52.                                             (RHC2)
```

Let `I=AhA` be the two-sided ideal generated by the native relative cross
word.  Exact integer multiplication in the signed-double-coset basis gives

```text
dim_C(I)=44,                  F notin I.                (RHC3)
```

Since `A` is a finite-dimensional complex `C*`-algebra with identity `F`,
`(RHC3)` implies that some simple summand of `A` is absent from the central
support of `h`.  Equivalently, there is an exact finite-dimensional unitary
representation `pi` of `H` such that

```text
pi(F)!=0,                    pi(FgF)=0.                 (RHC4)
```

Thus the relations internal to the signed source `K` and the single
relative word `g=J_1J_2` do not force positive native cross-Gram energy on
every stabilizer type.  In particular, decomposing only this twisted Hecke
interface cannot promote the two adjacent-character one-half calculations
to an arbitrary-profile theorem.

The scope is essential.  The representation in `(RHC4)` is a representation
of `H`, not an asserted representation of the larger packet containing
`J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3` as separately constrained actors.  Inducing it to a
larger finite packet can add other `F`-blocks on which `FgF` is nonzero.
Accordingly this claim does **not** refute
`binary-leavitt-arbitrary-profile-native-cross-gram` or full-group
`(APG1)`.  A positive full-group route must use an outside occurrence to
show that the missing `H`-type cannot occur, or must compute central support
in the larger actor packet itself.

No Property `(T)`, canonical-trace substitution, local computation,
literature theorem, or Lean compilation is used.  The finite exact audit
was run only through the MSI wrapper.
