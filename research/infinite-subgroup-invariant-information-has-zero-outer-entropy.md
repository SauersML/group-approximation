**Theorem.** Let `G ↷ (X, mu)` be a free p.m.p. action and `Λ <= G` infinite. The `G`-invariant sigma-algebra
generated by the `Λ`-invariant sets has outer Rokhlin entropy `0`. When `X` is ergodic,
`h^Rok_G(X) = h_G(X | F_Λ)`.

**Why.** The closed `G`-span of the `Λ`-invariant vectors in `L²_0(X)` is singular with `λ`. A
subrepresentation embedding into `λ` would receive a nonzero `Λ`-invariant vector, which `ℓ²(G)` does not
have. Seward's Theorem 1.2 then applies.

**Corollaries** (Section 2 of the artifact).
1. **Normal subgroups.** This recovers the free case of Alpeev–Seward Theorem 1.11(ii).
2. **Kun–Thom rigidity.** In every free action of a Theorem E group `G`, the `Γ`-invariant information, which
   in sofic actions must be `G`-invariant (Theorem C), has zero outer entropy. Over the wreath, the same holds
   for information fixed by the invisible lamps.
3. **Generalized Bernoulli shifts.** An essentially free generalized Bernoulli shift `K^(G/Λ)` over infinite
   `Λ` has Rokhlin entropy `0`. This includes the non-sofic action of Kun–Thom Corollary D, whenever it is
   essentially free.
4. **Affine lamp actions.** An essentially free affine lamp action `B^(X) ⋊ G ↷ B^X` has Rokhlin entropy `0`.

**Reading.** Positive Rokhlin entropy lives only on information that no infinite subgroup fixes. Every
nonsoficity mechanism in the graph works through invariant layers, and those carry no entropy. A lower bound
over a nonsofic group, or a deficit, must act on the mixing part of an action.
