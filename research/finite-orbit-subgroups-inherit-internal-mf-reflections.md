For `1<=i<=m`, let `Q_i` be countable virtually free and let

```text
W_i=U^(Q_i) semidirectProduct Q_i<=U
```

be its internal Leavitt reflection.  Put

```text
R=product_i Q_i,
B=product_i U^(Q_i),
P=product_i W_i=B semidirectProduct R<=U.                (FOS1)
```

For every subgroup `S<=R`, the preimage

```text
K_S=B semidirectProduct S<=P<=U                         (FOS2)
```

has complete MF reflection `S`:

```text
Rad_MF(K_S)=B,                       R_MF(K_S)~=S,       (FOS3)
Hom(S,M)~=Hom(K_S,M),                                  (FOS4)
cl_MF^(K_S)(N)=pi_S^(-1)(cl_MF^S(pi_S(N)))              (FOS5)
```

for every MF group `M` and every normal subgroup `N` of `K_S`.  The same
factorization holds for finite, compact, finite-dimensional unitary, and
finite-dimensional linear targets over every field.  Hence all these
residuals equal `B`, and the profinite and Bohr completions of `K_S` are
canonically those of `S`.

Let `S_i` be the image of `S` in `Q_i`.  If `S` is finitely generated and

```text
[Q_i:S_i]<infinity                                    (1<=i<=m),   (FOS6)
```

then `K_S` is finitely generated.  Its radical is normally generated by at
most

```text
sum_i [Q_i:S_i]                                        (FOS7)
```

copies of one fixed involution.  In particular, a finitely generated
subdirect product `S<=product_i Q_i` has an internal finitely generated
non-MF reflection whose radical is normally generated by at most `m`
involutions.
