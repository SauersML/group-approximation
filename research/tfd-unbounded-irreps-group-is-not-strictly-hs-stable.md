**Theorem.**  Let `Gamma` be generated by a finite set `S`.  Let `R` be any
finite set of words in `S^(+-1)` that are trivial in `Gamma`, and let
`|g|_S` denote word length over `S^(+-1)`.  A unitary tuple `tau: S -> U(k)`
is evaluated on words with `tau(s^(-1)) = tau(s)^*`.
Suppose

1. (**(T;FD)**) there are a finite set `S_0 subset Gamma` and `kappa_0 > 0`
   such that for every finite-dimensional unitary representation `pi` on
   `H` and every `v in H` orthogonal to `H^Gamma`,
   `max_(s in S_0) ||pi(s) v - v|| >= kappa_0 ||v||`; put
   `m = max_(s in S_0) |s|_S` and `kappa = kappa_0 / m`;
2. `Gamma` has irreducible finite-dimensional unitary representations of
   arbitrarily large dimension.

Let `L = max_(r in R) |r|`.  For every irreducible
`sigma: Gamma -> U(k+1)` there is a unitary tuple `tau: S -> U(k)` with

```text
max_(r in R) ||tau(r) - 1||_2 <= 2L / sqrt(k),                       (TU1)
max_(s in S) ||tau(s) - rho(s)||_2 >= kappa - 2/sqrt(k)               (TU2)
   for EVERY exact representation rho: Gamma -> U(k).
```

Here `||X||_2 = (tr_k X^* X)^(1/2)` is the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.

**Corollary.**  `Gamma` is not Hilbert--Schmidt stable in the same-dimension
sense: taking `sigma` of dimension `k+1 -> infinity`, the tuples `tau`
have relator defect `-> 0` but stay at distance `>= kappa/2` on generators
from every exact representation of the same dimension.  This holds for any
finite presentation and any choice of the `l^infty` or `l^2` combination
over generators and relators: the `l^2` sum over generators dominates the
maximum, and the `l^2` sum over relators is at most `|R|^(1/2)` times it.
Since strict HS stability does not depend on the finite presentation or
generating set, the conclusion is presentation-independent.

`tau` is the polar compression of `sigma` to the orthocomplement of one
unit vector.  The invariant that blocks strict repair is the (T;FD) gap on
the finite-dimensional representation `X -> sigma(g) X rho(g)^(-1)` of
`Gamma` on `Hom(C^k, C^(k+1))`.  That representation has no invariant
vectors because an intertwiner has image a `sigma`-invariant subspace of
dimension `<= k < k+1`.

No hyperlinearity, finite presentation, amenability or regular-trace input
is used.
