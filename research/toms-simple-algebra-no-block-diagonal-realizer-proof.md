Imports from arXiv:2606.12188v2, Proposition 4 and its proof:
- `psi_i = psi_i^top + psi_i^pt`, where `psi^pt` is a finite sum of point evaluations;
- the telescoped top map from stage `i` to `i+1` is the Section 4 diagonal map of
  multiplicity `M_i`, i.e. coordinate projections of `X_(i+1) = (X_i)^(M_i) x P_i`
  twisted by line bundles;
- `alpha_i = rank(s^top_(i+1))/rank(s_(i+1))`, `delta = prod alpha_i`, and
  `rank q_m / rank s_m = prod_(i<m) alpha_i`.
Branch traces: `doubling-ah-finite-stage-continuous-ranks-constant-proof`,
Steps 1–2. `sigma_x` has stage-`n` measure `beta_n delta_x + kappa_n`, `kappa_n` does
not depend on `x`, and `x -> sigma_x` is weak* continuous.

**Step 1 (robust Poincare–Miranda).** This is Step 1 of
`toms-line-subalgebra-no-nonconstant-rank-proof`, verbatim. If `V_b ⊂ [0,1]^K` are
relatively open with `{t_b = 1} ⊂ V_b`, there are `t_j in ∩_b V_b` with `t_j -> t*`,
and every `b` with `t* in V_b` has `t*_b = 0`.

**Step 2 (blocks).** Expanding `psi_(n,M)` into paths gives two kinds of block.
- A path through top maps only is a coordinate projection onto one of the `K`
  factors of `X_M = (X_n)^K x P`, twisted by a line bundle.
- A path through some point evaluation gives a constant field, twisted by
  later line bundles.
The top blocks have total rank `K r_n = r_M prod_(n<=i<M) alpha_i`.

**Step 3 (traces of stage elements).** For `a in M_infinity(B_M)_+`,
`d_(sigma_w)(a) = beta_M rank a(w)/r_M + C(a)` with `C(a) = integral rank a / r_M d kappa_M`.
For `P(w) = rank psi_(n,M)(a_n)(w) = sum_b R_n(w^(b)) + Pi`, where `Pi` is the
constant point-block rank, `d_(sigma_w)(a_n) = beta_M P(w)/r_M + C(psi(a_n))`.

**Step 4 (budget).** `a_n <~ a_M` gives `R_M >= P` pointwise, hence
`C(a_M) >= C(psi(a_n))`. The bands `d_(sigma_w)(a_M) <= f(sigma_w)` and
`d_(sigma_w)(a_n) >= f(sigma_w) - eps_n` give `beta_M (R_M(w) - P(w)) / r_M <= eps_n`. Every
block excess is non-negative by domination, so the top excesses
`delta_b(w) = kappa_b(w) - R_n(w^(b))` satisfy `sum_b delta_b(w) <= r_M eps_n / beta_M`.

**Step 5 (profile and levels).**
- `h` pushes forward to `h(z_l)` on top leaves and to constants on point blocks.
  So `tr_z psi_(1,n)(h) = prod_(i<n) alpha_i F_n(z) + c`, and
  `f(sigma_x) = delta F_n(x) + c'`.
- Let `x_min, x_max` put every leaf at a minimum or maximum of `h`. The bands at
  `sigma_x`, whose contamination term `C(a_n)` is constant in `x`, give
  `iota_max - iota_min >= (r_n / beta_n)(delta rho - eps_n)`, with
  `iota = R_n(x_max), R_n(x_min)`.

**Step 6 (all-wall point).** Let `gamma` be a path in the connected `X_n` from
`x_min` to `x_max`. Let `sigma(t)` have top coordinates `gamma(t_b)` and fixed
`P`-coordinates, and put `V_b = { t : kappa_b(sigma(t)) >= iota_max }`. The face
`{t_b = 1}` lies in `V_b`. Step 1 gives `t_j -> t*`. Each violator `b` (`t* in V_b`) has
`t*_b = 0` and excess at least `iota_max - iota_min`. So

```text
|X| <= (r_M / r_n)(beta_n / beta_M) eps_n / (delta rho - eps_n) = K eps_n / (delta rho - eps_n) <= K/2,
```

using `r_M / r_n = K / prod_(n<=i<M) alpha_i = K beta_M / beta_n` and `delta rho >= 3 eps_n`.

**Step 7 (the jump).** The non-violating top blocks go from `<= iota_max - 1` at
`t*` to `>= iota_max` at `t_j`. The other blocks do not decrease for large `j`,
by lower semicontinuity. So `R_M(sigma(t_j)) - R_M(sigma(t*)) >= K/2`. Combining the
upper band at `sigma(t_j)`, the lower band at `sigma(t*)`, the common constant
`C(a_M)` and continuity of `f(sigma_(sigma(t)))`:

```text
eps_M >= beta_M K / (2 r_M) = beta_M prod_(n<=i<M) alpha_i / (2 r_n) = beta_n / (2 r_n) >= delta / (2 r_n).
```

**Step 8 (conclusion and lines).** Since `eps_n -> 0`, choose `n` with
`3 eps_n <= delta rho`. Then `eps_M >= delta/(2 r_n)` for all `M > n`, which
contradicts `eps_M -> 0`.
- A commutative subalgebra generated by stage-central functions times a
  compatible family of orthogonal line projections has two properties when
  each line lies in one block of every `psi_(n,M)`:
  - its Cuntz-increasing sequences are block-diagonal with domination line by
    line, as in Step 7 of `toms-line-subalgebra-no-nonconstant-rank-proof`;
  - its suprema with continuous rank have uniformly convergent approximants, by
    the Dini argument there.
- Pushforwards of the lines of `theta^d` and of point-block lines qualify. So
  such a subalgebra contains no element of rank `tau(h)`. Square.
