**ESTABLISHED SCOPED SIGNED-SOURCE FENCE.**  Let
`W=Jhat_(r,m)` be one fresh endpoint partial Whitehead, where the actor
endpoint is `r in {2,4}` and its other endpoint `p` is outside the signed
actor coordinates.  Put

```text
Q=e_(L_0,lambda_0),              Q'=WQW^(-1),
K_r=L_0 intersect WL_0W^(-1).                              (EHS1)
```

Then `K_r` is exactly the subgroup of `L_0` whose constant actor matrices
have no off-diagonal entry in row or column `r`.  The two transported
characters agree on `K_r`, and

```text
|K_2|=256,          lambda_0|K_2 has signs 128/128,
|K_4|=512,          lambda_0|K_4 has signs 256/256.          (EHS2)
```

More explicitly, `K_2` is generated by the seven displayed `L_0`
generators not incident to `2`.  The analogous seven non-`4`-incident
generators together with

```text
x_25(1)=[x_24(1),x_45(1)]
```

generate `K_4`; this additional generator has positive character.  Since
`W` commutes with the mark, the same calculation gives the exact signed
detector-subgroup intersection

```text
{z^((1-lambda_0(g))/2)g:g in L_0}
 intersect
W{z^((1-lambda_0(g))/2)g:g in L_0}W^(-1)
= {z^((1-lambda_0(k))/2)k:k in K_r}.                    (EHS3)
```

The endpoint edge itself has full Grams, but only between the two moved
frames:

```text
T=Q'WQ=WQ,                 T^*T=Q,       TT^*=Q'.       (EHS4)
```

In the canonical regular trace the finite-character intersection formula
does give

```text
||QQ'||_2^2=tau(QQ')=tau(Q)/32    for r=2,
||QQ'||_2^2=tau(QQ')=tau(Q)/16    for r=4.              (EHS5)
```

These are Hecke coefficients, not positive meet or normalized-rank floors
in an arbitrary matrix profile.  Indeed there are nontrivial characters
`xi_r` of `L_0` which are trivial on `K_r`: one may take `xi_2` negative
only on the displayed generator `4->2`, and `xi_4` negative only on
`5->4`.  In the exact two-dimensional profile

```text
g |-> diag(lambda_0(g),lambda_0(g)xi_r(g)),
W |-> [[0,1],[1,0]],
```

all intersection relations `WkW^(-1)=k`, `k in K_r`, hold, while

```text
Q=diag(1,0),                 Q'=diag(0,1),              (EHS6)
```

so the common source meet is zero.  This is a representation of the exact
finite signed-source profile, not of the full Steinberg group; it sharply
fences any deduction which uses only `(EHS1)--(EHS4)`.

Short ordinary dressings do not improve the Grams.  Multiplication by an
element of `L_0` is scalar on `Q`.  If `n` normalizes
`(L_0,lambda_0)`, then `WnQ` is another full edge onto `Q'` and its cross
Gram with `WQ` is the unitary `nQ`, of squared Hilbert--Schmidt mass
`tau(Q)`.  No constant actor normalizer returns the transported signed pair
to the original one, because it cannot cancel the nonzero spare-row and
spare-column coefficients of `W`.  The inverse endpoint Whitehead does
return it, but `W^2=1` only cancels the same gauge tautologically.  Thus a
matching detector or normalizer word supplies neither a positive common
source nor a fixed multiplicity frame.

The remaining endpoint must be an ordinary mixed occurrence producing a
proper range or fixing the frame on an independently positive source.  No
Property `(T)`, canonical-trace hypothesis for the MF model, literature
input, local computation, or Lean compilation is used.
