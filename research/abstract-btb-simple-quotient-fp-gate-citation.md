This is exactly Corollary 4.2 of Fournier-Facio--Wu--Zaremsky,
[*Abstract twisted Brin--Thompson
groups*](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24687v2), in the manuscript revision
dated 24 August 2026.

For completeness, the short deduction in the source is as follows.  Finite
presentability of `SV_G` implies, by their Theorem B, that the original action
has type `[A_2]`: `G` is finitely presented, point stabilizers are finitely
generated, and there are finitely many diagonal pair-orbits.  Passing from
`G` to `G/K` preserves finite generation of point stabilizers and the pair-
orbit set.  The induced action of `G/K` is faithful.  Zaremsky's faithful
finite-presentation criterion therefore leaves exactly one condition:
`SV_{G/K}` is finitely presented if and only if `G/K` is finitely presented.
`\u220e`
