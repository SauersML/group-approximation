There is a C-star-diagonal

```text
D subset M_(2^infinity)
```

whose Gelfand spectrum is the Cantor space and which is not an AF diagonal.
The ambient CAR algebra `M_(2^infinity)` is UHF, hence AF.  Thus this is a
literal counterexample to the sharp universal form of STW Problem XLVII
recorded in `stw99-problem-xlvii-af-cartan-criterion`.

In fact the CAR algebra has Cantor-spectrum diagonals of every diagonal
dimension `n in {0,1,2,...,infinity}`, and therefore has at least countably
many pairwise non-conjugate Cantor-spectrum C-star-diagonals.
