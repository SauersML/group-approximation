## Proof

A purely infinite simple ring is not directly finite and its nonzero
idempotent `1-st` generates the whole ring as a two-sided ideal by
simplicity, so
`full-complementary-idempotent-elementary-full-mf-radical` applies and
`EL_4(R)` has full MF radical.

**The kernel collapses.**  By `unit-corner-embedding-normally-generates-commutators`,
`[R^x,R^x]` is generated by the conjugates of `jmath(EL_4(R))`, each of which
is isomorphic to `EL_4(R)` and so has full MF radical.  A homomorphism from
`[R^x,R^x]` to an MF group restricts to each generating subgroup and is
trivial there, so it is trivial.  So `[R^x,R^x] <= Rad_MF(R^x)`.

**The quotient is attained.**  `K_1(R)` is countable abelian, hence amenable,
hence MF by `amenable-implies-operator-mf`.  So `kappa` is itself a
homomorphism to an MF group, giving `Rad_MF(R^x) <= ker kappa`.

By the second prerequisite `ker kappa = [R^x,R^x]`, so the two inclusions are
equalities, every homomorphism to an MF group kills `ker kappa` and factors
through `kappa`, and the factorization is unique because `kappa` is onto.
