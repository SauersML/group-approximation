Use the same MF C-star amalgam and reduced von Neumann amalgam as the cited
weak-MF proof.  That proof already identifies the von Neumann closures with
`L(G)` and `L(H)` and uses their normal form for group injectivity.  Keeping
the reduced-amalgam trace rather than discarding it gives the regular
character on the double.
