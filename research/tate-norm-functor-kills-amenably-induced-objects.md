**ESTABLISHED (derivation; unreviewed).**
- **Setting.** `Γ` is torsion-free, `p` prime, `W = Γ ≀ Z/p`, and `T` is the Tate norm.
- **The functor.** `Y = cone([ℓ²(W/H)]) ⊗ q^*1_T`, and `Ψ(A) := W ⋉_r (cone([ℓ²(W/H)]) ⊗ T(A))`,
  an additive exact functor `KK^Γ → KK`. `Ψ(C) = W ⋉_r Y` is the crossed product of the cone of the
  Tate diagonal.

1. `Ψ` vanishes on the localizing subcategory `𝒜_Γ` generated by `Ind_K^Γ B` for amenable `K ≤ Γ`.
   `𝒜_Γ` contains the cellular class `<CI_Γ>`.
2. If `Γ` is infinite with property (T), then `C ∉ 𝒜_Γ`.
   - The objects `A` for which `λ_A : Γ ⋉_max A → Γ ⋉_r A` is a KK-equivalence form a localizing class
     containing `𝒜_Γ`.
   - `λ_C` kills the Kazhdan projection `p`, which has `[triv]_*[p] = 1`.

**Consequence.** `kazhdan-tate-crossed-product-inclusion-is-kk-equivalence` asks `Ψ` to kill the
image of the unit in the non-K-amenable quotient `KK^Γ/𝒜_Γ`. No argument that generates `C` from
amenably induced objects can prove it. A negative answer would need a reduced-side analogue of the
Kazhdan-projection pairing on `T((1 − γ)C)`.
