title: A centerless normal subgroup of a finitely presented group whose outer automorphism group is locally finite is finitely presented
---

**Statement.** Let `G` be finitely presented and `N ⊴ G` with trivial center, such that every
finitely generated subgroup of `Out(N)` is finite. Then `N` is a retract of a finite-index
subgroup of `G`. In particular `N` is finitely presented.

Contrapositive: a centerless group that is not finitely presented and has locally finite `Out`
is a normal subgroup of no finitely presented group.

Elementary; no novelty claimed. Proof: `locally-finite-out-normal-subgroup-retract-proof`.
