**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Γ` be Kazhdan with Kazhdan pair `(F_Γ, κ)`. Let `H ≥ Γ` be generated by
`P_Γ(H) = {g : gΓg^(-1) ⊆ Γ}`, let `σ : H -> U(M)`, `M = prod_U M_n`, and put `R = σ(Γ)' ∩ M`.

A projection `p ∈ R` is *roundable* if the corner representation `γ -> pσ(γ)` satisfies (H1) in `pMp`.

1. **A maximal roundable projection.** Roundable projections are closed under subprojections, equivalence in `R`,
   orthogonal sums and increasing limits; each step uses the Kazhdan corner lemma. So there is a largest roundable
   projection `p_max`, and `p_max ∈ Z(R)`.
2. **Compressor invariance.** For `g ∈ P_Γ(H)`, the map `θ_g = Ad σ(g)^*` sends `R` into `R` and roundable
   projections to roundable ones. Since it preserves the trace, `θ_g(p_max) = p_max`. Hence

   ```text
   p_max  ∈  σ(H)' ∩ Z(σ(Γ)' ∩ M),        σ = σ_rnd ⊕ σ_nr.
   ```

   Here `σ_rnd|Γ` satisfies (H1), and `σ_nr|Γ` has no nonzero roundable `Γ`-invariant corner.
3. **Traces.** Suppose `σ` is trace-preserving and `H` is icc modulo a finite center `Z`. Then both summands have
   trace `0` off `Z`, so with `Z = 1` both are trace-preserving `H`-models.

**For the Kun--Thom pair** (`Γ = EL_r(F_q[x_1..x_d]) < G`, `r >= 3`, `d >= 2`):
- **The hypotheses hold.** `G = <P_Γ(G)>`, because the compressors `I + E_ij` generate `SL_d(Z)` and
  `SL_d(Z)`-conjugates of `Γ` contain every `e_ij(x^v)`. `G` is icc modulo the scalars of `EL_r(F_q)`, which are
  trivial iff `gcd(r, q-1) = 1`.
- **Normal form.** With `gcd(r, q-1) = 1`, a `G`-model at which (H1) fails has a nonzero totally non-roundable
  summand that is again a trace-preserving `G`-model.
- **Wreath models.** Every trace-preserving model of the wreath `W` over `G` has `p_max = 0`: no nonzero
  `Γ`-invariant corner of its vertex rounds. `p_max` commutes with `σ(G)` and with the root lamp, hence with all of
  `σ(W)`. A nonzero corner would be a trace-preserving `W`-model with roundable vertex, which
  `kt-wreath-models-never-round-on-the-vertex` excludes.
- **One compressor.** The theorem applies to `V_A = <Γ, A>`. At the exact-vertex wreath models of
  `one-compressor-wreath-has-models-with-exact-origin-vertex`, `p_max = 1`.

**What extension buys here, and what not.** Extension by compressors makes roundability an actor-invariant
property. Nothing here produces a rounding or decides whether totally non-roundable `G`-models exist.
