**OPEN.**  For every separable tracial von Neumann algebra `(M, tau)`, every
`k >= 1`, every tuple `u_1, ..., u_k in U(M)` and every word
`w in F_k * <t>` with `deg_t(w) = m != 0`, there are a tracial von Neumann
algebra `(N, sigma)`, a unital trace-preserving `*`-embedding
`iota : M -> N`, and `T in U(N)` with

    w(iota(u_1), ..., iota(u_k), T) = 1 .

Call this the **tracial root theorem** (RT).

## Where it sits

- **It implies Kervaire--Laudenbach.**  Route `kl-via-tracial-root-extensions`:
  take `M = L(A)` for the coefficient subgroup `A` and `u_i = lambda(a_i)`.
- **It holds for Connes-embeddable algebras.**
  `connes-embeddable-tracial-algebras-solve-nonsingular-equations`.  That is
  Pestov's hyperlinear argument written with tracial coefficients.
- **It is one algebra's statement.**
  `root-extensions-descend-from-a-locally-universal-algebra`: RT for a single
  locally universal tracial von Neumann algebra gives RT for all separable
  ones.
- **Kervaire--Laudenbach delivers exactly part of it.**
  `kl-yields-tracial-roots-for-quotient-indicator-traces`: over a group
  `Gamma`, the conjecture for all quotients of `Gamma` gives RT for the algebra
  generated by the coefficients under every trace in the closed convex hull
  of the quotient indicators `1_K` (`K` normal in `Gamma`).  Conversely RT at
  the regular trace `1_{1}` is the conjecture.  So RT is a priori stronger
  than the conjecture only at traces outside that hull which are also not
  Connes-embeddable.

A failure of RT is a non-Connes-embeddable tracial algebra with an unsolvable
equation.  It would not refute `kervaire-laudenbach-nonsingular-conjecture`.

## Attempts

1. **Degree theory in `U(M)`.**  *Dies.*  The Gerstenhaber--Rothaus proof
   evaluates the degree of the word map on the compact manifold `U(d)`.
   - D. Jekel, *The unitary group of a II_1 factor is SOT-contractible*,
     arXiv:2508.05834v3 (3 Sep 2025), abstract, verbatim: "We show that the
     unitary group of any SOT-separable $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor $M$, with the
     strong operator topology, is contractible."
   - With the norm topology `U(M)` is infinite-dimensional and not compact,
     so it carries no fundamental class either.  The word map
     `T -> w(u, T)` acts on `pi_1` by multiplication by `m`, which obstructs
     nothing and supplies no root.
   - Popa--Takesaki (Comm. Math. Phys. 155, 1993) treated an earlier class of
     II_1 factors.  Their paper was not read here.
2. **Existentially closed factors.**  *Restatement.*  Farah--Goldbring--Hart--
   Sherman, *Existentially closed II_1 factors*, arXiv:1310.5138, study
   factors in which systems solvable in an extension are approximately
   solvable inside.  Solvability in some extension is the hypothesis there
   and the conclusion here.  This is the tracial case of
   `overgroup-room-does-not-supply-a-root`.
3. **Approximate roots.**  *Equivalent, not easier.*  If for every
   `eps > 0` some tracial extension `N_eps` contains `T_eps` with
   `|| w(u, T_eps) - 1 ||_2 <= eps`, then the tracial ultraproduct of the
   `N_eps` contains `M` diagonally and trace-preservingly, and `(T_eps)` is
   an exact root.  So RT is closed under this relaxation.  It gives no new
   root source: the extensions must still be produced.
4. **Determinants.**  *No invariant.*  On a II_1 factor the trace of the
   logarithm of a unitary is determined only modulo the real trace values of
   projections, so a de la Harpe--Skandalis-type determinant carries no
   information on `U(M)`.  This remark is not checked against a source.

## Exact gap

Produce a root in a trace-preserving extension when the coefficient trace is
neither Connes-embeddable nor in the closed convex hull of quotient
indicators.  No root source is known there.  Topology is gone by Attempt 1,
closure properties restate the problem by Attempt 2, and approximation
classes are exhausted by the Connes-embeddable case.
