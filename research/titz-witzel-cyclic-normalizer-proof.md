First work on one invariant subset, and let `N` be the order of the
restriction of `u`.  The normalizer hypothesis gives an integer
`q`, invertible modulo `N`, such that

```text
e u e=u^q,                    q^2=1 mod N.                 (1)
```

The second congruence follows by conjugating twice and using `e^2=1`.  Every
word therefore has a normal form `u^(alpha+beta q)e^epsilon`, with exponents
read modulo `N`.  Substitute the successive word definitions in `(TW2G1)`.
Straight collection with `e u^m=u^(qm)e` gives

```text
R_c   =u^(4+4q),
R_rho=u^(-6-7q)e,
R_b   =u^(-3-9q),
R_a   =u^(-9)e.                                            (2)
```

The dependency-free checker attached to the claim reconstructs all the words
from `(TW2G1)` and verifies these four normal forms in
`Z[q]/(q^2-1)`.

Put

```text
P=R_b,                         Q=R_rho R_a^(-1).            (3)
```

Equation (2) gives

```text
P=u^(-3-9q),                   Q=u^(3-7q).                  (4)
```

All these elements lie in the cyclic group generated by `u`.  The elementary
exponent identity

```text
-(-3-9q)+q(-3-9q)-2q(3-7q)=8                              (5)
```

uses only `q^2=1`.  Consequently (5) is the exact permutation identity

```text
u^8=P^(-1) (ePe) (eQe)^(-2).                              (6)
```

Normalized Hamming distance is bi-invariant and invariant under conjugacy.
The support inequality for a product, applied to (6), gives

```text
d_H(u^8,1) <= 2 d_H(P,1)+2 d_H(Q,1).
```

Finally, (3) and the same support inequality give

```text
d_H(P,1)=d_H(R_b,1),
d_H(Q,1)<=d_H(R_rho,1)+d_H(R_a,1).
```

Substitution proves `(TWCN3)` on this subset.  Normalized Hamming distance on
a disjoint invariant union is the size-weighted average of the normalized
distances on its subsets.  Averaging the same inequality over all subsets
proves `(TWCN3)` globally.  If all four gate defects tend to zero, its right
side tends to zero, contradicting a positive `limsup` for the mark.

For the block-quotient statement, evaluate the same words after passing to
the induced permutations of the finite block set.  The theorem just proved
gives `(TWCN3)` there.  If a relator word moves a block, it moves every point
of that block.  Equal block sizes therefore give, for each relator `R`,

```text
d_H,B(bar(R),1) <= d_H,Omega(R,1).                         (7)
```

Apply (7) to the three terms on the right of the quotient version of
`(TWCN3)`.  This is exactly `(TWCN4)`.
