`D_b = [x₂₃(1), x₃₄(b)]` and `C_a = [x₁₂(a), x₂₃(1)]` translate the two
outer-root character PVMs by `a ↦ ℓ(ab)` and `b ↦ ℓ(ab)`; their kernels are
exactly the radicals of `b_ℓ`. Unitarity makes character multiplicity
constant along every translation orbit, and averaged approximate
nested-commutator error is exactly twice the mass outside the required
shift graph (Fourier Parseval). Removes arbitrary multiplicity-gauge
recovery from the label-level paired-quotient route.
