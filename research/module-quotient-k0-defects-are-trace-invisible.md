For any countable abelian G-module V and invariant W, use C, B, J,
D, p and q̄ from
[module defect localization](module-k-defect-is-liftable-quotient-kernel.md).
Every tracial state on D factors through B. Every bounded positive
trace ρ on D therefore satisfies ρ_*(z)=0 for every z∈ker q̄_* in
K_0(D), in particular for im p_*∩ker q̄_*.

If V is torsion, any tracial state τ on C whose K_0 pairing vanishes
on im(K_0(J)→K_0(C)) annihilates J and factors through B. Thus such
a τ cannot distinguish a middle-term degree-zero defect.

All matrix pairings use unnormalized matrix traces. The general
quotient assertion does not require V torsion. Neither statement
decides K_1, constructs a defect, or excludes unbounded semifinite
traces, cyclic cocycles or index pairings. See Sections 3–4 of the
[proof document](artifacts/Cairn_Baum_Connes_General_Attempt.md).
[The torus example](torus-has-nonzero-k0-class-invisible-to-every-trace.md)
shows why vanishing of these pairings does not prove K-theory vanishing.
