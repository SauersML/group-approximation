Let `Γ` be a finite Coxeter graph on `S`. The *dimension* of `A_Γ` is the largest `|T|` over
subsets `T ⊆ S` generating a finite subgroup of `W_Γ`. If this dimension is at most 2, then
`A_Γ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture.

This contains every Artin group of large type (`m_st ≥ 3` for all `s ≠ t`) on at least 3
generators, the case proved earlier by H. Hendriks, Invent. Math. 79 (1985) 375–381.
