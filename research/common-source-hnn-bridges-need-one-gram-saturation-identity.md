title: Four context HNN bridges share the leakage source exactly when one source-Gram saturation identity holds
---

For selected context projections `p_c`, bridge unitaries `U_c`, and one
off-capacity source `Q`, the unique missing common-source datum is

```text
sum_c ||(1-p_c)U_cQ||_2^2=o(1).
```

It is sufficient, by spectral intersection and polar rounding, to produce
literal partial isometries with one source.  Ordinary HNN restriction data
do not force it because they transport every induced summand, not only the
selected target type.
