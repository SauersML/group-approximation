A torsion-free two-generator one-relator group `G` has a zero-exponent basis,
because `G` maps onto `Z`. Either some such basis has a peelable extreme letter,
and the first prerequisite makes `G` sofic, or none does, and `G` lies in the
class of the second prerequisite. The case split is exhaustive, and neither
prerequisite restates the target: the first is proved, and the second omits
every group the first covers.
