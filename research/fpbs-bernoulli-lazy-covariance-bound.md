Lemma 2.1. For a real mean-zero square-integrable FIID process Z, 0<=E[Z(o)Z(X_n)]<=rho^n E[Z(o)^2]. Applies to vertex Bernoulli shifts, including edge iid realizations via disjoint half-edge coordinates.
