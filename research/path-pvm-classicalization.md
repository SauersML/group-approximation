title: Path-shaped PVM classicalization has no outcome loss
---

For finite PVMs `P, Q`: compress `Q_j` into every `P_i` corner and apply
cardinality-free POVM orthogonalization there, keeping `P` fixed and
producing a commuting PVM `Q′` with
`Σⱼ ‖Q′ⱼ − Qⱼ‖₂² ≤ 5 Σᵢⱼ ‖[Pᵢ, Qⱼ]‖₂²`. Applied successively along
`L₀–R₀–L₁–R₁`, all three required edges commute exactly with universal
error bounds even when map-outcome sets grow. For exact finite abelian root
representations, total character-PVM commutator energy equals the
subgroup-averaged unitary commutator defect (Parseval). Simultaneous PVM
rounding is closed.
