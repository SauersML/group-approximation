The explicit infinite simple ICC Kazhdan group
`H=EL_16(L_(F_2)(1,2))` is a universal MF absorber with four
simultaneous forms of rigidity:

1. `Rad_MF(H)=H`;
2. every operator-norm asymptotic representation of `H` collapses to the
   trivial representation;
3. `C*_r(H)` and every unital C-star over-algebra containing it have no
   nonzero MF image;
4. every ergodic pmp action of `H` is weakly mixing and strongly ergodic.

For every countable group `B`, adjoining `H` as a normally generated kernel
preserves exactly the MF-visible quotient of `B`.  If `B` is MF, the direct
product `H x B` has radical exactly `H x {1}`, while its reduced group C-star
algebra still has no nonzero MF image.  Finitely presented versions follow
from the finitely presented Hilbert-hotel cover.

Tensoring `C*_r(H)` with `C(X)` for any nonempty compact metrizable `X`
produces a separable stably finite continuous field over `X` whose every
nonzero quotient is non-MF.

For the finitely presented cover, total radical is equivalent to a
dimension-independent gap for its fixed finite relator system away from the
trivial unitary tuple.

The model itself is simple and ICC.  Its group von Neumann algebra is a
property-`(T)` `II_1` factor and, as a C-star algebra, has no nonzero image in
an MF C-star algebra.

Its reduced group C-star algebra is a separable primitive stably finite
prime algebra, yet every one of its nonzero quotients is non-MF and
non-quasidiagonal.

Over every field, the universal finite-dimensional quotient of the group
algebra is only its scalar augmentation quotient.

The finitely presented single-defect Hilbert-hotel quotient also supports MF
camouflage surgery.  For every finitely presented MF group `Q`, it produces a
finitely presented non-MF group whose entire MF representation functor is
that of `Q` and whose exact MF radical is normally generated by one word.
For `Q=Z`, that one word classifies MF across the construction's entire
quotient lattice.  A torsion-free calibration quotient further separates
all-fields exact finite-dimensional visibility from norm-corona visibility
inside one finitely presented non-MF group.

At the matrix-corona level, stable projections and finite projective modules
are completely classified by eventual coordinate-rank germs.  This gives
exact comparison, cancellation, and the theorem that a finite projective
corona module carrying a binary Leavitt action must vanish.  Finite additive
root windows and three-root Heisenberg windows carry compatible rank-germ
multiplicities recording finite addition and multiplication.

The Whitehead calculation also holds over every countable ring with an
explicit binary Leavitt family: property `(T)` for the four-coordinate source
and the ambient elementary group forces the ambient group to have full MF
radical.  Faithful tracial dimension separately rules out every nonzero
finite projective tracial-ultraproduct module that is isomorphic to two copies
of itself, and hence every such module carrying a binary Leavitt action.  In
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt models, a conjugate child subgroup contained in
its parent has exactly the same macroscopic adjoint fixed-sector projection.
The remaining tracial frontier is the coherent positive-density module
decoder, which is recorded separately as an open implication rather than as
a claimed nonhyperlinearity theorem.

The radical quotient is moreover an epireflector onto countable MF groups.
MF limits are created inside groups, MF colimits are ordinary group colimits
followed by reflection, and MF-equivalences are stable under countable
colimits.  Rank germs classify projection-generated corona ideals and give
every corona representation an ideal-valued support length.  These calculi
produce Boolean full-radical towers and, over every finitely presented MF
base, infinitely many pairwise nonisomorphic finitely presented Grushko
thickenings with exactly the same MF-visible quotient.
