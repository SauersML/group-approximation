Literature import, credited in full.

Mark Braverman, Subhash Khot, Dor Minzer, *On Rich 2-to-1 Games*, in
Proceedings of the 12th Innovations in Theoretical Computer Science
Conference (ITCS 2021), LIPIcs vol. 185, Article 27, pp. 27:1--27:20,
Schloss Dagstuhl, doi `10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2021.27`.

The paper introduces the Rich 2-to-1 Games Conjecture and proves it
equivalent to the Unique Games Conjecture.  Nothing here reproves it and
nothing here strengthens it; the node exists so that the equivalence is a
visible, attributed edge of the graph rather than an unmarked assumption
inside a downstream argument.
