Let `M = prod_omega M_(n_i)(k) / d_omega` be a rank ultraproduct over **any** field `k`. Call `g`
in `M^x` **unipotent** when it has representatives `g_i` with `g_i - 1` nilpotent for ω-almost
every `i`, with no bound on the nilpotency index.

1. **Exact identity.** If `x - 1` is nilpotent and `m` is invertible in `k`, then
   `rk(x^m - 1) = rk(x - 1)`. So a unipotent `g` with `g^m = 1` in `M` equals `1`. For any group,
   a homomorphism with unipotent values kills the normal subgroup generated by elements of finite
   order invertible in `k`.
2. **EL_3.** Let `R` be a unital ring with `p 1 = 0` for a prime `p`, and
   `sigma : EL_3(R) -> M^x` a homomorphism. Suppose one element `h` has unipotent image, where
   `h` is:
   * `x_12(1)`, if `char k != p`;
   * the 3-cycle permutation matrix, if `char k = p = 2`;
   * `diag(-1,-1,1)`, if `char k = p` is odd.

   Then `sigma` is trivial. In particular every unipotent-valued rank model of `EL_3(R)` is
   trivial, for every such `R` and every field `k`. That covers ultraproducts of unitriangular
   groups for flags of any length.
3. **Filter.** In a nontrivial characteristic-`p` rank model of `EL_3(R)`, the image of
   `diag(-1,-1,1)` (`p` odd) or of the 3-cycle (`p = 2`) is not unipotent.

No simplicity of `R`, direct-finiteness failure, property (T) or compression is used.
