For each of the finitely many dimensions `d<=D`, maximize the positive part
of the transfer violation over tuples of relator defect at most `delta`.
If those maxima did not tend to zero, a subsequence in one fixed compact
unitary tuple space would converge to a defect-zero tuple with a positive
violation.  That tuple is an exact representation, contradicting the
appropriate established exact arithmetic transfer theorem.
