Let `G` be countably infinite with `h^Rok_sup(G) = 0`. Then `0 < ∞`, and the uniform bases on 2 and 3 points
satisfy `0 < log 2 < log 3 < ∞`. The prerequisite gives `2^G ≅ 3^G`, so `log 3 - log 2` lies in `D(G) \ {0}`.

The prerequisite gives more: all Bernoulli shifts of finite positive base entropy over `G` are isomorphic.
That covers the finite-entropy part of the strong form recorded on the target.
