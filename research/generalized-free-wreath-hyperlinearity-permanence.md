Let an ergodic compact-quantum-group action define the generalized free wreath
product `G wreath_{*,beta,F} H`.  This product is of Kac type exactly when
`G` and `H` are Kac.  In the Kac case, if the amalgamating dual quantum
subgroup `F` is coamenable, then the discrete dual of the generalized free
wreath product is hyperlinear if and only if the discrete duals of both `G`
and `H` are hyperlinear.
