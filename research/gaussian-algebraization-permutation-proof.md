Suppose first that the Gaussian action `sigma_pi` is a factor of a Haar
algebraic action `Lambda curvearrowright K`.  Pullback embeds its Koopman
representation into the Koopman representation on `L^2(K)`.  The first
Gaussian chaos is an invariant real subspace isomorphic to `pi`.  Fourier
characters identify the ambient Koopman representation with the permutation
representation on the countable dual group `K-hat`; after realification this
proves condition 2.

Conversely, suppose `pi` is an invariant real subrepresentation of
`ell^2(I,R)` for a countable `Lambda`-set `I`.  Functoriality of the Gaussian
construction says that the Gaussian algebra generated by the subspace `pi`
is an invariant probability subalgebra of the Gaussian action for
`ell^2(I,R)`.  Hence `sigma_pi` is a factor of the latter action.  But the
Gaussian action for a permutation representation is simply the coordinate
permutation action on `(R^I,gamma^I)`.  Choose any measure-space isomorphism
from the nonatomic standard Gaussian line to the circle with Haar measure
and apply it in every coordinate.  This conjugates the permutation action to
the action on

```text
K = T^I
```

by coordinate permutations.  The latter are continuous group automorphisms,
`K` is compact second-countable, and its product measure is Haar.  This proves
condition 1.

For `Lambda=Z`, every orbit in a countable permutation representation is
finite cyclic or a copy of `Z`; its complex spectral type is consequently a
sum of root-of-unity atoms and circle Haar measure.  Conversely, the spectral
theorem with multiplicity embeds every separable representation dominated by
that measure into a countable sum of finite-cycle and bilateral-shift
representations.  Taking infinitely many copies of each orbit type absorbs
all countable multiplicities, proving the final equivalence.
