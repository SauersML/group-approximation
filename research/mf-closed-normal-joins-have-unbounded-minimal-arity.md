For every `m>=2` there is a three-torsion-generated LEF Kazhdan group
`G_m` and `m` infinite simple locally finite normal subgroups `K_i`
such that every quotient by a proper subproduct is LEF, but the
quotient by their full product is just non-MF. A further simple
locally finite normal factor `L` satisfies
`cl_MF(K_1...K_m)=K_1...K_m L`, strictly, while every proper
subproduct is MF-closed. The inputs and `L` form an internal direct
product. Both the source and full quotient have no nontrivial finite
quotients and have torsion generators of orders `7,2,2`.
