**ESTABLISHED FINITE-FRAME FIREWALL.**  Let `A` be generated by a fixed
unitary tuple `u=(u_s)_(s in S)`, and let `(X,B)` be an `m`-dimensional
matrix-range test tuple.  Fix `q`, a finite set `Omega` of nonidentity
reduced words in `q+1` letters, and a finite moment order.  For every
sufficiently large `k` there are finite-dimensional unitaries

```text
T,H_1,...,H_q,             T_i=H_i T H_i^*,
T_0=T,                                                        (FLF1)
```

such that:

1. every `T_i` has order exactly `k` and uniform `k`-point spectrum;
2. `tr(w(T_0,...,T_q))=0` for every `w in Omega` (and hence the requested
   finite lamp table is exactly canonical);
3. after tensoring by `C^m`, all `T_i` and `H_i` act on the first factor,
   while

   ```text
   X~_s=I tensor X_s,          B~_s=I tensor B_s;              (FLF2)
   ```

4. the support function and gap are unchanged:

   ```text
   h(B~)=h_m(B),               Delta(B~;X~)=Delta_m(B;X).      (FLF3)
   ```

Taking `k` and `Omega` to infinity gives every fixed joint moment of the
orthonormal unitary lamp frame in
`full-hnn-regularity-is-a-quasiregular-unitary-orbit`, while an arbitrary
Choi separator survives on the common multiplicity factor.  This is
strictly stronger than the dimension-overflow firewall: even the complete
matrix-range support functional is blind to a fixed lamp frame, not merely
its scalar Gram matrix.

Therefore no argument using only finitely many instances of `(HQR2)` or
finitely many reduced words in the conjugates `a t a^(-1)` can prove
`sl3-hnn-central-dual-gap-vanishes`.  Such words do contain stable and base
letters syntactically, but after Britton reduction their data are only the
free lamp law.

This does not refute the central-dual claim.  The matrices `H_i` are free
conjugators on the frame factor.  They do not form one representation of
`SL_3(Z[1/p])`, do not transport the lattice tuple on the multiplicity
factor, and do not satisfy its arithmetic multiplication table.  The exact
surviving datum is thus narrower again: a proof must use a mixed canonical
word in which the **same arithmetic actor** both permutes the lamp frame and
transports the lattice coordinates.  Finite lamp moments, even all their
noncommutative cross-Grams, are insufficient.
