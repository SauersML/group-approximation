**ESTABLISHED RELATIVE-T AND LOCAL-REFLEXIVITY NO-GO; LLP/MF
HYPOTHESES NOT DECIDED.**

Let

~~~text
G=SL_3(Z),                 X=G
~~~

with G acting on X by left translation. Form the shared-center extraspecial
group E(X) and its semidirect product

~~~text
Pi_reg=E(G) semidirect G,                                (PTR1)
~~~

as in SCW1--SCW2. Write e for the identity site. Then Pi_reg is generated by
a finite generating set S of G together with A_e and B_e, and

~~~text
J=[A_e,B_e]
~~~

is a nontrivial central involution.

This example has all of the following properties.

1. Every exact finite-dimensional unitary representation of Pi_reg kills J:

~~~text
rho:Pi_reg -> U(d)             implies             rho(J)=I_d.   (PTR2)
~~~

Indeed, on the J=-1 space the restriction to every m-site extraspecial
subgroup is a multiple of its 2^m-dimensional spin representation. Its
dimension is divisible by 2^m for every m and hence is zero.

2. The pair (Pi_reg,G) has relative property (T). This is immediate from
property (T) of G: a Pi_reg-almost-invariant vector is in particular
G-almost invariant, so a Kazhdan pair for G produces a nonzero G-fixed
vector.

3. Pi_reg is LEF. More precisely, there are finite quotients

~~~text
q_n:G -> Q_n
~~~

which are injective on successively larger word balls, finite groups

~~~text
P_n=E(Q_n) semidirect Q_n,                               (PTR3)
~~~

and marked maps on S union {A_e,B_e},

~~~text
s |-> q_n(s),       A_e |-> A_1,       B_e |-> B_1,      (PTR4)
~~~

such that for every fixed marked word v,

~~~text
v=1 in Pi_reg     iff     v(P_n)=1 for all sufficiently large n. (PTR5)
~~~

The forward implication includes all relations, not merely a selected
finite presentation. The reverse implication gives local injectivity.

4. Let lambda_n be the left regular representation of P_n. Then the marked
unitaries lambda_n(PTR4) are asymptotically faithful matrix models of
Pi_reg. Every fixed relation has eventually zero defect even in operator
norm, while every fixed nonidentity word has eventually zero normalized
trace. In particular,

~~~text
tr(lambda_n(J))=0,
||lambda_n(J)-I||_2^2=2                                  (PTR6)
~~~

for every n. Thus the exact finite-dimensional radical in PTR2 is
discontinuous at zero even in the presence of the relative property-(T)
pair.

5. The reduced group algebra C_r^*(Pi_reg) is exact and hence locally
reflexive. The base E(G) is locally finite and amenable; G is an exact
linear group; and reduced crossed products by exact groups preserve
exactness. Consequently local reflexivity does not turn PTR2 into an
asymptotic Hilbert--Schmidt collapse.

The construction pinpoints the quantifier failure. An exact representation
of the whole group sees every finite site window at once, forcing
divisibility by every 2^m. The model P_n only has |Q_n| sites. For each
fixed window it is already an exact spin model, and the window can grow
without ever exceeding the available finite quotient. Relative property
(T) controls almost-invariant vectors for the actor; it supplies no
dimension-versus-window estimate for the spin sector.

This rules out any promotion theorem based only on exact
finite-dimensional invisibility, relative property (T), local reflexivity,
or exact lifting of each fixed finite relation/operator-system window.
It does **not** prove that C_r^*(Pi_reg) or C^*(Pi_reg) has LLP or is MF,
and therefore does not refute a theorem with a genuine LLP or MF
hypothesis. Nor is Pi_reg asserted to be finitely presented. Those are the
remaining possible sources of uniformity. No nonhyperlinear group is
constructed here.
