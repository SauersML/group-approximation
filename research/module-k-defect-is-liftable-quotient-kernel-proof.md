The genuine exact sequence 0→J→C→D→0 gives ker p_*=im i_*.
Since q_*=q̄_*p_*, restriction of p_* to ker q_* has precisely
im i_* as kernel and im p_*∩ker q̄_* as image. The first
isomorphism theorem proves the displayed identification in both
degrees. Exactness at K_j(D) gives im p_*=ker ∂.
