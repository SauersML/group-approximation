title: Full square-zero sections over nontorsion continuous-trace fields have spectral crowding at every level
---

Let `A` be a continuous-trace C-star algebra over a connected compact
Hausdorff space and assume its Dixmier--Douady class is not torsion.  If
`x in A` is full and square-zero, and

```text
m=min_t ||x_t||,
```

then, for every `lambda in (0,m)`,

```text
inf_t dist(lambda,spectrum(|x_t|))=0.
```

Thus no fixed nonzero spectral threshold below the top singular value can be
used to extract a continuous finite-rank source projection from a hypothetical
full nilpotent.
