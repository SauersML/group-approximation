Let a countable discrete amenable group `Gamma` act freely and minimally on a
compact Hausdorff space `X` with the uniform Rokhlin property (URP) and Cuntz
comparison of open sets (COS). Put `A = C(X) ⋊ Gamma`. Then `sr(A) = 1` and

```text
U(A)/U_0(A) -> K_1(A)
```

is an isomorphism. Every free minimal `Z^d`-action has (URP) and (COS), so
this holds for every `C(X) ⋊ Z^d` with a free minimal action, with no
comparison hypothesis.

In particular no such crossed product is a K1-witness for Problem XVIII.
