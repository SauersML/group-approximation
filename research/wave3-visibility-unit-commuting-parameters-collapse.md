Let R be a commutative F_7-algebra, x,y units, and z arbitrary. Set
A(x)=I+x(E_03+E_12), B(y)=I+y(E_10-E_23), C(z)=I+zE_31.
Conjugation by diag(x^-1,(xy)^-1,y^-1,1) sends this triple to
A(1),B(1),C(xyz). Its entries therefore lie in F_7[xyz].

If R is finite, the monogenic algebra S=F_7[xyz] has K_2(S)=0.
Ambient multivariable nilpotent parameters do not change this conclusion.
No assertion is made for nonunit first or second parameters or
noncommuting parameters, nor about unstable symplectic multipliers.
