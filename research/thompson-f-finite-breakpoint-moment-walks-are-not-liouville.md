**ESTABLISHED.** For `h ∈ F` let `Br(h) ⊂ D` be its set of breakpoints in `(0,1)` (`D` = dyadic rationals in
`(0,1)`). Let `µ` be a probability measure on `F` such that

1. the semigroup generated by `supp µ` is `F`, and
2. `E_µ|Br(h)| = Σ_h µ(h)|Br(h)| < ∞`.

Then `(F, µ)` is not Liouville: there is a nonconstant bounded `µ`-harmonic function `u(g) = Σ_h µ(h) u(gh)`.

**Corollary (finite first moment).** Condition 2 holds whenever `µ` has finite first moment for the word metric
of `{x0, x1}`, since `Br(gh) ⊂ Br(h) ∪ h^{-1}Br(g)` and `|Br(x0^{±1})| = 2`, `|Br(x1^{±1})| = 3` give `|Br(g)| ≤ 3|g|`. So every strictly non-degenerate
`µ` on `F` with finite first moment is non-Liouville. This is the extension that Kaimanovich (arXiv:1602.02971v2,
§7.A) expected to be "quite feasible", and it goes further. For `n ≥ 1` let `k_n` have slopes `1/2, 1, 2` on
`[0, 2^{-n}]`, `[2^{-n}, 1 − 2^{-n-1}]`, `[1 − 2^{-n-1}, 1]`. These are infinitely many distinct elements with exactly
2 breakpoints, so their word lengths are unbounded. Mixing a finitely supported strictly non-degenerate measure
with weights `w_n` on `k_n` that satisfy `Σ w_n < ∞` and `Σ w_n |k_n| = ∞` gives a measure with infinite first
moment that satisfies conditions 1–2.

**Sharper form.** Condition 2 can be weakened to "`Σ_w G(z,w) m(w) < ∞` for one dyadic `z`", where `G` is the
Green function of the chain `x ↦ h^{-1}(x)` and `m(w) = µ{h : w ∈ Br(h^{-1})}`.

**Consequence for `thompson-f-is-amenable`.** By Kaimanovich–Vershik and Rosenblatt, `F` is amenable iff some
symmetric `µ` with `supp µ = F` is Liouville. Any such witness must have `E_µ|Br(h)| = ∞`, and in fact an
infinite Green-weighted breakpoint count at every dyadic point. The invariant is the breakpoint-cocycle lamp
configuration `c(g_n) ∈ Z^D`. Every finite-moment member dies at the step where the lamp at `1/2` stabilizes,
and a break-fixing element `k` of the stabilizer of `1/2` turns the law of the stabilized lamp into a
nonconstant harmonic function.
