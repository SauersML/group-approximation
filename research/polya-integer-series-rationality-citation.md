**Source.** G. Pólya, *Über gewisse notwendige Determinantenkriterien für die
Fortsetzbarkeit einer Potenzreihe*, Math. Ann. 99 (1928), 687--706. It extends
É. Borel's theorem, in which the set is a disc.

**Verification status.** The capacity form is confirmed from secondary
accounts of the Borel--Pólya--Dwork--Bertrandias criterion: "Pólya replaced the
disc by a neighbourhood of a compact set with transfinite diameter" below one,
as summarized in the literature on Cantor's rationality theorem. The abstract of
A. Chambert-Loir and C. Noûs, *Potentiel et rationalité*, arXiv:2305.17210,
extends "the rationality theorem of Cantor, itself an extension of theorems of
Borel, Pólya, Dwork, Bertrandias and Robinson". The 1928 paper was not
re-read here.

**Downstream use.** Only the holomorphic, single-valued case with `K` a real
segment is used, in `torsion-free-small-spread-integer-atomic-proof`.
