Direct in the claim body: the amalgam shares `L` and attaches `C` as a
commuting product (no two-model conjugacy); the HNN's stable letter must
conjugate `rho(l,1)` to `rho(l,phi(l))`, two regular-trace models of `L`,
whose approximate unitary equivalence is the free-entropy question; finite
presentability is available only for the HNN form.
