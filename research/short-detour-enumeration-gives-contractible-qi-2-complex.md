title: A group with a short-detour enumeration is quasi-isometric to a contractible proper geodesic 2-complex
---

Let G be generated by a finite symmetric set S, with Cayley graph Γ.
A **short-detour enumeration** with constant L is an enumeration
g_0, g_1, g_2, … of G with the following properties, where
A_k = {g_0, …, g_k} and Γ[A_k] is the induced subgraph:

- each g_{k+1} has at least one Γ-neighbour in A_k;
- any two Γ-neighbours of g_{k+1} in A_k are joined by a path of length ≤ L
  in Γ[A_k].

**Theorem.** If G has a short-detour enumeration, then G is quasi-isometric
to a contractible, locally finite, proper geodesic 2-complex X. Its
1-skeleton is Γ, and its 2-cells are polygons of perimeter ≤ L + 2.

**Consequence.** If some group that is not of type F_3 has a short-detour
enumeration, the geodesic reading of Zaremsky Problem 1.3
(`zaremsky-1-03-qi-to-connected-space-implies-type-fn`) has answer no for
n = 3, and X is (n − 1)-connected for every n. Conversely, a yes answer
would force every group with a short-detour enumeration to be of type F_∞.

**Examples.**
- Breadth-first order is a short-detour enumeration for every group that is
  almost convex in Cannon's sense. Two neighbours of a vertex at radius r + 1
  push down to points of the sphere S(r) at distance ≤ 4, and almost
  convexity joins these by a path of bounded length inside B(r).
- A group with a short-detour enumeration is finitely presented, by
  `fg-group-qi-to-simply-connected-geodesic-space-is-fp`.
