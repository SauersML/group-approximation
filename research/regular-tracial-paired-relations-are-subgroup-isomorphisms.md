title: Exact regular-trace paired equations encode only a subgroup isomorphism
---

Under the hypotheses of
`regular-tracial-paired-relations-force-kernel-balance`, let

```text
H_i=p_i(K).
```

Then the fixed relation pairs generating `K` are the graph of an isomorphism

```text
phi:H_1 ~= H_2,
phi(a_s)=b_s.                                           (RPI1)
```

In particular a system of exact two-term relations between finite regular
charts cannot express a proper one-sided compression, a many-to-one finite
cover, or any index imbalance.  Its entire exact semantics is a relabeling of
one finite subgroup by an isomorphic copy.

This is why a Leavitt/atlas obstruction cannot emerge from accumulating more
paired covariance equations alone: as long as each new constraint still has
the form `A T = T B` and remains exact in the regular-trace model, it merely
enlarges the graph of a finite subgroup isomorphism.  The nonhyperlinear
information has to enter through a genuinely non-group-linear operation such
as multiplication coupling, a multi-term pencil, or finite-coordinate
spectral/rank selection.
