Fix one predicate context with commuting involutions

```text
Z=(Z_1,...,Z_k),               Z'=(Z'_1,...,Z'_k),      (CAT1)
```

where `k` is fixed.  Let `J,K,b` be the current S3 endpoint chart and let
`T_1,T_2` be a fresh two-bit tag.  Assume every member of each displayed
tuple is an involution, the members of each tuple commute, the endpoint bits
commute with `Z`, and the tag bits commute with `Z'`.

Adjoin a unitary bridge `u` satisfying

```text
uZ_i=Z'_i u                    (1<=i<=k),
uK=T_1u,
ub=T_2u.                                             (CAT2)
```

For a Boolean context atom `alpha`, write `P_alpha(Z)` and `P_alpha(Z')` for
the corresponding joint spectral projections.  Let

```text
P_exit=(I-K)(I-b)/4,          P_live=I-P_exit,
E=(I-T_1)(I-T_2)/4,          S=I-E.                   (CAT3)
```

Then exactly

```text
u P_alpha(Z)P_exit u^*=P_alpha(Z')E,
u P_alpha(Z)P_live u^*=P_alpha(Z')S.                  (CAT4)
```

Now let `C_n` be the product of all earlier survivor cuts, assume the bridge
commutes with every earlier tag bit, and put

```text
X_n=C_nE,                 C_(n+1)=C_nS.               (CAT5)
```

The two images in `(CAT4)`, after compression by `C_n`, lie respectively in
the first-hit exit `X_n` and in the child predicate carrier
`C_(n+1)P_alpha(Z')`.  The latter projection reduces the complete child
context tuple `Z'`.  No assertion is made that it reduces variables in other
BCS contexts; the groupifier runs a separate ladder for every fixed context
occurrence and amalgamates only the original shared logical involutions.

The normalized-HS estimate is fixed-degree.  After exactifying the two finite
elementary-abelian tables, let

```text
e_Z=sum_i ||uZ_i-Z'_iu||_2,
e_D=||uK-T_1u||_2+||ub-T_2u||_2,
e_C=sum_(earlier tags T)||[u,T]||_2.                  (CAT6)
```

For either choice `star in {exit,live}`,

```text
||(I-C_n P_alpha(Z')P'_star)u C_nP_alpha(Z)P_star||_2
 <= e_Z/2+e_D/2+e_C/2,                                (CAT7)
```

up to twice the already recorded source-prefix leakage, where
`P'_exit=E` and `P'_live=S`.  All constants depend only on the fixed context
arity.  When the level-`n` words are generated by the product clock, the
right side is a computable polynomial in `n` times the square root of the
named relator energy.
