Notation of `kms-hc1-a2-pro-p-completion-is-iwahori-criterion` and
`kms-hc1-iwahori-h2-serre-plus-kassel-all-degrees`. Fix a prime `p >= 5`.

- `Gamma = Gamma^A2_p = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b], [b,c,b], [b,c,c], [a,c,a], [a,c,c]>`,
  with pro-p completion `Gamma^`.
- `I_1` is the pro-p Iwahori subgroup of `SL_3(F_p[[t]])`.
- `L_p = n^+ + t sl_3(F_p[t])`, graded by height: `E_ij t^k` has height `(j-i)+3k`.
- The Kassel cocycle in degree `n = 3mp` (`m >= 1`) is
  `omega(X t^a, Y t^b) = a tr(XY)` if `a+b = mp`, and `0` otherwise.

Define the two **initial-form models** of `Gamma^`. Each is the graded object
cut out by the initial forms of the nine defining relators alone:

```text
Lambda_p   = FreeRes_{F_p}(a,b,c) / (a^[p], b^[p], c^[p], six Serre words)       (Zassenhaus / restricted model)
Lambda^P_p = FreeLie_{F_p[pi]}(a,b,c) / (pi a, pi b, pi c, six Serre words)      (lower p-central model)
```

Here `a, b, c` have degree 1, the Serre words are `[[a,b],a]` etc. (degree 3),
`a^[p]` has degree `p`, and `pi` has degree 1. By Quillen's theorem,
`u(Lambda_p) = F_p<X_a,X_b,X_c>/(X_i^p, Serre)` is the initial-form model of the
completed group algebra `F_p[[Gamma^]]`.

**(E) Restricted Kassel extension.** For every `m >= 1`, let
`N = L_p/(height > n) + F_p z` with bracket `[x,y]_N = [x,y]_L + omega(x,y) z`
and `z` central of degree `n`. Then `N` carries a unique restricted structure
with `(E_ij t^k)^[p] = 0` and `(h t^k)^[p] = h^p t^{pk}`, where `h` is diagonal
and `z^[p] = 0`. In `N`, `a = E_12`, `b = E_23` and `c = t E_31` satisfy
`a^[p] = b^[p] = c^[p] = 0` and the six Serre relations. As a restricted Lie
algebra, `N` is generated by `a, b, c`.

**(M) Excess of the models.** Hence there are graded surjections
`Lambda_p ->> N` and `Lambda^P_p ->> N` (with `pi = 0`). So

```text
dim (Lambda_p)_{3mp} >= dim (L_p)_{3mp} + 1   and   dim (Lambda^P_p)_{3mp} >= dim (L_p)_{3mp} + 1
```

for every `m >= 1`. For `gr_D` and `gr_P` of `I_1`, the degree-`3mp` piece has
dimension `dim (L_p)_{3mp}`, since both filtrations of `I_1` are the height
filtration.

**(O) Obstruction.** Suppose `Gamma^ = I_1`, which is the criterion of
`kms-hc1-a2-pro-p-completion-is-iwahori-criterion`. Then for every `m >= 1`
there is an element `r` of the closed normal subgroup `R = ker(F^ -> Gamma^)`
whose initial form has degree `3mp` and lies outside the ideal generated by
the initial forms of the nine defining relators. This holds for the Zassenhaus
filtration `D` and also for the lower p-central filtration `P`. In
other words, the relator set would be non-inert in every Kassel degree: the
graded ideal of all initial forms of `R` would be strictly larger than the
ideal generated by the nine initial forms. So no proof of Open 2 can use the
relators only through their associated graded. This rules out, at the exact
step `3mp`:

- Lie or restricted-Lie cohomology of the graded models;
- Gröbner or Anick resolutions of `F_p<X>/(X_i^p, Serre)`;
- Golod–Shafarevich or Hilbert-series counts in `gr F_p[[Gamma^]]`;
- graded-algebra splittings over the three vertex Heisenberg algebras.

The Kassel direction can die only through the non-homogeneous tails of the
relators. Those tails are the higher-degree terms of `a^p - 1` and
`[a,b,a] - 1` in `F_p<<X>>`, i.e. the terms of `(1+X)^p` and of the group
commutators beyond their initial Lie forms.

**(S) The graded form of the central-extension step is false.** Take the
statement "every central `F_p`-extension of `I_1` of Kassel degree `3mp` that
splits compatibly over the three vertex Heisenberg subgroups is trivial".
Its graded analogue fails. `N -> L_p/(height > n)` is a nonsplit restricted
central extension. Its restriction to each vertex subalgebra
`<a,b>, <b,c>, <c,a>` (span of `x, y, [x,y]`, heights `<= 2 < n`) splits,
compatibly on the pairwise intersections `F_p b`, `F_p c`, `F_p a`. So a proof
of the group statement must use group-level input with no graded shadow. The
vanishing of relative `K_2(F_p[t]/t^n, (t))` is such an input. The 3-spherical
Curtis–Tits amalgam is also group-level, but `A~2` is not 3-spherical.

Calibration: `restricted_kassel.py` checks the Jacobi identity, Jacobson's
criterion, the relator initial forms and the non-coboundary property for
`(p,m) = (5,1), (7,1), (5,2), (11,1), (7,2)`, all PASS. It also runs
`(3,1)`, where the script passes too; `p = 3` is outside the claim because
there the Serre words have the same degree as `a^[p]`.
