If every Thompson-like group `T(G_*)` of a cloning system on finite groups
embeds in `V`, then Problem 2.16 is answered: Q2 (embed in `V`) is yes, and Q1
(coCF) follows because `V` is coCF and coCF passes to finitely generated
subgroups. This is the strongest positive answer and it is consistent with
Lehnert's conjecture. Open: `cloning-system-groups-on-finite-groups-embed-in-v`.
