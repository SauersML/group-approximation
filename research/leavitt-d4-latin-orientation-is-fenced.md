For the displayed orientation of the degree-four Latin carrier triangle, if
three copy projections of `F` are trivial and the fourth is a nontrivial
element `h`, then the coefficient ratios generate a metacyclic packet with an
element `w` of exact order sixteen and `h=w^8`.

Those relations force every bridge syllable in the nineteen-syllable normal
form of `F` to be nontrivial and allow at most one of its four underlying
units to be the identity.  The full free-product word therefore remains
reduced of length greater than one.  Hence this orientation never has
`F=h_j`, for arbitrary coefficient units.
