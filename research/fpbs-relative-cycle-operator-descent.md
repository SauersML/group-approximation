For a free p.m.p. factor pi:Y->X of an infinite countable group, let H
be a bounded-degree generating base graphing and Phi a generating
subgraphing of pi*H. The conditional expectation of
P_Z(pi*H)-P_Z(Phi) is a base-measurable equivariant positive contraction
K with 0<=K<=P_Z(H) and Tr_X(K)=c(H)-c(Phi). The trace is half the
integrated incident-edge diagonal. No connectivity-preserving deletion
or base realization of a random edge law is asserted.

Section 1 supplies the proof and explains why finite-label source
graphings have a conditional-support ambient H. This is a written
deduction over the imported measured L2 identity, not an independently
verified proof or a claim of novelty. It is used to test the relative
exchange attempt recorded in the open reverse Bernoulli comparison.
