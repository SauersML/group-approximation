Citation import, not a reproof.

- J. Meier, *Geometric invariants for Artin groups*, Proc. London Math. Soc. (3)
  74 (1997) 151–173. Parts 1 and 2 of the target are quoted as Proposition 2.15
  of M. Escartín-Ferrer, *On the Σ^1 and Σ^2-invariants of Artin groups*,
  arXiv:2501.08692v3 (14 July 2025), p. 6, attributed to "[18] Meier, 1995".
- Symmetry: Escartín-Ferrer, Lemma 2.8, citing Blasco-García–Cogolludo-Agustín–
  Martínez-Pérez, *On the Sigma-invariants of even Artin groups of FC-type*, J. Pure
  Appl. Algebra 226 (7) (2022), Proposition 3.1.
- Finite generation of kernels: Escartín-Ferrer, Theorem 2.7 (Bieri–Renz, Comment.
  Math. Helv. 63 (1988)): for `G` of type `F_n` and `G' <= N <= G`, `N` is of type
  `FP_n` iff every `[χ]` with `χ(N) = 0` lies in `Σ^n(G, Z)`. Artin groups are
  finitely presented and `Σ^1(G) = Σ^1(G, Z)`.

Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv PDF of 2501.08692v3, pages 2 and 4–6. Meier's
original paper was not reread; the statement is as Escartín-Ferrer quotes it.
