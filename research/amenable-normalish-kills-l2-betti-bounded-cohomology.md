**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `G` be a discrete group containing an amenable normalish subgroup
(`no-amenable-normalish-subgroup-implies-cstar-simple` has the definition). Then:
1. `β_n^{(2)}(G) = 0` for every `n ≥ 0`. BKKO state this as a special case of Bader–Furman–Sauer, Theorem 1.3.
2. `H_b^n(G,E) = 0` for every `n ≥ 0` and every coefficient `G`-module `E` that is mixing, i.e. every nonzero vector has a
   finite stabilizer. Examples are `ℓ^p(G)` for `1 ≤ p < ∞`.
