Let `theta:B->M` be a unital star homomorphism into a von Neumann algebra,
and let `phi,psi:Z->B` be unital embeddings.  The compositions
`theta phi,theta psi` are unital.  Since `Z` is simple, they are injective.
Universal von Neumann uniqueness supplies unitaries in `M` which conjugate
one composition to the other in norm.  No faithfulness or normality
assumption on `theta` is needed.
