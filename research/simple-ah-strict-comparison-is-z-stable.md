Let `A` be a unital simple AH algebra, that is, a unital simple inductive
limit of finite direct sums of corners `p M_n(C(X)) p` over compact metrizable
spaces. If `A` has strict comparison of positive elements, then
`A ~= A tensor Z`.

So Problem XVIII has a positive answer on the whole AH class. Every algebra
of the form `Gamma(X, End E)` over a finite CW complex is such a corner, since
`E` embeds in a trivial bundle.
