Let `PB_3''` be the second derived subgroup of the pure braid group `PB_3`. The following hold.

- The abelian group `Q_2 = PB_3'' / [PB_3, PB_3'']` contains a free abelian subgroup of countably infinite rank.
- There are `2^aleph_0` normal subgroups `M` of `B_3` with `[PB_3, PB_3''] ≤ M ≤ PB_3''`.
- There is an infinite strictly ascending chain `M_1 < M_2 < …` of normal subgroups of `B_3` inside `PB_3''`.

All of these subgroups lie in `[PB_3, PB_3]`.
