**Established by citation.** Nathanial P. Brown and Alin Ciuperca,
[*Isomorphism of Hilbert modules over stably finite C-star
algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.0958), prove that if `A` is stably
finite, then a countably generated Hilbert `A`-module gives a compact element
of `Cu(A)` if and only if it is algebraically finitely generated and
projective. Such modules are exactly the ranges of projections over
`A tensor K`, giving the formulation in the claim.
