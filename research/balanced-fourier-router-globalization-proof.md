For completeness, compute `(BFR2)` inside `M tensor Q`.  Orthogonality of
`p_+,p_-` proves involutivity.  On the minus corner both private signs are
one; on the plus corner they are `Z_e,X_e`.  The Pauli and Hadamard identities
give `(BFR3)`.  Separate qubit factors let all finitely many routers coexist.

For the local algebra classification, centrality of `S` cuts every
representation into its two fibers.  The NAND projection equation on the
minus fiber gives `A=B=1`.  On the plus fiber, involutivity turns
`ABAB=-1` into `AB=-BA`; the universal complex star algebra of two
anticommuting self-adjoint involutions is `M_2(C)`.  This proves `(BFR4)` for
the algebra generated by `S,A,B`; adjoining the specified Fourier generator
does not remove either representation, since it is `1` on the scalar sector
and Hadamard on the Pauli sector.

Finally fix a global valuation.  The cited packet theorem supplies one exact
marked local context sector with those selector scalars whether its
assignment is allowed or forbidden.  Choose the corresponding summand
`(BFR5)` independently for every incidence.  Tensor products put nonscalar
router factors on independent qubits; direct-sum amplification equalizes all
remaining dimensions.  Since every amalgamated selector and phase is scalar
with the same value at its two endpoints, all identifications hold.  The
result is an exact marked finite representation even for a violating
valuation, proving failure of the decoder at zero defect.
