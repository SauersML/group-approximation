Increasing the Steinberg rank cannot turn `(BCL0)` into the isolated physical
authentication `(ACP6)`.  The exact product-ring representations in
`agent-free-compressor-named-row-retains-complement-gauge` exist in
`E_N(R_n)` for every `N>=3`; all additional high-rank commutator identities
hold exactly while the complement gauge survives.

Global higher-rank rigidity does not repair this local failure:

1. Property `(T)` of high-rank Steinberg groups controls almost invariant
   vectors in a fixed representation.  It does not turn an approximate
   homomorphism in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm into an exact one.
2. The de Chiffre--Glebsky--Lubotzky--Thom 2-Kazhdan stability mechanism is
   for the **unnormalized Frobenius norm**, whose submultiplicativity is used
   in the cohomological splitting argument.  Since

   ```text
   ||X||_F=sqrt(d)||X||_(2,d),                          (HRS1)
   ```

   normalized defect tending to zero gives no dimension-uniform entry into
   its Frobenius stability basin.  This obstruction remains at every rank.
3. Known 2-Kazhdan results for universal-lattice settings do not provide a
   normalized-HS theorem for the present noncommutative BCL coefficient ring,
   which contains the shared BCS algebra and a free ascending mapping-torus
   algebra.  Even a new proof of 2-Kazhdan for this ring would still face
   `(HRS1)`.
4. A global normalized-HS stability theorem for the full `G_BCL` would of
   course combine with
   `agent-free-compressor-full-corner-root-is-fd-invisible` to kill the
   payload root.  But establishing that theorem is already a branch-selecting
   result of the same strength as the desired robustification; it is not a
   consequence of raising rank.

Therefore the surviving route must prove a direct dimension-normalized
inequality using the named packet/HNN rank mismatch simultaneously.  Neither
local high-rank root rigidity nor published Frobenius stability supplies it.
