**REFUTED ROUTE.** Couple all three rank-five factorizations of `x_12(ee)`:

```text
[x_13(e),x_32(e)]=x_12(ee),
[x_14(e),x_42(e)]=x_12(ee),
[x_15(e),x_52(e)]=x_12(ee).
```

The star controls the two differences among the three paths, but the common
RHS remains a nuisance column. Its quotient-rank increment is two rather
than the required three, and an exact rectangular model retains arbitrary
unequal intermediate reservoirs. See
`atlas-first-common-rhs-return-star-has-rank-two`.
