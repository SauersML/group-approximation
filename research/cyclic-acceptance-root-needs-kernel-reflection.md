title: The cyclic acceptance return is one root coefficient, but positivity does not reflect its kernel
---

After clearing fixed denominators, the cyclic acceptance return is one
coefficient `C_acc` and therefore one Steinberg root.  The game gap supplies
a dimension-free lower bound on `||C_acc||_2` in terms of Gram mass.

It supplies no upper bound from the root-word defect.  A mixed
root-to-coefficient kernel-reflection theorem remains necessary and
sufficient; exact game and root packets on independent tensor factors give
a countermodel to every uncoupled argument.
