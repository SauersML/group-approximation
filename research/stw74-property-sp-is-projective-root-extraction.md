title: The LXXIV hinge is exactly extraction of a projection class from each finite root of the unit
---

Let `A` satisfy the hypothesis of Problem LXXIV.  The following are
equivalent:

1. `A` has property (SP).
2. Every nonzero `x` in `Cu(A)` dominates a nonzero class represented by a
   projection.
3. Whenever `0!=x` and `n x=[1_A]` for some finite `n`, there is a nonzero
   projection `p` in `A tensor K` with `[p]<=x`.

The finite-unit-root theorem makes the premise in (3) automatic for every
nonzero `x`.  Thus the precise module-theoretic hinge is not the invalid
cancellation of `n x=[1_A]` to `x=[1_A]`; it is the weaker task of extracting
one nonzero finite projective submodule from `x`.

In particular, a counterexample to Problem LXXIV has a nonzero class `x`
satisfying `n x=[1_A]` but dominating no nonzero projection class.
