For one hull, write the smallest reducing space explicitly as the closed word
span generated by `Ran(Q)`.  Since every amplified word is `w(A,A^*) tensor
I_K`, its span from `Ran(Q) tensor K` is the tensor product of the unamplified
word span with `K`, proving `(CRH1)`.

For the BTB recursion, assume `(CRH2)` at the parent.  Functional calculus and
finite packet correction commute with tensoring by `I_K`, so each selected
source and each group-word return is its unamplified projection tensored with
`I_K`.  Finite joins commute with this tensoring.  Apply `(CRH1)` to the
prescribed child packet family.  Induction proves `(CRH2)` at every vertex.
