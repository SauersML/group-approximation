The presentation of `D rtimes_alpha C_2` eliminates the second vertex copy
by writing it as `sAs`; the common-edge relations become `[s,C]=1`.  This is
the amalgam presentation `A*_C(C x C_2)`.  Its parity kernel is generated by
`A` and `sAs`, and amalgam normal form identifies that kernel with
`A*_C A`.

For each coset `x=aC`, set `s_x=asa^(-1)`.  This is independent of the
representative because `s` centralizes `C`.  Amalgam normal form says that a
reduced word in distinct consecutive `s_x` is nontrivial.  Thus their normal
closure is the free product of copies of `C_2` indexed by `A/C`, and
conjugation by `A` permutes the factors, proving the last isomorphism in
`(DSW1)`.

The two-block formula `(DSW2)` satisfies

```text
S tilde_sigma(d) S=tilde_sigma(alpha(d))
```

identically.  All other relations occur diagonally, proving the defect and
character assertions.  Restriction proves the converse and `(DSW4)`.
Equation `(DSW5)`, the conjugacy `sws=w^(-1)`, and amalgam normal form are
direct calculations.  Finally
`sl3-arithmetic-double-finite-representations-fold`, equivalently its
co-density proof applied to `s`, gives exact finite-dimensional collapse.
