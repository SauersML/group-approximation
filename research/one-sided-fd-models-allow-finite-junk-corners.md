title: Generated one-color finite-dimensional models can contain pointwise invisible finite corners
---

There is a nuclear-dimension-zero unital map `phi:A->B` satisfying the
properly-infinite-image hypothesis with the following property.  For every
finite `F subset A` and `epsilon>0`, there are a finite-dimensional
subalgebra `D subset B` and a unital homomorphism `theta:A->D` such that

```text
D=C*(theta(A)),
max_(a in F)||theta(a)-phi(a)||<epsilon,
```

but `D` has a nonzero finite minimal projection.  Thus neither one-sided
approximation nor trimming to the algebra generated by the one-color model
justifies splitting all of its minimal projections.
