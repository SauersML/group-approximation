Order the roots and digits and list the domain and target leaves
in the resulting lexicographic orders. A table gives a bijection
of these lists. For odd h its permutation sign is invariant under
refinement: replacing a matched leaf by h children inserts h-1
additional entries, an even number, so crossings with other leaf
blocks change by an even number. Within the new block, an odometer
power induces a power of an h-cycle, which is even when h is odd.
Thus the local section also changes no sign. All table equivalences
are generated by such refinements and the carry recursions.

To compose tables, refine their middle partitions to agree. The
resulting leaf permutation is the product of the two permutations,
so the sign defines a homomorphism epsilon:B->Z/2. Pure prefix
transpositions have epsilon=1 and chi=0. Therefore delta=epsilon-
(chi mod 2) is onto, and B_0=ker(delta) has index two in B.

The transported image of P=A5*A5 is perfect. Its image under any
homomorphism to Z/2 is trivial, so epsilon(lambda_g)=0 for all g.
Also chi(lambda_g)=0, because the original regular lambda_g is
an actual permutation of N and its near index is zero. The shift
s exchanges the two roots and has carry +1 on the second root;
hence epsilon(s)=1 and chi(s)=1. The generators of R_nu all have
delta=0. This proves R_nu<=B_0, and the pure prefix transposition
proves that R_nu cannot equal B.

Finite index gives B_0 finitely presented. Since s is in B_0 and
chi(s)=1, the restricted character is still onto. Its kernel is
ker(chi) intersect ker(epsilon), of index at most two in the
finitely generated ker(chi), and is therefore finitely generated.
The same fiber-product theorem and wreath-product construction
as in the required host claim now apply to B_0 and this same s.
This yields the smaller finitely presented germ ambient group.

Nothing in the parity calculation proves that the displayed
generators generate all of B_0. That is a separate generation
problem, and even its resolution would not present the whole
shell envelope E_nu.
