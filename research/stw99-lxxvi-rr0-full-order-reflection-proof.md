For a fixed cutoff c=(a-eta)_+, take the closure K of the nonzero
fiberwise equality set [c(x)]=[b(x)]. Brown--Ciuperca compactness and
finiteness of projection supports give a uniform eta spectral gap
for the chosen a|K. Compact-left reflection therefore supplies an
actual partial isometry embedding its support into Her(b|K).
Multiplying this partial isometry by c|K to the power one-half gives
the prescribed boundary data for exact strict positive-source
extension. Comparison is strict off K, so c<=_Cu b globally.

All cutoffs prove full order reflection on finite-dimensional compact
bases. Finite coordinate images of a compact metrizable space in
the Hilbert cube, Cu continuity, and an explicit limit lemma for
commuting order embeddings remove the dimension bound. Passing to
the ideal in the one-point compactification gives the locally
compact statement.
