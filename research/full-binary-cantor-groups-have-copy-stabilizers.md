title: Clopen partition stabilizers in a full Cantor overgroup of standard V are direct powers of the group

Let C be the binary Cantor space and V its standard prefix group.
Suppose V<=D<=Homeo(C) and D is full: every homeomorphism locally
agreeing with D belongs to D.

For every nonempty clopen U, the subgroup D(U) supported in U
is isomorphic to D by a piecewise-prefix identification C->U.
For an ordered partition of C into m nonempty clopen sets,
the subgroup preserving each part setwise is isomorphic to D^m.
In particular the setwise stabilizer of a proper nonempty U
is D(U) times D(C minus U), isomorphic to D times D.

Thus finite generation, or finite presentation, of D gives the
same property for these stabilizers. The setwise stabilizer of
a finite family of clopen sets is a finite extension of a direct
power of D and has the same finiteness implications.

The copy of V must act by the standard binary prefix action
(or an action globally conjugate to it). An arbitrary abstract
embedding of V is not enough for this assertion. Neither
rationality nor a contraction condition is assumed.
