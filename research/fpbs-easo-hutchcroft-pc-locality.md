**ESTABLISHED (imported).** If `(G_n)` are infinite vertex-transitive graphs
converging locally to a vertex-transitive graph `G` and `p_c(G_n) != 1` for
every `n`, then `p_c(G_n) -> p_c(G)`.

P. Easo and T. Hutchcroft, *The critical percolation probability is local*,
arXiv:2310.10983. See `fpbs-easo-hutchcroft-pc-locality-cite` for the verbatim
text. Status records an imported theorem, not independent verification.
