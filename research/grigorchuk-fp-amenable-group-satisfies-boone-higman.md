## Statement

Let `𝔊 = ⟨a,b,c,d⟩ ≤ Aut(T_2)` be the first Grigorchuk group and `φ` Lysenok's injective
endomorphism `a ↦ aca, b ↦ d, c ↦ b, d ↦ c`. Let

```text
H = ⟨𝔊, t | t^{-1} g t = φ(g)  (g ∈ 𝔊)⟩,
```

Grigorchuk's finitely presented amenable group that is not elementary amenable
(`grigorchuk-ascending-hnn-extension-is-finitely-presented`,
`grigorchuk-hnn-extension-is-fp-amenable-non-ea`). Then:

1. `H` embeds in the Röver–Nekrashevych group `V_2(S)` of an explicit self-similar group
   `S ≥ 𝔊` generated by a finite bounded automaton (12 generators, listed in the proof).
   The stable letter goes to the inverse of the element `s ∈ F` with
   `s(1u) = 11u, s(01v) = 10v, s(00w) = 0w`.
2. `S` is contracting, so `V_2(S)` embeds in a finitely presented simple group
   (BBMZ Corollary `cor:RN_BH`). Hence **`H` satisfies the Boone–Higman conjecture.**
3. `V_2(S)` is finitely presented and acts faithfully, highly transitively, with finitely
   generated stabilizers of finite sets, on the orbit of a rational point (BBMZ proof of
   Proposition `prop:contr_to_simple`). So `V_2(S)` has an action of type (A), and
   **`H` lies in the permutational class `B_A`**; so do all its finitely generated
   subgroups.

The key computation: `φ(g) = (ψ(g), g)` with `ψ(𝔊) = ⟨a, d⟩ ≅ D_8`, and the homomorphisms
`ψ∘φ^k` take values in `{1, a}` for every `k ≥ 2` (period 3 from `k = 2`). That makes the
new states finitary along the `0`-ray, so the extended automaton stays bounded.

## Scope

- A finitely presented amenable, non-elementary-amenable subgroup of a finitely presented
  simple group follows (`V_2(S)` is virtually simple and finitely presented, and the BBMZ
  host is simple). Nothing here is claimed about finiteness properties of `H` beyond `F_2`.
- Priority: the BBMZ survey arXiv:2306.16356v3 does not mention `H` or amenable inputs
