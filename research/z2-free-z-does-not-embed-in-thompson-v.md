The free product `Z^2 * Z = ⟨a, b, c | [a, b]⟩` is isomorphic to no subgroup of
Thompson's group `V`.

Imported from C. Bleak and O. Salazar-Díaz, *Free products in R. Thompson's group V*,
Theorem 1.2 (arXiv:0911.0979; Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 365 (2013), 5967–5997). See route
`z2-free-z-does-not-embed-in-thompson-v-citation`.

`closed-mcg-v-nonembedding-via-koberda`, via the survey arXiv:2306.16356v3, Theorem 4.6(1).
It is also used by `thompson-t-free-product-subgroups-have-no-disjoint-pair` and by
`thompson-v-acylindrically-hyperbolic-subgroups-lack-z2`.
