C*-simplicity: Bekka--Cowling--de la Harpe (1994), Publ. IHÉS 80, for lattices
in connected real semisimple Lie groups without compact factors and with trivial
center, `SL_n(ℤ)` included.  Non-acylindrical-hyperbolicity: SQ-universality of acylindrically hyperbolic groups (Dahmani--Guirardel--Osin; Osin, Trans. AMS 368 (2016), Theorem 1.4) against the Margulis normal subgroup theorem.  Imported with
credit, not reproved.
