**Problem XLIV of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**  Is
every stable, stably finite classifiable C\*-algebra of the form
`C_0(X) ⋊ G` for an action of an amenable group on a locally compact
Hausdorff space?  (Wu proved the purely infinite counterpart: stable
UCT Kirchberg algebras all arise this way.)

## Attempts

* Stability removes the unital `K`-obstructions of Problem XLV, so the
  question is a pure range problem: free minimal-type actions with
  prescribed `(K_*, T, ρ)`.  Wu's method rides pure infiniteness
  (absorbing constructions with no trace bookkeeping); the stably
  finite version must control invariant measures exactly, which is the
  same range-of-invariant-for-dynamical-models lemma missing at XLIII
  — these two problems fail or close together at the trace axis.
