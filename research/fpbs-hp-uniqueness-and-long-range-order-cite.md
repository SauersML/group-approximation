This is a citation import, not a reproof. The source is T. Hutchcroft and M.
Pan, arXiv:2409.12283v1, Theorem 1.4:

> Let G be a Cayley graph of a group Γ and H be an infinite subgroup of Γ. For
> each p ∈ [0, 1], there is a unique H-infinite cluster in G_p a.s. if and only
> if inf_{x,y∈H} τ_p(x, y) > 0.

The statement was read from the PDF text on MSI on 2026-09-12. This route
asserts only that the cited theorem says what the target records.
