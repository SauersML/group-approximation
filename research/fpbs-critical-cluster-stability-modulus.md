In the monotone uniform-label coupling on any locally finite nonamenable
Cayley graph, put c=p_c, B=|boundary_E C_c(o)|, and t=(q-c)/(1-c).
Then delta(q)=P(C_q(o)!=C_c(o))=E[1-(1-t)^B] tends to zero as q decreases
to c. Also theta(q)<=delta(q) and delta(q)<=t M+P(B>M) for every positive
integer M. Section 1 proves the statement using critical finiteness.
