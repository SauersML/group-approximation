Let `D = union_k D_k`, where `D_k` is generated by `x_12(s_w t_w)` with `|w| = k`, inside
`EL_3(L_(F_2)(1,2))`. So `D` is an increasing union of elementary abelian 2-groups. For every
`epsilon` in `[0,1]` there is a homomorphism `tau_epsilon : D -> M^x` into a rank ultraproduct
over `F_2` such that:
* every nontrivial element has displacement `epsilon/2`;
* any two equal-length tuples of pairwise orthogonal nonzero dyadic idempotents give
  restrictions that are conjugate generator by generator;
* `tau_epsilon` is injective for `epsilon > 0`.

The Boolean function `(epsilon/2)[a != 0]` on the algebra satisfies the scalar inequalities of
`leavitt-rank-models-have-uniform-root-displacement`.

Consequence: displacement ranks of root elements, their exact inequalities, and the dyadic root
restriction up to conjugacy cannot by themselves prove that rank models of `EL_3` over the
Leavitt algebra are trivial, or extract a Sylvester rank function. Proof: Section 2 of the
artifact (trivial summand plus regular modules of `D_k` along levels `k_i -> infinity`).
