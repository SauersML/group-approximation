For every infinite finitely presented group P with decidable word
problem, there is a bijection nu:P->N for which the following three
subgroups of E_nu=<V,hat(P)> are finitely presented. Use the exact
shell construction in `shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`,
and fix p=0^infinity and q=1*0^infinity. Singularities are relative
to V.

1. A_1={e: sing(e) subset {p}, e(p)=p}.
2. A_12={e: sing(e) subset {p}, e(p)=p, e(q)=q}.
3. A_2={e: sing(e) subset {p,q}, e(p)=p, e(q)=q}.

This is an OPEN construction problem. Finite generation of P and
E_nu, decidability of P's word problem, and a description of the
isotropy germ group do not prove these finite-presentation clauses.
The route `boone-higman-via-shell-germ-stabilizers` proves that the
three clauses would suffice for the full conjecture.

No uniform construction of nu from P is included in the assertion.
Computability of every successful nu now follows from
`shell-recursive-presentations-compute-enumerations`: the presentation
of A_1 computes nu by comparing conjugates of localized prefix flips.
Thus requiring nu to be computable would give an equivalent assertion.
Finite tables of its values remain insufficient, as
`shell-finite-prefix-cannot-certify-presentation` proves even for Z.

There is now a weaker sufficient target,
`decidable-inputs-have-fp-shell-envelopes`. The claim
`shell-finite-point-stabilizers-are-finitely-generated` supplies the
point-stabilizer condition for every enumeration, so proving E_nu
itself finitely presented suffices. The present three clauses imply
that envelope hypothesis, but their necessity has not been proved.

`virtually-cyclic-inputs-have-fp-shell-stabilizers` supplies all three
clauses for virtually cyclic inputs. Its normal-core proof cannot
handle other inputs: `shell-prefix-core-detects-finite-ray-enumerations`
shows the prefix germ has trivial normal core for every enumeration
of any non-virtually-cyclic input.

For a general candidate enumeration, the necessary test
`shell-fp-stabilizer-forces-fp-germ-group` requires its isotropy germ
group Q to be finitely presented. The exact description
`shell-germ-group-has-index-two-fiber-product` makes this equivalent
to finite presentation of R_nu x_Z R_nu, where R_nu is generated
by the regular input action on N and the cofinite shift as near
permutations. Neither this fiber-product condition nor its sufficiency
for the three global stabilizers has been proved universally.

The equivalent structural form of the germ presentation requirement
is `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate`. It specifies the subgroup,
shift direction, and exhaustion that a proposed construction must
supply, while retaining the separate finite-presentation clause for
R_nu. These requirements and the lift to global stabilizers remain open.

## Attempts

The shell construction gives an exact Cantor realization, but its
finite-generation and germ descriptions do not supply presentations
for these three global stabilizers. As detailed in the linked artifact,
finite presentation of A_1 would force finite presentation of its
isotropy germ quotient: the neighborhood kernel is simple and normally
generated by one nonidentity element inside A_1.

This provides a necessary test for a candidate enumeration. A finitely
presented germ quotient alone does not lift to a finite presentation
of A_1 or either two-point stabilizer; control of the neighborhood
kernel and the lifted relations is still missing.

2026-09-17 (swarm-0917-w5-pull2-boone-2, inverter): the explicit
perfect benchmark fails this hypothesis. By
`arithmetic-a5-near-group-is-not-finitely-presented`, the bijective-base
A_5*A_5 enumeration fails through its near group, and the three
stabilizers cannot all be finitely presented for it.
- Every generator of R_nu is an automorphism of the 60-regular tree
  with boundary X. This includes s, which is an edge flip followed by
  an odometer.
- The vertex stabilizers are locally virtually abelian, since they are
  unions of the level groups Z^(2h^D) ⋊ finite.
- R_nu contains Z^infinity, built from localized odometer pairs
  [lambda_x, s^118][lambda_y, s^118]^-1 transported down the tree.
- A finitely presented amalgam of locally virtually abelian groups
  lifts into a finite amalgam of virtually abelian groups, which has
  bounded abelian rank.
- So R_nu, Q and A_1 are not finitely presented, and R_nu has infinite
  index in the finitely presented host B_0.

Consequence for surviving enumerations. A successful nu must not make
the near shift s an automorphism of an input tree with such
stabilizers. Any near group acting cocompactly on a tree with locally
virtually abelian vertex stabilizers dies at the lifting step once it
contains Z^infinity.

Side result, in reasoning only and not written as a claim. For
one-ended P, a realization on N of c_k = s^k lambda(P) s^-k has one free
infinite orbit plus exactly k extra points. So gate windows containing
c_(-1), and ker(eta), are not realizable on N.

2026-09-17 (swarm-0917, c-shstab, calibration): tried to prove the
missing necessity, that E_nu finitely presented forces these clauses,
using the BHM complex of arXiv 2407.03149 Theorem 2.1. Stays OPEN; this
route dies at the edges.
- Setting. E_nu acts on the sublevel complex K<=2, which is 1-connected
  (Bestvina--Brady Morse count of hidden points). Up to finite index,
  cube stabilizers are SingFix(M,M') with |M'| <= 2, and every cube
  lies in the orbit of a non-singular cube. Edge stabilizers are
  finitely generated by `shell-finite-point-stabilizers-are-finitely-generated`.
- Homological chain. Let Z_0 be the augmentation kernel of C_0. Bieri's
  lemma for 0->A->B->C->0 (A FP_n and C FP_n imply B FP_n) reduces
  C_0 FP_2, hence A_1 FP_2, to Z_0 FP_2. From 0->Z_1->C_1->Z_0->0, Z_0
  FP_2 needs C_1 FP_2, which means edge stabilizers FP_2. These are
  A_12-type groups, so the argument is circular. Degree 2 only gives
  Z_1 finitely generated.
- The principle behind it is false. "A finitely presented group acting
  cocompactly on a contractible complex with finitely generated edge
  stabilizers has FP_2 vertex stabilizers" fails for G = F_2 x F_2. Let
  N be the Bieri--Stallings kernel of the map to Z sending every basis
  element to 1, and let G = N x| Z act on the line through Z. Vertex
  and edge stabilizers equal N, which is finitely generated but not
  FP_2.
- So complex bookkeeping alone cannot give necessity. Any proof must
  use shell-specific structure of the edge groups.
- K<=1 variant. The amalgam V *_(Fix_V(p)) A_1 maps onto E_nu, with the
  square relations as kernel, and it is finitely presented iff A_1 is.
  It does not separate E_nu fp from A_1 fp.
- Lift from Q fp to A_1 fp. Take V_p, lifts t_g fixing columns 0 and 1,
  [t_g, V(col0 u col1)], and lifted Q relators r_i = k_i. This presents
  A_1 iff the normal closure of V(col0 u col1) is the whole column
  kernel K = union_n s^n V(1C) s^-n. That needs t-conjugation to reach
  every column, but rho moves columns upward, so the domain induction
  does not close. Unresolved here, not refuted.
- Orientation remark, in reasoning only and not a claim. Suppose Q's
  gate holds with eps = +1, the orientation of V_p, so that c_0 lies in
  <c_1, ..., c_(N+1)>. Then ker(eta o d) is the ascending union of s^n
  B^ s^-n. Here B^ is generated by lifts of the window generators and
  V(X_M), for M large enough to absorb the correction supports. So A_1
  is an ascending HNN extension of a finitely generated base. No
  contradiction was found for eps = -1.
