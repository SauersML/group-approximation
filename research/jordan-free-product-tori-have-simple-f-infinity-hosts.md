**OPEN — conditional application of the proposed observed mapping-torus theorem.**

For `r,s≥1`, use the automorphism and observation in
`jordan-free-product-observations-separate-both-tails`. The mapping torus
`(Z^r*F_s)⋊_φ Z` and its observed restricted `V` lamp extension embed
in a simple group of type `F_∞` generated by two finite-order elements.

The mapping torus also has the amalgam description
`(Z^r⋊_φ Z)*_Z(F_s×Z)`, identifying the stable-letter subgroups.
For `r≥2` the observation is noninjective. The manuscript's additional
claim that `D` itself cannot embed in `V` uses the classical
Bleak–Salazar-Díaz obstruction for `Z²*Z`, arXiv:0911.0979; it is an
explicit literature dependency and is not independently re-audited here.
No new Boone–Higman priority for these individual groups is asserted.

## Attempts

The OPEN observation lemma supplies bilateral separation and type
`F_∞` for the base. The OPEN two-species construction and compact-core
finiteness then give the proposed simple host for the larger lamp
group, hence also the mapping torus. The conditional route records
these dependencies. Artifact §6 gives the binomial and reduced-word
mechanisms; the one-sided predecessor alone does not establish this
stronger full mapping-torus and lamp conclusion.
