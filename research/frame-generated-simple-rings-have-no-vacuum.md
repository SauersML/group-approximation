**ESTABLISHED** through `frame-generated-simple-rings-have-no-vacuum-proof`. Lane proof, not independently
reviewed. Elementary. It is the ring form of the fact that a Toeplitz algebra contains the compacts on its Fock
space as a proper ideal. No priority is claimed.

**Setting.** `R` is a nonzero unital ring, `B ⊆ R` a unital subring, and `s = (s_1, ..., s_k)`, `t = (t_1, ..., t_k)^T`
a row and a column over `R` with, for all `b in B`:
- **(T1)** `φ(b) := t b s` lies in `M_k(B)`;
- **(T2)** `b s = s φ(b)` and `t b = φ(b) t`.

Put `p = φ(1) = t s`, `e = s t` and the *vacuum* `q = 1 - e`. When `s t = 1`, (T2) follows from (T1), and this is the
isometric frame of `isometric-shift-frames-over-uhf-bases-fail-the-k-budget` with `t B s ⊆ M_k(B)`. For words
`α = (α_1, ..., α_m)` put `s_α = s_(α_1) ⋯ s_(α_m)` and `t_α = t_(α_m) ⋯ t_(α_1)`.

**Statement.** Assume `R` is generated as a ring by `B` and the entries of `s` and `t`.
1. **Structure.** `φ : B -> p M_k(B) p` is a unital ring homomorphism. `e` is an idempotent commuting with `B`,
   and `q s_i = 0 = t_i q`. `R` is spanned by the elements `s_α b t_β`.
2. **The vacuum ideal is small.** `R q R` is spanned by the `s_α q b t_β`. If `R q R = R`, then `s_α = 0` for every
   word of some length `n + 1`. If moreover `φ` is injective, then `s = 0`, `t = 0` and `q = 1`.
3. **Simple rings.** If `R` is simple, `p != 0` and `φ` is injective, then `q = 0`, that is `s t = 1`. `φ` is
   injective whenever `B` is simple and `p != 0`, for example for matricial `B`.
4. **Several frames.** Item 3 applies to any family of frames over `B` that concatenates into one row and one
   column satisfying (T1) and (T2). So in a simple ring generated by `B` and such frames, the frames fill the unit:
   `Σ_a s^(a) t^(a) = 1`.

## Consequences for the gate `fp-simple-resolvent-ring-with-divisible-unit-class`

- **No vacuum room.** In a simple ring generated by a base and frames over it, the vacuum is `0`, and
  `[1_R] = [p]_R` is the image of `φ_*[1_B]` (`isometric-shift-frames-over-uhf-bases-fail-the-k-budget`, item 1,
  which holds over any `B`). Over matricial bases that design is closed by the same node. A nonzero full vacuum
  needs generators outside the base and the frame.
- **`R_L`.** Take the frame `(s_1, s_2)`, `(t_1, t_2)` over `B = Q[N][(N+c)^(-1) : c >= 1]`. Then
  `φ(f) = diag(f(N+1), f(0))`, which is injective, and `p = I_2`. So `[1] = 2[1]` and `[1] = 0` in `K_0(R_L)`.
  - The resolvent vacuum of `R_L` in `resolvent-vacuum-rings-have-no-k0-state` is the vacuum `s_2 t_2` of the
    subframe `(s_1, t_1)`. It is filled by the second isometry `s_2`. The subframe alone does not generate
    `R_L`, so item 3 does not apply to it.
  - Filling a vacuum with further isometries over the same base gives an isometric frame of the summed index. It
    therefore falls under the isometric-frame dichotomy.
- **Where room remains.** `K_0(R)` must keep `[1]` of infinite order after `[1] = φ_*[1]`, which requires
  `[1_B] - φ_*[1_B]` to leave `[1_B]` of infinite order in the quotient. Under the Pimsner sequence that quotient is
  `coker(1 - φ_*)`.
  - For `K_0(B)` of finite rank, `K_1` detection forces `det(1 - φ_*) = 0`. This is the multi-type gap recorded
    on the gate: are the resulting `K_2` classes in the image of `K_2(N, R)`?
  - For `K_0(B)` of infinite rank, as in the level-indexed graph of the gate's Attempt 1, `1 - φ_*` can be injective
    with cokernel `Q`. That is the gate's remaining room. There the obstacle is finite presentation.
