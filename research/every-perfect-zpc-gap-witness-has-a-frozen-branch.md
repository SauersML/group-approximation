REFUTED.  The universal statement is not invariant under adding a
positive-probability verifier component.  The finite magic-square selector
has a perfect ZPC signed-permutation strategy, while each of its two frozen
branches forces `J=1`.  Padding any perfect ZPC gap witness by that component
preserves perfect ZPC value and a strict quantum gap but makes every global
frozen branch inconsistent.

This does not refute the existential possibility that one specially selected,
unpadded TailoredMIP witness has an orbitwise coherent equation-menu fibre.
It proves that such coherence must come from verifier-specific structure or a
special witness-selection theorem, not from perfectness, ZPC, and soundness
alone.
