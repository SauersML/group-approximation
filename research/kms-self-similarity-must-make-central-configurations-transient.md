**ESTABLISHED** by `kms-central-configurations-transient-proof` (lane proof, elementary
given the KMS lemmas it quotes; not reviewed; no priority claimed).

**Setting.** `M` is a Minsky machine with `K` glasses, and `G = G(M) = T ⋊ Q` is the
group of Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir (arXiv:1204.6506v5, §4.1). `T` is the abelian
normal subgroup of exponent `p` generated by the configuration letters, and `Q` is the
metabelian subgroup generated by the `a`- and `A`-letters. A faithful self-similar action
of `G` is one given, up to conjugacy, by a virtual endomorphism `φ: H -> G` on a
finite-index subgroup `H`, with trivial `φ`-core. Zaremsky's theorem would then embed
`G` in a finitely presented simple group
(`fp-self-similar-groups-embed-in-fp-simple-groups`). Whether hard KMS groups have such
actions is recorded as unknown by `bh-refute` (507f9fabf), citing Zaremsky.

**Theorem.** Let `Z_0 <= T` be the span of the fully marked configuration elements
`x_(q_i A_0) * a_1^(m_1) * ... * a_K^(m_K) * A_1 * ... * A_K`. Then:

1. `Z_0` is central in `G`, and it is infinite-dimensional whenever `M` has infinitely
   many inequivalent configurations.
2. **Transience.** In any faithful self-similar action, for every `0 != z ∈ Z_0` some
   iterate leaves: there is `k >= 0` with `φ^k(z) ∉ H`, or with `φ^k(z) ∉ Z_0` while
   `φ^j(z) ∈ Z_0 ∩ H` for `j < k`.
3. **Full-domain module maps die.** No faithful self-similar action comes from a `φ` with
   `T <= H` and `φ(T) <= T`. More generally, none comes from a `φ` with `N <= H` and
   `φ(N) <= N` for a nonzero normal subgroup `N <= T`.
4. **Counter halving dies.** Its structure is this: for one state and one unmarked glass,
   the cyclic `F_p[a, a']`-module generated by a configuration letter is the ring
   `R = F_p[a^(±1), (a-1)^(-1)]`. Appending a coin (the operation `* a`) is
   multiplication by `P(a) = (a^3 - 2a^2 + a + 1)/(a(a-1))`, whose numerator is the
   characteristic polynomial of KMS's three-periodic block. Over `F_p`, `P(a)^p = P(a^p)`.
   So the counter-halving (digit-renormalization) maps `φ(t^(λ^p)) = φ(t)^λ` are
   compatible with every `F_p`-polynomial relation, zero tests included, and are defined
   on all of `T` with values in `T`. By item 3 they are never faithful.
5. **Lamplighter-style division dies.** No faithful self-similar action comes from a `φ`
   that is the identity on a finite-index `Q_1 <= Q` and on `T` is division by a
   group-ring element `π`, that is `H ∩ T = πT` and `φ(πt) = t`. That is the shape of the
   lamplighter's standard self-similar action.

**What a positive answer must look like.** Items 2--5 leave only virtual endomorphisms
under which every central configuration element eventually leaves the domain or the
center, and which neither preserve `T` on a full domain nor divide it by a group-ring
element. The natural remaining shape: a map whose iterated domains meet `Z_0` in the
finite-codimension subspaces of the KMS residual-finiteness proof (spans of classes
containing a configuration with some counter at least `D`), lowering that bound by one
per level. By the translation obstruction of Attempt 1 on
`fp-rf-apapa-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`, such a map cannot be the counter shift
itself.
