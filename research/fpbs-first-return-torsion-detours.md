title: Bounded disjoint first-return detours give zero relative cost
---

Section 3.1. If for every k there are c_i=a b_i, b_i in K, first-return lengths 2<=d_i<=N with c_i^d_i in K and disjoint external power sets P_i, then relC(R_<K,a>;R_K)=0 in every specified free action. The additional cost is at most q+(1-q^(N-1))^k+eta.
