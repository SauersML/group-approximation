Fix any Kun--Thom Theorem E parameters `q` and `r,d>=3`.  Put

```text
R_+ = F_q[x_1,...,x_d],
R   = F_q[x_1^(+-1),...,x_d^(+-1)],
Gamma = EL_r(R_+),
G     = EL_r(R) semidirect SL_d(Z),
```

and let

```text
G_tilde = St_r(R) semidirect SL_d(Z)  ->  G              (FPS1)
```

be the natural actor cover.  Let `Gamma_tilde` be the subgroup of
`St_r(R)` generated by the polynomial root elements, and choose a lift

```text
h_tilde=x_12((x_1...x_d)^(-1))
```

of the marked `h`.  If `o=Gamma` and `y=h Gamma`, the minimal marked form
satisfies

```text
b(delta_o,delta_y)=1.                                   (FPS2)
```

**Claim.**  The cover `G_tilde` is finitely presented, `Gamma_tilde` is
finitely generated, and one finite table of their **images in `G`**, together
with the two lamps `{o,y}`, admits no Clifford-normalizer lifts with
scalar-only multiplication curvature.  This holds already at `r=3`; no
finite-presentation theorem for `EL_3(R)` and no unstable `K_2(3,R)`
calculation is needed.

## Finite presentability of the cover in every rank

The Laurent ring `R` is generated as a ring by finitely many units: the
finite field units and `x_i^(+-1)`.  For the spherical diagram `A_(r-1)`,
`r>=3`, Allcock's *Steinberg groups as amalgams*, Theorem 1.4(ii), finitely
presents the pre-Steinberg group: the diagram is 2-spherical without `A_1`
components and `R` is a rank-one module over the subring generated by those
finitely many units.  Allcock's Theorem 1.1 identifies the pre-Steinberg and
Steinberg groups for spherical diagrams.  Hence `St_r(R)` is finitely
presented for every `r>=3`.

The arithmetic group `SL_d(Z)` is finitely presented.  Its monomial action
on `R` induces an action on `St_r(R)`, and specifying the action of finitely
many acting generators on finitely many Steinberg generators gives a finite
presentation of the semidirect product `G_tilde`.

The subgroup `Gamma_tilde` is finitely generated.  Choose root elements for
a finite ring-generating set of `R_+` in every root position.  Root addition
and the Steinberg commutator relation

```text
[x_ij(a),x_jk(b)]=x_ik(ab)
```

generate all polynomial root elements; three distinct indices are available
because `r>=3`.

## Finite-image marked collapse in the cover

Every positive compressor `t` acts on exponents by a nonnegative integral
matrix, so its ring automorphism sends `R_+` into `R_+` and

```text
t Gamma_tilde t^(-1) <= Gamma_tilde.                   (FPS3)
```

The Kun--Thom compressor semigroup generates the external actor, and the
compressor conjugates of the polynomial root groups generate `St_r(R)`:
every Laurent exponent is an `SL_d(Z)` image of a nonnegative multiple of a
primitive coordinate exponent.  Thus `Gamma_tilde` together with finitely
many positive compressors generates `G_tilde`.

Let `q:G_tilde->Q` have finite image.  Equation `(FPS3)` becomes an inclusion
between conjugate finite subgroups of equal order, hence equality.  Therefore
`q(Gamma_tilde)` is normalized by every compressor and so is normal in
`q(G_tilde)`.  The marked `h_tilde` is a compressor conjugate of a polynomial
root element (the exponent `(-1,...,-1)` is primitive), whence

```text
q(h_tilde) in q(Gamma_tilde).                           (FPS4)
```

## One finite image table gives the contradiction

Choose a finite presentation `G_tilde=<S_tilde|Q_tilde>`, a finite generating
set `A_tilde` of `Gamma_tilde`, and a word for `h_tilde`.  In `G`, form one
finite actor table from the **images** of `S_tilde`, `A_tilde`, `h_tilde`, and
all prefix products occurring in the relators, inverse words and named
element words.  Include `(1,1,1)` and test

```text
U_a W_o U_a^* = W_o          (a in image(A_tilde)),
U_h W_o U_h^* = W_y.                                  (FPS5)
```

Suppose its Clifford multiplication curvatures were scalar.  Projecting each
normalizer to its symplectic action gives maps `s_g` satisfying every tested
prefix multiplication exactly.  Assign a cover generator `s_tilde` the map
labelled by its image in `G`.  Every cover relator vanishes, so this extends
to a homomorphism

```text
q:G_tilde -> Sp(K).                                    (FPS6)
```

Possible collisions among images of cover prefixes in `G` only identify
more symplectic maps and cause no ambiguity.  The target is finite, so
`(FPS4)` applies.  Equations `(FPS5)` make `v_o` fixed by
`q(Gamma_tilde)` and send it by `q(h_tilde)` to `v_y`.  Hence `v_y=v_o`,
contradicting `(FPS2)` because an alternating form has `b(v_o,v_o)=0`.

## Exact scope

This refutes the scalar-only finite-window section for every Theorem E rank,
including the endpoint choice `q=2`, `r=d=3`.  It does **not** refute
`minimal-marked-graph-crossed-product-ce`: normalized-HS microstates need
not admit a covariance-compatible retraction to block-preserving Clifford
normalizers.  The exact replacement forced on any CE construction is the
correlated projective-HS instability recorded in
`minimal-graph-ce-forces-correlated-actor-instability`.

The earlier stable-quotient proof remains valid when `r>=d+4`: there
Suslin--Tulenbaev identifies the Steinberg kernel with the finitely generated
stable `K_2(R)`.  The cover argument shows that this extra descent to
`EL_r(R)` is unnecessary.
