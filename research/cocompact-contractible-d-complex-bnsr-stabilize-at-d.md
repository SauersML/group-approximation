Let `G` act properly and cocompactly on a contractible CW complex `X` of
dimension `d`. Then `G` is of type `F_∞`, and `Σ^m(G) = Σ^d(G)` for every
`m ≥ d`. In particular `Σ^d(G) = Σ^{d+1}(G)`, so such a group does not answer
Zaremsky Problem 1.18 affirmatively.

This covers hyperbolic groups (via Rips complexes), CAT(0) groups acting
properly cocompactly, and groups of type F.
