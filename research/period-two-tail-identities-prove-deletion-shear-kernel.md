The vector w is omega^2 k_+(zeta,eta)+omega k_-(zeta,eta), so it
lies in the source; its four distinct binary wedge terms prove it
is nonzero. Using zeta=0eta and eta=1zeta gives the images of all
six needed basis vectors directly. In gw, three terms are wedges
entirely in the first field coordinate and are annihilated by P_0.
The other two terms are e_3 zeta wedge e_1 eta and
e_3 eta wedge e_1 zeta. Their projections cancel because both J
forms are symmetric in the tail arguments in characteristic two.
