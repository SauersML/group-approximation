The vector w is omega^2 k_+(zeta,eta)+omega k_-(zeta,eta), so it
lies in the source; its four distinct binary wedge terms prove it
is nonzero. Using zeta=0eta and eta=1zeta gives the images of all
six needed basis vectors directly. In gw, three terms are wedges
entirely in the first field coordinate and are annihilated by P_0.
The other two terms are e_3 zeta wedge e_1 eta and
e_3 eta wedge e_1 zeta. Their projections cancel because both J
forms are symmetric in the tail arguments in characteristic two.

For incomparable nonempty prefix words alpha,beta, their two periodic
concatenations satisfy zeta=s_alpha eta and eta=s_beta zeta. The
cross deletions vanish because the prefix cylinders are disjoint.
Substituting these identities gives exactly the same five-term image
and cancellation for every such pair.

Let U be the first binary field-coordinate pair and j(U) its
copy in the second. The subspace generated by Lambda^2 U,
j(p) wedge q+j(q) wedge p, and j(p) wedge p is annihilated by
P_0 and is invariant under every [[h,k],[0,h]]. The five-term
image gw lies in this subspace, proving the triangular extension
with the same source certificate. The diagonal cross generators
are recorded separately because in characteristic two they need
not be sums of off-diagonal symmetric generators.
