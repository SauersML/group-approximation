**Source.** G. Arzhantseva and M. Steenbock, *Rips construction without unique
product*, arXiv:1407.2441 (v1, 9 July 2014), <https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.2441>.
The arXiv journal-ref field gives Pacific J. Math. 322 (2023) 1--9.
- Read from the v1 PDF on 2026-09-12, with the text extracted by Ghostscript
  on MSI.
- The published version was not read.
- Page numbers are those of v1.

**Theorem 3** (p. 2). Let `Q` be a finitely generated group. Then there exists
a short exact sequence `1 -> N -> G -> Q -> 1` such that
- `G` is a torsion-free group without the unique product property which is a
  direct limit of Gromov hyperbolic groups,
- `N` is a subgroup of `G` with Kazhdan's Property (T) and without the unique
  product property.

"We provide, in particular, first examples of Property (T) groups without the
unique product property."

**Corollary 1** (p. 2). "There are torsion-free Gromov hyperbolic groups with
Kazhdan's Property (T) and without the unique product property."

**Corollary 2** (pp. 2--3). "There are torsion-free Property (T) groups G
without the unique product property such that all proper subgroups of G are
cyclic."

**How Corollary 1 is obtained** (pp. 2--3). The paper gives two ways.
1. Graphical small cancellation over free products, combined with Gromov's
   spectral criterion for property (T) (their Theorem 4, on graphs labelled by
   `m > 64` letters).
2. Their Theorem 5, which is Ol'shanskii [Ol93, Th. 2]: "Let `G = H_1 * H_2` be
   the free product of two non-elementary torsion-free Gromov hyperbolic groups
   and `M ⊆ H_1` be a finite subset. Then `G` has a non-elementary torsion-free
   Gromov hyperbolic quotient `Gbar` such that the canonical projection
   `G ->> Gbar` is surjective on `H_2` and injective on `M`."
   - `H_1` is a group from their Theorem 1, without unique products for the
     sets `A`, `B`.
   - `H_2` is "a Gromov hyperbolic group with Property (T) (e.g. a discrete
     subgroup of finite covolume in Sp(n,1))".
   - `M` is a finite subset of `H_1` containing `A`, `B` and `AB`.

"The first approach shows the existence of graphical small cancellation
presentations of such groups and the preceding argument provides first
explicit examples." The relators in the second way still come from
Ol'shanskii's theorem. This graph records no concrete presentation or normal
form for any of these groups.

**On Kaplansky's conjecture** (p. 4). "Open problem 1. Do the Rips-Segev groups
without the unique product property satisfy the Kaplansky zero-divisor
conjecture? Combining recent deep results [Sch14, LOS12, Ago13], we observe
that the Kaplansky zero-divisor conjecture holds for all torsion-free
CAT(0)-cubical Gromov hyperbolic groups over the field of complex numbers. Our
groups from Corollary 1 are not CAT(0)-cubical as they are infinite Property
(T) groups. Thus, it follows from our results that the CAT(0)-cubulation cannot
solve the Kaplansky zero-divisor conjecture for all Gromov hyperbolic groups
without the unique product property."

(p. 10) "The groups G we have just constructed are the only known examples of
torsion-free Gromov hyperbolic groups without the unique product property. It
is yet unknown whether these torsion-free groups without the unique product
property do satisfy the Kaplansky zero-divisor conjecture."

**Earlier examples** (footnote 1, p. 3). "Groups in [Pas77, Pro88] are
solvable, hence, a-T-menable; groups in [Car14] are a-T-menable as they have
`Z^k x F_m` as a finite index subgroup."
