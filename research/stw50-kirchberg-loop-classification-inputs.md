For a unital UCT Kirchberg algebra D with free finitely generated
K-groups and primitive unit e, the natural reduced loop invariant
Delta is the integral isomorphism

`pi_1 Aut(D) = Hom(K_1(D),K_0(D)) direct-sum Hom(K_0(D)/Ze,K_1(D))`.

The graph algebras with positive adjacency matrices used in the
artifact are UCT Kirchberg, with K_0=coker(I-M^t), K_1=ker(I-M^t)
and unit given by the all-ones vector. Their tensor products have
the torsion-free graded Kunneth groups. Prescribed scaled graded
K-theory isomorphisms between these algebras are realized by fixed
unital isomorphisms.

These are literature inputs and the elementary primitive-unit
specialization of Dadarlat's exact sequence, not new open-problem
solutions. The exact natural map and its vanishing kernel are
justified in Section 1 of the artifact.
