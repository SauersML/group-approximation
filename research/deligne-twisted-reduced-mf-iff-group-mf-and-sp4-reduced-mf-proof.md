Notation is as in the target. `Q_d = prod_n M_(d_n) / sum_n M_(d_n)`.

**Step 0. Elementary MF facts.**
- A separable C-star subalgebra of an MF algebra is MF, by definition.
- A finite direct sum of MF algebras is MF: interleave the two dimension sequences, so that `Q_d ⊕ Q_d'` sits
  block-diagonally in `Q_(d'')`.
- If `A` is MF, so is `A^op`. The transpose on each `M_(d_n)` is a *-anti-automorphism preserving the direct sum,
  so it induces `Q_d^op ≅ Q_d`.
- *Unitalization and lifting.* Let `iota : A -> Q_d` be an injective *-homomorphism of a unital `A`. The projection
  `e = iota(1)` lifts to projections `e_n in M_(d_n)`, and `e Q_d e ≅ prod e_n M_(d_n) e_n / sum`. So `A` embeds
  unitally in a corona `Q_(d')` with `d'_n = rank e_n` (drop the finitely many `n` with `e_n = 0`, or all `n` with
  `e_n = 0` if infinitely many; since `e != 0`, infinitely many `e_n` are nonzero).
  - A unitary `u in Q_(d')` lifts to unitaries `(u_n)`: take any lift `x_n`. Then `||x_n^* x_n - 1||` and
    `||x_n x_n^* - 1||` tend to 0, so `x_n` is invertible for large `n`, and its polar part `u_n` satisfies
    `||u_n - x_n|| -> 0`.

**Step 1. Exactness of every fibre.**
- `Gamma` is a countable subgroup of `GL_4(Z)`, so it has property A (`commutative-ring-linear-groups-have-property-a`).
- The quotient `E_q -> Gamma` has finite kernel, so with word metrics it is a coarse equivalence: it is Lipschitz,
  and preimages of bounded sets are bounded, with fibres of diameter at most `max_k |z^k|`.
- Property A is a coarse invariant (Willett, arXiv:math/0612492, l.494 and l.542, as quoted in
  `some-simple-kazhdan-lef-groups-are-not-exact-proof` step 5 and `exactness-separates-boone-higman-proof`). So
  `E_q` has property A.
- For countable discrete groups, property A is equivalent to exactness of `C*_r` (Guentner--Kaminker; Ozawa, quoted
  from arXiv:1406.5015 l.166 in the same step 6). So `C*_r(E_q)` and `C*_r(Gamma)` are exact.
- The unitary `lambda(z)` is central of order `q`, so `p_j = (1/q) sum_k exp(-2 pi i jk/q) lambda(z)^k` are central
  projections with `C*_r(E_q) = ⊕_j p_j C*_r(E_q)`.
- A direct summand `pB` (with `p` a central projection) of an exact `B` is exact. For an exact sequence
  `0 -> J -> D -> D/J -> 0`, the sequence for `pB ⊗_min -` is the image of the one for `B ⊗_min -` under the central
  projection `p ⊗ 1`, and it is a direct summand of it. A direct summand of an exact sequence is exact.

**Step 2. The fibres are the twisted algebras.**
- Write elements of `E_q` as `(g,k)`, with `(g,k)(h,l) = (gh, k+l+b(g,h))` and `z = (e,1)`.
- The range of `p_j` in `l^2(E_q)` consists of the `f` with `f(g, k-1) = exp(2 pi i j/q) f(g,k)`, since
  `lambda(z) f = exp(2 pi i j/q) f` there. So `f` is determined by `phi(g) = f(g,0)`, and
  `f -> q^(1/2) phi` is unitary onto `l^2(Gamma)`.
- Here `(lambda(h',0) f)(g,k) = f((h',0)^(-1)(g,k))`, and `(h',0)^(-1)(g,k) = (h'^(-1) g, k - b(h', h'^(-1) g))`
  up to the normalization `b(e,.) = b(.,e) = 0`, which holds for the normalized cocycle.
- So `lambda(h',0)` acts on `phi` by `phi(g) -> exp(2 pi i (j/q) b(h', h'^(-1) g)) phi(h'^(-1) g)`. Taking
  `g = h' h`, this is `delta_h -> c_(j/q)(h', h) delta_(h'h)`, that is `lambda_(c_(j/q))(h')`.
- Hence `p_j C*_r(E_q) ≅ C*_r(Gamma, c_(j/q))` for each `j in Z/q`, and `p_0 C*_r(E_q) ≅ C*_r(Gamma)`.
- The sign convention in `c_(±j/q)` is irrelevant below: `j` ranges over all of `Z/q`, and the statement is
  symmetric under `theta -> -theta` by Step 4(b).
- By Step 1, every `C*_r(Gamma, c_theta)` with `theta` rational is exact.

**Step 3. Fell absorption (the invariant).**
- Let `D` act faithfully on `H`, and let `Theta(g) in U(D)` satisfy `Theta(e) = 1` and
  `Theta(g) Theta(h) = c(g,h) Theta(gh)` exactly. Let `tau` be a second multiplier.
- Put `W(xi ⊗ delta_h) = Theta(h) xi ⊗ delta_h` on `H ⊗ l^2(Gamma)`. It is unitary. Then

  ```text
  (Theta(g) ⊗ lambda_tau(g)) W (xi ⊗ delta_h) = tau(g,h) Theta(g)Theta(h) xi ⊗ delta_(gh)
                                              = c(g,h) tau(g,h) Theta(gh) xi ⊗ delta_(gh)
                                              = W (1 ⊗ lambda_(c tau)(g)) (xi ⊗ delta_h).
  ```

- So the C-star algebra generated by `{Theta(g) ⊗ lambda_tau(g)}` is isomorphic to `C*_r(Gamma, c tau)`. It lies in
  `D ⊗_min C*_r(Gamma, tau)`, whose norm is the spatial norm on `H ⊗ l^2(Gamma)`.

**Step 4. (R1).** Fix a rational `theta`.
- *(a) `<=`.* Suppose `theta in P_op`, via maps `U_n` as in `deligne-sep7-norm-parameter-closed-subgroup`. The
  classes `Theta(g) = [(U_n(g))_n] in Q_d` are unitaries with `Theta(e) = 1` and
  `Theta(g)Theta(h) = c_theta(g,h) Theta(gh)` exactly, because the defects tend to 0.
  - `D = C*(Theta(Gamma))` is separable, since `Gamma` is countable, and it is MF.
  - Suppose `C*_r(Gamma)` is MF. It is exact by Step 1. By `mf-min-tensor-exact-mf-is-mf`,
    `D ⊗_min C*_r(Gamma)` is MF.
  - Step 3 with `tau = 1`, `c = c_theta` puts `C*_r(Gamma, c_theta)` inside it. So it is MF by Step 0.
  - This direction uses no finiteness of the order of `theta`.
- *(b) `=>`, `theta in P_op`.* Let `A = C*_r(Gamma, c_theta)` be MF. By Step 0 it embeds unitally in some `Q_(d')`,
  and the images of `lambda_(c_theta)(g)` lift to unitaries `U_n(g)`, with `U_n(e) = I` after replacing `U_n(e)` by
  `I` (which changes it by a null sequence).
  - The relations `lambda(g)lambda(h) = c_theta(g,h) lambda(gh)` hold exactly in `A`. So
    `||U_n(g)U_n(h) - c_theta(g,h)U_n(gh)|| -> 0` for every pair, and `theta in P_op`.
- *(c) `=>`, `C*_r(Gamma)` MF.*
  - By `twisted-c-star-opposite-inverts-cocycle`, `A^op ≅ C*_r(Gamma, c_(-theta))`, where `c_theta^(-1) = c_(-theta)`.
  - It is MF by Step 0 and exact by Steps 1 and 2, since `-theta` is rational.
  - By `mf-min-tensor-exact-mf-is-mf`, `A ⊗_min C*_r(Gamma, c_(-theta))` is MF.
  - Step 3 with `D = A` on `l^2(Gamma)`, `Theta = lambda_(c_theta)` and `tau = c_(-theta)` puts
    `C*_r(Gamma, 1) = C*_r(Gamma)` inside it. So it is MF.
  - The same argument with `theta -> -theta` shows `P_r = -P_r`.

**Step 5. (R2) and (R3).**
- By Steps 1 and 2, `C*_r(E_q) = ⊕_(j in Z/q) C*_r(Gamma, c_(j/q))` up to relabelling `j -> -j`. By Step 0 it is MF
  iff every summand is.
- By (R1), that holds iff `j/q in P_op` for all `j` and `C*_r(Gamma)` is MF. Since `P_op` is a subgroup, this is
  iff `1/q in P_op` and `C*_r(Gamma)` is MF.
- By `deligne-sep7-all-cover-mf-radical-classification` (`E_q` is MF iff `1/q in P_op`), this is (R2).
- (R3) is (R1) read for all rational `theta` at once. The equivalences at `1/2` use `1/2 in P_op`
  (`deligne-sep7-norm-parameter-closed-subgroup`) and (R2) at `q = 2`.

**Step 6. The consequences in the target.**
- *Refutation side.* This is (R1) at `theta = 1/3`. `Sp_4(Z)` is infinite and has property (T), since it is a lattice
  in the higher-rank group `Sp_4(R)`. So MF of `C*_r(Sp_4(Z))` answers `infinite-kazhdan-group-with-mf-reduced-cstar`.
- *Proof side.* This is the contrapositive of (R1, `<=`) at `theta = 1/3`:
  `[C*_r(Gamma, c_(1/3)) not MF] and [C*_r(Gamma) MF]` gives `1/3 not in P_op`. That is the flagship, by
  `deligne-sep7-all-cover-mf-radical-classification`.

∎
