Let Gamma be infinite and finitely presented, acting freely, and let Phi be a finite bounded-degree graphing of the orbit relation with chosen measurable paths for the group generators and chosen words for its own edge labels. Form the closed integer circulations obtained by routing the defining relators and by comparing each graphing edge with the word spelling it, and measure them by the mass remaining after cancellation, not by the length of a representing walk. If T_L is the total measure of roots where some such circulation has reduced mass above L, then tau_L(Phi) <= T_L for every L >= 3, and consequently C(R) <= c(Phi) - max(0, c(Phi) - 1 - beta_1 - T_L)/L, a genuine descent to a generating subgraphing rather than an inequality about an unknown minimizer. Hence 0 <= g(R) <= (L-1) eps + T_L, and a near-minimizing sequence whose circulation tails vanish in the iterated limit forces C(R) = 1 + beta_1; a uniform bound K on the total mean reduced circulation mass suffices, by Markov.

Finite presentability alone does not bound these routed circulation norms, and divergence of replacement-path lengths does not by itself refute the hypothesis, since a path followed by its reverse cancels. Those estimates are exactly what is missing.

Status records a written deduction, not independent validation.
