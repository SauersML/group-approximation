Let `H` be finite, `y` a central involution, and
`A=<y,z,a_1,...,a_k>` an elementary abelian subgroup.  If a group element
`e in H` acts as a prescribed scalar on every `A`-weight space in every
representation with `y=-I`, then `e in A`; its scalar table is therefore an
affine character of the selector bits.

The hybrid D8 row which evaluates a nontrivial predicate on one role and is
identically `+1` on the other is not a character.  It cannot be one
universally oriented finite-packet word, and in the finite regular packet it
has a fixed normalized-HS gap from every group word.  The row exists as a
rational group-algebra reflection, so the remaining positive target is a
selected-carrier or finite-matrix return theorem, not a larger universal
finite packet.
