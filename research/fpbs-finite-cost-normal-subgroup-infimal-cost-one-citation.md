Citation import, not a reproof. Source: M. Donoso-Echenique and E. Silva, *Free Burnside groups of large odd exponent have cost 1*, Section 2.

- **Theorem 2.2**, verbatim: "Theorem 2.2 ([Gab02b, Theorem 3.4]). Let G be a countable group and let N◁G be an infinite normal subgroup such that N has infinite index in G and cost(N)<∞. Then cost(G) = 1."
- **Remark following it**, verbatim: "In [Gab02b] it is assumed that N is finitely generated, but this can be replaced by the assumption that N has finite cost; see the footnote on page 884 of [HP20] and [Gab25, Theorem 2.56]".

Both sentences were read on 2026-09-17 from the arXiv PDF of that paper, extracted with pypdf. The paper uses `cost(G)` for the infimal cost. The same theorem appears in the archive as Gaboriau lecture notes Theorem 2.56, cited in `fpbs-hyperbolic-3-manifold-groups-fixed-price-one`.
