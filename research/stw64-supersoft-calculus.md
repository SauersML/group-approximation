Let A be separable, simple, unital, infinite-dimensional, and of stable rank
one, with nonempty normalized 2-quasitrace space. Put S=Cu(A) and u=[1_A].
The APRT map alpha from lower semicontinuous rank functions to S preserves
order and increasing suprema. For t>0, s_t=alpha(t*u-hat) has rank t*u-hat
and is supersoft. For every x in S,

    x+s_t = alpha(x-hat+t*u-hat).

Every soft x satisfies x<=alpha(x-hat). The semigroup has weak cancellation:

    x+z<=y+z' and z'<<z imply x<=y.

The normalized functional space is compact and evaluation at a Cuntz class
is lower semicontinuous. The radius of comparison is the infimum of R>0
such that x-hat+R*u-hat<=y-hat implies x<=y.

This is an explicit literature input, not a new resolution of a named problem.
In particular, alpha is not asserted to preserve compact containment.
