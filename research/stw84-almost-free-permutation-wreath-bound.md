Let `L` be a countable locally finite group, let `A` be a countable abelian
group of finite rational rank

`r=dim_Q(Q tensor_Z A)`,

and let `A` act on a countable set `S`.  Assume every point stabilizer
`A_s` is finite.  For the generalized permutation wreath product

`G=(direct_sum_S L) rtimes A`

one has, for `r>=1`,

`dim_nuc(C*(G)) <= (r+1) * 9^r - 1 < infinity`.            `(APW1)`

If `r=0`, then `G` is locally finite and the sharper exact value is zero.
No uniform bound on the orders of the stabilizers or of the finite
subgroups of `L` is required.

This genuinely passes the two previous action hypotheses.  For example,
take

`A=Z direct_sum (direct_sum_(n>=1) Z/2)`,

let `e_n` generate the `n`-th order-two summand, and let `S` be the disjoint
union of the coset actions `A/<e_n>`.  With `L=Z/2`, `(APW1)` gives nuclear
dimension at most `17`.  The action is not regular and has stabilizers in
infinitely many distinct torsion directions.  Moreover the subgroup
generated by the `Z`-generator and one lamp has infinite intersection with
the lamp kernel, so the kernel-finite theorem does not apply.
