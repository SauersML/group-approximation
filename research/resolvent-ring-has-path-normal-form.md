**ESTABLISHED** through `resolvent-ring-has-path-normal-form-proof`. Lane proof; proof-gap
review PASS by gq-referee-a (report in `artifacts`). It uses no Diamond Lemma: an explicit ring is built and the
universal properties are compared.

**Notation.**
- `A = Z[u][(u + 1 + kl)^(-1) : k in Z] ⊂ Q(u)`, with `σ(u) = u + l`.
- `D = A[x^(±1); σ]` is the skew Laurent ring, with `x a x^(-1) = σ(a)`.
- For `k in Z` write `m_k = v_l(k) + 1` (and `m_0 = ∞`).
- `θ : Z_(l) -> e R_l e` is the corner homomorphism.

**Statement.**
1. **The shift part.** The subring of `R_l` generated by `u, x, x', B` is isomorphic to
   `D` (`x' = x^(-1)`, `B = (u+1)^(-1)`). It meets the ideal `(e)` in 0, and
   `R_l / (e) ≅ D`.
2. **Path basis of the ideal.** For `a, b in Z`, `s >= 0` and `K = (k_1, ..., k_s)` with
   all `k_i != 0`, put

       [a|K|b] = x^a e x^(k_1) e x^(k_2) e ... e x^(k_s) e x^b .

   Then `(e) = ⊕_(a,b,K) G_K · [a|K|b]`, with coefficient groups:
   - `G_∅ = Z_(l)` for `s = 0`, acting through `θ`;
   - `G_K = Z / l^(min_i m_(k_i))` for `s >= 1`.

   Each summand is exactly that cyclic group: the elements `[a|K|b]` have the stated
   additive orders and are independent.
3. **Multiplication.**
   - `[a|K|b] · [a'|K'|b'] = [a | K, b+a', K' | b']`, where the letter `b + a'` is
     omitted when `b + a' = 0`.
   - `x^c [a|K|b] = [a+c|K|b]` and `[a|K|b] x^c = [a|K|b+c]`.
   - `f(u) [a|K|b] = f(-al)·[a|K|b]` and `[a|K|b] f(u) = f(bl)·[a|K|b]` for
     `f in A`, as elements of `Z_(l)` acting through `θ`.
4. **The corner.** `e R_l e = ⊕_K G_K [0|K|0]` is the tensor algebra over `Z_(l)` of
   `N = ⊕_(k != 0) (Z / l^(m_k)) z_k`, with `z_k = e x^k e`.

So `R_l ≅ D ⊕ (V ⊗_(Z_(l)) T_(Z_(l))(N) ⊗_(Z_(l)) W)`, where `V = D/Du` and
`W = D/uD` are free over `Z_(l)` with bases `x^a e`, respectively `e x^b`.
