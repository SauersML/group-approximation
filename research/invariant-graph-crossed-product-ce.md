For the explicit residually finite nonnormal infranormal Kazhdan pair
`Gamma<G`, with `X=G/Gamma`, `o=Gamma` and `y=h Gamma` the marked pair,
prove that there is a `G`-invariant graph `S` on `X` with

```text
{o,y} in S,        b_S nondegenerate on directSum_X F_2,
```

for which the tracial crossed product

```text
M_S = A_S crossed_product G
```

is Connes embeddable, where `A_S` is the twisted group von Neumann algebra of
`(directSum_X F_2, b_S)` at the central character `z |-> -1`.

Every such `A_S` is the hyperfinite `II_1` factor, so the difficulty is the
action, not the base — as for the complete graph.  The point of the
existential form is that the anticommutation pattern is a free parameter and
only one member of the family has to work.  Degenerate graphs are excluded
because they reintroduce a `G`-invariant abelian direction in the centre of
the base, which is the coordinate the Kun--Thom permutation normalization
argument acts on.

**Few orbits yes, finite degree no — the two are not the same thing.**  The
minimal member of the family is the single symmetrized marked double coset
`Gamma h Gamma`, and it is available: the corpus already builds it, as the
orbital target `E_orb = K_(X,E_*) semidirect G` at notes/NOTEPAD.md near line
9969, where the `z=-1` lamp algebra is identified as the AF twisted group
algebra `C*_r(directSum_X F_2, (-1)^(B_*))` rather than the full CAR algebra
— "a strictly weaker analytic target than the complete Clifford system".
That is the concrete witness for this claim's existential, and it is where
the attack should start.

What is *not* available is finite degree.  It was natural to hope that the
minimal member would also be locally finite, giving a Bogoliubov action
touching finitely many Majorana modes per generator — the regime where the
finite-mode permutation defects of
notes/FALSE_FERMIONIC_KUN_THOM_ENDPOINT.md §5 could be localized.
`marked-double-coset-infinite-degree` refutes that: the marked double coset
of the Laurent pair is an infinite union of left cosets, so **every**
invariant graph containing the marked edge, the minimal one included, has
infinite degree at every vertex.  The remaining freedom is in the *shape* of
an infinite-degree nondegenerate graph, not in its sparsity.

**The sub-question this left is closed (2026-08-18).**  It read: is the
minimal member nondegenerate?  Its radical is the `G`-submodule `ker(A)` of
`F_2[X]`, where `A` is the `F_2` adjacency operator of the orbital graph of
`Gamma h Gamma`; by `invariant-graph-clifford-phase` item 4 this vanishing is
equivalent to the quotient being monolithic and to the lamp algebra being a
factor.  A nonempty finite `F` in the radical would need every vertex to have
an even number of `S`-neighbours in `F`; for `|F|=2` this says the two
vertices are twins, `g Gamma h Gamma = Gamma h Gamma`.

The answer is yes, and by a stronger route than parity:
`minimal-marked-graph-private-neighbour` gives every finite vertex set a
vertex adjacent to exactly *one* of its members, so
`minimal-marked-graph-nondegenerate` kills the radical outright and there are
no twins.  The mechanism is a denominator cut — a coset of `Gamma` other than
the base one carries a Laurent denominator, one polynomial row shear in
`Gamma` aligns the denominators of any finite competitor family into a common
row, and the marked opposite-root neighbours `e_(qp)(a) h_(pq) Gamma` avoid
all of them for all but a `q^(-c L^(d-1))` fraction of degree-`L` parameters
(`private-neighbour-density`).

So the cheapest member carries the factorial model, and the existential above
no longer needs a search: `S = S_min` satisfies both algebraic conditions,
`vertex-supported-symplectic-completion` even exhausts its lamp algebra by
full matrix blocks generated by actual vertices with exact traces, and the
whole of this claim reduces to the single analytic hole
`minimal-marked-graph-crossed-product-ce` through the route
`invariant-graph-ce-from-minimal-graph`.  What is untouched is the action:
finite-degree graphs are still impossible
(`marked-double-coset-infinite-degree`), finite `G`-invariant symplectic
quotients retaining the mark are still impossible (the finite-rank
invariant-form no-go), and finite coordinate models are dead twice over
(`marked-graph-action-not-residually-finite`,
`lamp-coordinate-collision-obstruction`).
