Sturmian subshifts of quadratic slope are substitutive.

**Statement.** Let `α_1, ..., α_d` be quadratic irrationals, and `Λ_i = Z + α_i Z`.
Let `BEx(Λ)` be the group of bijections of `[0,1)^d` that are piecewise translations on
finitely many boxes `∏ [a_i, b_i)` with `a_i, b_i ∈ Λ_i`, with translation vectors in
`∏ Λ_i` (taken mod `Z^d`). Then `BEx(Λ)` embeds in a finitely presented simple group,
and so does every subgroup. For `d = 2` this is the group of rectangle exchange
transformations with this coordinate data.
