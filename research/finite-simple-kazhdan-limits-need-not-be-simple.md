**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed), folklore.** `SL_3(Z)` with its elementary generators is an infinite, finitely generated group
with property (T). It is the limit, in the space of marked groups, of the finite simple groups `SL_3(F_p)` for primes
`p ≡ 2 mod 3`, and it is not simple.

Use: this firewalls every attempt to obtain simplicity of a Kazhdan group from its finite simple models alone, including
shortcuts for the note's groups. Without (T), `Z` as the marked limit of `Z/p` already shows the phenomenon.
