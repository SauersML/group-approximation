title: Equivalent operator-MF formulations and stronger representation variants
---

For every countable group, the genuine norm-matrix-C-star-corona definition
of MF is equivalent to the unitary-sequence definition, to normalized local
operator-norm models with separation constant one, and to the variant with
strictly increasing positive matrix dimensions.  Each of PMatF, trace-free
PPermF, trace-PMF, PFF, and trace-inclusive PPF implies this MF property.

This hierarchy makes no identification with soficity or hyperlinearity.
