Let D be a unital C*-algebra and p a projection admitting a unital
representation rho:D->M_N(C) with 0<rank(rho(p))<N. In the full unital
free product C=C(S1)*D, let z be the universal circle unitary. Then

    ||[z,W p W*]|| = 1  for every W in U(C).

This holds for arbitrary mixed unitaries, including functional calculus
of linear combinations of words. In particular, it holds whenever D
is residually finite-dimensional and 0<p<1. Every unital algebra
generated by two projections has irreducible representations of
dimension at most two, so supplies this case.

By full-free-product inclusion, the same norm bound applies in A*T2
for any full-spectrum unitary v in A and unital D contained in T2.
For the LIX free-envelope candidate and p=s1 s1*, these conjugates
cannot satisfy the strict BRR commutator criterion, despite both
complementary projections being properly infinite and full in the
ambient algebra.

The finite-dimensional representation is a condition on D, not on
the whole Toeplitz algebra. This does not decide arbitrary Toeplitz
coefficients, survival of the free-envelope witness, or STW LXI.
