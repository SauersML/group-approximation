Citation import, not a reproof. Theorem 1 of M. G. Brin, *The ubiquity of Thompson's
group F in groups of piecewise linear homeomorphisms of the unit interval*, J. London
Math. Soc. (2) 60 (1999) 449–460, arXiv:math/9705205. The statement and the
definitions of orbital and of "approaches" were read verbatim from pages 1 and 3 of
the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-13.

The proof in the paper reduces to one orbital at a time and, in Lemma 2.1, produces
the copy of `F` from a pair `(g_0, g_1)` of elements of `H` satisfying the relations
of the presentation `⟨g_0, g_1 | g_1^{g_0 g_0} = g_1^{g_0 g_1}, g_1^{g_0 g_0 g_0} =
g_1^{g_0 g_0 g_1}⟩`, where the support of `g_1` is a proper subset of one orbital of
`g_0`. This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what the claim records.
