Artifact Section 1 proves that every positive-root subgroup for any
ordering of four indices injects into the elementary matrix group, by
ordered Steinberg root collection. The a,b pair has an acyclic support
of depth two, so its triple commutators vanish. The a,c and b,c pairs
have depth three, so their length-four relators vanish. For each latter
pair write C=1+P and the other generator as 1+Q. The support gives
P^2=Q^2=PQP=0 and [1+P,1+Q]=1+PQ-QP-QPQ, which commutes with 1+P.
The two remaining triple commutators therefore vanish. Each generator
is a product of commuting characteristic-seven root elements, so its
seventh power is one. These are precisely all eleven relators.

The lift restricts to K7 and provides a section of the pulled-back
stable Steinberg extension. Every central pushout retains that section.
