The first prerequisite gives the uniform lower singular value on the
Mayer--Vietoris quotient.  The square-free coordinate change and its inverse
are fixed word maps, so their differentials have universal normalized-HS
operator norms.  Transporting the quotient identifies exact-core tangent
motions with the gauge directions in `(GOI4)` and proves `(GIT1)`.  The last
prerequisite shows why this first-order conclusion does not integrate by a
dimension-free HS Taylor estimate, leaving exactly the nonlinear mechanisms
listed in `(GIT2)`.
