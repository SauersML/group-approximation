title: Threshold collapse forces exponential conditional pivotal counts at exponential near-critical scales
---

Theorem 6.1 and Corollary 6.2. Write lambda_0=-log rho. Collapse implies, for each 0<alpha<lambda_0/2 and all large n, some finite R_n>=n and t_n in (pc,pc+exp(-alpha*n)) with E[N|E]>=pc*(lambda_0-2alpha)*n*exp(alpha*n)/2. Consequently the liminf growth rate of 1+L_n(p0) is at least lambda_0/2 for every p0>pc. No useful bound on R_n is asserted.
