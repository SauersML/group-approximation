**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).**

1. **General firewall.** Let `P_0 ≤ Γ` be non-amenable and generated by a finite symmetric set `K`, with
   `h_K = |K|^{-1}Σ_{s∈K} s`. If operator-norm asymptotic representations `V_i` of `Γ` have unit vectors `ξ_i` with
   `‖V_i(s)ξ_i − ξ_i‖ → 0` for all `s ∈ K`, then `(V_i)` does not converge strongly to `λ_Γ`.
2. **Ring-induced models.**
   - **Setting.** Let `R` be exactly matricial, with unital maps `σ_i: R → M_{N_i}(F_i)` over finite fields that are
     injective and multiplicative on exhausting windows. Let `G = EL_n(R)` with `n ≥ 3`, and let `F_i` be the finite
     group generated by `σ_i` of the window in `GL_n(M_{N_i}(F_i))`.
   - **Hypothesis.** `Stab_G(e_1)` contains a finitely generated non-amenable subgroup.
   - **Conclusion.** For every unitary representation `π_i` of `F_i` that contains the nontrivial part of the
     permutation module on the orbit of `c_i = (1,0,…,0)^T`, `π_i∘σ_i` does not converge strongly to `λ_G`. This
     includes `λ_{F_i}` and `λ^0_{F_i}` (orthogonal to constants). The same holds for `G/Z`, with columns modulo
     `F_i^×`.
3. **When the hypothesis holds.**
   - For `n ≥ 4` and `R` finitely generated: `EL_{n−1}(R)` is infinite and Kazhdan.
   - For Pestov's ring with `n = 3`: `EL_2(F_q[u]) = SL_2(F_q[u])` contains a free group of rank 2 (Nagao).

This transplants the Magee–de la Salle obstruction for `SL_4(ℤ)`, invariant vectors for a non-amenable block, from
genuine representations to ring-induced asymptotic ones. It strengthens the constants remark in the Attempts of
`directly-finite-subshift-el-n-reduced-cstar-is-mf`.
