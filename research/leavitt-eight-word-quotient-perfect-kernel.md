For the explicitly defined quotient `Q=(C_3*C_2^3)/<<T_8>>`,

```text
Q=N semidirect C_2^3,
N=[Q,Q]=<b_x V b_x^-1:x in C_2^3>.
```

The compiled Thompson subgroup V is embedded, as authenticated by its
native prefix action in H, and `c=DA` belongs to it. The kernel N is
perfect and is exactly the intersection of all finite-dimensional
unitary representation kernels, also of all finite-quotient kernels.

Moreover `Q hyperlinear <=> N hyperlinear => V hyperlinear`. No
reverse implication from V is proved. A homomorphism of Q into a
finite permutation wreath product of copies of V retaining c exists
exactly when there is a homomorphism `N->V` retaining c; such a
homomorphism is not constructed here.

The artifact also proves that `SL_3(F_8)*_(S_3)V`, with its specified
finite-field c,b1,b2 core, has no group element b3 completing the test
packet: centralizing `<b1,b2>~=C_2^2` traps b3 in the finite vertex
group. This excludes that discrete amalgam construction only, not
arbitrary unitaries in its von Neumann algebra or approximate models.
