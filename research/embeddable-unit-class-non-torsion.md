Let `B` be a unital C\*-algebra whose unit class `[1_B]` is non-torsion
in `K_0(B)`, and let `D` be a unital C\*-algebra admitting a unital
*-homomorphism `D → B_ω` (norm ultrapower).  Then `[1_D]` is
non-torsion in `K_0(D)` — in particular `[1_D] ≠ 0`.

Consequences:

1. For `B = Q ⊗ O_∞` (`K_0 = Q`, `[1] = 1`): every unitally
   `(Q ⊗ O_∞)_ω`-embeddable unital algebra has non-torsion unit class.
   The `O_2`-exclusion in Kirchberg's embedding conjecture
   (`stw99-problem-xiv2-ssa-embeds-in-q-oinfty`) is thereby
   K-theoretically FORCED, not merely conventional: `[1_{O_2}] = 0`.
2. Combined with `ssa-kunneth-forces-solid-k-theory`: a strongly
   self-absorbing `D` satisfying the Künneth sequence against itself
   and embeddable as in STW Problem XV cannot have `K_0 = Z/n` (whose
   unit is torsion) nor `K_0 = 0`; among the Bousfield--Kan solid
   rings only `Z[J^{-1}]` and the products `Z[J^{-1}] × Z/n` (unit
   `(1,1)`, non-torsion) survive.  The XV residue is exactly the
   product solids.
3. Any hypothetical strongly self-absorbing algebra with torsion (or
   zero) unit class and `D ≇ O_2` would refute Problem XIV(2).
