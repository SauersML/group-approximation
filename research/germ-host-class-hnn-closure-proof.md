Lane proof (bh-free-20, 2026-09-18); not reviewed. Notation as in the claim. "§n of P"
refers to `last-section-f-infinity-tail-bh-proof`; its arguments are reused with base `V_d S`
replaced by an arbitrary `W ∈ 𝓒_d`.

## 1. Seeds

`B = V_d S` with `S` bounded:
- **(C1).** `B` is full and cone-closed, because `f^{(α)}` is again a cone map with local
  actions in `S`.
- **(C2).** BHM (proof of `thm:RoverNek`): `sing(B)` is a finite union of `V_d`-orbits of
  rational points, since singular rays of bounded automata are eventually periodic.
- **(C3).** This is the base case of §3 of P: BHM Appendix A transplanted to `V_{d,m}S`.

## 2. Hosts

`W` is `F_∞`: take `U = C` and `M = ∅` in (C3).

By (C2), choose a rational `q` whose tail class `O_q` avoids `sing(W)`.
- **Action.** Elements of `W` agree locally with `V_d` at the points of `O_q`, so `W` preserves
  `O_q` and is transitive on it. Since `W` is full, the action is highly transitive (BBMZ
  `prop:HighlyTransitiveAction`), and it is faithful because `O_q` is dense.
- **Stabilizers.** The pointwise stabilizer of any finite `M ⊆ O_q` is `Fix_{W_C}(M)`, which is
  `F_∞` by (C3), hence finitely generated.
- **Conclusion.** Zaremsky's theorem (BBMZ `thrm:action_to_simple`) gives a finitely presented
  simple `SV_W ⊇ W`. The action is of type (A), so `W` and its subgroups lie in `B_A`.

## 3. Closure

**A. A nonsingular block ray.** Primitive words `β ∈ X^m` up to rotation give pairwise
distinct tail classes `β^∞`, and their number is unbounded in `m`. By (C2), choose `m ≥ 1` and
a primitive `β ≠ L^m` whose tail class `O'` avoids `sing(W)`.

Put `λ = L^m` and `Φ = φ^m`. By induction on `m`, `Φ(g)` preserves `λC` and acts there as
`g^{(λ)}`, so `Φ` is cone-sectioned for `λ`.

`Φ(h)|_{C∖λC}` consists of the pieces `ψ(φ^{m−1−j}h)`, transported to `L^j(C∖LC)` for
`j < m`. So the tail sequences of `Φ` and of `φ` determine each other, and
`E_Φ(K) ≅ E_φ(K)` is `F_∞`.

**B. Embedding lemma over the block alphabet `Y = X^m`.** Fix a bijection
`b: Y∖{β} → Y∖{λ}`. Let `s ∈ V_{d^m} ≤ V_d` be the prefix replacement

```text
λu ↦ λλu,    βyv ↦ λ b(y) v  (y ≠ β),    xu ↦ xu  (x ∉ {β, λ}),    ββw ↦ βw.
```

Domain and image pieces are partitions of `C`, so `s` is a homeomorphism.

For `g ∈ K`, define the hat `ĝ`:
- on `λC` it acts as `g^{(λ)}`;
- on `xC` (`x ∉ {β, λ}`) it is the identity;
- on the slot `Σ_k = ⊔_{y≠β} β^{k+1}yC` it acts as `ν_k ψ_Φ(Φ^k g) ν_k^{-1}`, where
  `ν_k(zw) = β^{k+1}b^{-1}(z)w` and `ψ_Φ(h) = Φ(h)|_{C∖λC}`;
- it fixes `β^∞`.

The identity `sĝs^{-1} = \widehat{Φ(g)}` is checked piece by piece:
- on `λλC` both sides act as `g^{(λλ)}`;
- on `λ(C∖λC)`: `s∘ν_0 = (zw ↦ λzw)`, so the left side is `ψ_Φ(g)^{(λ)}`. That equals
  `Φ(g)^{(λ)}` there;
- on `Σ_k`, `k ≥ 0`: `s` maps `Σ_{k+1}` onto `Σ_k`, with `ν_k^{-1}sν_{k+1} = id`, so slot
  `k + 1` of `ĝ` becomes slot `k` of `\widehat{Φ(g)}`;
- the pieces `xC` and the point `β^∞` are fixed by both sides.

Injectivity of `K*_Φ → ⟨ĝ, s⟩` (`t ↦ s^{-1}`) follows from the nested clopen chain
`Z_j = s^{-j}(λC)`, exactly as in `last-section-ascending-hnns-embed-in-rover-nekrashevych-groups`.

Each piece of `ĝ` away from `β^∞` is in `W`: `g^{(λ)}` by (C1), and the slot pieces are
conjugates by `V_d`-elements extending `ν_k`. So `ĝ` is locally in `W` except at `β^∞`.

**C. The new host.** Let `W'` be the full closure of `⟨W, ĝ, s⟩`.
- **(C1).** `W'` is full and contains `V_d`. The germs of `ĝ` at `β^∞`, transported by the
  prefix maps `w ↦ αw`, are germs of `vĝv^{-1}` for suitable `v ∈ V_d`. So the germ groupoid
  of `W'` is prefix-closed, and `W'` is cone-closed.
- **(C2).** `sing(W') ⊆ sing(W) ∪ O'`.
- **(C3).** Repeat §1–§5 of P with base `W` in place of `V_d S` and the ray `β^∞` in place of
  `0^∞`:
  - `W'_U` is a finite germ extension of `W_U` with singular set `O' ∩ U`;
  - at `p' ∈ O'`, `W` is locally `V_d`, so its germ group is generated by the block dilation
    (β is primitive);
  - `(W')_{p'}` is generated by that dilation and the germs of the tail lift `ε(K) ≅ E_Φ(K)`,
    with `t^{-1}ε_g t = ε_{Φ(g)}·c_g`, and `c_g ∈ W` supported off the cone.

  The SingFix induction (§3 of P) has **base case exactly (C3) for `W`**. The step is an
  ascending HNN extension of `H × ε(K)`, and BHM `thm:SingFixFiniteness` gives
  `Fix_{W'_U}(M)` of type `F_∞`.

**D. From `φ^m` back to `φ`.** In `K*_φ`, the subgroup `N = ⟨K, t^m⟩` is the preimage of
`mZ` under the `t`-exponent map. So `N` is normal of index `m`, and `N ≅ K*_Φ`. By
Krasner–Kaloujnine, `K*_φ ↪ N ≀ Sym(m)`.

Take `m` disjoint cones `α_1 C, …, α_m C` with all `|α_i|` equal. Then `∏ N^{(α_i)}` and the
prefix replacements permuting the `α_i` give `N ≀ Sym(m) ≤ W'`, by (C1). So `K*_φ ↪ W'`. ∎
