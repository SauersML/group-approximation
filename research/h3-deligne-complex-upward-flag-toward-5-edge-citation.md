Citation import, not a reproof. Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX source of J. Huang and
P. Przytycki, arXiv:2509.06914, Theorem `thm:flag` [huang2024, Thm 7.1], verbatim: "Let
Λ=s_1s_2s_3 be the Coxeter diagram of type H_3 with m_{s_2s_3}=5, and ŝ_1<ŝ_2<ŝ_3. Then the
induced relation < on Δ_Λ^0 from Definition [def:order] is a partial order that is weakly
graded, bowtie free and upward flag."

In that paper's bibliography, `huang2024` is J. Huang, *Cycles in spherical Deligne complexes
and application to K(π,1)-conjecture for Artin groups*, arXiv:2405.12068. Its Theorem 7.1 was
not read directly. Huang–Przytycki use this theorem, with the `B_n` theorem of Haettel, to
verify Haettel's criterion for the almost spherical paths `434, 435, 535` (proof of Lemma
`lem:stpr`).
