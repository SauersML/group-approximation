Let `G` lie in the unpeelable class. Either some zero-exponent basis gives a
Magnus piece with an amenable envelope over `F_-` or `F_+`, and the first
prerequisite makes `G` sofic, or none does, and the second prerequisite
applies. The split is exhaustive. The second prerequisite covers strictly
fewer groups than the target: the nested-conjugator relators of the artifact,
§4, are unpeelable but have envelopes.
