**ESTABLISHED.** No novelty is claimed. The derivation is
`low-genus-closed-mcgs-embed-in-aut-free-groups-proof`.

For `g ∈ {0, 1, 2}`, the orientation-preserving mapping class group `MCG(S_g)`
of the closed orientable surface of genus `g`, and the extended mapping class
group, embed in some `Aut(F_n)`, hence in some `Out(F_m)`.

So both questions of Zaremsky Problem 3.10 about mapping class groups are open
only for `g ≥ 3`.
