title: Full finite-packet conjugation covariance cancels every router multiplicity action
distinct_from:
  right-regular-covariance-forces-one-common-router: that recovers a common multiplicity unitary from a special right-regular model; this needs no model or type selection once the complete conjugation table is imposed.
  second-layer-lookup-cancellation-no-go: that allows uncontrolled multiplicity actions on the constants; this shows that full conjugation covariance makes those actions harmless.
---

Let `K` be a fixed finite group generated by `S`, let `pi:K -> U(H)` be an
arbitrary finite-dimensional unitary representation, and fix `a in K`.  If a
unitary `C` satisfies

```text
max_(s in S) ||C pi(s) C^* - pi(a s a^(-1))||_2 <= eta,       (FCT1)
```

then, with `D=pi(a)^* C`,

```text
max_(s in S) ||[D,pi(s)]||_2 <= eta.                          (FCT2)
```

Consequently, for every fixed word `W(t)` in the router letter `t` and
constants from `K` whose exponent sum in `t` is zero, there is a computable
constant `L_(K,S,W)` such that

```text
||W(C)-pi(W(a))||_2 <= L_(K,S,W) eta.                         (FCT3)
```

The constant is independent of `dim(H)` and of the irreducible
multiplicities of `pi`.  In particular, if a finite truth-table construction
has

```text
W_F(a_x)=z or 1
```

on its selector-labelled routers and its group word `W_F` is balanced, then
full conjugation-table covariance forces the same output in every
representation and every external multiplicity.  There is no second-layer
lookup action left to return.

The remaining compiler problem is therefore sharply separated: encode the
selector-dependent automorphism `Ad(a_x)` by ordinary group relations.  This
claim does not solve that lookup.  In particular, replacing the full table by
only scalar sign covariances reopens the arbitrary multiplicity action ruled
out by `second-layer-lookup-cancellation-no-go`.
