With `x=b,y=B^(-1)`, Lemma 2.2 says `xyx=yxy`. Thus `w=xyx` satisfies
`wx=yw` and `wy=xw`; squaring centralizes `x,y`. If `xyx` and `yxy` differ
by `epsilon`, the two intertwining identities each have defect `epsilon`,
and two triangle inequalities give the `2epsilon` bound for `w^2` and
`4epsilon` for `w^4`.
