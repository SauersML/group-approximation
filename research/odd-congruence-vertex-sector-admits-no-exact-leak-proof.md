Use Serre's splitting `Gamma = A_1 *_E A_2` with `A_1 = SL_2(Z)`,
`A_2 = gamma_0 A_1 gamma_0^(-1)`, `gamma_0 = diag(2, 1/2)`, and
`E = Gamma_0(2)` glued to `Gamma^0(2)` by `phi = Ad(gamma_0)`.  Assume
`rho_0 = sigma tensor 1_N` on `A_1` with `sigma` factoring through
`SL_2(Z/m)`, `m` odd (sum over isotypes is handled identically,
blockwise).

**1. Edge fullness at odd level.**  `E` contains `Lambda(2)`, and
`Lambda(2)` surjects onto `SL_2(Z/m)` for odd `m` by strong
approximation; likewise the core of `phi(E)` in `A_2`.  So the edge
already has full congruence image on both sides.

**2. The second vertex is congruence with a multiplicity twist.**
`U|_(A_2)` is an approximate representation of `A_2 ~ SL_2(Z)`; by
vertex stability it is close to an exact `rho_1`.  Edge agreement plus
step 1 makes `rho_1` on the full-image subgroup `phi(E)` close to
`sigma' tensor 1_N` with `sigma' = sigma` twisted by `Ad(gamma_0 mod m)`,
which is INNER (`diag(2, 2^(-1)) in SL_2(Z/m)` since `2` is a unit).
For a coset generator `a` of `A_2` over `phi(E)`, conjugation fixes the
core of `phi(E)` and the uniform `(tau)` conjugation gap of
`sl2-z-inverse-p-over-sl2-z-is-codense-tau-pair` squeezes
`rho_1(a) approx sigma(a mod m) tensor Y_a`: at odd level the only
freedom is a multiplicity twist `a -> Y_a`.

**3. The twist factors through a trivial quotient.**  Approximate
multiplicativity of `rho_1` forces `a -> Y_a` to be an approximate
homomorphism `A_2 -> U(N)` that is approximately trivial on `phi(E)`.
Any such map descends approximately to `A_2 / <<phi(E)>>`.  But the
normal closure of `Gamma^0(2)` in `SL_2(Z)` is everything: modulo
`Lambda(2)` the question is whether the normal closure of an order-two
subgroup of `S_3 = SL_2(Z/2)` generated by a transposition-type element
is all of `S_3`, and it is.  Each generator of `A_2` is a fixed
bounded-length product of conjugates of `phi(E)`-elements, so
`Y_a approx 1` with constants depending only on those fixed words
(Gowers--Hatami handles the residual finite-group wobble).  Hence
`U|_(A_2) approx sigma-congruence tensor 1_N` too: both vertex images
are multiplicity-rigid.

**4. No leak.**  Every element of `Gamma` is a word in `A_1 cup A_2`,
so every `U(gamma)` is HS-pinned to `sigma(gamma mod m) tensor 1_N`.
Any `K` almost-commuting with `U(Lambda)` is close to `1 tensor W`
(irreducible `sigma`; blockwise in general), and then

```text
tau(K* U(gamma) K U(gamma)*) -> 1
```

for every `gamma`, because `sigma(gamma mod m) tensor 1_N` commutes
with `1 tensor W` exactly.  The exact profile `(EQL1)` demands the
value `0` at any `gamma notin Lambda` — contradiction.  All estimates
are quantitative with constants from the `(tau)` gap, the
Gowers--Hatami constant of the fixed finite groups, and the fixed
amalgam word lengths; no constant depends on `d_m`, `N`, or `m`.
