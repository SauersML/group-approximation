Use the expansion `(CPR5)`.  The first and third terms are bounded by the
two exit-transport errors because all exit maps are contractions.  The
middle term is bounded by the localized involution defect and then by
`||X-X^*||_F=||X^2-1||_F`.  The BS moment formula is `(GS12)`.
