**ESTABLISHED COEFFICIENT/GROUP SEPARATION FIREWALL.**  First, full binary
completeness has no nonzero finite-dimensional unital coefficient-algebra
model.  If `E` is finite-dimensional and

```text
t_i s_j=delta_(ij)1,                 i,j in {0,1},      (FCO1)
```

then `t_0s_0=1` makes left multiplication by `s_0`
injective, hence bijective.  Thus `s_0` is a unit and `t_0=s_0^(-1)`.
The crossed equation `t_0s_1=0` then gives `s_1=0`, contradicting
`t_1s_1=1`.  In particular no matrix coefficient specialization can satisfy
the full inverse/cross-zero table, even before using

```text
s_0t_0+s_1t_1=1.                                      (FCO2)
```

This does **not** make the corresponding bounded group-occurrence menu
collapse.  Let `K` be the finite subgroup of `GL_5(F_2)` generated by the
standard `UT_5(F_2)` payload roots and the opposite long root `x_51(1)`.
It contains

```text
z=x_15(1),
L=[x_12(1),[x_23(1),[x_34(1),x_45(1)]]]=z,
W=x_15(1)x_51(1)x_15(1),                              (FCO3)
```

together with the crossed-zero and shared-arm role copies from the
depth-two cousin packet.  All private-arm opposite-root and Whitehead rows
are literal finite matrix identities in `K`.

Work in the finite semidirect product

```text
G=(K^3) semidirect S_3,                                (FCO4)
```

where `S_3` permutes the three copies.  For every named base occurrence
`g in K`, put

```text
g^J=(1,g,g),       g^0=(g,g,1),       g^1=(g,1,g).    (FCO5)
```

Coordinatewise multiplication preserves every noncommuting payload,
crossed-zero, shared-arm, opposite-root, and Whitehead identity.  Their
long-root outputs are

```text
J =(1,z,z),
C_0=(z,z,1),
C_1=(z,1,z),                                        (FCO6)
```

and satisfy

```text
C_0C_1=J.                                             (FCO7)
```

The transpositions `(0 2)` and `(0 1)` carry the entire `J`-packet to the
`C_0`- and `C_1`-packets arm by arm, including `x_51(1)` and `W`.  Thus the
full second branch and completeness row live in the **same finite fused
opposite-root packet**, not in an independent scalar factor.

The mark `J` is nonidentity.  Hence the left regular representation of `G`
makes every row in the complete menu exact while

```text
||lambda_G(J)-1||_2=sqrt(2).                           (FCO8)
```

Therefore impossibility of a finite coefficient-algebra model does not by
itself give the bounded-area chromatic or atomic MF collapse.  Group
occurrences can distribute the three coefficient objects among finite chart
copies and fuse only their long-root outputs.  The missing row must force
the three packets onto one coefficient/multiplicity reservoir, rather than
merely transport every named arm coherently between conjugate copies.  No
Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, approximation theorem, trace hypothesis, or
literature result is used.

## Attempts

- **Use finite coefficient direct-finiteness.**  Equations `(FCO1)` already
  contradict finite-dimensional unital coefficients, but the group model
  `(FCO4)--(FCO8)` shows that the decoded coefficient operators have not
  been placed on one finite corner.
- **Attach the opposite long root before fusing completeness.**  Each copy
  of `K` contains it literally; the coordinate permutations simply carry
  the entire opposite-root packet to the two child objects.
- **Right-compose the full fold by each `s_j` and left-compose by each
  `t_i`.**  This destroys the three-sign model by introducing two
  intermediate arm labels, but not finiteness.  The four-bit transvection
  model in `full-fold-compose-return-has-finite-affine-model` satisfies both
  matching returns and both crossed-zero returns with the same named
  actors.
