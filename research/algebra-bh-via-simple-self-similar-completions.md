Let `A` be finitely generated with solvable word problem.
`decidable-algebras-embed-in-simple-self-similar-completions` gives a free `B`, a recursion `ψ` and
`A ⊆ O_ψ(B)`, with `B/J` ψ̄-minimal and aperiodic. By
`noninjective-ln-completions-have-self-similar-bases`, parts 3 and 4, `O_ψ(B)` is a
finitely presented simple algebra. `∎`
