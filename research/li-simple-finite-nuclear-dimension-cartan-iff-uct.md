**ESTABLISHED (literature).**  X. Li, *Every classifiable simple C\*-algebra has
a Cartan subalgebra*, arXiv:1802.01190, Corollary 1.4:

> A unital separable simple C\*-algebra with finite nuclear dimension has a
> Cartan subalgebra if and only if it satisfies the UCT.

Li derives it from his Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 (Cartan subalgebras in all
classifiable stably finite algebras), the classification theorems, Spielberg's
groupoid models for UCT Kirchberg algebras, and Barlak--Li Theorem 3.1.  He
restricts to the unital case only because classification in the stably
projectionless case was not complete.  The abstract states the existence half:
every classifiable simple C\*-algebra has a Cartan subalgebra.

**Bearing on the UCT problem.**  Among unital simple algebras of finite nuclear
dimension, a counterexample to the UCT is the same thing as an algebra with no
Cartan subalgebra.  For trivial-K-theory Kirchberg algebras the same equivalence
is derived directly, without a nuclear dimension input, in
`uct-cartan-trivial-k-kirchberg-uct-iff-cartan`.
