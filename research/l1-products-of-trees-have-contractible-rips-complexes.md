Let `T_1, …, T_k` be simplicial trees with unit edges and uniformly bounded
degrees, and let `X = V(T_1) × ⋯ × V(T_k)` with the l^1 metric
`d_1 = Σ_i d_{T_i}`. Then `VR_t(X)` is contractible for every integer
`t ≥ k(k+1) − 1`.
