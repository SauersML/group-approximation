## Statement

Assume (H_Ω), that `X_Ω^K` is `D_Ω`-quantum rigid over `k`, and that `Y` is `D_Y`-quantum rigid over `k`
and corner-deterministic. Then `Y_Δ` is `D`-quantum rigid over `k` for some `D`.

## Partial result (lane proof sketch, not reviewed)

Let `E_{(g,t)}(v)` be a `D`-quantum family for `Y_Δ` on `W_0`, with `D ≫ D_Ω, D_Y, K`. Write
`P_g(v) = Σ_t E_{(g,t)}(v)` for the germ marginals and `T_t(v) = Σ_g E_{(g,t)}(v)` for the tile marginals.

1. **Germs are classical.** `(P_g(v))` satisfies Q1–Q3 for `X_Ω^K`, since forbidden germ patterns are
   forbidden in `Y_Δ` whatever the tiles. So the germ marginals commute, by rigidity of `X_Ω^K`.
2. **Centrality implies transfer.** Suppose (C): every `P_g(v)` commutes with every `E_{(g',t)}(u)`.
   Then `E_{(g,t)}(v) = P_g(v) T_t(v)`, and for finite-dimensional `W_0` the family commutes:
   - Take a minimal idempotent `π` of the finite-dimensional commutative algebra generated by the germ
     marginals. On `π W_0` every `P_g(v)` is `0` or `I`, and Q3 forces the resulting germ field to be
     locally legal. By (H_Ω) it is `x_c` for a unique `c`.
   - (C) makes `π W_0` invariant.
   - Quantum R3 follows from Q1–Q3 as in the lift proof of `deterministic-time-lifts-inherit-quantum-rigidity`:
     `π T_t(v) = Σ_{G(t_1,…,t_r)=t} ∏_i π T_{t_i}(p_{Me_i}(v))`.
   - Iterating this `k` times and using the coherence argument of the transplant node gives
     **operator coherence**: `π T_t(v) = π T_t(v')` whenever `b_c(v) = b_c(v')`.
   - So `F_t(z) := π T_t(v)` for `b_c(v) = Mz` is well defined. It is a `D_Y`-quantum family for `Y` on
     `π W_0`, taking `D` large enough that coordinates at distance `2D_Y` have representatives in a common
     sector at building distance `≤ 2D`.
   - By rigidity of `Y` it commutes. Summing over `π` shows all `T`, hence all `E`, commute.
   - Arbitrary `W_0` should follow by the compactness/finite-presentation form of rigidity
     (`sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`). This is not written out.
3. **What is open.** Statement (C). Rigidity of `X_Ω^K` makes the germ marginals commute with each other,
   not with the tiles. A counterexample to the Statement must be a family in which the germ layer and
   the tile layer are entangled across far sites.

## Why it matters

The Statement, together with (H_Ω) and rigidity of `X_Ω`, would turn one corner-deterministic, rigid,
strongly aperiodic `Z²`-SFT into a quantum-rigid **free** SFT over a Kazhdan, one-ended, non-amenable
lattice. That is gate G2 of `research/artifacts/gq-bh-synthesis-master-route.md` outside the amenable
world, with minimality still separate.

## Lesson for general BH

- **Where transfer can break.** Rigidity transfer through a geometric decoration fails only through
  entanglement between layers: the base layer (germs) and the decoration layer (tiles).
- **What fixes it.** Determinism fixes coherence. Classical rigidity of the base alone does not fix
  entanglement.
- **Design target.** Make each germ projection a polynomial in the tile projections at the same site.
  Then (C) reduces to tile–tile commutation. This is not yet a proof: the coherence step itself
  conditions on germs, so the reduction is circular as it stands.
