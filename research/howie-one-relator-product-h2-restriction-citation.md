Citation import, second hand. The source read was J. Howie and H. Short,
*Coherence of one-relator products of locally indicable groups*,
arXiv:2001.01627v2, file `Coherent1relatorProdsArXivV2.tex`, fetched from
arXiv e-print on 2026-09-17. TeX macros are stripped in the quotes.

- **l.278–285:** "Theorem [H2, Theorem 3]. Let G=(*_{λ∈Λ} G_λ)/<<R^n>> be a
  one-relator product of locally indicable groups G_λ, where R is cyclically
  reduced of length at least 2 and not a proper power in *_λ G_λ and n≥1. Let C
  be the cyclic subgroup of G generated by R, and let M be a ZG-module. Then the
  restriction maps H^k(G;M) → H^k(C;M)×∏_λ H^k(G_λ;M) are isomorphisms for k>2
  and an epimorphism for k=2."
- **l.1315–1316:** "[H2] J. Howie, Cohomology of one-relator products of
  locally indicable groups, J. London Math. Soc. 30 (1984), 419--430."

**Trust surface.** The 1984 paper itself was not read. The statement and its
attribution come from Howie--Short, and Howie wrote both papers.
