**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, not reviewed). Two classical inputs are recalled, not re-read,
for `R = Z[1/P]` and `n ≥ 3`:
- Bass–Milnor–Serre: `SL_n(R, ℓ) = E_n(R, ℓ)`, since `R` is a ring of S-integers of `Q`, which
  has a real place.
- Relative elementary generation (Vaserstein–Suslin, Tits): `E_n(R, ℓ)` is generated by
  `x_ji(c) x_ij(t) x_ji(−c)` with `t ∈ ℓR` and `c ∈ R`.

Let `ℓ ∉ P` be prime. Since `R ⊆ Z_ℓ`, `Γ^P` (`s-integral-affine-structure-group-is-finitely-presented`)
acts on `X = Z_ℓ^D`. An **ℓ-splitting direction** is an open subgroup `H` of index `ℓ`, cut out
by a finitely supported functional `φ ∈ F_ℓ^(D)`, `φ ≠ 0`. Only the line `F_ℓ φ` matters. Let
`H = ker(e_{d_0}^*)` and `Q = {γ ∈ Γ^P : γ permutes the cosets of H}`.

**Claim.** `Q` is finitely generated.

**Proof.** It is the proof of `integral-affine-halving-stabilizers-are-finitely-generated`, with
these changes.
- `Q = R^(D) ⋊ Q_lin` still holds.
- The kernel of `Q_lin → F` is
  `P = {M : row d_0 of M ≡ λ e_{d_0}^T (mod ℓ), λ ∈ F_ℓ^×}`.
- `P` is generated by:
  - `x_ab(1)` and `x_ab(1/p)` for `a ≠ d_0`;
  - `x_{d_0 b}(ℓ)` for `b ≠ d_0`;
  - the diagonal `δ_{u,d}` for `u ∈ {−1} ∪ P` and all `d`.
- For the congruence kernel, use BMS and relative generation over `R`. The case `j = d_0` uses
  the same commutator trick, now with `x_{k d_0}(1)` and `t ∈ ℓR`.
- The level-`ℓ` quotient is the stabilizer of a line in `GL_n(F_ℓ)`, intersected with the image
  of `GL_n(R)`. It is generated by the images of the `x_ab(1)` with `a ≠ d_0` and of the diagonals.
- `x_{d_0 b}(ℓ r)` for `r ∈ R` is obtained by conjugating `x_{d_0 b}(ℓ)` with `δ_{p,b}^{±1}` and
  adding. So finitely many `F_{d_0}`-orbits of generators suffice, as before. ∎
