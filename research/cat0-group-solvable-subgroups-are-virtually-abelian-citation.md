Citation import. M. R. Bridson and A. Haefliger, *Metric Spaces of Non-Positive
Curvature*, Grundlehren 319, Springer 1999, Part II, Chapter 7, Theorem 7.8
("Solvable Subgroup Theorem"): if `Γ` acts properly and cocompactly by isometries
on a CAT(0) space, then every solvable subgroup of `Γ` is finitely generated and
virtually abelian. Checked on 2026-09-17 against the statement as quoted in the
literature (search of the book and of Bridson's ICM survey
https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/bridson/papers/bridsonicm.pdf); the page of the
book itself was not opened in this session, so the theorem number should be
re-verified by a referee. The statement is classical and widely cited.
