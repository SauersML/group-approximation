Section S3 of the artifact. `K` is a `K(X, 1)` for `X = pi_1(K)`, so
`H_2(X) = H_2(K)`. For the pair `(K, K(Q,1) v S^1)`, `H_3(K, B) = H_3(S^2) = 0`,
so `H_2(Q) = H_2(B) -> H_2(K)` is injective, and it is the coefficient map.
