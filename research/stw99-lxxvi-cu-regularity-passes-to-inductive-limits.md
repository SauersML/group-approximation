title: Cuntz semigroup regularity passes to sequential C*-inductive limits
---

Let `(A_n,phi_n)` be a C\*-inductive system and let `A=lim_n A_n`.  If every
first-factor map

```text
Cu(A_n) -> Cu(A_n tensor_min Z)
```

is an isomorphism, then so is

```text
Cu(A) -> Cu(A tensor_min Z).
```

Thus any sequential inductive limit of Cuntz semigroup regular algebras is Cuntz
semigroup regular; no injectivity of the connecting maps is required.
