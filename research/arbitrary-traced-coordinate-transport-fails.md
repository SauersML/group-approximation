There exist unital C-star coordinates with faithful tracial states for which
the proposed one-sided Kazhdan transport conclusion fails, even for an exact
representation.

Take every coordinate to be `A=C*_r(E)` with its faithful canonical trace and
take the exact left-regular representation of the literal group `E`.  The
lamp `c` centralizes the Kazhdan base, but after conjugation by `t` the element
`d=tct^-1` has

```text
u=[d,v_1],   u^2=w != 1.
```

If arbitrary-faithful-trace transport were true, exact centrality of `c`
would force `||[d,v_1]||_2=0`; faithfulness would give `u=1`, contradicting
`w!=1`.

The abstract operator-algebra failure is the same: for an infinite factor
`M` in standard form, the algebra generated by left and right multiplication
is `M or M'=B(L^2M)`, not a finite algebra.  Thus faithful traciality of the
coordinate algebra is not the missing comparison hypothesis.
