**ESTABLISHED.**  Take the data of
`affine-perfect-lamp-wreath-has-locally-finite-mf-radical`:
- `P = Gamma(3) ltimes Z^3`;
- `H <= GL_4(Z[1/2])`, the ascending HNN extension of `P` by
  `t = diag(2I, 1)`;
- `X = H/P`;
- the height `ht : X -> Z`, from `2^h`.

Put `Lambda = Gamma(3)` and form the restricted permutational wreath product

```text
W = Lambda^(X) semidirect H.
```

Then:

1. `W` is finitely generated, torsion-free and sofic, and it is not MF.
2. Its MF radical is

   ```text
   Rad_MF(W) = { f in Lambda^(X) :
                 prod_(ht(x)=r) f(x) in [Lambda,Lambda]  for every r in Z }.
   ```

   The product is taken in any order, since the condition is modulo
   `[Lambda,Lambda]`.  In particular,

   ```text
   [Lambda,Lambda]^(X) <= Rad_MF(W) <= Lambda^(X),
   ```

   and the universal MF quotient is `(Lambda_ab)^(Z) semidirect H`, which is
   residually finite.  Here `Lambda_ab` is finite.
3. `Rad_MF(W)` contains infinite, torsion-free Kazhdan subgroups:
   - `[Lambda,Lambda]` at any single site;
   - for two distinct sites `x, y` of equal height, the index-`|Lambda_ab|`
     subgroup `{(a,b) : ab in [Lambda,Lambda]}` of `Lambda_x x Lambda_y`.
     It projects **onto** the whole lamp `Lambda` at `x`.

**Why it matters.**  It refutes a tempting heuristic behind attacks on
`torsion-free-sofic-exact-mf-radical-over-z`: that the MF radical of a sofic
torsion-free group cannot contain an infinite Kazhdan subgroup.  That
heuristic generalizes `literal-mf-radical-has-no-infinite-kazhdan-subgroup`.

Any obstruction to the flagship must therefore use more than the following
properties of `K = Rad_MF(G)`:

```text
G sofic, torsion-free, f.g.;  K contains an infinite Kazhdan subgroup.
```

It must use at least one of:
- that `K` is itself Kazhdan, hence finitely generated;
- that `G/K ≅ Z`;
- finite presentation.

In `W` all three fail:
- the radical is an infinitely generated restricted sum;
- the quotient is the non-amenable group `(Lambda_ab)^(Z) semidirect H`;
- `W` is only finitely generated.

**Mechanism.**  The lamp group `C = Lambda_(x0)` at the base site
centralizes `P`.  Its conjugates by `t` and by `ell t`, with
`ell = (1, e_1)`, are lamps at the two distinct sites `t x0 != ell t x0`,
so they commute.  `kazhdan-centralizer-commuting-conjugates-kill-derived-subgroup`
(KC2) then kills `[C,C]`.

The lamp group is torsion-free, so torsion collapse
(`wreath-compression-mf-radical`) gives nothing on it.  The lamp group is
infinite and not locally finite, so the locally finite criterion does not
apply either.

The sofic kill (KC3) does not interfere.  The compressor group `<P,t> = H`
maps onto `Z`, so it is not Kazhdan.  This is the same escape that the
sharpness remark of `sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects` names.

**Companion with torsion (remark).**  With the lamp `SL_3(Z)`, which is
perfect and generated by torsion, the torsion-collapse theorem already gives
`Rad_MF = SL_3(Z)^(X)` exactly.  The same soficity proof applies.  That
group has torsion.
