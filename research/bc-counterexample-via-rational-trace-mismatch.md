**Dead.**
- The proposal compares the trace image `tau_*(K_0(C*_r L^x))` with Lück's
  bound `Lambda^(L^x)` on traces of assembly images, and looks for a rational
  value outside that ring.
- `leavitt-unit-group-contains-every-finite-group` gives `Lambda^(L^x) = Q`,
  so there is no rational value outside it.

Only an irrational trace survives, and nothing in the graph produces one.
