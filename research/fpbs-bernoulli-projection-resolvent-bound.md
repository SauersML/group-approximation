title: The averaged Bernoulli cluster projection is bounded by an explicit graph resolvent

For every countable bounded-degree graph and 0<p<1, its averaged
finite-cluster projection satisfies Q_p <= [I+p L_G/(2(1-p))]^{-1}.
In particular, for a nonamenable d-regular Cayley graph with nonlazy walk
norm r<1, ||Q_p||<=2(1-p)/(2(1-p)+p d(1-r))<1, including at criticality.
Sections 2-3 prove the finite inverse differential comparison and the
strong-limit extension. No infinite-volume derivative is asserted.
