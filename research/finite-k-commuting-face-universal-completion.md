OPEN.  Find a fixed finite nonabelian group `K`, generated by involutions,
and finitely many bounded commuting product-one tuples such that their
universal group is exactly `K`.  Equivalently, `K` must be the universal
completion of the diagram of elementary abelian `2`-subgroups selected by
the faces, with no extra infinite kernel.

Then couple a positive-density repetition of those K labels to good-code
columns `a_i`, arrange every face to have zero A-sum, and prove that the
resulting universal group is exactly `K x F_2^r`.  The columns must also pass
the finite twisted-intertwiner distance criterion of
`fixed-k-code-product-gap-is-a-twisted-intertwiner-test`.

`s3-cannot-be-a-homogeneous-parity-face-factor` shows that nonabelianity and
involution generation alone are insufficient.  The load-bearing resource is
a finite **abelian-subgroup amalgam with finite universal completion**.

## Attempts

- **`K=S_3`.**  Refuted by
  `s3-cannot-be-a-homogeneous-parity-face-factor`: distinct transpositions do
  not commute, so every face relation remains valid in a free product of
  involution colors.
- **Repeat fixed K labels at positive density.**  This can improve the finite
  packet's empirical spectral weight but does not change the universal
  completion; an infinite kernel in the abelian-subgroup amalgam survives
  every repetition.
- **Use code distance alone for the product gap.**  Insufficient in
  nontrivial K sectors.  The exact replacement is the finite twisted-
  intertwiner condition of
  `fixed-k-code-product-gap-is-a-twisted-intertwiner-test`.
