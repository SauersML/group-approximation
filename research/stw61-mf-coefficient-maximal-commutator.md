Let D be a separable unital MF C*-algebra and 0<p<1 a projection.
In the full unital free product C=C(S1)*D, with circle generator z,

```text
||[z,W p W*]||=1   for every W in U(C).
```

More generally, it suffices that D admit a unital homomorphism to
a norm matrix-product quotient detecting both p and 1-p; that
homomorphism need not be faithful. The proof does not require
finite-dimensional representations or coordinatewise homomorphic
lifts of D.

Full-free-product inclusion gives the same statement for a
full-spectrum v in A, a unital MF subalgebra D of T2 containing p,
and all W in U(C*(v,D)) inside A*T2. This excludes the strict BRR
commutator criterion for these coefficient algebras. It does not
settle unrestricted Toeplitz coefficients or STW LXI.
