Proof in Section 6 of the part 2 artifact.

1. **`H` on `Omega`.** For `h != 1` some edge `e` of `Cay(G,S)` has `h e != e`, and the base is
   nonatomic, so `mu(Fix h) = 0`. Edge stabilizers in `G` have order at most two, so `H`-orbits on `E` are
   infinite and `H ↷ Omega` is mixing.
2. **Subrelation.** `R^H_p`, generated by moves along open `S_H`-edges, is contained in `R_p`.
3. **Uniqueness.** The open `S_H`-edges of the coset `H` are Bernoulli bond percolation on `Cay(H,S_H)`.
   For `p > p_u(Cay(H,S_H))` there is one infinite cluster there (`fpbs-cluster-count-trichotomy`). So
   `R^H_p` restricted to `{1 in an infinite S_H-cluster}` is the orbit relation of `H ↷ Omega` restricted
   to that set.
4. **Permanence.**
   - The orbit relation of the free action of the nonsofic group `H` is nonsofic (part 1, Lemma 1.4).
   - Hence `R^H_p` is nonsofic, by restriction and amplification (Lemmas 1.2--1.3, the steps of
     `uniqueness-phase-cluster-relations-inherit-nonsoficity`).
   - A sofic `R_p` would make its subrelation `R^H_p` sofic (Lemma 1.1).
