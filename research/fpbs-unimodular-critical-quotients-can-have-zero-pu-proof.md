Section 5 explicitly imports Angel--Hutchcroft, arXiv:1710.03003,
Theorem 1.4, Proposition 4.2 and Lemma 4.3, and Aldous--Lyons,
arXiv:math/0603062, Theorem 8.11. On an ergodic component the latter gives
finite critical cells. Repeat the path/cut lifting and the 1/(m(C)m(D))
mass transport of Sections 1-2; neither calculation needs transitivity.
The resulting Palm law has finite first moments and preserves independent
sprinkling. Conditional uniqueness at the countable set of positive
rational sprinkling parameters makes the infimum uniqueness threshold
zero almost surely. The transitive critical susceptibility theorem is
not used. This is a written deduction over the specified published inputs.
