title: Compact Cuntz classes over stably finite algebras are projection classes
---

Let `A` be a stably finite C-star algebra. Every compact element
`x<<x` of `Cu(A)` is represented by a projection in `A tensor K`.

Equivalently, under the Hilbert-module model of `Cu(A)`, a countably
generated module represents a compact class exactly when it is algebraically
finitely generated and projective.
