**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`deligne-covers-of-sp2g-z-have-finite-residual-2z-citation`).

Let `g >= 2` and `n >= 3`. Let `G_n -> Sp_(2g)(R)` be the connected cover of
degree `n`, `Gamma_n` the preimage of `Sp_(2g)(Z)`, and `z` a generator of the
covering kernel `C_n = <z>`. Then

```text
1 -> C_n -> Gamma_n -> Sp_(2g)(Z) -> 1
```

is a central extension, `Gamma_n` is not residually finite, and

```text
Res_fin(Gamma_n) = <z^2>,                                   (DR2)
```

a cyclic group of order `n / gcd(n, 2)`. For odd `n` the whole kernel is
invisible to finite quotients. For even `n` exactly the subgroup of index two in
`C_n` is invisible, which is consistent with residual finiteness of the
metaplectic double cover.

`Gamma_n` is the quotient of the universal-cover preimage `Gamma~` by `<z^n>`,
since `G_n = G~ / nZ`. So `Res_fin(Gamma~)` is contained in the kernel `Z` and
contains `2Z`. The displayed equality for `Gamma_n` is as stated in the source.
It is used here only through the inclusion `<z^2> <= Res_fin(Gamma_n)`.

The source is a survey quoting Deligne's note. Deligne's note itself was not
read; this matches how `deligne-universal-cover-lattice-is-non-rf-kazhdan-citation`
uses it.
