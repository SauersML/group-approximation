**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `G` be a group, `Gamma <= G`, and `t in G` with
`t Gamma t^-1 <= Gamma`.  Put

```text
H = <Gamma, t>,        Lambda = union_(k>=0) t^-k Gamma t^k.
```

Then:

1. `Lambda` is an increasing union of conjugates of `Gamma`, normal in `H`,
   with `H/Lambda` cyclic;
2. `H` is hyperlinear if and only if `Gamma` is, and `H` is sofic if and only
   if `Gamma` is;
3. if `Gamma` is finitely generated and `t Gamma t^-1 != Gamma`, then
   `Lambda` is not finitely generated and `H` has no property (T).

Consequently every approximation-theoretic obstruction whose hypotheses hold
for one compressor and the compressed subgroup, with no property (T) of the
group they generate, is an obstruction for `Gamma` itself.  This applies to
every compression triple in the graph: OpenAI's Leavitt configuration, the
Kun--Thom pairs, and the Fournier--Facio skeleton.
