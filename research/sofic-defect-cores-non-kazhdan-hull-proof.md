Notation as in the target claim.

**Imported criterion** (`sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects`,
ESTABLISHED, verbatim):

> Let `H` be a countable sofic group, and let `Gamma <= G <= H` with `Gamma`
> and `G` Kazhdan and `Gamma` infranormal in `G`. Then
> `[g z g^-1, gamma] = 1` for all `g in G`, `z in C_H(Gamma)`, `gamma in Gamma`.

**Definition used** (Kun--Thom, arXiv:2608.06222, verbatim in
`research/artifacts/kun-thom-2608-06222-verified.md`): `Gamma` is infranormal
in `G` if the semigroup `P_Gamma = {g in G : g Gamma g^-1 <= Gamma}` generates
`G`.

**Step 1 (H1).** Let `G <= K` be Kazhdan, with `L <= G` infranormal in `G`.
Apply the criterion with `H = K` and `Gamma = L`. For `g in G` and
`z in C_K(L)` we get `[g z g^-1, l] = 1` for every `l in L`, that is
`g z g^-1 in C_K(L)`. This holds for `g` and for `g^-1`, so
`G <= N_K(C_K(L))`. Now `c in C_K(L)` and `[t c t^-1, l_0] != 1`, so
`t c t^-1 notin C_K(L)`. Hence `t notin N_K(C_K(L))`, so `t notin G`.

**Step 2 (the hull is the maximal compressor-generated subgroup).** Let
`L <= G <= K` and `P_L^G = {x in G : x L x^-1 <= L}`. Then
`P_L^G = P_L ∩ G`. So `L` is infranormal in `G` exactly when
`G = <P_L ∩ G>`, and every such `G` lies in `Hull_K(L)`. For `G = Hull_K(L)`
we have `P_L ∩ G = P_L`, which generates `G`, and `L <= P_L` because
`l L l^-1 = L`. So `L` is infranormal in `Hull_K(L)`.

**Step 3 (H2).** `t in P_L` because `t L t^-1 <= L`, and `c in P_L` because
`c L c^-1 = L`. Take any `G` with `L u {t} <= G <= Hull_K(L)` and
`G = <P_L ∩ G>`. By Step 2, `L` is infranormal in `G` and `t in G`. If `G`
were Kazhdan, Step 1 would give `t notin G`, a contradiction. So `G` is not
Kazhdan. The cases `G = Hull_K(L)` and `G = <L, t, c>` are included, since
both are generated by elements of `P_L`.

**Step 4 (H3).** If `K` is Kazhdan and `L` were infranormal in `K`, then
Step 1 with `G = K` would give `t notin K`, which is absurd. So `L` is not
infranormal in `K`, and `Hull_K(L) != K` by Step 2. It is not Kazhdan by
Step 3. If `K = <x_1, ..., x_k>` with every `x_i in P_L`, then
`Hull_K(L) = K`, a contradiction.

**Step 5 (Corollary).** `P = Gamma(3) ltimes Z^3` has property (T)
(`affine-congruence-source-is-kazhdan`, ESTABLISHED; Lean
`LiteralAffineCongruenceBase.p_hasKazhdanPropertyT`). Property (T) passes to
quotients, so `Gamma = q(P)` is Kazhdan. In `E` we have `t P t^-1 = alpha(P)
<= P` and `[c, P] = 1`, and these relations survive in `Q`. By hypothesis
`[q(t) q(c) q(t)^-1, q(p_0)] = q(w) != 1`. So `(q(P), q(t), q(c))` is a
Kazhdan compression core with surviving defect in the sofic group `Q`, and
Steps 3 and 4 give items 1 and 2. For item 3, `Q` is generated by `q(E)`
together with the images of the free letters. If every free letter compressed
`Gamma`, then all generators would lie in `P_Gamma`, and `Q = Hull_Q(Gamma)`,
contradicting item 1. The same argument applies to any generating set.

**Scope check.**

- Nothing here needs `q` injective on the core, or torsion-freeness.
- Nothing here needs finite presentation.
- Soficity of `Q` and property (T) of the tested subgroup are the only analytic
  inputs.
- The sharpness example `E` is non-Kazhdan and is consistent with Step 3.
