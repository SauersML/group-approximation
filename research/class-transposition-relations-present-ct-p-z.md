**OPEN.**

## Statement

For every finite set `P` of odd primes, the homomorphism `ε: Γ_P → CT_P(Z)` of
`class-transposition-presentation-has-central-kernel` is injective. Equivalently, `CT_P(Z)`
is presented by the class transpositions `t(A,B)` subject to:
- (R1) squares;
- (R2) conjugation by a transposition for which both boxes are regular;
- (R3) splitting of both boxes into corresponding `p`-children, for each `p ∈ P'`.

## What is known

- `ker ε` is central and `Γ_P` is perfect (`class-transposition-presentation-has-central-kernel`,
  lane proof). So the claim is equivalent to `ker ε = 1`, and it follows from
  `H_2(CT_P(Z); Z) = 0`.
- **A literature route to H_2 = 0, not checked at source here.** `CT_P(Z)` is the topological
  full group of the one-vertex `k`-graph groupoid `G_Λ` (`ct-p-z-is-a-one-vertex-k-graph-full-group`).
  That node records `H_*(G_Λ) = 0` (Farsi–Kumjian–Pask–Sims). Li's homological results for
  topological full groups (arXiv 2209.08087) relate `H_*([[G]])` to the groupoid homology; if they
  give acyclicity when groupoid homology vanishes, the claim follows. This lane read neither Li's
  statement of that relation nor its hypotheses. So this is a pointer, not an input.
- **Calibration, P = ∅.** For colour 2 alone this is a presentation of `V` by cone transpositions
  of Bleak–Quick type. Bleak and Quick prove that such a presentation presents `V` (literature,
  not re-read here).

## Attempts

1. **bh-pal-kourovka61, 09-18: central kernel.** Proved the kernel is central (every kernel word
   commutes with every fine transposition that is regular along it, and every generator is a
   product of such). Also proved `Γ_P` is perfect, and that each finite group `Sym(R)` of a box
   partition `R` embeds.
   - **Direct attack on `ker ε = 1`.** Write a kernel word `w = t_1 ⋯ t_k` as a product of elements
     `σ_i ∈ Γ_{T_i}`, where `T_i` is a `t_i`-invariant refinement of the partitions transported
     along `w` (Lemma 2 of the central-kernel node).
   - **Why it stalls.** The partitions `T_i` differ from step to step, and infinite-order elements
     of `CT_P(Z)` lie in no single `Γ_T`. So centrality plus the finite `Γ_T` alone does not force
     `w = 1`: perfect groups generated by involutions can have non-trivial central extensions in
     which the involutions lift to involutions.
   - **Precise gap.** Show that the loop of box partitions `S_k → S_{k-1} → ⋯ → S_0 = S_k` carried
     by `w` is null in `Γ_P`. That is a groupoid-of-fractions coherence statement for the
     partitions and their canonical bijections. It would also give an elementary proof that
     `CT_P(Z)` equals the full group of piecewise-canonical permutations.
