## Setting

- `P` is a finite set of odd primes, and `P′ = P ∪ {2}`.
- `Λ_P`, the boxes `B(r, m) = r + mẐ_P` and `Ẑ_P = ∏_{p∈P′} Z_p` are as in
  `ct-p-z-is-a-one-vertex-k-graph-full-group`.
- `Z = ⟨a⟩` acts on `Λ_P` by the mixed-radix odometer of `rcwa-groups-bh-via-sign-doubling-and-odometer-host-proof`,
  step 3: `a^n·(m, r) = (m, (r+n) mod m)`, with restriction `a^{⌊(r+n)/m⌋}`.
- `R_P` is the group of *class-wise increasing* elements of `RCWA_P(Z)`: every affine piece has
  positive slope.

## Statement

1. **`R_P` is `F_∞`.** `R_P` is the topological full group `F(I_l(Λ_P ⋈ Z) ⋉ Ẑ_P)`. Its elements
   are the homeomorphisms `f(r_i + m_i z) = s_i + n_i(z + c_i)`, with `c_i ∈ Z`, on the pieces of a
   box partition, restricted to the dense set `Z`. It is of type `F_∞`.
2. **Point stabilizers.** `Stab_{R_P}(0)` is generated by a copy of `R_P` together with `|P′|`
   further elements. So it is finitely generated whenever `R_P` is.
3. **A host.** With item 1, `R_P ↷ Z` is a faithful, cyclically cofinite host: the translation `a` is
   a single free orbit. It contains:
   - `CT_P(Z)`, the elements fixing `N_0` setwise;
   - the odometer groups `B = R_∅ = V_(2,1)(⟨a⟩)` and `B_p = V_(p,1)(⟨a⟩)` for `p ∈ P`;
   - the sign-doubled image of all of `RCWA_P(Z)`.
4. **The mixed-radix gluing.**
   - The image `G_(2,3) = ⟨B, B_3⟩` of `B *_⟨a⟩ B_3` lies in `R_{3}`.
   - `CT_{3}(Z)` has **infinite index** in `R_{3}`, since the cosets `a^k CT` are distinct.
     So the right comparison group is `R_{3}`, not `CT_{3}(Z)`.
   - If `G_(2,3) = R_{3}`, or has finite index in it, then `G_(2,3)` is finitely presented.
     The free kernel of `B *_⟨a⟩ B_3` is then finitely normally generated. That would be the
     first kernel removal for a gluing whose factors have no disjointly supported rigid
     stabilizers.
   - **Evidence for equality.** At the level of classes mod `6`, the piecewise translations
     with pure 2-adic pieces and those with pure 3-adic pieces generate all of `Sym(Z/6)`.
     Their transposition graph is `K_(3,3)`.

## Proof

**Item 1.**
- **Identification.** This is steps 2–3 of the RCWA route with the binary colour deleted. A
  class-wise increasing `h ∈ RCWA_P(Z)` has pieces `r_i(m) → s_i(n_i)` with integer shifts
  `c_i`, and extends to `ĥ` on `Ẑ_P`. Conversely, such a homeomorphism preserves `Z`.
- **Li's theorem.** Li arXiv:2110.04505v2, Example ex:ZS (III), quoted in
  `research/artifacts/gq-affq-kep-odometer-host.md` §1, gives `F_∞`. Its checks for
  `D = Λ_P ⋈ Z`:
  - every colour `p ∈ P′` has `p ≥ 2` edges at the vertex;
  - the action preserves degree;
  - condition (F) holds, since the action is pseudo-free: `a^n·(m,r) = (m,r)` with trivial
    restriction forces `m | n` and `0 ≤ r+n < m`, so `n = 0`. So `D` is right cancellative
    (l.1456).
  - `D^*(w,w) = St(Z, v) = Z` is `F_∞`;
  - `X = Ω_∞ = Ẑ_P`.
- **Rechecked at source** (Li's TeX source of arXiv:2110.04505v2, Theorem `thm:deg`
  l.2055, Example `ex:ZS` l.2371). Here `P = Z_{≥0}^k` with its standard Garside family, as
  in Li's own `thm:k-graphs`.
  - `Λ_P` is left cancellative, with one vertex and finitely many paths of each degree.
  - It has unique factorization: `(m, r) = (m′, r′)(m″, r″)` forces `r′ = r mod m′`.
  - Conditions (F) and (t < d) come from pseudo-freeness and from `p ≥ 2` edges per colour,
    via case (III).
  - No assumption involves the extra binary colour.
- **Sanity check.** For `P = ∅` this is `V_(2,1)(⟨a⟩)`, which is finitely presented by
  Nekrashevych.

**Item 2.** Let `f ∈ R_P` fix `0`. Its piece at `0` is `B(0, m) → B(0, n)`, `z ↦ (n/m)z`,
because `0 ≤ s < n` and `s + nc = 0` force `s = c = 0`.
- **Germs.** So the germ group at `0` is the multiplicative group `⟨P′⟩ ≅ Z^{|P′|}`.
- **Two tools.**
  - Any two nonempty clopen sets are joined by a bisection. Match the box counts by
    splitting `B(r,m) = B(r,2m) ⊔ B(r+m,2m)`, then map box to box by
    `z ↦ s + n(z-r)/m`.
  - So for fixed `q` there are `s_p ∈ Stab(0)` with `s_p(z) = pz` on `B(0,q)`, one for each
    `p ∈ P′`.
- **The kernel of the germ map** is the union of the rigid stabilizers of
  `Ẑ_P ∖ B(0, qm)`. These are the conjugates of `Rist(Y_0)`, `Y_0 = Ẑ_P ∖ B(0,q)`, by
  nonnegative words in the `s_p`.
- **`Rist(Y_0)` is a copy of `R_P`.** A bisection `Ẑ_P → Y_0` conjugates `F(𝒢)` onto
  `F(𝒢|_{Y_0}) = Rist(Y_0)`.
- **Conclusion.** `Stab(0) = ⟨Rist(Y_0), s_p (p ∈ P′)⟩`.

**Item 3.**
- **`CT_P(Z)`.** The argument of `ct-z-is-the-rcwa-group-fixing-the-nonnegative-integers`,
  step 1, shows that an RCWA map fixing `N_0` has positive slopes, so `CT_P(Z) ⊆ R_P`.
- **The odometer groups.** Their tables `u → v a^k` are class-wise increasing with
  `p`-power data.
- **Sign doubling.** This is step 1 of the RCWA route.
- **`a`.** It is a single free orbit on `Z`.

**Item 4.**
- **Infinite index.** `a^k` moves `N_0`, so `a^k ∉ CT_{3}(Z)` for `k ≠ 0`.
- **The kernel.** Since `B *_⟨a⟩ B_3` is finitely presented, its image is finitely presented
  exactly when the kernel is finitely normally generated.
- **The mod-6 computation.** Write `Z/6 = Z/2 × Z/3`.
  - "Add `3` on the class `r` mod `3`" swaps `(0,r)` and `(1,r)`.
  - "Add `2` on the even numbers" is the 3-cycle `r ↦ r+2` on the fibre `e = 0`.
  - Conjugating the first by such 3-cycles gives the transpositions of `(0,r)` and `(1,r′)`
    for all `r, r′`. These form a connected graph, `K_(3,3)`, so they generate `Sym(6)`. ∎

## Lesson for general BH

**The right comparison group for a glued image is the full group of the generated groupoid,
not its `N_0`-stable part.** Kohl's `CT_P(Z)` misses the translation and has infinite index.
Its odometer extension `R_P = F(Λ_P ⋈ Z)` is the natural home of every mixed-radix gluing, and
it is itself a finitely presented host on `Z`, by Li's Zappa–Szép theorem.

**Kernel removal becomes a generation problem.** For groupoid-coded factors, removing the
gluing kernel reduces to one question: do the factors' full groups generate the full group
of the groupoid they generate? When they do, Li's finiteness theorem does all the work.

For `B *_⟨a⟩ B_3` this is the concrete question `G_(2,3) = R_{3}`. It is true at the class
level mod `6`. It is the first test of kernel removal that disjoint-cone relators cannot
reach.
