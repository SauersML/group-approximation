Citation import, not a reproof.

**Primary source.** D. Gaboriau, *Coût des relations d'équivalence et des
groupes*, Invent. Math. 139 (2000) 41–98,
<https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/Cout/Cout.pdf>. The lane
`lit-scout` extracted the text on MSI on 2026-09-12. Critères VI.24(3),
verbatim:

> si Γ est une réunion croissante de groupes infinis Γ_n où Γ_1 est à prix
> fixe de coût 1, par exemple moyennable, et si Γ_{n+1} est engendré par Γ_n
> et des éléments γ ∈ Γ tels que γ^{-1}Γ_nγ ∩ Γ_n soit infini alors Γ est à
> prix fixe de coût 1.

Gaboriau attributes (3) to a suggestion of Alex Furman, "avec la même preuve".
VI.26(b) treats `SL(n,Z)`, `n >= 3`, by commuting transvections. This claim is
that argument over an arbitrary infinite commutative ring, extended by ring
automorphisms.

**Restatement used for the quotation below.** D. Gaboriau, *Around the orbit equivalence theory, measure
equivalence, cost and L2-Betti numbers*, lecture notes,
<https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/ME-Cost-L2-Lectures/ME-Cost-L2-lectures.pdf>.
The text was extracted on MSI with ghostscript on 2026-09-12. Section 2.5 says
its material "is essentially extracted form [Gab00]", that is, Gaboriau,
*Coût des relations d'équivalence et des groupes*, Invent. Math. 139 (2000).

**Theorem 2.45**, quoted up to typesetting of sub- and superscripts:

> If Γ is an increasing union of subgroups Γ = ∪_{n=0,···} ↗Γ_n such that
> Γ_{n+1} = ⟨Γ_n, γ_{n+1}⟩ is generated by Γ_n and some element γ_{n+1} ∈ Γ,
> satisfying |γ_{n+1}^{-1} Γ_n γ_{n+1} ∩ Γ_n| = ∞, then for every free p.m.p.
> action Γ ↷^α (X,µ), the rel-cost of R_{Γ↷X} in R_{Γ_0↷X} is trivial:
> rel-C(R_{α(Γ)}; R_{α(Γ_0)}) = 0. In particular, C(R_{α(Γ)}) ≤ C(R_{α(Γ_0)}).

The notes call this "a direct application of [Gab00, Lemme V.3]". Theorem 2.42
(chain-commuting groups) is [Gab00, Crit. VI.24]. Corollary 2.44 lists
`SL(n,Z)`, `n >= 3`, among the groups with fixed price one.

**Application.**
* Start with `Γ_0 = E_12(R)`.
* Enumerate, one element at a time, the countably many generators added at
  stages `K_1`, `K_2` and `K_3` of `fpbs-elementary-matrix-semidirect-fixed-price-one-proof`.
* Each new element commutes with, or normalizes, an infinite root subgroup
  already present, so the intersection condition holds.
* `Γ_0` is infinite abelian, so each of its free actions has cost one
  (Ornstein--Weiss, Levitt).
* Hence `C = 1` for every free action of `G`.

This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what is quoted here and
that the chain meets its hypothesis.
