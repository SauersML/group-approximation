Citation import, not a reproof. Tom Hutchcroft and Gabor Pete, *Kazhdan groups
have cost 1*, arXiv:1810.11015v2 (submitted 2018-10-25, revised 2020-04-09).
The arXiv identifier, title, authorship and abstract were checked against
`https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=1810.11015` on 2026-09-09; the
abstract reads "We prove that every countably infinite group with Kazhdan's
property (T) has cost 1, answering a well-known question of Gaboriau. It
remains open if they have fixed price 1."

This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what the target records it
as saying. It imports the infimal statement and explicitly not fixed price
one; the paper's own final sentence is quoted rather than paraphrased so that
the distinction cannot be lost downstream. The archive's use of this theorem
is at `research/artifacts/fpbs/creative-branches.md`, reference [HP].
