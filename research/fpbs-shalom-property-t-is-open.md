title: Property (T) is open in the space of marked groups

Let `F` be a free group on a finite set `X` and `N` a normal subgroup of `F`
with `F/N` Kazhdan. Then some finite `S` in `N` has `F/<<S>>` Kazhdan.

Equivalently, Kazhdan groups form an open subset of the space of `X`-marked
groups. In particular every Kazhdan group is a quotient of a finitely presented
Kazhdan group.

Use downstream: if the normal closures `<<R_k>>` increase to `N` and `F/N` has
(T), then `F/<<R_k>>` has (T) for all large `k`. This is a literature import
(Shalom).
