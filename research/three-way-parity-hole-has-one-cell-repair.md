**ESTABLISHED.**  Let the three label families all be `{0,1}`, and let

```text
E_even={000,011,101,110}.                              (PCR1)
```

Write an integral margin vector as

```text
b=(s_0,s_1;a_0,a_1;t_0,t_1),
s_0+s_1=a_0+a_1=t_0+t_1=N.                            (PCR2)
```

If `b` lies in the real cone generated by `E_even`, define

```text
q_000=s_0+a_0+t_0-N,
q_011=s_0+a_1+t_1-N,
q_101=s_1+a_0+t_1-N,
q_110=s_1+a_1+t_0-N.                                  (PCR3)
```

All four `q_v` are nonnegative and have the same parity.  Moreover the
unique real table with margins `b` is

```text
x_v=q_v/2                  (v in E_even).              (PCR4)
```

Consequently:

1. if the common parity is even, then `b` already belongs to the integral
   cell semigroup;
2. if it is odd, then for **every** odd-parity triple
   `u in {001,010,100,111}`, the padded margin vector

   ```text
   b+A_u                                                     (PCR5)
   ```

   belongs to the integral semigroup generated by `E_even`.

Indeed, adding `A_u` increases three margins and the common total by one.
Among the four numerators in `(PCR3)`, it adds one to the three even cells
at Hamming distance one from `u` and subtracts one from the complementary
even cell.  Before padding every numerator is a nonnegative odd integer, so
the decreased numerator is still nonnegative; afterward all four are even.
Equation `(PCR4)` is therefore a nonnegative integral table.

The six-unit hole from `bs14-two-exit-typed-transport-is-not-saturated` is
the sharp example.  Its four numerators equal one.  Adding, for example,
the odd triple `001` produces an integral even-cell table with three cell
multiplicities equal to one and the fourth zero.  Zero support-changing
cells cannot work, while one does, so the additive repair cost is exactly
one.

For the BS14 boundary problem this removes the abstract determinant-two
tetrahedron as an unbounded-padding obstruction.  The load-bearing word is
**support-changing**: `(PCR5)` prescribes one new source/first-exit/second-
exit margin triple which is not a cell of `E_even`; the final reconnection
uses only even cells.  A native operator proof must still realize that odd
triple by a compatible packet/type perturbation and satisfy the joint Gram
equations.  This theorem neither supplies nor assumes such a BS14 cell.
