**ESTABLISHED** by `sofic-groups-are-qca-surjunctive-proof`.

**Setting.** `G` is a group and `d >= 2`. Put `A_F = (x)_{g in F} M_d` for finite `F`, and let `A` be the UHF norm
closure. `lambda_g` shifts sites, `A_k -> A_{gk}`. A *QCA-endomorphism* is a unital *-homomorphism `alpha : A -> A`
with `alpha lambda_g = lambda_g alpha` and `alpha(A_e) <= A_M` for a finite `M` containing `e`. Then
`alpha(A_g) <= A_{gM}`. It is *strict* if it is not onto. Put `P = alpha(A)' cap A` and `K = M^-1 M`.

**Theorem.**

1. **Splitting (any group).** `A_e` lies in the linear span of `alpha(A_{M^-1}) . (P cap A_K)`. So `alpha` is
   surjective iff `P cap A_K = C1`. A strict endomorphism has a nontrivial, shift-invariant, locally generated
   relative commutant `P`, and `A` is generated by the commuting algebras `alpha(A)` and `P`.
2. **Sofic groups are QCA-surjunctive.** If `G` is sofic, every QCA-endomorphism, for every `d`, is an automorphism.
   The proof counts dimensions: on a sofic approximation `V`, the good copies of the local rule generate
   `M_d^{(x) |V_good|}`, and a separated family of copies of `P cap A_K` commutes with them. If `P cap A_K != C`
   this gives `d^{2|V|} >= d^{2(1-delta)|V|} 2^{c|V|}`, which is false for small `delta`.
3. **Clifford sector equals stable finiteness, for every prime `p`.** Label Weyl operators `W(a,b) = X^a Z^b` on
   `(x)_G M_{p^n}` by rows `u = (a,b) in F_p[G]^{2n}`. A QCA-endomorphism is *Clifford* if
   `alpha(W(u)) in C W(uT)` for some map `T`. Such a `T` is right multiplication by a matrix
   `T in M_{2n}(F_p[G])` with `T J T^* = J`, where `J = [[0,-1],[1,0]]`. The following are equivalent:
   - (i) `F_p[G]` is stably finite;
   - (ii) every Clifford QCA-endomorphism of `(x)_G M_{p^n}`, for every `n`, is surjective.

   For (ii) => (i): if `AB = 1 != BA` in `M_n(F_p[G])`, then `T = diag(A, B^*)` is symplectic, and
   `W(u) -> W(uT)` is a strict Clifford endomorphism. Hence **QCA-surjunctivity of `G` implies Kaplansky's stable
   finiteness of `F_p[G]` for every prime `p`**, the same consequence the classical conjecture has.
4. **Calibration off groups.** Take the 3-regular tree with the end-stabilizer `Aut(T, xi)`, an odd prime `p`, and
   `h = 2^-1 mod p`. Let `c, c'` be the two children of `v`, and set `alpha(X_v) = X_c X_c'`,
   `alpha(Z_v) = Z_c^h Z_c'^h`. This defines a strict, trace-preserving, radius-one, `Aut(T,xi)`-equivariant unital
   *-endomorphism. Its relative commutant contains `X_c X_c'^-1` and `Z_c^h Z_c'^-h`, so the splitting of part 1 is
   visible. The counting of part 2 fails only because the site set is non-amenable (`|F^-| ~ |F|/2`). So the proof of
   part 2 must use approximation of the site set, not just the symmetry or the trace.
5. **Closure of the "monotone dimension" seed.** Let `Phi` be a unital endomorphism and `dim` an additive dimension
   on idempotents with `dim(1) < infinity`.
   - If `dim(Phi(e)) <= dim(e)` for all `e`, then applying it to `1 - e` gives equality. So no such dimension can
     strictly drop under a unital map.
   - On the diagonal sector `LC(A^G) x| G`, `dim` restricted to clopen indicators is a `G`-invariant measure `mu`.
     Monotonicity under `Phi_tau` is then `tau_* mu = mu`. For full-support `mu` this forces surjectivity, and for
     `mu = lambda` it is `every-injective-ca-preserves-uniform-bernoulli-measure`.
   - On the quantum side, `tr o alpha = tr` automatically, because the UHF trace is unique.

   Strictness is invisible to every finite additive dimension. It lives in the relative commutant of part 1.

**Verification.** `experiments/qca-surjunctivity-2026-09-17/clifford_qca_check.py` (output in `.out`) checks:
- the plain Weyl relations and exact `beta`-preservation by `diag(A, B^*)` on `S3` and `Z/4` over `F_2` and `S3` over
  `F_3`;
- the symmetric Weyl relations and that `diag(A, B^*)` is symplectic and multiplicative, on `Z/4` and `S3` over `F_3`
  and on `Z/3` over `F_5`;
- teeth: `diag(A, B)` and a random `T` fail;
- the tree rule for `p = 3, 5, 7`: the generated algebra has dimension `p^2` against `p^4`, `X_c` is trace-orthogonal
  to the image, the relative commutant has dimension `p^2`, and the exponent-1 guess fails the phase.

**What this does not do.** It does not prove or refute the classical conjecture. We know no implication in either
direction between QCA-surjunctivity and classical surjunctivity. Both hold for sofic groups, and both imply stable
finiteness of `F_p[G]`. A strict classical pair gives only a non-unital `Ad V` with a nonlocal range projection (see
`split-ca-quantization-has-sharp-defect`), not a QCA-endomorphism.
