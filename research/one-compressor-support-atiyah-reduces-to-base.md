**ESTABLISHED** by [[one-compressor-support-atiyah-proof]].

Let `K` be a subfield of `C` closed under complex conjugation, let `G` be a group, let
`Gamma <= G` be torsion-free, and let `t in G` satisfy

```text
t Gamma t^-1  is a proper subgroup of  Gamma.                          (OC1)
```

Put `H = <Gamma, t>` and `N = union_(n >= 0) t^-n Gamma t^n`. Then

```text
N is normal in H,  H/N is infinite cyclic generated by tN,  and        (OC2)
N is a directed union of conjugates of Gamma,
```

and

```text
H satisfies Strong Atiyah over K   iff   Gamma satisfies Strong Atiyah over K.   (OC3)
```

**Ore compressor families.** Let `T <= G` with `s Gamma s^-1 <= Gamma` for `s in T`, let `M` be
the monoid `T` generates, and assume `M` is left Ore: any two elements have a common left multiple
in `M`. Put `H_T = <Gamma, T>` and `N_T = union_(m in M) m^-1 Gamma m`. Then `N_T` is a directed
union of conjugates of `Gamma` and is normal in `H_T`. If `H_T/N_T` is torsion-free elementary
amenable, then `H_T` satisfies Strong Atiyah over `K` iff `Gamma` does. Commuting families are the
basic case, with abelian quotient.

## Consequences

* **Where a counterexample can live inside a compression host.** Let `G` be torsion-free and let
  `Gamma <= G` satisfy Strong Atiyah over `K`. A matrix over `K[G]` with non-integral kernel
  dimension cannot have its support inside `<Gamma, t>` for a single strict compressor `t`, nor
  inside `<Gamma, T>` for a left Ore compressor family with torsion-free elementary amenable
  quotient. Kernel dimensions do not change on passing to a subgroup containing the support, so
  this is a statement about supports.
  Its support must use compressors with no common left multiples, the prefix pattern of a binary
  tree, or letters outside the compression semigroup of `Gamma`.
* **Fournier--Facio group.** In `fournier-facio-torsion-free-skeleton` the whole group is
  `G = <Gamma, t_1, t_2>` and has property (T).
  - For every nonempty positive word `w`, the element `t_w` is a strict compressor. The step
    uses (TL1) of `fournier-facio-compression-tree-lamp-group`.
  - So every support inside `<Gamma, t_w>` reduces Strong Atiyah to `Gamma`. There `Gamma` is a
    quotient of a torsion-free hyperbolic Kazhdan group, and Strong Atiyah for it is open.
  - Positive words ending in different letters have no common left multiple, by (TL3)-(TL5).
    So no Ore family contains both letters.
  - `G` has property (T), so no infinite elementary amenable quotient of `G` carries the
    reduction further.
* **Where the reduction stops.** `fournier-facio-one-compressor-subgroup-reduces-to-gamma` leaves
  two-compressor supports open for hyperlinearity and soficity. The same boundary holds here.
  - In the abstract ascending HNN extension along two compressions, the vertex `Gamma_w` lies
    inside both of its parents `Gamma_(w t_1^-1)` and `Gamma_(w t_2^-1)`, embedded through the two
    compressions.
  - So the normal closure of `Gamma` contains amalgams of two copies of `Gamma` along a Kazhdan
    edge group embedded by the two compressions.
  - The permanence results for graphs of groups need finite edge groups (Sanchez-Peralta,
    arXiv:2409.12268v2, abstract: the algebraic and center-valued Atiyah conjectures "are also
    closed under the graph of groups construction provided that the edge groups are finite").
  - Non-Ore two-letter supports are therefore exactly where approximation-free Strong Atiyah
    permanence gives out over compression hosts.

## What this does not settle

It does not decide Strong Atiyah for `Gamma`, for the Fournier--Facio group, or for the master
tester. It does not apply when `t` normalizes `Gamma`, since then `H/N` can be finite and the
finite-extension problem of Linnell--Schick enters.
