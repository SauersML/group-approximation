title: Finite elementary tensor blocks inherit almost divisibility from one pure factor
---

Let `A` be pure and let `B` be any C-star algebra.  Write `boxtimes` for the
canonical external product from `Cu(A) times Cu(B)` to
`Cu(A tensor_min B)`.  Given `n>=1`, finitely many

```text
alpha_i' << alpha_i in Cu(A),       beta_i in Cu(B),
```

there exists `gamma in Cu(A tensor_min B)` such that

```text
n gamma <= sum_i (alpha_i boxtimes beta_i),
sum_i (alpha_i' boxtimes beta_i) <= (n+1) gamma.
```

Hence a failure of almost divisibility in a tensor product of pure algebras
cannot be witnessed on a finite elementary tensor block with its compact
containments lifted in one factor.
