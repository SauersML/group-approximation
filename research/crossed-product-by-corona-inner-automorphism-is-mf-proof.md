Direct proof in the claim body.  A cyclic shift of length `N` whose
wrap-around block carries `U^N` is an exact covariant unitary for the diagonal
embedding `diag(U^-n Phi U^n)`; vectors supported away from the wrap see the
regular representation, so the norms converge; the multiple case is Shulman's
amalgam decomposition of `B x| F_m` plus Theorem 18, the restrictions to the
base being conjugate to `Phi (x) 1`.
