Stability replaces the approximate action of the base by an exact one;
(T;FD) of the associated subgroup moves the stable letter a bounded
distance onto its commutant; co-density identifies that commutant with
the commutant of the whole base; so the stable letter almost commutes with
every element of the base, while Britton's lemma says it commutes with
none outside the associated subgroup.  Section 11 of the artifact.
