The prerequisite supplies the normal simple finitary subgroup and
faithfulness of the literal action on it. The centralizer of all
finite transvections in `GL(V)` is the identity over `F_2`, so the
literal kernel equals the abstract centralizer of `L`. A homomorphism
retaining the head has kernel disjoint from `L`; commutators therefore
put that kernel in the centralizer. Its image consequently surjects
onto the literal image.

An operator generated by the original letters has unique eventual
positive-tail Laurent coefficients. Multiplying sufficiently far from
the boundary proves that these coefficients define a symbol
homomorphism. Its image contains `A=x_12(z)` of order two and
`B=x_12(1)x_21(1)` of order three. Over `F_2((z^-1))`, their
fractional-linear maps send the disjoint sets `|x|>1` and `|x|<=1`
into one another in the directions required for reduced-word
ping-pong. Hence they generate `C_2*C_3`.

The artifact proves nonamenability through the index-six rank-two
free kernel of its map to `C_2 x C_3`, including the invariant-mean
contradiction for a free group. Amenability of subgroups and quotients
then excludes any amenable head-retaining image. No matrix-corona
conclusion is inferred from this obstruction.
