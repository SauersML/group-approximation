For every infinite minimal binary subshift `(X,T)`, the group
`S=EL_3(LC(X,F_2) semidirect Z)` is infinite, simple, Kazhdan and LEF,
and has the explicit generating triple of orders `7,2,2` in the
unit-idempotent theorem, with `p` the letter-one cylinder.

The first two generators generate `SL_3(F_2) x SL_3(F_2)`. Thus every
such `S` is a quotient of `(SL_3(F_2) x SL_3(F_2)) * C_2`, with the finite
factor embedded injectively. No assertion that these are all the
relations, or that three is the minimum generator number, is made.
