The twisted group algebra is a trace-preserving von Neumann subalgebra of
the twisted Bernoulli crossed product, proving necessity by hereditary
Connes embeddability.

For sufficiency, fix a finite algebraic crossed-product window.  Replace its
base functions by finite cylinder functions with finite atomic value space.
Choose a finite quotient of the residually finite actor which is injective
on every coordinate and multiplication difference occurring in the window.
Its Bernoulli shift gives exact finite diagonal base matrices and exact
permutation covariance.  Tensor those permutation unitaries with canonical
projective microstates of the twisted group factor.  The projective factor
supplies the multiplier and kills the trace of every mixed word with
nonidentity group part; the permutation/diagonal factor supplies the
Bernoulli integral when the group part is the identity.  The resulting
finite matrices reproduce all selected products, adjoints, and traces.
A diagonal sequence over the windows is a Connes embedding.
