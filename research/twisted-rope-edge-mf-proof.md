Direct proof in the claim body.  The compatible product filtration gives HNN
quotients of finite groups (virtually free, residually finite); closedness of
`S` and the compatibility of `tau` keep Britton-reduced words reduced there;
tensoring the regular representations of finite quotients with the MF
embedding of `Gamma` pulled back along the retraction `R -> Gamma` separates
every nontrivial element, the direct summand `1` guarding against scalar
cancellation in the tensor product.
