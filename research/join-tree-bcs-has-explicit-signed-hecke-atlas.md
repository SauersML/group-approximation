Let `B` be a finite tracial BCS whose contexts admit a join tree `T`: for
every variable `x`, the contexts containing `x` induce a connected subtree.
Then one can compute a finitely presented group

```text
Gamma=K_D times F_D^(1) times ... times F_D^(m),          (JTA1)
```

a higher-dimensional signed projection `q_D in C[K_D]`, and a compatible
projection atlas `(BPA1)--(BPA3)` in `q_D C[Gamma]q_D`.

Root the join tree.  Use the rational multiplicities of
`tracial-bcs-rationalizes-full-overlap-distributions`.  At a child context
`c` with parent `p`, a coordinate permutation identifies the complete joint
sign tuple on `U_c intersect U_p`.  Inside the parent's transported `M_D`
block, apply a unitary which is block diagonal over those overlap fibers.
Adjoin a fresh direct-product free factor and use `(OBM3)` relative to the
parent's transported coordinate matrix units.  The resulting child partition
has exactly the prescribed atoms and agrees with the parent on every overlap
variable.

The running-intersection property guarantees that every variable already
seen before the child lies in its parent separator.  Thus the induction never
disturbs an earlier equality.  All corner unitaries and projections have
finite group-word support.  If a separator does not distinguish two supported
child assignments, `free-phase-context-escape-criterion` permits a genuine
block escape; if it does distinguish them all, the child context algebra is
already generated by the separator and no escape is needed.

Consequently acyclic overlap compatibility is completely solved.  In
particular a no-CE-trace BCS cannot admit such a join tree: the canonical
corner trace on `(JTA1)` would be Connes embeddable.  The unresolved
signed-Hecke construction begins at the first chord outside a join tree.
