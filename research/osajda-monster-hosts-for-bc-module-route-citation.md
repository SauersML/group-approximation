Direct literature import. Sources read from the arXiv PDFs on 2026-09-12; theorem numbers and
quotations are verbatim from the papers.

- **Gro03:** M. Gromov, *Random walk in random groups*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 13 (2003), no. 1,
  73--146, MR1978492.
- **HLS:** N. Higson, V. Lafforgue, G. Skandalis, *Counterexamples to the Baum--Connes
  conjecture*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 12 (2002), no. 2, 330--354.
- **Osa14:** D. Osajda, *Small cancellation labellings of some infinite graphs and
  applications*, Acta Math. 225 (2020), no. 1; arXiv:1406.5015v3 (17 May 2020). Abstract:
  "We construct the first examples of finitely generated coarsely non-amenable groups (that is,
  groups without Guoliang Yu's Property A) that are coarsely embeddable into a Hilbert space.
  Moreover, our groups act properly on CAT(0) cubical complexes. We construct the first examples
  of finitely generated groups, with expanders embedded isometrically into their Cayley graphs
  -- in contrast, in the case of the Gromov monster expanders are not even coarsely embedded."
  Theorem 1 = Theorem 2.7; Theorem 2 = Theorem 6.3 (verified in the text).
- **Osa18:** D. Osajda, *Residually finite non-exact groups*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 28 (2018),
  no. 2, 509--517; arXiv:1703.03791v3 (16 Jan 2019). Main Theorem and Section 3.3 quoted.
- **Wil11:** R. Willett, *Property A and graphs with large girth*, J. Topol. Anal. 3 (2011),
  no. 3, 377--384, MR2831267 (a coarse disjoint union of `D`-regular graphs, `D >= 3`, of girth
  tending to infinity has no property A).
- **HK01:** N. Higson, G. Kasparov, *E-theory and KK-theory for groups which act properly and
  isometrically on Hilbert space*, Invent. Math. 144 (2001), no. 1, 23--74 (a-T-menable groups
  satisfy Baum--Connes with coefficients, bijectively).

These are context imports for the module route; no claim of the graph is re-proved here.
