The pinned integral central extension B_c0 has a finite quotient F of
order7^28, exponent7 and exact nilpotency class5, in which its central
generator has order7. That generator belongs to gamma5(B_c0) but not
gamma6(B_c0).

The class c0 mod7 vanishes on a normal subgroup of K7 of index7^27.
Hence B_c0/<z^7> virtually splits and is residually finite if and only
if K7 is. Neither residual finiteness of K7 nor unbounded finite orders
of the integral central generator is asserted.
