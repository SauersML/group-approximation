**ESTABLISHED** through `countable-central-simple-cuntz-swindle-algebras-exist-proof` (lane proof,
elementary, not reviewed).

Let `K` be a field and let `V` be a `K`-vector space of dimension `ℵ_1`. Let `I ⊂ End_K(V)` be the
set of operators of countable rank, meaning `dim(aV) ≤ ℵ_0`. Put `Q = End_K(V) / I`. A *Cuntz swindle
structure* and `A_sw` are as in `finitely-presented-cuntz-swindle-algebras-exist`.

1. **Uniform simplicity.** `I` is a two-sided ideal and `Q ≠ 0`. For every `a ∈ End_K(V) \ I` there
   are `x, y ∈ End_K(V)` with `x a y = 1` exactly. So in `Q` every nonzero `a` has `x a y = 1`, and
   `Q` is simple.
2. **Centre.** `Z(Q) = K · 1`. More precisely, if `a − λ ∉ I` for every `λ ∈ K`, then some
   `b ∈ End_K(V)` has `[a, b] ∉ I`.
3. **Swindle with a unit.** `End_K(V)` carries a Cuntz swindle structure `(s, σ)` with
   `σ(a) = Σ_n s_2^n s_1 a s_1^* s_2^(*n)`, the infinite repeat. It also has a unit `u` with
   `σ² = Ad(u) σ`. Moreover `σ(I) ⊆ I`, so `(s, σ, u)` descends to `Q`. Thus `Q` is a simple
   algebra with centre `K`, carrying a Cuntz swindle with `σ² = Ad(u) σ`.
4. **Countable closure.** Let `K` be countable, for example `K = F_2`. Then there is a unital
   subalgebra `Q_0 ⊆ Q` of countable dimension with the following properties:
   - `Q_0` contains `s_i, s_i^*, u, u^(-1)`;
   - `σ(Q_0) ⊆ Q_0`;
   - every nonzero `a ∈ Q_0` has `x, y ∈ Q_0` with `x a y = 1`;
   - `Z(Q_0) = K`.

   So `Q_0` is a countable central simple Cuntz swindle algebra with `σ² = Ad(u) σ`.
5. **Consequences.**
   - By item 1 of `cuntz-swindle-factors-kill-k-theory-of-every-tensor`,
     `K_q(D ⊗ Q_0) = 0` for every `D` and `q`.
   - By universality (part 4 of `finitely-presented-cuntz-swindle-algebras-exist`), there is a
     unique swindle-intertwining map `A_sw -> Q_0` with `u ↦ u`.
   - The target `fp-central-simple-cuntz-swindle-algebra-exists` needs a central simple swindle
     algebra that is finitely presented. Items 1–4 give one with every property except finite
     generation and finite presentation.

## What this kills

The target lists one way it could be refuted: show that every finitely generated swindle algebra
has a nonzero trace, a nontrivial ideal, or no finite presentation.
- Item 4 shows that the swindle identity, together with `σ² = Ad(u) σ`, implies neither a trace
  (none exists anyway, since `HH_0 = 0`), nor a nontrivial ideal, nor a centre bigger than `K`.
- So any refutation must use finite generation in an essential way. The "infinite-multiple" gap
  noted in Attempt 2 of `finitely-presented-cuntz-swindle-algebras-exist` has a precise meaning:
  - `σ(a)` generates the same ideal as `a` in `Q`;
  - `σ` does not preserve finite rank, which is why the countable-dimension Calkin quotient fails;
  - moving to the next cardinal repairs this, because `ℵ_0 · ℵ_0 = ℵ_0`.
- The countable-rank quotient cannot itself be the finitely presented witness. `Q` has dimension
  `2^(ℵ_1)`, and `Q_0` is obtained by a non-effective closure. What remains open is exactly
  whether some **finitely generated** `σ`-closed subalgebra can be made simple with centre `K` and
  finitely presented.

## Remark on the swindle identity inside Q

In any swindle algebra with a unit `u` satisfying `σ² = Ad(u) σ`, the ideal `⟨σ(J)⟩` generated by
`σ(J)` is the smallest `σ`-invariant ideal containing `J`. This is proved in the route. So
`σ`-simplicity says `⟨σ(a)⟩ = A` for every `a ≠ 0`, while simplicity says `⟨a⟩ = A`. In `Q` both hold,
because `a ∈ ⟨σ(a)⟩` always holds (`a = s_1^* σ(a) s_1`) and `σ(a) ∈ ⟨a⟩` holds when every nonzero
element is full.
