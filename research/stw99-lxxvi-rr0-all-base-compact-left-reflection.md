Let D be separable, simple, stably finite, pure, and real rank zero.
For every compact metrizable X, every projection p in C(X,D tensor K),
and every positive b in C(X,D tensor K), the actual first-factor map
phi satisfies

```text
phi(p) <=_Cu phi(b)  implies  p <=_Cu b.
```

There is no bound on dim X. Combined with the all-base compact theorem,
the map identifies all compact classes and reflects every comparison
with compact left side, even against arbitrary positive right sides.

The proof also establishes an exact relative projection-into-positive
embedding lemma over finite-dimensional compact metrizable bases:
a prescribed embedding on a closed set extends whenever the fiberwise
Cu comparison is strict outside that set. Michael's selection theorem
applies to closed embedding spaces, with lower semicontinuity and
uniform local contractions supplied by explicit polar formulas.

This does not settle comparison with an arbitrary mixed noncompact
left side or realization of all mixed target classes. In particular,
it does not assert full Cu regularity of every such C(X,D).
