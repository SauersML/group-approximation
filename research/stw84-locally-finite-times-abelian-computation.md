title: Locally finite times abelian groups have nuclear dimension equal to rational rank
---

Let `L` be a countable locally finite group and `A` a countable abelian group.
Then

```text
dim_nuc(C*(L times A))=dim_Q(Q tensor_Z A).
```

The value may be infinite.  This computes Problem LXXXIV for a class that
contains many non-finitely-generated and non-virtually-abelian amenable
groups; neither noncommutativity nor unbounded finite subgroup complexity in
the `L` coordinate changes the answer.
