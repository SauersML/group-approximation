Notation: `G=Gamma_triangle`, `H=M=EL_3(J)`, `e_ab=S^a Q T^b`. Then
`QS=0`, `TQ=0` and `e_ab e_cd=delta_bc e_ad`. Commutators are
`[x,y]=xyx^(-1)y^(-1)`.

**Step 1 (T).** `J` is a finite-type `F_2`-algebra, so `H` has (T) by
`elementary-group-property-t-over-free-algebras`.

**Step 2 (a centralizer from the stable letter).** For every
`A in M_3(J)`:

```text
(I+Q e_12)(Q I+SAT) = Q I+SAT+Q e_12      (Q e_12 S = 0, since QS=0),
(Q I+SAT)(I+Q e_12) = Q I+SAT+Q e_12      (SAT Q e_12 = 0, since TQ=0; Q^2=Q).
```

So `x_12(Q)` commutes with `phi(M)=tMt^(-1)`. Put `c=t^(-1)x_12(Q)t`. For
`m in M`,

```text
c m c^(-1) = t^(-1) x_12(Q) phi(m) x_12(Q)^(-1) t = t^(-1) phi(m) t = m.
```

Hence `c in C_G(H)`. With `u=t` we have `uHu^(-1)=phi(H)<=H` and
`ucu^(-1)=x_12(Q)`. Taking `h=x_23(1)`,

```text
[ucu^(-1),h] = [x_12(Q),x_23(1)] = x_13(Q) = w.
```

So `w in D_G(H)`.

**Step 3 (the locally finite normal subgroup).** Let
`G_d = GL(span{e_ab : a,b<d} tensor M_3) = GL_(3d)(F_2)`. It lies in `M`:

- the elementary transvections with `i!=j` are the elements `x_ij(e_ab)`;
- the transvections with `i=j` and `a!=b` are
  `[x_ik(e_ac),x_ki(e_cb)]`, with `k!=i`;
- in characteristic two, `GL_n(F_2)=SL_n(F_2)` is generated by
  elementary transvections.

Since `phi(e_ab)=e_(a+1,b+1)` and `phi(1)=Q+ST=1`, we get
`phi(G_d)<=G_(d+1)`. Put `L_M=union_d G_d`. It is normal in `M`, because
`1+M_3(span e_ab)` is normalized by `GL_3(J)`.

Put `A_j=t^(-j) G_(2j+1) t^j` and `A=union_j A_j`.

- **Nested.** `A_j<=A_(j+1)` because `phi(G_(2j+1))<=G_(2j+3)`.
- **Exhaustion.** For `g in G_d`, `t^(-i) g t^i` lies in `A_j` once
  `d+j-i<=2j+1`.
- **Normal.** `t^(+-1)` preserves `A`. `M` normalizes each
  `t^(-i) L_M t^i`, since `M<=t^(-i) M t^i` by `phi^i(M)<=M`.
- **Locally finite.** Each `A_j` is finite.
- **Contains the mark.** `w=1+e_00 tensor e_13` lies in `A_0=G_1`.

**Step 4 (uniform support constant).** Conjugation by `t^j` identifies
`A_j` with `SL_(3(2j+1))(F_2)`, and sends `w` to
`phi^j(w)=1+e_jj tensor e_13`, which is an ordinary transvection. By
`binary-finitary-transvection-uniformly-controls-active-rank`
(Section 5 of the artifact), `C=4` works for every unitary
representation of every `A_j`, since `3(2j+1)>=3` as Section 5 needs.

**Step 5.** `uniform-finite-support-rank-gap-upgrades-compression-collapse`
gives `w in Rad_MF(G)`. The group `G` is countable and `w` is an
involution. `jacobson-coherent-quotient-is-faithful-and-has-mf-dichotomy`
identifies this `G` and this `w` (via
`jacobson-coherent-group-is-an-ascending-corner-extension`), so it forces
`Rad_MF(G)=L` and `gamma_triangle>0`.

**Consequence 2.** The map `psi` is well defined: both `u(.)u^(-1)` and
`phi` are homomorphisms `H->H`, and they agree on the root generators
`x_ij(a)`. If `rho:EL_n(J)->U(Q)` is a norm-corona representation with
`n>=4`, then `rho o iota o psi` is one of `G`. It kills `w`, so `rho`
kills `x_13(Q)`. Under `psi`, `c` maps to the lamplighter element
`diag(1,1,1,1+QT)`.
