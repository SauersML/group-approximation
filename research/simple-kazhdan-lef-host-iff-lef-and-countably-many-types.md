**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** For a set `𝒞` of finitely generated groups, the following are equivalent:
1. some infinite, finitely generated, simple Kazhdan LEF group contains a copy of every member of `𝒞`;
2. some countable LEF group contains a copy of every member of `𝒞`;
3. every member of `𝒞` is LEF, and `𝒞` has at most countably many isomorphism types.

For example, the finitely generated linear groups, and the finitely generated LEF groups with solvable word problem, lie
in one such group. The set of all finitely generated LEF groups does not, because the groups `G_X` realize continuum
many isomorphism types.
