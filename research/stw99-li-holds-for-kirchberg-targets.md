For every purely infinite strongly self-absorbing algebra `D`
(`O_2`, `O_∞`, `O_∞ ⊗ UHF`, and any other Kirchberg SSA) and every
countable discrete torsion-free amenable group `G`, all strongly outer
actions `G ↷ D` are cocycle conjugate — indeed Gabe--Szabo prove it
beyond this: for amenable actions of exact groups with the Haagerup
property, outer actions on each Kirchberg SSA are unique.  So STW
Problem LI is OPEN ONLY on the stably finite side (`Z`, UHF, `Q`),
where Szabo's CMP19 induction class is the frontier.
