Forget property (T). A witness `S` of `hyperlinear-fp-infinite-simple-kazhdan-group` is
finitely presented, infinite, simple and hyperlinear, which is the statement of the target.
If the witness is sofic, the target's preferred strengthening holds as well.

This route is independent of the amenable-orbit routes into the target. Those cannot reach a
Kazhdan group (`kazhdan-subgroups-of-amenable-orbit-full-groups-are-rf`), while this one can
only be met by one.
