The CFSG family order formulas, as in Wilson, [*The Finite Simple
Groups*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-84800-988-2), give the complete list
strictly after `A11` through `HS`: twenty groups `L2(q)` with

```text
q=343,347,349,353,359,361,367,373,379,383,
  389,397,401,409,419,421,431,433,439,443,
```

together with `Sz(32)`, `L3(9)`, `U3(9)`, and `HS`.  The family bounds are
the continuation of the preceding order audit: `q=449` already puts `L2(q)`
above `HS`; the next linear, unitary, symplectic, orthogonal, Suzuki/Ree, and
exceptional parameters are also above `HS`; and the sporadic order table has
no additional entry in this interval.

The exact arithmetic replay proves

```text
20160 divides |S|  iff  S=L3(9) or S=HS.             (SIH4)
```

The first candidate is impossible for an elementary reason.  Let `k` be a
finite field of odd order with no nontrivial cube root of unity; in particular
take `k=F9`.  If an involution of `PGL3(k)` is represented by `X`, then
`X^2=lambda I`.  Taking determinants shows `lambda` is a square, so rescale
to `X^2=I`.  If two such projective involutions commute, their lifts satisfy

```text
XY=mu YX,       mu^3=1.
```

Hence `mu=1`.  A projective elementary abelian 2-group therefore lifts to
commuting diagonalizable involutions.  Simultaneous diagonalization and
quotienting by the scalar sign leave rank at most two:

```text
rk_2(PGL3(F9)) <= 2.                                (SIH5)
```

But `A8` contains `V4 x V4`, generated by the two Klein four groups on two
disjoint four-point blocks, and has 2-rank at least four.  Thus
`A8` cannot embed in `PGL3(9)`, a fortiori not in `L3(9)`.  Lagrange and
`(SIH4)` now prove `(SIH2)`.

It remains to classify `A<HS`.  The official [ATLAS maximal-subgroup
table for HS](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/spor/HS/) lists

```text
M22; two U3(5):2 classes; L3(4):2; S8;
2^4.S6; 4^3:L3(2); two M11 classes; 4.2^4.S5.        (SIH6)
```

Order divisibility removes every entry except `M22`, `L3(4):2`, and `S8`.
The established maximal-subgroup audit of `M22` has no `A8`.  An index-two
`A8` in `L3(4):2` would be its unique derived subgroup `L3(4)`, which is
nonisomorphic to `A8`.  Hence every `A8<HS` is the unique index-two subgroup
of an `S8` maximal.  ATLAS gives one `S8` class, proving one `A8` class.

Finally `S8` normalizes its derived `A8`.  Maximality of `S8` and simplicity
of `HS` force `N_HS(A)=S8`; its quotient supplies the full outer automorphism
of `A8`.  This proves `(SIH3)` and the marked-family assertion.
