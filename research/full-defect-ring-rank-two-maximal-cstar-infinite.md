Let `R` be a countable unital ring with `s,t` satisfying `ts = 1` and
`R(1-st)R = R`.  Then for every `n >= 2`, `C*_max(EL_n(R))` contains a
proper isometry, so it is not stably finite, admits no faithful tracial
state, and is neither residually finite-dimensional nor MF.
