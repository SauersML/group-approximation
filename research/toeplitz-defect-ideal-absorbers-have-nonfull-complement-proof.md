- **The quotient.** The images `r_1, r_2` and `r_3, r_2` are pairs of isometries with orthogonal ranges
  in `O_3`, so π exists by the universal property of the full free product. It is onto because
  `r_1, r_2, r_3` generate `O_3`.
- **Item 1.** `π(e) = 1 − r_1r_1^* − r_2r_2^* = r_3r_3^* ≠ 0`. The image of the ideal `J` under a
  surjection is an ideal of `O_3`, and `O_3` is simple, so `π(J) = O_3`. Hence `π(B_J) = O_3` too.
- **Item 2.**
  - `||π(q)π(x) − π(x)|| ≤ ||qx − x||`, so `||π(q) − 1|| ≤ ||qx − x|| · ||π(x)^{-1}|| < 1`.
  - `1 − π(q)` is a projection of norm `< 1`, so it is 0.
  - The closed ideal of `B_J` generated by `1 − q` lies in `ker(π|B_J)`, which does not contain 1.
- **Item 3.**
  - Choose a self-adjoint `k_0 ∈ J` with `π(k_0) = π·1`: lift `π·1 ∈ O_3 = π(J)` and take the real part.
  - `u_0 = exp(i k_0) ∈ 1 + J`, and `π(u_0) = exp(iπ) = −1`.
  - Then `x_0 = u_0 − 1` has `π(x_0) = −2`, so `||π(x_0)^{-1}||^{-1} = 2`, and item 2 applies. Square.

**Model test.** The obstruction comes from unital quotients of `J`, not from the definitions.
- In the fold quotient `A_T -> T_2` (`iota_2 = iota_1`), `J` maps onto `K`.
- The finite-rank projections of `T_2` are an approximate unit for `K`, and their complements are
  properly infinite and full.

**What it kills and what it leaves.**
- It kills the route `toeplitz-defect-ideal-connected-via-absorbing-projections`.
- It leaves one escape. Multiply `u` by a null lift of `π(u)^*`: products of exponentials in
  `U(O_3) = U_0(O_3)` lift into `U_0(J~)`. The new unitary lies in `1 + (J ∩ ker π)`.
- Absorption could then work only on `J ∩ ker π`, and simultaneously for every unital quotient of `J`.
  Recorded in the Attempts of `toeplitz-defect-ideal-almost-commuting-projection-axiom`.
