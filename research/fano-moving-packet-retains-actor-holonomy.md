extraspecial packet from the order-seven/Fano closure, represented on its
`128`-dimensional marked Schrodinger space `H_0`.  Let `K` be the finite
binary actor group generated by the displayed center-chain arms,
Whiteheads, and the two Singer elements `t_1,t_2`.  Write

```text
U:K -> U(H_0)                                           (FMH1)
```

for the permutation/Clifford implementation of its action on `A`.
For every finite-dimensional unitary representation `sigma` of `K`,

```text
a |-> rho_0(a) tensor 1,       a in A,
k |-> U(k) tensor sigma(k),    k in K                 (FMH2)
```

is an exact marked representation of the entire finite packet and actor
table.  All root covariance relations hold because the second tensor factor
cancels under conjugation.

Take `sigma` to be the left regular representation of `K`.  Each Singer
element then has a nontrivial order-seven reservoir action:

```text
sigma(t_i)^7=1,                 sigma(t_i)!=1.         (FMH3)
```

the restriction of `lambda_K` to its finite subgroup is a positive multiple
of that subgroup's regular representation.  Following this signed source

transition boundary.  Nevertheless their common transition gauge is

the tautology `(FMH3)`.

identify that holonomy with the independently defined native/Hecke

coefficient-sensitive occurrence outside the finite Fano actor table.  This
claim does not refute that additional input or the full moving-cut claim.
