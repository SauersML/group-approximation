**OPEN, conditionally established** by `deligne-sp2g-covers-not-mf-from-operator-hs-stability`
from `sp2g-universal-cover-lattice-is-operator-hs-stable` (BDL Conjecture 1.8 for `Gamma~_g`).
Unreviewed.

Let `g >= 3`, `n >= 3`, `Gamma_n` the preimage of `Sp_(2g)(Z)` in the connected `n`-fold cover of
`Sp_(2g)(R)`, `z` the covering generator, `J = z^2` of order `m = n / gcd(n, 2)`. Then every
operator-norm asymptotic homomorphism `phi_k : Gamma_n -> U(d_k)` satisfies

```text
||phi_k(z^2) - I||_op -> 0,        Rad_MF(Gamma_n) = <z^2>.                 (MR2)
```

So `Gamma_n` is not MF. For odd `n` the whole covering kernel is the MF radical.

**Relation to the rest of the Deligne region.**
- By `deligne-cover-hyperlinearity-is-monotone-in-genus` the covers embed upward in genus preserving
  `z`, so `(MR2)` at `g = 3` gives it at every `g >= 3`, but says nothing at `g = 2`.
- `deligne-sep7-all-cover-mf-radical-classification` is stated for `Sp_4(Z)`. At genus `g >= 3`,
  `(MR2)` is the analogue of its finite alternative with `m = 2`: `Gamma_q` is MF iff `q` divides
  `2`.
- It does not decide hyperlinearity: MF implies hyperlinear, not conversely.

**What it needs.** Only operator-HS rounding for the `[T_2]` lattice `Gamma~_g`. The finite covers
themselves fail `[T_2]`, so BDL Proposition 1.5 cannot be applied to them through Conjecture 1.8. The
new point is that the rounding hypothesis sits on `Gamma~_g` and descends through the quotient map,
for central finite-residual elements of every order.
