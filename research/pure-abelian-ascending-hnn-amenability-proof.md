Injectivity embeds every stage of the directed system
`A --phi--> A --phi--> ...` in its direct limit `A_infinity`.  Translation by
one stage extends to an automorphism of `A_infinity`.  The standard normal
form map identifies `(PAH1)` with the semidirect product by this automorphism.
The kernel `A_infinity` and quotient `Z` are amenable, so their extension is
amenable.  Every amenable group is hyperlinear.
