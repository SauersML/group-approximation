Let `Gamma=EL_r(T)`, where `T=F_2<x,y|yx=1>`, and let the external `S_3`
be the forbidden-point stabilizer in
`affine-s3-endpoint-permutes-active-atoms`.  There are exact finite-dimensional
representations `rho_n` of the amalgam

```text
G_0=(Gamma x S_3) *_(S_3) C_aff                        (FAH1)
```

such that:

1. `J=-I` and the active projection `A` is the identity, so its normalized
   Hilbert mass is `a=1`;
2. the affine endpoint commutant has the positive local density promised by
   `(ASE4)`; but
3. the normalized adjoint density of the old compressor commutant is

```text
dim End_(Gamma x S_3)(rho_n)/dim(rho_n)^2
  =2/(9 |EL_r(F_(2^n))|) -> 0.                         (FAH2)
```

Consequently no covariance construction imposing only the fixed affine
endpoint table, identification of its `S_3` with the Toeplitz head, and
commutation with the residual `Gamma` action can prove `(SHD1)`.  A successful
cell must use ordinary words which see the infinite coefficient action itself
or must recurse inside the particular residual algebra that erases the local
endpoint density.  Finite head symmetry, even with exact active-channel
transitivity and active mass one, is insufficient.
