title: Cyclic conjugacy of partial-isometry swaps does not synchronize their sources
---

Transporting swap cells transitively around any finite cycle or
vertex-transitive graph does not identify their source projections.
Coordinate projections give an exact stationary model with mutually
orthogonal sources and zero transport defect.

A common-source argument therefore needs a literal common coordinate, a
pulled-back comparison, or a holonomy moment; graph expansion and equal
source ranks do not suffice.
