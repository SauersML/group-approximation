Choose a real cocycle representing the nonzero Maslov class and exponentiate
small real multiples. Universal coefficients give a second-homology class
on which the real class has value `alpha!=0`; triviality of the exponentiated
class would force `theta alpha in Z`. A null sequence can avoid that
discrete set. Pointwise convergence of the cocycles to one is immediate.
Jiang's Lemma 2.9 and the immediately following remark identify the
inclusion of constant scalar cocycles as an injective map into the second
cohomology of a Bernoulli action of a property `(T)` group. Hence all
selected classes remain non-coboundaries with measurable Bernoulli
coefficients.
