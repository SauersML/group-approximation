Let `Gamma <= G` be a pair as in `left-orderable-group-with-rigid-compression-pair`.
`Gamma` is Kazhdan, and it is left-orderable as a subgroup of the
left-orderable group `G`.  `Gamma` is infranormal and not normal in `G`.  A
finite infranormal subgroup is normal (`finite-infranormal-subgroup-is-normal`),
so `Gamma` is infinite.  Therefore `Gamma` is an infinite left-orderable Kazhdan
group.  ∎

The route records the direction of cost.  Any left-orderable nonsofic group
obtained from the rigid-defect mechanism answers Navas's Question 3 on the way.
