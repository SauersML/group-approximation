Citation import of a preprint. Source read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv HTML
rendering: A. A. Gaifullin, *Finite generation, algebraicity, and representation
stability for homology of Torelli groups*, arXiv:2606.13517 (submitted 11 June 2026).

- **Theorem A**, verbatim: "Suppose that k≤g−2 and b+p≤1. Then the abelian group
  H_k(I_{g,p}^b;Z) is finitely generated."

At `k = 2` the hypothesis is `g >= 4`. The import records only what the source
asserts. It has not been refereed, and this graph has not re-derived the proof.

Also read on 2026-09-13, for the weaker statements in the claim:

- Kassabov–Putman, arXiv:1807.01338v3 (Math. Ann. 376 (2020)), page 2,
  **Theorem A**, verbatim: "H_2(I_g^b) is finitely generated as a Z[Sp_2g(Z)]-module
  for g ≥ 3 and b ∈ {0,1}."
- Minahan–Putman, arXiv:2504.00211, **Theorem B** (from the HTML rendering):
  `H_2(I^b_{g,p}; Q)` is finite dimensional for `g >= 5` and an algebraic
  representation of `Sp_{2g}(Z)` for `g >= 6`.
