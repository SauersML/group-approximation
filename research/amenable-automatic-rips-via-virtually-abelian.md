Let `G` be amenable and automatic. By the first prerequisite `G` is virtually
abelian; `G` is finitely generated, being automatic. By the second prerequisite
some Rips complex of `G` is contractible.

This is a genuine reduction, not a restatement: the first prerequisite is a
structure theorem that mentions no complex. Virtually abelian groups are automatic
and amenable, so the second prerequisite is implied by the target; it is established
(every virtually abelian group acts geometrically on Euclidean space). So this route
reduces Zaremsky Problem 4.1 to `amenable-automatic-groups-are-virtually-abelian`.
