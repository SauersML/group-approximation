**OPEN.** Let `E ⊆ N_{≥1}` be finite and `U = N_{≥1} ∖ E`. Then
`G_U = ⟨a, t | [a, t^k a t^{-k}] = 1 (k ∈ U)⟩` embeds in a finitely presented
simple group. Here `G_U = A(Γ_U) ⋊ Z`, with `Γ_U` the circulant graph on `Z` whose
connection set is `U`.

**Why it matters.** It implies, with no hypothesis, that the set of c.e.
presentations of groups embeddable in finitely presented simple groups is
`Σ_3`-complete. This is item 5 of `boone-higman-is-equality-of-two-sigma-3-index-sets`.
Every `G_U` here has word problem decidable in polynomial time, uniformly in `E`.

## Known facts (lane proofs, elementary)

1. **`E = ∅`.** Then `G_U = Z ≀ Z ≤ F`, and it is Boone–Higman.
2. **Not in `F`.** Suppose `E ≠ ∅`, `d ∈ E`. Then `a_0, a_d` generate a free group
   of rank 2, so `G_U ⊄ F` (Brin–Squier).
3. **Not in `V`.** Take `d = max E`. Then `2d ∈ U`. The vertices `-d, 0, d` span an
   induced subgraph that is one edge `{-d,d}` plus the isolated vertex `0`. So
   `Z^2 * Z ≤ G_U`, hence `G_U ⊄ V`. The fact that `Z^2 * Z` does not embed in `V`
   is Bleak–Salazar-Díaz, Trans. AMS 2013, cited and not re-read.
4. **Not linear over `Z`.** `⟨a, t^{max E + 1}⟩ ≅ Z ≀ Z` is solvable and not
   polycyclic.
5. **Bounded-width realizations are never faithful (proved).** Let
   `ψ : A(Γ_U) -> ∏_k H_k` be a homomorphism. Suppose each coordinate `ψ_k` kills
   every `a_j` outside an interval `I_k` of diameter `< D`. Pick `d ∈ E` and
   `(L+1)d ≥ D`, and set
   `c_L = [a_0, [a_d, [a_{2d}, … [a_{Ld}, a_{(L+1)d}]…]]]`. Then:
   - **`c_L ≠ 1`.** Induct inward-out. The centralizer of a vertex generator is
     generated by its star, and the support of reduced words is well defined.
     `a_{(j+1)d}` is outside the star of `a_{jd}`, and the retraction killing a
     vertex kills each commutator with it.
   - **`ψ_k(c_L) = 1` for every `k`.** No `I_k` contains both `0` and `(L+1)d`.
     Killing `a_0` kills the outer bracket, and killing `a_{(L+1)d}` kills the
     innermost one.

   So any faithful action must move points between the regions indexed by
   different lamps. The naive "each lamp acts inside a window of fundamental
   domains of `t`" design used for `Z ≀ Z ≤ F` cannot extend to `E ≠ ∅`.
6. **No lamplighter hosts of the naive form (corollary of 5).** Suppose `E ≠ ∅`,
   and let `H` be any group. Then there is no embedding `G_U -> H ≀ Z` sending
   `a` into the base and `t` to an element with nonzero `Z`-component `s`.
   - **Why:** `a_j` is supported in `supp(a) + js`. So each coordinate projection
     of the base kills every `a_j` outside a set of `j` of diameter
     `≤ diam(supp a)/|s|`. The coordinate projections are jointly injective on the
     base, so item 5 applies.
   - **Scope:** this is not a general obstruction. A host `H` containing `G_U`,
     with `t` in the base, trivially works.

## Candidate hosts

- `2V`, which contains `Z^2 * Z`, or `DV_F` of
  `one-fp-simple-group-contains-every-brin-thompson-subgroup`, which contains
  every `nV`.
- The generator `t` should translate one coordinate. The generator `a` should
  mix fundamental domains `D_0, …, D_{max E}` in a second coordinate.
- A general theorem would need a shift-equivariant version of the embeddings of
  right-angled Artin groups into Brin–Thompson groups. That would cover all
  locally finite circulant non-commutation graphs.
