The second premise supplies a hyperbolic Kazhdan group with positive
second Betti number and flexible HS stability. The first gives a
nonhyperlinear finite central extension and explicitly proves that
extension hyperbolic. This is the existing Dogon--Vigdorovich-based
route with its geometric conclusion retained, not a new application
of Liu's compression theorem. The stability premise remains OPEN.
