**ESTABLISHED** (proof: `compression-rings-no-finite-dim-representations-proof`).

**Setting.** Let `R ≠ 0` be a finitely generated unital ring with `ψ ∈ Aut(R)`. Let `R_0 ≤ R` be a unital subring with
`ψ(R_0) ⊆ R_0`, and let `z ∈ R^×` commute with `R_0`. These are (R2)-(R4) of `matricial-compression-ring-exists`.
Put `I = ( ψ(z) p ψ(z)^(-1) − p : p ∈ R_0 )`, the two-sided ideal of (R5).

**Theorem.**
- **(A) Finite quotients.** If `J ⊴ R` and `R/J` is finite, then `I ⊆ J_ψ`. Here `J_ψ = ∩_k ψ^k(J)` is again an
  ideal of finite index.
- **(B) PI quotients.** If `J ⊴ R` and `R/J` is a nonzero ring satisfying a polynomial identity, then `I ≠ R`.
- **(C) Consequently (R5), `I = R`, forces all of the following:**
  - `R` has no nonzero finite quotient ring;
  - `R` has no nonzero PI quotient;
  - `R` has no nonzero ring homomorphism to `M_n(K)`, for any field `K` and any `n ≥ 1`.
  - Under (R1), for every exact embedding `R → ∏_ω M_(N_j)(k_j)` and every `N`, the set `{ j : N_j ≤ N }` is not
    `ω`-large. So `N_j → ∞` along `ω`.
- **(D) Dimension drop.** Assume (R1)-(R5), `R_0` generated by `r_1, …, r_m`, and an exact embedding `ι` with
  coordinates `x ↦ x_j`. Let `A_j^(k) ≤ M_(N_j)(k_j)` be the unital `k_j`-algebra generated by
  `ψ^k(r_1)_j, …, ψ^k(r_m)_j`. Then for each `K ≥ 1`, for `ω`-almost every `j`,

  ```text
  A_j^(0) ⊋ A_j^(1) ⊋ … ⊋ A_j^(K),   so   dim_(k_j) A_j^(0) ≥ K + 1.
  ```

  In particular the coordinate dimension of `R_0` is unbounded along `ω`.

## What this kills

Every candidate ring that has a nonzero finite-dimensional representation over some field dies at (R5). This holds
for any `ψ`, `R_0` and `z`. In particular:
- **PI rings.** Matrix rings `M_n(A)` over finitely generated commutative rings die, with any automorphism. So do
  monomial automorphisms of Laurent polynomial rings, quantum tori at roots of unity, rings finite over their
  centre, and the Weyl algebra in positive characteristic.
- **Residually finite rings.** Any finitely generated ring with a nonzero finite quotient dies. Examples are group
  rings `Z[Λ]` or `F_q[Λ]` (augmentation), and crossed products `LC(X,F_q) ⋊ Γ` in which `Γ` has a finite quotient
  acting through a finite partition of `X`. This contains Attempt 3 of `matricial-compression-ring-exists`.
- **Bounded-degree models.** Any (R1) embedding with bounded matrix sizes dies.

So a witness must have no finite-dimensional representations at all, like the simple infinite rings of
`simple-ring-with-centralizer-compressing-automorphism`. On top of that, `R_0` must be seen with unbounded dimension
in every exact model, and `ψ` must strictly drop that dimension at each iterate.

**Invariants and the step where they die.**
- In (A), the invariant is the finite set of ideals of index `|R/J|`, which `ψ` permutes. The compression becomes an
  equality in the finite quotient.
- In (B), it is Kaplansky's theorem together with Artin–Tate, which turn a PI quotient into a finite one.
- In (D), it is the `k_j`-dimension of the coordinate algebra: an equality `A_j^(k+1) = A_j^(k)` on an `ω`-large set
  makes the defect vanish.

In each case the failure occurs at (R5).

**Relation to the route.** In `xmf-via-matricial-compression-ring-proof`, Step 5 already derives that `EL_4(R)`
has no finite quotient, and hence that `R` has none. That derivation goes through (R1) and the MF-radical
machinery. Part (A) proves the ring statement directly from (R2)-(R5). Parts (B)-(D) are not consequences of that
step.
