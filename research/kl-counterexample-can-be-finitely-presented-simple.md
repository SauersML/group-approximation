**OPEN.**  If `kervaire-laudenbach-nonsingular-conjecture` fails, it fails over
a finitely presented simple group.

Such a group would be a finitely presented simple group that is neither
hyperlinear nor MF, and its word problem is solvable by Kuznetsov.

The only route recorded is
`kl-fp-simple-counterexample-via-decidable-counterexample-and-bh`: a
counterexample with solvable word problem, plus `boone-higman-conjecture` for
that one group.
