`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath` records Theorem E: the base `G` is residually
finite and `W = (Z/2Z) wr_(G/Gamma) G` is the permutation wreath product.
`residually-finite-groups-are-surjunctive` makes `G` surjunctive. With the
residually finite lamp `Z/2Z` and the `G`-set `G/Gamma`,
`rf-lamp-wreaths-over-surjunctive-bases-are-surjunctive` gives surjunctivity
of `W`.
