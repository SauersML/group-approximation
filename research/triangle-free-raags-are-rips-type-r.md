Let `Γ` be a finite simplicial graph without triangles. Then the marked group
`(A_Γ, V(Γ))` is of type R: there is `r_0` such that the Rips complex
`R_r(A_Γ, V(Γ))` is contractible for every `r ≥ r_0`.

Imported from Li–Sánchez Saldaña, arXiv:2608.24279, Theorem 1.5: route
`triangle-free-raags-are-rips-type-r-citation`.
