Section 9. Almost surely p_c(K_(m,epsilon))<=1/(1+h_n/2)<1, uniformly in rank m and epsilon for fixed n. The arbitrary-generating-set version uses h_H. The law of K is dependent; this does not prove Bernoulli nonuniqueness on the original Cayley graph.
