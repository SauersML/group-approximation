## Why sufficient

The second prerequisite says that an `F`-linear sofic group has a stably finite
group algebra over `F`. With `F = F_2` and the first prerequisite, `F_2[R^x]` is
stably finite. Stable finiteness at `n = 1` is direct finiteness.
