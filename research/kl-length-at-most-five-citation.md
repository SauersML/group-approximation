- J. Howie, *The solution of length three equations over groups*, Proc.
  Edinburgh Math. Soc. 26 (1983), 89--96.
- M. Edjvet and J. Howie, *The solution of length four equations over groups*,
  Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 326 (1991), 345--369.
- A. Evangelidou, *The Solution of Length Five Equations Over Groups*, Comm.
  Algebra 35 (2007), 1914--1948. The published abstract says the article
  "settles the KL conjecture for equations of length five".

Each result is stated for an arbitrary coefficient group and every nonsingular
equation of the given variable length. Klyachko--Thom, Algebr. Geom. Topol. 17
(2017), Remark 1.2, record the three together. Length one is trivial, and
length two is the square-root case: `t a t b = 1` becomes `(t a)^2 = b^(-1) a`
after substituting for `t`.

**Trust surface.** These statements are taken from the citation route
`length-five-kl-fence-citation`, which read Evangelidou's abstract and thesis
outline. The Howie and Edjvet--Howie papers were not re-read for this route.
