**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `I` be the left ideal of `F2[A8]` generated by the
aligned derivatives of the 234 boundary words, put
`M=F2[A8]/I`, and let `m=1+I`.  In the characteristic-two affine
countermodel write

```text
E=M semidirect A8,       mu=(m,e),
U=lambda_E(mu),          K=<h_a,h_b>~=S3,
n=[K:Stab_K(mu)]=|K.mu|.                              (AFO1)
```

Here `n` denotes the size of the `K`-conjugacy orbit of `mu`, equivalently
the orbit of `m` in `M`.  Both raw transpositions move `m`, by the exact
countermodel audit, so `n` is `2`, `3`, or `6`.

On this frame the five boundary residuals vanish and the collision residual
is a nonidentity involution in the left regular representation.  Therefore
its initial and final high-singular projections coincide and have normalized
rank `1/2`.  On the other hand

```text
rank(U-E_K(U))/dim l2(E) >= 1-1/n.                   (AFO2)
```

Consequently, if `n` is `3` or `6`, then for every sufficiently small
threshold `t>0`,

```text
rank(P_t)>dim(E)/2=rank(Z_t).                         (AFO3)
```

The map `Z_t:Ran(P_t)->Ran(Z_t)` cannot be injective, so `(PAI3)` fails.
Thus the universal claim
`atlas-six-row-polar-high-spaces-meet-source-at-acute-angle` can survive its
archived affine frame only if

```text
n=2,
Stab_K(m)=<h_a h_b>~=C3.                              (AFO4)
```

The only possible escape was the exact membership query

```text
(1+h_a h_b)m=0.                                      (AFO5)
```

The two raw letters are reconstructed from
`atlas-raw-compressor-source-words.json` by the authoritative
`raw_s3_letters` routine, using the archived inner alignment.  The
lightweight certificate
`atlas_affine_raw_c3_membership_certificate.py` evaluates `(AFO5)` against
the four archived dual functionals for the fully closed boundary ideal.  With


the identity has index `0`, the three-cycle has index `22`, and every one of

```text
1+h_a h_b notin I.                                   (AFO6)
```

Thus `n` is `3` or `6`, so `(AFO3)` holds.  This archived finite regular
frame refutes `atlas-six-row-polar-high-spaces-meet-source-at-acute-angle`
