OPEN.  In the exact raw `K~=S3` packet, decompose the relative chart operator
and the running class-11/class-30 hard prefixes under diagonal `K`
conjugation.  Construct fixed word-visible projections or mixed Gram
coordinates which, with dimension-independent normalized-HS loss,

1. suppress or determine the two extra standard-channel reduced sources from
   `s3-standard-operator-channel-has-fivefold-reduced-gauge`;
2. identify the remaining three coordinates with the extension labels
   `2,3,3 epsilon` whose `S4 -> S3` restrictions meet in `sigma`; and
3. make the same three coordinates available to the raw/comb path products
   on one common finite multiplicity cut.

The construction may use the classical-fold `S4 <- S3 -> S4` geometry only
to choose carrier coefficients.  It may not assume that the cross-chart hard
prefix is an exact `S4` representation in the microstate.  Authentication
must follow from named relators in the canonical packet.

## Attempts

- **Boundary rows are insufficient.**  All 234 first-boundary relations have
  an exact regular-margin affine escape with noncollapsed raw `S3`
  directions.  Hence classes 11 and 30 alone cannot isolate the extension
  triple.
- **A source word is not a channel equation.**  The raw and comb artifacts
  give literal words of lengths 25 and 175 and their Leavitt values.  Their
  existence does not itself add a microstate relation.  A successful attack
  must name the packet relator or normal-closure consequence which constrains
  their coefficient blocks.
- **Collision 19243 alone is insufficient.**  Its exact `S4` regular-margin
  model and its parent-block identity show that one involution equation does
  not retain two child branches.  It may be used only together with a mixed
  hard/comb coordinate which sees the proper refinement.
- **First valid observable to seek.**  Use a mixed common-`U` Gram coordinate
  coupling two different packet subgroups.  Bare A4 rectangle holonomies are
  gauge artifacts and cannot authenticate the triple.
- **The first mixed C3 Gram is still locally blind.**
  `atlas-mixed-c3-gram-has-local-zero-interval` gives a collision-zero
  exact-regular local family on which that gauge-invariant Gram coordinate
  sweeps an interval containing zero.  Therefore collision, regular profiles,
  and one mixed compression do not isolate the extension triple.  Any use of
  this coordinate must consume the full twelve common-product equations, not
  only the local `S3` collision cell.
- **Promising conditional endpoint.**
  `one-cross-gram-moment-extracts-the-common-polar-gauge` shows that once two
  hard/comb transports have common source and target and nearly full
  cross-Gram mass, one relative multiplicity gauge is exposed with a
  dimension-free square-root estimate.  The missing word theorem is to force
  that cross-Gram scalar before the two extension paths separate; the polar
  step afterward is already available.
