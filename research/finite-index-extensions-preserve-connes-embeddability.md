Let `N subset M` be an inclusion of finite factors of finite Jones index.
Then `N` is Connes embeddable if and only if `M` is Connes embeddable.

Consequently, if `N` is non-CE, every finite-index extension arising from a
finite Q-system, finite group crossed product, or finite-depth Morita
amplification is still non-CE.  If one such extension is independently
identified trace-preservingly with `L(Gamma)`, then `Gamma` is
non-hyperlinear.  But the finite-index construction itself does not produce a
canonical group trace; identifying the endpoint as a group factor is already
the decisive additional theorem.
