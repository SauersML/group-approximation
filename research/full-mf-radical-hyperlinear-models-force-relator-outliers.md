Let the established finitely presented group `Q=<X|R>` satisfy
`Res_MF(Q)=Q`. If `Q` is hyperlinear and `U_n` is any canonical regular
microstate sequence, then after passing to a subsequence there are one fixed
`r in R` and `epsilon>0` such that

```text
||r(U_n)-1||_op>=epsilon,
||r(U_n)-1||_2->0.
```

Otherwise all finite defining relators vanish in operator norm and the tuple
classes define a norm-corona homomorphism from `Q`. Full MF residual makes
that homomorphism trivial, contradicting the canonical HS separation of any
nonidentity word.

For

```text
p_n=1_[epsilon/2,infinity)(|r(U_n)-1|)
```

one has `p_n!=0` but

```text
tr(p_n)<=4 epsilon^(-2)||r(U_n)-1||_2^2->0.
```

Thus the normal-Kazhdan group is an explicit outlier factory. Turning this
forced sparse region into a constant HS obstruction still requires a
dimension-independent ownership, return, or capacity theorem.
