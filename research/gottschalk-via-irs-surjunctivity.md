**The argument.**
1. Suppose `G` is not surjunctive. By artifact Lemma 2.3, some finitely generated subgroup `K = F_r/N` is
   not surjunctive. The injective automaton restricts to the subgroup generated by its memory, and acts
   there factor by factor.
2. By `atomic-irs-strict-designs-are-nonsurjunctive-quotients` for normal `N`, the atomic IRS `delta_N`
   carries a strict design.
3. This contradicts `free-group-irs-carry-no-strict-design`.

**Prerequisites fail independently.**
- The atomic equivalence is established.
- The strengthening can fail without refuting the goal. Only
  `irs-strict-designs-yield-nonsurjunctive-groups` would turn a failure into a counterexample.
- Its co-sofic part is established (`co-sofic-irs-carry-no-strict-design`), and the hole is exactly the
  non-co-sofic IRS.

This route is a heretic strengthening, not a plan to prove the goal. It names the statement that
invariant-accounting arguments actually prove.
