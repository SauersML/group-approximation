**ESTABLISHED (import).**

**Statement.** A (bounded geometry, discrete) metric space of finite asymptotic
dimension has property A. In particular a finitely generated group of finite
asymptotic dimension has property A.

**Source.** R. Willett, *Some notes on property A*, arXiv:math/0612492,
Corollary 2.2.11 (Higson-Roe).
