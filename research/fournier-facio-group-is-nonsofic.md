The finitely presented torsion-free group `G` of
`fournier-facio-torsion-free-skeleton` is not sofic.

This is the conclusion of Fournier--Facio, *A torsion-free non-sofic group*,
arXiv:2608.02025, Theorem 1.3, for the same group whose internal compression
data is recorded in the skeleton node.
