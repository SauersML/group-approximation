Citation import. No proof is re-derived here.

C. Bleak, H. Bowman, A. Gordon, G. Graham, J. Hughes, F. Matucci, E. Sapir,
*Centralizers in the R. Thompson group V_n*, arXiv:1107.0672v3 (arXiv stamp
12 Sep 2011). Read on 2026-09-12 from the arXiv PDF, pages 1 and 4, by lane
`ex-q34-leavitt-hs`.

- **Abstract, last sentence:** "Along the way we give a short argument using
  revealing tree pairs which shows that cyclic groups are undistorted in V_n."
- **Introduction, first paragraph:** "Finally, we give a separate short argument
  which shows that all infinite-cyclic subgroups within V_n are embedded without
  distortion."
- **Section 1.1:** "In the final section of the paper we prove the following
  theorem." It is followed by **Theorem 1.3:** "Suppose alpha in V_n so that
  <alpha> ~= Z. The group <alpha> is undistorted as a subgroup of V_n."

Secondary restatement, not checked verbatim: Belk--Bleak--Matucci--Zaremsky,
*Progress around the Boone--Higman conjecture*, arXiv:2306.16356, Theorem 4.6(3).
An arXiv HTML summary gives it as the statement that groups with distorted cyclic
subgroups, such as finitely generated nilpotent groups that are not virtually
abelian or `BS(m,n)` with `|m| != |n|`, do not embed into `V`. It cites the paper
above together with Bleak--Matucci--Neunhoeffer and Burillo--Cleary--Roever.
