Stable finiteness makes positive elements of compact Cu class have
actual spectral-gap support projections. On a fixed-class stratum,
a persistent high spectral projection has the whole finite class,
so the support is locally continuous. This proves necessity by
restricting global comparison to compact subsets of equality strata.

For sufficiency fix c=(a-eta)+ and close the set where [c(x)]=[b(x)]
is nonzero. Its closure K is compact, a has a uniform gap there, and
cutoff persistence gives [a(x)]=[b(x)] on K. The support of a splits
K into finitely many clopen fixed-class pieces. The assumed bundle
equivalences supply an exact comparison vector on each piece.

Off K every nonzero source c has strict fiberwise comparison with b.
The exact relative-embedding theorem extends the prescribed vector.
For a noncompact base use a compact neighborhood of the cutoff
support, prescribe zero on its boundary, and extend by zero. A
compact-support approximate-unit argument verifies global hereditary
membership. Letting eta decrease to zero proves [a]<=[b].
