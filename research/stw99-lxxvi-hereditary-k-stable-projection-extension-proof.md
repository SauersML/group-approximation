For C=Her(d) and a frame v_0, let r=v_0v_0*. Strict comparison makes
C_r=(1-r)C(1-r) nonzero. Stable rank one makes U_1(C) act transitively
on E(p,d), with stabilizer U_1(C_r), and explicit polar corrections
give continuous local sections. Simplicity makes C_r full in C.
Hereditary K-stability and naturality identify its unitary inclusion
with a K-theory isomorphism in every homotopy degree. The principal
bundle long exact sequence makes E(p,d) weakly contractible.

Normalized straight-line contractions give uniform local connectivity.
Approximating a frame by d(x)e p(x) and normalizing proves lower
semicontinuity with moving coefficients, including prescribed singleton
boundary fibers. Michael's finite-dimensional selection theorem then
gives the exact relative frame. A monotone approximate unit and Dini's
theorem put the selected frame in the global hereditary module.

All nonstandard hypotheses appear explicitly in the claim. The
artifact gives the formulas and primary selection reference; no
real-rank-zero projection approximate unit is used.
