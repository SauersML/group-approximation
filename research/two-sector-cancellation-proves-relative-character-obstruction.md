The artifact identifies the full exterior-square source with two
copies of B_4 tensor B_4 and the full target with two copies of
Lambda^2 B_4, and derives the exact operator on arbitrary tensors.

If T eta=0 for nonzero eta, invertibility of c makes xi=R eta nonzero.
The source k_+(xi,eta) maps to an alternating diagonal in each target
sector, so both vanish.

If R eta=0, set tau=T eta, which is nonzero. The vector

    k_+(sigma tau,eta)+k_-(tau,sigma eta)

has two nonzero components in distinct source central sectors. The
two target contributions in each sector are equal with their
arguments reversed; symmetry makes their sum zero in characteristic
two. This source vector is fixed by scalar Frobenius and is binary.

The boundary representation contradicts a proposed left inverse,
and conditional expectation handles unrestricted ambient support.
The criterion uses only c=b^-1a, proving invariance under a shared
left change. No sufficiency claim is made when R and T are injective.
