Citation import of a preprint. Source read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv abstract and
HTML rendering: M. Ershov, *On finite presentability of some partial Torelli
subgroups of Aut(F_n)*, arXiv:2601.01377 (submitted 4 January 2026).

- **Theorem 1.1**, verbatim: "The groups IAC_{n,d} and IAR_{n,d} are finitely
  presented when n ≥ d+115. Moreover, the groups IAC_{n,1} and IAR_{n,1} are finitely
  presented for all n ≥ 26." Here `IAC_{n,d}` and `IAR_{n,d}` are the preimages
  under `ρ_ab` of `Col_{n,d}` and `Row_{n,d}`, the matrices whose first `d` columns
  (resp. rows) coincide with those of the identity.
- Abstract, verbatim: "It is a long-standing open problem whether the subgroup of
  IA-automorphisms IA_n=Ker ρ_ab is finitely presented for n≥4."
- Introduction (from the HTML): "it will not be enough to assume the result for
  smaller values of d. Instead, we will need to imitate certain steps of the proof
  for d=1, but in a more general setting." It also records that finite presentability
  of the surface partial Torelli groups `I_{g,b;d}` is open apart from `d = 0`.
