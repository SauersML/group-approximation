Citation import, not a reproof. Source: M. Abert and N. Nikolov, *Rank gradient, cost of groups and the rank versus Heegaard genus problem*, arXiv:math/0701361v3, Section 1.

- **Setting**, verbatim: "Let Γ be a finitely generated group. A chain in Γ is a sequence Γ = Γ 0 ≥ Γ 1 ≥ . . . of subgroups of finite index in Γ."
- **Farber chains**, verbatim: "We say that a chain (Γ n) is Farber, if the action of Γ on the boundary of its coset tree T = T (Γ , (Γ n)) is essentially free, that is, if almost every element of ∂T has trivial stabilizer in Γ. This is the case for example when the chain consists of normal subgroups of Γ and their intersection is trivial. Note that then ∂T is simply the profinite completion of Γ with respect to (Γ n) endowed with the normalized Haar measure."
- **Theorem 1**, verbatim: "Let (Γ n) be a Farber chain in Γ . Then RG(Γ , (Γ n)) = cost( E)− 1 where E denotes the orbit relation given by the action of Γ on the boundary of the coset tree T (Γ , (Γ n))."
- **Rank gradient**, verbatim: "RG(Γ , (Γ n)) = lim n→∞ d(Γ n)− 1 / |Γ : Γ n|".
