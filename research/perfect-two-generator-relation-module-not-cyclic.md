ESTABLISHED.  Let `Q = F_2/K` be a **perfect** group given as a quotient of the
free group of rank two.  Then the relation module `M = K/[K,K]` is **not
cyclic** as a `ZQ`-module.  Equivalently: `K` is not the normal closure in
`F_2` of a single element, so this presentation of `Q` is not one-relator.

## Proof

Coinvariants of the relation module are `M_Q = M ⊗_{ZQ} Z ≅ K/[F_2, K]`.  The
five-term exact sequence in integral homology for `1 → K → F_2 → Q → 1` gives

    H_2(F_2) --> H_2(Q) --> K/[F_2,K] --> H_1(F_2) --> H_1(Q) --> 0 .

Here `H_2(F_2) = 0`, `H_1(F_2) = Z²`, and `H_1(Q) = 0` because `Q` is perfect.
Exactness therefore makes `K/[F_2,K] ↠ Z²` surjective.

If `M` were cyclic as a `ZQ`-module, generated by some `[x]`, then `M_Q` would
be cyclic as an abelian group — the image of `[x]` generates it.  A cyclic
abelian group admits no surjection onto `Z²`.  Contradiction.

For the "equivalently": if `K = <<x>>_{F_2}` then `K` is generated by
`F_2`-conjugates of `x`, and modulo `[K,K]` that conjugation action factors
through `Q`, so `M` is the cyclic `ZQ`-module on `[x]`.  ∎

## What it blocks, and what it deliberately does not

It blocks the tidiest negative plan for Pestov's Question 4.9: take a
two-generator perfect nonsofic group and hope the kernel of `F_2 ↠ Q` is
secretly normally generated by one word, producing a one-relator presentation of
a nonsofic group for free.  That cannot happen, for the same generating set, on
homological grounds alone — no search is needed and no property of `Q` beyond
perfectness enters.  This is the kill behind
[[nonsofic-one-relator-via-perfect-quotient-compression]], and it applies in
particular to the two-generator perfect targets this corpus keeps producing —
including, were it ever certified, the candidate in
`notes/TORSION_FREE_NORMAL_GENERATION_HULL_QUOTIENT.md`, which carries a standing
prohibition banner and is **not** used as an input anywhere here.

It does not block: embedding `Q` in a one-relator group (that lane dies
differently, at [[torsion-free-one-relator-has-no-kazhdan-subgroup]]); using more
than two generators and eliminating them afterwards; a nonperfect nonsofic
target; or a one-relator group merely containing enough of `Q`.  The bound is
`rank(F) = 2` and perfectness, and both are load-bearing: over `F_n` the
argument gives only that `M_Q ↠ Z^n`, which still forbids cyclicity for every
`n >= 2`, but says nothing once `H_1(Q) ≠ 0`.

## Relation to the corpus

This is the general form of a remark already recorded on the Kervaire--Laudenbach
lane: [[simple-kl-via-abelianized-relation-module]] found the *first-homology*
test uninformative for degree kernels `N_m`, and closed by noting that over a
presentation source `P ↠ Q` the kernel's abelianization as a `ZQ`-module "is a
genuine obstruction to one-relator compression of that source, because `Q` need
not make it cyclic."  This node is that sentence, proved, in the case the
compression lane actually wants.
