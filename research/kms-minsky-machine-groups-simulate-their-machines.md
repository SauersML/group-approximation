ESTABLISHED (literature import; clauses 4--5 corrected 2026-09-13 after review by
`ex-verify-logic-quantum`).  For every Minsky machine `M` with `K`
glasses (deterministic or not) and every prime `p`, there is a finitely
presented group `G(M)`, computable from `M`, with the following properties.

1. `G(M)` lies in `A_p^2 A cap ZN_(K+1) A`.
2. It contains the normal subgroup `T` generated by the letters `x_u`, which is
   abelian of exponent `p`.
3. `G(M)/T` is finitely generated metabelian.
4. For **nonzero** elements of the semigroup `S(M)`, the configuration words
   `x_(q_i A_0) * a_1^(m_1) * ... * a_K^(m_K) * A_1^(al_1) * ... * A_K^(al_K)`
   are equal in `G(M)` iff the words `q_i a^m A^al` are equal in `S(M)`.  Here
   `f * a_i = f^(-1) f^(a_i) (f^(a_i^(-1)))^(-1) f^((a_i')^(-1))` and
   `f * A_i = [f, A_i]`.
5. The elements `x_(i,w)`, with `i in {1,2,3}^K` and `w in W cup W_0` nonzero, are
   independent: the surjection `G(M) -> Ḡ` sends them to the basis `z_(i,w)` of the
   elementary abelian group `T_1`.  Here `W_0` is the set of nonzero `q`-elements
   of `S(M)` (with `A_0`), and `W` those of the machine-free semigroup `Š`.
6. The letters of `G(M)` act on `T_1` by explicit automorphisms.  On `u in W_0`
   a letter acts trivially or through basis vectors indexed by `u` and by `u`
   followed by that letter, and which case applies depends only on the
   `A`-letters of `u`.

**Correction (clauses 4--5).**
- **What changed.**  The earlier text asserted clause 4 for all elements of `S(M)`, with
  `q_0 = 0`, and `T ≅ T_1`.  It followed the wording of KMS v5, Theorem 4.3(b) and Lemma 4.14.
- **Why both fail at zero.**  Under the displayed relations G1--G8 both fail for zero elements.
  - The automorphism formulas of v5 pp. 23--24, taken over `S(M)` without the stop relation,
    also satisfy G1--G8.
  - In that model, `x_(q_0 A_0)` is nontrivial, and the words of distinct stop configurations
    are distinct.
  - The v5 proofs cover nonzero elements only.
- **The published version.**  Bull. Math. Sci. 7 (2017) 309--352 keeps `q_0` nonzero in `S`
  (§3.1 p. 325, Remark 3.10 p. 328).
- **The full statements.**  They hold after adding the stop relations: see
  [[kms-group-with-stop-relations-simulates-all-words]].
- **Review.**  `research/artifacts/ex-review-logic-quantum-2026-09-12-part1.md` §1.4.
