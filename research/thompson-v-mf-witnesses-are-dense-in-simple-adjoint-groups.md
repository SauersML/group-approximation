## Setting

Notation is as in `thompson-v-mf-iff-opnorm-s4-s3-defect-gap-vanishes`:

```text
G0 = S4 *_<a> S3 = < a, b, c | a^2, b^3, (ab)^4, c^2, (ac)^3 >,      V = G0 / << r5, r6, r7, r8 >>,
D(rho) = max_(5 <= i <= 8) || rho(r_i) - 1 ||_op ,       delta_n = min{ D(sigma) : sigma nontrivial irreducible, dim sigma <= n }.
```

For a compact simple real Lie algebra `h`, `Inn(h)` is the subgroup of `Aut(h)` generated by the
operators `exp(ad Y)`, `Y` in `h`. It is a compact connected group. For a homomorphism `tau` into
`Inn(h)`, orthogonal for an `Inn(h)`-invariant inner product on `h`, write
`D(tau) = max_i ||tau(r_i) - 1||_op`.

## Statement

Let `sigma : G0 -> U(n)` be a nontrivial irreducible unitary representation with `D(sigma) < 1/2`.
Let `K` be the closure of `sigma(G0)`, `l = Lie(K)` and `m = [l, l]`.

1. **Splitting.** `m` is nonzero with trivial centre. It is an orthogonal sum
   `h_1 (+) ... (+) h_r` of simple ideals, and these ideals are the only simple ideals of `m`.
2. **Invariance.** Every `h_a` is invariant under `Ad(k)` for all `k` in `K`. So
   `tau_a(g) = Ad(sigma(g))|_(h_a)` is a homomorphism `G0 -> Aut(h_a)`, orthogonal for
   `Re tr(X^* Y)`, with `D(tau_a) <= 2 D(sigma)`.
3. **Density in the inner group.** `tau_a(G0)` lies in `Inn(h_a)` and is dense there. In particular
   the component group of the closure is invisible on `h_a`, and no outer automorphism occurs.
4. **Dimension.** `tau_a` is nontrivial and `delta_(dim h_a) <= 2 D(sigma)`. So along any sequence
   of witnesses with `D -> 0`, the minimal dimension of a simple ideal tends to infinity.
5. **Converse.** Let `h_j` be compact simple Lie algebras and `tau_j : G0 -> Inn(h_j)` nontrivial
   homomorphisms, orthogonal for `Inn(h_j)`-invariant inner products, with `D(tau_j) -> 0`. Then `V`
   is MF. Density is not needed here.

**Corollary (classical trichotomy).** For a family `X` in `{SU, SO, Sp}`, let `X(k)` stand for
`su(k)`, `so(k)` or `sp(k)`, over the `k` for which it is simple. Define:

```text
Gap(X):  there is eps > 0 such that for every k, every Inn(X(k))-invariant inner product on X(k),
         and every homomorphism tau : G0 -> Inn(X(k)) with dense image,  D(tau) >= eps.
```

Then

```text
V is not MF   <=>   Gap(SU)  and  Gap(SO)  and  Gap(Sp).
```

Only the family of dense images is needed. By item 3, no other family of homomorphisms can carry a
witness past the threshold `min(1/2, delta_(N_exc)/2)`, where `N_exc` is the largest dimension of an
exceptional compact simple Lie algebra.

## Why it matters

The w17 theorem `thompson-v-gap-witnesses-need-unbounded-lie-closure` left the closure `K` of a
witness as an arbitrary compact Lie group with large derived algebra. This result removes, for small
defect, every feature of `K` except one connected compact simple adjoint group `Inn(h)`. It removes:

- the torus;
- the finite component group;
- permutations of simple factors;
- outer automorphisms.

The two uses of simplicity of `V` are:

- the permutation action on the simple ideals, which is killed by Lemma O and the transfer tool;
- the finite group `Ad(K)|_h / Inn(h)`, which is killed by the transfer tool.

The principal-logarithm lemma, that an orthogonal automorphism within `sqrt 3` of the identity is
`exp` of an inner derivation, is what places the relator images inside `Inn(h)`.

For `SU`, `Inn(su(k)) = PSU(k)`. Since `||Ad(u) - 1||_(su(k)) <= eps` forces `u` to lie within
`eps` of the scalars, via eigenvalues `lambda_a conj(lambda_b)`, `Gap(SU)` is a gap for dense
projective unitary representations whose relator images are near scalars. That remark is not used.
