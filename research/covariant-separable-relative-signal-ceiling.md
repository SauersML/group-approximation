Let `rho` be a separable state on `C^d tensor bar(C^d)` invariant under
`U tensor bar(U)` for every unitary `U`, so
`rho = beta I + gamma |Omega_d><Omega_d|`.

**Theorem.** `gamma <= 1/(d+1)`.

Equivalently, in any covariant separable second-moment classicalization, the
coefficient carrying a relative trace such as `tau(UV*)` is at most
`1/(d+1)`. This does not rule out higher-order or whole-table de Finetti
arguments. It rules out the direct hope that a covariant separable two-register
endpoint retains a dimension-free fraction of the coherent multiplication
signal.
