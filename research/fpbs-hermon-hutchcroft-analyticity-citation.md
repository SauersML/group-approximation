Citation import, not a reproof. J. Hermon and T. Hutchcroft, *Supercritical
percolation on nonamenable graphs: Isoperimetry, analyticity, and exponential
decay of the cluster size distribution*, arXiv:1904.10448v3 (PDF dated
October 6, 2020), Section 1.1, read from the PDF on 2026-09-17. Verbatim:

> Let `H_v` denote the set of finite connected subgraphs of `G` containing `v`,
> and say that a function `F : H_v -> C` has subexponential growth if
> `limsup_{n->infinity} (1/n) log sup{|F(H)| : H in H_v, |E(H)| <= n} <= 0`.

> **Corollary 1.3.** Let `G = (V,E)` be a connected, locally finite,
> nonamenable, transitive graph, let `v in V` and let `F : H_v -> C` have
> subexponential growth. Then `E_p[F(K_v) 1(|K_v| < infinity)]` is an analytic
> function of `p` on `(0,p_c) ∪ (p_c,1)`.

The same section names the truncated susceptibility
`chi^f(p) = E_p|K_v| 1(|K_v| < infinity)` explicitly among the quantities that
"all depend analytically on p throughout the entire supercritical phase".
This route asserts only that the cited corollary says what the target records.
