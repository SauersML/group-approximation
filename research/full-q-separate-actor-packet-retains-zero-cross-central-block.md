**ESTABLISHED SEPARATE-ACTOR ZERO TYPE.**  In the exact binary
eight-summand chart put

```text
K=<L_0,r,l>,                 F=e_(K,chi),
g=J_1J_2,                    H=<K,g>,
P=<K,J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3>.                                  (SAP1)
```

Then adjoining the four separately constrained actors creates no new
`chi`-compatible twisted double coset:

```text
F C[P] F = F C[H] F.                                    (SAP2)
```

Indeed the exact binary coset audit gives

```text
                         H                  P
[group:K]              1344               20160
K-double cosets         156                 796
chi-compatible           52                  52.        (SAP3)
```

Every compatible `H` double coset remains a distinct compatible `P` double
coset.  Equality of the last entries in `(SAP3)` therefore proves `(SAP2)`.

The relative-interface calculation already proves that, in this
`52`-dimensional corner, the two-sided ideal generated by

```text
h=FJ_1J_2F                                                (SAP4)
```

has dimension `44` and misses the corner identity `F`.  By `(SAP2)` the
same statement holds in the separately constrained packet.  Consequently
there is an exact finite-dimensional unitary representation `pi` of `P`
such that

```text
pi(F)!=0,                    pi(FJ_1J_2F)=0.             (SAP5)
```

Thus separate occurrence of `J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3`, including the two source
sign flips, does not force positive cross-Gram energy for the literal native
quarter source on every finite packet type.  Any positive proof must use a
relation outside this complete finite source/native actor packet.

The scope remains finite-packet exact.  The representation in `(SAP5)` is
not asserted to extend to the whole Steinberg group, so this claim does not
alone refute `binary-leavitt-arbitrary-profile-native-cross-gram`.  It
proves that the missing full-presentation occurrence cannot be replaced by
finite stabilizer decomposition of the actors already in `(SAP1)`.

No Property `(T)`, canonical-trace substitution, local computation,
literature theorem, or Lean compilation is used.  The exact audit was run
once through MSI.
