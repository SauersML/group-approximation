Although `alpha_n` may not be a representation, `Ad alpha_n` is a genuine unitary
representation of `Gamma` on the Hilbert space `M_{d_n}` with normalized HS norm:
every scalar multiplier cancels under conjugation. Its fixed subspace is
precisely `C_n`. Orthogonal projection onto it is the trace-preserving conditional
expectation `E_n`. The Kazhdan inequality on the orthogonal complement of the
invariants gives the displayed bound in the claim, with the same `(F,kappa)`
for every dimension and every multiplier.

If a uniformly operator-bounded sequence `x_n` represents an element of the
ultraproduct commutant, each of the finitely many commutators indexed by `F`
tends to zero along the ultrafilter. The inequality gives
`||x_n-E_n(x_n)||_2 ->_omega 0`. Each `E_n` is operator-norm contractive, so
`E_n(x_n)` is still uniformly bounded and belongs to `C_n`. This proves inclusion
in `[C_n]_omega`; the reverse inclusion is immediate.

The models in the required affine-symplectic claim have exactly scalar
multipliers and a Kazhdan base, hence satisfy this argument while failing
flexible rounding. The counterexample does not import Liu's theorem and does
not assert anything about hyperbolic groups.

For the scalar-expander strengthening, let `E` be the Kazhdan Heisenberg cover
used there, with central generator `z`, and let `A` be the exponent of its finite
abelianization. From each central eigenspace representation with
`rho_n(z)=exp(2 pi i/n)I`, choose any irreducible summand `rho'_n` of dimension
`r_n`. Its determinant is a character of `E`, so

```
exp(2 pi i A r_n/n)=det(rho'_n(z))^A=1.
```

Therefore `n` divides `A r_n`, and `r_n>=n/A -> infinity`. Restriction along the
section gives a scalar-projective model `alpha'_n` of the base. Its commutant
equals that of `rho'_n(E)` because `E` is generated by section values and the
scalar centre; it is scalar by irreducibility. The same Kazhdan adjoint
inequality now has `E_n(x)=tr(x)I`.

No flexible rounding exists for this smaller sequence either: the rectangular
bimodule construction from §5 of the required claim uses only that `E` is
Kazhdan and that `rho'_n(z)` is a nontrivial scalar tending to one. Both still
hold. The regular trace calculation for the entire eigenspace is not inherited
by this arbitrary irreducible selection, and is not used.
