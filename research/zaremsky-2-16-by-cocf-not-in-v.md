If cloning systems on finite groups are always (locally) coCF, yet some such
`T(G_*)` does not embed in `V`, then Problem 2.16 is answered with Q1 yes and Q2
no. Such a `T(G_*)`, being coCF and not in `V`, would also refute Lehnert's
conjecture (`lehnert-conjecture-every-cocf-group-embeds-in-v`). Both requirements
are open; the standing candidates are `V_(G,θ)`, `θ ≠ id`.
