## Why sufficient

Elek--Szabo's embedding theorem places every countable sofic group inside a
countable simple sofic group.  Embed `W`; portability sends its nontrivial
invisible sign to a nontrivial element of the envelope's MF radical.  The
radical is normal, so simplicity makes it the whole envelope.  The universal
property of the radical then kills every homomorphism to an MF group.
