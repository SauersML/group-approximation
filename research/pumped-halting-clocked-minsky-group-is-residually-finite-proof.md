The full proof is Lemma B (Sections B1--B4) of
`research/artifacts/rf-fp-pumped-minsky-part2-2026-09-12.md`.

- **B1.**  Simplicity makes command domains disjoint on partial
  configurations, so Lemma 2.1 gives forward-then-backward normal forms.
  Forward computations are finite by universal halting.  Backward ones have
  length at most `2 l_tau + 1` because at least every other step adds a clock
  coin.
- **B2.**  Pumping up, lifting the `M`-chain, and unpumping shows that two
  nonzero `q`-words with equal `M`-classes, equal `A`-letters and equal
  `iota` coincide.  Zero-ness occurs exactly through stop in `M`, or through a
  drain at `iota = -l_tau`.
- **B3.**  In `S̄ = S(P(M)) + {a_pi^D = a_pi^(2D), a_rho^D = a_rho^(2D)}`,
  modulo the Rees ideal `I_R`, the invariants survive modulo `D`.
  - For `D > |iota| + max l_tau` and `D` larger than the pairwise differences
    of `iota`, prescribed elements stay nonzero and distinct.
  - For `R` above the exponents occurring in their finite classes they avoid
    `I_R`.
  - The quotient is finite.
- **B4.**
  - `F` is generated by the basis vectors of dead elements and by quotients of
    basis vectors with equal images.  It is invariant because letters act by
    right multiplication, with cases determined by `A`-letters.  The move from
    `W` to `W_0` under `A_0` is compatible with the quotients.
  - `T_1/F` is finite, and the image of `w` is nonzero.
  - `Ḡ/F = (T_1/F) x| A` with `A` finitely generated metabelian is residually
    finite.
  - Outside `T`, P. Hall's theorem applies.

The proof does not quote KMS's own semigroup argument (proof of Theorem 3.9).
Its `S̃` quotient with idempotent pumping letters kills every word carrying
`A_(K+1) A_(K+2)` (Section 5 of the artifact).
