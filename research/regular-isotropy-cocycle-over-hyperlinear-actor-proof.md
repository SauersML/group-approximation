The cocycle identity and the relation-normalizer convention give

```text
v_gv_h
 =b_g (u_g b_h u_g^*) u_gu_h
 =b_g(x)b_h(g^(-1)x)u_(gh)
 =v_(gh).                                               (HIP1)
```

Thus `g |-> v_g` is a unitary representation. The Cartan expectation in the
relation algebra is supported on the fixed set, so

```text
(tr_n tensor tau_R)(v_g)
 =integral_(Fix(g)) tr_n(b_g(x)) dmu(x).                (HIP2)
```

Assume `(HIC2)`. For `g,h in Gamma`,

```text
< v_g,v_h >_2
 =(tr_n tensor tau_R)(v_h^*v_g)
 =(tr_n tensor tau_R)(v_(h^(-1)g))
 =1_(g=h).                                              (HIP3)
```

Hence the algebraic map

```text
C[Gamma] -> M_n(L(R)),       sum_g a_g lambda_g |-> sum_g a_g v_g
```

preserves the canonical trace and the `L^2` norm. It therefore extends to an
injective normal trace-preserving star homomorphism
`L(Gamma)->M_n(L(R))`. Its range is exactly the von Neumann algebra
generated by the `v_g`, proving `(HIC3)`.

If `Gamma` is hyperlinear, `L(Gamma)` is Connes embeddable. Connes
embeddability passes to von Neumann subalgebras with the inherited trace, so
`N_b` contains no non-CE tracial algebra. This proves the payload-erasure
assertion.

For the calibration, let every integer act trivially on `T`. Then `u_k=1`
in the identity-relation algebra and
`b_(k+l)(z)=z^(k+l)=b_k(z)b_l(z)`. Haar Fourier orthogonality gives
`integral_T z^k dz=1_(k=0)`, while `z` generates `L^infinity(T)`. Thus
global diffuse phase cancellation is genuinely possible even with infinite
stabilizer; its output here is exactly the CE algebra predicted by `(HIC3)`.
