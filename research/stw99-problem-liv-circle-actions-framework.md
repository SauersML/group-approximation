**Problem LIV of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**
Find a general classification framework for `T`-actions on Kirchberg
algebras with stably finite crossed products, covering the gauge
actions of Cuntz--Krieger algebras (Bratteli--Kishimoto classify these
via trace-scaling automorphisms of AF algebras with finitely many
extremal tracial rays; the invariant recovers Krieger's dimension
group and shift equivalence — CDE observation) and gauge actions of
more general UCT Kirchberg models.

## Attempts

* Gabe--Szabo covers flows with isometric shift absorption, which
  forces `O_∞`-stable crossed products — categorically disjoint from
  this problem's stably finite crossed products; the wanted framework
  is the DUAL of a trace-scaling-`Z`-action classification on stably
  projectionless/AF-like algebras with arbitrary tracial cone base
  (Bratteli--Kishimoto need finitely many extreme rays).  So the
  problem reduces by Takai duality to: classify trace-scaling
  automorphisms of stable AF (more generally classifiable stably
  finite) algebras with INFINITE extremal tracial boundary — one more
  face of the recurring wild-boundary equivariant glueing frontier
  (cf. XXXVIII/XXXIX/XLII); Bratteli--Kishimoto's Rokhlin property is
  what the finitely-many-rays hypothesis buys.  Recorded reduction:
  any wild-boundary trace-scaling Rokhlin theorem immediately yields
  the framework by dualizing.
