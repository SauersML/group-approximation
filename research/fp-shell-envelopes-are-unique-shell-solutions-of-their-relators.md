**ESTABLISHED** (direct proof; not independently reviewed; no novelty
claimed).

Let `P` be infinite with finite generating set `S`, and let `nu: P -> N`
be a bijection. Use the shell construction of
`shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`: `rho_g` is the
regular action transported to the even integers (odd integers fixed),
`C_n = 0^n 1 C`, `p = 0^infinity`, and `hat(g)(0^n 1 z) = 0^(rho_g(n)) 1 z`.

For any permutation `pi` of `N` put `h(pi)(p) = p` and
`h(pi)(0^n 1 z) = 0^(pi(n)) 1 z`. This is a homeomorphism of `C`. Let `Y`
be a finite generating set of `V`. For a finite set `R` of words in the
letters `Y` and `S^(+-1)`, let `Sol(R)` be the set of tuples
`pi = (pi_s)_(s in S)` in `Sym(N)^S` such that every word of `R` evaluates
to the identity of `Homeo(C)` when `y` is sent to `y` and `s` to `h(pi_s)`.

1. `Sol(R)` is closed in `Sym(N)^S` with the topology of pointwise
   convergence.
2. Suppose `<Y, S | R>` presents `E_nu` through `y -> y` and `s -> hat(s)`.
   Then `Sol(R) = {rho}`, where `rho = (rho_s)_(s in S)`.

So if `E_nu` is finitely presented, its relabeling tuple `rho` is the only
point of a closed set cut out by finitely many relators. Such a presentation
exists because a finitely presented group has a finite presentation on every
finite generating set.

Contrapositive criterion: suppose every finite set of words valid in `E_nu`
has a second solution `pi != rho`, and the tuple need not be regular or come
from any group. Then `E_nu` is not finitely presented. The swap in
`free-shift-shell-envelopes-are-not-finitely-presented` is one way to
produce such solutions.

The same proof applies to the one-singularity stabilizer `A_1`. Replace `Y`
by a finite generating set of `Fix_V(p)`, and use a presentation of `A_1` on
`Y` and `hat(S)`. Such a presentation exists when `A_1` is finitely
presented, since `A_1` is generated by `Fix_V(p)` and `hat(S)` (see
`shell-finite-point-stabilizers-are-finitely-generated`).

This claim gives no finite presentation of any shell group. It does not
decide `decidable-inputs-have-fp-shell-envelopes`.
