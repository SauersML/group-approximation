The amenable lamp kernel gives the partial-assembly isomorphism.
The host splitting identifies ordinary wreath assembly with
μ_G⊕μ_(G,I). The stabilized comparison and going-down identify the
punctured topological groups with ⊕_[s]ℤ and 0. Rank functionals make
T injective; locality gives the finite retraction N with NT=1 and
ker N=K_0^inv(A). Taking kernels and cokernels of this block map
proves all three identities. Section 6 of the artifact supplies the
scope and the decreasing-tail caveat.
