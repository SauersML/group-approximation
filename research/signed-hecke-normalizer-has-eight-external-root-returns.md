**ESTABLISHED ROOT-NORMALIZER AUDIT.**  In the actor basis
`(2,4,5,6,7,8,9)`, let `L_0` and `lambda_0` be the order-`8192` signed
Hecke pair generated by the ten directed transvections

```text
6->5, 5->4, 4->2, 8->2, 9->4,
9->2, 9->7, 5->7, 6->7, 6->8.                         (ERN1)
```

with negative character on the first three arrows and positive character
on the last seven.  Among the `42` constant root transvections on these
coordinates, exactly `21` normalize both `L_0` and `lambda_0`.  Exactly
eight of those do not belong to `L_0`; in source-to-target notation they are

```text
4->7, 5->8, 6->9, 7->2,
8->4, 8->7, 9->5, 9->8.                               (ERN2)
```

Thus the same-channel obstruction does not extend to every two-exit word.
For any arrow `s->t` in `(ERN2)`, choose a spare coordinate `k`.  The
Steinberg commutator relation and `t_0s_0=1` give the exact binary-Leavitt
word

```text
[x_(t,k)(t_0),x_(k,s)(s_0)]=x_(t,s)(1).               (ERN3)
```

This is a coefficient-sensitive two-channel exit and a nonidentity return
to `Norm(L_0,lambda_0)`.  It preserves the signed Hecke projection `Q`, but
because it is an external involutive normalizer, its action on the
one-character isotypic carrier has the form

```text
N_(s,t) tensor E_(s,t),                                (ERN4)
```

with an uncontrolled multiplicity involution `E_(s,t)`.  Hence `(ERN3)` is
a genuine candidate second gauge-bearing occurrence, not yet an ungauged
bridge.  To close the EL20 interface one still needs a mixed relation which
identifies one `E_(s,t)` with the literal Whitehead gauge (so their product
cancels) while retaining the one-step Singer label movement.

The enumeration is exact over `F_2`: it closes the ten generators to all
`8192` elements while carrying the character bit, then tests conjugation of
the ten signed generators by each of the `42` transvections.  No Property
`(T)`, trace profile, literature theorem, or approximation input is used.
