The prerequisite gives the nonidentity element
`z in Rad_MF(E_3)`.  Thus `Rad_MF(E_3)` is nontrivial.  By
`universal-mf-quotient`, a countable group is MF exactly when its MF radical
is trivial.  Hence `E_3` is not MF.
