title: Virtually nilpotent actions on finite-dimensional spaces have a uniform nuclear-dimension bound
---

Let `V` be a finitely generated virtually nilpotent group, let `X` be a
compact metrizable space of covering dimension `r<infinity`, and let `V` act
on `X` by homeomorphisms.  Write `h(V)` for the Hirsch length and `d(V)` for
the polynomial-growth degree.  Then

```text
dim_nuc(C(X) rtimes V)
    <= 2 * h(V)! * 9^d(V) * (r+1)^2.                    (HWV)
```

The estimate is uniform over all spaces of dimension at most `r` and all
actions of all such groups with fixed upper bounds on `h(V)` and `d(V)`.
