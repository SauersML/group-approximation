**PROPOSED ESTABLISHED.** This is a written deduction over the established identity
`fpbs-graphing-cost-betti-cycle-dimension-identity`; see the route
`fpbs-bernoulli-morse-collapse-criterion-proof`.

**Setting.**

- `Gamma` is an infinite group, finitely generated by `S`, and `b` is its Bernoulli shift.
- `R` is a finite list of words that are trivial in `Gamma`. A *cell* is `(x, r)`, with boundary path the closed
  Schreier path from `x` reading `r`.
- A *Morse matching* is a Borel injective partial map `M` from cells to edges. Each matched edge lies on its cell's
  boundary and is traversed once. The map is *well-founded*: almost surely there is no infinite chain of removed edges
  in which each edge lies on the cell of the previous one.
- The mass `mu(M)` is the measure of the matched cells, and `z(Phi_S) = |S| - 1 - beta_1^(2)(Gamma)`.
- The *Morse deficit* is `m(R) = z(Phi_S) - sup_M mu(M)`.

**Theorem A.**

- Removing the matched edges leaves a generating subgraphing of cost `|S| - mu(M)`. So `C(b) <= |S| - mu(M)`, and
  `mu(M) <= z(Phi_S)`.
- If `m(R) = 0`, then `C(b) = 1 + beta_1^(2)(Gamma)`: tail compactness holds for `Gamma`, and so does fixed price.

**Theorem B.**

- `m` is nonincreasing under enlarging `R`.
- Let `P_L` be the list of all trivial words of length at most `L`. Then
  `lim_L m(P_L) = C_sub(S) - 1 - beta_1^(2)(Gamma)`, where `C_sub(S)` is the infimal cost of generating Borel
  subgraphings of the Cayley graphing `Phi_S`.

**Proposition C (calibration).** A relator `w^m`, with `w` of order `m` whose boundary cycle traverses each edge once,
contributes mass at most `1/m`. For an infinite one-relator group `<S | w^m>` this equals `z(Phi_S)`, so the local
ceiling is exactly sharp.

**Checks.**

- `Z^2` has `m = 0`, using sparse iid columns. The naive matchings are not well-founded.
- Products `Gamma_1 x Gamma_2` whose factors have generators of infinite order have `m = 0`, which recovers fixed
  price one.
- On `F_2 x F_2`, `m = 0`, and the unmatched cells have density exactly `beta_2^(2) = 1`.

**Decomposition.** For `Gamma` and `S`, `C(b) = 1 + beta_1` follows from (Q1), and (Q1) follows from `Target`,
(Q2) and (Q3):

- (Q1) some finite `R` has `m(R) = 0`;
- (Q2) depth compactness, `m(R) = lim m(P_L)`;
- (Q3) subgraphing attainment, `C_sub(S) = C(b)`.

None of (Q1)–(Q3) is established for any class where fixed price is open. The target
`fpbs-bernoulli-cycle-tail-compactness` stays OPEN.
