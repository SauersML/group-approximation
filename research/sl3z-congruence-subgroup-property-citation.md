**Sources.**
- H. Bass, J. Milnor and J.-P. Serre, *Solution of the congruence subgroup
  problem for SL_n (n >= 3) and Sp_2n (n >= 2)*, Publ. Math. IHES 33 (1967).
- J. Mennicke, *Finite factor groups of the unimodular group*, Ann. of Math. 81
  (1965).

**Pin.** The primary papers were not read here. The statement is pinned from the
English Wikipedia article "Congruence subgroup", raw wikitext fetched on MSI on
2026-09-13, line 116, verbatim:

> "its origin is in the work of Hyman Bass, Jean-Pierre Serre and John Milnor,
> and Jens Mennicke who proved that, in contrast to the case of SL_2(Z), when
> n >= 3 all finite-index subgroups in SL_n(Z) are congruence subgroups."

Here a congruence subgroup of `SL_n(Z)` is a subgroup containing some
`Gamma(m)`, which is the definition the same article uses.

**Surjectivity of reduction.** `SL_n(Z/m)` is generated by elementary matrices,
since `Z/m` is a finite product of local rings, and elementary matrices lift to
`SL_n(Z)`.

**Trust surface.** A secondary source for a classical theorem; the attribution is
as that source gives it.
