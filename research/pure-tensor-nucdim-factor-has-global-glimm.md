Let `A` be a pure C*-algebra and let `B` be a separable C*-algebra with finite
nuclear dimension.  Then `A tensor_min B` has the Global Glimm Property: for
every positive `a` and `eps > 0` there is a square-zero element `r` in the
hereditary subalgebra generated by `a` such that `(a-eps)_+` lies in the ideal
generated by `r`.

## Attempts

- Known sufficient condition: `pure-tensor-nucdim-no-pi-quotient-is-pure`
  (no quotient of the product has a nonzero compact properly infinite Cuntz
  class) gives purity, hence the Global Glimm Property.
- Thiel--Vilalta (arXiv:2204.13059) characterize the Global Glimm Property
  through nowhere scatteredness together with ideal-lattice and property (V)
  type conditions; verifying those for `A tensor_min B` needs control of the
  ideal lattice of a minimal tensor product, which is not available without
  exactness assumptions, and was not achieved here.
- Seth--Vilalta remark after their Theorem 5.6 that algebras with `m`-comparison
  and the bounded divisibility of `nucdim-factor-transfers-bounded-divisibility`
  are nowhere scattered; so a positive answer to the Global Glimm Problem would
  settle this claim.
- Assembling square-zero elements colour by colour from an order zero
  approximation of `B` fails for lack of control over the overlap of different
  colours.
