For each forbidden cell, the two packet implementers anticommute.  Because
the corresponding stable letters commute and are involutions, their external
factors are also involutions and carry the opposite anticommutation sign.
Across distinct cells all factors commute.  Symplectic reduction therefore
identifies the algebra generated by the external factors with the tensor
product of `n` Pauli algebras, namely `M_(2^n)(C)`.  Every finite-dimensional
module over this full matrix algebra is `C^(2^n) tensor C^L`, and the double
commutant theorem gives `(RMT1)`.

Any additional cell commuting with the old cell algebra factors through its
commutant.  Applying
`projective-holonomy-rank-forces-exponential-multiplicity` to that residual
representation gives `(RMT2)`.  Substitution of `m=2^nL` proves `(RMT3)`.
