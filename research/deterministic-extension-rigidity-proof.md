Notation as in the target. Let `(E_(b,f)(z))` be a `D`-quantum family for `X` on `W`, with
`D ≥ max(D_0, R)`. `B_D(z)` has diameter `2D`, so by (Q2) all operators at sites of `B_D(z)`
commute.

## Step 0. Expansion inside a ball

As in Step 0 of `quantum-rigidity-product-stability-proof`, a product of operators at sites of
`B_D(z)` is the sum, over the patterns `π` on `B_D(z)` that extend the prescribed letters and
occur in `X`, of `∏_(u ∈ B_D(z)) E_(π(u))(u)`. This uses (Q1), (Q2) and (Q3).

## Step 1. Marginals

Put `E^B_b(z) = Σ_f E_(b,f)(z)` and `E^F_f(z) = Σ_b E_(b,f)(z)`. Both are partitions of unity
commuting at a common site, and `E_(b,f)(z) = E^B_b(z) E^F_f(z)`.

## Step 2. The base marginal commutes

`(E^B_b(z))` is a `D`-quantum family for `Y`.
- (Q1) and (Q2) are inherited.
- (Q3): if `β` is a pattern on `B_D(z)` that does not occur in `Y`, then no pattern of `X` on
  `B_D(z)` with `B`-part `β` occurs, since `X` projects into `Y`. By Step 0,
  `∏ E^B_(β(u))(u) = 0`.

`Y` is `D_0`-rigid and rigidity is monotone in `D`, so all `E^B_b(z)` commute.

## Step 3. Transport identity

Let `s ∈ S'`. Then `zs` and `zW_s` lie in `B_D(z)`, since `D ≥ R`. In every pattern of `X` on
`B_D(z)`, the fibre letter at `zs` equals `θ_s` of the base letters on `zW_s` and the fibre
letter at `z`. Expanding `E^F_(f')(zs)` by Step 0 and collecting terms gives

```text
E^F_(f')(zs) = Σ_(p, f : θ_s(p, f) = f')  E^F_f(z) ∏_(w ∈ W_s) E^B_(p(w))(zw),
```

a sum of products of pairwise commuting operators.

## Step 4. Transport along paths

Let `z = u s_1 ⋯ s_n` with `s_i ∈ S'`. By induction on `n`, Step 3 puts every `E^F_f(z)` in
the unital algebra `𝒜` generated by the `E^F_(f'')(u)` and the `E^B` at the sites of the
windows `u s_1 ⋯ s_(i-1) W_(s_i)`.

## Step 5. Fibre operators commute with base operators

Let `u, z ∈ Λ`. Since `S'` generates `Λ` as a monoid, `z = u s_1 ⋯ s_n` with `s_i ∈ S'`. Every
`E^B(u)` commutes with the generators of `𝒜`:
- with the `E^F(u)`, which sit at the same site;
- with every `E^B`, by Step 2.

So `E^B(u)` commutes with `E^F(z) ∈ 𝒜`.

## Step 6. Fibre operators commute

Let `z, z' ∈ Λ`, and write `z = z' s_1 ⋯ s_n` with `s_i ∈ S'`. By Step 4, `E^F(z)` lies in the
algebra generated by the `E^F(z')` and some `E^B`. `E^F(z')` commutes with the `E^F(z')`
(orthogonal idempotents at one site) and with every `E^B` (Step 5). So it commutes with
`E^F(z)`.

## Step 7. Conclusion

All `E^B` and `E^F` commute, so all `E_(b,f)(z) = E^B_b(z) E^F_f(z)` commute. This is item 1.

**Item 2.** The projection `X → Y` is equivariant, so `Stab(y, f) ⊆ Stab(y) = 1`.

**Item 3.** By the transport rule and monoid generation, `f` is determined everywhere by
`y` and `f(e)`.
