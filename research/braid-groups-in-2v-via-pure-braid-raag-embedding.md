`PB_m` has finite index `m!` in `B_m`. By `pure-braid-groups-embed-in-raags` it
embeds in a finitely generated right-angled Artin group `A_Γ`. By
`every-raag-embeds-in-brin-thompson-group-2v` (Salo, arXiv:2103.06663; an
unrefereed preprint), `A_Γ` embeds in `2V`. So `PB_m` embeds in `2V`.
