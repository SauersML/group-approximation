**ESTABLISHED SCOPED CLOSED-LOOP FENCE.**  Retain the two exact nonnormal
compression-preimage identities `(CPW3)`, the two branch inverse and
crossed-zero rows, the transported reverse outputs, one individually fixed
arm per branch, and the literal parent-generation fold

```text
C_0C_1=J.                                               (CCF1)
```

This is the smallest finite full-Leavitt loop containing both compression
branches and an object-erasing return row.

The whole table has an exact finite marked model.  On `C^3` put

```text
J  =diag( 1,-1,-1),
C_0=diag(-1,-1, 1),       C_1=diag(-1, 1,-1),          (CCF2)
W_0=(0 2),                W_1=(0 1).                   (CCF3)
```

Then `C_0C_1=J` and `W_iJW_i^(-1)=C_i`.  Tensor with the two Pauli factors
of the one-arm range-sum model, so every inverse, crossed-zero, transported
output, fixed-arm, and fold occurrence is exact and the `J=-1` projection
has positive rank.  For each compression identity choose its label
Hilbert-hotel matrix on a separate finite label factor, give that matrix
identity action on multiplicity, and give the preimage and image
Whiteheads the same reservoir factor `W_i`.  Thus

```text
T_i K_i T_i^(-1)=J_i                                  (CCF4)
```

and all source/range Grams hold exactly, including the actual coefficient
preimages from `(CPW1)--(CPW3)`.

The remaining gauge is visible without an estimate.  The diagonal signs in
`(CCF2)`, including the fold word, lie in the normal character subgroup,
while the two compression/Whitehead chart edges map to the transpositions
in `(CCF3)`.  Hence their outer quotient is `S_3`.  The shortest alternating
closed loop obeys

```text
(W_0W_1)^3=1,                                          (CCF5)
```

but appending one source-moving edge has quotient `W_i`, not `1`.  More
generally, any word in this occurrence language whose chart-edge gauges
telescope to the identity has trivial `S_3` quotient and preserves the
marked character object.  A word which moves that object to the first
branch character has nontrivial quotient and acts nontrivially on the
three-dimensional reservoir.  The fold `(CCF1)` cannot change this quotient.

Thus closing a **finite** two-branch compression/fold path does not leave
one ungauged signed-Hecke move.  It either closes the label object as well,
or retains the finite `S_3` reservoir holonomy.  This rules out exactly the
closed loop generated by the two compression-preimage Whiteheads and the
range-sum fold; it does not rule out an additional full-Leavitt relation
whose occurrence leaves this three-character quotient.  No Property `(T)`,
Kazhdan input, trace profile, literature theorem, or Steinberg lift is used.
