title: Finite-ideal nuclear targets with algebraically simple regular layers solve LXXVIII

Let `B` be a nonzero unital separable nuclear C-star algebra with finite
ideal lattice and no normalized 2-quasitrace.  Assume that every simple
subquotient `D` of `B`

1. is algebraically simple, equivalently its Pedersen ideal is all of `D`;
2. has almost-unperforated `W(D)`.

Then `B` is `O_infinity`-stable and strongly purely infinite.  Consequently,
for every separable exact C-star algebra `A`, every nuclear star homomorphism

```text
phi:A->B
```

is strongly `O_infinity`-stable.  In particular every LXXVIII map into such
a target is `O_infinity`-stable.

The algebraic-simplicity hypothesis is not cosmetic: it converts the
possibly unbounded lower semicontinuous quasitrace supplied by a stably
finite nonunital layer into a bounded trace which extends to an ambient
quotient.  The Toeplitz--Cuntz example shows the conclusion fails without a
condition of this kind.
