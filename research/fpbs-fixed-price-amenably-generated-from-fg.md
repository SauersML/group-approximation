**Statement.** Assume every infinite finitely generated group has fixed
price. Let `Gamma` be a countably infinite group generated by finitely many
amenable subgroups `A_1, ..., A_m` (equivalently, by finitely many amenable
subgroups together with a finite set). Then `Gamma` has fixed price.

**Scope.** The class contains every finitely generated group, every countable
amenable group, and further groups that are neither finitely generated nor
locally finite, for example `Q * Z` and `SL(2,Q)` (generated by its two root
subgroups, each isomorphic to `Q`). See §3 and §5 of the artifact.

**What it adds.** The countable passage artifact
(`research/artifacts/fpbs/docs/fixed-price-countable-passage.md`, §6) notes
that its "no fixed pseudocost" shape (A) cannot be excluded by an argument
passing only through subgroups of `Gamma`. This claim applies the hypothesis
to one finitely generated **supergroup** `G_m` of `Gamma`. Gaboriau's
amalgamation formula then turns fixed price of `G_m` into
`C(s_Gamma) = C(Gamma)`, which excludes shape (A) on this class.

**Unconditional form.** Theorem 2' of the artifact: with `B_i` finitely
generated groups containing copies of the infinite `A_i`, the tower
`G_i = G_(i-1) *_(A_i) B_i` (`G_0 = Gamma`) ends in a finitely generated
`G_m`, and fixed price of `G_m` alone implies fixed price of `Gamma`.
