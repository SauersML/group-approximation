**ESTABLISHED.** Proof: `finite-image-exact-kazhdan-torus-models-proof`. It is elementary. It imports
three things:
- Shalom's openness of property (T);
- the stage-lift criterion `lef-mapping-torus-iff-stage-liftable-automorphism`;
- `lef-implies-operator-mf`.

The two commutator lemmas of Frobenius are reproved in the route. They are Lemmas 1 and 2 of
`thompson-gap-witnesses-need-nonamenable-image-proof`.

## Setting

This is the setting of the w17 support-rigidity node (`kazhdan-gapped-mapping-torus-models-are-lef-quotients`,
on the swarm branch). It is restated here so that this node is self-contained.

- `K` is a Kazhdan group generated by a finite symmetric set `S`, with `F = F(S)` the free group on `S`.
- `φ ∈ Aut(K)`, `G = K ⋊_φ Z`, and `t s t^-1 = φ(s)`.
- For each `s ∈ S`, fix words `w_s` and `v_s` in `F` representing `φ(s)` and `φ^-1(s)`. Let `ŵ : F -> F` be the
  endomorphism `s ↦ w_s`.
- By Shalom (`fpbs-shalom-property-t-is-open`), some finite set `R_0` of relators of `K` makes
  `K~ = <S | R_0>` Kazhdan. Fix a Kazhdan constant `κ > 0` of `K~` for `S`.
- Put `r_s = ŵ(v_s)^-1 s`, and `R_Γ = R_0 ∪ ŵ(R_0) ∪ {r_s : s ∈ S}`. This is a finite set of relators of `K`.
- An **exact model** is a homomorphism `U : F -> U(n)` with `U(r) = 1` for every `r ∈ R_Γ`. Its **image** is
  `M = U(F)`.
- A **covariant model** is a homomorphism `π : G -> U(Q)`, where `Q = ∏_i M_(n_i) / ⊕_i M_(n_i)`, together with
  lifts `π(s) = [U_i(s)]`. The class `π(t) = [T_i]` is arbitrary.
- It has **finite-image exact lifts** if, for all large `i`, `U_i` is an exact model with finite image `M_i`.
- Nothing is assumed about spectral gaps. The element orders in `M_i`, the character fields of `U_i` and the
  dimensions `n_i` may all be unbounded. Relators of `K` outside `R_Γ` need only satisfy
  `‖U_i(r) - 1‖ -> 0`, which the corona requires.

## Statement

**Theorem A (exact multiplicity-free wraps; any image).** Let `U` be an exact model and `T ∈ U(n)` a unitary with
`δ = max_s ‖T U(s) T* - U(w_s)‖ < κ`. Let `U♭ = ⊕_(σ ∈ Supp U) σ` be the sum of the irreducible constituents
of `U`, each taken once. Then:

1. `‖U♭(a)‖ = ‖U(a)‖` for every `a` in the group algebra `C[F]`.
2. `ŵ` induces an automorphism `α` of `M = U(F)`, with `α(U(x)) = U(ŵ x)`.
3. There is a unitary `V` with `V U♭(x) V* = U♭(ŵ x)` for **all** `x ∈ F`, exactly.
4. `α` is an isometry: `‖α(m) - 1‖ = ‖m - 1‖` for every `m ∈ M`.

**Corollary A.** Let `π` be a covariant model whose lifts `U_i` are exact models, of any image. Put
`π♭(s) = [U♭_i(s)]` and `π♭(t) = [V_i]`. This is a homomorphism `G -> U(∏ M_(d_i) / ⊕ M_(d_i))` with
`‖π♭(k) - 1‖ = ‖π(k) - 1‖` for every `k ∈ K`. Its wrap is exact at every stage, so `π♭` is induced by genuine
finite-dimensional unitary representations of the finitely presented group

```text
G_Γ = < S, t | R_Γ, t s t^-1 = w_s (s ∈ S) >.
```

So on exact models the wrap is never the obstruction: the approximate implementer `T_i` can always be replaced by
an exact one, with the same kernel on `K`.

**Theorem B (Frobenius core; finite images).** Let `π` have finite-image exact lifts. For large `i`, let `α_i` be
the automorphism of Theorem A. Let `E_i = {m ∈ M_i : ‖m - 1‖ < 1/2}` and `A_i = <E_i>`. Then:

1. `A_i` is an abelian normal subgroup of `M_i`, and `α_i(A_i) = A_i`.
2. `F_i = M_i / A_i` is a finite marked group, and `α_i` descends to a stage lift `ᾱ_i` of `φ` on it.
3. For every relator `r` of `K`, `U_i(r) ∈ A_i` for all large `i`.
4. **(Dichotomy.)** Let `k ∈ K` with `π(k) ≠ 1`. Then there are an infinite set `J` of indices and a
   `φ`-invariant normal subgroup `N` of `K` with the following properties:
   - `(K/N) ⋊_φ̄ Z` is LEF, hence operator MF;
   - the compression `π_J` of `π` to the indices in `J` still has `π_J(k) ≠ 1`;
   - either `k ∉ N`, or `N ≠ K`, `k ∈ N` and `π_J(N)` is abelian.

**Theorem C (witnesses).** Let `K` be Kazhdan. Suppose `G` has no LEF quotient `(K/N) ⋊ Z` with `N` a
`φ`-invariant normal subgroup and `N ≠ K`. This is (NSL+) of the w17 node, and it holds whenever
`Rad_MF(G) = K`. Then every covariant model of `G` with finite-image exact lifts is trivial on `K`.

## What this kills

**The class.** This is the second escape listed under "What survives" in the w17 support-rigidity node. There,
the kernel lifts are exact but their images contain nontrivial elements arbitrarily close to `1`. The finite-image
half of that escape dies here. It includes:
- representations of finite quotients of `K~_Γ = <S | R_Γ>` with irrational character values and growing element
  orders, for example Weil or cuspidal representations of `EL_n` over growing finite fields;
- induced and monomial representations of such quotients;
- any finite direct sum of these, together with any permutation, regular or bounded-exponent blocks. A finite
  direct sum of finite-image models has finite image.

In none of these cases is a spectral gap assumed, and the wrap unitaries `T_i` are arbitrary.

**The invariant.** The *Frobenius core* `A_i = <E_i>` of the stage image: the subgroup generated by the open
operator-norm ball of radius `1/2` about `1`.
- It is abelian and normal in every dimension. This is the dimension-free half of Jordan's theorem.
- It is carried onto itself by every *isometric* automorphism of `M_i`.
- Theorem A shows that the stage automorphism forced by a wrap of defect below `κ` is isometric. The
  multiplicity-free reduction `U♭_i` makes it inner in `U(d_i)`, and multiplicities do not affect operator norms.

**Where every member dies.** At the quotient `M_i -> M_i / A_i`.
- Every relator of `K` eventually enters the ball `E_i`, so it dies in `M_i / A_i`.
- The automorphism descends, so `M_i / A_i` is a finite approximant of a `φ`-invariant quotient `K/N`, carrying
  stage lifts.
- By the stage-lift criterion, `(K/N) ⋊ Z` is LEF. What the core itself carries is abelian.
- For a Kazhdan kernel with no LEF torus quotient, `N = K`. So every stage image `M_i` is abelian, and `π(K)` is
  an abelian quotient of a Kazhdan group without finite quotients, hence trivial.

## Consequence for (LK3) and (EKL1)

The w17 node split the witness condition (LK3) into (LK3-gap) = (NSL+) and (LK3-ng) (no non-gapped nontrivial
model). Theorem C shows:

```text
(NSL+)  ==>  (LK3-ng) restricted to models with finite-image exact lifts.
```

So finite-image exact models add **nothing** to stage lifts, in either direction:
- **Against (EKL1).** A proof of (EKL1) (LEF kernel ⇒ `Rad_MF(G) ≠ K`) through finite-image models, for example
  Weil or cuspidal representations with irrational characters, would already produce a LEF torus quotient
  `(K/N) ⋊ Z` with `N ≠ K`. It is therefore no stronger than the combinatorial stage-lift route.
- **For a witness.** A candidate that satisfies (NSL+) is automatically safe from every finite-image exact model.
  Its remaining MF threats are only those in the next section.

## What survives

A homomorphism `G -> U(Q)` that is nontrivial on the kernel of a witness, and in particular any proof that such a
`G` is MF, must have lifts `U_i` of one of two kinds:
- **(S1) Infinite exact images.** By Corollary A, these are genuine finite-dimensional unitary representations
  `ρ_i` of the finitely presented group `G_Γ`, with an exact wrap, such that:
  - `ρ_i(K~_Γ)` is infinite. So its closure is a compact Lie group whose identity component is not a torus.
    Otherwise the image would be virtually abelian and infinite, and its finite-index abelian subgroup, which is
    Kazhdan, would be infinite and finitely generated, contradicting F4 of the route;
  - the relators of `K` outside `R_Γ` act as nontrivial elements of that dense subgroup, tending to `1`.

  Dense Kazhdan subgroups of compact semisimple groups exist, for example through Galois conjugates of
  higher-rank arithmetic groups. The Frobenius descent does not terminate there, because nothing is discrete.
- **(S2) Inexact lifts.** No lift of `π|_K` is an exact model of any finitely presented Kazhdan cover containing
  `R_Γ`. Here the Kazhdan cover `K~` is itself not seen exactly at any stage.

Theorem A also compresses (S1) into a statement with no wrap at all:

```text
(S1) is nonempty  <=>  some sequence of finite-dimensional unitary representations of the finitely
                        presented group G_Γ has infinite kernel images, has every relator of G tending to 1
                        in norm, and is bounded away from 1 on some k ∈ K.
```

The direction `⇐` is immediate: such a sequence is a covariant model with exact lifts.
