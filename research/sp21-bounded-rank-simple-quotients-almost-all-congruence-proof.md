**Imported inputs (statements only; the sources were not re-read here).**

- **(SR) Margulis-form superrigidity.** For `Γ` as in the target, let `L` be a
  field of characteristic `0` and `X` an adjoint absolutely simple `L`-group.
  Every homomorphism `ρ: Γ -> X(L)` with Zariski-dense image has the form
  `ρ(γ) = τ(σ(γ))` for some field embedding `σ: k -> L̄` and some `L̄`-isogeny
  `τ: ^σG -> X`.
  - For these rank-one groups, superrigidity at each place is Corlette's
    theorem (archimedean) and Gromov--Schoen (nonarchimedean). The global form
    comes from Margulis' argument, as in the arithmeticity proofs.
  - **Trust surface.**
- **(ZD) Zariski density of finite groups of Lie type.** For a type `X` and all
  large `q`, the finite simple group `X(q)` (twisted or not), realized in the
  adjoint representation, is Zariski dense in `X` over `F̄_q`. Łoś applied to
  subgroups of bounded degree carries this to ultraproducts (Larsen--Pink,
  Nori).
- **(Ł) Łoś's theorem**, and the fact that a cocompact lattice is finitely
  presented.

**Proof.**
1. **An infinite family of fixed type.** Suppose the conclusion fails for some
   `r`. Take distinct non-congruence `N_i ⊴ Γ` and `φ_i: Γ ->> S_i` with `S_i`
   of Lie type of rank at most `r`. `Γ` has finitely many normal subgroups of
   bounded index, so `|S_i| -> ∞`. Pass to a subsequence of one fixed type `X`.
   Embed `S_i` in `GL_M(F_(q_i^e))` through the adjoint representation, with
   `e <= 3` and `M = dim X`.
2. **The ultraproduct.** Fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter `U` and put
   `K = ∏_U F_(q_i^e)`, a field. Define `ρ(γ) = (φ_i(γ))_U`, giving
   `ρ: Γ -> GL_M(K)`.
3. **The image is infinite.** If `ρ(Γ)` were finite, its kernel `Γ_0` would have
   finite index and be the normal closure of finitely many words, because `Γ`
   is finitely presented. By (Ł) the `φ_i` kill those words for `U`-most `i`.
   Then `|S_i| <= [Γ:Γ_0]` for `U`-most `i`, contradicting `|S_i| -> ∞`.
4. **Characteristic zero.** If `char K = p > 0`, step 3 contradicts
   `sp21-lattice-positive-characteristic-images-finite`. So `char K = 0`, and
   `q_i -> ∞` along `U`.
5. **Zariski density.** `K` is perfect, so the Zariski closure `H` of `ρ(Γ)` is
   defined over `K` with bounded-degree equations. If `H` were proper in `X`,
   (Ł) would put `S_i` inside a proper algebraic subgroup for `U`-most `i`,
   contrary to (ZD). So `ρ(Γ)` is Zariski dense in `X`.
6. **A Galois conjugate.** Let `A` be the ring generated by the entries of the
   `ρ(γ_j^(±1))` over a finite generating set. Apply (SR) with `L = Frac(A)`:
   `ρ = τ ∘ σ`. Let `A_E` be the finitely generated ring obtained from `A` by
   adjoining `σ(O)`, the coefficients of `τ` and finitely many inverses.
   - By (Ł), for `U`-most `i` the coordinate maps `A -> F_(q_i^e)` are ring
     homomorphisms, and they extend to `θ_i: A_E -> F̄_(q_i)`.
   - Then `φ_i(γ) = θ_i(ρ(γ)) = τ̄_i(θ_i σ(γ))` on generators, hence on all of `Γ`.
7. **Congruence along `U`.** `θ_i ∘ σ: O -> F̄_(q_i)` has kernel a prime `𝔮_i`.
   If `γ in Γ ∩ G(O, 𝔮_i)` then `θ_i σ(γ) = 1` and `φ_i(γ) = τ̄_i(1) = 1`. So `N_i`
   contains a principal congruence subgroup for `U`-most `i`, contradicting the
   choice of the `N_i`.
8. **The consequence for quotients `Γ/M`.** A simple quotient of `Γ/M` is a
   simple quotient `Γ/N` with `N ⊇ M`.
   - If `N` is congruence, `Γ/N` factors through `Γ̄/closure(M)`, which is finite
     by `normal-subgroups-of-arithmetic-lattices-are-congruence-open`. That
     gives finitely many.
   - Otherwise `N` is one of the finitely many exceptions. `QED`
