Distinct transpositions in `S_3` do not commute.  A commuting product-one
face therefore contains an even number of one transposition color and no
other nontrivial color.  Sending each color to a free involution satisfies
every homogeneous face relation.  Two colors already yield infinite
dihedral quotient, whereas two distinct transpositions generate `S_3`.
