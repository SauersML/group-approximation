**OPEN.**  Let `p` be prime, `G = Z/p`, `N` the norm functor of
`kk-tensor-power-norm-functor-to-kk-zp`, and `1_T = cone(P → C)` the Tate object
(cellular approximation by induced objects, `Res 1_T ≃ 0`).

1. **General.**  For every separable `A`, `θ = [σ] = 1` in
   `KK^G(1_T ⊗ N(A), 1_T ⊗ N(A))`.  Equivalently, the Tate part of every norm
   has zero phantom part.
2. **Adversarial non-nuclear case.**  For a torsion-free infinite hyperbolic
   `Γ` with property (T), the Tate diagonal
   `1_T ⊗ Δ : 1_T ⊗ τ(C*_r Γ) → 1_T ⊗ C*_r(Γ^p)` is a `KK^G`-equivalence.
   Here `Δ(λ_γ) = λ_(γ, …, γ)`.

**Relations.**
- Item 1 is implied by detection
  (`zp-restriction-and-crossed-product-detect-kk-g-contractibility`), for all
  separable algebras.
- A counterexample to item 1 for nuclear `A` refutes `MN(nuclear)`, and for
  separable `A` refutes `MN(all separable)`
  (`zp-detection-equals-meyer-nadareishvili-sufficiency`).
- Item 2 implies item 1 for `A = C*_r Γ`.
- Item 1 holds for `A ∈ B`: bootstrap norms lie in `B^G`, and phantoms in
  `B^G` vanish.

## Attempts

* **Bootstrap and calibration (proved, part 2 §7–8 and part 3 §11(d)).**
  - Item 1 holds for bootstrap `A`.
  - Item 2 holds for torsion-free a-T-menable `Γ`, where
    `C*_r Γ ∈ B`.
  These are the cases that tell us nothing.
* **Spectra analogy (dies at generation).**  In genuine `C_p`-spectra the
  Tate-local category is generated by `ẼC_p`, so geometric fixed points are an
  equivalence and there are no phantoms.  `KK^G` is not generated by `C` and
  `C(G)` as a localizing subcategory; that subcategory is `B^G`.  A non-cellular
  `A` gives a non-cellular `N(A)`, and the argument stops.
* **Tate diagonal (reduction proved, KK step open;
  `research/artifacts/uct-kk-norm-functor-2026-09-13-part3.md`).**
  - The source `1_T ⊗ τ(C*_r Γ)` has `θ = 1`.  So the phantom part of
    `1_T ⊗ N(C*_r Γ)` equals that of the cone `Z_Γ` of the diagonal.
  - The diagonal crossed by `G` is the subgroup inclusion
    `ΔΓ × G ≤ Γ ≀ G`.  `σ`-fixed cosets of `ΔΓ` in `Γ^p` correspond to
    elements of order dividing `p`.
  - With Baum--Connes with coefficients for `Γ ≀ G`
    (`hyperbolic-bcc-finite-wreath-products`), `Z_Γ ⋊ G` is invisible to
    K-theory with every trivial-action coefficient (cellular step sketched).
  - Dies: what remains is the `(1 − γ)`-part, the non-K-nuclear summand of
    Skandalis.  No available invariant sees KK-morphisms there.
* **No Tate diagonal for general `A` (dies).**  `a ↦ a^(⊗p)` is not linear, and
  `KK` has no Frobenius.  Unlike spectra, item 1 cannot be reduced to the unit
  object.
* **Self-tensoring (partial).**  At `p = 2` an equivariantly self-absorbing
  phantom vanishes (detection claim, Attempts).  `1_T ⊗ N(A)` is not
  self-absorbing in general.
  `N(A) ⊗ N(A) = N(A ⊗ A)` gives `θ_(N(A ⊗ A)) = θ_(N(A)) ⊗ θ_(N(A))`, which
  only moves the question to `A ⊗ A`.

**Sharpest target.**  Decide item 2.  The obstruction lives on the
`(1 − γ)`-parts of `1_T ⋊_r (ΔΓ × G)` and `1_T ⋊_r (Γ ≀ G)`.  A negative answer
would give the first separable `Z/p`-algebra whose restriction and crossed
product lie in `B` but which is not in `B^G`.  Using item 1 of
`zp-restriction-trivial-splits-cellular-plus-phantom`, that would need
`Z_Γ ⋊ G ≃_KK 0` together with a KK-invariant sensitive to `θ`.
