**OPEN.**  Let `p` be prime, `G = Z/p`, `N` the norm functor of
`kk-tensor-power-norm-functor-to-kk-zp`, and `1_T = cone(P → C)` the Tate object
(cellular approximation by induced objects, `Res 1_T ≃ 0`).  For every
separable `A`, is `θ = [σ] = 1` in `KK^G(1_T ⊗ N(A), 1_T ⊗ N(A))`?
Equivalently: does the Tate part of every norm have zero phantom part?

**Relations.**
- It is implied by detection for all separable algebras, by item 3 of
  `zp-restriction-trivial-splits-cellular-plus-phantom`.  The nuclear detection
  claim `zp-restriction-and-crossed-product-detect-kk-g-contractibility` gives
  it only for nuclear `A`, so no route from that claim is drawn.
- A counterexample with `A` nuclear refutes `MN(nuclear)`, and with `A`
  separable refutes `MN(all separable)`
  (`zp-detection-equals-meyer-nadareishvili-sufficiency`).
- It holds for `A ∈ B`: bootstrap norms lie in `B^G`, and phantoms in `B^G`
  vanish.
- For `A = C*_r Γ` it follows from
  `kazhdan-group-algebra-tate-diagonal-is-kkg-equivalence`.

## Attempts

* **Bootstrap and calibration (proved, part 2 §7–8 and part 3 §11(d)).**  It
  holds for bootstrap `A`, and for `C*_r Γ` with `Γ` torsion-free a-T-menable.
  These cases tell us nothing.
* **Spectra analogy (dies at generation).**  In genuine `C_p`-spectra the
  Tate-local category is generated by `ẼC_p`.  So geometric fixed points are
  an equivalence there, and there are no phantoms.  `KK^G` is not generated by
  `C` and `C(G)` as a localizing subcategory (that subcategory is `B^G`), and a
  non-cellular `A` gives a non-cellular `N(A)`.
* **Tate diagonal for group algebras (reduction proved, KK step open).**
  - The source `1_T ⊗ τ(C*_r Γ)` of the diagonal has `θ = 1`, so the phantom
    part of `1_T ⊗ N(C*_r Γ)` equals that of the cone of the diagonal
    (`research/artifacts/uct-kk-norm-functor-2026-09-13-part3.md`, §9–10).
  - With Baum--Connes with coefficients for `Γ ≀ G`, the cone's crossed product
    is K-invisible (§11(c), sketch).
  - Dies on the `(1 − γ)`-part, the non-K-nuclear summand of Skandalis.  See
    `kazhdan-group-algebra-tate-diagonal-is-kkg-equivalence`.
* **No Tate diagonal for general `A` (dies).**  `a ↦ a^(⊗p)` is not linear, and
  `KK` has no Frobenius.  Unlike spectra, the question cannot be reduced to the
  unit object.
* **Self-tensoring (partial).**  At `p = 2` an equivariantly self-absorbing
  phantom vanishes (detection claim, Attempts).  `1_T ⊗ N(A)` is not
  self-absorbing in general.  `N(A ⊗ A) = N(A) ⊗ N(A)` with
  `θ_(N(A ⊗ A)) = θ_(N(A)) ⊗ θ_(N(A))` only moves the question to `A ⊗ A`.
