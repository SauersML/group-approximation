**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `(M,tau)` be a diffuse finite von Neumann algebra with
faithful normal trace and separable `L^2(M,tau)`.  If `M` is not Connes
embeddable, then there are self-adjoint unitaries

```text
s_1,...,s_k in M,                                        (FSW1)
```

chosen from one complete orthonormal symmetry basis, a finite set `W` of
words in `k` involutions, and `epsilon>0` such that for every dimension `d`
and every tuple of matrix symmetries `S_1,...,S_k`,

```text
max_(w in W) |tr_d(w(S))-tau(w(s))| >= epsilon.          (FSW2)
```

To prove this, take the orthonormal symmetry basis supplied by
He--Tang--Zhang.  Its linear span is `L^2`-dense, so the von Neumann algebra
generated by the basis is all of `M`.  If `(FSW2)` failed for every finite
subtuple, word window and tolerance, diagonalize matrix symmetry tuples over
an exhaustion of the basis words.  In the tracial matrix ultraproduct their
images have exactly the same joint word moments as the basis symmetries.
The resulting trace-preserving homomorphism from their generated star algebra
extends to a trace-preserving embedding of `M`, contradicting non-CE.

Using exact matrix symmetries causes no loss.  Conversely, if one starts with
approximately self-adjoint unitaries satisfying `S_i^2 approximately 1`,
functional-calculus sign rounding changes them by `o(1)` in normalized HS
norm, and fixed-word telescoping preserves the diagonal argument.

The witness is exactly stable under common amplification:

```text
tr_(dm)(w(S tensor I_m))=tr_d(w(S)).                    (FSW3)
```

It is therefore a genuine finite, dimension-independent semantic
obstruction.  But it is only a **trace-profile** obstruction.  Ordinary
group relators impose multiplication equalities, while the target values
`tau(w(s))` in `(FSW2)` may be arbitrary intermediate scalars.  Turning
`(FSW2)` into nonhyperlinearity of a group still requires either

1. a trace-preserving embedding of `M` into a positive corner of a group
   factor; or
2. a finite matrix-only decoder promoting canonical delta-character
   microstates of a group to the moment profile in `(FSW2)`.

If the entire symmetry basis were projectively multiplicatively closed, the
index group would have exponent two and the resulting twisted group algebra
would be hyperfinite.  Hence the non-CE witness necessarily uses spread
structure coefficients; the 2026 basis theorem does not itself provide the
missing trace promotion.
