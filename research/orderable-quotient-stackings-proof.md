## Pins

- **l.329–345, Definition `def: relative stacking`:** "Consider an action
  σ:G=A⋆B→Homeo^+(R), not necessarily faithful. For a cyclically reduced word
  w=a_1b_1⋯a_nb_n with a_i∈A∖{id}, b_i∈B∖{id}, the trajectory of some x∈R under
  w is the multiset of points Ω(w,x)={x·σ(a_1b_1…a_ib_i)}∪{x·σ(a_1b_1…a_i)} …
  We say the trajectory of x under w is stable if: (1) Each element in Ω(w,x)
  occurs exactly once. (2) x·σ(w)=x."
- **l.685–690, Theorem `thm: spectral gap from stacking`:** "For any free product
  G=A⋆B and a cyclically reduced word w=a_1b_1…a_nb_n∈A⋆B with n∈Z_+,
  a_i∈A∖{id} and b_i∈B∖{id}. If there is a relative stacking of w in G′=A′⋆B′,
  where A′ (resp. B′) is the subgroup generated by a_1,…,a_n (resp. b_1,…,b_n),
  then for any boundary-incompressible w-admissible surface S in G, we have
  −χ^−(S)≥deg(S)." No hypothesis is placed on `A` or `B`.
- **l.931–938, Theorem `thm:StackingsRF`:** for countable right-orderable `A`,
  `B` and cyclically reduced `w` that is not a proper power, there exist
  `σ: G -> Homeo^+(R)` and `x` with `Ω(w, x)` stable.
- **l.448–463, Example `example: equations in groups`, and l.1456–1466 (proof of
  Theorem B):** a nontrivial `a ∈ A ∩ <<w>>`, written with the minimal number
  `k` of conjugates of powers of `w`, gives a boundary-incompressible
  `w`-admissible sphere with `k+1` boundary components, `−χ = k−1`, and
  `deg >= k`.

## Audit of the proof of Theorem `thm: spectral gap from stacking` (l.693–897)

The statement is quantified over all `A`, `B`. The question was whether its
proof secretly uses faithfulness of `σ`, or orderability of `A` or `B`. It does
not. Here is where the action enters.

- **Lemma `lemma: lambda` (l.705–710).** `λ: J_w -> R` sends the juncture of a
  prefix `u` to `x·σ(u)`. Injectivity comes from stability, and
  `λ(j)·σ(a_i) = λ(j')` holds along each arc by definition.
- **The orientation lemma (l.724–760).** This is purely topological, given
  injectivity of `λ`. Equal values force the same juncture of `S^1_w`, and then
  a pair of pants witnesses boundary-compressibility.
- **Lemma `lemma: segment count` (l.814–828).** This uses only injectivity of
  `λ` (a unique maximal juncture and a unique minimal juncture).
- **Lemma `lemma: sign changes` (l.832–869).** For a disk-piece the labels
  `φ_1,...,φ_k ∈ A` satisfy `φ_1⋯φ_k = id` in `A`. Hence
  `σ(φ_1)⋯σ(φ_k) = id`, and monotonicity of orientation-preserving
  homeomorphisms gives the contradiction. This uses only that `σ` is a
  homomorphism into `Homeo^+(R)`. A relation in `A` maps to a relation in the
  image, and the converse is never needed.
- **Normal form (l.470–680).** The only algebraic input is "any such w has
  infinite order" (l.487), which holds for every cyclically reduced element of
  length at least 2 in any free product.
- **Final count (l.884–895).** This is the Euler formula together with the two
  counts above.

The `A`-boundary components (the element `a` of Step 4 below) lie in annulus
pieces, where only `sc(P) >= 0 = 2χ(P)` is used. So the action never needs to
see `a`. This is why kernel elements are reached.

## Proof

**Step 1 (countable images).** `p(A')` and `q(B')` are finitely generated
subgroups of right-orderable groups, hence countable and right-orderable. `w̄` is
a cyclically reduced word in `p(A') * q(B')` of length `2n`, because every
letter survives by (L). It is not a proper power there, since a root in the
subgroup would be a root in `Q_A * Q_B`, which (P) excludes.

**Step 2 (stacking downstairs).** Theorem `thm:StackingsRF` gives
`τ: p(A') * q(B') -> Homeo^+(R)` and `x ∈ R` with `Ω(w̄, x)` stable.

**Step 3 (pull-back).** Put `σ = τ ∘ (p * q): A' * B' -> Homeo^+(R)`. For every
prefix `u` of `w`, `(p * q)(u)` is the corresponding prefix of `w̄`, so
`x·σ(u) = x·τ(ū)`. Hence `Ω(w, x)` under `σ` equals `Ω(w̄, x)` under `τ` as a
multiset, and it is stable. So `(σ, x)` is a relative stacking of `w` in
`G' = A' * B'`, not necessarily faithful, which the definition allows.

**Step 4 (spectral gap and injectivity).**
- Theorem `thm: spectral gap from stacking` applies verbatim: every
  boundary-incompressible `w`-admissible surface in `G = A * B` satisfies
  `−χ^−(S) >= deg(S)`. This is item 1.
- **Injectivity of `A`.** If `w` is conjugate into `B` there is nothing to do,
  but that cannot happen here since `n >= 1`. Suppose `a ∈ A∖{1}` lies in
  `<<w>>`. Take `a = ∏_(i=1)^k g_i w^(n_i) g_i^(−1)` with `n_i ≠ 0` and `k >= 1`
  minimal. By the Example this is a boundary-incompressible `w`-admissible
  sphere with `k+1` boundary components, so `−χ^− = k−1` and
  `deg = Σ|n_i| >= k`. Item 1 gives `k−1 >= k`, a contradiction. Nothing here
  uses `p(a)`; `a` may lie in `ker p` or outside `A'`.
- **Injectivity of `B`.** The rotation `b_1 a_2 ⋯ b_n a_1` has the same
  syllable subgroups, the same images and the same (L) and (P), since rotation
  is conjugation and preserves proper-power-ness. Steps 1–4 apply with the roles
  exchanged.
- **Item 3.** This follows from `<<w^k>> ⊆ <<w>>`.

**Step 5 (sharpness: a relative stacking forces (L) and (P)).** Let `(σ, x)` be a
relative stacking of `w` in `A' * B'`, and put `Q_A = σ(A')`, `Q_B = σ(B')`,
`p = σ|A'`, `q = σ|B'`. These are countable subgroups of `Homeo^+(R)`, hence
right-orderable (l.901).

- **(L).** If `p(a_i) = 1` with `i >= 2`, then the prefixes `a_1 ⋯ b_(i−1)` and
  `a_1 ⋯ b_(i−1) a_i` send `x` to the same point. If `p(a_1) = 1`, then
  `x·σ(a_1) = x = x·σ(w)`. Either way stability is violated, and likewise for a
  letter `b_i`.
- **(P).** The action of `A' * B'` factors through `Q_A * Q_B` (a homomorphism
  on a free product is determined on the factors), so the trajectory of `w` is
  the trajectory of `w̄`.
  - By (L), `w̄` is cyclically reduced of length `2n`. If `w̄ = ū^k` with
    `k >= 2`, then `ū` is cyclically reduced: write `ū = c v c^(−1)` reduced, so
    `ū^k = c v^k c^(−1)`, and cyclic reducedness forces `c = 1`. So the word `w̄`
    is `ū` repeated `k` times, and each `ū^j`, `1 <= j <= k`, is a prefix.
  - Put `y = x·σ(ū)`. If `y > x`, monotonicity gives
    `x < x·σ(ū) < x·σ(ū^2) < ... < x·σ(ū^k)`, contradicting `x·σ(ū^k) = x`. The
    case `y < x` is symmetric.
  - If `y = x`, then the distinct prefixes `ū` and `ū^k` give the same point,
    contradicting stability. So `w̄` is not a proper power.

This proves the equivalence stated under "Sharpness of the method".

**Step 6 (the Kervaire–Laudenbach form).**
- Take `B = <t>` and `q = id`, so `b_i = t^(k_i) ≠ 1` survives automatically.
- `R(A')` is normal in `A'` and `A'/R(A')` is right-orderable (a subgroup of a
  lexicographically ordered product), so `p: A' -> A'/R(A')` is admissible.
- The pushout identity `(G * <t>)/<<w>> = G *_(A') (A' * <t>)/<<w>>` holds by
  comparing presentations. An amalgamated free product contains its factors
  once the amalgamated subgroup embeds in both, which is Step 4 for `A'`. So `G`
  injects.
- Exponent sum plays no role anywhere.

**Standard facts used** (not re-read):
- the conjugacy and root structure of free products (Lyndon–Schupp Ch. IV);
- the embedding of factors in amalgamated free products;
- that a product of right-orderable groups, lexicographically ordered over a
  well-ordered index set, is right-orderable, and so are its subgroups.
