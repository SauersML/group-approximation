**Definition** (as on the swarm branch, `finitely-presented-cuntz-swindle-algebras-exist`). A *Cuntz
swindle structure* on a unital `K`-algebra `A` has two parts:
- elements `s_1, s_2, s_1*, s_2*` with `s_i* s_j = δ_ij` and `s_1 s_1* + s_2 s_2* = 1`;
- a unital endomorphism `σ` with `σ(a) = s_1 a s_1* + s_2 σ(a) s_2*` for all `a`.

## Construction

- **Space.** `αN = {0, 1, 2, ..., ∞}` is the one-point compactification of `N`. Let
  `X = (αN)^Z`, a Cantor space.
- **Insertion.** `h : X × αN -> X` inserts at position 0:
  - `h(x, n)_i = x_i` for `i < 0`;
  - `h(x, n)_0 = n`;
  - `h(x, n)_i = x_(i-1)` for `i > 0`.

  It is a homeomorphism.
- **Four partial homeomorphisms.**
  - `s_1(x) = h(x, 0)`, from `X` onto `{x_0 = 0}`;
  - `s_2` adds 1 at coordinate 0 (`∞ + 1 = ∞`), from `X` onto `{x_0 ≥ 1}`;
  - `S` is the shift, `S(x)_i = x_(i+1)`;
  - `τ` swaps coordinates 0 and 1.
- **Groupoid and algebra.** `Σ` is the inverse semigroup of all finite composites of
  `s_1^(±1), s_2^(±1), S^(±1), τ`. `𝒢` is its groupoid of germs on `X`, and `A = A_K(𝒢)` is the
  Steinberg algebra over a field `K`. Write `s_i` also for `1_([s_i])`, and similarly for the
  others.
- **Swindle endomorphism.** `σ(φ) = h ∘ (φ × id) ∘ h^(-1)`. On generators:
  - `σ(s_1) = τ s_1`;
  - `σ(s_2) = τ s_2 τ`;
  - `σ(S) = S τ`;
  - `σ(τ) = S^(-1) τ S`.

  So `σ(Σ) ⊆ Σ`, and `σ` induces a unital endomorphism of `A`.

## Statement

For every field `K`:

1. **Finite generation.** `A` is generated as a `K`-algebra by the 7 elements
   `s_1, s_1*, s_2, s_2*, S, S^(-1), τ`. For `K = F_2` it is generated by them as a ring.
2. **Swindle.** `(s_1, s_2, σ)` is a Cuntz swindle structure on `A`, and `σ` is given on these
   generators by the words above.
3. **Simple with centre K.** `𝒢` is second countable, ample, minimal and effective, with a
   Hausdorff (Cantor) unit space. It is not Hausdorff. Every nonzero `f ∈ A` has support with
   nonempty interior, so the singular ideal is zero in every characteristic. Hence `A` is simple by
   `cepss-nonhausdorff-steinberg-simplicity` (S), and `Z(A) = K`.

So `A` is a finitely generated central simple `K`-algebra with a Cuntz swindle whose endomorphism
preserves a finite generating set up to explicit words. Via the swindle, as in the swarm-branch
node `cuntz-swindle-factors-kill-k-theory-of-every-tensor`, all `K_q(D ⊗ A)` vanish.

## What this changes

- **Before.** On the swarm branch the target `fp-central-simple-cuntz-swindle-algebra-exists` said
  that any refutation must use finite generation.
- **Now.** Any refutation must use finite *presentation*. The target now follows from one explicit
  question: **is `A` finitely presented?** Equivalently, is the kernel of `F<7> -> A`, which is
  σ̃-stable for the lift σ̃ of `σ` to the free algebra, finitely generated as an ideal?
- **Other positive answers.** A finitely presented simple σ-closed cover would also do.
- **Why this is not obviously false.** The Houghton-type group `FSym(Z) ⋊ Z = ⟨S, τ⟩` sits in the
  units and is not finitely presented. That is only a warning, since subgroups of finitely
  presented algebras' unit groups need not be finitely presented.
- **Over K = Q** this also gives the simplicity, centre and K-theory parts of
  `fp-central-simple-q-ring-with-trivial-leavitt-k-theory` for a finitely generated `Q`-algebra.
  It is not a finitely generated ring. That node needs the analogue with `A_Q` finitely presented
  as a `Q`-algebra, plus a finitely presented ring of coefficients such as a finitely presented
  ring containing `Q`.

## Attempts

- **Attempt 1 (swarm-0917 w21, finite presentation, OPEN).** The candidate relator families are:
  - the Cuntz relations for `s_1, s_2`;
  - `S S^(-1) = S^(-1) S = 1` and `τ² = 1`;
  - the Houghton-type relations `(τ · S^(-1) τ S)^3 = 1` and `[τ, S^(-k) τ S^k] = 1` for `k = 2`;
  - the swindle relators `σ̃(x) s_1 = s_1 x` and `σ̃(x) s_2 = s_2 σ̃(x)` for the 7 letters `x`;
  - their σ̃-images up to a fixed depth.

  Two things are not checked: that these relators imply `[τ, S^(-k) τ S^k] = 1` for all `k ≥ 2`,
  and that they force the germ relations of `𝒢`. For the first, the swindle relators move `τ` to
  `σ̃(τ) = S^(-1) τ S`, and this is the mechanism by which Brin–Thompson closures recover infinite
  families. The w20 reduction on the swarm branch, "A_sw / M is finitely presented when M is
  finitely σ-generated", does not apply directly: universality needs a unit `u` with
  `σ² = Ad(u) σ`, and here `σ²` inserts two coordinates while `σ` inserts one.
  `αN × αN` is not homeomorphic to `αN`, since their Cantor–Bendixson ranks are 2 and 1, so the
  obvious `u` does not exist.
