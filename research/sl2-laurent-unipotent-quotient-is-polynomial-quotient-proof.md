Notation: `R = Z[t,t^{-1}]`, `G = SL_2(R)`, `T` the Bruhat–Tits tree of
`SL_2(Q((t)))`, `v_0 = [Q[[t]]^2]`, `v_1 = [D Q[[t]]^2]`. From the proof of
`sl2-laurent-integers-splits-over-gamma0-t`:
`Stab_G(v_0) = SL_2(Z[t])`, `Stab_G(v_1) = D SL_2(Z[t]) D^{-1}`, and a subgroup of
`Stab_G(v_0)` whose reduction `t ↦ 0` contains `SL_2(Z)` is transitive on the
neighbours of `v_0` (likewise at `v_1` after conjugating by `D`).

Put `V = < U_2(Z[t]), D U_2(Z[t]) D^{-1} >`.

**V ⊂ U_2(R).** Conjugating a unipotent by `D ∈ GL_2(R)` gives a unipotent over `R`.

**V is edge-transitive on T.** `V ⊇ E_2(Z[t])`, whose reduction is
`E_2(Z) = SL_2(Z)`, so `V ∩ Stab(v_0)` is transitive on the neighbours of `v_0`;
`V ⊇ D E_2(Z[t]) D^{-1}` gives the same at `v_1`. The induction of Step 3 in
`sl2-laurent-integers-splits-over-gamma0-t-proof` shows that `V` has one orbit on
edges, and so one orbit on vertices of each type.

**U_2(R) ⊂ V.** Let `u = I + N ∈ G` with `N ≠ 0` nilpotent. Over the field `Q((t))`
there is `h ∈ SL_2(Q((t)))` with `h^{-1}Nh = [[0,r],[0,0]]`. The matrix `E_12(r)`
fixes every lattice `Q[[t]]e_1 ⊕ t^k Q[[t]]e_2` with `k ≥ -v_t(r)`. These have both
types, so `u` fixes some vertex of the type of `v_0`. By edge-transitivity that vertex
is `g v_0` with `g ∈ V`. Then `g^{-1} u g ∈ Stab_G(v_0) = SL_2(Z[t])` is a unipotent
with polynomial entries, so it lies in `U_2(Z[t])`, and `u ∈ g U_2(Z[t]) g^{-1} ⊂ V`.
Since `U_2(R)` is generated by such `u`, `U_2(R) = V`. This is item 1.

**Item 2.** Conjugates of unipotents are unipotent, so `U_2(R)` is normal.
Edge-transitivity was shown for `V = U_2(R)`. For `g ∈ G`, pick `x ∈ U_2(R)` with
`x v_0 = g v_0`; then `x^{-1}g ∈ SL_2(Z[t])`. So `G = U_2(R)·SL_2(Z[t])`.

**Item 3.** From item 2,
`G/U_2(R) = SL_2(Z[t])U_2(R)/U_2(R) ≅ SL_2(Z[t])/(SL_2(Z[t]) ∩ U_2(R))`.
