If `W_3` is hyperlinear and not sofic, it is an explicit finitely
generated hyperlinear nonsofic group, which is the separation root
(Pestov's Question 3.4 answered by separation).  This route makes the
p = 3 summit fork win-win across both program roots: the
non-hyperlinear branch closes the main goal
(`nonhyperlinear-coset-wreath-from-p3-collapse`,
`nonhyperlinear-from-lamp-torsor-excess`), while the hyperlinear branch
plus a compression-free nonsoficity argument closes the separation.
The nonsoficity leg cannot come from Kun-Thom
(`arithmetic-integral-subgroup-is-incompressible`), which is exactly
why it is recorded as a separate open claim rather than imported.
