Citation import, not a reproof.

M. Migliorini, "Thompson's group T has quadratic Dehn function",
arXiv:2410.23088 (v1 30 Oct 2024, v2 19 Nov 2024), Forum Math. Sigma 13
(2025), e109. Read from the arXiv abstract page and the arXiv HTML on
2026-09-13.

- Main theorem, verbatim: "Theorem A. The Dehn function of Thompson's group T
  is quadratic."
- Abstract, verbatim: "We prove that Thompson's group T and, more generally,
  all the Higman-Thompson groups T_n have quadratic Dehn function."

Lorrain, arXiv:2608.03813v2, cites the result in its abstract as
"[Migliorini, 2025, Theorem A]". Zaremsky's list (version of July 12, 2026)
records it in the update to Problem 2.5.

This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what the claim records.
