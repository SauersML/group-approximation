Narutaka Ozawa, *Noncommutative real algebraic geometry of Kazhdan's property
(T)*, arXiv:1312.5431, Main Theorem, proves exactly this statement.  For a
finitely generated group and a finitely supported symmetric generating
probability measure `mu`, Ozawa's Main Theorem states that property `(T)` is
equivalent to existence of `kappa>0` and finitely many group-algebra elements
`xi_i` satisfying

```text
Delta_mu^2-kappa Delta_mu=sum_i xi_i*xi_i.
```

The theorem is an identity inside `R[Gamma]`, not merely positivity in the
full group C-star algebra, so all supports involved are finite.
