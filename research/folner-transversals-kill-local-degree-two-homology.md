**ESTABLISHED** (proposed; referee pending) through
`folner-transversals-kill-local-degree-two-homology-proof`, from item 4 of
`rf-weak-bass-defect-is-degree-two-approximation-defect`. Recalled imports: Fox calculus for
the cellular chains of a presentation complex; Hopf's theorem that `H_2` of a group is a quotient
of `H_2` of any 2-complex with that fundamental group; the five-term exact sequence; Weiss's
theorem that a residually finite amenable group has, for every nested residual chain, fundamental
domains of the `N_i` that form a Følner sequence (B. Weiss, *Monotileable amenable groups*, 2001).
Item 1 uses no imports beyond Fox calculus and needs no hypothesis on `K`.

**Notation.** `K` is generated by a finite set `S`, `n = |S|`, and `B_L` is the ball of radius
`L` in the word metric (`1 ∈ B_L`). `R ⊴ F(S)` is the set of relators, and `R_{≤L}` is the finite
set of relators of length `<= L`. Put `Γ_L = ⟨S | R_{≤L}⟩`, so `Γ_L → K` is onto. `k` is a field.
For a finite-index subgroup `H <= K` let `H_L` be its preimage in `Γ_L`, and set

> `I_L(H) = im(H_2(H_L; k) → H_2(H; k))` (the classes *carried by relators of length `<= L`*).

`I_L(H)` increases with `L`, and its union is `H_2(H; k)`. A *transversal* of `H` is a set
`T ⊂ K` with `K = ⊔_{t ∈ T} H t`.

1. **Følner bound on local classes (any group).** For every finite-index `H <= K`, every
   transversal `T` of `H`, every `L` and every field `k`,

   > `dim_k I_L(H) <= n · |T B_L \ T|`.

   More generally, if `T ⊂ K` only injects into `H\K`, then
   `dim_k I_L(H) <= n |T B_L \ T| + |R_{≤L}| ([K:H] − |T|)`.
2. **The non-local part.** With `M_L = ker(Γ_L → K)`, and `N` normal of finite index,

   > `b_2(N; k) − dim_k I_L(N) = dim_k H_0(N_L; H_1(M_L; k)) − b_1(N_L; k) + b_1(N; k)`.

   So `H_2` classes that are not carried by relators of length `<= L` are exactly the coinvariants
   of the long relations that die in `H_1` of the cover.
3. **Residually finite amenable groups: bounded relator sets carry `o(index)`.** Let `K` be
   finitely generated, residually finite and amenable, with a residual chain `(N_i)`. For every
   fixed `L` and every field `k`, `dim_k I_L(N_i) = o([K:N_i])`. There is no hypothesis on `cd K`
   or on finiteness of Betti numbers.
4. **Depth versus Følner radius (reduction, both directions).** For `δ, ε >= 0` and finite-index
   `H` put

   > `ℓ^δ(H) = min{L : dim_Q I_L(H) >= b_2(H; Q) − δ [K:H]}` (the *relator depth* of `H_2`),
   > `ρ_ε(H) = sup{L : some transversal T of H has |T B_L \ T| <= ε |T|}` (the *Følner radius*).

   Let `K` be infinite, finitely generated, residually finite and amenable, with `cd_Z K <= 2` and
   `b_2(K) < ∞`, and let `(N_i)` be a residual chain. The following are equivalent:
   - (a) `b_2(N_i)/[K:N_i] → 0`, i.e. `χ_Q(K) = 0`, i.e. the degree-2 approximation of
     `rf-weak-bass-defect-is-degree-two-approximation-defect` holds for `K`;
   - (b) for every `δ > 0`, `ℓ^δ(N_i) <= ρ_δ(N_i)` for all large `i`;
   - (c) there are `δ, ε >= 0` with `δ + n ε < 1` such that `ℓ^δ(N_i) <= ρ_ε(N_i)` for infinitely
     many `i`.

   The constant `1` in (c) comes from integrality of `χ_Q(K)`.
5. **Shape of a counterexample.** If `χ_Q(K) >= 1` in item 4, then for all `δ, ε` with
   `δ + n ε < 1`, `ℓ^δ(N_i) > ρ_ε(N_i)` for all large `i`, and `ρ_ε(N_i) → ∞` (Weiss). So, to
   carry all but a `δ`-fraction of `H_2(N_i; Q)`, one needs relators longer than the Følner radius
   of every fundamental domain of `N_i`. For every fixed `L`,
   `b_2(N_i) − dim I_L(N_i) = χ_Q(K) [K:N_i] + o([K:N_i])`: essentially all of `H_2` is non-local.
6. **Calibration: where the Kazhdan lattice fails.** Let `Γ` be the torsion-free lattice in
   `SL_3(Q_2)` of `pro-p-route-cannot-see-amenability-in-cd-two` (cd 2, type F, `V` vertex orbits,
   `χ = V`, a presentation with `6V + 1` generators and relators of length 3). Then `I_3(N) =
   H_2(N; Q)` has dimension `V[Γ:N] − 1`, and item 1 reads

   > `|T B_3 \ T| >= (V[Γ:N] − 1)/(6V + 1)` for every transversal `T` of every finite-index `N`.

   This is true, because `Γ` is non-amenable. So the Følner scheme proves nothing false for `Γ`,
   and it fails for `Γ` at exactly one step: the choice of a transversal with small boundary.
   That step is the only place amenability is used, and it is used nowhere else. For an amenable
   counterexample, the scheme fails at a different step: bounded relator depth (item 5).

**Obstruction (the Følner-tiling step of the programme).** Pushing Følner sets into `K/N_i` and
controlling `H_2` of `P ⊗ Z[K/N_i]` on Følner-tiled pieces, with the relation module `P` cut off at
any bounded relator length, proves only item 3. That holds for all residually finite amenable
groups. In a counterexample the part of `H_2` it misses is `χ_Q(K)[K:N_i] + o([K:N_i])` (item 5).
So no bounded-depth Følner argument can prove the target, whatever tiling is used. The
interchange of limits in item 2(b) of `rf-weak-bass-defect-is-degree-two-approximation-defect`
becomes a race between two radii of the same finite cover: the depth `ℓ^δ(N_i)` and the Følner
radius `ρ_ε(N_i)` (item 4). The remaining open statement is the upper bound `ℓ^δ(N_i) <=
ρ_ε(N_i)` along some residual chain, for some `δ + nε < 1`.

**Calibrations.** For finite `K` and `H = 1`, `T = K` gives the bound `0`. For `Z² = ⟨a, b |
[a,b]⟩` with `H = (mZ)²` and `T = [0, m)²`, item 1 gives `1 = b_2(H) <= 2|TB_4 \ T| = O(m)`. For
a finitely presented amenable residually finite group, item 1 with `L` the maximal relator length
and Weiss's transversals gives `b_2(N_i) = o([K:N_i])`. This is the known degree-2 case of Lück
approximation, here with an explicit bound.
