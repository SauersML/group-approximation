**Dead.**  Plan: show `E` is locally indicable and apply the theorem of
Jaikin-Zapirain--Lopez-Alvarez that locally indicable groups satisfy Strong
Atiyah over every subfield of `C` (Fisher--Ng, arXiv:2606.19606, Section 1.1;
Lueck, arXiv:2102.04549v2, Theorem 3.3).  That proof uses Hughes-free division
rings, not approximation, so it is the natural approximation-free candidate.

Killed by `universal-torsion-free-hosts-not-sofic-or-locally-indicable`: `E`
contains an infinite finitely generated Kazhdan group, which has finite
abelianization and so maps onto no `Z`.
