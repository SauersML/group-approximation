The restriction of the left regular representation of `H` to `C` is an
infinite multiple of the regular representation of `C`.  Equivalently, the
canonical group trace takes the identity coefficient of the character
idempotent and gives `(FTE1)`.  Hyperlinear microstates converge on the fixed
finite character sum defining `e_sigma`, so their squared normalized-HS norm
converges to the same positive number.  This contradicts any uniform
finite-matrix exclusion theorem `(FTE2)`.
