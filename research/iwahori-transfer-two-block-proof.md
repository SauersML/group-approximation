The dilation `(TBP3)` preserves every commutator norm by the two off-diagonal
block calculation `(TBP4)` and preserves edge defects under normalized direct
sum.  The affine conversion `P=(I+J)/2` gives the projection formulation;
`J=2P-I` gives the converse.
