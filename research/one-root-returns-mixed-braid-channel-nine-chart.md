**INVALIDATED ONE-ROOT RETURN.**  After cancelling the exposed
`x_89(r)` factor in the mixed-braid comparison, append one arbitrary
elementary root `x_ab(c)` to the residual asymmetric involution

```text
P=x_79(q_1)x_97(q_1)x_79(1).
```

If `x_ab(c)P` returned the channel-nine chart to the signed source, it
would be the shortest genuinely new nonconstant continuation of the
mixed-braid cell.

`one-elementary-root-cannot-return-mixed-braid-chart` proves that one of
the two complementary coefficients `q_0,q_1` survives in its original
matrix position after conjugating the positive signed root `x_79(1)`.
The result is never a constant matrix, so the proposed word cannot
normalize the signed Hecke source.
