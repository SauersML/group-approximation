On full Fock space, let L_i and R_i be left and right creation
operators and set V=sign(R_1+R_1*). Represent z by V and s_i
by V L_i. The left-right vacuum commutator errors cancel, so
V commutes with all represented h_i and hence with the image
of every W in U(H). The sign operator sends the vacuum into
the range of L_1 with norm one, giving a maximal commutator
with L_1L_1*. The Toeplitz defect remains a nonzero rank-one
projection. This proves the universal lower bound; the usual
unitary-projection bound gives equality.

For the explicit exponential family, scalar evaluations z=1
and z=-1 in the faithful coefficient Fock representation give
range projections with rank-one complements in the Wold shift factor.
The sine transform shows their missing lines differ for every
nonzero t, so the two range projections have join one. Any
common coefficient upper projection must therefore be one.
The same shift calculation proves nonzero overlap with the
defect and both range projections.
