**ESTABLISHED** through `split-finitary-sl-q-extensions-are-not-fp-proof`. This is a lane
proof; it has not been independently reviewed. The method is the standard K_2 argument, and
no priority is claimed.

## Setting

- `X` is a countably infinite set and `V = Q^(X)` has basis `(e_x)`.
- `SL_X(Q)` is the group of `X x X` matrices that differ from the identity in finitely many
  entries and have determinant 1. It is `SL_inf(Q) = E(Q)`.
- `Mon(X)` is the group of monomial matrices: `m e_x = mu_m(x) e_(sigma_m x)` with
  `sigma_m` a permutation of `X` and `mu_m : X -> Q^x`. It normalizes `SL_X(Q)`:
  `m (1 + c e_pq) m^-1 = 1 + (mu_m(p)/mu_m(q)) c e_(sigma p, sigma q)`.

## Statement

Let `M <= Mon(X)` with `M ∩ SL_X(Q) = 1`, and let `Γ = SL_X(Q) x| M`, the subgroup of `GL(V)`
generated by `SL_X(Q)` and `M`. If `Γ` is finitely generated, then `Γ` is **not** finitely
presented.

Equivalently: a finitely generated `Γ <= GL(V)` with `SL_X(Q) <= Γ <= SL_X(Q)·Mon(X)` that has
a monomial complement to `SL_X(Q)` is not finitely presented. No hypothesis is made on `M`
itself, which may be finitely presented.

**General form.** The proof works verbatim for any ring `Λ` whose `K_2(Λ)` is not a finitely
generated abelian group, with `E_X(Λ)` in place of `SL_X(Q)` and monomial matrices with unit
weights. For `Λ = Q`, Tate's computation gives `K_2(Q) ≅ Z/2 ⊕ ⊕_(p odd) (Z/p)^x`, which is not
finitely generated.

## Examples it kills

Both groups are finitely generated, contain `SL_X(Q)` and hence every `GL_n(Q)`
(`GL_n(Q) -> SL_(n+1)(Q)`, `g ↦ diag(g, det g^-1)`), and have a monomial complement.
1. **Euler host.** `V = Q[t, t^-1]`, `X = Z`, generated by multiplication by `t`, the shifted
   Euler operators `θ + 1/2` and `θ + 1/3` (`θ = t d/dt`), and `1 + e_01`, `1 + e_10`. Here
   `t (θ + c) t^-1 = θ + c - 1`, and the ratios `(k + c)/(k + c + 1)` feed every denominator
   into the entries at `(0,1)`. Its complement `⟨t, θ + 1/2, θ + 1/3⟩ ≅ Z^2 wr Z` is not
   finitely presented, so the retract argument already applies.
2. **Baumslag host.** Let `B = A x| Z^2` be Baumslag's finitely presented metabelian group,
   `A = Z[x^±1, (1+x)^-1]`, with `s, t` acting by multiplication by `x` and `1 + x`. Fix a group
   isomorphism `χ : A -> Q^x_(>0)` (both are free abelian of countable rank). `B` acts on
   `Q^(Z^2)` monomially: `s, t` translate the lattice, and `a ∈ A` acts by the diagonal
   `e_p ↦ χ(x^(-p_1)(1+x)^(-p_2) a) e_p`. Conjugating `1 + e_(0,(0,1))` by `a` gives coefficient
   `χ(x(1+x)^-1 a)`, which runs over all of `Q^x_(>0)`. With `1 + e_(0,(0,1))`, `1 + e_((0,1),0)`,
   `1 + e_(0,(1,0))` and `1 + e_((1,0),0)` added, the group contains `SL_(Z^2)(Q)`. Its complement
   `B` is finitely presented, so no retract argument applies. The theorem shows that this
   natural-looking candidate for Problem 2.7 is not finitely presented either.

## Consequence for the root and Problem 2.7

A finitely presented group that contains the finitary group `SL_X(Q)` as a normal subgroup,
acting by monomial automorphisms, must be a **non-split** extension over it. Each lift of the
complement then has to carry `K_2(Q)`-valued twisting. Linear finitary hosts with a
geometric complement (lamplighters, Baumslag groups, Houghton-type or Thompson-type
permutation groups with weights) are all excluded as finitely presented overgroups of
`GL_n(Q)`.

## Not claimed

- Non-split `Γ`, where `M ∩ SL_X(Q) ≠ 1` cannot be avoided. The lift of `M ∩ SL_X(Q)` to the
  Steinberg group is a nontrivial central extension, and the proof does not treat it.
- Complements acting on `SL_X(Q)` by non-monomial automorphisms.
- `E_(n+1)(R_T)` of `gl-n-q-embeds-in-e-n-plus-1-of-a-fp-ring`. A K_2 obstruction there needs
  `St_(n+1)(R_T)` to be finitely presented and `K_2(n+1, R_T)` to be central with a
  non-finitely-generated image of `K_2(Q)`. Neither is proved.
