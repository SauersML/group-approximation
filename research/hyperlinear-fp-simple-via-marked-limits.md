Take a candidate finitely presented infinite simple group and exhibit it as a
limit, in the space of marked groups, of hyperlinear groups.  Hyperlinearity
is closed in that space, so the limit would be hyperlinear.

Dead, invalidated by `fp-simple-groups-escape-soft-hyperlinear-permanence`:
the candidate is isolated, so the approximating groups are eventually the
candidate itself.
