title: Osin - an acylindrically hyperbolic group is not a product of two infinite groups and is not a torsion group
---

**ESTABLISHED.** Imported from arXiv:1304.1246, by D. Osin, *Acylindrically
hyperbolic groups*: Corollary `ar` and the first sentence of its proof. Let
`G` be acylindrically hyperbolic. Then:

1. if `G = G_1 x G_2`, then `G_1` or `G_2` is finite;
2. `G` contains a non-abelian free subgroup. So `G` is nonamenable and has an
   element of infinite order. In particular no torsion group is acylindrically
   hyperbolic.

Status records an imported theorem, not independent mathematical verification.
