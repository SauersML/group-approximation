Filter the complete simple-order rows strictly after `McL` through `L4(4)`
by `20160 | |S|`.  Only `L4(4)` survives, and direct division gives index
`48960`.  The entrywise inclusion of binary matrices in `F4` realizes
`GL4(2)` inside `SL4(4)`; its image remains injective in `PSL4(4)` because
the binary subgroup has trivial intersection with the scalar center of
`SL4(4)`.  This proves `(SIL44-1)--(SIL44-3)`.
