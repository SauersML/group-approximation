title: Tame refinement monoids are separative and unperforated (Ara-Goodearl Theorem 2.3)
---

**Theorem (Ara--Goodearl, *Tame and wild refinement monoids*,
arXiv:1405.7582v2, Theorem 2.3).**  A refinement monoid is *tame* if it is an
inductive limit of finitely generated refinement monoids (Definition 2.1).
Every tame refinement monoid is separative (`2x = 2y = x + y` implies
`x = y`), unperforated (`mx <= my` implies `x <= y`), and has Riesz
interpolation.

Classes of tame refinement monoids listed in Examples 2.2 of the same paper:
finitely generated refinement monoids; unperforated cancellative refinement
monoids; refinement monoids with `2x = x`; the graph monoids `M(E)` of directed
graphs (Theorem 3.1 there); primely generated conical refinement monoids
(Ara--Pardo, Theorem 0.1); and regular conical refinement monoids
(`2x <= x`, Pardo--Wehrung, Theorem 4.4).

Wild refinement monoids exist.  Any monoid embeds order-embeddingly into a
refinement monoid, so a non-separative monoid yields a wild non-separative
refinement monoid.  Ara--Goodearl also construct wild monoids that are
separative, unperforated and stably finite.
