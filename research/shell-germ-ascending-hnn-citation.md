The required claim gives Q finitely presented iff R x_Z R is, and
either property forces R finitely presented. Apply
[Martinez-Perez, Proposition 3.8, arXiv:1302.2745v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1302.2745v2)
to two copies of R=ker(eta) semidirect <s>, with the identity
identification of the cyclic quotients. Its ascending directions
agree automatically for identical factors. Its finitely generated
kernel case is included by taking B=ker(eta). Its other case is
precisely the strict form of the target's inclusions and union.
Conversely those conditions satisfy the same proposition. This
proves both directions. The source requires finite presentation
of R; a finitely generated ascending base alone does not replace it.
