## The pushout

Conjugation of `E` on `A` extends uniquely to `V = A tensor Q`, because `V`
is generated by `A` over `Q` and the action is by group automorphisms; `A`
acts trivially on `V`, being abelian.  Form `V semidirect E` and let

```text
D = { (-a, a) : a in A }.
```
`D` is a subgroup, since the second coordinate acts trivially on `V`, and it
is normal: conjugating by `(v,e)` replaces `a` by `eae^-1 in A` in both
coordinates.  Put `E_Q = (V semidirect E)/D`.

The composites `V -> V semidirect E -> E_Q` and `E -> V semidirect E -> E_Q`
are injective, because `D` meets `V x 1` and `1 x E` trivially: an element
of `D` with trivial second coordinate has `a = 1`, and one with trivial first
coordinate likewise.  Modulo `V`, the image of `D` is the image of `A`, so
`E_Q / V = E/A = G`.

## The lift

Let `g in G` have order `m > 1` and choose `e in E` over `g`, so
`e^m = a` lies in `A`.  Then `e` commutes with `a`, hence fixes `a/m in V`.
In `E_Q` put `e-tilde = (-a/m)e`.  The two factors commute, so

```text
e-tilde^m = (-a/m)^m e^m = (-a)(a) = 1.
```
Its image in `G` is `g`, of order `m`, so its order is exactly `m`, proving
`(RK1)`.

## Why it is an obstruction

If `E` was torsion-free and `G` has an element of finite order `m > 1`, then
`E_Q` contains an element of order `m` and is not torsion-free.  So passing
to a divisible kernel trades away exactly the property the cover was built to
have.  Nothing here says whether `E_Q` is MF.
