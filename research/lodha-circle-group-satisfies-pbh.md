## Statement

Let `S = ⟨a, b, l, s⟩ ≤ Homeo(R ∪ {∞})` be Lodha's finitely presented infinite simple group
of piecewise projective circle homeomorphisms (arXiv:1710.06220v2), as in
`lodha-circle-group-is-finitely-presented-and-simple`.

1. The action of `S` on `QP^1 = Q ∪ {∞}` is of type (A).
2. Every subgroup of `S` satisfies permutational Boone--Higman, has solvable word problem,
   and embeds in a finitely presented simple group.
3. `S` embeds in a finitely presented simple group that is MIF, and in a finitely presented
   simple group that is highly transitive.

**What this answers.** BFFHZ Question 3.4 (arXiv:2503.21882v2, TeX `main.tex` l.480--482):
"Does every finitely presented simple group embed in a finitely presented simple MIF group?"
Part 3 is a positive answer for `S`. The overgroup comes from BFFHZ Theorem C, not from a
proof that `S` itself is MIF. Whether `S` is MIF was not examined. BFFHZ l.246 name circle
groups such as Thompson's `T` among the finitely presented simple groups that are not MIF,
citing Le Boudec--Matte Bon, Proposition 4.7. That proposition was not read here, and it was
not checked whether it applies to `S`.

**A discrepancy with the source, recorded honestly.** BFFHZ l.494 write: "To the best of our
knowledge, the only existing source of finitely presented infinite simple groups for which PBH
remains open is non-affine Kac--Moody groups over finite fields". Their text does not mention
Lodha's `S` and gives no argument for it. The earlier census
`research/artifacts/q34-pl-circle-families-2026-09-13.md` §3 listed `S` as not decided, with
finite generation of `Stab_S(∞)` as the remaining clause. `lodha-circle-group-infinity-stabilizer-is-fg`
supplies that clause. An argument for `S` may exist elsewhere, so no priority is claimed.

**Scope.** Only `S` and its subgroups. Lodha l.1444 states that `S` is generated by `G_0'`
and `T`, where `G_0'` is the commutator subgroup of the Lodha--Moore group. If that
containment holds, part 2 covers `G_0'` too. It was not re-derived, so it is not claimed.
