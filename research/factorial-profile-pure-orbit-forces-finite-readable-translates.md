Let

```text
N=W*(D_0,pi(Q_G))
```

be the negative-corner game algebra, and assume that `N` is a finite factor.
If a nonzero projection `p_0 in N` has a finite `Q_G`-orbit `T=(p_t)` and
every `p_t` lies below a joint atom `E_(r(t))` of `D_0`, then the full
readable translate algebra

```text
D_infinity=W*(pi(g)D_0pi(g)^*:g in Q_G)
```

is finite dimensional.

Thus the profile-pure premise is a genuine weakening only through central
variation in a nonfactorial source. The stabilizer-quotient compiler itself
does not require factoriality and remains valid there.
