Let

```text
N=W*(D_0,pi(Q_G)) subseteq q_-Mq_-                     (FPO1)
```

be the negative-corner von Neumann algebra generated by the readable data
and the structural unreadable representation, and assume that `N` is a
finite factor. Put

```text
D_infinity=W*(pi(g)D_0pi(g)^*:g in Q_G),                (FPO2)
```

and assume that `D_infinity` is abelian, as it is in the
fixed-point/stabilizer GNS model. If a nonzero projection `p_0 in N` has a
finite `Q_G`-conjugacy orbit `T=(p_t)` and every `p_t` lies below one
joint readable atom `E_(r(t))` of `D_0`, then `D_infinity` is finite
dimensional.

Consequently, for factorial perfect tracial ZPC models with abelian full
readable translate algebra, the following are
equivalent:

1. `D_infinity` is finite dimensional;
2. there is one nonzero finite profile-pure projection orbit.

Thus the weaker source premise
`perfect-gap-zpc-has-profile-pure-finite-projection-orbit` can differ from
`perfect-gap-zpc-has-finite-readable-translate-algebra` only through
nonfactorial central variation, or through the requirement that a chosen
factorial component retain literal stabilizer-character/IRS provenance.
The stabilizer-quotient compiler itself does not require factoriality and
remains valid before central decomposition.
