Citation import, secondary. Read on 2026-09-13 from the ar5iv rendering of Cornulier–de la
Harpe, arXiv:1403.3796, §1.F (outline of Chapter 8):

"A large part of Chapter 8 is devoted to the Bieri-Strebel splitting theorem: let G be a
compactly presented LC-group such that there exists a continuous surjective homomorphism
π: G ↠ Z; then G is isomorphic to an HNN-extension HNN(H, K, L, φ) of which the base group H
is a compactly generated subgroup of ker(π)."

A discrete group is compactly presented exactly when it is finitely presented, and compactly
generated exactly when it is finitely generated. The original is Bieri–Strebel, Comment. Math.
Helv. 53 (1978). The numbered theorem in Chapter 8 and the 1978 paper were not re-read.

The identity `ker pi = <<H>>`: `pi` vanishes on `H`, and `G/<<H>>` is infinite cyclic,
generated by the stable letter. So `pi` induces a surjection `Z -> Z`, which is an
isomorphism.
