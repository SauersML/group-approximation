For a full clopen section `C` with bounded return times, its induced
cycle core equals `C intersection Y`. Writing `I` for the transient
ideal and `J=1_C I 1_C`, the embedded `K_n(J)` normally generates
`K_n(I)` inside that kernel for every `n>=1` over `F_2`. The kernel
is locally finite, including in the unit-group case.
