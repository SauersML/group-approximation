**ESTABLISHED ONE-PREFIX FENCE.**  Let `G_6` be the finite subgroup of
`GL_6(F_2)` generated by the complete cross-return model in
`private-middle-swap-crosses-returned-morita-branches-but-has-gl6-model`, and
let

```text
c=C_1=e_(7_1,8_1) != 1.                                (GPS1)
```

Retain one literal depth-changing coarse-to-fine cell

```text
x=s_(00)t_0,              y=s_0t_(00),
yx=e_(0,0),               xy=f_(00,00),
J_x=E_12(x)E_21(y)E_12(x),              J_x^2=1.       (GPS2)
```

In its finite typed model, name the coarse source involution `r_e` and the
fine target involution `r_f`.  Identify

```text
c=r_e.                                                   (GPS3)
```

Thus the source of the depth-changing occurrence is not a fresh abstract
mark: it is the prescribed nontrivial child which survived every row of the
six-coordinate closure.

The union still has an exact finite marked model.  The typed model of
`(GPS2)` may be chosen over one-dimensional leaf spaces, so all of its
operators lie in a finite signed-permutation group `H`.  Both `c in G_6`
and `r_e in H` are nontrivial involutions.  Amplify the left regular
permutation actions of `G_6` and `H` to one common finite degree.  Each of
these involutions then acts as the same number of disjoint transpositions,
so a coordinate permutation conjugates one to the other.  After that
conjugation, the subgroup generated by the two finite images is a finite
permutation group and realizes `(GPS3)` exactly.  The restriction to `G_6`
is faithful, hence `c` remains nontrivial.

What survives is precisely the typed-carrier escape:

```text
P_src=K direct_sum K,
P_tgt=K direct_sum K direct_sum K direct_sum K,
1_(P_src) != 1_(P_tgt).                                 (GPS4)
```

The word `J_x` swaps one coarse minimal corner with one fine minimal corner,
but no retained group relation identifies the two packet identities.  In
particular the enlarged table does not imply the common-carrier support row

```text
r_f J_x r_e J_x^(-1)=1                                 (GPS5)
```

with both reflections acting on one marked packet identity.  This is the
row which would invoke the `2`-versus-`4` rank floor.

Therefore the first literal depth-changing prefix occurrence, even when
its coarse source is fixed to the actual surviving GL6 child, does not
close Atomic Morita Return.  A live continuation must authenticate the fine
packet identity/reflection on that same marked carrier, or prove the
equivalent off-diagonal support leakage estimate; another typed prefix
swap alone remains finite.

This is a scoped occurrence-table amalgam, not a finite representation of
the full binary Leavitt Steinberg group.  No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input,
stability theorem, trace hypothesis, literature theorem, local computation,
or Lean compilation is used.
