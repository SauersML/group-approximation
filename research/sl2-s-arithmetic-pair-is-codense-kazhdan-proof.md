A unitary image of a unipotent conjugate to its own power has finite
order; the normal closure of a unipotent power is a congruence subgroup
(Serre), so finite-dimensional unitary representations factor through
congruence quotients; `SL_2(Z[1/p])` is dense in the prime-to-`pq`
congruence completion by strong approximation; (T;FD) is Lubotzky--Zimmer.
Section 10 of the artifact.
