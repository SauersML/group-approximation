Dead route. Farley proves a-T-menability of `V` through its proper action on a
CAT(0) cube complex of expansions. The same complex for `nV` (`n >= 2`) carries
a proper action with finite vertex stabilizers, but it is not CAT(0): see the
established obstruction `brin-thompson-nv-stein-farley-complex-is-not-cat0`.
The route as stated therefore gives nothing.

A cubical repair would need an infinite-dimensional complex, because
Fournier-Facio–Wu–Zaremsky (arXiv:2603.24687) give property `FW_infinity` for
abstract twisted Brin–Thompson groups.
