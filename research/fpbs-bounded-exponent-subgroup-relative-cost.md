Theorem 4.1. For countably infinite G satisfying g^N=e, every infinite H<=G, and every essentially free pmp action alpha, relC(R_G;R_H)=0 and C(alpha)<=C(alpha|H); consequently C*(G)<=C*(H). No centralizer commutativity, finite-generation, or normality hypothesis remains.
