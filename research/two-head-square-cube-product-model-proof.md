For a middle prefix root, the only nontrivial conjugations of the anchored
`Z` roots are elementary shears

```text
X_i: Z_i |-> Z_iZ_(i+1),
Y_i: Z_(i+1) |-> Z_iZ_(i+1).                          (1)
```

They fix the all-positive character.  Hence the full middle
`GL_3(F_2)` packet reduces `Q_+++`.  The outer Weyls use disjoint indices,
fix every `Z_i`, commute with the middle packet, and generate the ordinary
coordinate permutation group `S_3`.  This proves the product decomposition
`(TSC3)` on the fixed atom.

The identities `(TSC2)` are `(HSW2)`.  Algebraically,

```text
(X_iU_i)^3=(X_iL_iJ_i)^3
 =L_i^3(X_iJ_i)^3=L_i,
```

because `X_iJ_i=Y_iX_i` has order three.  The outer and middle Coxeter
relations separately give

```text
L_1L_2L_1=L_2L_1L_2,
J_1J_2J_1=J_2J_1J_2.
```

Since the factors commute, multiplying these identities gives the braid
for `U_i=L_iJ_i`; conversely that diagonal braid together with one factor
braid recovers only the other factor braid.  No cross-factor equality is
created.

For the finite model, let the elementary abelian group generated by
`Z_1,Z_2,Z_3` act in its regular representation.  The middle
`GL_3(F_2)` acts by the linear substitutions `(1)`, and the outer `S_3`
acts trivially on these signs.  Tensor the canonical permutation
implementer of `(ell,g)` by its left regular operator on
`C[S_3 times GL_3(F_2)]`.  This is a genuine representation of the finite
semidirect product and respects every named sign covariance and actor
relation.

The joint positive `Z` eigenspace is the trivial-character line.  Every
linear change fixes that line, and the outer factor fixes it trivially.
Therefore compression to `Q_+++` removes the canonical label implementer
and leaves exactly

```text
D_(L_i)=lambda(ell_i,1),
D_(J_i)=lambda(1,J_i),
D_(U_i)=lambda(ell_i,J_i).                            (2)
```

Equation `(2)` proves `(TSC5)`.  Since `J_i` is nonidentity in
`GL_3(F_2)`, its left regular operator is nonidentity.  This exact common
eighth-atom model proves the claimed two-loop parity fence.
