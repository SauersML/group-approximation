**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `R` be a countable unital ring.  Suppose there are

```text
0 != p = p^2,          x_0,x_1,y_0,y_1 in pRp,
y_i x_j = delta_(ij) p        (i,j in {0,1}).                     (PIC1)
```

Then for every `n >= 2`:

* every homomorphism from `EL_n(R)` to an MF group kills the corner
  subgroup `iota_p(EL_n(pRp))` of `corner-unitization-embeds-elementary-groups`;
* in particular it kills `e_12(p) != 1`;
* consequently `EL_n(R)` is **not MF**.

For `n >= 3` the kernel also contains every `e_ij(a)` with `a` in the
two-sided ideal `RpR`, by

```text
[e_ik(a), e_kj(p)] = e_ij(ap),
[e_ik(ap), e_kj(b)] = e_ij(apb),                                  (PIC2)
```

normality of the kernel and additivity in each root subgroup.  The
rank-three restriction is real: `(PIC2)` needs a third index.  Nothing
here says the congruence kernel of `RpR` is generated by those roots.

`(PIC1)` is a **finite algebraic certificate**: besides `p != 0` it is the
finite list `p^2 = p`, `px_i = x_i = x_ip`, `py_i = y_i = y_ip`, and the
four equations of `(PIC1)`.  It says exactly that `p` is a properly
infinite idempotent; no exhaustion `x_0y_0 + x_1y_1 = p` is required.  It
is checkable wherever those equalities and `p != 0` are checkable in the
given ring, which is not a decidability assertion about arbitrary finite
presentations.

**The conclusion is non-MF, not universal triviality.**  When `p != 1` the
ring can have a nonzero quotient with residually finite elementary group,
so `EL_n(R)` may admit nontrivial MF-target homomorphisms; see
`nonfull-order-two-defect-ring-has-properly-infinite-corner`.  That is the
difference from the full-defect theorem, whose conclusion is
`Rad_MF(EL_n(R)) = EL_n(R)`.
