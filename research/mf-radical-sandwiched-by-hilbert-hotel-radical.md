**ESTABLISHED** (route `mf-radical-sandwiched-by-hilbert-hotel-proof`), unreviewed. Restated 2026-09-13
after review by `un-verify-open`: the left bound is now the first-stage defect ideal `d(R)`.

Let `R` be a countable unital ring whose one-sided inverse defects have finite additive order, `n >= 4`,
`d = d(R)` the ideal generated by all defects and `hh = hh(R)` the smallest ideal with directly finite
quotient. If `EL_n(R/hh)` is MF, then

```text
EL_n(R, d)  <=  Rad_MF(EL_n(R))  <=  EL_n(R) n GL_n(R, hh).
```

So the maximal MF quotient of `EL_n(R)` lies between `EL_n(R/hh)` and `EL_n(R)/EL_n(R,d)`.

This is where the two calibrating results meet. The **paradox side supplies the radical**, through one-sided
Kazhdan transport; the **measure side supplies the hypothesis** that `EL_n(R/hh)` is MF, through LEF towers,
residually finite actions or exact matricial models.

**Sharpness.**
- **Left bound at the `hh` level:** conditional on `relative-torsion-defect-mf-radical-step`
  (`mf-radical-contains-hilbert-hotel-elementary-subgroup`); unconditional when `hh = d`.
- **Right bound, not sharp in general.** For the universal torsion-defect ring `A_m`, `m > 2`
  (`universal-torsion-defect-rings-have-exact-mf-radical`), `hh = d = I_m` and `EL_n(A_m/I_m) = EL_n(Z[z,z^-1])`
  is residually finite, hence MF, and the left bound is attained: `Rad_MF = E_n(A_m, I_m) = SL_fin(Z/m)`. But the
  congruence kernel is `{g in GL_fin(Z/m) : det g = +-1}`, a central `C_2` larger. So the relative `K_1`
  slack between the bounds is genuine, and it can lie outside the radical.

**Model tests** (artifact section 6): `d = hh = R` for simple rings that are not directly finite recovers
`cor:simple-infinite-ring`; `d = hh = 0` for the Pestov 9.1 ring; `d = hh = JeJ` for the Toeplitz-Jacobson
algebra, where the manuscript proves `Rad_MF(EL_n(J)) = EL_n(J, JeJ)`, the left bound.
