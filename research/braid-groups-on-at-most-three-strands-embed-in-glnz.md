title: B_1, B_2 and B_3 embed in GL_m(Z); B_3 embeds in GL_24(Z)
---

`B_1` is trivial, `B_2 ≅ Z` embeds in `GL_2(Z)` by
`sigma_1^k -> [[1, k], [0, 1]]`, and `B_3` has an injective homomorphism into
`GL_24(Z)`.

So the smallest open case of Zaremsky Problem 3.2
(`zaremsky-3-02-braid-groups-embed-in-glnz`) is `B_4`.
