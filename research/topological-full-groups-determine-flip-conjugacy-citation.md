**H. Matui, *Some remarks on topological full groups of Cantor minimal systems*, arXiv:math/0404117v3, §2:**
> "The following is the main theorem of [GPS2].
> Theorem 2.8 ([GPS2, Corollary 4.4, 4.6, 4.11, 5.18]). Let (X,φ) and (Y,ψ) be Cantor minimal systems.
> (i) The two Cantor minimal systems are orbit equivalent if and only if [φ] and [ψ] are isomorphic as abstract groups.
> (ii) The two Cantor minimal systems are flip conjugate if and only if [[φ]] and [[ψ]] are isomorphic as abstract
> groups.
> (iii) The two Cantor minimal systems are flip conjugate if and only if [[φ]]_0 and [[ψ]]_0 are isomorphic as
> abstract groups."

[GPS2] is Giordano–Putnam–Skau, *Full groups of Cantor minimal systems*, Israel J. Math. 111 (1999).

**S. Bezuglyi, K. Medynets, *Full groups, flip conjugacy, and orbit equivalence of Cantor minimal systems*,
arXiv:math/0611173v1 (Colloq. Math. 110 (2008)).**

Definition, item (2):
> "(X_1,φ_1) and (X_2,φ_2) are called flip conjugated if there exists a homeomorphism F: X_1 → X_2 such that
> F∘φ_1∘F^{-1} is equal to either φ_2 or φ_2^{-1}."

Theorem 5.13:
> "Let (X_1,φ_1) and (X_2,φ_2) be Cantor minimal systems. Then φ_1 and φ_2 are flip conjugated if and only if one of
> the following statements holds: (1) D([[φ_1]]) ≅ D([[φ_2]]); (2) [[φ_1]]_0 ≅ [[φ_2]]_0; (3) [[φ_1]] ≅ [[φ_2]]."

From its proof:
> "Notice that the cases (2) and (3) are already proved in [GPS]. ... by Theorem 5.8 every isomorphism between Γ_1
> and Γ_2 is spatial. By Lemma 5.12, this spatial isomorphism can be extended to the isomorphism of [[φ_1]] and
> [[φ_2]]. Then it follows from Corollary 2.7 of [BoTo] that φ_1 and φ_2 are flip conjugated."

[BoTo] is Boyle–Tomiyama, *Bounded topological orbit equivalence and C*-algebras*, J. Math. Soc. Japan 50 (1998),
317–329.

**Scope.** The GPS 1999 paper itself was not opened. Its corollary numbers are as printed by Matui. The claim uses only
item (ii) / case (3).
