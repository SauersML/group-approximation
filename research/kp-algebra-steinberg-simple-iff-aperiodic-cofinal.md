**ESTABLISHED (import; see `kp-algebra-steinberg-simple-iff-aperiodic-cofinal-citation`).**

1. For a finitely aligned `k`-graph `Λ` with boundary-path groupoid `𝒢_Λ`, and a commutative ring `R` with `1`,
   there is an isomorphism `KP_R(Λ) ≅ A_R(𝒢_Λ)`, sending `s_λ` to the indicator of `Z_Λ(λ *_s s(λ))`.
2. For a row-finite `k`-graph `Λ` without sources, `KP_R(Λ)` is simple iff `R` is a field and `Λ` is aperiodic and
   cofinal.
