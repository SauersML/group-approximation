Let `G = H semidirect_product Z` satisfy the weak Atiyah conjecture. If
`H` is hyperbolic and coherent, then `G` is coherent.

Consequently, if `H` is hyperbolic, virtually locally indicable, and
coherent, then `H semidirect_product Z` is coherent for every
automorphism defining the semidirect product.
