There are perfect finitely generated groups `C<A` with `C` normal,
residually finite and Kazhdan, such that every finite-dimensional
representation has `rho(C)=rho(A)`, yet the coset action is sofic and
relative-commutant collapse fails in a CE model.  Product microstates are
unions of uniform `C`-expanders with the complementary factor moving their
component set.  Therefore a successful arithmetic theorem must use
non-normal transverse Hecke geometry, not generic co-density and expansion.
