**ESTABLISHED (Theorem 1 of the artifact; lane proof, not externally
reviewed).**  Let `E` be separable, nuclear and stably finite, and
`R = R_qd(E)` its quasidiagonal radical.  Suppose `E/J` satisfies the UCT for
every ideal `J ⊆ R` of `E`.  If some `M_n(R)` contains a projection that is full
in `R`, then `R = 0`, so `E` is quasidiagonal.

Equivalently, a negative answer to STW Problem VII whose quotients by ideals
inside the radical satisfy the UCT has a radical generated by no projection.
With `stw07-qd-radical-criterion-compact-open`, that radical is also not
compact-free and contains a traced compact ideal.

Mechanism.
1. Spielberg's criterion makes the boundary subgroup
   `H = ∂(K_1(E/R)) ⊆ K_0(R)` singular.
2. Lemma 2.12 of Moutzouris gives a tracial state on the full corner
   `pM_n(R)p` vanishing on `H`.  Its kernel is `pM_n(J)p` for an ideal
   `J ⊊ R`.
3. The quotient corner carries a faithful trace vanishing on the image of `H`.
   Schafhauser's realisation (Moutzouris Theorem 4.1) embeds it in a simple AF
   algebra, killing that image.
4. Brown--Dadarlat Theorem 3.4 then makes `E/J` quasidiagonal, contradicting
   the minimality of the radical.

Singularity never has to survive a quotient of `R`, because the boundary is
taken for an extension whose quotient is already quasidiagonal.
