Let `T` be the Toeplitz algebra and put `A=T tensor_min Z`.  Then `A` is
separable, nuclear, non-simple, Z-stable, and hence pure and Cu-regular.
Nevertheless, there are nonzero projections `p,q` over the ideal
`K tensor Z` such that

```text
[p] = [q] in K_0(A),       but       [p] != [q] in Cu(A).
```

The same two compact Cuntz classes remain distinct after tensoring `A` with
Z.  Thus pureness is fully compatible with ambient-K0 noncancellation, but
the Toeplitz/index-boundary mechanism cannot provide an LXXVI counterexample.
