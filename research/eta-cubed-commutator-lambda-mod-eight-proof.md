Part 1, Proposition 3.1.

The map c_4 is 1 on the fat wedge, so it factors through
cbar_4: S^6 -> SU(2) with [cbar_4] = 6 nu' = eta_3^3. A regular preimage L of
y != 1 lies in the open top cell ≅ R^6. There a BSpin_8-structure is an
orientation, so L inherits the structure induced by its normal framing. By
Pontryagin–Thom, (L, framing) is the stabilization eta^3 = 12 nu ≠ 0 in
pi_3^s. Its image in Omega^8_3 is nonzero by the prerequisite. The class has
order two, so orientation signs do not matter.
