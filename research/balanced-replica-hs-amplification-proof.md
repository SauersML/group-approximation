Direct proof, given in the claim: expand `d_2` through `Re tau(U^*V)`, use
`conj(U)^* = conj(U^*)` to identify `tau(B(U)^*B(V)) = |tau(U^*V)|^2`, bound
`|a|^2 ≥ (Re a)^2`, and apply `1 - t^m ≤ m(1-t)` on `[0,1]` for the `m`-fold
statement.  No external input.
