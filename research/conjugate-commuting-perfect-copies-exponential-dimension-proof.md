Complete reducibility of the unitary restriction to the product of the
commuting copies gives tensor blocks; conjugation invariance equalizes the
mark densities of the `n` conjugate marks; summing the per-block densities
over the `n` positions bounds the weighted average number of nontrivial
factors from below by `n mu`; perfectness makes each nontrivial factor at
least two-dimensional.  Section 5 of the artifact.
