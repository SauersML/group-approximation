**ESTABLISHED AUTHENTICATION FIREWALL.**  Fix one selected simple packet
block

```text
S=P_f k[B_f] isomorphic to M_s(k),                    (NCG1)
```

over the splitting field, and fix any Steinberg rank `N>=3`.  There are
finite coefficient rings and exact finite-dimensional unitary
representations satisfying the complete rank-`N` Steinberg relations and
the named corner rows

```text
P^2=P,             [P,u_i]=[P,b_i]=0,
P(u_i-b_i)=0,                                       (NCG2)
```

in which `x_13(P)` has fixed nontrivial spectral density, but the physical
diagonal actions of one `u_i` and `b_i` agree on no asymptotically
positive-density near-exact carrier.

More precisely, there is a sequence with

```text
tr(1-P_(x_13(P),1))=1-1/p,                           (NCG3)
||(D_(u_i)D_(b_i)^(-1)-1)e_n||_2->0
       implies tr(e_n)->0.                            (NCG4)
```

`agent-free-compressor-authenticated-corner-pays-hs` is false if it is asked
to follow from the payload root, the full finite packet block, `(BCL0)`, and
arbitrarily high-rank Steinberg identities alone.

The conclusion does not refute the simultaneous full-corner route.  These
models do not satisfy the nonstationary HNN comparison on the selected block.
They prove instead that authentication and HNN payment cannot be separated
into two sequential lemmas: a successful normalized-HS proof must use the
stable-letter `B_f/A_f` mismatch while eliminating the complement gauge.
