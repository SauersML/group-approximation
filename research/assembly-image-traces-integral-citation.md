Published results:
- M. F. Atiyah, *Elliptic operators, discrete groups and von Neumann algebras*,
  Astérisque 32–33 (1976);
- J. Kaad and V. Proietti, *Index theory on the Miščenko bundle*, Kyoto J. Math. 62 (2022),
  arXiv:1807.05757, which reproves the L²-index theorem for principal bundles over compact
  Hausdorff spaces.

Statement and proof read from Gomez Aparicio–Julg–Valette, *The Baum–Connes conjecture: an
extended survey* (arXiv:1905.10081), §4.5.3, proof of Proposition 4.20, extracted on MSI:
"assuming Γ to be torsion-free, τ_* is always integer-valued on the image of μ_r in
K_0(C*_r(Γ))". The argument goes through geometric cycles `(Z, ξ)` with free proper actions
and `τ_*(μ_r(Z, ξ)) = Ind(D) ∈ Z`. Line references are in
`research/artifacts/kk-sl3z-congruence-2026-09-12.md`.
