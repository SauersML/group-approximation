**Item 1.** `rho'` is an action of `P` on `N` because `rho` is. The map `hat(g)`
permutes the cones `C_n = 0^n 1 C` by `n -> rho'_g(n)` and fixes `p`. It is a
bijection, continuous at every point of `C \ {p}` (locally a prefix replacement),
and continuous at `p`: a neighbourhood `0^k C` of `p` is the union of `{p}` and the
cones `C_n`, `n >= k`, and `hat(g)^(±1)` maps all but finitely many of these into
`0^k C` because `rho'_g` is a permutation. So `hat(g)` is a homeomorphism,
`g -> hat(g)` is a homomorphism, and away from `p` every `hat(g)` is locally in `V`.
The singular points of elements of `E_rho` lie in `V·p`. If `g != 1`, `rho'_g`
moves infinitely many `n`, so no element of `V` agrees with `hat(g)` near `p`
(elements of `V` act on the cones `C_n` near `p` by `n -> n+k` eventually). So the
germ of `hat(g)` at `p` is not a `V` germ, `hat` is injective, and the singular set
is exactly `V·p`. Fullness and finite germs are verbatim as in
`shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`: nothing there uses
transitivity or freeness of the regular action.

**Item 2.** The germ of `hat(g)` at `p` is determined by the near class of
`rho'_g`, and a `V` germ at `p` is a near translation `tau^k`. This identifies
`(E_rho)_p` with `<rho'(P), tau>` in `NearSym(N)` exactly as for the regular action.

**Item 3.** The index-two fiber product proof splits `N` by parity near infinity;
`rho'` acts on evens and fixes odds, and `tau^2` acts as `(s, s)`. It uses only
that `rho'_g` is `rho_g` on evens and the identity on odds, and that `rho` is
near-faithful. The ascending HNN gate is a statement about `R_rho` and `eta`.
The necessity proof maps the Schreier complex of `Stab(p)` into the `V` germ
groupoid with free loops for the finitely many letters `hat(s)`, `s in S`; it
uses only that `hat(s)` is locally `V` away from `p`.

**Item 4.** The independent-germ proof in
`shell-independent-germs-generate-stabilizers-proof` uses: `Q_x` finitely
generated (true, `P` is), representatives singular only at one point (item 1),
and disjoint-cone prefix interpolation in `V`. None of these use regularity.

**Item 5.** `E_rho` acts faithfully and highly transitively on the dense set
`Omega` (it contains `V`), and its point stabilizers are finitely generated by
item 4. Apply the twisted Brin–Thompson criterion.
