**Established by citation.**  This is the theorem STW label
`FiniteDimBoundaryImpliesGamma` in arXiv:2506.10902, with the
attribution chain they give: Kirchberg--Rordam and Toms--White--Winter
(Z-stability from finite-dimensional boundaries with comparison),
Sato's techniques, and the uniform-Γ formulation in
Castillejos--Carrion--Evington--Gabe--Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White-era
work (CETWW): complemented partitions of unity are constructed over a
compact finite-dimensional extremal boundary by partition-of-unity
arguments on the boundary space, and CPoU yields uniform property Γ.
