Notation as in `ce-exact-affine-compilers-force-nonhyperlinear-corners`. Assume
`J` has order exactly `n` in `Gamma` and that `Gamma` is hyperlinear.

## Step 1: the canonical corner is a twisted group von Neumann algebra

Let `tau_Gamma` be the canonical trace and `L(Gamma)` the group von Neumann
algebra. Put `M_omega = p_omega L(Gamma) p_omega`, with the normalized trace
`tau_omega = n tau_Gamma(p_omega . p_omega)` of (GC3). By (CS1),
`tau_omega(p_omega) = 1`. The GNS representation of the canonical model
`tau_omega` of the C*-corner generates exactly `M_omega`.

Choose a section `s : Gamma/<J> -> Gamma` with `s(e) = e`. Then
`s(x)s(y) = J^(k(x,y)) s(xy)`, and `alpha(x,y) = J^(k(x,y))` is the
classifying 2-cocycle of `0 -> <J> -> Gamma -> Gamma/<J> -> 0`. Put
`u_x = s(x) p_omega`.

**Eigenvalue of `J`.** Reindexing the sum (GC1),

```text
J p_omega = (1/n) sum_k conj(omega(J^k)) J^(k+1) = omega(J) p_omega.
```

**Twisted relations.** `u_x` is a unitary of `M_omega`. By centrality of
`p_omega` and the previous display,

```text
u_x u_y = omega(alpha(x,y)) u_(xy).
```

**Density.** Every `g in Gamma` has the form `g = J^k s(x)`, so
`g p_omega = omega(J^k) u_x`. Hence the `u_x` span a weakly dense
*-subalgebra of `M_omega`.

**Trace.** `tau_omega(u_x) = sum_k conj(omega(J^k)) tau_Gamma(s(x) J^k)`,
and this is `1` if `x = e` and `0` otherwise.

A tracial von Neumann algebra generated by unitaries `u_x` with these
multiplication rules and this trace is the twisted group von Neumann algebra
`L_(omega o alpha)[Gamma/<J>]` with its canonical trace. The GNS
representations of the two traces agree on the dense twisted group algebras,
so they are unitarily equivalent. Thus
`(M_omega, tau_omega) = L_(omega o alpha)[Gamma/<J>]`.

## Step 2: the canonical model is Connes-embeddable

`thom-central-corner-criterion` (Thom, arXiv:0810.2180, Lemma 3.3) applies
with central subgroup `C = <J>`. It gives: if `Gamma` is hyperlinear, then
`L_(beta o alpha)[Gamma/C]` embeds into `R^omega` for every character `beta` of
`C`. Take `beta = omega`. By Step 1, `(M_omega, tau_omega)` admits a
trace-preserving embedding into `R^U`, so `tau_omega` is a Connes-embeddable
model of the corner.

## Step 3: apply (CE) to the canonical model

By (CE), the observables (GC5) computed in the GNS representation of
`tau_omega` form a perfect tracial model of `B`. Its context projections are

```text
Q_(c,phi) = sum_(chi : D_c(chi) = phi) e_chi,     phi in F_2^(V_c),
```

and they lie in `M_omega`.

**It is Connes-embeddable.** Let `N` be the von Neumann algebra generated by
the `X_v`. It is a von Neumann subalgebra of `M_omega` containing the unit
`p_omega`, and the source trace is the restriction of `tau_omega`. Composing
the inclusion `N subseteq M_omega` with the embedding of Step 2 gives a
trace-preserving embedding of `N` into `R^U`.

**Its support is affine and safe.** This is verbatim the (CS2) argument:
- by (CS1), `tau_omega(Q_(c,phi)) > 0` exactly when
  `phi in D_c(X_(A_(t(c)))(omega))`;
- `X_(A_(t(c)))(omega)` is a coset of the annihilator of `A cap <J>`, and
  the affine map `D_c` sends it to a coset of the image subgroup;
- perfection puts that support inside `C_c`.

This is (CE1).

**Shared randomness.** With seeds `r` of mass `nu(r) > 0` and affine
`D_c^r`, the averaged value is `sum_r nu(r) val_r = 1` with each
`val_r <= 1`. So each such `val_r = 1`, and Step 3 applies seed by seed.

## Consequence 1

Suppose `B` has no Connes-embeddable perfect model with `Aff(S_c) subseteq C_c`
for every `c`. Steps 1--3 show that a CE-exact affine compiler into a corner
with `J` of order `n` and `Gamma` hyperlinear would produce one. So `Gamma` is
nonhyperlinear.

## Consequence 2

Suppose a compiler has (PC1), so `J != 1` at halting inputs. Write
`p_omega = (1 - J)/2` for `n = 2`, and assume its soundness decoder is (GC5)
with affine `D_c` on every Connes-embeddable model of the corner. Every such
model arises as a limit of approximate representations with `J` separated
from one: take microstates for the embedding into `R^U` and cut down by the
spectral projection of `J`. So an ultraproduct decoder that is exact on those
limits satisfies (CE).

At a halting input the corner is nonzero, and Consequence 1 applies whenever
that input's source is affine-unsafe.
