integral relative-Euler normalization. For E=C^d direct-sum H over
S2 times CP^(d-1) times a product of projective spaces, the Euler map
on K0 has image exactly d Z. The coefficients are d times integral
multinomial coefficients; the S2 Bott class times the distinguished
projective tautological line realizes exactly ±d. Hence the entire
finite-stage gauge component group is Z/d.

the initial characteristic unitary with a generator. Extension by the
identity on the new rank-r tautological block maps the section-space
fundamental-group integer to its product with the block's top Chern
number, ±1. A transverse section over D2 times the old base, paired
with a transverse section of the new block, proves this product formula
by signed zero counts. Thus the induced component map is an isomorphism.

evaluation proves simplicity; compatible traces give stable finiteness.
Even-cell K-theory gives K1 zero. Polar approximation and finite-stage
