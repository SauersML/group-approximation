Let `K` be compact Hausdorff of finite covering dimension, and let `(M,E)`
be a factorial tracially complete C-star algebra over `K` whose every
fibre is diffuse II1. If the uniform `2`-norm Hilbert `C(K)`-module
completion is countably generated, then every fibre-gap trace vanishes and

```text
T(M)={a |-> integral_K tau_x(a_x) dmu(x):
      mu a Radon probability measure on K}.            (AD-6)
```

No bound on the finite dimension, common ambient factor, fixed isomorphism
type, local triviality, hyperfiniteness, full fundamental group, property
Gamma, or metrizability is assumed.
