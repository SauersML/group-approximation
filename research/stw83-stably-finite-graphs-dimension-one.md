title: Every stably finite graph C*-algebra has nuclear dimension at most one
artifacts:

---

Let `E` be a directed graph.  If `C*(E)` is stably finite, then

```text
dim_nuc(C*(E)) <= 1.
```

Equivalently, the conclusion holds when `C*(E)` is AF-embeddable, or when no
return path in `E` has an entrance.  The bound is sharp for stably finite graph
algebras which are not AF.
