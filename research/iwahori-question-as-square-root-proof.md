`Gamma^0(2)/+-` is generated by `T^2` and `V`; conjugation by `diag(2,1)`
sends them to `T` and `V^2`; the amalgam presentation follows, and
solving the gluing for the second copy in terms of a square root `s` of
`T` reduces the extension condition to `(eps s)^3 = 1`.
