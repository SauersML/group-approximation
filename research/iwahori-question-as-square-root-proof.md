`Gamma_0(2)/+-` is generated by `T=xy` and `eps=xy^2xyx`.  In the vertex
conjugated by `diag(2,1)` these same two edge elements are `(x'y')^2` and
`x'y'x'y'^2x'`, respectively.  This gives `(SR1)`.  Writing `s=x'y'`, the
second edge identity becomes `eps=sx's^(-1)`; hence
`x'=s^(-1)eps s`, `y'=s^(-1)eps T`, and the only remaining vertex relation
is `(eps s)^3=1`.
