**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G` be a torsion-free group, `k` a field, and
`alpha, beta` nonzero elements of `k[G]` with `alpha beta = 0`. Put
`A = supp alpha` and, for `a in A`,

```text
U_a = { c^-1 a : c in A, c != a }.
```

Then for every `a in A` there are `k >= 2` and `u_0, ..., u_(k-1) in U_a`,
not all equal, with `u_(k-1) ... u_1 u_0 = 1`. So the subsemigroup of `G`
generated by `U_a` contains `1`, through a word that uses at least two
distinct elements of `U_a`.

**Consequences.**
1. *Dynamical exclusion.* Suppose `G` acts on a set `X`, and some `a in A`
   has a subset `Y` of `X` with `u(Y)` a proper subset of `Y` for every
   `u in U_a`. Then `alpha` is not a left zero divisor. Indeed a product of
   such maps sends `Y` into `u_(k-1)(Y)`, a proper subset, so it is not the
   identity.
2. *Linear groups.* Let `G <= GL_d(R)` be torsion-free, and let `C` be a
   closed convex cone with nonempty interior containing no line. If for some
   `a in A` every `u in U_a` maps `C \ {0}` into the interior of `C`, then
   `alpha` is not a left zero divisor, over every field. Take
   `Y = C \ {0}`. For example, `alpha = r_0 + r_1 x + r_2 y` with
   `x^-1` and `y^-1` positive matrices is not a left zero divisor.
3. *Right zero divisors.* The anti-automorphism `g -> g^-1` of `k[G]`
   exchanges left and right zero divisors and replaces `A` by `A^-1`. So the
   same holds for `beta alpha = 0` with `U_a` replaced by
   `{ c a^-1 : c in A, c != a }`.
4. *Support three.* For `supp alpha = {1, x, y}` the three sets are
   `U_1 = {x^-1, y^-1}`, `U_x = {x, y^-1 x}` and `U_y = {y, x^-1 y}`.
   A counterexample needs a positive relation in each of them.
5. *Orderable quotients.* With `phi: <A> -> L` into a left-ordered group and
   `a` minimising `phi` on `A`, every `phi(u)`, `u in U_a`, is at most `1`,
   and a product equal to `1` forces `phi(c) = phi(a)` for each `c` used.
   This recovers item 1 of `small-non-up-left-support-has-no-orderable-quotient`.

*Scope.* The argument is the cycle argument in the proof of
`small-non-up-left-support-has-no-orderable-quotient`, stated at the group
level. No novelty is claimed; the positive-relation form is not stated in
the sources read for the zero-divisor search artifact.
