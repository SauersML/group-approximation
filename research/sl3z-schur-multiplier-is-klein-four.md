```text
H_2(SL_3(Z); Z)  ≅  M(SL_3(Z))  ≅  Z/2 × Z/2 .
```

  divisible abelian group `A`. In particular `H^2(SL_3(Z); T) ≅ Z/2 × Z/2` and
  `H^2(SL_3(Z); R) = 0`.
- **Perfectness is elementary.** For distinct `i, j, k`, `e_ij(a) = [e_ik(a), e_kj(1)]`, and
  `SL_3(Z)` is generated by elementary matrices (Euclidean row reduction).
