Section 4. Given k disjoint torsion supports of orders <=n and an H-backbone replacing its letters in <=n-1 steps, the retained a-edge density is q+(1-q)prod_i(1-q^(d_i-1)), bounded by q+exp(-k q^(n-1)). Every missing a-edge has a route of length <=(n-1)(nL+1). Coding radius is finite and explicit.
