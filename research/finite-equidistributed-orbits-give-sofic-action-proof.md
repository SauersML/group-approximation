Full proof: Section 2 of `research/artifacts/sl3z-lattice-space-hecke-sofic-2026-09-12.md`. Soficity is
tested by Lemma LC: an action is sofic iff it has finite models of every level and error. Lemma LC is
proved in Section 2 of `research/artifacts/nonsofic-action-mixing-transfer-2026-09-12.md`, inside the
proof of `sofic-action-with-sofic-ergodic-components-is-sofic`.

1. **Null-boundary partitions.** `X` is metrizable. For a dense sequence `(x_i)`, all but countably many
   radii give balls with `μ`-null boundary, so `X` has a countable base of open `μ`-continuity sets.
   The partitions `P_m` generated by the first `m` of them increase and generate the Borel σ-algebra.
   Complements, finite intersections and translates keep boundaries null.
2. **Portmanteau.** Convergence on `C_c` to a probability measure gives tightness, hence convergence on
   bounded continuous functions. So `ν_k(B) → μ(B)` whenever `μ(∂B) = 0`.
3. **Models.** Let `σ_k : Γ → Sym(W_k)` be a sofic approximation. On `V_k × W_k`, let `g` act by
   (its restriction to `V_k`) `×` `σ_k(g)`, with partition `(q ∩ V_k) × W_k` for `q ∈ P_m`. A word `w`
   equals `1_(B_w) u_(h_w)` in the crossed product, with `μ(∂B_w) = 0`. The same reduction is exact on
   the model, because the first factor is an honest action matching the action on `X`. So

   ```text
   tr( w on V_k × W_k ) = ( |{v ∈ B_w ∩ V_k : h_w v = v}| / |V_k| ) · tr( S_w(σ_k) ),
   ```

   where `S_w` is the group word of `w`. If `h_w = 1`, this tends to `μ(B_w) = τ(w)` by step 2.
   Otherwise its modulus is at most `|tr S_w(σ_k)| → 0 = τ(w)`. Each `𝒲_m` is finite, so models of
   every level and error exist, and Lemma LC gives soficity. ∎
