title: Uniformly bounded local virtual polycyclicity forces finite nuclear dimension
---

Let `G` be a countable group such that every finitely generated subgroup is
virtually polycyclic, and suppose

```text
sup{h(H): H subset G finitely generated}=r<infinity.
```

Then `G` is amenable and `C*(G)` has finite nuclear dimension.  More precisely,
there is a finite number `N(r)`, depending only on `r`, such that

```text
dim_nuc(C*(G)) <= N(r).
```

The group `G` need not itself be finitely generated, virtually polycyclic, or
virtually solvable.  For example, bounded-Hirsch-rank factors can be combined
with countable locally finite groups having unbounded finite subgroup
complexity.
