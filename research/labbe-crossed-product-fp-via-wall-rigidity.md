- **Wall rigidity.** By the first claim, `Ω_U` is `D`-wall-rigid for `D >= D_0`.
- **Quantum rigidity.** The second claim, applied to `Ω_U`, gives `D'`-quantum rigidity.
- **Finite presentation.** The third claim then makes `LC(Ω_U, F_q) ⋊ Z^2` finitely presented.

The first claim is established; the second is open.
