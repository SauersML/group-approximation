Choose four-point permutations `a,b,d,k` with `[a,b]=d`, `d^2=b^2=1`,
`ka=ak`, and `kdk^(-1)=db^(-1)`.  On the six-clock choose diagonal `H`
such that `H(nu^(-1)ZX)H^(-1)=X`.  Then `A=P_k tensor H` simultaneously
solves (2.12) and the covariance form of (2.7); taking `D=A^(-1)` solves
(2.8).  The earlier Weyl assignment solves (2.4)--(2.6), and the involution
`b` leaves `w_beta^4=nu^(-4)I=omega I`.
