**ESTABLISHED EXACT-COMPLETENESS FIREWALL.**  Let `(M,tau)` be a finite
factor generated by Boolean involutions `(B_x)_(x in X)`.  Assume that for
every context `c`, their joint PVM `(p_(c,a)(B))_(a in R_c)` has full support:

```text
tau(p_(c,a)(B))>0                  (a in R_c).          (FCC1)
```

Let `(N,Tr)` be any finite tracial von Neumann algebra containing `M`
trace-preservingly.  For each context let `(A_(c,x))_(x in c)` be commuting
involutions in `N`, with joint PVM `p_(c,a)(A)`, and choose a proper support
set `K_c proper-subset R_c`.  Put

```text
E_c=sum_(a in K_c) p_(c,a)(A).                          (FCC2)
```

There do not exist a nonzero projection `Q in N` and partial isometries
`S_c in N` satisfying, for every context and incidence,

```text
S_c^*S_c=Q,
S_cS_c^*<=E_c,
A_(c,x)S_c=S_cB_x.                                     (FCC3)
```

The ranges in `(FCC3)` need not reduce any larger algebra, need not agree
between contexts, and may be maximally noncommuting.  The obstruction is the
common source itself.  Exact covariance makes `Q` commute with every global
variable.  The normalized trace on `Q`, restricted to the source factor, is
therefore exactly `tau`.  But cap range and polynomial covariance make every
excluded source atom vanish on `Q`, contradicting `(FCC1)`.

For `R_*`, every maximal Fano cap is proper and excludes three honest atoms.
Thus selected central, isotypic, or external multiplicity projections cannot
be made into exact covariant Fano-cap ranges over one nonzero common source
while preserving a full-support perfect factor model.  The strengthened POVM
decoder removes the need for range reduction, but it does not remove this
exact-completeness obstruction.

There is a sharp covariance dichotomy.

1. If separate selected type projections are linked only by retained
   transporter unitaries, arbitrary sheet holonomy has exact finite monomial
   models by `finite-sheet-transport-networks-are-exactly-matricial`.  Those
   transports do not provide one literal common initial projection carrying
   the global variables.
2. If global relations identify a nonzero common source and impose the
   covariance needed by the POVM decoder, `(FCC3)` applies and a full-support
   factor witness is destroyed.

Hence global commutation/covariance of sharp cap or type projections does not
close the cross-context seam.  A viable construction must either start from
a different perfect trace already supported on caps, retain genuinely soft
POVM effects rather than sharp cap ranges, or derive the common-source
covariance only through a finite-matrix/canonical-type obstruction which is
not an exact identity in the supplied full-support model.
