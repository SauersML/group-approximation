**Established by citation.**  Theorem 4.2 converts an `L`-family into a
computable BCS game and rational threshold with strict synchronous value on
the membership side and commuting value at most the threshold on the
nonmembership side.  The discussion following the proof that the NPA
hierarchy need not attain its limit applies Kleene's recursion theorem exactly
as in the claim.  Exact comparison of a fixed NPA SDP with a rational
threshold is decidable by quantifier elimination over the reals, and NPA
convergence supplies the final equality.

Source: Marco Fanizza et al., *The NPA hierarchy does not always attain the
commuting operator value*, arXiv:2510.04943v4, Sections 4.1--4.2.
