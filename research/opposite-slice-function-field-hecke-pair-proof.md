All groups below sit inside `GL_r(R_pm)`, `R_pm=k[x_1^(+-1),x_2^(+-1),x_3,...,x_d]`,
and `P=E_pm rtimes SL_2(Z)` is the decomposition (OTG1) of the gate claim.

## (HP1)

`k[x_1^(+-1)] subset R_pm`, so `Lambda_1 <= E_pm <= P`.  An element of
`Lambda_1 intersect Gamma` has all entries in

```text
k[x_1^(+-1)] intersect k[x_1,...,x_d] = k[x_1],
```

so it lies in `SL_r(k[x_1])`.  The ring `k[x_1]` is Euclidean, and row
reduction gives `SL_r(k[x_1])=EL_r(k[x_1])=Gamma_1`.  The reverse inclusion
`Gamma_1 <= Lambda_1 intersect Gamma` is immediate.

## (HP2)

By GKP Proposition 2.15(3),(2) (`gkp-sofic-action-toolkit`), soficity of
`P action P/Gamma` passes to `Lambda_1` and then to the `Lambda_1`-orbit of
`o=Gamma`.  That orbit is `Lambda_1/Stab(o)=Lambda_1/(Lambda_1 intersect Gamma)`,
which is `Lambda_1/Gamma_1` by (HP1).  This proves the first bullet of item 4.

Building picture (not used by items 3--6).  Put `t=x_1`, `G_t=SL_r(k((t)))`,
`L_0=k[[t]]^r` and `K=Stab_(G_t)(L_0)=SL_r(k[[t]])`.  The ring `k[t,1/t]` is
Euclidean: write a nonzero element as a unit times a polynomial with nonzero
constant term, and use the degree of that polynomial.  Hence
`Lambda_1=SL_r(k[t,1/t])` and

```text
Lambda_1 intersect K = SL_r(k[t,1/t] intersect k[[t]]) = SL_r(k[t]) = Gamma_1.
```

`G_t` is generated by the elementary matrices over the field `k((t))`, and
Laurent polynomials are dense in `k((t))`, so `Lambda_1` is dense in `G_t`.
Since `K` is open, every coset `gK` meets `Lambda_1`.  So `Lambda_1 L_0=G_t L_0`,
the set of type-zero vertices, and `Lambda_1/Gamma_1 ~= G_t/K` as
`Lambda_1`-sets.

Incompressibility.  `Gamma_1=Lambda_1 intersect K` is dense in the open set
`K`.  Suppose `l Gamma_1 l^(-1) <= Gamma_1` for some `l in Lambda_1`.  Taking
closures gives `l K l^(-1) <= K`.  Both are compact open with the same Haar
measure, since `G_t` is unimodular, so `l K l^(-1)=K`.  Hence
`l Gamma_1 l^(-1)=Lambda_1 intersect l K l^(-1)=Gamma_1`.  The compression
semigroup of `Gamma_1` in `Lambda_1` is therefore its normalizer, so no
Kun--Thom compression step acts inside the pair.

## (HP3)

Take a reduced alternating word `w=l_1 l_2 ... l_n` in `D_1`, with `n>=1`
and letters taken alternately from the two copies of `Lambda_1`, each outside
`Gamma_1`.  Its image in `D` is an alternating word whose letters lie in the
corresponding copies of `P`.  A letter lying in `Gamma` would lie in
`Lambda_1 intersect Gamma=Gamma_1` by (HP1), so every letter lies outside
`Gamma`.  By the normal form theorem for amalgamated free products (Serre,
*Trees*, I.1.2), the image is nontrivial.  The length-zero elements form
`Gamma_1`, which maps injectively into `Gamma`.  So (HP3) is injective.

## (HP4)

For a unit `u` and `i!=j`, let `delta_ij(u)` be the diagonal matrix with `u`
in slot `i`, `u^(-1)` in slot `j`, and `1` elsewhere.  In the `(i,j)` block,
the Whitehead identity

```text
diag(u,u^(-1)) = e_12(u)e_21(-u^(-1))e_12(u) . e_12(-1)e_21(1)e_12(-1)
```

shows `delta_ij(u)` is elementary.  Moreover

```text
delta_ij(u) e_ij(f) delta_ij(u)^(-1) = e_ij(u^2 f).                  (TR1)
```

Let `q:Lambda_1->F` be a homomorphism to a finite group, `u=x_1`, and let `m` be
the order of `q(delta_ij(x_1))`.  For `f in k[x_1^(+-1)]` choose `n` with
`x_1^(2mn) f in k[x_1]`.  By (TR1),

```text
q(e_ij(f)) = q(delta_ij(x_1)^(mn) e_ij(f) delta_ij(x_1)^(-mn))
           = q(e_ij(x_1^(2mn) f)) in q(Gamma_1).
```

The elementary matrices generate `Lambda_1`, so `q(Lambda_1)=q(Gamma_1)`.
For `q':E_pm->F`, use `u=x_1 x_2` and choose `n` so that `(x_1x_2)^(2mn) f`
has no negative exponent.  The same computation gives `q'(E_pm)=q'(Gamma)`.

`Gamma_1` and `Gamma` are Kazhdan by
`elementary-groups-over-fg-rings-have-property-t` (`r>=3`).  They are proper
subgroups, since `e_12(x_1^(-1))` lies in neither.  The codense Kazhdan theorem
now applies to `(Gamma_1<Lambda_1)` and `(Gamma<E_pm)`.  Restricting an orbit
atlas of the slice as under (HP2) gives an atlas of each smaller action with
the same error.  By that theorem it is not correctable to exact finite actions
at vanishing Hamming cost.

## Item 6

Let `S` be a left Ore monoid of positive monomial compressors.  As in the
commuting and Heisenberg telescope proofs, `s^(-1) Gamma s=EL_r(k[s^(-1)N^d])`.
If `w=us` with `u in S`, then `u N^d subset N^d`, and so

```text
s^(-1) N^d subset s^(-1)u^(-1) N^d = w^(-1) N^d.
```

Common left multiples make these cones a directed family.  Their union `M_S`
is a monoid, and `N_S=EL_r(k[M_S])`: each elementary matrix has finite support
and lies in one level, and the levels are nested.  Each `s^(-1)N^d` is the
image of `N^d` under an invertible linear map, so it contains no pair
`v,-v` with `v!=0`.  If both were in `M_S`, directedness would put them in one
level.  So `M_S intersect (-M_S)={0}`.

Order `Z^d` totally and compatibly with addition.  In `k[Z^d]`, leading terms
and trailing terms both multiply, so a unit has one term.  So a unit of
`k[M_S]` is `c x^u` with `x^(-u)` also in `k[M_S]`, that is,
`u in M_S intersect (-M_S)={0}`.  Finally,
`e_ij(x^(-v)) in EL_r(k[M_S])` would put `x^(-v)` in `k[M_S]`, that is,
`-v in M_S`.

For the opposite slice, the kernel ring is `R_pm`, with units `k^x x x^(Z^2)`.
For `0!=v in N^2 x {0}`, replace `x_1` by `x^v` throughout.  Then
`k[x^(+-v)] intersect k[N^d]=k[x^v]` is again a one-variable polynomial ring,
and (HP1)--(HP4) hold verbatim for `EL_r(k[x^(+-v)]) > EL_r(k[x^v])`.
