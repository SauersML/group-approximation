If `|S| ≤ 4`, then `A_Γ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture. Its Salvetti complex is then a
finite `K(A_Γ, 1)`, so `A_Γ` is of type F, and in particular of type `F_∞`.

So a counterexample to Zaremsky Problem 1.5 needs at least 5 generators, and a counterexample
to the K(π,1) conjecture has rank at least 5.

Proof in `artin-groups-of-rank-at-most-four-satisfy-k-pi-1-proof`. It depends on the
unreviewed preprint arXiv:2509.06914.
