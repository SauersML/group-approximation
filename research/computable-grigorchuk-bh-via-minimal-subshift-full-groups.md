Matte Bon (arXiv:1408.0762; the abstract is the only part read) embeds `G_ω` in `[[T]]'` for a
minimal subshift `X_ω`. If `X_ω` has decidable language for computable `ω` (not checked), then
`decidable-minimal-subshift-full-groups-satisfy-boone-higman` puts `G_ω` in a finitely presented
simple group. Both the decidability check and that claim are open.
