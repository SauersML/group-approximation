**REFUTED.**  Exact regular-quotient Kazhdan blocks have an orthogonal cyclic
right module of dimension one over the edge algebra and full physical support,
while its projection in `L2(M_d)` has normalized rank tending to zero.  A
positive adjoint carrier requires left-action or strict-compressor information
which right-module dimension alone does not contain.
