**OPEN.** Let `U` be the fixed finitely presented torsion-free universal
group used in
[[full-farrell-jones-fixed-fp-torsion-free-tester]]. Then `U` belongs to
Lueck's Full Farrell--Jones class.

By the established tester equivalence, this one assertion has exactly the
same truth value as Full Farrell--Jones for every torsion-free group. No
claim is made for torsionful groups.
