Immediate: `fp-simple-dehn-functions-realize-every-recursive-lower-bound` gives,
for each recursive `f`, a finitely presented simple group whose Dehn function is at
least `f`. That is the requested family under the adopted reading of Problem 1.16.
This is one of exactly two admissible routes into the root; the other is
`zaremsky-1-16-by-no`.
