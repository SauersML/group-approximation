Let `H₀` be a group with property (T) carrying a compression datum
(ι : Λ → H₀ with Λ Kazhdan, stable letter τ with τι(Λ)τ⁻¹ ⊆ ι(Λ),
mark c with [c, ι(Λ)] = 1) and a distinguished defect element
`d = [τcτ⁻¹, ι(λ₀)]`.  Suppose a concrete model group `M` (an
elementary matrix group over a ring) receives `H₀` with `d` mapping to
a nontrivial element, and in `M` every generator image is expressible
as an explicit word in conjugates of the image of `d`.  Then a FINITE
procedure produces a finitely presented Kazhdan group `G` whose
compression defect is all of `G`:

1. take the finitely presented Shalom cover Δ ↠ H₀ (Shalom's
   presentation theorem, proved in-repo);
2. impose on Δ the finitely many compression relators and, for each
   generator `x`, the saturation relator `x = V_x(d)`;
3. consistency: every imposed relator maps to a true identity of `M`,
   so `G` surjects onto the subgroup of `M` generated by the images —
   whence `G ≠ 1` and the image of `d` stays nontrivial;
4. (T) descends from Δ; finite presentation is by construction; every
   generator lies in ⟨⟨d⟩⟩, so the defect is ⊤ — BECAUSE THE
   SATURATION RELATORS ARE IMPOSED, not by inheritance from the model
   (CORRECTED 2026-08-21 same-day: normal generation does NOT pull
   back along the cover's surjection π : G ↠ M — from ⟨⟨πd⟩⟩ = M one
   gets only ⟨⟨d⟩⟩·ker π = G).  The model's saturation
   (`normalClosure_root_eq_top` at rank ≥ 5 with `q ≠ 0`) plays two
   other roles: it supplies the defect words V_x in the first place,
   and it certifies the consistency of step 3 on the model side.

Feeding `G` to the established normal-Kazhdan criterion at `K = ⊤`
gives `Res_MF(G) = G`, hereditarily.  The scheme replaces BOTH
published inputs of the Hilbert-hotel note: the note's f.p. logic
needed St_N(R) finitely presented FIRST (a quotient of a non-f.p.
group by finitely many relators need not be f.p. — Krstic--McCool was
genuinely load-bearing as written and is unverified at source), and
the naive direct-presentation bypass fails because (T) descends along
the wrong arrow; the Shalom cover fixes both at once, and the rank
drops from 20 to 16 because the in-repo (T) assets cover every rank
≥ 5 (the 20 existed only for the removed Ershov--Jaikin citation).

Torsion caution: consistency-via-`M` surjects `G` onto a matrix group,
which contains torsion over any ring, so this scheme as stated serves
the torsion-full track; a torsion-free transplant would need a
torsion-free model with a full-idempotent-like element — the wall
[[defect-forces-proper-self-embedding]] maps.
