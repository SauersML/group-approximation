Source: Vadim Alekseev and Andreas Thom, *Centralizers of sofic
approximations of Kazhdan groups*, arXiv:2608.05362v1, Theorem A.

Theorem A states exactly that a Kazhdan group admitting a sofic embedding
whose centralizer acts ergodically on the associated Loeb probability space
is locally embeddable into finite groups. It adds explicitly that finite
presentation upgrades this conclusion to residual finiteness.

The paper's technical input is Theorem 3.1: after inessential changes, the
centralizer of a sofic approximation of a Kazhdan group is a metric
ultraproduct of finite permutation groups. Proposition 2.9 turns that
finite-level centralizer structure, together with ergodicity, into LEF
approximations. The final residual-finiteness clause uses the standard fact
that finitely presented LEF groups are residually finite.

The contrapositive in the target is purely logical and adds no hypothesis.

Status: arXiv preprint, v1, submitted 2026-08-05. The full theorem statement
and its scope were checked 2026-08-31.
