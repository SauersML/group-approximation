Every minimal action of `Z` on the Cantor set has dynamical
comparison: if `μ(U) < μ(V)` for every invariant probability measure,
then `U` is equivariantly subordinate to `V` by clopen translation
pieces.  The founding cell of STW Problem XXXIII.
