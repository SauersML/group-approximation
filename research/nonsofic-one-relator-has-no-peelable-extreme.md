ESTABLISHED. Let `G = <a,t | w>` be torsion-free and not sofic. For every free
basis `(a,t)` of `F_2` in which `t` has exponent sum zero in `w`, both extreme
letters of the Magnus rewriting `W_0(a_m,...,a_n)` are unpeelable. Each forms
at least three syllables, or exactly two syllables of different absolute
exponent.

This is the contrapositive of [[peelable-extreme-one-relator-groups-are-sofic]].
It combines with the earlier constraints recorded at
[[nonsofic-one-relator-group]]: primitivity rank two, a nontrivial Linton
radical, and nonsoficity confined to the derived core of that radical.
