title: Spatial products of irreducible pure-factor representations contain no compacts
---

Let `A` and `B` be pure C-star algebras, and let

```text
pi_A:A -> B(H_A),       pi_B:B -> B(H_B)
```

be irreducible representations on infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces.  Then

```text
(pi_A(A) tensor_min pi_B(B)) intersect K(H_A tensor H_B) = {0}.
```

Consequently, the compact-representation counterexample mechanism for
Problem XCV must use a genuinely entangled representation of the minimal
tensor product, not a spatial product of irreducible factor representations.
