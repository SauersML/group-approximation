Uniformly enumerate finite van Kampen certificates for the compiled mark.
At the Kleene fixed index, halting would produce a proof of triviality while
the HALT semantic clause says the mark is nontrivial; hence the machine does
not halt, and completeness of enumeration still proves the mark nontrivial.
NONHALT radical membership then rules out a tracial matrix-ultraproduct
embedding.  Effective Higman is used only afterward; subgroup inheritance of
hyperlinearity transfers non-hyperlinearity to the finitely presented host.

No modulus is hidden here.  Negation of every finite-window collapse gives a
sequence whose first `n` relators have defect `1/n` while the mark stays
separated; its metric ultraproduct detects the mark.  Conversely every
ultraproduct homomorphism has representatives satisfying each fixed window.

The Fanizza-to-group gap is categorical.  In
`A=C^R`, `R={(1,1),(1,-1),(-1,1)}`, the coordinate signs `x,y` satisfy the
nonlinear relation `(1-x)(1-y)=0`, but their multiplicative subgroup is
exactly `C_2 times C_2`.  The character `x=y=-1` is therefore an exact
one-dimensional representation of every multiplicative word consequence,
yet cannot extend to `A`, since it sends the nonlinear relation to `4=0`.
Thus BCS soundness does not imply group-ultraproduct collapse without an
additional decoder.

For a word mark, tensor powers preserve canonical delta trace conditions and
can reduce qualitative profile collapse to a finite trace window.  This does
not apply automatically to additive group-algebra marks, whose evaluation
does not tensor multiplicatively.
