**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`gleason-yamabe-and-van-dantzig-structure-theorems-citation`).

1. **Gleason–Yamabe.** Let `G` be a locally compact group. For every open
   neighbourhood `U` of the identity there are an open subgroup `G'` of `G` and a
   compact normal subgroup `K` of `G'` with `K ⊆ U`, such that `G'/K` is
   isomorphic to a Lie group.
2. **Connected case.** If `G` is connected, its only open subgroup is `G`
   itself. So in item 1 one may take `G' = G`: a connected locally compact group
   has a compact normal subgroup `K` with `G/K` a Lie group, which is connected.
3. **van Dantzig.** Every totally disconnected locally compact group contains a
   compact open subgroup.

**Trust surface.** Items 1 and 3 are quoted verbatim in the citation route. Item 2
combines the quoted sentence on open subgroups of connected groups with item 1.
The group `G/K` in item 2 is connected as a continuous image of `G`.
