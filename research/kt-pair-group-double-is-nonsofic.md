**Literature input (arXiv:2608.06222 version 3, 20 Aug 2026, read
2026-08-22; arXiv comment "v2+v3 updated Theorem A").**  Kun--Thom,
*Nonsofic wreath products of residually finite groups*, Theorem A in its
current form:

> **Theorem A.**  Let `Gamma` be an infranormal but not normal subgroup of
> `G`, and suppose that both `Gamma` and `G` have Kazhdan's property (T).
> Then `(Z/2Z) wr_(G/Gamma) G` **and the group double `G *_Gamma G`** are
> not sofic.

The hypotheses are unchanged from version 1 (recorded in
`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath`; Theorems B, C, E and Corollary D are verbatim
identical across versions), so every consumer of that node is unaffected;
the new content is the second conclusion.  With the Theorem E pair
(`Gamma = EL_r(F_q[x_1..x_d])`, `G = EL_r(F_q[x^(+-1)]) rtimes SL_d(Z)`,
`r, d >= 3`) this gives an explicit nonsofic amalgamated free product of
two residually finite Kazhdan groups over a common (T) subgroup -- a
second nonsofic family from the same mechanism.  Trust surface: an
unrefereed preprint, like the wreath statement; the priority note of
`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath` applies verbatim.
