All identities are checked by `siblings.py` in the faithful normal form
`(word in F(u,v,w), b-exponent)` of `fpbs-mal-overgroup-is-free-by-cyclic`.

1. **Generators.** `M = <b, t> = <b, t b^-2>`. Also `psi_1(b) = psi_2(b) = 1`
   and `psi_1(t) = psi_2(t b^-2) = 0`. So by Reidemeister–Schreier with
   transversal `{b^i}`, `N_1` is generated by the `y_i` and `N_2` by the `z_i`.
2. **Identities (checked).**
   - (N1) `y_1 = y_-1 y_-2^-1 y_-1 y_-2 y_-1^-1`, and
     `y_-2 g y_-2^-1 = y_-1^-1` for `g = y_-1^-1 y_1^-1 y_-1 ≠ 1`.
   - (N2) `z_-1 = z_0 z_1 z_2^-2 z_0`, and
     `z_3^2 = z_1 z_0^-1 z_1 z_2 ≠ 1`.

   Conjugation by `b^k` shifts every index by `k`, and so do the identities.
3. **Stages.**
   - Shifting (N1) gives `y_{i+2} ∈ <y_{i-1}, y_i>` for all `i`. Hence
     `P_j ⊂ P_{j+1} = <P_j, y_{-j-2}>`, and by induction on `i` every
     `y_i` with `i ≥ -j-1` lies in `P_j`. So `∪ P_j = N_1`.
   - Shifting (N2) gives `z_{i-1} ∈ <z_i, z_{i+1}, z_{i+2}>`. Hence
     `Q_j ⊂ Q_{j+1} = <Q_j, z_{j+3}>`, and `∪ Q_j = N_2`.
   - The commensurated elements are `g_j = b^-j g b^j`, with
     `x_j g_j x_j^-1 = y_{-j-1}^-1 ∈ P_j`, and `g'_j = z_{j+3}^2 ∈ Q_j`.
4. **One-element lemma.** Let `H ≤ L = <H, x>` act freely, and let
   `1 ≠ g ∈ H` with `x g x^-1 ∈ H`. Let `A` meet almost every `<g>`-orbit.
   Then `R_H ∨ x|_A = R_L`. (Compare Gaboriau, Invent. Math. 139 (2000),
   proof of Critère VI.24.)

   Proof. For a.e. `w` pick `n` with `g^n w ∈ A`. Then
   `x w = (x g^-n x^-1) · x · (g^n w)`. This is an `R_H`-step, then an
   `x|_A`-step, then an `R_H`-step, since `x g^-n x^-1 ∈ H`. So `(w, x w)`
   lies in the join. The reverse inclusion is clear.
5. **Small sections.** `M = F_3 ⋊ Z` is torsion-free, so `g` has infinite
   order, and a free action restricts to a free, hence aperiodic, `Z`-action.
   An aperiodic Borel `Z`-action has a decreasing sequence of Borel complete
   sections with empty intersection (Kechris–Miller, *Topics in orbit
   equivalence*, LNM 1852, Lemma 6.7). So complete sections of measure `< ε`
   exist. This proves statement 3.
6. **Cost.** Fix `ε > 0`. Take the graphing
   `{y_-1, y_0, y_1} ∪ {x_j|_{A_j} : j ≥ 0}`, with `A_j` a complete section
   for `<g_j>` of measure `< ε 2^{-j-1}`. By step 4 and induction, its
   relation contains `R_{P_j}` for every `j`, hence equals `R_{N_1}`. Its
   cost is `< 3 + ε`. The same works for `N_2` with `{z_0, z_1, z_2}`. This
   proves statement 4.
