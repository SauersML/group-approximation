For a countably infinite group `G`, the following are equivalent:

1. some free ergodic p.m.p. action of `G` has positive Rokhlin entropy (POS);
2. some free ergodic p.m.p. action of `G` has Rokhlin entropy in `(0, infinity)`;
3. some free ergodic p.m.p. action of `G` has infinite Rokhlin entropy;
4. `h^Rok_sup(G) > 0`.

Source: Seward, arXiv:1501.03367v4, Corollary 7.7, together with the first paragraph of the proof of Theorem 7.6. There, an action of positive Rokhlin entropy is written as an inverse limit of free factors generated by finite partitions. The inverse-limit formula, Theorem 7.3, then shows that one of those factors has positive, finite Rokhlin entropy.

Seward records as open whether `h^Rok_sup(G) > 0` implies `h^Rok_sup(G) = infinity` for a fixed group. `rokhlin-supremum-dichotomy-with-centralized-self-copies` settles this for groups that contain their own square and have finite subgroups of unbounded order.
