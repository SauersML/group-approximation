title: Equivariant transitions coarsen to whole-map PVMs
---

On a free translation orbit, pull every source-conditioned response PVM to
one base block; under exact equivariance they coincide, and orbit-summing
gives a PVM indexed by whole equivariant response maps. With mean total
squared equivariance error `ε`, orbit averaging plus cardinality-free POVM
rounding gives an exact whole-map PVM within `40ε`, independently of orbit
size, response alphabet, and matrix dimension.
