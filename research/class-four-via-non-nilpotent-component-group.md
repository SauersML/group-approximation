title: A non-nilpotent unitary component group has a nonzero fourfold commutator
target: simple-cstar-unitary-components-class-four

Let G = U(A)/U_0(A). If G is not nilpotent then gamma_4(G) != 1. The
subgroup gamma_4(G) is generated by the simple commutators
[x_3, [x_2, [x_1, y]]] with x_i, y in G. This is the standard fact that
gamma_{c+1} is generated by simple commutators of weight c+1 in any
generating set. So some such commutator is nontrivial. Lifting x_i and y to
unitaries u_i and v gives [u_3, [u_2, [u_1, v]]] not in U_0(A).
