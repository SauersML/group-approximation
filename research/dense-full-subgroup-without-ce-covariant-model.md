**OPEN.** Prerequisite Q2 of `nonhyperlinear-via-full-subgroup-covariant-models`.

**Statement.** There exist
- an ergodic pmp countable equivalence relation `R` on a standard nonatomic probability space `(X, mu)` (intended:
  the property-(T) relation of `non-ce-property-t-equivalence-relation-exists`),
- a countable Boolean subalgebra `B` of the measure algebra that generates the `sigma`-algebra, and
- a countable subgroup `Gamma_0 <= [R]`,

such that:
1. `Gamma_0` is dense in `[R]` for `d_u(phi, psi) = mu(phi != psi)`;
2. for `g in Gamma_0`, `g B = B` and `Fix g in B`;
3. (`B`-fullness) if `A_1, ..., A_n in B` partition `X`, `g_1, ..., g_n in Gamma_0`, and `g_1 A_1, ..., g_n A_n`
   partition `X`, then the automorphism equal to `g_i` on `A_i` lies in `Gamma_0`;
4. `(L^infinity(X), Gamma_0)` has no covariant model, in the sense of
   `support-covariant-models-carry-fixed-point-trace`, in any Connes-embeddable tracial von Neumann algebra.

**Items 1--3 are free.** For any ergodic `R`, start from a countable dense subgroup `Gamma^0` and a countable
generating algebra `B^0`. Let:
- `B^(k+1)` be the Boolean algebra generated by `B^k`, `Gamma^k B^k` and `{Fix g : g in Gamma^k}`;
- `Gamma^(k+1)` be the group generated by `Gamma^k` and all finite `B^k`-pastings of elements of `Gamma^k`. Each
  pasting is in `[R]`, because its graph lies in `R` and it preserves `mu`.

Each stage is countable, and `B = ∪ B^k`, `Gamma_0 = ∪ Gamma^k` satisfy 1--3. The whole content is item 4.

**Necessary conditions.**
- `L(R)` is not CE, because the canonical model is a covariant model.
- `L^infinity(X) ⋊ Gamma_0` is not CE.
- Every trace `f u_g -> integral_(S_g) f dmu` coming from an equivariant field of subgroups has non-CE GNS algebra.

## Attempts

- **Density alone is not enough (verified).** For the relation `R_H` of cost at most 2 and the dense `F_3 <= [R_H]`
  of `nonce-relation-full-groups-contain-dense-free-subgroups`, the crossed product `L^infinity(X) ⋊ F_3` is CE, by
  the Brown--Dykema--Jung argument recorded under Attempts in `hyperlinear-group-actions-have-ce-covariant-models`.
  So "a dense subgroup of the full group of a non-CE relation has no CE covariant model" is false, and a proof of
  item 4 must use items 2--3.
- **Intended mechanism and where it breaks (verified).** The plan was: (a) every covariant model of a `B`-full group
  satisfies the support axiom; (b) `support-covariant-models-carry-fixed-point-trace` then puts `L(R)` inside the
  model. Step (a) is false. The crossed product `L^infinity(X) ⋊ Gamma_0` is always a covariant model, and there
  `u_g e(1_(Fix g))` has Fourier coefficient `1_(Fix g)` at `g != e`, so the support axiom fails whenever
  `mu(Fix g) > 0`. Such `g` exist: an element `d_u`-close to an involution with fixed set of measure `1/2` fixes a
  set of measure close to `1/2`. The naive correction `pi'(g) = pi(g) e(1_(X \ Fix g)) + e(1_(Fix g))` is unitary
  and covariant and satisfies the support axiom, but in the crossed product `pi'(g) pi'(h)` has the term
  `u_g e(1_(Fix h \ Fix g))`. This term is nonzero at the group element `g` whenever
  `mu(Fix h \ Fix g) > 0` and `h != e`, while `pi'(gh)` is supported on `{gh, e}`. So `pi'` is not multiplicative.
- **What `B`-fullness does give (verified).** Take `A in B` with `A ⊆ Fix g`. Then:
  - `g` commutes with every `h in Gamma_0` supported in `A`, and `f o g^-1 = f` for `f` supported in `A`;
  - `pi(g)` commutes with `e_A`, and `w_g = pi(g) e_A` is a unitary of `e_A M e_A` in the relative commutant of
    `N_A = W*(e(L^infinity(A)), pi(h) e_A : h in Gamma_0, supp h ⊆ A)`;
  - `w_g w_h = w_(gh)` on the pointwise stabilizer `St(A)`, because `pi(h) e_A = e_A pi(h)`.

  The support axiom on `A` is `w = e_A`. It would follow from:
  - (i) `N_A' ∩ e_A M e_A = C e_A`, and
  - (ii) `St(A)` has no nontrivial characters.

  (ii) is plausible from cut-and-paste commutator arguments but is not proved here. (i) fails in the crossed
  product, where `u_g e_A` lies in the relative commutant. So the transplant needs a CE-preserving passage from an
  arbitrary covariant model to one with irreducible local algebras `N_A`. No such passage is known, and this is
  exactly where the translation from full groups to group factors breaks.
