The global augmentation theorem provides one exact elementary-abelian action
and its finite-support spectral PVM with a family-size-independent modulus.
Restricting to any finite virtual table gives the same-Hilbert additive
rounding demanded by the same-basis interface.  The separate actor-word
energy theorem may then act on that single PVM.
