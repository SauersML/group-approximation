**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** If a countable group `G` contains an infinite amenable
normal subgroup, then `β_n^(2)(G) = 0` for every `n >= 0`.

Originally Cheeger--Gromov (Topology 25, 1986). Route: `l2-betti-infinite-normal-amenable-citation`.
