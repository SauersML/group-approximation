Decode the transported multiplicity support inside the scalable product
socle.  The normalizer relations reduce every rectangular enlargement to
the two alternatives `(SND2)`.  Transport the fixed standard-polynomial
evaluation through the same closed compressor cycle.  The full `T^k` branch
contains the transverse target factor and has the nonzero PI fingerprint,
whereas the source-confined branch has zero evaluation; hence the full branch
pays a fixed normalized-HS defect.  On positive low-defect mass only the
`K^k` branch remains, which is precisely same multiplicity reservoir/no
rectangular enlargement.

No fixed finite normalizer is used: the socle coordinate count is tied to
the moving reservoir, and the PI detector is what prevents the scalable
packet from merely renaming the escaped target.
