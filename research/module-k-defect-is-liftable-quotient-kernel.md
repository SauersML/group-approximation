Use X=V̂, Z=W^⊥, C=C(X)⋊_rG, B=C(Z)⋊_rG and J=C_0(X\Z)⋊_rG.
For p:C→D=C/J and q̄:D→B with q=q̄p, and either j=0,1,

```text
ker q_* / im(K_j(J)→K_j(C)) ≅ im p_* ∩ ker q̄_* ⊆ K_j(D).
```

The isomorphism is induced by p_*. Liftability is equivalently
∂z=0 for the six-term boundary K_j(D)→K_(j−1)(J).
A nonzero algebra kernel, a nonzero K_j(E) class, or a nonzero
ker q̄_* class without liftability is insufficient. This identifies
the location of a defect; it does not assert one exists.
See Proposition 4.2 of the
[proof document](artifacts/Cairn_Baum_Connes_General_Attempt.md#proposition-42-the-missing-class-is-liftable-from-c).
