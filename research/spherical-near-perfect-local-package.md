title: The spherical cloud supplies the local degree, codegree, and shared-target package needed for near-perfect packing
---

For every fixed finite regular-character unitary table with normalized-HS
multiplication defect tending to zero, after harmless dimension amplification
and a sufficiently large iid uniform spherical cloud, the radius-one candidate
system has simultaneously:

1. one-colour in/out degrees `(1+o(1))Np_d`;
2. negligible fixed-order intersections of distinct word-colour candidate
   neighborhoods outside `o(N)` roots;
3. uniform four-slot relation degrees and negligible repeated-slot codegrees;
   and
4. for all but `o(N)` roots, every good first-step edge of every tested
   relation has a literal common-target reservoir of size at least
   `(1-o(1))Nq_d`, with `q_d` the radius-`1/4` cap measure.

No divisibility assertion is included.  This is the natural probabilistic
input for a near-perfect coupled Schreier packing theorem rather than a
perfect-design/absorption theorem.
