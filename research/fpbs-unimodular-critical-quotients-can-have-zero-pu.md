There exists a unimodular random mass-marked multigraph, obtained by
contracting finite critical Bernoulli clusters of a bounded-degree
nonamenable unimodular random graph, with finite mean mass and degree,
uniform positive normalized expansion, and p_c=p_u=0 almost surely.
Section 5 deduces this from Angel--Hutchcroft's collapsed-threshold example
and Aldous--Lyons critical finiteness, using the explicit Palm contraction.
No representation as a critical quotient of a fixed Cayley graph is
established, and no second degree-moment claim is made for it. Thus the
listed generic quotient properties cannot alone resolve the Cayley
conjecture.
