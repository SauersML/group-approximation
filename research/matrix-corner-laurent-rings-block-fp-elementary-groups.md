## Statement

**(A) Ring form.** Let `S` be a unital ring with a unital map `Q -> S` and a grading `S = ⊕_(n ∈ Z) S_n`
(`S_i S_j ⊆ S_(i+j)`), and suppose some unit `v` of `S` lies in `S_1`. Suppose either
- (a) `S_0` is commutative, or
- (b) `S_0` admits a unital ring homomorphism into a finite factor.

Let `k >= 1` and let `R` be any ring isomorphic to `M_k(S)`. Then for every `N >= 3`, `E_N(R)` is not
finitely presented, and neither is `E_N(R)/C` for any finite normal subgroup `C`.

**(B) Crossed-product form.** Let a group `Γ` act on a compact space `X`, and let
`R ⊆ C(X, Q) ⋊_alg Γ = ⊕_(g ∈ Γ) C(X, Q) u_g` be a unital subring containing the constants `Q`
such that:
1. `R` is `Γ`-graded: `R = ⊕_g (R ∩ C(X, Q) u_g)`. This holds whenever `R` is generated by elements
   of the form `b u_g`.
2. There is a clopen `C ⊆ X` whose translates `{gC : g ∈ Γ}` form a finite partition of `X`, with
   `χ_C ∈ R`. Let `Γ' = {g : gC = C}`, of finite index `k`.
3. `u_g, u_g^(-1) ∈ R` for all `g ∈ Γ`.
4. Some homomorphism `φ : Γ' -> Z` is onto.
5. `R` admits a unital ring homomorphism `ρ` into a finite factor `M` with `ρ(χ_C) != 0`. For example,
   `Γ` countable acting freely and ergodically, preserving an atomless probability measure `μ`, with
   `M = L^∞(X, μ) ⋊ Γ`.

Then for every `N >= 3`, `E_N(R)` is not finitely presented, nor is `E_N(R)/C` for `C` finite normal.

## Consequences

- **Z-actions.** `Γ = Z`, `C = X`, `φ = id` gives the tracial harmonic ring, every odometer, Toeplitz
  and Bratteli–Vershik crossed product, and all their harmonic enlargements.
- **Virtually indicable groups over their odometers.** Let `Γ` act with an equivariant map onto a
  `Γ`-odometer `lim Γ/Γ_n`, where `Γ_n` is a chain of finite-index subgroups. Suppose some
  finite-index `Γ' ⊇ Γ_n` has infinite abelianization. Taking `C` to be the preimage of the closure of
  `Γ'` makes (B) apply, so `R` fails.
  - This covers free groups, surface groups, RAAGs and hyperbolic 3-manifold groups (virtually
    indicable by Agol), for any cofinal chain.
  - So spark S1 of `research/artifacts/gq-deep-adelic-1-k-budget.md`, perfect acting groups, does
    not escape the Laurent trap merely by `Γ` being perfect. It needs every finite-index subgroup
    that appears in the odometer chain to have finite abelianization, e.g. `SL_n(Z)`, `n >= 3`, with a
    congruence chain.
