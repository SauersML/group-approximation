For every prime `p`, let `A_p` be Abels' finitely presented solvable
subgroup of `GL_4(Q)` over `Z[1/p]`. Then the complex group algebra

```text
C[A_p]
```

is weakly stable but is not stable in the normalized rank metric.

Thus weak rank stability is strictly weaker than full rank stability even
among group algebras of finitely presented torsion-free amenable linear
groups.
