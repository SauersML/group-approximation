Dead: the attempt converts linear information about cycles into measurable deletion of redundant edges along an invariant random spanning forest, keeping connectivity for free and matching the established lower bound 1 + beta_1. It assumes the free uniform spanning forest is connected, which is false on some Cayley graphs of virtually free groups (fpbs-fusf-not-connected-on-some-cayley-graphs), and it also owes a realization of the forest inside the given action. The identity cost = 1 + beta_1 for every free action remains a stronger open target than Fixed Price.
