`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath` records Theorem E: the base `G` is residually
finite. `residually-finite-groups-are-surjunctive` makes `G` surjunctive, and
`doubles-of-surjunctive-groups-are-surjunctive` makes `G *_Gamma G`
surjunctive for the infranormal subgroup `Gamma`.
