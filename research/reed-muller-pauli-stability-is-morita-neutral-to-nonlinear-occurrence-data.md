**EXACT MORITA AND AFFINE FENCE.**

Let \(\mathsf P_k\) be the binary Pauli group with central involution \(J\),
and put

\[
 p_-=\frac{1-J}{2},\qquad d=2^k.
\]

For every unital \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\),

\[
 p_-\big(C^*(\mathsf P_k)\otimes_{\max}A\big)p_-
 \;\cong\; M_d({\mathbf C})\otimes_{\max}A.             \tag{RPM1}
\]

Under this isomorphism the Pauli coordinates are the canonical matrices
\(\sigma^X(a),\sigma^Z(b)\) tensored with \(1_A\). Every tracial state on
the right side is uniquely

\[
 {\rm tr}_d\otimes\tau_A,                               \tag{RPM2}
\]

and it is Connes embeddable if and only if \(\tau_A\) is. Moreover, the
right side has a unital finite-dimensional representation if and only if
\(A\) does.

Consequently, the Reed--Muller and Pauli rows of Chapman--Vidick--Yuen
cannot by themselves authenticate a nonlinear occurrence projection.
Indeed, for every tracial \(A\) and every projection \(e\in A\), the
coordinate model

\[
 \sigma^W(a)\otimes1_A,\qquad p_{\rm occ}=1\otimes e     \tag{RPM3}
\]

has **zero** code/Pauli defect, independently of \(e\). Thus no estimate
whose right side consists only of those coordinate defects can control a
nonconstant occurrence functional of \(p_{\rm occ}\), even at exact
completeness.

There are only two reducing placements:

1. If the occurrence data remain in the multiplicity algebra
   \(1\otimes A\), the stable coordinate test does not see them and the
   original source algebra remains uncompiled.
2. If they are forced into \(M_d\otimes1\), every perfect decoded source
   configuration already has a finite-dimensional model.

The binary affine escape is also closed. Ordinary word equations among a
commuting binary coordinate table have the form

\[
 x^{a_s}=(-1)^{b_s}.
\]

Their allowed joint characters are either empty or an affine subspace of
\(\mathbb F_2^k\), hence have power-of-two cardinality. In particular they
cannot retain exactly three qutrit atoms: an affine set containing
\(u,v,w\) also contains \(u+v+w\). A pointed trace may omit the fourth
character, but the coordinate presentation neither forces that trace face
nor prevents a finite direct sum supported on the same three characters.

This result uses the exact scope of Chapman--Vidick--Yuen,
*Efficiently Stable Presentations from Error-Correcting Codes*,
arXiv:2311.04681v2 (Discrete Analysis 2026): Theorem 4.1 stabilizes a
presentation of \(\mathbb Z_2^k\), and Theorem 5.10 rounds the braided test
to Pauli coordinates in a stabilized matrix factor. It does not round an
independent nonlinear occurrence algebra.

Therefore importing the 2026 Reed--Muller/Pauli stability module does not
authenticate the three hidden-sign/qutrit selector or the uniformly
nonaffine Lin atom. A surviving compiler must add a mixed, nonreducing
occurrence-conditioned return relation outside the code theorem and prove
that this new relation survives the rounding. The nonhyperlinear group
remains open.
