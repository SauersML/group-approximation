Let `Γ` be finitely generated amenable and `q` a prime power.

**1. Double.** Put `Δ = (Γ × Γ) ⋊ C_2` as in the required embedding claim. Then `Δ` is finitely
generated, amenable (finite direct products and index-two extensions preserve amenability), infinite
if `Γ` is, and `Γ` embeds in `[Δ, Δ]` with each image element a single commutator. If `Γ` is finite,
replace it by `Γ × Z`, which changes nothing below.

**2. A free minimal subshift.** By Gao--Jackson--Seward every countably infinite group has a free
minimal subshift `X ⊆ A^Δ` over a finite alphabet: minimal subflows generated by minimal hyper
aperiodic points. Fix one and put `R = LC(X, F_q) ⋊ Δ`.

**3. `R` is finitely generated as a ring.** Take the letter indicators `e_a`, `a ∈ A`, and the
unitaries `u_δ` for `δ` in a finite generating set of `Δ`. Their translates
`u_δ e_a u_δ^(-1) = e_(δ · [a])` and finite products give the indicator of every cylinder over a
finite window, and those span `LC(X, F_q)`.

**4. Property (T) and infiniteness.** `EL_n(R)` is finitely generated and Kazhdan for `n >= 3`,
by the required claim on elementary groups over finitely generated rings. It is infinite because
`e_12(LC(X,F_q))` is infinite.

**5. Simplicity modulo the centre.** The transformation groupoid `X ⋊ Δ` is ample and Hausdorff with
compact infinite unit space `X`; it is minimal because `X` is a minimal system, and effective because
the action is free. Its Steinberg algebra over `F_q` is `R`. The required simplicity claim gives that
`S = EL_n(R)/Z` is simple, with `Z ⊆ F_q^x 1_n`.

**6. Linear soficity.** `Δ` is amenable and acts minimally on the Cantor space `X`, so the required
rank-model claim embeds `R` unitally in a rank ultraproduct `∏_ω M_(N_i)(F_q)/{rank-null}`; its
models truncate the orbit representation to Folner sets, and minimality gives the rank lower bound.
The required projective claim then makes `S` `F_q`-linear sofic.

**7. The embedding.** `Δ ⊆ R^x` through `δ ↦ u_δ`, so `ι(Γ) ⊆ [R^x, R^x]`. By step 4 of the
embedding route, `γ ↦ diag(1, u_(ι(γ)), 1)` is an injective homomorphism `Γ -> EL_3(R)` that stays
injective modulo the centre. So `Γ` embeds in `S`. For `n > 3` pad with ones.

**Model tests.**
- `Γ = Z`: `Δ` is the infinite dihedral-by-`Z` group `(Z × Z) ⋊ C_2`, still amenable, and the
  envelope is the `Z^2`-type family. Consistent.
- `Γ` finitely presented solvable and not residually finite: the envelope contains a non-LEF
  subgroup, so the envelope is not LEF, and the conclusion only asserts linear soficity. Consistent
  with `gap-sofic-approximations-are-local-embeddings`.
- `Γ` non-amenable: step 6 fails, since Folner sets are used. This is the boundary of the route, and
  it is exactly where `sofic-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-sofic-groups` stays open.
