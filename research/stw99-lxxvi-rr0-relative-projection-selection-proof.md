Zhang's K-stability theorem makes proper projection Stiefel spaces
weakly contractible. Real rank zero extends this to embedding a fixed
projection into a strictly larger arbitrary hereditary module.
Polar-normalized straight-line contractions verify equi-local
connectivity uniformly, and local polar normalization of bdp verifies
lower semicontinuity for moving positive targets.

Michael's finite-dimensional selection theorem therefore gives an
exact relative embedding. The compact equality locus is closed and
has a uniform spectral gap by the stable-finite Brown--Ciuperca
theorem and norm persistence of spectral gaps. The required boundary
embedding follows from all-base projection rigidity. Finally,
projection lifting and Cu continuity pass compact-left comparison
reflection from finite polyhedra to every compact metrizable base.
