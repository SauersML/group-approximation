Pierre de la Harpe, *On simplicity of reduced C-star-algebras of groups*,
[arXiv:math/0509450](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0509450), Proposition 19,
states:

1. the direct product of two C-star-simple groups is C-star-simple; and
2. an inductive limit of C-star-simple groups is C-star-simple.

Iteration gives finite direct products. An upward directed union under
subgroup inclusions is the corresponding group inductive limit. A restricted
direct sum is the directed union of its finite-coordinate direct products, so
the final specialization follows as well.
