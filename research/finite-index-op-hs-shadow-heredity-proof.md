Section 4 of the artifact gives the complete proof. Restriction yields
`S(H) <= H intersect S(G)`. For any sequence of models of `H`, the finite
coset cocycle induces unitary block-permutation models of `G`; their
operator-norm multiplicative errors are a maximum of finitely many original
errors. For `h in H`, the base-coset block is the original `V_n(h)`.
Its normalized squared HS mass is `1/[G:H]` of the induced mass. Universal
HS invisibility in `G` therefore forces invisibility in every model of `H`.
