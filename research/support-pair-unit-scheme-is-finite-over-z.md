**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G` be a group such that `k[G]` has no zero divisors
for every field `k`. Let `S, T` be finite subsets of `G`. Let `A = A_{S,T}` be
the commutative ring

    Z[u_s (s in S), v_t (t in T)] / I,

where `I` is generated by `sum_s u_s - 1` and by the coefficients of
`(sum_s u_s s)(sum_t v_t t) - 1` at every element of `ST u {1}`. So for a
commutative ring `R`, a ring map `A -> R` is a pair `(u, v)` in `R[G]` with
`supp u in S`, `supp v in T`, `uv = 1` and `eps(u) = 1`.

1. **Finiteness.** `A` is a finitely generated `Z`-module. Write
   `r = rank_Z A`.
2. **Complex units.** For every field `K` of characteristic `0` there are at
   most `r` units `u in K[G]` with `eps(u) = 1`, `supp u in S` and
   `supp u^(-1) in T`. Every coefficient of such a `u` and of `u^(-1)` is an
   algebraic integer. In positive characteristic `p` there are at most
   `dim_{F_p} A/pA` such units over any field.
3. **Mod-p bound.** `r <= dim_{F_p} A/pA` for every prime `p`.
4. **Local mod-p bound.** Let `p` be prime and let `(ubar, vbar)` be an
   `F_p`-point of `A`, with maximal ideal `m`. Every `Qbar_p`-point of `A`
   has integral coordinates, by item 2, and so reduces to an `Fbar_p`-point.
   The `Qbar_p`-points reducing to `(ubar, vbar)`, counted with multiplicity
   (the length of `A (x) Qbar_p` at the point), number at most
   `ell_p(ubar) := dim_{F_p} (A/pA)_m`.
5. **Certificates.** Let `g in S` with `g^(-1) in T`, and let `mu(g)` be the
   length of `A (x) Q` at the trivial unit `(g, g^(-1))`.
   - If `ell_p(gbar) = mu(g)`, then no unit other than `g` with coefficients
     in the ring of integers of `Qbar_p` lives on `(S,T)` and is congruent
     to `g` modulo the maximal ideal. In particular no nontrivial unit of
     `Z[G]` on `(S,T)` is congruent to `g` mod `p`.
   - If `dim_{F_p} A/pA` equals the total multiplicity of a known list of
     `Qbar`-points, that list is every unit on `(S,T)` in every
     characteristic-zero field.

   Both are finite computations over `F_p` and `Q`.
6. **Radius bounds height.** Let `G` be torsion-free and virtually free
   abelian, for example the Promislow group `P`. Every finite `S` has a finite
   `T(S)` containing `supp u^(-1)` for every unit `u` of any `K[G]` with
   `supp u in S`. So for each `S` only finitely many units of `Z[G]` are
   supported in `S`.

   The existence of `T(S)` is property (U) of Friedman--Gustavson--Pappas
   (Thm. 2.3, cited in Tabei arXiv:2608.00103v1, Section 2.2). The adjugate
   argument of the proof gives `T(S)` explicitly. What is new here is the
   height bound.

   On `P`, for each radius `r` there is a finite `H(r)` bounding the height of
   every unit of `Z[P]` supported in `B(r)`. The units supported in `B(r)` are
   the `Z`-points of `A_{B(r), T(B(r))}`, which a Groebner basis over `Q`
   computes. So Higman's conjecture for `P` is decidable within any prescribed
   radius alone.

The hypothesis holds for every torsion-free elementary amenable group, and
for `P` (Kropholler--Linnell--Moody).

*Consequence for Tabei, arXiv:2608.02982v1, Section 6.*

- **Remark 6.3, first bullet.** It says "bounding the radius r alone does not
  finitize: for a fixed support, (1) is a system of polynomial equations in
  the integer coefficients that may have infinitely many integer solutions".
  Item 6 shows every fixed support has finitely many integer solutions, and
  the radius alone does finitize.
- **Proposition 6.1.** It needs both `r` and `H`; `r` suffices.
- **Problem 6.4.** It asks to bound `r` and `H` jointly; what remains is to
  bound `r`.

*Consistency checks against known data.*

- **Gardam's coefficients.** Gardam's complex units on his support pair have
  coefficients in `Z[zeta_8]` (arXiv:2312.05240v2, Section 2: "the
  coefficients in the theorem are at least algebraic integers"). Item 2 says
  that is forced.
- **Gardam's count.** He computes the normalized system on that pair modulo
  `1000000007`: dimension `0`, 17 trivial solutions and 16 nontrivial ones
  (Section 3). Item 1 predicts dimension `0` in every characteristic.
- **The rescaling lemma.** `rational-units-rescale-to-integral-units` is the
  special case of the argument over the discrete valuation rings `Z_(p)`.
