**Import.** H. Matui, *Homology and topological full groups of étale groupoids on totally
disconnected spaces*, Proc. London Math. Soc. 104 (2012), arXiv:0909.1624, Section 3.

Conventions (Matui 2012, Section 2):
- An étale groupoid is a locally compact Hausdorff groupoid whose range map is a local
  homeomorphism.
- A space is totally disconnected when its topology is generated by clopen sets.
- `H_n(G, A)` is the homology of the complex `C_c(G^(n), A)`, with
  `δ_n = Σ_{i=0}^n (−1)^i d_{i*}` and `δ_1 = s_* − r_*` (Definition 3.1).
- For `F ⊆ G^(0)` open, `G|F = r^{-1}(F) ∩ s^{-1}(F)`. `F` is `G`-full if
  `r^{-1}(x) ∩ s^{-1}(F) ≠ ∅` for every `x ∈ G^(0)`.

1. **Transformation groupoids.** Let a discrete group `Γ` act by homeomorphisms on a
   locally compact Hausdorff space `X`, and let `G_φ = Γ × X` be the transformation
   groupoid (Definition 2.1). Then `H_n(G_φ, A) ≅ H_n(Γ; C_c(X, A))` for all `n`, where
   `Γ` acts on `C_c(X, A)` by translation. Matui states this after Remark 3.2 with "One
   can check". The check is a direct chain-level identification, recorded in the route.
2. **Reduction.** Let `G` be an étale groupoid whose unit space is σ-compact and totally
   disconnected, and let `F ⊆ G^(0)` be open and `G`-full. Then `G` and `G|F` are
   homologically similar (Theorem 3.6(2)). Homologically similar étale groupoids have
   isomorphic homology with constant coefficients (Proposition 3.5(2)). Hence
   `H_n(G|F, A) ≅ H_n(G, A)` for all `n`.
