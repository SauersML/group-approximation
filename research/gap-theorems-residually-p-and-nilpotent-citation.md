Literature import, secondary. Source: R. Grigorchuk, *Milnor's problem on the growth of groups
and its consequences*, arXiv:1111.0512, TeX `Grigorchuk10.tex` on MSI (read 2026-09-13).

- l.1893–1896, verbatim: "\begin{thm} \label{gap1} (\cite{grigorch:hilbert}) Let $G$ be a finitely
  generated residually finite-$p$ group. If $\gamma_G(n)\prec e^{\sqrt{n}}$ then $G$ has polynomial
  growth. \end{thm}"
- l.1908–1911, verbatim: "\begin{thm}\label{lubmann} Let $G$ be a residually nilpotent finitely
  generated group. If $\gamma_G(n)\prec e^{\sqrt{n}}$ then $G$ has polynomial growth. \end{thm}"
  The survey attributes the residually nilpotent version to the arguments of [grigorch:degrees]
  combined with Lemma 1.7 of Lubotzky–Mann (l.1899–1905).

The primary sources were not read; this route asserts only what the survey states.
