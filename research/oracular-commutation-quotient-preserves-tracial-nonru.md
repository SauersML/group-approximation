**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `A` be a unital synchronous game star algebra with a
tracial representation `pi_tau` and no unital star homomorphism into
`R^omega`.  Let `I_or` be the star ideal generated by commutators of the
answer projections for a finite list of question pairs, and assume those
pairs commute in `pi_tau`.  Then

```text
A_or=A/I_or                                             (OCQ1)
```

still has a tracial representation and still has no unital star
homomorphism into `R^omega`.

Indeed, `I_or subset ker(pi_tau)`, so the perfect trace factors through
`A_or`.  Conversely, a unital map `A_or->R^omega`, composed with the quotient
map `A->A_or`, would be a forbidden unital map from `A`.

Applied to `oracularizable-tracial-nonru-game-exists`, one may therefore
impose exactly the sampled-pair commutators required by
`quadratic-pair-incidence-monomial-entry-forces-commutation` without losing
the `qc=1>qa` source separation.  What remains is not commutation or joint-PVM
existence: it is a finite monomial incidence construction whose linear
intertwiner relations reproduce the pair-dependent zero pattern without
adding an unrelated `R^omega` representation.
