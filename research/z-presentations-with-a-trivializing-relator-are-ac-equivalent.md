title: Adding any trivializing relator to a deficiency-one presentation of Z gives one Andrews–Curtis class

Let ⟨x_1, …, x_n | r_1, …, r_{n−1}⟩ be a presentation of Z, and let x ∈ F(x_1, …, x_n)
map to a generator of Z. For every word w such that ⟨x_1, …, x_n | r_1, …, r_{n−1}, w⟩
presents the trivial group, that presentation is AC-equivalent to
⟨x_1, …, x_n | r_1, …, r_{n−1}, x⟩.

This is Lemma `lem:all_ac_equiv` of Shehper et al., arXiv:2408.15332v2
(`sec/stable.tex` l.205–206, quoted in the artifact). The route writes out every
AC step.
