For every separable `Z`-stable C\*-algebra `A`, the first-factor
embedding induces an isomorphism `Cu(A) ≅ Cu(A ⊗ Z)`.  No
simplicity, nuclearity, or finiteness is needed.  This is the one arc
of the nonsimple Toms--Winter cycle (STW Problem LXXV) that is fully
known; the cycle's open content is exactly the other three arcs.
