`τ_R` is an amenable trace on `R` (Connes; Brown, Theorem 3.1.7(3)).  Under the
premise it is quasidiagonal.  It is faithful, and a faithful quasidiagonal
trace makes the algebra quasidiagonal (Brown, proof of Proposition 4.1.3, via
Voiculescu's abstract characterization).  Recorded by STW in the sentence
introducing Problem X.
