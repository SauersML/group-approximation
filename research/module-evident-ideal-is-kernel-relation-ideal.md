For a countable abelian G-module V and invariant W≤V, set X=V̂,
Z=W^⊥, Γ=V⋊G and Q=(V/W)⋊G. Under C_r^*(Γ)=C(X)⋊_rG,

```text
C_0(X\Z)⋊_rG = ideal_{C_r^*(Γ)}(λ(w)−1 : w∈W).
```

This identifies the evident ideal J with J_W; it does not assert
J=ker(C_r^*(Γ)→C_r^*(Q)). See Lemma 4.1 of the
[proof document](artifacts/Cairn_Baum_Connes_General_Attempt.md#lemma-41-the-evident-ideal-is-the-relation-ideal).
