THEOREM.  Let `Lambda_0 = <A>` be the finite-index property (T)
subgroup of `SL_3(Z)` generated by the parahoric 6-tuple
`A = (e_21(2), e_31(4), e_32(2), e_12(1), e_13(1), e_23(1))`, and
`B = h A h^(-1)` its `h`-twist, `h = diag(2, 1, 1/2)`.  For every
REGULAR-TYPE 2-power congruence tower of genuine representations
`sigma_a` of `Lambda` (isotypic weights Plancherel at each level, or
uniformly comparable to Plancherel — the canonical enemy format)
there is a uniform floor

```text
max_i || V lambda-image(A_i) V^* - lambda-image(B_i) ||_2
   >= epsilon_0 . sqrt(3) / 2                                    (LE1)
```

for every unitary `V` at every level, `epsilon_0` the Kazhdan
constant of `(Lambda_0, A)`.  Hence no exact twisted conjugator
exists in any 2-power ultraproduct; by
`two-power-sector-conjugator-dichotomy` (whose consolidation reduced
every bounded gauge to the same question) the ENTIRE
`Lambda`-exact face of the collapse `(RC3)` is closed:

- coprime levels: closed by
  `odd-congruence-lambda-exact-sector-collapses`;
- bounded `p`-part and pure 2-power towers OF REGULAR TYPE: closed
  by `(LE1)`.

SCOPE.  The regular-type hypothesis is genuinely used (Step 2 of the
route): pointwise regularity of the limit trace does NOT by itself
force Plancherel isotypic weights — a telescoping attempt to derive
it runs into sign cancellations — so a general-weight `Lambda`-exact
enemy is not yet excluded; what remains is a SHALLOW-WEIGHT ESCAPE
LEMMA (weights concentrated on constituents factoring through a
two-step-lower level force either a re-indexable tower, which the
floor then kills, or a fixed finite quotient carrying macroscopic
weight, which must be shown incompatible with the regular limit
trace — the sign-cancellation gap).  CONSEQUENCE as proved: any
enemy of `(RC3)` either has `Lambda`-microstates at uniform positive
distance from every genuine representation (uniform outlier), or has
`Lambda`-exact microstates of genuinely non-regular isotypic weight
profile — a new, sharply-named sector that no previous node had
isolated.

PROOF SKETCH (route has details).  A conjugator's representatives
are near-invariant unit vectors of the genuine unitary
representation `rho(gamma) xi = lambda(A(gamma)) xi
lambda(B(gamma))^(-1)` on Hilbert--Schmidt space; property (T) pushes
them near the invariant space, which is the intertwiner space
`Hom(lambda compose B, lambda compose A)`.  Both representations are
multiples of regular representations of their image quotients
`Q_A, Q_B`; by Schur, every intertwiner is supported in the
constituents factoring through the JOIN quotient `Q_AB`.  The
`Ad(h)`-weights sum to zero, so the skew-depth kernel of the
`B`-side has the same index as the `A`-side (`|Q_B| = |Q_A|`), while
the shallow `(1,3)`-direction places a visible `Z/4` inside the join
kernel's image, giving `|Q_AB| <= |Q_A| / 4`.  Hence the common
isotypic fraction is at most `1/4`; the mean-ergodic projection of a
unitary onto invariants has operator norm at most `1`, so its
Hilbert--Schmidt mass in the common part is at most `1/4`, giving
`dist_2(V, Inv) >= sqrt(3)/2` and, by the Kazhdan inequality,
`(LE1)`.
