After rounding the HNN letter to a balanced involution, use the closed two-return
`A_2` loop together with

```text
W_12E_12W_12^*=B_21.                                  (RCE1)
```

The established input proves exact fold collapse for every central `C_2`
sheet twist of an odd-congruence actor.  To establish the target it remains
to show that an ambient-extension outlier satisfying the full amalgamated-free
law either corrects to that sector or pays a fixed defect in `(RCE1)` or in
one of the two closed returns.

This is strictly narrower than the invalidated closed-loop route: the exact
counterpacket to that route is now excluded, as is every redistribution of
its central sheet sign.  The unresolved enemy is a noncentral multiplicity
gauge or a genuinely non-congruence actor outlier; scalar sign bookkeeping
cannot produce another counterexample.
