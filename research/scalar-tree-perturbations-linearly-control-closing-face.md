**ESTABLISHED (uniform metric subregularity of the scalar-tree face).**  Put

```text
B_ij=C^*(P_ij),
S_Delta=B_12+B_23+B_13,
E_epsilon^D(x)=epsilon(x)1_D
```

for any unital C-star algebra `D`.  The finite group `P_13` is generated by
`P_1 union P_3`.  Let

```text
d_13=|P_13|,
R_13=max_(g in P_13) length_(P_1 union P_3)(g),
C_Delta=d_13 R_13^2.                                      (STQ1)
```

For every ucp map `Phi:S_Delta->D`, define

```text
eta(Phi)=max{
  ||Phi|_(B_12)-E_epsilon^D|_(B_12)||_cb,
  ||Phi|_(B_23)-E_epsilon^D|_(B_23)||_cb},

r_13(Phi)=||Phi|_(B_13)-E_epsilon^D|_(B_13)||_cb.          (STQ2)
```

For every `Phi`,

```text
r_13(Phi)<=C_Delta eta(Phi).                              (STQ3)
```

In particular, for every `0<r<=2`,

```text
r_13(Phi)>=r   ==>   eta(Phi)>=r/C_Delta.                 (STQ4)
```

Thus the singleton face `(CGT6)` has a dimension- and codomain-independent
linear error bound: a fixed closing-face movement forces proportional
movement on at least one tree face.
For finite-dimensional `D`, `(STQ4)` is a dimension-uniform lower bound for
the finite Choi feasibility problem

```text
inf {eta(Phi): Phi in UCP(S_Delta,D), r_13(Phi)>=r}.       (STQ5)
```

**Separator scope.**  This closes the quantitative residual stated after
`(CGT6)`, but it does not by itself turn the large dual number `c_13` in
`(CGT4)` into a lower bound for `c_12` or `c_23`.  Equation `(CGT4)` is an
evaluation of a signed separator component on the fixed centered map, whereas
`(STQ3)` is a primal constraint on one ucp map.
`supporting-choi-component-does-not-force-primal-motion` proves by a
three-point state-simplex countermodel that even a norm-attaining closest-point
separator does not supply such a selection through generic complementary
slackness.  What `(STQ4)` proves is exactly what any primal repair in the
closing branch must pay once a triangle-specific lower bound on `r_13` is
available: proportional tree motion.

Proof: `two-sided-stinespring-controls-closing-face`.
