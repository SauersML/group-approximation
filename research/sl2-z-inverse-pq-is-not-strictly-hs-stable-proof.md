Let `G = SL_2(Z[1/pq])` and `G_0 = SL_2(Z[1/p])`.  We check the two
hypotheses of `tfd-unbounded-irreps-group-is-not-strictly-hs-stable` for `G`
with a finite generating set `S` of `G`.  `G` is finitely generated, since
`G_0` is by item 1 of `sl2-s-arithmetic-pair-is-codense-kazhdan` and `G` is
an S-arithmetic group.

**(T;FD) for `G`.**  By item 1 of `sl2-s-arithmetic-pair-is-codense-kazhdan`,
`G_0` has (T;FD).  Let `(S_0, kappa_0)` be a uniform finite-dimensional
Kazhdan pair for `G_0`, with `S_0 subset G_0 subset G`.  Let `pi` be a
finite-dimensional unitary representation of `G` on `H`.  By the
co-density consequence of items 3 and 4 there, `H^(G_0) = H^G`.  The
orthocomplement of `H^G` is `G`-invariant, and its restriction to `G_0`
is a finite-dimensional representation of `G_0` without invariant vectors.
So `max_(s in S_0) ||pi(s) v - v|| >= kappa_0 ||v||` for every `v` orthogonal
to `H^G`.  This is hypothesis 1 for `G`, with `m = max_(s in S_0) |s|_S`.

**Unbounded irreducibles.**  Let `l` be a prime not dividing `pq`.  The ring
map `Z[1/pq] -> F_l` induces `r_l: G -> SL_2(F_l)`.  This map is surjective:
`SL_2(F_l)` is generated by the elementary matrices `[[1,a],[0,1]]` and
`[[1,0],[a,1]]` (`F_l` is a field), and these lift to `SL_2(Z) subset G`.

`SL_2(F_l)` acts 2-transitively on the `l + 1` points of `P^1(F_l)`.  Given
distinct lines `L_1 != L_2` and `M_1 != M_2`, pick nonzero `v_i in L_i` and
`w_i in M_i`.  The linear map `g` with `g v_i = w_i` is invertible with some
determinant `c`.  Replacing `w_2` by `c^(-1) w_2` gives `g in SL_2(F_l)`
mapping `L_i` to `M_i`.

Let `chi` be the permutation character.  Burnside's lemma gives
`<chi, 1> = 1` (transitivity) and `<chi, chi> = 2` (the stabilizer of a point
has two orbits, by 2-transitivity).  So `chi - 1` is the character of an
irreducible representation `St_l` of dimension `l`.  Since `r_l` is
surjective, `St_l o r_l` is an irreducible unitary representation of `G` of
dimension `l`.  There are infinitely many such primes, so the dimensions are
unbounded.  This is hypothesis 2.

**Conclusion.**  The theorem applies with `sigma = St_l o r_l` and `k = l - 1`.
It gives tuples of dimension `l - 1` with defect `<= 2L/sqrt(l-1)` and
distance `>= kappa_0/m - 2/sqrt(l-1)` from every exact representation of
dimension `l - 1`.  Take `eps = kappa_0/(2m)`. ∎
