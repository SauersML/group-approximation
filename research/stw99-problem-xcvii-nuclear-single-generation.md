**Problem XCVII of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White.**  Must every unital simple
separable nuclear C*-algebra be generated by one operator?

The answer is **no**.  The counterexample in
`stw96-simple-nuclear-ah-not-singly-generated` is an AH algebra and is thus
nuclear.  This is not merely a nonexact or tensor-norm pathology.
