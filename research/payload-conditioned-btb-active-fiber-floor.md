**OPEN ONE-FIBER FLOOR TARGET.**  Use the protected characteristic-three
BTB wordization.  In a canonical normalized-HS microstate let

```text
Q_0=(1-J)/2 (1+B)/2 (1-ell)/2,
A_i=Q_0 P_i,                                             (BAF1)
```

after fixed structural correction.  For the maximal forbidden type `i`,
the BTB root cut and two-pair E5 cell give a corrected fiber `F_i<=A_i`
with

```text
tau(F_i)>=(1/18)tau(A_i)-C sqrt(E_pres).                (BAF2)
```

Construct a finite ordinary-group extension and a fixed relator menu `T`
such that, for every corrected maximal type,

```text
sum_(r in T)||(rho(r)-1)F_i||_2^2
 >= kappa tau(F_i)-C' sqrt(E_pres)                     (BAF3)
```

for one `kappa>0` independent of matrix dimension.  The exact perfect
tracial model must extend; there every forbidden `A_i`, hence every `F_i`,
is zero, so `(BAF3)` is vacuous.

This is strictly weaker as a **global architecture** than `(CBR3)`: it asks
for no returned projection, no overlap estimate, no reducing-hull
carry-forward, and no finite adaptive tree.  It is stronger locally because
the one active typed multiplicity presentation must have an actual HS floor
rather than merely place mass outside a chosen carrier.

## Sharp surviving Leavitt interface

There is a one-word sufficient form of `(BAF3)`.  On `P=F_i`, decode a
coarse `M_2` and fine `M_4` matrix-unit system with common identity `P`, let
`e` and `f` be minimal diagonal corners, and authenticate their reflections

```text
R_e=1-2e,                  R_f=1-2f
```

as ordinary words.  For one unitary word `W`, put

```text
r=R_f W R_e W^*.                                      (BAF4)
```

`unitary-offdiagonal-block-gram-is-support-leakage` and
`one-coarse-to-fine-prefix-isometry-closes-the-reservoir` give, after fixed
packet correction,

```text
tau(F_i) <= ||rho(r)-1||_2^2+C sqrt(E_pres).           (BAF5)
```

Thus a conditional non-semisimple compiler for this one same-object support
row proves `(BAF3)` with a singleton relator menu and a fixed constant.  It is
automatically vacuous in the perfect model because `F_i=0` there.  No full
Leavitt multiplication table or recursive carrier is analytically necessary.

The word **non-semisimple** is load-bearing.  If `e,f` are replaced by
literal finite-packet projections of traces `tau(F_i)/2` and `tau(F_i)/4`,
`literal-fiber-unequal-reflection-row-collapses-packet` shows that `(BAF4)`
and preservation of the literal `F_i` contradict canonical trace invariance
and destroy packet injection.  Therefore the remaining construction must
decode the two coefficient corners only inside finite matrix coordinates;
the group regular representation must not identify them with those literal
unequal-trace projections.  This is the root-local version of the prescribed
Leavitt carrier decoder, not another finite routing packet.

The singleton word is not itself that decoder.
`singleton-reflection-has-balanced-eight-dimensional-model` gives the
smallest exact specialization: on the four-dimensional marked spin packet
with one spectator qubit, the groupified fine reflection is
`t=C tensor diag(1,-1)`.  It is conjugate to the amplified coarse reflection,
so `t W R_e W^*=1` exactly, while replacing `t` by the physical
`C tensor I` leaves squared defect exactly `2`.  The model extends across a
finite native Whitehead occurrence chart.  Therefore the surviving compiler
must use a full-presentation coefficient-occurrence relation which kills the
spectator gauge on the prescribed root fiber; the one support word plus a
finite rectangular chart cannot do it.

The floor cannot come from property `(T)`, finite-subgroup stability, or a
fiberwise hyperlinear cross-edge presentation alone.  If the full active
specialization in `(BAF3)` has asymptotically exact models compatible with
the authenticated outer rows, amplifying those models keeps every compressed
relator defect asymptotically zero on the full active fiber.  The local-carrier
no-go explains why a Kazhdan correction does not change this conclusion.
Thus the ordinary relators must activate a genuinely payload-conditioned
typed presentation with a dimension-uniform matrix gap.  The non-CE BCS
supplies such a gap in projection/star-algebra language, but compiling that
gap into the compressed ordinary words in `(BAF3)` is the remaining
groupification interface.

## Attempts

- A property-`(T)` or stable finite-packet actor only corrects the active
  matrices toward an exact intertwiner algebra; spectator multiplicity remains
  in that algebra, so it gives no positive compressed relator floor.
- Ordinary untyped relators on the active multiplicity letters admit the
  trivial representation.  A viable attack must therefore use authenticated
  outer phases or another genuinely typed, payload-conditioned specialization;
  constructing that specialization is deferred to this claim.
- Nesting a fresh robust non-CE BCS verifier on the literal root fiber does
  localize its matrix gap exactly, but
  `nested-bcs-on-literal-btb-fiber-recurses-to-root-kill` shows
  that every nested forbidden atom is again a Reynolds projection.  A finite
  packet conversion to ordinary relator energy is possible only through a
  normal-quotient selector, so arbitrary Boolean support recreates the same
  groupification gate rather than closing `(BAF3)`.
- A direct same-object reflection built from literal finite packets also
  fails algebraically.  By
  `literal-fiber-unequal-reflection-row-collapses-packet`, if a stable word
  preserves the literal root object and conjugates a coarse packet corner of
  trace `tau(F)/2` to a fine corner of trace `tau(F)/4`, canonical trace
  invariance contradicts injection of the packet.  Allowing the word to move
  `F` restores regular-trace compensation.  Thus the viable Leavitt option
  must decode non-semisimple coarse/fine coefficient corners on the prescribed
  matrix carrier; replacing them by literal `M_2/M_4` packet projections
  destroys the source it was meant to charge.
- Enlarging the literal reflection language by any finite number of affine
  refinements and normalizers also fails uniformly.
  `affine-reflection-normalizers-have-quarter-overlap`
  puts all named commuting signs in one finite Boolean translation group and
  all connector actions in its finite linear group.  The regular model
  satisfies every reflection product and conjugacy row exactly, but every
  pair of distinct support halves has overlap and two-sided mismatch `1/4`.
  Tensoring with the positive selected BTB fiber makes the unpaid mismatch
  exactly `tau(F_i)/4` while compressed affine-relator energy stays zero.
  Hence no finite depth of XOR refinement, finite Clifford phase, or affine
  support normalizer can supply `(BAF3)`.  The surviving row must genuinely
  leave the affine reflection category: a non-semisimple global coefficient
  occurrence, or an active gauge presentation carrying the missing HS
  profile itself.
- The full signed Whitehead occurrence system does leave the affine category,
  but its residual profile is already a nonhyperlinearity-strength gate.
  `ordinary-leavitt-quotient-fences-whitehead-active-floor` passes one active
  coefficient coordinate to `Q=EL_5(M_3(L_2(k)))`; the Whitehead word retains
  exact order four, so the packet graft has a quotient `D *_(C_4) Q`.  If `Q`
  is hyperlinear, canonical microstates of this quotient pull back and, after
  tensoring an exact positive outer fiber, give asymptotically exact
  full-occurrence active-gauge models with the Clifford mark separated.
  Hence proving `(BAF3)` from those rows would already prove this explicit
  ordinary Leavitt group nonhyperlinear.  Exact finite-dimensional collapse,
  property `(T)`, and current character stability do not supply that
  promotion.
- Even making the finite packet act nontrivially on the actor does not block
  absorption.  `finite-packet-actions-have-positive-fiber-microstates`
  regularly induces a canonical microstate of any hyperlinear actor `K`
  across a finite action `P -> Aut(K)`.  The crossed conjugacy rows hold,
  every nonzero packet Reynolds fiber keeps its regular positive trace, and
  tensoring the positive outer forbidden atom gives compressed relator energy
  tending to zero.  Thus the missing relation cannot be finite covariance,
  a finite projective phase, or a finite occurrence orbit.  It must make the
  active specialization cease to be a finite extension of the actor, encode
  a genuinely noninvertible coefficient incidence, or prove the actor itself
  nonhyperlinear.
