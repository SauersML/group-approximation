**ESTABLISHED** (lane bh-invent-03, 2026-09-19; elementary; not reviewed). This is a check of import (I1) of
`free-group-houghton-group-is-of-type-f-infinity` against Farley–Hughes, *Finiteness properties of locally defined
groups* (arXiv:2010.08035). Read at source: Definition 3.12, Proposition 3.24, Proposition 6.21, Theorem 6.9 and
Example 3.30. It is independent of the referee bh-ref-t0.

## The model

Following FH Example 3.30 (Lehnert's `QV`), take the set to be the vertices only: `X = T^0 = F_2`, not `T^0 ⊔ ∂T`.
- **The semigroup.** `S` is the inverse semigroup generated by the translations `C^0(v) -> C^0(w)` between the
  vertex sets of cones of the same type (half-trees away from `o`), and the singleton bijections.
- **Its domains.** A composite restricts a translation to a sub-cone or to a point. So
  `D_S^+ = {singletons} ∪ {C^0(v) : v ≠ o}`.
- **The group.** `Γ_S ≅ F`. An element of `F` restricts injectively to the dense set `T^0` of isolated points.
  Conversely, a bijection that is a finite union of `S`-maps is a label-preserving quasi-automorphism
  (`free-group-houghton-group-has-lopsided-descending-links`, item 1). Pieces whose image contains `o` are split
  finitely often, until every image is a cone away from `o`.

## The check

1. **Nested domains (Def. 3.12(1)).** Two cones meet only if one apex lies on the geodesic from `o` to the other, and
   then they are nested. A singleton meeting a domain lies in it.
2. **Finite complementation (Def. 3.12(2)).**
   - `X ∖ C^0(v)` is the finite set of vertices on the geodesic from `o` to the parent of `v`, together with the
     finitely many cones hanging off that geodesic.
   - `X ∖ {w}` is `(X ∖ C^0(w)) ∪` the three child cones of `w`, or the four cones at `o` if `w = o`.
3. **The maximal S-structure.** `𝕊(D, D')` is the unique translation between cones of the same type, or the unique
   bijection between singletons, or `∅`. So `𝕊(D, D) = {id}`.
   - **Partitions.** The maximal partition of `C^0(v)` is `{v}` plus its three child cones, and a singleton is its
     own maximal partition.
   - **Types.** There are five domain types: the singleton and the cones of types `a, A, b, B`.
   - **Invariance.** Proposition 3.24 holds automatically, because the restriction of an `S`-map to a subdomain
     lies in `S`: compose with `id_(D') = s'^(-1) s'`.
4. **Conclusion.** Proposition 6.21 applies, so the maximal-partition scheme is `n`-connected for every `n`. By
   Theorem 6.9, `Δ^𝓔` is `n`-connected for all `n`, hence contractible. The cell stabilizers are finite, since every
   `𝕊(D, D)` is trivial (FH Cor. 6.15). ∎

## Lesson for general BH

For tree seeds, Farley–Hughes's combinatorial "compact ultrametric property" is exactly the nesting of cones plus
cofiniteness of their complements. It holds for **any** seed whose cylinder sets are nested, i.e. any seed whose
orbit carries a tree of cylinders. It fails for seeds over groups whose windows overlap without nesting, such as
`Z^2`: that is FH's `nV` warning.

**For the hard-actor route.** Finiteness proofs by this engine need an ultrametric, nested, system of cylinders
before anything else. Tree-like or hierarchical seeds have one. Generic SFT seeds over one-ended groups do not.
