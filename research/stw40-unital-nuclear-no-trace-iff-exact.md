Let `G` be a countable nonamenable group.  The following are equivalent:

1. `G` is exact;
2. there are a compact metrizable space `X` and a topologically amenable
   action `G acts on X` with no invariant probability measure;
3. `G` has an amenable action on a commutative unital separable nuclear
   C-star algebra with no invariant tracial state;
4. `G` has an amenable action on some unital separable nuclear C-star
   algebra with no invariant tracial state.

Thus the unital/nuclear/no-invariant-trace version of the construction
problem is completely solved: its exact hypothesis is **exactness**, and a
commutative witness already suffices.  STW Problem XL remains open because it
asks for the amenable action on a unital stably finite classifiable target;
the commutative witness is generally nonsimple and does not supply that
realization.
