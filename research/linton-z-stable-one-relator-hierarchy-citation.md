**Citation.** M. Linton, *One-relator hierarchies*, arXiv:2202.11324v3 (27 June 2024),
published in Duke Math. J. 174 (2025), no. 4.

**Check.** The statements in the claim were transcribed from the arXiv v3 PDF pages 3–4, 23,
24, 27–31, read on 2026-09-13. The Z-stable number and Definition 1.1 are on p. 3 and restated
on p. 23. Remark 6.2 is on p. 24, the graph of cyclic stabilisers on p. 27, Lemma 6.13 and
Theorem 6.14 on p. 29, Example 6.12 on p. 28, Example 6.15 on p. 30, and Theorem 7.1 on pp. 3
and 31.

Theorem 7.1's "Moreover" clause rests on Wise's theorem that hyperbolic groups with a
quasi-convex hierarchy are virtually special. Linton cites it as [Wis21, Theorem 13.3] in the
proof on p. 31. It is imported here only as quoted by Linton.
