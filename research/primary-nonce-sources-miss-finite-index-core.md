None of the currently cited primary constructions of non-Connes-embeddable
tracial algebras supplies the hypotheses of
`finite-index-projective-core-yields-central-witness`.

1. **MIP-star.**  Ji--Natarajan--Vidick--Wright--Yuen prove a separation of
   commuting and tensor-product correlations via recursive compression.  The
   operator-algebraic consequence refutes Connes embedding, but the paper
   does not identify a finite-index subfactor, a projective regular basis, or
   a finite scalar cocycle in the resulting non-CE algebra.

2. **BCS.**  Paddock--Slofstra construct finitely presented BCS star
   algebras and compare their representation/satisfiability classes.  Their
   Proposition 5.8 says that a tracial versus `R^U` separation for a linear
   constraint system exists if and only if a finitely presented
   nonhyperlinear group exists.  The paper gives no von Neumann
   finite-index or crossed-product decomposition of its nonlinear BCS GNS
   algebra.  The explicit local context algebras are finite-dimensional,
   hence infinite-index in a non-CE factor, while making the native global
   observables a projective basis is already blocked by
   `twisted-group-basis-forces-affine-context-support`.

3. **IRS and relation factors.**  Manzoor's proof first obtains a
   non-co-hyperlinear IRS `H`, then uses only inclusions

   ```text
   L(Gamma/H) < L(R) < L(R'),                           (PNS1)
   ```

   where `R'` has property `(T)`.  No inclusion is asserted to have finite
   index, and no projective basis or scalar cocycle is constructed.  The
   explicit algebra `L(R)` is generated by the IRS representation together
   with `L^infinity(X)`; this is a relation/groupoid algebra description,
   not a twisted group-factor identification.  The paper's further
   directions explicitly leave promotion from the metrically
   nonhyperlinear full group to a nonhyperlinear discrete group open.

4. **CDI property-(T) envelopes.**  Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana Proposition 3.8
   gives the most structured endpoint,

   ```text
   Q=M^B crossed_product_(beta,w) B.                    (PNS2)
   ```

   Here `B` is the infinite group used by the wreath-like construction.
   Thus `[Q:M^B]=infinity`; moreover the original input coordinate
   `M^e<Q` has infinite index because its relative commutant contains the
   diffuse tensor tail.  The cocycle `w` is `U(M^B)`-valued, not scalar.
   CDI Remark 1.1 explicitly records that it remains open whether group
   II1 factors are embedding-universal.  Consequently `(PNS2)` is not a
   hidden finite-index twisted-group core.

This is a source-availability statement, not a theorem that these factors
cannot contain some presently unknown finite-index projective subfactor.
It sharply identifies what a new extraction theorem must add: a diffuse
finite-index subfactor, a trace-orthonormal projective group basis for it,
and torsion of its scalar multiplier.  None of those three data is present
in the cited constructions.

Primary sources:

- Ji--Natarajan--Vidick--Wright--Yuen,
  [*MIP*=RE*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04383), abstract and the recursive
  compression/correlation separation.
- Paddock--Slofstra,
  [*Satisfiability problems and algebras of boolean constraint system
  games*](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.07901v2), Sections 2--5 and
  Proposition 5.8.
- Manzoor,
  [*There is an equivalence relation whose von Neumann algebra is not
  Connes embeddable*](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06697), Definition 2.12,
  Proposition 2.11, and the proof of Theorem 1.1.
- Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana,
  [*Embedding universality for II1 factors with property
  (T)*](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.07442), Remark 1.1 and Proposition 3.8.
