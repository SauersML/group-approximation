For every `m≥2,q≥2,d≥0` and prime `p∤q`, the group
`W_{m,q,d}=UT_m(Z[1/q])^(Z)⋊(P_d⋊Z)` defined in artifact §1 embeds
in `B_{d,2}`. It is generated by its `m-1` adjacent unit lamps at zero,
the shift `X`, and the top Newton twist `T_d`.

## Attempts

Artifact §9 gives the full two-root shell coordinates, finite-prefix
shift, radial twist profiles at both endpoints, and localized lamp
`u↦δ_p(a)u` on `U=N\δ_p(N)`. The nonnormality of `δ_p(N)` does not
prevent left multiplication by its elements from preserving its complement.
The proposed covariance is the required `δ_q^{P(n)}` action at position
`n`; shift displacement and infinitely many empty lamp positions detect
the acting polynomial, and disjoint translations detect the lamps.

Pending checks are actual membership of each map in the asserted full
group, the finite-prefix extension at both roots, all covariance and
faithfulness statements, and finite generation including denominators.
The submitted finite script checks formulas only and is not a proof of
this embedding claim.
