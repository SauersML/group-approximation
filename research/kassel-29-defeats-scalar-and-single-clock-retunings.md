```text

```

Consequently tensor/conjugate/amplification retunings generated by that
packet cannot kill `Delta_9` without killing the mark.

`x_(alpha+beta)=I`, scalar `x_(-beta)`, and involutive `x_beta`.  Freeing the
middle root with one cyclic Weyl clock also fails: (2.4), (2.5), and (2.12)
force the impossible congruence `3r=-1 mod N` at every clock order divisible
by three.  A surviving retuning must therefore use a noncyclic middle-root
packet or a genuinely non-scalar negative-long-root generator.

satisfies all seven relations in `S5` with `w_beta^4` of order three, but
noncentral. The exceptional-cover repair is fenced by
