title: Problem LXXXI holds for separable subhomogeneous algebras
distinct_from:
  stw82-subhomogeneous-maximum: that theorem computes nuclear dimension across an extension whose endpoints are subhomogeneous; this theorem turns LXXXI's hereditary commutative-shadow hypothesis into a one-dimensional bound on a single subhomogeneous algebra.
---

Let `A` be a separable subhomogeneous C-star algebra.  Assume that every
commutative hereditary subalgebra of every quotient of `A` has primitive
spectrum of covering dimension at most one.  Then

```text
dim_nuc(A) <= 1.
```

In particular, Problem LXXXI has a positive answer throughout the separable
subhomogeneous class: the only possible nuclear dimensions are zero and one
(and the advertised `infinity` alternative is unnecessary in this class).
