The q14 residual is the commutator defect of two exact square-zero rank-one-
quarter nilpotents.  Flexible rank stability of the four-dimensional V4
algebra corrects them to an exact commuting pair on a dimension differing by
`o(d)`.  Direct sums with the four elementary commuting modules listed in
the claim tune dimension and both ranks to `(4l,l,l)` at further `o(d)`
cost.  Each marginal then has exactly the repeated-natural transvection
Jordan type.  The near-conjugacy theorem realizes the corrected marginals by
rank-small conjugations of the two entire Atlas charts.  Word telescoping
preserves vanishing rank of every remaining residual.
