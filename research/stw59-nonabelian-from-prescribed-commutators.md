Take the one-term list (n, 1). The wedge has a single arm S^1 x S^(2n-1),
the central kernel is Z/n!, and the commutator has order N(n,1) = n. The
algebra has a unique trace, stable rank two, and free K1 of countably
infinite rank. These are the target's clauses verbatim.
