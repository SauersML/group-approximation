title: Use finite-dimensional compression invisibility

For a finite-dimensional representation `rho`, the compression-invisibility
theorem gives `rho(Lambda)=rho(E)`.  Passing to the corresponding finite
central quotient says that the image of the compressed copy is the whole
image group, including every surviving central class.

If two irreducible representations of that finite image have isomorphic
restrictions to the compressed subgroup, they are therefore isomorphic as
representations of the whole image.  In particular their central characters
coincide.  Hence the proposed pair with equal compressed restriction and
distinct central character cannot exist.  Nontriviality of relative `K_1`
does not alter this conclusion because the compressed image already contains
that central piece.
