**Setting** (`leavitt-cuntz-cone-halves-have-rf-amalgam-cover`, `leavitt-cone-cover-far-cross-relators-are-uniformly-small`).
- `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, `t_i s_j = delta_ij`, `s_1 t_1 + s_2 t_2 = 1`, `q = s_1 t_1`.
- `R_+ = <s_1, s_2, q>`, `R_- = <t_1, t_2, q>`, `R_0 = F_2 1 + F_2 q`.
- `Delta = St_20(R)`, `Delta_+-` generated by the `x_ij(a)`, `a in R_+-`, `Delta_0 = SL_20(F_2)^2`.
- `Gamma = Delta_+ *_(Delta_0) Delta_-`, `K = ker(Gamma -> Delta) = <<k_1, ..., k_r>>`, `z = x_13(q)`.
- `rho : Gamma -> U(M)`, with `M` finite-dimensional and normalized trace. `delta = max_i ||rho(k_i) - 1||_2`.
- **Shadow ideals.** `J_+ = {a in R_+ : rho(x_12(a)) = 1}` is a two-sided ideal of `R_+` with `|R_+/J_+| <= 2^d`
  (`positive-characteristic-steinberg-unitary-images-are-finite`, applied to `rho|Delta_+` through
  `St_20(R_+) -> Delta_+`). Here `rho(x_ij(a)) = 1` for all `i != j` iff `a in J_+`. Put
  `D_+ = dim_(F_2) R_+/J_+ <= d`. Define `J_-` and `D_-` likewise.
- **Cuntz words.** For `omega = (w_1, ..., w_L) in {1,2}^L`, put `s_omega = s_(w_L) ... s_(w_1)` and
  `s_omega^* = t_(w_1) ... t_(w_L)`. The **collision depth** `L*(J_+)` is the least `L` such that two distinct
  words of length `L` have `s_omega - s_omega' in J_+`. By pigeonhole, `L*(J_+) <= D_+ + 1`.

**Lemma (LR), uniform Leavitt reduction.** Write `P^m(u, v) = [x_im(u), x_mk(v)]` for fixed `i != k` and
`u in R_-`, `v in R_+`. Take distinct `i, a, b, c, k`, `u in R_-`, `v in R_+`, and generators `t = t_j`,
`s = s_j'`, so `eps = t s = delta_jj' in {0, 1}`. Then, with `g = x_ia(u) x_ab(t)`,

```text
|| rho(P^b(u t, s v)) - rho(g) rho(P^a(eps u, v)) rho(g)^(-1) ||_2  <=  c_L delta,      c_L = 6 C_1 + 4 c_0.   (LR)
```

- `C_1` is the constant of `(U2)` in `leavitt-cone-cover-far-cross-relators-are-uniformly-small`.
- `c_0` is the largest number of conjugates of `k_i^(+-1)` needed to write one of the finitely many Leavitt defects
  `[x_ab(t_j), x_bc(s_j')] x_ac(delta_jj')^(-1)`, over `j, j' in {1,2}` and `(a,b,c)` in `{(4,5,6), (5,4,6)}`.
- The bound does not depend on `u`, `v`, `rho`, `M` or `d`. For `eps = 0` the right side is `rho(g) rho(g)^(-1) = 1`.

**Theorem (linear head bound).** For every genuine `rho`,

```text
|| rho(z) - 1 ||_2  <=  2 L*(J_+) c_L delta  <=  2 (D_+ + 1) c_L delta  <=  2 (d + 1) c_L delta.              (HB)
```

By the mirror automorphism `g -> (g^*)^(-1)`, the same holds with `J_-`, `D_-` in place of `J_+`, `D_+`, and with
`c_L` replaced by `c_L c_theta`. Here `c_theta` depends only on the chosen normal generators.

**What this settles.**
- **(MW) at `m = 1` is true up to an explicit conjugation.**
  - `leavitt-cone-cover-far-cross-relators-are-uniformly-small` recorded that `(MW)` is open even for `m = 1`,
    "because Leavitt reductions cost normal generators".
  - `(LR)` shows that a Leavitt reduction inside a mixed product costs one fixed `O(delta)`, independent of every
    ring word. The only remaining obstruction to `(QC)` is **linear accumulation** along the reduction chain.
- **Explicit rate for (QC).** `(QC)` holds in dimension `d` with `delta(eps, d) = eps / (2(d + 1) c_L)`. This is
  the first explicit rate on the graph. The route `leavitt-cone-head-bound-is-finite-congruence-gluing` had
  compactness only.

## Class killed

**Members.** Every proposed counterexample family to `(QC)` whose shadow ring on either half has bounded
`F_2`-dimension, or more generally bounded Cuntz-word collision depth. Their dimension, multiplicity and twist are
unrestricted. Examples:
- twisted gluings `(sigma_+, U sigma_- U^*)` of any bounded ring shadow;
- the suggested `M_2(F_2)` twisted-gluing test, where `D <= 4` gives `||rho(z) - 1||_2 <= 10 c_L delta`;
- amplifications `rho ⊗ 1_n` and direct sums of boundedly many shadow types.

**Invariant.** The collision depth `L*(J_+-)` of the shadow ideals: the least length at which the Cuntz words stop
being distinct modulo `J_+-`.

**Death step.** The `(LR)` chain. It starts at `z = [x_14(q), x_43(1)]`, which is exact in `Delta_0`. It runs `L`
backward reductions to `P(q s_omega^*, s_omega)`. It swaps `s_omega` for a colliding `s_omega'`, exactly, because
`rho` kills `x(J_+)`. It then runs forward reductions until the first mismatched letter, where `eps = 0` kills the
term.

**Consequence.** A counterexample to `(QC)` with head `eps` and defect `delta` needs every pair of distinct Cuntz words
of length below `eps / (2 c_L delta)` to stay distinct modulo **both** `J_+` and `J_-`. Hence it needs
`D_+, D_- >= eps / (2 c_L delta) - 1`, and so `d >= eps / (2 c_L delta) - 1`.
