Let `M` be a sigma-finite, equivalently countably decomposable,
type-II-infinity factor.  Then every two unital embeddings `Z->M` are
approximately unitarily equivalent in norm, indeed modulo the norm-closed
ideal generated by the finite projections of `M`.

No separable-predual or separably acting hypothesis is imposed.  Thus this
includes countably decomposable factors whose preduals are nonseparable.
