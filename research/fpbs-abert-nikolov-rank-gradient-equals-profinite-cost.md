title: The rank gradient of a Farber chain equals the cost of the boundary action minus one

Let `G` be finitely generated and `(G_n)` a Farber chain, for example a descending chain of finite-index normal subgroups with trivial intersection. Then `RG(G,(G_n)) = C(E) - 1`, where `E` is the orbit relation of `G` on the boundary of the coset tree, which for a normal chain is the profinite completion `lim G/G_n` with Haar measure. This is an imported published result (Abert--Nikolov, Theorem 1). It is the only known action-dependent lower bound on cost.
