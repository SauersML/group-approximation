**INVALIDATED CHARACTER-TRANSPORT SHORTCUT.**  The proposed route transports
the active-mismatch character atom to an auxiliary atom and tries to compare
its controlled actors with commuting identity-label actors there.

`character-hnn-retains-and-or-holonomy` proves the exact dichotomy.  A common
edge carrying enough packet structure to authenticate the gauges transports
the controlled covariance and hence preserves the AND cocycle.  Two separate
edges, or an edge omitting the packet, leave an arbitrary relative
multiplicity holonomy and may have zero cross-Gram.  A new mixed row
controlling the relative stable word is precisely the still-open occurrence.
