**Sources read (2026-09-14):**
- **Capobianco–Kari–Taati:**
  - PDF from https://dmtcs.episciences.org/3918/pdf (DMTCS 19:3, 2017, #4, the arXiv:1507.02472v5 version), text extracted with `pdftotext -layout`;
  - read: the abstract, Definition 1, Proposition 5, Theorem 1 with the first line of its proof, Corollary 2, Example 3, and Theorem 2 with the sentence after it.
- **Doucha–Gismatullin:**
  - TeX e-print of arXiv:2008.10565, fetched to /scratch.global/sauer354/gk3-dual-surjunctivity/src/;
  - read: the post-surjectivity definitions and Observation, Theorem isom and its Corollary, the Section 3 closure results, and Theorem kap with its Claim and proof.
- **Bartholdi (with an appendix by Kielak):**
  - TeX e-print of arXiv:1605.09133;
  - read: the abstract and the main theorem.

**Scope.**
- CKT state their theorems for finitely generated groups. Their Proposition 5
  reduces post-surjectivity to the subgroup generated by the neighborhood, so
  Theorem 1 holds on every full shift. The proof of Theorem 1 starts with
  "By Proposition 5, it is sufficient to consider the case where G is
  finitely generated".
- Doucha–Gismatullin state "All sofic groups are dual surjunctive" with no
  finite-generation restriction.
- The CKT conference version ("An 'almost dual' to Gottschalk's Conjecture",
  AUTOMATA 2016, LNCS) was not read; nothing here depends on it.
