**ESTABLISHED.**  Fix unequal bits `i!=j` and one binary tail `b`.  In the
stabilized binary-Leavitt Steinberg group, put

```text
beta=h_(jb),                    eta_a=c_(ia).             (UFM1)
```

Then `beta` is a nontrivial involution, every `eta_a` centralizes `beta`, and
the family contains infinitely many distinct cosets modulo `<beta>`.  Hence

```text
sup_a Area([eta_a,beta])=infinity.                       (UFM2)
```

In particular there is no uniform first-mismatch area bound when the two
tail lengths are allowed to vary independently.

The coefficient product is zero for every `a`:

```text
r_a t_i s_j s_b=0.                                      (UFM3)
```

Choose an infinite prefix subfamily for which the coefficients `r_a t_i`
are distinct.  Their `x_12` root elements are distinct under the canonical
map to elementary matrices.  No quotient of two of them can lie in the
order-two `x_23` subgroup generated by `beta`, again by comparing elementary
matrix entries.  Thus their `<beta>`-cosets are distinct.  Apply
`finite-mark-centralizer-commutator-area-is-proper` to this section to obtain
`(UFM2)`.

There is no conflict with the equal-depth chromatic program.  After removing
the common prefix of two equal-depth addresses, the two tails have equal
length.  Freezing one of them then leaves only finitely many possibilities
for the other, so the proper-section argument above cannot start.
