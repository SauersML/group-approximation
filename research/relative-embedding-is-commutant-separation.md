For countable `C<=A`, scalar relative embeddability of `L(C)<=L(A)`
is equivalent to existence of a canonical CE actor model containing
countably many `C`-central projections with common commutation
stabilizer exactly `C`.

Equivalently, `A` is hyperlinear and every `g outside C` can be moved
by a `C`-central projection in some canonical CE actor model. The
model may depend on `g`; countable weighted direct sums combine the
witnesses. No uniform commutator lower bound is needed.

For a hyperlinear `A`, intersecting the commutation stabilizers of all
`C`-central projections in all canonical CE actor models gives the
least intermediate subgroup `K_C` with scalar relatively embeddable
inclusion into `A`. In particular `C` is scalar relatively embeddable
exactly when `K_C=C`, and intersections of scalar-RE subgroups are
scalar-RE.
The map `C -> K_C` is extensive, monotone, and idempotent. Global
relative-commutant collapse for `C<=A` is equivalent to `K_C=A`, or
to the absence of a proper scalar-RE intermediate subgroup containing
`C`.
