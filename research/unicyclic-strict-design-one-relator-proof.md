- With a connected difference graph there is no free factor, and the number of
  relators of `U_E = Hol_M(E)` is the first Betti number. So `U_E` is free or
  one-relator.
- A strict realization over `U_E` makes `U_E` nonsurjunctive, hence nonsofic by
  Gromov--Weiss.
