Every orientation of the degree-four length-ten shared-carrier triangle whose
abelian copy-constraint rank is seven, eight, or nine has coefficient-group
boundary either trivial or of reduced free-product length greater than one.
It therefore cannot kill a nontrivial element in one coefficient copy.

The 216 orientations consist of two at rank seven, five at rank eight, three
at rank nine, and 206 at rank ten.  This theorem exhausts the first ten and
leaves the high-rank bulk as a separate, less favorable family.
