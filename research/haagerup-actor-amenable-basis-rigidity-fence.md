Let `Gamma` be Haagerup and let it act trace-preservingly on an amenable finite
von Neumann algebra `B`.  Then `B rtimes Gamma` contains no property-`(T)`
`II_1` subfactor.  In particular, for every countable amenable group `K` and
every action `Gamma -> Aut(K)`,

```text
L(K) rtimes Gamma = L(K rtimes Gamma)
```

cannot contain the fixed property-`(T)` non-CE relation factor.

Thus replacing the abelian Haar base by a noncommutative amenable group basis
does not revive the native virtually-free/Haagerup actor.  A viable direct
group-basis envelope must already use a non-Haagerup actor such as
`SL_3(Z)`.
