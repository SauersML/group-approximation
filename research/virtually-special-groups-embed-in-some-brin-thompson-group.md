**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`virtually-special-groups-embed-in-brin-thompson-citation`).

Let `G` be a group with a finite-index subgroup that embeds in a right-angled
Artin group `A_Γ` on a finite simple graph Γ. Then `G` embeds in nV for some n.

Consequences recorded in the source:
- every virtually special group embeds in some nV;
- this includes all finitely generated Coxeter groups, many word-hyperbolic
  groups (among them all one-relator groups with torsion), all limit groups, and
  many 3-manifold groups (all compact nonpositively curved ones and all
  finite-volume hyperbolic ones);
- every surface group and every graph braid group embeds in some nV.

The dimension bound is `n = |V(Γ)| + |E^c(Γ)|` for `A_Γ` itself. The source calls
it far from sharp.
