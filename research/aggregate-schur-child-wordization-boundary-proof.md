Use the elementary-abelian normal form in the full `q`-MASA to compare the
fixed output of tail cancellation with the sector-dependent corrected words
`Q_s`. Then apply invertibility of group words to the two Toeplitz
relations; unitary invariance of normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm gives the
quantitative equality `(ACW5)`. Finally expand the product of two
reflections. It is a reflection only in the commuting case, where its
negative cut is the symmetric difference `(ACW6)` rather than the additive
union. Private orthogonal tags move the problem back to shared-carrier
coverage instead of solving it.
