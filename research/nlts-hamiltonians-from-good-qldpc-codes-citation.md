Literature import. Anshu--Breuckmann--Nirkhe, *NLTS Hamiltonians from good quantum
codes*, arXiv:2206.13228v4 (5 Dec 2024; STOC'23). Read from the v4 PDF (pp. 1--3)
and the v4 TeX source on 2026-09-12:

- Theorem 1 (No low-energy trivial states), quoted in Section 3 of the linked
  artifact and restated in the claim;
- Property 1 (clustering of approximate code-words) and the Hamiltonian
  `H=H_X+H_Z` built from the stabilizer rows (p. 2);
- the formal statement at the end of Section 2, with the explicit `eps` bound;
- Section 3, which proves Property 1 for families of good qLDPC codes;
- the open questions on p. 3, including the non-commuting question.

Existence of good qLDPC codes: Panteleev--Kalachev arXiv:2111.03654v2, TeX
`th:qLDPC`, "For every number R in (0,1) and finite field F_q there exists an
explicit family of quantum LDPC codes over F_q with the parameters
[[n, k>=Rn, d=Theta(n)]]_q". The theorem number was not read from the PDF.
