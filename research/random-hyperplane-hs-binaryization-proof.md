Direct proof, given in the claim.  The only inputs are the random-hyperplane
separation probability `theta/pi` for two unit vectors at angle `theta`, the
bounds `1 - cos theta ≤ theta` and `sin t ≥ 2t/pi` on `[0,pi/2]`, the identity
`E_x‖Mx‖^2 = ‖M‖_F^2 / D` for uniform `x` on the sphere, and Cauchy–Schwarz.
