## Definitions

Let `A` be a group acting on a set `Ω` and `P` a group. The **free permutational
wreath product** is `P ≀*_Ω A = (*_{ω∈Ω} P_ω) ⋊ A`, where each `P_ω` is a copy of
`P` (write `p_ω` for the copy of `p`), and `a · p_ω = p_{aω}`.

## Statement

Let `C ≤ A` and let `P` be any group. Write `A *_C (C × P)` for the amalgam of `A`
and `C × P` along `C` (so `P` commutes with `C` and with nothing else a priori).

1. **(W)** `A *_C (C × P) ≅ P ≀*_{A/C} A`, by `a ↦ a` and `(c, p) ↦ p_{ω_0} c`,
   where `ω_0 = C ∈ A/C`. The inverse sends `p_{aC}` to `a p a^{-1}`.
2. **(S)** If `A` acts on `Ω`, `ω_0 ∈ Ω`, `M ≤ A` and `C_M = M ∩ Stab_A(ω_0)`, then
   the subgroup of `P ≀*_Ω A` generated by `M` and `P_{ω_0}` is isomorphic to
   `P ≀*_{M/C_M} M ≅ M *_{C_M} (C_M × P)`.

With `Ω = A/C`: for every `M ≤ A`, `M *_{M∩C} ((M∩C) × P)` embeds in
`A *_C (C × P)`, extending the inclusions of `M` and `P`.
