Let `G` be a countable nonamenable group and let `alpha:G acts on A` be an
amenable action on a unital separable nuclear C-star algebra with nonempty
trace simplex.  Then `T(A)` contains a nonempty compact invariant subset
`X` such that the restricted `G`-action on `X` is minimal and strongly
proximal.  Moreover `X` is not a singleton.

Thus every proposed witness for STW Problem XL contains a genuine
nontrivial `G`-boundary directly inside its trace dynamics.  Boundary-type
trace behavior is not merely a promising construction template: it is
forced by amenability of the C-star action together with nonamenability of
the acting group.
