Let G be any group and s,t,u in G with u in <t>. Define R_a x(g)=x(ga)
on {0,1}^G, with pointwise arithmetic in F_2. The cellular automaton

    F(x)=x+(1+R_u)R_s(x R_t x)

is surjective whenever it is injective. Coincident memory addresses
are permitted. If t has finite odd order, injectivity furthermore
forces either u in <s> or <s,t> finite abelian.

This is a restricted family theorem, not Gottschalk's conjecture for
G. No converse injectivity statement is asserted.
