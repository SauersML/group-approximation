Choose finite or countable Schreier sets, let each group element act by a
permutation with arbitrary pointwise phases, take Hilbert direct sums, and
put a free-ultrafilter or Banach-limit charge on the union of their standard
bases.

This route is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-monomial-near-witness-impossible`.  Forgetting the phases
turns any successful witness of this form into an essentially free
measure-preserving near action, which would make the already nonsofic group
sofic.
