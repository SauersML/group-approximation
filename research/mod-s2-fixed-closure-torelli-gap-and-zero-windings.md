**ESTABLISHED** through `mod-s2-fixed-closure-torelli-gap-and-zero-windings-proof`. Unreviewed; no novelty
claimed (compactness, determinants and compact Lie structure theory, with (ST5) as the one input specific to
`Mod(S_2)`).

**Notation.**
- `M = Mod(S_2)`, `t_s` a separating twist, `omega = exp(2 pi i/3)`, `Lambda = {omega, omega^2}`.
- "Honest" means a homomorphism `M -> U(n)`.
- For a unitary `U`, `s(U)` is the *spread*: the length of the shortest closed arc of the unit circle containing
  the spectrum of `U`.
- For `U` with `||U - I|| < 2`, `kappa(U) = (1/2 pi i) Tr log U` uses the principal logarithm. For honest `rho`,
  `kappa(rho(t_s))` lies in `(1/10)Z`, because `det rho` is a character of `H_1(M) = Z/10`.
- `E_n = { c in U(1) : rho(t_s) = c I for some honest rho : M -> U(n) }`, the exact scalar values in dimension
  `n`. It lies in `mu_(10 n)`.
- `Ebar_(n0)` is the subgroup of `U(1)` generated by `E_1 u ... u E_(n0)`.
- `R_(n0)` is the class of finite direct sums of tensor words `A_1 (x) ... (x) A_l` (`l` arbitrary). Each factor
  `A_i` is an honest representation of dimension at most `n0` or the complex conjugate of one, and different
  words and factors may use different, unrelated representations.

**Statement.**

1. **(FC1) Pinning in bounded dimension.** For each `n` there is `s0(n) > 0` with the following property. If
   `rho : M -> U(n)` is honest and `s(rho(t_s)) < s0(n)`, then the mean eigenvalue direction
   `c = exp(i mu)`, where `mu` is the mean of the eigenphases lifted into the shortest arc, lies in `E_n`. In
   particular every eigenvalue of `rho(t_s)` is within angle `s(rho(t_s))` of `E_n`.
   - `Ebar_(n0)` is a finite group, and each of its elements is an exact value `sigma(t_s) = c I` of some
     honest `sigma`. By (ST5), `Ebar_(n0)` misses `omega` and `omega^2`.
2. **(FC2) Bounded-dimension tensor-sum closure.** Fix `n0`.
   - (i) *Gap.* Put `delta = min over lambda in Lambda of the angle from lambda to Ebar_(n0)`, which is
     positive, and `s0* = min(pi/2, s0(1), ..., s0(n0))`. Then every `W in R_(n0)` satisfies
     ```text
     || W(t_s) - lambda I ||  >=  2 sin( min(delta/3, s0*/2) / 2 )     for lambda in Lambda .
     ```
   - (ii) *Windings, explicit threshold.* Put `L = 10 lcm(1, ..., n0)` and
     `theta0 = min(pi/4, 2 pi/(3L))`. Every `W in R_(n0)` with `||W(t_s) - I|| < 2 sin(theta0/2)` has
     `kappa(W(t_s)) = 0`.
3. **(FC3) Fixed compact closure.** Let `rho0 : M -> U(n0)` be honest, `G = cl rho0(M)` and `g = rho0(t_s)`.
   There is `c(rho0) > 0` such that every continuous finite-dimensional unitary representation `pi != 0` of
   `G` satisfies:
   - (i) `|| pi(g) - lambda I || >= c(rho0)` for `lambda in Lambda`;
   - (ii) if `|| pi(g) - I || < c(rho0)`, then `kappa(pi(g)) = 0`.

   Equivalently, (i) and (ii) hold for every honest representation of `M` of the form `pi o rho0`. These
   include:
   - all subrepresentations and quotients of tensor words in `rho0` and its conjugate, so Schur functors,
     symmetric and exterior powers, and isotypic parts;
   - direct sums of these;
   - their twists by the characters of `M`, after replacing `rho0` by `rho0` plus the ten characters.

   Taking `rho0` to be the direct sum of finitely many honest representations covers anything built
   continuously out of finitely many fixed ones. The constant `c(rho0)` depends on `rho0` through `G`, and no
   uniformity in `rho0` is claimed.
4. **(FC4) Class kill: what a counterexample must look like.** Let `rho_k` be honest representations witnessing
   the failure of `mod-s2-exact-representations-have-an-opnorm-torelli-scalar-gap`
   (`||rho_k(t_s) - lambda I|| -> 0` for some `lambda in Lambda`). Or let them witness the failure of
   `mod-s2-near-identity-torelli-windings-vanish` (`||rho_k(t_s) - I|| -> 0` with `kappa(rho_k(t_s)) != 0`).
   Then:
   - (a) for every `n0`, only finitely many `rho_k` lie in `R_(n0)`;
   - (b) for every honest `rho0`, only finitely many `rho_k` have the form `pi_k o rho0` with `pi_k` a
     continuous representation of `cl rho0(M)`.

   So no fixed finite collection of honest representations, and no tensor-sum construction from
   representations of bounded dimension, produces a witness. Its generators must vary, with unbounded
   "closure complexity": dimension, or the constant `m` or the component group or the semisimple part of
   the closure appearing in the proof.
   - The invariants are the mean eigenphase and the distance of the spectrum from the exact-value group, both
     additive along tensor words and pinned by the determinant grid plus compactness of `Hom(M, U(n))`; and,
     for (b), the commutator-kill and bounded-replacement reduction of the proof.
   - Each member of the killed class dies at the step "a fixed compact group has near-omega
     irreducibles", which forces a representation of `M` of bounded dimension within the same distance of
     `omega`.
5. **(FC5) The M-input is necessary.** The compact group `T^2 x| C_3`, with `A = [[0,-1],[1,-1]]` and
   `g = (sqrt 2, sqrt 3)` in `T^2`, has finite abelianization and no continuous representation sending `g` to
   `omega I`. Yet its 3-dimensional irreducibles `Ind chi_v` have `||pi_v(g) - omega I||` tending to `0`: the
   value is `0.0021` at `v = (-486, -760)` (`torus_c3_near_omega.py`). So (FC3) does not follow from the
   structure of compact groups. It uses (ST5) together with compactness of `Hom(M, U(n))`, which holds because
   `M` is finitely generated, and this fails for continuous representations of a compact group.

**What this changes.**
- *The (J5) "not covered" case is closed parameter by parameter.* Subrepresentations of tensor words in a
  Jones representation `rho_x`, such as symmetric powers, stay at distance at least `c(rho_x) > 0` from
  `omega`. This needs no Schur-module spectrum and no hypothesis on the closure. What stays open there is only
  uniformity in `x`.
- *The (J5) suggestion "mix unrelated families" is dead in two forms.*
  - Tensor-sum mixtures of any representations of bounded dimension obey (FC2), whatever the eigenphase
    spacings.
  - Anything continuous in finitely many fixed representations obeys (FC3).
  - A witness has to be a genuinely varying sequence, for example quantum representations at growing level,
    or a family whose closures have unboundedly many components or growing semisimple part.
- *(Z) side.* The route `mod-s2-instability-via-vanishing-torelli-windings` needs (Z). The stability side needs
  its failure, and by (FC4) that failure can only come from such varying sequences. The threshold of (FC2 ii)
  is explicit.
- *Residual (R-a) of `mod-s2-near-scalar-torelli-closure-dichotomy` is not touched.* Its members are single
  representations with `eps -> 0` along a sequence, so their closures vary. (FC3) says only that the
  Peter-Weyl dual of each one closure is uniformly far from `omega`.
