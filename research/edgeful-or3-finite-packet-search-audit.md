For the four-cap input, the first three cited claims exhaust the three finite
ways to identify the cap coefficients already present in the packet:
identify them independently of cap, keep the cap labels central, or mix the
first two authenticated charts.  These alternatives respectively kill the

prove exact finite-dimensional invisibility, not normalized-HS invisibility;
the statements themselves identify their missing approximate collapse or
affine-Leavitt decoder.  Hence their finite presentations are not the
requested coefficient-link estimate.

branch.  The last two cited no-go theorems show why neither a universal word
lower bound nor exact common-source covariance can turn that frame into the
global link while preserving the intended exact model.  What remains is
explicitly representation-selective: a bound applying to canonical matrix
types but not to the faithful exact tracial model.

These cases are precisely the finite mechanisms currently offered by the
open compiler and its recorded attempts.  Their conjunction proves the
stated reduction to HS promotion without claiming that no future packet or
analytic estimate can solve the route.
