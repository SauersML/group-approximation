Citation import, with the specialization checked by hand.

**Source.** K. Hutchinson, B. Mirzaii, F. Y. Mokari, *The homology of SL_2 of
discrete valuation rings*, arXiv:2007.11159v2 (23 July 2020), §3.2 "Amalgamated
product decomposition". Read 2026-09-17 from the arXiv HTML rendering.
- **Setting.** `F` is a field with a discrete valuation `v`, `A` its valuation
  ring, `𝔪_A` its maximal ideal, `π` a uniformizer. No completeness is assumed.
- **Statement (verbatim).** "SL₂(F) ≅ SL₂(A) *_{Γ₀(𝔪_A)} SL₂(A)", with
  "Γ₀(𝔪_A) := {(a b; c d) ∈ SL₂(A) | c ∈ 𝔪_A}". The second factor is embedded
  through conjugation by a diagonal matrix built from `π`.
- The paper attributes the decomposition to Serre, *Trees*, Chapter II §1.4
  (Ihara's theorem). §3.3 of the same paper states that "GL₂(F) acts on the
  singular complex of the tree T_V without inversion, with a fundamental domain
  consisting of a single edge".

**Specialization.** Take `F = Q`, `v = v_l`, `A = Z_(l)`, `𝔪_A = l Z_(l)`,
`π = l`. For `α = diag(1, l)`,

    α [[a,b],[c,d]] α^-1 = [[a, b/l],[l c, d]],

so `α SL_2(Z_(l)) α^-1 ∩ SL_2(Z_(l))` consists of the matrices of `SL_2(Z_(l))`
with `c in l Z_(l)`, which is `Γ_0(𝔪_A)`. This is the amalgam in the target.

**Index `l+1`.** `SL_2(Z_(l)) -> SL_2(F_l)` is onto: the target is generated by
elementary matrices, which lift. `Γ_0(l)` is the preimage of the upper
triangular subgroup, of index `|P^1(F_l)| = l + 1`. The other factor is the same
by conjugation.

Not reproved here: the Bass--Serre theory behind the source statement.
