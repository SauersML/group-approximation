**ESTABLISHED by citation** (route `brown-finite-p-subgroup-classes-citation`).

Let `p` be a prime and `G` a group. Suppose `G` has a finite-index torsion-free normal subgroup
`H` such that `⊕_n H^n(H;F_p)` is finite. Then `G` has only finitely many conjugacy classes of
finite `p`-subgroups.

Source: K. S. Brown, *Cohomology of Groups*, Lemma IX.13.2. It is imported through the verbatim
statements in Leary–Nucinkis (Invent. Math. 2003, Example 7) and Leary (G&T 2005, introduction).

The lemma is sharp in the prime-power direction. Leary–Nucinkis and Leary build groups of type
VF with infinitely many conjugacy classes of subgroups isomorphic to any finite group `Q` that is
not of prime-power order.
