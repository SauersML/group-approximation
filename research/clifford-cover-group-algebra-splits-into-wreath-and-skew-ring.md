Let `E_S = Vtilde_S semidirect G` be the Kun--Thom Clifford cover with central involution `eps`, and
`W = E_S/<eps>` the Kun--Thom wreath. Then

```text
F_3[E_S]  =  F_3[W]  x  A_S,
```

where `A_S` is the skew group ring of `G` over the `F_3`-algebra generated by lamps `c_x`, `x in X`, with
`c_x^2 = (-1)^(q(x))` and `c_x c_y = (-1)^(b_S(x,y)) c_y c_x`, and `G` permuting sites up to signs, `alpha_g(c_x) = +- c_(gx)`.

`F_3[W]` is stably finite, so `F_3[E_S]` fails direct finiteness iff `A_S` does. By
`stable-finiteness-failure-refutes-surjunctivity`, that failure makes `E_S` not surjunctive.

Proof: artifact Section 3, by the central idempotents `(1 +- eps)/2` in characteristic three, with the
unit padding on the other factor.
