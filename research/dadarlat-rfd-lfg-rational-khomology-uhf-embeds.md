Let `A` be a separable exact residually finite-dimensional C\*-algebra.  If
its rational `K^0`-homology is locally finitely generated in the sense of
Dadarlat, then `A` embeds into a UHF algebra.

Here local finite generation means that `A` has an exhaustive family of
C\*-subalgebras `D` for which the vector spaces

```text
K^0(D) tensor_Z Q
```

are finite-dimensional.

**Established literature input.**  This is Dadarlat, Muenster J. Math. 11
(2018), Theorem 1.1.
