Let `Gamma` contain the synchronized V4 packet injectively, and let
`tau_delta` be its canonical group trace.  The forbidden selector and radical
atoms are nonzero finite group-algebra projections, so faithfulness gives a
strict regular failure mass

```text
q_delta>0.                                               (UVW1)
```

Choose a repetition count `L` satisfying both `(CRV4)` and

```text
(5/4)L q_delta>M_0.                                     (UVW2)
```

There cannot be a fixed family of group-algebra partial isometries and fixed
tag covariance identities which, in every tracial representation of
`Gamma`, have the repeated V4 failure atoms as their source projections and
pairwise disjoint target tag types in ambient capacity `M_0`.

Indeed evaluate the proposed identities in the left regular representation.
All defining-relator and covariance defects vanish.  The disjoint-tag
cross-Gram estimate makes every pair of distinct ranges orthogonal, while
the sum of source traces is `(5/4)Lq_delta`.  Applying
`gram-branching-capacity-with-overlap` with target identity gives

```text
M_0 >= (5/4)Lq_delta,
```

contradicting `(UVW2)`.

Thus `shared-context-disjoint-v4-tagging` must be genuinely matrix-only.  Its
partial isometries or tag types must be selected from finite multiplicity
data after exactification and need not exist in the canonical group factor.
Any proposed proof that writes all of them as fixed compressed group words,
or derives their exact sources and disjoint ranges universally from the
defining relations, has already assumed an impossible conclusion.
