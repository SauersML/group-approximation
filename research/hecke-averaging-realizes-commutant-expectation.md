Let `n >= 3`, `Gamma = SL_n(Z[1/p])`, `Lambda = SL_n(Z)`, and fix

```text
gamma_0 = diag(p, 1, ..., 1, p^(-1)),
Lambda gamma_0 Lambda = w_1 Lambda sqcup ... sqcup w_L Lambda,   (HA1)
```

with fixed coset representatives `w_i in Gamma` (finitely many, since
`Lambda` is commensurated).  Let `(M, tau)` be ANY von Neumann algebra
with faithful normal tracial state (a finite von Neumann algebra with `tau(1) = 1`; the normalizations `|| k ||_2 = 1` below need it), `pi : Gamma -> U(M)` any unitary
representation, and define the lazy Hecke average on `L^2(M)`:

```text
S(x) = (1/2) x + (1/(2L)) sum_(i=1..L) pi(w_i) x pi(w_i)^*.      (HA2)
```

Then:

1. `S` maps `pi(Lambda)`-central vectors to `pi(Lambda)`-central
   vectors, fixes `L^2(pi(Gamma)' cap M)` pointwise, and commutes with
   the trace-preserving conditional expectation `E` onto
   `pi(Gamma)' cap M`.
2. There is `delta_0 = delta_0(n, p) > 0`, independent of
   `(M, tau, pi)` and of the element, such that every unitary
   `k in pi(Lambda)' cap M` satisfies

   ```text
   || S^N(k) - E(k) ||_2 <= (1 - delta_0)^N || k - E(k) ||_2.    (HA3)
   ```

   In particular `E(k) = lim_N S^N(k)` in `|| ||_2`, and the leak
   scalar of `hecke-far-commutator-defect-formula` is

   ```text
   s = || E(k) ||_2^2 = lim_N || S^N(k) ||_2^2,                  (HA4)
   ```

   a limit of explicitly computable trace polynomials in `k` and the
   `L` fixed words `w_i`.

Consequences.  For the higher-rank pair the entire far sector of the
relative-commutant program is generated by iterating one explicit
finite convex combination of inner automorphisms: the
`Gamma`-commutant part of any `Lambda`-central witness is reached at a
geometric rate that never sees the algebra `M`.  What the theorem
deliberately does NOT decide is the value of `s` -- forcing `s = 1` in
matrix ultraproducts is exactly
`no-spherical-leak-in-matrix-ultraproducts`, consistent with row 2 of
`rc3-standard-tools-all-fail-audit` (this is (T) of the COMPLETION
acting on the leak module, not (T) of the discrete subgroup acting on
`L^2(M)`, so the audit's failure mode does not apply to it).  Applied
inside the GNS factor of an enemy character of the carrier
(`rcc-carrier-character-face-compression`), it turns the enemy's
`t`-unitary into a geometric limit of Hecke averages there as well.
