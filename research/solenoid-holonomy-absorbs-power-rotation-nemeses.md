**ESTABLISHED.**  There is a finite Connes-embeddable tracial von Neumann
algebra `M`, a Haar unitary `U in M`, and, simultaneously for every nonzero
integer `k` and every root of unity `zeta`, a unitary `T_(k,zeta) in M` with

```text
T_(k,zeta) U T_(k,zeta)^*=zeta U^k.                    (SHA1)
```

Consequently no countable approximation-specific nemesis library whose
only contact with a named monodromy word is through finitely many conjugacy
equations of the form `(SHA1)` covers all CE extension types.  The one type
of `U` above extends every gadget in the library simultaneously.

## Construction

Let `K=Q-hat` be the compact Pontryagin dual of the discrete additive group
`Q`, with Haar probability measure, and write `chi_r` for the character
indexed by `r in Q`.  Put `U=chi_1`.  It is Haar because `chi_1` has infinite
image.

Multiplication by every `k!=0` is an automorphism of `Q`; its dual is a
Haar-preserving automorphism of `K` carrying `chi_1` to `chi_k=U^k` (up to
the harmless inverse convention).  For every root of unity `zeta`, choose
`a_zeta in K` with `chi_1(a_zeta)=zeta`; translation by `a_zeta` carries
`U` to a scalar multiple `zeta U`.

Let `A` be the countable subgroup of `K` generated by all the `a_zeta` and
their images under the power automorphisms.  The countable affine group

```text
Lambda=A semidirect Q^times                              (SHA2)
```

is metabelian, hence amenable, and acts measure-preservingly on `K`.  In

```text
M=L^infinity(K) semidirect Lambda,                       (SHA3)
```

the canonical implementing unitaries give `(SHA1)`.  Since `Lambda` is
amenable, `(SHA3)` is an amenable finite von Neumann algebra and therefore
Connes embeddable.

## Consequence for Schur/Reynolds readouts

The Haar type also absorbs every finite menu of roots, powers, rational
phase choices, and their conjugacy compositions.  Thus diagonalizing the
actual finite monodromy and choosing a power or phase that its finite
spectrum fails cannot yield an ultraproduct obstruction: the approximator
may use this diffuse CE type instead.

The predicate Reynolds gap is not contradicted.  It detects the rank of a
noncommutative adjoint fixed algebra, whereas `(SHA1)` only tests the scalar
spectral dynamics of one holonomy unitary.  A viable bespoke readout must
therefore tie a raw lift to the packet inclusion itself, through an additive
Reynolds return or a charged proper-corner section.  `W^2=J` orients such a
lift after it is tied down, but adjoining a fresh existential lift does not
perform that incidence step.
