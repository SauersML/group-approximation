Let `A` be a unital separable C-star algebra.  Suppose that, for an arbitrary
ordinal `gamma`, it has a continuous increasing ideal filtration
`(I_alpha)_(alpha<=gamma)` such that

* `I_0=0` and `I_gamma=A`;
* `I_beta=closure(union_(alpha<beta) I_alpha)` at every nonzero limit
  ordinal `beta<=gamma`; and
* every successor quotient `I_(alpha+1)/I_alpha` is Z-stable.

No countability or cofinality hypothesis on `gamma` is required.

Then `A` is Z-stable and is generated by one element.

Consequently, the conclusion holds when `A` is unital, separable, nowhere
scattered, has finite nuclear dimension, and admits such a filtration with
nonzero simple successor quotients.  In particular, every unital separable
nowhere-scattered finite-nuclear-dimension C-star algebra with finitely many
ideals is singly generated.  No real-rank-zero or stable-rank-one hypothesis
is required.
