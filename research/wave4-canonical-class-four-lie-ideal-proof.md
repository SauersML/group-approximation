The universal class-four quotient is a finite seven-group generated by
order-seven vertex elements. Lazard's correspondence makes it an F7 Lie
algebra. In the free class-four algebra on the six vertex generators,
the exact vertex relations, marked face differences, and centrality
relations generate a rank389 ideal in dimension406. The central vector
lies in that ideal. The artifact gives the full algebraic reduction,
independently reviewed exact algorithm, and its limited scope.
