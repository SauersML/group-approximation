This is Takesaki's theorem that the spatial/minimal tensor product of two
simple C-star algebras is simple.  A modern published account is M. Rordam,
*Structure and classification of C-star algebras*, Proceedings of the
International Congress of Mathematicians 2006, Volume II, Section 2.3, which
explicitly attributes the result to Takesaki.  The original source is M.
Takesaki, *On the cross-norm of the direct product of C-star algebras*,
Tohoku Mathematical Journal 16 (1964), the corollary to Theorem 2.

Only this simplicity statement is imported here; no tensor-purity assertion
is taken from the literature.
