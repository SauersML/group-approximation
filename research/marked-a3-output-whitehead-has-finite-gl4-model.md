**ESTABLISHED FIRST OPPOSITE-ROOT DIRECT-CELL FENCE.**  Extend the direct
word `(DAC2)` by

```text
L=x_41(q),
W_q=x_14(q)Lx_14(q).                                  (MAW1)
```

Since `q^2=q`, direct multiplication on the `(1,4)` block gives

```text
W_q=[[1-q,q],[q,1-q]],
W_q^2=1,
W_q x_14(q) W_q^(-1)=x_41(q).                         (MAW2)
```

Thus `(MAW1)--(MAW2)` are a genuine payload-attached opposite-root
occurrence: the positive associator output itself is the first and third
letter of the Whitehead.

The complete bounded cell still has an exact finite marked model.  In
`GL_4(F_2)`, put

```text
X=x_12(1),       M=x_23(1),       Y=x_34(1),
Z=x_14(1),       L=x_41(1),       W=ZLZ.               (MAW3)
```

Then

```text
[X,[M,Y]]=Z,
[[X,M],Y]=Z,                                           (MAW4)
```

while replacing `X` by the identity gives the tagged edge-zero output.
Moreover `W` is the permutation matrix swapping coordinates `1,4`, so

```text
W^2=1,                 WZW^(-1)=L.                    (MAW5)
```

Equations `(MAW3)--(MAW5)` model every associator, edge-zero, opposite-root,
Whitehead-factorization, and output-conjugacy row exactly, and `Z!=1`.
The finite group generated by these matrices is a subgroup of
`GL_4(F_2)`.  Its left regular representation therefore has zero defect on
the whole cell and

```text
||lambda(Z)-1||_2=sqrt(2).                             (MAW6)
```

This specialization is possible because the private coefficients have
already been multiplied to the single scalar corner `q` before the
opposite-root word reads them.  The Whitehead sees only `Z`, not the separate
row and tagged-column occurrences.  Hence the smallest output-attached
opposite root does not break the finite escape.  A viable next cell must put
an individual private arm into an opposite-root coefficient before the
product `aC=q` is formed, and must simultaneously prevent the edge arm from
being specialized to zero.  No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, approximation
theorem, trace hypothesis, or literature result is used.
