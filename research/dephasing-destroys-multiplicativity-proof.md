Direct proof, given in the claim.  Double stochasticity of `P_U` is the row
and column normalization of a unitary; failure of multiplicativity is the
explicit witness `U = V = (1/√2)[[1,1],[1,-1]]`, where `P_{UV} = P_I = I` but
`P_U P_V` is the uniform matrix.  No external input.
