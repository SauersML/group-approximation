**Source.** S. P. Fisher and P. Sánchez-Peralta, *Division rings for group
algebras of virtually compact special groups and 3-manifold groups*,
arXiv:2303.08165 (v1 14 Mar 2023, v4 20 Feb 2025),
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08165>.

**Theorem 1.3**, as read from the arXiv HTML rendering of v4. For a torsion-free
group `G` and a division ring `k`:
1. if `G` is virtually the fundamental group of a compact special cube
   complex, then `k*G` embeds into a Linnell division ring, unique up to
   isomorphism;
2. if `G` is the fundamental group of a compact 3-manifold, then `k*G` embeds
   into a division ring, and the embedding can be made Hughes-free when `G` is
   locally indicable.

**Corollary 1.5.** If `G` is torsion-free and the fundamental group of a
3-manifold, then `k*G` is a domain for every division ring `k` and every
crossed product structure.

The abstract states the 3-manifold case for finitely generated torsion-free
3-manifold groups, and it states that the embedding holds for every division
ring `k`, so in every characteristic. The graph uses only the untwisted group
algebra `k[G]` over a field.
