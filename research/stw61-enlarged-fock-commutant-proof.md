Represent z by V=sign(R_1+R_1*) and v_i by L_i V on full Fock
space. The sign unitary commutes with L_i for i>=2 and with
L_1+L_1*, so the entire image of K_n commutes with V. The
distinguished projection and the defect map to L_1L_1* and
the vacuum projection; each has commutator norm one with V.
This proves the maximal norm for all unitary conjugates and
the lower distance bound 1/2. The scalar element (1/2)1 gives
the exact upper distance bound.

The prefix code {1,21,22} realizes T3 faithfully inside T2,
with binary Fock space equal to two copies of ternary Fock
space. Full unital free-product inclusion transports the norm
and distance conclusions isometrically, without extending
the constructed representation to T2. The preceding claim
supplies the original exponential family's escape from proper
coefficient corners.
