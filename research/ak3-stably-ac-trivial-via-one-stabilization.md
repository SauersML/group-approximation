AK(3) = ⟨x, y | x^3y^{−4}, xyxy^{−1}x^{−1}y^{−1}⟩ is a balanced presentation of the
trivial group in rank 2. The premise with m = 2 makes its one-stabilization
AC-trivial. Then (AC4), the AC path and (AC5), as in
`stable-ac-conjecture-via-one-stabilization`, show AK(3) is stably AC-trivial.

By `shehper-z-conjecture-iff-one-stabilization-ac`, the rank-2 premise is exactly
Conjecture `conj:general_Z` on 3 generators.
