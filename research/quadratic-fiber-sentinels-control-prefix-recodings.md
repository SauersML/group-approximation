The Q^2 fiber above Q^2 z, for z of finite total triple weight
k, has 7^k points. A Q^2-centralizer preserves that weight, forcing
balance of every finite prefix replacement.

For finitely many diagrams with prefix weights at most b, the
first letter crossing cumulative weight b can never be rewritten.
Every word acts on the suffix from that boundary by one common
A_4 multiplier, independent of its labels. Test the action on
total weight b+16 by appending padding and one variable weight-eight
sentinel. The weight-eight class detects every A_4 element, so
triviality on this stratum forces triviality of the common multiplier
and prefix action. Density proves faithful restriction.

The stratum is finitely many orthants: positive-label words are
finite in number, and U gaps are free coordinates. Splitting and
merging alter the ambient orthant ranks but preserve the number
of free suffix gaps on every piece. Thus the restricted action
lies in a known elementary amenable piecewise isometry group.
Directed unions close the entire finite-prefix construction.
