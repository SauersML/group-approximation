Let `G` have Kazhdan's property `(T)`. Suppose there is a sofic embedding

```text
pi : G -> product_U Sym(n)
```

whose centralizer in the metric ultraproduct acts ergodically on the
associated Loeb probability space. Then `G` is LEF.

If `G` is also finitely presented, then `G` is residually finite.

Equivalently, every finitely presented sofic Kazhdan group which is not
residually finite has nonergodic centralizer action for every sofic embedding.
Thus the Hayes--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli ergodic-centralizer conjecture cannot
hold for such a group, should one exist.
