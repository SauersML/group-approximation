Let tau be an injective CA on A^G and let H be the subgroup generated by
its finite memory. The same rule acts independently on each left coset
of H, so its induced CA on A^H is injective. If H is finite, this induced
map is surjective by finite counting. If H is infinite, the open
fiber-size premise supplies a cellular decoder with a countable fiber
over a transitive configuration. The established split-fiber theorem
forces this decoder to be bijective, hence its section tau on A^H is
bijective. Choosing preimages independently on the cosets proves
surjectivity on A^G. Section 6 gives the details and the converse.

The universal countable-fiber prerequisite remains open. This route
records a rigorous conditional implication and does not close the goal.
