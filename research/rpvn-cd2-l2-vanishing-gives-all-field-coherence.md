Let `G` be a finitely generated group residually in the class of groups
which are both poly-`Z` and virtually nilpotent. Assume
`cd_Q(G)<=2`. Then

`b_2^(2)(G)=0`

if and only if `G` is free-by-(poly-`Z` and virtually nilpotent).

When these equivalent conditions hold, `G` is coherent and `k[G]` is a
coherent ring for every field `k`.
