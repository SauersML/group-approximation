For a fixed countable group, all free p.m.p. actions have equal cost if and only if every measure-preserving equivariant factor map between free actions preserves cost. One direction is trivial; the other applies factor invariance to the two projections of the product a x b of arbitrary free actions, which gives C(a) = C(a x b) = C(b). The equivalence itself does not need finite generation, although the finite routing-plan characterisations built on it do.

Status records a written deduction, not independent validation.
