**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, swarm-0917-w14-w14-bh-follow, 2026-09-19; not
independently reviewed). Route: `ore-domains-in-free-crossed-products-grow-like-the-group-proof`.

Let `F` be any field, `𝒢` a Hausdorff ample groupoid that is **principal**
(trivial isotropy) with compact unit space `X`, and `A = A_F(𝒢)` its Steinberg
algebra. Let `C ⊆ A` be a subalgebra, unital or not (so corners `pAp` are
allowed), which is a **left Ore domain**, for instance a commutative domain.

1. **Orbit point.** Some `x ∈ X` has `Ann_C(e_x) = 0` in the faithful module
   `F^(X)`: the map `c ↦ c·e_x` is injective on `C`.
2. **Growth bound.** If `C` is generated by a finite set whose supports lie in a
   compact open `K ⊆ 𝒢` with `X ⊆ K`, and the unit of `C` (if any) is among
   the generators, then for that `x` and all `n`, `dim_F C_(≤n) ≤ |K^n ∩ s^(-1)(x)|`. Here
   `C_(≤n)` is the span of the products of 1 to `n` generators.
3. **Free actions.** For `𝒢 = X ⋊ G` with `G` finitely generated acting freely,
   `dim C_(≤n) ≤ |S^n|` for a finite `S ⊆ G`. So `growth(C) ≼ growth(G)`, and
   `GKdim C ≤ d` when `G` has polynomial growth of degree `d`. The same holds in
   `M_k(A)`, with the bound `k·|S^n|`.
4. **Tensor coefficients.** For any `F`-algebra `R` and a free action,
   `GKdim C ≤ d + GKdim R'` for an Ore domain `C ⊆ (LC(X,F) ⋊ G) ⊗ R`. Here `R'`
   is the subalgebra of `R` generated by the values of the coefficient functions
   of the generators of `C`.
5. **Consequences.**
   - `F[Z^(d+1)]` embeds, unitally or not, in no subalgebra, corner or matrix
     algebra of `LC(X,F) ⋊ Z^d` with `Z^d` acting freely on a compact
     zero-dimensional `X`.
   - For torsion-free nilpotent `H`, an embedding `F[H] → M_k(LC(X,F) ⋊ Z^d)`
     forces `deg(H) ≤ d`. Over `Z^2` only `Z` and `Z^2` survive; every
     nonabelian `H` has `deg ≥ 4`.
   - For a free minimal `Z^2`-SFT, or any free `Z^2` Cantor system `Ω`, the
     algebra `B_Ω` and its corners contain no `F_2[Z^3]`.
   - An embedding `F_2[Z^4] → B_Ω ⊗ L_∂` needs `L_∂`-coefficients that
     generate an algebra of GKdim at least 2. Coefficients in `F[u^±]`,
     `M_n(F[u^±])` or the Boolean core `LC(∂F_d)` never suffice.

The bound in 3 is sharp: `δ_(e_1), δ_(e_2)` span `F[Z^2] ⊆ LC(X,F) ⋊ Z^2`.

**Where it stops.** It does not apply to `L_∂` or `L(1,n)` themselves: their
groupoids have isotropy (they are not principal) and the source fibres of `K^n`
grow exponentially.
