Let `G` act on a commutative field `K` by automorphisms, and let `R = K ⋊ G` be the skew group ring (`α = 1`).
For a subgroup `H` and a finite set `E ⊆ K`, write `K^H(H·E)` for the subfield generated by the `H`-orbit of `E`
over the fixed field `K^H`.

**ESTABLISHED 2026-09-17** by [[birational-skew-group-rings-have-the-rank-condition-proof]].

**Theorem.**
1. **(Finite type.)** If `K` is finitely generated over its prime field, then `K` has finite-field point models over
   `G` in the sense of `finite-field-point-models-give-the-rank-condition`. This holds for every group `G` and every
   action.
2. **(Rank condition.)** Suppose that for every finitely generated `H ≤ G` and every finite `E ⊆ K`, the field
   `K^H(H·E)` is finitely generated over `K^H`. Then `K ⋊ G` has the rank condition.

**Examples covered by 2.**
- Every group of `k`-automorphisms of a finitely generated extension `K/k`, that is, every group of birational
  automorphisms of a variety over any field `k`. This includes:
  - Cremona groups acting on `k(x_1..x_n)`;
  - all projective linear and Möbius actions, such as `Q(x) ⋊ PSL_2(Z)`;
  - automorphism groups of function fields of curves and surfaces.
- Coefficients algebraic over `K^G`. Orbits are then finite, which recovers survival case (b) with no finite
  quotient argument.
- Fields algebraic over the prime field, with any action.
- Any action on a field of finite type over the prime field.

**Consequences for `some-field-crossed-product-fails-the-rank-condition`.**
- **Exact failing step.** With `α = 1`, a witness needs a finitely generated `H` and finitely many coefficients `E`
  such that `K^H(H·E)` is not finitely generated over `K^H`. Typically the orbit of a coefficient is algebraically
  independent over `K^H`, or generates infinite transcendence degree.
- Combined with the earlier point-model theorem, `H` must also be nonamenable and not bi-orderable. If the orbit field
  is a Bernoulli field, `H` must not be sofic.
- The surviving coefficient regimes with `α = 1` are fields of infinite transcendence degree carrying nontrivial
  algebraic relations along the orbit. An example is the function field of an `H`-invariant prime of
  `F_p[x_g : g ∈ H]`, an algebraic subshift. This theorem gives no point models there, since Lang–Weil needs
  finite type.
  The other surviving route is a nontrivial cocycle `α`, as in the ternary Leavitt route.
- Via `twisted-linear-profile-deficits-are-rank-condition-failures`, no skew group ring with birational coefficients
  yields a twisted linear profile with `Phi -> 0`. A profile-level obstruction to maximal Bernoulli Rokhlin entropy
  must use coefficient fields of infinite transcendence degree.

**Scope.** The conclusion is the rank condition only, not stable finiteness. If `G` acts non-faithfully, `K ⋊ G`
contains the group algebra of the kernel, and stable finiteness there is the open Kaplansky problem in characteristic
`p`.
