**ESTABLISHED, INTERNAL, NO SMALL CANCELLATION, NO PROPERTY `(T)`.**
Let `E` be a finitely presented torsion-free group generated by nontrivial
elements `e_1,...,e_n`, and let `1 != d in E`.  Put

```text
P = < E, t_1,...,t_n, c, z |
      t_i e_i t_i^-1 = e_i^2   (1<=i<=n),
      c t_i c^-1   = t_i^2     (1<=i<=n),
      d z d^-1     = z^2,
      z c z^-1     = c^2 >.                                   (EMB)
```

Then:

1. the natural map `E -> P` is injective;
2. `P` is torsion-free and finitely presented;
3. `normalClosure_P(d) = P`.

`P` is built by `n+1` HNN extensions over infinite-cyclic or free associated
subgroups followed by one amalgam over a free group of rank two with the
gadget `K=<x,y,z | x z x^-1=z^2, z y z^-1=y^2>` (`x=d`, `y=c`).  Killing `d`
kills `z`, then `c`, then every `t_i`, then every `e_i`.

Consequence for the torsion-free manuscript lane: the normal-generation
clause `(TFR3)` of `internal-torsion-free-single-word-router` costs nothing
once surjectivity is dropped, and `mf-radical-saturation-along-any-homomorphism`
shows surjectivity is never used.  What remains is exactly the passage to a
two-generated acylindrically hyperbolic target, isolated in
`two-generated-acylindrical-normal-generation-upgrade`.

Every valid output still contains `BS(1,2)=<d,z>`; this matches
`torsion-free-router-forces-nonhyperbolic-output`.
