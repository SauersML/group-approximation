Each scalar pair in `(HRT1)` has `P_iQ_i=0`, so the exact-extension clause
of the proposed router gives a zero-defect ordinary-group representation
with the displayed interface values and with marked central character `-1`.
Tensor the three representations.  Group words and relators evaluate
componentwise under tensor products, hence every routing and conjugacy
relator remains exact; the product central character is still `-1`.

The interface reflections multiply to `(A,B)=(-1,-1)`.  Applying the
universal zero-defect routing statement gives `(HRT3)`, while tensor closure
of the conjugacy relator gives `URU^*=S`.  A unitary cannot conjugate the
scalar `-I` to `I`.  This proves the obstruction independently of the
number or nature of the gadget's auxiliary generators.
