`explicit-sixty-relator-nonsofic-group` exhibits the group `P` with seven generators and sixty
relators, and proves it finitely presented, simple and not sofic. That is the target.

This route differs from `fp-simple-nonsofic-via-leavitt-unit-group` in which finite
presentation it uses. That route takes Khanh's Theorem 6.1 (through Krstić--McCool). This one
uses an explicit relator list, whose completeness comes from the in-repo spare-index theorem
and Khanh's Theorem 5.4.
