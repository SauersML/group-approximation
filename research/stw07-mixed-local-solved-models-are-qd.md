title: Mixed local traceless and faithful-UCT models give a quasidiagonal algebra
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/stw07-status-and-stable-character-reduction-2026-08-30.md
---

Let `A` be a separable unital nuclear stably finite C*-algebra.  Suppose
every finite subset of `A` is arbitrarily well approximated in norm by a
unital separable C*-subalgebra `B subset A` satisfying at least one of:

1. `B` is traceless;
2. `B` is nuclear, satisfies the UCT, and has a faithful tracial state.

Then `A` is quasidiagonal.  The type of local model may vary with the finite
set.  No faithful trace or UCT hypothesis is imposed on `A` itself.
