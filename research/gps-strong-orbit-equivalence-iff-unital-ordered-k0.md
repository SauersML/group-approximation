**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`-citation` route). Let `(X,T)` and `(X',T')` be minimal Cantor systems. Then `(X,T)` and
`(X',T')` are strongly orbit equivalent if and only if `K^0(X,T) = C(X,Z)/(1−T_*)C(X,Z)`, with cone
`{[f] : f ≥ 0}` and unit `[1]`, is isomorphic to `K^0(X',T')` as a unital ordered group.

Strong orbit equivalence means: an orbit-preserving homeomorphism whose two orbit cocycles each have at most one point
of discontinuity. If a cocycle is continuous, the systems are flip conjugate. Entropy is not an invariant.
