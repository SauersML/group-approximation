Citation import, not a reproof. Seth Hulbert and Matthew C. B. Zaremsky,
*Word length, Morse theory, and Vietoris-Rips complexes*, arXiv:2608.25614
(v1, 26 August 2026).

- Theorem B, as rendered in v1: "If Γ is triangle-free then VR_2(A_Γ) (using
  the standard word metric) is contractible."
- Theorem A (their criterion): "If D_t(S) is (n-|S|-1)-connected for all
  non-empty finite {1}≠S⊆G with diam(S)≤t such that len is constant on S, then
  VR_t(G) is (n-1)-connected." Here `D_t(S)` is the descending coface link
  (sets `S' ⊋ S` of diameter `≤ t` whose minimal word length is smaller).
- Remark in the paper: "A natural prediction in general is that for n the
  maximum size of a clique in Γ, equivalently the cohomological dimension of
  A_Γ, VR_t(A_Γ) should be contractible for all t≥n."

Read from the arXiv abstract page and the HTML rendering of v1 on 2026-09-13.
The paper is an unrefereed preprint, and the proof was not re-derived here.
