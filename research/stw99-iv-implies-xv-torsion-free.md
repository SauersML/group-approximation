CONDITIONAL SCOPE: this route establishes Problem XV on the
torsion-free-`K`-theory locus.  Given Problem IV, any separable
nuclear `D` with `K_*(D)` torsion-free satisfies the Künneth formula
against itself; the required solid-ring theorem then gives
`K_1(D) = 0` and `K_0(D) ≅ Z[J^{-1}]`, which is the `K`-theory of the
known strongly self-absorbing algebra `M_{J^∞}` (or of `Z`/`O_∞` when
`J = ∅`, `O_2` when `K_0 = 0` — the zero ring being the degenerate
solid case).  A full solution of XV additionally needs the
`K_0`-torsion case, where the same theorem confines any counterexample
to solid rings with torsion.
