Let alpha(z) = diag(z, 1) generate pi_1 U(2), and let omega: SU(2) ⊂ U(2).
Then

    <alpha, <alpha, <alpha, omega>>> = omega o eta_3 o eta_4 o eta_5 = 6 nu' ≠ 0
    in pi_6 U(2) ≅ pi_6 S^3 ≅ Z/12.

On T^3 x S^3, take the unitaries alpha o s_1, alpha o s_2, alpha o s_3 and
omega o z. Their nested commutator c_4 = [u_3, [u_2, [u_1, v]]] is not in
U_0(C(T^3 x S^3, M_2)).

The excess of this block is 6 − 2·2 = 2. By the excess bound, the unitary
component group of C(T^3 x S^3, M_2) therefore has nilpotency class exactly
four.

The next nesting gives eta_3^4 = 0, so this family of seeds stops at four.

eta_3^3 = 6 nu' is used without a re-verified theorem number.
