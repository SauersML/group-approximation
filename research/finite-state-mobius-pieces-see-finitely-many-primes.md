**ESTABLISHED (2026-09-18)** through
`finite-state-mobius-pieces-see-finitely-many-primes-proof`. Lane proof,
elementary (Baire category and Möbius rigidity).

**Setting.**
- `T_p` is the `p`-ary tree, and `∂T_p = Z_p`, as in
  `pgl2-q-in-rover-nekrashevych-group-of-rational-iwahori`.
- A homeomorphism `h` of `Z_p` is **locally Möbius at `z`** if it agrees with some
  `m in PGL_2(Q_p)` on a neighborhood of `z`. Such an `m` is a **piece** of `h`.
  `U_h` is the open set of such `z`.

**Statement.**
1. **Pieces control Möbius agreement.** Let `Λ = <h_1, ..., h_k>` be a group of
   homeomorphisms of `Z_p` with every `U_(h_i)` dense, and let all pieces of all
   `h_i` lie in a subgroup `Δ <= PGL_2(Q_p)`. If `γ in Λ` agrees with
   `m in PGL_2(Q_p)` on a nonempty open set, then `m in Δ`.
2. **Finite-state pieces.** Let `h in Aut(T_p)` be finite-state.
   - Every piece of `h` is affine, `z -> a z + b`.
   - There is `N >= 1` such that all pieces of `h` lie in `Aff(Z[1/N])`, a
     finitely generated group.
   - A Möbius map that is not affine agrees with no finite-state automorphism
     on any cylinder.
3. **Consequence for Γ_p.** Let `Γ_0 <= PGL_2(Q)` be finitely generated and act
   on `Z_p` by tree automorphisms, for example `Γ_p(1)`. Let `h_1, ..., h_k` be
   finite-state, each locally Möbius on a dense open set, and put
   `Λ = <Γ_0, h_1, ..., h_k>`. Then:
   - there is `N` with every Möbius map that agrees with an element of `Λ` on an
     open set lying in `PGL_2(Z[1/N])`;
   - `Λ` contains `d_q` or `t_(1/q) = (z -> z + 1/q)` only for primes `q | N`;
   - `Λ` does not contain `Γ_p`, nor the translations by `Z_(p)`.

   This holds whether or not `Λ` is self-similar or finitely presented.

**Reading.**
- A finite-state generator that does the work of infinitely many `d_q` must be
  nowhere locally Möbius on some nonempty open set.
- Digit substitutions by a non-affine permutation of `F_p` are finite-state
  maps of this wild kind. Whether wild finite-state maps can produce infinitely
  many dilations is not examined.
- Let `Λ ⊇ Γ_p` be finitely generated by elements that are each locally Möbius
  on a dense open set. Then item 1 says the pieces of the generators generate a
  subgroup of `PGL_2(Q_p)` that is not finitely generated. So some generator has
  infinitely many pieces, involving infinitely many primes.
