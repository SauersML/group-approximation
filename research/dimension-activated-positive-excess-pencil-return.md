**OPEN ADAPTIVE RECTANGULAR TARGET.**  Construct a finitely presented group
`Gamma`, an infinite-order word `w`, a constant `alpha>0`, and a finite
self-similar controller with the following semantics.  For every sufficiently
accurate canonical-trace matrix microstate `U in U(d)^S`, the controller
selects finite integers and a model-dependent matrix pencil

```text
p(U)>q(U),                 (p(U)-q(U))/p(U)>=alpha,
A_U:(C^d)^(p(U))->(C^d)^(q(U)).                        (DAP1)
```

If `P_U` is the exact orthogonal projection onto `ker A_U`, the group
relator defect must imply

```text
||(I_(p(U)) tensor (w(U)^2-1))P_U||_(2,p(U)d)=o(1),    (DAP2)
```

with a modulus independent of `d`, `p(U)`, the selected self-similar depth,
and simulated runtime.

No fixed group-algebra factorization is requested.  The choice of
`p(U),q(U),A_U` may inspect finite multiplicity coordinates and may be
nonfunctorial under passage to the regular representation.  This is
necessary: a fixed pencil, a bounded menu, or any exact factorization of the
whole kernel is ruled out by
`fixed-rectangular-kernel-return-factorization-is-impossible`.

The relative excess in `(DAP1)` is also necessary for rank--nullity alone.
`adaptive-rectangular-kernel-column-extraction` gives an explicit cyclic
shift calibration in which `p(d)>q(d)` and the entire kernel is returned
exactly, but `(p-q)/p->0` and every fixed canonical moment is correct.
Consequently a one-cell Hilbert-hotel defect cannot merely move to later
depths; the controller must replicate the defect extensively before return.

The target is sufficient without any packet alignment or spectral gap.
Rank--nullity gives `tr(P_U)>=alpha`; the dimension-free column extraction
then produces a contraction in the original `d`-dimensional model with mass
at least `alpha/2` and square-return defect `o(1)`.  The Fejer endpoint
`unnamed-positive-density-fixed-space-is-terminal` supplies the canonical
contradiction.

## Attempts and surviving implementation boundary

- A Leavitt/Toeplitz self-similarity may generate the unbounded rectangular
  index set, but exact self-similar identities are tracially functorial and
  cannot prove `(DAP2)` on their own.
- A dimension tape may choose the first failed finite-capacity instruction,
  but one missing row has relative excess `1/p(U)` and is invisible by the
  calibration above.  It must drive a constant-rate replication or code.
- The required advance is therefore exactly a runtime-independent local
  compiler from one dimension-triggered failure to an extensive family of
  returned rows.  Existing adaptive tape/PCP frontiers provide the right
  control interface; the rectangular endpoint reduces their payload from a
  supercritical Clifford table to one positive-rate kernel return.
