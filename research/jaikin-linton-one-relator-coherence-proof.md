Use Andrei Jaikin-Zapirain and Marco Linton, *On the coherence of one-relator
groups and their group algebras*, Annals of Mathematics (2) 201 (2025),
909--959, doi:10.4007/annals.2025.201.3.4, Theorem 1.1(1), proved as
Theorem 4.7.

They first prove homological coherence for groups of cohomological dimension
two with the relevant non-positive-immersion or locally-indicable hypotheses
(Theorem 1.2).  One-relator groups have vanishing second `L^2`-Betti number and
are virtually torsion-free and virtually locally indicable, so every finitely
generated subgroup has type `FP_2`.

The Magnus--Moldavanskii hierarchy gives a finite sequence ending in a free
product of a free group and a finite cyclic group, with each preceding stage an
HNN extension over the next.  Their promotion theorem for groups acting on
trees with coherent vertex stabilizers (Theorems 1.3 and 4.6) converts
homological coherence along this hierarchy into ordinary coherence.  Hence
every finitely generated subgroup of every one-relator group is finitely
presented.  QED
