For a canonical group embedding `theta`, distinct `g,h` satisfy

```text
||theta(g)-theta(h)||_2^2
 =2-2 Re tau(theta(g^(-1)h))=2.                         (PUA2)
```

Thus the image of the uncountable group `PU(M)` is an uncountable discrete
metric subspace and is not separable.  Dowerk--Thom's target hypothesis is
therefore absent.  Finally the identity into the same abstract group with
the discrete metric supplies an explicit discontinuous homomorphism to a
complete nonseparable SIN group, proving that completeness and bi-invariance
cannot replace Polishness.
