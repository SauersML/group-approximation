For B=V_(59,2)(<a>), let epsilon:B->Z/2 be prefix-table permutation
parity and let chi be the integer charge. Then

    R_nu <= B_0=ker(epsilon-(chi mod 2)) < B,       [B:B_0]=2.

In particular R_nu is not all of B: a pure prefix transposition
is outside R_nu. Equality with B_0 is not proved.

The smaller ambient B_0 is finitely presented, chi:B_0->Z is onto,
and its kernel is finitely generated. Thus B_0 can replace B in
the symmetric fiber product and index-two germ-host construction,
still giving a finitely presented ambient group for Q. Finite
presentation of R_nu, Q, or E_nu remains unproved.
