Apply Higman's embedding theorem to embed the finitely generated recursively
presented group `G` into a finitely presented group `H`.  If `H` were
hyperlinear, restriction of its finite-set unitary approximations to `G`
would make `G` hyperlinear.  Therefore nonhyperlinearity of `G` forces
nonhyperlinearity of `H`.
