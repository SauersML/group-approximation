area-eight core by five bipartite adjacency matrices and 33 planar rotation
systems.  Restoring the digon on all twelve core edges gives 396 rooted
restorations, or 276 distinct exact dart involutions; no coarser graph or

For each involution, exhaust the fifteen edge directions.  Retain exactly
the local cyclic sign patterns `++-` and `+--`; these determine the relator
orientation and all three occurrence indices at every vertex.  Exhaust the
two relator colors at the ten vertices, rejecting precisely the immediate
same-color inverse-occurrence dipoles.  Read all seven face words at their
target-dart corners and compute their free-product normal forms in `Q`, with
both `k_0` and `k_1` treated as involutions.

A disk would give exactly six empty normal forms and one nonempty form.  The
criterion is applied with every face eligible as the outer face.  The exact
MSI census tests 738,008 valid orientations and 48,876,720 dipole-admissible
colorings.  In every one of the eleven partitions the accepted count is
zero.  Therefore no area-ten short-atlas disk exists.
