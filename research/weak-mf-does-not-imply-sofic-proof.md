Fix a Kun--Thom Theorem E pair `Gamma < G` and put `D = G *_Gamma G`.

1. `G` is countable and residually finite, and `Gamma` is a subgroup, so
   `symmetric-double-weak-mf` gives an injective homomorphism of `D` into the
   unitary group of a norm matrix corona.
2. `Gamma` is infranormal and not normal in `G`, and both have property (T),
   so `kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic` says `D` is not sofic.
3. `G` has property (T), hence is finitely generated, and `D` is generated by
   the two vertex copies of `G`.

So `D` is a finitely generated operator-MF group that is not sofic.
