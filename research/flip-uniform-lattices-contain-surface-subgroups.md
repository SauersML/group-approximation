Every uniform lattice in a center-free complex semisimple Lie group contains a
surface group. More generally, every uniform lattice in a center-free semisimple
Lie group without compact factor that has an `sl_2`-triple with compact centralizer
satisfying the flip assumption contains a surface group. That includes every
rank-one group except `SO(1,2n)`.

J. Kahn, F. Labourie and S. Mozes, *Surface groups in uniform lattices of some
semi-simple groups*, arXiv:1805.10189. TeX source `SGL.tex`, fetched on MSI
2026-09-13.

- **Theorem A** (l.141--143), verbatim: "Let $\ms G$ be a center free, complex
  semisimple Lie group and $\Gamma$ a uniform lattice in $\ms G$. Then $\Gamma$
  contains a surface group."
- **Quantitative theorem** (l.186--194): for `G` center free, semisimple, without
  compact factor, an `sl_2`-triple with compact centralizer satisfying the flip
  assumption, and any `zeta > 0`, there is a cocompact Fuchsian group and a
  `P`-Anosov representation into `Gamma` whose limit map is `zeta`-Sullivan.
  Anosov representations are faithful (l.176).
- **Scope of the flip assumption** (l.197), verbatim: "The flip assumption is
  satisfied for all complex groups, all rank 1 group except $\mathsf{SO}(1,2n)$,
  but not for real split groups."
- **Second-hand** (l.149), not read at source: "Hamenstädt \cite{Hamenstadt:2015wa}
  had followed a similar proof to show the existence of surface subgroups of all
  rank 1 groups, except $\mathsf{SO}(2n,1)$". Bibliography l.5325--5326: U.
  Hamenstädt, *Incompressible surfaces in rank one locally symmetric spaces*.
