title: Rips type R passes to amalgams and HNN extensions over finite subgroups, with union generating sets
---

Let `C` be a finite group.

- **Amalgams.** If `(A, S_A)` and `(B, S_B)` are of type R and both generating
  sets contain every non-trivial element of `C`, then
  `(A *_C B, S_A ∪ S_B)` is of type R.
- **HNN extensions.** If `(A, S_A)` is of type R, `C ≤ A` is finite,
  `φ : C → A` is an injective homomorphism, and `S_A` contains the non-trivial
  elements of `C` and of `φ(C)`, then `(A *_{C,φ}, S_A ∪ {t})` is of type R.

In particular, for `C = 1` the free product `(A * B, S_A ∪ S_B)` of marked
groups of type R is of type R.
