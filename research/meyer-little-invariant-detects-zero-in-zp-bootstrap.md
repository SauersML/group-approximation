**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `p` be prime and `G = Z/p`. For a
separable `G`-C\*-algebra `A` put
`L_*(A) = KK^G_*(C ⊕ C(G), A) ≅ K_*(A ⋊ G) ⊕ K_*(Res A)`.

For `A, B ∈ B^G`:
- `L_*(A) ≅ 0` iff `A ≃_(KK^G) 0`;
- `f ∈ KK^G_0(A, B)` is invertible iff `L_*(f)` is invertible.

The identification of `L_*` uses Green–Julg and the Ind–Res adjunction.
`B^G` is generated by `C` and `C(G)`.
