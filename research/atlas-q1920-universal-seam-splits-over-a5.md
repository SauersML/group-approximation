Fix either of the two marked local groups `Q ~= 2^5:A5`, let `V ~= C2^5`
be its characteristic module, and let `P` be the universal exact seam
presentation obtained by adjoining the first-packet generators `z,c` and the
collision relations.  Put `N=normal_closure_P(V)`.

The forced bridge gives

```text
e = v v^c = d d^c in N cap J,
```

where `e` is a nonidentity element of the normal collision Klein four in
`J=S4`.  Since `N` is normal, it contains the normal closure of `e` in `J`,
hence it contains the labeled collision involution `c`.  The seam
`<c,z>=S3` then puts `z` in `N` as well.  On the other hand the canonical map

```text
phi: P -> Q/V ~= A5,     phi(c)=phi(z)=1
```

has kernel normally generated by `V,c,z`.  Therefore

```text
N = ker(phi),             P/N ~= A5.                       (Q1920-KER-1)
```

Killing `c,z` defines an exact retraction `P -> Q` for both markings.  Since
`Q` has an `A5` complement to `V`, this complement splits `(Q1920-KER-1)`:

```text
P = N semidirect A5.                                       (Q1920-KER-2)
```

The exact index-sixty Reidemeister--Schreier calculation and an independent
rank computation in the sixty-sheeted presentation cover agree:

```text
N_ab ~= C2^9,          dim ker(d1)=301, rank im(d2)=292.    (Q1920-KER-3)
```

Inside `N_ab`, both `V` and `V^c` inject as the same five-dimensional
subspace.  The image of `z` is zero, while the `A5`-orbit of the image of `c`
spans a four-dimensional submodule disjoint from the image of `V`.  Thus

```text
N_ab = image(V) direct_sum <image(c)^A5>,
dim image(V)=5,       dim <image(c)^A5>=4.                 (Q1920-KER-4)
```

In particular `z in [N,N]` but `c notin [N,N]`.  Consequently no completion
whose image of `N` is abelian can preserve the first-packet `S3`; any finite
faithful completion has a nonabelian kernel whose derived subgroup contains
an element of order three.  This rules out a purely 2-local kernel, but it
does not decide whether `N` is finite or determine the nonabelian subgroup
`V cap V^c`.

## Attempts

Embedding the moved modules into the canonical `Sp6(2)`, `Omega8+(2)`, and
`Omega8-(2)` parabolics gives no collision involution, but those exclusions
do not classify arbitrary overgroups.  Passing to an abelian kernel is now
also fenced by `(Q1920-KER-4)`, because it kills `z`.  The deferred exact
attack is the next derived quotient of `N`, where the surviving order-three
class of `z` must first appear.
