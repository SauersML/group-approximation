## Statement

There is a homeomorphism `φ : ∂L -> C_2`, from the free-product normal-form boundary of
`z2-free-z-normal-form-boundary-has-corner-singular-orbits` onto the binary Cantor set, such
that the group `Γ` below has these properties:
- it contains `V` and `φ(Z^2 * Z)φ^-1`;
- it is a finite germ extension of `V` (Belk--Hyde--Matucci, arXiv:2407.03149);
- it is full and clopen transitive;
- `sing(Γ)` is the union of the `V`-orbits of four rational points, the images of the corners;
- at every singular point `p`, `(Γ)_p ≅ Z^2` and `(V)_p ≅ Z` is the diagonal, normal with
  quotient `Z`;
- it has type `F_∞`.

Here `Γ` is the group of homeomorphisms of `C_2` that agree locally with `V` off finitely many
points and whose germs lie in the groupoid generated by `V` and `Z^2 * Z`.

Consequently `Z^2 * Z` lies in `B_A` and embeds in a finitely presented simple group.

## Proof

- **Applying the recoding theorem.** Apply `prefix-replacement-actions-recode-into-germ-extensions-of-v`
  to the automaton `𝒜` of the boundary node, with `v0 = q0` and `S = {x^±, y^±, t^±}`.
  - Out-degrees are at least 3.
  - `Σ(t^±) = ∅` and `Σ(x^±) = Σ(y^±) = C`, the four corners, all fixed and eventually
    periodic. This is item (3) of the boundary node.
  - So parts (1) and (2) of that theorem give `φ` and a full, clopen-transitive finite germ
    extension `Γ`, with `sing(Γ)` a finite union of `V`-orbits of rational points.
- **Choice of codes.** At the vertices `D_ab` and `E_ab`, which have 4 out-edges, use the
  complete code `{0, 10, 110, 111}`. The corner `(ab)^∞` runs around the cycle
  `D_ab -> E_ab -> D_ab` with code word `β_ab = c_{D_ab}(a) c_{E_ab}(b)`. Choose:

  | corner | `c_D(a)` | `c_E(b)` | `β` |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `(xy)^∞` | `0` | `10` | `010` |
  | `(xY)^∞` | `0` | `110` | `0110` |
  | `(Xy)^∞` | `0` | `111` | `0111` |
  | `(XY)^∞` | `111` | `10` | `11110` |

  - The remaining edges out of `D_ab`, `E_ab`, and all other vertices get arbitrary complete codes.
  - The four `β` are primitive and pairwise not cyclic rotations of one another. So the four
    corner images `c'_i = φ(c_i)` lie in four distinct `V`-orbits, and `(V)_{c'_i} ≅ Z` is
    generated by the one-`β` shift.
- **Germ groups.** Fix a corner `c = c_i` and its image `c'`.
  - *Basic germs.* A germ of `𝒢` from `c'` to `c'` is a product of basic germs along a
    trajectory. Every generator maps the `V`-orbit `O` of `c'` into itself: it is `V`-local off
    the corners, and it fixes the corners. The only corner in `O` is `c'`.
  - *Grouping.* Group maximal runs of `V`-germs. Each run starts and ends at `c'`, so it lies in
    `(V)_{c'}`. The remaining factors are germs at `c'` of `x^±`, `y^±`, which lie in the germ
    group of `Z^2` at `c`.
  - *The diagonal.* The diagonal `δ` acts near `c` as `[(ab)^M] -> [(ab)^(M+1)]` (item (4) of
    the boundary node). In code this inserts one `β`, so its germ generates `(V)_{c'}`.
  - *Conclusion.* `(Γ)_{c'}` is generated by the `Z^2`-germs, which form a faithful `Z^2`
    (item (4)). So `(Γ)_{c'} ≅ Z^2 ⊃ (V)_{c'} = <δ> ≅ Z`, with quotient `Z`.
  - *Other singular points.* The other singular points are `V`-translates of the `c'_i`, with
    conjugate germ groups.
- **Finiteness.** Part (3) of the recoding theorem at every `n` gives type `F_∞`. Part (4)
  gives type (A), hence `B_A` and a finitely presented simple envelope. ∎

## What this shows

- **Outside V.** `Z^2 * Z` is not a subgroup of `V` (`z2-free-z-does-not-embed-in-thompson-v`).
  Here it sits in a finitely presented full group of Cantor homeomorphisms that differs from `V`
  only at four orbits of rational points.
- **Outside the finite-nucleus RSG class.** The germ group `Z^2` at a rational point is not
  virtually cyclic, so by `finite-nucleus-rsg-abelian-stabilizers-have-rank-one-germs` `Γ` is not
  a finite-nucleus rational similarity group. The germ-extension method reaches flats where the
  finite-nucleus method cannot.
- **Abelianization.** By Belk--Hyde--Matucci `thm:Abelianization`, with `V` perfect,
  `Γ/[Γ,Γ] ≅ Z^4`, one `Z^2/<δ>` per corner orbit. So `Γ` is not simple. Simplicity is not
  needed, because the type (A) route supplies the simple envelope.
- **Status of BH for Z^2 * Z.** Already known by linearity and by `2V`. The new content is the
  host, and the fact that the automatic-structure boundary of the BBCMP normal form feeds the
  Belk--Hyde--Matucci machine end to end.
