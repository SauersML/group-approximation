**ESTABLISHED** by `symbol-blind-automata-on-pointed-memory-are-permutive-proof`.

**Setting.** Let G be any group and M a finite subset of G containing e.
Let sigma choose a point of each set partition of M, with sigma(discrete) = e,
and let

    tau_q(x)(g) = x(g sigma(pi_g x))    on [q]^G,

where pi_g x is the partition of M by equality of x(gm). By part 1 of
symbol-blind-automata-reduce-to-defect-counting, every symbol-blind automaton
at an alphabet of size at least |M|+2 has this form, up to composing with a
right translation, which is a bijective automaton.

- **Pointed.** M is *pointed* if the semigroup generated by M \ {e} does not
  contain e. Examples: M ⊂ {0,1,2,...} over Z; M \ {e} inside the positive
  monoid of F2 = <a,b>; any M whose non-identity elements have positive image
  under some homomorphism G -> R.
- **Permutive at e.** sigma is *e-permutive* if the local rule
  f(x) = x_{sigma(pi x)} is a bijection in x_e once the other values are
  fixed, at every q. Equivalently:
  - (P1) if {e} is a class of P, then sigma(P) = e;
  - (P2) for every partition rho of M \ {e} with classes C_1..C_k, let
    tau_rho(j) be the class of rho containing sigma(rho with e joined to C_j),
    read as C_j when the selected point is e; then tau_rho is a permutation.

  The number of e-permutive selectors is the product of k(rho)! over the
  partitions rho of M \ {e}: 2 for |M| = 3 and 48 for |M| = 4.

**Theorem.** Let M be pointed. The following are equivalent:

1. tau_q is pre-injective for one q >= |M| + |M^{-1}M|;
2. sigma is e-permutive;
3. tau_q is pre-injective for every q.

If they hold, then for every q, every finite F ⊂ G and every y in [q]^F,

    #{x in [q]^{FM} : tau_q(x)|_F = y} = q^{|FM|-|F|}.

Hence tau_q is surjective and preserves the uniform Bernoulli measure for every q.

**Corollaries on pointed memory, over every group.**

- symbol-blind-injective-automata-are-bijective holds, from injectivity at a
  single q >= |M| + |M^{-1}M|.
- symbol-blind-defect-counting-identities holds at every order. The count
  above is statement (b) of part 4 of the reduction, so identities (c) follow.
- The Myhill direction holds inside the class: pre-injective at one large q
  implies surjective at every q. For general automata this direction
  characterises amenability, so the proof has to use the symbol-blind
  structure. It does so through P1 and P2.

**Sharpness.**

- *Permutive does not give injective.* The minority rule on M = {0,1,2} over Z
  is e-permutive:
  - f = x_2 if x_0 = x_1 != x_2;
  - f = x_1 if x_0 = x_2 != x_1;
  - otherwise f = x_0.

  At q = 2 it is x_0 + x_1 + x_2 mod 2. On Z/6 it sends both (0,0,1,0,1,1) and
  (0,1,0,0,0,0) to (1,1,0,0,0,1). Pulling back along Z -> Z/6 gives two
  periodic configurations of Z with one image. So the rule is pre-injective and
  surjective at every q, but injective at no q >= 2.
- *Pointedness cannot be dropped.* On the non-pointed memory M = {0,-1,1}
  over Z, the centred minority rule is e-permutive:
  - f = x_1 if x_0 = x_{-1} != x_1;
  - f = x_{-1} if x_0 = x_1 != x_{-1};
  - otherwise f = x_0.

  It is pre-injective at q = 2 (it is x_{-1} + x_0 + x_1 mod 2, a surjective
  automaton over Z). At q = 3 the configurations ...0 1 [0 0] 1 0... and
  ...0 1 [2 2] 1 0... differ in two cells and have the same image. The other
  cells agree arbitrarily, and the outputs on the four affected cells are
  (1,1,1,1).

- *Pre-injective does not give permutive without pointedness.* On
  M = {0,-1,1,2} over Z, list the partitions by the equality pattern of
  (x_0, x_{-1}, x_1, x_2). Let R29 select 2 on 0001 and 0112, 1 on 0010 and
  0120, -1 on 0101, 0102 and 0110, and 0 on the rest. R29 is permutive in no
  coordinate. The pair-graph decider (artifact) shows it is pre-injective at
  every q and injective at none.

**Census calibration** (artifacts). `fd_sector_vs_measure.py` checks the
finite-defect sector up to defect 3 and uniform counts on small windows. By the
remark in the route, failing the finite-defect checks means failing
pre-injectivity at some q. `pair_graph_over_z.py` decides pre-injectivity and
injectivity over Z at every q exactly, by a finite pair graph on equality
patterns, where q = 2..2w decides every q.

- **Pointed memories.** Over Z these are six 3-point sets, {0,1,2,3} and
  {0,1,2,4}; over F2 they are {e,a,b} and {e,a,b,ab}. The rules that pass every
  check are exactly the e-permutive ones: 2 of 8, and 48 of 93312. Over Z the
  decider confirms that all of them are pre-injective at every q. On each
  memory it decides, only the identity is injective at every q.
- **3-point non-pointed memories:** {0,-1,1} and {0,-1,2} over Z, {e,a,A} over
  F2. Of the two e-permutive selectors, only the identity passes. The decider
  shows the other is not pre-injective.
- **Z {0,-1,1,2}.** 29 selectors pass defects 1 and 2. At defect 3 they split:
  - 4 pass and are e-permutive;
  - 1 passes and is not e-permutive (R29);
  - 4 fail and are e-permutive;
  - 20 fail and are not e-permutive.

  The decider agrees with the finite-defect checks on all 29. It also finds
  that exactly 4 of the 48 e-permutive selectors are pre-injective, and 44 are
  not.

  Every pre-injective selector passes the finite-defect checks (remark in the
  route). So the selectors pre-injective at every q on this memory are exactly
  five: the four e-permutive ones and R29. The only one injective at every q
  is the identity.

  The same holds on {0,-1,1}, {0,-1,2}, {0,1,2}, {0,1,3}, {0,2,3} and
  {0,1,2,3}: only the identity is injective at every q. So on these memories
  injective at every q implies surjective, trivially.
- **F2 {e,a,b,A}.** 12 selectors pass defects 1 and 2. At defect 3 they split:
  - 3 pass, all e-permutive, one of which selects A;
  - 7 fail but are e-permutive;
  - 2 fail and are not e-permutive.

**What is not proved.** For non-pointed memory:

- a characterisation of pre-injective selectors. e-permutivity is neither
  necessary (R29) nor sufficient (centred minority).
- whether pre-injectivity forces the uniform counts. In the census every rule
  that passes the finite-defect checks also passes the counts on small windows.
- whether injectivity forces surjectivity.

These are the remaining cases of symbol-blind-defect-counting-identities and
symbol-blind-injective-automata-are-bijective.
