Let `R` be a nonzero unital `F_2`-algebra generated by `u,u^{-1},p`,
where `u` is a unit and `p^2=p`. The group `EL_3(R)` is generated by

`c=[[0,0,1],[1,0,1],[0,1,0]]`,
`t=e_12(1-p)e_13(p)`,
`w=[[0,u,0],[u^{-1},0,0],[0,0,1]]`.

Their exact orders are `7,2,2`. Also `<c,t>=EL_3(F_2[p])`; for nontrivial
`p` this is `SL_3(F_2) x SL_3(F_2)` and `ct` has order `12`.
The idempotent need not commute with the unit. No dynamical or
approximation hypothesis is required.
