Let `(A,rho)` be a C*-probability space and let `(A_i)` be an upward directed
family of unital C*-subalgebras with norm-dense union.  If every
`(A_i,rho|A_i)` is selfless, then `(A,rho)` is selfless.

**Established (literature).**  This is Theorem 4.1 of Leonel Robert,
*Selfless C*-algebras*, arXiv:2309.14188v3 / *Advances in Mathematics* 478
(2025), 110403.  It is imported here, not reproved.
