**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Every word-hyperbolic group embeds in a
contracting rational similarity group.

Source: J. Belk, C. Bleak, F. Matucci, M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Hyperbolic groups
satisfy the Boone--Higman conjecture*, arXiv:2309.06224 (v3, 20 August 2025). The
precise statements and definitions, read from the arXiv TeX source on MSI, are
quoted in the linked artifact.
