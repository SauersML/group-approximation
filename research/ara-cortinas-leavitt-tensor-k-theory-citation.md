Literature import from Ara and Cortiñas, arXiv:1108.0352v3, Section 6.

- **Lemma 6.1.** Regular coherence of the polynomial path algebras
  `P(E)[t_1, ..., t_n]` (after Ara--Brustenga) is combined with flatness of the
  universal localization `P(E) -> L(E)` (Ara--Brustenga, Proposition 4.1). An
  involution then makes the result two-sided.
- **Proposition 6.2.** Apply Ara--Brustenga--Cortiñas Theorem 7.6 to
  `L_R(E) = L_2 ⊗ R`. The map `1 - N_E^t` is multiplication by `-1`, an
  equivalence, so its homotopy cofibre is zero.

The statements were checked against the PDF text on MSI
(`lit-groups/1108.0352.txt`). Neither proof was refereed here.
