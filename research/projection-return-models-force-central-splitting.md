title: Finite-dimensional models whose minimal projections return to the image force central splitting
---

Let `C subset B` be a separable unital C-star subalgebra such that every
nonzero positive element of `C` is properly infinite in `B`.  Suppose that
for every finite `F subset C` and `epsilon>0` there are a unital
finite-dimensional subalgebra

```text
D=direct_sum_j M_(n_j) subset B
```

and matrix units `e^(j)_(ab)` such that:

1. every element of `F` is within `epsilon` of `D`;
2. for every `j`, the minimal projection `e^(j)_(11)` is within `1/16` of
   some positive element of `C`.

Then the inclusion `C->B`, and hence every unital map with range `C`, is
O-infinity-stable.
