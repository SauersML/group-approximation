**Conventions.**  Tracial ultraproducts are `prod_omega (M_n, tau_n) =
l^infinity(M_n) / {x : lim_omega ||x_n||_2 = 0}`.  The quotient map is a
*-homomorphism of C*-algebras, so the closed unit ball of the ultraproduct is
the image of the closed unit ball of `l^infinity(M_n)` (lifting of contractions
through C*-quotients).  Every element of norm at most 1 therefore has a
representing sequence of contractions.

**Lemma M (moments determine the embedding).**  Let `(N, tau)` be a tracial von
Neumann algebra generated by a finite or countable family of contractions
`a_1, a_2, ...`, and let `(M, sigma)` be a tracial von Neumann algebra
containing contractions `b_1, b_2, ...` with
`sigma(p(b)) = tau(p(a))` for every *-polynomial `p` in finitely many
variables.  Then `a_i -> b_i` extends to a unital trace-preserving normal
*-isomorphism of `N` onto `W*(b_i)`.
*Proof.*  The map `p(a) -> p(b)` on the *-algebras generated is well defined
and isometric for `||.||_2`, because `||p(a)||_2^2 = tau(p(a)* p(a))` is a
moment.  It extends to a unitary `L^2(N) -> L^2(W*(b))` intertwining the left
actions of the generators, hence of the generated von Neumann algebras
(standard GNS argument), and it maps `1` to `1` and preserves traces.

**Lemma D (separable subalgebras of an ultrapower of an ultraproduct).**  Let
`omega` be an ultrafilter and `(M_n)` matrix algebras.  Every separable tracial
von Neumann algebra `N` that embeds trace-preservingly into
`(prod_omega M_n)^omega'` embeds trace-preservingly into `prod_V M_(d_k)` for a
suitable sequence of matrix algebras and a nonprincipal ultrafilter `V` on `N`.
*Proof.*  Fix contraction generators `a_1, a_2, ...` of `N` and write `P_k` for
the finite set of monomials of degree at most `k` in `a_1..a_k` and their
adjoints.  Their images in the double ultraproduct are contractions with the
same moments.  Lift twice through the C*-quotients to contractions: for each
`k` there are indices at which some tuple of matrix contractions `b^(k)` in
some `M_(d_k)` satisfies `|tr(m(b^(k))) - tau(m(a))| < 1/k` for all `m in P_k`.
(The set of good indices at each stage belongs to the ultrafilter, so it is
nonempty.)  Take any nonprincipal ultrafilter `V` on `N`.  The tuple
`b = [(b^(k))_k]` in `prod_V M_(d_k)` has exact moments equal to those of `a`,
so Lemma M gives the embedding.

**Proof of the claim.**
1. By `mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists` there is a finite Boolean constraint
   system `B_*` whose algebra `A(B_*)` has a tracial state `tau_*` but admits no
   unital *-homomorphism into any tracial matrix ultraproduct `R^U`.  Let
   `N = pi_(tau_*)(A(B_*))''`.  It is generated by the finitely many
   self-adjoint unitaries `pi(x_v)`, so it is separable.
2. Suppose `L(U)` is locally universal, and fix a unital trace-preserving
   embedding `N -> L(U)^omega'`.
3. Suppose, towards a contradiction, that `U` is hyperlinear.  By direction
   (1) ⟹ (2) of `hyperlinear-iff-group-factor-embeds-in-hyperfinite-ultrapower`,
   `L(U)` embeds trace-preservingly into a tracial matrix ultraproduct
   `prod_omega M_n`.  Applying this coordinatewise gives a trace-preserving
   embedding `L(U)^omega' -> (prod_omega M_n)^omega'`.
4. So `N` embeds into `(prod_omega M_n)^omega'`, and Lemma D gives a unital
   trace-preserving embedding `N -> prod_V M_(d_k)`.
5. Composing, `A(B_*) -> N -> prod_V M_(d_k)` is a unital *-homomorphism into a
   tracial matrix ultraproduct.  This contradicts step 1.

So `U` is not hyperlinear.
