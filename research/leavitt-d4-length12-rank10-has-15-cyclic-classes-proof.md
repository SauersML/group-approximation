The seven normal-chirality canonical keys `(face rotation; five-arc sizes)` are

```text
(0;113),(0;122),(0;131),(0;212),(0;311),(1;113),(2;113).
```

The eight inverse-chirality keys are the first five plus
`(1;113),(1;212),(2;113)`.  Direct orbit counting gives 99 and 102 oriented
systems respectively, totaling 201.  These cyclic-orbit keys are distinct;
profile reversal is deliberately not included in the quotient.
