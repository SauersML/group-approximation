For a prescribed finite canonical word window, use residual finiteness to
choose one finite quotient separating every nonidentity window word, every
nonidentity element of the finite packet, and every support word `g r`.
The normalized regular representation of that quotient is exact, restricts
to copies of the regular packet representation, and has zero trace on every
`g r`.  Therefore its packet projection has trace `tau_H(P)>0` while its
filtered return moment is zero.  Exhausting the word windows gives the
canonical sequence and refutes every positive return inequality with
vanishing defect error.
