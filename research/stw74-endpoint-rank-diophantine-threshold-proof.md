Let `q in M_k(D_(r,s))` be a projection.  The rank of the continuous
projection path `t |-> q(t) in M_(kN)` is constant on `[0,1]`.  At the two
endpoints there are projections `a,b in M_k(C)` such that

```text
q(0)=a tensor e_r,              q(1)=b tensor e_s.
```

If `alpha=rank(a)` and `beta=rank(b)`, constancy of rank gives the exact
Diophantine condition

```text
alpha r=beta s,                0<=alpha,beta<=k.           (DT1)
```

For a nonzero projection both integers are positive.  After writing
`r=g r_0`, `s=g s_0` with `g=gcd(r,s)` and coprime `r_0,s_0`, equation
`(DT1)` forces

```text
alpha=l s_0,                  beta=l r_0
```

for some positive integer `l`.  Hence necessarily

```text
k>=max(r_0,s_0)=m_0.                                      (DT2)
```

Conversely, if `k>=m_0`, choose endpoint projections of ranks
`alpha=s_0` and `beta=r_0`.  Then `a tensor e_r` and `b tensor e_s` both
have rank

```text
s_0 r=r_0 s=lcm(r,s).
```

Two projections of the same rank in the full matrix algebra `M_(kN)` are
unitarily conjugate, and its unitary group is path connected.  A unitary
path therefore joins the two endpoint projections by a continuous
projection path in `M_k(D_(r,s))`.  This proves `(DT)`.

Taking `k=1` in `(DT1)` shows that `D_(r,s)` is projectionless when `r!=s`.
For a first-level projection `P`, its coefficient algebra `C_P` is a
C-star subalgebra of the `N`-subhomogeneous algebra `D_(r,s)`, hence is
subhomogeneous, and it remains projectionless.  The entries of `P` generate
`C_P`, so the ideal generated by `P` in `M_(m_0)(C_P)` is the whole matrix
algebra.  Thus `P` is full, and its unital full corner is Morita equivalent
to `C_P`; in particular `Prim(C_P)` is compact.

For `j<m_0`, any projection in `M_j(C_P)` would also be a projection in
`M_j(D_(r,s))`, contradicting `(DT)`.  Since `P` occurs at `m_0`, the
coefficient algebra retains the exact first threshold.  Finally, choosing
coprime consecutive endpoint ranks `m-1,m` makes `m_0=m`, proving that every
finite threshold at least two occurs.
