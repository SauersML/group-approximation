**ESTABLISHED (import).**

1. If `Ψ` is a treeing of a p.m.p. equivalence relation `R`, then `C(Ψ) = C(R)`.
2. If a countable group `Γ` admits a free p.m.p. treeable action, then that action has cost `C_*(Γ)`, the
   infimum of costs over free p.m.p. actions of `Γ`.
3. In particular, a nonamenable group that admits a free p.m.p. action of cost one is non-treeable.
