The cases are exhaustive.  Values zero and one are established.  For finite
`asdim(X)≥2`, the first open claim combined with the Winter--Zacharias upper
bound gives equality.  At infinite asymptotic dimension, failure of property A
is established; property A is exactly the second open claim.  Thus proving the
two remaining requirements would settle the root, while the route makes no
claim that either requirement is currently proved.
