The quotient map `pi:P->R` has kernel `B`.  The ordinary subgroup
correspondence theorem gives a bijection between subgroups of `R` and
subgroups of `P` containing `B`, with inverse `K |-> pi(K)`.  Preimages
commute with arbitrary intersections, and the preimage of a generated
subgroup is the subgroup generated by the corresponding preimages because
all of them contain the kernel.  This proves the complete-lattice assertion.

For `S<=T`, the map on left coset sets

```text
K_T/K_S -> T/S,              xK_S |-> pi(x)S
```

is a bijection.  Conjugation commutes with `pi`, so taking intersections of
all conjugates gives the core formula, and an element normalizes `K_S`
exactly when its image normalizes `S`.  The normality and quotient formulas
are the corresponding normal-subgroup statements.

Every virtually free group is residually finite, finite products of
residually finite groups are residually finite, and residual finiteness
passes to subgroups.  Hence every actor `S` in the interval is MF.  The
target-factorization, radical, closure, residual, and completion identities
are exactly those of
`finite-orbit-subgroups-inherit-internal-mf-reflections`; no finiteness of
the orbit set is needed for these identities.  Combining them with subgroup
correspondence proves `(ILC6)`--`(ILC8)` and all additional target classes.
