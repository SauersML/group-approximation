REFUTED.  The proposed argument compressed the IRS representation to a
positive global readable atom, observed that every frozen equation word had
the required scalar compression, and invoked the multiplicative domain to
factor through the frozen solution-group quotient.  The multiplicative
domain does not kill the two-sided normal ideal.  Remote unreadable words can
move the atom to a fibre on which a different controlled equation is active;
the two-dimensional counterexample in
`scalar-relator-compression-does-not-kill-normal-ideal` is the exact algebraic
failure.
