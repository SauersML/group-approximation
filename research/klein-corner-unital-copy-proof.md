- `delta` is a unital ring endomorphism of `R`, since `t_i s_j = delta_ij`.
- `delta(g) w = w delta(g)` and `delta(g) d = d delta(g)`, so `delta_*(x)` commutes with `e_K` and the map is a unital homomorphism into the corner.
- `delta(g) k = delta(g') k'` forces `delta(g'^-1 g) in K`. That is scalar-diagonal only at `±1`, so `g = g'` and `k = k'`, and the group elements stay distinct.
