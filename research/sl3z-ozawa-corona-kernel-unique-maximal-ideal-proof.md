Full proof: `research/artifacts/quasitrace-kernel-maximal-ideal-property-s-2026-09-12.md` §1.

1. **Weak mixing.** Let `J ≠ A` and let `π` be a faithful representation of `A/J`.
   - `A` is generated by `ρ_∞(Γ)`. So a finite-dimensional `Γ`-invariant subspace is
     `A`-invariant and gives a finite-dimensional representation of `A`.
   - That is excluded by `kazhdan-block-algebras-contain-their-block-ideal`, so
     `π ∘ ρ_∞` is weakly mixing.
2. **Weak containment.** Boutonnet–Houdayer Corollary D gives `λ_Γ ≺ π ∘ ρ_∞`. So
   `‖q(y)‖ ≤ ‖π(y + J)‖`, first on the span of `ρ_∞(Γ)` and then on all of `A`.
   Hence `J ⊂ ker q = K`.
3. **Consequences.**
   - If `J ∩ K = 0`, then `J ⊂ K` gives `J = 0`, so `K` is essential.
   - A unit of a quotient `K/L` is a central projection of `A/L`. The kernel of
     `A → K/L` is then a proper ideal `J` with `J + K = A`, contradicting step 2.

`sl3z-ozawa-block-corona-has-unique-trace` supplies the identification
`A/K ≅ C*_r(Γ)`, with `q(ρ_∞(g)) = λ_g`.
