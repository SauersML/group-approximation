This is Theorem 1.2 of He--Tang--Zhang, arXiv:2605.15006v2.  Their
noncommutative Lyapunov lemma replaces a positive contraction by a projection
while preserving finitely many prescribed normal self-adjoint moments.
Starting from a dense sequence in the real self-adjoint `L^2` space, they
greedily choose pairwise orthogonal symmetries which norm the successive
residuals.  A divergence argument makes their real span dense; complexifying
gives a complete orthonormal basis of `L^2(M,tau)`.
