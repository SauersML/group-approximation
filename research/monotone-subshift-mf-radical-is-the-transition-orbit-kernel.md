For the binary subshift `X` forbidding `10`, let
`G_n=EL_n(LC(X,F_2) semidirect Z)`, `n>=4`. Then

`Rad_MF(G_n)=Res_fin(G_n)=GL_fin(Z x {1,...,n},F_2)`.

This is precisely the kernel of restriction to the two fixed points.
The maximal MF quotient is the residually finite group
`SL_n(F_2[z,z^{-1}]) x SL_n(F_2[z,z^{-1}])`.
Every homomorphism from `G_n` to an MF group factors uniquely through
this endpoint quotient.

The radical is infinite, simple and locally finite, hence itself LEF.
At `n=4`, the ambient group is Kazhdan and generated by four torsion
elements of orders `7,2,2,2`. The MF equality is not asserted in rank three.
