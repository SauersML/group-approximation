title: Classify the automatically full nuclear maps into a strongly purely infinite target

Let `phi,psi:Z->B` be unital embeddings.  Both maps are nuclear because
their domain `Z` is nuclear.  They are full as maps: for every nonzero
`a in Z_+`, simplicity of `Z` and unitality imply that the closed ideal of
`B` generated by `phi(a)` or by `psi(a)` contains `1_B`.  This is also the
fullness conclusion in `z-embedding-invariants-collapse-before-cu`.

Gabe, *Classification of O-infinity-stable C*-algebras*, Theorem 9.7 says
that a nuclear homomorphism from a separable exact domain into a strongly
purely infinite codomain is strongly `O_infinity`-stable.  Hence both maps
have that property.

The same required invariant-collapse theorem gives

```text
[phi]=[psi] in KK(Z,B).
```

Because the first variable `Z` is nuclear, Skandalis' canonical map

```text
KK_nuc(Z,B) -> KK(Z,B)
```

is an isomorphism.  The two maps therefore have the same nuclear `KK`
class.

Finally Gabe's Theorem B classifies nuclear, full, strongly
`O_infinity`-stable maps from a separable algebra into a sigma-unital target
by nuclear `KK`.  The target `B` is sigma-unital because it is unital, and
the theorem's unital clause upgrades asymptotic Murray--von Neumann
equivalence to asymptotic unitary equivalence by a norm-continuous unitary
path in `B`.  This proves the claim without passing to `B**` or any other
shadow algebra.
