Suppose Y is compact Hausdorff, A is a unital C*-algebra with
faithful tracial state t, a group acts on A preserving t, and
ι:C(Y)→A is an injective unital equivariant *-homomorphism.
Then Y has an invariant probability measure of full support.
In particular Y cannot have infinitely many pairwise disjoint
translates of a nonempty open set, including an infinite isolated orbit.

The conclusion applies to invariant nonzero finite-trace corners
after normalization. It does not automatically apply to multiplier
algebras with only a semifinite trace. For reduced group algebras
and conjugation actions it restricts the proposed unital
coefficient-embedding strategy, not all coefficient-removal mechanisms.
See Proposition 6.1 of the
[proof document](artifacts/Cairn_Baum_Connes_General_Attempt.md#proposition-61-faithful-trace-forces-full-support-invariant-measure).
