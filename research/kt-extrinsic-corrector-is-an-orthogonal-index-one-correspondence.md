**ESTABLISHED MINIMAL BIMODULE.**  In the canonical AFP paste put

```text
B_v=W^*(R,(v_gamma)_(gamma in Gamma)),
B_rho=W^*(R,(rho_gamma)_(gamma in Gamma)),              (KBC1)
```

and let `theta:B_v->B_rho` fix `R` and send
`v_gamma` to `rho_gamma`.  If `M` embeds in a finite tracial algebra `D`, a
unitary `z in R' cap D` closes the C-normalization seam if and only if

```text
z b=theta(b)z                         (b in B_v).        (KBC2)
```

For such a `z`,

```text
H_z=closure(B_rho z B_v)=closure(B_rho z)=closure(z B_v)
```

is the standard invertible `B_rho`--`B_v` correspondence for `theta`; its
left and right Murray--von Neumann dimensions are both one.  Moreover
`H_z` is orthogonal to `L^2(M)`.

Conversely, an ambient correspondence realized in `L^2(D)` with an
`R`-central **unitary** cyclic vector of full left and right support and
satisfying `(KBC2)` supplies the seam corrector.  Thus the positive branch
must create an entire orthogonal index-one bimodule, not merely a small
almost-invariant corner.  The finite `SL_3(Z)` relators impose the additional
holonomy condition that the same vector may replace `u_C` in the external
actor representation.
