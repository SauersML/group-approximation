Notation and the units `Û_s` as in `zd-derived-full-group-fp-excludes-periodic-quantum-tilings-proof`
(artifact `labbe-periodic-quantum-tilings-obstruct-fp-2026-09-13.md`, Sections 1–3). The only change is
that the finite presentation is replaced by the truncated presentations `P_n`.

1. **The group.** `G` is generated by the finite symmetric set `S` of rule 3-cycles `s(pi, e)`, and it is
   finitely generated, infinite and simple (`minimal-z2-subshift-derived-full-groups-are-fg-simple`).
2. **Fix `m`**, and let `n = n(m)` from `simple-group-truncated-quotients-are-trivial-or-exact`.
3. **Truncated presentations map to units.** Each `Û_s` is a combination of words of length `<= l_0`
   in the generators of `A_D`. Let `D_1(n)` bound the lengths of these combinations, all zero in
   `R_X = LC(X, k) ⋊ Z^2`:
   - `Û_s Û_(s^(-1)) - 1` for `s ∈ S`;
   - `Û_(s_1) ... Û_(s_j) - 1` for every word `s_1 ... s_j` of length `<= 2n` that is trivial in `G`
     (zero in `R_X` because `U_g U_h = U_(gh)` and `U_1 = 1`); length `<= 2n l_0 + 1`;
   - identity (★) `chi[pi] + chi[pi] (Û_s - 1) chi[pi]` for `s = s(pi, e)`;
   - the partition identity `sum_pi chi[pi] - 1`.

   By Lemma 1.2 of the ring artifact (backing `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`), all of them
   vanish in `A_D` for `D >= D_1(n)`. So `s -> Û_s` induces a homomorphism `phi : P_n -> A_D^×`.
4. **A nontrivial linear quotient.** Take `D >= D_1(n)` and a nonzero finite-dimensional `A_D`-module
   `V`. This gives `rho : P_n -> GL(V)`. If every `rho(s)` were `1`, identity (★) would make every
   `chi[pi]` act as `0`, and the partition identity would make `1` act as `0`, so `V = 0`. Hence the
   image `H = rho(P_n)` is a nontrivial finitely generated linear group.
5. **A LEF witness.** By Corollary 2 of `simple-group-truncated-quotients-are-trivial-or-exact`
   (Malcev through `finitely-generated-linear-groups-are-residually-finite`), `P_n` has a nontrivial
   finite quotient, and by the theorem there it has the marked `m`-ball of `G`.
6. Since `m` is arbitrary, `G` is LEF. ∎

**What was checked.** Steps 1, 3 (the four identities) and the module argument are those of the landed
fp-exclusion route. The new inputs are step 2 and step 5. The only property of `A_D` used is Lemma 1.2
for combinations of bounded length, with the bound allowed to grow with `n`.
