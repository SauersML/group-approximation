**Established by citation**, with the fibre identification written out on the
claim.

- N. Ozawa, arXiv:1304.3523, Section 5 constructs the strict closure of `A` over
  `∂_e T(A)` when it is closed, as a strictly separable continuous W*-bundle
  with fibres `π_τ(A)''`.  Theorem 15 gives (i) triviality ⟺ (ii) a sequence of
  approximately central approximate projections `p_n` with
  `‖E(p_n) − 1/2‖ → 0` in the uniform 2-norm.
- J. Castillejos, S. Evington, A. Tikuisis, S. White, *Uniform property Gamma*,
  arXiv:1912.04207, introduction, read from the PDF text on MSI: "in the case
  where T(A) is a Bauer simplex, Question C, can be formulated using the language
  of W*-bundles from [48]: is the W*-bundle obtained as the strict closure of A
  trivial?"  And: "By Ozawa's trivialisation theorem ([48, Theorem 15]), this
  happens when the W*-bundle has an appropriate version of uniform property Γ
  (which in the case of bundles coming from strict closures of C*-algebras is
  precisely uniform property Γ for the C*-algebra)."
- The converse direction, trivial ⟹ uniform Gamma, is Ozawa's (i) ⟹ (ii), which
  he calls obvious: constant sections from a central sequence of trace-1/2
  projections in `R` work uniformly over `K`.
