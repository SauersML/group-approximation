Let `H_k`, `k>=3`, be a Houghton group on the ray set
`Omega={1,...,k}xN`, and fix any finite presentation `H_k=<X|R>`.  Choose
distinct points `p,q,c_0,c_1,...` and the finitary three-cycle

```text
b=(p q c_0).
```

For every `M`, choose word representatives

```text
u_i=h_i b h_i^(-1)=(p q c_i),       1<=i<=M.
```

Then the complete graph on these vertices has exact Carmichael relations

```text
u_i^3=1,
(u_i u_j)^2=1                         for i!=j.           (HCA1)
```

The cube areas are uniformly bounded, but for every choice of the words
`h_i`,

```text
max_(1<=i<j<=M) Area_R((u_i u_j)^2) -> infinity.          (HCA2)
```

Thus finite presentability and highly transitive finitary permutation
geometry do not provide a bounded-area section.  In this standard amenable
example the entire obstruction is forced into the pair transporters.
