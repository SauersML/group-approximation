OPEN.  Starting with the bounded-degree robust games supplied by
`expander-cloud-reduction-preserves-pauli-dimension-gap`, construct a
finitely presented group `A`, finite typed offset sets

```text
D_X,D_Z,P_0,P_1 subset A,
```

and actual Cayley addresses for every occurrence, so that all four clauses
of `relative-coset-pauli-incidence-compiler` hold.  In particular one must
prove, not assume,

```text
distinct X clouds give distinct A/<D_X> cosets,
distinct Z clouds give distinct A/<D_Z> cosets,
<D_X>P_0<D_Z> intersect <D_X>P_1<D_Z> = empty.                  (CCP1)
```

Sapir--Osajda bounded-word expander embeddings solve the edge-length and
section-free naming part.  Their untyped statement does not imply `(CCP1)`:
the subgroup generated by all X-edge offsets may connect different embedded
clouds outside the selected subgraphs, and an ambient relation may put
opposite-sign pair offsets in one double coset.

The promising construction is a **colored relative graphical-small-
cancellation embedding** of the whole degree-reduced incidence graph.  Give
X-consistency, Z-consistency, sign-zero and sign-one edges disjoint label
alphabets.  Product-refining an Osajda small-cancellation label by a finite
edge type preserves the small-cancellation piece bound, but this is strictly
insufficient: `gsc-does-not-separate-sign-double-cosets`
exhibits arbitrarily strong `C'(lambda)` cycles which collide the two sign
double cosets.  What remains is a relative convexity/Freiheitssatz statement
using the actual Pauli incidence combinatorics to exclude both a mixed
two-sign contour and every composite van Kampen diagram producing one.
That relative separation statement is the exact open gate.

An arbitrary high-girth graph lift is not a substitute.  Covers can unwind
the contextual cycles, split equality clouds, and a decoder which averages
an `m`-sheet lift by direct sum proves only `m d >= D_N`; unbounded sheet
degree can erase the dimension lower bound.  The expander-cloud replacement
product is safe because its proof decodes on the same Hilbert space.

## Attempts

- **Untyped Sapir--Osajda embedding.**  This gives the bounded word ball for
  all local edges but does not control the subgroups generated by equality
  offsets or their sign double cosets.
- **Product-refine the small-cancellation labels by edge type.**  The piece
  estimate survives, but `gsc-does-not-separate-sign-double-cosets` shows
  that even arbitrarily strong ordinary `C'(lambda)` permits an exact sign
  collision.
- **High-girth graph lift.**  A general lift may unwind contextual cycles;
  direct-sum decoding gives only `m d >= D_N` for an `m`-sheet lift.  Only
  equality-cloud replacement, whose decoder stays on the original Hilbert
  space, is presently safe.

The attack is therefore not deferred: the next lemma is a relative
Greendlinger/Freiheitssatz theorem whose hypotheses use sign-consistency of
the actual Pauli incidence graphs, not just their piece length.
