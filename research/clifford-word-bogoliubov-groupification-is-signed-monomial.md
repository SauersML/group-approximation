Let `pi:Gamma -> O(H_R)` be a separable real orthogonal representation.  Fix
an orthonormal basis `(e_i)_(i in I)` and let `Cl_I` be the countable discrete
Clifford group

```text
<z,c_i (i in I) |
 z^2=c_i^2=1, z central, c_i c_j=z c_j c_i (i!=j)>.      (CBG1)
```

Its `z=-1` central corner is the tracial Clifford factor generated by the
Majoranas `c(e_i)`.  The Bogoliubov action of `pi` preserves the image of the
discrete word group `(CBG1)` if and only if

```text
pi(g)e_i in {+e_j,-e_j:j in I} for every g,i.            (CBG2)
```

Thus literal Clifford-word groupification is exactly signed permutation
(real monomial) action in some orthonormal basis.

When `(CBG2)` holds, write
`pi(g)e_i=(-1)^(epsilon(g,i))e_(g.i)`.  Then

```text
c_i |-> z^(epsilon(g,i)) c_(g.i),   z |-> z             (CBG3)
```

defines an action by automorphisms of `Cl_I`, and

```text
q_- L(Cl_I semidirect Gamma) q_-
  = Clifford(H_R) crossed_product Gamma,
q_-=(1-z)/2,                                             (CBG4)
```

with normalized corner trace.  The group `Cl_I` is locally finite and hence
amenable.  In particular, for Manzoor's free/virtually-free source actor,
`Cl_I semidirect Gamma` is amenable-by-sofic and therefore sofic and
hyperlinear.  The monomial groupification branch cannot produce a
non-hyperlinear group.

Necessity is rigid.  Clifford monomials indexed by finite subsets of `I` are
an orthonormal basis of the Clifford `L^2` space, and the first Majorana chaos
is exactly the closed span of the singleton monomials.  Since

```text
alpha_g(c_i)=c(pi(g)e_i)                                 (CBG5)
```

lies in the first chaos, it can equal a discrete Clifford group word only
when that word is `+/-c_j`; this is `(CBG2)`.

For a complex CAR model the identical argument says that a creation basis is
preserved up to phases and permutation, i.e. projective monomiality; a
countable semidirect group additionally requires the occurring phase group
to be countable.  General orthogonal rotations certainly define analytic
Bogoliubov automorphisms, but their images `(CBG5)` are linear combinations,
not group words.

The classification is intentionally about the canonical, generator-
preserving groupification.  An abstract accidental isomorphism of a
Bogoliubov crossed product with some unrelated group factor would not recover
the Majorana word or the IRS correlation and is not classified here.
