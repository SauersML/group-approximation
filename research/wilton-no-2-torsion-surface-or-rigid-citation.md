Citation import: H. Wilton, arXiv:1701.02505v3, Corollary 6.4, together with the Section 6
definition of a rigid group. Both are quoted verbatim in the claim from the arXiv PDF, fetched
2026-09-16.

Wilton deduces Corollary 6.4 from three results:
- Louder--Touikan's finite hierarchy for hyperbolic groups without 2-torsion;
- his Remark 6.2 on the lowest one-ended subgroups of a hierarchy;
- his Theorem 6.1, the quasiconvex surface subgroup theorem for one-ended hyperbolic graphs of
  virtually free groups with virtually cyclic edge groups.

Nothing is reproved here.
