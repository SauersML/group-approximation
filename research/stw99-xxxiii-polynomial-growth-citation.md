**Established by citation.**  Naryshkin, *Polynomial growth, comparison,
and the small boundary property* (arXiv 2021; Theorem A), exactly as
STW quote it: free actions of polynomial-growth (= virtually
nilpotent, Gromov) groups on compact metrisable spaces have dynamical
comparison.
