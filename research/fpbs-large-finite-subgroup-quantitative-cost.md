title: Finite subgroups yield a quantitative upper-cost estimate in bounded exponent

Theorem 7.1. If G is countably infinite of exponent N and contains a finite subgroup of order h>=3, then C*(G)<=1+((N-1)log(h)/h)^(1/N). The proof may reset finite-group graphings; no accumulation of earlier finite-extension costs occurs.
