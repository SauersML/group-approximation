Let `S` be finite nonabelian simple and suppose `20160=|A8|` divides `|S|`.
If

```text
44550 < |S|/20160 <= 48960,                          (SIL44-1)
```

then

```text
S ~= L4(4),             |S|/20160 = 48960.          (SIL44-2)
```

The endpoint is genuine, not merely arithmetic.  Entrywise scalar extension
gives the subfield embedding

```text
GL4(2) ~= A8  <  PSL4(4)=L4(4).                     (SIL44-3)
```

Here `|L4(4)|=987033600=48960*20160`.  The intervening rows after `McL` in
the complete simple-order table all fail divisibility by `20160`.
