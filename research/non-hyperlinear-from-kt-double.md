`D = G *_Gamma G` for the Kun--Thom Theorem E pair is finitely generated
(both vertex groups are), so if it is not hyperlinear it is a witness for
`non-hyperlinear-group` outright.  This is the negative horn of the fork whose
positive horn is `kt-double-mixed-word-selection-yields-hyperlinear-nonsofic`
into `hyperlinear-nonsofic-group`; the two routes together make the double a
decision point for both program roots.
