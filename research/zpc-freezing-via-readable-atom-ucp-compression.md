Choose a positive joint readable atom in the `J=-1` sector and compress the
IRS representation to it.  Perfect acceptance sends every selected frozen
equation word to its required scalar, so the proposed shortcut attempted to
descend this ucp compression through the frozen solution-group quotient.

This route is invalid: scalar compression places each named relator in the
multiplicative domain but does not kill its normal two-sided ideal.  A valid
descent must also authenticate all conjugate relators on the unreadable orbit
of the atom, which is the original remote-bit coherence problem.
