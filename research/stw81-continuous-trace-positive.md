Let `A` be a separable continuous-trace C-star algebra.  Suppose that every
commutative hereditary subalgebra of every quotient of `A` has primitive
spectrum of covering dimension at most one.  Then

```text
dim_nuc(A) <= 1.
```

Thus Problem LXXXI has a positive answer for separable continuous-trace
algebras, including those with infinite-dimensional irreducible
representations.
