Assuming the required OPEN retained-host hypothesis, take its
Gamma and put K=H=(E_nu)_p. Finite generation and core-freeness
are supplied by the induced-action claim. Finite bi-index is
part of the assumed host data, and K intersect E_nu=H holds
because K is already a subgroup of E_nu. This proves the new
claim conditionally, not the universal host hypothesis itself.
