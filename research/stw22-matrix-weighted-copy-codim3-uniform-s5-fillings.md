In normalized `M_n`, let `p=supp(a)`, `k=m rank(p)`, and `c=n-k`.  If
`a=0` the fibre is a point.  If `c>=3`, every weighted `S^5` in the ball of
radius

```text
rho_(mat,5)(epsilon)=min(epsilon,1)^3/276480
```

fills over `B^6` in the concentric `epsilon`-ball, uniformly in all matrix,
weight and copy parameters.  Every such fibre is five-connected.  For
projection weights the quadratic modulus is
`min(epsilon,1)^2/2560`.
