**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed)** by [[twisted-algebra-failure-root-of-unity-specialization-proof]].

Let `G` be any group, `K` any field and `α` any 2-cocycle `G × G -> K^x` for the trivial action.

**Theorem.** Suppose `M_n(K^α[G])` is not directly finite for some `n`. This includes any rank condition failure
`AB = I_d`, `r < d`, padded by zeros. Then for some prime `p` and finite field `F_q` of characteristic `p`,
`M_n(F_q[E_0])` is not directly finite. Here `E_0` is a finitely generated central extension of a finitely
generated `G_0 <= G` by a locally cyclic torsion group of order prime to `p`.

**Corollary.**
- `E_0` is not surjunctive.
- `INF(E_0)` and `RBS(E_0)` fail.
- Hence `∀G POS` fails, which refutes `every-group-has-positive-rokhlin-entropy-action`,
  `gottschalk-surjunctivity-conjecture`, and Kaplansky stable finiteness over finite fields.

**Mechanism.**
1. Take a residue map `ρ` of the finitely generated coefficient ring onto a finite field, with the witness
   coefficient of `BA - I` inverted (Nullstellensatz).
2. Extend `ρ` on the finitely many relevant cocycle values to a character `χ` of the whole value group into the
   divisible group `F̄_p^x`. Then `β = χ ∘ α` is a cocycle valued in roots of unity of order prime to `p`, and it
   carries the one-sided pair to `F̄_p^β[G]`. This needs no equivariance, because the action is trivial.
3. `F̄_p^β[G]` is a quotient of `F̄_p[M ×_β G]`. The central idempotent of the character of the finite group
   `M_1` generated by the relevant `β`-values kills the lifted defect `AB - I` and keeps `BA - I` nonzero.
   Padding with `1 - e` gives a one-sided pair in `M_n(F̄_p[E])`. This descends to `F_q` and to the finitely
   generated `E_0`.

**Impact (class killed).** Twisted group algebras cannot serve as *barriers* for
`some-field-crossed-product-fails-the-rank-condition` (need f489b4fe).
- Any trivial-action witness, with any cocycle over any field, is itself a counterexample to the flagship and to
  Gottschalk.
- It is exactly as hard to find as a non-stably-finite finite-field group algebra. The converse holds with
  `α = 1`.
- The ternary Leavitt and Deligne routes are the special cases where `α` is already torsion.

Over sofic `G`, a witness would give a nonsofic central extension of a sofic group.

With `bernoulli-field-rank-failure-gives-rokhlin-deficit` and the Lang–Weil kill, the only regimes where a rank
failure could be a genuine barrier (one that does not refute `INF`) are:
- a nontrivial action on a non-Bernoulli transcendental orbit field;
- a nontrivial action with a cocycle, including Bernoulli fields with a cocycle.

**Where it stops.** A nontrivial action needs an equivariant residue map, which is a finite-field point model of
the coefficient `G`-ring (artifact Section 6).
