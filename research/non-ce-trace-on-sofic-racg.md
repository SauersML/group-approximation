title: The fixed non-CE BCS trace pulls back to an explicit sofic right-angled Coxeter group
distinct_from:
  mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists: that supplies the tracial/non-RU BCS algebra; this identifies an ordinary sofic group already carrying its non-CE trace and isolates canonical-trace promotion as the remaining group problem.
  non-hyperlinear-group: a group may carry non-CE traces while its canonical delta trace is hyperlinear; this claim does not assert that the right-angled Coxeter host is nonhyperlinear.
---

Fix the finite BCS `B_*` from `mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists`.  Let `W_*`
be the right-angled Coxeter group generated by one involution `s_x` for every
BCS variable, with `[s_x,s_y]=1` exactly when `x,y` occur in a common context.
Then:

1. `W_*` is explicit, finitely presented, residually finite, and hence sofic;
2. the tracial but non-RU state `tau_*` on the BCS algebra pulls back along
   `C*(W_*) -> A_(B_*)` to a tracial state `tilde tau_*` on `C*(W_*)`;
3. `tilde tau_*` is not Connes embeddable; and
4. every forbidden BCS atom is a fixed group-algebra polynomial in the
   generators of `W_*`, and `tilde tau_*` vanishes on it.

Thus a non-CE trace already lives on a tame explicit sofic group.  The missing
operation is not construction of an exotic host but promotion of this trace
profile to constraints on a candidate group's canonical delta trace.
