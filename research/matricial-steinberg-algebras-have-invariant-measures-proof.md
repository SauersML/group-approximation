Artifact part 2, §3.
1. The ultralimit of normalized ranks is a Sylvester matrix rank function on the ultraproduct.
   Pulling it back along a unital homomorphism preserves the axioms and `rk(1) = 1`.
2. **Additivity.** Disjoint clopens give orthogonal idempotents, on which rank is additive.
3. **Invariance.** With `x = 1_B` and `y = 1_(B^(-1))`, `xy = 1_(r(B))` and `yx = 1_(s(B))`, and
   algebraically equivalent idempotents have equal rank.
4. **Extension.** A finitely additive probability on the clopen algebra of a compact metrizable
   totally disconnected space is countably additive on it, by compactness. Carathéodory extends it
   to a Borel probability measure.
5. **Item 3.** `M_m(∏_ω M_(N_n)(k)) ≅ ∏_ω M_(m N_n)(k)` is directly finite. For finite `k`, the
   groups `∏_ω GL_(m N_n)(k)` are ultraproducts of finite groups.
