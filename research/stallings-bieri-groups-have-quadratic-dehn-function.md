title: The Stallings--Bieri groups SB_n for n >= 3 have quadratic Dehn function and are of type F_{n-1} but not F_n
---

For `n ≥ 1` let `SB_n` be the kernel of the homomorphism
`F_2 × ⋯ × F_2 → Z` (`n` factors) that sends each standard generator to `1`.
This is the Bestvina--Brady group of the join of `n` copies of `S^0`. Then:

- `SB_n` is of type `F_{n-1}` but not of type `F_n` (Bieri);
- `SB_1` and `SB_2` are not finitely presented;
- for every `n ≥ 3`, `SB_n` has quadratic Dehn function (Carter--Forester).
