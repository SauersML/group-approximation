**Dead.** Balanced amplification makes many low-defect replicas, but the
replica state realizing `|chi(g)|^{2m}` is already a pure product across those
copies. `replica-definetti-saturation-no-go` therefore invalidates the intended
classicalization: a de Finetti conclusion about separability across replicas
cannot manufacture permutation assignment coherence inside one replica.

The residue is useful: a de Finetti attack must build symmetry on internal
assignment/context variables of the group-table relaxation.
