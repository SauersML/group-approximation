## Necessity and preservation of the ambient bundle

Let `q in M_k(D(X;x,r))` be a projection.  Connectedness makes its rank one
constant integer `d`.  At a marked point,

```text
q(x_i)=a_i tensor e_i
```

for a projection `a_i in M_k(C)`.  Thus

```text
d=rank(a_i) r_i,
0<=rank(a_i)<=k.                                        (1)
```

For a nonzero projection this gives `(VB)`.  In particular `d` is a positive
common multiple of all `r_i`, so `d>=L`; at an index of minimum marked rank,
(1) gives `k>=L/min_i r_i=m_0`.

## Correct any ambient projection at finitely many fibres

Conversely, fix an ambient projection `p in M_(kN)(C(X))` of rank `d`
satisfying `(VB)`.  At `x_i` choose a projection `a_i in M_k(C)` of rank
`d/r_i` and put

```text
b_i=a_i tensor e_i in M_(kN)(C).
```

The projections `p(x_i)` and `b_i` have the same rank.  Hence there is a
unitary `u_i in U(kN)` with

```text
u_i p(x_i) u_i^*=b_i.                                   (2)
```

Choose pairwise disjoint open neighbourhoods `O_i` of the marked points and
smaller neighbourhoods whose closures lie in the corresponding `O_i`.
Compact Hausdorff spaces are normal, so there are continuous functions

```text
chi_i:X->[0,1],
chi_i(x_i)=1,
supp(chi_i) subset O_i.                                 (3)
```

The finite-dimensional unitary group is path connected.  Choose a path
`gamma_i:[0,1]->U(kN)` from `1` to `u_i` and define

```text
U_i(x)=gamma_i(chi_i(x)),
U(x)=product_i U_i(x).                                  (4)
```

Outside `O_i`, the factor `U_i` is one.  The neighbourhoods are disjoint, so
at `x_i` all the other factors are one and `U(x_i)=u_i`.  Therefore

```text
q=U p U^*
```

satisfies `q(x_i)=b_i` for every `i`; hence
`q in M_k(D(X;x,r))`.  Unitary conjugation preserves the vector bundle and
its `K_0` class.  This proves the sufficiency in `(VB)` and shows that every
ambient projection class satisfying the arithmetic is hit.

## Exact first level and coefficient algebra

If `k>=m_0`, the constant rank-`L` projection in `M_(kN)(C(X))` exists:
indeed `kN>=m_0 min_i(r_i)=L`.  It satisfies `(VB)`, so the construction above
puts a conjugate in `M_k(D(X;x,r))`.  Together with the necessary bound this
proves `(FP)`.  At `k=1`, equation (1) permits a nonzero projection exactly
when all `r_i` are equal.

For a projection `P` at level `m_0`, its coefficient algebra `C_P` is a
subalgebra of the `N`-subhomogeneous algebra `D(X;x,r)` and is therefore
subhomogeneous and, when the marked ranks are unequal, projectionless.  Its
defining coefficients generate the ideal of `M_(m_0)(C_P)` generated by `P`,
so `P` is full.  The unital full corner is Morita equivalent to `C_P`, making
`Prim(C_P)` compact.  A projection in `M_j(C_P)` for `j<m_0` would also lie
in `M_j(D(X;x,r))`, contradicting `(FP)`.  Thus the exact threshold survives
passage to the coefficient algebra.
