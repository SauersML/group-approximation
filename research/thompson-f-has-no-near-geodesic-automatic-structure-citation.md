Citation import, not a reproof. The statement of Theorem 1.1 was read verbatim
from page 3 of arXiv:1801.01965v1 (J. D. Hauze, PhD dissertation, Binghamton
University, 2017; adviser M. Brin) on 2026-09-13. The proof occupies Chapter 4
of the dissertation (pages 15–80). It examines two specific families of elements
`f_k` and `g_k`, traces two different paths through the Cayley graph, and shows
that the length restriction along both paths forces contradictory properties.

This route asserts only that the dissertation proves what the claim records.
The dissertation is not known to have appeared in a refereed journal; the
imported proof was not re-derived this session.
