**ESTABLISHED SYNTHESIS.**  A finite-light-cone QCA challenge, after naming
its finitely many local registers, is a finite existential unitary extension
gadget.  Therefore a pre-enumerated meta-recursive QCA library which rejects
every separated complete canonical matrix-ultraproduct type has a finite
sublibrary and one positive optimized normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt residual
gap.  The finite choice is qualitative and need not be computable.

This statement uses complete tracial types and fixed finite auxiliary arity
for each gadget.  Scalar canonical word moments cannot fingerprint an
approximation.  If witnesses are restricted by an external correspondence
over quantifier-free profiles, the optimized residual need only be lower
semicontinuous; the closed nonextension neighborhoods, rather than an
unqualified continuity claim, are the safe input.

The resulting finite sublibrary gives a finitely presented nonhyperlinear
group only after all of the following independent conditions hold:

```text
finitely presented base,
one simultaneous exact marked completion,
canonical base-profile preservation in the finite extension,
and ordinary-word payment of the failed existential residual.       (MQC1)
```

Base-profile preservation follows, for example, from injectivity of the base
group into the final extension.  One exact completion with a nonzero mark does
not imply it.  If the base is merely recursively presented, selecting finitely
many QCA gadgets still does not produce a finite presentation: truncating the
base relators introduces extra canonical types not covered by the original
compactness argument.

Conversely, if every finite spacetime/capacity prefix has a finite matrix model
retaining a fixed marked mass, diagonalization gives a matrix-ultraproduct
model of the whole library.  Hence growing Clifford rank, PI degree, code
block size, or clock depth cannot be the semantic obstruction.  A QLTC may
amplify or synchronize an already non-CE semantic gap, but cannot create one
from exact matrix-realizable prefixes.

Normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm already has the correct tracial-verifier
meaning through maximally entangled vectorization.  Thus the strongest finite
endpoint of the QCA program is exactly
`central-sign-bcs-energy-decoder-implies-nonhyperlinear`: decode a fixed
non-CE BCS energy on one positive-density rounded central-sign carrier and
pay that ambient energy by ordinary presentation-word defect.  No dimension
oracle, defect schedule, online adversary, or infinite QCA depth remains after
that decoder is proved.

The unresolved cell is therefore the finite nonlinear
predicate-to-ordinary-word transducer on one common authenticated carrier.
The affine-control barrier prevents implementing it solely by commuting
involution parity equations.  Exact relative-commutant rounding can remove
transporter ambiguity for corrected coset fields, but does not provide the
rounding, the carrier density, or the nonlinear energy payment.

Even granting an exact ordinary-word verifier reflection does not let the
marked two-cycle cell make this payment.  By
`verifier-controlled-two-cycle-is-existentially-loss-blind`, its negative
spectral projection becomes a single zero-compression payload after adjoining
one flag qubit, but the same controlled swap exactly completes the cell at
every losing mass.  Thus `k=1` localization is solved conditionally; the
remaining transducer must be noncompletable in finite dimensions rather than
merely expose the losing carrier.

Nor can an infinite actor make the exact predicate reflection into one
universally scalar word.  `universal-central-selector-word-is-affine-for-any-actor`
uses the faithful regular trace to show that any word with prescribed scalar
values on every central marked selector atom lies in the elementary-abelian
selector subgroup and hence has affine phase.  Thus the nonlinear payment
cell must retain a non-scalar operator coordinate and use a matrix-only
estimate; moving from a finite packet to an infinite actor does not by itself
wordize the forbidden projection.
