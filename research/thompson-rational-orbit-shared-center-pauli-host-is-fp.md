**FINITE PRESENTATION AND EXACT SIGN INVISIBILITY ESTABLISHED;
HYPERLINEARITY OPEN.**

Let `V` be Thompson's group acting on the countable orbit `X=V.o` of a
rational point `o` in the binary Cantor set.  Let `E_X` be generated by

~~~text
J, A_x, B_x                    (x in X)
~~~

with `J` central, all generators involutions,

~~~text
[A_x,B_x]=J,
[A_x,A_y]=[A_x,B_y]=[B_x,A_y]=[B_x,B_y]=1     (x!=y),
~~~

and let `V` permute the site labels.  Then

~~~text
Pi_V=E_X semidirect V                                      (TVP1)
~~~

is finitely presented.

More explicitly, choose a finite presentation `V=<S|R>`, finite words
`T` generating `H=Stab_V(o)`, and `t in V` with `to!=o`.  A finite
presentation of `Pi_V` has generators `S,A,B,J` and relators

~~~text
R;
A^2=B^2=J^2=1;
[J,A]=[J,B]=[J,s]=1                         (s in S);
[A,B]=J;
[A,h]=[B,h]=1                               (h in T);
[u,t v t^(-1)]=1                            (u,v in {A,B}). (TVP2)
~~~

The marked involution `J` is nontrivial, but every exact
finite-dimensional unitary representation `rho` satisfies

~~~text
rho(J)=I.                                                  (TVP3)
~~~

The proof is not a new nonhyperlinearity theorem.  On the `J=-1` part,
an `m`-site window is exactly `M_(2^m) tensor I_r`, so fixed windows leave
the full multiplicity group `U(r)`.  Neither hyperlinearity nor
nonhyperlinearity of `Pi_V` is established here.

The literature boundary is exact.  Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell
prove that a sofic set action and hyperlinear actor make the corresponding
noncommutative generalized wreath crossed product Connes embeddable.  Their
Questions 4.2 and 4.4 leave the commuting-action and generalized-wreath
converses open.  Naming both Pauli generators makes every fixed tensor
window spatial, but does not remove the moving multiplicity reservoir.
Therefore a converse

~~~text
Pi_V hyperlinear  =>  V action X sofic                    (TVP4)
~~~

would be a new global theorem, not a consequence of the published wreath
permanence result.
