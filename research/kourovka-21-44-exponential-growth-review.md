**ESTABLISHED (referee bh-ref-d, 2026-09-19).** Verdict: **PASS.** No priority claimed.

- **`G_0`.** The setwise stabilizer of `{1, 5}` in `A_5` is `{1, (234), (243), (15)(23), (15)(24),
  (15)(34)} ≅ S_3`. It is generated by two of the double transpositions. Its pointwise stabilizer of
  `{1, 5}` is `⟨(234)⟩ ≅ Z/3`.
- **Step 1.**
  - The invariant holds for the generators: level-1 permutation in `G_0`, sections at 1 and 5 in `K`,
    trivial sections at 2, 3 and 4.
  - It is preserved by `(gh)|_x = g|_x h|_(x^g)`, because `G_0` preserves both `{1,5}` and `{2,3,4}`.
  - So `K` preserves `B = {1,5}*`.
- **Step 2.**
  - For `g ∈ N`, `g(uxw) = u ρ_u(x) w` with `x ∈ {2,3,4}`. Here `ρ_u` fixes 1 and 5, so it lies in
    `Z/3`.
  - The map `g ↦ (ρ_u)` is injective and a homomorphism.
  - So `N ↪ (Z/3)^B` is abelian of exponent 3.
- **Step 3.**
  - `F(d_t)` is trivial for even `t` and equals `b = (σ, b)` for odd `t`.
  - `σ² = b² = 1`.
  - `bσ = (σ, b)σ` has section product `σb = (bσ)^(-1)`. The binary criterion (`(g_1, g_2)σ` is
    level-transitive iff `g_1 g_2` is) and induction give level-transitivity. So `bσ` has infinite
    order, and `K/N ≅ D_∞`.
- **Step 4.**
  - `d_r ∈ N`, and its `ρ`-support is the ray `{5^k 1}`: `ρ_(5^k 1) = r`, and every other `ρ_u` is `1`.
  - Conjugation by `z^m`, with `F(z) = bσ`, moves the support by `F(z)^(∓m)`. It changes labels only by
    `r ↦ r^(±1)`, which stays nonzero.
  - Level-transitivity separates the conjugates at any level `k + 1` with `2^(k+1) ∤ (m − m′)`. So `N` is
    infinite.
- **Conclusion.**
  - `K = ⟨s, s′, d_s, d_(s′)⟩` is finitely generated and abelian-by-`D_∞`, hence solvable.
  - `N` is an infinite abelian group of exponent 3, so it is not finitely generated. So `K` is not
    virtually nilpotent: finitely generated virtually nilpotent groups are Noetherian.
  - Milnor–Wolf: a finitely generated solvable group has polynomial growth iff it is virtually nilpotent,
    and exponential growth otherwise. So `K` has exponential growth.
  - Growth passes up from finitely generated subgroups, so `H` and `P` have exponential growth. Neither
    is a 21.44 witness.
- **Non-injectivity of `Z⁺ → H`.**
  - The element is determined by the level-1 permutation, the labels `s_i ∈ S*`, and the streams
    `σ_i = 5^(P_i^(-1))`, with `σ_i ≠ σ_(i−1)`. That gives at most `C·(12·4)^k = C·48^k` values against
    `144^k` words, so the map is non-injective for large `k`.
  - I did not check the constant 3. It is irrelevant.
- **Mechanism remark.** The configuration transfers to any pair `{i, j}` in `W`: `Stab_(A_5){i,j} ≅ S_3`
  always contains swaps, and its pointwise stabilizer is `Z/3`. The claim holds as stated for spinal
  groups containing it.
