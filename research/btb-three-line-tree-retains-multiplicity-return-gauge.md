**ESTABLISHED EDGEWISE NO-GO.**  Fix a finite-depth version of the protected
binary-tetrahedral construction.  At a node let `P` be the fixed finite
packet generated by the protected carrier signs, the BTB factor, the context
Reynolds subgroup, and the two-pair E5 cell.  After exactification, every
`P`-reducing carrier has the form

```text
Q=direct_sum_(lambda in Irr(P)) 1_(V_lambda) tensor q_lambda.   (BTR1)
```

The conditioned fiber from `(CBR2)` is correspondingly

```text
F=direct_sum_lambda f_lambda tensor q_lambda,                  (BTR2)
```

for fixed packet projections `f_lambda in End(V_lambda)`.  The BTB and E5
calculations prove that the sum in `(BTR2)` has positive trace on the
relevant negative-center types.  They do not constrain the projections
`q_lambda` in the multiplicity spaces.

Suppose a proposed return at this edge uses only:

1. finite packet and finite-subgroup covariance;
2. the stabilized three-line/Murray--von Neumann source--range swap; and
3. relations involving this edge intertwiner alone, with no word containing
   intertwiners from two different adaptive edges and no payload-sensitive
   operator on the multiplicity space.

On every matching source--range type, Schur's lemma gives the return word the
form

```text
W_lambda=C_lambda tensor U_lambda,                           (BTR3)
```

where `C_lambda` is the authenticated finite-dimensional outer
intertwiner and `U_lambda` is an arbitrary unitary between equal
multiplicity reservoirs.  Torsion clauses merely restrict `U_lambda` to a
nonempty finite-order subset and do not choose its invariant subspaces.

The typed rectangular model for the three-line word permits the source and
range object identities to occupy the same analytic carrier projection
`q_lambda`.  Choose `U_lambda` to preserve `Ran(q_lambda)` (and satisfy any
finite-order clause separately on `q_lambda` and its complement).  Then

```text
W F W^* <= Q,
tau(Q W F W^* Q)=tau(F).                                  (BTR4)
```

Thus no positive `eta` in `(CBR3)` follows from these edgewise rows, even
after the source has been reduced to the positive `1/18` BTB--E5 fiber.

The obstruction persists simultaneously through a finite adaptive tree.
Choose positive regular packet multiplicities using the stationary-flow
construction.  Every protected central-sign character and every relevant
BTB negative-center type then occurs with positive multiplicity, and the
two restrictions at each edge agree.  Because no relation contains two
different edge intertwiners, choose the gauges `U_(sigma,i)` recursively,
each preserving the already chosen reached multiplicity carrier.  Equation
`(BTR4)` then holds at every reached edge at zero packet and covariance
defect.

This is not detected by adding finitely many canonical packet moments.
After amplifying the stationary model, put identical regular finite-window
models of each edge gauge on `q_lambda` and on its complement.  All fixed
word traces of the packet and edge covariance tables are unchanged, while
the ownership projection `q_lambda` remains invariant.  The analytic
carrier is not a group-algebra idempotent, so those moments do not record
which copy owns it.

Consequently the repaired BTB route needs a relation outside the stated
class.  Minimally, one word must involve two edge intertwiners or compare an
edge intertwiner with a payload operator acting nontrivially on its
multiplicity space.  Such a row must have nonzero multiplicity charge while
remaining vacuous when the forbidden BCS projection vanishes in the perfect
marked model.  More protected signs, finite root packets, first-hit tags, or
separate three-line swaps cannot supply that charge.

The tempting order-three controlled-reflection palette illustrates both
sides of the boundary.  If the second reflection belongs to the finite
packet reduced by `Q`, it preserves `Q` and gives no escape.  If only its
covariance of the order-three root and protected sign is imposed, it is an
actuator of the form `(BTR3)` and its multiplicity gauge can be aligned as
above.  If one instead fixes the complete irreducible `S_3` outer action, the
second reflection can force one genuine outer-type escape, but the reducing
hull then contains the complete two-dimensional reflection orbit and that
same palette cannot renew the escape.  Giving every level an independent
palette returns exactly to the independent-address capacity ledger: the
new outer orbit capacity is paid at its source.  Thus the small
root-controlled reflection is a valid one-shot cell, not a proof of the
finite-depth supercritical tree.
