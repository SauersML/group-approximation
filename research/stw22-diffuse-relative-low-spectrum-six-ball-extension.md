In a separable-predual `II_1` factor, let `H:B^6->eN` satisfy
`H^*H=p_h`, put `f=e-HH^*`, and let `b` have support `p_l` with
`tau(p_h)+tau(p_l)<tau(e)`.  Every boundary `x:S^5->eN` satisfying
`x^*x=b`, `fx=x` extends over `B^6` with those exact equations.  If
`b<=eta`, its displacement from any fixed `x_0^*x_0=b` is at most
`2sqrt(tau(b))<=2sqrt(eta tau(p_l))`.
