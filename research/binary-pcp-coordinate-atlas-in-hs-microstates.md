Compile the verifier of `simple-algebraic-pcp-binary-local-test` into
a finite or recursively self-similar contextual group atlas with these
properties.

1. Every proof coordinate is represented by one shared involution word across
   all checks which query it.
2. Only coordinates occurring in a common check are forced to commute; no
   global Boolean proof assignment is introduced.
3. Private low-degree/Hadamard auxiliaries may be installed using
   `mastel-slofstra-private-context-augmentation-preserves-defect`.
4. Every rejected constant-query predicate is compiled by the fixed-scale S3
   radical packet and contributes local group-word HS energy.
5. The exact honest/commuting model and its marked phase extend through the
   atlas.

The resulting quantitative bound must compare the verifier's rejection
probability directly with normalized-HS relator energy with constants
independent of proof length and matrix dimension.

## Attempts

- Making all proof-bit involutions commute converts the verifier into a
  classical CSP.  That destroys the contextual/non-CE input and fails the
  finite graph-of-finite-groups falsification test.
- Creating a private copy of each queried bit loses consistency between
  checks.  PCP soundness then says nothing until the copies are aligned with
  dimension-free cost.
