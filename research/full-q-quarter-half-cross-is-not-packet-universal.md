**ESTABLISHED SCALAR-FORMULA FENCE.**  Retain the exact eight-projective-
summand realization of the full Hecke packet and put

```text
Q=e_(L_0,lambda_0),
F=Q(1+r)(1+l)/4,       r=x_58(a_2),       l=x_69(a_3),
g=J_1J_2.                                                   (QHF1)
```

Let `G` be the finite subgroup of the binary matrix group on these eight
summands generated by the full packet, and let `lambda_G` be its faithful
left regular representation.  Then `F` is nonzero but

```text
lambda_G(FgF) != (1/2)lambda_G(F).                         (QHF2)
```

Indeed, with

```text
K=<L_0,r,l>,                                                (QHF3)
```

all coefficient roots defining `K` are simultaneously unitriangular in
the eight-summand chart.  Thus `K` is a finite `2`-group.  On the other
hand the literal adjacent Whiteheads act on the middle coordinates as

```text
J_1=(u_1 u_2),                 J_2=(u_2 u_3),              (QHF4)
```

so `g` has order three and cannot lie in `K`.

The group-algebra support of `F` is contained in `K`, whereas the support
of `FgF` is contained in the disjoint double coset `KgK`.  Faithfulness of
the regular representation now proves `(QHF2)`.  Therefore the exact
one-half compression found in the two adjacent-character constituents is
a feature of those quotient/stabilizer types, not a consequence of the
full finite packet relations alone.

This does **not** show that `FgF` vanishes, and it does not refute a
representation-universal positive lower bound for

```text
tr((FgF)^*(FgF))/tr(F).                                    (QHF5)
```

It fences only the proposed exact scalar identity.  No Property `(T)`,
canonical-trace substitution, computation, local build, literature input,
or Lean compilation is used.
