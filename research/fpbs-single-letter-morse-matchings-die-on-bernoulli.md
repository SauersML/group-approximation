**ESTABLISHED (written deduction).** Proof in `fpbs-single-letter-morse-matchings-die-on-bernoulli-proof`.

**Setting.**
- `Gamma` is generated by a finite set `S`, and `w = s_1^{e_1} ... s_l^{e_l}` is a trivial word (one cell type).
- `s in S` with `s^2 != 1` occurs `k >= 2` times, at occurrence bases `h_i = p_i^{-1}` (if `e_i = +1`) or
  `p_{i-1}^{-1}` (if `e_i = -1`), where `p_i` is the length-`i` prefix. Assume the `h_a` are pairwise distinct
  (true for cyclically reduced one-relator relators by Weinbaum's theorem).
- `Q = {h_a h_b^{-1} : a != b}`, and `Lambda_s = <Q>`. It depends only on the cyclic word.
- A *single-letter matching* is a Morse matching `M` for `{w}` (M1–M3 of `fpbs-bernoulli-morse-collapse-criterion`)
  whose removed edges are all `s`-edges, with the position in each cell an arbitrary Borel function of the cell.

**Theorem.**
1. **(Any free p.m.p. action.)** Put `delta = 1 - mu(M)`, and let `A_j` be the set of `s`-edges of height `<= j`.
   Then `mu(A_j Δ q A_j) <= 2 k delta` for all `q in Q`, and
   `(k - 1) mu{ht = J} <= k((1 - mu(A_J)) + delta)`.
2. **(Obstruction.)** On the Bernoulli shift of `Gamma` or any free factor of it,
   `1 - mu(M) >= (k - 1)(1 - rho)/(6 k^2)`,
   where `rho` is the Kesten spectral radius of `Lambda_s` at `Q`.

**Instance.** For a torsion-free one-relator `<S | w>` the Morse ceiling at `{w}` is `1`. So a letter with nonamenable
`Lambda_s` gives a uniform positive deficit for all its single-letter matchings. For `w = abaBabcdcDcd` this holds
at all four letters (Freiheitssatz witnesses, computed by the script). Yet that group,
`F(a,b) *_Z F(c,d)`, has fixed price. So this is an obstruction to the method, not to the group.

**Class killed.** Dual-forest / Borel-peeling matchings at one letter used `k >= 3` times, with any positions.
- *Invariant:* the Kesten gap `1 - rho(Lambda_s, Q)` on `L^2_0`.
- *Step where it dies:* (M3). Well-foundedness gives an exhausting height filtration whose sublevels are almost
  `Lambda_s`-invariant (the top property). The gap forces a single level of measure near `1`, which Lemma 3
  forbids.

**Survivors.** Multi-letter matchings, several cell types (`m >= 2`, transversal modulation), and longer word
lists.

**Calibration.**
- `k = 2` makes `Lambda_s` cyclic, and the dual forest works.
- For amenable `Gamma`, and on PW's coset action, (3.1) fails and nothing is claimed.
- In `abacad` (which is `F_3`) the letter `a` is obstructed while `b` (`k = 1`) reaches the ceiling.
