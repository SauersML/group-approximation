Start with a charged representation of `Gamma`, induce it to `G`, and put a
Banach-limit probability charge on the product basis indexed by
`G/Gamma`.  Allow direct sums, arbitrary multiplicity in each fibre, and
phase twists of the induced monomial actors.

This architecture is impossible by
`fournier-facio-compression-coset-charge-impossible`: the basis projection to
`G/Gamma` pushes any invariant charge to a forbidden invariant mean.  The
failure occurs before essential freeness or near multiplicativity is tested.
