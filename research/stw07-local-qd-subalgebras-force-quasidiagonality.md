Let `A` be a separable unital C*-algebra.  Suppose that for every finite
subset `F subset A` and every `epsilon>0` there is a unital quasidiagonal
C*-subalgebra `B subset A` such that

```text
max_(x in F) dist(x,B)<epsilon.                          (LQ1)
```

Then `A` is quasidiagonal.

Consequently, an injective directed inductive limit of separable unital
quasidiagonal C*-algebras is quasidiagonal.  No nuclearity hypothesis is
needed.
