ESTABLISHED.

The most direct self-similar checksum adds a shift `h` and a renormalizer `q`
with

```text
q h q^-1=h^2,
q c q^-1=c (h c h^-1),
[c,h c h^-1]=1.                                           (DPC1)
```

Conjugating and iterating `(DPC1)` tests commuting parities of adjacent
dyadic blocks at every virtual scale with no address-length loss.  These
relations nevertheless have an exact finite model in which two individual
lamps anticommute maximally.

Index modulo `9` and define

```text
f(d)=1  iff  d in {2,4,5,7}.                              (DPC2)
```

Let `E` be generated by a central involution `J` and involutions
`c_0,...,c_8`, with

```text
[c_i,c_j]=J^(f(j-i)),                                     (DPC3)
c_0 c_3 c_6=c_1 c_4 c_7=c_2 c_5 c_8=1.                  (DPC4)
```

This is a finite extraspecial group of order `2^(1+6)`.  In particular
`J!=1`.  One direct verification is to take the circulant alternating form

```text
b(e_i,e_j)=f(j-i)
```

on `F_2^9`.  Its radical is exactly the span of

```text
e_0+e_3+e_6,  e_1+e_4+e_7,  e_2+e_5+e_8;                (DPC5)
```

the leading `6 x 6` minor is nonsingular.  Quotienting by `(DPC5)` gives the
nondegenerate six-dimensional commutator space of `E`.

There are automorphisms `T,Phi` of `E` given by

```text
T(c_i)=c_(i+1),
Phi(c_i)=c_(2i)c_(2i+1),
T(J)=Phi(J)=J.                                             (DPC6)
```

The two factors in `Phi(c_i)` commute because `f(1)=0`.  Preservation of all
commutators is the nine-entry identity

```text
f(d)=f(2d-1)+f(2d+1)  in F_2.                             (DPC7)
```

Moreover `Phi` sends the three radical words respectively to the products of
radical words `(r_0r_1,r_0r_2,r_1r_2)`.  It therefore descends to `E`.
Since it preserves the nondegenerate quotient commutator form, it is an
automorphism.  Directly from `(DPC6)`,

```text
Phi T Phi^-1=T^2.                                         (DPC8)
```

Now take the finite semidirect product of `E` by the subgroup generated by
`T,Phi`, and represent it unitarily by its left regular representation.  Put

```text
c=c_0,       h=T,       q=Phi.
```

All relations `(DPC1)` and every recursively generated dyadic block check
hold exactly.  But `(DPC2)--(DPC3)` give

```text
[c,h^2 c h^-2]=[c_0,c_2]=J!=1.                            (DPC9)
```

Thus the checksum can hide a stationary noncommuting Pauli gauge behind
perfectly commuting block parities.  No squared-HS or first-hit argument
using only `(DPC1)` can recover the individual all-pairs lamp table: the
countermodel has zero premise defect and a maximal missing commutator.

This finite model is not a representation of the full arithmetic actor with
the root lamp centralized by `SL_3(Z)`; exact finite-dimensional co-density
would forbid that extension.  Therefore it does not refute a genuinely
arithmetic matrix-only relative-commutant theorem.  It proves that the
two-child self-similar checksum supplies no independent amplification beyond
that arithmetic input.
