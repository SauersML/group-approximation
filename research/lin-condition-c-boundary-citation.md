**Established by citation.** Huaxin Lin, *Tracial oscillation zero and
Z-stability*, [arXiv:2112.12036v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.12036) (revised
21 January 2024, to appear in Advances in Mathematics), Theorem 5.6. For a
separable non-elementary simple amenable C*-algebra whose tracial cone basis
satisfies condition (C), Z-stability and strict comparison are equivalent.

The examples given in the paper include:

- one-point compactifications of countable disjoint unions of `n`-cubes;
- Bauer simplices with finite-dimensional extreme boundary;
- non-Bauer simplices whose extreme boundaries consist of isolated points
  accumulating on compact finite-dimensional sets.

Condition (C) and its examples were read from the arXiv HTML of v6 on
2026-09-11.
