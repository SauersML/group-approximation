The full argument is Section 5 of the artifact.

1. **A continuous family of representations.**  For `t` in `T`, put
   `Pi_t(s_(e_i))=T_i`, `Pi_t(s_(g_j))=G_j`, `Pi_t(s_f)=tE_v`,
   `Pi_t(p_v)=E_v` and `Pi_t(p_u)=1-E_v`.
   * The Cuntz--Krieger relation at `u` holds because every word `w g_j`
     is either `g_j` or begins with exactly one loop.
   * At `v` it holds trivially.
   * This gives `Pi:C*(F_(n,k))->C(T,B(l^2 P))`.
2. **The range.**  `T_wG_j=|delta_(w g_j)><delta_v|` generates all matrix
   units, so `K subseteq D_0`.  Conjugating `E_v tensor z` by constant matrix
   units gives `C(T,K)`, so the range is `C(T,K)+D_0 tensor 1`.
3. **Injectivity.**  Let `U_lambda` multiply `delta_x` by `lambda^|x|`, and
   put `beta_lambda(Phi)(t)=U_lambda Phi(lambda t)U_lambda^*`.
   * `beta` is a strongly continuous action on the range.
   * It intertwines the gauge action.
   * Both vertex projections have nonzero image.

   So the gauge-invariant uniqueness theorem makes `Pi` injective.
4. **The quotient.**  `D_0/K` is generated by `n` isometries whose range
   projections sum to `1` modulo compacts.  So `D_0/K` is a nonzero quotient
   of the simple algebra `O_n`, hence equal to it.
5. **Conclusion.**  `dim_nuc(O_n)=1` by Winter--Zacharias, *The nuclear
   dimension of C\*-algebras*, Theorem 7.4.  This was checked in the TeX
   source of arXiv:0903.4914v1 on 2026-09-16; the journal numbering was not
   checked.  It also follows from `O_infinity`-stability and nuclearity of
   `O_n` via Bosa--Gabe--Sims--White, Theorem A, as cited in Evington's
   paper.  The required claim applies with `X=T`, `D=D_0` and
   `max(1,1)=1`, and gives `1<=dim_nuc<=2`.
