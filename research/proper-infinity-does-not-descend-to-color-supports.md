title: Proper infiniteness of an ambient support does not pass to nuclear-dimension color supports
---

There are a unital C-star algebra `B`, a properly infinite positive element
`h in B`, and a nonzero positive contraction `p<=h` which is finite.  Hence a
nuclear-dimension proof cannot infer proper infiniteness of an outgoing
order-zero color support merely from domination by a properly infinite image
element.
