Let `G` be the finite group generated by the recentered actor matrices and
let `j=J_2`.  The literal action of `j` swaps the two distinct prefix pairs
indexed by `8,9`, so `j != 1` in `G`.  The left regular representation of
`G` is faithful.  Its irreducible decomposition therefore contains a
constituent `sigma` with `sigma(j) != 1`.

Tensor the canonical five-qubit Schrodinger representation of the Pauli
group with `sigma`.  For every actor `g`, use

```text
g |-> S_g tensor sigma(g),                             (1)
```

where `S_g` is its canonical label implementer.  Since both tensor factors
are honest representations of the same finite actor group, every actor
product, covariance, braid, and seventh-power relation remains exact.

The label implementer `S_j` is the swap on two two-dimensional tensor
factors and the identity on the remaining three.  The antisymmetric line
in the swapped two-qubit factor, tensored with the remaining eight label
coordinates, is its negative eigenspace.  Hence

```text
dim(label)=32,             rank_-(S_j)=8.              (2)
```

Put `m=dim(sigma)` and `k=rank_- sigma(j)`.  Since `j^2=1`, `sigma(j)` is a
self-adjoint involution; since it is not the identity, `k>0`.  On its
positive reservoir subspace `S_j tensor sigma(j)` has the same signs as
`S_j`, while on its negative reservoir subspace the signs are reversed.
Equation `(2)` therefore gives

```text
rank_-(S_j tensor sigma(j))
 =8(m-k)+(32-8)k
 =8m+16k.                                              (3)
```

If `R` is a quarter-source reflection, then `rank_-(R)=8m`.  Unitary
conjugation preserves this rank.  Two finite-dimensional projections of
different ranks have operator-norm distance one: the larger range contains
a unit vector orthogonal to the smaller range.  Applying this to the
negative projections and multiplying by two proves the norm-two identity.

Finally, the commutant of the Schrodinger Pauli algebra in `(1)` is
`1 tensor B(V_sigma)`.  Intersecting it with the commutant of all actors
gives `1 tensor sigma(G)'=C`, by Schur irreducibility.  Thus no proper
nonzero reducing projection can remove the rank mismatch.
