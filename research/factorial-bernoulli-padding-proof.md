Start with any faithful perfect tracial model of `G` and decompose its
generated finite von Neumann algebra over the center. Exact defining
relations and zero losing projections disintegrate fiberwise, so almost every
factor component is again perfect. Choose one and call it `N_0`. It cannot
be Connes embeddable: a Connes-embeddable perfect component would yield
finite-dimensional strategies with loss tending to zero, contradicting
`v=omega*(G)<1`.

Let

```text
Y={0,1}^Z
```

with Bernoulli product measure. Let the infinite dihedral group generated by
reflections `r,s` act by

```text
(r y)_k=y_(-k),              (s y)_k=y_(1-k).
```

The product `w=rs` is the bilateral shift. This action is ergodic because
it contains the Bernoulli shift, and it is essentially free: a nonidentity
shift or reflection fixes only a null set of Bernoulli configurations.
Consequently

```text
R_B=L^infinity(Y) semidirect D_infinity^dih
```

is a finite factor. In the negative `J=-1` corner, the remote readable
involution `b=J^(y_0)` has spectral projections equal to the two
coordinate-zero cylinders. Conjugating by powers of `w` generates every
coordinate cylinder, hence the readable translate algebra contains the
diffuse algebra `L^infinity(Y)`.

Tensor the chosen component with this remote action. Because the old and new
generators are independent and together generate their respective factors,
the resulting game algebra is

```text
N=N_0 bar_tensor R_B,                                   (FBP2)
```

which is a factor. It is not Connes embeddable: if `N` were embeddable,
the trace-preserving expected subalgebra `N_0 bar_tensor 1` would be
embeddable as well.

Mix the original verifier into the padded game with probability `eta` and
use only identically accepting remote checks otherwise. Tensoring the old
perfect model with the Bernoulli action stays perfect, while every ordinary
quantum strategy loses at least `eta(1-v)`. This proves `(FBP1)`.

Finally suppose `0!=p` lies below a full readable profile and has finite
structural orbit. Some `w^n`, `n>=1`, fixes `p`. If the profile fixes
the zeroth Bernoulli bit to `a`, then conjugating its containment by every
power of `w^n` gives

```text
p<=1_(y_(kn)=a)  for every k in Z.
```

The meet of these commuting cylinder projections is zero, so `p=0`, a
contradiction. This is the non-diagonal projection argument from the remote
padding obstruction and completes the claim.
