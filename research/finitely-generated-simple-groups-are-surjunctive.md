**OPEN.** For every finitely generated simple group `S` and finite alphabet `A`,
every injective cellular automaton `A^S -> A^S` is surjective.

This is equivalent to Gottschalk's conjecture
(`gottschalk-via-finitely-generated-simple-groups`): a counterexample over any
group localizes to the subgroup generated by the automaton's memory, which embeds
in a finitely generated simple group, and non-surjunctivity passes to that
overgroup.

## Attempts

- **Reduction is the point, not a proof.** The equivalence lets any attack assume
  the host is simple: no proper normal subgroup, no proper finite-index subgroup,
  no finite quotient. That removes exactly the quotient and finite-approximation
  structure the permanence theorems peel.
- **The concrete nonsofic instances.** `R^x = L_{F_2}(1,2)^x` is a finitely
  generated (indeed finitely presented) simple nonsofic group
  (`leavitt-unit-group-is-simple-fa-nonsofic`), and Thompson's `V` is finitely
  presented infinite simple with `V <= R^x`. Surjunctivity of `R^x` is the root
  `leavitt-unit-group-surjunctive`; of `V`, the root `thompson-v-surjunctive`.
- **Why the toolkit is silent on simple hosts.** Permanence from sofic groups
  misses every finitely presented simple nonsofic group
  (`leavitt-tower-outside-sofic-permanence-closure`,
  `sofic-permanence-closure-omits-simple-fa-nonsofic-groups`); counting is dead
  where the sofic radical is full
  (`sofic-radical-localizes-garden-of-eden-windows`); coset peeling through a
  Cantor action is circular (`thompson-v-coset-stabilizers-contain-copies-of-v`).
  The surviving positive route is internal and measure-theoretic
  (`injective-measure-preserving-ca-is-surjective`).
