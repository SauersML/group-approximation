Let `A` be a unital simple pure C-star-algebra.  Then the canonical map

```text
U(A)/U_0(A) -> K_1(A)
```

is an isomorphism.

No separability hypothesis is needed: unitality gives sigma-unitality, and
Lin's published pure dichotomy applies in that generality.
