Centrality makes `e(B)` commute with the diagonal action. The exact
intertwining identity then makes `CTC` a partial isometry whose two
defects are written explicitly. Finite-dimensional rank equality
follows from the two conjugate cuts, including all central isotypic
cuts and their complements.

The explicit permutation on `G^3` intertwines both prescribed finite
factors but sends the second invariant projection outside the algebra
generated by the original pair. Its regular amplification extends to
the large packet, and regular restriction multiplicities allow the
other packet to be glued along the entire overlap. Thus this algebra
invariance inference fails on the full admissible domain as well.

Decomposing into irreducible `A x B` summands identifies the second
invariant defect as the contragredient-pair channel. A stochastic
tensor transition on nontrivial irreducibles has a rational stationary
vector. Clearing denominators gives integer representation
multiplicities with equivalent diagonal and second-factor restrictions
but no second-factor fixed vectors. The artifact proves this only for
the displayed reduced relations.

For any finitary group-algebra projection, extend the shift on its
finite coordinate support to a finite permutation. This places the
projection and its shifted conjugate as equivalent projections inside
one finite group algebra. Polar decomposition and cancellation in each
simple block explicitly pair the two defects, giving a unitary
completion in the original corner under every representation.
