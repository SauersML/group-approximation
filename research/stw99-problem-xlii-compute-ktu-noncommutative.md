**Problem XLII of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**
Compute `KT_u(A ⋊ G)` for outer amenable actions on unital simple
separable nuclear `Z`-stable algebras.

## Attempts

* Same bottleneck as XXXVII (the pairing), plus a new one: traces of
  `A ⋊ G` are no longer classified by an Ursu-type invariant-measure
  statement in general (that requires essential freeness notions on
  the noncommutative side — Kennedy--Schafhauser territory).  The
  natural closed sub-endpoint: for strongly outer actions, every trace
  on `A ⋊ G` is `τ ∘ E` for an invariant `τ ∈ T(A)` — this is exactly
  the von Neumann weak-Rokhlin mechanism of Matui--Sato and holds in
  their finite-extremal-boundary setting; its wild-boundary version is
  yet another instance of the equivariant-glueing frontier recorded at
  XXXVIII/XXXIX.
