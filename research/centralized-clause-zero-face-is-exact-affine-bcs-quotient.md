Let `Gamma` be generated by a finite decoded tuple `z_x`, auxiliary letters,
and central clause words `u_1,...,u_m`. Put

```text
D=sum_i (u_i-1)^*(u_i-1),
N=normal_closure_Gamma({u_i}),
barGamma=Gamma/N.                                      (CZQ1)
```

For a finite BCS residual list `R_B`, write

```text
H_B=sum_(R in R_B) R(z)^*R(z).                         (CZQ2)
```

Then the following are equivalent.

1. Every state `phi` on `C*(Gamma)` with `phi(D)=0` satisfies
   `phi(H_B)=0`.
2. Every residual vanishes as a group-algebra element in the clause
   quotient:

   ```text
   R(bar z)=0 in C[barGamma]          for every R in R_B. (CZQ3)
   ```

The same equivalence holds if item 1 is restricted to states tracial on the
decoded subgroup, because the quotient regular trace is such a state.

Consequently, for each BCS context `c`, `(CZQ3)` makes the images of its
decoded observables commuting involutions. Let

```text
A_c=<bar z_x : x in U_c> <= barGamma.                  (CZQ4)
```

Then `A_c` is an elementary abelian two-group. The assignments whose joint
spectral projections are nonzero in `C[A_c]` are exactly

```text
Hom(A_c,{+1,-1}),                                      (CZQ5)
```

viewed inside the full Boolean cube by evaluation on the generators. Thus
they form a linear subspace of the assignment cube. Vanishing of every
forbidden BCS projection is equivalent to

```text
Hom(A_c,{+1,-1}) subseteq Allowed_c.                   (CZQ6)
```

In particular, centralizing already-wordized Gram rows does not turn a
trace-level gap into nonlinear exact zero-face semantics. It reduces
Condition 3 of `finite-normal-clause-orbit-bcs-compiler` to the stronger
problem of constructing one exact group quotient whose context spectra are
affine sub-BCSs of the allowed predicates.

There is also a precise positive bookkeeping consequence. If a perfect
target representation sends every `u_i` to `1` and represents a finite
central phase group `B` faithfully by scalars, then

```text
N intersect B={1}.                                    (CZQ7)

```

Hence exact row completeness already proves the phase-intersection half of
Condition 4. What remains of Conditions 1/3/4 after
`centralized-gram-row-words-upgrade-trace-gap-to-vector-gap` is:

- authenticate row words which are exact in the perfect phase model;
- prove the exact affine quotient condition `(CZQ3)--(CZQ6)`; and
- realize the resulting central clauses in the required toric two-player
  syntax.

The first item preserves completeness and phase safety automatically. The
second is the genuine nonlinear obstruction; a finite conjugation orbit or
singleton central orbit supplies no selected-type escape from it.

The necessary half does not actually require centrality.
`noncentral-toric-orbits-have-affine-full-support-zero-face` applies the same
regular-trace test to an arbitrary finite noncentral toric clause orbit and
shows that normal closure erases its noncentral player action. Centrality is
used here only for the converse GNS factorization, and hence for the exact
equivalence in this claim.
