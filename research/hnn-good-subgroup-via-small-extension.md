## Why sufficient

The route was worked out against Mathlib's API at the pinned revision and is
recorded in `BooneGroupTower` so the next attempt starts from a plan rather
than from the statement.  It removes the induction entirely.

Goodness restricts `phi` to `phi1 : A1 =~ B1` on the comapped subgroups of `A`,
and

    psi = HNNExtension.lift (of . A.subtype) t hx
        : HNNExtension A A1 B1 phi1 ->* HNNExtension G Asub Bsub phi

is well defined, with `hx` supplied by `equiv_eq_conj` -- which is exactly the
already-proved `good_pinch`.  Its range is `liftedSubgroup phi A`, since both
are generated by `of '' A` and `t`.

Now take `g in A' cap of.range` and write `g = psi u`.  Every element of the
small extension has a reduced spelling (`NormalWord` exists, via `prod_smul`
and `prod_empty`), so pick a reduced word `w` for `u` there.  By the
prerequisite, its image is a reduced word for `g` in the big extension.  Since
`g` lies in `of.range`, Britton's Lemma in Mathlib's exported form
(`ReducedWord.toList_eq_nil_of_mem_of_range`) forces `w.toList = []`.  Hence
`u = of w.head` and `g = of (w.head : G)`, which lies in `A.map of` because
`w.head` is an element of `A`.  That is the missing inclusion; the other one is
already proved.

## Why the prerequisite is not the target restated

The prerequisite is a statement about *words*: which spellings are reduced, and
that one homomorphism commutes with taking products of letters.  It mentions no
intersection with `of.range`, does not mention `A'`, and would be just as true
if Britton's Lemma were false.  The step from it to the target is exactly one
application of Britton, and that is real content: it is what rules out
elements of `A'` that leave the image of `A` and come back.
