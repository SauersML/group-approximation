Section 3 extends the radial discovery argument to arbitrary edge states,
treats first crossing, frontier exhaustion and infinite execution, then
uses disjoint edge sets to factor the wall probability from branch
volume. Tonelli's theorem evaluates the positive double series; the
identity 4/9-3q(1-q)^2=(1-3q)^2(4-3q)/9 bounds it uniformly.
