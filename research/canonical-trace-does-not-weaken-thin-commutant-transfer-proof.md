Given an unrestricted failed transfer sequence, residual finiteness supplies
a finite quotient separating the first `n` nonidentity words.  Tensor the
approximate tuple with the quotient's exact regular representation and the
auxiliary unitary with the identity.  Relator defects and all relevant
commutator norms are unchanged, while every fixed nonidentity word has trace
zero eventually.  This produces a canonical-character failed sequence.
The reverse implication is immediate.
