title: A group with a finite model for E_VC G is finitely presented and has property BVC
---

If a group `G` admits a finite `G`-CW model for `E_VC G` (finitely many orbits of cells),
then:

- `G` has property BVC: finitely many virtually cyclic subgroups contain a conjugate of
  every virtually cyclic subgroup;
- `G` is finitely presented. In fact `G` admits a finite-type model for `EG`, so it has type
  `F_infinity`.

Source: von Puttkamer–Wu, arXiv:1607.03790v2, Proposition 1.1, parts (a) and (d).
Part (a) is also their Lemma 1.3: stabilizers of the finitely many orbits of 0-cells form a
witness, because each virtually cyclic subgroup fixes a vertex.
