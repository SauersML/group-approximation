The second prerequisite implies that `Sp_4(Z)` is residually finite:
every finite quotient of `E_3` factors through its covering-kernel quotient,
and these quotients separate all elements of that quotient. Therefore all
elements of `E_q` outside `K_q` are detected in finite quotients, so the MF
radical is contained in `K_q`.

In a norm-matrix corona representation of `E_q`, the image of `z_q` has
order dividing `q`. Its central Fourier projections split the image into
scalar character corners. Each nonzero corner lifts, after restriction to
the coordinates where its rank is positive and polar correction of
compressed unitaries, to a point-norm projective model at a parameter in
`S_q`. Conversely every parameter in `S_q` gives a corona representation
of `E_q` having exactly that scalar central character.

Thus the central elements invisible in all corona representations are
exactly the annihilator of `S_q`. Since `S_q` is a subgroup of the cyclic
character group, its annihilator is trivial precisely when `S_q` is the
whole character group, equivalently `1/q in P_op`. In that event one scalar
model detecting `K_q`, together with a faithful MF model of the quotient,
gives a faithful model of `E_q` by block direct sum.

If `P_op=R/Z`, every annihilator is trivial. If instead
`P_op=(1/m)Z/Z`, its intersection with `(1/q)Z/Z` is
`(1/gcd(q,m))Z/Z`; its annihilator is generated by `z_q^gcd(q,m)`.
This proves every displayed formula and the finite-or-all consequence.
The artifact gives the coordinate corner argument explicitly.
