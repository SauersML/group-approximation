Citation import. J. Harlander and S. Rosebrock, *The local structure of injective
LOT-complexes*, arXiv:2207.07991 (`src.tex`, md5 `6274ce657a3193a7bb7525da3f21e3d7`),
fetched on MSI 2026-09-13.

- l.133--136: angled 2-complexes and curvature `κ(d) = Σ ω(c_j) - (|∂d| - 2)`.
- l.148--155, Definition (Coloring test): (1) the curvature of every 2-cell is
  `<= 0`; (2) for every vertex `v`, if `c_1⋯c_n` is a simple reduced cycle in
  `lk(v)` then `2 - Σ ω(c_i) <= 0`.
- l.173--175, Theorem `thm:wise`: "If K satisfies Gersten's weight test, it is DR
  and therefore aspherical. In particular this holds when it is a zero/one angled
  2-complex which satisfies the coloring test." Attributed there to Sieradski
  (zero/one angles) and Gersten (general angles); those primary sources were not
  read.
