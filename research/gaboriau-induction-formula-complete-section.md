**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let R be a p.m.p. equivalence relation on (X,μ) and Y ⊂ X a
complete section (a Borel set meeting almost every R-class). Then
C(R) − 1 = μ(Y)(C(R↾Y) − 1), where C(R↾Y) is computed with the normalized measure μ↾Y/μ(Y).

**Source.** D. Gaboriau, *Around the orbit equivalence theory, measure equivalence, cost and
ℓ² Betti numbers*, lecture notes dated October 3, 2025, §2.4. Read 2026-09-17 from the
extracted text; line breaks and symbols normalised.

- Proposition 2.33: "(Induction Formula [Gab00]) Let Y ⊂ X be a complete section of the p.m.p.
  equivalence relation R. The cost of R and the cost of the restriction R↾Y are related
  according to the following formula: C(R) − 1 = µ(Y)(C(R↾Y) − 1). (Induction Formula) Of
  course, the cost of R↾Y is computed using the restricted normalized measure
  µY = µ↾Y / µ(Y)."

[Gab00] is D. Gaboriau, *Coût des relations d'équivalence et des groupes*, Invent. Math. 139
(2000), where the formula first appears.
