title: Thompson's group V is integrally acyclic
---

`H_k(V; Z) = 0` for every `k ≥ 1` (Szymik–Wahl). More generally, the homology
of the Higman–Thompson groups `V_{n,r}` is that of the base component of the
infinite loop space of the mod `n − 1` Moore spectrum. `V = V_{2,1}`, and the
mod 1 Moore spectrum is contractible.

Used in `thompson-v-central-extensions-split-proof`, which cites it in prose,
and in `braided-thompson-group-bv-is-rationally-acyclic` (Step D).
