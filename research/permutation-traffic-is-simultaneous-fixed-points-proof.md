Direct proof, given in the claim: a permutation matrix entry is nonzero only
on the graph of the permutation, so a nonzero summand is a graph morphism into
the Schreier graph of the action; the spanning tree determines it from the
root, and the non-tree edges impose exactly the simultaneous fixed-point
conditions.  The limit statement is a union bound over finitely many cycle
words.  No external input.
