For the fixed graph of groups consisting of the two `A8` chart vertices, the
thirty shortest `A4` context vertices, and their cyclic `C2/C3` incidence
edges, there is a fixed rational polytope `P_A4` with the following property.

Every exact representation produced by
`atlas-a4-context-network-simultaneous-hs-exactification`, after dividing all
vertex irreducible multiplicities by the common matrix dimension, determines a
point of `P_A4`.  Conversely every rational point of `P_A4` with a common
denominator is realized after the corresponding matrix amplification.

Thus matrix size and external multiplicity introduce no new **discrete** local
representation types beyond rational points of one finite polytope.  The only
continuous variables left by the compiler are unitary intertwiners on the
finite edge/cycle multiplicity spaces.
