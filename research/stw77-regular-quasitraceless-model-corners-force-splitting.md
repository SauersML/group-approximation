Let `E subset B` be a separable unital C*-subalgebra.  Suppose that for every
finite `F subset E` and `epsilon>0` there is a unital finite-dimensional
subalgebra

`D = direct_sum_j M_(n_j) subset B`

which approximates `F` within `epsilon` and has the following property.  For
one minimal projection `p_j` in each simple summand, the corner `p_j B p_j`
is simple, its Cuntz semigroup `W(p_j B p_j)` is almost unperforated, and it
has no normalized `2`-quasitrace.

Then the inclusion `E->B` is `O_infinity`-stable.  If `B` is exact, the final
corner hypothesis may equivalently be replaced by the absence of tracial
states on `p_j B p_j`.

In particular, this gives a positive answer to the nuclear-dimension-zero
case of STW LXXVII whenever its one-sided finite-dimensional models can be
chosen with regular simple quasitraceless minimal corners.  It is enough, for
example, that the corners be simple and `Z`-stable and have no normalized
`2`-quasitrace.
