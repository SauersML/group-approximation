Section 4. For sufficiently large odd n there is c_n with C^*(H)=C^*(B(m,n))=C^*(B(2,n))=c_n for every m>=2 and infinite H<=B(m,n), and 1<=c_n<=2-2/n. Supremal cost is not the cost of every individual action.
