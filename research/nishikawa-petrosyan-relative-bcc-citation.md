Source: Shintaro Nishikawa and Nansen Petrosyan,
*Dehn fillings, equivariant homology, and the Baum--Connes conjecture*,
arXiv:2509.15070v2, Theorem B (Theorem 6.2).

Theorem 6.2 has exactly the hypotheses in the target: `G` is countable
and hyperbolic relative to finitely many groups `H_lambda`; every
peripheral is residually finite and satisfies Baum--Connes with
coefficients with finite wreath products. Its conclusion is the same
finite-wreath Baum--Connes property for `G`.

The proof combines sufficiently deep Dehn fillings, residual finiteness of
the peripherals, permanence under group extensions, and the hyperbolic
finite-wreath theorem recorded in
[[hyperbolic-bcc-finite-wreath-products]]. No torsion-free hypothesis is
inserted. QED
