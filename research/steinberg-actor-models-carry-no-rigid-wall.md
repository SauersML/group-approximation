**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).**

**Setting.** `q` is a prime power, `r, d >= 3`, `R_+ = F_q[x_1..x_d]` and `L = F_q[x_1^(±1)..x_d^(±1)]`.
`Γ = EL_r(R_+) < G = EL_r(L) ⋊ SL_d(Z)` is the Kun--Thom Theorem E pair (`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath`),
with `SL_d(Z)` acting by monomial substitutions `φ_A(x^a) = x^(A a)`.

- `Ĝ = St_r(L) ⋊ SL_d(Z)`, where `A` sends the Steinberg generator `x_ij(f)` to `x_ij(φ_A f)`.
- `P : Ĝ -> G` is the natural map, with kernel `N_St = ker(St_r(L) -> EL_r(L))`.
- `Γ̂ <= St_r(L)` is generated by the `x_ij(f)` with `f ∈ R_+`, the image of `St_r(R_+)`.

**Theorem.** Let `ρ_n : Ĝ -> U(d_n)` be unitary representations with `lim_U ||ρ_n(m) - 1||_2 = 0` for
every `m ∈ N_St`, and let `σ : G -> U(prod_U M_(d_n))` be the model they define. Then

```text
σ(Γ)' ∩ prod_U M_(d_n) = prod_U (ρ_n(Γ̂)' ∩ M_(d_n)),
```

and `σ(G)` normalizes it.

**Consequences.**
- **No wall from Steinberg models.** Through `rigid-compression-defect-normalization-dichotomy`, no
  model in this class starts a witness for `hyperlinear-group-carries-nontrivial-rigid-defect` at the
  Theorem E pair. This covers representations through finite quotients of `Ĝ`, the natural finite
  construction for the `EL_3(F_q[x,y,z])` vertex.
- **K_2 twists do not help.** `N_St` may act nontrivially at every finite stage. At `r = 3` no
  stability theorem controls it and the `SL_3(Z)` transport argument of
  `actor-extension-lifts-vertex-rounding-to-the-laurent-vertex` is unavailable, yet the commutant is
  normalized.
- **Sharper necessary condition.** A trace-preserving model of the Theorem E actor with a
  non-normalized vertex commutant must not be pulled back, in the sense of (KC), from genuine
  representations of `St_r(L) ⋊ SL_d(Z)`, nor of any cover satisfying the hypotheses of
  `kazhdan-cover-actor-models-normalize-vertex-commutants`.

**Scope.**
- Nothing here asserts that Steinberg models with non-roundable vertex restrictions exist.
- Trace preservation is not assumed and not needed.
- The theorem does not decide `hyperlinear-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects` in either direction.
