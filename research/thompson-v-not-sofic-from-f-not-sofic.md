`F <= V`: `F` is the subgroup of prefix replacements `{alpha_i} -> {beta_i}` that preserve the
lexicographic order of both complete codes. Soficity passes to subgroups (restrict a sofic
approximation). So a sofic `V` would make `F` sofic.

In finitary form, by `thompson-f-sofic-iff-relator-system-unstable`: if almost-solutions of the two
Thompson relators almost commute uniformly, then `V` is not sofic.
