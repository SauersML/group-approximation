**REFUTED.**  The former claim asserted that a tracial state on arbitrary
unital C-star coordinates suffices for one-sided Kazhdan transport.  Its proof
placed the fixed-space projection in `M or JMJ` and claimed this algebra is
finite.  For an infinite factor in standard form,

```text
M or JMJ = M or M' = B(L^2 M),
```

which is not finite.  More concretely, constant coordinates
`A_n=C*_r(E)` with the canonical faithful trace satisfy all the old
coordinate hypotheses while the exact left-regular representation of `E`
violates the transport conclusion: `c` centralizes the Kazhdan base but
`tct^-1` has the nontrivial commutator whose square is `w`.

The correct successor is `left-right-finite-kazhdan-transport`: require the
C-star algebra generated by left and right multiplication in each coordinate
to be finite.  Matrices satisfy this, as do the infinite-dimensional classes
recorded in `left-right-finite-coordinate-obstruction`.
