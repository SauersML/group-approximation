Let `(A_k,tau_k)` be unital C-star algebras with tracial states, let `H` be a
group and `U_k : H -> U(A_k)` an asymptotic representation --- with respect to
the operator norm *or* to `||.||_(2,tau_k)`.  Let `Gamma` have property `(T)`,
let `iota : Gamma -> H`, and let `s in H` satisfy
`s iota(Gamma) s^(-1) <= iota(Gamma)`.  Then for every uniformly bounded
sequence `x_k in A_k` with

```text
||[x_k, U_k(iota gamma)]||_(2,tau_k) -> 0   for every gamma in Gamma,
```

the conjugated sequence `U_k(s) x_k U_k(s)^*` has the same property.

Only a tracial state on each coordinate is used; faithfulness, real rank
zero, comparison, and finiteness of the coordinates are not.  Property `(T)`
is used at exactly one point --- to place the fixed-space projection inside
the algebra generated by the representation --- and cannot be dropped.

Because the hypothesis may be taken in `||.||_2`, transport itself is
norm-agnostic: it is not the step at which the norm-to-trace passage fails.
