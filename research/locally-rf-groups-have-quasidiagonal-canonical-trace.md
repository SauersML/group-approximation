Let `K` be a countable group such that every finitely generated subgroup of
`K` is residually finite.  Then the canonical trace on
`C*_(max)(K)` is quasidiagonal.

The conclusion is stronger than Kirchberg's factorization property.  The
distinction between local residual finiteness and bare LEF is essential:
finitely generated LEF Kazhdan groups without the factorization property are
known.
