ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).  Let `G_e = G(P(M_e))` (prime `2`) be the pumped
search-machine group of [[rf-fp-completeness-via-pumped-minsky-groups]], and
`Phi_e` the subgroup generated by its full configuration words
`x_(q_i A_0) * a^(m) * A_1 * ... * A_(K+2)`.  Then `Phi_e` is central and
elementary abelian, and

```text
e in INF  ==>  Phi_e = 1,
e in FIN  ==>  Phi_e != 1, and Phi_e contains a nontrivial element of the profinite kernel.
```

Why:

- *Central.*  In the KMS model a full configuration word is the basis vector
  indexed by a word containing every `A`-letter, and every letter fixes such
  vectors.  Since `T` embeds in the model and is abelian, the word is central.
- *INF.*  The machine is simple and universally halting, so every full
  configuration reaches stop, and the stop word is trivial.
- *FIN.*  The pumped divergent configuration is full, and its word is
  nontrivial but profinitely invisible.

Consequence: any uniform finite-presentation amplifier sending a finitely
presented amenable group with a nontrivial central 2-subgroup to a nonsofic
group, and with trivial one to a sofic group, proves
[[sofic-recognition-finite-presentations-is-pi2-complete]].
