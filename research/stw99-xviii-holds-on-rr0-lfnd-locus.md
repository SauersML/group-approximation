Let `A` be a unital simple separable non-elementary stably finite
C\*-algebra with strict comparison, real rank zero, and locally
finite nuclear dimension.  Then `A ≅ A ⊗ Z`.

This is a new solved cell of STW Problem XVIII, obtained by chaining
the 2026-08-30 divisibility theorem for real rank zero
(`stw27-real-rank-zero-almost-divisibility`, which assumes neither
stable rank one nor nuclearity) through the pureness equivalence and
Winter's Cuntz-semigroup-regularity theorem.  Note it is NOT subsumed
by the sr1 cell: no stable rank one is assumed (and before Lin's
theorem is invoked nothing forces it), nor by the finite-boundary
cell: the trace simplex is unconstrained.
