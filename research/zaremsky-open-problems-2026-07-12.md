M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Some open problems*, version dated July 12, 2026
(https://zaremsky.github.io/open_problems.pdf, SHA-256
`d14c2ce7c26b60363693d1c549f5a25d51ab271e15e5ff4fa8215477d30cde50`).
The list was started on June 15, 2023, and its numbering is fixed.
It has 62 numbered problems in four sections:

1. finiteness properties, BNSR invariants and Dehn functions (25 problems);
2. Thompson groups (20 problems);
3. matrix groups, braid groups, etc. (10 problems);
4. Vietoris–Rips complexes (7 problems).

This claim is the conjunction of the 62 problem roots
`zaremsky-<section>-<two-digit number>-<slug>`. Each root is stated as its
question and is established only by an answer route. The single route into
this claim, `zaremsky-open-problems-2026-07-12-all`, requires every root and
lands once all 62 roots exist.

Status recorded in the list's own notes:

- answered: Problems 1.2, 1.15, 2.6, 2.15, 2.17 and 4.3;
- partly answered: Problems 2.5 (T has quadratic Dehn function) and 2.19
  (some hyperbolic group does not embed in V);
- open: the other 54.

"torsion-free". That version is tracked as its own root,
