**Dead.**  Invalidated by `tracial-domination-is-not-state-domination`.

The tempting argument.  This corpus already proves the architecture the
attack wants: a common carrier, a uniform local spectral gap on it, a
quarantined outlier sector, and exactification of the diffuse sector without
dimension-dependent loss.  `balanced-overlay-common-reducing-outlier-cut`
produces one common reducing threshold projection for all high
square-function outliers at cost `O(E/a)`, and
`gapped-face-pinchings-give-same-hilbert-code-pvm-rounding` converts a
uniform adjoint face-Hamiltonian gap into same-Hilbert code-PVM rounding at
cost `O(epsilon/gamma)`.  Read the threshold projection as the syndrome port
and the pinching gap as the domination constant.

Why it fails.  Both are inequalities of quadratic forms on `L^2(M, tau)`, the
GNS space of the normalized trace: the "energy" they control is
`||.||_2^2` at the maximally mixed weight.  (LSP1) is an operator inequality
against an **arbitrary** state, and on a QMA instance that state is a pure
witness.  Trace-weighted domination transfers nothing there -- with
`A = |psi><psi|` and `B = (I - |psi><psi|)/(d-1)` one has `tau(A) = tau(B)`
while `Tr(A |psi><psi|) = 1` and `Tr(B |psi><psi|) = 0` -- and the only
general comparison, `Tr(A rho) <= ||rho||_inf * d * tau(A)`, costs the factor
`d = 2^n`.

What would reopen it: a hypothesis bounding the witness's Radon--Nikodym
derivative against `tau` on the subalgebra generated by the ports.  No such
hypothesis is available for an arbitrary QMA witness, and manufacturing one
is not weaker than the problem.
