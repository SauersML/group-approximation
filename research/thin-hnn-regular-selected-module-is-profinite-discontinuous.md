Let

```text
A=SL_3(Z),  C=<a,b>,
G=<A,t | [t,a]=[t,b]=1>,
```

where `C` is the explicit thin profinitely dense property-`(tau)` subgroup.
In the canonical group-factor representation of `G`, put `v=lambda(t)` and
let `K_v` be the cyclic `Ad lambda(A)`-module generated by `v`.  Then

```text
K_v is unitarily equivalent to lambda_(A/C),            (THD1)
```

and

```text
K_v is not weakly contained in pi_FD^0.                 (THD2)
```

Thus the HNN relations, the canonical group character, and positivity of all
mixed moments do not imply selected profinite continuity.  Any proof of
`thin-selected-module-is-profinite-continuous` must use that the ambient
representation is a tracial **matrix ultraproduct**.  Conversely, a
hyperlinear embedding of this explicit HNN group would transport `(THD1)`
into a matrix ultraproduct and would be precisely a counterexample to that
open continuity claim.
