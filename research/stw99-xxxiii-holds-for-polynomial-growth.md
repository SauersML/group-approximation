Every free action of a finitely generated group of polynomial growth
on a compact metrisable space has dynamical comparison
(no minimality needed in Naryshkin's formulation for the comparison
statement on open sets with the invariant-measure gap).  Combined
with Kerr--Szabo, all free minimal actions of such groups with the
small boundary property are almost finite, generalising Niu's
`Z^d`-theorems — the mechanism recorded by STW.
