Let `Γ` be a finite Coxeter graph with vertex set `S`. Call `X ⊆ S` *free of infinity*
when `m_st ≠ ∞` for all `s, t ∈ X`. If `Γ_X` is of type K(π,1) for every free-of-infinity
`X ⊆ S`, then `Γ` is of type K(π,1).

Here `Γ` is *of type K(π,1)* when `E_Γ = (I × V) − ⋃_{r ∈ R} (H_r × H_r)` is aspherical,
where `I` is the Tits cone of the canonical representation `V` of `W_Γ` and `R` is the set
of reflections. Since `E_Γ → E_Γ/W_Γ` is a regular covering with `π_1(E_Γ/W_Γ) = A_Γ`
(van der Lek), this is the K(π,1) conjecture for `A_Γ`.
