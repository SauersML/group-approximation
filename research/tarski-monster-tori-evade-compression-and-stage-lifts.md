**ESTABLISHED.** Proof: `tarski-monster-tori-evade-compression-and-stage-lifts-proof`.
Items (i)-(iii) are unconditional. Item (iv) and the soficity part of (iii) assume `(RF_Γ)`.

## Setting

- `Γ` is a torsion-free cocompact lattice in `Sp(n,1)`, `n ≥ 2`, generated by a finite set `S`.
- `Q = F(S)/N` is the torsion-free Tarski monster quotient of `Γ` from
  `hyperbolic-rf-gives-torsion-free-lef-quotientless-kazhdan`. It is the direct limit of the
  torsion-free hyperbolic stages `G(j) = F(S)/N_j`, with `N_0 ⊆ N_1 ⊆ ...` and `N = ∪ N_j`.
- `ψ ∈ Aut(Q)`, and `P = Q ⋊_ψ Z`.
- `(RF_Γ)`: every torsion-free hyperbolic quotient of `Γ` is residually finite.

## Theorem

- **(i) Q is simple and centreless.**
- **(ii) Exact radical dichotomy.** Suppose `[ψ]` has infinite order in `Out(Q)`. Then
  `C_P(Q) = 1`, every nontrivial normal subgroup of `P` contains `Q`, and every homomorphism of
  `P` that is nontrivial on `Q` is injective. Hence `Rad_MF(P) ∈ {1, Q × {0}}`, and

  ```text
  (LK3) for (Q, ψ)   ⟺   P is not operator MF.
  ```

  If `[ψ]` has finite order, then `P` is virtually `Q × Z`. Under `(RF_Γ)` it is then MF, so (LK3)
  fails.
- **(iii) Every compression certificate is vacuous.** Every Kazhdan subgroup of `P` is `1` or `Q`.
  For infinite outer order, every `KazhdanCompressionCore` in `P` (as in `normal-kazhdan-defect-non-mf`)
  has `defectNormal = 1`, and `D_P(L) = 1` for every Kazhdan `L ≤ P`. Under `(RF_Γ)`, `P` is
  sofic, so no tracial-shadow kill (the hyperlinear variant of `normal-kazhdan-defect-non-mf`)
  reaches `Q`.
- **(iv) Stage-liftable automorphisms die.** Assume `(RF_Γ)`. Fix words `w_s` for `ψ(s)`. If
  infinitely many stages `G(j)` carry automorphisms `α_j` with `α_j(s) = w_s` in `G(j)` for all
  `s ∈ S`, then `P` is LEF, hence operator MF, and (LK3) fails. The same holds if any sequence of
  residually finite marked approximants of `(Q, S)` carries such lifts.

## What dies

**Class killed:** certifying (L2) of `torsion-free-lef-kazhdan-kernel-with-exact-mapping-torus-radical`
with the monster `Q` as kernel by any mechanism now in the graph.

| mechanism | named invariant | death step |
|---|---|---|
| normal Kazhdan compression defect | `C_P(Q) = 1` | (iii): the centralizing element of every core is `1` |
| intrinsic defect `D_G(L)` | Kazhdan subgroups are `1, Q` | (iii) |
| tracial-shadow kill | soficity of `P` | (iii), under `(RF_Γ)` |
| finite-residual bound | `Q` quotientless | gives only `Rad_MF(P) ⊆ Q`, which (ii) already has |
| stagewise automorphisms | stage-liftability | (iv), under `(RF_Γ)` |
| finite outer order | `[ψ]` of finite order | (ii) |

## What survives

A witness with kernel `Q` needs all of the following, each of which can fail independently:

1. an automorphism `ψ` of `Q` with infinite outer order (none is known);
2. `ψ` *born in the limit*: under `(RF_Γ)` it lifts to only finitely many stages `G(j)`, and to
   no residually finite approximants of `Q`;
3. a non-MF mechanism for the sofic group `P` that is neither a compression defect nor a tracial
   kill, since both are vacuous on `P`;
4. finite presentation of `P`, which is (L3) and is untouched here.

**Remark (cited, not load-bearing).** Each stage `G(j)` is a Kazhdan torsion-free hyperbolic group.
By Paulin's theorem, a hyperbolic group with infinite `Out` acts on an `R`-tree without a global
fixed point, and Kazhdan groups admit no such action. So `Out(G(j))` is finite, and every stage
lift in (iv) has finite outer order in its own stage.
