Koch-Hyde--O'Connor--Olive, *More Residually Solvable One-Relator Groups*,
arXiv:2606.13933v2, Theorem 1.8, prove that `F(X)/<<w>>` is residually
solvable whenever `w` is partially positive.  The paper was source-checked on
2026-08-30; its Definition/Question 1.7 gives exactly the sign convention used
in the claim.

A residually solvable group is residually amenable because every solvable group
is amenable.  Residually amenable groups are sofic: for each finite test set,
take a finite product of amenable separating quotients and then use an amenable
sofic approximation of that product.  This proves both conclusions.
