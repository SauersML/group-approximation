Stability supplies a single exact representation `rho` close to the tuple on
generators; a trivial word evaluates to `1` on `rho`, so its defect on the
tuple is at most the word length times the per-generator distance, linear in
length regardless of the stability rate.  The area bound is the separate
universal `hs-dehn-modulus-bounded-by-dehn-function`.
