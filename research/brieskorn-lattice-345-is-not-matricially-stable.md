**ESTABLISHED (written proof; not Lean-verified).**  Proof route:
`brieskorn-lattice-345-not-matricially-stable-via-gauge-pair`.

Let `Λ = <x, y | x^4 = y^3, (yx)^5 = x^12>`, the fundamental group of the
Brieskorn homology sphere `Σ(3,4,5)`, as in
`lifted-thompson-t-is-a-quotient-of-a-brieskorn-lattice`.  Then `Λ` is not
matricially stable in Dadarlat's point-norm sense.

Concretely, let `(X_q, Y_q)` be the gauge pairs of
`brieskorn-345-gauge-pair-has-no-eigencorner`.  They satisfy

```text
||X_q^4 - Y_q^3|| -> 0,     ||(Y_q X_q)^5 - X_q^12|| -> 0,
```

and no sequence of exact pairs `(X'_q, Y'_q)` with `X'^4 = Y'^3` and
`(Y'X')^5 = X'^12` has `||X_q - X'_q|| + ||Y_q - Y'_q|| -> 0`.  Neither
does any subsequence.

## Why it matters

- **Cohomology is silent.**  `Λ` is perfect.  `Λ\G̃` is a closed aspherical
  homology 3-sphere (item (C) of
  `lifted-thompson-t-is-a-quotient-of-a-brieskorn-lattice`), so
  `H_1(Λ) = H_2(Λ) = 0` and `H^2(Λ; Z) = 0`.
  - Dadarlat's obstruction and every winding-number obstruction of degree 2
    vanish.
  - `Λ` is also residually finite, a cocompact lattice and Haagerup.
  - So it is a candidate on which all the standard positive criteria are at
    least not refuted.  It still fails.
- **The obstruction survives the extra relation.**  The `K(3,4)`
  obstruction of `torus-knot-group-k34-is-not-matricially-stable` lives on
  the central twist `X = D^3 α`, `Y = D^4 β`.  That twist is killed by
  `(yx)^5 = x^12` (the `35/12 != 36/12` computation in item 1 of
  `brieskorn-345-gauge-pair-has-no-eigencorner`).  The gauge field restores
  the obstruction.
- **For the flagship.**  `T̄` is a quotient of `Λ` with `c -> z`.  So no route
  for `lifted-thompson-t-centre-has-commutant-eigencorners` can go through
  matricial stability of `Λ`, or of any group `H` with a surjection
  `H -> Λ` sending a central element to `c`, such as `K(3,4)`.  Every such
  `H` carries the same gauge pair, by pullback, with the same relative
  commutant when `H` is generated by lifts of `x, y`.  The uniform bound of
  that node also holds along every subsequence of `q`, so passing to a
  subsequence does not help.
- **Not claimed.**  Nothing about the MF property of `Λ` (it is residually
  finite, hence MF), and nothing about `T̄`.
