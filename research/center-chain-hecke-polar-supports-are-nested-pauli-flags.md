Retain

```text
H=<x_65(q),x_54(q),x_42(q)> ~= UT_4(F_2),
e=e_chi,                         tau(e)=1/64,
A_1=x_47(a_1),                   A_2=x_58(a_2),
T_1=eA_1e,                       T_2=eA_2A_1e.          (CHS1)
```

Inside the elementary subgroup generated by `H,A_1,A_2`, put

```text
W_1=<x_57(a_1),x_67(a_1)> ~= F_2^2,
W_2=<x_57(a_1),x_67(a_1),x_68(a_2)> ~= F_2^3,
z_i=|W_i|^(-1) sum_(w in W_i) w.                       (CHS2)
```

Both `W_i` are normalized by `H`, `W_1<=W_2`, and the two relevant
`H`-orbits are the affine cosets

```text
H.A_1=A_1W_1,                    H.(A_2A_1)=A_2A_1W_2. (CHS3)
```

Character averaging therefore gives the exact Hecke normal forms

```text
T_1=eA_1z_1,                     T_2=eA_2A_1z_2.        (CHS4)
```

All roots in `W_2` commute, and the orbit averages in `(CHS4)` commute with
`e`.  Consequently

```text
T_1^*T_1=T_1^2=p_1:=ez_1,
T_2^*T_2=T_2^2=p_2:=ez_2,        p_2<=p_1,              (CHS5)
tau(p_1)=1/256,                  tau(p_2)=1/512.        (CHS6)
```

Thus the exact spectrum of `T_i^*T_i` is `{0,1}`.  Relative to the corner
`e`, the `1`-spectral masses are respectively `1/4` and `1/8`; the support
projections are exactly `p_1,p_2`.  In particular each `T_i` is already a
self-adjoint partial isometry.  Its nonzero `+1` and `-1` spectral parts have
equal trace, namely `1/512` for `T_1` and `1/1024` for `T_2`.

The smaller support is a genuine common Pauli corner.  It reduces both
`A_1` and `A_2`, and also reduces `B_1=x_72(b_1)`.  On `p_2`, the pair
`A_1,B_1` is the exact Pauli pair because

```text
[A_1,B_1]=x_42(q),              x_42(q)p_2=-p_2.       (CHS7)
```

Moreover the cross-Gram and its polar gauge are

```text
G=T_1^*T_2=A_2p_2,              G^*G=p_2,
tau(G^*G)=(1/8)tau(e)=1/512,                              (CHS8)
[G,A_1p_2]=[G,B_1p_2]=0.                                  (CHS9)
```

Hence the moving-arm Hecke construction has no remaining spectral-support
or mixed-Pauli defect: it supplies an exact common polar subcorner of the
desired fixed mass and an exact relative gauge in the source Pauli
commutant.

This does **not** by itself establish the native selector claim.  The maps
in `(CHS1)` are compressions of the chained Pauli arms, whereas the native
claim quantifies over the fixed stabilized occurrences of the coefficients
`s_(00)t_0` and `s_(000)t_(00)` in the two unequal-scale selector rows.
One must still prove that those two prescribed row transports restrict,
after the allowed finite stabilization, to the two polar maps above (or to
unitarily equivalent maps with the same controlled label actions).  The
correct dyadic support traces alone do not supply that occurrence typing.
