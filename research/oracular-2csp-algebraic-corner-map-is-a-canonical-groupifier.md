ESTABLISHED.  Let `B` be a finite oracular 2-CSP and let `A(B)` be its
universal rational game star algebra: it is generated by the answer
projections, with the PVM relations, the literal marginal-identification
relations supplied by oracularity, and the vanishing forbidden-pair
products.

Suppose one constructs an ordinary group `Gamma`, a rational finite-support
projection

```text
q=q^*=q^2 in Q[Gamma],                                  (ACG1)
```

and a unital star homomorphism

```text
Phi:A(B)->q Q[Gamma] q,             Phi(1)=q.           (ACG2)
```

Then the following implication holds:

```text
Gamma hyperlinear and q!=0  =>  B has a perfect CE tracial model. (ACG3)
```

Moreover every canonical-profile matrix-microstate sequence for `Gamma`
with `||q(U_n)||_2` bounded away from zero produces that perfect CE model
directly in its matrix ultraproduct.  Thus, for a computable family `B_m`, a
uniformly recursively presented `(Gamma_m,q_m,Phi_m)` satisfying `(ACG1)--
(ACG2)` is an instance of
`oracular-2csp-mark-relative-canonical-groupifier` as soon as the perfect
side has one exact extension which detects `q_m`.  No finite-dimensional
rounding modulus, packet multiplicity theorem, or separate ultraproduct
decoder is needed.

## Proof

Let `tau_Gamma` be the canonical trace on `L(Gamma)`.  It is faithful, so a
nonzero algebraic projection has

```text
alpha=tau_Gamma(q)>0.                                  (ACG4)
```

If `Gamma` is hyperlinear, `(L(Gamma),tau_Gamma)` embeds trace preservingly
in a tracial matrix ultraproduct.  The normalized corner trace

```text
tau_q(x)=alpha^(-1)tau_Gamma(x),       x in qL(Gamma)q, (ACG5)
```

is therefore Connes embeddable.  The pullback `tau_q o Phi` extends
complex-linearly from the rational star algebra to a normalized positive
tracial state on `A(B) tensor_Q C`.  Since all defining game relations
already hold in `A(B)`, its GNS representation is a perfect CE tracial model
of `B`.
This proves `(ACG3)`.

For the sequence statement, pass to the tracial matrix ultraproduct.  The
canonical moment conditions give an exact trace-preserving homomorphism

```text
pi:C[Gamma]->prod_U M_(d_n)                            (ACG6)
```

and `pi(q)` is a projection of trace `alpha`; in particular it is nonzero.
Composing `Phi` with `pi` and normalizing the trace on the `pi(q)` corner
gives the same perfect CE state.  At finite coordinates `q(U_n)` need not be
an exact projection, but no coordinatewise correction is required: `(ACG6)`
is exact after passage to the ultraproduct.

On the perfect side, if a perfect representation of `A(B)` extends to an
exact unitary representation of `Gamma` with `q` acting nontrivially, then
`q!=0` in `Q[Gamma]`.  This is precisely the algebraic-survival certificate
needed by reverse Kleene.

## Exact construction boundary

Writing the images of the context atoms as `p_(c,a)` turns `(ACG2)` into the
finite compatible-atlas equations

```text
p_(c,a)p_(c,b)=0                         (a!=b),
sum_(a allowed for c)p_(c,a)=q,
sum_a a_x p_(c,a)=sum_b b_x p_(d,b)      (x in c cap d). (ACG7)
```

The first two rows have explicit finite signed-Hecke realizations, even in a
common finite packet type.  The third row is the whole unresolved bridge:
it must hold as an algebraic identity in one group corner while the finite
packet supporting `q` remains embedded.  Pairing its group-ring terms
independently destroys the projection identities; restoring those identities
recreates the signed-Hecke cycle holonomy.  For an instance with no perfect
CE tracial model, no finite graph of finite packet groups can satisfy
`(ACG7)` while retaining `q!=0`: its virtually free fundamental group is
hyperlinear, and the normalized canonical corner trace would then already be
the forbidden CE game trace.  This fences finite Bass--Serre gluing as a
closure theorem; it does not count a presentation in which the packet and
`q` collapse as a constructed atlas.

Consequently an algebraic-corner proof must supply a genuinely non-Bass--
Serre two-cell (or an equivalent block-escaping group-ring identity) and a
normal-form/injectivity theorem proving `q!=0` on the perfect side.  This is a
precise algebraic alternative to the analytic positive-density power-return
compiler; it is not supplied by local packet authentication alone.
