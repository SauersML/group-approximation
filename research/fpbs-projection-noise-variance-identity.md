For finite Bernoulli bond percolation and its averaged cluster projection
Q_p, -p(1-p)Q_p'-Q_p+Q_p^2 is positive semidefinite. Theorem 1 identifies
it exactly as sum_{|A|>=2} (|A|-1) P_hat(A)^*P_hat(A) in the normalized
Bernoulli product expansion. This concerns normalized cluster averaging,
not the connectivity matrix without its inverse cluster-size factor.
