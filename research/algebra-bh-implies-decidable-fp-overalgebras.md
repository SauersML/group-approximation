Let `A` be finitely generated with solvable word problem. By
`algebra-boone-higman-conjecture`, `A` embeds in a finitely presented simple algebra
`S`. By `fp-simple-algebras-have-solvable-word-problem`, `S` has solvable word
problem. So `S` is a finitely presented overalgebra of `A` with solvable word
problem.
