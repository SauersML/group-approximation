Citation import from M. Abért and B. Weiss, *Bernoulli actions are weakly contained in any free action*, arXiv:1103.1063v2, Section 1.

- **Definition.** "Let κ be a probability space. The Bernoulli action κ^Γ is defined as the set of maps from Γ to κ, endowed with the product measure and the shift action by Γ."
- **Theorem 1.** "Let Γ be a countable infinite group and let f be a free p.m.p. action of Γ. Then f weakly contains every Bernoulli action of Γ. In particular, all free factors of i.i.d.-s of Γ are weakly equivalent."
