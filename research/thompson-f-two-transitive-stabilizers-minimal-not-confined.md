title: Point stabilizers of a 2-transitive action of Thompson's group F act minimally on (0,1) and are not confined
distinct_from:
  thompson-f-two-transitive-rigid-stabilizers-fix-no-point: that claim says no rigid stabilizer group fixes a point of the abstract set; this claim derives the dynamical and subgroup-space consequences for the point stabilizers themselves
---

Let `F` act 2-transitively on `Ω`, `|Ω| ≥ 3`, with point stabilizer
`H = F_ω`. Then:

1. `H` is a maximal subgroup of `F`, `HF' = F`, and `H ∩ [F,F] ≠ 1`;
2. `H` acts minimally on `(0,1)`: every `H`-orbit is dense, and `H` has no
   proper nonempty closed invariant subset;
3. `H` is not confined in `F`: for every finite `P ⊆ F \ {1}` some point of
   `Ω` is moved by every element of `P`;
4. two-point stabilizers are nontrivial.

So a positive answer to Problem 2.20 needs a maximal, non-confined subgroup
acting minimally on `(0,1)` and containing no rigid-stabilizer group, with
exactly two double cosets.
