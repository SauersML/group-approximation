**ESTABLISHED INTERFACE AUDIT.**  Let `D(delta)` denote the assertion in
`affine-leavitt-steinberg-hs-coefficient-decoder`: for every normalized-HS
`delta`-representation `U`, there exist a projection `P` and contractions
`S_0,S_1,T_0,T_1` such that

```text
||T_iS_i-P||_2 <= omega(delta),
||S_0T_0+S_1T_1-P||_2 <= omega(delta),                 (ADI1)
||w_A(U)-I||_2^2 <= C tr(P)+omega(delta).              (ADI2)
```

Then `D(delta)` with one modulus tending to zero is equivalent, up to a
constant change of modulus, to the direct marked-root estimate

```text
||w_A(U)-I||_2^2 <= eta(delta),       eta(delta)->0.   (ADI3)
```

Indeed `(ADI1)` and
`approximate-relative-leavitt-cell-kills-active-trace` give

```text
tr(P)<=3 omega(delta).
```

Substitution in `(ADI2)` proves `(ADI3)` with
`eta=(3C+1)omega`.  Conversely, if `(ADI3)` is known, take

```text
P=S_0=S_1=T_0=T_1=0.
```

Then `(ADI1)` holds with zero defect and `(ADI2)` holds with
`omega=eta` (and any fixed `C`).  Thus the claimed coefficient objects can
always be chosen vacuously after the desired conclusion has already been
proved.

This does not refute marked-root collapse, and hence does not decide whether
`Gamma_A` is nonhyperlinear.  It sharply refutes the interpretation of the
current statement as a smaller root-to-coefficient extraction lemma or as an
independent route to collapse.

A nonvacuous replacement must prescribe `P` from the root microstate before
the Leavitt operators are chosen.  For example, first exactify the fixed
order-`p` cyclic subgroup generated by `w_A` and define `P` to be the sum of
its nontrivial spectral projections.  Then

```text
4 sin^2(pi/p) tr(P)
 <= ||w_A(U)-I||_2^2+o_delta(1)
 <= 4 tr(P)+o_delta(1),                                (ADI4)
```

with the exact inequalities when the cyclic subgroup is exact.  The genuine
extraction problem is then to construct `S_i,T_i` satisfying `(ADI1)` for
this prescribed `P` (or for an explicitly specified Fourier/packet
projection uniformly close to it).  Allowing an arbitrary existential `P`
does not express that problem.

The same audit applies to any proposed decoder whose only link from an
existential carrier to the marked word is an upper bound of the form
`||w-I||_2^2 <= C tr(P)+o(1)`: the zero carrier makes the decoder equivalent
to the conclusion.  A prescribed carrier, or an independently proved
two-sided comparison with a prescribed root spectral carrier, is required
before the relative Leavitt trace lemma becomes a genuine intermediate
step.
