By `sl2-q-in-permutational-boone-higman-class`, `SL_2(Q)` embeds in a group `Γ`
with a type (A) action. By Zaremsky's Corollary B
(`type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups`), every subgroup of `Γ`, in
particular `SL_2(Q)`, embeds in the finitely presented simple twisted
Brin--Thompson group `SV_Γ`. The argument restricts an embedding of `Γ`, so it
uses no generators of `SL_2(Q)`.
