title: Primitive ideals of a separable exact minimal tensor product form the product space
---

Let `A` and `B` be separable C-star algebras and suppose at least one is
exact.  The map

```text
(P,Q) |-> ker(A tensor_min B -> (A/P) tensor_min (B/Q))
```

is a homeomorphism

```text
Prim(A) times Prim(B) -> Prim(A tensor_min B).         (BK1)
```

It is compatible with quotients and ideals.  In particular, if ideals
`I triangleleft A` and `J triangleleft B` correspond to open subsets `U`
and `V` of the two primitive spectra, then the ideal
`I tensor_min J` of `A tensor_min B` corresponds under (BK1) to
`U times V`.
