**ESTABLISHED CYCLIC-ONLY SCOPE FENCE.**  Start with the exact actor from
`endpoint-order-five-actor-has-half-gram`:

```text
G=<M,W>|V=GL_4(F_2),       M^7=W^2=(WM)^5=1,
V=<C_3,s,t,s'>~=C_2^4.                              (CSA1)
```

Put `C=WM` and retain the raw spectral cuts

```text
R =1_(C_3=-1,s=+1),
R*=1_(C_3=-1,s=+1,s'=+1).                           (CSA2)
```

The contragredient character action gives the following exact counts:

```text
                         rank join     rank(join meet Wjoin)
join_(j=0)^4 C^j R C^-j       10                    8
join_(j=0)^6 M^j R M^-j       14                   13

join_(j=0)^4 C^j R* C^-j       5                    3
join_(j=0)^6 M^j R* M^-j       7                    3.       (CSA3)
```

All ranks in `(CSA3)` are measured in the fifteen-character nonzero
`V` orbit; covariance gives every one of those characters the same
multiplicity.  Thus cyclic aggregation does not preserve the raw one-half
ratio.  At the `R` level it changes it to `4/5` for `C` and `13/14` for
`M`.

More importantly, `(CSA3)` does not descend from either odd cyclic relator
alone.  There is an exact finite profile of the **truncated cyclic sheet
interface** in which

```text
p_2=e_(<C_1,C_2,C_3,v,w,s>,lambda)
```

has rank one, while for either `U=C` or `U=M` the invariant cyclic carrier

```text
E_U=join_(j=0)^(ord(U)-1) U^j p_2 U^-j               (CSA4)
```

has positive rank `ord(U)` and nevertheless

```text
E_U (W E_U W) =0,
(E_U W E_U)^*(E_U W E_U)=0.                          (CSA5)
```

The truncated interface retains the exact `M,W` action on `V`, the relations
`M^7=W^2=(WM)^5=1`, the basic endpoint equality `WC_2W=C_2`, and the one-step
`M` exit for `C_2`.  It deliberately does **not** retain the additional
literal conjugation relations saying that the subgroup
`diag(1,GL_3(F_2))` fixes all of signed `K_1`; those relations are exactly
what proves the stronger one-third theorem in
`endpoint-order-five-actor-has-half-gram`.

The cyclic-only model is finite and exact.  Let `G=GL_4(F_2)` act by left translation
on `ell^2(G)`.  Give the diagonal `V` algebra its equivariant character
bundle whose identity coordinate carries the character

```text
chi_0=(C_3=-1,s=+1,t=+1,s'=-1).                      (CSA6)
```

Let `L` be the diagonal involution which is `-1` exactly on the two-point
set `<W>={1,W}`.  Since that set is `W`-invariant, `[L,W]=1`.  Represent

```text
C_1=-1,       C_2=L,       v=w=+1.                   (CSA7)
```

Then the signed conditions defining `p_2` select the identity coordinate
and reject the `W` coordinate, because `W chi_0` has `s=-1`.  Hence
`p_2` is the identity-coordinate projection.  The marked involution `z`
commutes with this endpoint packet, so represent it by `-1`; then
`p_2<=(1-z)/2` and the model retains positive marked mass as well.  The two carriers in `(CSA4)`
are the coordinate projections of the left cosets `<C>` and `<M>`.  Their
`W` translates are the distinct cosets `W<C>` and `W<M>`, proving `(CSA5)`.

This model also pays the retained one-step sheet exit.  For example set

```text
rho(x_84(b_2))=L (M L M^-1),
```

so `M C_2 M^-1=C_2 x_84(b_2)` holds exactly; the unused `v` exit may be
sent to the identity.  Equivalently, all sheet operators live in the
finite diagonal lamp group generated by the `G`-translates of `L`.

Therefore taking an invariant join, an orbit direct sum, or the canonical
orthogonal coordinate refinement using only the `C`- or only the
`M`-translates retains positive signed-source mass but supplies no
representation-independent positive Whitehead cross Gram.  This is not a
countermodel to the full native packet: its signed-K1-returning `GL_3`
subgroup supplies precisely the omitted coupling and forces the established
one-third bound.  The scoped conclusion is that the odd order-five or
order-seven relation alone cannot replace that full return subgroup.
