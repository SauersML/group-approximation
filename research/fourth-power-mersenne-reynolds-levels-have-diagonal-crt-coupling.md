**ESTABLISHED; EXACT HARMONIC TARGET, NOT DIVERGENCE.**  Put

```text
M_n=4^n-1,        Q_n=SL_2(Z/M_n Z)/{+-I},
R_n=h(2),         H_n=<R_n>,
T_n={h(u):u in (Z/M_n Z)^x}/{+-I}.                    (FMR1)
```

Then

```text
ord_(M_n)(4)=n,        |H_n|=2n,        |T_n|=phi(M_n)/2,
[T_n:H_n]=phi(4^n-1)/(4n) -> infinity.                (FMR2)
```

The distinction between the first two orders is load-bearing.  If
`0<k<n`, then `0<4^k-1<M_n`, so `4^k` is not one modulo `M_n`; hence the
first equality.  In the quotient by the *global* two-element subgroup
`{+-I}`, the equality `R_n^k=1` means

```text
2^k=+1 mod M_n       or       2^k=-1 mod M_n.          (FMR3)
```

Squaring `(FMR3)` forces `n|k`.  At `k=n`, neither congruence holds for
`n>1`, since the two positive proper factors `2^n-1` and `2^n+1` multiply
to `M_n`.  At `k=2n`, the positive congruence does hold.  Thus
`|H_n|=2n`.  The diagonal torus has the stated size because its kernel in
the quotient is exactly `u=+-1`.  Finally the classical lower bound

```text
phi(N) >> N/log log N
```

shows that the last index in `(FMR2)` grows exponentially up to a
subexponential factor.

These levels do **not** turn the scalar Reynolds column into a tensor
product over the prime-power factors of `M_n`.  Under CRT, `R_n` maps to
one tuple

```text
(h(2) mod ell_1^a_1, ..., h(2) mod ell_r^a_r),         (FMR4)
```

and `H_n` is the cyclic subgroup generated by that tuple.  It is the
diagonal orbit with order `2n`, not the product of the cyclic factor
orbits.  Its missing quotient inside the product split torus is precisely
the growing label measured in `(FMR2)`.  Since both cubic Reynolds rows
left-translate this same diagonal orbit, ordinary CRT supplies no
factorwise inverse and no tensor amplification of a bad prime-level mode.

There is nevertheless a fully explicit remaining harmonic-analysis test.
Let `U_n={u(t):t in Z/M_n Z}` be the upper unipotent subgroup.  The scalar
column `C_(Q_n)` commutes with the right `U_n` action, so its Hilbert-space
domain and range split into the characters

```text
psi_a(t)=exp(2 pi i a t/M_n).                           (FMR5)
```

To refute the uniform Green estimate it is enough to find characters
`a_n` and vectors

```text
f_n in D_E cap L_2(Q_n)_(psi_(a_n)) cap ker(C_(Q_n))^perp
```

such that

```text
||f_n||_infinity /
max(||C_(1,Q_n)f_n||_infinity,||C_(2,Q_n)f_n||_infinity)
 -> infinity.                                          (FMR6)
```

Conversely, a positive proof must bound `(FMR6)` uniformly over `n` and
all right-unipotent characters, and then control their interaction in
`l_infinity`; an `L_2` block bound alone is insufficient.  The numerical
right-`U`-fixed block is the single character `a=0` and does not settle
this target.

Thus `M_n=4^n-1` supplies the desired explicit unbounded-index family, but
not a divergent scalar mode for free.  The exact missing theorem is a
uniform or divergent `l_infinity` Green estimate for the nontrivial blocks
`(FMR5)`, with the diagonal CRT orbit `(FMR4)` retained.
