title: Every sufficiently large elementary group over the binary Leavitt algebra is simple

Let `R=L_(F_2)(1,2)`.  For every `n>=3`,

```text
EL_n(R)=GL_n(R)
```

is a nontrivial simple group.  In particular the rank-sixteen Hilbert-hotel
group `EL_16(R)` is itself simple; no passage to a maximal normal quotient is
needed.
