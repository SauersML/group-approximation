## Statement

Let `P` be finite, and `δ` the degree cocycle of `G_P` with values in `Z^{P'}`. For a subgroup
`L ≤ Z^{P'}` and a base point `y`, let `S_L(y)` be the set of germs at `y` whose degree lies in `L`,
with the metric of the leaf.

1. **Slab dimension.** `asdim S_L(y) ≤ rank L`.
2. **Rank bound.** Let `ρ : 2V → CT_P(Z)` be an embedding, extended to `G̃_{2V}^{[r]}` (Matte Bon).
   Let `L_ρ` be the subgroup generated by the degrees of the pieces of all `ρ(g)`. Then
   `rank L_ρ ≥ 2r ≥ 2`. For `P = {3}` this means `r = 1` and `L_ρ` has finite index in `Z^2`.
3. **Dead designs for `P = {3}`.** No embedding `2V → CT_{3}(Z)` has all its slopes in any of the
   following:
   - `2^Z`: the odometer-fibre design, where every map acts on the 3-adic coordinate by isometries,
     as `V` does in item 6 of `conway-amusical-permutation-is-the-full-three-shift`;
   - `3^Z`: the 3-adic design with 2-adic fibres;
   - `6^Z`: the Higman–Thompson `V_6` design;
   - any cyclic group `⟨2^a 3^b⟩`.

   Each stack must contract two primes independently. So the second stack cannot hide in an
   odometer fibre.

## Proof

**Item 1.** Let `W ⊆ L` be a box of side `D`, and `r > 0`.
- **Normal form.** By the normal form of `brin-thompson-nv-needs-n-minus-one-primes-in-ct-z`,
  item 1, two germs at distance at most `r` differ by `push_λ pop^κ` with `|κ|, |d(λ)| ≤ r`.
- **Common ancestor.** Every `r`-chain in `S_L(y) ∩ δ^{-1}(W)` has a common ancestor at level
  `min W − r·1 ∈ Z^{P'}`. That ancestor need not lie in `S_L`.
- **Bounded components.** A germ is determined by its ancestor and a path `λ` with
  `d(λ) ∈ W − min W + r·1`. So the `r`-components have uniformly bounded size and diameter.
- **Covering.** Take `rank L + 1` families of `D`-bounded, `R`-disjoint boxes covering `L ≅ Z^ℓ`,
  with `R ≥ r`. Pull each box back by `δ` and split it into `r`-components.
  - `δ` is 1-Lipschitz, so pieces over distinct boxes of one family are `R`-disjoint. Pieces over
    one box are `r`-disjoint by construction.
  - This gives, for every `r`, a cover by `ℓ + 1` families of `r`-disjoint, uniformly bounded
    sets. Hence `asdim ≤ ℓ`. No geodesic or Hurewicz input is needed.

**Item 2.**
- Choose distinct points `x_1, …, x_r` of `C × C` in distinct orbits with trivial isotropy, and `y`
  over `Q = {x_i}`.
- **The map.** Matte Bon (Lemma `l-asdim-reduced-power` and Prop. `p-morphism-leaves`) gives an
  injective coarse map from a product of `r` leaves of `G_{2V}` into the leaf of `G_P` at `y`. Each
  leaf of `G_{2V}` is a product of two trees, so the product has `asdim = 2r`.
- **Its image lies in `S_{L_ρ}(y)`.** Every germ used is a germ of some `ρ̃(F)`. `F` agrees near the
  finite set `Q` with some `g ∈ 2V`: complete a disjoint box map using pure infiniteness, and
  `A = 2V`. So its degree lies in `L_ρ`.
- **Conclusion.** Injective coarse maps do not lower `asdim` (Prop. `p-sisto`). Item 1 then gives
  `2r ≤ rank L_ρ ≤ |P'|`.

**Item 3.** In each listed design, `L_ρ` is contained in a cyclic subgroup of `Z^2`, so its rank is
at most `1 < 2`. `∎`

## Comparing notes with bh-invent-02 (`G_(2,3) = ⟨B, B_3⟩ ≤ RCWA(Z)`)

- **Odometer hosts escape the rank bound.** `B = V_(2,1)(⟨a⟩)` and `G_(2,3)` contain the odometer
  `a : n ↦ n+1`, and `CT(Z)` does not (sign preservation). Their groupoids on `Ẑ_{3}` add the
  translation germs to `G_{3}`.
  - The fibres of `δ` then contain translation orbits, which have `asdim` 1.
  - The same argument gives only `asdim S_L ≤ rank L + 1`, so rank-one designs pass the dimension
    test there.
  - In short: the odometer is exactly the hidden coordinate that `CT(Z)` lacks and `B` has. Whether
    `2V ≤ B`, or `2V ≤ G_(2,3)`, is a sharp open question that no argument here excludes.
- **For finite presentation of `G_(2,3)`.** Its natural groupoid is the Zappa–Szép product of the
  arithmetic 2-graph `Λ_{3}` with `Z`, acting by the odometer self-similarly: `a(2x+1) = 2a(x)`,
  `a(3x+2) = 3a(x)`.
  - Li's Zappa–Szép finiteness corollary (arXiv:2110.04505, Cor. `intro:ZS`, as recorded in
    `research/artifacts/gq-bh-bh-kourovka-problems.md`; its hypotheses not checked here) is the
    natural route to `F_∞`.
  - Identifying `G_(2,3)` with that full group, or its derived subgroup, would be the analogue of
    `ct-p-z-is-a-one-vertex-k-graph-full-group`.
  - Our isotropy, slab and blind-commutant results all apply to that groupoid, since its germs are
    single affine maps.

**Lesson for general BH.**
- **The degree lattice limits what an embedding can use.** Germs whose degrees stay in a rank-`ℓ`
  sublattice live in an `asdim ≤ ℓ` slab of the leaf.
- **Consequence for product structures.** An embedded product of `m` tree directions needs `m`
  independent contraction directions. Hidden isometric coordinates (odometer fibres, units, carries)
  cost no dimension and so cannot supply them.
- **Where this bites.** This is the precise reason arithmetic hosts without translations cannot hide
  a second stack. It also explains why odometer hosts (`B`, `G_(2,3)`) are the right place to look
  for writers.
