For every von Neumann algebra `M` and every two unital embeddings
`phi,psi:Z->M`, one has

```text
M-rank(phi(a))=M-rank(psi(a))                 (a in Z).
```

Moreover every nonzero `phi(a)` and `psi(a)` is full in `M`.  Equivalently,
the inverse image under either embedding of every proper norm-closed ideal
of `M` is zero.

No countability, semifiniteness, factoriality, or central-decomposition
regularity is needed.  Thus a remaining von Neumann counterexample cannot be
caused by a support-projection cardinal or by different incidence with any
norm ideal; it would be a genuine failure of equal von Neumann rank to imply
norm approximate unitary equivalence for these ASH-domain maps.
