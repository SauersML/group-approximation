The robust approximate BCS gap is applied to the original shared Fanizza
selector words.  Local packet exactification then turns every support failure
into the conditional packet's fixed relative Pauli factor.  On the named child
carrier, parent absence is exactly the forbidden event, and
`child-carrier-relative-pauli-holonomy` converts its mass into a fixed local
relator-energy payment.

The Fanizza polynomial decompositions sum those local payments with their
existing exponentially decaying weights.
`fanizza-boone-mark-carrier-interface` supplies the separate surviving mark
and its positive initial carrier; no use is made of `D` as a nonhalting
separated mark.
