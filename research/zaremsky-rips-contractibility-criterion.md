title: "Zaremsky's criterion: small enclosing balls with a central centre make Vietoris–Rips complexes contractible"
---

Let `(X,d)` be a metric space with integer distances such that, for each `t`,
sets of diameter `t` have uniformly bounded cardinality. Fix `t_0`, and
suppose that for every `t > t_0` there is `r_t < t` with two properties:

- every `S ⊆ X` with `diam S = t` lies in a ball of radius `r_t` centred at a
  point of `X`;
- among those centres `Y(S) = {y ∈ X : d(y,s) ≤ r_t for all s ∈ S}`, some
  `y_0` lies within `t` of every other.

Then `VR_{t_0}(X)` is contractible.
