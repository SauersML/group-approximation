**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `B` be an affine building of type Ã₂ with boundary `Ω`,
and `Γ` a group of type rotating automorphisms of `B` acting freely on the vertex set
with finitely many orbits. Let `A = Γ \ 𝔗` (Γ-classes of type rotating tiles) and `M_1`,
`M_2` the `{0,1}` transition matrices of Robertson–Steger §7.

1. `M_1`, `M_2` satisfy (H0), (H1) and (H3).
2. The map `φ` is an isomorphism from the rank-two Cuntz–Krieger algebra `𝒜` onto
   `C(Ω) ⋊ Γ`. For `p ∈ 𝔚̄` one has `1_{Ω(p)} = φ(s_{w̄,w̄})` with `w̄ = ᾱ(p)`.
3. (H2), irreducibility of the combined transition graph, is proved in the paper only
   when `B` is the building of `PGL_3(K)` for a local field `K` of characteristic zero and
   `Γ` is a lattice acting freely on vertices. Then each `M_i` separately has an
   irreducible graph (Theorem `H(3)`, Corollary `H(3)+`). Those lattices are linear.

Not asserted here: primitivity of `M_i`, aperiodicity in the Kumjian–Pask sense, or
irreducibility for buildings that are not `PGL_3(K)`.

Source: G. Robertson, T. Steger, *Affine buildings, tiling systems and higher rank
Cuntz–Krieger algebras*, J. reine angew. Math. 513 (1999), arXiv:1302.5593, Theorem
`main2`, Proposition `M_1M_2`, Theorem `H(3)`, Corollary `H(3)+`.
