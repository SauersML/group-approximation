ESTABLISHED.  Let `tau` be a tracial state on `C*(G)`.  Define its projective
GNS kernel

```text
K_tau={g in G: |tau(g)|=1},
chi_tau(g)=tau(g) for g in K_tau.                      (NCT1)
```

Then `K_tau` is a normal subgroup, `chi_tau` is a conjugation-invariant scalar
character, and the `tau`-GNS representation satisfies

```text
pi_tau(g)=chi_tau(g) I,  g in K_tau.                  (NCT2)
```

Indeed equality in Cauchy--Schwarz gives
`pi_tau(g) xi_tau=chi_tau(g) xi_tau`.  Since `tau` is tracial, the same
zero `2`-norm identity holds after multiplication by every GNS vector, giving
the operator equality `(NCT2)`.  Normality and conjugation invariance follow.

Thus the raw GNS projective kernel always gives a phase-consistent character
face.  But that face is simply the state space of the corresponding twisted
group quotient.  It has a finite-dimensional point exactly when that quotient
has a finite-dimensional representation with the prescribed scalar
character.  Non-Connes-embeddability of the single trace `tau` says only that
`tau` itself is not a limit of matrix traces; it does not exclude unrelated
finite-dimensional states of the same quotient.

Consequently, proving

```text
F_(K_tau,chi_tau) intersect closure(S_fd(G)) = empty  (NCT3)
```

is already the full relative-FD character-face theorem required by
`toric-gap-is-a-relative-fd-central-word-face`.  GNS-kernel extraction proves
phase safety but does not lower finite-dimensional soundness.

## Why Hahn--Banach does not create word stabilizers

Hahn--Banach separates a non-CE trace from the closed finite-dimensional
state set by a general selfadjoint element of `C*(G)`.  A group-word
stabilizer is much more rigid: it requires equality

```text
|tau(g)|=1,
```

not merely a strict linear inequality.  The closed left kernel

```text
L_tau={a:tau(a* a)=0}
```

can therefore be strictly larger than the left ideal generated by the
binomials

```text
g-chi_tau(g),  g in K_tau.                            (NCT4)
```

A separating positive polynomial or losing projection need not lie in this
binomial ideal.  Hahn--Banach supplies no operation turning it into `(NCT4)`.

## Exact local affine-hull obstruction

The loss is already visible in one commuting Boolean context.  Let
`A=(C_2)^m`, let a vector state have positive spectral weight precisely on

```text
R <= {+1,-1}^m,
```

and let `Aff(R)` be its affine hull over `F_2`.  For `a in A`, the vector is
an eigenvector of `a` exactly when the character `r |-> r(a)` is constant on
`R`.  The collection of all such word-eigenvalue equations cuts out exactly
`Aff(R)`, because it is the annihilator of the difference span `R-R`.

Therefore every point of `Aff(R)`, including every forbidden point in
`Aff(R) minus R`, satisfies all scalar word stabilizers visible in the GNS
kernel of that context.  The losing projection distinguishes `R` from its
affine hull; subgroup-character equations do not.  In particular a nonlinear
BCS support cannot be recovered merely by reading the raw word eigenvalues of
its perfect trace.

## Abstract countercheck

Even trivial projective kernel is compatible with a non-CE trace on the same
group.  Let `tau_0` be non-CE and let `tau_reg` be the regular trace.  The set
of matrix-approximable traces is closed and convex, so a Hahn--Banach separator
shows that, for all sufficiently small `epsilon>0`,

```text
tau_epsilon=(1-epsilon) tau_0 + epsilon tau_reg        (NCT5)
```

is still non-CE.  For every `g!=1`, `tau_reg(g)=0`.  Equality
`|tau_epsilon(g)|=1` would force equality in the triangle inequality for both
summands and in particular `|tau_reg(g)|=1`, impossible.  Hence
`K_(tau_epsilon)={1}`.  Its maximal subgroup-character face is the whole state
space and contains the trivial one-dimensional state.

This proves that no functional-analytic implication

```text
non-CE trace => subgroup-character face gap
```

can hold without additional finite compiler structure, even before finite
presentation issues arise.

## Consequence

Starting from the fixed non-CE BCS trace, the only possible positive route is
to add finite syntax which converts its non-binomial zero-energy ideal into a
phase-safe binomial character face while preserving the entire finite-
dimensional gap.  The finite Morita, spherical-root, spin-word, and local
product-dilation versions of that conversion are ruled out in the neighboring
claims.  Any surviving construction is precisely
`central-word-state-face-compiler`.

## Attempts

The attempted positive construction took `K=K_tau` and used `(NCT2)` as the
toric determining clauses.  It succeeds perfectly on phase survival.  It
fails at `(NCT3)`: unrelated finite-dimensional quotient states remain, and
the local calculation shows that nonlinear losing projections have been
replaced by their affine hull.  Hahn--Banach separation does not repair this
because its witness is not a group binomial.
