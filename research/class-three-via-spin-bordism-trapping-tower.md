The prerequisite gives a simple unital C*-algebra A and unitaries u_1, u_2, v
with [u_2, [u_1, v]] not in U_0(A). That is the root statement verbatim.

This avoids the open excess-one persistence problem. Toms's trapping puts the
base dimension near four times the rank. There, spin bordism of the
block-diagonal locus sees eta squared through the twisting, while restriction
to a point cannot.
