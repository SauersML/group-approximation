**Dead.** Invalidated by
`compressed-coset-shifts-carry-strict-equivariant-embeddings`.

The implication is valid. An injective cellular automaton over `G` is a
continuous equivariant injection of the Bernoulli shift over the regular
transitive `G`-set `X = G`, so the premise would make it surjective. The premise
is false: a coset shift with infinite stabilizers over `R^x` carries a strict
embedding, and it has every Kazhdan and simplicity property the premise was
meant to use.
