**ESTABLISHED (literature, with a short derivation)** by
`reduced-free-product-k0-generated-by-factor-images-citation`.

Let `A_1, A_2` be separable unital C\*-algebras with GNS-faithful states `φ_1, φ_2`, and let
`A = (A_1, φ_1) * (A_2, φ_2)` be the reduced free product, with inclusions `j_l : A_l → A`. Then

```text
(j_1)_* + (j_2)_* : K_0(A_1) ⊕ K_0(A_2)  →  K_0(A)     is surjective.
```

**Group case.** For countable groups `G, H` with canonical traces,
`C*_r(G * H) = (C*_r G, τ) * (C*_r H, τ)`. The inclusions preserve the trace, so

```text
τ_*(K_0(C*_r(G * H)))  =  τ_*(K_0(C*_r G)) + τ_*(K_0(C*_r H)).
```

**Consequence without any regularity.** If `τ_*(K_0(C*_r G)) ⊆ Z` and `τ_*(K_0(C*_r H)) ⊆ Z`,
then `τ_*(K_0(C*_r(G * H))) ⊆ Z`. So the torsion-free trace conjecture passes to free products.

**Model test.**
- `G = H = Z`: `τ_*(K_0(C*_r F_2)) = Z`, as Pimsner--Voiculescu proved.
- `C_2 * C_3 = PSL_2(Z)`: `(1/2)Z + (1/3)Z = (1/6)Z`.
