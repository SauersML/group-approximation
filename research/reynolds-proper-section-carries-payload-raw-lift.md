
from `index-two-reynolds-hnn-is-a-proper-corner-section`.  Extend the finite

matrix microstates recover a raw unitary `W in pM_dp` satisfying

```text
W^2=Jp+o_HS(1),                                      (RRL1)
```

and the charged return through `v` uses this literal `W`, not merely its
projective class.  The recovery error must be bounded by a
dimension-independent constant times the presentation defect.


section does not qualify.
