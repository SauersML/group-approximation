The augmentation module `M` has index two in the full lamp module `L`, so
`M rtimes A` has index two in `L rtimes A`.  Finite presentability passes
both to finite-index subgroups and to finite extensions.  Cornulier's
finite-presentation theorem for `C_2 wr_X A` then gives precisely the three
conditions listed in the target claim.  For a 2-transitive action the
diagonal action on `X^2` has the diagonal and off-diagonal as its only two
orbits.  What remains is exactly finite presentability of `A` and finite
generation of a point stabilizer.
