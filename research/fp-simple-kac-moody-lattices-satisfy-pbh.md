**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G(F)` be as in `fp-simple-kac-moody-lattices-are-mif`: a
finitely presented simple non-affine Kac--Moody group over a finite field in the
sense of [CR09]. Then `G(F)`, and every subgroup of `G(F)`, satisfies the
permutational Boone--Higman conjecture, i.e. embeds in a group admitting an action
of type (A).

**What this answers.** BFFHZ, arXiv:2503.21882v2, l.754--767, write that "the only
existing source of finitely presented infinite simple groups for which PBH remains
open is non-affine Kac–Moody groups over finite fields [CR09]" and ask in Question
3.6: "Are finitely presented simple Kac–Moody groups highly transitive? Are they
MIF? Do they satisfy PBH?" The second part is Rybak's Proposition A. This node
records the third part, which follows at once. The first part stays open.

**Credit.** No novelty is claimed. This is the combination of Rybak's Proposition A
with BFFHZ Theorem C. Rybak states the MIF part and calls it a partial answer to
Question 3.6; the PBH consequence is BFFHZ's own "In particular" clause.
