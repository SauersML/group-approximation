**ESTABLISHED SCOPED ANALYTIC FENCE.**  Let the root-transporter packet be

```text
K=<z,r,h | z^2=r^2=h^2=1, [z,r]=1, hzh^(-1)=r,
              hrh^(-1)=z>.                            (RUD1)
```

It has the one-dimensional marked representation

```text
pi(z)=pi(r)=pi(h)=-1.                                  (RUD2)
```

Thus the marked and transported-root projections are both the identity,
and the root-valued overlap is maximal.  Nevertheless `h` has no nonzero
subprojection on which it is close to the identity.

More concretely, take the direct-product packet of any exact finite
same-center matrix packet with `(RUD2)`.  Let `F` be its common carrier and
let `S_1,S_2` be two full unitaries reducing `F`; they commute with the
root-transporter factor.  Use the `z` in that factor as the marked sign for
this scoped interface test.  Replace only the second proposed selector
occurrence by

```text
N_1=S_1,                 N_2=hS_2=-S_2.                (RUD3)
```

The relative word is genuinely root-valued:

```text
(S_1^(-1)hS_2)z(S_1^(-1)hS_2)^(-1)=r.                 (RUD4)
```

Its cross-Gram square is still `F`, and multiplication of the relative
polar by the scalar `-1` preserves every mixed Pauli-commutator test.  But
for every subprojection `P<=F`,

```text
||(N_2-S_2)P||_2^2=4 tau(P).                           (RUD5)
```

Consequently no subcorner of mass at least `gamma tau(F)-o(1)`, for fixed
`gamma>0`, authenticates the dressed occurrence as the undressed Whitehead
candidate with `o(1)` normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt error.  Polar cutting the
positive cross Gram retains the external sign; it cannot erase it.

This is a finite countermodel to the proposed **analytic interface**

```text
root-valued relative word + positive cross Gram + mixed Pauli tests
       => same-center native-selector attachment.
```

It is not asserted to extend to the full binary-Leavitt elementary or
Steinberg presentation.  Extra native occurrence relations may exclude the
one-sided sign, but such a relation must be used explicitly.  In particular,
`root-valued-relative-transport-forces-cross-gram` remains a valid route to
the arbitrary-profile scalar overlap after the two full-word restrictions
have already been authenticated; it is not an occurrence-typing theorem.

No Property T, Kazhdan input, canonical trace, or literature theorem is
used.
