**REFUTED ROUTE.** Append the twelve reverse-dictionary chart seams to the
three factorizations of `x_12(ee)` and treat chart surjectivity as an
independent occurrence of their common RHS.

The reverse seams contain only single-letter roots. None contains
`x_12(ee)`, so they project trivially on the return-star path/RHS columns and
leave its quotient-rank increment equal to two. See
`atlas-reverse-dictionary-does-not-close-ee-return`.
