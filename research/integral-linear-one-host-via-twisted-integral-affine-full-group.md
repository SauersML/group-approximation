`W` is finitely presented and simple. It contains `E(Z; D) ⋊ F`, acting 2-adically linearly on
finitely many coordinates and by `F` on coordinates, hence `Σ = E(Z) ⋊ Z` and every `GL_n(Z)`.
So every finitely generated subgroup of every `GL_n(Z)` lies in the single finitely presented
simple group `W`. ∎ (Lane proofs throughout; see the required nodes for status.)
