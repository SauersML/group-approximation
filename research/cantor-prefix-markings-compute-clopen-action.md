title: A recursive presentation with a marked standard V computes its Cantor action on clopen sets

Suppose D is finitely generated and recursively presented, and a
correctly marked copy of the standard binary prefix group V is
specified in D. Assume D has a faithful action on the binary Cantor
set C which restricts to the standard action of this marked V.

The presentation and marking compute the exact clopen set g(U)
for every word g in D and every clopen U given by a finite prefix
code. They compute the action on infinite binary inputs in the
usual oracle sense. Moreover, at most one faithful action on C can
extend the specified standard V action, even without the
effectiveness assumptions for that uniqueness assertion.

If p is a computable Cantor point and its D stabilizer has a known
finite generating set, membership in that stabilizer is decidable.

These are conditional algorithms for correct marked presentations
and existing faithful actions. They do not recognize which presentations
admit such actions, construct a finitely presented overgroup, or
bound the action's computational complexity.
