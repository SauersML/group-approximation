After refining coefficient regions over
`V_4(h_0,h_2)*V_4(k_0,k_1)*F(h_1,k_2)`, a maximal internal gate corridor
cannot terminate on a genuine longer identity in the six corner labels.
Every such label which is trivial in `G_0*G_1` is already trivial in the
displayed short group and therefore admits further refinement.  Thus a
maximal refined corridor reaches the outer boundary or is closed; it has no
long Leavitt termination face.
