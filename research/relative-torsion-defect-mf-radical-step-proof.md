Full derivation in artifact section 3.

**Radical descent.** For a normal `N <= Rad_MF(G)`, `Rad_MF(G)` is the preimage of
`Rad_MF(G/N)`. If `g in Rad_MF(G)` and `th : G/N -> H` with `H` MF, then `th . pi` kills
`g`. Conversely if `pi(g) in Rad_MF(G/N)` and `th : G -> H` with `H` MF, then
`N <= ker th`, so `th` factors through `pi` and kills `g`.

**The step.** Write `G = EL_n(R)`, `N = EL_n(R,J)`, `Gbar = G/N`. If `e in J` then
`ReR <= J` and there is nothing to prove, so assume `ebar != 0` in `R/J`. Two ring expressions
agreeing modulo `J` have elementary matrices differing by a factor `e_ij(a)` with `a in J`,
which is trivial in `Gbar`; so every elementary-matrix identity that the required claim's proof
derives from `ts = 1`, `es = te = 0`, `f_ij f_kl = delta_jk f_il` and `me = 0` holds in
`Gbar`. Run that construction in `Gbar`, with `S` the unital subring of `R` generated by
`s,t` and `Lbar` the image of `EL_3(S)`:

- `Lbar` is a quotient of `EL_3(S)` and `S` is finitely generated, so `Lbar` has property (T);
- the compressor, centralizer and commutator identities hold in `Gbar`, so `zbar` lies in the
  compression defect of `Lbar` inside `Bbar = <Lbar, vbar, cbar, ybar>`;
- `zbar^m = 1`, since `z^m` is the diagonal matrix at `1 + m f_12` and `m f_12 in J`;
- `zbar != 1`, because its image in `EL_n(R/J)` is nontrivial: `tbar fbar_12 sbar^2 = ebar != 0`;
- `<zbar>` is central in `Bbar`.

The one-sided compression criterion applied to `Bbar` kills `zbar` under every homomorphism from
`Bbar` to an MF group, hence from `Gbar`, since MF passes to subgroups. The printed propagation
through `[z, e_41(f_20)]` and `[e_24(t), e_41(f_10)]` then puts every `e_ij(aeb)` in
`Rad_MF(Gbar)`, and Steinberg relations with signed permutations give the image of
`EL_n(R, ReR + J)`. Radical descent lifts this back to `Rad_MF(G)`.
