Let `C_h` be the harmonic Cuntz ring of `harmonic-cuntz-ring-is-purely-infinite-simple`,
and let `EL_3(C_h)` be the subgroup of `GL_3(C_h)` generated by the elementary
matrices `e_ij(x)`, `i ≠ j`, `x ∈ C_h`. Let `Z = EL_3(C_h) ∩ {λI : λ ∈ Q^x}` and put

    Λ_h = EL_3(C_h) / Z.

1. **Simple.** For `n >= 3`, every normal subgroup of `EL_n(C_h)` either lies in
   the central scalar matrices or is all of `EL_n(C_h)`. Hence `Λ_h` is an infinite
   simple group.
2. **Finitely generated.** `EL_3(C_h)` is generated by the 42 matrices `e_ij(x)`
   with `i ≠ j` and `x ∈ {1, s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1, m_η, m_ζ}`.
3. **Contains every GL_n(Q).**
   - For `r >= 1`, `SL_(3r)(Q) = E_3(M_r(Q))` embeds in `EL_3(C_h)` through the
     unital embedding `M_r(Q) ⊂ C_h`, and it meets `Z` only in `±I`.
   - Take `3r >= n + 2`. Then `g ↦ diag(g, det(g)^(-1), 1, ..., 1)` embeds `GL_n(Q)`
     in `SL_(3r)(Q)` avoiding `±I`, so `GL_n(Q)` embeds in `Λ_h`.
4. **Contains Λ.** `Λ = L_Q(1,2)^x / Q^x` embeds in `Λ_h`. So does Thompson's `V`,
   and so does `SL_3(Z)`.
5. **Solvable word problem.** `Λ_h` has solvable word problem in the generators
   of part 2.
6. **Not finitely presented.** `Z = Q^x · I ≅ Z/2 × Z^(∞)` is central and not
   finitely generated. Since `EL_3(C_h)` is finitely generated, **`Λ_h` is not
   finitely presented**, and neither is `EL_3(C_h)/C` for any central `C ⊇ Z`. (The
   first version of this node said only "at most one of `EL_3(C_h)` and `Λ_h` is
   finitely presented", which was too weak.)

## Consequence

`Λ_h` is a single natural finitely generated simple group containing every
`GL_n(Q)`. It is built from a purely infinite simple ring: the algebraic shadow of a
dense subring of the Cuntz algebra `O_2`.
- **It is not itself the host.** By part 6, the routes `gl-n-q-via-harmonic-cuntz-host`
  and `rational-leavitt-fp-simple-via-harmonic-host` do not reach their targets
  (`harmonic-cuntz-simple-group-is-not-finitely-presented`).
- **What remains open.** Whether `Λ_h` embeds in some finitely presented simple
  group. A group that is finitely presented and simple cannot be a central quotient
  of `EL_N(C_h)`. It must be:
  - a quotient by a non-central normal subgroup, or
  - a group built from `C_h` without a full `L_Q(1,2)^x` inside,

  as in `leavitt-scalar-commutators-block-fp-central-quotients`, Consequences.
