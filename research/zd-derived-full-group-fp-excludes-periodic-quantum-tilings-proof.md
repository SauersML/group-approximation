Full proof: artifact, Sections 1–3. Outline with every input named.

**Inputs.**
- `G = D([[Z^2 ~ X]])` is generated by the rule 3-cycles `S = {s(pi, e)}` of legal `B(R)`-patterns,
  and it is finitely generated, infinite and simple (`minimal-z2-subshift-derived-full-groups-are-fg-simple`).
- `A_D` and Lemma 1.2 are from the ring artifact
  `research/artifacts/sft-crossed-product-presentation-rigidity-2026-09-13.md`, Section 1, which backs
  `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`. Lemma 1.2 says: a linear combination of words of length
  `<= D` in the generators that vanishes in `R_X = LC(X, k) ⋊ Z^2` vanishes in `A_D`.
- Malcev: finitely generated linear groups are residually finite
  (`finitely-generated-linear-groups-are-residually-finite`).

**Proof.**
1. **Units.** For `g ∈ [[Z^2 ~ X]]` put `U_g = sum_v chi[kappa_(g^(-1)) = v] u^v`. The cocycle identity
   gives `U_g U_h = U_(gh)`. For `s ∈ S` fix an expression `Û_s` as a combination of words of bounded
   length `l_0`.
2. **The constant.** Let `G = <S | ℛ>` with relators of length `<= L`. Choose `D_0` at least the length
   of each of these combinations, all zero in `R_X`:
   - `Û_s Û_(s^(-1)) - 1` and `Û_(s^(-1)) Û_s - 1`, where `S` is symmetric because
     `s(pi, -e) = s(pi, e)^(-1)`;
   - `Û_(s_1) ... Û_(s_m) - 1` for each relator, written with letters in `S`;
   - `chi[pi] + chi[pi] (Û_s - 1) chi[pi]` for `s = s(pi, e)`;
   - `sum_pi chi[pi] - 1`, over legal `pi` on `B(R)`.

   By Lemma 1.2 all of them vanish in `A_D` for `D >= D_0`. So `s -> Û_s` induces a homomorphism
   `G -> A_D^×`.
3. **The third identity holds in `R_X`.** `s` cycles three disjoint clopen sets `C = [pi]`, `s(C)` and
   `s^2(C)`. So for `x ∈ C` with `kappa_(s^(-1))(x) = v` we have `sigma^v x ∉ C`. Every coefficient of
   `chi_C U_s chi_C` therefore vanishes, and `chi_C (U_s - 1) chi_C = -chi_C`.
4. **Malcev.** A finite-dimensional `A_D`-module `V` gives a homomorphism `G -> GL(V)`. Its image is
   finitely generated and linear, hence residually finite, and it is a quotient of the infinite
   simple group `G`. So the image is trivial.
5. **Zero module.** Each `Û_s` acts as `1` on `V`. By the third identity every `chi[pi]` acts as `0`,
   and by the fourth, `1` acts as `0`. So `V = 0`. ∎

**Consequence used by `labbe-not-fp-via-periodic-quantum-tilings`.** A `Λ`-periodic family on a
finite-dimensional `W` defines a module on `W ⊗ k[Z^2/Λ]`: `e_a` acts at `zbar` by `E_a(zbar)` and
`u^g` translates, so `u^h e_a u^(-h)` acts at `zbar` by `E_a(zbar + h)`. Relations (R1)–(R4) hold
sitewise.
