title: Infinite-by-infinite wreath quotients force infinite nuclear dimension
---

Let `G` be a countable discrete amenable group.  Suppose that `G` admits a
surjection onto the restricted wreath product

```text
K wr H = (direct_sum_H K) rtimes H,
```

where `K` is an infinite virtually polycyclic group and `H` is an infinite
finitely generated virtually nilpotent group.  Then

```text
dim_nuc(C*(G))=infinity.
```

This obstruction is independent of the rational-rank obstruction from
abelianization.  Indeed,

```text
Q=D_infinity wr D_infinity
```

is finitely generated and amenable, has finite abelianization (hence
`rank_Q(Q_ab)=0`), and nevertheless has group C*-algebra of infinite nuclear
dimension.  Consequently every countable amenable extension of `Q` has
infinite-dimensional group C*-algebra, even though this need not be witnessed
by its abelianization.
