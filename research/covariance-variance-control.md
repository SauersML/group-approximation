title: Covariance controls total single-edge transition variance
---

Conjugating the explicit source Fourier cut by the actual unitary
compressor puts it within the covariance RMS defect of the entire coarse
target Fourier cut, a sum of fine extension-character cuts, so
`Σ_ζ ‖[U P_χ U*, Q_ζ]‖₂² ≤ 2·(covariance RMS defect)²`. The intrinsic
effects round to a fine-outcome PVM with total error below nine times the
squared covariance defect, independent of all fiber sizes. Positivity,
source gauge, and single-edge measurement recovery are closed. (The finite
Pauli model shows separate exact measurements need not be jointly
classical — that is the multi-edge gate, not this theorem.)
