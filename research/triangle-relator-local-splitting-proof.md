Notation as in the target.  Throughout, (L)LP is Enders--Shulman
arXiv:2403.12224 Definition 2.2 (source-verified): a unital `A` has
the LP (resp. LLP) if every ccp map from `A` into *any* quotient
C*-algebra `B/I` lifts to a ccp map into `B` (resp. lifts on every
finite-dimensional operator system of `A`).

**Step 1 (partial colimit).**  The colimit of the sub-diagram on
`{0, 1, 2, 3, 12, 23}` is `G_0 = P_12 *_{P_2} P_23`: a compatible
family on the sub-poset is determined by its values on `P_12, P_23`,
constrained exactly to agree on `P_2` (the legs through `P_0, P_1,
P_3` factor through these and impose nothing new, all arrows being
the given inclusions).  `G_0` is generated by the two finite vertex
groups, and it acts on its Bass--Serre tree with finite stabilizers,
so it is finitely generated virtually free.  By Enders--Shulman
Corollary 4.6 (finite tree product with finite edge groups, vertex
algebras finite-dimensional hence LP by Choi--Effros), `C*(G_0)` has
the LP, hence the LLP.  This is (TC1).

**Step 2 (the subgroup E).**  The map `P_1 *_{P_0} P_3 -> G_0` induced
by the inclusions is injective: a reduced word alternates letters from
`P_1 \ P_0` and `P_3 \ P_0`; by (H1) these sets lie in `P_12 \ P_2`
and `P_23 \ P_2` respectively, so the word is reduced in the amalgam
`G_0` and hence nontrivial (Serre, Trees, I.1.2).  Its image is
`E = <P_1 cup P_3>`.

**Step 3 (the colimit is a relator quotient).**  By (H2) the induced
map `q : E -> P_13`, `q|_{P_1} = incl`, `q|_{P_3} = incl`, is onto.
Set `N := ker q` and `Lambda' := G_0 / <<N>>_{G_0}`.  Claim:
`Lambda'` with the evident legs is the colimit of the full triangle.
Cocone-to-map: a cocone `(f_J)` gives `f_0 : G_0 -> H` (Step 1) with
`f_0 o iota = f_13 o q` on `E` (both sides agree on the generators
`P_1 cup P_3` by the cocone identities), so `f_0` kills `iota(N)` and
factors through `Lambda'`.  Map-to-cocone: a morphism `g` on
`Lambda'` restricts to legs on `P_12, P_23`; on `P_13` define
`f_13(q(e)) := g(iota(e))`, well defined because `g o iota` kills
`N`, a homomorphism, and compatible with the `P_1, P_3, P_0` legs by
construction.  The two constructions are mutually inverse, so
`Lambda = Lambda' = G_0 / <<N>>`.

**Step 4 (structure of N).**  `q` onto gives `[E : N] = |P_13|`.  `N`
acts on the Bass--Serre tree `T` of `E = P_1 *_{P_0} P_3` (vertices
`E/P_1 u E/P_3`, edges `E/P_0`).  Vertex stabilizers in `E` are
conjugates of `P_1, P_3`; since `N` is normal, `N cap P_1^e = (N cap
P_1)^e = (ker q|_{P_1})^e = 1` and likewise for `P_3`, so `N` acts
freely on `T`: `N` is free, `N = pi_1(N \ T)`.  Because `N = ker q`,
the double cosets collapse: `N \ E / P_1 = P_13 / P_1`, `N \ E / P_3
= P_13 / P_3`, `N \ E / P_0 = P_13 / P_0`, so `N \ T` is the stated
coset graph `L` (connected, as a quotient of the connected `T`), and

```text
rank N = 1 - chi(L) = 1 - [P_13:P_1] - [P_13:P_3] + [P_13:P_0].
```

For the split rank-2 groups of Lie type with `P_0` the Borel, `L` is
the incidence graph of the generalized `m`-gon (girth `2m`, Tits) and
the rank equals the number of chambers opposite a fixed chamber,
`|B w_0 B / B| = q^{l(w_0)} = q^m`, by the Bruhat decomposition.

**Step 5 (the C*-quotient).**  `C*(Lambda) = C*(G_0)/I` where `I` is
the closed ideal generated by `{u_x - 1 : x in <<N>>}` (universal
properties on both sides: unitary representations of `Lambda` =
unitary representations of `G_0` killing `<<N>>`).  The identities

```text
u_{nm} - 1 = u_n (u_m - 1) + (u_n - 1),
u_{n^{-1}} - 1 = -u_{n^{-1}} (u_n - 1),
u_{g n g^{-1}} - 1 = u_g (u_n - 1) u_{g^{-1}}
```

show that the `r` elements `u_{n_k} - 1` over a free basis `{n_k}` of
`N` already generate `I`.  This completes (TC2).

**Step 6 (transfer lemma, (TC3)).**  Let `A` be unital with the LLP,
`I` an ideal, `Q = A/I`, `pi : A -> Q`.

*LLP(Q) => locally split.*  Apply Definition 2.2 to the ccp map
`id_Q : Q -> A/I`: every finite-dimensional operator system `E c Q`
admits a ccp `psi : E -> A` with `pi o psi = incl`.  Upgrade to ucp:
pick a state `omega` on `E` and set `psi''(x) = psi(x) + omega(x)(1 -
psi(1))`; then `psi''` is a sum of cp maps, `psi''(1) = 1`, and `pi o
psi'' = incl` because `pi(psi(1)) = 1`.

*Locally split => LLP(Q).*  Given ccp `phi : Q -> B/J` and
finite-dimensional operator system `E c Q`, take a ucp section
`s : E -> A`.  Then `s(E)` is a finite-dimensional operator system in
`A` (`s` is unital, self-adjoint and injective), and `phi o pi : A ->
B/J` is ccp, so by LLP of `A` its restriction to `s(E)` lifts to a
ccp `Psi : s(E) -> B`.  Then `Psi o s : E -> B` is ccp and `pi_B o
Psi o s = phi o pi o s = phi|_E`.

*LP versions.*  `LP(Q) =>` apply the definition to `id_Q` and upgrade
to ucp as above: a global ucp splitting.  Conversely, with `s : Q ->
A` a global ucp section and `A` LP: for ccp `phi : Q -> B/J` lift
`phi o pi` globally to `Phi : A -> B`; then `Phi o s` is a ccp lift
of `phi`.

**Step 7 (conclusion, (TC4)).**  Apply Step 6 with `A = C*(G_0)`
(LP and LLP by Step 1) and `I = I_N`.
