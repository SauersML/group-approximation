With W_m the closed wall event and V_A,m,V_B,m the two height-zero
open ternary-tree cluster volumes through depth m, the uncapped search
satisfies N>=R_m=1_W_m*(min(V_A,m,V_B,m)-1) for each m. For 0<q<1,
E sup_m R_m <= E sum_m R_m <=
3(1-q)^3/((2-q)(1-3q(1-q)^2)) <= 27/5.
This bounds lower certificates, not E N or conditional infinite-cluster
search cost. The sum of the certificates is not asserted to be <=N.
