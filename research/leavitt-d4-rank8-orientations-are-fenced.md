Among the 216 cyclic/inverse orientations of the degree-four shared-carrier
triangle, exactly five have abelian copy-constraint rank eight.  None can
produce a nontrivial one-copy boundary, for arbitrary coefficient units.

Four form one cyclic/inverse class and are already killed by their four copy
projections.  The fifth has a distinct carrier matching pattern; exhaustive
free-factor normal form reduces its boundary to a two-parameter six-syllable
word which is either trivial or has length at least four.
