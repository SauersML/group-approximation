**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`quillen-k-theory-of-finite-fields-citation`). For every finite field `F_q` and every `n ≥ 1`,
`K_n(F_q) ≅ Z/(q^i − 1)` when `n = 2i − 1`, and `K_n(F_q) = 0` when `n` is even.

Over `F_2`: `K_1(F_2) = K_2(F_2) = 0`, `K_3(F_2) ≅ Z/3`, `K_5(F_2) ≅ Z/7`, and `K_even = 0`.
