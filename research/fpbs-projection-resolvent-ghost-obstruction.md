Independent vertex ghost marks of probability s give
Q_{p,s}<=[I+p L_G/(2(1-p))+s I/(1-s)]^{-1}. Section 4 proves this by
scaling independent constraint odds. However, separate bounds do not
bound Q_{p,0}-Q_{p,s} above by the corresponding resolvent difference:
the single edge with p=1/2,s=1/4 has respective diagonal values 15/64
and 9/56. The derivative form yields critical connectivity, so this
failed subtraction cannot close the universal critical L2 argument.
