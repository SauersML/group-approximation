Let `(N,tau)` be any finite factor with separable predual and normalized
trace. For `m>=1` and a positive contraction `a in N`, let

```text
F_(N,m)(a)={(v_1,...,v_m):
 v_j^*v_j=a and (v_iv_i^*)(v_jv_j^*)=0 for i!=j}.     (AF2-1)
```

Give tuples the Hilbert-sum `2`-metric. If

```text
m tau(supp(a))<1,                                      (AF2-2)
```

then every continuous map `S^2->F_(N,m)(a)` contained in the ball of
radius

```text
rho_AF,2(epsilon)=min(epsilon,1)^3/216000000           (AF2-3)
```

about one point extends over `B^3` inside the concentric
`epsilon`-ball. The modulus is independent of the factor type, matrix
size, II1 isomorphism class, copy number, weight, and numerical size of
the positive slack.

Every fibre satisfying (AF2-2) is two-connected. Together with the
factor-uniform path and loop results, the entire family is equi-`LC^2`.
Both type I and type II1 factors are included; no continuity or local
constancy of factor type is part of the statement.
