Let `phi,psi:Z->B` be unital embeddings into a unital C-star algebra.  If
`pi:B->M` is a unital star homomorphism into any separably acting von Neumann
algebra, then `pi phi` and `pi psi` are norm approximately unitarily equivalent
in `M`.

Consequently, a negative answer to LXIII cannot be detected in any
separably acting von Neumann representation of the pair-generated algebra,
including a type-III representation.  This is a statement about unitaries in
the von Neumann target; it does not lift those unitaries back to `pi(B)`.
