Construct totally computably from a machine index `m` a finite generating set,
a uniformly recursively enumerable relator stream presenting `Gamma_m`, and a
marked word `d_m`, such that

```text
m halts       => some exact representation of Gamma_m has d_m != I,
m does not halt
              => every homomorphism from Gamma_m to a tracial matrix
                 ultraproduct kills d_m.
```

Equivalently, the NONHALT clause says `d_m in Rad_hyp(Gamma_m)`. No
finite-coordinate decoder, computable defect threshold, convergence modulus,
matrix dimension bound, or runtime bound is part of this target. By
`hyperlinear-radical-presentation-compactness`, dimension-free finite-window
collapse exists semantically, but the fixed-point program never needs to know
its constants.

This is the weakest currently identified sufficient groupification interface
for the Fanizza native signal. It remains open: the exact ultraproduct decoder
must still overcome nonlinear BCS addition and multiplicity return.

## Attempts

- The stronger coordinatewise decoder
  `fanizza-native-signal-groupification` would imply this claim, but still pays
  for every finite-matrix multiplicity-return estimate.
- A purely exact ultraproduct decoder may use non-effective compactness by
  `hyperlinear-radical-presentation-compactness`; the unresolved step is to
  turn the native additive BCS relations into group-word consequences without
  admitting a rectangular or affine escape.
- An unconditioned cutoff below `sqrt(2)` cannot coexist with any exact
  finite-dimensional representation detecting the mark, by
  `reverse-kleene-unconditioned-collapse-forces-fd-invisibility`. This is the
  first falsification test for every proposed completion cell.
