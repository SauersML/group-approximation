**ESTABLISHED REDUCTION.**  Let `A` be the finite-dimensional
star-algebra generated by an exact corrected shared BCS and role tuple, let
`B_f in A` be the role-packed forbidden projection, and let `Q in A'` be a
reducing carrier.  Suppose `B_f` has full reducing support on `Q`, as in
`agent-aggregate-escape-forbidden-support-saturation`.

If a unitary `U` normalizes `A`, put

```text
R=UQU^*,                 D=UQB_fU^*.                   (ANP1)
```

Then `R in A'` and

```text
Hull_A(D)=R,                                             (ANP2)
Hull_A(Q join D)=Q join R.                              (ANP3)
```

Thus after the mandatory reducing-hull step, a normalizer transport does
not install a carrier whose size remembers `tau(QB_f)`.  It installs the
whole translated carrier `UQU^*`.  The role-packed BCS gap is used only to
certify full support; its quantitative forbidden density has disappeared
from the successor geometry.

For a sequence of normalizer words `U_0,...,U_(N-1)`, the reached carriers
are therefore exactly orbit hulls:

```text
Q_(n+1)=Q_n join U_n Q_n U_n^*.                       (ANP4)
```

In particular, if the words lie in a fixed finite normalizer packet, the
chain stabilizes inside the finite orbit hull of `Q_0`.  Repetition cannot
create a fresh payload-dependent layer after that stabilization.  The Pauli
seed flip is the extremal first example: its first orbit hull is already the
whole marked sector by
`agent-aggregate-escape-atomwise-seed-flip-saturates`.

There is a dimension-free perturbative form for a fixed normalizer packet.
After exactifying that packet and the shared tuple, if the corrected
transport is `O(sqrt(E))` from `(ANP1)`, then the complement of its reducing
hull inside `R` has trace `O(sqrt(E))`, with the constant from `(AFS4)`.
Hence enlarging the finite normalizer table, using longer finite packet
orbits, or replacing the seed flip by a finite permutation/Clifford
normalizer cannot supply the missing renewable row.
