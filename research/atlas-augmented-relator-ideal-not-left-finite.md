Let

```text
F=A8*A8,
Gamma_+=F/N,
P=F2[F],
I=ker(P -> F2[Gamma_+]).                               (RIL1)
```

Although `N` is finitely normally generated by the augmented Atlas relation
list and `I` is finitely generated as a two-sided ideal, one has

```text
N is a free group of infinite rank,
I is not finitely generated as a left P-module.        (RIL2)
```

More generally, for a normal subgroup `N normal F` and a field `k`,

```text
ker(k[F] -> k[F/N]) is left-finitely-generated
 iff N is finitely generated as an abstract group.     (RIL3)
```

Consequently there is no fixed finite family of left coefficients
`a_(j,r) in P` such that, in every module, the full augmented defect
orbit-span is already the sum of the images of

```text
a_(j,r)(r-1),   r in R_+.                             (RIL4)
```

In particular no universal bounded alternating-depth saturation argument can
prove augmented rank compactness.  Any positive proof must be
representation-specific: it must use normalized rank, the repeated-natural
chart type, and the simultaneous smallness of the thirteen generator images
to show that the infinitely generated left ideal nevertheless occupies less
than the whole finite module.
