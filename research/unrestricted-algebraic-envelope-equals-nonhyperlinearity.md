The following two existence statements are equivalent.

1. There are a compact second-countable abelian group `K`, a countable group
   `Lambda` acting continuously on `K` by automorphisms, and a non-Connes-
   embeddable tracial von Neumann algebra which embeds trace-preservingly in
   `L^infinity(K) rtimes Lambda`.
2. There is a countable non-hyperlinear group.

The equivalence remains true with "explicit, finitely generated, recursively
presented" imposed compatibly on the resulting semidirect product and on the
group in (2).

This is a graph-hygiene fence.  An envelope theorem restricted to one fixed
source factor, a prescribed independently hyperlinear actor, a nontrivial
base, or a concrete module class can still be useful.  But the unrestricted
form allowing *any* non-CE algebra is not a reduction of the main problem.
