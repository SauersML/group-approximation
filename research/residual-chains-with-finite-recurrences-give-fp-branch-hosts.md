Inputs this claim recorded under the older schema (not a proof; they do not establish it): `branch-host-v-recodings-need-recurrent-residual-chains`.

**OPEN.** Two parts.
- **(S) Sufficiency.** Let `G` be finitely generated, recursively presented and `EFRF^+`. Suppose it
  has a residual chain `(N_n)` and codes `D_n` for which:
  - the germ group `⟨Ĝ_p, t⟩` of the recoded host of
    `branch-host-v-recodings-need-recurrent-residual-chains` is finitely presented;
  - the annular `G`-data are the unique solution, beyond a finite seed, of finitely many of its
    recurrences `∏_i ρ_(n+δ_i)(g_i) = 1`.

  Then `Γ_ρ`, or a finitely presented group containing it, is finitely presented. That gives
  Boone–Higman for `G` through the type (A) / twisted Brin–Thompson wrapper.
- **(E) Existence beyond self-similarity.** Some finitely presented residually finite group has a
  chain pinned by multi-depth recurrences but no chain generated by a virtual endomorphism
  (item 5 of the parent).
  - Candidates are the unresolved regular-tree cases: closed `Mod(S_g)` and `Out(F_n)`, with
    characteristic-quotient chains, and hypothetical inputs with unbounded prime torsion.
  - The last kind can have no virtual-endomorphism chain, because a regular-tree action bounds the
    torsion primes.

**Why this is the right gate.**
- Necessity is proved in the parent, and the one-step case is exactly the regular-tree
  self-similar route. So (S) plus (E) is precisely the room the branch reduction adds over that
  route.
- The multi-depth form is also the chain version of the machine-transition germs (bh-free-22) and
  of the master route's G2 rigidity (`board/SYNTHESIS.md`). A finite nonabelian recurrence that
  pins an infinite sequence of finite permutation groups is a one-dimensional rigid tiling of the
  chain.

**Calibration.**
- For `G = Z` with `N_n = 2^n Z`, the one-step odometer recurrence exists. So does the finitely
  presented self-similar host, the odometer inside the Röver–Nekrashevych group `V_2(Z)`.
- No example of (E) is known.

**Lesson for general BH.** The residually finite sector needs one new object: a finitely presented
recurrence that computes the level actions of a residual chain, i.e. a virtual endomorphism with
memory. Decidability of the input (`EFRF^+`) is exactly what makes such a recurrence
conceivable, since the level actions are computable. Finite presentation asks for them to be
forced by local rules.
