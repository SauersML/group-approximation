**ESTABLISHED** (items 1–4, lane proof below, elementary; not yet refereed; no priority claimed). Item 5 is
the open question it isolates.

## The automaton (four-letter form of the test case)

`X = {0,1,2,3}`, `u = π(u_0,u_1,u_2,u_3)`.
- **Grigorchuk part** on `{0,1}`: `a = (0 1)(1,1,1,1)`, `b = (a,c,1,1)`, `c = (a,d,1,1)`, `d = (1,b,1,1)`.
- **Spine:** `ŝ_γ = (γ, 1, ŝ_γ, 1)` for `γ in {a,b,c,d}`.
- **Mover:** `n = (2 3)(1, 1, 1, n)`, recurrent (it loops at `3`), and it moves the spine letter `2`.
All states are involutions except `n`. The set is closed under sections and inverses
(`n^{-1} = (2 3)(1,1,n^{-1},1)`), and the activity is linear. `G = ⟨S⟩`.

(The three-letter form `n = σ_{(0 2)}(n,1,1)` also makes `0` do double duty, as a Grigorchuk letter and as an
odometer digit. The four-letter form keeps the one new feature, a recurrent state moving the spine ray.)

## Statement

1. **Run structure.** Write a point as `u y w` with `u in {2,3}^*` its maximal `{2,3}`-prefix (length `k`),
   `y in {0,1}`, and `v = y w` read up to its first letter in `{2,3}`. Then:
   - `a,b,c,d` act only when `k = 0`, as Grigorchuk's group on `v`;
   - `ŝ_γ` acts only on `2^k 0 w`, as `2^k 0 γ(w)`, i.e. as `γ' = (γ, 1)` on `v`, exactly when `u = 2^k`;
   - `n` acts only on `u`, as the odometer on `k` binary digits (`3` = 1, `2` = 0; carries run through 3s, and
     the overflow at the end of `u` is dropped).
2. **Decomposition.** `G` embeds in `Γ' × H'`, where
   - `Γ' = ⟨Γ, (Γ,1)⟩ ≤ Γ ≀ C_2` is generated by a bounded automaton (Grigorchuk states and their rigid copies
     `(x,1)`), so it is contracting and has a lift-presented action;
   - `H'` is the restriction of `⟨n, ŝ_a, …, ŝ_d⟩` to the points with `k >= 1`. It lies in
     `∏_{k>=1} Γ ≀ Z/2^k`, generated by the translation `n = (+1 mod 2^k)_k` and the lamps
     `ŝ_γ = (γ at position 0)_k`.

   By `lift-presentable-groups-closed-under-products-and-wreaths`, `G` is LP-embeddable, hence Boone–Higman,
   as soon as `H'` is.
3. **Abelian calibration.** With abelian lamps `A` in place of `Γ`, the same group is `A ≀ Z`: reduction of
   positions mod `2^k` is a homomorphism for abelian lamps, and it is injective in the limit. For `A = C_2` this
   is the lamplighter, which has a lift-presented action (`rover-nekrashevych-group-of-lamplighter-automaton-is-fp`).
   With Grigorchuk lamps, reduction mod `2^k` is not a homomorphism. For odd `j`, the lamps at `0` and `j`
   commute in every layer `k >= 1`, but they do not commute in `Γ'`. So `H'` is a genuinely 2-adic
   lamplighter: lamps at 2-adically close positions fail to commute.
4. **Why ray compression cannot work here.** The lamps fix the spine point `2^ω`, and their germs there form a
   copy of `Γ`, whose relators are the cycling relators of `polynomial-automata-reduce-to-cycling-relators`.
   The mover's orbit of `2^ω` is infinite: it is all integer points of the odometer, `3^j`-patterned rays. A
   compression that keeps the mover must remember `u` exactly, since the lamp acts only when `u` is all zeros
   and the odometer needs every digit. So no compression along finitely many rays, of the kind in
   `commonly-fixed-letters-compress-to-faithful-actions`, removes this obstruction.
5. **Open.** Is `H'`, the 2-adic Grigorchuk lamplighter, LP-embeddable, or at least Boone–Higman? A positive
   answer settles this test case, and a general method for 2-adic lamplighters over contracting lamp groups
   would cover every linear-activity instance of the shape "spine plus recurrent mover".

## Proof

**1** is read off the recursions. `a,b,c,d` fix `2, 3` with trivial sections, so they act only on the maximal
`{0,1}`-prefix. `ŝ_γ` keeps itself on `2`, dies (section `1`) on `3` and on `1`, and on `0` passes to `γ`. `n`
keeps itself on `3` with output `2`, passes to `1` on `2` with output `3`, and dies on `0, 1`. **2:** the layers
`k = 0` and `k >= 1` partition the dense set of points with a finite `{2,3}`-prefix. On `k = 0` the group acts
through `⟨Γ, (Γ,1)⟩ = Γ'`, with `n` trivial. On `k >= 1` the Grigorchuk states act trivially and the rest acts
as described. So `g ↦ (g|_{k=0}, g|_{k>=1})` is injective. A bounded automaton generates a contracting group
(Bondarenko–Nekrashevych), so `Γ'` has finitely presented `V_2` (FP2). **3:** for abelian `A` the map
`A ≀ Z -> ∏_k A ≀ Z/2^k` is a homomorphism, and an element in its kernel has lamp sums zero in every residue
class mod `2^k`; for large `k` the classes separate the finite support, so the element is trivial. For
`Γ`, lamps at `0` and odd `j` lie in different classes mod `2^k` for all `k >= 1`, hence commute there, while
on `k = 0` both act as `γ', δ'`. **4:** `ŝ_γ|_{2^m} = ŝ_γ` and `ŝ_γ` acts as `γ'` on `2^m 0 v` for every `m`, so its
germ at `2^ω` determines `γ`, and `γ ↦` germ is an injective homomorphism. `n^j(2^ω)` is the odometer image of
`0` under `+j`. ∎
