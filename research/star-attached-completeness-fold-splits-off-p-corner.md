**ESTABLISHED STAR-ATTACHED COMPLETENESS FENCE.**  In
`R=L_(F_2)(1,2)` put

```text
p=s_0t_0,                  q=s_1t_1,       p+q=1,
C_0=x_42(p),               C_1=x_42(q),    J=x_42(1),
D_0=x_24(p),               D_1=x_24(q),    L=x_24(1),
W_i=C_iD_iC_i,             W=JLJ.                         (SCF1)
```

Then

```text
C_0C_1=J,                 D_0D_1=L,
W_0W_1=W.                                                  (SCF2)
```

The last equality is the genuine opposite-root completeness reader: it uses
`p+q=1`, not only the two inverse rows.  Its `q` occurrence is literally the
first marked-star center `C_1=x_42(q)`, and `D_1` is its actual opposite
root.  Thus `(SCF1)` attaches the first object-erasing nonlinear word to the
positive marked-star packet without an occurrence substitution.

Nevertheless the attachment does not force the full-Hecke source.  Let
`G_q` be the literal projective `SL_9(F_2)` head containing the mark, the
three anchored cells, `A_1,B_1,B_2,B_3`, both adjacent prefix Whiteheads,
their braid, and `C_1,D_1`.  The two complementary roots `C_0,D_0` generate
a finite `SL_2(F_2)` factor `G_p` in the canonical elementary image, and

```text
[G_p,G_q]=1       in the elementary image.                 (SCF3)
```

The elementary image of the generated packet is therefore

```text
G_q times G_p.                                             (SCF4)
```

In the tensor product of the natural `511`-point permutation
representation of `G_q` with the left regular representation of
`G_p~=S_3`, every word in `(SCF1)--(SCF3)` and the complete marked
prefix/Whitehead packet is exact, both complementary roots remain
nontrivial, and

```text
rank(P_z)=6*128,              rank(E)=6*32,
rank(F)=0.                                                 (SCF5)
```

Here `E` is the three-root marked-star projection and `F` is the literal
full-Hecke quarter source.  Consequently the first genuinely
label-inconsistent completeness row, even with its reverse-product
opposite-root payload and with one child identified with the actual positive
star center, has zero incidence with `F` in an exact finite profile.

This does not refute a larger object-erasing source bridge.  It proves the
sharp local requirement: a successful word must make the `p` branch act
nontrivially on a named native arm or on the signed Hecke source, rather than
attach it at the same q-labelled endpoints where coefficient orthogonality
makes it a commuting direct factor.  In particular one needs at least one
additional incidence whose coefficient product crosses the `p/q` object
split and is nonzero; completeness plus the opposite root alone does not
provide it.

No Property `(T)`, canonical trace substitution, literature theorem, or
computation is used.
