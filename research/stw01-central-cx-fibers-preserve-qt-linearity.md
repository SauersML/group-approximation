Let `A` be a unital C\*-algebra, `X` a compact Hausdorff space and
`theta: C(X) -> Z(A)` a unital star homomorphism.  For `x in X`, let `J_x` be the
closed ideal generated by `theta(C_0(X \ {x}))` and `A_x = A/J_x`.  Then every
bounded 2-quasitrace on `A` is a trace iff, for every `x`, every bounded
2-quasitrace on `A_x` is a trace.

More precisely, an extreme normalized quasitrace `tau` on `A` is multiplicative on
the center.  Hence `tau o theta = ev_p` for exactly one `p`, and `tau` factors
through `A_p`.  The extreme points of `QT(A)` are exactly the pullbacks of the
extreme points of the fibers' `QT(A_x)`.

The mechanism is a central cut.  For `0 <= z <= 1` central, `x -> tau(zx)` is again
a bounded 2-quasitrace, because `z^(1/2)x` has `w*w = z x*x` and `ww* = z xx*`.
Moreover `tau = tau(z .) + tau((1-z) .)`.  Extremality forces `tau(zx) = tau(z)
tau(x)`.  The square-null ideal then contains `J_p`, and Haagerup's descent
(Proposition 3.2 of the 2014 paper) finishes the extreme case.  Krein--Milman on the
compact convex set `QT(A)` finishes the general case.

Consequences:

- `QL(C(X,B)) <=> QL(B)`.
- Fiberwise exact unital C(X)-algebras, which need not be exact, have only tracial
  bounded quasitraces.
- An `l^infinity` product is linear iff its factors and all its norm ultraproducts
  are.
- `C([0,1], prod_n M_n)` has quasitrace linearity.
