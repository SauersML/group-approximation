Let `A` be a unital C-star algebra, let `z_x in A` be finitely many
unitaries, and let `R_B` be the fixed list of BCS residual polynomials from
`non-ce-bcs-has-robust-approximate-energy-gap`.  Put

```text
H_B=sum_(R in R_B) R(z)^*R(z) in A_+.                 (CIT1)
```

Let `u_1,...,u_m` be unitaries in `A` and define the aggregate clause
energy

```text
D=sum_i (u_i-1)^*(u_i-1) in A_+.                      (CIT2)
```

Assume:

1. `D` commutes with every `z_x`; and
2. every state `phi` on `A` whose restriction to `C*(z_x)` is tracial and
   which satisfies `phi(D)=0` also satisfies `phi(H_B)=0`.

Then there is `eta>0` such that every finite-dimensional representation
`pi:A->M_d(C)` and every unit vector `xi in C^d` satisfy

```text
<xi,pi(D)xi> >= eta.                                  (CIT3)
```

In particular, these two assumptions prove the whole-face finite-dimensional
soundness required in Property 2 of `central-word-state-face-compiler`.  No
dimension-independent linear inequality between `H_B` and `D` is needed.

## Proof

Fix `(pi,xi)` and let `K` be the compact closure in `U(d)` of the group
generated by the matrices `pi(z_x)`.  Haar-average the vector density:

```text
rho=int_(k in K) k |xi><xi| k^* dk.                   (CIT4)
```

Then `rho>=0`, `Tr(rho)=1`, and `rho` commutes with every `pi(z_x)`.
Because `pi(D)` commutes with `K`, Haar invariance and cyclicity give the
exact, dimension-free identity

```text
Tr(rho pi(D))=<xi,pi(D)xi>.                           (CIT5)
```

The weighted decoder lemma in `central-word-state-face-compiler`, applied to
`rho`, gives

```text
Tr(rho pi(H_B)) >= beta_B^rob.                        (CIT6)
```

Indeed the spectral projections of `rho` reduce all decoded observables, so
the normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt BCS gap applies on each nonzero spectral
corner and their trace weights sum to one.

Suppose `(CIT3)` were false.  Choose finite-dimensional `(pi_n,xi_n)` with
`<xi_n,pi_n(D)xi_n> -> 0`, form their twirled densities `rho_n`, and let

```text
phi_n(a)=Tr(rho_n pi_n(a)).                            (CIT7)
```

The state space of `A` is weak-star compact, so a subnet converges to a state
`phi`.  Equation `(CIT5)` gives `phi(D)=0`.  Each `phi_n` is tracial on
`C*(z_x)`: for words `a,b` in the `z_x`, commutation of `rho_n` with that
algebra gives `phi_n(ab)=phi_n(ba)`, and this identity passes to the limit.
Assumption 2 therefore gives `phi(H_B)=0`.  On the other hand `(CIT6)` and
weak-star continuity give

```text
phi(H_B)>=beta_B^rob>0,
```

a contradiction.  Thus the positive uniform constant `eta` exists.

## Finite syntactic certificate for Assumption 1

Individual clause words need not be central.  It is enough that conjugation
by every decoded generator permutes the finite multiset

```text
{u_1,u_1^*,...,u_m,u_m^*}.                            (CIT8)
```

Indeed `(u-1)^*(u-1)=2-u-u^*`, so `(CIT8)` makes the sum `(CIT2)` invariant
under each conjugation and hence makes `D` commute with every `z_x`.  Thus a
finite normal clause orbit is a strictly weaker and more flexible target than
centralizing every clause word separately.

The remaining algebraic task is correspondingly sharp: construct a finite
phase-safe clause orbit for which zero aggregate energy, on states tracial on
the decoded BCS algebra, implies zero BCS energy.  Once that exact implication
is proved, whole-face quantitative soundness follows automatically from the
argument above; a PAZ lift, common Gram block, explicit rate, or commuting
density supplied by the compiler is unnecessary.

## Why unrestricted twirling is not enough

If `D` does not commute with the decoded algebra, `(CIT5)` can fail by an
order-one amount.  For example, let the decoded observables be the two Pauli
matrices `X,Z` on `C^2`, let `xi` be the `+1` eigenvector of `Z`, and take
`D=(1-Z)/2`.  The vector energy is zero.  Since `X,Z` generate `M_2(C)`, the
only density commuting with both is `I/2`, on which the energy is `1/2`.
Consequently standard-form doubling, conditional expectation, or Haar
twirling cannot by itself prove Property 2.  Aggregate conjugation invariance
is precisely the hypothesis that prevents this loss.
