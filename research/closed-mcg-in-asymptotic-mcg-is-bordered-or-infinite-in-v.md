**ESTABLISHED.** It rests on the inclusion input (I) of the derivation, which
is not re-proved.

**Setting.**
- `Σ` is a closed orientable surface of genus `g ≥ 3`, and `L ≤ Mod(Σ)` has
  finite index.
- `h ≥ 0`, and `Σ_h` is the closed genus-`h` surface with a Cantor set removed.
- `G` is `B_h` or `H_h` of `aramayona-funar-asymptotic-mcgs-are-fp-extensions-of-v`.
- `π: G → V` is the quotient of AF Proposition 4.2 or 4.6, using `V₂[Z₂] ≅ V`.
- `ρ: L → G` is an injective homomorphism, and `K = ker(π∘ρ)`.

(a) **Finite image in `V`.** If `π∘ρ(L)` is finite, then `K` has finite index
    in `Mod(Σ)` and embeds in `Mod(S_{h,n})` for some `n ≥ 1`. It therefore
    embeds in the mapping class group of the genus-`h` surface with `2n`
    punctures, so `closed-mcgs-virtually-embed-in-punctured-mcgs` holds for this
    `g`.

(b) **Infinite image in `V`.** Otherwise `π∘ρ(L)` is an infinite subgroup of
    `V`, and `K` is a nontrivial normal subgroup of `L` of infinite index. Every
    finitely generated subgroup of `K` embeds in some `Mod(S_{h,n})`, `n ≥ 1`.

(c) **Normal subgroups and quotients.** Write `PMod_c = PMod_c(Σ_h)`.
    - Every normal subgroup `N ⊴ G` satisfies `N ≤ PMod_c` or `N·PMod_c = G`.
    - `G` is not simple, since `PMod_c` is a nontrivial proper normal subgroup.
    - No quotient `G/N` with `N ≥ PMod_c` contains a copy of `L`.
    - If a simple quotient `G/N` contains a copy of `L`, then `N·PMod_c = G`:
      the image of `PMod_c` is all of `G/N`.
    - For `h ≥ 3`, `G` is perfect, so its commutator subgroup is `G` again.

(d) **Forgetful sections.** Suppose `h = g`, and let `F: Mod(Σ_g) → Mod(Σ)`
    fill in the Cantor set. If `F∘ρ = id_L`, then `ρ(L)` has no finite orbit on
    the ends of `Σ_g`, and `π∘ρ(L)` is infinite, so `ρ` is in case (b). Such a
    `ρ` is automatically injective.

**Why it matters.** The Aramayona--Funar groups are the natural finitely
presented hosts that contain bordered mapping class groups and act on a Cantor
set of ends through `V`. By this claim they give nothing beyond the live premise
`closed-mcgs-virtually-embed-in-punctured-mcgs`, unless `L` has infinite image
in `V` and a nontrivial kernel that lands in the compactly supported mapping
class group. Every forgetful section is of that kind. Case (b) is where a new
construction would have to live, and nothing here excludes it.

Case (c) also covers two host-derived candidates:
- **Commutator subgroups** are the host again, by perfection.
- **Simple quotients** containing `L` must be generated by the image of the
  compactly supported mapping class group.

**Scope.**
- **Input (I).** The derivation needs one fact about a compact connected
  genus-`h` subsurface `S ⊂ Σ_h` each of whose complementary components is
  noncompact with one boundary circle. Extending by the identity must give an
  injective homomorphism `Mod(S) → PMod_c(Σ_h)`. This is how we read AF
  Lemma 2.1, whose statement does not name the embedding. It is the
  infinite-type analogue of Farb--Margalit Theorem 3.18, and it is not re-proved
  here.
- **Hosts.** Only `B_h` and `H_h` are covered.
  - ABFPW's `B_{d,r}(O, Y)` has a sequence (1.3) of the same shape, but input
    (I) for suited submanifolds and non-embedding in `V_{d,r}` are not recorded.
  - For Funar--Kapoudjian's `B_∞`, the complementary components have infinite
    genus.
- **Case (b) is open.** No injective `ρ` with infinite image in `V` is known or
  excluded here.
- **Identification.** Part (d) identifies `Mod(Σ_g)` with `Mod(Σ, C)`, as scoped
  in `cantor-punctured-birman-sections-have-no-finite-end-orbit`.

The derivation is `closed-mcg-asymptotic-host-dichotomy-proof`.
