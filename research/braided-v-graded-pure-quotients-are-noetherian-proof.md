Full details: artifact §§3–4. Put `L_c(n) = gamma_c(PB_n)/gamma_{c+1}(PB_n)`, a finitely
generated abelian group.

1. **Functor.** For a surjection `f: [m] -> [n]`, cable strand `i` into `|f^{-1}(i)|`
   parallel clones and move them to the positions `f^{-1}(i)` by a permutation braid.
   This gives a homomorphism `PB_n -> PB_m`. Its effect on `gr(PB_n)` is determined in
   degree 1 by `A_{ij} ↦ sum of A_{ab}` over `a` in `f^{-1}(i)` and `b` in `f^{-1}(j)`.
   This matches Zaremsky's cloning formula on `H_1(PB_n)` (arXiv:1403.8132, Ex. 4.5).
   Every such map is a Lie-ring homomorphism and `gr(PB_n)` is generated in degree 1,
   so `L_c` is a well-defined functor `FS^op -> Ab`.
2. **Finite generation.** A `c`-fold bracket of classes `A_{ab}` involves at most `2c`
   strands `s_1 < … < s_r`. It is the image, under the surjection `[m] -> [r+1]` with
   `s_i ↦ i` and all other strands `↦ r+1`, of the same bracket in `L_c(r+1)`; the
   fibres over `1..r` are singletons. So `L_c` is generated by `L_c([j])`,
   `j <= 2c+1`, and it is noetherian by `fs-op-representations-are-noetherian` with
   `k = Z`.
3. **Transfer.** By `pure-braided-thompson-group-is-residually-nilpotent`, `gr_c` is the
   directed union of injective copies `L_c(PB_T)`. For a `V`-invariant `N <= gr_c`, let
   `N(n)` be the image in `L_c(n)` of `N ∩ L_c(PB_T)`, for any tree `T` with `n` leaves.
   - `N(n)` does not depend on `T`: `(T_2, 1, T_1)(T_1, q, T_1)(T_1, 1, T_2) = (T_2, q, T_2)`.
   - `N(n)` is invariant under bijections, via `(T, b, T)`.
   - `N(n)` is carried into `N(m)` by every `L_c(f)`. Expanding the leaves of `T` along
     the fibres of `f` changes nothing: `(T, q, T) = (T', κ(q), T')` is an equality of
     elements of `P_br`, where `κ` clones along consecutive fibres. Only moving the
     clones into position is a conjugation, by `(T', b, T')` with `b` a permutation braid,
     and a `V`-invariant `N` is preserved by it.
   So `n ↦ N(n)` is a subrepresentation. An element of `N' \ N` lies in some
   `L_c(PB_T)`, so `N ⊊ N'` forces `N(n) ⊊ N'(n)` for some `n`.
4. **Conclusion.** An ascending chain of `V`-invariant subgroups gives an ascending
   chain of subrepresentations of the noetherian `L_c`, which stabilizes.
