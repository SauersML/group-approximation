## Why sufficient

Write `G_n = EL_n(J)`, `N_n = SL_fin(Z)`, `T_n = G_n/N_n`, and let
`pi_m : G_n ->> EL_n(A_m)` be induced by `J ->> A_m` (onto, since
elementary generators lift).

**Kernel of `pi_m`.**  An element of `G_n` maps to the identity iff it
is congruent to `I` modulo `mI`, i.e. iff it is a finitary integral
automorphism with `g = I mod m`.  Its finitary determinant is `+-1` and
is `= 1 mod m`.  For `m > 2` this forces determinant `1`, so
`ker(pi_m) <= N_n` and `EL_n(A_m)/pi_m(N_n) = G_n/N_n = T_n`.  For
`m = 2`, `eta = diag(1 - 2e, 1) = I mod 2` lies in `ker(pi_2)`, so
`N_n ker(pi_2) = GL_fin(Z)` is the whole symbol kernel of the second
prerequisite and `EL_n(A_2)/pi_2(N_n) = EL_n(S)`.  This is `(ER2)`.

**Image of `N_n`.**  `pi_m(N_n) = E_n(A_m, I_m)`, because relative roots
lift from `I_m = I/mI` to `I` and the ambient map is onto.  It equals
`SL_fin(Z/m)`: reduction `SL_fin(Z) -> SL_fin(Z/m)` is onto, since
determinant-one matrices over `Z/m` are generated by elementary ones
(Chinese remainder theorem makes a pivot of a unimodular column a unit,
a diagonal pair `diag(u, u^-1)` is elementary, then clear and induct)
and elementary entries lift to integers.  The symbol kernel of
`EL_n(A_m)` is `pi_m(GL_fin(Z))`, the finitary matrices over `Z/m` of
determinant `+-1`, the sign supplied by `eta`.  This is `(ER3)`.

**Finite residual.**  Every finite quotient of `EL_n(A_m)` kills
`pi_m(N_n)`, a quotient of `SL_fin(Z)`, which has no nontrivial finite
quotient (third prerequisite, Step 5 of its route); and the quotient by
`pi_m(N_n)` is `EL_n(S)` or `T_n`, residually finite by the third
prerequisite.  So `Res_fin(EL_n(A_m)) = E_n(A_m, I_m)` for every
`n >= 2`.

**MF radical, `n >= 4`.**  In `A_m` the defect `e` is nonzero and
`me = 0`, so the first prerequisite gives
`E_n(A_m, I_m) <= Rad_MF(EL_n(A_m))`.  The quotient is residually
finite, hence MF (regular representations of separating finite
quotients), so every element outside `E_n(A_m, I_m)` survives some MF
homomorphism.  This is `(ER1)`.

**The caveats** on the claim are immediate: the first is the definition
of the radical plus the diagonal block-sum argument for countable
residually-MF groups; the second is the splitting of elementary and
relative elementary groups over a product ring together with
`binary-leavitt-all-ranks-full-mf-radical` for the Leavitt factor.
