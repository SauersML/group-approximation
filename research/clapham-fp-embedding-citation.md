## What is load-bearing

Two adjectives on the envelope: **finitely presented** and **solvable word
problem**.  Simplicity is not claimed and is not available -- that is the
whole difficulty of the conjecture this feeds.  A route that only needed
finite presentability could use Higman's embedding theorem instead; the
reason Clapham is cited rather than Higman is that every route here has to
carry decidability forward, because
`compilers-cannot-drop-the-decidability-hypothesis` shows the target
constructions are impossible without it.
