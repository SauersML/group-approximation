**Notation.** `F = F_2`, `J = F<S,T | TS = 1>`, `Q = 1 - ST`, `A = F[z, z^(-1)]`. For `m >= 3`, `E_m = EL_m(J)`,
`π : E_m -> SL_m(A)` is the symbol, and `L_m = ker π = GL_fin(F[N]^m)`, an infinite simple group.
`rho : M_n(A) -> M_(2n)(J)` is the ray gluing of `laurent-finitary-el-is-kazhdan-lef-inside-doubled-jacobson`
(`z -> u = [[S, Q], [0, T]]`, `z^(-1) -> [[T, 0], [Q, S]]`), and `Gamma^tw = π rho(SL_n(A))`.

**The two decompositions.** In each row, `A_0` and `B_0` are the complements, `C_0 = A_0 ∩ B_0`, and the amalgam is
`P = (L A_0) *_(L C_0) (L B_0)`, with complement amalgam `U = A_0 *_(C_0) B_0`.

| | `E` | `A_0` | `B_0` | `C_0` | `L A_0` | `L B_0` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (R3) | `E_3` | `EL_3(F[S])` | `EL_3(F[T])` | `SL_3(F)` | `H_+` | `H_-` |
| (R2n), `n >= 3` | `E_(2n)` | `EL_(2n)(F[S])` | `rho(EL_n(A))` | `rho(SL_n(F))` | `H_+` | `rho(H_bi(n))` |

Here `H_± = π^(-1) SL_m(F[z^(±1)])`, and `rho(H_bi(n)) = π^(-1)(Gamma^tw)`.

**Theorem.** For (R3) and for (R2n), with `Φ : P -> E`, `N = ker Φ`, `λ = π Φ|_U`, `K = ker λ` and `ℓ = Φ|_K`:
1. **Hypotheses of the lemma.** `A_0 ∩ L = B_0 ∩ L = 1` and `π(A_0) ∩ π(B_0) = π(C_0)`. Also `<A_0, B_0> = E`.
2. **Soficity.** `P` is sofic, and `C = L C_0` is locally finite.
3. **Kernel.** `N` is free, `N ∩ L = 1`, `[N, L] = 1`, and `N = {ℓ(k)^(-1) k : k in K}`. `K` is free.
4. **Head relators.** The words
   ```text
   (R3)   ρ_+ = [x_12(S), x_23(T)] · x_13(1)                          in U,
   (R2n)  k_Q = [x_(1+,2+)(S), rho(x_23(z^(-1)))] · x_(1+,3+)(1)       in U,
   ```
   lie in `K`, with `ℓ(ρ_+) = x_13(Q)` and `ℓ(k_Q) = x_(1+,3+)(Q)`, the head. Here
   `rho(x_23(z^(-1))) = x_(2+,3+)(T) x_(2-,3+)(Q) x_(2-,3-)(S)`, with commuting factors.
5. **Presentation of the kernel.** Let `r = ℓ(h)^(-1) h`, where `h` is the head relator. Then `r in N` and
   `N = ker ℓ · <<r>>_P`. `E ≅ U / ker ℓ`, and `P / ker ℓ ≅ E ×_Λ E`, where `Λ = π(E)`.
6. **Reduction.** `E` is sofic if and only if `P / ker ℓ` is. So soficity of `E_3` and of `E_(2n)` is the question
   whether a sofic amalgam of two LEF groups over a locally finite group stays sofic modulo a free normal subgroup
   `ker ℓ` of the complement amalgam `U`. `ker ℓ` centralizes `L`, and `U` is an amalgam of two linear groups over
   `F(z)` over a finite group.

  `SL_(2n)(F[z])`, and the only candidate `n x n` matrix for it in `Gamma^tw` is `diag(z, 1, ..., 1)`, with
  determinant `z`. Point 5 gives normal generators of `N` from `ker ℓ`
  (words in `A_0 ∪ B_0`) and one relator `r` (a word in `A_0 ∪ B_0 ∪ L`). Whatever role the index of `u ⊕ 1` plays
  is therefore recorded in `ker ℓ`, that is, in which words of the linear amalgam `U` become trivial in `E`. This last
  sentence is an interpretation, not part of the theorem.
