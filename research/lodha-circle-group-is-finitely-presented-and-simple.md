## Statement

Let `a(t) = t + 1`, `l(t) = −1/t`, and let `b` and `s` be the piecewise projective
homeomorphisms of `S^1 = R ∪ {∞}` given in the citation route. Let
`S = ⟨a, b, l, s⟩ ≤ Homeo(S^1)`.

1. `S` is a finitely presented, infinite, simple group.
2. `S` is generated by `T` and `s`, where `T = ⟨F, l⟩` is Thompson's group `T` in its
   piecewise `PSL_2(Z)` model and `F = ⟨a, b⟩`.
3. **Cantor model.** Let `Φ: 2^N → R ∪ {∞}` be Lodha's continued fraction map, and
   `x`, `x_1`, `p_0`, `y_10`, `y_110` his maps of `2^N`. Then for all `ξ ∈ 2^N`:
   `Φ(ξ)·a = Φ(ξ·x)`, `Φ(ξ)·b = Φ(ξ·x_1)`, `Φ(ξ)·s = Φ(ξ·y_10 y_110^{-1})` and
   `Φ(ξ)·l = Φ(ξ·p_0)`.

Actions are on the right. The definitions of `Φ`, `x`, `y`, `x_u`, `y_u` and `p_0` are
recorded in §0 of `research/artifacts/lodha-circle-group-type-a-action-2026-09-16.md`.

**Consumer.** `lodha-circle-stabilizer-fg-proof` uses parts 2 and 3 to build the circle
action from the Cantor model, and part 1 for finite presentation.
