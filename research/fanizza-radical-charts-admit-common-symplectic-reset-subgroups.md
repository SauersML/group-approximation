Let `A_f(x)` be the corrected symmetric alternating pencil of
`every-predicate-has-s3-equivariant-raw-radical-drop`, of size `4N`, and let
`ell_i`, for `i=1,2,3`, be the three radical-chart directions.  The
crossed-product chart has odd alternating commutator form

```text
B_i(x)=[[A_f(x),ell_i],[ell_i^T,0]].                         (CHR1)
```

For every selector assignment `x`, `B_i(x)` has rank `4N` and a
one-dimensional radical.  For each of the fixed predicates in the final
Fanizza BCS menu there is a functional `phi_i`, independent of `x`, such
that

```text
phi_i(r_i(x))=1                                             (CHR2)
```

for the nonzero radical vector `r_i(x)` of `(CHR1)`.  Therefore the fixed
hyperplane `W_i=ker(phi_i)` is complementary to the radical in every sector.
Since `(CHR1)` is alternating, its restriction to `W_i` is nondegenerate.

Let `L_i` be the finite phase-root packet subgroup generated by a basis of
`W_i` and the packet center.  In every selector sector its algebra is the
single full matrix algebra

```text
M_(D_0)(C),             D_0=2^(2N).                         (CHR3)
```

Both `M_(D_0)` simples of the ambient crossed-product chart restrict to the
unique simple `L_i` type.  Thus restriction to `L_i` turns the two orthogonal
chart types into two copies of one fixed-scale packet type; no nonlinear
group-algebra central coefficient is needed.

The artifact performs exact Gaussian elimination over `F_2`.  It exhausts
all Boolean predicates of arity at most three and directly verifies `(CHR1)`--
`(CHR3)` for the fixed Fanizza predicates `C13/C14`, `C17`, the three-bit C18
child guard, and the four-bit C18 residual.  It prints the supports of the
three fixed hyperplane witnesses, so the finite certificates are reproducible.

This closes the finite Morita-merge part of the return problem.  It does not
yet renew the selector tuple: the center of `L_i` still remembers the old
context.
