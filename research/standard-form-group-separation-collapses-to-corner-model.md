**ESTABLISHED** by `standard-form-group-separation-collapses-proof`. This is an
obstruction to a class of relaxations. It proves no new group is
non-hyperlinear.

Let `M` be a finite von Neumann algebra acting on `H`, let `G` be a
synchronous game with rule `V`, and let `A(G)` be its synchronous game
algebra: generated by projection-valued measures `{e_(x,a)}_a` with
`e_(x,a) e_(y,b) = 0` whenever `V(a,b|x,y)=0`. Suppose we have:
- projection-valued measures `P_(x,a)` in `M`;
- projection-valued measures `Q_(y,b)` in `M'`;
- a unit vector `xi in H`, such that `p(a,b|x,y) = <P_(x,a)Q_(y,b)xi,xi>` is synchronous and wins `G` with probability one.

Let `A_0` be the star-algebra generated by the `P_(x,a)`, let `e` be the
projection onto the closure of `A_0 xi`, and let `z` be the central support
of `e` in `A_0''`. Then:

```text
(SC1)  P_(x,a) xi = Q_(x,a) xi                   for all x, a;
(SC2)  omega_xi is a trace on A_0;
(SC3)  e_(x,a) -> P_(x,a) z  is a unital *-homomorphism  A(G) -> z M z;
(SC4)  conversely a unital *-homomorphism A(G) -> z M z, z != 0, with M in
       standard form, realizes a perfect synchronous correlation with
       xi = z Omega/||z Omega|| and Bob's measurements J P_(x,a) J.
```

**Consequence for the MIP* lane.** The game-side input of the assignment
already exists: `lin-pzk-tableau-rational-qc-not-qa-correlation` gives an
explicit rational perfect synchronous correlation `p_tab in C_qc \ C_qa`.
By `ce-standard-form-strategies-are-quantum-approximable`, realizing `p_tab`
in the standard form of `L(Lambda)` for a finitely presented `Lambda` would
prove `Lambda` non-hyperlinear. By `(SC1)`-`(SC4)` that realization problem is
*equal* to a corner model `A(B_tab) -> z L(Lambda) z`. This is the
`non-ru-bcs-is-satisfiable-in-a-group-factor` problem for the tableau game
algebra.

So the relaxations "use a non-trace vector", "let Bob hold arbitrary
commutant projections", and "drop the requirement that Alice's projections
be group elements" add no leverage for perfect synchronous witnesses. Every
such route dies at step `(SC1)`, where synchrony forces the vector to
intertwine Alice and Bob and makes the support corner tracial.

The non-synchronous case is not covered, and survives as
`explicit-group-standard-form-correlation-outside-qa`.
