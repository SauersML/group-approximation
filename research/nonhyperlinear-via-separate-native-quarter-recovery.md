In a hypothetical canonical microstate sequence, the atlas identities produce
the two fixed trace-quarter carriers `F,G` with reflection-commutator energy
`1/2-o(1)`.  The recovery theorem places `F` and `G`, up to `o(1)` in
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm, in the two commuting returned descendant
algebras.  The D8 carrier theorem instead forces the sum of those two
distances to be at least `1/8-o(1)`, a contradiction.

The forty-one-word certificate is the finite implementation boundary: once
the matrix-only return chooses the two descendant algebras, it is enough to
transport the fixed 37-word and four-word supports.  No whole coefficient
algebra, common carrier, or ordinary-word promotion is assumed.
