The relative presentation

```text
J = <t,s,c |
       t^3=s^2=(s t)^2=c^2=(c t)^3=1,
       t c s c t^-1 c s t c=1>
```

is `S4`.  Under this identification `<t,s>` is the standard `S3` complement,
`c` is a nonidentity element of the normal Klein four, the normal closure of
`c` is exactly that Klein four, and `J/<<c>> ~= S3`.

Consequently every matrix solution of the local collision relations is a
representation of this one finite marked group.  The solution `c=1` is the
quotient representation through `S4/V4 ~= S3`; a nontrivial collision seam is
precisely one on which the normal `V4` acts nontrivially.
