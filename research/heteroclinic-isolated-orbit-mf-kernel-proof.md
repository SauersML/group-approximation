Sections 2--4 supply the construction and proof. The orbit closure is
exactly the isolated joining orbit plus the two minimal components.
Functions vanishing on those components have finite support on the
orbit; their shifts give the full finite-support matrix ideal. The
finite-block argument identifies the entire elementary-group kernel.

The phase indicator gives a one-sided inverse with singleton transition
defect. At rank at least four the torsion-defect theorem kills its root,
whose normal closure is the simple kernel. The endpoint rings are LEF,
so their elementary groups are MF, giving equality of the radical and
the kernel. Every finite image kills the infinite simple kernel, then
kills the endpoint groups, which are generated by their simple rank-three
copies. Thus the ambient finite residual is the whole group.
