**ESTABLISHED (2026-09-12)** through
`char-zero-polynomial-embedding-via-noether-normalization`. The proof is standard
commutative algebra, and no novelty is claimed.

**Statement.** Let `K` be a field of characteristic zero and let
`Γ <= GL_N(K)` be finitely generated. Then there are integers `m >= 1`, `k >= 0`,
`M >= 1` and an injective homomorphism

```text
Γ  ->  GL_M(Z[1/m][s_1, ..., s_k]).
```

**The numbers.**
- `k` is the transcendence degree over `Q` of the field `K_0` generated by the
  entries of the generators and their inverses.
- `M = N d r`, where `d = [K_0 : Q(t_1..t_k)]` for a transcendence basis `t`, and
  `r` is the rank of the finite free module of the proof.
- `m` is one nonzero leading coefficient, from the Noether normalization.

**Example: Laurent coordinates.** Let `s = t + t^-1`. Then

```text
Z[t^(+-1)] = Z[s]·1 ⊕ Z[s]·t,   t^2 = s t - 1,   t^-1 = s - t.
```

- **Spanning.** `t^(n+1) = s t^n - t^(n-1)`, run in both directions, puts every
  `t^n` in `Z[s] + Z[s] t`.
- **Independence.** `t` is a root of `X^2 - sX + 1`, whose discriminant
  `s^2 - 4 = (s-2)(s+2)` is not a square in `Q(s)`. So `t` is not in `Q(s)`, and
  `a + b t = 0` forces `b = 0`, then `a = 0`.
- **The matrices.** Multiplication by `a + b t` on the basis `(1, t)` is the block
  `[[a, -b], [b, a + b s]]`, of determinant `a^2 + a b s + b^2 = N(a + b t)`.
  Replacing each entry by its block is a ring homomorphism
  `M_3(Z[t^(+-1)]) -> M_6(Z[s])`. It sends `SL_3(Z[t^(+-1)])` into `SL_6(Z[s])`,
  because the determinant of the image is the norm of the determinant.

**Rational denominators.** Suppose the generators of `Γ <= GL_n(Q(t))` and their
inverses have entries in `Z[t][1/f]`, with `f` a nonconstant polynomial. The
same proof gives `Γ <= GL_(nr)(Z[1/m][s])`, where `m` is the leading coefficient
of the top monomial of `f`.

**Consequence.** With `polynomial-linear-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`, this gives
the root `char-zero-linear-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`, through route
`char-zero-linear-bh-via-polynomial-s-integer-hosts`.
