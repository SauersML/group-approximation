## Why sufficient

Put `w = 1 - 2p`, a self-adjoint unitary of `Q`.  Let `E'` be the subgroup
of `U(Q)` generated by `pi(E)` and `w`; it is countable, being generated
by a countable set.  Inside `E'`:

- `L' = pi(L)` has property (T) (image of a property-(T) group);
- `pi(s) in E'` compresses `L'`: `pi(s) L' pi(s)^{-1} = pi(sLs^{-1}) <= L'`;
- `w` is an involutive compression witness for `(L', pi(s))`: `w^2 = 1`;
  `w` commutes with `pi(sLs^{-1})` by hypothesis 1; and the `L'`-conjugates
  `pi(gamma) w pi(gamma)^* = 1 - 2 pi(gamma) p pi(gamma)^*` commute
  pairwise by hypothesis 2.

The formalized involutive endpoint
(`InvolutionCollapseEndpoint.actualCoronaMFInvisible_of_involutiveWitness`,
the machine-checked content of `compression-torsion-collapse`) therefore
makes every commutator `[pi(gamma), w]`, `gamma in L`, invisible in
**every** corona representation of the countable group `E'`.

Now instantiate that universal statement at one specific corona
representation: the inclusion `E' -> U(Q)` itself, which is a homomorphism
into the unitary group of the very corona `Q`.  Invisibility at the
inclusion is equality: `[pi(gamma), w] = 1` in `Q`.  Since `w = 1 - 2p`,
this is `[pi(gamma), p] = 0`.

The packet extension: `pi(L)'` is a norm-closed unital subalgebra of `Q`;
if it contains every projection generator of `B`, it contains `B`.
