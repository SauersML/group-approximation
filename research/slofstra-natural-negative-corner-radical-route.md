**REFUTED NATURAL-CORNER ROUTE.** Let `chi_-` be the character obtained by
restricting Slofstra's permutation-valued finite-coordinate construction to
the `J=-1` spectral corner, and put

```text
H_-=S/ker(pi_(chi_-)).                                   (SNR1)
```

The proposed route was to find a nonidentity element of `Rad_sof(H_-)`, using
the regular-character unitary models of this tracial image to obtain a
permutation Hamming floor. `slofstra-natural-negative-corner-image-is-sofic`
proves instead that `H_-` is sofic and its sofic radical is trivial.
