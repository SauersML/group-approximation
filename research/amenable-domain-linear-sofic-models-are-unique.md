Let `K` be any field and let `A` be a finitely generated amenable
`K`-algebra with no zero divisors. Then `A` admits a linear sofic
representation, and all its linear sofic representations lie in one
conjugacy class in the normalized rank metric.

Concretely, after putting two representations on a common dimension
sequence, their values on each fixed element become rank-close after
conjugation by invertible matrices. The conclusion is about the paper's
maximally separating linear-sofic representations; it is not a uniqueness
claim for arbitrary injective maps into a rank ultraproduct with an
unspecified rank function.
