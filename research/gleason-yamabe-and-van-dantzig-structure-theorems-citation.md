**Citation.** Read 2026-09-19 from T. Tao, *254A, Notes 5: The structure of
locally compact groups, and Hilbert's fifth problem* (blog notes, 8 October 2011,
terrytao.wordpress.com). The material appears in book form in T. Tao, *Hilbert's
Fifth Problem and Related Topics*, GSM 153, AMS 2014.

* Theorem 1 (Gleason–Yamabe theorem): "Let G be a locally compact group. Then,
  for any open neighbourhood U of the identity, there exists an open subgroup G'
  of G and a compact normal subgroup K of G' in U such that G'/K is isomorphic to
  a Lie group."
* On connected groups: "if the locally compact group G is connected, then the
  only open subgroup G' of G is the full group G." The notes conclude that "any
  connected locally compact Hausdorff group is the inverse limit of Lie groups".
* Theorem 7 (van Dantzig's theorem): "Every totally disconnected locally compact
  group G contains a compact open subgroup H (which will of course still be
  totally disconnected)."

The original sources are:
- A. Gleason, *Groups without small subgroups*, Ann. of Math. 56 (1952) 193–212;
- H. Yamabe, *A generalization of a theorem of Gleason*, Ann. of Math. 58 (1953)
  351–365;
- D. van Dantzig, *Zur topologischen Algebra III*, Compositio Math. 3 (1936)
  408–426.
