Let `A` be a nonzero, separable, nowhere-scattered C*-algebra of real rank
zero.

* `A` has the Global Glimm Property.
* If `A` has finite nuclear dimension and no nonzero purely infinite simple
  subquotient, then `A` has generator rank one.  Equivalently,
  generators form a dense `G_delta` subset of `A`.
* If `A` is nuclear and purely infinite, then `A` has generator rank one,
  without a finite-dimensionality hypothesis.

In particular, every separable nowhere-scattered C*-algebra of real rank zero
and finite decomposition rank has generator rank one and is singly
generated.

Thus a real-rank-zero nowhere-scattered counterexample, if one exists, must lie
outside the finite-decomposition-rank region.  In the finite-nuclear-dimension
region it must have a nonzero purely infinite simple subquotient and cannot
itself be purely infinite.
