If every unitary table with small normalized-HS relator defect carries
`Theta(n)` independent index defects, the presented group has no
Hilbert--Schmidt models: a hyperlinear model would supply tables of vanishing
defect, and the density of the obstruction is bounded below along any such
sequence and is unchanged by the tensor amplification that would otherwise
dilute it.

The second prerequisite is established and is what makes the route survive
`additive-index-instability`; the first is the construction and carries all
the difficulty.  Stated as a separate route from the other topological ideas
in this graph because its hypothesis is about the *presentation* -- a lower
bound holding for every approximate representation -- rather than about one
model or one candidate group.
