Call a countable group **MF** when it embeds into the unitary group of a norm
matrix corona `Q = (prod_n M_(d_n)) / (oplus_n M_(d_n))`.

**Claim.**  Let `G` be an infinite finitely generated group of finite
exponent `N`. Then `G` is not MF. If moreover `G` is simple, then

```text
Rad_MF(G) = G,
```

i.e. every homomorphism `G -> U(Q)` into every norm matrix corona is trivial.

Bounded exponent is essential to this formulation; finite generation is used
only to fix an `m`. Finite presentation is **not** needed. Property `(T)`,
exactness, Leavitt coefficients, traces and torsion normal-generator
amplification are all absent.

**Permanence.**  Bounded exponent passes to subgroups and to quotients, so
the hypothesis is inherited in both directions (finite generation may be lost
on passing to a subgroup). Extensions change the exponent and are not
covered.

## Attempts

1. **Via the corona Burnside statement.** This is the live route
   `bounded-exponent-non-mf-from-corona-burnside`, requiring
   `operator-norm-restricted-burnside`. Both conclusions follow in two lines
   each; the whole difficulty is in the prerequisite, and from there in
   `matrix-restricted-burnside-gap`.

2. **Directly, from an invariant of the group.** No route is known that
   avoids the corona statement. Every attempt reduces to controlling the
   subgroup of `U(Q)` generated by the image, which is the prerequisite
   again.

3. **Note the hypothesis cannot simply be weakened to torsion.** The
   argument uses a *uniform* spectral gap `delta_N = 2 sin(pi/N)`
   (`finite-order-unitary-has-uniform-opnorm-gap`). For a torsion group of
   unbounded exponent the orders of individual elements grow, `delta_N -> 0`,
   and no separation constant survives. Bounded exponent is not a
   convenience here.
