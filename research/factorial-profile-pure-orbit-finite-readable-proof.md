Put

```text
a=sum_(t in T) p_t.                                    (FPF1)
```

No orthogonality is asserted here. Conjugation by `Q_G` permutes the finite
set `T`, so `a` commutes with `pi(Q_G)`. If `d in D_0` and
`p_t<=E_(r(t))`, then `d` is scalar on `E_(r(t))`, hence

```text
dp_t=p_td.
```

Therefore `a` also commutes with `D_0`, so `(FPO1)` gives `a in Z(N)`.
Factoriality implies

```text
a=cq_-                                                   (FPF2)
```

for a scalar `c`. Since `p_0!=0`, faithfulness of the finite trace gives

```text
c=tau_-(a)=|T|tau_-(p_0)>0.                             (FPF3)
```

Let `(E_l)_l` be the nonzero joint readable atoms. Multiplying `(FPF2)` by
`E_l` and using profile purity gives

```text
cE_l=sum_(t:r(t)=l) p_t.                                (FPF4)
```

In particular every readable atom occurs among the profiles. Conjugating
`(FPF4)` by arbitrary `g in Q_G` yields

```text
c pi(g)E_lpi(g)^*
 =sum_(t:r(t)=l) p_(gt).                                (FPF5)
```

Thus every translated readable atom belongs to the fixed finite-dimensional
vector space

```text
V=span_C{p_t:t in T}.                                   (FPF6)
```

Let `S` be the family of all translated readable atoms. Choose finitely many
members `S_0 subseteq S` forming a vector-space basis of `span(S)`; this is
possible by `(FPF6)`. The ZPC diagonal calculation makes `D_infinity`
abelian, so the projections in `S_0` commute. The unital star algebra
generated by finitely many commuting projections is finite dimensional. It
contains every member of `S`, hence contains `(FPO2)`; conversely it lies
in `D_infinity`. Therefore `D_infinity` is finite dimensional.

Conversely, if `D_infinity` is finite dimensional, `Q_G` permutes its
finitely many atoms and every atom lies below a unique `D_0` atom. Thus any
atom is a finite profile-pure orbit, proving the stated equivalence in a
factor.

Finally take a central decomposition of an arbitrary perfect tracial model
satisfying the profile-pure orbit hypothesis. The exact orbit equalities,
profile containments, game relations, and `J=-1` relation hold almost
everywhere. Since the original trace of `p_0` is positive, there is a
factorial component on which `p_0` is nonzero. The preceding argument makes
its readable translate algebra finite dimensional, and this component
remains a perfect tracial ZPC representation. A central decomposition of a
stabilizer character need not automatically remain inside the image of the
IRS-character map, so no claim of preserved IRS provenance is made without
an additional argument; this is precisely the residual distinction recorded
in the claim.
