Let `Gamma` be a torsion-free amenable group and let `K` be a field such
that the group algebra `K[Gamma]` has no zero divisors. Then

```text
K[Gamma] is weakly stable in the normalized rank metric
    if and only if
Gamma is residually finite.
```

Here weak stability is Bachner's algebraic notion: every linear-sofic
asymptotic representation lifts, in the rank ultraproduct, to genuine
finite-dimensional algebra representations. This statement is not
permutation stability of `Gamma` and not operator-norm matricial stability.
