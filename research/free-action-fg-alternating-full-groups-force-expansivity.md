- For minimal actions of a discrete group, the case "the alternating group is finitely generated" is contained in Garrido--Reid, arXiv:2501.00908v1, Corollary `cor:group_expansive_iff_alternating_compactly_gen`, read at source (TeX in `gq/src/bh-garrido-kodama/`). There it is derived from their Boolean-inverse-monoid theory, and it yields a piecewise dense expansive subgroup; for a free action that subgroup is the whole group.
- The argument below needs neither minimality nor that theory.

## Statement

Let `Λ` be a countable group with at least four elements, acting freely by homeomorphisms on a Cantor space `X`.
Let `[[Λ ~ X]]` be its topological full group. Call `g ∈ [[Λ ~ X]]` a *3-cycle* if there are pairwise disjoint
clopen sets `U_0, U_1, U_2` with `g(U_i) = U_{i+1}` (indices mod 3), `g^3 = 1`, and `g` the identity off
`U_0 ∪ U_1 ∪ U_2`.

1. If a finitely generated subgroup `Γ ≤ [[Λ ~ X]]` contains every 3-cycle, then there is a finite clopen partition
   `P` of `X` whose coding map `π_P : X → P^Λ` is injective. So `Λ ~ X` is conjugate to the subshift `π_P(X)`,
   i.e. the action is expansive.
2. In particular, if the action is not expansive, then none of `[[Λ ~ X]]`, `D([[Λ ~ X]])` and the group
   `A([[Λ ~ X]])` generated by the 3-cycles is finitely generated, so none is finitely presented. (`A` is Nekrashevych's
   `alt_3` of the transformation groupoid, which equals his `alt` when orbits are infinite: arXiv:1511.08241,
   Proposition `pr:anydegreegen`, read at source.)
3. For finitely generated infinite `Λ`, `A([[Λ ~ X]])` is finitely generated **iff** the action is expansive.
   The converse is Nekrashevych, arXiv:1511.08241, Theorem `th:finitelygenerated` (TeX l. 1076, read at source):
   an expansive groupoid whose orbits have at least 5 points has finitely generated `alt`. For a free action the
   groupoid of germs is the transformation groupoid, and for finitely generated `Λ` it is expansive exactly when the
   action is (his Proposition `pr:expansivegroups`, l. 966).

## Examples of non-expansive free actions (so item 2 applies)

- free odometers of any residually finite group, and more generally free equicontinuous (profinite) actions;
- diagonal products `Y × Z` of any action `Y` with an equicontinuous action on a Cantor space `Z`, when the product
  action is free. Points `(y, z)`, `(y, z')` with `z, z'` close stay close under the whole group, so no product of this kind is expansive.

## Lesson for general BH

- **Finite generation is expansivity.** For free dynamics, the cocycle partition of any finite generating set of a
  full-group subgroup that contains the 3-cycles is already a generating partition.
- **What it forces.** Over a free action, a full-group host whose alternating part is finitely generated (a fortiori
  finitely presented) is a subshift host. With "finite presentation forces finite type"
  (`zd-derived-full-group-fp-forces-finite-type`): a free minimal `Z^N` system whose derived full group is finitely
  presented is a minimal SFT (`free-minimal-zn-fp-derived-full-groups-live-only-on-sfts`).
- **Where complexity can live.** Equicontinuous, odometer or profinite data can never be the ambient dynamics of such a
  host; at most they appear as factors. This is the dynamical half of the carrier principle (SYNTHESIS): the complexity
  has to be carried by symbolic dynamics and the acting group.
