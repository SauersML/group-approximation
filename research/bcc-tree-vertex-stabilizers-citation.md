Hervé Oyono-Oyono, *Baum–Connes conjecture and group actions on trees*,
K-Theory 24 (2001), 115–134, Theorem 1.1,
[DOI 10.1023/A:1012786413219](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1012786413219).
The source statement is that a countable group acting without inversion
satisfies coefficient Baum–Connes iff its vertex stabilizers do.

Rechecked 2026-09-20 against Lück–Reich,
[*The Baum–Connes and the Farrell–Jones Conjectures in K- and L-Theory*](https://him-lueck.uni-bonn.de/data/bcsfinal.pdf),
the actions-on-trees permanence statement citing Oyono-Oyono Theorem 1.1;
and Flores–Pooya–Valette,
[arXiv:2212.09557v2, Introduction and Section 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.09557v2).
The latter distinguishes the coefficient statement from its ordinary
edge-and-vertex Proposition 2.1. The original full proof is imported,
not independently reproduced. The integration ledger records the bounded
source check rather than claiming new certification of this theorem.
