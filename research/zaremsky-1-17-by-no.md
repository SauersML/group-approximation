An action in setting (S) of the root that violates (UB) shows that the
filling function of the complex, the Dehn functions of the vertex stabilizers
and the distortion of cell stabilizers do not bound the Dehn function in the
form Brown's criterion suggests. That answers the upper-bound reading (R2) of
Problem 1.17 negatively. The literal two-sided reading (R1) is already false
(`cocompact-action-data-do-not-determine-dehn-function`) and does not feed this
route.
