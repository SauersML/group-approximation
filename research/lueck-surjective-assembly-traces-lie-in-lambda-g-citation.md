Wolfgang Lück, *The relation between the Baum-Connes Conjecture and the Trace Conjecture*, Invent. Math.
149 (2002) 123--152. Read on 2026-09-12 from the author's PDF (him-lueck.uni-bonn.de/data/trc.pdf,
the Inventiones typesetting).

Verbatim:
- **(0.1)** "Λ^G := Z[1/|Fin(G)|], the ring Z ⊂ Λ^G ⊂ Q obtained from Z by inverting all the orders |H|
  of finite subgroups of G."
- **Conjecture 0.2** (Modified Trace Conjecture for a group G): "The image of the composition
  K_0(C*_r(G)) →(i) K_0(N(G)) →(tr_N(G)) R is contained in Λ^G."
- **Theorem 0.3:** "The image of the composition Λ^G ⊗_Z K_0^G(E̲G) →(id ⊗ asmb^G) Λ^G ⊗_Z K_0(C*_r(G))
  →(i) Λ^G ⊗_Z K_0(N(G)) →(tr_N(G)) R is Λ^G. In particular the modified Trace Conjecture 0.2 holds
  for G, if the assembly map asmb^G : K_0^G(E̲G) → K_0(C*_r(G)) appearing in the Baum-Connes
  Conjecture is surjective."
- **p. 2:** "The original Trace Conjecture predicted that its image lies in the additive subgroup of R
  generated by the inverses of all the orders of the finite subgroups of G, and has been disproved
  by Roy [15]."

**The spectral consequence in the claim.** For `a ∈ M_n(C[G])` self-adjoint with `c ∉ spec(a)`, the
function `χ_(-∞,c)` is continuous on the spectrum. So `χ_(-∞,c)(a)` is a projection of
`M_n(C*_r G)`, whose class has trace in `Λ^G`. For `A ∈ M_n(C[G])` with `0` isolated in
`spec(A^*A)`, `χ_{0}(A^*A)` is the kernel projection, of trace `dim_vN ker A`.
