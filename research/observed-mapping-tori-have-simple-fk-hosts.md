**OPEN — proposed headline; not independently certified, not Lean formalized, no priority claim.**

For `k≥2` or `k=∞`, let `D` have type `F_k`, let `φ∈Aut(D)`, and
let `ρ:D→V` be a homomorphism satisfying
`⋂_{j≥0}ker(ρφ^j)=⋂_{j≥0}ker(ρφ^{-j})=1`.
Then `<D,t | t^-1gt=φ(g)>` embeds in a simple group of type `F_k`
generated by two elements of finite order.

In particular an embedding `D→V` suffices, without requiring `φ`
to extend to `V`, to be spatial, or to have a finite-state realization.
The homomorphism may instead be noninjective if its iterates separate
in both directions. The conclusion does not assert failure of `F_{k+1}`.

## Attempts

The conditional route requires the OPEN two-species host construction
and OPEN compact-core SingFix theorem. The first supplies an exact
bilateral shell embedding; the second supplies all finite-forest
finiteness and clopen finite-subset stabilizers. The classical BHM
Theorem 2.1 and BZ Theorem D/3.4 are explicit external inputs. The
one-sided predecessor's finite-presentation proof does not establish
the full finite-marked-set and restriction-group statements needed
here. See artifact §§1–5 and 7 for proof mechanisms and limitations.
