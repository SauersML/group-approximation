**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `F` be a free group of finite rank
and `H` a finitely generated subgroup. Put

```text
π̄(H ≤ F) = min { rk L : H ≤ L ≤ F }.
```

Let `D(F)` be the division closure of `Q[F]` in `U(F)`, a skew field by
Linnell's theorem. Write `I_{Q[F]}` for the augmentation ideal and
`^F I_{Q[H]} = Q[F]·I_{Q[H]}` for the left ideal generated by `I_{Q[H]}`. Then

```text
π̄(H ≤ F) = rk F − dim_{D(F)} ( D(F) ⊗_{Q[F]} I_{Q[F]} / ^F I_{Q[H]} ).
```

Moreover the `L²`-closure `H̃` of `H` exists and `rk H̃ = π̄(H ≤ F)`.

Source: Jaikin-Zapirain, *Free groups are L²-subgroup rigid*,
arXiv:2403.09515, Theorem `pibar` and its proof. The right-hand side is the
source's `rk(F) − β_0^{Q[F]}(I_{Q[F]}/^F I_{Q[H]})`. The source identifies
`D(F)` with its `D_{Q[F]}`. Both inequalities appear verbatim in the proof,
and the citation route quotes them.
