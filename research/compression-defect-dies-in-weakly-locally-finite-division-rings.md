Call a division ring `D` **weakly locally finite** if the division subring
generated by any finite subset is centrally finite.

**Claim.**  If `D` is weakly locally finite and `rho : H -> D^x` is a
homomorphism, then `D(H,L) <= ker rho`.

**Corollary (embedding obstruction).**  If `D(H,L) != 1` then `H` embeds in
the multiplicative group of no weakly locally finite division ring; hence for
every field `K`, `K[H]` embeds in no such division ring.

## Proof shape, and why the reduction is not free

One defect generator `u = [g z g^{-1}, l]` at a time.  Write
`g = s_1^{e_1} ... s_m^{e_m}` with `s_i` compressors, set
`K = <z, l, s_1, ..., s_m>` and `L_0 = L n K`.  The point needing care is
that each `s_i` still compresses `L_0` **inside `K`**: for `a` in `L_0`,
`s_i a s_i^{-1}` lies in `L` by compression and in `K` because `s_i` and `a`
do, hence in `L_0`.  So `u` is a defect generator of the pair `(K, L_0)`,
`rho(K)` sits in `E^x` for `E` the division subring generated by the finitely
many images, `E` is centrally finite by hypothesis, and
`compression-defect-dies-in-centrally-finite-division-rings` finishes it.

## This is the exact reach, and that is a theorem, not a limitation of effort

The natural next statement -- that **every** division-ring representation
kills the defect -- is **false**.  It fails for the faithful embedding of a
torsion-free elementary amenable group into its own Linnell skew field; see
`linnell-skew-field-can-be-centrally-infinite`.  So is the weaker hope that
every Linnell division ring is weakly locally finite.

Consequently this node is the end of the line for the repository's
compression machinery as a source of division-ring obstructions, and any
route from it to `strong-atiyah-torsion-free` is blocked by a counterexample
rather than by a gap.  What it does buy is a real and reusable skew-field
embedding obstruction for any group with a nontrivial intrinsic defect.
