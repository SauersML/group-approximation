Literature import: C. Schafhauser, arXiv 2024 preprint (cited by STW as
[Schafhauser24, Corollary 4.6]): if every strongly self-absorbing
`D ≇ O_2` embeds into `(Q ⊗ O_∞)_ω`, then every finite strongly
self-absorbing algebra is quasidiagonal.  Recorded by STW in the sentence
following Problem XIV.
