Let `A = lim (A_j, phi_j)` with unital injective connecting maps. If for
infinitely many `j` the canonical map `U(A_j)/U_0(A_j) -> K_1(A_j)` is
injective, then `U(A)/U_0(A) -> K_1(A)` is injective.

Consequence for Problem XVIII: a K1-witness has, in every presentation as a
unital injective inductive limit, only finitely many K1-injective stages. So
its building blocks cannot be Z-stable algebras, stable-rank-one algebras,
purely infinite simple algebras, unital simple AH algebras with strict
comparison, or URP crossed products `C(X) ⋊ Gamma` with (COS).
