**OPEN.** Let `Γ < G` be the Theorem E pair of `kun-thom-free-nonsofic-action`:
`Γ = EL_r(F_q[x_1..x_d]) < G = EL_r(F_q[x_1^±1..x_d^±1]) ⋊ SL_d(Z)`, with `r, d >= 3`.

Let `Y_Γ = ({0,1}, fair)^(G/Γ)` be the generalized Bernoulli action, and `Ω_KT = Y_Γ × {0,1}^G` the free, strongly ergodic, nonsofic action. For every finite `A` with `|A| = q >= 2` and every `k >= 1`:

    (a)  h^Rok_G( (A^k)^G × Y_Γ ) = k log q.

The upper bound `<=` holds by the sandwich below: `h(X') = h(X' | F_Γ) <= h(X' | B(Y_Γ)) <= k log q`, where the last step holds because the coordinate partition of `(A^k)^G` generates relative to `B(Y_Γ)`.

**Equivalent forms.** Let `X' = (A^k)^G × Y_Γ`. It is free, because the Bernoulli factor is. It is ergodic, because a mixing action times an ergodic generalized Bernoulli action over the infinite transitive `G/Γ` is ergodic.

- **(b)** `h_G(X' | B(Y_Γ)) = k log q`.
  - `(a) ⟺ (b)`. The σ-algebra `F_Γ` is the `G`-invariant σ-algebra generated by the `Γ`-invariant sets. It contains `B(Y_Γ)`, since the coordinate at the coset `Γ` is `Γ`-invariant and its translates generate. So `h(X' | F_Γ) <= h(X' | B(Y_Γ)) <= h(X')`, and `h(X') = h(X' | F_Γ)` by `infinite-subgroup-invariant-information-has-zero-outer-entropy`. Hence all three are equal.
- **(c)** `h_G((A^k)^G × Ω_KT | B(Ω_KT)) = k log q`.
  - `(b) ⟺ (c)`. Apply `seward-weak-containment-relative-entropy-equality` with the free action `X'`, `F = B(Y_Γ)` and `Y = {0,1}^G`. That `Y` is weakly contained in all free actions (`abert-weiss-free-actions-weakly-contain-bernoulli`).
- **(d)** The Bernoulli class-bijective extension of the nonsofic orbit relation `R_(Ω_KT)` has full relative Rokhlin entropy.
  - `(c) ⟺ (d)`. This is Proposition 4 of `entropy-profile-proofs-of-bernoulli-maximality-relativize-proof`.

**Why this is the decisive test.**
- **It is forced by entropy-profile proofs.** An entropy-profile proof of maximality over `G`, and in particular an entropy-profile proof of `every-group-has-positive-rokhlin-entropy-action`, proves (c). This is `entropy-profile-proofs-of-bernoulli-maximality-relativize`, consequence 1.
- **A failure kills them.** A deficit in (a) for one `k` rules out every entropy-profile proof of the flagship: every information-inequality argument that uses invariance, independence and locality, but not the identity of the code across sites.
- **The flagship does not seem to imply it.** The flagship is known for `G`, which is residually finite. (c) is the Bernoulli case of Seward's direct-product conjecture for one specific `Y`. Weak containment does not reach that `Y`: the Koopman representation of `Y_Γ` contains `λ_(G/Γ)`, which is not weakly contained in `λ` because `Γ` is Kazhdan and infinite, hence nonamenable.
- **Sofic counting is unavailable.** When `q^k` is even, `X'` factors coordinatewise onto `Ω_KT`. A factor of a sofic action is sofic, because the crossed product of the factor embeds in that of `X'`. So `X'` has no sofic models, and sofic entropy gives no lower bound.

## Attempts

- **Factor-of-iid side information cannot produce a deficit (sw-050, 2026-09-17).** Suppose a code reads auxiliary structure (a matching, a pointer field, a random order) that is a factor of an iid process of `G`. That process is weakly contained in all free actions by `abert-weiss-free-actions-weakly-contain-bernoulli`, and so is its factor. By `seward-weak-containment-relative-entropy-equality` with trivial `F`, the Bernoulli entropy relative to it equals `h^Rok_G((A^k)^G)`, which is `k log q` for the residually finite `G`. So no such code gives a deficit. A deficit in (c) must read the coset labels of `Y_Γ`, whose symmetry under `Γ` is the information that sofic actions must see as `G`-invariant (Kun–Thom Theorem C).
