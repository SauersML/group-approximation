Full proofs: Theorem M in part 2 (block size `1`), and Lemma 10 with
Theorem M_k in part 3 (block size `k`).  The monomial facts (HC) and (COV) are
in part 1, §1.

**Block size 1.**
1. For monomials, `d_H(pi M, pi M') <= ||M - M'||_2^2 / 2`, so
   `psi = pi_omega o rho` is a homomorphism.  Since
   `||(c; sigma) - (c; id)||_2^2 = 2 d_H(sigma, id)`, `psi(a) = 1` makes
   `b = rho(a)` diagonal.
2. `(c; sigma) f (c; sigma)^* = f o sigma^(-1)` for diagonal `f`, so
   `psi(l) . b = rho(l a l^(-1)) = b` for `l in Lambda`.
3. Let `lambda` be a sofic representation of `G`.  `sigma' = psi|G × lambda`
   has trace `0` off the identity and fixes `b ⊗ 1` on `Lambda`.
4. Part 2 of `normal-closure-fixes-gamma-fixed-algebra` gives
   `psi(n) . b = b` for `n in <<Lambda>>_G`, hence
   `rho(n a n^(-1)) = rho(n) b rho(n)^* = b`.

**Block size k.**
1. `rho(A) <= M_k(Z)`, with `Z` the diagonal ultraproduct over blocks.
   `W*(rho(A))` is abelian and countably generated, so it is generated by one
   self-adjoint element.  By Kadison's diagonalization theorem (Amer. J. Math.
   106 (1984)), a unitary `V in M_k(Z)` puts it into `Z ⊗ D_k`.  `V` is
   block-diagonal, so `V rho V^*` is again block-monomial.
2. `C = W*(rho(A), Z)` is described by projections `z_Π in Z` indexed by
   partitions of `[k]`.  `Ad rho(g)` preserves `C` (since `A` is normal), acts
   by `psi(g)` on `Z`, and preserves the center-valued trace.  So it induces
   rank-preserving bijections of atoms, on finitely many projections of `Z`.
3. Canonical ordering inside atoms gives `tau_g in Z ⊗ C[S_k]` with
   `tau_(gg') = (psi(g') . tau_g) tau_(g')`.  Internal lifts give a
   homomorphism `sigma~(g)(y, j) = (psi(g) y, tau_g(y)(j))` into
   `prod_omega Sym(Y × [k])`, with `sigma~(g) c sigma~(g)^* = Ad rho(g)(c)`
   for `c in C`.
4. Steps 3--4 of the block-size-1 case, with `sigma~` in place of `psi`, give
   `rho(n a n^(-1)) = rho(a)`.
