Finite-dimensional algebras are nuclear and satisfy the UCT, so a
complexity-rank-one decomposition of each trivial-K-theory Kirchberg algebra
gives it the UCT by Willett--Yu Theorem 1.2.  The Jaime--Willett equivalence in
`willett-yu-decomposability-forces-uct` then gives the UCT for every separable
nuclear algebra.
