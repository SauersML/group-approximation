Citation import, secondary.

**Source.** S. Rees, *The development of the theory of automatic groups*,
arXiv:2205.14911v1 (30 May 2022), p. 13. PDF text extracted with pypdf and read on
2026-09-16. Verbatim: "Various combinations of automatic groups are known to be
automatic [26, 7]: these include free products, direct products, certain amalgamated
products and HNN extensions of automatic groups, as well as subgroups of finite index in
automatic groups, groups with automatic groups as subgroups of finite index, quotients of
automatic groups by finite normal subgroups."

Here [26] is Epstein–Cannon–Holt–Levy–Paterson–Thurston, *Word Processing in Groups*
(1992), and [7] is Baumslag–Gersten–Shapiro–Short, *Automatic groups and amalgams*,
J. Pure Appl. Algebra 76 (1991).
