title: Uniform Roe nuclear dimension is monotone under passage to metric subspaces

---

If `Y ⊆ X` has the induced metric and `X` is a countable discrete
bounded-geometry metric space, then

```text
dim_nuc(C*_u(Y)) ≤ dim_nuc(C*_u(X)).
```

In particular, if `asdim(X)=n` and `X` contains `Y` with
`dim_nuc(C*_u(Y))=n`, then `dim_nuc(C*_u(X))=n`.
