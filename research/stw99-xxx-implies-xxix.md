finite or purely infinite by Kirchberg's dichotomy (via Rordam's
`Z`-stable comparison), so a positive Problem XXX turns any simple
separable nuclear non-elementary RR0 algebra into a dichotomous one;

reduce by separable inheritance of RR0, both projections, and the
dichotomy conclusions.
