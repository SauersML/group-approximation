title: A group acting on a tree with coherent vertex stabilizers and Noetherian edge stabilizers is coherent

Let `G` act on a simplicial tree without inversions. Suppose every vertex
stabilizer is coherent and every edge stabilizer is Noetherian (all of its
subgroups are finitely generated). Then `G` is coherent.

In particular, the fundamental group of any graph of groups with coherent
vertex groups and Noetherian edge groups, for example polycyclic-by-finite
edge groups, is coherent.
