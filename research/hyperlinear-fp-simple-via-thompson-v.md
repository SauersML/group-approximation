`V` is finitely presented, infinite and simple, so a proof that it is
hyperlinear witnesses the root directly.  The obstructions recorded by the
Thompson lane (no Folner sets for Cantor orbits, degenerate tracial cylinder
models, logarithmic seam area) bear on the first prerequisite, not on this
implication.  `V` has torsion, so this route does not give the torsion-free
strengthening.
