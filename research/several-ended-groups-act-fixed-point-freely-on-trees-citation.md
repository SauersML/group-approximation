Sources:
- J. R. Stallings, *On torsion-free groups with infinitely many ends*, Ann. of Math. 88 (1968),
  312–334;
- J. R. Stallings, *Group theory and three-dimensional manifolds*, Yale Mathematical Monographs 4,
  Yale University Press, 1971.

The statement as recorded in the standard summary (Wikipedia, *Stallings theorem about ends of
groups*, retrieved 2026-09-17):

> "e(G) > 1 if and only if one of the following holds: The group G admits a splitting
> G = H *_C K as a free product with amalgamation where C is a finite group such that C ≠ H and
> C ≠ K. The group G is an HNN extension G = ⟨H, t | t^(-1)C₁t = C₂⟩ where C₁, C₂ are isomorphic
> finite subgroups of H."

and, in tree-action form:

> "A finitely generated group G has more than one end if and only if G admits a nontrivial (that
> is, without a global fixed point) action on a simplicial tree with finite edge-stabilizers and
> without edge-inversions."

This is a literature import. Only the forward direction of the tree-action form is used.
