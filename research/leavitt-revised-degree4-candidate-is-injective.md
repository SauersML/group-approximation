```text
W'=r t e t a t^(-1)c t^2 b t^(-1)d t p t,
b=r^2 a r^(-2),                 d=p^2 c p^(-2),
```

the natural coefficient map

```text
EL_20(L_(F_2)(1,2)) -> <G,t | W'=1>
```

is injective.  Hence this revised degree-four word is not a
Kervaire--Laudenbach violation and cannot prove that the Leavitt group is
nonhyperlinear.
