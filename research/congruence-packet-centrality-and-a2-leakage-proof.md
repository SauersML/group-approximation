Conjugation invariance inside the left regular group algebra is precisely
membership in its center, proving `(DCQ3)`.  Property `(T)` for the fixed
finite-index lattice `D` applied to the conjugation representation gives
`(DCQ4)` with no dependence on the quotient.  Conditional expectation and
spectral rounding give the robust projection statement.

Since central `q_n` commutes with `d_n`, expanding
`(1+d_n)^*(1+d_n)/4` and taking normalized corner trace gives `(DCQ8)`.
Multiplication by `tau(q_n)` gives `(DCQ9)`, and the two alternatives
`(DCQ10)--(DCQ11)` follow immediately.
