If every w!=1 survived in some tracial matrix-ultraproduct homomorphism,
coordinate lifts would make every such w elementwise tracially visible.
The visibility premise would make the countable group D hyperlinear,
contrary to the first premise. Thus one w dies in all such homomorphisms.

Any operator-norm asymptotic representation gives a tracial-ultraproduct
homomorphism along every free ultrafilter. Hence all ultralimits of
||phi_n(w)-1||_2 vanish, so the ordinary sequence tends to zero. If tau is
an MF trace on a C-star completion A containing D faithfully, its matrix
models instead yield a trace-preserving map from A into a tracial matrix
ultraproduct. The image of i(w) is 1; therefore tau annihilates the
nonzero positive element (i(w)-1)^*(i(w)-1), excluding faithfulness.
This uses Liu-Thom nonhyperlinearity, not operator-norm blindness.
