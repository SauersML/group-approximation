Source: M. Rordam, *The real rank of certain simple C\*-algebras*, in
Advances in Operator Algebras and Mathematical Physics (Sinaia 2003), Theta
Series in Advanced Mathematics, Theta, Bucharest, 2005, 197--206.  Read from
the author's manuscript `https://web.math.ku.dk/~rordam/manus/RRnot0.pdf` on
2026-09-12.  Numbering is from that manuscript.

Verbatim abstract: "In [6] we constructed simple C\*-algebras that contain both
finite and infinite projections. We show here, using ideas of Villadsen, that
these C\*-algebras are not of real rank zero. However, these C\*-algebras do
have the weaker property (SP)."  Here [6] is Rordam, *A simple C\*-algebra with
a finite and an infinite projection*, Acta Math. 191 (2003).

* Proposition 4.1: "The distance from the element a defined in (4.1) to the
  self-adjoint invertible elements of QBQ is 1."
* Corollary 4.2: "The real rank of B is not zero."
* Proposition 4.5: the same distance statement in `Q(D rtimes_alpha Z)Q`.
* Corollary 4.6: "The real rank of D rtimes_alpha Z is not zero."
* Proposition 5.3: "The C\*-algebra B has property (SP)."
* Proposition 5.5: "The C\*-algebra D rtimes_alpha Z has property (SP)."
* Lemma 2.1 (Villadsen): "Let A be a unital C\*-algebra, let a be an invertible
  element in A, and let p be a projection in A such that pap = 0. It follows
  that p <~ 1 - p."

Acta 2003, read from arXiv:math/0204339: Theorem 6.10 gives the separable
nuclear UCT algebra `D rtimes_alpha Z`, simple, with an infinite and a
non-zero finite projection.  Before Question 7.6 it says: "The real rank of
the C\*-algebras found in Theorems 5.6 and 6.10 have not been determined, but
we guess that they have real rank >= 1."  Question 7.6 then asks for a
separable unital simple algebra with an infinite and a nonzero finite
projection that is (i) of real rank zero, or (ii) nuclear and of real rank
zero.
