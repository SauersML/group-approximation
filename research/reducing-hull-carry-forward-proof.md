Because `Q_-` reduces every `Y_j` and contains both `Q` and `D`, the closed
linear span of

```text
w Ran(Q join D),        w in the star-algebra generated by Y,
```

lies in `Ran(Q_-)`.  Its projection is the smallest `Y`-reducing hull `Q'`,
so `(RHC2)` holds and `Q join D<=Q'`.

For two finite-dimensional projections,

```text
tau(Q join D)=tau(Q)+tau(D)-tau(Q meet D).              (RHP1)
```

Moreover `QDQ` is a positive contraction on `Ran(Q)` and is the identity on
`Ran(Q meet D)`.  Hence

```text
tau(Q meet D)<=tau(QDQ)=tau(QD).                        (RHP2)
```

Since `D` is unitarily conjugate to `A`, `tau(D)=tau(A)`.  Combining
`Q join D<=Q'` with `(RHP1)--(RHP2)` proves `(RHC3)`, and `(RHC4)` gives
`(RHC5)`.  Substituting the role-packed BCS lower bound proves `(RHC6)`.

For the approximate statement, the corrected marked sector is an exact
projection.  Compress the transported source to it, take the source polar
support, and spectrally round.  The standard fixed-word estimates bound the
discarded trace and every changed overlap by the squared normalized-HS
leakage, hence by `O(sqrt(E))` after the fixed finite packet corrections.
The exact argument then applies.
