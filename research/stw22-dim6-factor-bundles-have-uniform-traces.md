Every countably generated factorial tracially complete C-star algebra over
a compact Hausdorff base of covering dimension at most six has only
uniform-`2`-continuous traces, uniquely obtained by integrating the fibre
traces against Radon probability measures.  Matrix size and factor type may
vary arbitrarily.
