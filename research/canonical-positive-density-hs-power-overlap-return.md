Given the block-encoded cyclic acceptance contraction `T` for a fixed
Culf--Mastel finite game, its matrix soundness constant `rho<1`, and any
prescribed fixed integers `N,L`, construct an effective ordinary group
extension, a rational finite-packet idempotent `q`, and a unitary group word
`u` such that:

1. the canonical packet trace is fixed and positive,
   `tau(q)=alpha>0`, independently of `N,L` and matrix dimension;
2. every canonical-profile normalized-HS microstate, after fixed-packet
   exactification, satisfies

   ```text
   ||q u^(kL) q||_2^2
      <= (alpha+o(1)) rho^(2kL)+o(1),       1<=k<=N;
   ```

3. on a perfect instance, one exact possibly properly infinite
   representation extends with `q!=0` and the perfect acceptance carrier.

The same `q` must occur at every sampled power.  Separate depth heads whose
canonical traces sum to `O(1/N)` do not satisfy the claim.  No operator
identification of `q u^(kL)q` with `T^(kL)` is requested.

## Attempts

- A Julia/Halmos block gives the first compression but allows amplitude to
  return through the complementary block at later powers.
- A finite shift register suppresses returns but makes the head density
  `1/(NL+1)`, cancelling the capacity contradiction.
- Identifying the level heads restores density but restores return amplitude.
- The live attack is overlap-supported groupification: the finite-game gap
  must bound only the scalar corner norms, which are invariant under a common
  multiplicity gauge.  It must do so without turning small overlaps into
  universal group-algebra orthogonality, since the latter would contradict
  the canonical regular representation as soon as `(N+1)alpha>1`.
- The sibling static-packing route removes the no-return tail by replacing
  powers with unrelated word translates.  Its new price is finite triangle
  consistency of all relative selected corners.
