**ESTABLISHED.**  Put

```text
Gamma_6=(A8_1*A8_2)/<<s_0,s_11,s_30,s_44,s_55,c_19243>>.
```

For either marked chart `A8_i<Gamma_6` and every `1 != a in A8_i`,

```text
<<a>>_(Gamma_6)=Gamma_6.                                (NG6)
```

In particular fix one standard transvection `t` in the first chart.  Then
`Gamma_6` has no nontrivial hyperlinear quotient if and only if every
homomorphism from `Gamma_6` to a tracial matrix ultraproduct kills `t`.
Equivalently, the entire remaining qualitative Atlas obstruction is the
membership of this one fixed group word in the hyperlinear radical.

This does not prove that membership.  It replaces a quantified assertion
about all nontrivial quotients by one fixed-word survival test.
