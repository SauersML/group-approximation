Section 10. For fixed odd n>=1003 and least prime p, standard B(m,n) admits connected finite-radius FIID graphs with half-degree <=2-2/p+epsilon, all jumps of original length <=n/p, uniform positive expansion independent of m and epsilon, and O_n(1+log(m/epsilon)) coding and original-generator replacement bounds. These graphs can use power edges absent from the original Cayley graph.
