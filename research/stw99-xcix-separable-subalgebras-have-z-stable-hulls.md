Let `P` be the common norm ultraproduct arising from
`stw99-xcix-zero-distance-z-saturated-limit`.  For every separable
C*-subalgebra `S subset P`, there is a separable unital C*-subalgebra

```text
S subset T subset P
```

such that `T` is Z-stable.

Equivalently, every countable packet in a vanishing-distance XCIX limit is
captured by a genuinely tensorially Z-absorbing separable algebra inside the
same ultraproduct.  This is stronger than placing one copy of `Z` in
`P intersect S'`, but it still does not assert that the generally
nonseparable algebra `P` tensorially absorbs `Z`.
