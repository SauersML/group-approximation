Compile the robust `K_3` relational atlas into a finite or recursively
self-similar ordinary group presentation such that:

1. each colour PVM is represented by a fixed-scale finite packet with the
   original PCP coordinate shared across all incident checks;
2. every edge, reference-triangle, and commutativity-gadget defect is bounded
   by normalized-HS group-relator energy with dimension-free constants;
3. a rejected local 3-colouring gadget produces the diagonal/crossed endpoint
   mass required by the three S3 radical charts;
4. an exact tracial model with the marked central sign extends through the
   compilation.

## Attempts

- Robust commutativity gadgets alone only force PVMs to commute.  Their
  orthogonality and relation-support equations are algebra-valued projection
  constraints, not scalar equations between group words.
- Globally commuting all proof coordinates gives a classical assignment and
  destroys the contextual/non-Connes-embeddable source.
- Private copies of shared coordinates make each local 3-colouring gadget
  satisfiable independently and erase PCP soundness.
- A plain graph of finite packet groups has stationary regular-representation
  flow and cannot manufacture the diagonal/crossed S3 endpoint laws.
