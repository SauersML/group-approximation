**ESTABLISHED RECENTERING FENCE.**  Put `q=s_1t_1` and use the two
commuting marked roots

```text
z=x_13(q),                    C=x_(18,19)(q),
F=((1-rho(z))/2)((1-rho(C))/2).                       (ROH1)
```

For `k=7,8,9`, recenter the three prefix pairs at `(18,19)`:

```text
X_k=x_(18,k)(a_(k-6)),       Y_k=x_(k,19)(b_(k-6)).  (ROH2)
```

They obey the three-pair Pauli table with center `C`.  The native
Whiteheads `J_1,J_2` swap respectively the pairs `(7,8)` and `(8,9)`.
All their indices, and those of

```text
v_1=B_2A_1=x_84(b_2)x_47(a_1),
v_2=B_3A_2=x_95(b_3)x_58(a_2),                         (ROH3)
```

are disjoint from `{1,3,18,19}`.  Hence `J_i,v_i`, and
`T_i=J_iv_i` commute with `z,C` and reduce all of `F`.  The recentering
therefore eliminates the old compression boundary exactly.

It does not leave the three-pair algebra invariant.  Add the two native
hub pairs

```text
X_4=x_(18,4)(q),       Y_4=x_(4,19)(q),
X_5=x_(18,5)(q),       Y_5=x_(5,19)(q).               (ROH4)
```

Then the five pairs indexed by `K={4,5,7,8,9}` have common center `C`.
The first head acts on the triple `{4,7,8}` and the second on
`{5,8,9}`.  On the commuting `X` roots, conjugation by `v_1` gives

```text
X_4 |-> X_4X_7,       X_7 |-> X_7,
X_8 |-> X_8X_4,                                      (ROH5)
```

and `J_1` subsequently swaps `X_7,X_8`.  Thus

```text
Ad(T_1): X_4 |-> X_4X_8,
         X_7 |-> X_8,
         X_8 |-> X_4X_7.                             (ROH6)
```

The second row has the identical table after `(4,7,8)` is replaced by
`(5,8,9)`.  These are the two native Singer order-seven actions.  In
particular, the invariant recentered label algebra on `F` is the five-pair
Pauli factor `M_32(C)`, not the original `M_8(C)`.

Fix canonical implementers `S_i,L_i` of the actions of `J_i,v_i` on this
five-pair factor.  In an arbitrary representation reducing `F`, their most
general restrictions have the form

```text
rho(J_i)|_F=S_i tensor D_i,
rho(v_i)|_F=L_i tensor E_i,                            (ROH7)
```

on each homogeneous Pauli block, where `D_i,E_i` are reservoir unitaries.
Here `D_i^2=1`, but the exact order-seven relation says only

```text
((S_iL_i) tensor (D_iE_i))^7=1,
and hence (D_iE_i)^7=1.                               (ROH8)
```

It does not imply `D_i=1`, because the recentered Pauli covariance does not
authenticate `E_i=1`.

There is an exact finite zero-defect model of this ambiguity.  Let `G` be
the finite subgroup of `GL_5(F_2)` generated by the six actor matrices for
`J_1,A_1,B_2,J_2,A_2,B_3`.  On

```text
H=ell^2(F_2^5) tensor ell^2(G)                        (ROH9)
```

represent the five-pair Pauli group in its Schrodinger representation on
the first factor.  If `U_g` is the canonical linear-change implementer and
`lambda` is the left regular representation of `G`, represent every actor
by

```text
g |-> U_g tensor lambda(g).                           (ROH10)
```

Set both central involutions `z,C` to `-I`, so `F=I`.  All Pauli rows,
covariance rows, actor relations, and both seventh-power identities are
exact.  Nevertheless

```text
D_i=lambda(J_i)!=1,
E_i=lambda(v_i),
(D_iE_i)^7=lambda(J_iv_i)^7=1.                        (ROH11)
```

Indeed `D_i` is a nontrivial involution and
`||D_i-I||=2`.  Thus the recentered carrier proves positive same-carrier
Singer covariance, but it does not kill the native Whitehead reservoir
gauge.  A positive endpoint must separately authenticate the center-chain
factor `v_i` as reservoir-trivial, or compare its actor holonomy with an
independent ungauged occurrence; mere preservation of `F` is insufficient.

This is a finite representation of the displayed Pauli semidirect packet,
not of the whole EL20 presentation.  No Property T, Kazhdan input, local
computation, trace assumption, or literature theorem is used.
