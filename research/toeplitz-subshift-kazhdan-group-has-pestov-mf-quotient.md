Let `Y` be an infinite minimal subshift over a finite alphabet `A`, with `alpha(f) = f o T^{-1}`. On
`V = sum_{i>=0} LC(Y,F_2) b_i`, put `M_f(g b_i) = (fg) b_i`, `s(g b_i) = alpha(g) b_{i+1}` and
`t(g b_{i+1}) = alpha^{-1}(g) b_i`, `t(g b_0) = 0`. Let `R` be the unital subalgebra of `End(V)` generated by `s`, `t`
and the letter indicators `M_{[x_0=a]}`, and put `e = 1 - st` and `J = ReR`. Then:

1. `R` is finitely generated, `J ~= M_inf(A_+)` is locally matricial (`A_+` the one-sided cylinder algebra), and
   `R/J ~= LC(Y,F_2) x| Z`, the Pestov ring. The Hilbert-hotel radical is reached in one step: `hh(R) = d(R) = J`.
2. For `n >= 3`, `EL_n(R)` is a finitely generated group with property (T).
3. For `n >= 4`, `Rad_MF(EL_n(R)) = EL_n(R,J) = ker(EL_n(R) -> EL_n(R/J))`. It is locally finite, nontrivial and proper,
   with no relative `K_1` slack, so `EL_n(R)` is not MF.
4. The maximal MF quotient `EL_n(LC(Y,F_2) x| Z)` is an infinite, finitely generated, simple Kazhdan group that is LEF.

**Scope (un-verify-3, 2026-09-13).** Items 3–4 hold for `n >= 4`. The maximal MF quotient is `EL_n` over the Pestov ring, a rank-`n` elementary group. Pestov's own group `S = EL_3(LC(Y,F_2) x| Z)` is its rank-3 case, where the lower bound, and so the radical, is not computed here.

Both calibrating mechanisms sit in one group. The defect of a genuine one-sided pair supplies the radical, and the
Pestov towers make the quotient LEF.
