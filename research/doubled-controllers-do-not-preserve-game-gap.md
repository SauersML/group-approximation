title: Orientation-invariant doubled controllers admit internal rotations which erase the game gap
---

Doubling a controller cancels scalar orientation signs but leaves an
arbitrary accepted-subspace multiplicity rotation.  Three exact dressed
acceptance projections can then have cyclic product of operator norm one
even though the undressed game compressor has a fixed spectral gap.

The strict product survives orientation-free only as a nonunitary Hecke
projection product, not as the zero corner of the ordinary doubled word.
