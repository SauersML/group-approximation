Notation as in `mod-s2-torelli-abelian-scalar-gap-is-a-monomial-census-limit`: `M = Mod(S_2)`,
`t_s = (a_1 a_2)^6`, `M_4 = pi^(-1)(Gamma(4))`, `v_c` the class of `g t_s g^(-1)` in `H_1(M_4)`,
`W = W(Gamma(4))`, `d = d(Gamma(4))`. A **level-two splitting** is one of the 10 decompositions
`F_2^4 = P + P^perp` into nondegenerate planes; each separating curve determines one through its
homology splitting mod 2.

## Statement

- **(G1) Homology.** `H_1(M_4; Z) = Z^54`. It is torsion-free.
- **(G2) Classes.** There are exactly 160 distinct classes `v_c`. Exactly 16 of them lie over each
  level-two splitting, and those 16 span a lattice of rank 10.
- **(G3) No torsion characters.** `W` has rank 106 and is saturated, so `Z^160/W = Z^54` is free.
  Hence `W^perp` is a connected torus, and every admissible `theta` is the reduction of a real
  character. The gcd of augmentations on `W` is `g = 10`.
- **(G4) Generators.** `W` is generated over `Z` by two families.
  - *Local relations.* 60 relations of augmentation 0, each supported over a single level-two
    splitting, with `l1`-norm 6 or 8 (6 per splitting).
  - *Transversal relations.* A 0/1 vector choosing one class over each of the 10 splittings lies in
    `W` for exactly 1024 of the `16^10` choices. These 1024 vectors form a **single orbit** under
    conjugation by `M`. Under the pure part `Gamma(2)/Gamma(4)` they form two regular orbits of
    size 512 (the hyperelliptic involution acts trivially). Each has `l1 = aug = 10`, and each maps
    to the level-two relation (all ones on the 10 classes of `Gamma(2)`).
  Alone, the transversal relations span a sublattice of index `2^5` in `W`.
- **(G5) Distance.** `d(Gamma(4)) = 1/30`, attained by the constant `theta = 3/10`.
- **(G6) All overgroups.** `d(Gamma') >= 1/30` for every `Gamma' >= Gamma(4)`, and
  `d(Gamma') = 1/30` for every `Gamma(4) <= Gamma' <= Gamma(2)`.

## What this changes

- **The 2-adic tower does not start to decay.** The census reached only levels whose
  Reidemeister-Schreier fits in memory, and `Gamma(4)` was listed as out of reach in the attempts on
  `mod-s2-torelli-abelian-census-limit-is-positive`. It is the first principal level above `Gamma(2)`,
  where `d_inf <= 1/30` is attained. The value stays `1/30`.
- **The torsion loophole is closed at this level.** Real characters of `M'` sum to zero over the
  cosets, because `H^1(M; R) = 0` (transfer). So beating the constant point `(1/g)Z` needs
  non-constant characters, which could only come from `tors(Z^X/W)`. By (G1) and (G3) there is none
  at `Gamma(4)`. This is the first exact test of the optimality question in
  `mod-s2-maslov-line-is-optimal-for-torelli-characters` (filed by w12-deligne-break) beyond the old
  census, and it answers yes there.
- **Sparse certificates exist at level four.** The transfer of the level-two relation to
  `Gamma(4)` has `l1 = aug = 10 * 2^10` (64 on each class), so (TA3) gives only
  `d >= 1/30720` from it. In fact the relation lifts *without spreading*: one class over each
  splitting, `l1 = 10`, in a single `M`-orbit. This is the level-four analogue of the `GQ(2,4)`
  point stars at level three. It supports the candidate sparse-lift route recorded on the
  positivity node: there it was noted that "the level-two case already differs".
- **What a uniform 2-adic proof along this pattern needs.** A uniform proof of `d(Gamma(2^k))
  >= 1/30` along this pattern needs, for every `k`, a transversal relation in `W(Gamma(2^k))`,
  i.e. 10 separating twists, one over each level-two splitting, whose product is trivial in
  `H_1(M_(2^k))`. At `k = 2` the 1024 such relations form one orbit, so one geometric relation in
  `M` (a genus-two surface-bundle or fibration relation with 10 separating twists and monodromy in
  `M_4`) would explain all of them. None is identified here.
