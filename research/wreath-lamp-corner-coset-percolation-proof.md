Pontryagin duality gives `L(W) = L^infinity({±1}^(G/Gamma)) x| G`, with
`u_g f u_g^* = f o g^(-1)` and `(g omega)_x = omega_(g^(-1) x)`. The fiber
representation `pi_omega(f) delta_h = f(h omega) delta_h`,
`pi_omega(u_g) delta_h = delta_(gh)` is covariant, and
`tau(x) = E_omega <pi_omega(x) delta_e, delta_e>`. Now `p` is the indicator of
`omega_Gamma = +1`, so `pi_omega(p)` is the indicator of the set of `h` with
`omega_(h^(-1) Gamma) = +1`. That set is a union of right cosets `Gamma h`,
one fair coin per coset. `Gamma g h = Gamma h` exactly when `g in Gamma`.
