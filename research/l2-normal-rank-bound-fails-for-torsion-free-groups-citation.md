Citation import, not a reproof. arXiv:2608.25988v1, Theorem 1.2: "For each
n ∈ N, there is a countable torsion-free group Γ_n such that β_1^(2)(Γ_n) = n and
n(Γ_n) = 1. Moreover, each Γ_n is locally free (and hence locally indicable)."
The conjecture it refutes is Osin–Thom arXiv:1108.2411v2, Conjecture 1.3. Read
from the PDF texts on 2026-09-12.
