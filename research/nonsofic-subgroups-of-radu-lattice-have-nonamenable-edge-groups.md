**ESTABLISHED** by `radu-nonsofic-subgroup-edge-group-filter-proof`, given the literature combination
theorem cited there.

Let `S` be a finitely generated nonsofic subgroup of Radu's BMW lattice `Gamma_R`. Then:
* on the vertical tree `T_v = Gamma_R/A`, `S` fixes no vertex, edge midpoint or end, and in the finite
  graph of groups of its minimal subtree some edge group `S ∩ gAg^(-1)` or `S ∩ gA_ev g^(-1)` contains a
  nonabelian free group;
* on the horizontal tree `T_h = Gamma_R/V`, the same holds with edge groups `S ∩ gV_a g^(-1)`.

In particular `S` meets some conjugate of `A` and some conjugate of `V` in a nonabelian free group.

Consequences:
* The supports of any one-sided inverse pair over `M_n(F_p[Gamma_R])`
  (`radu-bmw-lattice-group-algebra-not-stably-finite`) generate such an `S`.
* So does the memory of a strict factor of any strict automaton on `Gamma_R` (`radu-bmw-lattice-nonsurjunctive`).
* The letter-subgroup screen is the special case where `S ∩ V` is amenable.
