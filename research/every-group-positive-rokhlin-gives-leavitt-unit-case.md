`U = L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` is countably infinite: it is a countable group containing
the infinite group Thompson's `V`. So the universal statement
`every-group-has-positive-rokhlin-entropy-action`, applied to `G = U`, gives a
free ergodic p.m.p. action of `U` with positive Rokhlin entropy.

This is a pure specialization. It does not bear on the existence question for
`U` itself, which stays open unless the universal statement is established.
