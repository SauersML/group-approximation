Source: Matthew Gentry Durham, Yair Minsky, and Alessandro Sisto,
*Asymptotically CAT(0) spaces, Z-structures, and the Farrell--Jones
Conjecture*, arXiv:2504.17048v1, Corollary E.

The authors apply their colorable-HHG criterion to the hierarchical
structure of extra-large type Artin groups. The relevant proper-domain
stabilizers are central extensions of virtually free groups, so the
Farrell--Jones inheritance package applies. Corollary E states directly
that extra-large type Artin groups satisfy Farrell--Jones.

The paper's introduction identifies the conjectures here as the K- and
L-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjectures for group rings. It supplies no
A-theoretic assertion, so none is imported. QED
