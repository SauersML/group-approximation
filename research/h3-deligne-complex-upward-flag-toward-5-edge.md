Let `Λ = s_1 s_2 s_3` be the Coxeter diagram of type `H_3` with `m_{s_2 s_3} = 5`, ordered
`ŝ_1 < ŝ_2 < ŝ_3`. Then the induced relation `<` on the vertices of the Artin complex `Δ_Λ` is a
partial order that is weakly graded, bowtie free and upward flag.

Literature import through `h3-deligne-complex-upward-flag-toward-5-edge-citation`. This is the
`H_3` case of Conjecture `conj:compareB` of arXiv:2405.12068.
