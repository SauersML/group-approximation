**OPEN.** The group `Ĥ = (N x| <T, Σ>)/C` of
`rational-bs12-hull-embeds-in-a-fg-solvable-group` embeds in a finitely presented
group with an action of type (A).

**What is known about `Ĥ`.** It is 4-generated, solvable of derived length at
most 3, and has solvable word problem. It is not residually finite, since it
contains `(Q,+)`. So it is a genuine Boone--Higman input, and the
residual-finiteness routes of the graph do not apply to it.

**Relation to proved classes.**
- Finitely generated metabelian groups lie in `B_A` by the graph's metabelian
  and quasi-linear results. `Ĥ` has derived length 3, and its second derived
  subgroup is the non-residually-finite `Q`.
- Among the permanence results, `free-solvable-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`
  and `product-variety-free-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman` treat
  quotients of free groups by characteristic verbal subgroups. `Ĥ` is a
  quotient of such a group by a non-verbal central subgroup, `C`, and `B_A` is
  not known to pass to such quotients.
