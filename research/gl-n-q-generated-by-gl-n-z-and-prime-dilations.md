**ESTABLISHED (2026-09-17)** through
`gl-n-q-generated-by-gl-n-z-and-prime-dilations-proof`. Lane proof, not
independently reviewed; it is elementary and no novelty is claimed.

**Statement.** Let `n >= 2`. For a prime `p` put `e_p = diag(p,1,...,1)` and
`d_p = diag(p,p^-1,1,...,1)`.
1. `GL_n(Q)` is generated by `GL_n(Z)` together with `{e_p : p prime}`.
2. `SL_n(Q)` is generated by `SL_n(Z)` together with `{d_p : p prime}`.
3. The `e_p` commute pairwise and generate a free abelian group
   `E = ⊕_p Z e_p` of countable rank, and
   `GL_n(Q) = SL_n(Q) ⋊ ({±1} × E)` for the section `a -> diag(a,1,...,1)`
   of the determinant.

**Consequence for hosts.** An embedding of `GL_n(Q)` into a group `H` is the
same as an embedding of `GL_n(Z)` into `H` together with pairwise commuting
elements `h_p` (images of `e_p`) such that, for every finite set `S` of
primes, `GL_n(Z)` and `{h_p : p in S}` generate a copy of `GL_n(Z[1/S])` in
the standard way. So a single host for the ascending chain
`GL_n(Z[1/m!])` is exactly a host for `GL_n(Q)`, and what it must supply
beyond one copy of `GL_n(Z)` is a free abelian group of infinite rank whose
basis elements each realize one prime.
