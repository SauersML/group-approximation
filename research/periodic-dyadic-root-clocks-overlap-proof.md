Use one additive `F_5` coordinate for every root.  The two-dimensional
coweight lattice modulo four acts on root `(i,j)` by multiplication by
`2^(m_i-m_j)`, and `S_3` permutes all coordinates.  This gives a finite
semidirect product in which the six dyadic scaling equations and the
three-direction coweight relation hold exactly.  The nontrivial root
characters run in period-four doubling cycles.

The root/Weyl subgroup fixes the base coset in the finite coset action.  Add
commuting involutory lamps at the cosets and take the finite regular
representation.  The base lamp and its first coweight translate are distinct
balanced independent signs, hence their half projections have overlap
`1/4`.  Since the root module was made abelian, the construction fails
exactly at the adjacent-root Steinberg commutator, identifying the next input
which a positive estimate must use.
