# Double every projective sector intertwiner by its conjugate

Equation `(SG1)` and its conjugate make `(SG2)` strictly multiplicative.
Direct sum over the finite object set to obtain honest bisection unitaries.
Schur's lemma gives `(SG5)` for finite packet normalizers. Tensor the doubled
bisection action with an arbitrary contextual representation; every locally
satisfied word splits as identity on the finite sector module times the old
word on multiplicity.
