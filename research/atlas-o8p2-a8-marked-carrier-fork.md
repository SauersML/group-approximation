Let `G=O8+(2)` and `A~=A8<G`.  Choose a maximal subgroup `M` containing
`A`.  Lagrange and the complete [CTblLib maximal-subgroup
table](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Thomas.Breuer/ctbllib/ctbltoc/data/O8%2B%282%29.html)
force `M` into exactly one of the following nine maximal classes:

```text
three S6(2) classes,       |M|=1451520, [G:M]=120;
three 2^6:A8 classes,      |M|=1290240, [G:M]=135;
three A9 classes,          |M|=181440,  [G:M]=960.   (O8C1)
```

These triples are permuted by the triality outer automorphisms of `G`.  Every
other maximal-subgroup order in the table fails divisibility by `|A8|`.
Consequently the equality problem is not one undifferentiated `A8` conjugacy
class and must not begin with a raw packet scan.  It first splits into the
three geometric carrier types in `(O8C1)` (and their triality images), after
which complements and cross-carrier coincidences must be classified.

This is a carrier classification, not yet a claim that each carrier contains
only one `G`-class of `A8`, nor that different carriers give disjoint `A8`
classes.  Those complement/fusion questions are the exact next finite task.
