This is a citation import, not a reproof.

- **Primary:** Y. Shalom, *Rigidity of commensurators and irreducible lattices*,
  Invent. Math. 141 (2000), 1-54: every discrete Kazhdan group is a quotient of a
  finitely presented Kazhdan group.
- **Openness form:** stated in Y. Stalder, *Fixed point properties in the space
  of marked groups*, arXiv:0803.2592. The abstract attributes to Shalom that
  Kazhdan's property (T) defines an open subset of the space of marked finitely
  generated groups, and the paper gives an ultralimit proof. Read 2026-09-11.

The finite set `S` form follows from openness and the definition of the
Chabauty neighbourhoods. Quotients of Kazhdan groups are Kazhdan.
