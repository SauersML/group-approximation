`SL_3(R)` is a connected, noncompact, simple real Lie group with finite center, and `SL_3(Z)` is a
lattice in it (Borel--Harish-Chandra; the standard example of a non-uniform lattice, Furman,
arXiv:0901.0678, p. 5). So a nonsofic p.m.p. action of `SL_3(Z)` is a nonsofic p.m.p. action of a
lattice in a group of the first family.
