**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `(A_k, phi_k)` be an inductive system of unital C-star
algebras with unital connecting `*`-homomorphisms, and let `A` be its limit.
The following are equivalent.

1. `A` contains a full element `x` with `x^2=0`.
2. Some `A_j` contains a full element `z` with `z^2=0`.

Here full means that the closed two-sided ideal generated by the element is the
whole algebra.

For the unital form of Problem LXXIII (`a=1`, any `epsilon<1`), a unital
counterexample is therefore exactly a unital limit with no nonzero
finite-dimensional representation in which **no stage** contains a full
square-zero element.  The obstruction may be checked stage by stage; nothing
about the limit's primitive ideal space is needed.
