`Z^3 * Z` is isomorphic to a subgroup of `2V`. It is the right-angled Artin
group of the graph `K_3 ⊔ K_1` (a triangle plus an isolated vertex). So it is a
counterexample to Belk–Bleak–Matucci, arXiv:1602.08635v1, Conjecture 1.7 at
`n = 2`, and it refutes `z3-free-z-does-not-embed-in-2v`.
