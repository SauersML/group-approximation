Dead: the attempt obtains the missing inequality Cost(b) <= Cost(alpha) by reproducing a near-optimal graphing of an arbitrary free action inside the Bernoulli action, copying its finite statistics well enough to keep the completed cost. Reproducing arbitrary finite statistics is impossible (fpbs-source-statistics-not-reproducible-in-bernoulli): the product of a Bernoulli action with the two-point trivial action would produce almost invariant sets of measure one half in a strongly ergodic action. A transport theorem must preserve the achievable cost only, which is exactly the step still missing.
