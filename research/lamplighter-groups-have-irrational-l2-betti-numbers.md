**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Say `r` arises from `Γ` if `r = dim_vN ker T` for some
matrix `T` over `Q[Γ]` (equivalently over `Z[Γ]`, after clearing denominators).
- For every `p >= 2` some real arising from `Z/p ≀ Z` is irrational.
- The real `1/64 - (1/8) Σ_(k>=1) 2^(-(k² + 4k + 6))`, which is irrational, arises from
  `(Z/2 ≀ Z)^3`.
