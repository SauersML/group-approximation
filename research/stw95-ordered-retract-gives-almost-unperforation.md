Let `A,B,S,T,theta` be as in the inner-cofinality claim.  Suppose there is an
additive order-preserving map `sigma:T->S` such that `theta sigma=id_T`.
No preservation of suprema or compact containment is assumed.  Then `T` is
almost unperforated.
