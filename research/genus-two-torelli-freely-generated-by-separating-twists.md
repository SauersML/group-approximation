**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`genus-two-torelli-separating-twist-basis-citation`).

Let `I(S_2) = ker(Mod(S_2) -> Sp_4(Z))` be the Torelli group of the closed oriented genus-two surface.

1. `I(S_2)` is an infinitely generated free group, with one Dehn twist generator for each symplectic
   splitting of `H_1(S_2; Z)` (Mess, Theorem D as stated by Bestvina–Bux–Margalit).
2. Each generator is a Dehn twist about a separating simple closed curve. The splitting attached to a
   separating curve is spanned by the curves on its two sides, and two separating curves give the same
   splitting iff they differ by an element of `I(S_2)` (same source, same passage).
3. In particular `I(S_2)` is generated by Dehn twists about essential separating simple closed curves.
   Twists about inessential curves are trivial, and every element of an infinite free basis is
   nontrivial.

**Trust surface.** Mess's original paper was not read. The statement is quoted from the Bestvina–Bux–Margalit
paper, which also gives an independent proof in its Section 6 (not re-read).
