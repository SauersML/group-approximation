Let

```text
z=x_13(q),                 q=s_1t_1,
I={2,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,18,19}.
```

Let `Lambda_nat` be the subgroup generated by the elementary-root
occurrences which have actually been authenticated in the present native
two-scale **transport table**:

* the factors `X_1,Y_1,X_2,Y_2` of the literal prefix Whiteheads `J_1,J_2`;
* the four prefix-refinement roots and their two intermediate commutator
  roots in `(LPJ3)`;
* the returned-root diamond, opposite constant Whitehead, and odd native
  factor rows `(OSF1)--(OSF4)`;
* the center-chain arms and native Hecke heads in `(OWH1)--(OWH4)` and
  `(ABH1)--(ABH4)`;
* the support-idempotent return words in `(SRL1)--(SRL6)`; and
* the same-center Pauli arms and their adjacent prefix transports.

The two auxiliary roots `x_41(-),x_32(-)` used earlier to prove that the
marked carrier overlaps the separate root `C=x_42(q)` are deliberately not
generators of `Lambda_nat`: they are carrier-overlap witnesses, not factors
of either native scale transport.  Adjoining either to a proposed transport
would be an external dressing whose occurrence type must be proved.

Every elementary generator in this list has both root indices in `I`.
Since `I` is disjoint from `{1,3}`, the ordinary Steinberg commutator law
gives

```text
[Lambda_nat,z]=1.                                      (NLO1)
```

Consequently every two full words `g_1,g_2 in Lambda_nat` satisfy

```text
u=g_1^(-1)g_2,             u z u^(-1)=z.              (NLO2)
```

In particular this holds after all presently authenticated native
factorizations, prefix refinements, odd Whitehead returns, and first/second
scale coupling have been imposed.  It is therefore impossible for such a
pair to satisfy the root-valued relative occurrence required by `(ATM10)`
or `(NCG1a)`:

```text
u z u^(-1)=x_ab(c),       c!=0,       {a,b} disjoint {1,3}.  (NLO3)
```

The elementary-matrix quotient separates the root in `(NLO2)` from every
root in `(NLO3)`, because their unique nonzero off-diagonal entries occupy
different positions.

This is a fence for the **current authenticated occurrence closure**, not a
refutation of `binary-leavitt-arbitrary-profile-native-cross-gram`.  A live
root-valued attack must add an external stabilization containing a root
index `1` or `3`, and must then prove by an ordinary native occurrence row
that the dressed full word is one of the two prescribed unequal-scale
selector transports.  Merely multiplying one native word by such a mover
does not supply that typing; `root-valued-overlap-does-not-undress-external-selector`
already shows that the external factor can survive on every positive
subcorner.

The scoped finite escape remains exact.  The `GL_7(F_2)` model of
`literal-prefix-coupling-of-j1-j2-has-gl7-model`, with its surviving marked
child, realizes all two-scale prefix rows, while the direct product with a
central marked `C_2` realizes `(NLO1)--(NLO2)` for the entire abstract local
transport table.  Thus another local prefix compilation cannot force a
root-valued relative word; the first genuinely new datum must be an
externally indexed, occurrence-typed stabilization.

No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, canonical trace, literature theorem,
local computation, or Lean compilation is used.
