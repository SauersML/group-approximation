title: Every nonzero positive image of an O-infinity-stable unital map is properly infinite
distinct_from:
  stw99-lxxviii-finite-target-obstruction: that uses only the image of the unit to obstruct stability; this gives the necessary comparison relation for every nonzero positive element in the range.
---

Let `phi:A->B` be a unital `O_infinity`-stable \*-homomorphism.  For every
positive `a in A` with `phi(a)!=0`,

```text
phi(a) directSum phi(a) Cuntz-below phi(a).
```

Thus every nonzero positive element in the image of `phi` is properly
infinite.  This implication does not use exactness, nuclearity, simplicity,
or quasitraces.
