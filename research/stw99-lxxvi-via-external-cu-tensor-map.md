Let `A` be pure.  By
`stw99-lxxvi-external-cu-tensor-map-is-the-only-gap`, the map
`Cu(A)->Cu(A tensor_min Z)` is the composite of the canonical abstract
`Cu(Z)`-absorption isomorphism with the external-product morphism.  The
required claim makes the latter an isomorphism as well, so their composite is
an isomorphism.  Hence `A` is Cuntz semigroup regular.
