Fix a prime `p >= 5`. `Gamma = Gamma^A2_p` has generators `a, b, c` of order
`p` and Heisenberg vertex groups. `phi^ : Gamma^ -> I_1` is the surjection
onto the pro-p Iwahori of `SL_3(F_p[[t]])` with `a -> x12(1)`,
`b -> x23(1)`, `c -> x31(t)`.

- `D_n` is the Zassenhaus filtration. On `I_1` it equals the height
  filtration (as used in `kms-hc1-a2-iwahori-towers-deviate-only-at-kassel-degrees`),
  so `gr I_1 = L_p = n^+(sl3) + t sl3[t]` with `deg(t^k E_ij) = 3k + j - i`.
  This is a restricted Lie algebra with `(t^k E_ij)^[p] = 0` and
  `(h t^n)^[p] = h t^(np)`.
- `gr Gamma^` is the restricted Lie algebra generated by degree 1
  (Jennings). It is multigraded by letter degree only up to the first
  deviation; see (FD).
- The first deviation degree `d0` is the least `d` with
  `gr_d Gamma^ -> (L_p)_d` not injective. If there is none, `d0 = infinity`.
- `U` is the universal restricted Lie algebra generated in degree 1 subject to
  all relations of `L_p` of degree `< d0`. It is multigraded, and
  `gr_(d0) Gamma^` is a quotient of `U_(d0)`.
- The level-`n` space `W_n` in `U_(3np)` is spanned by three elements:
  - `K_n`, the Kassel line (the class of `H_2(L_p)_(3np)`, from
    `kms-hc1-iwahori-h2-serre-plus-kassel-all-degrees`);
  - `C_nA = (h_A t^n)^[p] - (h_A t^(np))~`;
  - `C_nB = (h_B t^n)^[p] - (h_B t^(np))~`.
  All three have multidegree `(np, np, np)`. It matches the 3-dim `W_n` of
  the chain, with coordinates `(kappa, alpha_A, alpha_B)`.

## Statements

**(RP) Root p-powers die.** Suppose `Gamma^/D_(pd) = I_1/D_(pd)` via `phi^`.
Let `y` in `gr_d Gamma^` map to a real-root vector `t^k E_beta`. Then
`y^[p] = 0` in `gr_(pd) Gamma^`. This holds for every odd `p`.

**(FD) First deviation.** Assume Open 1 (`H_2(L_p)` is the 6 Serre classes in
degree 3 plus one Kassel class in each degree `3np`). If `d0` is finite, then:
- `d0 = 3np` for some `n >= 1`;
- `ker(gr_(d0) Gamma^ -> (L_p)_(d0))` is the image of `W_n`, so it has
  dimension at most 3 and is multihomogeneous of degree `(np, np, np)`.

**(Crit) Criterion.** `phi^` is an isomorphism iff for every `n >= 1`
(inductively, with agreement below `3np`) the images of `K_n`, `C_nA`,
`C_nB` in `gr_(3np) Gamma^` vanish. In the chain's language this is
`V_n = W_n`.
- Level `n = 1` is `kms-hc1-witt-carry-syzygy-lifts-by-right-freeness`,
  pending landing.
- So the problem is exactly level `n >= 2`.

**(Z) Known levels.** Lower p-central agreement through class `c` gives
Zassenhaus agreement through `c`, because `P_n` is contained in `D_n` and
`D_n` behaves functorially under surjections. The towers node certifies
agreement to class 59 (`p = 5`), 62 (`p = 7`) and 65 (`p = 11`). So:
- `V_2 = W_2` and `V_3 = W_3` at `p = 5` (degrees 30 and 45);
- `V_2 = W_2` at `p = 7` (degree 42);
- only level 1 at `p = 11`.

**(P) The products route is obstructed.** The level-`n` evaluation `rho_n`
kills the ideal generated by gr I in degrees below `3np`. So a product
`u T v` of level-1 gr I elements with `(p,p,p)` words contributes 0 to
`W_2`.
- The level-2 value comes only from the Lie and restricted structure in
  degree `2p`, as in (FD).

**(M) The multigrading caveat of the w12 claim is resolved.** Components of
S-pair jumps that are not multihomogeneous lie in the ideal generated by
relations of degree `< d0`. By (FD) they cannot deviate first.

**(X) The direct step at `p = 5` is infeasible within this lane's limits.**
The target `u(N)` at the truncation `(10,10,10)` has PBW dimension
6,199,717, with 321,285 in the top degree (`hilb.py 5 10 10 10`). The
`(10,6,10)` box of the `(2p,p+1,2p)` slice alone has 665,313. The free side
at `(10,6,10)` has about `4.3e10` words. Because of (Z), none of this is
needed at `p = 5` anyway.

## Evidence

`torus_comm.py p 24 20` (`p = 5, 7, 11`) prints `ALL OK`. It checks the
matrix inputs of (RP) for the six vertex elements:
- `x^p = 1`;
- `g = [x, s]` is `x_beta(-2u t^(k0+m) + ...)`;
- `[g, x] = 1` and `x g = x^s`;
- every positive real root of degree `<= 20` arises exactly once.

The proofs are in the route `kms-hc1-root-p-powers-die-first-deviation-at-3np-proof`.

## Next step

Prove `V_n = W_n` for `n >= 2` for all `p`. There are two candidates:

1. **Virtual-vertex pairs.** Take pairs such as `<[c,a],[b,c]>`, whose image
   is a Heisenberg group with centre `x31(t^2)`. Then:
   - use the `t^2` conjugator `E(lambda E13)`, which gives
     `t^2 (E31 + lambda(h_A + h_B) - lambda^2 E13)`;
   - use T5 of the w12 claim, which reads `alpha_A + alpha_B`.

   The obstacle is that the virtual relations hold in `Gamma^` only modulo
   `D_(3np)`. The argument must show that this error lands in the ideal
   of (M).
2. **Cartan p-power relations at level n.** These need group input beyond
   (RP). Sub-Iwahoris of `SL_2(F_p[[t]])` are not finitely presented, so a
   rank-one argument cannot supply them.
