**Established by citation.**  Francesco Fournier-Facio, *Stability,
approximable quotients, and higher property (T)*, arXiv:2512.09180v2, Theorem
A, proves the stated embedding theorem.  Proposition B constructs a
property-(T) relatively hyperbolic host with no nontrivial approximable
quotients, and Proposition C supplies the quotient with the strong failure of
`(T_2)`.  The proof of Theorem A specializes the approximation class to the
unnormalized Frobenius norm and explicitly concludes Frobenius stability from
the absence of nontrivial Frobenius-approximable quotients.

The paper itself distinguishes this metric from hyperlinearity and recalls
that the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt analogue remains open.  No normalized-HS
conclusion is imported here.
