**Statement.** Let `G` be an elementary amenable group of finite Hirsch length. If `G` has
property BVC, then `G` is virtually cyclic. No finite presentation is assumed.

**Context.** Kochloukova–Martínez-Pérez–Nucinkis (2011) proved the Juan-Pineda–Leary conjecture
(finite `E_VC` models) for elementary amenable groups. von Puttkamer–Wu, arXiv:1607.03790,
Remark (b), record that the BVC form (their Conjecture B) was unknown for all elementary
amenable groups, known for solvable ones (Groves–Wilson). This node extends Proposition 1.12
from virtually solvable groups to elementary amenable groups of finite Hirsch length. The
argument is short; the lane found it stated in none of 1607.03790, 1704.05304 or 2210.15746
(bounded check). The remaining elementary amenable case is infinite Hirsch length
(`bvc-elementary-amenable-groups-have-finite-hirsch-length`).
