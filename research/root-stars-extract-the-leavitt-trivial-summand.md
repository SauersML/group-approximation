A nonidentity element of a same-row or same-column root group over
`R_0` becomes a square-zero perturbation of identity in `M_(4t)(F_2)`
of rank between `1` and `t`. Its image, moving complement, and fixed
homology are all positive-rank free modules after scalar extension.
Prefix-code module compression gives an ambient conjugator to `u`.

For an arbitrary character, averages over finite subgroups of each
infinite elementary abelian group converge to its invariant projection
with trace `phi(u)`. Every root lies in both a row and a column group;
inclusion and equality of faithful traces identify their projections.
Connectedness of the twelve root incidences identifies all of them.

Each full elementary generator commutes with a root subgroup having
the same row and a spare column. Thus the common projection is central.
Simplicity makes the representation on it trivial. The complementary
normalized character has value zero on `u`.

For completeness, the artifact rederives the single-sandwich division,
two-spare-coordinate transvection conjugacy, proper-corner orthogonality,
small-cylinder localization, and infinite conjugacy orbit. These prove
that every character vanishing at `u` is regular on the full group.
No finite-core character classification is a prerequisite of this route.
