An alternating form on `W=F_2 direct-sum K` has a unique expression

```text
beta((c,x),(d,y))=c ell(y)+d ell(x)+B(x,y),                 (RMW1)
```

where `ell:K->F_2` is linear and `B` is alternating bilinear.  Its value on
the radial edge is the Boolean polynomial

```text
p_beta(x)=ell((1+alpha)x)+B(x,alpha x).                    (RMW2)
```

Suppose this vanishes for every `x`.  Polarization gives

```text
B(x,alpha y)=B(alpha x,y)                                  (RMW3)
```

for all `x,y`.  Since `alpha` generates `K` as an `F_2`-algebra, `(RMW3)`
holds with `alpha` replaced by every scalar in `K`.  If
`lambda(z)=B(z,1)`, take that scalar to be `y` and obtain

```text
B(x,y)=lambda(xy).                                         (RMW4)
```

Alternation says `lambda(x^2)=0` for every `x`.  Frobenius squaring is a
bijection of `K`, hence `lambda=0` and `B=0`.  Equation `(RMW2)` then gives
`ell=0` because `alpha!=1`.  Thus `beta |-> p_beta` is injective.  The
annihilator of the span of
`{w_x wedge w_(alpha x):x in K^*}` is zero, proving `(RPC4)`.

The polynomial `(RMW2)` has Boolean degree at most two.  If `beta!=0`, it is
nonzero by injectivity, so the minimum-distance bound for `RM(2,n)` gives
weight at least `2^(n-2)`.  Also `p_beta(0)=0`; restricting from `K` to
`K^*` therefore proves `(RPC5)`.

For the operator statement, commutators in a class-two group generated by
involutions are central involutions.  Split the tracial representation over
the joint spectrum of this finite central commutator algebra.  Each central
character gives an alternating form `beta` on `W`.  On a `beta!=0` sector,
`(RPC5)` says that at least one quarter of the sampled pairs anticommute, and
each such additive commutator has squared normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm
four.  Therefore the total trace `m_bad` of all nonabelian sectors obeys

```text
m_bad <= eta.                                               (RMW5)
```

Let `z_0` be the sum of the `beta=0` central sectors and define

```text
Q'_x=z_0 Q_x+(1-z_0)I.                                    (RMW6)
```

The `Q'_x` are commuting reflections on the same algebra.  Replacing a
reflection by the identity on a corner of trace `m_bad` costs at most
`4m_bad` in squared normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm, proving `(RPC8)`.
Homogeneous parity relations survive the replacement.

Finally `(RPC4)` makes the class-two commutator quotient zero, which is
`gamma_2(Gamma)=gamma_3(Gamma)`.  Repeated commutation gives
`gamma_r(Gamma)=gamma_2(Gamma)` for all `r>=2`; every nilpotent quotient
therefore kills this subgroup and is abelian.
