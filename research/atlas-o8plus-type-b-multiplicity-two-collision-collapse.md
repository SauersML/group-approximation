Let `H` be the natural eight-dimensional `F2` module restricted from the
canonical singular-parabolic copy of `Q=2^5:A5` in `Omega8+(2)`.  For either
marked Q tuple, choose the embedding twist with

```text
H|_A = 1^4 + U^2,      dim_F2 End_A(H)=20,      A=<r,u>~=S3,
```

where `U` is the simple two-dimensional `F2[S3]`-module.  On `H^2`, every
A-central operator `c` satisfying

```text
c^2=1,
(c t)^3=1,
t c s c t^-1 c s t c=1
```

is `c=1`.  Hence the protected collision subgroup `<t,s,c>~=S4` cannot occur
in either Type-B marked twist at multiplicity two inside `GL_16(2)`.

This is an exact bounded-rank exclusion.  It does not assert collapse on
`H^m` for arbitrary `m`; that all-multiplicity coefficient-algebra problem
remains open.
