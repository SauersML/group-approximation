Let `L` be countable with property (T), let `H` be a countable group
containing `L` and an element `s` with `L_0 = s L s^{-1} < L` of finite
index `m > 1`.  Put `X = L/L_0`, `P = (Z/2)^X semidirect L`, and

```text
G = H *_L P .
```

Then `G` is not MF.  If `H`, `L`, and the inclusion data are finitely
presented, so is `G`.

**Why it matters.**  No Clifford relation, no central scalar, no
`u^2 = -1`, no manufactured marked word: a moved coordinate automatically
supplies the radical element.  The central sign of the manuscript is
thereby demoted from fundamental mechanism to first example — the
finite-orbit projection obstruction is what remains when the sign is
quotiented away.
