Citation import, not a reproof. S. R. Lee, *Geometry of Houghton's groups*,
arXiv:1212.0257 (December 2012), Theorem D, as read from the arXiv HTML
rendering on 2026-09-13: "For any n≥3 the Dehn function δ_{ℋ_n}(x) of ℋ_n
satisfies δ_{ℋ_n}(x) ≼ e^x." Zaremsky's list (Problem 1.24) records the
same result: "Lee proved the Dehn function of H_n is at most exponential."

The proof uses the extension `1 -> Σ_(n,∞) -> H_n -> Z^(n-1) -> 1`, the
finite presentation of Theorem C, and an action of `H_n` on an
`n`-dimensional CAT(0) cube complex whose vertices are elements of the monoid
of injective eventual translations, with a height function whose level sets
are cocompact. No lower bound beyond the general quadratic one is proved
there.
