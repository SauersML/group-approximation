Replace every original selector by a logical product of two private shares.
After the common symplectic chart merge, condition the two-sided Pauli bridge
on the shared logical products and identify only the old and new private gauge
charts with its transverse `Z` and `X` maximal abelian subgroups. Equation
`(TPG4)` renews the gauges, preserves the fixed spin scale, and acts
nontrivially on the multiplicity commutant. The incidence-rounding theorem
then returns the corrected packet to the next actual Fanizza context with a
dimension-independent loss and exact marked completeness.
