title: The role-hyperoctahedral type realizes every hybrid row locally but its D8 amalgam does not saturate the common source
---

One tautological hyperoctahedral type contains the literal D8 role swap and
every Boolean hybrid truth-table row.  Yet two copies amalgamated only over
that D8 admit regular vertex representations whose selected tautological
carriers are orthogonal.

Local hybrid-row realizability, common D8 source, and canonical packet trace
therefore do not imply cross-context source saturation.
