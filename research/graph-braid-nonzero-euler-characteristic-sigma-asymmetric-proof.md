`B_nΓ` is of type F, with finite `K(π,1)` `UD_nΓ` for sufficiently subdivided
`Γ`. The Euler characteristic of the group is that of `UD_nΓ`, and
`cd B_nΓ ≤ min(n, k(Γ))` by Farley–Sabalka's retraction. Apply the general
obstruction with `d = max(1, min(n, k(Γ)))`. Details: artifact part 1, §3.
