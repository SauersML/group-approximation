title: Exact commuting finite-spectrum lifts of countable commuting torsion families
---

A countable commuting family of m-torsion unitaries in a norm matrix
corona lifts to sequences of exactly commuting, exactly m-torsion unitary
matrices representing the same corona classes.  (Inductive Boolean
averaging plus spectral cut for the spectral projections; eventual
exactness of the partition relations; trivial-partition fix-up on the
finitely many exceptional coordinates of each element.)
