Let `d >= 2` and `r >= 1`. `C_{d,r} = X_r × X_d^ω`, and a point of `C_{d,r}` is *rational* if it has the form `αβ̄`
with `α in X_r × X_d^*` and `β in X_d^+`.

**(Stab)** Let `S ⊂ C_{d,r}` be a finite set of rational points. Then `Fix_{V_{d,r}}(S)` is an iterated ascending HNN
extension of some `V_{d,n}`. In particular `Fix_{V_{d,r}}(S)` and `Stab_{V_{d,r}}(S)` have type `F_∞`.

**(Germ)** Let `s = αβ̄` with `β` not a proper power, and `s in S`. Then the group of germs `(Fix_{V_{d,r}}(S))_s` is
infinite cyclic. It is generated by the germ of any element that agrees with the prefix replacement `αψ ↦ αβψ` near `s`.
