The first-factor inclusion `J -> J tensor_min Z` is a KK-equivalence, so it
sends the assumed equality to

```text
[p tensor 1_Z]=[q tensor 1_Z] in K_0(J tensor_min Z).
```

Stable rank one implies cancellation of projections.  Hence these two
projections are stably Murray--von Neumann equivalent in `J tensor Z`, and
their classes agree in `Cu(A tensor Z)`.  Their classes were distinct in
`Cu(A)`, so the first-factor Cu morphism is not injective.
