title: Every finite square-zero packet fuses canonically after one matrix stabilization
---

Let `A` be a C-star algebra, let `x_1,...,x_n in A` be square-zero, and set

```text
I=Ideal(x_1)+...+Ideal(x_n).
```

There is an explicit square-zero `W in M_2(A)` with

```text
Ideal(W)=M_2(I).
```

Consequently every positive `h in I` satisfies
`h tensor e_11 in Ideal(W)`.  No representation-theoretic hypothesis on `A`
is used.
