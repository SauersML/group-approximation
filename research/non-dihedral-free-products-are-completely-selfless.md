Let `G` and `H` be nontrivial countable discrete groups.  Then

```text
(C*_r(G * H), tau_(G*H))
```

is completely selfless if and only if it is not the case that

```text
G ~= C_2 ~= H.
```

Thus every nontrivial free product other than the infinite dihedral group has
simple reduced C-star algebra, stable rank one, strict comparison, and unique
normalized 2-quasitrace.
