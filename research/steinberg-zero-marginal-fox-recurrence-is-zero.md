If `f:F_p^3 -> F_p` satisfies `(SCA4)` and `P=H=0`, then `f=0`.

## Attempts

When `P=H=0`, all five cut marginals vanish and the recurrence becomes a
local rational propagation system even on the exceptional loci.  It remains
to prove that the graph generated by the rational partial maps has no
nonzero invariant component avoiding every exceptional locus.  Ordinary
polynomial degree is not preserved by these maps.

The ambient zero-marginal space is now identified exactly by
`steinberg-zero-marginal-space-is-biaffine-circulation`: it is the
`p^3-2p^2+1` dimensional circulation space of the biaffine point-line graph.
The root shears `q,s` generate a Heisenberg group acting simply transitively
on its chord edges, but the Fox recurrence is a branched signed relation,
not invariance under those shears.
