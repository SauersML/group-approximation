For a transitive finite action, a twisted transformation-group algebra is
Morita equivalent to the twisted group algebra of a point stabilizer.  The
forbidden four-point radical orbit has trivial stabilizer, so its twist is
irrelevant and the algebra is `M_4`; tensoring with `M_(D_0/2)` gives
`(PVH2)`.

The satisfying orbit is one point with stabilizer `V_4`.  Its scalar
commutator bicharacter has rank zero or two over `F_2`.  In rank zero the four
one-dimensional projective characters give `C^4`; in rank two a Pauli pair
generates the unique simple algebra `M_2`.  These are `(PVH3)--(PVH4)` and
exhaust the scalar projective possibilities.
