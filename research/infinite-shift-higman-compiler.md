Let `P` be an **infinite** finitely presented group with decidable word
problem.  Then a bijection

```text
nu : P -> N
```

can be chosen so that the Mallery--Zaremsky group `E_nu(P)` is finitely
presented.  Here `E_nu(P) <= Sym(N)` is generated by the `nu`-transported
left-regular action of `P` on the even numbers, acting trivially on the odd
numbers, together with the eventually 2-periodic group `E_2`.

Mallery--Zaremsky prove that every such `E_nu(P)` is finitely generated and
strongly shift-similar; finite presentability for a cleverly chosen `nu` is
the open clause.  Their Example 3.31 explicitly assumes `P` infinite and
permits any bijection `nu:P->N`.

**Primary source.** Brendan Mallery and Matthew C. B. Zaremsky,
*Houghton-like groups from “shift-similar” groups*, J. Comb. Algebra **9**
(2025), no. 3/4, 169--208, DOI `10.4171/JCA/106`, arXiv:`2202.00822`;
Proposition 3.27, Theorem 3.28, and Example 3.31 (p. 194).  Source checked
2026-08-30.

## Attempts

- **Choose an algorithmically periodic enumeration.**  Decidability lets one
  enumerate `P` without equality collisions, but no construction is known
  that makes the infinitely many germ relations consequences of finitely
  many relations.
- **Drop infinitude.**  This is impossible rather than merely deferred: a
  finite group admits no bijection to `N`, as recorded by the established
  refuter of the original claim.
- **Deferred live lane.**  Use the word-problem algorithm to choose `nu` so
  the conjugates generated by the germ maps fall into finitely many recursive
  relation orbits, while preserving strong shift-similarity.
