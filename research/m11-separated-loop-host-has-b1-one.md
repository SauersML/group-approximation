**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Proof: `m11-separated-loop-host-has-b1-one-proof`. The proof is
by hand. The script checks the self-replication identity, the shape of `c'^m`, the loop
cocycle on random words and ends, and a short relation census.

**Setting.** `X = {1, …, 11}`, with right actions. `α = (1, …, 11)` and
`β = (1,2,3,4,8)(5,10,7,11,6)`, so that `⟨α, β⟩ = M_11`. `b = (b, α, β, 1, …, 1)` and
`R_0 = ⟨M_11, b⟩`, as in `tits-hosts-for-transitive-local-actions` item 6. Put

- `c' = (1, 1, 1, c', bα, 1, …, 1)`: `c'` at letter `4`, `bα` at `5`, trivial root;
- `R' = ⟨M_11, b, c'⟩`.

This is the design proposed at the end of `m11-odometer-free-host-is-not-finitely-presented`.

**Theorem.**

1. **Fully self-replicating.** `c'@4 = c' · ((bα)@5)^{−1} ∈ R'`. So `R'@x ⊆ R'` for every
   `x`, and `R' ≅ R' ≀_X M_11` with root image `M_11`. `R'` is generated by the finite
   automaton `{c', bα, b, α, β, 1}`, and it has linear activity, so it is amenable.
2. **Infinite order.** `c'^m = (1, 1, 1, c'^m, (bα)^m, 1, …)`, and `bα` has infinite order
   (`m11-odometer-free-linear-host-h1-is-z55-squared` item 1). So `c'` has infinite order and
   `R'` is **not contracting**.
3. **The ray-loop test passes.** The loop subgroups of
   `fp-self-similar-groups-have-fp-ray-loop-subgroups` are:
   - `⟨b⟩ ≅ C_55` at `1^∞`;
   - `⟨c'⟩ ≅ Z` at `4^∞`;
   - trivial at every other periodic ray.

   All of them are finitely presented.
4. **`H_1(R')` is infinite.** At `4^∞` the loop cocycle is finitely supported: its only
   nonzero value on a generator is `κ(c', 4^∞) = c'`. So the loop character of
   `fp-self-similar-finitely-supported-loops-give-characters` is the `c'`-exponent
   sum. It is a surjection `e : R' ↠ Z`. Hence
   `H_1(R'; Z) ≅ Z[c'] ⊕ ⟨[b]⟩`, with `[b]` of order dividing `55`, and `b_1(R') = 1`.
5. **Finiteness.** `R'` is not of type `FP_{10}` over `Q`, and not `FP_∞`. Neither is its
   simple Tits host `(R' ≀ M_12) *_{R'×R'} (R' ≀ M_12)`. `FP_2` is not obstructed by
   anything on the graph:
   - `b_1 = 1` meets the bound of `fully-self-replicating-fp-infinity-groups-are-acyclic`
     item 3(d), and one odd class gives no excess in degree 2;
   - the ray-loop test passes (item 3);
   - Bartholdi's theorem does not apply, and Σ-invariants, `L²` invariants and congruence
     quotients are blind.

   Finite presentation of `R'` is open.

**Answers to the three checks.** (1) Yes: fully self-replicating. (2) Yes: `c'` has infinite
order. (3) No: `H_1(R')` is infinite. The separation of loop rays that saves the ray-loop test
is exactly what makes the loop cocycle at `4^∞` finitely supported, and hence produces the
character.

**What is left for the Tits route of `boone-higman-conjecture`.** A candidate that can be
`FP_∞` must have infinitely supported loop cocycles, while keeping its loop groups finitely
presented. `m11-balanced-loop-host-has-finite-h1` gives such a candidate. It adds the exit
`c°^{−1}` at letter `6`, and this gives `H_1` finite, unconditionally.
