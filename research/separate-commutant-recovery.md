Recover the coordinate commutants `C_m` separately from the ultraproduct
commutant and compare with their compressed images. Dead: recovery succeeds
but at shifted scales — `alpha(K_m) ≤ K_{r(m)}` yields `C_{r(m)} ≤ D_m`,
not `C_m ≤ D_m`, so the separate recoveries never meet (shifted-scale-recovery). Successor:
scale-index-route.
