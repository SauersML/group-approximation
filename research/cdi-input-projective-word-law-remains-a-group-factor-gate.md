Let `N` be the fixed non-CE property-`(T)` relation factor in
`non-ce-property-t-equivalence-relation-exists`, and let

```text
M_tilde=(N*L(F_2)) tensor R
```

be the factor used as input to CDI Theorem A.  For either `X=N` or
`X=M_tilde`, existence of a countable unitary generating sequence satisfying
the all-word law

```text
tau_X(w)=0  or  |tau_X(w)|=1                           (CDI-I1)
```

is equivalent to `X` being trace-preservingly isomorphic to a twisted group
factor `L_omega(Gamma)`.

The known construction gives no verdict on this recognition problem.

- Manzoor and the property-`(T)` relation envelope construct `N` as a
  relation factor containing a non-CE subalgebra.  They do not construct a
  projective group basis or prove a von Neumann invariant excluding all
  twisted group factors.
- CDI Lemma 5.1 proves only that `M_tilde` is generated by three unitaries.
  The concrete standard choice audited in
  `cdi-standard-generator-choice-fails-projective-gate` violates `(CDI-I1)`,
  but failure for one generating tuple is not invariant under changing the
  tuple.
- CDI Remark 1.1 explicitly says that embedding universality of ordinary
  group factors remains open.  The broader twisted-group-factor recognition
  required here is likewise not settled by their wreath-like envelope.

If `(CDI-I1)` is achieved with a finite phase group, the finite central
extension compiler in `cdi-noncartan-bridge-has-two-exact-gates` immediately
produces a non-hyperlinear group.  With unrestricted phases it produces a
non-CE twisted group factor but leaves the torsion/finite-central gate open.
Thus “choose better generators” is not a soft refinement of CDI: it is
exactly the missing twisted-group-factor structure theorem.
