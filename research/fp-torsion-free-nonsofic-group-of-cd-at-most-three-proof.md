Keep the notation of `cd-two-group-with-infranormal-kazhdan-pair-proof`: `pi : G -> R^x`,
`Gamma = EL_alpha(R)`, `G_L = EL_D(R)`, `G_hat = pi^-1(G_L)`, `Gamma_hat = pi^-1(Gamma)`.

**The ambient group is the whole cover.**
- `openai-nine-leaf-leavitt-configuration` gives `G_L = EL_D(R) ~= EL_9(R)`, the image of
  `EL_9(R)` under the ring isomorphism `M_9(R) ~= R` defined by the complete prefix code `D`.
- `leavitt-gl-equals-el-and-perfect-unit-group` gives `EL_9(R) = GL_9(R)`, and that isomorphism
  maps `GL_9(R)` onto `GL_1(R) = R^x`. So `G_L = R^x` and `G_hat = pi^-1(R^x) = G`.

**Nonsofic.** By `cd-two-group-with-infranormal-kazhdan-pair`, `Gamma_hat` is infranormal and not
normal in `G_hat = G`, and both groups are Kazhdan. Kun--Thom Theorem A, as recorded in
`kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic`, makes `G *_(Gamma_hat) G` not sofic.

**Finitely presented.**
- `G` is finitely presented by `ollivier-wise-kazhdan-rips-construction`, since `R^x` is.
- `Gamma_hat` is Kazhdan, hence generated by a finite set `S`.
- A presentation of `D` is two copies of a finite presentation of `G`, together with the finitely
  many relations identifying `s` in the first copy with `s` in the second, for `s in S`.

**Torsion-free.** A finite subgroup of an amalgam fixes a vertex of its Bass--Serre tree, so it is
conjugate into a copy of `G`, which is torsion-free.

**Dimension.**
- `cd G <= 2` (`ollivier-wise-kazhdan-rips-construction`), so `cd Gamma_hat <= 2`.
- In the Mayer--Vietoris sequence
  `H^n(G;M) + H^n(G;M) -> H^n(Gamma_hat;M) -> H^(n+1)(D;M) -> H^(n+1)(G;M) + H^(n+1)(G;M)`,
  both ends vanish for `n >= 3`. Hence `H^k(D;M) = 0` for `k >= 4`, and `cd D <= 3`. `QED`

**Where dimension two fails to follow.** `H^3(D;M)` is the cokernel of
`H^2(G;M)^2 -> H^2(Gamma_hat;M)`. For a `G`-module pulled back along the fold map `D -> G`, this
is the cokernel of restriction `H^2(G;M) -> H^2(Gamma_hat;M)`. By Shapiro's lemma and right
exactness of `H^2(G;-)`, that cokernel is `H^2(G; CoInd_(Gamma_hat)^G M / M)`. It does not vanish
for every `M`: `compressed-doubles-raise-cohomological-dimension` exhibits a coinduced module where
it is nonzero, so `cd D = 3`.
