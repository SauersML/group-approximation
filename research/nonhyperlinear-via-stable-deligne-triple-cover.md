Let `g` be a genus given by `deligne-stable-triple-cover-is-not-hyperlinear`. The group `E_3^(g)` is
a central extension of `Sp_(2g)(Z)` by `Z/3`. `Sp_(2g)(Z)` is finitely presented, being an
arithmetic group, and an extension of a finitely presented group by a finite group is finitely
presented. So `E_3^(g)` is a finitely presented, hence finitely generated, non-hyperlinear group.
