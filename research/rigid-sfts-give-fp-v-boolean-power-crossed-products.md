**OPEN.** Gate (P2′a) of `v-times-sft-full-groups-split-into-two-wreath-pieces`.

**Statement.** Let `Λ` be finitely presented and `X ⊆ A^Λ` a free SFT that is `D`-quantum rigid over some field
for some `D` at least its window. Then `N_X = LC(X, V) ⋊ Λ` is finitely presented.

Together with item 4 of `v-times-sft-full-groups-split-into-two-wreath-pieces`, this would give the group-side
analogue of `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`: for a free SFT, `N_X` is finitely presented iff `X` is
quantum rigid.

## Attempts

1. **Local presentation (bh-free-29, 2026-09-18).** *Reduced to far-site conditioning.*
   - **Presentation.** Generators: `S` and `v^(a)` for `v ∈ S_V`, `a ∈ A`. Relations:
     - (R1) the relators of `Λ`;
     - (R2) the relators of `V` in each letter;
     - (R3) a finite presentation of the local group `L_D = < t_q v^(a) t_q^-1 : q ∈ B_D >`.

     `L_D ≅ V^(L_D(X))`, one factor per pattern on `B_D` that occurs in `X`. It is finitely presented, and it
     is generated by these elements by the commutator induction, since `V` is perfect.
   - **What the presentation gives.** Call the presented group `Ñ`. Every translate `q L_D q^-1 ≤ Ñ` maps onto
     the corresponding subgroup of `N_X`, and injectively.
     - Every quotient of `V^m` is some `V^j` with `j <= m`, since `V` is simple.
     - None surjects onto `V^m` with fewer factors.

     So all local groups embed.
   - **The missing step.** Show in `Ñ` that conditioning at far sites commutes. For `d(p, q) > 2D` one needs
     `[v^[a@p], w^[b@q]] = [v, w]^[a@p ∧ b@q]`, where the right side is a product of local atoms along a chain of
     overlapping windows.
2. **Linearize, then apply rigidity.** *Controls only the image in a ring.*
   - The idempotents are there: `σ^(a)` is an involution, so `(1 - σ^(a))/2` is an idempotent of `k[Ñ]`. In the
     permutation representation it equals `E_a P_-`.
   - With a finite simple `Q ≤ V`, the element `1 - avg(Q^(a))` equals `E_a ⊗ f` there.
   - But the partition of unity `Σ_a E_a = 1` and the orthogonality `E_a E_b = 0` are linear relations. They hold
     in the permutation module and not in `k[Ñ]`.
   - Imposing them gives a ring `k[Ñ]/I`. In its corner cut by `P_-`, the family should be a `D`-quantum family
     (Q1–Q3); this is sketched, not checked. Rigidity would then make it commute. That controls only the image of
     `Ñ` in `(k[Ñ]/I)^x`, which should be `N_X`.
   - What remains is injectivity of `Ñ -> (k[Ñ]/I)^x`. Group rigidity is therefore "ring rigidity + faithful
     linearization", and the second half is where a proof must add something.
3. **Where V helps.** Thompson self-similarity may supply the injectivity. The copy endomorphisms of
   `v-times-sft-full-groups-split-into-two-wreath-pieces` item 5 restrict to `Ñ`, and a swindle in the `C`
   coordinate moves any word to a cone where it is short. Not attempted.

**Lesson for general BH.** The ring side and the group side of the master route differ in exactly one point:
rings carry the linear relations (partition of unity, orthogonality) that let rigidity act on the universal
object, and groups do not. For the Boolean-power group, the transferable question is whether a group presented by
local rules embeds in the linearization where those rules become idempotent relations. Every
"rigid ⇒ group fp" theorem on the route (P2′, the `EL_n` gate) is an instance of this, Steinberg relations
are the standard tool for linear labels, and even there the gate is open.
