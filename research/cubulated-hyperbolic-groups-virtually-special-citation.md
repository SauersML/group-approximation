Citation import, not a reproof.

- I. Agol (with an appendix by Agol, D. Groves and J. Manning), *The virtual
  Haken conjecture*, Doc. Math. 18 (2013), 1045–1087, arXiv:1204.2810.
  - The arXiv abstract, read on 2026-09-13 by lane z2-19-hyperbolic-nv: "We
    prove that cubulated hyperbolic groups are virtually special."
  - The theorem number, Theorem 1.1, comes from the graph's earlier import
    `hyperbolic-cubulated-rf-citation`. It states that hyperbolic groups acting
    properly and cocompactly on CAT(0) cube complexes are virtually special.
    This lane did not re-read the PDF.
- F. Haglund and D. T. Wise, *Special cube complexes*, GAFA 17 (2008), show
  that the fundamental group of a special cube complex embeds in a right-angled
  Artin group. A compact special complex gives a finitely generated one. This is
  also recalled in Belk–Bleak–Matucci, arXiv:1602.08635, p. 2.

This route asserts only that the sources say what the claim records.
