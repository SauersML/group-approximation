Let

```text
R=L_(F_2)(1,2),  U=R^x.
```

Then `U` and `GL_n(R)` for every `n>=1` are mutually isomorphic.  For every
`n>=2` one also has

```text
GL_n(R)=EL_n(R).
```

Every group in this family is nontrivial, finitely generated, simple, has
property `(T)`, and has full MF radical.  Equivalently, every homomorphism
from any one of them to an MF group is trivial.  Every finite-dimensional
linear representation of any one of them over any field is also trivial.

Thus the non-MF phenomenon is intrinsic to the full unit group of the binary
Leavitt algebra and is independent of matrix rank; rank twelve is only the
smallest rank used by the displayed four-block compressor.
