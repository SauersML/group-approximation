title: The Klein bottle group embeds in Thompson's group T, hence in V, but not in F
---

Let `K = <a, b | b a b^{-1} = a^{-1}>` be the fundamental group of the Klein
bottle. Then:

1. `K` is torsion-free and solvable, so it has no non-abelian free subgroup;
2. `K` is isomorphic to a subgroup of Thompson's group `T`, hence of `V`;
3. `K` is not bi-orderable while `F` is, so `K` is not isomorphic to any
   subgroup of `F`.

The observation is Corentin Bodart's, recorded in Zaremsky's list (update of
April 3, 2026, to Problem 2.15). Written derivation with an explicit
embedding: route `klein-bottle-group-embeds-in-v-but-not-in-f-proof`.
