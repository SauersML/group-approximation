If the plain arithmetic double `(SNR1)` were hyperlinear, the equivalence
would make `L(SL_3(Z)) subset L(SL_3(Z[1/2]))` relatively embeddable over
`L(SL_3(Z))`, contrary to the first prerequisite.  Hence `(SNR1)` is an
explicit finitely presented non-hyperlinear group.
