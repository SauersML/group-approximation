**ESTABLISHED** through `isometric-frames-over-rank-one-bases-fail-the-k-budget-proof`. Lane proof, not
independently reviewed. Elementary, given the graded node, apart from Bass's cancellation theorem in item 3.
No priority is claimed.

**Setting.** `R` is a unital ring and `B ⊆ R` a unital subring with `Q ⊆ B`. There is an isometric frame
`s = (s_1, ..., s_k)`, `t = (t_1, ..., t_k)^T` with `s t = 1` and `p = t s in M_k(B)`, as in
`isometric-shift-frames-over-uhf-bases-fail-the-k-budget`. Assume:
- **(R1)** `K_0(B) ⊗ Q` is spanned by `[1_B]`, and `[1_B]` has infinite order. So `m[p] = n[1_B]` for unique coprime
  integers `m >= 1` and `n >= 0`, and the *index* is `r = n/m`.
- **(R2)** `B` has finite stable range `d`.

**Statement.**
1. **Index `r != 1`.** `(m - n)[1_R] = 0`, so `ker(Q^x -> K_1(R))` is not finitely generated. This is item 1 of the
   matricial node, which holds over any `B`.
2. **Index `r = 1`, stable form.** For `j = m d`, the module `(p B^k)^j` is free of rank `j`.
3. **Index `r = 1`, graded.** Suppose `R` is `Z`-graded with `B ⊆ R_0`, `s_i in R_1` and `t_i in R_(-1)`. Then `M_j(R)`,
   graded entrywise, has a unit `U` of degree `1`. Hence either `ker(Q^x -> K_1(R))` is not finitely generated,
   or `E_(jN)(R)` is not finitely presented for any `N >= 3`.
4. **Automatic grading.** The universal ring generated by `B` and a frame, with the frame relations `t_i s_j = p_ij`,
   `b s = s (t b s)`, `t b = (t b s) t` and `s t = 1` and relations inside `B`, is graded in this way. So is every
   quotient of it by homogeneous relations.

## Examples

- **`R_L`.** `B = Q[N][(N+c)^(-1)]` is a principal ideal domain, so `K_0(B) = Z`, and projectives are free. The frame
  `(s_1, s_2)` has `r = 2`, and item 1 gives `[1] = 0`.
- **Noncommutative register bases.** Weyl-type bases such as `A_1(Q)` and its Ore localizations, and localizations
  of enveloping algebras, have `K_0 = Z` by Quillen's theorem for filtered rings with regular Noetherian associated
  graded ring, and finite stable range (Stafford for `A_1`). These inputs were not read at source. Any frame over
  such a base falls under items 1--3. This covers register relations of `sl_2` type and those outside it, as long as
  the base has no idempotents beyond what (R1) allows.

## Scope

- **Closed.** Graded frame designs over bases with (R1) and (R2). This is gate Attempt 5, case 3, for rank-one
  bases.
- **Open.**
  - Bases whose `K_0` has rational rank at least `2`. That is the multi-type gap of gate Attempt 10 when the rank
    is finite. When the rank is infinite, it is the dilation case with never-top directions, from point indicators
    `δ_n`.
  - Ungraded simple quotients.
