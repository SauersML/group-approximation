**ESTABLISHED** through `isometric-frames-over-rank-one-bases-fail-the-k-budget-proof`. Refereed:
gq-referee-a PASS, proof-gap lens (report 128e1b392, N1--N3 applied); gq-referee-b PASS, citation lens
(report 5d2294421, W1--W3 applied: the rank restriction of item 3 is now explicit). Elementary, given the graded node, apart from Bass's cancellation theorem in item 3.
No priority is claimed.

**Setting.** `R` is a unital ring and `B ⊆ R` a unital subring with `Q ⊆ B`. There is an isometric frame
`s = (s_1, ..., s_k)`, `t = (t_1, ..., t_k)^T` with `s t = 1` and `p = t s in M_k(B)`, as in
`isometric-shift-frames-over-uhf-bases-fail-the-k-budget`. Assume:
- **(R1)** `K_0(B) ⊗ Q` is spanned by `[1_B]`, and `[1_B]` has infinite order. So `m[p] = n[1_B]` for some integers `m >= 1`
  and `n`, and the *index* `r = n/m` is unique (the pair `(m, n)` need not be coprime; gq-referee-a, N1).
- **(R2)** `B` has finite stable range `d`, which is Weibel's `sr` (Vaserstein's normalization, where a field has
  stable range `1`; Bass's `SR_(d+1)`)
  (gq-referee-a, N2).

**Statement.**
1. **Index `r != 1`.** `(m - n)[1_R] = 0`, so `ker(Q^x -> K_1(R))` is not finitely generated. This is item 1 of the
   matricial node, which holds over any `B`.
2. **Index `r = 1`, stable form.** For `j = m d`, the module `(p B^k)^j` is free of rank `j`.
3. **Index `r = 1`, graded.** Suppose `R` is `Z`-graded with `B ⊆ R_0`, `s_i in R_1` and `t_i in R_(-1)`. Then `M_j(R)`,
   graded entrywise, has a unit `U` of degree `1`. Hence either `ker(Q^x -> K_1(R))` is not finitely generated,
   or `E_(jN)(R)` is not finitely presented for any `N >= 3`. Only ranks divisible by `j` are excluded. The
   infinitely generated classes live in `K_2(M_j(R)) ≅ K_2(R)`, but they are not symbols of units of `R`, and a
   rank transfer would need finite stable range of `R`, which gate hosts lack (gq-referee-b, W2).
3a. **Free unit-class projectives.** Suppose moreover **(R3)**: `K_0(B) = Z[1_B]`, and every finitely generated
   projective `B`-module `P` with `[P] = [B]` is free. Examples are principal ideal domains and local rings. Then
   `j = 1`, and `R` itself has a unit of degree `1`. Either `ker(Q^x -> K_1(R))` is not finitely generated, or no
   `E_N(R)`, `N >= 3`, is finitely presented.
4. **Automatic grading.** The universal ring generated by `B` and a frame, with the frame relations `t_i s_j = p_ij`,
   `b s = s (t b s)`, `t b = (t b s) t` and `s t = 1` and relations inside `B`, is graded in this way. So is every
   quotient of it by homogeneous relations.

## Examples

- **`R_L`.** `B = Q[N][(N+c)^(-1)]` is a principal ideal domain, so it satisfies (R3). The frame `(s_1, s_2)` has
  `r = 2`, and item 1 gives `[1] = 0`. Every graded frame design over a localization of `Q[N]` is closed at all ranks
  by items 1 and 3a.
- **Weyl-type and enveloping-algebra bases.**
  - Filtered rings: Weibel, *The K-book*, Ch. V, Theorem 6.4 with Remark 6.4.1. If `gr(A)` is Noetherian of finite
    flat dimension over `k = F_0 A`, `k` has finite flat dimension over `gr(A)`, and `k` is regular, then
    `K(k) ≃ K(A)`. Example 6.4.2 treats `U(g)`. For `A_1(Q)` with the Bernstein filtration this gives
    `K_0 = Z[1]`.
  - Ore localizations `S^(-1) A` of such a Noetherian regular domain `A`. The localization sequence of `G`-theory
    gives a surjection `K_0(A) = G_0(A) -> G_0(S^(-1) A) = K_0(S^(-1) A)`. The rank over the Ore division ring of
    fractions sends `[1]` to `1`. So `K_0 = Z[1]` (gq-referee-b, W3; the localization theorem was not read at source).
  - Finite stable range: Stafford for `A_1` (not read at source). For its Ore localizations it must be checked.
  - Frames over these bases fall under items 1--3: the `sl_2` pattern and beyond. Item 3a needs (R3), which can
    fail: `A_1` has stably free non-free right ideals. So in general they are closed only at ranks divisible by `j`.

## Scope

- **Closed.** Graded frame designs over bases with (R1) and (R2), for the ranks `N` divisible by `j`; and at every
  rank over bases that also satisfy (R3). This is gate Attempt 5, case 3, for rank-one bases.
- **Open (new).** Ranks prime to `j` over rank-one bases without (R3).
- **Open.**
  - Bases whose `K_0` has rational rank at least `2`. That is the multi-type gap of gate Attempt 10 when the rank
    is finite. When the rank is infinite, it is the dilation case with never-top directions, from point indicators
    `δ_n`.
  - Ungraded simple quotients.
