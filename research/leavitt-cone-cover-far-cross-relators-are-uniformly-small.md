**Setting** (`leavitt-cuntz-cone-halves-have-rf-amalgam-cover`,
`leavitt-cone-head-bound-holds-for-all-ring-shadows`).
- `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, `q = s_1 t_1`, `R_+ = <s_1, s_2, q>`, `R_- = <t_1, t_2, q>`, `R_0 = R_+ ∩ R_- = F_2 1 + F_2 q`.
- `Gamma = Delta_+ *_(Delta_0) Delta_-`. Here `Delta_+-` is generated by the `x_ij(a)` with `a in R_+-`.
- `p : Gamma -> Delta = St_20(R)`, and `K = ker p` is the normal closure of `k_1, ..., k_r`.
- `[g, h] = g h g^(-1) h^(-1)`.

Let `(M, tau)` be a tracial von Neumann algebra, for example `M_d(C)` with its normalized trace. Write
`||x||_2 = tau(x^* x)^(1/2)`. Let `rho : Gamma -> U(M)` be any homomorphism, and put
`delta = max_i ||rho(k_i) - 1||_2`.

**Theorem.** There is a constant `C_1`, depending only on the fixed normal generators `k_i`, with the following
properties. It is independent of `rho`, `M`, the dimension and all ring elements.

1. **(U1) Block commutation.** Let `I, J` be disjoint subsets of `{1, ..., 20}` with `|I|, |J| >= 3`. Let `G_I^+` be
   the subgroup of `Delta_+` generated by the `x_ij(a)` with `i, j in I` and `a in R_+`, and define `G_J^-` likewise
   from `Delta_-`. Then

   ```text
   ||rho(g) rho(h) - rho(h) rho(g)||_2 <= C_1 delta        for ALL g in G_I^+, h in G_J^-.       (U1)
   ```

2. **(U2) All far cross relators.** Take `a in R_+`, `b in R_-` and index pairs `(i,j)`, `(k,l)` with `i != j`,
   `k != l`, `j != k` and `i != l`. These are the Steinberg-commuting pairs, including the case of the same root. Then

   ```text
   ||[rho(x_ij(a)), rho(x_kl(b))] - 1||_2 <= 4 C_1 delta.                                    (U2)
   ```

   If `{i,j} ∩ {k,l}` is empty, the bound is `C_1 delta`; if the pairs share one index, it is `2 C_1 delta`.

3. **(U3) Middle-index coherence.** Take distinct `i, j, k, l`, and let `(a, b)` lie in `R_+ x R_-` or in
   `R_- x R_+`. Then

   ```text
   ||rho([x_ij(a), x_jk(b)]) - rho([x_il(a), x_lk(b)])||_2 <= 5 C_1 delta.                  (U3)
   ```

One may take `C_1 = 4 N_0 / kappa^2`.
- `kappa > 0` is the minimum, over `3 <= m <= 17` and both signs, of the Kazhdan constant of `St_m(R_+-)`. It is
  taken with respect to the root generating set `{x_ij(c) : i != j <= m}`, where `c in {1, s_1, s_2, q}` for `R_+`
  and `c in {1, t_1, t_2, q}` for `R_-`.
- `N_0` is the largest number of conjugates of `k_i^(+-1)` needed to write one of the finitely many commutators
  `[x_ij(c), x_kl(c')]`. These run over generators `x_ij(c)` of some `G_I^+` and `x_kl(c')` of some `G_J^-`, for
  every pair of disjoint blocks `I`, `J`.

**Why it matters.** The survivor named by `leavitt-cone-head-bound-holds-for-all-ring-shadows` was a stability
statement for the relators of `ker Phi_P`: far cross commutators and the coherence of products `x_ik(ab)` taken
through different middle indices. There it was noted that these were "only `delta`-trivial". That meant one relator
at a time, with constants growing with the word length of `a` and `b`. By `(U2)` and `(U3)`, both infinite families
are `O(delta)`-trivial with **one** constant. So the survivor is now a genuine single-parameter stability statement:
a representation of `Gamma` in which every relator of these two families is `C delta`-trivial must have its head
within `O(delta)` of the head of a `St_20(P)`-representation.

## Class killed (refutation side of `(QC)`)

**Members.** Every proposed counterexample family to `(QC)` in which the relators `ker Phi_P` produce the gap
between `rho` and the ring-induced class through *long* ring words. In such a family, the generator-level relators
`k_i` are small, but `||[rho(x_ij(a)), rho(x_kl(b))] - 1||_2` or the middle-index incoherence of `(U3)` stays large
compared with `delta` for suitable `a in R_+` and `b in R_-` of large length.

One example is a twisted gluing `rho_U = (sigma_+, U sigma_- U^*)` of a ring shadow `sigma`, with
`U in sigma(Delta_0)'`, whose twist is tuned to be invisible on the generators but not on long words. By `(U2)`,

```text
delta(rho_U) >= (4 C_1)^(-1) sup_(a in R_+, b in R_-) ||[sigma(x_12(a)), U sigma(x_34(b)) U^*] - 1||_2 .
```

- *Invariant:* the Kazhdan constant `kappa` of the cone-half Steinberg blocks `St_m(R_+-)`, which is uniform over
  all unitary representations, together with the finite normal-closure length `N_0`.
- *Death step:* double averaging. The generator-level smallness of the finitely many commutators
  `[x_ij(c), x_kl(c')]` spreads by `(T)` first to all of `G_I^+` and then to all of `G_J^-`.

**Consequence for proof attempts.** A proof of `(QC)` may treat the two infinite relator families of
`ker Phi_P ∩ K` named by `leavitt-cone-head-bound-holds-for-all-ring-shadows` as if they were finitely many relators.
Each costs at most `5 C_1 delta`.

## Where it stops: Kazhdan averaging reaches only half-subgroups and their far pairs

The double-averaging argument needs a Kazhdan subgroup of `Gamma` that contains the moving elements. This is the
precise obstruction to going further.
- **Fact.** Every subgroup `H <= Gamma` with property `(T)` is conjugate into `Delta_+` or into `Delta_-`. Indeed,
  `(T)` implies Serre's property FA (`property-t-implies-property-fa`), so `H` fixes a vertex of the Bass--Serre tree
  of `Gamma = Delta_+ *_(Delta_0) Delta_-`. The vertex stabilizers are the conjugates of the halves.
- **Consequence.** For a block `I ⊇ {1,2,3}`, the mixed group `<G_I^+, G_I^->` (same block, opposite halves) is
  not Kazhdan. It contains `a = x_12(s_1) in Delta_+ \ Delta_0` and `b = x_12(t_1) in Delta_- \ Delta_0`. The product
  `ab` is cyclically reduced of length 2, so it acts hyperbolically on the tree and fixes no vertex. Yet the image
  `EL_I(R)` of this group in `Delta` is Kazhdan. Uniformity of `(U1)` type is therefore available on `G_I^+` and on
  `G_I^-` separately, but not on words `g_1 h_1 g_2 h_2 ... g_m h_m` with `g_r in G_I^+` and `h_r in G_I^-`, uniformly
  in the alternation length `m`.
- **What the ring-shadow bound consumes.** The proof of `leavitt-cone-head-bound-holds-for-all-ring-shadows` needs
  exactly such uniformity. Its ideal-domination step averages the head's block root group `U_(IK)` over the full
  left and right actions of `E_I(B)` and `E_K(B)`, the images of `<G_I^+, G_I^->` and `<G_K^+, G_K^->`. It also
  uses that the six defects generate `B` as a two-sided ideal. For a shadow ring of size up to `2^N`, this uses
  alternations of unbounded length.
- **What conjugation does here.** In a general gluing, conjugating `rho(x_IK(M))` with `M in M(R_+)` by `rho(g)` with
  `g in G_I^-` produces a *mixed* block element `rho(g x_IK(M) g^(-1))`. That element lies in neither half. `(U2)` and
  `(U3)` give its approximate Steinberg behaviour uniformly for one alternation. Iterating them gives no bound that is
  uniform in `m`.

**Next target (MW), uniform mixed-block coherence.** This is recorded as the natural next target. It is neither an
established reduction nor a proved equivalence with `(QC)`.

There should be a constant `C` with the following property. For every genuine `rho`, every block partition
`I ⊔ K`, and every word `w = g_1 h_1 ... g_m h_m` as above, of any length `m`, the unitary
`rho(w) rho(x_IK(M)) rho(w)^(-1)` depends, up to `C delta`, only on the image of `w` in `EL_I(R)` and on `M`.

- *How it can fail.* `(MW)` is falsified by a family of genuine representations, with `delta -> 0`, in which this
  dependence error grows with `m`.
- *What it would give.* It would give an approximate action of `EL_I(R) x EL_K(R)` on the head's block root group,
  with uniform defect. Feeding that into a rounding version of the ideal-domination averaging would be the
  remaining step to `(QC)`.
- *Status.* `(U1)`–`(U3)` control each single mixed entry `[x_ij(a), x_jk(b)]` uniformly. They do not prove
  `(MW)`, even for `m = 1`: two words with the same image in `EL_I(R)` can differ by a Leavitt reduction
  `t_i s_j = delta_ij`, and that reduction costs normal generators `k_i`.
