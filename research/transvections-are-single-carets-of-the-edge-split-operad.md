independently reviewed. No priority claimed.

## Setting

- **The space.** `Ŝ_m` is the rational Cantorization of `S^m` from
  `closed-mcg-embeds-in-rational-projective-full-group`. It is the disjoint union
  of the `2^{m+1}` orthant clopens `Δ_ε = cone(ε_0e_0, …, ε_me_m)`,
  `ε ∈ {±1}^{m+1}`.
- **The edge split.** Let `E` be the split of a unimodular cone
  `cone(w_0, …, w_m)` at the midpoint `w_i + w_j` of one of its edges. It gives
  two unimodular halves.
- **The operad.** `𝒪_E ⊂ 𝒪_m` is the suboperad generated by `E` and the
  transformations, the permutation matrices `S_{m+1} = Stab(Δ)`. Its operations
  are the dissections obtained by iterated edge splits of single pieces.
- **The group.** `G_E ≤ 𝒯_m` is the group generated by the homeomorphisms
  `θ'∘θ^{-1}`. Here `θ, θ'` are families of `𝒪_E`-dissections of all orthants
  with the same number of pieces, matched bijectively. If `𝒪_E` satisfies Ore's
  condition, `G_E` equals the set of such fractions, which is the operad group
  `π_1(𝒪_E, 2^{m+1}·Δ)`.

## Statement

1. **Transvections.** For `i ≠ j`, the transvection `t = I + E_{ij}`
   (`x ↦ x + x_j e_i`) is one fraction `θ'∘θ^{-1}`:
   - `θ` splits once, at the edge `(ε_ie_i, ε_je_j)`, every orthant with
     `ε_i = −ε_j`, and leaves the others whole;
   - `θ'` splits once, at the same edge, every orthant with `ε_i = ε_j`, and leaves
     the others whole.
2. **Signed permutations** permute the orthants linearly. They are fractions with no
   split.
3. **Consequence.** `GL_{m+1}(Z) ≤ G_E` for every `m ≥ 1`.
4. **The operad is strictly smaller than the unimodular cones.** Not every
   unimodular cone is a piece of an `𝒪_E`-dissection. Example:
   `T = cone((0,1,3), (1,1,1), (2,1,0)) ⊂ Δ`, which has determinant `−1`.
   - The rows of its vertex matrix are `(0,1,2)`, `(1,1,1)`, `(3,1,0)`. They are
     pairwise incomparable, so `T` lies in no half `{x_i ≥ x_j}` of any first split.
   - Equivalently, the monoid of nonnegative integral matrices of determinant `±1` is
     not generated by the nonnegative elementary matrices and the permutations. This
     is surely classical; it was found here by an MSI search.
   - The search found 73 such row triples with entries at most 3, and 2389 with
     entries at most 5; each count includes the permutation matrix.
   - Contrast `m = 1`: nonnegative `SL_2(Z)` is free on `L, R`, and every unimodular
     interval is a Stern–Brocot node.

## Proof of 1

- `t` fixes every `e_k` with `k ≠ j`, and `t(e_j) = e_i + e_j`.
- **Orthants with `ε_i = ε_j`.**
  - `t(ε_je_j) = ε_ie_i + ε_je_j` is the midpoint of the edge
    `(ε_ie_i, ε_je_j)` of `Δ_ε`.
  - So `tΔ_ε` is the half of `Δ_ε`'s split at that edge that contains `ε_ie_i`.
- **Orthants with `ε_i = −ε_j`.**
  - Let `ε'` agree with `ε` except that `ε'_i = ε_j`. Then `ε'` is a same-sign
    orthant, and every orthant falls into exactly one such pair `{ε, ε'}`.
  - Split `Δ_ε` at `w = ε_ie_i + ε_je_j` into `A ∋ ε_ie_i` and `B ∋ ε_je_j`.
  - Since `ε_i + ε_j = 0`, `t(w) = ε_je_j`. So `tA = Δ_ε`.
  - `t(ε_je_j) = ε'_ie_i + ε'_je_j`, so `tB` is the half of `Δ_{ε'}`'s split
    containing `ε'_je_j`.
  - By the first case, `tΔ_{ε'}` is the other half of that split.
- **Conclusion.**
  - On each pair, `t` maps (`Δ_{ε'}` whole, `Δ_ε` split once) onto (`Δ_ε` whole,
    `Δ_{ε'}` split once), piece by piece linearly.
  - Coordinates other than `i` and `j` are untouched.
  - Labels match up to transformations, because any element of `GL_{m+1}(Z)` that
    maps `Δ` onto `Δ` is a permutation matrix.
- **Part 3.** `SL_{m+1}(Z)` is generated by the elementary transvections, and
  `GL_{m+1}(Z)` also by one diagonal sign. Both are covered by parts 1 and 2.

## Calibration: `m = 1`

- Part 1 reads: `x ↦ x + 1` on the Farey circle is one caret on each pair of
  quadrants. This is the classical picture of `PSL_2(Z) ≤ T`, and of the parabolic
  shift on continued fractions from `train-track-trace-groupoid-hosts-closed-mcg`.
- Part 4 has no analogue at `m = 1`.

## Lesson for general BH

- **Linear groups act by one kind of caret.** On an arithmetic Cantor model, a
  linear group acts by carets of a single kind: transvections are carets. So
  "twists are splits" holds exactly, in every rank, for elementary matrices.
- **The host problem becomes group-free.** It separates from the group completely:
  the question is whether the one-generator operad `𝒪_E` has Ore's condition and
  finite type.
- **Consequence for BH.** If it does in rank `m + 1`, then `GL_{m+1}(Z)` lies in an
  `F_∞` Thompson-like group, by Thumann (arXiv:1409.1085).
- **The mapping class group case.** If `𝒪_E` is also *saturated* (its group is all
  of `𝒯_m`), the same holds for every group with a faithful
  piecewise-integral-projective action, such as `Mod(S_g)` on `PML`.
- **Why the old obstacle is gone.** The infinitely many primes of the full operad
  (`unimodular-dissection-operad-has-finite-type`) are irrelevant for linear groups,
  because the linear group never needs them.
- **The price is part 4.** `𝒪_E` does not see every unimodular cone. Ore's
  condition for `𝒪_E` is a genuine confluence statement, the local-confluence
  question of lane bh-free-61. It is not the automatic common refinement of `𝒪_m`.
