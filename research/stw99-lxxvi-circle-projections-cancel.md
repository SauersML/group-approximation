title: Projection bundles over the circle cancel for simple stable-rank-one fibers

Let `D` be a simple C*-algebra of stable rank one.  Then projections over

```text
C(T,D) tensor K
```

have cancellation: if `P directSum R` is Murray--von Neumann equivalent to
`Q directSum R`, then `P` is Murray--von Neumann equivalent to `Q`.

More precisely, a nonzero projection bundle over the circle is determined by
its fiber class in `V(D)` and its clutching component in the `K_1` group of
the corresponding full fiber corner.  Stable rank one makes the passage from
unitary components to `K_1` injective, so stabilization cannot erase the
clutching component.
