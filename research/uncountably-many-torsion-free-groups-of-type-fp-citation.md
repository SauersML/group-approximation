Citation import, not a reproof. Source: Ian J. Leary, *Uncountably many groups of
type FP*, arXiv:1512.06609, published in Proceedings of the London Mathematical
Society (DOI 10.1112/plms.12135). Abstract read from the arXiv abstract page on
2026-09-18.

Verbatim (abstract): "We construct uncountably many discrete groups of type
$FP$; in particular we construct groups of type $FP$ that do not embed in any
finitely presented group."

"Uncountably many" is up to isomorphism: the second clause needs non-isomorphic
groups, since there are only countably many finitely presented groups, each with
countably many finitely generated subgroups. The graph already records the
precise form on `zaremsky-1-20-uncountably-many-simple-fp2-groups` (Theorems 1.2
and 1.3: the `G_L(S)`, `S ⊆ Z`, realize `2^aleph_0` isomorphism types when `L`
is not simply connected, and are of type FP when `L` and its universal cover are
acyclic).
