Citation import, not a reproof. Source: Ian J. Leary, *Subgroups of almost
finitely presented groups*, arXiv:1610.05813v5 (26 Apr 2018), published in
Mathematische Annalen (DOI 10.1007/s00208-018-1689-5). Read from the arXiv PDF,
pages 1–2, on 2026-09-13.

Verbatim:

1. Abstract: "We show that every countable group embeds in a group of type
   $FP_2$."
2. Definition (p. 1): "A group G is almost finitely presented or FP_2 if its
   augmentation ideal I_G is finitely presented as a module for its group
   algebra ZG (see [3, VIII.5] or [2] for more details). Every finitely
   presented group is FP_2, and every FP_2 group is finitely generated."
3. "Theorem 1.1. Every countable group embeds in an FP_2 group."
4. "Theorem 1.3. For any fixed l ≥ 4 and any set S of integers with 0 ∈ S,
   there is an FP_2 group J = J(l, S) and a sequence j_1, ..., j_l of elements
   of J such that j_1^s j_2^s ⋯ j_l^s = 1 if and only if s ∈ S."
5. Introduction (p. 1): "Neumann had already shown that there are uncountably
   many 2-generator groups, from which it follows that they cannot all embed
   in finitely presented groups [9]."

The groups `J(l,S)` are the groups `G_L(S)` of Leary, *Uncountably many groups
of type FP* (arXiv:1512.06609), for a flag complex `L` with perfect fundamental
group containing a non-null-homotopic edge loop of length `l` (Leary's proof of
Theorem 1.3, p. 2).
