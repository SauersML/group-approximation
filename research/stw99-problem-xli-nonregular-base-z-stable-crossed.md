**Problem XLI of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(a "characterise" challenge): for outer amenable actions
`G ↷ A` on unital simple separable nuclear NON-`Z`-stable algebras,
when is `A ⋊ G` `Z`-stable?

## Attempts

* This is the regularizing-dynamics problem, dual to XXXVIII (which
  preserves regularity).  The two obvious invariant constraints frame
  any answer: `rc`-type obstructions of `A` must be absorbed, and by
  the Giol--Kerr/Villadsen calculus absorption is a comparison
  statement on `G`-orbits of the obstruction classes — a
  characterisation candidate is that the induced action on `Cu(A)`
  (equivalently on the rank/perforation data) has "dynamical
  comparison relative to the obstruction": every perforation witness
  is dominated by its own translates.  For `Z ↷` Villadsen-type `A`
  shifting the seed coordinates, this predicts `Z`-stability of the
  crossed product exactly when the shift mixes the coordinate Euler
  classes (the mod-`ell` thresholds of the XXVII lane give the
  refutable finite test); no such example is computed in the
  literature in either direction, making this the cheapest concrete
  experiment in the cluster.
