title: "The loop braided Thompson group V_loop of Witzel–Zaremsky is of type F_infinity"
---

Let `V_loop = T(LB_*)` be the generalized Thompson group of the cloning system
on the loop braid groups `LB_n = ΣAut_n` (Witzel–Zaremsky, arXiv:1405.5491v3,
Section 10, Theorem 10.2). Claim: `V_loop` is of type F∞, i.e. of type F_n for
every n. This is the first half of Witzel–Zaremsky Conjecture 10.3.
