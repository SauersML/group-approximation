**INVALIDATED DOUBLE-GAUGE ROUTE.**  Enlarge the marked Fano/Heisenberg
packet by finitely many mixed coefficient normalizers, and multiply the
known Singer-moving Whitehead by a second gauge-bearing outer occurrence so
that the two reservoir factors cancel while their total label word still
moves the signed Hecke source once.

`finite-extraspecial-mixed-words-cannot-ungauge-singer-transport` invalidates
this route for every finite enlargement in which the extraspecial packet
remains normal.  The outer reservoir factors multiply to the projective
operator indexed by the total quotient element, not according to parity of
the number of letters.  Moving `L_0` to `tL_0t^(-1)` forces that quotient
element to be nontrivial, and some exact marked representation then sees a
nontrivial multiplicity action.  Universal cancellation would force the
total quotient to be trivial and would preserve the wrong subgroup.

This leaves only a nonnormal opposite-root/full-Leavitt occurrence which
exits the finite extraspecial atom algebra.
