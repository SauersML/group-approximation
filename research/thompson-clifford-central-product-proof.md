The rational tail orbit of Thompson `V` is highly transitive by the finite
prefix-table construction, and its point stabilizer is finitely generated by
Belk--Hyde--Matucci.  De Cornulier's finite-presentation theorem therefore
makes `(directSum_X D_8) semidirect V` finitely presented.  Finitely many
relations centralizing the base local center under generators of `V` identify
all its conjugates.  Lamp normal form leaves exactly one central involution
and gives the group `VCl` in `(TCP1)--(TCP2)`.

The first-digit partition produces two commuting copies whose common
intersection is that involution; the prefix swap exchanges them.  The sites
`0^n10^infinity` give independent Pauli pairs with the same commutator and
linear address cost.  Finally the infinite Pauli tensor product and the
permutation crossed product give a faithful trace representation carrying
the common sign to `-I`.  These steps prove every clause of the target.
