There exists a unital simple separable nuclear non-elementary stably finite
C-star algebra without uniform property Gamma.  It may be chosen to be an AH
algebra of quadratic dimension growth.

Andrew S. Toms constructs such an algebra in Theorem 5 of
arXiv:2606.12188v2.  The displayed algebra is an injective inductive limit of
homogeneous C-star algebras, so it is stably finite; Proposition 4 and Theorem
5 give the other adjectives and the failure of uniform property Gamma.
