Let `D` be a finite maximal binary prefix code and let
`F<=Sym(D)<=V` be finite and transitive on the leaves.  Fix
`d_0 in D` and let `A<=V_[d_0]` be an arbitrary subgroup of the
localized Thompson copy supported inside `[d_0]`.  Then

```text
Gamma=<F,A> isomorphic to A^D semidirect F,             (LTW1)
```

where `F` permutes the `|D|` direct-product coordinates.  Moreover

```text
Gamma is hyperlinear   if and only if   A is hyperlinear. (LTW2)
```

For the 27-cylinder extraspecial qutrit packet, this closes every off-code
construction obtained by placing any collection of tail actors in one leaf
and taking their `F`-orbit.  It includes two noncommuting actors
`h,k`: their wrapper is

```text
<h,k>^27 semidirect F.                                 (LTW3)
```

Thus two tail actors help only if the one-leaf subgroup `<h,k>` is already
nonhyperlinear.  Free, amenable, residually finite, linear, and all other
known-hyperlinear choices give a hyperlinear wrapper, even when the proposed
overlap projections are arbitrary additive elements of its group algebra and
are neither conjugacy nor Morita transports.

More sharply, suppose qutrit cells conjugated by elements of `Gamma`,
together with any finite family of mixed group-ring overlap coefficients,
completed a unital algebraic realization of the fixed no-CE BCS.  Then `A`
would have to be nonhyperlinear.  Hence the localized-tail construction is a
reflection of the original root problem, not a reduction of it.

The conclusion persists under the cylinder-swap embedding into
`EL_4(L_(F_2)(1,2))` and the lifted Thompson embedding into
`St_n(L_(F_2)(1,2))`, `n>=5`.  A genuine next object must therefore
leave the entire localized-Thompson wreath basin: in the Steinberg lane it
must use a non-Thompson root/coefficient return, while in the prefix lane it
must contain a word which couples different leaf tails rather than merely
replicating one leaf subgroup.
