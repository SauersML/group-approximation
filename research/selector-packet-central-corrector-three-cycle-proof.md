Represent `C_2^2` as the direct sum of its four characters, select one
character line `qC^4`, and let the payload cycle that line through two other
character lines.  Distinct characters make the packet commutant diagonal.
Therefore left multiplication by any packet-central unitary preserves the
payload's three-cycle support, whereas an involution has only one- and
two-cycle support.  Explicitly `(SW)^2e_0` is a nonzero multiple of `e_2`,
not `e_0`.  The swap construction in `(PCC4)` separately verifies that an
unrestricted selected-projection stabilizer corrector still exists.
