**Source**, read at source: D. E. Roberson and S. Schmidt, *Solution group
representations as quantum symmetries of graphs*, arXiv:2111.12362v1
(24 Nov 2021), J. London Math. Soc. 106 (2022) 3379--3410.

- **Definition 3.1 (p. 4).**  `Gamma(M,b)` is generated by `x_i` and `gamma`,
  with these relations:
  - `x_i^2 = 1`;
  - `x_i x_j = x_j x_i` when both variables occur in a common equation;
  - `prod_(i: M_ki = 1) x_i = gamma^(b_k)`;
  - `gamma^2 = 1`, and `gamma` is central.

  `Gamma_0(M)` is the homogeneous case, with `gamma = 1`.
- **Definition 3.2 (p. 4).**  The colored graph `G(M,b)` has vertices
  `(k, alpha)`, where `alpha` is a local solution of equation `k`.  The vertex
  colour is `k`.  All edges are present between `V_l` and `V_k` when
  `S_l ∩ S_k` is nonempty, and the edge between `(l,alpha)` and `(k,beta)` has
  colour `alpha △ beta`.
- **Theorem 3.8 (p. 6).**  "Let `M in F_2^(m x n)` and `b in F_2^m`.  Set
  `G = G(M,b)` and `Gamma = Gamma(M,0) = Gamma_0(M)`. ... Then there exists an
  isomorphism `phi: C^*(Gamma) -> C(Qut(G))` such that
  `Delta_G o phi = (phi (x) phi) o Delta_Gamma`."
- **Definitions 6.3 and 6.5 (p. 24).**
  - `Iso(G,G')` is the universal C*-algebra of a magic unitary respecting
    vertex colours and all edge-colour adjacency matrices.
  - `A(M,b)` is the universal C*-algebra with generators `x_i` subject to
    `x_i^2 = 1`, commutation within equations, and
    `prod_(i: M_ki = 1) x_i = (-1)^(b_k)`.
  - The paper notes that `A(M,b)` "is isomorphic to the algebra
    `p C^*(Gamma(M,b))` ... where `p = (1-gamma)/2`, which may be trivial for
    some `M` and `b`".
- **Theorem 6.7 (p. 24).**  "Let `M in F_2^(m x n)` and `b != b' in F_2^m`.
  Set `G = G(M,b)`, `G' = G(M,b')`, and `A = A(M, b+b')`.  Then there exists
  an isomorphism `phi: A -> Iso(G,G')`."
- **Proposition 6.4.**  `G ≅_q G'` iff `Iso(G,G')` is nontrivial.  Footnote 3
  on p. 23 states that quantum strategies there are commuting-operator
  strategies.

**Derivation of the consequences.**

1. Tracial states and unital *-homomorphisms into `R^U` are preserved by the
   *-isomorphism of Theorem 6.7.
2. So colored `G(M,b) ≅_qc G(M,0)` (a tracial state on `Iso`) iff `A(M,b)` has
   a tracial state, which is tracial satisfiability of `Mx = b`.  Likewise
   colored `qa`-isomorphism (a unital *-homomorphism `Iso -> R^U`) is
   `R^U`-satisfiability.
3. With Paddock--Slofstra Proposition 5.8 (`lcs-tracial-ru-gap-equiv-nonhyperlinear`),
   the colored CFI gap is equivalent to a finitely presented nonhyperlinear
   group.
4. Theorem 3.8 identifies the quantum symmetry of each side with the dual of
   `Gamma_0(M)`.

The decolouring results of the same paper (Theorems 4.7 and 4.13,
Corollary 4.14) concern `Qut` of a single graph under degree conditions.
Their extension to isomorphism algebras between two graphs is not used here.
