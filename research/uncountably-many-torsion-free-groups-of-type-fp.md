**ESTABLISHED (literature import)** by `uncountably-many-torsion-free-groups-of-type-fp-citation`.

There is a family of groups of type FP over `Z`, realizing uncountably many
isomorphism types. Each is finitely generated and torsion-free.

Torsion-freeness is not stated in the imported sentence; it is the standard
consequence of type FP: a group of type FP has finite cohomological dimension,
cohomological dimension does not increase on subgroups, and a nontrivial finite
cyclic group has infinite cohomological dimension (Brown, *Cohomology of Groups*,
VIII.2, recalled, not re-verified). Type FP implies type FP_1, i.e. finite
generation.
