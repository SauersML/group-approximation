Apply `norm-corona-sparse-character-atom-reduces-pointwise-centralizer` to
the countable common central root group.  In its prescribed pointwise
centralizer put every root unitary of the Heisenberg subsystem and the two
branch actors.  The resulting nonzero projection `P` commutes with all of
them and carries a global character `lambda` which is nontrivial on the
surviving marked root.

Consequently `h |-> P rho(h) P` is a unital representation, with unit `P`,
of every finite Heisenberg window.  All windows use the same corner because
the projection was chosen once for the whole countable subsystem.  Apply the
finite-group algebra decomposition from
`finite-root-heisenberg-multiplicity-germs` inside `P Q_d P`.  Its central
matrix-unit projections, restriction decompositions, and integral branching
maps remain in that corner and retain their intrinsic coordinate rank germs.

Finally `P Q_d` is generated by the one projection `P`, hence is a nonzero
finitely generated projective right `Q_d`-module even when the normalized
coordinate ranks of `P` tend to zero.  No assertion about a coherent action
of the infinite coefficient ring follows from these finite decompositions.
