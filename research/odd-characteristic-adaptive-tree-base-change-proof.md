Collect the finitely many denominators in the Boolean and fixed packet
tables into `D`, choose an odd splitting characteristic prime to `D`, and
reduce the finite polynomial presentation.  For each named source, a
countable-coordinate model with scalar assignment signs, the integral Pauli
matrices, and `0,1` partial bijections realizes the complete finite tree and
keeps that source nonzero.  A finite direct product separates the finite
context packet menu.  The elementary-matrix image proves every resulting
nonzero root has exact additive order `p`.  Context absorption is an
integral word calculation and preserves finite wordization.  In the perfect
BCS model all forbidden sources vanish, so the new root and arrow words may
be sent to the identity while the external marked BCS/Pauli tuple is left
unchanged.
