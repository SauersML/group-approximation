Let `A` be an Artin group with unsolvable word problem. If `AV_A` embedded in a
finitely presented group, then (1)⇒(4) of
`self-twisted-bt-group-fp-embedding-iff-solvable-wp` would make the word problem of
`A` solvable. So `AV_A` embeds in no finitely presented group.

With `artin-unsolvable-wp-from-twisted-bt-without-fp-host`, this route records an
equivalence.
