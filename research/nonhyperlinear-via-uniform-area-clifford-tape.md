Use the finite presentation, common nontrivial sign, and uniformly bounded
table representatives supplied by the tape claim.  The collapse theorem then
gives `(BAC3)` in every finite matrix tuple, while the common sign is
nontrivial in the exact group.  Hence its output group is nonhyperlinear.
