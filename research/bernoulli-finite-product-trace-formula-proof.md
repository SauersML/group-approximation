Locality gives N(y) with finite support, and the rank formula gives
NT=1 and r_x(y−TN(y))=0 for all finite x. Product-trace invisibility
kills this remainder. A cylinder has product measure
∏_(v∈F_s)ν(s(v)), giving the displayed formula. With uniform measure
this is in ℤ[1/p]; on an infinite host cylinders on n distinct sites
realize p^-n for every n. See Section 5 of the artifact.
