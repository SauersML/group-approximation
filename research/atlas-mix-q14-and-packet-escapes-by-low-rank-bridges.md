Take arbitrary direct sums and passive tensor amplifications of the
q14-exact square-root bridge and the multiplicity-two packet-collision
escape.  Add finitely or increasingly many outer-product corrections whose
total rank is sublinear, and then use the linear action on the underlying
binary vector spaces as permutation-unitary Hilbert models.

This architecture is invalidated by
`atlas-q14-packet-bridge-mixtures-have-uniform-wall`.  Before the unitary
lift, one fixed packet residual and q14 have a joint normalized-rank floor
`1/12-o(1)`.  Under the natural permutation lift, every surviving nonzero
rank becomes macroscopic normalized-HS defect, and the resulting `A8`
carrier has the wrong representation type in any case.
