**Statement.** Let `F` be a unital ring, let `ρ_1, …, ρ_l ∈ F`, let `I` be the
two-sided ideal they generate, and let `n >= 4`. Then the natural map induces

    St_n(F) / ⟨⟨ x_12(ρ_1), …, x_12(ρ_l) ⟩⟩  ≅  St_n(F/I).

If `F = Z⟨g_1, …, g_d⟩` is free, then `St_n(F)` is generated by `x_ij(1)` and `x_ij(g_k)`,
because a monomial `ab` gives `x_ij(ab) = [x_ik(a), x_kj(b)]`. So a finite presentation
`⟨X | Rel⟩` of `St_n(F)` gives the finite presentation `⟨X | Rel, x_12(ρ_t)⟩` of
`St_n(F/I)`, with each `x_12(ρ_t)` written as a word through these commutators.

## Proof

1. **Quotient by the full normal subgroup.** Let `K` be the normal closure of all
   `x_ij(r)` with `r ∈ I`. Then `x_ij(r̄) -> x_ij(r) K` is well defined on `St_n(F/I)`,
   since `x_ij(r + r') = x_ij(r) x_ij(r')` with `x_ij(r') ∈ K`. It respects (R1)–(R3), and it
   inverts the natural surjection `St_n(F)/K -> St_n(F/I)`.
2. **One index pair suffices for ρ.** Let `𝒩` be the normal closure of `x_12(ρ)`. By (R2),
   with `k, l ∉ {1, 2}`:
   - `x_1l(ρ) = [x_12(ρ), x_2l(1)]` and `x_k2(ρ) = [x_k1(1), x_12(ρ)]`;
   - `x_kl(ρ) = [x_k1(1), x_1l(ρ)]` for `k ≠ l`;
   - `x_2l(ρ) = [x_21(1), x_1l(ρ)]` and `x_k1(ρ) = [x_k2(ρ), x_21(1)]`;
   - `x_21(ρ) = [x_23(1), x_31(ρ)]`.

   So `x_kl(ρ) ∈ 𝒩` for all `k ≠ l`.
3. **The whole ideal.** For `a, b ∈ F` and distinct `i, k, l, j` (this is where `n >= 4`
   is used), `x_ij(aρb) = [x_ik(a), [x_kl(ρ), x_lj(b)]] ∈ 𝒩`. Since `I` is additively
   spanned by the elements `a ρ_t b`, (R1) gives `x_ij(I) ⊆ ⟨⟨x_12(ρ_t)⟩⟩`. So the two
   normal subgroups are equal.
