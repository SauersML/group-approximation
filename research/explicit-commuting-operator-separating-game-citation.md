Literature import.  `arXiv:2001.04383`, Section 12.3 ("An explicit separation"),
Theorem 12.13 and the surrounding construction of the verifier `V^sep`, read at
source.  The remarks in the claim body about the proof being non-constructive
for the commuting-operator strategy, about the authors' conjecture that
`val^co(G_M)=1` for all non-halting `M`, and about the believed `10^20` bound,
are all the source's own, from Section 1.2 and Section 1.3.

The claim is stated for the single game `G^sep = V^sep_(C_0)`, where `C_0` is
the universal constant of their compression theorem; nothing here is asserted
for other members of the family.
