Notation is that of the target claim.  Commutators are
`[g,h]=ghg^-1h^-1`, and the Steinberg relations used are

```text
x_ij(a)x_ij(b)=x_ij(a+b),     [x_ij(a),x_jk(b)]=x_ik(ab)   (i,j,k distinct).
```

In characteristic two, `x_12(a)^2=x_12(2a)=1`, and `X_12(R_0)` is abelian.
So `L` is an elementary abelian 2-group.  It is locally finite, with
exhaustion `F_k=x_12(M_(2^k)(F_2))`.  The statement is proved for any
exhaustion.

## Step 0: (3) implies (1), and (1) implies (2)

If `Theta=1`, then `||V_n(l)-1||_op->0`.  Since
`||Ad A-Ad B||<=2||A-B||` on the Hilbert--Schmidt space,
`sigma~(l)=1`, so `P_k=1` for every `k`.

`sigma~` is a homomorphism (manuscript transport theorem), so `(WAV1)` is a
projection with `sigma~(l)P_k=P_k` for `l in F_k`.  If `F_k<=F_(k')`, then
`P_kP_(k')=P_(k')`, so `P_(k')<=P_k` and `P_k-P_(k')` is a projection.  Its
norm is therefore `0` or `1`, and a Cauchy sequence is eventually constant.

## Step 1: coordinate commutants

By item 1 of `corona-representations-are-exact-on-locally-finite-subgroups`,
there are unitary lifts `rho_n` of `Theta` and `m_n->infinity` such that
`rho_n|F_(m_n)` is a genuine representation.  In particular
`||rho_n(l)-V_n(l)||->0` for each `l in L`.  For `n` with `m_n>=k`, put

```text
P_(k,n)=(1/|F_k|) sum_(l in F_k) Ad rho_n(l)   on (M_(d_n)(C),||.||_2).
```

It is the orthogonal projection onto the commutant `rho_n(F_k)'`, because it
is the average of a genuine representation of `F_k`.  Since
`||Ad rho_n(l)-Ad V_n(l)||->0` for the finitely many `l in F_k`, the sequence
`(P_(k,n))_n` lifts `P_k`.

Suppose `P_k=P_K` for all `k>=K`.  Fix `k>=K`.  Then
`limsup_n||P_(k,n)-P_(K,n)||=0`.  For large `n`, `P_(k,n)<=P_(K,n)`
exactly, so `P_(K,n)-P_(k,n)` is a projection of norm `<1`, hence zero.
Thus

```text
rho_n(F_k)' = rho_n(F_K)'         in M_(d_n)(C),   n>=n(k).
```

Taking commutants in the finite-dimensional algebra `M_(d_n)(C)` gives the
bicommutant identity

```text
C*(rho_n(F_k)) = C*(rho_n(F_K)).                         (1)
```

## Step 2: finite image along every ultrafilter

`C*(rho_n(F_K))` is abelian and spanned by the joint spectral projections
`E_(chi,n)=(1/|F_K|) sum_(g in F_K) chi(g) rho_n(g)` for
`chi in F_K^`.  These are orthogonal, sum to one, and are at most `|F_K|` in
number.  By `(1)`, each `l in F_k` has `rho_n(l)` in this algebra.  Since it
is a self-adjoint unitary, it equals

```text
rho_n(l)=sum_chi eps_(chi,n)(l) E_(chi,n),        eps_(chi,n)(l) in {+1,-1}.
```

Fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter `omega` and let `Theta_omega` be the composite
of `Theta` with the quotient onto `prod_omega M_(d_n)(C)`.  The coefficients
take finitely many values, so `eps_chi(l)=lim_omega eps_(chi,n)(l)` exists,
and

```text
Theta_omega(l)=sum_chi eps_chi(l) [E_(chi,n)]_omega       (l in L).
```

Hence `|Theta_omega(L)|<=2^|F_K|`.  So

```text
I={a in R_0 : Theta_omega(x_12(a))=1}
```

is an additive subgroup of index at most `2^|F_K|` in `R_0`.

## Step 3: the ideal generated by I contains one

Let `c=[R_0:I]`.  For every `m`, `I cap M_(2^m)(F_2)` has index at most `c` in
`M_(2^m)(F_2)`.  Choose `m` with `4^m>c`; then there is a nonzero
`a in I cap M_(2^m)(F_2)`.  The matrix ring `M_N(F_2)`, `N=2^m`, is simple,
so

```text
1_N = sum_i b_i a e_i          (b_i,e_i in M_N(F_2)).
```

In `R_0`, the unit `1_N=sum_(|alpha|=m) s_alpha t_alpha` is the unit of `R`.

## Step 4: the normal closure is everything

Let `N=ker Theta_omega`, a normal subgroup of `Delta` containing `x_12(a)`.
If `h in N`, then `[g,h]=(ghg^-1)h^-1 in N` for every `g in Delta`.

* For `j` not in `{1,2}` and `b in R`, `x_1j(ab)=[x_12(a),x_2j(b)]`.  This
  lies in `N`, since `[x_12(a),x_2j(b)]=x_12(a)(x_2j(b)x_12(a)^-1x_2j(b)^-1)`
  and `x_12(a)^-1` lies in `N`.
* For `i` not in `{1,j}`, `x_ij(eab)=[x_i1(e),x_1j(ab)] in N`.
* Multiplying these with `(b,e)=(e_i,b_i)` from Step 3 gives
  `x_34(1) in N`.
* `x_k4(r)=[x_k3(r),x_34(1)] in N` for `k` not in `{3,4}`.  Then
  `x_kl(rs)=[x_k4(r),x_4l(s)] in N` for `k` not in `{3,4}` and `l` not in
  `{k,4}`.  In particular `x_5l(1) in N` for every `l!=5`, including
  `l=4`.
* `x_kl(r)=[x_k5(r),x_5l(1)] in N` for all `l!=5` and `k` not in `{5,l}`.
* `x_k5(r)=[x_k6(r),x_65(1)] in N` for `k` not in `{5,6}`, and
  `x_65(r)=[x_67(r),x_75(1)] in N`.

So `N` contains every generator `x_kl(r)`, and `Theta_omega=1`.  The norm of
the corona is `||[x_n]||=sup_omega lim_omega ||x_n||` over nonprincipal
ultrafilters.  So `Theta_omega=1` for all `omega` gives `Theta=1`.  This
proves (2) implies (3).

## Remarks on scope

* Only item 1 of the exact-lift theorem is used.  Stable finiteness and the
  one-sided order lemma are not used, and neither is Property `(T)`.
* The kill concerns norm limits in the adjoint corona `B`.  Step 1 transfers
  a norm statement in `B` into exact coordinatewise commutant equalities.
  Weaker convergence (strong, or Hilbert--Schmidt along individual
  sequences) gives no such equalities, and the transport proof cannot use it
  because stable finiteness needs an honest projection of `B` with
  `sigma~(L)P=P`.  Any such projection satisfies `P<=P_k` for all `k`.  This
  remark is commentary.  The established statement is the equivalence for
  the window-average sequence `(WAV1)`.
