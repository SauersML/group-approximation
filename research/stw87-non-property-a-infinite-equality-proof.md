Sako, arXiv:1212.5900, Theorem 1.1, proves that `C*_u(X)` is nuclear exactly
when `X` has property A.  Thus the algebra here is not nuclear.  Finite nuclear
dimension implies nuclearity, so its nuclear dimension is infinite.  If
`asdim(X)` were finite, the Winter--Zacharias upper bound would make the nuclear
dimension finite, a contradiction.  Therefore the asymptotic dimension is
also infinite.
