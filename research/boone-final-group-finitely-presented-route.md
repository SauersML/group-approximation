## Why sufficient

Induction along the fold that defines the tower.  The base case is the first
prerequisite: `BaseGroup` is `<t, x, y | xy = yx>`.

For the inductive step, a stage is `HNNExtension G_n A_n B_n phi_n`, so the
second prerequisite applies provided `G_n` is finitely presented (induction
hypothesis) and `A_n`, `B_n` are finitely generated.  The associated subgroups
of the machine stages are the `G_{ab}^{MM}` and `G_{c0}^{M^2,1}`, finitely
generated by `Gsub_eq_closure`; the associated subgroup of the final stable
letter is `<<t>>`, and that is the third prerequisite.  The list
`machineIdentifications mm` is finite -- one entry per residue pair carrying a
quadruple, and the machine's table is finite -- so the induction terminates.

The result is an explicit finite presentation: the three base generators, one
stable letter per quadruple and one final `k`; the base relator, and for each
stable letter one relation per generator of its associated subgroup.

## Not a restatement

The permanence statement is generic and says nothing about this tower; the
presentation of the base group says nothing about HNN extensions; finite
generation of `<<t>>` is a fact about one subgroup of one stage.  What the
route contributes is the induction along the fold and the finiteness of the
identification list.
