Borisov--Sapir, Theorem 1.2, proves residual finiteness of the mapping torus
of every injective endomorphism of a finite-rank free group.  A finite quotient
of the mapping torus separating a prescribed base word restricts to a finite
marked quotient of the free group.  Conjugation by the stable letter maps its
finite base image injectively into itself and hence bijectively, so the
endomorphism descends to an automorphism while the prescribed word survives.
