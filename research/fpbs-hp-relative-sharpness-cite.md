Citation import, not a reproof. T. Hutchcroft and M. Pan, arXiv:2409.12283v1,
Theorem 1.8: for a Cayley graph `G` of a finitely generated group `Gamma`, a
subgroup `H`, and each `p < p_c(H;G)`, there is `c(p) > 0` such that
`P_p(|K_o ∩ H| >= n) <= exp(-cn)` for every `n >= 1`. Read from the PDF on
2026-09-11. This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what the target
records.
