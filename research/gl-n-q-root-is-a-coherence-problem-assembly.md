Assembly. Items 1 and 2 of the target are the statements of the first two required claims.

Item 3 is the third claim applied to the ascending union `GL_n(Q) = ∪_m GL_n(Z[1/m!])`. The union is
exhausted by these terms, since the finitely many denominators of a matrix and of its inverse divide some
`m!`.

The target's heuristic reading is not part of the claim, and this route does not support it.
