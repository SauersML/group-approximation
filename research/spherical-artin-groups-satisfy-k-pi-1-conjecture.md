Let `(W, S)` be a Coxeter system with `W` finite, acting on `V = R^S` as a reflection group
with reflection hyperplanes `𝓗`. Then `(V_C − ⋃_{H ∈ 𝓗} H_C)/W` is a `K(A_W, 1)`.

Consequences: spherical-type Artin groups are torsion-free, and the finite Salvetti complex
is a classifying space, so they are of type F.
