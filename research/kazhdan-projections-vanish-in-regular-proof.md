title: Invariant square-summable vectors are constant on infinite cosets, hence zero

If `u_lambda xi = xi` for all `lambda in Lambda`, then `xi` is constant on
every coset `Lambda w`. An infinite coset forces `xi = 0` on it. Property (T)
with no invariant vectors gives a uniform spectral gap for the Kazhdan
Laplacian.
