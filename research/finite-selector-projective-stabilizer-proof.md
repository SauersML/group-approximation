For `(PSC1)`, projective equality preserves the finite support of `z`, so
left translation embeds the projective stabilizer in the symmetric group of
that support.  The selected coefficient `(PSC2)` lies in the finite context--
selector group algebra.  Hence `(PSC3)` or `(PSC4)` places the relevant
infinite subgroup inside a finite projective stabilizer.  With only
normalization, Schur decomposition leaves the unrestricted multiplicity
matrices in `(PSC6)`; `packet-covariant-kernel-gauge` supplies exact finite
regular choices whose kernel-commutant density tends to zero.
