L. Bowen, *A measure-conjugacy invariant for free group actions*, Ann. of Math. 171
(2010), 1387–1400. Main theorem: `f(alpha)` does not depend on the finite generating
partition, and for Bernoulli shifts it equals the base entropy. This is a literature
import. The elementary splitting monotonicity used alongside it is re-derived in
`research/artifacts/free-group-f-invariant-bernoulli-preservation-2026-09-12.md`,
Section 2.
