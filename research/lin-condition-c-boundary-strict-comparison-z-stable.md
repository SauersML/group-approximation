Let `A` be a separable non-elementary simple amenable C*-algebra whose
tracial cone has a basis satisfying condition (C) of Lin, *Tracial
oscillation zero and Z-stability*. Then `A` is Z-stable if and only if `A`
has strict comparison of positive elements.

Condition (C): the extreme boundary of the basis is a countable union of
compact finite-dimensional sets `X_n`, pairwise disjoint from index two on,
together with the closed-convex-hull compatibility conditions stated in the
source.

So Problem XVIII has a positive answer on this whole tracial stratum.
