For `q in Z(C[Gamma])`, its finite support is conjugation invariant.  The
group `H` generated by that support is therefore a finitely generated normal
FC-group.  Its torsion radical `K` is finite and characteristic, and `H/K`
is free abelian.  Decompose `C[K]` into `H`-orbits of irreducible blocks.
On each orbit, Clifford theory identifies the corresponding corner with a
matrix algebra over a twisted Laurent algebra.  Its center is the Laurent
algebra of the radical sublattice and hence is a domain, so a central
idempotent selects either the whole orbit or none of it.  Thus `q in C[K]`.
Centrality under `Gamma` makes the selected irreducibles a union of
`Gamma`-orbits, and applying
`finite-normal-type-corner-is-twisted-group-algebra` to each inertia group
gives the asserted finite direct sum and trace formula.  Full details are in
`research/artifacts/finite-support-central-corner-collapse-2026-08-21.md`.
