For every finite `d>=0`, every separable-predual II1 factor `(N,tau)`,
every `m>=1`, and every positive contraction `a` with
`m tau(supp(a))<1`, the Hilbert-sum weighted-copy fibre has the modulus

```text
rho_W(epsilon)=min(epsilon,1)^3/216000000.             (AD-4)
```

for filling `S^d` inside `B^(d+1)`. Every such fibre is `d`-connected for
every finite `d`, hence weakly contractible; for each finite `d` the
family is uniformly equi-`LC^d`.
