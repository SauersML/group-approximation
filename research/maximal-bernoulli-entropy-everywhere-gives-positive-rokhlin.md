Over a countably infinite group the uniform Bernoulli 2-shift is free and ergodic.
By the prerequisite its Rokhlin entropy is `log 2 > 0`.

This route and `positive-rokhlin-everywhere-gives-maximal-bernoulli-entropy`
record an equivalence. The dependency cycle is intended.
