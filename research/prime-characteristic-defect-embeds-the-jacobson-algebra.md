**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `p` be a prime, `R` a unital ring of characteristic
`p`, and `s,t in R` with

```text
ts = 1,      st != 1.                                             (PC1)
```

Then the unital ring map

```text
J_(F_p) = F_p<S,T | TS = 1> --> R,      S |-> s,  T |-> t         (PC2)
```

is **injective**, and therefore embeds `EL_n(J_(F_p))` in `EL_n(R)`
entrywise for every `n >= 2`.

Consequently, for each fixed prime `p` and each fixed `n >= 2`, the
universal non-MF question restricted to rings of characteristic `p` is
equivalent to the single case `EL_n(J_(F_p))`: any counterexample there
transports, and any positive answer there is inherited by every such `R`
carrying a nontrivial defect.

**Scope.**  This says nothing about characteristic zero, including rings
of characteristic zero whose defect has finite additive order; that case
is `finite-order-defect-subring-is-a-jacobson-fiber-product`.
