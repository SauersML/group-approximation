Let `Lambda = SL_3(Z) <= Gamma = SL_3(Z[1/p])`, `h = diag(p, 1, p^(-1))`,
and let `n_m -> infinity` run through integers coprime to `p`.  Put
`G_m = SL_3(Z/n_m)`, let `pi_m = lambda_(G_m) o (Gamma -> G_m)` be the left
regular representation of the finite quotient, and `pi = prod_U pi_m` into
the tracial matrix ultraproduct `M`.  Write `V'_m = pi_m(lambda_(h,m))` for
the congruence slot of `h` (`lambda_(h,m) in Lambda`, `= h mod n_m`).

**THEOREM.**

(i) `pi` is a regular-trace representation of `Gamma` whose
`Lambda`-restriction is exact of coprime type, and the corrector of
`odd-congruence-lambda-exact-sector-collapses` is `W = 1` on the nose
(`V'_m = pi_m(h)` for every `m`).

(ii) At every finite level `pi_m(h) in W^*(pi_m(Lambda))`, because
`Lambda -> G_m` is onto.

(iii) Nevertheless `E_(pi(Lambda)'')(pi(h)) = 0`; in particular
`pi(h) notin pi(Lambda)''` while `|| pi(h) ||_2 = 1`.

**Consequence.**  The identification `(RECF1)` in
`sl3-re-coprime-face-unfaithful-proof`,

```text
pi(h) = V' in pi(Lambda)'',
```

justified there by "`V'` is a `||.||_2`-limit of image elements of
`Lambda`", is FALSE: the image elements `[sigma_m(mu_m)]` of the sector
proof have VARYING `mu_m`, so they lie in `prod_U sigma_m(Lambda)''`, which
strictly contains `pi(Lambda)''` (generated by constant sequences).  What
`W = 1` delivers is the coordinatewise statement
`pi(Gamma)'' subset prod_U sigma_m(G_m)''`; what the unfaithfulness argument
needs is `pi(h) in B = prod_U B_k`, and pointwise near-containment
`dist_2(pi_k(g), B_k) -> 0` (`g in Lambda` fixed) yields only
`pi(Lambda)'' subset B`.  The route is therefore invalid, and
`sl3-re-lambda-exact-coprime-face-is-unfaithful` returns to OPEN; its
surviving conditional form is recorded in that node's Attempts.  (This is
exactly the "choose exact representatives in each coordinate" gate that
`gao-re-hecke-tower-proof` declines to cross.)
