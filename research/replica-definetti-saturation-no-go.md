**No-go.** The balanced trace-sharpening coefficient `|chi(g)|^{2m}` is already
the coefficient of a pure product vector across the `m` replica blocks.
Therefore a de Finetti theorem whose conclusion is only closeness to a convex
mixture of product states **across those replica blocks** has nothing left to
classicalize there.

This does not refute `definetti-group-table-rounding`. It refutes the naive
tensor split. Any surviving de Finetti proof must place Bose/symmetric
extension structure over the **assignment/context/path variables inside the
group-table encoding**, not merely over independent copies of the same
coefficient experiment.
