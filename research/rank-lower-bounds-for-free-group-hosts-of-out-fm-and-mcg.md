title: A host Aut(F_n) or Out(F_n) of Out(F_m) needs n at least m, and a host of a closed genus-g mapping class group needs n at least 2g minus 1

**ESTABLISHED.** No novelty is claimed. The derivation is
`rank-lower-bounds-for-free-group-hosts-of-out-fm-and-mcg-proof`.

1. If `m ≥ 2` and `Out(F_m)` embeds in `Aut(F_n)` or in `Out(F_n)`, then `n ≥ m`.
2. If `g ≥ 2` and the mapping class group of the closed orientable surface of
   genus `g` embeds in `Aut(F_n)` or in `Out(F_n)`, then `n ≥ 2g − 1`.

**Comparison.** The mapping class group of the genus-`g` surface with one boundary
component embeds in `Aut(F_{2g})` by Dehn--Nielsen--Baer, one rank above the bound
in item 2. The bound only constrains the rank: the constructions in Zaremsky
Problem 3.10 go through large ranks
(`aut-free-embeddability-passes-to-finite-index-overgroups`).
