The tower and clopen lemma supply the required scalar polynomial actors.
The extension lemma embeds the index-`h` subgroup in a finite product
of them, then the whole source in a finite wreath actor. Its action
retains faithfulness, oligomorphicity, and type `F_∞` finite-subset
stabilizers. Apply the classical BZ Theorem D and cited Theorem 3.4
as recorded in artifact §2. All new prerequisites are OPEN. The
multi-scaling variant recorded in the extension lemma additionally
requires its corresponding base and tower; it is not silently inferred
from the scalar prerequisites in this route.
