With `e_n` as in `(TDC1)`, define in `St_5(R_f)`

```text
P_n=x_12(e_n),       Q_n=x_23(e_n),       Z_n=x_13(e_n).     (THC1)
```

For every `n`, these elements generate the order-`p^3` Heisenberg group:

```text
[P_n,Q_n]=Z_n,
P_n^p=Q_n^p=Z_n^p=1.                                     (THC2)
```

For `n!=m`, the two cells commute elementwise. Their central root elements
are independent. Hence the subgroup generated by the first `N` cells is the
direct product of `N` finite Heisenberg groups.

The proof is the Steinberg relation

```text
[x_12(a),x_23(b)]=x_13(ab)
```

together with `e_ne_m=0`, same-root additivity, and injectivity of the root
maps. On a simultaneous central-character fiber where `r` of the `Z_n` are
nontrivial, every representation has dimension divisible by `p^r`.

This supplies a genuine capacity observable absent from bare additive-root
characters. It does not by itself yield nonhyperlinearity: matrix dimensions
may grow as `p^N`, and the hyperfinite infinite tensor product absorbs all
finite prefixes. A successful return must additionally give the strict
Schur/Fanizza backward coefficient in `(HST2)`.
