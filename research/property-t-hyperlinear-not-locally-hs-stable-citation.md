**Established by citation.**  Fournier-Facio--Gerasimova--Spaas, *Local
Hilbert--Schmidt stability*, arXiv:2307.13155v2, Theorem 1.5 / `thm:T`.

The theorem says that every infinite hyperlinear property-(T) group fails
local HS stability.  This is stronger than failure of ordinary strict
same-dimension HS stability.
