ESTABLISHED.  There are bounded-degree spectral expanders carrying exact
same-Hilbert reflection strategies which pass every vertex-local even-parity
view and every two-copy overlap consistency check, while a constant fraction
of the global coordinate reflections anticommute.

Thus an ordinary expander agreement graph does not turn a classical LTC into
the operator-valued decoder `(BTA1)`.  A viable higher-arity construction must
also have robust exterior-square agreement: two global code sections whose
local restrictions are everywhere proportional must be globally
proportional.  `local-exterior-square-kernel-yields-pauli-strategy` gives the
general obstruction and its necessary robust inequality.  This is the first
noncontextuality layer beyond classical syndrome expansion.
