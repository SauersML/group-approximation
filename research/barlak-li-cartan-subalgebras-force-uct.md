**ESTABLISHED (literature).**  Barlak--Li, *Cartan subalgebras and the UCT
problem*, Adv. Math. 316 (2017), 748--769 (arXiv:1511.02697v3):

* **Theorem 1.1.**  If `(G, Σ)` is a twisted groupoid with `G` étale, Hausdorff,
  locally compact and second countable, and `C*_r(G, Σ)` is nuclear, then
  `C*_r(G, Σ)` satisfies the UCT.
* **Corollary 1.2.**  A separable nuclear C\*-algebra with a Cartan subalgebra
  satisfies the UCT.  With Katsura and Yeend (their Remark 3.3), a Kirchberg
  algebra satisfies the UCT if and only if it admits a Cartan subalgebra.
* **Proposition 1.6.**  Let `A` be separable with a Cartan subalgebra `B`, and let
  a countable group `Γ` act on `A` leaving `B` globally invariant.  If
  `A ⋊_r Γ` is nuclear, it satisfies the UCT.
* **Theorem 1.4 (the `O_2` case).**  For the Cartan pair `(O_2, D_2)` and an
  action `α : Z_p ↷ O_2` with `p` prime, `O_2 ⋊_α Z_p` satisfies the UCT whenever
  some automorphism `γ` makes `γ(D_2)` regular in the crossed product; this is
  automatic if `γ(D_2)` is not a masa there or if `α(γ(D_2))` is inner conjugate
  to `γ(D_2)`.

Their introduction also records the reduction of Problem II to crossed products
of `O_2` by outer `Z_p`-actions with `p` prime (citing [1, Theorem 4.17]).
