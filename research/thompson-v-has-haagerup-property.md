**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Thompson's group `V` has the Haagerup property:
it admits a proper action by affine isometries on a Hilbert space.

Source: D. Farley, *Proper isometric actions of Thompson's groups on Hilbert
space*, Int. Math. Res. Not. 2003, no. 45, 2409--2414, which also covers `F`
and `T`. This graph already uses the same source in the scope sections of
`thompson-v-not-hyperlinear` and `thompson-v-hyperlinear`.

Two standard facts turn it into the form consumed downstream (Cherix,
Cowling, Jolissaint, Julg and Valette, *Groups with the Haagerup property*,
Progress in Mathematics 197, Birkhauser 2001):

- the Haagerup property passes to subgroups;
- a countable group with both property (T) and the Haagerup property is finite.

Consequently every subgroup of `V` is a-T-menable, and every subgroup of `V`
with property (T) is finite.
