Let `P=N semidirect A5` be the reduced universal Q1920 seam and let
`V=C2^5` be the characteristic normal module of its marked subgroup
`Q=2^5:A5`.  By definition `N` is the normal closure of `V` in `P`, and the
reduced-seam calculation puts `z` in `N`.

Now let `G` be any quotient after imposing the remaining full forward-fan
relation `(z b)^3=1`, and let `K` be the image of `N`.  In `G/K` one has

```text
z=1,       b^2=1,       (z b)^3=1,
```

so `b^3=b=1`.  Hence `b` lies in `K`.  The subgroup `K cap Q` is normal in
`Q` and contains both `V` and `b`.  The only normal subgroups of
`Q=2^5:A5` containing `V` are `V` and `Q`, while `b` is not in `V`.
Therefore `Q<=K`.  Since `P` is generated by `N` and `Q`, its full quotient
satisfies

```text
G=K=normal_closure_G(V).                                 (Q1920-NG-1)
```

In particular every nontrivial simple quotient `S` of `G` has nontrivial
image of `V`.  The kernel of `Q -> S` is normal in `Q`; the exact normal
subgroup orders `1,2,32,1920` and nontriviality on `V` leave only

```text
|image_S(Q)| in {1920,960}.                              (Q1920-NG-2)
```

Thus every finite faithful full completion has a nonabelian simple factor
containing the marked `2^5:A5` or its central quotient `2^4:A5`.  A simple
factor which sees only the `A5` quotient of `Q` is impossible, because it
would kill the normal generator `V` and hence the whole factor.
