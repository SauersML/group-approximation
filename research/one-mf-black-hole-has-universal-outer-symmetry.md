Let `B` be a nonabelian simple countable group with full MF radical, let the
involution `d` normally generate `B`, put `D=<d>`, and define

```text
K_X=*_(D,x in X) B_x
```

for a countably infinite set `X`.  Then `K_X` is countable, centerless,
perfect, has full MF radical, and is normally generated by the single
involution `d`.  Permuting its amalgam factors induces an injective
homomorphism

```text
Sym(X) -> Out(K_X).                                    (UOS1)
```

Consequently there is one fixed countable centerless perfect group
`K_infinity` with full MF radical, normally generated by one involution, such
that

```text
every countable group embeds in Out(K_infinity).       (UOS2)
```

Every countable permutation group `Q<=Sym(X)` lifts along `(UOS1)` to a
faithful action `Q->Aut(K_infinity)`.  The corresponding split extension

```text
K_infinity semidirectProduct Q
```

has MF radical equal to the inverse image of `Rad_MF(Q)` and has the same
complete MF semantic closure as `Q`.  In particular, if `Q` is MF, then
`K_infinity` is the characteristic MF radical and the universal MF quotient
is `Q`.

There are continuum many pairwise nonisomorphic countable split extensions
with this same characteristic kernel and with amenable abelian universal MF
quotients.

The infinite generation of `K_infinity` is forced by `(UOS1)`: the
automorphism group of a finitely generated countable group is countable,
whereas `Sym(X)` is uncountable.
