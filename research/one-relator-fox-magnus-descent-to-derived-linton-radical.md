ESTABLISHED.  Let

    G = F/W,              W = <<w>>_F,

be a finitely generated one-relator group, with `F = F(x_1,...,x_d)`.  Take
the canonical Linton data

    N = <<r>>_F,          R = N/W = G_Q^(omega),
    H = F/N = G/R,        w in r^k [N,N],       k >= 1.

Then the mod-`k` Fox--Magnus homomorphism has kernel exactly `R'` after it is
descended to `G`, and hence

    G/R'  embeds in  ((Z/kZ)^d) wr H,                         (1)

where the wreath product is restricted.  Consequently `G/R'` is sofic and
therefore hyperlinear.  Under this embedding the radical layer is

    R/R' ~= (Z/kZ)[H],

recovering [[linton-radical-abelianization-is-truncated-group-ring]] inside an
embedding of the whole quotient rather than as an isolated module
computation.

There is a stronger finite-shell conclusion.  If

    E = G_Q^(n) = R semidirect F0

is the shell supplied by
[[one-relator-soficity-localizes-at-rational-derived-stage]], then

    E/R' is residually finite.                                  (2)

Thus every element of `G` outside `R'` survives in a concrete sofic quotient.
This is a separation statement, not an extension-closure claim: neither
soficity of `R'` by itself nor soficity of `G/R'` is asserted to imply
soficity of `G`.

## Proof

Because `H` is residually rationally solvable, it is torsion-free.  Hence `r`
is not a proper power, by the torsion theorem for one-relator groups, and
Lyndon's identity theorem gives

    N/N' ~= ZH

as a `ZH`-module freely generated by `[r]`.

Put `M = (ZH)^d`.  The Magnus relation sequence for
`1 -> N -> F -> H -> 1` is

    0 -> N/N' --D--> M -> I_H -> 0,                            (3)

where `D` is the vector of Fox derivatives reduced to `ZH` and `I_H` is the
augmentation ideal.  Equivalently, the Magnus homomorphism

    mu : F -> M semidirect H,       f |-> (D(f), f-bar)

has kernel `N'`.  In particular `D` is injective on `N/N'`.

The image `D(N/N')` is `k`-pure in `M`.  Indeed, (3) identifies its quotient
with `I_H`, and `I_H` is an additive subgroup of the free abelian group `ZH`,
so it has no integer torsion.  Therefore

    D(N/N') intersect kM = k D(N/N').                          (4)

Since `w r^(-k) in N'` and the `H`-coordinate of `r` is trivial, the ordinary
Magnus embedding gives

    D(w) = D(r^k) = k D(r).                                   (5)

Reduce the module coordinate modulo `k`:

    M_k = M/kM ~= ((Z/kZ)[H])^d,
    mu_k : F -> M_k semidirect H,
    mu_k(f) = (D(f) mod k, f-bar).

Equation (5) gives `mu_k(w) = 1`, so `W <= ker(mu_k)` and `mu_k` descends to
`G`.

The kernel is exact.  If `f in ker(mu_k)`, its `H`-coordinate is trivial, so
`f in N`.  Write `[f] = a[r]` in `N/N' ~= ZH[r]`.  Then

    D(f) = a D(r) in kM.

By (4), `aD(r)` lies in `kD(N/N')`; injectivity of `D` therefore gives
`a in kZH`.  The converse is immediate.  Meanwhile the image of
`W = <<w>>_F` in `N/N'` is the `ZH`-submodule generated by
`[w] = k[r]`, hence exactly `kZH[r]`.  It follows that

    ker(mu_k) = N'W.                                           (6)

Passing to `G = F/W` gives

    ker(G -> M_k semidirect H)
      = (N'W)/W
      = (N/W)'
      = R'.                                                     (7)

Additively, `M_k` is the restricted direct sum over `H` of copies of the
finite group `(Z/kZ)^d`, and `H` acts by translation.  Thus

    M_k semidirect H ~= ((Z/kZ)^d) wr H,

which proves (1).  The group `H` is residually rationally solvable, hence
sofic.  Its regular action is sofic, and standard restricted-wreath
permanence makes the ambient wreath product sofic.  Soficity passes to the
subgroup `G/R'`; hyperlinearity follows from soficity.

For (2), restrict (1) to `E/R'`.  Its `H`-coordinate lies in the free subgroup
`F0 = E/R`, so

    E/R' embeds in M_k semidirect F0.                            (8)

The left `F0`-action on `H` is free, so `H` is a disjoint union of regular
`F0`-orbits.  To separate a nontrivial element of the group in (8), first keep
the finitely many orbits meeting its finite lamp support.  A nontrivial acting
coordinate is separated in a finite quotient of the residually finite free
group `F0`.  If the lamp coordinate is nonzero, choose a finite quotient
`F0 -> Q` that is injective on all pairwise differences among the finitely
many support positions in one nonzero orbit.  Push each retained orbit's lamp
function to `Q` by summing along quotient fibres, retaining distinct orbit
labels.  The chosen support points remain distinct, so the selected nonzero
lamp value cannot cancel.  This produces a finite wreath-type quotient
separating the element.  Hence `M_k semidirect F0`, and therefore its subgroup
`E/R'`, is residually finite.  QED.

## Exact boundary of the method

For `k > 1`, the theorem removes the entire finite-lamp abelian layer
`R/R' ~= (Z/kZ)[H]`.  For `k = 1`, the lamp module is zero and (1) reduces to

    G/R' = G/R = H,

because `R = R'` is perfect.  This is not merely a weak choice of modulus: the
ordinary Magnus map already has kernel `N'`, so first-order Fox calculus has
exhausted exactly the information it can retain.

Accordingly, a completion of [[linton-shell-sofic]] must use genuinely
nonabelian information from `R'` while preserving enough of the `F0`-action to
approximate `R semidirect F0`.  Abstract soficity of `R'` would not suffice,
because arbitrary sofic-by-sofic extension and semidirect-product closure are
false.  In the essential `k = 1` regime every solvable quotient of `R` is
trivial, so iterating abelian, solvable, rational-derived, or first-order
relation-module quotients cannot reach the remaining core.
