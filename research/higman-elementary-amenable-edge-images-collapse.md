**ESTABLISHED** by `higman-elementary-amenable-edge-images-collapse-proof`.

**Statement.** Let `Q` be a quotient of
`H4 = < a, b, c, d | b^a = b^2, c^b = c^2, d^c = d^2, a^d = a^2 >` (`x^y = y^-1 x y`). For `M >= 1` put
`A_M = <a^M, c^M> <= Q`, and let `A = A_1` be the image of `<a,c>`.

1. **Dichotomy.** For every `M >= 1`, either `A_M` is free of rank 2 on `a^M, c^M`, or `A_M / A_M'` is
   finite. In the second case there is `L >= 1` with `A_(ML) <= A_M'`.
2. **Main theorem.** Let `X_0` be the class of groups that are finite, or abelian, or locally indicable
   with no nonabelian free subgroup, and let `X` be its closure under extensions and directed unions. If
   `A` lies in `X`, then `Q = 1`. The same holds for the image of `<b,d>`.
3. **What `X` contains.**
   - every elementary amenable group (in particular every locally solvable group);
   - every amenable left-orderable group, and so every amenable group of orientation-preserving
     homeomorphisms of the line,
     such as the Grigorchuk–Machì group of intermediate growth, which is not elementary amenable;
   - every subgroup of `PL_+([0,1])`, including Thompson's group `F`, and every free-subgroup-free group of
     piecewise projective homeomorphisms of the line, such as Monod's non-amenable groups `H(A)` and the
     finitely presented Lodha–Moore group.
4. **Trees.** If `Q != 1` acts on a tree without inversions and every edge stabilizer lies in `X`, then
   `Q` fixes a vertex. So a nontrivial quotient of `H4` never splits as an amalgam or HNN extension over
   elementary amenable (or locally solvable, or amenable left-orderable) subgroups.
5. **Faithful tower invariants.** In either flavour, the tower invariants `S_r` (`r >= -1`) of
   `higman-bounded-derived-length-tower-chain-proof` separate the free group `Phi = <alpha, gamma>`:
   `S_r(u) = 0` for all `r` only when `u = 1`.

**Mechanism.**
- **Full types.** All tower invariants of a word are read off one state `(w, v)`, on which `alpha` and
  `gamma` act by `alpha: (w, v) -> (w + (1, [v]), v)` and `gamma: (w, v) -> (w, v + [w])`. A rank count
  shows the action is free, which gives item 5.
- **Chain.** Take `1 != u` in the kernel of `Phi -> A_M`, inside `Phi'`, and let `r` be its lowest nonzero
  level. Conjugate by a power of the kind-1 letter, apply `iota_b` or `iota_d`, and repeat. Each step
  lowers the level by one and keeps the word in the kernel. After `r + 1` steps one letter has a
  nonzero exponent and the other has exponent 0, so a power of that letter lies in `A_M'`.
- **Parity flip.** The commutator step of `higman-virtually-solvable-edge-images-collapse-proof` raises
  the level by one and stays in the kernel. So the other letter is handled too, and `A_M / A_M'` is
  finite. This gives item 1.
- **Power pairs.** A transfinite induction on the closure rank uses the dichotomy. A power pair `H` over
  a group in `X_0` with finite abelianization is finite, since finitely generated locally indicable
  groups map onto `Z`. Extensions pass finite images down to power subgroups. So some `A_M` is finite,
  and `Q = 1` by `higman-virtually-solvable-edge-images-collapse`.

**What it kills for `higman-group-is-sofic`.** Consider a glued construction `G_1' *_A G_1'` that
receives a nontrivial quotient of `H4`. Its edge image `A` is not free, and all of its power pairs have
finite abelianization. So `A` is neither elementary amenable nor amenable left-orderable. An amenable
edge image must now be **power-perfect**: for every `M` some `A_(ML)` lies inside `A_M'`, while `a` and
`c` have infinite order. It must also lie outside `X`. The natural remaining candidates are subgroups of
topological full groups (Juschenko–Monod) and other amenable groups with simple or perfect pieces.

**Calibration.** Take `Q = H4`. There `A_M` is free, which is the first branch. Now drop the Higman
relations, for example `<a, c | [a, c]>`. The kernel is nontrivial, yet `A/A' = Z^2`, so the dichotomy
fails. The relations are used only through the substitution lemma for `iota_b` and `iota_d`. Item 5 is a
statement about the free group alone; `state_action.py` confirms freeness of the state action on all
reduced words of length at most 10 (118097 states, pairwise distinct).
