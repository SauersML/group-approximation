**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Bekka--de la Harpe--Valette, *Kazhdan's Property (T)*,
Theorem 1.7.11: let `G` be a locally compact group and `C` a closed subgroup contained in
the centre of `G`. If `G/C` has property (T) and `G/[G,G]` is compact, then `G` has
property (T).

**Discrete form used in the graph.** Let `Γ~` be a countable group and `C` a central
subgroup. If `Γ~/C` has property (T) and `Γ~` has finite abelianization, then `Γ~` has
property (T).

Both hypotheses are necessary (source Remark 1.7.12(i)). For example, `Γ × Z` fails the
second.
