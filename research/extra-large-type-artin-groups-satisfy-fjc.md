Every extra-large type Artin group satisfies the K- and L-theoretic
Farrell--Jones conjectures with respect to the family of virtually cyclic
subgroups.

This conclusion is independent of whether the group has torsion. It does
not assert the A-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjecture, Baum--Connes, or the
Full Farrell--Jones package in Lueck's sense.
