Britton makes `[t,r]` nontrivial.  In a finite-dimensional representation,
the `SL_3(Z)` image is a congruence image and the profinitely dense subgroup
`<a,b>` has the same image.  Since the stable letter centralizes that image,
it centralizes `r`, killing the marked commutator.
