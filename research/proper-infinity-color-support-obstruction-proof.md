## Proof

Let `H` be an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space, `B=B(H)`, and `h=1_B`.
Two Hilbert-space isometries with orthogonal infinite-dimensional ranges show
that `h` is properly infinite.  If `p` is a rank-one projection, then
`0<p<=h`, while `pBp=Cp`; thus `p` is finite.  This is exactly the failed
heredity step encountered when an order-zero approximation introduces a
support which is only known to sit below a properly infinite element.
