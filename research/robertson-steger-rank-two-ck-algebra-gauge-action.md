**ESTABLISHED (import)** through `robertson-steger-rank-two-ck-algebra-gauge-action-citation`.

**Statement.** Assume (H0)–(H3). For every `t = (t_1, …, t_r) ∈ T^r` there is an
automorphism `α_t` of `𝒜` with

`α_t(s_{u,v}) = t^{σ(u) - σ(v)} s_{u,v}` for all generators `s_{u,v}`,

where `t^m = t_1^{m_1} ⋯ t_r^{m_r}`. The map `t ↦ α_t` is an action, and `α_t` fixes
every `s_{u,v}` with `σ(u) = σ(v)`.

In the Ã₂ setting of `robertson-steger-a2-boundary-algebras-are-rank-two-ck` (a lattice
`Γ` in `PGL_3(K)`, `K` nonarchimedean of characteristic zero, acting type rotatingly and
freely on vertices with finitely many orbits), (H0)–(H3) hold and `r = 2`.

Source: G. Robertson, T. Steger, J. reine angew. Math. 513 (1999), arXiv:1302.5593,
`CK1.tex` lines 864–872.
