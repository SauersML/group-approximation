Literature import. A. I. Malcev, "On isomorphic matrix representations of infinite groups",
Mat. Sb. 8(50) (1940), 405–422; textbook treatment in B. A. F. Wehrfritz, *Infinite Linear Groups*
(Springer, 1973), Chapter 4.

- *Proof idea.* The entries of the finitely many generators and their inverses lie in a finitely
  generated subring `A` of `k`, so `Gamma <= GL_n(A)`. A finitely generated commutative ring has
  maximal ideals of finite index intersecting to its nilradical. Reducing modulo these ideals, and
  handling the nilradical by congruence subgroups, separates every nontrivial element in a finite
  quotient.
- *Trust surface.* The statement as quoted. It is standard, and no proof is carried in this graph.
