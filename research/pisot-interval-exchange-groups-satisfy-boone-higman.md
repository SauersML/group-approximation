no priority claimed).

## Statement

Let `β > 1` be an algebraic unit with property (F), and `K = Q(β)`.

1. Let `Γ ⊆ K` be a finitely generated subgroup and `ℓ ∈ Γ`, `ℓ > 0`. Then the group `IE(Γ,ℓ)` of
   right-continuous interval exchanges of `[0,ℓ)` with breakpoints and translations in `Γ` embeds in a
   finitely presented simple group. So does each of its subgroups.
2. **Minimal exchanges.** For every minimal interval exchange `T` whose lengths lie in `K`, every subgroup of
   its topological full group `[[T]]` embeds in a finitely presented simple group. This includes its
   derived subgroup `[[T]]'`, which is finitely generated, infinite and simple when `T` codes a minimal
   subshift (Matui).
3. **Sturmian angles.** For every irrational `α ∈ K`, the Sturmian Juschenko--Monod group `IE(Z+Zα)'` embeds
   in a finitely presented simple group.

## Proof

- Choose `N ≥ 1` with `NΓ ⊆ Z[β]`, possible since `Z[β]` spans `K`.
- Rescaling by `N` gives `IE(Γ,ℓ) ≅ IE(NΓ, Nℓ) ≤ V(Z[β],⟨β⟩,Nℓ)`: slope 1, breakpoints in `Z[β]`.
- Apply `pisot-stein-groups-are-contracting-rsgs`, items 2 and 3.
- For item 2, `[[T]] ≤ IE(Γ_T, 1)`, where `Γ_T` is generated by `1` and the lengths, as in
  `quadratic-interval-exchange-full-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`.
- Item 3 is item 1 with `Γ = Z + Zα`. ∎

## Examples

- **Degree 2:** `K = Q(√(m²+4))`, the fields with a unit of norm −1. This agrees with the earlier degree-2
  nodes, now without Cleary's finiteness theorem.
- **Degree 3:** the tribonacci field `Q(β)`, `β³ = β² + β + 1`, carries interval exchange groups of rank 3.
  - It is also the field of the Arnoux--Yoccoz interval exchange, whose lengths lie in `Q(1/β)`. Its full
    group falls under item 2, provided it codes a minimal subshift. Its minimality is classical and was not
    re-read here.
- **Every degree `d`:** the d-bonacci field, root of `x^d − x^{d−1} − … − 1`.

## Not covered

- Real fields in which no unit satisfies (F).
- Transcendental parameters.
- Amenability of rank ≥ 3 interval exchange groups, which is a separate open question; nothing here uses
  or decides it.
