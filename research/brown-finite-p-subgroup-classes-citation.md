This is a citation import, not a reproof. Sources read on 2026-09-17, from arXiv PDFs saved and
text-extracted in the session scratchpad:

1. I. J. Leary and B. E. A. Nucinkis, *Some groups of type VF*, Invent. Math. 151 (2003)
   135–165, Example 7, verbatim:

   > Example 7. In Lemma IX.13.2 of [16], K.S. Brown showed that any group G containing a
   > finite-index torsion-free normal subgroup H such that H∗(H; Fp) is finite can contain only
   > finitely many conjugacy classes of p-subgroups. This homological hypothesis on H is
   > considerably weaker than insisting that H be of type FP over Fp.

   Here [16] is "Brown, K.S.: Cohomology of groups. Graduate Texts in Mathematics 87, Springer
   1982".

2. I. J. Leary, *On finite subgroups of groups of type VF*, Geom. Topol. 9 (2005) 1953–1976,
   introduction, verbatim:

   > K S Brown's book 'Cohomology of Groups' contains a result that implies that a group of
   > type VF can contain only finitely many conjugacy classes of subgroups of prime power order
   > [4, IX.13.2].

The two independent secondary sources agree on the lemma number and its content. The book itself
was not opened in this session. "H∗(H;F_p) is finite" means the total mod-p cohomology
`⊕_n H^n(H;F_p)` is a finite set.
