title: "The Torelli group of Out(F_3) is not finitely presented"
---

`T_3 = ker(Out(F_3) -> GL_3(Z))` is not finitely presented; in fact
`H_2(T_3; Z)` is infinitely generated. This is Krstić–McCool (1997); a second
proof comes from the Bestvina–Bux–Margalit Main Theorem. The import is
`outer-torelli-rank-three-not-finitely-presented-citation`.

Role in Zaremsky Problem 3.1: it shows why the problem asks about "large enough"
ranks. Together with `ia-n-fp-iff-outer-torelli-fp` it gives
`ia-automorphism-group-rank-three-not-finitely-presented`.
