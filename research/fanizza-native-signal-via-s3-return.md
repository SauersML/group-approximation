For fixed `m`, compile each occurrence of the three machine-independent
nonlinear predicate shapes into its S3-equivariant radical packet.  Exactify
the finite packets, use the linear local payment theorem on every forbidden
carrier, and transport only after the fresh-context reset.  The original BCS
involutions, including `d_m`, remain the shared words; they are not replaced
by packet-center labels.

The finite BCS has a computable finite number of contexts.  Consequently the
fixed local exactification, payment, overlap, and telescoping constants sum to
computable `A_m,B_m`; no uniform bound in `m` is needed.  The local payments
give `(NSG1)`, while exactifying the original involution relator for `d_m`
gives `(NSG2)`.

For completeness, start with any exact BCS representation.  The context-local
packet extension retains `d_m`, and every reset/HNN incidence embeds the
already constructed base.  Hence the membership GNS representation with
`d_m!=I` survives in the final group.  The sole unresolved global input is
`s3-radical-chart-returns-to-fresh-context`; the Boone carrier interface and
the polynomial tape runtime are absent from this route.
