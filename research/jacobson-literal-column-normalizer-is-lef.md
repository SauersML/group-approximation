Let `J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>`, `Q=1-ST`, `I=JQJ`, and
`N=(J,+)^2`. Let `K_lit<=GL_2(J)` be generated by the constant
`GL_2(F_2)` and `h=[[Q,S],[T,0]]`. The actual subgroup

```text
H_lit=N semidirect K_lit
```

is finitely generated and LEF, hence MF. Its finite residual and exact
finite-dimensional unitary residual coincide and are

```text
I^2 semidirect GL_fin(F_2^(Z)),
```

a nontrivial perfect locally finite normal subgroup. Thus `H_lit` is
not residually finite and is not finitely presented.

Even cyclic models realize the original actor action near one boundary
and the inverse-transpose action near the other. Every fixed literal
actor relation is eventually exact. Increasing finite-column evaluations
of the faithful unilateral module extend these models to the whole
additive column; every nonzero translation is retained at norm distance
`2`. All actor relations are included in this result.

This is the actual two-row column normalizer inside the coherent
Jacobson group, with the third-row Weyl and opposite-column relations
absent. It proves that those two-row relations cannot force the marked
column to vanish in matrix coronas. It does not decide the unrestricted
coherent matrix gap or produce the requested non-MF group.
