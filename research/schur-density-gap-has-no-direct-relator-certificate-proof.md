The quotient of the free group algebra by the two-sided ideal generated by
`r-1`, `r in R`, is `C[Gamma]`. Hence `(DRC2)` would map to `G_f=0` in
`C[Gamma]`.

But `(CDF2)` maps to `(DRC1)`, and `P_f` is nonzero by hypothesis. Since
`2D` is invertible over `C`, `G_f` is nonzero, a contradiction.

When the selector subgroup and `J` embed, nonvanishing of `P_f` is especially
transparent: its expansion is a nonzero Fourier idempotent (or sum of
orthogonal Fourier idempotents) on the finite Boolean subgroup, and distinct
group elements form a linearly independent basis of `C[Gamma]`. Thus direct
ideal payment can occur only after the ambient group has algebraically
collapsed the relevant selector support, which is not the desired robust
finite-dimensional-only obstruction.
