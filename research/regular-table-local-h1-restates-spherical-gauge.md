**ESTABLISHED.**  At the qualitative asymptotic level of the spherical
program,

```text
regular-table defects are trivial in local nonabelian H1
```

means exactly that geometrically local source corrections exist and kill all
tested multiplication defects modulo `o(N)` roots.  By the exact
permutation gauge law and diagonalization, this is equivalent to the finite
tolerance conclusion of
[[spherical-short-holonomies-admit-local-gauge]].

Consequently [[regular-table-defects-are-short-edge-h1-trivial]] is not an
independent input to the gauge theorem.  It is a cohomological renaming of
the qualitative target.  The route
[[regular-table-local-h1-gives-spherical-gauge]] is therefore circular and
cannot count as progress toward Q3.4.

This result does **not** prove or refute the spherical gauge theorem.  A
noncircular route must construct the local zero-cochain, for example by an
explicit bounded defect-word filler or by an independently proved
matching-selection theorem.
