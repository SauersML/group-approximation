Let `w in Q * <t>` be nonsingular and `K = (K(Q,1) v S^1) cup_w e^2`. If
`K` is aspherical, the coefficient map
`H_2(Q; Z) -> H_2((Q * <t>)/<<w>>; Z)` is injective: the Schur kernel is
zero.

A nonzero Schur kernel therefore needs a non-aspherical relative
presentation, that is, an identity among relations whose coefficient content
is homologically nontrivial.
