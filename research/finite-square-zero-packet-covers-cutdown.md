title: Finitely many square-zero ideals cover every prescribed cutdown
---

Let `A` be a C-star algebra with no nonzero finite-dimensional
representations.  Given `a in A_+` and `epsilon>0`, there are finitely many
elements `x_1,...,x_n in A`, each satisfying `x_i^2=0`, such that

```text
(a-epsilon)_+ in Ideal(x_1)+...+Ideal(x_n).
```

Here a one-element packet with `x_1=0` is allowed when the cutdown vanishes.
No separability, nuclearity, unitality, or condition on the primitive ideal
space is assumed.
