## Statement

**Setting (Corollary 4.5 hosts over an abelian base; artifact §6).**
- `A` is an abelian group.
- `μ: A -> A` is injective, with `A/μA` finite and `∩_k μ^k A = 0`.
- `Γ <= Aut(A)` satisfies `γ(μ^k A) = μ^k A` for all `k`.
- The twist is `σ(γ) = μ^(-1)γμ`, and `σ(Γ) <= Γ`.

**Hypothesis (EP).** There are integers `m >= 0` and `P >= 1`, a finite-index
`Γ_0 <= Γ`, and `δ in Aut(A)` with `δ(μ^k A) = μ^k A` for all `k`, such that

```text
σ^(m+P)(γ) = δ σ^m(γ) δ^(-1)   for all γ in Γ_0.
```

**Theorem.** Under (EP), `A x| Γ` embeds in a host `A' x| Γ'` satisfying setting (L_Z)
of `untwisted-abelian-affine-hosts-have-polynomial-word-problem`. The host is
`A' = (μ^m A)^s`, with `s = |A/μ^m A|`. Hence every finitely generated subgroup of
`A x| Γ` has word problem decidable in time `C*l^C + C`, and in `F(2^n)`.

**Finite-state form.** Let `Γ` be generated by a finite set `S`, and suppose every
`s in S` has a finite `σ`-orbit `{σ^k(s) : k >= 0}`. Then (EP) holds with `δ = id`,
`Γ_0 = Γ`, `m` the largest preperiod and `P` the lcm of the periods.

## What it kills and what survives

- **Killed.** Every Corollary 4.5 host over an abelian base in which the generators have
  finitely many `σ`-states, even with preperiodic or non-injective `σ`. The same holds
  for every host whose twist becomes periodic up to a filtration-preserving conjugation
  on a finite-index subgroup. Artifact Proposition 6.1 is the case `m = 0`.
- **Meaning for `fp-self-similar-groups-with-arbitrarily-hard-word-problem`.** A hard
  witness of the form `A x| Γ` with abelian `A` has to be *infinite-state in a strong
  sense*: no power `σ^P` agrees with `Ad(δ)` on any `σ^m(Γ_0)`. The artifact's heuristic
  boundary "infinite-state transducers" is thus the exact boundary for abelian bases.
  A Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir group with word problem outside `F(2^n)` can be a
  Corollary 4.5 host over an abelian `A` only through such a twist. An example would be
  `σ` acting on the metabelian quotient by an automorphism of infinite order in `Out`.
- **Survives.** Twists with some generator of infinite `σ`-orbit, not virtually inner
  after any delay; non-abelian `A`; self-similar groups not of the form `A x| Γ`.
