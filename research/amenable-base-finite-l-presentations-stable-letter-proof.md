Fix one index and suppress it.

For (LNS1), the normal subgroup defining (L) contains
(langle!langle Qangle!angle). Therefore the identity on (X)
induces an epimorphism
[
B=F(X)/langle!langle Qangle!angle	woheadrightarrow L.
]
Amenability passes to quotients, so (L) is amenable. Notice that this
argument uses no invariance of the (L)-presentation: the substitutions may
fail to induce endomorphisms of (B), and adding all of their relator
iterates still only enlarges the normal subgroup by which (B) is
factored.

Now consider (LNS2). If there is no stable letter, it presents
(B/langle!langle Rangle!angle), hence an amenable group.

Suppose there is one stable letter (t), and let (A) be the subgroup of
(T) generated by the images of (X). The map (B	o A) is onto, so
(A) is amenable. For every word (win F(X)), the generator relations
give
[
t^{-1}w(X)t=sigma(w)(X)
]
in (T), by induction on word length. Thus (t^{-1}Atleq A), whether or
not (sigma) descended to (B) before the extra relations forced its
stable kernel.

Put (A_n=t^nAt^{-n}) for (ngeq0). From (t^{-1}Atleq A) one gets
(A_nleq A_{n+1}). Hence
[
N=igcup_{ngeq0}A_n
]
is a subgroup and a directed union of amenable groups, so it is amenable.
It is invariant under conjugation by (t): conjugation by (t) shifts
(A_n) to (A_{n+1}), while conjugation by (t^{-1}) shifts (A_n) to
(A_{n-1}) for (ngeq1) and sends (A_0) into (A_0). It is also
normalized by (A), since (Aleq N). As (T=langle A,tangle), this
proves (N	riangleleft T). The quotient (T/N) is generated by the image
of (t), so it is cyclic. Amenability is closed under directed unions and
extensions, proving (T) amenable.

This argument identifies the stable-kernel firewall without having to
compute the kernel. In the special case where (sigma) already induces an
endomorphism (phi:B	o B) and (R) is empty, that kernel is
[
K_infty=igcup_{ngeq0}ker(phi^n),
]
and (T) is the injective ascending mapping torus of (B/K_infty), as in
[[genuine-hnn-switches-over-amenable-bases-are-sigma1]]. The preceding
directed-union proof also covers the more general non-invariant
presentation.

Finally suppose there are (sgeq2) stable letters. Define a map on the
displayed generators by
[
xlongmapsto1quad(xin X),qquad
t_ilongmapsto u_iquad(1leq ileq s),
]
where (u_1,ldots,u_s) freely generate (F_s). Every relator in (Q) or
(R) maps to (1), and every conjugacy relator maps to
(u_i^{-1}1u_i=1). The presentation therefore induces an epimorphism
(T	woheadrightarrow F_s). Since (F_s) is nonamenable for (sgeq2)
and quotients of amenable groups are amenable, (T) is nonamenable.

Thus (LNS3) depends only on the finite integer (s). Uniformly in an input
code (e), the predicate (s(e)leq1) is decidable, so the amenable locus
is recursive ((Delta^0_1)); for (LNS1) the locus is all of
(mathbb N). If a computable many-one reduction sent
(mathrm{TOT}) to either locus, it would decide (mathrm{TOT}), a
contradiction. This proves both the algebraic and arithmetical assertions.
