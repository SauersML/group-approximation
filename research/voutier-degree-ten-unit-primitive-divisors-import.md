Apply Theorem 2(i) on page 253 of Paul M. Voutier, *Primitive divisors of
Lucas and Lehmer sequences, II*, J. Théor. Nombres Bordeaux **8** (1996),
251–274, with `a=u`, `b=1`. Section 3 of the artifact records the theorem,
its [primary source](https://www.numdam.org/item/JTNB_1996__8_2_251_0.pdf),
and the bound comparison. A primitive ideal divisor of `u^n-1` makes the
reduction of `u` have exact order `n`. The theorem applies in the ambient
number field and its bound depends on the degree of the ratio over `Q`.
