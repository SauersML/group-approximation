Let `B` be a countable group with `Rad_MF(B)=B`, and let `d in B` normally
generate `B`.  Put `D=<d>`.  For every countable group `Q`, define

```text
C_d(Q) = B *_D (Q x D),
```

where the second edge map is `x |-> (1,x)`.  There is a split epimorphism

```text
pi_Q:C_d(Q)->Q
```

which kills `B` and projects the second vertex onto `Q`.  It satisfies

```text
ker(pi_Q)=normalClosure(d).
```

More generally, for every group `T` satisfying `Hom(B,T)={1}`,
precomposition with `pi_Q` is a natural bijection

```text
Hom(Q,T) ~= Hom(C_d(Q),T).                         (C1)
```

In particular this holds for every MF group and for the unitary group of any
norm matrix corona.  Thus `C_d(Q)` and `Q` have exactly the same complete
outgoing MF representation functor, even though `d` remains nontrivial in
`C_d(Q)`.

The rank-twenty Hilbert-hotel group supplies a finitely presented pair
`(B,d)` with these properties.  Hence `C_d(Q)` is finitely presented whenever
`Q` is finitely presented.
