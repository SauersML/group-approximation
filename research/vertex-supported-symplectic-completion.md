Notation as in `minimal-marked-graph-nondegenerate`:
`V = F_2^{(X)}`, `b = b_{S_min}`, and `V_F = span{δ_x : x ∈ F}` for finite
`F ⊆ X`.

1. **Completion.**  For every finite `F ⊆ X` there is a finite `F' ⊇ F` with
   `b|_{V_{F'}}` nondegenerate and

   ```text
   |F' \ F| = dim Rad(b|_{V_F}) ≤ |F|,     hence |F'| ≤ 2|F|.
   ```

   Each added point is an actual vertex of `X`, obtained as a private
   neighbour of the support of a radical vector; adding it drops the radical
   dimension by exactly one.

2. **Vertex-supported exhaustion.**  Consequently `X` is the increasing union
   of finite sets `F₁ ⊆ F₂ ⊆ ⋯` with every `b|_{V_{F_n}}` nondegenerate, each
   `V_{F_{n+1}} = V_{F_n} ⊕ V_{F_n}^⊥` an orthogonal symplectic splitting, and

   ```text
   C*_σ(V_{F_n}) ≅ M_{2^{|F_n|/2}}(ℂ)
   ```

   at the central character `z ↦ −1`, the inclusions being tensor-factor
   inclusions.  So `A_{S_min}` is a `2^∞`-UHF algebra exhausted by full matrix
   Clifford blocks generated by graph vertices, with hyperfinite `II₁` tracial
   closure.

3. **Exact finite lamp microstates.**  For every finite lamp window `F` there
   are `D ≤ 2^{|F|}` and self-adjoint unitaries `W_x ∈ M_D(ℂ)`, `x ∈ F`, whose
   commutation signs are `(−1)^{b(δ_x,δ_y)}` **exactly** and for which every
   nontrivial Weyl word has normalized trace `0` — the canonical twisted-group
   trace on that window, matched exactly rather than approximately.

**Why it is recorded separately.**  It is what removes finite lamp
factoriality and finite trace realization from the list of things a
microstate construction for the minimal graph has to arrange.  After it, the
only unmet demand in the finite-window form of
`minimal-marked-graph-crossed-product-ce` is coherence of the implementers.

## Scope

The completions are not `G`-invariant and cannot be made so: by the
finite-rank invariant-form no-go (`notes/NOTEPAD.md`) no finite-rank
`G`-invariant alternating form retains the marked edge, and by
`marked-double-coset-infinite-degree` no invariant graph containing the mark
has finite degree.  Item 3 is a statement about lamps only; it supplies no
unitary implementing any element of `G`.
