Let `E_N` be the finite extraspecial Pauli group generated by a central
involution `J` and involutions

```text
X_1,Z_1,...,X_N,Z_N
```

with `[X_i,Z_i]=J` and all other pairs commuting.  Suppose
`f:E_N->U(d)` is an all-pairs `epsilon`-homomorphism `(GHS1)`, where
`epsilon<1/2`, and

```text
||f(J)-I||_2 > 42 epsilon.                                    (MCT1)
```

Then

```text
d >= (1-4epsilon^2) 2^N.                                     (MCT2)
```

The constants are independent of `N`.  Thus if a finite controller, after
being told `d`, emits the complete table of `E_N` for

```text
N > log_2(d/(1-4epsilon^2)),                                  (MCT3)
```

and retains `(MCT1)`, that same `d`-dimensional model is impossible.
