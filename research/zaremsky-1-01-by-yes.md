Let `n >= 2` and let `G` be of type `F_n`. Then `G` is finitely presented, so
by the required claim it embeds in a group `H` of type `F_∞`. This answers the
second part affirmatively, and since `H` is of type `F_{n+1}` it answers the
first part affirmatively too.
