Immediate. `rover-nekrashevych-bnsr-invariants-are-full` describes the character
sphere of `V_d(G)` for every finitely generated self-similar `G`. It also gives
`Σ^m(V_d(G))` for every `m` for which `V_d(G)` has type `F_m`. That is the
computation Problem 1.14 asks for.
