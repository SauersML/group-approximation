Let `B_f` be a Schur predicate packet over an odd finite splitting field
`k`, let `P=P_f` be its forbidden central idempotent, and put

```text
I=P k[B_f],                 C=P k[B_f^Q] subset I.       (BST1)
```

Collapse the allowed packet ideal to its augmentation and retain the complete
forbidden algebra:

```text
Bsharp=k(1-P) direct_sum I ~= k direct_sum I.            (BST2)
```

The packet group still maps to the units of `Bsharp` by
`g |->(1-P)+Pg`; this map retains every forbidden simple block.

On each forbidden selector sector the corrected child/full inclusion has

```text
I_x ~= M_(2D)(k),          C_x ~= M_D(k) direct_sum M_D(k).
```

After choosing the standard relative-Pauli matrix units there is therefore an
effective unital injection

```text
phi:I -> M_2(C) subset M_2(I),
phi_x(a)=(a,a) in M_(2D)(k) direct_sum M_(2D)(k).         (BST3)
```

Equivalently, writing `a=[a_rs]` in
`M_2(M_D(k))`, the `(r,s)` entry of `phi_x(a)` is
`diag(a_rs,a_rs)` in `C_x`.

Define the packet-headed Toeplitz algebra

```text
Rsharp=Bsharp *_k k<x,y>/(yx=1, 1-xy=P).                 (BST4)
```

For `a in I`, write

```text
hat_phi(a)=sum_(0<=r,s<2) x^r phi_rs(a)y^s.
```

Then the assignments

```text
Sigma(x)=x^2,                 Sigma(y)=y^2,
Sigma(lambda(1-P)+a)=lambda x^2y^2+hat_phi(a)             (BST5)
```

define a proper injective unital endomorphism of `Rsharp`.  In particular

```text
Sigma(P)=P+xPy=1-x^2y^2.                                  (BST6)
```

The ideal generated by `P` has the exact normal form

```text
<P> ~= M_fin(N,I),          E_ij tensor a |->x^iay^j.     (BST7)
```

Under `(BST7)`, `Sigma` replaces every matrix entry by the `2 by 2` block
`phi(a)` and replaces the site index `i` by the binary pair `2i,2i+1`.
Thus its restriction to `<P>` is injective.  The quotient by `<P>` is
`k[z,z^-1]`, where `Sigma(z)=z^2`, so the quotient map is injective as well;
these two facts prove injectivity on all of `Rsharp`.  Properness already
follows on the Laurent quotient.

Iteration gives the exact binary carrier identity

```text
Sigma^n(P)=1-x^(2^n)y^(2^n)
          =sum_(j=0)^(2^n-1) x^jPy^j.                    (BST8)
```

Most importantly, on every forbidden sector the old full `M_(2D)` coordinate
is moved into the new `2 by 2` Toeplitz-head coordinate, while the two
inequivalent `B_f^Q` child signs are retained as two copies.  Hence restriction
of a target `M_2(C_x)` module along `phi_x` adds the two child multiplicities.
This is exactly the non-type-preserving algebraic placement missing from the
one-step shift.

The claim does **not** by itself prove normalized-HS collapse.  The binary
tree admits stationary regular flow with exponentially growing finite matrix
dimension.  A successful global argument must still return at most one child
copy, or charge the other copy by the Fanizza/Schur predicate energy.
