Let `H=EL_16(L_(F_2)(1,2))`.  For every nonempty compact metrizable space
`X`, the C-star algebra

```text
A_X = C(X) tensor_min C*_r(H)
```

is separable, unital, infinite dimensional, and stably finite.  Every star
homomorphism from `A_X` to an MF C-star algebra is zero.  Consequently every
nonzero quotient of `A_X` is non-MF and non-quasidiagonal.

The algebra `A_X` is the trivial continuous field over `X` with constant
fiber `C*_r(H)`.  Thus total MF-invisibility occurs in stably finite
continuous fields over arbitrary compact metrizable parameter spaces.
