On the positive block-fixed sector forced by nonsoficity, let the internal
block dimensions and numbers of eigenvalues diverge, but choose dense
irrational phase sets converging to a fixed non-Haar probability law.  Hope
that absence of bounded power recurrence is enough to retain the regular
character while the biased law supplies flexibility for the compression
relators.

This is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-fixed-block-actor-is-charge-haar`.  Torsion-free essential
freeness computes every nonzero charged power moment to be zero, so the full
aggregate spectral law is Haar.  Dense support by itself is insufficient.
