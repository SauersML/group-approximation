Expand the squared tuple distance from `X` to an arbitrary matrix-range
point `Y`.  Coordinate contractivity bounds it by twice the deficit of the
pairing with `X`.  Normalize the tuple `X` by `sqrt(|S|)` to obtain
`(GDS2)--(GDS3)`; conjugation by the stable letter gives `(GDS4)` exactly.
