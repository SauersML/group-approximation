Let `B` have full MF radical, let `C<=B`, and let `i:C->G` be an injective
homomorphism into a countable group.  Form

```text
A(B,C;G)=B *_C G
```

using `i` on the `G` side, and put

```text
barG=G/normalClosure_G(i(C)).
```

For every MF group `M`, restriction to the `G` vertex induces a natural
bijection

```text
Hom(A(B,C;G),M) ~= Hom(barG,M).                       (BHA1)
```

Consequently

```text
R_MF(A(B,C;G)) ~= R_MF(barG).                         (BHA2)
```

If `barG` is MF, then the canonical map `A(B,C;G)->barG` is the universal
MF quotient and

```text
Rad_MF(A(B,C;G))
  =normalClosure_(A(B,C;G))(B).                       (BHA3)
```

If one element `c in C` normally generates `B` and `C=<c>`, then the group
in `(BHA3)` is normally generated by the single element `c`.  Amalgam normal
form keeps `c` nonidentity, so the attachment is non-MF whenever `B` is
nontrivial.

More generally, attach a finite or countable family `(B_j,C_j)` to one
group `G` along injections `i_j:C_j->G`.  Every map of the resulting
iterated amalgam to an MF group is uniquely a map from

```text
G/normalClosure_G(union_j i_j(C_j)).                  (BHA4)
```

If the groups and edge subgroups are finitely presented and finitely
generated respectively, a finite family produces a finitely presented
attachment.  Thus fixed MF-black-hole pieces act as exact semantic relation
cells: attaching them forces precisely the chosen subgroup relations in
every MF target without imposing those relations inside the amalgam itself.
