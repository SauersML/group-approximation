**Established (literature).**  I. Moutzouris, *Extensions of quasidiagonal
C\*-algebras and controlling the K_0-map of embeddings*, arXiv:2112.03224v3
(to appear in J. Operator Theory), read 2026-09-11.

Let `Y` be the class of finite direct sums of algebras that are either
separable ASH algebras, or crossed products `D x|_sigma Z` with `D` separable,
nuclear, unital, quasidiagonal, UCT, `sigma` minimal and `D` carrying a
`sigma`-invariant trace.  Every algebra in `Y` has the K_0-embedding property.

(Theorem 1.3)  If `0 -> A -> E -> B -> 0` is exact, `A` is separable,
`A tensor Q` is locally approximated by algebras in `Y`, and `B` is separable,
nuclear, quasidiagonal and UCT, then `E` is quasidiagonal iff it is stably
finite.

(Remark 1.4)  In particular this covers ideals that are separable, simple,
nuclear, unital, quasidiagonal and UCT, since then `A tensor Q` is ASH.
