Dead route, recorded so the approach is not retried as stated. The idea copies
`thompson-v-standard-cylinder-charts-are-commensurated`: let `nV` act on
groupoid charts and take the standard brick charts as the commensurated subset.
It is killed by the established obstruction
`brin-thompson-brick-charts-are-not-commensurated`: a single element of `2V`
breaks infinitely many bricks along a codimension-one face.

A repaired version needs walls that each break counts finitely, e.g. maximal
break faces rather than bricks. No such repair is recorded yet.
