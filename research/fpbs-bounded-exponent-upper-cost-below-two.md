title: Every countably infinite bounded-exponent group has upper cost strictly below two
---

Corollary 5.1. In the non-locally-finite case, choose finite F of size f and a of order m<=N with <F,a> infinite. Then C*(G)<=2-1/f-1/m<2-1/N. In the locally finite case C*(G)=1.
