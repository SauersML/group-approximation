Let `G` be a finitely generated group with `e(G) > 1`. Then `G` admits an action on a simplicial
tree without a global fixed point, with finite edge stabilizers and without edge inversions.

Imported, not re-derived: Stallings' theorem on ends of groups together with Bass–Serre theory.
