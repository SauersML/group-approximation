Any total compiler whose halting instances preserve a nontrivial involution
and whose nonhalting instances uniformly collapse that involution in
small-HS-defect matrix tuples effectively yields a finitely presented
non-MF group.  Kleene selects a nonhalting instance with a genuinely
nontrivial mark; the standard sign-twist HNN wrapper converts its HS collapse
into a norm-corona obstruction.  The compiler hypothesis itself remains the
open instantiation boundary.
