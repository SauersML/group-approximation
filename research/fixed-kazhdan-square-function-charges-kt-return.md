**REFUTED AS A TRACIAL KAZHDAN ARGUMENT.**  In the exact regular model
`L(G)`, every actor defect is zero and the exterior-return expectation is
zero, while the infinite-index quasi-regular module has the full Kazhdan
spectral gap.  Hence expansion propagates a legitimate exterior module; it
does not charge a relation defect.  A matrix-only inequality is not refuted,
but cannot follow from the tracial spectral-gap argument asserted here.
