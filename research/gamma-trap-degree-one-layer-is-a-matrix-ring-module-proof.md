Notation is as in the target. `G` is an arbitrary group satisfying (LR),
(R1)-(R3), (U), with all seven generators involutions. Operators are written
on the left and composed right to left. No import is used.

## Step 0. Linearisation

`N = <<u>>` is generated by conjugates of the involution `u`. So `W = N/[N,N]`
is an elementary abelian 2-group, that is, an `F_2`-vector space. `G` acts on
`W` by conjugation, `N` acts trivially, and `W` is spanned by the
`G`-conjugates of `v`. This is item 1.

For `x` in `N` and an involution `g`, `d_g(x) = x . gxg^{-1}`, which lies in
`N`. Its image in `W` is `(1+g) x-bar`. Every word in the relations below
starts from `u`, so it lies in `N`. Written additively in `W`:

- (R1): `(1+as)(1+c) v = (1+bs)(1+b1) v`;
- (R2): `(1+at)(1+c) v = (1+bt)(1+b1) v`;
- (R3): `(1+at)(1+c)(1+as)(1+c) v = v`;
- (U): `as v = at v = bs v = bt v = v`.

These are `rho_1..rho_7` of `levi_search.py`. Its `rho_8`, `rho_9` (the two
EXTRA words `(1+at)(1+as)(1+c) v = 0` and
`(1+at)(1+as)(1+c)(1+at)(1+c) v = 0`) follow from Step 3: they equal
`(1+at) x_13(S)` and `(1+at) x_13(ST)`, and `at` fixes every `x_13(a)`.

## Step 1. Left and right root operators agree on `v`

Put `L(S) = (1+as)(1+c)`, `L(T) = (1+at)(1+c)`, `R(S) = (1+bs)(1+b1)` and
`R(T) = (1+bt)(1+b1)`, all in `End(W)`. For a word `a = a_1 ... a_k` in
`{S,T}` put

- `L(a) = L(a_1) ... L(a_k)` (multiplicative);
- `R(a) = R(a_k) ... R(a_1)` (anti-multiplicative).

By (LR), every `L(.)` commutes with every `R(.)`.

*Claim:* `L(a) v = R(a) v` for every word `a`. The proof is induction on `k`.
The empty word is trivial. For `a = a_1 a'`,

`L(a) v = L(a_1) L(a') v = L(a_1) R(a') v = R(a') L(a_1) v = R(a') R(a_1) v = R(a) v`,

using, in order, the induction hypothesis, (LR), and (R1) or (R2).

## Step 2. The relation `TS = 1`

By (R3), `L(TS) v = L(T) L(S) v = v`. For words `a, b`,

`L(a TS b) v = L(a) L(TS) R(b) v = L(a) R(b) L(TS) v = L(a) R(b) v = L(a) L(b) v = L(ab) v`.

The steps use Step 1, (LR), (R3) and Step 1 again.

So `a -> L(a) v` is constant on the classes of the bicyclic monoid
`<S,T | TS=1>`. It therefore extends linearly to a map
`x_13 : J -> W` with `x_13(a) = L(a) v = R(a) v` on monomials. In particular
`x_13(1) = v`.

## Step 3. Four blocks and closure

Define, for `a` in `J`:

- `x_23(a) = (1+c) x_13(a)`;
- `x_14(a) = (1+b1) x_13(a)`;
- `x_24(a) = (1+c)(1+b1) x_13(a)`.

Let `U` be the span of all four families. For monomials `a` the six Levi
generators act as follows. Each line uses only (LR), (U), the fact that
`g^2 = 1`, and the definitions.

- `c`:
  - `c x_13 = x_13 + x_23` and `c x_23 = x_23`;
  - `c x_14 = x_14 + x_24` and `c x_24 = x_24`.

  Here `c` commutes with `1+b1`.
- `b1`:
  - `b1 x_13 = x_13 + x_14` and `b1 x_14 = x_14`;
  - `b1 x_23 = x_23 + x_24` and `b1 x_24 = x_24`.
- `as`:
  - `as x_13(a) = as R(a) v = R(a) as v = x_13(a)`, by (LR) and (U);
  - `as x_14(a) = (1+b1) as x_13(a) = x_14(a)`;
  - `as x_23(a) = x_23(a) + L(S) L(a) v = x_23(a) + x_13(Sa)`;
  - `as x_24(a) = (1+b1) as x_23(a) = x_24(a) + x_14(Sa)`.

  The same holds for `at` with `T` in place of `S`.
- `bs`:
  - `bs x_13(a) = bs L(a) v = L(a) bs v = x_13(a)`;
  - `bs x_23(a) = x_23(a)`;
  - `bs x_14(a) = x_14(a) + R(S) R(a) v = x_14(a) + R(aS) v = x_14(a) + x_13(aS)`;
  - `bs x_24(a) = (1+c) bs x_14(a) = x_24(a) + x_23(aS)`.

  The same holds for `bt` with `T` in place of `S`.

So `U` is invariant under the six Levi generators. It contains `v`, and the
generators' images span `G/N`. Hence `U = W`.

Define `phi(E_ij(a)) = x_ij(a)`. This is surjective. Compare with
conjugation in `P`, where left Levi elements act by `Y -> A Y` and right Levi
elements by `Y -> Y B^{-1}` on the block `Y` in rows 1,2 and columns 3,4. For
example:

- `(1 + S E_12) E_23(a) = E_23(a) + E_13(Sa)`;
- `E_14(a)(1 + E_43 S) = E_14(a) + E_13(aS)`.

These are exactly the lines above, so `phi` commutes with each generator.
This is item 2.

## Step 4. Invariant subspaces are matrix ideals

`K = ker phi` is an `F_2`-subspace of `M_2(J)`. It is invariant under the six
generator actions, hence under `g - 1` for each generator. In 2x2 block
coordinates these are:

- left multiplication by `S E_12`, `T E_12` and `E_21`;
- right multiplication by `E_12`, `S E_21` and `T E_21`.

The left algebra generated by `S E_12, T E_12, E_21` contains:

- `T E_11 = (T E_12)(E_21)`;
- `E_11 = (T E_12)(E_21)(S E_12)(E_21)`, using `TS = 1`;
- `E_12 = (T E_11)(S E_12)`;
- `E_22 = E_21 E_12`;
- `E_21`, and `S E_11 = (S E_12)(E_21)`.

So it contains `M_2(F_2)` and the scalars `S`, `T`. Hence it is all of
`M_2(J)`. Symmetrically, the right algebra generated by `E_12, S E_21, T E_21`
contains:

- `E_21 = (T E_21)(E_12)(S E_21)`;
- `E_12`, `E_11 = E_12 E_21` and `E_22 = E_21 E_12`;
- `S E_11 = E_12 (S E_21)`.

So it is `M_2(J)` too. Hence `K` is a sub-bimodule of `M_2(J)` over
`M_2(J)`. By the standard correspondence, `K = M_2(I)` with `I` the set of
`(1,1)`-entries of `E_11 K E_11`, a two-sided ideal of `J`. This is item 3.

## Step 5. Finite layers

If `W` is finite, then `A = J/I` is a finite-dimensional `F_2`-algebra with
`TS = 1`.

- In a finite-dimensional algebra, a one-sided inverse is two-sided. Left
  multiplication by `S` is injective, because left multiplication by `T`
  undoes it. Hence it is bijective, so `S y = 1` for some `y`, and then
  `T = T S y = y`. So `ST = 1`.
- Hence `A` is a quotient of `F_2[S, S^{-1}]`, that is,
  `A = F_2[x]/(p)` with `p(0) != 0` and `S -> x`.
- `1 + ST` lies in `I`, so `phi(E_13(1+ST)) = 0`. The head is
  `h = u . ust` with `ust = d_as d_c d_at d_c u` (`words.py`). Its image in
  `W` is `v + L(S) L(T) v = x_13(1) + x_13(ST) = phi(E_13(1+ST)) = 0`. So `h`
  lies in `[N,N]`.
- `dim W = 4 dim A = 4 deg p`.

This is item 4.

## Step 6. `P` itself

In `P`, `N = n(M_2(J))`. This is abelian and equal to its own abelianisation,
and the map `phi` is the identity. So `I = 0`. This is item 5.

## Check

`levi_search.py` builds `W_max` for given Levi images by exact `GF(2)`
elimination, independently of this proof.

- With the ring action of `SL_2(F_4)` (`p = x^2+x+1`) it returns
  `dim W_max = 8 = 4 deg p`.
- For random permutation Levi images in `S_4` and `S_5` it never returns a
  nonzero layer.
