Let `K` be compact Hausdorff with `dim(K)<=5`, and let `(M,E)` be a
factorial tracially complete C-star algebra whose intrinsic Hilbert
`C(K)`-module is countably generated.  Then every bounded positive trace on
every fibre gap vanishes.  Consequently every tracial state on `M` is
uniquely

```text
a |-> integral_K tau_x(a_x) dmu(x)
```

for a Radon probability measure `mu`, and is uniform-`2`-norm continuous.
Arbitrary matrix, diffuse, and mixed factor fibres are allowed.
