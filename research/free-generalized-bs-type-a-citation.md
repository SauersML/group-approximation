Citation import. Bux--Llosa Isenrich--Wu, *On the Boone--Higman Conjecture for
groups acting on locally finite trees*, arXiv:2408.05673v2, Remark 12.7 and the
definition of `BS_G` in §11. Read on 2026-09-12 from the PDF extracted on MSI;
the quotations are in the target node and the linked artifact.

## What is load-bearing

The words "finitely presented group that admits an action of type (A)". That
phrase is what lets `boone-higman-type-a-class-closed-under-finite-extensions`
pass the conclusion to finite-index overgroups, which the plain embedding of
their Theorem A does not.
