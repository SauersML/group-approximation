**ESTABLISHED (citation).** Arzhantseva--Steenbock construct:
1. torsion-free word-hyperbolic groups with property (T) and without unique
   products (Corollary 1);
2. for every finitely generated group `Q`, an exact sequence
   `1 -> N -> G -> Q -> 1` in which `G` is torsion-free, lacks unique products
   and is a direct limit of hyperbolic groups, and `N <= G` has property (T)
   and lacks unique products (Theorem 3);
3. torsion-free groups with property (T), without unique products, all of
   whose proper subgroups are cyclic (Corollary 2).

The groups in 1 and 3, and the subgroups `N` in 2, are infinite. Each is
torsion-free, and each is nontrivial because the trivial group has unique
products.

**What the source says about the zero-divisor conjecture.**
- It is unknown for the paper's hyperbolic groups without unique products.
- The Kazhdan examples cannot be cubulated, so cubulation cannot settle the
  conjecture for all hyperbolic groups without unique products.

The citation route quotes both passages.
