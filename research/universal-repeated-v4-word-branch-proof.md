Injectivity of the finite packet makes every nonzero selector-radical atom a
nonzero element of the group algebra.  The canonical trace is faithful, so
their total forbidden mass `q_delta` is positive.  Since repetition is a
fixed syntactic operation, enlarge `L` until `(UVW2)` holds.

In the left regular representation every defining group relation and every
claimed universal covariance identity is exact.  The tag Laplacian therefore
sets the ordered cross-Gram sum to zero.  The five-fourths identity makes the
total repeated source trace `(5/4)Lq_delta`, and the Gram capacity theorem
with parent `I` bounds it by `M_0`.  This contradicts `(UVW2)`.
