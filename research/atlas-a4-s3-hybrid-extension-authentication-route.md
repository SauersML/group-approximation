Compile the literal hard/comb transports into subgroup coefficient blocks.
The `A4` line idempotent and the two `S3` scalar idempotents are fixed finite
word averages, so their projection and transport losses are uniformly
Hilbert--Schmidt stable.  The coupling claim places their three rows on one
common multiplicity cut.  The hybrid fingerprint then reads the three
extension coordinates directly as `2,3,3 epsilon`.

This route avoids treating the three Racah intermediate channels as the
three extension labels and avoids asking the standard `S3` channel alone to
remove its five reduced operator sources.  After authentication, the
standard-channel Clebsch--Gordan calculus may still be used for the separate
literal-minor step.
