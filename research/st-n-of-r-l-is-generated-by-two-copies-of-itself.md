**Statement.** Let `N >= 3`, `P = St_N(R_L)`, `e = s_1`, `f = s_2`, and let `Σ^(g)` be the isometric twist of the last
coordinate.
1. **Images are corner subgroups.** `Σ^(e)(P) = H_(ee*)` and `Σ^(f)(P) = H_(ff*)`, the subgroups whose last-coordinate
   coefficients lie in the `e`-corner, respectively the `f`-corner.
2. **Two copies generate.** `P = <Σ^(e)(P), Σ^(f)(P)>`. Both twists are injective
   (`steinberg-groups-of-r-l-are-not-co-hopfian`), so `P` is generated by two proper subgroups, each isomorphic to `P`.
3. **Their intersection.** `Σ^(e)(P) ∩ Σ^(f)(P)` contains the copy of `St_(N-1)` on the first `N - 1` coordinates and the
   central `K_2(N, R_L) ≅ Q^x`.
4. **Iteration.** The words in `Σ^(e)` and `Σ^(f)` of length `k` give `2^k` copies of `P`, indexed by binary words.
   Their images `H_(s_w s_w*)` correspond to the cylinder sets of `{1,2}^N`, and consecutive levels are generated by
   their children. This is the structure of a Leavitt, or Thompson-type, binary tree of copies of `P`.

## Use for the shell window gate

A window-`N` enumeration `ν : P -> N` needs `s^-ε λ(P) s^ε ⊆ <s^(εj) λ(P) s^(-εj) : j <= N>`. The splitting of item 4
suggests enumerating `P` along the binary tree of twists, so that the shift moves between sibling copies. This is
offered as a design input for the construction arm, split with gq-typeA-design. No enumeration is constructed here.
