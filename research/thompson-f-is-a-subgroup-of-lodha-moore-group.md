**ESTABLISHED by citation.**  Let `G_0 = <a, b, c>` be the Lodha--Moore group
(arXiv:1308.4250).  Then `<a, b> = <x_s : s in 2^(<N)>` is Thompson's group `F`.

**Consequences.**
- If `F` is not sofic, then `G_0` is not sofic
  (`lodha-moore-not-sofic-from-thompson-f-not-sofic`).
- If `G_0` is sofic, then `F` is sofic.  The converse implications are not claimed.
