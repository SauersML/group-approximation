Literature import. J. McKee and C. Smyth, *Integer symmetric matrices having all
their eigenvalues in the interval [-2,2]*, arXiv:0705.3599v1 (24 May 2007),
Theorems 1, 2 and 3 (pages 3, 5, 6), with the conventions of Sections 2--3 and
Figures 1 and 6 (pages 4, 6). The PDF was fetched from arXiv on MSI on 2026-09-12
and pages 1--9 were read. Published in J. Algebra 317 (2007) 260--290; the
journal version was not checked.

Their proof represents `A + 2I` as a Gram matrix, reduces to subgraphs of the
line systems `D_n` and `E_8` (Proposition 7), and handles charges and entries
of modulus two separately (Lemmas 5 and 6).
