Jihao Liu's [*Nonhyperlinear groups exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf), Theorem 1.2/6.7, is the proposed analytic input. Andreas Thom has priority for universal-internality-to-normalization, [Theorem 1.2](https://andreasthom.github.io/pdf/nonhyperlinear.pdf); Liu Theorem 7.4 is another formulation. This route keeps that conclusion as a named prerequisite and applies the earlier native Leavitt compressor construction over arbitrary fields.

In R=L_k(1,2), GL_4(R)=EL_4(R) gives finite elementary factorizations of the three explicit invertible matrices u,k_+,k_-. Take S to be the unital subring generated by the four Leavitt generators and every coefficient appearing in these three factorizations. Then H_0=EL_4(S) and L_0=diag(EL_3(S),1) are Kazhdan, all three matrices lie in H_0, and the native Steinberg calculation gives H_0=<L_0,u,k_+,k_->. All three compress L_0.

In every tracial-matrix-ultraproduct homomorphism of H_0, normalization puts the image of u k_+ u^-1=x_14(a) in the commutant of L_0. Since x_21(1) lies in L_0, the image of x_24(a)=[x_21(1),x_14(a)] is trivial. The mark is nontrivial because ba=1. Hence H_0 is nonhyperlinear. It embeds into GL_4(R), which is isomorphic to R^x by the complete four-leaf prefix code. Hyperlinearity is hereditary to subgroups, so R^x is nonhyperlinear.

There is no assertion GL_4(S)=EL_4(S), no assumption k is finitely generated as a ring, and no use of characteristic two in the polynomial identities.
