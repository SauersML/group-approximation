Citation import, not a reproof. Checked on 2026-09-12.

* **Virtual fibering.** I. Agol, *The virtual Haken conjecture*,
  arXiv:1204.2810, with an appendix by Agol, Groves and Manning. The abstract
  (arXiv page) says the main theorem implies that "closed hyperbolic
  3-manifolds have finite-sheeted Haken covers, which resolves the virtual
  Haken question of Waldhausen and Thurston's virtual fibering question".
  Fibers of a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold have genus at least two. That is
  standard and was not re-read here.
* **Surface groups.** D. Gaboriau, *Around the orbit equivalence theory,
  measure equivalence, cost and L2-Betti numbers*, lecture notes
  (<https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/ME-Cost-L2-Lectures/ME-Cost-L2-lectures.pdf>,
  text extracted on MSI), Corollary 2.30 (Surface groups [Gab00]), up to
  typesetting: "Surface groups have fixed price. More precisely, the
  fundamental group of an orientable surface of genus g has cost
  C_*(π1(Sg)) = C^*(π1(Sg)) = 2g − 1."
* **Commensurated transfer.** Same notes, Corollary 2.49 (Commensurated
  subgroups), up to typesetting: "Assume N is a commensurated subgroup of an
  infinite countable group Γ. For every free p.m.p. action Γ↷(X,µ):
  C(R_{Γ↷X}) ≤ C(R_{N↷X})." Footnote 23 defines commensurated as: "for every
  g ∈ Γ the conjugate g−1Ng is commensurable with N". A finite-index subgroup
  meets that condition.

This route asserts only that the cited results say what is quoted here.
