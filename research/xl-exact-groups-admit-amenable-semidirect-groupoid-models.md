**(GSA_G).**  For every countable exact nonamenable group `G` there is an
amenable, minimal, effective, second countable étale groupoid `H` on a compact
metrizable space `X` with `C*_r(H)` unital, simple, stably finite and
classifiable, and an action of `G` on `H` by groupoid automorphisms such that
the semidirect groupoid `H x| G` is topologically amenable.

By `xl-groupoid-action-amenable-iff-semidirect-amenable`, (GSA_G)
implies Problem XL for `G`, and it is equivalent to XL for `G` restricted to
witnesses with a `G`-invariant untwisted Cartan subalgebra on which `G` acts
through Weyl groupoid automorphisms.  It contains (CMA_G) and the
invariant-AF-relation models as special cases.  Necessary conditions:
`X` is an amenable `G`-space, and no `H`-invariant continuous `G`-map
`X -> P(B)` into a nontrivial boundary exists.  Open for every nonamenable `G`.
