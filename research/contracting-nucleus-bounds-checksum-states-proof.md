Equation `(CSF1)` is the definition of contraction, applied to the finite
set of group labels occurring in the checksum words.  A Nekrashevych/full
group element is represented by finitely many prefix replacements decorated
by finitely many group labels.  Refine each source cylinder past the maximum
of their contraction depths.  Self-similarity replaces every decoration by
one of its sections, all of which now lie in the finite nucleus.  Hence the
set of possible deep local states is finite.
