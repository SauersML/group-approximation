REFUTED (see the end of the Attempts section; recorded 2026-08-26, the
same day it was proposed).  Originally: a positive-side hypothesis for the
main goal's arithmetic lane.  Fix a prime `p`, `Lambda = SL_3(Z) < Gamma = SL_3(Z[1/p])`.

**Claim.**  There is an injective homomorphism `pi : Gamma -> U(R)` into the
hyperfinite `II_1` factor such that, with `P = pi(Lambda)''`,

```text
||E_P(pi(g))|| < 1     for every g in Gamma - Lambda.                  (EXT)
```

By `kazhdan-generated-amenable-algebra-is-atomic`, `P` is automatically atomic,
so `pi|_Lambda` is a direct sum of pairwise inequivalent finite-dimensional
irreducible representations of `SL_3(Z)` -- all of which factor through
congruence quotients -- with positive weights.

**Payoff.**  By `exterior-hyperfinite-edge-model-amalgam-hyperlinear` the claim
implies that `Gamma *_Lambda (Lambda x K)` is hyperlinear for every hyperlinear
`K`: the double-swap group `E_p`, the arithmetic double `D_p`, and the
centralizer HNN group `G_3 = <Gamma, t | [t, Lambda] = 1>` are all hyperlinear.
That refutes `sl3-hnn-admits-no-amalgamated-free-haar-unitary` and
`sl3-outliers-carry-no-balanced-free-involution`, and -- through the
established `sl3-arithmetic-double-hyperlinear-iff-relative-embeddable` at
`p = 2` -- also `sl3-arithmetic-inclusion-is-not-re-over-lattice` and the
SL3 half of `arithmetic-pair-is-not-relatively-embeddable`; hence the
`invalidates` keys on every route consuming those four claims.  It is therefore the exact positive-side counterpart, at the level of
amenable lattice algebras, of the collapse conjectures in the arithmetic lane.

## Attempts

- **Finite-quotient and near-congruence models fail.**  If `pi` is an infinite
  tensor product of congruence representations padded with identities (the
  standard way to realize a residually finite group abstractly in `U(R)`),
  the tail estimates are uniform in the group element, and since `Lambda`
  surjects onto every `SL_3(Z/N)`, `p` not dividing `N` (strong approximation),
  every `pi(g)` is a `||.||_2`-limit of elements of `pi(Lambda)`; so
  `pi(Gamma) <= P` and `E_P(pi(g)) = pi(g)` is unitary -- (EXT) fails
  maximally.  Any model satisfying (EXT) must keep `pi(Gamma)` uniformly
  away from `P` while `pi(Lambda)` is a direct sum of congruence
  representations; that is the "outlier" regime of
  `sl3-amalgamated-free-enemies-are-ambient-extension-outliers`, seen from the
  construction side.
- **Where (EXT) lives.**  `pi(h)` for `h = diag(p, p^(-1), 1)` conjugates `P`
  onto `pi(h Lambda h^(-1))''`, another atomic algebra generated by the
  commensurable lattice; `P` and `pi(h)Ppi(h)^*` share the atomic algebra of
  `Lambda cap h Lambda h^(-1)`, of finite index in both.  No compressor exists,
  so `amenable-kazhdan-models-absorb-compression` does not apply, but the
  Hecke structure of this finite-index overlap is where any proof or
  refutation must act; the relative-commutant lane's
  `hecke-far-commutator-defect-formula` is the natural tool.
- **Deferred.**  This session recorded the hypothesis and its wiring; no
  construction was attempted beyond ruling out the near-congruence models.
- **Refuted.**  `sl3-atomic-ambient-is-absorbed-by-lattice`: inside `R` the
  ambient algebra `pi(Gamma)''` is amenable too, and `Gamma = SL_3(Z[1/p])`
  has property (T), so it is atomic as well; joint finite-block matching with
  `sl3-overlap-full-in-fd-ambient-images` then puts every `pi(g)` in the
  `L^2`-closure of `pi(Lambda)`, so (EXT) fails maximally.  The weaker form
  with a non-amenable Connes-embeddable ambient is closed by
  `sl3-pair-tracial-models-with-atomic-lattice-algebra-are-absorbed`
  (character rigidity).  The exterior-atomic mechanism is therefore dead for
  the arithmetic pair, not only for compressed pairs.
