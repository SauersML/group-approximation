**OPEN.**

**Setting.** Let `G` be a Cayley graph of an infinite finitely presented
group `Gamma`. The Voronoi attempt below uses finite presentation; the
statement itself needs only finite generation. Fix `k >= 2`. For Bernoulli bond percolation `P_p`, let
`F_k(p)` be the infimum of

```text
E_p[ #{k-simplices of Psi_omega containing o} ; o in K_inf ]
```

over measurable `Gamma`-equivariant fields `Psi_omega` such that:

- the vertex set of `Psi_omega` is the union of the infinite clusters;
- each component of `Psi_omega` is one infinite cluster and is `(k-1)`-connected;
- the analogous `i`-simplex densities are finite for `i <= k`.

Extra independent randomness is allowed. This is `F_k` of
`fpbs-unique-cluster-forces-higher-filling-density`.

**Statement (H_k).** `liminf_{p -> p_c+} F_k(p) < (k+1) beta_k^(2)(Gamma)`.

The natural strong form is `lim_{p -> p_c+} F_k(p) = 0`.

**Why it is a separate prerequisite.**

- H_k is not implied by `p_c < p_u`. With nonunique clusters no lower bound
  is known, but no upper bound is known either.
- H_k is not implied by any property shared by every invariant percolation
  with small density. By the established lower bound, every invariant
  percolation with a unique infinite cluster has `F_k >= (k+1) beta_k`, however
  small `theta` is. The sparse-spine collapse families recorded on
  `fpbs-fixed-price-one-percolation-kernel` are such percolations. The
  analogue of the strong form fails for them, so a proof of H_k must use
  something specific to Bernoulli percolation.
- For `p > p_u` the same lower bound holds, so `F_k(p) >= (k+1) beta_k` there.
  `F_k` therefore has to drop somewhere in `(p_c, p_u]` if H_k holds.

## Attempts

* **Cluster graph plus fan fillings (heuristic, not a proof).**
  - Use the cluster graph as the 1-skeleton. For every loop in a measurable
    generating family of `pi_1`, add a fan triangulation.
  - Cost: a loop of length `L` needs `L - 2` triangles.
  - Mean-field heuristic at `p = p_c + eps`: `theta ~ eps`, the infinite
    cluster is a tree of critical blobs, and `E[deg | K] - 2 = O(eps)`.
    Then the unconditioned density of independent loops is `O(eps theta)`
    at bounded length, which suggests `F_2 = o(1)`.
  - **Where it stops:**
    1. No measurable equivariant generating family of `pi_1` of the cluster
       with controlled total length is known. Long loops through the tree
       of blobs are not controlled by the local excess.
    2. Filling "all loops of length `<= L`" is simply connected only if `pi_1`
       of the cluster is generated by short loops. Under uniqueness with
       `beta_2 > 0` this must fail at density cost `>= 3 beta_2`.
* **Generic projection fillings are blind (obstruction).**
  - Let `X` be any invariant vertex set. Project a triangulated Cayley
    2-complex of `Gamma` onto `X` through Voronoi cells: edges join adjacent
    cells, and triangles span triples of cells meeting at triangles.
  - Result: a simply connected complex with unconditioned 2-cell density
    `O(1)`, where the constant depends on `Gamma` and on cell-boundary
    statistics.
  - **Where it dies:** the construction never looks at percolation. By the
    established bound it cannot go below `3 beta_2` in the unique case, so
    it gives nothing. A proof of H_k has to use the cluster's own edge and
    loop structure. In other words, the quantity has to see that
    near-critical clusters are locally tree-like.
* **k = 1 calibration (complete).**
  - `F_1(p) <= d theta(p) -> 0`, using the cluster graph itself, together
    with `theta(p_c) = 0` and right-continuity.
  - This is why the degree-one theory (`beta_1 > 0 => p_c < p_u`) closes.
  - For `k >= 2` the step that fails is filling `pi_1` of the cluster at
    vanishing cost.
