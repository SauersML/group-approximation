If a separable counterexample to Problem LXXXI exists, then there is a
separable stable counterexample and there is a separable unital
counterexample.  Explicitly, from any counterexample `A` one may take

```text
A tensor K                 and                 A~,
```

respectively.  Both still satisfy the hereditary commutative-shadow
hypothesis and have the same finite nuclear dimension, at least two, as
`A`.

Thus neither nonunitality nor failure of stability can be essential to a
negative answer.
