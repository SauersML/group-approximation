Let `G` be the group supplied by the first prerequisite.  By the second it is
hyperlinear; by hypothesis it is not sofic.

Compared with `hyperlinear-nonsofic-from-kt-reduced-cstar-mf`, this route
removes the unique-trace hypothesis.  Any finite amenable radical is allowed,
because traces off the radical are forced to vanish
(`bkko-reduced-traces-concentrate-on-amenable-radical`), and a finite normal
radical is tracially visible under operator MF.
