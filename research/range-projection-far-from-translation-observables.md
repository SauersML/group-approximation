Setup of `injective-ca-product-trace-zero-one-law`:
- `tau` is injective and not surjective over an infinite group `G`;
- `c = tau(b)` for a constant background `b`;
- `H_c = l^2` of the finite perturbations of `c`, and `P` is the range
  projection of the encoder isometry `V`;
- `A_G` is the quasilocal algebra, `pi_c` the translation representation, and
  `B_c = C*(A_G ∪ pi_c(G))`.

Then:
1. `dist(P, B_c) = 1/2`.
2. If `G` has property (T), its Kazhdan projection acts as `|c><c|`, so `B_c`
   contains every compact operator on `H_c`. `P` is still at distance `1/2`.

The Kazhdan projection gives only the vector state at `c`, which is `1` on `P`
whether or not `tau` is onto. So property (T) adds nothing on the operator side.
