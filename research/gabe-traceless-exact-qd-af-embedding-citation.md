This is Corollary C of James Gabe,
[*Traceless AF embeddings and unsuspended E-theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.08095),
Geometric and Functional Analysis 30 (2020), 323--333.
For a separable exact traceless C-star algebra, it proves the equivalence of
AF-embeddability, quasidiagonality, stable finiteness, and the absence of a
nonempty open compact subset of the primitive ideal space.  In particular,
the quasidiagonal implication imported by the Problem VIII reduction has
exactly the stated hypotheses.
