title: Two order-zero colours merge by direct sum exactly when their supports are orthogonal
---

Let `F` and `G` be unital C*-algebras and let

```text
alpha : F -> B,       beta : G -> B
```

be completely positive contractive order-zero maps.  The completely
positive map

```text
gamma : F directSum G -> B,
gamma(x,y)=alpha(x)+beta(y),
```

is a completely positive contractive order-zero map if and only if

```text
alpha(1_F) beta(1_G)=0.                              (S1)
```

Thus direct-sum reuse of an ideal colour and a quotient colour has an exact
support-orthogonality criterion; commutation of the two supports is not
enough.
