This is a correction to the research graph, not a new nuclear-dimension
theorem.  The claim
`stw83-two-non-k-extension-directions-remain-open` says that two special
circle-ideal extension directions are unresolved.  In particular, it does
not prove nuclear dimension at most one even for either of those special
models.  The root `stw83-problem-lxxxiii-graph-nuclear-dimension` asserts the
bound for **every** directed graph.

The route `stw83-two-non-k-extension-directions-route` has the openness audit
as its sole prerequisite, but its body only identifies the special models as
a live attack and proposes a future extension theorem followed by a permanence
argument.  It supplies neither proposed theorem and no reduction from the two
models to all graph C*-algebras.  Therefore that route does not contain the
implication which a Cairn route commits to and must not establish the root.

This obstruction invalidates only the miswired route.  It neither refutes the
openness audit nor establishes or refutes the STW83 root; the genuine special
cases and the universal problem remain open until their missing estimates are
proved.
