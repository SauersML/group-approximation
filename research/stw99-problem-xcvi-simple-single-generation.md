**Problem XCVI, first part, of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White.**  Must every
unital simple separable C*-algebra be generated by one operator?

The answer is **no** by `stw96-simple-nuclear-ah-not-singly-generated`.  The
counterexample is even nuclear and AH.

The general nowhere-scattered formulation implies this simple formulation:
every unital simple infinite-dimensional C*-algebra is nowhere scattered, while
the remaining simple algebras are matrix algebras and are singly generated.  The
converse implication was never formal, because a general nowhere-scattered
algebra need not be simple and single generation is not extension-permanent.
