Put `K=C_lc(X,GL_5(F_2))` and

```text
Q=<s,r | s^2=1, r^3=1>=C_2*C_3
```

with the action from the exact split normal form.  Set

```text
A=K semidirect <s>,              C=K semidirect <r>.
```

Both `A` and `C` are locally finite.  Indeed, a finite subset of either
group involves finitely many elements of `K`; adjoining their orbit under
the finite actor `C_2` or `C_3` still gives a finite subset of `K`, and the
subgroup it generates is finite because `K` is locally finite.  The split
normal form also gives the exact amalgam decomposition

```text
K semidirect (C_2*C_3) = A *_K C.                     (1)
```

Fix a finite set `E` in the group on the right of `(1)`, enlarged by the
products whose local multiplication table is to be preserved.  Choose
reduced `A *_K C` normal forms for its elements and fixed reduction sequences
for every retained equality `xy=z`.

Only finitely many vertex-group syllables occur in these words and only
finitely many elements of `K` are passed across amalgamation seams in the
chosen reductions.  Let `K_0<=K` be the subgroup generated by all those seam
elements.  It is finite.  By local finiteness of the vertex groups, choose
finite subgroups

```text
A_0<=A,             C_0<=C
```

which contain `K_0` and all the retained `A`- and `C`-syllables,
respectively.  Form

```text
M=A_0 *_(K_0) C_0.                                  (2)
```

The inclusions into `(1)` agree on `K_0`, so there is a natural homomorphism

```text
pi:M -> A *_K C.                                    (3)
```

Lift every element of `E` by reading its chosen normal form in `(2)`.  Every
step in each retained reduction is either multiplication within one selected
finite vertex subgroup or passage of one of the recorded elements through
the common subgroup `K_0`.  Therefore all retained products hold exactly:

```text
e_tilde f_tilde=(ef)_tilde.                           (4)
```

Moreover `pi(e_tilde)=e`.  Thus distinct members of `E` have distinct lifts:
equality of two lifts would remain equality after applying `(3)`.  The group
`M` is the fundamental group of a finite graph of finite groups, hence is
virtually free and residually finite.  A single finite quotient therefore
separates every pair in the finite lifted set.  Composing the lift with that
quotient gives an injective partial homomorphism of the requested
multiplication table into a finite group.  Hence `K semidirect (C_2*C_3)` is
LEF.

Apply this to the exact normal form from the first prerequisite.  The second
prerequisite converts LEF to an injective norm-matrix-corona representation.
The marked diagonal root is nonidentity, so that representation retains it.
Therefore the MF radical of `B_EL` is trivial.
