Citation import, not a reproof.  Haagerup–Musat, arXiv:1009.0778 (*Comm.
Math. Phys.* 303 (2011) 555–594): the abstract states that the paper "solves
in the negative an open problem in quantum information theory concerning an
asymptotic version of the quantum Birkhoff conjecture"; the counterexamples
are the factorizable channels whose Markov dilations are not inner, built from
the paper's factorization analysis.  The abstract was re-read on 2026-08-18
during ingestion of
`research/artifacts/hyperlinear-divergent-strategies-2026-08-18.md`.

This route asserts only that the cited paper says what this graph records it
as saying.  No node in this graph uses the finer structure of the
counterexamples — only the negative answer, as a scope condition on rounding
arguments.
