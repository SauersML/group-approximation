**ESTABLISHED (literature import)** by [[sl3z-congruence-subgroup-property-citation]].

Let `n >= 3` and let `H <= SL_n(Z)` have finite index. Then `H` contains
`Gamma(m) = ker(SL_n(Z) -> SL_n(Z/m))` for some `m >= 1`.

Two standard consequences are used downstream.
- **Congruence completion.** The profinite completion of such an `H` is its
  closure in `SL_n(Zhat) = prod_l SL_n(Z_l)`. Reduction `SL_n(Z) -> SL_n(Z/m)`
  is surjective for every `m`, since `SL_n(Z/m)` is generated by elementary
  matrices.
- **Finite datum.** `H` is the preimage of the subgroup `H/Gamma(m)` of
  `SL_n(Z/m) = prod_(p^e || m) SL_n(Z/p^e)`.
