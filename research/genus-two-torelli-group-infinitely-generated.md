title: "The Torelli group of the closed genus two surface is an infinitely generated free group"
---

`I_2 = ker(Mod(S_2) -> Sp_4(Z))` is a free group that is not finitely generated,
so in particular it is not finitely presented. McCullough–Miller proved that `I_2`
is not finitely generated, Mess that it is free of infinite rank; a second proof
comes from Bestvina–Bux–Margalit. The import is
`genus-two-torelli-group-infinitely-generated-citation`.

Role in Zaremsky Problem 3.1: the surface family needs "large enough" genus. The
open genera are `g >= 3`, where `I_g` is finitely generated
(`surface-torelli-fg-in-genus-at-least-three`).
