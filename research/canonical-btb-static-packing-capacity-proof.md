Because the base, finite context packets, and the independent involution
`ell` embed, the subgroup generated by `J` and `ell` is `C_2^2`.  The
canonical trace therefore gives `(BSP1)`.

For a literal finite-two-group character projection `e_i` and its commuting
exact order-three root `Z_i`, apply
`coprime-root-reynolds-prefix-overlap` with `p=3`.  This proves `(BSP3)`.
The restriction of canonical trace to every embedded finite context group
is its uniform regular trace.  Applying the zero-defect finite contextual
gap to that finite structural model gives `(BSP4)`.

Choose `i_0` maximizing `tau_G(e_i)`.  Then

```text
tau_G(d_(i_0))=(2/3)tau_G(e_(i_0))
              >=2 beta a/(3M).                        (BSQ1)
```

Conjugation preserves canonical trace.  If `K` conjugates of `d_(i_0)` are
pairwise orthogonal below `C`, their sum is a projection below `C`; hence

```text
2K beta a/(3M)<=K tau_G(d_(i_0))<=tau_G(C).            (BSQ2)
```

This is `(BSP5)`.  Under simultaneous orthogonality, sum over every type:

```text
K sum_i tau_G(d_i)
  =(2K/3)sum_i tau_G(e_i)
  >=(2K beta/3)a
  <=tau_G(C),                                          (BSQ3)
```

which proves `(BSP6)`.  Substituting `tau_G(C)=1/4` and `a=1/8` gives
`(BSP7)`.

Now add any finite packet refinement.  If its source atoms are orthogonal,
their traces add to at most the trace of the unrefined source.  If they
overlap, there is no projection-valued packing inequality that permits their
traces to be added.  Transporting a chosen family to pairwise orthogonal
word conjugates returns immediately to `(BSQ2)--(BSQ3)`.  Finite
Heisenberg polarizations are exactly such refinements, and repeated BTB
packets sharing the same source root use the same spectral projection
`S_(Z_i)`.

Finally, `(BSP9)` does not contradict this exact calculation.  Its carrier
`Q` is obtained by taking a reducing hull inside each representation and is
generally only a von Neumann-algebra spectral projection.  It is not a fixed
word or finite Fourier idempotent.  Thus applying an HNN relation directly
to `D_i=QP_iS_(Z_i)` would assume the missing analytic-carrier compiler.
This proves the stated static/analytic dichotomy.
