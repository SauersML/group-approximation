Read on 2026-09-12 from PDFs fetched on MSI and converted with ghostscript `txtwrite`
(lane ex-weak-soficity, `/scratch.global/sauer354/ex/ex-weak-soficity/`). The primary
papers (Annals 2001, Comm. Algebra 2002) were **not** re-read: the Annals landing page
returned HTML, not the PDF. Both statements below are quoted by named experts in the area,
and one of them is a coauthor of the primary.

**Maroti--Pyber, arXiv:2003.14270v2, page 1**, verbatim:

> A well-known theorem of Liebeck and Shalev [13, Theorem 1.1] is that there is a constant
> c such that whenever S is a non-trivial normal subset in a non-abelian finite simple
> group G then S^k = G for any integer k at least c · (log|G|/log|S|).

> Rodgers [17, Corollary 2.4] shows that if C_1, ..., C_k are conjugacy classes of the
> symmetric group Sym(n) of degree n ≥ 5 and prod_(i=1)^k |C_i| > n^(6(n−2)), then
> C_1 ··· C_k is equal to the alternating group Alt(n) of degree n or to Sym(n) \ Alt(n).

Their bibliography: "[13] M. W. Liebeck, A. Shalev, Diameters of finite simple groups: sharp
bounds and applications. Ann. of Math. (2) 154 (2001), no. 2, 383–406." and "[17] D. M.
Rodgers, Generating and covering the alternating or symmetric group. Comm. Algebra 30
(2002), no. 1, 425–435."

**Liebeck--Nikolov--Shalev, arXiv:1107.1528v1, page 2**, independently, verbatim: the
conjecture that `G` is "a product of N conjugates of A for some N ≤ c log|G|/log|A|" is
"known to be true (and widely applied) ... when the subset A is a conjugacy class, or more
generally, a normal subset of G; indeed, this is the main result of [8]", where "[8] M.W.
Liebeck and A. Shalev, Diameters of finite simple groups: sharp bounds and applications,
Ann. of Math. 154 (2001), 383–406."

**Nikolov--Pyber, arXiv:math/0703343, page 2**, verbatim: "for a finite simple group L of
Lie type of rank r over F_q we have k ≥ cq^r for the minimal degree k of a representation
of L". Item 3 of the claim is this sentence. The primary source is Landazuri--Seitz, not
re-read.
