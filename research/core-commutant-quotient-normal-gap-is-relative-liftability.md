Fix an exact finite-dimensional BS core `beta` and let

```text
Z_beta={Y:Y extends beta to an exact Iwahori endpoint}.   (CQN1)
```

The intrinsic zero set is the whole union `(CQN1)`, not a packetwise block
diagonal subset.  After `N=ord(beta(s))` is fixed, it is the union of the
core-commutant orbits indexed by the nonnegative restriction fiber for the
finite one-power quotient `A_N`.  Define

```text
d_q(X,beta)=inf_(Y in Z_beta) ||X-Y||_2,                 (CQN2)
```

and use its standard flexible version when `o(d)` padding is allowed.
The Hadamard directions in
`exact-cuspidal-hadamard-mixing-has-macroscopic-cross-blocks` have
`d_q=0`, even though every fixed duplicate-packet coordinate sees
macroscopic off-diagonal mass.  Thus `(CQN2)` is the smallest meaningful
normal distance.

For a sequence `(X_n,beta_n)` with vanishing row energy, the failure

```text
liminf d_q^flex(X_n,beta_n)>0                           (CQN3)
```

is equivalent to the resulting exact relative homomorphism

```text
Pi:A -> product_omega M_(d_n)                           (CQN4)
```

having no almost-full coordinate lift by finite-dimensional congruence
representations extending the prescribed cores.  This is the relative
liftability equivalence with every restriction-fiber/core-commutant kernel
direction already quotiented.  A true positive-distance soft nonextendible
face is therefore precisely a noncongruence relative lift of `(CQN4)`; it
is not an unidentified cross block inside a congruence packet.

Property `(tau)` does not manufacture that lift.  Once an exact candidate
endpoint `pi_n` has been selected, apply `(tau)` to the genuine bimodule
representation `pi_n tensor conjugate(sigma_n)` and polarize; this gives
`congruence-cross-type-mixing-has-uniform-polar-repair`.  Before selection,
the raw tuple `(X_n,beta_n)` is only an approximate representation of `A`.
Its word substitutions do not define a unitary action on the Hilbert--Schmidt
bimodule, so the `(tau)` inequality has no legal second representation.
Calling the algebra generated by the raw tuple an "adaptive endpoint
algebra" assumes exactly the coordinate lift in `(CQN4)` and is circular.

There is one further quantitative issue even if qualitative liftability is
known.  The established tangent quotient gap and mixed Taylor estimate give
a uniform linear normal bound inside a selected endpoint tube when the
normal coordinate is operator-small.  The scalar/cb Green modes are HS-small,
and coordinatewise operator-hard tails have energy-paid total rank by
`tangent-green-divergence-is-flexibly-rank-paid`.  But cutting those tails
coordinatewise need not preserve the exact BS core or its tangent
constraints.  Therefore converting qualitative `d_q^flex ->0` into a
uniform linear error bound still needs a **legal core-preserving hard-tail
truncation**.  This is not supplied by `(tau)`.

Hence the quotient-normal program has exactly two remaining possibilities:

1. a positive-distance sequence `(CQN3)`, equivalently a nonliftable relative
   ultraproduct extension; or
2. qualitative lifts exist, but the approach to the endpoint union runs
   through operator-hard small-rank coordinates which cannot yet be removed
   while keeping the core exact.

The known compressed Weil, cuspidal Hadamard, isolated metacyclic, long
return-phase, and rational-`H^2` families realize neither possibility in
the required form.

## Attempts

- **Adaptive property `(tau)`.**  Treating the algebra generated by the raw
  tuple as the second endpoint action fails because the cubic words hold only
  approximately, so there is no unitary bimodule representation to which the
  Kazhdan inequality applies.  Exactifying that action is the relative lift.
- **Hard-tail spectral cut.**  Coordinatewise coarea pays the cut rank, but
  the separate spectral projections need not reduce the common BS core or
  satisfy its tangent equations.  Taking their core-invariant hull can lose
  the rank bound.  A core-preserving joint cut is the remaining quantitative
  attack, not supplied here.
