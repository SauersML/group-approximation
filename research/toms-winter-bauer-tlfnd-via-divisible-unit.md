Let `A` be unital, simple, separable, nuclear and non-elementary, with Bauer
`T(A)`, tlfnd and strict comparison.

1. `strict-comparison-forces-tracially-divisible-unit` (open) gives tracially
   large order zero maps `M_N → A` for every `N`. That is condition (e) of
   `toms-winter-bauer-tlfnd-iff-tracially-divisible-unit`.
2. That theorem turns (e) into `A ≅ A ⊗ Z`.

Step 1 is the only open input, and on this locus it is also necessary.
