Citation import, not a reproof.

* C. M. Newman, L. S. Schulman (1981): for quasi-transitive `G` and each `p`,
  the number of infinite clusters is a.s. one of `0, 1, +infinity`.
* O. Haggstrom, Y. Peres (1999) for unimodular graphs; R. H. Schonmann (1999)
  in general: for every `p > p_u` the infinite cluster is unique a.s.

Both are recorded as surveyed in Choi--Seo arXiv:2508.08932v2, Section 2
(page 3), read from source 2026-09-11.
