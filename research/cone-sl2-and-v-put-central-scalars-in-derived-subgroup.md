**ESTABLISHED** by `cone-sl2-and-v-central-scalars-proof` (elementary; not independently
reviewed; no novelty claimed).

Let `R` be a unital ring with a binary Leavitt family `s_1, s_2, t_1, t_2`, and let
`K ⊆ Z(R)` be an infinite subfield. Write `p_a = s_a t_a` for a word `a`. For disjoint
cones `a, b` (incomparable nonempty words) whose union is not everything, that is
`p_a + p_b ≠ 1`, the **cone copy** of `SL_2(K)` on `(a, b)` is the image of
```text
[[α, β], [γ, δ]]  |->  α p_a + β s_a t_b + γ s_b t_a + δ p_b + (1 - p_a - p_b).
```
Let `H ≤ R^x` contain the standard copy of Thompson's `V` and the cone copy of `SL_2(K)`
on one such pair `(a, b)`. Then:
1. `K^x·1 ≤ [H, H] ∩ Z(H)`;
2. for every `C` with `K^x ≤ C ≤ Z(H)`, `H/C` is not finitely presented. In particular
   `H/Z(H)` is not finitely presented, and `H/Z(H)` is not a finitely presented simple
   group.

**Scope for `gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group`.**
- **The hypotheses hold for every overgroup of V and the root's copy of `GL_n(Q)`, for
  every `n ≥ 2`.** Take a complete code of size `n + 2` and the embedding
  `g ↦ diag(g, det(g)^(-1), 1)` through `M_(n+2)(R) ≅ R`. Its image contains
  `diag(A, 1, ..., 1)` for `A ∈ SL_2(Q)`, which is the cone copy of `SL_2(Q)` on the
  first two cones. So `⟨V, GL_n(Q)⟩` inside `R_L^x`, inside `(B ⊗ L_Q)^x`, or inside any
  characteristic-zero simple ring with a Leavitt family has no finitely presented
  central quotient.
- **Consequence for escapes.** In `leavitt-scalar-commutators-block-fp-central-quotients`,
  "What survives", the first escape, a subgroup `H` without a full `L_Q(1,d)^x`, is not
  available to a host containing `V` and `GL_n(Q)` in cone position. What remains:
  - quotients `H/M` with `M` not central;
  - subgroups that avoid `V`, or that carry `GL_n(Q)` other than through cone
    coordinates;
  - rings without an infinite central subfield, e.g. `L_Z(1,2)` with central units `±1`,
    where `integral-leavitt-unit-group-contains-q` realizes `(Q,+)` group-theoretically.
