Let `M` be any type-III von Neumann algebra, regarded only as a unital
C-star algebra.  The exact imported structural statement is
Blanchard--Kirchberg, *Non-simple purely infinite C-star algebras: the
Hausdorff case*, introduction: an AW-star algebra, viewed as a C-star
algebra, is type III if and only if it satisfies their definitions of pure
infiniteness.  They explain the theorem-number chain: AW-star algebras have
real rank zero; Kirchberg--Rordam Corollary 6.9, Proposition 4.7 and Theorem
4.16 say that a real-rank-zero algebra is strongly purely infinite exactly
when all its nonzero projections are properly infinite; equivalently one can
use Blanchard--Kirchberg Theorem 4.17.  Hence `M` is strongly purely infinite,
with no separability or nuclearity assumption.  This conclusion is
compatible with proper norm ideals in `M` and does not use C-star simplicity
or equivalence of arbitrary nonzero projections.

Let `phi:Z->M` be a unital embedding.  It is nuclear because `Z` is nuclear.
It is also full as a map.  Indeed, if `0!=a in Z`, simplicity of the unital
algebra `Z` says that the closed ideal generated by `a` contains `1_Z`.
Applying `phi` shows that the closed ideal of `M` generated by `phi(a)`
contains `1_M`.  Thus every nonzero image element is full even when `M`
itself has proper cardinal-rank ideals.

The precise first classification input is Gabe, *Classification of
O-infinity-stable C-star algebras*, Theorem 9.7: if `A` is separable and exact,
`B` is strongly purely infinite, and a star homomorphism `A->B` is nuclear,
then the map is strongly `O_infinity`-stable.  It applies to both `phi` and
`psi`.

Their nuclear KK classes agree.  One direct verification avoids projection
cardinal arithmetic.  Both `C` and `Z` belong to the bootstrap category, and
the unital inclusion `i:C->Z` induces an isomorphism on `K_0` and `K_1`.
The UCT applied to its mapping cone therefore makes that cone
KK-contractible, so `i` is a KK-equivalence.  Consequently pullback by `i`
is injective on `KK(Z,M)`, while

```text
i^*[phi]=[1_M]=i^*[psi].
```

Hence `[phi]=[psi]` in `KK(Z,M)`.  Since `Z` satisfies the UCT and the unital
algebra `M` is sigma-unital, Skandalis' canonical map

```text
KK_nuc(Z,M) -> KK(Z,M)
```

is an isomorphism; this exact scope is restated in Hua--White, Section 1.3,
immediately after Definition 1.7.  Therefore `KK_nuc(phi)=KK_nuc(psi)`.

Finally Gabe's Theorem B applies to nuclear, strongly `O_infinity`-stable,
full maps from a separable algebra into a sigma-unital algebra.  Its unital
clause upgrades asymptotic Murray--von Neumann equivalence to asymptotic
unitary equivalence.  Since `Z`, `M`, and both maps are unital, the
implementing path consists of unitaries of `M` itself.
