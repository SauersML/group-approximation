title: The external Cu tensor map is an isomorphism when one C*-factor is AF

If `C` is an AF algebra and `B` is any C*-algebra, the canonical external
product induces an isomorphism

```text
Cu(C) tensor_Cu Cu(B) -> Cu(C tensor_min B).
```

No exactness, separability, simplicity, or purity assumption on `B` is
required.
