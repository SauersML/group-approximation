The precise imported result is Li--Hadwin--Liu, *Approximate Equivalence in
von Neumann Algebras*, published Corollary 1.  It assumes a separable unital
ASH algebra `A`, a sigma-finite type-II-infinity factor `M`, and two unital
star homomorphisms `A->M` with equal `M`-rank on every element.  It concludes
norm approximate unitary equivalence, with every conjugacy error in the
norm-closed ideal `K_M` generated by finite projections.  Unlike their
Theorem 8 for general semifinite algebras, Corollary 1 does not assume that
`M` acts on a separable Hilbert space.

The Jiang--Su algebra is separable, unital, and ASH.  The global
von-Neumann-rank theorem proves that the range projections of `phi(a)` and
`psi(a)` are Murray--von Neumann equivalent for every `a in Z`, without any
countability hypothesis.  All assumptions of Corollary 1 are therefore met,
and its unitaries give the conclusion.
