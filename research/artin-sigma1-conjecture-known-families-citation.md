Citation import, not a reproof.

- Families 1–9: M. Escartín-Ferrer, *On the Σ^1 and Σ^2-invariants of Artin
  groups*, arXiv:2501.08692v3 (14 July 2025), Theorem 2.13 (right-angled) and
  Theorem 2.17, items 1–8, with references [1]–[4], [13], [16], [18], [19], [21] of
  that paper.
- Family 9 is also Theorem 1.3 of M. Escartín-Ferrer and C. Martínez-Pérez,
  *On the Σ-invariants of Artin groups satisfying the K(π,1)-conjecture*,
  arXiv:2309.03091v2 (11 December 2023), J. London Math. Soc. (2024); there it
  needs no `K(π,1)` hypothesis (their Remark 3.5 in 2501.08692v3).
- Families 10–11: arXiv:2501.08692v3, Theorem 1.2, Proposition 4.9, Theorem 4.10
  and Corollary 4.11. The proofs were read on pages 9–13.

Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv PDFs, pages 1–13 and 17–19 of 2501.08692v3 and
pages 1–4 of 2309.03091v2. The journal papers in families 1–8 were not reread.
