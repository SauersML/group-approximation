Let `(X,T)` be a compact zero-dimensional dynamical system with a
continuous onto equivariant map to `lim_j Z/j!Z`. Put
`R=LC(X,F_2) semidirect Z`. Then `EL_3(R)` contains the diagonal direct
limit `D=lim_j SL_(3j!)(F_2)`, with connecting maps repeating matrices
`j+1` times. Every countable locally finite group embeds in `D`.

The compatible regular representations also give a unital embedding
`F_2[L] -> M_3(R)` for every countable locally finite `L`.
No minimality, expansiveness, simplicity, or property (T) is assumed here.
