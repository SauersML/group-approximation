ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).

Let `(G, Gamma)`, `H = G wr Z`, `K = Gamma_0 x directSum_(n>=1) G_n` and
`X = H/K` be as in [[amenable-action-of-a-sofic-group-need-not-be-sofic]].
There `H` is finitely generated and sofic, and `H ↷ X` is amenable. Then

```text
Z wr^*_X H  =  ( *_(x in X) Z_x ) x| H  ~=  < H, v | [v, k] = 1, k in K >
```

is **not sofic**. The lamps `Z` are sofic and the actor `H` is sofic, and the
action is amenable. So [[free-wreath-over-amenable-action-is-sofic]] is false
as stated.

DERIVATION [[free-wreath-over-an-amenable-action-can-be-nonsofic-proof]].

**Mechanism in one line.** The subgroup generated by `G_0` and the root lamp
`v` is the centralizing HNN `< G, v | [v, Gamma] = 1 >`, and that group
contains the Kun--Thom double `G *_Gamma G`.

## What survives

- **Amenable image.** If the action factors through an amenable quotient of
  the actor, the free wreath product is sofic. The action is then sofic by GKP
  Theorem 2.17 and Proposition 2.15(1), and GKP Theorem 3.7
  ([[gkp-free-generalized-wreath-soficity]]) applies. This covers the
  amenable-base Mikhailova rope of
  [[amenable-base-makes-mikhailova-edge-co-amenable]], where `F x F` acts
  through the amenable group `Q x Q`.
- **Sofic actions.** GKP Theorem 3.7 already has free-product lamps. So the
  step "upgrade direct-sum lamps to free-product lamps" recorded in the
  refuted node is in the literature. The input that is actually missing in
  any application is soficity of the action, and amenability of the action
  does not provide it.

## Scope

The actor here contains a Kazhdan group. The example says nothing about actors
without infinite (T) subgroups, such as the product of free groups `K_e` in
the compiler rope. There the question is still whether the coset action is
sofic ([[compiler-rope-is-coset-wreath-of-an-a-t-menable-base]]).
