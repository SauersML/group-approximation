If `G` is polycyclic and automatic, then `G` has an abelian subgroup of finite index.

A. Harkins, *Combing lattices of soluble Lie groups*, PhD thesis, University of Newcastle,
2001, as reported in Rees's 2022 survey. Citation route:
`polycyclic-automatic-groups-virtually-abelian-citation`.
