Section 5. If p_n have uniqueness and -log(theta(p_n)^2)/n tends to gamma, then log(a_n(p_n))/n tends to -min(gamma,-log rho). The critical-reference integrated budget tends to (-log rho-gamma)_+. No approaching uniqueness sequence is asserted.
