Let `B` be a unital C-star algebra with no tracial state, and let
`phi:Z->B` be a unital embedding.  For the canonical inclusion `i:B->B**`,
the composite

```text
Cu(Z) --Cu(phi)--> Cu(B) --Cu(i)--> Cu(B**)             (1)
```

sends every nonzero class of `Cu(Z)` to the same properly infinite class.
In particular bidual passage erases the distinction between every finite
soft parameter, the infinite soft class, and every nonzero compact class.

This is a collapse theorem, not a counterexample to LXIII: the corresponding
classes may already have collapsed in `Cu(B)`.  It shows why global
injectivity of `Cu(B)->Cu(B**)` is the wrong generic target in the traceless
regime and why only reflection on the two actual embedding images can matter.
