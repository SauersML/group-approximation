Kopsacheilis--Winter, *Paper-folding models for the CAR algebra*,
[arXiv:2508.04837v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04837), Theorem A
(Theorem 4.1), states that the CAR algebra `M_(2^infinity)` admits a
Cantor-spectrum C-star-diagonal which is not an AF diagonal.  Since the CAR
algebra is UHF and therefore AF, every hypothesis and conclusion in the
target claim occurs verbatim in the theorem, apart from this standard
identification of the ambient algebra as AF.

Theorem B (Theorem 4.4) proves the recorded strengthening: for every
`n in {0,1,2,...,infinity}` it constructs such a diagonal of diagonal
dimension `n`, and explicitly concludes that the resulting family is
pairwise non-conjugate.  No classification theorem or construction from the
paper is re-proved here; this route imports exactly those numbered results
from the published-version v2 preprint.
