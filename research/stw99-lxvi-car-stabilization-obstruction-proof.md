The first matrix stage gives a unital injective homomorphism

```text
M_2(A)=A tensor M_2 -> A tensor M_(2^infinity).
```

If `v in M_2(A)` witnesses infiniteness of its unit, so that `v* v=1` and
`v v*<1`, its image is an isometry with proper range projection: injectivity
preserves the strict inequality.  Because the inclusion is unital, this is
an isometry for the unit of the CAR stabilization itself.  That unit is
therefore infinite.
