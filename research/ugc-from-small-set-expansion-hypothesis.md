implication, and `small-set-expansion-hypothesis` is its open premise. Both
are listed, so that the literature import stays a visible edge.

Unlike `ugc-from-rich-2to1-via-bkm-equivalence`, this route is one-way. No
reduction from UGC to SSEH is known. Proving SSEH would therefore prove
something possibly stronger than UGC. Refuting SSEH would not refute UGC.
