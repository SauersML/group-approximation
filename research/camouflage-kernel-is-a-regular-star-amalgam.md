Let `B` be a group with full MF radical, let `d` normally generate `B`, put
`D=<d>`, and form

```text
C_d(Q)=B *_D (Q x D),
pi_Q:C_d(Q)->Q.
```

For `q in Q`, write `B_q=qBq^(-1)` inside `C_d(Q)`.  Then

```text
K_Q=ker(pi_Q) ~= *_(D, q in Q) B_q,                 (RSA1)
```

the generalized free product of copies of `B` with their copies of `D`
identified pointwise.  The copies `B_q` generate `K_Q`, and distinct copies
meet exactly in `D`.  Moreover

```text
C_d(Q) ~= K_Q semidirectProduct Q,                  (RSA2)
```

where `Q` acts by the left-regular permutation

```text
r B_q r^(-1)=B_(rq)
```

and fixes `D` pointwise.

The kernel is itself an MF black hole:

```text
Rad_MF(K_Q)=K_Q.                                    (RSA3)
```

Indeed every homomorphism from `K_Q` to an MF group is trivial on every
vertex group `B_q`, hence trivial.  If `B` is perfect, then `K_Q` is perfect
as well, because it is generated by perfect subgroups.  For the simple
Hilbert-hotel group in the manuscript, every prescribed MF quotient is
therefore realized by a split extension whose kernel is simultaneously
perfect, full-MF-radical, and explicitly a regular star amalgam.
