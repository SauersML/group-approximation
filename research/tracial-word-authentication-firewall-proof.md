Let `M=L(Gamma)` with its canonical trace and evaluate every fixed group
word by the left regular representation.  All defining relators in the
finite menu have defect zero.  Fixed finite-subgroup averages, Borel
spectral cuts, polar supports, finite meets, compressions, and normalized
`L^2` inequalities are available in `M` exactly as in a matrix algebra.
Hence any derivation of `(TWA1)` using only those operations applies in `M`
and yields `eta=epsilon=0`.

If `Q'<=Q` and `tau(Q-Q')=0`, faithfulness gives `Q'=Q`; similarly `P'=P`.
The exact common-source calculation gives

```text
tau(Q)=tau(P)=tau(e)/128>0.                              (1)
```

Thus zero error authenticates both prescribed native rows on the fixed
positive corner.  The two affine selector equations then force the common
gauge fraction to be simultaneously `1/2` and `5/6`, which is impossible.
This is precisely the zero-loss case of the required firewall.

For completeness, the full signed Whitehead system does not evade the same
logic.  Its regular representation satisfies the literal word definition,
all coefficient occurrences, the cyclic amalgam edge, and the odd selector
row exactly, while the established calculation gives

```text
||(1-p_t)Wp_s||_2^2 >= 1/16.                            (2)
```

So adding that odd word to the finite menu cannot turn a tracial proof into
a finite-coordinate one.  Equations `(1)--(2)` prove the asserted scope.
