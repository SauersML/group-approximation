Put `S=Cu(A)`, `T=Cu(A tensor_min Z)` and let `phi:S->T` be the
first-factor Cu-morphism.  Assume that `x` satisfies `(SC1)` and that
`phi(x)<=phi(y)`.

First recover the ideal relation which strict comparison will need.  The
target inequality puts the ideal generated by `phi(x)` inside the ideal
generated by `phi(y)`.  The first-factor map identifies ideal lattices,
naturally carrying the ideal generated by a source class to the ideal
generated by its image.  Pulling the inclusion back gives

```text
x in Ideal_S(y).                                             (SC3)
```

Fix `x'<<x`.  By softness, choose `k>=1` such that

```text
(k+1)x' <= kx.
```

After applying `phi` and using the assumed comparison,

```text
(k+1)phi(x') <= k phi(x) <= k phi(y).                        (SC4)
```

Let `lambda` be any functional on `S` normalized by `lambda(y)=1`.
Only the generated ideal `J=Ideal_A(y)` matters.  Restricted to `Cu(J)`,
`lambda` is the functional associated to a densely defined lower
semicontinuous 2-quasitrace.  The first-factor quasitrace homeomorphism,
applied to `J -> J tensor_min Z`, lifts it to a functional `tilde_lambda` on
the corresponding target ideal with

```text
lambda = tilde_lambda o phi   on Cu(J).
```

Evaluation of `(SC4)` therefore gives

```text
lambda(x') <= k/(k+1) < 1 = lambda(y).                       (SC5)
```

Relation `(SC3)` and `x'<<x` imply `x'<=n y` for some `n`; equivalently,
`x'<=infinity*y`.  The functional characterization of almost
unperforation (strict comparison inside the generated ideal) applies to
`(SC5)` and yields `x'<=y`.

Choose a rapidly increasing sequence `x_1<<x_2<<...` with supremum `x`.
The preceding argument gives `x_n<=y` for every `n`, hence `x<=y`.  This is
`(SC2)`.  Applying it in both directions to two soft elements whose images
are equal proves injectivity and order reflection on the soft subsemigroup.

**Trust boundary.**  The standard correspondence between Cu functionals and
lower semicontinuous 2-quasitraces, and the functional characterization of
almost unperforation, are imported.  Ideal pullback and quasitrace pullback
for the actual first-factor map are supplied by the prerequisite.  The
softness estimate and the comparison proof are explicit above; no abstract
identification `Cu(A) tensor_Cu Cu(Z)=Cu(A tensor Z)` is used.
