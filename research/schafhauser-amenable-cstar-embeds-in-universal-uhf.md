**ESTABLISHED (literature import).**  Let `Q = M_1 (x) M_2 (x) M_3 (x) ...`
be the universal UHF algebra, and give `C*_r(G)` its canonical trace.

**Theorem B of Schafhauser, (1) implies (3).**  If `G` is a countable
discrete amenable group, then `C*_r(G)` admits a trace-preserving embedding
into `Q`.

Source: Christopher Schafhauser, *Subalgebras of simple AF-algebras*,
Ann. of Math. (2020), arXiv:1807.07381.

Because the embedding preserves the trace and the trace on `Q` is faithful,
it is automatically unital: `tau(1 - iota(1)) = 0` forces `iota(1) = 1`.
