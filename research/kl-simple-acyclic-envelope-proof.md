Artifact §2–§3. Let `(G, w, a)` be a failure: `deg_t(w) = m != 0` and
`1 != a in G` dies in `(G * <t>)/<<w>>`.

1. **Localize.** By the first prerequisite, `(A, w, a)` is a failure, where `A`
   is the finitely generated subgroup generated by the coefficients of `w`.
2. **Adjoin the prescribed group.** Given a countable `C`, the second
   prerequisite embeds `C` in a two-generated group `C'`. Put `B = A * C'`,
   which is finitely generated and contains `A` and `C`.
3. **Envelope.** The third prerequisite embeds `B` in a 2-generated simple
   acyclic `S`.
4. **Transfer.** The identity `a = prod_j u_j w^{e_j} u_j^{-1}` in `A * <t>`
   persists in `S * <t>`, since `A * <t> -> S * <t>` is injective. And `a != 1`
   in `S`. So `(S, w, a)` is a failure.
5. **`S` is infinite, hence nonabelian.** Finite groups are hyperlinear, so they
   satisfy the conjecture by the fourth prerequisite. Abelian simple groups
   are finite.
6. **Cyclic quotient.** The fifth prerequisite gives
   `(S * <t>)/<<w>> = C_|m|`.

Acyclicity of `S` is imported, never used in the argument; it is carried
through verbatim. ∎
