Every bounded 2-quasitrace on the full group C*-algebra `C*(PSL_2(Z)) = C^3 * C^2`
is a trace. Here `C^3 * C^2` is the universal unital C*-algebra of a three-block
partition of unity `e_1, e_2, e_3` and one projection `p`.

**This is equivalent to STW Problem I.** The implication into the root is
`stw01-problem-i-via-modular-group-algebra`, with the full proof in
`research/artifacts/stw01-modular-group-universal-test-2026-09-12.md`. The proof
reuses the reviewed Steps 0-3 of the three-projection test and replaces the two-block
dilation by a three-block one. For a finite-spectrum `y` and a projection `q` in a
`II_1` AW*-factor, the projection `r = c a^(-1) c*` in `M_3(M)`, with
`c = (a, (mq)^(1/2), (a - a^2 - mq)^(1/2))^T`, recovers `y` and `q` from
`f_1 r f_1` and `f_1 r f_2 r f_1`.

**Equivalent forms.** The statement holds iff it holds for `C*(Gamma)` for one
(equivalently every) countable group `Gamma` surjecting onto `PSL_2(Z)`, for example
`SL_2(Z)`, the braid group `B_3` (the trefoil knot group), or `F_2`. So Kaplansky's
quasitrace problem is the quasitrace problem for the full C*-algebra of the modular
group.

**Sharpness.** A two-block partition gives `C*(D_infinity)`, which is nuclear. Among
unital free products `C^m * C^n` with `m >= n >= 2`, `(3, 2)` is the least that can
carry a counterexample. `P_3` surjects onto `C^3 * C^2`, so this is the smaller host.

**Witness shape.** A counterexample can be taken as
`sigma(g(e_1 p e_1) + t e_1 p e_2 p e_1) != sigma(g(e_1 p e_1)) + t sigma(e_1 p e_2 p e_1)`,
with `g(0) = 0` and `t > 0`: two positive elements of one corner, tied by
`e_1 p e_1 - (e_1 p e_1)^2 = e_1 p e_2 p e_1 + e_1 p e_3 p e_1`.

## Attempts

- **Exactness (Haagerup).** Dead. `PSL_2(Z)` contains `F_2` with finite index, the
  full algebra of a subgroup embeds in the full algebra of the group, and `C*(F_2)`
  is not exact. The reduced algebra is exact, since virtually free groups are exact,
  so counterexamples live only on the full side.
- **Two-block partition.** Dead as a host: `C^2 * C^2 = C*(D_infinity)` is nuclear.
- **Projection counting.** Heuristically dead. By Cuntz's free-product sequence
  (primary not read), `K_0(C^3 * C^2) = Z^4`, generated by `[1], [e_1], [e_2], [p]`.
  A 2-quasitrace's values on projections are fixed by those four numbers, and a
  convex combination of the six characters realizes them. Witnesses need continuous
  functional calculus.
- **Corner algebra.** Not attempted to a conclusion. Deciding exactness of
  `e_1 (C^3 * C^2) e_1`, or finding a non-exact quotient of it that carries a faithful
  quasitrace, is the next concrete step.
