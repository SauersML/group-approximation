**OPEN (import not pinned at source).** Let `X* = <X | Y_1, ..., Y_k>` be a
cubical presentation in which `X` and every `Y_i` are compact graphs. Suppose
`X*` satisfies `C'(1/20)` and the `B(8)` condition (Jankiewicz--Wise,
*Cubulating small cancellation free products*, Definitions 2.2 and 2.3), and
`π_1 X*` is hyperbolic. Then `π_1 X*` acts properly and cocompactly on the
CAT(0) cube complex dual to the wallspace on the universal cover of `X*`.

**What is pinned.**

- Jankiewicz--Wise, Lemma 2.7, introduced as "The following appears as [Wis21,
  Cor 5.30]": "Let X* be B(8). Suppose that pieces have uniformly bounded
  diameter. Then for each wall W, the map N(W) → X̃* is a quasi-isometric
  embedding with uniform quasi-isometry constants."
- Jankiewicz--Wise, Theorem 2.4, from [FW21, Thm 3.5], gives properness and
  cocompactness only when "each Yi is compact and deformation retracts to a
  closed combinatorial geodesic wi".
- Jankiewicz--Wise, Theorem 3.1, is "a simplified restatement of [HW14, Thm
  7.12]": a relative cocompactness criterion for the dual cube complex.
- Martin--Steenbock (arXiv:1409.3678, section "Comparison to previous work of
  Wise on cubical small cancellation") on Wise's 2011 essay
  *The Structure of Groups with a Quasiconvex Hierarchy*:
  - "the generalised $B(6)$--condition [Def. 5.1] is a key ingredient to
    construct an appropriate wallspace structure for the group in [Th. 5.50],
    and to obtain the properness of the action on the dual CAT(0) cube
    complex."
  - "Indeed, properness is treated in Theorem 5.50 of [Wis11]."
  - "In Wise's Corollary 5.53 [Wis11] (and in other related results as
    mentioned above), cocompactness of the action follows from the
    hyperbolicity of the quotient group."
- The published version is cited by Jankiewicz--Wise as "[Wis21] Daniel T.
  Wise. The structure of groups with a quasiconvex hierarchy, volume 209 of
  Annals of Mathematics Studies. Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ,
  2021."

**What is not pinned.**

- The statements of Wise's Definition 5.1, Theorem 5.50 and Corollary 5.53, in
  the essay or in the book. Numbering may differ between the two versions.
- Whether the Jankiewicz--Wise `B(8)` condition, which they call "a special case
  of the B(8) condition within the context of C'(α) metric small-cancellation",
  meets those hypotheses for graph cones of rank at least two.

Until these statements are pinned verbatim, this claim stays OPEN.
