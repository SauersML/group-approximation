Choose the compatible reset plane `(CRH4)` and identify its three nonzero
directions equivariantly with the three radical directions.  For each
`h in S_3`, take a context-private copy of the proved one-direction affine
Clifford reset bridge, reparameterized to `h^(-1).a_0`.  Use independent
gauge shares in the six copies but identify every share product with the same
original logical BCS word.  Gauge doubling preserves every logical
constraint and the native mark.

Central-product the six reset packets over their common sign.  Each copy has
old and fresh full-character restriction `2 rho_15`, so the tensor product
has identical old/fresh restriction on both sides.  The factor-six
stabilization is fixed and multiplies every chart scale equally.  The selector
polarization theorem supplies one invariant fresh selector algebra containing
the three reset directions; the regular action permutes the six private
dummy/residual factors.

Regular-orbit induction makes the componentwise edge map an injective map of
finite `S_3` semidirect edge groups, even though
`unstabilized-reset-pencils-have-no-s3-symmetry` forbids an action on one
residual copy.  One stable word therefore implements the whole orbit.  In
finite matrices its six directional restrictions contain one common
multiplicity unitary, rather than six independent commutant rotations.

This is exactly a **holonomy synchronization** theorem.  It does not say that
the synchronized endpoint projections realize the two diagonal success
events and the crossed failure event in `(RNS1)`.  Such a claim cannot follow
from the induced finite edge alone: its regular stationary representation is
an exact finite-dimensional countermodel.  The endpoint conventions must be
tied to the global shared BCS words or to a non-finite return cell.
