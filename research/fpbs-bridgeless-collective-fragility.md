title: Connected graphs without bridges can have thinning thresholds tending to one

Proposition 1. For H_epsilon on T_(2r) square Z, almost surely pc(H_epsilon)>=q_0(epsilon,r)->1. The original H_epsilon is connected and has no bridges. This is NOT a counterexample for ordinary homogeneous Bernoulli percolation.
