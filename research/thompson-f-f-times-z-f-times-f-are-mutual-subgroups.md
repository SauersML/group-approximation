**ESTABLISHED** through `thompson-f-f-times-z-f-times-f-are-mutual-subgroups-proof`.

1. For dyadic rationals `a < b` in `[0,1]`, the subgroup `F_[a,b]` of elements of
   Thompson's group F supported in `[a,b]` is isomorphic to F.
2. `Stab_F(1/2) = F_[0,1/2] · F_[1/2,1]` is isomorphic to `F × F`, and F contains a
   subgroup isomorphic to `F × Z`.
3. Each of F, `F × Z`, `F × F` is isomorphic to a subgroup of each of the others.
   With word metrics, each coarsely embeds in each of the others.
4. F contains `Z^n` for every `n`, so the asymptotic dimension of F is infinite.

**Fence for Zaremsky Problem 2.4, parts (a) and (b).** Any quantity that is monotone
under coarse embeddings, such as asymptotic dimension, property A or coarse
embeddability in Hilbert space, takes the same value on F, `F × Z` and `F × F`.
Subgroup inclusions are also regular maps (Lipschitz with uniformly bounded fibres),
so the same holds for invariants monotone under regular maps. So none of these can
separate the groups in `thompson-f-qi-to-f-times-z-resolved` or
`thompson-f-qi-to-f-times-f-resolved`. This claim is a fence, not an answer: it
serves no route.
