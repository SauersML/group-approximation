Sources, read on MSI 2026-09-13 from the arXiv e-prints; labels are the TeX labels of the sources.
- **R. Sauer, E. Schesler, "An acylindrically hyperbolic group of non-uniform exponential growth", arXiv:2606.15945 (14 June 2026):**
  - `thm:G-subexponential`: Grigorchuk's bound, item 1;
  - `thm:BE`: Bartholdi–Erschler, Invent. Math. 189 (2012), Prop. 4.4 and Lemma 4.9, for the boundary orbit of `1^∞`;
  - `prop:BE-finite`: the transfer to `(X_n, ρ_n)` and `(X_n, η_n)` for `k ≤ r_n`, item 2, via `lem:local-convergence` and `cor:eta-local-convergence`;
  - `lem:separation`: item 3.
- **R. Sauer, E. Schesler, "A finitely presented group of non-uniform exponential growth", arXiv:2605.30163:** proof of Theorem 5.4 (source l.1392), item 4. The graph of translates of `{θ_n, ρ_n}` is complete bipartite, by two-point homogeneity of the level action (Bekka–de la Harpe–Grigorchuk, Appendix A2).
- **Item 5:** spherical transitivity (Bartholdi–Grigorchuk–Šunić 2003, §1), as recalled in both papers.

Not opened by this lane: the primary Bartholdi–Erschler paper. The constants are used only as absolute constants.
