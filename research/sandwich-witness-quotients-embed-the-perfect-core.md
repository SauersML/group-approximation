**OPEN.**

## Statement

Let `H` and `T` be finitely presented with `H <= [T,T]`, let `1 != g in H`, let
`S <= H` be an infinite simple (perfect) core, and put

    U = Z[T]<p,q> / (p(1-g)q - 1).

Let `I != U` be a two-sided ideal such that `PE_4(U/I)` is finitely presented and simple.
Then the composite `S -> U^x -> (U/I)^x` is injective.

## Where it is needed

Step 8 ("only if") of `non-directly-finite-ring-hosts-are-sandwich-ring-quotients-proof`
(line 138) asserts flatly "`S -> R^x` is injective" for an arbitrary witness `R = U/I`, with
no proof and no citation, and nothing in that route's `requires:` list yields it. The
assertion is load-bearing: it is what makes the witness ring an (NDF) host or a (DF) witness,
so without it the "only if" half of item 5, the Dichotomy, does not follow.

## Why it is not automatic

`S <= H <= T` maps to `(U/I)^x`, and its kernel, being normal in the simple group `S`, is `1`
or all of `S`. The relation `p(1-g)q = 1` only forces the image of `g` to differ from `1`. So
if `g` is not in `S`, nothing exhibited so far stops the entire core `S` from collapsing to `1`
in `(U/I)^x` — and then the witness ring is not an (NDF) host and the "only if" direction
fails.

The graph's own lemma for this, `ck-envelope-level-ideal-screen` (ESTABLISHED), does not close
it verbatim. Its hypothesis is "let `S` be an infinite simple group, choose `1 != g in S`", and
its proof of part (3) needs `g` in the two-sided ideal of `Z[S]` generated by the elements
`1-s`, i.e. it needs `g in S`. But `ck-sandwich-ring-has-fp-projective-simple-quotient` only
posits `1 != g in H`, with `H` possibly strictly larger than `S`. So the needed import is both
uncited and, as stated, quantified over a strictly stronger hypothesis than the hole supplies.

## What would settle it

Either a version of `ck-envelope-level-ideal-screen` whose fullness hypothesis is carried by
`1-g` for `g in H \ S` while still screening the `1-s` for `s in S` — the natural route is to
show `lev(M)` cannot contain any `1-s` once `1-g` is full in `U`, without assuming `g in S` —
or a normalisation of the hole that takes the marked element inside `S`, which would make
`ck-envelope-level-ideal-screen` apply verbatim and close this claim at once.
