## (b) ⇒ (a)

Take `D_n = d_n` and `π_n` the given same-dimensional corrections.

## (a) ⇒ (b)

Let `S` be a finite symmetric generating set. For a finitely generated group,
(b) says that for every asymptotic homomorphism `α_n : Γ -> U(d_n)`,

```text
η_n := inf over homomorphisms ρ : Γ -> U(d_n) of max_(s∈S) ||α_n(s) - ρ(s)||_2  ->  0,
```

since control on `S` controls every fixed word.

Suppose (b) fails. Then some asymptotic homomorphism `α_n` has `η_n >= c > 0`
for all `n` in an infinite set `A`. Choose a nonprincipal ultrafilter `ω`
containing `A`, and let `τ = lim_ω tr α_n`.

1. **(F) holds along ω.** Flexible stability (a) applied to the whole sequence
   `α_n` gives `π_n : Γ -> U(D_n)` with `D_n/d_n -> 1` and compression
   closeness for every `g`, in particular along `ω`.
2. **τ is trivial on Rad_fd(Γ).** Steps 1--2 of
   `injective-limit-trace-flexible-hs-repair-is-strict-proof` make `τ` a
   pointwise limit of normalized traces of genuine finite-dimensional
   representations `σ_k`. Each `σ_k` is trivial on `Rad_fd(Γ)`, so
   `τ(h) = 1` for `h ∈ Rad_fd(Γ)`.
3. **π_τ factors through the amenable quotient.** For `h ∈ Rad_fd(Γ)`,
   `||π_τ(h) - 1||_2^2 = 2 - 2 Re τ(h) = 0`. The trace is faithful on
   `L_τ(Γ)`, so `π_τ(h) = 1`, and `π_τ` factors through
   `Q = Γ/Rad_fd(Γ)`.
4. **(I) holds.** `L_τ(Γ) = π_τ(Q)''` is generated by a unitary
   representation of the amenable group `Q`. Averaging
   `x ↦ π_τ(q) x π_τ(q)^*` against an invariant mean on `Q` gives a conditional
   expectation of `B(H_τ)` onto the commutant `π_τ(Q)'`. So `π_τ(Q)'` is
   injective, and a von Neumann algebra is injective if and only if its
   commutant is. Hence `L_τ(Γ)` is injective.
5. **Contradiction.** By `injective-limit-trace-flexible-hs-repair-is-strict`
   there are homomorphisms `ρ_n : Γ -> U(d_n)` with
   `lim_ω max_s ||α_n(s) - ρ_n(s)||_2 = 0`. So `lim_ω η_n = 0`, contradicting
   `η_n >= c` on `A ∈ ω`. ∎

## Scope notes

- Finite generation is used only to reduce (b) to the generators. For
  countable amenable groups, the Hadwin--Shulman route in the claim body gives
  matricial stability in their Definition 3. Identifying that with Dogon's
  pointwise same-dimension notion is the usual ultrafilter-to-sequence
  argument (their Proposition 1 does it for finitely presented groups). It is
  not written out here, so the claim is stated for finitely generated groups.
- Step 4 is where amenability of the almost periodic quotient enters. For an
  infinite residually finite non-amenable group, the regular trace `δ_e` is a
  limit of finite-dimensional characters (Hadwin--Shulman Proposition 3), and
  `L(Γ)` is not injective. So the argument stops exactly at the groups where
  a separation could exist.
