Let G be any group and A a finite nonempty alphabet. Let tau,sigma be
cellular automata on A^G with sigma tau=id. On rational local observables
modulo constants set T=tau*, S=sigma*, E=I-ST, and let P_0 be the module
generated by all one-site observables. Write theta=E|P_0, and let delta
be the von Neumann rank of this map between finite pattern-orbit modules.

Let V_n be the Q[G]-span of w(P_0) over words w in T,S of length at most
n, and d_n the von Neumann dimension of its closure inside the finite
pattern-orbit module containing these words. All completions and
stabilizer weights are defined in Section 1 of the artifact.

Then delta=0 exactly when tau is surjective. For every n>=1,

    dim_G closure(sum_{j=0}^{n-1} S^j theta(P_0)) = n delta,
    d_(n+1) >= n delta.

Consequently liminf d_n/n=0 implies that tau is bijective. A sublinear
weighted count of the containing pattern orbits is also sufficient.
Every injective full-shift automaton has a local decoder, so the
criterion applies to all injective automata satisfying that extra bound.

The factorization through finite Hilbert modules is essential: the proof
does not assume that global pullbacks are bounded or that tensoring with
the group von Neumann algebra preserves arbitrary injections. It proves
neither a universal sublinear upper bound nor Gottschalk's conjecture.
