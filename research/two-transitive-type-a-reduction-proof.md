Complete direct proof, written out in the target node.  No Lean counterpart
exists.

The statement is deliberately about **two-element subsets** rather than
ordered pairs, because that is the form type (A) uses; the passage from one
to the other is the only place the argument does anything, and it goes
through because every two-element subset underlies an ordered pair of
distinct elements.
