**OPEN.** Notation as in `level15-lattice-has-invariant-parity-character-detecting-h2`.
Some group homomorphism `theta : D_C(N)^x -> Z/2` has `theta o sigma = theta` and
`theta(h^2) = 1`.

With `twisted-norm-equation-dies-under-invariant-parity-character`, this gives
Strong Atiyah for the level-15 lattice `H`
(`level15-torsion-free-lattice-satisfies-strong-atiyah`).

## Attempts

1. **Group level.** Holds: `level15-lattice-has-invariant-parity-character-detecting-h2`
   gives `theta_N` on `N`. Every character of `N` into `Z/2` factors through reduction
   mod 5 to a group of order 16.
2. **What any extension must avoid.** A homomorphism to `Z/2` kills squares and
   commutators of `D_C(N)^x`, and an invariant one kills `sigma(u) u^-1`. So the claim
   needs `h^2` outside the subgroup generated by `(D^x)^2`, `[D^x, D^x]` and the
   `sigma(u) u^-1`, where `D = D_C(N)`. Every complex scalar is a square, so the
   character must be invisible on `C^x`. Neither membership nor non-membership of
   `h^2` is decided.
3. **Real determinants.** Real multiplicative invariants such as the Fuglede--Kadison
   determinant take values in a divisible group, so they cannot detect parity.
4. **Untested.** A valuation- or reduction-type invariant on `D_C(N)` that remembers
   `N -> K`, e.g. through the 5-adic structure of `Gamma(15)` or the Farkas--Linnell
   construction. The obstruction is that no ring map from `D_C(N)` to a finite or
   5-adic ring is known.
