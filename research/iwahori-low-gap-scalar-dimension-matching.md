title: Low-gap Iwahori pairs carry an almost-full integral dimension matching
---

In the Iwahori transport setup, choose `a_n->0` with `E_n/a_n->0` and allow
an edge precisely when `lambda_(n,i,j)<a_n`.  Then there are integer edge
multiplicities `x_(n,i,j)>=0`, supported only on low-gap pairs, which match all
but `o(d_n)` source dimensions and all but `o(d_n)` target dimensions.

Thus there is no residual scalar Hall obstruction in
`iwahori-fractional-transport-integralization`.  The remaining obstruction is
operator-valued: realize these dimensions by mutually orthogonal physical
subspaces and make the corresponding partial intertwiners assemble close to
the original identity.
