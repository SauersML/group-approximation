Literature import. Read from the arXiv:math/0106063v6 PDF (7 May 2015, "Arxiv Version 1.0 of April 2015"),
pp. 321--322, by text extraction on 2026-09-13 (lane ex2-sl3z-cocompact-weak-containment).

p. 321: *"Recall: The Standing Assumptions (4.0.0 on page 43) are in effect, so, as always, Γ is a lattice
in the semisimple Lie group G ⊆ SL(ℓ,R)."*

p. 322: *"(16.1.1) Theorem (Margulis). Assume • G = SL(k,R), with k ≥ 3, • G/Γ is not compact, and •
ϕ: Γ → GL(n,R) is any homomorphism. Then there exist: • a continuous homomorphism ϕ̂: G → GL(n,R), and •
a finite-index subgroup Γ′ of Γ, such that ϕ̂(γ) = ϕ(γ) for all γ ∈ Γ′. Proof. See Exercise 16.4#2."*

Standing Assumptions, p. 43: *"(4.0.0) Standing Assumptions. Throughout this book: 1) G is a linear,
semisimple Lie group ... with only finitely many connected components, and 2) Γ is a lattice in G."*

**Trust surface.** The book deduces 16.1.1 as an exercise from the general Theorem 16.1.4, which it
proves in Section 16.5. That theorem has a compact error term, and the book says the error "does not
arise" in this special case. Only the statement was read, not the exercise solution. The original
source is Margulis, *Discrete Subgroups of Semisimple Lie Groups* (1991), not consulted here.
