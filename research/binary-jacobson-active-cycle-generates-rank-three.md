**ESTABLISHED.**  Put

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,       Q=1-ST.
```

Inside the upper-left `3 x 3` block of `EL_5(J)`, let

```text
a_12=[[S,Q,0],[0,T,0],[0,0,1]]
```

and let `C` be the subgroup generated by the three directed constant-cycle
roots

```text
x_12(1),       x_23(1),       x_31(1).                 (J3G1)
```

Then

```text
<a_12,C> = EL_3(J)                                      (J3G2)
```

in that block.  In particular the subgroup contains every head root
`x_ij(Q)`, including the nontrivial mark `x_13(Q)`.

Thus the internal-cycle alternative is the exact boundary of the preceding
amenable fences.  Constant roots in external target columns, even two
columns joined by their full constant `EL_2(F_2)`, remain in an amenable
upper parabolic.  The same three-cycle placed on the active rows does not
produce a smaller finite Hall gadget: together with one balanced occurrence
it already presents the whole rank-three elementary Jacobson core.  Any
matrix-only selector extracted from this package would therefore be a direct
attack on head-root MF-invisibility, not a consequence of an amenable or
typed-rectangular intermediate subsystem.
