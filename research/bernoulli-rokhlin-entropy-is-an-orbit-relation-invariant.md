**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed)** by [[bernoulli-rokhlin-entropy-orbit-relation-invariance-proof]].

Let `G ↷ (Y, ν)` be an essentially free p.m.p. action of a countably infinite group, and let (L, λ) be a
standard probability space. Put

    E_λ(G ↷ Y) := h_G(L^G × Y, λ^G × ν | B(Y)),

Seward's relative Rokhlin entropy of the diagonal action over the base factor.

1. **Invariance.** If `G ↷ Y` and `H ↷ Z` are free and orbit equivalent, then `E_λ(G ↷ Y) = E_λ(H ↷ Z)`.
   So `E_λ(R)` is well defined on measured relations generated by free actions.
2. **Bernoulli value (A1).** If Y is free and weakly contained in all free G-actions, for example
   `[0,1]^G`, then `h^Rok_G(L^G, λ^G) = E_λ(R_Y)`.
3. **Upper bound (A2).** For every free action `Γ ↷ W`, `E_λ(R_W) <= h^Rok_Γ(L^Γ, λ^Γ) <= H(λ)`.

**Consequence.** Seward's direct-product conjecture at X = `L^G` is equivalent to constancy of `R ↦ E_λ(R)`
on the orbit relations of free G-actions. A lower bound for `h^Rok_{U_*}` of Bernoulli shifts may be
sought on any measured relation that is isomorphic to the orbit relation of a weakly minimal free
`U_*`-action, with no reference to the group. The group consequences are in
`weakly-minimal-oe-transfers-rokhlin-supremum`.
