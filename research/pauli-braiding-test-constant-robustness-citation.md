Established by A. Natarajan and T. Vidick, *Robust self-testing of
many-qubit states*, arXiv:1610.03574.  The Pauli braiding test is developed in
Section 4.  Its robustness is independent of the number of tested qubits, and
the Magic-Square instantiation has perfect completeness, as recorded in the
paper's introduction and Section 4.
