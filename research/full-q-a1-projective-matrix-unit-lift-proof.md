Index the seven active projective summands by

```text
I=(q@2,q@4,q@5,q@6,e_1@7,e_2@8,e_3@9).                (1)
```

For a `q` summand put `S_q=s_1,T_q=t_1`.  For `e_m` put

```text
S_m=s_(0^m),                  T_m=t_(0^m).             (2)
```

For every label `i` in `(1)`, prefix cancellation gives

```text
T_iS_i=1.                                              (3)
```

Different labels may use the same Leavitt coefficient (the four `q`
labels do), so no coefficient-level assertion `T_iS_j=0` is made for
`i!=j`.  Orthogonality instead comes from the distinct ambient coordinates:

```text
(S_iT_j E_(c_i,c_j))(S_kT_l E_(c_k,c_l))
  =delta_(j,k) S_iT_l E_(c_i,c_l).                    (3a)
```

Indeed the matrix-coordinate product supplies `delta_(j,k)`, and in its
nonzero case prefix cancellation supplies `T_jS_j=1`.

The ambient coordinates in `(1)` are distinct.  Therefore, for `i!=j`, set

```text
Phi(x_ij(1))=x_(c_i,c_j)(S_iT_j),                      (4)
```

where `c_i` is the ambient coordinate of the summand.  Equations `(3)--(3a)` and
the ordinary Steinberg commutator rows prove every defining relation of
`St_7(F_2)` for `(4)`.  Hence `(4)` extends to the homomorphism `(LAF3)`.

Here is the complete coefficient audit for the literal packet:

```text
binary arrow       Leavitt coefficient S_target T_source
q5->q6             q=s_1t_1
q4->q5             q
q2->q4             q
q2->u2             b_2=s_00t_1
q4->u3             b_3=s_000t_1
q2->u3             b_3
u1->u3             b_3a_1=s_000t_0
u1->q5             a_1=s_1t_0
u1->q6             a_1
u2->q6             a_2=s_1t_00
u2->q5             a_2
u3->q6             a_3=s_1t_000
q4->u2             b_2
q5->u3             b_3
u1->q4             a_1.                                (5)
```

The first twelve rows are the pattern group `K`; the next two are `B_2,B_3`;
the last is `A_1`.  The two partial Whiteheads are also in the same image:

```text
J_1=Phi(w_(u2,u1)(1)),        J_2=Phi(w_(u3,u2)(1)),   (6)
```

because their off-diagonal entries are respectively

```text
S_2T_1=x_1, S_1T_2=y_1,       S_3T_2=x_2, S_2T_3=y_2.
```

Thus every literal generator in `(LAF1)` belongs to `Phi(St_7(F_2))`, and
its elementary image is exactly the binary matrix used in the finite audit.

For completeness, the finite-field Steinberg kernel here is trivial.  In
rank at least three the kernel is generated by Steinberg symbols of field
units; `F_2^x={1}`, and the normalized symbols involving `1` are the
identity.  Hence

```text
St_7(F_2)=SL_7(F_2).                                   (7)
```

The elementary image of `(4)` is faithful: on the direct sum `(1)` it is
the standard seven-dimensional binary action, and it is the identity on
the complementary projective summand.  If `Phi(g)=1`, its elementary image
is therefore the identity binary matrix, so `(7)` gives `g=1`.  This proves
injectivity of `Phi`.

Finally `(5)--(6)` show that the subgroup generated by the literal words is
the `Phi`-image of precisely the subgroup generated by their binary
matrices, namely `P_A`.  This proves `(LAF2)` and transports the complete
finite group algebra, not just its elementary quotient, into `Delta`.
