**Dead.** The proposed model takes a unitary representation carrying a
covariant copy of `C(Cantor)`, such as the Koopman representation with
Radon--Nikodym factors or a quasi-regular representation on a point orbit,
and compresses it to finite-rank subspaces that are almost invariant in
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm. `thompson-v-covariant-cantor-representations-are-not-amenable`
shows no such subspaces exist. Any HS model of `V` must come from a
representation without a covariant Cantor structure, or not be a compression
of any representation.
