Use `H,U,V,C_1,W` from `(QJM1)--(QJM2)`. The commuting
involutions `H,C_1,W` define four projections

```text
G_(sigma,tau)
 =E(1+sigma C_1)(1+tau W)/4,       sigma,tau in {+1,-1}. (QCS1)
```

They are pairwise orthogonal, have sum `E`, and for every qutrit atom
`z` satisfy

```text
tau(zG_(sigma,tau))=1/72.                               (QCS2)
```

Thus on `R=e_0+e_1` each sign quarter has trace `1/36` and all four
quarters recover `tau(ER)=1/9`.

Let `g_1=(c_0d_0d_1)` be the support-minimal complete router and put
`M={c_0,d_0,d_1}`. Its conjugates give

```text
U_m=x_(c_1,m)(T),          V_m=x_(m,c_3)(S),             m in M.
```

Before adjoining the qutrit torus, the group generated by these roots,
`H`, and `g_1` is

```text
N semidirect <g_1>,             |N|=2^12, |<g_1>|=3.    (QCS3)
```

The normal 2-group `N=1+mathcal n` has the twelve independent root
directions

```text
x_(c_2,c_1)(Q),                                      1
x_(c_1,m)(T),                                        3
x_(m,c_3)(S),                                        3
x_(c_2,m)(QT),                                       3
x_(c_1,c_3)(1),                                      1
x_(c_2,c_3)(Q).                                      1  (QCS4)
```

All mixed commutators, including `TS=1` and `QTS=Q`, are already
contained in this list.

Adjoining `C=<J,Z>` still gives a finite group:

```text
<C,g_1,H,U,V> is finite.                                (QCS5)
```

Indeed the finite scalar group `<C,g_1>` preserves the four-stage
`C`-invariant flag

```text
c_2 -> c_1 -> span_C(M) -> c_3.                         (QCS6)
```

The span of all scalar conjugates of the twelve root spaces is a
finite-dimensional strictly upper-triangular `F_2`-algebra. Path length
is at most three, so its coefficient closure uses only
`Q,T,S,QT,1` and the reductions `TS=1,QTS=Q`. Its algebra group is
finite and is normalized by `<C,g_1>`.

Consequently every linear combination of sign-branch partial isometries,
every cross term in its polar square, and every qutrit routing word made
from `C,g_1,H,U,V` lies in the complex group algebra of one finite
group. Its faithful regular representation has the canonical qutrit
weights and realizes all four projections in `(QCS1)` exactly.

This is an explicit approximate-model firewall. Two sign quarters have
total `R`-trace `1/18` and can fit one target `Ee_2`; any proposed sum
of three or four into that same target fails to be an isometry already
in the finite regular model. Cross terms or leakage remove the excess.
No Cuntz pair with two full-domain left inverses can arise in this finite
packet.

The long scalar cycle `g_2` destroys the flag `(QCS6)` and enlarges the
ambient group to `EL_28(J)`, where no finite regular head model exists.
Thus the exact next calculation is a sign-conditioned word involving
`g_2`. Recombining signs with the minimal router `g_1` alone is
completely absorbed.
