Let `N` be the normal closure of `V` in the reduced seam.  The forced V4
bridge supplies a nonidentity element of `N cap J`, where the collision
vertex is `J=S4`.  Its normal closure in `J` is the normal Klein four, which
contains the labeled collision involution `c`.  Thus `c` lies in `N`.
The first-packet subgroup `<c,z>=S3` and normality of `N` then put `z` in
`N` as well.

After the last forward relation `(z b)^3=1` is imposed, quotienting by the
image of `N` gives `b^3=b=1`, because `z=1` and `b^2=1`.  Hence `b` also
lies in the image of `N`.  The normal-subgroup lattice of
`Q=2^5:A5` from `atlas-q1920-is-radial-holonomy-extension` shows that `V`
together with the normal closure of `b` generates all of `Q`.  The full group
is generated by `N` and `Q`, so it equals the image of `N`, proving normal
generation by `V`.
