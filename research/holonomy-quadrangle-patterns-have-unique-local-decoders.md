**Setting.** A finite pattern `w: F × E -> S` has four axioms.
- (L): rows are injective.
- (R): columns are injective.
- (Q): quadrangle closure, meaning the classes `R(c, c')` are disjoint or equal.
- (H): a composite of classes that fixes one position is the identity on its domain.

Fix a split `E = U ⊔ V` with `U != ∅`. A **gadget** is a class of rows with equal pointer site set
`cU`. Given a value functional `T: V -> F_q`, a **local decoder** of the site `o` in the gadget `ρ` is
`σ: ρ -> F_q` with `Σ_c σ_c Σ_{e∈V} T_e [ce] = [o]`.

**Claim (Theorem A and Corollary B of the artifact).**

1. **Uniqueness.** Suppose the pattern satisfies (L), (R), (Q) and (H).
   - For every `T` and every site `o`, at most one gadget has a local decoder of `o`.
   - That decoder is unique.
   - `T` vanishes outside one component of the value graph Γ.
   - In the gadget, the decoder is a unit of the finite algebra `F_q[Π]`, where `Π ≤ Sym(U)` is
     generated by the pointer permutations of within-gadget classes.
2. **Class kill.** Consider split rotation-type codes `y_c = (j_c, Σ_e T_e x(ce)[j_c])`, with `j`
   constant on gadgets and every recovery gadget-local.
   - On any (L)(R)(Q)(H) pattern, `H(x(o) | y_F) >= (k-1) log q` and `Phi >= log q`.
   - So every (H)-respecting repair of the rotation gadget of
     `quadrangle-closed-patterns-do-not-force-domination`, and every gadget-local amplification firewall, is dominated.
   - **Invariant:** unit-ness of the pushed-forward decoder `s_ρ ∈ F_q[Π]`.
   - **Step where every member dies:** a second gadget must read `o` in the same intrinsic columns
     `e_x = φ^{-1}(x^{-1})` with different rows, which violates (R).
3. **What survives.** Decoders that mix rows with different pointer site sets. This matches the
   survivor of Corollary 4, item 3, of the artifact of `coset-local-decoders-force-gated-domination`.
