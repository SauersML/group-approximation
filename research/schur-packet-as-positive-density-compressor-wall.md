Construct a finitely presented property-`(T)` base `Gamma`, injective
compressor `alpha`, and Schur packet words sharing the self-referential BCS
selectors so that, in every sufficiently accurate marked matrix microstate:

1. finite packet exactification produces the Reynolds difference
   `R_A-R_B` on forbidden selector sectors;
2. after the two-chart alignment, that difference is contained in the
   `b`-low spectral space of the compressed adjoint Laplacian and orthogonal
   to the `a`-low spectral space of the original Laplacian, up to `O(delta)`
   density loss; and
3. the packet lower bound `(ARG1)` survives with the form `(SPW1)`.

Then `schur-wall-in-property-t-compressor-pays-hs-energy` controls every
context's forbidden mass. Together with the self-referential BCS signal gap,
this gives `hs-groupify-self-referential-bcs-signal`.

## Attempts

- The truncated `P/Q` charts supply a fixed transverse Pauli cell, but their
  current linear group is only a relative-Kazhdan semidirect product. One
  route is to enlarge the linear part to a higher-rank elementary group so
  that the whole base has property `(T)`, while preserving the Iwahori-style
  compressor and the local packet cell.
- A stabilized recursive packet tail remains useful algebraically, but no
  termwise finite Reynolds conjugacy is needed. It suffices to show its local
  `R_A-R_B` directions have low compressed energy and a fixed original
  spectral gap. This is the exact placement calculation still open.
- **Toeplitz universal-lattice substrate.**
  `schur-child-crosses-toeplitz-compressor` now embeds every fixed Schur child
  across one explicit proper self-image of a property-`(T)` elementary group.
  The purely local inference is false by
  `residual-action-can-erase-local-reynolds-density`: the rest of the
  compressed group may generate the full external multiplicity algebra.
  The live Toeplitz route therefore asks for the stronger recursive dichotomy
  `toeplitz-tail-retains-contextual-schur-density`, which sends precisely that
  residual erasure into the next self-similar tail rather than ignoring it.
- **Concrete polynomial-compressor wall.**
  `polynomial-compressor-has-explicit-finite-quotient-wall` shows that the
  existing property-`(T)` compressor `y->xy` has a fixed finite quotient
  `G^2<G^3` with a strictly positive normalized adjoint commutant jump. Thus
  existence of a compressor wall is no longer abstract. The remaining issue
  is coefficient covariance: identify the forbidden Schur Reynolds range
  with this intrinsic wall without allowing the tensor-decoupled model.
