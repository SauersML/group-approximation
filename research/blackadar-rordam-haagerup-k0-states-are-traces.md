**Established (literature).**  Let `A` be unital, exact and stably finite.
Every state on the ordered group `(K_0(A), K_0^+(A), [1])` has the form
`[p]-[q] -> (Tr tensor tau)(p-q)` for a tracial state `tau` of `A`.

The two inputs are Blackadar--Rordam, *Extending states on preordered
semigroups and the existence of quasitraces on C\*-algebras*, J. Algebra 152
(1992), 240--247: every state on `K_0(A)` of a unital algebra is induced by a
quasitrace.  And Haagerup: quasitraces on exact C\*-algebras are traces.

Status note: both are standard imports.  The Blackadar--Rordam application to
`K_0` was confirmed from the paper's published description, not from its
full text.
