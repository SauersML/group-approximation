The full proof is in `research/artifacts/lodha-circle-group-type-a-action-2026-09-16.md`.
It uses only the imported statements (L2) and (L3), which are parts 2 and 3 of the required
claim. Section numbers below refer to the artifact.

**Outline.**
1. **§1, the circle action through the Cantor model.**
   - `Φ` maps the rational sequences `Rat` onto `QP^1`, and only `0^∞`, `1^∞` go to `∞`.
   - The other rational sequences fall into pairs `{s01^∞, s10^∞}` on which `Φ` is constant.
   - For `S_C = ⟨x, x_1, p_0, w_{10,110}⟩ ≤ Homeo(2^N)`, (L3) and density of `QP^1` give a
     surjective homomorphism `ι: S_C → S` with `Φ(ξ·g) = Φ(ξ)·ι(g)` on `Rat`.
2. **§2, tree diagrams.**
   - `T_C` (cyclic diagrams) is a group equal to `⟨x, x_1, p_0⟩`, and
     `F_C = Stab_{T_C}(0^∞) = ⟨x, x_1⟩`. This is proved by rotation at `1^k`, with the
     number of zeros in the leaves as potential, and by `p_n = x_{1^n} p_{n+1}`.
3. **§§3–5, the product decomposition.**
   - Conjugation: `g^{-1} y_σ g = y_{σ·g}` for `g ∈ T_C`.
   - Amplification: `y_u = x_u y_{u0} y_{u10}^{-1} y_{u11}`.
   - Every balanced product of `y`'s lies in `F_C·N_m` for all `m`, where `N_m` is
     generated by the `w_{σ,τ}` with `|σ|, |τ| ≥ m`. The induction uses the potential
     `Σ 4^{max(0, m−|u_i|)}`.
   - Hence `N·T_C` is a subgroup, and `S_C = N·T_C` once all `w_{σ,τ}` lie in `S_C` (§6).
4. **§6, all `w_{σ,τ}` lie in `S_C`.** `T_C` matches independent pairs with the same
   gap pattern. The four patterns have representatives `(10,110)`, `(110,10)`, `(10,1101)`
   and `(0,1)`, via Lodha's identity at l.646 and the decomposition
   `w_{0,1} = x_0 x_1^{-1} w_{00,010} w_{011,100} w_{101,11}`.
5. **§7, the stabilizer.**
   - `0^∞·(Wf) = 0^∞·f` for `W ∈ N`, and no element of `T_C` sends `0^∞` to `1^∞`. So
     `Stab_S(∞) = ι(N·F_C)`.
   - `F_C` matches pairs with the same order pattern, and there are eight patterns. This
     gives the ten generators.
6. **§8, 2-transitivity.**
   - `F_C` maps `s01^∞` to `u01^∞` for all `s, u`, so `ι(F_C) ≤ Stab_S(∞)` is transitive
     on `Q`.
   - `l = ι(p_0)` swaps `∞` and `0`, which gives 2-transitivity.
   - Faithfulness holds because `QP^1` is dense.

**Checks.** `experiments/lodha-circle-stabilizer-2026-09-16/check_identities.py` verifies
exactly, on all 1024 rational sequences with words of length at most 9:
- the intertwining (L3);
- the amplification identities;
- the identity for `w_{0,1}` and Lodha's l.646 identity;
- `p_n = x_{1^n} p_{n+1}` for `n ≤ 4`;
- sample conjugations.

These are finite samples. The hand proofs in the artifact are what the route asserts.

**Not used.** Injectivity of `ι`, Lodha's standard forms, his Lemma `Taction`, and his
finite presentation proof.
