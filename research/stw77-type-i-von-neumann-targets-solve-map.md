distinct_from:
  stw77-bounded-operator-target-case: that treats one type-I factor; this treats measurable direct integrals of such factors.
  stw77-products-of-bounded-operator-targets-solve-map: that assembles arbitrary bounded products coordinatewise; this requires measurable selection over a diffuse center.
---

Let `A` be separable and unital, let `M` be a type-I von Neumann algebra
with separable predual, and let

```text
phi:A->M
```

be a unital homomorphism. If every nonzero positive element of `phi(A)` is
properly infinite in `M`, then `phi` is O-infinity-stable.

Neither exactness of `A` nor finite nuclear dimension of `phi` is needed.
