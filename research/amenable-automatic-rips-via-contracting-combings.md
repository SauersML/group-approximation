Let `G` be amenable and automatic. By the first prerequisite some word metric on `G`
admits a contracting combing. By Alonso's theorem (second prerequisite) `VR_t(G)` is
contractible for some `t` in that metric, which is a contractible Rips complex
`P_t(G,S)`.
