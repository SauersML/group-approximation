For every finite subgroup of the canonical base representation, the restricted
character is normalized regular.  Two trace-preserving embeddings of its
finite-dimensional complex group algebra into a finite factor are unitarily
conjugate by matching central blocks and matrix units.  Embed finite vertex
groups successively along a maximal tree and use this conjugacy for every
non-tree stable letter.  This constructs all finite Bass--Serre auxiliaries in
the same matrix-ultraproduct factor, exactly as detailed in the target claim.
