For a property-(T) subgroup `L` of `H`, every finite normal subgroup of `H`
contained in the normal closure of commutators
`[g z g^-1, ell]`, where `g` one-sidedly compresses `L`, `z` centralizes
`L`, and `ell in L`, is killed by every norm-matrix-corona representation
of `H`.
