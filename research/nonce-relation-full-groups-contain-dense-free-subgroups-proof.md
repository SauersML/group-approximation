**Part 1.** `non-cohyperlinear-irs-exists` gives an IRS `H` of `F_2` whose GNS algebra `L(F_2/H)` is not CE
("Passing between nonabelian finite-rank free groups preserves the conclusion"). The argument recorded in
`non-ce-property-t-equivalence-relation-exists` starts "from an ergodic non-co-hyperlinear IRS `H` of a free group":
"the GNS algebra `L(F/H)` embeds trace-preservingly into the von Neumann algebra of an ergodic relation realizing
`H` as its stabilizer distribution". Let `R_H` be that relation, the orbit relation of an ergodic pmp action
`a : F_2 -> Aut(X, mu)`. It is generated by `a(s_1), a(s_2)`, a graphing of cost 2, so `cost(R_H) <= 2`. CE passes to
trace-preserving subalgebras, so `L(R_H)` is not CE.

The space is nonatomic. An ergodic pmp relation on a probability space with an atom is transitive on finitely many
atoms of equal mass. Its von Neumann algebra is then a matrix algebra, which is CE.

**Part 2 (import).** F. Le Maître, *Highly faithful actions and dense free subgroups in full groups*, Groups Geom.
Dyn. 12 (2018), no. 1, 207--230. Abstract, source-read from the EMS Press page on 2026-09-17, verbatim: "In
particular, we obtain that the full group of a measure-preserving ergodic equivalence of cost less than m contains a
dense free subgroup on m generators." The topology is the uniform topology of the full group, the one in which Le
Maître's topological generators are defined (Le Maître, Invent. Math. 198 (2014) 261--268). With `cost(R_H) <= 2 < 3`
this gives a dense `Gamma ≅ F_3` in `[R_H]`.

**Part 3.**
- **Hyperlinear.** Brown--Dykema--Jung, *Free entropy dimension in amalgamated free products*,
  arXiv:math/0609080v5, Corollary 4.5, as quoted in `amenable-by-virtually-free-twisted-crossed-products-are-ce-proof`:
  "Let `M1 *_B M2` be the amalgamated free product of tracial von Neumann algebras `M1` and `M2` over a hyperfinite
  von Neumann algebra `B`, and assume each `Mi` has separable predual and is embeddable in `R^omega`. Then
  `M1 *_B M2` is embeddable in `R^omega`." We have `L(F_3) = L(Z) * L(Z) * L(Z)`, a free product over `B = C`, and
  `L(Z) ≅ L^infinity(T)` is abelian, hence CE. Two applications give `L(F_3)` CE. Condition 2 ⟹ 1 of
  `hyperlinear-iff-group-factor-embeds-in-hyperfinite-ultrapower` gives `F_3` hyperlinear.
- **GNS algebra.** `R_H` is ergodic on a nonatomic space and `Gamma` is countable and uniformly dense. By
  `dense-full-group-subgroups-generate-relation-factor`, `chi_R|Gamma` is extremal with GNS algebra `L(R_H)`, which is
  not CE.

Part 2 for a general `R` of cost `< m` is the same import, and Part 3 is the same argument with `F_m`.
