Source: O. Kharlampovich, A. Myasnikov, M. Sapir, *Algorithmically complex
residually finite groups*, arXiv:1204.6506v5 (22 Mar 2013); Bull. Math. Sci.
7 (2017) 309--352.  Read from the v5 PDF on 2026-09-12, pages 9--28.

- Section 4.1 defines `G(M)` by the relations G1--G8, with the command table
  `(7)`.
- **Theorem 4.3.** "(a) The group `G(M)` belongs to `A_p^2 A cap ZN_(K+1) A`.
  (b) The equality `x_(q_i A_0) * a_1^(m_1) * ... * a_K^(m_K) * A_1^(al_1) *
  ... * A_K^(al_K) = x_(q_j A_0) * a_1^(n_1) * ... * A_K^(be_K)` where
  `al_i, be_i in {0,1}` is true in `G(M)` if and only if the equality
  `q_i a_1^(m_1) ... A_K^(al_K) = q_j a_1^(n_1) ... A_K^(be_K)` is true in the
  semigroup `S(M)`."
- **Lemma 4.4.** "The subgroup `<H_1 cup H_2>` of `G` is metabelian and a
  semidirect product of the Abelian normal subgroup `H_1^(H_2)` of exponent
  `p`, and `H_2`."
- **Lemma 4.5.** "The normal subgroup `T` of `G` generated by all the elements
  `x_u, u in U`, is Abelian of exponent `p`."
- **Lemmas 4.10--4.11.**  The map to the model `Ḡ = T_1 x| <automorphisms>`
  (automorphisms displayed in formula `(13)` and on pages 23--24) extends to a
  surjective homomorphism.
- **Lemma 4.14.** "The normal subgroup `T` generated by the elements `x_u`,
  `u in U` in `G = G(M)` is the direct product of cyclic subgroups generated
  by the elements `x_(i,w)`, `i in {1,2,3}^({1,...,K})`, `w in W cup W_0`."
  Its proof shows that the images in `T_1` of the `x_(i,w)` with `w` nonzero are
  linearly independent.  For `G(M)` as displayed (G1--G8) this does *not* make
  `T -> T_1` injective: there is no stop relation, and `x_(q_0 A_0)` is
  nontrivial but maps to `1`.  Injectivity, `T_0 ~= T_1`, holds for the
  quotient `G_0(M)` by the stop relations `x_(q_0 w) = 1`
  ([[kms-group-with-stop-relations-simulates-all-words]], review correction by
  `ex-verify-logic-quantum`, 2026-09-13).

Nondeterminism: Section 4 is stated for a Minsky machine with numbered
commands, and KMS apply it to their nondeterministic pumped machines `M_n` in
the proofs of Theorems 3.9, 4.19 and 6.1.

The shape of the automorphisms on `W_0` recorded in clause 6 of the target is
read off the displays on pages 23--24, and is transcribed in part 1 of the
artifact.
