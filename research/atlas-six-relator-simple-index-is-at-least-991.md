Let `S` be finite simple, generated by two injective marked copies of `A8`
satisfying the six literal relators.  Then

```text
[S:A8] >= 991,       |S| >= 19978560.                (S6I991)
```

The established index-91 theorem reaches the interval.  There is no
finite-simple `A8` overgroup at indices `91..495`; at index `496` the only
carrier is the standard `A8<L5(2)`, and its two marking parities have no
six-relator pair.  The next possible carrier is the maximal `A8<M23` at
index `506`; its four double cosets and both marking parities likewise have
no survivor.  No finite simple group contains `A8` at indices `507..989`.
At index `990` the carrier is natural `A8<A11`, already excluded by the
complete labeled-chart census.  This proves `(S6I991)`.

Combined with `atlas-six-relator-finite-escape-is-simple-test`, every exact
finite-dimensional phase escape therefore forces a finite simple quotient
past the A11 boundary.  Larger simple targets and approximate
matrix-ultraproduct escapes remain open.
