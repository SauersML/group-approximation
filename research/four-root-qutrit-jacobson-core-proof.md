Split the first `R`-module coordinate along `D_27` and write `star`
for the second coordinate.  The leaf `d_0=0` gives the four identities

```text
e_12(s_0)   |-> 1+E_(0,star),
e_21(t_0)   |-> 1+E_(star,0),
e_12(s_00)  |-> 1+E_(0,star)(s_0),
e_21(t_00)  |-> 1+E_(star,0)(t_0).                      (FQJ4)
```

On the right, the coefficients in the last two lines are internal tail
coefficients.  The established coefficient map

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1> -> R,        S |-> s_0, T |-> t_0    (FQJ5)
```

is injective; the Jacobson orbit quotient supplies a left inverse.  Thus all
matrix groups below are literal subgroups over `R`, not coefficient
specializations.

By the one-chart theorem, the qutrit packet together with the first two roots
in `(FQJ4)` generates the full scalar group

```text
GL_28(F_2)=EL_28(F_2).                                  (FQJ6)
```

In characteristic two this group contains every permutation matrix.  Hence
conjugating the last two roots in `(FQJ4)` by scalar permutations produces

```text
x_ij(S),        x_ij(T)                 for every i!=j. (FQJ7)
```

Let `A` be the set of coefficients `r in J` for which
`x_ij(r)` belongs to the generated group for every `i!=j).  Formulae
`(FQJ6)--(FQJ7)` show that `1,S,T in A`.  Root additivity makes `A`
closed under addition.  If `r,s in A` and `i!=j), choose a third index
`k`; the Steinberg commutator relation gives

```text
[x_ik(r),x_kj(s)]=x_ij(rs).                             (FQJ8)
```

Thus `A` is also closed under multiplication.  It is a unital subalgebra
of `J` containing `S,T`, so `A=J`.  The generated group therefore
contains `EL_28(J)`.

Conversely, every generator in `(FQJ4)` lies in `EL_28(J)), and the
qutrit permutation matrices lie in its scalar elementary subgroup by
`(FQJ6)`.  The module chart is injective.  Hence the reverse containment
also holds and proves `(FQJ2)).  Taking `r=Q=1-ST` in the preceding
coefficient argument proves `(FQJ3)`.

For completeness, consider the stable Steinberg lift.  The established
Jacobson calculation gives

```text
bsr(J)=2,       K_2(J)=0,       K_(2,28)(J)=0.
```

Therefore `St_28(J)->EL_28(J)` is an isomorphism.  As in the polynomial
proof, stabilize to rank `n+26`, include coefficients `J->R`, and lift
the outer module-chart isomorphism through the universal central extensions.
This produces a homomorphic section

```text
s:H_J -> St_n(R).                                        (FQJ9)
```

The specified Thompson lift restricted to the finite packet differs from
`s|_F` by a finite central image of `F).  Each of the four standard root
lifts differs from its section lift by a central element of order at most
two.  Let `C` be generated by those five finite discrepancy sets.  Then
`C` is finite and central, and

```text
Gamma_J <= C s(H_J).                                    (FQJ10)
```

Projection maps `Gamma_J` onto `H_J), so its kernel is contained in the
finite group `C`.  This proves the finite-central assertion and removes
the cover ambiguity.  It does not decide hyperlinearity of the Jacobson
elementary core, which is why the claim stops at the explicit next overlap.
