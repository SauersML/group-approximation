Let `H = E ⋊ V` be the signed Thompson group in `L_(F_3)(1,2)^x`, `B = F_3[H] eps_-`, and let
`q_[0]`, `q_[1]` be the cylinder idempotents, as in
`signed-thompson-algebra-is-odd-measure-crossed-product` and
`hadamard-unit-conjugates-swap-idempotent-to-cylinder`.

Suppose `b', c' in B` satisfy `c' q_[0] b' = eps_-`, a witness for the open claim
`odd-measure-crossed-product-unit-below-cylinder-idempotent`. Let `L <= H` be generated by the
supports of `b'` and `c'` together with `z` and `tau_[0]`, and let `K` be its image in `V`. Then `K`
is **nonamenable**.

**Consequences.**
* The reflection group `E` is invisible: a witness may use any amount of it.
* Thompson projections that are finite, locally finite, abelian, solvable, or of subexponential
  growth are excluded.
* A witness whose projection lies in Thompson's group `F` would prove `F` nonamenable.
* The target is not decided.

Proposition 8 of the artifact.
