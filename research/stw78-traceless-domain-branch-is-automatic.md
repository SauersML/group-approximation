Let `A` be unital, simple, separable, nuclear, and `Z`-stable.  If `A` has
no normalized trace, then every unital star homomorphism

```text
phi:A->B
```

into an arbitrary unital C-star algebra is `O_infinity`-stable.

Consequently STW Problem LXXVIII is equivalent to its restriction to domains
`A` which admit a normalized trace.  Every such `A` is stably finite, since
the trace is faithful by simplicity.  Thus any counterexample must have a
stably finite, traceful domain; no quasitraceless or purely infinite domain
can occur.
