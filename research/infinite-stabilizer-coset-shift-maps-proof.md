The base coordinate of the image depends on a finite window `D` and is
invariant under the stabilizer. Some stabilizer element carries `D` to a
disjoint window, because only the finitely many elements of `D D^-1` fail to.
Independent changes on the two windows force the local rule to be constant, and
transitivity spreads constancy to every coordinate.
