The checked experiment constructs `q,T_0,T_1` from the same packet as the
mixing table, reduces `P_0P_1-P_1P_0` and `(P_0P_1)^2-P_0P_1` to zero, and
extracts the identity coefficients.  It obtains `tau(F)=1/4` and four equal
raw-cut overlaps `3/64`.  Orthogonal projection onto the cut algebra then
gives `(3/8)q`, whose squared norm is `9/128`; subtracting from `tau(F)=1/4`
gives `23/128`.
