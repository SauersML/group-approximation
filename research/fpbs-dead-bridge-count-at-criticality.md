Dead: the attempt inspects pivotality at one configuration law, arguing that a graph in which every edge lies on a finite cycle has few pivotal edges and therefore a small budget. Deleting one edge is not the operative perturbation. On H_epsilon over T_(2r) square Z every retained edge lies on a finite rectangle, yet the independent thinning threshold tends to one as epsilon decreases (fpbs-bridgeless-collective-fragility, fpbs-long-detours-with-no-bridges). Newly created pivotal edges must be tracked as the parameter changes, which this route does not do.
