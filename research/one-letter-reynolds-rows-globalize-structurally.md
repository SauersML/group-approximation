**ESTABLISHED HNN GLOBALIZATION FENCE.**  Let `L` be a group generated by a
finite family of subgroups `H_i`, and suppose a presentation adjoins one
stable letter `t` and target subgroup maps

```text
phi_i:H_i -> L'                                               (ORG1)
```

with Reynolds/HNN rows

```text
t h t^(-1)=phi_i(h)                 (h in H_i).         (ORG2)
```

Conjugation by one group element is a single homomorphism.  Therefore the
`phi_i` agree on every overlap in the presented group and extend
automatically to

```text
phi:<H_i:i> -> <phi_i(H_i):i>,       phi(g)=t g t^(-1). (ORG3)
```

If the source generated subgroup embeds, `(ORG3)` is injective.  Thus the
same letter intertwines the **whole** generated subgroup; there is no
additional character-selective degree of freedom.

Apply this to the role-packed BCS aggregate.  For a forbidden type
`i=(c,a)`, its projection `R_iP_i` is a character idempotent of the finite
central product

```text
H_i=D_role *_(<J>) <x_v:v in X_c>,                    (ORG4)
```

where `D_role` is the common diagonal role subgroup.  The group `H_i` depends
on the context `c`, not on which assignment character `a` is forbidden.
Whenever the forbidden menu covers every nonempty context and every shared
variable occurs in such a context,

```text
<H_i:i forbidden>=<D_role,x_v:v in X>.                 (ORG5)
```

Hence imposing `(ORG2)` with one proposed aggregate transporter determines
its conjugation on every shared variable and on `D_role`.  It transports all
characters of every context group, allowed and forbidden alike.  The role
labels only select source projections after this global action; they do not
make the action conditional on those projections.

To preserve the authenticated unique-spin role packet, the compiler must
also specify covariance on the transverse role generators.  Adding those
rows extends `(ORG3)` to the full corrected shared BCS/role algebra.  The
transporter is then a full structural intertwiner, and
`agent-aggregate-escape-normalizer-erases-payload` applies: the reducing hull
of the transported `QB_f` is the whole translated carrier.  If the transverse
rows are omitted, fixed-packet Schur form leaves an arbitrary multiplicity
gauge and does not authenticate a same-carrier aggregate return.

This argument is unchanged in normalized-HS approximation.  Exactify the
fixed context and role packets and average the one approximate stable letter
against the finitely many covariance equations.  The corrected letter lies
in the intertwiner space of the subgroup generated in `(ORG5)`, up to
`O(sqrt(E))`; imposing transverse covariance completes the full-algebra
intertwiner with the same fixed loss.

Therefore a one-letter HNN/Reynolds compiler cannot provide the genuinely
partial incidence required by the unary role-packed ladder.  A surviving
construction must either use several transporter pieces and a non-group-word
recombination, or encode a coefficient relation which is not conjugation of
the source context subgroups.  In particular, merely listing all forbidden
character HNN rows does not condition the letter on `B_f`.
