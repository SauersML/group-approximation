**ESTABLISHED** (route `linf-lattice-groups-admit-contracting-combings-proof`).

Let a group `G` act on `Z^n` by maps `g(v) = A_g v + t_g`, where each `A_g` is a
signed permutation matrix and `t_g ∈ Z^n`. Assume the action is transitive and the
stabilizer of `0` is finite. Let `S = {g ∈ G ∖ {1} : ‖t_g‖_∞ <= 1}` and let `d` be
its word metric. Then:

- (a) `S` is a finite symmetric generating set, and `d(g, h) = max(‖t_g − t_h‖_∞, 1)`
  for `g ≠ h`;
- (b) the *clamp combing* `s(g)(0) = 1`, `s(g)(m) = g` for `m >= max(‖t_g‖_∞, 1)`,
  and `s(g)(m) = σ(κ_m(t_g))` otherwise, is a geodesic combing that is contracting
  (definition in `contracting-combings-give-contractible-rips-complexes`) for every
  constant `C >= 2`. Here `κ_m` clamps each coordinate to `[−m, m]` and `σ` is any
  section of `g ↦ t_g` with `σ(0) = 1`.

**Examples.** `Z^n` with the generators `{−1,0,1}^n ∖ {0}`; `Z^n ⋊_ρ F` for any
finite group `F` and homomorphism `ρ : F → B_n`, including `Z^n × F`; the Klein
bottle group `⟨a, b | b a b^-1 = a^-1⟩`; the torsion-free `4_1` screw group
`{v ↦ R^k v + (a, b, k)}` with `R(x,y,z) = (−y, x, z)`.

Also covered: in dimension 3, `Z^3 ⋊ C_3` with `C_3` cyclically permuting the
coordinates (a rhombohedral group `R3` in its primitive basis).

Not covered, in any coordinates: the plane crystallographic groups with a rotation of
order 3, namely `p3`, `p3m1`, `p31m`, `p6` and `p6m`. Each contains `p3` with finite
index and is quasi-isometric to `Z^2`, so it could only act on `Z^2`, and so could its
subgroup `p3`. There each rotation of order 3 has trivial linear part, because `B_2` has
no element of order 3. `p3` is generated by its rotations of order 3, so it would act
by translations with finite kernel, and its infinite commutator subgroup would have to
fix `0`. Which other trigonal and hexagonal space groups in dimension 3 are covered is
not settled here.

By `contracting-combings-give-contractible-rips-complexes`, every such group has a
contractible Rips complex for some scale. These groups are amenable and automatic,
so they are positive cases of `amenable-automatic-groups-admit-contracting-combings`.
