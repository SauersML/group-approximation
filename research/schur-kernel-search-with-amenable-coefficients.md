**Dead.** It is invalidated by `amenable-coefficients-have-zero-schur-kernel`.
Every multiplier of an amenable coefficient subgroup is realized by an
embedded twisted regular representation and extends across the adjunction.
The localization theorem then kills the Schur kernel over every ambient
group. So unipotent, solvable and virtually abelian coefficient packets are
excluded, including inside `Sp_4(Z)`.
