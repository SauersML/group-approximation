Consider the proposed globalization of the corrected NAND Fourier cell over
the fixed Paddock--Slofstra non-RU BCS.  For every chosen incidence carrying
a shared Boolean sign `S`, adjoin private involutions `A,B` and impose

```text
[S,A]=[S,B]=1,
(S,A) and (S,B) have NAND support,
[A,B]=-S,                 W=[A,B]S=J=-1.               (BFR1)
```

Balancing all context-atom and shared-marginal ranks does not make this a
soundness coupling.

## Completeness: the non-CE tracial model always extends

Let `(M,tau)` be the tracial GNS von Neumann algebra of the source BCS and
write `S_x in M` for its shared signs.  If there are `N` routers, amplify by
`Q=M_2(C)^(tensor N)`.  For router `e` on variable `x`, put

```text
p_+=(1+S_x)/2,          p_-=(1-S_x)/2,
A_e=p_- tensor 1 + p_+ tensor Z_e,
B_e=p_- tensor 1 + p_+ tensor X_e,
F_e=p_- tensor 1 + p_+ tensor H_e,                     (BFR2)
```

where `X_e,Z_e,H_e` act on the `e`-th qubit.  Then `F_e` is a unitary,
commutes with `S_x`, and `F_e A_e F_e^*=B_e`.  Both NAND conditions hold and

```text
[A_e,B_e]=p_- - p_+=-S_x,
[A_e,B_e]S_x=-1.                                       (BFR3)
```

Every original context variable commutes with `S_x`, so it also commutes
with the attached operators needed in that context.  Thus all routers extend
simultaneously, the original non-CE payload is unchanged under finite matrix
amplification, and every marked word is `J=-1`.  Gauge/rank balance poses no
completeness obstruction.

## Exact source of the spurious finite sectors

The universal finite router in `(BFR1)` splits over the central spectral
projections of `S`.

- On `S=-1`, NAND forces `A=B=1`; this is a one-dimensional marked sector.
- On `S=+1`, the commutator relation is `ABAB=-1`, equivalently
  `AB=-BA`.  The two involutions generate the two-dimensional Pauli/Clifford
  algebra `M_2(C)`.

Consequently the star algebra generated by `S,A,B` in the marked router face
is exactly

```text
R_router isomorphic to C directSum M_2(C),              (BFR4)
```

with `J=-1` on **both** summands.  This is the first spurious Clifford
sector: the nonscalar plus block is not tied to predicate validity, while the
minus block is scalar and equally marked.  If the Fourier transporter is
also a generator, take `F=1` on the scalar summand and the Hadamard on the
Pauli summand; all of its local Clifford-table relations remain exact.

Fix now any global Boolean valuation `sigma_x in {+1,-1}` of all source
variables, including one which violates the BCS.  At each context choose the
marked assignment-coordinate/companion packet sector supplied by
`scalar-bus-packet-couplings-retain-all-global-assignments`; it has the
prescribed scalar selector values even for a forbidden assignment.  At every
router on `x`, choose

```text
sigma_x=-1:     the C summand of (BFR4),
sigma_x=+1:     the M_2(C) Pauli summand of (BFR4).      (BFR5)
```

Put different plus routers on independent qubits and cross-amplify the local
packet representations to a common dimension.  All shared selector images
are the same scalars `sigma_x I`, while every Fourier router is exact and
every word `W_e` equals the common `J=-I`.  Tensoring the packet and router
factors therefore gives an exact finite-dimensional representation of the
whole gauge-balanced attachment for the arbitrary valuation `sigma`.

In particular a violating `sigma` yields a marked exact representation from
which no satisfying BCS tuple can be decoded.  Approximate soundness fails at
defect zero; neither Gowers--Hatami stability nor a quantitative energy
estimate can remove this sector.

The corrected Fourier router therefore passes perfect completeness but
fails the other half of projective-kernel phase compilation in the strongest
possible way.  To progress, a payload relation must act nonscalarly on the
`M_2` summand of `(BFR4)` **and** exclude the scalar companion sectors chosen
at forbidden contexts, while still admitting the operator-valued extension
`(BFR2)`.  Rank balancing, independent Fourier qubits, and identifying all
central marks do none of these things.
