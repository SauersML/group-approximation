Write `R_C` and `R_A` for the coefficient rings of `C` and `A`.

## Item 1

For distinct `i,j,k`, `e_ij(f)=[e_ik(f),e_kj(1)]`, so `C` and `A` are
perfect.  By the same identity together with additivity, `EL_r` of a finitely
generated commutative ring is generated by the `e_ij(y)` with `y` running over
`+-1` and the ring generators and their inverse units.  So `C` and `A` are
finitely generated.  `C` is Kazhdan by
`elementary-groups-over-fg-rings-have-property-t`.  It is proper, since
`e_12(t^(-1))` (respectively `e_12(x_1^(-1))`) has an entry outside `R_C`.
In case (i), `F_q[t]` and `F_q[t,1/t]` are Euclidean, which gives `EL_r=SL_r`.

## (CR1)

Let `rho:A->U(n)` be a unitary representation and `L` the closure of
`rho(A)`, a compact group.  Put `u=t` in case (i) and `u=x_1x_2` in case (ii).
For `i!=j`, the element `delta=delta_ij(u)` lies in `A` (the Whitehead identity
in the proof of `opposite-slice-contains-function-field-hecke-pair`), and

```text
delta e_ij(f) delta^(-1)=e_ij(u^2 f).                                (TR1)
```

In a compact group, every neighbourhood of the identity contains infinitely
many positive powers of any given element.  (Cover by finitely many translates
of a small symmetric neighbourhood; two powers fall in one translate.)  So
choose `m_k->infinity` with `rho(delta)^(m_k)->I`.  For `f in R_A`, multiplying
by `u^(2m_k)` clears the negative exponents once `k` is large, so
`e_ij(u^(2m_k) f) in C`.  Then

```text
rho(e_ij(f)) = lim_k rho(delta)^(m_k) rho(e_ij(f)) rho(delta)^(-m_k)
             = lim_k rho(e_ij(u^(2m_k) f))  in  closure(rho(C)).
```

The closure of `rho(C)` is a group and the elementary matrices generate `A`,
so (CR1) holds.  A set and its closure have the same commutant and the same
fixed vectors.

## Item 3

The hypotheses of `exact-projective-codensity-has-a-uniform-trace-square-gap`
are:
- `C` and `A` finitely generated;
- `C` perfect and Kazhdan;
- (EPT1), `rho(C)'=rho(A)'`, for every finite-dimensional unitary
  representation `rho`.

Items 1 and 2 supply all of them, and `h in A\C` by properness.

## Item 4

A Kazhdan pair `(S_C,kappa)` gives a uniform conjugation gap on the
orthocomplement of `rho(C)'` in `(M_n,HS)`, for all `n`.  Co-density is item 2,
and `a_1=h` lies outside `C`.  The corollary of
`hnn-over-codense-kazhdan-subgroup-not-hyperlinear` now makes
`<A,s | [s,S_C]=1>=A *_C (C x Z)` non-hyperlinear whenever `A` is flexibly HS
stable.  Items 5 and 6 of
`binary-free-and-ordinary-coset-wreaths-share-hyperlinearity` turn this into
non-`RE/C` of `L(C) subset L(A)` and non-hyperlinearity of `A *_C A`.

In case (i), item 4 of `kt-double-hyperlinearity-requires-function-field-hecke-re`
gives the Kun--Thom double.  In case (ii), the normal-form lemma of that
claim's proof applies to `E_pm <= P <= G`, each containing `Gamma`.  It gives
`D_(E_pm) -> D_P -> D_G` injective, and hyperlinearity passes to subgroups.
