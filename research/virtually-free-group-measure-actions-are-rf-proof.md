Artifact part 1, §3, Theorem V.
- Let `F_r <= Γ` be free of finite index. A `Γ`-invariant measure of full support is `F_r`-invariant with full
  support, so `α|_(F_r)` is RF (`free-group-full-support-invariant-measure-actions-are-rf`).
- Then `α` is RF (`rf-actions-induce-from-finite-index-subgroups`).
- Minimal actions: any invariant probability measure of a minimal action has full support.
