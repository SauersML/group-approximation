Fix one index and suppress it.

For (LNS1), the normal subgroup defining `L` contains `<<Q>>`.
Therefore the identity on `X` induces an epimorphism

```text
B=F(X)/<<Q>> ->> L.
```

Amenability passes to quotients, so `L` is amenable. This uses no
invariance of the L-presentation: the substitutions may fail to induce
endomorphisms of `B`, and adding all of their relator iterates still only
enlarges the normal subgroup by which `B` is factored.

Now consider (LNS2). If there is no stable letter, it presents
`B/<<R>>` and is amenable.

Suppose there is one stable letter `t`, and let `A` be the subgroup of
`T` generated by the images of `X`. The map `B->A` is onto, so
`A` is amenable. For every word `w in F(X)`, induction on word length
and the generator relations give

```text
t^(-1) w(X) t=sigma(w)(X)
```

in `T`. Thus `t^(-1) A t<=A`, whether or not `sigma` descended to
`B` before the extra relations forced its stable kernel.

Put `A_n=t^n A t^(-n)` for `n>=0`. From `t^(-1)At<=A` one gets
`A_n<=A_(n+1)`. Hence

```text
N=union_(n>=0) A_n
```

is a subgroup and a directed union of amenable groups, so it is amenable.
It is invariant under conjugation by `t`: conjugation by `t` shifts
`A_n` to `A_(n+1)`, while conjugation by `t^(-1)` shifts `A_n` to
`A_(n-1)` for `n>=1` and sends `A_0` into `A_0`. It is also
normalized by `A`, since `A<=N`. As `T=<A,t>`, this proves
`N normal T`. The quotient `T/N` is generated by the image of `t`,
so it is cyclic. Amenability is closed under directed unions and extensions,
and `T` is amenable.

This identifies the stable-kernel firewall without computing the kernel. In
the special case where `sigma` already induces an endomorphism `phi:B->B`
and `R` is empty, that kernel is

```text
K_infinity=union_(n>=0) ker(phi^n),
```

and `T` is the injective ascending mapping torus of `B/K_infinity`, as
in [[genuine-hnn-switches-over-amenable-bases-are-sigma1]]. The directed-union
argument above also covers the more general non-invariant presentation.

Finally suppose there are `s>=2` stable letters. Define a map on the
displayed generators by

```text
x -> 1       (x in X),
t_i -> u_i   (1<=i<=s),
```

where `u_1,...,u_s` freely generate `F_s`. Every relator in `Q` or
`R` maps to the identity, and every conjugacy relator maps to
`u_i^(-1) 1 u_i=1`. The presentation therefore induces an epimorphism

```text
T ->> F_s.
```

Since `F_s` is nonamenable for `s>=2` and every quotient of an amenable
group is amenable, `T` is nonamenable.

Thus (LNS3) depends only on the finite integer `s`. Uniformly in an input
code `e`, the predicate `s(e)<=1` is decidable, so the amenable locus is
recursive (`Delta^0_1`); for (LNS1) the locus is all of `N`. A computable
many-one reduction from `TOT` to either locus would decide `TOT`, a
contradiction. This proves both the algebraic and arithmetical assertions.
