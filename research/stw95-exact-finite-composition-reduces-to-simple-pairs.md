title: Exact finite-composition pure tensor permanence reduces to simple composition-factor pairs

Let `A,B` be exact C*-algebras with finite composition series.  If the minimal
tensor product of every composition factor of `A` with every composition
factor of `B` is pure, then `A tensor_min B` is pure.

In particular, for exact pure `A,B`, their ideals and quotients remain pure,
so the finite-composition case of Problem XCV reduces to the case of simple
exact pure factors.
