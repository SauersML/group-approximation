The artifact proves the equivalence by passing from projection bundles of
fixed fiber `p` to principal `U(p(D tensor K)p)` bundles. The target corner
is Z-stable and hence K-stable, so the actual corner first-factor map has
the same higher homotopy kernels and cokernels as stabilization.

Sphere clutching proves necessity, including the component-conjugation
qualification. Weak equivalence of all corner unitary groups proves
sufficiency for finite CW bases; projection continuity and inverse limits
give all compact metrizable bases. Zhang's theorem supplies the condition
for simple real-rank-zero stable-rank-one corners.
