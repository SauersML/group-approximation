If `Gamma` is the fundamental group of a graph of virtually free groups with
virtually cyclic edge groups, and `Gamma` is hyperbolic and one-ended, then
`Gamma` contains a quasiconvex surface subgroup.

H. Wilton, *Essential surfaces in graph pairs*, arXiv:1701.02505. TeX source
`freesurfacesv3.tex`, fetched on MSI 2026-09-13.

- **Theorem** `thm: Surface subgroups` (l.1108--1109), verbatim:

  > Let $\Gamma$ be the fundamental group of a graph of virtually free groups with
  > virtually cyclic edge groups. If $\Gamma$ is hyperbolic and one-ended then
  > $\Gamma$ contains a quasiconvex surface subgroup.

- **Surface or rigid** (Corollary `lettercor: Surface or rigid`, l.169--171),
  verbatim: "Let $\Gamma$ be a one-ended hyperbolic group without 2-torsion. Either
  $\Gamma$ contains a quasiconvex surface subgroup, or $\Gamma$ contains a
  quasiconvex rigid subgroup." Here rigid means no nontrivial splitting over a
  virtually cyclic edge group (l.167).
- **Limit groups** (Corollary `lettercor: Limit groups`, l.177--178): every
  one-ended limit group contains a surface subgroup.
- Surface subgroup means (l.153) the fundamental group of a closed surface of
  non-positive Euler characteristic.
