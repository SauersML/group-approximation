Modulo two, the upper Iwahori image is generated by one transposition `s`.
Both `u` and `e` reduce to `s`, giving `chi_+` in `(TCF1)`.  Applying the
Nielsen formulas gives

```text
u |-> s^(-1)s=1,
e |-> s^(-1)s s=s,
```

which is `chi_-`.

The standard representation of `S_3` restricts to a transposition as
`1 direct_sum sgn`, so its pullback along the first character is
`1 direct_sum chi_+`.  The vertex representation `1 direct_sum sgn`
pulls back along the second character as `1 direct_sum chi_-`.  Align their
trivial lines and call the common projection `p`.

For any `x` commuting with the index-three subgroup, the three-coset average
commutes with the whole vertex group and preserves normalized trace.  The
standard packet is irreducible, hence the average of its rank-one `p` is
`I_2/2`.  On `1 direct_sum sgn`, `p` already commutes with all of `S_3`, so
its average stays `p`.  The three transposition-fixed lines in the standard
plane are distinct and have zero common intersection, whereas the trivial
summand is fixed by the whole group; this proves `(TCF5)`.

Finally append `n` identical trivial summands.  The two edge tuples agree on
those summands and on the displayed trivial line.  They differ only on the
remaining line, where the image of `u` is `-1` on the plus side and `+1` on
the minus side, while `e` agrees.  Hence the maximum normalized-HS defect is
exactly `2/sqrt(n+2)`.  All averages and intersections are direct sums with
the unchanged `n`-dimensional trivial block, proving the amplified claim.
