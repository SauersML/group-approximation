Write

```text
D = *_(i in I) (A_i,rho_i)
```

and, for each finite `F subset I` containing `i_0`, let `D_F` be the
C*-subalgebra of `D` generated by the factors indexed by `F`.  With the
restricted free-product state, `D_F` is canonically the finite reduced free
product over `F`.

Every `D_F` is selfless.  This is induction on `|F|`.  The singleton stage is
the selfless anchor.  If `j notin F`, associativity of reduced free products
gives a state-preserving identification

```text
D_(F union {j}) = D_F * (A_j,rho_j).
```

The induction hypothesis makes `D_F` selfless, while `A_j` is separable and
`rho_j` has faithful GNS representation.  Therefore
`robert-selfless-free-product-absorption` applies and makes the enlarged
finite packet selfless.

The family `(D_F)_(F contains i_0, F finite)` is upward directed.  Its union
is norm dense in `D`: every element of the algebraic free product is a finite
linear combination of words, and each such word uses only finitely many
factor indices.  The free-product state on `D` restricts to the finite
free-product state on every `D_F`.  Thus
`robert-directed-unions-preserve-selflessness` applies and proves that `D` is
selfless.  Notice that the directed-union theorem has no countability
hypothesis on the directed set.

For the group corollary, use the canonical trace-preserving identification

```text
C*_r(*_n G_n) = *_n (C*_r(G_n),tau_(G_n)).
```

Countability of each `G_n` makes every non-anchor reduced algebra separable,
and its canonical trace has faithful GNS representation.  The first part
therefore applies.  The final simplicity, stable-rank-one, strict-comparison,
and quasitrace conclusions are exactly
`robert-tracial-selfless-regularity`.
