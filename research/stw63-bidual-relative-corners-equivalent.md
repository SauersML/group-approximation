For a unital C-star algebra `B` and unital embeddings `phi,psi:Z->B`, retain
`pi,p,q,D` from `stw63-aue-is-relative-corner-equivalence` and put

```text
E = M_2((B**)_infinity) intersect pi(Z)'.              (1)
```

The coordinatewise isometric inclusion `B->B**` induces `D->E`, and the
images of `p` and `q` are always Murray--von Neumann equivalent in `E`.

This is the exact point at which the now-complete von Neumann theorem enters
the C-star problem.  It does not assert that their equivalence descends to
`D`.
