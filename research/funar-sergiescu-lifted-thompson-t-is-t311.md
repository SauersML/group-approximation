Let `T` be Thompson's group of piecewise-dyadic homeomorphisms of
`S^1 = R/Z`, with the Cannon-Floyd-Parry generators `A, B, C`.  Let `T̄` be the
group of lifts to `R`, and let `z(x) = x + 1`.

1. **Two generators of finite order.**  Put `beta = C^(-1)` and
   `alpha = C^(-1) B`, using the product `gh = g o h`.  Then `alpha^4 = beta^3 = 1`,
   and `A = beta alpha^2`, `B = beta^2 alpha`, `C = beta^2`.  So
   `T = <alpha, beta>`.
2. **The lifted presentation.**  There are lifts `a` of `alpha` and `b` of
   `beta` in `T̄` with

   ```text
   a^4 = z,   b^3 = z,   (b a)^5 = z^3,
   [bab, a^2 bab a^2] = 1,   [bab, a^2 b^2 a^2 bab a^2 b a^2] = 1,
   ```

   and these relations, together with the centrality of `z`, present `T̄`.
   In the notation of the source, `T̄ = T_(3,1,1)`.  The Euler number is
   `12*3 - 15*1 - 20*1 = 1`.

## Source (verbatim, from the extracted text of arXiv:0802.2996v2)

Funar and Sergiescu, *Central extensions of the Ptolemy-Thompson group and
quantized Teichmüller theory*, arXiv:0802.2996v2 (23 Oct 2009).

- Remark 2.1: "The group T has also another presentation with generators α
  and β and relations α4 = β3 = 1 [βαβ, α2βαβα 2] = 1
  [βαβ, α2β2α2βαβα 2βα2] = 1 (βα)5 = 1 If we set A = βα2, B = β2α and
  C = β2 then we obtain the generators A, B, C of the group T , considered
  above."
- Section 2.5: "Let us introduce the group Tn,p,q presented by the generators
  α, β, z and the relations: (βα)5 = zn α4 = zp β3 = zq [βαβ, α2βαβα2] = 1
  [βαβ, α2βα2βαβα2β2α2] = 1 [α, z] = [ β, z] = 1" and "It is easy to see
  that Tn,p,q are central extensions of T by Z."
- Proof of Proposition 2.2: "Last, the extension ~T ⊂ Homeo+(R) is known to
  have Euler number 1. The central element z acts as the unit translation on
  the line and in order to identify it with an element of t he family Tn,p,q
  it suﬃces to compute the rotation index associated to the elements α4, β3
  and ( βα)5. One obtains that ~T is actually isomorphic to T3,1,1."
- Introduction: "~T ⊂ Homeo+(R) is the group of lifts of piecewise-PSL(2 , Z)
  homeomorphisms of the circle to homeo- morphisms of the real line R."

In Section 2.1 of the source, `A, B, C` are the piecewise-dyadic maps. The
source states `~T` for the piecewise-`PSL(2,Z)` model.  The two models are
conjugate by the Minkowski question-mark homeomorphism, and its lift commutes
with `z`.  So the statement transfers to `T̄`.

## Independent check

`experiments/torus-knot-cover-2026-09-17/lift_relations.py` lifts `A, B, C`
to `R` exactly, over the rationals.  It then verifies, on the grid
`2^(-10) Z`, which contains every breakpoint of the words used:
- the substitution of Remark 2.1 in the convention `gh = g o h`;
- the values `p = q = 1` and `n = 3` for the lifts with translation numbers
  `1/4` and `1/3`;
- that both commutator relators lift to the identity.

The output is `experiments/torus-knot-cover-2026-09-17/output.txt`.  The
second commutator word in the source is printed differently in Remark 2.1
and in Section 2.5.  The script checks the Remark 2.1 form.  Nothing below
uses either commutator.
