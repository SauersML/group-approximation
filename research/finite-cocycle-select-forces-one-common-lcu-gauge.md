**ESTABLISHED POSITIVE CONSTRUCTION.**  Let `K` be a fixed finite group and
let `g |-> w_g` be a homomorphism from `K` to a payload group.  Put
`F=C_2^K semidirect K`, with `K` acting regularly on the lamps.  The orbit of
the lamp character negative at exactly one coordinate has trivial
stabilizer, so it gives one irreducible natural packet on `ell^2(K)`.  Its
central carrier is the explicit lamp-algebra projection

```text
e_nat=sum_(x in K) p_x,                                  (FCS0)
```

where `p_x` is the atom for the character negative only at `x`.  In the
canonical regular trace `tau(e_nat)=|K|/2^|K|>0`.

On this packet the lamps generate the coordinate diagonal algebra and the
shift words `L_g` act by `L_g e_x=e_(gx)`.  Make the payload commute with `F`
and adjoin one unitary generator `C` with the ordinary group relations

```text
[C, every lamp]=1,
C L_g C^(-1) L_g^(-1)=w_g                         (g in K).
                                                               (FCS1)
```

Because `C` commutes with every lamp, it commutes with `(FCS0)`; hence the
positive selected packet is reducing for `C`, not merely chosen in one
model.  On that packet every exact solution has

```text
C=sum_(x in K) |e_x><e_x| tensor w_x A                 (FCS2)
```

for one common right unitary `A` on the external multiplicity space.  There
are no coordinate-dependent gauges and `A` need not commute with the
payload.

Let

```text
q=e_nat |K|^(-1) sum_(g in K) L_g
```

be the selected uniform-control projection (of canonical trace
`2^(-|K|)`) and `P=|K|^(-1)sum_g w_g`.  Then

```text
qCq = q tensor P A,
||qCq||_(2,q)=||P||_2.                                  (FCS3)
```

Thus every zero test or HS-norm test of the finite group average is exactly
orientation independent.  Since the packet and `K` are fixed, finite-group
HS exactification makes the same conclusion robust with a
dimension-independent constant.

This does not contradict
`transitive-control-normalizer-forces-projectively-equal-payloads`: `C` does
not normalize the full matrix algebra generated by `F`; it sends a shift to
the payload-decorated shift `w_gL_g`.  The reducing free lamp-character orbit,
not full-matrix normalizer rigidity, makes the conclusion uniform.

The construction has a sharp syntax boundary.  Relations `(FCS1)` themselves
force

```text
w_(gh)=w_g w_h.                                         (FCS4)
```

More generally, if shifts act on the payload by `alpha_g`, they force the
cocycle law `w_(gh)=w_g alpha_g(w_h)`.  Hence arbitrary LCU word lists and
arbitrary coefficients cannot be synchronized this way without imposing new
payload relations.  It applies directly to uniform averages of a finite
subgroup, including Fourier expansions of one affine/character atom.

Finally `(FCS3)` does not turn `qCq=0` into an ordinary word equation.  For
`|K|>2`, `q` has relative rank `1/|K|`; grading anticommutation would exchange
two equal halves and overconstrain the complement.  The remaining gate is a
selected small-corner zero/return actuator, not LCU orientation.

## Attempts

- Transitive shifts succeed because the edge ratio
  `C_x C_(g^-1 x)^(-1)` is the uniform payload `w_g`.
- For an arbitrary list, those edge ratios depend on `x` and are controlled
  additive operators again; no ordinary relation names them.
- Twisted payload covariance enlarges the construction exactly to finite
  cocycles, but the cocycle equations are genuine new base relations.
- When `K=C_2`, the uniform corner is a half-corner and grading can test zero.
  Higher-rank character atoms still have a `1/|K|` corner and retain the
  partial-return obstruction.
