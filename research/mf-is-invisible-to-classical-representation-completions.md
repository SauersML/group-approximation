For every `r>=0` there is a split epimorphism of finitely presented groups

```text
q_r:Gamma_r->F_r                                      (CRC1)
```

such that `F_r` is MF and `Gamma_r` is non-MF, while `q_r` induces all of the
following isomorphisms and equivalences:

1. an isomorphism of profinite completions;
2. an isomorphism of Bohr compactifications;
3. an isomorphism of proalgebraic completions over every field;
4. an equivalence of finite-dimensional tensor representation categories
   over every field, preserving the forgetful fiber functor;
5. for every nonzero commutative ring `A`, an equivalence between the
   categories of representations on finitely presented `A`-modules;
6. for every `n`, a `GL_n`-equivariant isomorphism of integral representation
   schemes, their quotient stacks, and their affine character quotients.

The kernel of `(CRC1)` is nontrivial and normally generated by one element,
but none of the listed invariants can detect it.  Therefore MF is not a
profinite, compact-representation, proalgebraic, Tannakian, integral-moduli,
or classical deformation-theoretic property, even among finitely presented
groups joined by a split quotient.

At `r=0`, the visible group is trivial.  Hence there is a nontrivial finitely
presented non-MF group `Gamma_0` with

```text
profinite(Gamma_0)=1,
Bohr(Gamma_0)=1,
proalg_k(Gamma_0)=1             for every field k,
Rep_n(Gamma_0)=Spec(Z)          for every n.          (CRC2)
```

More strongly, every action of `Gamma_0` on every finitely presented module
over every commutative ring is trivial.  Thus the failure of MF can persist
after every classical finite, compact, and algebraic representation shadow
has disappeared.
