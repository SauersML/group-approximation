satisfying the six relators.  If

```text
[S:A8] <= 2200,
```

then necessarily

```text
S=HS,       [S:A8]=2200.                              (S6HS1)
```

Both chart subgroups are the derived subgroups of maximal `S8` subgroups in
the unique ATLAS class, and

```text
N_HS(A8)=S8,       N_HS(A8)/A8=Out(A8)=C2.           (S6HS2)
```

Consequently the entire next equality problem is one finite conjugator
family

```text
g in HS,       |HS|=44352000,                         (S6HS3)
```

after fixing the first marked chart.  There is no second inverse-transpose
run: the noninner coset of the `S8` normalizer already realizes it.

This is a genuine finite reduction, not an exclusion of `HS`.  The existing
HS packet screen uses all thirty packet words and therefore cannot be
reused to infer the weaker five-row statement.  The exact remaining target
is to evaluate the six literal words on `(S6HS3)`, preferably through the
known finite `S8` double-coset/seed decomposition rather than a blind scan.
