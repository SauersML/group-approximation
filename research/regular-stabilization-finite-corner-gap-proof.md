Identify the Hilbert--Schmidt matrix units with pairs `(x,y)` in
`Gamma x Gamma`.  Simultaneous left translation preserves `x^(-1)y`, and
each resulting orbit is regular.  Hence the adjoint representation on
Hilbert--Schmidt operators is a multiple of `lambda`.  The regular spectral
gap for a nonamenable finitely generated group, applied to the projection
`q`, gives `(RSF1)` after the equal-rank projection identity.
