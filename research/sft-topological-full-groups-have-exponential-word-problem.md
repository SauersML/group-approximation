## Statement

Let `X` be the space of one-sided infinite admissible edge sequences of a finite
directed graph with irreducible adjacency matrix, and assume `X` has no isolated
points. Let `G` be its etale groupoid and `Y ⊆ X` clopen. Let `S` be a finite
symmetric subset of the topological full group `[[G|Y]]`, and `Gamma = <S>`.

Then the word problem of `Gamma` over `S` is decidable in deterministic time at
most `C*2^(C*n)` on words of length `n`, where `C` depends on the graph and on
`S` but not on `n`.

## Consequence

For the recursive bound `T(l) = 2^l`, the class of all finitely generated
subgroups of all topological full groups `[[G|Y]]` of one-sided irreducible
shifts of finite type, over all finite graphs, shares one recursive word-problem
time bound in the sense of `complexity-bounded-host-classes-are-not-universal`.
The constant may depend on the graph, so letting the graph depend on the input
does not escape the bound.
