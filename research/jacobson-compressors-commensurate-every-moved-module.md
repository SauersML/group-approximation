**ESTABLISHED** (route `jacobson-compressors-commensurate-every-moved-module-proof`).

**Setting.** `F` is any field and `J = F<S,T | TS = 1>`. `R = F[x,x^-1]`, and `rho : GL_N(J) -> GL_N(R)`
is the symbol map. `V = F[x]^N` is the standard faithful `M_N(J)`-module, where `S` multiplies by `x`,
`T f = P_+(x^-1 f)`, and `P_+` deletes negative powers. For a subgroup `Delta <= GL_N(J)` put:

- `Mov(Delta) = span{(delta - 1)v}` and `Fix(Delta) = {v : delta v = v for all delta}`;
- `M(Delta) = sum over delta of (rho(delta) - 1) R^N`, the **symbol moved module**, an `R`-submodule of `R^N`;
- `K(Delta)`, the joint fixed `R`-submodule of `rho(Delta)` in `R^N`, called the **symbol fixed module**;
- `W(X) = X cap F[x]^N` for an `R`-submodule `X` of `R^N`.

Two subspaces are *commensurable* (`~`) when each has finite codimension in the sum.

**Theorem (MC).** Let `Gamma <= GL_N(J)` be finitely generated.

1. **Toeplitz shadow.** `Mov(Gamma) ~ W(M(Gamma))` and `Fix(Gamma) ~ W(K(Gamma))`.
2. **Symbol rigidity.** If `g in GL_N(J)` and `g Gamma g^-1 <= Gamma`, then `rho(g) M(Gamma) = M(Gamma)` and
   `rho(g) K(Gamma) = K(Gamma)`. This holds even when `rho(g)` compresses `rho(Gamma)` strictly.
3. **Commensuration.** Every such compressor `g` has
   - `g Mov(Gamma) <= Mov(Gamma)` with finite codimension `c(g)`;
   - `g Fix(Gamma) >= Fix(Gamma)` with finite codimension `c'(g)`.
4. **Index homomorphisms.** For every `H <= GL_N(J)`, the group `D_H` generated by the compressors of `Gamma` in `H`
   commensurates `Mov(Gamma)` and `Fix(Gamma)`. The relative indices
   `ind(h) = [Mov : h Mov]` and `ind'(h) = [h Fix : Fix]` are homomorphisms `D_H -> Z`, with `ind = c` and
   `ind' = c'` on compressors.

**Corollary (MC-rigid).** Let `Gamma <= G <= GL_N(J)` with `G` Kazhdan and `Gamma` infranormal in `G`, meaning
the compressors generate `G`. Then every compressor `g` satisfies `g Mov(Gamma) = Mov(Gamma)` and
`g Fix(Gamma) = Fix(Gamma)`. The same holds whenever `D_G` has no nonzero homomorphism to `Z`. `Gamma` need not be
Kazhdan.

## Obstruction (class killed)

**Class.** Two kinds of certificate.

- Rigid-compression-defect certificates (`DD(sofic)` and `DD(hyperlinear)`, together with their normalization forms
  in `rigid-compression-defect-normalization-dichotomy`) that place a non-corner rigid pair inside `GL_N(J_F)`, and whose
  compressor strictly shrinks the moved module or strictly enlarges the fixed module. This is the survivor recorded in
  `jacobson-corner-compressor-hulls-carry-fredholm-index` ("whose moved module is not commensurated by their
  compressors").
- Every index-type invariant built from the relative dimension of `Mov` or `Fix` on the compressor hull, which is
  the "Fredholm-index homomorphism nonzero on strict compressors" asked for in
  `compression-criterion-inert-on-modular-linear-extensions`.

**Invariant.** The pair of symbol modules `(M(Gamma), K(Gamma))` in `R^N`. Their Toeplitz shadows are the moved and
fixed modules up to finite dimension.

**Death step.** Compression of a submodule of `R^N` by an element of `GL_N(R)`. Inclusion forces equality, because
`R` has Krull dimension one and saturation has finite length. So the shadow is commensurated, the index is a
homomorphism, and property (T) of the hull makes it zero.

## What survives: a decomposition

A rigid pair `Gamma <= G <= H <= GL_N(J_F)` with a nontrivial rigid defect needs a compressor `g` with
`g Gamma g^-1 < Gamma` strictly, `g Mov = Mov` and `g Fix = Fix`. Put `L = Gamma cap GL_N(J, I)`, which is locally
finite when `F` is finite. Then exactly one of the following holds, and each can fail independently.

- **(S) Symbol compression.** `rho(g) rho(Gamma) rho(g)^-1 < rho(Gamma)` strictly, while `M` and `K` are preserved.
  If `F` is finite, `rho(Gamma) cap SL_N(R)` cannot have finite index in `SL_N(R)`, which is a lattice in
  `SL_N(F((x))) x SL_N(F((x^-1)))`. So the unimodular part of `rho(Gamma)`, which has finite index in `rho(Gamma)`
  because `Gamma` is Kazhdan, must be an infinite-covolume (thin) Kazhdan subgroup of `SL_N(R)`.
- **(F) Finitary compression.** `rho(g)` normalizes `rho(Gamma)`, and `g L g^-1 < L` strictly with
  `[Gamma : g Gamma g^-1] = [L : g L g^-1]`. So a locally finite normal subgroup of `Gamma` is strictly compressed
  while `Mov(Gamma)` and `Fix(Gamma)` are fixed.

**Why corners die and others need not.** For a corner, `g Mov = Mov` and `g Fix = Fix` make `g` block diagonal, and
normality in `GL_m(J)` finishes the argument (Step 3 of the FI proof). For a general `Gamma`, `Gamma` need not be normal
in the stabilizer of `(Mov, Fix)`. That is the only place the argument stops.

## Scope

- Nothing is claimed about the soficity or hyperlinearity of any group.
- Part 1 needs `Gamma` finitely generated. That is automatic for Kazhdan groups, but it is not automatic for `L`.
- The lattice remark in (S) uses `F` finite. The Theorem and the Corollary hold over every field.
