**ESTABLISHED** by `support-covariant-models-carry-fixed-point-trace-proof`.

**Definitions.** Let `(X, mu)` be a standard probability space and `Gamma` a countable group with a
measure-preserving action on `X`, for instance a countable subgroup of a measure full group `[R]`. A **covariant
model** of `(L^infinity(X), Gamma)` is a tracial von Neumann algebra `(M, tau)` (faithful normal trace), a
homomorphism `pi : Gamma -> U(M)` and a normal unital trace-preserving `*`-homomorphism
`e : L^infinity(X) -> M` with

```text
pi(g) e(f) pi(g)^* = e(f o g^-1)          (g in Gamma, f in L^infinity(X)).
```

It is **support-covariant** if moreover `pi(g) e(1_(Fix g)) = e(1_(Fix g))` for every `g`, where
`Fix g = {x : g x = x}`. The canonical model `(L(R), tau_R, g -> u_g, inclusion)` is support-covariant, and the
crossed product `L^infinity(X) ⋊ Gamma` is a covariant model that is not.

**Statement.**
1. In every support-covariant model, `tau(pi(g)) = mu(Fix g)` for all `g in Gamma`. Hence `W*(pi(Gamma))`, with
   `tau` restricted, is trace-preservingly isomorphic to the tracial GNS von Neumann algebra of the fixed-point
   character `chi(g) = mu(Fix g)`.
2. Let `R` be ergodic pmp on a nonatomic space and `Gamma <= [R]` countable and uniformly dense. Then every
   support-covariant model of `(L^infinity(X), Gamma)` contains a trace-preserving copy of `L(R)`. If `L(R)` is not
   Connes-embeddable, no support-covariant model lives in a Connes-embeddable algebra.
