**ESTABLISHED** by `higman-solvable-by-metabelian-edge-collapse-proof`.

**Class.** Call a group `S` *SbM* if some normal subgroup `S_0` of finite index has second derived
subgroup `S_0''` solvable of finite Hirsch length. SbM is closed under subgroups. It contains every
virtually metabelian group (`S_0'' = 1`) and every virtually solvable group of finite Hirsch length.

**Statement.** Let `Q` be a quotient of
`H4 = < a, b, c, d | b^a = b^2, c^b = c^2, d^c = d^2, a^d = a^2 >` (`x^y = y^-1 x y`).

1. **Powers.** Suppose that for some `m >= 1` the group `A = <a^m, c^m>` of `Q` has `A''` solvable of
   finite Hirsch length. Then `Q = 1`. The same holds for `<b^m, d^m>`.
2. **Edge images.** If the image of `<a,c>` or of `<b,d>` in `Q` is SbM, then `Q = 1`.
3. **Trees.** If `Q != 1` acts on a tree without inversions and every edge stabilizer is SbM, then `Q`
   fixes a vertex. So a nontrivial quotient of `H4` never splits as an amalgam or HNN extension over
   an SbM subgroup.

**New examples killed.** Item 2 reaches edge images of derived length 3 and infinite Hirsch length.
One example is the Heisenberg-type central extension `G = H ⋊ <t>` of `Z wr Z`. Here `H` is generated
by a central `c` and elements `x_i` (`i in Z`), with `[x_i, x_(i+1)] = c` and all other `x_i` commuting,
and `t` shifts the index. Then `G = <x_0, t>` has `G'' = <c^2>`, which is infinite cyclic
(`experiments/higman-solvable-by-metabelian-2026-09-17`). More generally item 2 covers every
(polycyclic, or finite-rank abelian)-by-metabelian image.

**Mechanism.** This is the proof of `virtually-metabelian-edge-images-collapse-higman-quotients` with
the error terms tracked. Put `N = A''`, `alpha = a^m`, `gamma = c^m`, `z = [gamma, alpha]`, and let `W`
be generated by the `gamma`-conjugates of `z`. Let `phi` be conjugation by `b^(2^m)`. The metabelian
proof needed `phi(W)` to be abelian modulo `N`, and that is not available. Here every elementary
commutator estimate is off by an element of `N` or of a conjugate of `phi(W') <= phi(N)`. Both have
Hirsch length at most `h(N)`. Two facts absorb the error:
- a cyclic `Q[T^±1]`-module is either finite-dimensional or free, and on a free one `T^e - 1` is
  injective;
- Hirsch length is additive, so `h(W) <= 1 + 2 h(N)`.

So the `gamma`-span of `z` in `A'/A'' ⊗ Q` is finite-dimensional. By symmetry (the `d` side) so is the
`alpha`-span. Hence `h(A) <= 2 + deg p · deg q + h(N) < ∞`, and the distinct-eigenvalue count of
`nilpotent-edge-images-collapse-higman-quotients` gives `Q = 1`.

**Where it stops (gap for derived length 3).** The hypothesis `h(A'') < ∞` enters three times:
- in the error subspace `U`, the image of `A' ∩ phi(W')^(w_0^-1)`;
- in `h(D ∩ N)`;
- in the final eigenvalue count, which needs all of `A` to have finite Hirsch length.

For `A'''= 1` with `A''` of infinite rank, the same argument at the second derived level would need
an element whose `phi`-image is `gamma^e` times a correction, as `phi(z) = gamma^e (z^gamma z)` is at the
first level. The generators `[z^(gamma^i), z^(gamma^j)]` of `W'` have no such form. In `phi` of a
commutator the power `gamma^e` cancels, which leaves no eigen-scaling to exploit. Neither suggested
route closes this.
- **Route (i).** A Mal'cev/unipotent argument needs a single nilpotent group containing both `W` and
  `phi(W)` with compatible filtrations. `phi(W)` is not in `W`, and `W ∩ phi(W)` is not
  `gamma^e`-invariant.
- **Route (ii).** Tracking `h(W ∩ phi(W))` is what this claim does. It closes exactly when the
  bottom `A''` has finite Hirsch length.

**What it kills for Attempt 5.** In the glued construction `G_1' *_A G_1'`, the amenable edge image `A`
must now be outside SbM. It must be not virtually metabelian, and its finite-index subgroups `A_0` must
have `A_0''` of infinite Hirsch length (or not solvable). For example `A` could be solvable of derived
length at least 3 with infinite-rank `A''`, or non-elementary amenable.
