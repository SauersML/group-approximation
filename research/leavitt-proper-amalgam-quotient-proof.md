The factor amenability and equality `<L_0,P>=L` are proved in the required
one-compressor claim. The all-positive-rank Leavitt equivalence transports
`universal-leavitt-el4-nonsofic` to `L=EL_3(R)`. Therefore the literal join
`J=<L,tau,c>` is nonsofic because it contains the nonsofic subgroup `L`.

The intersection `C=A cap B` is amenable. By the standard
Elek--Szabo/Paunescu amenable-amalgamation theorem, `A *_C B` is sofic. The
natural map onto the subgroup generated by the two factors is surjective. If
it were injective, `J` would be sofic. Hence its kernel is nontrivial.
