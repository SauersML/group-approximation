title: The ideal property collapses pure infiniteness to strong pure infiniteness
---

For a purely infinite C*-algebra `C` with the ideal property, `C` is
strongly purely infinite.

If `C` is separable and purely infinite, then the ideal property is
equivalent to `Prim(C)` having a basis of compact-open sets.  Thus a separable
purely infinite algebra with zero-dimensional primitive ideal space is
strongly purely infinite.
