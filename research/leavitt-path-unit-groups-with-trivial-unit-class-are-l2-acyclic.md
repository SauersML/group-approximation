**ESTABLISHED (proved on paper, unreviewed).** Let `K` be a field and `E` a finite graph with no
sinks, cofinal, satisfying condition (L), with `[1] = 0` in `K_0(L_K(E))`. Then
`β_n^(2)(L_K(E)^x) = 0` for all `n >= 0`. If `char K > 0`, the group is nonsofic.
