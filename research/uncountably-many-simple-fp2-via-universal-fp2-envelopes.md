If every countable group embeds in a simple group of type FP_2
(`every-countable-group-embeds-in-a-simple-fp2-group`, the affirmative answer
to Problem 1.25), then in particular every 2-generator group does.

By the established implication
`universal-fp2-simple-envelopes-force-uncountably-many` (item 1, a counting
argument over the continuum many 2-generator groups), there are exactly
`2^aleph_0` isomorphism types of simple groups of type FP_2. In particular
there are uncountably many.
