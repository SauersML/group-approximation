Consider a finite quotient in which the kernel `K` has exactly one elementary
abelian 3-layer `M ~= C3^4` directly below

```text
E=K/K' ~= N_ab ~= C2^9,
```

and `M` is the four-dimensional irreducible `F3[A5]` module generated by the
image of `z`.  Since `|E|` and `|M|` are coprime, the extension of `E` by `M`
splits; equivalently the relevant positive-degree cohomology of `E` with
coefficients in `M` vanishes.  Thus `E` acts on `M` as an elementary abelian
2-subgroup of `GL4(3)`, normalized by `A5`.

Every involution over `F3` is semisimple.  The commuting image of `E` is
therefore simultaneously diagonalizable, and `M` is the sum of at most four
common weight spaces.  The normalizing `A5` permutes those weight spaces.
There is no nontrivial homomorphism `A5 -> S_n` for `n<=4`, so every weight
space is `A5`-invariant and the image of `E` centralizes `A5`.

The exact four-dimensional module is absolutely irreducible, and its
centralizer in `GL4(3)` has order two.  Hence the image of `E` is contained in
the scalar subgroup `{+I,-I}` and is defined by an `A5`-invariant character

```text
E -> C2.                                                   (Q1920-MIX-1)
```

But the universal seam group is perfect: the local `Q1920` is perfect,
`(ct)^3=1` kills `c` in the abelianization, and the seam relations then kill
`z`.  Equivalently the `A5`-coinvariants of `N_ab` vanish.  Therefore every
character `(Q1920-MIX-1)` is trivial.  The `E`-action on `M` is trivial, in
contradiction with the exact seam relation

```text
c z c = z^-1.
```

The same scalar-character argument excludes a one-dimensional `C3` layer
directly below `N_ab`.

## Attempts

This proves nonexistence of the smallest mixed quotient of order
`2^9*3^4*60` (and its one-dimensional analogue); it does not exclude a
solvable kernel with additional derived layers between `N_ab` and the first
3-chief factor.  Such layers can act nonabelianly on the 3-factor, so the
simultaneous-weight-space argument no longer applies without another
induction step.
