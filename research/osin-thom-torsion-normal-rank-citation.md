Citation import from Denis Osin and Andreas Thom, *Normal generation and
`l2`-Betti numbers of groups*, Math. Ann. 355 (2013), 1331--1347,
[arXiv:1108.2411](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.2411), read on 2026-08-24.

The abstract states both facts used here: the inequality relating first
`l2`-Betti number to normal rank is conjectured for torsion-free groups, and
for every `n>=2`, `epsilon>0` the authors construct a simple group with
torsion and first `l2`-Betti number at least `n-1-epsilon`.  Simplicity then
gives normal rank one by the elementary observation that every nonidentity
element has nontrivial, hence total, normal closure.
