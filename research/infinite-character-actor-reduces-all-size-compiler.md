Construct a finitely presented semidirect/module packet with one countable
elementary-abelian coefficient module `M`, a finitely generated actor `A`,
and one D8 seed.  Require that every nontrivial character orbit relevant to
the seed has a uniform bottom `ell^2` spectral gap.  Prove a global
same-basis rounding theorem: every sufficiently accurate finite matrix model
decodes the `M`-action on the marked carrier to one finite-support PVM, and
the finitely many actor covariance relators control its full Dirichlet
energy.

The established infinite-PVM theorem then collapses the marked carrier
directly.  This avoids compiling a separately indexed finite actor for every
`n`.  The load-bearing word is **global**: fixed-window rounding is
insufficient, and any sofic/hyperlinear semidirect-product candidate must
fail exactly at this global PVM interface.
