The rank-one block formula makes `W` orthogonal and its selected diagonal
entry zero.  The simple spectrum of the regular `C_3` packet leaves only the
identity and inversion permutations of character lines, with arbitrary row
phases.  An involutive unitary is self-adjoint and therefore has symmetric
entry moduli.  The two possible values of `|U_10|` are `3/5` and `4/5`, while
`|U_01|=5/13` in both cases, proving the exact obstruction.
