Notation is as in the target. Roots are in α-coordinates, `B` is the Gram form, and `ℓ = ω_1`, so the X-roots
are the roots with `r_1 = 0`.

**Step 1 (monodromy in closed form; `mono.py`).**
- Put `q = b + zℓ`. Wall `r` meets the fibre at `z = −B(r,q)/r_1`. Translate so that the moving point `m` sits at
  `0`. Wall-strand `r` is then at `−B(r,q)/r_1`.
- Along the half-turn of letter `±s_i` at W-state `w`, the walls are `w^{-1}r`.
  - The real part of strand `r` is `A_r + D_r t`, with `A_r = −B(w^{-1}r, x)/r_1` and
    `D_r = 2B(α_i,x)B(w^{-1}r, α_i)/r_1`.
  - The imaginary part is `I_r sin(πt)`, with `I_r = −εB(w^{-1}r, Y)/r_1`. Here `Y = y` for `s_i` and `Y = s_i y`
    for `s_i^{-1}`. The second choice makes `s_i^{-1}` the `s_i`-image of the reversed `s_i` path.
  - So every crossing of the real projection is at an explicit time, and its sign is the sign of the difference of
    imaginary parts there.
- For `i ∈ X`, the strands `r` and `s_i r` rotate about a cluster partner at `t = 1/2`. The three points stay
  collinear, so every projection has a triple point there.
  - It is resolved by generic offsets `η_p ~ 10^{-9}` of the real parts.
  - The heights at the triple point are distinct, so the braid does not depend on `η`.
- Adjacency and non-degeneracy are asserted at every crossing.
- Braids are compared through the Artin action on `F_46`, composed with random `SL_2(𝔽_p)` images.
  - Equal braids give equal images, so a `False` is a certificate (the Artin action is faithful).
  - A `True` is a randomized test with three seeds.
- **Calibration** (`tests.log`).
  - `g g^{-1}` is trivial for every test word, at both base points (`test7.py`).
  - In `A_4` and `B_4`, which have no D-walls, every rank-2 relation holds (`test1.py`, `test4.py`), and so does
    every w15 identity (`test2.py`, `test5.py`).

**Step 2 (Part 1).**
- `D_E ⊇ L_{ij}` holds iff the normal `n` of `D_E` lies in `span(α_i, α_j)`.
- Normals of collision walls satisfy `n_1 = 0`, because `n = r/r_1 − r'/r'_1`.
  - For `i = 1` this forces `n ∈ ℂα_j`. That is an X-root, and no D-wall is an arrangement wall (w16). So no
    D-wall contains `L_{1j}`.
- `dwalls.py` rebuilds the 106 walls and 510 pairs from these normals and counts 9, 10 and 5 walls through
  `L_{23}`, `L_{24}` and `L_{34}` (`dwalls.log`).
- **Local group.** Every X-wall and D-wall through `L_{ij}` contains `ℓ`. Near a generic point of `L_{ij}`, the base
  complement is therefore (a line complement in `ℂ^2`) `× ℂ`, with `n = m_{ij} + k_{ij}` lines.
  - Its `π_1` is `F_{n−1} × ℤ`, generated by meridians `γ_1, …, γ_n` whose product is central.
  - It is a retract of `B°`. The ball's complement includes into `M°`, which includes into the local complement
    `M(𝒜°_{L})`, and the composite is a homotopy equivalence.
  - For `n ≥ 3`, two distinct `γ_i` map to two members of a free basis of `F_{n−1}`, so they generate `F_2`.
- For `(2,4)`, `n = 12`. The local lifts of `s_2^2` and `s_4^2` are the meridians `γ` of `H_{α_2}` and
  `H_{α_4}`, so they do not commute. `test4.py` and `test5.py` confirm this.

**Step 3 (Parts 2 and 3; `test1.py`, `test2.py`, `test4.py`, `test5.py`, `test8.py`, `test9.py`).**
- These are direct evaluations of the words of w15:
  - `a = p_2p_3` and `b = p_1p_3`;
  - `c = s_4^2`, `d = s_3^{-1}s_4^2s_3` and `w = (s_2s_3)s_4^2(s_2s_3)^{-1}`;
  - `u = wc` and `v = wd`.
- "Point-pushing" means that forgetting `m` gives the trivial 45-braid. That is exactly membership in the image of
  `F_45`.
- The local tests use base points whose coordinates `i, j` (or `1, 2, 4`) have size `δ ∈ {10^{-2}, 10^{-3}}`, with
  `ε = δ/7`.
- `random-base.log` and `default-base.log` record the base-point dependence.

**Step 4 (Part 4).**
- `H_1(B°) = ℤ^{121}`, one generator for each of the 15 X-planes and the 106 D-planes.
- So the image of `μ(B°)` in `H_1(PB_45) = ℤ^{\binom{45}{2}} = ℤ^{990}` has rank at most 121.
- A finite-index subgroup has image of finite index in `H_1`. Hence `μ(B°)` has infinite index in `PB_45`.

**What is not shown.**
- It is not shown that *no* lift makes the A_X relations hold. Part 1 rules out local lifts only.
- (Q1), and hence (H″) for H_4, is not decided.
