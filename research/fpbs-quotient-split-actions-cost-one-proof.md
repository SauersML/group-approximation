Written proof in Section 1 of the linked note, steps (a)--(g). It uses the
complete-section induction formula for the quotient relation restricted to
`A`, the marker lemma for aperiodic relations (Kechris--Miller, *Topics in
orbit equivalence*, Lemma 6.7), and uniqueness of disintegration along a
factor on which `N` acts trivially. This is dependency bookkeeping for a
written deduction, not a formal verification.
