If STW Problem VII has a negative answer, then there exists a separable
unital nuclear stably finite nonquasidiagonal C*-algebra `E` with all of the
following properties:

1. `E` has a character `chi:E -> C` and `I=ker(chi)` is stable and
   essential;
2. `chi` is the unique tracial state of `E`, and it is not faithful;
3. `I` is not traceless: it admits a lower-semicontinuous tracial weight
   taking a finite nonzero value;
4. every nonzero bounded trace on `I` is absent, so the weight in item 3 is
   necessarily unbounded.

Conversely, such an `E` is already a counterexample by its stated
nonquasidiagonality.  The claim is a counterexample normal form and does not
assert that an algebra satisfying items 1--4 exists.
