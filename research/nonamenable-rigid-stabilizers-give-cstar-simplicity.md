**ESTABLISHED (import).**

Let `X` be a Hausdorff space and `G` a countable group of homeomorphisms of `X`. Suppose that for every non-empty open
`U ⊆ X` the rigid stabilizer `G_U` is non-amenable. Then `G` is C*-simple, and so is every countable subgroup of
`Homeo(X)` containing `G`.

Here `G_U` is the rigid stabilizer of `U`: the elements acting trivially outside `U`. This is the standard definition,
not re-quoted from the source.
