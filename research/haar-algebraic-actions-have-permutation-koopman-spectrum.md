For an automorphism action on a compact abelian Haar group, Fourier
characters make the Koopman representation a permutation representation of
the dual group.  Every finite stabilizer cylinder has Haar mass zero or a
reciprocal integer; for a linear `F_p` subshift it is zero or `p^-r`.
Therefore arbitrary p.m.p. actions and IRS laws cannot be installed by a
mere Haar relabeling.  A non-base-equivariant crossed-product embedding
remains possible.
