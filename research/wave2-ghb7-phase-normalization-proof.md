Sections 1-3 give the complete quantitative proof. The scalar gap
puts each unitary relator within kappa/sqrt(2c) of its radial trace
phase. The prerequisite's multiplier exponent m=2401 supplies fixed
finite identities r_i^m in [F,N] for each commutator relator. Evaluating
them and telescoping each relator occurrence bounds the scalar phase's
mth-power defect by K kappa/sqrt(2c). Small original defect selects
the phase branch with argument less than pi/m, giving a linear bound
on its distance from 1. Near-one scalar seventh roots correct the
three power phases and leave every iterated commutator unchanged.
Both doubled copies receive the same phase, so gap, centrality and
copy mismatches are exactly invariant. The reverse bound kappa<=2delta
holds after every scalar rephasing. The exact-order-seven corollary
uses the commutator bounds directly without rephasing.
