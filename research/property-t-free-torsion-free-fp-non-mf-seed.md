**OPEN MINIMAL TORSION-FREE INPUT.**  Construct a finitely presented
torsion-free group `E` such that `E` is not MF, with no Property `(T)`,
Kazhdan subgroup, Kazhdan projection, or Kazhdan transport in the proof.

By `universal-mf-quotient`, this is exactly the existence of some

```text
1 != d in Rad_MF(E).
```

No acylindrical-hyperbolicity or full-radical hypothesis is needed at the
seed stage: `baumslag-solitar-radical-tether-preserves-mf-reflection`
supplies the former while preserving `d`, and routing `d` supplies the
latter.

## Attempts

- The binary-Leavitt absorber cannot be transferred: it is generated by
  involutions, so every homomorphism from it to a torsion-free group is
  trivial by `torsion-generated-mf-absorbers-die-in-torsion-free-targets`.
- A torsion-free Higman embedding would suffice if one first produced a
  recursively presented torsion-free non-MF seed, but that is the same
  missing analytic input at a weaker finiteness level; the embedding does
  not manufacture MF-radical membership.
