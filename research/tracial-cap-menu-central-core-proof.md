The proof is given in
`tracial-cap-menus-are-central-core-partitions`.  Domination of each summand
by the supplied trace gives a bounded density.  The trace identity makes
that density central, its support lies below every selected cap projection,
and finite central disjointification gives the required partition.
Conversely, normalized traces on the pieces of such a central partition
give the cap-supported convex decomposition.  The same density argument
makes a tracial projection postselection central, while the equality case
of Schwarz makes an exact compression reducing and hence central.
