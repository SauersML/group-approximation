Let G be a contracting self-similar group of automorphisms of the rooted
d-ary tree, d >= 2, and put B=V_d(G). For every finite set F of eventually
periodic points of the boundary, both the pointwise stabilizer Fix_B(F)
and the setwise stabilizer Stab_B(F) are finitely presented.

The attached route gives a written proof using an ascending HNN extension.
This strengthens the finite-generation conclusion of Belk--Matucci,
Proposition 6; finite presentation of these stabilizers is not being
attributed to that proposition. This is not a Lean-checked theorem.
