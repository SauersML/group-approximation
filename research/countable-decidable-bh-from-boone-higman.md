Survey arXiv:2306.16356v3, Corollary 3.5, verbatim: "If the Boone--Higman
conjecture holds, then, every countable group with a solvable word problem embeds
into a finitely presented simple group."

**Proof.** The survey proves Proposition 3.3 as follows: "the
Higman--Neumann--Neuman construction in [HNN] embeds every countable group with a
solvable word problem into a two-generated group with solvable word problem." So
a countable decidable `G` lies in a two-generated `G*` with solvable word problem.
Boone–Higman embeds `G*` in a finitely presented simple group, and `G` follows. ∎
