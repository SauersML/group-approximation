title: A cyclic recursively presented nonresidual free-group module can still give a sofic envelope
distinct_from:
  finite-type-linear-vfree-envelope-is-residually-finite: that excludes finite-type examples; this gives an infinitely related nonresidual example.

There is a cyclic recursively presented `F_p[F]`-module, for finite-rank free
`F`, which is not residually finite and whose semidirect product with `F` is
also not residually finite, yet is sofic.  Thus infinite presentation opens
the nonresidual module branch, but nonresiduality alone gives no
non-Connes-embeddability.
