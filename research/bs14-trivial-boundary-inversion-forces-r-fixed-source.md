Let an exact dilation have boundary space `QH`, compressed space `PH`, and

```text
R~=1_Q (+) R,
X~=[ C   B* ]
   [ B   A  ].                                        (TBI1)
```

Then the inversion relation

```text
X~ R~ X~=R~^(-1)
```

together with `X~^2=1` is equivalent to the linear intertwining equation
`X~R~=R~^(-1)X~`, whose blocks are

```text
R B=B,
B*R=B*,
A R=R^(-1)A.                                         (TBI2)
```

The involution equations additionally read

```text
C^2+B*B=1,
A^2+BB*=1,
CB*+B*A=0.                                           (TBI3)
```

Thus every nonzero source column of `B` lies in `Fix(R)`.  A joint CS
decomposition or an integer transportation of the two cubic carriers is not
sufficient unless its source cells satisfy `(TBI2)`.

The trivial boundary type is forced in the neutral two-carrier setup.  If a
boundary representation `(R_Q,S_Q)` is fixed pointwise by both `S_Q^2` and
`R_QS_Q`, then `S_Q^2=1`, `R_Q=S_Q^(-1)`, and the BS relation gives
`S_Q=S_Q^4`.  Hence `S_Q^3=1`; together with `S_Q^2=1` this yields

```text
R_Q=S_Q=1.                                           (TBI4)
```

On a multiplicity-one scalar BS cycle `(O,v)` of length `m`, `Fix(R)` is
nonzero exactly when the scalar return phase is `v=1`.  In that case it is
one dimensional, generated by the normalized orbit sum `z`.  For
`g=S^(-2)RS`,

```text
<z,gz>=(1/m) sum_(mu in O) mu^2.                     (TBI5)
```

Therefore the arbitrary convex numerical-range coordinate in the Gram-only
calculation collapses, under inversion, to one explicit orbit average.

This restriction still does not restore a uniform exit angle.  Take

```text
lambda_m=exp(2 pi i/(4^m-1)),
O_m={lambda_m^(4^j):0<=j<m},
v=1.                                                  (TBI6)
```

The orbit has length exactly `m`, and its allowed source satisfies

```text
|1-<z,gz>|
 <= (1/m) sum_j |1-lambda_m^(2*4^j)|
 <= 4 pi/(3m) ->0.                                   (TBI7)
```

Hence the exit cosine from `(SAE2)` tends to one even after imposing the
exact inversion-compatible source condition.  These long-cycle active cells
must be paid by the energy ledger; they cannot be eliminated by an angle
theorem.
