**ESTABLISHED.**

Let `Gamma <= G` be the Kun--Thom Theorem E pair,
`Gamma = EL_r(F_q[x_1,...,x_d])`,
`G = EL_r(F_q[x_1^(+-1),...,x_d^(+-1)]) semidirect SL_d(Z)`, `r, d >= 3`, and let
`t = E_12(1)` in `SL_d(Z)`, a strict compressor. For every prime `m` with
`q = 1 mod m` there are a genuine finite-dimensional unitary representation
`pi_m` of `G` and a projection `p_m` in `Z(pi_m(Gamma)')` with

```text
||Ad pi_m(t)(p_m) - p_m||_2 <= sqrt(2/m),
dist_2(p_m, {q in Z(pi_m(Gamma)') : q a projection, Ad pi_m(t)(q) = q})
    >= (1 - 1/m)/sqrt(2).
```

So the invariance defect tends to `0` while the distance to every invariant
central projection tends to `1/sqrt(2)`.

**Mechanism.** In the congruence quotient `E semidirect SL_d(Z/m)` with
`q = 1 mod m` the group algebra splits completely, so `E = EL_r(F_q)^X` with
`X = (Z/m)^d`, and the image of `Gamma` is all of `E`. For an induced
representation `Ind_E^(E semidirect SL_d(Z/m))(W_chi)` the minimal central
projections of `pi(Gamma)'` are the isotypic projections `z_psi`, one per
character `psi` in the orbit of `chi`, all of equal trace, and

```text
Ad pi(s)(z_psi) = z_(s . psi).
```

The compressor therefore acts on the centre of the commutant by the permutation
it induces on characters. For the unipotent `t = E_12(1)` that permutation has
all its nontrivial cycles of length exactly `m`, and an arc of half a long cycle
is almost invariant while the only invariant sets are unions of whole cycles.

## Why no spectral gap can repair it

The projection `p_m` is EXACTLY `Gamma`-central; nothing about it is approximate
in the `Gamma`-direction. What is only approximate is its invariance under the
single element `t`. Property (T) of `Gamma` governs the `Gamma`-direction and is
silent here, because the compressor direction is the cyclic, hence amenable,
group generated by `Ad pi(t)`. An arc of half a long cycle is precisely a
Folner set for that action, so almost-invariant projections are not an accident
of this construction but a structural feature of every model in which the
compressor permutes the atoms of `Z(pi(Gamma)')` with long cycles. Long cycles
are the generic case: any `t` of large order on the character set produces them.

So the rounding the median step wants is being asked of an amenable action, where
almost-invariance never implies proximity to invariance, and the Kazhdan
rounding available in the `Gamma`-direction cannot be substituted for it.

## What it kills, and what it does not

- It does **not** refute `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs` or (CCR). By
  `ccr-holds-for-all-genuine-fd-coordinate-models` the algebra `pi(Gamma)'` is
  exactly `Ad pi(t)`-invariant in this very model. Only individual projections
  fail to round.
- It **does** kill, dimension-free and already for exact representations of the
  genuine pair, any proof step of the form *an almost-invariant central
  projection of the `Gamma`-commutant is close in `||·||_2` to an exactly
  invariant one*. That is the shape the Hilbert--Schmidt transcription of the
  Kun--Thom median step needs. The obstruction is the absence of integrality:
  all `N` atoms carry the same trace `1/N`, and a cycle has no canonical arc,
  whereas the permutation proof draws its canonicity from integer orbit counts
  and isotropy orders with a residual index below two.
- It evades both fences recorded on `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs`. It is not
  small-block surgery, since `tr(p_m) -> 1/2` is macroscopic; and it is not
  amplification, since the multiplicity space is untouched and the cycle lives in
  the centre, whereas `sigma (x) 1` acts componentwise and changes neither
  estimate.

What remains licensed is a step that rounds the commutant ALGEBRA, which
Theorem A supplies for free in genuine models, or one that uses an integrality
invariant not available in the centre of a matrix commutant.
