**ESTABLISHED** (proof: `virtually-inner-compression-kill-proof`).

**Setting.**  `T` is a unital ring admitting an injective unital ring
homomorphism `T -> prod_omega M_(N_k)(F_k)` into an algebraic ultraproduct of
full matrix algebras over fields `F_k`.  `S <= T` is a unital subring,
`sigma in Aut(S)`, and suppose there are `m >= 1` and a unit `v in T^x` with

```text
sigma^m(s) = v s v^{-1}      for all s in S.
```

**Theorem.**  Let `R <= S` be a finitely generated unital subring with
`sigma(R) <= R`, and let `z in S` commute with every element of `R`.  Then `z`
commutes with every element of `sigma^{-1}(R)`, indeed of `sigma^{-j}(R)` for
all `j >= 0`.  So condition (G2) of
`simple-ring-with-centralizer-compressing-automorphism` fails for
`(S, sigma)`, whatever `R` and `z` are.

**Invariant and dying step.**  The invariant is the finite dimension of the
matrix models.  In a model, conjugation by the image of `v` maps the finite
dimensional algebra generated by the images of the generators of `R` into
itself, hence onto itself.  So the preimage `v^{-1} R v`, which contains every
`sigma^{-j}(R)` with `j <= m`, is invisible to commutators with `z`.

**Scope.**
- `m = 1` with `T` an overring is Attempt 1 on
  `simple-ring-with-centralizer-compressing-automorphism`.  The new content is
  that any positive power suffices, which brings in all automorphisms of
  finite order modulo inner ones, for instance gauge and Frobenius twists.
- No hypothesis is placed on `z` beyond commuting with `R`.
- It applies to every (G1) ring of the graph that is LEF as a ring, in
  particular to `LC(X,F_q) semidirectProduct Z` by
  `minimal-subshift-algebra-is-simple-lef-ring`.  See
  `subshift-diagonal-automorphisms-need-infinite-spatial-class` for which
  automorphisms of those rings have an inner power.

DERIVATION
virtually-inner-compression-kill-proof
