Injectivity of `T_f->Gamma` identifies its complex group algebra with a
finite-dimensional `C^*`-subalgebra of `C[Gamma]`, and the canonical group
trace restricts to its faithful regular trace.  Thus the nonzero forbidden
projection has positive trace.  Trace is invariant under each proposed
stable-letter conjugacy, whereas containment of the total exit below a
conjugate of `P_f` bounds its trace by `tau(P_f)`.  The established source
trace is `5tau(P_f)/4`, yielding `(EFR2)`.
