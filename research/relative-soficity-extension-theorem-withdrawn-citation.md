Bibliographic import.  The arXiv abstract page for arXiv:1807.07600
(Ji--Ogle--Ramsey, *Relative Amenability and Relative Soficity*) was read on
2026-08-17.  It records the paper as withdrawn, with the comment "Error in
proof of Theorem 3.15"; the submission history shows v2 and v4 marked withdrawn
and the final withdrawal dated 10 January 2019.

What makes this establishable rather than merely reported is that the claim it
supports is itself bibliographic — that the sofic-by-residually-amenable
extension statement has no standing source — and that is exactly what an arXiv
withdrawal record settles.  The claim explicitly does **not** assert the
statement is false, which no record could settle.
