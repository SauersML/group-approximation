Let `E` be the countable `F_2`-vector space generated by nonzero tree vectors
`e_x`, `x in {0,1}^*`, subject to

```text
e_x=e_(x0)+e_(x1),                                      (ACL1)
```

so that the `2^n` level-`n` leaves form a basis. Let `H=GL_fin(E)` and
`K=E rtimes H`. Then:

1. `K` is locally finite, hence amenable, sofic, and hyperlinear;
2. the `e_x` are commuting involutions;
3. every `e_x` is `H`-conjugate to every `e_y`;
4. every parent is the product of its two commuting conjugate children;
5. every finite-depth collection of these relations, together with arbitrary
   finite leaf permutations/conjugators, lies in one finite affine subgroup;
6. the left regular representation of that finite subgroup satisfies all
   those relations exactly while

   ```text
   ||lambda(e_empty)-I||_2^2=2.                         (ACL2)
   ```

Consequently no dimension-free strict normalized-HS ceiling follows solely
from a finite collection of binary prefix-clone axioms: split into two
commuting conjugate copies, iterate the split, and permute/conjugate finitely
many leaves. A successful bare-Thompson argument must use an additional
overlap, collision, or holonomy relation not valid in this affine model.
