Let Q be finitely presented, let 1->N->G->Q->1 be the finite
Ollivier–Wise construction, and let Gamma<=Q be any subgroup.
For H=pi^-1(Gamma), the double P=G *_H G satisfies Baum–Connes with
coefficients. This conclusion uses hyperbolicity of G and tree
permanence, and is independent of every new nonhyperlinearity input.
It applies to both the native four-by-four and earlier nine-leaf
Leavitt choices. No claim that an arbitrary hyperbolic cover inherits
a bad approximation property from its quotient is involved.
