L. Bowen, *Every countably infinite group is almost Ornstein*, arXiv:1103.4424v3. Read on 2026-09-18 from the arXiv PDF
(text extracted with `pypdf`), Section 1. Verbatim:

- "A probability space (K, κ) is a two-atom space if κ is supported on two atoms: i.e., there exist
  elements k0, k1 ∈ K such that κ({k0, k1}) = 1. We say that a group G is almost Ornstein if whenever
  (K, κ), (L, λ) are standard probability spaces, neither of which is a two-atom space and
  H(K, κ) = H(L, λ) then G↷(K^G, κ^G) is isomorphic to G↷(L^G, λ^G)."
- "Theorem 1.2. Every countably inﬁnite group is almost Ornstein."

The isomorphism is a measure-conjugacy of the Bernoulli shifts, i.e. a `G`-equivariant measure-space
isomorphism.
