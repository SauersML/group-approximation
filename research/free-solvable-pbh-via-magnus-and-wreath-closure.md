Direct proof (2026-09-13, lane `solve-bh-free-solvable`). Not independently reviewed.

`B_A` is closed under subgroups by definition: it is the class of groups that embed
in some group admitting an action of type (A).

**(a)**
- By `magnus-embedding-f-over-n-prime-into-wreath-product`, `F/[N, N]` embeds in
  the restricted regular wreath product `Z^r wr (F/N)`.
- `Z^r` is in `B_A`. It is isomorphic to the diagonal subgroup of `GL_r(Q)`
  generated by `diag(1, ..., 2, ..., 1)`, a finitely generated linear group in
  characteristic zero (`char-zero-linear-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`).
- By `permutational-boone-higman-closed-under-wreath-products`, with `A = Z^r`,
  `G = F/N` and `X = F/N` acting on itself by left multiplication (trivial
  stabilizers), `Z^r wr (F/N)` is in `B_A`.
- So `F/[N, N]` is in `B_A`.

**(b)** Induction on `d`. The case `d = 0` is the hypothesis.
- `N^(d-1)` is normal in `F`: it is characteristic in `N`, which is normal in `F`.
- `N^(d) = [N^(d-1), N^(d-1)]`, so (a) applied to `N^(d-1)` gives `F/N^(d) ∈ B_A`
  from `F/N^(d-1) ∈ B_A`.

**(c)** Take `N = F' = F^(1)`.
- `F/F' ≅ Z^r` is in `B_A`, as in (a).
- For `d >= 1`, `F^(d) = N^(d-1)` with `N^(0) = F'`.
- By (b), `S_(r,d) = F/F^(d)` is in `B_A`.

**Consequence.** By `type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups` (Zaremsky,
Corollary B), every subgroup of `S_(r,d)` embeds in a finitely presented simple
group and, if finitely generated, has solvable word problem. `∎`

**What the argument uses.** Only the Magnus embedding and closure of `B_A` under
restricted regular wreath products with lamp group `Z^r`. The top group is never
required to be linear.
