**ESTABLISHED EXACT-FD REDUCTION.**  Work over the odd splitting field in
the full relative affine-Leavitt ring `R_A=L_A(k[C_aff])`.  Put

```text
e=s_0t_0,               f=s_(00)t_(00),
x=s_(00)t_0,            y=s_0t_(00),
W=x_12(x)x_21(-y)x_12(x).                              (FWA1)
```

The complete coefficient table used by this word is

```text
yx=e,        xy=f,
fx=x=xe,     ey=y=yf,
(1-f)x=x(1-e)=0,       y(1-f)=(1-e)y=0.               (FWA2)
```

Consequently its elementary image on the two displayed coordinates is

```text
W=[[1-f,x],[-y,1-e]],
W^(-1)=[[1-f,-x],[y,1-e]],
W^2=diag(1-2f,1-2e),       W^4=1.                     (FWA3)
```

These are all the coefficient occurrences in the signed Whitehead word:
the first and third occurrences are the same root `x_12(x)`, and the middle
one is `x_21(-y)`.  In the full Steinberg presentation the named product
roots are authenticated by the usual spare-index triangles, for example

```text

x_13(y)=[x_12(s_0),x_23(t_(00))],                     (FWA4)
```

and the relations in `(FWA2)` are their further paired multiplication
triangles.  Thus the independent `UT_4` factors used in
`leavitt-range-sum-fold-does-not-pay-reynolds` cannot be attached to these
occurrences in an exact representation of the full coefficient group.

There is one harmless Steinberg-kernel issue in using `<W>` as an amalgam

that the chosen Steinberg lift has fourth power one.  Resolve it explicitly

```text

```

This group is finitely presented and still maps to `EL_5(R_A)`.  The image
of `W` has exact order four by `(FWA3)` and the faithful Leavitt model, so
`W` has exact order four in `Gamma_A'`.  The active root

Let `G_CZ` be the controlled two-qubit Pauli group and use the explicit


```text

```

Bass--Serre normal form embeds both factors, hence `J!=1` in `Gamma_FWA`.
The group is finitely presented.

Nevertheless every exact finite-dimensional unitary representation `rho`

```text

```

Indeed, apply the kernel-ideal proof of
