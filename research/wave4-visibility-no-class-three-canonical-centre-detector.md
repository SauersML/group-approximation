For the canonical face-zero central extension B_c0 of K7, its central
generator satisfies z in gamma_4(B_c0). Thus every homomorphism into
a group of nilpotency class at most three kills z.

In particular c0 mod7 does not inflate from H^2(K7^ab;F7), where
K7^ab=C7^4. It lies outside the span of cup products of degree-one
classes and their Bocksteins. No class-four or higher exclusion and
no conclusion about all finite central images is asserted.
