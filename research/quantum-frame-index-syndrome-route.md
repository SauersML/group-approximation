Cut the separated mark to its positive-density spectral carrier and attach the
stable frame index to every bounded-degree overlap chart.  The marked parity
twist and balanced-product expansion label a fixed fraction of that carrier by
nonzero local indices.  Local stability pays each label from its relator
neighborhood, and bounded incidence sums those payments without a volume
loss.  This is exactly the mass-transfer and local-payment interface of the
target.

No random hyperplane or global classical assignment is introduced, so an
exact finite-dimensional quantum solution has zero syndrome as required.
