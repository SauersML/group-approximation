Fix one completed block and transport every equally sized comparison block
to it by a Witt isometry which is the identity on the tested lamp span.  If
`S_g` and `S'_g` extend the same tested partial covariance, then
`Q_g=S'_g S_g^(-1)` fixes that partial covariance data.  Substitution in
`S'_gS'_h=S'_(gh)` gives `(CVC1)` exactly.  The symplectic equations and
the multiplication equations are the matrix identities `(CVC2)`, which
contain products of the unknown extension entries.

By contrast, after `K` and `S'` are fixed, replacing a Clifford lift by
`W(r_g)U_g` gives the linear formula `(CPC4)` from
`clifford-phase-space-curvature-is-a-twisted-f2-cocycle`.  Thus completion
selection and lift repair are two different stages: the first changes the
coefficient module and action, while only the second is a fixed
`F_2`-coboundary problem.  Since `private-neighbour-density` applies only
after a bad condition has been expressed as affine Laurent denominator
cosets, it cannot be invoked before an additional parameterization theorem
for `(CVC1)-(CVC2)` and the resulting class `[a_(S')]` is proved.
