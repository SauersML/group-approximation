The computation is `experiments/ghb7-kernel-index/psi_order.py`, run on MSI on
2026-09-12, with output `experiments/ghb7-kernel-index/psi_order-output-2026-09-12.txt`.

1. **The form.** The equations `X^T B X = B`, for `X` running over the three generator
   images, are a linear system in the 16 entries of `B` over `F_7`. Its solution space
   is 1-dimensional, spanned by the `B` in the claim. `B^T = −B` with zero diagonal,
   and `det B = 1`, so `B` is a nondegenerate alternating form. Hence
   `ψ(G) ⊆ Sp(B) ≅ Sp_4(F_7)`.
2. **The order.** `ψ(G)` acts faithfully on the 2400 nonzero vectors of `F_7^4`.
   Deterministic Schreier–Sims (`sympy.combinatorics.PermutationGroup.order`) on the
   three permutation images gives order `276595200 = 7^4 (7^2−1)(7^4−1) = |Sp_4(F_7)|`.
   The cross-check on the 400 points of `PG(3,7)` gives `138297600 = |PSp_4(F_7)|`.
   With step 1, `ψ(G) = Sp(B)`.
3. **Theoretical cross-check** (not needed for the certificate). `ψ(c) = I + E_31` is a
   transvection. The algebra spanned by the image is all of `M_4(F_7)`, as is the algebra
   spanned by 12 random conjugates of `ψ(c)`
   (`experiments/ghb7-kernel-index/psi_image.py`). So the normal closure of `ψ(c)` is an irreducible
   group generated by transvections preserving `B`, and McLaughlin's theorem identifies
   it with `Sp_4(F_7)`. Only the computation above is relied on.
4. **Kernel invariants.** `[G:H] = |ψ(G)| = 276595200`. The established kernel claim
   gives `χ(H) = [G:H]·1381/2401 = 159091200` and `b_2(H;Q) = χ(H) − 1 = 159091199`.
   `ghb7-l2-betti-two-is-orbifold-euler-characteristic` gives
   `b_2^(2)(H) = χ(H) = 159091200`.
