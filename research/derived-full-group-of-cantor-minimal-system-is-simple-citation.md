**Source.** H. Matui, *Some remarks on topological full groups of Cantor minimal systems*, Internat. J. Math. 17 (2006),
no. 2, 231–251, doi:10.1142/S0129167X06003448 (Crossref); arXiv:math/0404117v3.

**How it was read.** The arXiv PDF was extracted to text on MSI with `gs txtwrite`, and the passages below were read
there. The journal version was not compared.

**Verbatim.**
- "Theorem 4.9. Let (X,ϕ) be a Cantor minimal system. The commutator subgroup D([[ϕ]]_0) is simple."
- From its proof: "Take a nontrivial element γ ∈ N." Then "the commutator σγ^{-1}σγ belongs to ker sgn ∩ [[ϕ]]_x =
  D([[ϕ]]_x)", and "Then using Lemma 3.4 shows that N contains D([[ϕ]]_x)."
- §3: "The symmetric group S_{h(v)} has the normal subgroup A_{h(v)}, and so G_m has the normal subgroup H_m isomorphic
  to ⊕_{v∈V_m} A_{h(v)}."
- "Lemma 3.4. In the above setting, the commutator subgroup D(G) equals to the union ∪H_m. Furthermore, D(G) is simple
  if the Bratteli diagram B is simple, or equivalently R is minimal."
