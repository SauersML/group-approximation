Sections 4--5 of the artifact prove the claim. For `A=pi(g)`
and a finite coordinate packet `E`, include in a finite set `D`
the image-column supports of `A(V_E)` and inverse-row supports
of `epsilon_j A^(-1)`, for all `j in E`. Their common kernel
complements `A(V_E)` in `V_D`. Extend `A|V_E` to an invertible
map of `V_D` using that complement, and set it to identity
outside `D`. Its conjugation on every root in `GL(E)` is exactly
that of `A`; faithfulness on the normal finitary kernel upgrades
the literal equality to the abstract group.

For a finite word family include intermediate conjugates and the
inverse conjugates of those elements before choosing `E`. Then
both positive and inverse actor letters implement the required
action on every inspected element. A regular representation of
one containing finite packet realizes the whole covariance
diagram. The argument imposes no identity on an implementing
word beyond its prescribed conjugation action.
