**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `P` be a finitely generated group, `X ⊆ A^P` a nonempty
subshift and `k` a field. If `LC(X, k) ⋊ P` is finitely presented as a `k`-algebra, then `P` is
finitely presented.

**Why it matters.** It adds "`P` finitely presented" to the necessary shape of hosts in
`decidable-group-algebras-have-fp-cantor-crossed-hosts`. So torsion-free Farrell–Jones groups that
are finitely generated but not finitely presented can never serve.
