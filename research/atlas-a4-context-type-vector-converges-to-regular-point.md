For every canonical regular-atlas sequence and every simultaneous exactification
from `atlas-a4-context-network-simultaneous-hs-exactification`, normalize the
irreducible multiplicity vector at each finite-group vertex by the common
matrix dimension.

Then these normalized vectors converge to one fixed point of the rational
polytope `P_A4`:

- at each of the two `A8` chart vertices, the regular `A8` proportions;
- at every one of the thirty `A4` context vertices, the regular `A4`
  proportions `1:1:1:3`.

Thus the asymptotic atlas problem has **no discrete multiplicity optimization
left at leading order**.  The integer-flow polytope is useful for exact finite
certificates, but every hypothetical hyperlinear sequence approaches its one
regular point.  The remaining obstruction is entirely in the finite family of
unitary edge/cycle holonomies and the collision-19243 constraint.
