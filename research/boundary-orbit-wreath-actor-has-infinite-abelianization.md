Let H=<hat(G),gamma> and E=V_m(H) be the explicit construction in
`boundary-orbit-cyclic-wreath-germ-proof`, before any repetition of
the alphabet. There is a surjective homomorphism chi:E->Z that
sends gamma to 1 and vanishes on V_m(hat(G)). In particular E has
infinite abelianization and its commutator subgroup has infinite index.

This does not obstruct the finitely presented simple envelope supplied
by the twisted Brin--Thompson construction. It does rule out obtaining
finite presentation of E's commutator subgroup merely by treating it
as a finite-index subgroup of E.
