**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `R` be a p.m.p. equivalence relation and let `Φ` be a graphing of `R`
with `C(Φ) = C(R) < ∞`. Then almost every `Φ`-connected component is a tree, i.e. `Φ` is a treeing.

Imported from D. Gaboriau, *Around the orbit equivalence theory, measure equivalence, cost and ℓ2 Betti
numbers*, lecture notes, version of 3 October 2025, Proposition 2.23 and Definition 2.16. The verbatim
text is in `gaboriau-attained-finite-cost-graphing-is-treeing-citation`.
