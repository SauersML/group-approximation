**Established by citation.**  Connor Paddock and William Slofstra,
*Satisfiability problems and algebras of boolean constraint system games*,
arXiv:2310.07901v2 (2025).

Definition 3.1 presents the BCS algebra by the forbidden joint projections;
Lemma 3.4 and Corollary 3.5 prove `(PSD1)` and equivalence with the standard
relation-polynomial presentation.  Theorem 3.11 identifies perfect classical,
finite quantum, quantum-approximable, and commuting-operator strategies with
the corresponding scalar, matrix, `R^U`, and tracial representations.
