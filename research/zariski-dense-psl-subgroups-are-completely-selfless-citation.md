Narutaka Ozawa, *Proximality and selflessness for group C\*-algebras*, arXiv:2508.07938v8
(24 April 2026), read from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-12. Verbatim:

- **Theorem 14.** *"A group Γ with property P_PHP is completely C\*-selfless."*
- **Proposition 16.** *"Every Zariski-dense subgroup Γ ≤ PSL(d ≥ 2, R) satisfies property
  P_PHP."*
- Section 6: *"A group is completely C\*-selfless if its reduced group C\*-algebra is completely
  selfless."* Complete selflessness implies selflessness (Section 6 of the same paper).
