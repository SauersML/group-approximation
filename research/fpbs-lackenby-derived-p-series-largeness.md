title: A finitely presented group whose derived p-series has linear mod-p homology growth is p-large

Let `G` be finitely presented and `p` a prime. Write `D_0=G` and
`D_(i+1)=[D_i,D_i]D_i^p` for the derived `p`-series. Suppose
`inf_i d_p(D_i)/[G:D_i] > 0`, where `d_p = dim_(F_p) H_1(-;F_p)`. Then `G` is
`p`-large: some normal subgroup of `p`-power index maps onto a nonabelian free
group.

This is a literature import (Lackenby), carried for its use against property
(T). A `p`-large group has a finite index subgroup with infinite abelianization,
so it is not Kazhdan.
