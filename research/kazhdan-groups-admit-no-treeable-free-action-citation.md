Citation import from D. Gaboriau, *Around the orbit equivalence theory, measure equivalence, cost and ℓ2
Betti numbers*, lecture notes dated October 3, 2025,
https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/ME-Cost-L2-Lectures/ME-Cost-L2-lectures.pdf, Section 2.3,
page 12.

- **Theorem 2.22 ([AS90]).** "Infinite groups with Kazhdan property (T) do not admit any treeable free action."

The context is the paragraph after Remark 2.21 and Definition 2.18 ("treeable if it admits a free p.m.p.
action that is treeable"). [AS90] is S. Adams and R. Spatzier, *Kazhdan groups, cocycles and trees*, Amer.
J. Math. 112 (1990). The original paper was not read. Read 2026-09-17 by lane b-p-fp4 from the PDF above,
extracted with pypdf. Nothing is re-proved here.
