Citation import, not a reproof. Read from the PDF of arXiv:2305.15176v2 (dated
July 12, 2024 on p. 1, stamped 11 Jul 2024) on 2026-09-13, pages 1–4:

- Abstract: "We construct examples of finitely presented simple groups whose Dehn
  functions are at least exponential. To the best of our knowledge, these are the
  first such examples known."
- p. 2, Theorem 3.2 as restated in the introduction: "There exist finitely
  presented simple groups with at least exponential Dehn function."
- p. 4, Definition 1.1 (quasi-retract) and Citation 1.2: "Let G and H be finitely
  presented groups, viewed as metric spaces via word metrics coming from finite
  generating sets. Suppose H is a quasi-retract of G. Then δ_H ≼ δ_G." Attributed
  to Alonso [Alo90, Alo96].

Journal status "To appear in Michigan Math. J." is from the arXiv abstract page.
The proof of Theorem 3.2 in §3 was not re-read here.
