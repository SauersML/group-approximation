## Attribution

Jihao Liu, [*Nonhyperlinear groups exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf) (2026-09-20), Proposition 6.6. This node records or conditionally uses Liu's result; it does not claim that analytic contribution for Cairn.

Let C=pi(H)' cap M for a finitely generated Kazhdan group in a specified tracial matrix ultraproduct. Assume a sequence of uniformly operator- and 2-norm-bounded coordinate maps induces E_C on EVERY bounded sequence. If projections p_k in C satisfy tr(1-p_k)->0 and each p_k C p_k is internal in lifted coordinate corners, then C is internal in the original matrix dimensions.

Neither nested corners nor a scalar gap uniform in k is assumed. Adjoin scalars on 1-p_k; the resulting internal unital algebra B_k has ||E_C-E_(B_k)||_(infinity->2)<=2 sqrt(tr(1-p_k)). Transfer this UNIFORM estimate to coordinate maps and diagonalize in k. This is the exhaustion step of Liu Proposition 6.6, separated from block formation.
