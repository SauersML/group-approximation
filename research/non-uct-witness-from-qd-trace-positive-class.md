positive on every quasidiagonal trace, then Theorem E of the stw-bk artifact
gives a non-quasidiagonal trace `σ` with `A/J_σ` separable, nuclear and outside
the UCT class.  So the Problem VII mechanism of
`stw07-qd-trace-positive-class-kills-qd-extension` refutes Problem II at the same
time.  (Wired by the stw-uct lane from the stw-bk lane's established theorem.)
