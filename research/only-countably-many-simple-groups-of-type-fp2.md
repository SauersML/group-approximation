Up to isomorphism, there are at most countably many simple groups of type
FP_2 (over `Z`).

This is the negative answer to the first part of Zaremsky's Problem 1.20. It
implies the negative answer to the second part
(`only-countably-many-simple-groups-of-type-fp`).

## Attempts

- 2026-09-13 (lane z1-20-simple-fp2): no obstruction known. By
  `simple-fp2-uncountability-via-perfect-simple-quotients`, this claim says
  that every finitely presented group has only countably many perfect normal
  subgroups with simple quotient. A proof would have to control non-recursive
  complexity: groups of type FP_2 need not be recursively presentable (Leary,
  arXiv:1512.06609, Theorem 1.2(4)), so Kuznetsov's argument that simple
  recursively presented groups have solvable word problem does not apply.
- 2026-09-17 (c-fp2count, family minimal-counterexample, role inverter):
  forced structure and calibration; the target stays OPEN.
  - **Reformulation, ESTABLISHED**
    (`simple-fp2-countability-iff-scattered-kernel-spaces`). For finitely
    generated `H`, the perfect maximal kernels `MP(H)` form a `G_δ`, hence
    Polish, subspace of the Chabauty space. The target is equivalent to each
    of:
    - `MP(H)` is scattered for every finitely presented `H`;
    - `MP(H)` contains no Cantor set, for every such `H`;
    - every such `H` has only countably many maximal `N` with `H/N` of type
      FP_2, that is, with `N/⟨⟨F⟩⟩` perfect for some finite `F ⊆ N`.

    A counterexample yields a **perfect** finitely presented `H` with a compact
    Cantor family `K ⊆ MP(H)` with uniform moduli. Every `N ∈ K` has these
    properties:
    - it is not finitely normally generated;
    - it is neither r.e. nor co-r.e.;
    - it has infinite quotient;
    - `H_2(H) ↠ H_2(H/N)`.

    Isolation arguments that do not isolate *every* kernel prove nothing.
  - **Obstruction, ESTABLISHED**
    (`countable-simple-fp2-needs-all-three-hypotheses`). Each two of the three
    hypotheses (maximal, perfect, finitely presented ambient) admit an
    uncountable established witness:
    - Leary envelopes, via the FP_2 characterization, drop maximality;
    - the torsion-free hyperbolic Kazhdan group of
      `kazhdan-hyperbolic-continuum-non-fng-maximal-kernels` drops perfectness;
    - `A(G_V × (Z ⋉ {0,1}^Z))` drops finite presentability.

    So the injectivity step of any countable-invariant proof dies on the
    witness for whichever hypothesis it does not read. That kills
    Kuznetsov-type and finite-normal-generation arguments (witness 2),
    relation-module finiteness arguments (witness 1), and finite-generation
    Chabauty or Cantor–Bendixson arguments (witness 3). The target forces:
    - `A(G)` is not of type FP_2;
    - in every finitely presented group, all but countably many maximal kernels
      `M` have `M/⟨⟨F⟩⟩` non-perfect for all finite `F ⊆ M`.
  - **Dead end: Leary's `G_L(∅)` as the ambient.** Every witness ambient is
    perfect (item 4(i) of the reformulation). `G_L(∅)` maps onto `BB_L` with
    `H_1 ≠ 0`, so it is not a witness as it stands. Leary's kernels stay
    perfect, but his quotients are not simple.
  - **Heuristic dead end, not proved: Houghton-type obstruction to FP_2 of
    `A(G)`.** The idea was to restrict to the orbit closure of `δ_0` (the
    one-point compactification of `Z`) and look for a Houghton-type failure.
    It dies at the Cuntz halving `[C] = 2[C]` in `G_V`, which absorbs the
    index defect and makes far commuting pairs conjugate. No obstruction was
    found.
  - **Open direction (where it stops).** Test the second forced property on
    the Coulon–Fournier-Facio quotients `Q = colim G_m` of a perfect
    torsion-free hyperbolic `H`.
    - `Q` of type FP_2 forces `H_2(Q)` to be finitely generated.
    - Each step `G_{m+1} = G_m/⟨⟨r^n⟩⟩` with `H_1(G_m) = 0` gives, by the
      five-term sequence, `H_2(G_m) -> H_2(G_{m+1}) -> K_m/[G_m,K_m] -> 0`,
      with a cyclic cokernel term generated by `r^n`.
    - If infinitely many of these cokernels are infinite and their classes
      survive in the colimit, `H_2(Q)` has infinite rank, so `Q` is not FP_2.

    The argument stops at three unverified points: the order of
    `K_m/[G_m,K_m]`, injectivity of `H_2(G_m) -> H_2(G_{m+1})`, and survival
    in the colimit. Each needs small-cancellation asphericity for proper-power
    relators, imported with a verbatim citation.
- 2026-09-17 (swarm-0917-w5-z-fp2-countable, census-computation): **the
  quotient-of-a-fixed-actor class of the opposite route is dead.** See
  `fixed-actor-orbit-quotients-are-finitely-many` (established).
  - **Invariant.** The lattice of unions of the `m` pair orbits of `P ↷ X`.
    It bounds the invariant equivalence relations by `2^(m-k)`. A perfect normal
    subgroup with faithful orbit partition is the kernel of one of those
    relations.
  - **Where it dies.** Every perfect orbit quotient of one actor, iterated or
    not, is one of at most `2^(m-k)` actors.
  - **What survives.** Kernels of type [A_2] actions give a countable class.
    In any uncountable family of faithful [HA_2] actors, all but countably many
    members have, for every finitely presented cover `H → G`, a point stabilizer
    in `H` that is not finitely generated.
  - This closes the second alternative left open on
    `uncountably-many-fp2-groups-have-faithful-ha2-actions` (2026-09-13).
  - The FFWZ route to uncountability must vary the actor group. For almost all
    members, the action must not be an orbit quotient of any type [A_2] action.
- 2026-09-17 (same lane), **observation, not a node: perfect-set dichotomy.**
  - **Setting.** For finitely generated `H`, let `M_H` be the set of perfect
    normal `N` with `H/N` simple, inside the compact Chabauty space `N(H)`.
  - **`M_H` is `G_δ`.** Properness is clopen. "`g ∈ N` or
    `s ∈ <<N, g>>`" is open for each `g` and each generator `s`, since
    membership in a normal closure is witnessed by finitely many elements of
    `N`. "`n ∉ N` or `n ∈ [N, N]`" is open for each `n`.
  - **Dichotomy.** `M_H` is Polish, so it is countable or contains a Cantor set.
    So this claim is equivalent to: no finitely presented `H` has a
    Cantor-embedded family of perfect maximal kernels. Every point of such a
    family is a Chabauty limit of the others, hence not finitely normally
    generated (`fng-maximal-kernels-are-isolated-proper-normal-points`).
  - **Status.** OPEN.
  - **Prerequisites that can fail independently.**
    - (P1) Every simple group of type FP_2 is finitely presented. This implies
      the claim. It is refuted by one faithful [HA_2] actor that is not finitely
      presented (FFWZ Corollary 4.2).
    - (P2) Every faithful [HA_2] actor is an orbit quotient of a type [A_2]
      action. This bounds the FFWZ route by the countable class `𝒞`, but does
      not imply the claim.
