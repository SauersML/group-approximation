For the current component system D=(F_2^{A_4})^2, let a component's
weight be its number of non-111 right-t triples. Every finite
prefix diagram p eta -> q gamma eta, gamma in diagonal A_4,
commuting with Q^2 preserves prefix weight. All weight-preserving
diagrams of this form constitute an elementary amenable group.

This contains the old source symmetry group and all additional
pure finite-prefix Q,F_A-centralizing recodings. A concrete such
involution splits one non-u component into two by transferring
an x-triple when both affected y-components are all one. Thus
non-u count is not conserved, but the stronger group closure
still excludes every Gottschalk counterexample over these groups.

The conclusion uses faithful finite-weight restrictions and the
known Bieri-Sach orthant theorem. Arbitrary infinite component
permutations and arbitrary Q,F_A centralizers are outside its
finite-diagram hypothesis. No headline conjecture is resolved.
