Invariance for every input forces the effective memory to be a union of left
`H`-orbits, with the local rule invariant under `m -> hm`. So `h` lies in
`M_eff M_eff^-1`. For `h != 1` and distinct symbols `a != b`, the pattern
`1 -> a, h -> b` is a Garden of Eden. The localization theorem, applied over
`<M_eff>`, places `h` or `h^-1`, hence `h`, in the sofic radical. Section 2 of
the artifact, Corollary B.
