For a nonamenable locally finite Cayley graph G of degree d, contract the
finite p_c-clusters and retain each separate intercluster edge. Conditional
on the full critical configuration, t-Bernoulli percolation on this
multigraph is exactly percolation on G at p_c+(1-p_c)t after contraction,
including its infinite-component count. For cell masses m, degrees B and
an original edge expansion h>0, |boundary A|>=h sum_A m and h m<=B<=d m.
The quotient has p_c=0 almost surely. Fixed-parameter count statements
disintegrate; no uncountable exchange or general quenched uniqueness
monotonicity is asserted. Section 1 gives the proof.
