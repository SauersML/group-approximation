**ESTABLISHED COUNTERMODEL TO THE DENSITY-ONLY INFERENCE.**  The coherent
Schrodinger tower `pi_N` has central marked mass one, exact compatible
restriction densities, and polarized-atom trace `p^(-N)`.  Nevertheless no
nonzero matrix corner can carry four contractions whose two inverse defects
and binary-sum defect all tend to zero, because their corner-normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt defects have sum at least one by `(CHD3)`.

The refinement gauge contains `U(p^r)`, and a cyclic shift in this gauge is at
normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt distance `sqrt(2)` from the identity without
changing any finite-window density.  A carrier of normalized trace at least
`alpha` must aggregate at least `alpha p^N` level-`N` characters.  Aggregating
all `p^(2r)` Weyl return channels does not cure the problem: their mutually
orthogonal initial projections sum exactly to the source Reynolds
projection, so the full Parseval sum reconstructs the rectangular escape
rather than a same-reservoir return.

Thus compatible scalar branching densities, even with full marked central
mass, imply neither the Leavitt corner nor a coherent multiplicity gauge.
This does not refute a decoder using a genuinely new mixed/opposite-root
occurrence from the full elementary-group presentation.
