Let `N_a` be the order-256 mixed cell generated by
`X_(a,s),Y_(a+1,r)`, with indices modulo four, and put

```text
G_1=N_0,             G_2=<N_0,N_1>,
G_3=<N_0,N_1,N_2>.                                  (QMT1)
```

Then:

1. `G_1` is finite of order `256`.
2. `G_2` is infinite and residually finite. Its scalar intersection is

   ```text
   G_2 intersect GL_4(F_2)=U_4^-(F_2),                  (QMT2)
   ```

   the lower unitriangular group in the order
   `(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3)`. In particular `P notin G_2`.
   Adjoining the qutrit torus `C` preserves residual finiteness and all
   complete/diagonal atom tables.
3. The third cell is the sharp escape:

   ```text
   G_3=EL_4(L_(F_2)(1,2)).                              (QMT3)
   ```

   Indeed `G_3` contains all four scalar reverse-cycle transvections,
   hence `GL_4(F_2)` and `P`. Scalar conjugation of its raw `s_r,t_s`
   roots then generates every Leavitt elementary root. Thus `G_3` is
   nonsofic and not residually finite; its hyperlinearity remains open.

The two-cell group already contains the explicit Jacobson head

```text
H=[X_(1,1),Y_(2,1)]
  =x_(c_2,c_1)(Q_0),       Q_0=1-s_0t_0=s_1t_1.         (QMT4)
```

In `G_2` this word survives finite quotients. After the third-cell scalar
closure, its conjugates generate the finitary head radical of the
one-branch Jacobson core, so every exact finite-dimensional
representation kills it.

The qutrit trace candidate is therefore completely explicit. The
coordinates `c_1,c_2` are corresponding nontrivial `C` copies, so `H`
commutes with `C`. For every `C`-character projection `p_chi` in the
canonical trace,

```text
tau(p_chi H)=0,
||p_chi(H-1)||_2^2=2/9,
||(1-H)p_chi/2||_2^2=1/18.                              (QMT5)
```

Thus the head defect is present on every phase atom and cannot hide in a
qutrit-free sector. But `H` is diagonal on those atoms, so `(QMT5)`
alone has no Hall deficit. The next load-bearing object is a bounded
third-cell relation which transports this named head cut through only
one of the two qutrit-complement branches; the two-cell relations have
exact finite quotient models and cannot supply that transport.
