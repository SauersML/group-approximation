Turn microscopic leakage into the marked scalar `−I` via an additive
Fredholm/rank index composed with a finite character (`KInd ↦ (−1)^KInd`).
Dead: amplification `φ ⊗ I_k` preserves every normalized-HS defect but
multiplies an additive index by `k`, killing its parity (additive-index-instability). An
integer-index contradiction, or an index normalized by a canonically
recovered positive-density spin multiplicity, remains possible — that is
NOT this approach.
