OPEN.  For some central division algebra `D` of degree 3 over `Q`, every free pair
`A, B in Gamma_D = SL_1(O_D)` has the following property.  Let `X_p` be the 4-regular Schreier
graph of `H = <A, B>` on `P^2(F_p)`, for `p` not in `P_H`.  Then its largest absolute
nontrivial eigenvalue `lambda(X_p) = ||rho_p(A + A^-1 + B + B^-1)||` tends to `2 sqrt 3` as
`p -> infinity` over primes.

**Only the upper bound is at stake.**  A fixed word `w != 1` has separable `f_w`, so for
large `p` it fixes at most 3 points of `P^2(F_p)`.  Hence
`tr rho_p(Z^(2m)) / (p^2 + p) -> tau(Z^(2m))` for every `m`, and `liminf lambda(X_p) >= 2 sqrt 3`.

**Equivalent arithmetic form** (`cubic-division-ihara-traces-count-roots-mod-p`, part 4(a)).
At even lengths `n` with `n / log p -> infinity`,

```text
sum over cyclically reduced w of length n of  (#Fix_(P^2(F_p))(w) - 1)  <=  p^C (sqrt 3 + o(1))^n .
```

For words that are regular mod `p`, the summand is the number of roots of
`x^3 - Trd(w) x^2 + Trd(w^-1) x - 1` mod `p`, minus 1: the Frobenius trace at `p` of
the Artin representation of the cubic field `Q(w)`.  So the claim is an optimal Chebotarev
statement for the cubic fields generated by long words, at a single prime.

**Why it matters.**
- Strong convergence for `Gamma_D` implies this for the same `D`
  (`cubic-division-free-pair-p2-ramanujan-from-sc`).
- A refutation needs one free pair per `D` with a persistent `P^2` outlier, and would refute
  `cubic-division-congruence-representations-converge-strongly`.
- `P^2` carries the smallest nontrivial constituent of `l^2(SL_3(F_p))` (dimension `p^2 + p`).
  It is the most structured sector, and the one where the arithmetic reading is cleanest.

## Attempts

* **Trivial range.**  `|CR_n| (p^2 + p) <= p^C 3^(n/2)` as long as `n <= 2(C - 2) log_3 p` or so.
  All the content sits at `n >> log p`, where cyclically reduced words equidistribute in
  `SL_3(F_p)`.
* **Uniform spectral gap.**  Expansion for Zariski-dense subgroups of perfect groups
  (Salehi Golsefidy--Varju, GAFA 22 (2012); import, not re-read) gives `lambda(X_p) <= 4 - eps_0`.
  That is an exponential saving `(3 - eps_1)^n` in the traces, not the square-root saving.
* **Vertical Chebotarev.**  At fixed `n` the traces are sums of Frobenius traces with mean `0`
  and mean square at least `(1 + o(1)) n 3^n` over primes (part 5 of the counting node).
  - Effective Chebotarev under GRH (Lagarias--Odlyzko; import, not re-read) bounds the error
    for one word by about `sqrt x log x (n + log x)`, since `log disc(Q(w)) = O(n)`.
  - Summed over `3^n` words, the first moment over `p <= x` is controlled only for
    `n <= C' log_3 x`, and the second moment only for `n <= (1/2) log_3 x`.
  - Averages over primes also do not bound the extreme eigenvalue at a single prime.

  Dead for `n / log p -> infinity`, even conditionally.
* **Localised outliers** (sketch, not a proof).  At any sequence of base points the local limit
  of `X_p` is a Schreier graph of `F_2` with amenable stabiliser.  A stabiliser containing two
  bounded words that generate a free group would be reducible mod infinitely many `p`, hence
  not Zariski dense, against `cubic-division-lattice-subgroups-are-zariski-dense`.  The
  quasi-regular representation of an amenable stabiliser is weakly contained in `lambda_(F_2)`.
  So an outlier would have to be delocalised.
* **Numerics.**
  - No outlier beyond Tracy--Widom-size fluctuations for the certified pair up to `p = 967`
    (`research/artifacts/cubic-division-congruence-numerics-2026-09-13.md`), for three weightings.
  - Nonlinear elements and every degenerate principal series at once (`F_p^3 minus 0`):
    lane `ex2-cubic-ihara-bass`, artifact in progress.
