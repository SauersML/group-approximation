Let `pi` be any exact `alpha`-projective unitary representation of
`Sp_4(Z)` for either nontrivial Deligne multiplier.  There is no sequence of
nonzero finite-rank projections `P_n` such that, for every element of a fixed
finite generating set,

```text
||[P_n,pi(s)]||_HS / sqrt(rank P_n) -> 0.                 (DFC1)
```

For if `(DFC1)` held, the states

```text
Phi_n(T) = Tr(P_n T P_n) / rank(P_n)
```

would have a weak-star cluster point `Phi` on `B(H)`.  Cauchy--Schwarz gives,
for every generator `s` and bounded `T`,

```text
|Phi_n(pi(s) T pi(s)^* - T)|
 <= 2 ||T|| ||[P_n,pi(s)]||_HS / sqrt(rank P_n),          (DFC2)
```

so `Phi` is central under the twisted group C-star algebra generated by
`pi`.  Its restriction is therefore an amenable trace.  Pulling that trace
back along the full twisted algebra contradicts
`deligne-twisted-fibres-have-no-amenable-trace`.

Thus compression of the twisted regular representation, a discrete-series
Maslov representation, or any other single exact sector representation
cannot produce the vanishing normalized-HS defects sought in the Deligne
lane.  A countermodel, if one exists, must be an intrinsically varying
matrix-ultraproduct construction rather than a Folner exhaustion.
