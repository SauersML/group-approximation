**OPEN.** There exist:
- a finitely generated, nonzero, unital LEF ring `S`;
- `sigma in Aut(S)`;
- a `sigma`-invariant unit subgroup `C <= S^x` with `diag(c,1,1) in E_3(S)` for every `c in C`,
  for example `C <= [S^x, S^x]`;

such that the ideal `I_C` generated by `{c - 1 : (c,0) in Rad_MF(C semidirect_sigma Z)}` is all of `S`.

**Why it matters.** By (C1) of `unit-radical-saturates-elementary-mapping-torus`, `K = E_3(S)` with the
entrywise automorphism is then a nontrivial, finitely generated, LEF Kazhdan group with
`Rad_MF(K semidirect Z) = K x {0}`. That **refutes (EKL1)** of `exact-kazhdan-radical-kernel-cannot-be-lef`.

The claim moves the whole difficulty of (EKL1) out of property (T) and into a ring-theoretic transfer
problem. The Kazhdan kernel is supplied by EJK, and exactness of the radical is automatic from saturation.
What is left is a non-MF `Z`-extension of a LEF group, the phenomenon of
`mf-not-closed-under-integer-extensions`, realised inside the units of a finitely generated LEF ring.

**Prerequisites, each of which can fail on its own.**
- **(Q1) Nontrivial unit radical.** `Rad_MF(C semidirect_sigma Z) != 1`. Here `C` is a LEF group, as a
  subgroup of `GL_1` of a LEF ring. So (Q1) asks for a non-MF `Z`-extension of a LEF group inside the units
  of a finitely generated LEF ring. It fails in each of these cases:
  - `C` is amenable (`amenable-implies-operator-mf`);
  - `S` is residually finite;
  - `sigma` has a power that is inner in a LEF overring.

  All three are item (C2) of the saturation node.
- **(Q2) Saturation.** The radical generates the unit ideal. This is automatic in either of two cases:
  - `S` is `sigma`-simple;
  - `S = LC(Y,k) semidirect Gamma`, `C` is made of full-group units `u_h` and acts minimally on `Y`.
    The common fixed set of the radical is closed and invariant under `C` and `sigma`. It is not all of
    `Y` once the radical is nontrivial, so it is empty, and item 4 applies.

  (Q2) fails whenever `S` has a nonzero finite quotient ring. Given (Q1), it fails exactly when every
  radical unit is congruent to `1` modulo one proper `sigma`-invariant ideal.

**Forced features of any solution.**
- `S` is infinite, has no nonzero finite quotient ring, and so is not residually finite.
- `C` is non-amenable, and `C semidirect Z` is not MF.
- No power of `sigma` is inner in a LEF overring of `S`. So `[sigma]` has infinite order modulo the inner
  automorphisms coming from every LEF overring.
- `E_3(S)` has no nontrivial finite quotient, since the MF radical of `E_3(S) semidirect Z` lies in its
  finite residual.
- The group rings `k[Gamma]` die at the augmentation. The algebras `LC(Y,F_q) semidirect Z` of minimal
  subshifts die for full-group `C`, since those full groups are amenable (Juschenko--Monod). So either
  `Gamma` must have a non-amenable topological full group, as some minimal `Z^2`-actions do
  (Elek--Monod), or `C` must use units outside the full group.
- The skew Laurent ring `S[t, t^(-1); sigma]` is not a LEF ring. Otherwise `t` would implement `sigma`
  inside a LEF overring. By `exactly-matricial-rings-are-lef-rings`, the same holds for every exactly
  matricial overring. This is the same outcome as consequence 1 of
  `matricial-rings-rigidify-compression-centralizers`, reached here without compression.

**Natural test case.**
- Take `Gamma` finitely generated, acting minimally on a Cantor set `Y`, with `LC(Y,F_q) semidirect Gamma`
  a LEF ring. Take `tau` a homeomorphism normalising `Gamma`, and `sigma = Ad(tau)`.
- Take `C` inside the derived topological full group, which is `tau`-invariant and generally not
  finitely generated.
- (Q2) is then automatic as soon as `C` acts minimally on `Y`, and everything reduces to (Q1): the mapping
  torus `C semidirect_tau Z` is not MF.
- The finite-generation barrier is escaped here. A crossed-product ring that is finitely generated forces
  `Gamma` to be finitely generated, but it does not force `C` to be. The non-finitely-generated LEF kernel
  of `mf-not-closed-under-integer-extensions` is exactly of the kind that can live in `C`.

generated `Gamma`. Unresolved; the claim stays OPEN.**
- *Setup.* `Gamma` is finitely generated LEF, `alpha in Aut(Gamma)`, `W = Gamma semidirect_alpha Z` is not MF,
  and `S = LC(Y,F_q) semidirect Gamma` for a `W`-invariant subshift `Y`. Put `C = Gamma` itself, or its
  derived group.
- *Where it stalls.* (Q1) then needs a finitely generated LEF group with a non-MF mapping torus. This is
  itself an (EKL1)-type input without property (T), and it is not available in the graph.
- *The colouring variant.* Try to realise the language of `Y` by proper colourings of finite models of
  the `alpha`-orbit of a generating set. The union of the `alpha^m`-images of a generating set is
  infinite, so the colouring graph has infinite degree. Finite-palette colourings then do not exist
  uniformly.
- *Dead subcases.* Finite `Y` and group rings die at a finite quotient ring. `Gamma = Z` with a minimal
  subshift dies by Juschenko--Monod amenability of the full group.
