title: "Brin–Sapir for two one-bump generators with different supports: the group is Z^2, Z wreath Z, or contains a copy of F"
---

**Statement.** Let `f, g ∈ PL_o(I)` each have exactly one orbital, `A = supp f` and
`B = supp g`, with `A ≠ B`. Then exactly one of the following holds.

1. `A ∩ B = ∅`, and `⟨f, g⟩ ≅ ℤ²`.
2. The closure of one support lies inside the other, say `B̄ ⊂ A`; the translates
   `f^n(B)`, `n ∈ ℤ`, are pairwise disjoint; and `⟨f, g⟩ ≅ ℤ ≀ ℤ`. (Symmetrically with
   `Ā ⊂ B` and the translates `g^n(A)`.)
3. `⟨f, g⟩` contains a subgroup isomorphic to `F`. This is the case exactly when
   (a) `A` and `B` meet and neither contains the other; or (b) one contains the other
   and they share exactly one endpoint; or (c) `B̄ ⊂ A` and `f^n(B) ∩ B ≠ ∅` for some
   `n ≠ 0` (symmetrically `Ā ⊂ B` and `g^n(A) ∩ A ≠ ∅`).

In cases 1 and 2 the group is elementary amenable. So every subgroup of `PL_o(I)`
generated by two one-bump elements with different supports satisfies the Brin–Sapir
dichotomy (`every-subgroup-of-thompson-f-is-ea-or-contains-f`, stated for `F`; this
claim is about `PL_o(I)`), and the dichotomy is read off from the supports and the
dynamics of the larger bump on the smaller support.

The remaining two-generator one-bump case, equal supports, is not covered: there
`⟨f, g⟩` can be abelian, or `ℤ ≀ ℤ` (for `t` one bump on `(0,1)` and `h` a bump on a
wandering interval, `t` and `th` are one-bump elements with the same support
generating `ℤ ≀ ℤ`), and whether it is always elementary amenable or contains `F` is
open here.
