For a kernel word `n`, the two factor descriptions yield the two displayed
swap relations `(CSR2)`.  They hold with `n` nontrivial in the semidirect
product of the two pre-quotient coordinates by their transposition.  Hence
those relations cannot imply `n=1`, so the proposed finite presentation does
not perform rope compression.
