Given `G`, the hole supplies `G <= S <= Gamma` with `Gamma` finitely
presented, Kazhdan, normally generated by an element of `G`, and
`|S \ Gamma / S| < infinity`. Clause 1 of
`ck-finite-bi-index-hole-is-kazhdan-boone-higman` passes to a maximal
quotient `Gamma/M`, which is finitely presented, simple, Kazhdan and
contains `G`.
