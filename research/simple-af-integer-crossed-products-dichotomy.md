**Theorem.**  Let `D` be a separable simple non-elementary AF algebra, and let
`alpha in Aut(D)` have every nonzero power `alpha^n` properly outer.  Put
`A = D rtimes_alpha Z`, which is simple.  Exactly one of the following holds.

1. `D` admits a nonzero `alpha`-invariant densely finite lower semicontinuous
   trace.  Then `A` is stably finite.
2. `A` is purely infinite.  In this case every nonzero projection of
   `D tensor K` is infinite in `A tensor K`.

In particular no such crossed product contains both a nonzero finite and an
infinite projection.  No real rank hypothesis on `A` is used.

**Key lemma (coinvariant Tarski lemma).**  Let `G` be a noncyclic simple
dimension group and `alpha` an order automorphism.  Let `M_alpha = G^+ / ~`,
where `~` is the congruence generated by `y ~ alpha(y)`.  This congruence is
one-step: `y ~ y'` iff `y = sum c_i` and `y' = sum alpha^(k_i)(c_i)` with
`c_i in G^+`.  If some multiple `N[x]` of a nonzero `[x]` is infinite in
`M_alpha`, then `[x]` is infinite.  Thus `M_alpha` has property (QQ) for
infiniteness.

**Relevance to Problem XXIX.**  Rordam's separable nuclear mixed example is a
crossed product `D rtimes_alpha Z` whose coefficient algebra `D` is a
non-simple inductive limit of type I algebras carrying Euler-class data
(`rordam-mixed-examples-not-real-rank-zero`).  The theorem shows that
zero-dimensional simple coefficients can never produce the mixed phenomenon.
A counterexample of this shape needs a non-simple or non-AF coefficient
algebra, where the Key Lemma's two inputs (strict ordering by states, density
of `K_0` in affine functions) fail.

Novelty is unverified.  Two web searches found Brown's AF-embeddability theorem
and Rordam's pure infiniteness for full corner endomorphisms of simple
real-rank-zero algebras with comparability, but no statement of this
dichotomy.

## Attempts

* Extension to non-simple AF `D` with no nontrivial `alpha`-invariant ideal.
  `M_alpha` is still simple, but states of `K_0(D)` need not be faithful, so
  strict positivity has to be tested orbitwise across the ideal lattice.  The
  one-step congruence and approximate division survive inside each
  `alpha`-orbit of ideals; the missing piece is a uniform margin `eta > 0` for
  the defect `e` across the non-compact family of ideal quotients.
* Extension to simple coefficient algebras with property (SP) whose `V(D)` is
  a noncyclic simple dimension monoid and whose traces are determined by
  `K_0`, for instance simple real-rank-zero algebras of stable rank one with
  unperforated `K_0`.  The same proof applies once "positive `alpha`-invariant
  homomorphisms `K_0(D) -> R` come from invariant traces" is verified.  That
  needs exactness (Haagerup) or a quasitrace version of step (P5).
