The edge subgroup generated by `J,K,b,Z_1,...,Z_k` and the target subgroup
generated by `J,T_1,T_2,Z'_1,...,Z'_k` are isomorphic finite
elementary-abelian groups.  Map their named bases as in `(CAT2)` and use the
corresponding HNN edge.  Applying the fixed spectral-polynomial formula to
this table isomorphism gives `(CAT4)`.

Earlier tags are not members of either edge subgroup.  The off-diagonal
point-stabilizer relations make the bridge commute with them, so multiplying
by their survivor product gives `(CAT5)` without moving the prefix.  A child
context atom is a polynomial in the commuting `Z'_i`, hence reduces every
member of that context.

For `(CAT7)`, replace the `k+2` source involutions one at a time in their
degree-`k+2` joint spectral polynomial.  Every replacement costs one half of
its covariance defect.  Move the bridge through the earlier survivor product
one factor at a time.  This costs half the sum of the earlier tag commutator
defects.  The product clock and fixed two-point orbits give polynomial word
area at level `n`.

For exact completeness, take the countably amplified context-local marked
model.  Choose the target tag table with the same joint character
multiplicities as the source endpoint/context table; countable amplification
absorbs every nonzero finite multiplicity.  Spectral matching supplies the
bridge and earlier tags may act on separate factors.  Distinct original BCS
contexts are never made to commute.
