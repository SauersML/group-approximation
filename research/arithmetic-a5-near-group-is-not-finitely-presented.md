## Statement

Take the bijective-base enumeration nu of P=A_5*A_5 from
`a5-free-product-has-simple-arithmetic-shell-envelope`. Let
G <= B=V_(59,2)(<a>) be the image of its near shift group R_nu under
`a5-arithmetic-germs-embed-in-fp-odometer-host`. Then:

1. G acts faithfully by automorphisms on the 60-regular tree T whose
   boundary is the two-root Cantor set. The action is edge-transitive.
2. Every vertex stabilizer of G is locally virtually abelian.
3. G contains a free abelian group of infinite rank, generated by
   localized odometer pairs with pairwise disjoint supports.
4. Hence R_nu is not finitely presented. By
   `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate`, neither is the shell germ
   group Q, and neither is the one-singularity stabilizer A_1.
   So `decidable-inputs-have-fp-shell-stabilizers` fails for this
   enumeration.
5. R_nu has infinite index in the finitely presented host B_0.

The proof uses only the prefix tables. It applies verbatim to the
same arithmetic enumeration of A*A for any finite group A with
|A|>=4.

## Named invariant

**Abelian rank bound.** Suppose a finitely presented group G acts
on a tree with finitely many edge orbits and locally virtually
abelian vertex stabilizers. Then the torsion-free ranks of the
abelian subgroups of G are bounded.

Proof sketch:
- G lifts to a direct-limit amalgam G_m whose vertex groups are
  finitely generated and virtually abelian.
- In such an amalgam, abelian rank is at most one more than the
  largest vertex-group rank.

Every near group in this class dies at the lifting step as soon as
it contains Z^infinity.

This does not decide `a5-arithmetic-shell-envelope-is-finitely-presented`.
