Literature import. A. Smith, *Algebraic integers with conjugates in a prescribed
distribution*, arXiv:2111.12660v2 (16 Mar 2024), Theorem 1.5, page 4. The PDF was
fetched from arXiv on MSI on 2026-09-12 and the page was read. The statement is
quoted verbatim in the claim and in Section 0 of the artifact.

Smith attributes (2) ⇒ (1) to Serre, via upper semicontinuity of `log|Q|` and
integrality of resultants. (1) ⇒ (2) is the new part: coefficient adjustment
after Robinson and Fekete--Szegő, followed by geometry of numbers.
