**ESTABLISHED (import).**

**Statement.** If `(G, <)` is a group with a total order invariant under left
and right multiplication and `R` is a division ring, then the Malcev-Neumann
series ring `R((G; <))` of formal sums `Σ a_x x` with well-ordered support is a
division ring containing the group ring `R[G]`. Every free group is
bi-orderable.

**Sources.** J. Sánchez, *Free group algebras in division rings with valuation
II*, arXiv:1810.12449, §7 (quoting Malcev and Neumann); A. Clay and D. Rolfsen,
*Ordered groups and topology*, arXiv:1511.05088, Theorem 3.4.
