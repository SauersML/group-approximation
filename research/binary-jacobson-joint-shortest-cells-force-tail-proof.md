Let `bar(Gamma)` be the quotient of `Gamma_joint` by the normal closure of
the tail factor `G_P`.  Constants `(g,g) in G_Q x G_P` become the head
elements `(g,1)`.  In particular the controlled target `c=(b,1)` becomes
the same element as the constant transposition `b`.

The explicit overlap

```text
K={(g,1):g acts on <e_1,e_3> and fixes e_2}               (JTP1)
```

contains the head Weyl transposition `d=w_13`.  Hence `[h,d]=1`.  Put

```text
u=w_12,           b=w_23,           d=u b u,              (JTP2)
rho=u b.
```

The constant Weyl subgroup gives

```text
u^2=b^2=1,             (u b)^3=1,                          (JTP3)
```

while `h^2=1`, the overlap and the affine cell give

```text
h^2=1,                 [h,d]=1,          (h b)^3=1.        (JTP4)
```

Conversely `u=b d b`, using the `S_3` relations in `(JTP3)`.  Therefore the
group generated by `u,b,h` subject to `(JTP3)--(JTP4)` is the Coxeter group

```text
<d,b,h | d^2=b^2=h^2=(d b)^3=(b h)^3=1, [d,h]=1>
  ~= S_4.                                                   (JTP5)
```

Use the faithful permutation model

```text
d=(13),        b=(23),        h=(24),        u=b d b=(12). (JTP6)
```

It remains only to evaluate the controlled cell.  With `rho=u b=(123)` and
`a=h u`, its three conjugates are

```text
rho^2 a rho^(-2)=(14)(31),
rho   a rho^(-1)=(34)(23),
a                  =(24)(12).                             (JTP7)
```

Multiplying in the order displayed in `(JTP7)` gives

```text
(rho^2 a rho^(-2))(rho a rho^(-1))a=(14)(23).             (JTP8)
```

The right side of the folded controlled relation is `c=b=(23)`.  Thus
`(JTP8)` imposes `(14)=1`.  All transpositions of `S_4` are conjugate, so the
normal closure of `(14)` is `S_4`; consequently

```text
u=b=h=1 in bar(Gamma).                                    (JTP9)
```

The head factor `G_Q~=GL_3(F_2)` is simple, and the Weyl transposition
`u!=1` belongs to it.  Its kernel under the quotient map is therefore all of
`G_Q`.  Together with the already killed tail and `(JTP9)`, this proves
`bar(Gamma)=1`.

Finally let `phi:Gamma_joint->H` be any homomorphism with `phi(w)!=1`.  Its
restriction to the simple head factor is injective.  If its restriction to
the tail were trivial, `phi` would factor through `bar(Gamma)=1`, a
contradiction.  The tail restriction is consequently injective as well.
Because the two packet factors commute and have trivial centers, their two
images intersect trivially, proving the embedded product assertion.
