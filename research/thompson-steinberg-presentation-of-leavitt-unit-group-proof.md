The complete proof is Section 2 of the artifact. `Q` is the presented group, `P` the sixty-relator
group, `phi : Q -> L^x` the images. `phi` exists because all eighteen relators hold in `L^x`
(exact computation, PASS).

0. **V part.** Bleak--Quick gives `<a,b,c | (2.4)> = V`, so there is `nu : V -> Q`. Since `V` is
   simple, `phi o nu` is injective. So any identity among `a, b, c` that is true in `L^x` holds in `Q`.
1. **Leaf transvections.** `T(alpha,beta) = ĝ x ĝ^-1`, for any Thompson unit `g` sending the
   cones `00, 01` to `alpha, beta`, is well defined. Two choices differ by an element supported on
   the cone `1`, which is a word in the three (H) elements.
2. **Transport.** `V` acts transitively on tuples of pairwise disjoint cones with nonempty
   complement. So the relators give, for all such cones: disjoint, same-source and same-target
   commutation; `[T(al,be),T(be,ga)] = T(al,ga)`; `T(al0,be0)T(al1,be1) = T(al,be)`; and
   `T(al,be)^2 = 1`.
3. **Derived laws.** `[T(al,be), T(be d, ga)] = T(al d, ga)` and
   `[T(al, be d), T(be,ga)] = T(al, ga d)` follow from splitting and one commutator identity.
4. **beta : P -> Q.** Send `c` and `t` to Thompson words and `y_l` to the letter transvections
   `T(d_1 l_s, d_2 l_t)`. All sixty relators of `P` hold in `Q`: (W) and (K) by Steps 0-2, (L) and
   (D) directly, and (B), (C), (P), (Q) by Steps 2-3. `phi o beta` is the isomorphism `P -> L^x`,
   so `beta` is injective.
5. **beta is onto.** The subgroup `N` generated by all `T(alpha,beta)` lies in `beta(P)`:
   induct on suffix length through Step 3, and refine arbitrary cones by splitting. `N` is normal,
   since conjugation by `a, b, c` acts on refined pieces by transport. `Q/N` is a quotient of `V`
   in which `a` dies, by `(ax)^3 = a^2 = 1` and `x in N`. So `Q/N = 1` by simplicity of `V`.

Hence `beta`, and so `phi`, are isomorphisms.
