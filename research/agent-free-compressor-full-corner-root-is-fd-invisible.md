Let `R_BCL` be the characteristic-`p` coefficient ring, over the fixed
splitting field with `p` coprime to the finite packet orders, with the complete
corner presentation `(BCL0)--(BCL2)`, including the named packet
identification `(x_i-b_i)P_f=0`.  Let `G_BCL` be its rank-five Steinberg
wordization.  For every exact finite-dimensional unitary representation

```text
rho:G_BCL->U(d),                                       (FCI1)
```

one has

```text
rho(x_ij(P_f))=1                 for every i!=j.       (FCI2)
```

Thus the selected payload root is exactly finite-dimensionally invisible.
There is no stationary finite type, product-ring representation, or exotic
exact finite Steinberg representation retaining it after `(BCL0)` is added.

The conclusion is representation-wide and uses no classification of
finite-dimensional representations.  The common root kernel of `(FCI1)` is
a two-sided coefficient ideal, and its coefficient quotient is automatically
a finite ring.  Maschke semisimplicity of the named packet algebra implies
that a surviving primitive block under `P_f` embeds injectively in this
quotient; hence its fixed positive `A_f/B_f` Reynolds gap cannot collapse.
The finite regular coefficient module would then satisfy the three
nonretractive corner rows with nonzero `P_f`, contradicting
`agent-free-compressor-minimal-nonretractive-corner`.

The later audit `agent-free-compressor-bcl-root-is-abstractly-zero` proves a
strictly stronger statement: the complete rows already imply `P_f=0` in
`R_BCL`, so this root is trivial in `G_BCL` itself.  Consequently the proposed
robustification question

```text
exact finite representations kill x(P_f),
but must every HS asymptotic representation kill it?  (FCI3)
```

is automatic by fixed-word telescoping for this coefficient quotient.  The
finite-dimensional argument remains correct but is no longer load-bearing.
In particular, this root cannot be an injected cyclic edge in a literal A4
bridge.  A useful replacement must keep an abstractly nontrivial root and
separately couple its spectrum to the physical forbidden packet.
