The restricted direct product of the subgroups `<t>_x` is invariant under
the site-permutation action of `G`.  Inclusion on the lamp base together with
the identity on `G` therefore gives the injective semidirect-product map
`(AWB1)`.  Nonsoficity and the hyperlinear implication follow from closure of
the respective approximation classes under subgroups.
