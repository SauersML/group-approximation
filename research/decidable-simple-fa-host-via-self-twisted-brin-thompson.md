Let `G` be finitely generated with solvable word problem. Put `S = G`, with
`G` acting on itself by left translation, and let `H = G.V_G = SV_G`.

**Step 1 (fg, simple, contains G).** The translation action is faithful and
has one orbit. Belk--Zaremsky Theorem A (quoted in
`twisted-brin-thompson-groups-have-property-fa-citation`) gives that `H` is
finitely generated. Belk--Zaremsky Theorem 3.4 gives that `H` is simple. The
natural monomorphism `ι_∅ : G -> SV_G` embeds `G`. All three are summarized in
BBMZ Theorem 4.13, quoted in
`self-twisted-bt-group-fp-embedding-iff-solvable-wp-citation`: "If G is a
finitely generated group then G·V_G is a finitely generated simple group, and G
embeds isometrically into G·V_G."

**Step 2 (word problem).** By
`self-twisted-bt-group-fp-embedding-iff-solvable-wp`, (4)⇒(3), `H` has
solvable word problem.

**Step 3 (FA).** The action is faithful, so the abstract group `SV_G` of
Fournier-Facio--Wu--Zaremsky is the Belk--Zaremsky group `H` (FFWZ §2, quoted
in the artifact). `H` is finitely generated by Step 1. So `H` has property FA
by `twisted-brin-thompson-groups-have-property-fa`, part 3.

So `G <= H` with `H` finitely generated, simple, with solvable word problem,
and with property FA. `∎`

**Remarks.**
- `H` also has property NL and FW_∞. So it has no loxodromic action on any
  hyperbolic space and fixes a point in every finite-dimensional CAT(0) cube
  complex. This is stronger than FA.
- The trivial group gives `H = V`, which is consistent with the reported
  FA for `V` in Attempt 3 of the target.
