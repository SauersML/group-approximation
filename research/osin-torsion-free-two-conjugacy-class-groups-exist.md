title: There exist finitely generated torsion-free groups with exactly two conjugacy classes
---

There exist infinite, finitely generated, torsion-free groups `O` with exactly two conjugacy
classes, `{1}` and `O \ {1}`.

Source: D. Osin, *Small cancellations over relatively hyperbolic groups and embedding
theorems*, Ann. of Math. (2) 172 (2010), no. 1, 1–39. Recorded as used in von Puttkamer–Wu,
arXiv:1607.03790v2, page 2, Remark (a): "Osin [27] has constructed finitely generated
torsion-free groups with exactly two conjugacy classes. In particular these groups have BVC."
Barnea–Camina–Ershov–Lewis, arXiv:2210.15746v3, page 3, give the related form: "the main
theorem of a remarkable paper of Osin [Os] implies that for any n ≥ 2 there exist infinite
2-generated groups with exactly n conjugacy classes."
