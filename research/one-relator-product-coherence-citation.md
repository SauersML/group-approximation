Jaikin-Zapirain, Linton, and Sánchez-Peralta, *Group pairs, coherence and
Farrell--Jones Conjecture for K_0*, arXiv:2510.23518v2, Theorem 1.1, states
exactly the target claim.

The displayed theorem includes proper powers. The paper proves the
non-proper-power case and records that the proper-power case is the theorem
of Howie--Short; no proper-power hypothesis is missing from Theorem 1.1.
