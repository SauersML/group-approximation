## Setting

This is the smallest finite test of `gluing-kernels-descend-from-the-free-group-full-shift`: `Λ = Z/2` acting on
two points. Write `Ω = C × {1, 2}` (two copies of Cantor space), so `F(T) = V_(2,2) ≅ V`.
- `N = (V × V) ⋊ ⟨s⟩`. Here `(a, b)` acts by `a` on copy 1 and by `b` on copy 2, and `s` swaps the copies.
- `M = V(Z/2) = C(C, Z/2) ⋊ V`, generated by the diagonal `V` and the local swaps `σ_U` (swap the copies over a
  clopen `U ⊆ C`). Note `σ_C = s`.
- `Γ~ = M *_(V×Z/2) N / ⟨⟨[M_0, N_1]⟩⟩`. By (R1) of `v-times-sft-gluing-kernel-is-relatively-perfect`,
  `[M_U, N_(U')] = 1` in `Γ~` for all disjoint clopen `U, U'`.
- For incomparable words `u, u'`, let `w_(uu') ∈ V` swap `[u]` and `[u']` by prefix. The **braid defect** is
  `x(u, u') = ((1, w_(uu')) σ_[u'])^3`.

  The two factors share one piece, `[u'] × {2}`, and their product is a 3-cycle of pieces in `F(T)`. So
  `x(u, u') ∈ K`.

**Finite shadows.** Let `Q = {c_1, …, c_m}` be a partition of `C` into cones, used in both copies. Let `G_f(Q)` be
presented by:
- `M_f = (Z/2)^m ⋊ S_m`, generated by the swaps `σ_k` over `c_k` and the diagonal permutations of cones;
- `N_f = (S_m × S_m) ⋊ ⟨s⟩`;
- amalgamation over `S_m × ⟨s⟩`, with `s = σ_1 ⋯ σ_m`;
- the relators `[σ_k, (l j)^(i)] = 1` for `k ∉ {l, j}`. Here `(l j)^(i)` is the transposition of cones `l, j` in
  copy `i`.

Every relation of `G_f(Q)` holds in `Γ~`, so there is a homomorphism `G_f(Q) → Γ~` extending `M_f ⊆ M` and
`N_f ⊆ N`. Its composite with `π` is the permutation action on the `2m` pieces.

## Statement

1. **Finite shadows are exact up to parity.**
   - (a) The parity `χ : G_f(Q) → Z/2` is well defined: `σ_k ↦ 1`, and `N_f` and the diagonal `S_m` go to `0`
     (`s ↦ m`). It sends every braid defect to `1`. So no finite shadow is exact.
   - (b) For `m ≥ 2`, `G_f(Q) / ⟨⟨one braid defect⟩⟩ ≅ Sym(2m)`.
   - (c) For `m = 3, 4, 5`, `(π, χ) : G_f(Q) → Sym(2m) × Z/2` is an isomorphism. This is the computer certificate
     in the artifact.

     Hence in these shadows all braid defects equal the one central involution.
2. **Splitting kills the parity.** In `Γ~`, `x(u, u') = 1` for all incomparable `u, u'`.
3. **Every symmetric shadow lifts.** For every cone partition `Q` of `C` with `m ≥ 2` cones, `G_f(Q) → Γ~` factors
   through `Sym(2m)`, and `π` maps its image isomorphically onto the group of piece permutations of `Q × {1, 2}`.
   So every relation among piece permutations of one symmetric partition holds in `Γ~`. This includes all
   braid and commutation relations between local swaps and within-copy transpositions whose supports overlap.
   For the two-point model, this is the case left open in `v-times-full-groups-have-local-transposition-presentations`.

## Proof

**1(a).**
- On `M_f`: the sum of the `(Z/2)^m`-coordinates is `S_m`-invariant, so `χ` is a character.
- On `N_f`: `(a, b) s^e ↦ e·m` is a character.
- On `S_m × ⟨s⟩` the two agree: both are `0` on `S_m` and `m` on `s`.
- Commutators go to `0`.
- For a braid defect, `χ(((l j)^(2) σ_j)^3) = 3(0 + 1) = 1`.

**1(b).** Use the Coxeter presentation of `Sym(2m)` along the path
`(1,c_1) − ⋯ − (1,c_m) − (2,c_m) − ⋯ − (2,c_1)`. Its generators are the adjacent transpositions `a_k` in copy 1,
`b_k` in copy 2, and `σ_m`.

Its relations hold in the quotient:
- the relations among the `a`'s and `b`'s hold in `N_f`;
- `σ_m` commutes with `a_k, b_k` for `k + 1 < m`, by the disjoint-support relators;
- the braids of `σ_m` with `a_(m−1)` and with `b_(m−1)` are conjugates of the one killed braid defect, by a
  diagonal permutation and by `s`.

These generators generate the quotient: `σ_k` is a diagonal conjugate of `σ_m`, and the diagonal `S_m` is
generated by the `a_k b_k`. So the quotient is a quotient of `Sym(2m)` that maps onto `Sym(2m)`.

**1(c).** Coset enumeration gives `[G_f(Q) : H] = C(2m, m)`, where `H` is the image of `N_f`. So
`|G_f(Q)| ≤ C(2m, m) · 2 (m!)^2 = 2 (2m)!`. The map `(π, χ)` is onto, because a braid defect maps to `(1, 1)`.
So it is an isomorphism. By 1(b) the braid-added index is `C(2m, m)/2`; the enumeration confirms this.

**2.** Diagonal conjugation acts on braid defects: `v x(u, u') v^-1 = x(vu, vu')` for `v ∈ V` acting by prefix
on `[u] ∪ [u']`. The first factor is computed in `N`, the second in `M`. Every incomparable pair is diagonally
conjugate to `(0, 1)` or to `(00, 01)`. The two cases are `[u] ∪ [u'] = C` or not; the pair `(1, 0)` is the
`(w_(01), w_(01))`-conjugate of `(0, 1)`.

- *Doubling.* Split `w_(uu') = w_(u0,u'0) w_(u1,u'1)` in `V`, and `σ_[u'] = σ_[u'0] σ_[u'1]` in `M`. By (R1),
  `(1, w_(u0,u'0))` commutes with `σ_[u'1]` and `(1, w_(u1,u'1))` commutes with `σ_[u'0]`. So
  `x(u, u') = x(u0, u'0) · x(u1, u'1)`, with commuting factors.
- *Shadow.* For `(u, u') = (0, 1)`, all four cones `00, 01, 10, 11` are pieces of `Q' = {00, 01, 10, 11}`
  (`m = 4`). For `(00, 01)`, use `Q' = {000, 001, 010, 011, 1}` (`m = 5`).
  - By 1(c), `x(u0, u'0)` and `x(u1, u'1)` are the same central involution of `G_f(Q')`.
  - So in `Γ~` they are equal, and each squares to `1`.
  - Hence `x(u, u') = x(u0, u'0)^2 = 1`.

**3.** By 2, `G_f(Q) → Γ~` kills the braid defect. By 1(b) it factors through `Sym(2m)`. The composite with `π`
is the faithful piece-permutation action, so the image maps isomorphically. `∎`

## What is left for exactness of the two-point model

**Update (same day): done.** `two-point-gluing-presentation-of-v-is-exact` proves `K = 1` for this model. It uses canonical lifts
of all transpositions, including cross lifts between comparable cones; their naturality under the generators of
`M` and `N`; and Bleak–Quick. The two gaps listed below are closed there.

All relations inside symmetric partitions now hold. Exactness (`K = 1`) would follow from a presentation of
`V_(2,2)` whose relations each live in one piece-permutation group, plus split relations. Bleak–Quick's
transposition presentation is of this form (arXiv:1511.02123, Theorem 1.1: order, conjugacy and split
relations). Two points remain unchecked:
- lifts of transpositions between comparable cones in different copies, and their split relations;
- conjugacy relations whose two transpositions preserve no common *symmetric* partition. Non-symmetric partitions
  (different cones in the two copies) need the braid of a cross transposition with neighbours that overlap it in
  `C`.

## Lesson for general BH

In the computed finite shadows (3 to 5 cones), the gluing presentation fails to be exact only through an
abelian **label-sum character**, here the parity of copy swaps. Thompson self-similarity is exactly what kills it. The doubling `x = x_0 x_1`, with
`x_0, x_1` equal central involutions in a finer shadow, turns "exact up to a central `Z/2` in every finite
shadow" into an honest relation.

This is the group-level form of `H_0(G_V) = 0`, applied to one relator rather than to the whole kernel. Relative
perfectness says the swindle kills every central quotient of `K`. Here a finite computation first *makes* the
obstruction central, and then the swindle kills it.

The same shape is expected for any finite group `Q` acting on a finite set. There, the shadow `Q ≀ S_m` against
`S_m ≀ Q` carries the label-sum character to `Q^(ab)`, and splitting kills it. So the finite models of
P2′b-exactness are a finite, checkable programme. For the two-point model, the remaining difficulty is
consistency across partitions, not local relations.
