Let `B_sub=K(M_sub)` be the class-two two-group associated to `(SFP1)`.  It
has central involutions `J,z_e,z_f`, two commuting `p` generators and two
commuting `q` generators, hence order `2^(3+4)=128`.  Let `A_sub<=B_sub` be
generated by `J,z_e,z_f,p_1,q_1`.  In the sector

```text
J=-1, z_e=(-1)^e, z_f=(-1)^f,
```

the `A_sub` algebra is `M_2(C)` for every assignment.  Its simple `S_(e,f)`
has dimension two.  Every `B_sub` simple restricts as

```text
T_(e,f)|_(A_sub) = S_(e,f)             if (e,f)!=(0,1),
T_(0,1)|_(A_sub) = S_(0,1) directSum S_(0,1).          (SHP1)
```

For the baseline generating set `S_sub={p_1,q_1,z_e,z_f}`, the exact positive
spectral gap of the intertwiner Laplacian over all pairs of baseline simples is

```text
kappa(A_sub,S_sub)=4.                                  (SHP2)
```

Consequently, if two equal-`d` exact baseline representations have weighted
multiplicity mismatch

```text
L=2 sum_(e,f) |m_(e,f)-n_(e,f)|,
```

then every unitary transport `U` satisfies

```text
sum_(s in S_sub)||rho(s)U-U sigma(s)||_(2,d)^2 >= 2L/d. (SHP3)
```

The groupification problem is now sharply isolated: make the forbidden mass
in `(SHP1)` become an extensive mismatch between the two endpoint baseline
representations.  Once that happens, `(SHP3)` already pays for it.
