title: A K1-trivial unitary that almost commutes with a properly infinite full projection with properly infinite full complement is null (BRR Lemma 2.4(ii))
---

Let `A` be a unital C\*-algebra and `u ∈ U(A)` with `[u] = 0` in `K_1(A)`. Suppose some projection
`p ∈ A` satisfies `||up − pu|| < 1`, and `p` and `1 − p` are both properly infinite and full. Then
`u ∈ U_0(A)`.

This is the commutator criterion used throughout the STW Problem LXI cluster
(`stw99-problem-lxi-properly-infinite-k1-injective`), for example in
`trivial-unit-class-lxi-iff-universal-halving-homotopy` and in the Attempts of
`o2-free-circle-halving-homotopy`. Until now it was cited there without an import node.
