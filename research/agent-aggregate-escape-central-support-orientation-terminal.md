**ESTABLISHED TERMINAL REDUCTION.**  Keep the shared BCS/role algebra `A`
and its role-packed forbidden projection `B_f`.  Adjoin a central involution
word `K` commuting with `A`, and let

```text
T=(1-K)/2.                                               (ACO1)
```

Suppose one finite ordinary-group extension has both of the following
properties.

1. It has an exact finite-tracial representation with `K=-1`, the perfect
   BCS tuple, and hence `B_f=0`.
2. Every sufficiently accurate finite-dimensional presentation tuple, after
   fixed-packet correction, satisfies

   ```text
   ||T B_f||_2^2<=C_0 sqrt(E).                          (ACO2)
   ```

Then the presented group is nonhyperlinear.  More quantitatively, the robust
role-packed gap gives

```text
tau(T)<=(m/beta)(C_0+C_f)sqrt(E).                       (ACO3)
```

Thus `K` converges to the identity in every asymptotically exact matrix
model, while clause 1 proves that `K` is nontrivial.

Because the role summands are orthogonal and `T` commutes with them, `(ACO2)`
is equivalently the finite menu estimate

```text
sum_i ||T R_iP_i||_2^2<=C_0 sqrt(E).                   (ACO4)
```

No nonlinear aggregate reflection, successor carrier, transport
orthogonality, first-hit accounting, or iteration remains after `(ACO4)`.
This is the sharp one-row alternative exposed by full-support saturation.

## Exact remaining gate and firewall

The theorem does not construct `(ACO4)`.  A contextwise finite packet or
finite Bass--Serre attachment cannot prove it: marked induction retains
every forbidden selector character, and the canonical group trace gives
positive mass to `TB_f` whenever the finite seed/context packets and `K`
embed.  Therefore `(ACO4)` must be a genuinely matrix-only, cross-context
two-cell (or an equivalent nonamenable/non-Bass--Serre incidence).  Proving
it is already sufficient for the full nonhyperlinear conclusion, not merely
a local packet lemma.
