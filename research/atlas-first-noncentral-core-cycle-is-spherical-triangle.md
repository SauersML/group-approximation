the first new core edge into `H~=A4`, and retain the first packet vertex
`F'~=S3 x S3`.  Their pairwise marked intersections are cyclic of orders
`2,3,2`.  The Gersten--Stallings angles are `pi/2,pi/3,pi/3`, so their sum is
`7pi/6>pi`.

of finite groups.  The standard nonpositive-curvature developability theorem
cannot prove vertex injection.  The separate exact computation
`atlas-forward-core-triangle-has-faithful-q96-completion` resolves the
edge-generated triangle positively with a faithful order-96 completion;
the angle calculation explains why that direct finite computation was needed.
