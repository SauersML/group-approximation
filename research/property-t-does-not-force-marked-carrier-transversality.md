Let `K` be any infinite residually finite property-`(T)` group and let

```text
H=K times C_2,
G=Z semidirect H = D_infinity times K,
```

where `K` acts trivially on `Z=<w>` and the nontrivial element of `C_2`
sends `w` to `w^(-1)`. Then `G` is residually finite and hyperlinear.

For every inversion-invariant Borel set `E subset T`, the projection

```text
p=1_E(lambda(w))
```

has arbitrary prescribed trace `0<beta<1` but lies in
`L(H)' cap L(G)`. Hence every `H`-adjoint expander energy of `p` is exactly
zero. Property `(T)`, nonamenability, positive carrier density, full central
support, and tensor amplification do not imply no-capture. Conjugacy-orbit
geometry or a direct Reynolds-overlap inequality is indispensable.
