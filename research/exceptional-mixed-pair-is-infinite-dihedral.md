argument proves that `h=[g,r]=(gr)^2` has infinite order.  Hence `gr` has
infinite order.  The group generated by two involutions whose product has
infinite order is

```text
<g,r | g^2=r^2=1> = C2 * C2 = D_infinity.
```

In particular `g h g=h^(-1)=r h r`: the detected direction is bilateral,
virtually cyclic, amenable, sofic, and hyperlinear.
