Dead: the attempt builds hyperfinite subrelations of the Burnside Bernoulli action with cost tending to one and average torsion connector defects tending to zero, then finishes with the error-tolerant repair bound. In the stated large odd range no hyperfinite subrelation admits more than n sufficiently reliable connectors, so distinct connector lists have average defect at least epsilon_n(1-n/k) (fpbs-cap-hyperfinite-barrier). The constant is uniform over the action, the subrelation and the connector list. Only the hyperfinite version dies; the general small-average-defect target stays open and is not a lower bound above one on the cost.
