**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`compact-special-cube-complex-sln-z-citation`).

Let `X` be a compact nonpositively curved cube complex whose hyperplanes embed, are 2-sided, do
not directly self-osculate and do not inter-osculate (a compact special cube complex). Then
`pi_1 X` embeds in the fundamental group of the standard cube complex of a finitely generated
right-angled Artin group, and hence `pi_1 X` is isomorphic to a subgroup of `SL_n(Z)` for some `n`.

Consumers: a group with a finite-index subgroup of this kind has a finite-index normal subgroup
inside `SL_n(Z)`.
