**ESTABLISHED (written proof; not Lean-verified).**  Let `G` be countable,
let `L<=G` be finitely generated, fix a finite symmetric generating set
`S` of `L` with `e in S`, and put

```text
h_S = (1/|S|) sum_(s in S) u_s  in C[L],        eps(h_S)=1,
```

where `eps` is the trivial character.  For a unital C-star algebra `A` and
a homomorphism `rho:G->U(A)` put `y=rho(h_S)`, a self-adjoint contraction.
Say that `rho` has a **model spectral gap on `L`**, `(MSG)`, if

```text
1 is not an accumulation point of sp(y).                          (MSG)
```

1. **Substitute projection.**  In every faithful representation of `A`,
   `ker(1-y)=Fix rho(L)`.  Under `(MSG)` the projection
   `P=chi_{1}(y)` lies in `C*(rho(L))`, it is the orthogonal projection
   onto `Fix rho(L)`, `rho(l)P=P` for `l in L`, and for every `c>0` there
   is `a in C[L]` with `||rho(a)-P||<c` and `|eps(a)-1|<c`.
2. **(T) gives (MSG) uniformly.**  If `L` has property `(T)` with Kazhdan
   pair `(S,kappa)`, every `rho` into every unital C-star algebra satisfies
   `sp(y) subset [-1,1-kappa^2/(2|S|)] union {1}`, and `P` is the image
   of the Kazhdan projection `e_L`.
3. **Transport without (T).**  Theorem `thm:transport` of
   `non_mf_groups_exist.tex` holds for a given operator-norm asymptotic
   representation `(V_n)` of `G`, a subgroup `L<=G` and `u` with
   `uLu^-1<=L`, with property `(T)` of `L` replaced by `(MSG)` for the
   adjoint corona homomorphism `sigma~=[Ad V_n]` into `B`.  Theorem
   `thm:normal-kazhdan` holds for a finitely generated `K` normal in `G` with property `(T)`
   replaced by `(MSG)` for `K` along every corona homomorphism of `G`.
   In both proofs the substitute `P` of item 1 is used in place of the
   image of `e_L` or `e_K`, and nothing else changes.
4. **(MSG) is not automatic.**  For `G=L=F_2=<a,b>`,
   `S={e,a^(+-1),b^(+-1)}`, there is a `delta_e`-profile operator-norm
   model, exact on each coordinate, whose corona homomorphism `Theta` and
   whose adjoint homomorphism `sigma~` both have
   `sp(rho(h_S))=[-3/5,1]`, so `(MSG)` fails for both.  The obstruction
   sits on a block of vanishing normalized rank.
5. **Adjoint splitting of `delta_e` models.**  Let `L` be nonamenable and
   let `(V_n)` be an operator-norm model of `G` with
   `tr V_n(g)->delta_e(g)`.  Let `A_L=C*(sigma~(L)) subset B` and let
   `tau` be a limit trace along a free ultrafilter.  Then `A_L` has the
   character `omega` given by the exactly invariant unit vectors
   `I/sqrt(d_n)`, the GNS representation of `tau|A_L` is `lambda_L`, and
   `a -> (omega(a), pi_tau(a))` maps `A_L` onto `C + C*_r(L)`.  There is a
   positive contraction `e in A_L` with `omega(e)=1` and `tau(e)=0`.  If
   this surjection is injective, then `(MSG)` holds for `sigma~` on `L`,
   with `sp(y) subset [-rho_S,rho_S] union {1}`, where
   `rho_S=||lambda_L(h_S)||<1`.

## Decomposition and impact

For a Property-`(T)`-free reproof of the manuscript endpoints, the Kazhdan
input of the transport and normal-subgroup theorems is exactly one
prerequisite per subgroup: `(MSG)` in the relevant corona models.  Nothing
else in those proofs uses `(T)`.  The prerequisite can fail.  Item 4 shows
it is not a consequence of nonamenability, of the `delta_e` trace profile,
of exactness on coordinates, or of stable finiteness of the corona.  Any
proof of `(MSG)` for a non-Kazhdan `L` must use that `rho` extends to `G`
and is a corona model of `G`.  In item 4 `rho` factors through an amenable
quotient on the bad block, and inside a perfect simple `G` such as
`EL_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))` no model can do this on a nonzero corner.

Item 5 says that, for `delta_e`-profile models, every T-free proof already
has the shape of the Kazhdan projection: `omega=1` and `tau=0` on a
positive element of `A_L`.  The only missing content is projection-ness,
i.e. isolation of `1`.  Every continuous function of `y` that vanishes on
`[-1,rho_S]` has trace zero, so the part of `sp(y)` accumulating at `1`
is invisible to `tau` and detected only in operator norm.  In the language of
`binary-leavitt-atomic-morita-return`, the level-shifted orders
`U^*P_(k')U<=P_k` of window averages never stabilize.  An `(MSG)`
eigenprojection, when it exists, is a single projection and gives the
same-class inequality `U^*PU<=P` directly.
