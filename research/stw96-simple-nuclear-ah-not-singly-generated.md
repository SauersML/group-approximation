title: A unital simple separable nuclear nowhere-scattered C*-algebra need not be singly generated

There exists a unital simple separable nuclear nowhere-scattered C*-algebra
`A` such that

```text
C*(1,g) != A    for every g in A.
```

The example is the Villadsen second-type AH algebra constructed by Elliott,
Li, and Niu in arXiv:2608.13707.  Their Theorems 1.1 and 3.1 prove the stronger
statement that one fixed finite subset of `A` is contained in no singly
generated C*-subalgebra.

Consequently this single example refutes both parts of STW Problem XCVI and
also Problem XCVII.  Nuclearity is not an extra hypothesis to check by
classification: every AH algebra is an inductive limit of homogeneous
algebras, hence nuclear.
