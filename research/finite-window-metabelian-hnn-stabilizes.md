Let `M` be abelian, generated by finitely many `u_i` as a
`ℤ[t,t⁻¹]`-module, with automorphism `σ=t`. Define `B_N,A_N,C_N,P_N`
exactly as in artifact Section 3. If `J=M⋊_σℤ` is finitely presented,
then some natural map `P_N→J` is an isomorphism, and at that window
`A_N=B_N` or `C_N=B_N`. Consequently the abelian kernel is an increasing
union of conjugates of a finitely generated abelian group and has finite
rational rank.

## Attempts

The submitted finite-window proof uses the fixed generators `(u_i,t)`.
All finite relations appear in one window; finite presentation makes one
window exact. If both edge groups remain proper, the explicit Britton
word `[tat⁻¹,b]` is nontrivial despite mapping to a commutator inside
the abelian kernel. The direct-limit relation argument, finite-relator
stabilization and Britton hypotheses require review. The transition maps
are surjections, not embeddings. This is the specialized argument needed
here, not an asserted replacement for the general Bieri–Strebel theorem.
