Writing `r=1-e_(gKg^-1)`, the range compression is `qrq`.
A compression of one projection by another is a projection
exactly when they commute, as follows from the positive
identity `qrq-(qrq)^2=qr(1-q)rq`. Thus partial isometry
is equivalent to commutation of the two subgroup averages.

Those products have constant positive coefficients on `KL`
and `LK`, respectively. They agree exactly when these sets
agree, in which case their product is the average of the
finite subgroup `KL`. Taking identity coefficients gives
the trace formula. A proper intersection has index at least
two, giving the strict loss outside the normalizer and the
full-unitary characterization.
