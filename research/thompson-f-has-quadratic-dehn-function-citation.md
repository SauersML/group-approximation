Citation import, not a reproof.

V. S. Guba, "The Dehn function of Richard Thompson's group F is quadratic",
Invent. Math. 163 (2006), 313–342; arXiv:math/0211395v1 (26 Nov 2002). Read
from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-13.

- p. 3, verbatim: "Theorem 1 The Dehn function Φ(n) of the Richard Thompson
  group F is quadratic, that is, Φ(n) ∼ n²."
- p. 2 defines `p ≼ q` iff there is a positive integer `C` with
  `p(n) ≤ C q(Cn) + Cn` for all `n`, and `∼` as the induced equivalence.
- p. 1, presentation (2): `⟨x_0, x_1 | x_2^(x_1) = x_3, x_3^(x_1) = x_4⟩` with
  `x_(k+1) = x_1^(x_0^k)`.
- p. 3, verbatim: "Corollary 1 The isodiametric function of the Richard
  Thompson group F is linear."

Lorrain, arXiv:2608.03813v2, cites the result in its abstract as
"[Guba, 2006, Theorem 1]".

This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what the claim records.
