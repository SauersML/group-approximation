Established by citation.  A. Dogon, *Flexible Hilbert-Schmidt stability
versus hyperlinearity for property (T) groups*, Math. Z. (2023),
arXiv:2211.10492v3, Corollary 1.9, read from the PDF on 2026-08-21:

> Let `G` be a connected semisimple Lie group with infinite cyclic
> fundamental group and with property (T), and let `Gamma <= G` be a
> lattice.  Let `G~ ->> G` be the universal cover, `Gamma~ <= G~` be the
> pullback of `Gamma` under the covering map.  If `Gamma` is weakly
> ucp-stable, then `Gamma~` is not hyperlinear.

The following paragraph instantiates `G = Sp_(2g)(R)`, `g >= 2`,
`Gamma = Sp_(2g)(Z)`, `pi_1(Sp_(2g)(R)) = Z`, and records that `Gamma~`
"has been shown to be non-residually finite by Deligne" (P. Deligne,
*Extensions centrales non residuellement finies de groupes
arithmetiques*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 287 (1978), A203--A208, reference
[16] there).  Property (T) of `Sp_(2g)(R)` and its lattices for `g >= 2`
is classical (Kazhdan).  Used at statement level; the proof (Thom's
twisted-algebra criterion, projective representations with almost-trivial
cocycles, and the Nicoara--Popa--Sasyk property (T) theorem for projective
almost-invariant vectors) is Dogon's Sections 3 and 7.
