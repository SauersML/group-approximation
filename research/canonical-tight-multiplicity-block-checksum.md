OPEN.  Construct a recursively presented, subsequently Higman-compiled
group gadget with one positive-canonical-trace finite-group `pi` carrier
`P`, one fixed marked word `W`, and authenticated orthogonal multiplicity
selectors `(P_k)_(k>=1)` satisfying all of the following on every canonical
microstate sequence.

```text
 P_k<=P,                 P_kP_l=0 (k!=l),
 lim_N limsup_m tr(P-sum_(k<=N)P_k)=0,                  (CTM1)
```

and, for each fixed `k`, the `M_k` Amitsur--Levitzki stopping instruction
gives a finite-window estimate forcing

```text
 ||(W-I)P_k||_2 ->0.                                    (CTM2)
```

The constants and proof areas in `(CTM2)` may grow arbitrarily with `k` and
runtime.  The selectors may be decoded only after exactifying the fixed
finite CPU, but they must refer to the same carrier and mark and must obey
the canonical tightness in `(CTM1)`.

By `canonical-tight-orthogonal-checksum-collapses-one-mark`, `(CTM1)--(CTM2)`
are sufficient for the nonhalting canonical-profile collapse needed by
reverse Kleene.  They are also precisely what the raw multiplicity oracle
does not provide.  A direct sum of larger and larger multiplicity blocks
puts all mass at `k_m->infinity`; every fixed selector then has trace zero
and the tail in `(CTM1)` stays macroscopic.

Thus `depthwise-canonical-kleene-needs-no-uniform-runtime-gap` removes
uniformity in `k`, proof area, and simulated runtime, but it does **not**
remove:

1. dimension-independent validity of every one fixed-depth estimate across
   all matrix sizes;
2. one common positive-density carrier and one common marked word;
3. canonical tightness of the detected block-size distribution.

The third item is the concrete orthogonal-checksum target.  Without it the
same-model construction still needs a positive-rate return/PCP actuator or
the supercritical Clifford contradiction.

## Attempts

- **Static block-size selectors fail tightness.**  On a direct sum supported
  on multiplicities `k_m->infinity`, every fixed finite selector window has
  vanishing trace while the carrier stays macroscopic.  Orthogonality gives
  only the mass-weighted estimate of
  `orthogonal-checksum-controls-only-mass-weighted-syndromes`.
- **Depthwise Kleene does not repair the trace escape.**  It permits the
  estimate constant for selector `k` to grow arbitrarily, but the order of
  limits still fixes `k` before the matrix index.  A type that moves to
  infinity is therefore invisible at every fixed depth.
- **Live possibility.**  Couple multiplicity types to canonical trace data
  whose prescribed masses form a summable exhaustion of `P`, or construct a
  matrix-only proper transition which moves escaping type mass back into a
  fixed finite selector window.  Either construction must fail in the
  infinite witness representation so that it does not collapse the exact
  nonhalting mark.
