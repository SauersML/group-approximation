Let `M` be a Minsky machine with `K` glasses, deterministic or not.  Let `G(M)` be the
Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir group of arXiv:1204.6506v5 §4.1 (generators `L_0, L_1, L_2`;
relations G1--G8).  Put

```text
G_0(M) = G(M) / << x_(q_0 w) : w in U_0 >>,
```

where `U_0` is the set of divisors of `A_0 A_1 ... A_K`.  These are `2^(K+1)` extra relations,
computable from `M`.  Then:

1. `G_0(M)` lies in `A_p^2 A cap ZN_(K+1) A`.  The KMS model `Ḡ = T_1 x| <automorphisms>`
   (v5 pp. 22--24, with `z_(i,0) = 1`) is a quotient of `G_0(M)` through the map of Lemma 4.10.
2. For `al_i, be_i in {0,1}`,
   `x_(q_i A_0) * a^(m) * A^(al) = x_(q_j A_0) * a^(n) * A^(be)` holds in `G_0(M)` iff
   `q_i a^m A^al = q_j a^n A^be` holds in `S(M)` (with `q_0 = 0`), **zero elements included**.
   Every such word for a zero element is `1`.
3. The image `T_0` of `T` is the direct product of the cyclic groups generated by the `x_(i,w)`,
   `w in W cup W_0` nonzero.  So `T_0 -> T_1` is an isomorphism.

**Why it is needed.**
- Under G1--G8 alone, the words of distinct stop configurations are distinct.  The automorphism
  formulas over `S(M)` without the stop relation satisfy G1--G8 and separate them.
- The published version, Bull. Math. Sci. 7 (2017) 309--352, keeps `q_0` nonzero in `S`
  (§3.1 p. 325, Remark 3.10 p. 328).
- An argument that identifies a word with "the stop word" uniformly over all finite quotients
  needs clause 2.  Examples:
  - `pumped-divergent-minsky-word-lies-in-profinite-kernel`;
  - the basis transfer in `pumped-halting-clocked-minsky-group-is-residually-finite`.
