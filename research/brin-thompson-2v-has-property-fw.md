**Statement.** For every action of `2V` on a set `X` and every commensurated `A ⊆ X`
(`|gA Δ A| < ∞` for all `g`), the function `ℓ(g) = |gA Δ A|` is bounded on `2V`.
Equivalently, every action of `2V` on a CAT(0) cube complex has a fixed point.

**Status.** OPEN in the literature. Fournier-Facio–Wu–Zaremsky (arXiv:2603.24687),
Remark 6.7, verbatim: "To the best of our knowledge it is open whether 2V (more
generally SV_G for |S|≥2) can act with unbounded orbits on an infinite dimensional
CAT(0) cube complex. Relatedly, as far as we know, the Haagerup and Kazhdan
properties are open for 2V." Their Corollary 6.6 gives `FW_∞` (fixed points on every
finite-dimensional CAT(0) cube complex), so any unbounded action needs infinite
dimension.

**Why it matters here.** A yes answer makes every wall-space, cubical or tree route to
`brin-thompson-groups-nv-are-a-t-menable` die at the start, not only the routes that
need properness modulo `T_n`. It would also give a finitely presented simple group with
`FW` and unknown Haagerup status, so any Haagerup proof would need non-cubical cnd
functions. A no answer (an infinite Schreier graph of `2V` with at least two ends)
gives an unbounded cubical action of `2V`. In it, every element of `K_2 ∪ V^2`, and every
conjugate of one, is elliptic (`brin-thompson-nv-fibre-elements-are-cubically-elliptic`),
so the unboundedness must come from elements outside that set or from the group as a
whole.

## Attempts

- 2026-09-20 (swarm-0917-w21-w21-nv-pull), two reductions killed.
  - *Helly-type reductions die.* One tempting plan: cover `C²` by proper clopens `U_i`,
    show each `⟨V_{U_i}, V_{U_j}⟩` has bounded orbits, and conclude by a Helly
    property. No finite Helly number exists for commensurating actions. See
    `commensurating-actions-have-no-finite-helly-number`: for every `k` there is a
    group generated by `k+1` subgroups, every `k` of which generate a finite group,
    whose Coxeter wall action is proper. So bounded orbits of all `k`-fold joins never
    imply bounded orbits of the whole group. Such a proof must use more structure
    than the join pattern of the cover. This is consistent with FFWZ Corollary 6.6,
    where finite dimension is what restores a Helly number.
  - *Elementwise ellipticity is not enough.* Ellipticity of every element does not give
    bounded orbits. The Grigorchuk group is finitely generated and torsion, and its
    action on the orbit of a boundary ray has a two-ended Schreier graph. So
    extending `brin-thompson-nv-fibre-elements-are-cubically-elliptic` to all
    elements, for example to the baker map `β(x, ey) = (ex, y)`, would not settle this
    claim. (The Grigorchuk remark is recalled from memory, not cited verbatim, and is
    not load-bearing.)
  - *Open test.* Is the baker map `β` elliptic in every commensurating action of `2V`?
    Its centralizer appears to be virtually `⟨β⟩`, so
    `commensurating-loxodromics-split-their-centralizers` gives no obstruction. `β` is
    undistorted, since its exponent cocycle is `(−1, +1)`. A loxodromic `β` would
    refute this claim.
