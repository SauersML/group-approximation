# Multiply parallel and triangular swap matrices and identify the return word

Orthogonality of the packet projections kills every path except the unique
composable one. Multiplying `(RSC1)` gives `(RSC2)`; multiplying the three
terms selected by `p_0` in `(RSC4)` gives `(RSC5)`. The surviving products
are exactly the ordinary graph-of-groups return words, with multiplicity
matrices equal to the corresponding gauge loops.
