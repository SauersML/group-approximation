`A` is separable simple non-type-I of real rank zero, so the required
claim gives almost divisibility of `Cu(A)`.  With strict comparison,
`A` is pure, and for simple separable stably finite algebras pureness
is equivalent to Cuntz semigroup regularity
(`Cu(A) ≅ Cu(A ⊗ Z)`; STW Proposition `prop:cu-regular`,
(5) ⟹ (2)).  Winter's theorem (Invent. Math. 2012; nonunital by
Tikuisis) upgrades Cuntz semigroup regularity to `Z`-stability for
unital simple separable stably finite algebras with locally finite
nuclear dimension — the one place the dimension hypothesis enters.
