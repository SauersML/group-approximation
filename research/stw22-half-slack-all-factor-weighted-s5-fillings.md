positive contraction `a` satisfying `m tau(supp(a))<=1/2`, the weighted
fibre has the universal five-sphere modulus

```text
rho_(AF,5)(epsilon)=min(epsilon,1)^3/216000000.
```

Every such fibre is five-connected and the family is uniformly
equi-`LC^5` after combining lower degrees.
