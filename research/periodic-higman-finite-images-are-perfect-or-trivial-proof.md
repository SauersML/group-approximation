## Direct proof

`t F t^-1 <= F` makes `Ad(rho t)` an endomorphism of the finite group
`H = rho(F)`; it is injective because `rho t` is invertible in `Q`, and an
injective endomorphism of a finite group is an automorphism.  Applying `rho` to
the defining relations gives `(3)`.

For `(4)`: each generator `h_i` equals `alpha^-1` of a commutator, and `alpha`
is an automorphism, so every `h_i` lies in `[H,H]`.  A group generated by
elements of its own commutator subgroup equals it.

For `(6)`: with `alpha = 1`, relation `(3)` becomes `h_i = [h_i, h_(i-1)]`.
Expanding `[h_i, h_(i-1)] = h_i^-1 h_(i-1)^-1 h_i h_(i-1)` and cancelling gives
`h_(i-1)^-1 h_i h_(i-1) = h_i^2`, the cyclic Higman relations on four
generators.  Higman's group is infinite with no nontrivial finite quotient, so
its image in the finite group `Q` is trivial: every `h_i = 1`, hence `H = 1`.

## Literature used

Higman, *A finitely generated infinite simple group* — the four-generator group
with no nontrivial finite quotient.
