title: Every infinite projection in a simple C-star algebra is properly infinite
---

Let `A` be simple and let `p` be an infinite projection in `A`.  Then `p` is
properly infinite: it contains two orthogonal subprojections, each
Murray--von Neumann equivalent to `p`.

Consequently, under the hypothesis of Problem LXXIV, every nonzero projection
is properly infinite.  No nuclearity, separability, or real-rank hypothesis is
used.
