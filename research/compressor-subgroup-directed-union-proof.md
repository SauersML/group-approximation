`t Gamma t^-1 <= Gamma` gives `t^-k Gamma t^k <= t^-(k+1) Gamma t^(k+1)`, so
`Lambda` is a directed union.  `Gamma <= Lambda` normalizes it,
`t Lambda t^-1 = t Gamma t^-1 union Lambda = Lambda` and `t^-1 Lambda t = Lambda`.
So `Lambda` is normal in `H`, and `H/Lambda` is generated by the image of `t`.

Hyperlinearity and soficity are local and pass to subgroups, so `Lambda` has
either property exactly when `Gamma` does.

* **Finite quotient.**  If `H/Lambda` is finite, induce the approximations of
  `Lambda`.
* **Infinite quotient.**  Otherwise `H = Lambda x| <t>`.  With
  `alpha = Ad t`, the block-diagonal operator
  `pi(x) = diag_(0<=j<n) sigma(alpha^-j(x))` and the cyclic block shift
  `pi(t)` satisfy the conjugation relation on all but one of `n` blocks.
  Fixed words therefore have normalized Hilbert--Schmidt (resp. Hamming)
  defect tending to zero, and the regular character is preserved.

If `Lambda` were finitely generated it would equal one term
`t^-k Gamma t^k`, forcing `t Gamma t^-1 = Gamma`.  A Kazhdan `H` would have
finite abelianization, so `H/Lambda` would be finite and `Lambda` of finite
index, hence Kazhdan and finitely generated.

Full details: `research/artifacts/fournier-facio-one-compressor-reduction-2026-09-11.md`,
Section 1.  The cyclic cases are elementary instances of the sofic-by-amenable
and hyperlinear-by-amenable permanence theorems (Elek--Szabo;
Arzhantseva--Berlai--Finn-Sell--Glebsky, arXiv:1802.04688).
