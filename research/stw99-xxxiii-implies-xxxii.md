Part (1): `X` has finite covering dimension, so the action has the
small boundary property (STW footnote; boundaries of a basis can be
chosen inside skeleta of arbitrarily fine finite-dimensional covers).
Dynamical comparison (from the premise; the action of the amenable
group is amenable) plus SBP is almost finiteness for free actions
(Kerr--Szabo, CMP Thm A, for countably infinite amenable `G`), and
Kerr (JEMS Thm 12.4) turns free minimal almost finite into `Z`-stable
crossed products.

Part (2): an invariant probability measure for a topologically
amenable action forces amenability of the acting group, so a free
minimal amenable action of non-amenable `G` has none; dynamical
comparison then degenerates to dynamical pure infiniteness (`X ≾ V`
for every nonempty open `V`), Ma (TAMS Thm 1.1) gives a simple purely
infinite crossed product, exactness plus amenability of the action
gives nuclearity, and Kirchberg's `O_∞`-absorption theorem for
Kirchberg algebras gives `Z`-stability.
