Let `sigma:A->U(K)` be an exact finite-dimensional representation for

```text
A=SL_3(Z[1/p]),       h=diag(p,1,p^(-1)),
```

and let `V,L in U(M)`.  On `M tensor K` put

```text
X_g=I_M tensor sigma(g)       (g in SL_3(Z)),
H=V tensor sigma(h),
T=L tensor I_K,
T_1=H^*TH=V^*LV tensor I_K.                         (SFL1)
```

Let `eta` be the defect of the fixed unbalanced torus word `R_tor`.  Then

```text
eta=||V-I||_2,
|tr(T^*T_1)| >= 1-2 eta.                              (SFL2)
```

Consequently

```text
2 eta+|tr(T^*H^*TH)| >=1.                             (SFL3)
```

The canonical HNN/amalgamated-free law has

```text
tr(T^*H^*TH)=0                                         (SFL4)
```

because `T` and `H^*TH` are the stable-letter conjugates indexed by the
distinct cosets `C` and `h^(-1)C`.  Hence no sequence of spectator product
models `(SFL1)` can make both the unbalanced torus defect and even this
single mixed moment tend to zero.

In particular, the finite free-lamp/Morita amplification already recorded
in `finite-lamp-frame-is-matrix-range-neutral` cannot be combined
mechanically with the unbalanced torus/root package.  It is false that
every fixed finite set of amalgamated-free moments remains neutral under
that product construction: adjoining the length-two cross-Gram `(SFL4)`
blocks it.

This does not prove the finite-window endpoint.  It rules out only the
separated normal form in which the exact arithmetic actor occupies one
tensor factor and the denominator/free-lamp data occupy the other.  A
surviving model must entangle the denominator coordinate with the lattice
packet, so that no unitary `V` can be peeled off as in `(SFL1)`.  The exact
minimum moving datum is therefore the common actor covariance coupling the
unbalanced torus coefficients to the conjugate-letter frame, not a larger
collection of actor-free lamp moments.

The product restriction is no longer the final ambient-face boundary.
`unbalanced-torus-forces-cross-gram-on-ambient-face` upgrades the same
conclusion to arbitrary direct sums and arbitrary block mixing, with no
operator-Schmidt-rank bound, whenever the lattice packet has an exact
ambient reference and the fixed overlap covariance is small.  What remains
outside both statements is exactly the ambient-extension outlier sector.
