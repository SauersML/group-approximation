For canonical-delta microstates and a group word `g`,
`||g(U_n)-1||_2^2` tends to `0` if `g=1` and to `2` otherwise.  Therefore a
bounded row coefficient `R_Q(U_n)` is controlled by a fixed multiple of this
root defect in the identity case exactly when `||R_Q(U_n)||_2` already tends
to zero.  Naming the root does not authenticate the analytic coefficient;
the necessary extra input is one-coordinate kernel reflection.
