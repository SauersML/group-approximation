For both exhaustive branches of the common middle carrier equation, the
normal-chirality rank-nine class with five-face size word `(1,3,1)` either
has trivial coefficient boundary or has unavoidable nontrivial syllables in
more than one free-product copy.  It therefore cannot supply the required
one-copy marked boundary.
