**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Benoist--Quint, *Mesures stationnaires et fermés invariants des
espaces homogènes*, Ann. of Math. 174 (2011), Corollaire 1.2. Let `G` be a connected real Lie group with
simple Lie algebra, `Λ` a lattice of `G`, `X = G/Λ`, and `Γ` a Zariski-dense sub-semigroup of `G`. Then:

1. every atom-free `Γ`-invariant probability measure on `X` is the Haar probability measure;
2. every infinite closed `Γ`-invariant subset of `X` is `X`;
3. every sequence of distinct finite `Γ`-orbits in `X` equidistributes to the Haar probability measure.
