Fix one oscillator type and its little group.  Schur's lemma says that every
intertwiner on its isotypic block is the chosen oscillator intertwiner tensored
with an arbitrary multiplicity operator.  Substitution into the exact group
multiplication table gives the inverse factor-set equation `(PIC3)`.  Conversely,
the left twisted regular representation for the inverse factor set satisfies
that equation in finite dimension.  Tensor it with the oscillator projective
representation and induce from the little group to the finite parahoric.  The
two factor sets cancel, so the induced representation is honest and satisfies
the complete parahoric table.  This proves both the forced inverse cocycle and
the exact higher-dimensional escape.
