**Statement.** Let `B` be a unital ring with `2 = 0`, let `n = p + r` with `p, r >= 2`, and let
`pi : St_n(B) -> U(N)` be a unitary representation, `N < infinity`. Fix `i0 != j0`. For `e in B` put

```text
nu(e) = || pi(x_(i0 j0)(e)) - 1 ||_2      (normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm).
```

If `Q` lies in the two-sided ideal generated by `e_1, ..., e_s`, then

```text
nu(Q)^2  <=  C_(p,r) * ( nu(e_1)^2 + ... + nu(e_s)^2 ),     C_(p,r) = 4 / ((1 - 1/p)(1 - 1/r)).     (ID)
```

For `n = 20`, `p = r = 10`, `C < 5`. For `n = 4`, `C = 16`.

The constant depends on nothing but `p, r`: not on `B`, `pi`, `N`, `s`, or on how many terms an expression
`Q = sum_l b_l e_(i_l) b'_l` needs.

**Proof idea** (route has the details).
1. Reduce to finite `B` by `positive-characteristic-steinberg-unitary-images-are-finite` (`pi` factors through
   `St_n(B/I)` with `B/I` finite, and ideal membership passes to quotients).
2. Split the indices into `I` (size `p`, containing `i0`) and `K` (size `r`, containing `j0`). The block root group
   `X = {x_ik(a) : i in I, k in K} = M_(p x r)(B)` is abelian. Conjugation by root elements with both indices in `I`,
   or both in `K`, acts on `X` by left and right elementary matrices, and hence by row and column permutations.
   The spectral measure `mu` of `pi|X` on characters `c = (c_ik)`, `c_ik in Hom_(F_2)(B, F_2)`, is invariant under
   all of these.
3. Average the entry `c_(i0 j0)` over the two-sided elementary moves. It becomes the degree-two `F_2`-polynomial
   `sum_(j,l) c_(jl)(b'_j e b_l)` in uniform variables `b, b'`. Such a polynomial is either constant or takes the
   value `1` with probability at least `1/4`. Row and column permutations put a nonvanishing entry off the cross
   through `(i0, j0)` with probability at least `(1 - 1/p)(1 - 1/r)`.
4. Hence `kappa N(e) <= m(e) <= N(e)`, where `m(e) = nu(e)^2 / 4` and
   `N(e) = mu{ c : e not in J(c) }`, `kappa = (1/4)(1 - 1/p)(1 - 1/r)`. Here `J(c)` is the largest two-sided ideal
   on which every `c_ik` vanishes.
5. `J(c)` is a two-sided ideal, so `Q not in J(c)` forces some `e_i not in J(c)`. Therefore
   `N(Q) <= sum_i N(e_i)` and `(ID)` follows.

Step 3's pointwise inequality is checked exhaustively (every character, every element, `p = r = 2`) on six rings of
order 4 and 8, including `F_2[x]/x^3`, `T_2(F_2)` and `F_2[x,y]/(x,y)^2`, by the artifact script.

**Use.** It gives `leavitt-cone-head-bound-holds-for-all-ring-shadows`: the cone-cover head bound `(QC)` holds for
every ring-induced representation of the Cuntz-cone cover, radical rings included.
