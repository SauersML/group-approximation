Notation.  `M = L^infty(X) x|_{sigma,c} P` is generated by `A = L^infty(X)` and
unitaries `u_g` with `u_g f u_g^* = sigma_g(f)`, `u_g u_h = c(g,h) u_{gh}`,
`tau(f u_g) = delta_{g,e} int f`.  It acts on `L^2(M) = L^2(X) (x) l^2(P)`.
For a subgroup `H <= P` write `M_H = W^*(A, u_h : h in H)`; this is the twisted
crossed product of `H`, and `E_{M_H}(f u_g) = 1_H(g) f u_g`.

**Step 1 (twisted crossed products by amenable groups are injective).**  Let
`H` be amenable, `Q = M_H`, acting on `K = L^2(X) (x) l^2(H)`.  The commutant
`A'` of the left multiplication action of `A` on `K` is
`L^infty(X) (x)bar B(l^2 H)`, which is type I and therefore injective.  Each
`u_h` normalizes `A`, hence `A'`.  Let `m` be a left-invariant mean on `H`.
Define `Phi(T)` by `<Phi(T) xi, eta> = m(h -> <u_h T u_h^* xi, eta>)`.  This is
a unital completely positive map from `B(K)` to `B(K)` that maps `A'` into
`A'`.

For `T in A'` and `k in H`, `u_k u_h T u_h^* u_k^* = c u_{kh} T u_{kh}^* c^*`
with `c = c(k,h) in A`.  Since `u_{kh} T u_{kh}^* in A'`, the factors `c` cancel.
Invariance of `m` then gives `u_k Phi(T) u_k^* = Phi(T)`.  So
`Phi(A') <= A' cap {u_h}' = Q'`, and `Phi` is the identity on `Q' <= A'`.
Hence `Q'` is the range of a conditional expectation from an injective
algebra, so `Q'` is injective, and so is `Q`.  Its predual is separable, so
by Connes' theorem `Q` is hyperfinite.

**Step 2 (finite index reduction).**  Choose a free subgroup
`F <= P/N` of finite index `k`, free on `a_1, ..., a_r` (`r < infinity`, since
`P/N` is finitely generated).  Choose `s(a_i) in P` over `a_i`.  Freeness
extends `s` to a homomorphic section `s : F -> P`, and
`P_0 = N s(F) = N x| s(F)` has index `k` in `P`.  Let `g_1, ..., g_k` represent
`P/P_0`.  Then `M = sum_i u_{g_i} M_0`, with `M_0 = M_{P_0}` and
`E_{M_0}(u_{g_i}^* u_{g_j}) = 0` for `i != j`.  So `L^2(M)` is isometric, as a
right `M_0`-module, to `L^2(M_0)^k`.

The left action of `M` commutes with the right `M_0` action, so
`M <= End_{M_0}(L^2(M_0)^k) = M_k(M_0)`.  For `x in M`,
`(tr_k (x) tau)(x) = (1/k) sum_i tau(u_{g_i}^* x u_{g_i}) = tau(x)`, so this
unital normal inclusion preserves the trace.  If `M_0` embeds in `R^omega`,
so does `M_k(M_0)`, and hence so does `M`.

**Step 3 (free product decomposition).**  Put `Q = M_N`, which is hyperfinite
by Step 1.  For `j <= r` put
`M_j = M_{N x| <s(a_j)>}` and `B_j = M_{N x| s(<a_1,...,a_j>)}`.  By Step 1
(the group `N x| Z` is amenable), each `M_j` is hyperfinite, hence embeds in
`R^omega`.

- **Generation.**  `B_{j+1} = W^*(B_j, M_{j+1})`.
- **Freeness with amalgamation over `Q`.**  Take an alternating product of
  elements of `B_j ominus Q` and `M_{j+1} ominus Q`.  Its entries are
  `L^2`-limits of spans of `f u_{n s(w)}`, with `w` a nontrivial element of
  `<a_1,...,a_j>` or of `<a_{j+1}>`, respectively.  The product lies in the
  closed span of `f u_{n s(w')}` with `w'` a nontrivial reduced word in `F`,
  since alternating syllables do not cancel.  So `E_Q` of the product is `0`.

Freeness with amalgamation over `Q` with respect to the trace-preserving
expectations, together with generation, identifies `B_{j+1}` with
`B_j *_Q M_{j+1}`.

Brown--Dykema--Jung, *Free entropy dimension in amalgamated free products*,
arXiv:math/0609080v5, Corollary 4.5, source-read 2026-09-17, verbatim:
"Let `M1 *_B M2` be the amalgamated free product of tracial von Neumann
algebras `M1` and `M2` over a hyperfinite von Neumann algebra `B`, and assume
each `Mi` has separable predual and is embeddable in `R^omega`.  Then
`M1 *_B M2` is embeddable in `R^omega`."

Induction on `j`, starting from `B_1 = M_1`, shows that every `B_j` embeds in
`R^omega`.  In particular `M_0 = B_r` embeds, and by Step 2 so does `M`.  This is
`(TCE1)`.

**Parabolic hypotheses.**  `Sym_2(Z) ~ Z^3` and the Klingen radical (a
Heisenberg-type group) are amenable.  The Levi quotients `GL_2(Z)` and
`{+-1} x SL_2(Z)` contain the principal congruence subgroup `Gamma(4) <= SL_2(Z)`
with finite index.  `Gamma(4)` is torsion free, and torsion-free subgroups of
`SL_2(Z) = Z/4 *_{Z/2} Z/6` are free.  So both Levi quotients are virtually free.
In Step 3 the "limits" are strong limits of bounded nets (Kaplansky density),
so `E_Q` passes to the limit.
