**Source.**  E. Gardella, I. Hirshberg, L. Santiago, *Rokhlin dimension:
duality, tracial properties, and crossed products*, arXiv:1709.00222.  The PDF
was fetched on MSI on 2026-09-13 and extracted with ghostscript `txtwrite`.
The quotations below follow the extracted text, with sub/superscripts repaired.

**Corollary 3.27.**  "Let A be a unital C∗-algebra and let α: Z2 → Aut(A) be an
action. Then dim^c_Rok(α) ≤ 1 if and only if there exists a unital equivariant
homomorphism ϕ: (C(S1), Lt) → (A∞ ∩ A′, α∞). Moreover, if dim^c_Rok(α) = 1, then
every such map ϕ is automatically injective."  Their proof: "Since Lt: Z2 →
Aut(C(S1)) has Rokhlin dimension one, the 'if' implication is clear. The
converse is contained in Lemma 1.9 in [32], once it is combined with
Lemma 3.25."

**Proposition 3.30** (statement).  "Let G be a second countable compact group,
let A be a separable C∗-algebra, and let α: G → Aut(A) be an action with
dim^c_Rok(α) < ∞. Let D be a strongly self-absorbing C∗-algebra, and suppose that
A is D-absorbing. Then there exists a unital embedding D → κ_A(A^α_∞ ∩ A′)."

**Proposition 3.32.**  "Let G be a finite group and let α: G → Aut(O2) be an
action. If α has finite Rokhlin dimension with commuting towers, then it has the
Rokhlin property."  Proof: "By the equivalence between parts (1) and (3) of
Theorem 4.2 in [38], it is enough to construct a unital map O2 → (O2^α)∞ ∩ O2′.
This is an immediate consequence of Proposition 3.30."  Then: "The conclusion of
Proposition 3.32 is false if one only assumes dim_Rok(α) < ∞. See, for example,
Example 4.8 in [20]."

**Definition 3.33.**  "(Definition 3.6 in [38]). Let G be a finite abelian group,
let B be a unital C∗-algebra, and let β: G → Aut(B) be an action. We say that β
is strongly approximately inner, if there exist unitaries vg ∈ (B^β)∞, for
g ∈ G, satisfying βg(b) = vg b vg∗ for all b ∈ B and all g ∈ G."

**Before Theorem 3.34.**  "In the theorem below, we do not know whether we obtain
a similar conclusion if we only assume that dim^c_Rok(α) < ∞, or if we replace Z2
with a general finite (abelian) group."

**Theorem 3.34.**  "Let A be a separable, unital C∗-algebra, and let α: Z2 →
Aut(A) be an action. Assume that dim^c_Rok(α) ≤ 1 and that A absorbs the
UHF-algebra M2∞ tensorially. Then α has the Rokhlin property."  From the proof:
"We claim that α̂ is strongly approximately inner. Use Corollary 3.27 to find a
unital equivariant homomorphism ϕ: C(S1) → A∞ ∩ A′, and denote by w ∈ A∞ ∩ A′ the
image of the canonical unitary in C(S1). Let u ∈ A ⋊α Z2 denote the unitary
implementing α. Then uwu∗ = α∞(w) = −w in (A ⋊α Z2)∞. In particular,
w∗uw = −u = α̂(u). Moreover, since w commutes with the copy of A in A∞, it follows
that w∗aw = a = α̂(a) for all a ∈ A. [...] thus α̂ is strongly approximately
inner."  The proof ends through Lemma 3.10 in [38] and Proposition 3.30.  After
the proof: "It is not enough in Theorem 3.34 to assume that dim_Rok(α) ≤ 1. See,
for example, the comments in part (a) of Remark 3.18."

**Remark 3.18(c)** (extract).  "Whether the class of nuclear algebras satisfying
the UCT is preserved by actions with finite Rokhlin dimension, is in fact
equivalent to the UCT problem for separable, nuclear C∗-algebras. Indeed, Barlak
has observed that the UCT problem [...] can be reduced to the question of whether
the crossed products of certain pointwise outer actions of finite groups on O2
satisfy the UCT; see Theorem 4.17 in [4] for the precise statement. By
Theorem 4.19 in [20], any such action has Rokhlin dimension at most one, which
proves our claim."

**Bibliography keys.**  [4] is Barlak–Szabó, *Rokhlin actions of finite groups on
UHF-absorbing C\*-algebras*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 369 (2017).  [20], [32] and
[38] were not identified from the extracted pages read here.  [38] carries
Izumi's definition of strong approximate innerness (Definition 3.6), and it is
the source of Lemmas 3.8 and 3.10 and Theorem 4.2.
