**SHARP FULL-SCALE NO-GO.**  On a forbidden atom of
`boolean-violation-is-one-raw-radical-qubit-drop`, put

```text
d=2^(N-1),
A_forbid=directSum_(chi in V_4-hat) M_d(C),
B_base=M_(2d)(C).                                         (RFM1)
```

The signs of the literal words `P_a,Q_a` give the four central projections
of `A_forbid`.  If their multiplicities on an isolated carrier are `m_chi`,
then that carrier has dimension

```text
h=d sum_chi m_chi.                                       (RFM2)
```

Let any finite ordinary-group chart, including finite subgroup restrictions,
crossed products and HNN edges, act on the same matrix space.  If its proposed
next fixed-scale carrier is a sum of `n` copies of the baseline simple of
dimension `2d`, and all complementary target types are orthogonal and
explicitly tracked, then

```text
2d n <= h,              hence n <= (1/2)sum_chi m_chi. (RFM3)
```

Therefore the desired equality

```text
n=sum_chi m_chi                                          (RFM4)
```

is impossible for every nonzero forbidden carrier.  This remains true if
the chart first permutes, balances, or transports the four branch types: such
operations change their type coordinates but not the dimension of the
underlying carrier.

The bound is sharp.  If an external reservoir of dimension `r` is allowed to
join the target carrier, then

```text
n <= (h+r)/(2d).                                         (RFM5)
```

Thus `(RFM4)` requires `r>=h`: at least one whole additional copy of the raw
forbidden carrier.  That reservoir cannot be treated as free.  Clause 3 of
`raw-radical-branch-morita-reset` must either track it as a next type or charge
its entrance by ordinary relator energy.  In the first case a finite regular
representation supplies the matching stationary reservoir flow; in the
second case exact finite chart/HNN relations provide no positive charge.

There is a valid but weaker merge at the half-spin scale.  Choose a
symplectic complement to the radical spanned by `P_a,Q_a`.  Its finite packet
subgroup has one marked simple `sigma` of dimension `d`, and all four branch
simples restrict once to `sigma`; hence the restricted multiplicity is
exactly `sum m_chi`.  However, any proposed `2d`-dimensional next baseline
whose restriction is supported on this common `sigma` contributes two copies
by dimension.  If its restriction has other types instead, those are further
complementary sectors that must be tracked.  Either way the half-spin
restriction is a scale change, not a recurrent return to `B_base` with the
same multiplicity.

Consequently the raw radical words solve branch **labeling**, not full-scale
Morita reset.  Any positive completion must add a genuinely one-sided or
payload-charged mechanism that imports an equal-size reservoir only in finite
matrices while the exact marked model extends.  A finite chart/HNN atlas by
itself cannot do so.
