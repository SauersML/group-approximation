Sections 1--4 give the reductions. With fixed computable irrational
slope, the nonnegative mechanical itinerary has exactly the degree
of the intercept: prefix sums trap it between computable cuts whose
mesh tends to zero. The unique phase transition makes the seam
language equivalent to this itinerary, with fixed computable endpoint
languages. Uniform recurrence supplies one computable payload-`10`
bound, giving effective parity decoding and equivalence with the
binary interleaved language. Cylinder roots and normal forms transfer
that degree to the three-generator word problem. The seam theorem
keeps both endpoint factors fixed, and the normal-lattice theorem
supplies the just non-MF property. Distinct word-problem degrees give
distinct abstract isomorphism types.
