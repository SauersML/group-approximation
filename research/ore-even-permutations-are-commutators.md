**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`ore-even-permutations-are-commutators-citation`). For every finite set `Ω`, every
element of `Alt(Ω)` equals `aba^(-1)b^(-1)` for some `a, b ∈ Sym(Ω)`.

O. Ore, *Some remarks on commutators*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 2 (1951), 307–314, Theorem 1 (p. 308): "Every
element in the alternating group A_n is a commutator of Σ_n." Ore's Theorem 7 (p. 313, proof omitted there)
strengthens this, for `n ≥ 5`, to commutators of elements of `A_n`. Later literature attributes an earlier proof to
G. A. Miller; that attribution was not checked.
