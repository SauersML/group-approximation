The vectors `x_i J^(a_i)` are linearly independent in the elementary
abelian group generated by `x_1,...,x_k,J`, so `H_a~=C_2^k`.  Its Reynolds
projection factors as

```text
e_(H_a)=product_i (1+x_i J^(a_i))/2.
```

Since `JQ_-=-Q_-`, multiplication by `Q_-` replaces every factor
`J^(a_i)` by `(-1)^(a_i)`, proving `(CSR1)`.  The usual joint functional
calculus for commuting involutions identifies the product with the assignment
atom.
