For each binary word `u`, prefix cancellation gives

```text
t_u s_u=1.                                             (1)
```

For distinct ambient coordinates `i,j,k,l`, ordinary matrix
multiplication and `(1)` give

```text
(S_iT_j E_ij)(S_kT_l E_kl)
  =delta_(j,k) S_iT_l E_il.                            (2)
```

The Kronecker delta in `(2)` comes from the ambient matrix coordinates, not
from the Leavitt coefficients.  Thus repeated labels cause no problem.
Equation `(2)` proves all ordinary non-opposite Steinberg relations for

```text
x_ij(1) |-> x_(i,j)(S_iT_j),                           (3)
```

so `(3)` extends to a homomorphism

```text
Phi_I:St_I(F_2)->St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2)).                  (4)
```

On the direct sum of the projective summands
`S_iT_i E_ii`, the elementary image of `(4)` is the standard binary action.
It is therefore faithful.  Since `St_N(F_2)=SL_N(F_2)` for `N>=3`, the map
`Phi_I` is injective.  This proves the first assertion.

The labels in `(PPW3)` recover the complete coefficient table directly:

```text
q=s_1t_1,
a_m=s_1t_(0^m),             b_m=s_(0^m)t_1,
c_m=s_(0^(m+1))t_(0^m),     d_m=s_(0^m)t_(0^(m+1)).   (5)
```

Thus the anchored arms and chain roots are arrows between the labels in
`(PPW3)`, while `c_m,d_m` are the two arrows between the adjacent labels
`0^m,0^(m+1)`.  Their three-elementary Whitehead words, and every word made
from them, lie in the image of `(4)`.  Adding finitely many further prefix
levels simply adds further labels to `I` and repeats the same construction.

It remains to extend the zero-source profile.  Let `pi_N` be the permutation
representation of `SL_N(F_2)` on `F_2^N-{0}`.  A transvection fixes the
`2^(N-1)-1` nonzero vectors in its fixed hyperplane; its remaining `2^(N-1)`
points form two-cycles.  Hence

```text
rank pi_N(P_z)=2^(N-2).                                (6)
```

The negative-stabilizer certificate for the signed full-Hecke group uses
only the old chain coordinates.  If one of the three relevant bits is zero,
the corresponding negative simple transvection fixes the vector.  If all
three are one, the product of the positive derived root `x_52(q)` with the
second negative simple root fixes it and still has negative sign.  Spectator
coordinates do not affect this calculation.  Every point stabilizer
therefore contains a character-negative element, so the signed average `F`
vanishes on every orbit:

```text
pi_N(F)=0.                                             (7)
```

Finally consider the commuting star generated by the three roots from the
common source coordinate to the three target coordinates.  Its all-negative
character occurs exactly on orbits with source bit one.  There are
`2^(N-1)` such vectors and every orbit has size eight, giving

```text
rank pi_N(E)=2^(N-4).                                  (8)
```

Equations `(6)--(8)` prove `(PPW4)`.  Since `pi_N` is an exact
representation of the whole finite prefix-labelled chart, no relation in
that chart can imply positive `F` mass from either `P_z` or `E`.  The last
paragraph of the claim follows because an individual prefix monomial
`s_ut_v` always becomes one arrow after assigning its two endpoints the
labels `u,v`; only incompatible reuse or a sum across different label pairs
escapes this construction.
