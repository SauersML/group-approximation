The Boolean algebra `Clop(X)` is countable.  Indeed, a compact metrizable
zero-dimensional space has a countable clopen basis, and compactness writes
every clopen set as a finite union of basis elements.  Enumerate this Boolean
algebra and let `Q_n` be the finite clopen partition into atoms of the Boolean
algebra generated by its first `n` members.  Refining if necessary, arrange
`Q_(n+1)` to refine `Q_n`.

Let `P_n` be the subgroup of functions constant on each member of `Q_n`.
Then

```text
P_n ~= P^(|Q_n|),       P_n subset P_(n+1).            (1)
```

Every locally constant function has finite image and clopen fibres.  All of
those fibres occur in some finite stage of the enumeration, so

```text
LC(X,P)=union_n P_n.                                  (2)
```

This also proves countability because every `P_n` is countable.

Put `A=C*_r(P)`.  Selflessness makes `A` simple and uniquely tracial, while
the hypothesis makes it exact.  We prove by induction that every finite
power `A^(tensor_min q)` is selfless.  The assertion is given for `q=1`.
If it holds for `q`, apply the ordinary-selfless clause of
`complete-selflessness-tensor-permanence` to

```text
A^(tensor_min q) tensor_min A.
```

That clause requires exactness only of the second factor `A`, so the
induction closes.  The regular representation gives canonical
trace-preserving identifications

```text
C*_r(P_n) ~= A^(tensor_min |Q_n|).                     (3)
```

Thus every reduced algebra in (2) is selfless.  Subgroup inclusions are
isometric for reduced norms and preserve the canonical traces; their union
is dense in `C*_r(LC(X,P))`.  The directed-union theorem therefore makes the
limit selfless.  Robert's tracial regularity theorem gives all stated
regularity consequences.

Finally, finite minimal tensor powers of the exact algebra `A` are exact,
and exactness passes to the injective C\*-inductive limit described by
(2)--(3).  Hence `C*_r(LC(X,P))` is exact.

**Trust boundary.**  The imported inputs are the exact ordinary-selfless
tensor-permanence theorem, Robert's directed-union and tracial-regularity
theorems, reduced direct-product/tensor-product identification, and exactness
permanence for minimal tensor products and injective inductive limits.  The
countable clopen exhaustion and its group-algebra realization are proved
above.
