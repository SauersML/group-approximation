**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `pi:Gamma->U(M)` be any unitary representation and let
`J` be a central involution of `Gamma`.  Put

```text
p_+=(1+pi(J))/2,       p_-=(1-pi(J))/2,
W=p_+ + i p_-.                                          (FCS1)
```

Then `W` is a central unitary and

```text
W^2=p_+-p_-=pi(J).                                      (FCS2)
```

Thus every representation of `Gamma` extends to the ordinary group
presentation obtained by adjoining a central letter `w` with `w^2=J`.
This holds in matrices, matrix ultraproducts, finite factors, and properly
infinite representations, with no approximation or dimension dependence.

Consequently the fresh clause `w^2=J` cannot be an
approximation-specific extension nemesis.  Its orientation power begins only
after other relations identify `w` with a raw payload holonomy.  Those
incidence relations—not the square clause—must exclude the CE type.  An
equal-full-carrier identification recreates the Morita `M_2`; the surviving
possibility remains a charged one-sided proper-corner section.
