A nowhere-zero section of L would give homotopic nonvanishing loops
at the cylinder ends, but the gluing changes their winding number by
one. Hence L is nontrivial. If [L]−[1]=0, a stable isomorphism
L⊕F≅1⊕F and determinants would trivialize L. Every state on C(T²)
integrates against a probability measure; its unnormalized K_0
pairing is the integral of the pointwise rank difference 1−1=0.

This is a written deduction integrated from the supplied attempt, not
an independent referee report or a Lean proof.
