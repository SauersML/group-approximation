**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G` be countably infinite. Any two Bernoulli shifts `G ↷ (k^G, λ^G)` and
`G ↷ (k'^G, λ'^G)` with finite alphabets and non-point-mass base measures are weakly equivalent in
Kechris's sense.

Proof: Lemma 1.1, Lemma 2.1 and Proposition 2.2 of the artifact. Nontrivial Bernoulli shifts are
free, and Abért–Weiss put every finite-alphabet Bernoulli shift weakly inside every free action.
