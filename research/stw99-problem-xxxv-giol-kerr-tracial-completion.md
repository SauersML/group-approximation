**Problem XXXV of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**  Is
the tracial completion of a non-`Z`-stable Giol--Kerr crossed product
`C(X) ⋊ Z` the tracial completion of a Villadsen algebra of the first
type?  STW also record: the GK crossed product fails strict comparison,
its inclusion fails relative Γ (nonzero mean dimension), and whether it
has (absolute) uniform Γ is open — as is whether that question equals
Problem XX.

## Attempts

* The 2026 refutation wave sharpens the stakes: Problem XX is now
  POSITIVE (Vaccaro — V1 algebras have uniform Γ), so a positive
  answer here TRANSPORTS uniform Γ to the Giol--Kerr algebras
  (`giol-kerr-uniform-gamma-via-tracial-completion`), answering STW's
  side question and separating absolute from relative Γ on a natural
  example (relative Γ fails by Lindenstrauss--Weiss mean-dimension
  obstruction).  Conversely, if GK provably lacked uniform Γ, this
  problem would be REFUTED — making it a live two-sided test.
* Both objects have Poulsen-type trace simplices fed by
  point-evaluation towers; the completion comparison reduces to
  matching the W*-bundle-free data: the simplex affinely, and the
  fibrewise completions (hyperfinite II₁ everywhere in both cases).
  What is NOT automatic is matching the metric structure the algebra
  induces on the simplex (2-norm uniformities), which is exactly where
  a Vaccaro-style tracial local homogeneity argument for GK would have
  to operate.  No such computation is in the literature; the GK side's
  natural stages are recursive subhomogeneous rather than homogeneous,
  so tracial local homogeneity is plausible but unproved.
