Theorem E of `kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath` supplies the pair: `Gamma` and `G`
Kazhdan, `Gamma` infranormal and not normal.

1. Some compressor is strict; otherwise `G = <P_Gamma>` normalizes `Gamma`.
2. `Gamma` fixes the coset `Gamma`, so `e_Gamma` centralizes `Gamma`.
3. `t e_Gamma t^-1 = e_(t Gamma)`.
4. For `gamma in Gamma \ t Gamma t^-1`, `gamma e_(t Gamma) gamma^-1 =
   e_(gamma t Gamma)`, a different lamp.
5. The commutator is the product of two distinct lamps, which is nontrivial
   in the direct sum.
