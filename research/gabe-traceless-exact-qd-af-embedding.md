title: Traceless separable exact quasidiagonal algebras are AF-embeddable

Let `A` be a separable exact traceless C-star algebra.  Then the following
are equivalent:

1. `A` is AF-embeddable;
2. `A` is quasidiagonal;
3. `A` is stably finite;
4. `Prim(A)` contains no nonempty open compact subset.

Here tracelessness is in the lower-semicontinuous-weight sense used in the
source.

**Established (literature).**  This is Corollary C of James Gabe,
*Traceless AF embeddings and unsuspended E-theory*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 30
(2020), 323--333, arXiv:1804.08095.
