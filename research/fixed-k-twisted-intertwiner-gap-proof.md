Use the irreducible decomposition of a finite direct product.  Conjugation
on an ordered pair of isotypic sectors acts on an intertwiner `T` by left
`rho(k_i)`, right `sigma(k_i)^*`, and the character-difference sign.  Summing
the squared generator displacements gives `(FKG1)`.  The zero-energy blocks
are exactly intertwiners between identical representations of the subgroup
generated by the paired labels.
