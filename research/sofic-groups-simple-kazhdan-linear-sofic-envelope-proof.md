Artifact §3. Let `Γ` be finitely generated and sofic.

1. **Overgroup.** `Δ = C(Γ)` is finitely generated, infinite and sofic, and `δ_0 : Γ → Δ` is injective with
   `δ_0(Γ) ≤ [Δ,Δ]` (half-line overgroup claim, items 1–4). Artifact §5 gives an independent check through metric
   ultraproducts and Ore's theorem.
2. **Host.** `L = Z/2 ≀ Δ` acts on `Z = 2^Δ` minimally and topologically freely (artifact §2, Lemma 1), and
   `R = LC(Z,F_2) ⋊ L` is finitely generated by `u_s`, `u_a` and `1_{x(e)=1}`. As the Steinberg algebra of a minimal
   effective ample groupoid, `R` is simple with centre `F_2`.
3. **Group.** `G = EL_3(R)` is simple with trivial centre (binary criterion), Kazhdan (Ershov–Jaikin-Zapirain), and
   infinite and finitely generated.
4. **Linear soficity.** The counting models give a unital injective homomorphism of `R` into a rank ultraproduct over
   `F_2`, so `G = G/Z` is `F_2`-linear sofic by the projective transfer claim.
5. **Embedding.** `Δ ≤ L` gives `[Δ,Δ] ≤ [L,L]`. The map `ℓ ↦ diag(u_ℓ,1,1)` embeds `[L,L]` in `EL_3(R)` (Whitehead
   identity, item 3 of the elementary embedding claim). Composing with `δ_0` embeds `Γ` in `G`.
