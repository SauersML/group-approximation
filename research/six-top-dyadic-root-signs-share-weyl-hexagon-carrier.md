Fix `a>=2`, put

```text
G_a=SL_3(Z/2^a Z),
u_ij=x_ij(2^(a-1)),                    i!=j.             (SDC1)
```

The six `u_ij` are commuting independent involutions.  In the left regular
representation of `G_a`, define

```text
D_a=product_(i!=j) (I-lambda(u_ij))/2.                  (SDC2)
```

Then `D_a` is a projection with

```text
tr(D_a)=1/64,                                           (SDC3)
```

independently of `a`.  It is invariant under both signed simple-Weyl words

```text
n_12=x_12(1)x_21(-1)x_12(1),
n_23=x_23(1)x_32(-1)x_23(1),                            (SDC4)
```

and therefore under every transition in the closed chamber gallery

```text
123 -> 213 -> 231 -> 321 -> 312 -> 132 -> 123.          (SDC5)
```

More importantly, `D_a` is simultaneously the negative half-period carrier
for every oriented A2 chamber.  If `i,j,k` are distinct, put

```text
P=lambda(x_ij(1)),       Q=lambda(x_jk(1)),       m=2^(a-1).
```

Then

```text
D_a([P,Q^m]+I)=0,
D_a([P^m,Q]+I)=0.                                      (SDC6)
```

Thus all twelve half-period commutator-flip words needed by the six chamber
orientations live on one literal Weyl-invariant carrier of fixed positive
mass.  The same statements hold for any direct sum of regular
representations of `G_a`.

## Robust finite-packet consequence

The group generated by the six top-layer signs and the two signed Weyl words
is the fixed finite semidirect product

```text
E_6 rtimes W_tilde,         E_6=(C_2)^6,                (SDC7)
```

where `W_tilde` is the finite determinant-one signed permutation group.  It
is independent of `a`.  Consequently flexible finite-group normalized-HS
exactification makes the projection, its trace, and its Weyl invariance in
`(SDC2)--(SDC5)` robust with a modulus independent of the ambient matrix
dimension and of the depth `a`, once this last-layer packet has been decoded
with its regular character.  The identities `(SDC6)` are then robust in
terms of their own half-period word residuals.  Deriving those residuals
from a fixed generator defect by naive power telescoping may still lose a
depth-dependent factor.  In particular, neither intersection loss nor
Weyl-gallery holonomy is an obstruction on the exact regular congruence
face; half-period word extraction remains part of the depth ledger.

## Exact remaining boundary

`D_a` authenticates the half-period **commutator** carrier and its closed
Weyl returns.  It does not make the denominator element `h` act within that
same level: dyadic doubling sends the top layer into the adjacent conductor
layer and is rectangular before correction.  Nor does an arbitrary outlier
microstate automatically supply an exact copy of `(SDC7)`.  The surviving
common-carrier problem is therefore narrower: decode this fixed last-layer
sign packet from the six root words and control its loss under the one
denominator-depth transition.  Once that is done,
`robust-half-period-a2-flip-pays-simultaneous-doubling` supplies the analytic
payment.

The decoder has one sharp mandatory statistic.
`six-weyl-sign-proper-moments-miss-top-parity` constructs six exactly
commuting, exactly Weyl-covariant signs which match the regular packet on
all 62 nonempty proper sign-product moments while their all-negative atom is
zero.  Hence the full six-root parity moment cannot be inferred from the
proper moments or Weyl covariance.  Controlling that one moving-depth word
(or an equivalent genuinely six-way statistic) is necessary before the
rectangular `h`-transport problem even begins.
