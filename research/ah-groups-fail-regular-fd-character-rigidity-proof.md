Group-theoretic Dehn filling for acylindrically hyperbolic groups supplies a
proper infinite acylindrically hyperbolic quotient; quotienting its finite
radical gives a proper infinite ICC quotient `q:H->Q`.  This is a standard
consequence of Dahmani--Guirardel--Osin's hyperbolically embedded subgroup
filling theorem and Hull's small-cancellation refinements.

The pullback of `delta_Q` is a character of `H`.  Its GNS algebra is the
factor `L(Q)`, so it is extremal and infinite dimensional.  A nonidentity
element of `ker(q)` has character value one, proving the character differs
from `delta_H`.  It is therefore neither regular nor finite-dimensional.
