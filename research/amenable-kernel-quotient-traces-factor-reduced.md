Let π:Γ→Q be a surjective homomorphism of discrete groups with amenable
kernel N, J_N the closed ideal of C_r^*(Γ) generated by λ(n)−1 for n∈N,
and D=C_r^*(Γ)/J_N. The induced surjection q̄:D→C_r^*(Q) gives an
affine bijection T(C_r^*(Q))→T(D). Equivalently, every tracial state on
C_r^*(Γ) annihilating J_N factors uniquely through the reduced group
quotient.

The proof uses the BKKO trace-support theorem, not assembly or exactness.
It proves neither J_N=ker q nor injectivity in K-theory. Theorem 3.1 of
the [proof document](artifacts/Cairn_Baum_Connes_General_Attempt.md#theorem-31-tracial-factorization)
contains the full radical and conjugation-invariance argument.
