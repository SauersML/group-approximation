**ESTABLISHED** by `port-generated-square-completions-force-coset-support-proof`.
It has not been reviewed by an ex-verify lane.

**Setting.**
- `G` is a synchronous game and `τ` a perfect trace on `A(G)` with full
  support on answers.  Zero-weight answers can be killed by a quotient, which
  keeps the trace and the absence of `R^U` models.
- A *port-generated completion* squarifies each question block by a Latin
  square `L^q` with an identity port row:
  `u_((q,x),(q,y)) = e_(q,(L^q_x)^(-1) y)`.
  - Equivalently, every padding entry lies in the span of the port PVM
    `{e_(q,a)}_a`.
  - Harris's circulant completion is the case of a cyclic group.

**Theorem.**  The induced trace is closure-safe, in the sense of
`closure-safe-support-is-exactly-the-colored-iso-rung`, iff:

- **(L1)** every row set `{L^q_x}` is a regular permutation group `K_q`;
- **(L2)** for every `q != q'`, the coset hull `M_(qq') s_0` of the pair
  support lies in the rule `W_(qq')`.  Here `M_(qq') <= K_q x K_(q')` is
  generated by `{g : g s_0 in S_(qq')}`.

**Consequences.**
1. **Boolean answers.**  (L2) is affine safety, so
   `jnvwy-active-output-has-no-affine-safe-perfect-model` blocks every
   port-generated squarification of the unchanged verifier.
2. **Oracularized games.**  Context supports become left cosets of subgroup
   images `{(φ_i(k))_i}` in products of regular groups, i.e. torsor CFI
   relations.  So atom-by-atom gadgets realize only cosets
   (`torsor-cfi-port-profiles-are-cosets`).
3. **Sharpness.**  Padding outside the port algebra escapes.  The orbital
   colouring of `S_3` on points and 2-subsets has a classical closure-safe
   trace whose port support, point-in-pair incidence, is not rectangular.  So
   it is not a coset of any subgroup.

**Relation to coset systems.**  (L1) and (L2) say that the completed game,
after coarsening colours to components, is a coset system in the sense of
`coset-systems-are-torsor-isomorphism-games`: regular fibres `K_q` and
pairwise cosets `M_(qq') s_0`.  So among completions inside the port algebra,
the coset system is not one choice among several.  It is the only closure-safe
one.

**What this settles.**  Nonabelian local symmetry does not help while the
squarification stays inside the port measurement's own algebra: groups
appear, and cosets with them.  A compiler beyond the coset firewall needs
padding operators outside the port algebra, either auxiliary randomness or
new noncommuting operators.  Whether the JNVWY output is coset-safe for some
nonabelian regular structure on its multi-bit alphabets is not settled.
