For any group G and element t of order three, put e=1+t+t^2 in
R=F_2[G]. The following are equivalent: eRe is directly finite;
every injective scalar Boolean CA F=x+A(xR_t x) whose coefficient
a satisfies (1-e)ae=0 is surjective.

Ambient direct finiteness of R suffices; stable direct finiteness
is not needed. If H=<t> is normal, every a qualifies and eRe is
F_2[G/H]. Thus the whole arbitrary-feedback family in that case
is surjunctive exactly when F_2[G/H] is directly finite.

For nonnormal H and arbitrary feedback, this theorem supplies no
solution. An explicit finite control shows why injectivity of the
linear axis cannot alone establish injectivity of its compression.
Neither headline conjecture is resolved.
