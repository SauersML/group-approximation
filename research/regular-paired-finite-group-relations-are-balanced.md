title: Paired relations between two regular finite-group charts are balanced exactly when their kernels agree
---

Let `G` be a finite group, `K<=G x G`, and let `p_1,p_2:K->G` be the coordinate
projections.  Let `Reg_G` be the complex left regular representation of `G`.
Then the two pulled-back `K`-representations

```text
Reg_G o p_1,       Reg_G o p_2                           (RPB1)
```

are unitarily isomorphic if and only if

```text
ker p_1 = ker p_2.                                      (RPB2)
```

When `(RPB2)` holds, their exact intertwiner space contains a unitary.  Hence
it has **no** nonzero shrunk-subspace deficiency:

```text
dim Hom_K(Reg_G o p_2,Reg_G o p_1)(X) >= dim X          (RPB3)
```

for every `X<=C[G]`.  The same is true after any common external
amplification.

Conversely, if `(RPB2)` fails, the two pulled-back regular characters already
differ on an element of `K`; no unitary can intertwine them.

This gives a sharp finite test for a proposed collection of paired chart
relations.  Compute the subgroup of `G x G` generated by the relation pairs
and compare its two projection kernels.  If they agree, operator scaling of
that paired relation system cannot produce a shrunk-space obstruction at any
spectral cutoff, because the zero-energy space itself already contains a
unitary.
