Split configurations into even triples V=ker E and invariant triples
W=im E. The same local involution as in the central case gives
Q Psi(d,m)=m+mT^2d without requiring centrality. Under (1-e)ae=0,
the first output is homogeneous linear in d and equals identity
when m=0. Its zero-d restriction on the second output is the
corner operator e+eae.

The continuous linear dual of W is Re. Corner direct finiteness
therefore makes that injective zero-d operator bijective. Kernel
comparison, finite-output perturbations, and compactness eliminate
the first block and then the second. All finite modifications use
whole triples, respecting both constrained configuration spaces.

Conversely a one-sided inverse pair in eRe gives a quadratic norm
rule conjugate to the corresponding corner linear map plus identity
on V. Surjunctivity of this family reverses the inverse pair. For
normal H the explicit map gH -> ge identifies the corner with
the quotient group algebra.
