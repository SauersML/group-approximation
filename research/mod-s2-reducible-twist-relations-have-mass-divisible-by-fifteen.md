## Setting

As in `mod-s2-torelli-characters-have-a-metaplectic-maslov-line`:
- `M = Mod(S_2)`, `pi : M -> Gamma = Sp_4(Z)`, `K = ker pi` (Torelli).
- `F : K -> Z` is the separating-twist count (Mess basis). `Gamma' <= Gamma` has finite index and
  `H = pi^-1(Gamma')`.
- `L(H) = nu(K cap [H,H])` is the relation lattice, with `F(L(H)) = 5 m(Gamma') Z`, `m in {1,2}` (ML1).

Call `A <= M` **reducible** if it preserves the isotopy class of some nonempty essential multicurve
(setwise). Examples:
- stabilizers of a curve or a pants decomposition;
- centralizers of a Dehn twist;
- the image of `Mod(Sigma, d Sigma)` for any subsurface `Sigma` with an essential boundary curve.

The last example contains every lantern, chain, star or other relation supported in a proper subsurface.

## Statement

1. **(RT1) Reducible images are T-flexible.** If `A <= M` is reducible, then the Deligne class vanishes on
   `pi(A)` with circle coefficients: `alpha|_(pi(A)) = 0` in `H^2(pi(A); T)`. Equivalently, the preimage of
   `pi(A)` in `E_3` has a character `phi` with `phi(z) = omega`.
2. **(RT2) Localized Maslov count.** Let `B <= Gamma` satisfy `alpha|_B = 0` in `H^2(B; T)`. Let `A <= M` with
   `pi(A) <= B`, and let `H` be any finite-index preimage. Then
   ```text
   F( K cap [A cap H, A cap H] )  <=  15 Z .
   ```
3. **(RT3) Sums of local relations.** Let `L_loc(H)` be the subgroup of `L(H)` generated by
   `nu(K cap [A cap H, A cap H])` over all reducible `A <= M`. Then `F(L_loc(H)) <= 15 Z`. Hence:
   - `L_loc(H) != L(H)`, since `F(L(H)) = 5mZ` with `m in {1,2}`;
   - the constant vector `(1/3) 1` annihilates `L_loc(H)`, so the Torelli-abelian gap computed from local
     relations alone is `0`;
   - no product `t_(s_1)^(n_1) ... t_(s_k)^(n_k)` with `3 not| sum n_j` lies in the subgroup of `[H,H]`
     generated by the groups `K cap [A cap H, A cap H]`, `A` reducible.
4. **(RT4) Congruence-deep flexible images.** If `pi(A)` is finite or virtually free and `Gamma' <= Gamma(3)`,
   the conclusion of (RT2) holds for `A`. The reason is that `pi(A) cap Gamma(3)` is trivial or free.

## Reading: the precise obstruction

- **Invariant.** The restriction of Deligne's class to the monodromy group of the relation, in
  `H^2(-; T)`. Equivalently: `F/5 mod 3` on commutator Torelli words, which is the `z`-exponent mod 3.
- **Class killed.** Relations whose commutator expression is taken inside one reducible subgroup, and
  arbitrary products of such relations. This includes:
  - lifts of lantern and chain relations and of any relation supported in a proper subsurface;
  - "`H_2(Gamma')` classes supported near a single splitting orbit", meaning inside `Stab(s)`;
  - the candidate route of `mod-s2-torelli-abelian-census-limit-is-positive` ("a lantern or chain type relation
    among separating twists");
  - the candidates posed in `mod-s2-torelli-positive-relations-have-bounded-mass`.
- **Where every member dies.** At the divisibility clause `3 not| sum n_j`: `sum n_j = F` lies in `15Z`.
- **Consistency with the census.** The level-three GQ(2,4) stars have `F = 5`, and the level-four transversal
  relations have `F = 10`. By (RT3) neither is a sum of local relations: each is a genuinely global genus-two
  relation. Likewise `t_s^5` lies in `[M, M]`, but no power `t_s^j` with `15 not| j` lies in `[Stab(s), Stab(s)]`.

## What survives

A certificate needs a commutator word `prod_i [x_i, y_i] in K` whose letters jointly generate a subgroup with
`alpha` nonzero on its image in `H^2(-; T)`. There are two ways to have that.
- **Rigid monodromy.** The letters generate a subgroup whose image is Deligne-rigid, or at least not a
  T-coboundary subgroup. Every finite-index subgroup qualifies. By
  `sp4-deligne-rigid-infinite-index-subgroup-exists`, it is open whether any infinite-index one does.
- **Mixed handles.** Each handle `<x_i, y_i>` is reducible, but the individual commutators `[x_i, y_i]` are
  not in `K`. (RT3) does not decompose such words, because the `z`-exponent is additive only over factors
  that lie in `K`.

So a proof of the bounded-mass hole, or of (TAP), must produce genus-two relations over a base surface whose
monodromy is not T-flexible. In Lefschetz language, the monodromy of the separating-only fibration may not
preserve any curve, even up to T-coboundary.
