If a finite-simple six-relator escape has `A8` index at most `8640`, then

```text
S=O8+(2),       [S:A8]=8640.                         (S6O8P)
```

There is no possible carrier at indices `2201..8639`.  At equality, the
known local fork consists of the orthogonal class inside `Sp6(2)`, the
natural class inside `A9`, and two affine-complement classes inside
`2^6:A8`, together with cross-carrier positions.  Same-carrier orthogonal
and natural positions inherit earlier exclusions; the affine and
cross-carrier cells are the genuinely new six-word target.

This is a finite CFSG reduction, not an `O8+(2)` exclusion.
