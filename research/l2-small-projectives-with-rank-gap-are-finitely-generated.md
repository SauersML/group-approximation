**ESTABLISHED** through `l2-small-projectives-with-rank-gap-are-fg-proof`, from Lück's dimension
theory over `U(G)` (recalled imports).

`G` is any group, `U = U(G)` the algebra of operators affiliated to `NG`, and `Z[G] ⊆ NG ⊆ U`.
Modules are left modules. A projective `Z[G]`-module `P` is **`L²`-small** if
`dim_U(U ⊗_{Z[G]} P) < ∞`. For `a ∈ Z[G]`, the **`L²`-rank** of `a` is `dim_U(U a)`, the dimension
of the left ideal of `U` generated by `a`. Equivalently it is `1 − dim_NG ker(r_a)`, where `r_a` is
right multiplication by `a` on `ℓ²G`. The **trace ideal** `T(P)` is the two-sided ideal
`Σ_f f(P)` over all `f ∈ Hom(P, Z[G])`.

1. **Thin trace ideals.** Let `P` be `L²`-small and not finitely generated. Then for every
   `ε > 0` there is `0 ≠ a ∈ T(P)` with `dim_U(U a) < ε`. More precisely, for every dual basis
   `(x_i, f_i)` of `P` and every `ε > 0`, all but finitely many `i` satisfy
   `dim_U(U f_i(P)) < ε`.
2. **Rank gap gives finite generation.** Suppose that for some `c > 0` every nonzero `a ∈ T(P)`
   has `dim_U(U a) >= c`, for instance when `inf { dim_U(U a) : 0 ≠ a ∈ Z[G] } > 0`. Then every
   `L²`-small projective `Z[G]`-module `P` is finitely generated.
3. **Decomposition.** Every `L²`-small projective `P` splits as `P = P_0 ⊕ P'`. Here `P_0` is
   finitely generated, and `P'` has no nonzero finitely generated direct summand. `P'` is zero
   exactly when `P` is finitely generated. The `U`-dimension of every finitely generated
   projective `Z[G]`-module is an integer, and it is positive when the module is nonzero.

**Calibration.**

- *Free groups.* `F_n` satisfies the strong Atiyah conjecture, so every nonzero `a` has
  `L²`-rank 1. Item 2 (over `Q[G]`, see the last bullet) says that `L²`-small projective
  `Q[F_n]`-modules are finitely generated. This matches Cohn: `Q[F_n]` is a fir, so its
  projectives are free, and a free `L²`-small module has finite rank.
- *Finite groups.* The `L²`-ranks lie in `(1/|G|)Z`, so there is a gap. Item 2 matches the direct
  argument that such a `P` is a free abelian group of finite rank.
- *Torsion.* For `G = ⊕_N Z/2` with coefficients in `Q`, `Q[G]` has projectives
  `⊕_n Q[G]e_n` with `dim = Σ 2^{-n}` that are not finitely generated. Item 1 is consistent with
  this: `∏_{k<=n}(1+g_k)` has `L²`-rank `2^{-n}`, so there is no gap. (Items 1–2 hold verbatim over
  `Q[G]`; the integrality in item 3 needs `Z[G]` or a trace-integrality input.)

**Use.** This is the module-theoretic input of `amenable-cd-two-l2-rank-gap-forces-baumslag-solitar`.
There, `P` is the second syzygy of a finitely generated amenable group of cohomological dimension 2.
That syzygy is `L²`-small, and it is finitely generated exactly when the group is `BS(1,m)`.
