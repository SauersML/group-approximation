**Literature input.** G. Elek and E. Szabó, *Sofic representations of amenable groups*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.
139 (2011); arXiv:1010.3424v2 (29 Oct 2010).

> **Theorem 1.** Free products of sofic groups amalgamated over arbitrary amenable groups are sofic.

The paper's convention, verbatim from page 1: "We call a not necessarily finitely generated group sofic if all of its
finitely generated subgroups are sofic. Amenable and residually finite groups are sofic."

The abstract states the same result: "the free product of two sofic groups amalgamated over an arbitrary amenable
subgroup is sofic". It removes the monotileability hypothesis of Collins--Dykema. The introduction adds that Păunescu
has an independent proof.

Many nodes already quote this theorem inline (for example `higman-group-amalgam-splittings-proof` and
`permuted-free-product-linton-radical-gives-sofic-shell-proof`). This node gives it a claim of its own so that routes
can require it.
