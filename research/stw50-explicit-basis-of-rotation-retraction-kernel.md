For A=A_theta, or for its unital Kirchberg counterpart
A_theta tensor O_infinity, construct four explicit based automorphism
loops whose classes form a Z-basis of ker(rho), where rho is the
Bott-evaluation retraction defined in the artifact.

Here "basis" includes both independence and integral generation;
rank four or rational generation alone is insufficient. Together
with the two rotation loops this would solve STW Problem L for the
chosen algebra.

## Kirchberg alternative constructed (2026-09-05)

Section 7 of the new artifact gives the four loops
sigma_2, sigma_4, rho_1 sigma_1^{-1}, rho_2 sigma_3^{-1} in the
graph tensor model, followed by a fixed scaled classification
identification with the Kirchberg counterpart. Their four
invariant columns form an integral basis of the retraction kernel.
These are explicit model formulas using fixed nonconstructive
classification isomorphisms. The original A_theta alternative
remains open as `stw50-stably-finite-six-loop-basis`.

## Earlier attempt and what was missing

Subtracting the two rotation coordinates puts any already-constructed
loop in ker(rho), but supplies no nonzero residual loop. The graph
model approach must construct automorphisms, rather than just
endomorphisms or abstract KK classes, and compute the integral classes
of the resulting loops. The new construction supplies these classes
and loops in the Kirchberg setting.
