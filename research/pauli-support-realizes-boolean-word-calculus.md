Let

```text
Q_2=(prod_n M_2(C))/(directSum_n M_2(C)).
```

For subsets `S,T subset N`, there are involutions `x_S,z_T in U(Q_2)` such
that, writing `J_A` for the principal ideal generated by a rank-one projection
supported on `A`,

```text
Supp(x_S)=J_S,
Supp(z_T)=J_T,
Supp(x_S z_T)=J_(S union T),
Supp([x_S,z_T])=J_(S intersection T).                 (PB1)
```

Thus the product and commutator bounds for ideal-valued support are both
sharp on every pair of elements.  In particular `x_S` and `z_T` commute in
the corona exactly when `S intersection T` is finite.

The map

```text
S modulo Fin |-> conjugacy class of x_S
```

is injective.  Hence `U(Q_2)` has continuum many conjugacy classes of
involutions, distinguished by their corona support ideals.

Moreover every countable simple graph `Gamma` occurs as the exact commutation
graph of a family of nonidentity involutions `(u_v)_(v in V(Gamma))` in
`U(Q_2)`:

```text
[u_v,u_w]=1 iff {v,w} is an edge of Gamma.             (PB2)
```

The noncommuting pair commutators can simultaneously be assigned pairwise
almost-disjoint nonzero support ideals.
