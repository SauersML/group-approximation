**ESTABLISHED.** Let `N` have Kazhdan's property (T), and let `H` be a
torsion-free group containing a finite-index subgroup `H_0` that is the
fundamental group of a compact special cube complex.

1. Every homomorphism `phi: N -> H` is trivial.
2. If `N` is infinite and finitely presented, then no marked group `(N, S)` is a
   limit of torsion-free virtually compact special marked groups.

**Consequence for Steenbock's question.** Arzhantseva--Steenbock
(arXiv:1407.2441, Corollary `C: T`) state: "There are torsion-free Gromov
hyperbolic groups with Kazhdan's Property (T) and without the unique product
property." Hyperbolic groups are finitely presented. So for these hosts the
zero-divisor conjecture cannot be obtained from
`marked-limits-of-special-groups-have-domain-group-rings`. The same authors
already observe that such groups are not CAT(0)-cubical, which closes the
cubulation route (arXiv:1407.2441, introduction). Item 2 closes the
approximation route too, including approximation by quotients.
