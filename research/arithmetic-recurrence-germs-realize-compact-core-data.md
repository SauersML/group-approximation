For `q≥2`, `p∤q`, the two arithmetic models of artifact Sections 5–6
and 9 meet the hypotheses of `compact-core-singfix-finiteness` at
`k=∞` whenever supplied with the indicated faithful finite-type forward
profile group and injective shift:

* scalar dilations of `UT_m(ℤ[1/q])` on the left-coset tree of
  `δ_p^j UT_m(ℤ_p)`, `m≥2`;
* affine vectors `ℤ[1/q]^r⋊(UT_r(ℤ)×⟨qI⟩)` on the p-adic vector
  tree, `r≥1`.

This includes faithfulness and self-similarity of the base, its actual
nilpotent ascending HNN model, `F_∞` for every finite-root RN base via
the SZ finite-root adaptation, oligomorphic rational marked sets,
supported independent zooms, faithful depth-preserving annular
realizations, base cutoffs on every finite band, inverse-zoom shift
relations, and the localizations/full finite-germ group required by
the compact-core theorem. The full depth-zero isotropy must be
`⟨δ_q⟩` in the scalar model and `UT_r(ℤ)×⟨qI⟩` in the vector model,
and must be contained in the selected profile group.

Each full depth-zero group has type `F_∞` in its own right (cyclic in
the scalar case, finitely generated nilpotent times cyclic in the vector
case); this is not inferred from being a subgroup of `L`. Restriction
to every nonempty Boolean clopen atom must identify the supported full
group with the corresponding finite-forest construction. Marked-point
and Boolean-partition matching, and groupoid/localized membership, are
included in the assertion, not inferred just from radial formulas.

The two-end shell maps give faithful embeddings of the restricted
nilpotent or vector lamp semidirect products in the resulting actor,
including detection of finitely supported profiles. These are the
explicit constructions in artifact Sections 6 and 9, not an assumption
of eventual-tail injectivity for global profiles.

## Attempts

The submitted section formulas use `N∩H_j=δ_p^jN` in the nilpotent
case and residue subtraction/division in the vector case. The integer
base groups are finitely generated nilpotent; injective normal forms
must justify their ascending HNN descriptions. The finite-root SZ
argument needs descending-link connectivity along all admissible leaf
numbers. Matching rational balls supplies marked transitivity; radial
cutoffs are base maps. Faithfulness of shell actions is tested on
`uH₁` or on a primitive vector plus `pℤ_p^r`, so finite-support
profiles are not lost. Each of these precise geometric claims remains
pending review. This gate supplies the hypotheses, not the conclusion,
of the separate compact-core theorem; it does not assume the older
polynomial-tower finiteness claim.
