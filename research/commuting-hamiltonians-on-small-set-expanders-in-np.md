**ESTABLISHED BY CITATION** (Aharonov--Eldar, arXiv:1301.3407v1, Theorems 1 and 2).

For a bipartite graph `G=(L,R;E)` with constraints on the left and particles on
the right, right degree `D_R`, call `G` `eps`-small-set expanding if every set
`S` of at most `k` particles has at least `|S| D_R (1-eps)` incident constraints.

- **(AE1)** Let `H` be a commuting `k`-local Hamiltonian on `d`-dimensional
  particles, with `k,d` constant, whose incidence graph is `eps`-small-set
  expanding with `eps<1/2`, and put `gamma(eps)=2kd eps`. For every eigenvalue
  `lambda_i` of `H` there is a state `psi_i` generated by a constant-depth quantum
  circuit with `| ||H psi_i|| - lambda_i | <= gamma(eps)||H||`. The
  `gamma(eps)`-approximation problem for such instances is in NP.
- **(AE2)** For `eps<1/2`, approximating such instances within `gamma(eps)` is
  NP-hard. Deleting terms into bounded components, as for lattices, therefore does
  not make this regime trivial.

**Bearing on the root.** A commuting QMA-hard family at relative promise gap
`gamma` must have expansion error `eps>=gamma/(2kd)`, unless QMA is in NP. The
NLTS Hamiltonians of `nlts-hamiltonians-from-good-qldpc-codes` are commuting and
have no constant-depth low-energy states, so their incidence graphs are not in
this regime at small `eps`. The source adds that the smaller the expansion
error, "the weaker the qPCP theorem using these CLHs becomes".
