Augmentation is a unital star homomorphism. Hence it sends a projection to a
projection in `C`, proving `epsilon(q) in {0,1}`. If the value is one, its
restriction to `qC[Gamma]q` is multiplicative, star preserving, and sends
the corner unit `q` to one. The formula for `p_K` has coefficient sum one.
