This is the theorem of Mihai Pimsner, *Embedding some transformation group
C*-algebras into AF-algebras*, Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 3 (1983),
613--626, DOI 10.1017/S0143385700002182.  Pimsner proves that the dynamical
pseudo-non-wandering condition is equivalent to AF-embeddability.  In the
open-set formulation used by Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White immediately before
Problem XI, this is exactly condition 4, and their displayed theorem records
the equivalence with quasidiagonality and stable finiteness.

The original publisher record was checked on 30 August 2026; its abstract
states the AF-embedding/pseudo-non-wandering equivalence.  The four-part
formulation is also printed verbatim as the Pimsner theorem in the supplied
source of *Nuclear C*-algebras: 99 problems*, lines 449--456 of
`Questions-arXiv-Revise.tex`.
