Section 2; independent re-derivation requested from `w3-vf-linear`).

**The group.**
* Let `z(n) = nu_2(n) mod 2` for `n != 0`, with `z(0) = 0`, and let `X` be the orbit closure of
  `z` in `{0,1}^Z`.
* Put `A_X = C(X, F_2) x| Z`, generated by the shift and one cylinder function, and
  `H = EL_3(A_X)`.

**Statement.**
1. `H` is finitely generated and has property (T).
2. `H` has no nontrivial homomorphism into `GL_d(K)`, for any `d` and any field `K`. The odometer
   phases of `X` put `M_(2^j)(F_2)` unitally inside `A_X`, so `SL_(3·2^j)(F_2) <= H` for every `j`.
   A nontrivial representation of `SL_N(F_2)` has degree at least `floor(N/2)`. So every
   representation kills `x_12(1)`, which normally generates `H`.
3. `A_X` has a unital rank model over `F_2`, from periodic approximations of `z`. So `H` has a
   characteristic-two rank model with `rk((x_23(1) - 1)(x_12(1) - 1)) = 2/9`.
4. In the natural model the relative commutant contains nested idempotents of every normalized rank
   in `[0,1]`.
5. For `e = chi_(C^1_0)`, the corner pair `x_12(e)`, `x_23(e)` has the Kazhdan subgroup
   `EL_3((1-e) A_X (1-e))` in its centralizer.
   * **Fixed piece.** In the tensor-square model that subgroup fixes the pair's defect piece
     pointwise. The piece has normalized rank `1/18`.
   * **Globality fails.** The piece is not fixed by `x_12(e)`.

**Consequence.** Absence of finite-dimensional representations, even with property (T), does not
round almost-invariant pieces, does not make relative commutants atomic, and does not force the
two-root identity. So input (ii) of the rescue list in
`research/artifacts/two-root-identity-nonsofic-mechanism-2026-09-12.md` (Section 3, step 2) can
only matter through what `R^x` has and this group lacks:
* a coefficient ring with no rank model;
* the compression relation;
* fixed-ideal globality. `leavitt-two-root-defect-piece-carries-a-nontrivial-rank-model` shows that
  globality in degree `(1,0)` is already used in full.
