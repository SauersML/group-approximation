A finite strongly self-absorbing `D` is unital, separable, nuclear, and
stably finite (finiteness passes to matrix amplifications by Kirchberg's
dichotomy for strongly self-absorbing algebras: `D` is either purely
infinite or stably finite; STW note part 1 "is a special case of Problem
VII" this way).  So a positive answer to the Blackadar--Kirchberg problem
(every separable nuclear stably finite C*-algebra is quasidiagonal) gives
quasidiagonality of `D` directly.
