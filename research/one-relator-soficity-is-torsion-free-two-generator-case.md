The following statements are equivalent.

1. Every one-relator group is sofic.
2. Every two-generator one-relator group is sofic.
3. Every torsion-free two-generator one-relator group is sofic.

More precisely, every finitely generated torsion-free one-relator group
embeds in a torsion-free two-generator one-relator group. Consequently, a
nonsofic one-relator group exists if and only if a nonsofic torsion-free
two-generator one-relator group exists.
