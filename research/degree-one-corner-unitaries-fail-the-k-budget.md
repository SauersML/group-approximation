**ESTABLISHED** through `degree-one-corner-unitaries-fail-the-k-budget-proof`. Refereed: gq-referee-a FAIL at one step of item 1
(report e30cde7e0), repaired here by requiring `w in e R e`; gq-referee-b PASS, citation lens (report c3e534995,
W1 applied to the title). Elementary, given the graded and symbol nodes and Morita invariance of `K`-theory for full corners. No
Pimsner sequence is used. No priority is claimed.

**Statement.** Let `R` be a `Z`-graded unital ring with `Q ⊆ R`. Let `e in R_0` be a full idempotent (`R e R = R`;
this is automatic for `e != 0` when `R` is simple), and `w in e R_1 e`, `w' in e R_(-1) e` with `w' w = e = w w'`. Any `w, w'` with `w' w = e = w w'` can be
replaced by `e w e, e w' e`, which satisfy the same identities (gq-referee-a, e30cde7e0). Without `w in e R e`, the
element `w + 1 - e` need not be a unit: in `M_2(Q)[x^±1]` with `e = E_11`, `w = x`, `w' = E_11 x^-1`, it is
`diag(x, x + 1)`.
1. **Corner Laurent ring.** `e R e` is graded with degree-zero part `e R_0 e` and the unit `w` in degree `1`, so
   `e R e = (e R_0 e)[w^±1; Ad w]`. `u_e = w + 1 - e` is a unit of `R` commuting with `Q`.
2. **Dichotomy.** Either `Λ_e = {λ in Q^x : [λ e + 1 - e] = 0 in K_1(R)}` is not finitely generated, or the symbols
   `{λ, u_e}`, `λ in Q^x`, generate a non-finitely generated subgroup of `K_2(R)`. In the second case no `E_N(R)`,
   `N >= 3`, is finitely presented, since these are Steinberg symbols of commuting units of `R`.
3. **Unit class.** If `t[1_R] = n[e]` in `K_0(R)` for integers `t >= 1` and `n != 0`, the first case forces
   `ker(Q^x -> K_1(R))` to be non-finitely generated.
4. **Conclusion.** If `[1_R]` is a nonzero rational multiple of `[e]` in `K_0(R) ⊗ Q`, then `R` fails the scalar
   K-budget: `K_1` detection fails, or no `E_N(R)`, `N >= 3`, is finitely presented.

## Frame designs

Let `R` be generated by a base `B ⊇ Q` and an isometric frame `s t = 1`, `t s = p in M_k(B)` with `t B s ⊆ M_k(B)`, graded
as in `isometric-frames-over-rank-one-bases-fail-the-k-budget`, item 4, and simple. Put `φ(b) = t b s` and
`A = φ_*` on `K_0(B)`.
5. **Fixed idempotents give corner unitaries.** If `e in B` is an idempotent and `φ(e)` is equivalent to `e` in
   `M_k(B)`, then `w = s x` and `w' = y t` satisfy the hypotheses. Here `x y = φ(e)` and `y x = e`.
6. **The fixed relation holds unconditionally.** The map `K_0(B) -> K_0(R)` kills `(1 - A) K_0(B)`, because
   `φ(g) = t g s` is equivalent to `g` over `R`. So `t[1_B] - n[e] in (1 - A) K_0(B)` gives `t[1_R] = n[e]`.
7. **Multi-type matricial bases.** Let `B` be a finite product of `U_Q`-type rings, so `K_0(B) = Q^m`, and let `A`
   be the nonnegative rational matrix of `φ_*`.
   - (a) If `[1_B] in (1 - A) Q^m`, then `[1_R]` is torsion and `K_1` detection fails.
   - (b) Otherwise, suppose `ker(1 - A)` contains a nonzero vector `v >= 0` with `[1_B] in Q v + (1 - A) Q^m`.
     Then items 5, 6 and 4 apply, and `R` fails the budget.
   - (c) In particular, (a) or (b) covers every design in which `dim coker(1 - A) = 1` and `ker(1 - A)` contains a
     nonzero vector `v >= 0` outside `(1 - A) Q^m`. By Perron--Frobenius, this includes every irreducible `A` with
     spectral radius `1`.
   No Pimsner sequence and no resolvent vacuum is used, so this holds for the widened gate: finitely presented and
   simple, `Q̄ ⊆ R` unitally, and the K-budget.

## Answer to the parked question (gate Attempt 10)

In case (b) the forced `K_2` classes are Steinberg symbols `{λ, u_e}` of units of `R`. So they lie in the image of
`K_2(N, R)` for every `N >= 3`, and the answer is **yes**.

**Open:** `ker(1 - A)` meets the nonnegative cone only in `0` while `[1_B] ∉ (1 - A) Q^m`.
- Example: `A = [[2,1],[2,3]]`. Its eigenvalues are `1` and `4`, and the fixed vectors are `Q(1,-1)`.
- The left fixed functional `(2,-1)` is nonzero on `[1_B] = (1,1)`, so `K_1` detection is not excluded.
- No idempotent is fixed, and whether the lifts of `λ ⊗ (1,-1)` are symbols of units of `R` is not decided.

Also open: rank at least `2` of `coker(1 - A)` without the span condition, and ungraded simple quotients.
