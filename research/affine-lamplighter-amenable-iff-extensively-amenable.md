**ESTABLISHED (citation).** Let a group `G` act transitively on a set `X`, and let `P_f(X)` be the set of
finite subsets of `X`. The action is *extensively amenable* if some `G`-invariant mean on `P_f(X)` gives full
weight to the subsets containing any given finite set. The following are equivalent:
- the action `G ↷ X` is extensively amenable;
- the affine action of the permutational wreath product `P_f(X) ⋊ G` on `P_f(X)`, `(A,g)·E = A Δ gE`, has
  an invariant mean.

Moreover, every action of an amenable group is extensively amenable, and every extensively amenable
action on a nonempty set is amenable.
