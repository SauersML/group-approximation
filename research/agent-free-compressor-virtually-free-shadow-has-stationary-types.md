Let `A<=B` be finite groups and put

```text
C=B times A times C_2.
```

Write `C_0,C_1` for two copies of `C`, let `L=C_0*C_1`, and let `z` be the
nonidentity element of the last factor of `C`.  There are an epimorphism

```text
theta:L->B
```

and an injective proper endomorphism `alpha:L->L` such that

```text
theta(alpha(L))=A.                                      (VFS1)
```

Thus every finite inclusion is already a quotient shadow of a proper
self-compressor whose base is virtually free.

This strengthening does **not** improve carrier localization.  In the
ascending HNN extension

```text
E=<L,t | t l t^(-1)=alpha(l), l in L>,                 (VFS2)
```

every finite-dimensional unitary representation `pi:C->U(V)` extends to a
representation of `E` by identifying the two free factors and taking
`t=1`.  Consequently every irreducible `C`-type, including every type on
which either quotient kernel acts nontrivially, occurs in an exact
zero-defect model of the complete self-compressor.

In particular, same-dimension or flexible HS exactification of the virtually
free base cannot imply that the `theta`-kernel vanishes on a selected
carrier.  The quotient pair `B,A` exists only as an external shadow of
`theta`; the HNN relations themselves retain the full stationary `C`-type
simplex.  Any use of `(VFS1)` in the nonhyperlinear program still needs a
payload relation which excludes these stationary extensions on the
forbidden carrier.
