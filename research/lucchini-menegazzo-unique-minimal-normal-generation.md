**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** If `G` is a noncyclic finite group with a unique minimal normal
subgroup `N`, then `d(G) = max(2, d(G/N))`, where `d` is the minimal number of generators.

Lucchini--Menegazzo, *Generators for finite groups with a unique minimal normal subgroup*, Rend. Sem.
Mat. Univ. Padova 98 (1997), 173--191, Theorem 1.1.
