OPEN. Let `X⊆\{a,b\}^Z` be an infinite minimal subshift, `R=LC(X,F_2)⋊Z` and
`G_X=EL_3(R)`. The question is whether `G_X` is generated by two elements.
- Three elements suffice (`unit-idempotent-elementary-group-has-three-torsion-generators`).
- The most striking form would be two elements of orders 2 and 3. Then every `G_X` would be a quotient of `PSL_2(Z)`.

## Attempts

**(A1) Coprime separation.** If `x=ab` with `ab=ba` and coprime orders, then `<x>` contains both `a` and `b`. So one generator could carry two of the three pieces `c` (order 7), `t` (order 2) and `w` (order 2). This dies on centralizers:
- `GL_3(F_2)` has no elements of order 6 or 14. So no constant element of odd order commutes with a constant involution, and the centralizer of a transvection is a 2-group.
- `C(w)∩EL_3(F_2[p])=1` and `C(m')∩EL_3(F_2[p])=1`, where `m'=diag(u,1,1)Pdiag(u^{-1},1,1)`. Entry by entry, commuting with `w` or `m'` forces a coefficient in `F_2[p]` to equal a `u`-translate of another coefficient.
- Scalar units `vI` commute with `c`. If `v` is a permutation unit of finite order (a full-group element with finite orbits), conjugating `F_2[p]` by `vI` only produces locally constant functions, never `u`.
- Unipotent scalars `(1+r)I`, `r=ab`, `ba=0` (e.g. `r=(1+p)up`), lie in `EL_3` by `[e_12(a),e_21(b)]=diag(1+ab,1)` and add only the partial isometry `r` to the coefficient ring.

**(A2) (2,3)-generation through a constant 3-cycle.** `<P,y>` for the constant 3-cycle `P` and an involution `y∈EL_3(F_2[p])` is never `EL_3(F_2[p])`.
- Every involution in `GL_3(F_2)` is a transvection.
- `P` fixes the vector `(1,1,1)` and preserves the plane `x_1+x_2+x_3=0`. A transvection `x↦x+f(x)v` generating `SL_3(F_2)` together with `P` must do neither. That forces `v` and `f` to have odd weight, `f≠x_1+x_2+x_3` and `f(v)=0`. Only the six root transvections `e_ij(1)` qualify.
- Those six form one orbit of the stabilizer of `P` in `Aut(SL_3(F_2))`, generated by `Ad(P)` and inverse transpose. So Goursat's lemma gives a graph subgroup.
- Also `<P,I+αE_12+βE_13>⊆EL_3(F_2⟨α,β⟩)`, and a single root coefficient generates a commutative ring.

**(A3) Exact search.** Breadth-first search in `EL_3` over the free ring `F_2⟨u^{±1},p | p^2=p⟩`: MSI, script `search23.py` in the sk-generators lane, size cap 24 monomials per matrix, 250k elements.
- Sanity: `(c,t)` gave all 28224 elements of `SL_3(F_2)^2`.
- `(m',t)` and `(m',e_12(1)e_13(p))` closed at 48 and 210 elements (apparently finite; not proved).
- `(m',e_12(p))` reached the 250k cap and `((1+p)c+pe_12(1), w)` closed at 282 elements. Neither produced a root with coefficient `u` or `u^{-1}`.

**(A4) Tower-rotation partners exist** (sk-two-generators, proved in the artifact). Let `v` be an order-3 rotation of three disjoint tower levels. Then `vI`, `diag(1,v,1)` and `diag(1,1,v)` lie in `EL_3(R)`, by `e_12(α)e_21(β)e_12(α)e_21(β)=diag(1+αβ,1,1)` for `βα=0`.
- `vI` commutes with `c`, and `diag(1,1,v)` commutes with `w`.
- If the levels lie in `[a]`, then `diag(1,v,1)` commutes with `t`.

**(A5) Every tower pairing loses an essential generator.**
- `<c·vI, w·diag(1,1,v')>` is ring-deficient: its diagonal coefficients come only from the tower levels.
- `<t·diag(1,v',1), w·diag(1,1,v)>` lies in the parabolic subgroup for `\{1,2\}|\{3\}`.
- `<t·diag(1,v',1), c·vI>` has no coefficient `u`.

**(A6) Finite models.** In GAP (MSI) over the Fibonacci models with d = 15, 24, 39, `<c,t,w>` is absolutely irreducible with primitive-prime-divisor elements, while all three pairings in A5 are reducible. This is heuristic only.

Unproved routes worth trying:
- a partner of odd order in `C(w)` or `C(t)` with nonzero entries in both off-diagonal blocks, for example `diag(u,1,1)·[[A,0,B],[0,A,B],[C,C,D]]·diag(u^{-1},1,1)` with tower-unit blocks, whose words with the other pieces produce a constant of order 7;
- a general 2-generation theorem for elementary groups over rings generated by a unit and an idempotent.
- Value for the note is low: 2-generated infinite simple Kazhdan groups already exist (Coulon–Fournier-Facio, arXiv:2312.11684, Thm 1.5).
