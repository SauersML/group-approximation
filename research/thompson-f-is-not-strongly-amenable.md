**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Y. Hartman, K. Juschenko, O. Tamuz and P. Vahidi
Ferdowsi, *Thompson's group F is not strongly amenable*, arXiv:1607.04915v2
(27 Apr 2017), Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems (accepted). Abstract, verbatim:

> We show that Thompson's group F has a topological action on a compact metric
> space that is proximal and has no fixed points.

A group is strongly amenable (Glasner) when every proximal action on a compact
space has a fixed point. So `F` is not strongly amenable.

**Consequence for the amenability roots.** This decides neither root. It rules
out any argument for amenability that goes through strong amenability.
