Let D be separable, simple, stably finite, pure, and real rank zero.
For every second-countable locally compact Hausdorff X, every
projection p and arbitrary positive sections a,b in the stabilized
section algebra satisfy

```text
[p]+[a] <= [p]+[b]  implies  [a]<=[b]
```

in Cu(C_0(X,D)). The same holds with D tensor Z in place of D.
No stable-rank-one assertion about either section algebra is used.

For compact metrizable X, the actual first-factor Cu image is closed
under removing compact summands: if Phi(x)=k+y and k is compact in
the target, then y is in the image. The proof lifts k to a source
projection, uses order reflection to extract it as an actual compact
source submodule, and cancels its image in the target.

Consequently the universal two-frame Gram class [G_t]=[P]+[a] is
in the image exactly when [a] is. This sharpens the realization
reduction but does not realize arbitrary mixed target classes.
