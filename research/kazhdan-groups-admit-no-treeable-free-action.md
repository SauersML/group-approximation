**ESTABLISHED (import).** Every infinite countable group with Kazhdan property (T) is non-treeable: none of
its free p.m.p. actions has a treeing of its orbit equivalence relation.

Adams–Spatzier (1990), as stated in Gaboriau's lecture notes (3 October 2025), Theorem 2.22. The verbatim
text is in `kazhdan-groups-admit-no-treeable-free-action-citation`.
