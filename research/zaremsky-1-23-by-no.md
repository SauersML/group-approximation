title: Zaremsky Problem 1.23 is answered negatively by a RAAG kernel with smaller Dehn function than the Bestvina–Brady group

The required claim exhibits a finite graph `Γ` and a surjection
`χ : A_Γ → Z` with finitely presented kernel `K_χ` such that
`δ_{K_χ} ≃ n^2` and `δ_{BB_Γ} ≃ n^3`. Since `n^3 ⋠ n^2`, the Dehn function of
`BB_Γ` is not a lower bound for the Dehn functions of all finitely presented
kernels of maps `A_Γ → Z`, so the answer to Problem 1.23 is no.
