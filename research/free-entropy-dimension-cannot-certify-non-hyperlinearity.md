**OPEN.** A drafted direct proof is recorded as an attempt (see Attempts). It is not established because one
referee lens refuted it on 2026-09-17: items 1--3 were checked and hold, but the Consequence paragraph states a
universal kill of every free-entropy-dimension certificate that items 1--3 do not prove.

Let `Γ` be an infinite finitely generated group,
`X = (X_1,...,X_n)` a self-adjoint tuple generating the group algebra `CΓ`,
`UΓ` the algebra of operators affiliated with `LΓ`, and `dim` Lück's dimension
over `LΓ`. Put

```text
a(Γ) = dim ( Z^1(Γ; CΓ) · LΓ  inside  Z^1(Γ; UΓ) ),                  (FED0)
```

the dimension of the `LΓ`-module generated by the algebraic cocycles.

1. **(Unconditional window.)** For every such `Γ`, hyperlinear or not,

   ```text
   1 <= a(Γ) <= dim Z^1(Γ; UΓ) = β_1^(2)(Γ) + 1 = δ*(X).               (FED1)
   ```

2. **(Microstates sit inside it.)** If `Γ` is hyperlinear, then
   `a(Γ) <= δ_0(X) <= δ*(X)`.

3. **(Torsion does not narrow it.)** If `Γ` is generated by `g_1,...,g_m`
   of finite orders `n_1,...,n_m`, then `β_1^(2)(Γ) + 1 <= Σ_i (1 - 1/n_i)`.
   For exponent dividing `N` and `m >= 2` this ceiling `m(1 - 1/N)` is still
   `>= 1`, the floor.

**Consequence (the kill).** A proof of non-hyperlinearity through `δ_0`
compares a floor with a ceiling and needs the floor to exceed the ceiling.
For the published floor (algebraic cocycles, Shlyakhtenko's Theorem 3,
which contains Jung's `δ_0 >= 1`) and the published ceiling (`δ_0 <= δ*`
with the Mineyev--Shlyakhtenko value), that comparison fails for *every*
infinite finitely generated group. It fails at a single step, the inclusion
`Z^1(Γ; CΓ) ⊂ Z^1(Γ; UΓ)` together with monotonicity of Lück dimension.
Adding the exponent laws of a Burnside-type group changes only `β_1^(2)`,
and by item 3 it cannot move the ceiling below `1`.

So a `δ_0` certificate must bring a new floor mechanism, one not bounded by
the affiliated-cocycle dimension. By item 2, any such floor is false on every
hyperlinear group. Its proof would therefore already be a proof that the
microstate spaces are empty, with free entropy dimension contributing only
vocabulary. The invariant is `δ_0`, and the step where every member dies is
"floor > ceiling".
