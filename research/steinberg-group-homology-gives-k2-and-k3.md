**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`steinberg-group-homology-gives-k2-and-k3-citation`). For every associative unital ring `R`:
- (i) `St(R) → E(R)` is the universal central extension, with kernel `K_2(R)`, so `K_2(R) ≅ H_2(E(R);Z)`;
- (ii) for `n ≥ 5`, every central extension of `St_n(R)` splits, so `St_n(R)` is the universal central extension of `E_n(R)`;
- (iii) `K_3(R) ≅ H_3(St(R);Z)`.

The groups `K_n` here are Quillen's `π_n BGL(R)^+`, which agree with the classical `K_2`.
