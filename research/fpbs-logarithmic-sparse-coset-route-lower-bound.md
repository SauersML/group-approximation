Section 8, Proposition 3. If H is proper, a is outside H, and an invariant original-edge graph has external intensity delta and d_J(o,a)<=R almost surely, then 1<=delta |B_G(o,R)|. The degree bound forces R=Omega_G(log(1/delta)). This is not an action-cost lower bound and does not apply when H=Gamma.
