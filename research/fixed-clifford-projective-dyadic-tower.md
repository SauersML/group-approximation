For `a>=2`, put

```text
G_a=SL_2(Z/2^a),
L_a=ker(G_a->G_(a-1)).                                  (FCT1)
```

Then `L_a` is the additive group `sl_2(F_2)`. Write

```text
X(A,B,C)=[[A,B],[C,A]].
```

The dual functional

```text
ell_0(A,B,C)=A+B+C                                    (FCT2)
```

is fixed by the full conjugation action of `G_a`, which factors through
`G_1=SL_2(F_2)=S_3`. Let `chi_a` be the corresponding nontrivial character
of `L_a`.

The inertia group of `chi_a` is all of `G_a`. Consequently Clifford theory
gives an equivalence

```text
Irr(G_a | chi_a)
  <-> Irr_(omega_a)(G_(a-1)),                           (FCT3)
```

where the right side is the set of irreducible projective representations
of `G_(a-1)` for the extension multiplier `omega_a`. In particular the fixed
newest-layer orbit is not one finite Clifford label: it retains the complete
projective representation theory of the preceding dyadic quotient. Its
types, degrees and stabilizer multiplicities are therefore data of that full
twisted group algebra, not data determined by the four newest-layer orbits.

This sector has a deterministic anisotropic Iwahori shift. If `pi` lies over
`chi_a`, every irreducible constituent `theta` of its restriction to the
upper abstract Iwahori `B` satisfies

```text
n(theta)=a,                 m(theta)=a+1,              (FCT4)
```

for the upper/opposite conductors from
`pure-dyadic-regular-iwahori-mixtures-have-a-uniform-half-loss`.

Indeed `L_a` lies in `N_(a-1)` and `chi_a` is nontrivial, so the upper
conductor is `a`. Modulo `N_a`, the quotient `M_aN_a/N_a` is generated by
the upper-root class `X(0,1,0)`. Formula `(FCT2)` is nonzero on that class,
so `M_a` is not in the kernel. But `M_(a+1) subset N_a`, proving the opposite
conductor is exactly `a+1`.

Therefore the upper/opposite selected-irrep overlay cannot be computed by a
depth-independent finite transition matrix on the four newest-layer orbit
labels, even after adding orbit sizes. One off-diagonal transition already
carries the full twisted category `Rep_(omega_a)(G_(a-1))`. The remaining
weighted-Cheeger problem is precisely whether the successive projective
inertia categories in `(FCT3)`, with their restriction multiplicities under
the opposite embedding, have a uniform boundary or admit a Følner sequence.
Neither the spatial Bruhat--Tits norm nor the regular Plancherel table decides
that recursive twisted-category question.
