title: Tensoring a two-infinite algebra with the CAR algebra cannot leave its unit finite
distinct_from:
  stw99-lxvi-finite-car-corner-with-infinite-double: that asks for a non-tensorial unital CAR copy inside a finite algebra with infinite double; this proves why tensoring an existing finite non-stably-finite algebra by CAR cannot construct it.
---

Let `A` be a unital C\*-algebra.  If the unit of `M_2(A)` is infinite, then
the unit of

```text
A tensor_min M_(2^infinity)
```

is infinite.  Consequently, starting with a finite simple non-stably-finite
algebra and tensoring it with the CAR algebra can never produce the finite
CAR-containing seed required by Problem LXVI.
