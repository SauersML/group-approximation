## Why sufficient

Fix `B` and `phi,psi:Z->B`.  Approximate uniqueness gives approximate unitary
equivalence.  The universal central path hinge gives the quantitative path
property for `phi`.  The established upgrade theorem then constructs the
asymptotic implementing path by contracting and concatenating successive
transition unitaries.
