Full derivation: section 2 of the artifact. Outline:

- **Item 1.** `Ad pi_n` is a genuine unitary representation of `Gamma` on `L^2(M_n')`
  with fixed space `A_n`. A lift `z_n` of a contraction in `sigma(Gamma)' cap M` has
  `||[z_n, pi_n(s)]||_2 -> 0`. Kazhdan's inequality then gives
  `||z_n - E_(A_n) z_n||_2 <= kappa^(-1) max_s ||[pi_n(s), z_n]||_2`, and `E_(A_n) z_n` is a
  contraction in `A_n`.
  - The reverse inclusion is `||[x, sigma_n(s)]||_2 <= 2 ||sigma_n(s) - pi_n(s)||_2`.
  - For `Lambda`: it is generated by `t s t^(-1)`, and each of these is a fixed word in
    `F`. Asymptotic multiplicativity transfers (H1) to these generators, and
    `(F_Lambda, kappa)` is a Kazhdan pair because `Ad t^(-1)` is an isomorphism onto
    `Gamma`.
- **Item 2.** If `eps_n` stays bounded below on a `U`-large set, pick contractions
  `x_n in B_n` far from `A_n`. The element `(x_n)` lies in `sigma(Lambda)'`. It is not in
  `prod_U A_n`, because `E_(A_n)` is the `L^2`-orthogonal projection onto `A_n`. The
  converse direction is immediate.
- **Item 3.**
  - Let `rho = pi_n (+) pi~_n` on `C^(2n')`. The lower-left unit block `w` is almost
    invariant under `Ad rho`, since `||pi~_n(lambda_s) - pi_n(lambda_s)||_2 -> 0`.
  - The trace-preserving expectation onto `rho(Lambda)'` is contractive and is the
    projection onto the fixed vectors. It gives `y` with `||y|| <= 1` and
    `||y - w||_2 -> 0`.
  - `x = e_22 y e_11` intertwines `pi_n` with `pi~_n`, has `||x|| <= 1`, and satisfies
    `||x - 1||_2 -> 0`.
  - The polar part `v` intertwines, and
    `||v - 1||_2 <= ||1 - x^* x||_1^(1/2) + ||x - 1||_2`.
- **Item 4.**
  - `v` restricts `pi_n|Lambda` to `v^* v`, and the restricted representation is equivalent
    to `pi~_n|vv^*`.
  - The aspect ratio of `pi~_n` is `sigma_n(t) zeta_A sigma_n(t)^*`.
  - Restricting to `v^* v` lowers the multiplicity of type `j` from `m'_j` to `r_j`.
    Types with `r_j < m'_j/(1+c)` carry mass at most `((1+c)/c) tau(1 - v^* v)`.
  - `|F_a(lambda x) - F_a(x)| <= (1/4) log lambda` for all `a > 0`, and
    `||v^* y v - y||_2 <= 2 ||v - 1||_2` for contractions `y`.
  - Together these give the uniform estimate. Let `c -> 0` slowly along `U`.
