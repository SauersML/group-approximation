**Established by citation.**  Robert--Tikuisis (TAMS, Thm 3.1):
finite nuclear dimension gives `(m, m')`-pureness.
Antoine--Perera--Thiel--Vilalta (arXiv APTV24, extending
Antoine--Perera--Robert--Thiel Theorem D to the non-simple setting):
`(m, m')`-pure implies pure.  Both are quoted in exactly this role by
STW around Problems XXVIII and LXXV, and by Vilalta's Global Glimm
survey (Thm C attribution: finite nuclear dimension + Glimm halving
gives pureness — the halving hypothesis being dischargeable in the
nowhere scattered setting at stake in LXXV via the survey's Thm 5.2
weak-divisibility characterization... the import here is the
conjunction as STW themselves state it: locally finite nuclear
dimension machinery makes (i) ⟹ pureness available nonsimply).
