Fix trivial `C` coordinates `i,k` in the `h=J^2Z`-fixed block and
choose `1<=m<=6` intermediate coordinates `j_1,...,j_m` in the
nontrivial `C`-type killed by `h`. For every `a` and `r=0,1` put

```text
U_(a,r)=x_(i,j_a)(s_r),       V_(a,r)=x_(j_a,k)(t_r).
```

The full-Cuntz returns do not depend on the intermediate coordinate:

```text
[U_(a,r),V_(a,s)]
 =x_(ik)(s_rt_s)
 =[U_(b,r),V_(b,s)]                                  (QPC1)
```

for all `a,b,r,s`. Roots belonging to distinct intermediate coordinates
commute. Hence the group `M_m` generated by these `4m` roots has the
unique normal form

```text
X((u_a)) Y((v_a)) Z(w),
u_a in span{s_0,s_1}, v_a in span{t_0,t_1},
w in span{s_rt_s},                                      (QPC2)
```

and therefore

```text
|M_m|=2^(2m+2m+4)=2^(4m+4).                             (QPC3)
```

Let `Sigma_m<=K=GL_28(F_2)` permute the intermediate coordinates and
fix `i,k`. It acts on `M_m` by permuting the cells, so

```text
B_m=M_m semidirect Sigma_m
```

is finite of order `2^(4m+4)m!`. Its scalar intersection is

```text
B_m intersect K=<A> times Sigma_m,
A=x_(ik)(1).                                            (QPC4)
```

All literal parallel-cell relations -- the common-return equations
`(QPC1)`, cross-cell commutation, and scalar permutation covariance --
therefore have the finite-vertex cover

```text
K *_(<h,A> times Sigma_m) (<h> times B_m)
       -> EL_28(L_(F_2)(1,2)).                           (QPC5)
```

Every active root still has the complete three-by-three `h`-fibre
qutrit graph with squared block norm `1/27`. The cover is virtually
free and has exact regular microstates retaining all nine character
weights `1/9` and the scalar return `A`.

Thus the first explicit kernel relations between distinct
`K`-conjugates of the 256-cell do not create a normalized-HS trace
imbalance, even with their complete finite covariance group. The
firewall holds for every fixed finite number of parallel intermediate
coordinates. A load-bearing relation must make the cell incidence
non-parallel -- for example, reuse an output coordinate as the next
cell's input so that a nonconstant return is fed back into a coefficient
root -- or impose a genuinely noninjective quotient not absorbed by a
finite covariant vertex.
