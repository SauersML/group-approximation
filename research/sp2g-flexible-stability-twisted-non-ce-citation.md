Established by citation plus a subsequence argument.  A. Dogon, *Flexible
Hilbert-Schmidt stability versus hyperlinearity for property (T) groups*,
arXiv:2211.10492v3 (25 Aug 2023), Theorem 3.1 (p. 12).  The statement is
quoted verbatim in the target node; it was read from the PDF text
extracted on MSI on 2026-09-12
(`/scratch.global/sauer354/nh-dogon/2211.10492.txt`).

The subsequence step: conditions (1) and (2) pass to subsequences, so if
infinitely many twisted algebras were Connes embeddable, the subsequence
would meet all three hypotheses of Theorem 3.1 and contradict weak
ucp-stability.  The required claim supplies the implication from flexible
HS-stability to weak ucp-stability (its immediate direction).
