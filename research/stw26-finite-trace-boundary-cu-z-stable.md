Let `A` be a unital simple separable stably finite nuclear non-elementary
C-star algebra.  Assume that the extreme boundary of `T(A)` is nonempty,
compact, and finite-dimensional.  If

```text
Cu(A) is isomorphic to Cu(A tensor Z),
```

then `A` is `Z`-stable.  Thus STW Problem XXVI has a positive answer on
every compact finite-dimensional extremal-trace-boundary stratum.
