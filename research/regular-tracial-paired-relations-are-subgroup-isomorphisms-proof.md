The first prerequisite gives

```text
ker p_1=ker p_2
```

for the subgroup `K` generated by the exact relation pairs.  The second
prerequisite says that equality of the coordinate kernels is equivalent to
`K` being the graph of an isomorphism between its two coordinate images.
Because the displayed generator pairs belong to `K`, that isomorphism sends
each `a_s` to the corresponding `b_s`, proving `(RPI1)` and all stated
consequences.
