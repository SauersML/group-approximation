The manuscript gives the complete argument. On S1 times S^(2n+1)
times a product of projective spaces, relative K-theory and integral
Bott normalization force every positive Chern coefficient of the
mapping-torus difference class to be divisible by n!. The coefficient
formula is n! times binomial(|b|+n,n) times multinomial(|b|;b).

Degree-k sphere maps yield complement bundles clutched by the kth
power of the characteristic unitary, by the boundary homomorphism of
U(n)->U(n+1)->S^(2n+1). Their zero-count obstruction is ±k. This proves
exact factorial order after every line-twisted addition. Dense-tail
point evaluations make the explicit limit simple; the manuscript
proves finiteness, nuclearity, K1 vanishing, and finite-stage detection
of null-homotopies. The n!-power and first matrix stabilization already
contract at the initial stage.
