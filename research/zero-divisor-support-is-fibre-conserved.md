**ESTABLISHED as an obstruction** (proposed; referee pending) through
`zero-divisor-support-is-fibre-conserved-proof`, from
`amenable-finite-cd-domain-groups-are-virtually-solvable`,
`amenable-cd-two-kropholler-iff-locally-indicable` and
`rf-weak-bass-defect-is-degree-two-approximation-defect` (item 3). Recalled imports:
Stallings–Swan; Kropholler–Linnell–Moody (the rational group algebra of a torsion-free
elementary amenable group is a domain); Shapiro's lemma; submodules of free modules over a
PID are free.

**Notation.** For a group `H`, `μ(H) ∈ {4, 5, …} ∪ {∞}` is the least value of
`|supp a| + |supp b|` over pairs of nonzero `a, b ∈ Q[H]` with `ab = 0`. A pair realising it is
*minimal*. A pair is *normalized* if `1 ∈ supp a ∩ supp b`. `H^{(1)}_Q = ⋂ ker φ` over all
`φ ∈ Hom(H, Z)`. The *rational derived core* `H^{(∞)}_Q` is the end of the transfinite series
`H^{(α+1)}_Q = (H^{(α)}_Q)^{(1)}_Q`, with intersections at limit ordinals. A *counterexample* is a
torsion-free amenable group of cd `<= 2` that is not elementary amenable. By the imports, this is
the same as `Q[H]` not being a domain. For finitely generated `J`, `χ(J) = 1 − b_1(J) + b_2(J)`.
A *descent step* `J ⇝ J_1` passes to a finite-index `J' ≤ J`, then to `L = ker(φ : J' ↠ Z)`, then
to a nontrivial finitely generated `J_1 ≤ L` with `b_1(J_1) = 0`. `J` is of *type (I)* if no finite-index
subgroup maps onto `Z`.

1. **Support is conserved on fibres (any group).** Let `φ : H ↠ Z` with kernel `L`.
   - (a) `μ(L) = μ(H)`.
   - (b) Every minimal pair of `H` is concentrated on single cosets: `a = a' t^p` and
     `b = b' t^q` with `a', b' ∈ Q[L]`. A normalized minimal pair therefore lies in `Q[L]`.
   - (c) Every group has normalized minimal pairs. For each one, `J = ⟨supp a ∪ supp b⟩` is
     finitely generated and lies in `H^{(∞)}_Q`. Moreover `μ(J) = μ(H^{(∞)}_Q) = μ(H)` and
     `Hom(J, Z) = 0`.

   So the fibre half of descent is idempotent. Once a minimal pair is fixed, one step reaches a
   finitely generated `J` with `b_1(J) = 0` that lies in every fibre of every character of every
   term of the rational derived series. Further fibre steps do nothing.
2. **The finite-index step (amenable, cd `<= 2`).** Let `J` be a finitely generated
   counterexample and `J' ≤ J` of index `k`.
   - (a) `J'` is a counterexample and `μ(J) <= μ(J') < ∞`.
   - (b) If `b_1(J) = 0` and `b_2(J) < ∞`, then `χ(J') = k χ(J) = k(1 + b_2(J)) >= k`.
   - (c) In the situation of (b), suppose `J'` maps onto `Z` with fibre `L`. Then
     `H_2(L; Q) ≅ Λ^{ρ_2}` is free over `Λ = Q[t^{±1}]`, and
     `ρ_2 − ρ_1 = χ(J') >= k`, where `ρ_i = rank_Λ H_i(L; Q)`.
   - (d) Every `J_1` produced by a descent step from `J` has `cd J_1 = 2`, `b_1(J_1) = 0`,
     `χ(J_1) = 1 + b_2(J_1) ∈ [1, ∞]`, and `μ(J_1) >= μ(J') >= μ(J)`.
3. **Obstruction to termination.** Along any descent chain `J_0 ⇝ J_1 ⇝ ⋯`:
   - `μ` is non-decreasing, and constant on the fibre part of each step;
   - every invariant of the form "infimum of a quantity over zero divisors in `Q[J_i]`" is
     non-decreasing, since `Q[J_{i+1}] ⊂ Q[J_i]`;
   - for `i >= 1`, `cd J_i = 2`, `b_1(J_i) = 0` and `χ(J_i) >= 1`.

   So termination in type (I) cannot come from zero-divisor extremality, cd or `χ`. It needs an
   invariant that strictly drops from `J_i` to subgroups of fibres of its finite-index
   subgroups, a Hirsch length for non-elementary-amenable cd-2 groups. The finite-index step is
   the only non-idempotent step. Restricting a normalized minimal pair of `J` to a proper
   finite-index normal subgroup never gives an element pair: its supports generate `J`, so the
   pair does not lie in `Q[J']`. It gives a `k × k` coset-matrix pair `AB = 0` over `Q[J']` instead,
   and the leading-coefficient argument of item 1 has no matrix analogue: leading matrices such
   as `e_{11} x` and `e_{22} y` multiply to zero and carry no element pair.
4. **Calibrations.**
   - *`D_∞ = Z ⋊ Z/2`* (torsion). `(1 − s)(1 + s) = 0` gives `μ(D_∞) = 4`, but `μ(Z) = ∞` on the
     index-2 subgroup. So the finite-index step can destroy every element zero divisor. Item 2(a)
     is a genuinely cd-2-amenable input, through Degrijse's theorem, not a support estimate.
   - *Hantzsche–Wendt group `HW`* (torsion-free, amenable, cd 3). It is not locally indicable,
     but `b_1(HW) = 0` and `Q[HW]` is a domain. Every finitely generated subgroup is virtually
     `Z^m`, so there is no type (I) subgroup. Its descent exits through a locally indicable fibre
     (`Z^3 ↠ Z` with kernel `Z^2`). In cd 2 this exit is closed: by item 2(a), `μ(J') < ∞`, so by item 1 the fibre carries a
     minimal pair.

**Next step.** Find an invariant that drops strictly on descent steps, or prove that a
finitely generated counterexample `J` generated by a minimal pair is of type (I). The second
alternative means excluding every fibred finite-index `J'`. By item 2(c), when `b_2(J) < ∞`, the
fibre of any such `J'` has free Alexander `H_2` of rank at least `[J:J']`.
