Use the explicit finitely presented group `Gamma_triangle`, its faithful
bilateral linear action, and the nonidentity involution `w=x_13,Q`.
Strict positivity of the four-word infimum forces every norm-corona
homomorphism to kill `w`, so the group cannot embed in such a corona.
The required strict positivity remains open.

The presentation and equivalence use finite group algebra and the cited
algebraic presentation theorem. No property-T proof, renamed spectral
estimate, or theorem depending on such an estimate is accepted for the
remaining matrix inequality.
