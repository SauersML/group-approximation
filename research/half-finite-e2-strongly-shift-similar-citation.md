Citation import from Mallery--Zaremsky, *Houghton-like groups from
"shift-similar" groups*, arXiv:2202.00822v2, accepted version. The TeX source
was fetched on MSI on 2026-09-13 and read at the lines given. Not reproved here.

- **Slide and flip** (Definition "Slide and flip", source lines 748–757):
  "Thus $\slide$ sends each odd number to the next odd number, each even number
  besides 2 to the previous even number, and 2 to 1." and
  "$\flip\coloneqq (1~2)(3~4)(5~6)\cdots$".
- **Observation `obs:E2_fin_gen`** (source lines 809–810): "The group $E_2$ is
  generated by $\slide$, $\flip$, and $(1~2)$. In particular $E_2$ is finitely
  generated."
- **Definition "Half-finite support"** (source lines 817–818): "A permutation
  $g\in \Sym(\N)$ has \emph{half-finite support} if $g(i)=i$ either for all
  sufficiently large even $i$, or for all sufficiently large odd $i$."
- **Proposition `prop:half_fin_plus_E2`** (source lines 839–840): "Let
  $\Gamma\le\Sym(\N)$ be any group generated by permutations with half-finite
  support. Let $G=\langle \Gamma,E_2\rangle$. Then $G$ is strongly
  shift-similar."

The paper's text immediately after the proposition says the same for finitely
many generators: "Let $\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_k$ be any elements of $\Sym(\N)$
with half-finite support. Then $G=\langle \gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_k,E_2\rangle$
is a finitely generated strongly shift-similar group."
