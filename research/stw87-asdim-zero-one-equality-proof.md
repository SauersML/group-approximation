Li--Willett, arXiv:1705.01290, Theorem 2.2, prove that `asdim(X)=0` if and only
if `C*_u(X)` has nuclear dimension zero.  If `asdim(X)=1`, the upper bound gives
nuclear dimension at most one, while nuclear dimension zero would force
`asdim(X)=0` by the same theorem.  Hence it is exactly one.
