Let `H` be residually finite and let

```text
W={w_1,...,w_r} subset H minus {1}.
```

Form a graph on the vertex set `H` by joining distinct `x,y` whenever
`x^(-1)y` is conjugate to some `w_i` or `w_i^(-1)`.  Then this graph has
finite chromatic number.  More precisely, one finite quotient of `H`
separating every `w_i` gives a proper coloring by quotient values.

Consequently a pure self-hashing construction in a free or residually finite
address actor cannot obtain infinite chromaticity from finitely many literal
conjugacy-difference prototypes.  A successful bounded-area chromatic compiler
must use more than this template.
