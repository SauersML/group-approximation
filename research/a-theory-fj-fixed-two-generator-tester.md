Let `FJ_A` be the class of groups satisfying the Farrell--Jones conjecture
for nonconnective Waldhausen A-theory with coefficients and finite wreath
products.

There exists one two-generator finitely presented torsion-free group `E`,
simultaneously nonsofic and non-MF, such that

```text
E belongs to FJ_A
  <=>
every torsion-free group belongs to FJ_A.
```

The group `E` may be chosen as the witness in
[[two-generator-fp-torsion-free-master-tester]].

This is an exact reduction only; it does not prove the remaining membership
assertion for `E` or the global torsion-free A-theoretic conjecture.
