## Setting

- `p` is a prime and `m >= 1` is an integer with `p ∤ m`.
- `I_p(m) = { [[α,β],[c,δ]] ∈ GL_2(Z[1/m]) : c ∈ pZ[1/m] }`.
- `Γ_p(m)` is the image of `I_p(m)` in `PGL_2(Q)`, acting on `Z_p = ∂T_p` by
  Möbius maps.
- `Γ_p`, `V_p(-)` and the charts are as in
  `pgl2-q-in-rover-nekrashevych-group-of-rational-iwahori`.
- `d_u(z) = uz` for `u ∈ Z_(p)^×`, and `τ_x(z) = z + x`.

## Statement

1. **Self-similar.** `Γ_p(m) <= Γ_p`, and every section of an element of
   `Γ_p(m)` lies in `Γ_p(m)`. So `Γ_p(m) <= Aut(T_p)` is a self-similar group.
2. **Finitely presented.** `Γ_p(m)` has finite index in the finitely presented
   group `PGL_2(Z[1/m])`. Finite presentation of `GL_2(Z[1/m])` is imported from
   Borel–Serre (1976, `S`-arithmetic groups), the reference Zaremsky
   arXiv:2405.09722 uses for `GL_n(Z[1/m])`; it is not re-read here.
3. **Not finite-state.** Already `Γ_p(1)` contains `u ↦ u/(1+pu)`, which has
   infinitely many distinct sections (item 4 of
   `pgl2-q-in-rover-nekrashevych-group-of-rational-iwahori`). So no `Γ_p(m)` is
   contracting or lies in a contracting self-similar group.
4. **Exhaustion.** `Γ_p(m) <= Γ_p(m')` whenever `m | m'`, and
   `Γ_p = ⋃_{p ∤ m} Γ_p(m)`.
5. **Generators.**
   `Γ_p = ⟨ Γ_p(1), d_q : q prime, q ≠ p ⟩ = ⟨ Γ_p(1), Aff_1(Z_(p)) ⟩`,
   where `Aff_1(Z_(p)) = {z ↦ uz + x : u ∈ Z_(p)^×, x ∈ Z_(p)}`.
   - `Γ_p(1)` is the image of the Iwahori subgroup of `GL_2(Z)`, a subgroup of
     index `p+1` in `PGL_2(Z)`, hence virtually free and finitely presented.
6. **Hosts for the finitely generated pieces.**
   `PGL_2(Z[1/(pm)]) <= V_p(Γ_p(m))`, and `V_p(Γ_p(m))` is finitely presented by
   Scott's theorem (FP1 of `rover-nekrashevych-finite-presentation-criteria`).

## Reading

- Everything in `Γ_p` that one finitely generated subgroup of `PGL_2(Q)`
  touches already lies in a finitely presented self-similar group `Γ_p(m)`.
  So `rational-iwahori-group-lies-in-fp-self-similar-group` is exactly the
  problem of absorbing all the dilations `d_q`, for infinitely many primes `q`,
  into one finitely presented self-similar group together with the virtually
  free group `Γ_p(1)`.
- The filtration is by denominators prime to `p`. The level shifts of `V_p`
  supply the powers of `p`.
