**ESTABLISHED** (route `mf-radical-contains-hilbert-hotel-elementary-proof`, artifact section 4).

Let `R` be a countable unital ring whose additive group is torsion, for instance any algebra over a
field of positive characteristic, and let `n >= 4`. Then

```text
EL_n(R, hh(R)) <= Rad_MF(EL_n(R)),
```

where `hh(R)` is the Hilbert-hotel radical, the smallest ideal with directly finite quotient, and
`EL_n(R,I)` is the normal closure of the `e_ij(a)` with `a in I`.

So the ring-theoretic obstruction of the non-MF manuscript is not attached to a chosen one-sided
pair: it is attached to a canonical ideal, and it is the whole of that ideal that becomes invisible
to every MF image. The torsion hypothesis enters only through the finite additive order of the
defects; characteristic zero needs a different argument, as the manuscript's own remark after
`cor:one-sided-ring-maximal` records.
