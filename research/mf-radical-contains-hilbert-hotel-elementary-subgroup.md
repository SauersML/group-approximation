**CONDITIONAL / OPEN** (demoted 2026-09-13 after review by `un-verify-open`). The route
`mf-radical-contains-hilbert-hotel-elementary-proof` proves it from the open
`relative-torsion-defect-mf-radical-step`, so it does not fire.

Let `R` be a countable unital ring whose additive group is torsion and let `n >= 4`. Then, conditionally,

```text
EL_n(R, hh(R)) <= Rad_MF(EL_n(R)),
```

where `hh(R)` is the Hilbert-hotel radical, the smallest ideal with directly finite quotient.

**What is unconditional.** The first stage, `EL_n(R, d(R)) <= Rad_MF(EL_n(R))` with `d(R)` the ideal
generated by all defects, is `mf-radical-contains-defect-ideal-elementary-subgroup`. So this claim holds
unconditionally whenever `hh(R) = d(R)`, which is `hilbert-hotel-radical-is-reached-in-one-step`, and in
particular in every calibrating ring known here.
