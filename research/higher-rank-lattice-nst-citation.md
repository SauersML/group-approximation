**Source, verbatim.** Wikipedia, *Lattice (discrete subgroup)*, raw wikitext
fetched on MSI on 2026-09-13 with `action=raw`, line 102:

```text
*Lattices in rank 1 Lie groups have infinite, infinite index [[normal subgroup]]s while all normal subgroups of irreducible lattices in higher rank are either of finite index or contained in their center;{{sfn|Margulis|1991|pp=263-270}}{{sfn|Witte-Morris|2015|loc=Theorem 17.1}}
```

The references cited there are G. A. Margulis, *Discrete Subgroups of Semisimple
Lie Groups* (1991), pp. 263--270, and D. Witte Morris, *Introduction to
Arithmetic Groups* (2015), Theorem 17.1. Neither was re-read.

**Specialization.** `Sp_2n(R)` with `n >= 2` is connected, simple, of real rank
`n`, with finite center `{+1, -1}`. A finite-index subgroup of the arithmetic
lattice `Sp_2n(Z)` is a lattice. A lattice in a simple group is irreducible.
The quoted dichotomy then gives the form stated in the target.
