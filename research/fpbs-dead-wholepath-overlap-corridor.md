Dead: the attempt supplies the adaptive overlapping-path certificates of the capacity theorem for corridor-shaped targets. On the benchmark with m=2^j interfaces of width j squared the paid repair vanishes while the minimum whole-path overlap energy diverges, so every whole-path overlap certificate fails there while cut-based repair succeeds (fpbs-cut-corridor-separation). Related: infinitely many nominal alternatives do not help when they share one indispensable new edge. The capacity target itself is open and is to be attacked with fractional-cut budgets.
