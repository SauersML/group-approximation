Notation of `kms-hc1-ca-edge-defect-is-a-witt-carry-syzygy` (below: the
syzygy claim) and `kms-hc1-witt-carry-syzygy-kassel-coordinate-vanishes`
(below: the Kassel claim). Fix a prime `p >= 5`.

- `A = F_p<<Y_a,Y_b,Y_c>>`, `I` the relator ideal, `rho : A -> u(N)` with
  `N = N_(kappa,alpha)` the truncated Kassel extension.
- `U = u(h)`, the restricted Heisenberg algebra of the ca edge, with PBW
  basis `c^j e^m a^n`. `E` is the kernel of `F<<Y_a,Y_c>> -> U`. It is
  generated by the lowest forms of the ca-edge group relators: the two
  Serre words, `Y_a^p`, `Y_c^p` and `(Y_cY_a - Y_aY_c)^p`.
- `B = U (x) U`, the free `U`-bimodule on `b`, with `x (x) y <-> x b y`.
- `s1 = 2a(x)a - 1(x)a^2 - a^2(x)1` and `s2 = 1(x)c^2 - 2c(x)c + c^2(x)1`
  are the images of the lowest forms of `f_[a,b,a]{210}` and
  `f_[b,c,c]{012}`.
- `K = U s1 U + U s2 U` and `K+ = U s1 U^+ + U s2 U^+`, where `U^+` is the
  augmentation ideal.
- A **syzygy** is a pair `(U1,U2)` in `U` with `U1 a^2 = U2 c^2`. Its value
  is `zeta(U1,U2) = psi(U1 X_210 + U2 X_012)`, with the seeds `X_s` of the
  syzygy claim.

## Statement

> **(F) Right freeness.** `B/K` is a free right `U`-module. More precisely,
> `B/K` is isomorphic to `V (x) U`, with `V = U/(U a^2 + U c^2)`, by
> `ad(u)(b) w <- (u mod (Ua^2+Uc^2)) (x) w`.
>
> **(L) Right-context lift.** For every syzygy `(U1,U2)`, the element
> `R = U1 s1 + U2 s2` of `B` lies in `K+`.
>
> **(Z) Lift to I.** For every syzygy `(U1,U2)` of multidegree
> `(p-2,0,p)`, `(p,0,p-2)`, there is `T` in `I` whose lowest form lies in
> degree `3p` and satisfies `rho(T_low) = zeta(U1,U2) z`. Its component in
> multidegree `(p,p,p)` lies in `gr I`, given Open 1.

**Consequence: (E_p) and the n=1 case of Open 2 for every p >= 5.**

- Take the Witt-carry syzygy `sigma`. By (K) of the Kassel claim,
  `zeta(sigma) = 6 alpha_B`, so by (Z) the element `T_sigma` has row
  `(0,0,6)`.
- By the rotation lemma (T) of the syzygy claim, `tau_A T_sigma` and
  `tau_A^2 T_sigma` lie in `gr I` at `(p,p,p)`, with rows `(-6,-6,-6)` and
  `(0,6,0)`.
- The determinant of the three rows is `108(0-2) = -216`, which is a unit
  for `p >= 5`. So the image `V` of `gr I` in `W` has dimension at least 3.
- By (R1) of `kms-hc1-edge-s-pair-jumps-are-affine-coroot-p-power-relations`,
  `dim W <= 3`, so `V = W`.
- By (R2) there, `gr I = ker(A -> u(L_p))` through degree `3p`, and the
  Zassenhaus quotients of `Gamma^` and `I_1` agree through class `3p`.

This is the `n=1` case of Open 2 of
`kms-hc1-a2-pro-p-completion-is-iwahori-criterion`, for every prime
`p >= 5`. It uses Open 1, which is proved in
`kms-hc1-iwahori-h2-serre-plus-kassel-all-degrees`. It no longer needs the
lift, or the hypothesis that each edge slice has a single visible jump.

## Proof idea

- **(F).** `ad(u)(b) = sum u_(1) b S(u_(2))` is defined in `B`. Since
  `ad(a)^2 b = -s1` and `ad(c)^2 b = s2`, the map `u (x) w -> ad(u)(b) w`
  kills `(Ua^2 + Uc^2) (x) U` modulo `K`. The map
  `x b y -> x.(v0 (x) 1).y` into the smash-product bimodule `V (x) U` kills
  `s1` and `s2` and inverts it.
- **(L).** Apply `- (x)_U F_p` to `0 -> K -> B -> B/K -> 0`. Since `B/K` is
  right-free, `Tor_1^U(B/K, F_p) = 0`, so `K/K+ -> B (x)_U F_p = U` is
  injective. It sends `R` to `-U1 a^2 + U2 c^2 = 0`.
- **(Z).** Write `R = sum u_j s_(i_j) v_j` with `v_j` in `U^+`. Lift it to
  `A`, correct by edge-relator multiples with one `b` in their contexts,
  and subtract. The degree-`(2p+1)` part cancels exactly, and by the torus
  lemma the next degree is `3p`.
- On `m0`, each correction term dies. A term `u_j f1 v_j` dies because
  `v_j m0 = 0`. An edge-relator tail dies because it has `b`-degree 1, not
  `p`. What is left is `U1 X_210 + U2 X_012`.

## Status

- **Proved, every p >= 5:** (F), (L), (Z). Full proof in
  `kms-hc1-witt-carry-syzygy-lifts-by-right-freeness-proof`.
- **Checked exactly** (`lift_check.py`), at `p = 5, 7, 11, 13`:
  - the dimension formula of (F) at `(P,Q) = (p,p)`, and also at
    `(3,4)`, `(2,2)`, `(5,3)` for `p=5` and `(4,6)` for `p=7`;
  - `R` in `K+` for `sigma` and for every `e_k`, `k <= p-4`;
  - two controls (non-syzygy pairs) give `R` outside `K+`.
  At `p=13`, `B` has dimension 4146 in multidegree `(13,13)` and `K+` has
  rank 4036 (`lift_p13.out`).
- **Consequence (E_p), all p >= 5:** proposed-established. It rests on
  (S0) and (K) of the Kassel claim, (A) and (T) of the syzygy claim, (R1)
  and (R2) of the edge-jump claim, and Open 1. None of these has been
  refereed yet.

## Next rank (n >= 2): what changes

The level-`m` Kassel direction sits at `(mp,mp,mp)`, degree `3mp`, and its
extension `N^(m)` has its central `z` at `t^(mp)`.

- **Slices.** A `k`-th order tail moves one letter's degree by
  `k(p-1)`. So a `rho`-visible jump in `(mp,mp,mp)` from a `b`-shifted ca
  slice starts in `(mp, mp-k(p-1), mp)`. For `m=1` only `k=1` fits, from
  `(p,1,p)`. For `m=2` there are two options: `k=1` from `(2p,p+1,2p)`, or
  `k=2` from `(2p,2,2p)`.
- **Seeds change.** In `(2p,2,2p)` the seeds are the second-order tails
  `f2` of `f_[a,b,a]` and `f_[b,c,c]`, in multidegrees `(2,2p-1,0)` and
  `(0,2p-1,2)`. The context carries a second `b`, so the rule
  `rho(v) m0 = eps(v) m0` no longer holds for every right context.
- **Freeness survives.** The relations `s1` and `s2` are `b`-linear, so the
  edge algebra `T(Y_a,Y_b,Y_c)/(E,s1,s2)` is `T(V) (x) U`, which is right-free
  over `U` in every `b`-degree. So the Tor argument of (L) applies in every
  `b`-degree.
- **The Kassel step survives.** The conjugation argument of the Kassel claim
  needs `(t^m X)^[p]` for square-zero `X` to have zero Kassel part. The same
  trace argument should give this for every `m`, since every Jacobson word
  of a homogeneous `t^m X` pairs two powers of `ad(X)` under `tr`. This is
  expected, not checked.
- **The Witt carry does not.** The only Heisenberg p-power relations are
  still `e^p = 0` for the three edge commutators, at `delta`-degree `p`.
  At level `m >= 2` no edge commutator reaches `t^m`. So the level-`m`
  hidden direction has to come from `m`-fold iterated jumps, or from order-`m`
  tails. It cannot come from a single edge syzygy.

First falsifiable step for `m=2`: compute the `(2p,2,2p)` slice for `p=5`,
the `b`-degree-2 analogue of `lift_check.py` with the second-order seeds.
Then test whether its visible jumps span the 3-dimensional `W_2`.
