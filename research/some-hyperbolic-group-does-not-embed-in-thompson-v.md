There is an infinite, finitely presented, torsion-free, word-hyperbolic group
with property (T) that admits no injective homomorphism into Thompson's group V.
Every infinite hyperbolic group with property (T) is an example.

Proof route: `some-hyperbolic-group-does-not-embed-in-thompson-v-proof`.

Credit: the argument is Xiaolei Wu's, as recorded in Zaremsky's list, Problem
2.19. It rests on Farley's theorem that V has the Haagerup property.
