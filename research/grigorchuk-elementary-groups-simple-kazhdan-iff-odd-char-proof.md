Artifact §3.
1. **Finite generation (B1).** CEPSS (the proof of `lem:zeinterior`, quoted in the import) span `A_k(𝒢)` by
   `1_(Θ((α,g,β),C(βη)))`. Each such set equals the bisection product
   `Θ((α(g·η),e,∅),X^ω)·Θ((∅,g|_η,∅),X^ω)·Θ((∅,e,βη),C(βη))`, so `1_B = s_(α') u_h s_(β')^*`. With
   `u_g u_h = u_(gh)` and `G = ⟨a,b,c,d⟩`, `R_q` is generated by eight elements plus a generator of `F_q`. EJZ then
   gives (T) for `EL_n(R_q)`, `n ≥ 3`.
2. **Binary Leavitt family (B2).**
   - `s_x^* s_y` is the characteristic function of the composite germs of `w ↦ yw` then `xw' ↦ w'`. That composite is
     the identity of `X^ω` when `x = y` and empty otherwise.
   - `s_x s_x^* = 1_(C(x))`, and `C(0) ⊔ C(1) = X^ω`.
3. **Nonsofic (B3).** `d-ary-leavitt-groups-nonsofic-over-finite-fields` with `d = 2`.
4. **No MF quotient (B4).** `s = s_0`, `t = s_0^*` give `ts = 1` and `1−st = s_1s_1^*`, which is full since
   `s_1^*(s_1s_1^*)s_1 = 1`. Then apply `full-defect-ring-non-mf-at-rank-two`.
5. **Odd `q` (B5).** `grigorchuk-steinberg-algebra-simple-iff-char-not-two` makes `R_q` simple. The unit space is Cantor.
   `ample-steinberg-el-simple-mod-centre-without-hausdorff` gives `S` infinite and simple, with (T) by step 1. MF
   quotients of `S` are MF quotients of `EL_n(R_q)`, hence trivial.
6. **Even `q` (B6).**
   - The singular ideal `I` is nonzero and proper (`supp(1) = X^ω`).
   - `E_n(R_q,I)` is normal and noncentral: `[e_12(f), e_23(1)] = e_13(f)`.
   - `E_n(R_q,I)Z ≠ EL_n(R_q)`. The reduction to `EL_n(R_q/I)` kills `E_n(R_q,I)`, and its image is perfect and
     nontrivial, hence not central.
7. **Grigorchuk inside (B7).** `g ↦ u_g` is a homomorphism. It is injective since a faithful `g ≠ e` has a non-unit germ.
