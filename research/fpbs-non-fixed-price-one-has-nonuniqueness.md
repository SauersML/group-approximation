title: Groups not of fixed price one have a nonuniqueness phase on every Cayley graph
---

For every infinite finitely generated group not of fixed price one, each locally finite Cayley graph has p_c<p_u. This qualitative theorem is already due to Russell Lyons; the accompanying argument recovers it.

**Site versus bond (recorded 2026-09-09).** Lyons, arXiv:1109.5418, Section 1,
writes the proof for site percolation and remarks that essentially the same
proof applies to bond percolation. The bond statement recorded here does not
rest on that remark alone: the archive's own argument, through
`fpbs-quantitative-cost-threshold-bound` and
`fpbs-threshold-collapse-forces-price-one`, is written for independent bond
percolation coupled by i.i.d. edge uniforms (see
`research/artifacts/fpbs/fixed-price-percolation.md`). Evidence level: a
written deduction recovering a published theorem, not independent validation.
