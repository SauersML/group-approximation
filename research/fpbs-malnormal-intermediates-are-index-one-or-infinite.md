**ESTABLISHED** through `fpbs-malnormal-intermediates-are-index-one-or-infinite-proof`.

Let `K <= L` be countable groups with `K` infinite and malnormal. Let `α` be a
free p.m.p. action of `L`, and let `T` be a Borel equivalence relation with
`E_K ⊆ T ⊆ E_L`. Then for almost every `x`, the `T`-class of `x` is a single
`E_K`-class or the union of infinitely many `E_K`-classes.

**Applications.**
- It applies to `H_n <= H_{n+1}`, to `H_n <= Gamma_mal`, and to
  `L_{j+1} <= L_j`.
- Every enlargement of a stage relation, for example `R_n v Φ`, merges
  infinitely many stage classes wherever it merges any.
- No promotion argument, floor or refutation, can pass through a finite-index
  intermediate relation.
