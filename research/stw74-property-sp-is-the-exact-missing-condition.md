title: Problem LXXIV is exactly the property-SP gap
---

Let `A` be a unital simple C*-algebra in which every nonzero projection is
infinite.  Then the following are equivalent:

1. `A` is purely infinite;
2. `A` has property (SP): every nonzero hereditary C*-subalgebra of `A`
   contains a nonzero projection.

In particular, every counterexample to Problem LXXIV contains a nonzero
projectionless hereditary subalgebra, even though every projection visible
elsewhere in the algebra is properly infinite.
