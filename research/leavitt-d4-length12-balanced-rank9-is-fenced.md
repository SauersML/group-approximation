title: Every balanced rank-nine length-twelve triangle is fenced
---

Up to cyclic face rotations and simultaneous inversion there are two
carrier-balanced rank-nine systems.  Neither admits a nontrivial one-copy
coefficient boundary.  Hence the entire balanced rank-nine layer of the
minimal length-twelve `(1,3,3,5)` topology is globally fenced.
