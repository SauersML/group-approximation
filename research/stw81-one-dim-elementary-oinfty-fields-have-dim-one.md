Let `X` be a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff space with
`dim(X)<=1`, and let `A` be a separable nuclear locally trivial
`C_0(X)`-algebra with simple fibres.  Suppose every fibre is either
elementary or O-infinity-stable.  Then

`dim_nuc(A)<=1`.

More precisely, the elementary-fibre and O-infinity-stable-fibre loci are
clopen, the first restriction is continuous trace with spectrum of
dimension at most one, and the second restriction is O-infinity-stable.
Thus a nonzero `A` has nuclear dimension zero or one.

Every commutative hereditary subalgebra of every quotient of `A` has
primitive spectrum of dimension at most one.  Hence this class satisfies
both the hypothesis and the conclusion of STW Problem LXXXI, including
mixed fields with infinitely many ideals.
