Let `Gamma` be the Kun--Thom Kazhdan subgroup.  There are exact
finite-dimensional representations `rho_n` with canonical limiting character,
edge algebras

```text
B_n=C*(rho_n(Gamma)) subset M_(d_n),
```

unitaries `b_n in B_n` and `T_n in U(d_n)`, and

```text
x_n=T_n^*b_nT_n,
```

such that

```text
E_(B_n)(x_n)=0,                                        (FMD1)
dim_(B_n)(x_nB_n)=1,                                   (FMD2)
supp(|x_n|)=1,                                         (FMD3)
dim_C(x_nB_n)/d_n^2->0.                                (FMD4)
```

Thus canonical `Gamma`-character, exact Kazhdan actor decomposition, a
zero-expectation returned unitary, full physical support, and maximal right
module dimension do not authenticate a positive-density projection in the
ambient adjoint Hilbert space.  The right-module projection is right
`B_n`-invariant, but its normalized adjoint rank vanishes.

This is not a countermodel to the full Kun--Thom relative microstate problem:
the transporters `T_n` are chosen blockwise and do not satisfy the strict
compressor or the remaining full-actor relations.  It is an exact firewall
against using only canonical subgroup character, property `(T)`, and right
module dimension.  The strict full-actor compatibility is still the missing
ingredient.
