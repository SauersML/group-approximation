**Proof of the theorem.**
1. **Bounded normal generation.** Let `W_m` be the finite set of words of length `<= 2m` that are
   nontrivial in `Gamma`. For `u ∈ W_m` the normal closure of `u` is `Gamma`, since `Gamma` is simple.
   So for each `s ∈ S` fix words `c_1, ..., c_k` and signs `eps_j` with
   `s = c_1 u^(eps_1) c_1^(-1) ... c_k u^(eps_k) c_k^(-1)` in `Gamma`. The word
   `r(u, s) = s^(-1) c_1 u^(eps_1) c_1^(-1) ... c_k u^(eps_k) c_k^(-1)` is trivial in `Gamma`.
2. **The constant.** Put `2n(m) = max(2m, max |r(u, s)|)` over `u ∈ W_m`, `s ∈ S`. This is a finite
   maximum.
3. **Dichotomy.** Let `(Q, S)` satisfy every trivial word of length `<= 2n(m)`.
   - If some `u ∈ W_m` is trivial in `Q`: each `r(u, s)` has length `<= 2n(m)` and is trivial in
     `Gamma`, so it holds in `Q`, and there `s = prod c_j u^(eps_j) c_j^(-1) = 1`. Every generator is
     trivial, so `Q = 1`.
   - Otherwise every word of length `<= 2m` that is nontrivial in `Gamma` is nontrivial in `Q`, and
     every trivial one is trivial in `Q` because `2m <= 2n(m)`. The marked `m`-ball of a group is
     determined by its relators of length `<= 2m`, so the `m`-balls agree. ∎

**Corollary 1.** If `Gamma` is LEF, a finite marked group with the same relators of length `<= 2n` is a
nontrivial finite quotient of `P_n`. Conversely a nontrivial finite quotient of `P_(n(m))` has the
`m`-ball of `Gamma` by the theorem, and `m` is arbitrary.

**Corollary 2.** A nontrivial finitely generated linear quotient `H` of `P_n` is residually finite
(`finitely-generated-linear-groups-are-residually-finite`). Some `s ∈ S` is nontrivial in `H`, so some
finite quotient of `H`, hence of `P_n`, keeps `s` nontrivial. Then apply Corollary 1.

**Corollary 3.** A finite group `Q ≠ 1` has a maximal normal subgroup `M`, and `Q/M ≠ 1` is simple and
a quotient of `P_(n(m))`.

**Corollary 4.** The permutation group generated by the `F(S)`-set is a quotient of `P_(n(m))`, and it
is nontrivial because some generator moves a point.

**Scope.** Nothing here is specific to full groups. The argument is the quantitative form of the
isolation lemma recorded in `minimal-free-sft-full-group-pattern-models-are-exact`.
