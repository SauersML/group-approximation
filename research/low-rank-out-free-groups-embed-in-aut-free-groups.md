**ESTABLISHED.** Elementary; no novelty is claimed. The derivation is
`low-rank-out-free-groups-embed-in-aut-free-groups-proof`.

- `Out(F_0) = 1`.
- `Out(F_1) = Aut(Z) ≅ Z/2 = Aut(F_1)`.
- `Out(F_2) ≅ GL_2(Z)` embeds in `Aut(F_144)`.

So the first question of Zaremsky Problem 3.10 is open only for `m ≥ 3`.
