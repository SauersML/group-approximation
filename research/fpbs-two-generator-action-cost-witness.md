Theorem 6.2. For any countably infinite G and specified free pmp action alpha, if all pair-generated restrictions have cost <=1 then C(alpha)=1. Bounded exponent uses the subgroup theorem and binary-finite alternative. Unbounded finitely generated groups use cyclic compression. Countable exhaustion uses summable relative costs. The implication is not reversed.
