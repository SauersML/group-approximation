Let `Q_f` be the smallest `Y`-reducing projection containing `QB_f`.  Its
orthogonal complement

```text
T=Q-Q_f
```

also reduces `Y`.  Since `B_f` belongs to the finite-dimensional
star-algebra generated by `Y`, `T` commutes with `B_f`.  The definition of
the hull gives `TQB_f=0`, hence `TB_f=0`.  Applying `(AFS1)` to `T` gives

```text
0=tau(TB_f)>=(beta/m)tau(T),
```

so `T=0` and `Q_f=Q`.

For the approximate statement, `(AFS3)` and `TD=0` give

```text
||TB_f||_2=||TQB_f||_2
          =||T(QB_f-D)||_2<=delta.                     (1)
```

Because `T` commutes with the projection `B_f`,

```text
tau(TB_f)=||TB_f||_2^2<=delta^2.                       (2)
```

Apply `(AFS2)` to `T` and combine with `(2)`:

```text
(beta/m)tau(T)<=delta^2+C_f sqrt(E),
```

which is `(AFS4)`.  Taking `T=Q-Q_D` proves `(AFS5)`.
