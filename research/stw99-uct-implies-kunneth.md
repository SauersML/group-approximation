For separable nuclear algebras the bootstrap class carrying the UCT and
the class carrying the Künneth formula for tensor products coincide
(Rosenberg--Schochet, Schochet): membership in the bootstrap class gives
the Künneth sequence for all `B`, and its torsion-free-target
normalisation is the statement of Problem IV.
