Shrink the assumed linear seed threshold below one. Then
log(e/delta(U)^2)>=1, so its bound L^2 delta(U)^2 implies the
required logarithmic estimate with B=L^2 in the same universal host.
The required linear numerical estimate is open; this elementary
implication supplies no unconditional seed.
