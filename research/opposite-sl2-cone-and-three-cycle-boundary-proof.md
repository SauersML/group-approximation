Write `a=(1,A)`, `b=(1,B)` and let `Q=<A,B>`.

## The external group and its Laurent kernel

In the upper-left two-by-two block,

```text
A B^(-1) A = [[0,1],[-1,0]].                                 (OSB1)
```

The matrix on the right and `A` are the standard generators of
`SL_2(Z)`; its square is `-I`.  Hence `Q=SL_2(Z)`.

Let `E_pm=EL_r(R_pm)`.  Conjugating `Gamma` by `Q` puts it in
`E_pm`.  Conversely, take any exponent

```text
z=(z_1,z_2,n_3,...,n_d) in Z^2 x N^(d-2).
```

There is `q in SL_2(Z)` and `v_1,v_2 in N` with
`q(v_1,v_2)=(z_1,z_2)`: for a nonzero pair, send
`(gcd(|z_1|,|z_2|),0)` to it, and treat zero separately.  Thus the Laurent
monomial `x^z` lies in a monomial transform `q(k[N^d])).  Elementary
matrices are additive in their coefficient, so the conjugates
`q Gamma q^(-1)` generate every elementary matrix over `R_pm).  Therefore
the normal closure of `Gamma` under `Q` is `E_pm`, and projection to the
external factor proves

```text
P=E_pm rtimes SL_2(Z).                                        (OSB2)
```

## The positive cone is free and non-Ore

Act by Mobius transformations on `I=(0,infinity)).  One has

```text
A(I)=(1,infinity),       B(I)=(0,1).                          (OSB3)
```

The two images are disjoint.  Thus the first letter of every nonempty positive
word is detected by its image of `I); cancel it and iterate.  Since positive
matrices cannot differ by the scalar `-I`, this proves
`<A,B>_+` is the free monoid on two generators.

If `uA=vB` were a common left multiple, freeness would equate two words
whose last letters are different.  This is impossible.  Hence the positive
cone is not left Ore, and there is no single positive telescope level
containing both `A^(-1)Gamma A` and `B^(-1)Gamma B` by the common-left-
multiple mechanism used in the neighboring positive theorems.

## Every finite image normalizes the base image

Let `pi:P->F` have finite image.  Strict compression gives

```text
a Gamma a^(-1) subset Gamma,       b Gamma b^(-1) subset Gamma.
```

After applying `pi`, each inclusion is between finite subgroups of the same
cardinality, because conjugation is a bijection.  Hence both are equalities:

```text
pi(a)pi(Gamma)pi(a)^(-1)=pi(Gamma),
pi(b)pi(Gamma)pi(b)^(-1)=pi(Gamma).                           (OSB4)
```

Since `P` is generated by `Gamma,a,b`, equation (OSB4) proves
`pi(Gamma)` is normal in `pi(P)).  The normal closure (OSB2) consequently
satisfies

```text
pi(E_pm) subset pi(Gamma).                                    (OSB5)
```

For example, the elementary matrix `e_12(x_1^(-1))` belongs to
`E_pm` but not to `Gamma`.  If `L` is any finite-index subgroup of
`P` containing `Gamma`, apply (OSB5) to the quotient by the normal core
of `L`; it forces `e_12(x_1^(-1)) in L`.  Thus `Gamma` is not separable
in `P`, and every literal finite quotient identifies its base coset with
this Laurent coset.

This is only an exact-model obstruction.  A sofic action may use genuinely
approximate permutations, so (OSB5) does not prove the coset action nonsofic.

## Exact operator-algebraic status

The external projection sends `a` to the infinite-order matrix `A`, so

```text
a^k notin Gamma       for every k!=0.
```

Gao--Junge--Gao Appendix Theorem 7.1 applies exactly as in
`kt-q2-double-hyperlinear-iff-relative-embeddable` and gives

```text
P *_Gamma P hyperlinear
iff L(Gamma) subset L(P) is RE/C.                             (OSB6)
```

If `P action P/Gamma` is sofic, then
`sofic-coset-action-makes-amalgam-double-sofic` strengthens the left side
to soficity.  No converse is known.

The Kun--Thom nonsofic theorem cannot be substituted here.  The quotient
`P->SL_2(Z)` shows `P` does not have property (T): the infinite virtually
free group `SL_2(Z)` does not have property (T), and property (T) passes to
quotients.  Thus the opposite slice misses a stated hypothesis of
`kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic`.

## The rank-three bracket

For standard elementary transvections, two distinct non-opposite roots either
commute or form an adjacent-root Heisenberg pair (swap their order if
necessary).  The first two required claims settle those cases.  The opposite
pair above is therefore the only remaining two-root type.

For `d=3`, put

```text
U=I+E_12,       V=I+E_23,       W=I+E_31.
```

Their commutators contain the other three positive root generators:

```text
[U,V]=I+E_13,       [V,W]=I+E_21,       [W,U]=I+E_32.          (OSB7)
```

Together with inverses, these elementary matrices generate `SL_3(Z)`.
The same exponent-orbit argument used for (OSB2) shows that the normal closure
of `Gamma` is `EL_r(k[Z^3])`.  Hence this three-compressor slice is the
full Kun--Thom group `G`.  The claims `coordinate-action-not-sofic` and
`kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic` give the asserted negative endpoints,
while (OSB6) for the full vertex is exactly the still-open `RE/C` problem.
