**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Let `Γ` be a countable group with property (T), let `σ` be the Bernoulli
action of `Γ` on `([0,1], Leb)^Γ` (s-malleable and mixing), and let `ρ` be any measure-preserving action
of `Γ` on a standard probability space `(Y, ν)`. Let `V` be a countable group, or more generally a Polish
group of finite type. Then every measurable cocycle `w : Γ × (X × Y) → V` for `σ × ρ` is cohomologous to a
cocycle `w'` that does not depend on the `X`-variable, so `w'` comes from a cocycle of `ρ`.

With `H = Γ`: an infinite property (T) group is an infinite rigid subgroup of itself, normal hence
w-normal, and `σ|H = σ` is weakly mixing.

Source: S. Popa, *Cocycle and orbit equivalence superrigidity for malleable actions of w-rigid groups*,
Invent. Math. 170 (2007), Theorem 0.1, second sentence. Citation route
`popa-bernoulli-product-cocycles-come-from-cofactor-citation`.
