**ESTABLISHED (citation).** Let `S` be the set of dyadic fractions `t ∈ (0,1)` whose terminating
binary expansion has an odd number of ones. Jones' oriented subgroup `\vec F ≤ F` (the vacuum
stabilizer of Jones' chromatic representation at `Q = 2`) equals `{g ∈ F : gS = S}`.

The same source proves `\vec F ≅ F_3`, generated by `x_0x_1, x_1x_2, x_2x_3`, and that `\vec F` is its own
commensurator in `F`.
