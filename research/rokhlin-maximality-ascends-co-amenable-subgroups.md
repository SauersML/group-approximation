Let `H <= G` be countable groups with H infinite. Suppose H is co-amenable in the Følner sense: for every finite `S ⊆ G` and `δ > 0` there is a finite nonempty set `Ω'` of right cosets with `|Ω's \ Ω'| <= δ|Ω'|` for all `s ∈ S`. Let `q >= 2`.

1. **Theorem A.** If H is Rokhlin-maximal at q, meaning `h^Rok_H((A^k)^H, uniform) = k log q` for every `k >= 1` with `|A| = q`, then G is Rokhlin-maximal at q.
2. **Invariance.** H is Rokhlin-maximal at q if and only if G is.
3. **Conjectures.** If H is Rokhlin-maximal at every `q >= 2`, then G satisfies Gottschalk's surjunctivity conjecture and Kaplansky's direct finiteness conjecture.

**Proof outline.** Fix a generating partition P of `A^G` and an ε-window S for the time-zero coordinates. Take a Følner set `Ω'` of cosets for S and its interior `Ω''`.
- **Upper bound.** Over H, the factor Z generated by the Bernoulli coordinates on `Ω''` together with `σ-alg_H(∨_(ω ∈ Ω') r(ω)P)` has Rokhlin entropy below `|Ω'| H(P) + |Ω''| ε`, by sub-additivity.
- **Lower bound.** By weak containment of the complementary Bernoulli coordinates, `h^Rok_H(Z)` is at least the Rokhlin entropy of the H-Bernoulli shift on `Ω''`, which is `|Ω''| log q`.

Full proof: Sections 1 and 2 of the artifact.

**Scope.** No new maximal group follows today.
- Every group currently known to be maximal is sofic.
- A co-amenable sofic subgroup makes the overgroup sofic (Brude–Sasyk; a direct proof is Remark 4 in Section 2 of the artifact).
- The theorem is a permanence statement for the maximal class with no finite models. It becomes productive once a nonsofic maximal group exists.
- Over simple Kazhdan hosts it is empty, by `simple-kazhdan-groups-have-no-proper-co-amenable-subgroups`.
- Over Thompson's V it reduces subgroup induction to `thompson-v-is-sofic` (Section 4 of the artifact).

No novelty is claimed.
