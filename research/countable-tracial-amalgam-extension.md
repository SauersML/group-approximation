title: Compatible traces extend along a countable chain of finite-wire free amalgams
distinct_from:
  amalgam-edge-compatible-corona-embeddings: that asks for compatible norm-corona embeddings of one literal amalgam; this is a tracial von Neumann completeness statement for a countable iterated amalgam over finite-dimensional wires.
---

Let `(M_n,tau_n)` be finite tracial von Neumann algebras and let `D_n` be a
finite-dimensional tracial von Neumann algebra embedded trace-preservingly in
both `M_n` and `M_(n+1)`.  The countable iterated reduced amalgamated free
product

```text
M_0 *_(D_0) M_1 *_(D_1) M_2 *_(D_2) ...
```

exists with a faithful trace extending every `tau_n`.  Consequently, if
successive packet-stage group representations agree on their finite
multiplicity wire, their tracial models extend through the infinite free
amalgam.  No relation is introduced between selectors belonging to
nonadjacent stages beyond what their shared wires force.
