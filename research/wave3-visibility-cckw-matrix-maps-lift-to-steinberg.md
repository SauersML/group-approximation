For every associative unital F_7-algebra R and x,y,z in R, the assignments

    a -> X_03(x) X_12(x),
    b -> X_10(y) X_23(-y),
    c -> X_31(z)

define a homomorphism G_HB2(7)->St(R) lifting the CCKW matrix map to E(R).
This includes noncommuting block matrices and arbitrary finite local rings.
Consequently the pullback of the stable Steinberg central extension along
this map or its restriction to K7 splits, as does every central pushout.

This does not assert that every central extension of the actual matrix
image comes from the stable Steinberg extension. It therefore does not
settle finite central visibility or residual finiteness.
