Post-halt activation of a finite Clifford table cannot implement reverse
Kleene.  A fixed table only excludes dimensions below its spin size, while
ultraproduct coordinate dimensions grow freely.  Activating all ranks gives
a locally finite, canonically hyperlinear extraspecial union in which the
common sign survives.  Independently, relators emitted after a triviality
certificate cannot make the certified word nontrivial.  The shortcut fails
both matrix and recursion-theoretic quantifiers.
