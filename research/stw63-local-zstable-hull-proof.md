The union `phi(Z) union psi(Z)` is separable.  By the local hypothesis it is
contained in a unital `Z`-stable subalgebra `C subset B`.  The pairwise hull
theorem applies to this `C` and produces unitaries in `B` approximately
conjugating `phi` to `psi`.
