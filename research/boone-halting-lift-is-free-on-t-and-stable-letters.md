Let `M` be a modular machine with modulus `m`, and let `r_1, ..., r_n` be the
stable letters of its tower `G'_M` (one per residue pair carrying a quadruple,
the list `machineIdentifications M`). Let `t = t(0,0)` be the generator of the
base group `G = <t, x, y | xy = yx>`. Then the subgroup

    L = <t, r_1, ..., r_n>  <=  G'_M

(Simpson's `<t>'`, `towerTSub (machineIdentifications M)`, the associated
subgroup of the final stable letter `k`) is free of rank `n + 1` with free basis
`t, r_1, ..., r_n`.

More precisely, writing `H subseteq N x N` for the halting set and `t(s)`,
`s in Z x Z`, for the free basis of the normal closure `T` of `t` in `G`:

1. `L` is the multiple HNN extension of `T_M = <t(s) : s in H>` (free on that
   basis) with stable letters `r_i` and associated sub-basis subgroups
   `<t(s) : s in H, s in S_i>`, `r_i t(s) r_i^{-1} = t(sigma_i s)`, where `S_i` is
   the residue class of the `i`-th quadruple and `sigma_i` is the machine step on
   it;
2. the graph on `H` with an edge `s -> step(s)` for every halting configuration
   `s != (0,0)` is a tree rooted at `(0,0)`, and conjugating along it expresses
   every `t(s)` as `w_s^{-1} t w_s` with `w_s` the word of stable letters read
   along the computation from `s`. Tietze elimination along this tree gives
   `L = F(t, r_1, ..., r_n)`.

Consequence: the final group `G_M` is an HNN extension of `G'_M` along a free
group of finite rank, i.e. `G_M = < G'_M, k | [k, t] = [k, r_i] = 1 >`. This is
the structural fact behind the Collins--Miller description of Boone--Britton
groups (free associated subgroups), recovered here for the Aanderaa--Cohen
tower from the machine-checked chain.
