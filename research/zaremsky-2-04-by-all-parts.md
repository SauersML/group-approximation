Problem 2.4 asks six questions: whether F is quasi-isometric to F × Z, to F × F
and to T, whether F quasi-retracts onto F × Z and onto F × F, and whether T
quasi-retracts onto F. The problem is resolved when all six are answered, and
each part-claim is established exactly when its sub-question is answered, by
either answer. This is the only admissible route into the root.
