Intersect the decreasing finite-type systems obtained by retaining
cyclic word-graph edges. Their nonempty intersection lies in the
original subshift, satisfies the cycle condition, and contains every
subshift satisfying that condition. It is the chain-recurrent core;
its finite-field crossed product and all matrix groups are LEF.
