In `C_2*C_2`, the trivial-type projections `q_s=(1+s)/2` and
`q_t=(1+t)/2` satisfy `||q_tq_s||_2^2=1/4` in canonical trace, while their
meet in the left regular algebra is zero.  Canonical regular models through
finite dihedral quotients retain product norm `1/4` but have meet trace tending
to zero.  A surviving product mark therefore does not certify a common
selected carrier.
