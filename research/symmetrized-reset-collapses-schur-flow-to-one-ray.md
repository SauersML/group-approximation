Let `A_f<=B_f`, `C_f=B_f^Q`, and `C_f<=R_f` be the Schur packet, its
`Q` child, and the translation-symmetrized reset. Equivalently, use the
single finite overgroup `Rhat_f` of
`translation-symmetrized-full-schur-reset`, which already contains `B_f`.
Put

```text
D=2^N,       X_1={x:f(x)=1},       X_0={x:f(x)=0},
K_1=|X_1|,  K_0=|X_0|.
```

In every exact `J=-1` representation of the amalgamated incidence

```text
B_f *_C_f R_f,                                             (SRC5)
```

there is one integer `m>=0` such that the restriction to `B_f` has
multiplicities

```text
x in X_1:  m copies of the unique 2D-dimensional B_f-simple T_x,
x in X_0:  m copies of each of the two D-dimensional B_f-simples
           T_(x,+),T_(x,-).                                (SRC6)
```

Conversely, the `B_f` vector in `(SRC6)` restricts to multiplicity `m` on
every one of the `2^(k+1)` `D`-dimensional child simples. Therefore the cone
of `B_f` type vectors compatible with an `R_f` restriction is exactly the
single ray generated by `(SRC6)`.

For `Rhat_f` the same ray is forced directly, without the amalgam `(SRC5)`.

On that ray,

```text
total child multiplicity          =2^(k+1)m,
total full-packet multiplicity    =(K_1+2K_0)m,
Schur restriction surplus         =K_1 m,
Hilbert dimension on either side  =2^(k+1)D m.              (SRC7)
```

Thus every complementary finite representation-type escape is removed. The
only stationary escape remaining is the scalar `m`; Hilbert dimension
conservation in `(SRC7)` shows exactly how that scalar absorbs the predicate
surplus in a finite graph of groups.

Quantitatively, after fixed-table exactification of `B_f` and `R_f` and polar
alignment on their common `C_f`, the normalized weighted `L1` distance of the
`B_f` type vector from this ray is at most `C sqrt(E)`. Hence a subsequent
one-sided compressor need control only one scalar multiplicity current, not a
high-dimensional representation-ring reservoir.
