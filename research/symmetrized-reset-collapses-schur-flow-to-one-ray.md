Let `A_f<=B_f`, `C_f=B_f^Q`, and let `Rhat_f` be the finite
translation-symmetrized full reset containing `B_f` from
`translation-symmetrized-full-schur-reset`.
Put

```text
D=2^N,       X_1={x:f(x)=1},       X_0={x:f(x)=0},
K_1=|X_1|,  K_0=|X_0|.
```

For nonconstant `f`, every exact `J=-1` representation of the finite full
reset `Rhat_f` has one integer `n>=0` such that the restriction to `B_f` has
multiplicities

```text
x in X_1:  2n copies of the unique 2D-dimensional B_f-simple T_x,
x in X_0:  n copies of each of the four D-dimensional B_f-simples
           T_(x,epsilon,delta).                             (SRC6)
```

Conversely, the `B_f` vector in `(SRC6)` restricts to multiplicity `2n` on
every one of the `2^(k+1)` `D`-dimensional `Q`-child simples. Therefore the
cone of `B_f` type vectors occurring under the full reset is exactly the
single ray generated by `(SRC6)`.

The smaller amalgam `B_f *_C_f R_f` does **not** have this property: over an
inactive selector character, the two extensions of either fixed child type
can trade multiplicity. The independent `P`-branch flips in `Rhat_f` are
essential for removing that last reservoir.

On that ray,

```text
total child multiplicity          =2^(k+2)n,
total full-packet multiplicity    =(2K_1+4K_0)n,
Schur restriction surplus         =2K_1 n,
Hilbert dimension on either side  =2^(k+2)D n.              (SRC7)
```

Thus every complementary finite representation-type escape is removed. The
only stationary escape remaining is the scalar `n`; Hilbert dimension
conservation in `(SRC7)` shows exactly how that scalar absorbs the predicate
surplus in a finite graph of groups.

Quantitatively, after fixed-table exactification of `Rhat_f`, the normalized
weighted `L1` distance of the `B_f` type vector from this ray is at most
`C sqrt(E)`. Hence a subsequent
one-sided compressor need control only one scalar multiplicity current, not a
high-dimensional representation-ring reservoir.
