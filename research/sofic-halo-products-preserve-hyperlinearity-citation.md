**Established by citation.**  Alekseev--Bradford, arXiv:2601.18742v1,
Theorem `BigTechThm` and Corollary `BigSemiCoroll`, prove that semidirect
products of hyperlinear groups by a sofic hyperlinear action are hyperlinear.
Proposition `SoficActSoficAutoProp` and the halo corollaries show that a sofic
action on a set or graph induces such an action on any functorial halo whose
finite pieces lie uniformly in the relevant approximation class.

The same framework covers sofic, linear-sofic and weakly sofic permanence,
but the hyperlinear specialization is the one imported here.
