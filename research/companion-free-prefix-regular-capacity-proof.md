The pulse relations embed the subgroup generated by
`B_0,...,B_(N-1)` as `C_2^N`.  Hence in the canonical group trace

```text
tau(product_(k<N)(1+B_k)/2)=2^(-N).
```

Every exact full-chart HNN transport preserves the canonical trace of each
central type projection.  The same is true after a central-character linear
change, and traces add when paired source pieces are sent to orthogonal
target tags.  Therefore any lossless recombination under the common prefix
has at least the trace of the original complete baseline and is dominated by
the prefix Reynolds projection.  A common reducing hull can only increase
that trace.  Positivity and faithfulness give `(CPR3)`; `N/2^N<=1/2` gives
`(CPR4)`.
