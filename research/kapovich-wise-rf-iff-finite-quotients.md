The following universal statements are equivalent.

1. Every word-hyperbolic group is residually finite.
2. Every nontrivial word-hyperbolic group has a nontrivial finite quotient
   (equivalently, a proper subgroup of finite index).

Contrapositive form used by consumers: a non-residually-finite hyperbolic
group exists iff an infinite hyperbolic group with no nontrivial finite
quotient exists.  (A nontrivial finite group is its own finite quotient, so a
nontrivial hyperbolic group without finite quotients is infinite.)

Imported; see `kapovich-wise-rf-iff-finite-quotients-citation`.  The direction
(2) => (1) is the substantial one; (1) => (2) is immediate.
