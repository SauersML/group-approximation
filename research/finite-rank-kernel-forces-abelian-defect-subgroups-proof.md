## Why sufficient

**Step 1, `K <= N`.**  The quotient map `G -> G/N` has a locally
residually finite target and `L` is finitely generated, so the first
prerequisite gives `D_G(L) <= N`; hence `K <= N`, and every `a in K` has
`a - I` of finite rank.

**Step 2, `E` is finite dimensional.**  Choose generators `a_1, .., a_r`
of `K` and put `E_0 = sum_i (a_i - I)V`, a finite sum of finite-rank
images.  For `w in E_0`, `a_i w = w + (a_i - I)w in E_0`, so `a_i E_0 <=
E_0`; `a_i` is injective and `E_0` finite dimensional, so `a_i E_0 = E_0`
and `a_i^-1 E_0 = E_0`.  From

```text
ab - I = (a - I) + a(b - I),        a^-1 - I = -a^-1 (a - I),        (1)
```

induction on word length gives `(a - I)V <= E_0` and `a E_0 = E_0` for
every `a in K`.  So `E = E_0`.

**Step 3, `E` is `G`-invariant.**  For `g in G` and `a in K`,
`g (a - I) V = (g a g^-1 - I) g V = (g a g^-1 - I) V`, and `g a g^-1 in K`
by normality.  So restriction is a homomorphism `rho : G -> GL_k(E)`.

**Step 4, `K` is trivial on `E`.**  `E` is finite dimensional over the
field `k`, so the second prerequisite applied to `rho` gives
`rho(D_G(L)) = 1`; the defect there is the normal closure over the group
generated by all compressors, which contains the defect `D_G(L)` used
here, so in particular `rho(K) = 1`.

**Step 5, the additive embedding.**  Write `T_a = a - I` for `a in K`.
Its image lies in `E` by (FRK2) and its restriction to `E` is zero by
Step 4, so `T_a T_b = 0` for all `a, b in K`, and (1) becomes
`T_(ab) = T_a + T_b`.  Each `T_a` vanishes on `E` and lands in `E`, so it
factors through `V/E -> E`.  The map `a |-> T_a` is injective because `G`
acts faithfully on `V`.

**Step 6, the characteristic split.**  `Hom_k(V/E, E)` is a `k`-vector
space: torsion-free in characteristic zero, of exponent `p` in
characteristic `p`.  A finitely generated subgroup of a torsion-free
abelian group is free abelian of finite rank; a finitely generated
abelian group of exponent `p` is finite.

## What is not used

Malcev's theorem is not needed: the finite-dimensional sterility
prerequisite does the work of "finitely generated linear groups are
residually finite" on `E`.  No analytic collapse theorem, no MF
hypothesis, and no property (T) enters the proof; property (T) appears
only in the corollary about Kazhdan subgroups, through finite generation
and the absence of `Z` quotients.
