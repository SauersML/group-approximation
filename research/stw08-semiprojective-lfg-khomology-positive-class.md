Let `A` be separable, exact, semiprojective, and quasidiagonal.  If the
rational `K^0`-homology of `A` is locally finitely generated, then `A` embeds
into a UHF algebra, and in particular is AF-embeddable.

This conclusion does not assume the UCT.
