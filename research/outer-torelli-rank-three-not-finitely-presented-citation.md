Citation import, not a reproof. Source read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv PDF,
pages 1–2: M. Bestvina, K.-U. Bux, D. Margalit, *Dimension of the Torelli group for
Out(F_n)*, arXiv:math/0603177v1 (8 March 2006).

- **Main Theorem**, verbatim: "For n ≥ 3, we have: (1) T_n has a (2n − 4)-dimensional
  Eilenberg–MacLane space. (2) H_{2n−4}(T_n, Z) is infinitely generated. (3) T_n is
  finitely generated." Here `T_n` is the kernel of `Out(F_n) -> GL_n(Z)`.
- Page 1, verbatim: "When n = 3, we obtain that H_2(T_3, Z) is not finitely
  generated, and this immediately implies the result of Krstić–McCool that T_3 is
  not finitely presented [7]."
- Page 2, verbatim: "It is a general fact that if the second homology of a group is
  not finitely generated, then the group is not finitely presented."

The original theorem is Krstić–McCool (1997), reference [7] of the source; BBM
describe it as "proven in 1997, via completely algebraic methods". The general
fact follows from Hopf's formula. For a finite presentation `G = F/R`,
`H_2(G; Z) = (R ∩ [F,F])/[F,R]` is a subgroup of `R/[F,R]`, and `R/[F,R]` is an
abelian group generated by the images of the finitely many relators.
