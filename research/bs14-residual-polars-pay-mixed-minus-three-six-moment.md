**OPEN; MINIMAL TWO-ROW GRAM SEAM.**  In the setup of
`bs14-residual-polar-data-build-active-dilation`, after aligning the two
candidate source maps to one isometry `V` and deleting its long-packet tail
by `long-bs-packet-source-mass-is-frequency-small`, prove with a
dimension-independent normalized-HS bound that

```text
||V^*S^(-3)V-V^*S^6V||_F^2/d                         (M36)
```

is paid by the two cubic residual energies, the source-alignment Gram
defect, and the discarded source mass.

The individual polar rows already supply their own source/exit
compatibility.  By
`two-exit-gram-swap-builds-low-rank-involution-surgery`, `(M36)` is exactly
the remaining off-diagonal block of the joint forward/backward exit Gram
matrix.  Once it tends to zero, a quantitative Gram correction would give
one reflection on an `o(d)` active space while leaving the supplied
involution unchanged outside an `o(d)` invariant surgery hull.

Separate spectral or polar rounding of the two cubics does not imply
`(M36)`: it contains one exit from each row.  A proof must use their common
occurrence of the same physical `X` (or another genuinely mixed two-row
identity).  This claim does not include the later moving-congruence atom or
integer cell authentication.
