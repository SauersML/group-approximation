The off-diagonal part of a unitary relative to `P` is controlled by its
commutator with `P`.  Positive block density converts ambient normalized HS
control to normalized control inside each block.  Fixed-word telescoping
then gives vanishing relator defect on both block tuples, and same-dimension
stability closes them without moving the cut.
