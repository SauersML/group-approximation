**Item 1 (BCC for a-T-menable groups).** N. Higson, G. Kasparov, *E-theory and
KK-theory for groups which act properly and isometrically on Hilbert space*,
Invent. Math. 144 (2001) 23--74. Their main theorem proves the Baum--Connes
conjecture with coefficients (indeed the strong form) for every second countable
locally compact a-T-menable group; countable discrete Haagerup groups and
separable coefficients are the case used here.

This item was **not read in the original**; it is imported through two papers
that state and use it verbatim for exactly this purpose, both read from the PDFs
on MSI (`gs -sDEVICE=txtwrite`):
- Osajda, arXiv:1406.5015, p. 4: "There are groups without property A but
  satisfying the Haagerup property. For such groups the strong Baum-Connes
  conjecture holds [HK01]."
- Arzhantseva--Osajda, arXiv:1404.6807, Introduction: "...Higson--Kasparov
  [HK97] of the strong Baum-Connes conjecture (which is strictly stronger than
  the Baum-Connes conjecture with coefficients [MN06]) for all groups with the
  Haagerup property." Their abstract: "...the strong Baum-Connes conjecture and,
  hence, the Baum-Connes conjecture with arbitrary coefficients hold for them."

**Item 2 (non-exact a-T-menable groups exist).** D. Osajda, *Small cancellation
labellings of some infinite graphs and applications*, arXiv:1406.5015 (Adv.
Math.). Verbatim (p. 3--4, text extraction):
- "Theorem 2 (see Theorem 6.3 in the text). There exist finitely generated
  groups acting properly on CAT(0) cubical complexes and not having property A."
- "Acting properly on a CAT(0) cubical complex is equivalent to acting properly
  on a space with walls [...]. This implies in particular the Haagerup property,
  and hence equivariant coarse embeddability into a Hilbert space."

Arzhantseva--Osajda, arXiv:1404.6807, Theorem 1.2 (extraction): a graphical
small cancellation group `G` with the stated relators "has the Haagerup property
but is not coarsely amenable" -- i.e. a-T-menable and without property A.

**Property A = exactness.** N. Ozawa, *Amenable actions and exactness for
discrete groups*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 330 (2000) 691--695 (arXiv:math/0002185):
a countable discrete group is exact iff it has property A iff it acts amenably on
a compact space. So "no property A" in item 2 means "non-exact".

**Bounded check.** Higson--Kasparov itself was read only through the two citing
papers above and standard references (e.g. Valette, *Introduction to the Baum--
Connes conjecture*); the a-T-menable BCC isomorphism is textbook. The verbatim
quotes verified here are Osajda's Theorem 2 and the two Baum--Connes statements
of Osajda and Arzhantseva--Osajda.
