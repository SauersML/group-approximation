This route imports the theorem; it does not reprove it. Source: the PDF of arXiv:1609.05086v1
(U. Haagerup and K. K. Olesen, "Non-inner amenability of the Thompson groups T and V"), read on
2026-09-13.

- Theorem 3.4: "The Thompson groups T and V are not inner amenable."
- After its proof: "The proof of the above theorem shows that, in fact, every subgroup of V
  containing Λ is non-inner amenable."
- Theorem 3.1 (Effros): for an ICC group G, if LG has property Γ, then G is inner amenable. They
  conclude that LT and LV do not have property Γ.
- Section 1 credits Jolissaint with the inner amenability of F.
