Let `H=GL_3(2)<=A_8` be the aligned subgroup and let `b=x_21(f)` be the
transverse root from the explicit length-19 bridge.  The archived bridge
calculation proves

```text
<H,b>=A_8.
```

After replacing the nine `H`-syllables in the bridge by exact `H` alignment,
the resulting two-term covariance constraints on the relative chart unitary
are just

```text
rho(h)T=T rho(h)   (h in H),
rho(b)T=T rho(b).                                      (AHB1)
```

For a regular `A_8` chart, the zero-energy matrix space of `(AHB1)` is the full
commutant of the regular representation of `A_8`.  In particular it contains
many unitaries (the right regular representation), and hence neither it nor
any spectral low-energy superspace has a shrunk subspace.

Therefore the single-bridge mechanism is excellent for **propagating a
separately obtained H-alignment estimate**, as in the existing quantitative
`sqrt(2)` contradiction, but it cannot itself be turned into an
operator-scaling/noncommutative-rank certificate.  The interior word `19243`
or another genuinely mixed finite-coordinate operation is structurally
necessary after the paired covariance stage.
