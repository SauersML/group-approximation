title: Elementary two-by-two linear semidirect products over finitely generated rings have relative property (T)
---

If `R` is a finitely generated associative unital ring, then

```text
(EL_2(R) semidirect R^2, R^2)
```

has relative property `(T)`. In particular, some finite symmetric subset of
the semidirect product has a positive relative Kazhdan constant.
