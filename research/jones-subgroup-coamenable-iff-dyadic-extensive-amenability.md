**ESTABLISHED.** `\vec F` is co-amenable in `F` (i.e. `1_F ≺ λ_(F/\vec F)`) if and only if `F ↷ D` is
extensively amenable.

So the Aiello–Brothier–Conti question (Remark 4.7, arXiv:1901.10597) is literally
`thompson-f-dyadic-action-is-extensively-amenable`. The oriented subgroup gives no intermediate certificate.
