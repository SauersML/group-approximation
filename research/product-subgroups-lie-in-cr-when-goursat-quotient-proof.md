Direct proof (2026-09-18, lane bh-openq-papers). Not independently reviewed.

**0. Goursat.**
- `L_1 ⊴ F_1` and `L_2 ⊴ F_2`, and `θ : F_1/L_1 → F_2/L_2`, `aL_1 ↦ bL_2` for `(a,b) ∈ H`, is a
  well-defined isomorphism.
- `H = {(a,b) ∈ F_1 × F_2 : θ(aL_1) = bL_2}`.
- With `Q = F_1/L_1`, `φ_1(a) = aL_1` and `φ_2(b) = θ^(-1)(bL_2)`, this is
  `H = (φ_1 × φ_2)^(-1)(Δ_Q)`.

**1. Positive.** Assume `Q ∈ B_A`.
- *In `F_1 × F_2`.*
  - `Q × Q ∈ B_A`, and `Δ_Q` is a retract of it, via `(x,y) ↦ (x,x)`. So `Δ_Q ∈ CR(Q × Q)`
    by case 3 of `pbh-coset-wreaths-iff-identity-edge-hnns`.
  - `F_1 × F_2 ∈ B_A`, as a subgroup of `G_1 × G_2`.
  - By part 3 (preimages) of `pbh-coset-wreath-subgroups-closure-properties`,
    `H ∈ CR(F_1 × F_2)`.
- *Virtual retractions.*
  - By (LR) choose `K_i ≤ G_i` of finite index with `F_i ≤ K_i` and a retraction
    `r_i : K_i → F_i`.
  - Then `r = r_1 × r_2 : K_1 × K_2 → F_1 × F_2` is a retraction, and `K_1 × K_2 ∈ B_A`.
- *Pull back.* By part 3 again, `H' = r^(-1)(H) ∈ CR(K_1 × K_2)`.
- *Intersect.* `F_1 × F_2` is a retract of `K_1 × K_2`, so it lies in `CR(K_1 × K_2)` (case 3).
  By case 5 (finite intersections), `H' ∩ (F_1 × F_2) ∈ CR(K_1 × K_2)`. On `F_1 × F_2` the map `r`
  is the identity, so `H' ∩ (F_1 × F_2) = H`.
- *Return.* `K_1 × K_2` has finite index in `G_1 × G_2 ∈ B_A`. By part 2 of
  `pbh-coset-wreath-subgroups-closure-properties`, `H ∈ CR(G_1 × G_2)`.
- *The listed groups* come from (b) and (c) of the equivalence. The double comes from
  Britton's lemma, as in `virtual-retracts-satisfy-pbh-coset-wreath-closure-proof`, item 1.

**2. Necessity.**
- `H ∈ CR(G_1 × G_2)` makes membership in `H` decidable. This is the remark after "When (e)
  holds" in `pbh-coset-wreaths-iff-identity-edge-hnns`: `g ∈ H` iff `[g, m] = 1` in a `B_A`-group,
  whose finitely generated subgroups have solvable word problem.
- Fix finite generators of `F_1`. For a word `w` in them, `(w, 1) ∈ H` iff `w ∈ L_1` iff
  `φ_1(w) = 1`. So `Q` has solvable word problem.

**3. Free groups.**
- Free groups of finite rank are in `B_A` and have (LR) (Marshall Hall).
- A finitely generated `H` has finitely generated projections.
- *Mihailova realization.* Let `Q = ⟨x_1..x_m | r_1..r_s⟩` with `m ≥ 2` (add a generator if
  needed), and `P ≤ F_m × F_m` the fibre product of `F_m → Q`. Then `P` is generated by
  `{(x_i,x_i)}` and `{(r_j,1)}`, see `fibre-products-over-b-a-quotients-proof`. So `P` is
  finitely generated, with `p_i(P) = F_m`, `L_1 = ker(F_m → Q)` and `Q_P = Q`.
- *Under PBH.* The permutational conjecture puts every finitely generated `Q` with solvable
  word problem in `B_A`, which closes the chain of implications. ∎
