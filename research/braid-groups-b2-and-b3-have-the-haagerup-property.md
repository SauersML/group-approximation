`B_1 = 1`, `B_2 ≅ Z`, and `B_3` contains `P_3 ≅ F_2 × Z` with index 6, so all three admit proper conditionally
negative definite functions. Hence the negative answer to Zaremsky Problem 3.7 needs `n ≥ 4`, and `B_4` is the
smallest undecided case.
