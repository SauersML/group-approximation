title: Ambient expansion quantitatively forces long routes in near-unit-degree connected invariant subgraphs
root: true
---

Let G be a nonamenable d-regular Cayley graph with unnormalized edge Cheeger constant h. For an invariant random connected spanning subgraph H, put delta = E deg_H(o)/2 - 1. For every integer R >= 1,

    (1/d) sum_s P(d_H(o,s)>R) >= h/d - delta |B_G(o,R)|.

Thus for any sequence with delta tending to zero the liminf averaged tail at every fixed R is at least h/d. The mean route length has an explicit logarithmic lower bound as delta tends to zero. This is not a proof of either universal goal and is not claimed to be a new result in the literature.
