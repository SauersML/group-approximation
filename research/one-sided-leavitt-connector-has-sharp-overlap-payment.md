# A native one-sided Leavitt connector pays the missing child mass sharply once its support projections are decoded

**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `M` be a finite tracial von Neumann algebra, let
`P<=Q` be projections, put `E=Q-P`, and let `U` be unitary.  Then

```text
||UQU^*-P||_2^2
 = tau(Q)+tau(P)-2 tau(PUQU^*)
 >= tau(Q)-tau(P)=tau(E).                              (OLC1)
```

The constant is sharp: equality holds for `U=1`.  Equivalently, for the
support reflections `R_Q=1-2Q` and `R_P=1-2P`,

```text
tau(E) <= (1/4)||U R_Q U^* R_P-1||_2^2.                (OLC2)
```

Thus a single ordinary conjugacy row pays the entire complementary child
mass, with amplification-stable normalized-HS constant one.  No selector
permutation, common multiplicity gauge, or iteration is involved.

The binary Leavitt algebra contains the exact one-sided connector.  Put

```text
Q=e=s_0t_0=f_0+f_1,       P=f_0=s_(00)t_(00),
x=s_(00)t_0,              y=s_0t_(00).                 (OLC3)
```

Then `yx=Q` and `xy=P`.  In a `2x2` stabilization,

```text
J_x=E_12(x)E_21(y)E_12(x)
   = [[1-P,x],[y,1-Q]]                                  (OLC4)
```

is an involution carrying `diag(0,Q)` to `diag(P,0)`.  If `S` is the fixed
block-swap elementary word, `U=SJ_x` carries `diag(0,Q)` to `diag(0,P)`.
Hence the connector itself is already a fixed ordinary Steinberg/group word;
it is not a hypothetical rectangular operator.  In the exact properly
infinite Leavitt module `(OLC3)--(OLC4)` hold and both child projections are
nonzero, so completeness is preserved.  The finite-trace contradiction is
exactly the Hilbert-hotel deficit `tau(f_1)`.

This does **not** yet close the presentation.  Formula `(OLC2)` is a group
relator only after the decoded support reflections `R_Q` and `R_P` are
ordinary words on the same corrected carrier.  The coarse reflection is
available from the depth-one packet, whereas

```text
R_P=1-2s_(00)t_(00)                                    (OLC5)
```

is the depth-two joint-atom/controlled-AND reflection.  Existing Clifford
covariance exposes it only as `R_P tensor V`, with an arbitrary multiplicity
twist.  The elementary coefficient identity `UQU^*=P` does not by itself
identify the spectral projections produced by HS packet correction.

Consequently the native connector solves the **geometry, trace ledger,
completeness, and connector naming** parts.  The residual gate is precisely
same-carrier wordization/authentication of the fine support reflection.  If
the previously decoded crossed overlap is to be the paid complement `E`,
that identification is also part of this support incidence; it is not forced
merely by the coefficient `x`.
