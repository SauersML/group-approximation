Sections 1 to 3 of the artifact.

By the holonomy normal form the host is `< x_m : m in M \ {1} | cycle holonomies
of Gamma_E >`, one relator per cycle basis element. An edge coming from
`(s,m) ~_E (s',m')` carries the label `x_(m') x_m^(-1)`, which abelianizes to
`v_e = e_(m') - e_m` with the convention `e_1 = 0` for the identity address. So
the abelianization is `Z^(M \ {1}) / L`, where `L` is the image of the integral
cycle space under `e -> v_e`, generated by `b_1(Gamma_E)` vectors.

Taking one edge per independent coincidence, `Gamma_E` has `|S||M| - |S.M|`
edges on `|S|` vertices, so a connected `Gamma_E` has
`b_1(Gamma_E) = |S||M| - |S.M| - |S| + 1`. A free abelian group of rank
`|M| - 1` modulo a subgroup generated by that many vectors has rank at least

```text
(|M| - 1) - (|S||M| - |S.M| - |S| + 1) = |S.M| - (|S| - 1)(|M| - 1) - 1,
```

which is the bound. Finite abelianization forces the right side to be at most
zero, giving the cell bound and the coincidence count.

For the anchor form, let `psi` send every `e_m` to `1`. Then `psi(v_e)` is the
anchor charge of the edge, zero anchor charge on every cycle says `psi(L) = 0`,
and `psi` descends to a surjection onto `Z`. A subgroup with finite
abelianization has trivial image.

The lattice, not its rational span, is what decides membership: a nonzero
torsion image survives in a finite cyclic quotient, which is sofic.
