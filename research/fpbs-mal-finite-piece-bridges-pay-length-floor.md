**ESTABLISHED** through `fpbs-mal-finite-piece-bridges-pay-length-floor-proof`.

Let `L = F(a, b)` act freely and p.m.p. on `(X, μ)`, with `K = <a, bab^{-2}>`,
`|·|_{K,b}` the double-coset `b`-length, and `σ_b(X)` the fold-seed density
of `fpbs-mal-graphing-b-length-weighted-floor`.

**Claim (Theorem C).** Let `E ⊆ R_L` satisfy `R_K ∨ E = R_L`. Let
`F ⊆ E ∩ R_K` have finite classes, and let `G` be a graphing generating `E`
with all labels of `b`-length `≤ r`. If
- (i) `E` is hyperfinite, or
- (ii) `G` is a treeing of `E` in which every `F`-class is connected,

then

```text
r · relC(E ; F)  ≥  σ_b(X),
```

and this is `≥ 1/80` when the action is weakly contained in a Bernoulli
shift of `L`.

**Consequences** (artifact §4).
- **Cayley-subgraph bridges.** Bridges generated by `a`-, `c`- and
  `b`-edges on arbitrary Borel sets have `r = 1`. So their value is at least
  `σ_b ≥ 1/80`.
- **Approximate bridges.** Hyperfinite finite-piece bridges with value
  `ε → 0` need generators of `b`-length `≥ 1/(80 ε)`. The projected treeing
  is then a witness of exactly the P2 shape: mass `≤ ε`, with long labels.
  So the approximate-bridge route to `relC = 0` has no content beyond P2.
- **`Gamma_mal`.** The same holds for finite-piece bridges for
  `relC(E_{H_2}; E_{H_1})` on the Bernoulli shift, measured in
  `b_2`-length modulo `H_1`. This is part (D0).

**Invariant, and where each member dies.** The invariant is the `b`-length
of a generating graphing of `E`. Projecting to a transversal of the pieces
gives a treeing of `E|_Y`. Its labels are `K`-double translates of the
generators, and by Gaboriau's theorem its cost is at most `relC(E; F)`.
Theorem B, Step 3 (fold exactness), then forces the seed mass
`≤ r relC(E; F)` to be at least `σ_b`.

**Not covered.** Non-hyperfinite bridges without a treeing that has
connected pieces; mixed aperiodic/finite traces; and deep labels (P3, D1).
