**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `G` be an essentially principal étale groupoid whose unit
space is a Cantor set. Suppose `G` is purely infinite and minimal. Then any nontrivial
subgroup of `[[G]]` normalized by `D([[G]])` contains `D([[G]])`; in particular
`D([[G]])` is simple.

Here a clopen `A` is properly infinite if there are compact open `G`-sets `U, V` with
`s(U) = s(V) = A`, `r(U) ∪ r(V) ⊆ A` and `r(U) ∩ r(V) = ∅`. `G` is purely infinite if
every clopen subset of the unit space is properly infinite.

Source: H. Matui, *Topological full groups of one-sided shifts of finite type*, J. reine
angew. Math. 705 (2015), arXiv:1210.5800, Theorem `simple2` and Definition `pi`.
