title: Exact homotopy domination preserves quasidiagonality of all amenable traces
distinct_from:
  stw99-problem-x1-amenable-traces-quasidiagonal: that asks whether every amenable trace on every C-star algebra is quasidiagonal, with no exactness, homotopy-domination, or comparison-algebra hypothesis; this is Shulman's permanence theorem.
---

Let `A` and `B` be separable C-star algebras. Suppose there are star
homomorphisms

```text
f : A -> B,    g : B -> A
```

such that `g f` is homotopic through star homomorphisms to `id_A`. Assume
that at least one of `A` and `B` is exact.

If every amenable trace on `B` is quasidiagonal, then every amenable trace on
`A` is quasidiagonal.
