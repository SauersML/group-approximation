**ESTABLISHED CONDITIONAL LEMMA.**  Let `H` act transitively on `X=H/K`,
let `V:H->U(L)` be an exact unitary representation, and let `A_0` be a
self-adjoint involution in `V(K)'`.  Then

```text
A_(hK)=V(h) A_0 V(h)^*                                  (RCF1)
```

is transporter-independent and exactly `H`-equivariant.  Every relation on
a finite prototype of cosets transports to every translate with exactly the
same normalized Hilbert--Schmidt defect.  In particular no stabilizer word
length or filling area enters after `(V,A_0)` has been produced.

Suppose an approximate model can, on a retained carrier (allowing flexible
dimension), be corrected to such `(V,A_0)` with generator displacement and
base-lamp displacement at most `C delta`.  Then every **fixed finite**
prototype relation changes by `O(C delta)`, with a constant depending only on
that prototype, and all its contextual translates have the same bound.

This lemma does not supply the correction hypothesis.  Property `(T)` of
`H`, transitivity, or sharp two-transitivity alone does not exactify an
arbitrary normalized-HS microstate, nor does it round the raw lamp into
`V(K)'`.  It also does not assert uniform closeness between the corrected
field `(RCF1)` and raw word-addressed lamps at arbitrary transporter length.
Those are precisely the actor-stability and relative-commutant gates.
