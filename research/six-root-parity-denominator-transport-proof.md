Use `h x_ij(t) h^-1=x_ij((d_i/d_j)t)` with diagonal valuations
`(1,0,-1)`.  At parameter `2^(a-1)`, the three positive roots move above
level `a` and vanish, while the negative simple roots land at level `a-2`
and the negative long root lands at level `a-3`.  Steinberg commutators
between the surviving factors have level at least `2a-4`, which is zero
modulo level `a` for `a>=4`.  This gives `(SPT2)` and the stated orders.
