Let

```text
D=V4(a,b)*D8(c,d)
```

and let `F` be any finite-rank free group.  No nonsingular one-variable equation over
`D*F` can make `h=[c,d]` trivial in its relative quotient.  Therefore a
degree-three or degree-four search which treats every long corner as a free
formal generator cannot produce a genuine marked witness at any length.

An abstract two-relator incidence presentation can kill `h`, but if it is
claimed to come from a cyclic cover over `D*F`, either its proposed corner
map is noninjective or some asserted corner equality is false.  A successful
Leavitt construction must use a special identity of the Leavitt coefficient
group which is absent from the formal free-corner model.
