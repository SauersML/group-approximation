**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Every countable residually finite group `G` embeds in a 2-generator residually
finite group `Ĝ`.

Source: J. S. Wilson, *Embedding theorems for residually finite groups*, Math. Z. **174** (1980), 149–157, Theorem A.
