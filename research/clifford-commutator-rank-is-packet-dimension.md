Let `M(x) = (m_ij(x))` be an `m x m` matrix of affine-linear forms over `F_2`
in variables `x_1,...,x_k`.  Define the finite class-two 2-group `K(M)`
generated by `J, z_1,...,z_k, p_1,...,p_m, q_1,...,q_m` with `J` and the `z_l`
central involutions, the `p_i` commuting involutions, the `q_j` commuting
involutions, every `p_i` and `q_j` commuting with `J` and all `z_l`, and

```text
[p_i, q_j] = J^(c_ij) prod_l z_l^(a_ijl),
   where m_ij(x) = c_ij + sum_l a_ijl x_l.                        (CRP1)
```

The presentation is consistent: `K(M)` is realized as a central extension of
`F_2^(2m)` by `F_2^(k+1)` whose cocycle is read off the coefficient vectors of
the entries of `M`.

Fix the central character `J -> -1`, `z_l -> (-1)^(x_l)`, so that in the
resulting sector

```text
P_i Q_j = (-1)^(m_ij(x)) Q_j P_i.                                 (CRP2)
```

Then, writing `r = rank_F2 M(x)`, the sector algebra is

```text
M_(2^r)(C)  tensor  C[(C_2)^(2(m-r))],                            (CRP3)
```

so every irreducible complex representation of the sector has dimension exactly
`2^r`, and there are `2^(2(m-r))` isomorphic simple blocks.

This is the standard extraspecial / finite-Heisenberg phenomenon.  What is
being recorded is the dictionary itself: the commutation datum is a *bilinear
form between two independent Lagrangian-style generator sets*, not an
alternating form on one, so the admissible normalizations are `R M C` with
`R, C` independent, and the invariant is the ordinary rank rather than a
symplectic rank.
