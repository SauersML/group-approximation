## Proof

**Lower inclusion.**  `me = 0` in `R_0`, so `pi(mQ) = 0` and the two-sided
ideal generated by `mQ` lies in `K`.  That ideal is `m F_Z`, since the
`Q_(ij)` span `F_Z`.

**Upper inclusion.**  Let `a in K` and let `c` be any integer matrix
coefficient of its standard shift action.  The coefficient extraction
identity `(JSF3)` gives `Q T^i a S^j Q = cQ` in `J_Z`, so applying `pi`
gives `ce = 0` in `R_0`.  The additive order of `e` is exactly `m`, so
`m | c`.  Thus every coefficient of `a` is divisible by `m`; reducing the
standard action modulo `m` and using faithfulness of the shift
representation over `Z/m` (the prerequisite, at `C = Z/m`) gives that the
class of `a` in `J_(Z/m)` is zero, i.e. `a in m J_Z`.  This is `(FO2)`.

**Centrality.**  The symbol quotient `D` is commutative, so for any
`x in J_Z`, `[mx, J_Z]` has zero symbol and lies in `m F_Z`, hence in
`K`.  Therefore `m J_Z / K` is a central ideal of `R_0`.  Quotienting
`R_0` by it gives `J_Z / m J_Z = J_(Z/m)`.

**Fibre product.**  `D` is torsion-free as an abelian group, so the
kernel of `sigma` restricted to `m J_Z` is exactly `m F_Z`, which lies
inside `K`.  Consequently `K` is precisely the preimage in `m J_Z` of
`Lambda = sigma(K)`.  The map

```text
a |--> (a mod m J_Z,  sigma(a) mod Lambda)
```
therefore has kernel exactly `K`.  Its image is the fibre product
`(FO3)`: given a compatible pair, lift the first coordinate to some
`a in J_Z`; the difference between `sigma(a)` and the prescribed second
coordinate lies in `mD + Lambda`, and any element of `m J_Z` with a
prescribed symbol in `mD` can be added without changing the first
coordinate.

**Infinite additive order.**  Then `ce = 0` forces `c = 0`, so every
matrix coefficient of every `a in K` vanishes and faithfulness gives
`K = 0`.  Hence `J_Z` embeds in `R_0 <= R`.
