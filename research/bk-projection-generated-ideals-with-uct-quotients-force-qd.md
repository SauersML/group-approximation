**ESTABLISHED (Corollaries 2 and 3 of the artifact; lane proof, not externally
reviewed).**

1. **Problem VII for projection-generated ideal lattices.**  Let `E` be
   separable, nuclear and stably finite.  If every quotient of `E` satisfies
   the UCT and every nonzero ideal `J` of `E` contains a projection over `J`
   full in `J`, then `E` is quasidiagonal.  Two special cases:
   - finitely many ideals, each containing a full projection, with UCT simple
     subquotients;
   - real rank zero with finitely many ideals and UCT simple subquotients.
2. **QD extension property.**  Let `A` be separable, nuclear and quasidiagonal,
   with all quotients UCT and all nonzero ideals projection-generated.  Then an
   extension `0 -> K⊗A -> E -> B -> 0` with `B` separable, nuclear,
   quasidiagonal and UCT is quasidiagonal iff it is stably finite.  So `A` has
   the K_0-embedding property.

Item 2 is a new positive case of
`blackadar-kirchberg-for-uct-quotient-extensions`.  It covers non-split
extensions of classifiable pieces with projections, for example chains of three
or more simple UCT subquotients.  We do not know whether their tensor products
with `Q` are locally approximated by Moutzouris' class `Y`.
