Take k=F_2. This preserves the same binary Leavitt unit group used by the finite-presentation, simplicity and boundary consumers. The prerequisite is a Cairn application of Jihao Liu's [Theorem 1.2/6.7](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf) and Andreas Thom's [conditional normalization theorem](https://andreasthom.github.io/pdf/nonhyperlinear.pdf), with the native Leavitt construction separately credited in its proof.
