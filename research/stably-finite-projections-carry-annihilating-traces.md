**ESTABLISHED (Proposition B of the artifact; lane proof, not externally
reviewed).**  Let `E` be separable, exact and stably finite, `K ⊴ E`, and
`H = ∂(K_1(E/K)) ⊆ K_0(K)`.  For every nonzero projection `P ∈ M_n(K)` there is a
lower semicontinuous trace `σ_P` on `K` with the following properties:
- `σ_P(P) = 1`;
- its finiteness ideal is the ideal `I_P` generated by the entries of `P`;
- it annihilates `H` on that ideal, i.e. `σ_P^` vanishes on
  `ι_P^{-1}(H) = ∂_{I_P}(K_1(E/I_P))`.

Mechanism.
1. Exactness gives `ι_P^{-1}(H) = ker(K_0(I_P) -> K_0(E))`.
2. Stable finiteness of `Ẽ` makes this kernel singular in `K_0(I_P)`.
3. Lemma A of `research/artifacts/bk-radical-projection-trace-step-2026-09-13.md`
   gives a tracial state on the unital corner `P M_n(I_P) P` vanishing on the
   corresponding subgroup.
4. Extend it to `I_P` and set it `+∞` off `I_P`.

So the necessary condition of `af-killing-embeddings-force-projection-annihilating-traces`
always holds in the stably finite setting.
