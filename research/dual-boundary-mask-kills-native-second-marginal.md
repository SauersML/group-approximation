In the natural prefix representation let
lambda=epsilon composed with (t_11+t_010+t_0111), where epsilon
sums the coefficients of finitely supported boundary vectors. For
T=b_1^-1+b_2^-1+y+w+u, the five exact expansions on the complete
partition 000,001,010,011,1 in the artifact imply lambda T=0.
All three primitive roots are involutions.

Consequently U(g)=lambda(g^-1 e_(110^infinity)) satisfies L(U)=U,
and U(1)=1. The inverse ensures that every translated group-ring
row is annihilated with the correct order of multiplication.

Put U on odd input tracks and the constant 1 on even input tracks.
Each odd output vanishes by the assumed marginal. Call this output
X'. Since X' has zero odd tracks, bilinearity makes all its feedback
terms vanish, so F(X')=X'=F(X). The inputs X and X' differ already
at the identity. This proves the collision without a decoder,
computational assumption, or surjunctivity theorem.
