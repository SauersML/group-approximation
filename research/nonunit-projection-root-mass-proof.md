Full derivation in §2 of the artifact.

- **Generation.** `St_r(A_u × A_nu) = St_r(A_u) × St_r(A_nu)`, and `St_r(A_nu)` is generated by the root subgroups
  `x_ij(A_nu)`, `i ≠ j`.
- **Projection.** The fixed space of the normal factor `St_r(A_nu)` is the range of `P`: an irreducible
  constituent is either trivial on it or has no fixed vectors. So
  `range P = ∩_(i≠j) Fix(e_ij(A_nu))`.
- **Weyl symmetry.** The Weyl words `w_ij(1) = e_ij(1)e_ji(-1)e_ij(1)` lie in the group. They conjugate `e_12(A_nu)`
  onto every `e_ij(A_nu)` up to sign, so the fixed spaces all have the same dimension.
- **Fixed fraction.** `dim Fix(e_12(A_nu))/N = μ({χ : χ|A_nu = 1})`.
- **Count.** Codimensions of an intersection add at most, giving `τ(1 - P) <= r(r-1)·(1 - μ(χ|A_nu = 1))`.
