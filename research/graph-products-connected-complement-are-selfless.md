Let `Gamma` be a countable undirected simple graph with at least three
vertices and connected complement, and let `(G_v)_(v in V(Gamma))` be
nontrivial countable discrete groups.  Then

```text
C*_r(graph_product_(Gamma) G_v)
```

with its canonical trace is completely selfless.  Consequently it is simple,
has stable rank one and strict comparison, its canonical trace is its unique
normalized 2-quasitrace, and any two unital embeddings of the Jiang--Su
algebra into it are approximately unitarily equivalent.

No exactness, rapid-decay, amenability, linearity, or prior selflessness
hypothesis is imposed on the vertex groups.
