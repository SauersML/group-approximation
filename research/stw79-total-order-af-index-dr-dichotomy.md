Let `B` be a separable infinite-dimensional simple unital AF algebra with
totally ordered `K_0(B)`, and let `D` be a separable, simple, unital,
non-elementary nuclear UCT C*-algebra with torsion-free `K_0(D)` and finite
decomposition rank `d`. There is no restriction on `K_1(D)`. For every
unital essential extension

```text
0 -> J = K tensor B -> E -> D -> 0,
```

write `delta : K_1(D) -> K_0(B)` for its index boundary. Then

```text
dr(E) = d          if delta = 0,
dr(E) = infinity   if delta != 0.
```

Moreover, vanishing of `delta`, stable finiteness of `E`, quasidiagonality
of `E`, strong quasidiagonality of `E`, finite decomposition rank of `E`,
and the existence of an `E`-quasicentral approximate unit of projections
in `J` are equivalent. Every such `E` is nowhere scattered, has exactly
three ideals `0,J,E`, and has nuclear dimension at most `d+1`.

This is an established special case of the characterization sought in
`stw99-problem-lxxix-decomposition-rank-qd`, obtained by combining published
relative-quasidiagonality and decomposition-rank theorems. The root marker
tracks this restricted case, not a solution of the unrestricted problem.
No claim of a new literature theorem is made.
