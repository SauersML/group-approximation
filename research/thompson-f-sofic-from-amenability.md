Amenable groups are sofic. A Følner set `S` with `|sS Δ S| < epsilon |S|` for the generators gives a
sofic approximation on `S`: left multiplication where it stays in `S`, completed arbitrarily. That is
Weiss's original observation and the first instance recorded by Păunescu (Proposition 3.4 of
arXiv:1002.0605, as imported in `paunescu-sofic-action-class-permanence`, covers all actions of
amenable groups).
