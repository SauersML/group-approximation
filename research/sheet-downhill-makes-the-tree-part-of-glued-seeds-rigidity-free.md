## Setting

- **Sheets.** `Λ` is finitely generated and acts on a tree `T`, and each site `g ∈ Λ` lies in a
  sheet `S(g) ∈ V(T)`, equivariantly. For a splitting these are the vertex cosets, e.g. the cosets
  `hZ²` of the multiple HNN extensions in the tower. Adjacent sites lie in one sheet or in adjacent
  sheets.
- **The layers.** `Z` is an SFT with a base layer and a carrier layer `C`. The base contains a
  *pointer* `π`, constant on sheets, with values "origin" or an edge at the sheet.
- **(Fl) Origin flows.** For every `ω ∈ Y_D`, the pointers form a flow of `T` toward one origin
  sheet or toward one end. This is the pointer structure that
  `seeds-record-every-bass-serre-pointer` finds in every glued seed. It implies:
  - **(Forcing)** if `π(S)` is not the first edge from `S` toward `S'`, then `π(S')` is the first
    edge from `S'` toward `S`.
- **(DW) Downward determination.** Let `z` be a site whose sheet points to a parent sheet `P`. Then
  `c(z)` is a function of the base near `z` and of the carrier at finitely many sites of `P` within
  distance `R`. Carrier letters on an origin sheet obey arbitrary rules.
- **Hypotheses on the family.** In every `D`-family (`D >= R`, and at least the radius of the
  pointer rules):
  - **(F1)** the pointer marginals commute pairwise;
  - **(F2)** they commute with every base marginal.

  Both hold if the base is quantum rigid, as glued seeds are by monotone chains.

## Theorem 1 (pointer centrality)

Every pointer marginal commutes with every carrier marginal. Together with (F1) and (F2), the pointers
are central in the algebra generated by the family. Nothing is assumed about the other base letters
or about the carrier's rules inside a sheet.

*Proof.* Fix `b = [π(w) = e_0]`. We show `[b, C_c(z)] = 0` by induction on
`δ = d_T(S(z), S(w))`, for all `z` and `c` at once. Expand `C_c(z) = Σ_p [p] C_c(z)` over base
patterns `p` on the window of `z`. Each `[p]` commutes with `C_c(z)` inside the ball.
- **`δ = 0`.** By constancy along sheets, `b` equals the pointer marginal at `z`, as an operator.
  This follows from the rule `π(g) = π(g a)` for `a` in the vertex group, as in Step 1 of
  `e2-for-plane-lifts-is-clustered-floating-freedom`. So `b` sits at the same site as `C_c(z)`.
- **`p` does not point toward `S(w)`.** This covers the case where `p` says `S(z)` is the origin.
  By (Forcing), in every `ω ∈ Y_D` the pointer at `S(w)` is determined. So `[p] b = b [p] = ε [p]`
  in the base algebra, with `ε ∈ {0,1}`, using (F1) and (F2). Hence
  `[p] C_c(z) b = C_c(z) [p] b = ε [p] C_c(z) = b [p] C_c(z)`.
- **`p` points toward `S(w)`.** Then the parent sheet `P` is at distance `δ - 1` from `S(w)`. By (DW)
  and ball sums, `[p] C_c(z) = Σ_(g : Θ_p(g) = c) [p] ∏_(u) C_(g(u))(zu)`, with every `zu` in `P`.
  Each `C(zu)` commutes with `b` by induction, and `[p]` does by (F1) and (F2). ∎

## Theorem 2 (the tree part is free; E2 is one sheet)

Add three hypotheses.
- **(B)** All base marginals commute.
- **(UP)** Across each edge, the carrier on the parent sheet is also a function of the carrier on the
  child sheet near the edge, together with the base. So across-edge transport is bijective.
- **(Sheet)** In every `D`-family, the carrier marginals of one sheet commute with each other and
  with the base marginals of that sheet.
  - This holds, for example, if the sheet patterns of `(base, carrier)` form a `D`-quantum rigid
    `Z²`-SFT (or rigid SFT over the vertex group), and the restriction of a family to a sheet is a
    family for it. The restriction is a family, since forbidden sheet patterns are forbidden in `Z`.

Then `Z` is `D`-quantum rigid, over every field and on every module.

*Proof.*
- **Transport to any sheet.** Let `S(z) = S_0, …, S_k = S'` be the geodesic of `T`. Each step goes
  toward the pointer or against it. (DW) or (UP) then expresses each carrier letter of `S_j` as a sum
  of products of base patterns and carrier letters of `S_(j+1)`. So
  `C_c(z) ∈ alg(𝒜, carrier of S')`, where `𝒜` is the commutative base algebra.
- **Base letters are central.** Take `b` at `w` and transport `C_c(z)` to `S' = S(w)`. The
  coefficients lie in `𝒜`, which commutes with `b` by (B). The last step is (Sheet).
- **Carriers commute.** Transport both letters to one sheet. The coefficients are central, and
  (Sheet) finishes. ∎

## For the seed tower

- **bh-free-56's reversible space-time layer.** Suppose the carrier is transported sheet to sheet
  along the Bass–Serre tree of the rung, with bijective across-edge maps. Then the pointer and tree
  part of the glued seed costs nothing (Theorems 1–2), whatever the machine data. Gate 4 then equals
  (Sheet): rigidity of one sheet's carrier with its base.
  - On `Z²` sheets this is the permutive or no-two-sided-fault question that
    `machine-sheet-rigid-layers-force-zero-entropy-fates` already targets.
  - On the multiplicative edges, (UP)+(DW) is an exact requirement. The carrier of a sheet must be
    recoverable from its restriction to the edge sublattice (index `m` or `m²`, e.g.
    `Λ_i = mZ × mZ` against `Λ'_i = m²Z × Z`), and conversely, by sliding-block codes. A plain
    restriction of one carrier value per site generally loses information. The design has to store,
    at each edge site, the whole block of child values it stands for.
- **Relative seeds with a core subtree.** For a pointer flow toward a convex core subtree, as for
  `(K_M, T'_M)`, (Forcing) still holds when the target is outside the core. A projection argument does
  this: a geodesic to a convex subtree passes through the nearest point. For a target inside the core
  it can fail, so there the core's carrier must be handled by (Sheet) along the core, or the core
  transported toward a root.
- **bh-invent-15's gate (C_D).** Theorem 1 gives centrality of *pointer* marginals for any reader
  transported toward the origin or an end, with no hypothesis on the reader's alphabet. Other far
  base marginals, such as residues, commute with the linked bit `E` once `E` can be transported to
  their sheet, which is (UP). Without (UP) this remains the open part.

## Lesson for general BH

Glued seeds get rigidity of their tree part for free, in operator form. The pointer toward the origin
is a directed chain, and in the sense of `fate-couplings-are-rigid-only-along-directed-implication-chains`
it is the one transitive mechanism. Any carrier that each sheet inherits from its parent sheet
therefore commutes with the whole tree structure, whatever it computes and however many parent sites
it reads. With bijective transport across edges, all of E2 for such a seed is rigidity of a single
sheet.
- **Where CAP's quantum side lives.** In the tower, CAP is decided on one sheet and on the
  multiplicative edge rules, not on the tree.
- **The concrete obligation.** A permutive (or no-two-sided-fault) sheet carrier whose sheet can be
  reconstructed from its restriction to an index-`m²` sublattice.
