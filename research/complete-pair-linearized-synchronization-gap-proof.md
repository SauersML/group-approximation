Decompose an exact representation into code-character blocks.  On the
`chi`-to-`psi` block, the character difference is a codeword `z`, and the
linearized complete-pair commutators are the complete-graph coboundary
`z_j a_i-z_i a_j`.  Code distance gives its normalized Poincare gap and
identifies its kernel with simultaneous conjugation.  Equality-cloud
Poincare replaces occurrence tangents by their logical means at uniform
cost; bounded occurrence transfers the complete-pair estimate back to the
presentation.  Orthogonality of character blocks removes all dependence on
matrix dimension and multiplicity.
