**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `p` be odd.  In any group, suppose

```text
w^p=1,             J^2=1,             J central,
[w,s]=J.                                                (ODA1)
```

Then `J=1`.  Thus an odd-order Steinberg root cannot be identified with a
reflection in a marked `D_8` while retaining a nontrivial central sign.

This applies to the current affine-Leavitt packet.  Its finite splitting
field has characteristic `p>=5`, so

```text
w_A=x_12(A),                 w_A^p=1.                   (ODA2)
```

Although every finite-dimensional representation kills `w_A`, adjoining a
central involution by `[w_A,s]=J` kills `J` in the abstract group itself.
There is no characteristic-two specialization of the current active
idempotent construction.  Exact D8 grafts must therefore use a genuinely
involutive source such as Thompson V, while the affine-Leavitt route keeps
its native odd-order mark and must be attacked through its own HS decoder.
