Citation import. The theorem and its attribution to Lawton appear in:
* Ceccherini-Silberstein--Coornaert, *Cellular Automata and Groups*
  (Springer, 2010), Section 3.3;
* Seward, *Krieger's finite generator theorem for actions of countable groups
  II*, arXiv:1501.03367v4, Section 1. This source was read from the PDF on
  2026-09-11: "This conjecture has a simple topological proof when G is
  residually finite (this is due to Lawton ...)".

The periodic-point density argument recorded in the claim body is the whole
proof.
