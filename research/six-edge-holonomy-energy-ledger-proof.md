Expand the difference between the decoded six-fold product and the raw
six-fold product one factor at a time.  Supported decoded prefixes are
contractions and raw suffixes are unitary, so each summand costs only its
local normalized-HS transition error.  The literal raw product is the fixed
Coxeter holonomy word and its evaluation is controlled by its fixed van
Kampen area.

If the decoded holonomy moves one half of a carrier to an independent half,
the two projections are at squared normalized-HS distance one half of the
carrier trace.  Conjugating a projection by a unitary changes it by at most
twice the unitary's distance from identity, giving the carrier floor
`mu/8`.  Compression to successive carrier projections identifies failure
of a raw transition to remain in the next carrier with the same local error,
yielding the rounding-or-leakage dichotomy.
