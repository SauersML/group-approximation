**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Γ_R`, `Λ_v`, `Q = Γ_R/Λ_v` and `Y = Λ_v\T_v`, the Cayley graph of
`W = PGL_2(Z)` on `x, y, z`, be as in `radu-horizontal-quotient-is-arithmetic-lattice`. Let `C` be
the image of `<a,b,c>` in `Q`, which is the stabilizer of the base vertex `o` of `Y`. Let
`χ : C → {±1}` be the sign of `C` acting on `{xo, zo}`, so `χ(a) = χ(b) = 1` and `χ(c) = −1`, and put
`C_χ = ker χ = <a, b, cac, cbc>`.

1. **Cocycle embedding.** `d(g) = π_*[o, g·o] ∈ C_1(Y; Z)`, the projection of the geodesic in `T_v`,
   is a 1-cocycle, and `g ↦ (d(g), ḡ)` is a homomorphism `Γ_R → C_1(Y) ⋊ Q` with kernel
   `[Λ_v, Λ_v]`. So

   ```text
   G_2 = Γ_R/[Λ_v, Λ_v]  ↪  C_1(Y) ⋊ Q  ↪  Z[Q/C ⊔ Q/C_χ] ⋊ Q  ↪  Z^2 wr_(Q/C_χ) Q.
   ```

   Here the oriented `y`-edges form one orbit `Q/C` and the oriented `{x,z}`-edges one orbit `Q/C_χ`.
2. **The mark in the host.** `D_4 = <x, c> ⊂ Q` has order 8, normalizes `C_χ`, and meets it
   trivially. Let `ε` be the restriction to `D_4` of the sign character `ψ'` of
   `radu-delta-squared-orbit-is-signed-permutation-module` (`ε(x) = ε(z) = ε(c) = −1`), and put
   `e_0 = (o → xo)`. Then

   ```text
   (xz)^2 ↦ Σ_(g ∈ D_4) ε(g) g·e_0,        (xz)^4 ↦ 2 Σ_(g ∈ D_4) ε(g) g·e_0  ≠ 0.
   ```

3. **Soficity reduction.** If the set action `Q ↷ Q/C_χ` is sofic in the sense of
   Gao–Kunnawalkam Elayavalli–Patchell, Definition 2.1(5), then `G_2` is sofic. This is their
   Theorem 3.6 with lamp group `Z^2`, since `Q` is residually finite and subgroups of sofic groups
   are sofic.

**What changes.** The "split host" attempt recorded on `radu-nilpotent-by-arithmetic-quotients-are-sofic`
used the square complex `T_h × Y`. Its host is a quotient `C_1/B_1`, and soficity does not pass to
quotients. The graph `Y` has no 2-cells, so the host is an honest generalized wreath product. For
`k = 2` the positive horn becomes one question about one coset action, `radu-edge-coset-action-is-sofic`.
