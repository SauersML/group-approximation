Dead: the attempt bounds the normalized pivotal budget on the whole parameter interval and concludes that the critical neighborhood carries less than all of it. The whole-interval budget is already known and converges to exactly minus log rho (fpbs-integrated-pivotal-total-budget), so it can never give a strict inequality on a subinterval. An O(n) bound on the unnormalized total is automatic. What is needed is a bound that localizes near criticality.
