The following are equivalent.

1. There are a simple unital infinite C\*-algebra `B` and a unital embedding
   of the CAR algebra into `B` whose image contains a nonzero finite
   projection.
2. There are a simple unital C\*-algebra `A` with finite unit and a unital
   embedding of the CAR algebra into `A` such that `M_2(A)` has infinite
   unit.

Thus STW Problem LXVI is exactly
`stw99-lxvi-finite-car-corner-with-infinite-double`.
