Source: J. Belk, C. Bleak, F. Matucci, M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Hyperbolic groups satisfy the
Boone--Higman conjecture*, arXiv:2309.06224v3, TeX source read on MSI
(`gqsrc/bh-hhg/x/hyperbolic_boone_higman.tex`). Theorems are numbered within sections; labels are
given because the numbers below are read off the counter order, not printed in the TeX.

**Step 1. The four inputs from BBMZ.** Let `G ≤ R_{Γ,E}` be a full, contracting RSG.
- (a) *Finite presentation.* Theorem 3.1 (label `thrm:fin_pres`): "Every full, contracting RSG
  is finitely presented."
- (b) *High transitivity.* Proposition 5.4 (label `prop:HighlyTransitiveAction`): "Let X be a
  Hausdorff space that has a basis of clopen sets, and let G be any full group of homeomorphisms
  of X. Then G acts highly transitively (and hence oligomorphically) on each of its orbits."
  Belk--Fournier-Facio--Hyde--Zaremsky cite this as [BBMZ, Proposition 5.4], which fixes the
  number.
- (c) *Stabilizers.* Proposition `prop:fin_gen_stabs` (Section 5): "Let G ≤ R_{Γ,E} be a full,
  contracting RSG, and let S′ be a finite set of rational points in E. Then the stabilizer
  Stab_G(S′) is finitely generated."
- (d) *Faithfulness.* In the proof of Proposition 5.1 (label `prop:contr_to_simple`): "Let S be
  the G-orbit of a rational point ω in E. Note that S is dense in E, so G acts faithfully on S."

**Step 2. Type (A).** Let `S` be the `G`-orbit of a rational point. The action of `G` on `S` is
faithful by (d); `G` is finitely presented by (a); point stabilizers are finitely generated by (c)
with `|S′| = 1`; and by (b) `G` is transitive on ordered pairs of distinct points, so it has one
orbit of two-element subsets. These are the four conditions of type (A) in
`type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups`. This proves item 1 of the target. It is the
same verification BBMZ perform for Zaremsky's oligomorphic criterion; nothing is added except
the observation that it is a type (A) verification.

**Step 3. Inputs.**
- *Hyperbolic groups.* Theorem 4.1 (label `thrm:hyp_to_contracting`; = Theorem A,
  `thrm:hyp_embed`): "Every hyperbolic group embeds into a full, contracting RSG." This is
  `hyperbolic-groups-embed-in-contracting-rsgs`.
- *Contracting self-similar groups.* BBMZ Example `ex:rn` shows that the Röver--Nekrashevych
  group `V_n(G)` of a finite-state self-similar group `G` is a full RSG, and the remark after
  their definition of contracting says that for Röver--Nekrashevych groups contracting is
  equivalent to contracting of the self-similar group. A contracting self-similar group is
  finite-state, since every element has finitely many restrictions outside its finite nucleus.
  So a contracting self-similar group `G` lies in the full contracting RSG `V_n(G)`, which is
  how BBMZ derive Corollary 5.2 (label `cor:RN_BH`).
- *Subgroups.* `B_A` is closed under subgroups by definition.

With Step 2, this proves item 2.

**Step 4. Products.** `B_A` is closed under finite direct products and finite-index overgroups
(`boone-higman-type-a-class-closed-under-finite-extensions`, Zaremsky Propositions 5.5--5.6),
under free products (BFFHZ Corollary F, recorded in
`aut-free-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`), and under graph products over finite
graphs (`pbh-class-closed-under-graph-products`, re-verified twice on 2026-09-18). Every member of
`B_A` satisfies Boone--Higman (`type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups`). The mixed
examples use `aut-free-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`,
`char-zero-linear-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`, the spherical Artin nodes on main
(for example `e-type-artin-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`), and `Z^m ≤ V`. This
proves item 3.

**Step 5. Named-problem bearing.** Hyperbolic groups are automatic; finite direct products and
graph products of automatic groups are automatic (Hermiller--Meier 1995, not read at source).
Products of CAT(0) groups are CAT(0). These give the two partial statements on the target. They
are the only unpinned inputs, and neither is used in items 1--3.

**Scope.** Nothing here touches hyperbolic groups relative to anything, closed-surface mapping
class groups, or other items of Problem 5.3; it only moves the literature's hyperbolic and
contracting inputs from the Boone--Higman class into `B_A`.
