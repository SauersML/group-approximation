**Statement (OPEN).** Let `B = C*(⊕_k σ_k(SL(3,Z)))`, where `σ_k` is the
permutation representation on `ℓ²(P²(F_{p_k})) ⊖ C1` and `p_k → ∞`. Every bounded
2-quasitrace on `B` is a trace.

**Equivalent form.** By `sl3z-ozawa-block-qt-linearity-iff-corona-kernel-invisible`,
this is equivalent to the statement that every bounded 2-quasitrace on the corona
`A = B/⊕_k M_{n_k}` vanishes on the nonzero traceless ideal
`K = ker(A → C*_r(SL(3,Z)))`.

**Why it matters.**
- If true, it gives a non-exact algebra with only tracial quasitraces. It is not
  covered by Haagerup, by products of bounded nuclear dimension, or by stable
  ideals.
- Its negation `sl3z-ozawa-block-algebra-has-nontracial-quasitrace` refutes STW
  Problem I.

## Attempts

- **Exactness of `K`.** Would prove it (artifact Prop. 4.2). Open. `B` sits in the
  uniform Roe algebra of an expander family, so no ambient exactness is available,
  and the extension `0 → ⊕M → B → A` need not be locally split.
- **Cuntz infinitesimality of `K_+`.** Would prove it (Prop. 4.1). Rank-thin
  elements are infinitesimal in `∏M/⊕M`, but the comparison partial isometries lie
  outside `A`. Strict comparison of `A` with respect to `τ_0` is unknown.
- **Displacement by `Γ`.** `approximately-orthogonal-unitary-translates-kill-quasitraces`
  shows that elements with arbitrarily many approximately orthogonal unitary
  translates are null for every bounded quasitrace. The null elements form the
  positive part of a closed ideal. So it suffices that `K` be generated as a closed
  ideal by such elements. Elements concentrated near finitely many low-height points
  or lines of `P²(Q)` should qualify, but this was not proved. Dies at generation:
  nothing shows `K` is generated by localized elements, and expander geometry
  permits non-local ghost-type elements.
- **Haagerup directly.** Dead: `B` is not exact
  (`kazhdan-fd-block-representation-algebras-are-not-exact`).
- **Exactness of the corona `A`.** Would prove it, since exact `A` makes `K` exact.
  Open, but not excluded. The Wassermann–Kazhdan certificate does not descend to
  `A`: the corona has no finite-dimensional representation, so
  `Σ ρ_∞(s) ⊗ ρ̄(s)` has no invariant vectors and its Kazhdan projection is zero
  (artifact §5, item 3). The known non-exactness of `B` may sit entirely in the
  non-split extension by `⊕M`.
