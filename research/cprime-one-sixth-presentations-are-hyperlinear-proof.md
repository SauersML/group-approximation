Martin--Steenbock (arXiv:1409.3678) prove cubulability of finite classical
`C'(1/6)` quotients of free products of cubulable groups.  Apply this to the
free product of `k` infinite cyclic groups.  The same finite `C'(1/6)`
presentation is word-hyperbolic by classical small-cancellation theory.
Agol--Groves--Manning (arXiv:1204.2810) prove that hyperbolic cubulated groups
are virtually special, hence linear/residually finite.  Residually finite
groups are sofic, and `sofic-implies-hyperlinear` is established in this
repository.  This proves the claim outright.
