For

```text
A=PSL_2(Z[1/2]),  C=PSL_2(Z),
```

the transitive action `A action A/C`, equivalently the action on one color
of vertices in the dyadic Bruhat--Tits tree, is a sofic action in the sense
of Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell.

The current concrete route is
`dyadic-random-lifts-produce-sofic-vertex-action`.  It isolates the needed
finite object as amplified compatible permutation actions of the two
modular vertices with Bass--Serre normal-form separation.
