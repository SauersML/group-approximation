**ESTABLISHED** through `thompson-gap-witnesses-need-nonamenable-image-proof` (written proof). Unreviewed. The
Frobenius commutator lemma is classical (proof of Jordan's theorem); the extension to virtually solvable images
and its use against relator images of a simple group are the content here.

## Statement

All norms are operator norms. "Virtually solvable" means having a solvable subgroup of finite index.

**(A) General form.** Let `Q` be a group, `R` a subset of `Q`, and `Gamma = Q/<<R>>`. Assume `Gamma` has no
nontrivial virtually solvable quotient. This holds, for example, when `Gamma` is infinite and simple. Let
`sigma : Q -> U(n)` be a homomorphism whose image `L = sigma(Q)` is virtually solvable, and suppose

```text
||sigma(r) - 1|| < 1/2      for every r in R.
```

Then `L` is abelian, and `L` is generated by `sigma(R)`. In particular `sigma` factors through `Q^ab`.

**(B) Thompson's V.** Let `G0 = S4 *_<a> S3 = <a,b,c | a^2, b^3, (ab)^4, c^2, (ac)^3>` and `r5, ..., r8` be as in
`thompson-v-is-a-four-relator-quotient-of-s4-amalgam-s3`, with `D(rho) = max_i ||rho(r_i) - 1||`. If
`rho : G0 -> U(n)` has virtually solvable image and `rho` is not trivial, then

```text
D(rho) >= 1/2 .
```

More precisely, `D(rho) < 1/2` forces `rho` to be trivial. The sign character `eta` (`a, c -> -1`, `b -> 1`)
is the only nontrivial character of `G0`, and `eta(r5) = eta(r8) = -1`.

**(C) Thompson's T.** Let `G0' = Z/4 * Z/3 = <alpha> * <beta>` with the Lochak--Schneps relators `r_1, r_2`,
`r_P = (beta alpha)^5` of `thompson-t-lochak-schneps-presentation`. If `A^4 = B^3 = 1` in `U(n)`, the pair
`(A,B)` is not `(1,1)`, and `<A,B>` is virtually solvable, then `D(A,B) >= 1/2`. The one-dimensional pairs
already have `D >= 2 sin(pi/12) = 0.5176...`.

**(D) Amenable form (via Tits).** By the Tits alternative (J. Tits, *Free subgroups in linear groups*,
J. Algebra 20 (1972), Theorem 1: a finitely generated linear group over a field of characteristic 0 either
contains a nonabelian free subgroup or is virtually solvable), "virtually solvable" in (B) and (C) may be
replaced by "amenable", or by "contains no nonabelian free subgroup". Equivalently:

**every nontrivial representation of `G0` (for V) or of `Z/4 * Z/3` (for T) with relator defect below `1/2` has
an image containing a nonabelian free group.**

Finite, monomial (induced from characters) and virtually abelian images are special cases.

## Consequences for the hole `thompson-v-is-not-mf` (and for `thompson-t-has-full-mf-radical`)

- **Where every such witness dies.** Suppose a representation has virtually solvable image and all
  relator images within `1/2` of `1`.
  - The relator images and all their conjugates lie in the Frobenius ball, and they commute.
  - Their normal closure is abelian, and the quotient by it is a virtually solvable quotient of a simple group,
    so it is trivial. The whole image is therefore abelian.
  - The representation then factors through `G0^ab = Z/2` for V, or `Z/12` for T, where the relator `r5`
    (for V) or `r_P` (for T) pays at least `2 sin(pi/12) > 1/2`.
- **What an MF witness for V must look like.** A sequence of nontrivial irreducibles `sigma_j` with
  `D(sigma_j) -> 0`, as in criterion (3) of `thompson-v-mf-iff-opnorm-s4-s3-defect-gap-vanishes`, has
  `sigma_j(G0)` containing `F_2` once `D(sigma_j) < 1/2`.
  - In such a representation the Zassenhaus descent `h -> [g, h]` on relator images tends to the identity
    without ever reaching it, and never enters a torus normalized by `g`.
  - No finite quotient of `G0`, no induced-from-character or monomial model, and no representation of a
    virtually solvable group can serve as a witness, in any dimension.
- **Calibration.** The planted PSL(2,17) control of `thompson-v-mf-iff-opnorm-s4-s3-defect-gap-proof` has exact
  solutions with **finite** image. By (A), no group without virtually solvable quotients has any finite-image
  solution with defect below 1/2. So finite controls are structurally the wrong calibration class for V and T.
- **What is left of the hole.** V is not MF iff the uniform gap holds on the **Tits-free locus**: nontrivial
  irreducibles of `G0` whose image contains a nonabelian free group. Below 1/2 the amenable-image locus is
  empty.

## Check

`experiments/amenable-image-gap-witnesses-2026-09-17/abelian_and_monomial_check.py` computes the following.
1. `eta(r5..r8) = (-1, 1, 1, -1)`, exactly, from the Bleak--Quick words.
2. The minimum over nontrivial characters of `Z/4 x Z/3` of the three-relator defect, exactly: it is
   `2 sin(pi/12)`.
3. As a numerical sanity check, 419 random nontrivial monomial representations of `G0` (sums of inductions of
   `mu_12`-valued characters of random subgroups of `S4` and `S3`, glued along `a` by random monomial matrices;
   all relations of `G0` verified). Their minimum `D` is `1.9499`, consistent with `D >= 1/2`.
