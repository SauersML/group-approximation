**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed; queued with sk-referee-2).**

Setting:
- `(X,T)` is an infinite Cantor minimal system and `R = LC(X,F_2) ⋊_T Z`, in the conventions of
  `simple_kazhdan_sofic_group.tex`; `G = EL_3(R)`.
- For `g ∈ [[T]]`, `w_g = Σ_n u^n e_{n_g = n}`, and `I : [[T]] -> Z` is the index map.

- **(A)** `g ↦ diag(w_g, 1, 1)` is an injective homomorphism `[[T]]' -> G`.
- **(B)** `g ↦ diag(w_g, u^(-I(g)), 1)` is an injective homomorphism `[[T]] -> G`.

**Consequence.** For every infinite minimal subshift `X`, `G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ Z)` contains `[[T]]'`. That group is:
- infinite, finitely generated and simple (`derived-full-group-of-cantor-minimal-system-is-simple`,
  `derived-full-group-fg-iff-minimal-subshift`);
- amenable (`minimal-cantor-full-groups-are-amenable`).

So the infinite simple Kazhdan LEF group `G_X` (`infinite-simple-kazhdan-hyperlinear-group`) contains an infinite finitely
generated simple amenable group coming from the same subshift.

**Mechanism.**
- (A): `w` is an injective homomorphism into `R^×`, and
  `diag(aba^(-1)b^(-1),1,1) = diag(a,a^(-1),1) diag(b,b^(-1),1) diag((ba)^(-1),ba,1)` is a product of Whitehead matrices.
  The naive map `diag(w_g, w_g^(-1), 1)` is not a homomorphism (`whitehead-diagonal-map-is-not-multiplicative`).
- (B): the second slot cancels the `K_1` class of `u`.
  - `[[T]]` is generated by `T` and by level permutations of Kakutani–Rokhlin towers. The proof uses the flux across 0 on one orbit.
  - `T` maps to `diag(u,u^(-1),1)`, a Whitehead matrix.
  - Level permutations map into the tower copies of `GL_(3h)(F_2) = SL_(3h)(F_2)`, which lie in `G` (the tower transvections of the note).

**Scope.**
- (B) uses `GL_d(F_2) = SL_d(F_2)`. Over `F_q` with `q` odd, the tower step fails for odd permutations.
- (A) holds over every field.
- On `[[T]]`, the note's finite models restrict to periodic permutation models (artifact part 1 §3).
