An exact finite-dimensional representation of the elementary abelian lamp
group is the direct sum of its character spaces.  Record each character
with multiplicity equal to the rank of its spectral projection.  Conjugacy
covariance by an actor generator transports the corresponding projections;
vanishing squared-HS transport energy is exactly vanishing weighted mismatch
of the character multiset.  The canonical trace of a lamp word is its
Fourier moment, so canonical trace convergence makes all nontrivial finite
cylinder Fourier coefficients vanish, which is convergence to Haar product
measure.

For the exact co-density assertion, a finite orbit character has a
finite-index stabilizer `K`.  Its core `N` is finite index and normal in
`A`.  Equality of the images of `C` and `A` in every finite quotient gives
`CN=A`; consequently `N` is transitive on `A/C`.  An `N`-fixed coloring is
constant, so its value on every two-point augmentation lamp is trivial.

Finally, Pontryagin Fourier transform identifies `L(L)` with
`L^infinity(L_hat)` carrying Haar measure, and semidirect-product covariance
identifies `L(W)` with the crossed product in `(AGP3)`.  Hyperlinearity of a
countable group is Connes embeddability of its canonical group von Neumann
algebra.  These observations prove every assertion in the target.
