Let `phi=1_H` be the base-coset coefficient of `lambda_(A/H)`.  For orbit
vectors `delta_(xH)` one has

```text
<delta_(xH),delta_(yH)>=1_(xH=yH).                     (TPC1)
```

Every mixed Gram coefficient of a finite orbit list is already determined
by `(TPC1)`.  Under a tensor power,

```text
<delta_(xH)^tensor k,delta_(yH)^tensor k>
 =1_(xH=yH)^k=1_(xH=yH).                               (TPC2)
```

Direct sums, multiplicity amplification, and tensoring by an exact spectator
likewise leave all normalized coefficients of the distinguished orbit
unchanged.  In particular they provide neither:

1. a permutation carrier `Omega_n`,
2. liftable coordinate maps `ell_(x,n):Omega_n->N`, nor
3. the density-one covariance identities `(LHC2)`.

This is a sharp data-level no-go, not a proof that promotion is impossible:
an additional rounding theorem could construct charts from the unitary
models.  It proves that scalar coefficient multiplication/tensor
amplification alone supplies no new moment family beyond the already known
root profile.
