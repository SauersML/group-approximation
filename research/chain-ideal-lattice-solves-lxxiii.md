title: Problem LXXIII holds when the closed ideal lattice is linearly ordered
---

Let `A` be a C-star algebra with no nonzero finite-dimensional
representations, and assume its closed two-sided ideals are linearly ordered
by inclusion.  Then, for every `a in A_+` and `epsilon>0`, there exists
`x in A` such that

```text
x^2=0,       (a-epsilon)_+ in Ideal(x).
```

No separability, nuclearity, unitality, stable-rank, real-rank, or dimension
hypothesis is imposed.
