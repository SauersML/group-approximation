ESTABLISHED.  Let `A` be a unital
star algebra generated by finitely many context PVMs

```text
{p_(c,a):a in R_c},       sum_a p_(c,a)=1,              (TCM1)
```

and let `pi:A->M` have weakly dense image in a finite von Neumann algebra
with faithful normal trace `tau`.  Fix a finite menu `P` of support profiles
`K=(K_c)_c`, where `K_c subseteq R_c`, and put

```text
E_(c,K)=sum_(a in K_c) pi(p_(c,a)).                     (TCM2)
```

For a projection `E in M`, write

```text
core_Z(E)=1-z(1-E),                                     (TCM3)
```

where `z(x)` is the central support of `x`.  This is the largest central
projection dominated by `E`.  Define the central carrier of a profile by

```text
z_K=meet_c core_Z(E_(c,K)).                             (TCM4)
```

Then the following are equivalent.

1. The supplied trace is a finite convex mixture

   ```text
   tau=sum_(K in P) lambda_K tau_K,                     (TCM5)
   ```

   after zero coefficients are omitted, where every `tau_K` is a normal
   tracial state on `M` and

   ```text
   tau_K(pi(p_(c,a)))=0       whenever a notin K_c.     (TCM6)
   ```

2. There is a central orthogonal partition of unity `(w_K)_(K in P)` with

   ```text
   w_K <= z_K.                                          (TCM7)
   ```

3. The central profile carriers cover the identity:

   ```text
   join_(K in P) z_K=1.                                 (TCM8)
   ```

Thus an exact model-dependent menu of support branches is possible **only
by decomposing the model over its center**.  Noncentral local cap
projections do not provide another selection mechanism.

## Proof

Assume `(TCM5)`.  Put `psi_K=lambda_K tau_K`.  Since

```text
0 <= psi_K <= tau,
```

the Radon--Nikodym theorem for finite von Neumann algebras gives a bounded
positive density `h_K in M` such that

```text
psi_K(x)=tau(h_K x),       sum_K h_K=1.                 (TCM9)
```

Traciality forces `h_K` to be central.  Indeed for `x,y in M`,

```text
0=psi_K(xy-yx)=tau((h_K x-x h_K)y).
```

Taking `y=(h_K x-x h_K)^*` gives `[h_K,x]=0`.

If `a notin K_c`, `(TCM6)` and faithfulness give

```text
0=tau(h_K pi(p_(c,a)))
 =tau(h_K^(1/2) pi(p_(c,a)) h_K^(1/2)),
```

so `h_K pi(p_(c,a))=0`.  The central support projection
`s_K=supp(h_K)` is therefore dominated by every `E_(c,K)`, hence
`s_K<=z_K`.  Equation `(TCM9)` gives `join_K s_K=1`.  Ordering the finite
menu and setting

```text
w_1=s_1,
w_i=s_i meet (1-join_(j<i)s_j)                          (TCM10)
```

produces a central orthogonal partition with `w_K<=z_K`.  This proves
`1 => 2`, while `2 => 3` is immediate.

Conversely, from `(TCM7)` define, for each nonzero `w_K`,

```text
tau_K(x)=tau(w_K x)/tau(w_K),       lambda_K=tau(w_K). (TCM11)
```

Centrality makes `tau_K` tracial.  Since `w_K<=E_(c,K)`, it vanishes on
every excluded atom, and the partition of unity gives `(TCM5)`.  Finally,
`(TCM8)` implies `(TCM7)` by the same finite disjointification as `(TCM10)`.

## Exact-compression form

Let `q in M` be a nonzero projection.  The postselected functional

```text
phi_q(x)=tau(qxq)/tau(q)=tau(qx)/tau(q)                 (TCM12)
```

is tracial on `M` only if `q` is central, by the density argument above.
There is an even stronger multiplicative statement.  If

```text
x |-> q pi(x) q                                         (TCM13)
```

is a unital star homomorphism into `qMq`, then `q` commutes with every
unitary in `pi(A)`: equality in the two Schwarz identities gives

```text
(1-q)pi(u)q=0=q pi(u)(1-q).
```

Weak density then puts `q` in `Z(M)`.  If a finite direct sum of such
compressions is multiplicative, each coordinate compression is
multiplicative, so direct-sum bookkeeping cannot cancel this obstruction.

Nor does putting the selector in a larger multiplicity commutant evade the
classification.  Let `M subseteq N` be a unital inclusion of finite von
Neumann algebras with faithful trace `Tr`, and let `q in M' cap N` be a
nonzero projection.  The normalized trace on the reducing compression is

```text
x |-> Tr(qx)/Tr(q).
```

If `E_M:N->M` is the trace-preserving conditional expectation, its density
on `M` is `E_M(q)/Tr(q)`.  For `x in M`, bimodularity and `qx=xq` give

```text
xE_M(q)=E_M(xq)=E_M(qx)=E_M(q)x,
```

so that density is central.  A commutant selector can change the weights of
central representation types, but cannot change the local support inside a
factor type.

Consequently the theorem is also a **dilation firewall**.  Suppose the
given model is embedded trace preservingly in a larger finite tracial
algebra `N`, and projections `(q_K)` form a partition of unity, commute with
the old model, and obey

```text
q_K pi(p_(c,a))=0             for a notin K_c.          (TCM14)
```

The normalized traces on `q_K N q_K`, restricted to the old algebra, give
exactly a decomposition `(TCM5)`.  Hence such an external selector exists
only if the original central cores already satisfy `(TCM8)`.  This includes
central selector ancillas and arbitrary noncentral projections in the
multiplicity commutant.  A tracial dilation cannot refine a full-support
factor model into cap branches while preserving that model's trace.

## Exact LCS-OR interface

This also settles the proposal to make the seven cap labels jointly
classical and then feed their joint profiles into the finite exact LCS OR.
Let `(z_(c,lambda))_lambda` be mutually commuting label PVMs and put

```text
q_alpha=product_c z_(c,alpha_c).                       (TCM15)
```

The nonzero `q_alpha` form a joint profile partition.  Mutual commutation of
the labels alone is insufficient: the conditional functional
`x |-> Tr(q_alpha x)/Tr(q_alpha)` need not be tracial or multiplicative on
the old payload algebra.

The product-sign LCS OR obtains an exact branch precisely because every
activation projection commutes with that branch's payload.  If the dynamic
profile projections in `(TCM15)` obey this same requirement and the local
incidence equations give

```text
q_alpha pi(p_(c,t))=0    when alpha_c(t)=0,             (TCM16)
```

then the normalized corner traces are cap-supported tracial states.  The
conditional-expectation argument above turns them into central densities in
the old algebra, so `(TCM15)--(TCM16)` exist exactly when the central profile
carriers already cover the identity.  Conversely a central partition
`(w_alpha)` subordinate to those carriers defines the required commuting
label PVMs by summing the `w_alpha` with a fixed coordinate.

Thus there is no intermediate selector regime relevant to exact OR:

```text
labels commute only with each other
    => joint outcomes exist but do not give tracial LCS branches;

labels also reduce the payload, as exact OR requires
    => the selector is exactly a central cap-profile decomposition.       (TCM17)
```

In particular, a full-support factor model cannot acquire an OR-compatible
cap selector by adjoining a jointly classical register.

## Consequences for the R-star petals

For `R_*`, take `P` to be any finite menu of Fano-cap profiles and let
`E_(c,K)` be the sum of the surviving atoms implemented by the signed
three-face petals.  A perfect tracial model can be split among those affine
branches exactly when `(TCM8)` holds.  In particular, if `M` is a factor and
one context has all seven atoms nonzero, then every Fano-cap projection at
that context is proper.  A proper projection in a factor has zero central
core, so every `z_K=0` and no cap menu decomposes the model.

This applies to the diagonal/Fourier full-support factor model in
`extreme-traces-and-local-pruning-do-not-select-fano-caps`.  More generally,
it proves that the unresolved phrase “model-selective or shared noncentral
selection” cannot mean tracial postselection, exact corner compression, or
a direct sum of such compressions.  A successful construction must instead
do one of the following:

```text
find a different perfect trace already having central cap carriers;
or build a new representation not obtained by compressing the supplied one.
                                                               (TCM18)
```

The last alternative is a new compiler, not a decomposition theorem for
the original model.
