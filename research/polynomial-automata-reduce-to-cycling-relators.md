**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof below, elementary; its consequence for `V_d(G)` uses Theorem A of
`rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation`, not yet refereed; no priority claimed).

## Setting

`S <= Aut(X^*)` finite, closed under sections and inverses, `F = F(S)`, `N = ker(F -> G)`. Consider the section
graph on nontrivial states (edges `s --x--> s|_x`, `s|_x ≠ 1`). Assume

**(P1)** for every nontrivial `s`, at most one letter `x` has `s|_x` in the strongly connected component of `s`.

Subexponential activity implies (P1): two such letters give two first-return walks at `s`, hence exponential
activity (`persistent-subexponential-automata-generate-finite-groups`, step 1). Under (P1) every strongly
connected component is a single state with no loop or a simple cycle; for `s` on a cycle write `ℓ(s)` for its
cycle letter and `ν(s) = s|_{ℓ(s)}`, the next state on the cycle. `ν` is a permutation of the cycle states, of
finite order `r`.

- A reduced word `w = s_1 ⋯ s_n` is **special at `x`** if every `s_i` is a cycle state that receives exactly the
  point `ℓ(s_i)` (the rightmost receives `x`, and `s_{i+1}` outputs `s_{i+1}(ℓ(s_{i+1}))` to `s_i`), and
  `ν(w) = ν(s_1) ⋯ ν(s_n)` is reduced. Then `w|_x = ν(w)`. A word is special at at most one `x`, namely
  `ℓ(s_n)`.
- `w` is **cycling** if `ν^j(w)` is special (at some letter) for every `0 <= j < r`. The set of cycling words is
  `ν`-invariant, and decidable letter by letter.

## Statement

**Theorem.** For every finite `R ⊆ N`: `N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_lift` if and only if every cycling relator lies in
`⟨⟨R⟩⟩_lift`. So, by Theorem A, `V_d(G)` is finitely presented iff the cycling relators lie in a finitely
generated lift ideal. In particular:
1. if no nontrivial relator is cycling, then `N = K_∞` and `V_d(G)` is finitely presented
   (`linear-activity-odometer-automaton-has-fp-nekrashevych-group` is an instance, with cycling words
   `a b^e`, `b^e`, …, none a relator);
2. if the cycling relators lie in the normal closure of finitely many relators, `V_d(G)` is finitely presented
   (Grigorchuk's automaton: its cycling words are words in `b, c, d` along `1^ω`, whose relators are normally
   generated by `b^2, c^2, d^2, bcd`);
3. failures come only from cycling relators (`linear-activity-automata-can-have-non-fp-nekrashevych-groups`: the
   cycling relators are the Grigorchuk relators among the spine states `ŝ_γ`).

## Proof

"Only if" is immediate. For "if", let `M = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_lift` and assume the cycling relators lie in `M`.

**Weights.** Choose a linear order `λ` on the strongly connected components with `λ(C) > λ(C')` whenever `C'`
is reachable from `C ≠ C'` (the component graph is acyclic). Put `μ(s) = λ(C(s))` for nontrivial `s`, and let
`c(w)` be the multiset of weights of the letters of the reduced word `w`, under the (well-founded) multiset
order. Each letter's section is `1` or a single letter of weight `<=` its own, with equality exactly when the
letter is a cycle state receiving its cycle letter. So `c(red(w|_x)) <= c(w)` always, with equality iff `w` is
special at `x`.

**Induction on `c`.** Let `w in N` be reduced and nontrivial, and suppose every relator of smaller complexity
lies in `M`. All sections of `w` are relators. If `w` is special at no letter, all its sections have smaller
complexity, so they lie in `M`, and one lift step gives `w in M`. Otherwise `w` is special at exactly one `x`,
its other sections lie in `M`, and `w|_x = ν(w)`, a relator of the same complexity. If `w` is cycling, `w in M`
by assumption. If not, let `j >= 1` be least with `ν^j(w)` special at no letter. Then `ν^j(w)` lies in `M` (all
its sections are smaller), and descending `i = j-1, …, 0`, `ν^i(w)` has its special section `ν^{i+1}(w)` in `M`
and its other sections smaller, so `ν^i(w) in M`. So `w in M`, and `N = M`. ∎

## Where this leaves automaton groups of polynomial activity

Boone–Higman for all of them would follow if every such group has an action whose cycling relators are finitely
generated. Run compression (`commonly-fixed-letters-compress-to-faithful-actions`) removes the cycling
relators that live along a letter fixed by every state. Whether cycling relators along a periodic ray that some
states move can always be removed by another change of action is open.
