Let H and T be finitely presented groups with H embedded in [T,T].
Choose 1!=g in H and form

    U=Z[T]<p,q>/(p(1-g)q-1),       Q=EL_4(U).

There is a finitely presented property-(T) group Gamma fitting into

    St_4(U) ->> Gamma ->> Q,

and an embedding H->Gamma whose composite with Gamma->Q is the
upper-left diagonal embedding. Gamma is normally generated by g.
The first surjection is obtained by adding finitely many relators.

No finite presentation of Q or triviality/centrality/finiteness of
ker(Gamma->Q) is asserted. This supplies an explicit Steinberg cover
in place of an unspecified Shalom cover, not a removal of all covers.

For the Boone--Higman application take a finitely presented H containing
the computably presented simple core, and then embed H in [T,T] using
Chatterji--Kassabov Lemma 6. The simple core survives in Gamma.
