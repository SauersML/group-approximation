Soficity of free actions depends only on the stable orbit equivalence class of the orbit
relation (`sofic-free-actions-are-soe-invariant`), while mixing is not an invariant of that
class. So one could look for a free mixing action of some sofic group that is stably orbit
equivalent to the Kun--Thom free nonsofic action.

Dead: invalidated by `kun-thom-action-soe-class-is-rigid` for the diffuse-base Kun--Thom action
of any pair without finite normal subgroups. By Popa's OE superrigidity every such action is,
after passing to a finite-index subgroup and an invariant subset, conjugate to the Kun--Thom
action, and so is not mixing.
