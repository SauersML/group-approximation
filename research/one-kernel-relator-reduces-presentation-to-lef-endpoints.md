Section 3 takes any nontrivial normal quotient. At least one simple
endpoint factor remains, and the kernel of its endpoint product map
is normally generated by at most one involution. Finite presentation
would pass to that endpoint product. The product is infinite LEF with
no nontrivial finite quotients, contradicting the finite-presentation
LEF-to-residual-finiteness argument printed in the same section.
