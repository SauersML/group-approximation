Let

```text
Q = product_n M_(d_n) / directSum_n M_(d_n)
```

be the operator-norm matrix corona, let `G` be a countable group, and let
`u:G -> U(Q)` be an injective homomorphism.  Put `A=C*(u(G)) subset Q`.
Suppose the augmentation assignment does not extend to `A`: there is no
character `epsilon:A->C` with `epsilon(u(g))=1` for every `g`.  (In
particular, it suffices that `A` have no character.)  Then there is a proper
normal subgroup `N normal G` such that `G/N` is hyperlinear.

More precisely, for every free ultrafilter `omega`, the canonical map from
the norm corona to the normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt ultraproduct has kernel
`N_omega` on `G`; absence of augmentation forces `N_omega != G`, and its
image is a nontrivial hyperlinear quotient.

Consequently every nontrivial nonabelian simple weak-MF group admitting a
corona realization through which augmentation does not factor is
hyperlinear.  Equivalently, if such a simple group is not hyperlinear, the
augmentation character must factor through the C*-algebra generated by EVERY
faithful weak-MF corona realization.

This is a genuine fragment of `mf-implies-hyperlinear`, but not a solution.
Weak MF does not prevent the augmentation character from factoring through a
corona image; identity padding is the basic reason that normalized coordinate
traces can converge to it.
