For every countably infinite P and every enumeration nu in
`shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`, the subgroup

    (V)_p = <tau> <= (E_nu)_p

is not normal and has infinite index.

Thus neither of the two isotropy-germ alternatives in the convenient
finite-germ criterion, Belk--Hyde--Matucci Theorem 1.1, is available
for this construction. The more general Theorem 2.1 is not excluded.
This statement does not imply that E_nu fails finite presentation.

## Relation to the proposed polynomial germ tower

The 2026-09-20 manuscript proposes an affine base followed by successive
normal inclusions `J_{d-1}◁J_d`, with quotient `Z`. It does not assert
that the original cyclic prefix germ is normal in the entire final
germ group. Its finite-stabilizer and punctured-space finiteness lemmas
remain OPEN. This neighboring proposal neither contradicts nor changes
the present obstruction's scope; see the linked artifact §§5–7 and 11.
