title: The prefix germ in an infinite regular shell embedding is neither normal nor of finite index

For every countably infinite P and every enumeration nu in
`shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`, the subgroup

    (V)_p = <tau> <= (E_nu)_p

is not normal and has infinite index.

Thus neither of the two isotropy-germ alternatives in the convenient
finite-germ criterion, Belk--Hyde--Matucci Theorem 1.1, is available
for this construction. The more general Theorem 2.1 is not excluded.
This statement does not imply that E_nu fails finite presentation.
