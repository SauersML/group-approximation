## Notation

`C = {0,1}^N`. For a finite word `u`, `e_u : C → C_u` is `x ↦ ux`. The *cone copy*
`ι_u(g)` of a homeomorphism `g` of `C` is `e_u g e_u^{-1}` on `C_u` and the identity off `C_u`.
Let `V ≤ G ≤ N(V)`, where `N(V)` is the normalizer of Thompson's `V` in `Homeo(C)`.

## Statement

1. **Self-similar up to V.** If `g ∈ G` and `g(C_u) = C_u`, then `e_u^{-1} g e_u ∈ G`. More
   precisely it lies in the coset `gV` of `N(V)/V = Out(V)`, since it has the same core as `g`.
2. **Cone copies.** `ι_u(G) ≤ [[G]]` for every `u`, and `ι_u(g) ι_u(h) = ι_u(gh)`.
3. **Generation and normal form.** Every `h ∈ [[G]]` can be written
   `h = w · ι_{u_1}(g_1) ⋯ ι_{u_r}(g_r)` with `w ∈ V`, `{u_i}` a complete prefix code and
   `g_i ∈ G`. So `[[G]] = ⟨V, ι_0(G)⟩`. For `G = V ⋊ ⟨t⟩`,
   `[[G]] = ⟨V, t̂⟩` with `t̂ = ι_0(t)`.
4. **Faithful pieces.** In such an expression, `h = 1` iff `w = ι_{u_1}(v_1) ⋯ ι_{u_r}(v_r)` with
   `v_i ∈ V` and `g_i = v_i^{-1}` for all `i`.

## Use

Items 1–4 are the inputs of a Scott-type presentation of `[[G]]` from a finite presentation of
`G`. That presentation is recorded, with its one missing step, as Attempt 2 on
`fp-rsgs-have-fp-full-closures`.
