Literature import. Francesco Fournier-Facio and Matthew C. B. Zaremsky,
*Finiteness properties and Higman's rope trick*, arXiv:2607.21727v1 [math.GR],
submitted 23 July 2026.

Theorem B, verbatim: "Let G=F/R, and let Hig_ι(G) be as in Definition 1.5.
Suppose G is infinite and R≠1. Then H₃(Hig_ι(G),ℚ) is infinite-dimensional; in
particular Hig_ι(G) is not of type FP₃(ℚ)."

Definition 1.5 hypotheses, as read: `F` is finitely generated free,
`L = F_1 *_R F_2`, `π: L -> G` kills `F_2`, `ι: L -> P` is an embedding into a
finitely generated group, and `Hig_ι(G)` is the HNN extension with vertex group
`P x G` and edge group `L`, conjugating `ι x 1` to `ι x π`.

The statement and hypotheses were read from the arXiv v1 HTML on 2026-09-13.

`research/artifacts/zp-review-topology-2026-09-13-part1.md` §8d. The route asserts
only that the cited theorem says what the target records; it does not reprove
it.
