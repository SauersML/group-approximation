Literature import. The statement is Theorem B of Francesco Fournier-Facio and
Matthew C. B. Zaremsky, *Finiteness properties and Higman's rope trick*,
arXiv:2607.21727 (submitted 2026-07-23): for `G = F/R` infinite with `R != 1`,
the rope-trick group `Hig_i(G)` has `H_3(Hig_i(G); Q)` infinite dimensional,
so is not of type `FP_3(Q)`.

This route asserts only that the cited theorem says what this node records;
it does not reprove it. The exact hypotheses (the precise form of the rope-
trick construction `Hig_i`, and whether `Q` may be replaced by an arbitrary
field or `Z`) are to be checked against the arXiv source by z-verify-groups
before this import is relied on downstream.
