## Why sufficient

**1.**  Let `L=ker(H_K ->> G)` for the retraction killing `K`.  Then
`L cap G=1` and `H_K=L semidirect G`.  Acting on the Bass--Serre tree of the
amalgam, `L` is normal, so it meets every conjugate of `G` and of `Gamma`
trivially, while `L cap (Gamma x K)=K`.  Thus `L` acts with trivial edge
stabilizers, trivial stabilizers at the `G`-type vertices and stabilizers
conjugate to `K` at the others; since `H_K=LG`, the quotient graph is a star
with one `G`-type vertex and `|G/Gamma|` others, which is a tree.  Bass--Serre
gives `L = *_(G/Gamma) K` with no free part, generated by the conjugates
`g k g^(-1)`, well defined because `K` centralizes `Gamma`.

**2.**  The radical computation is the corpus theorem "complete sofic
residual of the free lamp" in `notes/NOTEPAD.md`, equations `(CSR4)--(CSR6)`:
`Rad_sof(H_K)` is the normal closure of `{[k,n] : k in K, n in N}` and
`H_K/Rad_sof(H_K) = G *_N (N x K)` embeds in `G x ((G/N)*K)`, residually
finite because `G`, `G/N` and `K` are and free products of residually finite
groups are residually finite.  Its forward inclusion imports the Kun--Thom
centralizer normalization; its reverse inclusion is the residually finite
quotient just displayed, which is the same argument as
`sofic-radical-soficization` clause 5 in a special case.  Under the normal
form of 1, imposing those commutators enlarges the second vertex group from
`Gamma x K` to `N x K`, so the kernel is exactly the map on lamps induced by
`G/Gamma -> G/N`.

**3.**  Write `M` for that kernel.  On each free factor the map
`K_(g Gamma) -> K_(g N)` is an isomorphism, so `M cap K_(g Gamma)=1`; as `M`
is normal, `M cap u K_(g Gamma) u^(-1) = u (M cap K_(g Gamma)) u^(-1) = 1`
for every `u`.  So `M` has trivial stabilizers on the Bass--Serre tree of the
free product `*_(G/Gamma)K`, acts freely, and is free.

**4.**  `Gamma` is not normal, so `Gamma != N` and the collapsing map is not
injective, so `Rad_sof(H_K) != 1`; by `sofic-radical-soficization` clause 3,
`H_K` is not sofic.

## Scope

The radical identification is imported from the corpus artifact rather than
reproved here, and that artifact's forward inclusion rests on the Kun--Thom
centralizer normalization; only the free-product normal form and the
freeness of the kernel are proved in place.  Nothing here is used by the
Connes-embeddability lanes: it is a structural statement about where the
difficulty sits, namely in the `G`-action rather than in the kernel.
