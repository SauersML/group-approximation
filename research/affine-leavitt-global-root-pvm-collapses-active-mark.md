ESTABLISHED.  In the notation of `(ALC1)`, let an exact
finite-dimensional representation of the abelian group `M` have spectral
PVM `(P_chi)`, and let the fixed actor generators be represented by
unitaries whose total PVM transport energy is `E_act`.  If `gamma>0` is the
Kazhdan/Schreier bottom gap from
`affine-leavitt-active-root-character-orbits-are-aperiodic`, then

```text
sum_(chi!=1) tr(P_chi) <= E_act/(2 gamma),                  (AGC1)
```

and therefore

```text
||rho(m_0)-I||_2^2 <= 2 E_act/gamma.                       (AGC2)
```

Thus a normalized-HS decoder which rounds the whole active root module and
makes actor transport energy vanish proves collapse of the nontrivial word
`m_0=x_16(A)` with a dimension-independent modulus.  Since `St_6(R)` is
finitely presented and `m_0!=1`, that decoder would prove an explicit
non-hyperlinear group.

This is a genuine alternative endpoint to the relative-Leavitt-cell trace
identity, but not a solution of the old decoder.  The unresolved input is
one global finite-support PVM for **all** of `(A R)^5` with controlled actor
transport.  Local root Fourier windows do not provide it.
