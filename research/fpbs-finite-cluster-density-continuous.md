title: Reciprocal finite-cluster density has uniform finite-ball approximations
---

For Bernoulli bond percolation on a locally finite connected graph, let kappa(p)=E[1/|K_p(o)|], with 1/infinity=0. Its induced-radius-R-ball approximation differs by a number between zero and 1/(R+1), uniformly in p. Consequently kappa is continuous.
