**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Γ` be virtually free, acting minimally and topologically freely on the Cantor
set `X`; let `k` be a field and `R = LC(X,k) ⋊ Γ`. The following are equivalent:
- (i) `X` carries a `Γ`-invariant Borel probability measure;
- (ii) the action is residually finite (Kerr–Nowak);
- (iii) `R` embeds unitally and injectively in some `∏_ω M_(N_n)(k)`;
- (iv) `R` has a unital ring homomorphism into some `∏_ω M_(N_n)(k)`.

So the dynamical input of the UN flagship (`free-group-cantor-elementary-group-lef-iff-invariant-measure`) holds
verbatim for virtually free acting groups, e.g. `SL_2(Z)` and free products of finite groups. It fails
topologically for every group containing `Z^2` (`z2-containing-groups-have-non-rf-measure-actions`), where the
ring may still have non-combinatorial models.
