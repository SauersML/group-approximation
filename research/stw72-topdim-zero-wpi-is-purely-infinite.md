Let `A` be a C-star algebra of topological dimension zero; equivalently,
`Prim(A)` has a basis of compact open sets.  Then

```text
A is weakly purely infinite
  <=> A is purely infinite
  <=> A is strongly purely infinite.
```

No separability, nuclearity, unitality, exactness, real-rank, stable-rank,
or Hausdorff-spectrum hypothesis is imposed.  Thus the weak-to-plain part of
STW Problem LXXII has a positive answer throughout the topological-dimension-
zero class, while the unrestricted problem remains open.  The weak-to-plain
step is Elliott--Rouzbehani, Theorem 2.1; their Corollary 2.2 gives the full
three-way equivalence.
