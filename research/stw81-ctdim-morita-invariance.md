If separable C-star algebras `A` and `B` are strongly Morita equivalent,
then

```text
ctdim(A) = ctdim(B).
```

Consequently the hereditary commutative-shadow hypothesis of Problem LXXXI
is strongly Morita invariant.  In particular,

```text
ctdim(A tensor K) = ctdim(A),
```

and `A` satisfies the shadow hypothesis if and only if its stabilization
does.
