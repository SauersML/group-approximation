Using the packet of `six-generator-one-jump-router-has-s3-radical-atlas`,
construct three stable-letter contexts with endpoint reuse pattern
`(00),(01),(11)`.  On each exactified forbidden atom of mass `s`, let the
four joint block operators of a context define its Bernoulli law by normalized
HS squared norm.  The construction must satisfy:

1. endpoint reuse gives the marginal drift bound of `(RRF2)`;
2. the three failure block spaces are placed in three distinct grandchildren
   of two consecutive Fanizza binary recurrence steps;
3. within one context, its failure atoms remain HS-orthogonal and have total
   mass at most `s`;
4. fixed hard-word bridges send every failure atom, after analytic compression
   by the forbidden packet support, to a fresh E5 first-hit layer with
polynomial word loss; and
5. the fourth grandchild and all positive parts of the first three form the
   next prefix carrier, which still reduces the original BCS words.

Then the total exit satisfies `(FCE2)--(FCE4)`, while the robust endpoint floor
and corner BCS gap give `(FCE3)`.  Two iterations of the groupified Fanizza
recurrence give `(FCE1)`, so the four-child ledger supplies a fixed HS floor.

The key change from the failed joint-selector formulation is that the failure
operators need not be relator defects.  They are orthogonal HS vectors routed
to the summable reservoir.  Analytic compression by the forbidden atom
removes allowed sectors exactly as in `(PHS2)`.

For the concrete endpoint choice, use
`s3-radical-affine-contexts-have-five-fourths-floor`.  Its context masses are
exactly `s/2,s/4,s/2`, so every context fits in one grandchild and the lower
exit sum has `s/4` slack beyond the abstract marginal floor.

## Attempts

- Choosing one of the three failures by pigeonhole loses a context-dependent
  branch choice.  Batching two binary steps supplies three fixed exit slots
  and removes that choice.
- Splitting diagonal failure events into their three Boolean atoms is safe
  inside one grandchild because the corresponding block operators are
  HS-orthogonal.  What is not yet written as a sparse presentation-level
  certificate is simultaneous preservation of the E5 prefix carrier under
  all seven fixed hard bridges.
