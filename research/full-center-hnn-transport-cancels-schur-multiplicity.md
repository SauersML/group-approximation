Let `B_sub=K(M_sub)` be the order-128 support-failure packet with central
selector group

```text
Z=<J,z_e,z_f> = (C_2)^3
```

and commutator-coefficient matrix

```text
L=[[J,J z_e],[z_f,1]].                              (FHC1)
```

For nonzero rows `r` and columns `c` in `F_2^2`, put

```text
A_(r,c)=<Z,P_r,Q_c> <= B_sub,
ell_(r,c)=[P_r,Q_c] in Z.
```

Assume `ell_(r,c)` is nontrivial.  Consider any finite graph of finite groups
whose vertex groups are copies of `B_sub` and whose edge maps are generated by
isomorphisms

```text
A_(r,c) -> A_(r',c')
```

that send `Z` to `Z`.  Then these edge transports cannot expose the ambient
Schur multiplicity jump.  More precisely, if the induced central
automorphism is `T in GL_3(F_2)`, a source central character `k` is paired with
the unique target character `k'` satisfying

```text
k=T^t k'.                                             (FHC2)
```

Whenever `k(ell_(r,c))=-1`, both edge restrictions are sums of the unique
two-dimensional Pauli simple, and an exact stable-letter transport forces

```text
dim H_k = dim H_(k'),
m_edge(k)=dim(H_k)/2=dim(H_(k'))/2=m_edge(k').         (FHC3)
```

Thus the fact that a forbidden `B_sub` simple has dimension four while an
allowed one has dimension two disappears on restriction to the full-center
Pauli edge.  It does not become a baseline multiplicity mismatch.

This failure is visible in the complete central-character atlas.  In the
central basis `(J,z_e,z_f)`, the forbidden character `101` has packet dimension
four.  There are involutive full-center Pauli charts from it to all five
rank-one characters

```text
001, 010, 100, 110, 111,                              (FHC4)
```

each of packet dimension two.  A forbidden ambient simple restricts to two
edge spins and a target ambient simple to one, but `(FHC3)` merely equates
their total Hilbert masses.  Applying all five charts gives the capacity bound

```text
tau(H_101) <= 1/6,                                    (FHC5)
```

not `tau(H_101)=0`.  The regular representation has mass `1/8` in every
central character and satisfies every such equality.

In particular, no gadget which consists only of a finite Bass--Serre network
of these full-center packet charts can prove a positive normalized-HS energy
lower bound proportional to the forbidden mass.  A successful return must
omit enough center to form genuine endpoint marginals, or use a non-Bass--
Serre compressor/holonomy that compares packet multiplicity before Hilbert
dimension cancellation.
