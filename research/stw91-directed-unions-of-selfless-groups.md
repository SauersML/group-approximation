Let `G` be a discrete group and `(G_i)` an upward directed family of subgroups
with union `G`.  If `(C*_r(G_i),tau_i)` is selfless for every `i`, then
`(C*_r(G),tau_G)` is selfless.  In particular, `G` is C*-simple.

The theorem does not assume that `G` itself belongs to any of the presently
known geometric or linear selflessness classes.
