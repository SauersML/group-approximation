title: A finite commuting zigzag of Jiang--Su copies forces pairwise uniqueness
distinct_from:
  joint-central-z-bridge-forces-approximate-uniqueness: that uses one copy commuting with both endpoint ranges; this permits different intermediate copies and only consecutive commutation.
---

Let `B` be unital and let `phi,psi:Z->B` be unital embeddings.  Regard the
endpoint maps as constant-sequence maps into `B_infinity`.  Suppose there are
unital embeddings

```text
rho_0=phi, rho_1, ..., rho_m=psi : Z -> B_infinity
```

such that

```text
[rho_j(Z),rho_(j+1)(Z)]=0       for j=0,...,m-1.
```

Then `phi` and `psi` are approximately unitarily equivalent in `B`.

Thus the universal joint-central-bridge claim can be weakened to connectedness
of the two constant copies in the finite commuting graph of Jiang--Su copies
inside the sequence algebra.
