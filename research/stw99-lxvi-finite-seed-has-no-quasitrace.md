title: A simple finite algebra with infinite two-by-two amplification has no nonzero bounded quasitrace
distinct_from:
  stw99-lxvi-car-stabilization-destroys-finite-seed: that rules out forcing a CAR copy by tensorial stabilization; this rules out every construction in which the CAR trace extends to a quasitrace on the ambient finite corner.
---

Let `A` be a simple unital C\*-algebra whose unit is finite and whose
two-by-two amplification `M_2(A)` has infinite unit.  Then `A` has no nonzero
bounded quasitrace.

Consequently, if `A` is a seed for Problem LXVI as in
`stw99-lxvi-finite-car-corner-with-infinite-double`, the unique trace of its
unital CAR subalgebra does not extend to a bounded quasitrace on `A`.
