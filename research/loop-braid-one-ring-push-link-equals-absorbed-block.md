Notation is from `loop-braid-one-ring-push-link-contains-absorbed-block` and its artifact.
- `θ : PLB_{[n]∖{v,w}} → P_vw` sends `A_cj ↦ A_cj A_vj A_wj` and fixes the other `A_ij`.
- π kills `x_v, x_w`.
- `Φ : X_{n−2}(F ∪ {c}) ↪ L := lk(b0) ∩ lk(A_vc b0)` is the proved embedding.

**Claim ((★′)).** `L = Φ(X_{n−2}(F ∪ {c}))`, for all n and F and all distinct free v, w, c.

By the artifact §4 (Lemma 4.1 and the reduction), this is equivalent to (K) and (C) together:
- **(K)** If `g' ∈ P_vw ∩ ker π` and `σ` is a Q-simplex of types avoiding {v, w, c} and F, and
  `g'σ ∈ lk(A_vc b0)`, then `g' ∈ P_σ`.
- **(C)** No vertex of L has a type meeting c.

**Evidence.**
- In 5040 random samples (n = 6, 7, two seeds), a vertex `g[(a,b)]` with `g ∈ P_vw` passes the
  rigorous non-adjacency test exactly when it passes the necessary test for lying in the image of Φ.
- In 1274 samples, no vertex with type meeting c passes.

**Why it matters.** Together with the inductive hypothesis of the w8 reduction, (★′) makes every
one-ring pair `(b0, A_vc^{±1} b0)` meet the t = 1 Björner condition of the Coning Statement
`loop-braid-forbidden-ring-coning-statement` exactly: `c(N−3) = η(N) − 1`. This is step (D1) of
the artifact §5 decomposition.
