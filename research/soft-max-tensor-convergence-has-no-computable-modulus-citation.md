Goldbring--Sinclair, arXiv:2509.15086v1, Corollary 5.8 states the claimed
nonexistence of an algorithm selecting `m` from a polynomial and target
accuracy.  The preceding paragraph cites RFD and uniform computability of
the positive softenings and identifies their inductive limit with
`C^*(F_n x F_n)`.  Hence the conclusion is precisely a missing computable
convergence modulus, not a failure of pointwise norm convergence.
