title: Rips complexes of the integer lattice Z^n in the l^1 metric are contractible at large scale
---

For every `n ≥ 1` and every `r ≥ n^2 (2n − 1)`, the Vietoris--Rips complex of
`(Z^n, d_1)` at scale `r` is contractible. Here `d_1` is the `l^1` metric,
which is the word metric for the standard generators, and a finite
`σ ⊆ Z^n` spans a simplex iff its diameter is at most `r`.
