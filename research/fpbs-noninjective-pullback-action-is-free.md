Needed if the non-Hopfian torsion-free group is to supply two free actions with different costs, one of them pulled back along the surjective noninjective endomorphism.

## Attempts

Rejected where it was raised, in Section 9 of the torsion-free note: the endomorphism has nontrivial kernel and kernel elements act trivially on any pullback, so the pullback is not essentially free. See fpbs-noninjective-pullback-is-not-free.
