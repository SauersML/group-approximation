**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `G` have `cd G = d < ∞` and act faithfully on `R` by
orientation-preserving homeomorphisms.

1. If `g_1, …, g_k ∈ G` are nontrivial and their open supports are pairwise disjoint, then
   `⟨g_1, …, g_k⟩ ≅ Z^k` and `k ≤ d`. The supports may be arbitrary open sets, not only intervals.
2. `G` contains no copy of Thompson's group `F`, of `F' = [F,F]`, or of `Z ≀ Z`.

**Consequences for Zaremsky Problem 1.22.**
- No overgroup of `F'` answers the problem. The same holds for overgroups of chain-group commutator
  subgroups, of locally moving groups of interval homeomorphisms, and of the Hyde–Lodha simple groups
  `G_ρ` (`hyde-lodha-groups-contain-thompson-commutator-subgroup`).
- No group whose faithful actions on the line have unboundedly many disjointly supported nontrivial
  elements answers it. This covers periodic and quasi-periodic supports like those of `λ(F') ≤ G_ρ`.
- So the route `lo-simple-type-f-via-thompson-like-subgroups` is dead.

**Model tests.**
- `BS(1,2)` acting by `x ↦ 2x`, `x ↦ x + 1` has `cd 2`, and no two nontrivial elements have disjoint
  supports. Item 1 holds with room to spare.
- `Z^2` generated by two disjointly supported homeomorphisms attains the bound.
- `F'` has disjointly supported families of every size and `cd = ∞`, so item 1 does not apply.
