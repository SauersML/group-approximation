**ESTABLISHED SCOPED DICHOTOMY.**  Let `P` be a fixed finite packet, let
`sigma` be an active packet type after its complete local `S_3` orbit has
been put in the reached carrier, and consider a finitely presented return
cell of either of the following two kinds.

1. Its return words act on `P` by automorphisms.  Write `Q_sigma` for the
   stabilizer of `sigma`, and

   ```text
   V_g V_h=omega(g,h)V_(gh)                              (SRD1)
   ```

   for the multiplier of chosen outer packet implementers.
2. After exactification of `P`, at least one return is a genuinely
   nonautomorphic compressed map between decoded packet carriers.

Suppose in the first case that the cell gives **indefinitely reusable**
dimension-uniform growth.  Precisely, in every finite-dimensional
approximate representation, with total squared relator energy `E`, it
produces numbers `0<=a_n<=1` such that

```text
lambda a_n <= a_(n+1)+C p(n)sqrt(E),       n>=0,        (SRD2)
```

where `lambda>1` and `p` is a fixed polynomial.  Fix a packet projection
`B_sigma` which is nonzero on `sigma`; assume the initial carrier contains
this marked `sigma` corner up to fixed packet-correction error:

```text
tr(B_sigma)<=a_0+C_0 sqrt(E).                           (SRD3)
```

Then

```text
tr(B_sigma)<=C_* sqrt(E),                               (SRD4)
```

with a constant independent of matrix dimension.  Consequently the inverse
twisted stabilizer algebra

```text
C_(omega^(-1))[Q_sigma]                                 (SRD5)
```

has no nonzero finite-dimensional star representation satisfying the return
relations: the active central character is in the projective
finite-dimensional radical.  More strongly, its twisted regular von Neumann
algebra is not Connes embeddable.  Thus an infinite stabilizer does not renew
the saturated `S_3` escape without already supplying the quantitative
projective radical that is equivalent to the nonhyperlinear-group problem.

The conclusion uses indefinite reuse, not merely one more displacement.  A
single infinite-order unitary may move a proper multiplicity subspace in a
finite-dimensional representation.  It is the uniform recurrence `(SRD2)`
at every depth, with summable normalized depth errors, that forces `(SRD4)`.

In the second case the stabilizer classification no longer applies, but the
exception is not an unclassified automorphic renewal.  By
`proper-packet-rank-growth-needs-compressed-two-cell`, equal-rank compressed
holonomy is trace-flat.  Unequal-rank holonomy is exactly a trace-cyclic
proper-corner cell.  If its source-fullness or containment is a fixed
group-algebra identity, or follows by operations functorial in finite
tracial von Neumann algebras, then
`universal-block-return-violates-the-regular-firewall` kills its positive
regular complement.  Hence the only surviving exception is a genuinely
finite-matrix coordinate authentication of the source Gram on unequal
carriers, false in the regular finite factor.  In the native Fanizza lane
this is precisely `single-triangle-trace-cyclic-return-cell`, reduced further
to `fanizza-matrix-coordinate-source-full-compression`.

Therefore, inside this finite-packet return class, the post-saturation fork
is exhaustive:

```text
automorphic indefinite renewal  ->  non-CE twisted stabilizer;
nonautomorphic renewal           ->  matrix-only unequal-carrier compression.
```

Finite holonomy is already countermodeled, and a tracially functorial proper
corner is already collapsed.  Neither is a third route.
