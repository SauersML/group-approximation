Let `Γ` be a finite Coxeter graph on `S`. Call `Γ` *of FC type* when every `X ⊆ S` with
`m_st < ∞` for all `s, t ∈ X` generates a finite subgroup of `W_Γ`. If `Γ` is of FC type, then
`A_Γ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture.

Examples: right-angled Artin groups, spherical-type Artin groups, and every Artin group whose
defining graph is built from spherical pieces joined by labels `∞`.
