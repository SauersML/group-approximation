Barlak--Li Theorem 1.8, implication (4) ⟹ (1): if every outer strongly
approximately inner `Z_2`-action on `O_2` leaves a Cartan subalgebra invariant,
then every separable nuclear algebra KK-equivalent to its `M_{2^∞}`-stabilization
satisfies the UCT.  In particular every `M_{2^∞}`-absorbing one does.
