For the primitive block projection, the standard character-idempotent
formula makes the coefficient at `k` proportional to `chi(k^(-1))`.
Coordinate flips and transpositions have tautological trace `N-2`, so they
belong to the support and generate the signed permutation group.

For the reflection complement `q`, Fourier inversion makes its coefficient
at `k` proportional to `Tr(Q rho(k^(-1)))`.  This trace is `N-1` at the
distinguished flip `s_0` and `N-2` at every star transposition `(0 i)`.
Those elements again generate the whole signed permutation group.

Conjugation of a group-algebra element conjugates its group-basis support.
Hence a group element preserving either projection normalizes the subgroup
generated by its support, namely `K_N`.  A unitary literal compression
commutes with its carrier projection by the standard positive corner-leakage
calculation, so it satisfies the same hypothesis.  All literal context
conjugates consequently remain in `qC[K_N]q`, where exact BCS compatibility
would be a finite-dimensional perfect strategy.  This proves the claim.
