**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `D = G *_Gamma G` be the `q = 2` Kun--Thom double of
`kt-q2-double-has-trivial-amenable-radical`.  For every fixed `k`, `D` has no
regular HS model in `U(k) wr S_n`.  In particular no regular HS microstates
of `D` approximately normalize a full masa.

Consequence for `hyperlinear-nonsofic-from-kt-ce-stabilizer`,
`kt-double-mixed-word-selection-yields-hyperlinear-nonsofic` and every other
separation route through the double: a hyperlinear model of `D`, if one
exists, is frame-incoherent.  Its matrices approximately permute no
decomposition into bounded-dimensional blocks, whatever phases or internal
rotations are allowed.  Constructions that assemble the two vertex copies
from permutation-indexed families of fixed-size pieces are excluded at the
model level.
