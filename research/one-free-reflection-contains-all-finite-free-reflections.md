Let `U=L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` and let

```text
W=U^(F_2) semidirectProduct F_2=U wr F_2<=U
```

be the internal free-reflection group.  For every `2<=r<infinity`, the group
`W` contains a finitely generated subgroup

```text
W_r=U^(F_r) semidirectProduct F_r
```

with

```text
Rad_MF(W_r)=U^(F_r),             R_MF(W_r)~=F_r.         (UFR1)
```

The radical in every `W_r` is normally generated by one fixed involution.
The `W_r` are pairwise nonisomorphic, so one finitely generated non-MF group
contains infinitely many pairwise nonisomorphic finitely generated non-MF
subgroups with pairwise nonisomorphic nonamenable MF reflections.

The group `W` is non-co-Hopfian.  More precisely, there is an injective
nonsurjective endomorphism `Phi:W->W`, and hence a strict descending chain

```text
W > Phi(W) > Phi^2(W) > ...                              (UFR2)
```

of isomorphic copies.  The universal MF reflection carries `Phi` to the
injective nonsurjective free-group endomorphism

```text
a |-> a,             b |-> b a b^(-1).                  (UFR3)
```

Thus both the proper self-similarity and its complete MF-visible shadow occur
inside one finitely generated subgroup of the simple Kazhdan MF black hole.
