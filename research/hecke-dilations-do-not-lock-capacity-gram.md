title: Elementary Steinberg and finite monomial dilations name the Hecke unitary but do not authenticate its capacity Gram
---

A finite-support Hecke unitary has both an exact fixed-rank Steinberg word
and an exact finite monomial realization.  Neither realization identifies
the decoded capacity source with the coefficient/packet carrier in an
arbitrary matrix representation.

Thus syntax is closed; the remaining interface is the matrix-only norm
reflection from one root word to the capacity-gated row coefficient.
