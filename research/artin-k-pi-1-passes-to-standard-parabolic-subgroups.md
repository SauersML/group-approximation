Let `Γ` be a finite Coxeter graph with vertex set `S` and let `T ⊆ S`. If `Γ` is of type
K(π,1), then `Γ_T` is of type K(π,1).

Literature import through `artin-k-pi-1-passes-to-standard-parabolic-subgroups-citation`.
So the class of Coxeter graphs satisfying the K(π,1) conjecture is closed under induced
subgraphs, and a minimal counterexample has all proper standard parabolic subgroups
satisfying the conjecture.
