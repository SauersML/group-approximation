If Deligne's triple cover `E_3` of `Sp_4(Z)` is hyperlinear and not sofic,
it is a finitely presented Kazhdan hyperlinear nonsofic group, answering
Pestov's Question 3.4 by separation.  The two inputs are the two open horns
recorded for `E_3`; the graph's Deligne lane otherwise feeds only
`non-hyperlinear-group`, so this route makes the cover a decision point for
both roots.
