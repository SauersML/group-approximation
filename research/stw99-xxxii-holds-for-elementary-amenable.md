Part (1) of STW Problem XXXII holds for all elementary amenable
groups (Kerr--Naryshkin, Corollary B): free minimal actions on
finite-dimensional compact metrisable spaces are almost finite, hence
have `Z`-stable crossed products.  With the locally-subexponential
cell (Kerr--Szabo CMP Thm 8.1 + Downarowicz--Zhang), the open content
of part (1) is exactly the amenable groups outside
(elementary amenable ∪ locally subexponential) — the first test cases
being groups of intermediate growth.
