**Established by citation.**  Glasner--Weiss (*Weak orbit equivalence
of Cantor minimal systems*, Internat. J. Math. 1995, Lemma 2.5 and
its circle): for a minimal Cantor `Z`-system, uniform measure
inequality `μ(U) < μ(V)` over the invariant simplex yields a clopen
partition of `U` with translates disjointly inside `V` — dynamical
comparison in the modern sense, as used throughout the orbit
equivalence and almost-finiteness literature (Kerr JEMS §8-9 records
exactly this genealogy).
