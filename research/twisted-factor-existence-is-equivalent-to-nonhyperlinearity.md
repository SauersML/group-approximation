The following are equivalent.

1. There are a countable group `Gamma` and a scalar multiplier
   `omega in Z^2(Gamma,T)` for which `L_omega(Gamma)` is not Connes
   embeddable.
2. There is a countable nonhyperlinear group.
3. There is a finitely generated nonhyperlinear group.

Moreover, in clause 1 one may require `Gamma` itself to be finitely
generated.  Thus the open node `non-ce-twisted-group-factor-exists` is an
exact reformulation of `non-hyperlinear-group`, not a weaker source theorem
from which the root remains to be proved.

The word "factor" is inessential here: the argument applies to the twisted
group von Neumann algebra whether or not its center is trivial.
