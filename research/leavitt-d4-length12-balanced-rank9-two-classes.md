Up to cyclic rotation of each face and global inversion, the twelve
carrier-balanced rank-nine systems for the minimal `(1,3,3,5)` topology
reduce to two normal-chirality classes, represented by five-face group-size
words `(1,3,1)` and `(2,2,1)`.
