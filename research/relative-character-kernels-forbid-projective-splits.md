Use the natural binary boundary representation of K=GL_2(F_4) and
the reduced projectors f_0,P_0. Let g=diag(a,b), where a,b are
invertible binary-linear operators on the two field-coordinate spaces.
After scalar extension to F_4, write the plus-sector action of
c=b^-1a as

    c u_+(eta)=u_+(R eta)+u_-(T eta).

If either R or T has a nonzero kernel, then P_0[g]f_0 has a nonzero
source kernel in the exterior square. Hence L P_0[g]f_0=f_0 has no
solution in the subgroup algebra or an unrestricted ambient group
algebra. A possible split therefore requires both R and T injective.

The obstruction is unchanged by replacing a,b with ha,hb for any
shared invertible binary-linear h. Relative F_4 linearity and
semilinearity are special excluded cases. Injectivity of both
relative blocks is not sufficient, and arbitrary chart coefficients
surrounding g are not covered.
