**Established by citation.** Marc A. Rieffel, *Dimension and stable rank in
the K-theory of C-star-algebras*, Proceedings of the London Mathematical
Society 46 (1983), 301--333, Theorem 10.12,
https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-46.2.301. For unital `A` of stable rank one
the canonical map `U(A)/U_0(A) -> K_1(A)` is an isomorphism. This is the same
theorem used in the stable-rank-one branch of
`stw58-simple-pure-k1-bijective-proof`.
