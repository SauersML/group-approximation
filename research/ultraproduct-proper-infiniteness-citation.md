Citation import, not a reproof.  Read from the PDF of arXiv:2309.17412v2,
Lemma 3.4 (semiprojectivity of `T_2` in quantitative form, with the sharp
constant `1/2`) and Lemma 4.5, on 2026-09-11.  The proof of Lemma 4.5 uses
`M_k(prod_omega A_n) = prod_omega M_k(A_n)`, lifts isometries with orthogonal
ranges to coordinates with defects below `1/2` for `omega`-many `n`, and applies
Lemma 3.4.
