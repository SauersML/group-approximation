Let `(P_a)_(a in R)` be a finite-dimensional context partition with every
listed `P_a` nonzero, and let

```text
d_x=sum_a a_x P_a,             x in S.                    (FEC1)
```

There is a unitary `u` commuting with every `d_x`, `x in S`, but moving at
least one context projection `P_a` if and only if the restriction map

```text
R -> {+1,-1}^S,              a |-> (a_x)_(x in S)         (FEC2)
```

is not injective.

When `(FEC2)` is not injective, choose distinct `a,b` with the same restricted
sign pattern, choose unit vectors in `P_a` and `P_b`, and apply a Hadamard
rotation on their span.  It commutes with all `(FEC1)` but moves both atoms.
Combining this rotation with the independent free-phase unitary `(OBM3)`
makes the two moved assignment projections leave the finite root block while
all marginals in `S` stay fixed.

When `(FEC2)` is injective, each `P_a` is a joint spectral projection of the
family `(d_x)_(x in S)`.  Every unitary commuting with that family fixes every
`P_a`.  Thus no conjugation-based cell can preserve all selected marginals
and move the context algebra.  In that case the supported context algebra is
already generated by those marginals; it contributes no residual local
degree of freedom beyond them.
