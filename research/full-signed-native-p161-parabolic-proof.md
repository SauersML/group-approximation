There are eight relevant equivalent projective summands.  Before separating
the two orthogonal summands at ambient coordinate `2`, they are

```text
q@2,
q@4,e_1@7,e_2@8,q@5,e_3@9,e_3@2,
q@6.                                                    (1)
```

Here the middle six are exactly the `G_6` block.  The first and last are the
two outer `q` summands of the signed center chain.  Although `q@2` and
`e_3@2` share one ambient coordinate, they are orthogonal because
`qe_3=e_3q=0`.

This also reconciles the earlier seven-summand `P_A` chart.  There its Levi
is `GL_5(F_2)` on
`q@4,e_1@7,e_2@8,q@5,e_3@9`, the two outer summands are `q@6,q@2`, and
`e_3@2` is fixed.  The endpoint Whitehead `W` adjoins that fixed summand to
the Levi.  Equivalently, in the native chart `<W,M_2,J_1>` first supplies a
five-dimensional block containing `e_3@2`, and `M_1` adjoins `q@4`.
Therefore the first prerequisite gives exactly the six-dimensional middle
Levi asserted here, not the old five-dimensional one.

To audit the Steinberg lift using distinct ambient coordinates, put
`e_3=s_000t_000` and use the literal idempotent Whitehead

```text
R_3=x_(10,2)(e_3)x_(2,10)(e_3)x_(10,2)(e_3).          (2)
```

On the `(10,2)` coordinate block this is

```text
[1-e_3  e_3]
[ e_3  1-e_3].                                        (3)
```

Every generator of `L_0` involving ambient coordinate `2` has that
coordinate as its **source** and is one of

```text
C_1=x_42(q),          d=x_82(b_2),          k=x_92(b_3).
```

Prefix orthogonality gives the required right-annihilation identities

```text
q e_3=0,              b_2e_3=0,             b_3e_3=0. (3a)
```

Indeed `t_1s_000=0`; no assertion about `e_3b_2` or `e_3b_3` is used.  The
factor `x_(10,2)(e_3)` has the same source column as the three roots above
and hence commutes with them.  For the other factor, the only possible
Steinberg correction is

```text
[x_(i,2)(a),x_(2,10)(e_3)]=x_(i,10)(a e_3),
```

which is trivial by `(3a)`.  All remaining generators of `L_0`, and also
`r,l`, are disjoint from coordinates `2,10`.  Therefore

```text
[R_3,L_0]=[R_3,r]=[R_3,l]=1.                           (4)
```

Conjugating `(PSP6)` by `R_3` hence changes only the endpoint `e_3`
summand.  The eight summands now occupy the distinct ambient coordinates

```text
q@6 ;
q@4,e_1@7,e_2@8,q@5,e_3@9,e_3@10 ;
q@2.                                                    (5)
```

For a `q` summand use `S_q=s_1,T_q=t_1`; for an `e_m` summand use
`S_m=s_(0^m),T_m=t_(0^m)`.  As in
`full-q-a1-projective-matrix-unit-lift-proof`, the operators

```text
x_(c_i,c_j)(S_iT_j),             i!=j,                 (6)
```

satisfy the defining relations of `St_8(F_2)`: distinct ambient coordinates
supply the Kronecker delta in matrix-unit products and `T_iS_i=1` supplies
the nonzero composition.  Thus `(6)` defines

```text
Phi_8:St_8(F_2) -> St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2)).                (7)
```

The finite-field kernel is trivial because `F_2^x={1}`; hence
`St_8(F_2)=SL_8(F_2)`, and the standard action on `(5)` proves that `Phi_8`
is injective.  This is the required literal Steinberg-lift audit.

Inside the middle six coordinates, the conjugated `G_6` is the complete
`GL_6(F_2)` Levi by the first required claim.  The two literal roots

```text
C_1=x_42(q),              C_3=x_65(q)                 (8)
```

are respectively one nonzero lower adjacent block

```text
beta_0: q@2 -> q@4
```

and one nonzero upper adjacent block

```text
alpha_0: q@5 -> q@6.                                  (9)
```

Conjugating `(8)` by the full middle `GL_6(F_2)` produces every column
`beta in F_2^(6x1)` and every row `alpha in F_2^(1x6)`.  Their Steinberg
commutators produce the remaining outer root

```text
x_62(q):q@2 -> q@6,                                   (10)
```

and hence every scalar `c in F_2` in the top-right block.  These thirteen
binary root coordinates form the full two-step unipotent radical
`U_(1,6,1)`; its multiplication has the usual central term `alpha beta`, and
its order is

```text
2^(6+6+1)=2^13.                                       (11)
```

Conversely, inspect `(PSP2)--(PSP3)`.  The roots with both endpoints in the
middle six lie in the Levi.  Every remaining root has one of the two outer
endpoints and is one of the `alpha`, `beta`, or central coordinates just
described.  Thus all of `L_0,r,l` lies in the parabolic generated by
`G_6,C_1,C_3`, while `(8)--(10)` show that those generators already contain
the whole parabolic.  This proves `(PSP7)--(PSP8)`.  Conjugating back by
`R_3` proves the statement for the original literal words.

Finally, `Q`, `r`, `l`, `F`, and `g=J_1J_2` are all elements of the finite
group algebra of this parabolic.  The literal subgroup `P_A` from the
second prerequisite is generated by `L_0,r,l,J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3,A_1`, all of
which lie in the present group: the last five actors lie in `G_6` and the
first three groups are explicit generators.  Hence

```text
P_A <= P_(1,6,1).                                      (12)
```

The exact identity `(A1E6)` from the third prerequisite says

```text
F in (F C[P_A] F)(FgF)(F C[P_A] F).                   (13)
```

The three factors on the right lie in `A_F`, proving `(PSP10)`.  For the
uniform constant, decompose

```text
C[P_(1,6,1)]=direct_sum_pi M_(d_pi)(C).                (14)
```

Because `(PSP10)` holds, the image of `X` is nonzero in every simple summand
in which `F` is nonzero.  The minimum over this finite set of the positive
ratios

```text
Tr_pi(X^*X)/Tr_pi(F)                                   (15)
```

is the constant `gamma` in `(PSP11)`.  This establishes the literal
Whitehead cross-Gram and no unequal-selector conclusion beyond it.
