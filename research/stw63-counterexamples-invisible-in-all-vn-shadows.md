If two Jiang--Su embeddings into a unital C-star algebra fail approximate
unitary equivalence, their images under any unital star homomorphism into a
von Neumann algebra are approximately unitarily equivalent in norm there.

Thus no von Neumann completion, representation, factor quotient, cardinal
rank, center-valued trace, or norm ideal of a W-star target can detect a
counterexample.  The unresolved universal C-star problem is exactly about
returning implementing unitaries from von Neumann envelopes to the original
C-star algebra, or about an invariant not preserved by passage to those
envelopes.
