**ESTABLISHED** by `fp-bounded-displacement-envelope-pumping-proof`.

Let `gamma_1, ..., gamma_k ∈ Sym(N)` with `sup_(i,x) |gamma_i(x) - x| < ∞`, and
put `G = < gamma_1, ..., gamma_k, sl, flip, (1 2) >` (`sl` and `flip` as in
`half-finite-generators-with-e2-are-strongly-shift-similar`). If `G` is finitely
presented, there is `m >= 1` such that every `gamma_i` lies in `E_m`, the group
of permutations `pi` with `pi(x+m) = pi(x) + m` for all sufficiently large `x`.

No support condition is assumed.

**Consequences.**
1. **No hardness.** Each such `gamma_i` is given by a finite table plus the
   periodic rule, so `n -> gamma_i(n)` is computed in time `O(n)` from unary
   `n`. All finitely presented envelopes generated by `E_2` and
   bounded-displacement permutations sit under one linear complexity bound.
2. **The target needs unbounded displacement.** Every witness for
   `hard-half-finite-permutations-have-fp-shift-envelopes` must include a
   generator `gamma_i` with `sup_x |gamma_i(x) - x| = ∞`. Bounded-displacement
   patterns fail however they are chosen: sparse hard defect blocks, hard
   decidable patterns whose orbit closure is a shift of finite type or the full
   shift, and Toeplitz-type non-minimal patterns. This is decided without
   computing any germ group.
3. **Beyond bounded displacement.** The proof uses that the relators are
   checked on windows of bounded width. With linear displacement, as allowed by
   `gamma(x) <= 4x` in the target, one relator at `x` sees points near `4^t x`,
   the conditions couple scales, and the pumping step does not apply.
