For every nonempty second-countable locally compact Hausdorff space `X`,
put

`A_X=C_0(X) tensor O_2`.

Then `Prim(A_X)~=X`, while no quotient of `A_X` has a nonzero commutative
hereditary subalgebra.  Nevertheless

`dim_nuc(A_X)=1`.

Thus the spaces `Prim(A_X)` can have any finite covering dimension or
infinite covering dimension while the LXXXI shadow invariant is empty.
The primitive-spectrum obstruction in Problem LXXXI is therefore not the
dimension of `Prim(A)` itself.  Only the topology of the type-I loci and
their accumulation against non-type-I strata can be detected by
commutative hereditary shadows.
