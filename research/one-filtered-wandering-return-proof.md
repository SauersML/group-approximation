The canonical trace restricts faithfully to the finite-dimensional algebra
`C[H]`, so a nonzero projection `P` has `tau(P)>0`.  In a canonical
hyperlinear microstate sequence the two fixed moments converge to
`tau(P)>0` and `tau(P r)=0`.  Passing to the limit in the proposed positive
return inequality gives `0>=c tau(P)>0`, a contradiction.  If every support
word `g r` is nonidentity, expansion of `P` in the group basis gives
`tau(P r)=0` term by term.
