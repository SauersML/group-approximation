title: "Bleak–Brin–Moore: Thompson's group F has finitely generated elementary amenable subgroups of EA-class alpha+2 for every alpha below epsilon_0"
---

**Source.** C. Bleak, M. G. Brin and J. T. Moore, *Complexity among the finitely
generated subgroups of Thompson's group*, arXiv:1711.10998 (v2, 8 Feb 2021),
Conjecture 1 and Theorem 1 (pp. 1–2).

**Theorem 1, verbatim.** "There is a transfinite sequence (G_ξ | ξ < ε_0) of finitely
generated elementary amenable subgroups of F such that:
• G_0 is the trivial group and G_{ξ+1} ≅ G_ξ + Z;
• G_ξ embeds into G_η if and only if ξ ≤ η;
• Given 0 ≤ α < ε_0 and n < ω, let ξ = ω^{(ω^α)·(2^n)}. If α > 0, then the EA-class
of G_ξ is ω·α + n + 2. If α = 0, then the EA-class of G_ξ is n + 1."

The authors continue: "In particular, for each α < ε_0, there is a ξ such that the
EA-class of G_ξ is α + 2. (If the EA-class of a finitely generated group is infinite,
it is always of the form α + 2.) Thus Theorem 1 improves previous work of the second
author [5], who demonstrated that there are finitely generated subgroups of F in EG
of class ξ + 2 for each ξ < ω²."

**Conjecture 1, verbatim.** "If G is a subgroup of PL_+(I), then either G is
elementarily amenable or else G contains a copy of F." The paper attributes the
dichotomy to Brin and Sapir and says: "While we do not settle Conjecture 1, this
paper explores the universe of F-less subgroups of PL_+(I)".

**Consequence for Zaremsky Problem 2.3.** The `F`-less (elementary amenable)
finitely generated subgroups of `F` reach every EA-class `α + 2` with `α < ε_0`. An
affirmative proof by induction over EA-class, or over any complexity rank that bounds
EA-class, must run through all ordinals below `ε_0`; no structure theorem of bounded
depth, and in particular none of depth `ω²`, can suffice.

**Scope.** Citation import; statements read from pages 1–2 of the arXiv PDF on
2026-09-13; proofs not re-derived.
