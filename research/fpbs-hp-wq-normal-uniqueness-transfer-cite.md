Citation import, not a reproof. T. Hutchcroft and M. Pan, arXiv:2409.12283v1,
Proposition 1.9: if `H_1 <= H_2 <= Gamma` with `H_1` s-normal in `H_2`, then
`G_p` has a unique `H_1`-infinite cluster a.s. iff it has a unique
`H_2`-infinite cluster a.s., so `p_u(H;G) = p_u(G)` for s-normal `H`.
Proposition 1.10: the same with wq-normal in place of s-normal. Read from the PDF
on 2026-09-11. This route asserts only that the cited results say what the target
records.
