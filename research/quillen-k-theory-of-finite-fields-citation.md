Citation import. C. Weibel, *The K-book: an introduction to algebraic K-theory* (Grad. Stud. Math. 145, AMS, 2013). Read
2026-09-13 from the author's chapter PDFs `https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~weibel/Kbook/Kbook.IV.pdf` and `Kbook.III.pdf`.
The text was extracted on MSI with ghostscript (`/scratch.global/sauer354/sk/sk-open-4/KIV.txt`, lines 543–549;

marked [·]. Every word is verbatim.

- **IV, Corollary 1.13.** "For every finite field F[_q], and n ≥ 1, we have K[_n](F[_q]) = π[_n] BGL(F[_q])[^+] ≅ Z/(q[^i] −1)
  [for] n = 2i−1, [and] 0 [for] n even."
  - It is derived there from "Theorem 1.12. (Quillen) The map BGL(F[_q])[^+] → BU identifies BGL(F[_q])[^+] with the homotopy
    fiber of ψ[^q] −1."
- **III, Corollary 6.1.1.** "K2(Fq) = 1 for every finite field Fq." In the multiplicative notation of that chapter this
  agrees with the even case above.

Primary source (not read): D. Quillen, *On the cohomology and K-theory of the general linear groups over a finite field*,
