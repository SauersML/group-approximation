Let M be a closed m-manifold with a BSpin_8-structure. Let X be a closed
connected 4r-manifold, Q -> X Hermitian of rank r, and W = Hom(Q, C^2). Put
B = M x X and E = C^2 (+) Q. Suppose the virtual bundle TX − W_R carries a
BSpin_8-structure tau, that is, a spin structure with lambda trivialized
mod 8. Then Toms's Definition 3.2 (arXiv:2609.09535v1), with the structure
on the locus Z(v) induced from TZ(v) ≅_s (TB − W_R)|_{Z(v)}, gives a function

    nu^{M,8}_{X,Q}: U(Gamma(End E))/U_0 -> Omega-tilde^8_m(SU(2)).

It vanishes on 1_E. For smooth f: M -> SU(2),

    nu^{M,8}((f o pr_M) (+) 1_Q) = <c_r(Q)^2, [X]>·[M, f]_red.

Neither X nor W_R needs a string structure of its own.

Complete argument. Not independently reviewed.
