**OPEN.** Let `G <= Aut(X^*)` be generated by the states of a finite invertible automaton. Then `G` embeds
in a finitely presented simple group. (Automaton groups have solvable word problem, so this is an instance
of `boone-higman-conjecture`; they are residually finite.)

## Known cases

- Contracting automaton groups (Nekrashevych; BBMZ survey Thm 5.1(12)), including all bounded-activity
  ones; finitely presented ones (Zaremsky arXiv:2405.09722 Thm 1.1).
- More generally every automaton group whose relator kernel is a finitely generated lift ideal:
  `lift-presented-automaton-groups-satisfy-boone-higman` (lane proof). This adds explicit hosts for the
  lamplighter and affine-lamplighter automata.
- Many further automaton groups are Boone–Higman for unrelated reasons (linear, or subgroups of V).

## What remains

The hypothesis of `lift-presented-automaton-groups-satisfy-boone-higman` belongs to the action, not the
group. Actions failing it:
- Skipper–Witzel–Zaremsky (arXiv:1712.05361, Thm 7.1 with `n = 2`, finite-state by its proof; Ex. 7.2) and
  every persistent action of a non-finitely-presented group (`persistent-automata-rn-group-fp-iff-group-fp`);
  by `persistent-subexponential-automata-generate-finite-groups` these persistent failures all have
  exponential activity;
- already at linear activity: `linear-activity-automata-can-have-non-fp-nekrashevych-groups`, via the
  obstruction `loop-retraction-forces-fp-eventual-section-groups` (a letter fixed by every state forces its
  eventual section group to be finitely presented).

The same group may still be covered through another action (Grigorchuk's group on `T_2`). No automaton group
is known that fails the hypothesis for every finite-state action. The route
`automaton-groups-bh-via-lift-presented-overgroups` reduces the claim to finding such an action or overgroup.
Whether this is a named problem in the literature was not checked here.
