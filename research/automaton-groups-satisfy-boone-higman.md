**OPEN.** Let `G <= Aut(X^*)` be generated by the states of a finite invertible automaton. Then `G` embeds
in a finitely presented simple group. (Automaton groups have solvable word problem, so this is an instance
of `boone-higman-conjecture`; they are residually finite.)

## Known cases

- Contracting automaton groups (Nekrashevych; BBMZ survey Thm 5.1(12)), including all bounded-activity
  ones; finitely presented ones (Zaremsky arXiv:2405.09722 Thm 1.1).
- More generally every automaton group whose relator kernel is a finitely generated lift ideal:
  `lift-presented-automaton-groups-satisfy-boone-higman` (lane proof). This adds the lamplighter
  and affine-lamplighter automata and every automaton group freely generated by its states.
- Many further automaton groups are Boone–Higman for unrelated reasons (linear, or subgroups of V).

## What remains

Automaton groups whose relator kernel is not a finitely generated lift ideal, e.g. the
Skipper–Witzel–Zaremsky examples (arXiv:1712.05361, Thm 7.1, Ex. 7.2) and non-finitely presented
persistent automaton groups (`persistent-automata-rn-group-fp-iff-group-fp`). The route
`automaton-groups-bh-via-lift-presented-overgroups` reduces the claim to an overgroup statement.
Whether this is a named problem in the literature was not checked here (bh-lit's survey is the place).
