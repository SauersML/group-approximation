title: Every finitely presented full Cantor group containing standard V has a faithful rank-seven clopen action
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/boone-higman-full-cantor-clopen-action-2026-09-08.md
---

Let D be a finitely presented full subgroup of Homeo(C) containing
the standard binary V. Its action on the countable set of proper
nonempty clopen subsets of C is faithful and oligomorphic. It has
exactly seven orbits of ordered pairs and five orbits of two-element
subsets. A point stabilizer is isomorphic to D times D, and
stabilizers of finite families are finitely presented as well.

Consequently this action has type (A), and D and each of its
finitely generated subgroups embed in a finitely presented simple
group. In particular they have decidable word problem.

No finite-state, arithmetic, or contraction hypothesis is imposed
on D. The theorem assumes that such a finitely presented full
actor already exists; it does not construct one for every
decidable input.
