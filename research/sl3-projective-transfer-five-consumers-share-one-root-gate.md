Put

```text
C=SL_3(Z),   A=SL_3(Z[1/2]),   D=A *_C A,
w=i_0(h)i_1(h)^(-1),   h=diag(2,1,1/2).
```

The five direct Cairn routes consuming
`projective-trace-square-transfer-for-sl3-pair` are not five independent
routes to the root goal.  For root-goal prioritization they have one common
core.

First, the direct HNN route already proves `non-hyperlinear-group` from the
transfer claim alone.  The other four direct routes prove reusable
intermediate statements: relative-commutant collapse, exclusion of an
amalgamated-free stable letter, collapse of the adjacent arithmetic double,
and a filtered return actuator.  They do not weaken the hypothesis needed
from the projective-transfer node.  Thus raw direct-consumer count measures
reuse, not five independent chances that a weaker theorem will reach the
root.

Second, the exact trace class consumed by the root argument is the canonical
character of the fixed double `D`, not arbitrary approximate assignments of
`A`.  The statement

```text
every canonical-character microstate sequence of D has
|tr(sigma_n(w))| -> 1                                  (PCA1)
```

is equivalent to non-hyperlinearity of `D`.  Indeed `w!=1` by amalgam normal
form.  If `D` were hyperlinear, a canonical microstate sequence would have
`tr(sigma_n(w))->0`, contradicting `(PCA1)`.  Conversely, if `D` is
non-hyperlinear there is no canonical microstate sequence, so `(PCA1)` holds
vacuously.  The weaker positive-density projective fold-sector formulation
has the same existence-level equivalence, as already recorded in
`sl3-positive-density-projective-fold-sector`.

Consequently neither canonical-double fold collapse nor its positive-sector
version is a mathematically weaker existence target.  They are sharper
certificate shapes for the same fixed candidate.

The best presently isolated **nonvacuous sufficient** statement below the
universal transfer is

```text
sl3-canonical-double-has-a-correctable-vertex.          (PCA2)
```

The joint near-exact sector and the one-near/one-far sector are already
closed.  Hence `(PCA2)` removes exactly the simultaneous-two-outlier sector.
But `(PCA2)` has a genuine unresolved fork: if the type-zero building action

```text
A acts on A/C
```

is sofic, `sofic-sl3-vertex-action-produces-two-outlier-double-models`
constructs canonical double microstates with both vertices uniformly
uncorrectable, refuting `(PCA2)` and making `D` sofic.  Proving `(PCA2)`
would instead prove that action nonsofic and make `D` non-hyperlinear.

Neither sign is currently supplied by the existing action literature in the
graph.  Locally compact sofic approximations of `SL_3(Q_2)` model unlabelled
local geometry; quotienting by the compact open vertex stabilizer does not
make the fixed dense arithmetic labels descend.  The missing datum is the
coherent deck-cocycle rounding in
`sl3-building-deck-cocycles-round-coherently`.  The Kun--Thom nonsofic
coordinate action concerns a different pair and does not decide this one.

Finally, exact finite-dimensional co-density does not cross this fork.
`projective-transfer-equals-rcc-for-perfect-pairs` and
`projective-transfer-linear-bound-equals-no-ultraproduct-leak` identify the
universal projective claim with universal matrix-ultraproduct relative-
commutant collapse.  The exact transfer theorem closes only exact
representations.  The fixed sofic calibration
`canonical-free-double-escapes-uniform-exact-projective-folding` proves that
exact folding plus canonical trace has no abstract compactness promotion.

Therefore the honest priority conclusion is:

```text
universal PTS: broad reusable theorem, one root obstruction;
canonical double: exact minimal trace class, but goal-equivalent;
correctable vertex: strongest nonvacuous sufficient gate, action-soficity fork.
```

The five-consumer cascade should not be ranked as five independent upstream
breakthrough opportunities for `non-hyperlinear-group`.
