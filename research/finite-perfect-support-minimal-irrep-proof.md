Finite-dimensional representation theory gives `(FPS1)`.  Compression by a
central summand preserves every exact zero relation, so each block remains a
perfect strategy.  Select a block minimizing the finite support vector.
The cited `M_7` factor example has no nontrivial central summand and every
local atom is positive, proving that this reduction has no further general
cap consequence.
