**OPEN.** This is the second clause of Brown's Question 3 (arXiv:math/0111286, §Questions, item 3, read from source): "Can a free group factor or a II$_1$ factor with property T contain a weakly dense, QD C$^*$-subalgebra? How about a Popa algebra?"

**Definition** (Brown–Dykema, arXiv:math/0207005, introduction): a Popa algebra is a unital simple C*-algebra `A` such that, for every finite `𝔉 ⊆ A` and `ε > 0`, there is a nonzero finite-dimensional subalgebra `B ⊆ A` with unit `e` satisfying `‖[x, e]‖ < ε` and `dist(exe, B) < ε` for `x ∈ 𝔉`.

**Known.**
- Every McDuff factor contains a weakly dense Popa algebra (Brown, Theorem `arbitraryMcDuff`).
- So do the interpolated free group factors `L(F_s)`, `1 < s < ∞` (Brown–Dykema).
- The QD clause holds for every separable II_1 factor (`separable-ii1-factors-have-weakly-dense-full-free-group-cstar`, unreviewed).
- For property (T) the Popa clause is open, as far as the bounded search in the artifact goes.

**Candidate.** `L(G_X)`, the property (T) factor of the note. `G_X` is generated by two locally finite tower unit groups, so `L(G_X)` is generated by two hyperfinite subalgebras.

## Attempts
- **Residually finite-dimensional copies (sk-free-5, 2026-09-13).** The weakly dense copy of `C*(F_∞)` satisfies Popa's approximation exactly on the corners `q_j`. It dies at simplicity: `C*(F_∞)` has many finite-dimensional quotients, and no simple QD hull of it inside `M` was found.
- **Scale constraint (sk-free-5).** By `kazhdan-group-factor-popa-corners-have-vanishing-trace`, Popa corners serving approximate Kazhdan generators at tolerance `η` have trace `≤ 4ε_S^{-2}(4η+2δ)²`. This is not an obstruction, since Brown–Dykema's corners in the full factor `L(F_s)` also vanish. It is a constraint on any construction.
- **Property (T) bimodule argument (sk-free-5).**
  - The vector `(p ⊗_B p)/τ(p)^{1/2}` in `L²M ⊗_B L²M` lies in a multiple of the coarse bimodule, which has no central vectors for diffuse `M`. It is almost central at relative scale `η` for the finite set.
  - The Kazhdan set of `M` need not lie in `A`. Replacing it by 2-norm approximants costs `‖λ_s − y_s‖_2/τ(p)^{1/2}`, which diverges on the vanishing corners above.
  - It dies at weak density. An obstruction would need a Kazhdan set inside `A`, or a relative-(T) inclusion `A''`-free in its hypotheses.
- **Tower structure of `G_X` (idea, not attempted).** `G_X = ⟨L_y, L_{y′}⟩`, with `L_y = ⋃_n GL_3(A(𝒫_n))` (route of `subshift-elementary-groups-are-character-rigid`).
  - Brown–Dykema's route uses the free product structure of `L(F_s)` and residual finite-dimensionality of full amalgamated free products of matrix algebras.
  - An analogue for `L(G_X)` would need the two hyperfinite algebras in a position whose full C*-hull keeps finite-dimensional representations on vanishing corners while becoming simple.
