**Live.** Let `G`, `p`, `Z` witness `bernoulli-cylinder-comparison-fails-for-some-group`, with `X = (Z/p)^Z` and
`I_1 = C_0(X \ {0})`. The steps below use `bernoulli-bc-splits-into-host-and-cylinder-comparison`.

1. By item 3, `mu_(G, I_1)` is not surjective in some degree. Item 3 holds for every `G` with the cylinder
   *-homomorphism as `T`, by Claim W of `research/artifacts/bc-bernoulli-rank-functionals-torsion-2026-09-13.md`.
2. By item 1, `mu_(G, C(X)) = mu_G ⊕ mu_(G, I_1)` is not surjective.
3. By item 2 of `amenable-kernel-bc-transfer-and-ktop-half-exactness` (Chabert--Echterhoff Corollary 3.5, no torsion
   hypothesis), reduced assembly for `F_p[Z] ⋊ G = F_p wr_Z G` with trivial coefficients is not surjective.

This needs no information about the host `G` itself.

**Size of the counterexample.** The group `F_p wr_Z G` is generated by `G` and one lamp for each `G`-orbit of `Z`. So it
is finitely generated when `G` is finitely generated and `Z` has finitely many orbits.
