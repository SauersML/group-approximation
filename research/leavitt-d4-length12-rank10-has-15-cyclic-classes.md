For the fixed degree-four `(1,3,3,5)` profile, the balanced rank-ten layer has
201 oriented embeddings: 99 NNN and 102 III.  Quotienting by cyclic rotation
of the target faces and cyclic rotation of the three five-face arcs gives
exactly fifteen classes, seven NNN and eight III.  Global inversion is
expected to pair some classes only after reversing the ambient profile; it is
therefore not used to claim an unverified count of eight.
