A group of type `F` has a finite `K(G,1)`, whose 3-skeleton is finite, so it
has type `F_3`. The required claim gives such a group with unsolvable word
problem. So not every group of type `F_3` has solvable word problem, and the
answer to Problem 1.2 is no.
