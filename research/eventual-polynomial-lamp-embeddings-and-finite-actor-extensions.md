With the artifact's notation, `N_q^(Z)⋊(E_d⋊Z)` embeds in `B_{d,2}`,
where `E_d` consists of integer profiles polynomial of degree at most
`d` independently at both ends, with finitely many exceptions. The
source is finitely generated. For fixed `h`, the shift-divisible-by-`h`
subgroup of the analogous eventual quasipolynomial source embeds in a
finite product of such hosts by residue classes.

For the resulting clopen actors, finite products and finite extensions
admit faithful oligomorphic actions with type `F_∞` group and finite
subset stabilizers whenever the input actors have these properties.
The same construction has the multi-scaling version over `Z[S^-1]`
with independent scalings and vector-valued polynomial profiles described
in artifact §10, conditional on the corresponding affine base and
polynomial tower finiteness assertions.

## Attempts

The shell construction allows finitely many exceptional shells, but its
faithfulness argument must compare two distinct `(1,2)` coordinates
on **each** shell; infinitely many empty shells do not detect a finitely
supported twist. The proposed module generators are a delta sequence
and `2(d+1)` half-line Newton sequences. The finite-product actor uses
a disjoint union; finite extensions use Kaloujnine–Krasner into
`B wr Q` with `Q` finite and its block action. Both the cocycle convention
and finite-subset stabilizer descriptions need verification. Residue
classes reduce fixed-period quasipolynomials to a finite product and a
finite extension. Multi-scaling additionally requires the commuting HNN
and vector germ versions, not a bare assertion that the scalar proof
automatically applies. These remain mathematical obligations; see
artifact §10 for the full proposed argument.
