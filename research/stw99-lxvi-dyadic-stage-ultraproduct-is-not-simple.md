Let `(A_n)_(n>=1)` be unital C\*-algebras with finite unit, and suppose that
there is a unital embedding `M_(k_n) -> A_n`, where `k_n -> infinity`.  For
every free ultrafilter `omega`, the norm ultraproduct

```text
prod_omega A_n
```

is not simple.

More precisely, the class of a rank-one projection from each displayed
matrix algebra is a nonzero projection which generates a proper ideal.
Consequently, replacing the reduced product in
`stw99-lxvi-reduced-product-assembles-finite-stages` by a norm ultraproduct
removes the scalar central projections but cannot supply the missing
simplicity.
