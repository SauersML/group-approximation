`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath` supplies the explicit pair of Theorem E and
nonsoficity of `W = (Z/2Z) wr_(G/Gamma) G` by Theorem A.
`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreaths-are-surjunctive` supplies surjunctivity of the same
`W`. `W` is generated by the finitely generated group `G` and one lamp,
because the coset action is transitive.
