Response 47, Section 3. Inputs: fixed price one for a direct product of two infinite groups containing an element of infinite order; nonuniqueness for T_4 x Z; and Lyons-Peres-Schramm, Minimal spanning forests, arXiv:math/0412263, Proposition 3.6 (the wired forest has expected root degree two, and the free forest contains it) together with Theorem 3.12 (for a Cayley graph the two forests agree almost surely if and only if p_c = p_u). Countability and translation invariance turn the almost-sure difference into positive expected degree at the root. The inclusion of the free forest in every finite stage is the previous claim. This is a written deduction from imported theorems, not an independent verification of them.
