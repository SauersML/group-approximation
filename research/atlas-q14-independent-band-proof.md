On `U+W+Z`, let both nilpotents map `W` into `U`, one by the identity and
one by `C`.  Their products vanish, and invertibility of `C` and `I+C`
makes all three V4 directions rank `m`.  Equal square-zero Jordan types give
the required chart conjugacy.  The induced ratio `Mbar Nbar^(-1)` on the
active quotient recovers `C` up to similarity; irreducible companion
matrices therefore produce widths tending to infinity.
