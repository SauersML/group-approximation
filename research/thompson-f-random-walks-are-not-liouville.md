title: Finitely supported strictly non-degenerate random walks on Thompson's group F have non-trivial Poisson boundary
---

**ESTABLISHED by citation.** V. A. Kaimanovich, *Thompson's group F is not
Liouville*, arXiv:1602.02971v2 (22 Mar 2016); published as a chapter of *Groups,
Graphs and Random Walks* (Cambridge University Press, 2017). Abstract, verbatim:

> We prove that random walks on Thompson's group F driven by strictly
> non-degenerate finitely supported probability measures μ have a non-trivial
> Poisson boundary.

The abstract adds that the boundaries are built from the Schreier graph on the
orbit of the dyadic rationals.

**Consequence for the amenability root.** A group is amenable iff some
non-degenerate symmetric probability measure on it has trivial Poisson boundary
(Kaimanovich–Vershik; Rosenblatt). An amenability proof for `F` through this
criterion must therefore use a measure with infinite support.
