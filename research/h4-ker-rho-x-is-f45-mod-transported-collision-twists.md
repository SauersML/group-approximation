**Setting.** As in `h4-case1-pure-normal-form-and-fibred-obstruction`.
- `𝒜` is the `H_4` reflection arrangement in `V = ℂ^4` (60 walls, `m_{s_3s_4} = 5`), with `M = V ∖ ⋃𝒜` and
  `P = π_1(M)`.
- `X = {s_2,s_3,s_4}` and `Y = {s_1,s_2,s_3}`. `ℓ` spans the line `L_X` fixed by `W_X`.
- `ρ_X = p_*`, where `p : V → V/L_X` is the projection. `K = ker ρ_X`, `F = ker(ρ_X|P_Y) ≅ F_3` and
  `N = ker(ρ_Y|P_X)`.
- The 15 walls through `L_X` are the *X-walls*. The other 45 walls meet each fibre `b + L_X` in the 45
  *fibre punctures*. Three of them are the *F-walls* `α_1, α_1+α_2, α_1+α_2+α_3`, whose meridians are
  `P_3 = s_1^2`, `P_2` and `P_1`.
- A rank-2 flat `E` is *bad* if no X-wall contains it. Its *collision wall* is the hyperplane `D_E = E + L_X`.
  Over `D_E` the fibre punctures of the walls through `E` collide.

**Theorem.**
1. **(Census, exact over `ℚ(√5)`.)**
   - There are 722 rank-2 flats, of which 316 are bad: 240 of type `A_1×A_1`, 70 of type `A_2` and 6 of type
     `I_2(5)`. They carry the 510 bad pairs of w15.
   - The bad flats span 106 collision walls, none of them a wall of `𝒜`. 16 of them carry one bad flat, 30
     carry two and 60 carry four.
   - Each F-wall lies in exactly 16 bad flats: 10 of type `A_1×A_1`, 5 of type `A_2` and 1 of type `I_2(5)`.
     That makes 48 in all, and no bad flat contains two F-walls.
2. **(Fibre-type completion.)**
   - `L_X` is modular in `𝒜° = 𝒜 ∪ {D_E}` (166 hyperplanes), so `p : M° → M_X°` is a fibre bundle with fibre
     `ℂ ∖ 45` points. Here `M_X°` is `V/L_X` minus 15 + 106 planes.
   - The map `σ(b) = b + c|b|ℓ` (`c ≫ 1`) is a section. It is defined on all of `M_X`, and there it realises
     the parabolic `P_X ⊂ P`.
   - So `G° = π_1(M°) = F_45 ⋊_μ B°`, where `B° = π_1(M_X°)` and `μ : B° → Aut(F_45)` is the pure-braid
     monodromy.
3. **(Exact presentation.)** Let `𝕄 ⊲ B°` be the normal closure of the collision-wall meridians, so
   `B°/𝕄 = P_X`. Then
   `P = (F_45/R) ⋊ P_X`, `K = ker ρ_X ≅ F_45/R`, with `R = ⟨⟨x^{-1}μ(γ)(x) : x ∈ F_45, γ ∈ 𝕄⟩⟩`.
   The conjugation action of `P_X` on `K` is induced by `μ`. Equivalently, `R` is the normal closure of the
   transported local relations:
   - for every bad flat `E` and every transport `β ∈ B°` of its collision disc `Δ_E`, let `δ` be the boundary
     loop of `β(Δ_E)` and `x_h` (`h ∈ E`) its puncture loops;
   - then `[δ, x_h] = 1`. For `|E| = 2` this is `[x_h, x_{h'}] = 1`. For `|E| = 3, 5` it says that the cluster
     product is central in the cluster.

   There is no further relation: the kernel of `G° → P` meets `F_45` exactly in `R`.
4. **(Consequences.)**
   - `K^{ab} = ℤ^{45}`, freely generated by the puncture classes.
   - For each generator `P_i` of `F` (`i = 1,2,3`), `C_K(P_i) ⊇ ℤ^2`. This comes from each of the 10
     `A_1×A_1` bad flats through its F-wall. So `K` is not free, and centralisers of elements of `F` in `K` are
     not cyclic.
   - Nothing like this happens in the free group `F_45`, where every centraliser is cyclic.
   - (H″) becomes a statement about the `𝕄`-twisted action of the preimage `Ñ ⊂ B°` of `N` on `F_45/R`.
5. **(Calibration.)** The same computation gives:
   - `A_3` and `A_4` (exact), and `B_3` and `B_4` (floating point, `dwalls.py`): no bad flats, so `L_X` is
     already modular and `K` is free.
   - `H_3` with `X = I_2(5)`: 5 bad flats, all of type `A_1×A_1`, on 5 collision walls, 2 of them through
     F-walls. There the Case 1 analogue holds (Lemma `lem:triple` of arXiv:2405.12068, as recorded in the
     target). So collisions through F-walls do not by themselves obstruct (H″).
   - The `A_3`-fixed line of `H_4` (the `ρ_Y` side) is worse: 547 bad flats on 319 collision walls. So
     neither retraction of `P` has a free kernel.

**What it does to Case 1.**
- The type-A proof of (H″) runs the `ℤ/2` arc Helly lemma in a free fibre group on which `N` acts by mapping
  classes.
- In `H_4`, the free fibre exists only in `G°`. There `N` lifts to `Ñ` only up to `𝕄`, which acts through the
  collision twists.
- So a proof has two tasks:
  - (i) a Helly argument for `Ñ` acting on the 45-punctured plane, modulo the collision twists;
  - (ii) the coincidences `[u,a] = [v,b]` that hold only modulo `R`. A disproof must come from (ii), or from
    `μ(Ñ)` being too small to realise the arc-Helly centre.
- The fibre punctures are linear in the base point: `y_h(b) = −h(b)/h(ℓ)`. So `μ` on explicit loops for the
  generators of `N` is an exact finite computation, and it is the next step.
