At the parameters supplied by the first required claim, (OTG3) of
`opposite-transvections-are-first-nonore-relative-gate` says that
`P *_Gamma P` is hyperlinear if and only if `L(Gamma) subset L(P)` is `RE/C`.
The inclusion is not `RE/C`, so this countable group is non-hyperlinear.
