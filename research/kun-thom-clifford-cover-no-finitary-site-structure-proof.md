Artifact Section 1, Proposition 1.1.

Fix an edge `{o, y}` and a finite site set `T`. The translates `g` with `g L(c_o)` or `g L(c_y)` meeting
`T` form finitely many left cosets of point stabilizers, which have infinite index. So some `g` avoids all
of them (Neumann).

(A2) with `U` empty kills `c_(go)` and `c_(gy)` in `p_T`. `G`-invariance of the commutator form gives
`[c_(go), c_(gy)] = eps`, so `p_T(eps) = 1` for every `T`. That contradicts (A1).
