For the explicit `q=2`, `r=d=3` Kun--Thom actor

```text
G=EL_3(F_2[x_1^(+-1),x_2^(+-1),x_3^(+-1)]) rtimes SL_3(Z),
```

let `K` be the finite elementary Kazhdan packet, let
`h=e_12((x_1x_2x_3)^(-1))`, and let `A,B` be the two named positive
compressors.  The finite set

```text
S=K union {h,A,B}                                           (FAI1)
```

generates `G`.  There are normalized-HS asymptotic representations of the
**whole group `G`** which stay a fixed positive distance on `S` from every
genuine same-dimensional representation of `G`.  In particular no universal
strict correction modulus can follow merely from adding both `A,B`
generators and all their semidirect actor relations to the Laurent interface.

The same full-actor tuples can even be extended, after a fixed doubling,
through every algebraic relation of the binary generalized wreath
presentation while keeping a trace-zero lamp.  Tensor with `M_2`, let
`z=diag(1,-1)`, and send the base lamp to `1 tensor z`.  It is central, so all
stabilizer, covariance, involution, and pairwise lamp-commutation relations
hold exactly; only the original actor defects remain.  What fails is
precisely the canonical distinct-site moment:

```text
tau(z (u_g z u_g^*))=1,                                  (FAI2)
```

whereas for `gGamma!=Gamma` the canonical Bernoulli lamps have product trace
zero.  The Clifford version likewise makes the returned Majorana equal to
the original one and therefore fails anticommutation maximally.

Thus the surviving restriction in canonical actor exactification is not
"extends across both compressors" or even "satisfies the wreath group
relations with a balanced lamp."  It is the positive-density two-site
Bernoulli/Majorana correlation itself, jointly with the full stabilizer
packet.  Any robust theorem which discards that correlation is refuted by
the source-specific strict actor countermodels above.  This does **not** say
that the fixed-doubled tuples stay far from exact representations: that would
be a flexible-stability statement, and is not supplied by local instability.
