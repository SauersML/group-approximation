title: Gauge-pair free-phase holonomy makes the common Gram automatic and leaves one mixed moment
---

A multi-piece Hecke unitary can mix opposite gauge-share atoms while fixing
their logical product.  Taking `S_c=V_c^*Q` then gives the literal common
source `S_c^*S_c=Q` for all contexts.

All remaining covariance is one finite mixed Hecke moment (or one packed
root coefficient).  This construction is exact-complete, but ordinary
word relations still must pay that matrix-only moment.
