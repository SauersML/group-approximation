Use the permitted dimension-at-infinity common-block architecture, but keep
the internal actor on every positive-charge fixed block inside amplifications
of a uniformly bounded spectral model.  Let the multiplicities and total
block dimensions diverge, hoping that dimension escape alone defeats the
bounded-block recurrence argument.

This architecture is invalidated by
`fournier-facio-fixed-block-spectrum-escapes`.  Nonsoficity forces some
nontrivial actor to fix positive block charge, and torsion-free essential
freeness forces the number of distinct eigenphases of that internal actor to
escape every finite cutoff there.  Growing multiplicity of finitely many
phases adds no freedom.
