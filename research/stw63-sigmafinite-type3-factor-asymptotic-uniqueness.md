title: Every sigma-finite type-III factor has asymptotic uniqueness without a separable-predual assumption

Let `M` be a sigma-finite, equivalently countably decomposable, type-III
factor.  No separable-predual or concrete representation hypothesis is
imposed.  Then any two unital embeddings `Z->M` are asymptotically unitarily
equivalent in norm.

This strengthens approximate uniqueness and extends the factor input used in
the separable-predual central-integration theorem.  Its simple-target proof
is specific to sigma-finite factors; the later theorem
`stw63-arbitrary-type3-vn-asymptotic-uniqueness` strictly subsumes this claim
by using full-map classification instead.
