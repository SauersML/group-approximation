title: "Every loop braid group LB_n = ΣAut_n is of type F_infinity"
---

For every n, the group `ΣAut_n` of symmetric automorphisms of `F_n` (every basis
element goes to a conjugate of a basis element, with no inverses), also called
the loop braid group `LB_n`, is of type F∞. Its finite-index subgroup `PΣAut_n`
(the kernel of `ρ_n: ΣAut_n → S_n`) is then of type F∞ too.
