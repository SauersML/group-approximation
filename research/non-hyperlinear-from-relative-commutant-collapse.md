`G = <SL_2(Z[1/2]), t | [t, SL_2(Z)] = 1>` is non-hyperlinear as soon as the

is that identity as an operator-algebra claim.  This route needs neither
flexible HS-stability nor the far-sector representation-variety analysis; it
is the direct II_1-factor form of the goal.
