1. **The algebras.**
   - The Fourier--Gelfand transform identifies `C*_r(V)` with `C(X)`. Since `V` is abelian and
     `Γ = V ⋊ G` splits, `C*_r(Γ) = C*_r(V) ⋊_r G = C(X) ⋊_r G`, with `G` acting on `X` by
     `(g ξ)(v) = ξ(g^-1 v)`.
   - The same holds for `V/W`: its characters are the characters of `V` trivial on `W`, so
     `(V/W)^ = W^perp = Z`.
   - The homomorphism `V -> V/W` induces `C*_r(V) -> C*_r(V/W)`, `u_v ↦ u_(v+W)`. In Fourier
     terms this is restriction `res : C(X) -> C(Z)`. Hence `q = res ⋊ G`.
   - `ker(res) = C_0(U)`, a `G`-invariant ideal. Write `i` for the inclusion.
2. **The transfer**, from item 2 of `amenable-kernel-bc-transfer-and-ktop-half-exactness`:
   - `mu_Γ` is surjective in degree `j` iff `mu_(G, C(X))` is;
   - `mu_(Γ')` is injective in degree `j` iff `mu_(G, C(Z))` is.
3. **The chase.** Suppose `mu_(G, C(X))` is surjective in degree `j` and `mu_(G, C(Z))` is
   injective in degree `j`. Let `x ∈ K_j(C(X) ⋊_r G)` with `q_* x = 0`.
   - (a) By surjectivity, `x = mu_(G, C(X))(y)` for some `y ∈ K^top_j(G; C(X))`.
   - (b) By naturality of assembly in the coefficients (item 3),
     `mu_(G, C(Z))(res_* y) = q_* mu_(G, C(X))(y) = q_* x = 0`. By injectivity, `res_* y = 0`.
   - (c) `K^top_j(G; ·)` is exact at the middle (item 3), so `y = i_* z` for some
     `z ∈ K^top_j(G; C_0(U))`.
   - (d) By naturality, `x = mu_(G, C(X))(i_* z) = i_* mu_(G, C_0(U))(z)`. This lies in the
     image of `K_j(C_0(U) ⋊_r G)`.

   So the K-theory sequence is exact at the middle.
4. **Contrapositive.** If it is not exact, then `mu_(G, C(X))` is not surjective or
   `mu_(G, C(Z))` is not injective in degree `j`. Step 2 turns this into the stated failure
   for `V ⋊ G` or `(V/W) ⋊ G`, with coefficients `C`.
5. **Finite generation.** `V ⋊ G` is generated by `G` together with module generators of `V`,
   and `(V/W) ⋊ G` is its quotient.

No assumption on `C_0(U)` is needed: the chase uses neither injectivity nor surjectivity of
`mu_(G, C_0(U))`.
