For the prescribed-endpoint group `pi:H->Q_- x Q_+`, the normal
subgroups are exactly `1`, `L=ker pi`, `pi^{-1}(Q_- x 1)`,
`pi^{-1}(1 x Q_+)`, and `H`. The simple locally finite group `L` is
contained in every nontrivial normal subgroup and has trivial
centralizer in `H`.

Thus `H` is centerless, directly indecomposable and just non-MF:
it is non-MF, but every proper quotient is LEF. Every noninjective
homomorphic image is isomorphic to `Q_- x Q_+`, `Q_-`, `Q_+`, or `1`.
The group retains its Kazhdan property, three torsion generators of
orders `7,2,2`, and absence of nontrivial finite quotients.
