For every abelian group `A` with trivial Thompson-`V` action, every central
extension

```text
1 -> A -> E -> V -> 1
```

has a unique homomorphic splitting.  Consequently a nontrivial self-similar
Clifford sign in a Thompson--Nekrashevych construction cannot be a central
extension class of the bare prefix-mixer `V`; it must involve the nontrivial
state/Clifford kernel or a genuinely relative extension.
