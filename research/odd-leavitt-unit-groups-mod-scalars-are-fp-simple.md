**ESTABLISHED (conditional on the imported criteria of Khanh arXiv:2609.08428v1
and on Ara--Cortiñas arXiv:1108.0352v3).** Let `p` be a prime and
`L_p = L_(F_p)(1,2)`. The groups `L_p^x / F_p^x` and `(L_p ⊗ L_p)^x / F_p^x`
are finitely presented and simple. For `p = 2` the scalars are trivial and these
are `L^x` and `(L ⊗ L)^x`.

`St_r` equals `GL_r` for `r >= 3` over both rings. The positive-characteristic
Boone--Higman class `positive-char-surface-linear-groups-satisfy-boone-higman`
lives in the second group.
