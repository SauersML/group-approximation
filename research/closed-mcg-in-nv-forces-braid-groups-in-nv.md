**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, elementary; not independently reviewed; no priority
claimed). Let `g ≥ 2`, `n ≥ 1`, and let `D` be any group. If some finite-index
subgroup `L ≤ Mod(S_g)` embeds in `D`, then some finite-index subgroup of the braid
group `B_{2g−1}` embeds in `D`. With `D = nV` this gives a necessary condition for
the Brin--Thompson instance of `closed-mcg-virtually-embeds-in-fp-full-cantor-group`.

## Proof

1. **A braid group in the bordered subsurface.** Let `S' ⊂ S_g` be a subsurface of
   genus `g − 1` with one boundary curve `∂`. Its complement is a genus-one surface
   with one boundary curve. By Birman--Hilden (Farb--Margalit, *A Primer on
   Mapping Class Groups*, §9.4; not re-read here), the Dehn twists about a chain
   of `2g − 2` curves in `S'` generate a copy of `B_{2g−1}` in `Mod(S')`.
2. **Extension by the identity is injective.** The kernel of `Mod(S') → Mod(S_g)`
   is generated by twists about boundary curves of `S'` that bound a disk or a
   once-punctured disk in `S_g`, or are isotopic to another boundary curve of
   `S'` (Farb--Margalit Theorem 3.18; not re-read). `∂` is essential, since both
   sides have positive genus, and it is the only boundary curve. So the map is
   injective, and `B_{2g−1} ≤ Mod(S_g)`.
3. **Finite index.** `L ∩ B_{2g−1}` has finite index in `B_{2g−1}` and embeds in
   `D`. ∎

## Consequences and scope

- **Calibration with `V`.** For `2g − 1 ≥ 5` a finite-index subgroup of
  `B_{2g−1}` contains `Z² ∗ Z`: take large powers of twists as in
  `closed-mcg-finite-index-subgroups-do-not-embed-in-v`, inside the braid
  subgroup. So braid groups do not virtually embed in `V`. This is consistent with,
  and weaker than, that node.
- **What a NO would give.** If some braid group `B_m` has no finite-index subgroup
  embedding in any `nV`, then for `2g − 1 ≥ m` no finite-index subgroup of `Mod(S_g)`
  embeds in any `nV`. That would kill the Brin--Thompson instance of the full-Cantor
  route for large genus. It would not touch other full Cantor hosts.
- **What a YES would not give.** Braid groups in `nV` say nothing about `Mod(S_g)`.
  But any construction putting pure braid groups `PB_m` into `nV` for all `m` is
  the natural stepping stone.
- **Known pieces.** `PB_3 ≅ F_2 × Z` is a right-angled Artin group, so it lies in `2V`
  (`every-raag-embeds-in-brin-thompson-group-2v`). Whether `PB_m`, `m ≥ 4`, embeds in
  a right-angled Artin group, which would put it in `2V` by Salo, was not checked
  against the literature in this pass.
