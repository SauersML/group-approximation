ESTABLISHED.  Let the quaternion packet be generated by `a,b,z` with

```text
z^2=1,       z central,       a^2=b^2=z,
b a b^(-1)=z a.                                          (QAI1)
```

In its complex group algebra define

```text
E=(1-z)/2,        J=i E a,        P=(E+J)/2.             (QAI2)
```

Then `E` is the central projection onto the `z=-1` sector, `J` is a
self-adjoint involution on `E`, and `P` is a projection.  Conjugation by `b`
sends `J` to `-J`, so in every finite-dimensional unitary representation

```text
tr(P)=tr(E)/2.                                            (QAI3)
```

Adjoin two otherwise free unitary variables `U_x,U_y` and, exactly as in
`one-sided-amitsur-oracle-has-two-unitary-corner-model`, put

```text
X=P U_x P,                 Y=P U_y P,
W_j=X Y^j X,
A_n=s_(2n)(W_1,...,W_(2n)),
R_n=((2n)!)^(-1) A_n^*A_n-P.                            (QAI4)
```

The countable positive relations `R_n=0` have a bounded exact infinite model
in which `z` is nontrivial.  Indeed, on `H direct_sum H` take

```text
z=-I,
J=[[I,0],[0,-I]],
a=-iJ,
b=[[0,I],[-I,0]],                                       (QAI5)
```

so `(QAI1)` holds and `P` is the upper-left half-corner.  The two Halmos
unitaries of the Fock creation isometries then satisfy every relation `R_n=0`.

Conversely, take any `D`-dimensional unitary evaluation of the quaternion
packet and `U_x,U_y`, and let

```text
r=rank(P)=rank(E)/2.                                     (QAI6)
```

If `r>0`, Amitsur--Levitzki in `P M_D(C) P isomorphic to M_r(C)` gives
`A_r=0`.  Therefore the instruction indexed by the endogenous corner rank has

```text
R_r=-P,
||R_r||_(2,D)^2=tr_D(P)=tr_D(E)/2
                   =||z-I||_(2,D)^2/8,                  (QAI7)
```

or equivalently

```text
||R_r||_(2,D)=||z-I||_(2,D)/(2 sqrt(2)).                 (QAI8)
```

Thus no separate positive-density hypothesis is needed: precisely the part of
the model that separates the fixed marked word `z` from the identity pays the
dimension-matched Amitsur residual, with an exact universal constant.  Extra
one-dimensional quaternion types are harmless because they have `z=1` and
lie outside `E`.

This is still not the missing group compiler.  Although `a,b,z,U_x,U_y` are
unitaries and `P` is now a fixed finite-group-algebra coefficient, the
relations `R_n=0` are countably many growing additive star-polynomials rather
than group relators.  The remaining terminal has been reduced to finitely
groupifying and PCP-localizing `(QAI4)` with runtime-independent HS loss; it
no longer includes construction of a bounded model, unitarization, moving
inverse generators, an external matrix corner, or carrier-density recovery.
