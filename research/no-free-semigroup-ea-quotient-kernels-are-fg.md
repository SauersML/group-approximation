title: In a finitely generated group without free subsemigroups, kernels of elementary amenable quotients are finitely generated
---

**Statement.** Let `G` be a finitely generated group with no free subsemigroup on two generators,
and `N` a normal subgroup with `G/N` elementary amenable. Then `N` is finitely generated.

This generalizes Rosset's theorem (solvable quotients). Imported through
`no-free-semigroup-ea-quotient-kernels-are-fg-citation`.
