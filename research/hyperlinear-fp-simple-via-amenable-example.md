## Why sufficient

Amenable groups are sofic: Følner sets give almost-invariant finite models.
Sofic groups are hyperlinear. So a finitely presented infinite simple amenable
group is a finitely presented infinite simple hyperlinear group, which is
exactly the target.

The route is one-directional. A hyperlinear example need not be amenable.
