The prerequisite records freeness of the `s`-only and `t`-only coefficient
subalgebras.  The ideal spanned by words longer than `n` gives a finite
quotient and separates every fixed nonzero polynomial for large enough `n`.
Apply this entrywise to a nonidentity invertible matrix; its inverse also
maps to the quotient, so the separating image is a finite group image.
