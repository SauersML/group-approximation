**ESTABLISHED (literature import).**  If `H_1` and `H_2` are countable
hyperlinear groups, then `H_1 * H_2` is hyperlinear.  In particular `H * Z`
is hyperlinear whenever `H` is.

A countable group is hyperlinear exactly when `L(H)` embeds trace-preservingly
into `R^omega`.  Since `L(H_1 * H_2) = L(H_1) * L(H_2)` (free product over the
scalars, with the regular traces), the statement is the case `B = C` of the
Brown--Dykema--Jung amalgamated free product permanence.
