## Statement

Let `S = ⟨a, b, l, s⟩ ≤ Homeo(R ∪ {∞})` be Lodha's group, as in
`lodha-circle-group-is-finitely-presented-and-simple`.

1. `S` preserves `QP^1 = Q ∪ {∞}` and acts on it faithfully and 2-transitively.
2. In Lodha's Cantor model, let `w_{σ,τ} = y_σ y_τ^{-1}` for independent binary words
   `σ, τ`. Then `Stab_S(∞)` is generated by the images of the ten maps
   `x`, `x_1`, `w_{0,1}`, `w_{0,10}`, `w_{01,1}`, `w_{00,11}`, `w_{01,11}`, `w_{00,10}`,
   `w_{01,10}`, `w_{01,110}` of `2^N`. In particular `Stab_S(∞)` is finitely generated.

Here "image" means the unique circle homeomorphism `h` with `Φ(ξ·g) = Φ(ξ)·h` on rational
sequences `ξ` (Lemma 1.4 of the artifact).

**Context.** This closes the gap named in
`research/artifacts/q34-pl-circle-families-2026-09-13.md` §6 item 2. Lodha names
`S_∞ = Stab_S(∞)` (arXiv:1710.06220v2, l.827) but gives no finite generating set for it.
