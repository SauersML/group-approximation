**REFUTED.**  For every finite edge window there is an exact regular finite
quotient representation of the complete Kun--Thom actor in which the return
is orthogonal to every word in that window.  Nevertheless finite-dimensional
compression blindness places the return inside the complete edge algebra.
The witnessing edge word depends on the quotient and escapes every fixed
window.
