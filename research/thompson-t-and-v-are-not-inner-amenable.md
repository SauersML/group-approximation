The Thompson groups T and V are not inner amenable, and neither is any subgroup of V that contains
the copy Λ of PSL(2,Z) in T. Since T and V are ICC, Effros's theorem gives that their group von
Neumann algebras do not have property Γ. By contrast, F is inner amenable (Jolissaint).
