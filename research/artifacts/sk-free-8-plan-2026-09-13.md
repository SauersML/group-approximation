# sk-free-8 plan: simple Kazhdan LEF groups of non-uniform exponential growth (2026-09-13)

Lane sk-free-8, a free lane of wave 10, with the problem-hunter temperament. G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2)⋊_T Z) for an infinite minimal subshift X. It is infinite, f.g., simple, Kazhdan and LEF (note on main 9047d0d3c9).

## 1. The printed questions (verbatim, from sources fetched on MSI 2026-09-13)
1. **Gromov 1981, recalled in Sauer–Schesler arXiv:2605.30163 l.257:** "Gromov … asked whether every finitely generated group of exponential growth has uniform exponential growth." Wilson 2004 answered it negatively with residually finite branch groups.
2. **Kourovka Notebook 21.117 (E. Schesler), arXiv:1401.0300, 21tkt.tex l.14290–14297:**
   > (a) Does there exist a finitely generated simple group that is of exponential growth but not of uniformly exponential growth?
   > (b) Does there exist a finitely generated hereditarily just-infinite group that is of exponential growth but not of uniformly exponential growth?

   Printed answer (l.14298): "Yes, Thompson's group $V$ provides an affirmative answer to both questions (R. Sauer, E. Schesler, Preprint, 2026, arXiv:2605.30163)."
3. **de la Harpe 2002, §7 of his survey, as quoted by Sauer–Schesler arXiv:2606.15945 (14 June 2026):** "In Section~7 of his survey~\cite{delaHarpe2002}, de la Harpe asked whether there exists a group of non-uniform exponential growth that has Kazhdan's property~(T)." Their answer: "There is an acylindrically hyperbolic Kazhdan group of non-uniform exponential growth", built as a common quotient of Aut(F_{4·2^n}).
4. **Cornulier, Séminaire Bourbaki 1064, arXiv:2002.09342, "Problèmes et questions ouvertes" (7):** uniform exponential growth of [[φ]]′ for infinite minimal subshifts φ. Quoted through `sk-questions-ggt-literature-2026-09-13-part2.md`; the French text is still to be quoted verbatim at source.

## 2. Status and the gap this lane targets
- V (simple, finitely presented, not Kazhdan; its soficity is unknown) answers 21.117(a).
- The Sauer–Schesler Kazhdan example is acylindrically hyperbolic, so it is NOT simple (acylindrically hyperbolic groups are SQ-universal).
- Wilson's groups are residually finite branch groups, not simple and not Kazhdan.
- **Open combination (bounded search: the three papers above and the Kourovka 21st edition):** an infinite f.g. SIMPLE group with property (T) and non-uniform exponential growth. Also a simple LEF (hence sofic) group of non-uniform exponential growth: V is not LEF, since f.p. + LEF ⇒ RF.

## 3. Target theorem
**Theorem A (target).** If R_X ≅ M_2(R_X) (for example X the period-doubling or Thue–Morse subshift, by recognizability of constant-length substitutions), then G_X has non-uniform exponential growth. So there are infinite f.g. simple Kazhdan LEF groups of non-uniform exponential growth.

Mechanism:
1. **Self-similarity.** R ≅ M_2(R) ⇒ G_X ≅ EL_3(M_{2^n}(R)) = EL_{3·2^n}(R) for all n.
2. **Engine (Theorem B).** EL_m(A), m = c·2^n, over a ring A generated by a finite set Σ, has generating sets T_n with γ(ℓ) ≤ exp(C ℓ^β) for ℓ ≤ c′·2^{n/2}, with C, β < 1 independent of n. This is the analog of Sauer–Schesler's main estimate for Aut(F_{4·2^n}):
   - truncated Grigorchuk generators as permutation matrices on X_n × [c];
   - a bounded number of transvections e_ij(σ), σ ∈ Σ ∪ {1};
   - commuting supports: e_ij(r) and e_kl(s) commute when j ≠ k and l ≠ i;
   - counting via the Bartholdi–Erschler inverted-orbit growth of the Grigorchuk group.
3. **Upper semicontinuity.** ω(G,S) = inf_ℓ γ_S(ℓ)^{1/ℓ} ≤ exp(C ℓ_n^{β−1}) → 1.
4. **Exponential growth.** G_X is infinite with (T), so non-amenable.
