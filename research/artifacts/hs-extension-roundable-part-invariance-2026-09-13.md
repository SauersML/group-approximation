# Compressor extension makes the roundable part of the vertex invariant

Lane `ex-hs-instability-extension`, 2026-09-13. Unreviewed.

Question of the lane: what does extension to the Kun--Thom actor buy *before* any rounding is assumed? One
unconditional answer is proved here. If `H ≥ Γ` is generated by elements that compress `Γ` into itself, then
the largest corner on which a model's vertex restriction rounds is an `H`-subrepresentation. So (H1) at
`H`-models is a zero-one property after an `H`-invariant split. Every wreath model of the full actor is
*totally* non-roundable: no nonzero `Γ`-invariant corner rounds.

## 0. Setting

- `Γ` is a finitely generated Kazhdan group with Kazhdan pair `(F_Γ, κ)`.
- `H` is a countable group containing `Γ` and generated by `P_Γ(H) = {g ∈ H : gΓg^(-1) ⊆ Γ}`.
- `M = prod_U M_n`, with `σ : H -> U(M)` a homomorphism and `R = σ(Γ)' ∩ M`.

**The actor qualifies.** For `Γ = EL_r(F_q[x_1..x_d]) < G = EL_r(F_q[x^(±1)]) ⋊ SL_d(Z)`, with `r >= 3`, `d >= 2`:
- **Compressors.** The elementary matrices `I + E_ij ∈ SL_d(Z)` substitute `x_j -> x_i x_j`, so they compress
  `Γ` into itself. They generate `SL_d(Z)`.
- **All of `G`.** Every `v ∈ Z^d` is `M w` for some `w ∈ N^d` and some `M ∈ SL_d(Z)` that changes signs, or is
  minus a transposition when the parity requires it. So the conjugates `M Γ M^(-1)` contain every `e_ij(x^v)`, and
  `G = <Γ, SL_d(Z)> = <P_Γ(G)>`.
- **Subgroups.** The same holds for `V_A = <Γ, A>` and for `<Γ, A, B>`, for positive compressors `A, B`.

## 1. Roundable corners

A projection `p ∈ R` with `τ(p) > 0` is *roundable* if the corner representation `γ -> pσ(γ)` of `Γ` in
`pMp = prod_U p_n M_n p_n` satisfies (H1). That is, there are genuine `π_n : Γ -> U(m'_n)`, with
`m'_n/rank(p_n) -> 1`, such that `||p_n σ_n(s) p_n ⊕ 1 - π_n(s)||_2 -> 0` along `U` for `s ∈ F_Γ`, in normalized
trace. `p` commutes with `σ(Γ)`, so `p_n σ_n(s) p_n` is `o_2(1)`-close to a unitary of `p_n`.

**Kazhdan corner lemma.** Let `π` be genuine on `C^m` and `P` a projection with
`max_s ||[P, π(s)]||_2 <= ε`. The projection of `P` onto `Fix(Ad π)` is a self-adjoint `X ∈ π(Γ)'` with
`||P - X||_2 <= ε/κ`. Its spectral projection `P_0 = 1_[1/2, ∞)(X) ∈ π(Γ)'` satisfies `||P - P_0||_2 <= 2ε/κ`.

**Lemma A.** Let `p, q ∈ R` be nonzero projections.
1. **Subprojections.** If `p` is roundable and `q <= p`, then `q` is roundable. Choose representatives `q_n <= p_n`.
   Then `||[q_n, π_n(s)]||_2 -> 0` relative to `τ(p)`. The corner lemma gives `P_0 ∈ π_n(Γ)'` close to `q_n`, and
   `π_n|P_0` is a genuine rounding of the `q`-corner. The ranks differ by `o(n)`, and the polar part of `P_0 q_n`
   identifies the two spaces.
2. **Equivalent projections.** If `p ~ q` in `R`, then `p` is roundable iff `q` is. Take `v ∈ R` with `v^*v = q`
   and `vv^* = p`. Conjugation by `v` carries the `q`-corner representation onto the `p`-corner representation,
   and it lifts to partial isometries at coordinates.
3. **Orthogonal sums.** If `p ⊥ q` are both roundable, so is `p + q`. Lift to orthogonal representatives. The
   off-diagonal blocks `q_n σ_n(s) p_n` tend to `0` in `||·||_2`, because `p ∈ R`. The direct sum of the two
   roundings rounds the sum.
4. **Increasing limits.** If `p_k ↑ p` and every `p_k` is roundable, then `p` is roundable. The corner `p - p_k`
   costs at most `3(τ(p - p_k)/τ(p))^(1/2)` when it is replaced by a trivial representation. Diagonalize `k`
   along `U`.

## 2. The maximal roundable projection is central in `R`

Let `𝒫` be the set of roundable projections together with `0`.
- **Joins.** If `p, q ∈ 𝒫`, then `p ∨ q - p ~ q - p ∧ q <= q` in `R` (Kaplansky). Items 1–3 give `p ∨ q ∈ 𝒫`.
- **Maximum.** `𝒫` is upward directed, so an increasing sequence attains `sup{τ(p) : p ∈ 𝒫}`. Its limit `p_max` is
  roundable by item 4 and dominates every element of `𝒫`.
- **Centrality.** For `u ∈ U(R)`, `u p_max u^* ~ p_max` is roundable by item 2, so it lies below `p_max`. Hence
  `p_max ∈ Z(R)`.

## 3. Compressor invariance

**Lemma B.** Let `g ∈ P_Γ(H)`, and put `θ_g(x) = σ(g)^* x σ(g)`.
- **`θ_g` maps `R` into `R`.** `x ∈ R` commutes with `σ(Γ) ⊇ σ(gΓg^(-1)) = σ(g)σ(Γ)σ(g)^*`.
- **`θ_g` preserves roundability.** The `θ_g(p)`-corner representation of `Γ` is the `p`-corner representation
  restricted to `gΓg^(-1)`, transported by `Ad g`. A rounding of the `p`-corner restricts to a rounding on the
  bounded words `gF_Γ g^(-1)`.
- **Conclusion.** `θ_g(p_max) <= p_max`, and `τ∘θ_g = τ`, so `θ_g(p_max) = p_max`. That is,
  `σ(g) p_max = p_max σ(g)`.

**Theorem.** `p_max ∈ σ(H)' ∩ Z(σ(Γ)' ∩ M)`. So `σ = σ_rnd ⊕ σ_nr` as `H`-representations:
- `σ_rnd|Γ` satisfies (H1);
- `σ_nr|Γ` is *totally non-roundable*: no nonzero projection of `σ_nr(Γ)' ∩ (1 - p_max)M(1 - p_max)` is roundable.

## 4. Traces of the summands

Suppose `σ` is trace-preserving, and every non-central conjugacy class of `H` is infinite, with a finite center
`Z`. For `p ∈ σ(H)' ∩ M`, the functional `τ_p = τ(p ·)/τ(p)` is a normal trace on `σ(H)'' ≅ L(H)`.
- **The form of `τ_p`.** Normal traces of `L(H)` have the form `τ_reg(· c)`, with `c` in the center of `L(H)`,
  which is `C[Z]`. So `τ_p(h) = 0` for every `h ∉ Z`.
- **Trivial center.** If `Z = 1`, both summands of the theorem are trace-preserving `H`-models.
- **The actor.** `G` is icc modulo its center, the scalars `ζ·1 ∈ EL_r(F_q)`. Conjugation by `e_ij(x^v)` and by
  substitutions moves every other element through infinitely many values. The center is trivial iff
  `gcd(r, q-1) = 1`, for example `q = 2`.

## 5. Corollaries

1. **Normal form for the counterexample side.** Take a trace-preserving `G`-model, with `gcd(r, q-1) = 1`. If (H1)
   fails at it, it has a nonzero summand that is a trace-preserving `G`-model and totally non-roundable. So a
   witness may be taken totally non-roundable.
2. **Wreath models are totally non-roundable.** Let `σ` be a trace-preserving model of the Kun--Thom wreath `W` over
   `G`, with `gcd(r, q-1) = 1`.
   - **`p_max` commutes with `σ(W)`.** It commutes with `σ(G)` by the theorem, and with the root lamp
     `e_Γ ∈ R` by centrality. Hence it commutes with every lamp `σ(g)e_Γσ(g)^*`.
   - **The corner is a wreath model.** `W` is icc modulo scalars as well, and those are trivial here. So
     `p_max σ p_max` is a trace-preserving `W`-model whose vertex rounds.
   - **So `p_max = 0`.** `kt-wreath-models-never-round-on-the-vertex` excludes such a corner.
   - This strengthens that theorem from "(H1) fails" to "no nonzero `Γ`-invariant corner rounds".
3. **One compressor.** The theorem applies to `V_A`. At the models of
   `one-compressor-wreath-has-models-with-exact-origin-vertex`, `p_max = 1`.
4. **What it does not do.** It produces no rounding, and it does not decide whether totally non-roundable
   `G`-models exist.
