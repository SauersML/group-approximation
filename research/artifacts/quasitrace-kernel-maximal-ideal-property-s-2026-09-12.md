# The trace kernel of Ozawa's SL(3,Z) block corona: unique maximal ideal and property (S)

Lane `quasitrace-k-stable`, swarm 7, 2026-09-12.

**Notation** (from `sl3z-ozawa-block-corona-has-unique-trace`):
- `Γ = SL(3,Z)` and `σ_k` is the permutation representation on `ℓ²(P²(F_{p_k})) ⊖ C1`;
- `B = C*(⊕_k σ_k(Γ))`, `I = ⊕_k M_{n_k}` with `n_k = p_k² + p_k`, and `A = B/I` with
  `ρ_∞ : Γ → A`;
- `τ_0` is the unique trace of `A`, `q : A → C*_r(Γ)` is its GNS map, and
  `K = N_{τ_0} = ker q ≠ 0`.

`A` is separable and is generated as a C*-algebra by `ρ_∞(Γ)`. By
`kazhdan-block-algebras-contain-their-block-ideal`, `A` has no finite-dimensional
irreducible representation.

## 0. Summary

1. **Prop 1.1.** `K` is the unique maximal ideal of `A`: every proper closed ideal of
   `A` lies in `K`. Its input is Boutonnet–Houdayer, Corollary D.
2. **Cor 1.2.** `K` is essential in `A` and has no nonzero unital quotient. So the
   unital-quotient obstruction to stability of `K` does not fire.
3. **Prop 2.1.** `K` admits no nonzero bounded 2-quasitrace iff `K` has
   Ortega–Perera–Rørdam property (S). Property (S) is therefore the exact intrinsic
   form of quasitrace invisibility of `K`, and it is implied by stability.
4. **Prop 2.3.** A one-translate stability criterion: if every `a ∈ K_+` has, for each
   `δ > 0`, a unitary `u ∈ A` with `‖a · uau*‖ < δ`, then `K` is stable.
5. **Open.** Whether `K` has property (S). The non-local, ghost-type part of `K` is
   where every attack stops (§3).

## 1. K is the unique maximal ideal

**Input (Boutonnet–Houdayer, arXiv:1908.07812v2, Corollary D; read from the PDF,
pages 1–5).**
- Let `G` be a connected semisimple Lie group with trivial centre and no compact
  factor, all of whose simple factors have real rank at least 2. Let `Γ < G` be an
  irreducible lattice.
- Let `π` be a weakly mixing unitary representation of `Γ`, meaning it has no nonzero
  finite-dimensional subrepresentation.
- Then `λ_Γ ≺ π`. Moreover `ker(Θ_{π,λ} : C*_π(Γ) → C*_λ(Γ))` is the unique proper
  maximal ideal of `C*_π(Γ)`, and `τ_Γ ∘ Θ_{π,λ}` is its unique tracial state.

This applies to `G = SL(3,R)`, which is simple with trivial centre and real rank 2,
and to its lattice `Γ = SL(3,Z)`, which is irreducible because `G` is simple.

**Proposition 1.1.** Every proper closed ideal `J` of `A` is contained in `K`. So `K`
is the unique maximal ideal of `A`.

*Proof.*
1. Since `J ≠ A`, choose a faithful nondegenerate representation `π` of `A/J` on a
   nonzero Hilbert space `H`. Put `π_Γ = π ∘ q_J ∘ ρ_∞`.
2. **`π_Γ` is weakly mixing.** Suppose `H_0 ⊂ H` is a nonzero finite-dimensional
   `π_Γ(Γ)`-invariant subspace. It is invariant under the C*-algebra generated by
   `π_Γ(Γ)`, which is `π(A/J)`, because `A` is generated by `ρ_∞(Γ)`. Compressing
   to `H_0` gives a nonzero finite-dimensional representation of `A`, and so a
   finite-dimensional irreducible one. That is excluded.
3. By Corollary D, `λ_Γ ≺ π_Γ`: `‖λ(x)‖ ≤ ‖π_Γ(x)‖` for every `x ∈ C[Γ]`.
4. `q` and `π ∘ q_J` are *-homomorphisms defined on `A` that send `ρ_∞(g)` to `λ_g`
   and to `π_Γ(g)` respectively. So `‖q(y)‖ ≤ ‖π(q_J(y))‖` for `y` in the dense
   *-subalgebra spanned by `ρ_∞(Γ)`, and by continuity for all `y ∈ A`.
5. Hence `J = ker(π ∘ q_J) ⊂ ker q = K`. ∎

Equivalently, `q` factors through every nonzero quotient of `A`. The same Corollary D,
part (i), gives uniqueness of the trace of `A` directly. That is an independent
check of item 2 of `sl3z-ozawa-block-corona-has-unique-trace`.

**Corollary 1.2.**
- (a) `K` is an essential ideal of `A`.
- (b) `K` has no nonzero unital quotient.
- (c) For every closed ideal `J` of `A`, `J + K ∈ {K, A}` forces `J ⊂ K` or `J = A`.
  So the would-be obstruction `K/(K ∩ J) ≅ A/J` unital, for a proper `J ⊄ K`, never
  occurs.

*Proof.*
- (a) If `J ∩ K = 0` for a closed ideal `J`, then `J ≠ A` because `K ≠ 0`. So
  `J ⊂ K` by Prop 1.1, and `J = J ∩ K = 0`.
- (b) Let `L ⊂ K` be a closed ideal with `K/L` unital, with unit `e`. `L` is an ideal
  of `A`. For `x ∈ A/L`, both `xe` and `ex` lie in `K/L`. So `xe = e(xe)` and
  `ex = (ex)e`, which gives `xe = exe = ex`, and `e` is a central projection of
  `A/L`.
  - The map `A → A/L → e(A/L) = K/L` is a surjective *-homomorphism. Its kernel `J`
    is a proper ideal if `K/L ≠ 0`.
  - By Prop 1.1, `J ⊂ K`.
  - The map is already onto when restricted to `K`, so `A = K + J = K`. This
    contradicts `A/K = C*_r(Γ) ≠ 0`.
- (c) is Prop 1.1 together with simplicity of `C*_r(Γ)`. ∎

A nonzero stable C*-algebra has no nonzero unital quotient. So (b) removes the only
elementary ideal-theoretic obstruction to `sl3z-ozawa-corona-trace-kernel-is-stable`.
