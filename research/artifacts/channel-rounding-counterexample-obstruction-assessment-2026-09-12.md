# Why a direct counterexample to bistochastic channel rounding is not in reach

Lane `channel-rounding-hunter`, 2026-09-12. Written for the stable-side lane
`nh-leavitt-hs-stable`, whose crux is the rounding of native EL_3-corner heat
maps of `L_(F_2)(1,2)^x`.

This assessment concerns the open claim `bistochastic-channel-rounding-fails`
(find self-adjoint bistochastic UCP `P_n` with `||P_n^2-P_n||_(inf->2)->0` yet
`inf_A ||P_n-E_A||_(inf->2) >= c`) and its positive dual
`bistochastic-near-idempotents-round-dimension-free`. It records why the
counterexample side is not settleable by a direct construction, and where the
one live route actually is.

## 1. What a counterexample would and would not settle

Main has one direction only. `leavitt-channel-rounding-forces-nonhyperlinearity`
is the established conditional theorem:

    dimension-free rounding holds  ==>  L_(F_2)(1,2)^x is nonhyperlinear.

The route `channel-rounding-fails-on-the-unstable-branch`, which derives
`bistochastic-channel-rounding-fails` from `leavitt-steinberg-hs-unstable`, is
the contrapositive of that implication. It is not its converse. So:

- a proof of rounding would prove `L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` nonhyperlinear;
- a family witnessing `bistochastic-channel-rounding-fails` would only refute
  this route to nonhyperlinearity. It would not prove `L_(F_2)(1,2)^x`
  hyperlinear, because the converse implication is not on main.

A counterexample is therefore cheaper than the full problem and still useful: it
closes a route. A proof of rounding remains the productive target for the stable
branch.

*Correction, 2026-09-12:* an earlier version of this section claimed the
two-sided equivalence "rounding <=> nonhyperlinear" and concluded that any
counterexample family solves the hyperlinearity question. That was wrong. Only
the forward implication is established. Sections 2–5 are unaffected.

## 2. The vanishing-perturbation route is closed in every dimension

The natural way to build a counterexample is a non-CP exact idempotent with a
non-algebra range, repaired to UCP by a perturbation vanishing in `inf->2`. This
is dead: `mub-sum-idempotents-have-uniform-cp-distance` shows that for two
mutually unbiased masas `D,C`, the idempotent `Q=E_D+E_C-E_0` has *exact*
`inf->2` distance `1/3` from every UCP map, with the optimum attained by
`(id+E_D+E_C)/3` (idempotence defect `2/9`). The lower bound needs only Kraus
mass orthogonal to `D+C`, so it holds with no common eigenbasis and no
self-adjointness. A counterexample cannot be a small UCP perturbation of a
sum-of-masas idempotent, in any dimension.

## 3. The three-state heat example fixes the shape of any counterexample

Section 5 of
`leavitt-kazhdan-heat-rounding-boundary-and-factorial-center-countermodel-2026-09-08`
gives a genuine self-adjoint bistochastic heat map `P_q` on `M_3` with
`||P_q^2-P_q||_(inf->2)->0`. Two direct constructions of the rounded algebra
both fail:

- the Hilbert-space spectral cutoff `1_[1/2,1](P_q)` is not positive (its slow
  eigenvector `(1,1,-2)` has three distinct coordinates, so it is no conditional
  expectation);
- the infinite-time limit `P_q^k -> E_(C1)` is scalar and stays `>= 2sqrt2/3`
  away in `inf->2`.

Yet `P_q` *does* round, to the coarse-graining algebra
`B={diag(a,a,b)}` generated by the identity and the near-fixed eigenvector.
Lesson: the correct algebra of a rounding is the `*`-algebra generated by the
near-fixed space, and neither the spectral cutoff nor the long-time limit
produces it. Any counterexample must defeat this generated-algebra construction,
not just the two failed ones.

## 4. The single remaining obstruction is uniform-in-word-length closure

Section 4 of the same artifact proves, for defect `epsilon` and `a_j=P(x_j)`
with `||x_j||<=1`,

    || P(a_1...a_k) - a_1...a_k ||_2  <=  (k-1) sqrt(2 epsilon) + k epsilon.   (4.5)

So the image of the operator unit ball is approximately closed under products of
each *fixed* length. Rounding is precisely the statement that this approximate
algebra is `inf->2`-close to a genuine subalgebra, i.e. that (4.5) can be made
uniform in `k`. Every proved positive class in the corpus (symmetric Markov,
Schur, identity-dominated, Weyl/projective-diagonal, bounded Kraus minorant, CP
reflections, near-fixed masa, supplied cb factorization, quantum-expander check
via `quantum-expander-relative-commutant-rounding`) is a class where this
uniformity is recovered by extra structure. Native multi-generator Kazhdan heat
has none of that structure: growing Kraus rank, vanishing purity, no near-fixed
masa.

## 5. The `inf->2` versus `2->2` gap is where the difficulty concentrates

Because the operator-norm ball sits inside the `2`-norm ball,
`||T||_(inf->2) <= ||T||_(2->2)`, so the `inf->2` idempotence defect is the
*weaker* hypothesis and the harder one; the conditional theorem deliberately
uses it. In the three-state example the defect was in fact controlled in
`2->2` (eqn 5.4), which is why self-adjoint spectral reasoning located `B`.
A genuine counterexample must instead keep the `2->2` idempotence defect from
vanishing, so that self-adjoint spectral theory cannot round it to the algebra
generated by its near-fixed space. Whether `||P_n^2-P_n||_(2->2)->0` already
forces rounding is itself open here: the near-fixed spectral projection need not
be positive (Section 3 above), so the reduction does not close, and this is the
sharpest sub-question a counterexample search should target. This assessment
does not open a graph node for it, to avoid adding an unverified claim to the
196-node rounding region; it is flagged here as the recommended sub-target.

## 6. Recommendation to the stable lane

- Do not spend the lane on a finite counterexample: by Section 1 that is the
  whole problem, and Sections 2-3 close the only elementary constructions.
- Spend it on the positive statement: upgrade (4.5) to a `k`-uniform bound for
  the native heat, using the specific structure of the EL_3-corner Laplacians
  (the near-fixed space is the joint kernel data of several corner generators).
- The one genuinely open reduction worth a helper is whether a persistent
  `2->2` idempotence defect is necessary for any counterexample; if it is, the
  counterexample search is confined to maps whose defect lives on the
  operator-norm-large, HS-large sector, matching item 1 of
  `bistochastic-channel-rounding-fails`.

No Cairn claim is established or refuted by this assessment; it is an orientation
document attached to the existing open claims.
