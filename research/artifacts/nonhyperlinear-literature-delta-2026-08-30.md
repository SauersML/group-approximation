# Nonhyperlinear-group literature delta — 2026-08-30

## Result

No primary source located in this sweep proves the existence of a
nonhyperlinear discrete group.  The recent literature still states the
problem as open, and every apparent shortcut below stops at a category,
trace, metric, or completeness mismatch already relevant to the repository.

This audit compared arXiv primary sources and version histories against the
repository with exact identifier searches.  It records papers which had no
repository hit before this artifact, plus the current published boundary
needed to interpret them.

## Current boundary, rechecked

| Source | Exact result | Why it does not close the root |
|---|---|---|
| [Dogon--Vigdorovich, arXiv:2506.20843v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.20843v2) | Theorem 1.3 makes a finite central extension nonhyperlinear **if** the relevant property-`(T;FD)` group is normalized-HS stable; for `SL_2(Z[1/p])` this is reduced to the Iwahori gluing question stated in the introduction. | Stability / Iwahori gluing remains open.  This is already represented by the arithmetic stability and commutant-transfer lanes. |
| [Paddock--Slofstra, arXiv:2310.07901v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.07901v2) | Proposition 5.8 identifies existence of a finitely presented nonhyperlinear group with existence of an LCS that is tracially satisfiable but not `R^U`-satisfiable. | It is an equivalence, not construction of such an LCS. |
| [Taller--Vidick, arXiv:2507.22444v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.22444v2) | RE-hardness for LCS games has completeness `1-epsilon`; the paper isolates perfect completeness as the step that would produce a nonhyperlinear group. | Positive noise is exactly the missing groupification seam. |
| [Manzoor, arXiv:2502.06697v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06697v2) | Theorems 1.1--1.2 give a non-CE equivalence-relation factor and a non-co-hyperlinear IRS on a free group. | The IRS need not be a point mass at a normal subgroup, just as the paper explicitly warns in its sofic analogue.  It therefore supplies no discrete quotient with non-CE canonical trace. |

These checks also rule out two recurrent misreadings: MIP*=RE supplies a
non-CE tracial algebra, not automatically a group factor, and the 2026
nonsofic constructions do not imply nonhyperlinearity because soficity is a
stronger approximation demand than hyperlinearity.

## Relevant primary sources absent from the repository

### Gao--Ioana--Vigdorovich, arXiv:2605.02242v1

[Characters of surface groups](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02242v1) proves
that the trace simplex of a free or closed surface group is Poulsen and notes
explicitly (Introduction, after Corollary 1.2) that some traces on these
groups are not pointwise limits of finite-dimensional traces, by the
refutation of Connes embedding.

This is a particularly clean 2026 citation for the **canonical-trace
firewall**.  Free and surface groups are residually finite, hence sofic and
hyperlinear, while their full group C-star algebras carry non-CE traces.
Therefore neither a non-CE group trace nor a non-CE GNS factor generated by
group unitaries implies that the underlying discrete group is
nonhyperlinear.  Only the canonical delta trace decides group
hyperlinearity.  The result corroborates
`non-ce-trace-does-not-promote-to-subgroup-character-gap` and
`non-ce-trace-on-sofic-racg`; it does not strengthen their group-theoretic
conclusions.

### De Bondt--Thom, arXiv:2607.19556v1

[Trace-norm rigidity for reduced products of unitary groups and matrix
algebras](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19556v1) works in exactly the normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt metric.  It proves compact-group Ulam stability, rigidity of
product-form isomorphisms, and coordinate recognition for **isomorphisms of
whole reduced products**; the strongest full-automorphism classification
also uses additional set-theoretic axioms.

This is adjacent to `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs` but does not prove it.
The Kun--Thom gate concerns an arbitrary embedded subgroup `sigma(Gamma)`
and whether its relative commutant is carried onto itself by one compressor.
Whole-ambient isomorphism rigidity neither identifies
`sigma(Gamma)'` coordinatewise nor upgrades the automatic one-sided
commutant inclusion to equality.  The paper is evidence that coordinates are
recoverable from the entire reduced product, not a solution of the embedded
relative-commutant problem.

### Brannan--Matsuda--Séguin, arXiv:2608.26362v1

[Representation stability for compact and discrete quantum
groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26362) appeared on 2026-08-26.  Its stated
main theorem gives Ulam representation stability for compact and amenable
discrete quantum groups, together with a unitary-compression result for
amenable locally compact quantum groups.

The theorem does not assert flexible normalized-HS stability for any of the
nonamenable discrete property-`(T)` candidates in this repository, nor does
it address canonical-trace separation.  It therefore does not discharge the
Dogon--Vigdorovich stability hypothesis.

## Game-algebra robustness papers absent from the repository

Three nearby papers improve robustness or self-testing, but none creates the
perfect tracial/non-`R^U` LCS required by Paddock--Slofstra:

- [Zhao, arXiv:2411.03259v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03259) characterizes
  robust self-testing for robust game algebras by uniqueness of amenable
  tracial states.
- [Kar, arXiv:2503.23500v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23500) gives the
  synchronous-game version and related robust self-testing consequences.
- [Vernooij--Zhao, arXiv:2505.05994v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05994)
  lifts PME-restricted robust self-tests to general strategies for perfect
  synchronous games.

All three start with an optimal or perfect target and analyze rigidity near
it.  They do not convert Taller--Vidick completeness `1-epsilon` into
perfect completeness and do not supply a tracially satisfiable,
non-`R^U`-satisfiable LCS.

[Fima--Mandal--Patri, arXiv:2411.19199v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19199)
studies universal quantum-homomorphism C-star and von Neumann algebras,
including Connes embeddability questions.  No theorem there identifies one
of its non-CE algebras with a discrete group von Neumann algebra carrying the
canonical trace, so it does not bypass the same groupification firewall.

## Repository consequences

1. Keep the root `non-hyperlinear-group` open.
2. Record De Bondt--Thom as an ambient-coordinate theorem, not as a
   relative-commutant lift.
3. Use Gao--Ioana--Vigdorovich as a modern, finitely generated
   canonical-trace firewall: arbitrary trace pathology on a hyperlinear group
   is expected and is not evidence for a nonhyperlinear canonical trace.
4. The two sharp published positive routes remain the Iwahori/HS-stability
   program and the perfect-completeness LCS program.  Manzoor's IRS result is
   a third boundary object, but it still needs a point-mass or algebraic-Haar
   groupification theorem.
