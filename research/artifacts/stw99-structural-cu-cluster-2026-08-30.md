## Wiring and reductions recorded

* **LXV ⟸ LXXIII (load-bearing)**: Jiang--Su embeddings reduce
  through dimension-drop semiprojectivity to scale-wise
  `(n,n+1)`-divisibility, which the Global Glimm Property supplies;
  the residue is coherence across `n`.  The expected characterisation
  (Dadarlat--Toms: no finite-dimensional representations) is exactly
  the Global Glimm hypothesis.
* **LXXIII = the LXXII recon minus the wpi crutch**: by
  Thiel--Vilalta 7.1, Global Glimm ⟺ nowhere scattered +
  ideal-filtered + (V); this lane's earlier weakly-purely-infinite
  recon isolated the join-fitting defect (`d'_2 ≤ ∞d''_1`) and one
  hereditary-positioning statement as the only gaps — the same two
  gaps are the whole Global Glimm problem, now without
  `∞`-absorption.  Counterexamples to LXXIII and to LX
  (separativity) both require realizing a bad refinement monoid as a
  C*-Cuntz semigroup: one breakthrough, two negative solutions;
  conversely join-fitting progress closes LXXIII's two-generator
  case.
* **LXXV decomposes** into LXXVI (pure ⟹ Cu-regular, peer-covered) +
  nonsimple-Winter (Cu-regular ⟹ Z-stable: needs ideal-lattice ×
  trace-space glueing, the session's recurring frontier in its most
  general form) + LXXXII (dimnuc of extensions, peer-covered); the
  candidate counterexample shape for Z-stable ⟹ finite dimnuc is an
  infinite ideal chain with expensive extensions.
* **LXVII (one-ideal classification)** = relative Conjecture D:
  Busby class in ideal-equivariant KK + trace-pasting datum;
  uniqueness available (Gabe--Ruiz + Szabo KK-uniqueness), existence
  is the open half — the same existence-vs-uniqueness asymmetry as
  LVII and LIII.
* **LXVIII/LXIX** form uniqueness/existence halves of Robert's
  W-stabilization program; the ideal-level pivot for LXVIII is
  whether a nonzero nuclear W-stable algebra can be traceless
  (expected impossible via Gabe traceless ⟹ O_∞-stable vs finiteness
  of W); LXIX reduces under LXVIII to a cone-range lemma, open only
  over non-Hausdorff primitive spectra (LXXI's territory).
* **LXIV (Cu of sr1 Villadsen--Toms)**: with sr1, ranks all occur, so
  the computation is `V(A)` = direct limit of Hopf-summand monoids
  under duplication, whose order the XXVII lane's mod-`ell` Chern
  criterion already decides; the Gardella--Perera two-morphisms
  question localizes beyond the soft-divider rounding window
  `rank ≥ n(n+1)`.
* **LXX (finite-vertex graph algebras)**: classification is done
  (ERRS), so the characterisation is RR0 + finite ideal lattice with
  AF/Kirchberg gauge subquotients + an arithmetic filtered-K
  realization statement (Smith normal form with positivity over the
  ideal poset) — flagged as closable with existing technology.
