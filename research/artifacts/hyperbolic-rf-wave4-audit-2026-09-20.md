## 1. The exact module of the canonical retraction kernel

Write Q=PSL2(31), and K=ker(H31->Q) for the retraction killing c.
The Q vertex splits this retraction. K is normally generated by
c, whose order is five. Hence K_ab is an F5[Q]-module generated
by v=[c]. The relator [a,c] says av=v. With the convention
[u,v]=u^-1 v^-1 u v, [b,c,b] gives
`-(b^-1-1)^2 v=0`, equivalent to `(b-1)^2v=0`.
The other two b,c commutators contain at least two c entries and
vanish automatically once K is abelian. All remaining relations
are in Q or say c^5=1.

Conversely, let V be the F5[Q]-module generated by v subject to
those two equations. Sending a,b to Q and c to v in V semidirect
Q satisfies every H31 relator. Its image is the whole semidirect
product because the Q-orbit of v generates V. Thus K_ab is
exactly V, not merely a quotient of an auxiliary module.

After imposing av=v, the module is the permutation module on
Q/<a>. Its 2976 basis elements are the left cosets q<a>, with
Q acting on the left. For every q in Q the remaining relation is

```
[q<a>] - 2[q b<a>] + [q b^2<a>] = 0.
```

These are 14880 rows with at most three nonzero entries.
