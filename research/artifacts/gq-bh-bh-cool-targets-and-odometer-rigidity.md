## 2. Candidates judged out of reach in one attempt

- **One f.p. simple group containing every BS(m,n) at once.**
  - Affine and odometer hosts see only finitely many primes (`affine-2v-hosts-reach-z-1-2n-by-doubling-but-not-q`).
    §3 adds that odometer generators cannot even carry `BS(2,3)` unless the odometer is sensitive to both 2 and 3.
  - Tree methods (BLIW) are per group. A f.g. GBS host has a f.g. modulus image, so it cannot contain all
    `BS(m,n)`.
- **H_3(Z) in some nV** (`heisenberg-group-embeds-in-some-brin-thompson-group`). This is an active program with
  landed obstructions for orbit growth up to `r^3`. The first open case is `W(Z^4)` or product-shift full groups.
  Not duplicated here.
- **BS(1,3) in 2V (removing the free coordinate from `bs13-embeds-in-brin-thompson-3v`).** The construction needs
  `s ≅ s ⊔ s ⊔ s` as systems. A minimal `s` (SMART's first-return map) cannot give that inside `C x C` without an
  extra product factor. No brick-local folding of the third coordinate into the tape was found.

## 3. Landed: odometer rigidity for Baumslag-Solitar relations

`odometer-generators-carry-no-nonsolvable-baumslag-solitar` (+ `-proof`, elementary lane proof). Take `a ≅ (+1) x id`
on `K x Y`, with `K` compact monothetic, and suppose `t a^m t^-1 = a^n`.
- If `nK = K`, then `t a t^-1` commutes with `a`.
- If `mK = K`, then `t^-1 a t` commutes with `a`.
- So for `|m|, |n| >= 2`, the map from `BS(m,n)` is not injective unless both `m` and `n` are non-invertible on `K`.

The key step is that the centralizer of the minimal rotation power `a^n = (+n) x id` consists of fibrewise
translations `(x, y) -> (x + delta(y), h(y))`, and these commute with `a`.

**How it was found.** In the affine host `2V_{BS(1,3)}` (generated by `2V`, `x -> x+1` and `x -> 3x` on the first
coordinate `Z_2`), `t = mu rho` satisfies `t a^2 t^-1 = a^3`. Here `a = x+1`, `rho` is the baker map and `mu = x -> 3x`,
using `rho a^2 rho^-1 = a` and `mu a mu^-1 = a^3`. Every twist `t = mu rho c` with `c` in the centralizer of `a^2`
also works. By the lemma, `t a t^-1` always commutes with `a`. Through the non-Hopfian map `a -> a^2` of `BS(2,3)`,
the kernel then contains `ker(phi^k)` for every `k` (remark in the route). So none of these hosts gives an explicit
faithful `BS(2,3)` through `x + 1`.

**What it tells a construction.** An explicit Thompson-like host for `BS(2,3)` needs a generator that is not a
2-adic odometer, e.g. a `Z_6` or `Z-hat` odometer together with the renormalizations dividing by 2 and by 3. Or it
needs a generator with non-equicontinuous (tree-boundary) dynamics, as in BLIW.

## 4. Honest verdict

No new "striking" theorem was found within one attempt. Every candidate from the directive was either already known
(§1) or blocked (§2). The landed lemma is a modest, checkable obstruction. Its use is to steer explicit-host
constructions away from odometer generators for non-solvable Baumslag-Solitar groups. Priority: not claimed.
