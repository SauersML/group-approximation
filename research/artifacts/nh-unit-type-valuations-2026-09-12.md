## 1. Reduction

Let `π_n` be (H1) roundings, each factoring through `St_r(A_u × A_nu)`.
- **Projection bound.** `nonunit-projection-bounded-by-root-spectral-mass` gives
  `τ(1 − P_n) <= r(r−1)·μ_n(χ_nu ≠ 1) = r(r−1)·[μ_n(depth_nu = 1) + μ_n(depth_nu > 1)]`.
- **Shallow term.** The shallow claim gives `μ_n(depth_nu = 1) <= q·η_n + η_n^t`.
- **Deep term.** The deep term is the open claim.
- **Conclusion.** Once `τ(1 − P_n) -> 0`, `π_n P_n ⊕ 1_(1−P_n)` is a unit-type rounding. It moves each generator by at most

## 2. Additive torus shells

The shallow claim gives an intertwiner for `t = diag(x_1, x_1, x_1^(-2), 1, ...)` on the block `S = EL_2(R_+) ⋉ R_+^2`.
It yields `|μ_n^(13)(E) − μ_n^(13)(D_t^(-1)E)| <= η_n^t` for every Borel `E`, where `D_tχ = χ(x_1^3 ·)`.

**Nilpotency index.** For a character `χ` of finite type, let `ν(χ)` be the nilpotency index of `x_1` in the origin factor
of `R_+/I_χ`, where `I_χ` is the largest ideal in `ker χ`.
- `I_(D_tχ) = (I_χ : x_1^3)`.
- The local factors of `R_+/(I : x)` are the quotients `A_m/Ann_(A_m)(x)`.
- So `ν(D_tχ) = max(ν(χ) − 3, 0)`, and `D_t^(-1){ν >= k} = {ν >= k+3}` for `k >= 1`.

Hence `μ_n{k <= ν <= k+2} <= η_n^t` for every `k >= 1`. These shells are additive, of fixed width `3`.

**Depth-one bound.** A constituent with origin factor `F_q` is `ρ_u ⊗ ρ_0`, with `ρ_0` a nontrivial irreducible of
`St_r(F_q)`. A proportion `>= c_(q,r) > 0` of its root spectrum has `ν = 1`. So depth-one origin mass is at most
`η_n^t/c_(q,r)`, which is the shallow claim again, for mixed types too.

## 3. Why compressors and torus do not close the deep regime

**Drift.** Both exact transports act on depth by drift:
- the compressor: `depth_nu -> ⌈depth_nu/d⌉`;
- the torus: `ν -> ν − 3`.

The maps `k -> k/d` and `k -> k − 3` generate a solvable affine drift, which is amenable.
- Take `G(k) = 1 − log k/log D` on `[1, D]`, the log-uniform profile.
- The compressor test costs `G(k) − G(dk) = log d/log D`.
- The torus test costs `G(k) − G(k+3) <= log 4/log D`.
- Once `log D >> 1/ε`, every finite set of these tests passes with defect `<= ε`.
- `η_n` and `η_n^t` have no rate along `U`, and `j` iterations cost `j·η_n`.

So no finite combination of the two lemmas bounds regime (i) uniformly. No representation-theoretic model realizing such
a profile was built. The profile only passes the tests.

**Where a rate-free bound could come from.** Transports acting on depth profiles through a non-amenable group, such as
`SL_d(Z)` on exponent directions, would give one (property (T) of `SL_d(Z)`, `d >= 3`). The obstacle:
- Only nonnegative `A` map `Γ` into `Γ`.
- A general `A` maps into `Γ` only the root subgroups over a cone subring `R_C = F_q[x^C]` with `AC ⊆ N^d`. `R_C` is finitely
  generated by Gordan's lemma, so relative (T) of `EL_2(R_C) ⋉ R_C^2` does exactify `σ(A)` there.
- Restriction to `R_C` relates annihilator monomial sets only by containment, `Mon_C(χ|R_C) ⊇ Mon(χ) ∩ C`. The action on
  shapes does not descend to the spectra.
- The projective-staircase argument fails for the same reason. Two positive compressors with different Perron vectors have
  no common invariant shape measure, but constituents inherit only `J_κ ⊇ φ_A^(-1) J_ρ`.

## 4. Depth-one compressor rigidity

Let `R_+/J_ρ = A_u × F_q`, let `A` be nonnegative, and put `θ = ψ∘φ_A`.
- `θ` maps onto `A_u` (the unit trick, item 2 of `unit-type-vertex-rounding-forces-compressor-commutant-rigidity`) and onto
  `F_q` (`ev_0∘φ_A = ev_0`).
- By Goursat, the image is either the product or the graph of a homomorphism `A_u -> F_q`. The graph is excluded because
  `θ(x_1) = (unit, 0)`.
- So `ρ∘Ad A` is irreducible, of depth one, with the same image.

Compressor matching therefore only permutes depth-one types, and the torus is needed there. This matches the
constant-term invariance remark of the shallow claim.
