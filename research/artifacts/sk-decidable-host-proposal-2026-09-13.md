### P1, replace l.282–293
Current:
```latex
Subgroups of LEF groups are LEF. Conversely, let $\Gamma$ be LEF and
generated by a finite set $S$, with finite models
$\varphi_n\colon B_n\to Q_n$ on word balls. Then
$\gamma\mapsto(\varphi_n(\gamma))_n$ embeds $\Gamma$ in the algebraic
ultraproduct $\prod_\omega Q_n$, and the doubled regular actions embed
this group in $P=\prod_\omega\operatorname{Sym}(Q_n\times\{1,2\})$ by even
permutations. Every even permutation of a finite set is a commutator of
two permutations~\cite[Theorem~1]{Ore}, so each $s\in S$ is a commutator
of two elements of $P$. Let $\Delta_0\le P$ be generated by $S$ and these
elements, and put $\Delta=\Delta_0\times\Z$. A countable subgroup of an
algebraic ultraproduct of finite groups is LEF, so $\Delta$ is an
infinite finitely generated LEF group with $\Gamma\le[\Delta,\Delta]$.
```
Replacement:
```latex
Subgroups of LEF groups are LEF. Conversely, let $\Gamma$ be LEF and
generated by a finite set $S$. On $\Gamma\times\Z$ let $\sigma$ raise the
level by one, and let $h_s$ multiply the first coordinate by $s$ on the
levels $m\ge0$. Then $[\sigma,h_s]$ multiplies level~$0$ by $s^{-1}$ and
fixes the other levels, so the infinite group
$\Delta=\langle\sigma,h_s:s\in S\rangle$ satisfies
$\Gamma\le[\Delta,\Delta]$. Every element of $\Delta$ is $f\sigma^k$, where
$f\colon\Z\to\Gamma$ acts levelwise, is trivial on low levels and constant
on high levels, and $\sigma f\sigma^{-1}$ is $f$ moved up one level. Given
$r$, let $\psi\colon B_{2r}\to Q$ be a finite model of $\Gamma$, put $M=3r$
and $H=Q^{\{-M,\dots,M\}}$, and let $\theta$ be the isomorphism from
$\{v:v_{M-1}=v_M\}$ onto $\{v:v_{-M}=1\}$ that moves each coordinate up by
one. Transversals give a bijection $t$ of $H$ with $t(ax)=\theta(a)t(x)$,
so $\tau(x,i)=(t(x),i+1)$ on $H\times\Z/5r$ conjugates left multiplication
by $a$ to left multiplication by $\theta(a)$. Then
$f\sigma^k\mapsto(\psi(f(m)))_{|m|\le M}\,\tau^k$ is injective and
multiplicative on the ball of radius $r$ of $\Delta$, so $\Delta$ is LEF.
```
Drop `\bibitem{Ore}` if nothing else cites it.

### P3, insert after l.345 (before `\end{proof}`)
```latex
Finitely generated subgroups of groups with solvable word problem have
solvable word problem. If $\Gamma$ has one, so has $\Delta$, since a word
determines the levels where $f$ changes and the values of $f$ there. A word
in the generators of $G$ multiplies out to a matrix with entries
$\sum_\ell f_\ell u_\ell$, where $\ell$ is given by an element of $\Delta$
and a finite set of lamps, and $f_\ell$ depends on finitely many
coordinates $x(\delta)$. Every pattern occurs in $Z$, so the word problem
of $\Delta$ decides whether these entries vanish.
```
