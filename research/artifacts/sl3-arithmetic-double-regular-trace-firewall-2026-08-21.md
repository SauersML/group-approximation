# SL3 arithmetic double: regular-trace firewall and action countermodel

Let

```text
C=SL_3(Z),     A=SL_3(Z[1/2]),     D=A *_C A,
w=i_0(h)i_1(h)^(-1),     h=diag(2,1,1/2).
```

The exact finite-dimensional fold theorem says every finite-dimensional
unitary representation of `D` kills the whole fold kernel.  This note tests
whether the extra data in the canonical regular character can promote that
theorem to matrix microstates.

## 1. The exact regular factor is maximally nonfolded

Write

```text
B=L(C),       P_j=L(i_j(A)),       M=L(D)=P_0 *_B P_1.
```

Since `h` is not in `C`, the word `w` is Bass--Serre reduced.  Therefore

```text
tau_M(w)=0,
||u_(i_0(h))-u_(i_1(h))||_2^2
 =2-2 Re tau_M(i_0(h)^(-1)i_1(h))=2.                     (1)
```

Thus the canonical trace does not merely permit a nonfolded pair; it makes
the two copies maximally separated in `L^2` at the marked generator.

Represent `A` on `L^2(M) direct_sum L^2(M)` by

```text
rho(a)=diag(lambda(i_0(a)),lambda(i_1(a)))
```

and let `V(x,y)=(y,x)`.  For `c in C`, the two group elements `i_0(c)` and
`i_1(c)` are equal in `D`, so

```text
[V,rho(c)]=0.                                             (2)
```

For `h`, (1) says that `V` is not an intertwiner.  The exact block swap used
by the finite-dimensional fold proof therefore survives in the regular
factor as a `C`-intertwiner with no `A`-intertwining conclusion.

## 2. Why the named rigidity mechanisms stop

Property (T) acts on the conjugation representation by projecting an almost
`C`-invariant vector to the `C`-invariant subspace.  The vector `V` in (2) is
already exactly invariant, so its projection is itself.  Property (T) has no
mechanism that turns a `C`-fixed vector into an `A`-fixed vector.  That final
step in exact matrices was finite-dimensional co-density, not property (T).

Relative amenability also points the wrong way.  The subgroup `C` has
infinite index in the property-(T) group `A`.  If `C` were coamenable, the
quasi-regular representation on `ell^2(A/C)` would weakly contain the
trivial representation.  Property (T) would then produce an invariant
vector, impossible on an infinite transitive set.  Hence `C` is not
coamenable in `A`.  Monod--Popa's group/von-Neumann coamenability
correspondence gives the same conclusion for `B subset P_j`.

Finally, Bass--Serre/Popa deformation data preserve the two vertex positions
in the literal amalgamated free product `P_0 *_B P_1`; they do not identify
them.  Any assertion based only on the internal tracial inclusion data would
apply to `M` itself and contradict (1).  The desired collapse must use the
extra statement that `M` embeds into a tracial **matrix** ultraproduct.

This is an exact scope firewall, not a proof that such an embedding exists.

## 3. Conditional explicit CE/sofic countermodel

Let `X=A/C`.  There is an exact group isomorphism

```text
<A,t | [t,C]=1> = ( *_(x in X) Z_x ) rtimes A,            (3)
```

where the free generator at `aC` is `ata^(-1)`.  If the action
`A curvearrowright X` is sofic, Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell's
generalized-wreath theorem makes (3) sofic.  Its subgroup generated by `A`
and `tAt^(-1)` is the adjacent-vertex double `D`, so `D` is sofic as well.

Canonical permutation microstates for `D` then satisfy every fixed
nonidentity trace condition.  Since `w` is nontrivial by amalgam normal form,

```text
tr(sigma_n(w))->0,
||sigma_n(w)-I||_2^2 ->2.                                 (4)
```

Equation (4) is the requested explicit microstate countermodel conditional
on one sharply isolated dynamical input.

## 4. The dynamical input is the 2-adic building action

Inside `SL_3(Q_2)`,

```text
C=A intersect SL_3(Z_2).
```

Dyadic rationals are dense in `Q_2`, and elementary generation gives density
of `A` in `SL_3(Q_2)`.  Since `SL_3(Z_2)` is open, every coset meets `A`, so

```text
A/C = SL_3(Q_2)/SL_3(Z_2),                                (5)
```

the type-zero vertex orbit in the affine Bruhat--Tits building.

The known positive criteria do not decide soficity of (5): the stabilizer
`SL_3(Z)` is neither amenable nor locally finite.  Congruence co-density also
makes every ordinary finite-quotient coset action collapse to a point.  Thus
regular double-fold collapse would prove a new nonsoficity theorem for this
explicit higher-rank building action; a positive sofic model for the action
would decisively refute the collapse.

## 5. Exact endpoint

Regular trace, Bass--Serre orthogonality, property (T), and relative
nonamenability do not prove the fold.  A necessary dynamical consequence is a
finite-coordinate theorem:

```text
the 2-adic vertex action is nonsofic,
```

or a still stronger obstruction directly excluding a tracial matrix
embedding of `L(D)`.  No internal von Neumann algebra rigidity argument alone
can distinguish those two outcomes.
