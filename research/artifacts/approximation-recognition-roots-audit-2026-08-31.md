# Outcome

No root in this lane was promoted.  The following remain open under Cairn's
stated conventions:

* weak norm-corona MF implies hyperlinear;
* `Pi^0_2`-completeness of amenability on finite presentations;
* `Pi^0_2`-completeness of soficity on finite presentations;
* a Property-T-free torsion-free finitely presented non-MF seed, including
  the stronger acylindrically hyperbolic marked-radical seed.

The pass produced the unconditional fragment
`mf-characterless-corona-image-has-hyperlinear-quotient` and corrected the
MF frontier after the refutation of invariant-corner visibility.

# MF versus hyperlinear

The surviving general mechanism is
`mf-hs-defect-gap-profile`: select operator-norm models for which the square
of the actual normalized-HS multiplication defect is negligible relative to
the marked trace gap.  The formerly advertised second mechanism,
`mf-invariant-corner-visibility`, is refuted by the integer compression-lamp
group and its marked commutator.

The refuter does not decide the root.  It proves that this word is invisible
in normalized HS norm in every OPERATOR-NORM almost representation.  A
hyperlinear model may be genuinely HS-only.  In particular, one must not use
the false permanence principle "amenable normal subgroup plus sofic quotient
implies sofic": Kun--Thom's 2026 nonsofic generalized wreath products are
counterexamples to precisely that direction of extension permanence.

The new characterless-image lemma supplies a different, exact fragment.  A
norm-corona embedding always maps to a matrix tracial ultraproduct.  If the
resulting group map were trivial, its coordinate ultratrace would be a
character of the generated corona algebra extending augmentation.  Therefore
failure of augmentation to extend gives a nontrivial hyperlinear quotient;
for a nonabelian simple group it is faithful.  This does not remove the root
gap because weak MF allows augmentation to factor through the image.

Primary-source convention check:

* Korchagin, arXiv:1704.06906v3, uses the weak norm-corona definition and
  explicitly notes that normalized-HS faithfulness can be lost.
* Schafhauser, arXiv:2306.02498v2, builds regular-trace control into his
  stronger group-MF definition; his implication to hyperlinearity cannot be
  imported into the weak convention.
* Shulman, arXiv:2603.13564v2, proves new weak-MF amalgam permanence but does
  not select the regular trace.
* Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Manzoor--Patchell,
  arXiv:2603.24502v5, use strong convergence to the left regular
  representation in their MF/PMF/PFF terminology, again stronger than the
  root convention.
* Bachner--Dogon--Lubotzky, arXiv:2508.17392v3, give the finite-normal-kernel
  fragments already represented in Cairn, not a general weak-MF converter.
* Alekseev--Thom, arXiv:2608.05362v1, concern centralizers of SOFIC
  approximations of Kazhdan groups and do not produce trace-visible unitary
  models for arbitrary weak-MF groups.

# Recognition roots

For finite presentations the unconditional placements remain

```text
AMENABLE_fp in Pi^0_2 \\ Pi^0_1,
SOFIC_fp     in Pi^0_2 \\ Pi^0_1.
```

The exact classifications on two-generator recursive presentations do not
compile to finite presentations for free.

For amenability, the positive branch of the existing MF-safe rope contains
free subgroups.  Finite packets of equations, one-edge HNN switches, finite
graphs of amenable groups, and raw finite L-presentation simulators have only
recursive or `Sigma^0_1` amenable loci.  No primary source located in the
2026 sweep supplies an amenability-preserving Higman compiler.

For soficity, the fixed positive rope remains an amalgam/HNN construction
over a nonamenable, nonseparable Mikhailova-type edge.  Shulman's operator-MF
theorem and the strong regular MF results above are not permutation-model
theorems.  Alekseev--Thom's centralizer theorem assumes a sofic embedding and
therefore cannot create one for the rope.  Thus the sofic-safe compiler stays
open.

# Torsion-free Property-T-free seed

Higman's four-cycle remains the narrow explicit candidate.  Exact
finite-dimensional sterility is insufficient: the missing statement is the
dimension-uniform operator-norm collapse of approximate four-cycle tuples.
Eilers--Shulman--Sorensen, arXiv:1808.06793v4, and Dadarlat,
arXiv:2007.12655v2, leave operator-norm matricial stability of the relevant
`BS(1,m)`, `m>1`, seams open.  The 2026 literature sweep found no theorem
closing that gap.  The Baumslag--Gersten one-seam candidate remains excluded
by Cairn's explicit primitive-shell operator-norm microstates.

# Exact blockers

1. MF/hyperlinear: construct the intrinsic HS-defect/trace-gap profile, or a
   genuinely HS-only model for every MF-visible word.
2. Amenability recognition: a finite-output `Pi^0_2` switch with amenable
   total branch and a free subgroup on failure, outside the recorded
   Bass--Serre/L-presentation no-go classes.
3. Sofic recognition: prove the fixed Mikhailova positive rope sofic, using
   local permutation models not arising from finite quotients.
4. Torsion-free seed: prove Higman's four-cycle operator-norm collapse, or
   construct a different torsion-free finitely presented MF-radical word
   without Kazhdan input.
