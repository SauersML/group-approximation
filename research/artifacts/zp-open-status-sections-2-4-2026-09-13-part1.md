# Open status of Zaremsky's problems, Sections 2–4, part 1: Thompson groups (2026-09-13)

Source list: M. C. B. Zaremsky, *Some open problems*, version of July 12, 2026
(https://zaremsky.github.io/open_problems.pdf, SHA-256 `d14c2ce7…30cde50`). Hub claim:
`zaremsky-open-problems-2026-07-12`. Part 2 covers Sections 3 and 4.

## How bounded this sweep is

- Tools: web search (US index) and arXiv abstract or HTML pages, fetched 2026-09-13.
  MathSciNet and zbMATH were not reached. Most items got one to three searches.
- "Nothing found" means only that those searches returned nothing relevant.
  It is not a literature proof of openness.
- Rows marked *(search summary)* rest on a search engine's summary, not on the source text.
  Check the source before a `-citation` route relies on them.
- Every "claimed answer" is an unrefereed preprint. The owner lane must verify it before any
  answer route is established.

| 2.16 Lehnert's conjecture | OPEN, advanced | **New after the list date:** Bodart–D'Angeli–Perego–Rodaro, arXiv:2608.02111 (3 Aug 2026). A f.g. group embeds in V iff it admits a faithful context-free action (class CF-TR). Every f.g. subgroup of V is virtually abelian or contains a free non-abelian semigroup, so groups of intermediate growth, the Basilica group and the Hanoi Towers group do not embed. The abstract says all known coCF groups embed in V. H. Jaspars, arXiv:2608.01168 (v2 17 Aug 2026): context-free subgroups and a hardest context-free membership problem. Earlier: Bleak–Matucci–Neunhöffer, arXiv:1312.1855. Nothing found on cloning systems T(G_*). |
| 2.18 F cobounded action on the hyperbolic plane | LITERAL READING: short NO (unreviewed); intended reading unclear | A non-injective F → Isom(H²) has abelian image, because nontrivial normal subgroups of F contain [F,F] (Cannon–Floyd–Parry, Thm 4.3). No abelian group of isometries of H² is cobounded. A faithful action puts a finite-index subgroup of F in PSL₂(R), which the Tits alternative excludes: F has no free subgroups and is not virtually solvable. The item was added 6/19/24, one day after Balasubramanya–Fournier-Facio–Zaremsky, arXiv:2406.12982 (v2 14 Apr 2026), mapped the poset of cobounded hyperbolic actions of F. Its v2 HTML never mentions the hyperbolic plane. The intended reading may be a space quasi-isometric to H². Sent to z2-18-f-h2-action. |
| 2.19 embeddings into nV | PARTLY ANSWERED | List note: some hyperbolic (T) group does not embed in V (X. Wu). **Q into 2V: claimed YES**, Kojima–Sheng, arXiv:2603.18410 (v1 19 Mar, v3 23 May 2026): nV contains continuum many copies of Q for n ≥ 2. X. Hyperbolic groups embed in rational groups (arXiv:1711.08369) and in f.p. simple groups (arXiv:2309.06224), but nothing found for 2V and up. GL_n(Z) into mV: nothing found. Sent to z2-19-glnz-q-2v and z2-19-hyperbolic-nv. |
| 2.20 F faithful 2-transitive action | OPEN | Le Boudec–Matte Bon classify 3-transitive actions for groups in Homeo(S¹) or Aut(T) under stabilizer conditions, arXiv:1906.05744. A search summary says the transitivity degree of F is at most 2, answering Hull–Osin. Its source (likely Le Boudec–Matte Bon, "Confined subgroups and high transitivity", Ann. H. Lebesgue 5 (2022) 491–522) was not read. Whether the degree is exactly 2 is the open question. |
