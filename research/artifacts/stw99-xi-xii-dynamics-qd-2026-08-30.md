title: STW Problems XI and XII --- dynamical quasidiagonality, statuses and the extension reduction (2026-08-30)
---

Problem XI (Z^d-crossed products of compact spaces: stably finite ⟹
quasidiagonal? AF-embeddable?) and Problem XII (AH real-rank-zero
coefficients under Z: quasidiagonal ⟹ AF-embeddable?), from the
quasidiagonality section of arXiv:2506.10902.

Solved surroundings: Pimsner's theorem (d = 1, full equivalence with the
compression criterion); Brown's AF ⋊ Z theorem (K_0-incompressibility);
Gabe's traceless theorem (separable exact traceless: AF-embeddable ⟺ QD
⟺ stably finite ⟺ no nonempty compact open subset of Prim); the
TWW/Gabe/Schafhauser AF-embedding theorem for faithful amenable traces
with the UCT (Tu gives the UCT for these groupoid algebras).

New structural remark recorded on the XI node: elementwise
incompressibility is necessary for stable finiteness for every d (a
single compressing group element manufactures an infinite projection in
its own Z-crossed subalgebra, and infiniteness of a projection passes to
the ambient algebra); and the trace-weight support filtration decomposes
any stably finite C(X) ⋊ Z^d into subquotients that are individually
covered by the Gabe (traceless) and TWW (faithful measured support)
regimes, so the problem's content is exactly quasidiagonality and
AF-embeddability along dynamical extensions — the d ≥ 2 analogue of the
glueing that Pimsner and Brown perform through compression conditions in
d = 1.  On zero-dimensional X the stable-finiteness half is the
type-semigroup paradoxicality programme (Rainone, Rainone--Sims,
Bönicke--Ma).

Sources: Pimsner ETDS 1983; N. Brown JFA 1998; Gabe GAFA (Theorem C);
Tikuisis--White--Winter Ann. Math. 185; Tu K-theory 1999; Rainone,
Rainone--Sims, Bönicke--Ma (type semigroups).
