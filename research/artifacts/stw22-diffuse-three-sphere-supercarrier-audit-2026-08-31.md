# Audit: factor-uniform diffuse three-sphere supercarriers

## Bootstrap

The degree-two Stiefel theorem supplies precisely the missing input one
dimension higher. Projection orbit fibres are products of corner unitary
groups. Their degree-one local connectivity permits controlled unitary
lifts of small projection `S^2` maps; the degree-two unitary modulus fills
those lifts. Thus fixed-trace Grassmannians are equi-`LC^2` and
two-connected.

The inclusive spectral-superprojection multifunction over `S^3` has these
Grassmannians as values. Its lower-semicontinuity proof is unchanged from
degree two and includes spectral atoms at the cutoff and a corner-preserving
polar-range correction. Michael with `n=2` selects the supercarrier.

After direct rotation, both unitary-lift multifunctions have stabilizers
which are products of diffuse corner unitary groups. They are
two-connected and equi-`LC^2`, so the same `n=2` selection applies.
Uncontrolled Jekel contractions are quantitatively harmless because every
represented frame remains supported on the selected small-trace carrier.

## Scope

All nonzero corners are diffuse II1 factors with separable predual.
Matrix factors require a separate finite-dimensional analysis. No
hyperfiniteness, full fundamental group, or common corner isomorphism is
used.
