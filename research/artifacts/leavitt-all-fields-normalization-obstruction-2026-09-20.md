## Theorem and proof

Assume all-trace compression normalization for Kazhdan pairs. For every field k, the unit group of R=L_k(1,2) contains a finitely generated nonhyperlinear subgroup. Therefore R^x is not hyperlinear, including in the finite-local sense for uncountable k.

Write the four standard generators as s,t,a,b, with

```
ts=ba=1, ta=bs=0, st+ab=1, e=ab=1-st.
```

Use exactly the matrices u,u^-1,k_+,k_- displayed in [the native normalization proof](leavitt-direct-normalization-obstruction-2026-09-20.md). Their invertibility holds in every characteristic: the inverse of 1+a t^3 is 1-a t^3 and the inverse of 1+s^3 b is 1-s^3 b. No involution claim is needed. The formula for u^-1 follows from the telescoping identity e+set+s^2et^2+s^3t^3=1, also valid in every characteristic.

By the existing all-field theorem GL_4(R)=EL_4(R), choose finite elementary factorizations of u,k_+,k_-. Let S be the unital subring of R generated by s,t,a,b and all coefficients in these three finite factorizations. This is a finitely generated associative ring with identity. It is countable even if k is not. It is nonzero as a subring of R. The elementary factorizations ensure

```
u,k_+,k_- in H_0=EL_4(S).
```

This is the reason to include the factorization coefficients. We do not assume that every unit over S is elementary, and we do not need GL_4(S)=EL_4(S). Their inverses belong to H_0 by reversing the words and negating the coefficients.

Set L_0=diag(EL_3(S),1). The Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain theorem supplies property (T) for H_0 and L_0. For i!=j<=3,

```
u x_ij(r)u^-1=x_ij(srt) in L_0    (r in S),
```

whereas k_+ and k_- centralize L_0. Also

```
u k_+ u^-1=x_14(a),   u k_- u^-1=x_41(b).
```

The identities are direct polynomial matrix identities using the displayed Leavitt relations. For every r in S,

```
[x_21(rb),x_14(a)]=x_24(r),
[x_41(b),x_12(ar)]=x_42(r).
```

Further commutators with roots of L_0 give x_i4(r) and x_4i(r), i=1,3. Together with the roots of L_0 these generate all of EL_4(S), so

```
H_0=<L_0,u,k_+,k_->.
```

The normalization premise applies. Given any pi:H_0->U(M), put D=pi(L_0)' cap M. Since k_+ centralizes L_0 and pi(u) normalizes D, pi(x_14(a)) belongs to D. Therefore

```
pi(x_24(a))=pi([x_21(1),x_14(a)])=1.
```

But a!=0 since ba=1, hence x_24(a)!=1. Every matrix-ultraproduct homomorphism kills this fixed element. Thus H_0 is nonhyperlinear.

Finally S embeds in R, hence EL_4(S) embeds in GL_4(R). The complete four-leaf prefix code gives the actual ring isomorphism M_4(R)~=R and the unit-group isomorphism GL_4(R)~=R^x. A hyperlinear group has only hyperlinear subgroups, so R^x is not hyperlinear.

## Why the witness was changed

The fixed-F_2 proof used k=diag(I_3,1+et) and the killed mark x_13(e). In the descent above, this extra k need not belong to EL_4(S) unless its elementary factorization coefficients are also included. Using k_+ itself avoids that unnecessary extra choice. The new mark x_24(a) is noncentral as well as nonidentity: its commutator with x_43(1) is x_23(a)!=1. Therefore it survives any quotient of H_0 by a central subgroup, and the same model-killing argument excludes hyperlinearity of such a quotient. No broader projective-host embedding is inferred without a specified map.

## Impact and limits

This reaches characteristic zero as well as every finite or infinite field. Only a finitely generated witness needs property (T); the full unit group over an arbitrary field need not be finitely generated. It says nothing here about finite presentation or simplicity outside the separately treated F_2 group. It also does not assert a finitely presented nonhyperlinear group over each field or solve the unrelated Thompson F question.

The proof is short on the group side because it uses the full Liu--Thom normalization chain. The separate direct-internality Leavitt proof is a weaker-input route for F_2: it bypasses the general normalization theorem, but uses scalar-commutant selection and the finite central-height argument instead.
