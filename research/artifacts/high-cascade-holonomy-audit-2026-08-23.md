# High-cascade audit: where a new theorem can still close non-hyperlinearity

The full `cairn frontier --goal non-hyperlinear-group` cone was audited on
2026-08-23, together with `cairn why` for the direct SL3 projective-transfer,
Kac--Moody regular-core synchronization, Atlas phase-lift, and canonical
trace-promotion terminals.

## Topological ranking versus independent mathematical content

`projective-trace-square-transfer-for-sl3-pair` has the largest immediate
consumer count: one direct HNN proof and four reusable SL3 interfaces.  The
five consumers share the same root gate, however.  Its current claim file
already reduces the remaining case to a universal two-primary
relative-commutant collapse; the extra consumer count does not represent
five independent chances.

`compact-hyperbolic-km-regular-microstates-synchronize` and
`canonical-trace-promotes-non-ce-profile` each close the goal directly, but
both are terminal formulations.  Bare face curvature, local finite-group
rounding, character rigidity, finite groupoids, capacity growth, and the
incorrect Shulman trace upgrade have already been fenced.  The CDI
image-character variant also preserves the input regularity gate: for the
distinguished non-CE factor character, proving that all faithful nonregular
characters are CE forces that character to be regular and has already solved
canonical trace promotion.

The Atlas phase-lift is the narrowest finite packet calculation, but its
bridge route through a six-relator quotient was refuted.  Exact exclusion of
additional finite simple carriers does not by itself exclude diffuse
matrix-ultraproduct holonomy.

## New narrowing obtained in this audit

`bounded-degree-holonomy-collapses-for-map-groups` removes a previously
unseparated part of the Kac--Moody terminal.  Local regular charts leave
holonomy in multiplicity algebras.  If those algebras have uniformly bounded
irreducible degree, compactness on each bounded block plus a weighted
bad-block estimate forces the entire holonomy to the identity, uniformly in
the number of blocks and their multiplicities.

Therefore a genuine counter-microstate for the character-rigid simple
Kac--Moody candidate must carry positive density in noncommutative holonomy
blocks whose irreducible degree diverges.  Finite projective labels, bounded
matrix-unit packets, repeated bounded sectors, and subhomogeneous
coefficient groupoids are now excluded in one statement.  The remaining
high-cascade theorem is correspondingly sharper:

> Couple the native Curtis--Tits relations to the growing multiplicity
> algebra strongly enough to exclude positive-density unbounded-degree
> holonomy.

This does not prove synchronization, but it removes every bounded-degree
version without assuming a convergence rate or a bounded number of blocks.
