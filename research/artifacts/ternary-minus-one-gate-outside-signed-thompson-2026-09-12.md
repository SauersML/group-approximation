# The ternary minus-one gate outside the signed Thompson group

Lane `w5-c3-outside`, 2026-09-12. Target: `sylvester-rank-functions-on-ternary-leavitt-units-kill-minus-one`
(link 1 of the audited ternary counterexample chain). `w5-c3-signed-thompson` showed that on `E ⋊ V` the
question is exactly the rank gate of Thompson's `V`. This artifact asks what a proof has to use outside
`E ⋊ V`, and records what the lane proved about those outside elements.

Notation: `K` a field, `R = L_K(1,2)` with generators `s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1`, words `μ`, cylinders `[μ]`,
`X = {0,1}^N`. Two words are *incomparable* when neither is a prefix of the other. `V <= R^x` is the group of
units `g = Σ_{c in C} s_(π(c)) t_c` over complete prefix codes `C, D` and a bijection `π : C -> D`.
For incomparable `σ, κ` and `λ in K` put `T_(σ,κ)(λ) = 1 + λ s_σ t_κ`. Since `t_κ s_σ = 0`, the element
`s_σ t_κ` squares to zero and `T_(σ,κ)(λ) T_(σ,κ)(μ) = T_(σ,κ)(λ + μ)`.

## 0. Summary

1. **Generation (Section 1, established).** `L_K(1,2)^x` is generated by `V` and the transvections
   `T_(00,01)(λ)`, `λ in K`. Over a prime field `F_p` one transvection suffices:
   `L_(F_p)(1,2)^x = ⟨V, x⟩` with `x = 1 + s_00 t_01`. So "outside `E ⋊ V`" is one element.
2. **Support conjugacy (Section 2, established).** Two units of finite order `m` with `char K ∤ m` are
   conjugate in `R^x` iff the same irreducible factors of `x^m - 1` occur in their spectral decompositions.
3. **Extension (Section 3, open).** Whether a detecting rank function on `F_3[V]` yields one on `F_3[G_3]`
   moving `z`, which would make link 1 equivalent to the `V` gate, and where the natural attempts die.

## 1. Generation by V and one transvection

**Theorem 1.1.** For every field `K`, `L_K(1,2)^x = ⟨V, T_(00,01)(λ) : λ in K⟩`. Over `F_p`,
`L_(F_p)(1,2)^x = ⟨V, T_(00,01)(1)⟩`.

*Proof.*
1. **EL_3.** The three-leaf code `α = (0, 10, 11)` gives the `K`-algebra isomorphism
   `ψ : M_3(R) -> R`, `e_ij ⊗ r -> s_(α_i) r t_(α_j)`. It carries `GL_3(R)` onto `R^x`. By
   `leavitt-gl-equals-el-and-perfect-unit-group` (`glAll_eq_elementary`, `n = 3`, every field),
   `GL_3(R) = EL_3(R)`. So `R^x` is generated by `ψ(x_ij(r)) = 1 + s_(α_i) r t_(α_j)`, `i != j`, `r in R`.
2. **Monomials.** `R` is spanned over `K` by the monomials `s_μ t_ν`, and `x_ij(r + r') = x_ij(r) x_ij(r')`.
   So `R^x` is generated by `T_(σ,κ)(λ)` with `σ = α_i μ`, `κ = α_j ν`, `i != j`. These words begin with
   different codewords of `α`, so they are incomparable.
3. **Splitting.** For a finite complete prefix code `W`, `s_σ t_κ = Σ_(w in W) s_(σw) t_(κw)`, and the
   summands have pairwise zero products because `κw` and `σw'` are incomparable. Hence
   `T_(σ,κ)(λ) = Π_(w in W) T_(σw,κw)(λ)`, a product of commuting factors.
4. **Conjugation into (00, 01).** Suppose `[σ] ∪ [κ] != X`. Extend `{σ, κ}` to a complete prefix code `C`.
   Its size is at least 3, since the complement of `[σ] ∪ [κ]` is a nonempty clopen. Take a complete code
   `D ⊇ {00, 01}` of the same size, obtained from `{00, 01, 1}` by repeatedly splitting a codeword other than
   `00, 01`. Choose a bijection `π : C -> D` with `π(σ) = 00`, `π(κ) = 01`, and let `g = Σ s_(π(c)) t_c`.
   Because `t_c s_σ = δ_(c,σ)` for `c in C`, we get `g s_σ t_κ g^-1 = s_00 t_01`, so
   `T_(σ,κ)(λ) = g^-1 T_(00,01)(λ) g`. If `[σ] ∪ [κ] = X`, then `{σ, κ} = {0, 1}`. Split with `W = {0, 1}`:
   both factors `T_(σ0,κ0)`, `T_(σ1,κ1)` have proper union, and step 4 applies to each.
5. **Prime fields.** Over `F_p`, `T(λ) = T(1)^λ`. ∎

**Corollary 1.2.** `G_3 = L_(F_3)(1,2)^x = ⟨V, x⟩` with `x = 1 + s_00 t_01` of order 3. Also
`L_(F_2)(1,2)^x = ⟨V, 1 + s_00 t_01⟩` with an involution.

**Where `z` sits.** The full level-one `SL_2(F_3)` is generated by `T_(0,1)(1)` and `T_(1,0)(1)`, and each is a
product of two `V`-conjugates of `x` (step 3, then step 4). Then `z = w_0^2` with
`w_0 = T_(0,1)(1) T_(1,0)(-1) T_(0,1)(1) = [[0,1],[-1,0]]`.

**Proposition 1.3 (transvection trees are conjugation-uniform).** Let `P_1, ..., P_k` be pairs of incomparable
words whose `2k` cylinders are pairwise disjoint and do not cover `X`. Put `A = ⟨T_(P_1)(1), ..., T_(P_k)(1)⟩`,
an elementary abelian 3-group when `K = F_3`. Every nonidentity element of `A` is conjugate in `G_3` to `x`.

*Proof.*
- **Signs.** Conjugation by `τ_([κ]) = 1 - 2 s_κ t_κ in E` sends `T_(σ,κ)(1)` to `T_(σ,κ)(-1)` and fixes the
  other factors, whose cylinders are disjoint from `[κ]`. So every nonidentity element is conjugate to a product
  of `T_(P_i)(1)` over a nonempty subset of indices.
- **Merging.** For such a subset, a single `g in V` sends the `P_i` to `(00w_i, 01w_i)` for a complete suffix code
  `{w_i}` of the subset size, completed away from `[00] ∪ [01]`. By step 3 the image product is `x`. The code
  sizes match because the union of the chosen cylinders is proper. ∎

**Consequence for rank functions.** In any Sylvester rank function on `F_3[G_3]`, the values
`rk(([g] - 1)^j)` for `j = 1, 2` are the same for all nonidentity `g` in every such `A`. These are
single-conjugation and locally-finite facts, so by `anti-central-sofic-data-cannot-force-defect-submultiplicativity`
(parts 3–4) they cannot by themselves force `rk(1 - [z]) = 0`.
