# A concrete marked root-kernel candidate in a hyperbolic KMS group

Date: 2026-09-12. This gives an explicit nontrivial word killed by two
natural families of finite images. Its vanishing in **every** finite image
is the remaining construction problem, and is not asserted here.

## 1. Presentation and marked word

Fix a prime `p>=7`. Use `[x,y]=x^-1 y^-1 x y` and left-associated iterated
commutators. Let

```text
Gamma = < a,b,c |
  a^p, b^p, c^p,
  [a,b,a], [a,b,b],
  [b,c,b], [b,c,c],
  [a,c,a], [a,c,c,a], [a,c,c,c] >.

r = [b,a],   s = [b,c],   w = (r s)^p.
```

This is `G_HC^(1)_2(p)` in Theorem 1.3 of
Caprace--Conder--Kaluba--Witzel,
[*Hyperbolic generalized triangle groups, property (T) and finite simple
quotients*](https://jlupub.ub.uni-giessen.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/163d9999-b75e-4eee-a519-dbc103924f17/content).
That theorem gives infiniteness, word-hyperbolicity, and property (T).

## 2. Why the marked word is nontrivial

The two root groups generated by `r` and `s` generate their free product:

```text
<r,s> = <r> * <s> = C_p * C_p.
```

To apply the geometric theorem precisely, index the simple roots by
`1=a, 2=c, 3=b`. The rank-two subsystems on `{a,b}` and `{b,c}` are of
type `A_2`. Their reflected root groups `U_(s_b alpha_a)` and
`U_(s_b alpha_c)` are therefore the commutator groups generated by `r`
and `s`. Both are cyclic of order `p`, and changing the Chevalley signs
just changes their generators to nonzero powers.

Theorem 6.9 of Caprace--Marquis,
[*Amalgams of rational unipotent groups and residual nilpotence*](https://perso.uclouvain.be/timothee.marquis/papers/KMLieQ.pdf),
asserts that these two reflected root groups generate their free product
for a nonspherical, 2-spherical rank-three matrix over any integral
domain. Thus it applies over `F_p`, despite the rational field in the
paper's title. The cyclically reduced word `(rs)^p` has free-product
length `2p`, so it is nontrivial and has infinite order.

## 3. Its image in the positive Kac--Moody group

Let `psi:Gamma -> U^+` be the canonical map to the positive subgroup of
the minimal Kac--Moody group over `F_p`. In that larger group, the
reflected root groups are conjugates, by a representative of the Weyl
reflection `s_b`, of the root groups for `a,c`. Their generated group is
therefore conjugate to the rank-two unipotent group of type `B_2`.

This finite group embeds in the upper unitriangular matrices of degree
four over `F_p`, after choosing the usual order on a symplectic basis.
For such a matrix `I+N`, one has `N^4=0`; hence
`(I+N)^p=I+N^p=I` for `p>=5`. It follows that

```text
psi(w)=1,   but   w != 1 in Gamma.
```

Every finite homomorphism obtained by composing `psi` with a finite
homomorphism of `U^+` consequently kills `w`.

The same Caprace--Marquis paper identifies the analogous kernel with a
nilpotent residual **over the rationals**. That identification has not
been imported over `F_p`, and would not by itself identify the full
finite residual anyway. A quotient map with nontrivial kernel and a
residually finite target does not make its source non-residually finite.

## 4. An independent all-block-size calculation

For any positive integer `k`, any field `F` of characteristic `p`, and
any `k`-by-`k` matrices `X,Y,Z`, put

```text
A = I + E_12(X),   B = I + E_23(Y),   C = I + E_31(Z)
```

in degree `3k`. Here `E_ij(X)` is the block matrix with sole nonzero
block `X` in position `(i,j)`. These matrices satisfy the presentation
of `Gamma`: each pair generates a group of nilpotency class at most two,
and all three generators have order dividing `p`.

Direct multiplication gives

```text
[B,A] = I - E_13(XY),
[B,C] = I + E_21(YZ),
[B,A][B,C] = I + N,
N = -E_13(XY) + E_21(YZ),
N^2 = -E_23(YZXY),   N^3 = 0.
```

Therefore `[B,A][B,C]` has order dividing `p`, and every representation
of this displayed form kills `w`. The calculation has no bound on `k`
and does not require `X,Y,Z` to commute. Over a finite field these are
actual finite images.

The calculation is a statement about this specified family. It does
not put arbitrary finite representations of `Gamma` into that block
form, and does not prove a bound on all finite simple images.

## 5. Exact remaining theorem and a useful restriction

The concrete finite-group statement that would solve the construction is:

> For every finite group `F` and every triple `A,B,C` satisfying the
> defining relators in Section 1, `([B,A][B,C])^p=1`.

If proved for any prime `p>=7`, this statement puts the explicit
nontrivial element `w` in the finite residual of the explicit hyperbolic
group `Gamma`. The already-proved first-three-statement equivalence then
produces the desired quotientless hyperbolic Kazhdan witness.

The original local torsion generators cannot replace `w`. Grave de
Peralta--Valentiner-Branth,
[*High-dimensional expanders from Kac--Moody--Steinberg groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05197),
Proposition 3.8 and Corollary 3.13, supply finite quotients injective on
every local spherical subgroup. Every finite subgroup of `Gamma` fixes
a point in its CAT(0) triangle complex and is contained, up to conjugacy,
in a local vertex group. Thus one such finite quotient has torsion-free
kernel, so `Gamma` is virtually torsion-free. In particular the proposed
residual word correctly has infinite order and lies outside the local
vertex groups.

No computation has been used to assert the universal finite-group
statement, and no certificate for it has yet been obtained.
