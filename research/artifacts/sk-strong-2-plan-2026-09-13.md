# sk-strong-2 plan: one group, many properties

Lane sk-strong-2 (SK swarm, wave 6), 2026-09-13. Lens: a construction that gives more properties at once, with a proof
as short as the note's.

## Target: Theorem S (all-properties theorem)
For every infinite minimal subshift X, and concretely for the Fibonacci subshift, the note's group G_X is:
1. infinite, finitely generated, simple, Kazhdan and LEF (the note);
2. a marked limit of the finite simple groups SL_{3N}(F_2), which form an expander family (sk-expanders-limits);
3. generated by three torsion elements when the alphabet has two letters (sk-generators, unreviewed);
4. a host for every finite group and for Matui's simple amenable group [[T]]' (sk-subgroups, unreviewed);
5. **not uniformly simple**, with unbounded conjugation-invariant norms coming from the finite models (NEW, this lane);
6. **C*-simple**, and C*_r(G_X) has a unique trace. The unique trace follows from BKKO, since G_X is simple and nonamenable. C*-simplicity is the NEW TARGET and is open.

This lane owns the new mathematics in items 5 and 6. Theorem S itself is a wiring node over existing claims.

## Why it matters
- **Item 5** is the quantitative face of LEF. The finite models carry a normalized rank that is subadditive and conjugation invariant. Transvections over short towers have small rank, so producing e_12(1) takes many conjugates. G_X is therefore unlike boundedly simple groups (to check against Gal–Gismatullin and the PSL_n over fields literature).
- **Item 6** would make C*_r(G_X) a simple unital C*-algebra with unique trace, generated by a Kazhdan LEF group. C*-simplicity does not come for free from simplicity: Le Boudec 2017 has nonamenable simple groups that are not C*-simple. It also frames the open question for sk-cstar-mf.

## Routes
- **Item 5.**
  - Let rk_ℓ(r) = rank φ_ℓ(r)/m_ℓ on the note's models φ_ℓ.
  - For a fixed product of m conjugates of g^{±1} and large ℓ, subadditivity gives rk(h − I) ≤ m·rk(g − I).
  - If V has no returns up to 2w, then rank φ_ℓ(e_12(e_V) − I) ≤ m_ℓ/(2w+1) + 1, while e_12(1) − I has rank m_ℓ.
  - So m ≳ 2w+1, and w is unbounded. Elementary, about 8 lines, no measure theory.
- **Item 6.** Candidate criteria:
  - Kennedy's intrinsic criterion (no nontrivial amenable uniformly recurrent subgroup);
  - Haagerup's Powers averaging property;
