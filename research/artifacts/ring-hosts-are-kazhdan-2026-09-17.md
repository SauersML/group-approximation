# Ring-host Boone--Higman hosts are automatically Kazhdan (2026-09-17, swarm-0917 w5)

## Observation

Every host produced by the three ring routes to `boone-higman-conjecture`
is a quotient of, or isomorphic to, an elementary group `EL_n(R)` with
`n >= 3` over a finitely generated ring `R`:

| route | host | why it is such a group |
|---|---|---|
| `boone-higman-via-central-simple-leavitt-tensor-host` | `(B ⊗ L)^x` | `≅ GL_3(B ⊗ L) = E_3(B ⊗ L)` (claim `central-simple-leavitt-tensor-unit-groups-are-simple`, part 2) |
| `infinite-simple-input-bh-via-elementary-leavitt-hosts` | `E_n(B ⊗ L)`, `n >= 4` | literally an elementary group |
| `boone-higman-via-projective-ring-host` | `PEL_4(L)` | quotient of `EL_4(L)` by its centre |

In each case the coefficient ring is finitely generated. `B` is finitely
presented, `L_(F_2)(1,2)` is generated by four elements, and the projective
host hypothesis asks for a finitely generated `L`. Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain
(`elementary-groups-over-fg-rings-have-property-t`) gives (T) for `EL_n(R)`.
(T) passes to quotients, because a Kazhdan pair maps to a Kazhdan pair. So
every such host is a finitely presented simple **Kazhdan** group.

## Consequence for the graph

- There are three new routes to `kazhdan-boone-higman-conjecture`, each with
  exactly the open premise of the corresponding Boone--Higman route, plus the
  established lemma `ring-elementary-bh-hosts-are-kazhdan`:
  - `kazhdan-boone-higman-via-central-simple-leavitt-tensor-host`
  - `kazhdan-boone-higman-via-elementary-leavitt-hosts`
  - `kazhdan-boone-higman-via-projective-ring-host`
- Through the established equivalence `ck-finite-bi-index-hole-is-kazhdan-boone-higman`
  and the route `ck-finite-bi-index-hole-via-kazhdan-boone-higman`, each of these
  premises now also settles the free-core reading of
  `ck-envelope-simple-core-has-finite-bi-index`.

## Where Kazhdan Boone--Higman really costs more

The only recorded routes to Boone--Higman whose hosts are not known to be
Kazhdan are the Thompson-like ones: twisted Brin--Thompson groups,
Rover--Nekrashevych groups, and shell or full groups of Cantor actions.
Their hosts are typically believed to be a-T-menable. Thompson's `V` is
a-T-menable (Farley). For `nV` the question is recorded as open
(`brin-thompson-groups-nv-lack-property-t`). So Kazhdan Boone--Higman is a
genuine strengthening only on the dynamical side. On the linear side it is
free.

Contrapositive, as a kill of a class: a refutation of Kazhdan Boone--Higman,
which by `ck-finite-bi-index-hole-is-kazhdan-boone-higman` also refutes the
hole, would refute all three ring-host premises at once, but it would say
nothing about the Thompson-like routes.

## What is not claimed

- Nothing about the fixed-core reading of the hole, where the core is the
  specific Boone--Higman--Thompson envelope.
- No new progress on the three open host-existence premises themselves.
  Per `cairn check`, these are the only missing inputs of the new routes:

  - `simple-inputs-have-fp-elementary-leavitt-tensor-hosts`
  - `decidable-group-ring-has-fp-projective-simple-host`
