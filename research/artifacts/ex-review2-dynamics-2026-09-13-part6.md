## 6.1 Quotient formula

**Lemma 1.2 (alignment cocycle).** The cocycle identity follows from
`w_1w_2p(x) = w_1a(x,w_2)p(w_2x) = (w_1a(x,w_2)w_1^(−1))·a(w_2x,w_1)·p(w_1w_2x)`.
- `p_*μ = nμ|_M`, because `p^(−1)(A) = NA` is a disjoint union of `n` translates.

**Lemma 1.3 (the torsor has no relative entropy).**
- *Cost.* `H(α_D | Σ_N) <= μ(D) log n`, since `D ∈ Σ_N` and at most `n` atoms meet each fibre.
- *Measurability.* `i(x) = min{i : w_ix ∈ D}` is `Σ_N`-measurable, because `w_i^(−1)D` is `N`-invariant (`N` normal),
  and it is finite a.e. by ergodicity. `p(w_ix)` is `N`-invariant in `x`, since `w_imx = (w_imw_i^(−1))w_ix`.
- *Recovery.* `x = w_i^(−1)τ(w_ix)p(w_ix)`.

**(2.1).**
- Alpeev–Seward 1.11(i) (verbatim in part 5) applies with index `n`, since `Stab_W(Nx) = N`.
- `h^Rok_W(X/N) = h^Rok_Q(X/N)`, because the invariant σ-algebras coincide.
- Sub-additivity and Lemma 1.3 then give `h^Rok_W(X) <= (1/n)h^Rok_Q(X/N)`.

**(2.2).**
- *Entropy of `β`.* `μ = (1/n)Σ_m μ_(mM)`, and the law of `α_N` under `μ_(mM)` is a relabelling of its law under
  `μ_M`. Concavity gives `H(β) = H_(μ_M)(α_N) <= H_μ(α_N) <= nH(α)`. Each alignment `ã_j` costs `<= log n`.
- *Every alignment is expressible.* `a(x, mw') = m·a(x,w')`, and `a(z, ŝ^(−1)) = ŝ^(−1)a(ŝ^(−1)z, ŝ)^(−1)ŝ`. So every
  `a(·,w)` is a function of translates of the `ã_j`.
- *The α-name.* `α(wp(x))` is the `a(x,w)`-coordinate of `β(wx)`.
- *Generation.*
  - For `E ∈ Σ_N`, `1_E = 1_(E∩M)∘p`.
  - `E ∩ M` is a.e. a function of the α-name.
  - `p_*μ ≪ μ`.
  - Hence `σ-alg_Q(β') = Σ_N`.

**Sanity check** (`W = Z × Z/2`, `X = {0,1}^W`). `nh_W = 2 log 2 = log 4 = h_KS(X/N)`. The quotient is a 2-to-1
factor of the shift on `({0,1}^2)^Z`, and finite-to-one factors preserve entropy over `Z`.

**Freeness model test.** A `Q`-action pulled back to `W` has equal entropy over both groups. So the formula must
fail without freeness, and it does.

## 6.2 Descent (Theorem D)

- **Finitely generated `Q`.**
  - `INF(W)` gives free ergodic `X` with `C < h^Rok_W(X) < ∞`.
  - `X/N` is free and ergodic (Lemma 1.1), with `nC < h^Rok_Q(X/N) < ∞`. So `INF(Q)` holds, and Seward II
    Theorem 1.10 gives maximality.
- **General `Q`.**
  - If `Q` is locally finite, it is amenable, hence sofic.
  - Otherwise, write it as a directed union of finitely generated `Q_i ⊇ Q_0`, with preimages `W_i` infinite and
    finitely generated. Apply the permanence imports.

## 6.3 Ascent equivalence

- **Argmax factor.** Put `σ(q) = argmax_(v ∈ π^(−1)(q)) x(v)`.
  - It is equivariant: `(w.x)` attains its argmax on `π^(−1)(π(w)q)` at `wσ(q)`.
  - It pushes Lebesgue measure to independent uniform choices, one per coset.
- **Freeness.**
  - `m.σ = σ` forces `m = 1`.
  - For `π(w') != 1`, `w'.σ = σ` needs `w'σ(q) = σ(π(w')q)` on infinitely many disjoint pairs of cosets, each of
    probability `1/n`.
- **Splitting.** `d'(π(w)q)(m) = (w.x)((w.σ)(π(w)q)m) = x(σ(q)m) = d(q)(m)`. So the map is `W`-equivariant, and `N`
  acts only on `σ`.
- **(a) ⇒ (c).** Seward's relative-entropy equality under weak containment (arXiv:1602.06680 Theorem 1.2; PASS
  verbatim in dynamics part 2, §13b) gives `h^Rok_W((A^k)^W × Ω) >= k log|A|`. Then (2.1) gives
  `h_Q(Ω/N × ((A^k)^N)^Q) >= nk log|A|`.
- **(c) ⇒ (b).** The lift is `(A^k)^W × Ω`, generated by the coordinate at `1` together with `σ(1_Q)`. So its entropy
  over `W` is at most `k log|A| + log n`, and (2.2) keeps the quotient finite.
  - The node's bound `log n + nk log|A|` is a harmless overestimate.
  - Ergodicity holds because the product of a Bernoulli shift with the ergodic `Ω/N` is ergodic.
- **(b) ⇒ (a).**
  - `X` is ergodic, since `W`-invariant sets lie in `Σ_N`.
  - The quotient formula gives `(C − r log n)/n < h^Rok_W(X) < ∞`, so `INF(W)` holds.
