## Structure of the gap

- Moments in `L(G)` of finite combinations `sum c_g lambda(g)` are polynomials
  in the coefficients and in the triviality pattern `delta(w(g) = e)` of words
  in finitely many group elements.
- So the moment tables realizable in `GF` are exactly the limits of polynomial
  tables built from marked-ball triviality patterns of countable groups.
- If all groups were hyperlinear, every such pattern would be approximated by
  unitary matrices, and `GF` would be the Connes-embeddable class.

Local universality asks every tracial moment table to be such a limit. The
first non-trivial instance is the moment table of the non-RU constraint algebra
of `mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists`. Corner closure removes the
central-character compression from the Boolean-to-linear-system boundary at the
ultrapower level. What remains is realizing a non-RU Boolean table by
triviality-pattern polynomials. That is the ultrapower form of
`non-ru-bcs-is-satisfiable-in-a-group-factor`, and it is no weaker than the
Paddock--Slofstra linear-system boundary.
