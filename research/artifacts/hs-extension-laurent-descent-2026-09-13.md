# Actor extension lifts vertex rounding to the Laurent vertex (d = 3, r >= 6)

Lane `ex-hs-instability-extension`, 2026-09-13. Unreviewed.

`laurent-vertex-rounds-for-every-model` records a converse gap: a unit-type rounding of the polynomial vertex
`Γ = EL_r(R_+)` extends to the Laurent Steinberg group `St_r(L)`. But the descent from `St_r(L)` to `Δ = EL_r(L)`
stays open, because the symbols `{x_i, x_j}` can have unbounded `p`-power order in `K_2(A_n)`. Such symbol twists
are the Deligne-type way a Laurent-vertex model could fail to round while the polynomial vertex rounds.

This artifact does two things:
- it writes the `St_r(L)` agreement (§1), which main records only in Attempts;
- it proves that at actor models the twists carry vanishing mass (§§2–3), when `d = 3` and `r >= 6`.

The mechanism is compressor transport, which is exact off small trace by property (T) of `Γ`, together with property
(T) of `Z^3 ⋊ SL_3(Z)`.

Notation: `R_+ = F_q[x_1..x_d]`, `L = F_q[x^(±1)]`, `G = Δ ⋊ SL_d(Z)`, `M = prod_U M_n`,
`q : St_r(L) -> Δ`.

## 1. Agreement on the Laurent Steinberg group, r >= 5

**Proposition A.** Let `r >= 5`, `d >= 1`, and `σ : Δ -> U(M)` a homomorphism. Let `π_n` be genuine representations
of `Γ` with `π = σ` on `Γ` in `M`, each factoring through `St_r(A_n)` for a finite quotient `A_n` of `R_+` in which
every `x_i` is a unit. Let `ρ'_n` be the canonical extension to `St_r(L)`. Then `σ∘q = ρ'` on `St_r(L)` in `M`.

*Proof.* By item 2 of `laurent-extension-agrees-iff-inverse-defects-commute` it suffices that each
`W_i = ρ'(x_23(x_i^(-1)))^* σ(e_23(x_i^(-1)))` lies in `π(Γ)' ∩ M`.

1. **`W_i` commutes in `M` with every generator `e_cd(I_cd)` of `K_i = K(R_+, x_i)`.** Here
   `I_cd = x_i^([c=3]+[d=2]) R_+`, as in `mixed-level-root-group-is-kazhdan-in-rank-at-least-five`. Put
   `h = x_i^(-1)`, `U = σ(e_23(h))`, `V = ρ'(x_23(h))`.
   - **Full roots.** If `c ≠ 3` and `d ≠ 2`, or `(c,d) = (2,3)`, then `x_cd(f)` commutes with `x_23(h)` in `St_r(L)`
     and `e_cd(f)` with `e_23(h)` in `Δ`. Also `π(e_cd(f)) = σ(e_cd(f)) = ρ'(x_cd(f))` for `f ∈ R_+`. So both `U`
     and `V` commute with it, and so does `W_i`.
   - **Row 3 at level `(x_i)`.** For `k ∉ {2,3}` and `c = x_i a`, `[x_23(h), x_3k(c)] = x_2k(a)` with `a ∈ R_+`.
     With `b = π(e_3k(c))`, both `U b U^* b^*` and `V b V^* b^*` equal `π(e_2k(a))`. So `U b U^* = V b V^*`,
     i.e. `V^* U b = b V^* U`, and `W_i b = b W_i`. This is the cancellation in item 1 of that criterion's proof.
   - **Column 2 at level `(x_i)`.** For `k ∉ {2,3}`, `[x_k2(x_i a), x_23(h)] = x_k3(a)`, and the same cancellation
     applies.
   - **The corner `(3,2)` at level `(x_i^2)`.** `e_32(x_i^2 ab) = [e_3k(x_i a), e_k2(x_i b)]` with `k ∉ {2,3}`, a
     product of generators already handled.
2. **`π_n(K_i) = π_n(Γ)` at every coordinate.** `x_i` is a unit in `A_n` and `R_+ -> A_n` is onto, so every
   `I_cd` maps onto `A_n`, and `π_n(e_cd(I_cd)) = π_n(e_cd(R_+))`.
3. **Kazhdan projection for `K_i`.** Let `(S_K, κ_K)` be a Kazhdan pair for `K_i`, which exists for `r >= 5`.
   Choose representatives `W_i = (w_n)_U`.
   - Item 1 gives `||[w_n, π_n(s)]||_2 -> 0` for `s ∈ S_K`.
   - The projection `E_n` onto the invariant vectors of `Ad π_n|K_i` satisfies
     `||w_n - E_n(w_n)||_2 <= κ_K^(-1) max_s ||[w_n, π_n(s)]||_2 -> 0`.
   - `E_n(w_n) ∈ π_n(K_i)' = π_n(Γ)'`, by item 2. So `W_i ∈ prod_U π_n(Γ)' ⊆ π(Γ)' ∩ M`. ∎

## 2. Laurent K_2 in rank r >= d + 3

Let `K = ker(q) = K_2(r, L)`. It is central in `St_r(L)`.
- **Stability.** For `r >= d + 3`, injective stability gives `K_2(r, L) ≅ K_2(L)`. This is the
  Dennis–Vaserstein–van der Kallen–Suslin–Tulenbaev theorem as imported in
  `polynomial-k2-vanishing-lifts-cones-to-laurent-steinberg-proof` (PKL7). `L` is noetherian of Krull dimension `d`.
- **The fundamental theorem.** For the regular rings `F_q[Z^j]`, the fundamental theorem and `K_2(F_q) = 0` give

  ```text
  K_2(F_q[Z^d])  =  K_2(F_q) ⊕ d·K_1(F_q) ⊕ C(d,2)·K_0(F_q)  =  (F_q^×)^d ⊕ Z^(C(d,2)).
  ```

  The first summand is generated by the Steinberg symbols `{u_0, x_i}`, with `u_0` a generator of `F_q^×`, and the
  second by the symbols `{x_i, x_j}`. This is the same calculation as (TKO3) of
  `two-coordinate-relators-leave-stable-laurent-k2`, which records `d = 2`.
- **Torsion and quotient.** `T_K = (F_q^×)^d` is the torsion subgroup, hence canonical. `K̄ = K/T_K ≅ Λ²Z^d` through
  `{x^a, x^b} -> a ∧ b`.
- **Equivariance.** `SL_d(Z)` acts on `St_r(L)` by substitution `φ_M`. `φ_M{x^a, x^b} = {x^(Ma), x^(Mb)}`, so the
  action on `K̄` is `Λ²M`.
- **`d = 3`.** `Λ²Z^3 ≅ (Z^3)^*` as `SL_3(Z)`-modules. So `SL_3(Z) ⋉ K̄ ≅ SL_3(Z) ⋉ Z^3` through the automorphism
  `M -> M^(-T)`, and this group has property (T) (`affine-semidirect-sl3-source-has-property-t`).

## 3. Descent at actor models

**Theorem B.** Let `d = 3`, `r >= 6`, and let `σ : G -> U(M)` be trace-preserving and satisfy (H1) on `Γ`. Then
`σ|Δ` rounds: there are genuine `ρ_n : Δ -> U(n'')`, with `n''/n -> 1`, such that
`max_(s ∈ F_Δ) ||σ_n(s) - ρ_n(s)||_2 -> 0` along `U`.

*Proof.*
1. **Unit type.** By the per-model chain, a rounding `π_n` has `τ(1 - P_n) -> 0`. This uses:
   - `nonunit-projection-bounded-by-root-spectral-mass`;
   - `shallow-nonunit-root-mass-vanishes-under-vertex-rounding`;
   - `vertex-rounding-deep-nonunit-root-mass-vanishes`;
   - the definition of `P_n` in `unit-type-vertex-rounding-forces-compressor-commutant-rigidity`.

   Replace the non-unit constituents by trivial ones. `π_n` stays a rounding, now of unit type through `St_r(A_n)`.
2. **Agreement.** Proposition A gives `σ∘q = ρ'` on `St_r(L)`.
3. **Central characters.** `ρ'_n(St_r(L)) = π_n(Γ)` and `K` is central. So each irreducible constituent has a
   character `ω : K -> C^×`. Let `ν_n` be the trace-weighted distribution of `ω`.
   - **L²-concentration.** For fixed `k ∈ K`, `∫|ω(k) - 1|^2 dν_n = ||ρ'_n(k) - 1||_2^2 -> ||σ(q(k)) - 1||_2^2 = 0`.
4. **Torsion part.** On a torsion generator `k = {u_0, x_i}`, a nontrivial value is a `(q-1)`-th root of unity, at
   distance `>= c_q > 0` from `1`. So `ν_n(ω|T_K ≠ 1) <= 3c_q^(-2) max_k ∫|ω(k) - 1|^2 dν_n -> 0`.
5. **Transport.** Let `M ∈ {I + E_ij}`, the nonnegative generators of `SL_3(Z)`, with `MΓM^(-1) = φ_M(Γ) ⊆ Γ`.
   - **Exact off small trace.** The argument of `compressor-transport-exactifies-under-vertex-rounding` uses only
     `MΓM^(-1) ⊆ Γ`. It gives a partial isometry `W_n` with `W_n π_n(γ) = π_n(φ_M γ) W_n` on projections of
     co-trace `<= η_n -> 0`.
   - **`W_n` intertwines the extensions.** Let `m_n` be the least common multiple of the multiplicative orders of
     `x_1, x_2, x_3` in `A_n`, so every `x_i^(m_n) = 1` there. For `g = x^(-c) f ∈ L` with `f ∈ R_+`, put `γ = x_ab(x^((m_n - 1)c) f) ∈ Γ`. Then
     `ρ'_n(x_ab(g)) = π_n(γ)`, and `ρ'_n(φ_M x_ab(g)) = π_n(φ_M γ)`, since `φ_M` commutes with this rewriting. So
     `W_n ρ'_n(y) = ρ'_n(φ_M y) W_n` for every `y ∈ St_r(L)`.
   - **Characters.** Central characters therefore match: `||(φ_M)_* ν_n - ν_n||_TV <= 2η_n`, where
     `(φ_M)_* ω = ω∘φ_M`.
6. **Property (T) on the symbols.** Condition `ν_n` on `{ω|T_K = 1}`, which has mass `-> 1`, to get `ν'_n` on the
   dual of `K̄`. Let `SL_3(Z) ⋉ K̄` act on `ℓ²(K̄^)` by `(M f)(ω) = f(ω∘φ_M)` and `(k f)(ω) = ω(k) f(ω)`. Put
   `ξ_n = √ν'_n`.
   - **Almost invariance.** `||M ξ_n - ξ_n||_2^2 <= ||(φ_M)_*ν'_n - ν'_n||_TV -> 0` for each generator `M`, and
     `||k ξ_n - ξ_n||_2^2 = ∫|ω(k) - 1|^2 dν'_n -> 0` for the three symbol generators.
   - **Kazhdan.** The group has property (T) (§2), so `ξ_n` is `o(1)`-close to an invariant vector.
   - **Support.** `K̄`-invariant vectors are supported at `ω = 1`. So `ν'_n(ω ≠ 1) -> 0`.
7. **Descend.** The constituents on which `ω = 1` on all of `K` carry trace `-> 1`, and they factor through
   `St_r(L)/K = Δ`. Compress `ρ'_n` to them and pad by trivial representations. This gives genuine `ρ_n` of `Δ`
   with `||σ_n(s) - ρ_n(s)||_2 -> 0` on `F_Δ`, by step 2. ∎

## 4. Reading

- **What extension buys.** At actor models (`d = 3`, `r >= 6`), rounding the polynomial vertex, rounding the Laurent
  vertex, and unit-type rounding are the same condition. So, for these parameters,
  `laurent-vertex-rounds-for-every-model` ⟺ (H1) for the Theorem E pair. The symbol twists in `K_2(L)` are the
  Deligne-type obstruction that could have separated them. The `SL_3(Z)`-symmetry spreads any twist over the whole
  dual of `Λ²Z^3`, and property (T) of `Z^3 ⋊ SL_3(Z)` forbids that spread.
- **Scope.**
  - For `d >= 4` the same proof works once `(SL_d(Z) ⋉ Λ²Z^d, Λ²Z^d)` has relative (T), and `r >= d + 3`. That import
    is not checked here.
  - Ranks `r <= 5` (for `d = 3`) are outside stability. There `K_2(r, L)` may be larger than the symbols.
  - Rank `r = 4` also lacks `K_i` Kazhdan.
  - The Bowen–Chapman parameters `(q, r, d) = (2, 3, 3)` are not covered.
- **No rounding is produced.** Everything is conditional on (H1).
