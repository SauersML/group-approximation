## Three-wall synthesis (assembled from established firewalls)

A proposed MF-invisibility proof is impossible if it belongs entirely to
one of:

* Class A -- a representation-functorial projection whose regular image has
  positive canonical trace, made properly infinite by fixed group words:
  refuted by the finite trace of the regular representation.
* Class B -- a fixed finite family of exact finite-subgroup packets and
  homogeneous rational-linear multiplicity relations (restriction,
  conjugacy, refinement, central cuts): the regular profile is a positive
  solution, so no Farkas certificate forces a positive coordinate to zero.
* Class C -- an infinite address graph in a residually finite actor whose
  adjacency is difference-in-finitely-many-conjugacy-classes: finitely
  colorable by one finite quotient, so no infinite-chromatic contradiction.

Possible escape mechanisms outside the three walls (not an exhaustive
classification): coordinate-selected
non-functorial carriers; nonlinear multiplicity holonomy (intertwiners,
phases, rank monodromy); address geometry beyond RF conjugacy prototypes;
adaptive moving-depth challenges with a depth-uniform defect modulus.

Canonical Cairn nodes for the three walls are respectively
`universal-relations-cannot-force-proper-infiniteness`,
`finite-packet-functoriality-has-positive-regular-profile`, and
`rf-conjugacy-difference-graphs-finitely-colorable`.  This section is a map
between them, not a fourth theorem.

## Selector-occurrence authenticity checklist

1. occurrence identity (the transported object uses THAT word occurrence,
   not an isomorphic implementer -- Schur gives only a multiplicity unitary);
2. analytic-vs-group-word typing of selectors;
3. common physical carrier, not merely equal representation types;
4. perturbative persistence under dimension-free exactification;
5. regular-representation sanity for any claimed universal attachment;
6. spectator (amplification) invariance of the terminal invariant.

## Three obligations of a non-MF proof

I. an exact finite-dimensional contradiction seed; II. occurrence semantics
forcing the same finite-coordinate objects into it; III. approximation
robustness with a dimension- and depth-uniform modulus.  Property (T)
discharges III; the graph's live routes fail at II.

This decomposition is diagnostic rather than a new implication.  Examples:

* exact finite-dimensional character collapse supplies I but not III;
* a fixed finite packet often supplies III, but Schur's lemma does not supply
  II for an ambient word occurrence;
* deeper Clifford or Leavitt packets give I and finite-depth II, but not a
  depth-uniform III;
* packet multiplicity functoriality gives II and III but fails I because its
  regular profile remains positive.

Terminal sharpness never repairs missing occurrence semantics.  A norm-one
rectangular leakage estimate is irrelevant until the presentation forces the
actual microstate word to act between the selected source and target.

## Quantifier and transfer failures recorded by the dossier

These are route diagnoses, not claimed new theorems.

* Uniform Ulam stability controls a supremum over all multiplication pairs;
  an MF sequence only has pointwise defect convergence.  The bad pair may
  move with the matrix coordinate.
* Exact finite-dimensional sterility does not imply MF invisibility without
  a point-norm rounding theorem.
* Operator-norm separation may live on a spectrally tiny subspace.  Bare MF
  gives no rate connecting multiplicative defect, dimension, and the rank of
  that subspace, so an adaptive tensor-power amplification cannot in general
  be scheduled from those data alone.
* A non-MF C-star algebra generated by unitaries does not automatically give
  a non-MF abstract generating group: a group corona embedding need not
  preserve the norm of arbitrary noncommutative polynomials in the chosen
  representation.
* Non-CE or linear-system data become a group obstruction only after an
  explicit groupification and an operator-norm stability theorem at the
  marked central element.
* The abstract route “point-norm matricial stability plus a nontrivial
  finite-dimensional radical” would work, but no independently verified
  example satisfying both inputs was produced here.

## Search heuristics retained as audit checks

* Authenticate the group-word/selector occurrence before optimizing an
  already dimension-free terminal inequality.
* A common matrix carrier permits rank comparison; it does not identify two
  ambient word occurrences or their multiplicity gauges.
* Prefer amplification-invariant terminal data: rank ratios, normalized
  selected-carrier trace, relative holonomy, and operator-norm kernel rows.
* Keep fixed-depth dimension-free gaps separate from adaptive-depth
  arguments.  The latter require all challenges to be uniformly addressable
  before the challenge depth is chosen after the coordinate.
* Test every universal exact selector in the regular representation, but do
  not mistake passing that firewall for an occurrence theorem.
