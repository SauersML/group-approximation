# Assembling rank models of EL_3(L_(F_2)(1,2)) from finitely represented coefficients

Lane `gk-ri-counter`, 2026-09-12, family R. It supports the claims
`toeplitz-coefficient-factoring-kills-leavitt-el3-rank-models` and
`toeplitz-defect-dies-in-every-finite-quotient-of-el3`.

## 0. Setting and target

Let `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)` and `G = EL_3(R)`. Then `G ≅ R^x` by
`leavitt-gl-equals-el-and-perfect-unit-group`, and `G` is simple by
`binary-leavitt-unit-group-is-simple`.

A *rank model* is a homomorphism `σ : G -> M^x`, where
`M = prod_ω M_(n_i)(k) / (rank-null)` with `char k = 2`. Because `G` is simple, a nontrivial `σ` is
injective.

The lane's target is a nontrivial `σ`. Such a `σ` necessarily has `N_23 N_12 != 0`, where
`N_ab = σ(x_ab(1)) − 1` (`rank-models-of-el3-satisfy-the-two-root-identities`, stated
negatively).

The starting point examined here is the left-regular models of `EL_3(S)` for subrings `S` with a
finite-dimensional representation (`fd-represented-coefficients-violate-two-root-identities`),
assembled along an increasing union of subrings, possibly with the finite-dimensional contribution
vanishing in the limit.

## 1. The Toeplitz pair, its defect, and the finitary group

In `R`, `t0 s0 = 1` and `s0 t0 != 1`. Put `e := 1 − s0 t0 = s1 t1 != 0`, and let `T` be the unital
subring generated by `s0` and `t0` (the Toeplitz subring).

* `e² = 1 − 2 s0 t0 + s0 (t0 s0) t0 = e`, `e s0 = s0 − s0 t0 s0 = 0`, and `t0 e = 0`.
* The elements `e_ab := s0^a e t0^b` (`a, b >= 0`) satisfy `e_ab e_cd = δ_bc e_ad`. Indeed
  `t0^b s0^c` equals `t0^(b−c)` or `s0^(c−b)`, and `t0^m e = 0 = e s0^m` for `m >= 1`.
  They are nonzero, since `t0^a e_ab s0^b = e`. A family of nonzero matrix units is linearly
  independent, so `K := span_(F_2) {e_ab}` is a copy of `M_∞(F_2)` (finitary matrices) inside `T`.
* Let `F_K` be the subgroup of `G` generated by `x_ij(k)`, `i != j`, `k ∈ K`. Its elements are the
  unipotent finitary matrices `1 + (finitary)` on the basis `{1,2,3} x N`. The generators are the
  transvections `1 + E_((i,a),(j,b))` with `i != j`. Commutators
  `[1 + E_((1,a),(2,c)), 1 + E_((2,c),(1,b))] = 1 + E_((1,a),(1,b))` supply the same-block
  transvections. So `F_K ≅ SL_fin(F_2) = union_n SL_n(F_2)`, which is infinite and simple: every
  `SL_n(F_2)` with `n >= 3` is simple, and no nontrivial scalar survives in the union.
* `x_12(e) = 1 + e_00 E_12` is a transvection in `F_K`, and `x_12(e) != 1` in `G`.

**Assembly must reach `T`.** `G` is generated by `x_ij(a)` for `a` in a generating set of the
algebra `R`, namely `s0, s1, t0, t1, 1`. The commutator `[x_ij(a), x_jk(b)] = x_ik(ab)` with
`i, j, k` distinct, together with `x_ij(a) x_ij(b) = x_ij(a+b)`, reach every coefficient. If
`x_12(a) ∈ EL_3(S) ⊆ GL_3(S)`, then `a ∈ S`. So any family of partial models, each defined on
`EL_3(S_k)`, that is defined on `x_12(s0)` and `x_12(t0)` for ω-almost all `k` has `S_k ⊇ T` for
ω-almost all `k`.

## 2. Theorem A: coefficient factoring through a directly finite ring kills the model

**Theorem A.** Let `σ : G -> M^x` be a homomorphism into any ring `M`. Suppose there are:
* a unital subring `S ⊆ R` containing `s0` and `t0`;
* a unital ring homomorphism `φ : S -> D` into a directly finite ring `D`;
* a map `ψ : EL_3(D) -> M^x` with `ψ(1) = 1`;

such that `σ(x_12(e)) = ψ(EL_3(φ)(x_12(e)))`. Then `σ` is trivial. The same holds for a rank
model `σ = lim_ω σ_k` when, for ω-almost all `k`, `σ_k(x_12(e))` agrees with
`ψ_k(EL_3(φ_k)(x_12(e)))` up to normalized rank `o(1)`. Here `φ_k : S_k -> D_k` is unital with `D_k`
directly finite, `S_k ⊇ T`, and `ψ_k(1) = 1`.

*Proof.*
1. `φ(t0) φ(s0) = φ(1) = 1`. Since `D` is directly finite, `φ(s0) φ(t0) = 1`, so `φ(e) = 0`.
2. `EL_3(φ)` acts entrywise, so `EL_3(φ)(x_12(e)) = x_12(φ(e)) = x_12(0) = 1`, and
   `σ(x_12(e)) = ψ(1) = 1`.
3. `x_12(e) != 1`, so `ker σ` is a nontrivial normal subgroup of the simple group `G`, and `σ` is
   trivial.
4. In the limit version, step 2 holds at ω-almost every stage up to rank `o(1)`, so
   `σ(x_12(e)) = 1` in `M`, and step 3 finishes.

QED

Only the value at the single element `x_12(e)` is used: no homomorphism property of `ψ` and
nothing about other root elements. `D` may be any `M_n(k)`, any rank ultraproduct, any commutative
ring such as `F_2[x, x^-1]` (the Laurent quotient of `T`), or any stably finite ring.

## 3. Theorem B: the Toeplitz defect dies in every residually finite quotient

**Theorem B.** Let `S` be a unital algebra over a prime field `F_p`, containing `s, t` with
`ts = 1 != st`, and put `e = 1 − st`. Every homomorphism from `EL_3(S)`, or from any group
containing `EL_3(S)`, into a residually finite group kills `x_12(λ e)` for every `λ ∈ F_p`. In
particular this holds for every homomorphism into a finite group.

*Proof.*
1. As in Section 1, `e_ab = s^a e t^b` are nonzero matrix units. The subgroup generated by
   `x_ij(λ e_ab)` (`i != j`, `λ ∈ F_p`) is `SL_fin(F_p)`, which is infinite and simple, and
   contains `x_12(λ e)`.
2. A homomorphic image of an infinite simple group is trivial or infinite simple. A residually
   finite group has no infinite simple subgroup, because residual finiteness passes to subgroups
   and a simple residually finite group is finite.
3. So `SL_fin(F_p)` maps trivially.

QED

**Consequence.** Take `S ⊇ T`. The left-regular models of any finite image of `EL_3(S)` (as in
`fd-represented-coefficients-violate-two-root-identities`) satisfy `σ(x_12(e)) = 1`. So do all
stagewise limits of such models.

## 4. The exact point where the assembly dies

Consider an assembly `σ = lim_ω σ_k` in which each `σ_k` on `EL_3(S_k)` factors, at least at
`x_12(e)`, through `EL_3(φ_k)` with `φ_k : S_k -> M_(n_k)(F_2)`, or through a finite image of
`EL_3(S_k)`.
* By Section 1, `S_k ⊇ T` for ω-almost all `k`.
* By Theorem A or Theorem B, `σ_k(x_12(e)) = 1` at those stages.
* So `σ(x_12(e)) = 1`, and `σ` is trivial.

**Vanishing contributions don't help.** Write `σ_k = τ_k ⊕ ρ_k` with `τ_k` factoring. Every bit of
positive rank of `σ(x_12(e)) − 1` then has to come from `ρ_k`, which is subject to no constraint
from the finitely represented coefficients. That is the original problem again.

## 5. Necessary conditions on a nontrivial model

A nontrivial rank model `σ` of `G` must satisfy all of the following.

* **(C1) Faithful displacement on the finitary group.** `σ` restricts injectively to
  `F_K ≅ SL_fin(F_2)`. All transvections are conjugate in `F_K`, so they share one displacement
  `c_0 = rk(σ(x) − 1) > 0`.
* **(C2) No factoring at the defect.** At `x_12(e)`, `σ` agrees with nothing that factors through a
  unital coefficient homomorphism of any `S ⊇ T` into a directly finite ring, or through a
  residually finite image of `EL_3(S)`, even up to rank `o(1)`.
* **(C3) The root maps are not algebra-like.** Suppose `σ(x_12(a)) = 1 + ρ(a)`, where `ρ` is an
  almost-representation of the algebra `T` in rank, meaning block form with multiplicative defect
  `o(1)`. In the limit `ρ(t0) ρ(s0) = 1`. Rank ultraproducts are directly finite, so
  `ρ(s0) ρ(t0) = 1` and `ρ(e) = 0`, contradicting (C1). This is the Toeplitz localization of
  `leavitt-algebra-has-no-unital-rank-model`.
* **(C4) Not monomial.** Truncated permutation actions, on monomials or on vectors of truncated
  modules, give Hamming models (`monomial-rank-models-are-hamming-models`), and `R^x` is nonsofic.

So a construction has to give `SL_fin(F_2)` positive rank displacement through non-monomial,
non-algebra-like maps, and extend them over the compressor `x_12(s0)` and over `x_12(t0)`
compatibly with the Steinberg relations.

## 6. Attempts that died here

* **Fock truncation of `T` on `F_2^(N)`, with any multiplicity.** `e` becomes a projection of
  normalized rank `1/n`, which vanishes in the limit. This is (C3).
* **Circulant truncation**, with `s0 ->` cyclic shift and `t0 ->` its inverse. It is a unital ring
  map into `M_n(F_2)`, so Theorem A applies and `e -> 0`.
* **Regular representations of finite images of `EL_3(S)` for f.d.-represented `S ⊇ T`.**
  Theorem B.
* **Permutation models on vectors of truncated `T^3` or `R^3`.** These are Hamming models, excluded
  by (C4).
* **Surviving shape, no construction known:** faithful non-monomial rank models of `SL_fin(F_2)`
  whose transvection displacement persists after extension over the compressor pair. This is the
  open negative form of `rank-models-of-el3-satisfy-the-two-root-identities`.
