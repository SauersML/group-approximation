## 0. Summary

Part 2 proved that every monomial HS model of `W` (block size `1`) kills
`K_even`.  Its only block-size-one input was that conjugating a diagonal by a
monomial acts by the permutation part.  For block size `k >= 2` this fails:
a block-monomial rotates inside blocks.

The repair is a finite atom cocycle.  Let `A` be an abelian normal subgroup
with trivial permutation part.  After a Kadison diagonalization inside the
blocks, `rho(A)` sits in `Z ⊗ D_k`, where `Z` is the diagonal ultraproduct over
the block index set.  Conjugation by `rho(g)` then permutes the atoms of
`rho(A)` inside each block.  Ordering each atom canonically turns this into an
`S_k`-valued cocycle over the permutation part.  The result is a genuine
sofic-type covariant pair on `Y × [k]`.

**Theorem M_k.**  The transport lemma of part 2 holds for every fixed block
size, provided the soft element lies in an abelian normal soft subgroup.

**Theorem 9.**  For every `k`, every HS model of `W` in `U(k) wr S_n` kills
`K_even`.  So `W` has no regular HS model in `U(k) wr S_n` for any fixed `k`.
Through `coarse-frame-normalizers-are-near-block-monomial`, no regular HS
microstates of `W` approximately permute an equal-rank decomposition of
bounded rank.  This is the fence already known for the `q = 2` double.

Unbounded block size stays universal (part 1 of the frame-extraction
artifact, §0 item 5), so this is the exact threshold.

## 1. Setting

A block-monomial model is a homomorphism
`rho : H -> prod_omega (U(k) wr S_(n_m))` with `k` fixed.  It acts on
`C^(n_m) ⊗ C^k`.  Put

```text
𝔅 = prod_omega (l^inf_(n_m) ⊗ M_k) = M_k(Z),        Z = prod_omega l^inf_(n_m),
```

the block-diagonal ultraproduct and its center.  `pi_omega` is the permutation
part, a homomorphism by (HC) of part 1, and `psi = pi_omega o rho`.  If
`psi(a) = 1`, then `rho(a) in U(𝔅)` by (HC).  Conjugation by a block-monomial
`(u; sigma)` preserves `𝔅`, acts on `Z` by `sigma`, and preserves the
center-valued trace `tau_Z = id ⊗ tr_k`.

## 2. The atom cocycle

**Lemma 10.**  Let `A <= H` be a countable abelian normal subgroup with
`psi(A) = 1`, and let `G <= H`.  There are a unitary `V in 𝔅`, a homomorphism
`sigma~ : G -> prod_omega Sym(Y_(n_m) × [k])` and a trace-preserving normal
embedding `theta : C -> D_omega(Y × [k])` of `C = W*(V rho(A) V^*, Z)` such that

```text
sigma~(g) theta(c) sigma~(g)^* = theta( V rho(g) V^* c V rho(g)^* V^* )     (g in G, c in C).   (ACOV)
```

*Proof.*
- *Diagonalize.*  `W*(rho(A))` is abelian and countably generated, hence
  generated by one self-adjoint `x in M_k(Z)`.  By Kadison's diagonalization
  theorem (R. V. Kadison, *Diagonalizing matrices*, Amer. J. Math. 106 (1984)
  1451--1468: a normal element of `M_k` over an abelian von Neumann algebra is
  unitarily equivalent to a diagonal one), there is a unitary `V in M_k(Z)`
  with `V x V^*` diagonal.  Then `V W*(rho(A)) V^* <= Z ⊗ D_k`.  `V` lifts to
  block-diagonal unitaries at finite stages, so `rho' = V rho V^*` is again a
  block-monomial model.  Replace `rho` by `rho'`.  Now
  `C = W*(rho(A), Z) <= Z ⊗ D_k = D_omega(Y × [k])`.  Let `theta` be this
  inclusion; it is trace-preserving.
- *Atoms.*  For `j, j' in [k]` let `z_(j~j')` be the infimum over `a in A` of
  the projections `[rho(a)_j = rho(a)_(j')]` in `Z`.  For a partition `Π` of
  `[k]` let `z_Π in Z` be the projection where the relation `~` equals `Π`.
  These partition `1`.  On `z_Π`, `C` consists of the elements of `Z ⊗ D_k`
  constant on the blocks of `Π`.  Indeed `C ⊇ Z`, and countably many
  `±`-valued, or bounded, functions separate the blocks of `Π` pointwise, so
  they generate all fibrewise-constant functions over `Z`.
- *Automorphisms.*  `alpha_g = Ad rho(g)` maps `C` onto `C`, because `A` is
  normal, and restricts to `psi(g)` on `Z`.  It maps relatively minimal
  projections of `C` over `Z` to relatively minimal ones, and it preserves
  `tau_Z` up to `psi(g)`.  So it induces, on each `z_Π`, a rank-preserving
  bijection `beta_g` from the blocks of `Π` onto the blocks of the partition at
  `psi(g)` of that point.  The data `(Π, beta_g)` take finitely many values,
  on projections of `Z`.
- *Canonical order.*  Let `tau_g in Z ⊗ C[S_k]` be the `S_k`-valued element
  that sends the `t`-th smallest index of a block `b` to the `t`-th smallest
  index of `beta_g(b)`.  Order-preserving bijections compose, and
  `alpha_(gg') = alpha_g alpha_(g')`.  So

  ```text
  tau_(g g') = (psi(g')^(-1) . tau_g) · tau_(g')   in Z ⊗ C[S_k],
  that is,  tau_(g g')(y) = tau_g(psi(g') y) o tau_(g')(y),
  ```

  as an identity between finitely many projections of `Z`.
- *Lift.*  Finitely-valued elements of `Z` lift to internal functions
  `Y_(n_m) -> S_k`.  Put
  `sigma~_m(g)(y, j) = (psi_m(g) y, tau_(g,m)(y)(j))`, where `psi_m(g)` is
  the permutation part of a representative.  Hamming defects of products are
  bounded by those of `psi` plus the measure where the cocycle identity fails
  at stage `m`.  Both tend to `0` along `omega`, so `sigma~` is a homomorphism.
- *Covariance.*  For `c in C` constant on the blocks of `Π`,
  `(sigma~(g) . c)(psi(g) y, tau_g(j)) = c(y, j)`.  This is the value of `c` on
  the block `b` of `j`.  `alpha_g(c)` takes the same value on `beta_g(b)`,
  which contains `tau_g(j)`.  So (ACOV) holds.  ∎

## 3. Transport and the wreath

**Theorem M_k.**  Let `Lambda <= G <= H` with `Lambda` and `G` Kazhdan,
`Lambda` infranormal in `G`, and `G` sofic.  Let `rho` be a block-monomial
model of `H` with fixed block size `k`, and `A <= H` an abelian normal
subgroup with `psi(A) = 1`.  If `a in A` commutes with `Lambda`, then
`rho(n a n^(-1)) = rho(a)` for every `n in <<Lambda>>_G`.

*Proof.*  Take `V`, `sigma~`, `theta` from Lemma 10 (with `rho` replaced by
`V rho V^*`, which does not change the conclusion).  `b = theta(rho(a))` is
fixed by `sigma~(Lambda)` by (ACOV), since `rho(l a l^(-1)) = rho(a)`.  Tensor
`sigma~` with a sofic representation of `G`, as in part 2, Theorem M.  Part 2 of
`normal-closure-fixes-gamma-fixed-algebra` makes `<<Lambda>>_G` fix `b ⊗ 1`.
By (ACOV) and injectivity of `theta`, `rho(n a n^(-1)) = alpha_n(rho(a)) = rho(a)`.  ∎

**Theorem 9.**  For every fixed `k`, every homomorphism
`rho : W -> prod_omega (U(k) wr S_n)` kills `K_even`.

*Proof.*  `A = K_even` is abelian and normal, and `psi(K_even) = 1` by part 1,
Lemma 1.  Run the proof of part 2, Theorem 7, with Theorem M_k in place of
Theorem M.  The deep congruence conjugates of part 2, Lemma 6, give `Lambda`
fixing `Gamma` and `h' Gamma`, so `a = e_Gamma + e_(h' Gamma)` commutes with
`Lambda`.  Then `f(h) = rho(e_Gamma + e_(h Gamma))` is a homomorphism on the
perfect group `N`, and conjugation by `G` finishes.  ∎

**Corollary 11.**
(a) `W` has no regular HS model in `U(k) wr S_n` for any fixed `k`.
(b) Let `F <= W` be a finite window, `delta > 0`, and `C^d = ⊕ V_i` an
    equal-rank decomposition of bounded rank `k`.  Microstates of `W` whose
    generators have `def_B <= delta` for `B = span{p_i}` are within
    `2 sqrt(3) delta` of `U(k) wr S_n` (`coarse-frame-normalizers-are-near-block-monomial`).
    For small `delta` along exhausting windows they would assemble into a
    regular bounded-block model, which (a) forbids.
(c) The same holds for every lamp quotient `W/U`, `U <= K_even`, as in part 2,
    Corollary 8(b).

## 4. Scope

- Unbounded block size is universal: `n` diagonal copies of any microstate.
  Lemma 10 breaks exactly there, since `M_(k_m)(Z)` with `k_m -> infinity` is
  not `M_k` over an abelian algebra, and atoms can be diffuse.
- The abelian normal hypothesis on `A` is used twice: to diagonalize
  `rho(A)` simultaneously, and to make `Ad rho(g)` preserve `C`.  For `W`,
  `A = K_even`.
- The trust surface of arXiv:2608.06222 travels through
  `normal-closure-fixes-gamma-fixed-algebra`.
