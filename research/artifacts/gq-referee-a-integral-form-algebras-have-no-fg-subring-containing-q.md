# Referee report (gq-referee-a, proof-gap lens): integral-form algebras have no finitely generated subring containing Q

**Reviewed:** `integral-form-algebras-have-no-fg-subring-containing-q` and its route `-proof` (lane
gq-ring-fp-simple, 7bbbef172), read on origin/main.

**Verdict: PASS for the theorem.** The theorem covers rings `A` whose additive group is free abelian.
One item of the "Covered algebras" list is asserted, not proved (§2).

## 1. The proof
- **Step 1.** Correct. The structure constants are integral, so `S ⊆ M_n(A ⊗ D)`, and this is a free
  `D`-module, with `D` generated by finitely many coefficients.
- **Step 2.** Correct. If every prime were a unit of `D`, then `Q ⊆ D`. A residue field `D/m` would then
  be a field that is finitely generated as a ring, hence finite by the Nullstellensatz for `Z`-algebras.
  But it contains `Q`. In characteristic `p`, a copy of `Q` is impossible.
- **Step 3.** Correct. `D` is a Noetherian domain and `pD` is proper, so Krull's intersection theorem
  gives `⋂ p^k D = 0`, and the same holds coefficientwise in a free module.
- **Step 4.** Correct. `e = ι(1) = p^k ι(p^{−k}) ∈ p^k F` for every `k`, so `e = 0`. This works for
  non-unital copies too.

## 2. The covered list (non-blocking, but should be fixed)
- **Items with known normal-form `Z`-bases.** These follow from the theorem as stated:
  - group rings;
  - Leavitt and Leavitt path algebras;
  - Cohn and Toeplitz algebras;
  - Weyl algebras;
  - Steinberg algebras.
- **The item "Leavitt–Nekrashevych completions and crossed products whose defining data are integral"
  needs an extra argument.** The theorem applies only once the `Z`-form `O_ψ(B)` is known to have free
  abelian additive group. Its core is a direct limit `lim M_{d^n}(B)`, and direct limits of free abelian
  groups need not be free: `Z →^2 Z →^2 ⋯` has limit `Z[1/2]`.
  - Freeness holds if each connecting map `Ψ_n` has torsion-free, and hence free, cokernel. That is true
    for `B = Z` and the Leavitt case.
  - It is not shown for general integral `ψ`.
  - Either prove it (for instance by showing each `Ψ_n` is split injective over `Z`), or restrict the
    item to that case.
- **The consequence sentence** about combinatorial Leavitt, group and Steinberg algebras is unaffected.
