## Quantifier correction

The unrestricted corner criterion is mathematically correct, but calling it
a universal compiler hides a circularity.  If a nonhyperlinear group already
exists, choose its own non-CE group von Neumann algebra, the unit corner, and
the identity embedding.  Conversely, any such corner embedding gives the
group immediately.  Therefore the unrestricted existence statement is
equivalent to the desired theorem.

The constructive target in Cairn is deliberately stronger: fix the explicit
non-RU BCS algebra before choosing the host, or give a finite algebraic
group-corner map/central-sign energy decoder.  Those requirements prevent the
source algebra from being chosen retrospectively as `L(G)`.
