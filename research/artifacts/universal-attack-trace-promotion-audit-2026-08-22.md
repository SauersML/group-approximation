## Quantifier correction

The unrestricted corner criterion is mathematically correct, but calling it
a universal compiler hides a circularity.  If a nonhyperlinear group already
exists, choose its own non-CE group von Neumann algebra, the unit corner, and
the identity embedding.  Conversely, any such corner embedding gives the
group immediately.  Therefore the unrestricted existence statement is
equivalent to the desired theorem.

The constructive target in Cairn is deliberately stronger: fix the explicit
non-RU BCS algebra before choosing the host, or give a finite algebraic
group-corner map/central-sign energy decoder.  Those requirements prevent the
source algebra from being chosen retrospectively as `L(G)`.

## Net status

Compactness removes adaptive dimension schedules, and canonical finite
sectors remove multiplicity guessing.  Neither operation promotes an
arbitrary trace.  The remaining live operation is still finite and static:
put the fixed non-CE BCS energy into one canonically weighted group-word
carrier with a dimension-independent normalized-HS payment.

The exact Bernoulli theorem is a useful positive benchmark, but its exactness
is load-bearing.  In normalized-HS microstates the lamp windows yield finite
weighted spectral atoms and approximately normalizing actor matrices, not a
single coherent finite permutation action.  Entrywise squared block norms
lose phases under composition, so independent Birkhoff rounding does not
repair multiplication.  This is the invariant Cartan-completion gate, not a
dimension or convergence-rate problem.

The report's two proposed universal endpoints must also remain separate.
`uniform-central-sign-gap-implies-nonhyperlinear` is a genuine sufficient
criterion once a fixed `delta_0>0` is proved.  By contrast, unrestricted
existence of a non-CE algebra inside an arbitrary group-factor corner is
already equivalent to the root goal.  A noncircular compiler must fix its
source algebra in advance (the explicit non-RU BCS source is the current
choice) and deliver either an algebraic corner map or ordinary-word energy
payment.
