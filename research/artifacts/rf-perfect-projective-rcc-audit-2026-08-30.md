# Residual-finite perfect-pair projective/RCC audit

Date: 2026-08-30.

## Outcome

For a finitely presented residually finite group `A=<X|R>`, a finitely
generated perfect subgroup `C=<S>`, and `A=<C,h>` perfect, the following are
equivalent:

1. the dimension-free all-assignment estimate
   `e_h <= L(q_C+delta)`;
2. exclusion of projective `C`-central / non-projective-`h` leaks in every
   tracial matrix ultraproduct;
3. ordinary relative-commutant collapse for regular-trace matrix-ultraproduct
   representations.

The equivalence of 1 and 2 is
`projective-transfer-linear-bound-equals-no-ultraproduct-leak`.  The new
point is 2 iff 3: perfectness removes both scalar-character branches and
residual-finite tensor camouflage removes the trace-profile restriction.

## Exact camouflage identities

Enumerate `A\{1}={g_1,g_2,...}` and choose finite quotients
`q_n:A->Q_n` separating `g_1,...,g_n`.  If `lambda_n` is the regular
representation of `Q_n`, put

```text
rho_tilde_n(x)=rho_n(x) tensor lambda_n(q_n(x)),
u_tilde_n=u_n tensor I.
```

Because every relator maps to the identity in `Q_n`,

```text
rho_tilde_n(r)-I=(rho_n(r)-I) tensor I,
[u_tilde_n,rho_tilde_n(g)]=[u_n,rho_n(g)] tensor I.
```

Thus normalized Hilbert--Schmidt relator defect and every trace-square
commutator energy are preserved exactly.  Meanwhile, for fixed `g_j!=1`,

```text
tr(rho_tilde_n(g_j))
 =tr(rho_n(g_j)) tr(lambda_n(q_n(g_j)))=0
```

for `n>=j`.  The amplified ultraproduct character is therefore the regular
character.  This is why canonical trace is not an extra rigidity input for
this auxiliary-unitary problem.

## Phase audit

If `u pi(s)u^*=z_s pi(s)` on generators of a perfect group, conjugating each
fixed relation shows that `s|->z_s` descends to a character.  Perfectness
forces all `z_s=1`.  In the forward direction, once the source phase is
already trivial and `[u,pi(h)]` is scalar, `A=<C,h>` makes the scalar packet
a character of `A`; perfectness of `A` removes the target phase.  No
property-(T) estimate is needed for this logical equivalence.

## Arithmetic specialization

For `n>=3`, both `SL_n(Z)` and `SL_n(Z[1/p])` are perfect by elementary
commutator identities.  The latter is finitely presented and residually
finite and is generated by the integral subgroup together with the standard
denominator diagonal.  Hence the theorem applies to every higher-rank
arithmetic pair in `projective-commutant-transfer-for-arithmetic-pair`.
It does not cover `n=2`, where finite character branches remain genuine.

For the `SL_3`, `p=2` lane, the conclusion is sharp:
`projective-trace-square-transfer-for-sl3-pair` is equivalent to regular RCC
condition `(RC3)` isolated in `sl3-pair-relative-commutant-route`.  The rate
problem and the unrestricted trace-profile problem are not additional walls;
regular RCC is the sole remaining wall.

## Current-literature boundary

Kun--Thom, *Nonsofic wreath products of residually finite groups*,
arXiv:2608.06222 (2026), proves a permutation fixed-point/centralizer theorem
for infranormal Kazhdan pairs.  It does not settle this unitary RCC: as
`arithmetic-integral-subgroup-is-incompressible` records, equal covolume
forces every one-sided compressor of `SL_n(Z)` in `SL_n(Q)` to normalize it,
so the arithmetic pair is not infranormal.

Alekseev--Thom, *Centralizers of sofic approximations of Kazhdan groups*,
arXiv:2608.05362 (2026), is likewise permutation-specific: its cluster
component theorem has no stated normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt unitary analogue.
The recent Ulam-stability theorems for the hyperfinite II_1 factor and nuclear
C*-algebras concern amenable/nuclear objects and do not correct the
nonamenable regular arithmetic sector.

Accordingly no current primary theorem found in this audit proves or refutes
regular RCC for `SL_3(Z)<SL_3(Z[1/2])`.  The contribution here is the exact
quantifier equivalence, not a claimed solution of that root.
