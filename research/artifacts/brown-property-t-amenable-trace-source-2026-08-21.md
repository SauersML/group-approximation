# Brown's property-(T) amenable-trace obstruction

Source checked on 2026-08-21:

- Nathanial P. Brown, *Kazhdan's Property T and C*-algebras*,
  arXiv:math/0602312, especially Definition 5.3, Theorem 5.4, Corollaries
  5.5 and 5.6: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0602312>.
- Bachir Bekka and Chi-Keung Ng, *Property (T) for locally compact groups
  and C*-algebras*, arXiv:1803.10933, for property (T) of the full group
  C*-algebra: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10933>.

Brown's Corollary 5.6 states that a property-(T) C*-algebra has an amenable
trace exactly when it has a nonzero finite-dimensional quotient.  The paper
also explicitly notes that C*-property (T) passes to quotients.  Bekka--Ng
prove, for a locally compact group `G`, that `G` has property (T) iff its full
group C*-algebra does; the discrete case is the one used here.

No normalized/unnormalized norm conversion is being made: amenability of a
trace is natively characterized by ucp maps asymptotically multiplicative in
the normalized tracial 2-norm (Brown Theorem 5.4), and Corollary 5.5 is the
property-(T) repair to actual finite-dimensional star-homomorphisms.
