## 2. sk-draft-rich (448 lines)

**Checked, PASS.**
- **Toeplitz construction:**
  - [K_m:K_{m+1}] ≥ 4 is needed there, so that all three values occur on K_n for p_0, p_1, p_2;
  - minimality: choose m with Fσ ∩ K_m ⊆ {1}; the pattern recurs along K_m, or along the 1-coset of K_{m+1} when 1 ∈ Fσ.
- **Freeness through a transversal T of K_n:**
  - tσ and t'σ are chosen in the 1-coset and the 2-coset;
  - tσδ, t'σδ ∈ K_m, each in a coset different from its origin, and not both lie in K_{m+1}, because their origins are distinct cosets;
  - the one outside K_{m+1} changes value;
  - tσδ = tγσ.
- **Finite models:** p_c is constant on K_n-cosets, and the model on ⊕_c F_2^{Δ/K_n} is well defined. Distinct u_g separate because g'^{-1}g ∈ K_n ⟺ gg'^{-1} ∈ K_n (K_n normal).
- **Embedding:** the 3×3 Whitehead identity and the injective homomorphism.

**Defects.**
- **R1, notation error, l.144–147.** "$R^\omega$" and "$L(G)\mathbin{\bar\otimes}R$" use R, which l.89 defines as the ring LC(X,F_2)⋊Z. A reader parses R^ω as an ultrapower of the ring. Use `\mathcal R` with "where $\mathcal R$ is the hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor", as general and minimal do.
- **R2, l.62:** the credit fix from part 1 §1. The locator "[GM, Theorem 2.6]" is unverified by me and belongs to sk-panel-referee-2.
- **R3, l.72–74:** "every residually finite group acts on such a subshift" → "every infinite finitely generated residually finite group".
- **R4, l.266–278.** The Γ-replacement paragraph gives only half of "small": V ∩ gV = ∅. The product formula also needs every f∘a, |a| ≤ w, constant on V. Add that clause.
- **R5, l.119:** "occurs in $x$" → "occurs in $x_{[0,\infty)}$".

**Assessment.** It proves the same results as general, with the headline over Z, which is better to remember. The proof over Δ is a sketch (R4), and there is one notation error (R1).
