## 1. The theorem

**Theorem S.** Let `G ↷ (X, mu)` be a free p.m.p. action and `Λ <= G` an infinite subgroup. Let `F_Λ` be the
`G`-invariant sigma-algebra generated by all `Λ`-invariant Borel sets and their `G`-translates. Then the outer
Rokhlin entropy `h_G(F_Λ)` is `0`. If `X` is ergodic, then `h^Rok_G(X) = h_G(X | F_Λ)`.

*Proof.*
1. **The subspace.** Let `H` be the closed linear span of `{ρ(g) ξ : g in G, ξ in L²_0(X)^Λ}`, where
   `L²_0` is the orthocomplement of the constants and `L²_0(X)^Λ` is the space of `Λ`-invariant vectors.
   `H` is `ρ(G)`-invariant.
2. **It is singular with `λ`.** Suppose `σ <= H` is a nonzero subrepresentation that embeds into `λ`. Let
   `P` be the orthogonal projection onto `σ`, which commutes with `ρ(G)`.
   - For `ξ` in `L²_0(X)^Λ` and `t` in `Λ`, `ρ(t) P ξ = P ρ(t) ξ = P ξ`. So `Pξ` is a `Λ`-invariant vector of
     `σ`.
   - Under the embedding it becomes a `Λ`-invariant vector of `ℓ²(G)`. Such a vector is constant on the
     right cosets `Λg`, which are infinite, so it is `0`. Hence `Pξ = 0`.
   - Then `P ρ(g) ξ = ρ(g) P ξ = 0` for every generator of `H`, so `P = 0` on `H` and `σ = 0`.
   - In the other direction, a nonzero subrepresentation of `λ` embedding into `ρ|_H` gives a nonzero
     subrepresentation of `ρ|_H` that embeds into `λ`, which was just excluded. So `ρ|_H` is singular with
     `λ`.
3. **Zero outer entropy.** For a `Λ`-invariant set `B`, the function `1_B - mu(B)` lies in `L²_0(X)^Λ ⊆ H`.
   So `B` and all its `G`-translates are `σ-alg(H)`-measurable, and `F_Λ ⊆ σ-alg(H)`. By (K),
   `h_G(σ-alg(H)) = 0`, and outer entropy is monotone in the collection of sets, so `h_G(F_Λ) = 0`.
4. **Ergodic case.** By `seward-rokhlin-entropy-subadditivity`,
   `h^Rok_G(X) <= h_G(F_Λ) + h_G(X | F_Λ) = h_G(X | F_Λ) <= h^Rok_G(X)`. QED

**Model test.**
- **The trivial model fails.** For finite `Λ` the conclusion fails already for the Bernoulli 2-shift over
  `Z × Z/2` with `Λ = Z/2`: the `Λ`-invariant sets generate a factor of Rokhlin entropy at least that of
  the Bernoulli 4-shift over `Z` modulo a swap, which is positive.
- **Real objects pass.** For every infinite `Λ`, the uniform Bernoulli shift over `G` has no nonconstant
  `Λ`-invariant functions, because its restriction to `Λ` is mixing. So the theorem says nothing about it,
  which is consistent with maximality.

## 2. Corollaries

**S1 (normal subgroups).** An infinite normal subgroup `N` acting trivially on a factor `Y` of a free action
makes every `Y`-set `N`-invariant. So `h_G(Y) = 0`. This recovers the free case of Alpeev–Seward,
Theorem 1.11(ii) (`alpeev-seward-stabilizer-growth-kills-outer-entropy`), and with it
`factors-fixed-by-large-finite-subgroups-have-zero-outer-entropy`.

**S2 (Kun–Thom rigidity is invisible to Rokhlin entropy).** Let `Γ < G` be a Theorem E pair.
- **What sofic actions do.** Kun–Thom Theorem C (`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath`) says that in every sofic
  p.m.p. `G`-action, `L^∞(X)^Γ` is `G`-invariant. The information separating the two is what makes
  non-sofic actions non-sofic.
- **What Theorem S says.** `Γ` is infinite, so in every free p.m.p. `G`-action, sofic or not, the
  sigma-algebra generated by the `Γ`-invariant sets has zero outer Rokhlin entropy.
- **Over the wreath.** The same holds in every free `W`-action for information fixed by the invisible lamps
  `N_0 = Rad_sof(W)`, or by any infinite subgroup.

**S3 (generalized Bernoulli shifts).** Let `Λ <= G` be infinite, `(K, κ)` nontrivial, and suppose
`G ↷ (K, κ)^(G/Λ)` is essentially free. Then its Rokhlin entropy is `0`.
- *Proof.* The coordinate at the base coset `Λ` is `Λ`-invariant, and its `G`-translates are all the
  coordinates. So `F_Λ = B(X)`, and Theorem S gives `h^Rok = h_G(F_Λ) = 0`.
- For the Kun–Thom pair this is the action of Corollary D, a free action of a residually finite Kazhdan
  group with no sofic model. Its Rokhlin entropy is `0`, whenever the action is essentially free. The
  finite central scalar matrices of `EL_r(R)` lie in `Γ` and act trivially, so freeness should be checked
  modulo that center.

**S4 (the affine lamp action).** `W = B^(X) ⋊ G` acts on the compact group `B^X` with Haar measure by
`(n, g).ω = n + g.ω`.
- The coordinate `ω(x_0)` is invariant under the infinite subgroup `B^(X \ {x_0}) ⋊ Stab_G(x_0)`, and its
  translates generate everything.
- So whenever this action is essentially free, its Rokhlin entropy is `0`.

## 3. Reading for the root questions

- **The principle.** Positive Rokhlin entropy lives only on information that no infinite subgroup fixes.
  - Symmetry under an infinite subgroup is exactly what makes information free in Rokhlin entropy.
  - The uniform Bernoulli shift, whose restriction to every infinite subgroup is mixing, is the extreme
    case where nothing is fixed.
  - This is one precise sense in which information and symmetry are dual.
- **Consequence for lower bounds over nonsofic groups.** Every nonsoficity mechanism in the graph works
  through information fixed by an infinite subgroup: invariant vectors of Kazhdan subgroups, Γ-invariant
  functions in Theorem C, invisible lamps.
  - Theorem S says all of that information has zero outer entropy.
  - So no lower bound can be extracted from those invariant layers. A lower bound must be a statement about
    information that every infinite subgroup moves: about mixing, iid-like parts of an action, where
    Koopman data is `λ^⊕N` and blind (`koopman-lambda-singular-part-has-zero-rokhlin-entropy`).
- **Consequence for deficits.** A compression built from invariant layers alone cannot lower the entropy of
  a Bernoulli shift, because a Bernoulli shift has no invariant layer. A deficit must move information
  between mixing parts.
