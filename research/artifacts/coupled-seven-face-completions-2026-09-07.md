# Coupled completions of the seven-face cancellation seed

Handwritten constructions and calculations, 2026-09-07. The explicit
completion below has universal unsigned group C2 x D8, of order 16.
It is an excluded candidate, not a direct-finiteness counterexample.
The last section proves a separate restriction on every completion
within the prescribed XOR fibers.

## Presentation reduction

First use only the seven seeds and F0,F1. Write

    x=X_0,   a=X_2,   b=Y_2,   c=Y_4,   d=Y_5.

S5, S6, S3, S4, S2, and S7 respectively give

    X_3=xd,             X_1=ad,
    X_5=abd^(-1),       Y_3=bd^(-1)c,
    X_4=xdbd^(-1),      Y_0=dbd^(-1)c.

Under these substitutions S1 becomes an identity. F1 gives

    Y_1=x^(-1)ad^2bd^(-1)c.

F0 then reads

    adx^(-1)ad^2bd^(-1)c = xdbd^(-1)c.

Cancel on the right by bd^(-1)c and then by d. This gives
adx^(-1)ad=x. Define h=x^(-1)ad; the resulting relation is h^2=1,
and a=xhd^(-1). The complete list of generators has become

    X_0=x,                  Y_0=dbd^(-1)c,
    X_1=xh,                 Y_1=hdbd^(-1)c,
    X_2=xhd^(-1),            Y_2=b,
    X_3=xd,                 Y_3=bd^(-1)c,
    X_4=xdbd^(-1),           Y_4=c,
    X_5=xhd^(-1)bd^(-1),     Y_5=d,
    X_6=1,                  Y_6=1.

At this stage x,b,c,d are unrestricted and h is an involution; every
substitution is reversible.

T2a gives xb=xdbd^(-1), so b and d commute. T2b gives
x d h b c=c, hence x=b^(-1) h d^(-1).

Using this commutation, T5a becomes

    xh d^(-1)b d^(-1)b c=xh c,

which is equivalent to b^2=d^2. T5b becomes

    bd^(-1)=xbhb.

Substitute x=b^(-1)hd^(-1), multiply on the left by b and on the
right by b^(-1), and use [b,d]=1. The result is

    bd^(-1)=hbd^(-1)h.

Set k=bd^(-1). Then k^2=1 and k commutes with d and h. Conversely,
b=kd and x=k d^(-1) h d^(-1) recover all four preceding relations.

Next T7a is Y_1=X_5b. Since b^2=d^2 and [b,d]=1, its right side
simplifies to xh. Its left side is hbc=khdc. Thus

    hdc=d^(-1)hd^(-1)h,
    c=(d^(-1)h)^3.

Put w=d^(-1)h, so d=hw^(-1). All generators are now

    X = (kw^2h, kw^2, kw^3h, kw, w, w^3h, 1),
    Y = (khw^2, kw^2, khw^(-1), kw^3, w^3, hw^(-1), 1).

The remaining relation T7b holds identically: its two products are
kw^4. Up to this point the presentation is exactly

    <w,h,k | h^2=k^2=1, [k,w]=[k,h]=1>.

Now T3a reads

    w^2 h w^3 = w h w^2.

Multiplication on the left by w^(-1) and on the right by w^(-2)
gives whw=h, equivalently hwh=w^(-1). T3b and T3c follow from this
relation: their two products are respectively w^3h and wh. The
three T4 relations also follow, with respective common products
kw^(-1)h, kw^3h, and kwh.

Finally T6a is kw^2h=khw^2, which is equivalent to w^4=1 after
using hwh=w^(-1). T6b and T6c already have equal products, kh and
kw^2h respectively. We have accounted for every relation and obtain

    Gamma = <w,h,k | w^4=h^2=k^2=1,
                         hwh=w^(-1), [k,w]=[k,h]=1>.

Thus Gamma is C2 x D8, where D8 has order eight. In particular the
reduction is a presentation identification, not only a surjection
onto a finite model.

## Independent model and product checks

For an explicit verification of the reverse presentation map, take
D8=<w,h | w^4=h^2=1,hwh=w^(-1)> and an independent central involution
k. The row and column labels, now with exponents reduced, are

    X = (kw^2h, kw^2, kw^3h, kw, w, w^3h, 1),
    Y = (kw^2h, kw^2, kwh, kw^3, w^3, wh, 1).

Each list has seven distinct elements. The following common products
check all 24 pairs directly; one can multiply using

    (k^e w^a h^b)(k^f w^c h^d)
        = k^(e+f) w^(a+(-1)^b c) h^(b+d).

| Pair | Common product | Pair | Common product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | 1 | S2 | w^2h |
| S3 | w^2 | S4 | h |
| S5 | kw | S6 | kw^2 |
| S7 | w | F0 | 1 |
| F1 | h | T2a | w |
| T2b | w^3 | T3a | w^3h |
| T3b | w^3h | T3c | wh |
| T4a | kw^3h | T4b | kw^3h |
| T4c | kwh | T5a | kw |
| T5b | kw^3 | T6a | kw^2h |
| T6b | kh | T6c | kw^2h |
| T7a | kw^2 | T7b | k |

These labels generate the full group: w=X_4, h=X_0^(-1)X_1, and
k=X_3X_4^(-1). This independently rules out an unintended smaller
group in the presentation calculation.

In F2[Gamma], set B=sum_i [X_i] and A=sum_j [Y_j]. The table gives
BA=1. The algebra has dimension 16 over F2, so multiplication by A
is an injective linear endomorphism with left inverse multiplication
by B. Finite dimension makes it bijective, and consequently AB=1.
Thus this coupled completion genuinely changes the universal group
from the earlier cube of order eight, but still has zero reverse
defect in its universal group algebra.

## Every completion within these XOR fibers has finite 2-group abelianization

Here the seven seeds and pivot are fixed, but all remaining pairs
may be changed within their XOR-product fibers. F0 and F1 are forced:
after removing the seeds and pivot, their two cells are the only
remaining cells in fibers 0 and 1 respectively.

Let K be any field of characteristic different from two. Consider a
homomorphism from the unsigned universal group of any such completion
to the additive group of K. Denote its row and column values by
r_i and s_j, with r_6=s_6=0. All matching equations become equality
of the corresponding row-plus-column values.

The seven seeds give

    r_3=r_0+s_5,             r_1=r_2+s_5,
    r_5=r_2+s_2-s_5,         s_3=s_2+s_4-s_5,
    r_4=r_0+s_2,             s_0=s_2+s_4.

F1 gives s_1=r_1+s_0-r_0. Substitution into F0 gives
2r_0=2r_2+2s_5, hence r_0=r_2+s_5. Put

    a=r_2,   b=s_2,   c=s_4,   d=s_5.

The two lists of additive values are therefore

    r=(a+d,a+d,a,a+2d,a+b+d,a+b-d,0),
    s=(b+c,b+c,b,b+c-d,c,d,0).

We now use only the requirement that each residual fiber admits a
pairing with equal values. If four values are (u,v,u,w), such a
pairing forces v=w, regardless of which pairing was selected: the
two occurrences of u contribute even multiplicity, so every other
value must still occur with even multiplicity.

In fiber 2, the remaining cells are 02,31,46,64. Their values are

    (a+b+d, a+b+c+2d, a+b+d, c).

Thus a+b+2d=0. Substitute b=-a-2d. In fiber 5 the remaining cells
50,63,14,41 then have values

    (-a+c-5d, -a+c-3d, a+c+d, -a+c-3d).

The same even-multiplicity argument gives 2a+6d=0, so a=-3d and
b=d. In fiber 7 the remaining cells 61,52,34,43 now have values

    (c+d, -2d, c-d, c-d).

Consequently c=-3d. Finally all six cells of fiber 3, ordered as
03,30,12,21,56,65, have values

    (-5d, -3d, -d, -5d, -3d, d).

The displayed duplicate pairs already contribute even multiplicity.
The remaining values -d and d must therefore agree. As 2 is
invertible in K, this forces d=0, and then a=b=c=0. Every generator
has zero image.

This holds over Q and over every finite prime field F_p for odd p.
The abelianization is finitely generated; the Q calculation rules
out a free abelian summand, and the F_p calculations rule out every
odd-primary torsion summand. Therefore the abelianization of every
such unsigned completion is a finite 2-group.

This last conclusion does not make all these universal groups finite
or amenable. It only shows that infinitude cannot be certified by a
nonzero map to Z, and that odd torsion cannot survive in their
abelianizations. The order-sixteen classification above applies to
its explicitly specified coupled completion.
