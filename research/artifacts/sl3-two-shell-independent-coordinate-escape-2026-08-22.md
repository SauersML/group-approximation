# The 42-point shell Laplacian does not select diagonal chart matching

Take the common 42-point Hecke sphere `Omega` and let `SL_3(Z)` act
diagonally on `Omega times Omega`.  The first- and second-coordinate fibers
give two complete 42-atom PVMs.  Both are exactly covariant under the same
actor representation, so every fixed shell Dirichlet form and every lattice
root/Weyl relator has zero defect.  All chart-internal first exits vanish.

The corresponding atoms intersect in only one of `42^2` coordinate cells.
Consequently their authentication deficit is exactly `41/42`.

Tensoring with regular representations of increasingly deep
`SL_3(Z/p^a)` quotients gives the canonical lattice character locally while
leaving this deficit unchanged.  Therefore the fixed 42-point spectral gap
controls distance to the full diagonal-action commutant, whose orbital
kernel includes independent-coordinate sectors; it does not project onto
the desired diagonal matching.

One extra denominator incidence is indispensable.  It must distinguish the
diagonal orbit from off-diagonal orbitals, equivalently compare two decoded
occurrences of the same building vertex through a raw denominator return.
The construction is not a representation of the ambient arithmetic group
and says nothing about uniformly noncorrectable lattice microstates.
