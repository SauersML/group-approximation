```latex
\begin{corollary}\label{cor:universal}
Every countable LEF group is a subgroup of an infinite finitely generated
simple Kazhdan LEF group. One such group contains every recursively
presented finitely generated LEF group, for instance every finitely
presented residually finite group and every $G_Y$ whose language is
recursive.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Let $\Gamma=\{\gamma_1,\gamma_2,\dots\}$ be countable and LEF, replaced
by $\Gamma\times\Z$ so that it is infinite. There are finite groups
$Q_k$ and maps $\varphi_k\colon\Gamma\to Q_k$ that are injective and
multiplicative on $\{1,\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_k\}$. Left multiplication by
$\varphi_k(\gamma)$ on $Q_k\times\{1,2\}$ is an even permutation
$\pi_k(\gamma)$, so for $j\le k$ there are permutations
$\alpha_{jk},\beta_{jk}$ with $\pi_k(\gamma_j)=[\alpha_{jk},\beta_{jk}]$
by Ore's theorem~\cite{Ore}. On $X_k=Q_k\times\{1,2\}\times\Z/4^{k+1}$
let $b_k$ add $1$ to the last coordinate, and let $a_k$ act on the level
$i$ by $\alpha_{jk}$ if $i=2^{2j-1}$ and by $\beta_{jk}$ if $i=4^j$, for
$j\le k$, and trivially on the other levels. Then $b_k^{-n}a_kb_k^n$ acts
on the level $i$ as $a_k$ acts on the level $i+n$. As distinct pairs of
powers of $2$ have distinct differences, all below a quarter of the
modulus, the word
\[
  w_j=\bigl[b^{-2^{2j-1}}ab^{2^{2j-1}},\,b^{-4^j}ab^{4^j}\bigr]
\]
evaluated at $(a_k,b_k)$ acts by $\pi_k(\gamma_j)$ on the level $0$ and
trivially on the other levels, for $j\le k$. In an algebraic ultraproduct
$\prod_\omega\mathrm{Sym}(X_k)$, sending $\gamma$ to the class of
$(\pi_k(\gamma)$ on the level $0)_k$ embeds $\Gamma$ in the group
generated by $a=(a_k)_k$ and $b=(b_k)_k$, since $w_j(a,b)$ is the image of
$\gamma_j$. Finitely many products and inequalities in the ultraproduct
hold in some coordinate, so this $2$-generated group is LEF, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:lef} embeds it in an infinite finitely generated simple
Kazhdan LEF group. For the second statement, apply this to the restricted
direct sum of representatives of the countably many recursively presented
finitely generated LEF groups. The groups $G_Y$ with recursive language
have solvable word problems by Corollary~\ref{cor:wp}. No countable group
contains all finitely generated LEF groups, since there are continuum many
groups $G_X$ and a countable group has countably many finitely generated
subgroups.
\end{proof}
```
