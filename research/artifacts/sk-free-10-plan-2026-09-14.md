# sk-free-10 plan: GL_3 over the subshift ring is G_X ⋊ Z, its normal subgroups, and outer automorphisms of G_X (2026-09-14)

Lane sk-free-10, a free lane of wave 12. R = R_X = LC(X,F_2) ⋊_T Z for an infinite minimal Cantor system, G_X = EL_3(R).

## Choice and why
What exists on main:
- The half-orbit Fredholm index ind: GL_n(R) → Z is a surjective homomorphism that vanishes on EL_n(R). It induces K_1(R) ≅ Z (sk-open-4 Theorem I), and ind(diag(u,1,1)) = −1.
- sk-free-6 Theorem D (PASS twice, sk-verify-15/16): every element of G_X is a product of ≤ 78 elementary matrices, via Kakutani–Rokhlin towers.
- The unstable question has been open: is ker(ind|_{GL_3}) = EL_3(R), i.e. does GL_3(R)/EL_3(R) → K_1(R) inject? sk-free-2's C2 made it conditional on sr(R) = 1. sk-algebraic-putnam reduced sr = 1 to the exchange property, which is still open.

Key observation: Theorem D's proof uses h ∈ G in ONE place, the vanishing of the half-line index at each cut (T2), which feeds dim ker = dim coker in the end correction (T3).
- Every other step uses only h ∈ GL_3(R) and the band width of h and h^{-1}: towers, local types, block Gauss reduction over ∏M_h(F_2), localization, and the second factor.
- The half-line index at (x, c) is ind(h): locally constant in x, invariant under moving the cut (finite-rank change), hence constant by minimality.

## Targets
**Theorem O1 (unconditional).**
- ker(ind: GL_3(R) → Z) = EL_3(R) = G_X, and every index-0 element is a product of ≤ 78 elementary matrices.
- So GL_3(R) = G_X ⋊ ⟨diag(u,1,1)⟩, GL_3(R)/EL_3(R) ≅ K_1(R) ≅ Z, and G_X = [GL_3(R), GL_3(R)].
- The unstable K_1 at n = 3 equals the stable one without any stable-rank input. This is the algebraic counterpart of the fact, which comes from Putnam's tsr 1 theorem, that GL(C(X)⋊Z) has K_1 = Z.

**Theorem O2.** The normal subgroups of GL_3(R) are exactly 1 and ind^{-1}(mZ), m ≥ 0.
- Proof: the paper's simplicity proof applies verbatim to subgroups normalized by G_X: [g,h] ∈ K when g ∈ K and h ∈ G, the tower copy H_V, and the level ideal. Then use O1.
- No Stepanov or stable-rank import.

**Theorem O3 (lower bound on Out(G_X)).**
- The centralizer of G_X in GL_3(R) is trivial, so GL_3(R)/G_X ≅ Z embeds in Out(G_X). The class of inn(diag(u,1,1)) has infinite order.
- Ring automorphisms α_φ from the automorphism group Aut(X,T) of the subshift give (Z × Aut(X,T))/⟨(−3,T)⟩ ↪ Out(G_X), using α_T = inn(u I_3) and ind(uI_3) = −3.
- Firewall: the upper bound (all automorphisms of this form, up to flips and transpose-inverse) needs standardness of automorphisms. That is owned by the live lane sk-normalizer-recognition, so not claimed here.

**Model tests.**
- diag(u,u,1) has index −2 and is not in G_X.
- diag(u,u^{-1},1) has index 0 and lies in G_X (Whitehead).
- Sturmian X: Aut(X,T) = ⟨T⟩, so the O3 subgroup is Z.
- Toeplitz X with a large Aut(X,T): the O3 subgroup is large.
