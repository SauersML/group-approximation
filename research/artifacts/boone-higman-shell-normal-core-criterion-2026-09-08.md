# Shell normal cores and the exact germ fiber product, 2026-09-08

The universal shell route remains open. This record proves a positive
control, classifies the limit of its presentation argument, and identifies
the germ presentation problem for arbitrary inputs more explicitly.

## A necessary quotient test

`shell-fp-stabilizer-forces-fp-germ-group` promotes the earlier artifact's
neighborhood-kernel argument to a separate Cairn claim. The map A_1->Q
onto the isotropy germs has kernel a strictly increasing union of copies
of V. That kernel is simple and not finitely generated. Any one of its
nonidentity elements normally generates it inside A_1. Therefore finite
presentation of A_1 forces finite presentation of Q by one extra relator.
No converse is proved.

## The normal-core repair has an exact boundary

The whole prefix germ H=<tau> is never normal in Q for an infinite input.
Nevertheless C=<tau^m> can be normal. Near index forces any such C to be
central, equivalently every input permutation is eventually m-periodic.
The regular action on the even integers then has a free orbit in a finite
ray permutation group, forcing the input to be virtually cyclic.

Conversely a signed cyclic-coset enumeration for a virtually cyclic input
of cyclic index d makes every input permutation eventually 4d-periodic.
Thus `shell-prefix-core-detects-finite-ray-enumerations` proves that some
enumeration has nontrivial prefix core exactly for virtually cyclic P.
For every enumeration of every other input, including Z^2, that core is
trivial. This rules out this specific normal-core repair for the universal
construction, without ruling out finite presentation by another argument.

## All finite singularity stabilizers in the positive family

For the signed-coset enumeration Q lies in Z^m semidirect S_m, the group
of eventual near permutations of m rays. Divide by the central diagonal
translation C=<tau^m>. The quotient and every subgroup of each of its
finite powers are virtually finitely generated abelian, hence finitely
presented.

For S(M,M'), take its joint germ map modulo the transported copies of C.
The kernel is a subgroup of finite index in Fix_V(M'), already known to
be finitely presented. The image is a subgroup of the preceding virtually
abelian product and is finitely presented. The extension is therefore
finitely presented. This proves all three open-route clauses for virtually
cyclic inputs, and in fact every finite S(M,M'). The finite-germ theorem
then makes E_nu and all its finite pointwise stabilizers finitely presented.

This proof uses a normal subgroup inside the prefix germ, not normality
of that entire germ. It also does not infer finite presentation of an
arbitrary subgroup from finite presentation of its ambient group.

## The universal germ group is a fiber product up to index two

The parity split produces a stronger structural description for arbitrary
P, with no finite-ray assumption. Let lambda(P) act regularly on all N,
put R_nu=<lambda(P),s> in the near permutation group, and let eta be its
index homomorphism to Z. The parity-preserving subgroup of Q is exactly

    F_nu = R_nu x_Z R_nu.

Its generators are (lambda_g,1), (1,lambda_g), and (s,s). The two indices
are synchronized. Conversely the normal closure of lambda(P) in R_nu is
ker(eta), so those generators give every pair with equal index. The
remaining shell shift conjugates (a,b) to (s*b*s^-1,a) and squares to
(s,s).

Thus Q is finitely presented exactly when F_nu is. When P is finitely
generated, the projection F_nu->R_nu has finitely normally generated
kernel, proving the further necessary condition R_nu finitely presented.
The universal shell route now has the concrete chain

    three shell stabilizers FP
        ==> A_1 FP ==> Q FP <==> F_nu FP ==> R_nu FP.

The last implication is not asserted reversible. In particular, a finite
presentation of R_nu alone does not supply the cross-factor relations of
the fiber product. Nor does a finite presentation of Q alone settle the
global shell stabilizers. Those are the next presentation problems.

When R_nu is finitely presented, the fiber-product problem has an
exact finite-relator formulation. Amalgamate two copies of R_nu
over their common shift s. This is a finitely presented group T.
The kernel of T->F_nu is normally generated by the mixed commutators

    [lambda_g^(1),s^k*lambda_h^(2)*s^-k], g,h in a finite input
    generating set, k in Z.

F_nu is finitely presented exactly when the commutators for a finite
band |k|<=B normally generate all of them. The proof uses the
finite-normal-generation criterion in both directions. This isolates
the missing uniform relation argument. Merely evaluating these words
as permutations gives no evidence for it: all of them vanish there
by the disjoint even/odd supports, with or without a finite presentation.

The subsequent node `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate` replaces
this relation test by an equivalent structural condition. Its source
was checked in the primary arXiv v2 PDF, Proposition 3.8 (the published
version numbers the corresponding result 3.6). The node states the
remaining data explicitly and retains finite presentation of R_nu
as a separate requirement. No such data have been constructed for
general inputs. Even supplying them would still leave the lift from
germ presentations to the three global shell stabilizers unresolved.

The subsequent effective-presentation record proves that all finite
point stabilizers of E_nu are finitely generated for every finitely
generated input and every enumeration. This supplies the shorter
route `boone-higman-via-fp-shell-envelopes`, which only requires a
finitely presented E_nu. The germ and ascending HNN tests in this
record remain necessary for the stronger A_1 presentation clause;
they have not been proved necessary for E_nu itself to be finitely
presented. See
`research/artifacts/boone-higman-shell-effective-presentation-2026-09-08.md`.
