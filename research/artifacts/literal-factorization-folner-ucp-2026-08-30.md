# Literal factorization property by Folner compression (2026-08-30)

## Statement and graph inputs

Let `K=E_T=ker(E->Z)`.  The established split normal form identifies
`E=K rtimes_alpha Z`, with the stable letter implementing the automorphism
`alpha` of the whole kernel (not merely the injective doubling endomorphism of
the original base).  The established telescope-core theorem says that every
finitely generated subgroup of `K` is residually finite.  We prove directly
that the canonical trace of `C*_(max)(E)` is amenable.  Throughout this audit,
an unadorned group C-star-algebra is maximal/full; a reduced algebra or crossed
product is explicitly subscripted by `r`.

## Full-to-reduced regular compression

Write `A=C*_(max)(K)` and identify
`C*_(max)(E)=A rtimes_(alpha,max) Z`.  Let

```text
q_r:A rtimes_(alpha,max) Z -> A rtimes_(alpha,r) Z
```

be the canonical quotient.  In the regular Hilbert-`A`-module representation
on `ell^2(Z) tensor A`, with `u a u*=alpha(a)`,

```text
Lambda(a)_(i,i)=alpha^(-i)(a),       Lambda(u)e_j=e_(j+1).
```

For a finite interval `F subset Z`, compression gives

```text
kappa_F(x)=P_F Lambda(q_r(x)) P_F in M_F(A).
```

A compression of a unital representation is u.c.p. (the unit of the corner is
`P_F`), so `kappa_F` is u.c.p. on the full crossed product.  No equality of
full and reduced crossed products is asserted.  Precomposition by `q_r` is
valid, and the full canonical trace also factors through `q_r`.

If `x=sum_(|r|<=R) a_r u^r`, then

```text
Lambda(x)_(i,j)=alpha^(-i)(a_(i-j)),
```

with the entry zero when `i-j` is outside the Fourier support.  This formula
exhibits the varying `alpha^(-i)` coefficients explicitly.

## One finite quotient and one Arveson extension

Fix a finite packet `Omega subset C[E]`, enlarge it by `1`, adjoints, and the
pairwise products needed below, and fix `F`.  The finitely many matrices
`kappa_F(x)`, `kappa_F(xy)`, and
`P_F Lambda(x)(1-P_F)Lambda(y)P_F` for `x,y in Omega` have entries in `C[K]`.
Collect every group element appearing in all these entries and let `H<=K` be
the subgroup generated by their union.  This is one finitely generated
subgroup; in particular it simultaneously contains every `alpha^(-j)`
translate which occurs for every `j` in the finite window and its
`R`-boundary.

The core theorem makes `H` residually finite.  Let `S subset H\{e}` be the
finite set of nonidentity elements appearing in the diagonal coefficients
`alpha^(-i)(a_0)`, for `x=sum a_r u^r in Omega` and `i in F`.  For each
`s in S`, choose a finite quotient separating `s`; their diagonal product,
followed by passage to its finite image, gives one quotient `q:H->Q` with
`q(s)!=e` for every `s in S`.  Let

```text
pi:C*_(max)(H)->M_|Q|
```

be the representation obtained from `q` and the left regular representation
of `Q`.  It is an exact unital star-homomorphism, and

```text
tr_Q(pi(h)) = 1  if q(h)=e,
              0  otherwise.
```

Thus its trace agrees with the canonical group trace on every collected
diagonal coefficient.

We must define a u.c.p. map on all of `A`, not merely on `C*_(max)(H)`.  The
algebraic inclusion `C[H]->C[K]` is isometric for the maximal norms:
restriction of `K`-representations gives one inequality, and inducing an
arbitrary representation of `H` to `K` gives the other.  Hence
`C*_(max)(H)` is a unital C-star-subalgebra of `A`.  Represent `A` faithfully
on a Hilbert space and apply Arveson's extension theorem to obtain one u.c.p.
map

```text
phi:A->M_|Q|,             phi|_(C*_(max)(H))=pi.
```

Unitality is preserved because the extension agrees at `1`.  No covariance
or approximate covariance of `phi` is needed: all varying orbit coefficients
were put into the single subgroup `H` before the extension was chosen.

## Multiplicativity estimate

Define

```text
Phi_F=(id_(M_F) tensor phi) compose kappa_F:
      C*_(max)(E)->M_(|F||Q|).
```

It is u.c.p.  For `x,y in Omega`, every entry of `kappa_F(x)`,
`kappa_F(y)`, and `kappa_F(xy)` lies in `C*_(max)(H)`, where `phi=pi` is
multiplicative.  Consequently

```text
Phi_F(xy)-Phi_F(x)Phi_F(y)
 =(id tensor pi)(P_F Lambda(x)(1-P_F)Lambda(y)P_F).     (1)
```

If the Fourier width of every member of `Omega` is at most `R`, the right side
of `(1)` has zero block columns except for the `R`-boundary

```text
partial_R F={j in F: dist(j,Z\F)<=R},        |partial_R F|<=2R.
```

After applying `pi`, its rank is at most `2R|Q|`.  Its operator norm is at
most `||x|| ||y||`, since regular representation, compression, and `pi` are
contractive.  For normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm in dimension `|F||Q|`,

```text
||T||_2^2 <= rank(T)/(|F||Q|) ||T||^2.
```

Thus

```text
||Phi_F(xy)-Phi_F(x)Phi_F(y)||_2
 <=||x|| ||y|| sqrt(2R/|F|).                            (2)
```

Choose `F=[-L,L]` with `L` large.  Notice that `Q`, `H`, and `phi` are
allowed to depend on this already-chosen `F`; the cancellation of `|Q|` in
`(2)` is why their dimensions cause no problem.

## Trace computation

For a Fourier monomial `a u^r`, the matrix `kappa_F(a u^r)` has zero diagonal
when `r!=0`.  When `r=0`, its diagonal entries are `alpha^(-i)(a)`,
`i in F`.  By the choice of `q` and invariance of the identity coefficient
under automorphisms,

```text
tr_(|F||Q|)(Phi_F(a u^r))
 =0                                      if r!=0,
 =|F|^-1 sum_(i in F) tr_Q pi(alpha^(-i)(a))
 =tau_K(a)                               if r=0.
```

Hence `tr compose Phi_F=tau_E` exactly on `Omega`.

For a finite subset of the C-star-completion, first approximate it in norm by
a finite Fourier packet.  U.c.p. contractivity absorbs the approximation in
both the trace and product estimates.  Since `E` is finitely generated,
`C*_(max)(E)` is separable; an exhaustion and diagonal choice of intervals
gives a sequence satisfying Brown's u.c.p.--Hilbert--Schmidt criterion.
Therefore `tau_E` is amenable and `E` has Kirchberg's factorization property.

## Failure modes checked

- **One map, not one map per orbit point:** `H` is generated after the whole
  finite interval and boundary are fixed; the single extension `phi` agrees
  with `pi` on all `alpha^(-j)` coefficients at once.
- **Arveson domain:** maximal subgroup C-star-algebras embed isometrically, by
  induced representations; the extension is genuinely defined on all
  `C*_(max)(K)`.
- **Full versus reduced:** only the regular compression uses the reduced
  crossed product.  It is precomposed with the canonical quotient from the
  full crossed product, exactly where the canonical group trace factors.
- **No accidental quasidiagonality:** `(2)` uses small normalized rank.  The
  operator norm of the boundary term can stay bounded away from zero, so the
  construction supplies amenability but not a quasidiagonal trace and does
  not contradict the established non-MF theorem.
