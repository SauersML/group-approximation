# Audit: fibrewise O-infinity colour collapse

Date: 2026-08-30

Claim: `stw81-fibrewise-oinfinity-absorption-collapses-base-colours`.

## Imported theorems and scope

1. Hirshberg--Rordam--Winter Theorem 4.6 applies to separable arbitrary
   `C_0(X)`-algebras over finite-dimensional locally compact metrizable
   spaces.  It does not require a continuous field.  Second countability
   plus locally compact Hausdorff gives the required metrizability here.

2. `O_infinity` is K1-injective and strongly self-absorbing, so fibrewise
   absorption globalizes.  Zero fibres cause no exception.

3. Bosa--Gabe--Sims--White applies to separable nuclear globally
   O-infinity-stable algebras without simplicity, unitality, or field
   hypotheses and gives nuclear dimension at most one.

## Exact conclusions

4. A nonzero nuclear-dimension-zero algebra is AF.  AF algebras are stably
   finite, whereas a nonzero O-infinity-stable algebra is strongly purely
   infinite, so the nonzero dimension is exactly one.

5. With simple fibres, quotient fibres are zero or the original simple
   fibre.  A nonzero commutative hereditary fibre would force that simple
   fibre to be elementary, impossible for a nonzero O-infinity-stable
   algebra.  Fibre norms detect nonzero elements, so every purported
   commutative hereditary shadow is zero.

6. In the mixed statement the clopen hypothesis, not local triviality,
   supplies the direct sum.  The elementary restriction is explicitly
   assumed continuous trace; no claim is made that elementary fibres alone
   force that property for an upper-semicontinuous field.

7. On the continuous-trace summand, hereditary subquotients have spectra
   locally homeomorphic to locally closed subspaces of the original
   one-dimensional spectrum.  The absorbing summand contributes no shadow.

## Boundary

This does not collapse colours for stably finite non-AF fibres.  Fibrewise
Z-stability also globalizes, but no theorem used here bounds an arbitrary
nonsimple Z-stable field by nuclear dimension one.  That remains the exact
one-dimensional seam.

Outcome: **PASS**.
