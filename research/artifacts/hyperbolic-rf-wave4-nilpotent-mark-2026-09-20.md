# Exact nilpotent quotients of the marked canonical cover

20 September 2026. The canonical central generator z of B=B_c0 lies in
gamma_5(B). Thus every quotient of nilpotency class at most four kills z.
This does not assert invisibility in all finite groups.

The input is the exact marked presentation in
`hyperbolic-rf-wave4-class-two-2026-09-20.md`. Its three vertex groups are
H0=C7^2 and two exponent-seven Heisenberg groups H1,H2. The corner words
have logarithms

    q_j(i)=i U_j-i^2 V_j/2+sigma_j i^3[U_j,V_j]/12,
    sigma=(0,-1,1).

The six face words r_i are the ordered products of these three corners,
and their marked relations are r_i=z^-1, i=1,...,6. The vertex Lie
relations are [U0,V0]=0 and, for j=1,2,
[Uj,[Uj,Vj]]=[Vj,[Uj,Vj]]=0. The source vertex-weight identities and
the marking were independently checked before this computation.

## Why finite-field Lie computation controls the universal quotient

Let P=B/gamma_5(B). The face relation at i=1 expresses z in the vertex
groups, so P is finitely generated by elements of order dividing seven.
Its abelianization has exponent seven. Each lower-central factor is
generated by commutators in these generators and is a quotient of tensor
powers of that abelianization; hence each factor is a finite elementary
abelian seven-group. Since the series terminates, P is a finite seven-group.

The Lazard correspondence applies because its class is at most four,
strictly less than seven. The associated Lie ring is generated by the
logs of the vertex generators. Each is killed by seven, so their Lie
subring is killed by seven, including all sums and iterated brackets.
Thus this is a Lie algebra over F7. Conversely the BCH group of every
F7 Lie algebra of class at most four is a class-four exponent-seven group.

It therefore suffices to test the marked relations in the universal
class-four F7 Lie algebra. No assumption that all finite quotients are
nilpotent, or that the original group has exponent seven, is being made.

For the correspondence and tensor construction, see Garaialde Ocaña and
González-Sánchez, *Transporting cohomology in Lazard correspondence*,
[Section 2, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.4654). That section
explicitly constructs the truncated associative algebra, its free Lie
subalgebra, and the exponential and logarithm operations used here.

## Exact tensor-algebra certificate

The standard-library script
`experiments/hrf-wave4-nilpotent-2026-09-20/class_four.py` represents
Lie polynomials by their associative word coefficients over F7, discarding
words of length greater than the selected class bound. Brackets are
XY-YX. In class four it uses the exact formula

    BCH(X,Y)=X+Y+[X,Y]/2
             +([X,[X,Y]]-[Y,[X,Y]])/12
             -[Y,[X,[X,Y]]]/24.

An independent truncated exponential/logarithm calculation on two free
symbols verifies that formula. All denominators are units modulo seven.
The bracket closure of the six free generators has dimension406, the
Witt dimension 6+15+70+315. The usual free-Lie embedding into the tensor
algebra is valid here; its computed dimension also matches every required
homogeneous component.

Set R_i=BCH(BCH(q_0(i),q_1(i)),q_2(i)) and Z=-R_1. The relation ideal
is generated by the five vertex Lie relations, R_i-R_1 for i=2,...,6,
and [Z,U_j],[Z,V_j] for j=0,1,2. These are exactly the marked presentation
after eliminating the central generator. Repeated right bracketing with
the six free generators, with exact modular row reduction, generates
the ideal: Jacobi reduces brackets with any Lie word to these operations.
Every new row is independent, and the final script explicitly checks
closure under all six generators and inclusion of every original relation.

The resulting ideal has dimension389. The quotient has dimension17,
and the marked vector Z reduces to zero. Consequently every class-four
image kills z, including P, so z lies in gamma_5(B).
