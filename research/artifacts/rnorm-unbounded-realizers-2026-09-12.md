# Unbounded kernel realizers for the hyperbolic Leavitt cover

Date: 2026-09-12. Lane `rnorm-unbounded-realizers` (swarm 5).
- **Target:** the unbounded case of the realizer hinge of
  `relative-normalization-modulo-kazhdan-kernel`, which
  `bounded-blockwise-inner-realizers-fail-off-kernel` left open.
- **Sibling:** lane `rnorm-infinite-orbit-measure`, which works on measure-type
  sizes (`component-space-sizes-miss-leavitt-cover-defect`).

## 0. Setting

- **The cover:** `1 -> N -> G -> R^x -> 1` from
  `hyperbolic-kazhdan-cover-of-leavitt-unit-group`, with `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`,
  `N` Kazhdan, and `C_G(N) = 1`.
- **The approximation:** `sigma_n : G -> Sym(X_n)` is a sofic approximation.
  Kun's edit gives blocks `B` of `sigma_n|N` with edited generators `s_B`.
- **Block groups:** `Pi_B = <s_B : s in S>` and `C_B = C_(Sym B)(Pi_B)`. `Pi` and
  `C` are the images in `S_U` of the blockwise products.
- **Realizers:** a realizer of `g` on `B` is an approximation
  `sigma_n(g) ≈ w(s_B) c` with `w` any word in `S` and `c in C_B`. It is bounded
  when `w` ranges over a fixed finite set.
- **Why this normal form:** by
  `kazhdan-kernel-blocks-invariant-under-sofic-invisible-quotient`, every element
  preserves most blocks, so realizers are the natural blockwise normal form. The
  sibling lane's surviving shape asks for a size read inside single `N`-blocks at
  unbounded word length in `N`, which is this regime.

## 1. Landed results

1. **Scale (`inner-realizers-are-at-least-half-the-sofic-radius`).** This is a
   finite-stage inequality. The mass realized by words of length at most `l` is
   at most the mass where the edited sofic radius is below `2l + L_g + 1`, up to
   `2 eps` and the fixed-word error. The proof is the bounded argument made
   quantitative: a realizer word `w` gives the nontrivial commutator word
   `[w^-1 g, s]` of length `2|w| + O(1)`, and that word fixes most of the block.
   Bounded realizers are the case of fixed radius.
2. **No centralizer factor (`kernel-block-realizers-need-no-centralizer-factor`).**
   Suppose `Q` is integrally acyclic in degrees 1 and 2 and has only trivial
   sofic morphisms. Then `sigma(G) <= Pi C` implies `sigma(G) <= Pi`.
   - The centralizer parts give a homomorphism `Q -> C/(C cap Pi)`, and
     `C cap Pi` is central in `C`.
   - The fibre product with `Q` is a central extension of `Q`. It splits because
     `H^2(Q; A) = 0`, which gives a homomorphism `Q -> S_U`. That homomorphism is
     trivial.
   - Expansion is not used.
3. **Regular blocks (`leavitt-cover-regular-realizer-models-iff-residually-finite`).**
   A realized approximation whose regular (Cayley) kernel blocks carry positive
   mass exists iff `G` is residually finite.
   - (⇒) After step 2, almost multiplicative realizers in a regularly acting group
     are exact homomorphisms `r_B : G -> Pi_B`, since `G` is finitely presented,
     and trace zero makes them separate `G`.
   - (⇐) The finite quotients are such models, each a single block, because every
     finite image of `G` is an image of `N`.

## 2. What this does to the route

**Positive side asked:** growing realizers should contradict (T)-exactified
transport on the kernel.
- **The regime:** by result 2 it is exactly `sigma(G) <= Pi`. Every element acts
  through the groups generated by the kernel's edited generators, by words of
  length at least about half the sofic radius (result 1).
- **Regular blocks:** by result 3 the regime there is populated iff `G` is
  residually finite.
  - If `G` is residually finite, the finite-quotient models are genuine actions of
    `G`. Blocks are exactly invariant and realizers are exact homomorphisms, so
    every statement true of genuine actions, in particular any exactified
    transport, holds in them.
  - So a contradiction from realizers plus exact transport on regular blocks is a
    proof that `G` is not residually finite.
  - That is Gromov's question for this group. It follows from `RNORM` through
    nonsoficity, so this part of the route cannot deliver `RNORM` without solving
    it.
- **Other blocks:** `sigma(G) <= Pi` constrains only the group generated by the
  edited kernel generators. Where `Pi_B` contains `Alt(B)`, every
  block-preserving permutation agrees with an element of `Pi_B` off at most two
  points, so realizers exist for every element and carry no information.
- **Result 4, no restriction at all (`kernel-block-realizers-exist-after-small-re-edit`).**
  Any sofic approximation can be re-edited into realized form without changing
  `sigma_n`.
  - Add product generators `t_i = s_i s_(i+1)`, re-edited by one 3-cycle
    `(a, s_i a, s_(i+1) a)` per block. That is `o(|X_n|)` points, since there
    are `o(|X_n|)` blocks.
  - Each block group becomes primitive with a 3-cycle, hence contains `Alt(B)`
    by Jordan's theorem.
  - Block invariance then realizes every element.

  So the realized regime is populated iff `G` is sofic, and a contradiction from
  unbounded realizers is a contradiction from `sigma_n` alone. With result 3:
  populated with regular blocks iff `G` is residually finite, and populated at
  all iff `G` is sofic.
- **The defect:** in a realized injective model the relative defect `d` of the
  cover lies in `Pi \ sigma(N)`. Realizers place `sigma(d)` in the block closure
  of `sigma(N)`. They never place it in `sigma(N)`, which is what `RNORM`
  would need.

**Negative side asked:** construct a model with unbounded realizers where nothing
contradicts.
- Any such model is a sofic approximation of `G`, hence a proof that `G` is sofic.
  By Corollary 5 of `research/artifacts/hyperbolic-leavitt-cover-relative-defect-2026-09-12.md`,
  that would refute `RDD`. So an unconditional construction is out of reach here.
- Result 3 is the conditional form: if `G` is residually finite, the
  finite-quotient models are exactly such models.

## 3. Recorded, not claimed

- **Representative dependence.** `Pi` depends on the edit and on the generating
  set it is read on, not only on `sigma : G -> S_U`. Result 4 settles the
  question this raised: every approximation can be re-edited into realized form.
- **Fixed eps.** The results use agreement `1 - o(1)`. With a fixed
  `eps in (0, 1/2)`, `sigma(g)` is only within `eps` of `Pi C`, and result 2 does
  not apply as stated. Result 1 holds at every `eps < 1/2`.

## 4. Red-flag check

- Result 1 uses trace zero and `C_G(N) = 1`.
- Result 2 uses acyclicity and trivial sofic morphisms of `R^x`.
- Result 3 uses finite presentability of `G` and the finite-quotient splitting.
- None uses property (T) of `G` beyond Kun's blocks for `N`. None proves
  nonsoficity or says anything about Thompson's `V`.
- The property (T) input to `RNORM` has to enter outside realizer structure.
