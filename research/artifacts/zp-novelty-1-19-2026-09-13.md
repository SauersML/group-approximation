# Novelty check: Zaremsky Problem 1.19 answered yes (2026-09-13)

**Claim checked.**
- Theorem 7: if H ≤ A_L has finite index and Σ^m(H) ≠ ∅, then L is (m−1)-connected. If Σ^m(H;R) ≠ ∅,
  then L is (m−1)-acyclic over R.
- Consequence, via Bestvina–Brady: if a finite-index subgroup of A_L maps onto Z with kernel of type F_n,
  then so does A_L itself. This is Zaremsky's conjecture 1.19, on the list since 6/15/23.

## Verdict

1. **Not found as stated.** Neither Theorem 7 nor a proof of the conjecture (in the F_n form Zaremsky
   states) appeared in the sources checked.
2. **The rational homological version is implicit in published theorems** (derivation below), although
   no source found states it for RAAGs. The chain is:
   - Fisher's virtual fibring theorem (2021, v3 2024) or the Hughes–Kielak BNSR/ℓ² theorem (2024);
   - multiplicativity of ℓ²-Betti numbers under finite index;
   - the Davis–Leary ℓ²-Betti numbers of A_L;
   - Bestvina–Brady.

   So the new content of the landing is:
   - the **homotopical** statement: for n ≥ 2, L simply connected, which ℓ²-invariants cannot see;
   - the direct Σ-invariant proof, which works over every coefficient ring R at once.
3. **No counterexample or partial result published.** The only related remark found is Schesler–Zaremsky,
   arXiv:2107.10958v2 p. 2: "For the question of which RAAGs virtually algebraically F_n-fiber, there is a
   very straightforward sufficient condition: if L is (n−1)-connected, then A_L algebraically F_n-fibers
   [BB97]. For RACGs, things are much more open." It records sufficiency only; necessity is not discussed.
4. **Credit framing.** "Answers 1.19 (F_n form) affirmatively; no earlier statement found in the sources
   checked."
   - Credit Meier–Meinert–VanWyk and Bux–Gonzalez (Σ of A_L itself), Bestvina–Brady, and Bieri–Renz.
   - Say that the rational homological case also follows from Fisher arXiv:2112.00397 Theorem A (or
     Hughes–Kielak arXiv:2401.05545 Theorem A) together with Davis–Leary.
   - Fisher's Theorem A (v1 Dec 2021) predates the list, so a reader could have derived the FP_n(Q)
     version before 1.19 was posed. Do not call the whole problem "long open" without this caveat.

## The implicit homological derivation (checked here, standard ingredients)

Let H ≤ A_L have finite index, and suppose H maps onto Z with kernel of type FP_n(Q).
1. Fisher, arXiv:2112.00397v3, Theorem A. For G virtually RFRS of type FP_n(Q), (2) implies (1): virtual
   fibring with FP_n(Q) kernel gives b_p^(2)(G) = 0 for all p ≤ n. The paper also states that "subgroups
   of right-angled Artin groups and right-angled Coxeter groups are virtually RFRS". So b_p^(2)(A_L) = 0
   for p ≤ n.
   - Alternative: Hughes–Kielak, arXiv:2401.05545, Theorem A: "Let G be a group of type FP_n(Q). If
     b_n^(2)(G) ≠ 0, then Σ^n(G;Q) = ∅." Apply it to H at each p ≤ n, since Σ^n ⊆ Σ^p. Then
     b_p^(2)(A_L) = b_p^(2)(H)/[A_L : H] = 0.
2. Davis–Leary, *The ℓ²-cohomology of Artin groups*, J. London Math. Soc. 68 (2003) 493–510, Cor. 2.
   - Quoted in Davis–Okun, arXiv:1002.2564, p. 3, item (1)(b): L²b^n(X̃; A) = b^n(K, ∂K) for the
     Salvetti complex X.
   - For a RAAG, K is the cone on (the barycentric subdivision of) L and ∂K is L. So
     b_p^(2)(A_L) = b̃_{p−1}(L; R).
   - This specialization is my derivation from the quoted formula; I did not read it in Davis–Leary.
3. So L is (n−1)-acyclic over Q, and by Bestvina–Brady (Invent. Math. 129 (1997)) BB_L is of type FP_n(Q).
- Over a field F of positive characteristic, Fisher's Theorem B has the same shape with D_{FG}-Betti
  numbers. The RAAG computation of those numbers was not checked.
- None of this reaches F_n for n ≥ 2. Kielak's survey arXiv:2510.01805 names the homotopical setting as a
  challenge for the theory.

## Sources read

- Fisher, arXiv:2112.00397v3 (HTML): Theorems A and B verbatim, and the virtually RFRS sentence. There is
  no RAAG application, and neither Davis–Leary nor Zaremsky is cited.
- Hughes–Kielak, *BNSR invariants and ℓ²-homology*, arXiv:2401.05545 (HTML): Theorem A verbatim. It has no
  RAAG, graph product or flag complex example.
- Kielak, arXiv:1809.09386 (JAMS 33 (2020)), abstract: an RFRS group virtually fibres with finitely
  generated kernel iff b_1^(2) = 0. That gives the n = 1 case of 1.19 through Davis–Leary.
- Kielak, *Virtual fibring of manifolds and groups*, arXiv:2510.01805 (HTML survey): RAAGs are RFRS. It
  has no fibring statement tied to flag complexes, no RAAG question, and no Davis–Leary, MMV,
  Bux–Gonzalez, Jankiewicz–Schreve or Zaremsky entries.
- Davis–Okun, arXiv:1002.2564, pp. 1–4 (PDF read directly).
- Schesler–Zaremsky, arXiv:2107.10958v2 (J. Topology), pp. 1–3 (PDF read directly): the quote above, and
  the Question "For which flag complexes L does W_L virtually algebraically F_n- and/or FP_n-fiber?"
  (RACGs).
- Zaremsky, arXiv:1705.07470 (AGT 19 (2019)), pp. 1–3, 13–14: commensurability of abelian splittings via
  Σ¹. It does not restate 1.19.
- Jankiewicz–Norin–Wise, arXiv:1711.11505, abstract: RACGs only.
- Kochloukova–Mendonça, arXiv:2010.03689, abstract: Σ criterion for [H,H] ⊆ K ⊆ H. It does not cover
  finite-index subgroups of A_L.
- Llosa Isenrich–Martelli–Py, arXiv:2112.06531 (HTML): nothing on this.
- Meier–Meinert–VanWyk (Comment. Math. Helv. 73 (1998)), abstract via OpenAlex: Σ of graph groups
  themselves.
- OpenAlex searches: virtual algebraic fibring of RAAGs; Σ invariants of finite-index subgroups; higher
  virtual algebraic fibering; RAAGs algebraically fibring. Nothing relevant.
- arXiv listings: Zaremsky (53 papers) and Kielak (39). Nothing restates or resolves 1.19.
