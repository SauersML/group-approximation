## 2. Part 1: tower model, generators, W_n = [[T]]′ (re-derived)
- **(M1) iterated.** Apply ζ to X = V_1 ⊔ TV_1 to get V_1 = V_2 ⊔ T²V_2, and inductively X = ⊔_{i<2^m} T^iV_m, T^{2^m}V_m = V_m, (V_m,T^{2^m}) ≅ (X,T). ✓
- **Full-group elements.**
  - L_π equals T^{β(πy)−β(y)} on the level V×{y}.
  - s_y equals T^N on V×{y}, since T^N(T^{β(y)}v) = T^{β(y)}Sv.
  - Partial 3-cycles are commutators of partial transpositions.
  - σ_{y,y′} = [s_y, L_{(y y′)}]. I recomputed: (v,y) ↦ (Sv,y), (v,y′) ↦ (S^{−1}v,y′), identity elsewhere. ✓
- **T_n ⊂ [[T]]′.**
  - Types 1–3 are even level permutations: L_s repeats each cycle of s on 8 sheets; δ_n is a double transposition.
  - Type 4 consists of commutators.
  - |T_n| = 20167 + |A|, independent of n. ✓
- **Step 1.**
  - Transitivity (SS5) gives Alt(F_x) for all x.
  - δ_n^g fixes F_x∖{(x,1),(x,2)} and swaps (x,i) ↔ (y,i), so ((x,1) b c) ↦ ((y,1) b c).
  - Alt(F_x) is 6-transitive, so Alt(F_x ∪ F_y) follows.
  - For |P∩Q| ≥ 2 and |Q| ≥ 4: (p z′ z)^{(z′ q w)} = (p q z).
  - Connectivity (SS4) and overlaps of 8 points give Alt(Y_n). ✓
- **Step 2.**
  - Conjugating by L_π ∈ Alt(F) sends ζ_B^{(i,j,k)} to ζ_B^{(πi,πj,πk)} and σ_{i,j} to σ_{πi,πj}, using L_π s_y L_π^{−1} = s_{πy}.
  - Complements: ζ_Vζ_B^{−1} = ζ_{V∖B}.
  - Intersections: I recomputed [(1 2 3),(3 4 5)] = (1 4 3) (1↦4, 4↦3, 3↦1, 2 and 5 fixed). Over v in exactly one of B, B′ one factor is trivial.
  - S-translation: ψζ_Bψ^{−1}(Sv,1) = (Sv,2) for v ∈ B, and sheets 4,5,6 are untouched by ζ_B.
  - The S-translates of the letter cylinders generate the clopen algebra of V (bounded alphabet, transported by ζ^m).
  - Twisted 3-cycles: χζ_Bχ^{−1} maps (S^pv,i) ↦ (S^qv,j) ↦ (S^rv,k), with 6 distinct sheets available among 8. ✓
- **Step 3.**
  - For U good, the pieces W_y are good, and their three translates are pairwise disjoint across y (injectivity of T for the same ε, goodness of U for different ε). So σ_U = ∏_y σ_{W_y}.
  - σ_{B×{y}} is the twisted 3-cycle (v,y) ↦ (S^{ε_+}v,y_+) ↦ (S^{−ε_−}v,y_−) ↦ (v,y), with ε_± ∈ {0,1}.
  - 3-transitivity of Alt(Y_n) moves the three levels into F, and conjugation respects twists.
  - `eng_cb` gives [[T]]′ ≤ W_n. ✓
- **Hypotheses used.**
  - *Minimality and infiniteness:* `eng_cb`, Matui's simplicity and finite generation, and Théorème `expo` (not an odometer).
  - *Aperiodicity and recognizability:* the tower partition.
  - *Constant length 2:* towers of height 2^m matching |Y_n| = 8·2^n.
  - *Bounded alphabet:* |A_m| = |A|, so |T_n| is bounded.
- **Model tests.**
  - *Sturmian subshifts* have no clopen V with X = ⊔_{i<2^m}T^iV for all m: such a tower would give the dyadic odometer as a factor, and Sturmian X_α have irrational rotation spectrum only. So the hypothesis fails, as it should.
  - *Odometers* have constant towers but infinite letter partitions (not subshifts), consistent with polynomial growth of their f.g. subgroups (Cornulier l.798).
