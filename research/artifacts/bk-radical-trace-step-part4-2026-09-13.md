# Blackadar--Kirchberg radical trace step, part 4: quotients of the radical are compact and Toeplitz-infinite (2026-09-13)

Lane `ex-bk-glued-uct`.  This builds on parts 1--3 (Lemma A, Theorem 7) and on
Theorem 1 of `research/artifacts/bk-spectral-extension-permanence-2026-09-12.md`
(extensions by exact compact-free ideals of nuclear quasidiagonal algebras are
quasidiagonal).  Throughout, `E` is separable and nuclear, `R = R_qd(E)`, and
`H = ∂(K_1(E/R)) ⊆ K_0(R)`.  `q : R -> R/K` is the quotient map.

## 1. Theorem 8 (no compact-free quotients of the radical)

**Theorem 8.**  For every ideal `K ⊊ R` of `E`, `Prim(R/K)` has a nonempty
compact open subset.  No UCT and no stable finiteness is assumed.

*Proof.*  `0 -> R/K -> E/K -> E/R -> 0` is exact, `R/K` is exact, and `E/R` is
nuclear and quasidiagonal.  If `Prim(R/K)` had no nonempty compact open subset,
the spectral extension theorem would make `E/K` quasidiagonal.  Minimality of
the radical then gives `R ⊆ K`.  QED

For `K = 0` this is the radical criterion
`stw07-qd-radical-criterion-compact-open`.  The new content concerns the
closed pieces `Prim(R) \ Prim(K)`: every nonempty closed subset of the form
`Prim(R) \ Prim(K)` with `K ⊊ R` contains a nonempty set that is compact and
relatively open.  For instance, no quotient of the radical is a cone or
suspension, and none has spectrum `[0,1]` or `R`.

## 2. Theorem 9 (projection-generated quotients force infinite quotients of E)

**Theorem 9.**  Let `E` be stably finite and not quasidiagonal, with `E/J`
satisfying the UCT for every ideal `J ⊆ R`.  Let `K ⊊ R` be an ideal of `E`
such that some `M_n(R/K)` contains a projection full in `R/K`.  Then `R/K` is
not stably finite, or `q_*(H) ∩ K_0^+(R/K) != {0}`.  In both cases `E/K` is not
stably finite.

*Proof.*  Suppose `R/K` is stably finite and `q_*(H)` is singular.
1. Let `D̄ = p̄ M_n(R/K) p̄` be the full corner, unital, exact and stably
   finite.  Lemma A gives a tracial state `τ_D` annihilating `q_*(H)`, since
   `K_0(D̄) ≅ K_0(R/K)` as ordered groups.
2. Transport `τ_D ⊗ Tr` along `D̄ ⊗ K ≅ (R/K) ⊗ K` and restrict to
   `(R/K) ⊗ e_11`.  This is a nonzero densely finite trace `τ̄` on `R/K`
   annihilating `q_*(H)`.
3. `τ = τ̄ ∘ q` is a nonzero densely finite lower semicontinuous trace on `R`.
   It annihilates `H`, because the pairing is natural under `q_*`.  This
   contradicts Theorem 7.

For the last sentence: `E/K` is an extension of `E/R` (quasidiagonal, hence
stably finite) by `R/K`, with boundary `q_* ∘ ∂` by naturality.
- If `R/K` is not stably finite, neither is `E/K ⊇ R/K`.
- Otherwise `q_*(H)` contains a nonzero positive class, and Spielberg's
  criterion (BD Proposition 4.1) makes `E/K` not stably finite.  QED

`K = 0` gives Theorem 1: a projection-generated radical would make `E` itself
not stably finite.

## 3. Consequences

**Corollary 10 (maximal ideals and elementary points).**  Keep the hypotheses of
Theorem 9.
1. For every ideal `K ⊊ R` of `E` with `R/K` simple and containing a nonzero
   projection, `E/K` is not stably finite.  If moreover `R/K` is stably finite,
   some `h ∈ H` has image a nonzero projection class in `R/K`.
2. **Type I.**  Suppose `E` is of type I, so that all quotients satisfy the
   UCT.  Let `P_1, ..., P_m` be closed points of `Prim(R)` with elementary
   quotients `R/P_i ≅ M_(n_i)` or `K`.  Then some `h ∈ H` has strictly positive
   rank `r_(P_i)(h) = (π_(P_i))_*(h) ∈ K_0(K) = Z` at every `P_i`.

*Proof of 2.*  Each `Tr ∘ π_(P_i)` is densely finite on `R`, because
`Ped(R)` maps onto `Ped(R/P_i)`, which consists of finite-rank operators.  Let
`V ⊆ R^m` be the real span of the rank vectors `(r_(P_i)(h))_i`, `h ∈ H`.
- By Gordan's alternative, either `V` contains a vector with all coordinates
  positive, or some `w ≥ 0`, `w != 0`, is orthogonal to `V`.
- In the second case `Σ w_i Tr ∘ π_(P_i)` is a nonzero densely finite trace
  annihilating `H`, contradicting Theorem 7.
- `V` is spanned by integer vectors, so a positive vector can be chosen in the
  image of `H`.  QED

So in a type I counterexample every finite set of elementary points of the
radical is seen positively by one index class.  Yet `H` contains no positive
class.  Positivity must fail at the non-closed (Toeplitz) points of `R`.

**Corollary 11 (real rank zero).**  If `R` has real rank zero, then for every
ideal `K ⊊ R` of `E`, every positive homomorphism `K_0(R/K) -> R` annihilating
`q_*(H)` is zero.

*Proof.*  Pull it back to `K_0(R)` and apply Corollary 6.  QED

## 4. Tests

- **Trivial model, failing stable finiteness.**  The Toeplitz algebra: `R = K`,
  and the only choice is `K = 0`, with `R/0 = K` generated by a projection.  The
  conclusion shape holds: `H` contains `±[e] > 0` and `T` is not stably finite.
  The hypothesis of Theorem 9 fails, so there is no contradiction, and the
  pattern shows the conclusion is not vacuous.
- **Real object, for Theorem 8.**  `C_0((0,1], T)~` is quasidiagonal with
  radical `0`, so Theorem 8 is vacuous there.  It is consistent that this
  algebra has a compact-free ideal `C_0((0,1), T)` whose quotient `T ⊕ C` is not
  quasidiagonal: Theorem 8 constrains quotients of the radical, not of `E`.
- **Type I calibration: the conclusion of Corollary 10.2 is necessary, not
  contradictory.**  Take `R = ⊕_N K` (AF) with `H = span{e_n - e_(n+1)}`, which
  is singular.  For every finite `F ⊆ N` and `m ∉ F`, the class
  `h = Σ_(i ∈ F) e_i - |F| e_m ∈ H` has positive rank on `F`.  So the
  conclusion can hold for radicals that do have the K_0-embedding property, and
  it only narrows the search.  A single generator `h`, by contrast, is excluded
  as soon as two points see it with opposite signs.

## 5. Shape of a glued UCT counterexample after parts 1--4

`E` stably finite, nuclear, not quasidiagonal, UCT quotients inside the radical.
Then `R = R_qd(E)` satisfies:
1. every nonzero quotient of `R` has a compact open primitive subset
   (Theorem 8);
2. `R` contains a traced compact ideal (radical criterion);
3. no nonzero densely finite trace on any quotient of `R` annihilates the index
   subgroup (Theorem 7);
4. every quotient `R/K`, `K ⊊ R`, generated by a projection makes `E/K`
   infinite (Theorem 9).

In words: **the radical is compact at every level, and each of its
projection-generated quotients is a Toeplitz-like infinite quotient of `E`.**
The surviving candidates have radicals whose quotients are either infinite or
not projection-generated, and whose densely finite traces all see an index.
