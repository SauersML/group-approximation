ORIGIN OF THE SURJUNCTIVITY-SOFICITY QUESTION
Research update: September 13, 2026.

Question: Does there exist a surjunctive group that is not sofic?
Equivalently: does surjunctivity imply soficity?

Finding: the earliest explicit formulation substantiated in this search is
Ceccherini-Silberstein and Coornaert, Cellular Automata and Groups (2010),
Open Problems, OP-11. This establishes an occurrence, not who first posed
the question. No source examined identifies the first proposer.

Evidence for 2010:
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-14034-1
https://irma.math.unistra.fr/~coornaer/livres.html
The authors' second edition explicitly says that its problem list appeared
in the first edition, and retains OP-11 on p. 527:
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-43328-3
Searchable reproduction of that passage:
https://dokumen.pub/cellular-automata-and-groups-2nbsped-9783031433276-9783031433283.html
The first-edition problem page itself was not retrieved. No unverified
first-edition page number or precise date of first posing is asserted.

Earlier sources examined:

1. Gromov, Endomorphisms of symbolic algebraic varieties (1999).
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/31470
Develops the approximation machinery and proves the forward surjunctivity
implication. No explicit formulation of this converse was located.

2. Weiss, Sofic groups and dynamical systems (2000), pp. 350-359.
https://www.jstor.org/stable/25051326
States universal surjunctivity and nonsofic-group existence questions and
proves the forward implication. No explicit converse question was located.

3. Ceccherini-Silberstein and Coornaert, Linear cellular automata: Garden
of Eden Theorem, L-surjunctivity and group rings (2006), Section 2.
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=adm&paperid=254&what=fullt
States that sofic groups are surjunctive and that no nonsurjunctive or
nonsofic example was known. This does not identify a proposer of the converse.

4. Pestov's 2008 survey and Pestov-Kwiatkowska's 2009 notes.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.3968
https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.4266
The relevant surjunctivity discussions did not yield an explicit converse.

5. Coornaert, Les groupes sofiques, lecture dated April 6, 2009.
https://irma.math.unistra.fr/~coornaer/gt3_sofique_2009.pdf
Slides 13 and 14 ask about nonsofic groups, sofic groups that are not LEA,
and hyperbolic groups, and state Gromov-Weiss surjunctivity. They do not
state this converse. The online file includes a September 2009 added note;
the lecture date should not be mistaken for the date of every annotation.

Remaining earlier lead:
Ceccherini-Silberstein and Coornaert, Cellular Automata and Groups,
Encyclopedia of Complexity and Systems Science (2009), pp. 778-791.
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-30440-3_52
The publisher's preview establishes the publication but does not expose
the full discussion or Future Directions section. Its contents cannot
establish an earlier occurrence until that section is actually inspected.
Bibliographic search results conflate this entry, a later revision, and
the 2010 monograph; their dates must not be combined.

Later explicit formulations:
Arzhantseva-Gal, December 30, 2013 preprint, p. 3:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.7682v1
Bowen-Chapman, November 10, 2025, Problem 1.1:
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.06586v1
Bowen-Chapman cite Gromov and Weiss alongside their question, but this
does not identify who first explicitly asked it. Their Theorem 1.2 solves
the analogous invariant-random-subgroup problem.

Editorial decision:
Use the descriptive title "A surjunctive group that is not sofic" and the
identifier SurjunctiveNonsofic. Credit each source for what it documents.
Do not name the problem after Bowen-Chapman or assign its original posing
to Ceccherini-Silberstein-Coornaert without further evidence. Historical
verification records retain the names used at the commits they audited.

To identify a person rather than an earliest located publication, useful
additional evidence would be an earlier dated text or confirmation from
the authors about the question's provenance. No authors have been contacted.
