## 2. What landed

1. **`lo-syllable-relators-keep-free-factors`** (literature derivation). For
   arbitrary `A`, `B`, if the syllable subgroups of a cyclically reduced relator
   are orderable, both factors inject.
2. **`normal-generators-of-free-products-have-non-lo-syllables`** (elementary
   given 1). A normal generator has a non-orderable syllable subgroup under every
   nontrivial quotient pair. Consequences for Kirby 66:
   - K1: that syllable subgroup is torsion-free and non-cyclic, so `m >= 2`;
   - K2: its factor is not orderable;
   - K3: bounded syllable rank;
   - K4: stability under quotients.

   It also implies clause (b) of `normal-rank-one-free-products-need-cyclic-abelianization`.
3. **`kirby-freedman-one-factor-injects-when-a-is-torsion-free`** (OPEN). This is
   the question as printed in 1995 Problem 5.7. The route
   `torsion-free-normal-rank-via-one-factor-injectivity` derives the Cairn
   normal-rank form from it.

## 3. Map of the remaining case

A counterexample `(A, B, w)` to the normal-rank form, with `A`, `B` torsion-free,
must satisfy all of the following:
- `A^ab ≅ Z/m`, `B^ab ≅ Z/n`, `gcd(m, n) = 1`
  (`normal-rank-one-free-products-need-cyclic-abelianization`);
- at most one factor has a nontrivial orderable quotient (same node);
- the cyclically reduced form of `w` has a non-orderable syllable subgroup of
  rank at least 2, and so has syllable length at least 4 (K1);
- the same persists in every nontrivial quotient pair (K4).

Methods that do not reach the mixed case, where one syllable subgroup is
orderable and the other is torsion-free but not:
- **Degree arguments over `U(N)`.** With both factors mapped into `U(N)` and the
  `B`-representation conjugated by `g`, the map `g ↦ w(ρ_A, g ρ_B g^{-1})` is
  homotopic to a constant through `ρ ↦ 1`, so its degree is 0. The Klyachko and
  Gerstenhaber–Rothaus mechanism needs an unconjugated letter, i.e. `B = Z`.
- **`L^2`-Betti numbers.** `β_1^(2)(A * B) >= 1`. But the torsion-free
  Osin–Thom bound fails for countable groups
  (`l2-normal-rank-bound-fails-for-torsion-free-groups`), and the finitely
  generated form is open.
- **Pushouts through the syllable group.** `(A * B)/<<w>>` is the pushout of
  `(A' * B')/<<w>>` over `A'` and `B'`. Nontriviality follows whenever both
  syllable subgroups inject into `(A' * B')/<<w>>`, which is item 1 in the
  orderable case. In the mixed case the needed injectivity is the Levin-type
  Freiheitssatz for torsion-free coefficients, known only for Klyachko shapes.

**Test candidates, not settled.** Torsion-free higher-rank lattices have no
orderable quotients by the normal subgroup theorem plus Deroin–Hurtado, as
recorded on the target node and not re-read. A pair of them with coprime cyclic
abelianizations would meet every constraint above only through relators whose
syllable subgroups are non-orderable. Whether such lattices with cyclic
abelianization exist was not checked.
