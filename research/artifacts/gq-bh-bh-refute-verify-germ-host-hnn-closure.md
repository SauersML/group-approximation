# Adversarial check: `germ-host-class-closed-under-sectioned-ascending-hnns` (bh-free-20) (bh-refute, 2026-09-18)

Coordinator-assigned item. I checked `germ-host-class-hnn-closure-proof` against its four referee points,
together with the parts of `last-section-f-infinity-tail-bh-proof` (P) that it reuses. The BHM imports
(Appendix A, `thm:SingFixFiniteness`, the cone-swap construction, `thm:RoverNek`) and P's base-case transplant
to `V_{d,m}S` (P's own referee point 3) were not re-derived. They enter only through (C3) for the seed.

Verdict: **PASS on all four points.**

## Point 1: `sĝs^{-1} = \widehat{Φ(g)}`, piece by piece

- **`s` is a homeomorphism.** The domain pieces `λC, βyC (y≠β), xC, ββC` and the image pieces
  `λλC, λb(y)C, xC, βC` are both partitions of `C`.
- **On `λλC`.** `λλu ↦ λu ↦ λg(u) ↦ λλg(u)`. This equals `Φ(g)^{(λ)}`, because `Φ(g)` acts on `λC` as `g^{(λ)}`.
- **On `λzC` (`z ≠ λ`).** `s^{-1}(λzw) = βb^{-1}(z)w ∈ Σ_0`. Then `ν_0^{-1}` gives `zw`, `ψ_Φ(g)` gives `Φ(g)(zw)`, and
  `ν_0` followed by `s` gives `λΦ(g)(zw)`.
- **Why a non-cone-preserving `ψ_Φ(g)` is harmless.** `ν_0` is defined on all of `C∖λC`, and `ψ_Φ(g)` is a
  homeomorphism of `C∖λC`, because `Φ(g)` preserves `λC`.
- **On `Σ_k`.** `s(β^{k+2}yv) = β^{k+1}yv`, so `s` maps `Σ_{k+1}` onto `Σ_k`, and `sν_{k+1} = ν_k`. So slot `k+1` of `ĝ`
  becomes slot `k` of `\widehat{Φ(g)}`.
- **The fixed parts.** The pieces `xC` and the point `β^∞` are fixed by both sides.
- **Continuity of `ĝ` at `β^∞`.** The slots shrink to `β^∞`, so `ĝ` is continuous there.
- **Homomorphism.** `g ↦ ĝ` is a homomorphism, since restriction to the `Φ(g)`-invariant set `C∖λC` is
  multiplicative.

## Point 2: `E_Φ ≅ E_φ`

Unwinding `φ^m`, the element `Φ(h)` has the piece `ψ(φ^{m−1−j}h)` on `L^j(C∖LC)` for `j < m`, and acts as
`h^{(L^m)}` on `L^mC`. So `(ψ_Φ(Φ^kg))_k` is the sequence `(ψ(φ^i g))_{i≥0}`, reindexed by the bijection
`i = mk + m−1−j`. The two tail homomorphisms have the same kernel.

## Point 3: nothing in P's §3–§5 uses more of `V_dS` than (C1)–(C3)

The uses are these:
- fullness (the finite germ extension, and "no new singular point" implies membership in `W`);
- `V_d ≤ W` (dilations, cone swaps, highly transitive support control);
- the base case, which is replaced wholesale by (C3) for `W`. So the "copies of `V_{d,m'}S`" fact is not needed.
- **The new block dilation.** It is `αw ↦ αβw`. `A ∖ t(A)` consists of `d^m − 1 ≡ 0 (mod d−1)` cones, so the
  cone-count condition for `t ∈ V_d` still holds.
- **The germ group at `p' ∈ O'`.** `O'` avoids `sing(W)`, so `W` is locally `V_d` there, and its germ group is
  cyclic, generated by the dilation, because `β` is primitive. The correction terms `c_g` lie in `W`, supported
  off the cone and off `M'`. So they lie in `H`, and BHM's three ascending-HNN conditions hold as in P §3.

## Point 4: cone-closure of `W'`

- The transported germ of a generator at `αx` is a germ of an element of `W'`:
  - for `W`, by (C1);
  - for `ĝ` at `β^∞`, via `vĝv^{-1}` with `v ∈ V_d` agreeing with `w ↦ αw` near `β^∞`;
  - for `s ∈ V_d`, directly.
- Germs of products are products of germs. So `f^{(α)}` is locally in `W'` on `αC`, and it is the identity off
  the clopen `αC`. Fullness then gives `f^{(α)} ∈ W'`.
- (C2): `sing(W') ⊆ sing(W) ∪ O'`.

## Step D

- `N = ⟨K, t^m⟩ ≅ K*_{φ^m}`: the base `⋃ t^{-i}Kt^i` is generated by `K` and `t^m`, since
  `t^{-1}kt = t^{-m}φ^{m−1}(k)t^m`.
- Krasner–Kaloujnine gives `K*_φ ↪ N ≀ Sym(m)`, which is realized in `W'` on `m` equal-length cones by (C1).

## Nit

- **N1.** The HNN convention (`t ↦ s^{-1}`) goes with `t^{-1}gt = φ(g)`. Stating the convention explicitly would
  remove a sign ambiguity. Harmless.

## Lesson for general BH

Permanence can be proved one singular orbit at a time. A cone-closed full host with rational singularities
and an `F_∞` stabilizer package absorbs a cone-sectioned ascending HNN extension. The new stable letter
creates exactly one new singular tail class, whose germ group is an ascending HNN extension of the tail group.
BHM's SingFix induction then propagates `F_∞`, with the old host's package as its base case. This is a
template for closure theorems of BH host classes: each new operation must be localized at a new orbit of
singular points with a computable germ group.
