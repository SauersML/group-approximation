## 1. V1: outer automorphisms
- **Setting:** `X` an infinite minimal subshift, `R = LC(X,F_2) ⋊_T Z`, `G = EL_3(R)`, `d_k = diag(u^k,1,1)`.
- **Step 1:** `Ad(d_k)` maps `e_ij(r)` to `e_ij(a_i r a_j^{-1})`. So `θ_k = Ad(d_k)|_G ∈ Aut(G)` and `θ_kθ_l = θ_{k+l}`.
- **Step 2:** the centralizer of `G` in `GL_3(R)` is `{I}`. Taking `r = 1` shows a centralizing `g` is diagonal with equal
  central entries. The centre of `R` is `F_2`: aperiodicity kills the `u^k` coefficients, `k ≠ 0`, and minimality makes `f_0`
  constant.
- **Step 3:** `θ_k` is inner iff `d_k ∈ G`, which is impossible for `k ≠ 0`. The half-orbit index `ind: GL_3(R) → Z`
  vanishes on `E_3(R)`, and `ind(d_k) = −k` (`subshift-el3-diagonal-recognition-fails-up-to-g-conjugacy`, reviewed PASS by
  sk-verify-16).
- **Step 4:** `G` is infinite simple, hence ICC. By CIOS II Proposition `inj` (trivial character), `Out(G) → Out(L(G))` is injective.
- **Consequence:** Connes's countability of `Out` for property (T) factors (recalled) gives `Out(L(G_X))` countably infinite.
- **Open:** is `Out(L(G_X)) = Out(G_X)`? What is `Out(G_X)`? Candidates are the normalizer of `G_X` in `GL_3(R)` modulo `G_X`
  (containing `Z` from the index), ring automorphisms from `Aut(X,T)` and the flip, and the transpose-inverse graph
  automorphism. A computation would give a genuine factor invariant.

## 2. V2: corner subfactors
- **Step 1:** `τ∘ι` is a character, so by character rigidity it equals `c·1 + (1−c)δ_e`.
- **Step 2:** the GNS space of `N = ι(G)''` splits into the trivial representation and the regular representation. These are
  disjoint because `G` is infinite, so a central `z ∈ N` has `Nz ≅ L(G)` and `ι(g)(1−z) = 1−z`.
- **Step 3:** the converse is `λ ⊕ 1`.
- **Step 4:** the corner count comes from `separable-factors-hold-countably-many-subshift-groups`, reviewed PASS by sk-verify-10.
- **Contrast:** `[[T]]′` has non-regular factor representations (Dudko–Medynets), so V2 is special to character-rigid groups.

## 3. What the tools can and cannot do
- **Commensurability.** Zhou, arXiv:2608.02327, abstract read at source on MSI (2026-09-14): "We construct two explicit
  countable discrete groups Γ_1 and Γ_2 that are both ICC and have Kazhdan's property (T). Although Γ_1 and Γ_2 are not
  isomorphic as groups, their group von Neumann algebras are isomorphic … a counterexample to Connes' rigidity conjecture for
  ICC property (T) groups." A web-search snippet describes a "mutually commensurable" family; not checked at source. Among
  infinite simple groups commensurable means isomorphic, so a family-internal counterexample must be non-commensurable.
- **Deformation/rigidity** (recalled):
  - Popa's malleable deformations need a free product, Bernoulli or Gaussian structure.
  - Negative-curvature routes need acylindrical hyperbolicity, which fails because `G_X` is simple, hence not SQ-universal
    (Dahmani–Guirardel–Osin).
  - The W*-superrigidity of Chifan–Ioana–Osin–Sun needs wreath-like products, which have abelian normal subgroups.
- **Blind invariants** (identical for all `X`): property (T), fullness, `R^ω`-embeddability, all ℓ²-Betti numbers zero
  (reviewed node), free entropy dimension 1 (Jung–Shlyakhtenko, recalled), countable fundamental group and countable `Out`
  (Connes, recalled).
