# Boone--Higman: restore the Steinberg-to-elementary boundary

## Correction

The earlier claim that the universal sandwich makes EL_4(U) finitely
presented by Krstic--McCool is unsupported and is withdrawn. The verified
finite-presentation theorem applies to St_4(U), which surjects onto
EL_4(U). Finite presentability does not pass to arbitrary quotients.

The ring lemma remains valid: the specified universal sandwich is finitely
presented and keeps the coefficient ring embedded when the supplied
sandwich witness exists. Its former claim to remove every group cover
was a separate, unjustified consequence.

## Verified sources

[Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain, arXiv:0809.4095v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.4095v2),
Section 6.1, printed page 35: Theorem 6.2 gives property (T) for Steinberg
groups; the paragraph after Proposition 6.3 explicitly states the
Krstic--McCool finite-presentation result for St_n(R), n>=4, and then
uses Steinberg groups as covers of elementary groups. The 1999 original
was not independently obtained here.

[Chatterji--Kassabov, arXiv:2601.22907v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22907v1),
proof of Theorem 3: after building the elementary group they introduce a
finitely presented cover, add subgroup relators, and add finitely many
normal-generation identities. Lemma 6 supplies the finitely presented
derived-subgroup overgroup used to put a whole finitely presented marking
in the diagonal corner.

Neither cited statement identifies a finitely presented Steinberg source
with its elementary image.

## Constructive replacement

The canonical proof finite-marking-normal-generator-cover-proof gives a
general repair: from a finitely presented P mapping onto Q and a finitely
presented H embedded in Q, lift the generators of H and impose its finitely
many relators. These relations hold in Q, so the marked H embeds upstairs.
If one element of H normally generates Q, add one normal-generation
identity per generator of P. The result remains a finitely presented cover,
now containing H and normally generated by the prescribed element.

Applied to St_4(U), this proves ck-steinberg-marked-cover. Thus an explicit
finitely presented Steinberg source can replace the unspecified Shalom
source. An uncontrolled kernel to the elementary group still remains.

## Effect on the Boone--Higman routes

The maximal-kernel route stays open and valid when its ambient Gamma is
the finitely presented marked cover furnished by the imported CK theorem
or the corrected construction. Its finite-presentation criterion must not
be applied unconditionally with Gamma=EL_4(U).

The matrix level-ideal and quotient double-coset obstructions remain valid:
their proofs never require finite presentation of the matrix group.
The double-coset obstruction also applies to the corrected marked cover
because its coset space maps onto the matrix coset space.

For an arbitrary proper normal subgroup M of the marked cover, its image
in the elementary group need not have been proved proper. Nor need M
contain the covering kernel. Consequently the matrix level-ideal screen
cannot simply be transferred to every maximal kernel upstairs. A later
quotient whose marking no longer maps to an elementary quotient needs a
separate analysis, as the existing quotient-and-cover obstruction states.

A possible replacement assumption is centrality of the cover kernel,
but it is not assumed or established for U here. Stable centrality and
central closedness of a finite-rank Steinberg group must not be confused
with centrality of its map to the elementary group over an arbitrary
noncommutative ring.

The projective-host route already explicitly requires finite presentation
of its final projective group, so it survives this correction unchanged.
The full Boone--Higman conjecture remains open. These are source-checked
corrections and an algebraic construction, not GPU results or Lean proofs.
