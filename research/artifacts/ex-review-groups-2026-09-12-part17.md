## 17.5 ex-fp-kazhdan-ring: `ledrappier-crossed-product-is-finitely-presented`. PASS

- **Observables.** `X(z)^p = 1`, and each `E_a(z)` is a polynomial in `X(z)`. Forbidden triangle patterns act

- **Rows.** From `[ab, cd] = 1` and the known commutations, `abcd = acdb = cdab`. So `acd = cda = cad`, and

## 17.6 `permutive-triangle-sfts-are-quantum-rigid`. PASS

- **Determinism.** Operator determinism follows from `Σ E = 1` and vanishing of forbidden patterns.
- **Row induction.** The four generator pairs reduce to distances `<= k-1`, in the row or the row above. The
  base cases `k <= 2` lie inside one ball of radius 1.
- **Periodicity.**
  - Rows evolve by a sliding block code, so an `N`-periodic row has only `N`-periodic rows above it.
  - Two of those rows coincide, so the half-plane above is vertically periodic. Its periodic extension has only
    occurring patterns.
- **Consequence.** In a free example, the row and column subshifts have no periodic point, so they are not sofic.
