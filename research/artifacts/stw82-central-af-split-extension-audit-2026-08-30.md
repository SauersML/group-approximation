---
title: STW LXXXII central AF split-extension checkpoint (2026-08-30)
---

For a unital extension `0 -> I -> E -> D -> 0`, assume that the quotient is
commutative AF and has a unital *-homomorphic section into `Z(E)`.  Then
`stw82-central-af-split-extensions-obey-maximum` proves

`dim_nuc(E)=dim_nuc(I)`.

The argument is local and does not invoke the unresolved arbitrary
extension formula.  Approximate `D` by finite clopen step algebras
`D_lambda=C^m`.  Their central minimal projections split
`E_lambda=I+s(D_lambda)` as a finite direct sum of the minimal
unitizations of the corners `p_jI`.  Every stage therefore has exactly the
nuclear dimension of `I`, and their directed union is dense in `E`.

This is stronger than the subhomogeneous-endpoint result in the direction
of the ideal: `I` is arbitrary.  It is narrower in the extension geometry:
the section must be central and the quotient zero-dimensional.  A merely
split finite-dimensional quotient does not produce the central projections
used by the proof, so no claim is made for arbitrary split extensions.
