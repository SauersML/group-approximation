# Raw denominator words select the diagonal shell orbital

Two Weyl charts parameterize the same Hecke vertex through section words
`a_i` and `b_i`.  Their quotient is a fixed lattice word:

```text
a_i=b_i d_i,   d_i in SL_3(Z).
```

Conjugating a lattice-central base projection therefore gives identical raw
branch projections.  In an approximate representation, the sum of their 42
squared discrepancies is bounded by a fixed constant times presentation
defect and the 42 base-centrality defects.  The constant is independent of
dimension and conductor depth.

For the independent-coordinate model on `Omega times Omega`, the incidence
square-function is exactly twice the projection off the diagonal.  Zero
incidence confines the carrier to the diagonal, of normalized trace `1/42`.
Thus the previous off-diagonal orbital escape cannot satisfy the raw
denominator identities at full density.

The remaining correctable-sector problem is one proximity estimate: charge
the squared distance from each conductor-decoded chart PVM to its raw lamp
conjugates by mixed-root energy and first-exit mass.  This result neither
proves that estimate nor treats lattice microstates uniformly far from every
exact representation.
