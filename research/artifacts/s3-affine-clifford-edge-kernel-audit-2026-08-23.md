# S3 affine-Clifford edge presentation and kernel audit — 2026-08-23

## Question

The one-direction reset calculation proves equality of the selected
restriction dimensions, but an equivariant HNN edge needs an actual injective
homomorphism.  Does the subgroup generated by the Schur packet, all three
rank-fifteen derivative words, and the balanced fresh generators admit such a
map?  What happens if the six regular-orbit copies are collapsed to one
literal fresh residual packet?

## Exact component presentation

Use the class-two presentation of the affine Schur packet.  Its center is

```text
C=<J,z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4> ~= F_2^5,
```

and its quotient by the center has the thirty generators `p_1,...,p_15` and
`q_1,...,q_15`.  The five alternating commutator forms are the restricted
coefficient matrices of the arity-four affine pencil.  Add the dummy Pauli
pair `x_0,z_0`, with `[x_0,z_0]=J`.  The source therefore has normal-form
dimensions

```text
dim C=5,       dim E/C=30+2=32,       |E|=2^37.        (KEA1)
```

On the fresh side retain the same relabelled Schur packet and add the four
balanced generators of `(GRS3)`:

```text
[p_-,q_-]=s,       [p_+,q_+]=J s,
```

with all cross commutators trivial and `s` one fresh selector coefficient.
Then

```text
dim C'=5,      dim E'/C'=30+4=34,      |E'|=2^39.      (KEA2)
```

In standardized old/fresh selector coordinates define

```text
phi(x_0)=p_-p_+,        phi(z_0)=q_-q_+,               (KEA3)
```

and use the selector relabelling on `C` and the corresponding conjugate
Schur generators on the thirty-dimensional residual quotient.  Because the
two controlled pairs commute with one another,

```text
[p_-p_+,q_-q_+]=s(Js)=J.                               (KEA4)
```

The executable audit checks all `32^2=1024` ordered generator commutators.
There are no failures.  The quotient map in `(KEA3)` has rank `32`, and the
center map has rank `5`.  Hence a kernel element first vanishes in the
quotient and is then killed by injectivity on the center; `phi` is an honest
group injection.  This is stronger than equality of character dimensions.

The three derivative matrices for

```text
0x2ce6, 0xb0eb, 0x9c0d
```

all have rank fifteen.  Each family `Q(D_a v)` therefore spans the complete
fifteen-dimensional residual `q`-Lagrangian, and the stacked family still has
rank fifteen.  Thus the derivative words introduce no unaccounted generator
or relation outside the audited presentation.

## Why one residual copy still cannot carry S3

Let

```text
Omega(D)=[[0,D],[D^T,0]]
```

be the full thirty-dimensional alternating commutator form.  This formulation
allows an arbitrary Clifford linear transformation mixing the `p` and `q`
Lagrangians.  If one residual copy admitted the two generators of the reset
`S_3`, then for

```text
K=Omega(D_1)^(-1) Omega(D_2)
```

the swap would force `K` to be similar to `K^(-1)`, and the shear would force
`K` to be similar to `I+K`.

Exact polynomial-nullity invariants refute both similarities:

```text
                         K       K^(-1)       I+K
nullity(x^4+x+1)         8          0           8
nullity(x^9+x^4+x^3+x+1)0          0          18.      (KEA5)
```

Thus the swap fails by the first row and the shear fails by the second.  This
strengthens the earlier strict row/column pencil audit: even a general
thirty-dimensional Clifford congruence cannot coherify the chosen residual
copy.

## The regular sixfold map

Central-product six component sources and targets over the one common `J`.
The normal-form dimensions are

```text
dim Z(E_reg)=1+6*4=25,
dim E_reg/Z(E_reg)=6*32=192,
dim E'_reg/Z(E'_reg)=6*34=204.                          (KEA6)
```

The block-diagonal component map has ranks `25` on the center and `192` on
the quotient, so its kernel is zero.  Consequently

```text
|E_reg|=2^217,             |E'_reg|=2^229.              (KEA7)
```

The exact regular permutations satisfy `s^2=u^2=(su)^3=1`, give a faithful
group of order six, and label the six factors by the three reset directions
with multiplicities `2,2,2`.  Simultaneous factor permutation commutes with
the block component map.  The audit verifies this equality on all `384`
basis-vector and reset-generator pairs.  Therefore the extension

```text
E_reg semidirect S_3  -->  E'_reg semidirect S_3        (KEA8)
```

is injective.  Its source and target orders are respectively `6*2^217` and
`6*2^229`.

## Exact boundary: private versus literally shared residuals

Regular induction requires the six residual Schur packets to remain private.
If corresponding residual `p` and `q` generators in all six factors are
identified with one literal thirty-dimensional fresh packet, the induced
linear map is

```text
F_2^(6*30) -> F_2^30
```

and has kernel dimension `150`.  Even keeping every dummy pair private, the
full source quotient map has rank only `30+12=42` and kernel `150`.  A concrete
kernel word is

```text
p_(factor 0,1) p_(factor 1,1).                          (KEA9)
```

It is nontrivial in the central product and becomes trivial after the literal
sharing quotient.

Hence the finite semidirect edge is valid precisely with private residual
Schur factors.  Only common logical BCS products may be identified.  The
audit does **not** attach the resulting fresh selector events to the global
Fanizza success/failure words; that remains the non-finite semantic claim
`s3-equivariant-endpoint-conventions-use-global-bcs-words`.
