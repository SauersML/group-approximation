# Goldbring--Sinclair max-U witness is canonical-trace blind

## Statement

Goldbring--Sinclair's separable witness from Theorem 3.23 can be chosen to
be the full group C-star algebra of a countable sofic group.  It therefore
fails property `max-U` while its canonical group trace is Connes embeddable.
The same full group C-star algebra is surjectively universal and hence also
carries factorial traces whose GNS factors are not Connes embeddable.  Those
traces cannot embed into a corner of the canonical group factor.

This simultaneously gives the strongest positive and negative conclusions
available from the witness:

```text
positive:  a non-CE factor is generated by a representation of the group;
negative:  the generating character is nonregular and cannot be promoted
           into a corner of the canonical group factor.
```

## Converting their algebra into a full group C-star algebra

Choose

```text
G = SL_3(Z).
```

Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma prove that `C^*(G)` fails LLP, hence it fails LP.
Set

```text
H = F_infinity * G,
D = C^*(H).
```

Following Goldbring--Sinclair Theorem 3.23, let `D_n=D`, put

```text
D_J = ell_infinity(D_n : n in J) = D^(|J|)
```

for each nonempty finite `J subset N`, and take the unital full free product

```text
A = *_(J) D_J.                                           (GS1)
```

For `m=|J|`, Fourier transform on the finite cyclic group gives

```text
D_J = D^m
    = D tensor_max C^m
    = C^*(H) tensor_max C^*(C_m)
    = C^*(H x C_m).                                      (GS2)
```

Full group C-star algebras take group free products to unital full C-star
free products.  Hence

```text
A = C^*(Gamma),
Gamma = *_(nonempty finite J subset N) (H x C_(|J|)).    (GS3)
```

Goldbring--Sinclair Theorem 3.23 now says that `C^*(Gamma)` fails property
`max-U` for every cofinal ultrafilter on the directed set of finite subsets
used in the construction.

## The group is sofic

`SL_3(Z)` is residually finite, hence sofic.  Free groups and finite cyclic
groups are sofic.  Soficity is preserved by direct products, free products,
and directed unions.  Every finite subset of the countable free product in
`(GS3)` lies in a finite sub-free-product, so `Gamma` is sofic.  Consequently
its regular character is hyperlinear and

```text
L(Gamma) is Connes embeddable.                            (GS4)
```

Thus the implication

```text
C^*(Gamma) fails max-U  =>  Gamma is nonhyperlinear
```

is false, even for the explicit separable group-built witness of the paper.

## A non-CE trace exists, but it is the wrong character

`D=C^*(F_infinity*G)` is surjectively universal: map `F_infinity` onto a
countable unitary generating set of any separable unital C-star algebra and
send `G` trivially.  Coordinate maps `D_J -> D` and the universal property of
the free product show that `A` is also surjectively universal.

Choose a separable unital C-star algebra `B` with a trace `tau_B` whose GNS
von Neumann algebra `M` is a non-Connes-embeddable finite factor.  Such a `B`
is obtained by taking a norm-separable C-star subalgebra generated by a
countable strong-dense unitary set in a separable-predual non-CE finite
factor.  A quotient

```text
q:C^*(Gamma)=A -> B
```

gives the factorial trace

```text
sigma = tau_B o q,
pi_sigma(C^*(Gamma))'' = M.                              (GS5)
```

Therefore `sigma` is non-CE, whereas the canonical regular trace is CE by
`(GS4)`.  The same group carries both characters.

Finally, `M` cannot embed normally and unitally into any nonzero corner
`pL(Gamma)p`: Connes embeddability passes from `L(Gamma)` to corners and
von Neumann subalgebras, contradicting non-embeddability of `M`.  Thus
`(GS5)` is a representation-generated factor, not a canonical group-factor
corner.

## Consequence for Cairn

Failure of `max-U` is an operator-norm statement witnessed by a state on a
maximal tensor product.  It neither selects a tracial witness nor identifies
the canonical group character.  Surjective universality supplies non-CE
traces independently, but the example proves that the remaining promotion

```text
arbitrary non-CE character -> canonical regular character
```

is exactly load-bearing.  No direct route from the Goldbring--Sinclair
`max-U` witness to a nonhyperlinear group or a non-CE corner of its canonical
group factor is valid.
