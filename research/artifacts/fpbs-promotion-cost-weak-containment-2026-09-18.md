## 0. Setting

- Conventions are those of
  `fpbs-finite-cost-transfers-up-weak-containment-2026-09-18.md` §1:
  labelled pieces `(A, γ)`, labelled families `Φ`, `cost(Φ) = Σ μ(A_i)`,
  and `S ∨ Φ` for the relation generated by `S` and the pieces. Every
  graphing inside `E_a` is a labelled family of the same cost.
- **Promotion cost.** Let `Γ` be countable and `H' ≤ H ≤ Γ`. For a p.m.p.
  action `a` of `Γ` put

  ```text
  P(a; H', H) = inf { cost(Φ) : Φ a labelled family of a,  E_{a|H'} ∨ Φ ⊇ E_{a|H} }.
  ```

  Labels may be arbitrary elements of `Γ`, not only elements of `H`.
- **The F_2 case.** `L = F(a, b)`, `φ(a) = a`, `φ(b) = b a b^{-2}`,
  `L_j = φ^j(L) = ⟨a, t_j⟩` with `t_j = φ^j(b)`, and `ρ` is the Bernoulli
  shift of `L`. For a p.m.p. action `c` of `L` put
  `Q_j(c) = P(c; L_{j+1}, L_j)`. Then `Q_j(ρ)` is the quantity `Q_j` of
  `fpbs-mal-floor-iff-deep-promotion-floor`.
- `L_j = ⟨L_{j+1}, t_j⟩`, since `a ∈ L_{j+1}`. So `L_j` is generated over
  `L_{j+1}` by one element.

## 1. Theorem M (promotion cost is antitone along weak containment)

Let `Γ` be countable and `H' ≤ H ≤ Γ` with `H = ⟨H', F⟩` for a finite set
`F ⊆ Γ`. Let `a`, `b` be p.m.p. actions of `Γ` on standard non-atomic
spaces `(X, μ)` and `(Y, ν)`, with `a` free and `a ≼ b`. Then

```text
P(b; H', H)  ≤  P(a; H', H).
```

### Proof

Assume `P(a; H', H) < ∞` and fix `ε > 0`. Choose a labelled family
`Φ = (A_i, γ_i)_{i ∈ I}` of `a` with `E_{a|H'} ∨ Φ ⊇ E_{a|H}` and
`cost(Φ) < P(a; H', H) + ε`. Put `δ = ε / (2|F| + 1)`.

1. **Finitely many words.** Fix `s ∈ F`. Then `⟨H', s⟩ ≤ H`, so
   `E_{a|H'} ∨ Φ ⊇ E_{a|⟨H', s⟩}`. Lemma 2 of the transfer artifact, with
   `K = H'`, needs only this inclusion and freeness of `a`. It gives a finite
   set `W_s` of words of value `s` (letters `h ∈ H'`, `i^+`, `i^-`) with
   `μ(U^a_s) > 1 − δ`, where `U^a_s = ⋃_{w ∈ W_s} D^a(w)`.
2. **One transfer.** Let `I_0 ⊆ I` be the finite set of piece indices used
   by the words in `⋃_{s ∈ F} W_s`, and `F_0` the finite union of the
   sets `F(w)`. Each `U^a_s` is a finite union of finite intersections of
   sets `(g^a)^{-1} A_i` with `g ∈ F_0` and `i ∈ I_0`. Lemma 1 of the transfer
   artifact, with these patterns and `η = δ`, gives a measure isomorphism
   `S : Y → X`. With `B_i = S^{-1} A_i` for `i ∈ I_0`, it satisfies
   `ν(B_i) = μ(A_i)` and `ν(U^b_s) > μ(U^a_s) − δ > 1 − 2δ`. Here `U^b_s` is
   computed from the same words with the pieces `(B_i, γ_i)`.
3. **The promotion in b.** Put

   ```text
   Ψ = { (B_i, γ_i) : i ∈ I_0 }  ∪  { (Y ∖ U^b_s, s) : s ∈ F }.
   ```

   Then `cost(Ψ) ≤ Σ_{i ∈ I_0} μ(A_i) + 2δ|F| ≤ cost(Φ) + ε < P(a; H', H) + 2ε`.
4. **Ψ promotes.** Fix `s ∈ F` and `y ∈ Y`. If `y ∈ D^b(w)` for some
   `w ∈ W_s`, then `s^b y = w^b y`, by the word calculus of the transfer
   artifact (`D^b(w)` has the same formula, and `w^b = v(w)^b` there, with no
   freeness of `b` needed). This is a chain of `h^b`-steps (`h ∈ H'`) and
   `Ψ`-steps. Otherwise `y ∈ Y ∖ U^b_s`, and the fix-up piece sends `y` to
   `s^b y`. So the graph of `s^b` lies in `E_{b|H'} ∨ Ψ` for every `s ∈ F`.
   The relation `E_{b|H}` is generated by the maps `g^b` for `g` in the
   generating set `H' ∪ F` (transfer artifact §1). Hence
   `E_{b|H'} ∨ Ψ ⊇ E_{b|H}`.

So `P(b; H', H) < P(a; H', H) + 2ε` for every `ε > 0`. ∎

**Remarks.**
- Only one stage is needed, because `H` is finitely generated over `H'`.
  So only one conjugator `S` is needed. The multi-stage device of Theorem A
  is not needed and would not apply. Labels outside `H` are never absorbed by
  an exhaustion of `H`.
- As in Theorem A, freeness is used only for `a`, in Lemma 2.
- The case `H' = {e}` with `Γ = H` finitely generated is Kechris's
  monotonicity of cost. The case `H = Γ` is Theorem A for groups finitely
  generated over the subgroup.
- What is new is `H ≠ Γ`. Graphings may use labels outside `H`, and these are
  exactly the "deep labels" of `fpbs-mal-floor-iff-deep-promotion-floor`.

## 2. Corollaries for the depth costs Q_j

**Corollary 1.** Let `c` be any free p.m.p. action of `L = F_2` on a
standard non-atomic space, and `j ≥ 0`. Then:

- (a) `Q_j(c) ≤ Q_j(ρ)`, with equality if also `c ≼ ρ`. In particular
  `Q_j(ρ) = max_c Q_j(c)` over free `c`, and `Q_j` is constant on
  weak-equivalence classes of free actions.
- (b) `Q_j(c) ≤ relC(E_{c|L_j}; E_{c|L_{j+1}}) ≤ Q_0(ρ) = r`.

*Proof.*
- (a) By Abért–Weiss Theorem 1 (node
  `abert-weiss-free-actions-weakly-contain-bernoulli`): "Let Γ be a countable
  infinite group and let f be a free p.m.p. action of Γ. Then f weakly
  contains every Bernoulli action of Γ". So `ρ ≼ c`. `ρ` is free. Apply
  Theorem M with `(a, b) = (ρ, c)`, `H' = L_{j+1}`, `H = L_j` and
  `F = {t_j}`. If `c ≼ ρ`, apply Theorem M with `(a, b) = (c, ρ)` as well.
- (b) A family with labels in `L_j` that generates `E_{c|L_j}` over
  `E_{c|L_{j+1}}` is admissible for `Q_j(c)`. This gives the first
  inequality.
  - For the second, let `c^{(j)} = c ∘ φ^j`, an action of `L`. It is free,
    because `φ^j` is injective and `c` is free. We have
    `E_{c^{(j)}} = E_{c|L_j}`, and `E_{c^{(j)}|K} = E_{c|L_{j+1}}` since
    `φ^j(K) = φ^j(φ(L)) = L_{j+1}`.
  - Pieces `(A, g)` of `c^{(j)}` correspond to pieces `(A, φ^j(g))` of
    `c|L_j`, with the same cost. So the middle term equals
    `relC(E_{c^{(j)}}; E_{c^{(j)}|K})`.
  - By Corollary 5 of the transfer artifact, this is at most
    `relC(E_ρ; E_{ρ|K}) = Q_0(ρ)`. ∎

**Corollary 2 (per-depth witnesses).** The following are equivalent:

1. `fpbs-mal-bernoulli-single-stage-floor`;
2. `inf_j Q_j(ρ) > 0`;
3. there is `c > 0` such that for every `j` there is **some** free p.m.p.
   action `a_j` of `F_2` on a standard non-atomic space with `Q_j(a_j) ≥ c`.

The actions `a_j` may be unrelated to each other and non-ergodic. They need
not be `θ`-invariant, need not extend to `Gamma_mal`, and need not be mixing
or factors of iid.

*Proof.*
- 1 ⇔ 2 is `fpbs-mal-floor-iff-deep-promotion-floor`.
- 2 ⇒ 3: take `a_j = ρ`.
- 3 ⇒ 2: `Q_j(ρ) ≥ Q_j(a_j) ≥ c` by Corollary 1(a). ∎

**Corollary 3 (refutation side).** The floor fails iff for every `ε > 0`
there are `j` and a free action `b ≼ ρ` of `F_2` with `Q_j(b) < ε`. For
example, `b` can be a free factor of `ρ` or a free factor of iid. This is
Corollary 1(a) with equality.

**Corollary 4 (Gamma_mal form).** For every free p.m.p. action `α` of
`Gamma_mal`, `P(α; H_1, H_2) ≤ P(β; H_1, H_2) = q_∞`. This is Theorem M with
`Γ = Gamma_mal`, `H' = H_1`, `H = H_2 = ⟨H_1, b_2⟩`, and Abért–Weiss. So a
single free action of `Gamma_mal` with positive promotion cost from
`E_{H_1}` to `E_{H_2}` would already give the floor, through Proposition Q
(2) of the deep-promotion artifact. Corollary 2 asks for less. A free `α`
of `Gamma_mal` gives, through `ψ_{j+2}`, the `F_2`-action `α|H_{j+2}`, with
`Q_j(α|H_{j+2}) = P(α|H_{j+2}; H_1, H_2) ≥ P(α; H_1, H_2)`. Corollary 2 needs
only such an `F_2`-action at each depth, and it may be different at each
depth.

## 3. Profinite witnesses reduce to a finite covering problem

Let `N_1 ⊇ N_2 ⊇ ⋯` be finite-index normal subgroups of `L` with
`⋂ N_n = {e}`, and `G_n = L / N_n`. Let `a` be the profinite action on
`X = lim G_n` with Haar measure. It is free, so Corollary 2 applies to it.

**Level-n problem.** Fix `n` and `j`. A *level-n family* is a finite list
`(C_i, γ_i)` with `C_i ⊆ G_n` and `γ_i ∈ L`. Its cost is
`Σ |C_i| / |G_n|`. For `x ∈ G_n`, let `W(x) ⊆ L` be the smallest set that
contains `e` and is closed under the moves

- `g ↦ h g` for `h ∈ L_{j+1}`;
- `g ↦ γ_i g` when `g x ∈ C_i`;
- `g ↦ γ_i^{-1} g` when `γ_i^{-1} g x ∈ C_i`.

The family is *admissible* if `t_j ∈ W(x)` for every `x ∈ G_n`. Let
`Q_j^{(n)}` be the minimum cost of an admissible level-n family, or `∞`.

**Proposition F.** `Q_j^{(n)}` is nonincreasing in `n`, and
`Q_j(a) = lim_n Q_j^{(n)}`.

*Proof.*
- **Identification.** A level-n set `C` pulls back to a clopen cylinder
  `C̃ ⊆ X`, and `(C̃, γ)` is a labelled piece of cost `|C| / |G_n|`. By
  freeness, `g ↦ g y` identifies `L` with the orbit of `y`. For `y` over
  `x ∈ G_n`, the point `g y` lies in `C̃_i` iff `g x ∈ C_i`. So the set of
  `g` with `g y` joined to `y` in `E_{a|L_{j+1}} ∨ (C̃_i, γ_i)` is exactly
  `W(x)`.
  - Hence the pulled-back family promotes iff `t_j ∈ W(x)` for every `x`.
    Every fibre has positive measure, and the graph of `t_j` together with
    `E_{a|L_{j+1}}` generates `E_{a|L_j}`.
  - So `Q_j(a) ≤ Q_j^{(n)}`. Level-n families are level-n' families for
    `n' ≥ n`, because `N_{n'} ⊆ N_n`. So `Q_j^{(n)}` is nonincreasing.
- **Approximation.** Take `Φ` admissible for `Q_j(a)` with
  `cost(Φ) < Q_j(a) + ε`. Apply step 1 of Theorem M: a finite set `W` of
  words of value `t_j`, using finitely many pieces `i ∈ I_0`, with union
  `U` of domains of measure `> 1 − ε`. Let `ℓ` be the total number of
  letters in `W`.
  - Cylinders are dense in the measure algebra of `X`. So there are a level
    `n` and cylinders `C̃_i` with `μ(A_i Δ C̃_i) < ε / (ℓ + |I_0|)` for
    `i ∈ I_0`.
  - Each domain `D(w)` is an intersection of `|w|` translates of the `A_i`,
    and translates preserve measure. So replacing `A_i` by `C̃_i` changes
    `μ(U)` by less than `ε`. The new union `U'` is a Boolean combination of
    translates of level-n cylinders. The projection `X → G_n` is
    `L`-equivariant, so `U'` is itself a level-n cylinder, and
    `μ(U') > 1 − 2ε`.
  - The level-n family `(C_i, γ_i)_{i ∈ I_0} ∪ (G_n ∖ U'_n, t_j)` is
    admissible, where `U'_n` is the image of `U'` in `G_n`. Its cost is
    `< cost(Φ) + ε + 2ε`. So `inf_n Q_j^{(n)} ≤ Q_j(a) + 4ε`. ∎

**Reading.** A profinite witness at depth `j` is a chain for which every
level-n family that realises the word `t_j` over `L_{j+1}` on all of
`G_n`, with arbitrary labels in `L`, has normalised size at least `c`.
Admissibility is a statement about paths in the infinite tree of `L`,
labelled by a finite `G_n`-colouring. It is not a finite-graph
connectivity statement. In the finite action on `G_n`, joining the
`L_{j+1}`-orbits inside each `L_j`-orbit costs only
`1/[L_{j+1}N_n : N_n] − 1/[L_j N_n : N_n]`, which tends to `0`. That is why
Proposition F keeps the tree.

## 4. What changes, and the next decomposition

- **The lower-bound side is no longer Bernoulli-specific.** Every recorded
  lower-bound argument for the floor uses the Bernoulli shift or its weak
  class:
  - w8 Theorem A uses iid seeds and the entropy estimate of Lemma 4;
  - w9 Theorem B is stated for actions weakly contained in Bernoulli.

  After Corollary 2, a depth-`j` bound on **any** free action of `F_2`
  suffices, provided the constant is uniform in `j`. Only the refutation side
  (Corollary 3) is tied to the weak class of `ρ`.
- **Deep labels cannot be removed by choosing a nicer action.**
  Corollary 1(b) caps every witness at `r`. Corollary 1(a) says that no
  witness beats `ρ`. So a witness helps only as a proof device, where some
  structure (a profinite tower, a finite model, an explicit geometry) makes a
  lower bound provable. It cannot be a source of extra cost.
- **New hole (W).** There are `c > 0` and, for each `j`, a free action
  `a_j` of `F_2` with `Q_j(a_j) ≥ c`. By Corollary 2 this is equivalent to
  the floor. It splits into two parts, each of which can fail on its own:
  - (W-prof) a profinite chain for each `j` with `inf_n Q_j^{(n)} ≥ c`,
    a statement about finite `G_n`-colourings of the tree of `L`
    (Proposition F);
  - (W-sof) a sofic or local-global limit of finite Schreier graphs of `F_2`,
    with a relative version of the Carderi–Gaboriau–de la Salle identity
    `C(a_u) = cC_u((a_n)_n)` (node
    `cgdls-ultraproduct-cost-equals-combinatorial-cost`, item 5), which is
    imported there only for absolute cost. A relative ultraproduct identity
    is **not** available in the graph, and is recorded as a need.
- **Heuristic, not a kill.** In the level-n problem, rank counting alone
  gives nothing. The vertex groups `N_n ∩ L_{j+1}` have total rank
  `Σ_O (1 + |O|)` over the `L_{j+1}`-orbits `O` of an `L_j`-orbit, while
  `N_n ∩ L_j` has rank `1 + Σ |O|`, because `rk L_j = rk L_{j+1} = 2`. So a
  surjection needs `e ≥ 0` extra edges. Any profinite bound must therefore
  use the fold structure of `L_{j+1} ≤ L_j` (malnormality, the triples
  `T_w`), not an Euler characteristic. This matches
  `fpbs-relative-fixed-price-free-pairs`, whose conjectured value is
  `(rk L − rk K)^+ = 0`.
