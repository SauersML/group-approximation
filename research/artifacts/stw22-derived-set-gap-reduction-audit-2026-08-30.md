# STW XXII: derived-set fibre-gap reduction audit

## Exact successor step

For the trivial UHF field over a compact metrizable zero-dimensional space,
restriction to the derived set gives a quotient map between the fibre gaps.
The kernel contributes no bounded trace.  Therefore the bounded-trace
obstruction is unchanged by one Cantor--Bendixson derivative.

Surjectivity uses the Dugundji extension theorem in the closed convex
operator-norm ball of the `L^2` completion of the hyperfinite factor.

## Damping mechanism

A positive kernel class can be changed modulo the central norm-fibre ideal
so that its representative vanishes on the entire derived set.  The
complement of the derived set consists of isolated points, where
orthogonalizers may be chosen independently.  The products

```text
u_j (b-epsilon)_+^(1/2)
```

are nevertheless `2`-norm continuous at every derived point, because their
`2`-norm is controlled by the vanishing positive section.  This yields
arbitrarily many Murray--von Neumann copies of every spectral cut and kills
all bounded traces on the kernel.

## Reach and boundary

Iteration proves the trace conclusion for every compact metrizable
scattered base of finite Cantor--Bendixson height, and at every point of
finite rank even when the global height is larger.  A limit ordinal is not
reached by any finite iteration, so no claim is made there.
