# Toeplitz coordinate Paulis give polynomial Gram localization without a Clifford orientation

## Audit of the Jacobi-expander route

The words in `toeplitz-head-jacobi-words-give-uniform-expanders` have a
uniform adjoint gap on the canonical irreducible spin module.  In an
arbitrary exact representation of the finite Jacobi table, however, a
normalizer has the form

```text
C_s tensor V_s,                                         (TCP1)
```

where `V_s` is an unconstrained representation on the spin multiplicity.
The channel generated by `(TCP1)` need not be the canonical spin channel
tensor the identity.  The maximally-entangled countermodel in
`normalizer-multiplicity-twist-breaks-prefix-expander-rounding` is exact.
Thus the Toeplitz normalizer words do not prove the depth-independent claim
`uniform-relative-clifford-tape-cell`.

The Toeplitz head nevertheless contains a twist-free replacement.  Its old
depth-`n` Pauli prefix exactifies as

```text
M_(2^n)(C) tensor I_r,                                  (TCP2)
```

with coordinate Pauli generators `P_i,Q_i`, `1<=i<=n`, represented as the
canonical Pauli matrices tensor `I_r`.  No normalizer orientation occurs in
these words.

## Polynomial relative-cell rounding

Let `Y` be a contraction and put

```text
epsilon_n(Y)^2=(1/(2n)) sum_(i=1)^n
 (||[Y,P_i]||_2^2+||[Y,Q_i]||_2^2).                    (TCP3)
```

The coordinate Pauli expectation gives

```text
dist_2(Y,I_(2^n) tensor M_r)
 <=sqrt(n/2) epsilon_n(Y).                              (TCP4)
```

Apply `(TCP4)` to a proposed new Pauli pair.  Conditional expectation,
functional calculus, and fixed-D8 flexible stability then exactify the new
cell inside the residual factor with loss

```text
O(sqrt(n) epsilon_n+delta_cell).                        (TCP5)
```

The finite head construction supplies polynomial-length words and
polynomial derivations for every old coordinate and every one-cell
inclusion relation.  Hence a microstate of defining defect `delta` gives

```text
epsilon_n+delta_cell <= poly(n) delta,                  (TCP6)
```

so `(TCP5)` is polynomial in `n`, independent of ambient matrix dimension
and external multiplicity.  This does not establish a depth-independent
modulus, but polynomial loss is the strength actually used in the
geometrically weighted sequential Schur ledger.

## Direct localization of the Fanizza Gram

The same estimate applies without first interpreting `Y` as a Pauli cell.
Let `E,H` be projections with `H<=E`, put `F=E-H`, let `U` be unitary, and
define

```text
X=FUE,                 q=E-X^*X.                        (TCP7)
```

Assume `E` and `H` commute with every old coordinate Pauli word.  Then for
each such word `W`,

```text
[X,W]=F[U,W]E,
||[q,W]||_2=||[X^*X,W]||_2<=2||[U,W]||_2.              (TCP8)
```

The first equality uses the support commutations.  The second bound follows
from

```text
[X^*X,W]=X^*[X,W]+[X^*,W]X                             (TCP9)
```

and `||X||<=1`.  Therefore, with

```text
epsilon_U^2=(1/(2n))sum_i
 (||[U,P_i]||_2^2+||[U,Q_i]||_2^2),                    (TCP10)
```

one has the exact dimension-free-in-matrix-size estimate

```text
dist_2(q,I_(2^n) tensor M_r)<=sqrt(2n) epsilon_U.       (TCP11)
```

This is precisely algebra-factor localization of the Gram deficiency when
the Toeplitz Pauli prefix is installed on the multiplicity coordinate: its
commutant is the complementary Fanizza label/residual factor.  It is immune
to `(TCP1)` because it never averages Clifford normalizers.

## Remaining compiler boundary

Equation `(TCP11)` is an analytic localization theorem, not yet a new
Fanizza relation.  To apply it, the fixed presentation must make the native
unitary `U` commute, at polynomial derivation cost, with every addressed old
coordinate Pauli word on the same selected C18 carrier.  The Toeplitz
one-cell inclusion table provides these rows when `U` is the new tape cell;
it does not automatically identify the unrelated Fanizza triangle chord
with that cell.

Thus the remaining global coherence gate is no longer actor orientation or
spin expansion.  It is a same-word incidence compiler placing the Fanizza
chord in the Toeplitz new-cell coordinate while retaining the original C18
selector cut.  Once those mixed coordinate commutators are available,
`(TCP11)` localizes the Gram with only polynomial depth loss, and the
finite-chart trace quantization can be applied on the label side.
