# Sofic recognition on finite presentations: what the machine groups give and where amplification dies

Lane `ex-complexity-sofic`, 2026-09-12.  Unreviewed.  Builds on
`research/artifacts/rf-fp-pumped-minsky-part1-2026-09-12.md` (and parts 2, 3).

## 1. A structural dichotomy for any sofic switch

Let `e |-> H_e` be a computable family of finite presentations with `H_e`
sofic for `e in INF` and nonsofic for `e in FIN`.

**(D1) No fixed killable seed.**  Suppose, for some nonsofic group `E`, every
`H_e` contains a copy `kappa_e(E)` given by words computed from `e`, and on
`INF` the image `kappa_e(E)` is a proper quotient of `E`.  Then some fixed
nontrivial `k in E` maps to `1` on `INF`.  If `E` is simple, `kappa_e(k) = 1`
for every nontrivial `k`, and this is a finite derivation.  So
`{e : kappa_e(k) = 1}` is `Sigma^0_1` and contains `INF`.  It must also contain
some `FIN` indices, otherwise `INF` would be `Sigma^0_1`.  On those indices the
seed is killed, and the switch must survive through other copies.  So a
completeness proof needs an unbounded family of seeds `kappa_(e,N)(E)` whose
killing events `N` are individually `Sigma^0_1`.

**(D2) Independent seeds need non-conjugate propagation.**  Conjugate copies
are killed together.  A finite presentation can carry infinitely many
independently killable copies in only two known ways:

- by *Higman propagation* (benign subgroups, HNN towers over free edges).  This
  is the existing compiler, whose positive branch is the Mikhailova rope
  (`sofic-safe-finite-presentation-compiler`);
- by *module propagation* inside an abelian normal subgroup, as in the
  Baumslag--Remeslennikov lemma and the Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Sapir
  machine groups.  There the copies are coordinates of an elementary abelian
  group.

In the second mechanism the seeds are abelian, so nonsoficity cannot be
embedded and must *emerge*.  The only known emergent mechanism with abelian
lamps is the Kun--Thom one: `(Z/2) wr_(G/Gamma) G` and `G *_Gamma G` are
nonsofic for infranormal non-normal Kazhdan pairs `Gamma <= G`
(`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath`, `kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic`).

## 2. The switchable center of the machine group

Let `M_e` be the clocked search machine of
`clocked-search-machine-halts-universally-exactly-on-inf`, let `P(M_e)` be its
pumped version, and let `G_e = G(P(M_e))` (prime `p = 2`).  Let `Phi_e` be the
subgroup generated by the *full configuration words*
`w(c) = x_(q_i A_0) * a^(m) * A_1 * ... * A_(K+2)`.

**Lemma (center switch).**  `Phi_e` is a central elementary abelian 2-subgroup
of `G_e`, and

```text
e in INF  ==>  Phi_e = 1,
e in FIN  ==>  Phi_e != 1, and Phi_e meets the profinite kernel nontrivially.
```

*Proof.*

- *Centrality.*  In the model `Ḡ` (KMS pages 22--24) a full configuration word
  maps to the single basis vector `z_(1,u(c))`, by Lemma 4.9, where `u(c)`
  contains every `A`-letter.  Every letter acts trivially on such a vector: `a_j`, `a_j'` and
  `A_j` because `u(c)` contains `A_j`, and `ã_j`, `ã_j'` because `u(c)`
  contains `A_0`.  So `w(c)` is central in `Ḡ`.  For a letter `y`, the
  commutator `[w(c), y]` lies in `T` and maps to `1`.  Since `T -> T_1` is
  injective (Lemma 4.14), it is trivial.  `T` is abelian, so `w(c)` is
  central.  (This is also the content of G5c and Lemma 4.8.)
- *INF.*  `M_e` is simple, so at a nonzero command exactly one command applies,
  and it is universally halting.  Hence every full configuration reaches
  command `0`, and so does its lift in `P(M_e)`.  So every full configuration
  word equals the stop word, whose image in `Ḡ` is `z_(1,0) = 1`, hence it is
  trivial in `G_e`.
- *FIN.*  The pumped divergent configuration `c'` of Lemma A is full, and
  `w(c') != 1` lies in every finite quotient's kernel
  (`pumped-divergent-minsky-word-lies-in-profinite-kernel`).  QED

So a finitely presented amenable group carries a canonical central coefficient
group that vanishes exactly on `INF`.  Any construction `Amp` with

```text
Amp(G, Phi) sofic when Phi = 1 (G amenable, residually finite),
Amp(G, Phi) nonsofic when Phi != 1,
Amp finitely presented, uniformly,                                  (AMP)
```

would give `SOFIC_fp` `Pi^0_2`-complete.

## 3. Amplifier candidates and where each dies

Fix an infranormal non-normal Kazhdan pair `Gamma <= G`, both finitely
presented (Theorem E of Kun--Thom gives residually finite ones; finite
presentation of the Steinberg versions is a separate import).

**(A1) Lamps on the whole abelian normal subgroup.**  Take
`(F_2[G/Gamma] tensor T_e) x| (G x A_e)`.  It is nonsofic for every `e`: the
partial-word coordinates of `T_e` are nonzero on `INF` too, and each nonzero
coordinate spans a copy of `(Z/2) wr_(G/Gamma) G`.  *Dies: wrong locus.*

**(A2) Lamps on full coordinates only.**  Give full coordinates coefficients
in `F_2[G/Gamma]` and partial coordinates coefficients `F_2`.  Completing a
partial word by its last letter `A_j` sends `x_u` to `x_u x_(uA_j)`, so a
lamp `delta_Gamma tensor x_(uA_j)` has to be chosen.  Then `g` in `G` and
`A_j` do not commute on the module: `[A_j, g]` shifts
`delta_Gamma -> delta_(gGamma)`.  Quotienting the partial coordinates to kill
the `G`-action leaks into the full coordinates through `A_j`, and kills the
lamps on `FIN`.  *Dies: the submodule is not invariant.*

**(A3) Central product of lamp copies.**

```text
Lambda = { (g_x)_(x in G/Gamma) in prod G_e :  g_x g_y^(-1) in Phi_e,
           finitely many differences },          H_e = Lambda x| G.
```

Since `Phi_e` is central, `(g, phi) |-> (g phi_x)_x` identifies `Lambda` with
`G_e x directSum_(G/Gamma) Phi_e`, and `G` acts only on the second factor.  So

```text
H_e ~= G_e x ( Phi_e wr_(G/Gamma) G ).
```

On `INF` this is `G_e x G`, which is sofic.  On `FIN` it contains
`(Z/2) wr_(G/Gamma) G`, which is nonsofic.  *Both polarities are right.
Dies at finite presentation.*

- On `FIN`, `H_e` is not even finitely generated, since `Phi_e` is not.  A
  finitely presented overgroup must generate the lamp coordinate at `Gamma`
  from finitely many elements.
- The machine letters, the only operators that manufacture configuration
  words, act *trivially* on `Phi_e`.  So the lamp coordinate at `Gamma` has to
  be the image of a machine module that carries partial coordinates.
- `G`-translates of those partial coordinates are exactly (A1).  Removing them
  requires identifying partial coordinates across all cosets, and those are
  infinitely many relations.

This is the Cornulier obstruction in another guise.  Permutational wreath
products `H wr_X G` are finitely presented only when point stabilizers have
finitely many orbits on `X` (Cornulier, *Finitely presented wreath products
and double coset decompositions*, Geom. Dedicata 122 (2006)).  No infranormal
non-normal Kazhdan pair with `Gamma \ G / Gamma` finite is known here:
Bruhat-type finiteness occurs for parabolic subgroups, which lack (T).

**(A4) Twisted doubles.**  Twisting the amalgam `G *_Gamma G` by a central
cocycle `gamma_1 = gamma_2 phi(gamma)` with values in `Phi_e` gives the untwisted
double, which is nonsofic, when `Phi_e = 1`.  *Dies: wrong polarity.*
Untwisted doubles over `Phi`-dependent subgroups are `Sigma^0_1`-switched,
since subgroup equality for finitely generated subgroups is certified finitely
(`finitely-generated-centralizer-switches-are-sigma1`).

**(A5) Amenable-by-sofic permanence.**  `Phi_e` is central, and any amplifier
extending `G_e` by a sofic quotient on which `Phi_e` acts would need "central
by sofic implies sofic" in reverse.  Whether a nonsofic central extension of a
sofic group by an elementary abelian group exists is itself unknown here.
Kun--Thom's wreath products show only that *non-central* amenable normal
subgroups can break permanence.

## 4. The question that remains

(A3) isolates one concrete finite-presentation problem.

> **Q.**  For an infranormal non-normal Kazhdan pair `Gamma <= G`, is there a
> finitely presented group `K_e`, computable from `e`, such that `K_e` is
> sofic when `e in INF`, and `K_e` contains `Phi_e wr_(G/Gamma) G` when
> `e in FIN`?  The relations of `K_e` would have to force lamp generators to
> vanish when `Phi_e = 1` and to spread over `G/Gamma` when it is not.

A positive answer makes `SOFIC_fp` `Pi^0_2`-complete through the center switch
plus Kun--Thom Theorem A.  (A1)--(A3) show that the naive lamp modules either
leak partial coordinates into the `INF` branch or need infinitely many
identification relations.  A solution must let machine letters act on lamp
coordinates without creating `G`-moved partial coordinates.
