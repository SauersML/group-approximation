# STW XXVII(2): high-rank dimension-drop audit (2026-08-30)

## Result

Let `E` have rank `M` over a finite CW complex of dimension `d`, and set
`D=ceil(d/2)`.  If

```text
M >= (2n+1)D+n(n-1),
```

then `E` splits as `nF direct_sum (n+1)G`.  This produces both a compact
`(n,n+1)`-divider and a unital endpoint representation
`Z_(n,n+1)-->End(E)`.

Endpoint factorization is exactly equivalent to such a bundle splitting:
the two central projections split the bundle, and Morita equivalence turns
a unital `M_k`-action on a summand into a multiplicity bundle tensored with
`C^k`.  Arbitrary dimension-drop fields may additionally use moving
interior representations; the theorem constructs an endpoint field but
does not classify those rank-jumping fields.

The construction absorbs an arbitrary reduced class
`delta=[E]-rank(E)[1]` by the Bezout identity

```text
delta = -n delta +(n+1)delta.
```

Ranks at least `D` turn `f[1]-delta` and `g[1]+delta` into honest bundles;
the Frobenius conductor `n(n-1)` makes the remaining scalar rank equal to
`na+(n+1)b`; stable cancellation then gives an actual, not merely stable,
decomposition.

## Villadsen consequence

For `X=(S^2)^N`, `D=N`.  Hence every bundle of rank

```text
M >= (2n+1)N+n(n-1)
```

has a compact divider, regardless of its coordinate-Hopf Chern data.  This
provides the high-rank half of the finite-stage phase diagram:

```text
M < n(n+1):
    every soft divider rounds to a projection;

prime n and (n+1)N_n > M:
    the mod-n Chern class forbids that projection divider;

M >= (2n+1)N+n(n-1):
    an endpoint dimension-drop field and compact divider always exist.
```

The middle ratio window remains open.  For multiplicity-free coordinate
summands, the Chern obstruction requires `M/N<n+1`, whereas the uniform
stable-range construction applies asymptotically when `M/N>2n+1`.  A
rank-jumping dimension-drop field could improve the positive threshold;
the induced map on ordinary `K`-theory supplies no obstruction by itself,
because the prime dimension-drop algebra has `K_0=Z` generated by its unit
and `K_1=0`.  Secondary constraints on an actual representation field are
not ruled out by this observation.

## Checks

- The stable-range realization and cancellation bound is the standard
  complex vector-bundle stability theorem; `D=ceil(d/2)` is a safe common
  bound for both steps.
- The Frobenius conductor for the coprime integers `n,n+1` is
  `n(n+1)-n-(n+1)+1=n(n-1)`.
- Both bundle comparisons are direct summand inclusions; no strict
  comparison, trace argument, or soft-element rounding is used.
- The dimension-drop map is unital; its extension from
  `C(X) tensor Z_(n,n+1)` is `C(X)`-linear because it is the direct sum of
  the two endpoint quotient representations with multiplicity bundles `F`
  and `G`.
