# UN (U3), part 2: exact matricial models of aperiodic SFT crossed products are periodic quantum tilings

Lane `un-converse`, 2026-09-13. Status: Proposition E is proved below, and Corollary G follows from two established
peer nodes. **Unreviewed.** Part 1: `research/artifacts/un-converse-stable-finiteness-boundary-2026-09-13.md`.

**Why.** `stably-finite-simple-rings-have-mf-elementary-groups` and `stably-mf-elementary-groups-force-matricial-rings`
bracket the ring condition for stable MF of elementary groups over simple rings. A simple finitely generated stably
finite ring that is **not** exactly matricial separates them: its elementary groups are MF (refuting the second claim)
or not (refuting the first). Here the non-matricial property of aperiodic `Z^2` SFT crossed products is tied to
finite presentation, through the quantum-rigidity program of the Labbé lanes.

## 0. Setting

- Let `Ω ⊆ A^(Z^2)` be a subshift of finite type with no periodic point, and `k` a field.
- `R_Ω = LC(Ω,k) ⋊ Z^2` is generated by cylinder idempotents and commuting units `u_1, u_2`, with
  `u^v χ_C u^(−v) = χ_(v·C)`.
- `P^a_z = u^z χ[x_0 = a] u^(−z)` is the tile idempotent at site `z`.
- **(Q1)–(Q3)** are the axioms of `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid` at scale `D`:
  - (Q1) partitions of unity at each site;
  - (Q2) commutation for `|z − z'| <= 2D`;
  - (Q3) vanishing of the products `∏_(h ∈ B_D) E_(π(h))(z + h)` over patterns `π` that do not occur.
- By `periodic-quantum-tilings-refute-quantum-rigidity` (item 2), over a finite field the nonzero finite-dimensional
  modules of the local presentation `A_D = C_D ⋊ Z^2` are exactly the *periodic quantum tilings* at scale `D`:
  families `E_a(z)` satisfying (Q1)–(Q3), periodic under a finite-index subgroup of `Z^2`.
- **Exactly matricial** means that `R_Ω` embeds unitally in an algebraic ultraproduct `∏_ω M_(d_k)(F_k)` over fields.

## 1. Proposition E

**Proposition E.** If `R_Ω` is exactly matricial, then for every `D >= 1` there are a finite field `F`, `d >= 1`, and a
periodic quantum tiling of `Ω` at scale `D` on `F^d`.

*Proof.*
1. **One coordinate.** Let `W ⊆ R_Ω` be the finite set consisting of:
   - `u_1^(±1)`, `u_2^(±1)`, and the tile idempotents `χ[x_0 = a]`;
   - their conjugates by `u^v` for `‖v‖_∞ <= 2D + 1`;
   - all products of at most `|B_D|` of these;
   - and the products of pairs of all these elements.

   By Łoś, ω-almost every coordinate `ψ` preserves every sum and product internal to `W` and keeps each nonzero
   element of `W` nonzero. Fix one such coordinate, with values in `M_d(F_k)`.
2. **A finite field.** The entries of `ψ(W)` generate a finitely generated subring `A ⊆ F_k`. `A` is a commutative
   domain and a Jacobson ring whose residue fields at maximal ideals are finite.
   - The product `a` of one nonzero entry from each nonzero `ψ(χ[x_0 = b])` is nonzero.
   - So some maximal ideal `m` misses `a`.
   - Reduction mod `m` is a unital ring map `π : M_d(A) → M_d(F)`, `F = A/m` finite. It keeps those idempotents
     nonzero and preserves the identities internal to `W`.
3. **Periodicity.** `U_i = πψ(u_i)` are commuting units of the finite ring `M_d(F)`, so `U_i^(o_i) = 1` for some
   `o_i >= 1`. Put `Λ = o_1 Z × o_2 Z` and `E_a(z) = U^z πψ(χ[x_0 = a]) U^(−z)` for `z ∈ Z^2`. Then `E(z + λ) = E(z)`
   for `λ ∈ Λ`, and `U_i E_a(z) U_i^(−1) = E_a(z + e_i)`.
4. **Axioms.** Identities of `R_Ω` inside `W` carry over. For `‖z' − z‖_∞ <= 2D`,
   `E_a(z) E_b(z') = U^z πψ(χ[x_0 = a] · u^(z'−z) χ[x_0 = b] u^(−(z'−z))) U^(−z)`, and likewise in the other order.
   - (Q2): the two cylinder functions commute in `LC(Ω,k)`.
   - (Q1): it holds at the origin and is transported by conjugation.
   - (Q3): a product over `B_D` for a pattern `π` that does not occur is, after conjugation by `U^z`, the image of
     `χ` of an empty cylinder, which is 0.
   - Nonzero: each `E_a(z)` is conjugate to a nonzero idempotent.

   So `F^d` carries a periodic quantum tiling at scale `D`. ∎

## 2. Corollary G

**Corollary G.** If `R_Ω` is exactly matricial, then `Ω` is not `D`-quantum rigid for any `D`, and `R_Ω` is not
finitely presented. Contrapositively, a finitely presented `R_Ω` (for instance a quantum rigid aperiodic SFT) is a
simple, finitely generated, stably finite ring that is not exactly matricial, whenever `Ω` is free and minimal.

*Proof.* By Proposition E, the local presentation `A_D` has a nonzero finite-dimensional module for every `D`.
- By item 1 of `periodic-quantum-tilings-refute-quantum-rigidity`, `Ω` is not `D`-quantum rigid.
- By `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`, `R_Ω` is not finitely presented.
- The contrapositive adds simplicity and finite generation, from `free-minimal-subshift-elementary-groups-are-simple-kazhdan`,
  and stable finiteness, from the unital rank model in the same node, since rank ultraproducts are stably finite. ∎
