# 5. The fence (landed 9492e7cb16)

`bootstrap-zp-actions-realize-every-cyclotomic-k-module`.  Köhler's invariant
for `Z/p` is `U = M_0 ⊕ M_1 ⊕ M_2`, a module over the path ring `K_p` of three
vertices `C(Z/p)`, `C`, `C_p` (Meyer--Nadareishvili arXiv:2604.12529v2,
Theorem 2.7).  For a `G`-algebra with contractible restriction:

* `M_0 = K_*(Res A) = 0`.  Meyer--Nadareishvili Proposition 5.1 then forces
  the normal form `Q(0, 0, M)` with `M = K_*(A ⋊ G)`, a Z/2-graded
  `O = Z[ζ_p, 1/p]`-module on which the dual action is `ζ_p`.
* Every countable `M` is exact.  The only non-formal check is that
  `α_12 = 1 − ζ` is bijective, which holds because `1 − ζ` is a unit of `O`.
  So every countable `M` is realized in `B^G` (Theorem 2.9).
* Köhler's UCT (Theorem 2.10) needs only the source in `B^G`.  So for every
  action `α` on `O_2` there is `x ∈ KK^G(A, (O_2, α))` from a bootstrap model
  inducing an isomorphism of invariants.  The action is in `B^G` iff `x` is
  invertible.

Answer to the brief's question.  An explicit `Z/p`-action on `O_2` whose
crossed product has a K-theoretic signature incompatible with the UCT does not
exist, whatever K-theoretic data is used, equivariant or not.  A witness is the
cone of `x`: an equivariant phantom with contractible restriction and zero
invariant.

# 6. Class groups (landed a8481ac4e9)

`cyclotomic-class-group-counts-zp-actions-on-stable-o2`.  Two inputs:
- Meyer's realization of `B^G`-classes by pointwise outer actions on Kirchberg
  algebras;
- Gabe--Szabó's dynamical Kirchberg--Phillips theorem (arXiv:2205.04933,
  Theorem A, verbatim in the proof route).

With these, bootstrap outer `Z/p`-actions on `O_2 ⊗ K` up to cocycle conjugacy
correspond to countable graded `O`-modules.  The rank-one torsion-free layer is
`Cl(Q(ζ_p))`.  All of its crossed products are one algebra, and the dual
actions are told apart by ideal classes: `h_23 = 3`.  At `p = 2`, `O = Z[1/2]`
and `[α̂] = −1`.

# 7. The equivariant face (landed f8d569a7ab)

`zp-algebras-with-contractible-restriction-are-cyclotomic`.  The cone of
`1 − χ` on `C` is, up to suspension, the free rotation circle `C(S^1)`.  If
`Res D ≃ 0`, then `D ⊗ C(S^1)` is locally induced, hence KK^G-contractible, so
`1 − χ` acts invertibly on `KK^G(D, D)`.  This is the Baaj--Skandalis dual of
the crossed-product theorem of part 1.

# 8. The sharpest open target (landed a8481ac4e9)

`zp-restriction-and-crossed-product-detect-kk-g-contractibility`.  For
`G = Z/p` and nuclear `D`: do `Res D ≃ 0` and `D ⋊ G ≃ 0` force
`D ≃_(KK^G) 0`?

In Meyer--Nest terms this asks whether the homological ideal of the exact
functor `(Res, ⋊ G) : KK^G → KK × KK` has no nonzero contractible objects.
Equivalently, whether `KK^G` is generated by induced algebras and
trivial-action algebras.  This is a noncommutative isotropy-separation
principle.

* **If true:** for actions on `O_2`, the crossed product is UCT iff the action
  is in `B^G`.  With Barlak--Li Theorem 4.16, whose absorption step is repaired
  in part 1, Problem II at `p` becomes: every outer strongly approximately inner
  `Z/p`-action on `O_2` is in `B^G`.
* **For outer actions:** by Gabe--Szabó Theorem A the question is whether a
  pointwise outer `Z/p`-action on `O_2 ⊗ K` with KK-contractible crossed
  product is cocycle conjugate to `α ⊗ id_(O_2)`.  By the criterion used in
  their Lemma 4.3 (via [102, Lemma 2.12]), that is a unital embedding of `O_2`
  into the fixed points of the central sequence algebra.
* **Attempts that die:**
  - adjunction composites act as the norm `N(s_1)`, which is `0` on the
    relevant objects;
  - isotropy strata do not exist for outer noncommutative actions;
  - the hypotheses are self-dual under Baaj--Skandalis.

# 9. Assessment

Neither side of Problem II moved, and the lane does not claim otherwise.

**What changed.**
1. A structure theorem that holds for every `O_2 ⋊ Z_p`: `Z[ζ_p, 1/p]`-linear
   in KK.
2. A verified repair of the one unverified step in the per-prime reduction.
3. A complete negative answer to the K-theoretic-certificate approach.
4. A reformulation of the positive side as noncommutative isotropy separation
   for `Z/p`.

The structural question "is K-theory all there is?" has a sharp form here.
Over contractible algebras every K-theoretic invariant is cyclotomic arithmetic,
and all of it is realized by bootstrap dynamics.  Whatever is not K-theory is an
equivariant phantom.
