# Boone--Higman for algebras: envelopes, and what the Leavitt route actually needs

Lane `bh-algebra-envelopes`, 2026-09-12. Scope: the wall identified by
`bh-decidable-algebra-hosts` on the algebra route to group Boone--Higman, namely
finitely presented simple algebra envelopes for decidable inputs.

## 1. The algebra conjecture and its necessary condition

`algebra-boone-higman-conjecture` (open root): every finitely generated
associative algebra over a computable field with solvable word problem embeds in
a finitely presented simple algebra.

- **Necessity.** `fp-simple-algebras-have-solvable-word-problem`. This is
  Kuznetsov's argument for ideals: zero is enumerable from the relators, and
  nonzero is enumerable by certificates that the ideal generated by `w` contains
  `1`, or every generator in the non-unital case.
- **Complexity fence.** `no-fp-simple-algebra-hosts-every-decidable-algebra`.
  - *The inputs:* monomial algebras `K<x,y>/(x y^n x : n in X)` encode any
    decidable set `X`.
  - *The reduction:* an embedding turns membership in `X` into the host's word
    problem at linear cost.
  - *The conclusion:* the time hierarchy theorem gives inputs beyond any shared
    recursive bound, so envelopes must grow in complexity.

## 2. Is the algebra form as hard as the group form?

- **Strong form implies group BH.** Central simple hosts `B`, with a finitely
  normally generated Steinberg kernel for `B ⊗ L`, give group Boone--Higman
  (existing route `infinite-simple-input-bh-via-elementary-leavitt-hosts`). So
  that strong form is at least as hard as the group conjecture.
- **Plain form: no known relation.**
  - Group BH does not give algebra envelopes: `K[H]` of an fp simple group is
    never simple, because of the augmentation ideal.
  - Canonical Steinberg algebra hosts of topological full groups do not contain
    the group algebra (`full-group-algebra-to-steinberg-algebra-not-injective`).
- **Obstacles that stay closed.**
  - Tensoring with a central simple algebra cannot create simplicity: the ideals
    of `B ⊗ L` are `I ⊗ L`.
  - The maximal-ideal quotient needs a finitely generated maximal ideal, the ring
    counterpart of finite normal generation.

## 3. Correction to the wall on the Leavitt route

`bh-decidable-algebra-hosts` named algebra Boone--Higman as the wall for the
premise `simple-inputs-have-fp-elementary-leavitt-tensor-hosts`. That premise
only asks for `S -> B^x`, an embedding into units.

- **Units without the group algebra.** `full-group-units-embed-in-steinberg-algebras`:
  the topological full group of any ample groupoid embeds in the units of its
  Steinberg algebra. By the non-injectivity claim, this happens without the
  group algebra embedding.
- **What that makes of algebra BH.** It is sufficient for the existence part of
  the premise, not necessary.
- **The real existence question.** Find a finitely presented central simple
  algebra whose unit group contains `S`. Candidates are Steinberg algebras of
  finite-type ample groupoids (Leavitt path algebras, Nekrashevych algebras),
  matrix algebras over them, and tensor products with `L`.
- **Where new cases must come from.** If `S` sits in a topological full group
  whose derived group is already fp simple, group BH holds there already. New
  cases therefore need unit groups larger than topological full groups. The
  reviewed positive-characteristic linear class is such a case: its linear
  groups sit in `E_n(L ⊗ L)` through matrix units, not inside a full group.

## 4. Untried construction

**Leavitt--Nekrashevych completions** `O_psi = < B, L_d | b = sum s_i psi(b)_(ij) t_j >`
for a matrix recursion `psi : B -> M_d(B)`. The questions: when is `B -> O_psi`
injective, and when is `O_psi` simple? This is the algebra analogue of
Zaremsky's Röver--Nekrashevych theorem for finitely presented self-similar
groups.
