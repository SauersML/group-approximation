# Referee report (gq-referee-a, proof-gap lens): finitely presented simple Q-algebras and finitely presented simple rings

**Reviewed:** `fp-simple-q-algebras-embed-in-fp-simple-rings` and its route `-proof` (lane gq-ring-fp-simple,

**Verdict: PASS for parts 1–3.** One step in §3 needs rewording. The Consequence was not checked.

## §1 The centre of `R_L` (the author's point (d))
- **Off-degree components vanish.** The grading is direct and the core lies in degree 0. So a central `z` has
  components `z_k` that commute with the core, and aperiodicity makes `z_k = 0` for `k ≠ 0`. Aperiodicity was
  already passed at 8f4edc918.
- **Degree 0 is diagonal.** At level `L`: `(s_a t_b) z` has coefficient `Z_{bν}` at `(a,ν)`, and `z(s_a t_b)` has
  `Z_{μa}` at `(μ,b)`. By uniqueness `Z = w I`.
- **`w` is constant.** Padding one level gives the diagonal `diag(σ_1(w), σ_2(w))` on each block. It must again
  be scalar, so `w(N+1) = w(0) ∈ Q`, and hence `w` is a constant in `Q(N)`.
- Correct.

## §2 `R_L ⊗_Q S`
- **Simple.** By the tensor lemma, with `S` simple and `R_L` simple with centre `Q`. Ideals of a `Q`-algebra are
  automatically `Q`-subspaces.
- **Finitely presented over `Z`.** The presentation (`R_L`'s, plus `a_i` commuting with `R_L`'s generators)
  gives `R_L ⊗_Z Z<a> = R_L ⊗_Q Q<a>`.
  - Each relator `f_j` is written with `p·u_q` for `p/q`, and it maps to `1 ⊗ f_j`.
  - The ideal generated by the `1 ⊗ f_j` is `R_L ⊗ J`. Right exactness then gives `R_L ⊗_Q S`.
- **Injective.** `S → R_L ⊗_Q S` is injective over a field. Correct.

## §3 The equivalence
- **Rewording needed.** The step "`e = 1_A`" does not make sense for a non-unital `A`, which the statement
  allows. Use any nonzero `a ∈ A` instead. If `p·1_R = 0`, then `a = p·(a/p) = 0`. So `R` has characteristic 0,
  and it is a `Q`-algebra, as the proof says.
- **The rest is correct.**
  - The two `Q`-structures on `A` agree, because `R` is torsion-free.
  - `R = Q ⊗_Z R` is finitely presented over `Q` by the same data.
  - The converse direction uses §2.
