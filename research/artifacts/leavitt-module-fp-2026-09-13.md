## Argument in brief

1. **Part (i), elementary.** The sheet-swapping involution `σ(aw) = (1-a)w` has
   centralizer `C_V(σ) ≅ C(C, F_2) ⋊ V`, with `V` acting by precomposition. The proof
   in `cantor-module-thompson-v-product-fp-via-mmn-centralisers` computes, for each
   commuting `g`, the pair `(first letter of g(0w), tail of g(0w))`.
2. **Import.** By Corollary 4.11 the centralizer is of type `F_∞`, hence finitely presented.
3. **Generation.** `U = L_(F_2)(1,2)^×` is generated by `V` and `x = 1 + s_(00) t_(01)`
   (`thompson-steinberg-presentation-of-leavitt-unit-group`), and `x^2 = 1`.
4. **Amalgam.** In `(M ⋊ V) *_V U`, six relators `x χ_w x^(-1) = x·χ_w`, for
   `w ∈ {00, 000, 01, 010, 1, 10}`, force `x` to normalize `M`. This uses the subgroup
   `{c(h,k)} ≤ V`: it commutes with `x` and has finitely many orbits on the generating
   indicators of `M`. The quotient is then isomorphic to `M ⋊ U`, because `M` is
   normal, the quotient by `M` is `U`, and `M` injects.
5. **Consequence.** `M ⋊ U` is finitely presented. `U` acts faithfully on `M` with at
   most three orbits, so the affine criterion `orbit-finite-affine-actions-are-type-a`
   makes the affine action of `M ⋊ U` on `M` of type (A). So `U`, and every subgroup
   of it, satisfies permutational Boone–Higman via BFFHZ.
