## 0. Summary

The wave-10 need asked to decide `RC(cdcDcd) = 1` on the Bernoulli shift of `F(c,d)`. Here `RC(v)` is the cheapest
way to generate the full orbit relation from Cayley edges of `F(c,d)` when the `v`-lines are given for free. This is
exactly the `B`-side of the Cayley subgraphing cost of the amalgam `F(a,b) *_{u=v} F(c,d)`, whose instance
`abaBab = cdcDcd` is the sharp test named in the w9 attempt on the target node.

Results (all written deductions, Sections 2-6):

1. **Lemma 1 (contact repair, general).** Let a countable group act freely and p.m.p. Suppose `H` is a subgroup,
   `t` an element, and `I = H cap t^{-1} H t` is infinite. Then for every `eps > 0` the relation of `<H, t>` is
   generated by `R_H` together with the `t`-edges out of a set of measure `< eps`.
2. **Theorem A (contact words).** Let `v` be a word in `F(c,d)`, `{s,t} = {c,d}` and `H_s = <s, v>`. If
   `H_s cap t^{-1} H_s t != 1` for some choice of `s`, then `RC_a(v) = 1` for **every** free action `a` of `F(c,d)`.
3. **Corollary A' (amalgams).** Let `n >= 1`, let `u` be a nontrivial element of `F_n = F(a_1, ..., a_n)`, and let `v`
   be as in Theorem A. Put `G = F_n *_{u=v} F(c,d) = <a_1..a_n, c, d | u v^{-1}>` and `S = {a_1..a_n, c, d}`. Then
   `C_sub^a(S) = n + 1 = 1 + beta_1^(2)(G)` for every free p.m.p. action `a` of `G`. So `G` has fixed price, the hole at
   `S` holds, and `S`-fixed price holds. This includes relators in which every letter is used at least three times,
   such as `abaBab = cccddd`.
4. **Proposition B (letter sandwich).** For every free action,
   `max(1, rho_d(H_c) + rho_c(H_d)) <= RC(v) <= 1 + min(rho_d(H_c), rho_c(H_d))`.
   Here `rho_t(H)` is the cost of repairing `R_H` to `R` using `t`-edges only.
5. **Corollary C (coupling to the Gamma_mal floor).** For `v0 = ccdcDD`, `H_c(v0) = <c, dcDD>`. If the `c`-saturated
   family attains `RC(v0) = 1` on the Bernoulli shift, that is if `rho_d(<c, dcDD>) = 0` there, then
   `C(beta_{Gamma_mal}) = 2`, and `fpbs-mal-bernoulli-single-stage-floor` fails.
6. **Class kill.** Letter-saturated contact repair dies exactly on the words with no contact:
   `rank(H_s cap t^{-1} H_s t) = 0` for both letters. These are `cdcDcd`, `ccdcDD` and 1484 of the 1539 classes of length
   at most 9 in which each letter is used at least 3 times. The invariant is that rank. The step where it dies is the
   small complete section of `E_{a|I}`: when `I = 1` that relation is trivial and its only complete section is the
   whole space. What is left is `rho_t(H_s)` over a malnormal rank-2 `H_s`. That is a free-pairs relative-cost
   problem, and the packing floor of `fpbs-malnormal-coset-cycles-linear-perimeter` applies to it.

Not decided: `RC(cdcDcd)` itself. By Proposition B, a family reaching `1` must be mixed, spending at least
`rho_d(H_c)` on `d`-edges and at least `rho_c(H_d)` on `c`-edges.

## 1. Setup

- `a` is a free p.m.p. action of `F = F(c,d)` on `(X, mu)`, with orbit relation `R = E_a`.
- For `g in F`, `Phi_g` is the graphing `{(x, g x)}`, and `Phi_S = union of Phi_s` over `s in S`.
- For a word `v`, the `v`-line graphing is `S_v = Phi_v`. It is given for free.
- The **relative Cayley cost** is

  ```text
  RC_a(v) = inf { mu(F) : F Borel subgraphing of Phi_c cup Phi_d, <F cup S_v> = R }.
  ```

  Here `mu(F)` is the edge measure, and `<.>` is the generated equivalence relation.
- For a subgroup `H` generated by a finite set `T`, the graphing `Phi_T` generates `R_H = E_{a|H}`, because the
  action is free.
- The **letter repair cost** is

  ```text
  rho_t(H) = inf { mu(E) : E subset Phi_t Borel, <R_H cup E> = R }.
  ```

**`RC >= 1`.** Take any admissible `F`. Then `F cup S_v` is a graphing of `R`, which is the orbit relation of a free
action of `F_2`. Gaboriau's lecture Cor 2.25: "The following groups are strongly treeable and have fixed price:
C∗(Fn) = C∗(Fn) = n for the free group of rank n." So `mu(F) + 1 >= 2`.

**Imports used below.**
- `fpbs-small-marker-induction-input` (ESTABLISHED, Gaboriau lecture Exercise 1.17). An aperiodic p.m.p. relation has
  complete sections of arbitrarily small measure.
- `fpbs-amen2-betti-cost-input` (Gaboriau lecture Theorem 2.64). `beta_1^(2)(Gamma) <= C(a) - 1` for every free
  p.m.p. action `a` of an infinite group.
- `one-relator-group-cost-equals-first-l2-betti-plus-one`. For an infinite `<S | w^m>`, with `w` not a proper power,
  `beta_1^(2) = |S| - 1 - 1/m`. Only the Betti part is used. It is the classical computation, recorded there with
  the Poulin–Wróbel cost statement.

## 2. Lemma 1: contact repair

**Lemma 1.** Let a countable group `Gamma` act freely and p.m.p. on `(X, mu)`. Let `H <= Gamma` and `t in Gamma`, and
suppose `I = H cap t^{-1} H t` is infinite. For every `eps > 0` there is a Borel `M subset X` with `mu(M) < eps` such
that

```text
< R_H  cup  Phi_t|_M >  =  R_{<H, t>},        where Phi_t|_M = {(x, t x) : x in M}.
```

*Proof.*
1. The action of `I` is free and `I` is infinite, so `E_{a|I}` is aperiodic. By `fpbs-small-marker-induction-input`
   it has a Borel complete section `M` with `mu(M) < eps`.
2. The inclusion of the left side in the right side is clear. For the reverse, `R_H cup Phi_t` generates
   `R_{<H,t>}`, so it is enough to show that `(x, t x)` lies in the left side for almost every `x`.
3. Fix `x` and choose `i in I` with `i x in M`, which is possible by completeness of `M`. Then:
   - `(x, i x) in R_H`, since `i in H`;
   - `(i x, t i x) in Phi_t|_M`;
   - `t i x = (t i t^{-1}) t x`, and `t i t^{-1} in H` because `i in t^{-1} H t`. So `(t i x, t x) in R_H`.

   Chaining the three gives `(x, t x)` in the left side. ∎

**Iterated form.** Suppose `Gamma = <H_0, t_1, ..., t_k>`, and put `H_j = <H_{j-1}, t_j>`. If every
`H_{j-1} cap t_j^{-1} H_{j-1} t_j` is infinite, then `R_Gamma` is generated by `R_{H_0}` together with `t_j`-edges of
total measure `< eps`. So a generating graphing of `R_{H_0}` extends to one of `R_Gamma` at Cayley cost `eps`. The
condition is on the contact subgroup `I`, not on commensuration. `t` need not normalize or commensurate `H`.

**Relation to known tools.** Lemma 1 is the one-step contact move of wq-normal absorption
(`fpbs-wq-normal-subgroup-cost-transfer`; Popa, Peterson–Sinclair, Gaboriau). The only point here is that the
repair edges can be taken inside the single Cayley letter `Phi_t`, which is what the Cayley subgraphing cost needs.
It is not claimed as new beyond that restriction.

When `I` is infinite, adjacent `H`-classes are joined by
infinitely many `t`-edges (Section 6, Lemma 6.1). So the hypothesis of `fpbs-infinite-contact-zero-relative-cost` holds for the class graph of
`R_H cup Phi_t`, and it would give `relC = 0` with repairs taken from that graphing by a marked-sprinkling argument.
Lemma 1 is the direct form: one explicit repair set, inside the single letter `Phi_t`, that is `eps`-small.

## 3. Theorem A and the amalgams

**Theorem A.** Let `v in F(c,d)`, let `{s, t} = {c, d}` and `H_s = <s, v>`. If `H_s cap t^{-1} H_s t != 1`, then
`RC_a(v) = 1` for every free p.m.p. action `a` of `F(c,d)`.

*Proof.*
1. A nontrivial subgroup of a free group is infinite, so Lemma 1 applies with `H = H_s` and `<H_s, t> = F(c,d)`.
2. `R_{H_s}` is generated by `Phi_s cup S_v`, because the action is free.
3. So `F = Phi_s cup Phi_t|_M` has `<F cup S_v> = R` and `mu(F) = 1 + mu(M) < 1 + eps`. Hence `RC <= 1`.
4. `RC >= 1` is Section 1. ∎

Since `H cap t H t^{-1} = t (t^{-1} H t cap H) t^{-1}`, it does not matter whether one tests `t` or `t^{-1}`.

**Corollary A'.** Let `A = F(a_1, ..., a_n)` with `n >= 1`, let `u in A` be nontrivial, and let `v in F(c,d)` satisfy
Theorem A. Put

```text
G = A *_{u = v} F(c,d) = < a_1, ..., a_n, c, d | u v^{-1} >,     S = {a_1, ..., a_n, c, d}.
```

Then for every free p.m.p. action `a` of `G`,

```text
C_sub^a(S) = C(a) = n + 1 = 1 + beta_1^(2)(G).
```

*Proof.*
1. **Upper bound.** Take `M` from Lemma 1 for the free action `a|_{F(c,d)}`, and put
   `F = Phi_{a_1} cup ... cup Phi_{a_n} cup Phi_s cup Phi_t|_M`. Its cost is `n + 1 + mu(M)`.
   - `<F>` contains `R_A`, since the action of `A` is free and generated by the `a_i`.
   - `S_v = Phi_v = Phi_u`, because `u = v` in `G`, and `Phi_u` is contained in `R_A`.
   - So `<F>` contains `<Phi_s cup S_v cup Phi_t|_M>`, which is `R_{F(c,d)}` by Theorem A.
   - Hence `<F>` contains `<R_A cup R_{F(c,d)}> = R_G`.
2. **Lower bound.** `C_sub^a(S) >= C(a) >= 1 + beta_1^(2)(G)`, by `fpbs-amen2-betti-cost-input`.
3. **Betti number.** `G` is infinite. The relator `w = u v^{-1}` is not a proper power:
   - In `A * F(c,d)`, the element `w` has cyclically reduced syllable length 2.
   - If `w = z^m` with `m >= 2`, conjugate `z` to cyclically reduced form, with `l` syllables.
     - If `l >= 2`, then `z^m` is cyclically reduced of syllable length `m l >= 4`.
     - If `l <= 1`, then `w` is conjugate into a factor.
     - Both contradict syllable length 2.

   So `one-relator-group-cost-equals-first-l2-betti-plus-one` with `m = 1` and `|S| = n + 2` gives
   `beta_1^(2)(G) = n`.
4. **Conclusion.** Steps 1–3 give `n + 1 + eps >= C_sub^a(S) >= C(a) >= n + 1`. ∎

**What is new and what is not.**
- **Not new.** Fixed price `n + 1` for `F_n *_Z F_2` is classical, via Gaboriau's amalgam formula over an amenable
  subgroup.
- **New.** The value is attained **inside the Cayley graphing `Phi_S`**, for every free action. So for these `G`:
  - the hole at `S` holds: `C_sub^b(S) = 1 + beta_1` for Bernoulli;
  - both (P1) and (P2) of `fpbs-hole-at-s-is-s-price-plus-invariant-cayley-cost` hold;
  - by Theorem B of `fpbs-bernoulli-morse-collapse-criterion` (currently PROPOSED ESTABLISHED), the Morse deficit
    satisfies `lim_L m(P_L) = 0`. That is the target node for these groups.

**Examples in which every letter is used at least 3 times.**
- `u = abaBab`, `v = cccddd`. Here `H_c = <c, ddd>`, and `ddd = d^{-1} ddd d` lies in `H_c cap d^{-1} H_c d`.
- `v` in {`cdcdcD`, `ccdcdcd`, `cdcdcDD`, `cddcddcD`}, among the 55 classes of Section 7.

These relators are outside `fpbs-one-relator-twice-used-letter-fixed-price`. They are also outside every single-letter
Morse class killed by `fpbs-single-letter-morse-matchings-die-on-bernoulli` and
`fpbs-letter-saturated-morse-matchings-die-on-bernoulli`, since those kill only fixed-list Morse constructions, while
Corollary A' goes through `C_sub` directly. The sharp test `abaBab = cdcDcd` is **not** covered, because neither side
has a contact (Section 6).

## 4. Proposition B: the letter sandwich

**Proposition B.** For every free p.m.p. action of `F(c,d)` and every `v not in <c> cup <d>`,

```text
max( 1, rho_d(H_c) + rho_c(H_d) )  <=  RC(v)  <=  1 + min( rho_d(H_c), rho_c(H_d) ).
```

*Proof.*
- **Upper bound.** Take `F = Phi_c cup E` with `E subset Phi_d` and `<R_{H_c} cup E> = R`. Since
  `<Phi_c cup S_v> = R_{H_c}`, the family `F` is admissible and costs `1 + mu(E)`. Symmetrically with `c` and `d`
  exchanged.
- **Lower bound.** Let `F = F_c cup F_d` be admissible, with `F_c subset Phi_c` and `F_d subset Phi_d`.
  - `<R_{H_c} cup F_d>` contains `<F_c cup S_v cup F_d> = R`. So `mu(F_d) >= rho_d(H_c)`.
  - Symmetrically, `mu(F_c) >= rho_c(H_d)`.
  - Add the two. `RC >= 1` is Section 1. ∎

So the **`s`-saturated family** `{Phi_s cup E : E subset Phi_t}` has value exactly `1 + rho_t(H_s)`.

**Lemma 4.1 (sliding).** For `H <= F` and a letter `t`,

```text
rho_t(H) = inf { c(E) : E a graphing with labels in H t H cup H t^{-1} H, <R_H cup E> = R }  >=  relC(R ; R_H).
```

*Proof.*
- Split `E` into pieces `{(x, g x) : x in A}` with `g = h t^{e} h'` fixed, `h, h'` in `H` and `e = ±1`.
- Replace each piece by `{(h' x, t^{e} h' x) : x in A}`. This is a piece of `Phi_t`, read backwards if `e = -1`, of
  the same measure `mu(A)`.
- The endpoints move inside `R_H`-classes, because `x ~_H h' x` and `h t^{e} h' x ~_H t^{e} h' x`. So `<R_H cup E>`
  is unchanged.
- The replaced family is a subset of `Phi_t` of measure at most `c(E)`.
- The inequality `>= relC` holds because `relC` allows all labels. ∎

**Consequence.** By item 4 of `fpbs-malnormal-coset-cycles-linear-perimeter`, packing certificates for a
malnormal pair with generator in `H t^{±1} H` are floored. So **no packing certificate proves `rho_t(H) = 0`** when
`H` is malnormal and finitely generated with `t not in H`. The floor is `delta(F_2, H, t) = 1/(8 A^2)`, in the
notation of that claim. For `H = <c, dcDD>` and `t = d` this is the constant `delta_mal`.

## 5. Corollary C: the letter-saturated route is coupled to finite cost of an ascending union

Take `v in F(c,d) \ <c>`. Then `phi_v : c -> c, d -> v` is an injective endomorphism of `F_2`, because `<c, v>` is
free of rank 2 when `c` and `v` do not commute. Let `Gamma_v` be the direct limit of
`F_2 -> F_2 -> ...` along `phi_v`, and let `H_n` be the image of the `n`-th copy. So
`(H_{n+1}, H_n) = (F_2, <c, v>)`, with `c = a` and `d = b_{n+1}`.

**Corollary C.** Suppose `rho_d(<c, v>) = 0` for the Bernoulli shift of `F(c,d)`, which by Proposition B means the
`c`-saturated family attains `RC(v) = 1`. Then the Bernoulli shift `beta_v` of `Gamma_v` has `C(beta_v) = 2`.

In particular, take `v0 = ccdcDD`. Then `<c, v0> = <c, dcDD> = <a, b a b^{-2}>`, which is the stage pair
`(H_2, H_1)` of `Gamma_mal`. If the `c`-saturated family attains `RC(v0) = 1` on Bernoulli, then
`fpbs-mal-bernoulli-single-stage-floor` fails.

*Proof.*
1. **Identify the restricted action.** `beta_v|_{H_2}` is the Bernoulli shift of `H_2 = F_2` over the base
   `[0,1]^{H_2 \ Gamma_v}`, which is standard and nonatomic. So it is conjugate to the Bernoulli shift of `F(c,d)`,
   with `a -> c` and `b_2 -> d`, and `H_1` corresponds to `<c, v>`.
2. **One stage.** By Lemma 4.1, `relC(R_{H_2}; R_{H_1}) <= rho_{b_2}(H_1) = 0`.
3. **All stages.** Now run the proof of Proposition 3, (1) ⇒ (2), in
   `research/artifacts/fpbs-relative-fixed-price-decomposition-2026-09-17.md`, Section 3.2.
   - The shift induced by `phi_v` is an automorphism of `Gamma_v` mapping `H_n` onto `H_{n+1}`.
   - It conjugates `beta_v` to itself, so `relC(R_{H_{n+1}}; R_{H_n}) = 0` for all `n`.
   - `C(R_{H_n}) = 2` by Gaboriau Cor 2.25.
   - The integer case of `fpbs-relative-stage-costs-bound-cost-by-stage-liminf` gives `C(beta_v) <= 2`.
   - `fpbs-locally-free-free-action-costs-are-c-star-or-infinite` gives equality.

   That proof uses nothing about `Gamma_mal` beyond its being the limit of one injective endomorphism of `F_2`.
4. **`Gamma_mal`.** For `v0 = ccdcDD = c^2 (dcDD)`, the subgroup `<c, v0>` equals `<c, dcDD>`, and only the
   subgroup enters `rho`. Running step 3 in `Gamma_mal` itself, with its own chain, gives `C(beta) = 2`. By the
   equivalent forms recorded in `fpbs-mal-bernoulli-single-stage-floor`, via
   `fpbs-shifted-stage-relative-cost-zero-or-infinite`, the floor fails. ∎

**Reading.**
- *Contact `v`.* If `<c, v>` has a `d`-contact, Theorem A gives `rho_d = 0` on every free action. `Gamma_v` is then
  in case (W) of `fpbs-locally-free-wq-normal-fg-subgroup-fixed-price`, and Corollary C gives nothing new there.
- *Malnormal `<c, v>`.* If `<c, v>` is malnormal in `F_2`, malnormality is transitive along the chain. So each
  `H_n` is malnormal in `Gamma_v`, and `Gamma_v` is **malnormally exhausted**. Corollary C then says:
  - a `c`-saturated certificate for `RC(v) = 1` on Bernoulli proves the OPEN
    `fpbs-locally-free-malnormally-exhausted-finite-cost` for the Bernoulli shift of `Gamma_v`;
  - for `v0`, it refutes the `Gamma_mal` floor.
- *Transitivity.* For `H <= K <= L` with `H` malnormal in `K` and `K` malnormal in `L`:
  - if `g in L \ K`, then `H cap g H g^{-1} <= K cap g K g^{-1} = 1`;
  - if `g in K \ H`, malnormality of `H` in `K` applies.

  Both `H_c(cdcDcd)` and `H_d(cdcDcd)` are malnormal (Section 7). So **both** saturated routes to
  `RC(cdcDcd) = 1` are at least as hard as finite cost for the Bernoulli shift of a malnormally exhausted locally free
  group. The groups are `Gamma_{cdcDcd}`, and its analogue with the letters exchanged.

## 6. Class kill: contact repair dies exactly at trivial contact

**Lemma 6.1 (contact multiplicity).** Let the action be free, `H <= F`, and `I = H cap t^{-1} H t`.
- Two forward `t`-edges `(h_1 x, t h_1 x)` and `(h_2 x, t h_2 x)` out of one `H`-class land in the same `H`-class
  iff `h_1 h_2^{-1} in I`.
- So the forward `t`-edges between two adjacent `H`-classes form one `I`-coset: there are `|I|` of them.

*Proof.* `t h_1 x = h'' t h_2 x` holds iff `h'' = t h_1 h_2^{-1} t^{-1}`, by freeness. So it holds iff
`h_1 h_2^{-1}` lies in `t^{-1} H t cap H = I`. ∎

**Lemma 6.2 (the class graph has cycles).** Let `Q_t` be the graph whose vertices are the `H`-classes and whose
edges are the individual `t`-edges.
- Suppose `H` has rank at least 2, `H != F` and `<H, t> = F`. Then `Q_t` has a cycle through the class of almost
  every point, so it is not a forest.
- An edge `e` of a repair set `E` is redundant iff its two end classes are joined in `Q_t` by a path of edges of
  `E \ {e}`.
- So when `I = 1`, `rho_t(H)` is a relative cycle-deletion cost of `Q_t`, and there is no contact to thin.

*Proof.*
1. `H * <t> -> F` is a surjection from a free group of rank `rk H + 1 >= 3` onto `F_2`. So it is not injective.
   Take a nontrivial kernel element `w = h_0 t^{e_1} h_1 ... t^{e_k} h_k`, cyclically reduced in the free product.
   Then `k >= 2`, since `H` embeds and `t not in H`.
2. Read `w` from a point `x` as a closed walk in `Q_t`. It is closed because `w x = x`. Each `h_i` stays inside one
   class, and each `t^{e_i}` crosses one `t`-edge.
3. The walk never re-uses an edge immediately in reverse.
   - For `t h t^{-1}` with `h in H \ 1`: the edge `(w', t w')` is followed by `(t^{-1} h t w', h t w')`. These are
     equal iff `h t w' = t w'`, that is iff `h = 1`.
   - The case `t^{-1} h t` is the same.
   - At the junction, cyclic reduction plays the same role.
4. A closed non-backtracking walk of positive length does not exist in a forest, even allowing multi-edges. So `Q_t`
   contains a cycle.

The redundancy statement is immediate from generation. ∎

**The kill.**
- *Class.* Letter-saturated contact repair, that is `s`-saturated families
  `F = Phi_s cup Phi_t|_M`, where `M` is a small complete section of `E_{a|J}` for some `J <= I`. This includes
  Theorem A, and the `Phi_t`-restriction of the infinite-contact repair of `fpbs-infinite-contact-zero-relative-cost`.
- *Invariant.* `rank(H_s cap t^{-1} H_s t)`, for `s = c` and `s = d`.
- *Where it dies.* Step 1 of Lemma 1. If `I = 1`, then `E_{a|I}` is the identity relation, and its only complete
  section is `X`. So the family is all of `Phi_s cup Phi_t`, which costs `2`.
- *By Lemma 6.1.* Every pair of adjacent classes is joined by exactly one `t`-edge. So there is nothing to thin, and
  the hypothesis of `fpbs-infinite-contact-zero-relative-cost` fails at the letter `t`.
- *What is left.* By Lemma 6.2, a cycle-deletion problem on `Q_t`. When `H_s` is malnormal, this is a free-pairs
  relative cost by Lemma 4.1, and packing certificates for it are floored (Section 4).
- *Words killed.* `cdcDcd` (both letters), `ccdcDD` (both letters), and every no-contact class in Section 7. That is
  472 of the 658 classes of length at most 8, and 1484 of the 1539 classes of length at most 9 with every letter
  used at least 3 times.

  - `cccddd` has contacts of rank 1 for both letters.
  - `cdcDcd` and `ccdcDD` have rank 0 for both letters and both signs.
- **`witnesses.py`.** An independent check by Stallings membership. It searches products of at most 5 generators.
