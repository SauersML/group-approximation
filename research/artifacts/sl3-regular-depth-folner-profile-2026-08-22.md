# The actual SL3 conductor branching still admits Folner depth profiles

For `G_a=SL_3(Z/p^a Z)`, successive group orders differ by `p^8`.  Inside
the regular representation of `G_a`, the fraction of the representation
factoring through `G_b` is `|G_b|/|G_a|`.  Hence the exact minimal-conductor
bands form a geometric tail of ratio `p^-8` below the displayed top level.

Arbitrary rational dimension weights on finitely many regular blocks are
realized exactly by choosing suitable integer multiplicities.  Mixing top
levels with a wide triangular profile and then resolving into genuine
minimal-conductor bands therefore convolves that triangular profile with a
fixed geometric probability kernel.  Total variation contracts under this
convolution.  All fixed one- and two-level translations have vanishing
variation as the width grows, and the largest conductor atom vanishes.

Moving the entire window to depths tending to infinity also gives the
canonical character of `SL_3(Z)` on every fixed finite set, because deep
regular congruence characters vanish on every fixed nonidentity element.

Therefore neither canonical-character normalization nor the genuine `p^8`
branching multiplicity supplies a one-dimensional weighted-shift gap.  The
remaining arithmetic question is joint: can the denominator generator
coherently intertwine all parahoric branch labels and mixed-root packets on
such a spread profile?  A depth-marginal inequality cannot decide it.
