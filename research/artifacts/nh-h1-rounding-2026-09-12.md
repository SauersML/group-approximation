# (H1) for the Theorem E vertex: finite images and the unit-type excess bound (2026-09-12)

Lane `nh-h1-rounding`. Target: hypothesis (H1) of
`hs-normalization-needs-coarse-actor-scale-pinning`, stable vertex rounding.

## Summary

1. **Scope of (H1).** (H1) is flexible Hilbert–Schmidt stability of the vertex
   `Γ = EL_r(F_q[x_1..x_d])`, tested only on restrictions `σ|Γ` of trace-preserving
   `σ : G → U(M)`. It is not settled here.
   - Becker–Lubotzky rule out strict stability: infinite hyperlinear Kazhdan groups are not
     HS-stable (§8).
   - Flexible HS stability of higher-rank Kazhdan groups is open.
2. **Finite images.** Every finite-dimensional unitary representation of `Γ` factors through
   a finite Steinberg group `St_r(R_+/I)`. This is the vertex version of the root-torsion
   argument in `char-p-root-torsion-collapse`.
3. **Theorem (unit-type excess bound).** Under (H1), take the rounding representations to
   factor through `St_r(R_+/I_n)`. Then `eps_n <= 4 tau(1 - P_n)^(1/2)`, where `P_n` projects
   onto the constituents that are trivial on every local factor of `R_+/I_n` in which some
   variable `x_i` is not a unit.
   - So (H1) with unit-type rounding gives (CCR) for every nonnegative compressor.
   - It needs no coarse actor gap (H2') and no median step.
4. **Payoff.** If every trace-preserving `σ : G → U(M)` rounds on `Γ` to unit-type
   representations, the explicit Kun–Thom wreath is not hyperlinear (§5).
5. **Where excess can live.** Only on non-unit congruence factors, where some `x_i` is
   nilpotent.
   - Genuine `G`-models have none.
   - The one-compressor firewall must use them.
   - No trace-level test can exclude them.

## 1. Setup

- **Rings:** `R_+ = F_q[x_1..x_d]` and `L = F_q[x_1^(±1)..x_d^(±1)]`, with `r, d >= 3` and `p = char F_q`.
- **Groups:** the vertex is `Γ = EL_r(R_+)` and the actor is `G = EL_r(L) ⋊ SL_d(Z)`.
- **Compressor:** `A ∈ SL_d(Z)` has nonnegative entries and acts by monomial substitution
  `x^β -> x^(Aβ)`. So `Λ = AΓA^(-1) = EL_r(S)`, where `S = F_q[y_1..y_d] ⊆ R_+` and
  `y_j = x^(A e_j)`.
- **(H1):** after `o(n)` padding there are genuine `π_n : Γ -> U(n')` with `n'/n -> 1` and
  `max_(s ∈ F_Γ) ||σ_n(s) - π_n(s)||_2 -> 0` along `U`.
- **Excess:** `A_n = π_n(Γ)'`, `B_n = π_n(Λ)'`, and
  `eps_n = sup {||x - E_(A_n) x||_2 : x ∈ B_n, ||x|| <= 1}`.

## 2. Finite images of vertex representations

**Lemma 2.1.** Let `π : Γ -> U(m)` be a unitary representation, and `φ : St_r(R_+) -> Γ` the
natural surjection. Then there is an ideal `I ⊆ R_+` of finite index such that `π∘φ` kills
every `x_ij(a)` with `a ∈ I`. Hence `π` factors through the finite group `St_r(R_+/I)`.

*Proof.*
- **(a)** The root subgroup `U_ij = e_ij(R_+) ≅ (R_+, +)` has exponent `p`. So `π(U_ij)` is an
  abelian group of commuting unitaries whose eigenvalues are `p`-th roots of unity. It is
  simultaneously diagonalizable, `|π(U_ij)| <= p^m`, and `A_ij = {a : π(e_ij(a)) = 1}` has
  finite additive index.
- **(b)** `I = ∩_(i≠j) A_ij` has finite index, and it is an ideal. For `a ∈ I`, `f ∈ R_+` and
  `k ∉ {i, j}` (possible because `r >= 3`), `e_ij(fa) = [e_ik(f), e_kj(a)]`. Since
  `π(e_kj(a)) = 1`, we get `π(e_ij(fa)) = 1`.
- **(c)** `St_r(R_+/I)` is the quotient of `St_r(R_+)` by the normal closure of
  `{x_ij(a) : a ∈ I}`, because its presentation is the Steinberg presentation with entries read
  mod `I`. So `π∘φ` factors through `St_r(R_+/I)`. For `r >= 3` this is a perfect central
  extension of the finite group `E_r(R_+/I)`, so it is finite. ∎

## 3. Unit-type quotients collapse the compression

Write the finite ring `R_+/I = ∏_m A_m` as a product of finite local rings.
- **Unit-type factor:** every `x_i` is a unit in `A_m`.
- **Non-unit factor:** some `x_i` lies in the maximal ideal, so it is nilpotent there.
- Put `A_u = ∏_(unit-type) A_m` and `A_nu = ∏_(non-unit) A_m`. For `r >= 3`,
  `St_r(R_+/I) = St_r(A_u) × St_r(A_nu)`.
- Every irreducible representation is `ρ_u ⊗ ρ_nu`.

**Lemma 3.1.** In `A_u` the image of `S` is all of `A_u`.

*Proof.* Each `y_j` is a unit of finite multiplicative order in `A_u`, so `y_j^(-1)` is a positive
power of `y_j` and lies in the image of `S`. Since `A ∈ SL_d(Z)`, `x_i = ∏_j y_j^((A^(-1))_(ij))`
lies there too. The `x_i` and `F_q` generate `A_u`. ∎

**Definition.** For `π` factoring through `St_r(R_+/I)`, let `P` be the sum of the isotypic central
projections of `π` over the irreducibles with `ρ_nu` trivial. Then `P ∈ Z(π(Γ)'')`.

**Lemma 3.2.** For every `γ ∈ Γ` there is `λ ∈ Λ` with `π(γ)P = π(λ)P`.

*Proof.* On `range(P)`, `π` factors through `St_r(A_u)`. `Λ = EL_r(S)` is generated by the
`e_ij(s)` with `s ∈ S`, so its image on `range(P)` is the image of the subgroup generated by the
`x_ij(s̄)`, `s̄` in the image of `S` in `A_u`. By Lemma 3.1 these are all generators of
`St_r(A_u)`. ∎

## 4. The excess bound

**Theorem 4.1.** Assume (H1), and let `P_n` be as in §3 for a factorization of `π_n` through some
`St_r(R_+/I_n)` (Lemma 2.1). Then

```text
eps_n <= 4 tau(1 - P_n)^(1/2).
```

*Proof.* Let `x ∈ B_n` with `||x|| <= 1`, and write `P = P_n`, `π = π_n`.
- **`PxP ∈ A_n`.** For `γ ∈ Γ` pick `λ` with `π(γ)P = π(λ)P` (Lemma 3.2). `P` is central in
  `π(Γ)''` and `x` commutes with `π(λ)`, so
  `PxP π(γ) = Px π(λ)P = P π(λ) xP = π(λ)P xP = π(γ) PxP`.
- **Estimate.** Since `E_(A_n)(PxP) = PxP`,
  `||x - E_(A_n) x||_2 <= ||x - PxP||_2 + ||E_(A_n)(x - PxP)||_2 <= 2 ||x - PxP||_2`,
  and `||x - PxP||_2 <= ||(1-P)x||_2 + ||Px(1-P)||_2 <= 2 ||1 - P||_2`. ∎

**Corollary 4.2.** Suppose that along `U` the rounding representations can be chosen with
`tau(1 - P_n) -> 0`. Then:
- (CCR) `σ(Λ)' ∩ M = σ(Γ)' ∩ M` holds for every nonnegative strict compressor `A`;
- `σ(G)` normalizes `σ(Γ)' ∩ M`.

*Proof.*
- **(CCR):** item 2 of `vertex-rounding-reduces-ccr-to-commutant-excess`.
- **Generation.** `SL_d(Z)` is generated by the elementary matrices `e_ij(1)` and their
  inverses, and each `e_ij(1)` is a nonnegative strict compressor. Every exponent vector in
  `Z^d` is an `SL_d(Z)`-translate of a nonnegative one, so `EL_r(L)` is generated by the
  `SL_d(Z)`-conjugates of `Γ`. Hence `G = <Γ, e_ij(1)>`.
- **Normalization.** Each `Ad σ(e_ij(1))` maps `σ(Γ)' ∩ M` onto `σ(Λ)' ∩ M = σ(Γ)' ∩ M`. ∎

**Remark.** On `range(1 - P_n)` one may replace `π_n` by the trivial representation. This keeps
(H1) exactly when that range has trace `o(1)`. So the hypothesis of the corollary is precisely
(H1) with unit-type rounding.

## 5. Endgame

Suppose `σ` extends to a trace-preserving representation of the wreath
`W = C_2 wr_(G/Γ) G`.
- The lamp `a_Γ` commutes with `Γ`, so it lies in `N = σ(Γ)' ∩ M`.
- Normalization puts `a_(tΓ) = σ(t) a_Γ σ(t)^*` in `N`.
- A `γ ∈ Γ \ tΓt^(-1)` therefore fixes `a_(tΓ)`. But it moves it to the lamp at
  `γtΓ ≠ tΓ`, which is at normalized 2-distance `√2` in the canonical trace.

So unit-type vertex rounding for all trace-preserving `σ : G -> U(M)` makes `W` non-hyperlinear.
The defect is `kun-thom-wreath-carries-rigid-defect`.

## 6. Consistency checks

- **Genuine `G`-models.**
  - A genuine finite-dimensional `ρ` of `G` kills `e_ij(J)` for a finite-index ideal
    `J ⊆ L` (`char-p-root-torsion-collapse`).
  - The restricted ideal `I = J ∩ R_+` has `R_+/I ⊆ L/J`. Each `x_i` is a unit of finite order
    in `L/J`, so its inverse is a positive power and it is a unit in `R_+/I`.
  - Hence `P_n = 1` and `eps_n = 0`. This recovers `ccr-holds-for-all-genuine-fd-coordinate-models`
    in this regime.
- **One-compressor firewall.** `kt-single-compressor-canonical-lamps-do-not-exactify-actor`
  shows the actor claim fails for `<Γ, t>`, a group with no Laurent torus. By Theorem 4.1 its
  models must break (H1) or carry order-one non-unit mass. Nothing in `<Γ, t>` inverts the
  `x_i`. This fits the torus reduction in `approximate-collapse-for-kt-compressor`: the torus
  carries the whole wall.
- **Trace-level tests are empty.** Characters of `(R_+, +)` that factor through unit-type finite
  quotients are weak*-dense in the dual. The ring `L` is residually finite through quotients
  `L/J` in which every `x_i` is a unit, and `R_+ ⊆ L`. So no computation with root-subgroup
  characters can exclude non-unit mass.

## 7. Where non-unit mass can live

- **Source of excess.** A non-unit factor has some nilpotent `x_i`. The compressor substitutes
  positive monomials, which can raise the `x_i`-adic valuation. So the image of `S` in
  `A_nu` can be a proper subring, irreducibles of `St_r(A_nu)` can split on `Λ`, and this is the
  only source of excess.
- **Weak* invariance does not exclude it.** Conjugation by the torus `diag(x_i^(±1))`
  rescales root coordinates, and the ultralimit trace is exactly invariant under it. That
  invariance kills only non-unit factors of bounded nilpotency order. Factors with nilpotency
  order growing along `U` pass every fixed test word.
- **Relation to cascades.** A slowly varying valuation-level observable shifted by the torus is
  the ungapped regime of the cascades in `hs-normalization-needs-coarse-actor-scale-pinning`
  §5. No model is known.
