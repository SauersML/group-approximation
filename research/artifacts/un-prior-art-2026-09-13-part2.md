## 3. (c) Matricial and ultraproduct embeddability; LEF and sofic full groups

**3.1 Linear sofic algebras (Arzhantseva–Păunescu arXiv:1212.6780).**
- Def `deflinearsofic` (l.411): "A countably generated algebra $A$ over a field $F$ is linear sofic if there exist an
  injective morphism $\Theta:A\to\Pi_{k\to\omega} M_{n_k}(F)/Ker\rho_\omega$. Moreover, if $A$ is a unital algebra we
  require that this morphism is unital."
- Thm `mainresult` (l.159): "A group $G$ is linear sofic if and only if its group algebra $\mathbb{C}G$ is linear sofic."
- Cor `sofkap` (l.1110): "Sofic groups satisfy Kaplansky's direct finiteness conjecture."
- On main: `linear-sofic-group-algebra-is-stably-finite` (the group-to-algebra passage over any field).

**3.2 The Pestov ring embeds in the continuous factor (Ara–Claramunt arXiv:1902.06476).** Setting: A = C_K(X)⋊_T Z,
K any field, T a homeomorphism of the Cantor set, and (abstract) "full ergodic $T$-invariant probability measures $\mu$".
- Def `definition-approx.alg` (l.614): "Let $E$ be a nonempty clopen subset of $X$, and let $\calP$ be a partition of
  $X \backslash E$. Define $\calB := \calA (E, \calP)$ as the unital $*$-subalgebra of $\calA$ generated by the elements
  $\chi_Z \cdot t$ for $Z \in \calP$."
- Prop `proposition-embed.Rn` (l.1201): the approximating rings are $\gotR_n = \prod_{W \in \V_n} M_{|W|}(K)$ with
  connecting maps from a generalized Bratteli diagram.
- Thm `theorem-completion.A.Ay` (l.1521): "Let $\gotR_{\rk}$ be the rank completion of the regular rank ring
  $\gotR_{\infty}$ with respect to the rank function $\rk_{\gotR_{\infty}}$. [...] We then have an embedding
  $\calA \hookrightarrow \gotR_{\rk}$ that induces a faithful Sylvester matrix rank function, denoted by $\rk_{\calA}$, on
  $\calA$."
- Prop `proposition-unique.rank` (l.1570)(ii): "$\rk_{\calA}$ [...] is a faithful Sylvester matrix rank function on $\calA$,
  and it is uniquely determined by the same property as in $(i)$, that is, for every clopen subset $U$ of $X$,
  $\rk_{\calA}(\chi_U) = \mu(U)$."
- Thm `theorem-vN.cont.factor` (l.1637): "There is an isomorphism of algebras $\gotR_{\rk} \cong \calM_K$, the von Neumann
  continuous factor over $K$."
- Thm `theorem-regular.Sylvester.space` (l.1783): for any homeomorphism T of a totally disconnected compact metric space,
  every Sylvester matrix rank function on C_K(X)⋊_T Z is regular.
- **Scope.** This is a RANK-METRIC matricial approximation (linear-sofic type). The Pestov answer uses the stronger EXACT
  local embedding into ∏_ω M_N(F_q) (LEF type, via return-word towers). Ara–Claramunt do not state the exact version, and
  the Pestov artifact credits Grigorchuk–Medynets for the mechanism.

**3.3 Stable finiteness does not imply linear soficity (Greenfeld arXiv:2210.11650).** Imported as
`stably-finite-algebras-need-not-be-linear-sofic`.
- Thm `main` (l.97): "Over an arbitrary field, there exists a finitely generated non linear sofic algebra which satisfies a
  polynomial identity and is thus stably finite."
- Thm `main_jac` (l.103) gives a finitely generated non linear sofic algebra that is Jacobson radical (from the
  Cohn–Sasiada simple radical ring); its full statement about the unital hull was not re-read.
- Neither example is simple (a finitely generated PI algebra that is simple is finite-dimensional over its centre).

**3.4 LEF full groups.**
- Grigorchuk–Medynets arXiv:1105.0719, Thm `TheoremLEF` (l.524): "Let $(X,T)$ be an arbitrary Cantor minimal system. Then the
  topological full group $[[T]]$ is an LEF group."
- Elek arXiv:1211.0621, Thm 1: "The measurable full group of a sofic equivalence relation is sofic." Thm 2: "The topological
  full group of a minimal Cantor homeomorphism is LEF." (a short proof of GM)
- Xin Ma arXiv:2209.00580, Thm C = Thm `thm: topo group of residually finite action`: "Let $\alpha: G\curvearrowright X$ be a
  minimal topologically free residually finite action of a countable discrete group on the Cantor set. Then $[[\alpha]]$ is
  LEF and thus sofic." Imported as `residually-finite-minimal-actions-have-lef-full-groups`. Ma notes (l.1287) that every
  minimal Z-action is residually finite (Pimsner Lemma 2, Kerr–Nowak Prop 7.1), so this generalizes GM. He also gets
  finitely generated simple sofic full groups from Toeplitz subshifts of free groups.
- LEF growth for minimal subshift full groups: arXiv:2106.09145 (upper and lower bounds via recurrence and complexity).
- Elek–Monod arXiv:1201.0257 (abstract): a minimal Cantor Z²-action whose topological full group contains a non-abelian free
  group. On main: `elek-monod-near-hit-has-torsion-no-kazhdan-subgroup`.
- Xin Ma arXiv:2110.11548 (on main): Følner groupoids have sofic full groups, and the Cuntz groupoid admits no Følner
  sequence (`cuntz-groupoid-admits-no-folner-sequence`).
- On main: `minimal-subshift-algebra-is-simple-lef-ring` (Z) and `free-minimal-crossed-products-are-simple-with-rank-models`
  (Z^d, rank models, unreviewed).
- **Gap (bounded).** No source states a ring-level exact local embedding LC(X,k)⋊Γ → ∏_ω M_N(k) for residually finite
  minimal free Γ-actions, the ring analogue of Ma's Thm C. Nor does any source give a matricial embedding of A_k(𝒢) for
  almost finite 𝒢 (Kerr, Matui). Both are UN targets.

## 4. (d) Kazhdan simple groups from Steinberg-algebra rings; direct finiteness and approximation

- **Property (T).** Ershov–Jaikin-Zapirain arXiv:0809.4095 and EJZK arXiv:1102.0031 give (T) for EL_n(R), n ≥ 3, R finitely
  generated (on main). A_k(𝒢) is finitely generated when 𝒢 is compactly generated and k is finite, so EL_n(A_k(𝒢)) is
  Kazhdan in the cases UN needs. This last sentence is my remark, unchecked against a source.
- **No published Kazhdan simple group from a Steinberg ring found.** Within this bounded search (15 sources plus web search),
  the only Kazhdan simple groups built from Leavitt or Steinberg rings are the repo's own: the non-MF paper's L_{F_2}(1,2)^× and
  the Pestov 9.1 group EL_3(LC(X,F_q)⋊Z)/Z.
- **Kazhdan subgroups of full groups (on main):** `kazhdan-subgroups-of-rover-nekrashevych-groups-are-rf` and
  `kazhdan-subgroups-of-amenable-orbit-full-groups-are-rf`. Full groups carry no infinite (T) structure, so elementary groups
  over the Steinberg ring, not full groups, are the right Kazhdan carriers.
- **Direct finiteness versus approximation.**
  - Group-ring side: Elek–Szabó math/0305440 Thm 1 (D(G) embeds in a continuous regular ring for sofic G) and AP Cor `sofkap`.
  - Ring side: every rank ultraproduct is stably finite (Greenfeld l.91–92), so a linear sofic algebra is stably finite. The
    converse fails (§3.3).
  - Group-over-ring side: I found nothing beyond the non-MF paper (Thm l.918, Cor l.1016, Cor l.1038).
- **The C*-shadow of the UN dichotomy is Rainone–Sims** (part 1 §2.2): for minimal 𝒢, completely non-paradoxical ⟺ faithful
  trace ⟺ stably finite (⟺ quasidiagonal if amenable). With almost unperforated S(𝒢), purely infinite otherwise.

## 5. Consequences for the UN hypotheses (unreviewed remarks)

- **(U1), paradox side.** Cor l.1016 already covers every countable simple ring that is NOT directly finite. Remark: M_m(R)
  is simple when R is, and E_k(M_m(R)) = E_{km}(R) for k ≥ 2 (intra-block transvections are commutators of off-block
  ones). So the same conclusion holds for every simple ring that is not STABLY finite. For minimal effective ample 𝒢,
  paradoxicality of [1_{G^(0)}] at any matrix level gives no MF quotient. The frontier of (U1) is the stably finite side
  and sharper conclusions (Rad_MF, non-simple rings). un-paradox is landing this remark, with proof, as
  `stably-infinite-ring-elementary-groups-have-no-mf-quotient` (artifact `un-paradox-stable-infiniteness-2026-09-13.md`):
  2n[1] ≤ n[1] in V(R) gives no MF quotient for EL_N(R), N ≥ 2n. The present artifact makes no separate claim.
- **(U2), measure side.** Known at group level for full groups (GM, Elek, Ma Thm C) and in rank-metric form for LC(X,K)⋊Z
  (Ara–Claramunt). Exact ring-level embeddings for residually finite or almost finite 𝒢 are not in the sources read.
- **(U3), converse.** The ring condition must be at least stable finiteness. Greenfeld shows stable finiteness alone is not
  linear soficity for finitely generated algebras, but not for simple rings. Rank models of EL_n over rings without rank
  functions must be non-multiplicative on R (`leavitt-algebra-has-no-unital-rank-model`,
  `block-unipotent-rank-models-of-leavitt-el3-are-trivial`).
- **(U4), middle.** Rainone–Sims needs almost unperforated S(𝒢). Boldrini–Prasad (on main) give minimal Cantor actions
  whose type semigroup is not almost unperforated, and their Steinberg rings are the natural test objects.
- **Algebraic Tarski for Steinberg algebras** (part 1 §2.7) is unstated in the sources read. It is the missing lemma that
  would make (U1)/(U2) a dichotomy at ring level.
