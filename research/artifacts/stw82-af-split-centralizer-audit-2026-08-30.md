title: STW LXXXII AF split-centralizer checkpoint (2026-08-30)
---

For a unital split extension `0 -> I -> E -> D -> 0`, suppose the quotient
is AF and the range of the unital *-homomorphic section commutes with `I`.
Then `stw82-af-split-centralizer-extensions-obey-maximum` proves

`dim_nuc(E)=dim_nuc(I)`.

At a finite-dimensional quotient stage `D_lambda=direct_sum M_{r_j}`, the
central block units split the middle algebra.  A block with `r_j>=2` has
zero ideal corner: the commuting hypothesis forces that corner to
annihilate every matrix commutator, hence the ideal generated by those
commutators, which is the whole matrix block.  A scalar block contributes
the minimal unitization of its ideal corner.  Therefore every stage has
exactly the nuclear dimension of `I`, and the dense AF-stage union proves
the result.

This strictly extends the commutative central-section theorem.  It does not
cover arbitrary split extensions: if the section acts nontrivially by
left/right multipliers on the ideal, the commutator-annihilation calculation
fails and the general colour-gluing problem returns.
