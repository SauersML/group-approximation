## (e) Verification of the changed steps
- **S1, seam construction.**
  - Existence of p. Minimality makes the forward orbit dense, so every word of X occurs in some prefix $x_{[0,M)}$. Uniform recurrence makes $x_{[-\ell,\ell]}$ recur at arbitrarily large positions. Take $p>\max(M,2\ell)$ at such a recurrence.
  - $y_\ell=x$ on $[-\ell,p+\ell]$. For $t\in[p,p+\ell]$, $y_t=x_{t-p}=x_t$ by $x_{[p,p+\ell]}=x_{[0,\ell]}$. For $t\in[-\ell,0)$, $y_t=x_{t+p}=x_t$ by $x_{[p-\ell,p)}=x_{[-\ell,0)}$.
  - Same words. A $(2\ell+1)$-window of the $p$-periodic $y_\ell$ is a translate of one centered in $[0,p)$. That window lies in $[-\ell,p+\ell)$, so it is a word of X. Conversely, every word of X of that length occurs in $x_{[0,p)}$ with its window inside $[0,p)$, so it occurs in $y_\ell$.
  - Growth of N_ℓ. $N_\ell$ positions carry all $p_X(2\ell+1)$ words, so $N_\ell\ge p_X(2\ell+1)\to\infty$ [Morse–Hedlund 1938].
- **S2, A and H.**
  - $\epsilon_{ab}\epsilon_{a'b'}=\delta_{ba'}\epsilon_{ab'}$, since $V\cap T^tV=\varnothing$ for $0<|t|\le2w$.
  - The $\epsilon_{ab}$ are nonzero ($V\ne\varnothing$) and linearly independent (distinct level or exponent), so $A\cong M_{2w+1}(\F_2)$ with unit $e=\sum_a\epsilon_{aa}$.
  - For $y,z\in M_3(A)$, $((1-e)I_3+y)((1-e)I_3+z)=(1-e)I_3+yz$, because $(1-e)y=y(1-e)=0$. So $y\mapsto(1-e)I_3+y$ is an injective homomorphism on $\GL_3(A)\cong\GL_d(\F_2)$.
  - The transvection between $(p,a)$ and $(q,b)$ with $p\ne q$ maps to $I_3+\epsilon_{ab}E_{pq}=e_{pq}(\epsilon_{ab})\in G$. Transvections within a row are commutators $[t_{xy},t_{yz}]=t_{xz}$ through a coordinate of another row. So $H\subseteq G$.
- **S3, k ∈ H.** Put $a=k-I_3$ and $b=k^{-1}-I_3$, both in $M_3(A)$ as in rev3.
  - $kk^{-1}=k^{-1}k=I_3$ gives $a+b+ab=0=a+b+ba$.
  - So $(eI_3+a)(eI_3+b)=(eI_3+b)(eI_3+a)=eI_3$, the unit of $M_3(A)$.
  - So $y=eI_3+a\in\GL_3(A)$, and $k=I_3+a=(1-e)I_3+y\in H$.
- **S4, the level ideal.** Content unchanged from rev3.
- **S5, the moved operator-algebra paragraph.** Verbatim rev3 (PASS in sk-review-2; notes N1–N3 of sk-review-5 are reflected), except "As $G$ is hyperlinear," in place of "So".
- **S6, compatibility with rev3 §2.**
  - §2 says "small, in the sense of Section 1 with $u_\ell$ for $u^t$, $\ell V$ for $T^tV$, products in $L$ for sums of exponents, and word length for $|t|$ … the proof of simplicity in Section 1 applies word for word".
