# The CK corner obstruction survives all nontrivial quotients and marked covers

The canonical simple-core coset action cannot be repaired by taking a
nontrivial quotient of the direct CK elementary envelope. Its double-coset
space contains an explicit copy of the quotient ring at the kernel's
elementary level. That ring remains infinite. The obstruction also passes
to covers preserving the marked core, even if no commuting root subgroup
lifts to the cover.

This strengthens the
[August 31 result](boone-higman-direct-ck-double-coset-no-go-2026-08-31.md),
which excluded the direct envelope but left quotient and cover repairs as
possibilities. It does not exclude a finitely presented simple quotient:
an infinite simple subgroup of a finitely presented simple group can have
infinite bi-index. The maximal-kernel route is therefore still open.

## 1. Every root kernel is the entire elementary level

Let `U` be a unital associative ring, `n>=3`, `Gamma=EL_n(U)`, and
`N` a normal subgroup. Write

```text
I=lev(N)={a in U : e_ij(a) in N for all i!=j}.
```

For each fixed ordered pair `i!=j`, the kernel of

```text
(U,+) -> Gamma/N,   a |-> e_ij(a)N
```

is exactly `I`. Indeed, elementary signed permutation matrices conjugate
`e_ij(a)` to `e_kl(a)` or `e_kl(-a)` for any other root. Normality and
inversion remove the choice of root and the possible sign. Thus testing
one root is equivalent to testing all roots. Steinberg commutators prove
that this common kernel is a two-sided ideal.

Consequently every root image is the additive group of `U/I`. This
argument does **not** identify the full subgroup `N` with `E_n(U,I)` or
with a congruence subgroup. No normal-subgroup classification is used.

## 2. The coefficient simple core and the matrix corner both survive

Suppose now that `Z[S]<=U` for an infinite simple group `S`, that
`1-s_0` is full in `U` for some `s_0!=1`, and that

```text
D={diag(s,1,...,1):s in S} <= Gamma.
```

These are the hypotheses satisfied by the direct universal-sandwich CK
construction. Let `N` be proper, `q:Gamma->Q=Gamma/N`, and `D_N=q(D)`.
The established
[level-ideal screen](../ck-envelope-level-ideal-screen.md) gives

```text
S -> (U/I)^x injective;       1-s notin I for every 1!=s in S.    (1)
```

In particular `U/I` is infinite. It is important to distinguish this
coefficient-ring injection from injectivity of the matrix-corner map.
The latter follows from the explicit identity

```text
[e_12(1),diag(s,1,...,1)] = e_12(1-s).                        (2)
```

Here `[a,b]=aba^-1b^-1`. If `q(diag(s,1,...,1))=1`, (2) puts
`1-s` in the root kernel, which equals `I` by section 1. Equation (1)
then gives `s=1`. Thus `D_N` is isomorphic to `S` and is centerless.

## 3. An infinite family of distinct double cosets in every quotient

Put `A=q({e_23(a):a in U})`. By section 1,

```text
A ~= (U/I,+).                                               (3)
```

The original corner commutes with this spare root, so `A` centralizes
`D_N`. Any element of `A intersect D_N` is therefore central in `D_N`.
As `D_N` is infinite simple, its center is trivial, and

```text
A intersect D_N = 1.                                       (4)
```

If `x,y in A` lie in the same `D_N` double coset, then
`y=d_1 x d_2` with `d_1,d_2 in D_N`. Commutation yields
`x^-1y=d_1d_2`, and (4) implies `x=y`. Combining this with (3) gives

```text
U/I -> D_N \ Q / D_N,
a+I |-> D_N q(e_23(a)) D_N
```

injective. Hence `|D_N\Q/D_N| >= |U/I| >= aleph_0`.

A quotient can kill individual spare-root elements. It cannot kill enough
of that root to make its image finite while retaining a nontrivial
quotient of this envelope. Allowing that possibility was the gap in the
earlier audit's proposed quotient repair.

## 4. Covers do not need commuting lifts

If `pi:P->Q` is surjective and `T<=P` satisfies `pi(T)=D_N`, there is a
well-defined surjection

```text
T\P/T -> D_N\Q/D_N,     T p T |-> D_N pi(p) D_N.             (5)
```

Thus `T` has infinite bi-index in `P`. Neither injectivity of `pi|T`
nor any structure of `ker(pi)` is needed. In particular the lack of a
commuting lift of `A` in the cover is irrelevant to the obstruction.

The original [Chatterji–Kassabov proof](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22907v1)
uses a finitely presented cover mapping the marked subgroup to an
elementary matrix corner. Its coefficient ring is generated by the old
ring and the two sandwich elements, so it is a quotient of the universal
sandwich ring. Its elementary group is consequently a quotient of the
corresponding universal elementary group. Formula (5) applies to that
marked cover too, using rank three or four as appropriate.

Similarly, including `Q` in a larger ambient group cannot identify double
cosets of the same `D_N`: every multiplier witnessing an identification
still belongs to `D_N`, and so the identification already holds in `Q`.

Finally, a finite enlargement of the stabilizer cannot help. If
`T<=H<=P` and `[H:T]=m<infinity`, write `H` as the union of `m` left
cosets of `T` on one side and `m` right cosets on the other. Every `H p H`
is a union of at most `m^2` double cosets of `T`. Finite `H` bi-index
would force finite `T` bi-index, contradicting the preceding result.

## 5. Exact scope and remaining work

The excluded family includes every proper normal quotient of the direct
envelope, each marked cover mapping onto such a quotient, overgroups with
the same stabilizer, and finite-index enlargements of these stabilizers.

The statement does not cover an arbitrary subsequent quotient of a cover
whose map no longer factors through the elementary quotient. It also does
not cover genuinely different embeddings, or stabilizers enlarged by
infinite index. Those modifications would need their own proof of finite
presentation, the required orbit finiteness, and core survival.

The finite-bi-index existence question over other envelopes stays open.
The maximal-kernel and relative-automorphism-image routes stay open.
Boone–Higman stays open. This proof changes which construction families
remain available; it supplies no finite normal generating set for a
maximal kernel.

## Verification

This is an algebraic proof, not evidence from finite matrix sampling.
The dependency is the already established level-ideal screen. The new
root-kernel lemma is proved using explicit elementary signed permutation
matrices. Characteristic two causes no issue because the sign disappears;
noncommutativity causes no issue because the signed-permutation entries
are central. The infinity assertion uses the infinite simple core and
would not follow for a finite core. No congruence-subgroup identification
or unproved finite-generation premise occurs.

Cairn claim and route sources carry both results and the excluded repair.
Graph compilation is structural validation, not independent verification
of the mathematical proof. No Lean kernel check is claimed for this result.
