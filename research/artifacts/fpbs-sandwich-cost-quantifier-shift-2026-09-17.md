# Shape (A) is a statement about finitely generated groups: the relative sandwich Bernoulli bound

September 17, 2026. Agent `b-t-nonamgen`, swarm-0917, role inverter, family
quantifier-shift. Target hole: `fpbs-fixed-price-non-amenably-generated-from-fg`,

## 0. Summary

`research/artifacts/fpbs/docs/fixed-price-countable-passage.md` (§6, Remarks)
says shape (A) of its Theorem G, "no fixed pseudocost", is invisible to
arguments that pass only through subgroups. It also says no source read there
transfers intermediate-relation cost bounds from a free action `a_0` down to
`s_Γ`. This artifact shows the following.

1. **Localization (unconditional, §2).** For every countable `Γ`, every p.m.p.
   action `a` and every finite `F ⊆ Γ`, the sandwich cost `p_F(a)` is the
   infimum of the sandwich costs `p^Δ_F(a|Δ)` over finitely generated
   `Δ ⊇ F`. The restriction of the Bernoulli shift `s_Γ` to `Δ` is `s_Δ`. So
   `PC(a) = sup_F inf_{Δ ⊇ F} p^Δ_F(a|Δ)`, and in particular
   `PC(s_Γ) = sup_F inf_{Δ ⊇ F} p^Δ_F(s_Δ)`.
2. **Quantifier shift (§3).** Consider the statement RBL: for every finitely
   generated `Δ`, every finite `F ⊆ Δ` and every free action `a` of `Δ`,
   `p^Δ_F(s_Δ) ≤ p^Δ_F(a)`. RBL implies fixed pseudocost for every countably
   infinite group, with no hypothesis H. So shape (A) is excluded everywhere
   by a statement about finitely generated groups only.
   - H is the case where `F` generates `Δ`, since then `p^Δ_F = C`.
   - RBL is the relative version for proper `F`. This is precisely the
     quantifier H leaves out.
3. **Decomposition of the flagship (§4).** `fpbs-fixed-price-universal`
   follows from:
   - FPC, fixed pseudocost for all countably infinite groups;
   - U_fin, which says every free action of a group with `C(Γ) < ∞` has finite
     cost.

   The flagship implies U_fin, and it implies FPC for groups of finite cost.
   The two premises fail independently: FPC gives no finiteness, and U_fin
   gives no comparison.
4. **Consequence for the target (§5).** Under RBL, the target hole is
   equivalent to U_fin for groups not generated by finitely many amenable
   subgroups. That is shape (B), a free-action instance of Tucker-Drob Q 7.6.

Nothing here proves RBL, FPC or U_fin. The hole stays OPEN.

## 1. Notation and imported tools

Let `Δ` be a countable group, `a = Δ ↷ (X, μ)` a p.m.p. action on a standard
probability space, and `F ⊆ Δ` finite. Define the **sandwich cost**

    p^Δ_F(a) = inf { C_μ(E) : E Borel, E_{a|⟨F⟩} ⊆ E ⊆ E_a } ∈ [0, ∞].

When `Δ = Γ` is the ambient group we write `p_F(a)`. This is the `p_F` of the
passage artifact §2.

`s_Δ` denotes the Bernoulli shift `[0,1]^Δ` with Lebesgue product measure and
`(δ·x)(η) = x(δ^{-1} η)`.

Imported tools:
- **[TD]** R. Tucker-Drob, arXiv:1211.6395, TeX `4_15_2012_Shift-minimal_groups.tex`.
  Verbatim statements are in route
  `tucker-drob-sandwich-sets-open-at-free-actions-citation` and route
  `tucker-drob-pseudocost-exhaustion-bounds-citation`. We use:
  - Lemma `lem:open` (6.2);
  - the definition of `A_{F,r}` and its isomorphism invariance;
  - Theorem `thm:KecOpen` (6.4);
  - Corollaries `cor:PCdef` (6.19), `cor:AFrPC` (6.20) and `cor:PC=C` (6.8);
  - Proposition `prop:analogue`(1) (6.26(1)).
- **[AW]** Abért–Weiss, arXiv:1103.1063v2, Theorem 1, node
  `abert-weiss-free-actions-weakly-contain-bernoulli`.

By the equivalence (1)⇔(2) of `lem:open`, `a ∈ A_{F,r}` iff `p_F(a) < r`.
Here `r ∈ ℝ ∪ {∞}`.

## 2. Localization (ESTABLISHED)

**Lemma 2.1 (localization of the sandwich cost).** Let `Γ` be countable, `a`
a p.m.p. action of `Γ`, and `F ⊆ Γ` finite. Then

    p_F(a) = inf { p^Δ_F(a|Δ) : Δ ≤ Γ finitely generated, F ⊆ Δ }.

If `F ⊆ Δ ≤ Δ'`, then `p^{Δ'}_F(a|Δ') ≤ p^Δ_F(a|Δ)`. So the infimum is also a
limit along any increasing exhaustion of `Γ` by finitely generated subgroups
containing `F`, and it can be restricted to `Δ` containing any fixed finite
set.

*Proof.* First note that `E_{(a|Δ)|⟨F⟩} = E_{a|⟨F⟩}` and
`E_{a|Δ} ⊆ E_{a|Δ'} ⊆ E_a`.

- (≤) and monotonicity: a relation `E` with
  `E_{a|⟨F⟩} ⊆ E ⊆ E_{a|Δ}` also satisfies `E_{a|⟨F⟩} ⊆ E ⊆ E_{a|Δ'}` and
  `E ⊆ E_a`.
- (≥): let `r` satisfy `p_F(a) < r`, so there is `E` with
  `E_{a|⟨F⟩} ⊆ E ⊆ E_a` and `C_μ(E) < r`. This is (1) of `lem:open`. By
  (1)⇒(2) there are a finite `Q ⊇ F` and `E'` with
  `E_{a|⟨F⟩} ⊆ E' ⊆ E_{a|⟨Q⟩}` and `C_μ(E') < r`. So, with `Δ = ⟨Q⟩`,
  `p^Δ_F(a|Δ) < r`.
- If `p_F(a) = ∞`, then (≤) gives equality. ∎

**Lemma 2.2 (pseudocost as sup-inf).** `PC(a) = sup_F p_F(a)`. Hence

    PC(a) = sup_{F finite} inf_{Δ f.g., F ⊆ Δ} p^Δ_F(a|Δ).

*Proof.* This is Lemma 2.1 of the passage artifact. `cor:PCdef` says
`PC(a) ≤ r` iff `a ∈ A_{F,r+ε}` for all finite `F` and all `ε > 0`, that is,
iff `p_F(a) < r + ε` for all `F` and `ε`, iff `sup_F p_F(a) ≤ r`. This holds
for all real `r`, so both sides agree in `[0, ∞]`. The second formula is
Lemma 2.1. ∎

**Lemma 2.3 (restricted Bernoulli shift).** For every subgroup `Δ ≤ Γ`, the
restriction `s_Γ|Δ` is isomorphic to `s_Δ`. Hence
`p^Δ_F(s_Γ|Δ) = p^Δ_F(s_Δ)` for every finite `F ⊆ Δ`.

*Proof.* Choose a transversal `T` of the right cosets, so `Γ = ⊔_{t ∈ T} Δt`.
Define `Φ : [0,1]^Γ → ([0,1]^T)^Δ` by `Φ(x)(δ) = (x(δt))_{t ∈ T}`.
- `Φ` is a bijection that carries the product measure `λ^{⊗Γ}` to
  `(λ^{⊗T})^{⊗Δ}`.
- It is `Δ`-equivariant:

      Φ(δ_0·x)(δ) = (x(δ_0^{-1} δ t))_t = Φ(x)(δ_0^{-1} δ) = (δ_0·Φ(x))(δ).

- The base `([0,1]^T, λ^{⊗T})` is a standard nonatomic probability space, so
  some measure isomorphism `θ` maps it onto `([0,1], λ)`. Applying `θ`
  coordinatewise is a `Δ`-equivariant isomorphism `([0,1]^T)^Δ → [0,1]^Δ`.

An isomorphism of actions carries `E_{a|⟨F⟩}`, `E_a`, all intermediate
relations and their costs to the corresponding objects. So `p^Δ_F` is an
isomorphism invariant, which is the isomorphism invariance of `A_{F,r}` in
[TD]. ∎

**Corollary 2.4.** For every countable `Γ`,

    PC(s_Γ) = sup_F inf_{Δ f.g., F ⊆ Δ} p^Δ_F(s_Δ).

So the pseudocost of the Bernoulli shift is determined by the Bernoulli
sandwich costs of the finitely generated subgroups, together with their
inclusions.

These three lemmas are node `fpbs-pseudocost-localizes-to-fg-sandwich-costs`,
with route `-proof`.

**Freeness of `s_Γ` for infinite `Γ`** (used below). Take `γ ≠ e` and any
`η ∈ Γ`, so `γ^{-1} η ≠ η`. If `γ·x = x` then `x(γ^{-1} η) = x(η)`, which is an
event of probability 0 for two independent uniform coordinates.

**Proposition 2.5 (the easy direction of the sandwich comparison).** Let `Δ` be
countably infinite, `a` a free p.m.p. action of `Δ`, `b` a p.m.p. action with
`a ≼ b`, and `F ⊆ Δ` finite. Then `p^Δ_F(b) ≤ p^Δ_F(a)`. In particular, [AW]
gives `p^Δ_F(a) ≤ p^Δ_F(s_Δ)` for every free `a`.

*Proof.* Let `p^Δ_F(a) < r`, so `a ∈ A_{F,r}`. By `thm:KecOpen`, `a` lies in
the interior of `A_{F,r}`. The proof of `cor:AFrPC` shows that `b` then lies
in `A_{F,r}`. That proof passes to an isomorphic copy of `b` by Kechris,
Proposition 10.1, and uses isomorphism invariance. So `p^Δ_F(b) < r`. For the
last assertion, `s_Δ` is free and `s_Δ ≼ a`, so apply the first part with the
roles `(s_Δ, a)`. ∎

This proposition is recorded here and not as a graph node, because it rests on
a step inside the proof of `cor:AFrPC` rather than on a quoted statement. No
route below uses it.

## 3. The relative sandwich Bernoulli bound

**RBL** (node `fpbs-relative-sandwich-cost-bernoulli-lower-bound`, OPEN).
For every finitely generated group `Δ`, every finite `F ⊆ Δ` and every free
p.m.p. action `a` of `Δ`, we have `p^Δ_F(s_Δ) ≤ p^Δ_F(a)`.

By Proposition 2.5, for infinite `Δ` RBL is equivalent to: *`p^Δ_F` is constant
on free actions of `Δ`*.

**Special cases.**
- **`F` generates `Δ`.** Then `E_{a|⟨F⟩} = E_a` and `p^Δ_F(a) = C(a)`. So RBL
  contains `fpbs-free-action-cost-at-least-bernoulli-cost` for infinite
  finitely generated groups. With Abért–Weiss maximality (the reverse
  inequality, as recorded in that node), this is H. So RBL ⇒ H. H is exactly
  the quantifier instance "`F` generates `Δ`" of RBL.
- **Finite `⟨F⟩`, or amenable `Δ`** (remark, not a graph node). Every relation
  in the sandwich contains the finite or hyperfinite `E_{a|⟨F⟩}` and lies
  inside `E_a`.
  - For amenable `Δ`, every sandwich relation is hyperfinite.
  - For finite `Δ`, every sandwich relation has finite classes.
  - In both cases the minimum is attained at `E = E_{a|⟨F⟩}`, whose cost is
    `1 − 1/|⟨F⟩|` (or 1 if `⟨F⟩` is infinite). This value depends only on
    freeness.
  - The formula used is Levitt's `C(E) = ∫ (1 − 1/|[x]_E|)` for hyperfinite
    `E`. It has not been re-imported verbatim here.

**Theorem 3.1 (RBL ⇒ fixed pseudocost; route
`fpbs-fixed-pseudocost-from-relative-sandwich-bound`).** Assume RBL. Then every
countably infinite group `Γ` has fixed pseudocost: `PC(a) = PC(s_Γ)` for every
free p.m.p. action `a` of `Γ`.

*Proof.* Let `a` be free and `F ⊆ Γ` finite. For every finitely generated
`Δ ≤ Γ` with `F ⊆ Δ`, the restriction `a|Δ` is free, and

    p_F(s_Γ) ≤ p^Δ_F(s_Γ|Δ)     (Lemma 2.1, ≤)
             = p^Δ_F(s_Δ)       (Lemma 2.3)
             ≤ p^Δ_F(a|Δ)       (RBL applied to Δ, F, a|Δ).

Taking the infimum over `Δ` and using Lemma 2.1 (≥) gives
`p_F(s_Γ) ≤ p_F(a)`. Taking the supremum over `F` with Lemma 2.2 gives
`PC(s_Γ) ≤ PC(a)`.

Conversely, `s_Γ` is free because `Γ` is infinite, and [AW] gives `s_Γ ≼ a`.
`cor:AFrPC` then gives `PC(a) ≤ PC(s_Γ)`. ∎

**What the proof actually uses.** Only the inequality
`inf_{Δ ⊇ F} p^Δ_F(s_Δ) ≤ inf_{Δ ⊇ F} p^Δ_F(a|Δ)` is needed, for each `F`,
along subgroups of the one group `Γ`. RBL is the uniform, subgroup-free form.
This is the quantifier shift: H controls `p^Δ_F` only when `F` generates
`Δ`. Shape (A) needs it for `F` a fixed finite subset of an ever larger `Δ`.

**Why this does not contradict §6 of the passage artifact.** That section
observes that openness moves membership in `A_{F,r}` only upward along `≼`
(from `s_Γ` to `a_0`), and that H constrains only subgroup orbit relations.
Both remain true. RBL is a new downward hypothesis. It concerns intermediate
relations, not subgroup orbit relations, but only on finitely generated
groups. Lemma 2.1 shows the downward transfer can be done one finitely
generated subgroup at a time.

**Open question.** Does the flagship, or FPC, or H, imply RBL? A positive
answer would make RBL equivalent to shape (A) exclusion. A candidate obstacle:
- `PC(a) = sup_F p_F(a)` only sees the supremum over `F`.
- RBL is a statement for each `F` separately.
- A group `Δ` with some `F` for which `p^Δ_F` depends on the action, but where
  the supremum over all `F ⊆ Γ` in every countable `Γ ⊇ Δ` is still constant,
  would separate them.

No construction is attempted here.

## 4. Decomposition of the flagship

**FPC** (node `fpbs-countable-groups-have-fixed-pseudocost`, OPEN). Every
countably infinite `Γ` has fixed pseudocost.

**U_fin** (node `fpbs-finite-cost-groups-free-actions-have-finite-cost`, OPEN).
If `Γ` is countably infinite and `C(Γ) < ∞`, then every free p.m.p. action of
`Γ` has finite cost.

**Theorem 4.1 (route `fpbs-fixed-price-from-fixed-pseudocost-and-finite-costs`).**
FPC and U_fin together imply `fpbs-fixed-price-universal`.

*Proof.* Let `Γ` be countably infinite and `b` free, where
`C(Γ) = inf { C(a) : a free }`.
- If `C(Γ) = ∞`, every free action has cost `∞`.
- Otherwise, fix `ε > 0` and a free `a` with `C(a) < C(Γ) + ε`.
  1. `cor:PC=C`(1) gives `PC(a) = C(a)`.
  2. FPC gives `PC(b) = PC(a) < C(Γ) + ε`. As `ε` is arbitrary,
     `PC(b) ≤ C(Γ)`.
  3. `prop:analogue`(1) gives `PC(b) ≥ C(Γ)`.
  4. U_fin gives `C(b) < ∞`, so `cor:PC=C`(1) gives
     `C(b) = PC(b) = C(Γ)`. ∎

This is the per-group criterion stated in §6 of the passage artifact ("a group
with C(Γ) < ∞ has fixed price iff it has fixed pseudocost and satisfies (U)"),
now made a graph route that does not pass through H or the countable passage.

**Necessity.**
- The flagship implies U_fin: some free action has finite cost, and all costs
  are equal.
- The flagship implies FPC for groups with `C(Γ) < ∞`: all costs equal
  `C(Γ) < ∞`, so all pseudocosts equal `C(Γ)` by `cor:PC=C`(1).
- For groups with `C(Γ) = ∞`, the flagship says nothing about pseudocost. FPC
  as stated is stronger there, and it is what RBL delivers.

**Independence of the premises.**
- FPC alone gives `PC(b) = C(Γ)` but no finiteness. This is shape (B), a
  free-action instance of [TD] Q 7.6, which is open.
- U_fin alone gives no comparison between `s_Γ` and an optimal action. That
  is shape (A).
- Neither premise implies the other by any argument known here.

**The resulting graph picture.**

    RBL (fg groups, relative) ──► FPC (all countable groups) ─┐
                                                              ├─► flagship
    U_fin (finite-cost groups: no infinite free cost) ────────┘

- RBL already contains H.
- U_fin is H-independent. It is not reduced to finitely generated groups.
  - For finitely generated groups, U_fin is automatic: a finite generating set
    `S` gives cost at most `|S|`.
  - The difficulty is entirely in groups that are not finitely generated,
    where free actions can have infinite cost.

## 5. Consequence for the target hole

The target says: under H, every countably infinite `Γ` not generated by
finitely many amenable subgroups (NAG) has fixed price.

**Proposition 5.1.** Assume RBL. Then the target's conclusion is equivalent to
U_fin restricted to NAG groups.

*Proof.* RBL ⇒ H (§3), so the target's conclusion is "every NAG group has
fixed price".
- That conclusion implies U_fin for NAG groups (Necessity, §4).
- Conversely, Theorem 3.1 gives FPC for every NAG group, and the per-group
  proof of Theorem 4.1 gives fixed price for every NAG group satisfying U_fin.
∎

So under RBL, shape (A) is gone everywhere and the target is exactly shape (B)
on NAG groups. By Theorem G, a counterexample `Γ` then has `C(Γ) < ∞`, fixed
pseudocost `C(Γ)`, and a free action `b` with `C(b) = ∞` whose orbit relation
is not treeable.

**Test group.** For `Γ_mal`, and more generally for locally free groups,
`fpbs-locally-free-free-action-costs-are-c-star-or-infinite` already gives
fixed pseudocost unconditionally. So FPC holds there without RBL, consistently
with Theorem 3.1. The residue is `fpbs-locally-free-free-actions-have-finite-cost`,
a special case of U_fin.

## 6. What was killed and what was not tried

- **Retract or quotient descents toward U_fin.** Groups generated by finitely
  many amenable subgroups are closed under quotients. So a finitely generated
  supergroup retracting onto a NAG group does not exist, because retracts are
  quotients. Any descent of U_fin from a finitely generated group through a
  retraction is therefore void.
- **Bernoulli noise.** `fpbs-bernoulli-noise-does-not-change-cost`
  (`C(a × b) = C(a)`) is stated only for finitely generated groups, where
  U_fin is automatic. It gives nothing for U_fin.
- **Not tried.**
  - A proof of RBL for any nonamenable `Δ` with proper `F`. Free groups are
    the natural first case, since all sandwich relations there are treeable.
  - A construction separating RBL from FPC.
