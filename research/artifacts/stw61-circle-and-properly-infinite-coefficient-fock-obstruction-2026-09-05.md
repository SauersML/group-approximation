# LXI: arbitrary circle and properly infinite coefficient products in the Fock obstruction

Date: 2026-09-05. Complete proof, internally reviewed with no unresolved
gap found. This is not external peer review or formal verification.
This extends the preceding mixed semicircular theorem by adjoining
the circle generator and unrestricted complementary coefficient
isometries. It does not resolve LXI.

## 1. The larger allowed algebra

For n>=2, let T_n be the universal unital C*-algebra generated by
n isometries v_1,...,v_n with pairwise orthogonal ranges. Set

```text
C_n=C(S^1)*_C T_n,
p=v_1v_1*,
e_n=1-sum_(i=1)^n v_i v_i*,
h=z v_1+v_1* z*,
K_n=C*(1,z,v_2,...,v_n,h) inside C_n.
```

Here z is the universal circle unitary. The coefficient algebra
D_n=C*(1,v_2,...,v_n) is a unital copy of T_(n-1). In particular,
for n>=3 it is itself properly infinite: it contains at least
two isometries with orthogonal ranges, both with initial
projection equal to the unit.

**Theorem.** For every W in U(K_n),

```text
||[z,WpW*]||=1.
```

Both WpW* and its complement are properly infinite and full.
Moreover, the following distances in the norm of C_n are exact:

```text
dist(p,K_n)=dist(e_n,K_n)=1/2,
dist(WpW*,K_n)=1/2   for every W in U(K_n).
```

Thus K_n is a proper subalgebra, despite containing the circle
generator and, when n>=3, a whole unital properly infinite
coefficient algebra. No identification of K_n with C_n is made.

The theorem allows every unitary in the generated algebra.
For example, arbitrary finite products

```text
W=a_0 f_0(z) exp(i t_1 h) a_1 f_1(z)
  ... exp(i t_m h) a_m f_m(z),
```

are covered, where a_j are arbitrary unitaries of D_n,
f_j:S^1 -> S^1 are arbitrary continuous unitary functions,
and t_j are arbitrary real numbers. Unitaries formed by further
sums, products, and functional calculus within K_n are covered
as well. This is not an assertion limited to this displayed
product form.

## 2. A representation which centralizes the entire allowed algebra

Let F_n be full Fock space on n letters, with vacuum Omega.
Let L_i and R_i be left and right creation operators, and write
E_Omega for the vacuum projection. On the word basis,

```text
[L_i,R_j]=[L_i*,R_j*]=0,
[L_i*,R_j]=delta_ij E_Omega,
[L_i,R_j*]=-delta_ij E_Omega.
```

Consequently every L_i+L_i* commutes with R_1+R_1*, while
L_i itself commutes with R_1+R_1* whenever i>=2. Put

```text
V=sign(R_1+R_1*),
```

using the Borel function equal to 1 on [0,infinity) and -1 on
(-infinity,0). Then V=V*=V^(-1), and

```text
[V,L_1+L_1*]=0,
[V,L_i]=0 for i>=2.
```

The reducing subspace generated from Omega by R_1+R_1* has
basis Omega,1,11,111,... and is the unilateral Jacobi model
S+S*. Under its sine transform, S+S* becomes multiplication
by 2 cos(theta), with vacuum weight (2/pi)sin(theta)^2 dtheta.
Symmetry about theta=pi/2 therefore gives

```text
<V Omega,Omega>=0,
L_1L_1* V Omega=V Omega.
```

The second equation holds because V Omega lies in the closed
span of the nonempty words consisting solely of 1's. In
particular, with P_1=L_1L_1*,

```text
||[V,P_1]||=1,
||[V,E_Omega]||=1.
```

For the first equality, [V,P_1]Omega=-V Omega. For the second,
[V,E_Omega]Omega=V Omega. The universal upper bound for a
unitary-projection commutator is one.

These are the same elementary left-right Fock relations and
sine-transform facts used in the preceding
[semicircular audit](stw61-mixed-semicircular-defect-complete-obstruction-2026-09-05.md).
For the classical creation-operator framework, see Nica--Speicher,
*Lectures on the Combinatorics of Free Probability*, Lecture 7,
Definitions 7.13 and Proposition 7.16 in the
[author-hosted primary text](https://www.math.uni-sb.de/ag/speicher/publikationen/Nica-Speicher.pdf).

The important change from the preceding representation is the
side on which the sign unitary is placed. Define

```text
pi(z)=V,
pi(v_i)=L_i V   for all i.
```

The L_i V are isometries with orthogonal ranges, so these
assignments define a unital representation pi:C_n -> B(F_n).
They give

```text
pi(p)=P_1,
pi(e_n)=E_Omega,
pi(h)=V(L_1+L_1*)V=L_1+L_1*.
```

For i>=2, the operators pi(v_i)=L_i V commute with V.
The same is true of pi(h) and pi(z)=V. Thus

```text
pi(K_n) is contained in {V}',
```

the commutant of V. This centralizes the image of the whole
allowed algebra, including all circle functions and arbitrary
unitaries of the properly infinite coefficient algebra D_n.
It does not centralize the images of p or of e_n. The Toeplitz
defect is retained as the nonzero rank-one E_Omega.

## 3. Maximal commutators and exact distance

Fix W in U(K_n), and put Y=pi(W). Since Y commutes with V,

```text
||pi([z,WpW*])||
  =||[V,Y P_1 Y*]||
  =||[V,P_1]||
  =1.
```

Contractivity gives the universal lower bound, and the usual
unitary-projection commutator bound gives the reverse inequality.
This proves the norm theorem for every unitary of K_n.

For any a in K_n, its image commutes with V, so

```text
1=||[V,pi(p)]||
 <=2||pi(p-a)||
 <=2||p-a||.
```

Hence dist(p,K_n)>=1/2. Taking a=(1/2)1 gives equality, since
p is a nontrivial projection. The same argument applies to e_n
using its nonzero represented commutator. Both projections
therefore lie outside K_n by a uniform, exact distance.

Conjugation by W is an isometric automorphism of C_n carrying
K_n onto itself. Consequently

```text
dist(WpW*,K_n)=dist(p,K_n)=1/2.
```

Finally, p is equivalent to the properly infinite unit, and
1-p contains v_2v_2*, which is another equivalent copy of the
unit. Thus both are properly infinite and full, as are their
unitary conjugates.

## 4. A full T_2 coefficient copy inside the original two-isometry problem

We now transfer the n=3 result isometrically into the original
C=C(S^1)*_C T_2. Denote the original Toeplitz generators by
s_1,s_2 and its defect by e=1-s_1s_1*-s_2s_2*. Put

```text
t_1=s_1,
t_2=s_2s_1,
t_3=s_2s_2.
```

These are three isometries with pairwise orthogonal ranges and

```text
1-sum_(i=1)^3 t_i t_i* = e+s_2 e s_2* != 0.
```

They give a faithful unital embedding T_3 -> T_2. Here is a
direct verification, so the transport does not assume an
extension of the representation from Section 2.

In the faithful standard Fock representation of T_2, every
finite word in {1,2} has a unique parsing as a sequence of
the three prefix words {1,21,22}, followed by either the
empty word or the single letter 2. Thus the binary Fock
space decomposes as

```text
F_2 = F_3 tensor C^2,
```

where t_1,t_2,t_3 act as the ternary left creation operators
tensored with the identity. This is two copies of the faithful
Fock representation of T_3. Faithfulness of each standard
Toeplitz Fock representation follows, for example, by gauge
averaging and faithfulness on its finite-dimensional fixed
point cores, as checked in Section 4 of the preceding audit.
The homomorphism T_3 -> T_2 is therefore injective.

The full unital free-product inclusion theorem now gives an
isometric embedding

```text
C(S^1)*_C T_3 -> C(S^1)*_C T_2
```

with z fixed. The precise source is Armstrong--Dykema--Exel--Li,
*On embeddings of full amalgamated free product C*-algebras*,
Proposition 2.2, with the common amalgamating algebra C unchanged
([primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0210448)).

Accordingly the following is a theorem in the original algebra C.
Let

```text
D=C*(1,s_2s_1,s_2s_2),
h=z s_1+s_1* z*,
K=C*(1,z,D,h).
```

The coefficient algebra D is a faithful unital copy of T_2:
its two generators are orthogonal isometries, and their
nonzero defect can be checked in Fock space by the same
prefix parsing, or by the usual faithful Toeplitz criterion.
It is therefore a genuine unital properly infinite coefficient
algebra, not a finite-dimensional or MF one.

For every W in U(K),

```text
||[z,W(s_1s_1*)W*]||=1,
dist(s_1s_1*,K)=1/2,
dist(e+s_2 e s_2*,K)=1/2.
```

This permits arbitrary products and unitary functional calculus
using all of D, all of C*(z), and the mixed self-adjoint h.
The Fock representation pi of C(S^1)*T_3 is used only to prove
the norm and distance inside that subalgebra. It is not asserted
to extend to a representation of C(S^1)*T_2. The isometric
inclusion is exactly what transports the conclusions.

For completeness, faithfulness of the coefficient copy D can
also be seen without appealing to a criterion: the two prefix
words {21,22} have a unique maximal parsing on each binary
Fock word, leaving a residual word which is empty, equal to 2,
or begins with 1. Prefixing 21 or 22 gives copies of the
standard two-letter Fock representation indexed by these
residual words. Thus the representation is faithful.

## 5. Why this is a substantive extension and its exact boundary

The earlier theorem allowed unitaries in the algebra generated
by the mixed self-adjoint letters. The present algebra also
contains z itself and a whole unital properly infinite
coefficient copy. Products with arbitrary coefficient unitaries
and independent circle functions are therefore included,
together with arbitrary further unitary functional calculus.
No common antipodal condition on the functions is needed.

This is not a fixed-corner argument. The family

```text
q_t=exp(i t h)(s_1s_1*)exp(-i t h),   t!=0,
```

belongs to the new class. The two scalar evaluations proved in
the preceding semicircular audit show that q_t is contained in
no proper original Toeplitz coefficient projection or its
f(z)-translate, and that q_t has nonzero compressions by e,
s_1s_1*, and s_2s_2*. The present theorem continues to obstruct
arbitrary allowed coefficient and circle products around this
mixed operation.

There remains a precise generator boundary. The distinguished
projection s_1s_1* is not in K, and cannot even be approximated
within distance less than 1/2 by K. Thus this theorem does not
cover arbitrary mixed unitaries involving that projection as an
additional coefficient, or every unitary of the full free
product. The fact that K is itself properly infinite does not
alter this missing-generator issue.

As before, full-free-product inclusion transports the same
norm obstruction to a full-spectrum unitary v in A and the
corresponding subalgebra of A*T_2. No universal strict
commutator, null-homotopy, surviving free-envelope component,
or solution of LXI is asserted here.
