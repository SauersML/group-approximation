# Hyperlinear cycle-synchronization bypasses: defect quotients and local gauge

**Integrated:** 2026-08-21  
**Status:** rigorous reduction dossier; neither hyperlinear => sofic nor universal hyperlinearity is claimed.

## 1. Why this dossier exists

The spherical lane has already crossed the easy-looking gates.  In particular,
`hs-defect-is-mean-square-sphere-displacement` recodes a unitary microstate as
a mean-square almost action on a sphere,
`spherical-tight-single-colour-near-perfect-matching` gives arbitrarily tight
near-perfect geometric matchings for every individual colour, and
`spherical-one-overlap-forest-rounding` closes every relation system that can
be built one shared map at a time.  The remaining obstruction in
`hypergraph-near-perfect-schreier-packing` is therefore genuinely cyclic:
when a relation `gh=k` reuses already chosen maps, the two indices

```text
sigma_g sigma_h(i)   and   sigma_k(i)
```

can be geometrically extremely close and nevertheless disagree in Hamming
distance by one full unit.

This dossier isolates two ways to bypass that index/geometry mismatch without
asking a hypergraph absorber to choose all colours simultaneously:

1. **quotient exactification:** keep the independently chosen matchings and
   quotient by the invariant congruence generated by their relation defects;
2. **local gauge trivialization:** keep the point set and correct the chosen
   permutations by geometrically local source permutations solving a finite
   nonabelian cocycle system.

Both routes use the whole finite table, but neither requires entrywise
dephasing, a common Cartan algebra, a random intermediate basis, or a
colour-by-colour Hamming rounding theorem.

A targeted external freshness/prior-art check was repeated on 2026-08-21.
Current 2026 literature still treats the existence of nonsofic and
nonhyperlinear groups as open, and targeted searches around permutation
stability, approximate actions, normal closures of relator defects, and
geometric unitary-to-permutation rounding did not locate this specific
finite-table defect-congruence reduction.  This is a scope statement, not a
priority claim.  Bradford's 2026 work on arbitrarily poor quantitative
permutation stability is also a reason to prefer the quotient lane: it does
not ask a Hamming-large defect to be repaired by a small Hamming perturbation.

## 2. The short-chord calculation

Fix a finite label set `S`, tested relations `R`, a unitary table `{U_s}`, and
a spherical cloud `x_1,...,x_N`.  Suppose the chosen partial matching for each
label shadows its unitary at radius `rho`:

```text
||U_s x_i - x_(sigma_s(i))|| <= rho.                    (C1)
```

For a tested relation `gh=k`, take a root `i` for which the three matching
edges are defined and put

```text
y = sigma_g(sigma_h(i)),
z = sigma_k(i).
```

If the pointwise unitary relation defect is

```text
||(U_g U_h-U_k)x_i|| <= zeta,
```

then

```text
||x_y-x_z||
 <= ||x_y-U_g x_(sigma_h(i))||
  + ||U_g x_(sigma_h(i))-U_g U_h x_i||
  + ||U_g U_h x_i-U_k x_i||
  + ||U_k x_i-x_z||
 <= 3 rho + zeta.                                      (C2)
```

Thus every cyclic Hamming mismatch is represented by a **short geometric
chord** on all but the roots lost to the matching domains or to the
mean-square-to-pointwise defect conversion.  The point of `(C2)` is not that
short chords must be equal indices; Cairn already records that this inference
is false.  The point is that the obstruction now has a concrete geometric
carrier that can be quotiented or gauged.

## 3. Bypass A: invariant defect congruence

Complete the near-total partial matchings arbitrarily to permutations on
`X=[N]`; all completion failures are put into the exceptional root sets.  For
each tested relation `r=(g,h,k)`, let `G_r` be the good roots on which `(C1)`
and the chosen pointwise relation bound hold.

Define `E` to be the **smallest equivalence relation on `X`** such that

1. `E` is invariant under every `sigma_s` and `sigma_s^{-1}`; and
2. for every `i in G_r`,

```text
sigma_g sigma_h(i)  E  sigma_k(i).                     (Q1)
```

Then every `sigma_s` descends to a permutation of `X/E`, and `(Q1)` makes the
relation `gh=k` exact on every quotient class meeting `G_r`.  There is a small
but important improvement over taking the quotient set with its uniform
measure.  For each class `C`, retain `|C|` formal copies:

```text
Y = { (C,t) : C in X/E, 1 <= t <= |C| }.               (Q2)
```

Invariance implies `|sigma_s C|=|C|`, so

```text
hat_sigma_s(C,t) = (sigma_s C,t)                       (Q3)
```

is a genuine permutation of `Y`, and `|Y|=N` exactly.

This weighted blow-up has two decisive consequences.

### 3.1 Relation errors cannot amplify

If a quotient class `C` meets `G_r`, choose `i in C cap G_r`.  For every
`j in C`, invariance of `E` gives

```text
sigma_g sigma_h(j)
 E sigma_g sigma_h(i)
 E sigma_k(i)
 E sigma_k(j).
```

Hence `gh=k` holds on every copy above `C`.  A bad quotient class must be
contained entirely in `X\G_r`; therefore the Hamming defect of `(Q3)` for
relation `r` is at most

```text
|X\G_r|/N.                                             (Q4)
```

Taking the invariant closure of the good defect pairs can merge huge pieces
of `X`, but it cannot magnify the *relation-error mass*.

### 3.2 Separation has one exact formula

For two tested words `u,v`, the induced permutations agree above class `C`
exactly when

```text
sigma_u(i) E sigma_v(i)
```

for one, hence every, `i in C`.  Therefore

```text
d_H(hat_sigma_u,hat_sigma_v)
 = (1/N) |{i in X : sigma_u(i) not E sigma_v(i)}|.      (Q5)
```

So the entire quotient bypass reduces Q3.4 on the spherical lane to one
noncollapse statement:

> choose the tight one-colour matchings so that the invariant congruence
> generated by the short relation-defect chords does not identify the images
> of distinct tested words on more than `o(N)` roots.

No lower bound on the number of quotient classes is needed; `(Q2)` removes
that distraction completely.

### 3.3 Group-theoretic interpretation and kill test

Ignoring exceptional roots for intuition, let `H=<sigma_s> <= Sym(X)` and let
`delta_r=sigma_k^{-1} sigma_g sigma_h`.  Then `E` is the orbit relation of the
normal closure in `H` of the relation-defect permutations `delta_r`.  The
quotient action simply factors out those accidental relator images.  The live
question is whether a tested nontrivial word enters that normal closure on a
positive fraction of roots.

This gives a sharp computational falsifier.  On finite spherical samples:

1. choose the single-colour matchings independently;
2. construct the good defect pairs;
3. close their union-find relation under the selected permutations;
4. measure `(Q5)` for every tested distinct pair.

If the congruence repeatedly percolates so that a nontrivial tested word is
class-trivial on positive density even as `rho` and the HS defect shrink, this
lane is dead.  If it remains noncollapsing, the experiment targets exactly the
new theorem rather than a proxy such as marginal degree regularity.

## 4. Bypass B: local permutation gauge

There is a dual strategy: instead of factoring the defect permutations out,
trivialize them by a geometrically local change of coordinates.

For total permutations define the relation holonomy

```text
delta_(g,h,k) = sigma_k^(-1) sigma_g sigma_h.           (G1)
```

Apply **source corrections** `c_s` and set

```text
tilde_sigma_s = sigma_s c_s.                            (G2)
```

A direct calculation gives the exact gauge law

```text
tilde_delta_(g,h,k)
 = c_k^(-1) delta_(g,h,k)
   (sigma_h^(-1) c_g sigma_h) c_h.                     (G3)
```

Thus the condition `tilde_delta=1` is the nonabelian cocycle equation

```text
c_k = delta_(g,h,k) (sigma_h^(-1)c_g sigma_h) c_h.      (G4)
```

The new target is not arbitrary permutation stability.  By `(C2)`, the
`delta_r` move almost every point only a short geometric distance; conjugation
by a geometric shadow of a unitary approximately preserves short movement.
The hoped-for theorem is therefore a **local coefficient** statement: solve
the finite system `(G4)` with corrections `c_s` that themselves move almost
every cloud point only `o(1)` in spherical distance.

If such `c_s` exist, then `sigma_s c_s` still shadows `U_s`: whenever both
matching and correction are good,

```text
||U_s x_i-x_(sigma_s c_s(i))||
 <= ||U_s x_i-U_s x_(c_s(i))||
  + ||U_s x_(c_s(i))-x_(sigma_s c_s(i))||,
```

which is the correction chord plus the original matching radius.  The exact
cocycle formula then gives the multiplication relations on `1-o(1)` roots,
and `trace-separation-sphere-distance-concentration` preserves Hamming
separation when the total geometric tracking radius is chosen sufficiently
small.

This turns the synchronization problem into a finite nonabelian cohomology
problem inside the group of short-edge permutations of a random spherical
cloud.  It is deliberately orthogonal to hypergraph absorption: the
matchings are chosen first, and only their cycle holonomies are solved.

## 5. What is actually new on the Cairn frontier

The two established algebraic/geometric reductions promoted with this dossier
are:

- invariant defect congruence + weighted blow-up exactifies the tested
  relations without amplifying their exceptional-root mass;
- cyclic relation mismatches produced by tight spherical matchings are short
  chords, with the explicit `3 rho + zeta` bound;
- source corrections obey the exact nonabelian gauge law `(G3)`.

The two new open gates are intentionally narrow:

- `spherical-defect-congruence-preserves-separation` — prove `(Q5)` stays
  regular-character separated after congruence closure;
- `spherical-short-holonomies-admit-local-gauge` — solve `(G4)` with
  geometrically local corrections.

Either gate yields a new route from hyperlinearity to soficity without closing
`hypergraph-near-perfect-schreier-packing` or
`hypergraph-absorption-schreier-matching`.

## 6. Boundary conditions that must not be blurred

- Geometric closeness is **not** Hamming closeness.  The quotient route never
  makes that inference; it replaces equality of indices by equality of
  congruence classes and then proves the exact weighted formula `(Q5)`.
- Normal closure can percolate.  Nothing here proves the quotient noncollapse
  theorem; that is recorded as an open claim rather than hidden in prose.
- Local permutation groups can have nontrivial holonomy.  Nothing here proves
  the gauge theorem; `(G3)` only exposes its exact algebraic form.
- The arbitrary completion of near-perfect matchings is charged to the
  exceptional root sets.  It is not assumed to preserve geometry there.
- These are finite-table reductions.  No uniform quantitative permutation
  stability theorem for an infinite presentation is being assumed.
