# Problem 141: fifth parallel research campaign

Date: 2026-09-20. **Problem 141 remains unsolved.** No non-residually-finite
word-hyperbolic group has been proved in this campaign.

The preceding campaign constructed a finite group of order 7^28 detecting
the canonical central element z of B_c0. That establishes virtual
splitting for this particular mod-seven extension. The present campaign
tests larger central orders and other finite-cover families.

## Completed results

1. [Central-power depth](hyperbolic-rf-wave5-central-power-depth-2026-09-20.md).
   Hall--Struik collection gives gamma_i(G)^p <= gamma_(i+p-1)(G) for
   groups generated by elements of order dividing p. Therefore
   z^(7^a) lies in gamma_(5+6a)(B_c0). An order-49 nilpotent detector
   must have class at least eleven. A central C7 extension of the
   previously constructed class-five detector cannot supply it.
   The proof was independently reviewed against the primary source.

2. [Abelian covers at other primes](hyperbolic-rf-wave5-audit-2026-09-20.md).
   Three distinct quadratic seventh-root phase vectors are linearly
   independent in every characteristic other than seven. Applying this
   to the face equations proves that every one-dimensional coefficient
   character has zero first cohomology in those characteristics.
   Every kernel of an abelian quotient of K7 consequently has finite
   seven-primary abelianization. This excludes prime-to-seven solvable
   refinements over those abelian quotients. Root independently reviewed
   the complete proof, including characteristics two and three.

3. [Exact group words](hyperbolic-rf-wave5-visibility-2026-09-20.md).
   The integral central extension now has an explicit six-generator,
   24-relator group presentation valid in every target characteristic
   and nilpotency class. GAP group quotients through p-class ten
   detect z with order seven. The class-ten quotient has order 7^390;
   all source relators and centrality were checked. These calculations
   do not assert that seven bounds all finite orders of z.

4. [The PSL2(19) square kernel at prime three](hyperbolic-rf-wave5-detectors-2026-09-20.md).
   Its full first homology with F3 coefficients vanishes. The proof uses
   a verified projective generator and all 25 tensor types, rather than
   selected invariant sectors. Combined with the previous F5 result,
   this excludes refinements with finite kernels supported on primes
   three and five, including for its nonzero central five-twists.
   Removing the explicit solvability assumption uses Burnside's
   two-prime theorem. Root replayed
   the group input, module certificates and full-generator certificate;
   the audit agent independently replayed the largest tensor calculation.

5. [Two nonabelian coefficient sectors](hyperbolic-rf-wave5-audit-2026-09-20.md).
   The specified seven- and 49-dimensional F8 representations have zero
   first cohomology. Root reviewed and replayed both exact computations.
   They do not settle all representations of the class-two cover.

## Work not yet promoted to a theorem

- The class-ten quotient is complete after splitting its computation
  into resumable phases. The class-eleven run stopped during finalization
  after all 24 lift relators were processed. Its finish receipt records
  return code -15 after about 60 seconds; there is no class-eleven result
  JSON. It is not a live computation and no order-49 detector is certified.
- Neither the abelian-cover theorem nor the two tested nonabelian
  sectors controls all finite seven-group covers in other characteristics.
- The PSL2(19) square-kernel result does not decide all coefficient
  primes or its integral abelianization.

The current gap remains a statement controlling all finite images of
one candidate, or an explicit new candidate with its finite-residual
witness proved. None of the family restrictions above quantifies over
all finite images. Cairn's dependency labels record the subsidiary
proofs; they are not a formal verification of Problem 141.
