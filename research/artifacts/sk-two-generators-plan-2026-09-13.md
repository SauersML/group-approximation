## New routes this lane tries

Each `1+ε_ab` (a≠b) equals `1+αβ` with `α=ε_ab`, `β=ε_bb` and `βα=0`, and in characteristic 2 one has `e_12(α)e_21(β)e_12(α)e_21(β)=diag(1+αβ,1,1)` whenever `βα=0` (checked by hand). The coordinate-3 version comes from conjugating by a constant permutation matrix.
   - `diag(1,1,v)` commutes with `w`, so `b=w·diag(1,1,v)` has order 6, with `b^3=w` and `b^4=diag(1,1,v)`.

   - Question: does `<a,b>` contain a generator with coefficient `p`, such as `t`?
