# Rokhlin entropy lower bounds without finite models, and where they stop on simple Kazhdan hosts

Lane `gk-p-inf-f`, 2026-09-12. This artifact supports:
- the claim `simple-kazhdan-groups-have-no-proper-co-amenable-subgroups`, with its route `simple-kazhdan-no-proper-co-amenable-subgroups-proof`;
- the Attempts entries on `bernoulli-rokhlin-maximal-on-fg-simple-kazhdan-groups` and `leavitt-unit-group-has-positive-rokhlin-entropy-action`.

The problem is INF on a nonsofic host: for a finitely generated simple Kazhdan group `G` (or `R^x`, or the tester host), show that the uniform Bernoulli shift over `G` has Rokhlin entropy `log |A|`, or at least positive Rokhlin entropy. This artifact takes the mechanisms that do not use finite models and states where each one stops. Only Proposition 1.1 is claimed as a theorem. Sections 1.2, 2.3, 3 and 4 are mechanism analyses: they say which hypothesis a mechanism consumes and which landed fact removes it on the canonical hosts.

Sibling lanes own neighbouring mechanisms, so this artifact does not restate them:
- `gk-p-inf-wild`: invariants that are monotone under factors or weak containment. Nontrivial Bernoulli shifts factor onto each other once `G` contains a nonabelian free group.
- `gk-p-inf-cost`: orbit-equivalence invariants (cost, `l^2`-Betti numbers).
- `gk-p-inf-kazhdan`: spectral gap and strong ergodicity.

## 1. Co-amenable subgroups

### 1.1 The theorem

**Proposition 1.1.** Let `G` be an infinite simple group with property (T), and let `H <= G` be co-amenable, meaning `G/H` carries a `G`-invariant mean. Then `H = G`.

*Proof.* By `no-invariant-mean-on-coset-space`, a Kazhdan group has no invariant mean on an infinite coset space, so `[G:H]` is finite. `G` acts on the finite set `G/H`, and the kernel `N` of this action is normal of finite index. Since `G` is simple, `N = 1` or `N = G`. If `N = 1`, then `G` embeds in the finite group `Sym(G/H)`, contradicting that `G` is infinite. So `N = G`: every `g` fixes the coset `H`, and `H = G`. QED

Instances:
- `R^x = L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` is infinite and simple with property (T) (`leavitt-unit-group-is-simple-fa-nonsofic`, and (T) through `R^x = EL_12(R)`).
- By `countable-group-embeds-in-fg-simple-kazhdan-group`, every countable group lies in such a host. The reduction `rokhlin-maximality-via-fg-simple-kazhdan-hosts` moves INF to exactly these hosts.

### 1.2 Why subgroup-induced lower bounds consume co-amenability

This subsection is a mechanism analysis, not a theorem.

Every lower bound for Rokhlin entropy known to the graph comes from an entropy theory on some group `H` where one exists: amenable `H` through Følner sets, sofic `H` through sofic entropy, free `H` through the f-invariant of Section 2. To bound `h^Rok_G(X)` for `X = (A^G, uniform)` from below along `H <= G`, one starts from a finite partition `P` that generates `X` under `G` with `H(P)` small, and uses the `H`-theory to bound `H(P)` from below.

- `P` generates only under `G`. The `H`-translates of `P` generate a factor that is typically far from `X`.
- The natural repair joins finitely many `G`-translates, `P_T = join_(t in T) tP`, and applies the `H`-theory to the `H`-factor generated by `P_T`. The cost is `H(P_T) <= |T| H(P)`.
- Coordinates of `X` at `1` are only approximately measurable with respect to `join_(g in B) gP` for a finite ball `B`. So the `H`-factor generated by `P_T` approximately contains the coordinates indexed by `H T'`, where `T' = { t in T : t B is contained in H T }`.
- The `H`-theory prices those coordinates at about `|T'| log|A|`. The resulting bound is `H(P) >= (|T'|/|T|) log|A|` minus approximation errors.
- To push `|T'|/|T|` to `1` at every scale `B`, the right action of `G` on `H\G` needs Følner sets. That is co-amenability of `H`, and it is how amenable-by-sofic and co-amenable permanence arguments run.
- For a non-co-amenable `H`, the achievable ratio decays as `B` grows, while `B` must grow as `H(P)` shrinks. So no bound uniform over generating partitions results.

Proposition 1.1 says that on a canonical host the only co-amenable subgroup is `G` itself. The induction returns the original question unchanged. This complements two landed facts:
- restriction and co-induction bound `h^Rok_G` only from above (`leavitt-unit-group-has-positive-rokhlin-entropy-action`, Attempts);
- maximality descends to subgroups (`bernoulli-rokhlin-maximality-passes-to-subgroups`), which is the wrong direction for a host.

## 2. Bowen's f-invariant: a lower bound with no finite models, and why it needs a tree

### 2.1 The functional

Let `F_r` be free on `s_1, ..., s_r` and act by measure-preserving maps on `(X, mu)`. For a finite partition `Q` put

    F(Q) = (1 - 2r) H(Q) + sum_(i=1..r) H(Q join s_i^-1 Q).

Writing `I(Q; R) = H(Q) + H(R) - H(Q join R)` for mutual information,

    F(Q) = H(Q) - sum_i I(Q ; s_i^-1 Q) <= H(Q),

because each mutual information is nonnegative. For the uniform Bernoulli shift over `F_r` with the time-zero partition `Q_0`, the partitions `Q_0` and `s_i^-1 Q_0` are independent, so `F(Q_0) = log|A|`. Refinements `Q_0^n = join_(|g| <= n) g Q_0` read disjoint sets of independent coordinates along the tree, so `F(Q_0^n) = log|A|` for every `n`.

### 2.2 The imported theorem and the resulting bound

Bowen, *A measure-conjugacy invariant for free group actions*, Ann. of Math. (2) 171 (2010), 1387–1400, shows that for a finite generating partition `P` the limit `f = lim_n F(P^n)` exists and does not depend on `P`.

The lower bound `f <= H(P)` for every finite generating `P` needs one more input from Bowen's proof: `F` does not increase under the splittings that pass from `P^n` to `P^(n+1)`. Given that, `f <= F(P) <= H(P)`, so `h^Rok(X) >= f(X)` for free-group actions with a finite generating partition.

- **Bernoulli shifts.** With `f = log|A|`, the Bernoulli shift over `F_r` has Rokhlin entropy `log|A|`, proved along a tree with no finite models.
- **Trust surface.** The invariance theorem is imported in the graph as `bowen-f-invariant-is-a-measure-conjugacy-invariant`, landed by `gk-p-bernoulli-mass`. The splitting monotonicity used for the one-sided bound, `F(beta join t gamma) <= F(beta)` for `t = s_j^(±1)` and `gamma <= beta`, was re-derived from submodularity by `gk-vf-positive` (Section 4.1 of `research/artifacts/gk-vf-positive-verification-2026-09-12.md`), in the form the ball refinements need. No node of this lane depends on it, so it still enters this artifact only as a mechanism model.

### 2.3 Where it stops

This subsection is a mechanism analysis.

1. **Relations.** The invariance of `f` rests on the Cayley graph of `F_r` being a tree: splitting a partition along one generator does not interact with the others. For a group with relations the analogous Bethe functional is not an isomorphism invariant. The graph already records the nonamenable window version of this failure (`window-entropy-deficits-are-not-isomorphism-invariant`).
2. **Graph-of-groups relatives.** Euler-characteristic weightings along a `G`-tree reduce a lower bound for `G` to bounds for vertex and edge groups. Property (T) implies FA (`property-t-implies-property-fa`), so every action of a canonical host on a tree fixes a vertex. The decomposition has `G` as a vertex group and returns the question. The same holds for cubical splittings, since (T) groups fix a point in every action on a CAT(0) cube complex (Niblo–Reeves; mentioned, not imported).
3. **Free subgroups.** A free subgroup `F <= G` of infinite index restricts the Bernoulli `G`-shift to a Bernoulli `F`-shift with base `A^(F\G)`, of infinite entropy. `f` is then infinite or undefined and says nothing about `G`-generating partitions. Transferring along `F` needs co-amenability (Section 1.2), which an infinite-index subgroup of a canonical host never has.
4. **Treeings of the whole orbit relation.** An f-type invariant for a treed orbit relation would bound partitions that generate the whole relation. Infinite Kazhdan groups admit no treeable free ergodic actions (Adams–Spatzier; mentioned, not imported), so the Bernoulli orbit relation of a canonical host has no treeing.

## 3. Percolation and factor-of-iid treeings

This section is a mechanism analysis.

A nonamenable `G` carries factor-of-iid percolations whose clusters contain treed subrelations (Gaboriau–Lyons). This gives a free group of partial transformations inside the Bernoulli orbit relation, measurable with respect to the Bernoulli coordinates. An f-type bound on the treed subrelation `S` would then have to control a `G`-generating partition `P`:
- **Encoding the treeing costs entropy.** The `S`-generators are partial `G`-translations with measurable domains, and encoding them costs entropy that `P` does not pay for. For factor-of-iid treeings the domains depend on the coordinates, so the time-zero computation of Section 2.1 no longer shows independence.
- **Generation does not pass to `S`.** `P` generates under `G`, not under `S`. Recovering coordinates along `S`-clusters needs the ambient relation to have Følner sets relative to `S`, the relation-level form of co-amenability in Section 1.2.
- **The relation-level (T) statement is not proved here.** For Kazhdan groups the Bernoulli orbit relation has property (T), and relative (T) for equivalence relations should force co-amenable subrelations to have finite index, just as Proposition 1.1 does for subgroups. Kazhdan groups also have cost `1` (`fpbs-kazhdan-groups-have-cost-one`), so treeings with extra room are unavailable. The cost side belongs to `gk-p-inf-cost`.

## 4. What a surviving mechanism must look like

Combining this artifact with the landed Attempts and the sibling lanes' obstructions, a lower bound for Bernoulli Rokhlin entropy on a simple Kazhdan host must:
1. use no finite models, since the host is nonsofic;
2. be an isomorphism invariant of the `G`-action that is not factor-monotone and not weak-containment-monotone, because nontrivial Bernoulli shifts over the host factor onto each other and are weakly equivalent (`nontrivial-bernoulli-shifts-are-weakly-equivalent`; `gk-p-inf-wild`, `gk-p-inf-cost`);
3. not see only the Koopman representation, which is base-blind (Attempts of `bernoulli-rokhlin-maximal-on-fg-simple-kazhdan-groups`);
4. not be induced from a proper subgroup or a proper treed subrelation, because co-amenability collapses (Proposition 1.1, Sections 2.3 and 3);
5. not decompose along trees or cube complexes, because of FA and FW.

The one lower-bound functional here that needs no finite models, the f-invariant, meets conditions 1 and 2 and fails conditions 4 and 5. No functional satisfying all five is known.
