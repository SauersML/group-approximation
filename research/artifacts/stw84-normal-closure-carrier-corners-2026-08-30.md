# Audit: normal-closure form of augmentation carrier corners

Date: 2026-08-30

## Support computation

For positive projections `e_1,...,e_N`, the kernel of their positive average
is the intersection of their kernels.  Here `ker(e_R)` is the `R`-fixed
subspace in every representation.  Intersecting the fixed spaces for the
finite subgroups in the orbit window gives the fixed space of the finite
group they generate.  Therefore the spectral support of the averaged
carrier is exactly the complementary averaging projection `e_H`.

## Ideal computation

The ideal generated by `e_H` is generated by the relations
`lambda_h=1`, `h in H`.  Closing those relations under conjugation imposes
them on the normal closure `M` and gives exactly the kernel of
`C*(G)->C*(G/M)`.  Since `M<=K`, this is an ideal of the augmentation crossed
product.  The carrier projection is full in that ideal, so its corner has
the same nuclear dimension by Morita invariance.

## Consequence for the alternating shift

Every nontrivial finite subgroup of `Alt_fin(Z)` normally generates the
whole simple kernel.  Thus every nonzero carrier corner is Morita equivalent
to the full unresolved augmentation crossed product.  This invalidates the
hope that support-corner compression alone reduces the complexity of the
alternating-shift example.

The positive carrier still contains quantitative information discarded by
the support map.  A future banded-Fourier argument would have to use its
eigenvalue weights or work with completely positive cutdowns, rather than
appeal only to the hereditary support corner.
