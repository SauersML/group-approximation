# Composite scaling blocks and intrinsic lamp-kernel reconstruction

Date: 2026-08-30.

## Result

For every `m>=2`, the marked orbital block is the graph on
`(Z/mZ)^3` joining pairs with unimodular difference.  Its central
graph-Clifford group has RACG quotient.  Although a composite block is
infinite and its full stabilizer need not be Kazhdan, its clique-cube
stabilizers are finite-by-Kazhdan.  The lamp kernel is exactly the subgroup
generated by the finite radicals of all maximal property-`(T)` subgroups.

This gives the intrinsic formula

```text
N_m=<FinRad(H): H maximal property-(T) in E_m>.
```

It is functorial under arbitrary abstract isomorphisms and closes the
lamp-kernel characteristicity gate independently of the Hecke-modulus proof.

## Arithmetic of one block

Reduction of `SL_3(Z)e_1` modulo `m` is the set of unimodular columns.
Thus adjacency is equivalent to inequality after reduction in every
`F_p^3`, `p|m`.  For `p^e||m`, the primary factor is complete
`p^3`-partite with parts of size `p^(3(e-1))`.  The degree is the Jordan
totient `J_3(m)`, and the clique number is the cube of the least prime
divisor.

Killing the common sign in a block gives the RACG `W_(Gamma_m)`.  A clique
`C` lifts to a finite Clifford subgroup `Q_C` of order
`2^(|C|+1)`.

## Intrinsic recognition

The general block amalgam gives a Bass--Serre tree.  Property `(T)` fixes a
vertex; an affine-side fixed subgroup has zero height and lies in a telescope
edge.  On the block side, property `FW` fixes a Davis cube.  Its stabilizer
is `Q_C semidirect K_C`, where `K_C` is finite-index in the affine
Kazhdan base, and its finite radical is exactly `Q_C`.

For a maximal clique, the special subgroup `W_C` fixes only the cube center:
`link(C)` is empty and `N_W(W_C)=W_C`.  The corresponding subgroup also
fixes only its block vertex in the Bass--Serre tree; the exact intersection
of the block kernel with every adjacent edge stabilizer is `<w>`.
These two uniqueness statements make the clique stabilizer maximal among
property-`(T)` subgroups.  Every site belongs to a maximal clique, giving
the reverse containment in the intrinsic formula.

## Adversarial audit

An independent hostile audit found no fatal gap and required two points to be
made explicit in the proof:

1. the Davis fixed-set/normalizer argument for a maximal clique;
2. the exact intersection `P_m intersect lDl^(-1)=<w>` for every adjacent
   edge, rather than merely observing that one lamp is outside the displayed
   edge.

Both appear in the landed proof.  The audit also checked finite radicals of
finite-index affine subgroups, the height-zero telescope argument, all
composite CRT cases, and cross-family functoriality.
