# hl-nh-el3-compressor, 2026-09-14

Lane question (top survivor of `ideas-nh-round3`, e0481caf4): does `EL_3(F_q[x,y,z])` have a
trace-preserving matrix-ultraproduct model with a diffuse commutant summand carrying a proper
trace-preserving compressor endomorphism? A construction answers Question 3.4. An obstruction makes
the Kun--Thom wreath non-hyperlinear. Target node: `hyperlinear-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects`.

Verdict: **not decided.** The natural construction family, models built from finite quotients and
their Steinberg twists, carries no wall, and this is now a theorem. No obstruction for arbitrary
models was found.

## 1. The question restated

- **Models are of the actor, not the vertex.** A compressor `t` of `Γ = EL_3(F_q[x,y,z])` lives in
  `G = EL_3(F_q[x^(±1),y^(±1),z^(±1)]) ⋊ SL_3(Z)` (the Theorem E pair at `r = d = 3`). So "a model of
  `EL_3(F_q[x,y,z])` carrying a compressor" means a homomorphism `σ : G -> U(M)`,
  `M = prod_U M_n`.
- **The wall.** Put `A = σ(Γ)' ∩ M`. For a compressor `t` (`t Γ t^-1 <= Γ`), `θ_t = Ad σ(t)*` maps
  `A` into `A`. It is an injective, trace-preserving `*`-endomorphism with image
  `σ(t^-1 Γ t)' ∩ M`. `σ(t)` normalizes `A` exactly when `θ_t` is onto. Since `G` is generated by
  compressors, `NORM` at `σ` is surjectivity of `θ_t` for a generating set of compressors.
- **Wold form.** If `θ_t` is not onto, `A ⊋ θ_t(A) ⊋ θ_t^2(A) ⊋ ...`, the `θ_t^k(A)` are the
  commutants of the increasing groups `t^-k Γ t^k`, and `L²(A) ⊖ L²(θ_t A)` is a nonzero wandering
  subspace for the isometry `θ_t`. This is the Wold shift of `un-tracial-support`.
- **Where a wall can be.** By established sector nodes it is invisible on a Haagerup image
  (`haagerup-targets-carry-no-relative-wall`), on the discrete type I summand and the
  finite-dimensional isotypic part (`trace-preserving-endomorphism-fixes-discrete-type-i-summand`,
  `fd-isotypic-part-of-a-kazhdan-image-carries-no-wall`), and under unit-type rounding of the vertex
  (`unit-type-vertex-rounding-forces-compressor-commutant-rigidity`).
- **Decisive property.** A model `σ` of the Theorem E actor with `θ_t` not onto, for a trace-preserving
  `σ` into a Connes-embeddable target. It exists iff `NORM(hyperlinear)` fails at this pair.

## 2. Construction side: what finite constructions give

| family | vertex restriction | wall? | node |
|---|---|---|---|
| genuine representations of `G`, e.g. through congruence quotients | exact | no | recorded in `hyperlinear-group-carries-nontrivial-rigid-defect`, Attempts |
| genuine representations of `St_r(L) ⋊ SL_d(Z)`, Steinberg kernel two-norm trivial | may fail to round | **no (new)** | `steinberg-actor-models-carry-no-rigid-wall` |
| genuine representations of any cover with a Kazhdan lift of the vertex compressed by lifted compressors | may fail to round | **no (new)** | `kazhdan-cover-actor-models-normalize-vertex-commutants` |
| pulled back from a free cover | everything | undecided | hypothesis fails; this is where a witness must live |

- **Why the Steinberg row is the natural test at `r = 3`.** At `r >= 6`, `d = 3` the `K_2` symbol
  twists are killed by `SL_3(Z)` transport and property (T) of `Z^3 ⋊ SL_3(Z)`
  (`actor-extension-lifts-vertex-rounding-to-the-laurent-vertex`). At `r = 3` the Steinberg kernel is
  outside stability and nothing controls it. It is the obvious source of non-roundable vertex models.
- **Why it still carries no wall.** The image of `St_3(F_q[x,y,z])` in `St_3(L)` has property (T)
  (Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain--Kassabov, pinned below), maps onto the vertex, and is compressed by the
  nonnegative elementary substitutions. Property (T) identifies the ultraproduct commutant with the
  exact commutants `ρ_n(Γ̂)' ∩ M_n`. A lifted compressor conjugates `ρ_n(Γ̂)'` into itself at each
  finite stage, and equal dimensions force equality.
- **Diffuse summands are not the issue.** `prod_U` of growing exact commutants is diffuse, and
  compressors act on it as automorphisms. What a witness needs is not diffuseness but a model that
  does not come from genuine finite-dimensional representations of a compressor-compatible Kazhdan
  cover.

## 3. Obstruction side

- **Character rigidity is inert here.** For a trace-preserving `σ`, `τ∘σ|Γ = δ_e` for every model. So
  a classification of characters of `Γ` says nothing about which commutants occur. The commutant is
  seen by the conjugation representation `γ -> Ad σ(γ)` on `L²(M)`, whose matrix coefficients
  `τ(σ(γ) x σ(γ)* y*)` are not characters of `Γ`. No character-rigidity theorem for
  `EL_3(F_q[x,y,z])` was pinned, and none is used.
- **Property (T) alone does not give an obstruction.** It identifies commutants only when the model is
  pulled back from genuine representations of a Kazhdan cover. For an arbitrary model, the lift to a
  free cover has no property (T), and the spectral-gap rounding of `Ad σ` gives nothing beyond the
  recorded `un-tracial-support` firewall TS1.
- **II₁ calibration.** Genuine representations into II₁ factors do not normalize:
  `kun-thom-wreath-factor-is-exact-nonnormalized-commutant`. So any obstruction must use the finite
  matrix stages, as `un-tracial-support` §3 already requires.

## 4. Model tests of the new theorem

- **`N = 1`.** Reproduces the recorded genuine-representation normalization.
- **Free covers.** Every model is pulled back from a free cover and satisfies (KC). The preimage of the
  vertex is free, so no Kazhdan lift exists there and the theorem makes no claim. Content sits
  exactly in the Kazhdan hypothesis on the lift.
- **Lift condition.** For `Γ̂ = P^-1(Γ)` the compression `t Γ̂ t^-1 <= Γ̂` is automatic, since the
  kernel is normal, but `P^-1(Γ)` contains the kernel and need not have property (T). The Steinberg
  instance uses the smaller lift `image(St_r(R_+))` and checks compression directly.
- **Dimension step.** Replacing matrix stages by a II₁ factor breaks the equal-dimension argument, and
  the Kun--Thom wreath factor shows the conclusion then fails.

## 6. Exact gap

- **G1 (witness side).** A trace-preserving model of the Theorem E actor with `θ_t` not onto must:
  - avoid every established sector above;
  - have a vertex restriction that is not flexibly roundable;
  - not be pulled back, with a two-norm-trivial kernel, from genuine finite-dimensional
    representations of `St_r(L) ⋊ SL_d(Z)` or of any cover with a compressed Kazhdan lift of the
    vertex.

  No such model is known for any infinite higher-rank Kazhdan vertex.
- **G2 (obstruction side).** Extend §4 of `kazhdan-cover-actor-models-normalize-proof` from genuine
  representations of a cover to models that are only close to such pullbacks. The exact dimension
  count does not survive perturbation of the compressor unitaries, so this needs a quantitative
  replacement for commutant no-growth at the finite stages, which is the input
  `un-tracial-support` §3 specifies.
