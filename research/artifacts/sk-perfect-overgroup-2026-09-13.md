# sk-perfect-overgroup: derived-subgroup overgroups for amenable and sofic groups (2026-09-13)

Lane `sk-perfect-overgroup`, wave 8 (ROSTER-F).
- **Brief:** does every f.g. amenable (or sofic) group embed in a f.g. perfect amenable (or sofic) group? This is gate G1
  of `sk-sofic-embedding-a` (e9d3f05c2b) for simple Kazhdan linear-sofic and sofic envelopes.
- **Status:** everything below is unreviewed and queued with sk-verify-7.

## 0. Answer
- **What the envelopes need.** They do not need a perfect overgroup, only `Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ]` for an overgroup `Δ` in the same
  class: `perfect-groups-embed-in-crossed-product-elementary-groups` item 3 already puts `[Δ,Δ]` inside
  `EL_3(LC(Y,k) ⋊ Δ)`.
- **That weaker statement holds** for amenable, elementary amenable, sofic, torsion-free and solvable groups, through one
  explicit f.g. overgroup `C(Γ)`: `groups-embed-in-derived-subgroup-of-half-line-overgroup`.
- **Consequences:**
  - the embedding step of `amenable-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-linear-sofic` is repaired
    (`amenable-groups-embed-in-kazhdan-linear-sofic-via-half-line`);
  - gate G1 of `sofic-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-sofic-groups` is closed.
- **Not settled here:** whether every f.g. amenable group has a f.g. PERFECT amenable overgroup. The literal question of
  the brief stays open, but it is no longer on any route.

## 1. The construction
Let `Γ = ⟨S⟩`, `X = Γ × Z`, and use these permutations of `X`:
- `σ(x,m) = (x,m+1)`, the level shift;
- `h_γ`, left translation by `γ` on the half `m ≥ 0` and the identity below;
- `δ_0(γ)`, left translation by `γ` on level 0 only.

Put `C(Γ) = ⟨σ, h_s : s ∈ S⟩`.

Key identity: with products as compositions, `σ h_s σ^(-1)` translates by `s` on levels `≥ 1`, so

    [σ, h_s] = (translate by s on levels ≥ 1) ∘ (translate by s^(-1) on levels ≥ 0) = δ_0(s^(-1)).

So the level-0 copy `δ_0(Γ) ≅ Γ` is generated by commutators.

## 2. Structure and permanence
- `C(Γ) = E ⋊ ⟨σ⟩`, where `E` is the group of `Γ`-valued sequences trivial at `−∞` and eventually constant at `+∞`,
  acting levelwise.
  - `E` is generated by the conjugates `σ^n h_γ σ^(-n)` (translation on levels `≥ n`) through telescoping products.
  - `E = ⋃_M E_M` with `E_M ≅ Γ^(2M+1)`, the sequences trivial below `-M` and constant from `M` on.
- So `C(Γ)` belongs to any class closed under finite direct powers, directed unions and extensions by `Z`:
  - amenable;
  - elementary amenable;
  - sofic (Elek–Szabó Theorem 1, clauses 1 and 3; node `elek-szabo-sofic-permanence`);
  - torsion-free;
  - solvable, with derived length going up by at most one.
- `C(Γ)` is generated by `|S| + 1` elements and is infinite.
- Hyperlinear inputs very likely also give hyperlinear `C(Γ)`, through the analogous permanence results, but no import
  node for them was located, so this is not claimed.

## 3. What is lost
- **Residual finiteness.** For non-abelian `Γ`, `C(Γ)` is not residually finite.
  - Take `a, b` with `[a,b] ≠ e`, `a_0 = δ_0(a)`, `b_N = σ^N δ_0(b) σ^(-N)`.
  - In any finite quotient `F`, `σ^(|F|!)` dies, so `π([a_0, δ_0(b)]) = π([a_0, b_N]) = 1`.
  - But `[a_0, δ_0(b)] = δ_0([a,b]) ≠ 1`.
  - So residually finite and LEF envelopes keep the Ore overgroup of `rf-groups-lie-in-derived-subgroups-of-rf-groups`.
- **Perfectness.** `σ` survives in `C(Γ)^(ab)`, and iterating `C` never produces a perfect group.

## 4. LEF firewall: the naive truncation fails
- **The natural finite model.** Levels `Z/N`, with `h_γ` supported on `[0, N/2)`. This creates a second boundary at
  level `N/2`, which carries suffix products instead of prefix products.
- **A counterexample.** For non-commuting `a, b`, write `c_(n,γ)` for translation on levels `≥ n`. The word

      W = c_(0,a) ∘ c_(1,b) ∘ c_(0,a^(-1)) ∘ c_(1, a b^(-1) a^(-1))

  is trivial in `C(Γ)`:
  - level 0: `a a^(-1)`;
  - levels `≥ 1`: `a b a^(-1) · a b^(-1) a^(-1) = e`.

  In the truncated model, at level `N/2` only the two `c_(1,·)` factors act, and they give `b · a b^(-1) a^(-1) ≠ e`.
- **Conclusion.** The naive model is not a partial homomorphism. Whether `C(Γ)` is LEF for LEF `Γ` stays undecided; it
  does not matter for the amenable and sofic uses.

## 5. The repaired amenable envelope
`amenable-groups-embed-in-kazhdan-linear-sofic-via-half-line`. For f.g. amenable `Γ`:
1. take `Δ = C(Γ)`;
2. take a Gao–Jackson–Seward free minimal subflow `X ⊆ A^Δ`;
3. `R = LC(X,F_q) ⋊ Δ` is simple, f.g., with centre `F_q`;
4. `EL_3(R)/Z` is infinite, simple and Kazhdan;
5. it is `F_q`-linear sofic by the Foelner rank model;
6. `Γ ≅ δ_0(Γ) ≤ [Δ,Δ]` embeds through `diag(u_δ,1,1)`.

The inputs are exactly those of `amenable-perfect-groups-embed-in-kazhdan-linear-sofic-proof`, plus the overgroup.

## 6. The sofic target
For `sofic-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-sofic-groups`:
- **G1 closed:** `C(Γ)` is sofic and `Γ ≤ [C(Γ),C(Γ)]`.
- **What remains:**
  - (G2) soficity, in the Hamming metric, of a non-LEF envelope. The algebraic-target barrier
    `gap-sofic-approximations-are-local-embeddings` applies, and so does `rank-models-give-no-hamming-models-through-vector-actions`.
  - For non-amenable `Γ`, a measured free minimal subshift over `C(Γ)` (`sofic-groups-have-measured-free-minimal-subshifts`).

## 7. Firewalls respected
- `diag(a,a^(-1),1)` is never used as a homomorphism.
- The swap double `γ ↦ (γ,γ^(-1))` is not used. It is not a homomorphism for non-abelian `Γ`, and sk-verify-4 demoted it (3396ad816) .
- Coordinate copies in permutational wreath products are not used: `wreath-products-keep-the-abelianization-of-the-base`.
  The level-0 copy here is not a base coordinate of a transitive wreath product with a `Γ`-stabilizer; it comes from
  commutators with half-line translations, which lie outside the restricted base.
