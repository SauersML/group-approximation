# Adjoint regularization audit: regular RCC versus linear projective transfer

Date: 2026-09-16.

## Outcome

For a finitely presented hyperlinear group `A=<X|R>`, a subgroup `C`
generated by words `S`, and a word `h` with `A=<C,h>`, the following are
equivalent, with no perfectness and no spectral gap:

1. regular-trace relative-commutant collapse in matrix ultraproducts;
2. the all-assignment linear projective inequality
   `e_h <= L(q+delta)`;
3. relative-commutant collapse for all matrix-ultraproduct representations.

Applied to `SL_2(Z)<=SL_2(Z[1/p])` for any prime `p`, together with
`arithmetic-hnn-nonhyperlinearity-equals-global-collapse` and Smith
maximality, it shows that the rank-two instance of
`projective-commutant-transfer-for-arithmetic-pair` is exactly
nonhyperlinearity of `H=A *_C (C x Z)`.  Before this, the graph recorded
the finite-matrix inequality as equivalent only for `n>=3`, and only for
one diagonal denominator; the perfect-pair route could not reach rank two
because `SL_2(Z)` has abelianization `Z/12`.

Nothing is decided: every arrow is an equivalence.

## Where perfectness was used before, and what replaces it

`rf-perfect-pair-transfer-equivalence-proof` uses perfectness twice.

- **Target phase.**  From `e_h(rho_n,u_n)->0` it only obtains
  `u pi(h) u^* pi(h)^*=z` for a scalar `z`, and perfectness of `A` kills
  the character `chi(h)=z`.  Replacement: apply the inequality to
  `rho direct_sum rho` and `u direct_sum I`.  The target commutator becomes
  `W direct_sum I` with trace `(1+tr W)/2`, and trace-square energy
  `<=epsilon` forces `||W-I||_2^2<=4 epsilon`.  The identity block pins
  the phase.  Doubling costs nothing in the source energies, by
  `1-|(1+t)/2|^2<=(1-Re t)`, which is `|1-t|^2>=0`.
- **Source phases.**  A projective leak gives
  `u pi(s) u^*=lambda(s) pi(s)`, and perfectness of `C` kills `lambda`.
  Replacement: pass to `Ad(rho)=rho tensor conj(rho)` and
  `T=Ad(U)`.  Then `[T,Ad rho(w)]=Ad([U,rho(w)])`, and
  `||Ad(W)-I||_2^2=2-2|tr W|^2`.  So the ordinary commutator energy of the
  adjoint witness is exactly twice the projective energy of the original
  witness, at every word, including `h`.

The adjoint step also keeps the trace regular:
`tr(Ad rho(w))=|tr rho(w)|^2`.  Regularization before `Ad` (tensoring with
canonical microstates of `A`) makes `|tr rho(w)|` small at nontrivial
words and leaves every commutator trace unchanged, since
`[U tensor I,rho(w) tensor sigma(w)]=[U,rho(w)] tensor I`.

The same two tricks appear inside
`regularize-kazhdan-project-and-compactify-transfer` (steps `(KRT2)=>(KRT3)`
and `(KRT3)=>(KRT1)`), but there they are sandwiched around a Kazhdan
projection that needs property `(T)` of `C`.  The present route replaces
that projection by the linearization theorem
`projective-transfer-linear-bound-equals-no-ultraproduct-leak`, so it
produces some finite `L` rather than a prescribed slope, and applies to
`SL_2(Z)`, which lacks `(T)`.

## Step audit

1. *Failure of (AR2) gives a leak sequence.*  This is exactly `(PL2)` iff
   `(PL3)` for the same presentation, word set and target word; the cited
   claim is established.
2. *Hyperlinear microstates restricted to generators have vanishing
   presentation defect.*  From `||phi(gg')-phi(g)phi(g')||_2->0` one gets
   `||phi(1)-I||_2=||phi(1)^2-phi(1)||_2->0` and
   `phi(x^-1)~phi(x)^*`; telescoping along a fixed relator gives
   `delta->0`, and along a fixed word for `g` gives
   `rho(w)~phi(g)`.  For arithmetic `A`, zero-defect regular
   representations of separating congruence quotients may be used
   instead.
3. *Tensor bookkeeping.*  Defects add (`(AP4)`), commutator traces are
   unchanged, traces at nontrivial words are bounded by the microstate
   trace.
4. *Adjoint bookkeeping.*  Defect grows by at most `sqrt 2` (`(AP3)`),
   traces square in modulus, commutator norms are given by `(AP8)`.
5. *Ultraproduct.*  Relators evaluate to `1`, so a representation
   `pi:A->U(M)` is defined; its trace vanishes at nontrivial elements; the
   witness is in `pi(C)'` since `S` generates `C` and commutants of unitaries
   are subgroups.  RCC puts it in `pi(h)'`, contradicting `2epsilon`.
6. *Reverse direction.*  Lift `pi` and a unitary `u` of the relative
   commutant; double; apply the inequality; `(AP6)`; generation; span by
   unitaries.  Unitaries lift to unitary representing sequences by polar
   decomposition.

The most delicate bookkeeping is the matching of the claim's
`L(A)`-embedding formulation with regular-trace unitary representations
(extension through GNS uniqueness) and the claim that commutants of
`pi(C)` and `pi(L(C))` agree inside the ultraproduct.  Both are standard.

## Numerical check

`experiments/projective-commutant-transfer-for-arithm-2026-09-16/verify_adjoint_identities.py`
tests `(AP0)`--`(AP8)`, tensor invariance of commutators, and a twisted
`12x12` clock/shift pair on random Haar unitaries (200 trials, dimensions
2--5 tensored with 1--3, seed `20260916`).  All equalities hold to
`<=5e-15` and all inequalities have nonpositive excess.  The clock/shift
pair has source commutator the scalar `exp(2 pi i/12)`, trace-square
energy `0`, ordinary commutator norm `0.5176`, and adjoint commutator norm
`5e-16`: the adjoint removes the twist exactly.  The output is stored
next to the script as `output.txt`.

## Arithmetic inputs for rank two

- Finite presentation of `SL_2(Z[1/p])`: Ihara's amalgam
  `SL_2(Z) *_(Gamma_0(p)) SL_2(Z)`, as in Serre, *Trees*, Chapter II.
  This is standard; the book was not re-fetched in this session.
- Residual finiteness: reduction modulo primes `l!=p`, proved in the
  route.
- Maximality: `smith-prime-localization-maximality-and-word-transfer`
  `(SPL1)`, which cites Gelander--Meiri arXiv:1511.05767, Section 7, and
  Cornulier for `n=2`.

## What remains open

The common truth value.  In rank two the target, regular RCC at every
prime, unrestricted RCC, the linear inequality and nonhyperlinearity of
`H` are now a single open assertion.  Any proof of the target in rank two
must therefore prove nonhyperlinearity of an explicit finitely presented
group; any hyperlinear approximation of `H` refutes all of them.
