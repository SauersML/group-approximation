## 1. The normal form, Steps 1–2
- **Step 1.** Conjugating by `x` gives `x^k u x^{-k} = u + kl`, so `b_k = x^k B x^{-k}` inverts `u + 1 + kl`.
  - `C` is commutative and receives a surjection from `A`. The composite `A → C → A` is the identity, so `C ≅ A`.
  - `U = Σ C x^k`, and the map `U → D` preserves degree, so `U ≅ D`.
  - `D → R_l → R_l/(e) = U` is the identity.
- **Step 2.** `A/uA = Z_(l)`: the integers `≡ 1 (mod l)` are inverted, and `q^{-1} = q^{l−2}/q^{l−1}`.
  - `uD + Du = ⊕_k x^k((u − kl)A + uA)`, so `M = Z_(l) ⊕ ⊕_{k≠0} Z/l^{v(k)+1}`.
  - `f(u) − f(0) ∈ uA`, so `f(−kl) − f(0) ∈ kl·Z_(l)`. Hence the two actions on `N` agree.

## 2. The normal form, Steps 3–5 (the author's three points)
- **Associativity of `R'`.**
  - `V` is a `(D, Z_(l))`-bimodule, because `Du·A = Du` and the right action of `u` is 0. Likewise `W` is a
    `(Z_(l), D)`-bimodule.
  - `μ` is `D`-balanced by construction. It is also `Z_(l)`-bilinear, because the left and right actions agree on `N`
    and `T^0 = Z_(l)`. So the product is well defined on `⊗_{Z_(l)}`.
  - Associativity of triple products and of mixed products with `D` reduces to `μ(wd, v) = μ(w, dv)`.
- **`φ` is onto.** `e'e' = e'`, `ue' = e'u = 0`, and `e' x^k e' = v_0 ⊗ z_k ⊗ w_0`.
- **Well-definedness of `ψ_T`.**
  - `e` commutes with `A`, so `f ↦ fe` is a ring map `A → eR_le` that kills `u`. Hence `fe = θ(f(0))`.
  - `e x^k u = kl·e x^k`, and `ue = 0`, so `kl·(e x^k e) = 0`. Since `k/l^{v(k)}` is a unit on the corner, this gives
    `l^{m_k}·(e x^k e) = 0`.
  - The same identity gives centrality. For `f = (u+q)^{-1}`, the difference `θ(f(0)) e x^k e − e x^k e θ(f(0))` equals
    `θ(f(0) − f(−kl))·e x^k e`, which is `kl·(…)·e x^k e = 0`.
  - `eR_le` is generated by `θ(Z_(l))` and the `e x^k e`, because `e f x^k e = θ(f(0))·e x^k e`. So the universal
    property of `T_{Z_(l)}(N)` applies.
- **`ψ` is a ring map.** The one nontrivial check is `ψ_W(w_0 d)·ψ_V(d' v_0) = e dd' e = ψ_T([dd'])`, which uses
  `f e = θ(f(0))`. Then `ψφ = id` on the generators.
- **The multiplication rules.**
  - `μ(w_0 x^b, x^{a'} v_0) = [x^{b+a'}]`, which is 1 when `b + a' = 0`.
  - `f x^a e = x^a f(u − al) e = θ(f(−al))·(…)`, and `e x^b f = θ(f(bl))·e x^b`.
  - All of these are correct.
- **The ideal `(e)`.** It corresponds to `V ⊗ T ⊗ W`, and `eR_le = e(e)e = T`.

## 3. Residual finiteness
- **The modules `(r, N, g, E)`.**
  - The relation `xu = (u + l)x` holds at the wrap, because `u + l = lN ≡ 0 (mod l^r)` on `V_{N−1}` when
    `l^{r−1} | N`.
  - `x^N` acts as `g` on every copy. `x^b|_{V_{ρ'}} = g^{⌊b/N⌋}`, and `x^a|_{V_0} = g^{⌈a/N⌉}`, since the partial step
    `ρ > 0` crosses the wrap once.
  - So the block formula is `g^{⌈a/N⌉} E g^{k_1/N} E ⋯ E g^{⌊b/N⌋}`.
- **The density lemma.** The idempotent checks hold: `((1+s)/2)^2 = (1+s)/2` in odd characteristic, and
  `(1+s+s^2)^2 = 1+s+s^2` in `F_2[C_3]`.
  - Take `τ*` with the largest number of `ε`'s.
  - In its expansion, the reduced word with `g*` in every slot occurs exactly once, with coefficient `c^{s+1}`.
  - Choosing 1 in any slot merges syllables and leaves at most `s` letters from `C_m`. The tuple `(α, n, β)` can be
    read off the reduced form.
  - Gruenberg's theorem gives a finite quotient that is injective on the finite support, and the regular
    representation stays faithful.
- **Separation, case `δ = 0`.** `ν = min v_l(c_τ)` forces `l^ν | k_i` on `F_0`.
  - Take `r = ν+1` and `N = l^ν`.
  - Invertible matrices and idempotents lift from `F_l` to `Z/l^r`.
  - The terms outside `F_0` act as 0, since either `c_τ ∈ l^{ν+1}G_τ` or some `N ∤ k_i`.
  - `l^ν Y ≠ 0`, because `Y ≢ 0 (mod l)` by independence within each block.
  - `θ(c)` acts as `c mod l^r` on `V_0`, because `(u+q)^{-1} e ↦ q^{-1}E`.
- **Separation, case `δ ≠ 0`, index slip.**
  - The `k`-term of `δ` applied to the basis vector of `V_j` gives `f_k(lj)·e_{j−k}`.
  - So choose `j` with `f_k(lj) ≢ 0 (mod l^r)` and apply `δ` to `e_j`. As written, the proof uses `V_{t+k}` together
    with `f_k(lt)`.
  - The rest is correct: `N` exceeds the spread, so the terms for different `k` land in different copies.
