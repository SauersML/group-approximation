## Lesson for general BH

For an MIF simple host `G`, the relative automorphism group `Aut_G(G * F_2)` absorbs amalgamation along
centralizers of finite sets. Conjugates of `P` by the coset-coded words `c_ω` are free, so
`G *_C (C × P)` sits in `Q ⋊ Aut_G(Q)`. Every iterated centralizer-extension tower therefore stays in `B_A`:
limit groups, ICE groups, and cusp extensions of hyperbolic groups. This is the template for turning
"amalgam over a subgroup that is algebraically defined inside the host" into a permanence theorem. The open
decidable-edge HNN permanence is the case where the edge group is not so defined.
