# Printed open questions near Boone–Higman in 2015–2026 papers (lane bh-openq-papers)

Scope: questions, problems and conjectures printed in research papers on Boone–Higman
and finitely presented simple groups, and on Thompson-like groups, Brin–Thompson
and twisted Brin–Thompson groups, Röver–Nekrashevych groups, topological full
groups, type (A) actions, germ extensions and word-problem complexity.
Complements the lists already on main:
- `research/artifacts/gq-bh-bh-lit-survey.md`, §3 (BBMZ survey, BBMZ-hyperbolic, Zaremsky
  2405.18354, BFFHZ, FFKLZ, FWZ, BHM, Kourovka 14.10);
- `research/artifacts/gq-bh-bh-bbmz-problems-status.md`;
- the zp-* Zaremsky problem-list nodes.

Questions already recorded there are not repeated here.

**Method.** arXiv search API; TeX e-prints fetched on MSI
(`/projects/standard/hsiehph/sauer354/gqsrc/bh-openq/`); `\begin{question|problem|conjecture}`
environments and "we do not know / remains open / natural to ask" passages
extracted and read. Wording below is verbatim from the TeX, quoted with its TeX line.
Papers read with no printed open question in scope:

## D. Solving pass (2026-09-18, same lane)

1. **The clopen-orbit wreath lemma** (`wreaths-over-clopen-stabilizer-cosets-lie-in-b-a`).
   - *Setting.* D is a finitely presented full clopen-transitive Cantor host, G ≤ D, and
     H ≤ G contains Stab_G(U) with finite index for some proper nonempty clopen U.
   - *Conclusion.* L ≀_{G/H} G ∈ B_A for every L ∈ B_A.
   - *Proof idea.* Push the lamp at gH onto the finite, pairwise disjoint set gH·U of
     clopens, then apply type (A) wreath closure for the clopen action.
   - *Scope.* No hypothesis on G itself.
2. **BH for the ADOT group** (`lamp-wreaths-over-psl2-tree-vertices-lie-in-b-a`).
   - *Statement.* For m odd, L ≀_{G/H} G ∈ B_A with G = PSL_2(Z[1/2m]) and
     H = PSL_2(Z[1/m]), so X is one type of vertices of the 2-adic tree.
   - *Host.* The Röver–Nekrashevych group V_2(Γ_2(m)) of the Iwahori group; the stabilizer
     of the ball Z_2 has index 3 in H.
   - *Answers.* The Boone–Higman form of the 2609.01868 question. Their self-similar
     question stays open.
3. **Main's first test case** (`lamp-wreaths-over-cyclic-cosets-of-f2-lie-in-b-a`).
   - *Statement.* Z ≀_{F_2/⟨a⟩} F_2 ∈ B_A.
   - *Proof idea.* A ping-pong copy of F_2 in V in which ⟨a⟩ is the exact stabilizer of a
     clopen set.
4. **Both test cases of the corrected wreath question are now proved.**
   - `pbh-closed-under-permutational-wreaths-with-infinite-stabilizers` asserted closure over
     every countable G-set. Lane bh-free-10 refuted it independently and first (8ccede47f,
     `pbh-wreath-closure-fails-over-undecidable-stabilizers`, via Mihailova's subgroup).
   - The corrected open form is `pbh-closed-under-decidable-permutational-wreaths`.
   - Items 2 and 3 settle both test cases it lists. This lane had drafted the same refutation
     over F_k/N and dropped it as a duplicate.
5. **A non-residually-finite double** (`double-of-psl2-z-half-along-psl2-z-lies-in-b-a`).
   - Item 2 with Z/2 lamps, fed into bh-free-10's criterion `free-permutational-products-preserve-pbh`,
     gives W *_C (C × K) ∈ B_A for W = PSL_2(Z[1/2m]) and C = PSL_2(Z[1/m]).
   - Hence the double PSL_2(Z[1/2]) *_{PSL_2(Z)} PSL_2(Z[1/2]) ∈ B_A. It is finitely presented and
     not residually finite, since PSL_2(Z) is profinitely dense in PSL_2(Z[1/2]).
6. **Lodha's question** (`fp-infinite-simple-group-not-two-generated`) was considered first, as
   suggested; lane remark, not landed as a node.
   - *Rank bounds that die.* Simple infinite groups have no finite quotients and no
     nontrivial linear representations (Malcev). So quotient, profinite and dimension-count
     bounds on d(G) all die.
   - *The one general tool left.* d(G) ≥ 1 + b_1^(2)(G). That needs a finitely presented simple
     group with b_1^(2) > 1.
   - *Why the known examples fail.* Every known finitely presented simple group has
     b_1^(2) = 0: Thompson-like groups are generated by chains of commuting infinite
     elements, Burger–Mozes lattices concentrate in degree 2, and Kac–Moody lattices have (T).
   - Judged L; not attempted further.
