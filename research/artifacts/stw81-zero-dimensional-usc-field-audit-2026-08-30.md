# Audit: arbitrary upper-semicontinuous fields over zero-dimensional bases

Date: 2026-08-30

Claim: `stw81-zero-dimensional-usc-fibre-formula`.

## Dimension formula

1. A finite set is reduced to a compact-open central corner.  The
   compact-open set contains the support of an ordinary compactly supported
   scalar cutoff, so the replacement error is controlled without assuming
   compactness of `X`.

2. Each fibre approximation has a c.p.c. return map split into `d+1`
   c.p.c. order-zero colours.  The order-zero cone correspondence and
   projectivity of cones over finite-dimensional algebras give exact global
   lifts through `A->A_x`.

3. The sum of the lifted colours need not be contractive globally.  At its
   anchor its norm is at most one; upper semicontinuity gives the uniform
   local bound `1+eta`.  The approximation errors are controlled by the
   same strict-sublevel argument.

4. A finite open cover of a compact zero-dimensional set admits a finite
   disjoint compact-open partition subordinate to it.  Characteristic
   functions are central projections, so equal-colour maps on distinct
   pieces have exactly orthogonal ranges.

5. Scaling every return colour by `(1+eta)^(-1)` makes the total return map
   c.p.c.  The scaling error is bounded by `eta max(1,||a||)` and can be
   chosen independently after the finite set is fixed.

6. Fibrewise norm control promotes to norm control in `A`; quotient
   monotonicity under evaluation gives the opposite inequality.  The proof
   uses only upper semicontinuity.

## Simple-fibre shadow conclusion

7. A quotient fibre is a quotient of a simple fibre and hence is zero or
   the whole fibre.  A separable hereditary subalgebra is generated by a
   strictly positive element, so its fibre is the corresponding hereditary
   subalgebra of the quotient fibre.

8. A nonzero commutative hereditary subalgebra of a simple fibre is full,
   forcing the fibre to be elementary.  Inside `K(H)`, commutativity forces
   the supporting Hilbert subspace to have dimension at most one.

9. The spectrum map of the commutative hereditary algebra into `X` is
   therefore injective.  Global properness is not assumed: on each compact
   neighborhood it is a compact-to-Hausdorff embedding into a
   zero-dimensional compact subspace.  This proves local, hence global,
   zero-dimensionality of the second-countable locally compact spectrum.

## Scope

No local triviality, continuous-field condition, fibre unitality,
simplicity, or uniform fibre model is used for the dimension formula.
Simplicity is invoked only for the trichotomy and hereditary-shadow
consequence.

Outcome: **PASS**.
