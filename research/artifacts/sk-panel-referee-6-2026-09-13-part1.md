## 2. Blocking or near-blocking

### 2.1 GAP: `EL_3(F)` is not generated by the images of the note's generators (l.147–164)
The text:
- l.147: "Let $F=\F_2\langle t_+,t_-,t_a:a\in A\rangle$ be the free algebra";
- l.153–155: "So $G$ and $\EL_3(M_{N_\ell}(\F_2))=\SL_{3N_\ell}(\F_2)$ are quotients of $\EL_3(F)$ with compatible generators";
- l.163–164: "Since $\EL_3(F)$ has property~\textup{(T)}, their Cayley graphs form a family of expanders~\cite{Kassabov}".

The generators of G at l.109–110 are `e_ij(s)` with `s ∈ {u^{±1}} ∪ {e_a}`. The redundant `s = 1` was dropped in rev1, "as $1=\sum_ae_a$". Their lifts `e_ij(t_±)`, `e_ij(t_a)` do NOT generate `EL_3(F)`:
- **Proof.** The unital ring map `ε: F → F_2` that kills every `t` sends each lifted generator to `I_3`, but `e_12(1)` to `e_12(1) ≠ I_3`. So the lifts generate a subgroup of `ker(EL_3(F) → SL_3(F_2))`.
- **What the argument needs.** A Kazhdan constant `κ(EL_3(F), Σ)` for a finite GENERATING set Σ. EJZ's explicit bound uses `x_0 = 1` (sk-review-2 part 2, verbatim: "We fix a generating set $\{x_0,x_1,\ldots, x_d\}$ for $R$, where $x_0=1$").
- **Repair.** Take `Σ = {e_ij(t) : t ∈ {1, t_±, t_a}}`. Its images generate G and `SL_{3N_ℓ}(F_2)`. In both quotients `e_ij(1) = ∏_a e_ij(e_a)` (resp. `∏_a e_ij(D_ℓ(e_a))`, since `Σ_a D_ℓ(e_a) = I`).
- **Transfer.** If every element of Σ is a word of length at most m in a set S, then `κ(Γ,S) ≥ κ(Γ,Σ)/m`. So the expander property also holds for the note's own generators, with m = |A|.
- **Status.** Checked by me. The statement of Theorem 1 ("with respect to the images of a fixed generating set") is unaffected.

### 2.2 Quantifiers in the marked limit (l.157–162)
"for large $\ell$ the word is trivial in $G$ if and only if it is trivial in $\SL_{3N_\ell}(\F_2)$" is stated per word. Marked convergence needs: for every L, all words of length at most L agree, for all large ℓ. That follows because there are finitely many such words, but a referee will ask for it. The space of marked groups is also used without a definition or a reference (§3, items 1–2).

### 2.3 Uncredited principle (l.163–164)
"(T) of a group ⇒ Cayley graphs of its finite quotients with the images of a fixed generating set are expanders" is Margulis's observation (1973). Its standard textbook source is Lubotzky's book. The note cites only Kassabov, who proved the specific theorem that `SL_n(F_q)`, n ≥ 3, form expanders with bounded generating sets. Credit both.

## 4. What would make it astonishing (approximation and expander view)
- **A1. The LEF characterization in the abstract.** rev3's §2 has two PASSes (sk-verify-3 part6, sk-lef-embedding-c): a f.g. group is LEF iff it is a subgroup of an infinite f.g. simple Kazhdan LEF group. For approximation readers this is the sentence they will quote. Put it in the abstract, stated exactly like that.
- **A2. A sharp sofic question**, replacing or preceding the rigidity question in §3. Once A1 is in, the natural sequel is the sofic analogue, and it has a crisp reason to be hard. The node `sofic-universal-envelope-forces-non-lef-simple-kazhdan` is unreviewed, but its argument is two standard facts:
  - LEF passes to subgroups;
  - finitely presented LEF groups are residually finite (Vershik–Gordon).

  So a host for `BS(2,3)` would be a sofic infinite simple Kazhdan group that is not LEF, and no such group is known. The soficity of `BS(2,3)` still needs a citation; sk-lit-locators should confirm one before the sentence lands.
- **A3. The Kazhdan constant depends only on |A|** (one clause).
  - EJZ's explicit bound (sk-review-2 part 2, PASS) depends on n and on the number d = |A|+2 of ring generators only.
  - So all G_X over a fixed alphabet, and all their finite models, share one Kazhdan constant. The expander family is therefore uniform across every infinite minimal subshift over A.
  - This is true, cheap (+1 line), and useful to expander readers. Cite `\cite[\S6]{EJZ}` only after sk-lit-locators confirms the journal section number; it is checked in arXiv v2 only.
- **Do not add:**
  - P-stability. G_X is not permutation stable by Becker–Lubotzky (J. Funct. Anal. 278 (2020) 108298, doi:10.1016/j.jfa.2019.108298, verified), but it answers no printed question.
  - The strong-convergence firewall of sk-cstar-mf: unreviewed.
  - MF of C*_r(G_X): open.
  - Numerical spectral gaps: they stay in Cairn.
