# Novelty check: Zaremsky Problem 1.14, BNS invariant of Röver–Nekrashevych groups (2026-09-13)

**Claims checked.**
- (i) Σ¹(V_d(G)) is the whole character sphere for every finitely generated self-similar G.
- (ii) Hom(V_d(G), R) ≅ {χ : Σ_x χ(g|_x) = χ(g)}.

## Verdict

1. **(i) Σ¹(V_d(G)) = S(V_d(G)): not found in print or on arXiv, within the sources below.**
   - No source computes any BNS or BNSR invariant of a Röver–Nekrashevych group.
   - Zaremsky's Σ computations cover other groups: Thompson's F (with Witzel), F_{n,∞} and Houghton groups
     (lower bounds), the Stein group F_{2,3} (with Spahn), the Lodha–Moore groups (with Lodha), pure braid
     groups, the McCool groups (with Ershov), and symmetric automorphism groups.
   - Relay as "answers the Σ¹ part of Problem 1.14 within the sources checked". The higher invariants Σ^m,
     m ≥ 2, remain open for RN groups of type F_m.
2. **(ii) The character description is essentially prior.** It is the real form of the known
   abelianization of V_d(G):
   - Zaremsky, arXiv:2405.09722v1 (HTML): "The abelianization of any Röver–Nekrashevych group V_d(G) is
     given by a straightforward procedure, described for example in [Nek18, Theorem 4.8]." Per that
     summary, one abelianizes G and imposes ḡ = ḡ_1 + ⋯ + ḡ_d, plus a signature term when d is odd.
   - Over R the signature term dies, which gives (ii).
   - [Nek18] = V. Nekrashevych, *Finitely presented groups associated with expanding maps*, LMS Lecture
     Note Ser. 444 (2018), arXiv:1312.5654.
   - Skipper–Witzel–Zaremsky, arXiv:1712.05361v3, p. 7: "See [Nek04, Theorem 9.14] for a description of
     the abelianization and commutator subgroup of V_d(G)."
   - Same paper, p. 8, proof of Theorem 3.3: ῑ_u(g) = ῑ_u(ρ(g)) + Σ_{i=1}^{d} ῑ_{ui}(g_i), and
     ι_u(g), ι_v(g) are conjugate by an element of V_d. That is exactly the recursion behind (ii).
   Credit (ii) to Nekrashevych (2004 Theorem 9.14; 2018 Theorem 4.8) and do not present it as new.
