# A finitely presented shell envelope has a finitely presented germ group, 2026-09-18

Lane `c-shenv` (swarm-0917, role transplanter, family reframing: replace
the group by its germ groupoid over the singular orbit). Target hole
`decidable-inputs-have-fp-shell-envelopes`, OPEN.

## Verdict

- **New necessary condition on E_nu itself.** If E_nu is finitely presented,
  then its isotropy germ group Q at p is finitely presented, with an
  explicit presentation `<tau, S | W_R>` read off from any finite presentation
  of E_nu (claim `shell-envelope-fp-forces-fp-germ-group`).
- **All germ gates now bind the envelope route.** Through the established
  `shell-germ-group-has-index-two-fiber-product` and
  `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate`, a successful enumeration for
  `decidable-inputs-have-fp-shell-envelopes` must make F_nu = R_nu x_Z R_nu and
  R_nu finitely presented and pass the ascending HNN gate. Before this, those
  tests were known necessary only for the stronger three-stabilizer
  hypothesis; the claim text of the target said explicitly that they "have not
  been shown necessary for finite presentation of E_nu alone".
- **The explicit perfect benchmark is refuted.** The arithmetic A_5*A_5
  enumeration has a non-finitely-presented near group
  (`arithmetic-a5-near-group-is-not-finitely-presented`), so its shell envelope
  is NOT finitely presented (claim `a5-arithmetic-shell-envelope-is-not-finitely-presented`).
  This settles, negatively, the OPEN benchmark
  `a5-arithmetic-shell-envelope-is-finitely-presented`. The same argument kills
  the square-spiral Z^2 envelope, by `square-spiral-z2-near-shift-group-is-not-finitely-presented`.
- **Converse direction not touched.** Q finitely presented does not give E_nu
  finitely presented. The lift from Q to A_1 (neighbourhood kernel K not finitely
  generated) and from A_1 to E_nu remain open.

The live dead end `c-shstab` ('germ complex converse dies at edges', 2026-09-18)
tried to force A_1 of type FP_2 from E_nu finitely presented through the
Belk-Hyde-Matucci complex and died at edge stabilizers. The present argument
does not pass through any complex of stabilizers or through A_1. It works in the
germ groupoid, where the neighbourhood kernel disappears for a geometric reason
(disjoint-support commutation), so the circularity at edge stabilizers never
arises.

## Setting

Notation from `shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`. P is
infinite and finitely generated by a finite set S, nu: P -> N a bijection,
C = {0,1}^N, p = 0^infinity, shells C_n = 0^n 1 C, hat(g) permutes the even
shells by rho_g and fixes p, and E = E_nu = <V, hat(P)>. Every element of E has
finitely many singular points, all in Omega = V.p, and E preserves Omega.
Q = (E)_p is the isotropy germ group at p; it is generated by the germs of
rho(S) and tau, where (V)_p = <tau>.

Germ groupoids. Let G_E be the groupoid of germs of elements of E at points of
Omega: arrows [e]_x : x -> e(x). Let G_V be the subgroupoid of germs of V. It is
transitive on Omega (V acts transitively on Omega) and its isotropy at p is
<tau> = Z (prefix replacements 0^a -> 0^b fixing p have germ tau^(b-a)).

Two facts about letters:

- (L1) A generator of V has germ in G_V at every point of Omega.
- (L2) hat(s) has germ in G_V at every point y != p: y lies in some shell C_n
  and hat(s) acts near y by the prefix replacement 0^n 1 -> 0^(rho_s(n)) 1.

## Theorem

If E_nu is finitely presented then Q is finitely presented. More precisely, fix
a finite generating set Y of V and put X = Y u hat(S). For any finite set R of
relators with E = <X | R>, there is a finite set W_R of words in tau and S,
computable from R and finitely many values of nu, with

    Q = <tau, S | W_R>.

## Proof

**Step 1. The presented groupoid.** Let Pi be the groupoid with object set Omega
obtained from G_V by adjoining one free loop sigma_s at p for each s in S. Its
isotropy at p is Z * F(S) = <tau> * F(S) (a connected groupoid with vertex group
Z, amalgamated over the discrete groupoid with a free groupoid of loops at one
object). For a finite set W of loops at p, Pi/W denotes the quotient by the
normal closure of W; its isotropy at p is (Z * F(S))/<<W>>.

**Step 2. The Schreier complex.** Let Y_R be the quotient of the Cayley 2-complex
of <X | R> by the left action of St_E(p). It is simply connected upstairs and the
action is free, so pi_1(Y_R, p) = St_E(p). Its vertices are the points of the
orbit E.p = Omega. An edge labelled a joins x to a(x) (up to orientation
conventions), and each relator r has one 2-cell based at every vertex.

Define Phi on edges. The edge of a at x goes to sigma_s if a = hat(s) and x = p.
Otherwise it goes to the actual germ [a]_x in G_V, which exists by (L1) and (L2).
This is a morphism from the free groupoid on the 1-skeleton to Pi.

**Step 3. Relator cells give finitely many loops at p.** Take a relator r at a
vertex x. Follow its trajectory letter by letter. If no hat letter is applied at
the point p, Phi of the boundary is the composite of actual germs, which is the
germ of r at x. That germ is the identity, since r = 1 in E.

Otherwise, split the trajectory at the steps where a hat letter acts at p.
Between two such steps the composite of G_V arrows is a loop at p in G_V, hence
a power tau^k. The segment from x to the first visit of p is an arrow gamma in
G_V. So Phi(boundary) = gamma^(-1) w_(r,x) gamma, where w_(r,x) is a word in
tau^(+-1) and sigma_s^(+-1).

For a fixed r, the trajectory from x visits p just before the j-th letter
exactly when x = (suffix of r of length j)^(-1)(p). There is one such x for each
hat position, so only finitely many pairs (r, x) occur. Let W_R be the finite set
of these words w_(r,x). The actual germ of w_(r,x) in Q is conjugate to the germ
of r at x, which is trivial. So Q is a quotient of Q_R := (Z * F(S))/<<W_R>>
by tau -> tau, s -> germ of hat(s). Since Phi kills every 2-cell, it induces a
homomorphism

    Phi: St_E(p) = pi_1(Y_R, p) -> Q_R.

Composed with Q_R -> Q, it is the germ map at p: this holds edge by edge.
Phi is onto: sigma_s = Phi(hat(s)), and tau = Phi(t) for any t in V fixing p
with germ tau, because (L1) applies at every point of the trajectory of t.

**Step 4. The neighbourhood kernel dies (the new step).** Let L be the kernel of
the germ map St_E(p) -> Q. Take l in L, so l is the identity on some clopen
neighbourhood U of p, and choose any word w in X representing l. The set

    B = {(suffix of w of length j)^(-1)(p) : the j-th letter of w is a hat letter}

is finite. U n Omega is infinite, so choose p' in U n Omega \ (B u {p}) and c in V
supported in U with c(p) = p'. It exists: take disjoint small cones around p and
p' inside U and a prefix swap. Since c is supported in U and l is the identity on
U, the two elements have disjoint supports, so they commute and l = c^(-1) l c.

Represent l by the word c^(-1) w c, with c spelled in Y. Its trajectory from p
first moves p to p' by V letters, then runs w from p', which never applies a hat
letter at p by the choice of p' outside B, and ends at l(p') = p'. It then returns
to p by V letters. By (L1) and (L2), every edge on this loop is sent to its actual
G_V germ. So Phi(l) is the image in Pi of the composite of those germs in G_V.
That composite is the germ of l at p, which is the identity. Hence L <= ker Phi.

**Step 5. Conclusion.** Let q in Q_R map to 1 in Q. Write q = Phi(g) with g in
St_E(p). Then the germ of g at p is trivial, so g lies in L and q = Phi(g) = 1.
Thus Q_R -> Q is an isomorphism and Q = <tau, S | W_R> is finitely presented.
QED.

## Corollaries

1. **Necessary conditions for the target.** If E_nu is finitely presented, then
   F_nu = R_nu x_Z R_nu and R_nu are finitely presented, by
   `shell-germ-group-has-index-two-fiber-product`. The ascending HNN condition of
   `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate` also holds. By
   `accessible-shell-inputs-admit-no-shift-normalizing-enumeration`, its window
   is N >= 1 for every finitely presented input that is not virtually cyclic.
   Thus the decomposition of the envelope hole becomes

       E_nu fp  ==>  [R_nu fp]  and  [ascending gate with window N >= 1]
                ==>  (converse open) nothing.

   Each bracket can fail on its own, and both have already failed for explicit
   enumerations.
2. **A5 benchmark refuted.** For the bijective-base A_5*A_5 enumeration, R_nu is
   not finitely presented, so E_nu is not finitely presented.
3. **Square spiral.** For the square-spiral enumeration of Z^2, R_nu is not
   finitely presented, so E_nu is not finitely presented.
4. **Second proof of the free-shift obstruction, when applicable.** If R_nu = P * Z
   and the fiber product (P*Z) x_Z (P*Z) is not finitely presented, then E_nu is
   not finitely presented. This is only a remark: the fibre-product
   non-finite-presentation is not proved here, and the established claim
   `free-shift-shell-envelopes-are-not-finitely-presented` has its own proof.

## Where the method stops (for the next worker)

- The argument is a germ-groupoid version of "relators are local". It says
  nothing about the neighbourhood kernel K inside A_1. So it cannot upgrade to
  E_nu fp ==> A_1 fp. That would need finitely many relations normally generating
  the relations between K and lifts of germs, and K is not finitely generated.
- General form, recorded but not claimed: let B <= E <= Homeo(X) with E finitely
  presented, every generator having finitely many points where its germ is not a
  B-germ, E preserving the B-orbit of p, and B having, inside every neighbourhood
  of p, elements supported there that move p to infinitely many points. Then
  (E)_p is a quotient of (B)_p * F_k by finitely many relators. So (E)_p is
  finitely presented whenever (B)_p is. This is a partial converse to hypothesis
  (3) of Belk-Hyde-Matucci Corollary 2.10 (arXiv:2407.03149).
