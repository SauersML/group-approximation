# Audit: unbounded Cuntz-semigroup perforation forced by a finite CAR projection

Date: 2026-08-30

## Result checked

If a simple unital C\*-algebra `B` contains both an infinite projection and
a unital CAR algebra with a nonzero projection `p` finite in `B`, then for
every `n>=1` there are a finite CAR subprojection `q_n<=p` and an integer
`m_n>2^n` satisfying

```text
(m_n+1)[1_B]=m_n[q_n]=[1_B],       [1_B] not<=[q_n].
```

Thus `Cu(B)` has almost-unperforation failures with coefficients tending to
infinity.

## Dyadic projection check

A projection in the CAR algebra is unitarily conjugate to a projection in a
finite tensor stage: approximate it by that stage within distance less than
one and apply the close-projections unitary lemma.  Tensoring the resulting
stage projection with `M_(2^n)` splits it into `2^n` mutually equivalent
subprojections, and conjugating back gives actual subprojections of the
original `p`.  Choosing one gives `q_n<=p` with `[p]=2^n[q_n]`.  No
divisibility assertion about arbitrary elements of `Cu(B)` is being used.
Since `q_n` is a subprojection of the finite projection `p`, it remains
finite in the ambient algebra.

## Fullness and finite comparison multiplier

Every nonzero `q_n` is full in the simple algebra `B`.  In `Cu(B)`, this says
that the ideal generated by `x_n=[q_n]` contains `u=[1_B]`, or
`u<=sup_k kx_n`.  The class of a projection is compact, so `u<=m x_n` for
some finite `m`.  Taking the least such integer is therefore legitimate.

The infinite projection makes the unit infinite, and simplicity makes the
unit properly infinite.  Consequently `ku=u` for every `k>=1`, and `u` is
the largest Cuntz class.  It follows that the least multiplier satisfies
`m_nx_n=u`, not merely `u<=m_nx_n`.

## Strict lower bound and perforation check

The CAR equation `[p]=2^n x_n` forces `m_n>2^n`.  Otherwise
`u=m_nx_n<=2^nx_n=[p]<=u`, so the finite projection `p` would be Cuntz
equivalent to the properly infinite unit.  The same projection-equivalence
argument proves `u not<=x_n`.  Hence

```text
(m_n+1)u=u=m_nx_n,        u not<=x_n,
```

which matches the definition of failure of almost unperforation with
`x=u`, `y=x_n`, and coefficient `m_n`.

## Relation to known and repository results

Rordam's mixed finite/infinite example already records that its projection
semigroup fails weak unperforation; see
[*A simple C\*-algebra with a finite and an infinite projection*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0205292).
The present statement does not claim priority over that general phenomenon.
Its new repository content is the quantitative CAR refinement: dyadic
halving of one finite projection produces an explicit sequence of
almost-unperforation failures whose coefficients exceed `2^n`.

This is stronger than `stw99-lxvi-z-stability-forbids-mixed-projections` as
a necessary condition on an LXVI witness: the earlier route invokes almost
unperforation supplied by Z-stability, whereas this route directly proves
unbounded perforation without assuming regularity.  It is also different
from `stw99-lxvi-car-corner-forces-unbounded-stability-indices`, which tracks
finiteness of matrix-amplified projection units rather than the intrinsic
order relation in `Cu(B)`.

## Limitation

This is a necessary obstruction, not a construction or a nonexistence proof
for Problem LXVI.  It does not show that an algebra with highly perforated
`Cu` can contain the required CAR copy, and it uses the existence of an
actual finite projection in that copy rather than only K-theoretic dyadic
divisibility.
