# AF killing embeddings, projection traces, and the uniqueness hypothesis of relative Lemma B (2026-09-13)

Lane `ex3-bk-ideal-equivariant`.  Target: the ideal-equivariant realisation of
`bk-relative-lemma-b` (Attempts, last bullet) and item 4 of
`bk-relative-boundary-descends-or-is-positively-detected`.  Result: a necessary
trace condition for killing a K_0-subgroup by an embedding into an AF algebra,
proof that stable finiteness always supplies it, and the consequence that the
uniqueness hypothesis (3) of relative Lemma B rules out AF targets.

## 0. Conventions

- An l.s.c. trace `σ : A_+ -> [0,∞]` is additive, positively homogeneous,
  `σ(x*x) = σ(xx*)`, lower semicontinuous.  Its *finiteness ideal* `I_σ` is the
  closed ideal generated by `{a ≥ 0 : σ(a) < ∞}`.  The span `m_σ` of that set is a
  dense *-ideal of `I_σ`, so it contains `Ped(I_σ)`; `σ|_{I_σ}` is densely finite
  and induces `σ^ : K_0(I_σ) -> R`, natural for *-homomorphisms carrying one
  finiteness ideal into another.  Traces on `M_n(A)` are `Tr ⊗ σ`.
- **Definition.**  `σ` *annihilates* `G ⊆ K_0(A)` *on its finiteness ideal* if
  `σ^` vanishes on `ι_σ^{-1}(G)`, where `ι_σ : K_0(I_σ) -> K_0(A)`.  Since
  `ker ι_σ = ∂(K_1(A/I_σ)) ⊆ ι_σ^{-1}(G)`, this includes vanishing on the inner
  boundary (residual condition (i) of the dichotomy artifact), so the pairing of
  `σ` with `G ∩ im ι_σ` is well defined.
- **Extension by +∞.**  If `ρ` is a densely finite l.s.c. trace on a closed ideal
  `J ⊴ D`, put `ρ^∞(x) = ρ(x)` for `x ∈ J_+` and `+∞` otherwise.  Since `J` is
  hereditary (`x + y ∈ J` iff `x, y ∈ J`), `x*x ∈ J` iff `xx* ∈ J`, and the
  sublevel sets `{x ∈ J_+ : ρ(x) ≤ c}` are closed in `D_+`, `ρ^∞` is an l.s.c.
  trace on `D` with finiteness ideal exactly `J`.
- **Corner traces.**  A tracial state `t` on a full corner `C = P M_n(J) P` of a
  separable `M_n(J)` extends to a densely finite l.s.c. trace `ρ` on `M_n(J)` with
  `ρ|_C = t` and `ρ^ = t^` under `K_0(C) ≅ K_0(J)` (Brown's stable isomorphism
  `M_n(J) ⊗ K ≅ C ⊗ K`, pulling back `t ⊗ Tr`; standard).

## 1. Theorem A (AF killing forces projection-normalised annihilating traces)

**Theorem A.**  Let `A` be a C*-algebra, `G ⊆ K_0(A)` a subgroup, `B` an AF
algebra and `φ : A -> B` a *-homomorphism with `φ_*(G) = 0`.  For every
projection `P ∈ M_n(A)` with `φ(P) ≠ 0` there is an l.s.c. trace `σ` on `A` with
`σ(P) = 1` that annihilates `G` on its finiteness ideal.

*Proof.*  (1) `Q := φ_n(P)` is a nonzero projection of `M_n(B)`.  Let `J_Q ⊴ B`
be the closed ideal generated by the entries of `Q`, so `M_n(J_Q)` is the ideal
of `M_n(B)` generated by `Q` and `Q` is full in it.  The corner
`C = Q M_n(B) Q` is a unital AF algebra, so it has a tracial state `t`.
(2) Extend `t` to a densely finite l.s.c. trace `ρ` on `M_n(J_Q)` (corner
traces, §0) and put `σ_n := ρ^∞ ∘ φ_n` on `M_n(A)`.  It is an l.s.c. trace, so
`σ_n = Tr ⊗ σ` for an l.s.c. trace `σ` on `A`, and `σ(P) = ρ(Q) = t(1) = 1`.
(3) If `a ≥ 0` has `σ_n(a) < ∞` then `φ_n(a) ∈ M_n(J_Q)`; hence
`φ(I_σ) ⊆ J_Q`, and `φ| : I_σ -> J_Q` gives `(φ|)_* : K_0(I_σ) -> K_0(J_Q)` with
`ι_{J_Q} ∘ (φ|)_* = φ_* ∘ ι_σ` and `σ^ = ρ^ ∘ (φ|)_*`.
(4) Let `x ∈ ι_σ^{-1}(G)`.  Then `ι_{J_Q}((φ|)_* x) = φ_*(ι_σ x) = 0`.  The
quotient `B/J_Q` is AF, so `K_1(B/J_Q) = 0` and `ι_{J_Q}` is injective by the
six-term sequence.  So `(φ|)_* x = 0` and `σ^(x) = ρ^(0) = 0`.  QED

**Remark A′.**  Only two features of `B` were used: every nonzero projection of
`M_n(B)` has a unital corner with a tracial state, and `K_1(B/J) = 0` for every
ideal `J`.  Theorem A therefore holds for such targets.  It says nothing about
quasidiagonal targets with `K_1` in their quotients, such as `C(T) ⊗ (AF)`,
where the boundary can be absorbed by index maps instead of traces.

## 2. Proposition B (stable finiteness supplies the traces)

**Proposition B.**  Let `E` be separable, exact and stably finite, `K ⊴ E`, and
`H = ∂(K_1(E/K)) ⊆ K_0(K)`.  For every nonzero projection `P ∈ M_n(K)` there is
an l.s.c. trace `σ_P` on `K` with `σ_P(P) = 1`, finiteness ideal `I_P` (the ideal
generated by the entries of `P`), which annihilates `H` on its finiteness ideal.

*Proof.*  (1) By exactness of the six-term sequences for `K ⊴ E` and `I_P ⊴ E`,
`H = ker(K_0(K) -> K_0(E))`, so
`ι_P^{-1}(H) = ker(K_0(I_P) -> K_0(E)) = ∂_{I_P}(K_1(E/I_P)) =: H_P`.
(2) `H_P ∩ K_0^+(I_P) = {0}`.  A positive class is `[e]` for a projection
`e ∈ M_k(I_P)`.  If `[e] = 0` in `K_0(E)`, then `e ⊕ 1_m ~ 0 ⊕ 1_m` in some
`M_(k+m)(Ẽ)`, and the projection `0 ⊕ 1_m ≤ e ⊕ 1_m` equivalent to it forces
`e = 0` because `Ẽ` is stably finite.
(3) `C_P = P M_n(I_P) P` is unital, exact and stably finite, and
`K_0(C_P) ≅ K_0(I_P)` as ordered groups (full corner).  The image `G_P` of `H_P`
is singular, so Lemma A of
`research/artifacts/bk-radical-projection-trace-step-2026-09-13.md` (Moutzouris
Lemma 2.12 plus Haagerup and Blackadar–Rørdam) gives a tracial state `t` on
`C_P` with `t^(G_P) = 0`.
(4) Extend `t` to `ρ` on `M_n(I_P)` (§0) and set `σ_P := ρ^∞` on `M_n(K)`, read
on `K`.  Then `σ_P(P) = 1`, `I_{σ_P} = I_P`, and `σ_P^ = t^` vanishes on
`H_P = ι_P^{-1}(H)`.  QED

No fullness of `P` is used, and nuclearity enters only through exactness.

## 3. Corollary C (the uniqueness hypothesis of relative Lemma B)

Read hypothesis (3) of `bk-relative-lemma-b` as (3′): every l.s.c. trace on `A`
that annihilates `G` on its finiteness ideal and takes a finite nonzero value
somewhere is a positive multiple of `τ`.  (Traces finite only on `0` annihilate
vacuously and must be excluded under any reading.)

**Corollary C.**  Assume (1), (2), (3′) of `bk-relative-lemma-b` and that some
projection `P ∈ M_n(A)` does not lie in `M_n(I)`.
(i) Every *-homomorphism `φ` from `A` into an AF algebra with `φ_*(G) = 0` has
`φ(P) = 0`.  In particular no faithful one exists.
(ii) `A` is not an ideal `K` of a separable exact stably finite `E` with
`G = ∂(K_1(E/K))`.  So at the top level of a Blackadar–Kirchberg
counterexample (`A = R`, `G = H`), and at every level `A = R/J` with `E/J`
stably finite, (3′) forces every projection over `A` into `M_∞(I)`.

*Proof.*  Some diagonal entry `P_ii ∉ I` (entries of a positive matrix whose
diagonal lies in `I` lie in `I`), so `(Tr ⊗ τ)(P) = ∞` by (2).  In (i),
Theorem A applied to `φ(P) ≠ 0` gives `σ` annihilating `G` with `σ(P) = 1`, and
in (ii) Proposition B gives `σ_P`.  Either is finite and nonzero on `P`, so it is
not a multiple of `τ`, contradicting (3′).  QED

## 4. What this does to the ideal-equivariant realisation

- **AF targets are excluded under uniqueness.**  The realisation asked for an AF
  target with a distinguished ideal carrying `τ` and killing `G`.  By C(i), once
  a projection lies outside `I`, no AF target works at all, ideal-equivariant or
  not.  A killing embedding under (3′) must go into a quasidiagonal algebra whose
  quotients carry `K_1` (Remark A′).
- **The necessary condition is automatic under stable finiteness.**  Theorem A's
  condition (a normalised annihilating trace at every projection) holds by
  Proposition B in every stably finite extension.  For real rank zero radicals,
  where projections generate every ideal, the uniqueness regime is empty, and the
  realisation question becomes whether that condition suffices.
- **No ordered-group obstruction.**  For a strictly ordered `(G, G^+)` and a
  singular subgroup `S`, the cone `(G^+ + S)/S` on `G/S` is strict: if
  `p + s = -(p' + s')` then `p + p' ∈ S ∩ G^+ = 0`.  Rationalise and take the
  isolated cone, which stays strict on the torsion-free group.  By Levi's theorem
  (recalled, not re-read: an isolated partial order on a torsion-free abelian
  group extends to a total order) there is a strictly positive `θ : G -> D` into a
  countable totally ordered group, hence a dimension group, with `θ(S) = 0`.
  For AF sources Elliott realises `θ` and property SP gives faithfulness,
  matching "AF radicals have the K_0-embedding property".  For non-AF sources,
  realising `θ` needs embeddings into non-simple AF targets with prescribed
  traces along a chain of ideals.  That is the non-simple codomain form of
  Schafhauser Theorem D, deferred (p. 4 of arXiv:1807.07381v2, read) to
  Carrión–Gabe–Schafhauser–Tikuisis–White.

## 5. Model tests

- `C(S²) ⊕ C`, `x = (0, 1, -1)`, `P = 1_C`.  The traces `+∞` on
  `C(S²) ∖ 0` and `c·tr` on `C` have finiteness ideal `0 ⊕ C`, where
  `ι^{-1}(Zx) = 0`, so they annihilate `x` with `σ(P) = c`.  No contradiction with
  Theorem A, (3′) fails, and the class is killable (Moutzouris Prop 5.6).
- Toeplitz `T`, `K ⊴ T`, `H = Z[e_0]`.  The only trace normalised at `e_0` and
  finite on `K` is `Tr`, with `Tr^(H) ≠ 0`.  So the conclusion of Proposition B
  fails, matching that `T` is not stably finite; by Theorem A no AF-valued map
  with `φ(e_0) ≠ 0` kills `H`.
- A trivial target: `φ = 0` kills everything, and Theorem A is vacuous because it
  needs `φ(P) ≠ 0`.
