# Two candidate sources for Kourovka 21.46 (f.p. simple groups of finite cd > 2): reading record (09-18)

Lane bh-free-extensions. Both TeX sources fetched on MSI as arXiv e-prints:
`/scratch.global/sauer354/bhfe/al/main.tex` and `/scratch.global/sauer354/bhfe/rsv/main.tex`.
Line numbers refer to those files. Statements are paraphrased; short phrases are quoted.

## A. M. Amir, N. Lazarovich, "Simple lattices in products of Davis complexes", arXiv:2605.09493 (May 2026)

- **Theorem A (l.85).** For infinitely many c, d there is a finitely presented, simple, uniform
  lattice in Aut(T_c) × Aut(X_{O_d}). Here O_d is the Odd graph on the (d−1)-subsets of
  [2d−1] (adjacent iff disjoint), and X_{O_d} is its right-angled Davis complex.
- **Prop. "the lattice Lambda" (l.1807).** Given an interlacing pair
  ({ζ_z}_{z∈Z} ⊆ Aut(O_d), {δ_D}_{D∈V(O_d)} ⊆ Sym(Z)), the group
  `⟨Z ∪ V(L) | z², D², [D,D'] ({D,D'} ∈ E(L)), z·D·δ_D(z)·ζ_z(D)⟩`
  acts simply transitively on the vertices of X_Z × X_L = T_c × X_{O_d}.
- **Proof of Theorem A (l.2347–2383).**
  - Such a lattice Λ' contains a non-residually-finite involutive BMW group (Radu, or
    Lazarovich's counting paper) in a hyperplane stabilizer.
  - Its projections are dense in the universal groups, whose "+" subgroups are simple of index 2.
  - Bader–Shalom makes every finite-index subgroup just-infinite.
  - So the finite residual Λ has finite index and is simple.
- **Discussion (l.2386–2407).**
  - All known f.p. simple groups have cd 2 or ∞. They ask whether other values occur.
  - Torsion-free uniform lattices in T_c × X_{O_d} have cd 3.
  - Λ' has torsion (it is generated by involutions). Even Λ'^+ = Λ' ∩ (Aut^+ × Aut^+) contains
    the involutions DD' for commuting D, D'.
  - "it is unclear whether the finite index simple subgroup Λ contained in Λ'^+ has torsion."
  - Question: a virtually simple Λ' with Λ'^+ torsion-free?
- **Aut^+ (l.793, l.812).** Generated by halfspace stabilizers. U^+ preserves the proper
  2-colouring of vertices.

## B. N. Rungtanapirom, J. Stix, A. Vdovina, arXiv:1808.03290 (2018)

- **Arithmetic lattices.** Torsion-free S-arithmetic quaternionic lattices acting simply transitively
  on products of d ≥ 1 trees. They are residually finite.
- **Doubling (l.2637, Prop. l.2655).** D(X) = (Γ/Γ_0)\(Y ⊠_{□^d} Y) is a 1-vertex cube complex whose
  universal cover is a product of trees.
- **Exact sequence (Prop. after l.2870).**
  `1 → ∏_{i=1}^d π_1(T_{v_i} ⊠_{□^1} T_{v_i}) → π_1(D(X_S)) → Γ_S ×_{Γ_S/Γ_{S,0}} Γ_S → 1`.
- **Theorem (l.2925).** For #S_0 ≥ 2, π_1(D(X_S)) is not residually finite. It contains the double
  of a 2-dimensional subcomplex through a local isometry; then Caprace's survey Prop. 4.15, based
  on Caprace–Monod.
- **l.754.** "It is tempting to ask if these non-residually finite lattices are also virtually
  simple." The exact sequence above answers this negatively; see
  `rsv-quaternionic-doubles-are-never-virtually-simple`.
