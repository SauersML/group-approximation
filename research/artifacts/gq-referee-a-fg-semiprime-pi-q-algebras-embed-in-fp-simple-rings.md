## The proof, step by step
- **Step 1.** Semiprime means the prime radical is 0. So `A` embeds in `∏_P A/P`.
- **Step 2, the author's point (a).**
  - For a prime PI ring, the Posner–Rowen theorem says that nonzero central elements are regular and that the
    central localization is a finite-dimensional central simple algebra.
  - Because those central elements are regular, `A/P → Q_P` is injective. It is unital.
  - `Q_P → Q_P ⊗ F̄_P` is injective, since it is a field extension. The target is `M_{n_P}(F̄_P)`.
- **The degree bound.** `n_P ≤ m` holds as follows.
  - Take the identity multilinear. Multilinearization never raises the degree, so this costs nothing.
  - A multilinear identity passes to central localizations and to scalar extensions. So `M_{n_P}(F̄_P)` satisfies
    an identity of degree `2m`.
  - By Amitsur–Levitzki, `2n_P ≤ 2m`.
  - **Wording:** the proof should say "multilinear" at this point.
- **Step 3.** `n_P | m!`, and the diagonal block maps are unital. Also `∏_P M_N(F̄_P) = M_N(∏_P F̄_P)`.
  - Every `F̄_P` has characteristic 0, because `A/P` is a nonzero `Q`-algebra.
- **Step 4, the author's point (b).** The product over all primes is harmless. `C_0` is generated by the finitely
  many entries of the images of the generators, and `M_N(C_0)` is a unital subring that contains those images.
- **Step 5, the author's point (c).**
  - `M_N(C_0) ⊆ M_N(C_0 ⊗_Q Q<x_1>)`, because `C_0 → C_0 ⊗ Q<x_1>` is injective.
  - `C_0` is a finitely generated commutative `Q`-algebra, so it qualifies by §2 of
    `char-zero-representable-rings-embed-in-fp-simple-rings` (PASS, 69a3cc64f), applied with `D = N` and `m = 1`.
  - Correct.

## Covered classes and the example
- **GK dimension.** GK dimension 0 means finite-dimensional. GK dimension 1 means PI, by Small–Stafford–Warfield;
  that citation is quoted from secondary summaries.
- **The example `Q<x,y>/(yx, y^2)`.**
  - It is represented by `x ↦ diag(t, 0)` and `y ↦ e_12`, and the relations check.
  - The basis `1, x^i` (`i ≥ 1`), `x^i y` maps to the linearly independent elements `I`, `diag(t^i, 0)`, `t^i e_12`.
  - It is not right Noetherian: the right ideals `Σ_{i ≤ n} Q x^i y` form a strictly ascending chain.
