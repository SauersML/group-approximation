## 16.3 `fp-torsion-free-nonsofic-group-of-cd-at-most-three` (a08c3599fc, a5d2106cb3). PASS

- **The ambient group is the whole cover.** `EL_D(R)` is the image of `EL_9(R) = GL_9(R)` under the
  prefix-code isomorphism `M_9(R) ≅ R`, so it is `R^x`. Hence `Ĝ = G`.
- **Nonsofic.** Kun–Thom Theorem A, as recorded in `kt-pair-group-double-is-nonsofic` (parts 8 and 14).
- **Finitely presented.** Two finite presentations, plus identifications of a finite generating set of the
  Kazhdan group `Γ̂`.
- **Torsion-free.** Finite subgroups of an amalgam are conjugate into a vertex group.
- **Dimension.** `cd D <= 3` by Mayer–Vietoris, and `cd D >= 3` by (DB1) with `n = cd Γ̂ = 2`. So `cd D = 3`.
- **Reading.**
  - This is a finitely presented torsion-free nonsofic group of cohomological dimension exactly three.
    Novelty was checked only against the graph, per the node.
  - It closes no root. By 16.1 it cannot be pushed down to dimension two as a double.
