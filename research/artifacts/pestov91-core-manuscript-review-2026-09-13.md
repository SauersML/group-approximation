# Chain-recurrent core results in the non-MF manuscript

2026-09-13. Paper proofs; no new Lean verification or priority claim.

The manuscript `non_mf_groups_exist.tex`, section `sec:chain-core`, now
prints four results:

- `lem:chain-core-models`: the nonempty chain-recurrent core has an LEF
  crossed-product ring, and wandering compressed-clopen defects cover
  its complement.
- `lem:transient-matrices`: restriction is onto, and its kernel is a
  locally matricial ideal generated by those defects.
- `thm:core-ring-reflection`: restriction is the common universal unital
  quotient into directly finite, stably finite and LEF rings, including
  every finite matrix amplification, over any finite field.
- `thm:core-mf-radical`: over F2 and in rank at least four, the full
  elementary and general linear restriction kernels are exactly the MF
  radicals, locally finite, with LEF quotients. For general linear
  groups the quotient is the restriction image, without a claim that
  all core units lift.

The proofs are self-contained apart from the manuscript's existing
torsion-defect proposition. In particular the finite ring models keep
distinct Laurent degrees separate even in the presence of periodic
points, and the local matrix proof uses freeness only off the core.
The general systems need not be expansive, finitely generated or Kazhdan.
The return-section extension to lower ranks and the graph construction
results remain in the research notes.
