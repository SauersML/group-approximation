## A4. Commuting odd-order partners exist (proved)
Let `R=LC(X,F_2)⋊Z` and `c,t,w` be the reviewed generators (orders 7, 2, 2). Let `V` be clopen with tower levels `L_0,L_1,L_2` (translates of `V`, pairwise disjoint), with matrix units `ε_ab`, and let `v=ψ(P_3)+(1-e)` be the order-3 rotation.
1. **Membership.** `diag(1,1,v)`, `diag(1,v,1)` and `vI` lie in `EL_3(R)`.
   - In characteristic 2, if `βα=0` then `e_12(α)e_21(β)e_12(α)e_21(β)=diag(1+αβ,1,1)`. Direct multiplication gives `[[1+αβ,αβα],[β,1+βα]]` after three factors, and `αβα=0`.
   - `P_3` is a product of transvections, and `1+ε_ab=1+αβ` with `α=ε_ab`, `β=ε_bb`, `βα=0` (a≠b).
   - Constant permutation matrices move the coordinate.
2. **Commutation.**
   - `vI` commutes with `c`, so `a=c·vI` has order 21, with `a^15=c` and `a^7=vI`.
   - `diag(1,1,v)` commutes with `w`, so `b=w·diag(1,1,v)` has order 6, with `b^3=w` and `b^4=diag(1,1,v)`.
   - If all levels lie in `[a]=\{x_0=a\}`, then `diag(1,v,1)` commutes with `t=I+(1+p)E_12+pE_13`: the condition is `(1+p)(v^{-1}+1)=0`.

## A5. Every pairing leaves an essential generator uncovered
Two generators carry at most two essential pieces. A1 on the node shows that no two of `c,t,w` can share one generator through constant partners. The three tower pairings each fail:
- **B** `<c·vI, w·diag(1,1,v')>`: every entry lies in `S=<u^{±1},v,v'>`. The diagonal part of `S` lies in the u-invariant algebra generated by the tower levels, which contains `[a]` only if the levels code X injectively. That fails for small sets `V` in general, so B is ring-deficient.
- **C** `<t·diag(1,v',1), w·diag(1,1,v)>`: all four pieces are block upper triangular for the coordinate splitting `\{1,2\}|\{3\}`. So `C` lies in a proper parabolic subgroup.
- **D** `<t·diag(1,v',1), c·vI>`: no entry involves `u` of nonzero total degree on all of X, so D is ring-deficient.

## A6. Finite-model check (MSI, GAP 4 via Sage 10.7, `fm.g`)
- **Models:** Fibonacci words abaab, abaababa, abaababaabaab (d = 15, 24, 39), with `u ↦ N`-cycle and `p ↦` the diagonal of a-positions.
- **Test:** MeatAxe absolute irreducibility, plus element orders divisible by primitive prime divisors of `2^d-1` and `2^{d-1}-1` (400 pseudo-random elements).

| pair | d=15 | d=24 | d=39 |

The reducibility matches A5: permutation-type entries fix `F_2^3⊗𝟙` in B, the subspace `x_3=0` is invariant in C, and D decouples points outside the supports. This is heuristic evidence only: finite simple groups are 2-generated, so a passing finite model proves nothing about `G_X`.

## Sharpest remaining target
A 2-generating pair needs a piece that is essential and not constant: an odd-order element of `C(w)` or `C(t)` with nonzero entries in both off-diagonal blocks, whose words with the other pieces produce a constant of order 7.
- Necessary conditions for any pair: the entries generate `R`; the pair lies in no parabolic subgroup; its images are absolutely irreducible in the finite models.
- Candidates have the shape `D_1·[[A,0,B],[0,A,B],[C,C,D]]·D_1^{-1}` with `D_1=diag(u,1,1)` and blocks built from tower units.
- Value for the note: low (prior art above). One lane at most, not a family.
