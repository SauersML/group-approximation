# UHF-type groupoid models for the K-budget: class verdict (lane gq-deep-adelic-1, 2026-09-17)

## 1. The target

The coordinator asked for a finitely generated simple ring `R ⊇ Q` with the UHF K-budget of
`research/artifacts/gq-deep-adelic-1-k-budget.md` and `gq-gq-en-ring-k-budget-shape.md`:
- `[1]` of infinite order and divisible in `K_0`;
- `Q^x` detected in `K_1`, with finitely generated kernel;
- finitely generated rational symbols in `K_2`.

The suggested source was "a finitely generated dense subring of a UHF-type C*-algebra built from an
odometer-type minimal action (Bratteli–Vershik / Toeplitz models) avoiding the Laurent trap", or a proof
that the class fails. The work was split with `gq-en-ring`, which takes counter and Bratteli engines
(`gq-gq-en-ring-counter-engine.md`). This lane took the groupoid readings and crossed products by
non-cyclic groups, including spark S1 (perfect groups).

## 2. Verdict: the class fails, except for one sharply described remnant

| Reading of "UHF-type model" | What kills it | Node |
|---|---|---|
| Rings of compactly supported functions, any coefficients, on the **AF groupoid** of the UHF algebra (tail equivalence of a Bratteli diagram, `K_0 = Z[1/n!]`) | no finitely generated subring contains `Q`: the generators live on a compact open subgroupoid, whose regular representation on a finite source fibre is a matrix representation | `af-groupoid-rings-have-no-fg-subring-containing-q`, via `fg-q-rings-have-no-matrix-representations` |
| Rings on the **orbit groupoid of a minimal Z-action** (odometer, Toeplitz, Bratteli–Vershik systems) containing the translation unit | `{λ, u}` is infinitely generated in `K_2`, so no `E_N` is finitely presented | `laurent-rational-symbols-block-fp-elementary-groups`; for every skew Laurent ring, `gq-gq-en-ring-k-budget-shape.md` §2 |
| Crossed products by a group `Γ` over an odometer factor, where some finite-index `Γ' ⊇ Γ_n` of the chain is **indicable** | the block `C` over `Γ'` gives `R ≅ M_k(χ_C R χ_C)`, and that corner is skew Laurent with a tracial base | `matrix-corner-laurent-rings-block-fp-elementary-groups` |

So perfection of `Γ` is not enough for spark S1. `Γ` perfect but virtually indicable (free groups, surface
groups, RAAGs, hyperbolic 3-manifold groups by Agol) still falls into the Laurent trap, through a block
corner.

**Remnant.** `Γ` such that every finite-index subgroup appearing in the odometer chain has finite
abelianization, i.e. property FAb along the chain. Examples: groups with property (T), such as
`SL_n(Z)`, `n >= 3`, with a congruence chain. Not decided here.

## 3. The remnant: what holds and what is open

Take `Γ = SL_3(Z)`, `Γ_n = ker(SL_3(Z) -> SL_3(Z/n!))`, the odometer `Ĝ = lim Γ/Γ_n`, and a Toeplitz
`Γ`-subshift `X -> Ĝ`, so that the diagonal is generated by translates of letter cylinders. Take
`R = ⟨LC(X,Z), f, u_s^(±1)⟩ ⊆ C(X,Q) ⋊_alg Γ`, with `f` a harmonic function as in
`tracial-harmonic-ring-keeps-rational-scalars`.

**Expected by the same arguments as the tracial node** (not written out, and not checked here):
- finite generation;
- `Q ⊆ R`, by harmonic tiling, since minimality gives finite covers by translates;
- simplicity, by compression; freeness holds because the odometer action is free and `X -> Ĝ` is
  equivariant;
- (K1), by Fuglede–Kadison, *if* `X` carries an invariant measure. For non-amenable `Γ` this needs a
  regular Toeplitz extension lifting Haar measure; existence was not checked (Cortez–Petite,
  Krieger).
- `[1]` divisible by every integer, since `[Γ : Γ_n]` is divisible by `n!`; so `K_2(Q) -> K_2(R)` is `0`
  (`matricial-divisibility-kills-rational-k2-symbols`).

**The two open risks**, both beyond the tools landed so far:
- **R1, non-divisible corners.** Letter cylinders `[a] ⊆ X` are not unions of fibres of `X -> Ĝ`, so
  their `K_0` classes can carry infinitesimals that are not divisible.
  `full-corners-must-kill-rational-k2-symbols` then requires `K_2(Q)` to die in `χ_[a] R χ_[a]` by some
  other means.
- **R2, torsion index units.** With property (T), every `Γ_n^ab` is finite but typically nonzero, e.g.
  `Γ(m)^ab ≅ sl_3(Z/m)` (Lee–Szczarba, recalled and not re-read). The block corners over `Γ_n` are then `Z/d`-graded with a
  degree-one unit `v`. The symbols `λ ↦ {λ, v}` factor through `Q^x ⊗ Z/d`, which is infinitely
  generated. No detector is known here.
  - The transfer to the degree-zero part sends `{λ, v}` to `± {λ, v^d}`. That only moves the question
    down the chain.
  - A proof would need `K_2` of crossed products by `SL_3(Z)`, e.g. through the Farrell–Jones
    conjecture for `SL_n(Z)` with coefficients (Bartels–Lück–Reich–Rüping). It must also handle the
    Nil terms of a non-regular base.

## 4. Consequence for the ring route

The ring route (`q-algebra-with-fp-simple-projective-elementary-group`) needs a finitely generated simple
host with the UHF K-budget. The groupoid models now leave only:
- (i) groupoids that are neither AF nor carry a translation unit on any block, e.g. transformation
  groupoids of FAb groups, subject to R1 and R2;
- (ii) non-groupoid mechanisms: the non-spatial matrix isomorphisms of `gq-gq-en-ring-k-budget-shape.md`
  §3, and gq-en-ring's counter engines.

Both are open. Nothing here decides the root `gl-n-q-embeds-in-fp-simple-group`.
