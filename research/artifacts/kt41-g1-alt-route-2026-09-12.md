# Gap 1 of Kun–Thom 4.1: direct two-component repair or swap encoding?

Lane `kt41-g1-alt`, 2026-09-12. Campaign: the negative answer to Bowen–Chapman
arXiv:2511.06586, Problem 1.1, unconditional. Gap 1 is the improvement of
composites of cluster candidates for approximate component actions of a Kazhdan
group (blueprint `kt-theorem-4-1-blueprint-2026-09-12.md`, step 5).

Question: is a direct formalization of Alekseev–Thom's two-component repair,
through Kun–Thom 2019 Proposition 3.3, shorter than kt-norm-paper's swap-encoding
route?

## Verdict

No. kt-norm-paper's swap-encoding route is already proved, both at fixed
parameters and along a diagonal, and it is landed on main:
- commit `2458bbc45`;
- paths `GroupApproximation/KunThom/CentralizerNormalization{FiberCoarea,SumPoincare,Parameters,CrossingRepair,SumApproximation,Improve}.lean`;
- all six are root-imported;
- their blobs equal the bytes of probe record
  `kt-norm-paper.green.0912-123604-64535`.

A direct route would re-prove every step of that route. It would also add a
correct-ball interface that `ExpanderDecomposition` does not carry. So this
lane opens no `TwoComponentRepair*` files.

What remains of gap 1 comes after the repair, and either route would need it:
- a producer of `ComponentFamily` from Kun's decomposition;
- a per-object scale for the cluster groupoid.

See L1 and L2 below.

## The two cited statements

**Kun–Thom 2019, Proposition 3.3**, cited there from Kun. Hypotheses:
- Γ is Kazhdan, with a symmetric generating set S ∋ 1 and Kazhdan constant κ;
- α > 0 is given.

Conclusion: there is a radius r with the following property. In any finite
S-labelled graph, every vertex set T whose points all have correct rooted
r-balls is close to a set U with

    |∂U| ≤ α|U|,   |U Δ T| ≤ (10/κ²)‖χ_T − Mχ_T‖² ≤ 5|∂T|/(dκ²).

**Kun–Thom 2019, Theorem 3.4** applies it to the graph of an ε-almost
automorphism inside G_n × G_n. Its last paragraph then repairs U:
- bound the row and column multiplicities of U by a coarea sum over their level
  sets;
- apply the Cheeger constant, since multiplicity one is the majority;
- delete and add points until U is the graph of a bijection.

**Alekseev–Thom, Proposition 3.3** runs the same argument on Y × Z for a partial
bijection b : Y ⇢ Z:
1. Restrict the graph B of b to points with correct balls.
2. Apply Kun–Thom 2019 Proposition 3.3 in the diagonal graph.
3. Bound the row and column multiplicities by α|U|/h, equations (2) and (3).
4. Correct U to the graph of a partial bijection b′ with
   d_Y(b, b′) ≤ C₁Δ_S(b) + ζ.

## Why a direct route is longer

Steps 3–6 would be shared. Beyond them, a direct formalization needs two new
pieces:

* **Correct balls.** Rooted r-ball isomorphism for labelled finite models, plus a
  correct-ball hypothesis on components (Alekseev–Thom Lemma 3.2).
  `ExpanderDecomposition` records edit negligibility and almost invariance, not
  correct balls, so producing them from `SoficApproximation` is a new module.
* **A uniform radius.** Kun's spectral projection with a radius r(α) uniform
  over all graphs, i.e. a locality argument for the Markov iterate. The repo's
  iterate, `KunThomRounding.pairIndicatorIterate`, is tied to
  `MultiplicativeApproximation`, and it gets uniformity by contradiction instead.

Neither piece is shorter than what it would replace, and steps 3–6 already
compile.

## What gap 1 still needs

**L1. A producer of `ComponentFamily K T`.**

The target is `CentralizerNormalizationImprove.ComponentFamily`, built from
`ExpanderDecomposition (S.restrict Γ) T`. The retained components must carry all
but a vanishing fraction of the mass. The fields are:

* **`expands`**: `∀ n i, DirectedCoarea.HasCheegerLowerBound (model n i) (T.image (act n i)) cheeger`.
  - **The completed component labels do not give this.**
    `ExpanderDecomposition.componentCompletedAction` is an arbitrary completion,
    the `Classical.choose` of `Localization.exists_completion`.
  - The repo proves only tagged expansion at a scale:
    `componentCompletedAction_taggedExpansion`, with constant `cheeger/4` at
    scales m where the local budget is below cheeger·m/2.
  - A completion that fixes one point x under every label makes U = {x} a set
    with empty directed boundary.
  - The repair needs the exact bound on small sets.
    `CentralizerNormalizationCrossingRepair.cheeger_mul_sourceDefect_extractCrossing_le`
    applies it to the complement of the crossing source. That lemma bounds the
    size of this set, and the size must end below η·m/(2|T|).
  - **Template for a producer**, as in
    `EssentialExpanderRepair.repairedActualGraph_expands_eventually` for a
    `MatchingCertificate`:
    1. On the actual generator graph of each component, run the maximal-cut
       repair `MaximalCutRepair.induce_retained_hasCheegerLowerBound`, with the
       local edit budget as the additive error.
    2. Complete the labels on the retained set.
    3. Transfer the bound with `CompletionGraphEditing.induce_boundaryCard_le_completed`.
  - The bridge from `FiniteMultiGraph.boundaryCard` of `generatorGraph` to
    `directedBoundary` of the label image already exists as
    `AlmostAutomorphism.crossingEdges_decide_mem` plus
    `generatorCrossing_card_le_directedBoundary`. It is stated only for the
    labels of a `SoficApproximation (K × J)` under label injectivity.
  - Correction, same day: the per-component form, which needs no injectivity,
    is `RetainedComponentExpansion.boundaryCard_generatorGraph_le_card_mul_directedBoundary`
    (constant `|T|`). `retained_completion_directedCheeger` in the same module
    assembles the three steps above on one finite model. It is in progress,
    WIP `f356985cb`.
  - The same exact bound supplies the tagged expansion at small scales that the
    re-separation summation needs (`hexp`, next section).
* **`size_tendsTo`**, uniformly in i.
  - Retain only components of size at least j(n), with j(n) → ∞ slowly.
  - Use `ExpanderDecomposition.smallBlockVertices_negligible` at each fixed size,
    then take a diagonal.
* **`locallyMultiplicative`**, uniformly in i for each (g, h, ε).
  - Enumerate the pairs of the countable group.
  - Retain the components on which the first j(n) pairs have relative error below
    1/j(n).
  - The bad mass is at most Σ_{pairs ≤ j(n)} j(n)·error, and each error sum is
    negligible by `componentMultiplicationError_sum_negligible`.
* **`labelsInjective`**: for t ≠ t′ in T, apply Markov to the fixed-point set of
  t⁻¹t′. Freeness makes that set negligible.

**L2. A per-object scale in the cluster groupoid.**
- `PartialClusterSystem` and `FinitePartialClusterData` carry one `scale` and one
  `radius` for all objects.
- `ImproveCloseAt` works at the per-object scale |model n i|/18, and component
  sizes differ.
- Before `exists_joint_improvement` can populate a groupoid, one of these is
  needed:
  - a variant of `FinitePartialClusterGroupoid` with the radius indexed by the
    source object, or
  - a split into size classes that is closed under candidates.

## Remark on the known threshold defect

The per-object term h·scale/2 does not vanish when the threshold h is fixed.
Two facts bear on this:
- The landed `BlockPatching.BlockArrows.card_hammingDisagreement_patch_le_reseparation`
  already takes the actual per-block defects, at a real vanishing scale.
- `exists_joint_improvement` makes the threshold h n → 0. Then even the threshold
  bound sums to at most h n·|Y n|/36 over retained blocks, so it vanishes.

The re-separation still needs tagged expansion at small scales. That is again the
exact Cheeger bound of L1.
