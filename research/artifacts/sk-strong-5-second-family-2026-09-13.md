## 2. Candidate 1: the lamplighter host over Z = the Bernoulli CAR crossed product
### 2.1 Identification (node `car-bernoulli-crossed-product-is-lamplighter-ring`)
- **Statement.** Let L = Z/2 ≀ Z act on 2^Z by the shift and the flip at 0, and let B = ⊗_Z M_2(F_2) be the algebraic
  infinite tensor product with tensor shift σ. Then LC(2^Z,F_2) ⋊ L ≅ B ⋊_σ Z.
- **Proof.**
  - The finite windows LC(H_n) ⋊ H_n, with H_n = (Z/2)^{[−n,n]}, are M_{2^{2n+1}}(F_2), because M_{δ_x}λ_{x−y} = E_{x,y}.
  - They sit inside each other as x ↦ 1⊗x⊗1, so their union is B.
  - The shift acts sitewise, as σ.
- **Credit.** Folklore; the C*-analogue is standard.

### 2.2 Generators and (T)
- R is generated by e_12^{(0)}, e_21^{(0)} and s^{±1}.
- So G_CAR has (T) by EJZ and is generated by 24 elementary matrices.

### 2.3 Qubit-shift models (node `car-shift-el3-is-marked-limit-of-qubit-shift-sl-groups`)
- **The models.** V_N = (F_2^2)^{⊗Z/N}; π_N cyclically permutes the factors; ρ_N sends the site-0 generators to factor 0
  and t_± to π_N^{±1}.
- **Onto.** ρ_N maps onto M_{2^N}(F_2).
- **Multiplicative.** φ_N(Σ b_j s^j) = Σ ι_N(b_j) π_N^j is multiplicative on bounded supports, since
  π ι(x^{(t)}) π^{-1} = ι(x^{(t+1)}).
- **Injective for N > 8L+4.** A marker 1 at site ⌊N/2⌋ separates the exponents j, and varying the pattern on [−2L,2L]
  spans all of the window W.
- **Consequences.**
  - Words of length L are trivial in G_CAR iff trivial in SL_{3·2^N}(F_2).
  - So G_CAR is the marked limit of finite simple groups and is LEF.
  - The Cayley graphs form expanders, because EL_3(F) has (T).
- The full proof is in the route node.

### 2.4 Simplicity, and why it is not shorter
- **What is known.** G_CAR is simple with trivial centre: the case Δ = Z of `lamplighter-bernoulli-crossed-products-simple-kazhdan-lef`,
  which has two PASSes. Its proof uses Steinberg groupoid simplicity, or the note's tower argument over L with the density
  patch (`tower-simplicity-holds-for-topologically-free-actions`, unreviewed).
- **Diagonal picture, LC(2^Z) ⋊ L.** The coefficients commute. But L is nonabelian and the action is only topologically
  free: the constant configurations are fixed by s. So the tower step needs word-length balls in L, patterns robust under
  flips, and the density patch.
- **CAR picture, B ⋊ Z.** The acting group is Z, but the coefficients b ∈ A_n do not act as scalars on far rank-one
  idempotents p. So the span of b σ^a(p) s^{a−b} is not closed under products: σ^{a−b}(b′) leaves A_n.
- **Why the subshift family wins.** The note's absorption step needs commutative coefficients and a free abelian action at
  once, and only the subshift family has both.
- **One saving.** The covering step is shorter for G_CAR: a nonzero element of a finite simple algebra A_m generates an
  ideal containing 1, which saves about 3 lines.

## 4. Candidate 4: published sandwich theorems for another family
- **The requirement.** A family would be shorter if its ring fact for Stepanov, Preusser or Bass–Vaserstein were short to
  verify.
- **The rings where these facts are immediate** (locally matricial rings) are not finitely generated, so EJZ fails.
- **For finitely generated simple rings with (T),** the facts are known only for amenable crossed products, via Stepanov
  (b), which is the note's own family.

## 5. Firewall (node `finite-simple-kazhdan-limits-need-not-be-simple`)
- **The example.** SL_3(Z), with elementary generators, has (T), is infinite and finitely generated, and is the marked
  limit of the simple groups SL_3(F_p), p ≡ 2 mod 3. It is not simple: the congruence kernel mod 2 is a proper,
  nontrivial normal subgroup.
- **Consequence.** A Kazhdan group that is a marked limit of finite simple expanders still needs its own simplicity proof.
  Every "simplicity from the models" shortcut fails at this example.

## 6. Dead end: PSL_3 of a finitely generated division ring
- **Why it looks attractive.** If some infinite division ring D were finitely generated as a ring, PSL_3(D) would be simple
  (Dieudonné) and Kazhdan (EJZ), with a two-line proof. Its LEF property would still need D to be a LEF ring.
- **Why it is dead.** Finitely generated fields are finite, and main has no infinite finitely generated division ring. To
  my knowledge their existence is an open problem, but I did not check the attribution.
- **Status.** Recorded as a dead end, not landed as a claim.
