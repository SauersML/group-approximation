# STW Problem LXXVI: first-factor order-cofinality audit (2026-08-30)

## What is new

The existing LXXVI graph identifies the ideal lattice, K-theory, and
quasitraces of `A` and `A tensor Z`, and separately proves order reflection
when the left source class is soft.  It did not record a comparison-theoretic
density property of the actual first-factor image.

The theorem now has its natural generality: every full star homomorphism has
order-cofinal Cu image.  Each way-below target window is dominated by a finite
sum of image classes, hence by one class from the stabilized source; a
countable sum gives one dominator for an arbitrary target class.  Unital
first-factor maps are full, so the tensor statement is a corollary.

For the actual `Z` first-factor map, the coefficient row can also be kept in
the ideal corresponding to the target.  Thus local windows have image
dominators inside the target ideal, and every target class has a global
image dominator generating exactly the same ideal.

## Full-map audit

If `c` lies in the ideal generated by `phi(A)`, approximate it by a positive
finite ideal sum `h=sum z_j*phi(a_j)z_j`.  Writing the column of
`phi(a_j)^(1/2)z_j` as `W` gives

```text
W*W=h,              WW*<=||Z||^2 diag(phi(a_1),...,phi(a_N)).
```

The equality of the Cuntz classes of `W*W` and `WW*`, plus the usual
perturbation comparison, gives domination by the finite sum of `[a_j]`.
This works for nonunital and nonseparable algebras and uses neither tensor
exactness nor slice maps.

For ideal matching, write `Ideal(a)=I tensor_min Z` using the nuclear-simple
ideal correspondence and apply the full-map theorem directly to the
restricted map `I->I tensor_min Z`.  In the global construction all
partial-sum dominators remain in `Cu(I)`; domination of `x` supplies the
reverse ideal inclusion.  This does not claim that the dominator belongs to
the hereditary subalgebra generated by `a`.

## Boundary sharpened by the diagonal theorem

This is upper or outer cofinality, not inner cofinality.  Even an
ideal-matched dominating first-factor class can extend far outside the
hereditary support of the target class.  The distinction is essential: the corrected theorem
`stw95-order-embedded-inner-cofinal-cu-image-forces-pure-product` shows that
order reflection plus genuine inner brackets diagonalizes to a Cu-isomorphism.
Thus the new automatic result removes lack of upper dominators as an LXXVI
obstruction but does not solve injectivity, surjectivity, or inner rank
realization.
