# One-compressor reduction for compression triples

Lane: `ff-hyperlinear-2` (universal no-go side for the Fournier--Facio group),
2026-09-11.  Nodes: `compressor-subgroup-is-directed-union-by-cyclic`,
`fournier-facio-one-compressor-subgroup-reduces-to-gamma`,
`fournier-facio-compression-subgroup-not-hyperlinear`.

## 1. General theorem

Let `G` be a group, `Gamma <= G` a subgroup and `t in G` with
`t Gamma t^-1 <= Gamma`.  Put

```text
H = <Gamma, t>,        Lambda = union_(k>=0) t^-k Gamma t^k.      (OC1)
```

1. `Lambda` is a subgroup, it is normal in `H`, and `H/Lambda` is cyclic,
   generated by the image of `t`.
2. Every finite subset of `Lambda` lies in one conjugate `t^-k Gamma t^k`.
3. `H` is hyperlinear if and only if `Gamma` is; `H` is sofic if and only if
   `Gamma` is.
4. If `Gamma` is finitely generated and `t Gamma t^-1 != Gamma`, then
   `Lambda` is not finitely generated and `H` does not have property (T).

**Proof of 1 and 2.**  `t Gamma t^-1 <= Gamma` is equivalent to
`Gamma <= t^-1 Gamma t`; conjugating by `t^-k` gives
`t^-k Gamma t^k <= t^-(k+1) Gamma t^(k+1)`.  So `(OC1)` is an increasing
union of subgroups, hence a subgroup, and every finite subset lies in one
term.  `Gamma <= Lambda` normalizes `Lambda`.  Also

```text
t Lambda t^-1 = t Gamma t^-1  union  Lambda = Lambda,
t^-1 Lambda t = union_(k>=1) t^-k Gamma t^k = Lambda,
```

so `t` normalizes `Lambda`.  Hence `Lambda` is normal in `H`, and `H/Lambda`
is generated by the images of `Gamma` (trivial) and `t`.

**Proof of 3.**  Hyperlinearity and soficity are local (a group has them iff
every finitely generated subgroup does) and pass to subgroups, so by 2
`Lambda` has either property exactly when `Gamma` does, and `Gamma <= H`
gives one direction.  For the other direction:

* If `H/Lambda` is finite of order `m`, induce: an approximate
  representation (permutation approximation) of `Lambda` on `C^d` (on `[d]`)
  induces one of `H` on `C^d tensor C^m` (on `[d] x [m]`), with the regular
  character preserved.
* If `H/Lambda` is infinite, then `H = Lambda x| <t>` because `Z` is free.
  Let `alpha(x) = t x t^-1`, an automorphism of `Lambda`.  Given a finite set
  `F <= Lambda`, a word-length bound `L` in `t`, and `n >> L`, choose an
  approximation `sigma` of `Lambda` that is good on the finite set
  `union_(0<=j<n) alpha^-j(F F)`.  On `C^d tensor C^n` put

  ```text
  pi(x)(xi tensor e_j) = sigma(alpha^-j(x)) xi tensor e_j,
  pi(t)(xi tensor e_j) = xi tensor e_(j+1 mod n).           (OC2)
  ```

  The relation `pi(t) pi(x) pi(t)^-1 = pi(alpha(x))` holds on every block
  except the wrap-around one, so every fixed word of `t`-length at most `L`
  has normalized Hilbert--Schmidt defect `O(sqrt(L/n))`.  Traces of
  `pi(x t^k)` vanish for `0 < |k| < n`, and for `k = 0` they are averages of
  `sigma`-traces, which are small for `x != 1`.  The same formula with
  permutation matrices gives a sofic approximation.

These are the elementary cyclic cases of the general permanence theorems
(Elek--Szabo for sofic-by-amenable; Arzhantseva--Berlai--Finn-Sell--Glebsky,
arXiv:1802.04688, for hyperlinear-by-amenable); the argument above does not
depend on them.

**Proof of 4.**  If `Lambda` were finitely generated it would lie in one term,
so `Lambda = t^-k Gamma t^k` for some `k`.  Then
`t^-(k+1) Gamma t^(k+1) <= t^-k Gamma t^k`, i.e. `t^-1 Gamma t <= Gamma`, and
together with `t Gamma t^-1 <= Gamma` this forces `t Gamma t^-1 = Gamma`.
If `H` had property (T): `H/Lambda` cannot be infinite cyclic (Kazhdan groups
have finite abelianization), and if it is finite then `Lambda` has finite
index, hence property (T), hence is finitely generated, a contradiction.

## 2. Application to the Fournier--Facio group

Use the notation of `fournier-facio-torsion-free-skeleton` and
`fournier-facio-group-contains-simple-wreath-shift`: `t = t_1`,
`J_k = t^k J t^-k`, `J_1 = pi(S)`.  Put `H_1 = <Gamma, t_1>`.

* Every `J_k`, the internal wreath product `S wr Z`, and the direct product
  `Gamma x (+)_(k<=0) J_k` are words in `Gamma` and `t_1`, so they lie in
  `H_1`.  The whole compression triple of OpenAI Proposition 2.3
  (`openai-expander-matching-criterion`) with `m = 1`, and every relation used
  by `fournier-facio-regular-models-carry-commutant-shift`, holds in `H_1`.
* Clause (W7) with `n = 1` makes `Gamma >= J_1 x t Gamma t^-1` an internal
  direct product with `J_1 != 1`, so `t Gamma t^-1 != Gamma`.  `Gamma` is
  finitely generated because it has property (T).
* By Section 1, `H_1` does not have property (T), and it is hyperlinear
  (sofic) exactly when `Gamma` is.

The same holds for `H_2 = <Gamma, t_2>`.

## 3. Consequences for the no-go

**(a) One compressor cannot see past `Gamma`.**  Any normalized-HS obstruction
whose hypotheses are satisfied by `H_1` proves that `Gamma` is not
hyperlinear.  Those hypotheses are property (T) of `Gamma`, the nesting
`t_1 (Gamma x J) t_1^-1 <= Gamma`, the simplicity or non-LEF property of
`J`, and the proper commutant shift with growing relative commutants.  In
particular, if `Gamma` is hyperlinear, `H_1` has regular tracial models in
`R^omega`, and in every one of them the commutant shift is realized.

**(b) The soficity analogue.**  An argument for OpenAI Proposition 2.3 that
does not use property (T) of the ambient group (in the current version, the
expanding approximation of the ambient group) would apply to `H_1` and prove
that `Gamma` is nonsofic.  So removing that hypothesis is equivalent to
proving nonsoficity of the compression subgroup itself.

**(c) Where ambient (T) enters.**  In the proof of Proposition 2.3 the
ambient hypothesis is spent in Step 2 and nowhere else.  Let `M(z)` be the
size of the `Gamma`-component of `z` and, on each ambient component `A`,
`f = M/(M + m_A)` with `m_A` a median.  Orbits of the subgroup
`t_i Gamma t_i^-1` refine orbits of `Gamma`, so `f` is almost nondecreasing
along each compressor permutation.  The `Gamma`-generators almost preserve
`f`, and permutations conserve mass, so the drift is small in `l^1`.  Only
expansion of the ambient components turns small drift into near-constancy,
which gives size matching and the injective matching of components.  In
`H_1` no ambient expansion is available, and sizes may grow along `t_1`: this
is the permutation Hilbert hotel, and Section 1 realizes it whenever `Gamma`
is sofic.

**(d) What a normalized-HS Step 2 would need.**  It would need a positive
element of the approximate `Gamma`-commutant, defined at finite level,
almost monotone under `Ad pi(t_i)^*` and encoding a component frame.  For
permutation models this element is the component-size function on the
diagonal.  In a finite von Neumann algebra the inclusion `A <= Ad u(t)(A)`
carries no refinement order on frames: `L(G)` itself properly compresses
`L(Gamma)`.  So such an element must come from matricial structure.  The
charged-basis fences (`fournier-facio-monomial-near-witness-impossible`,
`fournier-facio-bounded-block-near-witness-impossible`) already rule out
obtaining it from a normalized basis.

**(e) Two compressors without ambient (T).**  The reduction does not extend
to two compressors by elementary permanence.  In the pre-quotient double HNN
extension `E = <P, u_1, u_2 | u_i P u_i^-1 = P_i>`, write `P^(w) = w^-1 P w`
for positive words `w` in the letters `u_i`, so that `P^(w) <= P^(u_i w)`.
The kernel of the height map `E -> F_2` is the fundamental group of a tree
of such copies, and the family is not directed: `P^(u_1)` and `P^(u_2)` have
no common upper term.  The kernel therefore contains the genuine amalgam
`P^(u_1) *_P P^(u_2)` over the Kazhdan subgroup `P`, embedded as
`P_1 <= P` and `P_2 <= P`.  Amalgamated products over amenable subgroups
preserve soficity and hyperlinearity; over Kazhdan subgroups no such theorem
is available.  So a no-go that does not pass through `Gamma` must use the
second compressor essentially, either through ambient (T), as the permutation
proof does, or through the amalgamation over `Gamma` that two compressors
create.
