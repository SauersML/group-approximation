## 1. Fix the group before using any theorem

Throughout let

```
R_+=F_2[x_1,x_2,x_3],
R=F_2[x_1^(+-1),x_2^(+-1),x_3^(+-1)],
Gamma_6=EL_6(R_+),
G_6=EL_6(R) rtimes SL_3(Z),
D_6=G_6 *_(Gamma_6) G_6.
```

The integer matrix M acts on monomials by x^v -> x^(Mv). The inclusion
Gamma_6->G_6 is the polynomial-to-Laurent inclusion into the elementary
normal factor, with identity second coordinate. Both edge maps of the
double are this inclusion into the corresponding copy of G_6.

Kun–Thom Theorem E states residual finiteness and property (T) for this
actor and edge group at q=2,r=6,d=3, as well as infranormality and
nonnormality. Its current statement was rechecked at the source. In
particular the RF actor is G_6, not merely EL_6(R) or SL_3(Z). No
unsupported general claim that extensions of RF groups are RF is used.

## 2. Finite presentation of this actor and this double

R has a finite associative unital ring presentation: six generators
x_i,y_i, characteristic two, all pairwise commutation relations, and
x_i y_i=1. Thus the canonical Steinberg finite-presentation input
applies to St_6(R). Independently, Allcock's [*Steinberg groups as
amalgams*](https://msp.org/ant/2016/10-8/ant-v10-n8-p05-p.pdf), Theorem
12.1, records Splitthoff's theorem for commutative finitely generated
rings and finite-type root rank at least three; type A_5 qualifies.
The statement of this alternative source was rechecked, not its full
proof. The broader Krstic–McCool theorem remains an imported canonical
input, not a newly proved result here.

The ring R is regular noetherian of dimension three. The exact stability
threshold in [Weibel's *K-book*](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~weibel/Kbook/Kbook.pdf),
Remark III.5.5.2, is n>=dim(R)+3. Therefore

```
ker(St_6(R)->EL_6(R))=K2(6,R) ~= K2(R).
```

This is an isomorphism for the entire rank-six kernel, not a surjection
onto its stable image. The regular Laurent splitting in Theorem V.6.3
and Corollary V.6.3.1 gives, iteratively from F_2,

```
K0(R_j)=Z,  K1(R_j)=Z^j,  K2(R_j)=Z^(j choose 2),
R_j=F_2[x_1^(+-1),...,x_j^(+-1)].
```

The canonical kernel claim consequently supplies three generating
symbols {x_1,x_2}, {x_1,x_3}, {x_2,x_3}. Killing them normally in
St_6(R) kills exactly its entire kernel. This proves finite presentation
of EL_6(R). There is no arbitrary-quotient preservation assertion and no
substitution of the different two-Laurent-variable calculation.

The group SL_3(Z) is finitely presented; for a concrete source,
[Sakasai](https://msp.org/agt/2012/12-1/agt-v12-n1-p12-s.pdf), printed
pages 276 and 280, records its elementary-generator presentation (six
generators, thirteen relations in the latter discussion). That statement
was rechecked. Choose finite presentations for EL_6(R) and SL_3(Z).
Adjoin their finite conjugation table on generators for the monomial
action, including inverse actor generators if necessary. Rewriting moves
actor letters to one side; the resulting normal form and the actual
semidirect product map prove this presents precisely G_6. Hence G_6 is
finitely presented.

Gamma_6 is finitely generated by x_ij(1),x_ij(x_1),x_ij(x_2),x_ij(x_3)
for i!=j. The identities

```
x_ij(a+b)=x_ij(a)x_ij(b),
[x_ik(a),x_kj(b)]=x_ij(ab)  (i,j,k distinct)
```

produce every polynomial coefficient, simultaneously in all roots, by
induction on monomial length. A finite presentation of Gamma_6 is not
needed. If <S|T> presents G_6 and c_1,...,c_m generate the embedded
Gamma_6, then

```
<S,S' | T(S),T(S'), c_i(S)=c_i(S') for i=1,...,m>
```

presents D_6. Indeed the equalities identify the two subgroup maps on
generators and therefore on the whole subgroup; the universal property
of the amalgam identifies this presentation with the desired double.
This proves finite presentation of this exact D_6.

## 3. Surjunctivity has the correct fold interface

G_6 is residually finite by the exact Theorem E statement, hence
surjunctive. The fold D_6->G_6 is the identity on each vertex group and
is well defined because the two polynomial inclusions coincide after
folding. Its restrictions to both vertex groups are injective. Thus
the existing faithful-vertex graph-fold theorem applies. Equivalently,
the narrower `doubles-of-surjunctive-groups-are-surjunctive` theorem
applies directly to Gamma_6<=G_6.

There is no claim that an arbitrary amalgam of surjunctive groups is
surjunctive. There is also no reliance on subgroup separability of
Gamma_6, finite index, or a finitely generated fold kernel. The fold
kernel is free with the possibly infinite basis indexed by
G_6/Gamma_6 minus its base coset. Its action on generators is
`alpha_g(e_x)=e_(gx)e_(gGamma_6)^-1`, not generally a permutation of the
free basis. This is exactly the finitary action handled in the cited
permanence proof and its September 12 independent review.

For this cross-review, the surjunctivity permanence theorem is an explicit
existing input. Its specialization, the free-kernel formula and the
fold hypotheses were checked; this note does not claim to independently
reprove the entire local-automaton peeling argument or run its Lean
formalization.

## 4. Nonhyperlinearity uses the same parameters

`kt-q2-double-is-not-hyperlinear` quantifies over EVERY r,d>=3 with
q=2 and precisely the polynomial inclusions and monomial action above.
Specializing to r=6,d=3 therefore proves that this D_6 is not hyperlinear.
This uses the family-wide Liu–Thom consequence already recorded in the
graph; Liu's single fixed q=2,r=d=3 wreath theorem alone would not
justify the rank-six substitution. No statement about a quotient or a
different finitely presented group is used.

Consequently this same D_6 is finitely presented, surjunctive, and
nonhyperlinear. The new route requires the nonhyperlinear double family,
the surjunctive double family, Steinberg finite presentation, and the
rank-six three-symbol kernel claim. It does NOT combine the bare
existence of some finitely presented surjunctive nonsofic group with
nonhyperlinearity of an unrelated group.

Separately, the original ordinary binary wreath W at q=2,r=d=3 is
surjunctive by `kun-thom-nonsofic-wreaths-are-surjunctive`, using the
finite C_2 lamp, RF actor and exact left-coset action. Combined with
`kun-thom-lamp-wreath-is-not-hyperlinear`, this yields the surjunctive
nonhyperlinear profile for THAT W. No finite presentation of W follows
here; the finitely presented witness in this note is D_6.
