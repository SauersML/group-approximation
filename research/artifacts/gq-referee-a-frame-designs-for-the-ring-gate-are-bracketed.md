## Required wording fixes
- **W1 (the Statement's "It fails the K-budget in cases 2–7").**
  - The inhomogeneous branch of `unit-symmetries-of-a-core-are-graded-or-inessential` does not make `R` fail the
    budget. It shows that the core `A` is finitely generated, that `σ` has finite order on `K_*(A)`, and, by that
    node's item 4, that `A` inherits `K_1` detection and the divisible infinite-order unit class.
  - That is a reduction to the design over `A` without the symmetry, not an exclusion.
  - Say "fails the K-budget in cases 2–6 and in the graded branch of 7; the inhomogeneous branch of 7 reduces the
    design to its core". Add to "What remains": "(g) a design whose core, reached through an inhomogeneous unit
    symmetry, itself escapes".
- **W2 (item 8, `sl_2`-type relations).** `sl2-vacuum-hosts-admit-verma-type-modules` is **OPEN**. It records a
  heuristic no-go with a reduction, and its own text says "A full proof would fix a presentation class and build
  the module".
  - A node that is ESTABLISHED as a conjunction cannot cite it as an exclusion.
  - Mark the sub-bullet as heuristic, or move `sl_2`-type designs to "What remains".
  - The Statement's "In case 8 it fails gate condition 4" should also read "fails gate condition 4 or simplicity",
    matching item 8's own text.
