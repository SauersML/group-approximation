## The question (verbatim, Brown, arXiv:math/0111286, §Questions, Q3; source on MSI)
> "Can a free group factor or a II$_1$ factor with property T contain a weakly dense, QD C$^*$-subalgebra? How about a Popa algebra? Our constructions always give McDuff factors (so we have some place to hide the Popa algebra). Note that amenability can't be an obstruction since $L(G_1 \times G_2) \cong L(G_1) \bar{\otimes} L(G_2)$ and hence many non-amenable groups give McDuff factors and hence contain dense QD subalgebras by Theorem \ref{thm:arbitraryMcDuff}."

Known after 2001: Brown–Dykema (arXiv:math/0207005) construct weakly dense Popa algebras in the interpolated free group factors. The property (T) case is open in both clauses, as far as the bounded search goes.

## T1 (QD clause, every separable II_1 factor). Candidate theorem, elementary
Every separable II_1 factor M contains a weakly dense unital C*-subalgebra isomorphic to the full group C*-algebra C*(F_∞). So it contains a weakly dense residually finite-dimensional, hence QD, C*-subalgebra.

Construction:
1. Take orthogonal projections q_j with τ(q_j) = 2^{-j-1}, and let e_i = 1 − Σ_{j≥i} q_j, so that e_i ↑ 1.
2. Let (ρ_j) list a norming family of finite-dimensional representations of F_∞ (Choi: C*(F_2) is RFD), each repeated infinitely often, with M_{d_j} ⊂ q_jMq_j unital.
3. Put z_i = w_i + Σ_{j≥i} ρ_j(a_i), where w_i ∈ U(e_iMe_i) approximates a 2-norm-dense sequence of unitaries of M.
4. **Injectivity:** q_j reduces z_1, …, z_m for j ≥ m, so ‖π(x)‖ ≥ sup_{j≥m}‖ρ_j(x)‖ = ‖x‖.
5. **Weak density:** z_{i} → y in 2-norm along each subsequence approximating y.

Deliverables: a complete proof; a novelty check at source (Brown–Dykema introduction, Brown's memoir, later citations); a claim node plus route.

## T2 (Popa-algebra clause, property (T), the note's factor). Main target
Does L(G_X) contain a weakly dense Popa algebra (a simple C*-algebra with Popa's local finite-dimensional approximation property)?

Structure to use:
- G_X = ⟨L_y, L_{y'}⟩, generated by two locally finite tower unit groups (rigidity-b), so M = vN(R_y, R_{y'}) with R_y, R_{y'} hyperfinite.
- The LEF models by SL_{3N}(F_2).
- Character rigidity.

Scale analysis to record: by fullness (Connes), approximating projections of a weakly dense Popa algebra in a full factor have trace → 0 (τ ≈ 1 is excluded through relative commutants of the finite-dimensional algebra). So any construction must hide finite-dimensional structure on tiny corners, as Brown–Dykema do in L(F_s).

Routes:
- (a) a Brown–Dykema-type amalgamated free product of matrix algebras mapping onto tower algebras;
- (b) simple hulls of the T1 algebra inside M;
- (c) an obstruction from (T) plus tiny corners.

Deliverable: a theorem or an honest obstruction map.
