# Rank-two symplectic lift of the scalar CCKW family

## 1. Root conventions and the lift

Let R be any commutative unital F7-algebra, with no finiteness or
unit hypotheses on x,y,z. Use the alternating form with
J02=J13=1 and J20=J31=-1. In the simply connected C2 root system,
put alpha=e1+e2, beta=e2-e1, gamma=-2e2. The standard root matrices
are

```
x_alpha(x)=(I+x E03)(I+x E12),
x_beta(y)=(I+y E10)(I-y E23),
x_gamma(z)=I+z E31.
```

Let St(C2,R) denote the group with root-additivity and Chevalley
commutator relations, and pi its natural map to ESp4(R). We use
the lifts A~=X_alpha(x), B~=X_beta(y), C~=X_gamma(z).
The primary reference for the root-group presentation, including
the rank-two B2/C2 commutator relations over arbitrary commutative
rings, is Daniel Allcock, *Steinberg groups as amalgams*,
[author PDF](https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/allcock/research/steinberg.pdf),
Sections 2 and 7 and Lemma 11.1. The proof below uses only those
defining relations, not a centrality or stability theorem.

Root additivity gives X_delta(t)^7=X_delta(7t)=1. Thus all three
power relators hold in St(C2,R).

## 2. Direct verification of the eight commutator relators

Use [u,v]=u^-1 v^-1 u v and left-nested commutators. Signs of the
Chevalley structure constants depend on root parametrizations;
the root containments below do not depend on those signs.

For alpha and beta, the only root of the form i alpha+j beta with
i,j positive is alpha+beta=2e2. Hence

```
[U_alpha,U_beta] <= U_(2e2).
```

The right side commutes with both U_alpha and U_beta because
neither positive combination is a root. This proves
[A~,B~,A~]=[A~,B~,B~]=1.

For gamma and alpha, the roots in the positive span are

```
alpha, gamma, alpha+gamma=e1-e2, 2alpha+gamma=2e1.
```

They form a positive C2 system with simple roots alpha and gamma.
The commutator of the first two root groups lies in the product of
the last two. Both latter root groups commute with U_gamma, since
adding gamma again gives no root. Thus [C~,A~,C~]=1. The root group
U_(2alpha+gamma) is central in the positive subsystem; the only
further nontrivial commutator of a height-two root with a simple
root can land in that height-three root. The group generated by
this positive subsystem therefore has nilpotency class at most
three. Both [C~,A~,A~,C~] and [C~,A~,A~,A~] vanish.

For gamma and beta, use instead the positive system

```
beta, gamma, beta+gamma=-e1-e2, 2beta+gamma=-2e1.
```

Exactly the same root calculation proves [C~,B~,C~]=1 and both
length-four relators. All eleven presentation relators now hold
in St(C2,R), giving an exact lift of G_HB2(7).

This argument already avoids any injectivity assumption. The
alternative positive-unipotent argument is valid too: the
Chevalley relations collect any positive-root word into an ordered
product of the four root groups, and the symplectic matrix entries
uniquely recover their four parameters. Thus each positive
unipotent subgroup maps injectively. No assertion about injectivity
of the entire Steinberg map follows or is needed.

## 3. Central pushouts when the unstable kernel need not be central

Put S=St(C2,R), N=ker(pi), and let Q be the actual matrix image
of K7. Write S_Q=pi^-1(Q). The constructed homomorphism on K7
lands in S_Q. Suppose

```
1 -> D -> F -> Q -> 1
```

is a central extension and there is a homomorphism j:S_Q->F over
Q. Composing the constructed lift with j gives a lift of
K7->Q to F. Its pullback over K7 is therefore split.

For precision, central pushouts are covered even if N is not
central. Given an abelian group D and a homomorphism lambda:N->D
invariant under conjugation by S_Q, form

```
F=(S_Q x D)/{(n,lambda(n)^-1): n in N}.
```

The displayed subgroup is normal by the invariance assumption.
Its intersection with 1 x D is trivial, and the resulting quotient
is a central extension of Q by D receiving S_Q. This construction
includes centralizing N first, by quotienting S_Q by [S_Q,N],
and then pushing its abelian central kernel out. No assertion that
N itself is central is made.

The canonical class c0 has primitive integral evaluation by
`wave3-canonical-k7-class-has-primitive-evaluation`. In particular
c0 modulo m has nonzero evaluation for every m>1. The split
pullbacks above cannot realize any of these classes.

Equivalently, if B_c0 maps to such an F over the specified map to
Q, taking its central generator z to a generator of D, subtract
the exact lifted map on K7. The difference is a homomorphism
B_c0->D, because D is central. It kills z, since z lies in
[B_c0,B_c0]. Therefore the proposed cyclic detected centre is
trivial.

## 4. Scope

This extends the stable linear lifting obstruction to the
rank-two symplectic Steinberg construction for the commutative
scalar family. It requires neither vanishing of symplectic K2
nor centrality of the full unstable kernel. It does not assert
that every central extension of an arbitrary finite image Q
receives S_Q. Such extensions, noncommutative block families in
unstable symplectic settings, and unrelated finite quotients are
not classified by this result.
