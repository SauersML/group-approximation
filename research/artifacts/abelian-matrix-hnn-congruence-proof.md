# One marked congruence quotient for finitely many matrix HNN branches

Let `A_1,...,A_r` be integral `n`-by-`n` matrices with nonzero determinants,
and let `0!=y in Z^n`.  Pick a coordinate `y_j!=0` and set

```text
D = y_j product_(i=1)^r det(A_i).
```

The integer `D` is nonzero.  Choose a prime `p` not dividing `D`.  Then
`y_j mod p` is nonzero, while

```text
det(A_i mod p)=det(A_i) mod p != 0
```

for every `i`.  Hence every reduction `bar A_i` belongs to `GL_n(F_p)`.

Write the additive group `V=(F_p)^n`, and let `L` be the subgroup of the
finite group `GL_n(F_p)` generated by the `bar A_i`.  Use the standard affine
semidirect product with multiplication

```text
(v,A)(w,B)=(v+Aw,AB).
```

Its inverse formula gives

```text
(0,A_i)(bar v,1)(0,A_i)^(-1)=(bar A_i bar v,1).
```

Consequently the assignments

```text
v in Z^n |-> (bar v,1),
t_i        |-> (0,bar A_i)
```

respect `t_i v t_i^(-1)=A_i v` for every `i` and every `v`.  They therefore
define a homomorphism from the presented multiple HNN group to `V rtimes L`.
The target is finite and the marked element has image `(bar y,1)!=1` because
its `j`-th coordinate is nonzero.  This supplies one common marked finite
quotient for all branches.
