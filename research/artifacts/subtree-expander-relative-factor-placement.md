# Archived program note: sibling-factor placement

This note proposed a word-placement theorem for the self-similar sign tape.
It is archived rather than refuted: the proposed placement was never proved,
and its original consumer used it through an invalid independence inference.

The proposed target was a dimension- and depth-uniform exactification on a
large carrier

```text
M_N tensor M_r
```

with one sibling sign close to `M_N tensor I_r`, the other approximately
commuting with fixed expander unitaries on `M_N`, and discarded trace bounded
by relator energy.  Disjoint cylinder support was explicitly recognized as
insufficient, because commuting subalgebras can be perfectly correlated.

The exact analytic theorem
`expander-separated-factors-have-independent-traces` remains active and is
not affected by this archival move.
