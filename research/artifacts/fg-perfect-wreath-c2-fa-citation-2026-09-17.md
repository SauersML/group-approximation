# Import the Cornulier--Kar criterion for property FA of wreath products

Y. Cornulier and A. Kar, *On Property (FA) for wreath products*, J. Group Theory
14 (2011), no. 1; arXiv:1004.2582. Abstract, read verbatim on 2026-09-17 from
arxiv.org/abs/1004.2582:

> "We characterize permutational wreath products with Property (FA). For
> instance, the standard wreath product A wr B of two nontrivial countable groups
> A,B, has Property (FA) if and only if B has Property (FA) and A is a finitely
> generated group with finite abelianisation."

Apply it with `B = C_2`, which has FA because a finite group has bounded orbits,
and with `A` finitely generated and perfect, so that its abelianisation is
trivial. If `A` is trivial, `W = C_2` is finite and has FA directly. `∎`
