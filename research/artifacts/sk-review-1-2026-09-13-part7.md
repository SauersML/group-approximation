**Finite models from orbit points.**
- **F1.** x_{[-ℓ,ℓ]} recurs at arbitrarily large p. Every word of length 2ℓ+1 occurs in some x_{[0,M)}. Take p > max(M,2ℓ) ✓.
- **F2.** D(f)δ_n = f(T^nx)δ_n evaluates at points of X, so D is additive and multiplicative for all f ✓.
- **F3 (covariance).**
  - (P^iD(f)P^{-i})_{nn} = f(T^{(n-i) mod p}x), while D(f∘T^{-i})_{nn} = f(T^{n-i}x).
  - If n-i ∉ [0,p), the two points differ by a shift of p: T^m x versus T^{m+p}x with |m| ≤ |i|.
  - For |t| ≤ ρ we have |m+t| ≤ ℓ, so x_{m+t} = x_{p+m+t} by the return condition. So f agrees at both points when ρ+|i| ≤ ℓ ✓.
- **F4.** For fixed r, r' and large ℓ, π_ℓ(rr') = π_ℓ(r)π_ℓ(r') ✓.
- **F5.**
  - z_{[-ℓ,ℓ]} occurs inside x_{[0,p)} with center n, so f_j(T^nx) = f_j(z) = 1.
  - The P^j with |j| < p/2 have disjoint supports ✓.
- **F6.**
  - F contains I_3 and is closed under inverses, so π(g)π(g^{-1}) = π(I_3) = I_{3p}.
  - Products that stay in F are preserved, and injectivity comes from the differences ✓.
