## 1. An exact six-generator presentation

The marked presentation of B_c0 uses three finite vertex kernels. The first is C7^2 and the other two are Heisenberg groups of order 7^3 and exponent seven. Choose generators u_j=exp(U_j), v_j=exp(V_j), j=0,1,2, and let w_j=[u_j,v_j], using [x,y]=x^-1 y^-1 x y. The vertex relations are

    u_j^7=v_j^7=1, all j;
    w_0=1;
    w_j^7=[w_j,u_j]=[w_j,v_j]=1, j=1,2.

These are exactly the vertex groups, not larger approximating groups. Within each class-two vertex, the corner logarithm proved earlier is

    iU_j-(i^2/2)V_j+(sigma_j i^3/12)[U_j,V_j],
    sigma=(0,-1,1).

The class-two multiplication formula shows that its exact group word is

    c_j(i)=u_j^i v_j^(-i^2/2) w_j^((sigma_j+3)i^3/12).

All exponents here are reduced modulo seven only inside these exponent-seven vertex groups. Thus there is no division by seven and no assumption on the nilpotency class of the eventual target. In ordinary integer exponents one may use

    c_0(i)=u_0^i v_0^(3i^2),
    c_1(i)=u_1^i v_1^(3i^2) w_1^(6i^3),
    c_2(i)=u_2^i v_2^(3i^2) w_2^(5i^3),

with each exponent reduced to 0,...,6. Set C_i=c_0(i)c_1(i)c_2(i).

Eliminate the central generator from the marked equations C_i=z^-1 by z=C_1^-1. In addition to the thirteen vertex relators, impose

    C_i C_1^-1=1, i=2,...,6;
    [C_1,u_j]=[C_1,v_j]=1, j=0,1,2.

This gives six generators and exactly 24 relators, with marked word z=C_1^-1. It is the full integral central extension B_c0, not its reduction modulo z^7. In particular no relation z^7=1 has been inserted.

The derivation uses the exact canonical marking and the vertex-weight formulas from [the marked-presentation proof](hyperbolic-rf-wave4-class-two-2026-09-20.md). It is valid for every group target, including targets of nilpotency class at least seven.

## 2. GAP implementation and calibration

The presentation is implemented in [pquotient.g](../../experiments/hrf-wave5-pquotient-2026-09-20/pquotient.g). It uses GAP 4.15.1 core `PQuotient`, then `EpimorphismQuotientSystem`, without optional packages. The installed core starts with `/tmp/gap-4.15.1/gap --bare`.

The documented parameter is the **lower exponent-seven central class**, or p-class. In the convention P_1(G)=G and P_(n+1)(G)=[P_n(G),G]P_n(G)^7, the returned group is the quotient by P_(c+1). See the installed authoritative documentation `lib/pquot.gd`, sections `PQuotient` and `EpimorphismQuotientSystem`.

For this particular source, however, P_i(B_c0)=gamma_i(B_c0) for every i. Indeed B_c0 is generated by elements of order seven, so its abelianization has exponent seven. The quotient gamma_i/gamma_(i+1) is generated by multilinear commutators of i elements from that abelianization, and therefore also has exponent seven. Thus gamma_i^7 is contained in gamma_(i+1), and induction in the defining formula for P_i proves equality of the two series. Each such lower-central factor is finitely generated and has exponent seven; hence each bounded-class quotient is a finite seven-group. Consequently the completed calculations below are the universal ordinary nilpotent quotients of the stated classes, even if a later quotient has exponent greater than seven. The root lane proposed this observation and this lane independently checked the argument.

The class-five result is order 7^28, with marked z of order seven and central. Its five layer ranks are 4,3,4,6,11. This independently agrees with the complete finite Lie witness, using a group algorithm and the integer-word presentation.
