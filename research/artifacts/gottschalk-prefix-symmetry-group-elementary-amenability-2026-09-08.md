# The current prefix symmetry group is elementary amenable

Date: 2026-09-08. The group generated by the present prefix maps,
finite component permutations, and finite-mask-controlled global
diagonal A_4 actions is elementary amenable. This includes both
Lambda and Lambda_tilde from the invariant scalar-code construction.
Consequently no cellular automaton over these groups can be an
injective nonsurjective map of a finite-alphabet full shift, whatever
its finite memory or local rule.

The conclusion closes this particular source-symmetry construction
as a Gottschalk-counterexample route. It does not classify all
symmetries commuting with Q and F_A, or settle Gottschalk's conjecture.
The previously proved invariant code and nonlinear reversible
full-shift completion remain valid. An injective extension of the
special form x+B Q_s would still be a possible reversible control.

## The group being classified

Use the source from
[the invariant-code construction](gottschalk-balanced-triangular-invariant-scalar-code-construction-2026-09-08.md):

    X=D^N,    D=(F_2^{A_4})^2,    u=(1,1),
    U={u},    Z=D minus {u}.

The finite group A_4 acts diagonally on every component by left
translation on both tracks. Its action on Z is faithful, and Z
contains at least two labels: the pairs (delta_g,0) lie in Z and
detect the regular action. Let Gamma be generated by the following
homeomorphisms of X:

- all permutations of finitely many component positions;
- all H_i, which fix positions below i and apply to the remaining
  tail the prefix substitutions

      UU -> UU,    UZU -> UZ,    UZZ -> ZZ,    Z -> ZU;

- for any gamma in A_4 and any predicate depending on finitely many
  U/Z mask positions, the map applying gamma diagonally to every
  component when the predicate holds, and the identity otherwise.

Each prefix substitution preserves the ordered list of Z labels
and copies the remaining suffix. The source and target prefix lists
partition X, so the reverse substitutions define H_i^-1. A controlled
diagonal action preserves every mask and hence its own control
predicate; its inverse uses gamma^-1 under the same predicate.

The controls here inspect only U/Z masks. They do not inspect the
values of Z labels. This distinction is used in the proof. The
previous controlled involution K is one of these generators, while
unconditional diagonal translations use the constant-true predicate.

## A known orthant group and a direct boundary proof

Let P(m,d) be the group of permutations of m disjoint copies of N^d
which, on a finite partition into axis-aligned boxes, are integer
translations followed by coordinate permutations and a change of
copy. Each interval factor of a box is finite or a ray. Intersecting
partitions with inverse images of partitions verifies closure under
composition and inverse.

This group is elementary amenable. It is a subgroup of the piecewise
Euclidean isometry group of a finite union of lattice orthants: embed
the copies as N^d x {j} in Z^(d+1), and split bounded box coordinates
into finitely many singleton slices. Elementary amenability of the
ambient orthant group is the known theorem of Bieri and Sach,
[Theorem 4.4(i)](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.05788),
published in the
[Journal of the London Mathematical Society](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12503).
The orthant theorem is not a novelty claim of this artifact.

For completeness, the following boundary induction proves the
restricted version just used. For d=0, P(m,0) is finite. For d>0,
each copy has one affine germ on a sufficiently deep corner
[N,infinity)^d: a finite rectangular partition has a unique piece
unbounded in every coordinate. The target copies of these germs
are all distinct, since two translated corners in the same copy
intersect. The germs therefore give a homomorphism to

    (Z^d semidirect S_d)^m semidirect S_m,

whose image is virtually abelian. Its kernel is the increasing
union of groups K_N fixing all m deep corners pointwise. The
complement of these corners is invariant under K_N. Partition it
by the first coordinate i below N and that coordinate's value
a<N. Earlier coordinates range from N upwards and later ones
from zero upwards, giving m d N copies of N^(d-1).

Restriction embeds K_N into P(m d N,d-1). To check the only possible
coordinate issue, refine a map's pieces by their source and target
boundary pieces. Each boundary piece has one deleted constant
coordinate. If the original coordinate permutation matches the
deleted pair, delete them. Otherwise the retained source coordinate
mapping to the deleted target coordinate is constant on the refined
piece; the deleted source coordinate maps to a retained target
coordinate which is also constant. Pair these two retained constant
coordinates by a translation, and retain the remaining permutation.
Thus every refined map has the required form in dimension d-1.

Induction makes every K_N elementary amenable. Increasing unions
and extensions by virtually abelian groups preserve elementary
amenability, proving the assertion for P(m,d).

## A faithful finite-rank restriction for each finite set of generators

Fix finitely many elements of Gamma and expand them as words in
the displayed primitive generators. Include the inverses of those
primitives. Let L bound every component position inspected or moved
by this finite list, counting all three positions of each H_i prefix
test. Choose n>=L+4. The n-th non-u entry then always lies strictly
beyond every position directly edited or inspected by a primitive.

Let X_n be the invariant set of configurations with exactly n
non-u components. There is a bijection

    X_n = N^n x Z^n:

the coordinates g_0,...,g_(n-1) count initial U entries and the U
gaps between successive Z entries; the finite tuple records their
labels. After the last label the configuration is all U.

A fixed finite mask prefix fixes every completed gap, gives a lower
bound on the next gap, and leaves later gaps unrestricted. It thus
describes a rectangular box in gap coordinates. Partition by the
finitely many mask prefixes inspected by a primitive. On each piece,
H_i or H_i^-1 inserts or deletes one U entry and changes one gap by
a constant; a finite component permutation changes the fully
determined early gaps and possibly translates the next gap. A
controlled diagonal action leaves all gaps unchanged. The labels
are permuted and multiplied by one common A_4 element. Since Z^n
is finite, each label tuple can be treated as a separate orthant
copy. Consequently restriction defines a homomorphism

    <the chosen generators> -> P(|Z|^n,n).             (1)

We must prove that (1) is faithful; invariance of X_n alone would
not suffice. On every configuration with at least n non-u entries,
a primitive preserves the first n entries as a set of labeled
entries and changes no gaps after the n-th. Its behavior on those
first n entries depends only on their gaps and labels. Component
permutations cannot reach a later non-u entry, and each U insertion
or deletion occurs before the n-th entry. A controlled diagonal
action multiplies all labels, including the suffix, by one common
element of A_4.

It follows by composition that, for each first-n gap vector, a word
acts on the first n labels by one permutation sigma in S_n and one
common multiplier gamma in A_4. These depend only on the gaps, not
on the labels. All subsequent labels receive that same gamma and
their gaps remain unchanged. This description is identical whether
the configuration has exactly n or more than n non-u entries.

Suppose the word fixes X_n pointwise. It fixes every gap vector.
For each such vector, taking all n labels equal to z, for every
z in Z, forces gamma to fix all of Z, hence gamma=1. Varying one
label between two distinct elements of Z then forces sigma=1.
Thus the word also fixes every configuration with at least n
non-u entries. That set is dense in X, so continuity makes the
word the identity everywhere. This proves faithfulness of (1).

Every finitely generated subgroup of Gamma is therefore elementary
amenable. The integer n depends on the finite generating set; no
single X_n is claimed faithful for all Gamma. Taking the directed
union of these subgroups shows that Gamma itself is elementary
amenable.

## Extension to reversible data-controlled global diagonal actions

The same conclusion holds for the larger group Gamma_data obtained
by allowing any homeomorphism

    z -> gamma(z) z,

where gamma(z) in A_4 depends on finitely many complete component
values. The action is still one global diagonal action; only its
selection may depend on data. Such a map need not commute with Q
or F_A, and that compatibility is not asserted here. Every map of
this form that is an additional Q,F_A symmetry is nevertheless
covered by the group theorem.

Inverse locality is automatic. If gamma depends on a prefix p,
the map sends the entire cylinder [p] bijectively onto
[gamma(p)p]. Bijectivity forces p -> gamma(p)p to permute the
finite prefix alphabet; its inverse selects gamma(p)^-1 from
the output prefix. Thus a finite generating set and its inverses
again have a common bound L and a choice n>=L+4.

Use the n-th non-u entry as a sentinel. It never enters a position
inspected or permuted by a primitive; prefix insertions and
deletions preserve its non-u rank. For fixed gaps and first n-1
labels, every decision made by a word is independent of the
sentinel's initial label. This remains true after earlier global
actions, since their multipliers were themselves independent of
that label. The word's final common diagonal multiplier gamma
is therefore independent of the sentinel.

If the word fixes the exact-n stratum, varying just the sentinel
through Z forces gamma=1. Truncating a configuration after its
n-th non-u entry does not change any decision of the word.
Exact-n triviality therefore fixes its first n entries and gaps;
gamma=1 and unchanged later gaps fix the suffix. Density proves
faithfulness as before. Full-value controls give finite rectangular
partitions after also fixing the finite label tuple, so the same
orthant embedding applies. Directed unions prove Gamma_data
elementary amenable.

## Consequence for cellular automata and the precise remaining scope

Injective cellular automata over finitely generated amenable groups
are surjective, by the Garden-of-Eden theorem of Ceccherini-Silberstein,
Machi, and Scarabotti,
[Amenable groups and cellular automata](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.1686/),
Annales de l'Institut Fourier 49 (1999), 673-685, Theorem 3 and its
corollary. For either Gamma or Gamma_data, any finite memory set
lies in a finitely generated amenable subgroup M. The automaton acts
independently on left M-cosets. A collision for the restricted
M-automaton would give a collision on the whole group; injectivity therefore
makes the restriction surjective by the cited theorem. Choosing a
preimage independently on each coset makes the whole map surjective.

In particular this applies to every full-shift cellular automaton
over Lambda or Lambda_tilde, and to any new coefficient addresses
chosen from Gamma or Gamma_data. Increasing the memory to generate
an infinite subgroup inside either group cannot supply a Gottschalk
counterexample.
The exclusion concerns all finite alphabets and all local rules,
not just linear feedback or the already analyzed finite subgroup.

Gamma is nevertheless not locally finite: for v,w in Z,

    H_0^j(v,w,eta)=(v,U^j,w,eta)   for every j>=0,

so H_0 has infinite order. Infinite prefix dynamics were therefore
real, but do not escape amenability. General source symmetries
which recode component values or create/delete non-u components,
arbitrary Q,F_A centralizers, and constructions using groups
outside Gamma_data are not classified here. No implication that
an unclassified group is nonsurjunctive is asserted.
