**(d) The `PE_N` step.** If `G/C` were finitely presented with `C` finite (hence finitely presented), then `G`

## 2. Leavitt tuples make rational scalar powers elementary

- **`W` and its inverse.** Block shapes `(d,1) × (1,d)` and `(1,d) × (d,1)`. `W W^{-1} = diag(𝒴𝒳, 𝒳𝒴) = I`.
- **The three operations.** They take `W` to `P = [[0,−I_d],[1,0]]`:
  - `row_{d+1} += Σ x_i·row_i` gives `(1, 𝒳)`;
  - `row_i −= y_i·row_{d+1}` gives `(0, −e_i)`;
  - `col_{j+1} −= col_1·x_j` clears the last row.
- **`det P = 1`.** The permutation is a `(d+1)`-cycle, of sign `(−1)^d`, and there are `d` signs. So
  `P ∈ E_{d+1}(Z)`. **Correct.**
- **`W diag(u,I_d) W^{-1} = diag(𝒴u𝒳, 1) = diag(uI_d, 1)`** when `u` commutes with the `y_i`.
- **`D W^{-1} D^{-1} ∈ E`**, since diagonal conjugation preserves elementary matrices. Hence `diag(1, u^{×(d−1)}, 1) ∈ E`.
  **Correct.**
- **Collecting.** Whitehead's formula `diag(b,b^{-1}) ∈ E_2`, which holds over any ring, moves each `u` to position
  1 inside the commuting diagonal matrices. Signed permutations of determinant 1 conjugate `diag(q^{d−1},1,…)`
  around, so `q^{d−1}I_m ∈ E_m(S)` for `m >= d+1`. **Correct.**
- **Consequence.** `(Q^×)^{d−1}·I` is central, lies in the perfect group `E_m(S)` (`m >= 3`), and is not finitely
  generated. By the group lemma of `leavitt-scalar-commutators-block-fp-central-quotients` (passed, c81257d90),
  `PE_m(S)` is not finitely presented for `m >= max(3, d+1)`.
- **Relation to condition 3.** Condition 3 of `q-algebra-with-fp-simple-projective-elementary-group`, "rational
  scalars almost never elementary", fails. This is consistent with, and generalizes, the `d = 2` statement already
  on main for `R_L` (`Λ_N(R_L) = Q^×`).
- **W1 (scope).** State the rank range: `m >= d+1`. For `3 <= m <= d`, the node proves nothing directly.
