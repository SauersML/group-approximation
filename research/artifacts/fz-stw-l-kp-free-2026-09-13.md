## 0. Answers

1. **One model with phase-type loops: impossible.** Lemma N (§1) says no family of loops that fixes a common set P
   of projections, and whose K_1→K_0 values lie in span[P], generates π_1. This covers:
   - every loop fixing the vertex projections of any graph presentation of C (edge phases included);
   - every tensor edge phase on A;
   - any such family conjugated by one fixed isomorphism.

   The six-loop answer escapes only because its loops come from two presentations with different fixed spans
   (K_0 for the σ_j, ℤ[1] for the ρ_i), and ψ is the bridge between them.
2. **No other explicit one-model family was found (§2).** Katsura algebras and k-graphs with k ≥ 3 remain untested
   candidates. Both would cost more Lean than the present pair.
3. **An explicit isomorphism C ≅ A is not available (§2).**
4. **Dadarlat 6.3 (§3).**
   - Generation needs: the homomorphism, Bott periodicity for the codomain, the six values with det L = ±1, and
     injectivity.
   - Surjectivity of Δ is not needed; it follows from generation.
   - Independence needs neither injectivity nor surjectivity.
5. **Removing ψ would not remove Kirchberg–Phillips existence (§4).** [Rev §8(a)] also needs Nonempty(A ≃ D_θ).
   Given that, ψ costs little extra. **Recommendation: keep ψ.** No Lean time should go to one-model loops.
6. **§5 lists the lemma chain R1–R9 with owners.** None of it changes the L plan in RULES-wave3.

## 1. Lemma N (no-go for phase-type loops)

**Setting.** D is unital, K_0D ≅ K_1D ≅ ℤ², and [1] is primitive. This holds for D = C and D = A.

**Definition.** A loop α is *phase-type for P*, where P is a set of projections in ⋃_n M_n(D), if both hold:
- (i) (α_z ⊗ id_n)(p) = p for all p ∈ P and all z ∈ S¹;
- (ii) Δ¹⁰_α(K_1D) ⊆ E_P := span_ℤ{[p] : p ∈ P}.

**Lemma N.** If every loop in a set F is phase-type for the same P, then the classes of F do not generate
π_1(Aut_pn D, id).

**Proof.**
1. Let α ∈ F. By (i), α̂(p) = ι(p) in M_n(C(S¹,D)) for p ∈ P, so Δ⁰¹_α[p] = 0. By additivity, Δ⁰¹_α vanishes on E_P.
   Together with (ii), Δ_α lies in S_P := {(f,g) ∈ T(D) : f(K_1D) ⊆ E_P and g vanishes on the image of E_P}.
2. S_P is a subgroup and Δ is a homomorphism, so Δ(⟨F⟩) ⊆ S_P.
3. Suppose ⟨F⟩ = π_1. Lemma 1 says Δ is onto, so S_P = T(D).
   - Hom(K_1D, K_0D) contains a surjection, so E_P = K_0D.
   - Then every g vanishes on K_0D. But Hom(K_0D/ℤ[1], K_1D) ≅ Hom(ℤ, ℤ²) ≠ 0, a contradiction. ∎

Without the surjectivity half of Lemma 1, the same argument still shows that Δ(⟨F⟩) is a proper subgroup of T(D).

**N1 (graph presentations).** Let D ≅ C\*(E) for a finite graph E with no sinks, and take P = {p_v}.
- Any loop fixing every p_v is phase-type for P. Condition (ii) is automatic, because vertex classes generate
  K_0 C\*(E) = coker(1 − Mᵗ), so E_P = K_0D.
- Such loops have Δ⁰¹ = 0, so they realize only Hom(K_1, K_0).
- Examples: products of edge phases s_e ↦ z^{n_e} s_e, the h_j of [S50 §3], and the edge phases of every graph
  presentation of C obtained by moves.

**N2 (the product presentation of A).** Take P = {p ⊗ q : p, q vertex projections of E(1,2)} ∪ {1}.
- Since [p] = 2e_B and [q] = −e_B [S50 §4], E_P = ℤe = ℤ[1].
- Tensor edge phases α ⊗ id and id ⊗ α′, with α, α′ fixing the vertex projections of B, satisfy (i).
- They also satisfy (ii):
  - K_1A is generated by x = [V ⊗ 1] and y = [1 ⊗ V] (Künneth, [S50 §5]).
  - At x, the loop α ⊗ id has holonomy (α_z(V)V\*) ⊗ 1. By naturality of the Bott class under b ↦ b ⊗ 1, its
    class is Δ¹⁰_α[V] ⊠ e_B ∈ ℤe, because K_0B = ℤe_B.
  - α ⊗ id fixes 1 ⊗ V, so the value at y is 0. The argument for id ⊗ α′ is symmetric.
- So tensor phases realize at most Hom(K_1, ℤe) ⊕ Hom(K_0/ℤ[1], K_1). They miss the two directions x ↦ β and y ↦ β.

**N3 (one conjugator).** If F is phase-type for P on D′ and φ : D′ → D is a *-isomorphism, then φFφ⁻¹ is phase-type
for φ(P). So conjugating a whole family by one fixed isomorphism never helps.

**How the six loops escape.** On A:
- ρ_1 and ρ_2 are phase-type for P_A, with E = ℤ[1];
- the σ_j = ψh_jψ⁻¹ are phase-type for ψ(P_C), with E = K_0.

There is no common P. An explicit one-model answer therefore needs either two phase structures on one algebra with
spans like these, or loops of another kind. We found neither (§2).

## 2. Other one-model candidates, and an explicit C ≅ A

- **(a) Katsura algebras O(A,B).** From memory, not re-verified: K_0 ≅ coker(1−A) ⊕ ker(1−B) and
  K_1 ≅ ker(1−A) ⊕ coker(1−B).
  - Their obvious loops (edge gauge phases, rotations of the vertex unitaries) fix every vertex projection.
  - The ker(1−B) summand of K_0 is not a vertex span, so Δ⁰¹ can be nonzero there.
  - Not settled: whether the K_1→K_0 values of these loops leave span[p_v]. If they do not, Lemma N applies.
  - Lean cost: topological-graph C\*-algebras with their K-theory and UCT. None of this is planned, and it costs more
    than the graph-and-tensor pair.
- **(b) k-graphs with k ≥ 3.**
  - Product classes such as v⊠v⊠v have Bott-type holonomy, so Lemma N need not apply.
  - But B⊗B⊗B has K_* ≅ (ℤ⁴, ℤ⁴). A 3-graph with K_* ≅ (ℤ², ℤ²) would need a spectral-sequence computation.
  - Not pursued.
- **(c) Inner loops Ad(u_z).** Here u_z is a path of unitaries from 1 to a scalar, rescaled to a based loop.
  - u p u\* is unitarily equivalent to p in C(S¹,D), and [u w u\* w\*] = 0 in K_1 C(S¹,D). So Δ = 0.
  - These loops are null-homotopic by Lemma 1 and give nothing.
- **(d) An explicit C ≅ A.**
  - An isomorphism must send the class b to ±β + ke. Here β = v⊠v is a Künneth product class, not a combination
    of vertex classes of A.
  - We know no graph moves between a 1-graph algebra and a product 2-graph algebra.
  - The tensor isomorphisms we know for Kirchberg algebras (O_2⊗O_2 ≅ O_2, O_∞⊗O_∞ ≅ O_∞) are proved by
    intertwining, and we know no explicit formula for them.
  - Verdict: not available, and not worth Lean time.

## 3. Which parts of Dadarlat 6.3 the printed question needs

Let λ_1, …, λ_6 be the six loops of [Rev §8(b)], and let v_j := Δ_{λ_j}.

- **Generation (asked).**
  1. Given [α], write Δ_α = Σ n_j v_j. This needs T(A) identified with ℤ⁶, including Bott surjectivity, and
     det L = ±1.
  2. Then Δ(α · ∏ λ_j^{−n_j}) = 0.
  3. Injectivity gives [α] = ∏[λ_j]^{n_j}.

  Uses R1, R2, R3–R5 and R6.
- **Independence ([Rev §8(b)] adds it; the question does not ask).** Σ n_j v_j = 0 forces n = 0 by det L ≠ 0.
  Uses R1, R3–R5, and only the injectivity of the Bott map on K_0A.
- **Surjectivity of Δ is not needed.** Generation and spanning give it, and π_1 ≅ ℤ⁶ follows as a corollary.

## 4. What removing ψ would save

- [Rev §8(a)] requires both Nonempty(C ≃ A) and Nonempty(A ≃ D_θ).
- A ψ-free answer on A would still need χ : A ≃ D_θ. That brings in:
  - Kirchberg–Phillips existence;
  - KK-lifting (UCT) for A and D_θ;
  - Pimsner–Voiculescu for A_θ.
- Given χ, the marginal cost of ψ is:
  - K-theory of C (l-graph-k, already under way);
  - the Kirchberg property of C (l-kirchberg);
  - KK-lifting for C;
  - one more application of the same existence theorem.
- An existential Challenge over a model avoids χ and ψ. But it needs a UCT predicate, which ko-kk does not provide
  this wave. It is also not faithful to "the" algebra without the uniqueness theorem, which is a literature input.
- **Conclusion: keep ψ.**

## 5. Lemma chain (least Lean), with owners

- **R1. The invariant is a homomorphism** (elementary). For a unital D and a based loop α in Aut_pn D:
  - (a) Δ¹⁰_α : K_1D → K_1(SD) and Δ⁰¹_α : K_0D → K_0(SD) are well-defined group homomorphisms;
  - (b) they depend only on the based homotopy class;
  - (c) Δ_{αβ} = Δ_α + Δ_β for pointwise products (Eckmann–Hilton);
  - (d) Δ⁰¹_α[1] = 0.

  Owners: l-dadarlat (`deltaUnitary`, `circleClass_mul`) and ko-k1.
- **R2. The codomain.**
  - K_0(SD) ≅ K_1D.
  - Bott periodicity β_D : K_0D ≅ K_1(SD), with β_D[q] = [1 + (z−1)q].
  - With R7, T(D) ≅ ℤ⁶.
  - Independence uses only that β_D is injective; generation needs it bijective.

  Owner: ko-bott.
- **R3. The values on C.** Δ¹⁰_{g_ij}[W_k] = δ_ik β_C[p_j], and Δ⁰¹_{g_ij} = 0.
  - Identity (4) has landed (EdgePhase.lean, `edgePhase_W_mul_star_W`, a89a1ab7b).
  - Missing: the Bott class of the loop 1 + (z−1)q, from R2, and Δ⁰¹ = 0 from N1 plus vertex generation
    (l-graph-k).

  Owner: l-certificate.
- **R4. The values on A** [S50 §5 (12)]:
  - for ρ_1: Δ¹⁰(x) = e, Δ¹⁰(y) = 0, Δ⁰¹(β) = y;
  - for ρ_2: Δ¹⁰(y) = e, Δ¹⁰(x) = 0, Δ⁰¹(β) = −x.

  This needs naturality under b ↦ b ⊗ 1 and the graded external-product formula
  Δ⁰¹_{α⊗id}(u⊠v′) = Δ¹⁰_α(u) ⊠ v′ (and the sign for id ⊗ α). It is the least elementary value computation.
  Owners: ko-tensor and l-certificate.
- **R5. Transfer by ψ.** For any *-isomorphism ψ : C → A:
  - conjugation is a homeomorphic group isomorphism Aut_pn C → Aut_pn A, so it induces an isomorphism on π_1;
  - Δ_{ψαψ⁻¹} = ψ_* ∘ Δ_α ∘ ψ_*⁻¹;
  - for every pair of group isomorphisms K_*C → K_*A preserving [1], the six vectors form a ℤ-basis of T(A)
    (det L = 1 uniformly, [S50 §6 (15)–(16)], [Rev §3]).

  So the certificate quantifies over K-theory isomorphisms, not over ψ.
  Owners: l-certificate (integers) and l-dadarlat (continuity of conjugation).
- **R6. Injectivity (the deep step).** Let D be a unital UCT Kirchberg algebra with K_*D free of finite rank. If a
  based loop α has Δ_α = 0, then α is null-homotopic [Dadarlat 2007, Thm 6.3 and Rem 6.4].
  Candidate ingredients, which l-dadarlat should check against the paper:
  - **R6a.** Kirchberg–Phillips uniqueness, parametrized: the homotopy classification of unital
    *-homomorphisms from D into C(X) ⊗ D by KK, for compact metrizable X.
  - **R6b.** The comparison of π_1(Aut D) with loops of unital endomorphisms, and the exact sequence whose kernel
    term is K_0D / {x_*[1] : x ∈ KK(D,D)}.
  - **R6c.** The UCT: that kernel term vanishes, and KK(D, SD) → Hom(K_*D, K_*SD) is injective because Ext¹
    vanishes on free groups.

  Owners: l-dadarlat (R6a, R6b) and ko-kk (R6c). BLOCKER: ko-kk has no UCT predicate this wave.
- **R7. K-theory of the models** [S50 (7), (9)]:
  - K_0C = ℤe_C ⊕ ℤb with [p_2] = 2e_C − b and [p_3] = −e_C; K_1C = ℤ[U_1] ⊕ ℤ[U_2];
  - K_0B = ℤe_B, K_1B = ℤv;
  - K_*A has basis e, β, x, y.

  Owners: l-graph-k and ko-tensor (Künneth).
- **R8. Kirchberg property** of C, A and D_θ. Owner: l-kirchberg (Basic, CuntzCriterion and MinTensorSeparable
  have landed).
- **R9. Existence, [Rev §8(a)].** Nonempty(C ≃ A) and Nonempty(A ≃ D_θ).
  - Kirchberg–Phillips existence, plus KK-lifting for C, A and D_θ.
  - Pimsner–Voiculescu K-theory of A_θ (ko-crossed).
  - The uniqueness step of the intertwining is the case X = point of R6a. l-kp and l-dadarlat should build one
    uniqueness theorem, not two.

  Owner: l-kp.
