# Filtered elimination for the exactly marked K7 cover

20 September 2026. Lane: ghb7_matching.

This proves an isomorphism of the truncated Lie presentations, not just a map to a convenient quotient. It reduces six free generators to four and makes the class-five and class-six computations small enough to finish exactly. The explicit finite detector theorem and its independent structure-constant verifier are maintained by the root lane.

## 1. The two equations and their exact inverse linear part

Work over F7 in nilpotency class C with 2<=C<7. The six free Lie generators are U0,V0,U1,V1,U2,V2. The marked vertex-corner logarithms are

    q_j(i)=i U_j-(i^2/2)V_j+(sigma_j i^3/12)[U_j,V_j],
    sigma=(0,-1,1).

Let R_i=BCH(BCH(q_0(i),q_1(i)),q_2(i)), truncated at class C. The marked presentation requires all six R_i, 1<=i<=6, to agree; their negative is the central mark Z. Put

    E=R_2-R_1, F=R_3-R_1,
    S_U=U0+U1+U2, S_V=V0+V1+V2.

Their linear terms are

    E_lin=S_U-(3/2)S_V,
    F_lin=2S_U-4S_V.

The coefficient matrix in the unknowns (U2,V2) and its inverse are

    A=[[1,-3/2],[2,-4]],  det A=-1,
    A^-1=[[4,-3/2],[2,-1]].

All fractions are in F7. In the free class-C Lie algebra on only U0,V0,U1,V1, initialize

    T_0=-U0-U1, W_0=-V0-V1.

At each step evaluate E,F after substituting U2=T_k,V2=W_k, and simultaneously update

    T_(k+1)=T_k-4E+(3/2)F,
    W_(k+1)=W_k-2E+F.                              (1)

This is the exact formula implemented in the certificate.

## 2. Termination, uniqueness, and the quotient isomorphism

Use the lower-central filtration F^d. Every nonlinear term of E and F has total generator degree at least two. Substituting a change in F^d into such a term changes it only in F^(d+1), since at least one other positive-degree factor remains.

At the initial substitution E,F belong to F^2. If they currently belong to F^d, the correction in (1) belongs to F^d. Its contribution through the linear coefficient matrix is exactly -(E,F). All remaining change has degree at least d+1. Thus every iteration raises the residual filtration degree by at least one. After C-1 iterations, E=F=0 exactly in class C.

The solution is unique in every class-C target Lie algebra with fixed U0,V0,U1,V1. Two solutions have the same linear terms by invertibility of A. If their difference is in F^d, their equations force that difference into F^(d+1). Induction to F^(C+1)=0 proves equality.

Let L6 be the free class-C Lie algebra on six generators, and let J0 be the ideal generated by E,F. The inclusion of the first four generators gives a map L4->L6/J0. Evaluation U2=T_(C-1), V2=W_(C-1) gives a map L6/J0->L4, since the two equations vanish exactly. The composite on L4 is the identity. In L6/J0 the last two generators solve E=F=0 and hence, by uniqueness, equal those same evaluated polynomials in the first four. The other composite is also the identity. Therefore

    L6/ideal(E,F) is isomorphic to L4.             (2)

Applying this isomorphism to any additional relations gives the corresponding exact quotient, with no loss of relations and no hidden choice of a smaller image.

## 3. The remaining marked relations

After substitution, impose the vertex relations

    [U0,V0]=0,
    [U_j,[U_j,V_j]]=[V_j,[U_j,V_j]]=0, j=1,2,

the three remaining face differences R_4-R_1,R_5-R_1,R_6-R_1, and centrality of Z=-R_1 against the four free generators. Centrality against U2 and V2 follows automatically because they are Lie polynomials in those generators. This is exactly the six-generator marked Lie quotient under (2).

The original marking comes from the six face equations r_i=z^-1 in the canonical face-zero extension; see [the marked-presentation proof](hyperbolic-rf-wave4-class-two-2026-09-20.md), Sections 1-3. In particular the mark is not chosen retrospectively from an arbitrary nonzero central element.
