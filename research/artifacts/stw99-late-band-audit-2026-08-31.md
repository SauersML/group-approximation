## Closure audits

### LV: the KMS separation is valid

For irrational `lambda>0`, Kishimoto--Kumjian prove that the crossed product
of the quasifree flow has a unique lower-semicontinuous tracial ray.  The
quasifree KMS inverse temperature is the unique root

```text
exp(-beta)+exp(-lambda beta)=1.
```

The delicate point is cocycle perturbation: a selected KMS state is not
itself cocycle invariant.  The new claim
`stw99-lv-dual-trace-scaling-spectrum-cocycle-invariant` uses the canonical
crossed-product isomorphism for exterior equivalent flows.  It intertwines
the dual actions at the same real parameter, so it preserves the scaling
character on the unique tracial ray.  That character is
`s |-> exp(-beta(lambda)s)`.  Since `beta(lambda)` is strictly decreasing,
cocycle conjugacy forces equal parameters.  This validates the full positive
classification recorded at LV without time rescaling.

The underlying primary sources are Kishimoto--Kumjian, *Simple stably
projectionless C*-algebras arising as crossed products*, Canad. J. Math. 48
(1996), and the standard KMS/dual-weight correspondence.  The classification
argument itself is repository-original as of this audit; it is not promoted
as a literature result.

### LXII and LXXXIX: citation closures

[Szabo, *The uniqueness theorem for Kasparov theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.23029)
states exactly the equivalence requested in LXII for arbitrary separable
`A`, sigma-unital stable `B`, and absorbing Cuntz pairs.  STW v2's April 2026
addendum also records the solution and explicitly notes that it does not
settle LXI.

[Amrutam--Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell, *Strict comparison in
reduced group C*-algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06031) proves strict
comparison for reduced free-group algebras, settling LXXXIX.  It also settles
the free-group part of XCI through selflessness, not the universal XC/XCI
questions.

### XCV: the first assertion really is false

The cleanest certificate is the nuclear range example.  Choose a simple
unital nuclear UCT `Z`-stable algebra `E` with one trace and

```text
(K_0(E),K_0(E)_+,[1])=(Z,N_0,1),    K_1(E)=Z.
```

The simple pure Cu formula gives `Cu(E)=Cu(Z)`: ordinary Cu sees the compact
`K_0` branch and the trace branch, not `K_1`.  The UCT Kunneth formula gives

```text
K_0(E tensor E)=
  (K_0(E) tensor K_0(E)) direct_sum (K_1(E) tensor K_1(E)) = Z^2.
```

If `Cu(E tensor E)` were `Cu(Z)`, compact elements and their Grothendieck
completion would give `K_0(E tensor E)=Z`, a contradiction.  Thus the first
part is unconditionally refuted even in the nuclear class.  This does not
refute purity permanence: the tensor square is itself `Z`-stable and pure.

### XCVI/XCVII: post-STW literature refutations

[Elliott--Li--Niu, *A simple separable C*-algebra which is not singly
generated*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13707), Theorem 1.1, constructs a
simple unital separable AH counterexample.  AH implies nuclear, so XCVII is
also false.  Infinite-dimensional simplicity implies nowhere scatteredness,
so both formulations of XCVI fail.  The source is a v1 preprint dated
2026-08-13; that provenance is retained.

## Highest-impact surviving gates

1. **LXVI:** a witness must be an irregular, non-`Z`-stable, infinite-index,
   noncentral CAR inclusion with unbounded Cu perforation.  Every standard
   trace-preserving crossed/free/HNN construction and every Euler-rigid
   diagonal Rordam tower currently fails.  No proof that all such inclusions
   are impossible is available.
2. **LXXIII:** finite exact square-zero packets cover every requested
   cutdown, and one matrix stabilization fuses them.  The exact remaining
   lemma is support-preserving destabilization, equivalently one orthogonal
   Pedersen-equivalent pair in the original algebra.
3. **XC/XCI:** known results cover rapid-decay acylindrical groups, PHP
   groups, linear C*-simple groups, and large permanence closures.  A
   counterexample must escape those classes and the finite-subgroup ordered
   `K_0` comparison tests.
4. **XCIII/XCIV:** tracial centrality is abundant, but both problems require
   norm-central operator-algebra structure.  Hyperfiniteness alone supplies
   no dimension-free conversion.
5. **XCIX:** transporting central dimension-drop copies incurs a fixed
   `O(sqrt(gamma))` error.  Closedness and common-ultraproduct saturation do
   not reflect absorption to a coordinate.  A uniform error-improvement or
   positive-gap theorem remains the missing step.
