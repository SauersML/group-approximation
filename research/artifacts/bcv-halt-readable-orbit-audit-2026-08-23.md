# 2026-08-23 -- BCV readable-orbit audit

**Status:** proved from Bowen--Chapman--Vidick's explicit perfect-completeness
construction and Cairn's established diagonal extraction of the perfect
ZPC-IRS gap.  This does not prove the readable-freezing claim or a
nonhyperlinear group.

Primary source: Lewis Bowen, Michael Chapman and Thomas Vidick, *The
Aldous--Lyons Conjecture II: Undecidability*, arXiv:2501.00173, especially the
TailoredMIP theorem and the completeness construction in Question Reduction.

## 1. The finite-orbit answer on the HALT branch is yes

BCV effectively map a Turing machine `M` to a finite tailored game `G_M` with

```text
M halts     => G_M has a perfect Z-aligned signed-permutation strategy;
M diverges  => omega*(G_M) < 1/2.                         (1)
```

Let the perfect strategy in the first line act on the finite signed set
`Omega_+-`.  Let `D` be the finite Boolean algebra generated by all readable
diagonal observables, and let `Gamma_U` be the group generated by every
strategy observable, readable or unreadable.  Since

```text
Gamma_U <= Sym_+-(Omega),
```

its image is finite.  Therefore

```text
{u D u^*: u in Gamma_U}
```

is finite.  In particular, the full translate algebra generated by this orbit
is finite dimensional and is normalized by every unreadable generator.

This proves that the BCV HALT completeness strategy meets the finite-orbit
side of `finite-depth-readable-groupoid-closes-iff-full-orbit-is-finite`.  No
special choice among completeness strategies is needed.

## 2. The canonical question-reduction strategy has no invariant SamZ fiber

The finite-orbit statement must not be confused with existence of one fixed
readable atom.  BCV's Question Reduction makes the distinction exact.

The generalized Pauli basis game is tailored as an LCS: all its variables,
including `Pauli_X`, are unreadable.  The sampling apparatus introduces the
`SamZ` variables as readable variables and checks them against `Pauli_Z`.
Starting from an `m`-dimensional perfect strategy, the perfect-completeness
construction acts on

```text
H = C^(F_2^k) tensor C^m
```

and uses

```text
U(Pauli_X(alpha)) = X^alpha tensor I_m,
U(Pauli_Z(beta))  = Z^beta tensor I_m.                    (2)
```

Perfect consistency on the `Pauli_Z-Sample` edge makes the joint readable
`SamZ` projections

```text
E_z = |z><z| tensor I_m,       z in F_2^k.                (3)
```

The Pauli formula gives

```text
(X^alpha tensor I_m) E_z (X^alpha tensor I_m)^*
   = E_(z+alpha).                                        (4)
```

For every nonzero `alpha`, `E_z E_(z+alpha)=0`.  If `p` is a projection with
`p<=E_z` and is invariant under all unreadable generators, it is in particular
invariant under `X^alpha tensor I_m`.  Equation (4) then gives both

```text
p <= E_z,
p <= E_(z+alpha),
```

so `p=0`.  Any global readable assignment atom, if formed, refines a `SamZ`
atom and therefore cannot be a nonzero invariant fiber in this strategy.

The obstruction is not orbit infinitude: the Pauli-X translations permute the
finite set of `2^k` SamZ atoms transitively.  The invariant projection obtained
by summing the orbit is the identity, which forgets rather than freezes the
readable assignment.

## 3. Why neither fact resolves Cairn's perfect gap IRS

Cairn's `perfect-zpc-irs-quantum-gap-game` does not apply the HALT completeness
construction to its final witness.  Its contradiction argument shows that
there is **some non-halting** `M_0` for which

```text
omega_ZIRS(G_(M_0)) = 1,
omega*(G_(M_0)) < 1/2.                                  (5)
```

The value-one IRS is obtained by compactness of the ZPC-IRS strategy space.
The decreasing computable hierarchy uses finite pseudo-IRS polytopes; it does
not provide finite perfect strategies for `G_(M_0)`.  In fact (5) rules out
every finite-dimensional perfect strategy.

The logical branch mismatch is therefore exact:

```text
HALT:    explicit finite ZPC strategy => finite readable orbit;
NONHALT: compactness-selected perfect IRS + quantum gap => no such conclusion.
```

Nor does the existing compactness proof infer orbit finiteness.  It preserves
the fixed clopen cylinder equations explicitly imposed at every pseudo-IRS
level.  The LP contains no named finite translate algebra, no atom labels, and
no uniform orbit-size bound.  Consequently none of its limiting arguments has
finite-orbit structure as a premise or conclusion; adding such structure
would be a new constrained hierarchy, whose non-halting diagonal still has to
be proved feasible at value one.

## 4. Consequence

The BCV paper does not contain the missing readable-freezing theorem in
disguised form.  Its finite completeness strategies do have finite readable
orbits, but those strategies live on the wrong machine branch.  Its explicit
Pauli packet also demonstrates that finite orbit alone need not provide a
nonzero invariant readable atom.

A positive continuation must prove new structure for the particular
maximizing IRS in (5), for example:

1. a dimension-independent bound on the number of atoms in the full readable
   translate algebra, included as closed constraints before the diagonal
   extraction; or
2. a positive-trace joint readable atom fixed by every unreadable generator.

Without one of these additional assertions, finite groupoid closure and
readable freezing do not follow from BCV perfect completeness.
