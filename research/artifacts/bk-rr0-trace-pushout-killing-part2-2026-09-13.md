## 1. Lemma R1 (separating killing quotients)

**Lemma R1.**  Let `A` be separable and nuclear, and `G ⊆ K_0(A)` singular, with
`G = ∪_k G_k` increasing and finitely generated.  Suppose that for every `k`
there are \*-homomorphisms `ρ_{k,j} : A -> D_{k,j}` (`j ∈ N`) into
quasidiagonal algebras with `(ρ_{k,j})_*(G_k) = 0` and `∩_j ker ρ_{k,j} = 0`.
Then some faithful \*-homomorphism of `A` into a quasidiagonal algebra kills `G`.

*Proof.*
1. **Faithful models for one `k`.**  Each `D_{k,j}` has asymptotically
   multiplicative, asymptotically isometric c.c.p. models into matrices.
   Composed with `ρ_{k,j}` they give models of `A` with asymptotic kernel
   `ker ρ_{k,j}`.  If `(ρ_{k,j})_*[e] = (ρ_{k,j})_*[f]`, the equivalence is
   realised by partial isometries in some `M_r(D~)`, so the model ranks of `e` and
   `f` agree from some stage on.  Put `Ψ_n = ⊕_{j ≤ n} ψ^{(j)}_{m(j,n)}` with
   `m(j,n)` large enough that on the first `n` elements of a dense sequence the
   multiplicativity error is below `1/n`, and the ranks agree on the generators
   of `G_k`.
   - Because `⊕_j ρ_{k,j}` is injective, hence isometric,
     `‖Ψ_n(a)‖ -> sup_j ‖ρ_{k,j}(a)‖ = ‖a‖`.
   - So `Ψ` is a faithful `ρ_k : A -> Π M_(K(n)) / ⊕ M_(K(n))`.
   - The ranks of `Ψ_n(e)`, `Ψ_n(f)` agree for large `n`, which gives equivalent
     projections of uniform matrix size, so `(ρ_k)_*(G_k) = 0`.
   - No cancellation is needed anywhere: models are summed only at finite
     stages.
2. **Diagonal over `k`.**  Choosing a stage of `ρ_k` within `1/k` of isometric
   and multiplicative on the first `k` elements gives a faithful
   `ρ : A -> Π M / ⊕ M` killing every `G_k`, hence `G`.
3. **Conclusion.**  `A` is quasidiagonal: it has the separating quasidiagonal
   quotients `A/ker ρ_{1,j}` (`stw07-separating-qd-quotients-force-quasidiagonality`).
   Moutzouris Proposition 3.2 (`moutzouris-k0-killing-is-local`) turns `ρ` into
   a faithful \*-homomorphism into a quasidiagonal algebra killing `G`.  QED

This dissolves failure point 2 of part 1: gluing several trace kernels needs
no product target with cancellation.

## 2. Proposition R2 (trace pushout)

**Proposition R2.**  Let `A` be separable and nuclear with every quotient UCT,
`P ∈ A` a projection, and `σ` an l.s.c. trace with the following properties:
`σ(P) = 1`, finiteness ideal `I_P`, and `σ` annihilates a subgroup `G_0 ⊆ K_0(A)`
on `I_P`, where `G_0 ⊆ im(K_0(I_P) -> K_0(A))`.  Let
`N = {x : σ(x*x) = 0}`.  If `A/I_P` is quasidiagonal, there is a \*-homomorphism
`ρ : A -> D` into a separable quasidiagonal algebra with `ker ρ = N` and
`ρ_*(G_0) = 0`.

*Proof.*
1. **The kernel ideal.**  `N` is a closed ideal: `σ(a*x*xa) = σ(xaa*x*) ≤
   ‖a‖²σ(xx*)`, and lower semicontinuity gives closedness.  `N ⊆ I_P`, and `σ`
   induces a faithful densely finite trace `σ̄` on `I_P/N` with `σ̄(P̄) = 1`.
2. **UCT.**  `I_P/N` is UCT by two-out-of-three in
   `0 -> I_P/N -> A/N -> A/I_P -> 0`.
3. **The subgroup to kill.**  Let `S ⊆ K_0(I_P/N)` be the image of
   `ι_P^{-1}(G_0)`.  Then `σ̄^(S) = 0`, and by naturality `S` contains
   `∂_{A/N}(K_1(A/I_P))`.
4. **Killing `S` on the unital corner.**  The corner `C = P̄(I_P/N)P̄` is unital,
   UCT, with a faithful amenable tracial state `t`.  Put
   `K = Q·t^(K_0(C)) + Q ⊆ R`, a simple divisible dimension group with a unique
   state, and let `B` be the corresponding unital `Q`-stable AF algebra.
   `x ↦ t^(x)` is a positive unital map compatible with the traces and killing
   the image of `S`.  Moutzouris Theorem 4.1 (Schafhauser Cor 5.4, imported in
   `moutzouris-faithful-trace-zero-class-both-signs`) realises it by a unital
   faithful `C -> B`.  Stabilising,
   `θ : I_P/N ↪ C ⊗ K -> B ⊗ K` is injective with `θ_*(S) = 0`.
5. **Pullback extension.**  Let `D_0 = her(θ(I_P/N))`, which is AF.  `θ` is
   nondegenerate into `D_0` and extends to `θ̃ : M(I_P/N) -> M(D_0)`.  Let
   `μ : A/N -> M(I_P/N)` be canonical, and `A'' ⊆ M(D_0) ⊕ A/I_P` the
   C\*-algebra generated by `D_0 ⊕ 0` and `{(θ̃μ(a), π(a))}`.  It is separable,
   `D_0` is an ideal, and the quotient is `A/I_P`.
6. **Faithfulness and zero boundary.**  `a ↦ (θ̃μ(a), π(a))` is injective: its
   kernel lies in `Ann(I_P/N) ∩ I_P/N = 0`.  The map of extensions gives
   `∂_{A''} = θ_* ∘ ∂_{A/N} = 0`, by step 3.
7. **Conclusion.**  Brown–Dadarlat Theorem 3.4
   (`brown-dadarlat-qd-extensions-and-k0-hahn-banach`) makes `A''`
   quasidiagonal.  A class of `G_0` lifts to `ι_P^{-1}(G_0)`, its image lies in
   `S`, and `θ_*` kills it, so it dies in `K_0(A'')`.  QED

## 3. Theorem D (archimedean trace pushouts kill)

**Theorem D.**  Let `A` be separable, nuclear, of real rank zero, with every
quotient UCT, and `G ⊆ K_0(A)` singular.  Suppose that for every finitely
generated `G_0 ⊆ G` and every nonzero projection `Q ∈ A` there are a projection
`P ∈ A` and an l.s.c. trace `σ` such that:
- (H1) `Q ∈ I_P`, `G_0 ⊆ im(K_0(I_P) -> K_0(A))`, `σ(P) = 1`, the finiteness
  ideal of `σ` is `I_P`, `σ` annihilates `G_0` on `I_P`, and `σ(Q) > 0`;
- (H2) `A/I_P` is quasidiagonal.

Then some faithful \*-homomorphism of `A` into a quasidiagonal algebra kills `G`.

*Proof.*  Exhaust `G` by finitely generated `G_k`, and fix `k`.  Take a dense
sequence `(a_i)` in `A_+` and rational `ε > 0`.  Real rank zero gives a nonzero
projection `Q_{i,ε} ∈ her((a_i - 2ε)_+)` whenever that element is nonzero.
Proposition R2 applied to the data of (H1)–(H2) for `(G_k, Q_{i,ε})` gives
`ρ_{k,i,ε}` killing `G_k` with kernel `N ∌ Q_{i,ε}`.

Separation: let `a ∈ A_+` be nonzero, and pick `i, ε` with `‖a - a_i‖ < ε` and
`‖a_i‖ > 3ε`.  Then `(a_i - ε)_+ = d a d*` (Kirchberg–Rørdam), so
`Q_{i,ε} ∈ ideal(a)`.  If `a ∈ N` then `Q_{i,ε} ∈ N`, a contradiction.  So
`∩ ker ρ_{k,·} = 0`, and Lemma R1 concludes.  QED

## 4. What remains for `rr0-projection-traces-give-qd-killing`

- **(H1) is an archimedean condition.**  By Goodearl's max-state formula
  (recalled, not re-read), a state on `(K_0(I_P), [P])` vanishing on
  `S = ι_P^{-1}(G_0)` with `s([Q]) > 0` exists iff
  `inf{m/n : n[Q] ≤ m[P] + s, s ∈ S} > 0`.
  - For exact corners, states come from traces (Moutzouris (2.5)).
  - (H1) fails exactly at projections that are infinitesimal modulo `S` relative
    to every larger projection: the *non-archimedean exhaustions* of
    `bk-rr0-uct-stably-finite-algebras-are-qd`.
  - Part 1 (Theorem A) shows that AF-valued killing maps handle such `Q` only
    through traces normalised at `Q`, whose finiteness ideals miss `G_0`.
- **(H2) is failure point 1.**  For the radical `R` of a counterexample,
  `R/I_P` is the radical of `E/I_P`, and nothing known forces it quasidiagonal.
- **Strength of the target.**  With `G = 0` and `A = E` a real rank zero
  stably finite `E`, the trace condition is Proposition B of part 1 (with
  `K = E`).  So the open claim already contains
  `bk-rr0-uct-stably-finite-algebras-are-qd`, with no radical needed.  A
  counterexample to the claim for a stably finite `A` gives, through the Moutzouris
  Prop 3.2 extension by `⊕ C(T)`, a non-quasidiagonal extension.  Its stable
  finiteness is unverified here.

## 5. Model tests

- **Simple unital real rank zero `A`.**  `I_P = A`, `A/I_P = 0`, and every
  trace is faithful, so (H1) holds iff some tracial state vanishes on `G` —
  Lemma A.  Theorem D recovers the simple case, as in Moutzouris Prop 5.1.
- **AF `A` with lexicographic levels.**  (H1) fails at lower-level `Q`, yet `G`
  is killable (Elliott).  So Theorem D is sufficient, not necessary, and the
  non-archimedean case needs a different mechanism.
- **Toeplitz.**  The trace condition fails, since `T` is not stably finite, and
  Theorem D does not apply.
