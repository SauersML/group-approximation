# Vertex folding, subgroup intervals, and adjoining one involution

2026-09-13. Written proof, not Lean-verified.

Fix an infinite minimal binary subshift `Y` and a point `y in Y`.
For a finite directed graph `D=(V,E)`, with `V` nonempty, use this
same system and point at every vertex in the directed-seam construction.
Every edge retains its own distinct marker. Put

`d=ceil(log_2(2|V|+|E|))`, `ell=2d+4`, `n=3ell`,
`G_D=EL_n(R_D)`, `Q=EL_n(R_Y)`, `L_E=product_(e in E)L_e`.

The previous theorem gives an exact endpoint map

`1 -> L_E -> G_D --pi--> Q^V -> 1`,                  (1)

where `Q` is infinite nonabelian simple Kazhdan LEF and each `L_e`
is infinite simple locally finite. All groups here have no nontrivial
finite quotients. Matrix rank and code length stay fixed throughout.

## 1. Folding vertices gives an embedding, with an exact image

For a partition `P` of `V`, let `D/P` have the blocks of `P` as vertices
and the same labeled edges as `D`, with their endpoints sent to blocks.
There is a one-block factor map

`X_D -> X_(D/P)`

sending `(v,b)` to `([v]_P,b)` and keeping every marker. Its image is
exactly the folded seam system because all endpoints use the same
system and the same chosen point. Coefficient pullback and preservation
of the formal shift unit give an injective unital ring map, hence
an injective homomorphism

`G_(D/P) -> G_D`.

Its image is exactly

`K_P=pi^{-1}(Delta_P(Q))`,                            (2)

where `Delta_P(Q) subset Q^V` consists of tuples constant on each block.
To prove equality, marker cylinders pull back to the same individual
edge-orbit points, so the image contains all of `L_E`. Its endpoint
image is exactly `Delta_P(Q)` by surjectivity of the endpoint map for
the folded graph. Any element of the right side of (2) can therefore
be matched on endpoints by an element of the image; their difference
lies in `L_E`, proving the assertion.

Every `G_(D/P)` is three-generated by torsion elements of orders
`7,2,2`, at the same rank `n`. Use the original width `d` in its binary
code even though the folded alphabet is smaller. The delimiter proof
only requires distinct `d`-bit symbol labels; leading zeros are allowed.
It still gives `R_(coded X)=M_ell(R_(D/P))`, so the universal rank-three
marking applies. The embeddings need not identify the chosen three
generators with one another.

## 2. The entire intermediate-subgroup interval is a partition lattice

Let `H=K_{one block}`. Then

`H=pi^{-1}(diag Q)` is LEF Kazhdan,                    (3)

because the fully folded graph has one vertex and all its edges are
loops. Every subgroup between `H` and `G_D` is exactly one `K_P`.
More blocks give a larger subgroup, so the interval is the order dual
of partitions ordered by refinement. It has Bell-number size `B_|V|`.

Here is the subgroup argument, including possible infinite simple `Q`.
Subgroups in the interval correspond through (1) to subgroups between
`diag Q` and `Q^V`. Induct on the number of coordinates. The image in
all but the last coordinate is, by induction, a product `Q^b` whose
factors are existing diagonal blocks. A subdirect subgroup of `Q^b x Q`
determines normal subgroups `N` and `M` in the two factors and an
isomorphism `Q^b/N ~= Q/M`: two entries represent the same class
exactly when they occur together in that subgroup. This follows by
taking the kernels of its two projections.

Since `Q` is simple, either `M=Q`, giving a new independent coordinate,
or `M=1`. In the latter case `Q^b/N ~= Q`, and normal subgroups of a
finite product of nonabelian simple groups are products of factors.
Thus the last coordinate joins exactly one existing block, possibly
through an automorphism of `Q`. The contained full diagonal forces
that automorphism to fix every `q in Q`, so it is the identity. This
completes the induction. For the normal-product fact, commuting a
normal subgroup with one coordinate isolates its nontrivial component;
simplicity and the trivial center then give precisely a product of
the selected factors.

The subgroup `H` normally generates `G_D`. In the endpoint product,
commuting a diagonal element with an element supported in one coordinate
produces a nontrivial element of that coordinate; simplicity gives
the whole coordinate factor in the normal closure. Repeat at each
vertex, and use `L_E subset H`. If `|V|=2`, there are just two
partitions, so `H` is a maximal proper subgroup of `G_D`.

## 3. MF radicals along all these embeddings

Let `B_P` be the labeled edges not lying on a directed cycle of `D/P`.
The exact graph radical theorem gives

`Rad_MF(K_P)=product_(e in B_P)L_e`.                  (4)

If `P` is coarser than `R`, then `K_P subset K_R` and `B_P subset B_R`:
folding a directed cycle gives a closed walk, so a cyclic edge cannot
become noncyclic under a further fold. The induced map of maximal MF
quotients has kernel

`ker(K_P/Rad_MF(K_P) -> K_R/Rad_MF(K_R))`
`       ~= product_(e in B_R minus B_P)L_e`.          (5)

Indeed every `K_P` contains the whole edge kernel `L_E`, so its
intersection with `Rad_MF(K_R)` is `L_(B_R)`. Formula (5) follows.

Thus this family computes exactly when MF reflection fails to preserve
an embedding. In particular `H` is LEF and contains every edge kernel,
but all restrictions to `H` of MF-target maps defined on `G_D` kill
`L_(B_D)`. Their common kernel in `H` is exactly `L_(B_D)`, since the
maximal MF quotient map of `G_D` separates every other class. MF maps
of `H` itself can retain all of `H`, because `H` is LEF. The distinction
is whether the map extends to the ambient group.

## 4. One involution enlarges H to the whole group

Assume `|V|>=2`. Choose pairwise distinct functions
`f_v in LC(Y,F_2)`. There are arbitrarily many such functions since
`Y` is infinite and zero-dimensional. Extend the function defined as
`f_v` on the tagged endpoint `Y_v` to a locally constant function
`f` on `X_D`. Set

`a=e_(1,ell+1)(f)`.

Its endpoint coordinates `a_v=e_(1,ell+1)(f_v)` in `Q` are pairwise
distinct. It is a nonidentity involution. By the partition classification,
the subgroup `<H,a>` can only correspond to a partition on whose blocks
all the `a_v` are equal. Pairwise distinctness forces the discrete
partition. Therefore

`G_D=<H,a>`, `a^2=1`, `a notin H`.                    (6)

This can be made explicit in the three generators of `G_D`. Choose
`h` so that `Y` has at least `|V|` distinct length-`h` words `A_v`.
Such `h` exists: bounded word complexity would eventually give unique
extensions at every word, forcing a finite periodic system. Take
`f_v` to indicate `A_v`, and on `X_D` let `f` indicate the union of
the tagged words `((v,A_v(0)),..., (v,A_v(h-1)))`.

The binary cylinder compiler realizes each corresponding root in
position `(1,ell+1)`, with length at most `256(ell h)^2`. Their product
is `a`, with total length at most

`256 |V| (ell h)^2`.                                (7)

For the fixed quadratic Sturmian system one can take `h=|V|-1`, since
its length-`h` complexity is `h+1`. Both `H` and `G_D` already have
three torsion generators; (6) describes generation relative to this
particular embedded subgroup, not a claim that four generators are
necessary.

## 5. A maximal LEF subgroup in a just non-MF group

Take two vertices and one directed edge from the first to the second.
Then `G_D` is just non-MF, has exactly five normal subgroups, and
has radical `L=SL_infinity(F_2)`. The subgroup `H` is the folded
one-loop group. It is LEF Kazhdan, has exactly three normal subgroups
`1,L,H`, is maximal proper in `G_D`, and normally generates `G_D`.
Adjoining the involution in (6) gives the whole just non-MF group.

The inclusion induces a noninjective MF-reflection map

`H -> G_D/L ~= Q x Q`,

with kernel exactly `L` and image the diagonal `Q`. Thus the same
infinite simple locally finite group is retained by faithful MF models
of `H` and is killed by every MF-target homomorphism of the ambient
group. All these assertions concern actual subgroup embeddings, not
just quotients obtained by imposing relations.

## Scope

The common endpoint system and common seam point are required for
the stated folding factor. The coefficient field is `F_2`, and the
original rank and code width are preserved for every partition. The
group input is the existing exact graph radical and endpoint theorem;
the new arguments identify the embeddings, classify the subgroup
interval, and prove the one-involution generation statement. No Lean
verification or global priority assertion is claimed.
