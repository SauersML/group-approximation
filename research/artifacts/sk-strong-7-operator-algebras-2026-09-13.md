## 0. Verdict

**Main new statement.** It is proved and unreviewed (queued with sk-verify-7): `separable-factors-hold-countably-many-subshift-groups`.
- A separable II_1 factor contains `G_X` in its unitary group for only countably many `X`.
- So the R^ω-embeddable (T) factors `L(G_X)` fall into continuum many isomorphism classes, each containing only countably many `X`.
- Connes's rigidity conjecture for ICC Kazhdan groups therefore holds for this family modulo countable sets.

**The mechanism** is Ozawa's separability argument (PAMS 2004, Theorem 2). Proved in general in `kazhdan-simple-quotients-countable-in-separable-factors`:
- `Λ` is a Kazhdan group;
- the `N_i` are distinct normal subgroups of `Λ` with simple quotients;
- a separable II_1 factor contains only countably many of the `Λ/N_i` in its unitary group.

**How G_X enters.** Every `G_X` over a fixed alphabet is a simple quotient of the one Kazhdan group `EL_3(F_2⟨τ_+, τ_-, τ_a⟩)` (EJZ). Different subshifts give different kernels, because `e_12` of a cylinder word dies exactly when the word is absent.

**Why it is the right sentence for operator algebraists.**
- Ozawa's theorem used Gromov–Olshanskii groups. Right after the theorem he printed the wish to know whether they embed in `U(R^ω)` (§1.2).
- `G_X` gives a family of simple Kazhdan quotients of one Kazhdan group that is hyperlinear, even LEF, so the argument runs inside the Connes-embeddable world.
- Together with character rigidity (`subshift-elementary-groups-are-character-rigid`, unreviewed): `L(G_X)` is the only II_1 factor generated by a representation of `G_X`, and the map `X ↦ L(G_X)` has countable fibres.

**Not new, and credited.**
- "No separable II_1 factor contains every R^ω-embeddable separable II_1 factor" is due to Nicoara–Popa–Sasyk 2007 (JFA 242, 230–246, doi:10.1016/j.jfa.2006.05.015), as cited by Peterson (§1.4).
- The G_X family is a new witness only, and with simple groups.

## 1. Sources quoted verbatim

### 1.1 Ozawa, *About the QWEP conjecture* (arXiv:math/0306067 source `qwep.tex`)
- **l.1977ff:** "We say a group Γ has the property (F) (the factorization property) if the trace τ on the full C*-algebra C*Γ, defined by τ(s)=δ_{1,s} for s∈Γ, is liftable."
- **l.1765ff:** Theorem \ref{trace} (Kirchberg). The equivalent conditions include "(ii') The trace τ is liftable, i.e., there is a *-homomorphism θ: A → R^ω with a ucp lifting θ̂: A → ∏R such that τ = τ_ω θ."
- **l.2008ff:** "A group Γ with the property (F) is hyperlinear, and to the best of our knowledge, all groups known to be hyperlinear satisfy the property (F)." (Thom 2010 answered this.)
- **l.2138ff:** "Let τ be a trace on the full C*-algebra C*Γ of a group Γ with Kazhdan's property (T). Then, τ is liftable if and only if there is a sequence of *-homomorphisms π_n: C*Γ → M_{k(n)} such that τ(a)=lim_n tr_{k(n)}π_n(a). In particular, a group Γ with the properties (F) and (T) is residually finite."
- **l.2190ff:** "It is unknown whether there exists a simple property (T) group Γ which is hyperlinear. By the above theorem, the full C*-algebra C*Γ of such a group Γ cannot have the LLP."
- **l.1504:** "it seems unknown whether the reduced group C*-algebra C*_red Γ of, say, Γ = SL_3(Z) is QWEP or not."

### 1.2 Ozawa, *There is no separable universal II_1-factor* (arXiv:math/0210411v2 source `gro.tex`; Proc. AMS 132 (2004), no. 2, 487–490, doi:10.1090/S0002-9939-03-07127-2, Crossref)
- **Theorem 2:** "Let Γ and {Γ_α}_{α∈I} be as in Theorem 1 and let M be a separable II_1-factor. Then, the set {α ∈ I : the unitary group U(M) of M contains a subgroup isomorphic to Γ_α} is at most countable."
- **Theorem 1:** "there is a discrete group Γ with Kazhdan's property (T) which has uncountably many pairwise non-isomorphic quotient groups {Γ_α}_{α∈I} all of which are simple and ICC."
- **After Theorem 2:** "The following theorem and its corollary, which was suggested by S. Popa, confirm Connes' conjecture for {Γ_α}_{α∈I} 'modulo countable sets' and solve Problem 4.4.29 in [Sakai], Conjecture 4.5.5 in [Popa] and Problem III.45 in [de la Harpe]."
- **The printed wish:** "In connection with Connes' embedding problem, it would be interesting to know whether all (or at least one of) Γ_α's are embeddable into the unitary group U(R^ω) of the ultrapower R^ω of hyperfinite II_1-factors."
- **Proof, key step:** "Let Δ = {s∈Γ : u_α(s)η=η}. It is easy to see that Δ is a subgroup of Γ and that Δ contains the normal subgroups ker u_α and ker u_β. Since Γ_α and Γ_β are simple and ker u_α and ker u_β are distinct, we actually have Δ=Γ."

### 1.3 Brown, *Tracial invariants, classification and II_1 factor representations of Popa algebras* (arXiv:math/0111286, source `AFDtraces.tex`, §Questions)
- **Q3:** "Can a free group factor or a II$_1$ factor with property T contain a weakly dense, QD C$^*$-subalgebra? How about a Popa algebra?"
- **Q7:** "Can an infinite, simple, discrete group with Kazdan's property T be embed into the unitary group of an $R^{\omega}$-embeddable McDuff factor? (Compare with [robertson] where it is shown that no such embedding exists into the unitary group of $L({\mathbb F}_n)\bar{\otimes} R$ or, more generally, $L(\Gamma)$ for any a-T-amenable discrete group $\Gamma$.)"

### 1.4 Peterson, *On ultraproduct approximations and property (T) factors* (arXiv:2605.16669, source `UltraT.tex`, l.77)
> "Since there is no universal separable factor containing all separable factors that embed into $R^\mathcal U$ \cite{NiPoSa07}, it follows that for every complete theory $\operatorname{Th}(N)$ there are uncountably many pairwise non-isomorphic II$_1$ factors $M$ such that $M \equiv N$. This means that it is also not possible to use separability arguments such as in \cite{Co80, Oz04, NiPoSa07} to exploit rigidity properties such as property (T)."

The primary NPS07 paper was not read; its metadata is Crossref-confirmed.

### 1.5 Chifan–Drimbe–Ioana, *Embedding universality for II_1 factors with property (T)* (arXiv:2205.07442 source, l.188–196)
> "any countable family of separable II$_1$ factors \cite{Oz02} are not embedding universal."

They also show that the II_1 factors generated by representations of rank-one lattices such as those in Sp(n,1) form an embedding-universal family, in contrast with higher-rank lattices (Peterson). This contrasts with character rigidity of `G_X`, whose only II_1 factor representation is `L(G_X)`.

### Checks re-derived in this lane
- **Kazhdan estimate.** `‖ξ − Pξ‖ ≤ κ⁻¹ max_{s∈E}‖π(s)ξ − ξ‖`, by splitting `ξ` along the invariant subspace.
- **Invariant vector.** `π(s) = u_i(s) J u_j(s) J` is a representation, and `‖π(s)1̂ − 1̂‖ = ‖u_i(s) − u_j(s)‖_2`.
- **Stabiliser.** The stabiliser of `η` under `u_i` contains `N_i` and `N_j`. Distinct maximal normal subgroups give `N_iN_j = Λ`.
- **Distinct kernels.** `e_12(r)` is central in `EL_3(R)` only if `r = 0`: compare the `(1,1)` entries of `e_12(r)e_21(1)` and `e_21(1)e_12(r)`. The cylinder element `∏_t τ_-^t τ_{v_t} τ_+^t` maps to `e_{[v]}`, which is `0` iff `v ∉ L(X)`.
- **Model test.** SL_3(Z) has countably many normal subgroups (Margulis), so the lemma is vacuous there, not contradicted.

### Novelty (bounded)
- **Searched:** four web searches and three arXiv sources, namely Ozawa 2004, Chifan–Drimbe–Ioana 2022 and Peterson 2026.
- **Known:** the non-existence of a separable universal R^ω-embeddable factor (NPS07), and continuum many non-elementarily-equivalent full group factors (Peterson 2026, via Boutonnet–Chifan–Ioana).
- **Not found in print:** a continuum of simple hyperlinear Kazhdan groups whose group factors have countable fibres.
- The contribution is the witness family and its link to the note's groups. The argument is Ozawa's.

## 3. Other statements checked (not landed; routine, or owned elsewhere)
1. **Trace simplex.** Characters of `G_X` are exactly tracial states of `C*(G_X)`, so by `subshift-elementary-groups-are-character-rigid` (sk-characters-rigidity-b, unreviewed) the trace space of `C*(G_X)` is the segment `{c·1 + (1−c)τ_λ}`.
   - **Only amenable trace is trivial (§1.1).** Every f.d. representation of `G_X` is trivial: Mal'cev plus simplicity. So every liftable, equivalently amenable, trace on `C*(G_X)` is the trivial character. This needs no character rigidity.
   - **Regular trace is MF.** The note's exact finite models give a group homomorphism `G_X → U(∏_N M_{|Q_N|}/⊕)` through regular representations of `Q_N = SL_{3N}(F_2)`, with limit trace `δ_e`. So `τ_λ` is an MF trace but not amenable.
   - At the trace level this is the same fact as the missing factorization property, which is already in the note (sk-operator-algebras verdict 6).
2. **Kirchberg Cor. 1.2:** no faithful representation `G_X → U(R)`. Already node (b) of `brown-mcduff-witness-for-simple-kazhdan-lef-group`. This is the optional clause the panel referees discussed.
3. **Not QWEP.** `C*(G_X)` is not QWEP: it contains `C*(F_2)` with a conditional expectation (`F_2 ≤ SL_3(F_2[t^{±1}]) ≤ G_X`), and MIP*=RE refutes the QWEP conjecture. `L(G_X)` is QWEP. This is routine for every hyperlinear group containing `F_2`.
4. **No approximation property.** `G_X` fails Haagerup–Kraus AP, since it contains `SL_3(F_2[t])`, a lattice in `SL_3(F_2((t^{-1})))` (Lafforgue–de la Salle, recalled, not re-read). So `C*_r(G_X)` fails the OAP. Routine.

## 4. Open, and not attempted beyond a firewall
- **Brown Q3 for `L(G_X)`.** Does it contain a weakly dense QD C*-subalgebra? Any such algebra must avoid `C*_r(G_X)`, which is not QD by Rosenberg, since QD passes to subalgebras. No construction found.
- **MF of `C*(G_X)` (full).** An MF embedding, restricted to `C*(SL_4(F_q[t^{±1}]))` and built from the note's models, factors through congruence representations (sk-cstar-mf firewall, 79f422ef29). General MF embeddings are not covered.
- **QWEP of `C*_r(G_X)`.** It is the analogue of Ozawa l.1504 for `SL_3(Z)`; AP fails, so Haagerup's trick is unavailable.
- **C*-simplicity of `G_X`.** Open (sk-operator-algebras verdict 10).
