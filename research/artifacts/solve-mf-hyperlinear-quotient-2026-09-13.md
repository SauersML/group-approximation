# Nontrivial hyperlinear quotients of MF groups (2026-09-13)

Target: `mf-implies-hyperlinear`, through part 3 of
`mf-nonhyperlinear-residual-with-amenable-quotient-is-critical`: does every
nontrivial weak-MF group have a nontrivial hyperlinear quotient? The answer
is not settled here. Everything below is elementary. No literature source was
used beyond the repository, and there is no Lean.

## 1. What landed

- `gapped-normal-generators-give-a-hyperlinear-quotient`, with route
  `gapped-normal-generators-hyperlinear-quotient-proof`.
  - Take a finitely generated group normally generated by `a_1, ..., a_m`,
    and a nontrivial corona map in which every `rho(a_i)` has spectrum
    gapped at `1`.
  - Then some generator stays at a fixed positive normalized HS distance
    from `1` in a reblocked operator-norm model.
  - For finite-order normal generators the gap is automatic. Such a group
    has shadow residual equal to the whole group exactly when its MF radical
    is the whole group.
  - A finitely generated critical residual is not normally generated by
    finitely many elements of finite order.
- `hs-invisible-corona-maps-are-full-trace-paddings`, with route
  `hs-invisible-corona-maps-full-trace-padding-proof`.
  - A corona map is HS-invisible exactly when it has an exactly invariant
    projection of full trace.
  - So `R_(infinity->2)(G) = G` means that every corona map is a full-trace
    padding, and so is every remainder in its own trace.
  - Such a `G` has no nontrivial finite-dimensional unitary representation,
    and remainders have unbounded rank wherever an element stays visible.
- A paragraph on the criticality node recording both constraints.

## 2. The tracial questions

1. **Can `||rho(g) - 1||_(2,tau)` vanish for every `g` in every corona map of
   a nontrivial MF group?**
   - **For one map:** yes, by padding (`hs-invisibility-inflation-no-go`).
   - **For all maps:** this is `R_(infinity->2)(G) = G`. Equivalently, every
     MF character is trivial (the definition is in
     `torsion-normal-generator-mf-character-criterion`). Equivalently, every
     corona map is an iterated full-trace padding (new claim).
   - **Excluded when:**
     - `G` has property (T) (`kazhdan-mf-hyperlinear-fragment`);
     - `G` has a nontrivial normal Kazhdan subgroup. By
       `hyperlinear-killed-normal-kazhdan-mf-transfer`, a normal Kazhdan
       `K <= R_(infinity->2)(G)` lies in `Rad_MF(G)`, which is trivial for MF
       `G`;
     - some corona map does not factor augmentation
       (`mf-characterless-corona-image-has-hyperlinear-quotient`);
     - `G` is finitely generated, simple and torsion-marked
       (`simple-torsion-groups-have-a-macroscopic-mf-dichotomy`);
     - `G` is finitely generated and normally generated by finitely many
       elements of finite order (new).
   - **In general:** open.
2. **Traces on `C*(rho(G))` that are not ultralimit traces of `Q_d`.** Not
   used. The GNS image of such a trace need not be Connes-embeddable, so on
   its own it gives no hyperlinear quotient. No source on the tracial state
   space of `Q_d` was pinned, and nothing here depends on one.
3. **Ultralimit traces.** These are the traces that give hyperlinear
   quotients. The new padding claim says that invisibility under all of them
   is the same as an exact full-trace invariant projection.

## 3. Constraints on a critical residual

Let `R` be nontrivial and weak-MF, with no nontrivial hyperlinear quotient.
Every corona map of `R` has a hyperlinear tracial image, so
`R = Res_hyp(R) <= R_(infinity->2)(R)`. Combined with the recorded
constraints, `R`:

- is infinite and perfect;
- has no nontrivial finite or amenable quotient and no proper finite-index
  subgroup;
- has no nontrivial finite normal subgroup and no nontrivial normal
  subgroup with property (T);
- has no nontrivial finite-dimensional unitary representation;
- has only corona maps that are iterated full-trace paddings, with
  unbounded remainder ranks wherever an element stays visible in operator
  norm;
- if finitely generated, has in every nontrivial corona map and for every
  finite normal generating set a generator whose image has spectrum
  accumulating at `1`. In particular it is not normally generated by
  finitely many finite-order elements.

**Candidate screen (not a claim).** Higman's four-generator group is perfect,
torsion-free and fails property (T), since it acts on its Bass--Serre tree
without a fixed point. It has no nontrivial finite quotient. So it passes
the easily checked items. Its MF status was not examined.

## 4. The exact gap: the torsion-free branch

Each known way to see an element tracially needs a supply of projections of
rank comparable to that element's moving rank. The known supplies are:

- averaging over a finite normal subgroup;
- the spectral projection of a finite-order central mark;
- the Kazhdan projection of a normal Kazhdan subgroup;
- the span of conjugated active ranges of gapped normal generators (new).

Now take a torsion-free normal generator whose image has spectrum
accumulating at `1` in every corona map. The lifts then have no finite-rank
subspace outside of which they are exactly the identity. Two quantitative
walls stand in the way.

1. **No unnormalized trace-class control.** Operator-norm defects give none.
   `corner-renormalization-cost` (C3) and `schatten-one-corner-detector`
   convert unnormalized Schatten-one errors into corner HS errors. An
   operator-norm error `eps_n` in dimension `d_n` only bounds the trace norm
   by `eps_n d_n`.
2. **Soft support.**
   - Let `H_n` be the sum of the conjugated squares `|A - 1|^2`.
   - The threshold step `covariant-square-function-has-common-reducing-threshold`
     needs averaged normalized HS commutator bounds on `H_n` relative to
     `tau(H_n)`.
   - In a trace-invisible corona map `tau(H_n) -> 0`, and renormalizing to
     the threshold corner costs `tau(p)^(-1/2)` (`corner-renormalization-cost`
     (C2)). So the ambient commutator bound would have to be
     `o(tau(H_n)^(1/2))`.
   - The weak MF hypothesis provides nothing of that size.

A proof therefore has to supply one of two things:

- for some finite normal generating set, a corona map in which every
  generator's image is gapped at `1`, so that the new claim applies; or
- a soft support whose commutators are controlled at that scale.

Rank counting does not substitute: by part 3 of the padding claim, the
iterated remainders need no minimal element.
