### 11.1 `rf-kazhdan-group-not-flexibly-hs-stable`: PASS

**Property (T) for `Γ_g = Z^(2g) ⋊ Sp_(2g)(Z)`, `g >= 2`.**
- For each symplectic plane, `H_i = Π_i ⋊ SL_2(Z)_i` has relative (T).
- The complement of `H^(Π_i)` carries no `(S_i, κ_i)`-invariant unit vector, and `P_i` commutes with
  `π(H_i)`. So `κ_1 ||ξ - P_i ξ|| <= max_(S_i) ||π(s)ξ - ξ||`.
- The `P_i` are commuting spectral projections of `π|Z^(2g)`, so `||ξ - P_N ξ|| <= Σ_i ||ξ - P_i ξ||`.
- A Kazhdan pair of `Sp_(2g)(Z)` then finishes the argument on `H^(Z^(2g))`.

**Property (T) for the Heisenberg cover.**
- `[x_v, x_w] = z^(2ω(v,w))`.
- `φ(w) = [x_w]` is constant on `Sp_(2g)(Z)`-orbits, and transvections kill the coinvariants.
- `Sp_(2g)(Z)^ab` is finite.
- Apply BdlHV 1.7.11 with `C = <z>`.

**Non-rounding.**
- `σ_k(x)ξ = ρ~_k(x) ξ π_k(q(x))^*` on `M_(d_k × D_k)` is a genuine representation with
  `σ_k(z) = e^(2πi/k) != 1`, so it has no invariant vector.
- For `g in Γ_g`,
  `α_k(g) P_k - P_k π_k(g) = (α_k(g) - P_k π_k(g) P_k^*) P_k - P_k π_k(g)(1 - P_k^* P_k)`.
  The two pieces are HS-orthogonal. The first has norm tending to 0; the second has norm at most
  `√((D_k - d_k)/d_k)`.
- Words propagate the estimate to a Kazhdan set of `Γ~_g`, which gives a contradiction.

**The model.**
- `tr α_k(v,A) -> 0` unless `v ≡ 0` and `A ≡ 1 mod k`.
- The defect is a scalar of size `2π|ω(v,Aw)|/k`.

## 13. ex-weak-sofic-deep-wreath (75b54e248e, bd8c4bc889): `relator-width-over-nilpotent-by-locally-finite-groups`: PASS

  finitely many kernels `M`.
- **Relators inside `M`.**
  - `NM = F`, because `F/NM` is a finite quotient of `G`.
