# The coherent group is an ascending corner extension

2026-09-08. The faithful coherent group has the explicit description

```text
Gamma_triangle ~= < M,t | t A t^(-1)=phi(A), A in M >,
M=EL_3(J),
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,
phi(A)=Q I_3+S A T,             Q=1-ST.                (1)
```

Here `S A T` means multiplication on the left and right by the
scalar matrices `S I_3,T I_3`. The map `phi` is a proper
injective group endomorphism. The head mark is the same element
`x_13(Q)` in both descriptions.

The proof identifies the actual base subgroup as well as the stable
letter. In particular, the base must preserve the nonnegative
coordinate subspace: merely fixing every negative basis vector is
not sufficient. No matrix approximation or non-MF conclusion is
deduced from the ascending extension.

## 1. Faithful unilateral coordinates and the finitary subgroup

We use the identification of `Gamma_triangle` with its literal
bilateral group from
[the faithful-literal calculation](jacobson-coherent-quotient-is-the-faithful-literal-group-2026-09-08.md).
Write

```text
V_Z=directSum_(n in Z) F_2^3,
V_-=span{e_(i,n):n<0},
V_+=span{e_(i,n):n>=0}.
```

On one unilateral coordinate ray, let

```text
S e_n=e_(n+1),
T e_0=0,             T e_n=e_(n-1) for n>=1.
```

Then `TS=1` and `Q=1-ST` is the projection onto level zero.
This representation of `J` is faithful. The elementary proof is
the diagonal-partial-sum argument in
[the shift-faithfulness proof](../jacobson-shift-faithfulness-proof.md):
the words `S^a T^b` span, and the coefficient of `e_(n+d)`
in a finite linear combination applied to `e_n` is a partial
sum along the diagonal `a-b=d`. Consecutive differences recover
every coefficient. The argument works over `F_2` without change.

The same calculation identifies

```text
Q_nm=S^n Q T^m
```

with the matrix unit sending `e_m` to `e_n`. Their span is
the ideal of finite matrices, and its quotient in `J` is

```text
J / M_fin(N,F_2) ~= R=F_2[z,z^(-1)],
S |-> z,             T |-> z^(-1).                    (2)
```

Consequently `M=EL_3(J)` acts faithfully on `V_+`. Extend
each of its elements by identity on `V_-` when viewing it as a
bilateral operator. Denote the finitary linear group supported
entirely in `V_+` by `L_+`.

For clarity, `L_+` is contained in `M`. If two coordinates lie
on different outer rays, their elementary transvection is directly

```text
x_ij(Q_nm),             i!=j.
```

For two distinct levels `n!=m` on the same ray `i`, choose
another ray `j`. Put

```text
X=E_ij S^n Q,             Y=E_ji Q T^m.
```

Then `X^2=Y^2=YX=0`, because `Q T^m S^n Q=0` when
`n!=m`. Thus

```text
[I+X,I+Y]=I+XY=I+E_ii S^n Q T^m.
```

This gives the remaining finitary transvections. They generate
every finite invertible block over `F_2`, hence all of `L_+`.

The symbol map on `M` is therefore exact:

```text
1 -> L_+ -> M -> SL_3(R) -> 1.                        (3)
```

Indeed every elementary coefficient in `R` lifts to `J`, and
`EL_3(R)=SL_3(R)` by the elementary Euclidean reduction proved
in the [Laurent symbol calculation](jacobson-stable-letter-laurent-quotient-and-local-inner-action-2026-09-08.md).
An element with identity symbol differs from identity by finitely
many matrix entries, so belongs to `L_+`; the reverse inclusion
follows from (2).

## 2. The old-generator subgroup is exactly EL_3(J)

Inside the faithful literal group, put

```text
H=<G_Q,G_P,h>.
```

The head root `x_ij,Q` is `x_ij(Q)`, and the tail root
`x_ij,P` is `x_ij(ST)`. Thus both finite groups `G_Q,G_P`
lie in `M`. Their product on each common matrix parameter gives
the constant group:

```text
g_Q g_P=g in GL_3(F_2) <= GL_3(J).
```

The literal mixed involution has the elementary factorization

```text
    [ Q  S  0 ]
h = [ T  0  0 ] = x_12(S)x_21(T)x_12(S).              (4)
    [ 0  0  1 ]
```

The identities `QS=TQ=0`, `TS=1` and `ST+Q=1` verify
this factorization and `h^2=I`. Hence `H<=M`.

Conversely, `H` contains all constant elementary roots and all
head roots. Conjugation using (4) gives

```text
h x_23(1) h=x_13(S),
h x_13(1) h=x_13(Q)x_23(T).                            (5)
```

The two factors on the last right-hand side commute. Cancelling
the known head root therefore supplies `x_23(T)`. Constant
coordinate permutations supply every off-diagonal root with
coefficient `S` or `T`, as well as coefficient `1`.

The set of coefficients available in every off-diagonal position
is closed under addition by root addition, and under multiplication
by the third-index formula

```text
[x_ij(a),x_jk(b)]=x_ik(ab)       for distinct i,j,k.
```

It consequently contains the ring generated by `1,S,T`, namely
all of `J`. Thus `M<=H`, proving

```text
<G_Q,G_P,h>=EL_3(J).                                  (6)
```

This is the short extraction already established in
[the active-cycle generation proof](../binary-jacobson-active-cycle-generation-proof.md),
applied to the current authenticated generators. The existing
finite packets `B,C` are generated by these old generators and
therefore lie in the same base group. No new abstract root
presentation is being assumed in (6).

In particular the head is exactly

```text
w=x_13,Q=x_13(Q),
```

the transvection sending `e_(3,0)` to
`e_(3,0)+e_(1,0)`.

## 3. The corner endomorphism is proper and injective

Define `phi` by (1). For matrices `A,B`, the relations in `J`
give

```text
(Q I_3+S A T)(Q I_3+S B T)=Q I_3+S A B T,
Q I_3+S I_3 T=I_3.
```

Thus it preserves group multiplication and identity. On elementary
generators,

```text
phi(x_ij(a))=x_ij(S a T),                              (7)
```

so it maps `M` into `M`. It is injective because multiplication
on the left by `T I_3` and on the right by `S I_3` recovers
the original matrix:

```text
T phi(A) S=A.                                         (8)
```

Its unilateral action is identity on the entire level-zero block
and is the action of `A` shifted to levels at least one. It
preserves those two summands. Thus `w=x_13(Q)` is not in
its image, since `w` moves a level-zero vector. Hence

```text
phi(M) is a proper subgroup of M.                     (9)
```

Let `T_bil` be the bilateral level shift,
`T_bil e_(i,n)=e_(i,n+1)`. Extending base elements by identity
on negative levels, their actions give the exact identity

```text
T_bil A T_bil^(-1)=phi(A)       for every A in M.       (10)
```

This is the literal action of the stable letter `t`. In particular,
it sends `g_Q` to the level-one copy, `g_P` to its tail starting
at level two, and `h` to the mixed involution with its first swap
removed. These are exactly the seven original stable-letter
relations, extended to all of (6).

The coefficient symbols of `A` and `phi(A)` agree: in (2),
`Q` vanishes and the scalar symbols `z,z^(-1)` cancel.

## 4. The ascending HNN normal form is faithful

Let `P` be the group on the right-hand side of (1), with the
entire group law of `M` included as base relations. Equations
(6) and (10) define a surjective homomorphism

```text
P -> Gamma_triangle.                                 (11)
```

There is a direct normal-form argument proving injectivity; no
faithfulness of a proposed presentation is being inferred merely
from the stable-letter relations.

Every word in `P` can be put in the form

```text
t^(-a) A t^b,              a,b>=0, A in M.             (12)
```

The identities `t A=phi(A)t` and
`A t^(-1)=t^(-1)phi(A)` give this reduction. Explicitly, to
multiply two forms, compare their inner exponents:

```text
(t^(-a) A t^b)(t^(-c) B t^d)

 =t^(-a) [A phi^(b-c)(B)] t^(b-c+d)          if b>=c,

 =t^(-(a+c-b)) [phi^(c-b)(A) B] t^d          if b<c.
```

These formulas, together with forms for the generators and their
inverses, prove (12) for every word.

Now suppose a word in form (12) acts as identity on `V_Z`.
On every sufficiently negative basis vector, the middle element
`A` acts as identity even after the first shift by `b`.
The resulting action on such a vector is translation of its level
by `b-a`. Therefore `b=a`. The literal equality then reduces
to `A=I`, by conjugating with the invertible bilateral shift.
The base representation of `M` is faithful, so this is also the
identity of the abstract base group. Expression (12) is consequently
the identity in `P`.

Thus (11) is injective, proving the isomorphism (1) with the same
stable letter and head mark.

The exponent homomorphism has the explicit kernel

```text
height:Gamma_triangle -> Z,          height(t)=1,

ker(height)=union_(j>=0) t^(-j) M t^j.                 (13)
```

The subgroups in this union increase because `phi(M)<=M`.
Formula (12) proves both inclusions in (13). The union is normal,
the stable letter has infinite order, and therefore

```text
Gamma_triangle ~= (union_(j>=0) t^(-j) M t^j)
                   semidirect <t>.                  (14)
```

This is the split height sequence with quotient `Z`. It does not
assert a splitting of the different quotient sequence by the
finitary group `L`.

## 5. The exact coordinate characterization of the base

Within the faithful literal group one has

```text
M={g in Gamma_triangle : g|V_-=I and g(V_+)=V_+}.       (15)
```

The forward inclusion is its definition as the extended unilateral
group. For the converse, suppose `g` has the two stated block
properties. Its eventual negative action is identity, so its height
is zero by the same tail calculation as in Section 4. The positive
symbol therefore belongs to `SL_3(R)`.

By (3), choose `m in M` with that same positive symbol. The
operator `g m^(-1)` has identity symbol, so it belongs to the
bilateral finitary group `L` by the established symbol-kernel
calculation. Both `g` and `m` fix `V_-` pointwise and preserve
`V_+`. Their finitary difference thus has no nonidentity entries
on the negative block or across its boundary. Hence

```text
g m^(-1) in L_+ <= M,
```

which proves `g in M` and (15).

Pointwise fixing of the negative vectors alone is insufficient.
For example,

```text
y=I+e_(1,-1) epsilon_(1,0)
```

is an element of the bilateral finitary group `L`, has height
zero, and fixes every vector of `V_-` pointwise. But it sends
`e_(1,0)` to `e_(1,0)+e_(1,-1)`, so it does not preserve
`V_+` and does not belong to `M`. This is the cross-boundary
possibility excluded by the second condition in (15).

More generally `t^(-j) M t^j` is the corresponding base after
moving the cut to level `-j`: it fixes lower levels pointwise
and preserves their complementary coordinate subspace. Equation
(13) is a union over these cuts, not an assertion that its entire
kernel equals the stage-zero base.

## 6. Scope

This gives an exact ascending HNN description of the already
faithful literal group, with the explicit proper corner embedding
and the same nontrivial head. Its proof uses only elementary ring
identities, finitary transvections, the established symbol sequence,
and the bilateral normal form.

It does not turn the proper unilateral corner into a proper corner
of a finite complex matrix algebra. In particular the displayed
relations on ring coefficients do not supply a reconstruction of
those coefficients from an arbitrary unitary group representation.
The remaining non-MF question is still the positivity of the
operator-norm matrix criterion `gamma_triangle`; none of (1)--(15)
establishes that positivity.
