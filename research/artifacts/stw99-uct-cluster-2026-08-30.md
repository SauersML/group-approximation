Ingestion of the UCT section of arXiv:2506.10902 (Section: The UCT).

* II: the UCT problem.  Positive classes: type I, ASH, amenable groupoid
  algebras (Tu; Barlak--Li twisted), Dadarlat's local-UCT permanence.
  Reductions: quotient-permanence suffices; T-crossed products suffice
  (Kirchberg); Z/p and Z/q crossed products for coprime p, q suffice;
  Kirchberg algebras suffice, and the problem is equivalent to "every
  Kirchberg algebra with trivial K-theory is O_2".  Non-nuclear failure:
  Skandalis, C*_r of biexact Kazhdan groups.
* III: the R^ω-UCT problem, KK(A, R^ω) ≅ Hom(K_0(A), R) for nuclear A.
  Holds for finite strongly self-absorbing A (R^ω is separably D-stable
  as a quotient of D_ω; Dadarlat--Winter); the engine of Schafhauser's
  2024 KK-classification of finite strongly self-absorbing algebras.
  This is the exact UCT-residue of the classification/TWW machinery over
  the hyperfinite ultrapower, sibling to the session's X(2) lane.
* IV: the Künneth problem for tensor products; a priori weaker than the
  UCT, and genuinely weaker non-nuclearly (Skandalis's UCT-failures
  satisfy Künneth).
* V: Hausdorffness of KK(A, B) for separably Q-stable B; implied by the
  UCT (divisible target + continuity of α), and at B = R^ω by III.
  Carrión--Schafhauser's UCT-free KL-continuity is the neighbouring
  unconditional result.

Routes wired: II ⟹ III, II ⟹ IV, II ⟹ V.  The strongly self-absorbing
concentration of II is Problems XIII/XIV
(stw99-problem-xiv2-ssa-embeds-in-q-oinfty and the finite part).
