# Torsion-free exact MF radical over `Z`: breakthrough round 2

Date: 2026-08-26

## Executive status

The requested existence theorem is **not yet proved**.  The strongest complete
construction remains the Titz--Mite--Witzel direct product

```text
G=K x Z,
```

where `K` is finitely presented, torsion-free, simple, nontrivial, Kazhdan,
normally generated by one element, and

```text
Rad_MF(G)=K,                 G/K ~= Z.
```

This gives every requested clause except soficity (and hence the requested
route to hyperlinearity).  Soficity of `G` is equivalent to soficity of `K`.

Round 1 reduced that single missing clause to a finite permutation problem on
two variables `(e,u)`, one exact involution equation, four approximate
equations, and the separating word `u^8`.  Round 2 makes two kinds of genuine
progress:

1. it converts the opaque terminal equations into an involution-sandwich
   normal form and proves sharp quantitative constraints on every possible
   solution; and
2. it proves that *every* successful exact Kazhdan radical kernel must be
   sofic but non-LEF, eliminating all finite marked-limit constructions.

No scalable positive permutation family was found.  The surviving route must
use genuinely approximate, non-LEF models with nonabelian fibre monodromy.

## 1. Exact involution-sandwich normal form

Retain the derived words `h,a,x,c,f,d,s,rho` from the two-generator gate and
define

```text
beta=rho e rho^(-1),                 p=beta a.
```

The Titz--Witzel group has the exact presentation

```text
<e,u | e^2,
       c^2,
       rho^2 e u^(-1),
       beta a u d^(-1),
       p u p c>.
```

Thus the four approximate conditions have the transparent reading

```text
c^2=1,           u=rho^2 e,           d=p u,           c=p u p.
```

The last relation may equivalently be replaced by

```text
d^2 u^(-1)c=1,
```

so the last two couplings are the square-root chain

```text
d=p u,                         d^2=c u.
```

The words `h,a,f,s,beta` are exact conjugates of `e`, hence exact
involutions whenever `e^2=1`; `p` is a product of two exact matchings.  The
Tietze replacements are quantitatively equivalent in every bi-invariant
metric, with explicit defect bounds in
`titz-witzel-two-generator-involution-sandwich-normal-form`.

This is an exact theorem about the presentation and its asymptotic models,
not a positive sofic construction.

## 2. Sharp density constraints

Let

```text
delta_c=d(c^2,1),                    kappa=d([u,e],1).
```

Using only `e^2=1` and the first derived square gives

```text
d(u^8,1) <= delta_c+6 kappa,
d(u^8,1) <= delta_c+2 d(e,1).
```

Therefore, if the mark has limiting support `epsilon>0` while the square
defect vanishes, then

```text
limsup d_H([u,e],1) >= epsilon/6,
limsup d_H(e,1)     >= epsilon/2.
```

The second estimate improves the earlier three-generator occurrence bound
from coefficient `296` to coefficient `2`.

There is also a hidden-square constraint.  Put

```text
eta=d(u^2e,eu^2).
```

Then

```text
d(u^8,1) <= delta_c+6 eta.
```

Thus `e` must macroscopically fail to commute with both `u` and `u^2`.

Finally use the sandwich variables and put

```text
eta_J=d(pupc,1),          theta=d(p^2,1)=d([beta,a],1).
```

Then

```text
d(u^2,1) <= delta_c+2 eta_J+2 theta,
d(u^8,1) <= 4 delta_c+8 eta_J+8 theta.
```

A mark of limiting support `epsilon` therefore forces

```text
limsup d_H([beta,a],1) >= epsilon/8,
limsup d_H(beta,a)     >= epsilon/16.
```

Here `beta` and `a` are conjugate placements of the same matching `e`.
Consequently a positive model needs macroscopic support and two separate
macroscopic failures of commutation; a sparse or nearly block-diagonal
matching cannot work.

## 3. Rigorous exclusions of structured permutation models

### Cyclic normalizers

Suppose, blockwise, that `e` normalizes the cyclic group generated by `u`.
On one block write

```text
e u e=u^q,                       q^2=1.
```

Three gate relators reduce to

```text
R_rho=u^(-6-7q)e,       R_b=u^(-3-9q),       R_a=u^(-9)e.
```

An exact exponent identity in `Z[q]/(q^2-1)` yields

```text
d_H(u^8,1)
 <=2 d_H(R_rho,1)+2 d_H(R_b,1)+2 d_H(R_a,1).
```

Hence cyclic, dihedral and affine-normalizer models cannot solve the gate.
The block-quotient version says that a wreath/block lift over such a quotient
can work only if the mark is asymptotically invisible on the quotient and is
carried inside the fibres.

### Central abelian voltage lifts

In the standard voltage lift over the exact `C_2` quotient, both generators
flip the base and translate an abelian fibre.  Then `u^2` is a uniform central
fibre translation.  The hidden-square inequality specializes to

```text
d_H(u^8,1) <= d_H(c^2,1),
```

so the model collapses.  A viable fibre construction must therefore be
nonabelian and have genuinely noncentral square monodromy.

## 5. A global theorem: the kernel cannot be LEF

Assume

```text
Rad_MF(K semidirectProduct_phi Z)=K x {0}
```

for a nontrivial property-`(T)` group `K`.  The existing finite-residual
theorem forces `K` to have no nontrivial finite quotient.

On the other hand:

1. LEF implies operator-MF;
2. operator-MF implies weak quasidiagonality; and
3. Dadarlat, arXiv:2007.12655v2, Proposition 3.19 (attributed there to
   Ozawa--Thom), says that every infinite weakly quasidiagonal Kazhdan group
   has an infinite residually finite quotient.

An infinite residually finite quotient has a nontrivial finite quotient.  A
finite nontrivial `K` is itself a finite quotient.  Thus a LEF Kazhdan group
with no nontrivial finite quotient is trivial.

Therefore every nontrivial kernel for the exact-radical target is

```text
sofic but non-LEF, property (T), with no nontrivial finite quotient.
```

This categorically eliminates alternating-group marked limits and every
other route through exact finite local models.  It also explains why the
terminal Titz models must be genuinely approximate rather than repairable to
finite actions.

## 6. Current literature boundary

The primary-source sweep through August 2026 found no theorem closing the
remaining interface.

* Alekseev--Thom, arXiv:2608.05362, prove that a Kazhdan group with a sofic
  embedding whose Loeb centralizer acts ergodically is LEF; a finitely
  presented such group is residually finite.  Their Open Problem 6.1 asks
  whether finitely presented sofic property-`(T)` non-residually-finite
  groups exist.
* Titz Mite--Witzel, arXiv:2509.05054v2, remains the strongest viable
  finitely presented, torsion-free, simple Kazhdan source.  Its soficity is
  not known.
* Recent torsion-free simple/Tarski Kazhdan constructions do not prove
  soficity and do not supply the required finite automorphic presentation.
* Fournier-Facio, arXiv:2608.02025v2, supplies torsion-free nonsofic groups,
  which points in the opposite approximation direction.

## 7. What remains

There are now two honest surviving possibilities.

1. Construct a scalable finite-permutation solution of the Titz gate using
   nonabelian fibre monodromy.  It must evade every density and normalizer
   obstruction above.
2. Find a different torsion-free, sofic-but-non-LEF Kazhdan kernel with no
   finite quotients and a finite automorphic presentation plus a saturating
   cyclic compression defect.

The first is currently the cleaner route because all other target clauses
are already proved for the Titz direct product.  Neither route is completed
in this round.
