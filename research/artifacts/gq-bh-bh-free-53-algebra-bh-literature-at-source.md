# Algebra BH: the Bokut–Belyaev lineage, read at source (bh-free-53, 2026-09-18)

Strategy 2(m) of `research/artifacts/gq-bh-synthesis-master-route.md`. PDFs were fetched on MSI
and read page by page; e-prints were read as TeX. Content is paraphrased, not quoted at length.

## Read at source

1. **L. A. Bokut, "Embeddings into simple associative algebras"**, Algebra i Logika 15:2 (1976)
   117–142; transl. Algebra and Logic 15 (1976) 73–90, DOI 10.1007/BF01877233. Pages 73–74 read.
   - *Theorem 1:* over a countable field, an algebra `A` with `|A| <= |K_1 * K_2 * K_3|` embeds in
     a simple algebra generated by `K_1, K_2, K_3`.
   - *Theorem 1':* the general-field version, with a dimension condition.
   - *Corollary:* every countable algebra embeds in a 3-generated simple algebra.
   - *Theorems 2 and 3:* simple algebras that are sums of four subalgebras with condition (*),
     or of three nilpotent subalgebras.
   - The method is Shirshov's composition (Gröbner–Shirshov) lemma. **Nothing about finite
     presentation or word problems.**
2. **L. A. Bokut, "Embedding of rings"**, Uspekhi Mat. Nauk 42:4 (1987) 87–111; transl. Russian
   Math. Surveys 42:4 (1987) 105–138, DOI 10.1070/RM1987v042n04ABEH001443. §5.1 (pp. 126–127)
   and §5.5 (pp. 129–130) read.
   - *§5.1, simple envelopes:*
     - every algebra embeds in a simple one (Bokut [11]), proved by transfinite induction from a
       composition-lemma step;
     - Cohn's theorem for domains;
     - the Bokut 1976 theorems above.
   - *§5.5, Higman-type theorems:*
     - Higman and Murskii for groups and semigroups;
     - Bokut's recursively presented Lie algebras with unsolvable word problem inside finitely
       presented ones;
     - Belyaev's Theorem 10: every recursively presented associative algebra over a finitely
       generated field or commutative ring embeds in a finitely presented algebra. It is
       proved from Murskii via Lemma 2, where all relations are semigroup relations except one
       of the form `α + β = γ`;
     - Kukin: the Lie version in characteristic `≠ 2`;
     - Baumslag: metabelian Lie and associative algebras;
     - Dicks: HNN extensions of rings.
   - **The survey states no theorem that combines simplicity with finite presentation, and none
     that preserves the word problem.** So algebra BH was not a theorem in 1987.
3. **V. Ya. Belyaev (1978)** was already read at source on main
   (`belyaev-associative-higman-theorem`, artifact `belyaev-1978-decidability-analysis-2026-09-13.md`).
   It is Higman's theorem only, with no control of the word problem.
4. **T. Lescure, arXiv:2603.26222 (2026)**, e-print read. It gives a `KH` exact sequence for
   relative algebraic Cuntz–Pimsner rings (`lescure-kh-exact-sequence-for-algebraic-cuntz-pimsner-rings`).
   This is the modern tool relevant to gate G3r, not to gate A1.

## Not read

- **Evans–Mandelberg–Neff 1975**, Studies in Logic 80, 259–277,
  DOI 10.1016/S0049-237X(08)71952-9. The ScienceDirect page served a script shell with no PDF
  from MSI. The BBMZ survey lists it as a Boone–Higman-*type* theorem: simple and inside a
  finitely presented algebra. It is not a finitely presented simple envelope.
- **arXiv searches** (MSI, 2026-09-18) for finitely presented simple associative envelopes of
  algebras with solvable word problem returned nothing relevant. The Alahmadi–Alsulami–Jain–Zelmanov
  embedding papers (arXiv:1703.08734, 1806.00629) concern radical, nil and primitive algebras and
  Morita classes, going by their abstracts.

## Verdict for gate A1

The classical lineage splits the two properties:
- Bokut gives simplicity with finite generation;
- Belyaev gives finite presentation without simplicity;
- EMN give simplicity inside a finitely presented algebra.

No classical theorem removes gate A1, and gate A1 is open as stated on main
(`algebra-boone-higman-conjecture`). The repository's own finitely presented simple envelopes
(Attempts 6–10 there) cover only low-complexity inputs.

## Connection to gate G3r (landed with this artifact)

- `binary-self-similar-completions-over-free-bases-have-zero-kh`: every binary completion
  `O_ψ(K<X>)`, i.e. every finitely presented host of bh-algebra's self-similar route, has
  `KH = 0`. For those hosts, gate G3r is exactly a nil-term question.
- `group-algebras-embed-in-shell-essential-algebras`: `K[P] ⊆ A_nu/J`, which is central simple and
  contains a Leavitt pair. Gate A1 for group algebras is then finite presentation of one `A_nu/J`.
