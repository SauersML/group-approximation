## 1. (a) Theorem NH, step 2

The proof of local annihilation (part 1 §1) uses only four facts:
- every `f ∈ A_k(𝒢)` is a finite combination of indicators of compact open bisections (the definition, for every
  ample `𝒢`);
- the restriction identity `χ_W f χ_U = f · 1_{r∈W, s∈U}` for units `W, U`, which holds because `χ_W, χ_U` are
  supported on units;
- the unit space is compact, infinite, Hausdorff and zero-dimensional, so `s(B_j)` is clopen and the separating clopens
  exist;
- compactness, to refine to a clopen partition.

Hausdorffness of `𝒢` itself is never used. So `LocalAnnihilation A` holds, and with `IsSimpleRing A` (unital simple
`k`-algebra) the Lean criterion applies.
- Infinite: `[e_12(a−b), e_23(1)] = e_13(a−b)`. Checked.
- (T): gated on finite `k` and finitely generated `A`. Checked.
- Subsumption: it generalizes the reviewed Hausdorff theorem (part 1), which `distinct_from` names. No conflict.

**The computation.**

- The remaining equations are consistent.

## 3. (c) Theorem B

- **(B1).** "Let $f = \sum_{D\in F}c_D1_{D}$ … we may assume each element of $F$ is of the form
  $\Theta((\alpha, g,\beta), C(\beta\eta))$" (l.2512–2513), so these indicators span.
  - The germ `βηw ↦ α(g·η)(g|_η · w)` factors as `s_(α′) u_h s_(β′)^*`, with `α′ = α(g·η)`, `h = g|_η` and
    `β′ = βη`.
  - `u_g u_h = u_(gh)`.
  - So the ring is generated by the eight elements plus a generator of `F_q`, and EJZ gives (T).
- **(B2).** `s_x^* s_y = δ_xy`, `s_x s_x^* = 1_(C(x))`, and `C(0) ⊔ C(1) = X^ω`. This is a `CompleteMatrixFamily`
  (`right = s^*`, `left = s`).
- **(B3).** `R_q` is a nontrivial countable `F_q`-algebra with a binary family, so the reviewed, axiom-clean input
  (part 6 §1) makes `EL_n(R_q)` nonsofic.
- **(B4).** `ts = s_0^*s_0 = 1`, `1 − st = s_1s_1^*`, and `s_1^*(s_1s_1^*)s_1 = 1`, so the complement is full. The
  rank-two theorem applies.
- **(B5).** `q` odd, so the ring is simple (Theorem A). Then Theorem NH gives `S` infinite and simple, (B1) gives (T),
  and MF quotients of `S` compose to `EL_n(R_q)` and are trivial.
- **(B6).** `q` even.
  - `I = 𝒮(𝒢)` is a nonzero proper two-sided ideal (`prop:singularideal`; `1` is not singular).
  - `E = E_n(R_q,I)` is normal and noncentral.
  - If `EZ = EL_n(R_q)`, reduction mod `I` would send `EL_n(R_q)` onto the abelian image of `Z`. But `EL_n(R_q/I)` is
    nontrivial and perfect for `n ≥ 3`. So `EZ/Z` is a proper nontrivial normal subgroup.
- **(B7).** A germ `[g,w]` is a unit only if `g` fixes a neighbourhood of `w`, so a faithful action gives injectivity of
  `g ↦ u_g`. Checked.
- **Scope.** The node correctly does not claim nonsoficity of `S = EL_n/Z`. The corner trick of part 6 §3 needs
  `Z ⊆ k^× I`, and the scalar-centre theorem is only reviewed for Hausdorff groupoids.
- **Displays recomputed:** the six equations, the germ factorization, the family relations, and `s_1^*(s_1s_1^*)s_1 = 1`.
