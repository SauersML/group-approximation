## 1. Cohn compressor: multiplication order is correct

Work in
`C=Z<s,a,t,b | ts=ba=1, ta=bs=0>` and put `e=1-st`.
Then `e^2=e`, `es=te=0`, `ea=a`, `be=b`, and `bea=1`.
The equality `e=ab` is neither true by definition nor used.

In the ordered coordinates `(i,4)`, the four elementary factors of
`u_i` have successive products

```text
[1 0; t-1 1] [1 1; 0 1] = [1 1; t-1 t],
[1 1; t-1 t] [1 0; s-1 1] = [s 1; 0 t],
[s 1; 0 t] [1 -t; 0 1] = [s e; 0 t].
```

The middle bottom-left entry is `(t-1)+t(s-1)=ts-1=0`.
Its inverse is `[t 0; e s]`, on both sides, using only `ts=1`,
`e+st=1`, `es=te=0`. Therefore multiplying in the specified order
`u=u_3 u_2 u_1` gives the displayed upper-triangular u, and the
reverse inverse product gives its displayed inverse. In particular,
elementary membership is established before any conjugation argument.

For `i,j<=3`, column i of u is supported at row i with coefficient
s, and row j of `u^-1` is supported at column j with coefficient t.
It follows directly that
`u x_ij(r)u^-1=x_ij(srt)`. No coefficient is commuted past s or t.

The opposite-root identity used for the auxiliary diagonal elements
is valid over arbitrary associative rings:

```text
yx=0  implies
[x_41(x),x_14(y)]=diag(1,1,1,1+xy).
```

All extra terms in the commutator contain `yx`; moreover `(xy)^2=0`.
The three substitutions are `(x,y)=(a,t^3),(s^3,b),(e,t)`, with
reverse products zero. Thus `k_+,k_-,c` are elementary and centralize
`L=diag(EL_3(C),1)`.

To check their conjugates, let V be column 4 of u and W row 4 of
`u^-1`. In their original order the needed products are

```text
Va=(a,0,0,0)^T,       t^3 W=(0,0,0,1),
Vs^3=(0,0,0,1)^T,    bW=(b,0,0,0).
```

These give `u k_+u^-1=x_14(a)` and `u k_-u^-1=x_41(b)`.
For the third identity, both `uc` and `x_12(e)u` equal
`u+et E_14`; hence `ucu^-1=x_12(e)`. This is particularly useful
because it avoids any accidental cancellation of a noncommutative
coefficient in the outer-product expression.

## 2. The full ambient group really is generated by compressors

Let `J=<L,u,k_+,k_->`. The previous conjugates lie in J. For every
`r in C`, the Steinberg root identities give

```text
[x_21(rb),x_14(a)] = x_24((rb)a) = x_24(r),
[x_41(b),x_12(ar)] = x_42(b(ar)) = x_42(r).
```

Both coefficients are in the correct order: only `ba=1` is used.
The remaining mixed roots follow explicitly from

```text
[x_12(1),x_24(r)]=x_14(r),
[x_32(1),x_24(r)]=x_34(r),
[x_42(r),x_21(1)]=x_41(r),
[x_42(r),x_23(1)]=x_43(r).
```

Together with L these are every elementary root in rank four, so
`J=EL_4(C)`. The three extra generators u, k_+, k_- each conjugate
L into itself; the last two actually centralize it. Thus the precise
finite-compressor generation premise is met, not merely a weaker
one-compressor containment.

C is a nonzero finitely generated unital associative ring: binary
prefix creation and partial deletion on the free abelian group on
finite binary words realize its relations. EJZ therefore supplies
property (T) for both `EL_4(C)` and `EL_3(C)`. Their discrete
finite generation is valid; no property (T) of rank two is used.

## 3. The killed defect normally generates the Cohn source

For any homomorphism into the unitary group of a specified tracial
matrix ultraproduct, all-trace normalization makes the image of
`ucu^-1=x_12(e)` commute with the image of L. In particular it
commutes with `x_23(1)`, killing `d=x_13(e)`.

In its normal closure, the following two commutators have three
distinct indices at each step:

```text
[x_41(b),x_13(e)]=x_43(be),
[x_43(be),x_32(a)]=x_42((be)a)=x_42(1).
```

Elementary signed permutation matrices conjugate this root to every
unit-coefficient root up to sign. Inversion removes the sign, and
`[x_ij(1),x_jk(r)]=x_ik(r)` gives arbitrary coefficients. Thus the
normal closure is the whole source group. This proves triviality of
all its ultraproduct representations, not only noninjectivity or the
failure of a chosen canonical trace.

Every image of this countable group in a hyperlinear group is a
countable hyperlinear subgroup. Composing with its ultraproduct
embedding proves the corresponding full-hyperlinear-radical statement.
This uses the ordinary subgroup characterization of hyperlinearity,
not an MF-to-hyperlinear transfer.

## 4. Fullness and the corner embedding need no completeness relation

Suppose `R!=0`, `ts=1`, and
`1=sum_(j=0)^(m-1) alpha_j e beta_j`, with `e=1-st`.
Here `m>=1`. For all nonnegative i,j,

```text
e t^i s^j e = delta_(i,j) e.
```

For unequal exponents, cancellation of matched adjacent t,s pairs
leaves either `e s^k=0` or `t^k e=0`. Consequently the proposed

```text
v_0=s^m,  w_0=t^m,
v_1=sum_j s^j e beta_j,
w_1=sum_j alpha_j e t^j
```

satisfy `w_i v_j=delta_(i,j)`. In `w_1v_1`, the surviving term is
`alpha_j e beta_j`, with no reversal or commuting of its coefficients.
The cross terms vanish since `m-j>0`. Hence there is the asserted
unital Cohn-ring map; it need not be injective.

For the four length-two pairs the order of the deletion words is
reversed, exactly as required: the word `v_i v_j` has left inverse
`w_j w_i`. Thus the listed `T_i S_j=delta_(i,j)` are correct.
With `p=sum_i S_i T_i`, let
`Phi(A)=sum_(i,j) S_i A_ij T_j`. Then

```text
Phi(A)Phi(B)=Phi(AB),
pPhi(A)=Phi(A)p=Phi(A),
T_i(1-p)=0=(1-p)S_j.
```

These identities prove that `j(A)=1-p+Phi(A)` is a multiplicative
unital embedding of the unit group `GL_4(R)` into `R^x`, with recovery
`T_i j(A) S_j=A_ij`. The scalar 1 is not confused with p.
For an off-diagonal elementary root, `T_j S_i r=0`, so the same
opposite-root commutator calculation proves

```text
diag(j(x_ij(r)),1)
 =[x_12(S_i r),x_21(T_j)] in EL_2(R).
```

Thus the required rank-two group embedding holds without either
`p=1` or an isomorphism `M_4(R)=R`.

## 5. Rank-two normal generation, including conjugation back

Assume only `wv=ba=1` and `bv=0`, and set
`D=diag(1+vb,1)`. Then `(vb)^2=0`, D is elementary by the
opposite-root identity, and

```text
[D,x_12(ar)]=x_12(vr).
```

If N is its normal closure, this puts `x_12(vR)` in N. Set
`f=1-vw`, `x=vf`, `y=fw`, `q=vfw`, `c_0=1-f-q`, and
`z=x+y+c_0`. The elementary relations give

```text
f^2=f, q^2=q, fq=qf=0,
x^2=y^2=0, xy=q, yx=f,
c_0 x=x c_0=c_0 y=y c_0=0,
c_0^2=c_0.
```

For example `q^2=vf(wv)fw=vfw=q` and
`yx=f(wv)f=f`; all cancellations preserve order. Therefore
`z^2=q+f+c_0=1` and `zf=vf`.
The explicit six-transvection factorization

```text
diag(z,z^-1)
 =x_12(z)x_21(-z^-1)x_12(z)x_12(-1)x_21(1)x_12(-1)
```

puts `h=diag(z,z)` in `EL_2(R)`. For every r,

```text
h x_12(fr) h^-1=x_12(zfrz)=x_12(vfrz) in N,
```

because `frz` is just another element of R and `x_12(vR)` is already
in N. **Conjugating back** by h now gives `x_12(fr) in N`; that
membership was not assumed in taking the displayed conjugate.
Finally `r=vwr+fr` and additivity of the root subgroup yield every
`x_12(r) in N`. A rank-two elementary Weyl matrix gives every lower
root `x_21(r)`. Thus N is the entire `EL_2(R)`.

Apply this with `(v,w,a,b)=(S_1,T_1,S_2,T_2)`. The normal generator
is precisely the image of the universal source root `x_12(1)` under
`EL_4(C)->EL_4(R)->EL_2(R)`. Every map to a hyperlinear group kills
that element by Section 3, so it kills all of `EL_2(R)`. The two-
coordinate embeddings of `EL_2(R)` contain every elementary root
in each larger rank. This proves the full-radical claim for all n>=2.

## 6. Consequence and exclusion checks

The corner embedding puts the nontrivial group `EL_4(R)` inside
`R^x`; the root with coefficient `1_R` is nonidentity. The established
trivial-image statement for `EL_4(R)` therefore makes `R^x`
nonhyperlinear. It does not make every homomorphism of `R^x` trivial.

For a simple unital non-directly-finite ring, the complementary defect
is nonzero and hence full. For `L_k(1,m)`, m>=2, its second pair gives
`t_2(1-s_1t_1)s_2=1`. Thus the stated specializations meet the actual
fullness hypothesis, with countability as in the canonical claim.

No source-ring simplicity, field hypothesis, characteristic-two
identity, exhaustive two-copy decomposition, or Kazhdan property of
the arbitrary target `EL_2(R)` entered the deduction. The two
canonical routes' named premises suffice. This review does not claim
a fresh Lean certification, a transitive axiom audit, an unconditional
proof independent of Liu–Thom normalization, or validation of unrelated
claims in the broader comparison report.
