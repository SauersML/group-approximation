# Semidihedral and GL_2(F_3) blocks add no lifted-trace positivity case

Lane `w6-wf-stage1-b`, 2026-09-12. Target: stage one of the weakly finite reflection ideal on the ternary
anti-central summand, through `anti-central-lifted-trace-is-relatively-positive`. Mathematics on paper.

## Summary

- **Theorem 1.2.** The anti-central block of `F_3[GL_2(F_3)]` has two simple modules, `V` and `V (x) det`. Their
  projective covers are induced from the two semidihedral blocks. So `H_fin(GL_2(F_3)) = H_fin(SD)`, and every state
  from Corollary 3.2 of the forced-states artifact equals the lifted trace on `GL_2(F_3)`. This corrects the last
  clause of Remark 3.3 there: `GL_2(F_3)` carries no split beyond `t`.
- **Theorem 2.1.** The lifted trace is nonnegative on `(H_fin(H) + H_fin(GL_2(F_3))) ∩ K_0^+` iff it is nonnegative on
  `H_fin(H) ∩ K_0^+`. So a trace-lowering stable relation among projectives over `E x| V`, `SD` and `GL_2(F_3)` exists
  iff one exists over `E x| V` alone. **Proposition 2.2:** `E x| V` and `GL_2(F_3)` generate `G`.
- **Proposition 3.1.** The transpose-twisted evaluation `pi' = pi o alpha_*` kills `c_1`, as `pi` kills `c_2`, and
  `(pi, pi')` maps `S_-` onto `R x R`. So neither block is full, and `eps_- S_- (+) X ~= (c_i S_-)^m` never holds.
- No one-sided pair is constructed (Section 4).

Sources: `hadamard-spectral-conjugacy-and-forced-states-2026-09-12.md` (the forced-states artifact: Remark 2.3,
Proposition 3.1, Corollary 3.2, Remark 3.3) and `lifted-trace-state-extension-2026-09-12.md` (the extension artifact:
Section 0, Theorem 2.2).

## 0. Notation

- `R`, `G`, `z`, `eps_-`, `S_-`, `u = [eps_-]`, `H = E x| V`, `alpha(g) = (g^*)^(-1)`, `trl` and `H_fin(L)` are as in
  Section 0 of both sources. `pi : S_- -> R`, `[g] eps_- -> g`, is the evaluation, and `pi' = pi o alpha_*`.
- Modules are right modules. For a finite `K <= G` with `z in K`, the class of an idempotent matrix `p` over
  `eps_- F_3[K]` is the class of `p F_3[G]^n = (p F_3[K]^n) (x)_(F_3[K]) F_3[G]`. So for `K <= L` the map
  `K_0(eps_- F_3[K]) -> K_0(S_-)` factors through induction to `L`, and `trl(Ind M) = trl(M)` because
  `dim Ind M = [L : K] dim M`.
- `GL = GL_2(F_3)`, the constant units `sum s_i a_ij t_j`. `V = F_3^2` (row vectors) is its natural module, `det` its
  determinant character, and `V' = V (x) det`.
- `SD = <w, d, h> <= GL` has order sixteen. As in Remark 2.3 of the forced-states artifact, `c_2 = -eps_-(1 + [wh] + [dh])`,
  `c_1 = eps_- - c_2`, and `eps_- F_3[SD] = B_1 x B_2`. `B_1` carries the evaluation `rho_1`, and `B_2` its twist
  `rho_2 = rho_1 (x) chi` by the sign character `chi` with kernel `<w, d>`.
- `q_i` is a primitive idempotent of `B_i ~= M_2(F_3)` and `Q_i = q_i F_3[SD]` is the simple `B_i`-module. So
  `[c_i] = 2[q_i]` and `trl(q_i) = 2 . 2/16 = 1/4`. For a state `s`, `t = s(c_1)`.
- `p_+ = x^2 + x - 1` and `p_- = x^2 - x - 1`. Over `F_3`, `p_+ p_- = x^4 - 3x^2 + 1 = x^4 + 1`.

## 1. The projectives of GL_2(F_3) are induced from SD

**Lemma 1.1 (spectral blocks).** Let `k_+, k_-` be the idempotents of `F_3[x]/(x^4 + 1)` with `k_+ = 1` modulo `p_+`,
`k_+ = 0` modulo `p_-`, and `k_+ + k_- = 1`. Then `c_1 = k_+([hw]) eps_-` and `c_2 = k_-([hw]) eps_-`.

*Proof.*
- *A polynomial in `hw`.* `(hw)^4 = z` and `[z] eps_- = -eps_-`, so `y = [hw] eps_-` satisfies `y^4 + 1 = 0`. `p_+` and
  `p_-` are coprime (`p_+ - p_- = 2x` and `p_+(0) = -1`), so `k_+(y)` and `k_-(y)` are complementary idempotents.
- *Central.* `h^(-1) = zh`, so `(hw)^(-1) = z wh`, and `w (hw) w^(-1) = wh = z (hw)^(-1) = (hw)^3`. The only involution
  of the cyclic group `<hw>` of order eight is `z`, so `w` is not in it and `SD = <hw, w>`. So every element of `SD`
  conjugates `hw` to `hw` or `(hw)^3`.
  - `x -> x^3` is an automorphism `sigma` of `F_3[x]/(x^4 + 1)`, as `x^8 = 1`.
  - Modulo `p_+`: `x^2 = 1 - x`, `x^3 = -x - 1` and `x^6 = x - 1`, so `p_+(x^3) = -3 = 0`. So `sigma` preserves the
    maximal ideal `(p_+)`, hence also `(p_-)`, and fixes `k_+`.
  - So `g k_+(y) g^(-1) = k_+(y)` for every `g in SD`.
- *Which block.* `rho_1(hw) = [[1,1],[-1,1]]` has characteristic polynomial `p_+`, so `k_+(rho_1(hw)) = I`. Also
  `rho_2(hw) = chi(h) rho_1(hw) = -rho_1(hw)` satisfies `p_+(-x) = p_-(x)`, so `k_+(rho_2(hw)) = 0`. A central
  idempotent of `B_1 x B_2` that is `1` on `B_1` and `0` on `B_2` is `c_1`. QED

**Theorem 1.2.**
1. `V|_SD ~= Q_1` and `V'|_SD ~= Q_2`.
2. `V` and `V'` are the only simple modules of `eps_- F_3[GL]`. Their projective covers are `P(V) = Ind_SD^GL Q_1` and
   `P(V') = Ind_SD^GL Q_2`, of dimension six.
3. `K_0(eps_- F_3[GL])` is free on `[P(V)]` and `[P(V')]`. Induction `K_0(eps_- F_3[SD]) -> K_0(eps_- F_3[GL])` is an
   isomorphism, with `[q_1] -> [P(V)]` and `[q_2] -> [P(V')]`. All four classes have lifted trace `1/4`.
4. `H_fin(GL) = H_fin(SD)`, generated by `[q_1]` and `[q_2]`. Every state `s` has `s([q_1]) = t/2` and
   `s([q_2]) = (1 - t)/2`.
5. `alpha_*[q_1] = [q_2]`. So every `alpha_*`-invariant state, in particular every state of Corollary 3.2 of the
   forced-states artifact, equals `trl` on `H_fin(GL)`.

*Proof.*
- *Part 1.* `V|_SD` is `rho_1`. On `V'`, `hw` acts by `det(hw) rho_1(hw) = -rho_1(hw)`, since `det h = -2 = 1` and
  `det w = -1`. That matrix satisfies `p_-`, so `c_1` acts by `0` (Lemma 1.1), and `z` acts by `det(-I) . (-I) = -I`. So
  `V'|_SD` is a two-dimensional `B_2`-module, which is `Q_2`.
- *Part 2.* `F_3[SD]` is semisimple, so `Q_1` is projective and `Ind Q_1` is a projective `eps_- F_3[GL]`-module of
  dimension `3 . 2 = 6`. Frobenius reciprocity gives `Hom_GL(Ind Q_1, V) = Hom_SD(Q_1, Q_1) = F_3`, so `P(V)` is a
  direct summand of `Ind Q_1`.
  - A projective `F_3[GL]`-module is free over the Sylow subgroup `<n>`, `n = [[1,1],[0,1]]`, so its dimension is
    divisible by three. `V` itself is not projective.
  - If `dim P(V) = 3`, the kernel of `P(V) -> V` is a one-dimensional module on which `z` acts by `-1`. But every
    character `lambda` of `GL` has `lambda(z) = lambda(wd)^2 = 1`, as `(wd)^2 = -I`.
  - So `P(V) = Ind Q_1`, and likewise `P(V') = Ind Q_2`.
  - `V` and `V'` are absolutely simple, because their restrictions are. So the regular module `eps_- F_3[GL]` contains
    `P(V)^2 (+) P(V')^2`, of dimension `24 = dim eps_- F_3[GL]`, and there is no other simple module.
- *Part 3.* `K_0` of a finite-dimensional algebra is free on the classes of its indecomposable projectives
  (Krull–Schmidt), and `q_i F_3[GL] = Ind Q_i`. `trl(P(V)) = 2 . 6/48 = 1/4 = trl(q_1)`.
- *Part 4.* A finite `K <= GL` containing `z` contributes classes that factor through `K_0(eps_- F_3[GL])`, which is
  the image of `K_0(eps_- F_3[SD])` by part 3. Then `[c_i] = 2[q_i]` and `s(c_1) + s(c_2) = s(u) = 1`.
- *Part 5.* `alpha` is transpose-inverse on constants, so it preserves `GL` and `SD`, and `alpha_*(c_1) = c_2`
  (Proposition 3.1(4) of the forced-states artifact). So `alpha_*` maps the primitive idempotent `q_1` of `B_1` to a
  primitive idempotent of `B_2`, which is equivalent to `q_2`. An invariant state has `s([q_1]) = s([q_2])`, and the two
  values add to `1/2`. QED

**Remark 1.3 (Remark 3.3 of the forced-states artifact).** The unipotent `n` is not conjugate into `H` (Remark 3.3
there), but it adds no class: `H_fin(GL) = H_fin(SD)`, the only free datum on `GL` is `t`, and Corollary 3.2 normalizes
it. The clause "its modular blocks carry further unforced splits" should read "its modular blocks carry the same split
`t`". In the other direction, `<w, d>` and the quaternion group `<wd, h>` induce `[q_1] + [q_2]`, since both blocks
restrict to their natural two-dimensional module. So neither sees `t`.

## 2. Relative positivity with SD and GL_2(F_3) reduces to H

**Theorem 2.1.** Put `L_1 = H_fin(H) + H_fin(GL)`, a subgroup of `H_fin(G)` containing `u`. Then `trl >= 0` on
`L_1 ∩ K_0^+` iff `trl >= 0` on `H_fin(H) ∩ K_0^+`. Equivalently, the following are equivalent:
1. there are finite direct sums `P` and `Q` of projectives induced from finite subgroups of `H` or of `GL_2(F_3)` (each
   containing `z`), a finitely generated projective `X` and `r >= 0` with `P (+) S_-^r ~= Q (+) X (+) S_-^r` and
   `trl(P) < trl(Q)`;
2. the same with `P` and `Q` induced from finite subgroups of `H` only. By Theorem 2.2(4) of the extension artifact,
   this is `ternary-anti-invariant-swap-corner-is-full`.

*Proof.*
- `trl` is additive on `H_fin(G)` (Theorem 2.2(1) of the extension artifact), and `H_fin(H) <= L_1`. So positivity on
  `L_1` implies positivity on `H_fin(H)`.
- Suppose `trl >= 0` on `H_fin(H) ∩ K_0^+`. Theorem 2.2(3) there gives a state, and Corollary 3.2 of the forced-states
  artifact gives an `alpha_*`-invariant state `s`.
- `s = trl` on `H_fin(H)` (`anti-central-k0-states-equal-lifted-trace-on-signed-thompson`, part 2 with `Gamma = G`) and
  on `H_fin(GL)` (Theorem 1.2(5)). So `s = trl` on `L_1`, and `trl >= 0` on `L_1 ∩ K_0^+`.
- *Equivalent form.* As in the proof of Theorem 2.2(4) there, an `x in L_1 ∩ K_0^+` with `trl(x) < 0` is
  `[P] - [Q] = [X]`, with `P` and `Q` collecting the positive and negative coefficients, and equality in `K_0` is the
  stable isomorphism. Conjugate idempotents have equal classes, so conjugates of `GL` add nothing. QED

**Proposition 2.2.** `<H, GL> = G`.

*Proof.* For a word `mu`, `s_mu = s_(mu_1) ... s_(mu_k)` and `t_mu = t_(mu_k) ... t_(mu_1)`, so `t_mu s_mu = 1`. Put
`n = 1 + s0 t1 = [[1,1],[0,1]]` in `GL`, `tau = 1 + s_00 t_00` in `E` (the reflection `1 - 2P` on the cylinder `00`,
as `-2 = 1`), and `v = s_00 t_00 + s_01 t_10 + s_10 t_01 + s_11 t_11` in `V`.
- `tau s0 = s0 + s_00 t0` and `t1 tau = t1`, so `tau n tau = 1 + N` with `N = s0 t1 + s_00 t_10`. Each product of two
  terms of `N` contains `t1 s0 = 0`, so `N^2 = 0` and `n (tau n tau)^(-1) = (1 + s0 t1)(1 - N) = 1 - s_00 t_10`.
- `v = v^(-1)`, `v s_00 = s_00` and `t_10 v = t_01`. So `v (1 - s_00 t_10) v^(-1) = 1 - s_00 t_01 = x^(-1)` with
  `x = 1 + s_00 t_01`.
- `G = <V, x>` (`leavitt-prime-field-units-generated-by-v-and-one-transvection`). QED

**Remark 2.3 (the third option of the lane).** Every finite subgroup of the group generated by `SD`, `GL_2(F_3)` and
`E x| V` is a finite subgroup of `G`. So relative positivity on all their projectives is positivity on `H_fin(G)`, which
by Theorem 2.2(2) of the extension artifact is the extension of the whole lifted trace to a state (Remark 2.3 there).
That implies `anti-central-lifted-trace-is-relatively-positive`; it is not a partial case of it. The provable partial
case is Theorem 2.1.

## 3. The twisted evaluation, and blocks that are not full

**Proposition 3.1.**
1. `pi(c_1) = 1`, `pi(c_2) = 0`, `pi'(c_1) = 0` and `pi'(c_2) = 1`.
2. `(pi, pi') : S_- -> R x R` is a surjective unital homomorphism.
3. If an idempotent matrix `p` over `S_-` has `pi(p) = 0` or `pi'(p) = 0`, its entries generate a proper two-sided
   ideal. So no idempotent matrix over `B_1` alone or over `B_2` alone is full, and `eps_- S_- (+) X ~= (c_i S_-)^m`
   has no solution for any `m >= 1` and projective `X`.
4. `pi' = pi` on `F_3[H] eps_-`. So part 3 says nothing about idempotents over `H`, such as `e_-`.

*Proof.*
- *Part 1.* `w + d = h`, so `1 + wh + dh` evaluates to `I + h^2 = 0`. So `pi(c_2) = 0` and `pi(c_1) = pi(eps_-) = 1`.
  `alpha_*` fixes `eps_-` and exchanges `c_1` and `c_2` (Proposition 3.1(4) of the forced-states artifact), which gives
  the values of `pi'`.
- *Part 2.* `pi` is onto: `R ~= M_2(R)` by `x -> (t_i x s_j)`, and every `2 x 2` matrix over a ring is a sum of four
  invertible ones,

  ```text
  [[a,b],[c,d]] = [[a,1],[-1,0]] + [[0,1],[-1,d]] + [[1,b-2],[0,1]] + [[-1,0],[c+2,-1]],
  ```

  with inverses `[[0,-1],[1,a]]`, `[[d,-1],[1,0]]` and two unitriangular matrices. So `G` spans `R`. `alpha_*` is an
  automorphism, so `pi'` is onto. Given `(x, y)`, choose `a` and `b` with `pi(a) = x` and `pi'(b) = y`. Then
  `(pi, pi')(c_1 a + c_2 b) = (x, y)` by part 1.
- *Part 3.* The entries of `p` lie in `ker pi` or `ker pi'`, and neither contains `eps_-`. An isomorphism
  `eps_- S_- (+) X ~= (c_1 S_-)^m` gives `eps_- = sum_k a_k c_1 b_k`, and `pi'` turns it into `1 = 0`. The same holds
  with `pi` for `c_2`. An idempotent matrix over `B_1` has `p = c_1 p`, so `pi'(p) = 0`.
- *Part 4.* `alpha` fixes `H` pointwise (`anti-central-k0-states-can-be-chosen-transpose-invariant`, part 1). QED

**Remark 3.2 (stabilization).** `(pi, pi')` gives no obstruction to stabilized relations. `R ~= R^2`, so `K_0(R) = 0`,
and every relation `P (+) S_-^r ~= Q (+) X (+) S_-^r` maps to a relation between zero classes. After stabilization the
remaining invariants are additive ones such as `s_3` and states, which is why Theorem 2.1 works through states.
Proposition 3.1 filters one-term designs: a corner `eps_- = sum_k a_k p b_k` needs `pi(p) != 0` and `pi'(p) != 0`.

## 4. From no state to A and B, and where it stops

**Remark 4.1 (conversion).** Suppose `(K_0(S_-), u)` admits no state. By
`twisted-leavitt-corner-fullness-equals-absence-of-k0-states` (items 3 and 5), `S_-^(n+1) (+) Y ~= S_-^n` for some
`n >= 1` and projective `Y`, through an isomorphism `theta : S_-^n -> S_-^n (+) (S_- (+) Y)`. Let `iota` include the
summand `S_-^n` and `rho` project onto it.
- `A = theta^(-1) iota` and `B = rho theta` are `n x n` matrices over `S_-` with `BA = I` and `AB != I`.
- With `eps_+ = 1 - eps_-`, the matrices `A + eps_+ I` and `B + eps_+ I` do the same over `F_3[G]`.
- A trace-lowering relation rules out states directly: an `alpha_*`-invariant state would equal `trl` on `[X]`, by the
  proof of Theorem 2.1. The step from no state to the isomorphism of item 3 is Theorem E of that node's artifact, and
  this lane does not unwind it into matrices. So the second step of the lane has no explicit form beyond this, and
  Theorem 2.1 shows the first step supplies no relation that `H` does not already supply.

**Where it stops.**
- *Design side.* A trace-lowering stable relation built from `SD`, `GL_2(F_3)`, `h` and `c_2` exists iff one exists over
  `H` (Theorem 2.1). One-term designs through a single block are impossible (Proposition 3.1). No relation and no pair
  `A, B` is constructed.
- *State side.* Every state of Corollary 3.2 equals `trl` on `H_fin(H) + H_fin(GL_2(F_3))`. Whether every state has
  `t = 1/2` stays open. `GL_2(F_3)` does not decide it, because `[q_1]` and `[q_2]` are independent in
  `K_0(eps_- F_3[GL])`.
- *Next constants.* `alpha` preserves `GL_3(F_3)`, the constants over a three-leaf prefix code, and acts there as
  transpose-inverse, which sends a simple module to its dual. The simple module with the adjoint highest weight
  `(1, 0, -1)`, twisted by `det`, lies in the anti-central block and is self-dual. So `alpha_*` fixes its projective
  cover, and averaging forces nothing on that class. Whether other laws force it is not addressed here.
