## Seven relations force the central sign to collapse

Let H be generated by X_i,Y_j for 0 <= i,j <= 6 and a central J, with
J^2 = 1, X_6 = Y_6 = 1, and

    X_i Y_j = J X_k Y_l

for each displayed pair (i,j) ~ (k,l). Using label 6 as the normalized
pivot is merely a relabeling of the convention X_0 = Y_0 = 1.

Only P1,...,P7 are needed. P2 and P3 respectively give

    X_3^(-1) X_4 = J Y_2 Y_5^(-1),
    X_2^(-1) X_5 = J Y_2 Y_5^(-1).

P4 gives X_2^(-1) X_5 = J Y_3 Y_4^(-1). Hence

    X_3^(-1) X_4 = J Y_3 Y_4^(-1).                 (1)

Similarly, P5 and P6 respectively give

    X_0^(-1) X_3 = J Y_5 Y_6^(-1),
    X_2^(-1) X_1 = J Y_5 Y_6^(-1).

P7 gives X_2^(-1) X_1 = J Y_0 Y_3^(-1). Therefore

    X_0^(-1) X_3 = J Y_0 Y_3^(-1).                 (2)

Multiplying (2) by (1), and using centrality and J^2 = 1, yields

    X_0^(-1) X_4 = Y_0 Y_4^(-1).

But P1 gives X_0^(-1) X_4 = J Y_0 Y_4^(-1). Cancellation proves J = 1.
No commutativity of the X_i or Y_j was used.

To track the odd parity explicitly, temporarily replace the seven J's
by independent central symbols z_1,...,z_7. The same elimination yields

    z_1^(-1) = z_5 z_6^(-1) z_7 z_2 z_3^(-1) z_4,

or

    z_1 z_2 z_3^(-1) z_4 z_5 z_6^(-1) z_7 = 1.   (3)

Its exponent sum is 3, which is odd. Setting every z_i = J gives the
collapse above. This supplies the seven-relator parity certificate in
an explicit elimination form. The proof does not require a separate
topological interpretation of the word "face."

## Finite cube realization and precise scope

Identify 0,...,7 with the vectors of G = F_2^3, with XOR as the group
operation. Put

    U = sum_{g=0}^6 [g] in F_2[G].

All terms commute, every [g]^2 = 1, and there are seven terms. Thus
U^2 = 1. The XOR column verifies that every paired product agrees in G;
the pivot product 6 XOR 6 is the identity. Consequently this complete
matching is realized by the genuine two-sided pair B = A = U.

For a realization respecting the normalized generators X_6 = Y_6 = 1,
send both X_i and Y_i to i XOR 6. This sends paired products to the same
XOR values as before, and sends the pivot labels to the identity. The
corresponding unsigned factor is [6]U, whose square is again 1. Its
seven support labels are distinct and generate G.

The cube realization already proves that central-sign collapse is
insufficient to certify a nonzero reverse defect in an arbitrary
realization. In this example the stronger conclusion also holds: the
universal unsigned normalized group is exactly F_2^3, as the following
direct calculation shows.

## The universal unsigned group is exactly the finite cube

Let Gamma_7 have all 24 unsigned relations X_i Y_j=X_k Y_l and the
normalizations X_6=Y_6=1. We first derive an upper bound on this group
using only its defining relations, before using the cube realization
to show that no further collapse occurs.

P20, P17, P11, and P14 respectively identify the following row and
column generators:

    X_0=Y_0=x,   X_2=Y_2=y,   X_4=Y_4=z,   X_5=Y_5=t.

For example, P20 says X_0 Y_6=X_6 Y_0, which is X_0=Y_0; the other
three identifications follow in exactly the same way from their
displayed cells involving row or column 6. The remaining generators
are determined by P5, P6, P18, and P23:

    X_3=xt,   X_1=yt,   Y_3=tx,   Y_1=ty.

No commutativity has yet been imposed or inferred. In particular,
X_1=Y_1 and X_3=Y_3 do not follow from these formulas alone.

Now P7 gives

    (yt)(tx)=yx,

so cancellation on the left by y and on the right by x gives t^2=1.
P3 says t^2=y^2, hence y^2=1. Next P8 gives

    (yt)(ty)=(xt)(tx),
    y t^2 y = x t^2 x,

which yields x^2=y^2=1 using t^2=1. Finally P1 gives x^2=z^2, so
z^2=1. These four involution deductions do not use commutativity.

Three more relations give commutation with t:

    P2: zt=xty,
    P4: tz=ytx,
    P9: xty=ytx.

Thus zt=tz. P15 gives xz=zx. P24 reads

    xtz=ztx.

Since z commutes with both x and t, its left-hand side is zxt.
Canceling the common left factor z proves xt=tx. Substitute this
into P2 and use zt=tz:

    tz=zt=xty=txy,

so z=xy by cancellation of t. The already established xz=zx now
implies

    x(xy)=(xy)x,

and cancellation of the leftmost x gives xy=yx. Since x^2=1,
the relation z=xy is equivalently y=xz. In particular t commutes
with y as well, because it commutes with x and z.

Every generator is therefore a word in the three commuting
involutions x,z,t. This supplies a surjection

    C2 x C2 x C2 -> Gamma_7.

To see that no additional relation has reduced this group, use the
previously verified XOR realization X_i=Y_i=i XOR 6. It sends

    x -> 6=(1,1,0),
    z -> 2=(0,1,0),
    t -> 3=(0,1,1).

These vectors are linearly independent over F_2: a zero linear
combination first has zero coefficient of 6 by its first coordinate,
then zero coefficient of 3 by its last coordinate, and finally zero
coefficient of 2. Thus the composition

    C2^3 -> Gamma_7 -> F_2^3

is an isomorphism. Both arrows must be isomorphisms, proving
Gamma_7=C2^3.

The reverse product can consequently be checked inside the universal
group itself. Its row and column sums coincide:

    B=A=1+x+yt+y+xt+z+t in F_2[Gamma_7].

All seven summands commute and square to 1, so A^2=1 in
characteristic two. Hence BA=AB=1 already in F_2[Gamma_7], not only
after passage to the concrete cube quotient. The signed presentation
does collapse J, but the unsigned universal candidate is a finite
group and has no reverse defect. This explicitly separates failure
of the central-sign exclusion test from failure of direct finiteness.
