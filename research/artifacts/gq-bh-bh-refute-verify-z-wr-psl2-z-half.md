## 1. The node's proof, re-derived

- **Density (1).**
  - `φ(U(Z[1/n]))` is a finite quotient of `Z[1/n]`. Multiplication by `n` is onto, so it is bijective there
    and the order is prime to `n`.
  - The image of `u(1/n^k)` is the unique `n^k`-th "root" of `β`, and it lies in `⟨β⟩`. So
    `φ(U(Z[1/n])) = φ(U(Z))`, and likewise for the lower unipotent group.
  - `Z[1/n]` is a localization of `Z`, hence Euclidean, so `SL_2(Z[1/n])` is generated by elementary matrices.
  - Hence `φ(Γ) = φ(Λ)`. **Correct.**
- **Not residually finite (2).** Pick `λ ∈ Λ` with `ψ(λ) = ψ(g)`. Then `ψ(a_{og}) = ψ(a_{oλ}) = ψ(a_o)`, so
  `a_o a_{og}^{-1}` is a nontrivial element of every finite-index normal subgroup (`og ≠ o` for `g ∉ Λ`).
  **Correct.**
- **Consequence (3).** Subgroups of residually finite groups are residually finite, and `Aut` of a
  locally finite rooted tree is residually finite. **Correct.**

## 2. Reading of the source

  **no** embedding into any self-similar group, finitely presented or not.

## 3. Extra finding: a typo in the source's `(Ω)` check, harmless

- The source's `f` is `h ↦ P^{-1} h P` with `P = [[0,3],[-1,1]]` (l.765). A direct computation for
  `h = [[a,3b],[c,d]]` gives
  `P^{-1} h P = [[-b+d, a+b-3c-d], [-b, a+b]]`.
- The source (l.866–869, l.880–884) prints the `(2,1)` entry as `c`, which is a typo.
- With the corrected entry, (Ω)(ii) and (Ω)(iii) still hold:
  - integral `a, b, c, d` give an integral image;
  - an integral image forces `b, a, d ∈ Z` and `3c ∈ Z`, hence `c ∈ Z`.
- So, **granting (Ω)(i)** (`H_ω = 1`, from their item (2), not re-derived here), all hypotheses of their
  Theorem A(2) hold for `K = PSL_2(Z) ≤ PSL_2(Z[1/2])`. **Theorem A is then false as stated**, with this as a
  counterexample. The node already states this conditionally, and the typo does not change it.
