# Prime Heisenberg sofic groups with hyperfinite-invisible marks

For each prime `p`, start with the Bernoulli lamp vector space
`N=F_p^(F_2)` of finite support.  Finitary elementary transvections act
transitively on `N\{0}` and are generated, together with the free shifts, by
four adjacent transvections.  The resulting affine-shift group is LEF by
periodizing every finite coordinate window in a finite quotient of `F_2`.

Amalgamate the base lamp `delta_e` with the generator `y` of the finite
Heisenberg group `H_p`, while identifying their common central mark `w`.
The amalgam is finitely generated and sofic, and normal-form injectivity
keeps `w` nontrivial.

Conjugation by `y` has identity linear part on the Weyl basis and nonzero
phase `(u,v)|->-u`.  The prime Heisenberg trace formula therefore makes `y`
traceless in every nontrivial `w`-sector.  Finitary transitivity propagates
this to every nonzero Bernoulli lamp.  The free shift has Haar-null fixed
sets on the Bernoulli dual, so the represented action is properly outer;
injectivity would then contain an expected copy of `L(F_2)`, impossible.

Hence every finite injective representation kills `w`.  These groups embed
in `U(R^omega)` by soficity but not in `U(R)`, for every prime order.
