## 1. G1: the letter marking does not generate EL_3(F)

**The step.**
- In sk-strong-8 Theorem A, proof steps 1 and 3, and in route (iv) of sk-strong-6, `H` and every `SL_{3N_n}(F_2)` are
  called "marked quotients of `Λ = EL_3(F)`" for the marking `Σ = {e_ij(t) : i ≠ j, t a letter}`.
- A "Kazhdan pair `(Σ, ε)` with `Σ` the marking" then gives the uniform spectral gap.
- Item 3 of the claim `lamplighter-host-is-limit-of-finite-simple-expanders` says the same.

**The defect.**
- `Σ` does not generate `EL_3(F)`. The augmentation `F → F_2` (every letter ↦ 0) induces `EL_3(F) → SL_3(F_2)`. It kills
  every element of `Σ` and sends `e_12(1)` to `e_12(1) ≠ I`. So `⟨Σ⟩` is a proper subgroup of `EL_3(F)`.
- A Kazhdan set of a group generates it. Otherwise `ℓ²(G/⟨Σ⟩)` has a `Σ`-invariant unit vector with no `G`-invariant
  component: `δ_{⟨Σ⟩}` if the index is infinite, and its projection orthogonal to the constants if the index is finite.
- So `(Σ, ε)` is not a Kazhdan pair for `EL_3(F)`, whatever `ε` is.
- This is the rev2 expander-generator defect (sk-panel-referee-5) again, now in the lamplighter setting. sk-verify-7
  part 3 §1 checked that `Σ` generates the approximants, but not that it generates `Λ`.

**What is unaffected.**
- `H` and every approximant are generated by `Σ`, because `π(t_s t_s′) = 1` and `ρ_n(t_s t_s′) = I` hold exactly.
- Marked convergence uses only that a word in `Σ` evaluates in `EL_3(F)` and has entries of `W − I_3` in `F`. It is
  correct as written.

**Repair: either of the following.**
- **(R1)** Add `e_ij(1)` to the markings of `H`, of the approximants and of `Λ`.
  - Then the marking generates `EL_3(F)`, by `e_ij(r+r′) = e_ij(r)e_ij(r′)` and `[e_ik(r),e_kj(r′)] = e_ij(rr′)`.
  - EJZ (`elementary-groups-over-fg-rings-have-property-t`) gives a Kazhdan pair `(Σ ∪ {e_ij(1)}, ε)`.
- **(R2)** Keep `Σ`, and put `Σ⁺ = Σ ∪ {e_ij(1)}`. This is a finite generating set of the Kazhdan group `EL_3(F)`, with
  some Kazhdan constant `ε`.
  - In `H` and in every approximant, `e_ij(1) = [e_ik(t_s), e_kj(t_s′)]`, with `k` the third index. Over `F_2` this is a
    word of length 4 in `Σ`.
  - A unitary representation of an approximant with no invariant vectors pulls back to one of `EL_3(F)` with no invariant
    vectors. So each unit vector is moved by at least `ε` by some `x ∈ Σ⁺`, hence by at least `ε/4` by some `s ∈ Σ`.
  - So `(Σ, ε/4)` is a Kazhdan pair for every approximant, uniformly in `n`, and the Cayley graphs for `Σ` form an
    expander family (Margulis).

**Proposed replacement sentence** for both routes and for item 3 of the host claim:
> `H` and every approximant are quotients of the Kazhdan group `EL_3(F)`. In each of them `e_ij(1) = [e_ik(u_s), e_kj(u_s^{-1})]`
> has length 4 in the marking. So a Kazhdan constant `ε` of `EL_3(F)` for `{e_ij(1), e_ij(t)}` gives the constant `ε/4`
> for the marking, uniformly in `n`.

**For the note.**
- The note on main (md5 `274f1936…`, l.162–183) and `sk/drafts/rev3.tex` (l.160–181) already contain the repair for `G_X`:
  `EL_3(F)` "is generated by the matrices `e_ij(1)` and `e_ij(τ)`", and "in both quotients `e_ij(1)` is the image of
  `∏_a e_ij(τ_a)`".
- A rev4 text for the lamplighter host needs the analogous clause. Both proposals (sk-strong-6 (a2), sk-strong-8 (b))
  say only "as in Section 1", but Section 1's clause uses `Σ_a e_a = 1`, which has no counterpart here. Verbatim fix,
  to append after "form a family of expanders" / "form expanders":
  ```latex
  In both quotients $e_{ij}(1)=[e_{ik}(u_a),e_{kj}(u_a)]$ for $k\ne i,j$, since $u_a^2=1$.
  ```
  Here `[e_ik(x), e_kj(y)] = e_ij(xy)`, and `P_a^2 = I` holds in every model, so `e_ij(1)` has length 4 in the
  marking in `G` and in every approximant.
- sk-simpler-unify's §2 candidate drops rev3's clause; see part 3.

## 2. Item 1 re-derived

- **Partial group models.**
  - Write `ℓ = cδ`; `L` acts by `(ℓx)(h) = x(δ^{-1}h) + c(h)`, and `a_h = hah^{-1}` flips coordinate `h`.
  - In `L` and in `L_n` the product is `(c + δ·c′)δδ′`.
  - `(φ_n)_*` commutes with sums once `φ_n` is injective on the supports, and with shifts once `φ_n(δh) = φ_n(δ)φ_n(h)`
    on them. So `ψ_n` is multiplicative and injective on a fixed ball for large `n`.
- **Covariance (★).** `U(g)D(f′)U(g)^{-1}δ_y = f′(x_{g^{-1}y})δ_y` and `x_y = y∘φ_n`.
  - Directly for `ℓ^{-1} = c′δ′`: `(ψ_n(ℓ^{-1})y)(φ_n(h)) = y(φ_n(δ′)^{-1}φ_n(h)) + [φ_n(h) ∈ φ_n(supp c′)] = (ℓ^{-1}x_y)(h)`
    for `h` in a fixed ball and large `n`.
  - This checks the letter cases of sk-strong-8 and sk-strong-6's formula at once.
  - Lemma 1 (eventual multiplicativity of `Φ_n`) follows. **PASS.**
- **Nonvanishing (Lemma 2).**
  - `Φ_n(r)δ_y = Σ_ℓ f_ℓ(x_{ψ_n(ℓ)y}) δ_{ψ_n(ℓ)y}`.
  - Witnesses: `δ = δ′` gives disagreement on `supp(c+c′)`. For `δ ≠ δ′` choose a fresh `h ∉ supp c ∪ supp c′` with
    `x(δ^{-1}h) = 0` and `x(δ′^{-1}h) = 1` (Δ is infinite).
  - `y` realizes the pattern because `φ_n` is injective on the window. By (★) for `ℓ^{-1}` the targets are distinct, and
    the coefficient at `δ_{ψ_n(ℓ_0)y}` is `f_{ℓ_0}(ℓ_0x_y) = 1`.
  - sk-strong-6's version picks `x` outside the finitely many closed nowhere dense sets `Fix(ℓ^{-1}ℓ′)`. It is equivalent.
    **PASS.**
- **Surjection (Lemma 3).**
  - `D_n(p)δ_y = y(e)δ_y` and `U(q)D_n(p)U(q)^{-1}δ_y = y(q)δ_y`. The coordinates separate `Y_n`, so every `E_{y,y}` appears.
  - Conjugates of the flip give translations `T_{1_q}`, hence every `T_c`, and `E_{y,y′} = T_{y+y′}E_{y′,y′}`.
  - So `ρ_n` is onto `M_{2^{|Q_n|}}(F_2)`, and `EL_3(M_N(F_2)) = SL_{3N}(F_2)`: in-block transvections are commutators
    through a second block.
  - `ρ_n(w) = Φ_n(π(w))` for large `n`, using `φ_n(s^{-1}) = φ_n(s)^{-1}` and `ψ_n(a) = 1_{φ_n(e)} = 1_e`.
  - `N_n → ∞` since `|Q_n| ≥ |B_r|`. **PASS.**
- **Marked convergence.** For a word `W` in `Σ`, `W = 1` in `H` iff `π(W − I_3) = 0`. For large `n` this holds iff
  `Φ_n(π(W − I_3)) = 0` (Lemma 2, and `Φ_n(0) = 0`) iff `ρ_n(W − I_3) = 0`. **PASS.**
- **Simplicity of the approximants.** `SL_{3N}(F_2) = PSL_{3N}(F_2)` is simple, since `F_2^×` is trivial.
- **Characterization (sk-strong-8 Theorem B).**
  - (i)⇒(ii): finite `Γ` is replaced by `Γ × Z`.
  - `Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ] ≤ [L,L]` by `rf-groups-lie-in-derived-subgroups-of-rf-groups`, part 2 (two PASSes).
  - `[L,L] ↪ H` by `diag(u_ℓ,1,1)` (item 4 of the reviewed host node).
  - (ii)⇒(i): marked limits of finite groups are LEF, and LEF passes to subgroups. **PASS.**

  place of a finite `Γ`, since the Ore overgroup of a finite group can be finite. Add one clause.

## 3. Item 2 re-derived

- **Lemma A (Ore data, isolation words).**
  - `π_k(g)` has every cycle twice, so it is even, and Ore Thm 1 writes it as `[α,β]` in `Sym(Q_k × {1,2})` for every size.
    Example check: `(12)(34) = [(1324),(12)]`.
  - `(b^{-n}ab^n)(x,i) = (τ_{i+n}(x), i)`. Level-preserving elements act levelwise, so `w_j` acts on level `i` by
    `[τ_{i+p_j}, τ_{i+q_j}]`.
  - That is nontrivial only if `i+p_j ≡ s′` and `i+q_j ≡ s` with `s, s′ ∈ P_k`. Then `q_j − p_j = 2^{2j−1} ≡ s − s′`
    (mod `4^{k+1}`). Both sides lie in `(−4^k, 4^k)`, so they are equal.
  - `v_2(s − s′) = v_2(s′)` for `s > s′` powers of 2. So `s′ = 2^{2j−1} = p_j`, `s = q_j` and `i ≡ 0`.
  - The words `w_j` do not depend on `k`, and `ι(γ_j) = w_j(a,b)` because equality holds for all `k ≥ j`, a cofinite set.
    With repetitions (finite `Γ`) the independent choices of `α, β` are harmless.
  - Subgroups of an algebraic ultraproduct of finite groups are LEF: finitely many conditions hold on a set in `ω`.
    `ι(Γ) ≤ [Δ,Δ]`. **PASS.**
- **Theorem B.** `Γ × Z` is LEF (product of partial models); Lemma A; then `lef-groups-embed-in-simple-kazhdan-lef-groups`
  for the infinite 2-generated `Δ`. **PASS.**
- **Theorem C.**
  - `⊕_{C∈𝒞} C` is countable and LEF: a finite subset lives in finitely many coordinates, and product models are injective
    on it and multiplicative.
  - Recursive presentations are indexed by r.e. indices, so there are countably many. **PASS.**
  - The examples rest on recalled inputs: word problem of f.g. linear groups (sk-verify-7 N6), `WP(G_Y) ≡_T L(Y)`, and
    Matui / Grigorchuk–Medynets (not required).
- **Proposition D.** A countable group has countably many finite subsets, so countably many f.g. subgroups. The `G_X`
  realize continuum many types (reviewed), since the WP degree is an isomorphism invariant of f.g. groups. **PASS.**
