## 2. Representation facts (by hand, confirmed on MSI)

- **Singer cycle and F21.**
  - `m(c) = c³+c+I = 0` and `|F21| = 21`.
  - The commutant of F21 in `M_3(F_2)` is `F_2I` (size 2), and its centralizer in `GL_3(2)` is trivial.
  - `span_{F_2}F21 = M_3(F_2)` (dimension 9). This is used in Theorem A Step 4.
- **F4.**
  - `tr(I) = 1`, so `gl_3 = F_2I ⊕ sl_3`. `sl_3` is irreducible (every nonzero orbit spans all 8 dimensions), so `sl_3 ≅ 8`.
  - `H¹(GL_3(2), gl_3) = 0`. Cocycles `[[g,δ],[0,g]]` on generators satisfying the (2,3,7;4) relations number exactly `|B¹| = 256`. The equality `|Z¹| = |B¹|` is robust even if that presentation were incomplete.
  - So `Ext¹(3,3) = 0`. By hand: `H¹(Q,F_2) = 0` because `Q` is perfect, and `H¹(Q,8) = 0` because `8` has dimension 8 = `|Q|_2` and is projective.
- **F1.** `sl_3` has no F21-fixed vector. Under `Ad(c)`: fixed space dimension 2, `ker m` dimension 3, `ker m*` dimension 3. That matches `8|_{F21} = 2⊕3⊕3*`.
- **F5.** On `3⊗3`, `c⊗c` has fixed space dimension 0, `ker m` dimension 3 and `ker m*` dimension 6. That matches `3⊕3*⊕3*`.
- **S3.**
  - `Λ = span{vw^T+wv^T}` (8 elements) sits in `Sym` (64).
  - Of the 512 subspace complements of `Λ` in `Sym`, none is `GL_3(2)`-invariant.
  - `Stab(e_1)` (order 24) has no nonzero fixed vector on `Λ`.
  - The hand proof of Lemma S3 is correct: an equivariant quadratic `q` with `q(e_1) ∈ Λ^{Stab} = 0` is identically 0 by transitivity, so its polarization vanishes, a contradiction.

## 3. Theorem D chain, re-derived

- **Lemma Z.**
  - Peirce components: `x_{(1−f)f}·fS(1−f) = 0`, and fullness of `1−f` gives `x_{(1−f)f} = 0`. Symmetrically `x_{f(1−f)} = 0`.
  - `fSf = fS(1−f)Sf` makes `x_ff` central in `fSf`, and `Z(fSf) = Ff` (Morita invariance). Likewise for `x_{(1−f)(1−f)}`.
  - `fS(1−f) ≠ 0` gives `λ = λ′`. Correct.
- **Lemma X.**
  - `F21_V` has isotypic parts `e_VM_X` (type 3) and `e_{X∖V}M_X` (trivial), with idempotents `e_VI`, `e_{X∖V}I` in its span. So `C(F21_V)` is `rI_3` with `r ∈ (e_VR_Xe_V)^×` on the first part, and arbitrary on the second. Hence `Q_V ⊆ CC(F21_V)`.
  - Conversely, `x ∈ CC(F21_V)` commutes with `G_{X∖V}`, so `x ∈ G_V` by C2. `I+nI_3`, with `n ∈ fR_Vf′`, has order 2, lies in `G_X` by O1, and commutes with `F21_V`, because constants are central.
  - Lemma Z in `R_V` then puts every entry of `x−I` in `F_2e_V`, so `x ∈ Q_V`. Correct.
- **Theorem C.**
  - `T_t ∈ span K_V`, and `C(K_V)` acts as `id⊗End(P_t)` on `t⊗P_t` (`F_4`-linearly on `M_2`).
  - `g_t(a) = a⊗id` and `g_2(ω)` are `R_Y`-linear, torsion, hence in `G_Y` by O1, and they commute with `C(K_V)`. So they lie in `CC(K_V) = α(Q_V)`, of order 168.
  - `α(c_V) ≠ I` is trivial on types 1 and 2, so `M_3⊕M_{3*} ≠ 0`.
  - Two natural blocks would give `GL_3(2)²`, and a type-2 block `GL_3(2)×C_3`. Both exceed order 168, so there is one block `t_V` and `M_2 = 0`, and `α(Q_V) = g_{t_V}(GL_3(2))`.
  - `ψ_V|_{F21} = ρ_{t_V}`. For `t_V = 3`, `ψ_V` is an automorphism of `Q` fixing F21 pointwise, which is the identity because `C_Q(F21) = 1` and outer automorphisms swap 3 and 3*. Correct.
- **Corollary O.**
  - `N = S_VS_WM` is `α(Q_V)×α(Q_W)`-stable, of the form `t_V⊠t_W⊗P_N`. The diagonal `α(Q_{V⊔W})` acts as `(t_V⊗t_W)⊗id`.
  - N is a submodule of the semisimple `α(Q_{V⊔W})`-module M, whose constituents are 1 and `t_{V⊔W}`. If `P_N ≠ 0`, then `t_V⊗t_W` is a direct summand. That is impossible:
    - `(3,3)` and `(3*,3*)` are not semisimple (S3);
    - `3⊗3* = 1⊕8` contains 8.
  - So `S_VS_W = 0`. Correct.
- **Corollary O′.**
  - Additivity `S_X = S_V+S_{X∖V}`: with `S_VS_W = 0`, the diagonal is fixed-point free on `S_VM` and on `S_WM`, and trivial on the rest.
  - `α(L_X)` is block diagonal and trivial on `EM`. Square-zero `n ∈ EM_3E` gives `I+n ∈ G_Y` (O1) commuting with `α(L_X)`.
  - `C_{G_X}(L_X) = 1`: a centralizing g is `aI` with `a ∈ D_X` invertible, hence `a = 1`.
  - DP splits `E ≠ 0` into nonzero orthogonal idempotents, and simplicity of `M_3(R_Y)` gives a nonzero square-zero n. So `E = 0`. Correct.
- **Corollary T.** `S_VM = Fix(K_{X∖V})`, where `α(Q_{X∖V})` acts trivially, so `α(Q_X)` acts there as `α(Q_V)`. So `t_V = t_X`. Correct.
- **Theorem D.** For `t_X = 3` all types are 3, which is (Sing_V) for all V (Lemma 0), and (O′) holds. For `t_X = 3*`, apply the chain to `α∘γ_X` (W4). Then Theorem A applies. Correct.
