# Pauli sums near their 1-norm round to stabilizer states

Lane `ex-quantum-pcp`, 2026-09-12. Elementary proof, unreviewed, not
Lean-verified. No computation is used. No priority claim is made. A bounded
search (two web searches, and a repository grep for Pauli 1-norm, stabilizer
rounding and games QPCP) found no statement of Theorem SR in this form.

## 0. Summary

For an explicit Pauli decomposition `H = sum_P beta_P P` with weight
`W = sum_P |beta_P|`, call `W - lambda_max(H)` the *Pauli frustration*.

- **Theorem SR.** Some stabilizer state `phi` has
  `<phi|H|phi> >= lambda_max(H) - (1+sqrt2)(W - lambda_max(H))`.
- **Corollary PP.** If `H = sum_a w_a (I - s_a P_a)/2` with `w_a>=0`, then some
  stabilizer state has energy at most `(2+sqrt2) lambda_min(H)`.
- **Corollary NP.** Both promise problems are in NP once the NO frustration
  exceeds `2+sqrt2` times the YES frustration, or once `b > (2+sqrt2)a`.
- **Games consequence.** The corrected amplification lemma of Natarajan--Nirkhe
  (arXiv:2403.13084v1, Lemma 6) needs an input family with Pauli 1-norm at
  most one. Such inputs with promise `1-1/p` versus `1-1/q` and `p > (2+sqrt2)q`
  are in NP. The lemma still yields a positive constant gap for every constant
  `p/q>1`, so the window `1 < p/q <= 2+sqrt2` remains open.

## 1. Setting

`P` ranges over Hermitian `n`-qubit Paulis (`{I,X,Y,Z}^n` without phase); the
instance is an explicit list of pairs `(beta_P, P)` with rational `beta_P` and
polynomially many terms of arbitrary weight. Write `beta_P = w_P s_P` with
`w_P = |beta_P|` and `s_P in {+1,-1}`. Put `A_P = s_P P`, so
`H = sum_P w_P A_P`. For a state `psi` put `delta_P = 1 - <psi|A_P|psi>`, which
lies in `[0,2]`. Then

```text
sum_P w_P delta_P = W - <psi|H|psi>.                                    (1)
```

## 2. Theorem SR and its proof

**Theorem SR.** `max_(phi stabilizer) <phi|H|phi> >= lambda_max(H) - (1+sqrt2)(W - lambda_max(H))`.

*Proof.* Let `psi` be a top eigenvector, so by (1)
`sum_P w_P delta_P = W - lambda_max =: F`. Put `tau_0 = 1 - 1/sqrt2`.

**(SR1) The good set commutes.** Let `G = {P : delta_P < tau_0}`. If `P,Q in G`
anticommute, then `(A_P + A_Q)^2 = 2I`, so `<A_P> + <A_Q> <= sqrt2`. But both
expectations exceed `1 - tau_0 = 1/sqrt2`, which is a contradiction. So `G` is a set of
pairwise commuting signed Paulis.

**(SR2) Its group and the determined terms.** Let `A` be the group generated by
`G`, taken modulo signs, and `G* = {P : P in +-A}`. This contains `G` and every
term `P` that is a product of members of `G` up to sign, including `I`. All `A_P`
with `P in G*` commute. Measuring the commuting family `{A_Q : Q in G}` on
`psi` gives a distribution `mu` on sign patterns `x`; each `A_P` with `P in G*`
has a determined outcome `x_P` (a signed product of the `x_Q`), and
`E_mu[x_P] = <psi|A_P|psi> = 1 - delta_P`. Hence

```text
E_mu[ sum_(P in G*) w_P (1 - x_P) ] = sum_(P in G*) w_P delta_P,
```

and some pattern `x` of positive probability satisfies
`sum_(P in G*) w_P (1-x_P) <= sum_(P in G*) w_P delta_P`.

**(SR3) Stabilizer states in the eigenspace.** The joint eigenspace
`E_x = {v : A_Q v = x_Q v for all Q in G}` is nonzero, because `x` has positive
probability. So the group generated by `{x_Q A_Q}` is abelian and does not
contain `-I`. It extends to maximal abelian groups without `-I`, and each is the
stabilizer group of a stabilizer state lying in `E_x`. Let `nu` be the uniform
distribution on the finite set `S_x` of stabilizer states in `E_x`. Every
`phi in S_x` has `<phi|A_P|phi> = x_P` for `P in G*`.

**(SR4) Undetermined terms average to zero.** Let `P not in G*`.
(a) If `P` anticommutes with some `Q in G`, then `<phi|P|phi> = <phi|P x_Q A_Q|phi> = -<phi|x_Q A_Q P|phi> = -<phi|P|phi>`
for every `phi in S_x`, so the expectation is `0`.
(b) Otherwise `P` lies in the centralizer `C(A)` but not in `+-A`. `C(A)/A` carries the
nondegenerate symplectic form, so some Pauli `R in C(A)` anticommutes with `P`.
Since `R` commutes with `A`, `phi -> R phi` maps `S_x` bijectively to itself, and
`<R phi|P|R phi> = -<phi|P|phi>`. So `E_nu <phi|P|phi> = 0`.

**(SR5) Energy accounting.** By (SR2)--(SR4),

```text
E_nu <phi|H|phi> = sum_(P in G*) w_P x_P
                 >= sum_(P in G*) w_P (1 - delta_P),
lambda_max - E_nu <phi|H|phi> <= sum_(P not in G*) w_P (1 - delta_P)
                              <= sum_(P not in G*) w_P max{0, 1 - delta_P}.
```

Every `P not in G*` is outside `G`, so `delta_P >= tau_0`, and for `delta >= tau_0`
we have `max{0,1-delta} <= ((1-tau_0)/tau_0) delta = (1+sqrt2) delta`. Therefore
`lambda_max - E_nu<phi|H|phi> <= (1+sqrt2) sum_P w_P delta_P = (1+sqrt2) F`.
Some `phi in S_x` is at least the average. QED.

**Lower bound on the constant.** For `H = (X+Z)/2` on one qubit, `W=1` and
`lambda_max = 1/sqrt2`. Every stabilizer state has `<X>+<Z> <= 1`, so its energy
is at most `1/2`. Thus `(lambda_max - stab_max)/(W - lambda_max) = 1/sqrt2`, and
the optimal constant in Theorem SR lies in `[1/sqrt2, 1+sqrt2]`. Averages of
disjoint copies give the same ratios at every size.

## 3. Corollaries

**Corollary PP (Pauli-projector Hamiltonians).** Let
`H = sum_a w_a (I - s_a P_a)/2` with `w_a >= 0`, and `K = sum_a w_a s_a P_a`,
of weight `W = sum_a w_a`. Then `H = (W I - K)/2`, `lambda_min(H) = (W - lambda_max(K))/2`,
and by Theorem SR applied to `K`,

```text
min_(phi stabilizer) <phi|H|phi> = (W - stab_max(K))/2
  <= (W - lambda_max(K) + (1+sqrt2)(W - lambda_max(K)))/2 = (2+sqrt2) lambda_min(H).
```

Terms may have any weight and need not commute. Zero ground energy gives a
stabilizer ground state. That is the exact frustration-free case, where all
`s_a P_a` share a `+1` eigenvector and hence commute.

**Corollary NP.** (i) For explicit Pauli decompositions, deciding
`lambda_max(H) >= W - F_Y` versus `lambda_max(H) <= W - F_N`, with
`F_N > (2+sqrt2) F_Y`, is in NP. (ii) For explicit Pauli-projector Hamiltonians,
deciding `lambda_min(H) <= a` versus `lambda_min(H) >= b`, with `b > (2+sqrt2)a`,
is in NP.

*Proof.* The witness is a stabilizer tableau. Validity and each `<phi|P|phi>`,
which lies in `{0,+1,-1}`, are computed in polynomial time by linear algebra over
`F_2` (Aaronson--Gottesman). The energy is an exact rational. For (i), YES
instances have a stabilizer state of energy at least `W - (2+sqrt2)F_Y`, by
Theorem SR and monotonicity. NO instances have every state at most `W - F_N`.
The verifier accepts iff the energy exceeds a rational threshold strictly between
the two. (ii) is the same with Corollary PP. QED.

**Model tests.** `H = Z` has `F = 0` and the stabilizer `|0>` attains `W` (hypothesis
met, conclusion sharp). The transverse-field Ising chain at `g=1`, as a Pauli sum
`sum Z_iZ_(i+1) + sum X_i`, has `W = 2N`, `lambda_max ~ (4/pi)N`, product `|0^N>`
energy `N`. So `(lambda_max - stab)/F ~ 0.37`, inside the bound. The Clifford
family `L^(-1) sum_(i<=L) A_i` with pairwise anticommuting `A_i` has ratio
`1/sqrt L`.

## 4. Consequences for the quantum PCP region

- **Hamiltonian form.** By (ii), a QMA-hard family built from Pauli projectors
  (code checks plus Pauli-measurement terms, of any weight, commuting or not)
  must have YES energy `a >= b/(2+sqrt2)` at NO floor `b`, unless QMA is in NP.
  Frustration-free or near-frustration-free Pauli-projector outputs cannot carry
  a quantum PCP. This complements the positive-shift audit of the anchored route,
  whose frustration-free alternative already runs into QMA versus QMA_1. It is
  another row of the regime table in
  `research/artifacts/qpcp-landscape-sources-2026-09-12.md`.
- **Games form, the Pauli energy test.** Natarajan--Nirkhe (arXiv:2403.13084v1, Section 4.2, eq. (2e))
  shows that sampling `P` with probability `w_P/W` and accepting iff the outcome
  equals `s_P` has acceptance `1/2 + <H>/(2W)`. Acceptance at least `1 - eps` means
  frustration at most `2W eps`. So a family whose energy test has completeness
  `1-eps_c` and value at most `1-eps_s` over all states, with
  `eps_s > (2+sqrt2) eps_c`, is in NP. Constant-gap games built on this test need
  completeness error at least a `1/(2+sqrt2)` fraction of the soundness error
  inside the energy test. Perfect completeness of the energy test gives a
  commuting consistent Pauli system, decidable in polynomial time.
- **The Natarajan--Nirkhe amplification lemma** (Lemma 6, Section 4.3, "Hamiltonian
  promise gap amplification", eqs. (6), (7), (9)). Its input is an `ell`-local `H` with `-I <= H <= I` and
  promise `lambda_max >= 1-1/p` or `<= 1-1/q`. Its output is
  `H' = 2((I+H)/2)^(tensor k) - I`, with `lambda_max(H') >= 1-k/p` or
  `<= 2e^(-k/(2q)) - 1`, and `||H'||_(P,1) <= 1 + 2((1+||H||_(P,1))/2)^k`. Open
  problem 1 of the note asks for a QMA-complete input family with bounded Pauli
  1-norm to which the lemma applies; the note remarks that `||H||_(P,1) <= 1` keeps
  `||H'||_(P,1) <= 3`.
  - *NP side.* If the given decompositions have weight `W <= 1`, YES instances
    have frustration at most `1/p`, so Theorem SR gives a stabilizer state with
    energy at least `1 - (2+sqrt2)/p`. NO instances are at most `1-1/q`. So
    `p/q > 2+sqrt2` is in NP.
  - *What the lemma gives.* With `r = p/q` and `x = k/(2q)`, the acceptance gap of
    the energy test on `H'` is at least `(1 - x/r - e^(-x))/3`. At `x = ln r` this
    is `(1 - (1+ln r)/r)/3`, positive for every `r > 1` (e.g. `0.051` at `r=2`,
    `0.116` at `r = 2+sqrt2`).
  - *Open window.* Weight-one inputs with `1 < p/q <= 2+sqrt2` are not covered.
    Stabilizer witnesses cannot certify below ratio `1 + 1/sqrt2` (the example in
    Section 2), so closing the window needs other witnesses or hardness.
- **What is not claimed.** Nothing here bounds Pauli sums with weight above one or
  frustration comparable to `W`. Every Pauli Hamiltonian is affinely a
  Pauli-projector Hamiltonian, but QMA-hard families such as the XY model have
  constant relative frustration, far from this regime. The games conjecture and
  the root claim remain open.
