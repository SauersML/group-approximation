# sk-sl3z-bandwidth: distorted elements obstruct embeddings into GL_m over subshift crossed products

Lane `sk-sl3z-bandwidth`, 2026-09-13. Brief (ROSTER-G): does SL_3(Z) lie in no G_X?

**Answer.** SL_3(Z) lies in no G_X, and neither does any finitely generated group containing a distorted element of infinite order. The integer Heisenberg group is the smallest witness.
- The general statement: no finitely generated subgroup of GL_m(LC(X,F_q)⋊Z) contains such an element, for any subshift X and any finite field.
- So `every-lef-group-embeds-in-a-z-subshift-elementary-group` is false.
- The dimension-expansion route suggested in the brief is not needed.

## 1. Setting
Notation:
- F_q is a finite field.
- X is a compact zero-dimensional space with a homeomorphism T; any subshift qualifies. Minimality and infiniteness are not used.
- R = LC(X,F_q) ⋊_T Z = ⊕_j LC(X,F_q) u^j, with u f u^{-1} = f∘T^{-1}. Coefficients are unique.

**Module.** Let V = ⊕_{x∈X} ⊕_{t∈Z} F_q^m, with slot vectors δ_{x,t}⊗ξ. Put f δ_{x,t} = f(T^t x) δ_{x,t} and u δ_{x,t} = δ_{x,t+1}.
- The relation holds: u f u^{-1} δ_{x,t} = f(T^{t-1}x) δ_{x,t} = (f∘T^{-1})(T^t x) δ_{x,t}.
- M_m(R) acts entrywise on V.

**Faithful.** Write A = Σ_j A_j u^j with A_j ∈ M_m(LC(X,F_q)). Then A δ_{x,0}ξ = Σ_j A_j(T^j x) ξ δ_{x,j}. The terms lie in distinct slots, so A = 0 on V forces A_j(T^j x) = 0 for all x and j. So A = 0.

**Propagation.** p(A) = max{|j| : A_j ≠ 0}.
- A maps the slots {x}×[a,b] into {x}×[a−p(A), b+p(A)].
- p(AB) ≤ p(A)+p(B).

## 2. Theorem D (no distortion)
**Theorem D.** Let Γ ≤ GL_m(R) be generated by a finite symmetric set S with p(s) ≤ w for every s ∈ S. Let z ∈ Γ have infinite order, and put τ_S(z) = lim_n |z^n|_S / n, which exists by subadditivity (Fekete). Then τ_S(z) ≥ 1/(2wm). In particular, no infinite-order element of Γ is distorted.

*Proof.*
1. A word of length k in S has propagation at most wk, so p(z^n) ≤ w|z^n|_S.
2. Fix a slot vector v = δ_{x,t}⊗ξ and put ℓ(N) = max_{n<N} |z^n|_S. The Krylov space K_N(v) = span{z^n v : 0 ≤ n < N} lies in the slots {x}×[t−wℓ(N), t+wℓ(N)]. So dim K_N(v) ≤ m(2wℓ(N)+1).
3. Suppose τ = τ_S(z) < 1/(2wm), and choose ε > 0 with 2wm(τ+ε) < 1. By Fekete there is C with |z^n|_S ≤ (τ+ε)n + C for all n. So ℓ(N) ≤ (τ+ε)N + C, and m(2wℓ(N)+1) < N for all N ≥ N_0. Here N_0 depends on m, w, τ, ε and C, but not on v.
4. So z^0v, …, z^{N_0−1}v are linearly dependent, and p_v(z)v = 0 for some monic p_v of degree < N_0.
5. Let P be the lcm of all monic polynomials over F_q of degree < N_0; there are finitely many. P(z) kills every slot vector, hence V, so P(z) = 0 by faithfulness.
6. Write P = t^a Q with Q(0) ≠ 0. Since z is invertible, Q(z) = 0. So z is a unit of the finite ring F_q[t]/(Q), and z has finite order, a contradiction. ∎

## 3. Corollaries
**C1 (G_X and full groups).**
- With the note's generators e_ij(s), s ∈ {u^{±1}, e_a} (w = 1, m = 3), every infinite-order element of G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2)⋊Z) has stable length at least 1/6.
- Every finitely generated subgroup of G_X has no distorted element of infinite order.
- The same holds for finitely generated subgroups of the topological full group [[T]] of any Cantor system. They lie in GL_1(R) through g ↦ Σ_n u^n e_{\{n_g=n\}}, where the cocycle n_g is continuous and hence bounded.

**C2 (the Heisenberg group).** H = ⟨a,b | [a,c],[b,c]⟩ with c = [a,b].
- c has infinite order, and c^{n²} = [a^n,b^n].
- For n² ≤ N < (n+1)² we have c^N = [a^n,b^n]c^{N−n²} with N−n² ≤ 2n, so |c^N| ≤ 4n + 4·2n = 12n ≤ 12√N. So τ(c) = 0.
- By Theorem D, H is a subgroup of no GL_m(LC(X,F_q)⋊Z).
- H is finitely generated and residually finite, hence LEF.

**C3 (SL_n(Z)).** SL_n(Z), n ≥ 3, contains the upper unitriangular Heisenberg group, so it lies in no GL_m(LC(X,F_q)⋊Z), hence in no G_X. The same holds for BS(1,2), where a^{2^k} = t^k a t^{-k}, and for every finitely generated group with a distorted element of infinite order.

**C4 (refutation).** The open claim `every-lef-group-embeds-in-a-z-subshift-elementary-group` is FALSE, with H as witness. The note's Z-family is not universal for LEF groups. That is why Corollary `cor:lef` of rev3 needs the lamplighter hosts: there the acting group Z/2≀Δ has exponential growth, and the dimension count gives nothing.

**C5 (Z^d, recalled scope).** For LC(X,F_q)⋊Z^d with ℓ^∞ propagation, a ball of radius p has dimension m(2p+1)^d. The same proof shows that |z^n|_S = o(n^{1/d}) forces finite order.
- SL_3(Z) is excluded for every d, because its unipotent elements are exponentially distorted. This is Lubotzky–Mozes–Raghunathan, recalled but not re-read at source; this lane claims only the d = 1 statement.
- H is excluded only for d = 1.

## 4. Relation to the brief and to the banded-representation node
- The brief suggested Proposition B of `fp-subgroups-of-subshift-gl-have-banded-representations` (banded finite representations, injective on balls) combined with dimension-expansion bounds for SL_3(Z/M) in characteristic 2.
- Theorem D avoids both. Distortion is visible in the ring's propagation, and no finite presentation is needed.
- Whether (T) gives dimension expanders over fields of positive characteristic is a separate question (Lubotzky–Zelmanov proved it over C). This lane claims nothing about it.

## 5. Credit and novelty (bounded)
**Closest prior.** V. Cyr, J. Franks, B. Kra, S. Petite, *Distortion and the automorphism group of a shift*, J. Mod. Dyn. 13 (2018) 147–161, arXiv:1611.05913. Abstract, as returned by the search: "... the Baumslag-Solitar groups BS(1,n) and all other groups that contain exponentially distorted elements cannot embed into Aut(X) when h_top(X)=0. We further show that distortion in nilpotent groups gives a nontrivial obstruction to embedding such a group in any low complexity shift."

**Differences.**
- Their groups are automorphism groups, which act nonlinearly, and their obstruction depends on entropy and complexity.
- Ours are linear groups over the crossed product ring: every subshift qualifies and every sublinear distortion rate is excluded.
- Their method is range growth against complexity; ours is Krylov dimension against propagation.

**Contrast.** The automorphism group of a full shift does contain a distortion element (arXiv:2208.00685, ETDS 2023). So the linear slot structure is what excludes distortion here.

**Classical inputs.** The notion of a distortion element, and the quadratic distortion of the Heisenberg centre.

**Search bound.** Two web searches, plus a grep of main for distort / translation length / propagation / Krylov, which found no matching node. The permutation case (subgroups of [[T]]) may be folklore, and it is not claimed as new.

## 6. Model tests
- **Finite-order elements** (τ can be 0) are not excluded: Theorem D concerns infinite order only.
- **Units.** u ∈ GL_1(R) with S = {u^{±1}}: τ(u) = 1 ≥ 1/2.
- **Free subgroups.** Those of SL_2(F_2[u^{±1}]) ≤ G_X are undistorted, consistent.
- **Torus elements.** Powers of diag(u,u^{-1},1) have propagation n, so they are undistorted, consistent.
- **Lamplighter hosts** contain H (Corollary `cor:lef`). Their propagation balls grow exponentially, so there is no conflict.
- **Aut(A^Z)** contains distorted elements (arXiv:2208.00685). These are not F_q-linear slot operators, so there is no conflict.
