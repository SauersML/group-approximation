# Hilbert-hotel Steinberg factor-character audit

## Verdict

The finite-dimensional root-kernel argument has one useful exact extension,
but it does not extend to arbitrary finite factors.

For `L=L_(F_2)(1,2)` and `n>=3`, every nontrivial representation of
`St_n(L)` is injective on each root subgroup.  Hence one finite-dimensional
root von Neumann algebra forces the whole representation to be trivial.  This
is stronger than the existing exact finite-dimensional theorem because the
ambient finite von Neumann algebra may be infinite dimensional.

The extension stops there.  A finite factor may contain a diffuse abelian
algebra generated by infinitely many commuting involutions.  In the canonical
regular representation the root algebra is exactly `L((L,+))`, which is
diffuse.  Therefore factoriality cannot replace finite-dimensionality in the
coefficient-quotient proof.

## The root-faithfulness dichotomy

For a representation `pi`, let

```text
I_ij={a:pi(x_ij(a))=1}.
```

Weyl conjugacy identifies all the `I_ij`.  The spare-index Steinberg
commutators show that their common value is a two-sided ideal.  Simplicity of
the binary Leavitt algebra makes it zero or all of `L`.  Thus `pi` is trivial,
or every root map is injective.

If one root algebra is finite dimensional, it contains only finitely many
involutions, so it cannot contain an injective copy of the infinite additive
group of `L`.  This proves the positive theorem recorded in
`finite-root-algebra-forces-steinberg-triviality`.

## Why factoriality is insufficient

The ambient factor condition controls the center of the algebra generated by
all group elements.  It says nothing finite-dimensional about an individual
abelian root algebra.  For the regular representation,

```text
W*(lambda(x_ij(L)))=L((L,+))
                    ~=L^infinity(dual(L,+)),
```

and the right side is diffuse.  The Steinberg commutators still hold exactly,
but there is no nonzero coefficient kernel and hence no finite coefficient
quotient.

This also explains why a literal ring representation is the wrong target.
The root elements are complex unitaries representing the additive
`F_2`-group; they are not additive operators representing coefficients.
Multiplication appears only through cross-root commutators.  Recovering a
finite coefficient ring is a kernel argument, and that argument needs finite
root image.

## Character rigidity is still a genuine missing theorem

Quasisimplicity reduces kernels of nontrivial factor representations to the
central kernel, but it does not classify faithful factorial characters.  A
simple group can in principle have faithful nonregular characters.  Therefore
normal subgroup rigidity is not character rigidity.

The primary character theorem located in the audit is Lavi--Levit,
*Characters of the group EL_d(R) for a commutative Noetherian ring R*,
arXiv:2007.15547.  Its title hypotheses are load bearing: the binary Leavitt
algebra is noncommutative and non-Noetherian.  No theorem in the local corpus
or the targeted primary-source search classifies factorial characters of
`EL_n` or `St_n` over this Leavitt algebra.

There is a separate conditional certificate in the corpus using the
character-rigid Higman--Thompson prefix subgroup and the Dudko--Medynets
conjugacy condition.  The missing condition is not supplied merely by the
Steinberg root relations.

## The self-saturating Hilbert-hotel quotient

The quotient `G` from `hilbert-hotel-self-saturated-non-mf` has a marked root
`d` with

```text
normal_closure_G(d)=G.
```

This says that a representation killing `d` is trivial.  It does **not** say
that every nontrivial normal subgroup contains `d`, and it does not classify
factor characters or quotient-regular characters.

The normal-Kazhdan theorem proves `Res_MF(G)=G` in operator norm.  If `G` were
hyperlinear, existing node
`full-mf-radical-hyperlinear-models-force-relator-outliers` shows exactly what
would happen: every regular microstate sequence would hide a fixed
operator-norm relator defect on a vanishing-trace spectral corner.  Such a
corner is invisible in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  Neither defect
normal generation nor property `(T)` promotes it to positive trace.

Consequently the Hilbert-hotel quotient presently does not exclude a
Connes-embeddable regular component.  Proving that exclusion is precisely a
nonhyperlinearity theorem, and cannot be obtained from the full MF residual
without a new dimension-independent delocalization or character-rigidity
input.

## Exact frontier after this audit

The viable factor-character statement is not "factoriality reconstructs a
finite ring."  It is one of the following genuinely new targets:

1. classify faithful factorial characters of the Leavitt elementary group;
2. prove the missing Dudko--Medynets conjugacy condition for a compressible
   prefix subgroup inside that group; or
3. prove that every hypothetical CE regular microstate delocalizes the forced
   operator-norm outlier onto a positive-trace root carrier.

The first two are character rigidity.  The third is normalized-HS stability
in a more targeted form.  None is supplied by the current self-saturation
relations.
