# Audit: invariant coefficient symbols and coherently inner finite stages

Date: 2026-08-30

## Invariant coefficient row

The row-compression formula makes the coefficient symbol c.p.c.  A
finite-dimensional algebra crossed with `Z` by an automorphism is
subhomogeneous over the circle: a finite power of the automorphism is inner
on every matrix summand.  It therefore has nuclear dimension at most one.
The canonical map into the ambient crossed product is faithful by the
conditional expectation.

Commutation of the carrier with the coefficient stage and approximate
commutation with `U` give uniform quasicentrality on the compact outgoing
ranges after Fourier-polynomial approximation.  Cone stability repairs the
two weighted cutdowns to exact order-zero maps.  No total contractivity of
their sum is used.

## Perturbative stage correction

Uniform near-innerness on the unit ball of `D` says exactly that
`W=v^*U` almost commutes with `D`.  Haar averaging moves `W` into the exact
commutant without leaving the ambient algebra.  Norm distance less than one
makes the average invertible, and polar decomposition stays in the
commutant.  The resulting stage is a quotient of `D tensor C(T)`, so its
nuclear dimension is at most one.

Fourier polynomials provide local approximation of the crossed product.
Coordinatewise Arveson extension of the incoming maps justifies local
permanence without a separability assumption.

## Boundary

The theorem does not infer coherent finite stages from approximate
innerness.  The latter only controls a chosen finite set; the Haar argument
requires uniform control over the whole unit ball of one finite-dimensional
stage containing the coefficients and its implementer.  Orbit-window
finite subgroups in a general locally finite kernel provide the stage but
not its invariance or a coherent implementer.  This is the remaining seam
for the alternating shift and the unrestricted locally-finite-by-cyclic
case.
