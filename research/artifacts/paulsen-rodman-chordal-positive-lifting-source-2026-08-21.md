# Chordal positive operator-matrix lifting

Source checked 2026-08-21:

- Vern I. Paulsen and Leiba Rodman, *Positive completions of matrices over
  C*-algebras*, Journal of Operator Theory 25 (1991), 237--253.  Primary PDF:
  <https://www.theta.ro/jot/archive/1991-025-002/1991-025-002-002.pdf>.

Lemma 3.4 states that if `A` is a C*-algebra, `J` a closed two-sided ideal,
and a partial Hermitian matrix over `A/J` is partially positive with a chordal
pattern, then it has a partially positive lift over `A`.  The displayed proof
uses a perfect vertex-elimination ordering and lifts the positive clique
corner one simplicial vertex at a time.

This is a quotient-lifting theorem for chordal positivity data.  It does not
assert a lift across a nonchordal cycle and is not being promoted to LLP of
the full Deligne twisted algebra.
