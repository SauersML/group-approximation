## 8.2 ex-nonsofic-action-relative: `howe-moore-lattice-nonsofic-action-transfers-to-mixing` (db83b00ca5). PASS

- The coupling is `Ω = H x Z'`, with `Z' = Z x Bernoulli`.
- `V = Γ\H x Z'` is free with `Z` as a factor, so it is nonsofic.
- `W = H x_Γ Z'` is free.
- `H ↷ W` is ergodic, and Howe–Moore makes the coefficients `C_0` on `L^2_0(W)`. A discrete `Γ` leaves each
  compact set, so `Γ ↷ W` is mixing.
- Both `Γ`-orbit relations are restrictions of the ergodic `Γ x Γ` relation to fundamental domains of equal
  covolume measure. An element of `[[R]]` matches them, which is an orbit equivalence. Păunescu Theorem 1.5
  then carries nonsoficity to `W`.
- The dichotomy (every Howe–Moore lattice is in `𝒮` or has a free mixing nonsofic action) is correct.
  `mixing-free-nonsofic-action-of-sofic-group` stays open, since no lattice outside `𝒮` is known.
