# Blackadar--Kirchberg radical trace step, part 3: every densely finite trace on the radical sees the boundary (2026-09-13)

Lane `ex-bk-glued-uct`.  This builds on parts 1 and 2:
`research/artifacts/bk-radical-projection-trace-step-2026-09-13.md` (Lemmas A--C,
Theorem 1) and `research/artifacts/bk-radical-trace-step-part2-2026-09-13.md`

`research/artifacts/bk-t1-spectrum-simple-reduction-2026-09-12.md`.

A trace on `R` is *densely finite* if it is finite on the Pedersen ideal
`Ped(R)`.  Such a trace pairs with `K_0(R)` as follows.  Represent a class as
`[p] - [s(p)]` with `p ∈ M_N(R~)` and `p - s(p) ∈ M_N(Ped(R))`, and put
`τ^([p] - [s(p)]) = (τ ⊗ Tr)(p - s(p))`.

## 1. Theorem 7

**Theorem 7.**  Let `E` be separable, nuclear and stably finite, and
`R = R_qd(E)`.  Suppose `E/J` satisfies the UCT for every ideal `J ⊆ R` of `E`,
and put `H = ∂(K_1(E/R)) ⊆ K_0(R)`.  If `τ` is a densely finite lower
semicontinuous trace on `R` with `τ^(H) = 0`, then `τ = 0` or `R = 0`.  So if
`E` is not quasidiagonal, no nonzero densely finite trace on its radical
annihilates the boundary.

*Proof.*  Suppose `τ != 0` and `R != 0`.

1. **The ideal.**  `J = {a ∈ R : τ(a*a) = 0}` is a closed two-sided ideal, by
   lower semicontinuity and the trace property.  `J != R` because `τ != 0`.
2. **Bounded pieces.**  Let `h` be a strictly positive contraction of `R` and
   `C_k = her((h - 1/k)_+)`.  The `C_k` increase and their union is dense.
   `(h - 1/(2k))_+ ∈ Ped(R)`, and Fact T1 applies with
   `a = (h - 1/(2k))_+`, `ε = 1/(2k)`.  So `τ(c) ≤ 2k·τ((h - 1/(2k))_+) < ∞`
   for `0 ≤ c ≤ 1` in `C_k`, i.e. `τ` is bounded on `C_k`.  The kernel of
   `τ|_(C_k)` is `C_k ∩ J`, so `C̄_k = C_k/(C_k ∩ J)` is a hereditary
   subalgebra of `R/J` with a faithful bounded trace `τ̄_k`, compatible in `k`.
3. **Lifting the boundary.**  `R~` is locally approximated by the `C_k~`.  For a
   finitely generated `G_0 ⊆ q_*(H)`, perturbation of projections puts
   generators `[q(p_i)] - [s(q(p_i))]` with `p_i ∈ M_N(C_k~)` for all large `k`.
   These generate `L_k ⊆ K_0(C̄_k)`.  On them the pairing is computed in the
   bounded piece, so `τ̄_k^(L_k) = τ^(h_i) = 0`.
4. **UCT.**  `C̄_k` is full hereditary in the ideal `(I_k + J)/J` of `E/J`,
   where `I_k` is the ideal of `R` generated by `C_k`.  That ideal is UCT by
   two-out-of-three from `E/J` and `E/(I_k + J)`.  So `C̄_k~` is UCT (Brown's
   stable isomorphism for σ-unital full hereditary subalgebras).
5. **Killing on pieces.**  `C̄_k~` has the faithful tracial state
   `½ τ̄_k/||τ̄_k|| + ½ χ`.  Here `χ` is the character at infinity.  Faithful:
   `χ(a + λ) = 0` forces `λ = 0`, then `τ̄_k(a) = 0` forces `a = 0`.  Classes in
   `L_k` have rank `0`, so this state vanishes on `L_k`.  Lemma B gives a unital
   injective `φ_k : C̄_k~ -> B_k` into a simple AF algebra with
   `(φ_k)_*(L_k) = 0`.
6. **Assembly.**  Exactly as in Theorem 5, Step 3:
   - finite-stage compressions of `B_k` give models of `C̄_k~` killing `L_k`;
   - Arveson extension to `(R/J)~`, with the estimates of Moutzouris
     Proposition 3.5, gives asymptotically multiplicative, asymptotically
     isometric ucp maps killing `G_0`;
   - Proposition 3.3 (diagonal over finitely generated subgroups) and
     Proposition 3.2 give a quasidiagonal embedding of `R/J` killing `q_*(H)`.
7. **Contradiction.**  Lemma C makes `E/J` quasidiagonal.  By minimality
   `R ⊆ J`, contradicting `J != R`.  QED

## 2. What is recovered, and what the statement means

**Recovered.**
- Theorem 1 is Lemma A plus Theorem 7.  A full projection `p` gives a bounded
  trace `τ_D` on `D = pM_n(R)p` vanishing on `H`.  Transport `τ_D ⊗ Tr` along
  `D ⊗ K ≅ R ⊗ K` and restrict to `R ⊗ e_11`: this is a densely finite trace
  on `R` vanishing on `H`.
- Theorem 5 is Theorem 7 applied to the lower semicontinuous trace defined by a
  compatible family of corner traces on an approximate unit of projections.

**Meaning (Toeplitz traces must see the index).**  Tracial states of `E`
restrict to traces on `R` that annihilate the boundary.  Theorem 7 concerns the
traces on `R` that do not come from `E`, like `Tr` on `K ⊆ T`.  In a
counterexample every such densely finite trace pairs nontrivially with
`H = ∂(K_1(E/R))`: it "sees an index".  This is exactly the Toeplitz mechanism.
- In `T` (not stably finite), `Tr^(∂[z]) = ±1`.
- In a stably finite algebra, Spielberg's criterion only forbids positive
  classes in `H`.
- Theorem 7 says that, inside the radical of a counterexample, nonvanishing
  pairing is forced for all densely finite traces simultaneously.

**What it does not constrain.**  Take a compact traced ideal `K ⊊ R`
(`stw07-qd-radical-criterion-compact-open`).  A trace on `K`, extended by `∞`,
is not densely finite on `R`, so Theorem 7 says nothing about it.  No relative
version can be proved the same way.  Any ideal `J_σ ⊆ K ⊊ R` has `E/J_σ` not
quasidiagonal, by minimality of the radical, so a contradiction must use trace
data on all of `R`.  The missing tool is the relative Lemma B of part 2.

## 3. Tests

- **Trivial model failing the hypothesis.**  `T`: `R = K`, `Tr` densely finite,
  `Tr^(H) = Z != 0`.  The theorem's conclusion is vacuous there, and `T` is not
  stably finite.  Consistent.
- **Real object satisfying it.**  A stably finite extension of `C(T)` by
  `K ⊗ (C ⊕ C)` with index `(1, -1)`: `Tr ⊕ Tr` annihilates `H`, and the algebra
  is quasidiagonal (`R_qd = 0`), as the theorem requires.
- **Boundary of the method.**
  - Stably projectionless simple radicals with unique trace and nonzero pairing
    on `H`, and lexicographically chained real rank zero radicals with no
    densely finite trace at all.  In both, Theorem 7 gives no contradiction.
    Both kinds have the K_0-embedding property when they are simple UCT
    (classification, via Moutzouris' class) or AF, so they give no
    counterexamples themselves.  But a non-simple, non-AF radical of this shape
    is the surviving candidate.

## 4. Consequence for the glued UCT case

In the glued kind of counterexample (trichotomy case 3) with UCT quotients, the
quasidiagonal index carrier `R` has four properties:
1. a nonempty compact open primitive subset with a traced compact ideal;
2. no full projection;
3. no nonzero densely finite trace annihilating the index subgroup `H`;
4. `H ∩ K_0^+(R) = {0}` (Spielberg).

Items 2--4 are the constraints proved here.  The next hinge is relative: realise
killing when the only traces annihilating `H` live on proper ideals of `R`.
