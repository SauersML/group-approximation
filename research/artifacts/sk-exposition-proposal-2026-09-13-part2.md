## P7. l.123–129: periodic words (0 lines; precision)
Before:


Trade-off:
- Precision: "separated by a segment $w$" leaves open whether $w$ includes the first occurrence, and the join argument needs it to. The new text states the needed property ($vv'$ occurs in $X$).
- The negation "introduces no new word" becomes a positive statement.
- The rename $w\to v$ avoids the clash with the exponent bound $w$.

Existence check: in $x\in X$, let $x_{[0,p)}$ contain every word of length $2k+1$, and let $v'=x_{[0,2k)}$. By uniform recurrence, $v'$ recurs at some $q\ge p$. Then $v=x_{[0,q)}$ works, and $q$ can be taken arbitrarily large.
