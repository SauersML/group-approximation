# sk-cstar-mf: is C*_r(G_X) MF? A firewall through embedded Laurent lattices

Lane `sk-cstar-mf`, 2026-09-13. G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_q)⋊Z), X an infinite minimal subshift. Status of everything
new here: established with written proofs, unreviewed (queued with sk-verify-5).

## 0. Verdict
- **No positive result.** MF of C*_r(G_X) stays open (root `infinite-kazhdan-group-with-mf-reduced-cstar`, node
  `directly-finite-subshift-el-n-reduced-cstar-is-mf`).
- **New firewall.** No unitary representations of the note's finite models converge strongly to λ_{G_X}. That
  includes cuspidal and Steinberg-module constituents, and representations of any exactly matricial window model.
  An MF embedding would need asymptotic representations that are not genuine on any embedded SL_4(F_q[t^{±1}]).
- **Reduction.** G_X contains SL_n(F_q[t^{±1}]) for every n. So MF of C*_r(G_X) implies MF of C*_r of these
  positive-characteristic higher-rank lattices.

## 1. Source, verbatim (arXiv:2312.03220v2 e-print `SL4_v2.tex`, fetched on MSI)
Magee–de la Salle, *SL_4(Z) is not purely matricial field*, C. R. Math. 362 (2024) 903–910.
- **Theorem 1.1.** "Every finite dimensional unitary representation of $\SL_{4}(\Z)$ contains a non-zero
  $\SL_{2}(\Z)$-invariant vector."
- **Definition.** "$\{\rho_{i}\}$ strongly converges to the regular representation if for any $z\in\C[\Gamma]$,
  $\lim_{i\to\infty}\|\rho_{i}(z)\|=\|\lambda_{\Gamma}(z)\|$".
- **Corollary 1.3.** "$\SL_{4}(\Z)$ is not purely matricial field."
- **Status sentence.** "It does not seem to be known whether $C^*_r(\SL_3(\Z))$ or $C^*_r(\SL_4(\Z))$ is MF in the
  sense of Blackadar and Kirchberg."
- **Proposition 2.1.** "For all $N\in\N$, every non-trivial finite dimensional representation $\phi$ of
  $\SL_{4}(\Z/N\Z)$ has a non-zero $\SL_{2}(\Z/N\Z)$-invariant vector."
- **Strategy (§2), verbatim.**
  - "Step 1: We prove the representation is non-trivial when restricted to all elementary cyclic subgroups of level
    $p^{r-1}$."
  - "Step 2: We use Step 1 to prove that on restriction to a particular copy of the Heisenberg group modulo $p^{r}$,
    we find a particular type of character".
  - "Step 3: We take a non-zero vector in the isotypic subspace of the character of the Heisenberg group found in
    Step 2. By averaging this vector over a copy of $\SL_{2}(\Z/p^{r}\Z$) we find a non-zero
    $\SL_{2}(\Z/p^{r}\Z)$-invariant vector."
- **Reduction to congruence quotients**, via Bass–Milnor–Serre: "every finite dimensional unitary representation of
  $\SL_{4}(\Z)$ arises from a composition of homomorphisms $\SL_{4}(\Z)\to\SL_{4}(\Z/N\Z)\xrightarrow{\phi}U(M)$".
  Our models are congruence representations by construction, so no CSP import is needed here.

## 3. The firewall
**Embedded lattices** (`subshift-elementary-groups-contain-laurent-sl-n-lattices`).
- Take a tower V, TV, …, T^{k−1}V of disjoint sets. Then the corner e_W R e_W ≅ M_k(e_V R e_V).
- The first-return unit u_V = Σ_r u^r e_{V_r} generates a copy of F_q[t^{±1}] inside e_V R e_V: its powers are
  independent because the return times r_a(x) are injective in a.
- EL_3 of the corner embeds through x ↦ x + (1−e_W)I_3, and EL_3(M_k(S)) = EL_{3k}(S) (tower node, reviewed).
- So SL_{3k}(F_q[t^{±1}]) ≤ G_X.

**Congruence restriction** (`lef-models-are-congruence-representations-on-laurent-lattices`).
- For large ℓ the periodic-word model sends u_V to the first-return permutation Q_ℓ of the V-positions of y_ℓ.
- It sends the matrix units ε_ij = u^i e_V u^{−j} to exact partial permutations, and on bounded balls it agrees
  with the genuine ring homomorphism t ↦ Q_ℓ.
- So on finite subsets of the embedded lattice, ρ_ℓ∘φ_ℓ is a genuine representation through
  SL_{3k}(A_ℓ), A_ℓ = F_q[Q_ℓ] ≅ F_q[t]/(minimal polynomial), a finite PIR.

**Witness** (`periodic-word-model-representations-never-converge-strongly`).
- Take k = 2 and S, T free in SL_2(F_q[u]) (Nagao, item 3 of `ring-model-permutation-vectors-block-strong-convergence`,
  reviewed PASS), placed in the upper-left block of SL_4(F_q[u_V^{±1}]). Put z = S+S^{-1}+T+T^{-1}.
- ‖λ_{G_X}(z)‖ = 2√3.
- For large ℓ, ρ_ℓφ_ℓ restricted to SL_4(A_ℓ) has an SL_2(A_ℓ)-invariant unit vector, so ‖ρ_ℓφ_ℓ(z)‖ = 4.

**Scope.**
- The argument holds for every sequence of unitary representations of the models, and for any exactly matricial
  window models σ_ℓ over varying finite fields: F_ℓ[σ_ℓ(u_V)] is still a quotient of F_ℓ[t].
- The existing firewall `ring-model-permutation-vectors-block-strong-convergence` covered only representations
  containing the column permutation module. The heuristic "cuspidal-type irreducibles" in
  `directly-finite-subshift-el-n-reduced-cstar-is-mf` is now closed.

## 4. What is left, and what is not established here
- **Left.** An MF embedding of C*_r(G_X) needs operator-norm asymptotic representations whose restriction to every
  embedded SL_4(F_q[t^{±1}]) is far from genuine: no exact ring models, no representations of them. By Dadarlat-type
  reasoning this is only possible if SL_4(F_q[t^{±1}]) is not point-norm matricially stable (compare
  `matricially-stable-overgroups-of-sl4z-are-not-reduced-mf` for SL_4(Z)).
- **Recalled, not verified: the positive-characteristic analog of MdlS Cor 1.3.**
  - Every finite-dimensional unitary representation of SL_4(F_q[t^{±1}]) has finite image (Margulis superrigidity for
    lattices in positive characteristic).
  - Every finite quotient is congruence (Bass–Milnor–Serre, S-integers of function fields).
  - With §2, SL_n(F_q[t^{±1}]) for n ≥ 4 would then not be purely matricial field.
  - The two imports are not read from sources here. If true, this is folklore-level beyond MdlS and needs a
    literature check before any claim.
- **Not claimed.** Nothing here decides MF of C*_r(G_X), of C*_r(SL_n(F_q[t^{±1}])), or of C*_r(SL_3(Z)).
