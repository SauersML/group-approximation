# 2026-09-11 — Central extensions and the Schur kernel of a nonsingular adjunction

## Status

Proved here: five statements (S1–S5) about what adjoining a root of a
nonsingular one-variable equation does to second integral homology, and what
that says about Kervaire--Laudenbach over central extensions and about the
hyperlinear radical. Not proved: any case of the universal conjecture, any
nonzero Schur kernel, and no Lean verification is claimed.

Inputs: the extension lemma of
`nonsingular-adjunction-preserves-approximation-radicals` (coordinatewise
Gerstenhaber--Rothaus roots in any algebraic quotient of a product of unitary
groups), the universal coefficient theorem, the five-term exact sequence,
Hopf's formula, cellular homology, and the spectral theorem. Nothing else.

The point of the package: second homology gives a *degree-two* Kervaire--
Laudenbach test. A nonsingular equation killing a Schur multiplier class, over
**any** countable coefficient group, produces a nontrivial central element in
the hyperlinear radical of a central extension, hence a non-hyperlinear group
(S4). The coefficient group itself may be residually finite. The same object
decides Kervaire--Laudenbach over central extensions of well-behaved quotients
(S5).

---

## 0. Setting and notation

`Q` is a countable group, `w in Y = Q * <t>` has `m = deg_t(w) != 0`,
`R = <<w>>_Y`, `X = Y/R`, and `q : Q -> X` is the coefficient map.

**Schur kernel.**

    K_2(Q, w) = ker( q_* : H_2(Q; Z) -> H_2(X; Z) ).

**Universal coefficients.** For an abelian group `D` with trivial action there
is a natural exact sequence

    0 -> Ext(H_1 Q, D) -> H^2(Q; D) --ev--> Hom(H_2(Q; Z), D) -> 0,

natural in `Q` and in `D`. When `D` is divisible, `Ext(-, D) = 0` and `ev` is
an isomorphism.

For a central extension `1 -> Z -> E -> Q -> 1` with class
`alpha in H^2(Q; Z)` write `alpha_H = ev(alpha) : H_2(Q) -> Z`.

## S1. The Schur multiplier can only shrink

**Claim.** `q_*` is surjective, so `H_2(X) = H_2(Q)/K_2(Q, w)`.

*Proof.* `H_2(Y) = H_2(Q) (+) H_2(Z) = H_2(Q)` by Mayer--Vietoris for a free
product. The five-term exact sequence of `1 -> R -> Y -> X -> 1` reads

    H_2(Y) -> H_2(X) -> R/[R, Y] -> H_1(Y) -> H_1(X) -> 0.

`R/[R, Y]` is cyclic, generated by the class of `w`, because every conjugate of
`w` agrees with `w` modulo `[R, Y]`. The image of that generator in
`H_1(Y) = H_1(Q) (+) Z` has second coordinate `m != 0`, so it has infinite
order and `R/[R, Y] -> H_1(Y)` is injective. Exactness at `H_2(X)` makes
`H_2(Y) -> H_2(X)` surjective, and under `H_2(Y) = H_2(Q)` this map is `q_*`.
∎

**Finite certificate form (Hopf).** Choose `Q = F(S)/R_Q`, put
`F' = F(S) * <t>`, `R_Y = <<R_Q>>_{F'}`, and lift `w` to `w~ in F'`. Then
`R_X = R_Y <<w~>>` presents `X`, and

    H_2(Q) = H_2(Y) = (R_Y cap [F', F']) / [F', R_Y],
    H_2(X)          = (R_X cap [F', F']) / [F', R_X],
    [F', R_X]       = [F', R_Y] . [F', <<w~>>].

So the class of `r in R_Y cap [F', F']` lies in `K_2(Q, w)` exactly when

    r in [F', R_Y] . [F', <<w~>>].                              (S1C)

A certificate that `K_2 != 0` is an explicit identity of type (S1C) in the
free group `F'`, together with a proof that `r notin [F', R_Y]` — for
instance a 2-cocycle on `Q` that evaluates nontrivially on `r`. Both halves
are finite.

## S2. Projective unitary classes vanish on the Schur kernel

Let `P = prod_n U(d_n)`, `N` a normal subgroup of `P`, `U = P/N`, and let `A`
be the image in `U` of the scalar sequences `prod_n U(1) I_(d_n)`. Then `A` is
central in `U` and divisible, being a quotient of a divisible group, and

    T = U/A = P/(N . prod_n U(1))

is again an algebraic quotient of `P`. Let `[U] in H^2(T; A)` be the class of
`1 -> A -> U -> T -> 1`. For a homomorphism `phi : Q -> T` set
`beta_phi = ev(phi^*[U]) in Hom(H_2 Q, A)`.

**Claim.** `beta_phi(K_2(Q, w)) = 0` for every such `phi`.

*Proof.* `T` is an algebraic quotient of a product of unitary groups, so the
extension lemma of `nonsingular-adjunction-preserves-approximation-radicals`
gives `phi' : X -> T` with `phi = phi' o q`. Pullback of extensions and `ev`
are natural, so `beta_phi = beta_(phi') o q_*`, which vanishes on
`ker q_*`. ∎

**Corollary (finite coefficient groups).** If `Q` is finite then
`K_2(Q, w) = 0` for every nonsingular `w`.

*Proof.* Every `gamma in Hom(H_2 Q, U(1)) = H^2(Q; U(1))` is represented by a
normalized cocycle `omega`. The twisted regular representation
`lambda_omega(g) delta_h = omega(g, h) delta_(gh)` on `l^2(Q)` satisfies
`lambda_omega(g) lambda_omega(h) = omega(g, h) lambda_omega(gh)`, so it defines
`phi_gamma : Q -> PU(|Q|)` with `beta_(phi_gamma) = gamma` (one factor,
`N = 1`). `H_2(Q)` is finite and `U(1)` contains `Q/Z`, so these `gamma`
separate the points of `H_2(Q)`. The Claim kills `K_2`. ∎

More generally `K_2(Q, w) = 0` whenever the classes `beta_phi` separate the
points of `H_2(Q)`. So a Schur-kernel class must be invisible to every
projective unitary approximation of `Q`, in operator norm, normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt norm, rank, or any other normal subgroup `N`.

## S3. Aspherical adjunctions kill nothing

Let `B = K(Q, 1) v S^1` and `K = B cup_w e^2`, the 2-cell attached along a
loop representing `w in pi_1(B) = Y`.

**Claim.** If `K` is aspherical then `K_2(Q, w) = 0`.

*Proof.* `pi_1(K) = X`, so `K` is a `K(X, 1)` and `H_2(X) = H_2(K)`. In the
homology sequence of the pair `(K, B)`, `H_3(K, B) = H_3(S^2) = 0`, so
`H_2(B) -> H_2(K)` is injective. `H_2(B) = H_2(Q)`, and the map is `q_*`. ∎

A Schur-kernel certificate therefore needs a non-aspherical relative
presentation: an identity among relations whose `Q`-content is homologically
nontrivial.

## S4. The Schur kernel lands in the hyperlinear radical of every central extension

Let `E` be countable and `1 -> Z -> E -> Q -> 1` central with class `alpha`.
`Rad_hyp(E)` is the repository's convention: the intersection of the kernels
of all homomorphisms `E -> U(M)`, where `M` is a tracial ultraproduct of
matrix algebras and `U(M) = (prod_n U(d_n))/N_(2, omega)`.

**Claim.** `alpha_H(K_2(Q, w)) <= Rad_hyp(E)` for every nonsingular
`w in Q * <t>`.

*Proof.* Let `x in K_2(Q, w)`, `z_0 = alpha_H(x) in Z`, and suppose
`psi : E -> U(M)` has `psi(z_0) != 1`. We derive a contradiction.

*Step 1: spectral cut-down.* The unitaries `psi(z)`, `z in Z`, commute with
each other and with `psi(E)`. Enumerate `Z = {z_0, z_1, ...}`. Pick
`lambda != 1` in the spectrum of `psi(z_0)`. For each `k`, the joint spectrum
of `(psi(z_0), ..., psi(z_k))` projects onto the spectrum of `psi(z_0)`, so it
contains a point with first coordinate `lambda`. The spectral projection
`p_k` of a box of side `< 1/k` around that point is nonzero, lies in the von
Neumann algebra generated by `psi(Z)`, and there are scalars `chi_k(z_i)` with

    || (psi(z_i) - chi_k(z_i)) p_k || <= 1/k   (i <= k),   |chi_k(z_0) - lambda| <= 1/k.

Since `p_k` commutes with `psi(E)`, `psi_k(g) = psi(g) p_k` is a homomorphism
`E -> U(M_k)` for the corner `M_k = p_k M p_k` with normalized trace. `p_k`
lifts to projections in the matrix coordinates, so `M_k` is again a tracial
ultraproduct of matrix algebras and `U(M_k)` is an algebraic quotient of a
product of unitary groups.

*Step 2: exact scalars in the limit.* Fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter on `k`
and let `M_inf` be the tracial ultraproduct of the `M_k`, with
`psi_inf = (psi_k)_k : E -> U(M_inf)`. The operator-norm estimates give
`psi_inf(z_i) = chi(z_i) 1` exactly, with `chi(z_i) = lim chi_k(z_i)`; `chi`
is a homomorphism `Z -> U(1)` and `chi(z_0) = lambda != 1`. Unitaries lift
through `prod_k U(M_k) -> U(M_inf)`, so `U(M_inf)` is an algebraic quotient of
a product of unitary groups. The image `A` of the scalar sequences is `U(1) 1`.

*Step 3: compare classes.* `psi_inf` maps `Z` into `A` through `chi`, so it
induces `phi : Q -> T = U(M_inf)/A`. `psi_inf` is a morphism of central
extensions over `phi` with kernel map `chi`, so `phi^*[U(M_inf)] = chi_* alpha`
and `beta_phi = chi o alpha_H`. By S2, `beta_phi(x) = 0`, that is
`chi(z_0) = 1`, contradicting `chi(z_0) = lambda`. ∎

**Corollary S4a (non-hyperlinear groups from a Schur kill).** If some countable
`Q` and nonsingular `w` have `K_2(Q, w) != 0`, then some countable group is not
hyperlinear.

*Proof.* Pick `0 != x in K_2`. `Q/Z` is an injective cogenerator of abelian
groups, so there is `gamma : H_2(Q) -> Q/Z` with `gamma(x) != 0`. `ev` is onto,
so some central extension `E` of `Q` by `Q/Z` has `alpha_H = gamma`. `E` is
countable, and `gamma(x)` is a nontrivial element of `Rad_hyp(E)`. ∎

**Corollary S4b (Deligne).** Let `1 -> <z> = Z/3 -> E_3 -> Sp_4(Z) -> 1` be
Deligne's triple cover. If `alpha_H(K_2(Sp_4(Z), w)) != 0` for some nonsingular
`w`, then `z in Rad_hyp(E_3)`. `E_3` is finitely presented, and for a finitely
presented group `z in Rad_hyp` is exactly statement (DCM1) of
`deligne-central-mark-hs-collapse`: a sequence of almost representations with
vanishing relator defect and `||phi_n(z) - I||_2 >= c > 0` would, in the
ultraproduct, give a homomorphism not killing `z`. By
`deligne-sector-gap-is-exactly-nonhyperlinearity`, `E_3` is then
non-hyperlinear.

## S5. Kervaire--Laudenbach over central extensions

Keep `E`, `Z`, `alpha`. Let `w in E * <t>` have `deg_t(w) = m != 0`, and let
`w- in Q * <t>` be its image. Put `X = (Q * <t>)/<<w->>` and assume
`q : Q -> X` is injective. Put

    E_w   = (E * <t>)/<<w>>,
    E_w^c = (E * <t>)/<<w, [z, t] (z in Z)>>.

**Claim (i).** If `alpha_H(K_2(Q, w-)) = 0` then `E -> E_w` is injective. In
fact `w` has a root in a central extension of `X`, by a divisible group, that
contains `E`.

**Claim (ii).** `Z -> E_w^c` is injective if and only if
`alpha_H(K_2(Q, w-)) = 0`. The condition depends only on `alpha` and `w-`, not
on which lift `w` was chosen.

*Proof of (i).* Embed `iota : Z -> D` into a divisible group and put
`alpha_D = iota_* alpha`, so `ev(alpha_D) = iota o alpha_H` vanishes on `K_2`.
By S1 the rule `beta(q_* y) = iota(alpha_H(y))` is a well-defined homomorphism
`H_2(X) -> D`. `ev` is onto, so some central extension `1 -> D -> E~ -> X -> 1`
has `ev[E~] = beta`. Then `ev(q^*[E~]) = beta o q_* = ev(alpha_D)`, and `ev` is
injective for divisible `D`, so `q^*[E~] = alpha_D`. The preimage of `q(Q)` in
`E~` is isomorphic, as an extension, to the pushout
`E_D = (E x D)/{(zeta, -iota(zeta))}`, and `E` embeds in `E_D`.

Let `tau in X` be the image of `t` and `tau~ in E~` a lift. The coefficients of
`w` lie in `E <= E~` and map to those of `w-`, so `w(tau~)` maps to
`w-(tau) = 1` and lies in `D`. Choose `c in D` with `c^m = w(tau~)^(-1)`. `c`
is central and each occurrence of `t^(+-1)` contributes `c^(+-1)`, so
`w(c tau~) = c^m w(tau~) = 1`. So `E` embeds in a group containing a root. ∎

*Proof of (ii).* If the condition holds, `Z` is central in the group `E~` of
(i) and `w` has a root there. The universal property of `E_w^c` gives a map
`E_w^c -> E~` extending `E -> E~`, so `Z` injects. Conversely, the image `Z-`
of `Z` is central in `E_w^c`, and `E_w^c / Z- = (Q * <t>)/<<w->> = X`. If
`Z -> Z-` is injective, the preimage of `q(Q)` is the image of `E`, and `E`
maps onto it isomorphically: the kernel lies in `Z` because `q` is injective.
So the extension class of `E_w^c` pulls back along `q` to `alpha`, and
`alpha_H = ev(alpha)` factors through `q_*`, so it vanishes on `K_2`. ∎

**Consequences.**

(a) *Where a central failure must live.* Let `q` be injective. Every
coefficient killed in `E_w` lies in `Z`, and it dies in `E_w^c` too. So any
Kervaire--Laudenbach failure over `E` forces `alpha_H(K_2(Q, w-)) != 0`, in
particular `K_2(Q, w-) != 0`.

(b) *Positive cases.* If `q` is injective and `K_2(Q, w-) = 0`, every central
extension of `Q` satisfies the conjecture at every lift of `w-`. This covers
aspherical adjunctions (S3), and hyperlinear `Q` whose Schur multiplier is
separated by projective unitary classes (S2). Finite `Q` is covered as well,
but there the extension is virtually abelian and nothing is new.

(c) *Deligne.* `Sp_4(Z)` is residually finite, so `q` is injective for every
nonsingular `w-`. By (b), Kervaire--Laudenbach holds over `E_3` at every lift
of `w-` unless `alpha_H` meets `K_2(Sp_4(Z), w-)`. That is the same question
whose positive answer makes `E_3` non-hyperlinear (S4b).

**The gap between (i) and (ii).** (ii) characterizes survival in the
*centralized* quotient `E_w^c`. A Kervaire--Laudenbach failure needs survival
to fail in `E_w` itself, which can be strictly larger. So `alpha_H(K_2) != 0` is
necessary for a central failure, but it is not known to be sufficient.

## 6. What this changes, and the attempts it closes

* **A new falsification instrument for hyperlinearity.** S4a is a degree-two
  analogue of `kl-violation-refutes-hyperlinearity-route`. It does not need a
  Kervaire--Laudenbach failure, and it works over *any* coefficient group,
  including residually finite ones. If every countable group were hyperlinear,
  every nonsingular adjunction would inject Schur multipliers.
* **Dead searches.** By S2, no finite coefficient group has a nonzero Schur
  kernel. By S3, no aspherical adjunction does. More generally, a candidate
  class must be invisible to all projective unitary approximations of the
  coefficient group.
* **Deligne.** Both the non-hyperlinearity of `E_3` (S4b) and any
  Kervaire--Laudenbach failure over `E_3` (S5c) point at the same finite
  algebraic object: a nonsingular equation over `Sp_4(Z)` whose certificate
  (S1C) kills a class that Deligne's mod-three multiplier detects.
* **Not claimed.** No nonzero Schur kernel is exhibited. No priority is
  asserted for the classical ingredients. Whether the conjecture for all groups
  follows from Schur injectivity for all groups is not settled here: passing
  from a coefficient kill to a Schur kill needs a simple envelope with nonzero
  Schur multiplier, which is not supplied.

## S6. The Schur kernel localizes to the coefficient subgroup

Let `B <= Q` contain the coefficients of `w`, so `w in B * <t>`, and assume
`B -> B_w = (B * <t>)/<<w>>` is injective.

**Claim.** `K_2(Q, w) = iota_*( K_2(B, w) )`, where `iota : B -> Q` is the
inclusion.

*Proof.* `Q * <t> = Q *_B (B * <t>)`, and imposing `w = 1` in the second factor
gives the pushout `Q_w = Q *_B B_w`, as in
`kl-failure-localizes-to-the-coefficient-subgroup`. `B` injects into both
factors, so this is an amalgamated free product. Its Mayer--Vietoris sequence

    H_2(B) --(iota_*, j_*)--> H_2(Q) (+) H_2(B_w) --> H_2(Q_w) --> H_1(B) --> ...

is exact, with `j : B -> B_w`. A class `x in H_2(Q)` dies in `H_2(Q_w)` exactly
when `(x, 0)` lies in the image of `H_2(B)`: that is, `x = iota_*(y)` for some
`y` with `j_*(y) = 0`, i.e. `y in K_2(B, w)`. ∎

## S7. Amenable coefficient subgroups have zero Schur kernel

**Claim.** Suppose the subgroup `B` generated by the coefficients of `w` is
countable and every twisted group von Neumann algebra `L_omega(B)`
(`omega in Z^2(B; U(1))`) embeds trace-preservingly in a tracial ultraproduct
of matrix algebras. Then `K_2(Q, w) = 0` for every countable `Q` containing
`B` as the coefficient subgroup. This holds in particular when `B` is
amenable.

*Proof.* `B` is hyperlinear: `L(B)` is the case `omega = 1`. So `B -> B_w` is
injective by `kervaire-laudenbach-holds-for-hyperlinear`, and S6 reduces the
claim to `K_2(B, w) = 0`. Let `gamma in Hom(H_2 B, U(1)) = H^2(B; U(1))`, with
normalized representing cocycle `omega`. Embed `L_omega(B)` unitally and
trace-preservingly in `M = prod_omega M_(d_n)`. The images of the twisted
regular unitaries `lambda_omega(g)` satisfy
`lambda(g) lambda(h) = omega(g, h) lambda(gh)`, so they define `phi : B -> U(M)/U(1)`
whose multiplier is `gamma`. `U(M)` is an algebraic quotient of a unitary
product, so S2 gives `gamma(K_2(B, w)) = 0`. The groups `Hom(H_2 B, U(1))`
separate points, so `K_2(B, w) = 0`. For amenable `B`, every `L_omega(B)` is
injective and separable, hence embeds in `R^omega`. ∎

**Consequences.**

* A Schur kill needs a non-amenable coefficient subgroup, and one with a
  non-Connes-embeddable twisted group algebra.
* Classes of `H_2(B)` detected in `H_2` of a finite quotient of `B` cannot
  die, by S2 applied to finite projective representations. The killed class
  must be profinitely invisible.
* **Deligne sharpening.** Let `B <= Sp_4(Z)` be the coefficient subgroup and
  `x in K_2(B, w)` with `alpha_H(iota_* x) != 0 in Z/3`. If `L_(alpha|B)(B)`
  were Connes-embeddable, the projective homomorphism it defines would detect
  `x`, contradicting S2. So `B` must be non-amenable,
  `L_(alpha|B)(B)` must be non-Connes-embeddable, and `iota_* x` must be a
  Maslov-type class invisible to every finite quotient. Subgroups such as
  `SL_2(Z) x SL_2(Z)`, whose Schur multiplier `Z/12` is detected through the
  finite abelianization, are excluded.
