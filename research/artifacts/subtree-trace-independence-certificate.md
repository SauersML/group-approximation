# Archived target: sibling trace-independence certificate

The target asked for fixed adjoint-mixing words forcing

```text
|tr(xy)-tr(x)tr(y)| <= C sqrt(E)
```

for sibling sign observables, uniformly in depth and matrix rank.  Its
analytic conditional implication is valid once the two observables are
actually placed in an expander factor and its relative commutant, but the
required group-word placement was not proved.  The target is archived rather
than declared false.

In particular, reduced freeness in the intended completeness model and
disjoint support do not furnish soundness for arbitrary matrix tuples.
