## 2. Conditional theorem

Let `H ≤ Aut(T_2)` be self-similar and **finitely presented**, contain the odometer `τ` and the translations by
`Z_(2)`, and act **locally faithfully**: no nontrivial element is the identity on a nonempty open set. Then
`2V_H` is a finitely presented simple group containing `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩`. The ingredients are:
- `kep-2v-hosts-carry-q-x-2-iff-h-has-odometer-roots`, items 3 and 5–8;
- Li arXiv:2110.04505v2, Example `ex:ZS` (III);
- Matui arXiv:1210.5800v3, Theorem `simple2`.

In the critic's terms, divisibility lives in `H` (its `Z_(2)` translations) and in the baker conjugates, and
distortion lives in the baker map `u`.

## 3. The exact crux

**Construct** a finitely presented, locally faithful, self-similar `H ≤ Aut(T_2)` that contains `τ` and
`τ_c` for every `c ∈ Z_(2)`. By `odometer-root-hosts-are-non-affine-and-non-contracting` the roots `τ_(1/ℓ)`
are forced; they are the unique `ℓ`-th roots of `τ`.

Constraints already on main:
- not affine, not contracting, not in `Pol(∞)` (`odometer-root-hosts-are-non-affine-and-non-contracting`);
- not generated by finite-state automorphisms (`automaton-groups-see-only-smooth-periods`,
  `z-localized-in-automaton-groups-needs-exotic-odometers`);
- not linear over any field (`fg-linear-groups-have-roots-at-finitely-many-primes`);
- the roots cannot be linked by conjugation except affinely, so prime-moving amalgams of Iwahori-type pieces
  collapse to `PGL_2(Q_2)` (items 9–10). The translations must arise from **products of non-affine elements**.

A solution implies `z-semilocalized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group` (SL) with `S = {2}`. Local faithfulness is
compatible with being non-finite-state: the rational Iwahori group `Γ_2` has both properties but is not
finitely generated.
