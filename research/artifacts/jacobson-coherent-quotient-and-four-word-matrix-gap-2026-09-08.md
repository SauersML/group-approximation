# The coherent quotient and its four-error matrix gap

2026-09-08. Adjoining the proved nontrivial coherence word as a
relator defines a proper quotient of `Theta_shift`. The literal
bilateral action factors through this quotient and retains its head.
The new relation authenticates a third finite `GL_3(F_2)` packet,
with the full head and boundary factors included.

The corresponding matrix problem uses exactly the same admissible
finite-packet data as `gamma_shift`, with one additional word error.
Its positivity is equivalent to marked norm-corona collapse for the
new quotient. No positive lower bound is proved here.

## 1. The quotient is proper and retains the head

Let `Gamma=Theta_shift`, with the finite presentation and literal
bilateral action `pi` from
[the stable-letter construction](jacobson-stable-letter-recursion-and-matrix-gap-2026-09-08.md).
Use the following fully decorated elements:

```text
c_P=x_13,P,              k_P=x_31,P,
r_Q=x_23,Q,              r_P=x_23,P,
q_Q=w_23,Q,              q_P=w_23,P,
b=q_Q q_P,              zeta=r_Q r_P,
u=[h c_P h,k_P],         v=h u h,
D=h zeta h,
W=[u,D] zeta,
E=b v b D.                                             (1)
```

Here `[x,y]=x y x^(-1)y^(-1)`. The original mixed relators of
`Gamma` are `(h b)^3=1` and `W=1`. Define

```text
Gamma_triangle=Gamma/normalClosure_Gamma(E).            (2)
```

This adds one finite word to the established finite presentation.
The [coherence-word calculation](jacobson-missing-laurent-coherence-word-and-finite-cell-countermodel-2026-09-08.md)
proves `pi(E)=1`. On the other hand, the
[affine Laurent quotient](jacobson-laurent-cover-has-a-noncentral-affine-quotient-2026-09-08.md)
sends the image of `E` in `Gamma/L=Lambda x Z` to translation
by `z e_1`, which is nonidentity. Thus `E!=1` in `Gamma`,
and (2) is a proper quotient.

Since `pi(E)=1`, the entire normal closure in (2) lies in
`ker(pi)`. Consequently `pi` factors through an action

```text
pi_triangle:Gamma_triangle -> GL(V_Z).
```

The original head mark `w=x_13,Q` still sends `e_(3,0)` to
`e_(3,0)+e_(1,0)`, so it is nonidentity in `Gamma_triangle`.
More generally, the established finitary subgroup

```text
L=GL_fin(F_2^(Z x {1,2,3}))
```

embeds in this quotient: `pi` is faithful on `L`, so the normal
closure of `E` has trivial intersection with `L`. The old finite
packets `B,C` likewise retain their faithful literal actions.
The subsequent
[affine-colimit calculation](jacobson-coherent-quotient-is-the-faithful-literal-group-2026-09-08.md)
proves faithfulness of `pi_triangle` on the whole new group.

## 2. The complete third finite packet

All identities in this section hold in `Gamma_triangle`. The
finite `C` table gives the exact rank-one group

```text
<h,u> ~= GL_2(F_2),       v=h u h,       h=u v u.
```

Indeed `u` is the `x_21` root in the prescribed shifted
`GL_3(F_2)` generated by `c_P,k_P,h`. The finite group
`F=G_Q x G_P` gives the other rank-one group:

```text
<b,zeta> ~= GL_2(F_2),
b=zeta (b zeta b) zeta.
```

It is the diagonal copy of `<w_23,x_23>` in the two factors.
In particular `h,u,v,b,zeta,D` are involutions. The old mixed
relator says

```text
[u,D]=zeta.
```

Since `[u,D]=(u D)^2` and `zeta` is involutive, this
commutator centralizes both `u` and `D`: conjugation by either
involution inverts `(u D)^2`, which equals its own inverse.
Conjugating by `h` gives

```text
[u,zeta]=[D,zeta]=1,
[v,zeta]=D,
[v,D]=[zeta,D]=1.                                     (3)
```

The new relator `E=1` says

```text
b v b=D.                                              (4)
```

Name the six roots

```text
X_12=v,           X_21=u,
X_23=zeta,        X_32=b zeta b,
X_13=D,           X_31=b u b.                          (5)
```

The Weyl relation `(h b)^3=1` gives `h b h=b h b`.
Together with (4), it gives the only non-immediate transport
identity:

```text
b u b=b h v h b
     =b h(b D b)h b
     =(b h b)D(b h b)
     =(h b h)(h zeta h)(h b h)
     =h(b zeta b)h.                                  (6)
```

Consequently conjugation by `h` and `b` permutes all six roots
in (5) exactly as the coordinate swaps `w_12,w_23`. All roots
are involutions. Conjugating `[v,zeta]=D` by these swaps gives
every relation `[X_ij,X_jk]=X_ik` for distinct `i,j,k`.
The two commuting pairs `[v,D]=1` and `[zeta,D]=1` give,
under the same conjugations, every pair with a common row or a
common column. These are all the remaining nonopposite-root
commutations in three coordinates.

Thus (5) satisfies the elementary-root presentation of
`SL_3(F_2)`. The finite-field presentation is the one in
Steinberg's *Lectures on Chevalley Groups*, Section 6, Theorem 9
and its special-linear example; the statement and its use here
were checked in Section 5 of the coherence-word calculation.
The primary transcription is
[Generators and relations](https://math.soimeme.org/~arunram/Resources/LOCGS6GeneratorsAndRelations.html).
The rank-one identities above also express `h` and `b` in
the six roots, so no additional Weyl generator remains outside
this presented group. We obtain a surjection

```text
GL_3(F_2) -> <h,u,b,zeta>.                             (7)
```

Here are the boundary actions proving faithfulness and identifying
the actual packet. For each `n>=0`, use the ordered triple

```text
J_n=(e_(1,n+1), e_(2,n), e_(3,n)).
```

These triples are disjoint. Their union is all nonnegative-level
basis vectors except `e_(1,0)`. In the literal bilateral action,
`h` swaps the first two coordinates of each `J_n`, while `b`
swaps its last two. Direct evaluation of the words gives

```text
pi_triangle(u)
   =I+sum_(n>=0) e_(2,n) epsilon_(1,n+1),
pi_triangle(v)
   =I+sum_(n>=0) e_(1,n+1) epsilon_(2,n),
pi_triangle(zeta)
   =I+sum_(n>=0) e_(2,n) epsilon_(3,n),
pi_triangle(D)
   =I+sum_(n>=0) e_(1,n+1) epsilon_(3,n).              (8)
```

All sums act on finite-support vectors. The displayed operators
fix `e_(1,0)` and every negative-level basis vector. On each
`J_n`, the four generators are precisely

```text
h=w_12,       u=x_21,       b=w_23,       zeta=x_23.
```

In particular the boundary triple is
`J_0=(e_(1,1),e_(2,0),e_(3,0))`; it is included in this
packet. Restriction of the literal action to that triple is the
natural faithful action of `GL_3(F_2)`. The composite of (7)
with this action is faithful, so (7) is an isomorphism:

```text
<h,u,b,zeta> ~= GL_3(F_2).                             (9)
```

The finite-field literal action is used here only after deriving
the complete finite presentation. It does not substitute for the
abstract relations (3)--(6).

## 3. The same admissible matrix domain with a fourth error

Use exactly the finite data of `gamma_shift`:

```text
G=GL_3(F_2),
B=K x delta(G) ~= GL_6(F_2) x GL_3(F_2),
C=C_0 x C_far ~= GL_5(F_2) x GL_3(F_2),
Z=C_0 x delta(K_P),       K_P=GL(<e_1,e_3>),
F=G_Q x G_P <= B,
F_1=tau(G) x delta(G) <= B.
```

The embeddings are the authenticated ones from the stable-letter
construction. The specified isomorphism `alpha_F:F->F_1` sends
`g_Q` to `tau(g)` and `g_P` to `delta(g)`. Also

```text
h=h_0 h_1 in C,
h_0 swaps e_(2,0),e_(1,1) inside C_0,
h_1=(1,w_12) in C_0 x C_far.
```

An admissible tuple consists of a positive dimension `d`, exact
complex unitary representations and a unitary matrix satisfying

```text
rho_B:B->U(d),               rho_C:C->U(d),
rho_B|Z=rho_C|Z,
rho_B(w)!=I,
T in U(d),
T rho_B(f) T^*=rho_B(alpha_F(f))       for every f in F. (10)
```

Write `H=rho_C(h)` and `H_1=rho_C(h_1)`. Evaluate the
words `b,zeta,u,v,D,W,E` from (1) using these representations
on their common matrix space. The shared `c_P,k_P` have the
same values whether evaluated in `B` or `C`. Define

```text
gamma_triangle
 =inf_(tuples (10)) max(
       ||(H rho_B(b))^3-I||,
       ||W-I||,
       ||T H T^*-H_1||,
       ||E-I|| ).                                     (11)
```

Every norm in this artifact's matrix criterion is the operator
norm. The notation `W,E` in (11) means their evaluated matrices.
The old finite packets and the `F` intertwining are exact in the
domain. The third finite packet is a consequence when the relevant
word errors vanish; its table is not an additional restriction on
the domain of (11).

This domain is the identical nonempty domain used to define
`gamma_shift`. Concretely, the regular representation of `B`
can be glued on `Z` to `2016` copies of the regular
representation of `C`. The restrictions along `F` and `F_1`
are both `|B|/|F|` copies of the regular representation of `F`,
so a unitary intertwiner `T` exists. The head survives in the
regular `B` representation.

The old objective is the maximum of the first three errors in
(11). Adding a fourth error increases that maximum pointwise.
Every error in (11) is a distance between unitaries, hence at most
two. Taking infima on this same nonempty domain proves

```text
0<=gamma_shift<=gamma_triangle<=2.                    (12)
```

No strict inequality or positive lower bound is inferred.

## 4. Positivity is exactly marked norm-corona collapse

For each sequence of positive dimensions, let

```text
Q_d=product_n M_(d_n)(C) / directSum_n M_(d_n)(C),
```

where the direct sum is the ideal of sequences tending to zero in
operator norm. Then

```text
gamma_triangle>0
 iff every homomorphism Gamma_triangle->U(Q_d),
     for every dimension sequence, kills w.            (13)
```

First suppose `gamma_triangle=0`. Choose admissible tuples
whose four errors tend to zero. Their exact finite-group tables,
exact intersection and exact `F` intertwining pass to the norm
corona. The three old remaining relators and the new relation
`E=1` also become exact by (11). Thus the chosen generator
sequences define a homomorphism from `Gamma_triangle`.
Every matrix `rho_B(w)` is a nonidentity involution and has
operator-norm distance exactly two from identity. The mark
therefore survives in this corona homomorphism.

Conversely, suppose a corona homomorphism from `Gamma_triangle`
retains `w`. Lift the finitely many values needed from `B,C`
and the stable letter to unitary matrix sequences. Correct each
finite group in its original dimension using the finite-group
operator-norm correction theorem, as in
[the relative-rounding proof](jacobson-boundary-packet-relative-rounding-2026-09-08.md).
The corrections tend to zero in norm.

The corrected restrictions to `Z` are exact representations
which are uniformly close on that fixed finite group. Averaging
their intertwiner over `Z` and taking its unitary polar factor
conjugates one corrected representation by a unitary tending to
identity, making the two restrictions agree exactly.

The two representations of `F` inside the corrected `B` are
now exact. Write them as `rho_0(f)=rho_B(f)` and
`rho_1(f)=rho_B(alpha_F(f))`. The lifted `T` approximately
intertwines them, with

```text
epsilon=max_(f in F)||rho_1(f)T-T rho_0(f)|| -> 0.
```

Average once more, this time over the fixed finite group `F`:

```text
X=|F|^(-1) sum_(f in F) rho_1(f) T rho_0(f)^*.
```

This is an exact intertwiner and `||X-T||<=epsilon`. For
sufficiently late coordinates `epsilon<1`, so `X` is
invertible. Its unitary polar factor `T'` is an exact
intertwiner and satisfies `||T'-T||<=2epsilon`. This gives
the exact final condition in (10), in the original dimension.

All corrections just described tend to zero in operator norm.
Every fixed-word value consequently changes by a quantity tending
to zero, by telescoping its finitely many unitary factors. Thus
the four errors in (11) still tend to zero. In particular `E`
contains no stable letter, so the last correction from `T` to
`T'` does not change its evaluated value at all.

The exact corrected head involutions still represent the original
nonidentity corona element. Infinitely many are nonidentity;
restricting to those coordinates gives admissible tuples (10)
with objective tending to zero. Therefore `gamma_triangle=0`,
proving the other direction of (13).

Only fixed finite-group correction, finite averaging and finite
matrix polar decomposition occur in this argument. No estimate
for invariant vectors of an infinite subgroup is used.

## 5. What remains

The group (2) is a proper, finitely presented quotient with the
marked head retained and the complete third finite packet (9)
proved. Criterion (13) reduces its marked norm-corona obstruction
to the explicit four-error infimum (11).

Because `w!=1` in this group, a proof that `gamma_triangle>0`
would prove that it is non-MF. The present artifact supplies no
such proof. The subsequent affine-colimit calculation identifies
`Gamma_triangle` with its literal image and proves that this one
word normally generates the entire previous literal kernel. It
therefore makes positivity equivalent to non-MF for that explicit
literal group, while leaving positivity unresolved.
