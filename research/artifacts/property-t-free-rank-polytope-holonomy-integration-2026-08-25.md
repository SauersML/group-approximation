# Property-(T)-free rank-polytope and holonomy integration

Date: 2026-08-25

This artifact records the audited integration of the supplied
“Property-(T)-Free Non-MF — General Laws, Rank Polytopes & Holonomy Theory”
note into Cairn.  It is deliberately an integration ledger rather than a
claim: the authoritative reusable mathematics lives in the adjacent claim
and route nodes.

## New established reusable layer

- `finite-packet-rank-polytope-is-complete` records the arbitrary finite
  semisimple-packet polytope, quantitative `l_infinity`/Euclidean gaps,
  rational Farkas certificates, exact rational realization inside the
  polytope, direct-sum convexification, tensor-spectator invariance, and the
  `k`-row weighted-regression specialization.
- `finite-packet-holonomy-cuts-the-multiplicity-simplex` records the finite
  gauge-network theorem, cycle-holonomy fixed simplex, orbit-count law, and
  transitive-holonomy one-row collapse.
- `holonomy-rank-gap-is-a-carrier-local-mf-certificate` records the abstract
  MF compiler once finite word data authenticate the carrier, packet,
  transports, and selector reads.  Its proof normalizes trace inside a
  nonzero invariant corner, so it requires no positive ambient density.

## Scope fences retained

1. Rank-polytope completeness is completeness only for the authenticated
   rank/multiplicity data.  A point inside the polytope kills that proof
   route; it does not construct a representation of unrelated group
   relators.
2. Carrier locality requires a common nonzero invariant carrier and
   operator-norm control of the fixed obstruction words.  Ambient
   normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt control alone cannot be renormalized on a
   vanishing-density corner.
3. The finite packet exactification theorem does not authenticate that a
   presentation supplies the desired packet, common carrier, holonomy, or
   selectors.  Those are explicit hypotheses of the compiler.
4. The abstract theory does not close the binary-Leavitt Property-(T)-free
   goal.  `binary-leavitt-two-shared-gauge-selector-compiler` remains open:
   the mixed native occurrences must still be shown to reuse one
   multiplicity state on one carrier.

These fences preserve the central useful separation in the supplied note:
once construction-specific authentication is proved, the terminal
obstruction is finite rational convex geometry; before authentication, it
is only a theorem schema.
