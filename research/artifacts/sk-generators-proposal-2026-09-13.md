# sk-generators: small generating sets for G_X, and the Fibonacci example (proposal, 2026-09-13)

## Findings
1. For every binary alphabet, three matrices of orders 7, 2, 2 generate G_X.
   - The proof via Goursat's lemma takes about eight lines: route `unit-idempotent-three-generators-goursat-proof`. It targets the existing claim `unit-idempotent-elementary-group-has-three-torsion-generators`, whose first proof uses explicit word identities.
   - So every binary G_X is a quotient of the fixed virtually free group (SL_3(F_2)×SL_3(F_2)) ∗ C_2, with the finite factor embedded.
2. The theorem holds for any ring generated by a unit and an idempotent. So the first Fibonacci finite model, SL_15(F_2) with u ↦ P_5 and p ↦ diag(1,0,1,1,0), is generated by the same three matrices read in M_15(F_2).
3. Whether two elements generate G_X is OPEN (claim `binary-subshift-elementary-groups-are-two-generated`, with Attempts).
   - Coprime separation of commuting pieces dies on centralizers.
   - (2,3)-generation through a constant 3-cycle gives at most S_4 or a Goursat graph inside EL_3(F_2[p]).
   - An exact search over the free ring F_2⟨u^{±1},p | p²=p⟩ (MSI) found no coefficient-u root for the natural two-element candidates.
4. The note's generating set (l.94-95) contains a redundant coefficient: 1 = Σ_a e_a.

\begin{remark}
If $A=\{a,b\}$ and $p=e_a$, then $G_X$ is generated by
\[
 c=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 t=e_{12}(1+p)\,e_{13}(p),\quad
 w=e_{12}(u)\,e_{21}(u^{-1})\,e_{12}(u),

of orders $7$, $2$ and $2$. Indeed $\F_2[p]\cong\F_2\times\F_2$, so
$\EL_3(\F_2[p])\cong\SL_3(\F_2)^2$, and $\langle c,t\rangle$ projects onto
$\langle c,e_{12}(1)\rangle$ and $\langle c,e_{13}(1)\rangle$. These contain
elements of orders $7,4$ and $7,3$, while the maximal subgroups of $\SL_3(\F_2)$

$ct$ have orders $4$ and $3$, so $\langle c,t\rangle$ is not the graph of an
automorphism, and Goursat's lemma gives $\langle c,t\rangle=\EL_3(\F_2[p])$.

$ws=\operatorname{diag}(u,u^{-1},1)$ conjugates $e_{13}(1)$ to $e_{13}(u^{\pm1})$.
By~\eqref{eq:elementary} the coefficients $r$ with all $e_{ij}(r)$ in
$\langle c,t,w\rangle$ form a subring containing $u^{\pm1}$ and $p$, so it is $R$.
\end{remark}

## Proposal P3 (optional example; the user's call)
b965d63ba had a Fibonacci remark (its l.497-512); the short version cut it. An F_2 version, inserted at the end of "Finite models" after l.152:
```
For the Fibonacci subshift over $\{a,b\}$, the return word $abaab$ has the
cyclic windows $aba$, $baa$, $aab$, $aba$, $bab$ of length $3$, exactly the words of
length $3$ occurring in $X$. With $N=5$, $u\mapsto P$ and
$e_a\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(1,0,1,1,0)$ give the first finite model, inside
$\GL_{15}(\F_2)$.
```
- (b) Check: the Fibonacci words of length 3 are {aab, aba, baa, bab} (Sturmian, 3+1 words). The cyclic windows of abaab starting at positions 0..4 are aba, baa, aab, aba, bab. The letter a sits at positions 0, 2, 3.
- (c) Length: +5 lines.
- (d) Trade-off: makes the construction concrete for a first-time reader, and asks for no new trust. It does not shorten or strengthen anything.
- (e) Status: elementary. The same example is in b965d63ba.

## Not proposed
Two-element generation is open (claim `binary-subshift-elementary-groups-are-two-generated`). The obstructions recorded there:
- (A1) Commuting coprime pieces. C(w) ∩ EL_3(F_2[p]) = 1 and C(m′) ∩ EL_3(F_2[p]) = 1. GL_3(F_2) has no elements of order 6 or 14. Scalar permutation units of finite order add only locally constant coefficients. Unipotent scalars (1+r)I with r = ab, ba = 0 add only r.
- (A2) ⟨P, involution⟩ ⊆ S_4 or a Goursat graph.
- (A3) Exact free-ring search: sanity pair (c,t) recovered all 28224 elements. The pairs (m′,t) and (m′, e_12(1)e_13(p)) closed at 48 and 210 elements under the size cap. (m′, e_12(p)) reached the 250k cap, and ((1+p)c + p e_12(1), w) closed at 282 elements. Neither produced a coefficient-u root.

If two-generation is proved, the remark worth a sentence would be "every binary G_X is a quotient of PSL_2(Z)" (if orders 2 and 3), or "G_X is 2-generated".
