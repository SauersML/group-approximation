# Decoded shell atoms cannot be pointwise close to raw balanced lamps

Forty-two raw conjugates of a balanced lamp each have trace `1/2`; forty-two
raw positive joint atoms each have trace `1/4`.  A partial 42-atom PVM has
total trace at most one.

Expanding squared projection distances shows that its total squared distance
from the raw orbit is at least `42 alpha-1`.  The resulting floors are `20`
for half projections and `19/2` for quarter projections.

Therefore the raw denominator section identity is not a decoded-to-raw PVM
proximity theorem.  It correctly aligns two occurrences of each raw lamp,
but the decoded building atoms must instead be orthogonal joint spectral
functions of the full raw lamp family.  The next valid target is a
cylinder/character decoder tied covariantly to the denominator words.
