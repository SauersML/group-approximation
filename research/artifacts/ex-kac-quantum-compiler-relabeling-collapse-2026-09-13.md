# Relabeling collapse: when nonabelian fibres carry nothing beyond groups

Lane `ex-kac-quantum-compiler`, 2026-09-13, part 4.  Node:
`relabelable-coset-systems-are-solution-group-corners` with its `-proof`
route.  Definitions follow `coset-systems-are-torsor-isomorphism-games`.

## 0. The point

A coset system `S` over nonabelian groups gives a torsor pair
`X(S_1), X(S)` whose quantum symmetry is not a group dual (that node, T3).
The *algebra* `A(S)` carrying a possible `qc`/`qa` gap is only a synchronous
algebra: PVMs indexed by the sets `K_q`, with orthogonality outside the
relation sets `R_(qq') = c_(qq') D_(qq')`.  The group structure enters the
torsor digraph, not the algebra.  So a gap is a property of the relation sets
alone.  When those sets become cosets of abelian groups after relabeling each
`K_q`, the gap is the solution-group gap, and it yields a finitely presented
nonhyperlinear group.

## 1. Statements

**Relabeling.**  A *relabeling* of `S` is a family of abelian groups `A_q`
with bijections `β_q : K_q -> A_q`.  It is *affine* if every
`(β_q x β_(q'))(R_(qq'))` is a coset of a subgroup of `A_q x A_(q')`.

**Theorem R.**  Suppose `S` admits an affine relabeling.  Let `n` be the least
common multiple of the exponents of the `A_q`, and `ω = exp(2πi/n)`.

- **(R1)** `A(S) ≅ A(S^β)`, where `S^β` is the coset system over the `A_q`
  with relations `(β x β)(R)`.
- **(R2)** `A(S^β) ≅ p_ω C^*(Γ)`, where `p_ω = (1/n) sum_j ω^(-j) J^j`.
  - `Γ` is generated by `x_(q,χ)` for `q in Q` and `χ in Â_q`, together with a
    central `J` with `J^n = 1`.
  - Relations:

    ```text
    x_(q,χ) x_(q,χ') = x_(q,χχ'),     x_(q,1) = 1,     [J, x_(q,χ)] = 1,
    x_(q,χ) x_(q',χ') = J^(k(χ,χ'))   for (q,q') in E and (χ,χ') ⊥ D^β_(qq'),
    ```

    where `ω^(k(χ,χ')) = (χ (x) χ')(c^β_(qq'))`.
  - `Γ` is finitely presented.
- **(R3)** If `S` is tracial and not `R^U`, then `Γ` is not hyperlinear.

So on relabelable coset systems the non-group-dual symmetry of the torsor pair
is not what carries the gap.  The same algebra is the linking algebra of the
torsor pair over the abelian groups `A_q`, where the symmetry is a group dual,
and it is a corner of a finitely presented group algebra.

## 2. Proofs

**(R1).**  `A(S)` is presented by projections `p_q^s` (`s in K_q`), row sums
`sum_s p_q^s = 1`, and `p_q^s p_(q')^(s') = 0` for `(s,s') notin R_(qq')`.
Renaming generators by `β_q` gives the presentation of `A(S^β)`.

**(R2).**  In `A(S^β)` put `X_(q,χ) = sum_(a in A_q) χ(a) p_q^a`.
- These are unitaries, and `χ |-> X_(q,χ)` is a representation of `Â_q`.
- Let `(χ,χ')` annihilate `D = D^β_(qq')`, and let `c = c^β_(qq')`.  Products
  `p_q^a p_(q')^(a')` vanish off `cD`.  On `cD` the character `χ (x) χ'` is
  the constant `(χ(x)χ')(c)`.  And `sum_(all (a,a')) p_q^a p_(q')^(a') = 1`.
  Hence `X_(q,χ) X_(q',χ') = (χ (x) χ')(c)·1`.
- So `x_(q,χ) |-> X_(q,χ)`, `J |-> ω` defines a unital *-homomorphism
  `C^*(Γ)/<J - ω> -> A(S^β)`.

Conversely, in `C^*(Γ)/<J-ω>` put `P_q^a = |A_q|^(-1) sum_χ conj(χ(a)) x_(q,χ)`.
These are PVMs, because the `x_(q,χ)` represent `Â_q`.  For `(q,q') in E`:
- Averaging the cross relations over `D^⊥` gives, with
  `1_(cD)(y) = |D^⊥|^(-1) sum_(ψ in D^⊥) ψ(y) conj(ψ(c))`,

  ```text
  sum_(y notin cD) P_q^(y_1) P_(q')^(y_2) = 0.
  ```

- Multiply on the left by `P_q^(a)` and on the right by `P_(q')^(a')`.  PVM
  orthogonality leaves `[(a,a') notin cD] P_q^a P_(q')^(a') = 0`.

So the relations of `A(S^β)` hold, and the maps are mutually inverse on
generators.  Finally `C^*(Γ)/<J-ω> = p_ω C^*(Γ)`, since `J` is central of
order dividing `n`.

**(R3).**
- A tracial state on `A(S)` gives a representation of `Γ` with `J = ω`.  So
  `J^j != e` for `0 < j < n`, and the regular trace gives `τ(p_ω) = 1/n`.
- If `Γ` were hyperlinear, `L(Γ)` would embed trace-preservingly in `R^U`.
  Then `p_ω L(Γ) p_ω` embeds in a corner `p R^U p` with `τ(p) = 1/n`, which is
  again isomorphic to `R^U`.
- Composing `A(S) ≅ p_ω C^*(Γ) -> p_ω L(Γ) p_ω` gives a unital
  *-homomorphism `A(S) -> R^U`, contradicting the absence of `R^U` models.

## 3. Which systems are relabelable

- **All `K_q` abelian.**  Take `β = id`.  This is the linear-system arm,
  including the colored CFI graphs over `F_2`.
- **Nonabelian fibres whose relations factor through abelian data.**
  - The unconstrained `S_3` fibre adjoined to a linear-system gap, from the
    caveat on `tracial-non-ru-coset-system-with-nonabelian-fibre-exists`, is
    relabelable.  So its gap is inherited, as that caveat says.
  - More generally: suppose every relation touching a nonabelian `K_q` is
    agreement of `K_q/N` for a normal `N` with abelian quotient, or a
    bijection cell by cell.  Suppose moreover that the cell identifications
    around the constraint graph are affine for chosen abelian structures.
    Then the system is relabelable.
- **Single relations are always relabelable.**  A Goursat coset is a union of
  matched blocks of equal sizes.  Choosing cyclic labels on each block system
  makes it an abelian coset.  The obstruction can only come from several
  partitions of one `K_q` together with identifications around cycles.

**What is not decided here.**
- It is not shown that any coset system with `A(S) != 0` fails to be
  relabelable.
- Nonzero algebras force the coarse PVMs to be invariant under the holonomy
  of every cycle of cell bijections.  So the naive obstruction, a regular
  nonsolvable holonomy group, already kills the support.
- A genuine obstruction has to come from how several quotient partitions of
  one fibre interlock: a partition network with no abelian realization.
- Independently of relabeling, an algebra not of this form could still be a
  group-algebra corner through some other presentation.  Theorem R gives a
  sufficient condition for collapse, not an invariant.

## 4. Consequence for the two targets

- `tracial-non-ru-coset-system-with-nonabelian-fibre-exists` can go beyond
  `non-hyperlinear-group` only through a system that is **not** affinely
  relabelable.
- The same holds for the compiler.  A perfect-completeness compiler into
  relabelable coset systems is a perfect-completeness compiler into linear
  systems over abelian groups.  That is goal-level, as in
  `lcs-compiler-target-is-goal-equivalent`, whose `F_2` argument transfers
  verbatim.
