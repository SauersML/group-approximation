## 1. (a) Elementary and unit groups over Leavitt and Steinberg algebras

**1.1 K_1 and commutators (already imported).** Ara–Goodearl–Pardo arXiv:math/0111066 Theorem 2.4: in a
purely infinite simple ring the canonical map from units to K_1 is surjective with kernel the commutator
subgroup. The non-MF paper uses it in Thm [MF quotients of unit groups] (tex l.1218).

**1.2 Generators and finite presentation of L_K(1,d)^× (Khanh–Thanh, arXiv:2607.10351, July 2026).**
- Thm `thm:all-units` (l.439): "For every field $K$, the Leavitt general linear group is the full unit group:
  $\LGLtwo=L_K(R_2)^\times$." The abstract: ordered leaf sets of the rooted d-ary tree give copies of general
  linear groups inside L_d^×, and these generate L_d^× for every d ≥ 2.
- Thm `thm:finite-generation` (l.510): "Let $K$ be a field. Then $L_K(R_2)^\times$ is finitely generated if and
  only if $K$ is finite."
- Thm `thm:finite-presentation-reduction` (l.863): "Let $K=\mathbb F_q$, let $d\geq2$, and let $n=1+r(d-1)\geq5$
  for some $r\geq0$. The following conditions are equivalent: (i) $L_d^\times$ is finitely presented; (ii)
  $L_d^\times/K^\times$ is finitely presented; (iii) $E_n(L_d)$ is finitely presented; (iv) $K_2(n,L_d)$ is
  finitely generated. Moreover, $K_2(n,L_d)\cong H_2(E_n(L_d),\mathbb Z)$."
- Prop `prop:stable-Ktwo-Leavitt` (l.898): "For every finite field $K=\mathbb F_q$ and every $d\geq2$,
  $K_2(L_d)\cong\{\lambda\in K^\times:\lambda^{d-1}=1\}$." So K_2(L_d) = 0 when K = F_2 or d = 2.
- The repo already has the earlier Khanh verification artifact `khanh-2609-08428-leavitt-gl-verified-2026-09-11.md`
  and the node `binary-leavitt-units-generated-by-c3-and-c2-cubed`. I did not cross-check these against 2607.10351.

**1.3 General linear groupoid of a Leavitt path algebra (arXiv:2608.21026, August 2026).** Thm `thm:main`
(l.441): "Suppose that Condition (P) is satisfied. Then the general linear groupoid $\Ge(R)$ is generated by
the subgroupoids $\Ge(R,\mu)~(\mu\in \Lambda\cup\{0\})$." The proof runs Bergman's module-transfer theorem
(Bergman 1974, Thm 2.3). Condition (P) was not read.

**1.4 Normal subgroups of EL_n over noncommutative simple rings: no general theorem found.**
- Vaserstein's sandwich classification covers rings finite over their centre, Banach algebras and some
  regular rings. The search surfaced Vaserstein (Compositio 74, 1990) for GL_2 and Gerasimov (Mat. Sb. 134,
  1987) on units of free products. Neither was read; both are paywalled.
- The C*-algebra analogue exists (arXiv:1603.01884, "Normal subgroups of invertibles and of unitaries in a
  C*-algebra"; the search snippet says the commutator subgroup of the identity component is simple modulo
  its centre for simple unital C*-algebras). Not read.
- **On main:** the Pestov 9.1 artifact proves every normal subgroup of EL_3(LC(X,F_q)⋊Z) is central or
  everything (reviewed twice), and `free-minimal-crossed-products-are-simple-with-rank-models` proves
  simplicity and the centre for free minimal Z^d (unreviewed). For Leavitt rings the non-MF paper gets
  [H,H] from AGP 2.4 rather than from a normal-subgroup theorem.
- **Consequence for un-simplicity.** A uniform "EL_n(A_k(𝒢))/Z is simple for every minimal effective ample 𝒢"
  is, as far as this bounded search shows, not in the literature. It has to be proved, e.g. from the
  Steinberg simplicity criterion plus a Bak/Vaserstein-type absorption argument.

## 2. (b) Rank functions, states and paradox

**2.1 Steinberg algebra simplicity (BCFS arXiv:1204.3127).** Thm `thm:alg_simple` (l.430): "Let $G$ be a locally
compact, Hausdorff, étale groupoid such that $G^{(0)}$ is totally disconnected. Then $A(G)$ is simple if and only
if $G$ is both effective and minimal." (Complex coefficients in that paper; the repo nodes
`minimal-subshift-algebra-is-simple-lef-ring` and `simple-kazhdan-lef-group-from-minimal-subshift` already cite it
together with Steinberg arXiv:1408.6014 for general fields.)

**2.2 Tarski at the groupoid level (Rainone–Sims arXiv:1707.04516, C*-level).** Imported as
`rainone-sims-type-semigroup-dichotomy`.
- Thm `Tarski` (l.1103): "Let $(S,+)$ be an abelian monoid equipped with the algebraic ordering, and let $\theta$ be an
  element of $S$. Then the following are equivalent: (i) $(n+1)\theta\nleq n\theta$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$, that is
  $\theta$ is completely non-paradoxical. (ii) There is a non-trivial state $\nu: S\rightarrow[0,\infty]$ with
  $\nu(\theta)=1$."
- Thm `theoremsfcnp` (l.1196), for étale G with compact unit space: (i) C*_r(G) admits a faithful tracial state;
  (ii) C*_r(G) is stably finite; (iii) G satisfies the coboundary condition; (iv) G is completely
  non-paradoxical. "The implications $(i)\Rightarrow(ii)\Rightarrow(iii)\Rightarrow(iv)$ always hold. If $G$ is
  minimal, then $(iv)\Rightarrow(i)$ and all properties are equivalent. If $G$ is minimal and amenable, then
  properties $(i)$ through $(iv)$ are all equivalent to (v) The C*-algebra $C^*_{r}(G)$ is quasidiagonal."
- Thm `pitheorem` (l.1458) and Thm `dichotomy` (l.1512): for G étale, ample, minimal, topologically principal
  with almost unperforated S(G), "(i) The C*-algebra $C^*_r(G)$ is simple and is either purely infinite or stably
  finite. (ii) If $G$ is also amenable, then $C^*_r(G)$ is simple and is either purely infinite or quasidiagonal."
- Remark after `dichotomy`: this recovers and extends Bönicke–Li Thm 5.11 and Cor 5.13.

**2.3 C*-pure infiniteness (Brown–Clark–Sierakowski arXiv:1403.4959).** Abstract: for Hausdorff, étale, minimal,
topologically principal G, C*_r(G) is purely infinite simple iff all nonzero positive elements of C_0(G^(0)) are
infinite. For ample G, iff every nonzero projection in C_0(G^(0)) is infinite.

**2.4 Algebraic proper pure infiniteness of Steinberg algebras (Larki arXiv:1901.07094).** Imported as
`purely-infinite-steinberg-algebras-characterized`. Thm `thm3.4`: for G strongly effective, ample and
Hausdorff, K a field and 𝓑 a basis of compact open sets of G^(0), the following are equivalent: (1) A_K(G) is
properly purely infinite; (2) every 1_V (V ∈ 𝓑) is properly infinite; (3) for every invariant open U and nonempty
relatively compact open V ⊆ D := G^(0)∖U, 1_V is an infinite idempotent in A_K(G_D); (4) every nonzero one-sided
ideal in any quotient of A_K(G) contains an infinite idempotent. Cor `cor3.6`: locally contractive restrictions
give proper pure infiniteness.

**2.5 Rank functions on the Pestov ring (Ara–Claramunt arXiv:1902.06476).** Imported as
`cantor-z-crossed-product-embeds-in-continuous-factor`; details in part 2 §3.2.

**2.6 On main.** `leavitt-algebra-has-no-unital-rank-model` (L_{F_2}(1,2) admits no unital homomorphism into any
rank ultraproduct), `rank-condition-rings-carry-sylvester-rank-functions`, and the artifact
`leavitt-ring-rank-approximability-collapse-2026-09-12.md` (rk(1−st) = rk(1−ts) in every Sylvester rank function).

**2.7 What is missing (bounded).** I found no statement of the ALGEBRAIC Tarski dichotomy for Steinberg algebras:
"A_k(𝒢) (minimal, effective, ample) completely non-paradoxical at [1_{G^(0)}] ⟺ A_k(𝒢) carries a faithful Sylvester
rank function ⟺ A_k(𝒢) stably finite". Rainone–Sims proves the C*-version (faithful trace ⟺ stably finite ⟺
completely non-paradoxical, for minimal G). An invariant probability measure on G^(0) should give a rank function
on A_k(𝒢) through the regular representation, as Ara–Claramunt do for Z, but for general 𝒢 I found no source. This is
a clean UN target.
