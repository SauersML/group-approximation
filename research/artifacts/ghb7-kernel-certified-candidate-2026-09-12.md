# An explicit candidate for flexible HS stability: the congruence kernel of `GHB_2(7)`

Lane `nh-hyperbolic-stable-candidate`, 2026-09-12.

The premise `kazhdan-hyperbolic-b2-group-flexibly-hs-stable` asks for one
word-hyperbolic Kazhdan group `Γ` with `b_2(Γ;Q) >= 1` that is flexibly
Hilbert--Schmidt stable. Until now the only source-verified members of that class
were Gromov density-model groups, which exist with high probability but have no
certified presentation. This artifact certifies an explicit member: a torsion-free
finite-index subgroup of the Kac--Moody--Steinberg group `GHB_2(7)`.

Everything below except the stability question is proved. Stability stays open.

## 1. Source, read from the PDF

Caprace--Conder--Kaluba--Witzel, *Hyperbolic generalized triangle groups, property (T)
and finite simple quotients*, arXiv:2011.09276. Downloaded from arXiv on 2026-09-12
and extracted with ghostscript on MSI (`/scratch.global/sauer354/hs-cand/cckw2.txt`).

- **Theorem 1.3.** For each odd prime `p`,
  `G_{HB_2}(p) = <a,b,c | a^p, b^p, c^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b], [c,b,c], [c,b,b,c], [c,b,b,b], [c,a,c], [c,a,a,c], [c,a,a,a]>`
  is infinite hyperbolic, and for `p >= 7` it has Kazhdan's property (T).
  This is `GHB p` in `GroupApproximation/Kazhdan/KMSGroupPresentation.lean`.
- **Theorem 3.1** ("well known"). Let `G(T)` be a triangle of groups with trivial face
  group, vertex groups `X_i` generated by the images of their two edge groups, and let
  `r_i` be half the girth of the coset graph at `X_i`. If `1/r_0 + 1/r_1 + 1/r_2 <= 1`:
  - (i) `G` acts isometrically by simplicial automorphisms on a CAT(0) 2-dimensional
    simplicial complex `Y(T)`, with a 2-simplex as strict fundamental domain; the link
    at `v_i` is the coset graph and the stabilizer of `v_i` is `X_i`;
  - (ii) every finite subgroup of `G` is conjugate into some `G_{v_i}`;
  - (iv) if some homomorphism `ψ : G → F` to a finite group is injective on each
    `G_{v_i}`, then `Ker(ψ)` is a torsion-free subgroup of finite index;
  - (v) if the inequality is strict, `G` is non-elementary hyperbolic and `Y(T)`
    carries a `G`-invariant CAT(−1) metric.
- **Half girth type.** CCKW record that `G_{HB_2}(p)` has half-girth type `(3,4,4)`.
- **Proposition 7.2.** `U_3(p) = <a,b | a^p, b^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b]>` and, for `p > 2`,
  `U_4(p) = <a,b | a^p, b^p, [a,b,a], [a,b,b,a], [a,b,b,b]>`, with `|U_4(p)| = p^4`.
- **Proposition 7.15.** Assignments of `a, b, c` to explicit block unipotent matrices
  extend to homomorphisms `G_{HB_2}(p) → SL_{4k}(F_q)`.
- **Not in the source.** No Euler characteristic or Betti number of any KMS group or
  subgroup is computed; a text search for "Euler", "Betti", "cohomolog" finds nothing
  relevant.

## 3. The computation

Let `G = GHB_2(7)`, `ψ` as above, `H = Ker(ψ)`.

1. **Structure of the triangle of groups.**
   - Vertex groups: `X_0 = <a,b> ≅ U_3(7)` of order `343`, `X_1 = <c,b>` and
     `X_2 = <c,a>`, both `≅ U_4(7)` of order `2401`.
   - Edge groups: `<a>`, `<b>`, `<c>`, each cyclic of order `7`.
   - Face group: trivial.
   - Half girths `(3,4,4)`, and `1/3 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 5/6 < 1`. So Theorem 3.1(i),(ii),(v)
     apply: `G` acts on the CAT(−1), hence CAT(0) and contractible, 2-complex `Y`, with
     one orbit of triangles (trivial stabilizer), three orbits of edges (stabilizers of
     order `7`) and three orbits of vertices (stabilizers `X_0, X_1, X_2`).
2. **`H` is torsion-free, of finite index, and acts freely.** `ψ` is injective on each
   `X_i`, so Theorem 3.1(iv) makes `H` torsion-free of finite index. Every simplex
   stabilizer is a conjugate of a subgroup of some `X_i`, and `H ∩ g X_i g^{-1} = 1`
   because `H` is normal and `ψ` is injective on `X_i`. So `H` acts freely.
3. **Cell counts.** `H` is normal, so `G/H` acts on `H\Y`. For a simplex `σ`, the
   `H`-orbits in `G·σ` are the cosets of the image of `G_σ` in `G/H`. That image is
   isomorphic to `G_σ`, because `G_σ ∩ H = 1`. So the orbit `G·σ` splits into
   `[G:H]/|G_σ|` cells of `H\Y`:
   - vertices: `[G:H](1/343 + 1/2401 + 1/2401)`;
   - edges: `[G:H](3/7)`;
   - triangles: `[G:H]`.
4. **Euler characteristic.**
   `χ(H\Y) = [G:H]·(1/343 + 2/2401 − 3/7 + 1) = [G:H]·1381/2401`.
5. **Betti numbers.** `Y` is contractible and `H` acts freely, properly and cocompactly,
   so `H\Y` is a finite 2-dimensional `K(H,1)`. So `b_k(H) = 0` for `k >= 3` and
   `χ(H) = 1 − b_1(H) + b_2(H)`. Property (T) passes to finite-index subgroups and gives
   finite abelianization, so `b_1(H) = 0`. Hence

   ```text
   b_2(H;Q) = [G:H]·1381/2401 − 1.
   ```

6. **The index.** `[G:H] = |ψ(G)|` is divisible by `|ψ(X_1)| = 2401`, so it is a
   positive multiple of `2401`, and `b_2(H;Q) >= 1381 − 1 = 1380`.
7. **Hyperbolicity and (T) of `H`.** `H` has finite index in `G`, which is hyperbolic
   (Theorem 3.1(v), or CCKW Theorem 1.3) and Kazhdan (CCKW Theorem 1.3 at `p = 7`, and
   `hasKazhdanPropertyT_ghb7`). Both properties pass to finite-index subgroups.

**Conclusion.** `H = Ker(ψ)` is an explicit, finitely presented, torsion-free,
word-hyperbolic group with property (T), a finite aspherical 2-dimensional presentation
complex, and `b_2(H;Q) >= 1380`.

**`G` itself does not qualify.** `G` acts properly with finite stabilizers on the
contractible `Y` with quotient a single triangle, so `H^*(G;Q) = H^*(G\Y;Q)` and
`b_2(G;Q) = 0`. The kernel is needed.

## 4. What this changes

- **An explicit instance of the premise.** `kazhdan-hyperbolic-b2-group-flexibly-hs-stable`
  now follows from the single statement "`H` is flexibly HS stable"
  (`ghb7-congruence-kernel-flexibly-hs-stable`), with no probabilistic existence input.
  Through `kazhdan-hyperbolic-hs-stability-kills-hyperlinearity` that statement would
  give a non-hyperlinear hyperbolic group, hence a nonsofic and non-residually-finite one.
- **The calibration is unchanged, but sharper.**
  - If `H` is hyperlinear, stability of `H` forces a persistent prime degree-two class on
    a finite-index subgroup of `H` (`hs-stable-hyperlinear-kazhdan-hyperbolic-forces-persistent-class`),
    i.e. `H` is not good in Serre's sense. With `b_2(H) >= 1380` there are many classes to
    test.
  - If `H` is not hyperlinear, that is already an answer.
- **Candidate-specific structure a stability proof could use.** The class-wide screen
  `classwide-hyperbolic-kazhdan-hs-stability-covers-fp-kazhdan` forbids arguments that
  work for classes closed under Belegradek--Osin covers. `H` carries features those
  covers lack:
  - a free cocompact action on a CAT(−1) 2-complex whose links are the explicit coset
    graphs of `U_3(7)` and `U_4(7)`, with the EJZ representation angles already computed
    in Lean;
  - an explicit finite aspherical presentation complex.
  Local spectral data of this kind is what Garland-type cohomology vanishing and
  de la Salle-type spectral stability criteria consume.

## 5. Not done

- Flexible HS stability of `H`: open, and no attack was run in this lane.
- The exact index `[G:H] = |ψ(GHB_2(7))|`: not computed, and not needed for `b_2 >= 1`.
- Whether `H` is residually finite or hyperlinear: unknown.
- Novelty: the orbifold Euler characteristic count is standard (Brown, *Cohomology of
  Groups*, IX.7). What is new here is its use to certify an explicit member of the
  candidate class. The literature search was limited to CCKW.
