## What is computed

For an SFT with local rules, a finite window `W ⊆ Z^2` and a target `v`, the window algebra `A_W` is generated by
idempotents `e_a(z)`, `z ∈ W`, with the following relations:
- (Q1) orthogonal partitions of unity at each site;
- (Q2) commutation at ℓ¹-distance `<= 2`;
- (Q3) vanishing of every pattern on a subset of a ball `B_1(z)` that is not in the language. This covers illegal
  dominoes and triangles, and, where noted, locally legal patterns that do not extend to the 5×5 box around `z`.

All of these relations hold in the local tiling algebra `U_1` of `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`. Singular's
letterplace `twostd` computes a two-sided Gröbner basis of `A_W` truncated at degree `deg`, and each commutator
`[e_a(0), e_b(v)]` is reduced modulo it. Here `|v|_1 = 3`, one step beyond the (Q2) range.

- **Sound.** `certified` means the commutator lies in the ideal, so it vanishes in `U_1`.
- **Not a refutation.** `undecided` means only that no derivation was found in that window up to that degree.
- **Windows.** A site `w` is in the window with detour `d` if `|w|_1 + |v − w|_1 <= |v|_1 + d`. Detour 0 is the
  rectangle spanned by `0` and `v`.
- **Coefficients.** All computations are over `Q`.
- **Pipeline probe.** In `k⟨x, y⟩ / (x² − x, y² − y, xyx − xy)`, the element `xyx − xy` reduces to 0 and `xy − yx`
  does not. The probe passed in both jobs.

## Reading

1. **Calibration passes.** Every known rigid case is certified at every tested direction once the window is
   adequate. The full shift certifies nothing.
2. **The colour product is certified exactly where theory says.**
   - At `(2,1)` all 16 pairs are certified. This is as expected: the colour product is 4-way corner deterministic,
     so separations off the axes commute by determinism.
   - At `(3,0)` precisely the 8 pairs with equal row colour are undecided. For these the wall across columns 1–2
     gives a two-dimensional non-commuting family, so they are genuinely not in the ideal.
   - The 8 pairs with different row colours are certified: both products vanish.

   So the engine certifies exactly the determinism-derivable pairs and stops exactly at the wall pairs, on a case with
   two-sided faults.
   No calibration case needs a derivation beyond determinism. The right positive test for the noncommutative part
   would be a known-rigid SFT with a two-sided fault. A bounded search of main found no unconditional one.
3. **Labbé is out of reach for this engine.** At 19 tiles the window algebra has about 9000 relations on 114 variables
   and times out even at degree 3. A structure-aware engine is needed, for instance working with edge-colour
   marginals, or with the scale-2 data of `labbe-shift-is-quantum-rigid-iff-rigid-at-scale-two`. No conclusion about
   `Ω_U`.
4. **No self-simulating candidate was searched.** By `sfts-without-two-sided-faults-are-quantum-rigid`, a determinism
   certificate for a fixed-point tile set exists only when it has no two-sided fault, and then no search is needed.
   The design analysis on `free-minimal-sft-without-two-sided-faults-exists` found no explicit H1–H4 tile set with
   one-sided flow across every line. The one-way reversible-automaton design keeps the vertical line two-sided.

## Lesson for general BH

The bounded-range search is sound and cheap on small alphabets, and at `D = 1` it already sees the difference that
matters.
- It reproduced the determinism derivation in every tested case.
- It stops exactly on wall pairs.
- Its noncommutative reach beyond determinism is untested, since no unconditionally rigid Z^2 SFT with a two-sided
  fault was found on main.

Scaling to a real candidate (Labbé, or a fixed point) needs structure-aware generators rather than raw tile
idempotents. The certificate question itself sits, as predicted, only at the two-sided faults.
