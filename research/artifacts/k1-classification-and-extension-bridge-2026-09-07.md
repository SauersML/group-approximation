# The MF quotient is K_1, and what that says about stabilization

2026-09-07.  Audit of two forwarded notes taken together: a classification of
the MF images of the unit group of a purely infinite simple ring, and a
bridge stating both projects as one question about extending representations
along `R^x -> GL_n(R)`.  Both re-derived here.

## The classification

For `R` countable unital purely infinite simple, every homomorphism from
`R^x` to an MF group factors uniquely through `kappa : R^x -> K_1(R)`, and
`ker kappa = [R^x, R^x]` has full MF radical.  So

```text
Rad_MF(R^x) = [R^x, R^x],      R^x / Rad_MF(R^x) = K_1(R).
```

Three inputs, all checked.

- **`ker kappa = [R^x,R^x]`** is Ara--Goodearl--Pardo Theorem 2.4, already
  imported as `agp-purely-infinite-simple-k1-is-unit-abelianization`.
- **The normal closure of `jmath(EL_4(R))` in `R^x` is `[R^x,R^x]`.**  The
  inclusion `<=` is perfection of `EL_4(R)`.  For `>=`, the two constructions
  in the proof of AGP Theorem 2.4 supply, first, that every unit is congruent
  modulo `U(R)'` to one of the form `v = e + (1-e)v(1-e)` with `e != 0`
  nonzero idempotent, and second, that such a supported unit is realized by
  `diag(v,1,...,1)` inside a proper matrix corner against orthogonal
  idempotents `r_j ~ 1`.  Cancellation makes the corner conjugate to the one
  used by `jmath`: AGP Proposition 2.2, verbatim, "If `R` is a purely
  infinite simple ring, then `V(R)*` is a group.  In particular, any nonzero
  finitely generated projective `R`-modules which are stably isomorphic must
  be isomorphic."  Complements of two four-copy corners have the same `K_0`
  class and are nonzero, so they are isomorphic and the corners are conjugate.
- **`K_1(R)` is MF**, being countable abelian, hence amenable
  (`amenable-implies-operator-mf`).  So the quotient is attained.

The group-theoretic step is worth isolating: a group generated by subgroups
each of which has full MF radical has full MF radical, since a homomorphism
to an MF group dies on each generating subgroup.

**Consistency check.**  `K_1(L_k(1,2)) = 0` reproduces the total collapse of
the binary unit group already established and Lean-checked in
`binary-leavitt-all-ranks-full-mf-radical`.  Two independently derived facts
agreeing.

**Trust boundary.**  The two constructions borrowed from inside the proof of
AGP Theorem 2.4 were read in summary, not quoted line by line.  Proposition
2.2 and Corollary 1.7 were quoted verbatim.

## The loop-count convention, resolved

Ara--Brustenga--Cortinas Corollary 7.7, quoted verbatim, requires `k` a
principal ideal domain and reads

```text
K_1(L_k(E)) = coker(1-N^t : K_1(k)^(E_0 minus Sink) -> K_1(k)^(E_0))
              (+) ker(1-N^t : Z^(E_0 minus Sink) -> Z^(E_0)).
```

The convention question I raised is settled by the manuscript's own
presentation: `L_(F_2)(1,2)` has generators `s_0,s_1,t_0,t_1` with
`s_0t_0+s_1t_1 = 1`, so `L_k(1,n)` is the rose with `n` petals and `N = (n)`.
Then `1-N^t` is multiplication by `1-n`, injective on `Z`, with cokernel
`k^x/(k^x)^(n-1)` on `K_1(k) = k^x`.  The table stands as stated:
trivial for `L_k(1,2)` and for `L_(F_2)(1,n)`, `C_(gcd(q-1,n-1))` for
`L_(F_q)(1,n)`, and `Q^x/(Q^x)^2` for `L_Q(1,3)`.  So `L_(F_5)(1,3)^x` has
maximal MF quotient `C_2`, and the collapse is not automatic.

## The extension bridge

Both projects answer whether a representation of a unit group extends along
`iota_n : R^x -> GL_n(R)`, `u |-> diag(u,1,...,1)`.

- **Positive, purely infinite.**  `M_n(R)` is purely infinite simple by AGP
  Corollary 1.7, quoted verbatim as "The class of purely infinite simple
  rings is closed under Morita equivalence", and `K_1` is Morita invariant,
  so both Hom sets identify with `Hom(K_1(R), M)` and restriction is the
  identity under those identifications.  Restriction is a bijection.
- **Negative, stably finite.**  Every character of `GL_2` kills `E_2` when
  `2` is invertible, since `e_12(a) = [diag(2,1), e_12(a)]` and
  `e_21(a) = [diag(1,2), e_21(a)]`.  The AH witness has
  `diag(v,1) in E_2(A)` while the component character detects `v`, so no
  extension exists.  Passing to a countable unital `Q`-subalgebra containing
  `v`, `v^(-1)` and the factorization coefficients, then adjoining fullness
  witnesses, gives a countable simple stably finite ring with the same
  obstruction; the `Q`-algebra hypothesis is what keeps `2` invertible.

## Route closure recorded

`A` is MF, so every countable subgroup of `U(A)` is an MF group, and a group
with full MF radical admits no nontrivial homomorphism into `U(A)`.  So the
AH example cannot host the non-MF group through a unitary representation.
That closes one attempted interaction between the two constructions.
