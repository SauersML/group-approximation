# Finite-fiber folding destroys the apparent six-chart building expansion

The native dyadic `SL_3` packet has six Weyl depth directions and forty-two
parahoric branches in each direction.  That local degree does not itself
give expansion.

If branch histories may be recycled through a finite multiplicity fiber,
the transition space is a skew product `Z^2 times F`.  Every labelled edge
moves the `Z^2` coordinate by one fixed coweight vector and permutes `F`.
Taking a coweight Folner box times the entire fiber makes every one of the
252 labelled boundary ratios `O(1/N)`, independently of the fiber cocycle.
Local link, triangle, inverse, and Weyl identities among the permutations do
not alter this calculation.

Thus the actual nonamenable object is not the list of six depth profiles or
the list of forty-two local labels.  It is the global affine-building orbit
in which distinct branch histories remain distinct.  The missing arithmetic
estimate must prevent finite-fiber branch collisions, equivalently control
the cross-chart Gram overlap well enough to construct one global building
PVM.  Once that PVM exists, the already-established Schreier spectral gap
supplies the fixed boundary inequality.
