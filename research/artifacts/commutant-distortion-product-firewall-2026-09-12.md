# Commuting witnesses cannot distort a defect range: the product firewall

Lane `w5-commutant-distort`, 2026-09-12, family SUB. Target `some-commutant-element-distorts-defect-range-rank`.
Supports `commuting-subgroup-witnesses-cannot-distort-defect-ranges`. Notation follows Section 1 of
`research/artifacts/halving-expansion-and-commutant-faithfulness-2026-09-12.md` (clopen proper `U`, `G_U`,
frame triple `Theta`, `T = D_Theta`, `C_U`) and Section 1 of
`research/artifacts/two-root-defect-descent-gap-2026-09-12.md` (rank ultraproducts, Fact 1.1).

## 0. Summary

* **Section 1: reformulation.** A witness `Z` in `M_n(C_U)` distorts exactly when the `sigma(G_U)`-invariant
  right ideal `Z M_n(M)` carries a normalized defect different from `delta`. For an idempotent `e` in `C_U`,
  `rk(Te) - delta rk(e) = (delta_e - delta) rk(e)`, where `delta_e` is the normalized defect of the corner model
  `eMe`. So the target, and w4-sub-weyl's test ("one invariant summand with a different normalized defect"),
  are the same statement: some invariant piece is defect-inhomogeneous.
* **Section 2 (established): the product firewall.** Let `L <= G_U` be a countable infinite locally finite
  subgroup containing the two transvections of `Theta`, and `Lambda <= G_(U^c)` any countable subgroup that has
  some characteristic-two rank model `rho`. Then `L` and `Lambda` commute and meet trivially, and the tensor
  model `sigma = sigma_L (x) rho` of `L x Lambda` is nontrivial, fixed-point-free, has `delta = 3/8`, and
  satisfies `rk(TZ) = delta rk(Z)` exactly for every `Z` in `M_n(sigma(F_2[Lambda]))`.
* **Consequence for the escaping configurations.** `b = iota_A(w_23) iota_(A1)(w_12)` lies in `G_(A1)`, and
  `x^A_12(s_0) x^A_21(1)` lies in `G_A`. Placed off `U`, each generates an infinite cyclic `Lambda`, which has
  rank models. So neither yields a defect-inhomogeneous invariant summand from relations of `L`, relations of
  `Lambda`, commutation, rank calculus, nontriviality and fixed-point-freeness.
* **Section 3: where a witness must live.** Identities of `R^x` linking `G_U` to `Lambda` beyond commutation,
  i.e. units straddling `U` and `U^c` used as factors and not only as conjugators; or properties of `Lambda`
  that no rank model of `Lambda` has (for `Lambda = G_(U^c)`, isomorphic to `R^x`, that is the gate itself); or
  global inputs (corner locality, globality, compactness).

## 1. Distortion is defect inhomogeneity of an invariant piece

**Lemma 1.1.** Let `sigma` be a characteristic-two rank model of `R^x`, `U`, `Theta`, `T` as above, and `Z` in
`M_n(C_U)`. Put `J = Z M_n(M)`.
1. `J` is invariant under `sigma(G_U)`, and `rk(TZ) = rk(TJ)`.
2. If `Z = e` is an idempotent, then `T` commutes with `e`, `rk(T) = rk(Te) + rk(T(1-e))`, and
   `rk(Te) = delta_e rk(e)` with `delta_e` the normalized defect of the corner model `g -> e sigma(g) e` on `eMe`.

*Proof.* (1) `sigma(g) Z = Z sigma(g)` for `g` in `G_U`, so `sigma(g) J = Z sigma(g) M_n(M) = J`. And
`T Z M_n(M) = T J`, so the principal right ideals `TZ M_n(M)` and `TJ` coincide and have equal rank. (2) `T`
lies in the algebra generated by `sigma(G_U)`, which commutes with `e`. With `e` and `1-e` orthogonal idempotents
commuting with `T`, `T = eTe + (1-e)T(1-e)` is block diagonal, so ranks add. The corner map is a homomorphism
because `e` commutes with `sigma(G_U)`, and its defect at `Theta` is `Te = eTe`. QED

So at `delta = c_*` every invariant summand has normalized defect exactly `c_*` (Theorem 2.1 of the halving
artifact, and w4-sub-weyl's `near-minimal-defects-are-rank-independent-of-cylinder-commutants`). A distortion
by `kappa` is a summand whose normalized defect differs from `delta` by at least `kappa delta / rk(e)`.

## 2. The product firewall

**Theorem 2.1.** Let `U` be clopen proper, `Theta = (C_1, C_2, C_3)` a frame triple with union a proper subset of
`U`, `x = tau(C_1 <- C_2)`, `y = tau(C_2 <- C_3)`, and `T = (sigma(y) - 1)(sigma(x) - 1)`.
* `L <= G_U` is a countable infinite locally finite subgroup containing `x, y`.
* `Lambda <= G_(U^c)` is a countable subgroup with a rank model `rho : Lambda -> M_2^x`,
  `M_2 = prod_V M_(k_i)(Omega)`, `Omega` a field of characteristic two.

Then `<L, Lambda> = L x Lambda`, and there is a homomorphism `sigma : L x Lambda -> (M'')^x` into a rank
ultraproduct over `Omega` such that:
1. `sigma` is nontrivial and `Fix_r(sigma(L x Lambda)) = 0`;
2. `delta = rk(T) = 3/8`;
3. `rk(T Z) = delta rk(Z)` for every `n` and every `Z = 1 (x) rho(beta)`, `beta` in `M_n(F_2[Lambda])`; more
   generally for every `Z` in `M_n(1 (x) M_2)`, which commutes with `sigma(L)`.

*Proof.*
* **Commuting factors.** For `g` in `G_U` and `h` in `G_(U^c)`, `(g-1)(h-1) = p_U (g-1) p_U p_(U^c) (h-1) p_(U^c) = 0`,
  since `p_U p_(U^c) = 0`; likewise `(h-1)(g-1) = 0`. So `gh = g + h - 1 = hg`. If `g` lies in both groups,
  `g - 1 = p_U p_(U^c)(g - 1) = 0`.
* **Existence of `L`.** Lemma 1.1 of the halving artifact gives `v` in `V ∩ G_U` carrying `Theta` to a triple
  of equal-depth cylinders. The degree-zero units supported on `U` at depth `n` form finite groups with
  infinite locally finite union `L_0`, containing both transvections of the new triple; put `L = v^-1 L_0 v`.
* **Model of `L`.** Exhaust `L` by finite `H_1 <= H_2 <= ...`, let `lambda_n` be the left regular
  representation on `F_2[H_n]`, and `sigma_L(g) = [lambda_n(g)]_U`. Relations of `L` hold at all large stages.
  For finite `Q <= L` and `alpha` in `F_2[Q]`, the normalized rank is the regular one, because `F_2[H_n]` is free
  over `F_2[Q]` (Theorem 1.1(1) of `research/artifacts/locally-finite-conjugation-defect-firewall-2026-09-12.md`).
* **Tensor ultraproduct.** Choose representatives `rho_i(h)` of `rho(h)`, let `W = U (x) V` on `N x I`, and put
  `sigma(g, h) = [lambda_n(g) (x) rho_i(h)]_W` in `M'' = prod_W M_(m_n k_i)(Omega)`. Normalized ranks multiply,
  `rank(X (x) Y)/(mk) = (rank X / m)(rank Y / k)`, and `lim_W a_n b_i = lim_U a_n · lim_V b_i`. The relation errors
  of `sigma` are at most those of `rho`, which vanish along `W`, so `sigma` is a homomorphism, and
  `rk(sigma_L(alpha) (x) rho(beta)) = rk(sigma_L(alpha)) rk(rho(beta))`.
* **(2).** `<x, y>` is `UT_3(F_2)`, dihedral of order 8, and `rank((y+1)(x+1))` on `F_2[D_8]` is `3`
  (Corollary 2.1 of the firewall artifact). So `rk(T) = 3/8`.
* **(3).** `T Z = sigma_L((y+1)(x+1)) (x) rho(beta)` blockwise, so `rk(TZ) = (3/8) rk(rho(beta)) = delta rk(Z)`.
* **(1).** `sigma(x)` has rank-`1/2` displacement. If `sigma(g,1) w = w` for all `g` in `L`, fix `m`. The vectors
  of `im(w_(n,i))` fixed by `lambda_n(H_m) (x) 1` have codimension at most
  `sum_(h in H_m) rank((lambda_n(h) (x) 1 - 1) w_(n,i))`, which is `o(m_n k_i)` along `W`, and they lie in
  `Fix(lambda_n(H_m)) (x) Omega^(k_i)`, of normalized dimension `1/|H_m|`. So `rk(w) <= 1/|H_m|` for all `m`,
  and `w = 0`. QED

**Corollary 2.2 (escaping configurations used as commuting witnesses).** Take `U` inside the complement of `A1`,
for example `U = A0` with `Theta = (A000, A0010, A0011)`. Then `b = iota_A(w_23) iota_(A1)(w_12)` lies in
`G_(U^c)`, and `Lambda = <b>` is infinite cyclic, which has rank models (cyclic permutation stages). By Theorem
2.1 no polynomial in `sigma(b)`, and no matrix over them, distorts the defect range, and every invariant summand
built from them has normalized defect exactly `delta`. The same holds for `x^A_12(s_0) x^A_21(1)` with `U`
disjoint from `A`, and for every sofic, amenable, residually finite or `F_2`-linear sofic subgroup of
`G_(U^c)`.

**Remark 2.3 (the halves bound is tight here).** In the regular model of `C_2 x C_2` the disjoint halves
`a = x'+1`, `b' = y'+1` have `rho = 1/2` and `rk(aM + b'M) = rk(Ma + Mb') = 3/4`, so
`rk(aM + b'M) + rk(Ma + Mb') = 3 rho` with equality. The row-plus-column bound
(`disjoint-root-halves-row-plus-column-rank-at-most-three-rho`) therefore gives no distortion from data of one
locally finite subgroup.

## 3. Where a witness must live

Theorem 2.1 is a model of `L x Lambda`, not of `R^x`; like the other SUB firewalls it certifies which inputs are
inert. A proof of `some-commutant-element-distorts-defect-range-rank` must use at least one of:
1. **Straddling units as factors.** Elements of `R^x` outside `G_U x G_(U^c)`, entering an identity as factors
   together with elements of `G_U` or of `Lambda`, not only as single conjugators. A conjugate `u b u^-1` lying in
   `G_(U^c)` is again infinite cyclic and inert by Corollary 2.2; what is untested is an identity that uses `b`,
   `u` and elements of `G_U` at once. No combined HNN-over-product model exists for non-locally-finite `Lambda`.
2. **Properties of `Lambda` that no rank model of `Lambda` has.** For `Lambda = G_(U^c)`, which is isomorphic to
   `R^x`, this is the gate itself.
3. **Global inputs** about all rank models of `R^x`: the defect gap through corner locality, globality through
   property (T), compactness.

w4-sub-weyl's summand test ("one invariant summand with a different normalized defect, from an escaping
configuration") is Lemma 1.1(2) with `e` built from that configuration. Corollary 2.2 shows the configuration must
enter through item 1 or 3, not as a commuting witness.
