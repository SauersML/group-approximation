# un-open-1 plan: the MF radical of `EL_n(R)` is a ring radical

Lane `un-open-1`, 2026-09-13, open-ended. Everything below is a plan, not a result.

## The phenomenon I picked

Across the repository the same shape keeps recurring, once per family, each time proved by hand:

| ring `R` | `Rad_MF(EL_n(R))` | maximal MF quotient |
|---|---|---|
| `L_k(1,m)`, or any simple non-directly-finite ring | everything (tex Cor l.1016) | trivial |
| `LC(X,F_q) ⋊ Z`, `X` infinite minimal subshift | trivial (Pestov 9.1: LEF, so MF) | everything |
| `C(X) ⋊ Z`, `X` compact zero-dimensional | restriction kernel to the chain-recurrent core (`zero-dimensional-system-mf-radical-is-core-restriction`, printed `thm:core-mf-radical`) | the core group, LEF |
| binary Jacobson `F_2<S,T \| TS=1>` | finitary kernel, or trivial (`binary-jacobson-el3-rank-radical-is-the-finitary-kernel`) | `EL_n(F_2[x,x^{-1}])`, residually finite |
| universal torsion-defect ring | finitary `SL` over the cyclic ring | — |

In every settled row the answer is **the relative elementary subgroup of a two-sided ideal**, and the
quotient is the elementary group of the quotient ring. The ideal is described differently every time
(dynamics, graph combinatorics, defect words). Nothing on main says what that ideal *is* in general.

**Thesis.** There is one canonical ideal, defined with no dynamics and no groupoid, that computes all of
them, and the two calibrating papers are its two extreme values.

## The object

For a unital ring `R` let `d(R)` be the ideal generated by all `1 - st` with `ts = 1` (all defects of
one-sided inverses). Iterate `i_0 = 0`, `i_(a+1)/i_a = d(R/i_a)`, unions at limits; let `i(R)` be the
stable value. Then `R/i(R)` is directly finite, and `i(R) ⊆ I` for every ideal `I` with `R/I` directly
finite, because every defect dies in such a quotient. So

> `i(R)` is the **smallest ideal of `R` with directly finite quotient**: a Hilbert-hotel radical.

`s(R)` is the same with defects taken in every `M_m(R)`, so `R/s(R)` is stably finite.

## The target theorems

- **T1 (canonicity).** `i` and `s` exist, are functorial for surjections, Morita invariant, and
  `i(R) = R` iff `R` has no directly finite nonzero quotient; `i(R) = 0` iff `R` is directly finite.
- **T2 (lower bound).** char `p`: `EL_n(R, i(R)) ≤ Rad_MF(EL_n(R))`. One step is the printed
  proposition at tex l.1072 (`EL_n(R,ReR) ≤ Rad_MF(EL_n(R))`); the content is the transfinite step,
  which needs the kernel of `EL_n(R) ↠ EL_n(R/J)` to be `EL_n(R,J)`.
- **T3 (upper bound, cheap but decisive).** If `EL_n(R/i(R))` is MF then
  `Rad_MF(EL_n(R)) = EL_n(R, i(R))` and the maximal MF quotient is `EL_n(R/i(R))`. The hypothesis is
  supplied by the measure side: `R/i(R)` residually finite, LEF, or exactly matricial.
- **T4 (the dynamical avatar).** For `R = C(X,F_q) ⋊ Z` on a compact zero-dimensional `X`,
  `i(R)` is the ideal of functions vanishing on the chain-recurrent core. This would turn the printed
  core theorem into a corollary and give a Tarski-style reading: defects live exactly off the core.

## Why it matters

It replaces a dichotomy for simple rings with a structure theorem for all rings: one ring radical, one
formula, both papers as the extreme values `i = R` and `i = 0`. It predicts a new object — a finitely
generated Kazhdan group with a **proper nontrivial** MF radical whose maximal MF quotient is an infinite
simple LEF Kazhdan group (take the algebraic Toeplitz extension of a minimal subshift crossed product).

## What this does not duplicate

`un-converse`, `un-middle`, `un-tarski` and the `un-architect` ladder all assume `R` **simple**, where
`i(R) ∈ {0,R}` and the theorem degenerates to their dichotomy; this drops simplicity and asks for the
value, not the alternative. `un-k1-homology` computes the MF quotient of `GL_n` for purely infinite
groupoids via `K_1` and groupoid homology, i.e. the row `i = R` only. `un-low-rank-band` owns ranks
`3 ≤ N < 2p`. The zero-dimensional and Jacobson rows above are other campaigns' theorems; here they are
model tests and consumers, and they are credited, never reproved.
