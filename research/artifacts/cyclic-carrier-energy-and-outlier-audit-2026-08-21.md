# Cyclic-carrier energy and full-MF-radical outliers

**Date:** 2026-08-21

This note isolates two exact boundaries left after the normal-Kazhdan non-MF
construction.  Neither boundary proves a non-hyperlinear group, but each
removes a false shortcut and leaves a smaller quantitative target.

## 1. The exact endpoint of cyclic-carrier invariance

Let `G=<X|R>` be finitely presented, let `H=<S><=G`, let `w in G`, and let
`f` be a trigonometric polynomial.  Put

```text
p_s=[s,f(w)] in C[G].
```

If the matrix-only cyclic-invariance estimate

```text
sum_(s in S)||[U(s),f(U(w))]||_2^2 <= eta(def_R(U)),
eta(delta)->0,
```

holds, then every exact finite-dimensional representation kills every
`p_s`.  Thus `p_s` belongs to the finite-dimensional star-representation
radical of `C[G]`.  If the canonical carrier has positive commutator energy,
some `p_s` is nonzero, so `C*(G)` is not residually finite-dimensional.

Residual finiteness of `G` is already impossible.  For a nonzero
finite-support group-algebra element, choose a finite quotient injective on
its support (equivalently, separate all pairwise differences in that
support).  Its image is nonzero and is detected by the quotient regular
representation.

For each fixed matrix dimension `d`, this exact endpoint gives a modulus by
compactness of `U(d)^X`.  The hard quantifier is uniformity:

```text
eta_d(delta)->0 for every fixed d
```

does not imply

```text
sup_d eta_d(delta)->0.
```

## 2. An infinite-order hyperlinear firewall

Let `N_infty` be generated by an infinite-order central element `z` and
`x_i,y_i` for `i in Z`, with

```text
[x_i,y_i]=z,
```

and all commutators between different pairs trivial.  Every
finite-dimensional unitary representation kills `z`.  Indeed, on a
`lambda`-eigenspace of `z` of dimension `m`, let `q` be the order of
`lambda`.  For every `N`, the first `N` commuting Weyl pairs make the joint
`x`-spectrum a union of free `(Z/q)^N`-orbits.  Hence `q^N` divides `m` for
every `N`, and therefore `q=1`.

Adjoin a shift `a:x_i|->x_(i+1), y_i|->y_(i+1)` and call the resulting
finitely generated amenable group `N_sh`.  For any infinite sofic
property-`(T)` group `H`, form the restricted wreath product

```text
W=N_sh wr H
```

and let `w` be `z` in the identity lamp.  Then `W` is finitely generated,
recursively presented, sofic and hyperlinear.  The word `w` has infinite
order; every nonzero power has an infinite `H`-conjugacy orbit; and every
finite-dimensional representation of `W` kills `w`.  Consequently exact
finite-dimensional cyclic-carrier energy is zero for every `f`, while the
regular energy is

```text
sum_(s in S)||[lambda(s),f(lambda(w))]||_2^2 > 0
```

for any nonconstant `f` and a suitable finite Kazhdan set.  Thus even the
entire exact endpoint, with an infinite-order transverse carrier, does not
give a dimension-uniform HS modulus.  Higman embeds `W` into a finitely
presented group, preserving the exact finite-dimensional collapse and the
infinite conjugacy orbits, but not known hyperlinearity of the overgroup.

## 3. The weaker scalar-energy interface

For fixed data define

```text
E(U)=sum_(s in S)||[U(s),f(U(w))]||_2^2,
beta_fd=sup_{pi exact finite-dimensional} E(pi),
beta_app(delta)=sup_{d,U:def_R(U)<=delta} E(U),
E_reg=E(lambda_G).
```

The following two conditions suffice:

```text
beta_fd < E_reg,                                      (CE1)
limsup_(delta->0) beta_app(delta) <= beta_fd.         (CE2)
```

Indeed canonical microstates converge on the finite moment window defining
`E`, so their energies converge to `E_reg`, contradicting `(CE2)`.  This is
strictly weaker than matrix-only cyclic invariance, which requires the
approximate ceiling to tend to zero.  Direct sums do not disturb the exact
ceiling because energy is a dimension-weighted convex combination of block
energies.

Condition `(CE1)` alone still rules out residual finiteness: a finite quotient
injective on the supports of all `p_s^*p_s` has quotient-regular energy
exactly `E_reg`.  The wreath product above has `beta_fd=0<E_reg` but is
hyperlinear, proving that `(CE2)` is the load-bearing statement.

## 4. Full MF residual forces sparse operator-norm outliers

Let the established finitely presented group `Q=<X|R>` satisfy
`Res_MF(Q)=Q`.  If `Q` were hyperlinear and `U_n` were regular microstates,
then for every nonidentity `q in Q`,

```text
||q(U_n)-1||_2 -> sqrt(2).
```

The operator-norm defects of the finite relator list cannot all tend to zero.
Otherwise the tuple classes define a norm-corona homomorphism from `Q`, full
MF residual kills `q`, and `||q(U_n)-1||_op->0`, contradicting the displayed
HS separation.  Hence, after a subsequence, one fixed relator `r in R` and
one `epsilon>0` satisfy

```text
||r(U_n)-1||_op >= epsilon,
||r(U_n)-1||_2 -> 0.
```

For the spectral projection

```text
p_n=1_[epsilon/2,infinity)(|r(U_n)-1|),
```

one has `p_n!=0` and

```text
tr(p_n) <= 4 epsilon^(-2)||r(U_n)-1||_2^2 -> 0.
```

Thus hypothetical hyperlinear models of `Q` must hide a fixed operator-norm
relator failure on a vanishing-trace spectral region.  This is an explicit
outlier factory, not yet an error-delocalization theorem: fixed word
recursions and adjoint expanders cannot manufacture positive density from
such a region without an additional ownership or capacity mechanism.
