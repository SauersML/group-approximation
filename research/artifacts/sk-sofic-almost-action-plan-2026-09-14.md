# sk-sofic-almost-action: plan and first results (2026-09-14)

Lane `sk-sofic-almost-action`. Everything below is unreviewed.

**Problem.** For a finitely generated sofic `Δ` that is not LEF, build one almost action of
`G_Δ = EL_n(R_Δ)`, `R_Δ = LC(F_2^Δ,F_2) ⋊ (F_2 ≀ Δ)`, `n ≥ 3`, whose fixed-point proportion stays bounded away
from 1. Or find the precise obstruction.

**Inputs on main.**
- Theorem L: every character of `G_Δ` is `c·1 + (1−c)δ_e`, and the invariant random subgroups are trivial
  (`lamplighter-host-elementary-groups-are-character-rigid`, sk-verify-18 PASS).
- The soficity test (`almost-actions-of-irs-rigid-simple-groups-are-free-or-trivial`).
- Theorem A rank models (`sofic-lamplighter-bernoulli-crossed-products-have-rank-models`).
- The gap theorem (`gap-sofic-approximations-are-local-embeddings`).
- Alekseev–Thom ergodic centralizer (`kazhdan-ergodic-sofic-centralizer-forces-lef`).

## 1. Result R1: the lamplighter is not the hard part

**Proposition R1.** Let `Δ` be any group and `n ≥ 3`.
1. `EL_3(F_2[Δ]) ≤ G_Δ`.
2. `γ ↦ diag(u_γ,1,1)` is an injective homomorphism `[Δ,Δ] → EL_3(F_2[Δ])`.
3. With `Δ = C(Γ)`, the half-line overgroup, `Γ ≤ EL_3(F_2[C(Γ)]) ≤ G_(C(Γ))`.

So if `G_(C(Γ))` is sofic for a non-LEF `Γ`, then `EL_3(F_2[C(Γ)])` is a finitely generated sofic Kazhdan group
that is not LEF.

*Proof.*
1. `F_2[Λ] = span{u_ℓ}` is a unital subring of `R_Δ = ⊕_ℓ LC(Ω,F_2)u_ℓ`, and `F_2[Δ] ⊆ F_2[Λ]`. Elementary groups
   of subrings are subgroups, and `EL_3 ↪ EL_n` by blocks.
2. Whitehead: `diag(x,x^(−1),1) = w_12(x)w_12(1)` with `w_12(x) = e_12(x)e_21(x^(−1))e_12(x)` (char 2), for every
   unit `x`. Then
   ```text
   diag(a,a^(−1),1) · diag(b,b^(−1),1) · diag((ba)^(−1), ba, 1) = diag(aba^(−1)b^(−1), 1, 1).
   ```
   The first coordinate is `ab(ba)^(−1) = [a,b]`, and the second is `a^(−1)b^(−1)ba = 1`. So `diag(c,1,1) ∈ EL_3`
   for every product `c` of commutators of units. `γ ↦ diag(u_γ,1,1)` is multiplicative, and injective since the
   `u_γ` are distinct.
3. `Γ ≤ [C(Γ),C(Γ)]` (`groups-embed-in-derived-subgroup-of-half-line-overgroup`). `EL_3` over a finitely generated
   ring is Kazhdan (`elementary-groups-over-fg-rings-have-property-t`). LEF passes to subgroups. ∎

**Meaning.**
- The Hamming question for the hosts already contains a group-ring question with no space `Ω` in it: is
  `EL_3(F_2[Δ])` sofic for sofic `Δ`?
- It also contains the existence of a finitely generated sofic Kazhdan group that is not LEF. The simple case is
  `sofic-universal-envelope-forces-non-lef-simple-kazhdan`.
- **Bounded literature check, found no such group:** the Alekseev–Thom §5 examples (quoted on main in
  `sk-sofic-host-hamming` part 1 §2); two web searches on 2026-09-14 ("sofic property (T) not LEF", "soficity
  EL_n group ring"); a grep of main. `EL_3(F_2[Δ])` is `F_2`-linear sofic (it is a subgroup of the Theorem B host), so
  its soficity is an instance of "linear sofic ⇒ sofic?" (Arzhantseva–Păunescu, arXiv:1212.6780; the question is not
  re-read at source).

## 2. Result R2: Kazhdan cutting barrier (closes idea 2, Hamming-thin subsets)

**Proposition R2.** Let `H` be generated by a finite set `A` with Kazhdan constant `κ` for `A`. Let `X` be a finite
`H`-set and `Y ⊆ X` with `|aY Δ Y| ≤ ε|Y|` for `a ∈ A`. Then some `H`-invariant `Z ⊆ X` has
`|Y Δ Z| ≤ (2|A|/κ²) ε|Y|`.

*Proof.*
- On an orbit `O`, put `S = Y ∩ O` and `f = 1_S − (|S|/|O|)1_O`. Then `f ⊥` the invariant vectors of `ℓ²(O)`, which
  are the constants.
- So some `a` has `‖af − f‖² ≥ κ²‖f‖²`. Here `‖af − f‖² = |aS Δ S|` and `‖f‖² = |S||O∖S|/|O| ≥ min(|S|,|O∖S|)/2`.
- Sum over orbits: `Σ_O min(|Y∩O|, |O∖Y|) ≤ (2/κ²) Σ_a |aY Δ Y|`.
- Let `Z` be the union of the orbits where `Y` is the majority. Then `|Y Δ Z|` is exactly the left side. ∎

**Corollary R2′ (cut-downs of genuine cover actions).** Let `G = H/K` with `H` Kazhdan. Let `X_N` be finite `H`-sets
and `Y_N ⊆ X_N` with `|aY_N Δ Y_N| ≤ ε_N|Y_N|`, where `ε_N → 0`. Let `σ_N` be any completion of the partial maps
`a|_(Y_N ∩ a^(−1)Y_N)` to permutations of `Y_N`.
- If `σ_N` satisfies (AF1) for `K`, then so does the genuine action on the invariant `Z_N` of R2.
- *Proof.* For a word `w`, at most `|w|ε_N|Y_N|` points of `Y_N` have a prefix path leaving `Y_N`, because each
  letter is injective. On the other points, `σ_N(w)` is the genuine `w`. Then `|Y_N Δ Z_N| = O(ε_N|Y_N|)`.

**Corollary R2″ (Hamming-thin subsets of transitive vector models).** Take `H = EL_n(F_2⟨A⟩)` with `e_ij(1)` marked
(Kazhdan, Ershov–Jaikin). Let `H` act on `X = F_2^m` through a ring homomorphism `F_2⟨A⟩ → M_(m/n)(F_2)`, **with image
transitive on nonzero vectors**. This holds, for example, when the generated algebra is the full matrix algebra.
- Invariant sets are `∅`, `{0}`, `X∖0` and `X`.
- A nontrivial cut-down satisfying (AF1) forces `Z = X∖0` up to `O(ε_N|Y_N|)`. Then `ρ(w)` fixes more than half of
  the vectors, so `ρ(w) = I`.
- So the model is exact on balls, which is the gap regime (LEF).
- **Not checked:** whether Theorem A's models on `2^V × V` have transitive image. Symmetries of `V_k` commuting with
  the `σ_k(s)` give proper subalgebras, as in the LEF case where `V = Q` is a group.
- **Open piece (T5):** without transitivity, the argument gives only nested subspaces `T_r = span(Z ∩ P_r)`, where
  `P_r` is the common fixed space of `K ∩ B_r`. For `|h| ≤ s`, `hT_(r+2s) ⊆ T_r`, and the relators of length `≤ r`
  are exact on `T_r`. This is a partial action, not yet a local embedding.

## 3. Firewalls recorded

- **Idea 1 (configurations `2^V × V`) does not define a `G_Δ`-action.**
  - Elementary matrices over `R_Δ` are not piecewise-`Λ` maps.
  - In the dual `p.m.p.` action on `Ω × F_2^([n]×Λ)` (Haar on fibres), `G_Δ` fixes the base and acts linearly on
    fibres.
  - So configuration models of `Λ ↷ Ω` reach `G_Δ` only through fibre vectors, which is the regime of R2″.
- **Equivariant constructions.**
  - By Alekseev–Thom, no sofic embedding of a non-LEF `G_Δ` has an ergodic centralizer. So an almost action that
    commutes with a Loeb-ergodic almost action is excluded.
  - This bites for non-amenable `Δ` with expander models. Amenable models of `Λ` are never Loeb ergodic, so for
    amenable `Δ` there is no firewall from this.

## 4. Where a model must live (the precise gap)

By R2′ and R2″, a sofic approximation of a non-LEF `G_Δ`, or of `EL_3(F_2[Δ])`, must do one of two things:
- **(M1)** break the Steinberg relations of the free-algebra cover on a vanishing but nonzero set of points; or
- **(M2)** be a genuine action of the cover on non-gap sets, where the relators of `K` are nontrivial with
  fixed-point proportion → 1. These are "3-cycle-like" constituents; primitive Lie-type actions are expected to have
  small fixed-point ratios (Liebeck–Saxl, not read at source).

## 5. Targets, in order

- **T1 (test group, Ω-free).** Decide soficity of `E_0 = EL_3(F_2[C(BS(2,3))])`, or of any `EL_3(F_2[Δ])` with a
  non-LEF `[Δ,Δ]`.
- **T2 (M2, Kassabov-type patching).** Charts `SL_(3·2^m)(F_2)` from the UHF windows act freely on `o(d)`-complements
  (Consequence 3 of the soficity test). Glue the charts over Theorem A base points so that defects of `Δ` become
  alternating corrections supported on bad base points. First compute what a defect of one `BS(2,3)` relator does
  to a glued chart.
- **T3 (negative).** For a finitely presented Kazhdan cover `H ↠ G`, are sofic approximations of `G` through genuine
  `H`-sets local embeddings? A yes would force (M1). Test first against Kazhdan quotients onto alternating groups.
- **T4 (literature, bounded).** Is a finitely generated sofic non-LEF Kazhdan group known? Is soficity of `EL_n(K[Γ])`
  known for sofic non-LEF `Γ`? Read Arzhantseva–Păunescu §open questions and Alekseev–Thom §6 at source on MSI.
