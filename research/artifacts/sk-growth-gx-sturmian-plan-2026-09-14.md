## Choice: the matrix route, its exact boundary, and what it does prove
The directive is to use the matrix structure of R_X instead of dyadic self-similarity of X.

**M1. The matrix engine needs 2-divisibility of the unit in K^0; there is no way around it at the ring level.**
- Claim (routine): if M_3(R_X) contains a full system of 3·2^k × 3·2^k matrix units summing to 1, equivalently M_3(R_X) ≅ M_{3·2^k}(A) for a unital ring A, then [1_X] ∈ 2^k K^0(X,T).
- Proof sketch: K_0(M_3(R_X)) = K_0(R_X) ≅ K^0(X,T) (node `subshift-crossed-product-k0-is-coinvariant-group`, to check). Then 3[1_X] = 3·2^k[ε_11]. By Bezout (1 = 3a + 2^k b) in the torsion-free group K^0, [1_X] ∈ 2^k K^0.
- Corollary: for Sturmian X the dyadic matrix engine cannot start. Permuting "points" of unequal K_0-class is impossible in EL, and every decomposition leaves a remainder summand Q of irrational class.

**M2. Defect-point analysis: why one remainder summand Q kills the counting.**
- Write M_3(R_X) = End(P^{m·2^k} ⊕ Q), with [P] small and [Q] = 3 − m2^k[P] > 0; this is possible because K^0 is dense.
- Generation needs root elements e_{p,q}(x) and e_{q,p}(y). All their conjugates share the point q, and [e_{a,q}(x), e_{q,b}(y)] = e_{a,b}(xy) creates root elements at arbitrary pairs.
- The commuting-supports lemma, and with it the normal form and the count, fails at q. Record as an Attempt.

**G1. Positive theorem at the matrix engine's exact boundary (candidate).**
- Statement: let X be an infinite minimal subshift of zero entropy. Suppose that for infinitely many k there is a clopen partition X = W_1 ⊔ … ⊔ W_{2^k} with partial translations W_1 → W_i in the topological full pseudogroup: a piecewise uniform level model, no constant towers needed. Then G_X has non-uniform exponential growth.
- Proof plan:
  - (i) s_i = Σ_n u^n e_{A_n^{(i)}} and s′_i = Σ_n e_{A_n^{(i)}} u^{−n} give matrix units ε_ij = s_i s′_j with Σε_ii = 1, so R_X ≅ M_{2^k}(e_{W_1}R_Xe_{W_1});
  - (ii) the corner is LC(W_1,F_2) ⋊ T_{W_1}, the induced first-return map, via reduction of the transformation groupoid;
  - (iii) (W_1,T_{W_1}) is an aperiodic minimal subshift over return words with entropy 0 (Abramov plus the variational principle), so Krieger's embedding theorem recodes it binary and the corner has 3 ring generators;
  - (iv) the engine and Theorem A step 2 give ω → 1;
  - (v) (T) and non-amenability give exponential growth.
- Scope: by Lemma K the hypothesis implies [1_X] ∈ 2^k K^0. Conversely, with the realization of positive K^0-classes by clopen partitions (GPS or Glasner–Weiss, to check at source), divisibility gives the model. So G1 covers every zero-entropy minimal subshift with 1 ∈ ∩_k 2^k K^0, which contains Cor A2.
  - Firewall: whether this is strictly more than A2 is NOT established. For primitive substitutions, length divisibility seems to force dyadic eigenvalues through Host's criterion (checked on 0↦0010, 1↦01: det M = 2 and every return-word length is eventually divisible by 2^j, so 1/2^j are eigenvalues). Weakly mixing zero-entropy examples through Ormes' realization are not certified.

**K. Krieger's embedding theorem at source.** It closes Cor A2's recorded gate and G1's import. Target statement: a subshift X embeds into a mixing SFT Y if h(X) < h(Y) and the periodic-point counts of X are bounded by those of Y. The periodic condition is vacuous for aperiodic X.
