## 1. Corollary 3, second statement: PARTIALLY KNOWN

Statement: a f.g. group is LEF with solvable WP iff it is a subgroup of an infinite f.g. simple Kazhdan LEF group with solvable WP, a limit of finite simple SL_N(F_2) expanders.

**Known separately:**
- **Solvable WP only.** Thompson 1980: every f.g. group with decidable WP embeds (Frattini) in a f.g. simple group with decidable WP. The paper credits this.
- **Families.** Darbinyan–Steenbock's survey arXiv:2504.12707, e-print l.227–228, "Theorem family word problem": "For every recursively enumerable family 𝒢 of finitely generated groups with decidable word problem, there exists a finitely generated simple group H with decidable word problem such that each G∈𝒢 …". This comes from their JLMS 105 (2022) 2011–2045 paper, per the survey's \cite{ourpaper} (Crossref: doi 10.1112/jlms.12552). Their hosts also preserve left-orderability. There is no LEF, sofic or (T) clause.
- **LEF only.** Kionke–Schesler, Thm 1.2: f.g. RF groups embed in f.g. simple LEF groups. The paper credits this. Their e-print has no statement about the word problem: grep for "word problem|decidab|computab" finds only "recursive description" inside constructions.

**Not found:** a host that is LEF and has solvable WP at once, with or without (T). Nor any LEF-preserving version of Thompson's theorem.

**Credit:** the paper's framing ("in the form of Thompson's strengthening") is accurate. See item 4 for the missing credit to the overgroup construction.

## 4. The overgroups in the proof of Corollaries 3–4: KNOWN (MUST credit)

**(a) The wreath commutator (l.336–340).** In the paper, f_jk multiplies level i by μ_k(γ_j)^i, and [f_jk,t_k] multiplies by μ_k(γ_j), so Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ]. This is the Neumann–Neumann embedding, quoted from Bartholdi–Erschler arXiv:1403.5584, e-print l.149–163:
> "We recall the later construction by Neumann-Neumann, which uses wreath products rather than HNN extensions … starting with a countable group B, one imbeds it into a countable subgroup G of the unrestricted wreath product B≀≀Z in such a way that B is imbedded into the commutator group [G,G]. The group G is generated by Z and, for all b∈B, the function"

The paper applies it on finite models (Z/m_k in place of Z) inside an ultraproduct. The idea is the same.
- Crossref: B. H. Neumann and H. Neumann, *Embedding theorems for groups*, J. London Math. Soc. s1-34(4) (1959) 465–479, doi 10.1112/jlms/s1-34.4.465.

**(b) The half-line overgroup (l.358–363).** In the paper, h_γ multiplies levels m ≥ 0 by γ, and [β,h_γ] multiplies level 0 by γ^{-1}. The same half-line form appears in Darbinyan arXiv:1909.05720, e-print l.395–420:
- f_i(z^n) = x_i for n > 0, and 1 otherwise;
- "[z, f_i](z^n) = x_i if n = 0, 1 otherwise";
- "Observation 4.1. The group L is computable with respect to the generating set {z, f_1, f_2, …} ≤ H ≀ ⟨z⟩ if and only if H had decidable word problem."

Darbinyan (l.180) calls it "a modified version of B. Neumann's construction", first used in his *Group embeddings with algorithmic properties* (Crossref: Comm. Algebra 43(11) (2015) 4923–4935, doi 10.1080/00927872.2014.955579). That paper embeds every countable group with solvable WP into a 2-generated group with solvable WP.

What the paper adds is that this overgroup is LEF when Γ is, with explicit finite models (κ, θ, ζ). No source for that was found.
