# gk3-dual-surjunctivity — nonlinear bridge between Gottschalk and dual surjunctivity (2026-09-14)

Lane `gk3-dual-surjunctivity` executes survivor I5 of `ideas-gottschalk-2`.

**Question.** Does a nonlinear strict pair give a post-surjective,
non-pre-injective automaton, and conversely?

**Verdict: partial.** Neither conjecture is decided. The lane landed:
- an exact conversion theorem;
- the affine case;
- an exclusion over dual surjunctive groups;
- the precise place where each direction is blocked, as two OPEN existence
  claims wired into the Gottschalk goal.

No novelty is claimed. The additive argument is Doucha–Gismatullin's, and the
non-pre-injectivity of split decoders was already on main.

- **Doucha–Gismatullin** (arXiv:2008.10565): post-surjective ⇔ strongly

  finiteness in positive characteristic, whose proof contains the linear
  decoder Claim.
- **Bartholdi** (arXiv:1605.09133): non-amenable groups carry automata with
  Gardens of Eden and no mutually erasable patterns.

Already on main: `split-decoders-have-generic-cantor-fibers`, i.e. `σ∘τ = id`
with `σ` pre-injective forces `σ` bijective, with an explicit collision

## 2. Results

`post-surjective-decoders-convert-strict-pairs-to-dual-failures`
(route `post-surjective-decoder-conversion-proof`):
- **(a)** A strict pair with post-surjective decoder ⇔ a post-surjective,
  non-pre-injective automaton with an automaton right inverse (swap roles).
- **(b)** Decoders are post-surjective at every configuration asymptotic to
  `Y = τ(A^G)`. The lift is `x′ = τ(y′)`.
- **(c)** Affine decoders over finite abelian alphabets are post-surjective for
  any encoder. The lift is `x′ = x + τ(y′) − τσ(x)`.
- **(d)** Over dual surjunctive groups, in particular sofic groups, no strict
  pair has a post-surjective decoder, hence none has an affine decoder.
- **(e)** (b) never covers everything: configurations with infinitely many
  disjoint Garden-of-Eden occurrences are asymptotic to no point of `Y`.
- **(f)** An automaton right inverse forces `μ` to map `H`-fixed configurations
  onto `H`-fixed configurations for every `H ≤ G`, and to permute constants.

**OPEN nodes and routes:**
- `every-group-is-dual-surjunctive` (CKT's dual conjecture).
- `dual-surjunctive-groups-are-surjunctive`, via
  `dual-surjunctive-surjunctive-via-post-surjective-decoders`, which requires
  OPEN `strict-pairs-admit-post-surjective-decoders`.
- `surjunctive-groups-are-dual-surjunctive`, via
  `surjunctive-dual-surjunctive-via-automaton-right-inverses`, which requires
  OPEN `post-surjective-automata-admit-automaton-right-inverses`.
- The goal route `gottschalk-via-dual-surjunctivity-and-decoder-transfer`:
  every group dual surjunctive + dual surjunctive ⇒ surjunctive ⇒ Gottschalk.

## 3. Calibrations

- **Amenable groups.** Split decoders are surjective, hence pre-injective by
  the Garden of Eden theorem, hence bijective. So strict pairs do not exist and
  (a)–(d) are vacuous. This is consistent with CKT Corollary 2
  (post-surjective ⇔ reversible).
- **Sofic non-amenable F₂.** CKT Example 3 (majority) is surjective and not
  pre-injective, and CKT explain it is not post-surjective. That is consistent
  with (d) and CKT Theorem 2: no post-surjective non-pre-injective automaton
  exists there.
- **Linear finite-field case.** (c) is Doucha–Gismatullin's Claim for `T_b`.
  With (a) and (d) it recovers their direction "dual surjunctive ⇒ Kaplansky
  direct finiteness over finite fields".
- **Cross-alphabet toy** (not a Gottschalk object). Over any infinite group,
  `A ⊂ B`, `τ` the inclusion and `σ` a sitewise retraction `B → A`:
  - `σ` is post-surjective: lift sitewise.
  - `σ` is not pre-injective: change one symbol outside `A`.
  - `x` with infinitely many symbols outside `A` is asymptotic to no point of
    `Y = A^G`.
  - So the sufficient criterion (b) is not necessary. The proofs of (b), (c)
    and (e) never use equality of alphabets, and they check out on this toy.
- **Reversible automata.** The inverse is a right inverse and permutes the
  constants, consistent with (f).
- No computation was run; all statements are elementary proofs.

## 4. Attempts that failed

- **Direct patching.** For `x ∉ Y`, set `x′ = x` off a finite set `F` and
  `τ(y′)` on `F`. Decoder windows straddling `∂F` read mixed patterns, so
  outputs near `∂F` change uncontrollably. The linear case avoids this by
  additivity (c); no nonlinear substitute was found.
- **"Every asymptotic class meets `Y`".** False for every strict pair, by (e).
- **Space-time towers over `G × Z`.** For a post-surjective `μ` the orbit
  subshift `{(x_n) : x_{n+1} = μ(x_n)}` is not a full shift, and no injective
  encoding of a full shift into it was found. Symmetrically, from a strict
  pair, stacking `τ` in the Z direction gives injective maps whose images are
  not full shifts, so no dual object arises.
- **Sections of post-surjective maps.** (f) is the only general obstruction
  found. CKT Theorem 2 says there are no test objects over sofic groups, and
  none are known anywhere.

## 5. Exact gaps

1. **Decoder choice off the language of `Y`.** For a strict pair, can the local
   rule of `σ` on patterns not occurring in `Y` be chosen so that finite output
   changes at configurations with infinitely many Garden-of-Eden occurrences
   lift to finite input changes? Or can one change the alphabet or pass to
   another strict pair over the same group? This is
   `strict-pairs-admit-post-surjective-decoders`.
2. **Sections.** Does a post-surjective, non-pre-injective automaton over `G`
   admit an automaton right inverse after enlarging the alphabet, subject to
   the periodic-fiber condition (f)? This is
   `post-surjective-automata-admit-automaton-right-inverses`.
3. **Relation to direct finiteness.** Both conjectures already imply Kaplansky
   direct finiteness in positive characteristic, and the affine case (c)
   reduces to it over fields. So a nonlinear strict pair with a post-surjective
   decoder that is not reducible to a linear one would be the first genuinely
   nonlinear object on either side.
