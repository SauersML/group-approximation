## 2. Recount by orbit counting (`rc_orbit.py`)

For word lengths `l1 <= l2`, let `S(l1, l2)` be the set of ordered pairs of cyclically reduced
words, of lengths `l1, l2` in either order, with `|det| = 1`. Its size is `2 N(l1, l2)` for
`l1 != l2` and `N(l, l)` for `l1 = l2`, where `N(l1, l2)` counts ordered pairs with
`|a| = l1`, `|b| = l2`.

`N` is computed exactly, without enumerating pairs:
- a transfer-matrix count over (first letter, last letter, exponent sums) gives `c_n(p, q)`, the
  number of cyclically reduced words of length `n` with exponent sums `(p, q)`;
- `N(l1, l2)` is the sum of `c_{l1}(p, q) c_{l2}(r, s)` over `|ps - qr| = 1`.

The move group acts on `S(l1, l2)` and has order `64 l1 l2`. It is generated by:
- independent rotations and inversion of each relator (dihedral of orders `2 l1` and `2 l2`);
- the 8 signed permutations of `{x, y}`;
- swapping the two positions.

For each census representative `p` of a block, `rc_orbit.py` applies every group element by
brute force. That gives `|Stab(p)|`, and the minimum over the orbit of the census key: the pair
sorted by (length, string).

Checks:
1. Each representative is cyclically reduced, has `|det| = 1` and the stated length, equals its
   own orbit minimum, and does not repeat. An orbit has one minimum, so distinct representatives
   lie in distinct orbits.
2. The orbit sizes `64 l1 l2 / |Stab(p)|` sum to `|S(l1, l2)|`. The orbits partition
   `S(l1, l2)`, so no orbit is missed.

Together these give exactly one representative per class.
