## M2 `residually-finite-actions-give-matricial-crossed-products`: PASS

- **Simplicity (Lemma 2.1).** Re-derived. After u_h^{-1} gives f_e ≠ 0, pick a free point in {f_e ≠ 0}; free points are
  dense by topological freeness and Baire. A clopen W separates the finite support: χ_W P χ_W = cχ_W. Finitely many
  translates cover X, and 1 − Π(1 − χ_{V_i}) ∈ J.
- **Window computation.** Recomputed on basis vectors with P_n(g)δ_z = δ_{β(g)z} and D_n(f)δ_z = f(z)δ_z:
  - ψ(f u_g)ψ(f' u_h)δ_z = f'(β(h)z) f(β(gh)z) δ_{β(gh)z}, using that β is a genuine action;
  - ψ(f·(f'∘α(g)^{-1}) u_{gh})δ_z = f(w) f'(α(g^{-1})w) δ_w, with w = β(gh)z;
  - β(g^{-1})w = β(h)z, and d(α(g^{-1})w, β(g^{-1})w) < ε_n for g^{-1} ∈ F_n. So the two agree when f' is constant on
    ε_n-small sets.
  - ψ_n is additive on all of R, so bilinearity extends multiplicativity from monomials to the window.
- **Item 3.** Correct. M_m(∏_ω M_N(F_q)) ≅ ∏_ω M_{mN}(F_q), and a countable subgroup of an algebraic ultraproduct of
  finite groups is LEF. For an expansive action of a finitely generated group, R is generated by u_{s_i}^{±1} and the letter
  cylinders, so EJZ applies.
- **Definition check.** Kerr–Nowak Definition 2.1 (primary, see M4) uses a finite G-set E with a map ζ: E → X. The
  paper notes that for X without isolated points one may take E ⊆ X with ζ the inclusion, which is the form used

## M3 `matricial-steinberg-algebras-have-invariant-measures`: PASS

- Normalized-rank ultralimits satisfy the Sylvester matrix axioms (closed conditions), and pull back along unital maps.
- Rank is additive on orthogonal idempotents. x = (e f), y = (e; f)^T give xy = e+f and yx = diag(e,f), with x = pxq, so
  e+f and e⊕f have equal rank.
- Bisections: 1_B 1_{B^{-1}} = 1_{r(B)}, 1_{B^{-1}} 1_B = 1_{s(B)}, and 1_B = 1_B 1_{B^{-1}} 1_B.
- Countable additivity on the clopen algebra follows by compactness; Carathéodory extends it; invariance extends from
  clopens by monotone class.
- The Cuntz calibration μ(X) = 2μ(X) is correct.

## M4 `kerr-nowak-measure-free-group-actions-are-rf` (+ `-citation`): PASS (primary source now read)

The primary paper was fetched on MSI from Cambridge Core (DOI 10.1017/S0143385711000575) and read with pdftotext.
- D. Kerr and P. W. Nowak, *Residually finite actions and crossed products*, Ergod. Th. Dynam. Sys. 32 (2012),
  1582–1614, p. 1604: "THEOREM 5.2. Let X be a compact metrizable space and F_r ↷ X a minimal continuous action.
  Then the following are equivalent. (1) The action is residually finite. (2) There is an F_r-invariant Borel
  probability measure on X. (3) C(X) ⋊_λ F_r is an MF algebra. (4) C(X) ⋊_λ F_r is stably finite. (5) The action
  is F_r-finite in the Cuntz–Pedersen sense."
- §5 opening: "... group F_r, where r ∈ N ∪ {∞}". Lemma 5.1 (the (2) ⇒ (1) input): "Let X be a compact metrizable
  space and F_r ↷ X a continuous action. Suppose there exists an F_r-invariant Borel probability measure µ on X with
  full support. Then the action is residually finite."
- Ma's restatement, as imported, is faithful. The node's "secondary source" bound is lifted at review.

    finitely many window defects vanish in some 𝒜_{D'}, and a nonzero finite-dimensional 𝒜_{D'}-module (a covariant
    D'-family) gives a unital map exact on the window. Lemma S (M1) finishes.
