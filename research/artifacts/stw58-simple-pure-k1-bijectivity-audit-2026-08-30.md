# STW LVIII simple-pure K1 closure

The simple unital K1-bijectivity slice of Problem LVIII is already decided by
standard literature.

| branch for a unital simple pure `A` | input | conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| `sr(A)=1` | Lin, JFA 289 (2025), Theorem 3.6 | Rieffel, Proc. LMS 46 (1983), Theorem 10.12 gives `U(A)/U_0(A) ~= K_1(A)` |
| `A` purely infinite | Lin, Theorem 3.6 | Cuntz's nonstable K-theory gives K1-surjectivity for properly infinite algebras and K1-injectivity in the simple purely infinite case |

Thus every unital simple pure C-star-algebra is K1-bijective.  Unitality
supplies sigma-unitality; the published Lin theorem has no separability
hypothesis.

This does not settle non-simple pure algebras, nonunital conventions, or
K-stability.  The previous LVIII note understated Rieffel: stable rank one does
not leave K1-surjectivity open; Theorem 10.12 gives the whole isomorphism.
