## 1. sk-draft-rich Corollary 2 (l.205–293)
- **Overgroup (l.218–228).** Evenness of the doubled regular permutations, Ore Theorem 1 per coordinate, and
  `Δ ≤ P` f.g., infinite, residually finite, with `Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ]`. This is part 1 §3.
- **Array (l.230–237).** Index `≥ 4` (at least 3 suffices). The key identity `x(hk) = x(h)` for `h ∉ K_m`, `k ∈ K_m`.
  The action `(g·y)(h) = y(hg)` is a left action.
- **Minimality (l.239–244).**
  - Choosing `m` with `Fσ ∩ K_m ⊆ {1}` leaves at most one position in `K_m`.
  - If there is no such position, the pattern recurs on `σK_m`. If it is `1`, the pattern recurs on `σ` times the
    `1`-coset of `K_(m+1)` in `K_m \ K_(m+1)`, since `x(k) = 1 = x(1)` there.
  - Return sets containing cosets of finite-index subgroups are syndetic.
- **Freeness (l.246–256).**
  - `δ = σ^(-1)γσ ∈ K_m \ K_(m+1)` with `m < n`, by normality.
  - The `1`- and `2`-cosets meet `Tσ`, because `κK_(m+1)σ^(-1)` is a union of `K_n`-cosets.
  - Right multiplication by `δ ∉ K_(m+1)` moves each special coset to another coset of `K_(m+1)` inside `K_m`, and not both
    into `K_(m+1)`. So the value 1 or 2, unique at level `m`, changes for one of them.
  - `tσδ = tγσ`, and `(γ·(σ·x))(t) = x(tγσ)`.
- **Finite models (l.258–264).**
  - `k·p_c = p_c` for `k ∈ K_n`, by the key identity.
  - At most one `h_0 ∈ F` has `h_0σ ∈ K_n`, since `FF^(-1) ∩ K_n = {1}`, and `x(h_0σk)` runs over `{0,1,2}`.
  - Both inclusions of pattern sets hold.
- **Applying Theorem 1 over Δ (l.266–278).**
  - The model `f δ_(hK_n) = f(h·p_c)δ_(hK_n)` is well defined, since `(hk)·p_c = h·p_c`.
  - `u_g D(f) u_g^(-1) = D(f∘g^(-1))`.
  - Windows of `h·p_c` are patterns of `X`, and every pattern of `X` occurs.
  - Distinct `u_g` give distinct cosets once `FF^(-1) ∩ K_n = {1}` for a symmetric ball `F`.
  - The simplicity summary matches the Z proof, l.153–203 (checked line by line in §3b below in its f.g. form):
    `ε_ab = e_(aV)u_(ab^(-1))` multiply as matrix units when `V ∩ gV = ∅` for `0 < |g| ≤ 2w`; the scalar step uses
    freeness; translates of `V` cover `X`.
- **Whitehead (l.280–292).**
  - In characteristic 2, `e_12(c)e_21(c^(-1))e_12(c) = [[0,c],[c^(-1),0]]`.
  - Times the swap: `diag(c,c^(-1))`.
  - `diag(a,a^(-1),1)diag(b,b^(-1),1)diag((ba)^(-1),ba,1) = diag(aba^(-1)b^(-1),1,1)`.
  - `γ ↦ diag(u_γ,1,1)` is an injective homomorphism into `G`.

PASS.

## 2. sk-draft-general (l.52–332)
- **3a, setup.** `Γ` is f.g. and `A` finite (l.52–53). "Finite models" is defined at l.55–57. The theorem hypotheses are
  exactly those of the reviewed `finite-model-subshift-elementary-groups-simple-kazhdan-lef`.
- **3b, ring and (T) (l.111–140).**
  - `R` is generated by `u_t` (`S` symmetric) and the letter indicators.
  - `u_γe_au_γ^(-1)` is a cylinder indicator for the action `(γx)(h) = x(hγ)`.
  - EJZ gives (T).
- **3b, finite models (l.147–172).**
  - The compactness fact uses freeness. `D(f)` is well defined because `L_F(Y) ⊆ L_F(X)`.
  - `P_γD(f)P_γ^(-1) = D(f∘γ^(-1))` when `W ∪ Wγ^(-1) ⊆ F`. I checked the coordinates: `(γ^(-1)x')(h) = x'(hγ^(-1))`
    and `x'` agrees with `γy` on `Wγ^(-1)`.
  - For injectivity, the points `γy`, `γ ∈ E`, are distinct, so the `γ_0`-term is the only one contributing to
    `δ_(y')`.
  - Invertibility follows because the products `gg^(-1)` are preserved.
- **3b, simplicity (l.188–237).**
  - Small sets exist by freeness, and every clopen set is a disjoint union of small ones.
  - **Scalar step.** Commuting with `e_ij(e_V)` for all clopen `V` gives `g = cI` (take `V = X`). Then
    `e_Vc = ce_V` kills `c_γ` for `γ ≠ e` by freeness. The unit `c ∈ LC(X,F_2)^× = {1}` comes from the `u_e`-coefficient
    of `cc^(-1) = 1`. Neither the simplicity nor the centre of `R` is needed.
  - `ε_abε_(a'b') = δ_(ba')ε_(ab')`, from `aV ∩ ab^(-1)a'V = a(V ∩ b^(-1)a'V)`.
  - `ψ` is injective and multiplicative, and `A ↦ I − ψ(I_d) + ψ(A)` is a group embedding.
  - Transvections with `p = q` are commutators through a third column `p' ≠ p`, using `[t_xy,t_yz] = t_xz`.
  - `fu_ae_Vf'u_b = f e_(aV)(f'∘a^(-1))u_(ab) ∈ {0, ε_(a,b^(-1))}`, since `f∘a` and `f'` are constant on `V`.
  - `k − I = (ghg^(-1) − h)h` and `k^(-1) − I = h(ghg^(-1) − h)`, using `h^2 = I` in characteristic 2.
  - Hence `k = I − ψ(I) + ψ(B)`, with `B` invertible by injectivity of `ψ`, `k ∈ H`, and `N ∩ H ≠ 1`.
  - `d = n|B_w| ≥ 3`, including `w = 0`.
  - Level ideal: `[e_pl(s),e_lq(r)] = e_pq(sr)` and `[e_pl(r),e_lq(s)] = e_pq(rs)`. Translates of `V` cover `X`.
- **3c, operator-algebra sentences (l.174–185).**
  - Infinite f.g. simple implies ICC, so `L(G)` is a II_1 factor.
  - `L(G)⊗̄R` is McDuff and separable. It embeds in `R^ω` once `L(G)` does.
  - `g ↦ λ_g ⊗ 1` puts `G` in its unitary group.
  - Kirchberg (T)+FP ⇒ RF, and `G` is not RF.
  - The theorem numbers are those verified by sk-operator-algebras and sk-referee-2 (arXiv versions).
- **3d, Proposition 2.**
  - (a) The word `x_[0,m+2ℓ)` has prefix and suffix `x_[0,2ℓ)`, so it has period `m`. The periodic `y` agrees with it on
    `[0,m+2ℓ)`, so every `(2ℓ+1)`-window of `y` is a word of `x`, and all such words of `X` occur. Patterns on subsets of
    `[−ℓ,ℓ]` are restrictions.
  - (b) is part 1 §7.
  - (c) is part 1 §8. The minimality case `h_0σ = e` uses the `1`-coset of `K_(n+1)` in `K_n \ K_(n+1)`.
