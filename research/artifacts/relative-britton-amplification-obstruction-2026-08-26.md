# A finite-matrix obstruction to naive relative Britton separation

## Status

This note gives a zero-literature, finite-dimensional counterexample to the
following *local amplification principle*:

> Given a finite-dimensional vertex representation and an exact compatible
> edge representation, amplification alone lets one choose an edge-compatible
> stable-letter unitary which separates every prescribed finite family of
> reduced HNN words in operator norm.

The principle is false.  The counterexample does **not** disprove an
existential MF-permanence theorem for HNN extensions or amalgamated free
products: such a theorem may replace the supplied approximants by richer
ones.  It proves that compatibility of the supplied approximants, followed
only by multiplicity amplification, is insufficient.  A genuine proof needs
an additional relative-separation construction.

No external theorem is used below.

## 1. The group and a nontrivial reduced word

Let

\[
G=F(a,b,c), \qquad S=\langle a,b\rangle\leq G,
\]

and form the identity HNN extension

\[
H=\langle a,b,c,t\mid tat^{-1}=a,\ tbt^{-1}=b\rangle.
\]

Consider

\[
w=tct^{-1}c^{-1}.
\]

The element \(w\) is nontrivial without appealing to Britton's lemma.  Define
a map on the displayed presentation by

\[
a\longmapsto 1,\qquad b\longmapsto 1,
\qquad c\longmapsto c,\qquad t\longmapsto t
\]

into the free group \(F(t,c)\).  Both defining relations map to the identity,
so this is a homomorphism.  Its value on \(w\) is the freely reduced word

\[
tct^{-1}c^{-1},
\]

which is not empty.  Hence \(w\neq 1\) in \(H\).

In HNN terminology this is precisely the elementary reduced alternating word:
the syllable \(c\) is outside the associated subgroup \(S\).

## 2. An exact compatible finite approximant

On \(\mathbf C^2\), put

\[
X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C=-I_2.
\]

Because \(G\) is free on \(a,b,c\), the assignments

\[
\pi(a)=X,\qquad \pi(b)=Z,\qquad \pi(c)=C
\]

define an exact two-dimensional unitary representation of \(G\).  It has no
multiplicative defect, and it strongly separates the vertex syllable used in
\(w\):

\[
\|\pi(c)-I_2\|=2.
\]

The two edge embeddings in the identity HNN extension agree literally, so
the edge-compatibility condition at this finite coordinate is exact.

The edge image already generates the entire matrix algebra.  Indeed,

\[
e_{11}=\frac{I+Z}{2},\qquad e_{22}=\frac{I-Z}{2},
\]

and

\[
e_{12}=e_{11}Xe_{22},\qquad e_{21}=e_{22}Xe_{11}.
\]

Thus the unital algebra generated by \(\pi(S)\) is \(M_2(\mathbf C)\).

## 3. Every compatible amplified intertwiner kills the word

Fix any amplification multiplicity \(N\geq1\).  The amplified vertex
representation is

\[
\pi_N(g)=\pi(g)\otimes I_N
\quad\text{on}\quad \mathbf C^2\otimes\mathbf C^N.
\]

Let \(T\) be any candidate stable-letter unitary satisfying the exact edge
relations

\[
T(X\otimes I_N)T^*=X\otimes I_N,
\qquad
T(Z\otimes I_N)T^*=Z\otimes I_N.
\]

We compute its form directly.  Relative to the two eigenspaces of
\(Z\otimes I_N\), write \(T\) as a \(2\times2\) block matrix with
\(N\times N\) blocks.  Commutation with \(Z\otimes I_N\) forces

\[
T=\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\0&D\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Commutation with \(X\otimes I_N\) then forces \(A=D\).  Consequently

\[
T=I_2\otimes U
\]

for some \(U\in U(N)\).  In particular \(T\) commutes with

\[
\pi_N(c)=-I_2\otimes I_N.
\]

Therefore the value of the nontrivial reduced word is always

\[
T\pi_N(c)T^*\pi_N(c)^*=I_{2N}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
  \|T\pi_N(c)T^*\pi_N(c)^*-I_{2N}\|=0
}
\]

for every multiplicity \(N\) and every exactly edge-compatible amplified
intertwiner \(T\).

Thus no positive operator-norm separation constant exists, even for the
single reduced word \(w\), even though the input representation is exact and
\(c\) itself is separated from the identity by the maximal distance \(2\).

## 4. What exactly fails

The failure is not a shortage of multiplicity.  The problem is that this
coordinate has

\[
C^*(\pi(S))=C^*(\pi(G))=M_2(\mathbf C).
\]

Consequently the commutant available for an identity-edge stable letter is
also the commutant of the whole vertex image.  Tensor amplification changes
the commutant from scalars to \(I_2\otimes M_N\), but that new multiplicity
algebra still commutes with every amplified vertex operator.  It supplies
room, not relative motion.

The same calculation applies to every direct sum of identical copies of this
finite approximant.  Hence taking more copies, choosing a larger permutation
on the multiplicity space, or choosing a Haar/random unitary in that space
cannot help: all such choices commute with \(\pi_N(c)\).

## 5. The missing hypothesis/construction

A valid relative Britton lemma must ensure more than:

1. small vertex-group defects;
2. faithful norm separation of each vertex syllable from the identity; and
3. exact or asymptotic agreement of the two edge restrictions.

It must also retain information *relative to the edge algebra*.  At minimum,
for a syllable \(d\notin B\), the chosen finite model must admit an
edge-central gauge \(U\) for which

\[
\|U\pi(d)U^*-\pi(d)\|
\]

is bounded below.  For longer alternating words, the required condition is
stronger: the corresponding alternating noncommutative polynomial in the
edge-central gauges and vertex syllables must be nonzero with a quantitative
operator-norm lower bound.

One way to phrase the needed finite-coordinate datum is:

> **Relative separating packet.**  For each finite family \(\mathcal W\) of
> reduced alternating words and tolerance \(\varepsilon\), choose the vertex
> approximant together with edge intertwiners, rather than amplifying an
> arbitrary previously chosen approximant, so that every word in
> \(\mathcal W\) has multiplicative defect at most \(\varepsilon\) and remains
> a fixed positive distance from the identity.

Calling this a hypothesis merely restates the desired endpoint.  To prove MF
permanence from scratch, one must construct such packets from the original
corona data.  The elementary tensor-synchronization argument does not do so.

An equivalent representation-theoretic diagnosis is that the finite model
must contain enough nontrivial \(B\)-bimodule directions.  Pure tensor
multiplicity adds copies of the same \(B\)-bimodule and cannot create a
relative direction absent from the original coordinate.

## 6. Consequence for the compatible-corona route

The counterexample rules out the proposed proof step in the form

\[
\text{compatible finite coordinate}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\text{Britton separation after ordinary amplification}.
\]

It does not rule out

\[
\text{compatible faithful corona embeddings}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\text{existence of newly constructed relative separating coordinates}.
\]

But the second implication is the substantive permanence theorem, not a
formal amplification argument.  Any unconditional proof must visibly build
the missing relative \(B\)-bimodule/coset geometry or provide another direct
finite-matrix separation mechanism.  Without that construction, the
compatible-corona HNN/AFP gap remains real.
