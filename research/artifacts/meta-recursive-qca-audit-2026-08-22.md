## Corrections retained during integration

1. Exact extendability is closed for fixed finite gadgets over complete
   matrix-ultraproduct tracial types.  For quantifier-free scalar profiles
   with an externally restricted witness correspondence, continuity is too
   strong; lower semicontinuity/closed nonextension neighborhoods are the safe
   formulation.
2. An infinite exhaustive library has a finite residual subcover, but that
   does not alone give a finitely presented group.  The base must itself be
   finitely presented (or separately and faithfully compiled), the selected
   gadgets need one simultaneous exact completion, and the final extension
   must preserve the base canonical profile.
3. A common exact representation retaining the mark does not imply base
   injectivity or canonical-profile preservation.
4. Finite tracial soundness means a positive infimum over all dimensions,
   equivalently exclusion of a zero-energy matrix-ultraproduct strategy.  The
   absence of an exact strategy in each individual dimension is insufficient.
5. In a central-sign decoder, ambient energy is carrier mass times normalized
   corner energy.  The approximate central involution must be spectrally
   rounded and the decoded letters block/polar corrected before applying the
   BCS gap.
6. Parity relations describe an affine character space, but an arbitrary
   representation can occupy a subset of it.  Uniform support requires a
   canonical twisted-regular or otherwise authenticated sector.
7. Relative-commutant synchronization requires an actual positive-density
   reducing carrier and exact actor/commutant correction.  Flexible dimension
   alone does not return exact upstairs relations to a downstairs corner, and
   sharp two-transitivity controls pairs but not general QCA triple patterns.

## Net conclusion

Self-reference can select or describe a semantic obstruction, but cannot be
its normalized-HS source.  If a meta-recursive QCA excludes every separated
matrix-ultraproduct type, compactness exposes a finite static core.  If every
finite prefix only exceeds a larger finite capacity, diagonalization builds an
ultraproduct model.  The remaining live construction is finite: transduce a
fixed non-CE BCS forbidden-projection energy into ordinary group-word energy
on one common positive-density central-sign carrier.
