# Novelty check: Zaremsky Problem 1.23 answered no (2026-09-13)

Requested by z-verify-topology (review PASS, `zp-review-topology-2026-09-13-part1.md` §5) on the landing

**Claim checked.** Γ = {A,B} * (C–D–E–F). The character killing A and B has kernel F_2 × F_3, which has
quadratic Dehn function. BB_Γ has cubic Dehn function by Chang–García-Mejía–Migliorini, arXiv:2507.07566,
Corollary 1 (their Γ_1). So δ_{BB_Γ} is not a lower bound for all finitely presented kernels of maps onto Z.

## Verdict

1. **Comparison: NOT FOUND in print or on arXiv, before or after July 12, 2026, within the sources below.**
   No source says that the Bestvina–Brady Dehn function fails to be a lower bound over all kernels. No
   source compares the Dehn function of BB_Γ with that of another kernel of the same A_Γ.
2. **Restricted version (χ(v) ≠ 0 for every vertex v): NOT ADDRESSED anywhere found.** The closest statement
   is Kropholler–Llosa Isenrich's remark (below): outside Bestvina–Brady groups, Dehn functions of coabelian
   subgroups of RAAGs were essentially unstudied, and a general study "would be interesting".
3. **Credit framing.** The answer is an elementary observation on top of published work:
   - the cubic lower bound is Chang–García-Mejía–Migliorini's;
   - the graph Γ_1 and the cubic Bestvina–Brady group come from Brady, *Branched coverings of cubical
     complexes and subgroups of hyperbolic groups* (J. London Math. Soc.), as CGM §1.3 credits;
   - the quadratic kernel F_2 × F_3 is standard.
   Relay it as "answers the literal question negatively; no earlier statement found in the sources checked",
   not as a significant new theorem. Zaremsky added the problem on 1/20/26, after CGM v2 (2025-12-30). He
   may have meant characters with no dead vertices, which is the open restricted version, so keep that
   caveat in any relay.

## Sources read, and what they say

- **Chang–García-Mejía–Migliorini**, *Complete classification of the Dehn functions of Bestvina–Brady
  groups*, arXiv:2507.07566v2 (HTML; GAFA 2026, doi:10.1007/s00039-026-00731-7).
  - The paper uses only the all-ones height function.
  - It has no sentence on kernels of other characters, no minimality statement, and no mention of Zaremsky.
  - Its only questions: Q1, whether the Dehn function of every finitely presented BB group is equivalent to
    a polynomial; Q2, whether some BB group of type F_∞ with quadratic Dehn function is not CAT(0).
  - §1.3: "The graph Γ₁ and Γ₂ were considered by Brady in [5], where he constructed Bestvina–Brady groups
    with cubic and quartic Dehn functions, respectively."
- **Kropholler–Llosa Isenrich**, *Dehn functions of coabelian subgroups of direct products of groups*,
  arXiv:2103.05093v3 (PDF pp. 1–3, 20–25; J. London Math. Soc. 107 (2023) 123–152).
  - §5.3, p. 23, verbatim: "The precise finiteness properties of arbitrary coabelian subgroups of
    right-angled Artin groups have been computed in [28, 13], generalising the results for Bestvina–Brady
    groups [3]. In contrast our understanding of their Dehn functions seems to be mostly limited to
    Bestvina–Brady groups [16, 14, 15, 1], which are the cocyclic subgroups of RAAGs obtained by mapping
    all generators to the same generator of Z. It would be interesting to perform a general study of the
    Dehn functions of coabelian subgroups of RAAGs."
  - Theorems 5.6 and 5.8: for A_Γ a product of four (P-split) or three (split) RAAGs, the kernel has
    quadratic Dehn function. Γ_1 is a two-fold join, so these do not cover it, and nothing is compared
    with BB_Γ.
- **Carter–Forester**, *The Dehn functions of Stallings–Bieri groups*, arXiv:1509.07539 (HTML; Math. Ann.
  368 (2017)). Theorem 4.2 concerns three-fold joins with the all-ones height function only; other
  characters are not discussed.
