## 1. What the directive's host would have to be

**The configuration module is a module over an operator algebra.** This is read from
KMS Bull. Math. Sci. 7 (2017), pp. 338--340 (scratchpad `bhwp/kms-bms.txt`, lines
1560--1700). Let `V` be the `F_p`-space with basis `W ∪ W_0`, so
`T_1 = V^(3^k)` with coordinates `i in {1,2,3}^k`.
- **`A_j`.** It acts as `1 + ρ_(A_j)`, where `ρ_d(w) = w d` in `Š` (for `w in W`) or in
  `S(MM_k)` (for `w in W_0`), and `z(i, 0) = 1`. The clause "identity if `w` contains
  `A_j`" is automatic, since then `w A_j = 0`.
- **`A_0`.** It acts as `1 + ψ̂`, with `ψ̂(w) = ψ(w)` on `W` and `0` on `W_0`.
- **`a_j`, `a'_j`.** They act blockwise, by the scalar triangular `3×3` pattern of
  (11) in the `j`-th coordinate tensored with the projection `π_j` onto words without
  `A_j`, plus `ρ_(a_j) π_j` in the diagonal blocks with `i_j = 1`, and the identity on
  `1 - π_j`.
- **`ã_j`.** It needs in addition the projection onto `W` and the operator `v A_j -> v a_j A_j`.

So `M <= GL_(3^k)(E)`, where `E <= End_(F_p)(V)` is generated by the finitely many
operators above, and `G(MM_k) ≅ T_1 x| M` sits in the affine group of the
`E`-module `V^(3^k)`. This is an observation read off the formulas; it is not landed as
a node. The Minsky relations enter only through `ρ_d` on `W_0`.

**`E` is not commutative.** For example `ρ_(a_j) ρ_(A_j) ≠ ρ_(A_j) ρ_(a_j)`, since
`w A_j a_j = 0` while `w a_j A_j ≠ 0` for suitable `w`. The landed claim shows this is
necessary, not incidental: over any commutative algebra of positive characteristic the
host's word problem is uniformly bounded, while the hard `G(MM_k)` beat every bound.

## 2. Why the commutative self-similar hosts cannot be adapted

- **The `F_p[s]` hosts.** Their self-similarity comes from a uniformizer `s_1`: the
  ring is free of finite rank over a copy of itself.
- **The analogue for `E`.** It would need a virtual endomorphism compatible with the
  Minsky relations. Scaling counters, `a -> a^2` on letters, maps `S(MM)` to
  `S(MM^(2))`, the semigroup of the machine with doubled increments. That is a
  different semigroup, so it is not an endomorphism.
- **The shift.** Attempt 1 on the target already records that the shift breaks at zero
  tests.
- **Nothing better found.** No endomorphism of `E` with finite-rank image was found.

## 3. Proof ideas of the landed claims

- **Claim 1.**
  - Lift the generators to polynomial data `(v, W)` over `P_0 = F_q[x]`; a word of
    length `l` gives degree `<= d*l`.
  - Triviality is `N + 1` membership tests in `L + I P_0^N`.
  - Encode `u in P_0^N` as `Σ u_i y_i` in `P = P_0[y_1..y_N]`. Then membership in the
    submodule becomes membership in the `y`-homogeneous ideal generated by `I`, the
    linear forms of `L` and all `y_i y_(i')`.
  - Dividing by a fixed graded Gröbner basis stays in degree `<= d*l + 1`, and each
    step strictly lowers the leading monomial. So the cost is polynomial in `l`.
- **Claim 2.** Diagonalize past `2^n`, realize the problem in `A_p^2 A` with
  `kms-hard-fp-rf-groups-lie-in-apapa-variety`, and transfer the word problem along the
  embedding.
