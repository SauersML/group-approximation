## 1. What was re-derived by hand (all correct)

### 1.1 The half-line overgroup (l.358–381)
- **[β,h_s] multiplies level 0 by s^{-1}.** Compose right to left on (x,m):
  - h_s^{-1} gives (s^{-1}x,m) if m ≥ 0;
  - β^{-1} lowers the level;
  - h_s multiplies by s iff m−1 ≥ 0;
  - β restores the level.

  The net effect is x at levels m ≥ 1, s^{-1}x at m = 0, and x at m < 0. Γ acts faithfully by left multiplication on level 0, generated by the s^{-1}, so Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ]. Δ is infinite because β has infinite order.
- **Normal form.** β^k h_s β^{-k} multiplies the levels m ≥ k by s. In a word of length ≤ r each letter h_s^{±1} has exponent sum |k| ≤ r before it, so f(m) is a product of ≤ r letters, f(m) = 1 for m < −r, and f(m) = f(r) for m ≥ r. The normal form fβ^ℓ in Γ^Z⋊Z is unique.
- **θ is a group isomorphism** from {v_{3r−1} = v_{3r}} onto {v_{−3r} = 1}, both of index |Q| in P = Q^{[−3r,3r]}:
  - it is coordinatewise;
  - it is injective because v_{3r} = v_{3r−1};
  - its inverse is v_m = w_{m+1} for m < 3r, with v_{3r} = w_{3r}.
- **κ.** Take right transversals T_A, T_B of A = dom θ and B = im θ, and a bijection τ: T_A → T_B. Then κ(at) = θ(a)τ(t) is a bijection of P with κ(vx) = θ(v)κ(x) for v ∈ A.
- **ζvζ^{-1} = θ(v) on A:** ζvζ^{-1}(x,i) = ζ(vκ^{-1}(x), i−1) = (κ(vκ^{-1}(x)), i) = (θ(v)x, i).
- **Iterates stay in the domain.** For v = (ψ(f(m)))_m with f as above:
  - ψ(1) = 1, since 1·1 = 1 stays in the ball.
  - After j shifts up, membership in A needs f(3r−1−j) = f(3r−j), which holds for j ≤ 2r−1.
  - After j shifts down, membership in B needs f(−3r+j) = 1, which holds for j ≤ 2r−1.
  - Only j ≤ |ℓ|−1 ≤ r−1 is used, so ζ^ℓ v ζ^{-ℓ} = (ψ(f(m−ℓ)))_m for |ℓ| ≤ r.
- **Multiplicativity.** If g = fβ^ℓ, g′ = f′β^{ℓ′} and gg′ all have length ≤ r, then gg′ = f″β^{ℓ+ℓ′} with f″(m) = f(m)f′(m−ℓ). In the model, vζ^ℓ·v′ζ^{ℓ′} = v·(ζ^ℓv′ζ^{-ℓ})·ζ^{ℓ+ℓ′}, whose coordinates are ψ(f(m))ψ(f′(m−ℓ)) = ψ(f″(m)). All three elements lie in the ball of radius r ⊆ 2r.
- **Injectivity on the ball.** vζ^ℓ = v′ζ^{ℓ′} forces ℓ ≡ ℓ′ mod 5r, so ℓ = ℓ′ as 5r > 2r. Then v = v′ as left multiplications, so f = f′ on [−3r,3r], hence everywhere by the tail properties. So Δ is LEF.
- Note N1 (no change): the ball of radius r of Γ would suffice for ψ; radius 2r is harmless.

### 1.2 Word-problem transfer (l.438–457)
- **WP(Δ):**
  - the change levels are the exponent sums of β before the letters h_s^{±1};
  - the values f(m) between consecutive change levels are words in S;
  - the word is trivial iff ℓ = 0 and every such value is trivial in Γ.
- **Λ = ⊕_Δ F_2 ⋊ Δ.** A normal form is a word for δ plus a finite multiset of lamp positions. Equality and the values c(δ′) are decidable through WP(Δ), cancelling equal positions mod 2.
- **The key identity.** With (fu_ξ)(f′u_η) = f(f′∘ξ^{-1})u_{ξη} and ξ: x ↦ δx+c, ξ^{-1}x = δ^{-1}(x−c), so (ξ^{-1}x)(e) = x(δ)+c(δ). Hence u_ξe_Uu_ξ^{-1} = e_{ξU} is the indicator of {x(δ) = 1+c(δ)}, as printed. More generally (ξ^{-1}x)(δ′) = x(δδ′)+c(δδ′), so polynomial coefficients in the coordinates are preserved under the product rule.
- **Deciding triviality.** R_Δ = ⊕_ξ LC(Ω,F_2)u_ξ. After merging equal ξ and equal coordinates (decidable), a coefficient vanishes on Ω = F_2^Δ iff it vanishes at all assignments of its finitely many independent coordinates. So WP(G_Δ) is decidable relative to WP(Γ).
- **Converse.** Word problems and LEF both pass to finitely generated subgroups.
- **Host properties.** The half-line Δ is infinite, f.g. and LEF with Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ], so the remaining steps (lamplighter host, configuration models, Theorem 2, Whitehead identities) apply unchanged. The host is infinite, f.g., simple, Kazhdan, and a limit of SL_N(F_2) expanders.

### 1.3 Continuum many non-embeddable G_X (l.501–502, l.528–533)
- **Embeddings.** If H ≤ G with both finitely generated, substituting words for the generators gives a computable many-one reduction WP(H) ≤ WP(G).
- **Degrees.** By the proof, WP(G_{X_α}) ≡_T L(X_α) ≡_T α. For each S ⊆ N, α_S = [0;1+χ_S(0),…] is irrational and has the degree of S.
- **Conclusion.** Along a continuum antichain of degrees, an embedding G_{X_α} ≤ G_{X_β} would give deg α ≤ deg β, which is impossible. So none embeds in another, and in particular there are continuum many isomorphism types.

### 1.4 Osajda sentence (l.460–463)
- Osajda's groups are f.g. and residually finite, so LEF. Corollary 3 puts them in infinite simple Kazhdan LEF hosts.
- Exactness of a discrete group is equivalent to property A (Guentner–Kaminker, Ozawa) and passes to subgroups, so the hosts are not exact.
