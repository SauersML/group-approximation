# Ideas lane nh-sp4-covers (2026-09-13)

Target: `non-hyperlinear-group`. Lens: the refutation side of the Sp4
dichotomy, i.e. finite cyclic central covers `E_x^(n)` of finite-index
`Gamma' <= Sp_4(Z)` along classes `x in H^2(Gamma',Z)` of infinite order
(`sp4-finite-index-scalar-data-iff-hyperlinear-covers`, conditions FIC1-FIC3).
Nothing here is claimed as proved. Status of each approach is a verdict on its
cheapest kill test only. No Cairn node is edited.

Notation: `E_CE(Gamma') <= H^2(Gamma',T)` is the closed subgroup of twists
`omega` with `L_omega(Gamma')` Connes embeddable; `E^0` is its identity
component. By FIC1-FIC2, a circle through `y` not contained in `E_CE(Gamma')`
gives some non-hyperlinear `E_y^(n)`: `L(E_y^(n))` splits over the sectors
`e(k y / n)`, and a non-CE sector makes the group non-hyperlinear.

## Kill-test inputs checked in this lane

- **b2 >= 2 exists, and is large.** Hoffman--Weintraub, *Cohomology of the
  Siegel modular group of degree two and level four*
  (www.math.lsu.edu/~hoffman/papers/level4.pdf), Theorem 1.2: "The Betti
  numbers h_i = dim H^i(Gamma(4), Q) of the principal congruence subgroup of
  level 4 in Sp_4(Z) are 1, 0, 118, 1112, 481 for i = 0, ..., 4". Theorem 3.2
  (cited there as [21]): `H^2(A_2(n)^*, Q) -> H^2(A_2(n), Q)` is onto, "In
  other words, H2(A2(n), Q) is pure of weight 2". Theorem 1.1 gives the Hodge
  numbers of the smooth compactification `A_2(4)^*`, including
  `h^{2,0} = h^{0,2} = 6` and `h^{1,1} = 226`. So `b_2(Gamma(4)) = 118`,
  against `b_2 = 1` at the Maslov level (FIC3).
- **Dogon's stability route is already in the graph.**
  `dogon-sp2g-weak-ucp-nonhyperlinear` (arXiv:2211.10492v3, Cor. 1.9, g >= 2),
  `flexible-hs-stability-passes-to-kazhdan-kernel-quotients`, and
  `infinite-hyperlinear-kazhdan-group-is-not-hs-stable-citation`
  (Fournier-Facio--Gerasimova--Spaas arXiv:2307.13155; Becker--Lubotzky
  arXiv:1809.00632).
- **Normalized-HS almost-commuting unitaries are near commuting.**
  Hadwin--Shulman, *Stability of group relations under small Hilbert--Schmidt
  perturbations*, arXiv:1706.08405 (J. Funct. Anal. 275 (2018)); abstract
  level: `Z^2` is flexibly stable in normalized HS.

## Approach 1. Hecke stability of the CE torus (SURVIVES, structural)

**Idea.**
1. Let `Gamma_0` be torsion-free and `Gamma' <= Gamma_0` of index `m`, e.g.
   `Gamma(4)` and its finite-index subgroups (`Gamma(N)` is torsion-free for
   `N >= 3`).
2. **Corestriction.** Given microstates `rho_k : Gamma' -> U(d_k)` for
   `L_omega(Gamma')` (normalized-HS defect `eps_k -> 0`, traces
   `tr rho_k(h) -> 0` for `h != e`), tensor-induce them to `Gamma_0` on
   `(C^{d_k})^{tensor m}`.
   - The slotwise cocycle identity for a transversal and
     `||tensor_i A_i - tensor_i B_i||_HS <= sum_i ||A_i - B_i||_HS` for
     unitaries bound the defect by `m eps_k`.
   - For `g != e`, the trace is a product over the cycles of `sigma_g` of
     `tr rho_k(t^{-1} g^{|c|} t)`. Each argument is `!= e` because `Gamma_0`
     is torsion-free, so the trace tends to 0.
   - The cocycle of tensor induction is the corestriction (standard norm map;
     not re-read at source here). So `cor(E_CE(Gamma')) <= E_CE(Gamma_0)`.
3. **Restriction.** For finite-index `Lambda`, `L_{res omega}(Lambda)` sits in
   `L_omega(Gamma')` with finite index, so `res omega` is CE iff `omega` is.
4. **Conjugation.** Conjugation by commensurator elements transports twisted
   factors.
5. **Hecke operators.** `T_g = cor o c_g o res` therefore maps `E_CE(Gamma')`
   into itself. The connected image of `E^0` lies in `E^0`.
6. **Conclusion.** `E^0` is a subtorus whose tangent space `W` is a rational,
   Hecke-stable subspace of `H^2(Gamma',R)`.

**What it would prove.**
- At `Gamma(4)`, the 118-dimensional refutation torus splits into finitely
  many rational Hecke pieces: the Maslov / trivial-isotypic line (the old
  problem) and genuinely new automorphic pieces. The weight-3 holomorphic 2-form
  part is at least 12-dimensional real, by `h^{2,0} = 6`.
- `E^0` is a sum of such pieces, and contains the whole rational Hecke-submodule
  generated by any CE eigen-direction.
- Any a priori bound `dim E^0 < d`, for `d` the smallest dimension of a
  non-Maslov rational Hecke piece, would exclude that piece. Then some
  `E_y^(n)` is non-hyperlinear.

**Kill tests run.**
- The hand check of the tensor-induction defect and trace estimates passes on
  torsion-free `Gamma_0`. At `Sp_4(Z)` itself torsion breaks the trace step,
  so the tower must stay torsion-free.
- Repo scan: no Maslov/sp4/deligne node discusses Hecke stability of `E_CE`.
  The Hecke hits (`sp4-metaplectic-class-level-two-computation`, the theta
  nodes) are about the metaplectic class.
- Not checked: semisimplicity of the Hecke action on `H^2(Gamma(N),R)`, which
  is needed to say "sum of Galois orbits of eigen-systems" rather than
  "Hecke submodule".

**Verdict: survives.** A reformulation, not a mechanism. The missing input is
an upper bound on `dim E^0`, which is itself a non-CE statement.

## Approach 2. Hecke amplification of small twists (KILLED)

**Idea.** Start from CE microstates at a tiny twist `t y` on a Hecke eigen-class
`y` and iterate `T_p` to push the twist to a large parameter, hoping the defect
stays below the forced sector gap.

**What it would prove.** A contradiction with a sector gap, hence non-CE.

**Kill test (hand computation).** Tensor induction multiplies the defect by
`deg T_p`. The parameter is multiplied by the eigenvalue `lambda_p`, and
`|lambda_p| <= deg T_p` on the torus. Equality holds on the trivial-isotypic
(Maslov) line, where nothing is gained. On other pieces the ratio is `< 1`,
so iteration contracts twists relative to defect.

**Verdict: killed.** Hecke iteration cannot amplify a twist faster than it
amplifies the defect.

## Approach 3. Unbounded non-Maslov classes and central distortion (KILLED as a mechanism)

**Idea.** Only the Maslov/Kaehler direction should be a bounded class. The other
`117` directions at `Gamma(4)` are unbounded, so their central `Z` is distorted
in `E_x`. Hope that distortion plus property (T) forces the central element to
collapse in normalized-HS models.

**What it would prove.** Collapse of the `e(1/n)` sector, hence non-CE.

**Kill test.** The integral Heisenberg group has a distorted centre and is
residually finite, hence hyperlinear. So distortion alone gives no
obstruction. The boundedness input is also unverified for non-uniform
`Sp_4(Z)`. The Burger--Monod JEMS 1999 injectivity `H^2_b -> H^2`, seen at
abstract/summary level, was for cocompact higher-rank lattices.

**Verdict: killed** as a mechanism. The boundedness split is unclear, and it is
recorded only as a way to tell the Maslov piece apart.

## Approach 4. Integrality / index invariants of approximate projective representations (KILLED)

**Idea.** Use determinant or Bott-index integrality, e.g. `k [omega] = 0` for
genuine `k`-dimensional projective representations, extended to normalized-HS
microstates along two independent circles (`b_2 >= 2`) to force a rational
constraint that a whole circle cannot meet.

**What it would prove.** `E^0` has no positive dimension along the constrained
directions.

**Kill test.** Hadwin--Shulman (arXiv:1706.08405): in normalized HS, almost
commuting unitaries are near commuting, and `Z^2` is flexibly stable. So the
Exel--Loring / Bott obstructions disappear in exactly the norm that
hyperlinearity uses. Determinants are not normalized-HS continuous.

**Verdict: killed.**

## Approach 5. Sector-relative weak ucp-stability along a new Hecke piece (UNCLEAR)

**Idea.**
- Dogon's route needs weak ucp-stability of the Kazhdan base for all
  asymptotic representations.
- By FIC2 every `E_y^(n)` with `n` large is non-residually-amenable, and by
  `sp4-finite-index-fd-projective-multiplier-is-finite` only finitely many
  twists are exactly realizable.
- So it would suffice to stabilize only those approximate representations whose
  twist lies in a fixed non-Maslov Hecke piece `V`. Those miss the trivial
  isotypic line, where the known local-HS-stability no-go
  (`infinite-hyperlinear-kazhdan-group-is-not-hs-stable-citation`) is witnessed
  by the regular / finite-quotient models.

**What it would prove.** Non-CE of the circles in `V`, hence a non-hyperlinear
`E_y^(n)`.

**Kill test.**
- The graph already has the full Dogon reduction and the
  Kazhdan-kernel-quotient permanence. Neither separates sectors by isotypic
  type.
- No known no-go restricts stability relative to a nontrivial Hecke eigen-class.
  It was not searched beyond the repo nodes named above.

**Verdict: unclear.** The first test is whether the
Fournier-Facio--Gerasimova--Spaas local non-stability witnesses can be arranged
with twist in `V`. If they can, the approach dies.
