# HS scale pinning: reduction, excess lemma, assembly, cascades (2026-09-12)

Lane `hs-scale-pinning`. Target: `hs-normalization-needs-coarse-actor-scale-pinning`,
the corrected conditional behind `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs`.

## Summary

1. **Reduction (established).** Under (H1), (CCR) is equivalent to the vanishing of
   the finite-stage commutant excess of the genuine vertex representations.
   Moreover (H3), in the multiplicative form the median step needs, follows from
   (H1) and property (T) of `Lambda`. So (H3) is redundant.
   Node: `vertex-rounding-reduces-ccr-to-commutant-excess`.
2. **Excess lemma (established).** For any finite-dimensional unitary representation
   and any subgroup, the 2-norm excess of the subgroup commutant over the group
   commutant is at most `4 * (jump mass)^(1/2) + 2c`, where the jump mass is the
   trace of `{zeta_B >= (1+c) zeta_A}`. Node:
   `commutant-excess-bounded-by-aspect-ratio-jump-mass`.
3. **Assembly.** With (1) and (2), (H1) plus the coarse actor gap (H2), stated at a
   generating set made of vertex generators and strict compressors, gives (CCR)
   by the median step. The only part not written to established standard is the
   mass-weighted functional-calculus bookkeeping over many blocks (step S4).
4. **Cascades.** A multiplicity cascade with order-one jump mass contradicts (CCR)
   through (2), so under (H1) it forces (H2) to fail. Cascades refute (H2); they do
   not break the assembly. No cascade model for a Theorem E actor is known.
5. **Additive transport is vacuous.** The additive `L^1` form of (H3) carries no
   information at large irreducible dimension (example in section 6).

## 1. Setup

`Gamma < G`, `t` a strict compressor, `Lambda = t Gamma t^(-1) <= Gamma`, `(F, kappa)`
a Kazhdan pair for `Gamma`. `sigma_n : G -> U(n)` is a trace-preserving asymptotic
representation, `M = prod_U M_n`.

(H1): after `o(n)` padding, genuine `pi_n : Gamma -> U(n')`, `n'/n -> 1`, with
`max_(s in F) ||sigma_n(s) - pi_n(s)||_2 -> 0`.

Put `A_n = pi_n(Gamma)'`, `B_n = pi_n(Lambda)'` (so `A_n <= B_n`), `E_n` the
trace-preserving conditional expectation onto `A_n`, and

```text
eps_n = sup { ||x - E_n x||_2 : x in B_n, ||x|| <= 1 }.
```

Decompose `pi_n = sum_i m_i rho_i`, with `rho_i` distinct irreducible of dimension `d_i`,
and `rho_i|Lambda = sum_j c_ij theta_j`, with `theta_j` distinct irreducible of dimension
`e_j`. Then `B_n` has summands of multiplicity `m'_j = sum_i m_i c_ij`. The aspect ratios
are `zeta_A = sum_i (m_i/d_i) z_i` and `zeta_B = sum_j (m'_j/e_j) w_j`, over the minimal
central projections. They commute, and `zeta_B >= zeta_A`: on the `(i,j)` part,
`m'_j d_i >= (m_i c_ij)(c_ij e_j) >= m_i e_j`.

## 2. Reduction (proof of node 1)

**(a) Commutants.**
- `sigma(Gamma)' cap M = prod_U A_n`. The inclusion from right to left holds
  because `||[x, sigma_n(s)]||_2 <= 2 ||sigma_n(s) - pi_n(s)||_2` for contractions
  `x in A_n`.
- For the other inclusion, lift a contraction `z` to `z_n` with `||[z_n, sigma_n(s)]||_2 -> 0`.
  Then `||[z_n, pi_n(s)]||_2 -> 0`. `Ad pi_n` is a unitary representation of `Gamma`
  on `L^2(M_n')`, and its fixed space is `A_n`. So
  `||z_n - E_n z_n||_2 <= kappa^(-1) max_s ||[pi_n(s), z_n]||_2 -> 0`, and `E_n z_n` is
  a contraction in `A_n`.
- `Lambda` is generated by `lambda_s = t s t^(-1)`, and `(F_Lambda, kappa)` is a Kazhdan pair
  for it, since `Lambda` is isomorphic to `Gamma` via `Ad t^(-1)`. Each `lambda_s` is a fixed
  word `w_s` in `F`, so asymptotic multiplicativity gives
  `sigma_n(lambda_s) ~ sigma_n(w_s) ~ pi_n(w_s) = pi_n(lambda_s)` in 2-norm. The same
  argument then gives `sigma(Lambda)' cap M = prod_U B_n`.

**(b) (CCR) iff `lim_U eps_n = 0`.**
- (`<=`) is immediate from (a).
- (`=>`): if `lim_U eps_n = delta > 0`, choose contractions `x_n in B_n` with
  `||x_n - E_n x_n||_2 >= delta/2`. Suppose `(x_n)` lay in `prod_U A_n`, i.e. were
  `2`-close to contractions `a_n in A_n`. Then `||x_n - E_n x_n||_2 <= ||x_n - a_n||_2 -> 0`,
  a contradiction.

**(c) Flexible transport.** Put `pi~_n(lambda) = sigma_n(t) pi_n(t^(-1) lambda t) sigma_n(t)^*`, a
genuine representation of `Lambda`. Then `||pi~_n(lambda_s) - pi_n(lambda_s)||_2 -> 0`.
- Let `rho = pi_n (+) pi~_n` and `w` the lower-left unit block in `M_(2n')`. The vector
  `w` is almost invariant under `Ad rho`.
- The trace-preserving conditional expectation onto `rho(Lambda)'` is contractive and is
  the orthogonal projection onto the fixed space. It gives `y` with `||y|| <= 1` and
  `||y - w||_2 -> 0`.
- The corner `x = e_22 y e_11` intertwines, `pi~ x = x pi`, with `||x|| <= 1` and
  `||x - 1||_2 -> 0`. Its polar part `v` intertwines, and
  `tau(1 - v^* v) <= tau(1 - x^* x) -> 0`,
  `||v - 1||_2 <= ||1 - x^* x||_1^(1/2) + ||x - 1||_2 -> 0`.

**(d) Multiplicative transport (H3 from H1).**
- The aspect ratio of `pi~_n` as a representation of `Lambda` is `sigma_n(t) zeta_A sigma_n(t)^*`.
- `v` restricts `pi_n|Lambda` to `v^* v`, where type `j` has multiplicity `r_j <= m'_j`. The
  restricted aspect ratio `zeta_sub = sum_j (r_j/e_j) w_j v^* v` commutes with `zeta_B` and
  equals `v^* (pi~ restricted) v`.
- Types with `r_j < m'_j/(1+c)` carry mass at most `((1+c)/c) tau(1 - v^* v) -> 0`.
- Write `F_a(x) = x/(x+a)`. Its log-derivative is at most `1/4`, so
  `|F_a(lambda x) - F_a(x)| <= (1/4) log lambda`.
- Combining with `||v^* y v - y||_2 <= 2 ||v - 1||_2` for contractions `y`, uniformly in
  `a > 0`,

```text
|| F_a(zeta_B) - sigma_n(t) F_a(zeta_A) sigma_n(t)^* ||_2  ->  0   along U.
```

This is the multiplicative, all-scales form of (H3), obtained from (H1) alone.

## 3. Excess lemma (proof of node 2)

Let `pi` be any unitary representation of a group `Gamma` on `C^n`, and `Lambda <= Gamma` any
subgroup. With the notation of section 1 and `0 < c < 1`:

```text
sup_(x in ball B) ||x - E_A x||_2  <=  4 tau(zeta_B >= (1+c) zeta_A)^(1/2) + 2c.
```

**Proof.**
- **Pieces.** Split `C^n` into `H_ij = C^(m_i) (x) C^(c_ij) (x) W_j`, of mass `m_i c_ij e_j / n`.
  Both `zeta_A` and `zeta_B` are constant on `H_ij`, with ratio
  `R_ij = m'_j d_i / (m_i e_j) >= c_ij^2`.
- **Low pairs.** Call `(i,j)` low if `R_ij < 1 + c`. Then:
  - `c_ij = 1`;
  - `sum_(i' != i) m_(i') c_(i'j) < c m_i`;
  - `d_i - e_j < c e_j`.
  Low pairs form a partial matching. Two low partners `j` of one `i` would need
  `e_j + e_(j') > d_i`. Two low partners `i` of one `j` would need `m_i + m_(i') > m'_j`.
- **Split the estimate.** Let `P` be the projection onto the low pieces. Since
  `||x - E_A x|| <= 2`,
  `||x - E_A x||_2 <= 4 ||1 - P||_2 + ||P (x - E_A x) P||_2`.
- **The low part.**
  - `x in B` respects the types `j`, and at most one `i` is low for each `j`. So
    `P x P = sum_low x_j^(i,i) (x) 1_(W_j)`.
  - Also `P E_A(x) P = sum_low y_i (x) 1_(W_j)`, with
    `y_i = d_i^(-1) sum_(j') e_(j') Tr_(C^(c_ij'))(x_(j')^(i,i))`.
  - For a low pair the terms with `j' != j` have total weight `d_i - e_j < c d_i`, so
    `||x_j^(i,i) - y_i|| <= 2c`.
  - Hence `||P(x - E_A x)P||_2^2 <= n^(-1) sum_low m_i e_j (2c)^2 <= 4 c^2`. QED

Every jump factor `1 + c` therefore costs at most `4 * (its mass)^(1/2)` of excess.
Conversely, excess of 2-norm `delta` forces jump mass at least `((delta - 2c)/4)^2`.

## 4. Assembly (H1) + (H2') implies (CCR)

**(H2')** is (H2) of the conditional, restated at a generating set
`S = F_Gamma cup {t_1^(+-1), ..., t_k^(+-1)}` whose `t_i` are all strict compressors. For the
Theorem E pairs this is available: `G` is generated by `Gamma` and positive-entry
compressors. It asks for:
- partitions of unity `{q_(n,A)}` with `sum_A ||[sigma_n(s), q_A]||_2^2 -> 0` for `s in S`;
- a block Poincare gap `kappa'` for the compressed tuple, in the sense of
  `median-poincare-concentration`;
- `sum_A ||[q_A, F_a(zeta_A)]||_2^2 -> 0`, uniformly in `a`.

Steps:
- **S1.** By section 2(b), it suffices that `eps_n -> 0`.
- **S2.** Transport: section 2(d), once for each `t_i`.
- **S3.** One-sidedness: `zeta_(B_i) >= zeta_A`.
- **S4.** Build the median observable.
  - Let `m_A` be the `tau_(q_A)`-median of `zeta_A`, and `f = sum_A q_A F_(m_A)(zeta_A)`.
  - For `s in F_Gamma`: `f` almost commutes with `pi_n(s)`, hence with `sigma_n(s)`.
  - For `t_i`: `sigma_n(t_i) f sigma_n(t_i)^* ~ f_i := sum_A q_A F_(m_A)(zeta_(B_i))`, which is
    `>= f`. Trace conservation gives `tau(f_i - f) -> 0`, so `||sigma(t_i) f sigma(t_i)^* - f||_2 -> 0`.
  - *Not written to established standard:* the mass-weighted estimate converting the
    block-wise errors into the global 2-norm errors above. It is the same bookkeeping as in
    `tracial-median-from-block-decomposition`.
- **S5.** `median-poincare-concentration` on each block pins `f` near `1/2` on most mass, and
  `f_i` likewise. So `zeta_(B_i)/zeta_A -> 1` in measure, for each `i`.
- **S6.** The excess lemma gives `eps_n <= 4 tau(zeta_B >= (1+c) zeta_A)^(1/2) + 2c -> 2c`, for
  every `c`. So (CCR) holds.

**Consistency check.** Property (T) of `Gamma` alone gives S1-S3 but not S5. The one-compressor
sofic wreath carries a rigid defect with only `Gamma` Kazhdan. So the gap of the actor must
enter, and it enters only at S5.

## 5. Cascades

- **Cascades refute (H2').** Suppose (H1) holds and a cascade occurs: jump mass
  `tau(zeta_B >= 2 zeta_A) >= c_0` along `U`. By section 3, `eps_n >= ...` stays bounded below,
  so (CCR) fails, and by section 4, (H2') fails. Cascades are counterexamples to (H2'), not
  to the assembly.
- **What a cascade needs.**
  - A level observable `h = level/L` commutes with `pi_n(Gamma)` and satisfies
    `||[sigma_n(t), h]||_2 = O(1/L)`, while its variance is of order one. So the approximate
    commutant of `sigma_n(G)` contains slowly varying non-scalar observables that the
    compressors shift.
  - That is a diffuse, ungapped regime for the actor.
  - Genuine `G`-models cannot do this (`ccr-holds-for-all-genuine-fd-coordinate-models`). A
    cascade needs Hilbert-hotel surgery on boundary levels of mass `O(1/L)`, compatible
    with the relations of `G`.
- **Status.** No such model is known for a Theorem E actor. No numerics were run, because the
  candidate model is not yet defined.
- **Hypotheses that would give (H2').** A coordinate description of commutants of Kazhdan
  representations into tracial matrix ultraproducts, i.e. a positive answer to Alekseev--Thom
  OP 6.2 for `G`, points toward gapped atoms. This lane did not derive (H2') from it.

## 6. Additive transport is vacuous

- **Example.** Let `pi` be irreducible of dimension `n`, restricting to `Lambda` as two
  inequivalent halves. Then `A = C`, `B = C (+) C`, `zeta_A = 1/n` and `zeta_B = 2/n`.
- **Additive measure.** `||zeta_B - zeta_A||_1 = 1/n -> 0`.
- **Excess.** The excess is `1/2`, attained by `z_1 - z_2`, and the jump mass is `1`.
- **Consequence.** Additive `L^1` transport of `zeta` sees nothing at large irreducible
  dimension. The uniform transform `F_a` of section 2(d) is the correct form, and it is
  automatic.
