# A direct perfect-input shell route, 2026-09-08

The conjecture remains open. The reduction here arranges that the
shell envelope is simple before asking for its finite presentation.

## A perfect envelope that preserves the word problem

Starting with a finitely presented decidable P, form M(P) from
P times P by adding s,d identifying the first factor with the
second and with the diagonal. The base embeds by Britton's lemma.
Membership in the associated subgroups is decided by testing the
two coordinates for identity or equality, so the multiple-HNN
word problem is decidable.

The base dies in M(P)_ab, leaving Z^2 generated by s,d. Killing
the base retracts M(P) onto the free subgroup <s,d>; this also
makes membership in that subgroup decidable with explicit coordinates.

Inside K=A_5 * A_5 choose a of order two from the first factor and
b of order three from the second. The kernel of
<a,b>=C_2*C_3 -> C_2 times C_3 is free on
u=a*b*a*b^-1 and v=a*b^-1*a*b. The proof gives its Schreier
basis and a membership algorithm inside K. Amalgamate M(P) with
K by s=u,d=v. The resulting W(P) is infinite, finitely presented,
perfect, and contains P. Its word problem is decided by amalgam
reduction using the two established edge-membership procedures.

This is a uniform construction given a finite presentation and a
word-problem algorithm for P. The membership checks are essential:
decidability of the two vertex groups alone would not justify the
word-problem assertion for an arbitrary amalgam.

## The shell envelope is already simple

For every input and enumeration, the shell realization has the same
Cantor orbits as V. The finite-germ simplicity theorem makes its
derived subgroup simple. Since V is perfect and E_nu is generated
by V and hat(P), the abelianization of E_nu is a quotient of P_ab.
Thus perfect inputs give simple E_nu for every enumeration.

After Clapham and W(P), every Boone--Higman input embeds in such
an infinite perfect finitely presented decidable P. The new route
`boone-higman-via-perfect-shell-envelopes` therefore only asks for
an enumeration making the already simple E_nu finitely presented.
The further twisted Brin--Thompson step is unnecessary in this route.

## What remains open

`perfect-decidable-inputs-have-fp-shell-envelopes` is an OPEN
presentation problem. The perfect wrapper does not supply relations
for its shell action. The earlier extraction theorem shows every
successful enumeration is computable, but does not find one.
The earlier germ tests remain necessary for the stronger A_1
presentation condition, not known necessary for this direct route.

In particular, simplicity does not let us repair a poorly chosen
E_nu by taking a proper quotient: its only proper quotient is
trivial. An uncomputable enumeration gives a simple shell group
with no recursive presentation, by the extraction theorem. A
computable enumeration alone has not been shown sufficient.

The finite-ray positive control cannot itself handle these perfect
inputs. Indeed, an infinite virtually cyclic group has a normal
infinite cyclic subgroup <t> of finite index d. If the group were
perfect, its conjugation homomorphism to Aut(<t>)=C_2 would be
trivial, making t central. For coset representatives r_i, write
g*r_i=t^(c_i(g))*r_(sigma_g(i)). Centrality makes sum_i c_i(g)
a homomorphism to Z, and its value at t is d. This contradicts
perfectness. Thus an infinite perfect input is not virtually cyclic,
and the earlier prefix-core classification makes that core trivial
for every enumeration. This excludes that particular sufficient
finiteness argument, not finite presentation of E_nu itself.
