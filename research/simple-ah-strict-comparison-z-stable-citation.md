**Established by citation.** Andrew S. Toms,
[*Characterizing classifiable AH algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0932),
arXiv:1102.0932, which closes a gap in Theorem 1.2 of arXiv:0910.2061.
Abstract verbatim: "We observe almost divisibility for the original Cuntz
semigroup of a simple AH algebra with strict comparison. As a consequence,
the properties of strict comparison, finite nuclear dimension, and
Z-stability are equivalent for such algebras".

The chain behind the equivalence is the one written out in
`toms-gamma-counterexample-strict-comparison-proof`:

1. Strict comparison plus almost divisibility is pureness.
2. AH algebras have locally finite nuclear dimension.
3. Winter (Invent. Math. 2012) upgrades pureness plus locally finite nuclear
   dimension to Z-stability.

The journal reference of Toms's note was not verified in this pass; the
arXiv statement is what is imported.
