Let `(A_i)_(i=1)^42` be projections in a finite tracial algebra with

```text
tau(A_i)=alpha
```

for every `i`.  Let `(E_i)_(i=1)^42` be a partial PVM, so the `E_i` are
pairwise orthogonal and

```text
s=sum_i tau(E_i)<=1.                                   (DRM1)
```

Then

```text
sum_i||E_i-A_i||_2^2
 =s+42 alpha-2 sum_i tau(E_iA_i)
 >=42 alpha-s
 >=42 alpha-1.                                         (DRM2)
```

The only input is `tau(E_iA_i)<=tau(E_i)`.

For the native balanced lamp projections, `alpha=1/2`, so

```text
sum_i||E_i-A_i||_2^2>=20.                              (DRM3)
```

For any fixed positive joint atom of two independent balanced lamps,
`alpha=1/4`, so

```text
sum_i||E_i-A_i||_2^2>=19/2.                            (DRM4)
```

The same lower bounds hold asymptotically when canonical trace tests make
the `42` raw traces `alpha+o(1)`.

Consequently the decoded-to-raw proximity target `(RDI10)` in
`raw-denominator-incidence-kills-off-diagonal-shell-orbit` is impossible for
the native raw lamp orbit.  Its raw section-word estimate remains correct,
but it compares two parameterizations of the same family of balanced
projections; it does not authenticate a partial 42-atom PVM.

There is also an exact spectator realization of the mismatch.  Put the raw
lamp algebra on one tensor factor and any exact covariant 42-atom shell PVM
on another.  Let lattice/root words act on the packet factor and shell
permutations act covariantly on the shell factor.  All internal mixed-root
and first-exit energies vanish while `(DRM2)` persists.  This is the fixed
capacity version of the tensor firewall already visible in the arithmetic
hexagon lane.

The viable incidence target must therefore be nonlinear: decoded shell
atoms must be recovered as mutually orthogonal functions or subprojections
of the JOINT algebra generated by the 42 commuting raw lamp coordinates,
not be close to the individual half projections themselves.  Equivalently,
one needs a cylinder/character decoder (or a global spectral PVM) whose
covariance is tied to raw denominator words.  First-exit mass can control
loss of those joint atoms, but cannot overcome `(DRM2)` for pointwise raw
proximity.

This obstruction concerns the congruence-correctable chart program.  It is
independent of the uniformly noncorrectable lattice sector.
