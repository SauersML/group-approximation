Let `K <= SL_3(Z)` be finitely generated. Suppose `N ⊲ K` is infinite,
finitely generated, of infinite index, and not virtually solvable. Then:

- `K` and `N` are both Zariski-dense in `SL_3`;
- `[SL_3(Z) : K]` is infinite.

So the free-by-free and surface-by-free incoherence mechanisms, and any
fibration of a subgroup over a free group with finitely generated fibre, can
live in `SL_3(Z)` only inside a Zariski-dense thin subgroup whose fibre is
itself Zariski-dense.
