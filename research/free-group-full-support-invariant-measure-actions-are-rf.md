**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `F_r` (`r ∈ N ∪ {∞}`) act continuously on a Cantor space `X` preserving a
Borel probability measure of full support. Then the action is residually finite in the sense of Kerr–Nowak.

No novelty is claimed: this is the full-support form of Kerr–Nowak Theorem 5.2 (minimal case). It removes the
dependence on the unread primary source for every use in the UN program, and it is the base case of
`virtually-free-group-measure-actions-are-rf`.
