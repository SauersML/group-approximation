**Setting.**
* `R_mu` on `(X, mu)` is the Manzoor relation of `non-ce-relation-is-not-treeable` item 3. `L(R_mu)` is not Connes
  embeddable.
* `R_T = S` is the property-(T) envelope of `non-ce-property-t-equivalence-relation-exists`, built by Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana
  (arXiv 2205.07442, Theorem D and its proof) from `R_mu`. Concretely:
  - `P ⊇ R_mu` is an ergodic pmp relation on `X`, and `T` is an ergodic hyperfinite relation on `(Z, nu)`;
  - `pi : A = F_3 -> [P × T]` is a homomorphism with `{u_pi(g)}'' = L(P × T)`;
  - `G ∈ WR(A, B)` is a property-(T) group with `B` icc hyperbolic, written `G = A^(B) ⋊_(alpha,v) B` with
    `alpha_b = Ad(rho_b) sigma_b` and `v_(b,c) = rho_b sigma_b(rho_c) rho_(bc)^(-1)`, where `rho : B -> A^B`;
  - `S` on `((X × Z)^B, (mu × nu)^B)` is the smallest equivalence relation containing `(P × T)^(B)` and the graphs of
    `tau_b = kappa(pi^B(rho_b)) lambda_b`.
* *Free host* is as in `nonce-relation-hosts-force-root-or-q1-failure`: an essentially free pmp action
  `Lambda ↷ (W, zeta)` with `zeta` sigma-finite, a set `A_W` with `0 < zeta(A_W) < infinity`, a subrelation
  `S' ⊆ R_Lambda(W)|A_W`, and a map `r : A_W -> X` pushing normalized `zeta` to `mu` and bijective on a.e. class.
  The host is *finite* if `zeta(W) < infinity`.

**Statement.**
1. **(Restriction.)** Let `R_0 ⊆ R` be pmp relations on `(X, mu)`. Every free host of `R` restricts to a free host
   of `R_0` with the same action `Lambda ↷ (W, zeta)` and the same set `A_W`. If `R = R_1 × Delta_Y`, then composing
   `r` with the projection to the first factor turns a free host of `R` into one of `R_1`.
2. **(The R_T branch implies the R_mu branch.)** `S ⊇ R_mu × Delta_Y` with `Y = Z × (X × Z)^(B \ {e})`, where the
   `e`-coordinate `X × Z` carries the first factor. So every free host of `R_T`, finite or infinite, with actor
   `Lambda`, yields a free host of `R_mu` of the same kind with actor `Lambda`. Consequently:
   - the disjunction in `nonce-relation-admits-free-host` is equivalent to its `R_mu` disjunct alone: `R_mu` has a
     finite free host;
   - the set of actors that host `R_T` is contained in the set of actors that host `R_mu`. Any obstruction proved for
     `R_mu` holds for `R_T`, but not conversely. In particular the poly-Haagerup exclusion of
     `kazhdan-relations-have-no-poly-haagerup-host` removes no case of the target claim.
3. **(The envelope is a Kazhdan orbit relation.)** Let `j : G -> A^B ⋊_sigma B` be `j(x, b) = x rho_b b`, and let
   `A^B ⋊_sigma B` act on `(X × Z)^B` by `Phi(x b) = kappa(pi^B(x)) lambda_b`. Then `j` is an injective homomorphism,
   `Phi ∘ j` is a pmp action of the property-(T) group `G`, and its orbit relation is exactly `S = R_T`.
4. **(Its stabilizers are infinite.)** For a.e. `xi`, `Stab_G(xi) = ⊕_(c ∈ B) Stab_pi(xi_c)` inside `A^(B)`.
   Infinitely many summands are nontrivial, so `Stab_G(xi)` contains `Z^(N)` and is not finitely generated. The
   native Kazhdan generator of `R_T` is therefore neither essentially free nor finite-stabilizer. Item 3 of the
   hosting claim (a finite-stabilizer generator gives a non-CE crossed-product corner) does not apply to it.

Derivation: `property-t-envelope-hosts-restrict-to-manzoor-hosts-proof`.

**What is not claimed.**
* No converse. Item 2 does not say a host of `R_mu` gives a host of `R_T`. The natural lift of a host of `R_mu`
  through the CDI construction must lift `tau_b`, whose coordinate part `pi^B(rho_b)` has infinite support for some
  `b`. By `cdi-wreath-tail-cocycle-is-intrinsically-nonsplit`, no change of section avoids this. The lifted maps then
  lie in the full group of the unrestricted product of the host action, and the group generated by them has no reason
  to act essentially freely. Whether `R_mu` has a free host with a normally poly-Haagerup actor is OPEN.
* Nothing is claimed about other property-(T) relations that do not contain `R_mu × Delta`.
