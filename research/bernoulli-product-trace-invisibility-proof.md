Sections 1–4 of the artifact give a complete direct proof. Represent
a K-class by a difference of actual projections using the projection
approximate unit. Approximate by configuration-local self-adjoint
operators within 1/8. A one-site change is a uniformly trace-norm
bounded finite block. The relative spectral trace is continuous by
the resolvent identity and strong-* convergence against a fixed block.
It vanishes on dense finite configurations for a rank-invisible class.

Interpolate ν_θ=(1−θ)δ_0+θν. Polynomial differentiation is a finite
coordinate sum, converted by covariance into the full one-site trace.
The explicit binomial polynomials h_N in Section 4 converge on two
complex spectral contours, which controls the relative differences in
trace norm uniformly. Thus the differentiated identity passes to
spectral projections, its derivative is zero, and its initial trace
at δ_0^G is zero. There is no unproved exchange of an infinite sum
and a derivative, no growth assumption, and no assembly hypothesis.

This is the supplied report's deduction, with a written argument, not
an assertion of independent referee review or historical priority.
