**ESTABLISHED.** The binary Leavitt unit group is not sofic
(`openai-leavitt-unit-nonsofic`), so the implication "every group is sofic,
hence the conjecture holds by Elek--Szabó" has a false premise. The
conjecture stays open on nonsofic groups, and every counterexample must be one.
