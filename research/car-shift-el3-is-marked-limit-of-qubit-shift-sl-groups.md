**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `R = B ⋊_σ Z` with `B = ⊗_Z M_2(F_2)` (see `car-bernoulli-crossed-product-is-lamplighter-ring`).

- `R` is generated by `e_12^{(0)}, e_21^{(0)}` (site 0) and `s^{±1}`. So `G = EL_3(R)` is finitely generated by the
  `e_ij(g)` with `g` among these four, and it has property (T).
- Let `V_N = (F_2^2)^{⊗ Z/N}` and let `π_N` cyclically permute the tensor factors. Let
  `ρ_N: F = F_2⟨t_1,t_2,t_+,t_−⟩ → End(V_N)` send `t_1, t_2` to `e_12, e_21` acting on factor 0, and `t_±` to `π_N^{±1}`.
  Then `ρ_N` is onto `M_{2^N}(F_2)`.
- Let `π: F → R` be the analogous surjection. A word of length `L` in the generators `e_ij(t)` of `EL_3(F)` is trivial in
  `G` if and only if it is trivial in `EL_3(M_{2^N}(F_2)) = SL_{3·2^N}(F_2)`, for every `N > 8L+4`.

Consequences:
- `G` is the limit, in the space of marked groups, of the finite simple groups `SL_{3·2^N}(F_2)`, so it is LEF, sofic and
  hyperlinear.
- Their Cayley graphs, with respect to the images of the fixed generating set, form an expander family, since `EL_3(F)`
  has (T) (Margulis; Kassabov for quotients of universal lattices).

Credit: EJZ for (T). The idea of approximating the tensor shift by cyclic permutations of the factors of a finite tensor
power is folklore-level; the novelty check was a grep of main only.
