Lane proof. Notation as in the claim. A point `z = Σ z_i p^i` of `Z_p` is the path
`z_0 z_1 ...`. For a vertex `v` of level `n`, read as an integer `0 <= v < p^n`, put
`ρ_v(w) = v + p^n w`. For `h in Aut(T_p)`, `h(ρ_v(w)) = ρ_(h(v))(h|_v(w))`.

**Möbius rigidity.** Two elements of `PGL_2(Q_p)` that agree on three points are
equal. So a piece of `h` on a nonempty open set is unique.

**Item 1.**
- Adjoin the inverses: `h_i^-1` is locally Möbius on `h_i(U_(h_i))`, dense
  open, with pieces the inverses of pieces of `h_i`, which lie in `Δ`.
- For each word `w` in the `h_i^(±1)` and each letter `s`, the set `w^-1(U_s)` is
  dense open, since `w` is a homeomorphism. The countable intersection `Z*` of
  all of them is dense, by Baire's theorem on the compact space `Z_p`.
- Let `z in Z*` and `γ = s_1 ⋯ s_n` a word. Every intermediate point
  `s_(j+1) ⋯ s_n(z)` lies in `U_(s_j)`. If `f` agrees with `m` near `g(x)` and
  `g` agrees with `m'` near `x`, then `f g` agrees with `m m'` near `x`. So `γ`
  agrees near `z` with a product `δ` of pieces, and `δ in Δ`.
- If `γ` agrees with `m` on an open `V`, pick `z in V ∩ Z*`. Then `m` and `δ`
  agree near `z`, so `m = δ in Δ` by rigidity.

**Item 2.**
- *Pieces are affine.* Let `h` agree with `m` on the cylinder of `v`, of level
  `n`. Then the state `s = h|_v` equals `m_1 = ρ_(h(v))^-1 m ρ_v` on all of `Z_p`.
  - Suppose `m_1(w) = (a w + b)/(c w + d)` with `c != 0`. Its pole
    `P = -d/c in P^1(Q_p)` is not in `Z_p`, since `m_1` is finite on `Z_p`. Also
    `P != ∞`.
  - The states `s|_(0^k)` agree on `Z_p` with `ρ_(r_k)^-1 m_1 (w -> p^k w)` for
    suitable integers `r_k`, whose poles are `P/p^k`. These are pairwise distinct
    Möbius maps, so by rigidity the states `s|_(0^k)` are pairwise distinct.
  - States of states are states, so this contradicts finiteness of the state
    set. So `c = 0`, `m_1` is affine,
    and so is `m`. This also proves the last bullet of item 2.
- *Rational coefficients.* Let `s(w) = a w + b'` be an affine state of `h`.
  - The states `s|_(0^k)` are `w -> a w + (b' - [b']_k)/p^k`, where `[b']_k` is
    the truncation. Finitely many of them means finitely many shifted tails of
    the digit expansion of `b'`. So the expansion is eventually periodic, and
    `b' in Q`.
  - The state at the level-one vertex `1` is `w -> a w + (a + b' - [a + b']_1)/p`.
    The same argument makes `a + b'` rational, so `a in Q`. Also `a in Z_p^x`,
    since `s` is an isometry.
- *One Z[1/N].* `h` has finitely many states, so the pairs `(a, b')` of its
  affine states form a finite set. Let `N` be the product of the numerators of
  the `a`'s and the denominators of the `a`'s and `b'`'s.
  - A piece on the cylinder of `v` is
    `m(z) = ρ_(h(v)) m_1 ρ_v^-1 (z) = a z + (h(v) - a v + p^n b')`, where `v` and
    `h(v)` are integers.
  - So `m in Aff(Z[1/N]) = {z -> a z + b : a in Z[1/N]^x, b in Z[1/N]}`. This group
    is generated by `z -> z+1` and the `z -> ±r z` for the primes `r | N`.

**Item 3.**
- `Γ_0` lies in `PGL_2(Z[1/N_0])` for some `N_0`, and each of its elements is its
  own piece on all of `Z_p`.
- By item 2, the pieces of the `h_i` lie in `Aff(Z[1/N_i])`. Item 1 applies with
  `Δ = PGL_2(Z[1/N])`, `N = N_0 N_1 ⋯ N_k`. Here `U_γ = Z_p` for `γ in Γ_0`.
- `d_q = [[q,0],[0,1]]`. If `λ d_q in GL_2(Z[1/N])` with `q ∤ N`, then
  `v_q(λ) >= 0`, and `v_q(det) = 2 v_q(λ) + 1` is odd, so the determinant is not a
  unit. Contradiction.
- `t_(1/q) = [[1,1/q],[0,1]]`. Here the determinant `λ^2` must be a unit, so
  `v_q(λ) = 0`. Then the entry `λ/q` has `v_q = -1`. Contradiction when `q ∤ N`.
- `Γ_p` contains every `d_q`, and `Z_(p)` contains every `1/q`, `q != p`. So
  neither lies in `Λ`.
