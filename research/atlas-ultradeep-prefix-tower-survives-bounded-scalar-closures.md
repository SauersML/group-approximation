**ESTABLISHED ATLAS-SPECIFIC NO-GO.**  On the residual marked face of
`atlas-ef-dual-rows-create-the-prefix-depth-tower`, none of the following
four depth-free closures can eliminate the ultra-deep enemy:

1. a fixed finite label or selector packet, including an exact left-regular
   `A8` margin;
2. finitely many scalar commutator-phase rows read by a bounded-average
   prefix transcript;
3. a strict Renyi-2 address-rate or soft-covering inequality; or
4. any fixed chain generated by diagonal Whitehead compression, the branch
   swap, and multiplication.

More precisely, write `N=2^n` for the number of dual-prefix Heisenberg pairs
at prefix depth `n`.  The Schrödinger representation of

```text
H(F_2^N)
```

has dimension `2^N`, sends the common marked center to `-I`, and restricts
compatibly when coordinates are enlarged.  Every polarized character atom
has normalized trace

```text
2^(-N)=2^(-2^n).                                      (AUP1)
```

Tensoring by `Reg(A8)` gives the exact canonical Atlas finite-factor margin
and does not change `(AUP1)`.  A fixed menu of `C` label/tag atoms therefore
captures at most

```text
C 2^(-2^n)                                             (AUP2)
```

of the marked carrier.  At a larger coordinate level `M>N`, the complete
depth-`n` Heisenberg table still has relative commutant

```text
I_(2^N) tensor U(2^(M-N)),                             (AUP3)
```

also unchanged by the regular-A8 tensor factor.  Thus exact regular finite
margins do not select a positive-density polarized atom and do not identify
the multiplicity reservoir between levels.

The stronger self-copy test also survives.  For every prescribed finite
window, coordinate permutations implement both binary branch embeddings
exactly in a sufficiently large Schrödinger model, preserve the common
marked center, and include every transverse Heisenberg shear in that
window.  Every finite literal positive-root Atlas fragment, including the
range-sum fold and synchronized coefficient occurrences, has in addition an
exact marked left-regular model.  Hence adjoining more bounded E/F return
rows inside the positive unipotent radical cannot create a normalized-HS
floor.

The other two scalar closure proposals are sharp no-gos rather than missing
estimates.  Finitely many bounded-average paired prefix phase tables have
dimension-independent approximate rank and polynomial-size marked Weyl
models.  The dual-prefix address alphabet also saturates, rather than
exceeds, the Renyi-2 information rate.  Neither mechanism can turn the rare
atoms `(AUP1)` into one common positive-density reservoir.

Finally, the canonical bounded non-positive completion does not repair the
problem.  Whitehead compression followed by the branch swap and
multiplication has an exact finite rectangular model in which

```text
M_D tensor I_m     becomes     M_(2D) tensor I_(m/2)  (AUP4)
```

with zero typed defect.  Iterating any fixed number of those cells only
repeats the label/multiplicity exchange.  Therefore the known opposite-root
word is not by itself a same-reservoir return.

The conclusion is deliberately narrower than a model of the full `4,648`
word Atlas packet.  These coherent models do **not** show that all mixed
Atlas words can be satisfied simultaneously.  They show exactly where a
full-packet proof must depart from the exhausted tower language: it must use
one genuinely non-scalar mixed row whose finite-matrix decoding couples the
coarse and refined packet on the same multiplicity reservoir.  By
`one-coarse-to-fine-prefix-isometry-closes-the-reservoir`, it is enough to
authenticate the single coarse-to-fine selector covariance for the
three-elementary partial-swap word.  No further finite-depth divisibility,
fixed tag, phase-table, entropy, or diagonal-copy argument can substitute
for that authentication.
