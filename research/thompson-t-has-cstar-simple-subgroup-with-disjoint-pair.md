**OPEN.** There is a subgroup `H ≤ T` with nontrivial `a, b ∈ H`, `supp(a) ∩ supp(b) = ∅` on
the circle, such that `C*_r(H)` is simple.

By `thompson-t-disjoint-pair-cstar-simple-subgroup-criterion` this implies that `F` is
non-amenable (`thompson-f-not-amenable-from-cstar-simple-disjoint-pair`). Conversely, if `T` is
C*-simple, `T` itself is a witness (`thompson-t-disjoint-pair-subgroup-from-t-cstar-simple`).
So the claim is equivalent to non-amenability of `F`. Its interest is that a witness can be a
small explicit subgroup with a combinatorial proof of C*-simplicity.

## Attempts

- **`H = T`, or micro-supported minimal subgroups.** C*-simplicity of such `H` is governed by
  rigid stabilizers (Le Boudec–Matte Bon, arXiv:1605.01651, Corollary 1.3, Theorem 1.6), and
  in `T` these are built from copies of `F`. Circular.
- **Free products `A * B` (`B ≠ 1`) with the disjoint pair in `A`.** These are C*-simple
  (Paschke–Salinas; not re-read), so an embedding would decide the problem. Constraints:
  - Classical ping-pong needs a region moved by every nontrivial element of `A`, which would
    lie in `supp(a) ∩ supp(b) = ∅`, so it dies.
  - By `free-product-disjoint-pair-no-support-separating-element`, no `w ∈ H \ A` may carry
    the support of one element of `A \ {1}` off the support of another. So `H` has no
    north–south dynamics between the supports.
  - For `h = βa` with `β ∈ B \ {1}` and `k` with `h^k` having fixed points (elements of `T`
    have rational rotation number, Ghys–Sergiescu; not re-read), no element of `A \ {1}` can
    have support compactly inside `S^1 \ Fix(h^k)`, and the supports of `a, b` must interleave
    near the attracting and repelling ends in both directions.

  Status: not excluded, no construction. The most concrete target is `(Z^2) * Z ↪ T` with the
  `Z^2` generated by a disjoint pair.
- **Acylindrically hyperbolic subgroups with trivial amenable radical** (C*-simple by
  Dahmani–Guirardel–Osin; not re-read). No candidate containing a disjoint pair is known.
- **2026-09-16 update: the free-product and acylindrically hyperbolic branches are closed.**
  A disjoint pair generates `Z^2`, and `Z^2 ∗ Z` does not embed in `V`
  (`z2-free-z-does-not-embed-in-thompson-v`). A `Z^2` inside a nontrivial free product
  yields `Z^2 ∗ Z` by Kurosh (`thompson-t-free-product-subgroups-have-no-disjoint-pair`),
  so the target `(Z^2) * Z ↪ T` above does not exist. A `Z^2` inside an acylindrically
  hyperbolic group is elliptic and becomes a free factor with a loxodromic
  (`ah-elliptic-torsion-free-subgroup-is-free-factor`,
  `thompson-v-acylindrically-hyperbolic-subgroups-lack-z2`). Also every witness `H` has
  every point stabilizer non-amenable, so `H ∩ F` is non-amenable, and no witness is
  linear over a field (Haagerup–Olesen mechanism plus Brin–Squier and the Tits
  alternative). Remaining open: non-acylindrical strongly faithful tree actions and
  C*-simplicity proofs not based on free-group dynamics. Details:
  `research/artifacts/thompson-t-disjoint-pair-witness-obstructions-2026-09-16.md`.
