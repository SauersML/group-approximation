Let `C` be a pseudounitary modular category over `C`, with its canonical spherical structure, so that
categorical dimensions are positive. Examples are unitary modular tensor categories. Let:
- `C_ad` be the fusion subcategory generated by all `X tensor X*`;
- `C_pt` be the fusion subcategory generated by the invertible objects;
- `s` be the unnormalized S-matrix.

1. **(GN1) Centralizer criterion.** For a fusion subcategory `K`, its Mueger centralizer `K'` is the
   fusion category generated by the simple `X_i` with `s_ij = d_i d_j` for all simple `X_j` in `K`.
2. **(GN2)** `C_ad = (C_pt)'`.
3. **(GN3) Consequence used downstream.** A simple object `a` lies in `C_ad` iff `s_(a,u) = s_(a,0)` for
   every invertible simple `u`.

(GN3) follows from (GN1) and (GN2).
- An invertible `u` has `d_u > 0` and `d_u^2 = d_(u tensor u*) = 1`, so `d_u = 1`.
- `s_(a,0) = d_a`.
- The simple objects of `C_pt` are exactly the invertible simples.
- Mueger's centralizer `K'` is the full subcategory of objects that centralize `K`. Its simple objects
  are therefore exactly the simples `X_i` satisfying the condition of (GN1).
- Hence a simple `a` lies in `(C_pt)'` iff `s_(a,u) = d_a d_u = s_(a,0)` for every invertible `u`.

The condition in (GN3) is homogeneous in `s`. It is also unchanged when one index is dualized, because
`C_ad` and the set of invertibles are both closed under duals. So it holds equally for the S-matrix
conventions of `dln-galois-symmetry-of-modular-twists`.
