**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G ↷ (X,μ)` be a p.m.p. action of a countable group, and let `Γ <= G` act ergodically
on `X`. Let `K` be a compact second countable group, `α : G × X → K` a measurable cocycle, and
`Y = X ×_α K` with `g(x,h) = (gx, α(g,x)h)` and measure `μ × Haar`. Write `B_Λ = L^∞(Y)^Λ` for `Λ <= G`.
Then for every `g ∈ G` with `gΓg^(-1) <= Γ`:

```text
g · B_Γ = B_(gΓg^(-1)) = B_Γ .
```

The same holds for every homogeneous extension `Y_M = X ×_α K/M` (`M <= K` closed). These include all
finite extensions `X ×_α {1,...,k}` (take `K = S_k`). The reason is that `L^∞(Y_M)` is a `G`-invariant
subalgebra of `L^∞(Y)`, and `B_Λ(Y_M) = B_Λ ∩ L^∞(Y_M)`.

**Consequence (class-killing).** Kun--Thom Theorem C says: if `Γ <= G` are Kazhdan, `Γ` is infranormal
(generated, together with `G`, by compressions), and `G ↷ Z` is sofic, then `L^∞(Z)^Γ` is `G`-invariant.
Suppose this is applied to a compact or homogeneous extension `Z` of an action `X` on which `Γ` acts
ergodically. Then its conclusion holds automatically, because `G` is generated by compressions. Such an
application can never yield a contradiction.

For a mixing action `X` of an infinite group, every infinite `Γ` acts ergodically. So **no finite,
isometric or compact-group extension of a mixing action can carry the Kun--Thom obstruction**. This holds
although finite extensions of sofic actions are sofic (the extension algebra sits in `M_k(L^∞(X) ⋊ G)`
with the diagonal preserved), so nonsoficity would descend to `X`. Together with
`kazhdan-fixed-algebra-blind-to-subgroup-ergodic-actions` (factors, restrictions), this closes the
"import the obstruction through an extension that soficity lifts along" design for every extension of
finite or compact type.

**What survives.** An extension whose Γ-fixed algebra is not a homogeneous space of a compact group. The
Kun--Thom generalized Bernoulli coordinate over `G/Γ` is of this kind: the fixed algebra is diffuse, and
the compression acts on it like a one-sided shift. The step that dies is the compact compression lemma:
in a compact group, `zLz^(-1) <= L` forces equality. A surviving design needs a non-compact (for
example relatively weakly mixing) extension along which soficity still lifts.
