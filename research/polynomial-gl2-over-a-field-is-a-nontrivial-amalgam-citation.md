Literature import.

- H. Nagao, *On GL(2, K[x])*, J. Inst. Polytech. Osaka City Univ. Ser. A **10** (1959), 117–121.
- J.-P. Serre, *Trees*, Chapter II, §1.6, which states Nagao's theorem. Chapter I, §4.1 gives the Bass–Serre tree of an
  amalgam and its vertex stabilizers.

**Source status.** The primary paper and the book were not re-read in this lane. A Crossref query from MSI did not
locate a copy of Nagao's paper. The statement recorded here is the textbook form. It is the one the artifact
`leavitt-rank-three-no-inverse-2026-08-18` already quotes and uses, with `GL_2(F_2[X]) = GL_2(F_2) *_(B(F_2)) B(F_2[X])`.
Nontriviality and the stabilizer description are formal consequences of the decomposition.
