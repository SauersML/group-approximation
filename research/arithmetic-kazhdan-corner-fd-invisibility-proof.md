Property `(T)` makes the heat powers converge in maximal norm to the
Kazhdan projection.  Exact finite-dimensional co-density identifies the
lattice-invariant and ambient-invariant subspaces, so the denominator kills
that corner in every finite-dimensional representation.  The quasi-regular
vector of the integral coset is lattice-fixed but is moved by the
denominator, proving that the maximal-C-star element itself is nonzero.
