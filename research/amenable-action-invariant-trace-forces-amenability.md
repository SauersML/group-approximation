Let `G` be a countable discrete group with an amenable action
`α : G ↷ A` on a unital nuclear C\*-algebra preserving a tracial state
`τ`.  Then `G` is amenable.

Consequences for STW Problem XL
(`stw99-problem-xl-amenable-actions-exact-groups`): any amenable action
of a non-amenable exact group on a unital stably finite classifiable
algebra `A` moves every trace, and more: the induced affine action on
the nonempty compact simplex `T(A)` admits NO invariant probability
measure (its barycenter would be an invariant trace).  In particular
targets with finitely many extremal traces are impossible — any
witness for Problem XL needs a trace simplex carrying a
measure-fixed-point-free affine `G`-action, forcing an
infinite-dimensional (in practice Poulsen-type) simplex.
