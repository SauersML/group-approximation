If every self-adjoint `A in M_n(Z[G])` has a spectrum that is a weak limit of
symmetric integer matrix spectra, the limit theorem gives every Serre
inequality. With `Q=x` applied to `B^*B`, that is `det(B) >= 1` for every
integral matrix `B`.

Honest scope: the second prerequisite is open for every nonsofic group and may
be strictly stronger than the conjecture. The route is recorded because it
isolates a one-variable approximation problem that does not involve
approximating `G`.
