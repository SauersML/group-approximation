Murray and von Neumann proved that a `II_1` factor has a unique normalized
quasitrace and proved its additivity, hence it is the unique tracial state.
This is recalled immediately before Problem I in
Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902v2, with references to their
Theorem XIII and subsequent additivity theorem.  Scaling handles arbitrary
bounded 2-quasitraces on `R`.

For the norm warning, the standard matrix union in `R` is countable and its
norm closure is therefore separable.  But `R` contains a diffuse abelian von
Neumann algebra; its characteristic projections contain an uncountable
family at pairwise operator-norm distance one.  Hence `R` is norm
nonseparable and that matrix union cannot be norm dense.  Hyperfiniteness
only asserts strong, equivalently bounded-set 2-norm, density.
