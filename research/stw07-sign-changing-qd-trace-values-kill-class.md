**ESTABLISHED (Theorem B of the artifact).**  Let `A` be separable unital
quasidiagonal and `x in K_0(A)`.  If there are quasidiagonal tracial states
`tau_+`, `tau_-` with `x^(tau_+) > 0 > x^(tau_-)`, then there are
asymptotically multiplicative, asymptotically isometric c.c.p. maps
`phi_n: A -> M_(k_n)` with `(phi_n)_*(x) = 0` for all large `n`.

A faithful model is balanced, with `n`-dependent multiplicities, against
models tracking `tau_+` or `tau_-`, whose values are eventually of the
opposite sign.
