title: The monotone subshift has a two-end Laurent quotient with an infinite simple finitary group kernel

Let `X` be the binary subshift forbidding `10`, `R=LC(X,F_2) semidirect Z`,
and `A=F_2[z,z^{-1}]`. Evaluation at the two fixed points gives the
unital ring exact sequence

`0 -> M_fin(Z,F_2) -> R -> A x A -> 0`.

For every `n>=3` the induced group sequence is exactly

`1 -> L -> EL_n(R) -> SL_n(A) x SL_n(A) -> 1`,

where `L=GL_fin(Z x {1,...,n},F_2)` is infinite, simple and locally
finite. The quotient is residually finite and the finite residual of
`EL_n(R)` is exactly `L`. The kernel statement concerns the whole
congruence kernel, not only the subgroup generated by its elementary roots.
