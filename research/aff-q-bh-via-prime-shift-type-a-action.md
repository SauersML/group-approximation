Deduction.
- **The action is faithful, 2-transitive and contains `Aff(Q)`.** This is
  `gl-n-q-lies-in-prime-shift-permutation-group`, items 4 and 5. `A_1 <= Sym(Q)` acts faithfully and
  2-transitively on `Q`, so it has one orbit of two-element subsets, and it contains `Aff(Q)`.
- **So the action is of type (A)** (the four conditions of `type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups`).
  - `A_1` is finitely presented, by the first requirement.
  - Every point stabilizer is finitely generated. They are all conjugate to `Stab_(A_1)(0)`, which is finitely
    generated by the second requirement.
- **Conclusion.** By `type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups`, every subgroup of `A_1` embeds in a
  finitely presented simple group, and `Aff(Q)` in particular.

This is item 5 of `gl-n-q-lies-in-prime-shift-permutation-group`, specialized to `A_1`.
`prime-shift-hnn-groups-are-not-finitely-presented` shows the first requirement forces `Π_1 -> A_1` to be
non-injective. So a positive answer to `prime-shift-hnn-map-to-a1-is-injective` kills this route.
