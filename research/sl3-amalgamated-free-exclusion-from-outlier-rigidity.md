Suppose an amalgamated-free Haar unitary existed in a regular-trace matrix
model of `SL_3(Z[1/2])`.  The established localization theorem
`sl3-amalgamated-free-enemies-are-ambient-extension-outliers` would force
its lattice packet to satisfy the uniform separation `(AOR1)`.  The second
requirement rules out exactly such an outlier-carried unitary.  Therefore no
amalgamated-free HNN letter exists.
