# Eliminate the Magic-Square faces to two central-product dihedral factors

Use the row and first-two-column products to eliminate five entries. The
remaining face commutators give `u=v=u^{-1}` for `u=(ae)^2`, `v=(bd)^2`;
the signed final product gives `J=u`. Collection gives the 32 normal forms in
the claim, and the standard Pauli model shows that they are distinct.
