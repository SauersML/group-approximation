If every finite symmetry-basis window had arbitrarily accurate matrix moment
models, a diagonal ultraproduct would reproduce all basis word moments and
embed the generated von Neumann algebra.  Completeness of the basis makes
that algebra the original non-CE algebra.  Common tensor amplification
preserves every normalized moment exactly.  Projective closure is excluded
by the second requirement, so this finite witness does not carry a group
law for free.
