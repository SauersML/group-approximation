**ESTABLISHED TWISTED-FIBRE COUNTERMODEL.**  Let

```text
G=Z/2 x Z/2
```

and let `alpha` be its nondegenerate order-two multiplier, so the canonical
twisted generators are two anticommuting self-adjoint unitaries.  Then

```text
C^*(G,alpha) ~= M_2,             C^*(G) ~= C^4.          (VTR1)
```

There are ucp maps

```text
i:C^*(G,alpha) -> M_2(C^*(G)),
r:M_2(C^*(G)) -> C^*(G,alpha),
r i=id,                                                    (VTR2)
```

such that `i` is a complete-order embedding, but `r` is nonfaithful and
kills a nonzero positive multiplicative defect of a canonical twisted
generator.  The C-star algebra generated by the range is

```text
C^*(i(C^*(G,alpha))) ~= M_2 direct_sum C,                (VTR3)
```

and `r` restricts to the quotient star-homomorphism erasing the scalar
boundary summand.

Therefore none of the following features, by themselves, forbids the
positive-boundary-ideal escape required by a hypothetical odd Maslov
retract:

1. simplicity of the twisted source fibre;
2. source and target being central-character fibres of one finite central
   extension;
3. residual finite dimensionality, indeed finite dimensionality, of the
   untwisted target;
4. a fixed finite matrix amplification and exact complete-order splitting.

What fails in this example is precisely canonical generator covariance: the
extra untwisted coordinates are state values rather than a projective
coefficient action.  Hence the Maslov-specific survivor must impose some
weaker form of canonical covariance strong enough to see the cocycle but not
already covered by the equivariant Morita no-go.

The proof is `v4-state-summand-twisted-retract-proof`.
