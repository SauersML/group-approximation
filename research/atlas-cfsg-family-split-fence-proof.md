The diagonal maps `(CF8-1)` are faithful because one faithful block is
present, land in `SL_(4m)(2)` because every `4 x 4` invertible binary matrix
has determinant one, and survive projectivization because the binary scalar
center is trivial.  The groups `PSL_(4m)(2)` are simple for `m>=2`.
Therefore the classical branch contains infinitely many exact `A8`
carriers of unbounded rank and multiplicity.  The natural embeddings in
`A_n` give a second infinite branch.  This proves the stated finite-census
fence.
