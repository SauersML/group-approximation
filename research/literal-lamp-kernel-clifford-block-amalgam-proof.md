## Direct proof

Sections 4-5 of the artifact.  Uniqueness of `t tau^n` in `V = T x| Z` and
`tau^n B tau^(-n) = 2^n Z^3 x| SL_3(Z)` give the coset coordinates and the
action formula `(n,[u]) -> (n+m, [u_s + A_s 2^m u])` for `s tau^m`.  Applying
it to the marked ordered pair shows that the neighbours of `(n,[u])` are
exactly `(n,[u + 2^(n-1) eps])` for the seven nonzero `eps in (Z/2)^3`, because
the first columns of `SL_3(Z)` are the primitive vectors of `Z^3` and these
realize every nonzero class mod two.  So each component is a complete graph on
one coset of `2^(n-1)Z^3/2^n Z^3`, blocks at level `n` correspond
equivariantly to sites at level `n-1`, and block stabilizers are telescope
levels.

By the universal description of the literal lamp kernel (the group generated by
site involutions in which all commutators along the marked orbit equal one
central involution), the presentation of `N_E` is the union of the block
presentations with the copies of the centre identified; that is the
presentation of the amalgamated free product over `<w>`.  Each block group maps
onto the Clifford lamp group of its eight sites, of order `2^9`, and is
generated by eight involutions with those relations, so it has order at most
`2^9` and the map is an isomorphism; in particular `w` is nontrivial, as
already known from `prop:witness`.  The two displayed corollaries follow from
the Bass-Serre tree of that star-shaped graph of groups, on which
`E = N_E x| V` acts because `V` fixes `w` and permutes the blocks
transitively, and from `(*_{<z>} P_i)/<z> = *_i (P_i/<z>)`.
