transcribed from the printed prologue of `arXiv:2506.20843v2`, which attributes
the non-residual-finiteness to Deligne (`Deligne`) and Raghunathan
(`Rag_torsion`) on central extensions of `S`-arithmetic groups, and the upgrade
to non-residual-amenability to Margulis' normal subgroup theorem.

repository.  What has been read is Dogon--Vigdorovich's assertion of them for
exactly this `Gamma~`.  If this node ever becomes load-bearing for a printed
claim rather than for lane triage, the two primary sources should be checked
directly.
