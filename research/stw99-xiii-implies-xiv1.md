Let `D` be a finite strongly self-absorbing algebra.  `D` is unital
simple separable nuclear with a unique tracial state, which is
faithful by simplicity.  Under the premise (Problem XIII), `D`
satisfies the UCT, so the Tikuisis--White--Winter quasidiagonality
theorem applies: `D` is quasidiagonal.  The McDuff-type upgrade
recorded in STW's own footnote to Problem XIV converts
quasidiagonality of a strongly self-absorbing algebra into
`Q ≅ Q ⊗ D`: a unital embedding `D → Q_ω` exists by quasidiagonality
plus the unique (hence QD) trace, strong self-absorption moves it into
`Q_ω ∩ Q'`, and the McDuff characterisation finishes.  (Under the
premise's stronger "known list" form the conclusion is immediate:
finite known strongly self-absorbing algebras are `Z` and
UHF-of-infinite-type, all `Q`-absorbed.)
