In the degree-three length-thirteen `(1,5,7)` cyclic template, take the
nonidentity central-palindrome realization of the source holonomy and the
three-syllable realization of the target holonomy.  If the common-carrier
equation is soluble for arbitrary coefficient units, then the two resulting
holonomies are already conjugate inside the coefficient group.

In particular they have equal order.  Thus the proposed choices conjugate to
`ab` and `cd`, of orders two and four, cannot occur.  This conclusion is
independent of coefficient length or any finite search library.
