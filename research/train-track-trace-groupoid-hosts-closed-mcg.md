**OPEN** (lane bh-mcg, 2026-09-18). This is a construction proposal with explicit
gates. No priority is claimed and no literature search on this exact object was
made. Let `S = S_g`, `g ≥ 2`.

## Construction

- **Split moves.** Fix the finitely many `Mod(S)`-orbits of complete (maximal,
  birecurrent) train tracks. A *split move* at a large branch `b` of a track `τ`
  is one of the left, right or central splittings (Penner--Harer). Moves at two
  distinct large branches of `τ` commute: either order gives the same track.
- **Trace space.** Let `Ω_τ` be the space of infinite splitting sequences from `τ`,
  identified modulo the commutation relation. These are infinite traces in the
  partially commutative monoid of moves. The cylinder topology comes from finite
  traces. The space should be a Cantor space once finitely many dead ends are
  pruned.
- **Groupoid.** `𝒢` is generated by two kinds of germs: tail equivalence of traces
  (equal after finite prefixes that reach the same track), and the action of
  `Mod(S)`, `f·(τ, s) = (fτ, fs)`. Take a compact open transversal `Ω = ⊔_i Ω_{τ_i}`
  over orbit representatives. The candidate host is the full group `D = [[𝒢|_Ω]]`.

## Gates (each OPEN)

- **G1, the Serret gate.** For every `f ∈ Mod(S)` and every representative `τ_i`,
  the sets `Ω_{fτ_i}` and `⊔_j Ω_{τ_j}` must be related by finitely many
  cylinder-to-cylinder tail identifications. This is what makes `f` act on `Ω` as a
  homeomorphism in `D`.
  - Known analogue: Agol's theorem that maximal splitting sequences of measured
    train tracks suited to the stable lamination of a pseudo-Anosov are
    eventually periodic and eventually agree (arXiv:1008.1606; recalled, not
    re-read).
- **G2, Cantor regularization.** Laminations that are non-filling or not uniquely
  ergodic, where splitting sequences branch or stop, must be blown up or pruned so
  that `Ω` is a Cantor space and `f` stays continuous. This is the analogue of
  doubling the rationals in the Farey model below.
- **G3, fullness and clopen transitivity.** `D` should be purely infinite: every
  cylinder should split into cylinders at the same track. This is plausible,
  because every track has at least two distinct splittings.
- **G4, finite presentation.** Brown's criterion on a complex of finite split
  traces (a "split forest" complex), modelled on the Stein--Farley complexes of
  `nV`. The higher-rank-graph theorem of X. Li (arXiv:2110.04505), used by
  `odometer-2v-is-fp-simple-and-contains-bs12`, is the closest imported tool. Here
  the rank, the number of large branches, varies from track to track.
- **Faithfulness reduces to equivariance.** Laminations are the limits of infinite
  traces, and G1 and G2 should make the limit map `Ω → PML(S)` equivariant for
  `Mod(S)`. The action of `Mod(S_g)` on `PML` is faithful for `g ≥ 3`. For `g = 2`
  its kernel is the hyperelliptic involution, which is absent from any
  torsion-free finite-index `L` (recalled; not re-read). So an equivariant limit
  map makes the action of `L` on `Ω` faithful.

With G1--G4, `D` satisfies the hypotheses of
`fp-clopen-transitive-full-cantor-groups-have-type-a-actions`, and route
`closed-mcg-cantor-host-via-train-track-traces` gives Boone--Higman for `Mod(S)`.

## Calibration: genus one

- On the torus, a complete track has one large branch with two splittings, so
  traces are ordinary binary sequences. They correspond to continued-fraction
  (Farey) codings of slopes.
- G1 is Serret's theorem: two irrationals are `GL_2(Z)`-equivalent if and only if
  their continued fractions have equal tails (classical; not re-read).
- G2 is the doubling of rational slopes.
- The resulting full group is `V`-like, and it contains `PSL_2(Z)` through
  `PSL_2(Z) ≤ T ≤ V`.
- **Rank one is the right answer here.** `SL_2(Z)` contains no `Z² ∗ Z`, while
  for `g ≥ 2` Koberda's twist powers force `Z² ∗ Z`.

## Why the partially commutative quotient is essential

- Disjoint curves `c_1, c_3` and a curve `c_2` meeting both give `Z² ∗ Z` from
  twist powers. The two commuting twists should act on independent trace
  coordinates, namely splits in disjoint regions.
- **Heuristic, not proved.** A coding where mapping classes act by finite prefix
  replacement on a one-sided shift of finite type would put `L` in that shift's
  full group. If such groups contain no `Z² ∗ Z`, as `V` does not
  (Bleak--Salazar-Díaz), that coding cannot work.
- So the commutation quotient is not a convenience: it is forced.

## Attempts

1. **Reduction of G1 and G2 to local germs** (lane bh-mcg, 2026-09-18; reasoning
   only, not a proof).
   - **Compactness.** `Ω` is compact, and `D` is full. So `f ∈ L` lies in `D`
     exactly when `f` is a groupoid germ near every trace: some prefix of `s`
     reaches a track `ρ` for which `fρ` is, up to comb (slide) equivalence, a split
     descendant of some representative `τ_j`. Finitely many cylinders then follow
     automatically. Slides must therefore be added to the tail equivalence.
     Penner--Harer: a complete track carried by `τ` is obtained from `τ` by splits
     and shifts (recalled, not re-read).
   - **Generic traces.** For a trace converging to a filling, uniquely ergodic
     `λ`, deep tracks should become carried by every track carrying `λ`. That
     would give the local germ.
   - **Partial traces are the decisive case.** Consider a trace that splits only
     inside a subsurface `Y`, with the complementary region frozen. Let `f` be
     supported off `Y`, for example `T_c^n` with `c ⊂ S ∖ Y`. Then the germ needs
     `T_c^n ρ` to be a split-and-slide descendant of finitely many representatives,
     uniformly in `n`.
   - **"Twists are splits."** A Dehn twist about a curve carried by `ρ` in a
     suitable position is a composition of splits in the annulus around `c`. This
     is the higher-genus analogue of the genus-one fact that the parabolic `x ↦ x+1`
     fixing `∞` acts on eventually constant continued-fraction tails by a shift. It
     is recalled from the train-track literature, not verified here.
   - **Status.** The gate now reads: every mapping class supported on the frozen
     complement of a partial trace acts there by finitely many split and slide
     moves. That is the sharpest form of G1 found in this pass.
