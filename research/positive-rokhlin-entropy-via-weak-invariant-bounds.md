**Dead.** A positive base-independent lower bound through a weak-equivalence invariant, applied to
the Bernoulli 2-shift, would give each group a free ergodic action of positive Rokhlin entropy.

Invalidated by `weak-invariants-give-no-bernoulli-rokhlin-lower-bound`. All nontrivial Bernoulli
shifts are weakly equivalent, while their Rokhlin entropy is at most the base entropy, which tends
to `0`. Its prerequisite is refuted by the same claim.
