**OPEN.**

Let `P` be an infinite perfect finitely generated group with solvable word problem, let
`nu : P -> N` be a bijection, and let `E_nu = <V, hat(P)> <= Homeo(C)` be the shell
envelope of `shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`. Let `G_nu` be the germ
groupoid of its action on `C` and `A_nu = A_(F_2)(G_nu)`.

**The claim:** for some `P` and some computable `nu`, `A_nu` is finitely presented as an
`F_2`-algebra.

## What is already known

By `germ-steinberg-algebras-over-v-are-fg-central-simple`:
- `A_nu` is a finitely generated `F_2`-algebra, on `1_([0])` and the images of a finite
  generating set of `E_nu` (which is finitely generated when `P` is);
- `E_nu <= A_nu^x`, so `P <= A_nu^x`;
- `A_nu` is simple with centre `F_2` (the action is minimal because `V <= E_nu`), under
  Hausdorffness of `G_nu` or the hypotheses of `cepss-nonhausdorff-steinberg-simplicity`.

For `P = 1` this is `A = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, which is finitely presented. So the gate is not
vacuous, and the whole content is the cost of the implanted germs.

## Why it is worth separating from the group gate

The group gate of the same construction is finite presentation of `E_nu`, which by
`bhm-singfix-condition-forces-fp-germ-groups` forces the germ group `Q_nu` to be finitely
presented, and by `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate` reduces to a near-shift group
together with a finite-window condition. Attempt 1 of
`steinberg-resolvent-shell-envelope-is-finitely-presented` records that no one-ended input
is known to pass both clauses.

The algebra gate is a different finiteness condition on the same data:
- relators may use the idempotents of `C(C, F_2)`, not only group words, so a group
  relation that holds only "near the singular point" can be written as an equation
  multiplied by an idempotent;
- `A_nu` contains `L_(F_2)(1,2)` as the subalgebra generated by the `V`-part, so the
  relations of `V` are already compressed into the two Leavitt relations;
- no analogue of the SingFix consequence is known: nothing on main forces a finitely
  presented `A_nu` to have a finitely presented germ group.

Whether these differences are real is exactly what is open. A proof that the algebra gate
implies the group gate would collapse the two.

## What it would give

`boone-higman-via-germ-steinberg-algebra-presentation`: with the Leavitt-tensor criterion
`leavitt-tensor-elementary-host-criterion`, a finitely presented central simple `A_nu`
whose tensor `A_nu ⊗ L_(F_2)(1,2)` has finitely normally generated unstable Steinberg
kernel puts the perfect group `P` in a finitely presented simple group. By
`boone-higman-iff-infinite-simple-inputs` that suffices for Boone--Higman.

## Attempts

None yet. The obvious first test is `P = Z`, where `E_nu` is a one-point germ extension of
`V` by a single cofinite-shift-like germ, and the question is whether the germ generator can
be presented over `L_(F_2)(1,2)` by finitely many relations involving the cylinder
idempotents.
