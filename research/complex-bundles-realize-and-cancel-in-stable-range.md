title: Complex bundle K-classes realize and cancel above half the base dimension
---

Let `X` be a finite CW complex of dimension `d` and put
`D=ceil(d/2)`. Every element of `K^0(X)` whose rank is an integer `r>=D`
is represented by a complex vector bundle of rank `r`. If two rank-`r`
complex vector bundles have the same `K^0`-class and `r>=D`, then they are
isomorphic.
