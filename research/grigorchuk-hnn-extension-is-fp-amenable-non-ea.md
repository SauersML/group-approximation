**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** The first Grigorchuk group `𝔊` has a
finitely presented HNN extension `𝔊^` that lies in Grigorchuk's class `SG` of
subexponentially amenable groups, hence is amenable, and is not elementary
amenable.

The extension is built from Lysenok's presentation of `𝔊`: finitely many
relators together with their images under a substitution `σ` that induces an
injective, non-surjective endomorphism of `𝔊`. Adding a stable letter
conjugating each generator to its `σ`-image absorbs the infinite families of
relators. This construction is described in secondary sources; the relators
are not quoted here.
