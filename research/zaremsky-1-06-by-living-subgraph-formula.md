The Σ¹-conjecture gives, for every Artin group `A_Γ` and every character `χ`, the
explicit rule `[χ] ∈ Σ^1(A_Γ)` iff `Liv^χ` is connected and dominant. The rule is
read directly off the labelled graph and the vertex values `χ(v)`, which is what
Problem 1.6 asks for.
