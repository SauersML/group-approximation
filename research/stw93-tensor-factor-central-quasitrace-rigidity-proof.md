Tensoring representatives with `1` preserves norms and norm-centrality, giving
the asserted unital embedding.  The Jiang--Su algebra is simple, nuclear, and
has strict comparison, so Matui--Sato's theorem gives property (SI).  Applying
the standard trace-kernel pullback argument of Matui--Sato and Bosa--Brown--
Sato--Tikuisis--White--Winter shows that `F(𝒵)` has its unique limit
quasitrace.  The restriction of a normalized quasitrace along a unital
*-homomorphism is a normalized quasitrace, hence must equal this one.
