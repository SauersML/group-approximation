**OPEN.** Is there a torsion-free, finitely presented, metabelian group `G` that is self-similar (it has a
faithful state-closed action on a regular rooted tree) and contains `Z wr Z`?

## Source

- Kourovka Notebook, arXiv:1401.0300v46 (1 September 2026), Problem 21.41, posed by A. Dantas and S. Sidki.
  The comment records that `Z wr Z` itself is self-similar (Dantas–Santos–Sidki, J. Algebra 567 (2021)
  564–581). That action is intransitive; Dantas–Sidki, Groups Geom. Dyn. 12 (2018), show that no transitive
  one exists.
- Kochloukova–de Sousa Luiz, arXiv:2509.05798 (2025), Introduction. They state Dantas's question without
  "torsion-free", say they could not find an example, and **conjecture a negative answer**: no finitely
  presented self-similar metabelian group contains `Z wr Z`.

## What is known (all negative)

1. **The standard candidate fails.** Baumslag's group `B = Z[x^{±1}, (1+x)^{-1}] ⋊ Z^2` is torsion-free,
   finitely presented and metabelian, and `⟨a, t⟩ ≅ Z wr Z` in it. It is not self-similar. This is
   Kochloukova–Luiz, Section "Examples", item 2, and main's reviewed node
   `baumslag-char-zero-metabelian-group-is-not-self-similar`.
2. **A class fails.** Kochloukova–Luiz's Main Theorem excludes every finitely presented `A ⋊ Z^s` (`s ≥ 2`) whose
   module `A` is a cyclic, torsion-free domain of Krull dimension 2 with every `A/pA` an infinite domain, and on
   which no nontrivial element of `Q` acts by an algebraic number.
3. **No affine witness.** This is a lane lemma, proved below.
   - `Z wr Z` is linear over no global field.
   - So by `affine-self-similar-hosts-are-exactly-global-field-linear` (ESTABLISHED, not reviewed), no finitely
     generated affine self-similar group over `Z_p` or `F_q[[π]]` contains `Z wr Z`, finitely presented or
     not.

**Lemma.** `Z wr Z` embeds in no `GL_n(E)` with `E` a global field.

*Proof.*
- Suppose `Γ = Z wr Z ≤ GL_n(E)`. Because `Γ` is finitely generated and solvable, the Lie–Kolchin–Mal'cev theorem
  gives a finite-index `Γ_0 ≤ Γ`, a finite extension `E'` of `E`, and a finite set `S` of places such that
  `Γ_0 ≤ T_n(O_S)`, the upper triangular group over the `S`-integers of `E'`.
- `T_n(O_S)` has a finite series with abelian factors. The unipotent factors are copies of `(O_S, +)`, which has
  torsion-free rank `[E':Q]` in characteristic 0 and is torsion in characteristic `p`. The diagonal factor is
  `(O_S^×)^n`, which is finitely generated by the `S`-unit theorem.
- So `T_n(O_S)` has finite torsion-free (Hirsch–Zaitsev) rank, and so does every subgroup.
- But `Γ_0` meets the base `Z^(∞)` of `Γ` in a subgroup of finite index, and that subgroup is free abelian of
  infinite rank. ∎

## Where a witness would have to live

A witness `G`, with `A` its derived subgroup viewed as a module over `Q = G/A`, must satisfy all of the following:
- its module `A` has Krull dimension at least 2, since some element of `A` generates a free `Z[t^{±1}]`-module;
- it must avoid the hypotheses in item 2, for instance through a non-cyclic `A`, a non-domain `A`, or some
  `A/pA` that is finite or not a domain;
- its action is not affine over a complete discrete valuation ring (item 3).

The Dantas–Santos–Sidki action of `Z wr Z` works by decimating along the sublattice `mZ` of the acting group.
For `B` it fails, and the node on `B` explains why: the support of the module is a curve on which
restriction to sublattices is birational, so nothing contracts.

## Lesson for general BH

Self-similarity of a metabelian group is governed by the geometry of its module. Finite presentation
(Bieri–Strebel) forces the module to be tame. In every case checked (item 2 and `B`), tame modules of Krull
dimension 2 are rigid under the twisted module maps that self-similarity needs. So the evidence says the self-similar route to BBMZ 5.3(7)
should not use the finitely presented metabelian group itself as the host once the module has dimension at
least 2. It should embed the group in a larger self-similar group that forgets the module structure, or use
V-type or Brin–Thompson hosts, as main's metabelian chain does.
