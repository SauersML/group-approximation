Each of the following specified groups is surjunctive, nonhyperlinear, and
has stably finite group algebra over every field:

1. Jihao Liu's fixed binary coset wreath W for q=2,r=d=3, defined in
   [[kun-thom-lamp-wreath-is-not-hyperlinear]].
2. The rank-six double D_6=G_6 *_(Gamma_6) G_6 for q=2,r=6,d=3, defined in
   [[fp-surjunctive-nonhyperlinear-group-exists]]. This witness is also
   finitely presented. No finite-presentation assertion for W is made.

Thus neither surjunctivity nor all-field group-algebra stable finiteness
implies hyperlinearity. These examples are not counterexamples to
Gottschalk or Kaplansky. The Leavitt coefficient ring is a different ring
from the group algebra of its unit group: an inverse defect in the former
does not supply one in the latter.

conditional normalization/double argument, Kun--Thom the group pairs,
Phung the all-field transfer, and the named Cairn prerequisites the
separate surjunctivity and rank-six finite-presentation arguments.
No literature-priority claim is made for this combined profile.
