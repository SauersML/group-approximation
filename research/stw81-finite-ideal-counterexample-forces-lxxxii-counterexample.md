title: Every finite-ideal counterexample to LXXXI contains an extension counterexample to LXXXII
distinct_from:
  stw82-every-counterexample-reflects-to-a-separable-subextension: that reflects an already given extension counterexample to a separable subextension; this theorem first extracts an extension counterexample from a hypothetical LXXXI failure inside the offending algebra's own finite composition series.
---

Let `A` be separable with finite ideal lattice and suppose that every
commutative hereditary subalgebra of every quotient of `A` has primitive
spectrum of dimension at most one.  If

```text
2 <= dim_nuc(A) < infinity,
```

then a subquotient of `A` admits an extension

```text
0 -> I -> E -> D -> 0
```

such that

```text
dim_nuc(I) <= 1,
dim_nuc(D) <= 1,
dim_nuc(E) >= 2.
```

In particular this extension violates the maximum formula in Problem
LXXXII.  Thus, among separable algebras with finite ideal lattice, a negative
answer to LXXXI forces a negative answer to LXXXII with both endpoint
dimensions at most one.
