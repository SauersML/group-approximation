**OPEN.** There is a free minimal `Z^2`-SFT `Ω` that is `D`-quantum rigid for some `D`.
Equivalently (`sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`), some free minimal `Z^2`-SFT has a
finitely presented crossed-product algebra over some field.

**Proof claimed (2026-09-18, lane proof, not independently reviewed; referee bh-ref-engines):** route
`free-minimal-z2-rigid-sft-via-crossing-wire-tiles`, through `minimal-crossing-wire-fixed-point-shift-is-quantum-rigid`.

**Payoff.** Combine it with `free-minimal-crossed-products-are-simple-with-rank-models` over a
finite field. The result is a finitely presented infinite-dimensional simple algebra over a finite
field with a faithful rank function (`fp-simple-algebra-over-finite-field-with-rank-model`). That
is gate (a) of `sofic-fp-simple-kazhdan-group-from-fp-simple-ring`.

## Attempts

- **Positive entropy: dead.** `positive-entropy-sft-crossed-products-are-not-fp`.
- **Walls with a finite side: dead** for minimal zero-entropy shifts. They force positive entropy
  (`research/artifacts/sft-crossed-product-presentation-rigidity-2026-09-13.md`, Proposition 4).
- **First-order obstructions: classified.** Walls are exactly the obstructions visible to two-dimensional
  and finitely filtered families. Equivalently, the commutator ideal of the local tiling algebra is
  idempotent (`sft-wall-rigidity-iff-idempotent-commutator-ideal`).
  - Periodic quantum tilings of a free SFT refute rigidity and must be contextual
    (`periodic-quantum-tilings-refute-quantum-rigidity`).
- **Labbé's shift passes first order.** `labbe-shift-is-wall-rigid`. What remains there is vanishing of an
  idempotent commutator ideal. Route: `free-minimal-rigid-sft-via-labbe-shift`, with
  `labbe-crossed-product-fp-via-wall-rigidity` into its first requirement.
- **Local-to-global principle: open.** `wall-rigid-z2-sft-is-quantum-rigid` would settle this claim with
  Labbé's shift.
- **Permutive triangles (in some recoding): live but constrained.** They give rigidity
  (`permutive-triangle-sfts-are-quantum-rigid`). A free example needs row and column subshifts with
  no periodic point, hence non-sofic ones, generated by bounded-radius bipermutive rules. No example
  is known here.
  - Algebraic shifts such as Ledrappier's have dense periodic points.
  - **Labbé's shift: heuristically excluded.** It has four nonexpansive fault directions, one irrational. A
    triangle-permutive SFT should be expansive off its three side directions (not checked).
  - Whether some aperiodic minimal SFT is triangle-permutive is open. Such rows form a bipermutive
    cellular automaton orbit inside a non-sofic subshift, and no obstruction was found.
- **Deterministic aperiodic Wang tiles (Kari–Papasoglu, 4-way): not permutive as they stand.**
  - Edge-colour determinism determines a column only with a seed at infinity.
  - As operators, corner determinism propagates commutation for diagonal separations by `ℓ_1`-induction.
    It fails along the axes: the colour-product tile set (constant rows and columns) is 4-way deterministic
    and not rigid.
  - Whether some recoding is triangle-permutive was not checked.
- **Heuristic against.** Every finite presentation theorem recorded for Cantor groupoid algebras and
  full groups (Leavitt, graph, Nekrashevych, Matui, Belk–Bleak–Matucci–Zaremsky) runs through
  compression or through a group law, and an invariant measure of full support excludes compression.
  Rigidity would need a third mechanism, or a group-law recoding without periodic points.
  - A candidate third mechanism is geometric: crossing fault networks make difference sets connected with
    no superposition (`labbe-shift-is-wall-rigid`). Whether that suffices beyond first order is
    `wall-rigid-z2-sft-is-quantum-rigid`.
- **Hierarchical determinism with complete crossing: free case done, minimality open (bh-g2-fixedpoint-a,
  2026-09-18).** `crossing-wire-fixed-point-tile-sets-are-quantum-rigid` builds a free (aperiodic) SFT that is
  `1`-rigid over every field. It is a DRS fixed-point tile set with side-local margins, a deterministic zone,
  and crossing plus fan-out gadgets that bring every pair of input bits into one tile. This is the third
  mechanism asked for in "Heuristic against": neither compression nor a group law, but co-location plus
  determinism. What remains is **minimality**. Two ways to get it:
  - a minimal sub-SFT of that shift, which is automatically rigid by `quantum-rigidity-passes-to-sub-sfts`;
  - a Durand–Romashchenko-style minimal fixed-point construction (arXiv:1802.01461) whose extra layers
    keep (L1)–(L4).
- **Minimality added (bh-g2-fixedpoint-a, 2026-09-18).**
  - `minimal-crossing-wire-fixed-point-shift-is-quantum-rigid` runs the Durand–Romashchenko minimality
    mechanism (arXiv:1802.01461 §3: variable zoom, (p1)–(p4), diversification slots) on crossing-wire tiles.
    It adds slots for crossing windows.
  - The slots are forced by their frames, hence classical. So the rigidity proof goes through level by level,
    for a two-way head zone and for a one-way zone alike.
  - This gives a free minimal SFT that is `1`-rigid over every field, and so the statement above, subject to
    review.
