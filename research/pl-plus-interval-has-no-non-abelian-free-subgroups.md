Let `PL_+([0,1])` be the group of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of `[0,1]` that
are piecewise linear with finitely many breakpoints. Breakpoints may be any reals in
`(0,1)` and slopes any positive reals. `PL_+([0,1])` contains no non-abelian free
subgroup. So neither does any of its subgroups, for example Cleary's `F_τ`.

Imported from Brin–Squier, with Monod's restatement and generalisation: route
`pl-plus-interval-no-free-subgroups-citation`.
