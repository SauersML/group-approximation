The stated edge set is a maximal tree: the q14 bridge connects the two chart
vertices, and each `A4` bridge is then a leaf attached through its `C3` edge.
Bass--Serre's standard maximal-tree presentation gives `(TIR1)`.  Substituting
`s_e=1`, then `b_e=bar_b_e`, in

```text
L_e=<a_e,b_e | a_e^3=b_e^2=(b_e a_e)^3=1>
```

gives the corresponding packet cube.  Eliminating the bridge vertices gives
`(TIR2)` and then `(TIR3)` by elementary Tietze moves.

For irredundancy, collapse every vertex group of `Pi`.  The resulting quotient
is the fundamental group of the underlying graph, freely generated by the
twelve non-tree edges.  Omitting the filling of `s_e` while retaining all
other fillings and collision still maps onto the infinite cyclic group
generated by the image of `s_e`; therefore that filling is independent.

Finally use the identity alignment of the two `A8` charts.  The literal label
pairs defining the packet are `(2,3,3)` triangles in `A8`, and q14 is the
commutator of an involution with itself.  Hence this alignment factors through
the packet-plus-q14 quotient.  It is among the exact packet alignments audited
by `atlas-a4-19243-classical-collapse`, which proves that collision is
nonidentity there.  Collision is therefore independent of all twelve cycle
fillings, completing the proof.
