**INVALIDATED ONE-EXIT ROUTE.**  Attach one genuinely nonnormal
coefficient/opposite-root letter to the known Whitehead transporter, hoping
that the new occurrence contributes the same reservoir involution and
cancels it while retaining the source and target cuts.

`one-nonnormal-dressing-cannot-preserve-moving-full-gram` invalidates this
route.  Every exact transporter from `Q` to `JQJ^(-1)` is `J` times a source
normalizer.  A single relative factor outside that normalizer changes the
range Gram.  For the shortest literal opposite root `x_82(1)`, conjugating
the source root `x_28(1)` gives the `(2,8)` swap and leaves `L_0`, so the
failure is explicit.

A surviving nonnormal construction needs at least two exits whose product
returns to `Norm(Q)`, followed by a separate proof that this returned
normalizer cancels the Whitehead multiplicity gauge.
