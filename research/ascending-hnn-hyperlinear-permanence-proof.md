Britton normal form identifies the height kernel with the directed union

```text
N=union_(n>=0) t^(-n) K t^n
```

after reversing the stable-letter convention if necessary.  Every finite
subset of `N` lies in one conjugate copy of `K`; since hyperlinearity is a
finite-subset approximation property, `N` is hyperlinear.  The quotient by
`N` is infinite cyclic.  Apply Corollary 1.1 of
Arzhantseva--Berlai--Finn-Sell--Glebsky, arXiv:1802.04688: an extension with
hyperlinear normal subgroup and amenable quotient is hyperlinear.
