Citation import. Both statements were read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv e-print TeX sources, fetched and extracted on
MSI (`/scratch.global/sauer354/sk/sk-cstar-simple/`).

**M. Kennedy, *An intrinsic characterization of C*-simplicity*, arXiv:1509.01870v5** (`main.tex`), theorem label
`thm:c-star-simple-iff-no-amenable-urs`, verbatim up to macros:

> A discrete group $G$ is C*-simple if and only if it has no non-trivial amenable uniformly recurrent subgroups.

The same statement opens the paper as `thm:c-star-simple-iff-no-urs-beginning`.

**A. Le Boudec and N. Matte Bon, *Subgroup dynamics and $C^\ast$-simplicity of groups of homeomorphisms*,
arXiv:1605.01651v3** (`subgroupdynamics-arxiv-v3.tex`), theorem label `thm-cstar-chab`, attributed to Kennedy,
verbatim up to macros:

> A countable group $G$ is $C^\ast$-simple if and only if it has no non-trivial amenable uniformly recurrent subgroup;
> equivalently if and only if the conjugacy class of every amenable subgroup of $G$ accumulates at the trivial subgroup
> in $\sub(G)$.

**Scope.** Only the statements were read, not the proofs. The (3)⟺(4) step in the target is the elementary Chabauty
reformulation written there. Journal data are recalled and not checked: Kennedy, Ann. Sci. Éc. Norm. Supér. (2020);
Le Boudec–Matte Bon, Ann. Sci. Éc. Norm. Supér. (2018).
