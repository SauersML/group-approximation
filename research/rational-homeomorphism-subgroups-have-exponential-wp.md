title: Finitely generated groups of rational Cantor homeomorphisms have single-exponential word problem
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/boone-higman-finite-state-and-shell-germ-limits-2026-09-08.md
---

For any fixed finite symmetric collection of asynchronous finite-state
transducers defining homeomorphisms of A^N, |A|>=2, the word problem
in the group they generate is decidable in deterministic time 2^O(n),
where n is word length. The constant may depend on the transducers.

In particular every finitely generated subgroup of the rational
homeomorphism group has such a word-problem algorithm. This conclusion
requires neither a finite presentation nor a contracting action.
