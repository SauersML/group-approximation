Let `A` be a sigma-unital, purely infinite C-star algebra of real rank zero.
Then its multiplier algebra `M(A)` is purely infinite.

No separability, nuclearity, unitality, or exactness assumption is imposed.
The conclusion does not assert that `M(A)` has real rank zero: examples show
that its real rank can be one.
