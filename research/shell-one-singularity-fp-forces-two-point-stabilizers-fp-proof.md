The full proof is Sections 2 to 6 of the artifact. Its skeleton follows.

Localized copies. Let s(0z)=00z, s(10z)=01z, s(11z)=1z and let sigma swap
0z and 1z. By the shell claim the germ group (E)_p is generated by the
germs of hat(P) and s, so every germ at p of an element of E fixing p is
the germ of an element of A_1. A homeomorphism fixing p, locally V off p,
with such a germ, lies in A_1 (compare with an element of A_1 of the same
germ; the quotient is locally V everywhere, hence in V). Therefore
f |-> (0z |-> 0f(z), 1z |-> 1z) is an isomorphism of A_1 onto the subgroup
G_p of A_1 supported in 0C, and onto G_00 (support in 00C) from G_p. Its
sigma conjugates G_q, G_10 are one-singularity copies at q. The rational
stabilizer claim, for the trivial self-similar group, makes
W=Fix_V({p,q}) finitely presented and Fix_V(p) finitely generated.

Generation. With K_2 the elements of V that are the identity near p and
q, matching germs gives A_2=G_p G_q K_2 and A_12=G_p <s_q> K_2, where
s_q in W is the shift at q supported in 1C. Using simplicity of V, cone
swaps generate each V(X), which gives the overlap lemma
V(X_1 union X_2)=<V(X_1),V(X_2)> for overlapping clopen sets. Hence
K_2=<K(0C),K(1C),V(Y)> with Y=01C union 11C.

Presentation. Take finite presentations of G_p, G_q, W. Identify the two
copies of finite generating sets of Fix_V(p) localized in 0C and of its
sigma transport. Impose [G_p,G_q]=1, [G_00,V(Y)]=1 and [G_10,V(Y)]=1 on
finite generating sets. In the resulting group the copy of K_2 inside the
copy of W is normal. The only nonformal case is conjugating V(Y) by g in
G_p. Write g=hu with h in K(0C) agreeing with g on 01C, found by clopen
prefix interpolation, and u in G_00. The commutator relation lets u pass
through V(Y), and h lies in W, which normalizes K_2.

The quotient by this copy is generated by commuting images of the two
germ groups. It maps isomorphically onto A_2/K_2 via the joint germ map.
A kernel element therefore lies in the copy of K_2, where the map is
injective. So A_2 is finitely presented. For A_12, drop G_q and replace
[G_p,G_q]=1 by commutators of G_p with Fix_V(q) localized in 1C. The
quotient is then Q x Z, with Z generated by the shift at q.
