Citation import, not a reproof. The source is Tom Hutchcroft, *Percolation on
hyperbolic graphs*, arXiv:1804.10191. It was read from the TeX source on MSI;
Section 5.1 of the artifact has the quotes. Markup is transcribed.

* `thm:pell2`, label line 304, statement line 305: "Let G be a connected, locally finite,
  quasi-transitive, nonamenable, Gromov hyperbolic graph. Then
  p_c(G)<p_{q→q}(G) for every q∈(1,∞)."
* `thm:nonunimodular`, label line 551, statement line 552: "Let G be a connected, locally finite graph,
  and suppose that Aut(G) has a quasi-transitive nonunimodular subgroup. Then
  p_c(G)< p_{q→q}(G) for every q∈(1,∞)."

Take `q=2`.

Section 1.3 of `research/artifacts/fpbs/docs/free-product-critical-l2-2026-09-12.md`
lists the same two classes. It numbers the second theorem as Theorem 2.9.
