Let `tau` be a character of a countable group `G`, with tracial GNS
representation `pi_tau:G->U(M)`.  The pointwise power

```text
tau^(n)(g)=tau(g)^n                                      (DTP1)
```

is a character, realized by the diagonal tensor representation

```text
g |-> pi_tau(g)^(tensor n).                              (DTP2)
```

This observation does **not** propagate non-Connes-embeddability from `M` to
the GNS algebra of `tau^(n)`.  That GNS algebra is

```text
M_n^diag=W*(pi_tau(g)^(tensor n):g in G),               (DTP3)
```

which can be much smaller than the full tensor product `M^(bar tensor n)`.
There is no general equality, conditional expectation onto a copy of `M`, or
other inheritance map supplied by `(DTP2)`.

The proposed raw pointwise polarization also has a phase error.  Powers
converge to `1` when `tau(g)=1` and to `0` when `|tau(g)|<1`, but need not
converge when `|tau(g)|=1` and `tau(g)!=1`; the sign character of `C_2` gives
the sequence `(-1)^n`.  Cesaro averages of the powers repair the scalar
limit, but their GNS representation is only a direct sum of diagonal tensor
representations and still does not contain the full tensor product algebra.

Thus trace polarization alone cannot turn one non-CE character into a family
of polarized non-CE characters.  Survival of the original non-CE GNS algebra
requires an additional recoverability or expectation theorem.
