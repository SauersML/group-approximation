Let pi from (Y,nu) to (X,mu) be a measure-preserving equivariant map between free p.m.p. actions of a countable group. Freeness of the target makes pi a bijection from each individual source orbit onto its image orbit, so a target graphing split into restrictions of group transformations lifts to a source graphing of the same cost, and a lifted path ending at xg lifts to a path ending at yg. Hence C(Y) <= C(X). The reverse inequality, cost descent, is the difficulty.

Status records a written deduction, not independent validation.
