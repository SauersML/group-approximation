[Elliott--Li--Niu, Theorems 1.1 and 3.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13707)
construct a simple unital separable AH algebra

```text
A = direct_limit A_n
```

and a finite set `E` in its initial `C(M)` stage such that `E` is contained in
no singly generated subalgebra of `A`.  The obstruction is not generator rank
or a cardinality argument.  At stage `n`, a hypothetical generator `g` has a
distinguished trivial rank-one corner.  The locus `Z(g)` where the off-diagonal
entries from this corner vanish is the zero locus of a bundle section.  The
top Chern class

```text
c_(2(2^n-1))((zeta_n-theta) direct_sum (zeta_n-theta)) != 0
```

and the Thom--Porteous formula show that the projection
`Z(g) -> M` is nonzero on `H_d`, where `d=dim(M)>=2`.

If `g` generated the coordinate functions defining `E`, compression to the
rank-one corner would approximate this projection by a map factoring through
the compact planar spectrum of the scalar corner `q_1 g q_1`.  Every compact
subset of the plane has zero homology in degree `d>=2`, contradicting the
nonzero Thom--Porteous class.  Thus `C*(1,g)!=A` for every `g`.

The remaining properties needed for the STW questions follow without further
classification input:

* AH algebras are separable and nuclear, and the construction is explicitly
  unital and simple.
* The example is infinite-dimensional, since every finite-dimensional simple
  algebra `M_n` is generated by one nilpotent shift.
* A simple unital infinite-dimensional C*-algebra is nowhere scattered.  Its
  only nonzero quotient is itself; if it had a minimal projection, simplicity
  would make it elementary, and unitality would then make it finite
  dimensional.
