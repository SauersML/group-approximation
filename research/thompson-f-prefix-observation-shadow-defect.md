**OPEN.** Notation as in `thompson-f-flow-certificate-observation-gate` and
`thompson-f-exact-tail-observations-have-zero-defect`: `S ⊆ M` finite, `I` the largest generator index
in the normal forms of `S`, `D = max_(s ∈ S) deg s`, `K = I + D + 1`, and

`π_K(y) = (t_0, t_1, ..., t_(K−1))`   (the *prefix observation*).

By Lemma 1 of `thompson-f-exact-tail-observations-have-zero-defect-proof`, `split_s` descends to a
well-defined partial map `σ_s` of `K`-prefixes, so a rule reading `π_K` is a flow on the
partial-action system `(X_K, {σ_s}_(s ∈ S))`, `X_K` being the set of `K`-tuples of finite binary trees.

**Statement (1).** There are a finite `S ⊆ M` and `λ > 1` with `δ_λ(π_K) > 0`.

**The complementary question (2).** If instead `δ_1(π_K) = 0` for every finite `S`, does
`δ_1(id_M) = 0` follow?

**Why 1 and 2 would settle the flow route.** (They are *sufficient*, not exhaustive: they are not the
only uncovered observations — see the `distinct_from` note above and the `## Attempts` entry of
`thompson-f-exact-tail-observations-have-zero-defect`.)
- A positive answer to 1 proves `thompson-f-is-not-amenable`: by Theorem A of the gate it gives a rule
  with `inf out > λ sup in`, hence after rescaling a certificate with ratio `> 1`, and Lemma 2.1(a) of
  `thompson-f-finite-state-flows-cannot-prove-nonamenability-proof` turns that into non-amenability.
  So 1 is at least as hard as the root.
- A negative answer to 1 in the strong form `δ_1(π_K) = 0` for every finite `S`, together with a
  positive answer to 2, proves `thompson-f-is-amenable`, by Corollary A.3 of the gate (`D_λ` is
  nonincreasing in `λ`, so `δ_1 = 0` gives `δ_λ = 0` for every `λ >= 1`).
- Question 2 is exactly the claim that the tail carries no certificate power.
  `thompson-f-exact-tail-observations-have-zero-defect` proves the finite-state shadow of it: reading
  the *entire* tail exactly, and the prefix only through a tree automaton, already has defect zero. It
  does not give 2, because `δ` is not subadditive over a factorization of an observation.

**Status of the two pieces.** Each can fail on its own, which is the point of recording them
separately.
- 1 could be true while every *known* prefix flow fails, since `σ_s` is not injective on `X_K` (trees
  shift out of the window), so the in-sum counting on `X_K` differs from that on `M`.
- 2 could fail even if 1 has a negative answer: a certificate on `M` may need to correlate the prefix
  with the tail, which is exactly the freedom
  `thompson-f-exact-tail-observations-have-zero-defect` leaves open together with `(Ξ_(K,Q), |y|)`.

**First falsifiable steps.**
- Compute `δ_1(π_K)` on truncations: the finite linear program `sup_G (inf_y out_G − sup_z in_G)` over
  the sub-system of `X_K` generated by trees of depth `<= h`, for `S = S_(2,3)` and `S_(3,5)`. A
  truncation with a positive value and a verified extension to all of `X_K` answers 1.
- Or: exhibit an approximately `σ`-invariant law on `X_K`. The tower models of
  `thompson-f-exact-tail-observations-have-zero-defect-proof` do not give one — Stage 4 of
  `experiments/thompson-f-shadow-defect-2026-09-17/output.txt` measures `255/256` for `π_K` — because
  splitting lowers the depth of the trees it touches while the window admits only fresh full-depth
  trees on the right. Any law answering 2 negatively must fix that depth bookkeeping inside a bounded
  window, where no idempotent measure is available (Section 5 of
  `thompson-f-finite-state-flows-cannot-prove-nonamenability-proof`).

## Attempts

- **Tower models (the ones that kill every other observation).** Every shadow law used so far —
  B.1, B.2, B.3 and Theorem B of `thompson-f-flow-certificate-observation-gate`, and Theorem C of
  `thompson-f-exact-tail-observations-have-zero-defect` — builds a random forest from depth-`H` towers
  over an idempotent state measure. *Dies at the depth bookkeeping inside the window:* `split_s` replaces
  a tree of the prefix by subtrees of depth `H − j`, `1 <= j <= d`, while the `K`-prefix admits only
  fresh depth-`H` towers at the positions that shift in from the right. Stage 4 of
  `experiments/thompson-f-shadow-defect-2026-09-17/output.txt` measures the residue exactly: total
  variation `255/256` for `π_K` on `S_(2,3)`, against `0` for the exact-tail observation in the same
  model. Randomising the tower depth does not help either: several prefix positions are subtrees of one
  tree `t_a`, so their depths are perfectly correlated on the split side and independent on the other,
  and the two depth vectors stay at total variation near `1`. This is the step where the absence of an
  idempotent measure for size and depth (Section 5 of
  `thompson-f-finite-state-flows-cannot-prove-nonamenability-proof`) finally bites.
- **Deferred: the finite linear program.** `δ_1(π_K)` restricted to the sub-system of `X_K` generated by
  trees of depth `<= h` is a finite LP, and Theorem A of the gate says its value is the exact defect of
  that truncation. Not run here; the truncation is not a sub-system (splitting leaves the depth bound
  only downward, but the right-shift brings in trees from outside the window), so a positive truncated
  value needs a separate extension argument before it answers 1.
- **Why coarsening cannot decide it.** Lemma A.4 of the gate makes dead observations a down-set, so no
  coarsening of `π_K` — in particular none of the finite-state readings already killed — can settle
  either direction here.
- **Tail truncation (2026-09-19, swarm-0917-w19-w19-f-break): items 1 and 2 are not separate pieces.**
  This is proved in `thompson-f-prefix-observation-is-lossless`. For every uniform-degree `S`, which
  includes every Guba set, the result is `δ_λ(Ψ) <= δ_λ(Ψ ∘ τ_(J+d))` for every observation `Ψ`.
  Here `τ_j` trivialises all trees past position `j`, and `J = m` for `S_(m+1,m+d+1)`.
  - *Proof idea.* Push `μ` through `τ_(J+d)` and `η` through `τ_J`. Splits commute with truncation,
    so `ρ^(η')_s = (τ_(J+d))_* ρ^η_s` exactly.
  - *Consequences.*
    - `δ_λ(π_K) = δ_λ(id_M)`. So item 1 is **equivalent** to `thompson-f-is-not-amenable`, and item 2
      is **true**.
    - `thompson-f-not-amenable-from-prefix-defect` is a reformulation of the root, not a reduction of it.
  - *The step that fails in "Status of the two pieces".* The first bullet worries that `σ_s` is not
    injective on `X_K`. That does not matter, because `δ` is defined by laws on `M`, and the truncation
    moves those laws into the window with every split intact.
  - *What is left.* This node is now exactly as hard as the root. Any progress on it has to come from
    the finite-window system `(X_(m+d+1), {σ_s})` itself.
