**ESTABLISHED** through `fg-piecewise-linear-hosts-omit-natural-gl-n-q-proof`.
Not independently reviewed. The argument is elementary and no priority is claimed.

**Setting.** Let `K` be a field and let `Γ`, `X`, `S` and a cocycle
`c : Γ × X -> GL_N(K)` be as in `fg-groups-have-fg-derivative-images`, with each
`c(s, -)` finitely valued. Let `R ⊂ K` be the subring generated by the entries
of the matrices `c(s, x)` and `c(s, x)^-1`, for `s in S` and `x in X`.
Examples are the linear part of piecewise affine maps and the Möbius piece of
piecewise projective maps (for `PGL_2`, lift the finitely many pieces to `GL_2`).

**Statement.**
1. **Finite coefficients.** `c(γ, x) in GL_N(R)` for all `γ, x`, and `R` is a
   finitely generated commutative ring. So `R` does not contain `Q`.
2. **Fixed-point rigidity.** Let `x in X` and let `P <= Γ_x` have no nontrivial
   residually finite quotient. Examples are divisible abelian groups such as
   `(Q,+)`, and `SL_n(Q)` for `n >= 2`. Then `c(P, x) = 1`. If moreover germs at
   fixed points are determined by the cocycle, as for piecewise affine maps
   (and for right germs of piecewise projective maps), then `P` acts as the
   identity near `x`.
3. **No natural rational derivative at a fixed point.** The ring generated by
   the entries of `c(H, x)`, for `H <= Γ_x`, lies in `R`, so it never contains
   `Q`. So a finitely generated `Γ` with such a cocycle can never contain:
   - (a) `GL_n(Q)`, `n >= 1`, fixing a point `x` with `c(g, x) = g`: the
     natural linear derivative at the origin of `Q^n`, `R^n`, `A_f^n`, or of
     every component of `⊔_p Q_p^n`;
   - (b) `(Q,+)` fixing `x` with `c(u_q, x) = [[1,q],[0,1]]`, or any unipotent
     derivative whose entries generate `Q`;
   - (c) `Aff(Q) = Q x| Q^x` with the dilations fixing `x` and `c(d_a, x) = a`;
   - (d) `PSL_2(Q)` acting on `RP^1` by its Möbius maps inside a finitely
     generated group of piecewise projective homeomorphisms. The Borel
     subgroup fixes `∞`, and its matrix entries generate `Q`.

**Consequence for gluing affine hosts (lane `gq-scott-union`).** The chain
`GL_n(Z[1/m!])`, `m >= 1`, has union `GL_n(Q)` and a common fixed point, the
origin. So no gluing that keeps the natural affine action of the chain on a
common space, with its linear-part cocycle, is finitely generated, let alone
finitely presented. This covers:
- Scott's `Sc(n)` for `GL_n(Z)` on `Z_2^n`;
- Zaremsky's self-similar affine hosts for `GL_n(Z[1/m])` on `Z_p^n` with `p ∤ m`;
- adelic V-type groups of locally affine maps of `A_f^n ∪ {∞}`;
- the one-point compactification of `⊔_p (Q_p^n ∪ {∞_p})`.

These hold even when elements have infinitely many pieces, as long as each
generator has finitely many linear parts. A successful host must realize every
divisible subgroup of `GL_n(Q)` either without fixed points or with trivial
derivative at them.

**What this does not exclude.** The obstruction is about realizations, not
abstract embeddings. Belk--Hyde--Matucci's `(Q,+) <= T̄` acts on `R` without
fixed points, by lifts with finitely many `PSL_2(Z)` pieces up to period, so
parts 2 and 3 say nothing about it. There the denominators come from torsion
of `T` and the central extension `Z -> T̄ -> T`, not from matrix entries. On
`R`, `T̄` is faithful on germs at `+∞`, because its elements commute with
`x -> x + 1`. That is the shape a gluing of the chain would have to copy.
