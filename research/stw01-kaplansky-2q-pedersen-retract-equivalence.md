title: Kaplansky, 2Q, finite Pedersen, and bidual-retract formulations are equivalent
distinct_from:
  stw99-problem-i-quasitraces-are-traces: that asks whether the 2Q condition holds; this establishes only its equivalence to three other unresolved assertions.

The following assertions are equivalent:

1. Every type `II_1` AW-star factor is a type `II_1` W-star factor.
2. Every 2-quasitrace on a unital C-star algebra is a trace.
3. A finite C-star algebra is a W-star algebra if and only if all of its
   maximal abelian self-adjoint subalgebras are W-star algebras.
4. Every type `II_1` AW-star factor is a star-homomorphic retract of its
   bidual; equivalently, its canonical inclusion into its bidual has a
   star-homomorphic left inverse.
