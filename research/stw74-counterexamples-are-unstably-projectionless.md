title: Every LXXIV counterexample has a full projectionless corner with properly infinite stabilization
---

If `A` satisfies the hypothesis of Problem LXXIV but is not purely infinite,
then there is a nonzero full hereditary subalgebra `D` such that

```text
D has no nonzero projections,
D tensor K is stably isomorphic to A tensor K,
every nonzero projection in D tensor K is properly infinite.
```

So a counterexample cannot be stably projectionless.  Its obstruction is the
failure to descend properly infinite projections from matrix levels into the
original hereditary corner.
