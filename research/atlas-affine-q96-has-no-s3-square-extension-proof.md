The verifier constructs `AGL5(2)` as the group of `6 by 6` homogeneous
affine matrices generated by all five translations and all elementary linear
transvections.  Its computed order is `319979520`.  It then loads the literal
five-dimensional affine matrices `c,u,t` and checks that their closure has
order `96`.

Every possible `r` lies in `C_G(c)`, and every possible `z` lies in `C_G(u)`.
GAP enumerates those centralizers exactly, then filters by element order and
the displayed inversion equations.  Both lists are empty.  No random sampling
or bound on word length is used.

For the collision calibration, every `s` lies in the normalizer of `<t>`.
The verifier enumerates that finite normalizer and filters by `s^2=1`,
`sts=t^-1`, collision `q=1`, and `|<t,s,c>|=24`; four candidates survive.
Since either missing order-three generator is necessary for the marked
`S3 x S3`, emptiness of both exact fibers proves the claim.
