title: Near-unit-cost graphings can have reciprocal shortest-route growth
root: true
---

On F_r x Z, keep all vertical edges and independent epsilon-density horizontal edges. The graph is connected almost surely, graphing cost is 1+r epsilon, and a horizontal neighbor distance has mean (2-epsilon^2)/(epsilon(2-epsilon)). Thus graphing excess times mean shortest replacement length tends to r, not zero. These are graphings of one action, not different-cost actions.
