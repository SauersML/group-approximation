Let `mu` be the ergodic non-cohyperlinear IRS of the nonabelian free group
`F` used in the Manzoor relation construction, let

```text
chi_mu(w)=mu({S:w in S}),
```

and let `pi_mu:F->U(P_mu)` be its GNS representation. Let `H` act on a
countable abelian group `A`, and suppose an exact covariant packet in a finite
tracial von Neumann algebra satisfies the hypotheses of
`centralizer-aperiodic-haar-actor-marginals-force-regular-trace`. Thus its
generated algebra is canonically `L(A rtimes H)` with the regular trace.

For every injective homomorphism `theta:F->H`, there is no trace-preserving
embedding

```text
Psi:P_mu -> L(A rtimes H)                              (MHA1)
```

such that, for every member `s` of a free generating set of `F`,

```text
Psi(pi_mu(s)) = B_s U_(theta(s)),
             B_s in U(L(A)).                          (MHA2)
```

The coefficients `B_s` may be arbitrary base unitaries, not group-basis
lamps or finite Fourier polynomials. Hence the obstruction applies to every
single-actor-grade placement of the source generators.

In particular take `H=Gamma(3)<SL_3(Z)` and any centralizer-aperiodic module
`A`. Embedding the Manzoor GNS algebra, and therefore placing the canonical
source subalgebra inside the CDI relation factor, cannot be achieved by
decorating an injective free subgroup of `Gamma(3)` with Haar lamp
coefficients. Any successful algebraic envelope must send at least one
canonical IRS generator to a genuinely nonhomogeneous sum of two or more
actor grades (or use an embedding that does not preserve this canonical
generator system at all).

This is a sharp boundary rather than a general no-embedding theorem. Finite
or infinite actor-grade mixtures can have nonzero intermediate trace, so the
argument deliberately leaves that concrete Fourier-mixing route open.
