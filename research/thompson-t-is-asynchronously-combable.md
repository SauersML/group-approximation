**ESTABLISHED by citation** (route `thompson-t-is-asynchronously-combable-citation`).

Richard Thompson's group `T` is asynchronously combable. It has a finite generating
set and a combing, a choice of a word for each element, that satisfies the
asynchronous fellow traveller property. The same holds for the braided
Ptolemy–Thompson group `T*`.
