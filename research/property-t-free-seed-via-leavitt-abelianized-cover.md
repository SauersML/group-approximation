**What this route changes.**  Every current route to
`property-t-free-manuscript-results` needs the binary-Leavitt collapse
`property-t-free-leavitt-full-mf-radical` (endpoint 2) *and* this seed
(through `property-t-free-torsion-free-via-embedding-router`, endpoint 6).
The two halves were recorded as independent, because the Leavitt absorber is
generated by involutions and dies in every torsion-free group.  This route
spends the first half on the second.  Once the collapse is established, the
only further input the goal needs is the single analytic hole
`full-mf-radical-abelianized-cover-is-not-mf`.  That hole is a special case of
`free-abelianized-cover-reflects-mf`, not an equivalent restatement of it.

**Distinct from** `torsion-free-countable-non-mf-from-abelianized-cover`: that route feeds the full descent statement (D) with a Kazhdan-dependent non-MF input and lands in the countable class; this one feeds only the weaker full-radical instance with the Property-T-free Leavitt collapse, and continues through the torsion-free Higman embedding to the finitely presented seed.

## Steps

1. **Base.**  Let `Delta = St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))`.  The ring is generated by
   `1, s_1, s_2, t_1, t_2`, and `St_n(R)` for `n >= 3` is generated by the
   `x_ij(r)` with `r` in a ring generating set, because
   `x_ij(a+b) = x_ij(a) x_ij(b)` and `[x_ij(a), x_jk(b)] = x_ik(ab)`.  So `Delta`
   is finitely generated.  By `property-t-free-leavitt-full-mf-radical`,
   `Rad_MF(Delta) = Delta`.
2. **Finite presentation.**  `full-mf-radical-has-fp-cover-same-generators`
   gives a finitely presented `G = F/R` with `F` free of finite rank,
   `R = <<r_1, ..., r_s>>`, `G ->> Delta` and `Rad_MF(G) = G`.  Its derivation
   is the linear word certificate, which contains no Property `(T)`.  Since
   `Delta != 1`, `G != 1`.
3. **Cover.**  Let `E = F/[R,R]`.  By `free-abelianized-cover-is-torsion-free`,
   `E` is torsion-free and finitely generated.
4. **Analytic hole.**  By `full-mf-radical-abelianized-cover-is-not-mf`, `E` is
   not MF.  By `universal-mf-quotient`, `E` therefore has a nontrivial element
   in `Rad_MF(E)`.
5. **Recursive presentation.**  `E` has a recursively enumerable set of
   relators, so it is recursively presented.  The normal closure `R` of the
   finite set `{r_j}` is recursively enumerable: enumerate the products of
   conjugates of `r_j^(+-1)` and freely reduce them.  The subgroup `[R,R]` is
   generated by the commutators of pairs from that enumeration, and it is
   normal because `R` is normal.  So the words of `[R,R]` are enumerated by
   enumerating products of those commutators and their inverses.
6. **Finite presentation, torsion-free.**  By `torsion-free-higman-embedding`,
   `E` embeds in a finitely presented torsion-free group `T`.
7. **Non-MF passes up.**  Subgroups of MF groups are MF
   (`mf-positive-controls`).  So `T` is not MF, and it is the seed.

## Property-T audit

- Step 1 consumes the goal's own Property-T-free collapse.
- Step 2 is the linear-certificate calculus.
- Steps 3, 5 and 6 are combinatorial group theory, with Chiodo imported
  verbatim.
- Step 7 is the basic heredity.
- The analytic content lies entirely in step 4.

## How the hole can fail on its own

A single finitely generated full-MF-radical group whose free abelianized cover
is MF would refute step 4.  It would be the first quotient-nonclosure witness
with abelian kernel, and in that case
`free-abelianized-cover-reflects-mf` would be false.  Only the abelianized
cover of the particular finite presentation from step 2 is needed.  Steps 1
and 2 may be replaced by any Property-T-free finitely generated nontrivial
full-MF-radical group.
