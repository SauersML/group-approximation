**ESTABLISHED** by `thompson-t-square-central-quotients-no-small-corners-proof` (written proof,
unreviewed, not Lean-verified). The route is self-contained. It re-derives in full the one
exact-pair inequality it takes from the wave-17 node `thompson-t-defect-gap-central-square-locus`
(held in commit `92b3a78f7d`); see "Dependency" below.

## Setting

- `G0 = Z/4 * Z/3 = <a, b | a^4, b^3>`, and `T = G0 / <<r_1, r_2, r_P>>` (Lochak--Schneps,
  `thompson-t-lochak-schneps-presentation`).
- Words: `X = bab`, `J = a^2`, `W = J b^2 J`, `r_1 = [X, JXJ]`, `r_2 = [X, W X W^(-1)]`,
  `r_P = (ba)^5`, and the square-commutator `c = [a^2, b] = a^2 b a^(-2) b^(-1)`. As words in
  `a^(+-1), b^(+-1)` they have lengths 20, 34, 10 and 6.
- For an exact pair `(A, B)` (unitaries with `A^4 = B^3 = 1`) put `eps_i = ||r_i(A,B) - 1||`,
  `D = max(eps_1, eps_2, eps_P)` and `gamma = ||A^2 B - B A^2|| = ||c(A,B) - 1||`.
- Let `F` be any group with a chosen generating pair `a, b` satisfying `a^4 = b^3 = 1`, and `pi` a
  unitary representation of `F` on a finite-dimensional space. A projection `P != 0` is an
  **eps-corner** for `(F, pi)` if
  - (C1) `||[P, pi(a)]|| < eps` and `||[P, pi(b)]|| < eps`;
  - (C2) `||P (pi(r) - 1) P|| < eps` for `r = r_1, r_2, r_P`;
  - (C3) `||P (pi(a) - 1) P|| >= 1` or `||P (pi(b) - 1) P|| >= 1`.

  This is the corner notion of the wave-20 criterion `thompson-t-mf-iff-finite-quotient-corners`
  (held in commit `46e8cd93e6`), which says: `T` is MF iff finite quotients of `G0` carry `eps`-corners
  for every `eps > 0`.
- Call `F` **square-central** if `a^2` is central in `F`, i.e. `c = 1` in `F`. Equivalently, `F` is a
  quotient of `G0 / <<[a^2, b]>> = Z/4 *_(Z/2) Z/6 = SL(2, Z)`, with `a -> S` and `b -> -ST`.

## Statement

1. **(Transfer of the square-commutator.)** Let `P` be an `eps`-corner for `(F, pi)` with `eps < 1/4`,
   and put `eta = ||P (pi(c) - 1) P||`. Polar rounding of the compressions gives a nontrivial exact pair
   `(A, B)` in `U(rank P)` with

   ```text
   eps_2 <= 175.9 eps,     eps_P <= 52.5 eps,     D <= 175.9 eps,     gamma <= eta + 30.9 eps.
   ```

2. **(Uniform corner gap on the square-central class.)** Every `eps`-corner, for every `F` and `pi`,
   satisfies `eps >= 1/4` or

   ```text
   2373 eps + 45 ||P (pi([a^2, b]) - 1) P||  >=  2/3.
   ```

   In particular, if `F` is square-central, then every `eps`-corner has `eps > 1/3560`. The bound does
   not depend on `|F|`, on `diam F`, on `rank P` or on whether `F` is finite.

3. **(Named instances.)**
   - Every congruence quotient `SL(2, Z/q)`, with `a -> S` and `b -> -ST`, has corner level
     `c(F) >= 1/3560`. The same holds for `SL(2, Z)` itself, for `2I = SL(2, 5)`, for every quotient of
     `Z/4 *_(Z/2) Z/6`, and for every finite-dimensional unitary representation of any of them.
   - In all of them `r_1 = 1` identically, because `J = a^2` is central
     (`sl2z_square_central.py` checks this exactly in `SL(2, Z)`).

## What this kills

- **The named test family of the wave-20 corner criterion.** That node proposes the congruence
  quotients `SL(2, Z/q)` as "a natural test family" for refuting the target (proving `T` MF), and its
  diameter bound `c(F) >= 1/(18 diam F)` only forces them to exponential size. Item 3 kills the whole
  family outright: no congruence quotient of any size carries a corner of level below `1/3560`.
- **Every arithmetic model through SL(2, Z).** Since `T` is `PPSL(2, Z)`
  (`thompson-t-is-isomorphic-to-ppsl2z`), a natural source of candidate MF witnesses is compressions of
  representations of `SL(2, Z)` and its finite quotients: Farey, congruence or modular-form models. All
  of them are square-central. None of them carries a witness, and not merely a whole-representation
  witness: an almost-invariant corner of level below `1/3560` does not exist.
- **Named invariant.** The compressed square-commutator `eta = ||P (pi([a^2, b]) - 1) P||`.
- **Death step.** The rounding of a corner to an exact pair moves `gamma` by at most `30.9 eps` (Part 1).
  The exact pair then meets the wave-17 inequality `5 eps_2 + 2 eps_P + 45 gamma >= 2/3`, which only
  uses `r_2` and `r_P`.
- **Corner form of the far-sector condition.** A corner witness of level `eps` in any finite quotient of
  `G0` must have `||P (pi([a^2, b]) - 1) P|| >= 2/135 - 52.8 eps`. So the finite groups carrying MF
  witnesses of `T` must be far from square-central on the corner. In particular, `a^2` cannot be
  central in them, nor central modulo a normal subgroup that acts trivially on `ran P`.

## What this does not do

- It proves nothing about corners in groups where `a^2` is far from central on the corner. That sector
  is exactly the far sector (FC) of `thompson-t-near-central-pairs-have-golden-defect-gap` (held in
  commit `7894aba6e8`), and the target stays OPEN.
- The constant `1/3560` is crude. It comes from the operator-norm Lipschitz bounds of the transfer and
  the constant 45 of the imported inequality. Using that node's numerically observed constant 30 in
  place of 45 would improve it, but that constant is not proved.

## Side calibration (computation, recorded in the artifacts)

`low_index.py` lists the transitive permutation representations of index at most 10 of the
relator-dropped groups:
- `Delta(4,3,5) / <<r_1>>` has an `A_5` quotient (`a` of order 2) in which `r_2` survives;
- `Delta(4,3,5) / <<r_2>>` has an `A_6` quotient (`a` of order 4) in which `r_1` survives;
- `G0 / <<r_1, r_2>>` has the cyclic quotient `Z/12`, in which `r_P` survives.

So no two of the three long relators force the defect gap: each proper sub-presentation has a
nontrivial finite image of defect zero on the relators it keeps. Any proof of the target must use all
three relators. This is a weak calibration and is not claimed as high impact. It does show that the
imported inequality, which avoids `r_1`, can only work on a sector (square-central) where `r_1` holds
automatically.
