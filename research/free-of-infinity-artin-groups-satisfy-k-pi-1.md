For every finite Coxeter graph `Γ` with `m_st < ∞` for all `s, t`, the space `E_Γ` is
aspherical, so `A_Γ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture.

With `artin-k-pi-1-reduces-to-free-of-infinity-parabolics` this gives the full conjecture
(route `artin-k-pi-1-via-free-of-infinity-diagrams`).

## Attempts

- Known in this case: spherical; affine; rank three; dimension at most 2; every diagram whose
  commutation graph `Λ^c` has no embedded 4-cycle, which includes dimension at most 3
  (preprint arXiv:2509.06914); complete bipartite diagrams (arXiv:2405.12068); trees cut by
  edges of label at least 6 into spherical, locally reducible or star components
  (arXiv:2305.16847, arXiv:2405.12068); diagrams with a cycle whose links are spherical or
  locally reducible trees (arXiv:2305.16847); non-compact hyperbolic simplex groups up to
  `H^4` (arXiv:2405.12068).
- First uncovered almost spherical diagrams: the four compact hyperbolic Coxeter 4-simplex
  diagrams that are trees, `[5,3,3,3]`, `[4,3,3,5]`, `[5,3,3,5]` and `[5,3,3^{1,1}]` (lane
  z1-05-artin-kpi1, see `compact-hyperbolic-4-simplex-artin-groups-satisfy-k-pi-1`).
