Let `E` be the literal finitely presented group of `non_mf_group_notes.tex`
(Definition `def:E`), with central mark `w`, base `B = Z^3 ⋊ SL_3(Z)`
generated by `v_1, v_2, v_3, x, y, z`, doubling element `t`, and lamp `c`.
Let `W` be a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal tracial state
`τ` that is injective (hence hyperfinite when its predual is separable).
Then every group homomorphism `ρ : E → U(W)` satisfies `ρ(w) = 1`.

Equivalently: every character of `E` whose GNS closure is injective — every
uniformly amenable trace on `C*(E)` in Brown's sense (Theorem 3.2.2 of
N. P. Brown, *Invariant means and finite representation theory of
C\*-algebras*, Mem. AMS 184 (2006), no. 865) — is trivial on `w`.

**Consequences.**

* `E` does not embed in `U(R)`; no quotient of `E` in which `w` survives
  embeds in `U(R)`; so `E` cannot witness
  `non-mf-subgroup-of-hyperfinite-factor-unitary-group`, and the route
  `non-mf-unitary-subgroup-via-literal-group` is dead.  Together with
  `kazhdan-groups-in-hyperfinite-unitary-group-are-rf` and
  `clifford-witness-has-no-hyperfinite-marked-representation`, every non-MF
  group of the one-sided-compression family has its non-MF certificate
  killed by hyperfinite representations.
* `E` is sofic (`literal-group-sofic`), so `E ↪ U(R^ω)` with `w ≠ 1`, while
  `E` does not embed in `U(R)`: the sign survives the ultrapower of `R` and
  no finite injective algebra.  The proof breaks in `R^ω` exactly at
  injectivity.
* The theorem is the Hilbert--Schmidt-exact counterpart of the manuscript's
  `literal-central-mark-corona-invisible`, with a different mechanism: the
  corona argument needs property (T) of the base and finiteness of matrix
  algebras; this argument needs only that `SL_3(Z)` acts on `T^3` with null
  fixed sets and is not amenable.

**Mechanism (details in the proof route).**  With `ρ(w) = −1` the eight
lamps of one block are pairwise anticommuting involutions and generate a
unital copy of `M_16`; the level-one translations permute those eight
generators without fixed points, so their implementing unitaries have
trace zero, and `χ = τ ∘ ρ` vanishes on the non-integral half-translations.
Conjugating by `t` spreads this to every nonzero translation, so
`ρ(Z^3)'' ≅ L^∞(T^3)` with Haar trace.  The conditional expectation of
`ρ(m)`, `m ∈ SL_3(Z) \ {1}`, onto that abelian algebra intertwines the
identity with the action of `m`, hence is supported on the Haar-null fixed
set of `m` and vanishes.  So `χ|_B = δ_e`, `ρ(B)'' ≅ L(B)` with `B`
nonamenable, inside the injective `W`: impossible.
