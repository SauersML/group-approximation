title: The standard large odd two-generator Burnside group has positive edge expansion
---

Imported Adian nonamenability, recorded in Donoso-Echenique–Silva, introduction. With odd n>=1003, B(2,n) is nonamenable. Its standard degree-four Cayley graph has an edge expansion constant h_n>0. No effective numerical value or exponent-uniform lower bound is asserted.
