Let `G` be of type `F_∞` with finite cohomological dimension and not of type F
(`some-f-infinity-group-of-finite-cd-is-not-of-type-f`). By the backward
direction of `g-times-z-type-f-iff-g-f-infinity-with-finite-cd`, `G × Z` is of
type F. So `G` is a group with `G × Z` of type F and `G` not of type F, the
negative answer to Problem 1.21.
