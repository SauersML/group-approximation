title: Two commuting infinite subgroups of SL_3(Z) generate a polycyclic group
distinct_from:
  sl3z-infinite-order-centralizers-are-solvable: that is the element-level centralizer statement; this is its consequence for pairs of commuting infinite subgroups, including direct products
---

If `A, B <= SL_3(Z)` are infinite and `[A, B] = 1`, then `<A, B>` is
polycyclic. In particular `SL_3(Z)` contains no subgroup isomorphic to
`Z x F_2`, to `F_2 x F_2`, or to `A x B` with `A` infinite and `B` not
virtually solvable.

So the mechanism that makes `SL_n(Z)` incoherent for `n >= 4`, Stallings'
finitely generated, not finitely presented subgroup of `F_2 x F_2`, is not
available in `SL_3(Z)`: see the refuted claim `sl3z-contains-f2-times-f2` and
the dead route `sl3z-incoherent-via-f2-times-f2`.
