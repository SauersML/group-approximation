Source: Shintaro Nishikawa and Nansen Petrosyan,
*Dehn fillings, equivariant homology, and the Baum--Connes conjecture*,
arXiv:2509.15070v2, Theorem C (Theorem 5.2).

Definition 1.2 says that a countable discrete group `G` satisfies the
Baum--Connes conjecture with coefficients with finite wreath products
precisely when `G wr F` satisfies Baum--Connes with coefficients for every
finite group `F`. Theorem 5.2 states that this property holds for every
hyperbolic group.

The proof strengthens Lafforgue's Baum--Connes-with-coefficients theorem.
The technical input constructs gamma elements for wreath products and
shows that the controlled equality `gamma_G=1` passes to the wreath
product. Thus the source proves the finite-wreath statement itself, not
merely Baum--Connes with coefficients for `G`. QED
