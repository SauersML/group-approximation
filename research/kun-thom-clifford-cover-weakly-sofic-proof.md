Every finitely generated subgroup of `Vtilde_S` is generated by finitely many
`c_u` together with the central involution `z`, and the defining relations
make it a finite `2`-group.  So `Vtilde_S` is locally finite, hence LEF,
hence sofic, hence weakly sofic.  It is normal with quotient `G`, residually
finite by Kun--Thom Theorem E (`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath` carries the
verbatim statement).  Glebsky, Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 39 (2023) 1097--1104,
Theorem 1.1 -- normal weakly sofic kernel with residually finite quotient
implies weakly sofic -- then applies; the theorem is quoted in full from the
published PDF in `weakly-sofic-not-sofic-proof`.

Nonsoficity is the established chain: `invariant-graph-clifford-phase` gives
the surjection from the free-lamp amalgam sending the marked word to
`z^(b_S(o,y))=z!=1`, and `kun-thom-clifford-radical-phase` places that word in
the sofic radical, which every homomorphism to a sofic group kills.

The wall consequence is Theorem 3 of the cited note read in the target group
of the ultraproduct, using that Lemma 1 and Theorems 2 and 3 there are proved
for an arbitrary target group.  Complete argument in the cited note,
Theorem 9 and Corollary 10.
