For the generation statement, put `w_n(k)=popcount(k) mod 2`.  If a cyclic
binary word of length `2^n` has a period, its least period divides `2^n`.
For every `a<n`, however,

```text
w_n(0)=0 != 1=w_n(2^a).
```

Thus `w_n` is aperiodic.  For distinct coordinates `i,j`, some cyclic
translate of `w_n` distinguishes them; otherwise `j-i` would be a period.
The Boolean algebra generated by the translated subsets is therefore the
full power set of the finite coordinate set.  Its characteristic functions
are precisely the diagonal algebra generated by
`P_n^k D_n P_n^(-k)`.  This gives every `E_(ii)`, and
`E_(ii)P_n^(i-j)E_(jj)=E_(ij)` gives the full matrix algebra.

Property `(T)` then has exactly the required finite-quotient consequence:
the fixed Kazhdan set in `EL_3(F_2<x,y>)` has a uniform gap in every
nontrivial subrepresentation of the permutation representation of
`SL_(3D)(F_2)` on its nonzero natural vectors.  Transitivity identifies the
invariant vectors with constants.  This statement is only linear; a Pauli
actor still needs a symplectic doubling/completion.

For the compatibility obstruction, Skolem--Noether is not needed.  Assuming
`(AMH1)`, the matrix

```text
C=(G_n^(-1) tensor I_2)G_(n+1)
```

commutes with every `a tensor I_2`.  The elementary matrix units show
directly that `C=I_(2^n) tensor Q_n`, proving `(AMH2)`.  Induction makes every
compatible normalizer a tensor product of two-dimensional factors.  Products
of any number of such normalizers are still tensor products, so the whole
actor preserves the Segre set of decomposable tensors.  The vectors

```text
e_0 tensor e_0,       e_0 tensor e_0 + e_1 tensor e_1
```

already lie in different orbits at level two.

Finally, in the standard matrix units of a self-similar groupoid algebra,
write the action of a tree automorphism on a cylinder as

```text
g(uw)=g(u) (g|_u)(w).
```

Applying this formula on the source and range of `E_(u,v)` gives `(AMH3)`.
Pure head normalization therefore forces all level sections to agree.  The
odometer recursion has one identity section and one odometer section at
every level, so it violates this condition.  This locates the compiler gap
at an exact tail-state multiplier rather than at the already-solved finite
quotient spectral gap.
