ESTABLISHED as a reduction.  Let

```text
C=SL_3(Z) < A=SL_3(Z[1/2]),       X=A/C,
L=direct_sum_X C_2,                W=L rtimes A.             (AGP1)
```

Assume that a matrix microstate sequence has been rounded to one exact
finite-support spectral PVM for `L`, while the actor generators transport
that PVM with vanishing normalized-HS energy.  Then the PVM atoms, repeated
according to their matrix ranks, form a finite weighted model of the dual
action

```text
A acting on L_hat={-1,1}^X.                                 (AGP2)
```

Canonical trace tests on every fixed finite lamp window say that the
empirical atom measures converge cylinderwise to Haar product measure.
Actor covariance says that the finite weighted transports are
asymptotically equivariant.  Thus the enemy remaining after global PVM
rounding is precisely a finite atomic approximation of the generalized
Bernoulli/coset action, not an exact finite `A`-action.

Co-density does kill the **exact** version.  If a character has a finite
exact `A`-orbit, the core `N` of its stabilizer has finite index; `CN=A`
makes `N` transitive on `A/C`, so the character is constant and every
two-point augmentation seed is invisible.  This does not kill the
asymptotically equivariant finite atomic models above.  Turning those models
into exact finite quotient actions is an additional stability/correctability
statement.

At the von Neumann level the identification is literal:

```text
L(W) = L^infinity({-1,1}^(A/C)) rtimes A.                    (AGP3)
```

It follows by Fourier transform on the abelian lamp group.  Hence
hyperlinearity of `W` is exactly Connes embeddability of the generalized
Bernoulli crossed product.  A global PVM decoder forces a Cartan-shaped
finite-stage representative of a hypothetical embedding, but it does not
by itself prove that no such representative exists.

The arithmetic transverse packets therefore do not presently bypass the
old gate.  They would eliminate it only after proving one of the existing
open approximate-to-exact transfer statements, such as
`sl3z-regular-microstates-are-congruence-correctable` or
`projective-commutant-transfer-for-arithmetic-pair`.  Those are precisely
the matrix-coordinate assertions which rule out the finite atomic enemy.
