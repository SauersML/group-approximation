separated.  For an escaping family of endpoint corrections this produces

`finite-mark-centralizer-commutator-area-is-proper` therefore diverges with
the endpoint correction.  No bounded post-hoc R1--R5 filling exists for this
construction.  This invalidates only the separate-transport strategy; it
