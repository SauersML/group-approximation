Let `K_f` be the phase-root packet of
`every-predicate-has-s3-equivariant-raw-radical-drop`, and put

```text
D_0=2^(2N).                                                    (RTC1)
```

Choose linear functionals `ell_1,ell_2` on the noncentral generator space
whose restrictions to every forbidden radical are the dual basis, and put
`ell_3=ell_1+ell_2`.  Each assignment

```text
tau_i(g_v)=J^(ell_i(v))g_v                                   (RTC2)
```

fixing the center is an involutive group automorphism.  Form the three finite
ordinary semidirect products

```text
R_i=K_f semidirect_(tau_i) C_2.                               (RTC3)
```

For every marked selector sector `x`, the sector algebra of every `R_i` is

```text
M_(D_0)(C) direct_sum M_(D_0)(C).                             (RTC4)
```

On a satisfying sector, the `K_f` algebra is one `M_(D_0)` block and the
central twist is inner, so the two summands in `(RTC4)` are its two extension
types.  On a forbidden sector, the `K_f` algebra is four
`M_(D_0/2)` blocks labeled by the two raw radical signs.  The nonzero
translation `ell_i` pairs them into two free orbits; crossing each orbit by
`C_2` gives one `M_(D_0)` block.

The lifted `S3` symmetry conjugates `tau_1,tau_2,tau_3`, so the three groups
in `(RTC3)`, including their embeddings of `K_f`, form three conjugate
fixed-scale charts.  On a forbidden atom their restriction matrices are the
three perfect matchings of the four raw radical types.  Consequently local
finite-group exactification and shared-subgroup polar alignment compare all
three charts with constants depending only on the fixed predicate packet.
