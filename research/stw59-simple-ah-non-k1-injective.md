There are a separable, simple, unital, nuclear, stably finite, infinite-dimensional
AH algebra A and v in U(A) such that K1(A)=0, v is not in U0(A), and v^2
is in U0(A). Moreover diag(v,1_A) is null-homotopic in U(M2(A)).

Explicitly, r_i=2^(i+1), X_i=S4 times product_(j<i) CP^(r_j),
E_i=C2 direct-sum direct-sum_(j<i) L_(j+1)^(direct-sum r_j), and
A_i=Gamma(End(E_i)). A is the limit of the unital injective twisted maps
a -> pr_i^*a direct-sum (a(x_i) tensor 1_(L_(i+1))), where the
projections of the points x_i have dense tails in every earlier base.
The witness is the image of a generator S4 -> U(2) of pi4(U(2))=Z/2.

This negates the universal assertion in STW Problem LIX. It asserts an
order-two subgroup of U(A)/U0(A), not a calculation of the entire group.
