Full proof in the artifact, §2–3. Outline:

1. **Scalars.** Under `R_R ≅ R_R^d`, `x ↦ (t_i x)_i`, the scalar `λ` becomes `λ I_d`. So `[λ] = [λ]^d` and
   `[λ]^(d−1) = 1`. Hence `[−1] = 1` when `d` is even or `−1` is a `(d−1)`-th power.
2. **Transpositions.** A transposition on a clopen `A` made of `m` cylinders acts on
   `1_A R ⊕ 1_(α(A))R ⊕ 1_W R` as a swap plus the identity. That has the class of `1 − 2·1_A = ∏(1 − 2e_(w_i))`, and
   each factor has class `[−1]` via `e_w R ≅ R`. So `κ(α) = [−1]^m`.
3. **Generation.** `V_d = [[𝒢_d]]` is generated by transpositions (Matui, `H_1 = 0`, Lemma `Tgenerate`). The swap of
   `Z(1)` and `Z(2)` has `m = 1`, so `κ(V_d) = ⟨[−1]⟩`.
4. **MF quotients.** Every homomorphism to an MF group factors through `κ`, and `ker κ = [H,H]`
   (`purely-infinite-unit-group-max-mf-quotient-is-k1`). That gives items 1, 2 and 4. For item 3, `[−1] ≠ 1` comes
   from ABC Cor 7.7 with the vertex-inclusion map, and the kernel is `D(V_d)` by Matui's abelianization `Z/2`.
