For every odd integer `n>=3`, the full exact
`BS(1,4)`-plus-involution/two-cubic Iwahori presentation has a finite-
dimensional unitary representation in which the marked unipotent has order
exactly `n`. One may take the left regular representation of the finite
congruence image

```text
Q_n=im(PSL_2(Z[1/2])->PSL_2(Z/n)).                      (UIT1)
```

The reduction of the root matrix `u(1)` has order `n`, and the regular
action is faithful. All skeleton, involution, Weyl, and cubic relations hold
literally because the marked matrices are reductions of the arithmetic
generators.

Consequently the full relations imply neither a uniform bound on the odd
root order, containment of its spectrum in one fixed finite set, one fixed
nonzero polynomial `q` with `q(U)=0` in every exact packet, nor factorization
through congruence levels in a bounded finite family.

This does not contradict the two positive qualitative facts. Exact
`BS(1,4)` covariance alone makes the finite spectrum a union of periodic
fourth-power orbits, hence odd torsion; and the full exact arithmetic
presentation makes each finite-dimensional representation factor through
some odd congruence quotient. What fails is every **uniform level-selection
strengthening**. Thus exactifying the BS skeleton cannot hand the remaining
cubic/Weyl repair to a fixed torsion stratum: a successful noncongruence
compiler must authenticate a moving level or repair without naming it.
