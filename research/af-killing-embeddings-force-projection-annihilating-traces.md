**ESTABLISHED (Theorem A of the artifact; lane proof, not externally reviewed).**
Let `A` be a C*-algebra, `G ⊆ K_0(A)` a subgroup, `B` an AF algebra and
`φ : A -> B` a *-homomorphism with `φ_*(G) = 0`.  For every projection
`P ∈ M_n(A)` with `φ(P) ≠ 0` there is a lower semicontinuous trace `σ` on `A`
with `σ(P) = 1` that *annihilates `G` on its finiteness ideal*: with `I_σ` the
closed ideal generated by `{a ≥ 0 : σ(a) < ∞}` and `ι_σ : K_0(I_σ) -> K_0(A)`,
`σ^` vanishes on `ι_σ^{-1}(G)`.  This includes `∂(K_1(A/I_σ))`, so the pairing is
well defined.

Mechanism: pull back `ρ^∞ ∘ φ`, where `ρ` extends a tracial state of the unital
AF corner `φ(P) M_n(B) φ(P)` and is `+∞` off the ideal generated by `φ(P)`.
Annihilation follows from `K_1` of AF quotients vanishing, which makes
`K_0(J) -> K_0(B)` injective.

The same proof works for any target in which every nonzero projection has a
unital corner with a tracial state and every quotient has `K_1 = 0`.  It gives
nothing for quasidiagonal targets whose quotients carry `K_1`.
