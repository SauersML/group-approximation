**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `Y=(Y_1,...,Y_s)` be a finite tuple of unitaries on a
finite-dimensional Hilbert space and let `Q_-` be a projection reducing
`Y`.  Suppose

```text
Q<=Q_-,                 [Q,Y_j]=0,
A<=Q,
D=UAU^*<=Q_-                                             (RHC1)
```

for a unitary `U`.  The projection `A` need not reduce `Y`, and `U` need not
normalize the algebra generated by `Y`.

Let `Q'` be the projection onto the smallest common reducing subspace for
`Y` containing `Ran(Q join D)`.  Then

```text
Q<=Q'<=Q_-,             [Q',Y_j]=0,                    (RHC2)
tau(Q')>=tau(Q)+tau(A)-tau(QD).                         (RHC3)
```

Consequently, an overlap estimate

```text
tau(QD)<=(1-eta)tau(A)+epsilon                          (RHC4)
```

immediately gives

```text
tau(Q')>=tau(Q)+eta tau(A)-epsilon.                    (RHC5)
```

There is no additional successor-carrier compatibility theorem to prove:
the reducing hull itself is the next carrier.  In particular, if `A=QB_f`
for the role-packed forbidden idempotent and

```text
tau(QB_f)>=(beta/m)tau(Q)-epsilon_f,
```

then

```text
tau(Q')>=(1+eta beta/m)tau(Q)-epsilon-eta epsilon_f.    (RHC6)
```

The same conclusion has the fixed-presentation normalized-HS form.  First
correct the marked finite packet and the finitely many context tuples.  If a
word transport has `O(sqrt(E))` leakage outside the corrected `Q_-`, polar
and spectral correction of its compressed range changes its trace and the
overlap in `(RHC4)` by `O(sqrt(E))`.  Applying the exact statement afterward
only changes the error constant, never the matrix-dimension dependence.

This theorem does not construct `(RHC4)`.  It removes only the purported
second half of the unary decoder.  The remaining groupification problem is
one payload-sensitive overlap-loss row on each of finitely many reached
carriers.
