For the Laurent elementary group compressor in the Kun--Thom construction,
every finite-dimensional unitary representation `rho` satisfies
`rho(Lambda)=rho(E)`.  Hence the compression is invisible in all exact finite
dimensions; only genuinely approximate representations can obstruct relative
commutant collapse.
