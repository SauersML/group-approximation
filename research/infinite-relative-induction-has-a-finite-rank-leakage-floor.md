**ESTABLISHED.** Let \(\Gamma\) be a nonamenable group generated by a finite
symmetric set \(S\), let \(B<\Gamma\) be amenable, and let
\(\beta:B\to U(H_\beta)\) be any unitary representation. Put

\[
 \pi=\operatorname{Ind}_B^\Gamma\beta .
\]

Write \(\lambda_\Gamma\) for the left regular representation and normalize
its \(S\)-gap by

\[
 \kappa_S=
 \inf_{0\ne\xi\in\ell^2\Gamma}
 {1\over |S|\|\xi\|^2}
 \sum_{s\in S}\|\lambda_\Gamma(s)\xi-\xi\|^2>0.          \tag{IFL1}
\]

For every nonzero finite-rank orthogonal projection \(P\) on \(H_\pi\),

\[
 {1\over |S|\operatorname{rank}P}
 \sum_{s\in S}\|(1-P)\pi(s)P\|_{\mathrm F}^2
 \ \geq\ {\kappa_S\over2}.                              \tag{IFL2}
\]

Here \(\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm F}\) is the **unnormalized** Frobenius norm, and
the denominator in (IFL2) is the finite corner rank. Thus (IFL2) is a
normalized-trace leakage bound on the proposed finite corner; it is not an
ambient trace statement, since the induced space is infinite-dimensional.

The bound is independent of \(\beta\), \(\dim H_\beta\), and
\(\operatorname{rank}P\). In particular \(\pi\) has no nonzero
finite-dimensional invariant subrepresentation and admits no sequence of
finite-rank cuts whose generator leakage tends to zero after normalization
by the cut rank.

There is also a direct compression consequence. On \(PH_\pi\), put

\[
 C_s=P\pi(s)P|_{PH_\pi}.
\]

If \(U_s\in U(PH_\pi)\) and
\(\eta_s=\|C_s-U_s\|_{\mathrm F}/\sqrt{\operatorname{rank}P}\leq1\), then

\[
 {1\over\operatorname{rank}P}\|(1-P)\pi(s)P\|_{\mathrm F}^2
 =1-{\|C_s\|_{\mathrm F}^2\over\operatorname{rank}P}
 \leq 2\eta_s.                                         \tag{IFL3}
\]

Consequently no finite-rank compression of \(\pi\) can have all its marked
coefficients converge in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm to unitaries:
(IFL2)--(IFL3) force

\[
 \max_{s\in S}\eta_s\geq {\kappa_S\over4}.               \tag{IFL4}
\]

For the Iwahori lane,

\[
 \Gamma=PSL_2(\mathbb Z[1/2]),\qquad
 B=\langle r,t\mid rtr^{-1}=t^4\rangle\cong
 \mathbb Z[1/4]\rtimes\mathbb Z .
\]

The subgroup \(B\) is amenable and \(\Gamma\) is nonamenable. Hence
(IFL2) applies to every
\(\operatorname{Ind}_B^\Gamma\beta\), including every finite-dimensional
core \(\beta\) with \(\beta(t)^N=1\).

This closes one tempting induction/Stinespring shortcut. The finite
three-sheet vertex cells may be assembled inside the natural infinite
relative induced model, but one cannot then extract a low-leakage finite
host by a Følner cut, spectral cut, or arbitrary finite-rank compression.
The viable host must instead be constructed **after quotient descent** in
the finite representation
\(\operatorname{Ind}_{B_N}^{A_N}\beta\), or equivalently from a complete
relative positive-definite table. That construction must control the full
translated normal-row packet; local core multiplicative-domain data do not
supply it.

This is a proof-method firewall, not a counterexample to the optimized
Iwahori energy estimate. It leaves open whether the concrete two cubic rows,
after moving the exact core, force the required finite \(A_N\)-host.

The obstruction is sharp at the group-theoretic level. If \(\Gamma\) is
amenable and \(B=\{e\}\), diagonal projections onto Følner sets in
\(\lambda_\Gamma\) make the left side of (IFL2) tend to zero. Thus the
finite-rank floor is exactly the nonamenability obstruction to truncating
the regular-like induced host.
