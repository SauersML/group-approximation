title: An integer power series at infinity that continues outside a compact set of capacity below one is rational
---

**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `K` be a compact subset of `C` with
connected complement and transfinite diameter (logarithmic capacity)
`cap(K) < 1`. Suppose `f(w) = sum_(k>=0) a_k w^(-k)`, with every `a_k in Z`,
converges for large `|w|` and continues to a single-valued holomorphic function
on the Riemann sphere minus `K`. Then `f` is a rational function.

A segment of length `l` has capacity `l/4`, so the theorem applies to every
real interval of length below `4`.

This is Pólya's extension of Borel's rationality theorem, replacing a disc with
a compact set of transfinite diameter below one. Dwork, Bertrandias, Robinson
and Cantor extended it further.
