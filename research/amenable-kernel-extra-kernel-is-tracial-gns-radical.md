In the setting of
[trace factorization](amenable-kernel-quotient-traces-factor-reduced.md),
put E=ker(q̄:D→C_r^*(Q)). Then

```text
E = intersection_{ρ∈T(D)} ker π_ρ.
```

Every *-homomorphism from D into an algebra with a faithful tracial
state kills E, including nonunital maps and finite-dimensional
representations. This is an algebra/GNS-kernel statement, not E=0 or
vanishing of its K-theory. See Corollary 3.2 of the
[proof document](artifacts/Cairn_Baum_Connes_General_Attempt.md#corollary-32-the-extra-kernel-is-the-tracial-gns-radical).
