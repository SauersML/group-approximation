title: Jiang--Su embedding uniqueness is closed under arbitrary direct products of codomains
---

Let `(B_j)_(j in J)` be any family of unital C\*-algebras such that every two
unital embeddings `Z->B_j` are approximately unitarily equivalent.  Then the
same is true for

```text
B = product_(j in J) B_j.
```

No separability, uniform stable rank, or tensorial absorption assumption on
the product is required.  In particular arbitrary products of codomains in
any known positive class for LXIII form a further positive class.
