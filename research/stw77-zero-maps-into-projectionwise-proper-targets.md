title: Zero-dimensional maps into projectionwise properly infinite targets are O-infinity-stable
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/stw77-map-oinfty-audit-2026-08-30.md
---

Let `A` be separable, let `B` be unital, and let `phi:A->B` be a unital
homomorphism of nuclear dimension zero.  Suppose every nonzero projection of
`B` is properly infinite.  Then `phi` is O-infinity-stable.

No proper-infiniteness hypothesis on the nonzero positive elements of
`phi(A)` is needed.  In particular this proves the zero-dimensional case of
STW LXXVII for every target with projectionwise proper infiniteness.
