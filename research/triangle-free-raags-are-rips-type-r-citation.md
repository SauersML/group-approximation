Citation import, not a reproof. Kevin Li and Luis Jorge Sánchez Saldaña,
*Contractible Rips complexes of groups via metric gluings*, arXiv:2608.24279
(v1, 25 August 2026).

- Definition: "A marked group (G,S) is of type ℛ if there exists r_0∈ℝ_≥0
  such that for all r∈ℝ with r≥r_0, the Rips complex R_r(G,S) is
  contractible."
- Theorem 1.5: "Let Γ be a finite simplicial graph without triangles. Then the
  right-angled Artin group (A_Γ,Γ^(0)) is of type ℛ."
- Method: successive metric gluings of squares in the Salvetti cover. Their
  Lemma 2.1 passes contractibility to increasing unions, and their Corollary
  2.5 makes the inclusion of glued Rips complexes a homotopy equivalence at
  scale `r ≥ 2` when the gluing locus lies within distance 1 of one of its
  points.

Read from the arXiv abstract page and the HTML rendering of v1 on 2026-09-13.
The paper is an unrefereed preprint, and the proof was not re-derived here.
