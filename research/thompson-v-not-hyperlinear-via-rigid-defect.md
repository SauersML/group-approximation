**Dead.** If `hyperlinear-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects` held and some
infranormal Kazhdan pair inside `V` had a nontrivial rigid defect, `V` would
be nonhyperlinear. The second premise is refuted, and the route is
invalidated, by `thompson-v-has-no-rigid-compression-defect`. The hyperlinear
defect criterion can only reach `V` through a larger group such as the
Leavitt configuration, and nonhyperlinearity of that group does not descend
to `V`.
