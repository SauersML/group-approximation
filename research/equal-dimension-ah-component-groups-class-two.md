Let A be a unital inductive limit of finite direct sums of blocks
Gamma(X, End E) with X a connected finite CW complex and
dim X <= 2·rank E. Then U(A)/U0(A) is nilpotent of class at most two.

For dim X < 2·rank E the block component group equals K^1(X). In the
equal case it is a central extension of K^1(X) by a quotient of
H^(2r)(X;Z). Direct limits preserve class at most two. So class three or
more needs cofinally many blocks above twice the rank; every LIX-type tower
built so far is excluded.
