Lagrange filters the official `McL` maximal list to `(MCLA8-1)`.  The
required established input already excludes `A8` from `U4(3)` and `M22`.
The remaining four structural types are removed directly by, respectively,
the wrong index-two socle, nonsplitting of the double cover, and projection
through the normal elementary-abelian subgroup.  Hence `McL` has no `A8`.
