**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Let `H <= Aut(T_d)` be self-similar.

1. **(FP1, Scott.)** If `H` is finitely presented, then `V_d(H)` is finitely presented.
2. **(FP2, Nekrashevych.)** If `H` is contracting, then `V_d(H)` is finitely presented.
   - `H` need not be finitely presented: the nucleus generates a contracting self-similar group with the same Rover--Nekrashevych group.
3. **(S)** `[V_d(H),V_d(H)]` is simple for every self-similar `H`.
4. **(Ab)** For even `d`, `V_d(H)^ab` is `H^ab` modulo `g = g_1 + ... + g_d` for each wreath recursion `g = sigma(g_1,...,g_d)`. For odd `d` one adds a `Z/2Z` summand and the term `sgn(sigma)`.
5. **(FP3)** Finite presentation of `V_d(H)` does not by itself give finite presentation of `[V_d(H),V_d(H)]`. If `V_d(H)^ab` is finite, the commutator subgroup has finite index, so it is finitely presented whenever `V_d(H)` is.
6. **(R, regrouping.)** Let `H` be finitely generated by `a_1,...,a_n`.
   - The recursion `g -> sigma^m(g_1,...,g_d, ..., g_1,...,g_d)`, with `m` blocks, is a faithful self-similar action of `H` on `T_(md)` with the same sections.
   - Let `A` be the integer matrix whose `i`-th column is the image of `(a_i)_1 + ... + (a_i)_d`.
   - For even `m` with `1/m` not an eigenvalue of `A`, `V_(md)(H)^ab` is finite.
   - Zaremsky combines this with FP1, S and a wreath-product embedding. Every finitely presented self-similar `H` then embeds in the finitely presented simple group `[V_(md)(H),V_(md)(H)]`.

## Answer to "which H give a finitely presented V_d(H)"

- **Known sufficient conditions.** The imported ones are FP1 (`H` finitely presented) and FP2 (`H` contracting).
- **Higher finiteness.** Zaremsky also states that his proof embeds every self-similar group of type `F_n` in a simple group of type `F_n`.
- **No characterization.** No necessary-and-sufficient condition is imported, and none is claimed. The sources contain no germ-level criterion.
- **The commutator host.** A finitely presented simple commutator host needs, beyond FP1 or FP2, a representation with finite abelianization. That is what (R) supplies.

Finite presentation is not what stops the Rover--Nekrashevych route to the full conjecture. Property (T) does:
`deligne-lattice-embeds-in-no-rover-nekrashevych-group`.
