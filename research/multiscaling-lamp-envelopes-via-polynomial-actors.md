The first prerequisite supplies the full multiscaling family of faithful
oligomorphic clopen actors, each of type `F_∞` with all finite-subset
stabilizers of type `F_∞`. It explicitly includes the affine base,
all polynomial layers, and all rational marked stabilizers needed for
the finiteness induction. Thus the conditional multiscaling clause of
the second prerequisite has its actual mathematical hypotheses.

Use that clause to embed vector eventual-polynomial lamps in a two-root
actor. Fixed residue classes handle period `h` via finite products, and
the finite actor extension construction restores all shifts. Global
polynomial profiles are subgroups of the eventual profile groups.
Further finite products and finite extensions use the same disjoint
union and finite wreath actor constructions. For a finite-index
overgroup, embed the normal core in the product actor and apply the
finite-quotient construction.

Finally apply Belk–Zaremsky, arXiv:2001.04579, Theorem D and the
manuscript's cited Theorem 3.4 for the simple type `F_∞` host and two
finite-order generators. These are explicit classical inputs, not
Lean certification. Both new mathematical prerequisites remain OPEN;
this route establishes nothing unconditionally.
