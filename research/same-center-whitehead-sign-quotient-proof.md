The first prerequisite identifies the common active packet as three Pauli
pairs and computes the actions of `J_1,J_2` as the two adjacent
transpositions.  The Pauli subgroup `E` is normal under those actions.
Quotienting by `E` therefore gives the permutation group generated by
`(12),(23)`, namely `S_3`.  Composing with its sign homomorphism proves
`(SQS2)` and the even-parity assertion.

For the finite model, let `E` act by the standard three-qubit Pauli
matrices.  Let `S_12,S_23` permute tensor factors and replace each by its
negative.  Squaring removes the sign, conjugation of every Pauli matrix is
unchanged, and

```text
(-S_12)(-S_23)(-S_12)
 =-S_12S_23S_12
 =-S_23S_12S_23
 =(-S_23)(-S_12)(-S_23).
```

Thus all packet identities are exact, while the operator-norm difference
from either untwisted swap is exactly two.  Tensoring with arbitrary finite
multiplicity proves the profile-uniform statement.

Finally the explicit commutators in
`middle-index-heisenberg-packet-is-not-center-chain-invariant` show that the
center-chain factors in `(SQS5)` do not belong to this normalizer packet.
The order-seven relations are therefore genuine outside-actor relations,
not counterexamples to the sign quotient.  Compressing them back to the
same-center carrier introduces precisely the boundary terms isolated by
the existing order-seven compression and moving-cut claims.
