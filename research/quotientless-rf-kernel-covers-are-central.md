**ESTABLISHED** through `quotientless-rf-kernel-covers-are-central-proof`. The ingredients are classical
(Baumslag, Mal'cev, Hall, Schur); no priority is claimed. It is recorded as a class kill for
`torsion-free-sofic-quotientless-kazhdan-group-exists` (SQK).

**Setting.** `E` is a finitely generated group with no nontrivial finite quotient, and `N ◁ E`. Write
`Q = E/N` and `c: E -> Aut(N)` for the conjugation action.

**(QC1) Centrality.** `c` is trivial, so `N <= Z(E)` and `N` is abelian, in each of these cases:

- (a) `N` is finitely generated and residually finite;
- (b) `N` is torsion-free abelian of finite rank;
- (c) `N` is torsion-free nilpotent of finite Hirsch length.

**(QC2) Outer version.** Suppose the induced map `E -> Out(N)` is trivial. This holds, for example,
when `N` is finitely generated and `Out(N)` is residually finite. Then `E = N · C_E(N)` and
`Q ≅ C_E(N)/Z(N)`, with `Z(N)` central in `C_E(N)`.

**(QC3) Torsion-free covers.** Suppose also that `E` is torsion-free, and `N` is as in (QC1) or (QC2).
Then every finite subgroup of `Q` is abelian. In (QC1), if `N` has rank `n` (for (a), `N ≅ Z^n`), every
finite subgroup of `Q` is abelian and generated by at most `n` elements. In (QC2) with `Z(N) = 1`, `Q` is
torsion-free.

**(QC4) The sources that die.** Let `n >= 3`, let `R` be a nonzero unital ring, and let `Z` be any central
subgroup of `EL_n(R)`. Then `EL_n(R)/Z` contains a finite subgroup mapping onto `S_n`. So no group
containing `EL_n(R)/Z` is `E/N` for a torsion-free, finitely generated, quotientless `E` and an `N` as in
(QC1) or (QC2). This covers every elementary source on the SQK cone:

- `EL_3(LC(X,F_q) ⋊ Z)` (`lef-kazhdan-group-without-finite-quotients-exists`);
- `Q_0 = EL_3(LC(X,Z) ⋊ Z)` (`char-zero-subshift-elementary-group-is-lef-kazhdan-quotientless`);
- the simple groups `EL_N(A)/Z` of `odometer-nekrashevych-elementary-simple-kazhdan-not-mf`,
  `paradoxical-steinberg-elementary-simple-kazhdan-not-mf` and
  `paradoxical-cantor-actions-give-simple-kazhdan-not-mf`;
- the Leavitt and Kun--Thom elementary groups.

## The killed class and where it dies

**Class (torsion-killing covers).** Build an SQK witness `E` as a cover `E ->> Q` of a known LEF or
sofic quotientless Kazhdan group `Q` with torsion. The kernel `N` is chosen small, so that soficity has a
chance to lift. "Small" means finitely generated and residually finite (for example `Z^k`, polycyclic,
free, surface, f.g. linear), or finite-rank abelian or nilpotent. Covers with `(E, N)` relative (T) and
central `N`, such as Deligne-type or Euler-class covers, are included.

**Invariant.** The isomorphism types of finite subgroups of `Q`.

**Death step.** Quotientlessness of `E` makes the action on `N` trivial: the image of `E` is finitely
generated and residually finite (Baumslag or Mal'cev), and it is a quotient of `E`. So the cover is
central, and Schur's theorem, via `torsion-free-central-cover-forces-abelian-finite-subgroups`, makes
every finite subgroup of `Q` abelian. Every elementary source contains signed permutation matrices
mapping onto `S_3`.

**What survives.** A torsion-free quotientless cover of an elementary source must have a kernel `N`
outside (QC1)--(QC2). So `E -> Out(N)` is nontrivial, and `N` is neither finitely generated residually
finite nor torsion-free nilpotent of finite rank. Two natural shapes of such kernels:

- (i) not finitely generated and of infinite rank, e.g. a relation module `R/[R,R]`, which carries a
  nontrivial action;
- (ii) kernels such that `E` acts on `N` by outer automorphisms, through a nontrivial quotientless image in
  `Out(N)`. For finitely generated `N` this forces `Aut(N)` and `Out(N)` to be non-residually-finite, so
  `N` itself is not residually finite.

In (i), with `N` torsion-free abelian, the only recorded soficity mechanism (the permutation-module
envelope) dies by `relation-cover-sofic-envelope-forces-trivial-kernel`. In (ii), the kernel carries an
outer action of a group with no finite quotients. Producing such a kernel is a source problem of the same
kind as SQK.
