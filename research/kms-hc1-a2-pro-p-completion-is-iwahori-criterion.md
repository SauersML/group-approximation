title: The pro-p completion of the A~2 root-word quotient is the Iwahori iff H^2 restricts injectively to the three vertex groups
---

Let `p` be an odd prime. Let `Gamma=Gamma^A2_p` be the triangle quotient from
`kms-hc1-root-word-infinite-in-a2-quotient`, with `r=[b,a]`, `s=[b,c]` and
`w_p=(rs)^p`. Let `I_1` be the pro-p Iwahori subgroup of `SL_3(F_p[[t]])`,
meaning the matrices that are upper unitriangular mod `t`. Define

```text
phi: a -> x_12(1),  b -> x_23(1),  c -> x_31(t).
```

Write `U_ab=<a,b>`, `U_bc=<b,c>` and `U_ca=<c,a>` for the three Heisenberg
vertex groups of order `p^3`. Let `P_k` be the lower p-central series. Then:

- **(D) Canonical map.** `phi` is a homomorphism `Gamma -> I_1` and it kills
  `w_p`. It injects on each `U_v`. Its image is dense. The p-central series
  of `I_1` is the height filtration, and the increments
  `dim P_k(I_1)/P_{k+1}(I_1)` are `3,3,2` repeating. So `phi` induces a
  surjection `phi^: Gamma^_p -> I_1` from the pro-p completion.
- **(R) Criterion.** The following are equivalent:
  1. `phi^` is an isomorphism;
  2. restriction `H^2(I_1,F_p) -> H^2(U_ab,F_p)+H^2(U_bc,F_p)+H^2(U_ca,F_p)`
     is injective;
  3. for every `k`, `|Gamma/P_k(Gamma)| = |I_1/P_k(I_1)|`.

  If they hold, every finite p-group image of `Gamma^A2_p` kills `w_p`.
- **(K) Congruence form.** Put `U_+ = SL_3(F_p[t]) cap I_1`. This is the
  group generated by the positive real affine root groups over `F_p`, and
  `phi` maps `Gamma` onto `U_+`. By the Key Lemma of Capdeboscq--Lubotzky--Rémy
  (arXiv:1508.00526), which uses the congruence subgroup property, `I_1` is the
  pro-p completion of `U_+`. Condition (R) is therefore equivalent to this:
  the kernel of `Gamma -> U_+` lies in every normal subgroup `N` of `Gamma`
  with `Gamma/N` a finite p-group. The
  amalgam theorem for `U_+` needs a 3-spherical diagram. `A~2` is not
  3-spherical, so that theorem does not give the kernel.
- **(C) Reduction to a relation count.** Let `r(I_1)=dim H^2(I_1,F_p)`. This
  is the minimal number of relations of `I_1` as a pro-p group. It is finite
  for `p>=5` by CLR Theorem 0.1. For `p=5,7,11,13` the following are equivalent:
  1. `phi^` is an isomorphism;
  2. `r(I_1)<=9`;
  3. every class in `H^2(I_1,F_p)` is inflated from the order-`p^6` quotient
     `I_1/U_{>=3}`, where `U_{>=3}=1+t.(Iwahori order)`.

  The certification lemma behind this says the following. Suppose inflation
  `H^2(I_1/P_j) -> H^2(I_1)` is onto and `|Gamma/P_{j+1}|=|I_1/P_{j+1}|`.
  Then `phi^` is an isomorphism. The lemma is proved for every odd `p`. The
  equivalence also needs `img_3=9` and the tower (T) through class 4. Here
  `img_k`, the dimension of the inflation image from `I_1/P_k`, is
  `dim H^2(I_1/P_k) - dim P_k/P_{k+1}`. Note that
  `r(I_1)>=img_3=9` always holds.
  So, for p-group images of `Gamma^A2_p`, the root-word hole is the `A~2`
  case of CLR's relation bound `r(P)<=(a^2(l+1)^2+3a(l+1))/2` at `l=2`,
  `a=1`. CLR prove that bound only for `A_l` with `l>=3`.
  If (R) holds, `Gamma^A2_p` is not residually p, since `w_p!=1`.

Computed (experiments/kms-hc1-cross-char-reps-2026-09-17):

- **(T) Towers.** For `p=5` and `p=7`,
  `|Gamma^A2_p / P_31| = p^80`. The increments are `3,3,2` repeating, exactly
  those of `I_1`. So condition 3 holds for every `k<=31`. Every p-group image
  of `Gamma^A2_p` of p-class `<=30` factors through `I_1/P_31(I_1)` and kills
  `w_p`. This extends the regularity-gate bounds (5-class `<=12`, 7-class
  `<=14`) from `kms-hc1-reflected-root-word-regularity-gate` to class 30 for
  the A2 quotient. The same `3,3,2` pattern holds with `w_p` killed for
  `p=11` through class 24 (`11^64`) and for `p=13` through class 20
  (`13^54`). These two runs stop below height `3p`. In the language of (C),
  for `p=5,7` every minimal relation of `I_1` beyond the nine from `Gamma`
  has p-central degree `>=31`.
- **(M) Multiplier check.** Via the p-covering group,
  `dim H^2(Gamma/P_k,F_p)` is `6` at `k=2`. For `k>=3` it cycles
  `11,12,12` with `k mod 3`. So `img_k=3` at `k=2` and `img_k=9` for
  `3<=k<=16` (`p=5`), `3<=k<=12` (`p=7`) and `3<=k<=8` (`p=11,13`). The case
  `k=3` is the input to
  (C). The larger `k` only cross-check (T), because on the `Gamma` side
  `img_k<=r(Gamma^)<=9` holds automatically.
- **(T3) p=3 fails.** For `p=3` the increments are
  `3,3,2,3,3,8,8,15,21,41,53,95`. They leave the `I_1` pattern at class 6,
  where `w_3` survives with order 3; it has order 9 by class 12. So for
  `p=3`, `phi^` is not an isomorphism. Some nonzero class of `H^2(I_1,F_3)`
  vanishes on all three vertex groups. The `p=3` tower agrees through class
  5 and `img_3=9` (`mu3.out`). By the certification lemma,
  `dim H^2(I_1,F_3)>=10`: the pro-3 Iwahori of `SL_3(F_3[[t]])` has no
  presentation with nine relations. So the formal `A~2` value 9 of CLR's
  bound fails at `q=3`.
- **(S) The Serre route is obstructed.** In the central extension of
  `n^+(sl_3)+t sl_3[t]` by the cocycle `omega(Xt^a,Yt^b)=a tr(XY)[a+b=p]`, the
  generators `E_12`, `E_23` and `E_31 t` satisfy the Serre relations. The
  subalgebra they generate has dimension 3 at height `3p`, while `I_1` has
  dimension 2 there. This was checked for `p=3,5,7,11`. So a graded Lie
  algebra bounded above only by the Serre relations cannot give condition 3
  past class `3p-1`. By (T), the group tower removes this central direction
  at class `3p` for `p=5,7`.

Scope. Condition (R) covers p-group images of `Gamma^A2_p` only. It says
nothing about images that are not p-groups, and nothing about `Gamma_p` itself
beyond what the B2 edge towers in the experiment directory record.

## Attempts

1. **Graded Lie-algebra cohomology (Lazard-style bound on `r(I_1)`).** This
   route is obstructed. The associated graded `L_p = n^+(sl_3)+t sl_3[t]` has
   `dim H_2(L_p)=infinity`: there is a Kassel class in every degree `3mp`, and
   `I_1` is not p-adic analytic. So no purely graded argument gives
   `r(I_1)<=9`. What survives is a deviation bound: the towers can first
   differ only at a degree in `D_p={k : H_2(L_p)_k != 0}`. Computation gives
   `D_p={3}+{3mp}`, with dimensions 6 and 1, in ranges up to degree 141 for
   `5<=p<=23`. Together with pq towers (class 50 for `p=5`, class 45 for
   `p=7`, class 35 for `p=11`), condition (R) holds for p-images of class
   `<=59` (`p=5`), `<=62` (`p=7`) and `<=65` (`p=11`). Open: prove `D_p={3}+3pN` for all `p>=5`, and that each
   Kassel direction dies in `Gamma^`. See
   `kms-hc1-a2-iwahori-towers-deviate-only-at-kassel-degrees`.
2. **Integral Lie homology, all primes at once.** The Chevalley `Z`-form
   `L_Z` of `L_p` is generated in degree 1, so `H_2(L_p)=H_2(L_Z)(x)F_p`.
   Exact Smith normal forms through degree 90 give
   `H_2(L_Z) = Z^6 + sum_n Z/n (at n delta) + sum_n (Z/3)^6 + (Z/2)^3`.
   Hence `D_p={3}+3pN` through degree 90 for every `p>=5`, and condition 3
   holds for `k<=min(3p,91)` for every `p>=5`: p-images of class `<=90`
   kill `w_p` for all `p>=31`, with no group computation. The `(Z/3)^6`
   family is the `p=3` failure at class 6. Open 1 becomes one statement over
   `Z` (the p-torsion of `H_2(L_Z)`, or of the Serre-presented `A~2` Lie
   ring, is `Z/n` at `n delta` away from 6). Open 2 (Kassel directions die
   in `Gamma^`) is untouched. See `kms-hc1-iwahori-integral-h2-serre-plus-hc1`.
3. **Explicit 2-cocycles, all degrees; group-level Kassel classes.** Open 1
   is proved. For every `p>=5`, `H_2(L_p)` is the six Serre relations plus one
   Kassel class at each `n delta` with `p | n`, in all degrees. The dual
   cocycles of weight `-(nu + m delta)` with `m>=4` are killed type by type.
   By the root-system count, `Q(nu)` is in `{0,1,3,4}`:
   - at `nu=0` the Cartan triples force an affine profile with slope `B`
     and `nB=0`;
   - at `Q=3,4` they force 0;
   - at `nu` a root they gauge the Cartan part away, and triples of root
     vectors kill the boundary term.

   The weights with `m<=3` have degree `<=13` and fall in the exact table.
   Consequences:
   - `H_2(L_Z)(x)Z[1/6] = Z[1/6]^6 + sum_n Z[1/6]/n`, so Serre plus
     `HC_1` of the Iwahori order, by the Kassel cocycle and Kostant's
     Laplacian;
   - condition 3 holds for `k<=3p` for every `p>=5`;
   - `r(I_1)<=9` is equivalent to Open 2 alone.

   Open 2 was attempted at group level through Dennis--Stein symbols and
   relative `K_2`, and is blocked. The relative group is
   `K_2(F_p[t]/t^n,(t))=0` (Hesselholt--Madsen), so the Kassel `HC_1` class
   has no `K_2` counterpart in the Steinberg group of the Iwahori order. But
   transporting that to `Gamma^` needs the Steinberg relations in `Gamma^`,
   and those are what is to be proved. Separately, `U_+` is not finitely
   presented (Behr), so no finite set of group relations suffices, and the
   argument must use the pro-p topology. This remains heuristic support for
   Open 2, not a proof. See `kms-hc1-iwahori-h2-serre-plus-kassel-all-degrees`.
4. **Restricted (Zassenhaus / group-algebra) initial forms.** This route is
   obstructed in every Kassel degree. The degree-`p` relations `a^[p]=0`
   are exactly what the ordinary Lie computations of Attempts 1–3 do not
   see, so one might hope they kill the Kassel class. They do not. For every
   `p>=5` and `m>=1`, the Kassel extension `N` of `L_p/(height>3mp)` has a
   restricted structure, by Jacobson's criterion. The key facts are:
   - `(ad e)^3=0` for root vectors;
   - the factor `pk` kills `omega(h t^{pk}, .)`.

   In `N`, `a^[p]=b^[p]=c^[p]=0` and the Serre relations hold. So both
   initial-form models `FreeRes/(a^[p],...,Serre)` and
   `FreeLie_{F_p[pi]}/(pi a,...,Serre)` exceed `L_p` by at least one
   dimension at every `3mp`. If the criterion holds, some element of the
   relator subgroup `R` has an initial form in degree `3mp` outside the ideal
   of the nine initial forms. So no argument that uses the relators only
   through their associated graded can prove the criterion. This covers
   restricted-Lie cohomology, Gröbner/Anick resolutions of
   `F_p<X>/(X^p, Serre)` and Golod–Shafarevich counts. The graded form of
   the central-extension statement "splits over the three vertex groups
   implies trivial" is false: `N` splits over each vertex subalgebra. What is
   left is group-level input with no graded shadow, namely the
   non-homogeneous tails of `(1+X)^p` and of the Heisenberg commutators. See
   `kms-hc1-kassel-classes-survive-restricted-initial-forms`.
