Let `G_0` be a finitely generated group failing `P` (H2).

**Seed.**  By clause 4 of [[local-approximation-properties-are-marked-closed]]
there is a finitely presented group `E` failing `P`; fix a finite
presentation code `d` for it.  By heredity (clause 1 there), no group
containing `E` has `P`.

**(a)**  `P` is isomorphism-invariant and hereditary, the trivial group has
`P` by (H1), and `E` embeds in no group with `P`: these are the Markov
clauses, so the Adian--Rabin theorem applies.  In the form machine-checked
in `Computability/AdianRabinGeneral` (instantiated for soficity in
`Computability/SoficMarkov`), the construction takes a finitely presented
group with unsolvable word problem and produces, computably from a word `w`,
a finite presentation whose group is free when `w = 1` and contains `E`
when `w != 1`.  Free groups are residually finite and have `P` by (H1).
Thus the word problem, a `Sigma^0_1`-complete set, many-one reduces to
`P_fp`, and its complement to `NON-P_fp`.  A `Pi^0_1`-hard set is not r.e.
and a `Sigma^0_1`-hard set is not co-r.e.

**(b)**  Let `S_e = G(d, e)` be the parametric switch of
[[arbitrary-forbidden-seed-hereditary-property-switch]] with seed `d`:
`S_e ~= 1` for `e in INF` and `S_e ~= E` for `e in FIN`, uniformly as a
countably generated recursive presentation.  Enumerate its generators as
`c_1, c_2, ...`, set `c_i = 1` for `i <= 0`, and let `Q_e = B2(S_e)` be the
two-generator bidirectional bridge of
[[bidirectional-hnn-bridge-trivial-value-is-residually-finite]].  This is a
recursive presentation on two generators, uniformly in `e`.  For
`e in INF`, `Q_e ~= B2(1)` is residually finite and has `P` by (H1).  For
`e in FIN`, the HNN base `S_e * F(a, b) ~= E * F(a, b)` embeds in `Q_e`, so
`Q_e` contains `E` and fails `P` by heredity.  Since `INF` is
`Pi^0_2`-complete and `FIN` is `Sigma^0_2`-complete, (b) follows.

**(c)**  The countable free product `Q = *_e Q_e` is recursively presented
uniformly, and each `Q_e` is the subgroup generated by its two generators.
By the explicit Higman embedding [[mikaelian-explicit-higman-embedding]],
`Q` embeds in a finitely presented group, hence in Higman's universal
finitely presented group `V`, by a map computable on generators.  The image
of the generating pair of `Q_e` is a pair of words `(u_1(e), u_2(e))`
computable from `e`, and `<u_1(e), u_2(e)> ~= Q_e`.  Compose with (b).

**(d)**  Hardness plus membership is completeness.  For the finite
presentation statements, `P_fp` is `Pi^0_2` by hypothesis and not `Pi^0_1`
by (a); `NON-P_fp` is `Sigma^0_2` and not `Sigma^0_1` by (a).

The hyperlinear upper bound is recorded for finite presentation codes; for a
recursive presentation the same certificate is used with the first `N`
relators only, exactly as in (SOF1) of
[[sofic-recognition-has-a-pi2-upper-bound]], and the same area bookkeeping
(subadditivity and unitary invariance of the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt
distance) converts small relator defects into a model of the ball.
