For the ascending-HNN wreath product with an involutive lamp value, any two
sites of the coset space that have the **same height** have invisible lamp
difference: their difference lies in the literal MF radical.  Since two sites
have equal height exactly when they differ by an element of the telescope
`N = U_n t^-n Gamma t^n`, the subgroup generated by those differences is the
kernel of the pushforward of the lamp base along the height map -- the
`Phi_infinity` of the dossier -- so

    R_K = ker Phi_infinity  <=  Rad_MF,

unconditionally: property (T) of the base and the HNN relation are the only
inputs.  When the visible quotient is MF the inclusion is an equality, which
computes the MF radical of the wreath product outright.

The surprise is that **no iteration is needed**.  Passing from one stage to the
whole telescope looks like it requires re-running the collapse on successive
quotients, because a base move shifts a coset only by the level `-1` copy.  It
does not: see [[level-copies-are-kazhdan-and-compressed]].
