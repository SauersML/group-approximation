The naturality identities `(ANW2)--(ANW4)` apply simultaneously to every
word in the two proposed algebras and to their canonical word-spectral
corner.  Amplify a nonzero exact model by `I_2` and choose
`Z=diag(1,-1)` in the new factor.  Then `Y=P tensor Z` is traceless in the
normalized corner trace and has positive normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.
It commutes exactly with every amplified word.  Thus `(ANJ1)` reads a
positive number on the left and zero on the right for every `kappa`.

The same calculation is insensitive to the simple degree, transitivity, or
internal conjugation gap of the base word algebra, so it applies to every
currently named scalable candidate.
