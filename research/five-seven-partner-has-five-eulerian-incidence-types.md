Let `H=C_3`, `z=sum_H[h]`, and let `beta in F_2[G]` have support seven and
`z beta=beta z=z`.  Draw a bipartite multigraph whose vertices are the left
and right `H`-cosets met by the support and whose edges are the seven support
elements.  If `m=|supp(beta) intersect H|`, then `m` is `1` or `3`, and after
removing those base-cell edges the graph is Eulerian.  Consequently its
cycle-size type is exactly one of

```text
m=3:  4, or 2+2;
m=1:  6, 4+2, or 2+2+2.
```

Every 2-cycle is a doubled cell belonging to `N_G(H)`; cells outside the
normalizer have capacity one.  These five types exhaust the coefficient
geometry of the seven-term side of the unique unresolved `(5,7)` rank
profile.
