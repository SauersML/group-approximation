Let `R` be any algebra over the finite field `F_p`, not necessarily
commutative, and let

```text
U_n^+(R)={I+X:X is strictly upper triangular},        n<infinity.  (FPR1)
```

Every finitely generated subgroup of `U_n^+(R)` is finite.  More precisely,
if `g_1,...,g_m in U_n^+(R)` and `X_i=g_i-I`, the `F_p`-span `B` of all
products

```text
X_(i_1)...X_(i_k),             1<=k<n,                 (FPR2)
```

is a finite-dimensional nilpotent algebra, `B^n=0`, and

```text
<g_1,...,g_m> <= 1+B.                                 (FPR3)
```

In particular `1+B` is a finite `p`-group.  The statement remains true when
the generators are presented as finitely many positive elementary roots
`x_ij(r)` and finitely many words in them: use all elementary roots occurring
in those words as the finite generating list.

Now take the positive-root part of the affine-Leavitt Steinberg presentation
over its characteristic-`p` coefficient algebra.  Fix **any finite fragment**
whose letters are positive roots and whose relators are identities holding
among those literal roots in `U_n^+(R)`.  This may include simultaneously:

1. arbitrary finite additive root tables;
2. arbitrary finite three-root Heisenberg multiplication tables;
3. literal reuse of every coefficient occurrence across those tables;
4. every finite positive-root commutator consequence among the chosen
   coefficients; and
5. the Leavitt range-sum fold

```text
C_0 C_1 D^(-1)=1                                      (FPR4)
```

from `leavitt-range-sum-fold-is-first-object-erasing-row`, with the actual
`s_i,t_i,A` coefficient occurrences rather than independent path packets.

Let `H` be the finite subgroup generated by the fragment.  If the fragment
contains a chosen nonzero root

```text
z=x_ij(A),                 A!=0,                        (FPR5)
```

then `z!=1` in `H`.  Its left regular representation is an exact finite
matrix model of the entire fragment and satisfies

```text
tau(lambda_H(z))=0,
||lambda_H(z)-I||_2=sqrt(2).                           (FPR6)
```

Since `z` has order `p`, its `p` spectral phases occur with equal regular
multiplicity.  Thus its nontrivial spectral carrier has normalized trace

```text
tau(1-1_{ {1} }(lambda_H(z)))=(p-1)/p.                (FPR7)
```

Consequently no dimension-independent estimate which collapses the marked
root can follow from any finite positive-root fragment, even after all
coefficient occurrences in that fragment have been synchronized literally
and the scalar range-sum fold has been imposed.  In particular, adding such
a fragment cannot be the missing extra hypothesis in a same-reservoir
payment theorem: it contributes zero defect in the marked regular model.
The independent auxiliary factor in the earlier fold countermodel is not the
essential loophole.

For `coherent-heisenberg-multiplicity-decoder`, this identifies a strict
language boundary.  A successful compatibility/payment theorem must use at
least one relation which leaves the positive unipotent radical: for example
an opposite-root, Weyl/Whitehead, or genuinely global coefficient-return
relation.  More positive-root addition, multiplication, shared occurrences,
Heisenberg windows, or same-output folds can never provide the missing
finite-matrix obstruction.

This does not model the full affine-Leavitt elementary presentation, its
negative-root relations, or its external branch implementers.  Its point is
exactly that the load-bearing relation must occur outside the locally finite
positive-root subgroup.
