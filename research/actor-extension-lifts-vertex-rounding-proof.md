Full derivation in §§2–3 of the artifact.

1. **Unit type.** Premises 2–5 give `τ(1 - P_n) -> 0`, as in `kt-wreath-models-never-round-on-the-vertex-proof`
   steps 1–3. Replace the non-unit constituents by trivial ones.
2. **Agreement.** Premise 1 gives `σ∘q = ρ'` on `St_r(L)`.
3. **Stable `K_2`.** For `r >= d + 3 = 6`, injective stability (the
   Dennis–Vaserstein–van der Kallen–Suslin–Tulenbaev theorem, as imported in
   `polynomial-k2-vanishing-lifts-cones-to-laurent-steinberg-proof`) and the fundamental theorem give
   `ker q = K_2(L) = (F_q^×)^3 ⊕ Λ²Z^3`. It is central, generated by the symbols `{u_0, x_i}` and `{x_i, x_j}`, and
   `SL_3(Z)` acts on it by `Λ²`.
4. **Characters.** Let `ν_n` be the trace-weighted distribution of central characters. Step 2 gives
   `∫|ω(k) - 1|^2 dν_n -> 0` for fixed `k`. Nontrivial torsion values are at distance `>= |1 - e^(2πi/(q-1))|` from
   `1`, so torsion twists have mass `-> 0`.
5. **Transport.**
   - For `M = I + E_ij`, `MΓM^(-1) ⊆ Γ`. The proof of premise 6 uses only this, and gives `W_n` intertwining `π_n`
     with `π_n∘φ_M` off trace `η_n -> 0`.
   - With `m_n` the lcm of the orders of `x_i` in `A_n`, put `γ = x_ab(x^((m_n-1)c) f)` for `g = x^(-c) f`. Then
     `ρ'_n(x_ab(g)) = π_n(γ)` and `ρ'_n(φ_M x_ab(g)) = π_n(φ_M γ)`, so `W_n` intertwines the extensions.
   - Hence `||(φ_M)_*ν_n - ν_n||_TV <= 2η_n`.
6. **Property (T).**
   - `Λ²Z^3 ≅ (Z^3)^*`, so `SL_3(Z) ⋉ Λ²Z^3 ≅ Z^3 ⋊ SL_3(Z)`, which is Kazhdan by premise 7.
   - Its natural representation on `ℓ²` of the character group has almost invariant unit vectors `√ν'_n`, by
     Hellinger `<=` TV for the generators `M` and by step 4 for the symbols.
   - Kazhdan moves `√ν'_n` by `o(1)` onto invariant vectors, and those are supported at `ω = 1`.
7. **Descend.** Compress `ρ'_n` to the constituents trivial on `K`, of trace `-> 1`. They factor through
   `St_r(L)/K = Δ` and round `σ|Δ` by step 2.
