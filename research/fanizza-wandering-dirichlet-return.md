OPEN.  Extend the two-copy native group by one infinite-order word `w` and a
fixed finite packet so that, for every fixed `t`, finite-dimensional
microstates satisfy

```text
tr(Sigma_t(w)^* D_R Sigma_t(w))
 <= C_m C' tr(Sigma_t(w)^*(1-Re w)Sigma_t(w))
    +omega_t(def),                                    (FWD1)
```

where `omega_t(delta)->0`, while the exact HALT representation extends after
countable amplification and retains the signal.

The group normal form must also keep

```text
w^k, X_D^R w^k
```

pairwise distinct for all `k`.  Then the canonical marked Cesaro norm is
`t/2`, whereas `(FWD1)` and the unitary telescoping identity bound every
finite microstate by `2C_mC'+o(1)`.  Taking one fixed
`t>4C_mC'` proves nonhyperlinearity.

The difficult clause is the finite-matrix-only comparison.  It cannot arise
solely from a finite packet character table or a literal group-algebra
identity, since either would also constrain the regular trace.  A viable
construction must use finite multiplicity/rank during packet decoding while
the exact infinite witness realizes the return by Hilbert-hotel absorption.
