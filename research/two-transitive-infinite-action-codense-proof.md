Transport a stabilizer-fixed unit vector along the orbit; 2-transitivity
makes every pairwise inner product the same number `c`; a Gram matrix
`(1-c)I + cJ` of arbitrarily large size has rank at least `N-1` unless
`c = 1`, and the rank is bounded by the dimension.  Section 9 of the
artifact.
