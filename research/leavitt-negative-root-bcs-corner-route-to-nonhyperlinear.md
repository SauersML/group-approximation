This route records a calibration of the Property-`(T)`-free MF route
`agent-leavitt-not-bcs-corner-closes-full-radical`: its one open hole is at
least as strong as the flagship goal, for one explicit group.

By `lin-explicit-fixed-bcs-gap-via-generic-conversion`,
`omega_q^s(B_loop)<1`, so `omega_qa^s(B_loop)<1`; by
`paddock-slofstra-bcs-forbidden-projection-dictionary` a unital star
homomorphism `A(B_loop)->R^U` would be a perfect quantum-approximable
strategy.  Hence `A(B_loop)` has no Connes-embeddable tracial state.  The
hole `agent-leavitt-not-bcs-negative-root-corner` supplies a unital star
homomorphism

```text
A(B_loop) -> P_z C[Delta] P_z,   Delta=St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2)),
P_z=(1-z)/2,  z=x_13(s_1t_1).                                 (NHR1)
```

`P_z` is a nonzero projection because `z!=1` (its image in `E_20(R)` has
`13`-entry `q!=0`).  `nonce-bcs-unital-algebraic-corner-map-forces-nonhyperlinear`
then says `Delta` is not hyperlinear.  `Delta` is finitely generated
(Steinberg groups of a finitely generated ring in rank at least three are
generated by the root elements of the ring generators) and explicitly named,
so this is an explicit non-hyperlinear group.

**What this changes.**  The MF endpoint `Rad_MF(Delta)=Delta` concerns norm
microstates and was obtained with Property `(T)` without solving the
hyperlinearity problem; the hole additionally delivers nonhyperlinearity of
`Delta`, an open flagship statement.  So the "Property-(T)-free"
route through the negative root corner does not trade Property `(T)` for a
cheaper algebraic step: it trades it for an explicit nonhyperlinearity
certificate.  Workers on the hole should budget it as a flagship-level
construction and use the necessary filters of
`leavitt-corner-bcs-support-coefficient-gate`: the support subgroup must be
nonhyperlinear, and its matrix-entry ring must kill the mark in every
finite-dimensional representation.  An MF-only substitute, a corner model
whose trace is only required to be non-MF rather than non-CE, is the natural
weaker target that this route does not supply.
