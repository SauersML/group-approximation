Literature import, partly secondary. Pins in `research/artifacts/hl-intermediate-growth-pins-2026-09-13.md`.

- Items 1–2: Grigorchuk and Sidki, author PDF `sidkigrig3.pdf` (IJAC 14 (2004)), p. 1: "G(3), the
  2-generated infinite 3-group constructed by him and Gupta"; "The groups G(2),G(3) have trivial
  centers"; and "The main result of [?] is that the group of automorphisms of G(3) is a
  {2,3}-torsion group which splits as AutG(3) = (InnG(3))(VD), where V is an elementary abelian
  3-group of infinite rank and D is elementary abelian of order four which normalizes V." The cited
  paper of Sidki was not read; this item is secondary.
- Local finiteness (elementary): a finitely generated subgroup `H` of `V ⋊ D` meets `V` in a
  subgroup of index at most 4, which is finitely generated, elementary abelian, hence finite; so `H`
  is finite. Quotients of locally finite groups are locally finite, and `Out(𝔊𝔖)` is a quotient of
  `V D` by item 2.
- Item 3: Bartholdi–Grigorchuk–Šunić, *Branch groups*, arXiv:math/0510294, `spinal.tex` l.710–716:
  "The Gupta-Sidki groups from~\cite{gupta-s:burnside} act on the regular tree $\tree^{(p)}$ where
  $p$ is an odd prime and are defined by the vector $E=(1,-1,0,0,\dots,0)$"; l.717–730: a defining
  vector with $\sum_{s \in O_k(m)} \varepsilon_s \equiv 0 \pmod{p^{k+1}}$ defines an infinite
  2-generated $p$-group, and "in case the defining vector $E$ does satisfy the condition above, the
  obtained $p$-group is just-infinite, not finitely presented branch group" (attributed there to
  Vovkivsky). For `m = p = 3`, `E = (1, -1)` gives `1 + (-1) = 0`.
