**ESTABLISHED.** Let `Γ < G` be infranormal and not normal, with `Γ` and `G`
Kazhdan. Then `G` admits a free, ergodic, strongly ergodic p.m.p. action that
is not sofic. For the Theorem E pair of `kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath`

```text
Γ = EL_r(F_q[x_1,...,x_d])  <  G = EL_r(F_q[x_1^(±1),...,x_d^(±1)]) ⋊ SL_d(Z),   r, d >= 3,
```

`G` is residually finite, hence sofic. So:

1. **Păunescu's question fails for free actions.** Păunescu, arXiv:1002.0605v5,
   Section 3: *"The goal would be to prove that every (free) action of a sofic
   group is sofic. While this remains open we shall prove this fact for a
   family of groups."* A sofic group can have a free ergodic action that is
   not sofic. Refutes `sofic-groups-in-paunescu-sofic-action-class`.
2. **A nonsofic ergodic equivalence relation generated by a free action of a
   residually finite group**, by Păunescu Proposition 1.15 (for free actions,
   soficity of the action equals soficity of its orbit equivalence relation).

This is Kun--Thom, arXiv:2608.06222v3, **Corollary 3.2**, verbatim from the v3
PDF (artifact): *"Under the assumptions of Corollary D, the group G admits a
free ergodic nonsofic p.m.p. action. This action is strongly ergodic."* The
graph derives it from Corollary D in `kun-thom-free-nonsofic-action-proof`.

## The witness

`X = ({0,1},ν)^(G/Γ) × ({0,1},ν)^G` with `ν` the fair measure and `G` acting
diagonally by permuting coordinates (generalized Bernoulli over `G/Γ` times the
ordinary Bernoulli shift).

## Definition in force

Păunescu Definition 1.4 = Kun--Thom Definition 2.2: `L^∞(X) ⋊ G` embeds
trace-preservingly in a tracial ultraproduct of matrix algebras, with
`L^∞(X)` in the diagonal and every `u_g` a permutation. Because the canonical
trace vanishes on `u_g` for `g ≠ 1`, `g ↦ Θ(u_g)` is a sofic representation.
So a group with a sofic action is sofic, whether or not the action is free.

## Remark on sofic entropy (not a separate claim)

For a free action, finite approximately equivariant models along a sofic
approximation `Σ` assemble, in the ultraproduct, into an embedding as above.
Conversely an embedding yields models along the sofic approximation
`g ↦ Θ(u_g)`. So this action has no approximately equivariant finite models
along any sofic approximation of `G`, i.e. its sofic entropy is `-∞` for every
`Σ`, in the convention where `-∞` records the absence of models.

**Trust surface.** Corollary D is an unrefereed preprint theorem (see
`kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath`). The step from D to the free statement uses only
that soficity passes to factors, which is immediate from the definition (restrict
the embedding) and is stated in the same paper on p. 7.
