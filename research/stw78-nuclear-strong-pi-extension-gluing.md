Let

```text
0 -> J -> E -> Q -> 0
```

be a short exact sequence of separable nuclear C-star algebras.  If `J` and
`Q` are strongly purely infinite, then `E` is strongly purely infinite.

More generally, let `E` be the closure of an increasing sequence of ideals
`E_n`, with `E_0=0`, such that every layer `E_n/E_(n-1)` is separable,
nuclear and strongly purely infinite.  Then `E` is strongly purely infinite.

No corresponding extension-permanence assertion is made here without
nuclearity.  The proof passes through `O_infinity`-absorption, whose exact
extension and inductive-limit permanence are available in the separable
category.
