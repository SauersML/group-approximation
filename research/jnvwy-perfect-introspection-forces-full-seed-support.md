**ESTABLISHED.**  Consider the typed JNVWY introspection verifier of
arXiv:2001.04383v3, Figure 16, before oracularization and answer reduction.
Let `V` be the binary seed space of its source sampler and let `L:V->V` be
one role marginal.  In every perfect PCC strategy, and in every perfect
synchronous commuting or Connes-embeddable tracial model, the marginal of
the `(Sample,role)` measurement has

```text
tau(S_z)=1/|V|  for every z in V.                       (PIS1)
```

Consequently, if there are seeds `z_0,z_1,z_2` with

```text
L(z_0)+L(z_1)+L(z_2) != L(z_0+z_1+z_2),                (PIS2)
```

then **every** perfect model has an affine-unsafe positive joint-answer
support on the edge

```text
(Introspect,role) -- (Sample,role).                     (PIS3)
```

Thus choosing a noncanonical perfect HALT strategy cannot repair the
nonlinear `ComputeSampler` graph isolated in
`jnvwy-canonical-halt-strategy-is-affine-unsafe`.

## Reduce a PCC strategy to a faithful tracial model

For completeness, this argument does not assume that the strategy is the
honest EPR strategy.  In a finite-dimensional PCC strategy, consistency of
a projection `E` on a Schmidt vector `vec(D)` gives

```text
E D = D E^T,
```

and hence `E` commutes with the reduced density `D^2`.  Therefore
`tau(T)=Tr(D^2 T)` is tracial on the algebra generated by all the
measurements.  On a sampled edge, PCC commutation and consistency give

```text
<psi| A_a tensor B_b |psi> = tau(A_a B_b).              (PIS4)
```

Pass to the support of `D` and then quotient by the trace-zero ideal, so
`tau` is faithful.  The self-loops and their equality test identify the two
players' copies of every question PVM.  The standard synchronous
commuting-operator construction gives exactly the same faithful tracial
model, and a CE model is already of this form inside a tracial matrix
ultraproduct.  It is therefore enough to prove `(PIS1)` in one faithful
finite tracial algebra.

## The exact Pauli checks give the complete Weyl table

Write `H=F_q^M`, regarded as an `F_2`-space using the paper's fixed binary
basis, and let

```text
{P_h^W : h in H},  W in {X,Z},
```

be the PVMs for the two `(Pauli,W)` questions.  Put

```text
W(v)=sum_h (-1)^Tr(h dot v) P_h^W.                      (PIS5)
```

Let

```text
E={r ind_m(u) : u in F_q^m, r in F_q} subseteq H.       (PIS6)
```

Perfect Point--Pauli consistency identifies `W(r ind_m(u))` with the
`Tr(r a)` parity of the `(Point,W),u` answer PVM.  Now fix `e,f in E` and
let `gamma=Tr(e dot f)`.

If `gamma=0`, the two Point parities are identified, through the
Point--Pair and Pair--Pair consistency checks, with the two marginals of
one Pair PVM.  They commute.  If `gamma=1`, the Point parities are
identified with `Variable_1` and `Variable_5` in a perfect Magic-Square
strategy.  The exact (`epsilon=0`) case of Magic-Square rigidity makes
these observables anticommute.  State-dependent equality becomes operator
equality because `tau` is faithful.  Hence the verifier's exact acceptance
equations imply

```text
X(e) Z(f)=(-1)^Tr(e dot f) Z(f) X(e)  for all e,f in E. (PIS7)
```

The tested vectors span all of `H` over `F_2`.  Indeed, for every Boolean
point `y in {0,1}^m`, the interpolation vector `ind_m(y)` is the coordinate
vector `e_y`; as `r` ranges over `F_q`, the vectors `r e_y` span that whole
`F_q` coordinate over `F_2`.  Since the operators in each family in
`(PIS5)` are characters of one PVM,

```text
W(v+v')=W(v)W(v').                                      (PIS8)
```

Equations `(PIS7)` and `(PIS8)` therefore extend the Weyl relation to all
`v,w in H`.

Fourier inversion gives

```text
P_h^Z=|H|^(-1) sum_w (-1)^Tr(h dot w) Z(w).
```

Conjugating by `X(v)` and using the full Weyl relation yields

```text
X(v) P_h^Z X(v)^*=P_(h+v)^Z.                            (PIS9)
```

All Pauli-Z atoms are thus unitarily conjugate.  Traciality and
`sum_h P_h^Z=1` imply

```text
tau(P_h^Z)=1/|H|  for every h.                          (PIS10)
```

Notice that this is an exact algebraic consequence of the acceptance
equations.  It does not invoke the published robust Pauli theorem, whose
finite-parameter error remains nonzero when its game error is set to zero.

## Sampling transfers uniformity and exposes the nonlinear graph

Let `pi:H->V` be the coordinate projection used in the Pauli--Sample test,
and let `S_z` be the sum of the `(Sample,role)` projections over all payload
answers having seed component `z`.  Perfect Pauli--Sample consistency and
faithfulness give

```text
S_z=sum_(h:pi(h)=z) P_h^Z.                              (PIS11)
```

Equation `(PIS10)` proves `(PIS1)`.

On the Intro--Sample edge, the verifier accepts only

```text
((y,a),(z,b)) with y=L(z) and a=b.                      (PIS12)
```

Since `tau(S_z)>0`, summing the joint atoms above `S_z` shows that for each
`z` there is at least one payload `a_z` such that

```text
((L(z),a_z),(z,a_z))                                    (PIS13)
```

has positive joint trace.  Choose such payloads for the three seeds in
`(PIS2)`.  The odd xor of the three answers in `(PIS13)` has equal payload
bits on its two ends, seed `z_0+z_1+z_2`, and claimed coarse seed
`L(z_0)+L(z_1)+L(z_2)`.  It is rejected by `(PIS2)`.  If the xor of the
serialized payloads is not a legal payload encoding, it is rejected even
earlier, so variable payload syntax creates no escape.  This proves the
affine-unsafety assertion.

The remaining quantifier gap is downstream and sharply located.  This
theorem rules out every alternative perfect strategy for the **same
introspection verifier**.  Existing reflection results follow each honest
perfect proof refinement through answer reduction, but they do not yet say
that every exotic perfect strategy of the already answer-reduced verifier
must decode pointwise to one of these Intro--Sample atoms.
