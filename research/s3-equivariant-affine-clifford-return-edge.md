OPEN.  Use the compatible reset plane `(CRH4)` and the lifted radical
`S_3=GL_2(F_2)` to construct finite source and fresh edge groups `E,E'` and
an `S_3`-equivariant injection

```text
phi:E -> E'.                                             (SCE1)
```

The source orbit must be the three radical comparison directions, while the
fresh orbit must expose the endpoint events appearing in `(RNS1)`.  The
twenty-qubit affine Clifford packet supplies equal full-character
restriction `2 rho_15` in every direction of `(CRH4)`.  Gauge doubling must
keep all original BCS words fixed while the old direction labels are sent to
private junk and the fresh endpoint words are installed.

Once `(SCE1)` is an honest group map, use one stable letter for the extended
semidirect edge as in
`finite-symmetry-equivariant-edge-has-one-holonomy`.  The three comparison
maps are then conjugates of one word and cannot acquire independent
multiplicity rotations.  Their endpoint laws satisfy `(RNS1)` up to
`O(sqrt(E))`, which proves
`three-chart-affine-clifford-return-incidence` after the already established
finite packet alignment.

The remaining finite audit is stronger than checking three restriction
dimensions separately.  The three old/fresh edge embeddings and their
intersections must close to isomorphic `S_3`-semidirect groups, so the three
directional maps are restrictions of one `phi`.  A common residual plane is
necessary but not sufficient.  In particular, an attempted construction
must check every relation in the subgroup generated by the three directional
edge packets and must verify that the fresh endpoint events form the required
`S_3` orbit rather than three independently named projections.

The selector-only part of that audit is closed by
`s3-reset-plane-has-common-invariant-selector-polarization`: the three reset
translations lie in one `S_3`-invariant fresh Lagrangian, and one
`S_3`-centralizing Clifford maps the old polarization onto it while retaining
the dummy spin.  What remains is to show that the residual derivative words
and the fresh Schur generators extend this selector map to the full generated
edge group.  The current one-direction rank-swap relations do not imply that
extension automatically.
