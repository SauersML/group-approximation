The complete proof is Section 2 of the artifact.

1. **Vertex groups.** Each pair of generators satisfies
   `<x,y | x^p, y^p, [x,y,x], [x,y,y]>`. This presents `Heis(p)`, with centre
   generated by `z=[x,y]` and normal form `x^i y^j z^k`. So `Gamma^A2_p` is the
   fundamental group of a triangle of groups with:
   - three `Heis(p)` vertex groups;
   - cyclic edge groups `<a>`, `<b>` and `<c>`;
   - trivial face group.
2. **Local geometry.** In `Heis(p)` with `A=<x>` and `B=<y>`, the
   abelianisation rules out alternating relations of length `2` and `4`. So
   the coset graph has girth at least `6`. Also `z^k ∉ BAB ∪ ABA` for
   `k ≢ 0`, so `B` and `z^k B` are at link distance at least `4`.
3. **CAT(0) development.** Girth `6` gives Gersten--Stallings angles at most
   `pi/3` and angle sum at most `pi`. Hence the triangle of groups is
   developable, the vertex groups inject, and the development, built from
   equilateral Euclidean triangles, is CAT(0).
4. **Straight angles.** The centre element `r` fixes the vertex `v_ab`, and
   `s` fixes `v_bc`. Every nontrivial power turns the edge `[v_ab,v_bc]` through
   link distance at least `4`, which is Alexandrov angle `pi`.
5. **Free product.** For an alternating word
   `g = r^(k_1) s^(l_1) ... r^(k_n) s^(l_n)` with nonzero exponents, the
   translates of this edge concatenate into a path of length `2n` with angle
   `pi` at every breakpoint. That path is a local geodesic, hence a geodesic.
   So `d(v_bc, g v_bc) = 2n`, `g ≠ 1`, and `<r>*<s> -> Gamma^A2_p` is
   injective. Taking `g=(rs)^m` gives infinite order.

Geometric inputs are standard (Bridson--Haefliger, *Metric spaces of
non-positive curvature*, Part II Chapters 1, 4, 5, 12, and Part I Chapter 7;
Stallings, *Non-positively curved triangles of groups*, 1991). The theorem
numbers were not re-verified in this lane, as recorded in the artifact. All
group-theoretic computations are elementary and given in full.
