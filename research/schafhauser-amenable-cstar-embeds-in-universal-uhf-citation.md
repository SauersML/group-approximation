This is an imported theorem, credited to its author and not reproved here.
The arXiv TeX source of arXiv:1807.07381 was read on 2026-09-17.

Verbatim, from the abstract:

> Also, for any countable, discrete, amenable group $G$, the reduced group
> C$^*$-algebra $\mathrm{C}^*_r(G)$ admits a trace-preserving embedding
> into the universal UHF-algebra.

Verbatim, the theorem:

> \begin{theoremB} For a countable, discrete group $G$, the following are
> equivalent: (1) $G$ is amenable; (2) $L(G)$ admits a trace-preserving
> embedding into $\mathcal{R}$; (3) $\mathrm{C}^*_r(G)$ admits a
> trace-preserving embedding into $\mathcal{Q}$. \end{theoremB}

with `\mathcal{Q} = \bigotimes_{n=1}^\infty \mathbb{M}_n` and the trace

> the usual faithful trace given by $\sum_{g \in G} c_g \cdot g \mapsto c_e$.

The proof of (1) implies (3) in the source uses Higson--Kasparov, Lück's
rationality of traces, and Tu's UCT theorem for amenable groupoids.  All of
this is internal to the source.  The target states only (1) implies (3),
plus the one-line remark that a trace-preserving map into `Q` is unital.
