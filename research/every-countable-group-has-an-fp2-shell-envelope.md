**OPEN.**

## Statement

For every countable group `H` there are a finitely generated perfect group `P ≥ H` and a
bijection `ν: P → N` such that the shell envelope `E_ν = ⟨V, hat(P)⟩ ≤ Homeo({0,1}^N)` of
`shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions` has type `FP_2` over `Z`.

By `perfect-input-shell-envelopes-are-simple`, such an `E_ν` is a simple group of type `FP_2`
containing `H`. This answers Zaremsky Problem 1.25 (route
`every-countable-group-embeds-in-simple-fp2-via-fp2-shells`).

## What must be shown (reduction, proved)

`E_ν` is a finite germ extension of `V` with the single singular orbit `Ω = V·0^∞`, and `V` acts on `Ω^2` with
finitely many orbits. By `bhm-singfix-finiteness-holds-for-type-fp-n` with `n = 2`, `E_ν` has type
`FP_2` as soon as `SingFix_(E_ν)(M,M')` has type `FP_2` for all `M ⊆ M' ⊆ Ω` with `|M'| ≤ 2`.
- The pieces with `M = ∅` are point stabilizers of `V` at rational points, which are `F∞` (BHM Appendix A).
- What remains is the one-point group `A_1 = SingFix({p},{p})` and the two-point groups. `gq-typeA-design` records the latter as reducing to `A_1` in the finitely presented setting; that reduction needs re-checking over `FP_2`.

**Necessary conditions.**
- If `E_ν` is `FP_2` through this route, then `A_1` is `FP_2`.
- Then so is the germ group `Q_ν = A_1/⟨⟨k⟩⟩`, since the kernel `RStab_V(p)` is normally generated by one element (`bhm-singfix-condition-forces-fp-germ-groups`, item 2), and adding finitely many relators preserves `FP_2`.
- Then so is its index-two subgroup `F_ν = R_ν ×_Z R_ν` (`shell-germ-group-has-index-two-fiber-product`).
- Then so is `R_ν = ⟨λ(P), s⟩`, which is a retract of `F_ν` through the diagonal.

## Why this is more flexible than the Boone–Higman shell gate

- `P` need not be finitely presented, recursively presented or decidable. Any finitely generated perfect overgroup of `H` may be used, for example a two-generated simple one (`countable-group-embeds-in-two-generator-simple-group`).
- `ν` need not be computable. The computability gate `shell-recursive-presentations-compute-enumerations` concerns finite presentations only.
- The Röver–Nekrashevych obstruction `rover-nekrashevych-groups-have-co-re-word-problem` does not apply: shell envelopes carry no finite-state data.
- `FP_2` allows the infinitely many far relations of `R_ν` and `F_ν` to be redundant only homologically, as in Leary's and Bestvina–Brady's groups. This is the mechanism that separates `FP_2` from finite presentation.
