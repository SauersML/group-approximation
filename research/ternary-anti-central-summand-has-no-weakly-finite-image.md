**OPEN.** Let `G = L_(F_3)(1,2)^x`, `z = -1` and `S_- = eps_- F_3[G]`. Every nonzero quotient ring of
`S_-` (equivalently, every quotient of `F_3[G]` in which `[z] = -1`) contains a one-sided invertible
square matrix that is not invertible.

**It is the counterexample.** By `rank-condition-rings-have-weakly-finite-images` it is equivalent to `S_-`
failing the rank condition, which is `ternary-anti-central-unit-class-is-nonpositive` and refutes
Gottschalk on `G` (route `nonpositive-unit-class-from-no-weakly-finite-image`).

**Why this form.**
- **No ranks.** It needs no states, no rank functions and no matrix approximation.
- **What an attack looks like.** Take a hypothetical weakly finite `F_3`-algebra `W` generated by a copy of
  `G` with `z = -1`, and derive a contradiction from weak finiteness alone. Any argument using only
  "one-sided inverses are two-sided" in `W` is enough.
- **Not usable as they stand.** The rank-gate arguments use rank inequalities, so they don't apply
  directly.

**Refuted by** any nontrivial characteristic-three rank model of `G`. It moves `z`, restricts to a
Sylvester rank function on `S_-`, and its null quotient is weakly finite
(`anti-central-rank-functions-give-weakly-finite-leavitt-images`).

## Attempts

- 2026-09-12 `w4-upg-state`: opened as the rank-free form of the corner (artifact Corollary C.2).
  - **In R.** The evaluation quotient `S_- -> L_(F_3)(1,2)` fails weak finiteness, and so does every
    quotient through it.
  - **What a proof needs.** A quotient not through `R` must still contain the defect. A proof has to show
    that any weakly finite quotient forces the evaluation kernel to collapse the group, which
    perfection and simplicity forbid.
  - **No mechanism yet.**
- 2026-09-12 `w5-wf-obstruct`: certificate form and group form (artifact
  `research/artifacts/weakly-finite-representations-of-ternary-leavitt-units-2026-09-12.md`).
  - **Certificate.** This claim is `1 ∈ J_omega(S_-)` for the stagewise ideal of
    `weakly-finite-reflection-ideal-detects-weakly-finite-images`. Refuting Gottschalk on `G` needs only stage
    one, `J_1(S_-) != 0`, a stable-finiteness failure of `F_3[G]`; that follows from this claim without
    Theorem C.
  - **Group form.** Equivalent to `ternary-leavitt-units-have-no-weakly-finite-representation` (no nontrivial
    homomorphism of `G` into the units of any weakly finite `F_3`-algebra), through
    `ternary-weakly-finite-representations-give-anti-central-images`. Also equivalent to
    `ternary-weakly-finite-representations-kill-two-root-defect`, through
    `weakly-finite-leavitt-representations-killing-defect-are-trivial`: the rank criterion with its one rank
    step replaced by weak finiteness of corners.
  - **Where it stops.**
    - Stage one needs an exact one-sided pair over `S_-`, and the natural isometry lifts are left zero
      divisors.
    - Every landed anti-central firewall model lives in a weakly finite algebra.
    - An obstruction using only `E⋊V` would show that `V` is not sofic.
    - No mechanism.
