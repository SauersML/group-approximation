Let

```text
A=C*_r(F_2) tensor Z,       D=A_omega intersect A',
I=J_tau intersect D,        iota:F(Z)->D,
E=J_(tau_Z) intersect F(Z), K_Z=Ideal_D(iota(E)).
```

For every `h in (K_Z)_+`, every `epsilon>0`, and every `n>=1`,

```text
n[(h-epsilon)_+]<=[1_D] in Cu(D).                       (1)
```

In particular, each nonzero spectral cut of every positive element of
`K_Z` has infinite central Cuntz capacity.

More concretely, if

```text
y=sum_(j=1)^r x_j* iota(e_j) x_j,
```

where `x_j in D` and `(e_j)` are positive contractions in `E`, then for
every `n` there are pairwise orthogonal positive elements
`b_1,...,b_n in D`, each Cuntz equivalent to `y`.  Thus same-slice property
(SI) survives arbitrary ambient coefficients and finite positive sums,
even though it need not solve the mixed equation for an arbitrary source in
the full trace-kernel ideal.

This strengthens the unconditional localization of the capacity
obstruction: a finite-capacity witness to an extra ordinary trace must lie
outside `K_Z` after every nonzero spectral cut.  The theorem does not assert
the corresponding statement for the larger saturated ideal `K_Z^sat` or
for all of `I`.
