title: Exact tensoring preserves strong pure infiniteness from either factor
---

Let `C` and `D` be C*-algebras.  If `D` is exact and at least one of `C`
and `D` is strongly purely infinite, then

```text
C tensor_min D
```

is strongly purely infinite.  No nuclearity, simplicity, separability, or
ideal-lattice hypothesis is required.
