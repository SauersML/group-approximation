Fix the Sturmian subshift `Y` of slope `sqrt(2)-1` and put
`Q=EL_6(LC(Y,F_2) semidirect Z)`, an infinite simple Kazhdan LEF group
with decidable word problem. For every Turing degree `d` there is a
Kazhdan group `H_d`, generated by three elements of orders `7,2,2`,
with `deg_T WP(H_d)=d` and exact sequence
`1 -> SL_infinity(F_2) -> H_d -> Q x Q -> 1`.

Its kernel is its MF radical, its normal lattice has exactly five
elements, and every proper quotient is LEF. The proper quotient types
are the fixed list `Q x Q`, `Q`, `1`; there are no nontrivial finite
quotients. Thus there are continuum many pairwise nonisomorphic such
extensions with the same abstract kernel and maximal MF quotient.
