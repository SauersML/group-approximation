The pigeonhole construction `(BRG2)` produces more low-frequency characters
than the affine kernel and its conjugate contain.  Evaluate every translated radial mask on
one remaining character and use `p_r(1)=0` to obtain `(BRG3)`.  Fourier
orthogonality gives unit distance from the exact kernel.
