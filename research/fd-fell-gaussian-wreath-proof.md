For each `j`, let `(Omega_j,mu_j)` be the standard Gaussian probability space
of the real representation `V_j`.  For a unit vector `v in V_j`, write

```text
s_v(omega)=sign(<omega,v>) in {-1,1}.                  (FDG1)
```

The Gaussian action satisfies

```text
rho_j(g).s_v=s_(rho_j(g)v).                            (FDG2)
```

Put `a_(g,j)=s_(rho_j(g)xi_j)`.  For a finite list `g_1,...,g_k`, its Gaussian
covariance matrix is

```text
G_j(i,l)=<rho_j(g_i)xi_j,rho_j(g_l)xi_j>
        =<xi_j,rho_j(g_i^(-1)g_l)xi_j>.                (FDG3)
```

By `(FDC2)`, these matrices converge to the Gram matrix of independent
standard Gaussians indexed by the distinct cosets among `g_iN`.  Taking
signs is almost surely continuous for a Gaussian vector.  Therefore all
joint moments of the `a_(g,j)` converge to those of independent centered
Rademacher lamps indexed by `L/N`; repeated cosets give the same limiting
lamp.  In particular this recovers every finite Bernoulli lamp moment, not
only pairwise traces.

Form the canonical crossed product

```text
M_j=L^infinity(Omega_j) rtimes_(rho_j) L.              (FDG4)
```

Its group unitaries have the regular crossed-product trace, so every mixed
word with nonidentity actor grade has trace zero.  When the grade is the
identity, `(FDG3)` gives convergence to the corresponding canonical lamp
moment.  Hence the marked tuples consisting of the actor generators and the
seed `s_(xi_j)` converge in all tracial moments to the canonical generators
of `L(W_N)`.

Each `M_j` is Connes embeddable.  Indeed `K_j=ker(rho_j)` has finite index in
`L` and acts trivially on the Gaussian algebra.  The algebra generated by
the base and `L(K_j)` is

```text
L^infinity(Omega_j) tensor_bar L(K_j),                 (FDG5)
```

which is Connes embeddable because the first factor is amenable and `K_j` is
a subgroup of the hyperlinear group `L`.  Equation `(FDG4)` is a finite-index
(possibly cocycle) extension of `(FDG5)`, and Connes embeddability is
preserved under finite extensions.  Approximating each finite set of moments
inside `M_j` by matrices and diagonalizing in `j` gives a trace-preserving
embedding of `L(W_N)` into a matrix ultraproduct.  Thus `W_N` is hyperlinear.

To verify strictness from separability, let `E=F_m->Q` with `Q` a finitely
generated infinite simple amenable group.  It is sofic and not residually
finite.  Choose permutation
microstates for generators of `Q` and extend the assignment freely to an
exact finite-image representation `pi_j:E->Sym(d_j)`.  On the realification
of the Hilbert--Schmidt space, use left multiplication by the permutation
matrix `pi_j(g)` and the unit vector `I`.  Its real coefficient is
`tr(pi_j(g))`, which converges to `1_(g in N)`.  This is `(FDC2)` with finite
image, while `N` is nonseparable.  Thus approximate coefficient data
cannot be rounded formally to the invariant branches excluded by
`sparse-relator-defects-defeat-orbitwise-classicalization`.
