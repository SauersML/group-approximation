- Q is the additive group of the rationals.
- Kojima and Sheng, "On subgroups of Brin-Thompson groups nV",
  arXiv:2603.18410 (v1 March 19, 2026; v3 May 23, 2026; unrefereed), state in
  the abstract that nV contains continuum many copies of Q for n ≥ 2, and that
  this fails for n = 1.
