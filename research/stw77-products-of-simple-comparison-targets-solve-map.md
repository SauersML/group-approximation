Let `A` be separable, exact, and unital, let `(B_lambda)_(lambda in Lambda)`
be any family of nonzero unital simple C-star algebras such that every
`W(B_lambda)` is almost unperforated, and put

```text
B=product_(lambda in Lambda) B_lambda.
```

If `phi:A->B` is a unital homomorphism of finite nuclear dimension and the
unit of `B` is properly infinite, then `phi` is O-infinity-stable.

Consequently STW LXXVII has a positive answer for every such product target.
For this class the pointwise range hypothesis can be weakened drastically:
proper infiniteness of `phi(1_A)=1_B` alone suffices.

The index set may be uncountable, the product need not be separable or
simple, and no nuclearity, exactness, or finite-dimensional ideal-lattice
hypothesis is imposed on the factors.
