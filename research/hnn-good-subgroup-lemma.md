OPEN, and the only step of the Novikov--Boone chain with mathematical rather
than bookkeeping risk.  **Simpson's Definition 2 and its lemma.**  Let
`phi : Asub =~ Bsub` inside `G`, let `A <= G` be *good* --

    Good A phi  :=  phi (A cap Asub) = A cap Bsub,

in Lean the two implications spelled out on elements -- and let
`A' = liftedSubgroup phi A` be the subgroup of `HNNExtension G Asub Bsub phi`
generated by `of '' A` together with the stable letter `t`.  Then

    A' cap of.range  =  A.map of.

## What is already proved, and what is not

* the easy inclusion `A.map of <= A' cap of.range`
  (`map_of_le_liftedSubgroup_inf`), which needs no Britton theory;
* goodness itself as a definition, and the algebraic step it licenses:
  `good_pinch` and `good_pinch_inv` -- conjugating `of a` by `t`, for
  `a in A cap Asub`, stays inside `of '' A`, via
  `HNNExtension.equiv_eq_conj`;
* the restriction `goodEquiv` of `phi` to the comapped subgroups of `A`, and
  the lift `smallLift` of the small extension into the big one.

What is missing is the reverse inclusion: that nothing *else* in `A'` lands in
the range of `of`.  This is where Britton's Lemma enters, and it is the only
place in the chain where it does.

## Why the single-letter form is enough

Mathlib's `HNNExtension` carries one stable letter, so the multi-letter
statement of the source has to be assembled stage by stage up the tower of
[[boone-hnn-tower-embeds-base-group]], each stage's conclusion feeding the next
stage's goodness hypothesis.  Everything the single-letter case needs is
present in `Mathlib/GroupTheory/HNNExtension.lean` at the pinned revision:
`NormalWord` extends `ReducedWord`; `prod_smul` and `prod_empty` give
`(g . empty).prod phi = g`, so every element has a reduced spelling with no
extra work; and `ReducedWord.exists_normalWord_prod_eq`,
`ReducedWord.map_fst_eq_and_of_prod_eq`, `NormalWord.prod_injective`, `equiv`,
`of_injective` and Britton itself
(`ReducedWord.toList_eq_nil_of_mem_of_range`) are all exported.
