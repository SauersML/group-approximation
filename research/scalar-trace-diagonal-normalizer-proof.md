The balanced sign diagonal has normalized trace zero and lies in the diagonal
masa, so it normalizes that masa with identity permutation component.  Its
diagonal conditional expectation is itself, not zero.  These three exact
computations prove the claimed separation between scalar trace, permutation
displacement, and Cartan expectation.
