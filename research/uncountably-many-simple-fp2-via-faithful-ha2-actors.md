Let `G_i ↷ S_i` be faithful actions of type `[HA_2]`, with the `G_i` of type FP_2
and pairwise non-isomorphic, `i` ranging over an uncountable set.

By `ha2-actions-give-fp2-twisted-brin-thompson-groups` (Fournier-Facio–Wu–Zaremsky,
Corollary 4.14 and Theorem 3.6, with Belk–Zaremsky simplicity), each twisted
Brin–Thompson group `SV_{G_i}` is simple and of type FP_2, and `G_i` embeds in it
via `ι_∅`.

Counting: a group of type FP_2 is finitely generated. It has countably many
finite subsets, hence countably many finitely generated subgroups. Suppose the
`SV_{G_i}` realized only countably many isomorphism types. Then the `G_i`, each
a finitely generated subgroup of one of these groups, would realize only
countably many types, a contradiction. So the simple FP_2 groups `SV_{G_i}`
realize uncountably many isomorphism types.
