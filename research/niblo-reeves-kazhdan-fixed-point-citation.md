**Source.** G. A. Niblo and L. D. Reeves, *Groups acting on CAT(0) cube
complexes*, Geometry & Topology 1 (1997) 1--7,
<https://msp.org/gt/1997/1-1/gt-v1-n1-p01-s.pdf>. The PDF was fetched and read
through `pdftotext` on 2026-09-13.

**Verbatim (p. 3):** "Theorem B If G is a group satisfying Kazhdan's property
(T) and X is a finite dimensional CAT(0) cube complex on which G acts
cellularly (and therefore isometrically), then the action has a global fixed
point."

**Verbatim (abstract):** "We show that groups satisfying Kazhdan's property (T)
have no unbounded actions on finite dimensional CAT(0) cube complexes".

The proof in the paper restricts the simplicial metric on the 1-skeleton to a
conditionally negative definite kernel and applies the kernel characterization
of property (T) from de la Harpe--Valette. It was not re-derived here.
