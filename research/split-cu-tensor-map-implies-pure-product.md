title: A split canonical Cuntz tensor map is enough for purity permanence
---

Let `A` and `B` be pure C-star algebras, and let

```text
theta: Cu(A) tensor_Cu Cu(B) -> Cu(A tensor_min B)
```

be the canonical Cu-morphism.  If `theta` admits a Cu-morphism right inverse,
then `A tensor_min B` is pure.  Thus the canonical map need not be an
isomorphism; a Cu-splitting already suffices.
