The full proof is §0–§4 of the artifact. It uses only the two imported
cost facts listed in `requires`, together with standard facts about free
groups: axes in the Cayley tree and the Schreier index formula.

1. **Coset field (Lemma 0).**
   - Set `T(x) = {g : gx E x}` and `S(x) = K\T(x)`.
   - `T(gx) = T(x)g^{-1}` for `g ∈ T(x)`. So `S(kx) = S(x)k^{-1}`, and
     `d = |S|` is `E`-invariant.
2. **Commensurator (Lemma 1).**
   - Let `T_K` be the union of the axes of `K`, and `M` its stabilizer.
   - Finite-index subgroups have the same axis union. So
     `Comm_F(K) ⊆ M`.
   - `K` has finitely many orbits of vertices on `T_K ⊆ ⋃_k kP`, and `M`
     acts freely. So `[M:K] < ∞` and `M = Comm_F(K)`.
   - The Schreier formula gives `rk M − 1 = (rk K − 1)/[M:K]`, which is at
     least 1.
3. **Finite part (Lemma 2).**
   - `S_*(μ|Y_fin)` is a finite `K`-invariant measure on the countable set
     of finite subsets of `K\F`. So a.e. `S(x)` has a finite `K`-orbit,
     which gives `[K : K ∩ h^{-1}Kh] < ∞` for every `Kh ∈ S(x)`.
   - Apply the same at `hx`, where `Kh^{-1} ∈ S(hx)`, on an `F`-invariant
     conull set. This gives the other index, so `h ∈ Comm_F(K)`.
4. **Pricing (Lemma 3).**
   - On `Y_d`, form the covering relation `Ê` on
     `{(x, Kh) : Kh ∈ S(x)}` with `μ × counting`. It is generated by the
     maps `θ_g(x,Kh) = (gx, Khg^{-1})`.
   - Graphings of `E|Y_d` lift with the same normalized cost, so
     `C_1(Ê) ≤ C_1(E|Y_d)`.
   - `A = {(x, K)}` is a complete section of normalized measure `1/d`, and
     `Ê|A ≅ E_{a|K}|Y_d`.
   - `E_{a|K}|Y_d` has cost `rk K`, since a free basis is a treeing
     (`gaboriau-treeable-free-action-realizes-min-cost`).
   - `gaboriau-induction-formula-complete-section` gives
     `C_1(Ê) = 1 + (rk K − 1)/d`.
5. **Assembly.** Sum over the `E`-invariant pieces `Y_d` with
   `d ≤ [M:K]`. This gives `C(E|Y_fin) ≥ rk(M) μ(Y_fin)`.
