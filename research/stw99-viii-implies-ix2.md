Brown, Mem. AMS 184 (2006), proof of Proposition 4.1.3: u.c.p. maps
asymptotically multiplicative in norm and recovering a faithful trace give an
injective, hence isometric, `*`-homomorphism into `∏M_k/⊕M_k`, so
`‖a‖ = lim sup ‖φₙ(a)‖` and `A` is quasidiagonal by Voiculescu's abstract
characterization.  Then Problem VIII applies.
