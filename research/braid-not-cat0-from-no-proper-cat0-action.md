Contrapositive of the lemma in `braid-proper-cat0-action-from-geometric-action`:
a geometric action of `B_n` on a complete CAT(0) space is a proper action on a
proper CAT(0) space. So if some `B_n` has no proper isometric action on a
proper CAT(0) space, that `B_n` is not CAT(0).
