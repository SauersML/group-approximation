Let E be a finitely generated group and C a subgroup of Z(E). If E/C
is finitely presented, then C is finitely generated as an abelian group
and E is finitely presented.

Consequently, for finitely generated E and central C,

    E/C is finitely presented
      iff E is finitely presented and C is finitely generated.

No simplicity, perfection or initial finite presentation of E is assumed.
This standard central-extension fact prevents projectivization from
repairing failure of finite presentation in a finitely generated group.
