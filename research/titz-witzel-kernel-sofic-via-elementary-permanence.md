**Dead.**  The attack builds the kernel from already sofic groups by the
standard closure operations.  Examples: embed it in a sofic overgroup,
realize it as a marked limit of sofic groups (for instance finite quotients
of a covering group), or find a sofic normal subgroup with amenable
quotient.

It is killed by `titz-witzel-kernel-not-elementary-sofic`.  Every group
reachable by finitely or transfinitely many such steps from the trivial
group is elementary sofic, and the kernel is not.  Isolation also rules out
every nontrivial marked limit of groups converging to the kernel, sofic or
not.
