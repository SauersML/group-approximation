Arulseelan--Manzoor, arXiv:2508.21709, source fetched on MSI
(`/scratch.global/sauer354/nh-lu/x2508.21709`).  Theorem `SNoCompPres`
(`applications.tex` l.47--53) proves that no locally universal tracial von
Neumann algebra has a computable presentation: a locally universal algebra has
a weakly effectively enumerable universal theory, a computable presentation
would make that theory computable (Goldbring--Hart Proposition 2.7, cited at
l.51), and Theorem `SUndecidable` (l.25--27) shows the universal theory is not
computable.  `SUndecidable` in turn uses Theorem `MIPcoEncoding`, which assumes
`MIPco = coRE` (Lin, announced).  The import is therefore conditional on that
preprint, as stated on the claim.
