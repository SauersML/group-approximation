Fix `D`.  Consider the balanced repeated-LDPC occurrence presentation with
bounded-degree equality expanders, bounded parity faces, and the complete
logical-pair overlay.  Suppose all occurrence reflections generate a finite
type-I algebra whose irreducible central blocks have dimension at most `D`.

If the average squared equality, parity-word, and pair-commutator defects
are at most `epsilon`, then on the same Hilbert space there is one joint PVM
supported on the classical code such that replacing every occurrence by the
corresponding marginal reflection costs at most

```text
 Omega_D(epsilon),        Omega_D(epsilon)->0,            (BTO1)
```

in average squared normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  The exact bounded-face
PVMs can be reconstructed from those shared marginals with the same uniform
modulus.

Consequently the unresolved occurrence-synchronization and diffuse-Newton
gates contain no bounded-type noncentral obstruction.  Any countersequence
must retain positive trace on irreducible blocks of unbounded dimension
already in the algebra generated by the occurrence reflections.
