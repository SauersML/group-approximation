The verifier forms the displayed finitely presented group in GAP 4.14.  Exact
coset enumeration returns order `96`.  It then closes each of the three
marked generator subsets and obtains orders `4,12,12`.  The first is the
presented Klein four group.  Each latter subgroup is generated by an
involution and an order-three element whose product has order three, hence is
the standard `A4` triangle group of order twelve.  Equality of the computed
orders proves that none of the three local vertices collapses in the
universal completion.
