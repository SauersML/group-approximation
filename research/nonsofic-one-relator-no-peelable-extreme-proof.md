If some zero-exponent basis gave a peelable top or bottom letter, the
prerequisite would make `G` sofic. The degenerate case `m = n` forces
`w` conjugate to `a^(+-1)` for torsion-free `G`, so `G` is infinite cyclic and
sofic.
