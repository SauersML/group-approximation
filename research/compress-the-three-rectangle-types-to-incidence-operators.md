of `eF_2[H]e=F_4`; absorbing them into `u` gives one `Q_u`.  Three selected
base elements disappear after adding `z`, while one selected base element
becomes a nonzero `F_4` scalar.  A doubled cell becomes one normalizer
monomial.  Applying these rules to the exhaustive three rectangle types
gives items 1--3, and the established normalizer compression gives item 4.
No rank or Garden-of-Eden conclusion is inserted into this bookkeeping.
