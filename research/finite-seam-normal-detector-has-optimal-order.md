Every finite subgroup distinguishing all normal subgroups by intersection
has order at least `2^(|V|+|E|)`, attained by the constructed elementary
abelian subgroup. For a family with closure `N(J,S)`, the sharp ordinary
subfamily bound is `|S|+|J minus inc(S)|`. Its maximum is the width of
the incidence poset. The bound also suffices for MF closure, without an
assertion of sharpness for that coarser closure.
