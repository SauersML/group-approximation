Let `A` be separable and nuclear with a faithful trace.  Nuclearity makes every
trace on `A` amenable.  Under Problem II, `A` satisfies the UCT, so the
AF-embedding theorem embeds `A` into a unital simple AF algebra.  Subalgebras of
AF algebras are quasidiagonal, so `A` is quasidiagonal.  The same conclusion is
Tikuisis--White--Winter's theorem that faithful traces on separable nuclear UCT
algebras are quasidiagonal (Ann. of Math. 185 (2017)).

Contrapositive, for the negative side: a separable nuclear algebra with a
faithful trace that is not quasidiagonal refutes Problem II.  Through
`stw99-ix1-implies-vii-simple`, so does a simple nuclear stably finite algebra
that is not quasidiagonal.
