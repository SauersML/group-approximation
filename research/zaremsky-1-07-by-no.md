A counterexample to the Juan-Pineda–Leary conjecture, together with a counterexample to the
von Puttkamer–Wu conjecture, answers both parts of Problem 1.7 negatively. By
`bvc-counterexample-from-jpl-counterexample`, the first requirement already gives the second.
