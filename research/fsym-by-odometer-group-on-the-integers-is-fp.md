**Setting.**
- `Z ⊂ {0,1}^N` is the set of eventually constant sequences, least significant digit first. These are the 2-adic
  expansions of the integers: eventually 0 for `n >= 0`, eventually 1 for `n < 0`.
- `B = V_(2,1)(<a>)` acts on `{0,1}^N` by prefix tables with odometer labels, and `a` acts on `Z` as `n -> n+1`.
- `c` is the digit complement, `c(n) = -1-n`.

**Statement.**
1. **Lemma (finitary symmetric extensions).** Let a finitely presented group `Q` act on a countably infinite set
   `X`, and assume:
   - `Q` has finitely many orbits on `X^4`;
   - the setwise stabilizer of each 2-subset is finitely generated.

   Then the semidirect product `FSym(X) ⋊ Q` is finitely presented.
2. **The line group.** `B` preserves `Z` and acts on it faithfully, with `B ∩ FSym(Z) = 1`. It is transitive on
   ordered pairs of distinct integers, has finitely many orbits on `Z^4`, and `Stab_B(0) ∩ Stab_B(-1)` is finitely
   generated. Hence `L_od = FSym(Z) ⋊ B <= Sym(Z)` is finitely presented. It contains the Houghton group
   `H_2 = FSym(Z) ⋊ <a>` and `FSym(Z) ⋊ V`.
3. **L_od builds the Houghton-like groups of G_od.** Let `G_od` be as in
   `odometer-kernel-preimage-is-strongly-shift-similar`.
   - For rays `i ≠ j` of `[n] x N`, identify ray `i` with `N` and ray `j` with `Z_(<0)` through `c`. This gives an
     embedding `eps_ij : L_od -> H_n(G_od)`, which is the identity off rays `i` and `j`. Its germ image is the
     twisted diagonal `{ (b on i, cbc on j) }`.
   - For `n >= 3`, `H_n(G_od)` is generated by the `n(n-1)/2` subgroups `eps_ij(L_od)`, `i < j`.
   - For `n = 2`, `H_2(G_od) = < eps_12(L_od), G_od x 1 >`.

**Reading.**
- The odometer system is rigid at two levels: the germ group `B` is finitely presented, and on the two-ended set
  `Z` the near action is the restriction of a genuine action. Given both, the lemma lifts finite presentation
  through `FSym` with no local-stability obstruction.
- The one-ended group `G_od` loses the genuine action, since `K` moves points across the end of `N`.
- For `n >= 3`, `H_n(G_od)` is glued from finitely many copies of the finitely presented `L_od` along finitary
  symmetric groups. Whether finitely many gluing relations suffice is exactly
  `odometer-houghton-like-groups-are-finitely-presented`.

**Lesson for general BH.**
- Finite presentation lifts through the finitary symmetric group whenever three things hold:
  - the germ action is realized by a genuine action of a finitely presented group;
  - that action has finitely many orbits on 4-tuples;
  - its pair stabilizers are finitely generated.
- This is the permutation-group shadow of Zaremsky's oligomorphic criterion for type (A). The Houghton group
  `H_2 = FSym(Z) ⋊ Z` is not finitely presented, and `Z` has infinitely many orbits on pairs, so the orbit
  condition cannot be dropped.
- The lemma covers the known split positive cases: Nucinkis–St. John-Green's `Q~V`, and `L_od` here.
- It splits the general lifting problem, from finitely presented germs to the finitary preimage, into two parts:
  - (a) realizability: does the germ action lift to a genuine action on `X ⊔ F`? This is Cornulier's near-action
    theory;
  - (b) an oligomorphic count.
- Free-shift enumerations fail (a) and (b) together. Houghton groups fail (a), and get finite presentation instead
  from three rigid translation directions.
- The decisive open point for split one-ended hosts: can the index-one shift normalize a realizable oligomorphic
  germ group at all? For `V` acting on one of its orbits, Rubin rigidity suggests that it cannot. That suggestion
  is a heuristic, not proved.
