There is a unital separable simple AF algebra `B` and two unital star
homomorphisms `alpha,beta:C^2->B` which are unitarily equivalent in `B**`
but are not approximately unitarily equivalent in `B`.

Moreover `B` is nuclear, exact, locally reflexive, of real rank zero and
stable rank one, and the four nonzero corner projections defining the maps
are full.  Hence none of these regularity or fullness hypotheses, alone or
together, is a generic theorem returning von Neumann implementing unitaries
to a C-star algebra.  This does not produce two inequivalent Jiang--Su
embeddings; it rules out a domain-independent descent shortcut.
