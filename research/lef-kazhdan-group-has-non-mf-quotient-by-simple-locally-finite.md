There is an exact sequence `1 -> L -> G -> H -> 1` in which
`L ~= SL_infinity(F_2)` is infinite simple locally finite, `G` is
LEF Kazhdan, and `H` is just non-MF Kazhdan with every proper quotient
LEF. Both groups have three torsion generators of orders `7,2,2` and
no nontrivial finite quotients. Their normal lattices have seven and
five elements, respectively.

Thus operator MF and LEF are not preserved by quotienting by an
infinite simple locally finite normal subgroup. The construction is
the directed two-cycle and deletion of one of its two edge orbits.
