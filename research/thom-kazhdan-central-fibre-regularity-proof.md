Thom's submitted source proves that the displayed group is finitely generated,
property `(T)`, and LEF, and identifies the central kernel of its surjective
noninjective shift endomorphism.  Repetition of kernels of finite quotients
puts that kernel in the finite residual; Malcev then kills it in every
finite-dimensional unitary representation.  The matrix-group extension
exhibits exactness.  Cutting `C*_r(G)` by a nontrivial central character gives
an exact locally reflexive property-`(T)` algebra with no finite-dimensional
representation.  LEF gives Connes embeddability of `L(G)`, hence a faithful
hyperlinear trace on the corner.  The prerequisite excludes amenable traces,
and therefore excludes quasidiagonality.
