## Proof

Every entry of `U in M_n(B)` satisfies `u = pup`, hence `(1-p)u = 0` and
`u(1-p) = 0`.  For `U, V in M_n(B)`,

```text
(U + (1-p)I)(V + (1-p)I)
  = UV + U(1-p)I + (1-p)V + (1-p)^2 I
  = UV + (1-p)I,                                                  (1)
```

the two cross terms vanishing entrywise and `(1-p)^2 = 1-p`.  So `iota_p`
is multiplicative.  It sends the unit `pI_n` of `M_n(B)` to `I_n`, and

```text
iota_p(U^(-1))iota_p(U) = iota_p(U^(-1)U) = iota_p(pI_n) = I_n,
```

so it lands in `GL_n(R)` with `iota_p(U)^(-1) = U^(-1) + (1-p)I_n`.

**Injectivity.**  If `U + (1-p)I = V + (1-p)I` then `U = V` as matrices
over `R`, and both have entries in `B`.  Equivalently, `U` is recovered
from `iota_p(U)` as `p iota_p(U) p` entrywise.

**Elementary generators.**  For `i != j` and `a in B`, the corner
elementary matrix is `pI_n + aE_ij`, since `p` is the unit of `M_n(B)`.
Its image is `pI_n + aE_ij + (1-p)I_n = I_n + aE_ij`, which is `(CU2)`.
These generate `EL_n(B)`, so the restriction of `iota_p` is an injective
homomorphism onto the subgroup of `EL_n(R)` generated by the elementary
matrices with entries in `B`, proving `(CU3)`.

**Nontriviality.**  `I_n + pE_12 = I_n` would force `p = 0`, so `p != 0`
gives `e_12(p) != 1`.
