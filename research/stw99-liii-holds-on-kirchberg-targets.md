Outer actions of countable discrete amenable groups on stable
Kirchberg algebras are classified up to cocycle conjugacy by
equivalence in the equivariant `KK`-category (Gabe--Szabo, Theorem B),
with extensions beyond discrete groups under isometric shift
absorption.  This is exactly the purely infinite instance of STW
Problem LIII's challenge; the challenge's remaining content is the
stably finite counterpart, factored on its node into equivariant
existence + equivariant `Z`-stability + Szabo's KK-uniqueness.
