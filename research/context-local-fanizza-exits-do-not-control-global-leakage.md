title: Context-local Fanizza exits do not control the localized non-CE leakage functional
---

The localized leakage functional is bounded by an explicit mixed energy
involving the capacity cut, every exit, and the full shared BCS tuple.
Context-local packet bridges do not bound that mixed energy.

Exact finite quotients of the local Bass--Serre packet graph retain zero
local relator defect while a private exit has a fixed commutator with a
shared variable outside its context.  A genuinely mixed capacity-gated
two-cell is therefore necessary.
