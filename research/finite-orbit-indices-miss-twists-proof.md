Notation as in the target.

1. **Finite orbits.** Let `x in X_+` and `c = mu(x) > 0`. By invariance, `mu` is
   constant `c` on the orbit `O = Q_s . x`. Then `|O| . c = sum_(y in O) mu(y) <= 1`,
   so `|O| <= 1/c`.
2. **Trivial action of groups without finite quotients.** Let `A <= Q_s` have no
   nontrivial finite quotient. Each `A`-orbit in `X_+` lies in a `Q_s`-orbit,
   hence is finite. On a finite orbit `O`, `A` acts through a homomorphism
   `A -> Sym(O)`. Its image is a finite quotient of `A`, hence trivial. So `A`
   fixes every point of `X_+`, and `pi(A) = 1`.
3. **Data-derived quantities.** By definition a data-derived quantity of `B` is a
   function of `pi(B)` and `mu`. For `B = A`, `B = eps_u(A)` and `B = 1` the
   images are all trivial: `eps_u(A) <= A` and `pi(A) = 1`. So every
   data-derived quantity takes the same value on the three groups. Any bound
   comparing `pi(B)` with `pi(eps_t(B))` is a statement about the subgroup `P`
   and the images, and is compatible with any behaviour of `eps_t` inside
   `ker pi`.
4. **The copy of V in `Q`.**
   - By `leavitt-unit-group-carries-nontrivial-rigid-defect` and Theorem 2 of
     `research/artifacts/hyperbolic-leavitt-cover-relative-defect-2026-09-12.md`,
     `[EL_alpha(R), J] = 1` for `J = V_(1000)`, and `J` is isomorphic to `V`. So
     `J <= C_(R^x)(EL_alpha(R)) = Q`. For the cover, `Q = Z/N` is identified
     with `C_(R^x)(EL_alpha(R))` through `pi : G -> R^x`
     (`relative-defect-iff-surjective-compressor-endomorphisms`, Consequences).
   - `V` is infinite and simple (`thompson-v-finitely-presented-infinite-simple`).
     A homomorphism from `V` onto a finite group has a normal kernel of finite
     index. Simplicity makes the kernel trivial or all of `V`, and finiteness of
     the image rules out a trivial kernel. So every finite quotient of `V` is
     trivial.
5. **The group `A`.**
   - `eps_u` is injective on `Q` (item 2 of the source claim), so each
     `eps_u^k(J)` is isomorphic to `V`.
   - A homomorphism from `A = <eps_u^k(J) : k >= 0>` to a finite group is trivial
     on each generating subgroup, hence trivial. So `A` has no nontrivial finite
     quotient.
   - `eps_u(A)` is generated by the `eps_u^(k+1)(J)`, so `eps_u(A) <= A`.
6. **The witness is not compressed.** Let `z` be a lift of the defect element
   `j in J` (Theorem 3(c) of the artifact): `[z, Gamma_hat] <= N`, but
   `u_hat z u_hat^-1` is not in `Z`. Suppose `[z] = eps_u([z''])` with `z'' in Z`,
   i.e. `z = u_hat^-1 z'' u_hat k` for some `k in K = N`. Then
   `u_hat z u_hat^-1 = z'' . u_hat k u_hat^-1`, which lies in `Z . N = Z`, a
   contradiction. So `[z]` is not in `eps_u(Q)`, and since `eps_u(A) <= eps_u(Q)`,
   not in `eps_u(A)`. As `[z] in J <= A`, `eps_u` restricts to a strict
   self-embedding of `A`.
7. **Into the model.** For an injective homomorphism `sigma` of a host
   containing the configuration, item 5 of
   `realized-relative-twists-embed-in-outer-twist-group` embeds `Q`, and hence
   `A`, in `Q_s`, compatibly with `eps_u`. By step 2 applied inside `Q_s`, the
   image of `A` acts trivially on every finite-orbit datum.
8. **No property (T), no soficity.** Steps 1-3 are elementary and hold for any
   group with a mass-preserving action. Steps 4-7 are algebra in `R^x` and its
   cover. Property (T) and soficity are not used, so the statement correctly
   proves no nonsoficity.
