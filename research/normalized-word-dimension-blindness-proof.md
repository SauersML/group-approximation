Word evaluation commutes with direct sums, giving `(NSD4)`.  Normalized trace
averages the identical blocks and normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm cancels
the number of copies exactly as in `(NSD5)`.  Thus the complete finite
normalized word-statistic transcript is unchanged between dimensions `d`
and `kd`, proving the claim.
