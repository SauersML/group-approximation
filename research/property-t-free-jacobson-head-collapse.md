For J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>, prove that every norm-corona representation of
St_5(J) kills x_13(1-ST), with no use of property (T), Kazhdan
projections, or a theorem whose proof depends on either.

## Attempts

The rank-weighted cutoff argument proves the ordinary head-collapse
statement, but its Section 2 uses one-sided Kazhdan transport. The
elementary uniform support-rank bound and cutoff construction alone
give no replacement for that step. A property-(T)-free proof must
establish the necessary transport or a different obstruction using
additional algebraic structure. This retains that exact missing proof
obligation; it does not reopen the mathematical head statement.

**The root-kernel route is (T)-free in finite dimensions and blocked at
the corona (2026-09-08).**  `uniform-root-kernel-invariance-kills-one-sided-defect`
gives a Kazhdan-free kill of the defect roots: if the two root maps are
genuine additive homomorphisms and the conjugation and commutator tests
hold to within `2 sin(pi/m)` **for every coefficient at one coordinate**,
the common kernel is invariant under left multiplication by `s` and `t`,
`R/I` is finite, and `ts = 1` forces `st = 1` there.  Applied to an exact
finite-dimensional representation of `E_n(R)`, `n >= 3`, the hypotheses
hold with error zero, so no property (T) is needed there at all.

It does not transfer.  `root-kernel-invariance-is-not-pointwise-obtainable`
builds finite models over `J` in which all three root maps are exactly
additive, conjugation transport is exact, the constant Weyl relations are
exact, and every fixed commutator test is eventually **exact** -- and in
which, at every coordinate `N`, the coefficient `s^N` has
`X_N(s^N) = 1` while `Y_N(t s^N)` is at distance `2` from the identity.
So kernel invariance is not obtainable from pointwise corona data, and the
uniform lemma cannot be run against a corona representation.  Reviving this
route means excluding those models with Steinberg relations they do not
realise -- they prescribe nothing at the root positions into a third
coordinate -- which is not done.

**Rank pigeonhole on a locally finite certificate (stability-approximation,
swarm-0917, dies).**  The certificate itself needs no (T): with
`V_+=span(zeta_i : i>=0)`, `H=SL_fin(V_+)`, `u` the bilateral shift cell
`[[S,e],[0,T]]` (so `uHu^(-1) <= H`), and `c` a finitary transvection in the
second block centralizing `H`, some `l in H` gives `[ucu^(-1),l]` a finitary
transvection conjugate to `x_13(Q)`, hence `z in D_E(H)` with `H` locally
finite.  By `corona-representations-are-exact-on-locally-finite-subgroups`
every coordinate may be taken exact on a finite stage `H_m`, and
`binary-finitary-transvection-uniformly-controls-active-rank` bounds the
moved-space rank `R_m=dim Fix(H_m)^perp` of each exact stage by `4r`,
`r=rank(sigma(z)-1)`.  Since `R_m` increases in `m`, and the commutant corner
dimension `D_m` of `sigma(H_m)` on the moved space changes by at most
`O(r(R_(m+1)-R_m))`, a pigeonhole over `m` gives, for every `epsilon>0`, all
but `O(1/epsilon)` stages with `sigma(u)` carrying `Fix(H_m)^perp` onto
`Fix(H_(m+1))^perp` up to rank `epsilon r` and commutant codimension below
`epsilon r^2`.

*Where every member dies.*  The invariant this class controls is the pair
(moved-space rank, commutant dimension) of exact finite stages.  The step
that fails is the passage from "`sigma(ucu^(-1))` lies within rank
`epsilon r` of a unitary normalizing the good stage and codimension `epsilon
r^2` of its commutant" to "`sigma(ucu^(-1))` commutes with `sigma(l)` up to
`o(r)` rank".  Codimension slack `epsilon r^2` is unbounded against the
fixed finite group generated by `l` and the stage generators, so frame
averaging over that group gives no bound, and nothing else in the class
touches the specific element.  Calibration: `G'=SL_fin(F_2^(Z)) semidirect
<u>` carries the whole configuration (exact finite stages, compressor,
centralizer, `C=4` support bound, the pigeonhole itself), yet by
`binary-jacobson-finitary-whitehead-shift-is-marked-lef` the mark survives
in an injective corona representation.  This is the rank-weighted form of
the no-go already recorded in `mf-group-with-finite-central-compression-defect`
and `corona-representations-are-exact-on-locally-finite-subgroups`: any
T-free proof must use a relation of `St_5(J)` absent from `G'` (a second
noncommuting coefficient packet or the Laurent symbol relations), not finer
rank bookkeeping on the locally finite certificate.  No new node was
created; the refinement `rank(1-T_m)=2p(1-2^(1-m))` (row constant `2`
instead of `4`) changes no conclusion.
