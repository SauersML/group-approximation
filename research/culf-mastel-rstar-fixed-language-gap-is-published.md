**ESTABLISHED BY CITATION AND AN ELEMENTARY CHECK.**  Let

```text
R_*={x in F_2^4: x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=1}\{1000}.            (CRS1)
```

There is a total computable map from a Turing-machine index `m` to a finite,
nonsuccinct CSP instance using **only** the relation `R_*`, and a constant
`s_*<1` independent of `m`, with

```text
m halts      => inf_tau def_(R_m)(tau)=0,
m nonhalts   => inf_tau def_(R_m)(tau)>1-s_*.            (CRS2)
```

This holds in Culf--Mastel's constraint-variable, constraint-constraint, and
assignment-with-commutation weighted algebras.  The infimum is over finite-
dimensional traces.  A zero-defect sequence on the completeness side gives a
perfect Connes-embeddable tracial model in a matrix ultraproduct; conversely a
perfect CE model on the soundness side would give finite-dimensional defects
tending to zero and contradict `(CRS2)`.

The fixed-language specialization is direct.  By
`rstar-fano-ghost-affine-hull-criterion`, `CSP({R_*})` is NP-complete.  Also
`R_*` is non-two-variable-falsifiable: after fixing any two coordinates,
the odd-parity equation has exactly two completions in the other coordinates,
and deleting the single word `1000` removes at most one.  Thus the projection
of `R_*` to any coordinate pair is all of `F_2^2`.  Culf--Mastel Theorem 4.14
therefore applies with `Gamma={R_*}` by both its Boolean and non-TVF clauses;
Corollaries 4.17 and 4.18 give `(CRS2)`, the commutation-weighted version, and
the total exponential-time expansion to one finite instance.

There is consequently no missing quantum-polymorphism condition in the
**fixed-language** step.  A copy of `R_*` with two designated visible
coordinates and two private coordinates is already a commutativity gadget:
every classical pair extends, while any exact context model makes the two
visible PVMs commute.  Culf--Mastel Proposition 6.2 supplies the corresponding
dimension-independent weighted-algebra maps, so the replacement preserves a
constant soundness gap.

This does **not** provide the stronger commutation used by Fano support
pruning.  In the assignment-with-commutation algebra the commutator weight is

```text
mu_comm([P_a(x),P_b(y)])
 =sum_(i:x,y in V_i) pi(i),                              (CRS3)
```

so it controls only pairs which occur in one common CSP context.  The Fano
neighborhood argument needs the PVM algebras of distinct neighbors of one
context to commute mutually, even when those neighbors never occur together.
Oracularizability gives center--neighbor commutation, not this
neighbor--neighbor condition.

The distinction is real already in `M_2 tensor M_2`: the algebra generated by
`Z tensor 1` commutes with each of `1 tensor Z` and `1 tensor X`, while the
latter two do not commute.  Thus pairwise oracular star data cannot justify
cliquifying the neighborhood.  Adding the missing `R_*` commutativity gadgets
would impose a new condition on the perfect source model; Culf--Mastel do not
prove that their HALT models admit it.

Hence the fixed relation, effective finiteness, perfect CE completeness,
constant CE/matrix gap, and all **contextwise** commutation are settled.  The
remaining Fano gate is precisely a uniform cap selector or a new theorem
producing mutually commuting neighborhoods without destroying completeness.
