# Extend the sofic positive core over the compressors

Do not build microstates for `W` from scratch. Start from microstates that
already exist for a subgroup, and extend.

`positive-core-bernoulli-ce` (established) gives genuine matrix microstates for
`W₀ = (⊕_{X₀} C₂) ⋊ Γ` over the positive orbit `X₀ = TΓ/Γ`, because the
positive stabilizers `ℓΓℓ⁻¹` are separable in `Γ` and the action is therefore
sofic. `W` is generated by `W₀` together with the positive external matrices
`L₊`. So the only thing left is `simultaneous-dilation-splice`: dilate the
compressor endomorphisms to unitaries obeying the `SL_d(ℤ)` relations, keeping
Connes embeddability. The corpus states the consequence as `(FSD20)`.

## Why this route is structurally unlike the nine dead ones

Every architecture invalidated on `hyperlinear-wreath-model` — finite
quotients, free-probability carriers, half-space tensors, one-variable Laurent
fibers, graded towers, sofic-action permanence, target-class enrichment,
masa-normalizing tracial actions, block-monomial fibers — tries to produce a
model of the whole group by design, and dies because the coordinate action
cannot be modelled by permutations. This route never models the coordinate
action. It inherits an honest one on a sub-orbit where it genuinely exists, and
the entire difficulty is relocated into an extension problem about von Neumann
algebras.

That relocation is why the standing diagnostics do not apply. There is nothing
to check against `no_strict_compression_of_invariantSize`: no new carrier is
being proposed, and the invariant-size question never arises because the core's
microstates are given, not built. (T)-compatibility is satisfied by the same
token — `N₀` contains `L(Γ)`, a diffuse (T) algebra, and it is Connes
embeddable as a matter of established fact. `finite-quotient-blindness` does
not bite either: the finite Schreier approximations used for `X₀` are honest
finite quotients of `Γ`, and they see the positive part of the compression
precisely because the *positive* stabilizers are separable, whereas `Γ` itself
is not separable in `G`.

## Where it can still fail

The gate is genuinely hard and is now identified with a named open problem —
Connes embeddability of amalgamated free products over a nonamenable base
(Gao–Junge Problem 1) — plus a quotient step for the relations, which has no
general permanence at all. The Ore reduction is closed off. And there is an
independent hazard from the rigidity side: if `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs`
is established, `W` is not hyperlinear and every route into this claim dies,
this one included. Note that that obstruction lives entirely in the relative
commutant of `σ(Γ)` and never mentions a masa, so it is not deflected by any
structural property of the model — including the ones that keep
`growing-fiber-models` alive.

The honest read is that this is the most *advanced* live route on the flexible
side, in the sense of having the least left to do, while `growing-fiber-models`
is the most *independent*, in the sense of not sharing a failure mode with it.
