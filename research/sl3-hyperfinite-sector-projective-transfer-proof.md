The character criterion supplies flexible normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt
corrections of the amenable-limit `SL_3(Z[1/2])` assignments to exact
finite-dimensional representations.  Pad the auxiliary unitary by the
identity on the `o(d)` correction corner.  Finite word telescoping and the
`2`-Lipschitz commutator estimate transport all source and target
trace-square energies across the correction.  The exact projective
co-density estimate then sends the corrected target energy to zero, and
the vanishing corner transports the conclusion back.  These are precisely
the steps written out in the target claim.
