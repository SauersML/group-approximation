Finite-dimensional unitary representations of `SL_n(Z)`, `n >= 3`, have
finite image (Margulis superrigidity into compact groups) and factor through
congruence quotients (Bass--Milnor--Serre CSP).  Aka--Gelander--Soifer
(arXiv:1205.1140) construct a four-generated free subgroup dense in the
profinite completion of `SL_n(Z)`.  It therefore surjects onto every
`SL_n(Z/m)`, giving co-density, and is Zariski dense.  Bourgain--Varju
(arXiv:1006.3365) give arbitrary-modulus expansion for the reductions of any
fixed Zariski-dense generating set.  Since these reductions are the whole
congruence quotients here, this is precisely the required property `(tau)`.
