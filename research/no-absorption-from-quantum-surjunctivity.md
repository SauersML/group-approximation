If `A^G -> A^G x Y` is an absorption with `|A| = d`, then by (2) implies (1) of
`monomial-quantum-automata-are-topological-bernoulli-absorptions`, `(x)_G M_d` carries a shift-equivariant,
finite-propagation, unital *-endomorphism that is not onto. `quantum-surjunctivity-conjecture` says there is none.
