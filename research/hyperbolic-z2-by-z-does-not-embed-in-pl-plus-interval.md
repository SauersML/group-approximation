Let `A` be in `GL_2(Z)` with no eigenvalue equal to 1. Then `Z^2 ⋊_A Z` is isomorphic to
no subgroup of `PL_+(I)`, hence to no subgroup of Thompson's group `F`. More generally, if
`G ≤ PL_+(I)` has a finitely generated nontrivial normal abelian subgroup `N`, then some
nonzero homomorphism `N → R` is fixed by the conjugation action of `G`.

When `A` is hyperbolic with positive eigenvalues (trace ≥ 3), `Z^2 ⋊_A Z` is bi-orderable
and solvable.
