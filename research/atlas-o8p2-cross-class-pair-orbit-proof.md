The simultaneous `S3` action is transitive on ordered unequal index pairs.
For equal subgroup types this gives the first two orbits of `(O8P1)`; for
mixed type, equality versus inequality of the triality indices gives the
other two.  Inversion identifies the `N--O` and `O--N` orientations.  The
same action is transitive on each type's three diagonal classes, proving
`(O8P2)`.

Incidence counting from `(O8F1)` and the known carrier subgroup counts gives
unique calibrated carriers: `960*9=8640` for each natural class and
`120*36=4320` for each orthogonal class.  Finally quotient the two abstract
markings by their ambient normalizers.  The normalizer quotients are trivial
for `N_i` and `C2=Out(A8)` for `O_i`, yielding `(O8P3)`.
