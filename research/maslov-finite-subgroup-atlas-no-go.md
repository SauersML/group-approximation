Fix a group `Gamma`, a multiplier `alpha`, and any finite family of finite
subgroups `H_i <= Gamma` together with overlap subgroups `K_ij <= H_i cap H_j`.
Consider an obstruction that remembers only nonnegative multiplicity vectors of
`alpha|H_i`-projective irreducibles and demands equality of their restrictions
on the overlaps.

Such an atlas always has a nonzero exactly compatible multiplicity system in
the `alpha` sector.  Consequently no homogeneous positive-cone separation
using only these finite-subgroup restriction multiplicities can prove
`maslov-mod3-projective-defect-gap`.

The obstruction is global, not a shortage of finite local projective models.
