**UNIVERSAL GOAL; OPEN.** Prove action-independence of cost for every countably infinite group, or produce and verify two free p.m.p. actions of one group with unequal costs. No proof or counterexample route is supplied. Even for finitely generated groups, Bernoulli maximality supplies only one of the inequalities needed to prove equality for arbitrary actions.
