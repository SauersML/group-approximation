The all-variables-commuting quotient of a structural BCS group is the Boolean
group on the global variable set and `J`.  Its negative regular corner is the
uniform direct sum of all global assignments with `J=-1`; restriction to any
context is therefore a multiple of the negative regular representation of the
context Boolean group.  Taking the central product with each actual finite
role/address packet preserves this statement for every combined source pair.

For a finite group `F` with central involution `J`, the projection
`(1-lambda(J))/2` commutes with `lambda(F)`.  Decomposing `F` into left cosets
of a subgroup `H` containing `J` proves `(FMT2)`.  The common multiple in
`(FMT3)` then proves `(FMT4)`, exactly as in the unmarked stationary-flow
argument.

Finally, equivalent finite-dimensional subgroup representations are related
by a unitary.  Use these unitaries as stable letters, independently for all
non-tree edges.  This realizes every covariance relator exactly and leaves
`J=-I` throughout.
