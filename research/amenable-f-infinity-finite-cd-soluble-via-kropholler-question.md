Let `G` be amenable of type `F_∞` with `cd G < ∞`. Forget type `F_∞`. Then
`amenable-groups-of-finite-cd-are-virtually-solvable` gives that `G` is virtually
soluble. ∎

This route is a specialisation, and a lossy one: the target keeps type `F_∞`. With
`F_∞`, type `FP` comes for free (`FP_∞` plus finite cd, Brown, *Cohomology of Groups*,
VIII.6), which is exactly what Degrijse's Theorem A pays the domain hypothesis for.
