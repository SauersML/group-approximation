Notation as in the claim. `ε_ij = u^i e_V u^{-j}` (`0 ≤ i,j < k`), `W = ⊔_i T^iV`, `u_V = Σ_r u^r e_{V_r}`, where the
first-return time takes finitely many values `r` on clopen sets `V_r`.

1. **Windows.** `V`, the sets `V_r`, and the sets `T^iV` are finite unions of cylinders. Once `2ℓ+1` exceeds all
   their radii, `D_ℓ` evaluates their indicators correctly at every position `t` of `y_ℓ`, since each window of
   `y_ℓ` of length `2ℓ+1` is a word of `X`.
   - So `φ_ℓ(ε_ij) = P^i D_ℓ(e_V) P^{-j}` is a partial permutation matrix.
   - The relations `φ_ℓ(ε_ij)φ_ℓ(ε_i'j') = δ_{ji'}φ_ℓ(ε_ij')` hold exactly. At a `V`-position `t`, the window is
     a word of `X`, and `V ∩ T^{i'-j}V = ∅` in `X`.
2. **First return in the model.** `Q_ℓ = φ_ℓ(u_V) = Σ_r P^r D_ℓ(e_{V_r})` sends `δ_t`, for a `V`-position `t`, to
   `δ_{t+r(t)}`.
   - For large `ℓ`, `t + r(t)` is the next `V`-position of `y_ℓ`: the window around `t` is a word of `X`, and in
     `X` the first return time on `V_r` is `r`.
   - `y_ℓ` is periodic, so "next `V`-position" is a cyclic permutation of each orbit of `V`-positions. So `Q_ℓ` is
     a permutation matrix on the `V`-block and zero elsewhere.
   - Likewise `φ_ℓ(u_V^{-1}) = Q_ℓ^{-1}` on the `V`-block.
   - For each bound `D`, `φ_ℓ(u_V^a) = Q_ℓ^a` for `|a| ≤ D` once `ℓ` is large, because `u_V^a` is a finite sum of
     terms `e_{T^j V_{a,j}} u^j` with `|j|` bounded by `D·max r`.
3. **Genuine ring homomorphism.** Let `A_ℓ` be the subring of `End(F_q^{V-positions})` generated by
   `Q_ℓ^{±1}`. It is finite and commutative.
   - Define `Ψ_ℓ` on `M_k(F_q[t^{±1}]) ≅ M_k(F_q[u_V^{±1}]) ⊆ e_W R e_W` by `t ↦ Q_ℓ` and
     `ε_ij ↦ φ_ℓ(ε_ij)`. It is a unital ring homomorphism into `End(F_q^{W-positions})`, and it factors through
     `M_k(A_ℓ)`.
   - By steps 1–2, `Ψ_ℓ` and `φ_ℓ` agree on each fixed finite set of elements for large `ℓ`.
4. **Group level.** `Λ` is the image of `SL_{3k}(F_q[t^{±1}]) = EL_3(M_k(F_q[t^{±1}]))` under
   `x ↦ x + (1 - e_W)I_3`. The map `x + (1-e_W)I_3 ↦ Ψ_ℓ(x) + (I - Ψ_ℓ(e_W I_3))` is a genuine homomorphism
   `Λ → GL_{3m_ℓ}(F_q)` factoring through `SL_{3k}(A_ℓ)`.
   - On a finite `F ⊆ Λ`, the entries involved are finitely many elements of bounded degree in `u_V^{±1}`, so
     `φ_ℓ = Ψ_ℓ` on `F` for large `ℓ`.
   - Composing with `ρ_ℓ` gives genuine unitary representations of `Λ`.
5. **Strong convergence restricts.** For `z ∈ C[Λ] ⊆ C[G]`, `‖λ_G(z)‖ = ‖λ_Λ(z)‖`, and
   `ρ_ℓφ_ℓ(z) = ρ_ℓΨ_ℓ(z)` for large `ℓ`.
