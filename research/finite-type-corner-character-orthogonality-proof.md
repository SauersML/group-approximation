The standard central-idempotent formula for `C[K]` proves self-adjointness,
idempotence, and centrality of `(FTC1)`. The coefficient of the identity is
`d_rho chi_rho(1)/|K|=d_rho^2/|K|`, giving the canonical trace. Augmentation
is evaluation in the trivial representation and therefore vanishes by
orthogonality because `d_rho>1`. Evaluation in any scalar character of
`Gamma` is evaluation in the one-dimensional representation `eta|_K` and
vanishes by the same orthogonality.
