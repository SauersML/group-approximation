Let `A=<X|R>` be a finitely presented **hyperlinear** group, let `S` be a
finite set of words in `X`, let `C<=A` be the subgroup generated by the
elements represented by `S`, and let `h` be a word with

```text
A=<C,h>.                                                (AR0)
```

No perfectness, residual finiteness, property `(T)` or spectral gap is
assumed.  Write `[U,V]=UVU^*V^*`, `tr` for the normalized trace and
`||.||_2` for the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  For an assignment
`rho:X->U(d)` and `U in U(d)` put, as in
`projective-transfer-linear-bound-equals-no-ultraproduct-leak`,

```text
delta(rho)=max_(r in R)||rho(r)-I||_2,
q(rho,U)=sum_(s in S)(1-|tr([U,rho(s)])|^2),
e_h(rho,U)=1-|tr([U,rho(h)])|^2.                        (ARD)
```

The following are equivalent.

1. **Regular-trace collapse.**  For every tracial matrix ultraproduct `M`
   and every unitary representation `pi:A->U(M)` with
   `tau(pi(g))=0` for all `g!=1`,

   ```text
   pi(C)' cap M = pi(A)' cap M.                         (AR1)
   ```

2. **Linear projective transfer.**  There is `L<infinity` such that every
   dimension `d`, every assignment `rho:X->U(d)` and every `U in U(d)`
   satisfy

   ```text
   e_h(rho,U) <= L(q(rho,U)+delta(rho)).                (AR2)
   ```

3. **Collapse for every trace profile.**  `(AR1)` holds for every unitary
   representation `pi:A->U(M)` into a tracial matrix ultraproduct, with no
   condition on `tau(pi(g))`.                            (AR3)

Moreover `(AR2)=>(AR3)` holds for every finitely presented `A` with
`(AR0)`, with no hyperlinearity hypothesis; hyperlinearity is used only in
`(AR1)=>(AR2)`.

The two elementary identities that replace perfectness are, for
`W in U(k)` and complex conjugation in a fixed basis,

```text
||W tensor conj(W)-I||_2^2 = 2-2|tr(W)|^2,              (AR4)
tr(W direct_sum I_k)=(1+tr(W))/2,
1-|tr(W direct_sum I_k)|^2 <= ||W-I||_2^2/2.            (AR5)
```

`(AR4)` turns source phases into honest commutators of the adjoint tuple;
`(AR5)` turns the projective target energy of a doubled witness back into
an honest commutator.

**Consequences.**

- Character-twisted projective leaks are not separate obstructions.  If
  assignments `rho_n` with `delta(rho_n)->0` and unitaries `U_n` have
  source commutators `[U_n,rho_n(s)]` approaching scalars in `||.||_2`
  (for instance scalars forming a nontrivial character of `C`), while
  `e_h(rho_n,U_n)` stays bounded below, then `(AR2)` fails, hence `(AR1)`
  fails: some
  regular-trace representation carries an *untwisted* `C`-central unitary
  that does not commute with `pi(h)`.  Explicitly the witness is the
  ultraproduct of `Ad(U_n tensor I)` against the adjoint of the regularized
  tuple.
- The trace-profile warning in
  `projective-transfer-linear-bound-equals-no-ultraproduct-leak` is void for
  hyperlinear ambient groups: unrestricted and regular-trace collapse are
  the same assertion, and both are the linear projective inequality.
- None of the three assertions is established here for any particular
  arithmetic pair.
