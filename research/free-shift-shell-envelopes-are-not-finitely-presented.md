title: A shell envelope with free-product near shift group cannot be finitely presented

Let P be infinite and finitely generated. If the canonical map
P*<s>->R_nu is injective, then E_nu is NOT finitely presented.

Consequently every enumeration giving a finitely presented shell
envelope must introduce some nonidentity reduced word of P*<s>
that becomes identity as a near permutation. This is a necessary
condition on E_nu itself, not merely on its singularity stabilizers.

It does not refute the existential universal shell route: that
route can choose an enumeration with additional near relations.
Nor are additional near relations asserted sufficient for finite
presentation. The proof applies whether or not P is perfect or nu
is computable.
