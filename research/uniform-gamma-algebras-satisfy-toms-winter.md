Let `A` be a separable, simple, unital, nuclear, non-elementary C*-algebra
with uniform property Gamma.  Then `A` is Z-stable if and only if `A` has
strict comparison of positive elements, and both are equivalent to finite
nuclear dimension.

Castillejos--Evington--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:1912.04207, Theorem A: "The
Toms--Winter conjecture holds among separable, simple, unital, nuclear,
non-elementary C*-algebras which have uniform property Gamma."  The paper
extracts this from its Theorem 5.6, which combines Matui--Sato's property (SI)
with the passage from uniform Gamma to a uniform McDuff property.

So the remaining direction of Toms--Winter in the unital case is exactly:
does strict comparison give uniform property Gamma?  Carrion et al.,
arXiv:2310.20594, state the same reduction for uniform tracial completions
after their Question 1.5.
