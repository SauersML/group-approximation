**Established (literature import, unrefereed preprint).** The Dehn function of
Thompson's group V satisfies `δ_V ≼ n^6`.

History of the upper bound: `n^11` (Guba 2000), then `n^6` (Lorrain,
August 2026). The lower bound `n^2` holds because V is not hyperbolic.

The preprint was not refereed as of 2026-09-13, and the argument has not been
re-derived in this graph.
