**ESTABLISHED.**  Conventions: `[a,b]=a b a^-1 b^-1`; for a group `G`,
`TorNor(G)` is the normal closure of its finite-order elements, as in
`torsion-generated-mf-absorbers-die-in-torsion-free-targets`.

**(STF1) Steinberg groups are torsion-generated.**  Let `R` be any unital
associative ring and `n>=3`.  For distinct `i,j` put

```text
w_ij = x_ij(1) x_ji(-1) x_ij(1)  in St_n(R).
```

Then `w_ij^8=1`, and every homomorphism `f:St_n(R)->H` with `f(w_12)=1`
is trivial.  Hence `TorNor(St_n(R))=St_n(R)`: every homomorphism from
`St_n(R)`, from `EL_n(R)`, or from any quotient of either, to a torsion-free
group is trivial.  No commutativity, characteristic or finiteness hypothesis
on `R` is used.

**(STF2) Rng version: idempotents and additive torsion.**  Let `I` be an
associative ring without unit, `n>=3`, and `EL_n(I)` the subgroup of
`GL_n(Z+I)` generated by the elementary matrices `e_ij(a)`, `a in I`.

1. If `a in I` and `ma=0` for some `m>=1`, then `e_ij(a)^m=1`.
2. If `e in I` is a nonzero idempotent, the word
   `u=e_12(e)e_21(-e)e_12(e)` satisfies `u^2=diag(1-2e,1-2e,1,...)` and
   `u^4=1`; either `u^2!=1` or `2e=0` and `e_12(e)` has order `2`.  So
   `EL_n(I)` has nontrivial torsion.
3. For every homomorphism `f:EL_n(I)->H` to a torsion-free group and every
   idempotent `e in I`, `f` kills `e_ij(a)` for all `a` in the two-sided ideal
   of `I` generated by `e`.

**(STF3) The rng escape gate.**  Suppose `EL_3(I)` is finitely generated,
torsion-free and has no nontrivial finite quotient.  Then `I` is finitely
generated as a ring, `I=I^2`, `I` is additively torsion-free, `I` contains no
nonzero idempotent, and, unless `I=0`, `I` is noncommutative.

**(STF4) Consequences.**  Let `n>=3` and let `Gamma` be a group normally
generated by images of groups `St_n(R)` over unital rings (a *normally
elementary* group).  Then:

1. every homomorphism from `Gamma` to a torsion-free group is trivial; in a
   torsion-free group every subgroup that is a homomorphic image of such a
   `Gamma` is trivial;
2. if moreover `Gamma` has no nontrivial finite quotient, then `Gamma` has no
   proper finite-index subgroup, so `Gamma` has neither a nontrivial
   torsion-free quotient nor a nontrivial torsion-free finite-index subgroup;
3. for every `KazhdanCompressionCore` whose source is such a `Gamma` and whose
   ambient group `E` is torsion-free, `iota` is trivial, the defect set is
   `{1}` and the defect normal subgroup is trivial.

**The obstructed class.**  Call a construction for the root
`torsion-free-sofic-exact-mf-radical-over-z` *elementary-sourced* if its
Kazhdan kernel `K`, or its compression source `L` on the extrinsic or
defect-core routes, is obtained from a normally elementary Kazhdan group by
(i) taking a homomorphic image, (ii) inserting it into the ambient group, or,
when that group has no finite quotients, (iii) passing to finite index.  The
ambient group `G` of the root is torsion-free, so every member dies at the
same step: the image of the Weyl word `w_12`, of order dividing `8`, is
trivial, and (STF1) propagates this to the whole source.  The invariant is the
universal torsion-free quotient, which is trivial for every normally
elementary group.  The class contains every Ershov--Jaikin--Kassabov source
of type `A_(n-1)`, `n>=3`, used in the graph:

- `EL_3(LC(X,F_q) semidirect Z)` from
  `lef-kazhdan-group-without-finite-quotients-exists`, the graph's witness
  refuting "LEF + (T) + no finite quotient implies trivial";
- the simple Kazhdan non-MF groups `EL_N(A)/Z` of
  `paradoxical-steinberg-elementary-simple-kazhdan-not-mf` and
  `paradoxical-cantor-actions-give-simple-kazhdan-not-mf`;
- the Leavitt and Kun--Thom elementary sources of `universal-leavitt-el4-nonsofic`
  and `kun-thom-actors-have-no-torsion-free-finite-index-subgroups`;
- the Kida--Tucker-Drob parabolic `H(Z[t,t^-1])` and its quotient `J`
  underlying `torsion-free-lef-kazhdan-non-rf-seed` (normally generated by
  the `EL_3` factor; see the proof).

**What survives.**  The obstruction does not touch torsion-free infinite-index
Kazhdan subgroups of normally elementary groups, torsion-free finite-index
subgroups of normally elementary groups that do have finite quotients (the
seed; these are then ruled out as kernels by
`mapping-torus-mf-radical-lies-in-finite-residual`), relative elementary
groups `EL_3(I)` over rngs, or Kazhdan groups whose property `(T)` comes from
buildings or small cancellation (Titz--Witzel lattices, Hull--Osin quotients
of torsion-free hyperbolic Kazhdan groups).  On the rng level, (STF3) names
the exact remaining gate: a finitely generated, noncommutative, additively
torsion-free, idempotent-free rng `I` with `I=I^2` for which `EL_3(I)` is
Kazhdan, torsion-free and without finite quotients.  The imported Kazhdan
theorem `ershov-jaikin-kassabov-steinberg-groups-have-property-t` is stated
for `St_Phi(R)` and `E_Phi(R)` over finitely generated rings in the unital
sense in which `St_n(R)` is defined, so it does not by itself supply property
`(T)` for such `EL_3(I)`.

Other Chevalley types are not claimed here.

**Referee check (2026-09-17, ref-02).**  SOUND.  "Rank at least three" in the
title means `n>=3` for `St_n` (root system `A_(n-1)`, Lie rank at least two);
the bound is sharp, since `St_2(Z)` maps onto `Z`.  Re-derived (C1)--(C2),
(P1)--(P6), the inversion `h w h^-1=w^-1` and hence (W8); the one-trivial-Weyl-
word propagation of Steps 3--4; the `u^2=(1-2e)I_2` computation of (STF2); the
subring, `I/I^2` and determinant-trick arguments of (STF3); (STF4) against
the definitions in `GroupApproximation/Sofic/KazhdanCompressionCore.lean`; and
the normal generation of `H(R)` by its `EL_3` factor.  The sign script passes.
Every nontrivial `EL_n(R)`, `n>=3`, contains `w_12` with `w_12^2=diag(-1,-1,1,...)`,
so the conclusion is consistent with all torsion-free lattices and linear
groups: none contains a nontrivial Steinberg image.  (W8) is classical and
should be read as such: by Milnor, *Introduction to Algebraic K-Theory*
(Ann. of Math. Studies 72, 1971), §10, the kernel of `St_n(Z)->SL_n(Z)` has
order two for `n>=3`; it contains `w_12^4`, so `w_12^8=1` in `St_n(Z)` and,
by functoriality along `Z->R`, in every `St_n(R)`.  The self-contained
derivation above avoids this input.  The torsion-generation consequences
(STF1), (STF4) are elementary and are not claimed as new.
