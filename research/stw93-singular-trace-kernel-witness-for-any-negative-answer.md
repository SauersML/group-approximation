different from the limit trace `τ_ω`, then there is a positive contraction
`h∈J_τ∩D` such that

```text
τ_ω(h)=0 < q(h).
```

Consequently `q` is not dominated on `D_+` by any finite constant multiple of
`τ_ω`.
