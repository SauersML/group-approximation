Write `chi_V` for the indicator of a clopen `V`, so `u chi_V = chi_(T(V)) u`.

**Corner isometry.** With `s = u chi_U` and `t = chi_U u^-1`,

```text
t s = chi_U u^-1 u chi_U = chi_U = p,
s t = u chi_U u^-1 = chi_(T(U)),
```

so `p - st = chi_U - chi_(T(U)) = chi_(U \ T(U))`, using `T(U) <= U`. Both `s` and `t` lie in
`pRp`: `p s p = chi_U u chi_U = chi_(T(U)) u chi_U = u chi_U = s` since `chi_(T(U)) chi_U =
chi_(T(U))` and `u chi_U = chi_(T(U)) u`; similarly for `t`.

**Inflation.** For an idempotent `p` and `s,t` in `pRp` with `ts = p`, put `s' = s + 1 - p`
and `t' = t + 1 - p`. Then `t(1-p) = 0` and `(1-p)s = 0`, so

```text
t' s' = ts + t(1-p) + (1-p)s + (1-p)^2 = p + (1 - p) = 1,
s' t' = st + (1-p),      1 - s't' = p - st.
```

So `(s',t')` is a one-sided pair of `R` and its defect is the corner defect `p - st`. Applied to
the corner isometry above, the defect is `chi_(U \ T(U))`, which therefore lies in `d(R)` and
hence in `hh(R)`.

**Calibration computation.** On `X = Z u {-inf,+inf}` with `U = {+inf} u Z_(>=0)`, the collar is
`{0}`, and the ideal generated by `chi_({0})` contains `chi_({n}) = u^n chi_({0}) u^-n` for every
`n`, hence all finitely supported functions and their products with `u^j`; that ideal is
`M_fin(Z,k)`, with quotient the crossed product over the two fixed points, `k[z,z^-1]` twice.
