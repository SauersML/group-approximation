For a perfect matching `M` of `[2n]` write `h_M` for the fixed-point-free
involution of `[2n]` swapping the two ends of every pair of `M`.

A law `mu` on perfect matchings of `[2n]` is *exactly 2-wise uniform* if
`Pr_mu[e in M] = 1/(2n-1)` for every pair `e`, and
`Pr_mu[e, f in M] = 1/((2n-1)(2n-3))` for disjoint pairs `e, f`. Every
`t`-wise uniform law with `t >= 2` is 2-wise uniform. Write
`D_mu = d nu_(mu,m) / d U_(2n)` for the pull-back density, as in
`finite-moment-pairing-2to1-hardness`.

**Theorem.** Fix `m >= 2`, real `q > 1`, `C >= 1`, and a sequence
`theta_n` with `log(1/theta_n) = o(n)`. There is `n_1` such that for every
`n >= n_1`, every set `E` of colorings of `[2n]`, and every exactly 2-wise
uniform law `mu` with

```
||1_E D_mu||_q <= C    and    nu_(mu,m)(E) >= theta_n,
```

every subgroup `H <= S_(2n)` that contains `h_M` for all `M in supp(mu)`
contains `A_(2n)`. Equivalently: no structure on the labels whose
automorphism group does not contain `A_(2n)` (a proper nonempty subset, a
nontrivial partition, a vector space or affine space, a projective line, a
code, a design, a graph other than empty or complete) is preserved by the
swaps of all kernel pairings.

For the BKM roughly balanced sets `E_(K,m,n)` the mass `nu_(mu,m)(E)` is the
same for every law and is bounded below by a positive constant at the chosen
`K` (claim `pullback-smoothness-needs-exponentially-many-pairings`), so the
hypothesis on `theta_n` holds there.

**Corollary (single orbit).** Under the same hypotheses on `mu` and
`n >= n_1`, if `mu` is uniform
on one orbit of a group `G <= S_(2n)` acting on matchings, then `mu` is the
uniform law on all `(2n-1)!!` perfect matchings. In particular if the
automorphisms of a regular 2-to-1 instance fixing a left vertex `u` act
transitively on the edges at `u`, and the pairing law at `u` is exactly
2-wise uniform and meets the moment hypothesis, then that law is uniform on
all pairings: the construction is fully rich at `u` in the sense of BKM
Appendix B. (Here an automorphism acts on the labels of `u` by some
permutation `g_u`, and the kernel pairing of the image edge is `g_u` applied
to the kernel pairing of the edge, so the edge-uniform law pushes forward to
the uniform law on one `G`-orbit, `G` the image group of the `g_u`.) The
orbit of a matching `M` need not consist of matchings whose swaps lie in
`G`, so this corollary has its own proof and is not a special case of the
theorem.

**Scope.** Every step except the order step is an exact finite identity; the
order step imports Maroti's bound. The theorem is about individual laws; it
does not construct or refute any NP-hard instance, and it leaves open laws
whose kernel involutions generate a group containing `A_(2n)`, including
laws spread over many orbits of a small group. The M12 computation in the
artifact script shows that the order step cannot be removed: the 396
fixed-point-free involutions of the 5-transitive Mathieu group `M_12` carry
an exact 2-wise uniform law.
