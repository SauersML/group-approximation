**ESTABLISHED (Goldbring--Sinclair, arXiv:2509.15086v1, Corollary 5.8).**
Let `C^*(F_n x F_n)_epsilon` be the universal C-star algebra on unitaries
`u_1,...,u_n,v_1,...,v_n` satisfying

```text
||[u_i,v_j]|| <= epsilon.
```

Every positive softening is RFD and has a standard computable presentation,
uniformly in `epsilon`.  For a star polynomial `p`, put

```text
alpha_(m,p) = ||p|| in C^*(F_n x F_n)_(1/m),
alpha_p     = ||p|| in C^*(F_n x F_n).
```

There is no algorithm which, on input `p` and positive rational `q`, returns
an `m` with `|alpha_(m,p)-alpha_p|<q`.

Therefore a runtime-independent self-aware compiler cannot be obtained by
softening exact cross-commutation in operator norm and effectively waiting
for the full maximal-tensor norm to stabilize.  The scope matters: this does
not exclude a direct normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt estimate which avoids
computing the universal operator norm.
