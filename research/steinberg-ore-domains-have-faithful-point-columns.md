**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `k` be any field.

1. **Faithful point column (groupoids).** Let `𝒢` be an ample Hausdorff groupoid with compact unit
   space `𝒢^(0)`, and `A_k(𝒢)` its Steinberg algebra (locally constant compactly supported functions,
   convolution product). For `η ∈ 𝒢^(0)` let `𝒢_η = s^(-1)(η)` and let `A_k(𝒢)` act on the
   finitely supported functions `k^(𝒢_η)` by `f · δ_γ = Σ_(s(α) = r(γ)) f(α) δ_(αγ)`.
   Let `C ⊆ A_k(𝒢)` be a nonzero subring (unital or not) that is a domain satisfying the left Ore
   condition `Ca ∩ Cb ≠ 0` for all nonzero `a, b ∈ C` (every commutative domain does). Then there is
   `η ∈ 𝒢^(0)` such that `c |-> c · δ_η = c|_(𝒢_η)` is injective on `C`. Equivalently the annihilator
   of `δ_η` in `C` is zero, so `C · δ_η ≅ C` as left `C`-modules.

2. **Faithful point column (crossed products).** Let `P` be a group acting on a compact Hausdorff space
   `X`, `LC(X, k)` the locally constant functions, and `B = LC(X, k) ⋊ P` (free left `LC(X, k)`-module on
   `{v_p}`, `v_p f = (p·f) v_p`, `(p·f)(y) = f(p^(-1) y)`). For `x ∈ X` the formula
   `π_x(f v_p) δ_q = f(pq·x) δ_(pq)` defines a representation `π_x` of `B` on `k^(P)`, and
   `π_x(Σ_p f_p v_p) δ_e = Σ_p f_p(p·x) δ_p`. For every nonzero left Ore domain `C ⊆ B` there is
   `x ∈ X` with `c |-> π_x(c) δ_e` injective on `C`. No freeness, minimality, finite type or complexity
   hypothesis is used.

3. **Orbit growth bound.** In part 2 let `P` be generated by a finite symmetric set, with balls
   `B_P(N)`. Let `S ⊆ C` be finite, put `supp(Σ f_p v_p) = { p : f_p ≠ 0 }`, let `m >= 1` bound `|p|` on
   all supports of elements of `S`, and let `V_n(S)` be the span of all products of between `1` and `n`
   elements of `S`. Then

   `dim_k V_n(S) <= |B_P(mn)|` for all `n >= 1`.

   The same proof in part 1 gives `dim V_n(S) <= |W_n ∩ 𝒢_η|`, where `W_n` is the union of all products
   of at most `n` members of a finite family of compact open bisections carrying `S`.

4. **Hosted groups.** If `G` is a group with finite generating set `T`, `k[G]` is a left Ore domain, and
   `φ : k[G] -> B` is an injective algebra homomorphism (unital or not), then there is `m >= 1` with
   `|B_G(n)| <= |B_P(mn)|` for all `n >= 1`.

5. **Polynomial-growth acting groups.** If `|B_P(N)| <= K N^D` for all `N >= 1`, then every left Ore
   domain subalgebra of `B` has Gelfand–Kirillov dimension at most `D`. In particular the Laurent
   polynomial ring `k[Z^(D+1)]` has no injective algebra homomorphism into `LC(X, k) ⋊ P`, unital or not,
   for any compact `X` and any action. For `P = Z^2`: no `LC(X, k) ⋊ Z^2`, whatever `X` is, contains
   `k[Z^3]`.

**Class killed.** Crossed-host witnesses `(P, X)` for
`decidable-group-algebras-have-fp-cantor-crossed-hosts` with `P` of polynomial growth, whatever the space.
This covers the pure `Z^2` lane with any free minimal SFT, of any entropy or language complexity.
- **Invariant.** The column of one point. For an Ore input it is faithful, and it is supported on the
  orbit ball `B_P(mn)`.
- **Failing step.** The embedding of `k[Z^(D+1)]`, a decidable, torsion-free, Farrell–Jones input.
  It fails before finite presentation, simplicity or rigidity is ever used.
- **Earlier results.** `poly-growth-cantor-hosts-need-exponential-complexity` needed complexity
  `e^(o(N))`, and Attempt 8 on `decidable-group-algebras-have-fp-cantor-crossed-hosts` needed slow `X`.
  Here the escape through exponential complexity is closed for Ore inputs.

**What escapes.**
- Non-Ore inputs. `k[F_2]` is a domain but not Ore: for free generators `a, b`,
  `k[F_2](a - 1) ∩ k[F_2](b - 1) = 0`, because the augmentation ideal of a free group ring is a free left
  module on `a - 1, b - 1` (recalled, not load-bearing). Whether `LC(X, k) ⋊ Z^2` can host `k[F_2]` for an exponential-complexity `X` stays open.
  Note that the growth bound would fail there if the lemma extended to all domains.
- Acting groups or groupoids whose orbits grow exponentially. Examples:
  - `P = Z^2 × F_d`, which carries `B_Ω ⊗ L_∂ ≅ LC(Ω × ∂F_d, k) ⋊ (Z^2 × F_d)`;
  - the Cuntz groupoid of `L(1,2)`.
  
  In these the count is vacuous. The faithful column still exists and is a constraint on target (E) of
  `decidable-algebras-embed-in-z2-sft-boundary-product-hosts`: any `F_2[Z^4]` there acts faithfully on a
  single column `k^(Z^2 × F_d)`.
- Unit-group embeddings `G ≤ B^x` without linear independence of the group elements.

**Recalled examples for part 4 (not load-bearing).** These show that Ore inputs beyond free abelian ones
exist:
- If `G` is torsion-free elementary amenable, then `k[G]` is a domain (Kropholler–Linnell–Moody 1988).
- If `G` is amenable and `k[G]` is a domain, then it is an Ore domain (Tamari 1957).

Given both, part 4 applies to every torsion-free virtually polycyclic `G`: its growth degree is at most
that of `P`. The ESTABLISHED statement uses only the Ore hypothesis as stated. Part 5 needs neither
citation, because a commutative domain is Ore.
