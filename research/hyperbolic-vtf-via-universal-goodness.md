Assume every word-hyperbolic group is good. By
`good-cocompact-proper-groups-are-virtually-torsion-free` (G), each one is virtually
torsion-free. The normal core of a torsion-free finite-index subgroup contains
`R_f(G)`, so `R_f(G)` is torsion-free.

The hypothesis is stronger than needed. Surjectivity of inflation
`H^q(G_hat;F_p) -> H^q(G;F_p)` in large degrees, for the primes dividing the orders of
finite subgroups, already suffices ((G') of the same node). The route is one-way: no
argument is known that universal residual finiteness implies universal goodness.
