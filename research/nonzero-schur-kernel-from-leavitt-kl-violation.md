The Leavitt unit group is superperfect (Khanh, arXiv:2609.08428v1, unverified
here), so its own Schur multiplier is zero. The kill therefore lives over the
amalgam `R^x *_(<a>) (<a> x Z)`, not over `R^x` itself.
