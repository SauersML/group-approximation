**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `S` be a unital ring with an injective unital ring
homomorphism `S -> prod_omega M_(N_n)(k_n)`, where the `k_n` are commutative
fields.  Let `R_0 <= S` be the unital subring generated by a finite set
`{r_1, ..., r_m}`.  Let `w, z` be units of `S` with

```text
w R_0 w^(-1) <= R_0,      z r = r z  for all r in R_0.
```

Then `w z w^(-1)` commutes with every element of `R_0`.  Consequently, for
the transported defect ideal

```text
I_S(R_0, w, z) = ( w z w^(-1) p w z^(-1) w^(-1) - p : p in R_0 )_S
```

we get `I_S(R_0,w,z) = 0`.

**Consequences.**
1. *Outer compressors are forced.*  Take `(R, psi, R_0, z)` with (R1)–(R4) of
   `matricial-compression-ring-exists` and `I != 0`.  No unit `w` of any
   exactly matricial ring `S` containing `R_0 u {z}` satisfies
   `w x w^(-1) = psi(x)` for all `x in R_0 u {z}`.  In particular the skew
   Laurent ring `R[t, t^(-1); psi]` is not exactly matricial.  Nor is the
   crossed product `LC(X,k) semidirect Lambda` exactly matricial, when `psi`
   is induced by a homeomorphism normalizing the `Gamma`-action on `X` and
   `Lambda` is the group generated by `Gamma` and that homeomorphism.
2. *Residually finite extended actions die.*  With `R = LC(X,k) semidirect Gamma`,
   suppose `Lambda` acts minimally, topologically freely and residually
   finitely.  Then `residually-finite-actions-give-matricial-crossed-products`
   makes `LC(X,k) semidirect Lambda` exactly matricial, and consequence 1 kills
   the recipe.  For free `Lambda`,
   `free-group-crossed-product-matricial-iff-invariant-measure` turns this into
   the following: every `Lambda`-invariant probability measure kills it.
3. *Ring parallel of MF non-closure.*  `mf-not-closed-under-integer-extensions`
   shows MF fails for `Z`-extensions of LEF groups.  So a surviving
   (XMF)-recipe needs the ring-level analogue: an exactly matricial `R` whose
   skew Laurent extension by `psi` is not exactly matricial.
