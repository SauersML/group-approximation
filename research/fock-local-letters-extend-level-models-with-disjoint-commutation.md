**ESTABLISHED** (proof: Section 2 of the linked artifact, route
`fock-local-letters-disjoint-commutation-proof`).

**Setting.**
- `G = L_(F_q)(1,2)^x` acts faithfully on `U_∞ = LC({0,1}^N, F_q)`, and `L = ∪_j GL_(2^j)(F_q)` is the level
  unit group.
- An element `g` is supported on a clopen `A` if it preserves `LC(A)` and is the identity on `LC(A^c)`.
- `B_g^A` is the set of level elements that preserve both `LC(A)` and `LC(A^c)` and are conjugated into
  `L` by `g`.

**Theorem.** Let `I ⊆ G` be countable, with supports `A_g`. The Fock tower `a -> Λ(a)` on `Λ(U_j)`
extends to a rank model of the group `𝒢_loc(I)` generated by `L` and letters `t_g`, with relations:
* **(R1)** the relations of `L`;
* **(R2)** `t_g a t_g^(−1) = g a g^(−1)` for `a ∈ B_g^(A_g)`;
* **(R3)** `[t_g, a] = 1` for level `a` supported on `A_g^c`;
* **(R4)** `[t_g, t_h] = 1` for `A_g ∩ A_h = ∅`.

The model is injective on `L`. Each letter is `X_(g,j) ⊗ 1` on
`Λ(U_(A_g)^(j)) ⊗ Λ(U_(A_g^c)^(j))`, so (R3) and (R4) hold exactly at every stage. In odd
characteristic the letters are even (parity-preserving), which (R4) needs because reordering wedge
factors costs a sign. In characteristic three the model can be taken anti-central on `Λ^odd`.

*Precision repairs from w3-vf-linear (Section 28 of its verification artifact).*
- `B_g^A` now requires `a` to preserve `LC(A^c)` as well as `LC(A)`. Block upper triangular level
  elements broke `Λ(a) = ρ(a) ⊗ Λ(a|)`.
- Even letters are stated explicitly for odd characteristic.
- The verdict is PASS after these repairs.

**Consequence.** A proof that every rank model of `G_3` or `R^x` is trivial cannot rest on (R1)–(R4).
It must use at least one of:
- a multiplication-table relation among letters with overlapping supports;
- a partial conjugation by a level element moving mass across the support;
- a Steinberg commutator of non-level root elements such as `[x_12(t0), x_23(s0)] = x_13(1)`.
