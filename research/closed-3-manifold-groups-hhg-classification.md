title: A closed oriented 3-manifold group is hierarchically hyperbolic iff no prime factor is Nil, Sol or non-octahedral flat
---

Let `M` be a closed oriented 3-manifold. Then `π_1 M` is a hierarchically hyperbolic group
if and only if `M` has no Nil, Sol or non-octahedral flat manifold in its prime
decomposition.

A flat manifold is octahedral when its crystallographic point group is conjugate into
`O_3(Z)`, the signed permutation matrices. By Hagen and Petyt–Spriano, as reported in the
source, this is equivalent to cocompact cubulation and to being a hierarchically
hyperbolic group. The `(3,3,3)` triangle group is an example of a crystallographic group
that is not a hierarchically hyperbolic group.
