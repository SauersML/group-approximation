Let `K` be a commutative field, `G` a group, and `K*G` a crossed product with any action `σ` and any 2-cocycle `α`.

**ESTABLISHED 2026-09-17** by [[virtually-biorderable-crossed-products-rank-condition-proof]].

**(A) Finite-index lift.** Let `N ≤ H` have finite index `n`, and write `K*N ⊆ K*H` for the sub-crossed product.
- Then `K*H` embeds as a unital subring of `M_n(K*N)`.
- So if `K*N` has the rank condition, so does `K*H`.

**(B) Virtually bi-orderable groups.** Suppose every finitely generated subgroup of `G` has a finite-index subgroup
with a bi-invariant order. Then `K*G` has the rank condition.

**Examples covered.**
- Virtually free groups, such as `PSL_2(Z)` and `SL_2(Z)`.
- Braid groups (the pure braid group is bi-orderable, Kim–Rolfsen).
- Virtually special groups, which embed virtually in right-angled Artin groups, and RAAGs are bi-orderable. These
  include cubulated hyperbolic groups and closed hyperbolic 3-manifold groups (Agol, Haglund–Wise).
- Virtually torsion-free nilpotent groups.

**Consequences for `some-field-crossed-product-fails-the-rank-condition`.**
- A witness needs a finitely generated subgroup `H`, generated by the supports of `A` and `B`, with no bi-orderable
  subgroup of finite index. Together with the earlier results, `H` must also be:
  - nonamenable, or the action and cocycle are not inflated from an amenable quotient;
  - and, when `α = 1`, carrying coefficients whose orbit field is not finitely generated over `K^H`.
- **Survivors.** The groups that remain are those like lattices in `SL_n(R)` with `n >= 3`. Finite-index subgroups of
  `SL_3(Z)` are not left-orderable (Witte 1994). Groups with unbounded finite subgroups also survive, such as the unit
  group of a Leavitt algebra, which contains `GL_(2^k)(F_3)` for all `k` and so is not virtually torsion-free.
- The ternary Leavitt route stays alive for the second reason.
- **Rokhlin route.** By `twisted-linear-profile-deficits-are-rank-condition-failures`, twisted linear profiles give no
  Rokhlin deficit at any virtually special group. A small-`Phi` twisted linear profile must live over a group with no
  virtually bi-orderable finitely generated subgroup carrying the data, for instance a higher-rank lattice.

**Calibration.** The Steinberg algebra `LC(X,k) ⋊ F_2` of a paradoxical action fails the rank condition over the
bi-orderable group `F_2`. The argument uses that the coefficients form a field, which is exactly where the
Malcev–Neumann division ring exists, and it does not apply to `LC(X,k)`.
