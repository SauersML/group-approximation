Direct proof, contained in the claim node.  The only inputs are the
product rule along a word, the identity `L U_s R = lambda_r I` at an exact
model (which turns each first-order term into a conjugate of `H_s` times
the scalar `lambda_r`), the invariance of the trace under conjugation, and
`conj(lambda_r) lambda_r = 1`.  Inverse letters carry the opposite sign,
which is why the exponent sum `n_s(r)` appears.  No property of the group,
the class, or the dimension is used.
