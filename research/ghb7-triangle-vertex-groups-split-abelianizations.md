**ESTABLISHED (exact computation).** Let `X_0 = ⟨a,b⟩`, `X_1 = ⟨c,b⟩`, `X_2 = ⟨c,a⟩` be the vertex
groups of `G = G_{HB_2}(7)`, with edge groups `⟨a⟩, ⟨b⟩, ⟨c⟩` of order 7
(`cckw-ghb2-kms-group-structure`).

| vertex | order | `[X,X]` | `X^ab` | edge generators a basis of `X^ab` | classes | `|C_X(x^j)|`, `j=1..6` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `X_0 = ⟨a,b⟩` | 343 | 7 | 49 | yes | 55 | `a`: 49, `b`: 49 |
| `X_1 = ⟨c,b⟩` | 2401 | 49 | 49 | yes | 97 | `c`: 343, `b`: 49 |
| `X_2 = ⟨c,a⟩` | 2401 | 49 | 49 | yes | 97 | `c`: 343, `a`: 49 |

**Consequences.**
1. **(SPLIT)** of `triangle-edge-matching-reduces-to-one-edge-holonomy` holds for `G`: each vertex
   abelianization is `(Z/7)^2` with its two edge generators as a basis.
2. **Irreducible degrees.** Each vertex group has exactly 49 linear characters. The remaining
   irreducibles all have degree 7: 6 of them for `X_0` (`49 + 6·49 = 343`, `55` classes), 48 for
   `X_1` and `X_2` (`49 + 48·49 = 2401`, `97` classes).
3. **Edge restrictions of nonlinear irreducibles.** Column orthogonality gives
   `Σ_χ |χ(g)|^2 = |C_X(g)|`, and the 49 linear characters contribute 49.
   - Where `|C_X(x^j)| = 49` (`a`, `b` in `X_0`; `b` in `X_1`; `a` in `X_2`), every nonlinear
     irreducible character vanishes at `x^j`, `j ≠ 0`, so it restricts to a multiple of the
     regular representation of the edge group.
   - At `c` in `X_1` and `X_2`, `Σ_(χ nonlinear) |χ(c^j)|^2 = 294` for every `j ≠ 0`. So some
     nonlinear irreducibles restrict non-uniformly to `⟨c⟩`, and eigenvalue counts on the edge `c`
     are not determined by the linear-character part.

**Method.** The quotient `ψ : G → SL_4(F_7)`, `a ↦ I + E_03 + E_12`, `b ↦ I + E_10 − E_23`,
`c ↦ I + E_31`, is injective on the three vertex groups (`cckw-ghb2-kms-group-structure`, item 5).
So the matrix groups generated by the images are the vertex groups. The script enumerates them by
closure, computes the derived subgroup as the normal closure of the commutators of the generators,
checks that `x^i y^j` (`0 ≤ i,j < 7`) meet 49 distinct cosets of it, counts conjugacy classes, and
counts centralizers by brute force.
