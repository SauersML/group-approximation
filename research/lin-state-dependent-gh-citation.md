Established by Junqiao Lin, *Tracially embeddable strategies: Lifting MIP*
tricks to MIPco*, arXiv:2304.01940v3, Theorem 5.1.  The theorem states both
the `rho`-seminorm approximation and the intertwining identity for every
element of `A'`; the latter is the feature not present in Cairn's existing
normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt finite-matrix import.
