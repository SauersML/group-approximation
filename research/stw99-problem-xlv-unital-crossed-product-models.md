**Problem XLV of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**
When is a unital classifiable C\*-algebra of the form `C(X) ⋊ G`, `G`
amenable acting on a compact Hausdorff space?

## Attempts

* Unlike XLIV, genuine obstructions exist and the answer is a
  characterisation: for `G = Z`, Pimsner--Voiculescu forces
  `[1_A] ∈ K_0` to lie in the image of `1 − α_*`-coinvariant classes
  from `C(X)`, and minimal `Z`-actions put `[1]` into the positive
  part of a dimension-group quotient — e.g. unital algebras whose
  `K_0` positive cone is too rigid (finite-rank torsion-free with
  `[1]` extremal-ish) cannot be minimal `Z`-crossed products.  The
  problem is to make the known necessary conditions (existence of a
  `G`-shaped exhaustion of `(K_0, [1], T, ρ)`) sufficient; every
  sufficiency technique on record is the XLIII/XLIV range machinery,
  so this node rises and falls with those.
