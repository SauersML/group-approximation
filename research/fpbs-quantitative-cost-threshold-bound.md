title: A finite-cluster-corrected bound relates maximal action cost to the uniqueness window
---

For every infinite finitely generated Gamma and its simple undirected degree-d Cayley graph G, C*(Gamma)-1 <= (d/2)(p_u-p_c) - kappa(p_c)+kappa(p_u). Here C* is the maximal free-action cost and kappa is reciprocal finite-cluster density.
