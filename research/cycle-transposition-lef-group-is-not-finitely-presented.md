**ESTABLISHED** by `cycle-transposition-not-fp-via-houghton-fp2` (not
independently reviewed).

Let `Gamma = <sigma, tau> <= prod_(n>=3) Sym(Z/n)` be the group of
`cycle-transposition-lef-group-has-no-regular-tree-action`, with
`sigma_n = (x -> x+1)` and `tau_n = (0 1)`. Then `Gamma` is **not finitely
presented**. In fact it is not of type `FP_2` over `Q`.

**Mechanism.**
- The restricted part `L = Gamma ∩ (+)_n Sym(Z/n)` is a locally finite normal
  subgroup.
- `Gamma/L` is the permutation group of `Z` generated by the shift and `(0 1)`,
  i.e. `FSym(Z) x| Z`.
- Finite presentation would descend over `Q` along `L`
  (`fp2-over-q-descends-along-locally-finite-kernels`), and `FSym(Z) x| Z` is
  not of type `FP_2` over `Q` (`houghton-h2-is-not-fp2-over-q`).

**Consequence for the self-similar route.** The open
`fp-rf-group-with-unbounded-prime-torsion`, and so
`some-fp-rf-group-acts-on-no-regular-rooted-tree`, needs a genuinely different
finitely presented residually finite group. It could be an overgroup of
`Gamma`, but not `Gamma` itself.

The same argument kills every finitely generated block-diagonal group whose
asymptotic quotient `G/(G ∩ (+) F_m)` is not of type `FP_2` over `Q`.
