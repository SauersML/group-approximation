If STW Problem I has a negative answer, then there is a separable, unital,
simple, stably finite, non-elementary C*-algebra `B` such that

```text
T(B)=empty
```

but `B` carries a faithful normalized nonadditive `2`-quasitrace.

Consequently Kaplansky's problem is equivalent to its restriction to this
traceless separable simple stably finite class.  Every such witness is
necessarily nonexact.
