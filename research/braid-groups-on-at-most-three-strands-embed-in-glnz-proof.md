`B_1` and `B_2` are immediate.

For `n = 3`, `braid-group-z-linear-iff-pure-braid-mod-center-z-linear` gives
`P_3 = U_3 × <Delta^2>`. Here `U_3` is the kernel of forgetting the third strand
`P_3 -> P_2`. That kernel is the fundamental group of the fiber of the
Fadell–Neuwirth fibration, the plane minus two points, so `U_3 ≅ F_2`. It is
generated by `A_13 = sigma_2 sigma_1^2 sigma_2^(-1)` and `A_23 = sigma_2^2`.

- `F_2` embeds in `SL_2(Z)`: the matrices `[[1, 2], [0, 1]]` and
  `[[1, 0], [2, 1]]` generate a free group of rank 2 (Sanov, 1947; ping-pong on
  the regions `|x| > |y|` and `|x| < |y|` of `Z^2`).
- So `U_3` embeds in `GL_2(Z)`, and the step (3) ⇒ (1) of that equivalence
  gives `B_3 -> GL_((2+2) · 3!)(Z) = GL_24(Z)` injective.

Check of the full twist: with `A_12 = sigma_1^2`,
`A_12 A_13 A_23 = sigma_1^2 sigma_2 sigma_1^2 sigma_2`, and the braid relation
gives `(sigma_1 sigma_2)^3 = sigma_1 (sigma_2 sigma_1 sigma_2) sigma_1 sigma_2
= sigma_1^2 sigma_2 sigma_1^2 sigma_2`. So `Delta^2 = A_12 A_13 A_23`, and
forgetting strand 3 sends it to `A_12`, as the splitting requires. ∎
