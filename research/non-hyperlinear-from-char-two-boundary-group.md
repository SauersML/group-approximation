Take k=F_2, d=2, N=3 in the displayed boundary group family. The premise
says this explicit group is nonhyperlinear, proving the root. It does not
assert finite presentation of this boundary group.

**Attribution.** The analytic input is Jihao Liu,
[*Nonhyperlinear groups exist*](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf),
Theorem 1.2/6.7. The Leavitt and boundary steps are the separately recorded
Cairn conditional applications, not theorems attributed to Liu's paper.
