**ESTABLISHED (citation).** Let `k` be a division ring of any characteristic,
and let `G` be a torsion-free group.
1. If `G` is virtually the fundamental group of a compact special cube
   complex, then `k[G]` embeds in a division ring (a Linnell division ring).
2. If `G` is the fundamental group of a compact 3-manifold, then `k[G]`
   embeds in a division ring. The embedding can be taken Hughes-free when `G`
   is locally indicable.

In particular `k[G]` is a domain in both cases, so Kaplansky's zero-divisor
conjecture holds for these groups over every field, including positive
characteristic. Subrings of division rings have no zero divisors, so the same
holds for every subgroup of such a `G`.
