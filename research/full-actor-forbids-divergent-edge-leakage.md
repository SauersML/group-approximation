**REFUTED.**  The attempted assertion was that canonical approximate tables
of the full residually finite Kazhdan actor, with an actual strict compressor
letter, must have bounded aggregate edge leakage

```text
||(1-E_(C*(phi_n(Gamma))))Ad(phi_n(t))
    E_(C*(phi_n(Gamma)))||_HS^2.
```

`vanishing-weight-block-carries-full-actor-edge-leakage` disproves this by
putting arbitrarily large unnormalized leakage on a physical block of
vanishing normalized trace.  The assertion can only be repaired by adding a
co-location hypothesis tying the leakage to the positive-trace sector where
the reduced exterior-return expectations vanish, or by normalizing the
leakage in a padding-stable way.
