Let `pi:C->U(H)` and `H_0,H_1` be a finite-dimensional joint packet in
which both `H_j` implement the same partial conjugacy between

```text
D=C intersect hCh^(-1)       and       h^(-1)Dh.
```

For every `m>=2` and `V in U(m)`, the amplified packet

```text
pi'=pi tensor I_m,
H'_0=H_0 tensor I_m,
H'_1=H_1 tensor V                                      (PMG6)
```

satisfies exactly the same one-parahoric transport equations and has the
same normalized source Hecke multiplicity flow.  Its fold coefficient is

```text
tr(H'_0(H'_1)^*)=tr(H_0H_1^*) tr_m(V^*).               (PMG7)
```

Choosing `V` to be a cyclic shift makes `(PMG7)` zero.  Moreover every
source/coset conjugation Dirichlet form vanishes on `I tensor V`, so no
spectral gap of the index-forty-two coset packet sees this escape.

Therefore a one-edge multiplicity-flow or expander inequality cannot prove
the majority-phase terminal.  A successful argument must couple the
multiplicity factor through a global loop relation on a common carrier.
