Restriction of `lambda_G` to `Lambda` is an orthogonal direct sum of copies
of `lambda_Lambda`, indexed by right cosets of `Lambda` in `G`.  Equation
`(KCS3)` therefore gives `lambda_G(p_<=K)=0`.

Restriction to the finite subgroup `B` is a direct sum of regular
representations of `B`.  A nonzero projection in `C[B]` acts nontrivially in
the faithful regular representation, so `lambda_G(z)!=0`; moreover

```text
tau_G(z)=tau_B(z)=rank(lambda_B(z))/|B|>0.
```

This proves `(RUP1)--(RUP2)`.  Each equality, operator domination, or
universal trace inequality in `(RUP3)` would survive under `lambda_G`, where
its right-hand bounded-type projection is zero and its left-hand packet
projection is nonzero.  Hence all are impossible.  The marked version is
the same calculation inside the finite subgroup generated by the packet and
its central sign.
