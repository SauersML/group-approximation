Let \((B,\tau)\) be a tracial selfless C*-probability space:
\(B\ne\mathbb C\), the trace induces a faithful GNS representation,
and the first-factor inclusion into the reduced free product with
itself is existential. Then for every \(m\ge1\) and \(k\ge0\),
canonical stabilization gives an isomorphism
\[
 \pi_k(U(M_m(B)),1)\ \cong\ K_{k+1}(B).
\]
For \(k=0\), the source denotes path components and the target is
\(K_1(B)\). Thus all consecutive matrix inclusions induce
isomorphisms on every unitary homotopy group.

No nuclearity, exactness, real rank zero, or separability is assumed.
The proof treats the tracial selfless subclass; it does not derive
selflessness from pureness or solve the full LVIII question.
