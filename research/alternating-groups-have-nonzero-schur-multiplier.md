title: The alternating groups A_n with n at least 4 have nonzero Schur multiplier
---

**ESTABLISHED** by literature import `alternating-schur-multiplier-citation`.

For every `n >= 4`, `H_2(A_n; Z)` is cyclic of order `2`, except for `n = 6`
and `n = 7`, where it is cyclic of order `6`. In particular it is nonzero for
every `n >= 4`.
