Part (2) of STW Problem XXXII holds for groups with paradoxical
towers and their products with arbitrary groups: minimal amenable
topologically free actions have dynamical comparison, hence (no
invariant measures for nonamenable acting groups) purely infinite
simple nuclear crossed products — Kirchberg algebras, in particular
`Z`-stable.  Covers all nonamenable hyperbolic groups and
Baumslag--Solitar groups.
