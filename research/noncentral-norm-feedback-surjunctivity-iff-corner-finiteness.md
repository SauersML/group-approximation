Let G be any group, let t have order three, and put e=1+t+t^2
in R=F_2[G]. Surjunctivity of every norm-feedback rule

    F_a(x)=x+a E(xR_t x),       a in R,

is equivalent to direct finiteness of eRe. Neither normality of
<t> nor preservation of its invariant subspace by a is assumed.

More precisely, injectivity of F_a forces injectivity of the
corner-linear map B=e+eae. If B is bijective, it also forces
(1-e)ae=0, and F_a is explicitly conjugate to B plus unchanged
even-parity coordinates. Thus any injective nonsurjective rule
in this norm family would itself yield an injective nonsurjective
corner-linear map.

For finite G, F_a is bijective exactly when (1-e)ae=0 and e+eae
is a unit of eRe. The unrestricted family x+A(xR_t x), with its
additional feedback from the even-parity subspace, is not decided.
No counterexample or universal direct-finiteness proof is supplied.
