In the coset Bernoulli crossed product, every infinite-order actor word has
Haar spectral measure because all its nonzero moments vanish.  Commuting
independent root words similarly have joint Haar measure, and actor
conjugation transports their spectral projections exactly.  Thus every
finite moving-root PVM window has the claimed equal ranks and covariance,
while all literal Steinberg and Coxeter words have zero defect.

The base coordinate and its `h`-translate are nevertheless independent
half projections of overlap `1/4`.  Therefore these tracial PVM data cannot
close the multiplicity holonomy.  If one refuses to move the spectral
coordinate, the preimage of one equal arc under doubling is two separated
half-arcs; summing symmetric-difference energy against any permutation of
the equal bins gives at least one.  If one moves the coordinate, covariance
is exact but adjacent chambers retain an unconstrained off-wall
multiplicity gauge.  This proves the stated dichotomy and the need for a
matrix-only same-reservoir invariant.
