Every operation listed in the claim is standard inside a finite tracial von
Neumann algebra.  Finite-group averaging is a finite sum.  Spectral and
support projections are Borel functional calculus.  Polar decomposition is
internal to the algebra.  The trace-preserving conditional expectation onto
a von Neumann subalgebra exists, and fixed matrix amplification remains
finite.

Therefore a natural fixed decoder that works in all exact tracial
representations also works for the faithful left regular representation.
The two displayed traces in `(FFC2)` are equal by `tr(AB)=tr(BA)`, while
faithfulness gives positive trace to the nonzero positive projection `H`.
This is the required contradiction.

The conclusion is deliberately limited to tracially functorial decoders.
It does not rule out a finite-dimensional multiplicity selection which has
no canonical counterpart in a diffuse finite von Neumann algebra.  That
nonfunctorial selection is exactly the remaining interface.
