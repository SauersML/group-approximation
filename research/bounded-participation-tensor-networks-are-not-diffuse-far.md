Let `H` be a parity presentation satisfying `(SUN2)` with small-set constant
`alpha>0`.  For every `f in F`, let

```text
(Q_1^(f),...,Q_L^(f))
```

be an exact reflection representation of every local commuting parity face.
Form a factorwise-exact tensor-network tuple

```text
Q_i = tensor_(f in F) Q_i^(f).                            (BTN1)
```

For a factor define its nonabelian coordinate support

```text
A_f={i: [Q_i^(f),Q_j^(f)] !=0 for some j}.                (BTN2)
```

Assume bounded participation:

```text
# {f:i in A_f} <= B for every coordinate i.               (BTN3)
```

Then at most `B/alpha` factors are nonabelian.  Indeed, in an exact parity
representation the support of every nonzero commutator row is a Tanner
stopping set.  Hence `A_f` is either empty or has size at least `alpha L`,
whereas double counting `(BTN3)` gives

```text
sum_f |A_f| <= B L.                                       (BTN4)
```

Suppose moreover that the algebra generated by each factor tuple has
irreducible type at most `D_0`, uniformly in `L` and `f`.  Abelian factors
do not increase irreducible type, while the tensor product of the at most
`B/alpha` nonabelian factors has type at most

```text
D = D_0^(ceil(B/alpha)).                                  (BTN5)
```

Any direct sum of networks obeying the same constants still has type at
most `D`.  Therefore
`bounded-type-noncentral-code-sectors-round-uniformly` rounds every such
tuple from vanishing balanced parity and complete-pair energy to a commuting
code PVM.  It cannot be a diffuse far countersequence.

Consequently moving finite-dimensional corners, bounded-bond tensor
networks, and their arbitrary central direct sums do not realize the
remaining obstruction when each factor separately respects the local
faces.  A genuine countersequence must violate at least one of the following:

1. factorwise exactness, through cancellations between tensor factors;
2. bounded participation, so a typical coordinate belongs to unboundedly
   many nonabelian factors; or
3. bounded bond type, by placing nonvanishing trace on an intrinsically
   unbounded-type factor.

The theorem does not exclude those three high-complexity regimes.
