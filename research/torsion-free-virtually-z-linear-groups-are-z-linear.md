title: A torsion-free group with a finite-index subgroup in GL_m(Z) embeds in GL_(m[G:H])(Z)
---

Let `G` be a torsion-free group and `H <= G` a subgroup of finite index `d`.
If `H` has an injective homomorphism `rho: H -> GL_m(Z)`, then `G` has an
injective homomorphism into `GL_(md)(Z)`, namely the induced representation.

Without torsion-freeness the conclusion can fail only through a finite normal
subgroup of `G` meeting `H` trivially; see the proof.
