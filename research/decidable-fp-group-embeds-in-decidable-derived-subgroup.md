Let `H` be a finitely presented group with solvable word problem. Then
there is a finitely presented group `T` with solvable word problem and an
embedding `H <= [T,T]`.

The construction is explicit. Let `H' = H x <t>` with `t` of infinite order.
Given generators `x_1, ..., x_n` of `H`, use the generators
`y_0 = t`, `y_i = x_i t` of `H'`. Amalgamate each `<y_i>` with the central
commutator `z_i = [a_i,b_i]` of its own copy `B_i` of the integral Heisenberg
group. `T` is the resulting star of groups.

## Why the extra factor

The Chatterji--Kassabov amalgam over `<x_i>` is fine for finite
presentation. To decide its word problem, however, one must decide whether
a syllable lies in `<x_i>`, and that is the power problem for `x_i` in `H`.
Groups with solvable word problem and unsolvable power problem exist, so a
decidable `H` does not by itself give a decidable `T`. In `H'` the projection
`pi : H' -> Z` has `pi(y_i) = 1` for every `i`. So `w` lies in `<y_i>` exactly
when `w = y_i^{pi(w)}`, which is one instance of the word problem.
