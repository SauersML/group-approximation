Finite direct products of countable discrete C-star-simple groups are
C-star-simple.

More generally, let G be the union of an upward directed family of
C-star-simple subgroups. Then G is C-star-simple. In particular, the
restricted direct sum of countably many C-star-simple groups is
C-star-simple.

**Established (literature).** This is Pierre de la Harpe, *On simplicity of
reduced C-star-algebras of groups*, arXiv:math/0509450, Proposition 19(i),(ii).
