Complete direct proof in the target node: the i.i.d. measures `nu_q` on
the dual Cantor group are `Gamma`-invariant, atomless, and converge to
the Dirac mass at the trivial character; their Koopman-GNS
representations of the wreath carry almost-invariant vectors without
lamp-invariant vectors.  Popa's correspondence between relative
property (T) of `(A rtimes Gamma, A)` and rigidity of
`L(A) subset L(A rtimes Gamma)` is used at statement level.
