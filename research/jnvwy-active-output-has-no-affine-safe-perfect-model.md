**ESTABLISHED FIREWALL.**  Consider a genuine recursive round of the
unchanged JNVWY compression verifier in which `ComputeSampler(lambda)` is
used (equivalently, the nontrivial branch isolated in
`jnvwy-introspection-has-no-affine-safe-perfect-model`).  After the typed
oracularization and the literal low-individual-degree answer reduction, the
output verifier has no perfect tensor-product strategy, perfect
commuting-operator strategy, or perfect faithful-tracial model whose positive
answer support is affine-safe on every sampled edge.

Indeed, a hypothetical affine-safe perfect output model first coarse-grains,
by `jnvwy-perfect-answer-reduction-reflects-affine-safety`, to an affine-safe
perfect model of the typed oracularized verifier.  Then
`jnvwy-perfect-oracularization-reflects-affine-safety` reconstructs an
affine-safe perfect model of the introspection verifier.  This contradicts
`jnvwy-introspection-has-no-affine-safe-perfect-model`.

Thus noncanonical low-degree extensions, auxiliary PCP syzygies, and exotic
perfect strategies cannot repair the odd-xor obstruction.  The unchanged
MIP*=RE-to-LCS program cannot enter the affine-profile compiler at this
stage; a successful non-hyperlinear compiler must alter the recursive
sampler/proof interface or bypass affine support altogether.
