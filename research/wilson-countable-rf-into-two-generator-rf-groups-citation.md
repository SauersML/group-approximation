J. S. Wilson, *Embedding theorems for residually finite groups*, Math. Z. **174** (1980), 149–157,
doi:10.1007/BF01293535. Quoted verbatim from p. 149 of the Springer PDF (read by sk-strong-1 on 2026-09-13):

> **Theorem A.** Every countable residually finite group G can be embedded in a 2-generator residually finite group
> Ĝ. Further, Ĝ may be chosen so that, if G is soluble, residually soluble or residually nilpotent, then so is Ĝ.

p. 150: "The confirmation that all countable residually finite groups can be embedded in 2-generator residually finite
groups answers a question proposed by B. Hartley at the Symposium on Infinite Groups and Group Rings at the University
of Warwick in 1978".

Secondary confirmation: M. Valiunas, arXiv:2002.11683, Theorem 1.5, quotes the same statement "[Theorem A]".
