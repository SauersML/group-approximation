There is a property `(T)` ergodic countable probability-measure-preserving
Borel equivalence relation `R` such that its property `(T)` II1 factor
`L(R)` is not Connes embeddable.

Starting from an ergodic non-co-hyperlinear IRS `H` of a free group, the GNS
algebra `L(F/H)` embeds trace-preservingly into the von Neumann algebra of an
ergodic relation realizing `H` as its stabilizer distribution.  A
property-`(T)` relation-embedding theorem places that relation algebra inside a
property-`(T)` ergodic relation algebra.  Connes embeddability passes to von
Neumann subalgebras, so the envelope cannot be Connes embeddable.
