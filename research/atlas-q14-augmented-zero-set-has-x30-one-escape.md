Put

```text
Gamma_+=(A8*A8)/<<thirty packet words,q_19243,q_14>>. (QZE1)
```

There is a finite tracial factor `(M,tau)` and a common relative unitary `W`
with exact regular `A8` margins satisfying every relator in `(QZE1)` such
that

```text
x_30(W)^2=1.                                          (QZE2)
```

Consequently no trace-polynomial sum of squares, conditional-expectation
inequality, projection-Gram positivity argument, or representation-theoretic
inequality valid in all finite tracial von Neumann algebras can prove the
opposing strict ceiling `x_30^2<1/2` from the exact augmented relations.

The exact finite-dimensional fork is equally sharp.  The following are
equivalent:

1. `(QZE1)` has an exact finite-dimensional model whose two chart
   restrictions are faithful regular multiples of `A8`.
2. `Gamma_+` has a nontrivial finite quotient.

If either holds, there is such a matrix model with

```text
x_30(W)^2=1.                                          (QZE3)
```

Thus an exact matrix escape would not merely touch the q14 threshold
`1/2`; after passing to a regular finite quotient it violates every proposed
upper ceiling maximally.

It follows that `atlas-a4-t30-energy-ceiling-below-q14-wall` cannot be a
pointwise upper inequality on a nonempty exact zero set.  Its only possible
content is the vacuous alternative: a dimension-independent positive residual
gap excluding packet plus 19243 plus q14 matrix microstates altogether.  The
finite-factor point `(QZE2)` shows that this gap, if true, is exactly a
matrix/Connes-embedding-sensitive obstruction.
