Citation import, second hand for Gersten. J. A. Barmak and E. G. Minian, *A new
test for asphericity and diagrammatic reducibility of group presentations*,
arXiv:1601.00604 (`itest.tex`, md5 `8ba74874c596f760a5de2dda09f11675`), fetched on
MSI 2026-09-13.

- l.410--412, Proposition [Gersten] `adian`: an Adian presentation
  `<x_i | U_j = V_j>` with `len(U_j) = len(V_j)` is DR if either the left graph
  `L(P)` or the right graph `R(P)` has no cycles (Gersten, *Reducible diagrams and
  equations over groups*, Prop. 4.15, not read).
- l.414: "the presentation associated to a LOG `Γ` is Adian, and in this case
  `L(P) = T(Γ)` and `R(P) = I(Γ)`", with `I(Γ)`, `T(Γ)` defined at l.335.
- l.119: "Any diagrammatically reducible (DR) presentation is aspherical."

With `U_e = s λ` and `V_e = λ t`, the left graph joins the first letters `s` and
`λ`, the right graph the last letters `λ` and `t`, matching `T(Γ)` and `I(Γ)`.
