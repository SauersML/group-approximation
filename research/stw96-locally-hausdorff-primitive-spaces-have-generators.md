title: Locally Hausdorff real-rank-zero primitive spaces glue fibrewise Z-stability

Let `A` be a nonzero separable C*-algebra of real rank zero whose primitive
ideal space is locally Hausdorff.  If every nonzero simple ideal-quotient of
`A` is `Z`-stable, then `A` is `Z`-stable and has generator rank one.

More generally, the same conclusion holds if `A` has a continuous ideal
filtration indexed by an arbitrary ordinal and the primitive ideal space of
every successor quotient is locally Hausdorff.

Consequently, every nonzero separable, nowhere-scattered,
finite-nuclear-dimension C*-algebra of real rank zero with locally Hausdorff
primitive ideal space has generator rank one.  A generic element is a
generator.  The same is true under the arbitrarily filtered version of the
local-Hausdorff hypothesis.

No composition series is assumed in the first assertion.  In particular, the result
applies to zero-dimensional locally Hausdorff spectra with perfect or Cantor
pieces, and to non-Hausdorff spectra that admit Hausdorff neighborhoods at
every point.
