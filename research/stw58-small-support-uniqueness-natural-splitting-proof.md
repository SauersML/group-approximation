1. Fix N=k+2. In a nonzero order-zero endpoint of a unital
   Z_(L,L+1) map, the first N diagonal entries generate an actual
   full hereditary matrix algebra M_N(D), where D has stable rank
   one. Its fixed support has every normalized quasitrace dimension
   at most N/L. Taking L arbitrarily large makes this arbitrarily small.
2. The reviewed matrix stable range and full hereditary K-theory
   invariance show that this one support carries based representatives
   of every class in K_(k+1)(B).
3. If u and v have the same stable class and their support dimensions
   sum to less than 1/(k+1), uv* is stably null and lies in the
   hereditary algebra generated by |u-1|+|v-1|. The small-support
   theorem contracts it, proving based uniqueness.
4. Arbitrarily small representatives form an intrinsic subgroup.
   Uniqueness makes the small representatives of each stable class
   define one homotopy class. This proves that stabilization restricts
   to an isomorphism on that subgroup and defines the canonical section.
5. Pulling back a normalized quasitrace along a unital homomorphism
   preserves the size bound. For a nonunital homomorphism, normalize
   the restriction by its value on the image of the unit, a number
   in [0,1]. Naturality of stabilization and uniqueness give naturality
   of the section, including matrix corners.
6. Any representative below the threshold can be compared with a
   sufficiently small representative of its stable class. Hence it
   already belongs to the canonical stable summand, proving the
   support-size gap and the stated finite-product criterion.

The artifact contains the full argument and distinguishes group
sections from continuous sections of spaces. Global localization of
arbitrary sphere families remains unproved.
