In the ring and notation of `toeplitz-head-tail-unital-self-embedding`, put

```text
e_0=f,       e_1=xfy,       a=xf,       b=fy,
s=1+e_0+e_1+a+b.                                             (TSG1)
```

Then

```text
s^2=1,        s e_1 s=e_0,                                  (TSG2)
```

and the unital subring generated by `sigma(T)` and `s` is all of `T`:

```text
T=<sigma(T),s>.                                               (TSG3)
```

Thus the proper recursive tail has a single finite involutive head actuator.
At the elementary-group level, the tail elementary group together with a
fixed finite collection of root elements carrying coefficient `s` generates
the full elementary group. This is the algebraic finite-description input
needed by the Toeplitz survive-or-recurse attack; it is not yet its
normalized-HS conditional-expectation estimate.
