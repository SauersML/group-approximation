**Setting.** `T` is a triangle of groups with:
- finite vertex groups `X_0, X_1, X_2`;
- edge groups `A_0, A_1, A_2` and trivial face group;
- each `X_i` generated by the images of its two edge groups.

`Γ_i` is the coset graph of those two images in `X_i`, `r_i` is half its girth, and `G` is the
colimit.

**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** If `1/r_0 + 1/r_1 + 1/r_2 <= 1`:
- (i) `G` acts on a CAT(0) simplicial 2-complex `Y(T)`. A 2-simplex is a strict fundamental domain,
  isometric to a triangle with angles `π/r_i`. The link at `v_i` is `Γ_i`, and the stabilizer of `v_i`
  is `X_i`; in particular the vertex groups inject into `G`.
- (ii) Every finite subgroup of `G` is conjugate into some `X_i`.
- (iii) If no edge monomorphism is surjective, `G` is infinite.
- (iv) A homomorphism to a finite group that is injective on each `X_i` has torsion-free kernel.
- (v) If `1/r_0 + 1/r_1 + 1/r_2 < 1`, then `G` is non-elementary hyperbolic and `Y(T)` carries a
  `G`-invariant CAT(−1) metric.
