**OPEN.** Let `k = F_3`, `R = L_k(1,2)`, `PG = R^x/{±1}`, and let `K_4 <= PG` be generated by the
level-two diagonal sign units `sum_(|alpha|=2) eps_alpha S[alpha] T[alpha]` and the regular Klein group
`u_x = sum_(|alpha|=2) S[alpha xor x] T[alpha]`, so `|K_4| = 32`. Put `e_(K_4) = 2 sum_(k in K_4) [k]`.
Exhibit `b, c` in `k[PG]` with `c e_(K_4) b = 1`.

Since `K <= K_4`, this implies `projective-ternary-klein-idempotent-is-full`, and through it the swap
target and a Gottschalk counterexample on `PG`.

## Attempts

- *Natural splitting is balanced.* `rho(e_(K_4)) = delta^2 Psi^2`, and `delta^2`, `Psi^2` pass both
  multiplication invariants of `ternary-adjoint-image-is-balanced`. The level-one splitting of `e_K`
  does not (`klein-level-one-splitting-is-outside-adjoint-image`). It dies at membership: whether
  `delta^2`, `Psi^2`, or any splitting, lie in `rho(k[PG])` is open. The image contains
  `n (x) n^op` and `n (x) 1 - 1 (x) n^op` for every square-zero `n`, and all their products. No
  further linear invariant is known.
- *Membership would not suffice.* `rho` is not injective on `k[PG]`: the level-one `GL_2(k)`
  contributes `24` classes whose images lie in a `16`-dimensional span. Equality in `k[PG]` must be
  arranged modulo `ker rho`, and direct finiteness does not pass back from quotients. Section 6 of
  the artifact.
- *Screens that bind.* A witness needs:
  - support generating a nonamenable, non-locally-finite subgroup;
  - a forward table with a nonsofic two-core;
  - `PG` not `F_3`-linear sofic.
  `F_3`-linear soficity of `PG` refutes this claim.
