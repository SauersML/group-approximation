Published results:
- P. Jolissaint, *Rapidly decreasing functions in reduced C*-algebras of groups*, Trans.
  Amer. Math. Soc. 317 (1990) 167–196;
- V. Lafforgue, *Propriété (T) renforcée et conjecture de Baum–Connes*, Quanta of Maths,
  Clay Math. Proc. 11 (2010) 323–345.

Read from Gomez Aparicio–Julg–Valette, *The Baum–Connes conjecture: an extended survey*
(arXiv:1905.10081), Remark 6.18, extracted on MSI:
- "The group Γ = SL3 (Z) does not have property (RD) (see [Jol90]). Moreover, there is no
  unconditional completion B(Γ) that is a dense subalgebra of C*_r(Γ) stable under
  holomorphic calculus. The following argument is due to Lafforgue (see [Laf10])."
- The survey then embeds the amenable group `Z^2 ⋊ Z` and cites Jenkins's non-symmetric
  `ℓ¹`. Line references are in `research/artifacts/kk-sl3z-congruence-2026-09-12.md`.
