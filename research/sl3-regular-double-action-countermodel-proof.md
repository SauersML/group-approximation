The presentation `t_x=a t a^(-1)` for `x=aC` identifies the centralizer HNN
with the free generalized wreath product over `A/C`.  Soficity of the action
therefore makes the HNN sofic by the generalized-wreath theorem.  Bass--Serre
normal form embeds the adjacent vertex double `A*_C A`; subgroups of sofic
groups are sofic.  In canonical sofic microstates every nonidentity word has
trace tending to zero, so the nontrivial fold word has squared normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt distance tending to two from the identity.
