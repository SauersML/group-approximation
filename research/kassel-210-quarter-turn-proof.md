Relations (2.4)--(2.5) give triangular conjugation by `a` and `b`, while
(2.10) gives `[c,B]=ad^(-1)`. Invert the latter triangular automorphism to
obtain `B^(-1)c^(-1)B=c^(-1)a^(-1)d`, substitute into conjugation by
`w=bB^(-1)b`, and collect commuting root factors. This gives the three
quarter-turn identities; their fourth powers centralize the roots.
