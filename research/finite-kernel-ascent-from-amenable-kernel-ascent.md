A finite group is amenable. Applying the amenable-kernel ascent at every `q` gives the finite-kernel ascent at every
`q`.

**Only the split, locally finite shape is needed** (artifact Section 4.2).
- By the Kaloujnine–Krasner embedding, `W` embeds in `C ⋊ Q`, where `C` is the countable `Q`-invariant subgroup of
  `N^Q` generated by the base components of `W`.
- `C` lies in the locally finite variety generated by `N`.
- Maximality of `C ⋊ Q` passes to `W` (`bernoulli-rokhlin-maximality-passes-to-subgroups`).

The Kun–Thom wreath has exactly this shape.
