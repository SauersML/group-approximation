Linear independence of the group basis gives `gS=S` from `gq=q`.  A
permutation of the nonempty finite set `S` has finite order, so some `n>0`
fixes every support point.  For `h in S`, `g^n h=h` implies `g^n=1`.
Equality of coefficients along each permutation cycle follows from the same
basis comparison.  Apply this to `b^(-1)a` for `(FSS2)`.
