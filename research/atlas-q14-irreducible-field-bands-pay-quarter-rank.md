ESTABLISHED.  Put the marked natural transvection in the form

```text
1+N,       N=E12 tensor I_m                            (IFB1)
```

on `F2^4 tensor F2^m`.  For `C in GL_m(F2)`, define the full second-chart
conjugator

```text
T(C)=diag(C,I_m,I_m,I_m).                             (IFB2)
```

Then

```text
T(C) N T(C)^(-1)=E12 tensor C.                        (IFB3)
```

Thus q14 is exact, and if `I+C` is invertible, the three nontrivial V4
nilpotents `N`, `TNT^(-1)`, and their sum all have rank `m`.

Let `f_m in F2[t]` be irreducible of degree `m>=2`, and take `C_m` to be its
companion matrix.  There is a constant `D`, depending only on the fixed
twelve packet words and collision, such that for every `m>D`, at least one
literal residual satisfies

```text
rank(w(T(C_m))-I) >= m.                               (IFB4)
```

Consequently

```text
max_w rank(w(T(C_m))-I)/(4m) >= 1/4.                  (IFB5)
```

In particular the growing field bands from
`atlas-q14-independent-stratum-has-unbounded-band-width` cannot furnish an
augmented rank countermodel.  Any surviving independent-stratum enemy must
use genuinely noncommutative or non-primary coefficient data rather than one
irreducible field parameter.

### Proof

Every first-chart matrix has scalar `F2` blocks.  From `(IFB2)`, every block
of its second-chart conjugate belongs to

```text
F2[C,C^(-1)] <= M_m(F2).                              (IFB6)
```

Therefore every scalar block entry of every fixed packet or collision
residual is the evaluation at `C` of one fixed Laurent polynomial

```text
p(t) in F2[t,t^(-1)].                                 (IFB7)
```

There are finitely many such polynomials.  They are not all zero.  If they
were, specialization at `t=1` would make the identity relative alignment an
exact packet-and-collision model, contradicting
`atlas-a4-19243-classical-collapse`.

Choose one nonzero `p`, multiply by a power of `t` to obtain a nonzero
ordinary polynomial `q(t)`, and let `D>=deg q` also dominate the corresponding
degrees for the finite residual list.  For `m>D`, irreducibility and
`deg f_m=m` imply

```text
q(C_m) !=0 in F2[C_m] ~= F_(2^m).                    (IFB8)
```

Since nonzero field multiplication is invertible on the `m`-dimensional
binary space, `p(C_m)` has rank `m`.  It is a block of one `4m by 4m`
residual, proving `(IFB4)` and `(IFB5)`.

Finally, an irreducible polynomial of degree at least two has neither zero
nor one as a root, so both `C_m` and `I+C_m` are invertible.  Hence this is
exactly the constant-rank independent q14 band, not a degenerate endpoint.
