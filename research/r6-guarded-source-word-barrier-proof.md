# Evaluate the guarded AND on the regular C2 times S4 character table

The guard formula `(RGS1)` rewrites R6 as `(RGS2)`.  A word in the abelian
group generated by `gamma,Z,Y` is one of its eight characters, while
`1-2GPS` has the one-negative-value truth table `(RGS6)`, proving the word
barrier.  In `C_2 times S_4`, the commuting subgroup generated by
`gamma,d,e` is elementary abelian of order eight and the regular
representation contains every character.  Expanding `GPS` leaves identity
coefficient `1/8`, which proves `(RGS8)` and the finite stationary escape.
