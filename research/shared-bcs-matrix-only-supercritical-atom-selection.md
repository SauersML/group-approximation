**OPEN FINAL SELECTION FACE.**  In finite-dimensional normalized-HS
microstates of the fixed shared non-CE BCS extension, construct at every
addressed recursive level a finite family of locally exactified central-sign
assignment atoms `A_(n,i)` and an active carrier of mass `a_n` such that

```text
sum_i tr(A_(n,i))>=a_n+v_n-Cp(n)sqrt(E),               (SAM1)
v_n>=beta a_n-Cp(n)sqrt(E),                            (SAM2)
```

where the same original BCS variable matrices occur in every context and
`beta>0` is dimension independent.  The selected family must come with a
finite ordinary-group authentication of its incidence with the next
recursive carrier, but it need not itself be one projection or one group
word.

The construction must be **matrix-only** in the following precise sense.
In the exact infinite perfect BCS completion, forbidden mass is zero and all
addressed auxiliary relations coexist while the mark survives.  In the
canonical regular representation, the positive Plancherel companion atoms
are allowed to avoid `(SAM1)`; no trace-functorial supercritical inequality
is asserted there.

Once `(SAM1)--(SAM2)` are available, no local projection technology remains:

- `central-sign-assignment-atom-is-index-two-reynolds-surplus` names every
  atom;
- `index-two-reynolds-atoms-have-orthogonal-hnn-transports` moves the
  selected atoms into pairwise orthogonal character destinations with fixed
  HS constants; and
- `partitioned-child-surplus-collision-supplies-contraction` converts their
  total mass into factor `(1+beta)^(-1)`.

## Mandatory fences

The selection cannot depend only on scalar forbidden mass.  Substituting a
classically unsatisfiable CSP would then activate the same finite graph of
finite groups, contradicting
`multiplicity-surplus-is-blind-to-the-non-ce-input`.  It must consume a
cross-context operator observable of the original shared tuple.

Nor may it consist solely of finite-subgroup amalgams and HNN covariance.
`canonical-types-extend-every-finite-bass-serre-actor` extends every canonical
matrix-ultraproduct type through all such actors.  At least one
payload-sensitive two-cell, proper-corner collision, or equivalent
nontransport relation is therefore essential.

Fixed determinant, index, parity, or projective-holonomy arithmetic is not
that two-cell.  `fixed-rank-index-authenticator-has-gap-or-functoriality`
shows that a fixed readout is either discontinuous in normalized HS norm or,
after a uniform spectral gap is supplied, continuous tracial functional
calculus.  `atlas-fixed-packet-arithmetic-has-no-hs-gap` gives the exact
amplification argument for every finite multiplicity congruence.  Hence a
successful selection must create an extensive payload-sensitive operator
collision; it cannot merely read a bounded residue of the selected type
vector.

This is the exact residue of `typewise-schur-tail-partition-compiler` after
removing aggregate reflection wordization, atom transport, target
orthogonality, and the contraction estimate.
