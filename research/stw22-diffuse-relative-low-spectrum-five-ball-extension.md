Let `(N,tau)` be a separable-predual `II_1` factor, `e in N` a projection,
and

```text
H:B^5->eN,       H(y)^*H(y)=p_h,
f(y)=e-H(y)H(y)^*.
```

Let `b` be a positive contraction with support `p_l` and assume

```text
tau(p_h)+tau(p_l)<tau(e).                             (TR5-1)
```

Every continuous `x:S^4->eN` satisfying `x^*x=b` and `fx=x` extends to
`X:B^5->eN` with the same equations and exact boundary.  If `b<=eta`, then

```text
||X(y)-x_0||_2<=2sqrt(tau(b))<=2sqrt(eta tau(p_l))    (TR5-2)
```

for every fixed `x_0^*x_0=b`.
