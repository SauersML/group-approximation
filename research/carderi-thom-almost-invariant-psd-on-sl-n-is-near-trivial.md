**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `q` be a prime power and `ψ: SL_n(F_q) → C` a positive definite function with `ψ(1) = 1`. Suppose some non-central `g ∈ SL_n(F_q)` and some `ε ∈ (0,1)` satisfy `|1 − ψ(x^{-1}gx)| < ε` for all `x`. Then `|1 − ψ(h)| < 9(2ε + 16/q)^{1/2}` for every `h ∈ SL_n(F_q)`.

The proof uses Gluck's estimate. For non-central `h` and every nontrivial normalized irreducible character `χ_π` of `SL_n(F_q)`, `|χ_π(h)| < 8/q`. Route: `carderi-thom-almost-invariant-psd-sl-n-citation`.
