title: A fixed finite trace window robustifies the Pauli quarter-carrier holonomy
---

For the two atlas quarter-carrier projections, fixed trace moments force the
reflection commutator to have squared normalized Hilbert--Schmidt distance
`1/2` from the identity.  The estimate is stable under a fixed packet defect
and a fixed finite canonical trace window, with constants independent of
matrix dimension.

This closes the analytic holonomy floor; groupifying the two derived
reflections on one authenticated carrier remains a separate compiler task.
