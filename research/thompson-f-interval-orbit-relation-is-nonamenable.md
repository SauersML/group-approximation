**REFUTED.** Statement: there is an `F`-quasi-invariant σ-finite Borel measure `μ` on `(0,1)`
such that the measured orbit equivalence relation of `F` is not amenable.

Why it was worth recording: this is how Monod proved that the groups `H(A)` of piecewise
projective homeomorphisms are non-amenable without free subgroups (arXiv:1209.5229, Theorem
1.1). A non-amenable measured orbit relation of any non-singular action forces the acting
group to be non-amenable (`thompson-f-not-amenable-from-orbit-relation`).

Refuted by `thompson-f-interval-orbit-relation-is-hyperfinite`: the orbit relation is the
dyadic affine relation, generated by a solvable group.
