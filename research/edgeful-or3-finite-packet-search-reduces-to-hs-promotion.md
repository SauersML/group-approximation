**ESTABLISHED.**  For the two explicit packet inputs currently listed in
`edgeful-or3-coefficient-link-compiler`, no remaining task is a bounded
enumeration of slot frames and packet-term bijections.

* The ten-coordinate branch cannot align its root skeleton uniformly:
  `ten-coordinate-or3-slot-frames-have-a-six-clique-obstruction` already
  fails on a satisfiable six-variable formula, before off-diagonal packet
  terms are considered.
* In the four-cap branch, a cap-independent coefficient phase forces the
  marked sign trivial, while central cap controls leave all equations
  sectorwise and supply no cross-cap holonomy.
* The smallest noncentral two-chart mixer destroys the packet retraction but
  still has a finite Clifford evaluator faithful on every cap atom.
* A Thompson-simple third actuator and the native odd `p`-Heisenberg graft
  do destroy all exact finite packet evaluations.  Their use in normalized
  HS microstates is conditional, respectively, on an unknown approximate
  collapse theorem or on the open affine-Leavitt HS coefficient decoder.
* The noncentral Fano Naimark carrier supplies a finite rectangular frame,
  but a representation-universal word lower bound would collapse the
  carrier already in its faithful regular trace.  Exact common-source
  covariance is likewise incompatible with the full-support factor model.

Therefore the surviving edgeful-OR3 route must add a genuinely
dimension-independent **canonical-matrix-only** estimate: an HS collapse of
an exact finite-invisibility actuator, a finite-matrix alignment/payment
inequality for the noncentral carrier, or an equivalent trace-promotion
theorem.  Finite enumeration may still discover the local packet used by
such a theorem, but finite slot incidence by itself can no longer establish
the coefficient-link compiler.

This is a prioritization theorem, not a refutation of the open compiler.  It
shows that its five-claim cascade should not be valued as a small finite CSP
after the accumulated firewalls; its remaining analytic content is of the
same type as the other canonical-HS promotion frontiers.
