Let `Q` be the group of `defect-saturation-full-mf-radical` and
`A=C*_r(Q)`.  Then `A` is separable, unital, generated by two unitaries,
simple, has a unique tracial state, has stable rank one, is stably finite,
and is not MF.

So a separable unital simple monotracial `C*`-algebra of stable rank one can
fail to be MF.

The maximal algebra is a separate matter: the proper isometry of the
manuscript needs a property-(T) subgroup strictly compressed by a
conjugation, and that configuration is not claimed for `Q`.
