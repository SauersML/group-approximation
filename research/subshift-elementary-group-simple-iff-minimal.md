**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed)** via `subshift-elementary-group-simple-iff-minimal-proof`.

Let X ⊆ A^Z be an infinite subshift and n ≥ 3. Then EL_n(LC(X,F_2) ⋊ Z) is a simple group if and only if X is minimal.

So minimality is the exact dynamical content of the simplicity in the Pestov 9.1 construction (`infinite-simple-kazhdan-hyperlinear-group`).
