**Presentation.** `A(Gamma_A) x| Z` is presented by generators `a_i`
(`i in Z`) and `b`, with relators `[a_i, a_j]` for `|i-j| in A` and
`b^-1 a_i b = a_(i+1)`. Tietze elimination of `a_i = b^-i a_0 b^i` turns the
relator `[a_i, a_(i+n)]` into the `b^i`-conjugate of `[a_0, b^-n a_0 b^n]`,
and the relator for `j-i = -n` into its inverse. So the normal closure is
generated by the words `[a, b^-n a b^n]`, `n in A`, which is (GS1).

**(1) Word problem.** Given `w`, compute the exponent sum `e` of `b`. If
`e != 0` the image of `w` in `Z` is nontrivial. If `e = 0`, tracking the
prefix `b`-heights rewrites `w` as a word in `a_i^(±1)` with `|i| <= |w|`,
i.e. an element of `A(Lambda)` for the finite induced subgraph `Lambda` on
`[-|w|, |w|]`. Its edges are computable because `A` is decidable. The map
`A(Lambda) -> A(Gamma_A)` is injective: killing the generators outside
`Lambda` is a retraction back, since an induced subgraph's relators are
exactly the relators among its generators. Right-angled Artin groups on
finite graphs have solvable word problem, so `w = 1` is decidable.

**(2) Commutation detects edges.** If `n in A`, `u_n` is a relator. If
`n notin A`, retract `A(Gamma_A)` onto `A({0,n})` by killing every other
vertex generator; the induced graph on `{0,n}` has no edge, so the retract is
free on `a_0, a_n`, where `[a_0, a_n] != 1`. Hence `u_n = [a_0, a_n] != 1`.

**(3) Diagonalization.** Replace `T` by the monotone recursive
`T+(m) = m + max_(k<=m) T(k)`; a bound for `T+` is a bound for `T`. List all
pairs `(M_j, C_j)` of deterministic machines and positive integers, and assign
input `n_j = j+1` to pair `j`. Decide `n_j in A` by running `M_j` on `u_(n_j)`
for `C_j*T+(C_j*l) + C_j*l + C_j` steps, with `l = 4n_j + 4`. If it halts with
answer "trivial" put `n_j notin A`; otherwise put `n_j in A`. Every clock is
computable, so `A` is decidable.

Suppose some algorithm decides the word problem of `G_A` within
`C*T+(C*l) + C*l + C`. It equals some `M_j` with `C_j = C`. On `u_(n_j)` it
halts within the clock. If it answers "trivial", then `n_j notin A` and
`u_(n_j) != 1` by (2); otherwise `n_j in A` and `u_(n_j) = 1`. Either way it
is wrong. `∎`
