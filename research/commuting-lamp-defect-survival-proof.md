## Direct proof

In the Clifford witness, `t c t^-1` is the lamp at `tau o`, while its
`v_1`-conjugate is the lamp at `j(v_1) tau o`.  The two sites are distinct by
the range computation in the linear witness.  The element `u` maps to the
product of those two lamp generators.  Modulo the central sign, the Clifford
lamp group becomes the elementary abelian `2`-group with the sites as a basis.
The product of two distinct basis elements is nontrivial.
