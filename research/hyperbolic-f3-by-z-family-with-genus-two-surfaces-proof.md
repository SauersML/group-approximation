Notation as in the claim. `phi_1 = (a -> abb, b -> ca, c -> babb)`. Upper case is inverse.

**Step 1: automorphism.** Let `tau_w = (a -> a . w(b, c), b -> b, c -> c)`. It is a Nielsen
automorphism with inverse `a -> a . w(b, c)^-1`. Then

```text
phi_1(tau_w(a)) = phi_1(a) . w(phi_1(b), phi_1(c)) = abb . w(ca, babb) = phi_w(a),
```

and `phi_1 o tau_w` agrees with `phi_w` on `b` and `c`. So `phi_w = phi_1 o tau_w` is an automorphism.

- `phi_1` is an automorphism, with inverse `b -> cA`, `a -> aaCaC`, `c -> bcAcAA` (Step 1 of
  `hyperbolic-f3-by-z-with-genus-two-surface-certificates-proof`).
- So `phi_w^-1 = tau_w^-1 o phi_1^-1`.
- `phi_w` is positive because `X` and `Y` are positive words.

**Step 2: matrix.** Order the rows and columns `a, b, c`. Column `x` counts the letters of
`phi_w(x)`:

```text
M_w = [[1+i+j, 1, 1], [2+3j, 0, 3], [i, 1, 0]]
```

- Expanding gives `chi_w(t) = t^3 - (i+j+1) t^2 - (i+3j+5) t + 1`, so `det M_w = -1`.
- Evaluating: `chi_w(1) = -2(i + 2j + 2)` and `chi_w(-1) = 2(j + 2)`. Both are nonzero for all
  `i, j >= 0`.
- A rational root of the monic integer cubic `chi_w` divides `1`, so there is none, and `chi_w` is
  irreducible over `Q`.
- `M_w` is primitive. Its graph has the loop `a -> a` (since `a` occurs in `phi_w(a)`) and the
  cycle `a -> b -> c -> a` (`b` occurs in `phi_w(a)`, `c` in `phi_w(b)`, `a` in `phi_w(c)`).
  So it is strongly connected and aperiodic, and its Perron--Frobenius root is `> 1`.

Step 2 of `hyperbolic-f3-by-z-with-genus-two-surface-certificates-proof` uses exactly these
properties: irreducible, no root `±1`, and a Perron--Frobenius root `> 1`. That argument gives:

- `phi_w` is fully irreducible (no power fixes a proper free factor);
- `phi_w` is atoroidal (a non-atoroidal fully irreducible automorphism of `F_3` comes from `N_{3,1}`
  and has eigenvalue `±1`);
- by Brinkmann, `G_w` is hyperbolic;
- as a free-by-cyclic group with `n >= 2`, it is one-ended.

**Step 3: the certificate is independent of `w`.** Let `f_w` be the rose map of `phi_w`, and
take the pairing `p` stored in `surface_phi1028.json`. The checker `verify_surface.py` rebuilds
the fatgraph from four inputs: `phi0`, `power = 1`, the `partial^-` words `bc, BC`, and `p`. Every
object it tests is a function of:

- (a) the boundary words `bc`, `f_w(bc)^-1 = f_w(c)^-1 f_w(b)^-1 = BBAB . AC`, `BC`, and
  `f_w(BC)^-1 = f_w(c) f_w(b) = babb . ca`. These use `phi_w(b) = ca` and `phi_w(c) = babb` only.
  The `f`-corners are the block junctions of the two `partial^+` words, so they are also
  independent of `w`.
- (b) the pairing `p`, which is fixed.
- (c) the gate relation of `f_w`. It is generated by `Df(x) = ` first letter of `phi(x)` and
  `Df(X) = ` inverse of the last letter of `phi(x)`. For every `w` in the claim, `phi_w(a)` begins
  with `a` and ends with `b`, because it ends in `abb` or in `Y = babb`. So
  `Df = (a->a, A->B, b->c, B->A, c->b, C->B)` for all `w`, and the gates are `{a}, {b}, {c}, {A, C}, {B}`.

So every check of Step 3 of `hyperbolic-f3-by-z-with-genus-two-surface-certificates-proof`
holds for `phi_w` exactly as it does for `phi_1`:

- `p` is a fixed-point-free involution with inverse labels;
- the boundary words are cyclically reduced;
- (L) holds;
- (2), (3) and (4) hold;
- `X` is connected, with `chi(X) = 6 - 8 = -2`.

The only `w`-dependent checks in `verify_surface.py` are the inverse and the matrix test, and Steps 1
and 2 prove those. As a sanity check, `family_certificates.py` substitutes each of the 128 words
`w` of length `<= 7` into the unchanged certificate file. It runs the checker with the explicit
inverse `tau_w^-1 o psi_1` and compares the characteristic polynomial with the closed form.
All 128 pass (`family_phi1_len7.log`).

**Step 4: surface subgroup.**

- `phi_w` is positive, so (TT) holds.
- By Step 3, `X` is a legal `f_w`-folded fatgraph with (2), (3), (4) and `chi(X) = -2`.
- By `legal-f-folded-fatgraphs-give-surface-subgroups`, `S*_{f_w}(X)` is `pi_1`-injective in `G_w`.
- `S*_{f_w}(X)` is closed, orientable and connected, with `chi = -2`, so it has genus 2. This is
  Step 4 of the two-automorphism proof, verbatim.

**Step 5: infinitely many isomorphism types.** For `G = F x|_phi Z`,

```text
H_1(G) = Z (+) Z^3 / (M - I) Z^3.
```

When `det(M - I) != 0`, the second summand is finite of order `|det(M - I)| = |chi_M(1)|`.

- For `G_w` that order is `2(i + 2j + 2)`. It is an isomorphism invariant of `G_w`: it is the order
  of the torsion subgroup of `H_1(G_w)`.
- Taking `j = 0` and `i = 0, 1, 2, ...`, it takes every even value `>= 4`.
- A fixed order is attained only for the finitely many `(i, j)` with `i + 2j + 2` fixed.
- So the family has infinitely many isomorphism classes. `QED`
