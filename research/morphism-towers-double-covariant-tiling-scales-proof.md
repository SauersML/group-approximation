Artifact §1–§5. Notation: `Q = [0,2D']^2`, `Box = [0,4D']^2`, and `ev_Q` the evaluation of functions on `S^Q` in
the commutative algebra generated by the `E'_a(c)`, `c ∈ Q`. `ev_Q` is a unital homomorphism and kills illegal
patterns by (Q3) (items E1–E5).

1. **Morphism facts (M1–M3).** Legal vertical dominoes have equal widths, and legal horizontal ones equal heights.
   `ω` of a legal box is the block concatenation, and it is legal. With shapes `>= 2`, the `c`-th column offset is
   at least `2c` and at least `w(ρ(0)) + 2(c-1)`.
2. **Lemma 1.** If `w(a) != w(b)`, then `E'_a(z) E'_b(z+e_2)` expands over the ball `z + B_(D')` into illegal patterns,
   so it is `0` by (Q3). Partitions of unity then give `M_(=m)(z) = M_(=m)(z+e_2)`. Heights are symmetric.
3. **Lemma 2.** `A_l B_l = B_l A_l = 1`, and `B_1 B_2 = B_2 B_1`, component by component, using Lemma 1 and that
   `u_1`, `u_2` commute.
4. **Lemma 3 (support).** On legal `ρ` with pointer column `0`, the letter of `ω(ρ)` at `(z_1, j + z_2)` depends only
   on `ρ` restricted to `[0, ⌊z_1/2⌋] × [0, ⌈z_2/2⌉]` (by M3).
5. **Lemma 4 (classical tower step).** Leaving the block at width `i+1` means reading `ω` of the shifted box `ρ(·+e_1)`
   at pointer column `0`. The support set shifted by `e_1` stays in `Q` for `z_1 <= 4D'-1`.
6. **Proposition 5.** For `z ∈ Box`, `(E_b(z) v)_(ij) = ev_Q(f^b_(ij,z)) v_(ij)`, by induction along monotone paths.
   - Base: the definition of `E_b(0)`.
   - Step `z -> z+e_1`: `u_1 ev_Q(f_(0j,z)) u_1^(-1)` is an evaluation on the shifted support set inside `Q`, so it
     commutes with `M_(=i+1)(0)`. The cross terms `i != i'` vanish, and Lemma 4 identifies the result.
7. **Theorem A.**
   - (Q1): orthogonality of the parent idempotents on each summand.
   - (Q2) for `|z-z'| <= 4D'`: conjugate by `A^m`, `m = min(z,z')`, into `Box`, where both are diagonal with
     entries in a commutative algebra.
   - (Q3) at base point `(2D',2D')`: the sites are `Box`. The product is diagonal with entries
     `ev_Q(prod_h f^(π(h))_(ij,z_0+h))`. A legal `ρ` in the support would exhibit `π` as a sub-block of the legal word
     `ω(ρ)`, so the function is `0`.
   - Nonzero, since `W_T ⊇ W'`. The dimension count is over the summands. ∎
