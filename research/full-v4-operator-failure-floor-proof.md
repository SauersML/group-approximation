The common `K_f` radical spectral decomposition supplied by `(VRC2)` makes
all three chart labels functions on one four-point set.  Insert the four
failure triples `(RAF3)`.  Their coordinate sums are two on `000` and one on
each other atom, which is exactly `(VOF3)`.  Orthogonality lets the identity
be summed over forbidden selector atoms, proving `(VOF4)`.

The unique forbidden `T_f` simple contains each `K_f` radical simple once by
`(VRC3)`.  Hence the four projections have equal rank and `e_(a,000)` has
one quarter of the mass of `p_a`.  Summation proves `(VOF5)`.  The explicit
spectral-product formulas show that no analytic choice of atom or coupling is
hidden in the construction.
