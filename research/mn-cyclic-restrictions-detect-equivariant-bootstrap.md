**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** Let `G` be a finite group. `B^G ⊆ KK^G`
is the class of objects KK^G-equivalent to an action on a separable type I
C\*-algebra.

1. **Arano–Kubota.** If `Res^G_H A ≃ 0` for every cyclic `H ⊆ G`, then
   `A ≃_(KK^G) 0`.
2. `A` lies in the localizing subcategory generated by the `C(G/H) ⊗ A`, for
   `H` cyclic.
3. `A ∈ B^G` iff `Res^G_H A ∈ B^H` for every cyclic `H ⊆ G`.
   `B^G` is generated by the `C(G/H)` with `H` cyclic.
4. If `A ∈ B^G`, then `A` and `A ⋊ H` lie in `B` for every cyclic `H ⊆ G`.

**Literature status of the converse of 4.** The authors do not know whether
it holds. The citation route quotes their sentences. At `G = Z/p` the converse
is the detection question, by
`zp-detection-equals-meyer-nadareishvili-sufficiency`.
