Stability replaces the approximate `G`-action by an exact one; property
(T;FD) of the stabilizer moves the two Pauli generators a bounded distance
onto the exact commutant of the stabilizer; co-density makes that
commutant central for the whole host; the prototype commutation relator,
now an exact conjugate of a relation between the two centralized
generators, forces them to commute, which is `J = 1` up to the stated
error.  Section 9 of the artifact.
