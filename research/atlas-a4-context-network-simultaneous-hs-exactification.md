Form the finite graph of groups with two `A8` chart vertices, one `A4` vertex
for each shortest pair-cube, and cyclic `C2`/`C3` edge groups identifying every
context generator with the literal chart element it uses.

For every canonical regular-atlas sequence whose packet defect tends to zero,
the induced unitary assignment to this fixed graph-of-groups presentation is
`o(1)`-close in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm to an **exact** representation
of its fundamental group in the same matrix dimension, simultaneously on all
vertex generators and graph stable letters.

The exactified images of the stable letters corresponding to non-tree edges
are `o(1)` from the identity, because the original atlas assignment sends all
of them to `1`.  Hence all thirty local A4 exactifications come with coherent
near-identity overlap intertwiners rather than unrelated choices of regular
cores.

Equivalently, after this compiler step the remaining continuous atlas data can
be placed on the finite cycle space of a fixed virtually-free graph of groups;
all finite vertex-group laws are exact.  Vertex representation types are
integer multiplicity vectors satisfying the finite edge-restriction equations.
