If `w_M=1` in the presented group, every homomorphism from that group sends
`w_M` to the identity.  The contrapositive proves `(PIC2)=>(PIC1)` without a
trace, dimension, or factor assumption.

The proof of `kleene-self-destroying-microstate-lemma` uses completeness only
after the fixed-point machine has been shown not to halt: it invokes `(PIC1)`
to obtain canonical `sqrt(2)` separation in hypothetical hyperlinear
microstates.  It never extends the representation in `(PIC2)` to a trace on
the final group.  Therefore replacing a tracial completeness model by
`(PIC2)` leaves that proof unchanged.

Finally, infinite cardinal multiplicities satisfy `aleph_0=2 aleph_0`, so an
exact representation may satisfy restriction/branching equations which have
no finite positive integer solution.  This observation concerns the chosen
representation's type decomposition, not an equality of finite-trace
projections in the universal group algebra; the latter is independently
forbidden by the regular-trace argument.
