Assume the marked affine-Leavitt Steinberg group is hyperlinear and take the
canonical microstates supplied by the atlas criterion.  Nontrivial marked
separation gives a positive-density nontrivial phase carrier.  On that
carrier the source-saturated checksum gives `(ASR1)`, while finite rank gives
`(ASR2)`.  Their fixed gap is `p^(-2)-p^(-4)>0`, contradicting vanishing
presentation and trace-window error.

This route uses one scalar cross-typed checksum.  It does not require the
full paired boundary recurrence, a physical common-factor decoder, the
four-operator standard-polynomial bridge, or literal equality of Reynolds
ranges.  The rectangular Gram contraction is necessarily extracted from the
finite matrix coordinates; the canonical subgroup-intersection formula rules
out every fixed group-word actuator.
