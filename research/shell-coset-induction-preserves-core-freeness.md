Let E=E_nu be a shell envelope of an infinite finitely generated
input, p=0^infinity, and H=E_p. For any abstract embedding E<=Gamma,
the action of Gamma on Gamma/H is faithful and has finitely
generated point stabilizers. The E orbit E/H is the original
highly transitive shell orbit Omega.

If Gamma is finitely presented, this action has type (A) exactly
when H has finite bi-index in Gamma, that is, H\Gamma/H is finite.
In that case E, and hence its input, embeds in a finitely presented
simple group. Neither a Cantor action of Gamma nor finite
presentation of E is required.

There exist computable shell envelopes E with decidable word
problem and no finite presentation for every infinite decidable
finitely generated input. Clapham supplies finitely presented
overgroups of these E. The conclusion above applies to their
coset actions, but supplies no finite bi-index assertion.
