**Established by citation.** Artem Dudko and Konstantin Medynets, *Finite
factor representations of Higman--Thompson groups*, Groups Geom. Dyn. 8
(2014), no. 2, 375--389, DOI `10.4171/GGD/230`, arXiv:1212.1230.

Corollary 3.6(2) states that every indecomposable character of `G_(n,r)` is
the regular character or a scalar character arising from the abelianization.
Definition 3.4 and the paragraph following it identify the abelianization as
trivial for even `n`. Specializing to the classical simple group
`V=G_(2,1)` leaves only the regular and trivial characters. The passage from
extreme characters to the displayed interval of all traces is the standard
Choquet decomposition of the character simplex.

Theorems 2.9--2.10 of the same paper supply the general compressible-action
mechanism, but Corollary 3.6(2) is the precise final statement consumed here.
