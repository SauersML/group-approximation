`R = K*G` (commutative field `K`, action `σ`, cocycle `α`) has the rank condition in each of these cases:

- **(a) Amenable quotient.** `σ` and `α` are inflated from an amenable quotient of `G`. Følner count of
  `K`-dimensions.
- **(b) Algebraic coefficients.** `α = 1`, and the coefficients of the matrices in question are algebraic over
  `K^G`. They lie in a finite `G`-stable extension on which `G` acts through a finite quotient, and the resulting
  skew group ring is finite dimensional.
- **(c) Invariant measure on complex points.** `char K = 0`, `α = 1`, and `Emb(K_0, C)` carries a `G`-invariant
  probability measure, where `K_0` is the `G`-subfield generated by the coefficients. Then `K_0 ⋊ G` maps into
  the stably finite affiliated-operator ring of `L^∞(Emb(K_0,C)) ⋊ G`.

**Examples of (c).**
- Bernoulli fields `k(x_{g,i})` with `G` permuting the variables.
- Monomial actions of subgroups of `GL_n(Z)`.

**Outside all three.** `Q(x) ⋊ PSL_2(Z)` by Möbius substitutions: nonamenable, transcendental over the fixed field
`Q`, and with no invariant measure on transcendental points of `P^1(C)`.

With `twisted-linear-profile-deficits-are-rank-condition-failures`, no twisted linear profile gives a Rokhlin
deficit in these regimes.
