Let `(A,phi)` and `(B,psi)` be separable completely selfless
C-star-probability spaces.  Then

```text
(A tensor_min B, phi tensor psi)
```

is completely selfless.

Consequently, finite direct products of countable completely selfless groups
are completely selfless.

**Established (literature).**  This is the tensor-product clause of Narutaka
Ozawa, *Proximality and selflessness for group C-star-algebras*,
arXiv:2508.07938v7, Theorem 11.
