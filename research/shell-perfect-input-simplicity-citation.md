The required construction makes E_nu a finite germ extension of
B=V and proves equality of their orbits on the whole Cantor set.
V is simple, locally moving, and has no global fixed point. Thus
[Belk--Hyde--Matucci, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.03149v1#S1.SS2)
makes E_nu' simple. It contains V=V', so it is nontrivial.

Since E_nu is generated by V and hat(P), and V is perfect, the
map P->(E_nu)_ab is onto. It factors through P_ab. If P is
perfect this abelianization vanishes, so E_nu=E_nu' is simple.
Finite generation follows from the construction when P is finitely
generated. No finiteness property of the relations follows from
the simplicity theorem.
