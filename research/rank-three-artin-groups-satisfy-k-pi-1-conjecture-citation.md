Citation import, not a reproof. Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv LaTeX source of
E. Delucchi, G. Paolini and M. Salvetti, *Dual structures on Coxeter and Artin groups of rank
three*, arXiv:2206.14518, Geom. Topol. 28 (2024), no. 9, 4295–4336 (bibliographic data from
the bibliography of arXiv:2607.24659). Parts read: the abstract and the introduction.

Verbatim, Main Theorem [Artin groups]: "Let G_W be an Artin group of rank 3. (i) G_W is a
Garside group. (ii) The K(π,1) conjecture holds for G_W. (iii) The word problem for G_W is
solvable. (iv) The center of G_W is trivial unless W is finite."

Abstract: "Within this framework, we prove the K(π,1) conjecture, the triviality of the
center, and the solubility of the word problem for rank-three Artin groups."
