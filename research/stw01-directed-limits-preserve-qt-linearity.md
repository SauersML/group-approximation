title: Unital directed inductive limits preserve quasitrace linearity without injective maps

Let `(A_i,phi_ij)` be a directed system of unital C*-algebras and unital
*-homomorphisms.  If every bounded 2-quasitrace on every `A_i` is a trace,
then every bounded 2-quasitrace on the C*-inductive limit

```text
A=lim_i (A_i,phi_ij)
```

is a trace.  The connecting maps need not be injective, and no exactness or
uniform approximation constant is assumed.
