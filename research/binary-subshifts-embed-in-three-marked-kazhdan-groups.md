The map taking a nonempty binary subshift `X` to
`G_X=EL_3(LC(X,F_2) semidirect Z)`, marked by the fixed three matrix
formulas `(c,t,w)`, is a topological embedding with closed image in the
space of three-marked groups. Moreover `X subset Y` holds exactly when
there is a marked epimorphism `G_Y -> G_X`.

Every image group is a marked quotient of the fixed Kazhdan group
`U=EL_3(F_2<u,v,p>/(uv=vu=1,p^2=p))`, itself generated by torsion of
orders `7,2,2`. The sets `{c,c^{-1},t,w}` have a common positive Kazhdan
constant. No numerical constant is claimed.

Finally `G_X` is simple exactly when `X` is infinite and minimal; those
instances are LEF, sofic, hyperlinear and operator MF. This is a statement
about marked groups, not a classification of unmarked isomorphisms.
