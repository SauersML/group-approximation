**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `G` be a countably infinite group, `L` a finite set
with a probability vector `λ`, and `E_G(L^G)` the set of ergodic `G`-invariant Borel
probability measures on `L^G`, with the weak* topology.

1. **Approximate Bernoulli models (Corollary 6.1).** Let `G ↷ (X, μ)` be a free
   ergodic p.m.p. action with `h^Rok_G(X, μ) < H(λ)`. For every open neighbourhood
   `U` of `λ^G` in `E_G(L^G)` there is a `G`-equivariant isomorphism
   `φ: (X, μ) → (L^G, ν)` with `ν ∈ U`. This is the case of Corollary 6.1 where the
   partition `𝓚` is trivial; the corollary also controls a Bernoulli factor.
2. **Upper semicontinuity (Lemma 6.2).** The map `ν ↦ h^Rok_G(L^G, ν)` on
   `E_G(L^G)` is upper semicontinuous. Lemma 6.2 proves the same for Rokhlin entropy
   relative to the `G`-invariant σ-algebra generated by a countable family of clopen
   sets; item 2 is the case of the empty family.
