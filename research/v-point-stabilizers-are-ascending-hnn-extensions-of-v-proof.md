**Item 1.** `C \ 0^n C` is the disjoint union of the `n` cones `0^i 1 C`, `0 <= i < n`.
The elements of `V` supported there form a copy of the Higman–Thompson group `V_(2,n)`,
and `V_(2,n)` is isomorphic to `V_(2,1) = V` (Higman). An element of `V` that is the identity
on some neighborhood of `p` is the identity on some `0^n C`. So `K = union K_n`.

**Item 2.** `t` maps `0C` onto `00C`, `10C` onto `01C` and `11C` onto `1C`, so it is a
bijection of `C` given by finitely many prefix replacements, that is, `t` is in `V`.
- It fixes `p`, and near `p` it is `0^a w -> 0^(a+1) w`. Every element of `Stab_V(p)` is
  `0^a w -> 0^b w` near `p`, so the germ group `(V)_p` is infinite cyclic, generated by the
  germ of `t`.
- For `n >= 1`, `t(0^n C) = 0^(n+1) C`. So `v` is the identity on `0^n C` iff `t v t^-1`
  is the identity on `0^(n+1) C`, which gives `t K_n t^-1 = K_(n+1)`.

**Item 3.**
- If `g` is in `Stab_V(p)` with germ `(germ t)^m`, then `g t^-m` is the identity near `p`,
  so it lies in `K`. Hence `Stab_V(p) = K <t>`.
- `K` is normal: conjugation preserves "identity near `p`" for elements fixing `p`.
- `K ∩ <t> = 1`, because `t^m` has nontrivial germ for `m != 0`.
- Put `D = K_2`. By item 2, `t^-1 D t = K_1 <= D`, and
  `union_(n >= 0) t^n D t^-n = union K_(n+2) = K`.

Standard fact: if `G = N ⋊ <t>` with `N = union_(n>=0) t^n D t^-n` and `t^-1 D t <= D`,
then `G` is isomorphic to `D *_phi`, where `phi(d) = t^-1 d t`. The reasoning:
- `D *_phi` is `(direct limit of D --phi--> D --phi--> ...) ⋊ <s>`.
- The natural map sends each copy `s^n D s^-n` isomorphically onto `t^n D t^-n`,
  compatibly with the inclusions. A direct limit of injective maps is injective.
- The cyclic quotients match.

`phi` is injective (it is a conjugation) and not surjective (`K_1 != K_2`).

**Item 4.** An ascending HNN extension of a group of type `F_n` along an injective
endomorphism has type `F_n`. For `n = 2`, the presentation of `D` plus the finitely many
relations `t^-1 d_i t = phi(d_i)` presents `D *_phi`. The general case is the standard
Bieri–Geoghegan argument (a graph of groups with one vertex of type `F_n`). `V` is of type
`F_infinity` (Brown), so `Stab_V(p)` is too.
