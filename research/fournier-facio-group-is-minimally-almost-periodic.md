Let `G` be the finitely presented torsion-free group fixed in
`fournier-facio-torsion-free-skeleton`.  Every finite-dimensional unitary
representation of `G` is trivial.  In particular `G` is minimally almost
periodic.

The conclusion is stronger than the previously recorded finite-dimensional
shadow statement: no quotient of `G`, rather than only the distinguished
simple subgroup, can survive in a finite-dimensional unitary representation.
