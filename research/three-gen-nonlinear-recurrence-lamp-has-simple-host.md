For every `q,a≥2`, the group `G_(q,a)=⟨A,X,T⟩` of
`three-generator-recurrence-lamp-is-nonlinear` is torsion-free of
derived length exactly three, is nonlinear over every field, and
embeds in a simple group of type `F_∞` generated by two finite-order
elements. Its action profile is `f(n)=a^|n|`, not a polynomial.

## Attempts

The proposed host specialization uses scalar lamps (`m=2`) and the
two recurrent tails `a^n` and `a^(n+1)`. It embeds the source in the
general profile group after adjoining constant dilation; it does not
claim that constant dilation was already generated by `A,X,T`.
The source's algebraic properties and the general recurrence host
construction are separate open prerequisites. Section 7 of the
artifact records all commutator and eigenvalue arguments. No theorem
for linear or metabelian groups supplies this source's conclusion.
