title: Finite induced failure ledgers name every Gram deficit but do not bypass common-source saturation
---

Finite induction decomposes every unwanted selected-type deficit exactly,
but compression to the analytic source turns the type decomposition into a
POVM unless that source reduces every type projection.

Even granting a projection-valued tagged ledger, it proves only that total
failure mass fits inside available capacity.  A Klein-four regular model
saturates this bound with zero accepted intersection and zero energy.
Source saturation or a mark-relative capacity surplus is still necessary.
