title: A positive Toms-Winter theorem implies Winter's Cuntz semigroup conjecture
---

If STW Problem XVIII has a positive answer, then STW Problem XXVI has a
positive answer.

If `Cu(A) ≅ Cu(A ⊗ Z)` then `A` is pure (Rordam; STW Proposition
`prop:cu-regular` (3) ⟹ (5)), in particular has strict comparison.  A
positive Toms--Winter conjecture then makes the simple separable
nuclear stably finite non-elementary `A` `Z`-stable.  (Elementary
algebras are excluded automatically: `Cu(K) ≇ Cu(K ⊗ Z)`.)
