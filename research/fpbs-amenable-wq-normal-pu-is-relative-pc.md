**ESTABLISHED.** Let `H` be an infinite amenable wq-normal subgroup of a
finitely generated group `Gamma`. Then for every Cayley graph `G` of `Gamma`,

```text
p_u(G) = p_c(H;G),
```

where `p_c(H;G)` is the infimum of the parameters at which some cluster meets
`H` in an infinite set almost surely.

So the global uniqueness transition is the *existence* transition for clusters
that are infinite along `H`. This is the relative analogue of reading `p_u` as a
critical point, and it holds on every generating set at once.

Section 2 of `research/artifacts/fpbs/docs/relative-susceptibility-reduction.md`
gives the two-line derivation.
