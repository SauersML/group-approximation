**ESTABLISHED (proposed; unreviewed).**  Derivation: [[galois-psd-monomial-subpair-no-infranormal-trace-proof]].
It is an obstruction for the nonsofic side of [[rank-three-finite-twisted-kazhdan-cone-is-sofic]].

## Setting

As in [[monomial-origin-level-subpairs-cannot-certify-rank-three-cone]]: `R_+ = F_q[x_1..x_d]`,
`Gamma = SL_3(R_+)`, `G = EL_3(R) x| SL_d(Z)`, `U_k = < e_ij(x^m) : |m| ≥ k >`, `H ≤ SL_d(Z)`,
`B = B_(H,k) = < U_k, H > = Ũ x| H`, `C = B ∩ Gamma`, `K0 = cone(H · R_+^d)`, `K` its closure.  A Kun-Thom
certificate needs `B`, `C` Kazhdan and `C` infranormal and not normal in `B`: the compressor set
`P_C = { t ∈ B : t C t^-1 ≤ C }` must generate `B`.  Write `H_≥0 = H ∩ M_d(N)` for the nonnegative monomial
automorphisms.

## Theorem

**(N1) Compressors are nonnegative.**  Let `K0` be salient.  If `t = u σ ∈ P_C` with `u ∈ Ũ`, `σ ∈ H`, then
`σ ∈ H_≥0`.  Conversely `C ∪ H_≥0 ⊆ P_C`.  Hence `C` is infranormal in `B` **if and only if** `H_≥0`
generates `H` as a group.

**(N2) The Galois-twisted PSD cone is the residual geometry.**  Let `O = Z[√2]`, `H = Sym^2 SL_3(O)` acting on
`Sym_3(O) ≅ Z^12` with basis `ω v v^T`, `ω ∈ {1, √2}`, `v ∈ {e_i, e_i + e_j}`.  Write `v ↦ (v_+, v_-)` for the
two real embeddings.  Then

```text
K0 ⊇ { v : v_+ positive definite },   K = { v : v_+ PSD } = PSD × Sym^2 R^3 ,
```

so `K0` is salient while `K` has the 6-dimensional irrational lineality space `V_- = { v_+ = 0 }`.  This is
exactly the case (M5) of [[monomial-origin-level-subpairs-cannot-certify-rank-three-cone]] leaves open, and
the (M3) wreath-product argument does not apply.  `H` is infinite and Kazhdan, and `psi(C) = 1`.

**(N3) But its nonnegative part is S_3.**  A symmetric matrix over `O` lies in the orthant spanned by the basis
iff `X_+ + X_-` and `X_+ - X_-` are both diagonally dominant with nonnegative entries.  From this,
`Sym^2 g` is nonnegative only if every column of `g` is a unit `ε = ±(1+√2)^n`, `n ≥ 0`, times a vector in
`± {e_i, e_i + e_j}`, and `det g = 1` forces all `n = 0`.  So `H_≥0` is the group of the six coordinate
permutations of `Sym_3`, of order 6.

**(N4) Consequence.**  For `H = Sym^2 SL_3(Z[√2])` and every `k ≥ 1`, `C` is **not** infranormal in `B_(H,k)`:
its compressors map into a subgroup of order 6 of the infinite group `H`.  Kun-Thom Theorem A never applies,
whatever the answer to property (T) for `Ũ` and `C`.  Together with (M2)–(M4), no Sym^2 PSD-type monomial
subpair, over `Z` or over `Z[√2]`, certifies nonsoficity of `T_3`.

**(N5) What a monomial certificate now needs.**  A subpair `B_(H,k)` can certify only if `H ≤ SL_d(Z)` is an
infinite Kazhdan group **generated by nonnegative integer matrices**, with `K0` salient and `K` non-pointed.
Nonnegative generation is the new condition, and it is strong: the finite-`H` case (M4) follows from it
again, since a finite `H` generated by nonnegative integer matrices consists of permutation matrices, and
those make `C` normal.  The geometry itself exists without (T): `H = < [[1,1],[0,1]] >` on `Z^2` is
nonnegatively generated, `K0` is salient, and `K` is a half-plane.

**Scope: the basis matters.**  (N1) is about `H` as a subgroup of `SL_d(Z)`, so it depends on the lattice
basis.  (N3) kills `Sym^2 SL_3(Z[√2])` in the basis `ω v v^T` named in the monomial node.  Conjugating by
`GL_12(Z)`, or passing to a finite-index subgroup, gives another `H` and is not covered.  Every such
conjugate still has `K0 ⊆ { v_+ PSD }`, so it is salient whenever its basis vectors have nonzero `v_+`, and
the unit-diagonal limits of (N2) suggest, but do not prove, that its closure is again non-pointed.  So the open case (N5) includes this
question: is there a lattice basis of an `SL_3(O)`-invariant lattice, inside `{ v_+ PSD }`, whose simplicial
cone is mapped into itself by a generating set of a finite-index subgroup of `SL_3(O)`?

## Computation

`experiments/galois-psd-monomial-cone-2026-09-17/nonneg_sym2_sl3_zsqrt2.py` checks (N3) on the radius-3 ball
(33438 elements) of `SL_3(Z[√2])` in elementary, unit-diagonal and signed-permutation generators: exactly
the six predicted nonnegative elements occur.  It also checks `det Sym^2 g = 1` on a sample and exhibits the
line `± E_11` of `V_-` in `K` as the limit of `(√2)^ε u^(-2n) E_11`.  Output: `out-radius3.txt`.

## Open

- (N5) with property (T): is there an infinite Kazhdan `H ≤ SL_d(Z)` generated by nonnegative matrices with
  `cone(H · R_+^d)` salient?  If none exists, every origin-level monomial subpair is dead, and the nonsofic
  side of [[rank-three-finite-twisted-kazhdan-cone-is-sofic]] needs non-monomial `B ⊇ U_k`.
- (N1) for non-monomial `B ⊇ U_k` is not proved.  The leading-monomial argument needs the `EL_3(R)` part `u`
  of a compressor to lie in `1 + M_3(F_q[P])` with `0 ∉ P`, which holds only when `B ∩ EL_3(R) = Ũ`.
