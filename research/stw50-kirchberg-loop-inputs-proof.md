Use Dadarlat Theorem 6.3 and Remark 6.4. The kernel of the natural
reduced KK map is K_0(D) modulo the images of the unit under
KK(D,D). A primitive unit in free K_0 is a basis element, so the
UCT lifts an endomorphism taking it to any prescribed K_0 class.
This kernel is zero. The odd UCT has zero Ext terms and the image
is precisely the unit-annihilating odd homomorphisms.

Use the finite graph K-theory theorem with rows indexed by sources,
and the graph simplicity and pure-infiniteness criteria. Bootstrap
closure supplies nuclearity, UCT, and the graded Kunneth formula.
Finally apply the unital Kirchberg--Phillips classification theorem
to scaled K-theory. The artifact identifies the primary sources
and checks each hypothesis for the two matrices actually used.
