Source: Benjamin Bachner, *Linear sofic representations of amenable
algebras*, arXiv:2602.05039v3, Section 6.1.

Abels' group `A_p` is finitely presented and solvable, hence elementary
amenable. It is torsion-free, and Kropholler--Linnell--Moody imply that
`K[A_p]` has no zero divisors for every field `K`. It is a finitely
generated linear group over characteristic zero, hence residually finite by
Malcev. Bachner's Theorem 6.4 therefore makes `C[A_p]` weakly stable.

For the negative half, Bachner invokes Elek--Grabowski, *Almost commuting
matrices with respect to the rank metric*, Groups Geom. Dyn. 15 (2021),
Theorem 4, whose Abels-group construction gives asymptotic rank-metric
solutions that stay uniformly far from genuine ones. Hence `C[A_p]` is not
rank stable.

This is a separation of weak stability (only maximally separating
linear-sofic representations must repair) from full stability (every
asymptotic homomorphism must repair); it does not assert that `A_p` is
unstable in every other metric convention.

The source and the stated complex-field conclusion were checked 2026-08-31.
