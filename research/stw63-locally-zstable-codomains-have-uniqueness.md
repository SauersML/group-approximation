title: Locally Z-stable unital codomains have unique Jiang--Su embeddings
distinct_from:
  stw63-zstable-hull-gives-pairwise-uniqueness: that is a criterion for one chosen pair; this identifies a codomain class for which the criterion applies to every pair.
---

Let `B` be a unital C\*-algebra with the following local absorption property:
every separable subset of `B` is contained in a unital `Z`-stable
C\*-subalgebra of `B`.  Then every two unital embeddings

```text
phi,psi:Z->B
```

are approximately unitarily equivalent.

This conclusion allows `B` to be nonseparable and does not require `B`
itself to be known `Z`-stable.
