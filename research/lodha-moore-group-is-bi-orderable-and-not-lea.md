**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `G_0` be the Lodha--Moore group, the finitely presented
subgroup of Monod's group `H` of piecewise projective homeomorphisms of `R`
(Lodha--Moore, arXiv:1308.4250).  Then `G_0` is:

1. finitely presented, torsion-free and nonamenable;
2. bi-orderable, hence locally indicable, left-orderable and with unique
   products;
3. not residually amenable, hence not LEA, hence not LEF.

So each atlas cell `!lea + P` and `!lef + P`, for `P` in
{left-orderable, locally indicable, bi-orderable, unique product}, has a
witness.  None of these cells waits for `left-orderable-non-sofic-group`.
Only `!sofic` and the approximation properties between LEA and soficity stay
behind that claim.
