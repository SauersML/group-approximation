All six rank-seven and all forty-two rank-eight triangle embeddings for the
minimal degree-four `(1,3,3,5)` topology have mixed face chirality.  None is
an admissible coefficient-only triangle compiler without an additional
carrier-closure gadget.  The first carrier-balanced systems have rank nine.
