Every nonzero quotient image of a positive element has spectrum
accumulating at zero, since otherwise its support is a nonzero projection.
The published spectral softness criterion therefore makes every source
class strongly soft and hence functionally soft. Tensoring a stably
projectionless algebra with Z preserves that property, by projection
lifting to a finite dimension-drop stage and point evaluation. The
simple-exact tensor-ideal theorem identifies every target quotient with
`(A/I) tensor Z`, so the entire target Cu semigroup is soft too.

The actual first-factor soft isomorphism consequently covers both whole
semigroups. For simple projectionless coefficient fields, all ideals are
open-support ideals, and Seth--Vilalta supplies purity in every base
dimension.
