There exist an infinite residually finite property-(T) group `Gamma`, an
orthonormal basis `X` of a Hilbert space `H`, an invariant probability
charge `mu` on `P(X)`, and a faithful genuine representation

```text
pi:Gamma -> U(H)
```

which is an essentially free amenable `mu`-near representation, but for
which

```text
mu_bullet(P)=0
```

for every finite-rank projection `P` on `H`. In particular every
finite-dimensional invariant sector has zero charge.
