**ESTABLISHED** through `thompson-f-has-vanishing-zg-cohomology-citation`.

For Richard Thompson's group F, the cohomology with coefficients in the group
ring vanishes in every degree: `H^n(F; ZF) = 0` for all `n ≥ 0`. Moreover, F admits
a free resolution of `Z` over `ZF` of finite type.

Source: K. S. Brown and R. Geoghegan, *An infinite-dimensional torsion-free
FP_∞ group*, Invent. Math. 77 (1984), 367–381, Theorem 7.2 (p. 379); the
finite-type resolution is stated on p. 368.

**Use in the graph.** `H^*(G; ZG)` is the classical coarse invariant of groups of
finite type. Together with `thompson-products-have-vanishing-zg-cohomology`, this
shows that it cannot separate F from `F × Z` or `F × F` (Zaremsky Problem 2.4,
parts (a) and (b)). A nonzero `H^k(T; ZT)` would still separate F from T (part (c)),
provided the quasi-isometry invariance of `H^*(G; ZG)` among groups of type F_∞ is
imported.
