For either arithmetic realization in the linked artifact, let `L` be
the scalar finite forward observation lattice or the weighted matrix
profile group, with `σL⊂L` injective and the full constant depth-zero
isotropy included. Let `K` be generated by the arithmetic base and
the radial automorphisms for finitely many generators of `L`.

Then `K` is a finitely generated faithful self-similar tree group, and
for every finite root number `r`, its RN group satisfies
`V_(b,r)(K)=Γ_r`, the full finite-germ group of this recurrence data.
These displayed actions are noncontracting: arbitrarily large powers
of constant q-dilation recur as their own identity-ray sections.
No type `F_∞` hypothesis on `K` itself is made.

## Attempts

Artifact Section 10 checks the proposed section rule: the identity
branch of a radial is its forward shift, other sections are in the
arithmetic base. To prove the full-group equality, one must localize
words in `K` to match all exceptional germs, use prefix charts to
match their images, and prove that the remaining map belongs to the
base. Those localization and actual-membership assertions remain
open; equality of abstract germ expressions alone does not prove
them. The persistent q-dilation powers obstruct a finite nucleus
for the displayed actions only, not every action of an abstract group.
