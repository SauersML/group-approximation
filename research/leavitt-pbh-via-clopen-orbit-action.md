Let `U = L_(F_2)(1,2)^×` act on `M = C(C, F_2)` as on
`leavitt-unit-clopen-indicator-stabilizer-is-fg`, and let `𝒪` be the family of
clopen sets with `U·χ_[0] = {χ_A : A ∈ 𝒪}` (step 2 of
`leavitt-cantor-module-fp-via-indicator-stabilizer`). Consider the action of `U` on
the countable set `𝒪`. BFFHZ's definition of type (A) is quoted verbatim on
`orbit-finite-affine-actions-are-type-a`.

1. **Finitely presented.** `leavitt-unit-group-finitely-presented`.
2. **Faithful.** `R` is simple (`leavitt-algebras-l-k-1-n-are-simple` is used on the
   existing route `leavitt-pbh-via-cantor-module-affine-actor` for faithfulness on
   `M`). An element fixing every `χ_A`, `A ∈ 𝒪`, fixes a spanning set of `M` (step 2 of
   the sibling route: `𝒪` contains all proper nonempty clopens), hence acts trivially
   on `M`, hence is `1` since `U` acts faithfully on `M`.
3. **Finitely generated stabilizers.** `U` is transitive on `𝒪`, so all point
   stabilizers are conjugate to `Stab_U(χ_[0])`, finitely generated by the premise.
4. **Finitely many orbits of pairs.** Step 3 of the sibling route: at most 16
   `V`-orbits on `𝒪 × 𝒪`, each `U`-orbit a union of them.

So the action of `U` on `𝒪` is of type (A), and `U` (with all its subgroups) satisfies
permutational Boone--Higman.

**Trust surface.** Faithfulness of `U` on `M` is the same input used on
`leavitt-pbh-via-cantor-module-affine-actor`; it is not re-derived here.
