## Setting

A *compression core datum* over a finite alphabet `S` consists of:

- letters `p_1, ..., p_k, t, c` in `S`;
- words `w_1, ..., w_k` and `p_0` in the letters `p_i`;
- the compression relators `W_t = { t p_i t^-1 w_i^-1 }`;
- the centralizer relators `W_c = { [c, p_i] }`;
- the defect word `sigma = [t c t^-1, p_0]`.

Let `Q = F(S)/N` with `W_t u W_c <= N` and

```text
Q = << sigma N >>^Q .                                         (DG)
```

A *permutation model* is a homomorphism `rho : F(S) -> Sym(X)` with `X` finite.
Put `G_rho = < rho(p_1), ..., rho(p_k) >`, a finite permutation group. Let `d_H` be
the normalized Hamming distance. A sofic approximation of `Q` over `S` is a
sequence of models `rho_n` with `d_H(rho_n(w),1) -> 0` for `w in N` and
`d_H(rho_n(w),1) -> 1` for `w notin N`.

## Theorem

**(A) Exact normalization and centralization.** Suppose that along a subsequence

```text
rho_n(t) G_(rho_n) rho_n(t)^-1 <= G_(rho_n)   and   rho_n(c) commutes with every rho_n(p_i).
```

Then `d_H(rho_n(s),1) -> 0` along it for every `s in S`. In particular a nontrivial
`Q` has no sofic approximation over `S` of this form. The hypothesis holds whenever
`W_t u W_c` is satisfied exactly. No relation of the subgroup `< p_i >` and no
property (T) is used.

**(B) Exact core action with approximate centralizer.** Suppose `P_0 = < p_1..p_k | W_P >`
is a finite presentation of a Kazhdan group with `W_P <= N`. Suppose that along a
subsequence:

- `rho_n(W_P) = 1` exactly, so `rho_n` restricts to a genuine finite `P_0`-action;
- `rho_n(t)` normalizes `G_(rho_n) = rho_n(P_0)`;
- `max_i d_H([rho_n(c), rho_n(p_i)], 1) -> 0`.

Then `d_H(rho_n(s),1) -> 0` for every `s`. So `Q = 1` if the sequence is a sofic
approximation. The centralizer relators may fail; only normalization of the finite
core image by the compressor is exact.

**(C) Unitary and linear models.** Replace `Sym(X)` by `GL_d(k)` over any field `k`,
`G_rho` by the unital algebra `A_rho` generated by the `rho(p_i)`, and `d_H` by the
normalized rank distance `rk(g-h)/d`. In the unitary case the normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt distance also works. If `rho_n(t) A rho_n(t)^-1 <= A` and `rho_n(c)`
commutes with each `rho_n(p_i)`, the conclusion of (A) holds. So no nontrivial `Q`
satisfying `(DG)` has a hyperlinear or linear-sofic approximation of this form.

**(D) Covers.** Let `pi : H ->> Q` be an epimorphism and `p_i, t, c in H` elements
satisfying the core relations in `H`. Suppose `Q = << pi(sigma) >>`. If `Q != 1`, then:

1. `sigma` lies in the finite residual of `H` and in the kernel of every
   finite-dimensional linear representation of `H`;
2. `delta_(ker pi)` is not a weak limit of invariant random subgroups of finite
   `H`-actions, so `ker pi` is not co-sofic in `H`;
3. no sofic approximation of `Q` is pulled back, through a section, from genuine
   finite actions of `H`;
4. no hyperlinear or linear-sofic approximation of `Q` is pulled back from genuine
   finite-dimensional representations of `H`.

Derivation: `compression-exact-models-kill-defect-generated-groups-proof`.

## The class this kills, and where every member dies

The invariant is **the cardinality (or dimension) of the finite core image
`G_rho`** (respectively `A_rho`). Every member dies at the same step: a
compressor that maps a finite group or a finite-dimensional algebra into itself
maps it onto itself. Then the transported centralizer commutes with `p_0`, so the
defect acts trivially. By `(DG)` every generator then acts almost trivially.

Applied to `sofic-preserving-single-defect-kazhdan-router` (Attempt 7 there), with
`P = Gamma(3) ltimes Z^3`, this kills the following for every `(SPR1)` output:

- sofic approximations pulled back from finite actions of the source `R = E * F_2`,
  or of any residually finite or other cover in which the core relations hold;
- the "genuine partner-lift", or co-sofic kernel, route;
- congruence-type models, where `P` acts through `Gamma(3)/Gamma(3m) ltimes (Z/m)^3`
  and `t` acts by the dilation mod odd `m`;
- every model in which `t` acts by an automorphism, or an exact normalizer, of a
  finite image of `P`;
- hyperlinear models pulled back from finite-dimensional representations of covers.

**Survivors.** A surviving approximation over a generating set containing the core
letters must satisfy one of two conditions for all large `n`:

- (S1) the compressor does not normalize the permutation group generated by the
  `P`-generators; if `P` acts exactly, this is the only remaining escape;
- (S2) both the centralizer relators and the core relations are violated.

The exceptional set of Attempt 3 of the router must therefore meet the
compression step at every scale.

## Calibration

- **The defect-generation hypothesis `(DG)` is necessary in (D).** The LEF simple
  Kazhdan group of `simple-kazhdan-lef-group-from-minimal-subshift` has no finite
  quotients. It is a quotient of a finitely presented Kazhdan cover `H` by Shalom's
  theorem, and LEF gives homomorphisms from `H` onto finite groups whose kernels'
  invariant random subgroups converge to the Dirac mass at the kernel. So property
  (T) together with the absence of finite quotients does not kill cover lifts; the
  defect clause does.
- **Exact normalization cannot be relaxed to approximate compression in general.**
  The literal finitely presented group `E` is sofic (`literal-group-sofic`) and carries a
  nontrivial marked compression defect that every finite quotient kills
  (`literal-group-not-stable-in-finite-actions`, `unsquared-defect-finite-model-invisible`).
  Applied to `H = E`, the argument of (A) shows that its sofic approximations must
  violate the exact normalization hypothesis at every large scale. The obstruction is
  global (cardinality), not local: at points where all relators hold, the defect need
  not act trivially, because `p_0` does not lie in the compressed subgroup.
- **What is not claimed.** No lower bound on the density of the violation set is
  asserted, and the theorem does not decide soficity of any `(SPR1)` output.
