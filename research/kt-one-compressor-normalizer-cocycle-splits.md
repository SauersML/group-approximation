Let `X=G/Gamma`, `Y=X x {0,1}` and

```text
R=L(Sym_fin(Y)).
```

For every proper positive monomial compressor `A`, put
`H_A=<Gamma,A>`.  Then

```text
Sigma_A=Sym_fin(Y) rtimes H_A
```

is sofic.  Hence `R rtimes H_A=L(Sigma_A)` is Connes embeddable and there is
a trace-correct homomorphism

```text
u_A:H_A -> N_(R^omega)(R),       Ad(u_A(h))|R=alpha_h.
```

For the Kun--Thom specialization `q=2`, the restricted action is outer, so
the trace clause is automatic and the pullback normalizer cocycle splits as
a bare group extension.

In particular this applies separately to the two named compressors `A` and
`B` in `kt-two-positive-compressors-generate-full-obstruction`.  Together
with `kt-two-compressor-normalizer-cocycle-splits`, it localizes any failure
of the full lift strictly beyond both obvious subgroups: a nonsplit relation
must use `Gamma` and at least two compressor directions simultaneously.
