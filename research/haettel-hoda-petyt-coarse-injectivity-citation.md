Citation import. T. Haettel, N. Hoda, H. Petyt, *Coarse injectivity, hierarchical
hyperbolicity, and semihyperbolicity*, Geom. Topol. 27 (2023), 1587–1633;
arXiv:2009.14053v5 (May 7, 2022). Read from the arXiv PDF on 2026-09-13 (pages 1–4).

- Definition (pp. 1–2), verbatim: "A metric space is said to be coarsely injective if
  there is a constant `δ` such that for any family `{B(x_i, r_i) : i ∈ I}` of balls with
  `d(x_i, x_j) ⩽ r_i + r_j` for all `i, j ∈ I`, the `δ`-neighbourhoods of those balls have
  nonempty total intersection." Also: "a group `G` is said to act properly on a metric
  space `X` if `{g ∈ G : gB ∩ B ≠ ∅}` is finite for every metric ball `B` of `X`."
- **Theorem A** (Proposition 2.16, Corollary 3.6), verbatim: "Let `(X, 𝔖)` be a
  hierarchically hyperbolic space with metric `d`. There exists a metric `σ` on `X` such
  that `(X, σ)` is coarsely injective and quasi-isometric to `(X, d)`. Moreover, `σ` is
  invariant with respect to the automorphism group of `(X, 𝔖)`."
- p. 3, verbatim: "We therefore have that every hierarchically hyperbolic group acts
  properly cocompactly on a coarsely injective space".
- Abstract: "hierarchically hyperbolic groups—including mapping class groups of
  surfaces—are coarsely injective".
- p. 4, verbatim: "It is clear that every Helly group is coarsely injective." Hughes and
  Valiunas constructed a hierarchically hyperbolic group that is not biautomatic, hence
  not Helly (p. 4, citing [HV22]).
