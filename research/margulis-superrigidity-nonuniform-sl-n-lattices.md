**ESTABLISHED by citation.** Let `k >= 3` and `Γ` a lattice in `G = SL(k,R)` with `G/Γ` not compact. Let
`ϕ : Γ → GL(n,R)` be any homomorphism. Then there are a continuous homomorphism `ϕ̂ : G → GL(n,R)` and a
finite-index subgroup `Γ' <= Γ` with `ϕ̂(γ) = ϕ(γ)` for all `γ ∈ Γ'`.

Instance used: `Γ` any finite-index subgroup of `SL_3(Z)`.

Source: D. Witte Morris, *Introduction to Arithmetic Groups*, arXiv:math/0106063v6 (7 May 2015),
Theorem 16.1.1. Citation route `margulis-superrigidity-nonuniform-sl-n-lattices-citation`.
