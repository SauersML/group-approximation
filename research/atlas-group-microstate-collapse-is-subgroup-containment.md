ESTABLISHED, by the exact evaluation in `atlas_asc_countermodel_energy.py`.

Call a microstate **inner** if its relative chart unitary is a group element:
`U = lambda_E(mu)` for some overgroup `E >= A_8` in whose regular
representation both charts sit, `beta = mu alpha mu^(-1)`.  The affine
characteristic-two family of the rank-three audit is of this kind -- its
second chart is conjugation by `mu = (m, e)` inside `E = M semidirect A_8`.

For every inner microstate, with `Z = Stab_(A_8)(mu)`:

```text
<U, rho(x)U> = delta(x in Z),
sum_s l_s^2  = E(Z) := sum_(x in Z) (W_pkt)_x,
a^2 + b^2    = 4 - 2[h_a in Z] - 2[h_b in Z]  in {0, 2, 4},
||pi_U(s)-1||_2^2 = 0 or 2 for every word.
```

Every trace is a delta function because traces of a regular representation
are, and because the trace is conjugation invariant.  Consequently:

1. **The collapse is a containment.**  `ker rho(W_pkt)` is the `H`-fixed
   space, and `rho(x)U = U` exactly when `x in Z`, so

   ```text
   E(Z) = 0   <=>   H <= Z   <=>   a^2+b^2 = 0.
   ```

   Confirmed numerically where it could have failed: `E(H) = 0` exactly,
   `E(1) = 1516`, no cyclic subgroup generated by a support element of
   `W_pkt` has zero energy (energies from 998 to 1796), and over all 5050
   subgroups generated by pairs of support elements the only zero-energy one
   is `A_8` itself.

2. **Nothing is approximate.**  Defects are `0` or `sqrt(2)` and the
   covariance is `0`, `2` or `4`.  An inner microstate therefore cannot sit
   near the `1/40` floor of `raw-swap-forces-two-s3-covariance-energy`: it
   either collapses exactly or misses by a macroscopic amount.

3. **The class is already excluded, and consistently so.**  Inner microstates
   are essentially permutation microstates, and `Q` is nonsofic, so no inner
   microstate can kill the whole separating packet.  The characteristic-two
   countermodel is exactly this: it kills all 234 boundary words with
   covariance `4`, and it is the interior separator `c_19243` that it fails.
   The computation puts a number on that failure -- `sum_s l_s^2 = 1372`
   there, so the constant of
   [[atlas-linear-energy-controlled-by-full-packet-defect]] is at least 686.

The open content of the collapse claim is therefore entirely in the
**non-inner** microstates, where the defects are genuinely continuous and no
stabilizer subgroup exists to read them off.
