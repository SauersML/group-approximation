**Source.** B. Durand, A. Romashchenko, *The expressiveness of quasiperiodic
and minimal shifts of finite type*, arXiv:1802.01461; published in Ergodic
Theory and Dynamical Systems 41 (2021).

The definition of an effective shift, Definition 1 (simulation) and Theorem 7
are quoted verbatim in the target node from the body of the arXiv PDF, extracted
to text on 2026-09-17 (Section 1.1 for the definition of effective shifts,
Section 1.2.3 for Definition 1 and Theorem 7). The proof of Theorem 7(a) is in
Section 6.3 of the paper ("Proof of Theorems 6–7"), with combinatorial lemmas in Section 7. It builds a self-simulating
tile set with a standardly aligned grid of macro-tiles and a computable
canonical configuration of `A`, and proves minimality of the resulting SFT
(Claim 5 there). That proof was skimmed, not re-verified; the node rests on the
published statement.
