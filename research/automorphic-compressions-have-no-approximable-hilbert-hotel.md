**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof in `automorphic-compression-orbit-rigidity-proof`; elementary; not
independently reviewed). This refutes `schreier-hilbert-hotel-compression-data-exist`.

**Lemma (profinite orbit rigidity).** Let `Gamma` be a finitely generated group, and let
`A <= Gamma_0 <= Gamma` be finitely generated subgroups such that

```text
A N = Gamma_0 N   for every finite-index normal subgroup N of Gamma.        (PE)
```

Let `H <= Gamma` be a Chabauty limit of finite-index subgroups, and put `X = Gamma/H`. Then every
finite `A`-orbit in `X` is `Gamma_0`-invariant, so it is a single `Gamma_0`-orbit.

**Theorem.** Let `Gamma` be finitely generated, `alpha in Aut(Gamma)`, and `Gamma_0 <= Gamma` finitely
generated with `A = alpha(Gamma_0) <= Gamma_0`. Then (PE) holds. Consequently, for every `H` that is a
Chabauty limit of finite-index subgroups (no `alpha`-invariance, (IS) or profinite closedness is needed),
and every `g in Gamma_0`:

```text
if A g x_0 is finite, then g x_0 in A x_0            (x_0 = H in X = Gamma/H).
```

So the data (HH) of `schreier-hilbert-hotel-data-give-matricial-compression-rings` never exist.

**Invariant and death step.**

- *Invariant.* The closure of `Gamma_0` in the profinite completion. An automorphism permutes the
  characteristic subgroups `K_m`, so in every finite quotient the compressed image `alpha(Gamma_0)` and
  `Gamma_0` have the same size, hence are equal. So `alpha(Gamma_0)` is profinitely dense in `Gamma_0`.
- *Death step.* The local embedding `X -> Gamma/H_n` of Step 7 of `schreier-hilbert-hotel-ring-reduction-proof`
  carries the finite room block `A q` to an `A`-invariant set. On `Gamma/H_n` the groups `A` and
  `Gamma_0` act through the same permutation group, so the block is `Gamma_0`-invariant. Pulling back,
  `A q` is `Gamma_0`-invariant and contains `x_0 = g^(-1) q`. The Hilbert hotel has no room outside
  the copy of itself.

**Casualties.**

- `schreier-hilbert-hotel-compression-data-exist` is false, including the open candidate
  `Z wr_(Z[1/2]) BS(1,2)` of its Attempt 6 and every host with `H` not profinitely closed.
- Route `matricial-compression-ring-via-schreier-hilbert-hotel` is vacuous.
- **Corollary (the whole tensor-lamp template dies).** Suppose all of the following:
  - `X = Gamma/H` with `H` a Chabauty limit of finite-index subgroups;
  - `t : X -> X` is any bijection with `t(gamma x) = alpha(gamma) t(x)`;
  - `R = M_d(k)^(tensor X) x| Gamma`, with `psi` induced by `(t, alpha)`;
  - `R_0` is generated by `M_d(k)` at finitely many sites and by `u_s^(+-1)` for `s` in a finite
    generating set of `Gamma_0`, where `alpha(Gamma_0) <= Gamma_0` and `psi(R_0) <= R_0`;
  - `z` is a unit of the lamp algebra `B = M_d(k)^(tensor X)` centralizing `R_0`.

  Then `psi(z)` also centralizes `R_0`, so the ideal in (R5) is `0`. The mechanism: `t` permutes the
  finite `Gamma_0`-orbits, and it permutes the finitely many finite orbits carried by `R_0` among
  themselves. So the support of `psi(z)` stays off the sites of `R_0`. The proof is Step 4 of the
  route.
- Item 5 of the target (the Grigorchuk-Lysenok local witness) is consistent with the Lemma. There the
  substitution `sigma` is not an automorphism, and `sigma(G)` has strictly smaller level images than
  `G`, so (PE) fails.

**What survives for the ring gate `matricial-compression-ring-exists`.** A witness must avoid (PE) or
approximability. At least one of the following must hold:

- the compression `psi` of `R_0` is not induced by an automorphism of a finitely generated group that
  acts on an approximable set;
- the matricial embedding (R1) does not come from finite actions of `Gamma` on sets (Hamming-sofic
  models do not suffice for (R1));
- the compressed subgroup is not finitely generated.
