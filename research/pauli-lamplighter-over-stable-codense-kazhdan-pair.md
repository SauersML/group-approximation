**Data.**  `G` a finitely generated group; `G_0 <= G` finitely generated by
`S_0`, with Lubotzky--Zimmer property (T;FD) (uniform Kazhdan constant
`kappa > 0` for `S_0` over all finite-dimensional unitary representations);
the pair finite-dimensionally co-dense
(`two-transitive-infinite-action-is-finite-dimensionally-codense`):
`pi^(G_0) = pi^G` for every finite-dimensional unitary `pi`; and
`g_1 in G \ G_0`.  The **Pauli lamplighter** is

```text
Gamma = < G, X, Z, J |  relators of G,
                         [X, Z] = J,
                         [s, X] = [s, Z] = 1   (s in S_0),
                         [X, g_1 Z g_1^(-1)] = 1 >.                 (PL1)
```

It maps onto `E_Y x| G`, the Pauli group on qubits indexed by `Y = G/G_0`
with `G` permuting qubits, in which `J = -1`; so `J != 1` in `Gamma`.

**Theorem.**  Let `U` be a finite-dimensional unitary tuple for the
generators of `Gamma` with `Def(U) <= t` (over the finitely many relators
displayed in `(PL1)` and a finite set of relators of `G`), and suppose the
`G`-part of `U` is within `eta` on generators of an exact finite-dimensional
unitary representation `rho` of `G`.  Then

```text
||U(J) - I||_2 <= 2t + 8(t + 2eta)/kappa + 4|g_1| eta.             (PL2)
```

**Corollary.**  If `G` is HS-stable in the same-dimension sense (any
modulus: for every `eta` there are `delta` and finitely many relators of
`G` such that defect below `delta` puts the tuple within `eta` of an exact
representation), then `Gamma` is not hyperlinear.

*Proof of the theorem.*  Put `k = U(X)`, `k' = U(Z)`, and let
`P : M_d -> rho(G_0)'` be the projection onto the commutant of
`rho(G_0)`, i.e. onto the invariant vectors of the finite-dimensional
unitary representation `Ad rho|_(G_0)` on `(M_d, <,>_HS)`; it is the
conditional expectation onto `rho(G_0)'`, hence contractive in operator
norm.  For `s in S_0`, `||rho(s) k rho(s)^* - k||_2 <= ||U(s)kU(s)^* - k||_2 +
2 eta <= t + 2eta`, so by (T;FD) `||k - Pk||_2 <= (t+2eta)/kappa`, and
likewise for `k'`.  Write `X' = Pk`, `Z' = Pk'`.  By co-density applied
to `pi = Ad rho`, `X'` and `Z'` commute with all of `rho(G)`; in
particular `rho(g_1) Z' rho(g_1)^(-1) = Z'`.  The relator
`[X, g_1 Z g_1^(-1)] = 1` gives `||[k, U(g_1) k' U(g_1)^(-1)] - I||_2 <= t`;
replacing `k, k', U(g_1)` by `X', Z', rho(g_1)` costs at most
`4(t+2eta)/kappa + 4|g_1| eta` (commutators are Lipschitz on the
operator-norm unit ball), so `||[X', Z'] - I||_2 <= t + 4(t+2eta)/kappa +
4|g_1|eta`.  Finally `||U(J) - [k,k']||_2 <= t` and `||[k,k'] - [X',Z']||_2 <=
4(t+2eta)/kappa`.  Add. ∎

*Proof of the corollary.*  If `Gamma` were hyperlinear, microstates for its
canonical trace would give tuples with defect tending to zero on any
finite set of relators and `||U(J) - I||_2 -> sqrt 2` since `J != 1`;
stability of `G` supplies `rho` with `eta -> 0`, and `(PL2)` forces
`||U(J) - I||_2 -> 0`. ∎

**What the proof does and does not use.**  No Pauli dimension bound, no
Gowers--Hatami exactification of a large group, no all-pairs table, no
PCP, no area: the only approximation steps are the single (T;FD)
projection and the single prototype relator.  Finite-dimensionality enters
exactly once, through co-density, which fails for `l^2(G/G_0)`; this is
the Hilbert-hotel mechanism in its minimal form.  The hypothesis doing the
work that the compilers could not do is **HS-stability of the host** `G`:
it converts the approximate `G`-action into an exact one, after which
every transported relator is an exact conjugate of a prototype.

**Consistency checks.**  Co-density forces `[G:G_0] = infinity`, so `Gamma`
is never a finite extension of `G`.  If `G` is hyperlinear and HS-stable
it is residually finite (`hyperlinear-hs-stable-is-residually-finite`),
so the host is either itself nonhyperlinear or a residually finite group
with an infinite-index finitely generated (T;FD) subgroup that is
profinitely dense and finite-dimensionally co-dense.  An infinite
hyperlinear Kazhdan group is never HS-stable, so the host must not have
(T) while its stabilizer has (T;FD): the same tension Dogon--Vigdorovich
exploit, resolved here by a subgroup instead of a central extension.  The
existence of a host is `stable-group-with-codense-kazhdan-subgroup`.
