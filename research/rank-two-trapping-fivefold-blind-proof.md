Part 3, Proposition 8.1.

The map f is 1 on the fat wedge, so f = fbar o q with q the degree-one
collapse onto S^N. A regular preimage of y != 1 lies in the open top cell.
So [M, f]_red is the unit-map image of the stable class of fbar, the iterated
Samelson product of the leaves. The leaves are multiples of alpha or have the
form omega o h.

Samelson products are natural: <a o g, b o h> = <a, b> o (g ∧ h). We have
<alpha, alpha> = 0 and <alpha, omega> = omega o eta_3 (prerequisite).
<omega, omega> generates pi_6(S^3) ≅ Z/12, with stable image y in pi_3^s. So
the stable class is eta^a·y^b·(leaf images) with a + b = 4.

These products all vanish. eta^4 = 0. eta^3·y is a multiple of 12·nu^2 = 0.
eta^2·y^2 and eta·y^3 vanish, since eta·nu = 0 and eta kills 3-torsion.
y^4 lies in pi_12^s = 0.
