Citation import. Read on 2026-09-18 from the arXiv PDF, text extracted with `pdftotext`:
U. Hamenstädt and S. Hensel, *Spheres and projections for Out(F_n)*, arXiv:1109.2687v3
(19 April 2014), published in J. Topology (doi:10.1112/jtopol/jtu015).
- Theorem 4.3 and its statement are on p. 15.
- Section 4 opens on p. 14 with Remark 4.1 and Theorem 4.2 (Dehn--Nielsen--Baer for `S_{g,1}`).
- The introduction states the same result as Theorem 3: "Map(S_{g,1}) is a coarse Lipschitz retract
  of Out(F_{2g})".

The proof works in the doubled handlebody `W_{2g}`. It intersects sphere systems with an embedded copy
of `S_g^1 × {1/2}` and surgers the result to binding arc systems. Not reproved here.
