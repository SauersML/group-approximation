The full proof is in the artifact (Sections 0–7). Notation: `φ_u` means applying
`φ_{c_1}`, then `φ_{c_2}`, and so on, for `u = c_1…c_ℓ`. `S` is the finite semigroup
of the `φ_u` with `u ≠ ""`, and `e_p` is the idempotent power of `φ_p`.

1. **Cloning system (§1).** The axioms (2.5) and FCS1–3 hold coordinatewise.
   Injectivity of the cloning maps is exactly `ker φ0 ∩ ker φ1 = 1`. F-type triple
   lists modulo refinement are `T(G_*)` (guide §5.1), and all triple lists give the
   V-type model.
2. **Action (§2).** For a nonempty word `p`, `Ω_p` is `G × {0,1}^*` modulo
   `(h,x) ~ (φ_p h, xp)`. A list `{(a_i,b_i,h_i)}` sends `[h,x]` to
   `[φ_p^m(h)·φ_u(h_i), b_i u]` whenever `x p^m = a_i u`. This does not depend on
   `m`, on the representative, or on the refinement, and it is a right action.
3. **Faithfulness (§3).** `M := {h : t(s(h)) = e for all idempotents t ∈ S and
   s ∈ S ∪ {id}}` is trivial. On `M` the only idempotent of the semigroup generated
   by `φ0|_M, φ1|_M` is the zero, so the semigroup is nilpotent, and iterated cloning
   is injective. Take `P` with one word for each idempotent of `S`. An element acting
   trivially on `⊔_{p∈P} Ω_p` then has `a_i = b_i` and `h_i = e` literally.
4. **Group and subgroup (§4).** Injectivity of `μ` on refinement classes makes the
   V-type model a group, `𝒱 ≤ Sym(Ω)`, and gives `T(G_*) ↪ 𝒱`.
5. **Orbits and generation (§5).** Each `Ω_p` is one `𝒱`-orbit. `𝒱` is generated by
   the image of `V` (finitely generated, by
   `thompson-v-finitely-presented-infinite-simple`) and the finitely many elements
   `{("0","0",h),("1","1",e)}`.
6. **Context-free loop languages (§6).** A pushdown automaton stores `h` in its
   state. Its stack holds `x` with each letter decorated by `φ` of the suffix below
   it, over the finite stack alphabet `{0,1} × End(G)`. Letters act either by a
   bounded rewrite of the top of the stack (T1) or, when `x` is short, by rewriting
   the whole stack (T2). Acceptance is `h ∈ ker e_p` and stack in `p^*#`, so it
   recognises `{w : [e,""]_p·w = [e,""]_p}`.
7. **Conclusion (§7).** `𝒱` is finitely generated and has a faithful action with
   finitely many orbits and context-free loop languages. By
   `faithful-context-free-action-groups-embed-in-v`, `𝒱` embeds in `V`, and hence
   so does `T(G_*)`.

The artifact's Section 9 records a computational sanity check that is not part of
the proof: script
`experiments/zaremsky-2-16-lehnert-conjecture-2026-09-16/twisted_power_cf_action.py`.
It checks step 3 exhaustively for 234677 pairs over six small groups, and steps 2,
3 and 6 on random data.
