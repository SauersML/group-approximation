title: Thompson's group F is torsion-free and contains free abelian groups of every finite rank, so its cohomological dimension is infinite
---

**ESTABLISHED** through `thompson-f-has-infinite-cohomological-dimension-proof`.

Let `F` be Richard Thompson's group, acting on `[0,1]` by orientation-preserving
piecewise-linear homeomorphisms with dyadic breakpoints and slopes powers of 2.

1. `F` is torsion-free.
2. For every `n >= 1`, `F` contains a subgroup isomorphic to `Z^n`.
3. Hence `cd_Z F = ∞`.

This is classical: Brown–Geoghegan (1984) show `F` is an infinite-dimensional
torsion-free group of type `F_∞`. The proof route gives the short direct
argument, so no citation is load-bearing.
