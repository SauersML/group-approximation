Direct reading of the published paper.  The full text of Rev. Mat. Iberoam.
39 (2023) 1097--1104 was fetched from EMS Press (open access) and converted
with `pdftotext`; Theorem 1.1, Lemma 1.2, Definitions 2.1--2.3 and the
remark after 2.3, Propositions 3.2--3.3, the proof of Lemma 1.2, the proof
of Theorem 1.1 in Section 3.1, and the bibliography were read on 2026-08-14.

The claim is a report on the structure of that proof, so the route is the
reading itself.  Nothing here is reproved, and the imported step
(Lemma 1.2, direction (2) implies (1), citing J. Algebra 477 (2017) 147--162)
was **not** read — that is the remaining literature debt for anyone who needs
an explicit metric.
