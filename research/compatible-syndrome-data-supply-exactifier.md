Use the common tuple's actual joint effects `E_S=W^*Pi_S W`. Its third
clause says all infeasible effects vanish. The support characterization
therefore supplies an unrestricted conditional CPTP recovery returning a
state on the original Hamiltonian space and satisfying every term outside
the measured fault set. This proves the existing exactifier claim, which
does not demand efficient preparation or preservation of the input.
All statements refer to the same ports as the tuple's domination bound.
