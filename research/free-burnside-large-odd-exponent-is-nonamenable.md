title: Free Burnside groups of sufficiently large odd exponent are non-amenable, hence infinite
---

**Literature input.** For `m >= 2` and every sufficiently large odd `N`, the
free Burnside group `B(m,N) = F_m / <<w^N : w in F_m>>` is non-amenable. In
particular it is infinite, since every finite group is amenable.
