Citation import from D. Gaboriau, *Around the orbit equivalence theory, measure equivalence, cost and ℓ2
Betti numbers*, lecture notes dated October 3, 2025,
https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/ME-Cost-L2-Lectures/ME-Cost-L2-lectures.pdf, Section 2.3
"Cost and Treeings", page 12.

- **Definition 2.16 (Treeing [Ada90]).** "A graphing Φ is said to be a treeing if (almost) every Φ[x] is a tree."
- **Proposition 2.23.** "If Φ is a graphing of a p.m.p. equivalence relation R such that C(Φ)=C(R)<∞, then Φ is a treeing."
- The sentence after the proof: "The above result (Prop. 2.23) states that when a (finite cost) graphing realizes the infimum in the definition (2.8) of the cost then it is a treeing."
