Every coordinate factor `B(H_j)` has uniqueness by the arbitrary-cardinal
rank theorem.  The product-closure theorem chooses the coordinate unitaries
with one common finite-set tolerance and assembles them into a unitary of
`M`.  If a finite-dimensional coordinate is present, no unital Jiang--Su
embedding into the product exists and the assertion is vacuous.
