Let `B` be a unital C-star algebra, let `e in B` be a projection, and put

```text
A=B tensor K(H),       p=e tensor 1_(B(H)) in M(A).
```

Then

```text
dim_nuc(A+C p)=dim_nuc(A)=dim_nuc(B).                    (SCP1)
```

No fullness, complementation, quasidiagonality, or regularity of the two
support ideals is required.  The proof uses an exact exhaustion.  For
finite-rank projections `P_n` increasing strongly to one, the stage
generated by `B tensor P_n K P_n` and `p` is

```text
(B tensor M_(rank P_n)) direct_sum C.                    (SCP2)
```

Indeed the tail projection `e tensor (1-P_n)` annihilates the finite matrix
stage.  These stages are nested and dense in `A+C p`, so no transition-band
or additional colour occurs.

This class genuinely reaches the remaining nonquasidiagonal overlap.  There
is a unital dimension-one Toeplitz coefficient algebra `B` and a projection
`e in B` such that, after stabilization, both `e tensor 1` and
`(1-e) tensor 1` are nonfull, their support ideals are proper essential and
noncomplemented, and the extension by their common overlap has a nonzero
Toeplitz boundary map.  It therefore has no projectional quasicentral
approximate unit.  Formula `(SCP1)` nevertheless computes the adjoined
algebra's nuclear dimension as one.
