**ESTABLISHED TWO-EDGE DICHOTOMY.**  Let `A_0,A_1` be the corrected
finite-dimensional star-algebras generated by two copies of the same shared
BCS and role tuple.  Let `phi,psi:A_0->A_1` be star-isomorphisms and let
unitaries `U,V` implement the two parallel edges:

```text
U a U^*=phi(a),              V a V^*=psi(a).           (TEH1)
```

Their holonomy

```text
W=V^*U                                                     (TEH2)
```

automatically normalizes `A_0`, with induced automorphism
`psi^(-1)phi`.  This is an identity, not a presentation-dependent
heuristic:

```text
W a W^*=V^*phi(a)V=psi^(-1)(phi(a)).                   (TEH3)
```

Let `Q in A_0'` be a structural reducing carrier and let `B_f in A_0` be
the role-packed forbidden projection.  Its full reducing support on `Q` gives

```text
Hull_(A_0)(Q B_f)=Q.                                   (TEH4)
```

Put `D=W Q B_f W^*`.  Equations `(TEH3)--(TEH4)` imply

```text
Hull_(A_0)(D)=W Q W^*.                                 (TEH5)
```

Thus a two-edge loop does not return a child whose hull remembers
`tau(QB_f)`.  It returns the whole translated carrier.

There is a sharper same-object conclusion.  If the two edges implement the
same structural map, `phi=psi`, then `W in A_0'`.  If the proposed return is
also required to preserve the reached carrier,

```text
W Q W^*=Q,                                             (TEH6)
```

then `W` commutes with both `Q` and `B_f`, and hence

```text
D=Q B_f,
tau(QD)=tau(QB_f).                                     (TEH7)
```

The desired overlap-loss constant is exactly zero.  If `(TEH6)` is dropped,
the only possible growth is the payload-independent orbit growth
`Q join WQW^*`; after a finite normalizer orbit stabilizes it cannot renew.

The statement is dimension-uniform under the standard fixed-packet
correction.  Exactify the finite structural packets, average each approximate
edge into its fixed covariance intertwiner space, and take its polar part.
For a fixed two-edge cell this changes `(TEH3)--(TEH7)` by `O(sqrt(E))` in
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  The quantitative support estimate for
`B_f` then fills all but `O(sqrt(E))` of `WQW^*`.  No positive fraction of
the returned hull remains payload-selected.

This rules out the minimal two-edge holonomy as the unary role-packed return.
The load-bearing word must fail to intertwine the full shared algebra on at
least one edge.  It must act only on a genuinely payload-selected corner and
make that partial incidence visible to ordinary relator energy.  A full
algebra edge, even when paired with a different edge map, has already erased
the role-packed density before the reached-hull recurrence is applied.
