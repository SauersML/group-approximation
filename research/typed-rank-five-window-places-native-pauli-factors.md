Dead route.  The proposed step was to choose finitely many rank-five
Steinberg `(St2)--(St3)` triangles involving the raw and comb coefficient
occurrences and infer, after finite-packet exactification, that

```text
q,T_0,T_1 in A_raw,          comb,Q_P in A_comb
```

up to vanishing normalized Hilbert--Schmidt error for two commuting returned
algebras.

`mixed-steinberg-loops-admit-morita-rectangular-model` refutes this language,
not merely one attempted list.  Every finite pasted diagram of typed
rank-five/six commutator triangles is an associativity diagram for
rectangular coefficient maps.  It holds exactly with source object `E`,
target object `F=E direct_sum E`, and different object identities `1_E` and
`1_F`.  Hence no finite subset of those triangles can erase the object type
or place target factors back in the identical returned source reservoir.

The first object-erasing successor is now identified explicitly by
`leavitt-range-sum-fold-is-first-object-erasing-row`: the fixed long-root word
`r_fold=C_0C_1D^(-1)` compares the two target range paths with one source
diagonal return.  It is not generated by this typed triangle language.
However `leavitt-range-sum-fold-does-not-pay-reynolds` proves that this scalar
row, even with literal occurrence reuse, does not identify the packet
reservoir or charge its boundary.  The surviving requirement is therefore a
payload-coupled cross-reservoir row tying a fold occurrence to the five
native factors, not another typed multiplication window.
