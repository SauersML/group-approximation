Citation import, not a reproof.

* I. Benjamini, R. Lyons, Y. Peres, O. Schramm, *Group-invariant percolation on
  graphs*, Geom. Funct. Anal. 9 (1999), Theorem 1.1: on a unimodular nonamenable
  quasi-transitive graph, critical percolation has no infinite cluster a.s.
* T. Hutchcroft extends `N_infinity(p_c) = 0` to every quasi-transitive graph of
  exponential growth.

Both statements are recorded as surveyed in Choi--Seo arXiv:2508.08932v2,
Section 2 (page 3), read from source 2026-09-11. This route asserts only that
the cited theorems say what the target records.
