**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** **Setting.** As in
`thompson-f-metabelian-pairs-obey-sharp-linear-commutator-bound`. For a law `v` (a nontrivial word)
and a group `Γ`, `V(Γ)` is the verbal subgroup generated by the values of `v` in `Γ`. `dl` denotes derived
length, and `D = max_j d(R_j(A,B), 1)`.

**Statement.**
1. **Laws.** If `V(F) ≠ 1`, there is `N_v < ∞` such that every pair `(A, B)` whose generated group
   satisfies `v` has `d(AB, BA) ≤ N_v · D`.
2. **Derived length.** `F^(ℓ) = [F, F]` for every `ℓ ≥ 1`. So for each `ℓ` there is `N_ℓ < ∞` with
   `d(AB, BA) ≤ N_ℓ · D` whenever `dl <A, B> ≤ ℓ`. Here `N_1 = 0`, and `N_2 ≤ 3` in the sharper form
   `2 D_1 + D_2`.
3. **Exponent.** For every `e ≥ 1`, pairs generating a group of exponent dividing `e` satisfy
   `d(AB, BA) ≤ N_(e) · D` with `N_(e) < ∞`.
4. **Skew products.** Let `(A_0, B_0)` act on `[n_0]`, let `K ≤ Sym(k)`, and let `c, d : [n_0] → K`. The
   skew product `A(p,i) = (A_0 p, c(p) i)`, `B(p,i) = (B_0 p, d(p) i)` generates a group of derived
   length at most `dl <A_0, B_0> + dl K`.
   - Abelian fibres over commuting bases give metabelian pairs, so claim
     `thompson-f-metabelian-pairs-obey-sharp-linear-commutator-bound` applies.
   - Fibres and bases of bounded derived length obey item 2.
5. **Firewall.** Every pair `(P, Q)` on `k` points is the skew product over the commuting translations
   of `Z/N × Z/N` with constant cocycles `c ≡ P`, `d ≡ Q`, and it has the same `D` and `t`. So a linear
   bound that is uniform over skew products with commuting bases and arbitrary fibres is the linear
   inequality for all pairs. By `thompson-f-sofic-iff-defect-to-separation-ratio-vanishes` that is the
   nonsoficity of `F`.

**Consequences.**
- **Witnesses.** A family with `D/t → 0` must generate groups of unbounded derived length, or
  nonsolvable groups. It must also escape every law `v` with `V(F) ≠ 1`, and every bounded exponent.
  The only pair on main below ratio `1/3` (`4/13`, 16 points) generates a nonsolvable group.
- **Skew-product searches.** What can lower the ratio is the fibre group, not the cocycle pattern.
  Searches with abelian, or bounded-solvable, fibres over solvable bases are bounded below by
  `1/N_ℓ`, and with unrestricted fibres they are as general as all pairs.
- **All laws.** If `F` satisfies no group law, item 1 covers every law. This is attributed in the
  literature to Brin–Squier and was not read at source here.

**Scope.** The constants `N_ℓ` for `ℓ ≥ 3` and `N_(e)` are not computed. One import (pinned in
`thompson-f-is-lea-only-if-amenable-proof`): every nontrivial normal subgroup of `F` contains `[F,F]`.
It decides nothing about `thompson-f-is-sofic`.
