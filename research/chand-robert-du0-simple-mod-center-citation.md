This is an imported theorem, credited to its authors and not reproved here.
The arXiv TeX source of arXiv:2108.09796 was read on 2026-09-17.

Verbatim, from the introduction:

> Let $A$ be a unital C*-algebra. Let $U_0(A)$ denote the connected
> component of the identity of the unitary group of $A$. Let $DU_0(A)$
> denote the commutator (or derived) subgroup of $U_0(A)$.

> \begin{theoremA} If $A$ is simple and unital then $DU_0(A)$ is simple
> modulo its center. \end{theoremA}

Verbatim, from the proof of Theorem A:

> If $A=\C$, the theorem is trivial. Assume that $A\neq \C$.

> Let $G$ be a normal subgroup of $DU_0(A)/Z(DU_0(A))$ not consisting
> solely of the identity element.

and the proof ends with

> It follows that $G=DU_0(A)/Z(DU_0(A))$.

So the theorem is about abstract normal subgroups, which is the form used
downstream.  The target restates it with no strengthening.
