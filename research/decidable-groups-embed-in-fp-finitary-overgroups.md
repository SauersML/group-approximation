**OPEN.** Recorded as a prerequisite of `pbh-via-fp-finitary-overgroups`.

## Statement

For every finitely generated group `G` with solvable word problem there are a
countably infinite set `X` and a finitely presented group `Gamma` with
`Alt_fin(X) <= Gamma <= Sym(X)` and `G <= Gamma`.

## Why this form

By `fp-finitary-permutation-overgroups-are-type-a-actors`, faithfulness,
finitely many orbits of pairs and finitely generated stabilizers hold for free
in every finitely generated overgroup of `Alt_fin(X)`. So the only content of
this claim is finite presentation. It can fail by itself: nothing in the
automatic part forces any overgroup to be finitely presented.

## Established necessary conditions

1. **Quotient.** Put `N = Gamma ∩ FSym(X)` and `Q = Gamma/N`. Then `Q` is
   finitely presented (item 4 of the lemma), and `G / (G ∩ N)` embeds in `Q`
   with `G ∩ N` locally finite (item 5). An input with no nontrivial locally
   finite normal subgroup must embed in the finitely presented quotient `Q`.
2. **Hosts.** Every obstruction to a host class that passes to quotients by
   locally finite normal subgroups also rules out finitary extensions of that
   class, for inputs with trivial locally finite radical (Consequences of the
   lemma).
3. **Word problem.** `Gamma` embeds in the finitely presented simple group
   `SV_Gamma` (item 3), so `Gamma` has solvable word problem. This is the
   classical fact that finitely presented simple groups do (Kuznetsov). So
   the claim cannot be proved by any construction that works for all
   recursively presented `G`. Decidability of `G` must be used.

## Known kills of candidate constructions

- **Split lampshufflers.** `FSym(Lambda) x| Lambda` over the regular action is
  never finitely presented for infinite `Lambda` (Genevois--Tessera,
  Theorem 1.5). Over free `Lambda`-sets with `Lambda` virtually indicable it
  is not even `FP_2` over `Q` (`lampshuffler-groups-are-not-fp2-over-q`). So
  the trivially split extension of a host by the finitary group of a free
  orbit does not work.
- **Houghton type with two ends.** `H_2` is not `FP_2` (Brown). Twisting a
  Houghton group by an infinite fibre forces zero translation
  (`every-countable-group-lies-in-a-faithful-ha2-actor`), so the translation
  quotient `Z^{n-1}` cannot carry a nontrivial input.
- **Full Cantor quotients.** When `Q` already contains a copy of standard `V`
  acting on clopens, `Q` itself has a type (A) action
  (`fp-full-binary-cantor-groups-have-type-a-actions`). The finitary layer is
  then redundant for the input, so a construction of that kind proves nothing
  beyond the Cantor route.

## Converse

Whether permutational Boone--Higman implies this claim is open. The natural
candidate, `Gamma` generated by `FSym(S)` together with a type (A) actor
`Lambda` acting on `S`, needs a finite presentation that is not proved here.

## Attempts

- **Split extension over a free orbit.** Take a host `Lambda` containing `G`
  and form `FSym(Lambda) x| Lambda`. It dies at finite presentation
  (Genevois--Tessera Theorem 1.5; `lampshuffler-groups-are-not-fp2-over-q`).
- **Split extension over a type (A) orbit.** Take a type (A) actor
  `Lambda ↷ S` and form `FSym(S) x| Lambda`. Its finite presentation is not
  proved here. Cornulier's criterion covers wreath products `B wr_S Lambda`,
  not `FSym(S)`, so it does not decide the question. Even where it works it
  needs a type (A) actor containing `G` as input, so for this claim it is
  circular.
- **Deferred.** The live attack is a non-split finitary extension of a
  finitely presented decidable host `Q` in which `G` lifts. Two conditions
  must hold: the lift exists, and the extension kernel is finitary. They are
  not yet separated into claims.
