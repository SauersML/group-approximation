Section 2. If G=<H,a> and arbitrarily many pairwise disjoint nonidentity power sets of ab_i, b_i in H, have order bounded by M, then rel-C(R_G;R_H)=0 for every free pmp action. Additional cost is bounded by q+(1-q^(M-1))^k+2 eta. No zero-cost attaining graphing is asserted.
