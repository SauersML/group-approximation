The group word `i_0(h)i_1(h)^(-1)` is reduced of amalgam length two, so its
regular trace is zero and the two vertex unitaries have squared `L^2`
distance two.  The block swap commutes with the common diagonal copy of `C`
but exchanges the two inequivalent vertex restrictions, giving an exact
`C`-invariant and non-`A`-invariant vector.  Property (T) projection therefore
does nothing to it.  Finally, property (T) of `A` and infinite index of `C`
exclude coamenability of `C` in `A`, and the Monod--Popa correspondence
transfers this to the group-factor inclusion.
