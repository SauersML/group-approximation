Let K be finitely generated, recursively presented, and torsion-free. Apply
the stronger torsion-order-preserving theorem to obtain K->H with H finitely
presented and Wh(K)->Wh(H) injective. Since Tord(K) is empty and
Tord(H)=Tord(K), H is torsion-free. This is exactly the target. QED
