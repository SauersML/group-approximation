title: A packet-defined return carrier forces the wandering word into relative character kernels

Let `Gamma=<S|R>` be finitely presented, let `H<=Gamma` be generated by a
fixed finite list of packet/pulse words, and let

```text
chi:Gamma -> T,                  chi|H=1.                     (TIC1)
```

Suppose a proposed finite-matrix decoder assigns to every sufficiently
accurate canonical microstate `U` a contraction `X_U` using only the
exactified restriction `U|H`.  More precisely, the same exactification
choices and the same contraction are admissible after the scalar twist

```text
U^chi(s)=chi(s)U(s).                                      (TIC2)
```

If both `U` and `U^chi` are claimed to satisfy

```text
||(w(U)-1)X_U||_2<=epsilon,
||(w(U^chi)-1)X_U||_2<=epsilon,                          (TIC3)
```

then

```text
|chi(w)-1| ||X_U||_2<=2 epsilon.                         (TIC4)
```

Consequently a twist-invariant decoder cannot give a fixed-positive-mass
approximate return for `w` unless `chi(w)=1` for every character trivial on
`H`.  Equivalently, the image of `w` is trivial in the relative
abelianization

```text
Gamma/(normalClosure(H)[Gamma,Gamma]).                    (TIC5)
```

Indeed circle-valued characters separate points of every discrete abelian
group, including torsion points.

This applies without any exact-representation or residual-finiteness
hypothesis.  Scalar twisting preserves every relator defect and preserves
the canonical trace profile: for every nonidentity fixed word `g`,

```text
tr(g(U^chi))=chi(g)tr(g(U))->0.                          (TIC6)
```

It therefore applies directly to analytically compressed Fanizza exits and
Thompson first-hit cuts whenever those carriers are formulas in the
packet/pulse letters.  An HNN or covariance bridge which occurs with nonzero
free exponent in the proposed return word has a circle-valued scalar gauge
and is ruled out immediately.  A viable wandering return must be
exponent-balanced in every such bridge (typically a commutator or a genuinely
coupled cycle), or else its carrier construction must itself depend on the
bridge phase.

The latter alternative is not supplied by the current one-sided pulse and
Schur cells.  Their projected exits, prefix cuts, and packet exactification
are all formed before the bridge multiplicity unitary is chosen.  Thus these
cells transport positive mass to a first-hit sector, but they cannot by
themselves turn one unbalanced transporter into an approximate identity on
that mass.

This is a relative-character firewall, not a no-go for all wandering
promotion.  Exponent-balanced Britton words survive it.  Those words still
face the separate regular-energy/localized-commutator fences already recorded
in Cairn.
