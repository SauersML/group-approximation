**OPEN.** There are a countable hyperlinear group `H` and
`Gamma <= G <= H`, with:

- `Gamma` and `G` Kazhdan, and `Gamma` infranormal in `G`;
- `g in G`, `z in C_H(Gamma)` and `gamma in Gamma` with
  `[g z g^-1, gamma] != 1`.

**Why it matters.** By `sofic-groups-kill-rigid-compression-defects`, every
such `H` is nonsofic. This claim therefore establishes
`hyperlinear-nonsofic-group` and answers Question 3.4 negatively.

**The cheapest source.** By `rigid-compression-defect-normalization-dichotomy`,
it is enough to find one homomorphism `sigma` from an infranormal Kazhdan pair
into a tracial matrix ultraproduct whose relative commutant is **not**
normalized by `sigma(G)`. The group generated by `sigma(G)` and a
non-normalized centralizing unitary is hyperlinear and carries the defect. So
a counterexample to `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs` suffices, and that
counterexample may be trace-preserving or not.

## Attempts

- **Canonical wreath algebra.** Normalization fails in the wreath's own
  factor `L(W)` (`kun-thom-wreath-factor-is-exact-nonnormalized-commutant`),
  but `L(W)` is not known to embed in a matrix ultraproduct. That embedding
  is `hyperlinear-wreath-model`.
- **Single compressor.** The one-compressor wreath is sofic and its canonical
  microstates keep a nontrivial defect
  (`kt-single-compressor-canonical-lamps-do-not-exactify-actor`). But the
  compressor group generated by `Gamma` and one strict compressor is not
  Kazhdan: its wreath is sofic with a nontrivial defect, which the Hamming
  criterion forbids for a Kazhdan compressor group. So it is not a witness.
  Any witness must use a Kazhdan group of compressors.
- **Ultraproducts of genuine representations.** For ultraproducts of genuine
  finite-dimensional representations of `G`, normalization holds.
  - Property (T) of `Gamma` makes every bounded asymptotic commutant
    2-norm-close to the exact finite-dimensional commutant.
  - Commutant no-growth then gives equality at each finite stage.
  - So a witness needs representations of `G` that are not flexibly
    exactifiable.
- **Ultraproducts of genuine representations of Kazhdan covers
  (hl-nh-el3-compressor, 2026-09-14).** Normalization still holds for models
  pulled back from genuine finite-dimensional representations of a cover of
  `G`, with kernel two-norm trivial, in which a Kazhdan lift of `Gamma` is
  compressed by lifted compressors
  (`kazhdan-cover-actor-models-normalize-vertex-commutants`). The vertex need
  not round, and the commutant may be diffuse.
  - At the Theorem E pair this covers the Steinberg actor
    `St_r(L) ⋊ SL_d(Z)` for every `r, d >= 3`, including `r = 3`, where the
    Steinberg kernel is outside stability
    (`steinberg-actor-models-carry-no-rigid-wall`).
  - So a witness must also avoid every such cover. Every model is pulled
    back from a free cover, where no Kazhdan lift exists. Artifact
    `research/artifacts/hl-nh-el3-compressor-2026-09-14.md`.
