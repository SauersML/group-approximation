Complete hand proof, artifact §§2, 5, 6. Refereed 2026-09-16. The McCool quotation was checked
against the arXiv TeX, and Lemma 5.1 and (C1)/(C3) were rechecked by an independent script. See the
referee section of `notes/zaremsky-2-12-loop-braided-v-type-f-infi-swarm-2026-09-16.md`.

Write `A_ij: x_i ↦ x_j x_i x_j^{-1}`.

1. **Fundamental domain (§2).**
   - `LB_n = P ⋊ S_n`, and cloning maps permutation braids to permutation braids with the permutation
     predicted by Lemma A of the PG artifact (Lemma 2.1).
   - So `[g, Γ] ↦ [s_g, Γ]` is a well-defined P-invariant retraction onto Q, which gives a strict
     fundamental domain with pointwise-fixing stabilizers.
   - Under dangling, `Q ≅ OM(K_n)`.
   - The stabilizer of `[P_π, Γ]` is `P_π (PLB_{n−m})κ_Γ P_π^{-1}`, since `κ_Γ` is injective.
2. **McCool's presentation.** P is generated by the `A_ij`, with relators `[A_ij, A_kl]`,
   `[A_ij, A_kj]` and `[A_ij A_kj, A_ik]`.
   - Source: McCool 1986, quoted in Ershov–Zaremsky arXiv:2505.18826v3 §2.2. The quoted
     presentation is invariant under inverting generators and reversing words.
   - Each relator involves at most 4 indices.
3. **Cabling (Lemma 5.1).** In `PLB_{m+1}`:
   - `(A_pq)κ_k = A_{p̂q̂}` if `p, q ≠ k`;
   - `(A_pk)κ_k = A_{p̂,k+1} A_{p̂,k}`;
   - `(A_kq)κ_k = A_{k,q̂} A_{k+1,q̂}`.

   These come from three base computations at `k = 1`, e.g. `(σ_1β_1)κ_1 = σ_2σ_1β_1β_2`, which is
   `A_13 A_23`. They are transported by `(w g w^{-1})κ_k = (w)κ_j (g)κ_j ((w)κ_j)^{-1}` for permutation
   braids w and pure g, using (CS1). A script checks them for `n ≤ 7`.
4. **Consequences (Cor. 5.2, Lemma 5.3, `n ≥ 6`).**
   - `P_{(a,b)}` is generated by the `A_ij` with `i, j ∉ {a, b}`, together with `A_ib A_ia` and
     `A_aj A_bj`.
   - (C1) `A_ij` lies in the edge stabilizer `P_{{v,w}}` whenever `{i, j}` avoids both pairs.
   - (C3) Each generator h of `P_v` lies in `P_{{v,w}}` for every w avoiding `{a, b} ∪ S(h)`.
5. **Colimit (Theorem 6.1, `n ≥ 7`).**
   - Set `φ(A_ij) := ι_v(A_ij)` for v avoiding `{i, j}`. This is independent of v by (C1) and the
     connectivity of `OM(K_{n−2})` (diameter 2 for `n − 2 ≥ 5`).
   - Relators map to 1 inside one vertex group avoiding their ≤ 4 indices, so φ is a homomorphism
     inverse on the left to ψ.
   - φ is onto: by (C3), `ι_v(A_ib A_ia) = ι_w(A_ib A_ia) = φ(A_ib) φ(A_ia)`, and similarly for the
     other generators.
