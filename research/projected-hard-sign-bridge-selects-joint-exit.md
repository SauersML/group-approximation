Let `H` be the exactified central support projection of one forbidden
Schur--Clifford atom, let `A` be its named relative hard involution, and put

```text
P=H(I-A)/2.                                             (PHS1)
```

Although `P` is a nonlinear joint spectral projection and is not the
negative projection of any scalar group word on the whole packet, it is a
perfectly legitimate right compression in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.
It satisfies `AP=-P`.  Hence, for every unitary transport `U` and target hard
involution `B`,

```text
tau(P)-tau((I-B)U P U^*/2)
 <= (1/4)||BU-UA||_2^2.                                (PHS2)
```

This is `(HST1)` with the *joint* source projection retained.  Every allowed
sector on which `A` may also be negative disappears after right
multiplication by `P`; it contributes neither to the left side nor to the
intertwining estimate.  Thus the group presentation needs only the ordinary
hard-sign covariance word `BU=UA`.  It does not need a word whose negative
support is `H AND (A=-1)`.

For the mixed router take `H=QF`, `A=b`, so

```text
P=QF(I-b)/2=X.                                         (PHS3)
```

The product-clock bridge can make `B` the degree-`2n` E5 hard pulse.  Equation
`(PHS2)` then puts the compressed mass in its negative spectral space.
Promoting this to a first-hit boundary requires the additional hypothesis
that the compressed source character is trivial on all lower E5
coefficients.  Leading degree alone does not supply it;
`triangular-e5-pulses-admit-persistent-tail-character` gives an exact
countercharacter.

Consequently the joint violating exit is transported to the correct hard-sign
space without ever compiling the joint event into a scalar group word.
Possible transported mass from allowed `A=-1` sectors is harmless: the lower
bound is compressed by `P`, and only the first-detection component of `UP`
is entered in the reservoir ledger.

This does **not** prove first-hit localization or return `I-P`.  In
particular it does not solve the remaining problem of retaining the allowed
`K=+1,b=-1` sector in the next active carrier.
