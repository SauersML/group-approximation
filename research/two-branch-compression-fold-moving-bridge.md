**INVALIDATED CLOSED-LOOP ROUTE.**  Use both binary compression-preimage
Whiteheads, return their two range signs through the literal fold
`C_0C_1=J`, and close the chart path before taking Singer conjugates.  The
intended cancellation was that the compression edge gauges telescope around
the closed path while one literal Whitehead label move remains.

`closed-compression-fold-loop-retains-s3-gauge` gives an exact finite marked
model of this entire occurrence table.  The two branch Whiteheads act as
the two transpositions of a three-character orbit and the fold signs form
its diagonal normal subgroup.  A closed branch word has trivial outer
permutation, whereas a word moving the marked source once has a nontrivial
outer permutation and retains its multiplicity action.  The fold does not
alter that quotient.  Thus this finite closed loop does not provide the
ungauged typing `(ESB2)`.
