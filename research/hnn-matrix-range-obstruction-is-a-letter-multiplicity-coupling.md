Let `C` be generated by a finite symmetric set `S` not containing the
identity and let

```text
G=<A,t | [t,C]=1>,       C<A.
```

Suppose `phi_n:G->U(d_n)` is a canonical hyperlinear approximation and the
`C`-generator tuple stays distance at least `epsilon` from
`MR_(d_n)(C^*(C),S)`.  Write in the tracial ultraproduct

```text
T=[phi_n(t)],        X_s=[phi_n(s)].
```

Then there is a uniformly bounded tuple `B=(B_s)_s` in `T' cap M` such that,
for the trace-preserving expectation `E_T:M->W^*(T)`,

```text
sum_s||B_s||_2^2=1,
Re sum_s tau(B_s^*X_s)>=epsilon/2,

(sum_s||B_s-E_T(B_s)||_2^2)^(1/2)
   >= epsilon/(2 sqrt(|S|)).                                     (LMC1)
```

Consequently a counterexample to selected ucp capture cannot be carried by
the intrinsic abelian spectral algebra of the Haar stable letter.  It needs
a positive-density operator in the larger multiplicity commutant
`T' cap M` which correlates with the nontrivial lattice generators.  For the
large-prime arithmetic HNN this is the precise dual outlier left after the
exact congruence face has closed.
