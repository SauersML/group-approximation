Deroin and Hurtado, *Non left-orderability of lattices in higher rank
semi-simple Lie groups*, arXiv:2008.10687v1 (24 Aug 2020), p. 3, read from the
PDF:

> **Theorem 1.3.** Let Γ be an irreducible lattice in a connected real
> semi-simple Lie group G with finite center and with real rank at least two.
> Then, Γ is not left-orderable and moreover every Φ : Γ → Homeo+(R) is
> trivial. In particular, if G is a real algebraic semi-simple Lie group of
> rank two, it contains no left-orderable irreducible lattice.

Abstract (p. 1): "We prove that an irreducible lattice in a real semi-simple
Lie group of real rank at least two and finite center is not left-orderable."

Remark 1.4 (p. 3) attributes the statement as a conjecture to Ghys and to
Witte Morris, and credits Witte Morris with the case where `G` is simple and
`Gamma` has `Q`-rank at least two.

Bound: only v1 was read, not later arXiv versions or a journal version.  The claim
restates Theorem 1.3 and adds only the standard special case `SL_n(Z)`.
