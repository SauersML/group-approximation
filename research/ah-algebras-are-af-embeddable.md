Every separable approximately homogeneous C*-algebra, including a
nonunital AH algebra built from locally compact homogeneous blocks, admits
an injective *-homomorphism into a separable AF algebra.

**Established (literature).**  This standard AF-embedding theorem is recorded
explicitly in the introduction of Mikael Rordam, *A purely infinite
AH-algebra and an application to AF-embeddability*, Israel J. Math. 141
(2004), arXiv:math/0205292.  It is imported here rather than reproved.
