Let `H` be finitely generated with solvable word problem and `n >= 2`.

## Lemma (extension)

Let `H <= M` be groups and `n >= 1`. There is an injective homomorphism
`E : Aut_H(H * F_n) -> Aut_M(M * F_n)` with `E(α)` restricting to `α` on
`H * F_n`.

*Proof.* The subgroup of `M * F_n` generated by `H` and the basis
`x_1, ..., x_n` is the free product `H * F_n`, by the normal form theorem for
free products. For `α ∈ Aut_H(H * F_n)` let `E(α)` be the endomorphism of
`M * F_n` given by `E(α)(m) = m` for `m ∈ M` and `E(α)(x_i) = α(x_i)`. It exists
and fixes `M` pointwise by the universal property of the free product. On
`H * F_n` it agrees with `α` on the generators `H` (both identity) and `x_i`, so
it restricts to `α`.

For `α, β` the endomorphisms `E(α) E(β)` and `E(αβ)` agree on `M`, and on each
`x_i`: `E(α)(E(β)(x_i)) = E(α)(β(x_i)) = α(β(x_i))`, because `β(x_i)` lies in
`H * F_n`, where `E(α)` restricts to `α`. So `E` is multiplicative and
`E(id) = id`. Then `E(α^-1)` is a two-sided inverse of `E(α)`, so `E(α)` lies in
`Aut_M(M * F_n)`. `E` is injective because `E(α)` restricts to `α`. ∎

## (1) implies (2)

Suppose `H` embeds in a group admitting an action of type (A). By BFFHZ
Theorem C, (i) implies (iv) (`fp-simple-highly-transitive-groups-satisfy-pbh`),
`H` embeds in a finitely presented simple MIF group `M`; identify `H <= M`. By the
lemma, `Aut_H(H * F_n) <= Aut_M(M * F_n)`. Since `n >= 2`, BFFHZ Theorem E
(`relative-automorphism-of-fp-simple-mif-group-has-type-a-action`) gives
`Aut_M(M * F_n)` an action of type (A).

## (2) implies (1)

For `g ∈ H` let `τ_g` fix `H` pointwise, send `x_1` to `x_1 g`, and fix `x_j` for
`j >= 2`. Then `τ_g τ_h (x_1) = τ_g(x_1 h) = x_1 g h`, so `g -> τ_g` is a
homomorphism, `τ_g` is invertible with inverse `τ_(g^-1)`, and it is injective
because `τ_g(x_1) = x_1 g`. This is the embedding BFFHZ use in their proof of
Theorem C (arXiv:2503.21882v2, l.661--663). So `H` embeds in every group that
`Aut_H(H * F_n)` embeds in, in particular one with an action of type (A). ∎

## Corollary

Let `S` be finitely presented and simple. By
`simple-envelope-forces-solvable-word-problem`, `S` has solvable word problem,
and it is finitely generated. By Theorem C, (i) if and only if (iv), `S` embeds
in a finitely presented simple MIF group exactly when `S` satisfies (1), which by
the theorem holds exactly when (2) holds for `S`. ∎
