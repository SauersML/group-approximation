**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `P` be a group with finite symmetric generating set `S`, word
length `|.|` and left-invariant metric `d(g, h) = |g^(-1) h|`, so `B_n(g) = g B_n`. Let `A` be a
finite alphabet, `X ⊆ A^P` a subshift with `(g.x)(h) = x(g^(-1) h)`, and `k` a field. Put
`B_X = LC(X, k) ⋊ P`, generated by `e_a = χ[x(1) = a]` and `u_s` (`s ∈ S`). For `x, y ∈ X` put
`Δ(x, y) = {p ∈ P : x(p) ≠ y(p)}`. Call a set `Δ ⊆ P` *`m`-connected* if it cannot be split into
two nonempty parts at distance `> m`.

1. **Coarse connectivity.** Suppose `B_X` has a presentation on the generators `e_a, u_s` whose
   relators are linear combinations of monomials of length `<= n`. Then for all `x, y ∈ X` the set
   `Δ(x, y)` is `2n`-connected.
2. **No finite asymptotic pairs.** If `P` is infinite, `X` is minimal and `B_X` is finitely
   presented, then no two distinct points of `X` differ on a finite set.
3. **Boundary layers determine patterns.** Under the hypotheses of part 2, let `r` be a radius
   defining `X` as an SFT (`fp-crossed-products-force-sft-over-any-group`). For every finite
   `F ⊆ P`, put `∂F = {p ∈ F : p B_(2r) ⊄ F}`. Restriction `L_F(X) -> L_(∂F)(X)` is injective,
   so `|L_F(X)| <= |A|^|∂F|`. In particular:
   - if `P` is amenable, `|L_(F_j)(X)| = e^(o(|F_j|))` along every right Følner sequence, so `X`
     has zero topological entropy;
   - over `Z^d`, `log |L_(B_N)(X)| = O(N^(d-1))`;
   - if `P` is infinite virtually cyclic, `|L_K(X)|` is bounded independently of the finite set
     `K`, so `X` is finite. That contradicts minimality of an infinite `X`. So no infinite minimal subshift over such `P`, over
     any field, has a finitely presented crossed product.

**Why it matters.** For `decidable-group-algebras-have-fp-cantor-crossed-hosts`, a simple host is
minimal, so every host over every acting group is *boundary-deterministic*: its `F`-patterns are
read off from a layer of width `2r`.
- This kills every locally flexible construction: tile flips, free bits, and positive-entropy
  simulations of row content, over all amenable `P` at once.
- It sharpens the `Z^2` band of that node's Attempt 8. The upper edge moves from `e^(o(N^2))` to
  `e^(O(N))`, so the band is `e^(Θ(N))`.
- It explains why the known finitely presented examples look as they do:
  - boundary shifts of free groups have line-shaped difference sets;
  - Ledrappier's shift has no finitely supported difference, because `F_2[x^(±1), y^(±1)]` is a
    domain.

**Calibration.**
- Part 1 over `Z^2` is the wall certificate of `sft-crossed-product-fp-iff-quantum-rigid`, with
  explicit width `2n`.
- Part 3 recovers `P = Z` (`minimal-subshift-crossed-product-is-not-finitely-presented`, over any
  field) and the `Z^2` entropy kill for minimal shifts.
- The theorem says nothing quantitative over one-ended non-amenable `P`, where `|∂B_N|` is
  comparable to `|B_N|`.
