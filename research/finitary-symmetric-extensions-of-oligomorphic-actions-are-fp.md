## Statement

Let `G` be a finitely presented group acting on an infinite set `S` such that
1. `G` has finitely many orbits on injective 4-tuples of `S`, and
2. the setwise stabilizer `G_{{a,b}}` is finitely generated for every `a ≠ b` in `S`.

Then `FSym(S) ⋊ G`, with `G` acting by conjugation of supports, is finitely presented.
The action need not be faithful. If every nontrivial element of `G` moves infinitely
many points of `S`, then `FSym(S) ⋊ G` acts faithfully on `S` (an element `(π, g)` acting
trivially has `g = π^{-1}` finitary, so `g = 1` and `π = 1`) and contains `Alt_fin(S)`, so by
`fp-groups-with-a-locally-moving-simple-subgroup-are-isolated` it is isolated, and by
`fp-finitary-alternating-overgroups-have-type-a-actions` its action on `S`
is of type (A).

## Examples

- Houghton-type contrast: the regular action of an infinite `G` fails condition 1, which
  agrees with `lampshuffler-groups-are-not-fp2-over-q` and Genevois--Tessera.
- For `SV_G` acting on the proper clopen subsets of its cube (`G` of type (A)),
  conditions 1 and 2 hold. In particular `FSym(Ω) ⋊ V` over the proper clopens `Ω` of
  the Cantor set is a finitely presented isolated group. See
  `permutational-boone-higman-iff-finitary-alternating-hosts`.
