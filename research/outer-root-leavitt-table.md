title: Commuting outer roots retain the Leavitt table
---

The distant-root placement is exact: the packets `x₁₂(a tᵢ)` and
`x₃₄(sⱼ b)` commute, while the fixed middle root gives
`[x₁₂(a tᵢ), [x₂₃(1), x₃₄(sⱼ b)]] = x₁₄(δᵢⱼ a b)`. Removes the local Pauli
noncommutation from the three path contexts without losing the two
diagonal-one and two cross-zero identities. Stated source-first in
`Leavitt/OuterRootLeavittRouting.lean`.
