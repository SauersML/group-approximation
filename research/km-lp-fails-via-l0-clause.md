Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma, arXiv:2006.01874, Corollary E (source-verified
in research/artifacts/isw-ddjmo-cr-cohomology-sources-2026-08-21.md):
a countable property (T) group admitting an ergodic p.m.p. action with
nonvanishing second cohomology valued in `L^0(X,R)` has a full
C*-algebra without the lifting property.  Property (T) is supplied by
`simple-kazhdan-kac-moody-lattices-exist`; the open input supplies the
action and the class.
