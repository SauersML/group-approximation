**(A), step 1: `psi` induces a cp contraction of reduced crossed products.**
- Let `A ⊆ B(H)` be faithful. The regular representation acts on `l^2(G) ⊗ H`, and
  `x = sum_(g ∈ T) a_g u_g` (with `T` finite) has matrix entries
  `x_(h,k) = alpha_(h^-1)(a_(h k^-1)) ∈ A`.
- The reduced norm is the supremum over finite `F ⊆ G` of the norms of the compressions
  `[x_(h,k)]_(h,k ∈ F) ∈ M_F(A)`. Positivity is tested on the same compressions.
- Applying `psi` entrywise gives `psi(alpha_(h^-1)(a)) = beta_(h^-1)(psi(a))`, by
  equivariance. These are the entries of `sum psi(a_g) u_g`.
- `psi` is cp and contractive, hence completely contractive. So
  `||sum psi(a_g) u_g||_r <= ||x||_r`, and the map extends to a cp contraction
  `psi ⋊ G`. Since `(q ⋊ G)(psi ⋊ G) = id` on `C_c(G, A/I)`, it is `id` everywhere.

**(A), step 2.**
- Let `x ∈ ker(q ⋊ G)` and choose `x_n ∈ C_c(G, A)` with `x_n -> x`.
- Put `y_n = x_n - (psi ⋊ G)(q ⋊ G)(x_n)`. Its coefficients `a_g - psi(q(a_g))` lie in `I`,
  so `y_n ∈ C_c(G, I)`.
- `y_n -> x - (psi ⋊ G)(q ⋊ G)(x) = x`. The reduced crossed product of an invariant ideal is
  the closure of `C_c(G, I)` inside `A ⋊_r G`, so `x ∈ I ⋊_r G`.

**(B).**
- *Standard facts used, not re-read here:*
  - restriction and induction preserve weak containment (Fell);
  - `S'` is co-amenable in `Γ'` iff `1_(Γ') ≺ lambda_(Γ'/S')` (Eymard);
  - `sigma ⊗ lambda_(Γ'/S') ≅ Ind_(S')^(Γ') Res sigma`;
  - `lambda_Γ` restricted to `S` is a multiple of `lambda_S`.
- *Representations of the quotient.* Let `J` be the closed ideal generated by the
  `u_m - 1`. Representations of `C*_r(Γ)/J` are the `pi ≺ lambda_Γ` with `pi|_M = 1`, that
  is `pi = sigma ∘ q`.
- *They are tempered.*
  - `sigma ≺ sigma ⊗ lambda_(Γ'/S') ≅ Ind_(S')^(Γ')(sigma|_(S'))`.
  - Via `q|_S`, `sigma|_(S')` is `pi|_S ≺ lambda_Γ|_S ≅ ∞ · lambda_S`.
  - Hence `Ind(sigma|_(S')) ≺ Ind lambda_(S') = lambda_(Γ')`, and `sigma ≺ lambda_(Γ')`.
- *Conclusion.* For `y ∈ C[Γ]`, `||y + J|| = sup ||sigma(q y)|| <= ||lambda_(Γ')(q y)||`. The
  reverse inequality holds because `lambda_(Γ') ∘ q ≺ lambda_Γ`, as `M` is amenable. So
  `C*_r(Γ)/J -> C*_r(Γ')` is isometric on a dense subalgebra, and `ker = J`.

**(C).**
- *The ideal.* The closed ideal of `C(X)` generated by `xi ↦ xi(w) - 1`, `w ∈ W`, has zero
  set `W^perp = Z`, so it is `C_0(U)`. The ideal it generates in `C(X) ⋊_r G` is
  `C_0(U) ⋊_r G`.
- *Case 1.* `X = W^ × V'^` equivariantly and `Z = {1} × V'^`. The retraction
  `r(omega, zeta) = (1, zeta)` gives the unital equivariant `*`-homomorphism
  `psi = r^* : C(Z) -> C(X)` with `res ∘ psi = id`. Apply (A).
- *Case 2.* Let `G_0 = ker(G -> Aut(V/W))`, of finite index. `q` is injective on `G <= Γ`,
  and `q(G_0)` has index `|V/W| [G : G_0] < ∞` in `Γ'`, so it is co-amenable. Apply (B).
- *Case 3.* Apply (B) with `S = G`.

**(D) 1.**
- *Coordinates.* `X = mu_p^G`, `Z` = configurations constant on left cosets `gH`, and `G` acts
  by left translation. Let `e ∈ C(Z)`, `e(zeta) = zeta(1)`. For `h ∈ H` and `zeta ∈ Z`,
  `(h·e)(zeta) = zeta(h) = zeta(1)`, so `h·e = e`.
- *`psi(e)` is `H`-invariant.* Suppose `psi` is an equivariant cp splitting. Then
  `psi(e) = psi(h·e) = h·psi(e)`.
- *`H` acts mixingly on `X`.* For cylinder sets `C_1, C_2` on finite windows `F_1, F_2`,
  `hF_1 ∩ F_2 = ∅` for all but finitely many `h ∈ H`, so `hC_1 ∩ C_2 ≠ ∅`. So `H` is
  topologically transitive on the compact metrizable `X`, and has a dense orbit by Baire.
- *Contradiction.* `psi(e)` is continuous and `H`-invariant, hence constant. But
  `psi(e)|_Z = e` takes all `p` values on the constant configurations.

**(D) 2.**
- *Push the mean to finite subsets.* Suppose `m` is a `Γ'`-invariant mean on
  `Γ'/G ≅ A := F_p[G/H]`, where `Γ'` acts by `(b, g)·a = b + g·a`.
  - `[G : H] = ∞`, since `H` is amenable and `G` is not, so `A` is infinite. Translation
    invariance gives `m({0}) = 0`.
  - The support map `A -> P_fin(G/H)` is `G`-equivariant. It pushes `m` to a `G`-invariant
    mean on the nonempty finite subsets.
- *Stabilizers are amenable.* The stabilizer of `F` contains `∩_(gH ∈ F) g H g^-1` with finite
  index.
- *Lemma: a `G`-set `Y` with amenable stabilizers and an invariant mean `m` forces `G`
  amenable.*
  - In each orbit fix `y_0`, with stabilizer `S` and a left-invariant mean `m_S` on `S`.
  - Set `F_f(g y_0) = m_S(s ↦ f(g s))`. This is well defined by left invariance of `m_S`.
  - `F_(k·f)(y) = F_f(k^-1 y)`. So `f ↦ m(F_f)` is a left-invariant mean on `G`.
- This contradicts non-amenability of `G`.
