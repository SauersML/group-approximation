For every countably infinite input P and enumeration nu, the
shell envelope E_nu is full as a subgroup of Homeo(C): a
homeomorphism locally agreeing with E_nu already belongs to E_nu.

In particular, every nonempty clopen supported subgroup of E_nu
is a copy of E_nu. A clopen setwise stabilizer is E_nu times E_nu,
and finite clopen partition stabilizers are finite direct powers.
For finitely generated P these stabilizers are finitely generated
for every enumeration, without an arithmetic hypothesis.

Fullness does not imply finite presentation. The existing computable
free-near enumerations give full, finitely generated, decidable
shell groups that are not finitely presented. For perfect inputs
those same groups are simple.
