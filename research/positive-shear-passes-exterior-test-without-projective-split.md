On the free binary boundary module, let s_0,s_1 prepend their digits
and put a=[[1,s_0],[0,1]], b=[[1,s_1],[0,1]], and g=diag(a,b).
The operator P_0[g]f_0 is injective on the entire exterior-square
source, including arbitrary finite tensors in both character sectors.

Nevertheless L P_0[g]f_0=f_0 has no solution in the ambient group
algebra. A separate finite representation of the subgroup generated
by the chart and g sends the creators to zero, fixes the chart,
and has f_0 nonzero but P_0 zero on its exterior square.

Thus passing the complete natural exterior-square test does not
establish the group-ring split. This is an exact control, not a
Kaplansky counterexample.
