**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof, elementary, not reviewed; no priority claimed).

## Setting

Let `Γ` be a finitely generated group, `Γ = Γ_0 > Γ_1 > ...` a chain of finite-index
subgroups with trivial intersection, `X = lim Γ/Γ_n` the associated profinite `Γ`-space
with the translation action, and `k` a field. Let

    R = C(X, k) ⋊ Γ

be the algebraic crossed product (the Steinberg algebra of the transformation groupoid
`X ⋊ Γ`). Write `A_n = C(Γ/Γ_n, k) ⊆ C(X,k)` for the level-`n` subalgebra.

## Statement

1. **Level preservation.** Each `A_n` is `Γ`-invariant, because translation by `Γ`
   permutes the level-`n` cosets. So `R_n := A_n ⋊ Γ` is a subring of `R`, and
   `R = ⋃_n R_n`.
2. **Level rigidity.** Every finitely generated subring `S ⊆ R` with `Γ ⊆ S` satisfies
   `S ⊆ R_n` for some `n`. Indeed each of the finitely many generators is a finite sum
   `Σ f_γ u_γ` with `f_γ ∈ C(X,k)` locally constant, hence in some `A_m`; take `n` to be
   the largest such `m`.
3. **Unit class at one level.** The action of `Γ` on `Γ/Γ_n` is transitive, so
   `C(Γ/Γ_n, Z)_Γ ≅ Z`, generated by the class of `1`. For a free action, `R_n` is
   `Γ_n`-graded Morita equivalent to `M_[Γ:Γ_n](k[Γ_n])` and `K_0(R_n) ≅ K_0(k[Γ_n])`,
   in which `[1_{R_n}]` is `[Γ:Γ_n]` times the class of a minimal idempotent. In
   particular `[1]` is divisible in `K_0(R_n)` only by the divisors of `[Γ:Γ_n]`, never by
   every integer.
4. **Consequence for the gate.** No finitely generated subring of `R` containing `Γ`
   satisfies condition 3 or 4 of `fp-simple-resolvent-ring-with-divisible-unit-class`
   (`R ≅ M_n(R_n)` for every `n`, and `[1]` divisible by every integer). `R` itself
   satisfies them when the indices `[Γ:Γ_n]` involve infinitely many primes, since then
   `C(X,Z)_Γ = colim Z` along the index maps is `Z[1/P]` for that prime set; but `R` is not
   finitely generated, by item 2.

## Why this kills the perfect tiling spark

Spark S1 of `research/artifacts/gq-deep-adelic-1-k-budget.md` proposes replacing the
integer shift by a finitely generated perfect group `Γ` acting minimally and freely on a
Cantor set with divisible coinvariants, so that the symbols `{λ, u_γ}` vanish
(`γ ↦ {λ, u_γ}` is a homomorphism from a perfect group to an abelian group) while the
tiling still produces `Q`. In the profinite model — the only free minimal Cantor model
available for a residually finite `Γ`, and the one that makes the coinvariants divisible —
the proposal fails before the symbols matter:

- perfectness of `Γ` does kill the `{λ, u_γ}`, so the `K_2` half is genuinely bought;
- but divisibility of `[1]` needs infinitely many levels, and by item 2 finitely many
  ring generators reach only one level.

So the mechanism has the same defect as the designs bracketed in
`frame-designs-for-the-ring-gate-are-bracketed`, for a different reason: there a frame is
present and the unit class is bounded by the frame data; here translations preserve the
level filtration, and a frame is exactly what a finite presentation would need in order to
reach deeper levels. Any repair must add a non-invertible compression, which puts the
design back inside that bracket.

## Scope

- **Correction (same lane): the non-profinite case is partly decided on main.** Item 2
  fails for non-profinite actions: a Toeplitz subshift has an odometer factor with unbounded
  periods, its crossed product is finitely generated (`minimal-subshift-algebra-is-simple-lef-ring`,
  item 1), and `[1]` is then divisible by every period, because the translates of a
  level-`n` clopen are equivalent idempotents. But over `Z` such rings are LEF (same node,
  item 3), and a finitely presented simple LEF ring is finite
  (`lef-simple-rings-are-not-finitely-presented`). So over `Z`, periodic approximability
  replaces level preservation as the obstruction. What remains open is a free minimal Cantor
  action of a finitely generated perfect group whose crossed product is not LEF and has
  divisible unit class. Non-LEF-ness forces a genuinely non-finite mechanism (a Leavitt-type
  compression is the known one), which leads back to `frame-designs-for-the-ring-gate-are-bracketed`.
- Items 1-2 need no freeness, minimality or perfectness. Item 3 uses freeness.
- `k` is a field only to make `K_0(k)` free on `[k]`; any ring with `K_0` finitely
  generated gives the same conclusion in item 3.
