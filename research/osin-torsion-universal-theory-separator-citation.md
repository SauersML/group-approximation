Literature import, not a derivation.

Source: D. Osin, *On the universal theory of torsion and lacunary hyperbolic
groups*, arXiv:0903.3978 (March 2009); Groups Complexity Cryptology **1**
(2009), no. 2, 311--319.

Imported content: the universal theory of the class of torsion groups is
properly contained in the universal theory of the class of finite groups.
Since every finite group is torsion, the inclusion is automatic and the
content is properness: some universal sentence holds in all finite groups and
fails in a torsion group. The paper also derives undecidability of the
universal theories of several natural classes and constructs a lacunary
hyperbolic group with undecidable universal theory; only the separation
statement is imported here.

The trust caveats -- that the explicit `(OS1)`/`(OS2)` shape was not
re-derived from the paper in this lane, and that a general universal sentence
yields a disjunctive rather than single-conclusion implication -- are
recorded in the claim body and travel with it.
