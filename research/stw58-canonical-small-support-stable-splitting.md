Let B be nonzero simple unital finite pure with nonempty normalized
quasitrace space, and let k>=1. Define I_k(B) to consist of classes
in pi_k(U(B),1) admitting, for every epsilon>0, a based representative
u with sup_(x,tau) d_tau(|u(x)-1|)<epsilon.

Then I_k(B) is a subgroup and canonical stabilization restricts to
an isomorphism I_k(B) -> K_(k+1)(B). Its inverse is a canonical
group section, natural for *-homomorphisms using supported unitary
maps, and compatible with all matrix corner inclusions. Consequently
pi_k U(B) is canonically K_(k+1)(B) direct sum ker(stabilization).

The infimum of uniform support size over representatives of a class
is either zero or at least 1/(k+1); it is zero exactly on I_k(B).
In particular a globally factored based family whose factors each
have support size below that threshold belongs to the stable summand.

No continuous section of unitary spaces or vanishing of the unstable
kernel is proved. The remaining localization assertion I_k(B)=pi_k U(B)
is precisely the injectivity question in this slice of LVIII.
