**Proposed ESTABLISHED** through `thickenable-z-pair-split-quotient-meridian-proof`. Unreviewed.

F = F(x, y, z). A Z-pair (r_1, r_2) and the moves (P1), (P2) are as in
`one-stabilization-ac-trivial-from-primitive-relator`. "Thickenable" means that the
presentation complex embeds in an orientable 3-manifold. A word is *separable* if, for
some basis of F, it lies in the free factor generated by two of the basis elements.

## Theorem A′ (meridian lemma for Z-pairs)

Let (r_1, r_2) be a thickenable Z-pair, let {i, j} = {1, 2}, and put G_j := F/⟨⟨r_i⟩⟩.
Then the following are equivalent:
1. G_j is freely decomposable;
2. G_j ≅ π_1(S³ − K) ∗ Z for some knot K ⊂ S³, possibly the unknot, and the class of
   r_j^{±1} is conjugate in G_j to a meridian of K lying in the free factor π_1(S³ − K).

If r_i is separable, (1) holds, since then G_j = ⟨two basis elements | r_i⟩ ∗ Z and the first
factor has H_1 of rank at least 1. By Shenitzer's theorem (Proc. AMS 6 (1955) 273–279),
(1) holds exactly when r_i is separable.

## Theorem A′ is false without the hypothesis

The unconditional statement "each relator is a meridian of a knot-group free factor modulo
the other" is false. Two thickenable Z-pairs witness this:

| pair | Z-pair | thickenable | r_2 separable |
|---|---|---|---|
| (x^{−1}y^{−1}, x^{−4}y^{−3}z^{−3}) | y = x^{−1}, then x = z^{−3} | yes | no: Whitehead graph is a 6-cycle; Aut-minimal set of 9 words, all use x, y, z |
| (x^{−1}, x^{−4}y^{−1}x^{−1}z^{−1}x^{−1}zy^{−1}z^{−1}) | x = 1, then y^{−2}z^{−1} = 1 | yes | no: Aut-minimal length 7, minimal set of 160 words, all use x, y, z |

In the tools' notation (capital letter = inverse) the pairs are (`XY`, `XXXXYYYZZZ`) and
(`X`, `XXXXYXZXzYZ`).
Thickenability was checked with `thick2.c` and, independently, with the brute-force rotation
system search `neuwirth_bruteforce.py`. Separability was checked with
`whitehead_minimal_set.py`. Outputs are in `split-quotient-counterexamples.log`.

In each row G_1 = F/⟨⟨r_2⟩⟩ is freely indecomposable by Shenitzer, so it is not a knot group
∗ Z, and conclusion (2) fails for i = 2. In the thickening, E = H ∪ h_2 then has
incompressible genus-2 boundary, while E[μ] is a solid torus. This is exactly the situation
excluded by the hypothesis "∂M compressible" in Jaco's handle addition lemma.

**This is a new class of certificates.** Theorem A′ says nothing about thickenable Z-pairs
in which neither relator is separable. Certificates of this type for
`one-stabilization-ac-trivial-from-thickenable-z-pair` are not touched by Corollary C′.

## Corollary B′ (torus relators)

Let p, q ≥ 2 be coprime and suppose r_i = x^p y^{−q}, so G_j = T(p,q) ∗ ⟨z⟩, where
T = T(p,q) = ⟨x, y | x^p = y^q⟩. Put Q = G_j/⟨⟨x^p⟩⟩ = Z/p ∗ Z/q ∗ Z. Then the image of
r_j in Q is conjugate to an element of Z/p ∗ Z/q of cyclic syllable length 2.

The same holds for any r_i with F/⟨⟨r_i⟩⟩ ≅ T(p,q) ∗ Z, which is freely decomposable. In that
case Q is the quotient by the centre of the unique conjugacy class of T(p,q) free factors,
and its image in Z/p ∗ Z/q is taken up to automorphisms, which preserve the length.

## Corollary C′ (one-relator routes from AK(n) are dead, with Aut(F_3))

Start from the AK(n) pair s = (x^n y^{−(n+1)}, xyxy^{−1}x^{−1}y^{−1}) in F_3, n ≥ 2. Consider
any finite sequence of moves (P1), (P2) in which one fixed relator is changed only by
conjugation and inversion, while the other is arbitrary. No such sequence ends at a
thickenable Z-pair.

Both s_1 and s_2 lie in ⟨x, y⟩, so the fixed relator stays separable and Theorem A′ applies.
- **Second-relator routes.** In Z/n ∗ Z/(n+1), xyxy^{−1}x^{−1}y^{−1} has cyclic syllable
  length 6.
- **First-relator routes.** F/⟨⟨xyxy^{−1}x^{−1}y^{−1}⟩⟩ = B_3 ∗ ⟨z⟩ = T(2,3) ∗ ⟨z⟩, with
  u = xy, v = xyx and u³ = v². In Z/3 ∗ Z/2 = ⟨ū⟩ ∗ ⟨v̄⟩ the image of x^n y^{−(n+1)} is
  (ū²v̄)^n (ūv̄)^{n+1}, of cyclic syllable length 2(2n+1) ≥ 10.

The first-relator case also holds in rank 2. It follows from Theorem A and Corollary B of
`thickenable-balanced-presentations-have-meridional-relators` by the same computation.
That node only records the second-relator case.

**Meaning for AK(3) and Lackenby–Guo.** Any certificate for
`one-stabilization-ac-trivial-from-thickenable-z-pair`, and in rank 2 any thickenable
presentation in the AC orbit, has the form α(r_1, r_2) with α ∈ Aut(F_3) and neither of the
following:
- r_1 a conjugate of s_1^{±1}, and r_2 representing a conjugate of s_2^{±1} modulo
  ⟨⟨s_1⟩⟩;
- the same with the roles of the relators exchanged.

So a certificate route must, after its automorphisms are moved to the end, change both
relators beyond conjugation. Keeping either the torus relator or the braid relator is
ruled out, and the extra generator and Aut(F_3) do not rescue a one-relator route. Routes
that change both relators are not touched.

**What this does not rule out.** An endpoint whose relators are both non-separable, such
as the pairs in the table, gives no meridian condition at all. There is also no sound
Whitehead-graph filter for the thickenable goal: requiring a cut vertex in both relators'
Whitehead graphs would discard both counterexamples.
