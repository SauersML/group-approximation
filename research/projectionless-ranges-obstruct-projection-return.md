title: Nontrivial projectionless ranges obstruct minimal-projection return
---

Let `C subset B` be a nontrivial unital C-star subalgebra whose only
projections are `0` and `1`.  Then there are a finite set `F subset C` and
`epsilon>0` for which no unital finite-dimensional subalgebra

```text
D=direct_sum_j M_(n_j) subset B
```

can simultaneously satisfy:

1. every element of `F` is within `epsilon` of `D`;
2. for some system of matrix units of `D`, every `e^(j)_(11)` is within
   `1/16` of a positive element of `C`.

Thus the projection-return hypothesis is impossible for every nontrivial
unital projectionless range, independently of proper infiniteness.
