**ESTABLISHED** through `fpbs-mal-sibling-bns-kernels-have-finite-cost-proof`.

Let `M = <b, t | r> ≅ F_3 ⋊ Z` and `K = [M, M]` be as in
`fpbs-mal-overgroup-is-free-by-cyclic`. Besides `Gamma_mal = ker (0,1)`,
consider:
- `N_1 = ker (1,0) = K ⋊ <t>`, generated by `y_i = b^i t b^-i`;
- `N_2 = ker (1,2) = K ⋊ <t b^-2>`, generated by `z_i = b^i t b^-2 b^-i`.

**Theorem.**
1. **Ascending stages.** `N_1 = ∪_j P_j` with
   `P_j = <y_{-j-1}, y_{-j}, y_{-j+1}>`, and `N_2 = ∪_j Q_j` with
   `Q_j = <z_j, z_{j+1}, z_{j+2}>`. Both unions are increasing, and
   `P_{j+1} = <P_j, x_j>` and `Q_{j+1} = <Q_j, x'_j>`, with
   `x_j = y_{-j-2}` and `x'_j = z_{j+3}`.
2. **Commensurated steps.** There are `g_j ∈ P_j ∖ {1}` with
   `x_j g_j x_j^-1 ∈ P_j`, and there are `g'_j = x'_j^2 ∈ Q_j ∖ {1}`.
3. **Zero stage cost.** For every free p.m.p. action of `N_1` (resp. `N_2`)
   and every `ε > 0`, there is `A` with `μ(A) < ε` and
   `R_{P_j} ∨ x_j|_A = R_{P_{j+1}}` (resp. with `Q`, `x'`).
   So `relC(R_{P_{j+1}}; R_{P_j}) = 0` on every free action.
4. **Cost bound.** Every free p.m.p. action of `N_1` or `N_2` has cost at
   most `3`.

**Class obstruction.** List the data that a floor proof for Gamma_mal might
rest on:
- (a) being `K ⋊ Z` over the common free kernel `K`, normalised by one
  element;
- (b) being the kernel of a character on a boundary line of the non-fibred
  set, whose Alexander width is finite;
- (c) being an increasing union of finitely generated free stages, each
  obtained from the previous one by adding one generator.

`N_1` and `N_2` have (a) and (c), and (b) holds by the Remark in
`fpbs-mal-overgroup-is-free-by-cyclic`, which is cited. Yet their stage
relative cost is `0` on every free action. So no argument that uses only
(a) to (c), their fibre-rank and Alexander numerics, or the pattern "`R_K` ∨
one normalising transformation" can prove a positive floor.

What separates Gamma_mal is the step type. In `N_i` each new generator
commensurates the stage, `P_j ∩ x^-1 P_j x ≠ 1`. In Gamma_mal each stage pair
`H_n < H_{n+1}` is malnormal. Malnormality is transitive along the chain, so
`H_n ∩ x^-1 H_n x = 1` for every `x ∈ Gamma_mal ∖ H_n`, and Gaboriau's
one-element step never enlarges a stage. A floor proof must therefore use
malnormality of the stage pair, or something strictly finer.

**Not claimed.** Fixed price `3` for `N_i`. That would need
`β_1^(2)(N_i) = 2`, which is not proved here.
