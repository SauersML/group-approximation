For every infinite countable group `G` and every free Borel action `G ↷ X`, there is an equivariant
class-bijective Borel map from `X` to the free part `Free(2^G)` of the 2-shift.

**Source.** B. Seward and R. D. Tucker-Drob, *Borel structurability on the 2-shift of a countable
group*, arXiv:1402.4184, Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 167 (2016) 1–21. Abstract, read from arXiv on
2026-09-12, verbatim: "We show that for any infinite countable group G and for any free Borel action
G ↷ X there exists an equivariant class-bijective Borel map from X to the free part Free(2^G) of
the 2-shift G ↷ 2^G."

Trust surface: the statement is read from the abstract, and the proof was not re-derived here.
