Citation import, not a reproof. T. Haettel, D. Kielak, P. Schwer, "The 6-strand
braid group is CAT(0)", arXiv:1304.5990v2 (5 Jan 2016), Geom. Dedicata 182
(2016) 263. Read from the arXiv PDF text on 2026-09-13:

- Theorem 4.17 (introduction): "For every n ≤ 6 the diagonal link in the
  orthoscheme complex of non-crossing partitions NCP_n is CAT(1)."
- Corollary 4.18: "For every n ≤ 6, the n-strand braid group is CAT(0)."
- Their Definition 2.29: a group is CAT(0) "if and only if it acts properly
  discontinuously and cocompactly by isometries on a CAT(0) space".

The deduction of Corollary 4.18 uses Brady–McCammond, "Braids, posets and
orthoschemes", Algebr. Geom. Topol. 10 (2010) 2277–2314, arXiv:0909.4778, as
quoted by HKS: Theorem 2.34 (= [BM, Theorem 5.10 and Lemma 5.8]: if for all
`3 <= k <= n` the diagonal link of `|NCP_k|` has no unshrinkable short loop,
then `|NCP_n|` is CAT(0)) and Proposition 2.35 (= [BM, Proposition 8.3]: if
`|NCP_m|` is CAT(0) for all `m <= n`, then `B_n` is CAT(0)). HKS say their
proof uses no computer calculation. Brady–McCammond had already proved
`n = 4, 5`, the case `n = 5` with computer assistance.

This route asserts only that the cited results say what is recorded here.
