Setting `c=1` kills the packet and leaves the marked `E=C_2^3`, so
`Q=N semidirect E` with `N=<<c>>_Q` generated by `c_x=b_x c b_x^-1`.
The required exact certificate implies that every map from `Q` to a
finite group kills `c`, and hence `N`. A map to a finite permutation
wreath product retaining `c` therefore gives, on some base coordinate,
a homomorphism `N->V` retaining `c`.

For such a map put `z_x=psi(c_x)`. Rewriting each conjugate of each
packet word gives the complete coordinate system in the artifact.
Conversely, any solution defines a map into `V`: on the eight
three-digit prefix cells let `b_i` flip digit `i` and let `c` apply
`z_x` on cell `x`. The group-law calculation and the explicitly
verified mixed coordinates prove all defining relations. Thus the
finite-wreath witness can always be normalized to this map into `V`.

Finally `d_x=1` forces `A_x=1`, and the square and cube relations
force `z_x=1`. Invariance under either of the first two bit flips
makes every displayed `d_x` equal to one, proving the stated
limited exclusion. No existence argument for the remaining system
is supplied or used.
