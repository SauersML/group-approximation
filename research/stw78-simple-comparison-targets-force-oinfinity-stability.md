Let `A` be a separable exact C*-algebra and let `B` be a nonzero unital
simple C*-algebra such that `W(B)` is almost unperforated and `B` has no
normalized `2`-quasitrace.  Then every nuclear star homomorphism

```text
phi:A->B
```

is strongly `O_infinity`-stable.  In particular, every unital homomorphism
from a unital simple separable nuclear C*-algebra into `B` is
`O_infinity`-stable.

Consequently STW Problem LXXVIII has a positive answer whenever the target
is simple with almost-unperforated Cuntz semigroup.  This includes every
simple `Z`-stable target, without assuming nuclearity or exactness of the
target.
