The equivalence with finite presentation of `V_d(G)`, and so the explicit hosts in consequence 2, rest on Theorem A of `rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation`, which is itself not yet refereed; the Theorem as stated (hypothesis on `N`) uses only the sufficiency half of Theorem A.

## Setting

As in `rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation`: `X = {0,…,d-1}`, `d >= 2`; `G <= Aut(X^*)` is
generated by a finite set `S = S_0 ∪ S_0^{-1}` closed under sections, where `F = F(S_0)` is free on `S_0`
and inverses are formal; `N = ker(F -> G)` is the relator kernel; lift ideals and `K_∞` are as there.

## Statement

**Theorem.** If `N = ⟨⟨R⟩⟩_lift` for a finite `R ⊆ N` (equivalently, by Theorem A of
`rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation`, `V_d(G)` is finitely presented), then `G` embeds in
a finitely presented simple group. Explicitly: for every even `m` such that `1/m` is not an eigenvalue of
Zaremsky's matrix `A` (all but finitely many even `m`), with `V = V_{md}(G)` for the regrouped action of `G`
on `T_{md}` and `e` the exponent of the finite group `V^ab`, the map
`δ_e(g) = L_{v_1}(g) ··· L_{v_e}(g)` (cone copies on `e` pairwise disjoint cones) embeds `G` in the simple
group `[V,V]`, which has finite index in `V` and is finitely presented.

## Consequences

1. **Recovers the finite-state cases of the known theorems.** `G` finitely presented (Scott FP1) or
   contracting (Nekrashevych FP2) satisfies the hypothesis, by consequence 1 of Theorem A.
2. **New hosts for non-fp, non-contracting automaton groups.** The lamplighter automaton group `Z/2 ≀ Z`
   (`rover-nekrashevych-group-of-lamplighter-automaton-is-fp`, `N = K_∞`) and the affine lamplighters and
   `G^+ = AGL_1(F_q[t^{±1}])` of `rn-groups-of-affine-lamplighters-f-q-wr-z-are-fp` get explicit f.p. simple
   commutator hosts `[V_{md}(G),V_{md}(G)]`. Boone–Higman for these particular groups was already known by
   other means (`lamplighters-in-v-show-near-action-neatness-is-no-bh-obstruction`,
   `char-p-linear-groups-satisfy-permutational-boone-higman`); what is new here is the self-similar host.
3. **Automaton groups freely generated by their states.** Automata that freely generate free groups
   (Aleshin, free of rank 3: Vorobets–Vorobets math/0601231; the bireversible `2n`-state automata of
   Steinberg–Vorobets–Vorobets math/0610033) satisfy the hypothesis with `N = K_∞ = 1`. This is a special
   case of consequence 1, since free groups of finite rank are finitely presented.

## Scope

- Automaton groups are residually finite, so this is a residually finite route only.
- The hypothesis depends on the action. Skipper–Witzel–Zaremsky (arXiv:1712.05361, Thm 7.1 with `n = 2`,
  finite-state by its proof; Ex. 7.2) give finite-state actions whose `V_d` is not finitely presented, and by
  `persistent-automata-rn-group-fp-iff-group-fp` persistent actions satisfy it only for finitely presented
  groups. The group may still be covered through another self-similar action: Grigorchuk's group is, on `T_2`
  (SWZ Ex. 7.2, citing Belk–Matucci). No automaton group is known that fails the hypothesis for every
  finite-state action; see `automaton-groups-bh-via-lift-presented-overgroups`, whose overgroup may be the
  same group with another action.
