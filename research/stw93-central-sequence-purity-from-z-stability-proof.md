The algebra `A` is separable and 𝒵-stable by construction.  Theorem 3.6 of
Perera--Thiel--Vilalta, arXiv:2512.17261, states for every separable C*-algebra
`E` that 𝒵-stability is equivalent to pureness of `E_ω∩E'` (and of
Kirchberg's corrected central sequence algebra).  Applying it to `A` proves the
claim.  The theorem has no nuclearity hypothesis.
