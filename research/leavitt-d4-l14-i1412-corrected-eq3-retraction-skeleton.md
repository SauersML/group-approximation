For the corrected 29-syllable third carrier equation of inverse class
`1;412`, retraction to the four coefficient copies gives exactly four
coefficient relators.  With the generator numbering

```text
1=g1, 2=g2, 3=g4, ..., 12=g13,
```

they are

```text
R3 = g10^-1 g11 g10^-1 g12,

R2 = g9^-1 g7^-1 g5^-1 g1^-1 g13^-1 g10
     g9^-1 g7^-1 g5^-1 g1^-1 g13^-1 g11,

R1 = g8^-1 g6^-1 g4^-1 g2^-1 g12^-1 g5 g7 g9
     g8^-1 g6^-1 g4^-1 g2^-1 g12^-1 g10,

R0 = g11^-1 g4 g6 g8 g11^-1 g9.
```

In particular the bare copy retractions cannot fence this carrier: four
relators on twelve generators have abelianization of rank at least eight.
The next valid step is the corrected color-compatible noncrossing scheme
output and its Tietze certificates.  Those outputs are not stored in the
repository at the present checkpoint; the earlier output from the
mislabelled generator map is not a certificate for this corrected word.
