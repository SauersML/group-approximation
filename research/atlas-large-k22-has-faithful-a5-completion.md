Write the two `S3` vertices of the large rank-three component as

```text
<r,u> ~= S3,          <y,b> ~= S3,
```

where `r,y` have order three, `u,b` are involutions, and each involution
inverts its adjacent order-three generator.  The four distinct core
pair-cubes are

```text
(r^-1 b)^3=(u y)^3=((u r)y^-1)^3=(r b y)^3=1.          (A4-K22-1)
```

This entire local presentation has a faithful finite completion in `A5`.
On five points take the one-based images

```text
r=[1,2,4,5,3],          y=[2,3,1,4,5],
u=[2,1,4,3,5],          b=[3,2,1,5,4].                 (A4-K22-2)
```

Both vertex images have order six, and each of the four mixed
order-three/involution pairs has order twelve, so all `S3` vertices and all
`A4` edges inject.  The generated group has order sixty and consists of even
permutations, hence is exactly `A5`.  Moreover

```text
<r,y,b>=A5,             u=r^-1 b y^-1.                 (A4-K22-3)
```

Thus the fourth edge is not a local obstruction.  The exact
regular-representation multiplicities also match at atlas scale one:

```text
336 Reg(A5)|_S3 = 3360 Reg(S3),
336 Reg(A5)|_A4 = 1680 Reg(A4).
```

The remaining global seam is compatibility with collision and the forward
fan.  In view of `(A4-K22-3)`, gluing this local carrier identifies the marked
`A5=<r,y,b>` with the subgroup generated by the first `C3` and the second
`S3` in a forward-collision carrier.  That cyclic gluing, not the isolated
`K2,2`, is the next possible obstruction.

As bounded calibration, the index-at-most-twelve low-index search finds
twelve faithful local quotients: images of orders `60`, `168`, and `1920`.
This census is not used to claim a classification of the universal group.
