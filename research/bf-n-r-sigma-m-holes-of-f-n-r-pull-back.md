For `n >= 2`, `r >= 1`, `m >= 1` and `psi` in `Hom(F_{n,r}, R)`: if
`[psi o pi]` lies in `Sigma^m(bF_{n,r})` then `[psi]` lies in
`Sigma^m(F_{n,r})`. For `m >= 2` and every `r`, `[(a chi_0 + b chi_1) o pi]`
does not lie in `Sigma^m(bF_{n,r})` for `a, b >= 0` not both zero, where
`chi_0, chi_1` are the first- and last-leaf depth characters of
arXiv:1502.02620.

Along the way: `F_{n,r}` is isomorphic to `F_{n,1}` by conjugation with a
self-similar PL homeomorphism `[0,1] -> [0,r]` that preserves the endpoint
slopes.
