title: "Holes of Sigma^m(F_{n,r}) pull back to holes of Sigma^m(bF_{n,r}); for r ≡ 1 mod (n-1) the endpoint hull is a hole"
---

For `n >= 2`, `r >= 1`, `m >= 1` and `psi` in `Hom(F_{n,r}, R)`: if
`[psi o pi]` lies in `Sigma^m(bF_{n,r})` then `[psi]` lies in
`Sigma^m(F_{n,r})`. If moreover `r ≡ 1 mod (n - 1)` and `m >= 2`, then
`[(a chi_0 + b chi_1) o pi]` does not lie in `Sigma^m(bF_{n,r})` for `a, b >= 0`
not both zero, where `chi_0, chi_1` are the first- and last-leaf depth
characters of arXiv:1502.02620.
