OPEN.  Construct a family of perfect-complete two-player tests `L_N` with a
finite set of local relation/answer templates independent of `N` such that:

1. value above `1-eta` for one fixed `eta>0` extracts, on the original local
   Hilbert spaces, a Pauli factor of dimension `D_N->infinity`;
2. within each basis, consistency is decoded by an algebraic agreement or
   local-testable-code theorem, not by putting all copies of one mixed edge
   in a connected equality fiber;
3. every question has bounded incidence in the compiled constraint
   hypergraph, or every unbounded-answer packet has a recursive finite
   local-circuit presentation with a uniform normalized-HS soundness
   modulus;
4. after pure X and pure Z agreement contours are contracted to their
   factors, every essential mixed contour either has relative length at
   least eight with no repeated two-syllable piece, or satisfies `(BG2)` of
   `balanced-greendlinger-separates-sign-cosets`;
5. the perfect strategy is compatible with the exact coset-Clifford model,
   so the central mark remains nontrivial.

The closest published analytic input is the Natarajan--Vidick quantum
low-degree test.  The precise missing sparsification can be expressed as a
**twisted-commutation agreement sampler**: replace the complete distribution
on X/Z point pairs by a bounded-incidence algebraic sampler while proving
that, conditional on passing the two same-basis agreement tests, small sampled
twisted-commutator energy implies small uniform twisted-commutator energy.
The implication must be operator-valued and entanglement-sound; ordinary
scalar expander mixing is not enough.

The exact mixed sampler is now completely rigid.
`two-untwisted-field-matchings-force-complete-cross-commutation` proves that,
after exact same-basis linearity, the two algebraic matchings `a=b` and
`a=alpha b` force every untwisted cross commutator in an arbitrary group;
canonical-tensor untwisting then forces the complete Weyl table at arbitrary
spectral multiplicity.  Its remaining task is precisely quantitative: obtain
a normalized-HS soundness modulus independent of `n`.  The current direct
ratio-circuit propagation has loss `O(n^(1+log_2 3))`.

Once this claim is proved, attach a relative graphical-small-cancellation
label to its bounded incidence complex and apply
`relative-c-sixth-incidence-closes-placement`.  The same-Hilbert dimension
bound then supplies the terminal contradiction without any equality-fiber
two-edge contour.

## Attempts

- **Use the published quantum low-degree test unchanged.**  Its complete
  X-point/Z-point distribution retains the threshold rectangles and its
  plane answers have growing alphabet.
- **Fix the field.**  The `d/q` term and tensor-code distance lose uniform
  robustness, as proved in
  `fixed-field-plane-point-loses-uniform-low-degree-robustness`.
- **Degree-reduce by equality clouds.**  This preserves same-Hilbert game
  soundness but creates the fatal two-pair contours proved in
  `equality-synchronized-lifts-create-two-pair-contours`.
- **Use an ordinary scalar expander sampler for the mixed pairs.**  No
  operator-valued theorem currently promotes sampled twisted commutators to
  the uniform Weyl relations after entangled agreement decoding; this is the
  explicitly deferred attack in the body of the claim.
- **Restrict to one linear matching.**  The undetected errors are exactly
  alternating bilinear forms.  A second field-generator matching kills all
  of them, but does not by itself rule out nonlinear character-fiber actions.
