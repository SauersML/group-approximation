---

Apply `permutation-valued-ternary-relative-distribution` to the whole unitary
table. By its conclusion the compatible triple law decodes to one common latent
family of multiplicative bistochastic kernels (or partial bijections completed
to such kernels), with the cubic/table and regular-trace errors controlled.
These are exactly the matrices required by `regular-character-markovization`.
