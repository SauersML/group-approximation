The imported theorem and its proof quantify only combinatorial properties of
an exact closed picture witnessing `J^k=1`.  No step starts from an
approximately multiplicative matrix map or outputs a lower bound on its
normalized-HS relator energy.  The paper's explicit statement that
finite-dimensional operator solutions for its odd-prime examples remain open
confirms that the exact theorem does not settle the approximation question.
