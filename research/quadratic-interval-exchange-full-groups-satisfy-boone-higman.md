## Statement

Let `T` be a minimal right-continuous interval exchange of `[0,1)`: a bijection that translates finitely many
intervals. Assume that all its discontinuity points and translation amounts lie in a real quadratic field `K`.
Let `[[T]]` be the group of right-continuous bijections of `[0,1)` that agree with a power of `T` on each piece
of a finite partition into intervals whose endpoints lie in the `T`-orbits of the discontinuities. Then:

- every subgroup of `[[T]]` embeds in a finitely presented simple group;
- in particular this holds for `[[T]]'`.

When the minimal interval exchange codes a minimal subshift, `[[T]]` is its topological full group. Then
`[[T]]'` is an infinite, finitely generated, simple group (Matui).

## Proof

1. **The translation group.** Let `Γ_T ⊆ K` be the subgroup generated by `1`, the discontinuities and the
   translations. It is finitely generated, of rank at most 2, since `dim_Q K = 2`.
   - `T` maps `Γ_T` to itself, so every element of `[[T]]` is an interval exchange with breakpoints and
     translations in `Γ_T`.
   - So `[[T]] ≤ IE(Γ_T)`.
2. **A quadratic Stein host.** Let `λ ∈ (0,1)` be a unit of infinite order of `K`, replaced by `±λ^{±1}` if
   needed. `Z[λ]` spans `K`, so `NΓ_T ⊆ Z[λ]` for some integer `N ≥ 1`.
   - Rescaling by `N` embeds `IE(Γ_T)` in `V(Z[λ],⟨λ⟩,N)`.
   - Every subgroup of that group embeds in a finitely presented simple group by
     `stein-v-groups-act-with-type-a-on-breakpoints`.
   ∎

## Remarks

- **Amenability.** `Γ_T` has rank at most 2. Juschenko--Matte Bon--Monod--de la Salle prove interval exchange
  groups with translation group of rank at most 2 amenable (arXiv:1503.04977, as recorded on main). So these
  `[[T]]'` are amenable, finitely generated, infinite and simple, and none is finitely presented
  (`interval-exchange-groups-are-lef`).
- **Examples.** These include minimal rotations by quadratic angles, the Sturmian case, and all minimal
  `k`-interval exchanges with lengths in `Q(√d)`, such as the self-similar exchanges with quadratic stretch
  factors.
