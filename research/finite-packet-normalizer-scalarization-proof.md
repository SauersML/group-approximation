# Factor normalizers by Schur and compare simple tensors

After choosing one intertwiner for each induced packet automorphism,
`C_alpha^*U` commutes with the irreducible packet and hence equals `I tensor
Q`. Multiplying such factorizations gives `(NS2)`. Equality of a nonzero
simple tensor with the identity forces both tensor factors to be reciprocal
scalars, yielding `(NS3)`.
