gives an infinite hyperbolic group w.o.p. The asphericity ("we prove that the
presentation is aspherical ... our asphericity implies asphericity of the
Cayley complex and thus cohomological dimension at most 2") comes from the same
proof in Section 2: the isoperimetric inequality `|∂D| > αℓ|D|` holds for every
reduced diagram, so no reduced spherical diagram exists. Read from the PDF text
