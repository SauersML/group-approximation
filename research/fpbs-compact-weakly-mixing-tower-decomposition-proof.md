Artifact Section 2. Let pi: Y -> X be a free factor with C(X) < infinity.

1. Tower. Set F_0 = B_X. If Y is relatively weakly mixing over Y_{F_alpha}, stop. Otherwise the imported dichotomy gives an intermediate invariant sigma-algebra F_{alpha+1} strictly containing F_alpha, with Y_{F_{alpha+1}} -> Y_{F_alpha} relatively compact. At a limit lambda, F_lambda is generated by the earlier stages. Each Y_{F_alpha} is a standard free action, because it factors onto the free X.
2. Countable length. For a dense sequence e_n in L²(Y), each alpha -> ||P_alpha e_n||² is nondecreasing and bounded, so it has countably many positive jumps. Every successor stage produces a jump for some n. So there are countably many successors, and the recursion stops at a countable beta.
3. Induction. Successors use the compact hypothesis with finite base cost C(X). A limit lambda is countable. Along a cofinal sequence alpha_r, the sigma-algebras increase to F_lambda and contain B_X, with C(X) < infinity. So the increasing-factor continuity of `fpbs-traffic-variational-cost-identities` gives C(Y_{F_{alpha_r}}) decreasing to C(Y_{F_lambda}). All terms equal C(X). The top step Y -> Y_{F_beta} uses the weakly mixing hypothesis.
4. Converse. For finitely generated G every free action has finite cost, and fixed price makes all free factors cost-preserving.

The deduction uses no property of cost beyond monotonicity under factors, which is part of (10.1), and continuity. It is a supplied deduction, not external validation.
