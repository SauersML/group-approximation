**ESTABLISHED LITERAL FAITHFULNESS.**  In

```text
Delta=St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))
```

the literal subgroup generated by

```text
K=<L_0,r,l>, J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3,A_1                    (LAF1)
```

is isomorphic to the finite binary group `P_A` of
`full-q-a1-binary-extension-closes-zero-cross-block`.  In particular it has
order

```text
20478689280.                                           (LAF2)
```

This is not merely a quotient seen by restricting to eight projective
summands.  The seven active summands, one in each of the distinct ambient
coordinates `2,4,5,6,7,8,9`, carry a complete system of projective matrix
units.  Every root in `(LAF1)` is the image of the corresponding standard
binary root under one homomorphism

```text
Phi:St_7(F_2) -> St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2)).                  (LAF3)
```

The natural map `St_7(F_2)->SL_7(F_2)` is an isomorphism.  Composition of
`Phi` with the elementary projection acts faithfully on the direct sum of
the seven projective summands.  Hence `Phi` is injective, and its restriction
to `P_A<=SL_7(F_2)` identifies `(LAF1)` with `P_A` exactly.

Consequently every group-algebra identity proved in the finite binary head,
including full central support of `FJ_1J_2F` after adjoining `A_1`, is an
identity for the literal Steinberg words.  No central `K_2` phase from the
ambient Leavitt ring is available on this head: the lift is already defined
over `F_2`, whose finite-field Steinberg kernel is trivial.

No Property `(T)`, canonical trace, computation, or approximation theorem is
used.
