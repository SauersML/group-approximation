The preceding projection-source audit supplies weak contractibility
of proper projection embeddings. Real-rank-zero approximate units and
Rordam--Winter projection cancellation form a compatible tower of
finite projection frames. Its restriction bundles have weakly
contractible fibers, which proves weak contractibility for arbitrary
strict module embeddings.

A large spectral projection is fixed by a uniformly small target
unitary correction. The remaining strictly compared module embeddings
can move arbitrarily, but the weighted source tail bounds that motion
uniformly. This gives equi-local connectivity of precisely the strict
embedding family. Transported source projections and pointwise tail
embeddings give lower semicontinuity. Michael's theorem then extends
the prescribed singleton boundary values exactly.
