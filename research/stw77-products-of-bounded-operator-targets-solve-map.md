Let `A` be separable and unital, let `(H_lambda)` be an arbitrary family
of separable infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, and let

```text
phi:A->product_lambda B(H_lambda)
```

be a unital homomorphism. If every nonzero positive element of `phi(A)` is
properly infinite in the product, then `phi` is O-infinity-stable.

Neither exactness of `A` nor finite nuclear dimension of `phi` is needed.
The conclusion is uniform over an arbitrary, including uncountable, product.
