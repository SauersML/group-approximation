## Why sufficient

Let `S` be the finitely generated unital subring generated by
`v_0,v_1,w_0,w_1`, which satisfies the hypothesis with witness
`w_1(1-v_0w_0)v_1 = 1`.  The printed ring proof supplies inside `EL_4(S)`
a property-(T) subgroup `L = EL_3(S)` and a compressor `u` with
`uLu^(-1)` strictly contained in `L`, the strictness coming from
`e_12(1) in L \ uLu^(-1)`.

`elementary-rank-descent-to-two` gives an injective homomorphism
`Psi : EL_4(S) -> EL_2(R)`, and injectivity carries the configuration over:
`Psi(L)` has property (T) as an isomorphic image, and
`Psi(u)Psi(L)Psi(u)^(-1) = Psi(uLu^(-1))` is strictly contained in
`Psi(L)`.  For `n >= 2` compose with the standard block inclusion
`EL_2(R) <= EL_n(R)`, which is again injective.

Then `maximal-group-cstar-infinite-under-strict-compression` applies to
`Psi(L) <= EL_n(R)` and `Psi(u)`.
