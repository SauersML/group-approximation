Notation: `G = K_0(D)` is a countable simple dimension group, noncyclic because
`D` is non-elementary.  `E: A -> D` is the canonical faithful conditional
expectation and `u` the implementing unitary.  By Effros--Handelman--Shen,
`G` satisfies (R), (U), (O), (D) of
`coinvariant-tarski-lemma-simple-dimension-groups` for any order unit `x_0`,
with `Gamma = Z` acting through `alpha_*`.  Let `Sigma = G^+ / ~` be the
coinvariant monoid.  (GH): a preordered abelian monoid with order unit `v` has
a state `f` with `f(v) = 1` iff `(n+1)v <= nv` fails for all `n`
(Goodearl--Handelman; Tarski, as Theorem 4.11 of Rainone 2017).

**(P1) Compression into `D`.**  `A` is simple (Kishimoto; Olesen--Pedersen).
Let `a in A_+` be nonzero.  By Kishimoto's lemma (argument of Lemma 5.4 in
Rordam 2005) there are `h in D_+` and `lambda > 0` with
`||hah - hE(a)h|| < lambda < ||hE(a)h||`.  The hereditary subalgebra of `D`
generated by `b = (hE(a)h - lambda)_+ != 0` contains a nonzero projection `p`.
Rordam's Lemma 5.1 writes `b = s^* h a h s` and identifies `her_A(b)` with
`her(x^* x)`, isomorphic to `her(x x^*) subset her_A(a)` for
`x = a^(1/2) h s`.  The isomorphism is implemented by the polar decomposition
of `x`, so it carries `p` to an equivalent projection in `her_A(a)`.

**(P2) Monoid map.**  `alpha(p) = u p u^*` is equivalent to `p`, so
`[p] |-> [p]` defines a monoid map `Sigma -> V(A)`.  Nonzero elements go to
nonzero elements, and an equation `[x] = [x] + [e']` with `e' != 0` gives
infinite projections.  `Sigma` is simple by (U).

**(P3) State case.**  Suppose `Sigma` has a state `f` with `f([x]) = 1` for
some nonzero `x`.  Simplicity makes `f` finite, and it pulls back to a positive
`alpha_*`-invariant homomorphism `G -> R`.  For the AF algebra `D`, this is the
`K_0` map of a unique densely finite lower semicontinuous trace `tau`.
Uniqueness gives `tau o alpha = tau`.  Then `tau o E` is a nonzero lower
semicontinuous trace on `A`.  Its null ideal is a proper closed ideal, hence
zero, and its domain contains the Pedersen ideal.  So `(tau o E) tensor Tr` is
finite and faithful on every projection of `A tensor K`, and `A` is stably
finite.  Conversely an invariant trace gives such a state.

**(P4) No-state case.**  If `D` has no nonzero invariant densely finite trace,
then by (P3) and (GH), for every nonzero `x in G^+` there is `k` with
`(k+1)[x] <= k[x]`.  By the coinvariant Tarski lemma, `[x]` is properly
infinite in `Sigma`, so every nonzero projection of `D tensor K` is infinite in
`A tensor K` by (P2).  By (P1) every nonzero hereditary subalgebra of `A`
contains a projection equivalent to such a projection, hence an infinite
projection.  For simple `A` this is pure infiniteness.  A purely infinite
algebra is not stably finite, so the two cases are exclusive.
