Every envelope is a subgroup of Sym(N) containing the same E_2, which
is generated by alpha,lambda,tau and contains FSym(N).
Indeed alpha-conjugates of tau generate the adjacent transpositions
along its bi-infinite orbit, and the germs of alpha and lambda generate
the infinite dihedral germ quotient of E_2.

First, the centralizer of FSym(N) in Sym(N) is trivial. A commuting
permutation u satisfies (u(i) u(j))=(i j) for every transposition.
For fixed i choose distinct j,k different from i. Then u(i) belongs
to both {i,j} and {i,k}, so u(i)=i.

If an isomorphism f:G->G' between two such envelopes fixes E_2
pointwise, it fixes FSym(N) pointwise. For g in G and t in FSym(N),

    f(g) t f(g)^-1 = f(g t g^-1) = g t g^-1,

since g t g^-1 is finitary. The centralizer calculation gives f(g)=g
as permutations. Thus G=G' as actual subgroups.

Fix a countable abstract group A. There are at most countably many maps
E_2->A because E_2 has three generators. Choose, for each actual envelope
G isomorphic to A, an isomorphism G->A. If two restrictions to E_2 agree,
composing one isomorphism with the inverse of the other fixes E_2, so
the envelopes coincide. Thus only countably many actual envelopes are
isomorphic to A.

For a fixed actual envelope G, choose finite generators p_1,...,p_d of P.
There are only countably many possibilities for their transported
permutations beta_1,...,beta_d in G. Once that tuple and nu(1) are fixed,
nu is determined: applying the permutation corresponding to p to
2nu(1) gives 2nu(p). Thus each G comes from at most countably many nu.

Combining the two countability statements proves the assertion for A.
There are only countably many finitely presented abstract groups,
proving the finite-presentation assertion. Finally, the set of bijections
P->N has cardinality continuum; partitioning it into countable
isomorphism-class fibers leaves continuum many classes.

This argument uses no classification of automorphisms of FSym(N), no
decidability assumption on P, and no assumption that a particular good
enumeration exists.
