The Dehn function of `BS(1,4)` is exponential (Gersten/Thurston; exponential
distortion of the cyclic base).  `BS(1,4)` is HS-stable (Levit--Vigdorovich),
so `hs-length-control-is-linear-under-stability` gives a length-linear
control modulus.  The two diverge exponentially in length.
