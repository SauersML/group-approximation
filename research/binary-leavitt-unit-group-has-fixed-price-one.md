**ESTABLISHED (proved on paper).** Every free p.m.p. action of `L_(F_2)(1,2)^x` has cost one.

**Rank gradient.** The group is infinite and simple, so it has no proper finite-index
subgroups and no Farber chains. Rank gradient is undefined for it.

**Profile.** Fixed price one and `β_*^(2) = 0` are the values of an infinite amenable group.
The group itself has property (T), is simple and finitely presented, and is nonsofic.
