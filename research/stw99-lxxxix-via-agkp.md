`F_2` is nonelementary hyperbolic, hence acylindrically hyperbolic; it
is torsion-free, so it has no nontrivial finite normal subgroup; it
has Haagerup's rapid decay property (Haagerup, Invent. 1979).  The
required theorem (AGKP Theorem B) then gives selflessness of
`C*_r(F_2)`, and Robert's selflessness ⟹ strict comparison.  (AGKP
Section 3.4 also derives the free-group case directly from
Louder--Magee strong convergence; STW record both in Addendum 25.1.)
