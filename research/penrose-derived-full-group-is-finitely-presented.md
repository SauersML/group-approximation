**OPEN.** Let `𝒫` be the topological full group of Penrose tilings
(`penrose-tiling-group-is-an-amenable-orbit-full-group`). Its derived subgroup
`D(𝒫)`, which is finitely generated, infinite and simple, is finitely presented.

**Why it matters.** `D(𝒫)` is sofic unconditionally. So a finite presentation
would give a finitely presented infinite simple sofic group, through
`fp-simple-amenable-orbit-via-penrose-group` and
`hyperlinear-fp-simple-via-amenable-orbit-full-group`, with no amenability
input. Together with the open amenability of `𝒫` (ChJN, arXiv:1602.04255, line 95)
it would also answer `fp-infinite-simple-amenable-group`.

## Attempts

- **Expert heuristic against it.** Nekrashevych, *Semigroups, groups, and algebras
  of dynamical origin*, arXiv:2509.05524 (TeX source read on MSI, line 3362): "Full
  groups of shift-invariant groupoids, on the other hand, are rarely finitely
  presented. They are often sources of groups with interesting finiteness
  properties like amenability or intermediate growth." This is a remark, not a
  theorem.
  - Context there: Section 7 presents the Penrose tiling semigroup (Kellendonk)
    as a contracting self-similar inverse semigroup over the one-sided SFT
    `{x_n x_(n+1) != 20}`. Theorem `th:contractingalmostfinite` makes such
    shift-invariant groupoids almost finite.
  - Finite presentation is proved there (line 3359, "a particular case of a more
    general result of" Belk--Bleak--Matucci--Zaremsky) only for the full group of
    the groupoid generated by the contracting groupoid **and the shift**. That
    groupoid is purely infinite, carries no invariant measure, and lies outside
    the amenable orbit certificate.
  - That survey's Penrose groupoid uses tiles up to isometry; ChJN's `𝒫` uses
    pointed vertices up to translation. We did not check that the two full groups
    coincide.
- **Compression-based finiteness methods** (Matui's Brown-criterion proof for
  one-sided SFTs, Li arXiv:2110.04505, Nekrashevych's contracting groupoids). They
  all need compressible clopen sets, which the full-support invariant measure of
  `penrose-tiling-group-is-an-amenable-orbit-full-group` forbids.
- **Obstructions through finite models.** The action is free, so there are no
  finite orbits, and `periodic-approximable-subshifts-host-no-fp-simple-group` has
  nothing to act on. Whether the `Z^2 ⊕ Z/5` coding of `X` is a subshift of finite
  type, which would decide whether a Matui-style SFT neighbourhood can differ from
  `X`, was not checked.
- **Homology.** Li's exact sequence (quoted as Theorem `th:Li` in the survey) was
  not evaluated for `Lambda = Z^2 ⊕ Z/5`.
