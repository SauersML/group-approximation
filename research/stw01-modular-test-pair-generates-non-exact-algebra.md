In `Q = C^3 * C^2 = C*(PSL_2(Z))` let `h = e_1 p e_1` and `k = e_1 p e_2 p e_1`. The
C*-subalgebra `C*(h, k)` is not exact. More precisely, it surjects onto an isomorphic
copy of `Q`.

**Consequence.** Haagerup's theorem cannot certify the single-pair additivity
`stw01-modular-single-pair-quasitrace-additivity` through any C*-algebra containing
both `h` and `k`. The corner `e_1 Q e_1` contains a subalgebra mapping onto `Q` itself.

Proof: `stw01-modular-test-pair-non-exact-proof`, and Section 3 of
`research/artifacts/stw01-modular-single-pair-test-2026-09-12.md`.
