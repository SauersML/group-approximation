title: A block diagonal sum of pairwise Gram states averages their game coverage instead of gluing it
---

A direct sum of partial Gram strategies is exactly a classical mixture of
those strategies.  Its certified question mass is therefore at most the
best single block's mass, not the mass of the union of all certified rounds.

For `k` uniformly weighted contexts, even the optimistic pair-carrier bound
remains `2/k`; orientation and private-variable issues can only lower it.
Thus block diagonalization cannot replace one full-game Gram state.
