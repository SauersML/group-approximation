Let A satisfy the hypotheses of stw64-supersoft-calculus and put
s_t=alpha(t*[1_A]-hat), for finite t>0. Then

    rc(A) = inf { b-a : 0<a<b<infinity and s_a<<s_b }.

The infimum of the empty set is infinity. More precisely,
s_a<<s_b implies rc(A)<=b-a, and b-a>rc(A) implies s_a<<s_b.
No assertion is made at the boundary b-a=rc(A)>0.

Thus compact containment on the canonical scalar supersoft family cannot
be borrowed from the ordinary order on real ranks when rc(A)>0.
