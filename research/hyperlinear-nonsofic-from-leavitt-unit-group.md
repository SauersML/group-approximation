One step: a group that is hyperlinear and not sofic *is* the separation.  The
second prerequisite is the established nonsoficity theorem for
`L_{F_2}(1,2)^x`, so the entire content of this route sits in the first.

Recorded separately from the stability routes into the same target
(`leavitt-steinberg-unstable-branch`, `flexible-side-witness`) because it uses
no stability hypothesis, no cover, and no central quotient -- and because the
route is only available at all since 2026-08-01, when an unconditional
nonsofic group first existed to put in the second slot.
