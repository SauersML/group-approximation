Direct proof, given in the target under `## Proof`. Under a proper action an
elliptic element of infinite order is impossible, and a hyperbolic element has
an axis (Bridson--Haefliger, Theorem II.6.8(1)). So `|a^k|_S >= k l / λ`, and no
infinite cyclic subgroup is distorted. The listed groups contain distorted
cyclic subgroups: `BS(1,2)`, `BS(1,4)` and the integral Heisenberg group. The
applications (a) and (b) quote Bridson (Proc. AMS 127, 1999) and Haglund
(arXiv:0705.3386), as recorded in the target.
