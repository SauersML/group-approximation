Use the finite binary restriction on

```text
q@2, q@4, q@5, q@6, e_1@7, e_2@8, e_3@9, e_3@2.       (1)
```

The signed pattern group `K=<L_0,r,l>` has order `2^15`, with root-arrow
closure and character `chi` as in
`full-q-relative-word-twisted-hecke-central-support-proof`.  On the four
moving middle coordinates the additional actors are the literal matrices

```text
J_1=(u1 u2),                   J_2=(u2 u3),
B_2=x_(u2,q4)(1),              B_3=x_(u3,q5)(1).       (2)
```

Let `P` be the subgroup of `GL_8(F_2)` generated by `K` and `(2)`.
Canonical left-`K` row reduction enumerates

```text
[P:K]=20160,                 |P|=660602880.             (3)
```

Right multiplication by the twelve pattern generators partitions the
`20160` left cosets into `796` double cosets.  On a double coset `KxK`, the
twisted corner element `FxF` is nonzero exactly when the propagated
`chi`-sign is consistent around that orbit.  Exact binary propagation gives

```text
52 compatible double cosets,       744 incompatible.   (4)
```

For comparison, the subgroup `H=<K,J_1J_2>` has `52` compatible double
cosets.  Inclusion `H<=P` sends each of them to the same subset `KxK` of
`P`.  Two such subsets cannot merge after passing to `P`: if

```text
KxK=KyK
```

for `x,y in H`, then `y=k_1xk_2` for elements `k_i in K<=H`, so they were
already the same `H` double coset.  Hence the `52` compatible `H` cosets
inject into the `52` compatible `P` cosets in `(4)` and exhaust them.

The signed double-coset sums are a disjoint-support basis of a twisted
corner.  Exhaustion therefore proves the literal algebra equality

```text
F C[P] F=F C[H] F.                                     (5)
```

The exact integer calculation for the right side of `(5)` gives

```text
dim(F C[H] F)=52,
dim((F C[H] F)(FgF)(F C[H] F))=44,
F notin (F C[H] F)(FgF)(F C[H] F).                    (6)
```

Equations `(5)--(6)` prove the same central-support failure for `P`.
Finite-dimensional semisimplicity then supplies a simple `P`-block on which
`F` is nonzero and `FgF` is zero, proving `(SAP5)`.

`experiments/full_q_separate_actor_extension_audit.cpp` implements the
binary normal form and sign propagation in `(3)--(4)`.  As a redundant
check, it reconstructs the compatible signed-orbit multiplication modulo
the odd prime `1000003` and again obtains ideal dimension `44`.  The proof
does not infer nonmembership from modular rank: exact nonmembership is
transported through the algebra equality `(5)` from the fraction-free
integer computation in the required relative-interface claim.

The audit was compiled and run on MSI only.  It completed in `4.5` seconds.
