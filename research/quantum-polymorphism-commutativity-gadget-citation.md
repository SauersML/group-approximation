**Established by citation.**  Culf--van Dobben de Bruyn--Zeman,
arXiv:2604.01408v1, characterize commutativity gadgets for finite relational
templates in the finite-dimensional, quantum-approximate and
commuting-operator models.

Their nonrobust characterization says that it is enough to test whether all
quantum polymorphisms at arity `|A|^2` are classical.  Their robust
characterization identifies robust gadgets with stable commutativity of the
weighted polymorphism algebra at that same bounded arity.  Theorem
`implications-equivalences`, items (ix)--(xii), proves in particular

```text
qa commutativity gadget
  <=> robust qa commutativity gadget
  <=> robust finite-dimensional q commutativity gadget,
```

with the analogous `qc` equivalence, in both the appropriate non-oracular and
oracular forms stated there.

Consequently, for a fixed template satisfying the exact polymorphism
hypothesis, vanishing weighted matrix defect forces the distinguished local
commutators to vanish with a dimension-independent modulus.  The theorem does
not convert the relational weighted algebra into scalar group relators.
