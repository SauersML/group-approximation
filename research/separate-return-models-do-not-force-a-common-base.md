**METHOD-SPECIFIC NO-GO (established here; proof in
`separate-return-common-base-no-go-proof`).**  Let `N` be a noncommutative
Connes-embeddable finite algebra and let `beta_1,...,beta_r` be automorphisms.
Suppose each cyclic crossed product `A_i=N rtimes_(beta_i) Z` embeds in a
matrix ultrapower.  None of the following formal operations produces, from
those data alone, one embedding of `N` on which every `beta_i` is spatial:

1. The pointwise tensor formula `x |-> tensor_i pi_i(x)` is not linear (and
   hence is not a star homomorphism), already on scalar multiples of `1`.
2. Taking the scalar free product of the `A_i` keeps `r` distinct copies
   `N_i`.  The desired algebra is the amalgamated free product
   `*_(N) A_i = N rtimes F_r`; identifying the `N_i` is precisely the
   nonamenable-amalgamation/common-embedding step, not a consequence of free
   independence.
3. A literal direct-sum symmetrization over the orbit
   `{pi_0 compose beta_g:g in F_r}` with return unitaries permuting the orbit
   blocks requires an invariant probability weight on the regular `F_r`
   orbit.  For `r>=2` none exists, and finite truncations have a uniform
   boundary loss.  Finite quotient permutations avoid that counting loss
   only if embeddings whose labels differ by the quotient kernel are already
   identified; that is additional joint spatialization data.

Also, free independence of the proposed implementing unitaries is irrelevant:
the tensor-with-`lambda(F_r)` construction in
`free-lattice-joint-spatialization-proof` supplies free Fourier labels *after*
one common covariant embedding has been found.  It cannot manufacture that
embedding.

This is not a counterexample to joint spatialization for the explicit
homogeneous-quotient action.  Such a counterexample would prove the target
factor non-Connes-embeddable.  It rigorously fences the tempting argument that
the separately solvable `Z` problems combine by routine tensor/free/direct-sum
operations.
