title: Additive indices are not amplification-stable
invalidates: [additive-index-transducer]
---

Replacing a microstate by `φ ⊗ I_k` preserves every normalized-HS defect,
trace, separation, and scalar word value, while an additive Fredholm/rank
index is multiplied by `k`. Doubling kills its parity while a marked scalar
`−I` remains `−I`; amplification by the order of any finite character gives
the same no-go. Closed: determinant/parity shortcuts from microscopic
leakage to the marked sign without a canonical multiplicity normalization.
