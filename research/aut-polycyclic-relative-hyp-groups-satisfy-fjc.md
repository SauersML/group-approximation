Let `G` be one-ended and hyperbolic relative to finitely many conjugacy
classes of virtually polycyclic subgroups. Then, for every
`X in {A,K,L}`,

```text
Aut(G) belongs to FJC_X,
Out(G) belongs to FJC_X.
```

Here `FJC_X` is the fibred Farrell--Jones conjecture in `X`-theory with
respect to the family of virtually cyclic subgroups.

No torsion-free, finite-center, finite-`Out(G)`, non-relatively-hyperbolic
peripheral, or peripheral-preserving-automorphism hypothesis is assumed.
