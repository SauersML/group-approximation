title: Wrong-target leakage of a partial isometry depends only on its final range
---

Let `(M,tau)` be finite tracial, let `T` be a partial isometry with final
projection `F=T T^*`, and let `P` be any projection.  Then

```text
||(1-P)T||_2^2 = tau((1-P)F).
```

Equivalently, the leakage charged by a branching-capacity argument is a pure
range-projection quantity; it is independent of the phase of `T` and of its
initial projection once `F` is fixed.
