**ESTABLISHED TWO-SCALE BOUNDARY; THE GLOBAL ROOT REMAINS OPEN.**

Retain

~~~text
S=F_2[x_1,y,x_3],
P=F_2[x_1,x_1y,x_3],
T_N=S/(x_1,y,x_3)^(N+1),
P_N=image(P),
V_N=T_N/P_N.
~~~

Multiplication by `x_1` descends to an `F_2`-linear map
`X_N:V_N->V_N`. Give `V_N` the orthonormal basis of residue monomials

~~~text
x_1^a y^b x_3^c,   a+b+c<=N,   a<b.
~~~

Write `v_N=dim_F2(V_N)` and `k_N=dim ker(X_N)`. If `N=2m` or
`N=2m+1`, respectively, then

~~~text
v_(2m)=m(m+1)(4m+5)/6,
v_(2m+1)=(m+1)(m+2)(4m+3)/6,                              (NIA1)

k_N=dim coker(X_N)=N(N+1)/2.                              (NIA2)
~~~

As a complex matrix on this monomial carrier, `X_N` is a partial
permutation. Its optimal two-sided normalized-HS inverse defect is

~~~text
inf_Y max{||YX_N-I||_2,||X_NY-I||_2}
 =sqrt(k_N/v_N)
 =sqrt(6/N+O(1/N^2)).                                     (NIA3)
~~~

The infimum is attained by the adjoint partial inverse and even by a
unitary extension of that partial inverse. Equivalently, the normalized-HS
distance from `X_N` to the unitary group is exactly `sqrt(k_N/v_N)`.

This gives a genuine but decoupled thin-carrier absorber. Tensoring the
regular wreath model from `kt-single-laurent-return-has-profinite-coset-models`
with this coefficient carrier keeps

~~~text
||[p_N,U_h,N]||_2=1/sqrt(2)
~~~

while the two scalar inverse defects in (NIA3) tend to zero.

It is not a coherent full-Laurent microstate. The named class

~~~text
eta_N=[y] in V_N
~~~

belongs to `ker(X_N)` because `x_1y=x_2 in P_N`. Therefore every candidate
inverse has the dimension-independent pointed defect

~~~text
(YX_N-I)eta_N=-eta_N.                                     (NIA4)
~~~

The small error in (NIA3) is obtained only by hiding this fixed return
inside a boundary of relative linear dimension `k_N/v_N`.

Canonical root amplification makes the failure terminal for this
truncation scheme. Let `A_N=(V_N,+)`, so `|A_N|=2^(v_N)`. The map induced
by `X_N` has kernel of cardinality `2^(k_N)` and image of cardinality
`2^(v_N-k_N)`. For every function `Y:A_N->A_N`, and even allowing the
best bijective completion,

~~~text
|Fix(YX_N)|/|A_N| <= 2^(-k_N),
|Fix(X_NY)|/|A_N| <= 2^(-k_N).                            (NIA5)
~~~

Both bounds are sharp. Thus the optimal normalized counting or
point-carrier inverse defect is

~~~text
sqrt(1-2^(-k_N)) -> 1,                                   (NIA6)
~~~

not zero. Equivalently, on `ell^2(A_N)`, the composition operator of
`X_N` has rank fraction `2^(-k_N)`, so every two-sided generalized inverse
has normalized-HS error at least `sqrt(1-2^(-k_N))`; the Moore--Penrose
inverse attains it.

Hence the first inverse is cheap only in the noncanonical monomial-index
metric. Once it is required to act on the finite additive root group, the
Fourier packet, or the coset/lamp carrier needed for canonical mixed
moments, the defect approaches the maximum. The exact `1/sqrt(2)` wall
therefore cannot coexist with a vanishing **coherent canonical** inverse
defect through these nilpotent truncations.

This does not prove a uniform obstruction for arbitrary matrix microstates:
a moving finite word window may use a different noncongruence carrier.
It does prove that the new profinite intermediate-ring models cannot be
upgraded to the full Laurent actor merely by completing multiplication by
`x_1` on `T_N/P_N`. The missing step must control the pointed vector
`[y]`, not just normalized rank.
