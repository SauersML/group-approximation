title: The Iwahori Nielsen involution is uniformly bi-Lipschitz on HS representation coordinates
---

For `Bbar=<u,e|e^2=1>` and
`alpha(u)=u^-1e`, `alpha(e)=u^-1eu`, pullback by `alpha` is
`3`-bi-Lipschitz for the maximum normalized-HS distance on the two generator
images.  Thus the opposite Iwahori extension loci have quantitatively
equivalent coordinate geometry.
