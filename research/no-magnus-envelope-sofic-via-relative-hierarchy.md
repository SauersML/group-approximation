Let `G` have no finite amenable envelope in any zero-exponent basis. If some
basis gives a relative amenable hierarchy of the Magnus piece, the first
prerequisite makes `G` sofic. Otherwise `G` lies in the second prerequisite's
class. The split is exhaustive. The second prerequisite asks for no more than
the target, since it covers a subclass of the target's groups.
