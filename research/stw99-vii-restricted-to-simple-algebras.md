Every SIMPLE separable nuclear stably finite C\*-algebra is
quasidiagonal.  Recorded as its own target because the general
Problem VII reduces pressure onto it (non-simple algebras filter
through their simple subquotients in all known attacks) and because it
is exactly the case reachable from the trace problems: route
`stw99-ix1-implies-vii-simple`.

## Attempts

* Under the UCT this is the Tikuisis--White--Winter theorem territory
  (with faithful traces supplied by simplicity + Blackadar--Handelman
  + Haagerup in the unital case).  The UCT-free route now runs
  X(2) ⟹ IX(1) ⟹ this, so the hyperfinite quasidiagonality problem
  dominates the entire simple case of Blackadar--Kirchberg —
  sharpening STW's "heart of the matter" remark into graph structure.
