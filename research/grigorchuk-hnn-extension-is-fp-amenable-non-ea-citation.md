Citation import, not a reproof.

- Primary source: R. I. Grigorchuk, "An example of a finitely presented amenable
  group not belonging to the class EG", Sb. Math. 189 (1998).
- Verified secondary statement: M. G. Benli, R. Grigorchuk, P. de la Harpe,
  "Amenable groups without finitely presented amenable covers"
  (arXiv:1206.2072), Remark 2.23(iii), read from the arXiv HTML render on
  2026-09-13: "It is shown in [Grig–98] that 𝔊 has a finitely presented
  HNN-extension 𝔊^ which is in 𝒮𝒢∖ℰ𝒢. Any proper quotient of 𝔊^ is metabelian
  and virtually abelian [SaWi–02, Theorem 2.3]." The introduction of the same
  paper lists `𝔊^` [Grig–98] among the "sporadic examples of finitely presented
  groups" in `AG ∖ EG`.
- `𝒮𝒢`, the subexponentially amenable groups, is contained in the class `AG` of
  amenable groups, so `𝔊^` is amenable.

This route asserts only that the cited sources say what the target records.
