Antoine--Perera--Thiel--Vilalta, Theorem 6.6 in
[Pure C*-algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11052), proves for every
separable locally subhomogeneous C*-algebra of stable rank one and topological
dimension zero that pureness is equivalent to Z-stability (and to their
finite-nuclear-dimension/global-Glimm condition).

Thus the hypotheses imply `A ~= A tensor_min Z`.  For a Z-stable algebra the
first-factor embedding is approximately unitarily equivalent to an
isomorphism, so its induced Cu morphism is an isomorphism.  This is precisely
Cuntz semigroup regularity.
