title: Square-zero contractions form a stable relation in every C-star algebra
---

**ESTABLISHED (literature import plus a short universality check).**  For
every `eta>0` there is `delta>0` with the following property.  If `B` is any
C-star algebra and `y in B` satisfies

```text
||y||<=1,        ||y^2||<delta,
```

then there is `z in B` with

```text
z^2=0,        ||z-y||<eta.
```

The constant `delta` depends only on `eta`, not on `B`.

Two facts give this.

1. The universal C-star algebra of one contraction `x` with `x^2=0` is the
   cone `C_0((0,1], M_2)`, with `x` sent to `t e_21`.  Universality is checked
   directly.  Let `x` be a square-zero contraction on a Hilbert space `H` and
   put `h=(x*x)^(1/2)`.  Since `x^2=0`, the range of `x` lies in `ker x`, so
   `H_1=closure range(h)=(ker x)^perp` is orthogonal to
   `H_2=closure range(x)`.  The polar isometry `v` of `x=vh` maps `H_1` onto
   `H_2`.  Identifying `H_2` with `H_1` through `v`, the operator `x` is
   `h tensor e_21` on `H_1 tensor C^2`, plus zero on the complement.  So
   `f tensor e_ij -> f(h) tensor e_ij` defines a `*`-homomorphism from
   `C_0((0,1], M_2)` sending `t e_21` to `x`; its image is generated by `x`,
   because `t e_21` generates the cone.
2. Cones over finite-dimensional C-star algebras are projective, hence
   semiprojective (T. A. Loring, *Lifting Solutions to Perturbing Problems in
   C*-Algebras*, Fields Institute Monographs 8, AMS 1997, chapters on
   projective and semiprojective algebras).  For a finitely presented
   universal algebra, semiprojectivity is equivalent to stability of its
   defining relations; this is the stability statement above.

Theorem numbers in Loring's book were not rechecked in this session; the
statements used are the standard ones: projectivity of `C_0((0,1], M_n)` and
the equivalence of semiprojectivity with stability of relations.
