**THEOREM.**  Use the words and four nontrivial relators from the
two-generator Titz--Witzel gate `(TW2G1)--(TW2G2)`.  Let a finite set be a
disjoint union of subsets invariant under permutations `e,u`.  On every
subset `Omega_j`, assume

```text
(e|Omega_j)^2=1,
(e|Omega_j) <u|Omega_j> (e|Omega_j)=<u|Omega_j>.          (TWCN1)
```

Write the four relator words, in their displayed order after `e^2`, as

```text
R_c=c^2,
R_rho=rho^2 e^(-1)u^(-1),
R_b=b x u^(-1)d^(-1),
R_a=a c d b.                                               (TWCN2)
```

Then normalized Hamming distance satisfies the dimension-independent bound

```text
d_H(u^8,1) <= 2 d_H(R_rho,1)
              +2 d_H(R_b,1)+2 d_H(R_a,1).                (TWCN3)
```

In particular, no sequence solving the two-generator gate with `u^8`
macroscopically nontrivial can have `e_n` normalize `<u_n>` at every stage.
The stronger blockwise form also excludes disjoint sums with different power
automorphisms on different blocks.  It does not exclude wreath lifts with
nontrivial fibre cocycles.  The square defect of `c` is not needed for the
obstruction.

**BLOCK-QUOTIENT COROLLARY.**  Suppose instead that `e,u` preserve a system
`B` of equal-sized blocks and that their induced permutations `bar(e),bar(u)`
on `B` satisfy the blockwise cyclic-normalizer hypothesis `(TWCN1)`.  Then

```text
d_H,B(bar(u)^8,1) <= 2 d_H,Omega(R_rho,1)
                     +2 d_H,Omega(R_b,1)+2 d_H,Omega(R_a,1).  (TWCN4)
```

Consequently a wreath/block lift over a cyclic-normalizer quotient can solve
the gate only if the surviving mark becomes asymptotically invisible on the
block quotient and is carried inside the fibres.  In particular, fibre
cocycles do not rescue a family whose quotient `bar(u)^8` moves a positive
proportion of its blocks.
