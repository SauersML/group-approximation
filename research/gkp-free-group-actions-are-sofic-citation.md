**Literature import.** Gao, Kunnawalkam Elayavalli, Patchell, *Soficity for
group actions on sets and applications*, arXiv:2401.04945v3. The v3 HTML was
fetched on MSI on 2026-09-12 and the statement below extracted from it, with
the math markup flattened.

**Definition 2.1** (setting): "Let G be a countable discrete group, X be a
countable discrete set, α : G ↷ X be an action, A be a finite set,
φ : G → Sym(A) be a map (not necessarily a homomorphism)". The action `α` is
sofic if for all finite `F ⊆ G`, `E ⊆ X` and `ε > 0` there is a finite set `A`
and a unital, `(F, ε)`-multiplicative map `φ : G → Sym(A)` that is an
`(F, E, ε)`-orbit approximation of `α`.

**Text preceding the theorem**: "It is still open whether all actions by
sofic groups are sofic. However, this does hold for amenable groups and free
groups."

**Theorem 2.19.** "Any action α : G ↷ X where G is a free group is sofic."

The proof begins: "Fix finite subsets F ⊆ G, E ⊆ X, and ϵ > 0. Let the free
generators of G be {g_1, g_2, ⋯} (either a finite set or a sequence,
depending on whether G is finitely generated or not)." So both finite and
countably infinite rank are covered.

**Match with the target.** The target is Theorem 2.19 read in the setting of
Definition 2.1: countable free group, countable set. Nothing is added. The
journal version (Res. Math. Sci. 12 (2025), 48) was not consulted; the
theorem numbers are those of arXiv v3.
