title: HNN extensions have exactly the torsion orders of their vertex groups
distinct_from:
  hnn-torsion-theorem: that is the torsion-free specialization; this records the exact finite-order spectrum and applies when the vertex group has torsion.
  hnn-cyclic-reduction-lemma: that is the word-combinatorial conjugacy input; this is the resulting group-theoretic torsion-order theorem.
---

Let H be an HNN extension of a vertex group V along two injective edge
maps. Then

    Tord(H) = Tord(V),

where Tord is the set of orders of nonidentity finite-order elements.

Thus an HNN extension creates no new finite orders: every finite-order
element is conjugate into V, while the canonical copy of V in H preserves
all orders. This is established by
[[hnn-torsion-orders-via-cyclic-reduction]].
