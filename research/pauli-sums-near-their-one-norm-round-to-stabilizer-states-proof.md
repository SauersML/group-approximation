Write `beta_P = w_P s_P` and `A_P = s_P P`. Let `psi` be a top eigenvector and
`delta_P = 1 - <psi|A_P|psi>`, so `sum_P w_P delta_P = F`.

1. `G = {P : delta_P < 1 - 1/sqrt2}` pairwise commutes: anticommuting `A_P, A_Q`
   satisfy `(A_P+A_Q)^2 = 2I`, so `<A_P>+<A_Q> <= sqrt2 < 2(1-delta)` is impossible.
2. Let `A` be the group generated by `G` modulo signs, and `G*` the terms `P` in
   `+-A`. Measuring `{A_Q : Q in G}` on `psi` determines outcomes `x_P` for all
   `P in G*`, with mean `1-delta_P`. Choose a positive-probability pattern `x`
   with `sum_(G*) w_P(1-x_P) <= sum_(G*) w_P delta_P`.
3. The joint eigenspace `E_x` is nonzero, so `{x_Q A_Q}` generates an abelian group
   without `-I`. Its maximal extensions without `-I` give the finite set `S_x` of
   stabilizer states in `E_x`, all with `<A_P> = x_P` for `P in G*`.
4. For `P` not in `G*`: if `P` anticommutes with a member of `G`, then `<phi|P|phi>=0`
   for all `phi in S_x`; otherwise `P` is a nonzero class in `C(A)/A`, which is
   nondegenerate symplectic, so some `R in C(A)` anticommutes with `P`. `R` permutes
   `S_x` and flips `<P>`, so the uniform average over `S_x` is zero.
5. Hence the average energy over `S_x` is at least `sum_(G*) w_P(1-delta_P)`, and
   `lambda_max` minus it is at most `sum_(P not in G*) w_P max{0,1-delta_P}`. Outside
   `G`, `delta_P >= 1-1/sqrt2` and `max{0,1-delta} <= (1+sqrt2)delta`, giving at most
   `(1+sqrt2)F`. Some member of `S_x` attains at least the average.

The lower-bound example is a one-qubit computation: stabilizer states satisfy
`<X>+<Z> <= 1`. Full details are in Section 2 of the linked artifact.
