Let `H=<u,v> = GL_2(F_2) = S_3` be the constant matrix subgroup of the binary
Leavitt unit group, and let `delta` be the scalar self-embedding.  Put

```text
H_n = <H,delta(H),...,delta^n(H)>.
```

The layers commute pairwise, so `H_n` is finite of order at most `6^(n+1)`.
For the recursive kernel idempotents `q_n=lambda^n(z)` of
`leavitt-recursive-kernel-idempotents`, one has

```text
q_n in F_2[H_n].
```

Consequently the algebra generated by all `q_n` is a directed union of
finite-dimensional group algebras and is directly finite.  No one-sided
inverse defect can be built from finitely many recursive packet idempotents
alone; every such proposed correction remains inside a finite subgroup.
