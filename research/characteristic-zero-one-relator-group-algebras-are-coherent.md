Let `G` be any one-relator group and let `K` be any field of characteristic
zero.  Then the group algebra `K[G]` is coherent: every finitely generated left
ideal is finitely presented as a left `K[G]`-module.

The conclusion covers both torsion and torsion-free one-relator groups.  The
characteristic-zero quantifier is part of the theorem; this node makes no
all-fields claim.
