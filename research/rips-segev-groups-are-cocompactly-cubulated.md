**OPEN.** Every Rips--Segev torsion-free group without the unique product
property admits a proper cocompact action on a CAT(0) cube complex.

**Status in the literature.** Martin--Steenbock (arXiv:1409.3678, TeX read
2026-09-13) prove cubulability for quotients of free products of cubulable
groups by finitely many relators satisfying the *classical* `C'(1/6)`
condition. Their "Open problem" paragraph, verbatim: "It is therefore natural
to ask, in light of Agol's theorem, whether our approach can be extended to
cubulate some generalised Rips--Segev groups."

**Known obstruction for the generalized family.** Arzhantseva--Steenbock
(arXiv:1407.2441) produce generalized Rips--Segev-type torsion-free hyperbolic
groups with property (T). These are infinite Kazhdan groups, so they are not
CAT(0)-cubical, as the same authors observe. So a positive answer must use
specific features of the original Rips--Segev graphs.
