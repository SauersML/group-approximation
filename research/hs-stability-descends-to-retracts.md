Let `H` and `L` be finitely presented groups and suppose there are
homomorphisms

```text
i:H -> L,             r:L -> H,             r i=id_H.             (HDR1)
```

If `L` is same-dimension stable for normalized Hilbert--Schmidt approximate
representations, then so is `H`.

Consequently, if `H` is infinite, hyperlinear and property (T), no
same-dimension HS-stable ambient group `L` can retract onto `H`.  In
particular the stable finite-bi-index search cannot use a split extension,
semidirect product projection, or free-product retraction onto such a
stabilizer.
