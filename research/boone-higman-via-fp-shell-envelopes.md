Given a finitely generated group G with decidable word problem,
Clapham embeds it in a finitely presented group P_0 with decidable
word problem. Put P=P_0 times Z to ensure an infinite input, and
choose nu from the OPEN shell-envelope hypothesis. Then E_nu is
finitely presented and contains P faithfully.

Its action on Omega=V*0^infinity is faithful because Omega is dense
in the Cantor set. It is highly transitive because V is a subgroup.
Every point stabilizer is finitely generated by the independent-germ
claim, which holds for every enumeration of every finitely generated
input. The twisted Brin--Thompson criterion therefore gives the
finitely presented simple group Omega V_(E_nu) containing E_nu,
hence G.

The only unresolved construction in this route is finite presentation
of E_nu for an appropriate enumeration. Finite presentation of A_1,
A_12,A_2, or Q is not assumed. The older route through those three
stabilizers remains a sufficient way to obtain the required E_nu,
but the point-stabilizer proof does not depend on that stronger route.
