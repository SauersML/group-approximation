title: A non-nilpotent unitary component group has a nonzero fivefold commutator
target: simple-cstar-unitary-components-class-five

Let G = U(A)/U_0(A). If G is not nilpotent then gamma_5(G) != 1. The subgroup
gamma_5(G) is generated by the simple commutators
[x_4, [x_3, [x_2, [x_1, y]]]] with x_i, y in G. So some such commutator is
nontrivial. Lifting x_i and y to unitaries u_i and v gives
[u_4, [u_3, [u_2, [u_1, v]]]] not in U_0(A).
