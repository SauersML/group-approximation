**Dead.** The proposed model restricts V's natural action on the Cantor set to a
finite set of points, repairs a vanishing fraction of points, and reads off
permutations. That would be a sofic, hence hyperlinear, approximation.
`thompson-v-cantor-orbits-have-no-folner-sets` shows every finite point set has
a boundary of at least `c_X` times its size, so the repair fraction never
vanishes. The tracial analogue, spatial cylinder models, dies by the same
halving argument, recorded in `thompson-v-hyperlinear`.
