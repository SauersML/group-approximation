Let `A` be a separable unital C\*-algebra with a C\*-diagonal
(Cartan with the unique extension property, i.e. PRINCIPAL Weyl
groupoid) `D ⊆ A` whose spectrum is the Cantor set.  Then there is a
SELF-ADJOINT element `b ∈ A` with

```text
A = C*(d_0, b),      hence      A = C*(d_0 + i b) :
```

`A` is SINGLY GENERATED (`d_0` any self-adjoint generator of `D`).
The upgrade from the first landing (which produced a non-self-adjoint
`b`, hence only 2-generation): symmetrising the weighted normalizer
sum keeps the recovery argument working because all coefficient
contributions are positive — see the proof's final section.

Scope of the answer to STW's Cartan question (posed in prose before
Problem XCVI): single generation via a Cartan is now settled IN FULL
for the entire Cantor-DIAGONAL class —
which contains all infinite-dimensional AF algebras with their
Stratila--Voiculescu diagonals, all crossed products of free Cantor
systems with their canonical diagonals, and the algebras of principal
ample Hausdorff twisted groupoids generally.  (CORRECTION over the
first landing: graph algebras with cycles are NOT covered — their
gauge diagonals are Cartans with isotropy, not C*-diagonals; the
cycle-free unital case is finite-dimensional.  The non-principal
Cartan case remains open, as the residue already recorded.)  The remaining Cartan cases are non-principal Cartans
(isotropy blocks the diagonal-averaging step, exactly the
Mitscher--Spielberg phenomenon) and non-Cantor spectra (no clopen
refinements); the 2-to-1 residue is recorded in the proof.
