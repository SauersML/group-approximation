Let `A` be a separable unital C\*-algebra with a C\*-diagonal
(Cartan with the unique extension property, i.e. PRINCIPAL Weyl
groupoid) `D ⊆ A` whose spectrum is the Cantor set.  Then there is a
single element `b ∈ A` with

```text
A = C*(d_0, b),
```

where `d_0` is any self-adjoint generator of `D`.  In particular `A`
is 2-generated, and generated by three self-adjoint elements.

Scope of the answer to STW's Cartan question (posed in prose before
Problem XCVI): single generation via a Cartan is settled up to the
standard 2-to-1 reduction for the entire Cantor-DIAGONAL class —
which contains all unital graph algebras with their standard
diagonals, all AF algebras with AF diagonals, and all crossed
products of minimal free Cantor systems with their canonical
Cartans.  The remaining Cartan cases are non-principal Cartans
(isotropy blocks the diagonal-averaging step, exactly the
Mitscher--Spielberg phenomenon) and non-Cantor spectra (no clopen
refinements); the 2-to-1 residue is recorded in the proof.
