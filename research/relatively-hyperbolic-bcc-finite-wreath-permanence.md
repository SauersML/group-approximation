Let `G` be a countable discrete group hyperbolic relative to a finite
family `{H_lambda}`. Assume that every `H_lambda`

1. is residually finite, and
2. satisfies the Baum--Connes conjecture with coefficients with finite
   wreath products.

Then `G` satisfies the Baum--Connes conjecture with coefficients with
finite wreath products. In particular, for every finite group `F`,
`G wr F` satisfies Baum--Connes with coefficients.
