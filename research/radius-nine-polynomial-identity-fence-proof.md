The sharp form of the Amitsur--Levitzki theorem says that the standard
polynomial `s_(2n)` is an identity of `M_n(C)` and that no nonzero polynomial
identity of `M_n(C)` has smaller degree.  Taking `n=5` proves that a polynomial
of degree at most nine which is an identity in all matrix sizes is already the
zero free polynomial.  It also shows directly that the only standard
identities fitting in the radius-nine window concern sizes at most four.

For `(R9-PI-1)`, every word evaluation satisfies

```text
w(U tensor I_k)=w(U) tensor I_k.
```

The normalized trace of the right side equals the normalized trace of `w(U)`.
The same calculation applied to `(r(U)-I)^*(r(U)-I)` preserves every squared
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt relator defect and hence their sum `D`.  This is
also the raw-tuple version of the spectator-amplification mechanism in the
established spectator-amplification mechanism.

It follows that a finite list of dimension-bounded PI or exterior-power
cases cannot cover the all-size moment body: amplifying any representative
leaves the point being certified fixed while exceeding every dimension in the
list.  A dimension-indexed identity of growing degree cannot be rewritten as
one radius-nine affine functional.  The final paragraph of the claim merely
separates this equality-based fence from the still-open possibility of a
universal matrix trace inequality.
