Use an exact marked endpoint representation in which one of the three active
atoms is nonzero.  Britton embedding preserves that endpoint representation
through the chart HNN extension.  In the freely attached site packet choose
the independent pulse sign to be negative on the same induced summand.  The
amalgam identifies only the common central mark, so induction from the two
vertex representations gives an exact representation of the amalgam.

The returned active projection is nonzero, but the pulse-positive survivor
projection is zero on that summand.  Therefore the route's asserted
first-hit-prefix inclusion fails at zero relator defect.
