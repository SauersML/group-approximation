Let `S` be a finite nonabelian simple group, `W_S = S^(G*/Gamma*) x| G*`, and
`r` the configuration carrying one nonidentity value `s` at one site.  Then

    Rad_MF(W_S) = S^(G*/Gamma*) = normal closure of r in W_S,

so that adding the single relation `r = 1` gives

    W_S / <<r>>  =  G*,

turning a sofic non-MF group into a residually finite MF group while changing
none of the finite, profinite, compact, all-fields finite-dimensional-linear,
or norm-matrix-corona data.

With `S = A_5` the relation can be taken to be an involution: `r` is the lamp
carrying `(1 2)(3 4)` at the base site.  That single involution lies in every
finite-index subgroup of `W_{A_5}` and is killed by every representation in
all five target classes.

The radical is nevertheless **not** finitely generated as an abstract group:
the site set is infinite, and a finite subset of a restricted direct sum
generates a subgroup supported on finitely many sites.  Singly normally
generated in the ambient group, infinitely generated on its own.
