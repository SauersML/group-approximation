Artifact Section 1, Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 1.3.

1. **Elementary generation.** The code `(0, 10, 11)` gives `M_3(R) ≅ R`, and `GL_3(R) = EL_3(R)` by the required
   node. So `R^x` is generated by `1 + s_(α_i) r t_(α_j)`, `i != j`.
2. **Monomials.** Expand `r` in monomials `s_μ t_ν`. Additivity of `x_ij` gives generators `1 + λ s_σ t_κ` with
   `σ, κ` incomparable.
3. **Splitting.** For a complete suffix code `W`, `1 + λ s_σ t_κ = Π_w (1 + λ s_(σw) t_(κw))`, with commuting
   factors.
4. **Conjugation.** When `[σ] ∪ [κ]` is proper, a prefix-replacement unit sends `(σ, κ)` to `(00, 01)`. Otherwise
   split once first.
5. **Prime fields.** `T(λ) = T(1)^λ`.

For the proposition: conjugation by a sign `τ_([κ])` inverts one factor, and one prefix replacement merges the
remaining factors into `(00w_i, 01w_i)`, whose product is `x` by step 3.
