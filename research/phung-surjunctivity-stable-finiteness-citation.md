Import Xuan Kien Phung, *A geometric generalization of Kaplansky's direct
finiteness conjecture*, arXiv:2111.07930, Theorem B and its proof in Section 5.
Primary source checked 2026-09-20: https://arxiv.org/html/2111.07930 .

The introduction defines surjunctivity with finite alphabets and Theorem B
has no extra hypothesis on the field. Its proof embeds the field in an
algebraic closure, represents matrix multiplication as composition of
linear cellular automata, and applies Theorem C to affine n-space. Theorem C
is proved from finite-alphabet surjunctivity by finite-type descent.
This route imports that theorem; it does not claim a new verification of
all algebraic-geometric inputs.
