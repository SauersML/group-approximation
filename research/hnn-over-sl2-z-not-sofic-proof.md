Permutation stability makes the restriction to the base a genuine
congruence action; the graph of the stable letter's image is an
almost-invariant subset of the diagonal action on pairs; Selberg's `(tau)`
gives uniform Cheeger constants on every orbit, so the graph is close to an
invariant partial permutation, which commutes with the whole image of the
base since `SL_2(Z)` and `SL_2(Z[1/p])` have the same image in every
congruence quotient; Britton's lemma says the stable letter commutes with
no element of the base outside `SL_2(Z)`.  Section 13 of the artifact.
