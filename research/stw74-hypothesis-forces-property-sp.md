title: The LXXIV projection hypothesis forces property SP
---

Every unital simple C*-algebra in which all nonzero projections are infinite
has property (SP).

## Attempts

Fullness of `a>0` gives `1 Cuntz-below a directSum ... directSum a` for some
finite amplification, and the unit is properly infinite by the defect-tower
argument.  What is missing is a diagonalization principle that produces a
nonzero projection in `Her(a)` itself.  Real rank zero and, more generally,
property (SP) supply that step by definition; neither follows formally from
the current projection hypothesis.
