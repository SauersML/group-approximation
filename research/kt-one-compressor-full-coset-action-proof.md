Let `R_j=k[A^(-j)N^d]`.  It is a polynomial ring: the monoid
`A^(-j)N^d` is freely generated by the columns of `A^(-j)`.  Suslin
stability gives

```text
Gamma_j=EL_r(R_j)=SL_r(R_j).
```

We first prove the stabilizer assertion.  Fix `g=eL in E rtimes SL_d(Z)` and
`a in Gamma_j` outside `Gamma_j intersect g Gamma g^(-1)`.  Put
`R_L=k[L N^d]`, another polynomial monoid ring.  Since

```text
g Gamma g^(-1)=e SL_r(R_L)e^(-1),
```

the matrix `e^(-1) a e` does not belong to `SL_r(R_L)`.  Its determinant is
one, so at least one of its entries has a nonzero coefficient at some
Laurent monomial `x^z` with `z notin L N^d`.

Identify `R_j` with `k[t_1,...,t_d]` by
`t^v |-> x^(A^(-j)v)`.  Let `I_M` be the ideal spanned by monomials of total
`t`-degree at least `M`.  The quotient `Q_M=R_j/I_M` is finite.  The supports
of the entries of `e` and `e^(-1)` are finite.  Therefore, for the fixed
exponent `z`, only finitely many monomials `x^(A^(-j)v)` can contribute to
the `x^z` coefficient of

```text
e^(-1) b e,                 b in M_r(R_j).
```

Choose `M` larger than the total degrees of all those `v`.  Then the
coefficient functional at `x^z` annihilates
`e^(-1) M_r(I_M)e`.  If some

```text
c in Gamma_j intersect g Gamma g^(-1)
```

had the same image as `a` in `SL_r(Q_M)`, the `x^z` coefficients of
`e^(-1)c e` and `e^(-1)a e` would coincide.  The first is zero because all
entries of `e^(-1)c e` lie in `R_L`, whereas the second is nonzero by
choice.  This contradiction proves that the finite quotient separates `a`
from the entire stabilizer.

The same argument applies to the stabilizer of every point in every
`Gamma_j`-orbit in `X`: replacing `g` by an orbit representative changes
only the two fixed finite Laurent supports.  Since `Gamma_j` is residually
finite and all its point stabilizers are separable, each transitive orbit has
exact finite Schreier approximations.  The orbitwise criterion in
`gkp-sofic-action-toolkit` therefore makes `Gamma_j action X` sofic.

The groups `Gamma_j` form an increasing union `N_A`.  Increasing-union
permanence for sofic actions gives `N_A action X` sofic.  Finally

```text
H_A=N_A rtimes <A> ~= N_A rtimes Z,
```

and the cyclic-extension theorem proved in the cited ascending-HNN artifact
makes `H_A action X` sofic.  Taking two disjoint identical copies of an
action preserves its orbit approximations, proving the assertion for
`Y=X x {0,1}`.
