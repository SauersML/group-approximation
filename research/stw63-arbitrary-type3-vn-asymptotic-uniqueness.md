title: Every type-III von Neumann codomain has asymptotically unique Jiang--Su embeddings
artifacts:
  - research/artifacts/stw63-z-embedding-audit-2026-08-30.md

Let `M` be an arbitrary type-III von Neumann algebra.  Then any two unital
embeddings

```text
phi,psi:Z->M
```

are asymptotically unitarily equivalent.  No factoriality, sigma-finiteness,
separable-predual, or separable-action hypothesis is imposed on `M`.

This does not assert that `M` is simple as a C-star algebra.  In particular,
proper norm ideals coming from different projection cardinalities may still
exist.  The proof instead uses that each of the two maps is full and applies
classification of full strongly `O_infinity`-stable maps.
