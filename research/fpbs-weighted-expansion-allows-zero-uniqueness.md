The graph with layers of size 2^k and complete bipartite adjacency between
consecutive layers is locally finite and satisfies
|boundary A| >= (3/5) sum_{v in A} deg(v). Nevertheless every positive
Bernoulli parameter has a cofinite unique infinite cluster almost surely,
so p_c=p_u=0. This graph has no unimodular probability law on its rootings.
Section 4 proves all statements. It refutes the expansion-only shortcut
for critical quotients, not the Cayley conjecture or a hypothesis retaining
the genuine unimodular critical-quotient law.
