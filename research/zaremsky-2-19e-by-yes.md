The question "Does Q embed in 2V?" is answered affirmatively by
`rationals-embed-in-brin-thompson-group-2v`: the additive group of the
rationals embeds in the Brin–Thompson group 2V.

Credit: Kojima and Sheng, arXiv:2603.18410, Theorem 1.2 (v3, May 23, 2026). The
graph's proof route is an explicit mixed-radix odometer construction. It was
unreviewed when landed on 2026-09-13.
