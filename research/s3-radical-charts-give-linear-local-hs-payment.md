Fix one predicate `f` and its three finite crossed-product charts `R_i` from
`three-radical-twist-crossed-products-have-uniform-scale`.  Let `q` be the
normalized Hilbert mass of the marked forbidden selector sectors after fixed
packet exactification.  Suppose three matrix transport words compare the
three `R_i` charts through their shared `K_f` endpoint restrictions, with the
endpoint type marginals identified by the common-subgroup alignments.

There are constants `c_f,C_f>0`, depending only on these finite tables, such
that their total normalized-HS transport energy satisfies

```text
E_transport >= c_f q-C_f eta,                                  (RLP1)
```

where `eta` is the normalized weighted type mass discarded by packet
exactification and overlap alignment.  In an asymptotic microstate sequence,
`eta=o(1)`, so

```text
E_transport >= c_f q-o(1).                                    (RLP2)
```

Indeed, on every forbidden simple fiber the three restriction matrices are
the three perfect matchings of the four radical characters.  Their common
endpoint marginals satisfy `(RNS1)`.  Therefore the sum of the three failed
matching masses is at least one on the normalized fiber by `(RNS2)`, and at
least `q` after integration over the forbidden carrier.  Up to the discarded
mass, one finite chart consequently has an extensive weighted irreducible
multiplicity mismatch.  Equation `(FGMP1)` charges that mismatch to ordinary
group-generator intertwining energy.  Summing the three fixed tables proves
`(RLP1)`.

This is the relative rank-one groupification **payment theorem**.  It does
not construct the transport words or the cross-level return; those are the
remaining presentation-level obligations.
