Under Problem XIII every strongly self-absorbing algebra is on the
known list: `Z`, UHF of infinite type, `O_∞`, `O_∞ ⊗ UHF`, or `O_2`.
For each `D ≇ O_2` on the list, `Q ⊗ O_∞` is `D`-stable
(`Q ⊗ UHF ≅ Q`; `O_∞ ⊗ O_∞ ≅ O_∞`; everything is `Z`-stable), so the
McDuff characterisation gives a unital embedding
`D → (Q ⊗ O_∞)_ω ∩ (Q ⊗ O_∞)'`, in particular into
`(Q ⊗ O_∞)_ω` — which is Problem XIV(2)'s assertion.  So XIII settles
XIV(2); note the `O_2`-exclusion is K-theoretically forced regardless
(`embeddable-unit-class-non-torsion`).
