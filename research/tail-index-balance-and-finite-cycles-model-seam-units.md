Section 4 computes the two Laurent symbols of a unit and compares the
codimensions of the image of a two-tail subspace. The exponents agree,
leaving a finitary unit times the common shift. Copying a finite table
onto a sufficiently long cycle gives an injective finite general linear
model. The nonempty clopen defect makes the rank-three detector apply.
