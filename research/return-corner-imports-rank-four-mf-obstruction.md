Section 3 applies the printed finite-order defect theorem in
`EL_(nk)(P)`, with `nk>=4`. For `n>=2` elementary flattening and the
first-`k` tower levels embed that group into `EL_n(R_X)`; the scalar
root with indices `1,k+1` becomes `e_12(d)`. For `n=1`, the root with
indices `1,2` in `EL_k(P)` becomes the unit `1+d u^{-1}` after adding
the complementary identity. Restriction of any MF-target map kills
these nontrivial elements. In the positive case ring-LEF supplies
finite models for all general linear groups and their subgroups.
