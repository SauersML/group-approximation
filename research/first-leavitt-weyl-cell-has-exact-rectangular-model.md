ESTABLISHED.  Let `p` be the characteristic of the affine packet, let
`zeta` be a nontrivial `p`th root of unity, and let `X,Z in U(p)` be the
standard Weyl pair

```text
X Z = zeta Z X.                                           (FWC1)
```

On the single Hilbert space `K=C^p tensor C^p`, put

```text
X_src=X tensor 1,          Z_src=Z tensor 1,
X_0  =X tensor 1,          Z_0  =Z tensor 1,
X_1  =1 tensor X,          Z_1  =1 tensor Z,
U_0  =1,                   U_1  =SWAP.                    (FWC2)
```

Then exactly:

```text
[X_i,Z_j]=zeta^(delta_(i,j)) 1,
[X_0,X_1]=[Z_0,Z_1]=1,
U_i X_src U_i^*=X_i,
U_i Z_src U_i^*=Z_i.                                    (FWC3)
```

The reverse transports are `U_i^*`, so both diagonal return laws are exact.
Every noncentral Pauli word in either packet has normalized trace zero on
this fixed central-phase sector.  The
source packet generates

```text
M_p tensor 1_p
```

and pays for its one Weyl label factor with multiplicity `p`; the two-child
packet generates all of `M_(p^2)`.  The common central mark is the scalar
`zeta 1`, hence remains a fixed positive normalized-HS distance from `1`.

This is the concrete first-level instance of `rectangular-escape` for the
depth-zero and depth-one packets in
`leavitt-prefixes-form-exponential-heisenberg-packet`.  It satisfies exactly
all data obtainable from:

1. the source and two-child finite Heisenberg multiplication tables;
2. the two branch transport and return marginals;
3. the diagonal and crossed commutator table; and
4. the phase-sector trace tests on those Pauli words.

If literal canonical trace on the finite packet is required, take the left
regular representation of the finite group generated by the two Weyl pairs
and `SWAP`.  Its nontrivial central-character summands are amplifications of
the model `(FWC2)`, while the full regular trace vanishes on every
nonidentity packet word.  Thus fixed canonical trace side tests do not remove
the rectangular sector; they only prescribe its Plancherel weight.

Consequently no scalar recurrence can follow from that one-cell packet.
The next relation must identify the **same finite multiplicity reservoir**
after a mixed return; adding more marginal branch/return tests does not do so.

There is a finite analytic certificate for the missing identification.  The
Pauli words linearly span `M_(p^2)`, while the standard polynomial `s_(2p)`
is not an identity of `M_(p^2)`.  Hence one may fix `2p` target Pauli words
`Y_1,...,Y_(2p)` and a constant

```text
kappa_p=||s_(2p)(Y_1,...,Y_(2p))||_2>0.                  (FWC4)
```

Amitsur--Levitzki gives `s_(2p)=0` on `M_p tensor 1`.  Therefore any
matrix-coordinate mixed return which places these same `Y_j` within `delta`
of the source factor pays

```text
kappa_p <= 2p (2p)! delta                               (FWC5)
```

up to the corresponding target-table decoding error.  The constant depends
only on the fixed field characteristic and not on ambient multiplicity.

There is also a square-summable form, better suited to an HS energy ledger.
For any amplification `B=M_p tensor 1_m` and any contractions
`D_1,...,D_(2p)` carrying the target table,

```text
sum_j dist_2(D_j,B)^2
 >= kappa_p^2/(2p ((2p)!)^2) - O_p(target_error).          (FWC6)
```

Indeed choose the trace-preserving conditional expectation `E_B`, apply
Amitsur--Levitzki to `(E_B D_j)`, telescope the `(2p)!` monomials, and use
Cauchy--Schwarz.  This is the desired dimension-free trace-square floor
**after source confinement**.  It leaves only the mixed-word placement of
the returned target words near that same source factor.

The conclusion `(FWC5)` cannot be obtained from a literal universal
group-word confinement relation.  If, in every tracial representation on a
surviving central phase, the full two-child target algebra were contained in
the source `M_p` factor, it would give a unital embedding
`M_(p^2)->M_p`, which is impossible and would kill the phase in the regular
representation as well.  Thus the required mixed return is necessarily
matrix-coordinate: it must read finite multiplicity/rank (or a non-CE
coordinate moment), not merely impose another exact conjugacy or normalizer
word.
