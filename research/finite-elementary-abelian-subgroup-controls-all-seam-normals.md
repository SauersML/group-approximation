The graph group `G_D` contains `A ~= C_2^(|V|+|E|)` with basis
involutions `a_v,b_e` such that
`normalClosure(a_S b_T)=N(T union inc(S),S)` and
`A intersection N(J,S)=<a_v:v in S,b_e:e in J>`.
Every nontrivial normal subgroup is singly normally generated by an
involution in `A`, with canonical word length at most
`256(2|S|+|J|)ell^2`.

Every quotient and its MF reflection has a single-relator description
relative to `G_D`. Any family of relators has the same normal closure
as a subfamily of size at most `|V|+|E|`, giving the same bound for
ordinary and MF consequence witnesses inside this fixed group.
