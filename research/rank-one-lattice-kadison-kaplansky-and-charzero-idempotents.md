Let `Gamma` be a torsion-free lattice in a simple Lie group of real rank
one with finitely many connected components. Then

1. the reduced group C-star algebra `C_r^*(Gamma)` has no idempotents
   other than `0` and `1`; and
2. for every field `k` of characteristic zero, the group algebra
   `k[Gamma]` has no idempotents other than `0` and `1`.

The first statement is the Kadison--Kaplansky conclusion. The second is
Kaplansky's idempotent conjecture for this class in characteristic zero.
No conclusion in positive characteristic is asserted.
