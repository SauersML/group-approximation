Let C=Z<s,a,t,b | ts=ba=1, ta=bs=0>, with NO relation st+ab=1.
For G=EL_4(C), every homomorphism from G into any tracial matrix
ultraproduct is trivial. Equivalently every homomorphism to any
hyperlinear group is trivial, so Rad_hyp(G)=G. In particular this
nontrivial finitely generated Kazhdan group is nonhyperlinear.
