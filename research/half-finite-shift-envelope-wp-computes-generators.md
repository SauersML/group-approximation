**ESTABLISHED** by `half-finite-shift-envelope-wp-computes-generators-proof`.

Let `gamma_1, ..., gamma_k in Sym(N)`, and put

```text
G = < gamma_1, ..., gamma_k, sl, flip, (1 2) >  <=  Sym(N),              (HFW1)
```

with generating set `X` consisting of these `k+3` permutations and their
inverses (`sl` and `flip` as in `half-finite-generators-with-e2-are-strongly-shift-similar`).
No support condition on the `gamma_i` is needed for this claim.

Suppose the word problem of `G` over `X` is decidable in non-decreasing time
`T`. Let `D : N -> N` be non-decreasing with `gamma_i(x) <= D(x)` for all
`i` and all `x`. Then there is an absolute constant `C` such that, for every `i`
and `n`, the value `gamma_i(n)` can be computed from `i` and `n` in time

```text
C * D(n+3)^2 * ( T( C * D(n+3)^2 ) + D(n+3)^2 ).                           (HFW2)
```

The algorithm makes at most `C * D(n+3)^2` word-problem queries, each on a word
of length at most `C * D(n+3)^2`.

**Consequence for hardness.** If some `gamma_i` has a linear displacement bound
`gamma_i(x) <= K*x + K`, and its evaluation map `n -> gamma_i(n)` (with `n` in
unary) lies outside `F(R)` for `R(n) = (n+3)^2 * ( T((n+3)^2) + (n+3)^2 )` (notation of
`uniform-wp-bound-excludes-bh-universal-targets`), then the word problem of `G`
lies outside `F(T)` for every finite generating set.

No novelty is claimed. The argument is conjugation of transpositions, spelled
out with explicit bounds.
