Let `G` be any group and `A in M_n(Z[G])` self-adjoint. Let `Gamma_A` be the
graph on `G x [n]` whose edges are the nonzero off-diagonal entries of `r_A`,
with maximum degree `Delta`. Let `T_(k,k,k)` be the tree with a center and
three legs of `k` edges.

Suppose that for some `k >= 1`, `Gamma_A` has no subgraph (not necessarily
induced) isomorphic to `T_(k,k,k)`. Then:

1. **Growth.** Every sphere of radius `r >= 2k` in `Gamma_A` has at most
   `Delta^(2k)` vertices.
2. **Stabilizers.** Each component `C` has a stabilizer `K_C` that is finite or
   virtually infinite cyclic.
3. **Decomposition.** There are self-adjoint `A_C in M_(J_C)(Z[K_C])`, one for
   each orbit of components, such that `mu_A = sum_C mu_(A_C)`. The sets `J_C`
   partition `[n]`, and spectral measures are taken for the trace
   `sum_i tau(X_ii)`.
4. **Consequences.** `det_N(G) r_(Q(A)) >= 1` for every nonzero `Q in Z[x]`.
   Eigenvalues of `r_A` are algebraic integers, and conjugates carry equal
   mass.

**Norm criterion.** The hypothesis holds whenever `||r_|A||| < 3/sqrt 2`, where
`|A|` takes absolute values of coefficients.
- `rho(T_(k,k,k))` increases to `3/sqrt 2`.
- A tree subgraph `T` gives `rho(T) <= ||r_|A|||`.

For nonnegative `A`, this extends the Serre range from norm at most `2` to norm
below `2.1213...`.

**Sharpness.** For every finitely generated `H` and `eps > 0`, some nonnegative
integral matrix over `H` has norm below `3/sqrt 2 + eps` and a connected graph
with stabilizer `H` (`cubic-serre-violations-transfer-to-norm-near-3-over-sqrt2`).
So the constant cannot be raised for the structural conclusions 1--3.
