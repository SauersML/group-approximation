`K_0(R^ω) ≅ R` is divisible and `K_1(R^ω) = 0`, so the normalised form of
the UCT for `A` says precisely that `α : KK(A, R^ω) → Hom(K_0(A), R)` is
an isomorphism.  Nothing else is used.
