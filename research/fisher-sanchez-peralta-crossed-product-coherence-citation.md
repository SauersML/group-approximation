Fisher and Sánchez-Peralta, *Division rings for group algebras of virtually
compact special groups and 3-manifold groups*, Journal of Combinatorial
Algebra 10 (2026), 153--193, DOI 10.4171/JCA/89, Corollary 1.6
(Corollary 4.5), proves exactly the target.

The paper defines the relevant class as fundamental groups of finite
reducible two-complexes without proper powers. Its result is for arbitrary
crossed products over arbitrary division rings, not merely ordinary group
algebras over fields. The paper explicitly notes that the new input removes
the characteristic-zero restriction in the virtually compact special case.
