**ESTABLISHED** by [[vertex-division-closure-escapes-coset-span-proof]].

Let `K` be a subfield of `C` closed under complex conjugation, `C <= A` groups,
and suppose `A` contains an element `s` that normalizes `C` and has infinite
order in `N_A(C)/C`. Then, inside `U(A)`,

```text
(1 + s)^(-1) in D_A,   but   (1 + s)^(-1) not in span_(D_C)(A).
```

So `D_A` is not contained in the free left `D_C`-module
`span_(D_C)(A) = (+)_(Cg) D_C g` of
[[subgroup-cosets-are-independent-over-division-closure]]. The hypothesis is
met whenever `A` contains `C x Z` with `C` the first factor, so it is met for
every edge group, property (T) included.

## Consequence for the coproduct reduction

`R = D_A *_(D_C) D_B` of [[atiyah-amalgam-rank-from-cohn-coproduct]] is
generated by `D_A` and `D_B`, not by group elements. By the above, a generating
set of `R` already escapes the `D_C`-coset decomposition of `l^2(G)`. So the
methods that the Bass--Serre tree supplies -- orthogonality of coset subspaces,
translation length, finite-support normal forms in `K[G]` -- see no more of `R`
than the subring `span_(D_C)(G)`, and cannot decide injectivity of
`phi: R -> U(G)`, let alone the maximality statement
[[kazhdan-edge-coproduct-rank-is-inner-rank]].

This is an obstruction to a method. It neither proves nor refutes any Strong
Atiyah statement, and it is independent of property (T): the escape happens
already for an amenable edge, where the conjecture is known by other means.
