Established by Fournier-Facio--Gerasimova--Spaas, *Local Hilbert--Schmidt
stability*, arXiv:2307.13155 (published in J. Algebra 2025).  Their final
property-`(T)` theorem states that an infinite hyperlinear property-`(T)`
group is not locally Hilbert--Schmidt stable.  This is the local conclusion
used by the target.

Becker--Lubotzky, *Group stability and Property (T)*, arXiv:1809.00632,
proved the earlier classical (non-local) normalized-HS instability theorem.
It does **not** by itself justify the word "locally" in the target; the FFGS
strengthening is essential.  Neither result rules out flexible HS stability.
