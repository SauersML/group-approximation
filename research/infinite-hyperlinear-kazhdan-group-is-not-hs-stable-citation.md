Established by Becker and Lubotzky, *Group stability and Property (T)*,
arXiv:1809.00632.  Their main theorem states that an infinite hyperlinear
property-(T) group is not stable with respect to `U(n)` with normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt metric.  The paper treats flexible stability separately;
only the strict conclusion is imported here.
