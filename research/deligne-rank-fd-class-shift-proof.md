Artifact Proposition 2.2.
1. **Tensoring.** For an `alpha'`-model `rho` and an exact `beta`-projective representation `pi` of dimension `e`,
   the defect operator of `rho (x) pi` for `alpha' beta` equals (old defect operator) `(x) beta(g,h) pi(gh)`. Its
   rank is `e` times the old rank, so the normalized defect is unchanged.
2. **Inverse class.** The contragredient of `pi` realizes `beta^(-1)`.
3. **Cohomologous cocycles.** Rescaling each `rho(g)` by `lambda(g)` turns an `alpha'`-model into an
   `alpha' . delta(lambda)`-model with the same defect.
