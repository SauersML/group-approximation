Let `Gamma < Lambda`, let `Gamma curvearrowright (X,mu)` be pmp, and let
`Lambda curvearrowright (Y,nu)` be its pmp coinduction.  Inside

```text
M = L^infinity(Y) rtimes Lambda
```

the algebra generated by the distinguished `Gamma`-coset coordinate
`A_0 ~= L^infinity(X)` and the canonical unitaries `{u_g:g in Gamma}` is
trace-preservingly isomorphic to the **faithful** crossed product

```text
A_0 rtimes Gamma ~= L^infinity(X) rtimes Gamma.          (CR1)
```

It is not the Feldman--Moore algebra of the orbit relation when the source
action has stabilizers.  In particular, for the free-group realization of the
Manzoor non-cohyperlinear IRS, `(CR1)` is Connes embeddable and its algebraic
map onto the non-CE relation algebra remains singular.  Coinduction to a
larger actor therefore does not provide the canonical inclusion required by
the relation-to-group compiler; any successful inclusion in the ambient
coinduced crossed product would have to be genuinely non-coordinate and use
additional structure of `Lambda`.
