Let `G` admit a finite model for `E_VC G`. By `finite-evc-model-implies-fp-and-bvc`, `G` is
finitely presented and has BVC. By `finitely-presented-bvc-groups-are-virtually-cyclic`,
`G` is virtually cyclic.
