Soficity of `V action V/Gamma`, soficity of `V`, and GKP permanence give
canonical permutation microstates of the binary restricted wreath.  If their
actor restrictions were flexibly exactifiable, Kazhdan averaging would round
the root lamp into the commutant of the exact `Gamma` representation.
Transport by the exact compressor and apply finite-dimensional commutant
no-growth.  A fixed element of `Gamma\tGamma t^(-1)` would then fix the
transported lamp, contradicting canonical separation of the two distinct
coordinate lamps.  Failure of a uniform distance floor would yield a
correctable subsequence, so the obstruction is uniform after subselection.
