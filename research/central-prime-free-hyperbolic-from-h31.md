## Why sufficient

Take `H=H_31` and `p=7`. The first prerequisite proves that `H_31`
is non-elementary and word-hyperbolic, and that vanishing of
`H^2(F;F_7)` for every finite quotient is equivalent to all finite
image centers having order prime to `7`. The second prerequisite is
exactly that uniform vanishing statement. Together they give the
required concrete input.

The uniform vanishing prerequisite remains open. Neither the known
hyperbolicity and property (T) of `H_31` nor the proved first-kernel
cohomology calculation removes it.
