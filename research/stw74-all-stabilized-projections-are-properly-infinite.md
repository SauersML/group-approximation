title: The LXXIV hypothesis makes every stabilized projection properly infinite
---

Let `A` be unital and simple, and suppose every nonzero projection in `A` is
infinite.  Then every nonzero projection in `A tensor K` is properly infinite.

Thus the unresolved part of Problem LXXIV is invisible not only to `V(A)` but
to the entire stabilized projection semigroup `V(A tensor K)`.
