Let

```text
A=C*_r(F_2) tensor Z,       D=A_omega intersect A',
I=J_tau intersect D,        E=J_(tau_Z) intersect F(Z).
```

Let `Theta` be the family of all unital homomorphisms `theta:F(Z)->D`, with
no trace-preservation hypothesis, and define

```text
K_Z^sat=Ideal_D({theta(E):theta in Theta}).
```

Then

```text
K_Z subset K_Z^sat subset I,
```

where `K_Z` is the ideal from
`stw93-jiang-su-slice-ideal-is-quasitrace-null`.  Every normalized
`2`-quasitrace on `D` annihilates `(K_Z^sat)_+`, and pullback along the
quotient map induces a bijection

```text
QT_1(D/K_Z^sat) ~= QT_1(D).
```

Consequently every singular quasitrace witness to a negative answer to XCIII
survives after quotienting by `K_Z^sat`, and the remaining obstruction is
exactly annihilation of `I/K_Z^sat` by quasitraces on `D/K_Z^sat`.

This strengthens the unconditional localization of the XCIII obstruction
without asserting that `K_Z^sat=I` or that the root is solved.
