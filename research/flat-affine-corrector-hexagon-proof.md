Reduction modulo two is an equivariant homomorphism of `A_2` coweight
modules, hence gives a homomorphism of the corresponding affine Weyl
semidirect products.  Every triangle, inverse, Weyl conjugacy, and Coxeter
hexagon word therefore evaluates to the identity.  A nonzero coweight still
maps to a nonzero translation, which moves the base lamp to a distinct
commuting coordinate.  The finite regular representation gives overlap
`1/4`.  Conjugating a coefficient element outside the translation vector's
stabilizer produces the explicit affine tail `(FAC10)`, locating the first
relation omitted by this flat model.
