title: Stable rank one of finite simple real rank zero algebras gives their weak cancellation
target: finite-simple-rr0-algebras-have-weak-cancellation

If `tsr(C) = 1`, then `tsr(M_n(C)) = 1` for all `n` (Rieffel's matrix formula).
Unital C\*-algebras of stable rank one have cancellation of projections: in a
unital algebra of stable rank one, `p direct_sum r ~ q direct_sum r` implies
`p ~ q` (Rieffel; see also Rordam, Acta 2003, page 29: "It is known that all
C\*-algebras of stable rank one have the cancellation property").  Applied in
`M_n(C)` for large `n`, this gives cancellation in `V(C)`, and cancellation
implies weak cancellation.
