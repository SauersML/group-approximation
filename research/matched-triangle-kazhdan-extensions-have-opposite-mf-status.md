For one fixed infinite minimal binary system `Y`, let
`Q=EL_36(LC(Y,F_2) semidirect Z)` and `L=SL_infinity(F_2)`.
There are two groups, each with exact sequence
`1 -> L^3 -> G -> Q^3 -> 1`, property (T), three torsion generators
of orders `7,2,2`, no nontrivial finite quotients, and the same
18-element normal lattice. Both are centerless and directly
indecomposable.

For the directed-cycle triangle the group is LEF and its MF radical
is trivial. For the acyclic triangle its MF radical is the full
kernel `L^3`, so it is non-MF. The common quotient here is the
endpoint quotient; their maximal MF quotients are different.
