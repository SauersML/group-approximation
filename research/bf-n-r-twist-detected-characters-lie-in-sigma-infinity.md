title: "A character of bF_{n,r} whose braid part is nonzero on large full twists lies in Sigma^infinity"
---

For `n >= 2`, `r >= 1`: let `chi = pi^* psi + phi` with `psi` any character of
`F_{n,r}` and `phi` in the invariant braid characters `W` (see
`pure-braided-bht-braid-characters-dimension-n-n-minus-1`). If the full-twist
value `tau_phi(k)` is not zero for all large `k`, then `[chi]` lies in
`Sigma^infinity(bF_{n,r})`.

For `n = 2` this covers every character with a nonzero braid component (the
case `n = 2`, `r = 1` is Case 1 of the proof of Zaremsky's Theorem 5.1,
arXiv:1803.02717). For `n >= 3` it misses exactly the braid components in the
`(n(n-1) - 2)`-dimensional full-twist-invisible subspace `W_0`.
