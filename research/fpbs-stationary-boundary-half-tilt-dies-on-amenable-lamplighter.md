**ESTABLISHED.** Proof in
`fpbs-stationary-half-tilt-lamplighter-proof`.
Elementary; unreviewed. Notation `Delta`, `phi_lambda`, `chi^nu_lambda`,
`p^nu_t`, `h_nu`, `kappa_p(n)` is that of
`fpbs-boundary-tilted-susceptibility-kernel`.

**The group.** `L = Z/2 wr Z = (⊕_Z Z/2) ⋊ Z`, with elements `(f,z)`, product
`(f,z)(f',z') = (f + tau_z f', z + z')` and `(tau_z f)(y) = f(y - z)`. Put
`a = (1_0, 0)` (switch the lamp at the head) and `t = (0,1)`.
`Lambda = L x Z`, with the central factor generated by `u`, and
`S = {a, t^(±1), u^(±1)}`. `H = Cay(Lambda,S)`.

**The boundary.** `Z = {0,1}^Z`, with `Lambda` acting by
`(f,z)·omega = f + tau_z omega` and `u` acting trivially. Let `mu` be the law
of `a^(b) t^(e) a^(b')` with `b, b'` fair bits and `e = +1` with probability
`3/4`, `e = -1` with probability `1/4`, all independent. The walk
`X_n = (f_n, z_n)` has a.s. limiting lamps `Phi = lim f_n`, and `nu` is the
law of `Phi`.

**Theorem.**

1. **(A stationary boundary.)** `nu` is `mu`-stationary (`nu = sum_g mu(g) g_* nu`),
   and quasi-invariant under `Lambda`. For every `n >= 1`,

   ```text
   P(X_n = g | Phi = zeta) = mu^(*n)(g) Delta_zeta(o,g)    for nu-a.e. zeta.
   ```

   So `nu` is the hitting law of the walk on its lamp boundary, and
   `Delta_zeta(o,.)` is the Doob transform density.
2. **(Linear information.)** For every `n >= 1`,

   ```text
   I(X_n ; Phi) = H(X_n) - H(X_n | Phi) >= (log 2)(n/2 + 1) - log(2n+1) - 3.3 .
   ```

3. **(Positive Radon–Nikodym growth.)** `nu(h_nu >= (log 2)/12) > 0`.
4. **(The kill.)** `H` is amenable, `gr(H) >= sqrt 2` and `p_c(H) <= 1/2`. So
   `p^nu_t(H) = p_c(H)`: `chi^nu_(1/2)(p) = infinity` for every `p > p_c(H)`,
   although `nu` is stationary, quasi-invariant, has positive growth on a set
   of positive measure, and `pi_nu` is weakly contained in the regular
   representation (Hulanicki, as in item 6 of the tilt claim).
5. **(Universal ceiling, every Cayley graph.)** Let `G` be any Cayley graph and
   `nu` any quasi-invariant probability on a `Gamma`-space. If
   `nu(h_nu >= h) > 0` then

   ```text
   p^nu_t(G) <= exp(-h/2).
   ```

   On `T_d` with the visual measure (`h_nu = log(d-1)` everywhere) this is
   the exact value `p^nu_t = (d-1)^(-1/2)` of item 5 of the tilt claim, so the
   ceiling is sharp.
6. **(Constraint on [BT] and on the l2 threshold.)** Put
   `h^*(nu) = sup{h : nu(h_nu >= h) > 0}`.
   - A boundary can witness [BT] only if `h^*(nu) < 2 log(1/p_c(G))`.
   - If `pi_nu ≺ lambda_Gamma`, then `p_(2->2)(G) <= p^nu_t(G) <= exp(-h^*(nu)/2)`.
   - If `nu` is `mu`-stationary for a probability `mu` with
     `supp mu ⊆ B_L` whose support generates `Gamma` as a semigroup, and
     `Phi` is a boundary map with the conditional law of item 1, then
     `h^*(nu) >= E_nu h_nu >= limsup_n I(X_n;Phi)/(L n)`, so
     `p^nu_t <= exp(-limsup_n I(X_n;Phi)/(2 L n))`.

**Class killed (item 4).** Arguments for [BT], or for
`chi^nu_(1/2)(p) < infinity` at one `p > p_c`, whose inputs are:

- (a) facts valid on every Cayley graph with `gr > 1` and `p_c < 1`;
- (b) the properties of `(Gamma,Z,nu)` killed by the Cauchy example, except
  that Radon–Nikodym growth is only positive on a set of positive measure
  (quasi-invariance, cocycle identities, tilted mass transport, duality,
  log-convexity, Zimmer-amenability, `pi_nu ≺ lambda`); and, in addition,
- (c) `nu` is `mu`-stationary for a finitely supported `mu` whose support
  generates the group as a semigroup, `nu` is the hitting law of the
  `mu`-walk on a boundary, positive mutual information growth
  `I(X_n;Phi) >= c n`, and `Delta_zeta(o,.)` is the Doob-transform density of
  the conditioned walk.

Run on `(H, Z, nu)` every input holds and the conclusion fails. **The step
where it dies** is the passage from these inputs to finiteness of
`sum_y tau_p(o,y) phi_(1/2)(y)` above `p_c`. There `tau_p >= theta(p)^2` on
`H`, and `phi_(1/2)` is not summable.

**What survives.** The survivor recorded in [BT], "stationarity or
harmonicity of `nu`", is removed. A proof of [BT] must use nonamenability of
`Gamma` itself, for example through `rho < 1`, Kesten, or `pi_nu ≺ lambda`
combined with a spectral gap. It cannot use only properties of the boundary.
Item 5 also shows the boundary must have small growth,
`h^* < 2 log(1/p_c)`. A boundary whose Radon–Nikodym growth reaches
`2 log(1/p_c)` on a set of positive measure cannot witness [BT] at all.

**Scope.** `mu` is not symmetric. The kill therefore does not cover an
argument that needs `nu` to be stationary for a *symmetric* `mu`. The natural
symmetric instance is the switch-walk-switch walk on `Z/2 wr Z^3`, which is
non-Liouville by `random-walk-separators-of-boone-higman`. Its unfrozen
window is not an interval, so Step 2 of the proof does not transfer as
written. The Kaimanovich–Vershik identification of
`I(X_n; bnd)/n` with the Avez entropy is not imported, and is not used.
Item 2 is proved by counting lamps.
