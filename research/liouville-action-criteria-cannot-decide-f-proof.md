Notation is as in the claim. "Theorem A" and "Corollaries 2–4" refer to
`amenably-coupled-actions-admit-symmetric-liouville-measures`.

**Item 1.** Theorem A is the first sentence. The listed `F`-actions are LAT:
- *Orbits in `(0,1)`.* This is Corollary 2.
- *Lamplighters.* This is Corollary 4. The group `P_f(X) ⋊ F` is an extension of an abelian group by `F`, so it
  is amenable whenever `F` is.
- *Cosets `F/K` with `F = HK`, `H` amenable.* Every coset is `hkK = hK`, so `H` is transitive on `F/K`.
- *`K ⊇ [F,F]`.* Here `F = HK` with `H ≅ Z²`, as follows.
  - `F/[F,F] ≅ Z²` via `g ↦ (log₂ g′(0⁺), log₂ g′(1⁻))`.
  - Let `a ∈ F` be supported in `[0, 1/2]` with `a′(0⁺) = 2`, for instance the conjugate of `x_0⁻¹` by the
    affine rescaling of `[0,1]` onto `[0,1/2]`, extended by the identity. Let `b ∈ F` be supported in `[1/2, 1]`
    with `b′(1⁻) = 2`, built the same way.
  - `a` and `b` have disjoint supports, so `H = ⟨a, b⟩` is abelian. Its image in `Z²` is generated by `(1, 0)`
    and `(0, 1)`, so `H[F,F] = F` and `F = HK`.
  - (`x_0⁻¹` has slope 2 at `0` and slope `1/2` at `1`. With `σ(t) = t/2`, the map `σx_0⁻¹σ⁻¹` on `[0,1/2]`,
    extended by the identity on `[1/2,1]`, has slope 2 at `0⁺`. Its breakpoints are dyadic, including one at
    `1/2`, and its slopes are powers of 2, so it lies in `F`. For `b`, conjugate by `t ↦ (t+1)/2` instead. Since
    `x_0⁻¹` has slope 2 at `0` and `x_0` has slope 2 at `1`, the conjugate of `x_0` has slope 2 at `1⁻`.)

**Item 2.**
- If `F` is amenable, then for every `F`-set and every finite `Y`, `H = F` works, because each transitive
  `F`-set is one `F`-orbit. So "not LAT" implies "`F` is not amenable".
- By item 1, "no non-degenerate Liouville measure" implies "not LAT".
- *Regular action.* `F` acts on itself by left multiplication, so `H`-orbits are right cosets `Hy_0`. If `Y = {e, x_0, x_1} ⊆ Hy_0`, then `x_0 y_0⁻¹, x_1y_0⁻¹, y_0⁻¹ ∈ H`. So `x_0, x_1 ∈ H`
  and `H = F`. Hence the regular action is LAT iff `F` is amenable.
- The regular action has no non-degenerate Liouville measure iff `F` is not amenable:
  - one direction is Theorem A;
  - the other is the Kaimanovich–Vershik statement quoted in `juschenko-zheng-liouville-actions` ("this will
    insure that there is no non-degenerate Liouville measure on the group itself, thus, by renowned result of
    Kaimanovich and Vershik, [6], the group is not amenable").

**Item 3.**
- `T` is Corollary 3.
- `P_f(F_2) ⋊ F_2` is Corollary 4.
- `SL_2(R_alg)` is `juschenko-zheng-question-5-fails-for-countable-groups`.
- For `F`, Corollaries 2 and 4 give Liouville measures for `F ↷ D` and `P_f(D) ⋊ F ↷ P_f(D)` without any
  hypothesis. Extensive amenability of `F ↷ D` is equivalent to the root by
  `thompson-f-amenable-iff-dyadic-action-extensively-amenable`. Amenability of the lamplighter action is
  equivalent to that extensive amenability by `affine-lamplighter-amenable-iff-extensively-amenable`.
- So no implication "Liouville ⟹ (amenable action, EA, or amenable group)" holds on the actions in the class.
  In particular none holds for these two actions of `F`, whose Liouville property is a theorem, independent of
  the root. ∎
