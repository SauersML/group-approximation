An internal identity outside `Q` has length at least ten.  Spending the 30
corners among six internal faces and one nonempty outer face gives 52
designated budgets, every one with an internal digon.  Contract all such
digons topologically and compare with the complete six-, four-, and
two-vertex cubic core partitions.  Thirteen budgets survive: twelve even
budgets with one required length-ten identity, and the one odd budget stated
in the target.

The even budgets lie in five partitions of the already complete 276-map
area-ten atlas.  Exhaust their 88 exact maps, all valid orientations, and all
dipole-admissible relator colors.  Exactly 84 cyclic length-ten words can be
the missing internal identity; exact free-factor moved-vector certificates
show all 84 are nontrivial.  Hence only the odd budget remains.
