Notation: `G = K_0(D)`, `E: A -> D` the faithful conditional expectation, and
`Sigma = G^+ / ~` for the action of `Z` through `alpha_*`.  Steps (P1)--(P4)
follow `simple-af-integer-crossed-products-dichotomy-proof`; here are the
changes.

**Hypotheses of the lemma.**
* `G` is a dimension group, so (R) and (P) hold.
* (ND) holds by condition 3 of `af-k0-order-divisible-iff-no-elementary-subquotient`.
* Closed ideals of an AF algebra correspond bijectively and equivariantly to
  order ideals of `K_0` (Elliott).  So `alpha_*` leaves no order ideal other than
  `0` and `G` invariant: the action is minimal.

So `coinvariant-tarski-lemma-minimal-nowhere-scattered` applies, and `Sigma` is
simple and conical by its Step 1.

**(P1) Simplicity and compression.**  `A` is simple because `alpha` is minimal
and properly outer (Kishimoto; Olesen--Pedersen, Theorem 7.2).  Let `a in A_+`
be nonzero.  Rordam's Lemma 5.4 in *The real rank of certain simple
C\*-algebras* (2005) is stated for an arbitrary C\*-algebra with all nonzero
powers properly outer.  Its proof uses Kishimoto's Lemma 3.2 to give `h in D_+`
and `lambda` with `||hah - hE(a)h|| < lambda < ||hE(a)h||`.  The hereditary
subalgebra of `D` generated by `(hE(a)h - lambda)_+` contains a nonzero
projection, since `D` is AF.  By Rordam's Lemma 5.1, `her_A(a)` contains an
isomorphic copy of that hereditary subalgebra, implemented by the polar
decomposition of `x = a^(1/2) h s`.  So `her_A(a)` contains a projection
equivalent in `A` to a nonzero projection of `D`.

**(P2)** is unchanged: `[p] |-> [p]` defines a monoid map `Sigma -> V(A)` that
is nonzero on nonzero elements and carries infinite elements to classes of
infinite projections.

**(P3) State case.**  Suppose `f` is a state on `Sigma` with `f([x]) = 1`.

* Simplicity of `Sigma` makes `f` finite and faithful.
* `f` pulls back to an `alpha_*`-invariant positive homomorphism
  `beta: G -> R`, faithful on `G^+ \ {0}`.
* For an AF algebra, such a `beta` is the `K_0` map of a unique lower
  semicontinuous trace `tau` finite on projections of `D tensor K`, which is
  densely finite since `D` is the closure of an increasing union of
  finite-dimensional subalgebras.  Uniqueness gives `tau o alpha = tau`.
* The rest is as before: `tau o E` is a nonzero lower semicontinuous trace on
  the simple algebra `A`.  Its null ideal is zero, its domain contains the
  Pedersen ideal, and `A` is stably finite.
* Conversely, an invariant densely finite nonzero trace restricts to a nonzero
  invariant positive homomorphism on `G`, which is a state on `Sigma`
  normalized at some `[x]` with `0 < tau(x) < infinity`.

**(P4) No-state case.**
* By Tarski (Goodearl--Handelman; Rainone, Theorem 4.11), every nonzero
  `theta in Sigma` has `(k+1) theta <= k theta` for some `k`.
* By the lemma (consequences part), `theta` is properly infinite.  So by (P2)
  every nonzero projection of `D tensor K` is infinite in `A tensor K`.
* By (P1), every nonzero hereditary subalgebra of `A` contains an infinite
  projection, which for simple `A` is pure infiniteness.

Exclusivity: a purely infinite algebra is not stably finite.
