Artifact Section 7, Consequence B.

1. **Cocycle on the ultrapower.** Take the cochains `c_n` with defects `-> 0` built in
   `z2-by-f3-bernoulli-lacks-degree-one-cocycle-expansion`: `chi` on the base, marker eigenfunctions on `t_3`.
   On the Loeb ultrapower `L` of `X`, `w(s, [x_n]) = [c_n(s, x_n)]` has Loeb-null relator defects, so it is a cocycle.
2. **The action `Y`.** Let `Y` be a standard model of the separable invariant factor of `L` generated by the sets
   `{w(s, .) = 1}` and the diagonal copy of `X`.
   - `X` is a factor of `Y`, so `Y` is free, and `X ≺ Y` by `abert-weiss-free-actions-weakly-contain-bernoulli`.
   - `Y ≺ X`: Loeb-measurable sets are approximated by internal sets, whose joint translate measures are `U`-limits
     of those in `X`. This is the easy half of Conley–Kechris–Tucker-Drob, recalled.
3. **No untwisting.** If `w = delta F + chi'` on `Y`, write `F = [F_n]` `U`-a.e. Then for `s` among the generators of
   `A_0 = Z^2 x| F(t_1, t_2)`, `mu{F_n(s x) - F_n(x) != chi(s) - chi'(s)} -> 0` along `U`.
   - `chi - chi'|_(A_0) != 0`, because `chi` does not extend to `Gamma`.
   - This contradicts the spectral gap estimate (artifact Lemma S, threshold `kappa/(kappa + 16)`).
4. **Rigid side.** `popa-bernoulli-cocycle-superrigidity` gives superrigidity of `X`, with relative (T) as in the
   example's route.
