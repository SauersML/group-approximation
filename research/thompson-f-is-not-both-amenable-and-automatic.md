**OPEN.** Richard Thompson's group `F` is non-amenable, or `F` has no automatic
structure (or both).

This is the consequence named in the parenthetical of Zaremsky Problem 4.1
(`zaremsky-4-01-amenable-automatic-contractible-rips`). It follows from either
disjunct (routes `thompson-f-not-amenable-and-automatic-if-nonamenable` and
`thompson-f-not-amenable-and-automatic-if-not-automatic`) and from a yes answer
to Problem 4.1 (route `thompson-f-not-amenable-and-automatic-via-rips`).
