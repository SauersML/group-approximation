The reflection statements are the adjunction, its limit/colimit formulas,
and the class of morphisms it inverts.  The topology statement identifies
the kernel of the reflection unit intrinsically.

The corona statements follow from rank-germ classification of projections,
finite ideal generation, coordinate compression, and the elementary word
identities defining the ideal-valued support.

The two tower constructions apply the product and restricted-sum radical
formulas to the simple and finitely presented Hilbert-hotel groups.  Normal
subgroup isolation by coordinate commutators gives the Boolean tower, while
Grushko uniqueness separates the finitely presented free-product kernels.
