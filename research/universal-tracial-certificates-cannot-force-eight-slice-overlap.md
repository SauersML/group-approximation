The canonical regular trace of any marked extension containing the
eight-slice packet has a fixed positive deficit between source mass and every
conjugated-capacity overlap, while all defining relators vanish.  Therefore
no universal cyclic SOS, finite positive-definite Hecke inequality, or
finite-level NPA dual certificate can force the desired overlap.  Any valid
actuator must use genuinely matrix-ultraproduct-only information.
