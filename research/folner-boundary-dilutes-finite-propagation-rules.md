Represent site observables diagonally on
`ell^2(F_n) tensor C^q`. Each generator translation is exact wherever its
finite propagation path remains inside `F_n`; complete the resulting partial
permutation arbitrarily on the complement. A word `r` differs from the
identity only at sites in the `K_r`-boundary. Since the pointwise difference
of two unitaries has squared norm at most four, normalized counting gives
`(AFP1)`.

Choose `F_n` Folner for the propagation sets of the first `n` relators. Then
every standard relator vanishes in the ultralimit. Interior averaging of a
positive-density local mark gives `(AFP2)`. For `H=Z`, cyclic or interval
truncations give squared defect `O(1/n)` for each fixed propagation radius.

This firewall does not rule out a matrix-only supercritical multiplicity or
holonomy inequality on one positively dense, coherently returned carrier. It
rules out obtaining that inequality from amenable locality alone.
