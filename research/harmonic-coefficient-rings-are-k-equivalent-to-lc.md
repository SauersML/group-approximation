## Setting

As in `harmonic-elimination-puts-q-in-odometer-crossed-products`:
- `Γ ⊋ Γ_1 ⊋ ...` are finite-index normal subgroups with trivial intersection, and `K = lim Γ/Γ_n`.
- `X` is a compact totally disconnected `Γ`-space, and `π : X -> K` is a continuous `Γ`-map.
- `f = f_K ∘ π` with `f_K(y) = 1/(1 + ℓ(y))`.

Assume that no fibre `π^(-1)(y)` has interior. This holds when `X` is infinite and minimal: then `π` is
onto, and a fibre with interior `W` would give `X = g_1 W ∪ ... ∪ g_k W`, so `π(X)` would be finite.

Let `F ⊆ C(X,Q)` be the `Q`-subalgebra generated by `LC(X,Q)` and all translates `f ∘ γ`, `γ ∈ Γ`.

## Statement

1. **Crossed-product form.** Let `R ⊆ C(X,Q) ⋊_alg Γ` be a unital subring containing `LC(X,Q)`, `f`
   and every `u_γ^(±1)`, and generated by them together with any subset of `LC(X,Q)`. Then
   `R = F ⋊_alg Γ` and `R ∩ C(X,Q) = F`. For `R_T` of `sl3-toeplitz-harmonic-ring-is-simple-and-k1-rational`
   (which contains `LC(X,Q)`, by its part 1 and `Q ⊆ R_T`):
   `R_T = F ⋊_alg SL_3(Z)`.
2. **K-invisibility.** The inclusion `LC(X,Q) -> F` induces isomorphisms `K_n(LC(X,Q)) -> K_n(F)` for
   every `n >= 0`, compatibly with the `Γ`-actions. So `K_n(F) ≅ LC(X,Z) ⊗ K_n(Q)` for
   `n = 0, 1, 2`, and the idempotents of `F` are the clopen indicators.
3. **Local structure.** For a finite `Φ ⊆ Γ`, let `F_Φ` be generated by `LC(X,Q)` and the `f ∘ γ` with
   `γ ∈ Φ`. Then `F_Φ` is the pullback of `LC(X,Q)` and `⊕_(γ ∈ Φ) LC(S_γ, Q)[t]` over
   `⊕_(γ ∈ Φ) LC(S_γ, Q)`, where `S_γ = π^(-1)(γ^(-1) x_∞)` is the singular fibre of `f ∘ γ` and `t`
   records its germ.

## Consequence

The coefficient ring of a harmonic ring carries no `K`-theory beyond that of `LC(X,Q)`. Any difference
between `K_*(F ⋊ Γ)` and `K_*(LC(X,Q) ⋊ Γ)` can therefore come only from the crossed product: twisted
Nil terms of `F` under cyclic subgroups, in the Farrell–Jones description. See
`cantor-crossed-q-rings-need-odd-divisible-coinvariants`.
