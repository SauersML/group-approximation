Let `x_(ij)` be nine involutions, commuting within every row and column.
Write `R_i=product_j x_(ij)` and
`C_j=product_i x_(ij)`.  Put source involution ports on one transverse pair
of lines and make the other four line products scalar:

```text
R_1=Z,       C_1=W,
R_2=alpha_2, R_3=alpha_3,
C_2=beta_2,  C_3=beta_3,                               (TPH1)
```

where all `alpha_i,beta_j` are scalar signs.  Define the fixed reordering
holonomy

```text
Omega=(C_1 C_2 C_3)^(-1)(R_1 R_2 R_3),                 (TPH2)
kappa=alpha_2 alpha_3 beta_2 beta_3.
```

Here each `R_i,C_j` in `(TPH2)` denotes its three-cell word before
substituting `(TPH1)`.  Then

```text
Omega=kappa W Z,
[W,Z]=W Z W Z=Omega^2.                                  (TPH3)
```

Moreover `Omega` belongs to the derived subgroup of the group generated by
the nine cell involutions: the row-major and column-major products have the
same image in the abelianization because every cell occurs once in each.

Consequently, in every realization whose cell-generated image has central
derived subgroup, `WZ` is central and the two source ports commute.  This
covers all extraspecial/Pauli packet realizations; in an irreducible Pauli
sector the holonomy is merely a scalar sign.  Therefore no fixed
class-two Pauli completion of a single transverse two-port square can absorb
an arbitrary noncommuting pair.

The identity is robust as a fixed-word estimate.  If the six line products
have normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt defects `eta_(R_i),eta_(C_j)` from the
targets in `(TPH1)`, then

```text
||Omega-kappa WZ||_2
 <= sum_i eta_(R_i)+sum_j eta_(C_j),

||Omega^2-[W,Z]||_2
 <= 2(sum_i eta_(R_i)+sum_j eta_(C_j)).                 (TPH4)
```

Thus the transverse square is not a transparent Pauli absorber: it quotients
the source pair to commuting ports in every class-two sector.  The only live
use of this cell is to realize `Omega` noncentrally and then control its
square with a dimension-independent estimate.  Establishing such a
noncentral holonomy in the perfect commuting source while excluding its
finite-dimensional escape is the exact two-port decoder gate; `(TPH3)` alone
does not provide that gap.
