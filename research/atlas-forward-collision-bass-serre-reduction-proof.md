The displayed presentations give `|F|=18`, `|A|=9`, `|C|=144`, `|K|=6`
and `|B|=2`.  Both edge maps are injective: `A=<r,z>` is the complement
`C3^2` in `C`, while `B=<b>` is generated by the shared involution, and
`b=s t` is an involution in `K`.  Bass--Serre theory therefore identifies
the universal pre-collision group with the fundamental group `(A4-BS-1)` of
a tree of finite groups.  Such a group is virtually free.  The usual Euler
characteristic formula for a finite graph of finite groups gives

```text
(1/18+1/144+1/6)-(1/9+1/2)=-55/144.
```

The negative value proves that the group is infinite.

Restriction of a finite group's regular representation to a subgroup is the
subgroup regular representation repeated by the index.  Hence

```text
(1120k Reg(F))|_A = 2240k Reg(A)
                  = (140k Reg(C))|_A,
(140k Reg(C))|_B  = 10080k Reg(B)
                  = (3360k Reg(K))|_B.
```

After unitary identifications along the two edges, the universal properties
of the amalgams give the asserted exact `G0` representation.

For collision, group the final two letters `s t` as `b`.  In
`D *_B K`, where `D=F *_A C`, the word becomes `(A4-BS-3)`.  The three
`K` syllables `t,s,t^-1` lie outside `B`.  The `D` syllable `c` lies outside
`B`: it is in `F-A`, whereas `B` lies in `C` and `A intersect B=1`.  Finally
`c b c` is an `F-C-F` reduced word in `F *_A C`, since `c in F-A` and
`b in C-A`; it cannot lie in `C`, hence cannot lie in `B`.  Thus all six
alternations are reduced.  The first syllable is in `K-B` and the last in
`D-B`, so the word is cyclically reduced.  The amalgam normal-form theorem
proves that it is nontrivial and not conjugate into a vertex group, hence is
hyperbolic on the Bass--Serre tree.  In particular it is not in the normal
`C2^4` subgroup of `C`.

Lastly two cyclic conjugates of `q` begin with the identical `D` syllable
`c`.  This makes `c` a relative piece of syllable length one.  Since
`|q|=6`, strict relative `C'(1/6)` would require `1<6/6`, which is false.
No stronger small-cancellation or vertex-injection conclusion is used.
