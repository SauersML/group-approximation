**ESTABLISHED (import).**

**Statement.** Every countable subgroup of `GL(n,R)`, for `R` any commutative ring, has finite decomposition complexity, and countable groups with finite decomposition complexity have Property A. So finitely generated subgroups of `GL_n(F_q[t^{±1}])` and of `GL_n` over finite products of such rings are exact.

**Source.** E. Guentner, R. Tessera and G. Yu, *Discrete groups with finite decomposition complexity*, Groups Geom. Dyn. 7 (2013), 377–402, doi:10.4171/GGD/186, p. 378.
