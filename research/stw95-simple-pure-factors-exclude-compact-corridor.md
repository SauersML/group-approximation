title: Simple pure factors cannot realize the compact-bearing corridor for STW Problem XCV
---

Let `A` and `B` be nonzero simple pure C-star algebras.  No irreducible
representation `pi:A tensor_min B -> B(H)` on an infinite-dimensional
Hilbert space has image containing a nonzero compact operator.

Consequently, every compact-bearing counterexample to pure tensor permanence
must have at least one nonsimple factor.  This conclusion requires no
unitality, exactness, nuclearity, selflessness, or Z-stability.
