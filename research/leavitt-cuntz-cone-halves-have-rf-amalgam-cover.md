**Setting.** `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, `q = s_1 t_1`, `Delta = St_20(R) = GL_20(R)` (Khanh, `leavitt-steinberg-map-iso-from-rank-three`),
`z = x_13(q)`. Let `*` be the anti-involution `s_i <-> t_i` of `R`.

```text
R_+ = unital subring generated by s_1, s_2, q        (Cuntz cone)
R_- = (R_+)^* = unital subring generated by t_1, t_2, q
Delta_+- = <x_ij(a) : a in R_+->  <=  Delta,         Delta_0 = Delta_+ ∩ Delta_-          (CC1)
```

**Statement.**
1. **Halves are residually finite.** `GL_20(R_+)` and `GL_20(R_-)` are residually finite. So `Delta_+` and `Delta_-`
   are finitely generated, residually finite, and each contains `z`. The map `g -> (g^*)^(-1)` exchanges them.
2. **Finite edge group.** `R_+ ∩ R_- = F_2·1 + F_2·q ≅ F_2 x F_2`. Hence `Delta_0 = SL_20(F_2) x SL_20(F_2)`, and
   `z` is a transvection in the first factor.
3. **Residually finite cover.** `Delta = <Delta_+, Delta_->`. The amalgam `Gamma = Delta_+ *_(Delta_0) Delta_-` is
   residually finite, and the inclusions induce a surjection `p : Gamma -> Delta`. Its kernel `K` is finitely normally
   generated. `K` contains the mixed commutator relator
   `kappa = [x_12(s_1), x_23(t_1)] · z^(-1)`, which is nontrivial in `Gamma`. The head `z` itself lies in the finite
   edge group and survives in a faithful MF (norm-corona) representation of `Gamma`.
4. **Consequences.**
   - (a) *Goal decomposition.* `Rad_MF(Delta) = Delta` holds iff every MF representation of the residually finite
     group `Gamma` that is trivial on the finitely many normal generators `k_1..k_r` of `K` kills `z`. Every
     Property-(T)-free proof of `property-t-free-leavitt-full-mf-radical` must use some `k_i`. No argument whose
     relations hold in `Delta_+`, in `Delta_-`, or in both amalgamated along `Delta_0` can collapse the head.
   - (b) *LNC3.* No diagram `(LNC3)` of `agent-leavitt-not-bcs-negative-root-corner` lifts to `C[Gamma]`. If lifts of
     the `A_x` satisfied the BCS relations in `(1-z)/2 · C[Gamma] · (1-z)/2`, then the residually finite group `Gamma`
     would be nonhyperlinear. So every certificate is **K-essential**: for every lift, some defining identity holds
     only modulo the augmentation ideal of `K`.

**Class killed.** One-sided (cone-supported) collapse arguments and cone-supported corner certificates.
- *Invariant:* the faithful MF representation of the residually finite cover `Gamma`, together with its regular
  trace, in which `tau(z) = 0`.
- *Step where every member dies:* the first use of `pi(z) = 1`, or of a BCS identity, that does not pass through a
  relator of `K`.
- The first such relator is the negative-root commutator `z = [x_12(s_1), x_23(t_1)]`, which is exactly the corner
  this hole is named after.

**Remark (not load-bearing).** For `n >= 3`, `EL_n` over a finitely generated associative ring has (T) (Ershov and
Jaikin-Zapirain, Invent. Math. 179 (2010)). So `Delta_+-` are Kazhdan and residually finite. Property (T) of the
halves cannot collapse the head; the (T)-proof of `binary-jacobson-steinberg-head-root-is-mf-invisible` needs (T) of
`Delta` itself.
