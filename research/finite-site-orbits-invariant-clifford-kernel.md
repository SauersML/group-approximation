title: Finite site orbits yield finite invariant Clifford-lamp kernels
artifacts:
  - GroupApproximation/Sofic/FiniteOrbitInvariantKernel.lean
---

Let a group `H` act on a site set `X`.  If every `H`-orbit in `X` is
finite, then every finite subset of the presented Clifford lamp group over
`X` lies in a finite `H`-invariant subgroup.
