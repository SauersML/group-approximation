**ESTABLISHED.** Let `T_0` be a nonamenable torsion-free group whose proper
subgroups are all cyclic.

1. `T_0` is generated by two elements and is not acylindrically hyperbolic.
2. If the center of `T_0` is nontrivial, it is an infinite amenable normal
   subgroup, and hence wq-normal.
3. If the center of `T_0` is trivial, then every maximal cyclic subgroup of
   `T_0` is malnormal, and no infinite amenable subgroup of `T_0` is wq-normal.

So a centerless `T_0` satisfies hypotheses 2 and 3 of
`fpbs-residual-price-one-non-ah-class-nonuniqueness`. It belongs to that class
exactly when it has fixed price one. It then lies in part (R1) of
`fpbs-wq-normal-hull-is-almost-malnormal`, since it has infinite amenable
subgroups.

**Example.** Ol'shanskii--Sapir credit A. Yu. Ol'shanskii, *An infinite simple
torsion-free Noetherian group* (Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR Ser. Mat. 43 (1979)), with
a nonamenable torsion-free group all of whose proper subgroups are cyclic. The
title of that paper calls its group simple. A simple nonabelian group has
trivial center, so item 3 applies. That group's simplicity is taken from the
title only. Its fixed price is not recorded in the repo or in the sources read
for this node.
