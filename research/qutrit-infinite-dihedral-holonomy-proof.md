## The relative intertwiners

Retain the simultaneous decomposition of the `h`-fixed block

```text
F_2^16=F_2^10 direct-sum V_[1,1]^3.
```

Choose `i` in the trivial summand and `j` in the nontrivial summand.
The elementary roots

```text
X=I+s_0E_(ij),             Y=I+s_1E_(ji)
```

commute with the normal form of `h` because that normal form is the
identity on all sixteen coordinates. If `D` is the rectangular matrix
with `DN_J=N_hD`, then

```text
(XD)N_J=N_h(XD),             (YD)N_J=N_h(YD).           (QID5)
```

Thus `XD` and `YD` are literal alternative prefix equivalences, with
relative holonomies `X` and `Y`. Their inverses are exact because
`X^2=Y^2=1` in characteristic two.

## Infinite order from the positive-word leading term

Set `a=s_0` and `b=s_1`. On the active two-coordinate block,

```text
W=XY=[1+ab  a; b  1].                                   (QID6)
```

The subalgebra generated by `s_0,s_1` is the free positive-word
subalgebra of the Leavitt algebra. In the matrix product `W^n`, the only
way to obtain total positive degree `2n` in the `(1,1)` entry is to
choose the degree-two term `ab` from the `(1,1)` entry of every factor.
It contributes

```text
(ab)^n=(s_0s_1)^n
```

with coefficient one. All other contributions have smaller total
degree. Positive words of a fixed length are linearly independent, so
this term cannot cancel. Therefore `W^n!=I` for every `n>=1`.

A group generated by two involutions has the dihedral normal forms
`W^n` and `XW^n`. Since `W` has infinite order, no two such normal
forms coincide and

```text
<X,Y> isomorphic to <X,Y | X^2=Y^2=1>
                 isomorphic to D_infinity.              (QID7)
```

The first nonconstant return is already the commutator
`[X,Y]=W^2`. Direct multiplication gives

```text
W^2=
[1+ab+(ab)^2   aba;
 bab             1+ba],                                 (QID8)
```

so it is not a scalar or finite-code permutation matrix.

For `n>0`, the `(2,1)` entry of `W^n` has the unique leading word
`b(ab)^(n-1)`, so the same entry survives unchanged in the reflection
`XW^n`. The negative powers use the symmetric leading words in
`(YX)^n`; the remaining reflection normal forms are identical after
interchanging `a,b` and the two coordinates. Together with the cases
`X,Y`, this shows that every nonidentity element of `<X,Y>` has a
nonzero positive-degree matrix entry. Hence

```text
<X,Y> intersect K={1},       K=GL_28(F_2).              (QID9)
```

## All nine character blocks

Let `J_h=P_h^(-1)JP_h`. Its action on the trivial and nontrivial
summands chosen above is different, while its cross-block matrix entries
vanish. Therefore

```text
E_(ij)J_h+J_hE_(ij) !=0,
E_(ji)J_h+J_hE_(ji) !=0,
(E_(ij)J_hE_(ij))=(E_(ji)J_hE_(ji))=0.                 (QID10)
```

Conjugating `J_h` by `X` or `Y` consequently adds a nonzero
homogeneous degree-one term. Neither conjugate is scalar, so neither
`X` nor `Y` normalizes `C`. Both centralize `h`. The same order-nine
intersection argument used for the D8 seam now gives

```text
C intersect vCv^(-1)=<h>,             v=X,Y.            (QID11)
```

For the joint character
`chi_(alpha,beta)(J)=omega^alpha`,
`chi_(alpha,beta)(Z)=omega^beta`, compatibility on `<h>` is

```text
2alpha'+beta'=2alpha+beta.
```

The intersection has order three, so every compatible block has squared
canonical norm `3/81=1/27`. This proves `(QID3)`.

In particular each source atom sees all three target atoms in its
`h`-fibre. On the uniform weight vector, each fibre has total mass
`1/3` and each cell mass `1/9`. The active marginal still has

```text
tau(q)=1/3,                  tau(R)=2/9;                 (QID12)
```

there is no scalar Hall deficit.

## The amenable infinite-vertex cover

The subgroup `D=<X,Y>` commutes with `h`. By `(QID9)`,

```text
H=<h,X,Y>=<h> times D_infinity,
H intersect K=<h>.                                      (QID13)
```

Thus the relations proved above give a surjection

```text
K *_(<h>) H -> <K,X,Y>.                                 (QID14)
```

This is an actual finite-presentation consequence: one may present the
second vertex as

```text
H=<h,X,Y |
   h^3=X^2=Y^2=1, [h,X]=[h,Y]=1>.
```

The scalar vertex `K` is finite, `H` is virtually cyclic, and the edge
`<h>` is finite. Standard closure of sofic and hyperlinear groups under
amalgamation over amenable subgroups shows that the group on the left of
`(QID14)` is sofic and hyperlinear.

There is also an explicit approximation picture. Replace
`D_infinity` by finite dihedral quotients of increasing rotation order,
take regular representations of the two vertices in common multiples,
and identify their regular `<h>` restrictions. The resulting
amalgamated microstates retain the uniform regular restriction to `C`,
hence `(QID12)`, while every fixed nontrivial dihedral word remains
trace-zero once the rotation order is large enough. All relations and
atom tables `(QID1)--(QID3)` therefore have zero asymptotic
Hilbert--Schmidt defect.

The natural homomorphism `(QID14)` could have additional kernel; no
quotient-permanence assertion is made. What is exact is the requested
firewall: the first infinite-order, noncommuting, nonconstant-word
prefix holonomy still admits a sofic regular-multiplicity countermodel.
