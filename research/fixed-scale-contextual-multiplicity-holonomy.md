Assume the coherent local packet representations supplied by
`shared-selector-context-packet-coherence`, and let their forbidden context
mass be bounded below by the fixed non-CE BCS constant `beta_B`.  Construct a
finite recurrent group/holonomy network such that:

1. restriction across a forbidden rank gate adds one baseline `A_f` spin
   multiplicity, as in `(PRM1)`;
2. packet multiplicity transports with weighted normalized-HS cost controlled
   by `scaled-kazhdan-transport` or an equally dimension-free mechanism;
3. a return cycle compares source and target at one **fixed irreducible packet
   size**, rather than allowing the doubled simple to absorb the surplus;
4. the returned extensive multiplicity mismatch is bounded above by a sparse,
   bounded-overlap sum of group-relator energies.

Equivalently, the network may realize a contractive recursion with
`lambda<1` on a positive density of levels, so
`contractive-computation-ladder` turns marked signal into a constant energy
floor.

## Attempts

- **Finite graph of finite groups: impossible.**  Its representation types
  admit positive stationary integer flows and its fundamental group is
  virtually free/hyperlinear; surplus leaks into complementary packet types.
- **Raw restriction multiplicity: invisible.**  A forbidden `B_f` simple is
  twice as large, so Hilbert dimension is conserved.  The return must first
  normalize to the common `A_f` packet size.
- **Active attack.**  Feed the explicit restriction matrices into the existing
  Kazhdan compressor/multiplicity framework.  The unresolved estimate is
  exactification-covariance at the self-normalized defect scale: transport
  error must be `o` of the extensive surplus, uniformly in ambient dimension.
