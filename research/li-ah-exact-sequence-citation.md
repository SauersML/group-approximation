**Source.** X. Li, *Ample groupoids, topological full groups, algebraic K-theory spectra
and infinite loop spaces*, Forum Math. Pi, arXiv:2209.08087.

**How it was read.** The arXiv e-print TeX was fetched on MSI on 2026-09-14 into

from that file. Theorem numbers are given by TeX label only.

**Verbatim.**
- Corollary `cor:AHConj`: "Let $G$ be an ample groupoid whose unit space $\Gn$ is locally
  compact Hausdorff and has no isolated points. Assume that $G$ is minimal and has
  comparison. Then there is an exact sequence $H_2(\bmD(G)) \to H_2(G) \to H_0(G,\Zz/2)
  \xrightarrow{\zeta} H_1(\bmF(G)) \xrightarrow{\eta} H_1(G) \to 0$." (The TeX typesets the
  sequence with `xymatrix`; the arrows are transcribed.)
- Right after it: "In particular, this proves Matui's AH-conjecture for all purely
  infinite minimal ample groupoids, which was not known before."
- TeX l.804: "Another class of groupoids with comparison is given by purely infinite
  minimal groupoids, in the following sense: An ample groupoid $G$ is purely infinite
  minimal if and only if for all compact open subspaces $U, V \subseteq \Gn$ with $V \neq
  \emptyset$, there exists a compact open bisection $\sigma \subseteq G$ such that
  $\rms(\sigma) = U$ and $\rmr(\sigma) \subseteq V$ [...]. By definition, it is clear that
  purely infinite minimal groupoids have comparison."
