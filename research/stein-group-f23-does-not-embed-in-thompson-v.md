title: The Stein group F_{2,3} does not embed in Thompson's group V
---

There is no injective homomorphism from the Stein group `F_{2,3}` to
Thompson's group `V`. Here `F_{2,3}` is the group of all piecewise-linear
orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of `[0,1]` with breakpoints in `Z[1/6]`
and slopes of the form `2^m 3^n` (`m, n` in `Z`).
