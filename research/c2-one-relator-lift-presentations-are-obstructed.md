**ESTABLISHED** through `c2-one-relator-lift-presentations-are-obstructed-proof`. Unreviewed.
Beyond the (computer-assisted) injectivity of `π_1(C) → G`, the proof is by hand. It uses
Hopf's formula, Lyndon's identity theorem for `G`, Fox calculus and Magnus' conjugacy theorem.
Its only other computed inputs are four free reductions, which
`lift.py` checks (`basics()`): the basis words carry `u_0` to `w` and `u_1` to `g w g^{-1}`, and
`e(u_1) = 2e(u_0)`. The explicit class formula for two eliminations is a consistency check,
verified by Fox calculus in the exact normal form of `G = F_4 ⋊ Z` (`check_all()`).

## Setting

This is the pair of `admissible-ab-nonzero-two-cell-base-exists`:
- the group is `G = ⟨x, y | w⟩` with `w = yxYXXyyxYXYxYYxyXy`;
- the basis is `a = XyxYx`, `b = Xyyyx`, `c = XYxyx`, `d = y`, `e = xyX`;
- the cells are `u_0 = dEbcBADDed` and `u_1 = bcBAAbDcBd`, with `g = Xy`;
- `H = π_1(C) = ⟨a,…,e | u_0, u_1⟩ ≤ G`, injective by
  `rank-five-degree-one-two-cell-configuration-is-a-core`. The pair is admissible, with
  `ρ(H,G) ∈ {0,1}`.

`N = ⟨⟨w⟩⟩ ≤ F(x,y)`. Since `w` is not a proper power, `N_ab = N/[N,N] ≅ ZG` is free on `[w]`
(Lyndon). `[n]` denotes the class of `n ∈ N`, and `Φ : N_ab ≅ ZG → Z[H\G]`, `λ ↦ Hλ`, is the map
onto the permutation module on right cosets. A **lift** of a presentation `H ≅ ⟨Z | r⟩` is a map
`f : F(Z) → F(x,y)` with `f(z) = z` in `G` for every `z ∈ Z`.

## Statement

- **(O1) Homology.** `H_ab ≅ Z^4`, and `H_2(H) ≅ Z` is generated by the class of `u_0^{-2} u_1`
  in Hopf's formula. It maps to `−1` times the generator `[w]` of `H_2(G) ≅ Z`. Consequently, every
  one-relator presentation `H ≅ ⟨Z | r⟩` has `|Z| = 4` and `r ∈ [F(Z), F(Z)]`.
  - Examples: `u_0` contains `c` and `a` once each. Eliminating `c` gives
    `r = eDDEddAbDBeDDEddad`, and eliminating `a` gives `r = eDDEddbCBeDDEddbDcBd`.
- **(O2) The class of a lift.** For every one-relator presentation `H ≅ ⟨Z | r⟩` and every lift
  `f`, we have `f(r) ∈ N` and `Φ[f(r)] = ±(Hg − 2H)`.
  - Changing `f` changes `[f(r)]` by `Σ_z (∂_z r)·ω_z`. Each `∂_z r ∈ ZH` has augmentation
    `e_z(r) = 0`, so `Φ` does not see the change.
  - The two cells contribute `Φ[w] = H` and `Φ[g w g^{-1}] = Hg`. The relator `r` is `±` the
    `H_2` generator `u_0^{-2}u_1`, and that fixes the coefficients `(−2, 1)`.
  - For an elimination of a once-occurring `z_0`, the class of the lift by basis words is
    exactly `g − (∂_{z_0} u_1)(∂_{z_0} u_0)^{-1}`. For `z_0 = c` this is `g − P_1 − P_2`, with
    `P_1 = xyXY` and `P_2 = XyyxYXXyyxYXYYYxxyXY` in `H`.
- **(O3) Obstruction.** `g ∉ H`: since `d = y ∈ H`, `g = Xy ∈ H` would put `x, y` in `H` and give
  `H = G`, but `H_ab ≅ Z^4` and `G_ab ≅ Z^2`. So `Hg − 2H` involves two distinct cosets and is not
  `±Hh`. A conjugate `v w^{±1} v^{-1}` has class `±v`, with image `±Hv`. Hence no lift makes
  `f(r)` conjugate to `w^{±1}`.
- **(O4) Consequence.** There is no relative presentation `G = ⟨H, x, y | z = f(z) (z ∈ Z)⟩`,
  for any one-relator presentation `⟨Z | r⟩` of `H`, in which `x, y` go to the standard pair or
  to a pair Nielsen-equivalent to it in `G`.
  - By Tietze moves and Magnus' theorem, such a presentation exists iff `f(r) ∼ w^{±1}` for a
    lift `f`, after an automorphism of `F(x,y)` in the Nielsen case.
  - This is exactly the shape with `|R| − |T| = 4 − 2 = 2 = χ(G) − χ(H)`. By
    `relative-realization-is-relative-efficiency` it would prove `RP(H,G)`, so this route to
    `ρ = 0` is closed.

## Why finite quotients missed it, and computations

- **(C1) Finite quotients see nothing.** Every homomorphism from `G` to `A_5`, `S_5`, `PSL(2,7)`,
  `A_6` or `PSL(2,11)` has image of order at most 55. In all of them `φ(g) ∈ φ(H)`, so the coset
  obstruction is invisible there.
  - This is why the finite-field test of the class condition (`jtest.py`, `F_3, F_5, F_7` over
    `A_5, PSL(2,7), S_5`) found no obstruction.
  - An exact search also found nothing. `lift_search.py` tested 790,608 lifts of the
    `c`-elimination, with each `n_z` a single conjugate `h w^{±1} h^{-1}`. It covered up to two
    modified generators with `|h| ≤ 4`, three with `|h| ≤ 2`, and four with `|h| ≤ 1`. None had
    `f(r) ∼ w^{±1}`, as (O3) predicts.
- **(C2) Slide route, b-coordinate.** Building the slide group `Γ''` from the `b`-coordinate of the
  certificate, instead of the unit coordinate `c`, gives the same `t ≠ 1` counts in `A_5` (40),
  `S_5` (40), `PSL(2,7)` (16), `A_6` (52), `PGL(2,7)` (16), `PSL(2,11)` (68) and `S_6` (144).
- **(C3) Superseded diagnostics** are in `RESULTS.md`. They cover the (a,b)-coset membership
  `T_i ∈ ⟨bDB, eDDEdd⟩` (`sl_test.py`, `sl_exact.py`, `stallings.py`), which (O3) makes
  unnecessary.

## What this leaves

`ρ(H,G) = 0` now needs a relative presentation outside (O4). Three shapes remain.

1. **The slide-group route, `T = {x, y, t}`.** Keep the five relations `z = f(z)` for
   `z ∈ {a,…,e}`. That is, `Γ'' = ⟨x,y,t | f(u_0), f(u_1)⟩ ≅ G` compatibly with `H`, where
   `f(z) ∈ F(x,y,t)` and `f(z) = z` in `G` under `t ↦ τ`.
   - The slide groups of `admissible-ab-nonzero-two-cell-base-exists` have `τ = 1`.
   - For each candidate, the question is whether `t = τ` follows from the two relators.
   - The argument of (O2) and (O3) gives no obstruction here that we can see. With three
     generators the relation module has rank 2, and the two cell classes only need to form a
     basis of it.
   - Candidates should be screened with exact invariants in the normal form, such as the
     `Z[H\G]` images of the two cell classes. Small finite quotients are blind here, by (C1).
2. **Two generators with relators mixing `H` and `T`,** i.e. relators not of the form `z = f(z)`.
3. **Two generators mapping to a generating pair of `G` that is not Nielsen-equivalent to
   `(x, y)`.** If such a pair gives `G ≅ ⟨x,y | f(r)⟩`, the argument of (O2) still runs in its
   relation module. But `κ_0, κ_1` are replaced by the classes of lifts of `u_0, u_1` for the new
   pair, which need not be group elements, so (O3) does not follow.

The other alternative is an EG counterexample. That needs an invariant killing every relative
presentation of every shape, and by `relative-realization-is-relative-efficiency` no stable-module
invariant can do that. The `Z[H\G]` image of (O2) is not such an invariant: it depends on the
shape of the presentation.
