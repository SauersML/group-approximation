The full derivation is in the artifact, §1-§3.

- **Lemma 1** (minimality, topological freeness, generation).
  - The orbit of `x` contains `x + ⊕_Δ Z/2`, which is dense.
  - For `ℓ = (δ, f)` with `δ ≠ e`, choose `h, δ^(-1)h` outside the cylinder window and `supp f` with different values.
  - `R` is generated by `u_s`, `u_a` and `1_[x(e)=1]`.
- **Lemma 2** (simplicity and centre). Free points are dense (Baire). A free point gives a clopen `U` with
  `1_U r 1_U = 1_U`; minimality covers `Z`. A central element has coefficients supported in fixed-point sets with empty
  interior.
- **Lemma 3 and Proposition 4** (models).
  - Words of length `≤ r` act on `2^(Q_n) × Q_n` by the affine formula `(c,q) ↦ (c + Σ_(h∈supp f) 1_(q'φ_n(h)), q')`,
    `q' = qφ_n(δ^(-1))`. This depends only on the element.
  - Every configuration is legal, so the diagonal evaluation is defined.
  - `π_n` is multiplicative on fixed pairs for large `n`, so `π` is a unital ring homomorphism into the ultraproduct.
    It is injective by simplicity.
- **Theorem 5.**
  - (T) from EJZ.
  - Simplicity modulo centre from the Steinberg theorem, and the centre is trivial since `Z(R)^× = {1}`.
  - LEF from the ultraproduct of the `GL_(nN)(F_2)`.
  - `[GL_1 R, GL_1 R] ↪ E_2(R)` from the identity `diag(a,a^(-1))diag(b,b^(-1))diag((ab)^(-1),ab) = diag(1,a^(-1)b^(-1)ab)` and Whitehead's lemma.
