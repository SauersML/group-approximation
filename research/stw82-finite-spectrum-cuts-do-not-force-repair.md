There are a separable nuclear C*-algebra `A`, a projection `p in M(A)`, and
a proper essential ideal

```text
L=closure(ApA)
```

with all of the following properties.

1. `L` is stable AF, and in particular has real rank zero.
2. `L` has an approximate unit `(h_n)` of positive contractions, each with
   finite spectrum, such that

   ```text
   norm([h_n,a])->0 for every a in A,       [h_n,p]=0.    (FSN1)
   ```

   In fact `g_n=p+(1-p)h_n` is a finite-spectrum positive quasicentral
   approximate unit for the larger ideal `J=L+C p` inside `K=A+C p`.

3. Nevertheless, for `K=A+C p` and `J=L+C p`, the extension

   ```text
   0 -> J -> K -> A/L -> 0                             (FSN2)
   ```

   is not quasidiagonal.  Thus `J` has no approximate unit of projections
   quasicentral in `K`.
4. More sharply, every nonzero spectral projection of `h_n` obtained by a
   cutoff away from zero has commutator norm one with one fixed element of
   `A`, for all sufficiently large `n`.

Hence none of the following, separately or together, makes the spectral
repair in `stw82-quasicentral-support-thickening-preserves-dimension`
automatic: real rank zero of `L`, positive quasicentral cuts of finite
spectrum, exact commutation of those cuts with `p`, or the existence of a
spectral gap at each individual stage.  A quantitative gap condition must
also dominate the commutator error; pointwise finite-spectrum repair is not
enough.

One explicit example is obtained from the Toeplitz algebra `T=C*(S)` on
`ell^2(N_0)`.  Let `e=1-SS^*`, let `H=ell^2(N_0)`, and put

```text
A=T tensor K(H),
p=e tensor 1_(B(H)),
L=K(H) tensor K(H).
```

Then `L` is the proper essential ideal generated by `p`.  There are diagonal
finite-rank contractions `a_n` whose entries equal one on the first `n`
coordinates, decrease to zero in steps of size `1/n` over the next `n`
coordinates, and vanish thereafter.  If `Q_n` is the projection onto the
first `n` coordinates, then

```text
h_n=a_n tensor Q_n                                      (FSN3)
```

has finite spectrum, satisfies `(FSN1)`, and approximates `L`.  Yet every
nonzero positive-threshold cut of `(FSN3)` is `P tensor Q_n` for a nonzero
finite initial-coordinate projection `P`, and its commutator with
`S tensor e` has norm one.

The obstruction is intrinsic, not an artifact of this chosen approximate
unit.  The boundary map of `(FSN2)` sends the circle generator to the
nonzero compact rank-one class in `K_0(J)`, ruling out every projectional
quasicentral approximate unit for `J`.
