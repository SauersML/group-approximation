The prime-ideal product/factorization theorem is obtained from Propositions
2.17 and 2.16 of E. Blanchard and E. Kirchberg, [*Non-simple purely infinite
C-star algebras: the Hausdorff
case*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2003.06.008), Journal of Functional
Analysis 207 (2004), 461--513.  For separable C-star algebras every prime
ideal is primitive, which gives the displayed primitive-spectrum
homeomorphism.  Exactness passes to ideals and quotients, so the theorem
applies after either factor is localized.

The ideal/open-set compatibility is the naturality of the displayed map:
primitive ideals not containing `I tensor_min J` are exactly the pairs whose
two coordinates do not contain `I` and `J`.  Only this primitive-ideal
theorem is imported; no Cuntz-semigroup regularity or tensor-purity conclusion
is taken from the source.
