Let `Gamma <= GL_d(k)` be a nontrivial group over an arbitrary field.  If its
amenable radical is trivial, then `C*_r(Gamma)` is selfless.  Consequently,
for linear groups, C*-simplicity of the reduced algebra is equivalent to
selflessness.

**Established (literature).**  This is Theorem 1.1 (the main theorem) of
Itamar Vigdorovich, *Selfless reduced C*-algebras of linear groups*,
arXiv:2602.10616v3 (18 June 2026; to appear in *Proceedings of the London
Mathematical Society*).  The equivalence stated above is exactly the four-way
equivalence in that theorem.  It is imported here, not reproved.
