For every `g` the membership cylinder `{H:g in H}` has measure zero or one.
Intersect the measure-one cylinders for the set `K` of probability-one
memberships and the complements of the measure-zero cylinders outside `K`.
There are only countably many, so the intersection is conull and every
subgroup in it equals `K`.  Conjugation invariance makes the resulting Dirac
mass invariant exactly when `K` is normal.  The GNS representation of its
indicator character is the regular representation of `Gamma/K`.
