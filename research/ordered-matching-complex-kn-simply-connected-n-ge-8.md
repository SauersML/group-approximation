Let `OM(K_n)` be the simplicial complex with:

- vertices the ordered pairs `(a, b)` of distinct elements of `{1, …, n}`;
- simplices the sets of pairwise disjoint pairs.

Claim:

- `OM(K_n)` is connected for `n ≥ 5`;
- `OM(K_n)` is simply connected for `n ≥ 8`.

This is the fundamental domain of the pure loop braid group acting on the descending links
`L_n(LB_*)`; see artifact §2.
