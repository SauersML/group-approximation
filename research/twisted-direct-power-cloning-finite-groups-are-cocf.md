For every finite group `G` and every homomorphism `θ: G → G`, the Thompson-like
group `V_(G,θ)` is co-context-free (coCF).

`V_(G,θ)` is the Witzel–Zaremsky Thompson-like group of the *twisted
direct-power* cloning system on the finite groups `G_n = S_n ⋉ G^n`: the
representation map `ρ_n: G_n → S_n` is the projection, and the cloning map
duplicates the split leaf's `G`-label, twisting the new copy by `θ`. Every
`G_n` is finite, so this is a cloning system on finite groups in the sense of
Zaremsky Problem 2.16, and this claim answers its coCF sub-question
affirmatively for this family. For `θ ≠ id` these are the proposed potential
counterexamples to Lehnert's conjecture (no obvious faithful action on Cantor
space).
