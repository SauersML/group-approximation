Expanding `q_sq_t=(1+s+t+st)/4` and using distinct reduced words gives
`tau(q_sq_t)=1/4`.  The meet is the invariant subspace for the group generated
by `s,t`; the infinite left regular representation has no nonzero invariant
vector.  In the regular representation of the finite quotient
`D_(2n)`, that meet is the one-dimensional constant subspace, of normalized
trace `1/(2n)`, while the product trace remains `1/4`.  These quotient regular
characters converge on every fixed word window to the canonical character of
`C_2*C_2`, proving the canonical-profile counterexample.
