Citation import, not a reproof. Source: A. Jaikin-Zapirain, *Free groups are
L²-subgroup rigid*, arXiv:2403.09515. Read from the arXiv TeX source
(`l2subgrouprigid25.tex`, fetched from `arxiv.org/e-print/2403.09515`) on
September 19, 2026.

- Definition of `π̄` (Corollary `crit`), verbatim:
  "$\bpi(H\leq F)=\min\{\rk(L)\colon H\le L\le F\}$".
- Ideals, verbatim: "We denote by $I_{\Q[G]}$ the augmentation ideal of
  $\Q[G]$ and if $H$ is a subgroup of $G$ we denote by ${}^G I_{\Q[H]}$ the
  left ideal of $\Q[G]$ generated by $I_{\Q[H]}$."
- Betti numbers (§ universal division rings, property (P1)), verbatim:
  "$$\beta_i^{k[G]}(M)=\dim_{\mathcal D_{k[G]}} \Tor_i(\mathcal D_{k[G]},
  M).$$"
- Identification of `D(F)` (§ universal division rings), verbatim: "the
  division $\Q[F]$-ring $\D(F)$ mentioned in the introduction is isomorphic to
  $\D_{\Q[F]}$ as a $\Q[F]$-ring. Thus, for any left $\Q[F]$-module $M$ we can
  define its {\bf $i$th $L^2$-Betti numbers} in a pure algebraic way as
  $\beta_i^{\Q[F]}(M)$." The introduction defines `D(G)` as "the division
  closure $\mathcal D(G)$ of $\mathbb{Q}[G]$ in $\mathcal U(G)$", and notes "In
  this paper we make use of Linnell's solution of the Strong Atiyah Conjecture
  for free groups".
- Theorem `pibar`, verbatim: "Let $K$ be a field of characteristic 0 or 2,
  $F$ a free group and $H$ a finitely generated subgroup of $F$. Then
  \begin{equation} \bpi(H\leq F) =\rk(H)-\beta_1^{K[F]}(I_{K[F]}/{}^FI_{K[H]}).
  \end{equation} Moreover, if $\widetilde H$ is the $\D_{K[F]}$-closure of $H$,
  then $\rk(\widetilde H)=\bpi(H\leq F) $."
- Proof of Theorem `pibar`, first inequality, verbatim (valid for every
  `K` considered there, in particular `K = Q`):
  "$$\bpi(H\leq F)\geqslant \rk (F)-\beta_0^{K[F]}(I_{K[F]}/{}^FI_{K[H]})=
  \dots$$" This is display `onedirection`.
- Proof of Theorem `pibar`, second inequality, verbatim: "Let $\widetilde H$
  be the $L^2$-closure of $H$ in $F$. Then \begin{multline}\bpi(H\leq F)\leq
  \rk(\widetilde H)=\rk(F)-\chi^{\Q[F]}(I_{\Q[F]}/{}^FI_{\Q[\widetilde H]})=\\
  \rk(F)-\beta_0(I_{\Q[F]}/{}^FI_{\Q[\widetilde H]})=
  \rk(F)-\beta_0(I_{\Q[F]}/{}^FI_{\Q[ H]})=\\
  \rk(H)-\beta_1^{\Q[F]}(I_{\Q[F]}/{}^FI_{\Q[H]}).\end{multline}" This is
  display `inclosed`. The existence of the `L²`-closure is the paper's
  Theorem `main`: "Free groups are $L^2$-subgroup rigid."

Together, the two displays at `K = Q` give the target's formula:

```text
π̄(H ≤ F) = rk F − β_0^{Q[F]}(I_{Q[F]}/^F I_{Q[H]}),
β_0^{Q[F]}(M) = dim_{D(F)} D(F) ⊗_{Q[F]} M.
```

The rank formula `rk(H̃) = π̄` is the "Moreover" clause. Here `F` has finite
rank, because `rk(F)` appears in the formula. The proofs were not re-checked
here.
