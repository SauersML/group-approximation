Let `n` independent forbidden Schur flip cells act on one invariant packet
carrier.  Factor their packet implementers from the commuting involutive
stable letters.  The resulting cancellation operators on the external
multiplicity space are `n` independent Pauli pairs.  Hence their generated
algebra and its commutant have the exact form

```text
C^m = C^(2^n) tensor C^L,
C*(old cancellation cells) = M_(2^n)(C) tensor I_L,
Comm(old cancellation cells) = I_(2^n) tensor M_L(C),             (RMT1)
```

for a uniquely determined integer `L`, and `m=2^nL`.

Suppose `r` further forbidden flip cells commute with the old cell algebra
and are projectively independent modulo it.  Their cancellation operators
then live in the residual factor `M_L(C)`.  If their binary cocycle form has
rank `2r`, then

```text
2^r divides L.                                                    (RMT2)
```

Thus the total supercritical inequality is exactly residual:

```text
n+r > log_2 m       if and only if       r > log_2 L.             (RMT3)
```

Consequently the remaining dimension-diagonal compiler need not sense the
ambient matrix dimension or defeat the already paid factor `2^n`.  It only
has to run on the residual commutant in `(RMT1)` and exactify
`r>log_2 L` new cells with dimension-independent loss.
