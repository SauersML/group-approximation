Let `G` be finitely generated with `Res_MF(G) = G`, normally generated by an
element `a` of finite order `m >= 2`.  Then there are a finitely presented
group `P = <x_1,...,x_k | R>` and `alpha = x_1` such that

1. `alpha^m = 1` and `alpha` normally generates `P`;
2. `P` surjects onto `G` with `alpha` mapping to `a`;
3. `Res_MF(P) = P`;
4. there is `C_0 > 0` with

   ```text
   max_i ||U_i - I||_op <= C_0 max_(r in R) ||r(U) - I||_op
   ```

   for every dimension and every unitary tuple.

With `L` the number of conjugate occurrences in fixed normal-generation words
for the generators and `delta_0` the threshold of the compactness step,
`C_0 = max(L+1, 2/delta_0)`.  Only `delta_0` is non-effective; the torsion
relation supplies its linear step with no quadratic term and no perfectness.
