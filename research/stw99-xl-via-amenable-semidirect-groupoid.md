(GSA_G) supplies `H` with `A = C*_r(H)` unital simple stably finite
classifiable and `G` acting on `H` with `H x| G` amenable.  The criterion makes
the induced action `alpha : G acts on A` amenable.  One direction for XL in
general; both directions for witnesses with a `G`-invariant untwisted Cartan
subalgebra through which `G` acts by Weyl groupoid automorphisms.
