The algebras `(FDG1)` form an increasing sequence.  If `D_n` is normalized
by `S`, it contains `s w D_0 w^(-1) s^(-1)` for every `|w|<=n`, hence it
contains `D_(n+1)`; the reverse inclusion is automatic.  Thus (1) implies
(2).  Conversely, if `D_n=D_(n+1)`, then for `s in S`,

```text
s D_n s^(-1) subset D_(n+1)=D_n.
```

Applying the same inclusion to `s^(-1)` gives equality, hence (1).  Once
equality occurs, induction gives `D_(n+k)=D_n` for every `k`, proving (3);
(3) trivially implies (1).  This proves the equivalence and the strict-growth
statement.

For the ZPC countermodel, let

```text
Y={0,1}^Z,       X=Y x {0,1}
```

with product measure.  Let the central fixed-point-free involution `J` flip
the last coordinate.  Let the named readable involution `u` flip the last
coordinate exactly when the zeroth Bernoulli bit is one:

```text
u(y,e)=(y,e+y_0).
```

Let two remote unreadable involutions act on the Bernoulli coordinates by
the two reflections

```text
(r y)_k=y_(-k),          (s y)_k=y_(1-k),
```

and leave the last coordinate unchanged.  Their product is a bilateral
shift.  Thus `J,u,r,s` are pmp involutions, `J` is central and free, and `u`
is Z-aligned.  Place `u` at a verifier vertex outside the ZPC neighborhoods
of `r,s` (all readable variables in their own finite neighborhoods may be
the identity) and take an always-accept controlled-linear verifier.  This is
a perfect ZPC strategy: every required local commutator is exact.

The Boolean algebra `D_0` generated by the readable choice of `u` is the
zeroth coordinate algebra.  Its translates under powers of `rs` are the
distinct Bernoulli coordinate algebras, so `D_infinity` is infinite
dimensional and `D_n<D_(n+1)` for every `n`.  Thus even depth-one ZPC
verification is compatible with a readable partition whose translate
groupoid never closes at finite depth.

Finally, a unitary is monomial relative to a finite Boolean algebra exactly
when it permutes its minimal projections, equivalently when it normalizes the
algebra.  Therefore any finite monomial coefficient presentation purporting
to retain this readable partition contradicts the equivalence just proved.
