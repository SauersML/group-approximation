For every nonempty `R subset {+1,-1}^k`, there are a finite group `K_R`, an
irreducible representation `rho_R` of dimension greater than one, and
commuting involutions `d_1,...,d_k in K_R` whose joint spectrum on `rho_R` is
exactly `R`.

If `r=|R|>=2`, take `V=C^R`, let `d_j delta_a=a_j delta_a`, and let `K_R` be
the finite signed-permutation group generated by the `d_j` and every
permutation of the basis `R`. The coordinate signs distinguish the basis
points, so their joint spectral projections are the rank-one diagonal
projections. Conjugating these by all basis permutations supplies every
matrix unit. Hence the defining representation is irreducible and its block
corner is `M_r(C)`.

If `r=1`, use `K_R=C_2 times S_3` and tensor the required character of `C_2`
with the two-dimensional standard representation of `S_3`; each coordinate
acts by its prescribed scalar sign.

In both cases the primitive central block projection `z_(rho_R)` has positive
canonical trace and is annihilated by every scalar character. Inside the
corner, the joint spectral projections of the `d_j` are indexed exactly by
the allowed assignments. Thus every individual BCS context has a complete
signed-Hecke seed. Only the cross-context overlap equations remain global.
