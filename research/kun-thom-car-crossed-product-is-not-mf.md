Let `Gamma<G` be the Kun--Thom Kazhdan compressor pair, put
`X=G/Gamma`, and let `A_CAR=Cl(X)` be the unital CAR algebra generated by
self-adjoint unitaries `(c_x)_(x in X)` satisfying

```text
c_x c_y = -c_y c_x   (x!=y).
```

For the permutation Bogoliubov action of `G`, neither the full nor the
reduced C-star crossed product

```text
A_CAR crossed_product G
```

admits a unital star-homomorphism into a norm matrix corona
`product_n M_(d_n)/directSum_n M_(d_n)`.  In particular, both C-star crossed
products are **not MF**.

The point is categorical.  The canonical Majoranas and actor unitaries in
either crossed product give a representation of the complete Kun--Thom
Clifford extension in which its central sign `z` is the scalar `-1`.
`kun-thom-clifford-extension-not-weak-mf` proves that every representation
of that extension in a norm matrix corona sends `z` to `+1`, a contradiction.

This does **not** refute `kun-thom-clifford-crossed-product-ce`.  Connes
embeddability of the canonical tracial von Neumann closure asks for a
trace-preserving embedding into a tracial matrix ultraproduct; it neither
supplies nor requires an operator-norm embedding of the norm-dense C-star
crossed product.  Thus this theorem is the exact MF firewall around the open
Clifford CE route.
