The classical reduction (Dadarlat--Eilers; CGSTW Lemma 5.15/5.16):
unitize to the unitally absorbing case; Paschke duality identifies
`KK(A,B) ≅ K_1(Q(B) ∩ φ̄(A)')`, the trivial class is represented by
the image of the conjugating unitary path at time 1, the relative
commutant is properly infinite by absorption, and `K_1`-injectivity of
properly infinite unital algebras (the premise) makes the unitary
null-homotopic inside the commutant, which Dadarlat--Eilers convert to
proper asymptotic unitary equivalence.  (Now superseded as a proof of
the target by Szabo's direct theorem, but recorded as the structural
link: LXI sits above LXII.)
