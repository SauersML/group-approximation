**ESTABLISHED.**  Fix a prime `p` and integers `M>=2L>=2`.
On

```text
K_M=l2(F_p^M)
```

take the Schrödinger representation of `H(F_p^M)` with a fixed nontrivial
central character.  Let `H_s` be the coordinate Heisenberg subgroup on the
first `L` coordinates, and let `H_0,H_1` be its two images on the even and
odd coordinates among the first `2L` coordinates.  Coordinate permutations
of `F_p^M` extend the two injections and hence give unitaries `U_0,U_1` with

```text
U_i pi(H_s) U_i^*=pi(H_i),                             (HSR1)
```

exactly.  The two child groups commute modulo their common center and jointly
generate the coordinate group `H_t=H(F_p^(2L))`.  Thus every finite
Heisenberg table, both branch conjugacy tables, the common marked center, and
all transverse coordinate shears in this window hold with zero defect.

Write `A_s` and `A_t` for the matrix algebras generated by `H_s` and `H_t`.
After reordering tensor coordinates,

```text
A_s=M_(p^L) tensor I_(p^(M-L)),
A_t=M_(p^(2L)) tensor I_(p^(M-2L)).                    (HSR2)
```

Let `R_s,R_t` be the orthogonal Reynolds projections on `End(K_M)` onto the
respective commutants, with normalized trace on the operator Hilbert space.
Because `A_s<=A_t`,

```text
R_t<=R_s,
tr_ad(R_s)=p^(-2L),
tr_ad(R_t)=p^(-4L),                                    (HSR3)
```

and therefore

```text
tr_ad(R_s-R_t)=p^(-2L)-p^(-4L)>0.                      (HSR4)
```

This is literal rectangular escape: the source packet has label dimension
`p^L` and multiplicity `p^(M-L)`, whereas the paired child packet has label
dimension `p^(2L)` and multiplicity `p^(M-2L)`.  The common central-root
carrier is nevertheless the identity, and every relator listed above has
zero normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt defect.  Tensoring with any fixed finite tag
packet multiplies both multiplicities by the tag dimension and leaves every
number in `(HSR3)--(HSR4)` unchanged.

Consequently no dimension-free estimate of the form

```text
tr_ad(R_s-R_t)
 <= C_L (Heisenberg-table defect
          + branch-self-copy defect
          + transverse-shear defect
          + fixed-tag defect)                          (HSR5)
```

can hold, even at one fixed depth.  Nor can those relations imply a
dichotomy saying that failure of a common source-sized paired reservoir is
paid by one of their own word defects: this model takes the failure measured
by `(HSR4)` and makes every proposed payment zero.

This does **not** refute `paired-same-reservoir-boundary-lemma`.  That claim is
allowed to use relations absent here: a same-object coefficient return in
the full affine-Leavitt elementary group and the paired Steinberg
multiplication triangles.  It proves that at least one such genuinely mixed
coefficient-return row is logically necessary.  Compatible Heisenberg
density measures, exponentially many polarized characters, reused branch
implementers, and fixed selectors cannot contribute the missing boundary
payment by themselves.

## Proof of the density calculation

For a nontrivial central character, the finite Stone--von Neumann theorem
identifies the restriction to `H(F_p^r)` with its unique irreducible
`p^r`-dimensional representation tensored with a multiplicity space.  This
gives `(HSR2)`.  The commutant of `M_n tensor I_m` in `End(C^n tensor C^m)`
is `I_n tensor M_m`, whose dimension divided by `(nm)^2` is `n^(-2)`.
Taking `n=p^L` and `n=p^(2L)` proves `(HSR3)`.  The coordinate inclusion
gives `A_s<=A_t`, hence `R_t<=R_s`, and subtraction gives `(HSR4)`.
