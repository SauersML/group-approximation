If `C*_r(G)` has strict comparison with respect to its unique trace `τ`, then
any bounded quasitrace `ρ` induces the same order on the Cuntz semigroup as
`τ`, and the standard argument (Blackadar--Handelman dimension functions,
uniqueness of the state on `Cu` compatible with the comparison) forces
`ρ = τ`.  STW, footnote 152: "Strict comparison with respect to the unique
trace would show that there is a unique quasitrace."
