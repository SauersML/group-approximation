**ESTABLISHED** by `lodha-simple-group-s-is-two-generated-proof` (lane proof, bh-free-13, 2026-09-18; not
independently reviewed).

**The printed question.** Y. Lodha, *A finitely presented infinite simple group of homeomorphisms of the
circle*, arXiv:1710.06220v2, section "Open questions", TeX `FPSimple.tex` l.1700–1702, read at source:

> Is $S$ generatable by $2$ elements? What is the smallest number of relations needed to provide a
> presentation for $S$?

Lodha notes just before (l.1694–1695) that `S ≅ <T, s>`, and so is generated by three elements.

**Answer to the first part: yes.** `S` is generated by a rotation `t ∈ T` of finite order and one further
element. The second part, the least number of relations, is not addressed here.

**Priority.** Bounded arXiv searches (2026-09-18: "Lodha simple group piecewise projective generated",
"two-generated homeomorphisms circle simple group", and the abstract of Bleak–Harper–Skipper
arXiv:2206.05316 on 3/2-generation of `T`) found no statement that `S` is 2-generated. The method is
Bleak–Elliott–Hyde's, in the circle form Belk–Hyde–Matucci use for `TA` (arXiv:2407.03149v1, Theorem
`thm:TA-2-gen`); see `fg-simple-circle-groups-with-t-are-two-generated` for credit.

**Where it sits.** `fp-infinite-simple-group-not-two-generated` (Attempt 1) listed `S` as a surviving
candidate for a finitely presented infinite simple group that needs three generators. It is not one.
