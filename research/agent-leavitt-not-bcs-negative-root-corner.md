Let `B_loop` be the effectively specified finite BCS of
`lin-explicit-fixed-bcs-gap-via-generic-conversion`.  Let

```text
R=L_(F_2)(1,2),          Delta=St_20(R),
q=s_1t_1,                z=x_13(q),
P_z=(1-z)/2 in C[Delta].                                  (LNC1)
```

Construct finite group-ring expressions `A_x in P_z C[Delta]P_z`, one for
each generator of the finite BCS algebra `A(B_loop)`, such that

```text
1 |-> P_z,        x |-> A_x                                (LNC2)
```

defines a unital star homomorphism

```text
A(B_loop) -> P_z C[Delta]P_z.                              (LNC3)
```

No injectivity, trace matching, stability, positive ambient rank floor, or
centrality of `z` is required.  This exact finite diagram is sufficient for
the full Property-`(T)`-free MF collapse by
`agent-leavitt-not-bcs-corner-closes-full-radical`.

The point of the formulation is that the carrier is no longer selected from
an arbitrary microstate: it is the prescribed marked spectral projection
itself.  If that projection survives at even vanishing ambient density, its
own matrix rank supplies the trace normalization.  Thus the open content is
purely algebraic occurrence typing inside the negative root Hecke corner.

## Attempts

The literal projection/Halmos-reflection encoding does put arbitrary
projections in a two-by-two involution corner, but it carries a nonregular
character and centralizing its grading collapses every proper projection;
`agent-groupfactor-bcs-reynolds-free-holonomy-trichotomy` records the exact
failure.  Freely adjoining a central sign is also circular at the group
factor level, while `agent-bloop-unique-product-quotient-obstruction` shows
that any successful central-sign support quotient must already fail unique
product.  The attack is therefore deferred at the genuinely native step:
find the BCS projection generators as finite expressions in this noncentral
Leavitt root corner, with their overlap relations holding exactly.

### Coefficient-ring gate and the flagship calibration (swarm-0917, reframing)

Two exact facts now fence this hole.

1. *It is a nonhyperlinearity certificate.*  By
   `nonce-bcs-unital-algebraic-corner-map-forces-nonhyperlinear`, `LNC3`
   makes `Delta=St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))` nonhyperlinear; the route
   `leavitt-negative-root-bcs-corner-route-to-nonhyperlinear` records this as
   a route to `non-hyperlinear-group`.  The Property-`(T)`-free MF route
   therefore passes through a flagship-level statement.
2. *Support filter* (`leavitt-corner-bcs-support-coefficient-gate`,
   established).  Let `X` be the support of the images and `S_X` the ring
   generated by `q` and the matrix entries of `pi(X)^(+-1)`.  Pushing `LNC3`
   along `rho_*:GL_20(S_X)->GL_(20k)(K)` and applying Malcev shows that
   every unital fd representation of `S_X` kills `q`, and every hyperlinear
   quotient of `<X,z>` kills `z`.  Dead at this step: finite-dimensional,
   commutative and PI coefficient rings (degree-zero core, bounded-prefix
   inventories), the one-sided range algebra `F_2<s_1,t_1>` (character
   `s_1,t_1 |-> 1`), and the finite spin reservoir.

Where the idea stops.  The gate is passed by any `S_X` containing the
complementary isometry pair `s_2,t_2` (Dedekind defect `q=1-s_2t_2`), for
example the complementary Toeplitz algebra `T_2=F_2<s_2,t_2>`.  For
coefficients in `T_2`, `pi(<X,z>)` is an extension of a finitely generated
subgroup of `GL_20(F_2[x,x^(-1)])` (linear, residually finite) by a
locally finite finitary congruence kernel containing `z`.  Ring homomorphisms
cannot see `z` there (every proper quotient of `T_2` kills its unique minimal
ideal, which contains `q`), so killing this class needs a group-level
hyperlinearity theorem for locally-finite-by-linear groups that no current
permanence result supplies.  Conversely, a construction supported in `T_2`
would give a nonhyperlinear locally-finite-by-residually-finite group, which
would be a separate surprise.  The next falsifiable step is to decide whether
`E_20(T_2)` has a hyperlinear quotient or model in which `x_13(1-s_2t_2)`
survives.
