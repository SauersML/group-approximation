Let `H<=G`, let

```text
pi:L(G)->M
```

be a trace-preserving embedding into a finite tracial von Neumann algebra,
and let `x in L(G)`. Then

```text
E_(pi(H)' cap M)(pi(x))
 =pi(E_(L(H)' cap L(G))(x)).                           (EGC1)
```

Thus reducibility, tensor amplification, and fixed vectors elsewhere in the
ambient algebra cannot increase the fixed component of an element coming
from the canonical group algebra. The relative-fixed-algebra payment of such
a carrier is determined entirely inside `L(G)`.
