**ESTABLISHED** (lane proof in `schreier-hilbert-hotel-ring-reduction-proof`;
elementary; not independently reviewed; no novelty claimed for the ingredients).

**Data (HH).**

- `Gamma` is a finitely generated group and `alpha in Aut(Gamma)`.
- `H <= Gamma` satisfies `alpha(H) = H`.
- `H` is a Chabauty limit of finite-index subgroups `H_n`: for every `gamma`, `gamma in H_n` for all
  large `n` iff `gamma in H`. It suffices that `H` is closed in the profinite topology of `Gamma`.
- (IS) Every `gamma != 1` moves infinitely many points of `X = Gamma/H`.
- `Gamma_0 <= Gamma` is finitely generated with `alpha(Gamma_0) <= Gamma_0`.
- (HH) Some `g in Gamma_0` satisfies both:
  - `alpha(Gamma_0) meet gHg^(-1)` has finite index in `alpha(Gamma_0)`, so the point `g x_0` has a
    finite `alpha(Gamma_0)`-orbit, where `x_0 = H`;
  - `g notin alpha(Gamma_0) H`, so `g x_0` lies outside `alpha(Gamma_0) x_0`.

**Statement.** Let `k` be a finite field. Given (HH), the algebraic crossed product

```text
R = M_2(k)^(tensor X) x| Gamma       (restricted infinite tensor product, Gamma permuting sites)
```

together with the following data satisfies (R1)-(R5) of `matricial-compression-ring-exists`:

- `psi` is induced by the site map `t(gamma H) = alpha(gamma) H` and by `u_gamma -> u_(alpha(gamma))`;
- `R_0` is generated by `M_2(k)` at `x_0` and by `u_s^(+-1)` for `s` in a finite generating set of `Gamma_0`;
- `z` is the flip `w = [[0,1],[1,0]]` tensored over the finite `Gamma_0`-orbit of `t^(-1)(g x_0)`.

The ring `R` is simple.

**Also proved in the route (necessary conditions on (HH)).**

- (N1) `Gamma` and `Gamma x|_alpha Z` are residually finite.
- (N2) The extended action of `Gamma x|_alpha Z` on `X`, with the generator acting by `t`, is **not**
  a Chabauty limit of finite actions. So the rigidity of
  `matricial-rings-rigidify-compression-centralizers` is evaded exactly as it must be: the
  non-matricial skew Laurent ring is `M_2(k)^(tensor X) x| (Gamma x|_alpha Z)`.
