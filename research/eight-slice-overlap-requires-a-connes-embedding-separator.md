The proposed eight-slice overlap inequality would vanish on every matrix
ultraproduct model while remaining strictly positive in the marked regular
trace.  Combined with the shared BCS gap, it is already the final
Connes-embedding separator.

Therefore proving it is not routine finite-packet stability.  Its proof
must use a matrix-only coupling to the original non-CE moments and cannot be
a positivity argument valid in all finite tracial algebras.
