title: Larsen–Tiep uniform character bound for finite quasisimple groups of Lie type
---

**ESTABLISHED (literature import).** There is an absolute constant `c > 0` with the following property. Let `G` be a finite quasisimple group of Lie type, `χ` an irreducible character of `G`, and `g ∈ G`. Then

`|χ(g)| ≤ χ(1)^{1 − c·log|g^G| / log|G|}`.

The route is `larsen-tiep-uniform-character-bound-citation`.

Used by `subshift-tower-unit-group-characters-are-constant`, where it gives decay of the character ratios of `GL_N(F_2)` at elements whose support is a fixed fraction of `N`.
