If every homomorphism from a group `Gamma` to every tracial matrix
ultraproduct unconditionally decodes a unital representation of a fixed
star-algebra `B`, then `B` has a Connes-embeddable tracial state.  The same
holds when decoding lands in a nonzero corner or fixed matrix amplification.

Therefore an algebra with no CE trace can only be decoded through a
mark-relative nonzero-corner condition (or with an independently proved
mark-dead CE basepoint).  Exact mark-alive completeness does not repair an
unconditional decoder.
