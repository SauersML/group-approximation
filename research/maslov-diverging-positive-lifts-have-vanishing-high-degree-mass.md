Given a homomorphism from a nontrivial Maslov fibre into a tracial matrix
ultraproduct, choose the unital `k_n`-positive generator-system lifts from
`maslov-central-regular-lifts-have-diverging-positive-order`.  Prove that
they can be chosen so that, for

```text
B_n=C^*(phi_n(S_alpha))
   =direct_sum_j(M_(r_(n,j)) tensor I_(m_(n,j))),
```

one has

```text
(1/d_n) sum_(r_(n,j)>k_n) r_(n,j)m_(n,j) ->_omega 0.       (MH1)
```

This is the remaining range-algebra formulation.  Unlike a
bound involving `k_n/d_n`, `(MH1)` is unchanged by common amplification.
It asks the Kassel root/Weyl relations to control the irreducible degree
distribution of the algebra generated by their coordinate images, not the
ambient coordinate dimension and not merely scalar moments.  The equivalence
recorded below shows that this condition has exactly the strength of the
desired coordinate ucp liftability; it is not an independently weaker
arithmetic intermediate.

## Attempts

- `high-degree-range-mass-is-exactly-tracial-ucp-liftability` proves that
  `(MH1)` is equivalent, for an arbitrary finite-dimensional operator
  system, to the desired coordinate ucp liftability: the forward implication
  is the Smith-block replacement already recorded, while the converse takes
  full positivity order for ucp lifts.  Thus this hole is an exact
  reformulation of the Maslov liftability seam, not a weaker arithmetic
  intermediate statement.
- `generic-cp-noise-maximizes-range-block-degree` proves that the range-block
  statistic is maximally lift-dependent.  An arbitrarily small cp
  perturbation of any `k_n`-positive lift can make its generated algebra the
  full `M_(d_n)`, without changing the tracial quotient class, central trace,
  or any bounded Kassel word defect.  Hence one must construct a special
  correcting lift; character rigidity and root/Weyl relations cannot force
  `(MH1)` for arbitrary coordinate representatives.
- `positive-range-block-extraction-recreates-maslov-microstate`
  closes the proposed direct character-rigidity argument.  Compressing to a
  positive-mass family of supercritical blocks and polar-correcting does not
  yield a forbidden exact finite-dimensional representation; it yields
  another tracial-ultraproduct homomorphism with the same allowed
  central-regular trace.  A new blockwise HS stability theorem would still be
  required to pass from those approximate blocks to exact representations.
- Scalar central-regular moments and bounded Chevalley word defects cannot
  prove `(MH1)`: they are unchanged after adjoining or amplifying a
  trace-negligible block, as recorded by
  `vanishing-corner-reduction-maps-defeat-tracial-cp-upgrade`.
- A useful attack must instead act on the central decomposition of
  `C^*(phi_n(S_alpha))`.  The concrete target is to show that a
  positive-trace family of irreducible blocks with degree `>k_n` would turn
  the Kassel quarter-turn and rank-one braid relations into a forbidden
  finite-dimensional nontrivial Maslov-sector representation, with an error
  bound depending on `k_n` but not on the ambient multiplicities.  No such
  blockwise arithmetic correction theorem is presently proved.
