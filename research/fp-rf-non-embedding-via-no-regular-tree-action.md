A self-similar group is by definition a subgroup of `Aut(T_d)` for some
`d >= 2`, and so is each of its subgroups. If `G` embeds in `Aut(T_d)` for no
`d`, then `G` embeds in no self-similar group at all, finitely presented or not.
The same `G` witnesses the target. `∎`
