There exists a fixed infinite simple Kazhdan LEF group `S`, generated by
eighteen involutions, such that every countable locally finite group
embeds in `S`. In particular `Q/Z` and all `GL_n(overline(F_p))` embed
in `S`. This `S` is sofic, hyperlinear, and operator MF; it has no
nontrivial finite quotient and is not finitely presented.

The host is `EL_3(LC(X,F_2) semidirect Z)` for one fixed minimal binary
subshift with universal odometer factor. This is a written extension of
the existing 9.1 construction, not a new Lean verification or a claimed
solution of the finitely presented simple sofic problem.
