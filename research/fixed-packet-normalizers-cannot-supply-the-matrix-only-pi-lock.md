title: Fixed packet normalizers cannot supply the matrix-only polynomial-identity lock
---

Type-preserving normalizers preserve a fixed finite packet factor in every
tracial representation, robustly and dimension-independently.  Therefore a
PI confinement conclusion derived only from such normalizers is universal
tracial and would also eliminate the positive regular packet corner.

The matrix-only PI lock must instead use multiplicity return, a scalable
commutant, or source-specific non-CE moments.
