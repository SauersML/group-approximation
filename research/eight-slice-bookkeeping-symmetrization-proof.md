from the distinct bookkeeping supports, including the capacity slice `d_4`.

```text
tau((e_0+e_1)d_0)=(2/4)(1/8)tau(P_f)=tau(P_f)/16,
tau(e_2(d_1+d_2))=(1/4)(2/8)tau(P_f)=tau(P_f)/16,
```

and the `11` calculation is identical to the first.  Each source therefore
contains two joint atoms.  Arbitrary permutations of the six source atoms,
extended by the identity off their union and on the capacity atoms, form the
required finite monomial `S_3` action.
