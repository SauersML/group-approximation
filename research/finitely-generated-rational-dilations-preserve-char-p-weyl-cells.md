ESTABLISHED.  Let `Lambda` be any finitely generated subgroup of
`Q^x`.  There is a finite set of primes `S_Lambda` such that for every prime
`p` outside `S_Lambda` there is a finite ring, a finite elementary group, and
an action of the whole group `Lambda` on it with elements `D,M` satisfying

```text
D M-M D=1,                                                (RDW1)
a.M=a M,          a.D=a^(-1)D       for every a in Lambda, (RDW2)
```

where the scalars on the right are reduced modulo `p`.  Consequently all
Steinberg root tables, the paired commutator word, every multiplication
relation in `Lambda`, and every iterated rational root-dilation relation are
simultaneously realized in one exact finite group.  In particular no
finitely generated rational dilation torus can supply the
characteristic-zero exclusion required by
`scalar-trace-reflection-is-julia-root-target`.

## Exact finite model

Choose generators `q_1,...,q_s` of `Lambda`, write them as reduced rational
numbers, and let `S_Lambda` contain the prime divisors of all their
numerators and denominators.  If `p` is outside this set, reduction defines a
homomorphism

```text
bar : Lambda -> F_p^x.                                    (RDW3)
```

Put

```text
V=F_p[t]/(t^p),             R=End_(F_p)(V)=M_p(F_p),       (RDW4)
M(f)=t f,                   D(f)=d f/dt.                   (RDW5)
```

As in the bare Weyl cell, the truncated top boundary is cancelled by
characteristic `p`, and

```text
D M-M D=I_V.                                               (RDW6)
```

For `a in F_p^x`, define the diagonal operator

```text
H_a(t^j)=a^j t^j,               0<=j<p.                   (RDW7)
```

Then `H_a H_b=H_(ab)` and direct evaluation on the monomial basis gives

```text
H_a M H_a^(-1)=a M,
H_a D H_a^(-1)=a^(-1)D.                                  (RDW8)
```

Thus `a |-> Ad(H_a)` acts on `R`, and composing `(RDW3)` with this action
gives an action of the whole abstract group `Lambda`.  Its kernel is
harmless: every relation among the rational dilations is still satisfied,
and all powers of every selected dilation are present.

Let

```text
G_(p,Lambda)=E_3(R) semidirect bar(Lambda),               (RDW9)
```

where `bar(Lambda)` acts entrywise on elementary matrices by `Ad(H_a)`.
This is a finite group.  Inside it the Steinberg relations and `(RDW6)` give

```text
[e_12(D),e_23(M)] [e_12(M),e_23(D)]^(-1)
  =e_13(I_V),                                             (RDW10)
```

while the actor elements implement `(RDW8)` on every root subgroup.  The
element `e_13(I_V)` is nonidentity.  Hence the left regular representation
of `G_(p,Lambda)` is an exact canonical-trace finite model retaining the
commutator-unit root and all the rational dilation covariance.

## Scalar-return consequence

In complex matrices the corresponding coefficient target still has the
dimension-free obstruction

```text
||[Y,X]-I||_2>=1                                         (RDW11)
```

for every `X,Y`, by normalized trace cyclicity.  The exact finite models
above therefore preserve the full counterexample of
`char-p-weyl-blocks-local-commutator-decoder` after adjoining any fixed
finite list of rational scaling actors and all relations generated by them.

This rules out a tempting repair of scalar Julia reflection: dyadic root
transport, finitely many adjacent rational scales, and arbitrarily deep
iteration of those scales do not distinguish characteristic zero.  A
successful presentation must instead use a datum absent from `(RDW9)`, for
example dilation support meeting every prime, an adaptively generated prime
challenge, or a genuinely finite-coordinate trace/rank constraint not
expressible by rational torus covariance.  The theorem does not assert that
such stronger data are impossible.
