**ESTABLISHED.**  Write the native degree-four tuple as

```text
D=(x_12(Q),x_23(1),x_14(Q),x_43(1)),
center(D)=x_13(Q)=w.                                     (JWT1)
```

Let a constant Weyl permutation `sigma in S_4` act on root positions.  If
`D^sigma` contains the remaining opposite root `k=x_31(1)` as one of its two
constant Pauli generators, then its center is necessarily

```text
x_21(Q)  or  x_41(Q).                                   (JWT2)
```

Both are constant-Weyl conjugates of the marked center `w`, and explicit
redesigned packets do exist; for example the cycle
`sigma:1|->2, 2|->3, 3|->1, 4|->4` gives

```text
D^sigma=(x_23(Q),x_31(1),x_24(Q),x_41(1)),
center(D^sigma)=x_21(Q).                                (JWT3)
```

However the residual relations do not force either center in `(JWT2)` to
reduce the native residual carrier

```text
e_res=F_+(1-x_13(1))(1-x_23(ST))/4.
```

In fact, in the left regular representation the residual projection fails
to commute with each moved center.  Conjugating its defining head root
`w=x_13(Q)` produces respectively the new Fourier factors `x_23(Q)` and
`x_43(Q)`, neither of which belongs to the elementary abelian subgroup
supporting `e_res`.  Thus `(JWT3)` is a literal degree-four packet only on a
new negative center cut; it cannot be substituted for `D` on `e_res`
without a new joint compression and a new proof that the moved center
survives.  This does not rule out accidental reduction in a special quotient;
it proves that reduction is not an exact consequence of the residual cuts.

No constant-Weyl conjugate of `D` can contain the mixed occurrence
`u=x_21(T)`: Weyl conjugation permutes root positions but preserves the root
coefficient, whereas every entry of `D` has coefficient `Q` or `1`.
Indeed the entire conjugated packet remains over the finite coefficient
subring generated by `Q`, which does not contain `T`.

Consequently constant-Weyl target redesign does not by itself close the
residual polar seam.  The `k` redesign trades literal occurrence for an
unauthenticated moved-center carrier, while the `u` redesign is algebraically
impossible.  A successful redesign must use a genuinely mixed conjugator or
prove that the residual polar transports `e_res` onto one of the moved
negative center cuts in `(JWT2)`.
