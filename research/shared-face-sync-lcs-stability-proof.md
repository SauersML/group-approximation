Tietze-collapse all exact equality copies to obtain the homogeneous LCS
group.  Conversely expand a representation of that group constantly across
all occurrences.  These operations identify the exact representation
spaces.  The requested dimension-independent movement estimate is therefore
uniform HS stability for this sequence of redundant presentations.  Bare
expander averaging lands outside the representation space because spectral
means from different coordinates need not preserve local commutation.
