Property (T) gives H1(G,H_pi)=0 for every unitary Hilbert representation
by Delorme--Guichardet. The linked artifact cites the primary theorem
and fixes all presentation and cochain conventions.

First there is a uniform exact degree-one Hodge gap g>0:

```text
||d0* f||^2+||d1 f||^2>=g ||f||^2.
```

Otherwise genuine coefficient representations with unit cochains of
energy tending to zero have a Hilbert ultraproduct containing a unit
cocycle orthogonal to all coboundaries. Full H1 vanishing makes it a
coboundary, a contradiction. The finite-word cochain maps pass to this
ultraproduct with uniform bounds. Put mu=g/2.

If the uniform curved inequality failed, choose delta_j<1/j and unit
cochains f_j violating it with C=j. Positivity gives
`delta_j max_s||f_(j,s)||_op<sqrt(mu/j)`. In the normalized rectangular
HS ultraproduct take the closed subspace generated by classes with
bounded HS representatives A_j satisfying
`delta_j||A_j||_op->0`. It is invariant under each T_s and its inverse.
For every defining relator,

```text
||(T(r)-I)A_j||_2
 =||A_j(U_j(r)*-I)||_2<=delta_j||A_j||_op->0.
```

Consequently this subspace carries a genuine G representation. Every
component of the limiting unit cochain f lies in it. Its Hodge energy
is at most mu by the violated inequality, contradicting the exact
lower bound g=2mu. At delta=0 the exact gap applies directly. This
proves `(KCH1)` uniformly in both rectangular dimensions.

For the heat statement, write

```text
L0=2h(I-K),       K=(1/(2h))sum_s(T_s+T_s*).
```

Every T_s and T_s* is an operator-norm isometry on rectangular matrices.
The exponential expansion
`exp(-tL0)=exp(-2ht)sum_(k>=0)(2ht)^k K^k/k!`
is therefore an operator-norm contraction. In particular A_t is a
contraction and each component of d0 A_t has operator norm at most 2.
Free-word telescoping and the exactness of rho give

```text
||d1 d0 A_t||_2^2
 =sum_r||A_t(U(r)*-I)||_2^2<=M delta^2.
```

Apply `(KCH1)` to f=d0 A_t. Self-adjointness and positivity of L0 yield

```text
E'(t)=-2||L0 A_t||_2^2<=-2mu E(t)+2(M+4C)delta^2,
N'(t)=-2E(t).
```

Integrate the first scalar inequality and then the second identity to
obtain `(KCH2)`. Every heat trajectory is in a fixed finite-dimensional
matrix space. The ultraproduct only establishes the uniform constants;
no infinite-time or dimension interchange enters these estimates.

For the boundary statements, use exact U=I_d and
rho=I_(d-k) direct_sum sigma with sigma nontrivial irreducible. The
contraction A0=I has energy at most 4h k/d, but every exact intertwiner
has image in rho^G and hence normalized mass at most 1-k/d. This rules
out O(delta^2) mass loss already at delta=0. For the basin assertion,
dilute arbitrary U to U direct_sum I_((m-1)d), with fixed m large
enough to lie in the specified HS basin around the identity. A flexible
correction of this tuple supplies an almost invariant original-rank
corner inside a genuine representation. Invariant averaging, spectral
rounding, and polar identification give error O(beta) and relative
padding O(beta^2), where beta is the retained-column error normalized
by sqrt(d). The boundary artifact includes the full calculation and
the linear-error, quadratic-padding version of this transfer.
