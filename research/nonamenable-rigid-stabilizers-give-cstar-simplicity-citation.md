**A. Le Boudec and N. Matte Bon, *Subgroup dynamics and $C^\ast$-simplicity of groups of homeomorphisms*,
arXiv:1605.01651v3** (`subgroupdynamics-arxiv-v3.tex`), theorem label `thm-non-amenab-rigid-stab`, verbatim up to macros:

> Let $X$ be a Hausdorff space, and let $G$ be a countable group of homeomorphisms of $X$. Assume that for every non-empty
> open $U \subset X$, the group $G_U$ is non-amenable. Then $G$ is $C^\ast$-simple.
>
> More generally every countable subgroup of $\homeo(X)$ containing $G$ is $C^*$-simple.

The introduction states the first sentence as `cor-intro-csimple`.
