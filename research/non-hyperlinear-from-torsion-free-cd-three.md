Forget finite presentation, torsion-freeness and the upper bound on cohomological dimension in the prerequisite. Its specified Ollivier–Wise lifted-pair double is an explicit nonhyperlinear witness. Its negative conclusion uses Jihao Liu's [Theorem 1.2/6.7](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf) and Andreas Thom's [conditional normalization/double theorem](https://andreasthom.github.io/pdf/nonhyperlinear.pdf), with Ollivier–Wise and the separate Leavitt structural inputs retained in the source proof. No assertion of exact cohomological dimension three is made.
