The exceptional triple cover restricts centrally to `3.A6`, but its
extension over `S6` is noncentral: odd permutations invert the `C3` kernel.
Under `Sp4(F2) ~= S6`, root transvections are transpositions. Their lifts
therefore exchange the two nontrivial kernel-character sectors, so no
representation containing all root lifts can have the kernel act as the
single scalar `omega I`.
