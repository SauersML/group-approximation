**ESTABLISHED.** Let `G = <X | R>` be an infinite finitely presented simple group that is not sofic,
and `w_Q(a)` the relator width (least `K` with every `a_x` a product of at most `K` `Q`-conjugates of
relator values `r(a)^(+-1)`).

**Theorem.** For every `N` there is `K = K(G, N)` such that `w_Q(a) <= K` for every finite group `H`
with `|H| <= N`, every `n`, every `Q = H wr Sym(n) = H^n ⋊ Sym(n)` or `Q = H wr Alt(n)`, and every
`a in Q^X`.

**Consequence.** No sequence of weak models of `G`, with any bi-invariant lengths, has all its
targets of the form `H wr Sym(n)` or `H wr Alt(n)` with `|H|` bounded. This is the depth-two case with
the richest top. For the binary Leavitt unit group and every finitely presented simple nonsofic group,
the wreath-shaped room left on `fg-simple-weakly-sofic-groups-are-sofic` is now:
- unbounded bases under symmetric tops;
- small tops of large depth (`simple-nonsofic-relator-width-over-iterated-wreath-products`);
- classical groups of unbounded rank.

**Mechanism.**
1. **Top.** Match abelianization by a fixed exponent block `prod_r r^(n_(x,r))`, then cover the even
   top part with a balanced block `r*^k r*^-k`. Here `r*` is a relator whose top moves a fixed
   proportion of the moved set (Lemma 1.2 of
   `simple-group-support-and-semisimple-rank-models-collapse-proof`), and the covering is Rodgers'
   theorem.
2. **Base, moved part.** A commutator `[(h; 1), c r*(a) c^-1]` with `h` supported on a set `S` disjoint
   from its image is a *dipole*: `h_i` at `i` and a twisted `h_i^-1` at `sigma(i)`, for every `i in S`.
   One round realizes a matching of size of order `eps_0 |Omega_0|`, and conjugating by `Sym(Omega_0)`
   places it anywhere. Two matchings form a Hamiltonian path on the moved set, so boundedly many
   rounds solve for every base coordinate except one. The defect at the last coordinate lies in
   `[H, H]`, and nested commutators `[[(k at i; 1), c r*(a) c^-1], (l at i; 1)] = [k, l] at i` absorb
   it with `4 cw(H)` letters.
3. **Base, fixed part.** On points fixed by the whole top tuple, the coordinates are genuine tuples
   in `H`. They lie in the locally finite variety generated by `H`, where the remaining error is a
   product of boundedly many balanced pairs `(u r u^-1)(v r^-1 v^-1)`. Every other block is balanced
   too, so the two parts never interfere.
