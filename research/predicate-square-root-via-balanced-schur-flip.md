The two-sign Reynolds cell supplies `p`, the section `up`, and an unused
range complement of trace `tau(A_a)/2`.  The Schur flip-square word `d`
commutes with the complete assignment atom and hence with the refined source
`p`; its corner square is `Kp`.  Therefore `up` and `udp` are source-full
sections with identical final projection, and their relative source corner
is `pdp`.  The same-range calculation supplies `(PSR1)--(PSR2)` without
altering the unused complement.  Fixed-table exactification and the
subgroup-Hecke estimate give the stated robust form.
