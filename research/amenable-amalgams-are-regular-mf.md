If `G_1,G_2` are countable amenable groups and `H` is a common subgroup, then

```text
G_1 *_H G_2
```

is regularly operator-MF realized.  Shulman's amenable-amalgam theorem makes
the full group C-star algebra of this group MF.  The canonical map to the
reduced group C-star algebra pulls its regular trace back to the full algebra,
and the canonical group unitaries are injective there.  Thus the full MF
algebra itself carries the regular realization.
