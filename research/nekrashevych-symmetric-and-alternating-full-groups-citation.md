Literature import, not a reproof. Source: V. Nekrashevych, *Simple groups of
dynamical origin*, arXiv:1511.08241.

- Definition 3.1 (multisection of degree `d`) and Definition 3.2 (`S_d(G)`,
  `A_d(G)`, `S(G) = S_2(G)`, `A(G) = A_3(G)`) were read from the ar5iv HTML
  rendering on 2026-09-13 and are quoted verbatim in the target node.
- Theorem 4.1 (minimal groupoid of germs: every non-trivial subgroup of `F(G)`
  normalized by `A(G)` contains `A(G)`, so `A(G)` is simple) and Theorem 5.6
  (expansive, all orbits with at least 5 points: `A(G)` is finitely generated)
  likewise.
- The sentence that `S(G)/A(G)` is a quotient of `H_0(G, Z/2Z)` was read from
  the arXiv abstract page.

The route asserts only that the paper says what the target node records it as
saying. It asserts nothing about which groupoids satisfy the hypotheses; in
particular it does not assert that any product groupoid is expansive.
