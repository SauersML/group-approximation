**ESTABLISHED imports**, through `fpbs-fibered-3-manifold-cost-inputs-citation`.

1. **Virtual fibering.** Every closed hyperbolic 3-manifold has a finite
   cover that fibers over the circle (Agol, arXiv:1204.2810). The fiber is a
   closed surface of genus at least two, so the cover's fundamental group is
   `pi_1(S_g) semidirect Z`.
2. **Surface groups have fixed price.** The fundamental group of a closed
   orientable surface of genus `g` has fixed price `2g-1` (Gaboriau 2000).
3. **Commensurated transfer.** If `N` is commensurated in an infinite
   countable group `Gamma`, for instance of finite index, then every free
   p.m.p. action satisfies `C(R_(Gamma↷X)) <= C(R_(N↷X))` (Gaboriau).
