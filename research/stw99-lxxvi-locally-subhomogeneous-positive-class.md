title: Pure locally subhomogeneous stable-rank-one algebras of topological dimension zero are Cu-regular

---

Let `A` be a separable locally subhomogeneous C*-algebra with stable rank one
and topological dimension zero.  If `A` is pure, then the first-factor map

```text
Cu(A) -> Cu(A tensor_min Z)
```

is an isomorphism.  The algebra need not be simple or unital.

This gives a non-simple positive class for STW Problem LXXVI; the conclusion
is stronger at the C*-level because such `A` is Z-stable.
