Let `C` be a unital C*-algebra.  In the metric space of separable
C*-subalgebras of `C` that contain `1_C`, the Z-stable algebras form a closed
subset.  Equivalently, if `A_n` are Z-stable, all contain `1_C`, and
`d(A_n,B) -> 0`, then `B` is Z-stable.

This qualitative zero-distance result does not provide the positive uniform
radius asked for in Problem XCIX.
