Literature import of Meshulam, arXiv:1308.3769, main theorem as stated in the
abstract. The extension to `p = n^(-1+η)` is the monotonicity argument in the
claim body: `p = n^(-1+η) >= (6+7c) log n / n` for large `n`, and the property is
increasing in the triangle set.
