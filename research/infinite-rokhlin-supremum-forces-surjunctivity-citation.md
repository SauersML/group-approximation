Citation import from B. Seward, *Krieger's finite generator theorem for actions of countable groups II*, arXiv:1501.03367v4. The PDF text was extracted on MSI on 2026-09-12, and the statements below were read there.

* **Theorem 6.6.** For `H(L, lambda) < infinity`, `h^Rok_G(L^G, lambda^G) = min{H(L, lambda), h^Rok_sup(G)}`.
* **Theorem 7.6.** For `H(L, lambda) = infinity`, `h^Rok_G(L^G, lambda^G) = infinity` iff some free ergodic p.m.p. action of `G` has positive Rokhlin entropy.
* **Corollary 4.1.** "Assume that hRok(kG;uG) = log(k) for every k ∈ N. Then G satisﬁes Gottschalk’s surjunctivity conjecture and Kaplansky’s direct ﬁniteness conjecture."

Seward's introduction summarizes these as `INF => RBS => INV+CPE+GOT+KAP`. The artifact records the statements verbatim.
