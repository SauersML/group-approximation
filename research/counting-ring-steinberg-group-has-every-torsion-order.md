**Statement.** Let `R_count = Z<u,x,e | xu-ux-x, ue, eu>` and `r_n = e x^n e`.
Fix `m >= 4`, let `G = St_m(R_count)`, and let `G_res` be the intersection of
all finite-index normal subgroups of `G` (the finite residual).

- **(S1)** `G` is finitely presented and has property (T).
- **(S2)** For each `n >= 1`, `x_12(r_n)` has order exactly `n` in `G`. Its image
  in the finite quotient `E_m(M_n(Z/n))` also has order exactly `n`.
- **(S3)** `G_res` lies in `K_2(m, R_count) = ker(St_m(R_count) -> E_m(R_count))`.
- **(S4)** `G / G_res` is a finitely generated residually finite Kazhdan group
  with an element of order exactly `n` for every `n >= 1`.

**Consequence.** If `G_res` is the normal closure of finitely many elements, then
`G / G_res` is finitely presented. It would then prove
`fp-rf-group-with-unbounded-prime-torsion`. That is the open premise
`counting-steinberg-residual-finitely-normally-generated` of the route
`unbounded-prime-torsion-via-counting-ring-steinberg`.
