There is a representation of `C(K_2^+)` in `M_2(C)` for which a fundamental
matrix entry is nonnormal.  Therefore finite dimensionality alone does not
force a weak/free quantum reflection to factor through `H_2^(infinity+)`,
and hence cannot justify replacing weak Hadamard equivalence by strong
Hadamard equivalence in finite-dimensional models.

This uses the definition of `K_n^+` as the free complexification of `H_n^+`
in Brannan--Gromada--Hernandez Palomares--Priebe,
*Complex Hadamard Matrices -- Quantum Symmetries, Equivalence and Non-Local
Games*, arXiv:2608.14858v1, Section 3.3.
