For the degree-four length-ten cyclic-cover profile `(1,3,3,3)`, eliminate
the unary relator and call the three residual relators `R0,R1,R2`.  If any two
are already identities in the free product of the four coefficient copies,
then every coefficient-copy retraction of the third is also the identity.

Consequently no direct two-source design can make the remaining relator equal
to `h` in one coefficient copy.  This is a global copy-flux obstruction, not
a bounded packet census.
