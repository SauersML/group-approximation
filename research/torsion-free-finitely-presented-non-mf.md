There exists a torsion-free finitely presented group that is not MF in the
norm-matrix-corona sense.

The present finite-normal mechanism cannot prove this directly: every finite
subgroup of a torsion-free group is trivial, whereas its final obstruction is
a nontrivial finite normal subgroup.
