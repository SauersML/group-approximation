For `gKg^(-1) <= K`, both finite groups have cardinality `|K|`, hence they
are equal and `g` normalizes `K`.  Thus the compressor semigroup is the
normalizer subgroup itself.  If that semigroup generates `G`, the normalizer
is all of `G`.
