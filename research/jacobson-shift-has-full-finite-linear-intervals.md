In `Theta_shift`, put `alpha=Ad_t`, let `H` be the subgroup
generated by the original letters, and set `K_i=alpha^i(K)` and
`Theta_i=alpha^i(H)` for every integer `i`. Then

```text
<K_i,K_(i+1)> ~= GL_9(F_2),
[K_i,Theta_(i+2)]=1.
```

More generally, for every integer `j` and `n>=2`,

```text
<K_j,...,K_(j+n-2)> ~= GL_(3n)(F_2),
```

with the specified elementary roots on levels `j,...,j+n-1`
and their standard inclusions. These groups are faithful under the
literal bilateral action. The abstract relations are proved first;
faithfulness of the displayed action is then used to exclude a
kernel, rather than to infer relations from a quotient.

A compatible `GL_4` seed extends two overlapping general linear
groups by one coordinate. Applying this to successive packets gives
`GL_7,GL_8,GL_9`; the resulting commutation of neighboring pure
swaps gives centralization of entire distant packets. This permits
induction with matching root words on every overlap.

The finite-field presentation input is Steinberg's Section 6,
Theorem 9, already used for the six-coordinate packet. No estimate
uniform over matrix approximation dimensions is proved here.
