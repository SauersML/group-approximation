**ESTABLISHED** through `approximate-burnside-models-low-degree-mass-proof`.

**Setting.** Fix `m, N >= 2` and `F = F_m`. Let `u^(k) in U(d_k)^m` be an
approximate exponent-N model in the sense of
`hyperlinear-bounded-exponent-trace-rigidity-iff-finiteness`:
`|| w(u^(k))^N - I ||_2 -> 0` for every word `w`, and `tr w(u^(k)) -> tau(w)`.
The unital `*`-algebra generated by `u^(k)` in `M_(d_k)` is a direct sum of
matrix blocks, so `C^(d_k)` splits canonically into isotypic components of
the representation `w -> w(u^(k))` of `F`. For `c >= 1` let `P_k^(c)` be the
projection onto the sum of the isotypic components whose irreducible type has
dimension `<= c`, and put

```text
alpha_k(c) = tr P_k^(c)          (low-degree trace mass).
```

Let `K_c` be the intersection of the kernels of all homomorphisms
`B(m,N) -> U(d)`, `d <= c`, pulled back to `F`, and `C(m,N,c) = [F : K_c]`.
It is finite (route, Step 0).

**Statement.**
1. **Mass bound.** Suppose `tau` is the regular trace of an `m`-generated group
   `G = F/L` (so `tau(w) = 1` if `w in L` and `0` otherwise). Then

   ```text
   limsup_k alpha_k(c) <= C(m,N,c) / |G|     for every c.
   ```

   If `G` is infinite, `alpha_k(c) -> 0` for every `c`.
2. **Robust form.** Suppose instead that for each `k` there are orthogonal
   subspaces `V_(k,1), ..., V_(k,r_k)` of dimension `<= c`, with projections
   `P_(k,j)`, `W_k` their orthogonal complement, `beta_k = sum_j tr P_(k,j)`, and

   ```text
   delta_k = max_i || u_i^(k) - E_k(u_i^(k)) ||_2 -> 0,
   E_k(x) = sum_j P_(k,j) x P_(k,j) + P_(W_k) x P_(W_k).
   ```

   Then under the hypothesis of clause 1, `limsup_k beta_k <= C(m,N,c)/|G|`,
   and `beta_k -> 0` when `G` is infinite.

## Consequences

- **Invariant and step where the class dies.** The invariant is low-degree
  trace mass. Every counterexample `G` to
  `hyperlinear-fg-bounded-exponent-groups-are-finite` is an infinite group of
  exponent `N`, and its microstates are approximate exponent-N models with the
  regular trace. By clause 1, for every `c` their mass on irreducible pieces of
  dimension `<= c` tends to 0. By clause 2, no model can be Hilbert--Schmidt
  close to block-diagonal with bounded blocks on a non-vanishing fraction of
  the space. This kills, uniformly, every construction assembling microstates
  from bounded-dimensional pieces: direct sums of low-dimensional (projective
  or approximate) representations, averages over a compact family of small
  representation varieties, and microstates of groups all of whose irreducible
  representations have bounded dimension (virtually abelian with bounded index,
  bounded-degree linear, and so on). The death step is Step 3 of the route: the
  weak-* limit of the small pieces lives on `Hom(B(m,N), U(<= c))`, whose
  points all kill the finite-index subgroup `K_c`, and positive-definiteness
  on `sum_(j <= s) w_j` with `w_j in K_c` caps the mass by `1/s`.
- **Where a counterexample must live.** The trace mass has to escape to
  irreducible pieces of unbounded dimension that are not HS-almost reducible
  into bounded blocks. So the approximating algebras are genuinely
  high-dimensional in the sense of their irreducible types. This strengthens
  `bounded-dimension-microstates-bounded-exponent-group-is-finite` (total
  dimension) to a statement about the spectrum of the approximating algebra.
- **The finite exact counterpart** is
  `partial-burnside-finite-quotients-low-degree-plancherel-bound`, which gives a
  radius that is uniform over all modules for abelian-by-bounded tops in the
  Wave 4 census.
