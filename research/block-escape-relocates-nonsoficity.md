title: Orbit resolution fails through invariant blocks, and every separated block escape inherits nonsoficity
---

Fixing almost all members of an orbit of subsets need not fix almost all
underlying sites: invariant block systems give exact counterexamples.

In the Kun--Thom coset setting, every invariant block quotient that still
separates the marked pair yields an intermediate coset action and wreath
quotient carrying the same sofic-radical mark.  That quotient and its action
remain nonsofic, so block escape relocates rather than resolves the problem.
