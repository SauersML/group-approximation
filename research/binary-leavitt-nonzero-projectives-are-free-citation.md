Literature import. Bergman (Trans. AMS 200 (1974), Theorem 6.1) computes
`V(L_K(1,n)) = <x | x = n x>`. At `n = 2` the relation `x = 2x` leaves the two classes `0` and
`x = [R]`, so a nonzero finitely generated projective has class `[R]`, and since `V` is the monoid
of isomorphism classes, it is isomorphic to `R_R`. Ara--Moreno--Pardo (2007), Theorem 3.5, give
the same monoid as the graph monoid of one vertex with two loops.
