**ESTABLISHED.**  In

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,             Q=1-ST,
E=EL_3(J),                     w=x_13(Q),
```

put

```text
h=[[Q,S,0],[T,0,0],[0,0,1]],
g=x_31(1).                                                   (JPF1)
```

Thus `h` is the literal balanced S/T-bearing involution from `(J3GP2)`, and
`g` is one raw constant root outside the monomial-symbol subgroup.  For every
fixed `N`, let `C_N` be the `N`-pair finitary Clifford packet of
`binary-jacobson-head-sector-has-native-clifford-tower`, embedded in any
rank-three block containing the head.  Then

```text
H_N=<h,g,C_N>                                                (JPF2)
```

is a finite group, and its element `w in C_N` is nontrivial.  Hence `H_N`
has a faithful finite-dimensional unitary representation retaining the head
and the complete degree-`2^N` Pauli packet.

The natural representation-dependent polar candidate is genuinely present,
not vacuous.  In the left regular representation of `H_N`, set

```text
P=(1-lambda(w))/2,
A=P lambda(g) P.                                            (JPF3)
```

Then `P!=0` and `A!=0`, so the polar decomposition of `A` has nonzero source
and range.  The same is true for the S/T-returned occurrence because

```text
[h,w]=1,
P lambda(hgh) P=lambda(h) A lambda(h).                       (JPF4)
```

Nevertheless these polar operators cannot authenticate the common PI return
required by `opnorm-common-intertwiner-pi-gap-is-sparse-terminal`: the faithful
finite model has zero relator defect, a nonzero head cut, and the full fixed
Clifford target.  Any purported universal conclusion producing the terminal
common intertwiner from only `(JPF2)--(JPF4)` would contradict that exact
model.

Thus polar decomposition itself is not the missing selector.  One raw
opposite root, its literal S/T return, and arbitrarily large but fixed
finitary packet depth remain finite and marked.  A load-bearing Jacobson
selector must consume an additional raw incidence whose interaction makes
the monomial actor explore an unbounded part of the finitary kernel; merely
polarizing the first opposite-root compression does not cross the seam.
