Citation import, read on 2026-09-13 from the PDF of arXiv:1607.03790v2, page 4:

"Note that in any virtually cyclic group there are only finitely many distinct finite subgroups
up to conjugacy (using Lemma 1.4 or the fact that virtually cyclic groups are CAT(0)). Using
this fact one immediately obtains
Lemma 1.7. If a group G has BVC, then G has finitely many conjugacy classes of finite
subgroups. In particular, the order of finite subgroups in G is bounded."
