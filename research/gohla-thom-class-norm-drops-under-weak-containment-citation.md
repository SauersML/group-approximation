Citation import. arXiv:2403.09582v2 was fetched with `curl` on MSI on 2026-09-13 and read page by page from the
PDF, pages 4--20. The statements quoted verbatim in Section 1 of the artifact are:
- Definition 2.2 (weak containment as containment in a metric ultraproduct);
- Definition 2.8(i) (cosystolic inequality);
- Theorem 2.10 (lattices in `PSp_2d(Q_p)`, `d >= 4`, large `p`);
- Lemma 2.13 (isometric injection into ultraproducts; surjectivity needs expansion, the isometry does not);
- Corollary 2.14 and the displayed inequality in its proof;
- Theorem 2.16 and its proof sketch;
- Theorem 3.4 (vanishing on `A`-invariants of `A`-free algebras);
- Theorem 3.18 (following Deligne; non-residually-finite extension by `Z/(p-1)`);
- Remark 3.14.

Item 1 of the claim is the chain of inequalities in the proof of Corollary 2.14. The (first) isometry half of Lemma
2.13 is proved in the source without any expansion hypothesis, and that is the only half used downstream.
