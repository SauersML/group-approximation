Verbatim.

- **p. 1.** "(4) G_p: For 1 ≤ p < ∞, the Schatten p-norm defined by
  ||A||_p = Tr(|A|^p)^{1/p} (For p = 2, this is the Frobenius norm)." "(3) G_HS:
  The (normalized) Hilbert–Schmidt norm, or simply HS-norm, ||A||_HS =
  (1/√n)||A||_Frob."
- **p. 2.** "In response, Lubotzky and Oppenheim [17] solved almost all cases and
  gave non-G_p-approximated groups for 1 < p < ∞. These examples are the so
  called 'ℓ-adic Deligne central extensions'". "We prove that ℓ-adic Deligne
  central extensions are not G_1-approximated". "Whether all groups are sofic,
  hyperlinear or MF are three major open problems in group theory." "The reader
  is referred to [7, 8, 15, 6] for efforts to prove they are not sofic, and to
  [12] for efforts to prove that real Deligne central extensions are not
  hyperlinear."
- **p. 3, Definition 1.2.** "A finitely generated group Γ is said to be of
  Deligne-type if there is a central element J ∈ Γ of order 2 such that
  J ∈ ker_RF(Γ)."
- **p. 3, Theorem 1.3.** "Let Γ be a group of Deligne-type, and let
  1 ≤ p ≤ q ≤ ∞. If Γ is G_q-stable, then it is not G_p-approximated. In
  particular, if Γ is Frobenius stable, then it is not G_p-approximated for any
  1 ≤ p ≤ 2."
- **p. 3, Corollary 1.4.** "There exist finitely presented groups which are not
  G_1-approximated." Next paragraph: "In fact, by the more recent work of
  [3, Theorem 1.3] and the original work of Deligne [11], it also follows that the
  degree 4-cover of Sp_2g(Z), for g ≥ 3, is not G_1-approximated."
- **p. 3, Proposition 1.5.** "Let Γ be a group of Deligne type. Assume that the
  following holds: (∗) For every ||·||_op-asymptotic homomorphism
  φ_n : Γ → U(k_n), there exists a sequence of homomorphisms ψ_n : Γ → U(k_n)
  such that for all x ∈ Γ, lim_n ||φ_n(x) − ψ_n(x)||_HS = 0. Then Γ is not MF."
- **p. 3, Proposition 1.7.** "Let 1 ≤ p ≤ q ≤ ∞. Let Γ be a finitely generated
  group with a finite normal subgroup N such that Γ/N is G_q-approximated. If Γ
  is G_p-approximated, then it is G_q-approximated."
- **p. 3, Conjecture 1.8.** "Let Γ be a finitely generated group such that
  H^2(Γ, V) = 0 for every unitary representation of Γ on a Hilbert space V, then
  Γ is operator-HS-stable."
- **p. 4, Lemma 2.2** (from [9]). "Let A ∈ U(d). Then there is a unitary B ∈ U(d)
  such that B^2 = 1 and ||B − A|| ≤ ||1 − A^2||, for all unitarily invariant
  norms."
- **p. 5, proof of Theorem 1.3.** "Since U_n^2 = 1, this implies that it must
  have an eigenvector with eigenvalue −1, which implies ||U_n − 1||_q ≥
  ||U_n − 1||_op ≥ 2." **Remark 3.1:** "In the above proof the fact that J is
  central is never used".
- **p. 7, references.** [3] U. Bader, A. Lubotzky, R. Sauer, S. Weinberger,
  *Stability and instability of lattices in semisimple groups*, J. d'Analyse
  Mathématique 151 (2023), 1–23. [12] A. Dogon, *Flexible Hilbert-Schmidt
  stability versus hyperlinearity for property (T) groups*, Math. Z. 305 (2023),
