The elementary group functor preserves finite products, giving the displayed
factorization of `E_n(R)`.  A nonidentity element of order `p` acts in the
left regular representation as disjoint `p`-cycles.  Its `1`-eigenspace has
one dimension per cycle and therefore normalized dimension `1/p`.
The tensor-product elements in `(RNSA1)` are again nonidentity order-`p`
permutations with the same cycle calculation.  This proves `(RNSA2)` and
the failure of additivity.
