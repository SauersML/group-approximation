Establishes the equivalence in the target from published theorems.

- Gow, arXiv:2601.04431v2, Theorem 4.11 (Theorem A): 2Q ⟺ every type `II_1`
  AW*-factor is a W*-factor. Already imported as
  `stw01-gow-four-way-equivalence-citation`.
- Berberian [Ber83, Theorem 3]: an AW*-algebra is a W*-algebra iff it is normal
  and has a large W*-corner.
- Saitô–Wright 1991 [SW91, Corollary 4.7]: every AW*-factor is normal; hence for
  factors the criterion reduces to possession of a large W*-corner.
- Arulseelan–Hanson, arXiv:2609.10218v1, Corollary 1.5: an AW*-algebra is a
  W*-algebra iff it has a large W*-corner (removes normality in general).

This route imports an equivalence only. It establishes neither of the two
equivalent open assertions, and must not be read as a route into
`stw99-problem-i-quasitraces-are-traces`.
