Let `A,B` be C*-subalgebras of a common `B(H)`.  If `A` is Z-stable and

```text
d(A,B) < 1/6422957,
```

then

```text
d_cb(A,B) < 1/42
```

and the scaled Cuntz semigroups `(Cu(A),Sigma(A))` and
`(Cu(B),Sigma(B))` are isomorphic.

This does not imply that `B` is Z-stable without additional hypotheses.
