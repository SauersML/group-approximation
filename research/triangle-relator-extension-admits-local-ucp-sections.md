OPEN CLAIM.  There is an infinite finitely presented simple Kazhdan
group `Lambda` as supplied by `simple-kazhdan-kac-moody-lattices-exist`
(e.g. `Lambda_(2,4,6)(q)`, `q > 1764^3`) together with a
triangle-of-finite-groups presentation `Lambda = colim P_J` satisfying
(H1)-(H2) of `triangle-colimit-llp-reduces-to-relator-local-splitting`,
such that the associated relator extension

```text
0 -> I_N -> C*(G_0) -> C*(Lambda) -> 0,
G_0 = P_12 *_{P_2} P_23 virtually free,  I_N generated by the
r free relators of N = ker(P_1 *_{P_0} P_3 ->> P_13),  r as in
the equivalence node's index formula,
```

is locally ucp-split: every finite-dimensional operator system of
`C*(Lambda)` admits a ucp lift into `C*(G_0)`.

By the equivalence node this is exactly LLP of `C*(Lambda)`, and via
`llp-hole-via-triangle-relator-local-sections` it closes the program
hole `non-rf-kazhdan-group-with-llp-full-c-star-algebra`, hence the
goal.  Stakes both ways: refuting it for all candidates makes every
simple Kazhdan Kac--Moody lattice an LLP failure of a new kind (no
finite-dimensional representations at all — no
Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma mechanism), per
`kac-moody-lattice-is-non-hyperlinear-or-llp-failure`.

## Attempts

- **The presentation half is cheap; the sections are the hard half.**
  The colimit presentation of 2-spherical lattices by rank-`<= 2`
  finite subgroups is Abramenko--Mühlherr [AM97], C. R. Acad. Sci.
  Paris 325 (1997) 701-706, identified at source through
  Caprace--Rémy (who consume it for finite presentation at
  `q_min > 2`); reading [AM97] itself and checking (H1)-(H2) for its
  exact vertex subgroups is a bounded literature task that any
  establishment of this claim must include.  Caution from
  `km-lattices-admit-no-cocompact-npc-model`: whatever those vertex
  subgroups are, the resulting triangle is NOT nonpositively curved
  and its links contain short cycles — no NPC geometry of the
  presentation complex is available to power the sections.
- **Patterned Gram lifting (necessary form).**  A local section on
  `span{u_g : g in F^{-1}F}` forces a positive lift of the
  `Lambda`-Gram matrix `[u_{g^{-1}h}]` over `C*(G_0)` with unit
  diagonal and entries constant on difference classes.
  Paulsen--Rodman chordal elimination
  (`chordal-partial-positive-operator-matrices-lift`) dies here: it
  completes free entries one simplicial vertex at a time, while the
  difference-pattern constraint couples every completed entry to its
  whole class, and no elimination order respects it.  A genuinely
  new completion argument would have to update whole difference
  classes at once (a group-equivariant Schur complement), which no
  published tool provides.
- **The naive set-theoretic section dies on positivity, not on
  algebra.**  Choosing coset representatives `sigma : Lambda -> G_0`
  and mapping `u_g-bar to u_{sigma(g)}` is well defined on any
  operator system where `pi` is injective on supports, but cp-ness
  fails structurally: positivity over `C*(Lambda)` is tested only
  against representations killing `N`, positivity over `C*(G_0)`
  against all representations, including those detecting `N` —
  matrices that are `Lambda`-positive with small support need not be
  `G_0`-positive.  Any successful section must damp the
  `N`-detecting representations (an averaging/dilation step), and
  property (T) of `Lambda` is exactly what makes crude averaging
  suspicious: a `G_0`-equivariant or global construction would give
  a *global* ucp splitting, i.e. the LP, which is expected to fail
  (see next bullet).  Approaches must therefore be irreducibly
  local.
- **Global splitting is plausibly dead, and that is consistent.**
  By the equivalence node, global ucp splitting equals the LP of
  `C*(Lambda)`; its expected failure is the separate open claim
  `compact-hyperbolic-km-lattices-fail-lp` (ISW Corollaries D/E,
  source-verified; the building-side `H^2_c` is nonzero by DDJMO
  plus the proper-retraction argument, and only the bridge to
  `H^2(Lambda, Z Lambda)` or `H^2(Lambda, R)` is missing — both
  cohomologies UNRECORDED; an earlier vanishing assertion for
  `H^2(Lambda; R)` in this bullet was WRONG and is repaired,
  2026-08-21).  Nothing published refutes LOCAL splitting here,
  matching the every-mechanism-vacuous audits
  (`twin-lattices-exclude-known-llp-fences`).
- **Effros--Haagerup positive permanence is mute.**  Local splitting
  is automatic when the quotient is nuclear (Choi--Effros /
  Effros--Haagerup); `C*(Lambda)` is as far from nuclear as
  possible (`Lambda` infinite Kazhdan simple, no finite-dimensional
  representations), so no known positive criterion applies — the
  claim sits strictly beyond the published local-splitting
  technology, which is the point of the hole.
- **The averaging bullet above is now sharp, in both directions
  (2026-08-23).**  "Crude averaging is suspicious under property (T)"
  has become a theorem with an exact scope.
  `position-window-ucp-sections-collapse-to-folner-vectors` proves
  that *any* section of orthogonal-position/window shape —
  coefficients `c_g in C*(G_0)` indexed by group positions, middle
  factors arbitrary group words, `sum_g c_g^* c_g = 1` — collapses
  under the trivial character to a finitely supported
  right-translation-invariant `l^2(Lambda)` vector, which cannot
  exist; with a Kazhdan constant `kappa` the approximate form already
  costs `delta >= kappa^2/2`.  Positivity is never used, so the kill
  is of the shape and not of any estimate, and it applies to *local*
  sections too, since local splitting must in particular handle the
  system spanned by a finite generating set.  The same computation
  over the finite face succeeds —
  `explicit-finite-face-ucp-section-is-edge-equivariant` gives a
  closed-form ucp section of `C*(P_13)` into `C*(E)` which is a right
  `C*(P_1)`-module map, so the defect is concentrated on one edge —
  and the boundary between the two cases is exactly finiteness of the
  index set.  Any surviving construction must use nonorthogonal,
  off-fibre interference.
- **The hole now has one live route (2026-08-23).**
  `local-ucp-sections-from-finite-relator-reservoir` reduces it to
  `finite-relator-reservoir-repairs-every-fd-packet`: a bounded
  word-depth statement about corrections drawn from the native relator
  span `span{u_a(u_n-1)u_b}`, upgraded to an exact section by the RFD
  compactness lemma
  `finite-reservoir-compactness-lifts-ucp-repairs`.  Two facts frame
  what that leaf can cost.  By
  `fd-representation-packets-never-obstruct-ucp-sections` every finite
  packet of finite-dimensional representations is repairable
  unconditionally, so no finite-dimensional computation can decide the
  leaf, and none can decide this claim either; and the leaf's own
  Attempts show the packetwise statement is already a theorem once the
  reservoir is allowed to depend on the packet.  The whole open
  content is uniformity of the relator depth in the packet.
