For each of the two marked order-1920 local rectangle images in the complete
degree-at-most-twelve census, there is a unique elementary abelian normal
subgroup

```text
V ~= C2^5,          Q/V ~= A5.                              (Q1920-MOD-1)
```

The only normal subgroup orders are `1,2,32,1920`; in particular the central
line `Z(Q)` of order two has no invariant order-sixteen complement inside
`V`.  The quotient `V/Z(Q)` is irreducible: the two noncentral module orbits
both have size fifteen and each spans all of `V`.  The extension by `A5`
does split, with four conjugacy classes of complements.

Let `A=<r,u>` and `B=<y,b>` be the two radial `S3` subgroups.  Both inject
into `Q/V`.  Their images meet in one involution and generate `A5`, whereas
`A cap B=1` upstairs.  In both markings, the third labeled involution in
`A` and the third labeled involution in `B` project to that shared element.
Their quotient in `Q` is a noncentral involution `v in V`; its conjugacy
orbit has size fifteen and

```text
normal_closure_Q(v) = V,          <A,B> = Q.                 (Q1920-MOD-2)
```

Thus the entire order-32 kernel is exactly the normal holonomy generated by
the mismatch between the two radial lifts.  This is the structural feature
which separates the surviving order-1920 classes from the A5 and PSL3(2)
local completions.
