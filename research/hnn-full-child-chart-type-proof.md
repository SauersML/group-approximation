Conjugacy of every basis element `c in C_0` extends linearly to the group
algebra and proves `(HFC1)`.  The statement for padded charts follows by
applying the product isomorphism.  Britton's lemma proves base injectivity.
In the approximate setting the standard finite Haar average produces an
intertwiner within the maximum covariance defect; its polar unitary still
intertwines the exact finite representations.  Functional calculus then
transports all central type projections simultaneously.
