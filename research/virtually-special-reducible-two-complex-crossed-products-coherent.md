Let `G` be virtually compact special and the fundamental group of a finite
reducible two-complex without proper powers. For every division ring `k`,
every crossed product

`k * G`

is a coherent ring.

In particular, for every field `k` of arbitrary characteristic, `k[G]` is
coherent. This supplies the positive-characteristic conclusion for every
virtually compact special torsion-free one-relator group.
