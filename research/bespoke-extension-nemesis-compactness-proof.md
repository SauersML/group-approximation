Projection of the compact type space of exact gadget extensions makes every
`C_i` closed.  The complements cover the compact separated base-type space,
so a finite subcover gives `(BEN2)`.  A full microstate ultraproduct would
extend every fixed gadget and hence lie in the empty intersection.
