D. Osin and A. Thom, *Normal generation and ℓ²-Betti numbers of groups*, arXiv:1108.2411v2
(Math. Ann. 355, 2013). Read from the TeX source `norm_3.tex`, fetched on MSI on 2026-09-14.

- **Theorem `main2`, l.103–108**, verbatim: "Let $G$ be a finitely generated group. If $G$ is a
  limit of left orderable amenable groups in the space of marked group presentations, then
  \beta_{1}^{(2)}(G) \leq \beta_1(G)-1 \leq \nrk(G)-1."
- **Abstract, l.63**: "The conjecture is proved for limits of left-orderable amenable groups."
- **l.472**, the authors' own assessment: "the inequality (\ref{naive}) is derived from a stronger
  inequality $\beta_{1}^{(2)}(G) \leq \beta_1(G)-1$. This means that our theorem does not have any
  nontrivial applications to either of the above mentioned open problems." The problems named
  there are Wiegold's, Kervaire's and Whitehead's (l.466–470).

The paper's proof was not re-derived. `β₁^(2)` in the source is the first ℓ²-Betti number of the
group, which this graph writes `b₁^(2)`.
