Suppose the second half of Problem LXIV has a positive answer: two
DISTINCT unit-compatible Cuntz-semigroup morphisms
`Cu(Z) → Cu(A)` into a stable-rank-one Villadsen--Toms algebra `A`.
`Z` is an inductive limit of one-dimensional NCCW complexes with
trivial `K_1`, and `A` has stable rank one (Elliott--Ho--Toms), so
Robert's classification of homomorphisms from such domains into
stable-rank-one targets applies in BOTH directions: the existence half
lifts each scale-compatible `Cu`-morphism to a unital *-homomorphism
`Z → A` inducing it, and the uniqueness half says approximate unitary
equivalence of the lifts would force equality of the two morphisms.
Unital homomorphisms from the simple `Z` are embeddings.  So the two
lifts are unital Jiang--Su embeddings with distinct `Cu`-maps —
exactly the target refuter, and with it Problem LXIII fails.

Consistency check: the stw63 audit's item 14 proves `Cu`-map equality
on stable-rank-one targets WITH strict comparison; the Villadsen
algebras of the first type fail strict comparison, which is precisely
the room a Gardella--Perera pair must exploit.  Conversely, a positive
LXIII forces a negative answer to LXIV's two-morphism question on
every stable-rank-one target — the two STW problems are a linked
pair through Robert's functor.
