**Lemma (finite subgroups are rank stable).** Let `H` be finite and `s : H -> GL_n(F)` a map with
`rk(s(g)s(h) − s(gh)) <= εn` for all `g, h`. Then there is a representation `ρ` of `H` with
`rk(ρ(h) − s(h)) <= (|H|+1)|H|² εn` for all `h`.
* Put `V_0 = ∩_(g,h) ker(s(g)s(h) − s(gh))` and `U = V_0 ∩ ∩_h s(h)^(−1) V_0`.
* `U` is `s(H)`-invariant and `s` is exact on it.
* Take `ρ = s|_U ⊕ id` on a complement.

**Moving levels.** Let `σ = lim_ω σ_k` be a rank model of `G`, and `H_1 <= H_2 <= ...` finite
subgroups. There are `N_k -> ∞` along ω and representations `ρ_k` of `H_(N_k)` with
`max_(h ∈ H_(N_k)) rk(σ_k(h) − ρ_k(h))/n_k -> 0`.

**Normal form for `R^x = EL_3(L_(F_2)(1,2))`.** Let `σ` be a nontrivial characteristic-two model and
`H_N ≅ GL_(3N)(F_2)` the finitary level subgroups generated by `x_ij(s0^a (1 − s0 t0) t0^b)`,
`a, b < N`.
* `ρ_k` has transvection displacement `-> c_0 = rk(σ(x_13(1 − s0 t0)) − 1) > 0`.
* So every nontrivial element of `H_(N_k)` has `ρ_k`-displacement `>= c_0/4 − o(1)` at stage `k`.
* Some generator `g` of `R^x` has `lim_ω dist_rk(σ_k(g), ρ_k(H_(N_k))) > 0`. Otherwise, rounding the
  generators into `ρ_k(H_(N_k))` and evaluating words gives a level-factoring model with the same
  limit, which `transvection-displacement-forces-exact-local-embedding` excludes.
* Conjugation by `x_12(t0)` is locally inner on `H_M`, via `γ_M = 1 + T_(M+1) E_12 ∈ H_(M+1)`.
  For each fixed `M`, at least one of the corrections `ρ_k(γ_M)^(−1) σ_k(x_12(t0))`,
  `ρ_k(γ'_M)^(−1) σ_k(x_23(s0))` and `ρ_k(γ''_M)^(−1) σ_k(x_13(1))` is not `o(1)`. Otherwise the
  Toeplitz commutator fails by `ρ_k(u_13(M,M))`, which has displacement `c_0`.
