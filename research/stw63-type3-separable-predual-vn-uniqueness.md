Let `M` be a type-III von Neumann algebra with separable predual.  Then every
two unital embeddings

```text
phi,psi:Z->M
```

are approximately unitarily equivalent in norm.

The factor case is the direct specialization of Hua--White Theorem A.1.  The
nonfactor case is not merely pointwise factor uniqueness: the proof below
uses measurable selection to assemble, for each finite set and tolerance, a
single decomposable unitary having the required uniform essential-supremum
bound.
