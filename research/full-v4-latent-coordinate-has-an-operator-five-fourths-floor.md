Use the full translation packet `T_f` from `(VRC1)`.  On a forbidden selector
atom `a`, let

```text
e_(a,r),       r in F_2^2,                              (VOF1)
```

be the four joint spectral projections of the two commuting radical words.
They are mutually orthogonal, sum to the forbidden selector projection
`p_a`, and have equal rank in every finite-dimensional `T_f` module by the
transitive `V_4` action.

Choose the affine radical coordinates and the three failure conventions of
`s3-radical-affine-contexts-have-five-fourths-floor`.  They define three
commuting projections `F_(a,00),F_(a,01),F_(a,11)`, each a fixed sum of the
four projections in `(VOF1)`.  The four pointwise failure triples are

```text
000 : (1,0,1),
011 : (0,1,0),
101 : (0,0,1),
110 : (1,0,0).                                        (VOF2)
```

Therefore, as an exact operator identity in the forbidden corner,

```text
F_(a,00)+F_(a,01)+F_(a,11)=p_a+e_(a,000).             (VOF3)
```

Summing over all forbidden selector atoms gives explicit commuting packet
projections `F_00,F_01,F_11`, the forbidden predicate projection `P_f`, and
an explicit positive projection `E_0` such that

```text
F_00+F_01+F_11=P_f+E_0 >= P_f.                        (VOF4)
```

Since all four radical atoms have equal multiplicity,

```text
tau(E_0)=tau(P_f)/4,
tau(F_00)+tau(F_01)+tau(F_11)=5 tau(P_f)/4.            (VOF5)
```

All operators in `(VOF4)` are effective finite linear combinations of packet
words: selector atoms and radical atoms are products of `(1 plus_or_minus g)/2`
for commuting involutions.  After flexible normalized-HS exactification of
the fixed finite group `T_f`, `(VOF4)--(VOF5)` persist up to the standard
`o(1)` discarded mass and fixed word-telescoping loss.

This closes the common-coordinate extraction problem for the three S3
failures.  There is no longer a need to infer a common classical coupling
from three bistochastic transports.  It does **not** close groupification:
the left regular representation has positive `P_f` and satisfies every group
relator, so `(VOF4)` cannot by itself imply relator energy.  The remaining
operation must route the three named blocks to orthogonal recurrent exits,
or impose a finite-dimensional-only non-scalar multiplicity holonomy on
them.
