In the canonical regular-atlas setting of
`raw-swap-forces-two-s3-covariance-energy`, let

```text
K=<h_a,h_b> ~= S_3
```

be the six-element subgroup generated by the two raw second-chart letters and
let `E_K` denote the corresponding Reynolds projection.  Every separating
microstate sequence satisfies

```text
1-||E_K(U_n)||_2^2 >= 1/320-o(1).                     (SR320)
```

This improves the previously recorded `1/480` estimate.  The improvement uses
the fact that both named generator defects are controlled by the distance to
the **same** commutant projection, instead of merely retaining their two
nonnegative summands inside the six-term Reynolds average.
