No screened word of degree four, five, or six realizes the direct transport
compiler consisting of unary pivot corners and two trivalent relators that
conjugate the commuting source pair `(a,b)` to the noncommuting target pair
`(c,d)`.

The complete degree-four permutation screen uses the actual eight Leavitt
coefficients.  The sparse screens deliberately repeat an abstract tail packet
`x` and cover degrees through six and lengths through ten.  A successful
redesign must therefore use a coupled higher-valence relation or a longer
carrier outside these families; merely moving or duplicating one tail packet
in the short word does not work.
