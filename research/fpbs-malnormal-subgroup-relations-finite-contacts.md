**ESTABLISHED.** Let `Gamma` be a countably infinite group with a free p.m.p.
action `a` on `(X, mu)`. Let `Lambda <= Gamma` be a subgroup and `Phi ⊆ E_a` a
graphing of finite cost `c(Phi)`. Put

    I(gamma) = Lambda ∩ gamma^{-1} Lambda gamma,
    Q(Lambda) = { gamma : I(gamma) infinite }.

1. **Counting bound.** The number `N_gamma(x)` of `Phi`-edges joining the
   classes `Lambda x` and `Lambda gamma x` satisfies
   `∫ N_gamma dmu <= 2 |I(gamma)| c(Phi)`.
2. **Finite contacts.** There is a `Gamma`-invariant conull set on which any
   two `E_{a|Lambda}`-classes `Lambda y` and `Lambda gamma y` with
   `gamma notin Q(Lambda)` are joined by only finitely many `Phi`-edges.
3. **Obstruction.** Let `S` be any subrelation of `E_{a|Lambda}`, and use the
   criterion of `fpbs-infinite-contact-zero-relative-cost`. For every
   finite-cost generating graphing, the components of its infinite-contact
   class graph lie in `<Q(Lambda)>`-orbits. So the connectivity hypothesis can
   hold only if `<Q(Lambda)> = Gamma`. That condition forces `Lambda` to be
   wq-normal.
4. **Almost malnormal subgroups.** If `Lambda` lies in a proper almost
   malnormal subgroup `M`, then `<Q(Lambda)> ⊆ M`. The criterion then fails
   for every free action and every finite-cost graphing.
5. **`Gamma_mal`.** Take `Gamma_mal = union H_n` along `a -> a`,
   `b -> b a b^{-2}`. Its stages are malnormal (Stallings-graph certificate,
   `research/artifacts/fpbs-amalgam-descent-amenably-generated-2026-09-16.md`
   §5.1). So contact repair fails for every subgroup inside a stage, which
   includes every finitely generated subgroup and the stepwise pairs
   `(E_{H_{n+1}}, E_{H_n})`.

**The invariant and the step.**
- **Invariant.** `W_1(Lambda) = <Q(Lambda)>`.
- **Step.** Every "repair from a subgroup orbit relation by infinite contacts"
  dies at the connectivity hypothesis when `W_1(Lambda) != Gamma`, whatever
  the action and the graphing.

This does not assert `relC(E_a; E_{a|Lambda}) > 0`. The `L²` relative
cokernel bound gives no obstruction there.
