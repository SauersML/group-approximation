`F <= V`, and hyperlinearity passes to subgroups. In finitary form
(`thompson-f-hyperlinear-iff-relator-system-hs-unstable`): if unitary almost-solutions of the two Thompson
relators almost commute uniformly in the dimension, then `V` is not hyperlinear.
