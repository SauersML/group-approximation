Manzoor Theorem 3.5 proves that a game satisfying `(MIG1)` yields a
non-co-hyperlinear IRS on a free group.  The game's IRS strategy gives a trace
`tau` on the group generated by the answer involutions and the central
involution `J`.  Cutting with `1-J` gives the correlation trace.  If it were
amenable, its restriction would be a quantum-approximate correlation, contrary
to the strict value gap.  Amenable traces form a face, so the original IRS
trace is nonamenable as well.  Pullback along the natural free-group quotient
retains its GNS algebra and nonamenability.

Manzoor's main theorem then transports the example to every nonabelian free
group.  No step asserts that the IRS is Dirac or normal.
