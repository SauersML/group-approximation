title: Any non-CE twisted group factor gives a countable nonhyperlinear central extension
distinct_from:
  torsion-dense-schur-multiplier-compiles-twisted-nonce: that theorem produces a finite central extension, and under finite presentability can preserve finite presentation; the present theorem needs neither torsion approximation nor finite presentation and targets existence of a countable nonhyperlinear group.
---

Let `Gamma` be countable, let `omega in Z^2(Gamma,T)`, and suppose the
twisted group von Neumann algebra `L_omega(Gamma)` is not Connes embeddable.
Put

```text
C = subgroup of T generated by { omega(g,h) : g,h in Gamma }.
```

Then `C` is countable, `omega` is a `C`-valued cocycle, and the explicit
central extension

```text
Gamma_tilde = C x_omega Gamma,
(c,g)(d,h) = (c d omega(g,h), gh)
```

is a countable nonhyperlinear group.

In particular, neither a finite phase group nor torsion approximation of the
cohomology class is needed to solve the bare existence problem for a
nonhyperlinear group.  Those extra hypotheses are needed only when one wants
a finite central extension, a positive-trace central corner, or finite
presentability of the resulting extension.
