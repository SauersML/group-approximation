Choose `K,L,k` from the defect-core claim and take the trivial automorphism:

```text
G = K x Z.
```

Regard `L` as `L x {0}`.  The product formula for the intrinsic defect gives

```text
D_G(L x {0}) = D_K(L) x D_Z(1) = K x {0}.
```

Apply `split-cyclic-kazhdan-defect-exact-mf-radical`.  It yields every clause
of `(TFZ1)` and `(TFZ2)`.  Since `K` is finitely presented and
`K=<<k>>^K`, this route also supplies the preferred strengthening:
`Rad_MF(G)=K x {0}` is finitely presented and normally generated by `(k,0)`.
