The paired-word theorem replaces the unknown forbidden Reynolds subspace by
four fixed packet words.  On the forbidden carrier their algebra is `M_2^4`;
on the returned side it is `C^16`, so the adjoint return bound gives a fixed
normalized-HS payment unless mass leaves through character `011`.  The
one-sided reservoir theorem makes all successive `011` exits summable rather
than stationary.  Scaled Kazhdan transport bounds the four word drifts at the
actual carrier weight, and bounded word length turns them into the named local
relator energy required by `(CPR1)`.
