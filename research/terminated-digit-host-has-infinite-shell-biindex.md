For a fixed-base shell envelope with the specified terminated-digit
embedding E_nu<=H_(h,r), let S be the image of its original point
stabilizer (E_nu)_p. Then S has infinite bi-index in H_(h,r),
and in every overgroup of H_(h,r).

More precisely, there is a continuous E_nu-equivariant surjection
pi:C_X->C to the original binary Cantor space. For each L>=1,
a canonical prefix involution gamma_L in H_(h,r) maps the fiber
F_p=pi^-1(p) to a set meeting exactly r*h^L fibers. The number
of fibers met is invariant under both left and right multiplication
by S. The gamma_L therefore lie in distinct S double cosets.

In particular the existing embedding of the A5 arithmetic envelope
in H_(59,4), and then a finitely presented simple overgroup, does
not supply a finite-bi-index host for its retained shell stabilizer.
The simple-group embedding itself remains valid.
