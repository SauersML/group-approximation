Choose a nonzero coordinate `y_j` of the marked vector `y`.  Infinitely many
primes avoid the nonzero integer

```text
y_j product_i det(A_i),
```

so choose one such prime `p`.  Reduction modulo `p` makes `bar y` nonzero and
makes every `bar A_i` invertible.  Let

```text
V=(F_p)^n,
L=<bar A_1,...,bar A_r><=GL_n(F_p).
```

Both `V` and `L` are finite.  In the affine semidirect product `V rtimes L`,
send the base copy of `Z^n` to its reduction in `V` and send `t_i` to the
linear element `bar A_i`.  Conjugation by that element acts on `V` as
`bar A_i`, so for every base vector `v`,

```text
t_i v t_i^(-1) |-> bar A_i(bar v) = overline(A_i v).
```

Thus every defining relation of the multiple ascending HNN presentation is
satisfied and the universal property gives a homomorphism to the finite group
`V rtimes L`.  Its value at `y` is `bar y!=0`, proving the claim.  No
commutativity or amenability assumption on the subgroup generated by the
stable letters is used; only their finitely many induced integer matrices
enter the common congruence choice.
