Let `A` be simple and unital and suppose every nonzero projection in `A` is
infinite.  Put `u=[1_A]`.  Assume the following unit-level fragment of almost
unperforation in `Cu(A)`:

```text
for every x and n>=1,
(n+1)u <= n x  implies  u<=x.                 (UAU)
```

Then every nonzero element of `Cu(A)` equals `u`, `A` has property (SP), and
`A` is purely infinite.  In particular the conclusion holds whenever
`Cu(A)` is almost unperforated.

The hypothesis (UAU) only compares the compact unit with another element; no
global cancellation, stable rank, quasitrace, or regularity assumption is
used.
