If every relator in the finite presentation had operator-norm defect tending
to zero along a canonical microstate sequence, the generator classes would
define a homomorphism from `Q` into the norm matrix corona. Full MF residual
makes this homomorphism trivial. Hence every nonidentity word would tend to
the identity in operator norm, contradicting its canonical normalized-HS
distance `sqrt(2)`.

The finite relator list gives one relator and one positive operator-norm lower
bound on a subsequence. Its HS defect still tends to zero. Spectral Markov
inequality at half that lower bound produces a nonzero spectral projection of
trace at most four times the squared HS defect divided by the squared lower
bound, and this trace tends to zero.
