**Data.**
- A finite group `H`, variables `[n]`, and contexts `k in [m]` with nonempty
  variable sets `S_k`.
- Nonempty local relations `R_k subseteq H^(S_k)`.

**Torsor CFI graph `G_H(R)`.**  This is a colored digraph.
- Vertices are `(k, alpha)` with `alpha in R_k`, and vertex colour `k`.
- The arc from `(k,alpha)` to `(l,alpha')` has colour
  `(k, l, (alpha_i^(-1) alpha'_i)_(i in S_k ∩ S_l))`.

**Coset constraint algebra `A_H(S,T)`.**  For `T_k subseteq H^(S_k)` it is
generated by a PVM `(p^k_g)_(g in T_k)` for each context, with
`p^k_g p^l_(g') = 0` whenever `g` and `g'` disagree on `S_k ∩ S_l`.  These
are `H`-valued variables that commute inside a context, with joint local
spectrum in `T_k`.

**Theorem.**  For torsor CFI data `R, R'` on the same `(H,[n],S)`, put
`T_k = {g : g R_k = R'_k}`.  Then

```text
A(Iso(G_H(R), G_H(R'))) ≅ A_H(S,T),
v_((k,alpha),(k,beta)) |-> p^k_(beta alpha^(-1)),
```

and all cross-context entries are `0`.
- Each nonempty `T_k` is a left coset `h_k Stab(R_k)`.
- For `R' = R` the comultiplication is
  `Delta(p^k_g) = sum_(h in K_k) p^k_h (x) p^k_(g h^(-1))`, with `K_k = Stab(R_k)`.

**Corollary.**  The span of `{p^k_g}` is a Hopf *-subalgebra `≅ C(L_k^op)`,
where `L_k = {g : p^k_g != 0}`.  If `L_k` is nonabelian, for example if the
classical automorphisms restricted to context `k` generate a nonabelian
group, then `Qut(G_H(R))` is not a group dual.

Over `H = F_2` this is Roberson--Schmidt, arXiv:2111.12362v1, Theorems 3.8
and 6.7.  The case of a general finite group, with the left-coset form and the
corollary, was not found in a bounded check: the local copies of five papers
and one arXiv abstract search (artifact, §2 Credit).

**What it does for the rung.**
- Torsor CFI pairs over a nonabelian `H` are a family of colored digraphs
  whose quantum symmetry can be genuinely quantum.
- Their `qc`/`qa` gap is exactly the gap of an `H`-valued coset constraint
  system.
- Whether such a gap forces a finitely presented nonhyperlinear group is
  not known.
- Their scalar port profiles are cosets, by
  `torsor-cfi-port-profiles-are-cosets`.
