**OPEN.** There are a nontrivial group `G` with a finite presentation having
as many relators as generators, and `w in G * <t>`, such that
`(G * <t>)/<<w>> = 1`.

By `deficiency-zero-kervaire-failure-yields-whitehead-counterexample`,
establishing this would:
- refute the unimodular Kervaire--Laudenbach conjecture;
- establish `nonaspherical-subcomplex-of-contractible-2-complex-exists`, a finite
  counterexample to Whitehead's asphericity conjecture, through the route
  `whitehead-counterexample-via-deficiency-zero-kervaire-failure`;
- produce a finitely presented group with torsion that is neither hyperlinear nor
  MF.

## Attempts

1. **Shape of a witness (2026-09-13, ideas-bridges).** From the established
   theorem and main's Kervaire reductions, a witness `G`:
   - has torsion;
   - has no nontrivial hyperlinear or MF quotient, since killing passes to
     quotients through the same presentation;
   - has cyclic abelianization generated by the `G`-part of `w`;
   - has a balanced presentation whose complex is a non-aspherical subcomplex of a
     contractible complex.

   So a counterexample search can be restricted to balanced presentations with
   torsion and no small finite quotients. Every certified hit is a triple
   refutation.
