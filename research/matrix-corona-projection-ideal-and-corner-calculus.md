Let `p,q` be stable projections over

```text
Q_d=(prod_n M_(d_n)(C))/(directSum_n M_(d_n)(C))
```

with coordinate ranks `r_n` and `s_n`.

### Corners

If `S={n:r_n>0}`, then

```text
p M_k(Q_d) p
 ~= (prod_(n in S) M_(r_n)(C))/(directSum_(n in S) M_(r_n)(C)).   (CI1)
```

Thus every nonzero stable projection corner is itself a norm matrix corona,
and its stable projection monoid is the rank-germ monoid bounded by fixed
multiples of `r_n` on `S`.

### Principal ideals

Write `I(p)` for the closed two-sided ideal generated by `p`.  Then

```text
q in I(p)
 iff q <=_MvN p^(directSum m) for some m
 iff s_n<=m r_n eventually for some m.                (CI2)
```

Consequently

```text
I(p)<=I(q)
 iff r_n<=m s_n eventually for some m.                 (CI3)
```

The projection `p` is full exactly when

```text
d_n<=m r_n eventually for some m,                     (CI4)
```

equivalently when its coordinate-rank density is bounded below away from
zero on a cofinite tail.  In that case the corner in `(CI1)` is a full corner
and is strongly Morita equivalent to `Q_d`.

### Boolean ideal geometry

For `S subset N`, let `e_S` have coordinate rank one on `S` and zero off
`S`.  Then

```text
I(e_S)<=I(e_T) iff S is contained in T modulo a finite set.
```

Hence `P(N)/Fin` order-embeds into the lattice of projection-generated ideals
of every infinite matrix corona.  In particular each such corona has
continuum many projection-generated ideals and continuum-sized antichains of
nonzero ideals.
