title: The smallest Titz--Witzel building has no independent lattice from which soficity could be transferred
---

Let `X=X_1^2` be the smallest exotic `C~_2` building of Titz Mite--
Witzel, let

```text
K=Gamma_1^2 < barGamma=barGamma_1^2,
```

and regard both groups as subgroups of `Aut(X)`.  Every lattice
`Lambda<Aut(X)` is either `K` or `barGamma`.  In particular, there is no
second residually finite, amenable, LEF, or otherwise already-sofic lattice
on this same building whose approximation could be imported by finite-index
or commensurability permanence.  Finding a sofic lattice in `Aut(X)` is
exactly equivalent to proving that `K` is sofic.

This also closes the tempting measure-equivalence variant.  Lattices in a
common locally compact group are measure equivalent, but here there is no
independent common-ambient donor: every lattice is already one of the same
finite-index pair.  Moreover, ordinary measure equivalence would not by
itself transfer group soficity, since soficity of a group does not assert
that all of its free p.m.p. actions are sofic.
