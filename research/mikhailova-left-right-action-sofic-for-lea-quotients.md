Let `q:F_m->H` be onto.  If `H` is LEA (initially subamenable), then
the free-cover left-right action

```text
F_m x F_m acts on H,       (u,v).h=q(u)h q(v)^(-1)     (MLQ1)
```

is sofic in the sense of Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell.

Here LEA means that every finite multiplication packet in `H` injects,
as a partial group, into an amenable group.  For a requested finite actor
window and finite orbit packet, choose one LEA model containing all word
prefixes and all left-right products used by that request.  The images of
the free generators extend to a genuine homomorphism `p:F_m->K` into the
amenable model group, and the partial embedding intertwines the requested
part of `(MLQ1)` with

```text
K x K acts on K,            (a,b).k=a k b^(-1).         (MLQ2)
```

Every action of the amenable group `K x K` is sofic.  Pulling its charts
back through `p x p` and relabelling the finite orbit packet proves the
claim.

This contains the amenable and LEF quotient cases, but it does not reach
the compiler host: every LEA group is sofic, whereas that host is the fixed
finitely presented nonsofic group.

There is also an exact no-go for the most tempting extension of this
construction.  Suppose `H` is finitely generated by `S` and there are
finite sets `A_n`, permutations `L_(s,n) in Sym(A_n)`, and global root
coordinates `z_n:A_n->H` satisfying

```text
Pr_(a in A_n)[z_n(L_(s,n)a)=s z_n(a)] -> 1             (MLQ3)
```

for every `s in S`.  Then `H` is amenable.  Therefore a proof for the
nonamenable property-`(T)` compiler quotient cannot come from one
approximately equivariant finite `H`-valued coordinate or from a Følner
patch in `H`.  A surviving GKP witness must use genuinely varying
auxiliary-color charts, as in the one-color-code criterion, rather than a
global coordinate taking values in `H`.
