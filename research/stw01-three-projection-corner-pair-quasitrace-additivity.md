Let `P_3 = C^2 * C^2 * C^2` be the universal unital C*-algebra of three projections
`r_1, r_2, r_3`, and put `h_2 = r_1 r_2 r_1` and `h_3 = r_1 r_3 r_1`. Then every bounded
2-quasitrace `sigma` on `P_3` satisfies

```text
sigma(h_2 + h_3) = sigma(h_2) + sigma(h_3).
```

**This statement is equivalent to STW Problem I.** Problem I implies it trivially. The
implication from this statement to Problem I is Theorem 1 of
`research/artifacts/stw01-three-projection-corner-pair-test-2026-09-16.md`, and its
consequence for the three-projection claim is the route
`stw01-three-projection-via-corner-pair-additivity`. That implication carries the same
external trust surface as `stw01-problem-i-via-three-projection-algebra` (Gow's
quotations, Blackadar–Handelman primary not fetched).
- **The dilation.** For positive contractions `a, b` in an AW*-factor `M`, the projections
  `P_1 = 1 (+) 0`, `P_2 = v_a v_a^*` with `v_a = (a^(1/2), (1 - a)^(1/2))^T`, and the analogous `P_3`
  in `M_2(M)` compress to `a (+) 0` and `b (+) 0`.
- **The pullback.** Pulling back the 2-quasitrace `tau~` on `M_2(M)` carries a defect
  `D_tau(a, b)` to the pair `(h_2, h_3)`.
- **Inputs.** Unlike the reviewed witness `sigma(g(r_1 r_2 r_1) + beta r_3)`, no functional calculus
  and no scalar appear. The external inputs are those of the reviewed universal test (Gow
  arXiv:2601.04431v2, Theorem A, Theorem 2.22, Corollary 3.10; Blackadar–Handelman
  Corollary II.1.10 as quoted by Gow), without the finite-spectrum density step.

**A normalized, one-sided form (Theorem 2 and Corollary 4.2 of the artifact).** Suppose
Problem I fails, and fix a sign. Then some witness `sigma` has these properties:
- `sigma(1) = 1`, `sigma(r_i) = 1/2` and `sigma(r_i r_j r_i) = 1/4` for all `i != j`, the values of
  the canonical group trace of `Z_2 * Z_2 * Z_2`;
- invariance under `r_2 <-> r_3`;
- a defect of the prescribed sign.

The sign is reversed by the automorphism `r_2 -> 1 - r_2`, `r_3 -> 1 - r_3`, which sends
`h_j -> r_1 - h_j`. So each one-sided inequality, even for normalized quasitraces, is
already equivalent to Problem I.

**Where the difficulty sits.**
- `C*(r_1, h_2, h_3)`, with unit `r_1`, is the universal unital C*-algebra of two positive
  contractions (`stw01-three-projection-corner-pair-is-universal-pair`). So the pair
  satisfies no relation, and `C*(r_1, h_2, h_3)` is not exact. No exactness argument
  reaches any algebra containing the pair.
- `sigma` is a trace on each two-projection subalgebra `C*(r_1, r_j)`, so it is linear on
  `C*(h_2)` and on `C*(h_3)`.
- The defect, if any, lives in the free position of `h_2` relative to `h_3`, seen only through
  quasitraces that extend to `P_3`. Additivity on the universal pair for all quasitraces on
  that algebra is trivially equivalent to Problem I. The content here is that quasitraces
  extending to `P_3` suffice.

## Attempts

- **Exactness of a host (Haagerup).** Dead: every C*-subalgebra containing `h_2, h_3` contains
  `C*(h_2, h_3)`. That algebra is not exact, because `C*(r_1, h_2, h_3)` is either it or its
  unitization, and is the non-exact universal pair algebra.
- **Traces on two-projection subalgebras.** `sigma` is linear on `C*(r_1, r_2)` and
  `C*(r_1, r_3)`. Dead: nothing relates `h_2` to `h_3`, and no nuclear subalgebra contains both.
- **One-sided inequalities.** Dead as a simplification: each one is equivalent to Problem I, by
  the sign-reversing double flip.
- **Elementary two-sided bounds.** Monotonicity and the matrix sign flip
  `D_(sigma_2)(X, W X W) = -2 D_sigma(a, b)` give `sigma(h_2 + h_3) in [1/4, 1]` for a normalized
  witness, against the trace value `1/2`. Dead: this does not confine the defect.
- **Pinching.** `D_sigma(h_2, h_3) = -(1/2) D_(sigma_2)(X, W X W)` for `X = Y Y^*`, where `Y` has
  first column `(r_2 r_1, r_3 r_1)^T`. Dead: a restatement.
- **Averaging.**
  - Averaging with `r_2 <-> r_3` keeps the defect.
  - Averaging with the double flip kills it, so a counterexample is never flip-invariant, and
    nothing forces invariance.
  - Averaging over all permutations of `r_1, r_2, r_3` mixes three distinct corner pairs, whose
    defects can cancel.
- **Free-triple normalization.** Not reached. Matching the arcsine law of `r_1 r_2 r_1` under the
  group trace needs a nonlinear functional calculus, which destroys the defect identity.
- **Projections, `K_0`, residual finite-dimensionality, the index-two `F_2`.** These are recorded
  in §5 of the artifact. None passes from projections or finite-dimensional traces to
  `h_2 + h_3`.
