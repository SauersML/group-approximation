Literature import. Source read from the PDF of L. Bartholdi, R. I. Grigorchuk,
Z. Šunić, *Branch groups*, arXiv:math/0510294v2, on 2026-09-13.

- Item 3: Theorem 4.1 (p. 51), verbatim: "The first Grigorchuk group 𝔊 is not
  finitely presented." The survey gives three proofs, from [Gri80], [Gri84],
  and the infinite Schur multiplier (Theorem 4.3, [Gri99]:
  `H_2(𝔊, Z) = (Z/2)^infinity`).
- Item 2: the survey's first proof of Theorem 4.1 lists the group's
  just-infiniteness among its properties. Chapter 5 derives it in general:
  after Lemma 5.3 (p. 59), "all finitely generated torsion weakly branch groups
  are just-infinite branch groups". The first Grigorchuk group is the survey's
  basic example of a finitely generated torsion branch group.
- Item 1: the first Grigorchuk group is an infinite finitely generated 2-group
  (Grigorchuk 1980). The survey uses this throughout, e.g. "it is torsion" in
  the proof of Theorem 4.1.
- The level-one fact: `b = (a, c)`, `c = (a, d)`, `d = (1, b)` have trivial root
  label, so they act on level one trivially; the level-one stabilizer is the
  kernel of the action on the two level-one vertices, of index 2, and `a` swaps
  them.

This route asserts only that the cited survey records these facts about the
first Grigorchuk group.
