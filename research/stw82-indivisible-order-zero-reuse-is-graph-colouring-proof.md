Fix one part `C`.  If the direct-sum map `Phi_C` is order zero, then for
distinct `v,w in C` the positive elements which are the units in the `v`
and `w` summands are orthogonal in the domain.  Their images must therefore
be orthogonal:

```text
phi_v(1)phi_w(1)=0.                                   (G4)
```

Thus `C` is independent in `G_phi`.

Conversely, suppose `C` is independent.  The two-map support criterion
shows that the hereditary algebras generated by the ranges of `phi_v` and
`phi_w` are orthogonal for every distinct `v,w in C`.  Hence values coming
from different summands annihilate each other in both orders.  Orthogonality
within each summand is preserved because `phi_v` is order zero, so `Phi_C`
is order zero.

It remains to check contractivity, which is not an extra hypothesis.  Put
`h_v=phi_v(1)`.  Pairwise orthogonality gives

```text
norm(sum_(v in C) h_v)=max_(v in C) norm(h_v)<=1.      (G5)
```

The norm of a positive map from a unital C*-algebra is the norm of its
value at the unit, so `Phi_C` is contractive.  Complete positivity is
preserved under the finite orthogonal sum.  Applying this equivalence to
every part of the partition proves (G2), and minimizing over partitions is
the definition of the chromatic number in (G3).

For the final consequence, choose a proper `(r+1)`-colouring and use its
colour classes as the partition in (G2).  The first map `psi`, the outgoing
maps, and the approximation error are unchanged.  Each restriction of the
regrouped outgoing map is cpc order zero by what was just proved, which is
exactly an `r`-dimensional nuclear approximation.
