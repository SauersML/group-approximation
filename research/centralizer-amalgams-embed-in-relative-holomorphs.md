## Statement

Let `G` be a group, `F = {z_1, ..., z_k} ⊆ G \ {1}` finite and nonempty,
`C = C_G(F) = ∩_i C_G(z_i)`, and `P ≤ G` any subgroup. Put `Q = G * <t>` and let
`Aut_G(Q)` be the automorphisms of `Q` restricting to the identity on `G`. For
`h ∈ G` let `μ_h ∈ Aut_G(Q)` be `t ↦ t h`.

1. There is an explicit `c ∈ Q` such that, in `Q ⋊ Aut_G(Q)`, the subgroup
   generated by `μ(G)` and `c P c^{-1}` is isomorphic to `G *_C (C × P)`, by
   `h ↦ μ_h` on `G` and `p ↦ c p c^{-1}` on `P`.
2. Hence, for every subgroup `M ≤ G`, the amalgam
   `M *_{M∩C} ((M∩C) × P)` embeds in `Q ⋊ Aut_G(Q)`.

Explicitly: pick `s, w ∈ G \ {1}`, put `a_i(h) = t h z_i h^{-1} t^{-1}`,
`k(h) = a_1(h) s a_2(h) s ⋯ s a_k(h)`, `c(h) = k(h) w k(h)`, and `c = c(1)`.

## Remarks

- No hypothesis on `G` is needed: no finite presentation, simplicity or MIF.
- Special cases: `P = Z^m` gives extensions of centralizers. If every `z_i` is
  central, then `C = G` and the statement gives `G × P`.
