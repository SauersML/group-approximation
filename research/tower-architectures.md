Implement the compressor as a level shift on a graded set. Dead: the top
level carries more than `(k−1)/k` of the mass uniformly in the height, so
towers lose `Θ(1)` against exponential amplification (level-shift-obstruction). Calibration:
the same count applies to `BS(1,2)`, where the wreath product IS sofic — the
obstruction is in the shape, not the group.
