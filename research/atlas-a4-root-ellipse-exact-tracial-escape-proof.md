Let `i_1,i_2:A8 -> Gamma_A4` be the two chart maps.  Every defining relator
lies in the kernel of the canonical two-chart Leavitt map.  By
`atlas-charts-overlap-trivially`, both maps are injective and

```text
i_1(A8) intersect i_2(A8)=1.                          (1)
```

Take the canonical trace on `L(Gamma_A4)` and include this finite tracial
algebra trace-preservingly in a finite factor `M`, for example by taking its
tracial free product with a diffuse finite factor.  The two finite-dimensional
subalgebras generated by `i_1(A8)` and `i_2(A8)` are trace-preserving copies of
the regular group algebra of `A8`.  Matching matrix units gives a unitary
`U in M` with

```text
U lambda(i_1(g)) U^*=lambda(i_2(g))     for every g in A8.        (2)
```

Equation `(2)` realizes the two chart restrictions through one common relative
frame.  All thirty packet relations and collision `q_19243` are defining
relations of `Gamma_A4`, hence evaluate exactly to one.  The local `(2,3,3)`
subgroups are the named regular `A4` cells, and
`atlas-a4-packet-centralizer-product-compiler` supplies the corresponding exact
shifted centralizer witnesses.  Thus this is an exact regular context network,
not merely an abstract word representation.

For a chart transvection `t`, the covariance cocycle is

```text
V_U(t)=lambda(i_2(t)i_1(t)^-1).
```

The element in parentheses is nonidentity by `(1)`.  The canonical group trace
therefore gives

```text
tau(V_U(t))=0.                                         (3)
```

Apply `(3)` to `t01,t12,t23,t30`.  By definition
`x_ij=sqrt(1-Re tau(V_U(tij)))`, so every `x_ij=1`.  Hence `S=8` and

```text
S^2+S x_12+x_12^2=64+8+1=73.
```

This proves `(A4-TR-ELLIPSE)` and the asserted obstruction to every universal
finite-tracial certificate.
