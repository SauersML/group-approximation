# Instantiation

One substitution. The general theorem
`nonsofic-orbit-forces-commuting-counterexample` needs exactly two things: a
countable **sofic** group `G`, and a subgroup `H ≤ G` whose coset action
`G ↷ G/H` is **not** sofic. Both are already established in this graph, for
the same pair.

## Soficity of the acting group

From `kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath`, Theorem E: for `q` a prime power and
`r, d ≥ 3`, with `R_+ = F_q[x_1,…,x_d]` and `R = F_q[x_1^{±1},…,x_d^{±1}]`,
the groups `Γ = EL_r(R_+)` and `G = EL_r(R) ⋊ SL_d(Z)` are **residually
finite** Kazhdan groups with `Γ` infranormal and not normal in `G`.

Residually finite ⟹ LEF ⟹ sofic, both kernel-checked in this repository
(`Sofic/LEFSofic.isSofic_of_isLEF` and the residually-finite-to-LEF step in
the same file), and `G` is countable, being generated by countably many
elementary matrices over a countable ring together with `SL_d(Z)`.

## Nonsoficity of the coset action

`coordinate-action-not-sofic`, established: the coset action `G ↷ G/Γ` of
this pair is not a sofic action in the Gao–Kunnawalkam Elayavalli–Patchell
sense. Its route derives this from GKP Theorem 3.6 plus Kun–Thom Theorem A,
and Kun–Thom Corollary D gives the measure-theoretic form independently.

## Apply

Take `H = Γ`. The theorem returns `X = (G × G)/ΔΓ` with the two coordinate
actions, free and sofic and commuting, and nonsofic combination. Explicitly

```text
X = (G × G)/ΔΓ,   ΔΓ = {(γ,γ) : γ ∈ Γ},
g ·_1 (a,b)ΔΓ = (ga, b)ΔΓ,   g ·_2 (a,b)ΔΓ = (a, gb)ΔΓ.
```

`□`

## Why all three prerequisites are load-bearing

- Drop `kun-thom-nonsofic-wreath` and there is no reason `G` is sofic; the
  transfer principle then says nothing, since a nonsofic `G` makes both
  coordinate actions suspect rather than the combination.
- Drop `coordinate-action-not-sofic` and the hypothesis of the transfer
  principle is unmet — and, until August 2026, unmeetable by any known group.
- Drop the transfer principle and there is no counterexample, only a nonsofic
  coset action, which is a statement about one group and answers no published
  question.

## Alternative instances, deliberately not used

Any sofic group with a nonsofic action would do, and by
`gkp-sofic-action-toolkit` the action need not be transitive. The Kun–Thom
Bernoulli form (Corollary D) is *not* an alternative input: it is nonsoficity
in Păunescu's measure-theoretic sense for `G ↷ (K,κ)^{G/Γ}`, not soficity of a
set action, and the transfer principle is stated for set actions only. The
Leavitt unit group of `openai-leavitt-unit-nonsofic` is not an alternative
either — it is nonsofic as a *group*, which is the wrong kind of object here;
the construction needs a **sofic** group carrying a bad action.
