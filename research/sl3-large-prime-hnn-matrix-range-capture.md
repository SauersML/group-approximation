Fix a prime `p>=11`,

```text
Lambda=SL_3(Z),       Gamma=SL_3(Z[1/p]),
G_p=<Gamma,t | [t,Lambda]=1>,
```

and a fixed symmetric generating set `S` of `Lambda`.  For every
hyperlinear approximation `phi_n:G_p->U(d_n)`, put
`X_(n,s)=phi_n(s)`.  The claim is

```text
dist_(2,S)( (X_(n,s))_(s in S),
            MR_(d_n)(Lambda,S) ) -> 0,                            (HMR1)
```

where `MR_d` is the ucp matrix range `(GMR1)` of the canonical generators
in `C^*(Lambda)`.

This is the exact extension-conditioned UCP seam left by the large-prime
analysis.  It is strictly weaker in quantifiers than
`sl3-z-weakly-ucp-stable`: a tuple here comes with the rest of a canonical
microstate of `Gamma` and a Haar stable-letter microstate asymptotically
centralizing it on `Lambda`.  By
`kazhdan-generator-matrix-range-capture-exactifies`, `(HMR1)` makes the
lattice restriction flexibly close to a genuine representation.  The
large-prime depth-free and coprime theorems then force relative-commutant
collapse, contradicting the canonical HNN stable letter.

## Exact scope of the hole

Property T acts only after `(HMR1)` supplies a Stinespring representation;
it cannot prove `(HMR1)` by itself
(`property-t-alone-cannot-correct-tracial-choi-lifts`).  Factorization of
`Lambda` supplies some congruence ucp approximation of the regular trace,
not a ucp point close to this prescribed tuple.  Conversely, only generator
coordinates are needed: the multiplicative-domain calculation in
`kazhdan-matrix-range-capture-proof` propagates them to every fixed word.

A refutation must therefore exhibit a canonical `G_p` microstate sequence
whose lattice generator tuple stays a positive normalized-HS distance from
the matrix range.  By the already closed exact face, such a sequence is
necessarily a uniform lattice outlier.  This is sharper than asking whether
arbitrary regular lattice microstates are correctable, while remaining a
genuine matrix-order problem rather than a restatement of relative-
commutant collapse.

## Attempts

- **Factorization does not select the prescribed tuple.**  Residual
  finiteness and property T give the canonical trace of `Lambda` the
  factorization property, hence produce a congruence sequence lying in
  `(GMR1)`.  They do not compare that sequence with the given `X_n`.
  Replacing existence by prescribed proximity is exactly `(HMR1)`.
- **Universal generator-system lifting is refuted.**
  `sl3-generator-system-has-no-universal-lp` shows that the canonical
  generator operator system cannot have LP: enough unitaries would promote
  it to LLP of `C^*(SL_3(Z))`, contradicted by Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma.  This
  explicitly invalidates `sl3-hnn-capture-via-universal-generator-lp`.
  A proof must use the selected tracial star homomorphism, not a lifting
  theorem valid for arbitrary quotient maps.
- **Kazhdan averaging starts one step later.**  The conjugation action of
  the Stinespring representation gives a genuine unitary representation and
  repairs its almost-invariant corner, but there is no Stinespring
  representation until a nearby ucp point has been supplied.  The general
  inference from property T to such a point is false
  (`property-t-alone-cannot-correct-tracial-choi-lifts`).
- **The whole adjoint complement is the wrong spectral target.**  One might
  try to prove every non-invariant adjoint vector tempered and then invoke
  `tempered-selected-conjugation-vector-obeys-rcc`.  Exact congruence models
  already contain fixed finite-quotient non-tempered Koopman sectors
  (`canonical-congruence-adjoint-complement-is-not-tempered`).  Only the
  cyclic module generated by the selected stable-letter leak could
  conceivably be forced tempered; that is an alternative selected-vector
  seam, not a proof of `(HMR1)`.
- **No global word lifting remains after the matrix-order step.**  The proof
  `kazhdan-matrix-range-capture-proof` shows that normalized-HS proximity on
  the fixed symmetric generator set makes the Stinespring corner
  asymptotically invariant, and the multiplicative-domain estimate plus
  telescoping controls every fixed word.  Thus attacking larger word
  windows before resolving complete positivity adds no strength.
- **Every counterexample has a central dual certificate.**
  `central-letter-averages-matrix-range-separators` applies Hahn--Banach and
  a slowly growing Cesaro average under `phi_n(t)`.  Failure of `(HMR1)`
  therefore supplies a normalized matrix-range separator with a fixed gap
  which asymptotically commutes with the stable letter.  The separator is
  uniformly operator bounded, so it survives in the tracial ultraproduct.
  `hnn-matrix-range-obstruction-is-a-letter-multiplicity-coupling` further
  shows that a fixed amount lies outside `W^*(phi(t))`: the obstruction is
  a genuinely nonabelian stable-letter multiplicity coupling.  The letter
  commutant is still large, so an arithmetic host relation must charge that
  residual component.
