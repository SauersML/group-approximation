Section 10 of the artifact proves the claim. Exactly central relator
unitaries simultaneously diagonalize into sectors reducing all generators.
On each sector the Hopf-kernel and exponent-2401 argument of the premise
puts all eight commutator phases among the 2401st roots of unity.
The union of sectors with some nontrivial commutator phase has normalized
rank at most delta_0^-2 times the sum of the eight squared commutator
defects. On its complement the scalar seventh-root correction makes
a genuine representation; on it use the trivial representation in
the original dimensions. Orthogonal squared errors add. The good-sector
error is bounded by the power-relator term and the bad-sector error
by four times its rank fraction, giving the precise inequality.
