title: Rational rank of the abelianization is a universal lower bound for group-algebra nuclear dimension
---

Let `G` be a countable discrete group and let

```text
r(G)=dim_Q(Q tensor_Z G_ab) in {0,1,2,...,infinity}.
```

For the full group C*-algebra,

```text
dim_nuc(C*(G)) >= r(G).
```

For amenable `G`, full and reduced group C*-algebras agree, so the same bound
applies to the group algebra in STW Problem LXXXIV.  In particular, an
amenable group with infinite-rank abelianization has group C*-algebra of
infinite nuclear dimension.
