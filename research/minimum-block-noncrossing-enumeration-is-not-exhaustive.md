title: Minimum-block noncrossing enumeration is not an exhaustive splitter
---

Keeping only globally minimum-block monochromatic noncrossing partitions can
discard locally maximal cancellation branches.  A global picture fence must
enumerate all coarsening-maximal partitions or prove an extension lemma for
its exact color word.
