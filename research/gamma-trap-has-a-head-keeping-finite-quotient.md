**ESTABLISHED (explicit finite group, checked by three independent
implementations).**

## Setting

`Gamma_trap` is the finitely presented group of the w10 trap-shadow artifact
(`experiments/p22-trap-shadow-2026-09-17/trap_group.py` in the
`w10-ptl-break` lane; the word lists are copied verbatim into
`words.py` here). It has seven involution generators:

- left `as, at, c` (images of `x_12(S), x_12(T), x_21(1)`);
- right `b1, bs, bt` (images of `x_34(1), x_43(S), x_43(T)`);
- `u` (image of `x_13(1)`).

Write `d_g(x) = x g x g`, `Lambda_s = d_as d_c`, `Lambda_t = d_at d_c`,
`M_s = d_bs d_b1`, `M_t = d_bt d_b1`, and let the head be
`h = u . Lambda_s Lambda_t u` (the image of `x_13(Q)`, `Q = 1 + ST`). The
relators are:

- (R1) `Lambda_s u = M_s u`, (R2) `Lambda_t u = M_t u`,
  (R3) `Lambda_t Lambda_s u = u`;
- (LR) every left generator commutes with every right generator;
- (UU) the eight words `u, us, ut, ust, w1, ws, wt, r` commute pairwise;
- (INV) all seven generators are involutions.

`EXTRA_RELS` are the 30 further commutation relations of the artifact, all
true in `P_{2,2}(J)`.

## Theorem

There is a finite group `G` and elements `as, ..., u` of `G` such that:

- all 77 relators (R1)-(R3), (LR), (UU), (INV) and `EXTRA_RELS` hold;
- the right `d`-orbit `Z` of `u` is finite (`|Z| = 13`), and (C1)
  `[bzb, czc] = 1` holds at `z = u` for every right `b`;
- `h != 1`.

So `Gamma_trap`, and `Gamma_trap` with `EXTRA_RELS`, has a finite quotient
keeping the head.

## Construction

1. **Levi module.** Let `A = F_2[x]/(x^4 + 1)`, a local ring of order 16 in
   which `x` is a unit of order 4. Let `W = M_2(A)`, so `dim_{F_2} W = 16`.
   Put `S -> x` and `T -> x^{-1}`; then `TS = 1` holds in `A`. The left
   generators act by `Y -> aY` with `a = x_12(x), x_12(x^{-1}), x_21(1)`. The
   right generators act by `Y -> Yb` with `b = x_12(1), x_21(x), x_21(x^{-1})`.
   All six are involutions, and left commutes with right.
2. **Class-two group.** `N_W = W x (W (x) W)` with product
   `(w,z)(w',z') = (w+w', z+z'+w w'^T)`, where `z` is an `n x n` matrix and
   `n = 16`. The centre contains `C = W (x) W`. `G0 = <six Levi matrices>`
   acts by `A.(w,z) = (Aw, A z A^T)`. Form `N_W x| G0`.
3. **The unipotent generator.** `u = (E_11, c_u)` with `c_u` a random
   `16 x 16` matrix. `c_u` is seeded by `random.Random(0)` in the scripts and
   written out by `verify_hit.py 0` as `hit_cu_seed0.txt`.
4. **The quotient.** Every relator `l = r` of the list has `l^{-1} r` of the
   form `(0, D, 1)` with `D in C`. Let `K <= C` be the `G0`-span of these `D`
   (dimension 243 of 256). `K` is `G0`-stable and central in `N_W`, so
   `G = (N_W / K) x| G0` is a finite group, and every relator holds in it.
5. **The head.** `h = (0, H, 1)` with `H` not in `K`, so `h != 1` in `G`.
   The subgroup generated by the seven elements is the required finite
   quotient of `Gamma_trap`.

**Three independent checks.** The scripts share only the word lists and the
six Levi matrices.

- `tensor_search.py` / `tensor_model.py` use the (w, z, A) group law with a
  Kronecker-product closure. Over ten moduli, the head survives only for
  `x^4 + 1` with random `c_u`, in 3 of 4 trials.
- `verify_hit.py` recomputes the group law, `K`, `G0`-stability of `K`, all
  relators and the head from scratch. For `c_u` seeds 0..11 the head
  survives for 10 of the 12 seeds (all except 4 and 5). A scratch cross-run
  of `tensor_model` on seeds 0..7 agrees seed by seed, including `dim K`.
- `check_matrix.py` embeds `N_W x| G0` in `GL_33(F_2)` by
  `(w, Z) -> [[I, w, Z], [0, 1, w^T], [0, 0, I]]` and
  `A -> diag(A, 1, A^{-T})`, and evaluates every word by plain matrix
  multiplication. Seed 0 with `EXTRA_RELS`: all 77 relator quotients are
  `U(0, D)`, `dim K = 243`, and the head survives. Seed 4: the head dies,
  matching the other two.

## Consistency with the orbit trap

The w10 orbit trap (`double-parabolic-head-dies-on-commuting-right-orbits`,
lane `w10-ptl-break`, not yet on main) says that (F) finite `Z`, together
with (C1) `[bzb, czc] = 1` on `Z` and (C2) `[bz'b, az'a] = 1` on `d_c(Z)`,
forces `h = 1`. In `G`, `|Z| = 13`. `verify_hit.py orbit 0` finds:

- (C1) fails at 4 points of `Z`, always with `b = b1`, but holds at `z = u`;
- (C2) fails at 8 pairs, for example `(bs, as)` and `(bt, at)` at the
  second orbit point.

So the theorem is consistent with the orbit trap, and the failure sits
exactly where Sharpness 3 of the orbit trap said noncommutativity must enter.

## Where the head dies again

`verify_hit.py depth LO HI k` adds to the relators all instances of (C1) and
(C2) at the right words `z = d_(b_1) ... d_(b_j) u` with `j <= k`. They are
all true in `P_{2,2}(J)`, because `<<u>>` is abelian there.

- `k = 0` (the base point only): the head still survives for seeds
  0, 1, 2, 3, 6, 7.
- `k = 1`: the head dies for all 8 seeds, and `K` grows by exactly one
  dimension.
- Over `x^8 + 1` (`n = 32`, `dim C = 1024`) the head already dies in
  `Gamma_trap`, for seeds 0..2.

## Consequences

1. **The open reduction of the orbit-trap lane is answered negatively.** The
   eight (UU) commutations do not force (C) on `Z` in finite quotients. The
   implication "every finite quotient of `Gamma_trap` kills `h`, hence
   `P_{2,2}(J)` is not LEF" has a false premise. The same holds with
   `EXTRA_RELS`.
2. **Class kill.** Any argument for the head collapse fails if its relator
   input lies in `Gamma_trap ∪ EXTRA_RELS ∪ {(C1), (C2) at z = u}`. This
   includes finite-dimensional, exact-representation and hence norm-corona
   arguments, and any four-index finite-presentation non-LEF witness built on
   that set.
   - The invariant is the `G0`-module `C/K`, of dimension 13, which carries
     `H != 0`.
   - Every member of the class dies at its final step, the one that
     concludes `h = 1`: `G` is a finite unitary representation (the regular
     representation) that satisfies the input exactly and has `h != 1`.
3. **The w10-last1 layer test comes out against collapse.** The w10-last1
   spark said to test only the layer `[U*,U*]/[U*,U*,U*]`, which is
   class two. It does not kill the head for the `Gamma_trap` relations. The
   head of `G` lives in the central layer `C/K`, and its W-level image is 0,
   because `Q = 1 + x x^{-1} = 0` in `A`.
4. **What a surviving route must consume.** In this family, the first
   relators that kill the head again are (C1) and (C2) at orbit depth one.
   These are transport relations of `P` beyond the base point. The next
   finitely presented candidate for a non-LEF witness is therefore
   `Gamma_trap + EXTRA_RELS + (C) at depth <= 1`. Its finite quotients are
   open. This family does not refute it.
