**ESTABLISHED** through `relator-width-over-towers-of-bounded-commutator-width-proof`. Unreviewed.

Let `G = <X | R>` be finitely presented with no nontrivial finite quotient, and `n_(x,r)` integers with
`e_x = sum_r n_(x,r) e_r`. Let `Phi_m` be the free group on `X` of the variety generated by `Sym(m)`, which
is finite.

**Theorem.** Fix `m` and `c`. Let `𝒲` be a class of iterated permutational wreath products
`W = T_1 wr ... wr T_d` with transitive vertex groups `T_i <= Sym(m_i)`, `m_i <= m`. Assume `𝒲` contains
the subtree towers of its members, and every element of `[H, H]` is a product of at most `c` commutators
of `H`, for every `H in 𝒲`. Then for every `W in 𝒲` and every `a in W^X`, each `a_x` is a value of the
fixed template
```text
T_x = prod_r r^(n_(x,r)) · prod_(r in R) (r r^-1)^(M),        M = |Phi_m| + m - 1 + 2cm ,
```
so `w_W(a) <= sum_r |n_(x,r)| + 2|R| M`.

**Corollaries.** The bound holds, uniformly in depth, over:
1. **Simple towers.** Towers of nonabelian simple transitive groups of degree `<= m`, by
   `iterated-simple-wreath-bounded-commutator-width` (classification-dependent). This reproves
   `relator-width-bounded-over-simple-iterated-wreath-products`.
2. **Full-cycle towers.** Towers whose vertex groups each contain a full cycle `zeta_i` with
   `[T_i, <<zeta_i>>] = [T_i, T_i]`, with `c = m!`, by `full-cycle-wreath-towers-bounded-commutator-width`
   (elementary). This includes all towers of cyclic groups acting regularly, symmetric groups, and
   alternating groups of odd degree, in any mixture. So solvable towers of unbounded derived length,
   such as `C_2 wr C_3 wr C_2 wr C_3 ...`, are included.
3. **All towers of bounded degree.** Every tower of transitive groups of degree `<= m`, with
   `c = 3(m! + m + 2)`, by `transitive-wreath-towers-bounded-commutator-width` (elementary). This subsumes 1
   and 2, and establishes `simple-nonsofic-relator-width-over-iterated-wreath-products`.

**Consequence for weak soficity.** A finitely presented simple group, sofic or not, has no weak models,
with any bi-invariant lengths, in any of these tower classes. For
`simple-nonsofic-relator-width-over-iterated-wreath-products`, this leaves only towers containing
vertex groups with no full cycle that are not simple, or simple levels without full cycles mixed with
non-simple levels. There no commutator width bound is on record.

**Mechanism.**
1. **Components.** All conjugators have root part in the root tuple group `<p>`, which is the normal
   closure of the relator root labels within itself, by (E1). Coordinates fixed by `<p>` carry genuine
   subtree tuples. They are treated recursively inside the same balanced pairs, which are trivial there.
2. **Top block.** The root label of `A^-1 a_x` lies in `[Phi_m, Phi_m]`, so it is a product of at most
   `|Phi_m|` pairs `[u, v r v^-1]` with `u, v in <p>`.
3. **Dipole block.** On each `<p>`-orbit `O`, the sum of base coordinates modulo `[H, H]` is a
   homomorphism on everything used. Dipoles along a spanning tree of `O` solve every coordinate but one,
   leaving a defect in `[H, H]`.
4. **Defect block.** The defect costs `c` nested commutators `[[(k at i; 1), L], (l at i; 1)] = [k, l] at i`,
   each four letters.
