For a finite loopless multigraph, define M=sum b_AB, Q=sum b_AB^2,
R=sum b_AB^3 and T=sum b_AB b_AC b_BC over unordered quotient pairs
and triples. The Bernoulli partition generator satisfies
(1-p)L M=-Q and (1-p)L Q=-R+6T. The parallel-pair count
S=(Q-M)/2 satisfies (1-p)L S=3T-sum b_AB binom(b_AB,2).
The finite-volume cluster density satisfies
kappa''=2 E S/[|V|(1-p)^2]. These are finite identities, with no
asserted infinite-volume integrability or threshold separation.
