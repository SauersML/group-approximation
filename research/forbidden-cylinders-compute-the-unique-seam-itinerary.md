words using uniform cylinder witnesses. The finite recognizability decoder
makes tagged nonoccurrence a finite conjunction of those tests. At each
length exactly one seam prefix occurs; dovetail the rejection tests for
all other candidates. The recovered itinerary computes beta. The forward
