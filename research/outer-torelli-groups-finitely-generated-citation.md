Citation import. Source read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv PDF, pages 1–2:
Bestvina–Bux–Margalit, *Dimension of the Torelli group for Out(F_n)*,
arXiv:math/0603177v1.

- Main Theorem (3), verbatim: "For n ≥ 3, we have: ... (3) T_n is finitely
  generated."
- Page 1, verbatim: "We remark that T_1 is obviously trivial and T_2 is trivial by a
  classical result of Nielsen [11]."
- Page 2, verbatim: "Nielsen proved in 1924 that T_3 is finitely generated [11].
  Ten years later, Magnus proved that T_n is finitely generated for every n [8]."

Finite generation of `IA_n` follows: an extension of a finitely generated group by
a finitely generated normal subgroup is finitely generated.
