## Statement proved (stronger than the target)

**Lemma (AM2G).**  Let `P` be a nontrivial finitely presented torsion-free
group.  Then there are a finitely presented, torsion-free, acylindrically
hyperbolic group `Q` generated by two elements and an injective homomorphism
`g:P->Q` with

```text
normalClosure_Q(g(P)) = Q.
```

**Target.**  If moreover `1!=p in P` and `normalClosure_P(p)=P`, then
`normalClosure_Q(g(p))` is normal in `Q` and contains `g(normalClosure_P(p))
= g(P)`, hence contains `normalClosure_Q(g(P))=Q`.  Also `g(p)!=1` by
injectivity.  This is exactly `two-generated-acylindrical-normal-generation-upgrade`.

The perfectness obstruction recorded on the target (a valid `Q` admits no
HNN splitting) is respected: `Q` is a single amalgam, and its generating pair
`(s,b)` consists of two elliptic elements at adjacent vertices of the
Bass--Serre tree, while `a=s^{m+1}` is the edge element that links them.

No small-cancellation theorem, Greendlinger lemma, Property `(T)` or
Kazhdan input occurs.  The only imported theorem is Minasyan--Osin, used for
acylindrical hyperbolicity; everything else is free-product normal forms.

## Imported tools

* **(AM)** Amalgam `X *_F Y` over injective maps: each factor embeds (Mathlib
  `Monoid.PushoutI.of_injective`, as used in
  `GroupApproximation/Algebra/PushoutITorsionFree.lean`).  Its presentation is
  the free product of presentations of `X` and `Y` plus the relations
  `u_i = phi(u_i)` for a generating set `u_i` of `F`.
* **(TF)** `amalgam-torsion-permanence-needs-no-cyclic-reduction`: an
  amalgamated free product of torsion-free groups is torsion-free.
* **(MO)** `fpbs-minasyan-osin-weakly-malnormal-splittings`, verbatim from
  arXiv:1310.6289, Corollary `cor:amalg-intr`: "Let `$G$` split as a free
  product of groups `$A$` and `$B$` with an amalgamated subgroup `$C$`.
  Suppose `$A\ne C\ne B$` and `$C$` is weakly malnormal in `$G$`. Then `$G$`
  is either virtually cyclic or acylindrically hyperbolic."  Here `C` is
  weakly malnormal if some `g in G` has `|C^g cap C|` finite.
* **(NF)** Normal forms in free products `X * Y` (every element is uniquely
  an alternating product of nontrivial syllables) and reduced words in free
  groups.
* **(FP)** An amalgam of finitely presented groups over a finitely generated
  subgroup is finitely presented.

## Step 0: the generating list

Choose pairwise distinct nontrivial elements `p_1,...,p_m` of `P` that
generate `P`, with `m>=3`.  Start from the nontrivial members of a finite
generating set, delete repetitions, and if fewer than three remain append
powers `h^r` of one nontrivial `h`.  Because `P` is torsion-free, `h` has
infinite order, so infinitely many powers are distinct and the list can be
padded.  Put `N=m+1`.

## Step 1: a free subgroup of `A = P * <s>`

Let `A=P*<s>` with `<s> ~= Z`.  Put

```text
x_0 = s^N,      x_k = p_k s^k   (1<=k<=m),      C_1 = <x_0,...,x_m> <= A.
```

**Claim 1.**  `x_0,...,x_m` freely generate `C_1`, and `s notin C_1`.

*Proof.*  Write `x_k^{-1} = s^{-k} p_k^{-1}`.  Take a nonempty freely reduced
word `W` in the `x_i^{±1}` and concatenate the syllables.  Merging can happen
only between adjacent syllables from the same factor.

* A `P`-syllable meets a `P`-syllable only at a junction `x_k^{-1} x_j`, with
  product `p_k^{-1} p_j`.  Free reduction forces `j != k`, and the `p_i` are
  pairwise distinct, so the product is nontrivial.
* A maximal run of `s`-powers between two `P`-syllables (or a word end) is
  the optional tail `s^k` of an `x_k`, then a block `x_0^n` (`n in Z`), then
  the optional head `s^{-j}` of an `x_j^{-1}`.  Its exponent is one of
  `k+nN-j`, `k+nN`, `nN-j`, `nN`, with `1<=k,j<=m<N`.  The first vanishes
  only if `n=0` and `k=j`, which is the excluded cancellation
  `x_k x_k^{-1}`.  The second and third never vanish, since `0<k,j<N`.  The
  fourth occurs with `n != 0`, since otherwise there is no run.

So after the merges the syllables alternate between nontrivial elements of
`P` and nontrivial powers of `s`, no further merge occurs, and by (NF) the
element is not `1`.  Hence the basis is free.  A nonempty `W` containing some
`x_k^{±1}` with `k>=1` has a nontrivial `P`-syllable, while `W=x_0^n` gives
`s^{nN} != s`.  So `s notin C_1`.  ∎

## Step 2: a free subgroup of `B = F(a,b)`

Put

```text
y_0 = a,     y_k = b^{-k} a b^{k+1}   (1<=k<=m),     C_2 = <y_0,...,y_m> <= B.
```

**Claim 2.**  `y_0,...,y_m` freely generate `C_2`.  Every nontrivial element
of `C_2` has reduced form beginning with `a`, `a^{-1}` or `b^{-1}`.  In
particular `b notin C_2`.

*Proof.*  Each `y_i^{±1}` has the shape `b^α a^ε b^β`.  The pair `(α,β)` is
`(0,0)` for `a^{±1}`, `(-k,k+1)` for `y_k`, and `(-(k+1),k)` for
`y_k^{-1}`.  In a freely reduced product `g_1...g_L` of basis letters, the
`b`-exponent between the `a`-letters of `g_i` and `g_{i+1}` is
`β_i+α_{i+1}`:

| `g_i` \ `g_{i+1}` | `a^{±1}` | `y_j` | `y_j^{-1}` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a^{±1}` | `0` | `-j` | `-(j+1)` |
| `y_k` | `k+1` | `k+1-j` | `k-j` |
| `y_k^{-1}` | `k` | `k-j` | `k-j-1` |

A zero entry occurs in exactly these cases:

* `a^ε a^ε`, which has the same sign by free reduction;
* `y_k y_{k+1}`, with letters `a·a`;
* `y_k^{-1} y_{k-1}^{-1}`, with letters `a^{-1}·a^{-1}`;
* `y_k y_k^{-1}` and `y_k^{-1} y_k`, which free reduction excludes.

So no `a`-letter cancels.  The reduced form is
`b^{α_1} a^{ε_1} ... a^{ε_L} b^{β_L}`, with `L>=1` letters `a^{±1}`.  That
gives freeness.  Since `α_1 in {0,-j,-(j+1)}` is at most `0`, the first
letter is `a^{±1}` or `b^{-1}`.  `b` begins with `b`, so `b notin C_2`.  ∎

The script `experiments/amalgam-two-generation-2026-09-17/check_amalgam.py`
also builds the Stallings graph of `C_2` for `1<=m<=6`.  The graph is folded,
has rank `m+1`, is not a covering (so `C_2` has infinite index), and has no
`b`-edge leaving the base vertex.

## Step 3: the amalgam

By Claims 1 and 2, `phi: x_i |-> y_i` (`0<=i<=m`) is an isomorphism
`C_1 -> C_2`.  Put

```text
Q = A *_{C_1 = C_2} B
  = < P, s, a, b | s^N = a,  p_k s^k = b^{-k} a b^{k+1}  (1<=k<=m) >.
```

* `P -> A -> Q` is injective by (AM), twice.
* `Q` is torsion-free by (TF), since `A=P*<s>` and `B` are torsion-free
  (a free product is an amalgam over the trivial group).
* `Q` is finitely presented by (FP): the display adds three generators and
  `m+1` relators to a finite presentation of `P`.

## Step 4: `Q = <s,b>`

Let `R=<s,b> <= Q`.  Then `a=s^N in R`, so `B=<a,b> <= R`.  Each
`p_k = (b^{-k} a b^{k+1}) s^{-k}` then lies in `R`.  Since `P`, `s`, `a`,
`b` generate `Q`, `R=Q`.  ∎

## Step 5: `normalClosure_Q(P) = Q`

Adding the relators `p_k=1` (which generate `P`, so kill `P`) to the display
of Step 3 and eliminating `a=s^N` gives

```text
Q / normalClosure_Q(P) = G_0 = < s, b | r_k = b^{-k} s^N b^{k+1} s^{-k}, 1<=k<=m >.
```

**Claim 5.**  `G_0 = 1` whenever `m>=3`.

*Proof.*  In `G_0`, relation `r_k` says `s^N b^{k+1} = b^k s^k`.  Using
`r_k` and then `r_{k+1}`:

```text
b^k s^k b = s^N b^{k+2} = b^{k+1} s^{k+1},   hence   s^k b = b s^{k+1}.     (D_k)
```

From `(D_1)` twice, `s^2 b = s(sb) = (sb)s^2 = b s^4`.  `(D_2)` says
`s^2 b = b s^3`.  So `s=1`, and then `r_1` reads `b^{-1}b^2 = b = 1`.  ∎

The script verifies this derivation as free-group identities, with an
explicit product of conjugates of relators for `m=3,...,8`:

```text
X_k := b^k r_k b^{-k} = s^N b^{k+1} s^{-k} b^{-k},
Y_k := b^{-k} X_k^{-1} X_{k+1} b^k = s^k b s^{-(k+1)} b^{-1},
s   = b^{-1} Y_1^{-1} (s Y_1^{-1} s^{-1}) Y_2 b.
```

## Step 6: acylindrical hyperbolicity

We apply (MO) to `Q = A *_C B` with `C=C_1=C_2`.

* `C_1 != A`, because `s notin C_1` (Claim 1).
* `C_2 != B`, because `b notin C_2` (Claim 2).
* **Weak malnormality.**  Take `g=bab in B`, and let `1 != v in C_2` have
  reduced form `b^{α_1} a^{ε_1} ... a^{ε_L} b^{β_L}`, where `α_1<=0` and
  `β_L>=0` (Claim 2, and its mirror for the last block).  Then

  ```text
  g v g^{-1} = b a b^{1+α_1} a^{ε_1} ... a^{ε_L} b^{β_L+1} a^{-1} b^{-1}.
  ```

  If `α_1 != -1`, this word is reduced.  If `α_1=-1`, then `g_1=y_1` and
  `ε_1=+1`, so it begins `b a a`.  In either case the reduced form begins
  with `b`.  Every nontrivial element of `C_2` begins with `a^{±1}` or
  `b^{-1}`, so `C_2 cap g C_2 g^{-1} = 1`.  Both subgroups lie in `B`, which
  embeds in `Q`, so the same holds in `Q`.  The script finds the same witness
  `bab` independently by a product-graph search for `2<=m<=6`.
* `Q` is not virtually cyclic, because it contains `F(a,b)`.

So `Q` is acylindrically hyperbolic by (MO).  ∎

## Conclusion

`g:P->Q` is injective.  `Q` is two-generated (Step 4), finitely presented
and torsion-free (Step 3), and acylindrically hyperbolic (Step 6).  Finally,
`normalClosure_Q(g(P))=Q` (Step 5).  This proves (AM2G) and the target.
