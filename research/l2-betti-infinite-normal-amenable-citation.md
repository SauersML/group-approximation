W. Lück, *L²-Invariants from the Algebraic Point of View*, arXiv:math/0310489, Section 6.2,
read from the PDF text on MSI on 2026-09-12. With
`B_d := {G | b_p^(2)(G) = 0 for 0 ≤ p ≤ d}` (6.3), `d` a non-negative integer or `∞`:

> **Theorem 6.4.** Let d be a non-negative integer or d = ∞. Then: (i) The class B∞ contains
> all infinite amenable groups; (ii) If G contains a normal subgroup H with H ∈ Bd, then
> G ∈ Bd; [...]

Take `d = ∞` and `H` the infinite amenable normal subgroup: (i) puts `H` in `B_∞`, and (ii)
puts `G` in `B_∞`.
