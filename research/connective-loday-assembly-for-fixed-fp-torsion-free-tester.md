**OPEN.** For the fixed finitely presented torsion-free group `U` from
[[higher-whitehead-universal-finitely-presented-torsion-free-group]], the
connective integral Loday assembly

```text
K(NU;Z) -> K(ZU)
```

is a weak homotopy equivalence.

By [[connective-loday-assembly-fixed-fp-torsion-free-tester]], this one
computation is exactly equivalent to connective integral Loday assembly
being a weak equivalence for every torsion-free group.
