ESTABLISHED.  A PI-degree stratification of the q14 locus has two exact
limits.

First, bounded PI degree does not reduce repeated-natural q14 charts to
bounded-width amplified phases.  For `h>=2`, put

```text
B_h=F2[x,y]/(x^h,y^h),       M_h=the left regular B_h-module.       (PIF1)
```

Thus `dim_F2 M_h=h^2`.  Let `X_h,Y_h` be left multiplication by `x,y`, and
on `F2^4 tensor M_h` set

```text
T_h=I+E13 tensor X_h+E14 tensor Y_h.                           (PIF2)
```

Then `T_h^2=I` and `T_h` commutes with the marked nilpotent
`E23 tensor I`.  Hence the two marked transvections obey q14 exactly.  The
Morita coefficient algebra of the two charts contains `X_h,Y_h` and is
exactly the regular image of `B_h`.  It is generated by two elements and
satisfies the degree-two commutator identity for every `h`, but

```text
dim B_h=h^2,
J(B_h)^(2h-2) !=0,
J(B_h)^(2h-1)=0.                                      (PIF3)
```

Thus even PI degree one leaves both state width and radical Loewy length
unbounded.  (The already established unit-packet calculation gives this
particular triangular family a packet residual of normalized rank `1/4`, so
`(PIF2)` is a fence on the proposed PI reduction, not an Atlas
countermodel.)

Second, the twelve packet relations, collision, and q14 cannot imply a
nonzero polynomial identity for the joint chart algebra.  The augmented
Atlas quotient maps onto the binary Leavitt unit group

```text
Q=L_F2(1,2)^x,
```

and the induced algebra map has the quotient chain

```text
F2[Gamma_+] -> F2[Q] -> L_F2(1,2).                    (PIF4)
```

The last surjection is
`leavitt-unit-group-algebra-surjects-onto-leavitt`.  For every `r`, the
elements

```text
e_(u,v)=x_u y_v,             |u|=|v|=r,               (PIF5)
```

are matrix units and give a unital copy of `M_(2^r)(F2)` in the Leavitt
algebra.  A nonzero free polynomial fails on some finite binary matrix
algebra (equivalently, the free algebra is residually finite-dimensional),
so an algebra containing all the matrix algebras in `(PIF5)` is not PI.
Any polynomial identity following algebraically from the augmented Atlas
relations would pass through `(PIF4)`, a contradiction.

Consequently Artin--Wedderburn does not prove that a hypothetical Atlas
rank-microstate countermodel has PI degree tending to infinity.  A bounded
PI degree controls only the noncommutative sizes of semisimple blocks; it
does not bound center degrees, the number of central blocks, or radical
Loewy length.  The one-parameter Laurent lane has been closed separately,
but a bounded-PI enemy may still use a growing multivariable center or
radical.  Conversely, the packet relations cannot remove that enemy by a
universal bounded PI or central-simple-degree identity, because the exact
Leavitt image is non-PI.

The surviving PI target is therefore necessarily **finite-matrix-specific**:
one must prove from small packet residual ranks that a positive-density
corner has bounded PI degree (and simultaneously control its center/radical),
or produce a fixed PI evaluation that separates two forced positive-density
packet carriers as in `fixed-pi-degree-fingerprints-packet-label-factor`.
Neither statement is supplied by Artin--Wedderburn or by the defining
relations alone.
