**ESTABLISHED DIRECT-CELL FIREWALL.**  Retain the tagged coefficients

```text
C_(ij)=B_i+D_(ij),
A_rB_s=delta_(rs)q,              A_rD_(ij)=0.           (DAC1)
```

For any coefficient `a`, define the direct `A_3` word

```text
N(a,C)=[x_12(a),[x_23(1),x_34(C)]].                    (DAC2)
```

The ordinary Steinberg relations give the fixed two-step calculation

```text
[x_23(1),x_34(C)]=x_24(C),
N(a,C)=x_14(aC),                                       (DAC3)
```

and the alternative bracketing

```text
[[x_12(a),x_23(1)],x_34(C)]=x_14(aC).                  (DAC4)
```

Thus `(DAC3)--(DAC4)` are one direct noncommutative associator filling, not
a gluing of conjugated marked cells.  For `i<j`, `(DAC1)` gives

```text
(A_i+A_j)C_(ij)=q,                  A_jC_(ij)=0,        (DAC5)
```

so

```text
N(A_i+A_j,C_(ij))=x_14(q),
N(A_j,C_(ij))=1.                                        (DAC6)
```

This is exactly the desired marked-versus-edge-zero payload incidence at
the algebraic level, with no pairwise mark seam and no chart conjugator.

It still cannot supply the uniform-area compiler.  Every letter and every
relator in `(DAC1)--(DAC6)` lies in the positive unitriangular group
`U_4^+(R)`.  For any finite family of tagged addresses, let `H` be the
subgroup generated by all positive root occurrences in these cells.  By
`finite-positive-root-fragments-have-marked-regular-models`, `H` is finite,
all literal coefficient reuse, associator rows, and any included range-sum
rows remain exact, while `x_14(q)` is nonidentity.  Its left regular
representation satisfies

```text
||lambda_H(x_14(q))-1||_2=sqrt(2).                     (DAC7)
```

Consequently no dimension-independent local estimate can charge the marked
output to defects of this direct associator menu.  The two bracketings merely
restate coefficient associativity inside a locally finite algebra group.
The first viable direct diagram must leave the positive radical through an
opposite-root/Weyl occurrence **and** attach that occurrence to the private
payload, rather than append an unrelated normalizer.  No Property `(T)`,
Kazhdan input, matrix approximation theorem, trace hypothesis, or literature
result is used.
