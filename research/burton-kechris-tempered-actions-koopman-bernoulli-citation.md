Published dependency: P. Burton, A. S. Kechris, *Weak containment of measure preserving group
actions*, arXiv:1611.07921v5. The target claim quotes the §4 definition of the Koopman
representation and the §10.5 statements defining tempered actions, identifying κ^{s_Γ}_0 ≅ ∞·λ_Γ
(citing [KL, page 39]), closing tempered actions downward under ≼ (Proposition 4.1), and noting
that i_Γ is not tempered for non-amenable Γ. This route imports them; it is not an independent
derivation. The Convention paragraph of the target is the standard definition of weak containment
of representations and is used only in that form.
