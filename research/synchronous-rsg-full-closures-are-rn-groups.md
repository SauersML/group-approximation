## Setting

- `X = {0, …, d−1}`, `d ≥ 2`. Over the full shift `X^ω` (BBMZ's `Σ_Γ` for the one-vertex graph with `d` loops,
  `E = X^ω`), BBMZ-hyperbolic (arXiv:2309.06224, Definition 2.32) call `G ≤ R_d` a **rational similarity group
  (RSG)** if for every pair of proper cones `C_α, C_β` some `g ∈ G` maps `C_α` to `C_β` by the canonical
  similarity. The **full closure** `[[G]]` is the group of homeomorphisms locally agreeing with `G`
  (Definition 2.24).
- `Aut_fs(X^*)` is the group of finite-state automorphisms of the rooted tree `X^*`. For a subgroup
  `A ≤ Aut(X^*)`, a **table over `A`** is a homeomorphism `f` with a cone partition `{C_{u_i}}` of `X^ω`, a
  cone partition `{C_{v_i}}`, and `h_i ∈ A`, such that `f(u_i w) = v_i h_i(w)`. When `A` is self-similar, the
  tables over `A` form the Röver–Nekrashevych group `V_d(A)`; tables over `{1}` form Higman–Thompson `V_d`.
- `G` is **locally synchronous** if `G ≤ V_d(Aut_fs(X^*))`: every element is a table over finite-state tree
  automorphisms. The **table group** `H_G` is the subgroup generated by all table entries of all elements of
  `G`, at all refinements.

## Statement

**Theorem S.** Let `G` be a finitely generated, locally synchronous RSG over the full `d`-shift.
1. `H_G` is a finitely generated, finite-state self-similar group. It is generated by the finite section- and
   inverse-closed set `T` of table entries of any finite symmetric generating set of `G`.
2. `[[G]] = V_d(H_G)`.
3. So, by Theorem A (`rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation`, applied with generating set `T`),
   `[[G]]` is finitely presented **if and only if** the relator kernel of `H_G` is a finitely generated lift
   ideal.

**A characterization, the converse of BBMZ's remark.** BBMZ note that the Röver–Nekrashevych group of any
finite-state self-similar group is a full RSG (quoted from the arXiv HTML: "Any Röver–Nekrashevych group of a
finite-state self-similar group is a full RSG"). Item 2 gives the converse on the synchronous side: the full
locally synchronous RSGs over the full `d`-shift are exactly the groups `V_d(H)` with `H` a finitely generated
finite-state self-similar group.

## Consequences for Question 1.4

- **Positive cases.** For a finitely presented locally synchronous RSG `G` over the full shift, `[[G]]` is
  finitely presented whenever the relator kernel of `H_G` is a finitely generated lift ideal. That covers:
  - `H_G` finitely presented (Scott);
  - `H_G` contracting (Nekrashevych);
  - relator kernel equal to `K_∞`, as for the lamplighter automaton
    (`rover-nekrashevych-group-of-lamplighter-automaton-is-fp`).
- **The presentation of `G` is irrelevant on this class.** Whether `[[G]]` is finitely presented depends only
  on `H_G`, and `H_G` depends only on the set of table entries of `G`. Finite presentation of `G` enters
  Question 1.4 here only by restricting which `H_G` can occur.
- **Shape of a counterexample over the full shift.** A counterexample to Question 1.4 over the full shift is one
  of two things:
  - (a) not locally synchronous, like the asynchronous test family `Γ_t` of `fp-rsgs-have-fp-full-closures`;
  - (b) a finitely presented locally synchronous RSG `G` whose table group `H_G` is a finite-state group with
    `V_d(H_G)` **not** finitely presented.

  Case (b) would settle the question left open in `rover-nekrashevych-fp-iff-finite-lift-presentation`,
  whether some finite-state group has a non-finitely-presented Röver–Nekrashevych group. The known non-finitely-
  presented example `V_3(L)` under the persistent action (c2bdf2618d) is infinite-state, so it does not give (b).

## Open (precise)

- **Realizability.** Given a finite-state `H` with `V_d(H)` not finitely presented, if one exists, is there a
  finitely presented locally synchronous RSG `G` with `H_G = H`? The group `V_d(H)` itself has table group `H`,
  but it is then not finitely presented. If every finite-state `H` is realized by a finitely presented `G`,
  Question 1.4 on this class is **equivalent** to "every finite-state self-similar group has a finitely
  presented Röver–Nekrashevych group".
- **Edge shifts.** Over a general edge shift `Σ_Γ`, the analogue of `V_d(H)` is a Nekrashevych-type group of a
  self-similar action over the graph `Γ`. Theorem A is proved only for the full shift, so item 3 does not yet
  extend.
