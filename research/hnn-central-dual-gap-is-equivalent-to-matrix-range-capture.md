Let `C` be generated by a finite unitary tuple indexed by `S`, `m=|S|`,
and let `(X_n,T_n)` be any sequence of unitary tuples such that

```text
sum_(s in S)||T_nX_(n,s)T_n^*-X_(n,s)||_2^2 ->0.                (DPE1)
```

Put

```text
D_n=dist_(2,S)(X_n,MR_(d_n)(C^*(C),S)).                         (DPE2)
```

Then the following are equivalent:

1. `D_n->0`;
2. every tuple `B_n` satisfying

   ```text
   sum_s||B_(n,s)||_2^2=1,
   sup_(n,s)||B_(n,s)||_op<infinity,
   sum_s||T_nB_(n,s)T_n^*-B_(n,s)||_2^2->0                     (DPE3)
   ```

   has matrix-range separation gap tending to at most zero.

Quantitatively, for every normalized tuple `B` and
`D=dist_(2,S)(X,MR_d)`,

```text
Delta_d(B;X)<=D,                                                (DPE4)
```

while the distinguished tuple `B_s=X_s/sqrt(m)` satisfies

```text
Delta_d(B;X)>=D^2/(2sqrt(m)).                                   (DPE5)
```

Under `(DPE1)` this distinguished tuple obeys `(DPE3)`.  Therefore, for the
arithmetic centralizer HNN group,

```text
sl3-hnn-central-dual-gap-vanishes
 iff sl3-large-prime-hnn-matrix-range-capture.                  (DPE6)
```

The central-dual formulation remains a useful language for locating a
counterexample inside the stable-letter multiplicity commutant, but it is
not a logically smaller closure theorem.  Any proof of the dual inequality
already proves primal ucp capture, and any primal capture proof bounds all
dual directions automatically.

## Proof

For `(DPE4)`, choose `Y in MR_d` with `||X-Y||_(2,S)` arbitrarily close to
`D`.  Since `h_d(B)` dominates `Re<B,Y>`, tuple Cauchy--Schwarz gives

```text
Delta_d(B;X)<=Re<B,X-Y><=||B||_(2,S)||X-Y||_(2,S).
```

Now use `||B||_(2,S)=1` and take the infimum.  Equation `(DPE5)` is
`generator-diagonal-is-a-central-matrix-range-separator`.  Its centrality
defect is `m^(-1)` times `(DPE1)`.  Thus `(1)` implies `(2)` by `(DPE4)`,
and `(2)` applied to the diagonal tuple implies `(1)` by `(DPE5)`.
