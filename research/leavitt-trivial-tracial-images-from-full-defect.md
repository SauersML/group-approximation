R=L_(F_2)(1,2) is countable and nonzero. Its standard generators satisfy ts=ba=1, ta=bs=0. The defect e=1-st is full because bea=1. The first prerequisite therefore kills every tracial matrix-ultraproduct homomorphism of EL_4(R). The four-leaf prefix code and the second prerequisite identify R^x with GL_4(R)=EL_4(R). Precomposition by that isomorphism proves the assertion for the whole unit group. No finite presentation or simplicity input is needed.

The analytic input is Jihao Liu's [Theorem 1.2/6.7](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf), through Andreas Thom's [conditional normalization theorem](https://andreasthom.github.io/pdf/nonhyperlinear.pdf). The full-defect algebra is the separately credited Cairn manuscript. This application does not reattribute either analytic result to Cairn.
