title: A hyperbolic nonhyperlinear witness cannot be sofic
target: nonsofic-hyperbolic-group
requires: [nonhyperlinear-hyperbolic-group, sofic-implies-hyperlinear]
---

Take the witness from the first premise. If it were sofic, the second
would make it hyperlinear, a contradiction. Its hyperbolicity is
unchanged. This is a one-way implication, not an identification of
the two approximation problems.
