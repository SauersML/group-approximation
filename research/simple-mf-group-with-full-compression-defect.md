**ESTABLISHED (written proof, exact computer check of the cell; not
Lean-verified).**  This is a counterexample to a class of approaches.  It is
not a refutation of the goal, and `property-t-free-manuscript-results` stays
OPEN.

Notation is that of `non_mf_groups_exist.tex`:

```text
D_G(L) = << [u c u^-1, l] : u in G, u L u^-1 <= L, c in C_G(L), l in L >>_G .
```

MF means operator-MF: an injective homomorphism into
`U(prod_n M_(k_n) / directSum_n M_(k_n))`.

## Theorem

There is a countable group `S` with elements `u, c, l` such that, with
`L = < u^k l u^-k : k >= 0 >`, the following hold.

1. `S` is simple.
2. `u L u^-1 <= L`, `c` lies in `C_S(L)`, and `delta = [u c u^-1, l]` is
   not `1`.  Hence `D_S(L) = S`.
3. `S` is MF.  Since `S` is simple, it follows that `Rad_MF(S) = 1`.
4. `L` is locally finite: it lies in a copy of the finitary alternating group
   of a countable set.  `u` has infinite order.  The triple `(u, c, l)`
   generates, together with a finitary alternating group, an elementary
   amenable subgroup of `S`.

Thus

```text
(SDC)   G simple  and  D_G(L) != 1   ==>   Rad_MF(G) = G
```

is false.  It fails even when `L` is locally finite and the defect cell
lives in an elementary amenable subgroup.

Derivation: `simple-mf-group-with-full-compression-defect-proof`.

## The class this kills, and where every member dies

The manuscript criterion is: property (T) together with `D_G(L) = G` implies
`Rad_MF(G) = G`.  Consider any Kazhdan-free replacement whose hypotheses are
properties of `(S, L, u, c)`.  Such properties include:

- ambient simplicity;
- the full defect `D = G`;
- countability;
- a locally finite or amenable compressed subgroup;
- an infinite-order compressor;
- a cell inside an amenable subgroup;
- hyperlinearity (`S` lies in `U(R)` for the hyperfinite II_1 factor `R`);
- embeddability into the unitary group of a UHF algebra.

Every such replacement is false.  The invariant is the pair `(S, L)`.  Every
member dies at the same step, specializing the collapse to `S`, where the
conclusion `Rad_MF(S) = S` contradicts the MF embedding.

This makes the Wave 6 barrier `thompson-commutator-full-defect-bars-t-free-collapse`
unconditional for simplicity and the bare cell.  That barrier needs
amenability of `F` to turn into a refutation; this one does not.

**Survivors.**  A Kazhdan-free collapse must use a hypothesis that fails for
`S`.  The hypotheses of the manuscript endpoints that `S` does not satisfy
are:

- finite generation or finite presentation of the ambient group. `S` is
  not finitely generated;
- torsion-freeness. `S` contains `Alt(5)`;
- a spectral input on `L` or on the models;
- the ring data of the Leavitt or `EL_20` hosts, meaning Steinberg
  relations and root-subgroup compression.

## Attempts

1. **Derived full groups of minimal subshifts, looking for a finitely
   generated example.**  The idea was to find a cell in `D([[phi]])`, which
   is finitely generated, simple and amenable, hence MF.  Three sources of
   cells were checked and all failed:
   - Matui's lamplighter subgroup `Z_2 wr Z <= D([[phi]])`, which exists for
     every non-odometer. Its lamps `tau_V` pairwise commute, so it is an
     abelian lamplighter, and those have no cells;
   - odometer full groups, which are locally virtually abelian;
   - column-permutation lamps over a first-return map, and cells with
     `u = phi`. In every instance checked by hand, both died by minimality:
     the pattern at position `m-1` recurs, so the commutator at the
     transported position is tied to one that is already forced trivial.
     No general proof is claimed.

   OPEN sub-question: does `D([[phi]])` of some minimal subshift contain a
   triple `(u, c, l)` with `[c, u^k l u^-k] = 1` for all `k >= 0` and
   `[u c u^-1, l] != 1`?  A yes gives a finitely generated simple MF group
   with full defect, which would also kill the finite-generation survivor.
2. **Periodic and locally residually finite hosts.**  These are dead by a
   short argument.
   - If `u^n = 1` and `u L u^-1 <= L`, then `u L u^-1 = L`, so `u c u^-1`
     centralizes `L` and every cell is trivial. Simple locally finite
     groups therefore have no cells.
   - A locally residually finite host has trivial defect by
     `locally-residually-finite-targets-kill-compression-defects` applied to
     the identity map.
   - The countable closure in the proof cannot be taken inside a direct
     limit of finite groups.
3. **Finite generation by embedding.**  Countable simple groups embed into
   finitely generated simple groups (Hall, Schupp), and the cell persists.
   But MF is not known to pass to those overgroups. This is not pursued
   here.
4. **Finite generation, settled through Nekrashevych's alternating full
   groups (swarm-0917-w14-ptm-follow).**  The finite-generation survivor
   is dead: see `fg-simple-mf-group-with-full-compression-defect`.
   - Take the three-lane cell group
     `Delta = FAlt(Z x Z/3) x| <t, rho>`, whose lane velocities are
     `(1, 0, -1)`.  It is elementary amenable, and it is generated by
     elements of order 3, since `t = (t rho^-1) rho`.
   - Let `Delta` act freely and minimally on a subshift `X` of `2^Delta`
     (Gao-Jackson-Seward).
   - Then `A(Delta x| X)` is finitely generated and simple (Nekrashevych,
     Theorems 5.6 and 4.1).
   - It contains `Delta`, because a freely acting element of order 3 is the
     3-cycle of a multisection.
   - It is MF, because it lies in the unitary group of the AF-embeddable
     crossed product `C(X) x|_r Delta` (Barlak-Li UCT together with the
     AF-embedding theorem).

   The OPEN `D([[phi]])` sub-question of Attempt 1 is no longer needed for
   finite generation.  Its obstruction, that disjoint supports die by
   Poincare recurrence, does not apply to free actions of non-cyclic
   groups, because there the cell commutes through the algebra of `Delta`.
