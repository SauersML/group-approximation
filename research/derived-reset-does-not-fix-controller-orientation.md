title: The equal-scale derived reset does not select a controller orientation
---

The controller's two orientations have identical restriction to the Pauli
and system edge.  Tensoring with the balanced four-point derived reset
multiplies both rows equally and cannot distinguish them.

Identifying the controller with the reset destroys exact completeness, while
a balanced double pass retains an uncontrolled multiplicity commutator.
Orientation therefore requires an explicit source tag or a mixed
controller--Hadamard table.
