## The group

Let `D = Z[1/2]` and let

```text
N = D x D x (D/Z),
(x,y,z)(x',y',z') = (x+x', y+y', z+z'+xy'),
```

the third coordinate read modulo `Z`.  This is the Heisenberg group over `D`
with its centre reduced modulo `Z`; it is nilpotent of class two, its centre
is `0 x 0 x (D/Z)`, and

```text
[(x,y,z),(x',y',z')] = (0,0, xy' - x'y).                (DH1)
```

The map

```text
alpha(x,y,z) = (2x, y/2, z)
```

is an automorphism: applying it to a product gives
`(2x+2x', (y+y')/2, z+z'+xy')`, and the product of the images gives
`(2x+2x', (y+y')/2, z+z'+(2x)(y'/2))`, the same element.  Its inverse is
`(x,y,z) |-> (x/2, 2y, z)`, defined because `D` is `2`-divisible.  Put

```text
G = N x| _alpha <t>,     t n t^(-1) = alpha(n).
```

## The configuration

Put `a = (1,0,0)`, `b = (0,1,0)`, `L = <a>`.

**`L` is infinite cyclic and properly compressed.**  `a^n = (n,0,0)`, since
the cocycle term `x y'` vanishes on the first axis, so `L ~= Z`.  And

```text
t a t^(-1) = alpha(a) = (2,0,0) = a^2,
```

so `t L t^(-1) = <a^2>` has index two in `L`; in particular
`t in Comp_G(L)`.

**`b` centralizes `L`.**  By `(DH1)`, `[b, a^n] = (0,0, 0 - n) = (0,0,-n)`,
and `n in Z` is zero in `D/Z`.  This is the one place the quotient by `Z` is
used, and it is why the second axis may carry a centralizer at all.

**The defect.**  `t b t^(-1) = alpha(b) = (0, 1/2, 0)`, so by `(DH1)`

```text
k = [t b t^(-1), a] = (0, 0, -1/2).                     (DH2)
```

Then `k != 1` because `-1/2` is not an integer, `k^2 = (0,0,-1) = 1`, and `k`
lies in the centre of `N` and is fixed by `alpha`, so `k` is central in `G`.
Thus `<k> ~= C_2` is a nontrivial finite central subgroup, and by definition
of the intrinsic defect `k in D_G(L)` with the witnesses `u = t`, `c = b`,
`l = a`.

## `G` is MF

`G` is generated by `a`, `b`, `t`: conjugating by powers of `t` gives
`(2^m,0,0)` and `(0,2^n,0)` for every `m,n in Z`, which generate the two
`D`-axes, and `(DH1)` on those elements gives `(0,0,2^(n-m))`, which generate
`D/Z`.  `G` is an extension of `Z` by the class-two nilpotent group `N`, so it
is solvable, hence elementary amenable.  By
`elementary-amenable-groups-are-quasidiagonal`, `C*(G)` is quasidiagonal,
hence MF, so `G` embeds in the unitary group of a norm matrix corona.

## What this witness adds

Its MF-ness is quoted from the literature and is independent of every
approximation argument in this program, so the survival of `k` cannot be
attributed to a defect of the corona machinery.  The defect subgroup `<k>` is
finite, central, and of order two, and being finite it has property (T) — so
the property-(T) hypothesis that `finite-normal-compression-obstruction` needs
really is a hypothesis about `L`, not about the killed subgroup.

By `compression-defect-dies-in-finite-dimensions`, every exact
finite-dimensional unitary representation of `G` kills `k`.  So this single
group also exhibits the gap between the exact half of the mechanism and its
approximate half.

Not claimed: that `c = b` may be taken to be an involution here.  It may not —
`b` has infinite order.  The corona witness supplies that refinement.
