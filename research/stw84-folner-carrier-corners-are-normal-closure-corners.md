Retain the notation

```text
G=K rtimes_alpha Z,
J=ker(C*(G)->C*(Z))=I_K rtimes_alpha Z.
```

Let `F<=K` be finite, let `N>=1`, and form the Følner carrier

```text
h=N^(-1) sum_(j=0)^(N-1) alpha^j(e_F),
e_F=1-|F|^(-1)sum_(g in F)lambda_g.                    (NCC1)
```

Put

```text
H=<alpha^j(F):0<=j<N><=K.                              (NCC2)
```

Local finiteness makes `H` finite.  The support projection of `h` is exactly

```text
s(h)=e_H=1-|H|^(-1)sum_(g in H)lambda_g.               (NCC3)
```

Let `M=normal_closure_G(H)`, and let

```text
Q_M=ker(C*(G)->C*(G/M)).                               (NCC4)
```

Then the closed ideal generated by `s(h)` is exactly `Q_M`.  Moreover,

```text
s(h)Js(h)=s(h)C*(G)s(h)                               (NCC5)
```

is a full corner of `Q_M`.  Consequently

```text
dim_nuc(s(h)Js(h))=dim_nuc(Q_M),                       (NCC6)
```

with equality also when the common value is infinite.

Thus the carrier-corner program is a normal-closure filtration, not a
finite-propagation approximation by intrinsically smaller algebras.  It can
reduce the problem only when the finite orbit-window subgroup has proper
normal closure in `K`.

For the alternating shift

```text
K=Alt_fin(Z),       alpha=shift,
```

every nontrivial carrier has `M=K`, because `K` is simple.  Hence every
nonzero carrier corner is full in the entire augmentation crossed product:

```text
dim_nuc(s(h)Js(h))=dim_nuc(J).                         (NCC7)
```

In particular, a uniform bound on those corners is equivalent to the
original desired bound for `J`; banded Fourier compression cannot gain
nuclear dimension merely by passing to the support corner.  Any successful
argument for this example must exploit the positive values of `h` before
taking support, or add structure beyond fullness/Morita equivalence.
