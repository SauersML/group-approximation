There is a fixed finitely presented solvable group `A` of derived length three
with undecidable word problem.  In particular `A` is amenable and, by
[[amenable-implies-operator-mf]], operator-MF.

The existence and derived-length statement are due to O. G. Kharlampovich.
The amenability implication is elementary for solvable groups; the MF
implication is the separately source-checked Cairn literature input.
