From `(FPR2)` and `beta_k>=beta`,

```text
sigma_k-beta tau
=(beta_k-beta)tau+(1-beta_k)rho_k
```

is positive on the full group C-star algebra.  The positive cone is closed
under pointwise weak-star limits, so `(FPR1)` gives `(FPR3)`.

For `h in H`, apply the two positive functionals to
`(1-u_h)^*(1-u_h)`.  The value under `phi_H` is zero, hence the value under
`tau` is zero.  Thus `pi_tau(h)=1` in the tracial GNS representation and
`tau` factors through `Q=G/H`.

Regard both functionals on `C^*(Q)`.  Domination by the canonical trace says
that `tau` extends normally to `L(Q)`: there is

```text
0 <= a <= beta^-1 1,    tau(x)=tau_Q(ax).               (FPR7)
```

Because `tau` is tracial, `a` is central.  If `z` is the support of `a`, the
GNS von Neumann algebra of `tau` is `zL(Q)` (with its equivalent weighted
trace).  Connes embeddability passes from a finite von Neumann algebra to
its central summands, so non-embeddability of the `tau`-GNS algebra forces
non-embeddability of `L(Q)`.

When `Q` is ICC, centrality makes `a` scalar; normalization makes it `1`.
Equivalently, the canonical quotient character is extreme, so its convex
decomposition containing `tau` can only be trivial.

For the diagonal-power specialization, equality in Cauchy--Schwarz shows
that `|tau(g)|=1` exactly when `pi_tau(g)` is scalar.  Hence `H_tau` is a
normal subgroup and those scalars form a character `chi:H_tau->T`.
Recurrence of a point in the compact monothetic group generated by `chi`
gives unbounded `n_k` with `chi(h)^(n_k)->1` simultaneously for the
countable set `H_tau`.  Outside `H_tau`, scalar powers tend to zero.  This
proves `(FPR5)`.  Finally `(FPR6)` is simply positivity of
`tau^n-beta tau` evaluated on `(sum_i c_i u_{g_i})^*(sum_i c_i u_{g_i})`.
The determinant of the resulting matrix for `{e,g}` gives `(FPR8)`.

For the `S_3` sharpness check, the standard character has class values
`(2,0,-1)`.  Its multiplicity in its `n`-th tensor power is

```text
m_n=<chi_std^n,chi_std>
   =(2^n-(-1)^n)/3.
```

Hence its normalized trace weight is `2m_n/2^n`, which tends to `2/3` (and
is at least `1/2` for `n>=2`).  Meanwhile the normalized character values
off the identity are `0` and `(-1/2)^n`, so the tensor characters converge
to the canonical character of `S_3`.  The retained GNS algebra is exactly
the standard central block of its finite group von Neumann algebra.
