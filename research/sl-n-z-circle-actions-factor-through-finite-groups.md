**Statement.** Let `n >= 3` and let `Γ` be a subgroup of finite index in
`SL(n,Z)`. Then every continuous action of `Γ` on the circle factors through a
finite group action: the image of `Γ -> Homeo(S^1)` is finite.

**Source.** D. Witte, *Arithmetic groups of higher Q-rank cannot act on
1-manifolds*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 122 (1994), no. 2, 333--340. The theorem is
proved there more generally for arithmetic subgroups of Q-simple groups of
Q-rank at least 2.
