title: The Stallings–Bieri group SB_3 = ker(F_2^3 → Z) is finitely presented but not of type F_3
---

Let SB_3 be the kernel of the homomorphism F_2 × F_2 × F_2 → Z that sends every standard generator to
1. Then SB_3 is finitely presented and not of type F_3. This is Stallings' 1963 example of a finitely
presented group with H_3 not finitely generated.
