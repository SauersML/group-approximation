**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed; sk-free-8).**
- **Setting.** Let `A` be a unital ring of characteristic 2 generated as a ring by a finite set `Σ`, let `m ≥ 3`, and index matrices by `Y_n = X_n × [m]` with `X_n = {0,1}^n`.
- **Generating set.** `T_n(Σ) ⊆ EL_(Y_n)(A)` consists of four types (notation of `sauer-schesler-grigorchuk-level-estimates`):
  1. the truncated Grigorchuk generators acting as permutation matrices on the first coordinate;
  2. the permutation matrices of `Sym({η_n} × [m])`;
  3. the transposition of `(ρ_n,1)` and `(θ_n,1)`;
  4. the transvections `e_((ρ_n,2),(ρ_n,3))(σ)` for `σ ∈ Σ ∪ {1}`.
- **Conclusion.** `T_n(Σ)` generates `EL_(Y_n)(A)`, and there are `C > 0` and `β ∈ (0,1)`, depending only on `|Σ|` and `m`, such that for all large `n` and all `ℓ ≤ r_n/3`, `γ^(T_n(Σ))(ℓ) ≤ exp(C ℓ^β)`.
- **Credit.** This is Sauer–Schesler's estimate for `Aut(F_(4·2^n))` (arXiv:2606.15945, Theorem 2), transferred to elementary matrix groups with ring coefficients. Types 2 and 4 are replaced by `Sym(m)` on a fibre and by transvections carrying the ring generators.
- **Special case.** `A = F_2` gives such generating sets for the finite simple groups `SL_(m·2^n)(F_2)`.
