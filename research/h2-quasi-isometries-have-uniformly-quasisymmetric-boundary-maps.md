title: Quasi-isometries of H^2 with fixed constants induce uniformly quasisymmetric boundary maps
---

For all `K ≥ 1`, `C ≥ 0` there is `K'` such that every `(K,C)`-quasi-isometry `f` of the
hyperbolic plane extends to a homeomorphism `∂f` of the circle at infinity, and `∂f` is
`K'`-quasisymmetric in Markovic's normalization (it extends to a `K'`-quasiconformal
self-map of the disc). Moreover `∂(f∘g) = ∂f ∘ ∂g`, and quasi-isometries at bounded
distance from each other have the same boundary map, so a quasi-action gives a
homomorphism to `Homeo(S^1)`.

Classical (Efremovich–Tikhomirova, Mostow; in the language of Gromov products the
boundary map of a quasi-isometry is power-quasisymmetric with exponent and constant
controlled by `K, C`). The extension to a quasiconformal disc map with controlled constant
then follows from the Beurling–Ahlfors extension on the disc model.
