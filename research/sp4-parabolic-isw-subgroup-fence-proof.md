Write the hypothetical copy as `A rtimes F`, with `A = Z^2`.  Projection to
the Levi is injective on `F`: its kernel is an amenable normal subgroup of a
nonabelian free group, hence trivial.  The image of `A` in the virtually free
Levi is an amenable normal subgroup of the non-elementary group generated by
the image of `F`, hence finite.  Replacing `A` by a finite-index subgroup
`A_0`, we may therefore assume that `A_0` lies in the unipotent radical.

For the Siegel parabolic, `A_0` gives an `F`-invariant rank-two rational
subspace of

```text
Sym^2(Q^2).
```

But a non-elementary subgroup of `SL_2(Z)` is Zariski dense in `SL_2`, and
the three-dimensional symmetric-square representation is irreducible.  This
is impossible.

For the Klingen parabolic, project `A_0 < H_3(Z)` to
`H_3(Z)/Z(H_3(Z)) = Z^2`.  Since `A_0` is abelian, its image is isotropic for
the nondegenerate alternating commutator form, hence has rank at most one.
Its rational span is invariant under the non-elementary Levi image of `F`;
irreducibility of the standard representation forces that span to be zero.
Thus `A_0` lies in the cyclic centre of `H_3(Z)`, contradicting
`rank(A_0)=2`.

The same argument survives the finite changes between `(SPF2)` and the
literal integral parabolics by intersecting a hypothetical copy with the
finite-index Jacobi/Siegel subgroup first.
