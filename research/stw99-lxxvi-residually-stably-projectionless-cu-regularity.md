Let A be a separable pure C*-algebra such that every quotient A/I,
including A itself, is stably projectionless. Then the actual
first-factor map

```text
Cu(A) -> Cu(A tensor Z)
```

is an isomorphism on the entire Cuntz semigroup.

In particular, if D is a separable simple stably projectionless pure
C*-algebra, then

```text
C0(X,D) is pure and Cu-regular
```

for every second-countable locally compact Hausdorff space X. There is
no covering-dimension bound, no K1-vanishing hypothesis, and no nuclearity
or Z-stability assumption on D.

This is a full higher-dimensional positive class for STW LXXVI, including
arbitrary compact metrizable bases. The residual hypothesis is essential
to this proof: stable projectionlessness of A alone does not imply that
all its quotient images are soft.

The proof makes every source and target Cu class functionally soft and
then applies the actual soft first-factor isomorphism. To control every
target quotient, it uses the published tensor-ideal theorem for a simple
exact factor, applied with that factor equal to Z.
