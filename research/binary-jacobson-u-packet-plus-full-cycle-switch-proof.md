Put

```text
Delta=diag(t^(-1),1,1,1,1).
```

The symbol calculation in the balanced packet gives

```text
Delta mu(P) Delta^(-1)
 =<x_13(1),x_21(1),x_41(1)>.                            (JPP1)
```

Indeed, the `Q`-labelled generators have trivial symbol, while before
conjugation the only nontrivial generator symbols are

```text
x_13(t),              x_21(t^(-1)),              x_41(t^(-1)).
```

For the two added occurrences,

```text
m=Delta mu(omega) Delta^(-1)
  =[[0,t^(-1)],[t,0]] on <e_1,e_2>,                    (JPP2)

Delta mu(tau) Delta^(-1)=x_23(t^(-1)).                 (JPP3)
```

In particular `m^2=1`.

Order the active four-coordinate space as

```text
e_4 | (e_1,e_2) | e_3.
```

Every generator in `(JPP1)--(JPP3)` lies in the corresponding block-upper
parabolic; the fifth coordinate is fixed.  Its unipotent radical is
two-step nilpotent.  On the middle two-dimensional block, the image is
generated by

```text
x_21(1),             m.
```

Both are involutions, so this middle image is a quotient of

```text
C_2*C_2=D_infinity.
```

It is amenable.  The symbol group `mu(H)`, being an extension of a subgroup
of this dihedral group by a subgroup of the nilpotent radical, is therefore
amenable.

The exact symbol sequence in
`binary-jacobson-elementary-mark-is-finite-quotient-invisible` has locally
finite kernel

```text
ker(mu)=GL_fin(N x {1,...,5},F_2).
```

Hence `H cap ker(mu)` is amenable, and the exact sequence

```text
1 -> H cap ker(mu) -> H -> mu(H) -> 1
```

proves that `H` is amenable.

The finite-packet prerequisite proves that `w in P` is nonidentity.  The
regular-strength form of `amenable-implies-operator-mf` comes from
quasidiagonality of the canonical trace on `C*_r(H)` and supplies
operator-norm models whose traces converge to `delta_1`.  Since `w` is a
nontrivial involution, involution rounding changes the coordinate trace by
`o(1)` and gives `(JPA2)`.

Finally,

```text
Delta mu(x_31(1)) Delta^(-1)=x_31(t).                   (JPP4)
```

Relative to the displayed block order, `(JPP4)` is a lower-block root and
is not in the parabolic.  Thus retaining `k=x_31(1)` separately crosses
exactly the firewall which contains `P`, `omega`, and `tau`.
