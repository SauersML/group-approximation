**ESTABLISHED (written deduction).** Proof in `fpbs-fixed-position-morse-matchings-die-on-bernoulli-proof`.

**Setting.**
- `Gamma` is generated by a finite set `S`, and `r` is a trivial word.
- `s in S` satisfies `s^2 != 1`. One `s`-occurrence of `r` is designated, and its boundary edge is traversed once.
- `Q` is the set of elements at which the cell `(x, r)` traverses `s`-edges, normalized so that the designated one is
  `1`. Put `P = Q \ {1}` and `Lambda = <P>`.
- A fixed-position matching `M_A`, for Borel `A ⊆ X`, sends `(x, r)` to the `s`-edge at `x` for every `x in A`.
  It is a Morse matching (M1–M3 of `fpbs-bernoulli-morse-collapse-criterion`) iff (WF) holds: no infinite chain
  `x_(j+1) in P.x_j` stays in `A`.

**Theorem.**
1. **(Heights, any action.)** If (WF) holds and `delta = mu(X \ A)`, there are increasing Borel sets `X_k`, with
   conull union and increments at most `delta`, such that `mu(p.X_k Δ X_k) <= 2 delta` for every `p in P`.
2. **(Obstruction.)** If `Lambda` is nonamenable and the action is the Bernoulli shift of `Gamma` or a factor of it,
   then
   `mu(M_A) <= 1 - min(1/4, 3(1 - rho)/32)`,
   with `rho` the Kesten spectral radius of `Lambda` at `P ∪ P^-1`.

**Instances.**
- For `<S | w>` torsion-free, this is a Morse deficit of at least that amount against the ceiling `1`, for all
  fixed-position matchings at `{w}`.
- For `m >= 2` it covers `<w̄>`-saturated windows (Corollary 7 of the artifact).
- Assuming the import [K], it extends to every action weakly contained in Bernoulli.

**Class killed.** Transplant Poulin--Wróbel's Theorem 3.4 cutting to Bernoulli by replacing their `Z/n` phase
windows (Lemma 3.3) with any other Borel rule: iid markers, local cutoffs, or anything else.

- *Invariant:* the Kesten gap of `Lambda` on `L^2_0`.
- *Step where it dies:* termination of the replacement operator `theta`, which is (M3). Termination makes the
  height sublevel sets almost invariant under `Lambda`.

**Survivors.** Matchings whose position varies (the dual-forest type), transversal-modulated schemes for `m >= 2`,
and longer trivial words.

**Calibration.**
- For amenable `<P>` there is no obstruction, and iid markers work.
- On PW's coset action (GAP) fails, and PW succeed.
- The class already fails at `r = c s b s a s`, a presentation of `F_3` whose group has fixed price. So this is an
  obstruction to the method, not to the group.
