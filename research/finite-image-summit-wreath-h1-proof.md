Project the finite-index kernel `N` to the actor.  Its lamp kernel has
exponent two, while its projected finite-index `S`-arithmetic subgroup has
property `(T)` and therefore finite abelianization.  Hence `N^ab` is torsion
and admits no homomorphism to the real vector space `u(d)`.  The adjoint
module factors through the finite image `H`; finite-group cohomology in
positive degree vanishes by averaging.  Inflation--restriction then gives
`H^1(W_3,u(d)_Ad rho)=0`.  Vanishing on the actor forces the coboundary
primitive into the actor commutant, proving the relative statement.
