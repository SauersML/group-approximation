ESTABLISHED.  Let `G` be a one-relator group and
`R = G_Q^(omega)` its Linton radical.  If `R' = 1`, then `G` is sofic (and
hence hyperlinear).

This includes the residually rationally solvable case `R = 1` but is strictly
formulated to allow nontrivial abelian radicals.  The point is not that an
abelian normal subgroup and a sofic quotient form a sofic semidirect product;
that inference is unavailable.  Instead,
[[one-relator-fox-magnus-descent-to-derived-linton-radical]] embeds the whole
quotient `G/R' = G` into an explicit finite-lamp restricted wreath product.
