The binary Leavitt group is nontrivial: x_12(1) is a nonidentity unit in M_4(R), and the complete-prefix-code isomorphism embeds it in R^x. The prerequisite makes every tracial matrix-ultraproduct homomorphism trivial, so none is faithful. This rules out hyperlinearity.

The prerequisite is the credited Cairn application of Jihao Liu's [internality theorem](https://jihaoliu.org/ai-results/nonhyperlinear-groups-exist-2026-09-20.pdf), Theorem 1.2/6.7, and Andreas Thom's [conditional normalization theorem](https://andreasthom.github.io/pdf/nonhyperlinear.pdf). No new analytic mechanism is asserted by this elementary consequence.
