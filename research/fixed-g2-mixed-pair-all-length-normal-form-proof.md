The two roots `V` and `U_2` are involutions. Their Steinberg
commutator is

```text
P=[V,U_2]=x_(c_0,c_2)(ST).
```

The root `P` is an involution. It commutes with `V` because the two
roots have the same row, and with `U_2` because they have the same
column. It is nonidentity. Hence

```text
(VU_2)^2=P,                    |VU_2|=4,                (DAN3)
```

and the subgroup is dihedral of order eight.

Move every occurrence of `P` to the left, cancel adjacent squares, and
use `U_2V=PVU_2`. This collects every word into `(DAN1)`. The form is
unique: the `(c_0,c_3)` entry detects `delta`, the `(c_3,c_2)` entry
detects `gamma`, and after they are fixed the `(c_0,c_2)` entry detects
`epsilon`. The relevant coefficients `S,T,ST` are nonzero in the
faithful Jacobson half-line model.

If `delta=gamma=0`, all coefficients lie in `F_2[q]` because
`ST=1-q`. If only `delta` is nonzero, they lie in

```text
B_+=F_2<S,q,a=qT>.
```

If only `gamma` is nonzero, they lie in

```text
B_-=F_2<q,T>.
```

Adjoining `P` changes neither ring. The established finite truncations
show that both rings are residually finite, and entrywise separation
shows that every finitely generated subgroup of the corresponding
`GL_28` is residually finite. This conclusion still includes `C`,
`g_2`, and arbitrary scalar conditioning.

It remains to inspect `delta=gamma=1`. Put `Y=VU_2`. Equation
`(DAN3)` gives

```text
PVU_2=Y^3=Y^(-1)=U_2V.                                 (DAN4)
```

Thus the two possible collected words are exactly the two carriers in
the preceding rank theorem. Both have the hidden-`Z` initial projection

```text
G_(sigma,+)(1+Z)/2,
```

and both vanish in the negative-`tau` rows.

For any integer `j`, left multiplication by `Ee_b g_2^j` is a
contraction with range in `Ee_b`. Therefore it cannot enlarge the
already computed initial support. Substituting the exact ranks
`43/1152` and `7/192` proves `(DAN2)`.

This is an all-length statement for one fixed shifted pair, not for the
group generated by every scalar conjugate of both raw roots. A new
carrier must change at least one raw root position. That positional
change is precisely what can replace the shared sign `Z` by an
independent hidden sign and is the next case not covered by the
dihedral normal form.
