Section 3 of the artifact.

1. **Free conormal module.** `I` is generated by the `u_h`, so `I/I^2` is generated over
   `B` by the `[u_h]`. `d` is `B`-linear on `I/I^2`, since `d(ri) = r di + i dr == r di`
   modulo `I`. In characteristic `2`, `d(u_h) = (2X_h + 1) dX_h = dX_h`, a basis of
   `(+)_h B dX_h`. So the `[u_h]` are independent and `d` is an isomorphism.
2. **Semilinear Jacobian.** `hat tau^*(r u_h) = hat tau^*(r) hat tau^*(u_h)` with
   `hat tau^*(r) == tau^*(bar r)` modulo `I`, and
   `d(hat mu_h^2 + hat mu_h) = d hat mu_h = sum_{h'} (partial hat mu_h/partial X_{h'}) dX_{h'}`.
   Transport by `d^{-1}`.
3. **Row span.** `sigma o tau = id` gives `tau^* sigma^* = id_B`, so `tau^*` is onto and
   the image of the semilinear map is the `B`-span of the rows.
4. **Constant slice.** Evaluation at a constant configuration is a ring map `B -> F_2`,
   base change is right exact, and equivariance identifies the row at `(g,i)` with the
   `g`-translate of row `i` of `J_tau(c)`. The quotient `F_2[G]^n / (row module)` is `0`
   iff every unit row is `x J_tau(c)` for some `x`, i.e. `X J_tau(c) = 1`.
5. **Lemma C.** Adding `u_h P` to an encoder rule changes its partial in `X_h` at every
   Boolean point `c` by `P(c)`, because `partial u_h / partial X_h = 1` and `u_h(c) = 0`,
   and leaves the rule unchanged on points. Choosing `P` as a finite-memory representative
   of `I + J` makes the Jacobian `I` at every Boolean point. A `B`-element is determined
   by its values on points, so `J_B = I`, the rows form a basis and `C = 0`.
