Paddock--Slofstra Example 4.2 states that `MIP*=RE` implies existence of a
synchronous algebra with a tracial state but no homomorphism to `R^U`, hence a
finite BCS which is tracially satisfiable but not `R^U`-satisfiable.  The
companion artifact records how this sits immediately before their linear-system
boundary, Lemma 5.7 and Proposition 5.8.
