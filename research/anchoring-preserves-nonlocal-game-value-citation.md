**Established by citation.**  Bavarian--Vidick--Yuen, *Anchored parallel
repetition for nonlocal games*, arXiv:1509.07466v2, Definition 1 and the lemma
immediately following it, prove `(AV1)`--`(AV3)` for both entangled and
classical values.

The proof is self-contained in the cited lemma.  On the event that neither
question is replaced, whose probability is `(1-alpha)^2`, the anchored
verifier and strategy are exactly those of `G`; on the complementary event the
verifier accepts.  Restriction and extension of strategies give the two
inequalities before the supremum is taken.
