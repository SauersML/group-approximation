Let gamma:T^r->Aut(A) be a point-norm continuous action on a unital
C*-algebra with unitaries u_j satisfying gamma_z(u_j)=z_j u_j.
Suppose epsilon:K_0(A)->Z is a group homomorphism with epsilon([1])=1.

Then gamma_*:Z^r->pi_1(Aut(A),id) is split injective. A retraction is

    [alpha] |-> (epsilon(Bott[t |-> alpha_t(u_j)u_j*]))_(j=1,...,r).

Bott here includes stabilization. Thus no stable-rank hypothesis or
identification of the full homotopy type of Aut(A) is required.
