## Setting

- `C = {0,1}^N ≅ Z_2` (least significant digit first), `X = C × C`, `τ(x) = x + 1`, and for `c ∈ Z_2`,
  `τ_c(x) = x + c`.
- `H ≤ Aut(T_2)` is self-similar with `τ ∈ H`. Its translation part is `T_H = {c ∈ Z_2 : τ_c ∈ H}`, a
  subgroup containing `Z`.
- `2V_H` is the group of homeomorphisms of `X` that, on the rectangles of some finite partition, have the
  form `(p x, w y) ↦ (p' η(x), w' y)` with `η ∈ H`. For `H = ⟨τ⟩` this is the odometer host `2V_τ` of
  `odometer-2v-is-fp-simple-and-contains-bs12`.
- `s = τ × id` and `u(x, y_0 y) = (y_0 x, y)`, so `u s u^-1 = s^2`.

## Theorem

1. **Centralizer.** `C_{2V_H}(s)` consists of the maps `(x, y) ↦ (x + a(y), v(y))` with `v ∈ V` and
   `a : C → T_H` locally constant. For `H = ⟨τ⟩` this is item 1 of
   `odometer-2v-doubling-has-no-compatible-cube-root`.
2. **Roots of the natural shape.** For an odd `ℓ ≥ 1`, some `r ∈ 2V_H` with `r^ℓ = s` acts trivially on the
   second coordinate iff `1/ℓ ∈ T_H`, and then `r = τ_(1/ℓ) × id`.
3. **The native copy.** If `T_H ⊇ Z_(2)`, then `⟨τ_c × id (c ∈ Z_(2)), u⟩ ≅ Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩`. It is the natural
   affine action on `X ≅ {0,1}^Z`, where `u` is the shift and `τ_c × id` adds `c` with carries to the right;
   `u (τ_c × id) u^-1 = τ_(2c) × id`.
4. **What H must be.** A finitely generated such `H` contains the odometer and its roots at every odd
   prime. By `odometer-root-hosts-are-non-affine-and-non-contracting` it is not affine, not contracting, and
   not in `Pol(∞)`. By `automaton-groups-see-only-smooth-periods` it is not generated by finite-state
   automorphisms. Whether such an `H` exists is open. A finitely presented one would give a finitely presented
   residually finite group containing `Z_(2)`, i.e. `z-semilocalized-embeds-in-fp-rf-group` (SL) with
   `S = {2}`.

## Where divisibility and distortion live (critic rules)

- **Divisibility:** in the coefficient group `H` (the translations `Z_(2)`, odd part) and in the baker
  conjugates `u^-k (τ_c × id) u^k` (`2`-part).
- **Distortion:** the baker map `u`, which conjugates `s` to `s^2`, as in `2V_τ`.
- **`SL_3(Z)`:** not addressed; this is the first rung `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩` of the `Aff(Q)` ladder only.

## Not claimed

Finite presentation and simplicity of `2V_H` for general `H`. For `H = ⟨τ⟩` both come from Li's and Matui's
theorems (`odometer-2v-is-fp-simple-and-contains-bs12`). For a larger `H` they need those theorems' hypotheses
re-checked. The one-coordinate group `V_2(H)` is finitely presented when `H` is a finitely presented self-similar
group (Scott), and its commutator subgroup is simple (`rover-nekrashevych-finite-presentation-criteria`).
There, however, no element conjugates `τ` to `τ^2`: `τ` is minimal on `C` and `τ^2` is not, which is why the host
needs the second coordinate.

## Lesson

The `Q ⋊ ⟨2⟩` rung in Thompson-type hosts over the odometer comes down to one coefficient question. Either
the coefficient group already holds the odometer's roots at all odd primes, which is the open odometer-root host
and, in finitely presented form, (SL); or the roots must move the second coordinate, and along the host's own
dyadic tower that fails (`odometer-2v-doubling-has-no-compatible-cube-root`, items 2 and 6). The doubling itself
is never the problem; the baker map supplies it for free.
