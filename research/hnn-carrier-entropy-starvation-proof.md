Complete direct proof in the target node.  Inputs at statement level,
refereed: Hayes (arXiv:1505.06682, IMRN 2018) — 1-bounded entropy
monotone under wq-normalizing generation; Jung (arXiv:math/0510576,
GAFA 2007) — strong 1-boundedness of `L(SL_n(Z))`, `n >= 3` (property
(T) route also in Hayes–Jekel–Kunnawalkam Elayavalli,
arXiv:2107.03278).  The only computation is the double-coset one:
commensuration of `SL_n(Z)` in `SL_n(Z[1/p])` puts every group unitary
in the quasi-normalizer of `L(SL_n(Z))`, and the stable letter
centralizes it, so the quasi-normalizer generates the carrier factor.
