**ESTABLISHED.**  Put `F3=F(a,b,c)`, `K=<b,c>`, and
`P=F3 times F3`.  For `h in K`, set

```text
x=(a,1),
alpha(h)=(h,1),
r(h)=(h,h),
beta(h)=x r(h) x^-1=(a h a^-1,h),
gamma_h=alpha(h)^-1 x r(h)=(h^-1 a h,h).
```

Let `Gamma=<gamma_h:h in K>`, the subgroup called
`Higman.Star.graphSub`, and let

```text
M=<alpha(b),alpha(c),beta(b),beta(c)> <= P.
```

Adjoin two free stable letters `u_b,u_c`, each centralizing `M`:

```text
E=<P,u_b,u_c | [u_b,M]=[u_c,M]=1>
  = P *_M (M times F(u_b,u_c)).
```

For `h in K`, write `U_h` for the corresponding word in the stable-letter
free group and put

```text
p_h=alpha(h) U_h,
q_h=r(h) x^-1 U_h x,
L=<x,p_b,p_c,q_b,q_c>.
```

Then

```text
L intersect P = Gamma.                                      (PR1)
```

In particular `L` is a five-generated cutter and `(E,P->E,L)` is a
torsion-free benign witness for `Star.graphSub`.

The proof has three exact intersection calculations.  In
`C=M times F(u_b,u_c)`, let `Q` be generated by the two graph lifts
`(alpha(h),U_h)` and `(beta(h),U_h)`, and put

```text
delta_h=alpha(h)^-1 beta(h)=gamma_h x^-1,
Delta=normalClosure_M {delta_b,delta_c}.
```

Projection of the two graph lifts to the stable free factor is the fold
`K*K -> K`; therefore

```text
Q intersect M = Delta.                                      (PR2)
```

The retraction `F3->K` killing `a` gives a fold map `M->K` whose kernel is
the same `Delta`.  Moreover

```text
alpha(k) delta_h alpha(k)^-1
  = gamma_(h k^-1) gamma_(k^-1)^-1.
```

These are the oriented Cayley-edge differences.  Telescoping paths in the
left Cayley graph of `K=F(b,c)` shows

```text
Delta=<delta_h:h in K> <= Gamma,
Gamma intersect M = Delta.                                  (PR3)
```

The matched-subgroup normal form in the amalgam
`P *_M (M times F(u_b,u_c))` now gives
`<Gamma,Q> intersect P=Gamma`.  Since
`p_h^-1 x q_h=gamma_h`, `x=gamma_1`, and `L=<x,Q>`, this is `(PR1)`.

Finally, the witness preserves the operator-MF positive resource in the
precise sense relevant at this leaf.  The base `P` is residually finite and
hence regularly operator-MF realized.  Regular operator-MF realizations are
closed under central HNN extensions over arbitrary subgroups, so applying
that theorem twice makes `E` regularly operator-MF realized.  No
separability of `M`, noncentral HNN permanence, PFF model, or GKMP strong-MF
hypothesis is used.

This closes the mathematical datum
`Higman.Star.ConjugatorGraph.graph_benign`.  It does not by itself prove MF
permanence of the later quotient-valued graph HNN used by the final Higman
rope; that separate step is the tensor-synchronization theorem in the MF
compiler route.
