**Problem LXIX of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902.**
If separable nuclear `A` is homotopic to zero ideal-preservingly, must
`A ⊗ W ⊗ K` be an inductive limit of one-dimensional NCCW complexes?

## Attempts

* 1-NCCW complexes are themselves ideal-preservingly null-homotopic
  and the property passes to limits and `W ⊗ K`-stabilizations, so
  the hypothesis is necessary; the question is the converse range
  statement.  Ideal-preserving null-homotopy trivializes `KK`-data
  fibrewise over the ideal lattice (each subquotient is
  `KK`-contractible), so under Robert's conjecture (LXVIII) the
  target is determined by its trace cone, and the problem becomes a
  RANGE computation: every trace cone of such an `A` is realized by a
  1-NCCW limit.  Cones of lsc traces on nuclear algebras with
  Hausdorff-ish ideal data are exactly the dual cones of
  Choquet-type lattices realized by 1-NCCW towers (Elliott's
  range-of-cone technology); the missing piece is only the
  non-Hausdorff primitive spectrum case, the same
  wild-ideal-lattice frontier as LXXI.  Recorded: LXVIII ⟹ LXIX
  modulo that cone-range lemma.
