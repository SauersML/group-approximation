The artifact gives the full finite-support embedding proof. If
F=H intersect lamps and Q=image(H->G), normality makes the finite union
S of supports of F Q-invariant. Restricting to S embeds H in A^S semidirect
Q, which embeds in (A^S semidirect image(Q->Aut(A^S))) times Q, an RF group.
Locally finite subgroups of a fixed hyperbolic group are finite by its
finite-subgroup order bound, giving the corollary. Torsion-free subgroups
intersect the locally finite lamp group trivially.
