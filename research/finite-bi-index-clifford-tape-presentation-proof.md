The displayed presentation is finite because `S,R,T` and `H\L/H` are
finite.  The relations `[a,t]=1` make the conjugate attached to `gH`
independent of its right-coset representative in every exact representation.
Conjugating the last family of relators and using the double-coset
decomposition gives `(FCT2)` for every pair of distinct cosets.

For consistency, form the `F_2`-vector space with basis `X` and alternating
bilinear form `b(e_x,e_y)=1` for `x!=y`, `b(e_x,e_x)=0`.  The standard
class-two central extension with commutator form `b` is `K_X`; permutations
of `X` act on it.  Sending the presentation generators to
`K_X semidirect L` respects every relation and sends `J` to its nonidentity
central element.  Hence `J` is nontrivial in `Gamma(L,H)`.
