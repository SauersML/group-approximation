**ESTABLISHED PREFIX-WINDOW FENCE.**  Let `I` be a finite set of distinct
ambient Steinberg coordinates, with `9<=|I|<=20`, and assign to every `i in I`
a finite binary word `u_i`.  Put

```text
S_i=s_(u_i),                 T_i=t_(u_i),
E_ij=x_(i,j)(S_iT_j).                                  (PPW1)
```

Repeated words are allowed.  The subgroup generated by the roots in
`(PPW1)` is a literal copy of `SL_I(F_2)` in
`St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))`.  In particular every finite packet obtained by
adjoining fresh prefix coordinates and using only arrows whose coefficient
is

```text
s_(u_target)t_(u_source)                               (PPW2)
```

for one globally consistent coordinate labelling remains inside a finite
projective head.

This includes all three anchored native cells, both adjacent prefix
Whiteheads, their braid and relative words, the center-chain roots, the
`B_2,B_3` arms, `A_1`, and the marked root after assigning

```text
u_1=u_2=u_3=u_4=u_5=u_6=1,
u_7=0,                    u_8=00,       u_9=000.       (PPW3)
```

In particular the description of `A_1` as an index-`31` "outside actor" in
the finite coset audit is relative only to the smaller packet `P`.  It is
still the ordinary projective arrow from label `0` to label `1`, and lies in
the literal `SL_7(F_2)` head.  It is not an occurrence outside the
projective-prefix language.

It also includes every finite continuation which fits in the available
ambient coordinates, obtained by adding coordinates labelled `0^m` and the
corresponding adjacent prefix arrows.  Such a
continuation is not a nonprojective Leavitt occurrence: it merely enlarges
the finite binary matrix chart.

For every such enlargement to `N=|I|` coordinates, the permutation
representation of the literal `SL_N(F_2)` on the `2^N-1` nonzero vectors
has

```text
rank(P_z)=2^(N-2),       rank(F)=0,
rank(E)=2^(N-4),                                       (PPW4)
```

where `z=x_13(q)`, `F` is the fixed full-Hecke quarter source, and

```text
E=product_(D in {x_42(q),x_52(q),x_62(q)})(1-D)/2.
```

Hence no finite collection of consistently prefix-labelled transports can
force positive `F` mass from a surviving mark or from the marked three-root
star.  The obstruction is representation-theoretic, not a failure to add
enough prefix depth.

Therefore an escape from the source seam must contain a genuinely
label-inconsistent occurrence.  Concretely it must reuse an ambient
coordinate with two incompatible prefix labels, compare sums coming from
different source/target label pairs in one root coordinate (as in
`s_0t_0+s_1t_1=1`), or impose an equivalent object-erasing payload return.
An uncancelled `s_u` or `t_v` is not by itself enough: it is still of the
form `(PPW2)` after adjoining the empty-word coordinate.

This is a scoped finite-window obstruction.  It does not construct a
representation of the full infinite Steinberg group and therefore does not
refute the arbitrary-profile native cross-Gram claim.  It proves that the
requested nonprojective source occurrence cannot be obtained by extending
the existing prefix-labelled chart in the same manner.  No Property `(T)`,
canonical trace, literature theorem, or computation is used.
