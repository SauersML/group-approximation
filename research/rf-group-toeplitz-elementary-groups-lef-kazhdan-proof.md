Artifact part 2, §7, Corollary T.
1. **Finitely generated.** With `U = {x : x(e)=1}`, `u_g 1_U u_g^(-1) = 1_(gU)` gives all one-coordinate cylinders,
   which generate `LC(X_Γ,F_q)`. So `R_Γ` is generated by `u_s^(±1)` (`s` in a finite generating set of `Γ`) and `1_U`.
2. **Matricial and simple.** The action is minimal, topologically free and RF
   (`rf-toeplitz-subshifts-over-residually-finite-groups`), so `residually-finite-actions-give-matricial-crossed-products`
   gives simplicity and the unital injective embedding.
3. **(T), finite generation.** `elementary-groups-over-fg-rings-have-property-t`, `elementary-group-fg-from-fg-coefficient-ring`.
4. **Simple modulo centre.** The transformation groupoid is Hausdorff, ample, minimal and effective (topological
   freeness) with infinite compact unit space: `steinberg-elementary-groups-are-simple-mod-centre`, and
   `Z <= F_q^x I` (`minimal-effective-steinberg-algebras-have-scalar-centre`).
5. **LEF.** `GL_N(R_Γ)` embeds in `∏_ω GL_(N N_n)(F_q)` (`exactly-matricial-rings-have-lef-general-linear-groups`).
   If `g ∈ EL_N(R_Γ)` maps into the scalars, the scalar is `ω`-almost constant because `F_q^x` is finite, and
   injectivity gives `g ∈ F_q^x I`. So `S_(Γ,N)` embeds in `∏_ω GL_(N N_n)(F_q)/F_q^x`, an ultraproduct of finite
   groups; being finitely generated, it is LEF.
