## Statement

Let `P` be countable, `nu : P -> N` a bijection, `K` a field, and `J` the singular ideal of
`A = A_K(G_nu)`, with the parity convention of `shell-cantor-embeddings-are-finite-germ-extensions`.
Then:
1. `K[P] -> A/J`, `g |-> u_g + J`, is injective.
2. `L_K(1,2) -> A/J`, generated by `s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1`, is a unital embedding.
3. `A/J` is central simple (`shell-germ-essential-algebras-are-central-simple`). If `P` is finitely
   generated, `A/J` is finitely generated by `s_0, s_1, t_0, t_1` and the `u_g` with `g ∈ S`.

So every finitely generated group algebra has an explicit, finitely generated, central simple
envelope. That is Bokut's kind of envelope (Theorem 1 of Algebra i Logika 15 (1976); see
`research/artifacts/gq-bh-bh-free-53-algebra-bh-literature-at-source.md`). Here it also comes
with `P` in the units and a unital Leavitt pair.

## Proof

1. `g |-> u_g` is a group homomorphism into `A^x`, so it extends to `K[P] -> A`. Suppose
   `x = Σ_(g ∈ T) c_g u_g ∈ J`, with `T` finite. Fix an even `n`. The regular action is free on
   the even integers, so the shells `C_(rho_g(n))`, `g ∈ T`, are pairwise distinct. Hence the
   germs of the `hat(g)` at a point `y ∈ C_n` are pairwise distinct, and `x` takes the value
   `c_g` on the open bisection `{[hat(g), y'] : y' ∈ C_n}`. Since `supp x` has empty interior,
   every `c_g = 0`.
2. `L_K(1,2)` is simple, so its map to `A/J` is injective, and it is unital.
3. The generation statement is part 1 of `germ-steinberg-algebras-over-v-are-fg-central-simple`.
   The germ class of `[0]` is a word in `s_0, t_0`, and the cylinder indicators come from the
   Leavitt letters. `∎`

## Consequence for Strategy 2 (gate A1)

- **The candidate.** For a finitely generated decidable `P`, `K[P]` embeds in the finitely
  generated central simple `A_nu/J`, and `P <= (A_nu/J)^x`. So gate A1 for `K[P]` holds as soon
  as one `A_nu/J` is finitely presented.
- **The obstruction.** By `generic-shells-have-infinitely-generated-singular-ideals`, this fails
  for generic `nu` whenever `A_nu` is finitely presented. So the enumeration must be
  non-generic, or `A_nu` must itself fail to be finitely presented while its quotient `A_nu/J`
  is finitely presented.
- **Gate A2 (`K`-theory).** For the Leavitt tensor, `KH((A_nu/J) ⊗ L) = 0` by
  `abc-leavitt-kh-vanishing-and-nil-splitting-citation`. The nil part is open.
