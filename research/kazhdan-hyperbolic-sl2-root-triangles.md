**Data.** `q = 2^f ∈ {8, 16}`, `k = F_q ⊂ F = F_{q^2}`, `s ∈ F \ k`. The edge groups
`E_12, E_13, E_23` are copies of `(k, +) ≅ (Z/2)^f`, and the face group is trivial. The vertex groups and
embeddings are:

| vertex | group | first edge ↦ | second edge ↦ |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A_1` | `SL_2(k)` | `E_12 ↦ u(k)` | `E_13 ↦ v(k)` |
| `A_2` | `SL_2(k)` | `E_12 ↦ u(k)` | `E_23 ↦ v(k)` |
| `A_3` | `SL_2(F)` | `E_13 ↦ u(k)` | `E_23 ↦ v(sk)` |

Here `u(a) = [[1,a],[0,1]]` and `v(b) = [[1,0],[b,1]]`. Each embedding may be precomposed with any
automorphism of its edge group; these are the gluing data. Write `Λ` for the colimit.

**THEOREM (established; one step computer-assisted; unreviewed).** For every `s` and every choice of
gluing data:
- **(K1)** The vertex groups `SL_2(8), SL_2(8), SL_2(64)`, respectively `SL_2(16), SL_2(16), SL_2(256)`,
  are finite simple. The edge groups are nontrivial, and each vertex group is generated by its two edge
  groups, so every link is connected.
- **(K2)** The half-girths are `(3, 3, r_3)`, with `r_3 >= 4` proved and `r_3 = 5` computed. So `Λ` is
  infinite and non-elementary hyperbolic. It acts on a CAT(−1) 2-complex with vertex stabilizers
  `A_1, A_2, A_3`, and these inject.
- **(K3)** `Λ` has Kazhdan's property (T).

So every such `Λ` is a hyperbolic Kazhdan member of the class of
`hyperbolic-simple-triangle-group-without-finite-quotients`. A member with no nontrivial finite quotient
would decide that claim and `kazhdan-hyperbolic-group-without-finite-quotients` together. By
`simple-vertex-groups-vtf-iff-some-finite-quotient`, it would also decide Lück's question.

**The numbers.** Order the edge groups `E_12, E_13, E_23`, and let `σ = σ_2(Γ_s)`. Kassabov's matrix is

```text
[[1, -q^{-1/2}, -q^{-1/2}], [-q^{-1/2}, 1, -σ], [-q^{-1/2}, -σ, 1]],   det = (1 - 2/q - σ)(1 + σ).
```

With the Lanczos values of `σ`, the determinant is about `0.57` for `q = 16` and about `0.11` for
`q = 8`. The certificate `σ < 1 - 2/q` alone already makes it positive.

**Why this shape.**
- The two `SL_2(q)` root vertices have the tiny angle `q^{-1/2}`, but only girth 6.
- One subfield-root vertex supplies the girth `>= 8` needed for hyperbolicity.
- The positive-definite criterion then tolerates its angle up to `1 - 2/q`, not just the uniform Żuk
  bound `1/2`.
- For `q = 4` the same link has `σ_2 ≈ 0.844 > 1/2 = 1 - 2/4`, so the method fails there.

**Novelty: not assessed.** Web search was exhausted this session, and no arXiv search was run. Hyperbolic
Kazhdan triangle groups with finite simple vertex groups may already be known.
