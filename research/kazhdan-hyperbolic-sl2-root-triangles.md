**Data.** `q = 2^f >= 8`, `k = F_q ⊂ F = F_{q^2}`, `s ∈ F \ k`. The edge groups `E_12, E_13, E_23` are
copies of `(k, +) ≅ (Z/2)^f`, and the face group is trivial. The vertex groups and embeddings are:

| vertex | group | first edge ↦ | second edge ↦ |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A_1` | `SL_2(k)` | `E_12 ↦ u(k)` | `E_13 ↦ v(k)` |
| `A_2` | `SL_2(k)` | `E_12 ↦ u(k)` | `E_23 ↦ v(k)` |
| `A_3` | `SL_2(F)` | `E_13 ↦ u(k)` | `E_23 ↦ v(sk)` |

Here `u(a) = [[1,a],[0,1]]` and `v(b) = [[1,0],[b,1]]`. Each embedding may be precomposed with any
automorphism of its edge group; these are the gluing data. Write `Λ` for the colimit.

**THEOREM (established; unreviewed).** For every `q = 2^f >= 8`, every `s` and every gluing:
- **(K1)** The vertex groups `SL_2(q), SL_2(q), SL_2(q^2)` are finite simple. The edge groups are
  nontrivial, and each vertex group is generated by its two edge groups, so every link is connected.
- **(K2)** The half-girths are `(3, 3, r_3)` with `r_3 >= 5` (exactly `5` where computed). So `Λ` is
  infinite and non-elementary hyperbolic. It acts on a CAT(−1) 2-complex with vertex stabilizers
  `A_1, A_2, A_3`, and these inject.
- **(K3)** `Λ` has Kazhdan's property (T).

How the cases are proved:
- For `q >= 32` every step is by hand.
- For `q = 8, 16` one step, the link bound at `A_3`, is an exact integer computation.

So every such `Λ` is a hyperbolic Kazhdan member of the class of
`hyperbolic-simple-triangle-group-without-finite-quotients`. A member with no nontrivial finite quotient
would decide that claim and `kazhdan-hyperbolic-group-without-finite-quotients` together. By
`simple-vertex-groups-vtf-iff-some-finite-quotient`, it would also answer Lück's question negatively.

**The numbers.** Order the edge groups `E_12, E_13, E_23`, and let `σ = σ_2(Γ_s)`. Kassabov's matrix is

```text
[[1, -q^{-1/2}, -q^{-1/2}], [-q^{-1/2}, 1, -σ], [-q^{-1/2}, -σ, 1]],   det = (1 - 2/q - σ)(1 + σ).
```

It is positive definite as soon as `σ < 1 - 2/q`.
- The Lanczos values are `σ ≈ 0.683` for `q = 8` and `≈ 0.496` for `q = 16`, so the determinant is about
  `0.11` and `0.57`.
- For `q >= 32` the proven bound `σ <= (14q/(q^2-1))^{1/8}` suffices.

**Why this shape.**
- The two `SL_2(q)` root vertices have the tiny angle `q^{-1/2}`, but only girth 6.
- One subfield-root vertex supplies girth `>= 10`, which gives hyperbolicity.
- The positive-definite criterion then tolerates its angle up to `1 - 2/q`, not just the uniform Żuk
  bound `1/2`.
- For `q = 4` the method fails: the `A_3` link has `σ ≈ 0.844 > 1/2 = 1 - 2/4`.

**Literature check (bounded).** On 2026-09-13, five arXiv abstract searches from MSI were run: triangle
of groups with property (T), triangles of groups Kazhdan, hyperbolic Kazhdan finite simple vertex,
generalized triangle groups with property (T), representation angle hyperbolic.
- They returned Caprace–Conder–Kaluba–Witzel (arXiv:2011.09276) and arXiv:1708.09772 (a sixteen-relator
  hyperbolic Kazhdan group).
- Neither of those, nor anything else found, has finite simple vertex groups. The two papers were not
  read for this purpose.

Novelty is **not** established. Web search was unavailable.
