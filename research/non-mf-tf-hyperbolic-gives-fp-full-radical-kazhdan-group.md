**ESTABLISHED** through `non-mf-tf-hyperbolic-gives-fp-full-radical-kazhdan-group-proof`.

Let `P` be any torsion-free non-elementary word-hyperbolic group. Consider:

- **(a)** some torsion-free word-hyperbolic group is not MF;
- **(b)** for every finite `M ⊂ P` there are a torsion-free non-elementary word-hyperbolic group `Q`
  and an epimorphism `π : P → Q`, injective on `M`, such that
  - `Rad_MF(Q) = Q`, that is, `Q` has no nontrivial homomorphism to any MF group;
  - `Q = ⟨q, s q s⁻¹⟩` for some `q, s ∈ Q`, so `Q` is 2-generated and normally generated by the
    single element `q`;
- **(c)** `P` has a nontrivial torsion-free word-hyperbolic quotient `Q` with `Rad_MF(Q) = Q`.

## Theorem (NMH)

1. **(Fixed-host equivalence.)** (a), (b) and (c) are equivalent.
2. **(Kazhdan host.)** Take `P` finitely presented, torsion-free, non-elementary, hyperbolic and
   Kazhdan (`torsion-free-hyperbolic-kazhdan-partner-exists`). Then every `Q` in (b) is also
   - infinite, finitely presented and Kazhdan;
   - without nontrivial finite quotients, hence not residually finite.
3. **(Product witness up to soficity.)** For such `Q`, the pair `(Q × Z, Q × 0)` satisfies every
   clause of `torsion-free-sofic-exact-mf-radical-over-z` and of its preferred strengthening except
   soficity:
   - `Q × Z` is finitely presented and torsion-free;
   - `Q × 0` is nontrivial, Kazhdan, finitely presented, and normally generated by one element;
   - `Rad_MF(Q × Z) = Q × 0` and `(Q × Z)/(Q × 0) ≅ Z`.

   By part 2 of `finite-outer-order-radical-witnesses-cross-alekseev-thom`, it is a witness
   **exactly when `Q` is sofic**. The witness then lies in region (AT).
4. **(Trichotomy.)** At least one of the following holds:
   - **(T1)** every torsion-free word-hyperbolic group is MF;
   - **(T2)** some torsion-free word-hyperbolic Kazhdan group is not sofic, which settles
     `nonsofic-hyperbolic-group`;
   - **(T3)** the flagship holds, and so does its preferred strengthening, with a witness `Q × Z`
     whose kernel is word-hyperbolic.

   (T3) with a hyperbolic kernel also settles:
   - `fp-sofic-kazhdan-group-not-residually-finite` (Alekseev--Thom 6.1);
   - `torsion-free-non-residually-finite-hyperbolic-group`.

## What this changes

- **Refuting the flagship is at least as hard as a hyperbolic dichotomy.** A proof that no witness
  exists, even with `K` finitely presented and normally generated by one element, forces (T1) or
  (T2). So it would show that every torsion-free hyperbolic group is MF, or produce a nonsofic
  hyperbolic group.
- **The sofic half is a question about hyperbolic groups only.** For the unconditional
  full-radical group `D` of `defect-saturation-full-mf-radical`, soficity is unknown and no
  conjecture predicts it. Here the kernel is word-hyperbolic, so the open question "is every
  hyperbolic group sofic?" (Gromov) covers it. A positive answer to that question would give the
  flagship from (a) alone.
- **No Rips step and no soficity of all hyperbolic groups.** Only the one group `Q` must be sofic.
  The kernel is finitely presented, so no automorphism and no automorphic presentation is needed.
- **The candidate kernels can be taken inside one fixed linear lattice's quotients.** Take `P` a
  torsion-free cocompact lattice in `Sp(2,1)`. Once (a) holds, candidate kernels `Q` exist among
  the quotients of `P`, injective on any prescribed ball. Taking `M` to be the balls of `P`, the
  residually finite group `P` is a marked limit of its own quotients with full MF radical. This
  does not say that every sofic full-radical hyperbolic group is a quotient of `P`.

## Not claimed

- **The converse of part 4.** The proof does not show that a witness with a word-hyperbolic kernel
  `K` must have `Rad_MF(K) = K`. That would follow from finiteness of `Out(K)` for hyperbolic
  Kazhdan `K` (Paulin's R-tree theorem plus fixed points of Kazhdan groups on R-trees) and part 1
  of `finite-outer-order-radical-witnesses-cross-alekseev-thom`. That theorem is not imported
  verbatim in the graph, so the converse is left as a remark.
- **(T1) is not claimed to be equivalent to residual finiteness.** The implication "non-RF gives
  non-MF" for hyperbolic groups is open (`hyperbolic-rf-question-equals-non-mf-question`).
