Citation import, not a reproof. Kevin Li and Luis Jorge Sánchez Saldaña,
*Contractible Rips complexes of groups via metric gluings*, arXiv:2608.24279
(v1, 25 August 2026).

- Abstract: "We show that the class of groups of type ℛ is closed under
  taking graphs of groups with finite edge groups and, in particular, under
  taking free products."
- Theorem 1.3 (amalgamated products), as rendered in v1: for `A *_C B` with
  `C` finite, if `(A,S_A)` and `(B,S_B)` are of type ℛ with generating sets
  containing all non-trivial elements of `C`, then "(A∗_CB,S_A∪S_B) is of
  type ℛ."
- Theorem 1.4 (HNN extensions): for `A *_{C,φ}` with `C` finite, if `(A,S_A)`
  is of type ℛ and `S_A` contains the non-trivial elements of `C` and of
  `φ(C)`, then "(A∗_{C,φ},S_A∪{t}) is of type ℛ."
- The authors leave open whether a finite `C` can be replaced by an edge
  group `(C,S_C)` of type ℛ.

Read from the arXiv abstract page and the HTML rendering of v1 on 2026-09-13
(the generating-set hypotheses were read from the rendering, not from the
PDF). The paper is an unrefereed preprint, and the proof was not re-derived
here.
