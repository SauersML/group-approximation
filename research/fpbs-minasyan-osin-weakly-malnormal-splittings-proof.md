> `\begin{cor}\label{cor:amalg-intr}` Let `$G$` split as a free product of groups `$A$` and `$B$` with an amalgamated subgroup `$C$`. Suppose `$A\ne C\ne B$` and `$C$` is weakly malnormal in `$G$`. Then `$G$` is either virtually cyclic or acylindrically hyperbolic. `\end{cor}`

> `\begin{cor}\label{cor:HNN-intr}` Let `$G$` be an HNN-extension of a group `$A$` with associated subgroups `$C$` and `$D$`. Suppose that `$C\ne A\ne D$` and `$C$` is weakly malnormal in `$G$`. Then `$G$` is acylindrically hyperbolic. `\end{cor}`
