`rank(Gamma)>=(tr Gamma)^2/tr(Gamma^2)` for any positive semidefinite
`Gamma`, with `tr Gamma=N` and off-diagonal entries bounded by `epsilon`.
For sharpness, normalized `+-1` codewords of a binary code of relative
distance `(1-epsilon)/2` have pairwise inner products of modulus at most
`epsilon`, and such codes have exponentially many words in the length.
