# Compare the sibling partial swaps before and after closing their paths

Prefix cancellation proves `(DLB2)`.  The standard three-elementary
factorization proves `(DLB3)--(DLB4)`.  Equality of the forward D16 blocks
then identifies two orthogonal child projections and kills them.  Closing
each path first gives `(DLB6)`, so both D16 blocks are the source identity
and the charge vanishes.  These exhaust the forward and same-source return
interpretations of the two native depth-one branches.
