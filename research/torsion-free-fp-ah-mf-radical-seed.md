**OPEN INTRINSIC SEED.**  Construct a finitely presented, torsion-free,
acylindrically hyperbolic group `E` and a nonidentity element `d in E` such
that

```text
d in Rad_MF(E).                                         (TFS1)
```

The construction and proof of `(TFS1)` may not invoke Property `(T)`, a
Kazhdan subgroup, a Kazhdan projection, Kazhdan transport, or a previously
proved non-MF seed whose proof has such a dependency.

Higman's four-generator torsion-free group is a concrete first candidate:
all of its finite-dimensional unitary representations are trivial.  To use
it here one still needs a point-norm theorem at one generator (or full
matricial stability); exact finite-dimensional sterility alone does not imply
`(TFS1)`.  The four Baumslag--Solitar occurrence seams are the currently
identified stability obstruction.
