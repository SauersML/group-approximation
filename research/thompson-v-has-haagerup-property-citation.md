Farley (IMRN 2003, no. 45, 2409--2414) constructs proper actions of Thompson's
groups `F`, `T` and `V` by isometries on CAT(0) cube complexes. Proper actions on
CAT(0) cube complexes give proper affine isometric actions on Hilbert space
(Niblo--Reeves), i.e. the Haagerup property.

The two consequences in the claim are textbook (Cherix--Cowling--Jolissaint--
Julg--Valette 2001). Subgroups inherit a proper affine action by restriction.
A group with property (T) has a fixed point in every affine isometric action,
so a proper one forces it to be finite.
