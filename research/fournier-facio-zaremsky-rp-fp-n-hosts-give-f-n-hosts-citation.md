Literature import, not a reproof. The statement of Theorem A, Lemma 2.1 and
the outline of the proof in Section 2 were read from the arXiv HTML rendering
of arXiv:2607.21727v1 on 2026-09-13. The theorem is quoted verbatim in the
target claim. The ingredients are Higman's embedding theorem (1961), the
existence of a universal finitely presented group, the permanence of `FP_n`
under ascending HNN extensions (Bieri, *Homological dimension of discrete
groups*, 1981) and Lemma 2.1. Each step as recorded in the target is
elementary given those ingredients.
