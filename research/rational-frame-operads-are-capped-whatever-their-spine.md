## Setting

A **rational-frame dissection operad** is a dissection operad in the sense of
`symmetric-ore-operads-containing-their-carrier-are-full-groups` with the following structure.
- **Model tiles.** Every model tile `Y_c` is a clopen subset of `A^N` for one finite alphabet `A`.
- **Operations.** Each generating operation partitions `Y_c` into clopens `P_1, …, P_k`, together
  with homeomorphisms `φ_i : P_i → Y_{c_i}`. Each `φ_i` is **rational**: it is the restriction of a
  map defined by an asynchronous finite-state transducer.
- **Transformations** are rational as well.
- **The object.** `X` is identified with a clopen partition of `A^N` by rational maps.

No Ore condition, finite spine, propagation bound or square shape is assumed.

## Statement

1. **Rational groups.** The group generated by the fractions `e∘d^{-1}` acts faithfully on `A^N`
   by rational homeomorphisms, extended by the identity off `X`.
   - So every finitely generated subgroup has word problem in time `2^{O(n)}`, with the constant
     depending on the transducers.
   - The whole class is not universal, by `complexity-bounded-host-classes-are-not-universal` with
     `T(l) = 2^l`.
   - This includes the full groups `[[Λ]]` that such operads present.
2. **Refinement growth is not the cap invariant.** Branching squares, a split-refinement function
   `ρ` of any size, and an Ore condition that is a hard global theorem are all compatible with
   Statement 1. So rung R-b alone (Ore as a halting theorem, `ρ` of the order of running times)
   cannot refute CAP. The invariant is the complexity of the **frames**, that is, of the carrier's
   action on cut shapes.
   - A machine operad must admit no rational-frame realization.
   - An abstract operad must admit no coding of its boundary `∂X` in which the generators act
     rationally.
3. **Ore encodes mortality.** Ore quantifies over every pair of operations with the same output.
   Suppose the reconciliation of pairs of operations runs a machine `M`, with configurations read
   off the pair. Then:
   - Every configuration that some pair encodes is an input, whether or not it is reachable from
     an initial configuration.
   - If every configuration is encoded, Ore holds only if `M` halts from **every** configuration,
     that is, only if `M` is mortal. Totality on initial configurations is not enough.
   - Mortal machines exist for every decidable set (`every-decidable-set-has-a-mortal-decider`).
     So the family `𝒪_M` should be indexed by mortal deciders.
   - If the encoding is also faithful (a halting run gives a common multiple of degree about the
     running time), then Ore for `𝒪_M` is equivalent to mortality of `M`.
     - Immortality is undecidable (Hooper 1966, recalled, not re-read).
     - Ore would then be undecidable for finite branching-square presentations, in contrast with
       unary squares, where it is automatic.
   - The encoding is not constructed here.

## Proof of 1

- **Frames of deep pieces.** A piece at depth `d` of an expansion is identified with its model
  tile by a composite of `d` generator frames and transformations. Composites of rational maps
  are rational.
- **Fractions.** A fraction `e∘d^{-1}` agrees on each `d`-piece `P` with
  `frame_e(P)^{-1} ∘ frame_d(P)`. Inverses of rational homeomorphisms are rational
  (Grigorchuk–Nekrashevych–Sushchanskii; recalled).
- **Patching.** A map that is rational on each piece of a finite clopen partition is rational: a
  transducer first reads the prefix that determines the piece. So each fraction, and each product
  of fractions, is a rational homeomorphism of `A^N`.
- **Faithfulness.** Faithfulness is by construction, since the group is defined by its action.
- **The time bound.** `rational-homeomorphism-subgroups-have-exponential-wp` gives it. It composes
  transducers and never computes a common refinement. ∎

## Instances

- **Classical rational-frame operads.** Higman–Thompson `V_{n,r}` and Brin–Thompson `nV`: their
  frames are prefix maps, and coordinates can be interleaved into one sequence with bounded buffering
  (recalled, not re-read).
- **`𝒯_1`.** The rational projective host of the circle, in its Stern–Brocot coding. Möbius maps
  in `PGL_2(Z)` act on Stern–Brocot codes by finite transducers (Raney; recalled, not re-read).
- **Not covered.**
  - `𝒯_m` for `m ≥ 2`: rationality of `GL_{m+1}(Z)` on an edge-split coding of `Ŝ_m` is not
    known here. Its word problem is presumably controlled by integer linear algebra instead.
  - Operads whose frames are defined by unbounded computation. These are the only
    machine-operad candidates.

## Design constraints for the combined rungs R-b + R-c

1. **Squares.** Branching squares
   (`branching-squares-are-the-only-operadic-escape-from-the-cap`).
2. **Machine.** A mortal machine, since Ore sees rogue configurations. This is the same lesson the
   seed tower drew for forced marks at terminal configurations (bh-invent-03 plan; mortal
   deciders).
3. **Frames.** Non-rational frames: the cut shapes carried into a piece must be transformed by a
   map that is not finite-state. Otherwise Statement 1 caps the host whatever the machine does.
4. **Cancellativity.** Once frames are non-rational, a geometric realization is still possible
   in principle, and it gives cancellativity for free. But the frames are then themselves the hard
   carrier (R-c), and the frame maps must be finitely many homeomorphisms whose composites carry
   the computation.

Constraint 4 says R-b and R-c cannot be separated: an operad escapes the cap only when its frames
are a hard carrier. That is the carrier principle in operadic form, and it matches the seed side.
- There, forcing length is not the cap invariant;
  `baumslag-gersten-seed-forcing-length-is-non-elementary` is the example.
- There, evaluation complexity is bounded by the forcing radius
  (`seed-evaluation-is-bounded-by-the-forcing-radius`).

## Lesson for general BH

**Refinement growth is not the cap invariant; frame complexity is.** A dissection operad whose
pieces are identified by finite-state maps generates a group of rational homeomorphisms. So its
word problem is single-exponential however hard its Ore condition is, and however large its
common refinements get.

A machine can make Ore a halting theorem, but that alone never breaks the cap. It must be a
mortal machine, because Ore sees every configuration. Breaking the cap also needs frames that
compute, and those frames are the hard carrier.

So the operadic ¬CAP question is the carrier question again. Is there a finite set of
homeomorphisms, the frames, whose composites carry an arbitrary decidable computation while the
operad they define stays cancellative, Ore and finitely presented? This is exactly the seed
side's question with "frames" in place of "forced configuration".
