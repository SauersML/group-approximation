Use the ordered binary summands

```text
q@2, q@4, q@5, q@6, e_1@7, e_2@8, e_3@9, e_3@2
```

and abbreviate them by `q2,q4,q5,q6,u1,u2,u3,u4`.  The source factor
`A_1=x_47(s_1t_0)` restricts to the single transvection

```text
a=x_(u1,q4)(1).                                        (1)
```

This is the same literal source-normalizer occurrence used in the two
common Hecke tails and in the paid sheet detector.  Faithfulness of the
binary matrix action shows that adding `(1)` to the sixteen generators of
`P` produces

```text
|<P,a>|=20478689280=31|P|.                             (2)
```

The order computation is a Schreier--Sims calculation in the faithful
permutation action on the `255` nonzero vectors of `F_2^8`.  The independent
left-`K` coset enumeration below gives the same order, so `(2)` is not used
as an unsupported classification identification.

Retain the canonical pattern-row normal form and the signed character
propagation from the separate-actor calculation.  Breadth-first right
multiplication by the twelve pattern roots, `J_1,J_2,B_2,B_3`, and `a`
gives exactly `624960` left `K`-cosets.  Right multiplication by the twelve
pattern roots then decomposes them into `14528` double cosets.  Propagating
the `chi` sign around each right orbit leaves `176` compatible orbits; the
other `14352` are inconsistent.  The compatible orbits contain `2256`
left cosets in total and each has at most `16`, which makes the complete
signed multiplication replay small.

For every compatible orbit `D`, let `c_D` be its signed orbit sum.  These
have disjoint group-basis support and form an integral basis of

```text
B=F C[P_A] F.                                          (3)
```

There are `176` of them.  Multiply every ordered pair of signed orbit sums,
reduce each group term by the exact pattern normal form, and collect it in
the basis `(3)`.  This gives integral structure constants.  The orbit of
`g=J_1J_2` is basis component `4`, while the identity is component `0`.

Starting from the `g` component, close under left and right multiplication
by all `176` basis elements, doing row reduction modulo `1000003`.  The
echelon rank is

```text
176.                                                    (4)
```

The vectors being reduced are reductions of integral coefficient vectors.
Rank `176` in `(4)` exhibits a square integral minor whose determinant is
nonzero modulo `1000003`.  That determinant is therefore nonzero over
`Z`, so the same ideal has rank at least `176` over `Q`; it cannot exceed
`dim_Q(B)=176`.  Thus the rational and complex ideal generated by `FgF` is
all of `B`, proving `(A1E6)`.

For a finite group algebra, semisimplicity identifies two-sided ideals with
sums of simple blocks.  Therefore `(A1E6)` is exactly the assertion that
every simple `P_A`-block meeting `F` also meets `FgF`.  Restricting such a
block to `P` shows that none of the old zero-cross blocks can extend with
the canonical action `(1)`.
