If some cloning system on finite groups has a Thompson-like group `T(G_*)` (or a
finitely generated subgroup of it) that is not coCF, then Problem 2.16 is
answered: Q1 (coCF) is no, and Q2 (embed in `V`) is no as well, because every
finitely generated subgroup of `V` is coCF. This route does not bear on Lehnert's
conjecture. Open: `some-finite-cloning-system-group-is-not-locally-cocf`.
