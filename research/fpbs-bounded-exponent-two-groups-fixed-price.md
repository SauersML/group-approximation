title: Every countably infinite bounded-exponent 2-group has fixed price one
root: true

Corollary 8.1. For every r>=1, all free pmp actions of every countably infinite G satisfying g^(2^r)=e have cost one. No assertion is made for 2-groups with unbounded element orders.
