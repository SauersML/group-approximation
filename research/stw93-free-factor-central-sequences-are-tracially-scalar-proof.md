Kirchberg--Rørdam central surjectivity identifies the displayed quotient with
`L(F₂)^ω∩L(F₂)'`.  The free group factor is full, so this relative
commutant is `ℂ`.  The embedding is induced by `[(b_n)]↦[(b_n⊗1)]` and
preserves both the norm and the tracial `2`-norm.  Therefore subtracting the
scalar image `τ_ω(x)1` puts the result in `J_τ∩F(B⊗𝒵)`.
