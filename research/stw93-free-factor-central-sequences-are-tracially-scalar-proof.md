The canonical map from `F(B)` to `L(F₂)^ω∩L(F₂)'` has kernel
`J_τ∩F(B)`.  The free group factor is full, so the target is `ℂ`; the
image contains the constant scalars, and hence is all of `ℂ`.  The displayed
quotient is therefore `ℂ` directly, without any central-surjectivity input.
The embedding into `F(B⊗𝒵)` is induced by
`[(b_n)]↦[(b_n⊗1)]` and preserves both the norm and the tracial `2`-norm.
Therefore subtracting the scalar image `τ_ω(x)1` puts the result in
`J_τ∩F(B⊗𝒵)`.
