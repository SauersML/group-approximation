For the tagged seam of two infinite minimal binary subshifts over
`F_2`, its ring `R` satisfies `Rad_MF(R^x)=GL_fin(Z,F_2)` and, for
every `n>=2`, `Rad_MF(EL_n(R))=GL_fin(Z x {1,...,n},F_2)`.
The maximal MF quotient of the unit group is the image of endpoint
restriction; no surjectivity for units is claimed.

At rank three the maximal MF quotient is precisely the product of
the original simple groups `EL_3(R_-) x EL_3(R_+)`. The ambient group
is Kazhdan with four explicit torsion generators of orders `7,2,2,2`,
has exactly five normal subgroups and no nontrivial finite quotients;
every proper quotient is LEF.
