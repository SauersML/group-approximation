The ring is generated by the shift unit, its inverse, and the letter-one
idempotent: multiply translates of that idempotent and its complement
to obtain cylinder functions. Apply the algebraic decoder theorem.
Both letters occur, so the finite coefficient algebra is `F_2 x F_2`
and the first two generators give its full elementary group. The
universal property of the free product supplies the asserted epimorphism.
The existing subshift theorem supplies the group properties; its scalar
centre is trivial in characteristic two. Section 4 gives the details.
