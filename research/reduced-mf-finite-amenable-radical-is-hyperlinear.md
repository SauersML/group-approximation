ESTABLISHED.  Let `G` be countable with `C*_r(G)` MF and with finite amenable
radical `R_a(G)`.  Then `G` is hyperlinear.

In particular trivial amenable radical is not needed: finite amenable radicals
(central involutions, finite normal Clifford cores) are allowed.  So **any
nonsofic group with MF reduced C-star algebra and finite amenable radical is a
hyperlinear nonsofic group** and answers Pestov's Question 3.4 negatively.
