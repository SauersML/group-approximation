Christensen--Sinclair--Smith--White--Winter, Corollary 4.6 in
[Perturbations of nuclear C*-algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.4953),
states that for every separable strongly self-absorbing `D`, the D-stable
members of the space of separable subalgebras of a fixed unital `C`
containing `1_C` form a closed subset.  The Jiang--Su algebra Z is separable
and strongly self-absorbing, so taking `D=Z` gives the claim.
