Let `R` be a countable unital ring with `s,t` satisfying `ts = 1` and
`R(1-st)R = R`.  Then for every `n >= 2`, every homomorphism from
`EL_n(R)` to an MF group is trivial, so

```text
Rad_MF(EL_n(R)) = EL_n(R).
```

Property (T) of `EL_2(R)` is not assumed and is not used; the Kazhdan input
stays inside the rank-four theorem.

The rank-four calculation may be run on the finitely generated unital
subring generated by `v_0,v_1,w_0,w_1`, where the fullness witness is the
single element `w_1(1-v_0w_0)v_1 = 1`.  Its image under the corner
embedding still contains the normal generator of `EL_2(R)`, so the finite
subring exhaustion at the end of the printed ring proof becomes unnecessary.

**Formalization note.**  The rank recorded in the manuscript's Lean markers
is four; those markers do not transfer to this statement.
