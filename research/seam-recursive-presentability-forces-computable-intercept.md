For every allowed intercept beta, computability of beta, decidability
of WP(H_beta), recursive presentability of H_beta, computable
enumerability of its identity words, and computable enumerability of
its nonidentity words in the fixed marking are equivalent.
This allows arbitrary recursively enumerable presentations, not only a
chosen presentation, and asserts no uniform recovery of marking translations.
For noncomputable beta the word problem is neither c.e. nor co-c.e.
