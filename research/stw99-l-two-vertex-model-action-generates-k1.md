Let `B = C*(E(1,2))` with vertex projections `p, q`, let `T_1, T_2` be
its two five-entry rows, and put `V = T_1 T_2^*`.  Let `gamma_z` multiply
the single edge `a_{12}^{(1)}` by `z^{-1}` and fix everything else.  Then

```text
gamma_z(V) V^* = 1 + (z^{-1} - 1) s_{a_{12}^{(1)}} s_{a_{12}^{(1)}}^*,
Delta_gamma([V]) = -[q] = e_B,
```

`v = [V]` GENERATES `K_1(B) = Z`, the `K_0` component of
`Delta_gamma` is zero, and `Delta_gamma : K_1(B) -> K_0(B)` is the
isomorphism carrying `v` to `e_B`.
