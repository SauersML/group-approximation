There are a unital nuclear-dimension-zero embedding

```text
phi:Z->O_2
```

such that every nonzero positive element of `phi(Z)` is properly infinite in
`O_2`, but `phi` does not satisfy
`stw77-nucdim-zero-projection-return`.

This refutes only the proposed selection hinge, not STW LXXVII: the map is
itself O2-stable and hence O-infinity-stable.
