Citation import. M. Hagen, J. Russell, A. Sisto, D. Spriano, *Equivariant hierarchically
hyperbolic structures for 3-manifold groups via quasimorphisms*, arXiv:2206.12244v2
(11 Aug 2023); Ann. Inst. Fourier 75 (2025), no. 2, 769–828. Read from the arXiv PDF on
2026-09-17 (pp. 1–3 and Section 3).

- **Theorem 1** (= Theorem 3.3), verbatim: "Let M be a closed oriented 3–manifold. Then
  π1M is a hierarchically hyperbolic group if and only if M has no Nil, Sol, or
  non-octahedral flat manifolds in its prime decomposition."
- p. 3, verbatim: "This says that the flat pieces are quotients of E3 by crystallographic
  groups with point group conjugate into O3(Z)". Also: "For crystallographic groups in any
  dimension, being octahedral is equivalent to cocompact cubulation [Hag14]. Petyt–Spriano
  showed that this is in turn equivalent to being an HHG [PS23]." And: "many
  crystallographic groups, such as the (3, 3, 3)–triangle group, are not HHGs."
- Proof of Theorem 3.3 (outline, as read): the geometric cases S³, S²×R, H³ give
  hyperbolic groups; the H²×R and PSL₂(R)~ cases are central extensions of Z by a
  hyperbolic surface group (their Corollary 4.3); the non-geometric case is hyperbolic
  relative to Z² and non-geometric graph manifold groups; then [BHS19, Theorem 9.1] handles
  relative hyperbolicity and prime decompositions.

The proofs are not re-derived here.
