**OPEN.** Let `Gamma < G` be the Kun--Thom Theorem E pair. For every trace-preserving
`sigma : G -> U(prod_U M_n)` satisfying (H1) of `hs-stable-vertex-rounding-for-every-model`,
the algebra `Q = sigma(G)' cap prod_U M_n` has a maximal abelian subalgebra of the form
`prod_U C_n`, where each `C_n` is spanned by a finite partition of unity of `M_n` by projections.

With (H1) for every model, this gives `kt-centralizer-normalization-hs` through
`hs-normalization-via-vertex-rounding-and-liftable-masa`. It is the localization face (L) of
property (T) in the Hilbert--Schmidt row
(`research/artifacts/unified-compression-rigidity-2026-09-12.md`, Section 3). Its Hamming
analogue is Kun--Thom Lemma 2.3 for `G`: the `sigma(G)`-fixed diagonal algebra is an
ultraproduct of partition algebras of expander components.

## Attempts

- **Genuine coordinates.** Holds for ultraproducts of genuine representations of `G`: minimal
  projections of the exact commutants give the partitions, and ultraproducts of masas of
  finite-dimensional algebras are masas.
- **Separable pieces lift.** Every separable abelian subalgebra of `Q` is generated by one
  self-adjoint `h`. Spectral projections of lifts of `h` on a grid with random offset and slowly
  shrinking mesh have vanishing summed boundary. So `h` lies in some liftable abelian subalgebra
  of `Q`. This does not give maximality, which is the whole content.
- **Maximal liftable algebras.** If a liftable abelian `C <= Q` is not maximal abelian in `Q`,
  refining the blocks by spectral projections of a block-diagonal lift of a witness gives a
  strictly larger liftable abelian subalgebra. Zorn's lemma does not apply: an uncountable chain
  of liftable algebras need not have a liftable upper bound, and countable saturation only
  handles countable chains.
- **Property (T) of `G` at the ultraproduct level.** Gives the scalar gap on `C' cap M` once a
  masa is given (`hs-block-gap-iff-liftable-masa-of-actor-commutant`, Section 1 of its artifact),
  but supplies no masa. Section 4 of `research/artifacts/hs-coarse-gap-aspect-scales-2026-09-12.md`
  records why: the cascade observables are already invariant in the ultraproduct.
