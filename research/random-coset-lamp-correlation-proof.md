Conjugation in `(RCL2)` sends the lamp at `x` to the lamp at `gx`.  Distinct
coordinate lamps form the standard basis of the elementary abelian lamp
group, so their product is nonidentity unless the coordinates coincide.
The canonical group trace therefore gives `(RCL3)`.  Integrating this bounded
measurable coefficient proves `(RCL5)`.

Each `U_g` is the same acting-group element in every wreath factor, so its
canonical coefficient is zero off the identity, proving `(RCL6)`.  Expanding
`V_gV_h` leaves alternating translated lamps, whereas `V_(gh)` contains only
the base lamp and its `gh`-translate; choosing a subgroup/coset action where
the corresponding coordinates are distinct gives inequality.  Hence the
assignment `(RCL7)` is not multiplicative and cannot be the GNS
representation of `phi_mu`.
