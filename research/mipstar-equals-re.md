**Established by citation.**  Ji, Natarajan, Vidick, Wright and Yuen,
`MIP* = RE` (arXiv:2001.04383).  Their Corollary 12.10:

```text
MIP* = RE.
```

More precisely (their Theorem 12.9) there is a polynomial-time computable map
from Turing machines `M` to two-player one-round nonlocal games `G_M` such that

- if `M` halts on the empty input then `val*(G_M) = 1`, and moreover the value
  is attained by a finite-dimensional projective, consistent and commuting
  strategy;
- if `M` does not halt then `val*(G_M) <= 1/2`.

Consequently, for every `0 < eps < 1` it is undecidable to determine whether a
given nonlocal game has entangled value `1` or at most `1 - eps`, promised one
is the case.

**The consequences this repository actually uses.**  Undecidability of the
entangled value forces `C_qa != C_qc`, a negative answer to Tsirelson's problem,
and hence -- through Fritz and Junge et al., promoted to an equivalence by
Ozawa -- the failure of Connes' Embedding Conjecture.  Every node in this graph
that begins "by `MIP*=RE`" is consuming this theorem, including
`mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists`, `perfect-zpc-irs-quantum-gap-game` and
`non-ce-bcs-has-finite-dimensional-forbidden-mass-gap`.

**What it does not give, in the authors' own words.** Their arXiv v3
Section 1.4 asks whether the counterexample "can eventually lead to a
construction of a group that is not sofic, or even not hyperlinear". That
question is the goal `non-hyperlinear-group` of this program.

The obstruction is an exact trace mismatch. For a countable discrete group
`G`, hyperlinearity asks for matrix microstates for the canonical regular
character

```text
delta_e(g)=1 if g=e, and 0 otherwise,
```

equivalently for a trace-preserving embedding of `(L(G),delta_e)` into
`R^U`. `MIP*=RE` implies the existence of a non-Connes-embeddable
tracial von Neumann algebra, but it does not identify such an algebra with a
group von Neumann algebra carrying this regular trace.

It is therefore misleading to summarize the gap as "an algebra but not a
group". Every separable tracial von Neumann algebra is generated by a
countable family of unitaries and hence is the GNS closure of a character
`chi` on a countable free group. The free group is sofic and hyperlinear,
so the existence of a non-CE character on a group does not imply that the
group is nonhyperlinear. Factoring by the group kernel of the GNS
representation makes the induced representation faithful, but still does
not make `chi=delta_e`: faithfulness only says that a nonidentity group
element does not act as the identity, whereas regularity requires its trace
to be zero.

Thus the missing theorem is trace selection, converting a bad arbitrary
character into a bad canonical regular character. The fixed sofic RACG
countercheck is [[non-ce-trace-on-sofic-racg]]. A quotient presentation adds
no free permanence step: unrestricted quotient permanence for
hyperlinearity is equivalent to universal hyperlinearity by
[[hyperlinear-quotient-permanence-equivalence]]. Consequently this theorem
does not establish `non-hyperlinear-group`.
