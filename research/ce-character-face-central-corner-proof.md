The set of CE traces on a separable group C-star algebra is weak-star closed:
a weak-star limit embeds by the diagonal map into a tracial ultraproduct of
the CE GNS algebras, hence again into an ultrapower of the hyperfinite
factor.  It is therefore compact.  Apply the same finite-intersection
argument as in the finite-dimensional face theorem to obtain `F`.

Normality of the graph of `chi` gives phase survival in `Q_F`.  If `Q_F`
were hyperlinear, its group von Neumann algebra and every positive central
corner would be CE.  On the defining `mu_q` Fourier corner, each relation in
`(CEF3)` becomes exactly `(CEF4)`.  Pullback therefore supplies a CE trace in
the forbidden finite face, proving the contradiction.
