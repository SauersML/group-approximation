Take `G = U = L_(F_2)(1,2)^x`, which is countably infinite, and `p = 2`. The first prerequisite supplies `n`,
the finite subgroups `F_i` and the modules `Q_i`. Theorem E of the second prerequisite gives an isomorphism
between the Bernoulli shifts over `U` with base entropies `n log 2` and `n log 2 + rho log 2`, where
`rho = sum_i dim(Q_i)/|F_i| > 0`. These entropies differ.
