title: Uniqueness separates a theta-squared atom from a uniformly bounded spectral remainder
---

Theorem 4.1. At uniqueness, a_n(p)=theta(p)^2+integral_[0,rho] t^n d nu_p, with total remainder mass 1-theta(p)^2. For general p, a_n+D_n=theta^2+f_n+b_n. These are walk-sampling measures, not spectra of the connectivity convolution operator.
