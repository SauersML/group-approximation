The conclusion follows by reading the three canonical nodes involved.

1. `stw83-two-non-k-extension-directions-remain-open` explicitly says that no
   nuclear-dimension-one proof for either minimal model is recorded and that
   the remaining circle-by-circle question is whether a third colour can be
   reused.  Thus its proposition records a missing proof rather than supplying
   the missing two-colour approximation.
2. `stw83-problem-lxxxiii-graph-nuclear-dimension` asks for nuclear dimension
   at most one for every graph C*-algebra, a strictly broader conclusion than
   either named special case.
3. The body of `stw83-two-non-k-extension-directions-route` says that a
   solution *should* first prove an extension theorem and *then* test a
   permanence argument.  Those are proposed proof obligations, not deductions
   from its sole prerequisite.

By the Cairn route contract, a route's existence asserts its implication and
its body must carry the argument.  The audited status plus two unproved future
steps does not carry that argument.  Hence the exact route named in the target
claim is unsound and is invalidated, without making any assertion about the
truth or falsity of the STW83 conjecture itself.
