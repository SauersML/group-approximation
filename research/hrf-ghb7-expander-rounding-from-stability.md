The plus entries of a tuple satisfying the doubled relators
asymptotically give an asymptotic representation of GHB2(7), by the
finite-presentation word argument in Section 2 of the artifact. Flexible
HS stability rounds it. The three copy-equality relators make the minus
entries asymptotically equal to the plus entries, so the same genuine
representations round all six entries. The scalar gap is not needed
for this direction.
