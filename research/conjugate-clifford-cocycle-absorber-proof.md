# Tensor the Clifford projective action with its complex conjugate

Schur's lemma produces `(CC2)`. The conjugate intertwiners have cocycle
`conjugate(c)=c^{-1}`, so `C_alpha tensor conjugate(C_alpha)` is an honest
finite-dimensional representation. Tensor it with an arbitrary exact model
of the original homogeneous face group. The finite factor implements the
desired automorphisms while all cocycle phases cancel, leaving the original
contextual multiplicity model unchanged.
