**OPEN.** Call this statement E_X. For every recursive non-decreasing
`U: N -> N` there are a finitely presented simple group `S`, a finitely
generated amenable `H = <Y> ≤ S` and `δ > 0` with `X^δ_(H,Y) ⋠ U`. The profile
`X^δ` is defined in `short-expander-subgroups-force-folner-growth`: the largest
order of a finite subgroup of `H` with a symmetric generating set of at most
`L` elements, each of length at most `L`, and Cheeger constant at least `δ`.

## What is known

- **E_X implies E.** Apply E_X to `U'(L) = L·U(L^3)` and use item 4 of
  `short-expander-subgroups-force-folner-growth`. This is the route
  `fp-simple-folner-growth-via-short-expanders`.
- **The conjecture implies E_X.** Under Boone--Higman every `Δ_U` embeds in a
  finitely presented simple group. The finite subgroups `Q_t` of Step 7 of
  `higman-thompson-finite-subgroup-profile-is-recursive-proof` are generated by
  elements of length `<= 2k_t + 1`, and `|Q_t| > t U(t^2 k_t)`. With the
  property (T) Cheeger bound of Brieussel--Zheng (BZ2 in
  `decidable-amenable-groups-have-no-recursive-folner-bound-proof`), they are
  uniform expanders on these generators. This second point is not checked
  here, so this implication is recorded as expected, not established.
- **Hosts that cannot work.** By `higman-thompson-finite-subgroup-profile-is-recursive`,
  every finitely generated subgroup `K` of every `V_d`, and of every
  `V_d(G)` with `G` torsion-free self-similar, has `FS_K ≼ R♯` for one fixed
  recursive `R♯`. So no witness for E_X past `R♯` lies in these groups.
- **Necessary condition on a single host.** Suppose one finitely presented
  simple `S` supplies witnesses for every recursive `U`. Then `FS_S` is above
  every recursive function. Since `S` has solvable word problem, `FS_S ≼ R`
  with `R` recursive would make the order problem of `S` decidable (test
  `g^k = 1` for `k <= R(L)`). So such an `S` needs finite subgroups on short
  generators whose orders escape every recursive bound; an undecidable order
  problem is one way this could happen.

## Where to look

- Brin--Thompson groups `nV`, `n >= 2`. The pumping step of
  `higman-thompson-finite-subgroup-profile-is-recursive` fails there, because
  the depth cocycle is a vector. Belk and Bleak are recalled to have shown
  that the torsion problem of `2V` is undecidable (J. Belk, C. Bleak, *Some
  undecidability results for asynchronous transducers and the Brin--Thompson
  group 2V*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 369 (2017)). This was not read at source.
  If it is correct, `FS_(2V)` escapes every recursive function, so `2V` passes
  the necessary condition above. It remains to find finite subgroups with a
  uniform Cheeger constant, inside one amenable finitely generated subgroup.
- Röver--Nekrashevych groups `V_d(G)` with `G` containing torsion, where the
  kernel `K_n <= G^(d^n)` of Step 4 can be large.
