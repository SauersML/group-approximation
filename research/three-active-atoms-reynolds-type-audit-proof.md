The affine envelope fixes a splitting field of characteristic `p>=5`, while
the channel firewall computes the sharp return-frame size as `p^2`.  Hence
its minimum is at least 25, independently of the four involution-character
labels in the finite packet.  The active idempotents use the
characteristic-not-two spectral cuts `(1 plus or minus g)/2`, so the proposed
binary specialization does not preserve the packet.  Finally, the separated-
channel rectangular model shows that carrier projections alone do not define
maps between the two adjoint Reynolds ranges.  These observations prove both
the numerical and the operator-typing assertions of the target claim.
