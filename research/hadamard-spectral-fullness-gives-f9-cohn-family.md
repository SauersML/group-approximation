Proposition 2.2 of the artifact. From `c f_+ b = 1_-`, put `sigma_1 = f_+ b`, `sigma_2 = [u] f_+ b`,
`tau_1 = c f_+` and `tau_2 = c f_+ [u]`. The relation `f_+[u] = [u]f_-` kills the cross products, and
`[u]^2 = 1` gives `tau_2 sigma_2 = 1_-`.
