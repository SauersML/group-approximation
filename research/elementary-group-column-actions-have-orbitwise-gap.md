**Setting.**
- `𝒜` is a unital ring, `W` a unital left `𝒜`-module that is finite as a set, `n ≥ 2`, and `V = W^n` (columns).
- `M_n(𝒜)` acts on `V` by `(hx)_k = Σ_j h_(kj) x_j`.
- `EL_n(𝒜)` is generated by the `e_ij(a) = I + aE_ij` with `i ≠ j` and `a ∈ 𝒜`.

**Theorem G.** Let `EL_n(𝒜) ≤ H ≤ GL_n(𝒜)` and let `O ⊆ V` be an `H`-orbit. Let `g : V → V` be additive, for instance
any `g ∈ M_n(𝒜)`. If `g` moves fewer than `|O|/(2n²)` points of `O`, then `g` fixes `O` pointwise.

So the image of `H` in `Sym(V)` has orbitwise gap `1/(2n²)`, in the sense of
`orbitwise-gap-sofic-approximations-are-local-embeddings`. So does its restriction to every `H`-invariant subset of
`V`. The same holds with `W` replaced by `eW` for a non-unital homomorphism `𝒜 → End(W)` with `e = φ(1)`
(artifact §2, remarks).

**Why it matters.** Corollary C of `orbitwise-gap-sofic-approximations-are-local-embeddings` needs only a common
orbitwise gap for the images. Family (E) supplied one only for semisimple image algebras, and the radical case was
target T5 of `research/artifacts/sk-sofic-almost-action-plan-2026-09-14.md`. Theorem G closes T5. The consequence is
`column-model-kazhdan-cut-downs-certify-only-lef`.

**Tests.**
- *Root subgroups are load-bearing.* Permutation matrices acting on `F_2^m` have no orbitwise gap: on the orbit of
  `e_1` a transposition moves `2` of `m` points. Their image contains no root subgroup `T_ij`.
- *Additivity is load-bearing.* Affine actions `x ↦ ρ(h)x + c(h)` are not covered, because the `T_ij`-orbits need not
  be cosets.
- *Computation.* `experiments/elementary-orbitwise-gap-2026-09-16/` checks 17 cases, including rings with nonzero
  radical, `Z/4`, a non-commutative ring of order 16 and `T_2(F_2)`. Over matrices `g ∈ M_n(𝒜)` and group words, the
  minimal moved fraction found is `1/2`, above the proved `1/(2n²)`.
- *Constant.* `1/(2n²)` is not claimed sharp. For arbitrary additive `g` the constant `1/2` fails, even for
  semisimple `𝒜`. Take `𝒜 = W = F_2 × F_2` and `n = 2`, so `V = F_2^2 × F_2^2`. Let `O` be the orbit where both
  factors are nonzero, so `|O| = 9`. An additive `g` whose fixed set is the hyperplane `{x_1 + y_1 = 0}` moves `4` of
  the `9` points. Whether `1/2` holds for `g ∈ M_n(𝒜)` is open.

**Credit.** An elementary root-subgroup count. No novelty is claimed for the method. The bounded arXiv check of
2026-09-16 (artifact §7, abstracts only) found no statement of this gap for rings with a radical. That check is not a
full audit.
