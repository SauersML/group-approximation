**ESTABLISHED** through `mod-s2-short-generated-torelli-relations-pin-one-thirtieth-proof`. Short proof;
unreviewed; no novelty claimed.

**Notation.** As in `mod-s2-torelli-abelian-scalar-gap-is-a-monomial-census-limit` [TA]: right cosets `X`
of `M' = pi^(-1)(Gamma')`, classes `v_c`, the relation lattice `W(Gamma') <= Z^X` and the distance `d(Gamma')`.
For `w in Z^X` put `aug(w) = sum_c w_c` and `||w||_1 = sum_c |w_c|`.

**Condition (SG*) at `Gamma'`.** `W(Gamma')` has a generating set `S` such that every `w in S` has
`aug(w) in 10 Z`, and the only integer `n` with `|n - aug(w)/3| < ||w||_1 / 30` (if any) is `n = 3 aug(w)/10`.

**Condition (SG)** (the case used in practice). `W(Gamma')` is generated by vectors `w` with
- `aug(w) = 0` and `||w||_1 <= 30`, or
- `|aug(w)| = 10` and `||w||_1 <= 20`.

(SG) implies (SG*): for `aug = 0` the open interval `(-l/30, l/30)` with `l <= 30` contains only `0`; for
`aug = 10` the interval around `10/3` of radius `l/30 <= 2/3` contains at most the integer `3`
(the next integers `2` and `4` are at distance `4/3` and `2/3`). The case `aug = -10` is symmetric.

**Split form (SG0) at `Gamma'`.** `(SG0) = (SG-a) + (SG-b)`, and it implies (SG):
- **(SG-a)** some `w_1 in W(Gamma')` has `aug(w_1) = 10` and `||w_1||_1 <= 20`, and `aug(W(Gamma')) <= 10Z`;
- **(SG-b)** the augmentation-zero sublattice `W_0 = W(Gamma') cap ker(aug)` is generated by vectors of
  `l1 <= 30`.

(SG0) implies (SG), because `W = W_0 + Z w_1` when `aug(W) = 10Z`. The two parts are independent
questions. (SG-a) asks for one short relation of augmentation 10. It holds wherever `A_+ = 10`, and also
at every computed level with `A_+ = 20`. (SG-b) asks for short generation of the augmentation-zero
relations.

**Theorem.**
1. **(SG-pin)** For every finite-index `Gamma' <= Sp_4(Z)` satisfying (SG*), `d(Gamma') = 1/30`.
2. **(Decomposition)** Suppose (SG*) holds at `Gamma(2N)` for every `N >= 1` (for instance, suppose
   (SG-a) and (SG-b) hold there). Then `d_inf = 1/30`, which is the sharp form of
   `mod-s2-torelli-abelian-census-limit-is-positive`.
   - Each instance "(SG*) at `Gamma(2N)`" is a finite exact computation (one Z-basis of `W`), independent
     of the others.
   - A failure at one level does not refute the sharp form.
3. **(Checks)** (SG) holds at `Gamma(2)`: in class coordinates `W = Z w` with `w = +-(1, ..., 1)`, so
   `||w||_1 = |aug(w)| = 10` (route, Step 4). It also holds, by the LLL bases of `exact_d.py`, at `Gamma_U`, `Gamma_(max_0)`, `Gamma_(max_1)`, `Gamma_(max_2)` of
   `mod-s2-three-sylow-torelli-census` (row types `(|aug|, l1)` at most `(0, 16)` and `(10, 18)`). It fails at
   `Sp_4(Z)` (`W = 5Z`, `aug = 5` not in `10Z`), in accordance with `d(Sp_4(Z)) = 1/15`.
   - At `Gamma_Phi` (180 classes, `W` rank 150) the plain LLL basis has a row `(10, 22)`, so it fails
     (SG). (SG0) holds: `||w_1||_1 = 16`, and `W_0` has `l1 <= 24` (`run-check-phi.log`).
   - At `Gamma(4)` (`level4_sg.py`: 512 Pbar cosets, 160 classes, `H_1 = Z^54`, `W` rank 106), (SG) holds
     on the LLL basis, with row types at most `(0, 18)` and `(10, 18)`. (SG0) also holds: `w_1 >= 0`,
     `||w_1||_1 = 10`, and `W_0` has an LLL basis of `l1 <= 18`.
   - At the index-two groups `Gamma(8) < G_chi < Gamma(4)` (`level8_sg.py`, one `chi` per
     `Sp_4(F_2)`-orbit, 15 orbits of total size 1023, so every `chi`), (SG0) holds at every orbit.
     - The relation `w_1` is `>= 0` with `||w_1||_1 = 10`, and `W_0` has `l1 <= 22`.
     - Fourteen orbits have 160 classes and `W` rank 106. One orbit (`chi2`) has 192 classes, rank 138 and
       `H_1 = Z^54 + Z/2`; there the plain LLL basis fails (SG) (a row `(10, 24)`), and the split passes
       (`W_0` `l1 <= 20`).
     - The independent box certificate also gives `1/30` at all 15 orbits (`exact-level8-all-fast.json`).

**What it changes.** The census below `Gamma_U` showed that no single relation certifies `d = 1/30`
(least `||w||_1 = 12` with `3 not| aug`, so (TA3) gives only `1/36`). The theorem replaces the single
certificate by a checkable property of a generating set: short generators pin the lift of any near-`1/3`
character to the constant `3/10` on `W`, and then the positive transfer relation `5 1_X` excludes it.
The open problem becomes a question about generation of `W(Gamma(2N))`, the relations among conjugates of
`t_s` in `H_1(M_(2N))`, by relations of bounded shape. A proof that `W(Gamma(2N))` is generated by relations
of two kinds would give the sharp conjecture:
- augmentation `0` and `l1 <= 30` (for example, relations supported on few cosets);
- `l1 = |aug| = 10`.

The theorem does not say whether such generation holds. (SG) is sufficient, not necessary. Among the
levels `Gamma(2N)` it has been checked only at `Gamma(2)` and `Gamma(4)`. `Gamma(6)` (25920 Pbar cosets)
and `Gamma(8)` are out of reach of the present exact pipeline. Two observations matter here:
- The generator lengths grow slowly along the computed tower. The largest aug-0 generator `l1` is 16
  on the first 3-adic step (`max_1`), 24 at `Gamma_Phi`, 18 at `Gamma(4)`, and 22 at the `G_chi`, all
  below 30. Plain LLL bases fail (SG) twice (`Phi`, `chi2`), always through an aug-10 row; the split
  form never failed.
- An unbounded growth of the least generating `l1` of `W_0` would not refute the sharp form, only this
  route to it.
