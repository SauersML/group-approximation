ESTABLISHED.  Let `D` be any finite subset of `Z\{0}`.  Consider the finite
shift-stencil presentation

```text
Gamma_D=<t,p,q,J |
  p^2=q^2=J^2=1,
  [J,t]=[J,p]=[J,q]=1,
  [p,q]=J,
  [a,t^r b t^(-r)]=1
       (a,b in {p,q}, r in D)> .                         (FSP1)
```

Choose an integer

```text
m>max_(r in D)|r|.                                      (FSP2)
```

Let `K_m` be the central product of `m` copies of the one-qubit Pauli
group, with generators

```text
J,p_i,q_i       (i in Z/mZ),
[p_i,q_i]=J,
[p_i,p_j]=[q_i,q_j]=[p_i,q_j]=1       (i!=j).            (FSP3)
```

The cyclic permutation `sigma:i|->i+1` acts on `K_m`.  There is a
homomorphism

```text
Gamma_D -> K_m rtimes <sigma>,
t |-> sigma,       p |-> p_0,       q |-> q_0,       J |-> J.  (FSP4)
```

Indeed `t^r b t^(-r)` is the indicated Pauli generator at site `r mod m`.
By `(FSP2)` this site is different from zero for every `r in D`, so every
off-site commutator in `(FSP1)` holds exactly.

Represent `K_m` on `(C^2)^(tensor m)` by Pauli `X,Z` in the corresponding
tensor factor, put `J=-I`, and implement `sigma` by cyclic permutation of
the tensor factors.  This gives an exact `2^m`-dimensional unitary
representation of `(FSP1)` with

```text
||J-I||_2=2.                                             (FSP5)
```

Consequently no finite collection of translation-invariant relative-offset
tests on a single shift orbit can force collapse of the common Clifford sign,
or make the full-dyadic terminal seam visible in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt
error.  Increasing the matrix dimension simply chooses a longer exact cycle;
the first alias occurs at offset `m`, outside the finite tested stencil.

This obstruction is stronger than a depth union-bound warning: the periodic
models have zero defining-relator defect.  A successful full-tree compiler
must therefore use a genuinely nonperiodic scale-conversion relation, a
section-free growing consistency component such as the separated Cayley
expander target, or one matrix-coordinate relation whose defect already
packs all offsets.  Adding more fixed offsets to `(FSP1)` cannot help.
