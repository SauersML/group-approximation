Use the disjoint projective images in `(OTF2)` to decode positive words and
prove freeness.  Right cancellation and the disjoint last letters give
`(OTF3)`.  Sum blockwise squared defects under the stated uniform lamp
decoder for `(OTF4)`.  For each fixed radius, separate the finitely many
positive matrices in a finite congruence quotient of `SL_d(Z)` and take its
finite Bernoulli wreath, proving exactness of the whole bounded cone window.
Finally use normality of the image of `Gamma` in every finite quotient to
identify the precise stabilizer-moved lamp correlations that this local model
cannot retain.
