**INVALIDATED NATIVE-OCCURRENCE CELL.**  Strengthen the authenticated
intermediate fold arm by replacing its abstract transporter `W` with the
literal opposite-root factorization

```text
W=UVU,
```

where `U` is the root occurrence carrying the same `s` coefficient as the
reused right-composition arm `S`, and `V` carries the same `t` coefficient
as the reused final arm `T`.  Two fixed root-position transports identify
`U` with `S` and `V` with `T`.  This is the smallest opposite-root cell in
which both sides of the partial Whitehead and the final fold return occur in
one bounded word table.

`partial-whitehead-fold-return-has-finite-s6-model` gives an exact marked
model of the whole table.  Thus literal three-factor Whitehead sharing is
still weaker than same-reservoir coefficient multiplication.
