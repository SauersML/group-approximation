Given the thin co-dense `(tau)` pair `(SL_n(Z), C)` of
`sln-z-thin-codense-tau-pair` and flexible HS-stability of `SL_n(Z)`,
`hnn-over-codense-kazhdan-subgroup-not-hyperlinear` makes
`<SL_n(Z), t | [t, C] = 1>` a finitely presented non-hyperlinear group.  The
open hypothesis is flexible stability of the plain higher-rank lattice, the
most-studied group in the subject -- the flagship member of the
"any-one-of-these-stabilities-suffices" family, covered by neither the
central-extension route (Dogon) nor the S-arithmetic route
(Dogon--Vigdorovich).
