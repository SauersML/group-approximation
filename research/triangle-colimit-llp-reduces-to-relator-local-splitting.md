**Setting.**  A *triangle of finite groups* is a diagram of finite
groups and injections over the poset of proper subsets of `{1,2,3}`:

```text
P_0  <=  P_1, P_2, P_3  <=  P_12, P_13, P_23      (P_0 := P_emptyset)
```

(all inclusion arrows commuting), subject to

```text
(H1)  P_1 cap P_2 = P_0 inside P_12,   P_2 cap P_3 = P_0 inside P_23;
(H2)  P_13 = <P_1 cup P_3>.
```

Let `Lambda := colim` of the diagram in groups.  The rank-`<= 2`
subgroup presentations of 2-spherical rank-3 Kac--Moody lattices
recorded in `non-rf-kazhdan-group-with-llp-full-c-star-algebra` are of
this shape ((H1)-(H2) are the standard parabolic/Levi intersection and
generation facts for the finite rank-2 groups of Lie type at the
vertices), with either pair `{i,j}` allowed to play the role of `13`.

**THEOREM.**

```text
(a) G_0 := P_12 *_{P_2} P_23 is finitely generated virtually free and
    C*(G_0) has the lifting property (LP), hence the LLP.       (TC1)

(b) The canonical map P_1 *_{P_0} P_3 -> G_0 is injective, with image
    E := <P_1 cup P_3>; the induced map q : E ->> P_13 is onto with
    kernel N free of finite rank

      r = 1 + [P_13 : P_0] - [P_13 : P_1] - [P_13 : P_3],

    namely N = pi_1(L) for the connected bipartite coset graph L with
    vertices P_13/P_1 u P_13/P_3 and edge set P_13/P_0.  Moreover

      Lambda = G_0 / <<N>>,      C*(Lambda) = C*(G_0) / I_N,

    where I_N is the closed two-sided ideal generated by the r
    elements u_{n_k} - 1, {n_k} any free basis of N.            (TC2)

(c) (Transfer lemma; general.)  Let A be unital with the LLP and
    I an ideal, Q = A/I.  Then Q has the LLP if and only if the
    extension 0 -> I -> A -> Q -> 0 is locally ucp-split (every
    finite-dimensional operator system E c Q admits a ucp
    s : E -> A with pi o s = incl).  If A has the LP: Q has the LP
    if and only if the extension admits a global ucp splitting. (TC3)

(d) Consequently  C*(Lambda) has the LLP  <=>  the r-relator
    extension 0 -> I_N -> C*(G_0) -> C*(Lambda) -> 0 is locally
    ucp-split;  and  C*(Lambda) has the LP  <=>  that extension is
    globally ucp-split.                                         (TC4)
```

Proof: `triangle-relator-local-splitting-proof`.  The LP input in (a)
is Enders--Shulman arXiv:2403.12224 Corollary 4.6 (finite tree
products of finite groups; source-verified, see artifact); everything
else is self-contained.

**Instantiation: two distinct families (corrected 2026-08-21).**
(i) For the Kac--Moody lattices of
`simple-kazhdan-kac-moody-lattices-exist`, the triangle presentation
is the Abramenko--Mühlherr amalgam ([AM97], C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris
325 (1997) 701-706, identified at source through Caprace--Rémy) by
the rank-`<= 2` finite subgroups of the twin root datum; `r` is given
by the index formula of (TC2), and by
`km-lattices-admit-no-cocompact-npc-model` the links of ANY triangle
of finite groups presenting such a lattice contain short cycles
(`2/g_12 + 2/g_13 + 2/g_23 > 1`) — the presentation is never
nonpositively curved.  (ii) The triangles whose links are generalized
`m_ij`-gon incidence graphs (girth `2 m_ij`, `sum 1/m_ij <= 1`) ARE
nonpositively curved, and for them the Bruhat chamber count gives
`r = q^{l(w_0)} = q^{m}` with the relators literally the
girth-length-`2m` cycles of the link — but their colimits are
Gromov-hyperbolic uniform lattices on the CAT(-1) development
(Fuchsian-building type), provably NOT Kac--Moody lattices (same
node).  The theorem applies verbatim to both families; the
"large girth of the links" geometry lives only in family (ii), whose
non-residual-finiteness (needed for the non-RF hole) and per-instance
property (T) are unrecorded.

**Where the Enders--Shulman engine dies (obstruction located).**
Their amalgam theorem (Theorem 4.2, read at proof level) corrects two
liftable *-homomorphisms to agree *exactly* on the amalgamated `F` by
conjugating one lift with a unitary built via Lemma 4.1 from finitely
many partial isometries matching the minimal projections of the matrix
summands of `F`; the corrected lifts then glue by the universal
property of `A *_F B`.  Two independent failures at the triangle:

1. **The amalgam base is infinite-dimensional.**  Written tree-first,
   the triangle's last step is the pushout of `C*(G_0) <- C*(E) ->>
   C*(P_13)` with `E` infinite virtually free.  Lemma 4.1's finite
   frame of minimal projections does not exist for `C*(E)`; unitary
   equivalence of representations of an infinite-dimensional algebra
   is no longer detected by projection data, and Voiculescu-type
   substitutes give only *approximate* unitary equivalence, useless
   here because the gluing step needs *exact* agreement on the base.
2. **Nothing descends along the non-injective leg.**  Even granted
   exact agreement on `C*(E)`, the universal property produces a
   morphism on the honest amalgam `A *_{C*(E)} B`, never on its
   quotient: the triangle additionally requires the glued lift to
   annihilate the lifted relator ideal, a constraint of a different
   type that no step of arXiv:2403.12224 addresses (their only
   quotient-crossing tool is the unitary lift through a connected
   unitary group in Lemma 4.1(ii)).

By (TC3)-(TC4) failure 2 is not a removable defect of their method:
*any* proof of LLP for `C*(Lambda)` must produce local ucp sections of
the relator extension, because LLP of the quotient is equivalent to
their existence.

**No tree-shaped escape exists for the Kac--Moody instances.**  For
`Lambda` simple, Kazhdan and finitely generated (the
`simple-kazhdan-kac-moody-lattices-exist` class): property (T) implies
Serre's property FA (Watatani), so every action of `Lambda` on a tree
has a fixed point and every graph-of-groups decomposition is trivial —
the tree permanences (Enders--Shulman Cor 4.6/4.7,
Fournier-Facio--Willett's amalgams and graphs of groups with finite
edge groups) can only apply with a vertex group equal to `Lambda`
itself; simplicity voids the central- and amenable-extension
permanences (no nontrivial normal subgroups); finite generation voids
increasing unions (a stage equals `Lambda`); subgroup permanence would
need an LLP overgroup, none recorded.  This is the `Lambda`-side
analogue of `leavitt-llp-permanence-fence`: every published positive
route is circular at `Lambda`, and by (TC4) the one non-circular
formulation is the local-splitting statement.  The open content is
isolated as `triangle-relator-extension-admits-local-ucp-sections`,
consumed toward the program hole by
`llp-hole-via-triangle-relator-local-sections`.

**Necessary matrix form, and where Paulsen--Rodman dies.**  A local
ucp section on the operator system `span{u_g : g in F^{-1}F}`
(`F c Lambda` finite) produces, for each `F`, a positive matrix
`[T_{g,h}] in M_F(C*(G_0))` with `T_{g,g} = 1`, `pi(T_{g,h}) =
u_{g^{-1}h}`, and `T_{g,h}` depending only on `g^{-1}h`: a lift of the
`Lambda`-Gram matrix `[u_{g^{-1}h}]` respecting the difference
pattern.  This is a *patterned* positive-lifting problem adjacent to
`chordal-partial-positive-operator-matrices-lift`, but Paulsen--Rodman
elimination completes one *free* entry at a time by Schur-complement
choice, while here the pattern is complete and the constraint couples
every entry to its whole difference class — the elimination order
cannot respect the pattern, which is exactly where that tool dies.
(Sufficiency of Gram-lifting for cp-ness on the span is a separate
gap: positivity in `M_n` of the operator system is tested against
matrices that are not of Gram form.)

**LP-side remarks (corrected 2026-08-21).**  An earlier version of
this paragraph asserted `H^2(Lambda; R) = 0` via a "contractible
cocompact development"; that justification was WRONG — the
development of the triangle presentation is proper and cocompact but
is never CAT(0) (`km-lattices-admit-no-cocompact-npc-model`), and its
contractibility is unknown, so `H^2(Lambda; R)` is UNRECORDED in both
directions.  The Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma LP fences (Corollary D:
property (T) with `H^2(Gamma, Z Gamma) != 0`; Corollary E: property
(T) with an ergodic p.m.p. action satisfying
`H^2(Gamma, L^0(X, R)) != 0`, in particular `H^2(Gamma, R) != 0` —
both source-verified verbatim) target exactly the *global* splitting
side of (TC4); whether either fires at `Lambda` is the open claim
`compact-hyperbolic-km-lattices-fail-lp`, whose Attempts record why
no current model computes the needed cohomology and what each
outcome buys.
