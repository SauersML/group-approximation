**ESTABLISHED (import).** T. Lescure, *On the K-theory of algebraic Cuntz–Pimsner rings*,
arXiv:2603.26222. Read from the arXiv e-print `main.tex` (fetched on MSI, 2026-09-18). The
abstract, Theorem A and Theorem B are at l.51 and l.67–84; the Nekrashevych-algebra example is
at l.600–626.

**Standing hypotheses (l.67).**
- `R` has local units.
- The `R`-correspondence `𝒳 = (X, X', ⟨,⟩)` satisfies condition (FS) of Carlsen–Ortega (l.113),
  and it comes with a functional homomorphism to a canonical system `R^(I)`.

**Theorem A.** Let `E` be homotopy invariant, split-exact and `M`-stable. Then `E` sends
`R -> 𝒯_𝒳` to an isomorphism, where `𝒯_𝒳` is the Toeplitz ring.

**Theorem B.** Let `(E_n)` be homotopy invariant, `M`-stable and excisive. Let `𝓘 ◁ R` have
local units and act on `X` from the left by compact operators. Then there is a long exact
sequence

    … -> E_n(𝓘) --(E_n(i) - E_n(𝒳))--> E_n(R) --E_n(j)--> E_n(𝒪_(𝒳,𝓘)) -> E_(n-1)(𝓘) -> …

The paper says both theorems hold for Weibel's `KH` and for periodic cyclic homology. By its
Proposition 2.13, the map `E_n(𝒳)` is `E_n` of the left action `𝓘 -> 𝒦_R(𝒳)`, followed by
`𝒦_R(𝒳) -> M_I(R)` and Morita invariance.

**The case used here** (a lane specialization, not stated in the source). Take `R` unital, `X = R^d` with left action through a unital
homomorphism `ψ : R -> M_d(R)`, `X' = R^d` with the standard pairing, and `𝓘 = R`.
- (FS) holds, since `1 ∈ M_d(R) = 𝒦_R(X)`, and the functional homomorphism is the identity.
- `𝒪_(𝒳,R)` is the ring generated by `R` and Leavitt letters `s_i, t_i` with `t_i s_j = δ_ij`
  and `b = Σ_(ij) s_i ψ(b)_(ij) t_j` for all `b`. This is the completion `O_ψ(R)` of
  `leavitt-nekrashevych-completion-embeds-and-is-fp` and
  `noninjective-ln-completions-have-self-similar-bases`, whether or not `ψ` is injective.
- `E_n(𝒳) = E_n(ψ)`, read through Morita invariance.

So `KH_n(R) --(1 - KH_n(ψ))--> KH_n(R) -> KH_n(O_ψ(R)) -> KH_(n-1)(R)` is exact.
