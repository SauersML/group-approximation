For the binary Leavitt algebra `L=L_F2(1,2)` and `r>=3`, every
finite-dimensional unitary representation of `St_r(L)`, and hence of
`EL_r(L)`, is trivial.

Consequently the regular-A8 rank-five criterion has no exact solution in
any finite dimension: its stability/hyperlinearity fork concerns only an
infimum along growing dimensions.  The theorem gives no positive lower
bound on that infimum and makes no assertion about infinite-dimensional or
asymptotic representations.
