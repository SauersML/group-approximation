title: Universal relator-ideal identities cannot force a Hall-deficient regular-chart support graph
---

Fix the regular-`A_8` Fourier matrix units used by
`regular-chart-wedderburn-coefficient-compiler`, and regard the relative chart
unitary `U` as a noncommuting variable together with its adjoint.  Let
`R_1(U),...,R_m(U)` be coefficient expressions obtained from any finite family
of genuine atlas relator residuals.

There is **no** weighted-Hall-deficient bipartite graph `E` on those fixed
Fourier corners for which every forbidden block

```text
p_u U p_v,       (v,u) notin E,
```

belongs to the algebraic two-sided `*`-ideal generated by the `R_j` (allowing
arbitrary finite star-polynomial multipliers built from the fixed chart
coefficients and `U`).  Equivalently, the exact bounded noncommutative ideal
search in `experiments/nc_path_ideal_certificate.py` can certify individual
coefficient consequences, but it can never close
`atlas-wedderburn-hall-deficient-support-certificate` by producing enough
universal forbidden-block identities to make a deficient cut.

This does **not** refute the Hall route.  It locates its matrix-specific
content: the support reduction must use finite-multiplicity coordinate data
that are not universal algebraic consequences in arbitrary finite tracial
models.
