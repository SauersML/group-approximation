Let

```text
V=PSL_2(Z)=<x,a | x^2=a^3=1>
```

and let `H=Gamma_0(2)` be the index-three Iwahori subgroup.  With the
standard modular generators, the parabolic

```text
t=xa
```

belongs to `H`.  The restriction image

```text
res: Rep_+(V) -> Rep_+(H)                              (CIR1)
```

contains uncountably many distinct two-dimensional representation types.
In particular it is not a finitely generated positive monoid, and its
Grothendieck image cannot be encoded by the finite-rank integer restriction
operator used for graphs of finite groups.

Therefore the square-free torsion frame

```text
C_2*C_3*C_3
```

does not turn Iwahori repair into a finite representation-semigroup problem.
It exactifies the three torsion spectra, but the relative eigenspace angle
already carries a continuous edge character before the second modular chart
is attached.  Any flexible quotient-lifting theorem must either align this
continuous edge data analytically or first prove that the regular/congruence
branch collapses it to a discrete moving-level packet.  Gerasimova--Shchepin's
finite-edge integer-flow criterion cannot be applied directly.
