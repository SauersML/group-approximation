**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `Γ` be amenable. If some nonempty SFT over `Γ` has no finite orbit, then
`Γ ∉ 𝔑`. Equivalently: an amenable group in `𝔑` has every nonempty SFT with a finite orbit.

It recovers `z2-containing-groups-have-non-rf-measure-actions` for amenable groups containing `Z^2`. Candidate further
non-members **without** `Z^2`: `BS(1,n)` (weakly aperiodic SFTs reported by Aubrun–Kari; not imported here, see
`measure-rf-class-is-exactly-virtually-free-groups` Attempts), lamplighter groups and infinite torsion groups (status
unknown in this lane).
