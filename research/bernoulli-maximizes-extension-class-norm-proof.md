Sections 2--4 of the artifact.
1. **Item 1 (Lemma L).**
   - (=>) Choose a Borel section of `X -> X/A`. Its failure to be equivariant is `b : Gamma x Z -> A` with
     `alpha_0 = delta b`.
   - (<=) From `alpha_0 = delta beta`, the formula `T_(g,a)(z,t) = (gz, t + a + beta_g(gz))` on `Z x A` is a free action
     of `Gamma~ = Gamma x_{alpha_0} A`, verified line by line in the artifact.
   - Cellular `H^2(BGamma, M)` and group cohomology agree because both are `Ext^2_{ZGamma}(Z, M)`.
2. **Item 2 (Lemma M).** This is the chain of inequalities in the proof of Gohla--Thom Corollary 2.14, for the constant
   family `P_j = M(Z')`.
3. **Item 3.** Abert--Weiss gives `Bern ≺ Z` for free `Z`; apply item 2.
4. **Item 4.**
   - (=>) Items 1 and 2.
   - (<=) Take cocycle representatives `c_n = theta(alpha_0) + delta zeta_n` with `||c_n|| -> 0`. They vanish in the
     ultrapower. So `theta(alpha_0) = -delta zeta` with `zeta` determined by finitely many elements of `M(Bern)_𝒰(A)`.
   - The separable invariant subalgebra generated by those elements and the diagonal `M(Bern)` is, by Mackey's point
     realization, a free standard action `Y`. `Y` is contained in the ultrapower, so `Y ≺ Bern`, and `Y` is liftable by
     item 1.
