## Setting

Let P_G(r) be as in `graph-houghton-groups-embed-in-eventually-rigid-g-automorphisms`, with X = N × {1..r} and
Q = G * F(X). Let

    Φ = { φ ∈ Hom_G(Q, G) : φ(x) = 1 for all but finitely many x ∈ X },

with P_G(r) acting by α·φ = φ ∘ α⁻¹.

**Invariance.** Take φ ∈ Φ and α ∈ P_G(r) with data (E, σ, w_j). For x on ray j outside E, with σ(x) outside the
support of φ, we get φ(α(x)) = φ(w_j) φ(σx) φ(w_j)⁻¹ = 1. So φ ∘ α ∈ Φ.

## Theorem

Let G be finitely generated, infinite, simple and MIF, and let r ≥ 2. Then:

1. **Highly transitive.** Given two k-tuples of distinct elements of Φ, choose a finite E ⊂ X with |E| ≥ 2 containing
   all their supports. By BFFHZ `prop:2trans`, some β ∈ Aut_G(G * F(E)) carries the first tuple to the second on
   G * F(E). Extended by the identity on X ∖ E, β lies in P_G(r). It still matches the tuples, because every φ
   involved is trivial on X ∖ E.
2. **Faithful.** Let α ≠ 1. Some x has u = x⁻¹α(x) ≠ 1, with u ∈ G * F(E′) for a finite E′. Since G is MIF, some
   ψ ∈ Hom_G(G * F(E′), G) has ψ(u) ≠ 1. Extend ψ by 1 to get φ ∈ Φ. Then φ(α(x)) ≠ φ(x). This is BFFHZ
   `lem:faithful` word for word.
3. **Point stabilizers finitely generated.** By transitivity it is enough to treat φ_0, the homomorphism killing X. Let
   S be a finite generating set of G. Then Stab(φ_0) is generated by:
   - generators of H_r;
   - the Nielsen move x_1 ↦ x_1x_2 and the inversion x_1 ↦ x_1⁻¹;
   - the partial conjugations c_s: x_1 ↦ s⁻¹x_1s, for s ∈ S;
   - the slides slide_j(s) and slide_j(x_1), for s ∈ S and 1 ≤ j ≤ r.

   Every listed element fixes φ_0: every slide does, since φ_0(u x u⁻¹) = 1. The reduction runs as follows. Take
   α ∈ Stab(φ_0).
   - Multiply α by an element of H_r ≤ Stab(φ_0) to make its germ trivial.
   - Multiply by slides, which also lie in Stab(φ_0), to make every w_j = 1.
   - The result is a finitary element of Stab(φ_0) ∩ Aut_G(G * F(E′)). By BFFHZ `prop:fg_stab` (the T-map argument)
     that group is generated by their set Y: basis permutations, inversions, Nielsen moves, and the partial
     conjugations x_i ↦ g⁻¹x_ig.
   - Every element of Y is a product of FSym(X)-conjugates of the listed generators, because c_g c_h = c_{gh} on one
     letter.

So P_G(r) ↷ Φ satisfies conditions 1, 3 and 4 of `type-a-action-gives-boone-higman-for-subgroups`. It satisfies
condition 2, finite presentation of P_G(r), exactly when the open node
`eventually-rigid-g-automorphism-groups-are-finitely-presented` holds. Example of G: Thompson's V, which is finitely
presented, simple and highly transitive, hence MIF (BFFHZ introduction, citing Hull–Osin Thm 5.9 and Le Boudec–Matte
Bon Prop A.1).

## Lesson for general BH

BFFHZ's action localizes. For groups of G-automorphisms that are eventually rigid (Houghton germs at infinity),
finitely supported homomorphisms to G form an invariant set. On that set, high transitivity, faithfulness and finite
generation of stabilizers follow from the finite-rank BFFHZ lemmas, together with slides and H_r. So among the type
(A) conditions only finite presentation of the actor needs new work: all the transitivity content is inherited from
finite rank, and the only genuinely infinite-rank question is finiteness.
