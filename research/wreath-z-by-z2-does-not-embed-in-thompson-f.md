`Z ≀ Z^2 = (⊕_{Z^2} Z) ⋊ Z^2` is isomorphic to no subgroup of Thompson's group `F`. More
generally, it is isomorphic to no subgroup of `PL_+(I)` whose right derivatives at fixed
points all lie in one cyclic subgroup of `R_{>0}`. `Z ≀ Z^2` is bi-orderable and
metabelian.

Contrast: `F` contains `Z ≀ Z`, `Z^2 ≀ Z` and `(Z ≀ Z) ≀ Z`. So among bi-orderable
metabelian groups this is the first wreath product outside `F`. It is the leading
candidate witness for `biorderable-subgroup-of-v-without-free-subgroups-not-in-f`, if it
embeds in `V`.
