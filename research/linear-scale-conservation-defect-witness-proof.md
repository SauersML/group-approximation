## Why sufficient

* **Linear models.** `literal-group-sofic` and `sofic-implies-linear-sofic-over-every-field`
  give an injective `sigma : E -> M^x` over any field `k`.
* **The configuration.** `literal-kazhdan-clifford-inputs` supplies the Kazhdan affine base
  `Gamma`, the doubling compressor `t` and the centralizing `c`.
* **The defect is nontrivial.** `literal-mf-radical-is-unsquared-defect` says that
  `u = [t c t^-1, v_1]` normally generates the MF radical, which contains the nontrivial
  central involution `w` by `literal-group-fails-mf-conventions`. So `u != 1`. This is
  Theorem 4 of the ladder artifact.
* **Conservation holds.** `<Gamma, t>` is generated by compressors of `Gamma`, so
  `rank-ultraproduct-compressors-conserve-fixed-right-ideals` applies in every degree.
* **Conclusion.** `sigma(u) != 1` because `sigma` is injective.
