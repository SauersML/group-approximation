On complete tracial types, each optimized gadget residual is the value of an
existential continuous-logic formula.  Separate zero residuals make the
union of the disjoint auxiliary relation types finitely approximately
satisfiable.  Countable saturation realizes the union exactly.  For a
matricial base type, take an ultrapower and flatten the iterated
matrix-ultraproduct.
