**Setting** (`leavitt-cuntz-cone-halves-have-rf-amalgam-cover`, `leavitt-st20-nonhyperlinear-iff-cone-bound-and-half-rounding`).
- `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, `q = s_1 t_1`, `Delta = St_20(R) = GL_20(R)` (simple, infinite, finitely generated), `z = x_13(q)`.
- `Delta_+-` are the subgroups of `Delta` generated by root elements with entries in `R_+ = <s_1, s_2, q>` and
  `R_- = <t_1, t_2, q>`; `Delta_0 = Delta_+ ∩ Delta_- = SL_20(F_2)^2` is finite and contains `z`.
- `Gamma = Delta_+ *_(Delta_0) Delta_-`, `p : Gamma -> Delta` onto, `K = ker p` the normal closure of `k_1, ..., k_r`.
- Norms are normalized Hilbert-Schmidt, `[a, b] = a b a^-1 b^-1`.
- A **compression pair** in a group `G` is `(L, u)` with `L <= G` and `u ∈ G` such that `u L u^-1 <= L`.

**Theorem.**
1. **Bass-Serre localization.** Let `(L, u)` be a compression pair in `Gamma` with `L` infinite and Kazhdan. Then
   `L` and `u` lie in one common vertex stabilizer `g Delta_+ g^-1` or `g Delta_- g^-1`, where `g ∈ Gamma`.
2. **Hilbert-Schmidt transfer.** Let `Lambda` be any group, `(L, u)` a compression pair in `Lambda`, and `(S, kappa)`
   a Kazhdan pair of `L`. For every finite-dimensional unitary representation `rho` of `Lambda`, every `c ∈ Lambda` and
   every `l ∈ L`,

   ```text
   ||rho([u c u^-1, l]) - 1||_2  <=  (2/kappa) · max_(s ∈ S) ||rho([s, c]) - 1||_2 .          (HT)
   ```

3. **Head bound from a half defect.** Suppose **(HEC)** of `leavitt-cone-half-carries-rigid-compression-defect`
   holds. That is, there are:
   - a compression pair `(L, u)` in `Delta_+` with `L` Kazhdan;
   - `c̄ ∈ C_Delta(L)` and `l ∈ L` with `[u c̄ u^-1, l] ≠ 1` in `Delta`.

   Then there is `C < ∞` such that every finite-dimensional unitary representation `rho` of `Gamma` satisfies
   `||rho(z) - 1||_2 <= C · max_i ||rho(k_i) - 1||_2`. So `(QC)` holds with a linear modulus, and `Delta` is not
   hyperlinear iff `(HR)` holds.
4. **Exactly the half configurations can enter.** Let `(L̄, ū)` be a compression pair in `Delta` with `L̄` infinite
   and Kazhdan. Some lift `(L, u)` of it is a compression pair in `Gamma` with `L` Kazhdan iff some conjugate
   `(h L̄ h^-1, h ū h^-1)`, `h ∈ Delta`, is a compression pair inside `Delta_+` or inside `Delta_-`. By the mirror
   automorphism `g -> (g^*)^-1`, the `Delta_-` case is the `Delta_+` case.

**Proof idea.**
- (1): Watatani's theorem gives a fixed vertex. Edge stabilizers are finite, so an infinite `L` fixes exactly one
  vertex. `u` carries it to the unique fixed vertex of `u L u^-1`, which also fixes it.
- (2): The Kazhdan inequality for `Ad rho|_L` on `(M_d, ||·||_2)` puts `rho(c)` within `kappa^-1` times the displacement
  of the commutant `rho(L)'`. The algebra spanned by `rho(L)` is carried into itself by `Ad rho(u)`. It has finite
  dimension, so the inclusion is an equality, and `Ad rho(u)` preserves `rho(L)'`.
- (3): Lift `c̄` to `c ∈ Gamma`. Then `[s, c] ∈ K` for `s ∈ S`. Simplicity of `Delta` writes `z` as a fixed product of
  conjugates of the defect, modulo a fixed element of `K`. Then apply (HT).

## What this settles and kills

- **New decomposition of (QC).** `(QC)` <= `(HEC)`. `(HEC)` is a purely algebraic statement about
  `GL_20(R)` and its Cuntz-cone subgroup, with no analysis and no representations in it. It can fail on its own:
  it fails whenever `Delta_+` carries no strict compression of a Kazhdan subgroup, and
  `leavitt-cone-half-carries-rigid-compression-defect` records how its candidates die.
  With `leavitt-st20-nonhyperlinear-iff-cone-bound-and-half-rounding` we get:

  ```text
  (HEC)  and  (HR)   =>   Delta = St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2)) is not hyperlinear,
  ```

  and `(HEC)` alone makes non-hyperlinearity of `Delta` equivalent to `(HR)`.
- **Class killed: mixed-compressor (Kun-Thom / OpenAI) certificates for (QC).**
  - *Members.* Every argument that bounds the head in genuine representations of `Gamma` through a rigid compression
    defect `[u c u^-1, l]`, with `(L, u)` a compression pair of `Delta` that is not `Delta`-conjugate into one
    half. This includes the nine-leaf configuration behind `openai-leavitt-unit-nonsofic` and every Kun-Thom
    configuration whose compressors are units `sum s_mu t_nu` mixing `s` and `t`.
  - *Invariant.* The Bass-Serre tree of `Gamma`, whose edge groups are finite. Infinite Kazhdan subgroups have a
    unique fixed vertex, and compressors must fix it.
  - *Death step.* Lifting the compressor relation `u L u^-1 <= L` to `Gamma` exactly. By (1) and (4) it holds only
    inside one half. Any other lift holds only modulo `K`, hence only approximately in `rho`. Then the step
    "`Ad rho(u)` preserves `rho(L)'`" is lost: Hilbert-Schmidt-small `Ad` defects give no operator-norm control
    (`hs-analogue-of-normal-kazhdan-criterion-is-false`).
  - *Survivors.* Exactly the half configurations of (HEC).
- **Where the K-essential content sits.** The compressor relation and property (T) are used inside `Delta_+`, where
  they hold exactly. `K` enters twice: through the centralizing relations `[s, c] ∈ K`, and through simplicity of
  `Delta` when the head is spread. This matches `leavitt-cuntz-cone-halves-have-rf-amalgam-cover` (4a), because `c`
  has to lie outside `Delta_+`.
