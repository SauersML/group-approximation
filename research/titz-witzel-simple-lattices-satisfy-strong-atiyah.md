**OPEN.**  Let `Gamma^_i^q` be the five simple finite-index subgroups of the
Titz Mite--Witzel lattices (`titz-witzel-simple-kazhdan-cat0-lattices-exist`).
They are finitely presented, torsion-free, simple, Kazhdan, of type F, and act
on exotic `C~_2` buildings.  The claim is that for each of them, and every
finite matrix `A` over `Qbar[Gamma^_i^q]`, `rk_(N(Gamma^_i^q))(A)` is an
integer.

**Why it is the gate.**
- It is implied by `master-host-satisfies-algebraic-strong-atiyah`, because
  Strong Atiyah passes to subgroups and the master host contains every finitely
  presented torsion-free group.  It is also implied by the root over `Qbar`.
  So one non-integral rank over one `Gamma^_i^q` refutes both.
- By `strong-atiyah-permanence-closure-omits-simple-kazhdan-groups`, no group
  containing `Gamma^_i^q` is obtained from amenable, residually finite or
  locally indicable groups by the operations:
  - subgroups;
  - directed colimits;
  - arbitrary extensions;
  - graphs of groups;
  - subdirect products;
  - marked limits.

  So every proof of the master-host statement must prove Strong Atiyah
  directly for some group containing `Gamma^_i^q`.
- Unlike `deligne-torsion-free-lattice-satisfies-strong-atiyah`, no piece of
  `Gamma^_i^q` already satisfies Strong Atiyah, because it has no pieces.

**Tools that die here** (details in `strong-atiyah-simple-kazhdan-atoms-proof`,
Section 5):
- Lück approximation and pro-`p` methods: no finite quotients.
- Crossed-product and locally indicable extension induction: no normal
  subgroups.
- Hughes-free division rings: perfect.
- Bass--Serre and Cohn-coproduct reductions: FA.
- Marked approximation: isolated.

**Surviving directions.**
- **Sofic approximation plus an integrality mechanism.**  Soficity of the
  smallest lattice is open: `titz-witzel-kernel-sofic` and its negation.
- **Building geometry.**  `Gamma_i^q` acts freely and cocompactly on a
  2-dimensional CAT(0) building.  For matrices supported on the cellular
  chain complex this gives the L²-Betti numbers `(0, 0, chi)`.  General
  matrices are not of this form.
- **Ring-theoretic structure of `Qbar[Gamma^_i^q]`.**  An example is a
  Sylvester rank function argument that does not use local indicability.

## Attempts

1. **Go down to Radu's sublattice and use local indicability (2026-09-17).**  *Dies* at a
   finite abelianization.  The gate node itself is FA and has no pieces.  So the first place
   where Bass--Serre tools come back is the torsion-free index-four subgroup
   `Λ_R = π_1(S_R) = ker(Γ_R → C_2 × C_2)` of Radu's lattice.  This subgroup embeds in the
   smallest kernel (`radu-bmw-lattice-embeds-in-titz-witzel-kernel`), so `SA(Γ_1^2)` implies
   `SA(Λ_R)`.  That makes `SA(Λ_R)` a necessary sub-gate, and it has tree splittings and a
   free-by-arithmetic structure.  The hope was to get it from the Jaikin-Zapirain--López-Álvarez
   theorem for locally indicable groups.  It fails:
   - **The presentation.**  `Λ_R = <F_1, F_2 | r_1, r_2>` with `F_1 = ba`, `F_2 = yx`, and
     - `r_1 = F_2 F_1^-1 F_2^-2 F_1^2 F_2^2 F_1^-1 F_2^-1 F_1`,
     - `r_2 = F_1 F_2^-2 F_1^-1 F_2 F_1 F_2^-1 F_1^-1 F_2^2 F_1`.

     Both relators have exponent sum `1` in `F_1` and `0` in `F_2`.  So `H_1(Λ_R) = Z`, generated by the image of `F_2`.
   - **The kernel of `Λ_R → Z`.**  Put `a_i = F_2^i F_1 F_2^-i`.  The kernel is generated by
     the `a_i`, and its relations include the conjugates
     - `a_(k+1)^-1 a_(k-1)^2 a_(k+1)^-1 a_k` (from `r_1`),
     - `a_k a_(k-2)^-1 a_(k-1) a_(k-2)^-1 a_k` (from `r_2`).
   - **The finite abelianization.**  So `H = <a_0, a_1, a_2, a_3>` is a quotient of the group
     `N_[0,3]` on those generators with the relations that involve only `a_0, ..., a_3`.  Its
     abelianization is `(Z/4)^2` (`N_[0,j]` has `(Z/4)^2` for `3 <= j <= 7`, and
     `N_[0,2]` has `Z ⊕ Z/4`).  So `H` is a nontrivial finitely generated subgroup with finite
     abelianization.  The argument does not need `N_[0,3] → Λ_R` to be injective.
   - **Consequence.**  `Λ_R` is not locally indicable, and neither is any group that contains
     it.  Locally indicable permanence never reaches `Λ_R`.  Neither does Lück approximation,
     because `Λ_R` is not residually finite.
   - **What is left for `SA(Λ_R)`.**
     - The graph of groups from `Γ_R = V *_(V_a) E_a`, restricted to `Λ_R`: free vertex
       groups over finite-index free edge groups.
     - The extension `1 → Λ_v ∩ Λ_R → Λ_R → ρ(Λ_R) → 1`.  Its kernel is free and its quotient
       is a finite-index subgroup of the cocompact arithmetic lattice of
       `radu-horizontal-quotient-is-arithmetic-lattice`.  No recorded permanence theorem crosses
       a free kernel under a non-amenable quotient.
   - **Checks.**  The relators are verified by normal forms in `Γ_R`, and the kernel relations
     by a free-group conjugacy check.  The abelianizations are Smith normal forms.  See
     `experiments/strong-atiyah-atoms-2026-09-17/`.
