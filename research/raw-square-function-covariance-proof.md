Apply `||[X,U]||_2<=2||X||_2` to each positive square `R_a^*R_a`, use
`||R_a^*R_a||_2<=||R_a||_op||R_a||_2<=2||R_a||_2`, average, and apply
Cauchy--Schwarz.  This gives `(RSC2)` for every unitary independently of any
symmetry of the residual list.
