Let `Γ` be generated by the finite set `E`.

1. **Overgroup.** `Δ = C(Γ)` is infinite, finitely generated and LEF, `Γ ≅ δ_0(Γ) ≤ [Δ,Δ]`, and `WP(Δ) ≡_T WP(Γ)`
   (`groups-embed-in-derived-subgroup-of-half-line-overgroup`,
   `half-line-overgroup-keeps-lef-and-solvable-word-problem` item 2). In the note this is Lemma C(a): a word in
   `β^{±1}, h_γ^{±1}` is trivial iff its `β`-exponent sum is `0` and the finitely many values of the level function,
   words in `E`, are trivial in `Γ`; an oracle for `WP(Γ)` answers the last question.
2. **Host.** `H = G_Δ = EL_3(LC(F_2^Δ,F_2) ⋊ Λ)` is infinite, finitely generated, simple, Kazhdan and an expander
   limit of finite simple groups `SL_{3N}(F_2)` (`lamplighter-bernoulli-crossed-products-simple-kazhdan-lef`,
   `lamplighter-host-is-limit-of-finite-simple-expanders`), and `ξ ↦ diag(u_ξ,1,1)` embeds `[Λ,Λ] ⊇ [Δ,Δ] ⊇ Γ`
   in `H` (Lemma B of the note).
3. **`WP(H) ≤_T WP(Δ)`.** An element `x ↦ δx + c` of `Λ` is a word for `δ` and words for the points of `supp c`, so an
   oracle for `WP(Δ)` decides equality in `Λ` and computes `c`. A word in the generators of `H` multiplies out to a
   matrix with entries `Σ_ξ p_ξ u_ξ`, each `p_ξ` a polynomial over `F_2` in finitely many coordinates `x(δ)`; after
   collecting equal `ξ` and equal coordinates with the oracle, the word is trivial iff every coefficient of its
   difference from `I_3` vanishes at every assignment, which is a finite check since `F_2^Δ` is the full shift
   (Lemma C(b) of the note; the same reduction is
   `crossed-product-el-word-problem-is-group-wp-plus-language` with the language of the full shift).
4. **`WP(Γ) ≤_m WP(H)`.** Substituting for each generator of `Γ` its word in the generators of `H` sends a word `w`
   to a word `ι(w)`, and `w = 1` in `Γ` iff `ι(w) = 1` in `H`, because the embedding is injective.

So `WP(Γ) ≤_m WP(H) ≤_T WP(Δ) ≤_T WP(Γ)`, and the two word problems have the same Turing degree. ∎

**Model tests.**
- `Γ` with solvable word problem: `H` has solvable word problem, the case of
  `lef-wp-groups-have-decidable-simple-kazhdan-lef-hosts`.
- `Γ = G_X` with `L(X)` of degree `d` (`subshift-elementary-group-word-problem-degree`): the host of `G_X` has word
  problem of degree `d`, consistent with `every-turing-degree-is-a-simple-kazhdan-lef-word-problem`.
- The reductions are Turing reductions, not many-one in both directions: `WP(H)` is not claimed to be computably
  enumerable when `WP(Γ)` is. Consistently, `H` is not recursively presented in general, since a finitely generated
  recursively presented simple group has solvable word problem.
