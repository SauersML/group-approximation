Let `Λ` be the Coxeter diagram of `A_Λ` (edges for `m_st ≥ 3`, with arbitrary labels). If `Λ`
is a complete bipartite graph `K_{m,n}`, then `A_Λ` satisfies the K(π,1) conjecture.

This includes the stars `K_{1,n}` with all labels 3. Before this theorem the K(π,1)
conjecture was known for these stars only for `n ≤ 4` (types `A_2, A_3, D_4, D̃_4`).
