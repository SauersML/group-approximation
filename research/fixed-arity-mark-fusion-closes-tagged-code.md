**INVALIDATED FIXED-FUSION ROUTE.**  Replace all pairwise mark seams in the
tagged sheared-diagonal diagram by one fixed ternary, or fixed higher-arity,
cell whose boundary is a product of conjugated marked involutions and the
one literal outer mark.

`fixed-arity-mark-fusion-has-finite-character-model` realizes every such
single product-fusion prototype in a finite character-permutation model.
The fused marks are genuinely distinct conjugates, the higher-arity boundary
is exact, and the marked carrier remains nonzero.  Thus non-pairwise fusion
is not load-bearing merely because it has arity at least three.  A viable
fusion row must contain additional nonabelian payload incidence which cannot
factor through a finite character packet.
