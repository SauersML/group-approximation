Norms `||·||_2` on `M_d` are normalized by `d`. All matrices below are unitaries or compressions of
unitaries, so every operator norm is at most 1. Two elementary facts are used throughout:

- **(P1)** If `||U_k||_op, ||V_k||_op ≤ 1`, then
  `||U_1⋯U_ℓ − V_1⋯V_ℓ||_2 ≤ Σ_k ||U_k − V_k||_2`, by telescoping and `||XYZ||_2 ≤ ||X||_op||Y||_2||Z||_op`.
  In particular, for unitaries, `||U_1⋯U_ℓ − I||_2 ≤ Σ_k ||U_k − I||_2`, and conjugation preserves
  `||·||_2`.
- **(P2)** Let `P : C^D → C^d` be a co-isometry, and let `A, B` have operator norm at most 1. Then
  `||P A P* P B P* − P A B P*||_{2,d} = ||P A (I − P*P) B P*||_{2,d} ≤ ((D−d)/d)^{1/2}`, since `I − P*P`
  is a projection of rank `D − d`. By induction, for unitaries `A_1, …, A_ℓ`,
  `||P A_1 P*⋯P A_ℓ P* − P A_1⋯A_ℓ P*||_{2,d} ≤ (ℓ−1)((D−d)/d)^{1/2}`.

**A presentation of `G`.**
- Let `S` be the disjoint union of the sets `X_0, X_1, X_2`.
- `R` consists of:
  - (R1) the words `x y z^{-1}` with `x, y, z` in one `X_i` and `xy = z` in `X_i`;
  - (R2) the words `ι_{e,i}(x) ι_{e,j}(x)^{-1}` for `x ∈ A_e`.
- Then `⟨S | R⟩` presents the colimit `G`. For each `g ∈ G` fix a word `w_g` in `S` representing `g`.

## (⇒) Stability implies (EM)

1. **Define the maps.** Let `ρ_{i,n}` be as in (EM). Put `τ_n(s) = ρ_{i,n}(s)` for `s ∈ X_i ⊂ S`, and
   extend to words. Set `σ_n(g) = τ_n(w_g)`.
2. **Relator defects.** Relators of type (R1) evaluate exactly to `I`, because each `ρ_{i,n}` is
   genuine. For (R2), `||τ_n(ι_{e,i}(x)) τ_n(ι_{e,j}(x))^{-1} − I||_2 = ||ρ_{i,n}(ι_{e,i}x) − ρ_{j,n}(ι_{e,j}x)||_2 → 0`.
3. **Asymptotic multiplicativity.** Fix `g, h`. The word `w_g w_h w_{gh}^{-1}` represents `1`. So in
   the free group on `S` it equals a fixed product `∏_{k≤m} u_k r_k^{±1} u_k^{-1}` with `r_k ∈ R`. By
   (P1),

   ```text
   ||σ_n(g)σ_n(h)σ_n(gh)^{-1} − I||_2 ≤ Σ_k ||τ_n(r_k) − I||_2 → 0.
   ```

   So `σ_n` is an asymptotic representation of `G`.
4. **Generators.** For `x ∈ X_i`, the word `w_x x^{-1}` also represents `1`. The same estimate gives
   `||σ_n(x) − ρ_{i,n}(x)||_2 → 0`.
5. **Apply stability.** It gives genuine `π_n` with `D_n/d_n → 1` and
   `||σ_n(g) − P_n π_n(g) P_n*||_2 → 0` for all `g`. With step 4, `||ρ_{i,n}(x) − P_n π_n(x) P_n*||_2 → 0`
   for `x ∈ X_i`. That is (EM).

## (⇐) (EM) implies stability

1. **Vertex asymptotic representations.** Let `σ_n : G → U(d_n)` be an asymptotic representation.
   For each `i`, the composite `φ_{i,n} : X_i → G → U(d_n)` is an asymptotic representation of the
   finite group `X_i`. Since `X_i` has finitely many pairs, the all-pairs defect
   `ε_{i,n} = max_{x,y ∈ X_i} ||φ_{i,n}(xy) − φ_{i,n}(x)φ_{i,n}(y)||_2` tends to `0`.
2. **Strict vertex rounding.**
   - `gowers-hatami-finite-group-hs-stability` gives flexible correction with universal constants
     once `ε_{i,n} < 1/16`. So every finite group is flexibly HS stable.
   - A finite group is amenable. So `amenable-flexible-hs-stability-is-strict-hs-stability` upgrades
     this to strict stability: there are genuine `ρ_{i,n} : X_i → U(d_n)`, on the same `C^{d_n}`, with
     `max_{x ∈ X_i} ||φ_{i,n}(x) − ρ_{i,n}(x)||_2 → 0`.
3. **Edge mismatches vanish.** For an edge `e = {i,j}` and `x ∈ A_e`, the elements `ι_{e,i}(x)` and
   `ι_{e,j}(x)` are equal in `G`, so `φ_{i,n}(ι_{e,i}x) = φ_{j,n}(ι_{e,j}x)`. By the triangle
   inequality, `||ρ_{i,n}(ι_{e,i}x) − ρ_{j,n}(ι_{e,j}x)||_2 → 0`.
4. **Apply (EM).** It gives genuine `π_n : G → U(D_n)`, `D_n/d_n → 1`, with
   `||ρ_{i,n}(x) − P_n π_n(x) P_n*||_2 → 0`. Hence `||σ_n(x) − P_n π_n(x) P_n*||_2 → 0` for every `x` in
   every `X_i`.
5. **All elements.** `G` is generated by the images of the `X_i`. Fix `g`, and write `g = x_1⋯x_ℓ`
   with each `x_k` in some `X_{i_k}`. Then

   ```text
   ||σ_n(g) − P_n π_n(g) P_n*||_2
     ≤ ||σ_n(g) − σ_n(x_1)⋯σ_n(x_ℓ)||_2                                   (→ 0: asymptotic multiplicativity, finitely many steps)
     + Σ_k ||σ_n(x_k) − P_n π_n(x_k) P_n*||_2                             (→ 0: step 4 and (P1))
     + ||P_n π_n(x_1) P_n*⋯P_n π_n(x_ℓ) P_n* − P_n π_n(g) P_n*||_2         (≤ (ℓ−1)((D_n−d_n)/d_n)^{1/2} → 0: (P2)).
   ```

   So `G` is flexibly HS stable. ∎

**Remarks.**
- The theorem needs no property (T), no developability, and no injectivity of the vertex groups
  into `G`.
- Property (T) enters `ghb7-flexibly-hs-stable` only through the descent to the congruence kernel.
