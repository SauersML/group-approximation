Citation import, read on 2026-09-13 from the PDF of arXiv:1607.03790v2, page 5:

"Lemma 1.11. [16, 5.6] If a group G has BVC, then any finite index subgroup also has BVC.
Combining this with the main result of [11], we have
Proposition 1.12. Virtually solvable groups have BVC if and only if they are virtually cyclic."

Here [11] is Groves–Wilson, Bull. London Math. Soc. 45 (2013) 89–92, and [16] is
Kochloukova–Martínez-Pérez–Nucinkis, Bull. London Math. Soc. 43 (2011). The KMN statement
behind 5.6 (subgroups of finite index of groups of Bredon type `FP_n` for the family of
virtually cyclic subgroups have the same type) and their Lemma 2.3 (Bredon type `FP_0` for
that family is exactly BVC) were read from the ar5iv rendering of arXiv:0903.4079. The
Groves–Wilson paper was not re-read.
