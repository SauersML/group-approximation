He--Tang--Xu--Zhang, *The nonseparable case of Kadison's problem on
orthonormal bases of unitaries for type II_1 factors*, arXiv:2608.18405v1,
Lemma 3.4, proves precisely `(RSN1)`.  The proof takes
`q=(1+r/||r||_infinity)/2`, finds an extreme point of the positive-contraction
slice preserving all constraints, uses the strict density gap to show that
extreme point is a projection, and sets `s=2p-1`.
