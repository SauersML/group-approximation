Let `P_f` be one forbidden selector carrier and let the four equal-rank V4
radical atoms be

```text
e_0,e_1,e_2,e_3,             sum_r e_r=P_f,
tau(e_r)=tau(P_f)/4.                                  (ESB1)
```

Choose the conventions of `(VOF2)`, after relabeling the three nonzero
radical atoms if necessary, so that

```text
F_00=e_0+e_1,          F_01=e_2,          F_11=e_0+e_3. (ESB2)
```

Tensor with a fixed three-bit bookkeeping PVM
`d_0,...,d_7`, with every atom of trace `1/8`, and define

```text
G_00=F_00 tensor d_0,
G_01=F_01 tensor (d_1+d_2),
G_11=F_11 tensor d_3,
C   =P_f  tensor d_4.                                  (ESB3)
```

Then the four displayed projections are pairwise orthogonal and

```text
tau(G_00)=tau(G_01)=tau(G_11)=tau(P_f)/16,
sum_b tau(G_b)=3 tau(P_f)/16=(3/2)tau(C).              (ESB4)
```

Every `G_b` is a subprojection of a genuine failure projection for its own
context.  In particular, unlike the tempting symmetrization
`F_01 -> F_01+e_0`, `(ESB3)` never declares the successful crossed atom
`e_0` to be a failure.  The price is only one fixed eight-dimensional
bookkeeping factor.

The three `G_b` are sums of exactly two equal-rank joint radical/book atoms.
Consequently a finite monomial group on the joint atom table contains an
`S_3` which permutes the three blocks transitively and fixes the four-atom
capacity block `C` setwise.  This is a fixed finite edge packet: after
finite-group normalized-HS exactification, the three actual failure channels
have one common scale and one symmetry orbit, independently of ambient
matrix dimension.

Three book bits are optimal among binary equal-atom implementations with a
capacity slice disjoint from all sources.  If each diagonal failure uses `r`
book atoms, equal rank forces the crossed failure to use `2r` atoms.  The two
diagonal sources, the crossed source, and one disjoint capacity slice
therefore require at least

```text
r+r+2r+1 >= 5                                             (ESB4a)
```

book atoms.  Two bits supply only four; three bits supply eight and `(ESB3)`
uses five of them.

Finally, for arbitrary unitaries `U_b` and pairwise orthogonal exits
`E_b<=C`, the projection trace inequality gives

```text
sum_b ||U_b G_b U_b^*-E_b||_2^2
 >= sum_b tau(G_b)-tau(C)
 =tau(P_f)/16.                                         (ESB5)
```

Thus the bookkeeping symmetrization retains a dimension-free contextual
payment.  It does not construct the exits: imposing them as literal
group-algebra conjugates would contradict the regular trace.  Its role is to
make all three **semantically correct** sources compatible with the one
holonomy already supplied by the induced `S_3` reset.
