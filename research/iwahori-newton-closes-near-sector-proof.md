The compatibility map has, at a compatible point, differential equal to the
Mayer--Vietoris mismatch map, whose right inverse is bounded by `1/sigma_0`
uniformly in dimension (infinitesimal rigidity); its second derivative is
bounded by a fixed constant because the relators are fixed words in
operator-norm-one unitaries.  Newton--Kantorovich converges from any `pi`
inside the tube of radius `sigma_0^2/(8L)` and lands on the compatible
locus within `(2/sigma_0) def(pi)`.
