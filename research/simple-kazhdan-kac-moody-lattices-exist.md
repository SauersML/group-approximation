**Literature fact (Caprace--Remy, *Simplicity and superrigidity of twin
building lattices*, Invent. Math. 176 (2009) 169--221, arXiv:math/0607664,
introduction, "Simple Kazhdan group corollary", read at source).**  Let
`A` be an `n x n` generalized Cartan matrix that is indecomposable, of
neither spherical nor affine type, and 2-spherical (every `2 x 2`
submatrix is of spherical type), and let `Lambda = G_A(F_q)` be the
(split, minimal) Kac--Moody group of Tits' functor over the finite field
`F_q`.  If `q > 1764^n`, then

```text
Lambda / Z(Lambda) is finitely presented, simple and Kazhdan,        (KM1)
```

and there are infinitely many isomorphism classes of infinite groups with
these three properties.  Caprace--Remy attribute the observation that these
are "the first infinite finitely presented discrete groups to be both
simple and Kazhdan" to Y. Shalom.  The simplicity theorem behind `(KM1)`
(same introduction): for `W` irreducible, infinite and non-affine and
`q >= |S|`, every finite-index subgroup of `Lambda` contains
`[Lambda, Lambda]`, which has finite index, and `[Lambda, Lambda]` modulo
its finite centre is simple.

**Explicit instance.**  The rank-three matrix

```text
A = [[ 2,  0, -1],
     [ 0,  2, -1],
     [-2, -3,  2]]                                                   (KM2)
```

has `a_12 a_21 = 0`, `a_13 a_31 = 2`, `a_23 a_32 = 3`, so its Weyl group is
the Coxeter triangle group with `(m_12, m_13, m_23) = (2, 4, 6)`: every
rank-two subdiagram is spherical (`A_1 x A_1`, `B_2`, `G_2`), the diagram
is connected, and `1/2 + 1/4 + 1/6 = 11/12 < 1` makes `W` an infinite
hyperbolic (hence non-affine) Coxeter group.  Thus for any prime power
`q > 1764^3`, `Lambda_(2,4,6)(q) := G_A(F_q) / Z` is an explicit infinite
finitely presented simple Kazhdan group; it is generated by finitely many
finite root subgroups (copies of `(F_q, +)`), so it is a quotient of a free
product of finite abelian groups.

**Consequences for this programme.**  Such `Lambda` has NO nontrivial
finite-dimensional unitary representation (a finitely generated linear
group is residually finite, and `Lambda` is simple and infinite), so every
subgroup is finite-dimensionally co-dense, and by
`non-rf-kazhdan-weak-ucp-iff-non-hyperlinear` its weak ucp-stability, its
flexible stability on hyperlinear approximations, and its
non-hyperlinearity are one and the same statement
(`kac-moody-lattice-is-non-hyperlinear-or-llp-failure`).

**Caution recorded from the same source.**  Caprace--Remy note that closed
strongly transitive automorphism groups of buildings "do not enjoy
Howe--Moore property in general" (any non-spherical proper parabolic is an
open subgroup neither compact nor of finite index).  So the far-defect
formula of the arithmetic Hecke lane (`hecke-far-commutator-defect-formula`),
which runs on Howe--Moore for the Schlichting completion, does NOT transfer
to commensurated pairs `(Lambda, Lambda_v)` of Kac--Moody lattices; the
Kac--Moody class is a candidate for the stability/LLP routes, not for the
relative-commutant-collapse route.
