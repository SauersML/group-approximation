Assume a scalar Gao witness exists.  Its carrier contains the diffuse
property-`(T)` factor `P=L(SL_3(Z))`.  The prerequisite supplies compact
completely positive maps converging pointwise on `P`.  Compose their
restrictions with the trace-preserving expectation `E_P:calB->P`.
The resulting compact completely positive maps on `P` converge pointwise
to the identity.  Property `(T)` of `P` upgrades convergence to uniform
convergence on its unit ball.  A compact operator cannot uniformly
approximate the identity on the unit ball of the infinite-dimensional
Hilbert space `L^2(P)`.  This is a contradiction, so no scalar Gao witness
exists.
