## Direct proof

Because `P` is torsion-free and `u != 1`, `u` has infinite order.  In
`B=P times <z>`, an equality `u^m z^n=1` projects to `u^m=1` in `P` and
to `z^n=1` in the second factor.  Hence `m=n=0`, so
`A=<u,z> ~= Z^2`.

On exponent vectors the map `phi` is the unimodular shear

```text
(m,n) |-> (m,m+n),
```

with inverse `(m,n) |-> (m,n-m)`.  It is therefore an automorphism of
`A`.  Britton's lemma embeds the base `B` into `H=HNN(B,A,phi)`.  In
particular the image of `z` retains infinite order.  The established
`hnn-torsion-theorem` applies to the torsion-free base `B` and makes `H`
torsion-free.  Since `P` is finitely presented and `A` is generated by
`u,z`, adjoining `z,s` and the two displayed HNN relations gives a finite
presentation of `H`.

The element `z` commutes with `P` in the direct-product base and commutes
with `s` by the second HNN relation, so it is central in the group generated
by `P,z,s`, namely all of `H`.  Finally the first HNN relation gives

```text
(s u s^-1) u^-1 = (u z)u^-1 = z,
```

because `u` and `z` commute in `B`.  The left side belongs to the normal
closure of `u`, proving the claimed promotion.
