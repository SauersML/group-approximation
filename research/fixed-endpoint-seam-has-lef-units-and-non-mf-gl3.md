For the shift on Z union its two fixed endpoints, over F2, the ring is
not directly finite but its unit group is GL_fin(Z,F2) semidirect Z,
which is LEF. Its GL_3 is not MF and has the full finitary kernel as
MF radical. This rules out an unrestricted rank-one reformulation for
these dynamical rings, but does not decide GL_2 or EL_3.
