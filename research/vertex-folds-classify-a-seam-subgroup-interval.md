With a common minimal endpoint system and common seam point, the
folded graph group embeds as the preimage of the corresponding block
diagonal subgroup of `Q^V`. All subgroups between the fully diagonal
LEF subgroup `H` and `G_D` arise uniquely this way. The interval is
the dual partition lattice. For nested partitions, the induced MF
reflection map has kernel exactly the product of edge factors that
lose their cycles in the larger group.
