Let `G` be countable and let `z in G` have finite order.  Fix finite word
data with the following **authentication property**.  For every norm-matrix-
corona homomorphism

```text
rho:G->U(Q_d)
```

with `rho(z)!=1`, after passing to an infinite coordinate subsequence there
are nonzero projections `P_n`, lifting one fixed nontrivial spectral atom of
the finite-order unitary `rho(z)`, such that:

1. `P_n` is invariant under every word in one fixed finite obstruction;
2. on `P_n C^(d_n)`, fixed approximate packet relations exactify in operator
   norm to one fixed semisimple packet;
3. fixed transport words authenticate a finite permutation-holonomy network
   with group `H` on the packet sectors;
4. fixed selector words have demanded source-rank vector `r` and target
   fingerprint map `L`;
5. every packet, transport, and selector defect tends to zero in operator
   norm.

If

```text
r notin P=L(Delta^H),                                  (CLM1)
```

then

```text
z in Rad_MF(G).                                        (CLM2)
```

If `z` normally generates `G`, then `Rad_MF(G)=G`.

No positive ambient density of `P_n` is required.  Normalize trace inside
the nonzero corner `P_n M_(d_n)(C)P_n`; there

```text
||X||_(2,P_n) <= ||X||_op.                             (CLM3)
```

If `epsilon=2 dist_infinity(r,P)>0`, at least one selector row has
corner Hilbert--Schmidt defect at least `epsilon`, hence operator defect at
least `epsilon`, contradicting clause 5.  A rational separator gives the
corresponding weighted explicit gap.  Tensor spectators and direct-sum
mixing cannot dilute the conclusion because both are already absorbed by
the rank polytope.

This is a theorem schema, not an authentication theorem for any particular
presentation.  In particular it does **not** establish
`binary-leavitt-two-shared-gauge-selector-compiler`: the native common
carrier, cross-occurrence holonomy, and selector words remain that node's
open construction-specific obligations.
