Let `B` be exact and let

```text
0=I_0 subset I_1 subset ... subset I_m=A
```

be a finite chain of closed ideals.  If
`(I_k/I_{k-1}) tensor_min B` is pure for every `k`, then
`A tensor_min B` is pure.
