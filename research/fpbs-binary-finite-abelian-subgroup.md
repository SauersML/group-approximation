title: Every infinite binary-finite group contains an infinite abelian subgroup
---

Section 5. A binary-finite group satisfies the Shunkov normalizer-quotient condition because every pair of quotient elements lifts to a finite two-generated subgroup. If all abelian subgroups were finite, the descending chain condition and Suchkova-Shunkov would force an infinite Chernikov group without an infinite abelian subgroup, a contradiction. Local finiteness of the whole group is NOT claimed.
