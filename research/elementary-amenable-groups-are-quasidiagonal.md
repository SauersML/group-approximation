**ESTABLISHED (literature).**  For every countable elementary amenable group
`G`, the full group C-star algebra `C*(G)` is quasidiagonal.

Consequently `C*(G)` is MF, and `G` embeds in the unitary group of a
sequential norm matrix corona: `G` is an MF group.  In particular every
countable solvable group is MF.

This is used here only as an off-the-shelf source of MF ambient groups for
countermodels.  Nothing in the program's own analytic machinery is involved,
which is the point: an obstruction that survives inside such a group cannot
be blamed on an exotic ambient approximation.
