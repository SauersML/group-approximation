Set every error in `(BFE1)--(BFE4)` equal to zero and apply the inequalities
in `L(Gamma)`.  Equations `(BFE1)--(BFE2)` make the active traces
nondecreasing.  Equation `(BFE3)` charges at least `eta tau(q_0)` in every
first-hit layer, whereas `(BFE4)` gives total trace at most one.  Letting the
number of layers tend to infinity forces `tau(q_0)=0`.

The canonical trace on `L(Gamma)` is faithful.  A nonidentity group word is
`sqrt(2)` from the identity there, so any trace-functorial lower coupling from
that word to `q_0` contradicts the vanishing conclusion.
