**ESTABLISHED LITERAL-GHOST NO-GO.**  Let `H_1,H_2` be finite elementary
abelian subgroups of a group `Gamma`, and let

```text
e_(H_i,chi_i)=|H_i|^(-1) sum_(h in H_i) chi_i(h) h       (LCG1)
```

be rank-one character idempotents in `C[Gamma]`.  If

```text
e_(H_1,chi_1)=e_(H_2,chi_2),                             (LCG2)
```

then `H_1=H_2` and `chi_1=chi_2` under that identification.

Consequently, if `H_i` is the commuting involution group of context `i`,
literal identification of their forbidden one-ghost assignment projections
(or of their complements) forces the two full context subgroups to coincide.
All variables from both contexts then lie in one elementary abelian group
and commute.  Repeating this over a connected context-overlap graph
classicalizes the entire observable family.

Thus the factor-`m` gain in `common-meet-local-sectors-pay-one-deficit`
cannot be obtained by simply amalgamating the natural rank-one affine ghost
idempotents.  A viable shared-deficit groupifier needs a higher-rank common
rejection sector, a nonliteral overlap with a controlled meet, or another
packet whose common projection does not have full group-basis support in
each context algebra.
