OPEN.  Put the finite menu of symmetric Fanizza hard frames and mixed-CNOT
router packets into the local finite group `H` of
`thompson-site-pulses-localize-hard-exits`.  Identify the level-`n` source
hard word with `A_n`, its fresh target sign with `B_n`, and its transporter
with `s_n`, while preserving the original logical BCS words and the active
recurrence carrier.  Prove that all identifications fall into finitely many
bounded-arity `V`-orbits and that the packet exactifications align with the
pulse prefix at polynomial cost.

The desired conclusion is that the analytically projected joint exit `P_n`
obeys `(TSP6)` at every recurrence level.  Then `(TSP7)--(TSP8)` place it in
the summable first-hit sector `X_n`, removing
`e5-tail-character-suppression-for-projected-exits` from this route.

## Attempts

- A separate independent lamp pulse has the correct trace budget but is
  useless unless the literal packet hard sign is identified with its local
  source factor; this claim records that finite incidence obligation.
- Identifying original BCS variables across all sites would add forbidden
  cross-context commutations.  Only private packet/frame words may be
  site-local; shared logical words must remain the existing recurrence words.
- The finite packet menu makes the number of local roles finite, but the
  active-return projection is analytic rather than a group word.  Its
  approximate reduction and placement must use the established packet
  complement estimates, not a universal projection relation.
