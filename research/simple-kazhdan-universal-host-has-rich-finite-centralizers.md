There is a fixed infinite simple Kazhdan LEF group `S`, generated by
eighteen involutions, with the universal locally finite core
`D=lim_j SL_(3j!)(F_2)` from the universal-odometer construction, such that
for every finite `F <= D` and positive `r` there is an infinite simple
Kazhdan LEF group `H` containing every countable locally finite group
and an embedding

`F x (H^r semidirect Sym(r)) -> S`

restricting to the prescribed inclusion on `F`. The symmetric group acts
by permuting factors. In particular `C_S(F)` contains such an `H`.
This is asserted for finite subgroups of the specified core `D`, not
arbitrary finite subgroups of `S`; `H` may depend on `F,r`.
