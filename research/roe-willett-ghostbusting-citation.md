**Established by citation.** John Roe and Rufus Willett,
[*Ghostbusting and property
A*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2013.07.004), Journal of Functional
Analysis 266 (2014), 1674--1684; see also
[arXiv:1303.6532](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.6532).  Their main theorem says
that a bounded-geometry discrete metric space `X` has property A if and only
if every ghost operator in its uniform Roe algebra is compact.

Since every compact operator is a ghost and lies in `C*_u(X)`, the stated
ideal equality follows.
