For every group G, integer q>=2, and finite positive track count N,
put R=Z/qZ. Let H:(R^N)^G -> (R^N)^G have coordinate rules

    H_i(x)(g)=epsilon_i x_i(g)+B_i((x_j)_(j!=i))(g),

where epsilon_i is a constant unit of R and B_i uses no value of
track i. Suppose every B_i is a finite sum of products of at most
three unary lookup factors. If H is injective, then H is surjective.

This claim remains open. Stable changes put every finite-alphabet
CA into this class, as proved in the linked artifacts. Even the
special coefficients +1 and -1 suffice for that reduction.
Bijectivity in each own track with the others fixed is not a
proved substitute for a known bijective invariant fiber.
