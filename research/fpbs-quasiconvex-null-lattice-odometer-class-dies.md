**Why this matters for the flagship.** For a cocompact `Sp(n,1)` lattice `Gamma` (property (T); fixed price open), the BB route needs an amenable ergodic PDR imp action whose boundary
class passes every filter. The odometer line on `F_2` produced a class passing the Dini, shadow and quasiconvex-null
filters. The step named by `fpbs-match-rigid-ray-codes-die-on-hyperbolic-groups` was to transplant it to `Gamma` and
decide (MR). This claim does it.
- **Positive half.** The quasiconvex-null filter, the one filter the `F_2` class was built for, is passed on every
  torsion-free nonelementary hyperbolic group. This settles the general case left open by
  `fpbs-quasiconvex-null-small-classes-exist-on-free-boundary`, for that filter.
- **Negative half.** The class is match-rigid, so it dies. The lattice odometer transplant with transverse insertions
  is a dead end for BB certificates.

Together with `fpbs-transverse-skeleton-ray-codes-are-match-rigid`, the surviving odometer-type candidates must have
off-orbit matches, produced by insertions that fellow-travel cosets of the skeleton on unbounded lengths.

## Theorem

Let `Gamma` be torsion-free, nonelementary and hyperbolic.

**Theorem 3 (transverse killing elements).**
- There are a malnormal quasiconvex free subgroup `F = <a, b>` and `c ∈ Gamma` such that no nontrivial power of `c` is
  conjugate into `F`. Take `F_0 = <x, y>` malnormal quasiconvex free (Kapovich), `F = <y, yxxyx>` and `c = x`.
- Let `S ⊂ Gamma \ <c>` be finite with `S ∪ {c}` generating `Gamma`. There is `m_0` such that for
  `m >= m_0` the semigroup `W` generated by `A_m = {c^m, c^{m+1}} ∪ {c^m gamma c^m : gamma ∈ S}` has the following
  properties:
  1. `W` generates `Gamma` as a group;
  2. every element of `W^±` satisfies (I1) of the skeleton claim, with a function `D` independent of `m`;
  3. `F s F ≠ F s^{-1} F` for all `s ∈ W`.
- **Semigroup Neumann lemma.** `W` is contained in no finite union `∪_i B_{R_i} K_i B_{R_i}` of double neighbourhoods
  of infinite-index quasiconvex subgroups `K_i`.

Consequently there are `s_1, s_2, ... ∈ W` with:
- `|s_j| > |s_{j-1}| + K_1` (with `K_1` from the proof), so the double cosets `F s_j^{±1} F` are pairwise distinct;
- `s_j ∉ B_{R_i} H_i B_{R_i}` for `i <= j`, where `(H_i, R_i)` enumerates all pairs of an infinite-index quasiconvex
  subgroup and an integer.

**Theorem 4 (the lattice odometer class).** On the dyadic odometer `Omega = lim Z/2^{n+2}`, take the symmetric
paperfolding letters of Step 2 of `fpbs-quasiconvex-null-small-classes-exist-on-free-boundary-proof`, before its
overwrites, as `F`-letters. Put `G(y) = s_j^{±1}` at `y ≡ ±r_j mod q_{n_j}`, with `r_j = q_{n_j}/8 + 1`, sparse
stages `n_j` and `n_1` large. Then:
1. `G` is a symmetric skeleton code with (I1)--(I3). Its forward coding is injective, since the positions of `s_j`
   read off `y mod q_{n_j}`.
2. `nu = sum_g c_g g_* zeta_* m_Omega` is ergodic, nonatomic and **quasiconvex-null**: `nu(Gamma Lambda_H) = 0` for
   every infinite-index quasiconvex `H`. In fact `zeta(x) ∉ Gamma Lambda_H` for every `x`.
3. (R), (MR) and (MR±) hold. By Theorem 1 of `fpbs-match-rigid-ray-codes-die-on-hyperbolic-groups`, `nu`, its
   Maharam extension and every imp action over it have totally dissipative diagonal. None is a BB boundary class.

For a cocompact `Sp(n,1)` lattice, pass to a torsion-free finite-index subgroup (Selberg). Fixed price is invariant
under finite index, and cocompact rank-one lattices are hyperbolic.

## Calibration

- **`F_2`.** With `Gamma = F_0 = F_2`, Theorem 4 gives a second quasiconvex-null odometer class on `F_2`, with
  single-letter insertions from `W` in place of killing words. It dies, consistent with cost 2 and with
  `fpbs-odometer-killing-class-dies-by-support-rigidity`.
- **Script.** `malnormal_search.py` finds `F = <y, yxxyx>` among the first 767 pairs of short words. It checks:
  - the Stallings core graph (4 vertices, 5 edges, rank 2);
  - malnormality, by the Kapovich--Myasnikov fibre-product criterion (every non-diagonal component is a tree);
  - that there is no `x`-labelled cycle.

  The controls `<x, yxY>`, `<xx, yy>` and `<x, yy>` are correctly reported non-malnormal.
- **Lebesgue.** The class is carried by rays that leave every coset of `F` after bounded time. A Lebesgue-typical ray
  of a surface group does not, so there is no conflict with Hopf--Tsuji--Sullivan.

## What this leaves (named open statement)

**Off-orbit matches on a lattice.** Construct, on a torsion-free cocompact `Sp(n,1)` lattice, a quasiconvex-null
ray code with a positive-measure set of off-orbit matches `g zeta(x) = zeta(x')`, `x' ∉ x + Z`. Or prove that every
quasiconvex-null class on such a lattice with conservative diagonal must fail the Dini or shadow filter. By this claim
and `fpbs-transverse-skeleton-ray-codes-are-match-rigid`, off-orbit matches are necessary for any ray-code certificate,
and for skeleton codes they require insertions fellow-travelling skeleton cosets on unbounded lengths.
