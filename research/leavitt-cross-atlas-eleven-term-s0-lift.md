Use the twelve adjacent-transvection atlas generators `p_0,...,p_11`, with
`p_0,...,p_5` in chart one and `p_6,...,p_11` in chart two.  In the group
algebra `F_2[Q]`, let `A` be the sum of the group elements represented by

```text
p0 p1 p2,       p3 p5 p4,       p6 p7 p8,       p9 p11 p10,
p1 p0,          p6 p8,          p0 p2 p9,       p3 p1 p7,
p5 p3 p1,       p5 p3 p4,       p5 p11 p10.                    (ELA1)
```

Then all eleven group elements are distinct and

```text
evaluation(A)=s_0.                                      (ELA2)
```

The first four terms are the generating set of
`binary-leavitt-unit-group-four-generated`.  Hence the subgroup generated by
the support of `A` is the entire nonsofic group `Q`.  This removes the fatal
virtually-free localization of the support-three lift.

There is also an exact bounded minimum statement.  Fix the first four terms
of `(ELA1)` and allow correction terms from the radius-three Cayley ball in
the twelve adjacent transvections.  Among all odd corrections evaluating to
the required residual, the minimum support is seven; consequently eleven is
the minimum total support in this anchored radius-three universe.

The upper bound is `(ELA1)`.  For the lower bound, parity leaves correction
weights `1,3,5` below seven.  The exact pair/triple meet-in-the-middle program
`experiments/kaplansky_minimum_lift_certificate.py` exhausts all such subsets
and finds none.  It uses the canonical binary-prefix normal form, so both the
equality and the nonexistence calculation are exact rather than numerical.

No left inverse is asserted.  Exact Gaussian elimination finds none supported
in the radius-four ball, and this finite fence must not be promoted to a
global no-go.
