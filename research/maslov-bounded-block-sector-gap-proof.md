Write the lifted finite presentation as generators `S` together with the
central generator `z`, and a finite relator list `R`.  Substitute
`z = omega I_k` and, on the compact space `U(k)^S`, define

```text
F_k(V) = sum_(r in R) ||r(V,omega I_k)-I_k||_(2,k)^2.       (BBP1)
```

This is continuous.  It has no zero: a zero would be an exact
finite-dimensional unitary representation of `E_3` sending `z` to
`omega I_k`, whereas `deligne-triple-cover-fd-central-invisibility` says
that every such representation kills `z`.  Compactness therefore gives

```text
mu_k = min_(V in U(k)^S) F_k(V) > 0.                       (BBP2)
```

Fix `K` and set `c_K^2 = min_(1 <= k <= K) mu_k > 0`.
Suppose `U` has common invariant blocks `V_j` of dimensions `k_j`.  Relator
words preserve those blocks, and normalized Hilbert--Schmidt squares add
with dimension weights:

```text
sum_r ||r(U)-I||_(2,d)^2
 = sum_j (k_j/d) F_(k_j)(V_j)
 >= c_K^2 sum_(j:k_j<=K) k_j/d.                            (BBP3)
```

This is `(BBG1)`.  If every relator defect tends to zero, its left side tends
to zero, hence the mass in blocks of size at most `K` tends to zero.
Decomposing the finite-dimensional C*-algebra generated by each tuple into
irreducible common invariant blocks gives the stated packet-size
consequence.  Notice that the constants may decay with `K`; compactness at
fixed size does not supply the all-dimensional gap in
`maslov-mod3-projective-defect-gap`.
