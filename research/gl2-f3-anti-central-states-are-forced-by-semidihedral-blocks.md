Let `R = L_(F_3)(1,2)`, `G = R^x`, `z = -1`, `S_- = eps_- F_3[G]`, `u = [eps_-]`, `H = E x| V`, `alpha(g) = (g^*)^(-1)`,
and `trl`, `H_fin` as in `anti-central-state-exists-iff-lifted-trace-relatively-positive`. Let `GL = GL_2(F_3)` be the
constant units, `SD = <w, d, h>` its semidihedral Sylow subgroup, and `c_1`, `c_2` the evaluation and twisted blocks of
`eps_- F_3[SD]`, with primitive idempotents `q_1`, `q_2`.

1. `eps_- F_3[GL]` has exactly two simple modules, the natural module `V` and `V (x) det`. Their projective covers are
   `Ind_SD^GL (q_1 F_3[SD])` and `Ind_SD^GL (q_2 F_3[SD])`, of dimension six. So induction
   `K_0(eps_- F_3[SD]) -> K_0(eps_- F_3[GL])` is an isomorphism, and `H_fin(GL) = H_fin(SD)` is generated by `[q_1]`
   and `[q_2]`, each of lifted trace `1/4`.
2. Every `alpha_*`-invariant state on `(K_0(S_-), u)` equals `trl` on `H_fin(GL)`.
3. `trl >= 0` on `(H_fin(H) + H_fin(GL)) ∩ K_0^+` iff `trl >= 0` on `H_fin(H) ∩ K_0^+`. So a stable relation
   `P (+) S_-^r ~= Q (+) X (+) S_-^r` with `trl(P) < trl(Q)`, among projectives induced from finite subgroups of `H`
   and `GL`, exists iff one exists among projectives induced from `H` alone.
4. `H` and `GL` generate `G`. So positivity on the projectives of every finite subgroup of the group they generate is
   positivity on `H_fin(G)`, which implies `anti-central-lifted-trace-is-relatively-positive` rather than being a case
   of it.

Also `c_1 = k_+([hw]) eps_-`, where `k_+` is the idempotent of `F_3[x]/(x^4 + 1)` belonging to `x^2 + x - 1`. Part 1
corrects the last clause of Remark 3.3 of `hadamard-spectral-conjugacy-and-forced-states-2026-09-12.md`: the modular
blocks of `GL_2(F_3)` carry no split beyond `t = s(c_1)`. No trace-lowering relation and no one-sided pair is
constructed.
