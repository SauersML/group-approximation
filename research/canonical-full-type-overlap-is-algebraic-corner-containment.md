Let `q,r` be projections in `C[Gamma]` and `u in Gamma`.  For the canonical
group trace, `tau(q u^* r u q)=tau(q)` holds if and only if
`u q u^* <= r` in `C[Gamma]`.  Full canonical overlap is therefore literal
algebraic corner containment; merely positive overlap is insufficient.
