Direct proof in artifact §1, Steps 1–5. It imports only EJZ (Invent. Math. 179 (2010), Theorem 1.1: `EL_n` of a finitely generated associative unital ring has (T) for `n ≥ 3`) and the simplicity of `PSL_d(F_2)` for `d ≥ 3`.
- **Step 1.** `R` is generated by `u_t^(±1)` and the letter indicators, and the elementary identities give finite generation of `EL_n(R)`.
- **Step 2.** `R` is simple (a clopen `U` separated from its translates by the support of `r`, then minimality) and `Z(R) = F_2`, so `Z(EL_n(R)) = 1`.
- **Step 3.** Finite models: `Σ f_γ u_γ ↦ Σ D_Y(f_γ) P_γ` on `F_2^Y`.
  - It preserves listed sums and products because windows lie in `F`.
  - It is injective on the list, since every `F`-pattern of `X` occurs in `Y` and no nontrivial `γ^(-1)γ'` fixes a point of `Y`.
  - The no-fixed-point condition comes from the compactness fact: for `g ≠ e` the clopen sets `{x(h) ≠ x(hg)}` cover `X`.
- **Step 4.** Towers `E_(γδ)(W) = e_(γW) u_(γδ^(-1))` multiply as matrix units when the `γU` are disjoint; left and right absorption.
- **Step 5.** Extraction as in the manuscript, with `U = C` (no translate), partition condition `B_(2w+2)`, and `d = n|B_(w+1)| ≥ 3`.
