## Setting

- `F` is any field, `J = F<S,T | TS = 1>`, `I = (1 - ST)`, and `R = F[x,x^-1]`.
- `rho : M_N(J) -> M_N(R)` is the symbol map. Its kernel `M_N(I)` is exactly the set of finite-rank elements of
  `M_N(J)` acting on `V = F[x]^N`.
- For a subgroup `Delta <= GL_N(J)`:
  - `A(Delta)` is its commutant in `M_N(J)`, so `C_(GL_N(J))(Delta) = A(Delta)^x`;
  - `C_R(Delta)` is the commutant of `rho(Delta)` in `M_N(R)`;
  - `M_N(I)^Delta = A(Delta) cap M_N(I)` is the space of finite-rank intertwiners.
- `Delta` acts by conjugation on `M_N(I)`. `delta_Delta : C_R(Delta) -> H^1(Delta, M_N(I))` is the lifting class:
  lift `xi` to any `X` with `rho(X) = xi`, and take the cocycle `gamma |-> gamma X gamma^-1 - X`.

## Theorem (commutant growth splits)

Let `Gamma <= GL_N(J)` be any subgroup and `g in GL_N(J)` with `Gamma' = g Gamma g^-1 <= Gamma`.

1. **Symbol rigidity.** `rho(g) C_R(Gamma) rho(g)^-1 = C_R(Gamma) = C_R(Gamma')`.
2. **Lifting sequence.** For `Delta` equal to `Gamma` or `Gamma'`, the sequence
   `0 -> M_N(I)^Delta -> A(Delta) -> C_R(Gamma) -> H^1(Delta, M_N(I))` is exact, with last map `delta_Delta`.
   Moreover `delta_(Gamma') = res o delta_Gamma`.
3. **Splitting.** `A(Gamma) <= A(Gamma') = g A(Gamma) g^-1`, and there is an exact sequence
   ```text
   0 -> M_N(I)^(Gamma') / M_N(I)^Gamma -> A(Gamma') / A(Gamma) -> B(g) -> 0,
   B(g) = ker(res o delta_Gamma) / ker(delta_Gamma)  ~=  im(delta_Gamma) cap ker(res : H^1(Gamma) -> H^1(Gamma')).
   ```
   Call the left term **type (a)** (finitary) growth and `B(g)` **type (b)** (lifting) growth.

## Corollary 1 (a defect needs infinite growth)

Let `Gamma <= G <= H <= GL_N(J)`, with `Gamma` infranormal in `G` and `G` Kazhdan. More generally, it is enough that
the group `D_G` generated by the compressors of `Gamma` in `G` has no nonzero homomorphism to `Z`.

Suppose every compressor `g` of `Gamma` in `G` has `dim A(g Gamma g^-1)/A(Gamma) < infinity`. Then:
- `D_G` normalizes `A(Gamma)` (and `D_G = G` in the infranormal case);
- the rigid compression defect is trivial, meaning `[g z g^-1, gamma] = 1` for all `g in D_G`, `z in C_H(Gamma)` and
  `gamma in Gamma`.

So a nontrivial rigid defect inside `GL_N(J_F)`, which is what `DD(sofic)` or `DD(hyperlinear)` would need to certify
anything, forces some compressor with infinite-dimensional growth of type (a) or of type (b).

## Corollary 2 (polynomial-elementary subgroups have scalar centralizers)

Suppose `N >= 2` and `Gamma` contains `x E_N(F[p]) x^-1` for some `x in GL_N(J)` and some `p in {S, T}`. Then
`C_(GL_N(J))(Gamma) = F^x`. So every compression defect of `Gamma`, in every `H <= GL_N(J)`, is trivial for every
`g`, with no Kazhdan hypothesis.

For `N >= 3`, `E_N(F[S])` and `E_N(F[T])` are isomorphic to `E_N(F[y])`. For finite `F` these are Kazhdan.
`E_N(F[T])` preserves every finite-dimensional space `V_(<= d)` of vectors of degree at most `d`, so the class
includes Kazhdan subgroups whose action on `V` is locally finite.

## Calibration: the corner compressor

Take `Gamma = E_m(J) + 1` in `GL_(m+n)(J)` with `m >= 3`, and the bilateral shift `u` of the corner blocks. Then:
- `B(u) = 0`;
- type (a) is infinite-dimensional: it contains all finite-rank maps between `Q V_1` and `V_2` in both directions.

So all growth of the corner compressor is finitary, and it comes from the growth of `Fix`. This is the same
phenomenon that FI measures by an index.

## Obstruction recorded (class killed)

- **Class.** Every "commutant index" certificate that tries to certify or kill a rigid defect inside `GL_N(J)` by the
  relative dimension of `A(Gamma)`.
- **Invariant.** The pair `(M_N(I)^Gamma, im delta_Gamma)`.
- **Death step.** The index exists only when both growth types are finite-dimensional. Then Corollary 1 makes it a
  homomorphism, and property (T) kills it. So the certificate never fires, and the defect is trivial.
- **Unconditional statement.** The naive lemma "`A(Gamma)` has finite codimension in `A(g Gamma g^-1)`" is false:
  the corner gives a counterexample, of type (a).

## What is left (OPEN)

- Under MC-rigidity (`g Mov = Mov`, `g Fix = Fix`, which is automatic on Kazhdan hulls), can type (a) growth still
  be infinite-dimensional?
- Can type (b) growth be nonzero at all? This needs a class in `H^1(Gamma, M_N(I))` that is lifted from the symbol
  commutant and dies on `g Gamma g^-1`.
- A negative answer to both, for Kazhdan `Gamma`, kills `DD` on all of `GL_N(J_F)`.
- A Kazhdan infranormal pair with infinite growth of type (a) or (b) is the only shape of a nontrivial rigid defect
  left in `GL_N(J_F)`.
