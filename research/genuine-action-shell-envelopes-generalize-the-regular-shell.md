## Statement

Let `P` be an infinite group generated by a finite set `S`, and let
`rho: P -> Sym(N)` be a homomorphism such that every `g != 1` moves infinitely
many points (equivalently, the induced near action is faithful). Transfer `rho`
to the even integers, fixing the odd ones: `rho'_g(2n) = 2 rho_g(n)`,
`rho'_g(2n+1) = 2n+1`. On `C = {0,1}^N` put `p = 0^∞` and

    hat(g)(p) = p,     hat(g)(0^n 1 z) = 0^(rho'_g(n)) 1 z.

Then:

1. `g -> hat(g)` is an injective homomorphism into `Homeo(C)`, and
   `E_rho = <V, hat(P)>` is a finitely generated finite germ extension of `V`
   with singular set `Omega = V·p`.
2. The isotropy germ group at `p` is `Q_rho = <germs of rho'(P), tau>`, with
   `tau: n -> n+1` the cofinite shift, and `(V)_p = <tau>`. The near group is
   `R_rho = <rho(P), s> <= NearSym(N)`, `s: n -> n+1`, with near index `eta`.
3. The statements of `shell-germ-group-has-index-two-fiber-product`,
   `shell-germ-fp-is-an-ascending-hnn-gate` and `shell-envelope-fp-forces-fp-germ-group`
   hold with `(rho, Q_rho, R_rho)` in place of the regular data.
4. Every `S(M,F)` with `M ⊆ F ⊆ Omega` finite, and every finite pointwise
   stabilizer of `E_rho` on `Omega`, is finitely generated, as in
   `shell-finite-point-stabilizers-are-finitely-generated`.
5. Hence if `E_rho` is finitely presented, the twisted Brin–Thompson criterion
   (`twisted-brin-thompson-finite-presentation-criterion`) embeds `E_rho`, and so
   `P`, in a finitely presented simple group, exactly as in
   `boone-higman-via-fp-shell-envelopes`.

## What does not transfer

These nodes use the regular action and are not claimed for general `rho`:
- `one-ended-shell-inputs-admit-no-shift-normalizing-enumeration` and
  `accessible-shell-inputs-admit-no-shift-normalizing-enumeration` (window
  `N >= 1` forced). They fail for general `rho`: by
  `eventually-periodic-genuine-shell-actions-give-fp-envelopes`, the one-ended
  input `Z^2` has a genuine action with `s^-1 rho(P) s = rho(P)` near infinity
  (window `N = 0`).
- The "exactly virtually cyclic" half of `shell-prefix-core-detects-finite-ray-enumerations`.
  For genuine actions the corresponding class is exactly the virtually abelian inputs.

## Scope

Nothing here makes any `E_rho` finitely presented for an input that is not
virtually abelian. The extra freedom is in the choice of `rho`; the open
universal step is recorded as `decidable-inputs-have-fp-genuine-action-shell-envelopes`.
