title: A group with property BVC has finitely many conjugacy classes of finite subgroups, hence bounded orders of finite subgroups
---

If `G` has property BVC, then `G` has only finitely many conjugacy classes of finite subgroups.
In particular there is a bound `c` with `|F| <= c` for every finite subgroup `F <= G`.

Source: von Puttkamer–Wu, arXiv:1607.03790v2, Lemma 1.7 (page 4). Reason: every finite subgroup
is conjugate into a witness `V_i`, and a virtually cyclic group has only finitely many finite
subgroups up to conjugacy.
