**INVALIDATED NATIVE-WEYL CLOSURE.**  Expand the root-position conjugator on
the literal external-return coordinate pair as

```text
n=x_78(1),       m=x_87(1),       w=nmn,
wnw^(-1)=m.                                             (NWS1)
```

The first equation is the characteristic-two Weyl word and the second is
its native root-position mixed square.  This is the smallest closed word in
which the external-return occurrence and the Whitehead/root-position
transport occur with unequal odd parity after the opposite occurrence is
eliminated.

`native-weyl-return-gauges-have-s3-model` proves that `(NWS1)` does not
identify the two reservoir operators.  Its exact gauge table is the three
reflection presentation of `S_3`, not a cancellation relation.
