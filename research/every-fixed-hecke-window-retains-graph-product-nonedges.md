Let `Theta_h` be the native Hecke orbital graph on `X=A/C`, and let `F` be
any finite union of Weyl- and denominator-translated shells containing the
first shell.  In the exact graph-wreath model

```text
G_cc=(graph_product_(x in X) C_2) rtimes A,             (FWG1)
```

the subgroup generated by the lamps `(c_x)_(x in F)` is exactly the graph
product on the induced graph `Theta_h[F]`.  This is the special-subgroup
normal-form theorem for graph products.

Every clique `K subseteq F` therefore has its honest Boolean Fourier PVM

```text
E_(K,epsilon)=2^(-|K|) product_(x in K)(1+epsilon_x c_x).  (FWG2)
```

These PVMs have canonical traces `2^(-|K|)` and are perfectly compatible on
all overlaps:

```text
sum_(eta restricted to L = epsilon) E_(K,eta)
 =E_(L,epsilon)                         (L subseteq K). (FWG3)
```

All actor covariance, Weyl transport, denominator transport between the
chosen windows, and every native mixed-root/edge commutator also hold
exactly in `(FWG1)`.  Thus neither a chordal nor an expanding NERVE of a
finite clique cover adds any consistency not already present in this model.

Nevertheless the two vertices

```text
x=hC,                    y=h^(-1)C                     (FWG4)
```

belong to every such `F` and are a nonedge.  Hence their lamps generate
`C_2*C_2`.  The obstruction persists after a positive local cylinder cut.
If `K` is any clique in the common link of `x` and `y`, and
`e=E_(K,epsilon)`, then

```text
tau(e)=2^(-|K|),
||e(c_xc_y-c_yc_x)||_2^2=2 tau(e),                     (FWG5)
||e[P_x,P_y]_add||_2^2=tau(e)/8,                       (FWG6)
```

where `P_z=(1-c_z)/2`.  Thus a positive carrier obtained from the shared
commuting coordinates does not merely fail to prove commutation: it retains
a fixed commutator density.

Consequently no bounded family of native shell cliques, overlap equations,
and actor transports can yield a dimension-free nonedge-commutation
inequality valid in all tracial representations.  Adding a fill-in clique
containing `x,y` would add the missing relation rather than derive it.
Finite-dimensional-only rank pressure from a fixed family remains bounded
and is absorbed by spectator multiplicity, as in
`native-hecke-edge-rank-integrality-is-bounded`.

The surviving alternatives are genuinely global: use windows whose radius
diverges with the microstate and solve the coset-action coherence problem,
or introduce a new finite-dimensional-only selector that is not a
consequence of the native clique atlas.  This statement remains inside the
congruence-correctable sector and does not address
`sl3-noncorrectable-lambda-sector-has-no-projective-leak`.
