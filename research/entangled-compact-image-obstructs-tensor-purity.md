title: An irreducible compact-bearing tensor representation obstructs purity
---

Let `A` and `B` be C-star algebras.  Suppose the minimal tensor product
`D=A tensor_min B` has an irreducible representation

```text
pi:D -> B(H)
```

on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space such that `pi(D)` contains a
nonzero compact operator.  Then `D` has a nonzero elementary ideal-subquotient
and therefore is not pure.
