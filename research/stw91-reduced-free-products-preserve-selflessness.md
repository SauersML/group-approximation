Let `(A,tau)` and `(B,sigma)` be selfless C*-probability spaces.  Then their
reduced free product

```text
(C,rho)=(A,tau) * (B,sigma)
```

is selfless.  No exactness, nuclearity, complete selflessness, or
group-theoretic hypothesis is required.

Consequently, if countable discrete groups `G` and `H` have selfless reduced
group C*-algebras, then

```text
C*_r(G * H)
```

is selfless.  In particular `G*H` is C*-simple.  This gives finite
free-product permanence for positive instances of Problem XCI even when the
factors are outside the currently known PHP, linear, or rapid-decay classes.
