**ESTABLISHED (classification of the proposed mechanisms).**  “The finite
model recognizes and destroys itself” has three distinct meanings.

```text
(A) An external enumerator reads a rational matrix code and appends a nemesis.
(B) Fixed group words recover dimension/multiplicity inside the same tuple.
(C) A fixed-point program reacts to proofs about its own fixed marked word.
```

Branch `(A)` has the finality dilemma of
`post-candidate-nemesis-has-a-finality-dilemma`: appending changes the group
the candidate was meant to approximate; testing the new challenge rejects
the witness before it triggers.

Branch `(B)` cannot read raw capacity from finite normalized word data.  By
`amplification-natural-words-cannot-read-packet-multiplicity`, spectator
amplification multiplies dimension and anonymous multiplicity while leaving
every authenticated word, normalized trace and HS defect unchanged.
Amitsur--Levitzki detects only the PI degree of the algebra actually generated
by authenticated letters; scalar/reducible sectors need not expose the full
ambient matrix algebra.

Branch `(C)` is valid by threshold-free and depthwise reverse Kleene.  It
avoids numerical crossing moduli because it enumerates proofs, not rational
matrix witnesses.  Its price is load-bearing: the compiler must already prove
qualitative canonical-profile collapse, or same-mark fixed-depth estimates
uniform over every dimension.

Thus self-reference is a scheduler, not an HS amplifier.  It cannot turn an
arbitrary diagonal defect schedule into a collapse theorem, reveal
amplification-invisible dimension, or make a pre-challenge tuple approximate
its post-challenge group.
