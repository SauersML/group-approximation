**ESTABLISHED (classification of the proposed mechanisms).**  “The finite
model recognizes and destroys itself” has three distinct meanings.

```text
(A) An external enumerator reads a rational matrix code and appends a nemesis.
(B) Fixed group words recover dimension/multiplicity inside the same tuple.
(C) A fixed-point program reacts to proofs about its own fixed marked word.
```

Branch `(A)` has the finality dilemma of
`post-candidate-nemesis-has-a-finality-dilemma`: appending changes the group
the candidate was meant to approximate; testing the new challenge rejects
the witness before it triggers.

Branch `(B)` cannot read raw capacity from finite normalized word data.  By
`amplification-natural-words-cannot-read-packet-multiplicity`, spectator
amplification multiplies dimension and anonymous multiplicity while leaving
every authenticated word, normalized trace and HS defect unchanged.
Amitsur--Levitzki detects only the PI degree of the algebra actually generated
by authenticated letters; scalar/reducible sectors need not expose the full
ambient matrix algebra.  Allowing an adaptive-looking coherent query circuit
does not enlarge this access: by
`coherent-word-oracles-cannot-branch-on-their-own-tracial-profile`, word
evaluation, fixed controls and gapped functional calculus remain blockwise.
Trace, measurement feedback and an independent tensor copy are not internal
group-word primitives.

Branch `(C)` is valid, but has two sharply different forms.

- **Seeded proof self-reference is unconditional.**  A single marked element
  `1!=z in Res_MF(H)` gives the one-generator switch compiler of
  `marked-mf-radical-seed-gives-proof-triggered-compiler`.  Reverse Kleene
  then produces a self-aware finitely presented non-MF group.
- **The bare threshold-free compiler is not a seed-free breakthrough.**  By
  `threshold-free-mf-compiler-equivalence`, its TFM1--TFM2 interface is
  equivalent to already having a marked finitely presented MF-radical seed.
  The fixed point schedules the branch and certifies nontriviality; it does
  not manufacture the analytic collapse.

The stronger finite CPU support-profile programme can still seek a new
construction of the collapse, but that constructional independence is extra
information not expressed by TFM1--TFM2.  Moreover
`monotone-mf-switch-positive-branch-barrier` shows that an append-on-halt
compiler cannot have an MF halting branch while the same nonhalting radical
mark survives.

Thus self-reference is a scheduler, not an operator-norm or HS amplifier.  It
cannot turn an arbitrary diagonal defect schedule into a collapse theorem,
reveal amplification-invisible dimension, or make a pre-challenge tuple
approximate its post-challenge group.
