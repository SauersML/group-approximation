Bulatov--Zhivny, *Satisfiability of commutative vs. non-commutative CSPs*,
arXiv:2404.11709 (v4, 2025), prove as part of their finite-domain
classification that **tractable CSPs of bounded width have no satisfiability
gap of any kind** between classical assignments and their operator-valued
counterparts. Their work also identifies unbounded-width/affine regimes as a
source of operator gaps.

This is imported only as a landscape theorem. It does not say that a group
multiplication table, or the regular-character face relevant to Q3.4, is
bounded width.
