**ESTABLISHED TYPE AUDIT.**  The decomposition

```text
A=P_(+,+)+P_(+,-)+P_(-,+),       X=P_(-,-)
```

from `s3-active-complement-is-three-equal-scale-affine-atoms` is a
four-label decomposition of a finite involution packet.  It is not the
orthogonal decomposition of the source adjoint Reynolds range into the
`p^2` target-sized summands from
`leavitt-target-return-channel-count-firewall`.

There are two independent obstructions to that identification.

First, the affine Toeplitz coefficient algebra is deliberately formed over
a splitting field of characteristic `p>=5`.  Its source and target Reynolds
traces are therefore

```text
tr_ad(R_s)=p^(-2),       tr_ad(R_t)=p^(-4),
```

so source coverage needs at least `p^2>=25` unit-weight target-return
channels.  Three active packet labels are not “three of four” channels for
this packet.  Specializing the count to `p=2` is unavailable: the central
idempotent formulas for the involution packet use division by two, and the
semisimple splitting-field construction of `A` fails in characteristic two.

Second, even when two finite decompositions have the same number of labels,
a packet projection on the representation space is not a supported map

```text
X_j=R_t X_j R_s
```

on the adjoint multiplicity space.  Producing such an `X_j` requires a mixed
payload relation which puts the finite carrier coefficient and the Leavitt
transport on one authenticated multiplicity cut.  Separate carrier labels
retain the rectangular escape.

Consequently the three active atoms supply useful finite labels but no
Reynolds source-coverage premise.  Any successful use of them must first
construct payload-coupled returned contractions and prove their source Gram
mass; the raw cardinality `3+1` provides neither statement.
