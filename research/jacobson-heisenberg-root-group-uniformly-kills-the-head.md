**Notation.** `J = F_2<S,T | TS=1>`, `Q = 1 - ST`, `I = JQJ` (the two-sided ideal generated by `Q`, i.e. the additive span
of all `uQv`). `H(J)` is the unitriangular Heisenberg group over `J`: the subgroup of `EL_3(J)` generated by
`x_12(J)` and `x_23(J)`. It has elements `x_12(a) x_23(b) x_13(c)`, relations

```text
x_ij(a) x_ij(b) = x_ij(a+b),   [x_12(a), x_23(b)] = x_13(ab),   x_13(J) central,          (HJ1)
```

and contains the head `z = x_13(Q)`. `||.||_2` is the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm on `M_n`.

**Theorem.**
1. **(Local finiteness.)** `H(J)` is locally finite, hence amenable.
2. **(Exact.)** Every finite-dimensional unitary representation `rho` of `H(J)` satisfies `rho(x_13(c)) = 1` for all
   `c in I`. In particular `rho(z) = 1`.
3. **(Uniform.)** For every `n` and every map `phi : H(J) -> U(n)` with
   `E_h ||phi(gh) - phi(g)phi(h)||_2 < epsilon` for all `g`, where `E` is an invariant mean on `H(J)`, and in
   particular whenever `delta(phi) := sup_(g,h in H(J)) ||phi(gh) - phi(g)phi(h)||_2 < epsilon`,
   ```text
   ||phi(x_13(c)) - 1||_2 < 111 epsilon      for every c in I.                                 (HJ2)
   ```
   So `||phi(z) - 1||_2 <= 111 delta(phi)`, with no dependence on `n`.
4. **(Transport.)** Let `theta : H(J) -> Gamma` be any homomorphism, and `pi : Gamma -> U(n)` any map. Put
   `delta_theta(pi) = sup_(g,h in H(J)) ||pi(theta(gh)) - pi(theta g) pi(theta h)||_2`. Then
   `||pi(theta z) - 1||_2 <= 111 delta_theta(pi)`. Examples of `theta` include:
   - any root triple `(i,j,k)` of `EL_m(J)` or `St_m(J)`, `m >= 3`, composed with any conjugation;
   - `Delta = St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2))` via `S -> s_2`, `T -> t_2`. There `1 - s_2 t_2 = s_1 t_1`, so `theta z` is the marked
     root `z = x_13(s_1 t_1)` of `agent-leavitt-not-bcs-negative-root-corner` (LNC1).
5. **(Sharpness.)** No finite part of the uniform hypothesis suffices. For every finite `F` in `H(J)`, the finite
   group `<F, z>` has its regular representation `lambda`. Extend it by `1` off `<F,z>`. The resulting map is exactly
   multiplicative on `<F,z>`, yet `||lambda(z) - 1||_2 = sqrt 2`. Hence `H(J)` is a countable amenable group that is
   **not HS-stable**, and the head witnesses this: its finite-window approximations of the regular trace are never
   near genuine finite-dimensional representations.

**Consequence for `EL_20(J)` and the target hole.** By `leavitt-corner-trace-gate-collapses-to-jacobson-hyperlinearity`,
`EL_20(J)` is hyperlinear iff it has hyperlinear approximations `pi_k` whose trace at the head stays away from `1`.
Any such sequence satisfies, for every root triple and every conjugate copy `g H(J) g^(-1)`,

```text
liminf_k delta_(g H g^-1)(pi_k) >= (1/111) liminf_k ||pi_k(z) - 1||_2 > 0,                   (HJ3)
```

while the defect on every fixed finite set tends to `0`. The survivor class of the refutation side is therefore
confined to approximations with **non-uniform Heisenberg defect**. The defect must escape to root elements of
unbounded Toeplitz degree on every root Heisenberg group simultaneously. The invariant is
`limsup_k delta_H(pi_k)`. It cannot be seen in the tracial ultraproduct, because there `H(J)` acts exactly but on an
infinite-dimensional corner.
