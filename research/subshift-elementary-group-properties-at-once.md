**ESTABLISHED (as reliable as its inputs; several are unreviewed).** For every infinite minimal subshift `X`, the group `G_X = EL_3(LC(X,F_2) ⋊_T Z)` has all of the following properties at once:
1. infinite, finitely generated, simple, with property (T);
2. it is the marked limit of finite simple groups `SL_{3N}(F_2)` that form an expander family, so it is LEF, sofic and hyperlinear;
3. when `|A| = 2` it is generated by three elements of orders 7, 2 and 2;
4. it contains every finite group and the simple amenable group `[[T]]'`;
5. it is not uniformly simple;
6. `C*_r(G_X)` has a unique tracial state.

Open, and not included: C*-simplicity (`subshift-elementary-groups-are-cstar-simple`).
