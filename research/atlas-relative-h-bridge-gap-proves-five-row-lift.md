Let `B(U)` be the six-relator energy on the right side of `(RHB1)`.  The
upper half of `(FBE2)`, identity `(FBE3)`, and `(RHB1)` give

```text
A(U)
 <=C_H D_H(U)
 <=C_H C_B B(U).
```

This is `(FPL4)` with `C_5=C_H C_B`, uniformly in the external regular
multiplicity.
