Apply Miles Gould, *The Selfless Dichotomy*, arXiv:2606.09654v2 (revised
2026-07-22), Theorem 2 and Corollary 3.  Theorem 2 states for every selfless
C-star probability space `(A,phi)` that `A` is simple, gives stable rank one,
strict comparison, and uniqueness of the normalized `2`-quasitrace when
`phi` is tracial, and gives pure infiniteness when `phi` is nontracial.
Corollary 3 states that every selfless C-star algebra is pure.

The missing nonfaithful case used in that synthesis is Theorem 1, repeated
as Theorem 2.5: a selfless C-star probability space with nonfaithful state is
purely infinite and simple.  The target records exactly these conclusions
and does not infer that any additional group algebra is selfless.
