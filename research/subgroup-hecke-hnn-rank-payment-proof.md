For `k in K`, the HNN relation and `phi(k)e_H=e_H` give

```text
kue_H=u phi(k)e_H=ue_H.
```

Averaging over `K` proves `(SHA3)`.  In an approximate representation,
averaging the corresponding errors gives

```text
||(1-q)Up||_2 <= |K|^(-1)sum_k epsilon_k.
```

Projection geometry gives

```text
||(1-q)Up||_2^2
 =tau(p)-tau(qUpU^*) >= tau(p)-tau(q),
```

and Jensen proves `(SHA5)`.  The canonical trace computation in `(SHA6)` is
the identity coefficient of the two subgroup averages.  Britton's lemma
gives base injection and standard unitary induction from `B` supplies the
common infinite completion.

For the final literal-involution assertion, multiply `qup=up` by `u^*` and
expand.  The two-term group-basis equality in `(SHA8)` forces the unordered
support `{c,ca}` to be `{1,a}`.  Nondegeneracy leaves only `c=1` or `c=a`;
coefficient comparison gives respectively the whole target or the identical
signed spectral projection.
