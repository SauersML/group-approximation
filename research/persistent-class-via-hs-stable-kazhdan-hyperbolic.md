## Why sufficient

The open premise supplies `Γ`. The established theorem gives a residually finite
hyperbolic finite-index subgroup `H_0 <= Γ`, a prime `p`, and a class in
`H^2(H_0;C_p)` that restricts nontrivially to every finite-index subgroup. That is
exactly the target, with `H = H_0`.

This is a genuine reduction: the premise is a unitary rigidity property, not a
cohomological statement. But the target is at least as hard as the premise's
profinite shadow, so this route does not escape Gromov's question. It translates it.
