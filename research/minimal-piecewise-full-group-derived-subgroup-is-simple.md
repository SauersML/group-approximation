**OPEN.** A. Garrido and C. D. Reid, *Locally compact piecewise full groups of
homeomorphisms*, arXiv:2501.00908v1 (1 Jan 2025), §`sec:questions`, "Simplicity of the
derived group", TeX l.3032–3036:

> There are several results in the literature showing that if a piecewise full group
> $G$ satisfies some additional conditions, then the derived subgroup $\Der(G)$ is
> simple (and therefore coincides with $\Al(G)$). However, it appears the following
> question is still open in general.
> Let $G$ be a minimal piecewise full group of homeomorphisms of the Cantor space. Is
> $\Der(G)$ simple?

The authors state it for both the discrete and the non-discrete (locally compact)
settings. The discrete case is the question of whether the alternating group `Al(G)`
(Nekrashevych) equals the derived subgroup of the full group. For that case,
`gq-bh-openq-papers-list.md` §B already records Nekrashevych's remark, arXiv:1511.08241
l.115 and l.148, without a node.

**Relevance.** Finitely presented simple groups on main are mostly of the form
`Al(G) = Der(G)` for expansive minimal groupoids (Nekrashevych, Matui). A general
equality `Der = Al` would remove one hypothesis from those constructions.
