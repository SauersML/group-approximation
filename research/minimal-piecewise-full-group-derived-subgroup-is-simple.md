**OPEN.** A. Garrido and C. D. Reid, *Locally compact piecewise full groups of
homeomorphisms*, arXiv:2501.00908v1 (1 Jan 2025), §`sec:questions`, "Simplicity of the
derived group", TeX l.3032–3036:

> There are several results in the literature showing that if a piecewise full group
> $G$ satisfies some additional conditions, then the derived subgroup $\Der(G)$ is
> simple (and therefore coincides with $\Al(G)$). However, it appears the following
> question is still open in general.
> Let $G$ be a minimal piecewise full group of homeomorphisms of the Cantor space. Is
> $\Der(G)$ simple?

The authors state it for both the discrete and the non-discrete (locally compact)
settings. The discrete case is the question of whether the alternating group `Al(G)`
(Nekrashevych) equals the derived subgroup of the full group. For that case,
`gq-bh-openq-papers-list.md` §B already records Nekrashevych's remark, arXiv:1511.08241
l.115 and l.148, without a node.

**Relevance.** Finitely presented simple groups on main are mostly of the form
`Al(G) = Der(G)` for expansive minimal groupoids (Nekrashevych, Matui). A general
equality `Der = Al` would remove one hypothesis from those constructions.

## Attempts

**Attempt 1 (bh-garrido-kodama, 2026-09-18): an exact fragmentation reformulation; the question stays OPEN.**
- **Result.** `alternating-group-is-generated-by-local-commutators` (lane proof). For a minimal
  groupoid of germs, `Al(G) = A(𝒢)` is exactly the subgroup generated by commutators `[h,k]` of
  pairs supported in a common clopen set `U` that has two disjoint translates. The proof is a
  wreath-product trick with the 3-cycle over `U, σU, τU`, followed by Nekrashevych's theorem.
- **Reformulation.** `Der(G)` is simple if and only if every commutator of `G` is a product of such
  local commutators.
- **Sufficient condition.** `G` is generated by elements supported near at most two points.
- **Same question in the literature.** It is also Matui's and Nekrashevych's question: arXiv:1511.08241
  l.115, l.140–148, and after Theorem `th:simple` ("It would be interesting to find an example of a
  minimal groupoid of germs with A ≠ D"). It is Matte Bon's question too: arXiv:1512.02133 l.121, on
  whether `[[𝒢]]_t′ ≤ [[𝒢]]′` can be strict.
- **Where Matui's proof needs more than minimality.** Matui, arXiv:1210.5800 Theorem `simple2` (read
  at source), uses pure infiniteness twice. The first is to displace the supports of `α` and `τ`
  (Lemma `normalize2`). The second is to compress `supp α ∪ supp γ` into an arbitrary small clopen
  set. Both are fragmentation steps, and the reformulation isolates exactly them.
- **What a counterexample must contain.** A commutator of `[[𝒢]]` that cannot be broken into
  commutators of pairs supported in 3-displaceable sets. Candidates would be groupoids with no
  comparison, where supports cannot be compressed, and with no invariant measure to replace
  comparison, for instance hyperbolic germs combined with a failure of comparison. I did not
  construct one.
