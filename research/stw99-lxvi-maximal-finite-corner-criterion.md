title: A dyadically divisible finite projection in a simple mixed algebra is an LXVI seed
distinct_from:
  stw99-lxvi-finite-nonstably-finite-car-seed: that is the unresolved existential target; this is a proved criterion reducing it to one projection-monoid construction inside a mixed finite-infinite algebra.
---

Let `B` be a simple C\*-algebra containing an infinite projection, and let
`0!=p in B` be a finite projection.  If `[p]` starts a coherent dyadic chain

```text
x_0=[p],                 x_n=2x_(n+1) in V(B),
```

then `pBp` is simple and unital, its unit is finite, it is not stably finite,
and it contains a unital copy of the CAR algebra.

No matrix amplification index is prescribed.  Hence such a pair `(B,p)`
would give the finite non-stably-finite CAR seed equivalent to Problem LXVI.
