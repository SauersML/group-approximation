kind: claim
title: (T)-compatibility excludes Haagerup carriers
invalidates: [free-probability-carriers]
---

A carrier must contain `L(Γ)` for `Γ` Kazhdan — a diffuse (T) factor. A von
Neumann algebra with the Haagerup property contains no diffuse (T)
subalgebra, so free group factors and free-probability carriers built from
them are excluded, however Connes-embeddable they are. Three candidate
witnesses in a row satisfied the compression requirement and failed a
different one (no (T); wrong overlap structure; Haagerup).
