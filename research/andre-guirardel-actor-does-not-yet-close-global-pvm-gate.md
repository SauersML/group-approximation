The exact actor-side ingredients are present: one nonzero augmentation
lamp normally generates the module, all nontrivial character orbits are
infinite uniform Schreier expanders, and every finite-dimensional exact
representation kills the lamp.

The route remains open for three independent reasons:

1. finite presentation of the Andre--Guirardel actor is not established;
2. finite generation of its point stabilizer is not established;
3. property `(T)` has not been upgraded to a dimension-uniform global PVM
   decoder for the infinitely many conjugate lamps.

The first two are exactly the conditions in
`augmentation-lamp-finite-presentation-criterion`.  The third is
`property-t-must-upgrade-global-augmentation-rounding`.  Even after the
PVM is decoded, `infinite-character-actor-word-energy-interface` is needed
to transfer the exact Schreier gap to finite-presentation word defect.
