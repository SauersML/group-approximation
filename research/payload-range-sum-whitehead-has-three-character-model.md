**ESTABLISHED EXACT COUNTERMODEL TO THE MINIMAL OUTPUT-AUTHENTICATED
PROTOTYPE.**  The smallest literal table which simultaneously contains the
two binary inverse rows, authenticates their reverse products through the
same Whitehead occurrences, and records parent generation has the following
abstract form.  There are commuting involutions `J,C_0,C_1`, two commuting
Pauli pairs `(X_i,Z_i)`, two Whiteheads `W_i`, and their transported pairs

```text
X_i'=W_iX_iW_i^(-1),             Z_i'=W_iZ_iW_i^(-1),
```

such that

```text
[X_i,Z_i]=J,                    [X_i,Z_j]=1 (i!=j),     (PCR1)
[X_i',Z_i']=C_i,               C_0C_1=J.               (PCR2)
```

In the binary Leavitt coefficient table, `(PCR1)` is the common-output
version of `t_i s_j=delta_(ij)A`; the transported commutators name the
reverse products `s_i t_i`; and the last equality is precisely

```text
s_0t_0+s_1t_1=A
```

in one characteristic-two root subgroup.  Fixed spare-index role rotations
put the two reverse products in that same output root, so every discrepancy
in `(PCR1)--(PCR2)` is a fixed ordinary Steinberg/Whitehead null word.  This
is the first prototype that really defeats the earlier scalar-mark Pauli
reuse: if `J=-I`, conjugacy forces `C_0=C_1=-I`, contradicting
`C_0C_1=J`.

It still does **not** imply the atomic three-row compiler.  There is an exact
twelve-dimensional unitary model of `(PCR1)--(PCR2)` in which `J` has a
nonzero negative spectral projection.  The three commuting signs
`J,C_0,C_1` are the three nontrivial characters of a two-bit diagonal
packet, and the Whiteheads permute their equal-rank negative supports.
Thus the range-sum is exact and both reverse products are attached to their
inverse payload pairs, but each Whitehead sends part of the prescribed
`J=-1` atom through the `J=+1` sector.

Consequently output-level occurrence authentication is not yet same-atom
return.  The next prototype must couple at least one **individual
coefficient arm** of each Whitehead to a selector inside the prescribed
`J=-1` atom, or otherwise forbid the three-character orbit.  Merely naming
the inverse commutator, its Whitehead-conjugate reverse commutator, and their
literal range sum leaves this exact marked escape.

No Property `(T)`, Kazhdan input, stability theorem, or literature result is
used.
