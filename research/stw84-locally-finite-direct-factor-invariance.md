title: A countable locally finite direct factor does not change group-algebra nuclear dimension
---

Let `L` be a countable locally finite group and let `H` be any countable
amenable group.  Then

```text
dim_nuc(C*(L times H))=dim_nuc(C*(H)).
```

This equality includes the value `infinity`.  Thus arbitrary locally finite
direct factors are invisible to nuclear dimension even when `L` is infinite,
nonabelian, and has finite subgroups of unbounded order.
