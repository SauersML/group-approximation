Expand the finite group-algebra projection `P_lambda=q_lambda F` in the
group basis of the fixed packet.  Multiplying by the wandering word gives a
fixed finite linear combination of ordinary words.  The normal-form
hypothesis says every word in that support is nonidentity, so every term has
canonical trace tending to zero.  The finite-matrix return inequality then
forces the corresponding forbidden type mass to zero.  Finiteness of the
marked dual and of the context set permits summation and minimization of all
constants.
