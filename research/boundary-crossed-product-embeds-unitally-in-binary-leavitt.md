For every field `k` and every `d >= 2` there is an injective unital ring homomorphism
`LC(∂F_d, k) ⋊ F_d -> L_k(1,2)`. So for every `N` there is an injective group homomorphism
`EL_N(LC(∂F_d, k) ⋊ F_d) -> EL_N(L_k(1,2))`.
