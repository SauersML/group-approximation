**OPEN.**  Statement (2) of `dadarlat-tracially-af-universal-uhf-reduction`:
every separable nuclear simple unital tracially AF algebra `A` with
`K_0(A) ≅ Q` as scaled ordered groups and `K_1(A) = 0` is isomorphic to the
universal UHF algebra `Q`.  Equivalent to Problem II.

## Attempts

* **Lin's classification.**  The isomorphism theorem for tracially AF algebras
  (Lin; the existence and uniqueness theorems quoted in Dadarlat 2003, Theorems
  5.2 and 5.3) assumes the domain satisfies the UCT.  That assumption is what
  lifts the K-theory isomorphism to a KK-element and produces a unital
  homomorphism `Q → A` inverting it.  Without it `A` has the right ordered
  K-theory but no KK-element realizing the isomorphism.  Dies at the existence
  step.
* **Dadarlat's local form.**  Statement (3) of the same theorem is a
  finite-dimensional approximation property of trivial-K-theory RFD algebras:
  after adding a finite-dimensional representation, finite sets lie close to
  finite-dimensional subalgebras.  No construction of the subalgebra `B` from
  `K_*(D) = 0` is known.  Dies at producing `B`.
