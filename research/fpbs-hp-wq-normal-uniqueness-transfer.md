**ESTABLISHED (imported).** Let `G` be a Cayley graph of a finitely generated
group `Gamma` and let `H_1 <= H_2 <= Gamma` with `H_1` wq-normal in `H_2`. Then
for each `p` there is a unique `H_1`-infinite cluster almost surely if and only
if there is a unique `H_2`-infinite cluster almost surely. In particular, if
`H <= Gamma` is wq-normal then `p_u(H;G) = p_u(G)`.

A subgroup `H` is s-normal when `|sH ∩ Hs| = infinity` for every `s`, and
wq-normal (Popa) when every intermediate subgroup `H <= I < Gamma` has some
`g` outside `I` with `g I g^(-1) ∩ I` infinite. Infinite normal and commensurated
subgroups are s-normal, and s-normal subgroups are wq-normal.

Hutchcroft--Pan, arXiv:2409.12283v1, Proposition 1.9 (s-normal case) and
Proposition 1.10 (wq-normal case), read from the PDF on 2026-09-11. The v1 text
of Proposition 1.10 says "Proposition 1.8 remains true"; the statement it
modifies is the s-normal Proposition 1.9, as the surrounding paragraph shows.
