**Theorem 3 (Kochloukova).** Let `G` be a non-trivial group with a finite `K(G,1)` CW-complex `Y`
of dimension `n` and `χ(Y) = 0`. Let `N ⊴ G` contain `[G,G]`, be of homological type `FP_{n−1}`, and
have `G/N` cyclic-by-finite. Then:
1. `N` is of type `FP_n`;
2. `vcd(G/N) = cd(G) − cd(N)`;
3. either `[G:N] < ∞` or `cd(N) = cd(G) − 1`.

**Stallings' theorem.** A finitely generated group of cohomological dimension 1 is free.

**Consequence for n = 2.** Suppose `G` has a finite 2-dimensional `K(G,1)` with `χ = 0`, and some
surjection `φ: G → Z` has a finitely generated kernel `N`. Then `N` is free, possibly trivial.
