The prerequisite gives the stated normal-quotient formula and unique
factorization through Z for every MF target. It also supplies the other
representation targets in the older claim, as follows. Any image of the
finitely generated group P in a finite-dimensional linear group over a
field is finitely generated linear and therefore residually finite; a
countable residually finite group is MF. Thus all such maps factor through
chi. Maps to compact groups then kill N because their finite-dimensional
unitary representations separate points (Peter--Weyl). The same conclusion
holds for maximally almost periodic groups by their separating unitary
representations. Finite, residually finite, and profinite targets are
included. These factorizations commute with postcomposition, giving the
claimed representation functors and compactifications. The quotient
P/normalClosure(d) is Z, whereas P contains nontrivial N and is non-MF.
