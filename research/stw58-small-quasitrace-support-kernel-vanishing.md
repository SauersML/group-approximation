Let \(B\) be nonzero simple unital finite pure with
\(QT_1(B)\ne\varnothing\), let \(X\) be compact metric of covering
dimension \(d\ge1\), and put \(A=C(X,B)\). If \(u\in U(A)\) has
\([u]=0\) in \(K_1(A)\) and
\[
 \sup_{x\in X,\ \tau\in QT_1(B)}d_\tau(|u(x)-1|)
 <\frac1{d+1},
\]
then \(u\in U_0(A)\).

More generally, it suffices that \(u-1\in\operatorname{Her}(h)\)
for a positive \(h\) satisfying the same uniform dimension bound.
The support need not be full, may vanish at some parameter points,
and need not have a nonzero orthogonal complement inside \(A\).

Consequently every representative of any nonzero unstable kernel
class in \(\pi_kU(B)\to K_{k+1}(B)\), \(k\ge1\), has uniform
quasitrace support dimension at least \(1/(k+1)\). Based families
receive based contractions. No such nonzero kernel is constructed.

The remaining localization gap is deforming or globally fragmenting
an arbitrary stably null family into sufficiently small supports.
The general LVIII root remains open.
