Subgroups of amenable groups are amenable, while an amenable property-(T)
discrete group is finite.  If `|H|<infinity`, the image of
`H x H -> HgH`, `(h_1,h_2) |-> h_1 g h_2`, shows `|HgH|<=|H|^2`.
Therefore a finite union of `H`-double cosets is finite.  This contradicts the
assumed infinite index.
