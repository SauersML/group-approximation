The corona algebra `Q(B) = M(B)/B` of every σ-unital stable
C\*-algebra `B` is `K_1`-injective (it is properly infinite and its
unitary group's `π_0` injects into `K_1`).  So STW Problem LXI holds
on the class of examples that most matters for the classical
`KK`-uniqueness application — which is consistent with Szabo's 2026
proof of `KK`-uniqueness going through multiplier-algebra features:
the corona locus was never where LXI could fail, and a counterexample
must be a properly infinite unital algebra far from corona structure,
with `O_∞ * O_∞` the Blanchard--Rohde--Rordam reduction target.
