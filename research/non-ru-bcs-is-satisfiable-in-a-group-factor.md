OPEN.  Let `B_*` be the finite Boolean constraint system of
`mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists`, whose BCS (synchronous) algebra
`A(B_*)` has a tracial state but no unital *-homomorphism into a tracial
matrix ultraproduct `R^U`.  Find a countable group `Lambda` and
self-adjoint unitaries `x_v in L(Lambda)`, one per variable `v` of `B_*`,
such that `x_u x_v = x_v x_u` whenever `u, v` share a context and every
constraint polynomial of `B_*` vanishes on the `x_v`; equivalently, a
unital *-homomorphism

```text
A(B_*) -> L(Lambda).                                         (GFB1)
```

Any such `Lambda` is non-hyperlinear (`non-hyperlinear-from-group-factor-bcs-model`):
the image of `(GFB1)` generates a subalgebra of `L(Lambda)` that is the GNS
algebra of a tracial state of `A(B_*)`, every tracial state of `A(B_*)`
has non-Connes-embeddable GNS algebra, and Connes embeddability passes to
subalgebras.

This is the weakest sufficient form of the transfer problem: the
algebraic-envelope, trace-promotion, and twisted-factor holes each imply
it or a variant of it, while this hole constrains neither the host group
nor the position of the involutions.  By
`character-group-realizability-parseval-criterion` it is equivalent to the
existence of a state `psi` on `C^*(W_*) (x)_max C^*(Lambda)`, `W_*` the
right-angled Coxeter host of `non-ce-trace-on-sofic-racg`, whose
`Lambda`-marginal is regular, whose `W_*`-marginal vanishes on the
constraint ideal, and which satisfies the Parseval condition `(PGR3)`.

## Attempts

- **Abelian and amenable hosts are excluded.**  An abelian `Lambda` gives a
  model in `L^infinity(hat Lambda)`, i.e. a measurable family of classical
  assignments, so `B_*` would be classically satisfiable; but a classical
  assignment is a one-dimensional model, hence an `R^U` model.  An amenable
  `Lambda` gives `L(Lambda)` hyperfinite, hence inside `R subset R^U`.  So the
  host must be non-amenable, and in fact non-hyperlinear: the hole is the
  goal's constructive-transfer form, not a weakening of it.
- **Cocycle-twisted realisations over a normal subgroup.**  Inside
  `L(N rtimes Lambda')` the unitaries `f_w u_(phi(w))`, with
  `phi : W -> Lambda'` a homomorphism and `f` a 1-cocycle into
  `U(L(N))`, form a representation of `W`; its character vanishes off
  `K = ker phi` and on `K` equals the character of the representation
  `f|_K : K -> U(L(N))`.  So this mechanism only relocates the problem to
  realising `tau|_K` inside `L(N)`: with `K = W` it is circular, and with
  `K` proper it forces the non-CE trace to be supported on a proper normal
  subgroup, which nothing in `B_*` supplies.
- **Closure operations cannot manufacture a host.**  By the closure list of
  `character-group-realizability-parseval-criterion`, tensor products,
  convex combinations, pullbacks, induction and hyperfinite seeds only
  produce hyperlinear hosts from hyperlinear hosts.  A positive answer
  therefore needs a construction outside that list; the algebraic
  compact-abelian envelope (Pontryagin duality) is the one such
  construction currently on the table, and it is the peers' locked lane.
- **Embedding universality of group factors is open in the literature.**
  Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana's universality theorem for property-(T) factors
  leaves open (their Remark 1.1, as recorded in
  `cdi-property-t-envelope-has-nonabelian-base`) whether every separable
  tracial von Neumann algebra embeds into some `L(Lambda)`; a positive
  answer for the single algebra `A(B_*)''_tau` is all this hole needs.
