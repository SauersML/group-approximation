**ESTABLISHED (literature)** by `lueck-surjective-assembly-traces-lie-in-lambda-g-citation`.

Let `G` be a discrete group and `Λ^G = Z[1/|F| : F ≤ G finite] ⊆ Q`. If the Baum--Connes assembly map
`K_0^G(E̲G) → K_0(C*_r G)` is surjective, then

```text
τ_*( K_0(C*_r G) )  ⊆  Λ^G .
```

**Consequences used on main.**
- **Gapped projections over groups with surjective assembly.** Let `a ∈ M_n(C[G])` be self-adjoint with
  `c ∉ spec(a)`. Then `τ_*(χ_(-∞,c)(a)) ∈ Λ^G`. In particular a von Neumann kernel dimension outside
  `Λ^G`, irrational ones included, is never spectrally isolated over such `G`: `0` is an accumulation
  point of `spec(A^*A)`.
- **The lattice-transfer instance.** In the gapped instance of
  `fractional-matrix-trace-gives-projection-in-g-star-z` (Corollary C), take the group `G` with an
  irrational L²-Betti number on the shared complex. Then `G` itself must have non-surjective assembly.
  So amenable and other a-T-menable lamplighter examples cannot carry the gap. For a-T-menable groups
  surjectivity is the Higson--Kasparov theorem, which was not re-read.

**Scope.** Lück records that the unmodified trace conjecture, where the image is the additive subgroup
generated by `1/|F|`, was disproved by Roy. The ring `Λ^G` is the corrected target.
