Start with the internal reflection

```text
K_0=U wr F_2<=U,                    R_MF(K_0)~=F_2,
```

whose radical is normally generated by one involution.  For `m>=2`, apply
the finite-wreath lift with the regular actor `C_m`; for `m=1`, retain the
starting group.  This gives

```text
K_m=K_0 wr C_m<=U,                 Q_m=F_2 wr C_m.
```

The group `Q_m` is residually finite, hence MF.  The lift theorem gives
`(EV2)` and `(EV4)`, and the regular cyclic actor moves the original radical
generator through every coordinate.  The same target-factorization argument
identifies all the other residuals and proves `(EV5)`.

The base `F_2^m` has index `m` in `F_2 wr C_m`.  It is torsion-free and has
cohomological dimension `m`, so `vcd(Q_m)=m`.  Universal MF reflection is an
isomorphism invariant; the different virtual cohomological dimensions make
the `K_m` pairwise nonisomorphic.  For `m>=2`, virtual cohomological dimension
exceeds one, excluding virtual freeness; Muller--Schupp then excludes a
context-free word problem.
