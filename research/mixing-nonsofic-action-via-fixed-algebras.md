Take a sofic group `G` with an infranormal Kazhdan pair, a mixing free action of
`G`, and run Kun--Thom Theorem C: exhibit a `Γ`-fixed function whose
`G`-translates are not `Γ`-fixed, and conclude nonsoficity as in Corollary D,
possibly after passing to a factor or to a restriction.

Dead: invalidated by `kazhdan-fixed-algebra-blind-to-subgroup-ergodic-actions`.
On a mixing action every infinite subgroup acts ergodically, so the `Γ`-fixed
algebra is the constants, and for any sofic embedding the normalized fixed
diagonal algebra meets the embedded action only in the constants. Factors and
restrictions to infinite subgroups stay mixing, and finite subgroups compress
only normally. Nothing is left for Theorem C to contradict.
