Let `𝒢` be a minimal, ample, Hausdorff, second countable groupoid with compact unit space `X`, and let
`k` be a countable field of characteristic 0. Then `A_k(𝒢)` is stably finite iff `X` carries a
`𝒢`-invariant Borel probability measure.

When there is no such measure, `2n[1] <= n[1]` in `V(A_k(𝒢))` for some `n`, and `EL_N(A_k(𝒢))` has
no nontrivial MF quotient for `N >= 2n`.

So in characteristic 0 the ring hypothesis of the one-sided transport mechanism (stable infiniteness)
coincides with Tarski's paradox side. Nothing is claimed about MF-ness in the presence of a measure.
