## What is load-bearing

The word **strongly**.  Zaremsky's Proposition 5.3, which is the only reason
this envelope is of any use to a Boone--Higman route, hypothesizes strong
shift-similarity and not shift-similarity; Theorem 3.28's "strongly" is the
clause that makes the two compose.  Finite generation is used only to keep
the envelope in the class where finite presentability is a meaningful thing
to ask for.
