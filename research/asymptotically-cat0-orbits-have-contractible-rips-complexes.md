Let `G` act geometrically (properly discontinuously and cocompactly, by isometries) on a
proper asymptotically CAT(0) geodesic metric space `𝔛`, for instance a CAT(0) space or a
hyperbolic space. Let `x_0 ∈ 𝔛` and let `X = G.x_0` carry the metric induced from `𝔛`.
Then `VR_t(X)` is contractible for some `t`, and `G` acts geometrically on it.
