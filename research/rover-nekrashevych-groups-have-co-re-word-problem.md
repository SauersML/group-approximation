title: "Finitely generated subgroups of Röver–Nekrashevych groups of finitely generated self-similar groups have co-r.e. word problem"
---

Let `d ≥ 2` and let `G ≤ Aut(T_d)` be a finitely generated self-similar group:
for every `g ∈ G` and every vertex `v`, the section `g|_v` lies in `G`. Then
every finitely generated subgroup `K` of the Röver–Nekrashevych group `V_d(G)`
has co-recursively-enumerable word problem. That is, for a finite generating
set of `K`, the set of words representing non-identity elements is
recursively enumerable.

This covers `G` itself, `V_d(G)` (generated by `V_d` and `G`), and its finite-index
subgroups such as the commutator subgroup when the abelianization is finite.
In particular it covers the simple groups of Llosa Isenrich–Schesler–Wu
(arXiv:2510.01952v1), which are commutator subgroups `[V_m(j(Γ)), V_m(j(Γ))]`
of Röver–Nekrashevych groups of finitely generated self-similar groups
`j(Γ)`.

Together with `fg-groups-with-re-or-co-re-word-problem-are-countable`, this
shows that such constructions produce only countably many isomorphism types.
They cannot answer the first part of Problem 1.20 affirmatively without an
input that is not finitely generated self-similar data. Proof in
`rover-nekrashevych-groups-have-co-re-word-problem-proof`.
