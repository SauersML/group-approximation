Contrapositive of `universal-fp2-simple-envelopes-force-uncountably-many`.

Suppose simple groups of type FP_2 realize only countably many isomorphism
types (`only-countably-many-simple-groups-of-type-fp2`). The established
implication says that if every 2-generator group embeds in a simple group of
type FP_2, there are `2^aleph_0` such types. So some 2-generator group, a
countable group, embeds in no simple group of type FP_2.
