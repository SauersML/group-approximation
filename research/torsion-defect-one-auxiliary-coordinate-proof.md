## Why sufficient

This is the proof printed as `prop:torsion-defect-ring` in
`non_mf_groups_exist.tex`.  Its only analytic input is the prerequisite
(the manuscript's compression criterion) applied inside a subgroup, and
its only external input is property (T) for `EL_3` over a finitely
generated ring (Ershov--Jaikin-Zapirain, Theorem 1.1).  It consumes no
rank-weighted or rescaled Hilbert--Schmidt transport and no uniform
support bound; see the note at the end.

Let `R` be countable unital, `ts = 1`, `e = 1 - st` of finite additive
order `m`, and `n >= 4`.  Let `S` be the unital subring generated by
`s, t`, and `L = EL_3(S)` on coordinates `1, 2, 3` of `EL_4(S)`; `L` has
property (T).  Put `f_(ij) = s^i e t^j`; since `es = te = 0`,
`f_(ij) f_(kl) = delta_(jk) f_(il)`.  Write `D(a) = diag(1,1,1,a)`; for
`i != j`, `D(1 +- f_(ij)) = [e_41(f_(ii)), e_14(+-f_(ij))]` lies in
`EL_4(S)`.  Let `u` be the compression-cell compressor of the
manuscript (`s, s, s, t^3` on the diagonal, last column `e, et, et^2`),
put `w_(ij) = (1 + f_(ji))(1 - f_(ij))(1 + f_(ji))`, `r = w_14 w_25`,
and `v = u D(r)`.  Since `D(r)` centralizes `L`, `v L v^-1 <= L`.  Put

```text
c = D(1 + f_02),   y = e_42(f_10),   z = D(1 + f_12),   l = e_21(1),
B = < L, v, c, y >.
```

Then `c in C_B(L)`; from `r f_02 r^-1 = f_05`, `r f_12 r^-1 = f_45`,
`f_02 t^3 = f_05`, `t^3 f_45 = f_12 t^3`, direct multiplication gives
`v c v^-1 = e_14(f_02)` and `v z v^-1 = z`.  So
`d = [v c v^-1, l] = e_24(-f_02)` lies in `D_B(L)`, and
`[y, d] = D(1 - f_12) = z^-1` because `f_02 f_10 = 0` and
`f_10 f_02 = f_12`; hence `z in D_B(L)`.  The element `z` commutes with
`L`, with `v`, and with `c` and `y` (`f_12 f_02 = f_02 f_12 = f_12 f_10
= 0`), so `<z>` is central in `B`; `z^m = D(1 + m f_12) = 1` and
`z != 1` since `t f_12 s^2 = e != 0`.  So `<z>` is a nontrivial finite
central, hence normal Kazhdan, subgroup of `B` inside `D_B(L)`.  The
prerequisite applied to the countable group `B` kills `z` under every
homomorphism from `B`, hence from `EL_n(R)`, to an MF group.  Then

```text
[z, e_41(f_20)] = e_41(f_10),        [e_24(t), e_41(f_10)] = e_21(e)
```

are killed, hence every `e_(ij)(aeb)` by the Steinberg relations and
signed permutations, hence `E_n(R, ReR)`.  For `e != 0`, `e_21(e) != 1`.
If `R` has characteristic `p > 0` then `pe = 0`, and the contrapositive
gives direct finiteness from MF of `EL_4(R)`.

All identities were rechecked exactly in the normal form `s^i t^j` of
`Z<s,t | ts = 1>` on 2026-09-09.
