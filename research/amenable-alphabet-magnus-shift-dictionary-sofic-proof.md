Date: 2026-09-17. Written proof, not a Lean certificate. Section numbers refer
to [[nonsingular-magnus-extremes-separable-piece-sofic-proof]] (below: "the
NME proof"). Every step there except Lemma 2 is re-checked here with the
alphabet `Q` amenable instead of locally finite.

## 0. Setting

`G = <a,t | w>`, `t` of exponent sum `0`, `a_i = t^(-i) a t^i`, a cyclic
permutation of `w` equal to `W_0(a_0,...,a_n)`, `n >= 1`. By the Freiheitssatz
and Magnus--Moldavanskii,

    G = < B_0, t | t^(-1) c t = phi(c)  (c in F_-) >,   phi(a_i) = a_(i+1),

and by Reidemeister--Schreier the kernel `N` of `a -> 0, t -> 1` is
`< a_j (j in Z) | W_j (j in Z) >`, with `B_0 -> N` the inclusion of the piece.

**Hypothesis (AA).** For finite `E_- <= B_0 \ F_-`, `E_+ <= B_0 \ F_+`,
`E_1 <= B_0 \ {1}` there are an amenable `Q` and `psi : N -> Q` with
`psi(b) not in psi(F_-)` for `b in E_-`, `psi(b) not in psi(F_+)` for
`b in E_+`, and `psi(b) != 1` for `b in E_1`.

## 1. Configurations and shift representations

Given `psi`, put `x_j = psi(a_j)`. Then `W_0(x_j,...,x_(j+n)) = psi(W_j) = 1`
for all `j`. Conversely, such a configuration defines `psi`. By Lemma 1 of the
NME proof (its proof uses only the window relations, not the alphabet),
`rho(a) = x`, `rho(t) = sigma` defines

    rho : G -> S = Q^Z rtimes <sigma>,     rho(b)_j = b(x_j,...,x_(j+n))  (b in B_0),

and `pi_0 o rho|_(B_0) = psi|_(B_0)`.

## 2. The target is sofic

Let `H = rho(G)`, `L = H cap Q^Z`, `pi_M : L -> Q^([-M,M])` the restriction,
and `K_M = ker pi_M`.

**Lemma A.** `L` and `H` are sofic.

*Proof.* `pi_M(L) <= Q^(2M+1)` is a subgroup of a finite product of amenable
groups, hence amenable, hence sofic. The `K_M` decrease to `{1}`, so `L`
embeds in `prod_M pi_M(L)`; products and subgroups of sofic groups are sofic
([[elek-szabo-sofic-permanence]]). `L` is normal in `H` with `H/L = Z`
(generated by the image of `sigma`; if `H/L` were finite the argument is the
same), so `H` is sofic by the amenable-extension clause. ∎

This replaces Lemma 3 of the NME proof, whose only use of local finiteness was
to make `pi_M(L)` amenable.

## 3. The enlarged stabilizer

`D_M = rho(F_-) K_M` (a subgroup, `K_M` normal in `L`), and
`D = intersect_M D_M`. So `rho(F_-) <= D <= L`.

**Lemma B.** `H action H/D` is sofic.

*Proof.* Identical to Lemma 4 of the NME proof. Step 1: for each `k`,
`sigma^k D sigma^(-k) = intersect_M sigma^k D_M sigma^(-k)`, and
`sigma^k D_M sigma^(-k)` contains the normal subgroup `sigma^k K_M sigma^(-k)`
of `L`, with `L / sigma^k K_M sigma^(-k) = L/K_M = pi_M(L)` amenable. So its
normal core has amenable quotient, and (C2) of
[[separating-sofic-quotient-sets-make-an-action-sofic]] makes
`L action L/(sigma^k D sigma^(-k))` sofic. Step 2: the `L`-orbits of `H/D` are
`L`-isomorphic to these coset spaces, so `L action H/D` is sofic by GKP
Proposition 2.16 ([[gkp-sofic-action-toolkit]]). Step 3: `L` is co-amenable in
`H`, so [[sofic-actions-restrict-to-co-amenable-subgroups]] gives the claim. ∎

The amenable quotient `pi_M(L)` was the only input of Step 1; local finiteness
was not used.

## 4. Separation at coordinate 0

**Lemma C.** For `b in B_0`:
* (i) `rho(b) in D` implies `psi(b) in psi(F_-)`;
* (ii) `rho(b) in sigma^(-1) D sigma` implies `psi(b) in psi(F_+)`;
* (iii) `rho(b) = 1` implies `psi(b) = 1`.

*Proof.* Lemma 5 of the NME proof, with `r_0` replaced by `psi|_(B_0)`:
`pi_0(D_0) = pi_0(rho(F_-)) = psi(F_-)`; `sigma^(-1) rho(F_-) sigma = rho(F_+)`
and `sigma^(-1) K_1 sigma` is trivial at coordinate `0`; (iii) is `pi_0`. ∎

## 5. Fold and conclusion

Let `E <= G` be finite. Fix Britton-reduced forms of `E^(-1)E \ {1}`, and let
`E_+` (syllables between `t` and `t^(-1)`), `E_-` (between `t^(-1)` and `t`),
`E_1` (elements of `t`-length `0`) be as in section 7 of the NME proof, so
`E_+ <= B_0 \ F_+`, `E_- <= B_0 \ F_-`, `E_1 <= B_0 \ {1}`. Take `psi` from
(AA). By Lemma C the hypotheses of Lemma 6 of the NME proof (the fold with
enlarged stabilizer `D >= rho(F_-)`, whose proof uses only `rho(F_-) <= D`,
`sigma^(-1) rho(F_-) sigma = rho(F_+)` and the three separations) hold, so
`Phi : G -> Z wr^*_(H/D) H` is injective on `E`. That wreath product is sofic by
[[gkp-free-generalized-wreath-soficity]], using Lemma A and Lemma B. A group
that is locally embedded, finite set by finite set, into sofic groups is sofic
(Corollary of [[hnn-folds-into-free-coset-wreath]]). ∎

## 6. Consistency checks

* **Recovers the NME theorem.** Under (N) and (S), Lemma 2 of the NME proof
  yields a configuration over the locally finite, hence amenable, group
  `P_inf` with `pi_0 o rho = r_0` separating as (S') requires. So (AA) holds.
* **Does not "prove" residual finiteness.** The target `H` is
  residually-amenable-by-`Z`; for `BS(2,3)` the NME calibration (section 9)
  shows the alphabet is necessarily unbounded, and nothing here produces
  finite quotients of `G`.
* **Amenable alphabets are not automatically available.** For BG the chain
  obstruction [[bg-shift-chains-die-in-residually-linear-alphabets]] kills
  every residually periodic, residually linear, residually solvable or compact
  alphabet, so (AA) at a singular extreme is a statement about exotic amenable
  groups and is not a relabelling of residual finiteness.
* **Radical constraint.** By `magnus-piece-meets-nontrivial-linton-radical`, a
  nontrivial Linton radical meeting `B_0` must die in every target with trivial
  rationally perfect radical. `pi_M(L)` may be perfect (the BG chain group is
  perfect), so the targets here are not in that ruled-out class; this is
  consistent with the constraint, not a proof that (AA) can hold.
