title: Arbitrary products of sigma-finite type-II-infinity factors have uniqueness
---

If `(M_j)_(j in J)` is any family of sigma-finite type-II-infinity factors,
then every two unital embeddings

```text
Z -> product_(j in J) M_j
```

are approximately unitarily equivalent in norm.  The product itself may be
non-sigma-finite, have nonseparable predual, and act only on nonseparable
Hilbert spaces.
