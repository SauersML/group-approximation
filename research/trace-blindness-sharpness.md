title: The trace-blindness of II₁ factors is sharp
invalidates: [invariant-size-argument]
---

Take `R = ⊗_{n∈ℤ} M₂` with the Bernoulli shift `θ` and `N = ⊗_{n≥0} M₂`:
then `θ(N) ⊊ N`, `θ` is properly outer, and `M = R ⋊_θ ℤ` is a `II₁`
factor whose implementing unitary satisfies `uNu* ⊊ N`. So no
conjugation-invariant strictly-monotone size exists in `II₁`, and NO
invariant argument — index, entropy, or undiscovered — can settle the open
branch. The obstruction, if there is one, can only be stability.
