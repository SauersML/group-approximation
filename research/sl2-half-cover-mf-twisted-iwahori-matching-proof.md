The full proof is in the target note under `## Proofs`. This route records its dependency chain, and every step is
self-contained.

1. **Presentation (P).** Serre, *Trees*, II.1.4 writes `SL_2(Z[1/2])` as an amalgam. Lifting to the universal
   cover:
   - vertex preimages are `B_3`;
   - the edge preimage is generated by `x^2`, `T_A` and `U_A`, which holds because it contains `ker(B_3 ->
     PSL_2(Z)) = <x^2>` and its image has index 3;
   - the lift exponents are exact, as computed in `lifts.py`.
2. **Block reduction.** Finite-spectrum functional calculus, followed by near-identity unitary conjugation, makes
   the vertex relations exact. Then `c = x^2` is exactly central. On each `c`-eigenspace the relators `(T)` and
   `(U)` become the two defects of `delta` with `gamma = e^(-i theta/6)`.
3. **Theorem D.** `Delta(0) = 0` from the trivial representation. `Delta(pi) = 0` from the pullback of `SL_2(F_3)`
   into `2T`, with `-I -> -1`. That pullback is an exact finite-group representation, and `control_pi.py` checks
   it.
4. **Theorem B.** `Z(Delta)` is closed under tensor products (defects add), complex conjugation, and limits (the
   Lipschitz bound `|gamma - gamma'| = 2 sin(|theta - theta'|/12)`). It contains `0` and, by step 3, `pi`.
5. **Theorem A.**
   - (<=): the twisted sector at `pi/m`, direct-summed with regular representations of congruence quotients of the
     residually finite group `G`.
   - (=>): exactify, pass to the angles that recur infinitely often, and use the subgroup of step 4. If that
     subgroup does not reach `pi/m`, then some `z^j` with `m` not dividing `j` becomes asymptotically trivial,
     which contradicts faithfulness.
6. **Theorem C.** Two facts give the bound:
   - `det(A B^-1)` lies in `e^(i n theta/6) mu_6`;
   - `||A - B|| = 2 sin(max|phase|/2) >= 2 sin(|tau|/(2n))`.

   The winding is locally constant because no eigenvalue of `A B^-1` equals `-1` while `delta < 2`.
