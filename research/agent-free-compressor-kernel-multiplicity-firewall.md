Let `theta:F->B` be an epimorphism from a nonabelian finitely generated free
group to a finite group.  Let `D` be a finite group with an action

```text
beta:B->Aut(D),
```

and form the quotient-covariant packet group

```text
Gamma=D semidirect_(beta theta) F.                    (FKM1)
```

Fix any finite-dimensional representation `pi` of `D semidirect B`.  For
every finite-dimensional representation `kappa` of `F`, the formulas

```text
rho(d)=pi(d) tensor 1,
rho(f)=pi(theta(f)) tensor kappa(f)                   (FKM2)
```

define an exact representation of `Gamma`.  In particular, all finite root
relations internal to `D` and all covariance rows which read only the finite
quotient action are blind to the second tensor factor.

This blindness persists on every nonzero packet carrier.  If `p in C[D]`
acts under `pi` as a nonzero projection and `1!=k in ker(theta)`, then for
every `M` there is a choice of `kappa` for which the adjoint carrier

```text
K=End(pi(p)H_pi tensor H_kappa)                       (FKM3)
```

has fixed fraction at most `1/M` under `Ad rho(k)`.  Its normalized adjoint
density in the whole matrix algebra is nevertheless

```text
(rank pi(p)/dim H_pi)^2,                              (FKM4)
```

independent of `kappa`.

Consequently a payload compiler cannot obtain
`agent-free-compressor-payload-kernel-fixed-sector` from a finite root table,
its quotient covariance, and root spectral support alone.  The surviving
relation must act on the free-kernel multiplicity itself.  Equivalently, it
must be a mixed payload--kernel row which fails in `(FKM2)` when the forbidden
payload carrier is nonzero; adding more finite roots or more relations whose
`F`-action factors through `theta` cannot suffice.
