**ESTABLISHED** by citation, `hyperbolic-cubulated-rf-citation`.

A word-hyperbolic group acting properly and cocompactly on a CAT(0) cube complex is
virtually special, hence linear over `Z` and residually finite.

Finite classical `C'(1/6)` presentations, and finite `C'(1/4)–T(4)` presentations giving
hyperbolic groups, satisfy the hypothesis by Wise's cubulation theorem.
