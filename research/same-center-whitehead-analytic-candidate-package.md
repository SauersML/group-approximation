Let

```text
Delta=St_20(L_(F_2)(1,2)),       z=x_13(s_1t_1).
```

If a norm-matrix-corona representation keeps the involution `z` nontrivial,
active-core reblocking gives an equivalent asymptotic representation on
which its negative spectral projection has normalized mass at least one
fixed `alpha>0`.  The commuting double-commutator construction and the
three same-center Leavitt factorizations then give a common projection `F`
with

```text
tau(F) >= alpha/4-o(1)
```

and an exactified packet decomposition

```text
FH=(C^2)^(tensor 3) tensor M.                          (SCP1)
```

On this one physical carrier the two literal adjacent-prefix Whitehead
words have the normal forms

```text
W_1|_F=SWAP_(1,2) tensor V_1,
W_2|_F=SWAP_(2,3) tensor V_2.                          (SCP2)
```

Their ordinary Coxeter braid implies that `V_1,V_2` are unitarily conjugate.
Removing the two canonical swaps inside the recovered `M_8(C)` label algebra
therefore exposes the two reservoir gauges on the same carrier.  Multiplying
inside that finite coordinate by the fixed label reflections gives the
canonical analytic candidates

```text
T_1^cand=C_(1/4) tensor V_1,
T_2^cand=C_(1/8) tensor V_2.                           (SCP3)
```

If the **actual** native selector rows with source fractions `1/2` and `1/4`
are authenticated as `(SCP3)`, up to `o(1)` normalized-HS error on one
positive model-dependent reducing subcorner, the affine rank calculation
forces the common positive-sign fraction to converge both to `1/2` and to
`5/6`.  Quantitatively the two row defects have sum at least `1/3-o(1)`.

The authentication hypothesis in the preceding paragraph is not part of
this claim.  In particular, `(SCP3)` is finite-coordinate analytic
multiplication, not an assertion that either unequal controlled selector is
an ordinary group-word occurrence.  Full self-Grams of `W_1,W_2` and their
braid do not supply that missing typing.

Every unconditional assertion above uses only finite-order rounding,
ordinary Steinberg/Leavitt identities, a fixed finite packet exactification,
the Coxeter braid, and finite-dimensional rank.  It uses no Property `(T)`,
Kazhdan input, asymptotic-commutant theorem, or literature result.
