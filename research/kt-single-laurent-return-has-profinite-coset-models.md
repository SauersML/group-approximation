**ESTABLISHED EXACT COHERENT FIREWALL; THE GLOBAL ROOT REMAINS OPEN.**

Use the pinned rank-three characteristic-two actor

~~~text
P=F_2[x_1,x_2,x_3],
Gamma=EL_3(P),
h=e_12(x_2/x_1).
~~~

Put `y=x_2/x_1` and

~~~text
S=P[y]=F_2[x_1,y,x_3] subset F_2[x_1^(+-1),x_2^(+-1),x_3^(+-1)],
H=<Gamma,h>.
~~~

Then

~~~text
H=EL_3(S).                                                   (SLR1)
~~~

For `N>=1`, let

~~~text
T_N=S/(x_1,y,x_3)^(N+1),
P_N=image(P->T_N),
Q_N=EL_3(T_N),
Gamma_N=EL_3(P_N).
~~~

The quotient map `q_N:H->Q_N` is onto, maps `Gamma` onto `Gamma_N`, and

~~~text
q_N(h)=e_12(ybar) notin Gamma_N.                            (SLR2)
~~~

More strongly, the finite coset actions `Q_N/Gamma_N` locally converge to
`H/Gamma`: every prescribed finite set of distinct cosets remains distinct
for all sufficiently large `N`, and every prescribed finite multiplication
table in `H` is eventually exact and injective. Consequently

~~~text
W_H=(direct_sum_(H/Gamma) C_2) rtimes H
~~~

is residually finite through the finite quotients

~~~text
W_N=(direct_sum_(Q_N/Gamma_N) C_2) rtimes Q_N.              (SLR3)
~~~

In the normalized left regular representation of `W_N`, let `a_z` denote
the lamp at `z in Q_N/Gamma_N`, and set

~~~text
V_N=lambda_N(a_(Gamma_N)),
p_N=(1+V_N)/2,
U_N=lambda_N(q_N(h)).
~~~

Every element of `q_N(Gamma)` fixes the base coset, hence commutes with
`p_N`. Nevertheless `q_N(h)Gamma_N` is a different coset, and the regular
character gives

~~~text
tr(p_N)=1/2,
tr(p_N U_N p_N U_N^*)=1/4,
||[p_N,U_N]||_2=1/sqrt(2).                                 (SLR4)
~~~

The normalized regular characters of `W_N` converge to the canonical
character of `W_H`. Thus these are not merely algebraic finite
counterexamples: they reproduce every fixed canonical mixed moment of the
actual coherent `Gamma union {h}` wreath packet while retaining the maximal
named-return wall. In particular, all relations among any finite Kazhdan
packet `K subset Gamma` and `h` that hold in the actor `H` hold exactly in
every model.

The wall is stable under arbitrary sublinear padding. If `D_N=|W_N|`,
`r_N=o(D_N)`, and an arbitrary `r_N`-dimensional block is appended to both
`p_N` and `U_N`, then

~~~text
||[p_N direct_sum p_N', U_N direct_sum U_N']||_2^2
 >= (D_N/(D_N+r_N))/2 = 1/2-o(1).                          (SLR5)
~~~

It is also quantitatively perturbation-stable: for contractions
`p_N_tilde` and unitaries `U_N_tilde`,

~~~text
||[p_N_tilde,U_N_tilde]||_2
 >= 1/sqrt(2)
    -2||p_N_tilde-p_N||_2-2||U_N_tilde-U_N||_2.            (SLR6)
~~~

Therefore no relative same-dimensional or flexible exactification theorem
whose input is only the coherent packet `Gamma union {h}` can force a
`Gamma`-central half projection to become `h`-central. The collapse in a
genuine finite-dimensional representation of the full Laurent actor uses
additional Laurent directions, equivalently the requirement that `x_1`
remain invertible. Any successful Kun--Thom matrix argument must propagate
those later-word relations; the smallest coherent packet is provably
insufficient.
