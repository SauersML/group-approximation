Caprace--Hume prove that a group acting on an irreducible non-spherical
non-affine building is acylindrically hyperbolic if a chamber has finite
stabilizer and its orbit contains an apartment.  Their application verifies
these hypotheses for orthogonal forms of Kac--Moody groups over arbitrary
fields.

The source states the application for **orthogonal forms**.  It does not
state it for every group carrying the Kac--Moody label and does not identify
those forms with the simple irreducible twin-building lattices used by
Caprace--Remy.  Importing the theorem with this qualifier proves exactly the
claim and no character statement.
