Evington--Tikuisis, *The real and stable rank of tracially complete C-star-algebras*,
[arXiv:2604.24206v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24206), prove real rank zero
in Theorem A and stable rank one in Theorem B for every factorial tracially
complete C-star-algebra with CPoU.  Their Theorem C adds, under the type-II1
hypothesis, `T(M)=X`.  These are exactly the hypotheses and the three
conclusions of the target.
