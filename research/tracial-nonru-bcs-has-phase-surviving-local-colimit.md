**OPEN.** Let `B_*` be the finite Boolean constraint system of
`mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists` (tracially satisfiable, not
`R^U`-satisfiable). Do there exist `n >= 2` and finite local data `D` for
`B_*` and `n`, in the sense of
`finite-readout-bcs-decoders-are-phase-surviving-local-colimits`, such that
`J` has order exactly `n` in the local colimit `Lambda(D)`?

By (LC1) this is the same as an exact finite-readout decoder of `B_*` into
some group corner. A positive answer solves
`non-ru-bcs-is-satisfiable-in-a-group-factor` through
`nonru-bcs-group-factor-via-phase-surviving-local-colimit`, and the witness
`Lambda(D)` is a finitely presented nonhyperlinear group generated by finitely
many finite subgroups, with `J^(n/p)` in its finite residual for some prime
`p | n` (LC2, LC3).

## Constraints on any witness

- **Nonabelian local groups are needed where the support is affine-unsafe.**
  If a context group `A_c` is abelian, its decoder is an abelian readout,
  so the induced trace factors through a coset refinement of the context game
  (CD1 of `group-corner-decoders-split-into-coset-and-matrix-readouts`). If,
  moreover, the readouts are signed group elements, the decoder is affine and
  (CS2) of `group-corner-exact-compilers-force-source-affine-safety` forces
  affine-safe supports. The active JNVWY outputs have no affine-safe perfect
  model (`jnvwy-active-output-has-no-affine-safe-perfect-model`), and the
  magic-square AND gadget of `matrix-conj-forces-unsafe-support` has a fixed
  affine value gap (`affine-safe-value-of-magic-square-and-gadget-below-one`).
- **The gluing must be cyclic** (LC4). Acyclic incidence graphs always carry a
  finite-dimensional representation that keeps the phase, and `B_*` has no
  finite-dimensional perfect model.
- **No finite-dimensional certificate exists** (LC3.1). Every
  finite-dimensional representation of `Lambda(D)` kills `p_omega`, so survival
  of `J` must be certified by an infinite-dimensional or combinatorial argument
  (van Kampen pictures, curvature, an infinite quotient where `J` survives).

## Attempts

1. **Holonomy view (2026-09-13, hl-nh-compiler).** Along a spanning tree of
   `Inc(D)`, the forest induction of (LC4) builds regular-type
   representations of all local groups; each non-tree edge leaves a unitary
   mismatch, the stable letter of the finite graph of groups. For `B_*`,
   (LC3.1) says no choice of local types in any finite dimension trivializes
   these mismatches on the `omega`-sector. This is the exact-level form of the
   stable-letter bookkeeping in
   `atlas-a4-context-network-simultaneous-hs-exactification`; there the
   mismatches are only required to be Hilbert--Schmidt small, which is the
   approximate question that hyperlinearity would answer. Not a construction.
2. **Curvature certificates (untested).** For abelian local groups the
   Slofstra--Zhang planar-picture argument gives survival under incidence
   degree/girth bounds (`slofstra-zhang-high-girth-phase-survival`). An
   analogue for nonabelian finite local groups would bound closed pictures
   over the complex of groups. Subdividing a variable by equality contexts
   does not change `Lambda(D)`, so girth cannot be manufactured for free; the
   curvature has to come from the local groups themselves, e.g. large girth of
   the coset graphs of pairs `H_v, H_w` inside `A_c` for variables that share
   cycles. Developability theorems for non-positively curved complexes of
   groups are the natural import; none is pinned at source on main, and
   hyperlinearity of the resulting CAT(0) colimits is not known, so no
   firewall follows either.
3. **Curvature certificates, tested (2026-09-13, hl-nh-local-colimit).**
   - **Local cap.** Commuting nonscalar readouts through subgroups meeting in
     `J` have coset graphs with 4-cycles, and functionally dependent readouts
     force larger intersections (`commuting-nonscalar-readouts-force-coset-four-cycles`).
     So a context group can supply at most a right-angle corner at a pair of
     nonscalar variables.
   - **Necessary.** Any barycentric curvature certificate needs at least four
     contexts per incidence cycle of nonscalar variables, and equality or
     negation subdivision cannot supply them
     (`barycentric-curvature-certificates-need-incidence-girth-eight`).
   - **Sufficient.** Local data of incidence girth at least 12, with variable
     subgroups meeting pairwise in `J` and proper inclusions, keeps `J` at full
     order for arbitrary finite nonabelian local groups
     (`central-local-data-of-incidence-girth-twelve-keeps-the-phase`). The proof
     is Prytula's cone metric plus Bridson--Haefliger II.12.28, imported. It
     recovers the `(3,6)` Slofstra--Zhang case for `p = 2`.
   - **What survives of the question.** For such local data survival is
     automatic, so what remains is a design and girth question:
     `some-nonru-bcs-has-central-local-data-of-girth-twelve` (OPEN), which feeds
     `non-hyperlinear-group` directly.
   - **For `B_*` itself.** Cycles with fewer than four nonscalar contexts rule
     out barycentric certificates. Any positive answer through curvature needs a
     source, or a `B_*`-simulating system, whose incidence graph has no such
     short cycles.
