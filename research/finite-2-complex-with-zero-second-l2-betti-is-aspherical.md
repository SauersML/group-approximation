**ESTABLISHED** through `finite-2-complex-zero-second-l2-betti-aspherical-proof`.

Let K be a finite connected 2-dimensional CW complex with fundamental group G,
and assume G is infinite. If

  χ(K) + b₁^(2)(G) = 0,

then π₂(K) = 0, so K is aspherical. Equivalently, b₂^(2)(K̃; N(G)) = 0 already
forces the universal cover K̃ to be contractible.

No novelty is claimed.
