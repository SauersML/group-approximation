Citation import, not a reproof. Source: C. Llosa Isenrich, E. Schesler and
X. Wu, *Infinitely presented simple groups separated by homological finiteness
properties*, arXiv:2510.01952v1 (2 Oct 2025). Read on 2026-09-13 from the arXiv
abstract page and, through fetches that quoted it, the arXiv HTML.

Verbatim:

1. Abstract: "Given a finitely generated linear group $G$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, we
   construct a simple group $\Gamma$ that has the same finiteness properties as
   $G$ and admits $G$ as a quasi-retract. As an application, we construct a
   simple group of type $\mathrm{FP}_{\infty}$ that is not finitely presented.
   Moreover we show that for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there is a simple group of
   type $\mathrm{FP}_n$ that is neither finitely presented nor of type
   $\mathrm{FP}_{n+1}$. Since our simple groups arise as Röver--Nekrashevych
   groups, this answers a question of Zaremsky."
2. "Theorem 1.4. Let H be a finitely generated subgroup of GL_n(ℚ) for some
   n∈ℕ. There exists a simple group G that has the following properties: (1) G
   has the same finiteness properties as H, (2) H is a subgroup of G, (3) G
   admits a quasi-retract onto H."
3. "Corollary 1.2. For every n∈ℕ there is a simple group of type FP_n(ℤ) that is
   neither of type FP_{n+1}(ℤ) nor finitely presented."
4. "Corollary 1.3. There is a simple group of type FP_∞(ℤ) that is not finitely
   presented."
5. "Question 1.12 (Zaremsky). Does every countable group embed in a simple group
   of type FP_2(ℤ)? Recall that Leary proved in [Lea18] that every countable
   group embeds in a FP_2(ℤ) group."

"Same finiteness properties" covers the homotopical properties `F_m` and the
homological properties `FP_m(R)` over commutative unital rings `R ≠ 0` (their
Theorem 1.1 is stated for both). This reading of the definition comes from a
summarizing fetch, not from the PDF.
