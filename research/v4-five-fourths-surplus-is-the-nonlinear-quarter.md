**ESTABLISHED.**  On one forbidden atom let `P,Q` be the two commuting raw
radical involutions and let `e_r`, `r in F_2^2`, be their four equal-rank
joint spectral atoms.  In the synchronized V4 failure profile `(VOF2)`, two
of the three failure projections are affine halves of this square and the
third is one singleton atom.  After relabeling coordinates,

```text
F_00=sum_(ell_0(r)=1)e_r,      rank fraction 1/2,
F_11=sum_(ell_1(r)=1)e_r,      rank fraction 1/2,
F_01=e_(r_0),                  rank fraction 1/4.       (VQN1)
```

The support reflections of the first two projections are words in `P,Q`
(up to a scalar central sign).  The support reflection

```text
1-2e_(r_0)                                            (VQN2)
```

is not.  Indeed every word in the elementary abelian group `<P,Q>` is a
character.  A nonconstant character is negative on exactly two of the four
atoms; a constant word is negative on zero or four.  No such word selects
one atom.

Consequently the two literal Pauli-half supports have total trace exactly
`tau(P_f)`, the available parent capacity.  The strict term

```text
tau(F_00)+tau(F_01)+tau(F_11)-tau(P_f)=tau(P_f)/4     (VQN3)
```

is entirely the singleton `(VQN2)`.  An HNN/Hecke edge built only from the
named half reflections can at best rearrange capacity one and cannot produce
the five-fourths contradiction.

Affine S3/V4 chart changes do not alter this conclusion: they permute affine
hyperplanes and atoms, so they merely move which singleton carries `(VQN2)`.
A larger finite crossed product may contain a rank-one reflection in one
chosen simple block, but this alone gives no proper return: it also has exact
finite representations and all complementary types must be included in the
capacity ledger.  For the raw full-scale merge that accounting is precisely
the equal-reservoir deficit of
`raw-radical-full-scale-merge-needs-an-equal-reservoir`.

Thus the positive one-sided route has a sharply localized target.  It must
authenticate the singleton support reflection on the same contextual
carrier, or place that quarter through a charged proper-corner section whose
companion reservoir is charged.  The literal words `P,Q` and the existing
finite S3 chart alone cannot do it.
