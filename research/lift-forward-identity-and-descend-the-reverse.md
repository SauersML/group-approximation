Localize both rules to H=<M union S>; over G they act independently
on its left H-cosets. Transfer the same local functions to P using
the marked lifts. The injectivity assumption on lifted(S)lifted(M)
makes its product partition identical to the target SM partition.
Every source input pattern on this finite set therefore corresponds
to a target input pattern. The identity sigma tau=id lifts literally
to sigma_P tau_P=id.

Surjunctivity of P makes tau_P bijective, so tau_P sigma_P=id.
For x in A^H, its pullback x composed with pi intertwines both source
rules with the target rules. Pullback is injective because pi is
onto. The reverse identity therefore descends to H and hence to G.
New identifications in MS present no problem: an identity of local
functions remains an identity after identifying variables.

For the finite-presentation assertion, impose precisely the forward
rectangle equalities on formal memory generators. The map to H
preserves every defining relation. Forward products equal in H are
equal in the presented group by construction; unequal target
products cannot become equal there because of the map to H.
Thus the same left inverse lifts. A reverse identity upstairs would
descend, contradicting the supplied strict defect. Choosing one
anchor per product class gives the stated relator bound.
