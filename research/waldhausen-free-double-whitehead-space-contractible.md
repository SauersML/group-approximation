Let F be a finite-rank free group, let R be any subgroup of F, and form the
double

    L = F *_R F.

Then Waldhausen's Whitehead space Wh_Z(L) is contractible. In particular the
ordinary Whitehead group Wh(L)=pi_1 Wh_Z(L) vanishes.

No finite-generation assumption on R is needed. This is established by
[[waldhausen-free-double-whitehead-space-proof]].
