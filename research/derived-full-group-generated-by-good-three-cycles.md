**ESTABLISHED (import).** Let `(X, φ)` be an infinite minimal subshift over `Z`.
- A clopen set `U` is *good* if `φ^(−1)U`, `U`, `φU` are pairwise disjoint.
- For a good `U`, `σ_U ∈ [[φ]]` is the element that is the identity off `φ^(−1)U ∪ U ∪ φU` and cyclically permutes `U → φU → φ^(−1)U → U` by the actions of `φ`, `φ^(−2)`, `φ`.

Then `[[φ]]′` is generated by the `σ_U` with `U` good.

Source: Y. Cornulier, "Groupes pleins-topologiques [d'après Matui, Juschenko, Monod, …]", Séminaire Bourbaki 1064 (2013), arXiv:2002.09342, Définition `d_bon` and Lemme `eng_cb`. The proof uses simplicity of `[[φ]]′` (Matui) and Matui, Internat. J. Math. 17 (2006), Lemma 5.1.
