Let `B` be a diffuse nonamenable finite factor and put

```text
Q=B tensor_bar L(Z).
```

Write `t` for the Haar generator of `L(Z)`.  This is exactly the second
vertex algebra in the central HNN/amalgamated-free-product realization

```text
N *_B (B tensor_bar L(Z)).                              (CHR1)
```

For `0<r<1`, define the Poisson maps

```text
Phi_r(b tensor t^n)=r^|n| b tensor t^n.                 (CHR2)
```

Then:

1. `Phi_r` is normal, unital, trace preserving and completely positive,
   and `Phi_r -> id_Q` pointwise in `L^2` as `r -> 1`.
2. Its `L^2` implementation is compact **relative to `B`**: under

   ```text
   L^2(Q)=direct_sum_(n in Z) L^2(B)t^n,
   ```

   it is the norm limit of the finite sums

   ```text
   sum_(|n|<=m) r^|n| e_n,                              (CHR3)
   ```

   where `e_n` is a rank-one projection in the right-`B` Hilbert-module
   sense.
3. It is not compact over the scalars: `Phi_r` fixes the
   infinite-dimensional subspace `L^2(B)` pointwise.  No scalar-compact
   operator can do this.
4. `Q` has no nonzero amenable corner.  First,

   ```text
   Z(Q)=1 tensor L(Z),
   ```

   and for every nonzero central projection `z`, the finite algebra `zQ`
   contains the expected unital copy `B tensor z`.  Amenability of `zQ`
   would force amenability of that expected copy and hence of `B`.
   For a general nonzero projection `p in Q`, let `z` be its central
   support.  Amenability of finite von Neumann algebras is Morita invariant,
   so amenability of `pQp` would imply amenability of `zQ`, already
   excluded.  Equivalently, in the direct-integral decomposition over
   `L(Z)`, almost every nonzero fiber is a nonzero corner of `B`.
5. The inclusion `B subset Q` is nevertheless coamenable/amenable relative
   to `B`, because the quotient tensor leg `L(Z)` is amenable.

For the arithmetic central HNN endpoint take

```text
B=L(SL_3(Z)),       N=L(SL_3(Z[1/2])).                  (CHR4)
```

The stable letter therefore supplies exactly the deformation in `(CHR2)`:
base-relative compactness and base-relative amenability, but neither scalar
compactness nor any nonzero scalar-amenable corner.  Popa's
property-`(T)` contradiction cannot start from this datum.  The maps fix
`B` identically, so uniform convergence on the unit ball of `B` is automatic
and perfectly compatible with relative compactness; scalar compactness is
precisely what fails.

Consequently an HNN commutant unitary does not manufacture the missing
uniform scalar-compact deformation in
`sl3-gao-carrier-has-uniform-popa-compactness`, nor does it force an
amenable/hyperfinite corner of the algebra generated together with the
lattice factor.  Any successful Popa route must use additional
finite-coordinate tightness of a hypothetical matrix/Gao carrier, not the
Haar leg, property `(T)`, or relative amenability alone.
