The body: Haagerup-Thorbjornsen linearization reduces strong convergence to a
single amplified self-adjoint matrix; PFF makes the ambient deterministic-
strong; the B-shift dilates to a finite cyclic permutation, Haar-conjugated
into a uniformly random N-cycle; Bordenave-Collins gives strong asymptotic
freeness of the random cycle from the PFF family. Open point: scalar vs
operator-valued (L-corner) Bordenave-Collins.
