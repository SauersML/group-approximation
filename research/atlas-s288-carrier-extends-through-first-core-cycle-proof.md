## Extend the A4-square side

Fix `rho=(1 2 3)` and a double transposition `beta` in `A4`, and retain

```text
C_0=A4 x A4,
r=(rho,rho),       z=(rho,rho^-1),       b=(beta,beta).       (S576-1)
```

Let `c` swap the factors, as in the S288 carrier.  Choose a transposition
`delta in S4-A4` which commutes with `beta` and conjugates `rho` to
`rho^-1`.  Let `D` apply the outer automorphism `Ad(delta)` to both A4
factors.  Then `c,D` are commuting involutions.  Put

```text
u=cD.                                                        (S576-2)
```

Direct calculation gives

```text
u r u=r^-1,       [u,z]=[u,c]=[u,b]=1.                       (S576-3)
```

Consequently

```text
H'=(A4 x A4) semidirect <c,D>,       |H'|=576,
<r,z,b> = A4 x A4,                   order 144,
<r,z,c,u> ~= S3 x S3,                order 36,
E=<b,c,u> ~= C2^3.                                          (S576-4)
```

Thus `u` is exactly the missing first-chart involution: it inverts the first
order-three direction and commutes with the other local S3 factor.

## The collision and new edge inside S4 times C2

Use the Coxeter generators `a,s,b` of `S4`, with

```text
a^2=s^2=b^2=[a,b]=(a s)^3=(s b)^3=1.
```

Adjoin a central involution, again labeled `c`, and put

```text
L=S4 x C2(c),       u=a b,       y=b s.                       (S576-5)
```

Then

```text
J=<b,y,c> ~= S3 x C2,       |J|=12,
<u,y> ~= A4,                |<u,y>|=12,
<b,c,u>=<a,b,c> ~= C2^3=E.                                  (S576-6)
```

Indeed `u y=(a b)(b s)=a s` has order three, which is precisely the new core
relation `(u y)^3=1`.  Since `c` is central in `J`, the collision word remains
the same S3 identity used in the S288 construction.

## Exact regular amalgam

The two labeled copies of `E` in `H'` and `L` have matching regular
restrictions on 576 points:

```text
Reg(H')|_E       =72 Reg(E),
12 Reg(L)|_E     =12*6 Reg(E)=72 Reg(E).                      (S576-7)
```

Choose a permutation conjugacy between these labeled E-actions and let
`Q_1<=S_576` be generated by the resulting copies of `H'` and `L`.  Both
vertex embeddings remain faithful, so every subgroup order in `(S576-4)`
and `(S576-6)` survives.  This proves collision, the forward relations, the
existing adjacent core edge, and the new edge simultaneously.

The MSI verifier realizes `H'` explicitly on the 144-point regular A4-square,
checks orders `576,144,36,8`, verifies the `S4 x C2` orders `48,12,12,8`, and
checks the multiplicity identity `(S576-7)` exactly.
