unity. A nonzero word difference has modulus at least `2sin(pi/12)`.

at most `e^2/g^2`. It reduces both vertices, and replacing its four

matrices. They have zero diagonal, and the same holds after any word
derivative. The residual is diagonal, proving `L*f=0`. The artifact
gives a twelve-dimensional balanced block, its exact defect, and a
regular `A_4` comparison with identical generator multiplicities.
Trivial dilution preserves stationarity and makes its normalized-HS
distance and defect tend to zero.
