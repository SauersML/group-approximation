**OPEN.** Let `G` be a countably infinite group and `A`, `B` finite alphabets with `|A| != |B|`.
Then there is no equivariant homeomorphism `A^G -> B^G`. By Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon, such a map would
be a sliding block code with a sliding-block inverse.

Routes into this claim:

* `alphabet-conjugacy-invariance-from-gottschalk`. A conjugacy composed with the inclusion of the
  smaller shift is a strict automaton.
* `alphabet-conjugacy-invariance-from-bernoulli-maximality`. A conjugacy pushes uniform Bernoulli
  measure to a measure generated by a partition of entropy at most `log |B|`.

So a conjugacy across alphabet sizes over a group `G` would at once refute surjunctivity of `G` and
give a Bernoulli entropy deficit certificate over `G`. That makes it a sharp negative target.

## Attempts

* **Sofic groups.** Sofic entropy separates `log |A|` from `log |B|`. This is context only, not
  imported, and it uses finite models.
* **Design filters.** A conjugacy is a pair `(phi, phi^-1)` whose forward and reverse composites are
  both identities. The filters of `forward-relations-of-a-counterexample-have-a-nonsofic-core` and
  `table-groups-are-memory-holonomy-groups` apply to both composites, so the realized table core
  must be nonsofic on both sides. Over a host with decidable word problem, existence of a conjugacy
  with fixed memories is Sigma^0_1.
* **Where it dies.** No lower-bound mechanism for nonsofic hosts is known. By
  `borel-structurability-invariants-are-bernoulli-base-blind`, measure-free Borel invariants of the
  free parts cannot separate alphabet sizes. The topological classification needs an invariant that
  sees the exact sliding-block structure.
