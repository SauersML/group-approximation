Every torsion-free group `G` of type F_∞ with `cd G = ∞` has a subgroup
isomorphic to Thompson's group `F`.

This is the nonexistence answer to Zaremsky Problem 2.8
(`zaremsky-2-08-infinite-dim-torsion-free-f-infinity-without-f`).

**Strength.** `F` lies outside Kropholler's class LH𝔉, and LH𝔉 is closed under
subgroups, so LH𝔉 groups contain no `F`. This claim would therefore contain
Kropholler's theorem (torsion-free LH𝔉 groups of type FP_∞ have finite cd, for
F_∞ groups) as a special case. It would also give finite cd for every torsion-free
F_∞ group that is residually finite, coherent, or satisfies the Tits alternative.
Of the known machines producing torsion-free infinite-dimensional F_∞ groups, only
Thompson-like groups are known, and they all contain `F`.
