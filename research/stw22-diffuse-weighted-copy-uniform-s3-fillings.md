Let `(N,tau)` range over all II1 factors with separable predual. For
`m>=1` and a positive contraction `a in N`, give

```text
F_(N,m)(a)={(v_1,...,v_m):
 v_j^*v_j=a and (v_iv_i^*)(v_jv_j^*)=0 for i!=j}
```

the Hilbert-sum `2`-metric. Whenever

```text
m tau(supp(a))<1,                                      (W3-1)
```

every continuous map `S^3->F_(N,m)(a)` contained in the ball of radius

```text
rho_W,3(epsilon)=min(epsilon,1)^3/216000000            (W3-2)
```

about one point extends over `B^4` inside the concentric
`epsilon`-ball. The modulus is independent of `N,m,a` and the numerical
size of the positive slack.

Every fibre satisfying (W3-1) is three-connected. Together with the lower
degree theorems, the family is uniformly equi-`LC^3`.
