Literature import, credited in full.

Sanjeev Arora, Boaz Barak, David Steurer, *Subexponential Algorithms for
Unique Games and Related Problems*, FOCS 2010, pp. 563--572; J. ACM 62(5),
Article 42 (2015), doi `10.1145/2775105`.

Verified on 2026-09-18 from the author PDF `boazbarak.org/Papers/ssesubexp.pdf`
(version dated April 8, 2010):

* the abstract, and pp. 2--3 (the reduction-time remark and the dependence on
  completeness near 1);
* Section 1.4, p. 6 (regular, weighted, self-loop conventions; `exp(·)` and
  absolute `O(·)` constants);
* Theorem 2.1 with its restated proof, p. 8 (the proof's output has measure at
  most `2δ`);
* Theorem 5.1, p. 14, and the regularity assumption of Section 5.1;
* Appendix A, p. 23 (reduction to regular instances).

The claim quotes these statements verbatim.
