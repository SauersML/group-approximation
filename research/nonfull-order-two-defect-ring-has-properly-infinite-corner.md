**ESTABLISHED.**  Let `B = L_{F_2}(1,2)` be the binary Leavitt algebra,
realized as the unital `F_2`-algebra generated by the four operators

```text
X_0 v_k = v_(2k),   X_1 v_k = v_(2k+1),   Y_0, Y_1 the partial inverses,
Y_i X_j = delta_(ij) 1_B,   X_0Y_0 + X_1Y_1 = 1_B                 (NF1)
```

on the `F_2`-space with basis `(v_k)_(k>=0)`.  On the free right
`B`-module `(+)_(r>=0) u_r B` let `S` be the forward shift, `T` the
backshift, and `M_fin(N,B)` the matrices with finitely many nonzero
entries.  Put

```text
R = F_2<S,T> + M_fin(N,B).                                        (NF2)
```

Then `R` is a countable unital ring and

```text
TS = 1,   e = 1 - ST = E_00(1_B) != 0,   2e = 0,                  (NF3)
ReR = M_fin(N,B),   R/ReR = F_2[z,z^(-1)],   eRe = B.             (NF4)
```

Consequences.

1. `e` is a nonzero defect of additive order two and is **nonfull**, since
   `R/ReR` is nonzero.
2. `R` admits **no** full-defect pair whatsoever: in the nonzero
   commutative quotient `F_2[z,z^(-1)]`, `ts = 1` forces `st = 1`, so the
   image of any defect is zero and cannot generate the unit ideal.
3. `eRe = B` satisfies the certificate `(PIC1)` with `p = e`, by `(NF1)`.
   Hence `EL_n(R)` is not MF for every `n >= 2`.
4. `R` is not a product in disguise: its centre is `F_2 1`, so it has no
   nontrivial central idempotent.
5. `EL_n(R) ->> EL_n(F_2[z,z^(-1)])` is a **nontrivial** MF-target
   homomorphism, the target being residually finite.  So the conclusion
   for `R` is non-MF, and **not** `Rad_MF(EL_n(R)) = EL_n(R)`.

Points 1--3 together say that the hypothesis of
`properly-infinite-corner-forces-non-mf-from-rank-two` is strictly weaker
than the ambient fullness hypothesis of
`full-defect-ring-non-mf-at-rank-two`: this ring satisfies the first and
provably fails the second.  Point 5 says the weakening costs something
real, namely the strength of the conclusion.
