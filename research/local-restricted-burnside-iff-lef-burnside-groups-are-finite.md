**ESTABLISHED** through `local-restricted-burnside-iff-lef-burnside-proof`.

**Definitions.**
- A group `L` is **LEF** if for every finite `K ⊆ L` there are a finite group
  `Q` and an injective map `φ: K -> Q` with `φ(xy) = φ(x)φ(y)` whenever
  `x, y, xy ∈ K`. This is the LEF instance of
  `local-approximation-properties-are-marked-closed`.
- `LRB(R, C)` and `LRB(m,N)` are as in
  `local-restricted-burnside-iff-partial-burnside-finite-quotients`: every finite
  group generated by `m` elements whose words of length `<= R` satisfy `h^N = 1`
  has order `<= C`, for some `R, C`.

**Statement.** Fix `m, N >= 2`.
1. `LRB(m,N)` holds iff every `m`-generated LEF group of exponent dividing `N`
   is finite.
2. **Quantitative form.** If `LRB(R, C)` holds, every `m`-generated LEF group of
   exponent dividing `N` has order `<= C`.
3. **Witness form.** If `LRB(m,N)` fails, there are finite `m`-generated marked
   groups `Q_n` whose words of length `<= n` satisfy `h^N = 1`, with
   `|Q_n| > n`, converging in the space of marked groups to an infinite LEF
   quotient of `B(m,N)`.
4. **Coulon stages.** Let `N` be odd with `N >= n_1(F_m)`, and let `G_k` be the
   stages of `free-burnside-is-a-limit-of-hyperbolic-groups`.
   - If every `m`-generated LEF group of exponent dividing `N` is finite, then
     `G_k` is not residually finite for every sufficiently large `k`.
   - If infinitely many `G_k` are residually finite, then `B(m,N)` itself is LEF.
     In particular, if every hyperbolic group is residually finite, every free
     Burnside group `B(m,N)` with `N` odd and `N >= n_1(F_m)` is an infinite LEF
     group of bounded exponent.

So at a large odd exponent the local restricted Burnside principle, and with it
the route `non-rf-hyperbolic-group-via-local-restricted-burnside`, is exactly the
LEF form of the Burnside problem: are finitely generated LEF groups of exponent
`N` finite? Residually finite ones are (Zelmanov), and hyperlinear ones are the
open `hyperlinear-fg-bounded-exponent-groups-are-finite`. No novelty is claimed,
and no literature search for the LEF form was done.
