Equation `(TJS1)` is the upper Julia cross-Gram corner.  Fixed Schur
complements and the other listed operations are available in every finite
tracial multiplicity algebra and commute with trace-preserving
homomorphisms, so the fixed-packet Julia firewall applies verbatim.  A
finite-matrix-only loss cannot arise from them.

For the group-word alternative, multiply

```text
 [u,v]=uvu^(-1)v^(-1),
 u^(-1)=[I -Y;0 I],       v^(-1)=[I 0;-X I].
```

The four block products give `(TJS2)`.  Tracial cyclicity cancels
`tr(YX)-tr(XY)` but leaves `tr(YXYX)`.  Hence the standard `GL_2`
commutator is not a trace-zero coefficient decoder.  By contrast two
adjacent roots in `GL_3` multiply into a third root with no backtracking
corner, which is exactly the Steinberg calculation already recorded in
`one-additive-commutator-suffices-for-julia-return`.
