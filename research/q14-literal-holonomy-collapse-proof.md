# Substitute the literal frame into q14 and use simplicity of A8

Under the mixed commutator, the literal second-chart involution
`u b u^(-1)` equals `b`.  The q14 relator is therefore the nontrivial first
chart word `(ab)^2=t20`.  Its normal closure in `A8` is all of `A8`, proving
source collapse.  The independent retraction to D16 proves that the marked
finite packet survives, so the failure is specifically loss of the external
charger rather than loss of the payload.
