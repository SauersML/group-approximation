**OPEN.** There is a lattice `Λ` in a group `H` that is either a connected, noncompact, simple real Lie
group with finite center, or an isotropic simple algebraic group over a non-Archimedean local field,
such that `Λ` has a p.m.p. action that is not sofic (Păunescu Definition 1.4).

Such `Λ` is linear, so every finitely generated subgroup is residually finite, and `Λ` is sofic. By
`howe-moore-lattice-nonsofic-action-transfers-to-mixing`, `Λ` would then have an essentially free mixing
nonsofic action, answering `mixing-free-nonsofic-action-of-sofic-group`.

## Attempts

* **A Kun--Thom pair inside `Λ`.** Theorem C needs Kazhdan `Γ <= G <= Λ` with `Γ` infranormal and not
  normal. Every recorded pair lives in `EL_r(F_q[x^(±1)]) ⋊ SL_d(Z)`, where the compressors are monomial
  substitutions, i.e. outer ring endomorphisms. Linear groups do admit strict compressions of Kazhdan
  subgroups: in `GL_4(Q)`, `Γ = SL_3(Z) ⋉ Z^3` and `t = diag(1,1,1,1/2)` give
  `tΓt^(−1) = SL_3(Z) ⋉ 2Z^3`. But `⟨Γ, t⟩` maps onto `Z` by the determinant, so it is not Kazhdan.
  For `SL_n(Z)` itself the compression semigroup is the normalizer
  (`arithmetic-integral-subgroup-is-incompressible`). Dies at: a *Kazhdan* ambient group generated by
  compressors, inside a linear group.
* **Mixing candidates directly.** Blind to Theorem C
  (`kazhdan-fixed-algebra-blind-to-subgroup-ergodic-actions`) and to Theorem 4.1 through the embedded
  crossed product (`permutation-centralizer-blind-to-weakly-mixing-actions`). Not needed anyway: any seed,
  mixing or not, suffices.
* **Through `SL_3(Z)`.** An instance: `simple-lattice-nonsofic-action-from-sl3z`.
* **Transfer between lattices of one `H`.** Done: `paunescu-class-is-measure-equivalence-invariant`
  shows the lattices of a Howe--Moore group are all in Păunescu's class or all outside it. So the
  claim is equivalent to "for some such `H`, every lattice of `H` has a free mixing nonsofic action".
  The choice of lattice is free, e.g. a cocompact torsion-free one, but the choice of `H` is not.
