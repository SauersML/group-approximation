**Established by citation.**  William Slofstra, *A group with at least
subexponential hyperlinear profile*, arXiv:1806.05267, Theorem 1.2, proves the
stated profile bound.  Section 2 constructs the infinite Clifford group, its
shift semidirect product, and the HNN relation `tzt^-1=z^2`; Theorem 2.1
imports the Birget--Ol'shanskii--Rips--Sapir quantitative Higman theorem.
The proof of Theorem 1.2 converts the controlled area bound into a long
approximate Clifford family and then into the dimension lower bound.

The same paper states immediately after Definition 1.1 that a finitely
presented group is nonhyperlinear exactly when some word has infinite
hyperlinear profile at a fixed positive separation for every sufficiently
small defect.  It also explicitly says that the constructed group's
hyperlinearity is unknown.
