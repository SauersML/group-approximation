title: A decidable model of a finite system embeds in a finitely presented simple group, which then carries the same solution
target: local-boone-higman-conjecture
requires:
  - boone-higman-conjecture
---

Let `g` solve `u_i(y) = 1, v_j(y) ≠ 1` in a finitely generated group with solvable
word problem. The subgroup `G` generated by `g` is finitely generated with
solvable word problem (rewrite its generators in the ambient ones). By
`boone-higman-conjecture`, `G ≤ S` with `S` finitely presented and simple. The
words `u_i(g)`, `v_j(g)` have the same values in `G` and `S`, so `g` solves the
system in `S`.

This is (a) ⇒ (b) of `local-boone-higman-iff-boone-higman`, and together with
`boone-higman-via-local-boone-higman` it makes the two claims an explicit
equivalence.
