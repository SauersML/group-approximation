**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed).** Let `F` be a field and `s >= 0`.

**(A) Graded units.** Let `R` be an `F`-algebra graded by an abelian group `Γ`
of torsion-free rank `<= s`. Suppose every element of `R_0` is algebraic over `F`.
Let `u_1, …, u_{s+1}` be commuting homogeneous units of `R`, and let
`φ : F[Z^{s+1}] -> R` send `t_i ↦ u_i`. Then `φ` is not injective.

**(B) Locally matrix-over-dimension-s algebras.** Let `R` be a directed union of
subalgebras `R_λ`. Suppose each `R_λ` embeds in a finite product `∏_j M_{n_j}(A_j)`
of matrix rings over commutative `F`-algebras `A_j` of *local dimension `<= s`*: every
finitely generated `F`-subalgebra of `A_j` has Krull dimension `<= s`. Examples are
`F[Z^s]` and every finitely generated `F`-algebra of dimension `<= s`.
Then `R` contains no polynomial ring `F[x_1, …, x_{s+1}]`. Hence it contains no
`F[Z^{s+1}]`.

The hypothesis cannot be weakened to "Krull dimension `<= s`": a field
`K = F(y_1, …, y_{s+1})` has dimension 0 and contains `F[y]`.

**Application to the host** `H = B_Ω ⊗ L_∂` over `F_2`. Transport the gauge
grading of `L_∂` along `L_∂ ≅ L(E_d)` (`boundary-crossed-product-is-a-leavitt-path-algebra`).
Take the `Z^2`-grading of `B_Ω` by the crossed-product degree. Together they give
a `Z^3`-grading of `H` with core `LC(Ω, F_2) ⊗ L(E_d)_0`, which is locally finite.
Consequences:
1. No embedding `F_2[Z^4] -> H` sends the four generators to `Z^3`-homogeneous units.
2. The same holds with `B_Ω` replaced by any `B` graded by a rank `<= 2` group
   with algebraic core.
3. No two commuting gauge-homogeneous units of a corner `p L_∂ p`, with `p` a
   gauge-degree-0 idempotent, generate `F_2[Z^2]`. The same holds for
   `p M_k(L_∂) p` with `p` diagonal of degree 0. Every idempotent of `L_∂` is
   Murray–von Neumann equivalent to such a `p`, by `V(L(E)) = M_E`
   (Ara–Moreno–Pardo).
4. By (B), no `F_2[Z^4]` lies in a subalgebra of `H` that is locally embeddable in
   finite products of matrices over commutative algebras of local dimension `<= 3`.
   Examples:
   - directed unions of `M_n(F_2[Z^3])`;
   - `(LC(X) ⋊ Z^r) ⊗ D` for a `Z^r`-odometer `X`, `r <= 3`, and a locally
     matricial `D`. Here `LC(Z^r/Λ) ⋊ Z^r ≅ M_{[Z^r:Λ]}(F_2[Λ])`, and `X` is the
     inverse limit of the finite quotients `Z^r/Λ`.

**Why it matters.** The fork "does `F_2[Z^4]` embed in `B_Ω ⊗ L_∂`?" decides (E).
- A NO answer refutes `decidable-algebras-embed-in-z2-sft-boundary-product-hosts`.
- The monomial and full-group count kills only elements supported on single group
  elements or on partitions.
- (A) kills every construction whose generators are homogeneous for the coarse
  lattice-plus-gauge grading. These include sums `Σ f_q δ_q` over many `q` of the
  same `Z^3`-degree, so all "rotate within one gauge level" constructions die.
- (B) kills every construction that factors through periodic or tower
  approximations of bounded commutative dimension. That is the algebraic shadow of
  the analytic `C(T^2) ⊆ O_2` argument.
- A YES construction must therefore use units that are inhomogeneous in every
  rank-3 grading with algebraic core. Its generated subalgebra must be locally
  non-PI of the relevant kind: through free-subalgebra or infinite-product
  behaviour.

**Proof outline** (route `algebraic-core-gradings-bound-homogeneous-laurent-rank-proof`).
- (A) The `s+1` degrees are `Z`-dependent modulo torsion. That gives a nontrivial
  monomial `w = ∏ u_i^{n_i}` in `R_0`. Its minimal polynomial `p` gives a nonzero
  `p(t^n) ∈ ker φ`.
- (B) A polynomial ring lies in one stage. It is a domain, so it injects into one
  factor `M_n(A)`.
  - Let `A'` be the subalgebra generated by the matrix entries of the `x_i`.
  - Cayley–Hamilton over `A'` makes `S = A'[x_1, …, x_{s+1}]` a finitely generated
    commutative algebra, integral over `A'`. So `dim S = dim A' <= s`.
  - The domain `F[x]` meets some minimal prime `P` of `S` trivially. So `F[x]`
    embeds in `S/P`, a finitely generated domain of transcendence degree `dim <= s`.
    This is a contradiction.
