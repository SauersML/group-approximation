**ESTABLISHED SIGNED-SOURCE OPNORM DICHOTOMY.**  Retain

```text
T=x_78(t_00),       Y=x_78(s_0t_00),
D=TY,               C=P_zP_D                          (PSD1)
```

from `native-t00-y1-collision-pays-marked-opnorm`.  Thus `C` is a nonzero
projection on every paid coordinate and

```text
zC=-C,                    (T-Y)C=-2YC.                (PSD2)
```

Let `(L_0,lambda_0)` be the actual order-`8192` signed source pair from
`center-chain-mixed-hecke-overlap-is-common-pauli-source`, and in any
unitary representation put

```text
Q=|L_0|^(-1) sum_(g in L_0) lambda_0(g)g.             (PSD3)
```

The marked root `z=x_13(q)` commutes with `L_0`, whose displayed root
indices lie in `{2,4,5,6,7,8,9}`.  For `g in L_0` define the fixed ordinary
group word

```text
r_g=z^((1-lambda_0(g))/2)g.                           (PSD4)
```

Then

```text
(1/2)||(T-Y)Q||_op
 + max_(g in L_0)||(r_g-1)C||_op >=1.                (PSD5)
```

Consequently there is an exact alternative:

```text
max_g||(r_g-1)C||_op >=1/2,                           (PSD6)
```

or else

```text
||(1-Q)C||_op<1/2,       ||QC||_op>1/2,
||(T-Y)Q||_op>1.                                      (PSD7)
```

Thus escape of the paid carrier from the **actual** signed-Hecke source is
not an unnamed analytic event.  It pays at least one half of one member of
the fixed `8192`-word menu `(PSD4)`.  On a sequence, finiteness permits a
subsequence on which the same word `r_g` pays.  If the detector branch is
absent, a paid vector lies within distance less than one half of `QH`, and
the native `T/Y` mismatch survives with operator norm greater than one after
source compression.

The inequality is C*-algebraic and therefore applies directly in a norm
corona representation.  Coordinate lifts incur only `o(1)` after rounding
the fixed involutions and exactifying the fixed finite source group; no
trace profile or positive rank is required.

This closes the formal operator-norm localization-or-payment dichotomy, but
does not identify any `r_g` as a defining relator or prove that its localized
defect is small in a microstate.  It also gives no normalized-HS mass for
`QC` and no control of the seven moving Whitehead boundaries.  Those are the
remaining occurrence and order-seven seams, respectively.  No Property T,
canonical trace, stability theorem beyond fixed finite-group correction,
literature input, or local computation is used.
