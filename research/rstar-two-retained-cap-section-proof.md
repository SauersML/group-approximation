Because `|K|<=2`, choose `j in {2,3,4} minus K`.  After prescribing the bits
on `K` and setting `x_j=1`, at least one coordinate remains free.  Set all
but one of the remaining free coordinates to zero and choose the final bit
so that the four-bit sum is odd.  The result is in `R_*`: odd parity holds,
and `x_j=1` excludes the ghost `1000`.  This proves that `(RTC2)` projects
onto every assignment on `K`.  It is a maximal cap by the prerequisite.

For the operator statement, let `P_u` be the joint spectral projection of
the retained context observables for `u in F_2^K`.  Choose one section value
`s(u) in C_(e_j)` for every active `P_u`.  For every nonretained coordinate
`k`, put

```text
Xhat_k=sum_(u:P_u!=0) (-1)^(s(u)_k) P_u.                 (RTC4)
```

These are commuting self-adjoint involutions in the algebra generated by
the retained observables.  On `P_u` the new tuple is exactly `s(u)`, so all
forbidden local atoms remain zero and `Xhat_j=-I`.  No compression or change
of trace is involved.
