This is a citation route. The quotes were read on 2026-09-16 from the arXiv PDFs and are recorded verbatim in
artifact §0.2 and §0.3.

1. **Gohla--Thom, Remark 3.16** (arXiv:2403.09582v2): "there do exist p.m.p. actions of F2 × F2 that are
   not weakly contained in finite actions", citing MIP* = RE (reference [23] there).
2. **Fournier-Facio--Willett** (arXiv:2603.18456v2):
   * Remark 5.23: "F2×F2 is not RFD [155] (and so also not FD)". Reference [155] is Ozawa's "About the QWEP
     conjecture".
   * Definition 5.10: RFD is weaker than FD.
   * Page 68: "As remarked by Kechris [113], property MD implies property FD".
   * Definition A.1 defines MD as: every p.m.p. action is a weak limit of finite actions.
   * So `F_2 × F_2` does not have MD, which is the claim.

Kechris's proof of MD ⇒ FD, and Ozawa's reduction, were not re-read. Artifact §4 recalls the
Koopman/Gaussian mechanism. The route rests on these two published sources and no inference is added.
