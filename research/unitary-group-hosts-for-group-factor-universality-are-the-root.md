**Statement.**
1. **Characters realize everything.**  Let `(N, tau)` be a separable tracial
   von Neumann algebra generated by unitaries `u_1, u_2, ...`, and let
   `pi: F_infinity -> U(N)` send the free generators to the `u_i`. Then
   `phi = tau ∘ pi` is a normalized positive-definite conjugation-invariant
   function (a character) on `F_infinity`, and its GNS von Neumann algebra is
   `N`. Yet `L(F_infinity)` is Connes-embeddable.
   So "`N` is the character von Neumann algebra of `G`" imposes no condition on
   `L(G)`. The canonical trace of `L(G)` is `delta_e`, while `phi` is a
   different character. In particular, for a countable group
   `G <= U(M)` generating a tracial `M`, the step "`tau_M` restricted to `G`
   embeds `M` into `L(G)^omega`" fails in general.
2. **The candidate is no easier than the root.**  The following are equivalent:
   - (a) some countable group is not hyperlinear (`non-hyperlinear-group`);
   - (b) there are a separable II_1 factor `M` and a countable subgroup
     `G <= U(M)`, viewed as a discrete group, with `L(G)` not
     Connes-embeddable.

**Consequence.**  Taking `U` inside the unitary group of a non-Connes-embeddable
factor, e.g. the algebra of the non-RU Boolean constraint system from
`mipstar-bcs-tracial-nonru-exists`, does not reduce
`some-countable-group-factor-is-locally-universal`. Any embedding of such an
`M` into `L(G)^omega` already makes `G` non-hyperlinear (item 1 of
`group-approximable-tracial-algebras-closed-under-corners`), and by item 2
producing such a `G` is exactly the root.
