After finite-packet correction, Schur's lemma gives `(FHC1)` and turns every
finite mixed relator into a matrix word on the common multiplicity factors.
Take any asymptotically exact model of that finite typed presentation and
tensor every letter with `I_2`.  Word evaluation, normalized trace, and
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt defect commute with this amplification.

The rank-one spectator projection in `(FHC2)` commutes with every amplified
letter.  Choosing an arbitrary packet-reducing multiplicity carrier to be
this half gives `(FHC3)` simultaneously for every edge and every cross-edge
word.  The same construction works for a prescribed reached carrier only
when the active model is block diagonal on that carrier and its complement.
In the language of `relative-fixed-algebra-payment-lemma`, such a carrier
belongs to the full fixed algebra, so the no-capture parameter is zero even
when the adjoint channel has a Kazhdan gap.

For the canonical BTB recursion, however, the fixed seed amplifies as
`e_0 tensor I_2`, and the minimal reducing hulls amplify with `I_2` as well.
The spectator half is a proper reducing subprojection, not automatically a
reached hull.  The proof therefore establishes the universal/local-carrier
no-go only.  It leaves path-specific `(CBR3)` open, with two possible exits:
an active-fiber floor `(FHC4)`, or a proof that the seed/minimal-hull recursion
itself prevents the required block-diagonal active model.
