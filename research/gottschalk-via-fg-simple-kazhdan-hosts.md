The first prerequisite is the established equivalence between the conjecture
and its restriction to finitely generated infinite simple Kazhdan groups. The
second is that restriction. Modus ponens gives the conjecture.

Like `gottschalk-via-surjunctive-fixed-host`, this route pairs an exact
reduction with an open hole equivalent to the goal. Its content is the shape of
the host: simple, Kazhdan, and outside every permanence closure.
