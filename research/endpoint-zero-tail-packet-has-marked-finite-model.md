representation of the binary Leavitt ring, let

```text
alpha=000...,                         beta=1000...,
K=span_F2{alpha,beta}.
```

```text

c_k=s_(0^(k+1))t_(0^k),              d_k=s_(0^k)t_(0^(k+1)),
a_k=s_1t_(0^k),                       b_k=s_(0^k)t_1,
p=s_0t_0,                             q=s_1t_1,

```

On the ordered basis `(alpha,beta)`, all `e_(0^k),c_k,d_k,p` are the same
projection onto `alpha`, every `a_k` is the matrix unit sending `alpha` to
`beta`, every `b_k` is the opposite matrix unit sending `beta` to `alpha`,
and `q` is the projection onto `beta`.
