Section 5 uses a finite invariant clopen partition of the core to place
the image in a finite product of GL_l(k(z)), l<=2. The cited primary
Guentner--Higson--Weinberger Theorem 4 gives Haagerup, so a Kazhdan
image is finite. Its finite-index locally finite kernel is finitely
generated and therefore finite. A finite compressed subgroup has equal
conjugate, and hence trivial intrinsic compression defect.
