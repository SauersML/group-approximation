Citation import, not a reproof. R. Lyons and Y. Peres, *Probability on Trees and
Networks*, Cambridge University Press 2016, Chapter 2, §§2.1, 2.5, 2.6. The statements
recorded in the target were read from the authors' online PDF
(`https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/prbtree/book.pdf`, printed pages 26, 39, 41, 44) on
2026-09-17. Theorem 2.11 is due to T. Lyons (1983); Theorem 2.17 to Kanai (1986), in
the flow-pushing form proved on p. 44; Pólya (1921). This route asserts only that the
cited text says what the claim records.
