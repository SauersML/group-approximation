**ESTABLISHED (unreviewed)** by
`z2-sft-boundary-hosts-need-unboundedly-hard-sft-languages-proof`.

**Convention.** For a `Z^2`-SFT `Ω ⊆ Σ^(Z^2)` and a recursive `T`, say that
`Ω` is **`T`-decidable** if, for some constant `C`, the set of globally
admissible `n × n` patterns of `Ω` is decidable in time `C·T(Cn) + Cn^2 + C`
(the convention of `minimal-z2-sfts-have-no-uniform-language-time-bound`).
For `d >= 1` put `L_∂(d) = LC(∂F_d, F_2) ⋊ F_d` and
`B_Ω = LC(Ω, F_2) ⋊ Z^2`.

**Theorem.** Let `T : N -> N` be recursive. There is a two-generated group `H`
with solvable word problem such that for **no** `T`-decidable `Z^2`-SFT `Ω`
and no `d >= 1` is there an injective `F_2`-algebra homomorphism, unital or
not,

```text
F_2[H] -> B_Ω ⊗ L_∂(d).
```

**Word-problem bound behind it.** If `G` is generated by a finite set and
`φ : F_2[G] -> B_Ω ⊗ L_∂(d)` is injective, and the `n × n` language of `Ω` is
decided in time `t(n)`, then the word problem of `G` is decided in time
`2^(C l^2) (max_(k <= Cl) t(k) + 1)` on words of length `l`, for a constant `C`
depending on `G`, `φ`, `Ω` and `d`.

**Consequence for the decomposition of
`decidable-algebras-embed-in-z2-sft-boundary-product-hosts`.** Its prerequisite
(H) is now a theorem: any witnessing family of SFTs has no common recursive
language bound. Together with (Q), the target implies the OPEN joint statement
`hard-quantum-rigid-free-minimal-z2-sfts-exist` (route
`hard-rigid-z2-sfts-from-z2-sft-boundary-host-embeddings`). That statement
implies `free-minimal-z2-sft-is-quantum-rigid` (no converse is known), and it can fail
on its own: if the finitely presented crossed products `B_Ω` over free minimal
`Z^2`-SFTs had one recursive language bound, the target would be refuted.

The embedding need not be unital, and no hypothesis on `Ω` (minimality,
freeness, finite presentation of `B_Ω`) is used.
