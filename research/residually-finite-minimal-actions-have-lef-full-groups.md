**ESTABLISHED by citation** (`residually-finite-minimal-actions-have-lef-full-groups-citation`). Xin Ma, *Soficity,
amenability, and LEF-ness for topological full groups*, arXiv:2209.00580, read from the TeX source on MSI on 2026-09-13.

- **Thm C** (`thm: main 2`): "Let $\alpha: G\curvearrowright X$ be a minimal topologically free residually finite action of a
  countable discrete group on the Cantor set. Then $[[\alpha]]$ is LEF and thus sofic."
- **The body statement** (`thm: topo group of residually finite action`) reads the same with "its topological full group
  $[[\alpha]]$ is LEF".
- **Relation to Grigorchuk–Medynets** (paper, l.1287): every minimal Z-action on the Cantor set is residually finite (Pimsner
  Lemma 2, Kerr–Nowak Prop 7.1), so Thm C recovers Grigorchuk–Medynets arXiv:1105.0719 Thm `TheoremLEF`: "Let $(X,T)$ be an
  arbitrary Cantor minimal system. Then the topological full group $[[T]]$ is an LEF group."

**Scope for the UN program.** This is a group-level statement about full groups. It does not give an exact local embedding
of the ring LC(X,k)⋊G into an algebraic matrix ultraproduct, which is what EL_n over that ring needs. It is the natural
dynamical input for such a ring-level theorem.
