Let `pi: G -> Q` be a split epimorphism, with `G` of type `F_d` and `Q` having
a finite `K(Q,1)` of dimension `d` with `chi(Q) != 0`. Then for every nonzero
character `psi` of `Q`, the class `[psi o pi]` does not lie in `Sigma^d(G)`.

This is the mechanism behind the explicit characters outside higher BNSR
invariants of pure braid groups: the top range
(`pure-braid-top-bnsr-invariants-are-center-nonvanishing`) and products of
natural projections.
