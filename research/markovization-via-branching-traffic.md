`traffic-branching-rounding-hierarchy` supplies a positive graph law near the
canonical finite-action values on enough connected branching tests.
`branching-traffic-markov-reconstruction` then converts precisely that finite-
defect shared-vertex consistency into one common multiplicative doubly
stochastic table, including inverse and trace control. That is the conclusion
of `regular-character-markovization`.
