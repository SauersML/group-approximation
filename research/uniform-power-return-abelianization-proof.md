Conjugation vanishes in free abelianization, so the return word has exponent
vector `(1-m)[w]_ab`.  A product of at most `C` conjugates of a fixed finite
relator list has exponent vector in a fixed bounded subset of the free
abelianization.  Letting `m` grow forces `[w]_ab=0`.
