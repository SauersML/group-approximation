Let `J` be a finite set, let `S_J` be a set of `8|J|` sites partitioned into
`|J|` blocks of eight, and let

```text
M_J  =  free product over i in J of ClLamp(8), amalgamated over the common
        central involution <zeta> = Z/2,
```

so `M_J` is generated by the `8|J|` involutions `c_s` together with `zeta`,
with `[c_s, c_s'] = zeta` when `s` and `s'` share a block and no relation
across blocks.  Then `M_J` is finitely generated and residually finite.

**The point is the proof, not the statement.**  Residual finiteness of `M_J`
is already used inside `literal-telescope-core-lef-proof`, where it is
obtained from Karrass--Pietrowski--Solitar: `M_J` acts on the Bass--Serre tree
of a finite star of finite groups with finite vertex stabilizers, hence is
virtually free, hence residually finite.  That is input (S5) of
`notes/LITERAL_GROUP_IS_SOFIC_2026-08-14.md`, and it is the last piece of
outside group theory in the whole soficity chain.

Section 8 of `notes/E_SOFICITY_SELF_CONTAINED_2026-08-16.md` removes it.  Take
the two natural quotients of `M_J`:

```text
q : M_J ->> C(S_J)                 add the missing cross-block relations
r : M_J ->> M_J/<zeta>  =  *_{i in J} (Z/2)^8
```

`C(S_J)` is the full Clifford group on all `8|J|` sites, finite of order
`2^(8|J|+1)`, and `q(zeta) != 1` in it; `ker r = <zeta>` because `zeta` is
central.  Hence `ker q ∩ ker r = ker q ∩ <zeta> = 1`, so `(q,r)` embeds `M_J`
into `finite × (free product of finitely many finite groups)`, which is
residually finite.

The only tree-theoretic input that survives is that a group acting FREELY on a
tree is free (used for free products of finitely many finite groups), which is
strictly weaker than virtual freeness for finite stabilizers.

**Consequence for the trust surface.**  With this claim and
`literal-base-presentation-complete`, the route
`literal-telescope-core-lef-via-central-embedding` derives soficity of `E`
with no literature input at all: residual finiteness of the telescope levels
comes from congruence quotients rather than Mal'cev, and the final
`Z`-extension step is the construction formalized in
`GroupApproximation/Sofic/SoficIntegerExtension.lean` rather than a citation
of Elek--Szabó.

The argument uses nothing specific to blocks of size eight or to this site
set: any finite central amalgam of finite groups over a common central
subgroup that survives in one finite quotient is residually finite by the same
two-quotient embedding.
