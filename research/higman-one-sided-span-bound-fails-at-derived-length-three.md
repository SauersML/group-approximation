**ESTABLISHED** by `higman-one-sided-span-counterexample-proof` (obstruction).

**Statement.** There is a group `G` generated by `a, b, c` of infinite order with
`b^a = b^2` and `c^b = c^2` such that, for every `m >= 1`, the subgroup `A = <a^m, c^m>` satisfies
`A''' = 1`, and the image of `z = [c^m, a^m]` in `V = (A'/A'') ⊗ Q` has infinite-dimensional span
under conjugation by `c^m`. Consequently `A''` is abelian of infinite rank.

**Construction.** `G_1 = T ⋊ BS(1,2)` is a rational form of the metabelian calibration group of
`higman-metabelian-edge-collapse-proof` (`T` induced from `b` acting by `1/2`, `c = 1 ⊗ 1`). Let
`L = Q[G_1] / Q[G_1](1 - b - bc)` and `G = L ⋊ G_1`, with `a -> (0,a)`, `b -> (0,b)`,
`c -> ([1], c)`. The relation `c^b = c^2` in `G` is exactly the defining relation of `L`.

**Detector.** A `Q(t^(1/2^∞))`-valued function `f(tau, h) = t^lambda(tau) F(h)` on `G_1` satisfies the
harmonic identity `f(g) = f(gb) + f(gbc)`. It is invariant under left multiplication by `<a,c>'` and
scales by `t` under `c`. So `(r,u) -> f(r)` is a homomorphism `A' -> Q(t^(1/2^∞))` that kills `A''`,
intertwines `c^m`-conjugation with multiplication by `t^m`, and is nonzero on `z`.

**What this kills.** Step 2 of `higman-metabelian-edge-collapse-proof` and of
`higman-solvable-by-metabelian-edge-collapse-proof` shows that the `c^m`-span of `zbar` is
finite-dimensional. It uses only the `b`-side relations, together with `A'' = 1`, respectively
`h(A'') < ∞`. This example satisfies everything except that hypothesis, and the conclusion fails.
- **The one-sided method is dead at derived length 3.** It bounds `A'/A''` from one Baumslag--Solitar
  pair, then from the other, then multiplies the two bounds. For opposite pairs with `A''' = 1` and `A''`
  of infinite rank, no argument of this shape can work. A collapse theorem for such pairs must couple
  `b^a = b^2, c^b = c^2` with `d^c = d^2, a^d = a^2` before it bounds `A'/A''`. The mirror image under
  `a -> b -> c -> d -> a` defeats the `d` side alone.
- **The level-2 candidate.** The candidate `[z, z^(a^m)]` for a second-level eigen-shift is not
  available as stated, because conjugation by `b^P` does not preserve `<a^m, c^m>`.

**What it does not kill.** Soficity is not affected. This is an obstruction to a proof method for
Attempt 5 of `higman-group-is-sofic` (amenable edge images in the glued amalgam). A derived-length-3
opposite pair in a genuine Higman quotient is still open.
