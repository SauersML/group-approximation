`F_2 × F_2` is residually finite. Free groups are residually finite, and a nontrivial `(g, h)` survives in a
finite quotient of whichever coordinate is nontrivial. So `f2xf2-not-stable-in-finite-actions` exhibits a
residually finite group that is not stable in finite actions. ∎
