Let `G` be a one-ended word-hyperbolic group and let `Phi` be any
automorphism of `G`. Torsion in `G` is allowed. For each
`X in {A,K,L}`, the suspension

```text
G_Phi = G semidirectProduct_Phi Z
```

satisfies the fibred Farrell--Jones conjecture in `X`-theory with respect
to the family of virtually cyclic subgroups.

This is the empty-peripheral specialization of Andrew--Guerch--Hughes,
Theorem A (equivalently Corollary 5.23 with empty peripheral systems). It
closes the one-ended case without the previously required
virtual-torsion-freeness hypothesis. It does not cover arbitrary
infinitely-ended hyperbolic groups with torsion.
