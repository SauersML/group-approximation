title: Finite systems of projection comparisons lift through injective inductive limits
---

Let `A=lim(A_i,phi_i)` be an injective sequential inductive limit. Every
projection in `A tensor K` is Murray--von Neumann equivalent to the image of
a projection over one stage. Moreover, a finite family of Murray--von
Neumann subequivalences among stage projections which holds in `A tensor K`
holds after applying the connecting maps to one sufficiently late common
stage.

In particular, if stage projections `p_i,q_i` have limit images satisfying

```text
n[q_i] <= [p_i] <= (n+1)[q_i],
```

then the two projection comparisons hold simultaneously at some later
stage.
