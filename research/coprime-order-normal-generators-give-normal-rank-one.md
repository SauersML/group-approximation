**ESTABLISHED** by `coprime-order-normal-generators-proof` (direct elementary
proof).

**Statement.** Let `A` and `B` be groups. Take `a ∈ A` with `a^m = 1` and
`<<a>>_A = A`, and `b ∈ B` with `b^n = 1` and `<<b>>_B = B`, where
`gcd(m, n) = 1`. Then `<<ab>> = A * B`. In particular `A * B` has normal rank at
most 1, and exactly 1 when `A * B` is nontrivial.

**Credit and scope.**
- This is the argument Chen–Lodha sketch for `(Z/2) * (Z/3)` and for simple
  groups with torsion elements of coprime orders (arXiv:2510.26073, l.222). Here
  the factors need not be simple; each only has to be normally generated by the
  chosen element.
- No novelty is claimed.
- It explains why every normal-rank lower bound for two-factor free products needs
  a hypothesis excluding such torsion. The hypotheses in
  `left-orderable-free-products-have-normal-rank-above-one` and
  `torsion-free-free-products-have-normal-rank-above-one` are of that kind.

**Example.** `finite-perfect-free-pairs-have-normal-rank-one` applies this to any
two nontrivial finite perfect groups.
