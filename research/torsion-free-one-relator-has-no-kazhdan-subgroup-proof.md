## Direct proof

Brodskii's theorem makes a torsion-free one-relator group locally indicable.  A
nontrivial finitely generated subgroup `P` therefore surjects onto `Z`.  If `P`
had property (T) then so would `Z`, since (T) passes to quotients; it does not.

Three lines, one classical input, and no hypothesis on the ambient group beyond
torsion-freeness.  The applications to the Fournier-Facio and Kun--Thom
witnesses are recorded in the claim; they need only that those groups are
nontrivial, finitely generated and Kazhdan, which the corresponding nodes of
this graph already state.
