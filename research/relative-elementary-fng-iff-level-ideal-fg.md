Let `U` be a unital associative ring, `n >= 3`, and `I <| U` a two-sided
ideal.  Write

```text
E_n(U,I) = << e_ij(a) : a in I, i != j >>_{E_n(U)}.
```

Then `E_n(U,I)` is finitely normally generated in `E_n(U)` if and only if
`I` is finitely generated as a two-sided ideal of `U`.

DERIVATION
[[relative-elementary-fng-iff-level-ideal-fg-proof]]

## Proof

Suppose first that `I = sum_l U a_l U` for `l=1,...,m`.  Let `N` be the
normal closure in `E_n(U)` of the finite set `e_12(a_l)`.  Elementary signed
permutation matrices change the pair of indices, so `N` contains
`e_ij(+-a_l)` for every `i != j`.  The Steinberg identities

```text
[e_ki(x),e_ij(a)] = e_kj(xa),
[e_ij(a),e_jk(x)] = e_ik(ax),
e_ij(a+b)=e_ij(a)e_ij(b)
```

show that `N` contains `e_ij(u a_l v)` for arbitrary `u,v in U`, and then
`e_ij(a)` for every `a in I`.  Hence `N=E_n(U,I)`.

Conversely suppose `E_n(U,I)` is normally generated by finitely many
elements `h_1,...,h_r`.  By the definition of `E_n(U,I)`, each `h_k` is a
finite product of conjugates of elementary matrices whose entries lie in
`I`.  Across all these finitely many expressions, only finitely many entries
`b_1,...,b_t in I` occur.  Let `J=sum_q U b_q U`.  Then every `h_k` belongs
to the normal subgroup `E_n(U,J)`, so

```text
E_n(U,I) = <<h_1,...,h_r>> <= E_n(U,J).
```

The reverse inclusion follows from `J <= I`, hence the two relative
elementary subgroups are equal.  If `a in I`, then `e_12(a)` belongs to
`E_n(U,I)=E_n(U,J)`.  Reduction modulo `J` kills `E_n(U,J)`, while the image
of `e_12(a)` is the identity only if `a in J`.  Thus `I <= J`; since
`J <= I`, we have `I=J`, proving that `I` is finitely generated.

## Consequence for the CK gate

For the direct envelope `Gamma=EL_4(U)`, if a candidate maximal normal
subgroup `M` can be proved to equal the relative elementary subgroup of its
level ideal, then

```text
M finitely normally generated  <=>  lev(M) finitely generated as ideal.
```

This is an exact reduction, not a solution: normal-structure theorems over
general noncommutative rings may place `M` strictly above the relative
elementary subgroup of its level, and the universal sandwich ring has not
been shown to satisfy a sandwich classification with the required finiteness
control.
