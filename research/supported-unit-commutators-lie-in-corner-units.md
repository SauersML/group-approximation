Let R be a countable purely infinite simple unital ring, e a nonzero
idempotent, and v in [R^x,R^x] with v=e+(1-e)v(1-e). Then v belongs to
`MFQuotientUnits.cornerUnitSubgroup R`.

Here the corner-unit subgroup is generated by images of EL_m(S), m>=2,
where S is countable and has ts=1 with 1-st a full idempotent. In this
case a single image of EL_2(R) already contains v.

The corresponding manuscript interface is `MFQuotientUnits.AGPStepOne`;
the supplied theorem is `MFQuotientUnits.agpStepOne`. This does not assert
the reduction of arbitrary units to supported form or the canonical K1
identification. Compilation evidence is tracked separately from the
mathematical argument.
