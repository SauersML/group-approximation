The parabolic spectrum is approximately `z -> z^4`-invariant
(`parabolic-spectrum-is-doubling-invariant`); the same holds for the
opposite parabolic; the involution `S` identifies the two spectral measures;
the half-parabolics of `SL_2(Z[1/2])` add square-root constraints.  If the
joint spectral data of an approximate representation subject to all these is
forced near an atomic (congruence) configuration with a uniform modulus
(`approximate-doubling-invariant-joint-measure-is-atomic`), the far sector
is closed: every low-defect representation is near a congruence one.
