Under the hypotheses of `observed-mapping-tori-have-simple-fk-hosts`,
put `T=<D,t | t^-1gt=φ(g)>`. Then `V^(Z)⋊T` embeds in a simple
group of type `F_k` generated by two finite-order elements, where
`^(Z)` denotes the restricted direct sum and the action is

`t e_n(v)t^-1=e_{n+1}(v)`,

`g e_n(v)g^-1=e_n(ρ(φ^n(g))vρ(φ^n(g))^-1)`.

No finite-presentation hypothesis on this larger source is imposed.

## Attempts

on one shell. The key remaining injection check detects the shift by
shell displacement and then uses tail separation to rule out a
nontrivial observed profile equal to finitely many lamps. The same
compact-core actor would contain the whole source. It is insufficient
to infer this stronger conclusion merely from a simple host for `T`;
the explicit common realization and faithful semidirect product are
