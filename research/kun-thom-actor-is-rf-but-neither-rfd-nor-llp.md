## Residual finiteness

Kun--Thom, arXiv:2608.06222v1, Theorem E, proves directly that both
(Gamma) and (G) are residually finite Kazhdan groups.  Thus finite
quotients separate every nonidentity **group element** of (G).

The semidirect product has a split inclusion and retraction

## The full group (C^*)-algebra does not have LLP

Ioana--Spaas--Wiersma, *Cohomological obstructions to lifting properties for
full (C^*)-algebras of property (T) groups*, GAFA 30 (2020), Corollary B,
proves that
[
 C^*(operatorname{SL}_n(mathbb Z))	ext{ fails LLP}qquad(nge3). 	ag{KTA10}
]
The LP and LLP pass to subgroups; see
Fournier-Facio--Willett, Corollary 3.17.  Applying this to (KTA4) gives
[
 C^*(G)	ext{ fails LLP}.                                           	ag{KTA11}
]
Thus the Fournier-Facio--Willett LLP--RFD finite-corner theorem cannot be
applied to the actual Kun--Thom actor: both of its hypotheses fail
separately.

## What remains open about finite-corner stability

Failure of LLP or RFD does not by itself disprove very-flexible stability in
the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  That stability problem for (G)
remains open.  Two exact reductions show its difficulty.

First, (G) has property ((T)), so arbitrary finite-dimensional exact
corners do not give a weaker escape than flexible stability.  If
[
 phi_n:G	o U(k_n)
]
is an asymptotic representation and there are exact
(ho_n:G	o U(D_n)) and isometries
(V_n:mathbb C^{k_n}	omathbb C^{D_n}) with
[
 |V_n^*ho_n(s)V_n-phi_n(s)|_{2,k_n}	o0
]
on a finite Kazhdan generating set, the compression projection has
(o(k_n)) total leakage.  Kazhdan projection-rounding inside the
finite-dimensional commutant produces a (ho_n(G))-invariant projection
of rank (k_n+o(k_n)).  Restriction to that projection gives a flexible
exact correction.  This is the argument of
(	extit{tfd-low-leakage-compressions-flexibly-reduce}), and full property
((T)) supplies its finite-dimensional gap.  Consequently, for this actor,
[

 	ext{flexible HS stability}.                                     	ag{KTA12}
]

Second, very-flexible stability passes to retracts.  Applying the retraction
in (KTA3) would make (operatorname{SL}_d(mathbb Z)) very-flexibly
HS-stable, hence flexibly HS-stable by the same Kazhdan rounding.  The latter
is precisely the open arithmetic stability problem

[

and no current theorem establishes or refutes the left side.

The live Kun--Thom route still needs its source-specific canonical actor
exactification, not the general LLP--RFD shortcut.
