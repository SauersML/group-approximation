Let alpha generate pi_1 U(2) and omega: SU(2) -> U(2) be the inclusion. Then

    <alpha, <alpha, omega>> = omega o eta_3 o eta_4,

the generator of pi_5 U(2) = Z/2.

The collapse T^2 x S^3 -> S^5 is injective on [ -, U(2)]. So the unitaries
alpha o pr_1, alpha o pr_2 and omega o pr_3 in C(T^2 x S^3, M_2) have a triple
commutator outside U0. The component group of this nonsimple algebra has
nilpotency class at least three, with dim X = 2·rank + 1.
