**Problem XLVIII of Schafhauser--Tikuisis--White, arXiv:2506.10902**
(stated by them "very vaguely": is there a reasonable framework for
classifying Cantor Cartans in AF algebras up to conjugacy by
automorphisms?).  Encoded in the standard sharp form for such
questions: in a fixed AF algebra (say the irrational-rotation AF
algebra of Mitscher--Spielberg), the conjugacy relation on
Cantor-spectrum Cartan subalgebras is NOT classifiable by countable
structures (not Borel-reducible to isomorphism of countable
structures).  A positive answer refutes every "reasonable framework"
in the descriptive-set-theoretic sense; a smooth or countable-structure
classification proves one exists.

## Attempts

* Some STW authors expect wildness ("potentially so many that they
  cannot be reasonably classified").  The standard machine: build a
  Borel family of Cantor Cartans indexed by a turbulent parameter
  space — twisted spectra of orbit-breaking data are the natural
  candidate, since DPS orbit-breaking takes a minimal-system parameter
  (a point/Choquet datum) and inequivalent points give non-conjugate
  Cartans in cases with enough K-theory to see them.  What is missing
  is a CONJUGACY-invariant separating a continuum of them inside ONE
  AF algebra: the known invariants (spectrum, unique extension,
  Weyl groupoid homology) are countable-valued.  Producing a
  continuous conjugacy invariant of Cantor Cartans would settle the
  encoded form positively via Hjorth turbulence; conversely a proof
  that the Weyl groupoid plus its homology is a complete invariant
  would land the classification in countable structures and refute
  the encoded form.
