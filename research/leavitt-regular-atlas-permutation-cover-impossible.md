There do not exist finite sets `Omega_n` carrying two free regular `A_8`
actions such that every member of the fixed normal generating set `bar S` of
the universal Leavitt atlas has Hamming defect tending to zero.

Equivalently, the finite complex-of-groups presentation admits no sequence of
finite almost-covers with honest regular vertex charts and `o(1)` failed
2-cell lifts. Rational transportation tables and finite-sheet permutation
absorbers cannot be glued into such a cover: their missing joint return
statistics are not merely an unsolved coupling problem but an impossible one.
