**OPEN, conjectural.** There is a hyperlinear word-hyperbolic Kazhdan group `Γ`
with `b_2(Γ;Q) >= 1` that is flexibly Hilbert--Schmidt stable.

By `hs-stable-hyperlinear-kazhdan-hyperbolic-forces-persistent-class`, such a `Γ`
is residually finite and not good, and a finite-index subgroup carries a prime
degree-two class that survives every finite cover. Every residually finite
candidate is hyperlinear, so this is the form the premise takes for all the
natural candidates: congruence-type lattices and random groups known to be
residually finite.
