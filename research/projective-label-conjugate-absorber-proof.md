# Cancel a projective cocycle with the conjugate projective representation

In `(C_g tensor conjugate(C_g))(C_h tensor conjugate(C_h))`, the two scalar
factors are `c(g,h)` and its complex conjugate, so their product is one.
Hence `T` is honest. Evaluating every relator under `(PA4)` gives
`T_(w(ell)) tensor w(Y)=I tensor I` by `(PA2)`.
