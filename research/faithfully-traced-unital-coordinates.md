Let `C_ftr` be the class of pairs `(A,tau)` with `A` a unital C-star algebra
and `tau` a faithful tracial state.  Then

1. every `A` in `C_ftr` is finite (every isometry is unitary), and the norm
   ultraproduct and norm corona of any sequence from `C_ftr` are finite;
2. `C_ftr` is closed under nonzero corners: `tau(p)>0` automatically, and
   `(pAp,tau_p)` with `tau_p(x)=tau(x)/tau(p)` lies in `C_ftr`;
3. projections lift from `prod_k A_k/directSum_k A_k` to coordinate
   projections after discarding finitely many coordinates, and the resulting
   corona corner is the corona of the coordinate corners.

These are the true coordinate-level facts used by the compression argument.
They do **not** imply Kazhdan transport for arbitrary faithfully traced
coordinates.  The formerly included fourth assertion, that the algebra
generated by left and right multiplication on the GNS Hilbert space is
always finite, is false: for an infinite factor `M`,
`M or M'=B(L^2 M)`.  The corrected transport criterion is
`left-right-finite-kazhdan-transport`.
