Let `C_ftr` be the class of pairs `(A,tau)` with `A` a unital C-star algebra
and `tau` a faithful tracial state.  Then

1. every `A` in `C_ftr` is finite (every isometry is unitary), and the norm
   ultraproduct and the norm corona of any sequence from `C_ftr` are finite;
2. `C_ftr` is closed under nonzero corners: `tau(p)>0` automatically, and
   `(pAp, tau(p . p)/tau(p))` lies in `C_ftr`;
3. projections lift from `prod_k A_k / directSum_k A_k` to `prod_k A_k`, and
   the corner of the corona at a lifted projection `p=(p_k)` is the corona of
   the corners `p_k A_k p_k`;
4. the tracial ultraproduct `M` of `(A_k,tau_k)` is a finite von Neumann
   algebra, and the algebra `M or JMJ` generated by the conjugation action on
   `L^2(M,tau_omega)` is finite.

These are exactly the four properties the matrix-coordinate compressor
argument uses of `M_d` with its normalized trace.  Item 1 is the property the
manuscript itself isolates as load bearing: in the ultraproduct formulation
(`notes/NO_ONE_SIDED_GROWTH.md`) the chain is `P <= VPV^*`, `P ~ VPV^*`, and
"the norm-matrix corona is finite, so `P = VPV^*`", the finiteness being
proved by polar correction of a representing sequence --- an argument which
never mentions matrices.  Nothing else about
matrices is used, and no real-rank-zero, nuclearity, simplicity, comparison
or classification hypothesis is needed.
