There is `K_2<infinity`, independent of dimension and dyadic conductors,
such that every pair of exact same-dimensional modular-vertex
representations factoring through arbitrary pure dyadic quotients admits an
exactly compatible same-dimensional pair with

```text
D(Pi_+,Pi_-) <= K_2 def(Pi_+,Pi_-).                     (PDR1)
```

If `kappa_B` is the fixed congruence Poincare constant of the Iwahori and
`C_bot` is the fixed finite-diagram repair constant for the conductor-at-
most-one quotient, one admissible bookkeeping constant is

```text
K_2=2 sqrt(3)/kappa_B
    +C_bot(1+4 sqrt(3)/kappa_B).                        (PDR2)
```

Thus no exact pure-dyadic selected-irrep, projective-inertia, or diffuse
cross-conductor packet can be a Dogon--Vigdorovich repair enemy. Together
with the odd-congruence theorem, the exact congruence obstruction is reduced
to the mixed CRT label-stabilization gate recorded in
`mixed-crt-iwahori-repair-is-labeled-pure-dyadic-rounding`.
