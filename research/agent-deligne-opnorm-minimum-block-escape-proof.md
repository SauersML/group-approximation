For `k>=1`, put

```text
delta_k = min_(V in U(k)^S)
          max_(r in R) ||r(V,omega I_k)-I_k||_op.         (OMP1)
```

The minimum exists because `U(k)^S` is compact and the displayed function is
continuous.  It is strictly positive.  Indeed, `delta_k=0` would give a tuple
at which every lifted relator is exact, hence a `k`-dimensional unitary
representation of `E_3` sending its covering generator `z` to
`omega I_k`.  This contradicts
`deligne-triple-cover-fd-central-invisibility`, which says that every
finite-dimensional representation of `E_3` kills `z`.

For fixed `K`, let

```text
epsilon_K = min_(1<=k<=K) delta_k > 0.                   (OMP2)
```

Suppose a tuple `U` has a common reducing block `H_0` of dimension
`k<=K`, and denote the restricted tuple by `V`.  Every relator word respects
`H_0`, so its restriction is the corresponding relator word in `V`.  Hence

```text
max_r ||r(U,omega I)-I||_op
  >= max_r ||r(V,omega I_k)-I_k||_op
  >= delta_k >= epsilon_K,                               (OMP3)
```

which proves `(OME1)`.

Now decompose the finite-dimensional C-star algebra generated by a tuple into
irreducible common reducing blocks.  If a vanishing-defect sequence retained
a block of dimension at most `K` along infinitely many coordinates, `(OME1)`
would give the fixed positive defect floor `epsilon_K` on that subsequence, a
contradiction.  Thus its minimum irreducible block dimension escapes every
fixed cutoff.  For a tuple of unitaries, a finite-dimensional common invariant
subspace is automatically reducing (apply each unitary and use equality of
dimensions), so the invariant-subspace formulation is equivalent.

Finally, a finite-dimensional `K`-subhomogeneous coordinate algebra is a
direct sum of matrix algebras of sizes at most `K`.  Coordinate lifts of a
unital homomorphism from the twisted fibre to its norm corona may be chosen
unitary on the finite canonical generating set: arbitrary lifts are
asymptotically unitary, and polar correction changes them by operator norm
`o(1)`.  The corrected coordinate tuples therefore have common reducing
blocks of size at most `K`, while their relator defects still tend to zero in
operator norm.  This contradicts `(OME1)` and proves the stated corona
consequence.
