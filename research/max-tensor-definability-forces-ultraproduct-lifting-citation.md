Goldbring--Sinclair, *On definability of C*-tensor norms*,
arXiv:2509.15086v1, Theorem 3.13 and Corollary 3.15 prove that definability of
the maximal tensor norm over `T` forces property `max-U` for every model of
`T` and every ultrafilter.  Their Theorem 3.23 constructs a separable
C-star algebra failing property `max-U` for every cofinal ultrafilter under
consideration, using Pisier's ultraproduct characterization of LP.  These
statements give exactly the claim and include no hypothesis about
hyperlinearity.
