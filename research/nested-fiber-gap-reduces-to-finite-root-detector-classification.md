Because the canonical root fiber is a projection in a fixed finite packet
algebra, make the nested shared tuple reduce it and apply the established
dimension-free BCS correction on that corner.  This proves `(NBF1)`.

For each nested forbidden type, any proposed finite ordinary packet bridge to
a relator word has the form covered by the finite root-detector theorem.  Its
regular representation after root killing proves that `(NBF2)` is impossible
unless the forbidden packet projection vanishes in the corresponding normal
quotient.  Arbitrary Boolean support is not such quotient data.  The same
criterion applies to a proposed coefficient-ring controlled sign once its
spectral identification is required uniformly in unitary representations.
Summing the finitely many types proves the stated recursion to the original
groupification gate.
