For each finite actor factor, embed the finite orbit-span of the test
coefficients into a common finite vector space, fixing the test space.
Extend the factor action trivially on a complement. Free generators'
partial linear isomorphisms extend to automorphisms. Free-product
universality and finite actor quotients then give injective exact local
models in a finite affine group times a finite actor group.

For the Jacobson column the explicit windows are `W_n=A_n+eta(A_n)`.
Finite joint-character spans in exact complex representations are
invariant under left precomposition by `S,T`; finite-dimensional
one-sided inverse cancellation forces them to annihilate `JQJ`.
Laurent evaluation and finite actor quotients prove the reverse residual
containment. Coordinate projections from constant shears generate all
coefficients from one vector, giving finite generation. The explicit
actor-kernel word and the finite-presentation obstruction are proved
directly in the same artifact.
