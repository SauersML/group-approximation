Construct one finite presentation in either of two equivalent-looking ways:

1. authenticate the ascending-HNN parent/child algebra comparison with a
   dimension-independent operator-norm checksum; or
2. round the Thompson even-lamp presentation to a finite exact spectral state
   machine with dimension-independent operator-norm control.

Either construction combines with its exact finite-memory detector to kill a
nontrivial marked word in every norm-matrix corona.  The endpoint uses no trace,
Hilbert--Schmidt renormalization, Kazhdan moving corner, or finite central
spectral cut.

The bare exact-to-corona inference is false by
`bare-self-copy-mf-calibration`, even when the generated parent and child
C-star algebras agree exactly.  Consequently the first alternative means a
direct dimension-independent commutant-coercivity checksum, not merely an
algebra-distance estimate.  The finite noncommutative packet route is recorded
separately at `finite-noncommutative-packet-compression-collapse`: it repairs
the failure with rank-weighted Hilbert--Schmidt geometry and Kazhdan transport,
so it does not establish this deliberately no-renormalization endpoint.
