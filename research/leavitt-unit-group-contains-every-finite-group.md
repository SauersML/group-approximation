**ESTABLISHED.** Let `L^x = L_(F_2)(1,2)^x`. Every finite group is
isomorphic to a subgroup of `L^x`. Consequently

```text
Lambda^(L^x) = Z[ 1/|H| : H <= L^x finite ] = Q.
```

**Why it matters for Baum--Connes.** Lück (Invent. Math. 149 (2002)) shows
that traces of reduced assembly images lie in `Lambda^G`. For `G = L^x` that
ring is all of `Q`. So the trace test can detect a Baum--Connes failure for
`L^x` only through an irrational trace of a projection in some
`M_n(C*_r L^x)`, and no rational trace value is an obstruction. See
`baum-connes-counterexample-group-exists`.
