There is a finitely generated torsion-free property-`(T)` group `Gamma_0`
with epimorphisms onto alternating groups of unbounded degree.  Namely, take a
torsion-free finite-index normal subgroup of `Out(F_5)`.  Property `(T)` comes
from Kaluba--Nowak--Ozawa and finite-index permanence; unbounded alternating
quotients come from Gilman's residual-alternating theorem and remain
surjective after restriction to `Gamma_0`, apart from finitely many bounded
degrees.

Consequently every convergent subsequence of these finite marked quotients has
an infinite finitely generated LEF, hence sofic, property-`(T)` limit `K`, and
`K` is a quotient of `Gamma_0`.  For a sequence

```text
q_i : Gamma_0 ->> Alt(n_i),       n_i -> infinity,
```

the two extra properties needed for the desired seed have exact finite-model
gates:

```text
K is torsion-free
iff every persistent nonidentity word has unbounded order in q_i(Gamma_0);

K is simple
iff every persistent nonidentity word eventually has bounded normal-
    generation certificates for the marked generators, with both the number
    of conjugates and the marking lengths of their conjugators bounded.     (AMG1)
```

The bounds in the second line may depend on the persistent word.  Simplicity
would imply that every nonidentity element normally generates `K`, hence that
`K` has no nontrivial finite quotient.  It would therefore give precisely a
finitely generated torsion-free LEF/sofic Kazhdan seed with full finite
residual.  Since an infinite simple LEF group cannot be finitely presented,
this route can only target an infinitely presented kernel; a finite
automorphic presentation would require additional self-similarity data not
present in the alternating-quotient theorems.

Neither `(AMG1)` condition is supplied by the cited constructions.  In
particular, abstract simplicity of each finite alternating quotient is not
enough: its normal-generation witnesses may use conjugators of marking length
tending to infinity.  Alekseev--Thom explicitly record a weaker missing input
for the Bartholdi--Kassabov Kazhdan mother: even arranging that every fixed
nontrivial mother-group element is killed in only finitely many of the chosen
alternating quotients is currently out of reach.

One must also pass to `Gamma_0`; the full `Aut(F_5)` or `Out(F_5)` cannot have
a nontrivial torsion-free quotient.  Bridson--Vogtmann prove that `Aut(F_n)` is
normally generated by an involution, and the same remains true in every
quotient.  Thus every nontrivial quotient retains a nontrivial involution.

Accordingly this is a genuine alternate seed program, not a completed seed.
The precise unresolved step is to choose alternating quotient maps satisfying
simultaneously the torsion-order and bounded normal-certificate conditions in
`(AMG1)` (and, for the mapping-torus application, to add a finite automorphic
presentation).
