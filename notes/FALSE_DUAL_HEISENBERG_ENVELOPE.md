# The dual Heisenberg envelope turns parity into a central radical

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

The one-orbit parity spatializer can be made into a literal element of a
finitely generated discrete group.  The construction uses two elementary
abelian lamp modules in evaluation position: the ordinary finite lamp
module and the orbit module generated by the characteristic function of
the edge coset.  Their binary Heisenberg commutator is the desired central
sign.  No nondegeneracy of the full pairing is needed.

For the fixed Kun--Thom pair `Gamma<G`, this gives an explicit group

```text
E=H(A,T) semidirect G                                  (DHE1)
```

with all of the following properties:

1. `H(A,T)` is a countable locally finite two-group;
2. `E/H(A,T)=G`, and `G` is residually finite;
3. `E` is finitely generated;
4. a nontrivial central involution `z` belongs to
   `Rad_sof(E)`; and
5. in the negative central corner `pL(E)`, one dual lamp exactly
   spatializes the one-orbit parity automorphism on the entire edge.

The negative corner remains a possible tracial endpoint:

```text
pL(E) Connes embeddable
  ==> E is a hyperlinear nonsofic group.                 (DHE2)
```

There is, however, a decisive operator-norm correction.  Theorem 1 of
`FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_OPERATOR_STERILITY.md` proves that every homomorphism
from `E` to a norm matrix ultraproduct kills `z`.  Thus `E` is **not**
operator-MF.  The value of `(DHE1)` is now a compact explicit tracial
target, not an operator-MF target.

## 2. The primal and dual orbit modules

Let `G` act on itself by left translation and put

```text
A=F_2^(G),                                             (DHE3)
```

the finitely supported functions on `G`.  Write `a_y` for the delta function
at `y`.  Let

```text
ell(a)=sum_(y in Gamma) a_y in F_2.                   (DHE4)
```

The sum is finite because `a` has finite support.  Thus `ell` belongs to the
algebraic dual `A^*=Hom_F2(A,F_2)`, even when `Gamma` is infinite.  Give
`A^*` the contragredient action

```text
(g dot t)(a)=t(g^(-1) dot a).                         (DHE5)
```

Then `ell` is fixed by `Gamma`.  Let

```text
T=span_F2{g dot ell:g in G} subset A^*.               (DHE6)
```

Both `A` and `T` are countable elementary abelian groups.  The evaluation
pairing

```text
<t,a>=t(a) in F_2                                    (DHE7)
```

is `G`-invariant.  More concretely, `g dot ell` is the characteristic
functional of the left coset `gGamma`:

```text
(g dot ell)(a_y)=1  iff  y in gGamma.                 (DHE8)
```

## 3. The binary Heisenberg group

On the set

```text
H(A,T)=A times T times F_2                            (DHE9)
```

define

```text
(a,t,c)(a',t',c')
 =(a+a',t+t',c+c'+t(a')).                            (DHE10)
```

Bilinearity of evaluation makes `t(a')` a normalized two-cocycle, so
`(DHE10)` is associative.  Put

```text
z=(0,0,1),  u_a=(a,0,0),  v_t=(0,t,0).               (DHE11)
```

Then `z` is central of order two, the two displayed lamp families are
commuting involutions, and

```text
v_t u_a v_t^(-1)=z^(t(a))u_a,
[v_t,u_a]=z^(t(a)).                                   (DHE12)
```

The group has nilpotency class two and exponent at most four.  It is locally
finite: a finite set of triples lies in the subgroup formed from the
finite-dimensional spans of its `A`- and `T`-coordinates together with
`F_2 z`, and that subgroup is finite.

The diagonal `G`-action on `(a,t,c)` preserves `(DHE10)` by `(DHE7)`.  We
may therefore form

```text
E=H(A,T) semidirect G.                                (DHE13)
```

If `G` is generated by a finite set `S`, then `E` is generated by

```text
S union {u_(a_e),v_ell}.                              (DHE14)
```

Indeed the `G`-orbits of `a_e` and `ell` span `A` and `T`, respectively,
and `z=[v_ell,u_(a_e)]`.  Thus the particular group used below is finitely
generated.

## 4. The exact one-edge spatializer

Inside `E`, put

```text
J=<z,A,G>=C_2 z times (A semidirect G),
K=<z,A,Gamma>=C_2 z times (A semidirect Gamma).        (DHE15)
```

Define the parity character

```text
epsilon(u_a gamma)=ell(a),
chi=(-1)^epsilon.                                      (DHE16)
```

It is well defined because `ell` is `Gamma`-fixed.  It is trivial on
`Gamma` and on `z`.  In the negative central corner set

```text
p=(1-u_z)/2,
M=pL(J),  P=pL(K),  V=p u_(v_ell).                    (DHE17)
```

The projection `p` is the unit of the corner, and `V` is a self-adjoint
unitary there.  Equation `(DHE12)` and `pu_z=-p` give, for every `k in K`,

```text
V(pu_k)V^*=chi(k)pu_k.                                (DHE18)
```

Thus `V` exactly implements the edge automorphism from
`FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_SPATIALIZATION_CERTIFICATE.md`.  Unlike the half-circle
unitary in `FALSE_FREE_HALF_CIRCLE_SPATIALIZER.md`, it is a group element
and requires no discontinuous functional calculus.

The price is equally explicit: the overgroup is now `E`, and proving its
operator-MF property is the new gate.

## 5. The Kun--Thom word becomes one dual lamp

Fix a strict compressor `s in G` and choose `gamma in Gamma` with

```text
s^(-1) gamma s notin Gamma.                            (DHE19)
```

Let

```text
F=G *_Gamma (Gamma times <v>),  v^2=1, [v,Gamma]=1,   (DHE20)
```

and let

```text
omega=[s v s^(-1),gamma].                             (DHE21)
```

For the chosen Kun--Thom pair, the centralizer-normalization theorem gives

```text
omega in Rad_sof(F).                                  (DHE22)
```

This is the same radical witness used in `(OVS21)`.

Because `v_ell` is an involution fixed by `Gamma`, there is a homomorphism

```text
Phi:F->E,   Phi(g)=g (g in G),   Phi(v)=v_ell.         (DHE23)
```

The dual lamps commute, so direct calculation gives

```text
Phi(omega)=v_r,
r=s dot ell + gamma s dot ell in T.                   (DHE24)
```

The functional `r` is nonzero.  Indeed `(DHE8)` identifies its two
summands with the characteristic functions of `sGamma` and
`gamma sGamma`.  Equality of those left cosets would say
`s^(-1)gamma s in Gamma`, contrary to `(DHE19)`; distinct left cosets are
disjoint.

There is a useful functorial point here.  Although a sofic radical need not
behave monotonically under arbitrary subgroup inclusions, `(DHE23)` is
enough to put `v_r` in the radical of the whole target.  If
`psi:E->S_0` is any homomorphism to a sofic group, then
`psi composed Phi:F->S_0` kills `omega` by `(DHE22)`.  Hence

```text
v_r in Rad_sof(E).                                    (DHE25)
```

Choose `y` in the symmetric difference of `sGamma` and `gamma sGamma`.
Then `r(a_y)=1`, so `(DHE12)` yields

```text
[v_r,u_(a_y)]=z.                                      (DHE26)
```

The full sofic radical is a normal subgroup, being an intersection of
normal kernels.  Equations `(DHE25)--(DHE26)` prove the main algebraic
statement.

**Theorem 1 (central-radical Heisenberg envelope).**  For the fixed
Kun--Thom pair,

```text
1 != z in Z(E) intersect Rad_sof(E).                  (DHE27)
```

In particular `E` is nonsofic.

The quotient by the central sign is nonsofic as well.  The element `v_r`
remains nontrivial in `E/<z>`, and every homomorphism from this quotient to
a sofic group, pulled back to `E`, kills it.  Hence

```text
1 != image(v_r) in Rad_sof(E/<z>).                    (DHE28)
```

This observation is also why the finite-kernel theorem of
Bachner--Dogon--Lubotzky does not shortcut the analytic gate: its required
hyperlinearity hypothesis on the quotient would itself solve the problem.

## 6. The exact FALSE endpoint

Apply `FALSE_FINITE_RADICAL_CORNER.md` to `(DHE27)`.

**Corollary 2.**  If `E` is operator-MF, then there is a hyperlinear
nonsofic group.

**Proof.**  An injective norm-matrix-corona representation of `E` retains
the central involution `z`.  Compress to the nonzero spectral corner where
`z=-1` and normalize the matrix traces on that corner.  Centrality makes
the compressed maps asymptotically multiplicative in operator norm, hence
in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  The resulting tracial-ultraproduct
image retains `z`, so it is hyperlinear.  It cannot be sofic because every
homomorphism from `E` to a sofic group kills `z` by `(DHE27)`.  End proof.

There is an equivalent von Neumann endpoint.  The map

```text
E->U(pL(E)),  x |-> pu_x                               (DHE29)
```

is injective: equality of two such Fourier vectors would force equality of
their group indices (the alternative index differing by `z` changes the
sign).  Therefore Connes embeddability of `pL(E)` makes the fixed group `E`
itself hyperlinear and, by `(DHE27)`, nonsofic.

The Heisenberg description also identifies the finite algebra:

```text
pL(H(A,T)) = L(A) crossed T,
pL(E)      = (L(A) crossed T) crossed G,              (DHE30)
```

where `t in T` acts on the Fourier unitary `u_a` by the sign
`(-1)^(t(a))`.  The first algebra is hyperfinite because `H(A,T)` is
locally finite.  The second crossed product is the unresolved part.

The kernel algebra and its action are now computed exactly in
`FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_CENTER_PAULI_DECOMPOSITION.md`.  If `q:A->F_2^(G/Gamma)`
is cosetwise parity and `K=ker(q)`, then

```text
pL(H(A,T)) isomorphic L(K) tensor R_(G/Gamma),
```

with a center-valued Pauli permutation action.  The center crossed product
is already Connes embeddable, and the central cocycle becomes an exact
coboundary on an ordinary Bernoulli gauge cover.  The unsolved part is
precisely the normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt transport of the coset-indexed
Pauli factors.  The same decomposition also shows that the subalgebra
generated by the dual Pauli lamps and `G` is exactly

```text
L((direct-sum_(G/Gamma) C_2) semidirect G),
```

the original hard Kun--Thom generalized wreath algebra.  Hence the
Heisenberg tracial endpoint contains, rather than bypasses, that original
hyperlinearity problem.

## 7. Why known permanence does not prove the endpoint

The exact sequence

```text
1 -> H(A,T) -> E -> G -> 1                            (DHE31)
```

has amenable kernel and residually finite quotient.  Neither operator-MF
nor hyperlinearity is known to pass through an arbitrary extension in this
orientation.  The relevant action is not an innocuous profinite action:
its pairing records the distinction between `sGamma` and
`gamma sGamma`, while every honest finite model that could detect `z`
would contradict `(DHE27)`.

The halo-action results audited in `FALSE_HALO_ACTION_AUDIT.md` require an
appropriate sofic/hyperlinear action.  Establishing that condition here
would already supply a finitely generated hyperlinear nonsofic local target,
so it is circular for constructing the first example.  Likewise, Shulman's
symmetric-amalgam theorem does not pass to the quotient which imposes
commutation of all conjugates of `v_ell`.

More explicitly, the one-vertex group `H_theta` from
`FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_GROUP_CORNER_AND_COVER.md` maps onto `E`: send its edge
implementer to `v_ell`.  The additional quotient relations are

```text
[g v_ell g^(-1),h v_ell h^(-1)]=1,   g,h in G.        (DHE32)
```

Operator-MF does not pass to arbitrary quotients, so the already banked MF
information on an untwisted double cannot be pushed through `(DHE32)`.

The tempting operator-norm theorem would have been:

```text
H(A,T) semidirect G is operator-MF.                    (DHE33)
```

This statement is false.  The Kazhdan--stable-finite obstruction in
`FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_OPERATOR_STERILITY.md` proves that every norm-corona
model kills `z`.  Even operator-MF of the quotient `E/<z>` is not currently
banked; by `(DHE28)`, proving that quotient hyperlinear would already finish
FALSE.  Thus `FALSE_FINITE_KERNEL_AUDIT.md` should be read here as a sector
decomposition, not as an automatic source of the faithful complementary
sector.

## 8. The negative multiplier and a two-seed criterion

The sector needed for FALSE is smaller than operator-MF of all of `E`.
Write

```text
Q=E/<z>=(A directSum T) semidirect G.                  (DHE34)
```

Use the section which sends `(a,t,g)` to `(a,t,0)g in E`.  From `(DHE10)`
and the semidirect-product rule, its central multiplier in the character
`z |-> -1` is

```text
sigma((a,t,g),(a',t',g'))=(-1)^(t(g dot a')).         (DHE35)
```

Thus the negative sector asks only for maps

```text
W_n:Q->U(d_n)                                         (DHE36)
```

such that, for every fixed `x,y in Q`,

```text
||W_n(x)W_n(y)-sigma(x,y)W_n(xy)||_op -> 0.           (DHE37)
```

No injectivity or norm-separation hypothesis is imposed.

**Theorem 3 (one negative sector already proves FALSE).**  If maps
`(DHE36)--(DHE37)` exist, then there is a hyperlinear nonsofic group.

**Proof.**  Equation `(DHE37)` is equivalently an operator-norm asymptotic
representation of `E` on which `z` is the scalar `-1`.  Restrict it along
`Phi:F->E` from `(DHE23)`.  This gives an operator-norm, hence normalized-
Hilbert--Schmidt, asymptotic representation of `F`.

The radical word `omega` maps to `v_r`.  Choose `a_y` as in `(DHE26)`.
Since `[v_r,u_(a_y)]=z`, the two corresponding matrix unitaries
asymptotically anticommute.  Trace invariance under conjugacy gives

```text
tr_(d_n)(W_n(image(v_r))) -> 0.                        (DHE38)
```

Consequently

```text
||W_n(image(v_r))-1||_2^2 -> 2.                       (DHE39)
```

The tracial-ultraproduct image of `F` therefore retains `omega`.  It is
hyperlinear, and it cannot be sofic because `omega in Rad_sof(F)`.  End
proof.

The same target can be stated using only two matrix involutions.  Put

```text
X_(n,g)=U_n(g)X_nU_n(g)^*,
Z_(n,g)=U_n(g)Z_nU_n(g)^*.                            (DHE40)
```

Here `U_n:G->U(d_n)` is pointwise asymptotically multiplicative and
`X_n,Z_n` are self-adjoint involutions.  The defining relations of the
negative Heisenberg sector are exactly, pointwise in fixed indices,

```text
[X_(n,g),X_(n,h)] -> 0,
[Z_(n,g),Z_(n,h)] -> 0,                               (DHE41)

Z_(n,g gamma)-Z_(n,g) -> 0,       gamma in Gamma,    (DHE42)

Z_(n,g) X_(n,h) Z_(n,g)^*
 -(-1)^(1_(h in gGamma))X_(n,h) -> 0.                 (DHE43)
```

All limits in `(DHE41)--(DHE43)` are in operator norm.  The cosets `gGamma`
are the only linear identifications among the orbit generators of `T`, so
these relations and asymptotic multiplicativity of `U_n` give
`(DHE37)` by collecting every fixed word into Heisenberg normal form.
Conversely, a negative projective sector supplies `(DHE40)--(DHE43)` by
taking the two seed lamps.

**Corollary 4 (two-seed Weyl endpoint).**  Existence of
`U_n,X_n,Z_n` satisfying `(DHE40)--(DHE43)` proves FALSE.

**Correction.**  This sufficient endpoint is empty.  Theorem 1 of
`FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_OPERATOR_STERILITY.md` uses only `(DHE42)`,
`(DHE43)`, property `(T)`, and the strict compressor to prove that no such
operator-norm sequence exists.  In particular `(DHE36)--(DHE37)` cannot
exist either.  The finite Pauli realization below remains correct, but it
cannot be equipped with the required coherent operator-norm `G`-transport.

The Weyl part has no finite-window obstruction.  Let `C subset G` and
`R subset G/Gamma` be finite.  On `ell^2(F_2^C)`, let `X_h` translate the
`h`-coordinate and let `Z_R0` multiply the basis vector `delta_b` by

```text
(-1)^(sum_(h in C intersect R0)b_h),       R0 in R.   (DHE43a)
```

The `X_h` commute, the `Z_R0` commute, and

```text
Z_R0 X_h Z_R0^*=(-1)^(1_(h in R0))X_h.                (DHE43b)
```

Thus every finite coset-incidence matrix has an exact Pauli realization.
The hard condition is a common
operator-norm asymptotic `G`-transport satisfying both the coset
identifications `(DHE42)` and all shifted incidences `(DHE43)`.  Honest
finite-quotient transport would make `Gamma` profinitely separable in the
required orbit and is excluded by
`FALSE_ONE_ORBIT_PARITY_SEPARABILITY_NO_GO.md`.  A solution must therefore
be genuinely nonmonomial.

## 9. A fold obstruction inside the full symmetric amalgam

The new external envelope also clarifies why the existing full symmetric
amalgam cannot already hide an operator-norm approximation to `v_ell`.

Let `A_J` be a unital C-star completion of the vertex whose chosen trace has
GNS image `M=pL(J)`, let `C_0 subset A_J` be the even-edge algebra, and put

```text
B=A_J *_C_0 A_J.                                      (DHE44)
```

There is a fold homomorphism

```text
fold:B->A_J                                           (DHE45)
```

which is the identity on both vertex copies.  Let `S subset Gamma`, `q in K`,
and `c>0` be the fixed Kazhdan data from
`FALSE_TWISTED_VERTEX_INNERNESS_GAP.md`, and write `w_k` for the copy of
the corresponding vertex unitary in the first factor of `B`.  For every
`m` and every unitary `W in M_m(B)`, functoriality of the C-star norm
followed by the GNS map gives

```text
max_(k in S union {q})
 ||W(1_m tensor w_k)W^*-chi(k)(1_m tensor w_k)||
 >= c.                                                (DHE46)
```

Indeed the image of `W` under `GNS composed fold` is a unitary in
`M_m(M)`, and its maximum tracial `2`-norm defect is at least `c` by
`(TVG3)`.  Each such `2`-norm is bounded above by the C-star norm of the
corresponding element before applying the homomorphism.  This proves
`(DHE46)`.

**Corollary 3 (full-fold no-go).**  No sequence of unitaries in finite
matrix amplifications of Shulman's full symmetric even-edge amalgam can
implement the parity automorphism in operator norm, even on the fixed set
`S union {q}`.

Thus finite matrix stabilization, continuous half-circle approximation,
or another unitary chosen internally in the full amalgam cannot produce
the required spatializer.  The Borel half-circle construction is genuinely
tracial, while the Heisenberg generator is genuinely external.  The
operator-sterility theorem now also excludes operator-MF of the external
group.  What remains is a genuinely tracial construction: select a suitable
trace on the reduced half-circle side or prove Connes embeddability of the
negative corner `pL(E)`.

## 10. Status

The completed part is algebraic and exact:

```text
dual orbit lamp
  -> exact edge spatializer
  -> nonzero dual radical lamp
  -> central radical involution.
```

No numerical experiment or asymptotic estimate enters the algebraic proof.
The operator-norm candidates `(DHE33)` and `(DHE37)` are now rigorously
excluded by `FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_OPERATOR_STERILITY.md`.  The fold no-go
`(DHE46)` also rules out the most direct internal use of the already MF
symmetric full amalgam.  Future work on this envelope must therefore target
a genuinely tracial representation of `(DHE30)`, in particular Connes
embeddability of `pL(E)`, rather than more operator-norm functional calculus
in the regular vertex.

## 11. Literature boundary

The three current permanence results most liable to be confused with
`(DHE33)` do not prove it:

* Shulman, *The MF property for amalgamated free products*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13564>, proves symmetric-amalgam and selected
  free-group crossed-product permanence, not arbitrary amenable-kernel
  semidirect-product permanence.
* Alekseev--Bradford, *Sofic actions, halo products, and metric
  approximations of groups*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18742>, requires
  the automorphism action itself to have the relevant approximation
  property; `FALSE_HALO_ACTION_AUDIT.md` explains why verifying it here is
  already circular.
* Bachner--Dogon--Lubotzky, *On L1-approximation of groups*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17392>, Proposition 1.6 assumes both that the
  total finite extension is MF and that its quotient is hyperlinear.  In
  `(DHE28)` the quotient is already nonsofic, so its hyperlinearity would be
  the desired counterexample rather than an available hypothesis.

Accordingly, the present note claims an exact algebraic reduction and a
new compact tracial target.  Its formerly proposed MF target is disproved
in `FALSE_DUAL_HEISENBERG_OPERATOR_STERILITY.md`.
