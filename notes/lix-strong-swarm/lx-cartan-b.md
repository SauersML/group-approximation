## Mathematics (settled before authoring)

Notation: `Φ = oddDiagApp p` (natural, `Λ`-linear chain map `W ⊗ C(X) → C(X)^{⊗p}`, T ↦ `tupT`),
`D_j(u)(σ) = tupEval (u^{⊗p}) (Φ(e_j ⊗ σ))` for `u ∈ C^q`, `σ` a `(pq − j)`-simplex.  `c̄_{a,b} :=
wdPhi p a b 1 1` (`= 1` if `a` even, `= 1` if `a` odd and `b` even, `= 0` if both odd: `wdPhi_one_one_*`).

**D-form Cartan (cochain level, every degree).**  For cocycles `u ∈ C^q`, `v ∈ C^{q'}` and every `i`,

    D_i(u ⌣ v) = Σ_{a+b=i, a ≤ pq, b ≤ pq'} κ(a,b) · (D_a u ⌣ D_b v) + δ(h),
    κ(a,b) = ε(q,q') · c̄_{a,b} · (−1)^{b(pq−a)},   ε(q,q') = (−1)^{q q' p(p−1)/2}  (riffle Koszul sign).

Route: compare two natural `Λ`-chain maps `oddSrc ⟶ oddTgt p (2p) 2` (T acting as `tupT²` on `2p` slots):
* `A = AW^{⊗p} ∘ Φ` (slotwise Alexander–Whitney, interleaved `x₁'x₁''…x_p'x_p''`);
* `B(e_i ⊗ σ) = riffle( Σ_{AW splits σ = σ'·σ''} R_{σ',σ''}(ψ_W(e_i)) )`, extended `Λ`-linearly, where
  `R(T^s e_a ⊗ T^{s'} e_b) = (−1)^{b·|σ'|} concat(Φ(T^s e_a ⊗ σ'), Φ(T^{s'} e_b ⊗ σ''))`.
Both send `e_0 ⊗ x` to `x^{⊗2p}`, so `acyclicModelsHomotopy` (free source, acyclic target) gives `A ≃ B`.
Evaluating against `(u⊗v)^{⊗p}`: boundaries die (E4'), the W-part of the source differential dies
(`tupT²`-invariance of the alternating functional; the norm gives `p = 0`), the S-part is `δ` of the
homotopy's evaluation.  `E∘A(e_i⊗σ) = D_i(u⌣v)(σ)`; `E∘B(e_i⊗σ) = Σ κ(a,b)(D_a u ⌣ D_b v)(σ)`.

**Even classes, the total operation.**  For `q, q'` even every `κ(a,b)` with `a, b` even is `1`
(`c̄ = 1`, `b` even, `ε = 1`) and every `(odd, odd)` pair has `c̄ = 0`.  So, summing over even `i`,

    P_tot(x ⌣ y) = P_tot(x) · P_tot(y),   P_tot(x) := Σ_{j even, j ≤ pq} TotalHOf.of X (pq − j) [D_j x],

for every space `X` and even-degree classes `x, y`, with no vanishing lemma.

**V (bad residues).**  For `q` even, `[D_j u] = 0` unless `j ≡ 0 or −1 mod 2(p−1)`.  Route: `r` a
primitive root; `A_r : W → W` semilinear for `T ↦ T^r`, `A_r(e_{2k}) = r^k e_{2k}`,
`A_r(e_{2k+1}) = r^k (1+T+⋯+T^{r−1}) e_{2k+1}` (augmentations `r^k`, `r^{k+1}`); `σ_r = tupPermAll (π_r⁻¹)`
(`π_r i = r·i`), `σ_r T = T^r σ_r`; `σ_r∘Φ` and `Φ∘(A_r⊗1)` are natural `Λ`-chain maps into
`oddTgt p p r` agreeing in degree 0, hence homotopic; `tupEval (u^{⊗p}) ∘ σ_r = tupEval (u^{⊗p})` for `q`
even.  So `[D_j u] = μ_j [D_j u]`, `μ_j ≠ 1` off the two residues.  With V, `P_tot(h) = h^p + c_2 h` on
degree-2 classes (the `j = 2p` term is a bad residue).

**V' is NOT built (finding, sent to lx-pzero and lx-design).**  The graded Cartan for general `X` also
needs `[D_a x] = 0` for `a > d(p−1)` (good-residue terms `a = (d+2t)(p−1)` occur once `d ≥ 2(p−1)`).  R7
instability is by definition and does not give it; for singular chains the classical proof needs a
carrier construction (diagonal supported on nondegenerate affine simplices).  The consumer spaces
(`Y = ∏ CP`, `N`, the flag space) have even cohomology generated by degree-2 classes and `z`, where the
graded components, instability and Wu follow algebraically from `P_tot` and `P_tot(h) = h^p + c_2 h`.

**T² corner (the one odd statement).**  `[D_{2(p−1)}(t₁⌣t₂)] = (−1)^{(p−1)/2}[D_{p−1}t₁ ⌣ D_{p−1}t₂]` on
`S¹ × S¹`: every other term vanishes by degree on `S¹` (the `a = p` term is an `H⁰` class, zero by
restriction to points).  Hence `c_2 = (−1)^{(p−1)/2} c_1²`, a unit.
