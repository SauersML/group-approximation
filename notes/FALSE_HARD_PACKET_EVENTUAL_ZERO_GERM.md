# The hard boundary packet has an all-depth eventual-zero germ quotient

Date: 2026-08-13

## Outcome

The repeated failure to find the complementary child in the six hard
boundary pairs is structural, not merely a depth-six or depth-nine search
artifact.  All six pairs preserve the same three-dimensional path sector,
and so does the algebra generated by all six pairs together.

Use the standard path representation of the binary Leavitt algebra over
`F_2`.  A monomial `alpha beta*` replaces an initial prefix `beta` by
`alpha`.  Let `V_0` be the span of the three eventually-zero paths

```text
000 0^infinity = 0^infinity,
010 0^infinity,
10  0^infinity.
```

Exact prefix arithmetic proves that every hard generator `q,l` and each of
their Leavitt stars preserves `V_0`.  Restriction therefore gives a
three-dimensional star representation

```text
rho_0 : A_hard -> M_3(F_2),
```

where `A_hard` may be the star algebra of any one hard pair or the combined
star algebra of all six pairs.

For the parent `e=p_alpha`, its recovered zero child `f=p_(alpha0)`, and the
complementary child `h=p_(alpha1)`, the representation satisfies

```text
rank rho_0(e) = rank rho_0(f) = 1,
rho_0(e) = rho_0(f),
rho_0(h) = 0.                                      (EZG1)
```

Consequently no element of `A_hard` can implement an equivalence from `e`
to `h`.  Indeed, if `x* x=e` and `x x*=h`, then after applying `rho_0`,

```text
E^2 = X* (X X*) X = 0,
```

while `(EZG1)` makes `E` a nonzero idempotent.  This contradiction is purely
multiplicative; it uses neither positivity nor characteristic zero.  The
generic endpoint is kernel-checked in
`Leavitt/ZeroRangeObstruction.lean` as
`idempotent_eq_zero_of_factors_reverse_zero`.

Thus combining the two hard boundary classes at arbitrary word depth still
cannot produce the missing complementary-child equivalence from the hard
generators alone.

## Interaction with the compressor packet

The complete scalarized raw swap and comb compressor both preserve `V_0`
and act as the identity there.  This does **not** refute the known properly
infinite packet in `TRUE_RAW_COMPRESSOR_PROPERLY_INFINITE_PACKET.md`: that
packet uses the parent `p_11`, which is killed by `rho_0`, and the longer
range cylinders `p_11000` and `p_111`.

The source-word factorization nevertheless identifies a possible additional
handle.  Of the six distinct atlas syllables in the raw word, four leave the
germ sector in at least one orientation; of the 28 distinct comb syllables,
eleven do.  Their complete products return and act trivially.  Intermediate
source-word excursions therefore carry information which this particular
quotient does not see in the completed compressor elements.

This does not prove that such an excursion is necessary.  The existing
properly infinite packet already uses the completed raw/comb elements once
the characteristic-two projections `p_11,p_001` have been recovered.  The
germ theorem instead closes the hard-only substitute and leaves the honest
remaining gate unchanged:

> Recover `p_11,p_001` and the completed compressor corners in one finite
> multiplicity algebra; an intermediate source-prefix holonomy is an optional
> route to that recovery, not an assumed ingredient.

The final properly infinite identities and the uniform trace contradiction
are already exact and Lean-checked.  The missing theorem remains the
matrix-coordinate recovery of those characteristic-two projection formulas.
