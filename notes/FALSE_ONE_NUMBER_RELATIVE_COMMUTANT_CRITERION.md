# A one-number relative-commutant criterion for FALSE

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

The finite relative-commutant problem can be measured by one scalar.  Let
`Gamma<G` be a Kun--Thom compressor pair, choose `t in G` and
`gamma in Gamma` such that

```text
h=t^(-1) gamma t notin Gamma,                          (ONR1)
```

and form the free-lamp amalgam

```text
H=G *_Gamma (Gamma times <k>).                        (ONR2)
```

Its standard marked word is

```text
w=[t k t^(-1),gamma].                                 (ONR3)
```

For the framed pair used in this repository, the Kun--Thom theorem gives

```text
1!=w in Rad_sof(H).                                   (ONR4)
```

Suppose `pi_n:G->U(d_n)` are normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt almost
representations on increasing finite windows, with base multiplication
defect `epsilon_n->0`.  Choose the `Gamma`-subwindows slowly enough that
they still exhaust `Gamma`, and let `W_n` be the finite-dimensional unital
C-star algebra generated by the matrices assigned to the selected
`Gamma`-subwindow.  Put

```text
x_n=pi_n(t)^* pi_n(gamma) pi_n(t),
Delta_n=1-||E_(W_n)(x_n)||_2^2
       =||x_n-E_(W_n)(x_n)||_2^2.                    (ONR5)
```

Here `E_(W_n)` is the trace-preserving conditional expectation.  The main
criterion is

```text
Delta_n>0 eventually and epsilon_n^2/Delta_n->0
  ==> a hyperlinear nonsofic group exists.             (ONR6)
```

Thus no higher moment, asymptotic-freeness, block-multiplicity, or random
matrix hypothesis is needed.  A successful FALSE model only has to make
the Hilbert--Schmidt size of the predecessor escape dominate the
relation-error scale.

## 2. Trace-gap amplification

We first isolate the quantitative conversion used in `(ONR6)`.

**Lemma 1 (trace-gap/defect amplifier).**  Let `K` be countable and let

```text
1!=q in Rad_sof(K).                                    (ONR7)
```

Suppose `phi_n:K->U(e_n)` are maps on increasing multiplication windows.
Let `eta_n` bound the multiplicative defect on the `n`th window and put

```text
kappa_n=1-Re tr_(e_n)(phi_n(q)).                       (ONR8)
```

If `kappa_n>0` eventually and

```text
eta_n^2/kappa_n->0,                                   (ONR9)
```

then there is a hyperlinear nonsofic group.

**Proof.**  First remove all scalar phases from the relation errors.  Put

```text
theta_n(g)=phi_n(g) directSum conjugate(phi_n(g))
           directSum 1_(e_n) directSum 1_(e_n).       (ONR10a)
```

For a tested product `gh`, set

```text
D_n(g,h)=phi_n(gh)^* phi_n(g)phi_n(h).
```

If its defect is `delta_n(g,h)=||D_n(g,h)-1||_2`, then the relative
unitary for `theta_n` has real normalized trace

```text
r_n(g,h)=(1+Re tr(D_n(g,h)))/2
        =1-delta_n(g,h)^2/4.                         (ONR10b)
```

Likewise, if `a_n=tr(phi_n(q))`, then

```text
s_n=tr(theta_n(q))=(1+Re a_n)/2=1-kappa_n/2
```

lies in `[0,1]`.  Choose integers `L_n->infinity` such that

```text
L_n eta_n^2->0,       L_n kappa_n->infinity.          (ONR10c)
```

Such a choice exists by `(ONR9)`.  For example, when both scales tend to
zero one can take an integer asymptotic to
`(eta_n^2 kappa_n)^(-1/2)`; zero defects and trace gaps bounded away from
zero are easier.  Define the single tensor amplification

```text
Psi_n(g)=theta_n(g)^(tensor L_n).
```

Equation `(ONR10b)` and `1-r^L<=L(1-r)` for `0<=r<=1` give

```text
||Psi_n(g)Psi_n(h)-Psi_n(gh)||_2^2
 =2-2r_n(g,h)^L_n
 <=L_n delta_n(g,h)^2/2.                             (ONR10d)
```

Thus all multiplication defects tend to zero by `(ONR10c)`.  On the marked
word,

```text
tr(Psi_n(q))=(1-kappa_n/2)^L_n
             <=exp(-L_n kappa_n/2)->0.
```

Hence the maps `Psi_n` define a homomorphism from `K` to a tracial matrix
ultraproduct in which `q` is nontrivial.  Its countable image is
hyperlinear.  If that image were sofic, the quotient homomorphism from `K`
would kill `(ONR7)`, a contradiction.  End proof.

The conjugate and identity summands in `(ONR10a)` are load bearing.  They
turn every relative relation trace into a number in `[0,1]`, replacing the
linear tensor-telescoping loss by the quadratic identity `(ONR10d)`.  A raw
tensor power can accumulate an uncontrolled scalar phase and only yields
the weaker condition `eta_n/kappa_n->0`.

## 3. Haar averaging over one finite commutant

Let

```text
C_n=W_n'.                                              (ONR13)
```

Haar measure on the compact unitary group `U(C_n)` implements the
trace-preserving conditional expectation onto the bicommutant:

```text
integral_(v in U(C_n)) v x v^* dv
  =E_(C_n')(x)=E_(W_n)(x).                            (ONR14)
```

Applying `(ONR14)` to the unitary `x_n` from `(ONR5)` gives

```text
integral Re tr(v x_n v^* x_n^*) dv
  =Re tr(E_(W_n)(x_n)x_n^*)
  =||E_(W_n)(x_n)||_2^2
  =1-Delta_n.                                         (ONR15)
```

Therefore one may choose `v_n in U(C_n)` satisfying

```text
1-Re tr(v_n x_n v_n^* x_n^*)>=Delta_n.               (ONR16)
```

This choice is deterministic; no concentration estimate is required.

## 4. Proof of the criterion

Map the first factor in `(ONR2)` using `pi_n` and map the lamp generator
`k` to `v_n`.  Fix normal forms in `H` and evaluate them with these
matrices.  Since `v_n` commutes exactly with every matrix in the current
`Gamma`-window, the lamp--`Gamma` relations in that window have zero defect.
Every other fixed multiplication relation uses only finitely many
multiplications from `G`, so its defect is at most a word-dependent constant
times `epsilon_n`.

The chosen representative `(ONR3)` evaluates exactly as

```text
[pi_n(t)v_n pi_n(t)^*,pi_n(gamma)].                   (ONR17)
```

Cyclic invariance of normalized trace and the definition of `x_n` give

```text
tr((ONR17))=tr(v_n x_n v_n^* x_n^*).                 (ONR18)
```

Consequently `(ONR16)` says that the trace gap of the marked radical word
is at least `Delta_n`.  To make the diagonalization explicit, enumerate the
multiplication table of `H`.  At stage `m`, let `c_m` be a finite telescoping
constant for its first `m` entries.  Because `epsilon_n^2/Delta_n->0`, pass to
a subsequence on which `c_m^2 epsilon_n^2/Delta_n<1/m` and use only the first
`m` elements of `Gamma` when defining `W_n`.  Replacing a larger
`Gamma`-algebra by this smaller one can only increase `Delta_n`, since
conditional expectation is orthogonal projection.  The selected
subwindows still exhaust `Gamma`, while the induced `H`-window defect
`eta_n` now satisfies `eta_n^2/Delta_n->0`.

Lemma 1 applies with `K=H`, `q=w`, and `kappa_n>=Delta_n`.  It produces a
hyperlinear image of `H` in which `w` survives.  Equation `(ONR4)` then
forces that image to be nonsofic.  This proves `(ONR6)`.

## 5. Exact interpretation of the remaining gate

The number `Delta_n` is the squared normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt distance

```text
dist_2(x_n,W_n)^2.                                    (ONR19)
```

For honest finite-dimensional representations of the compressor pair, the
finite-representation matching theorem forces the predecessor into the
relevant finite `Gamma`-algebra and hence makes this escape disappear.  The
criterion therefore cannot be satisfied by simply matching exact or
profinite vertex representations more accurately.

On the other hand, `(ONR6)` is strictly weaker than asking for a fixed
positive lower bound on `(ONR19)`.  The escape may tend to zero, provided it
does so more slowly than the multiplication defect.  This is the sharp
rate-sensitive form of the relative-bicommutant endpoint:

```text
one nonliftable predecessor seam with
squared relation defect=o(squared seam size).         (ONR20)
```

Equivalently, the relation defect itself need only be little-oh of the
Hilbert--Schmidt seam size.  This square-scale improvement matters for a
shrinking active corner.  If the wall occupies trace fraction `r_n`, has
order-one size there, and the active relation error has amplitude
`alpha_n->0`, then

```text
Delta_n asymptotic r_n,
epsilon_n^2=O(r_n alpha_n^2),
```

so `(ONR6)` holds independently of how fast `r_n->0`.  Arbitrary
order-one boundary completion is still insufficient, but no comparison
between `alpha_n` and `sqrt(r_n)` is required.

It is also the precise finite target to test in any future Shulman lift,
paired microstate construction, or noncommutative-fiber model.  Merely
showing that the relative commutant is nontrivial, without the comparison
in `(ONR20)`, is insufficient because the escaping corner could have
vanishing normalized trace faster than the relation defect.
