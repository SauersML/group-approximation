# A localized Baumslag--Solitar subgroup has a stabilizer-surviving paired class

Date: 2026-08-11

## 1. Outcome

There is an exact odd-prime paired Bockstein class which survives every
polynomial double-coset stabilizer.  It appears after separating the
coefficient rings of the ambient Laurent elementary group and the
polynomial subgroup.

Fix an odd prime `p`, put `q=p+1`, and set

`A=Z[1/q]`, `R=A[x^(+-1)]`, `R_0=Z[x]`,
`E=E_r(R)`, `Gamma=E_r(R_0)`                         `(LBS1)`

in stable rank.  Inside `E`, let

`h=e_12(x^(-1))`,
`c=diag(q,1,q^(-1),1,...)`.                           `(LBS2)`

Then `<h,c>` is `BS(1,q)`, and it has a finite-support mod-`p` two-cycle
whose ordinary and cyclotomic Bocksteins are respectively the marked
translation `[h]` and the stable-letter class `-[c]`.  No polynomial
double-coset stabilizer contributes its homology class.

Thus this is the first exact stabilizer-surviving paired class in the
Laurent-fold search.  It is not yet a complete FALSE proof.  The load-
bearing remaining global issue is whether the mismatched inclusion

`E_r(Z[x]) < E_r(Z[1/q][x^(+-1)])`                    `(LBS3)`

can be placed in a residually finite Kazhdan infranormal pair with the
required radical theorem.  The usual same-coefficient Kun--Thom proof does
not automatically establish infranormality for `(LBS3)`.

## 2. The exact Baumslag--Solitar subgroup

Conjugation by `(LBS2)` gives

`c h c^(-1)=h^q`.                                     `(LBS4)`

The matrices are faithful on the upper-left three coordinates, so

`B=<h,c> ~= BS(1,q)`
` =<h,c | c h c^(-1)=h^q>`.                           `(LBS5)`

Equivalently,

`B ~= Z[1/q] semidirect Z`,                           `(LBS6)`

where the normal translation subgroup is generated by the conjugates of
`h` and the quotient map

`nu:B->Z`                                             `(LBS7)`

sends `c` to `1` and `h` to `0`.

Because `q=1 mod p`, there is also a character

`chi:B->mu_p`, `chi(h)=zeta_p`, `chi(c)=1`.           `(LBS8)`

Let `O=Z[zeta_p]` and `pi=1-zeta_p`.  Reduction modulo `pi` is the trivial
`F_p` coefficient system.

## 3. An explicit paired bar cycle

In the integral normalized bar complex, define

`A_c=[c|h]+[c h|c^(-1)]-[c^(-1)|c]`,                 `(LBS9)`

and

`T_q(h)=sum_(j=1)^p [h^j|h]`.                         `(LBS10)`

Direct telescoping and `(LBS4)` give

`partial A_c=[h]-[h^q]`,
`partial T_q(h)=q[h]-[h^q]`.                          `(LBS11)`

Hence

`Z_p=T_q(h)-A_c`                                      `(LBS12)`

satisfies

`partial Z_p=p[h]`.                                   `(LBS13)`

Its reduction modulo `p` is a two-cycle and

`beta_1([Z_p])=[h]`.                                  `(LBS14)`

For the cyclotomic boundary, only the first face changes.  Since
`chi(h^j)=zeta_p^j`, `chi(c)=1`, and `chi(ch)=zeta_p`,

`partial_chi Z_p`
` =p[h]+sum_(j=1)^p(zeta_p^j-1)[h]`
`   -(zeta_p-1)[c^(-1)]`.                             `(LBS15)`

The coefficient of `[h]` is zero because

`sum_(j=1)^p zeta_p^j=0`.                             `(LBS16)`

Therefore

`partial_chi Z_p=pi[c^(-1)]`,
`beta_chi([Z_p])=[c^(-1)]=-[c]`.                     `(LBS17)`

## 4. Both Bocksteins are injective

The standard one-relator presentation complex of `BS(1,q)` is aspherical.
With trivial `F_p` coefficients, the exponent sums of the relator in the
`c,h` generators are `0` and `1-q=-p`.  Its degree-two cellular boundary
therefore vanishes modulo `p`, and

`H_2(B,F_p) ~= F_p`.                                  `(LBS18)`

The class `[Z_p]` is nonzero by `(LBS14)`, so it generates `(LBS18)`.

For the cyclotomic first homology, the degree-one boundary is

`d_1(a[h]+b[c])=(zeta_p-1)a`.                         `(LBS19)`

Thus every cycle is a multiple of `[c]`.  The `c`-component of the Fox
boundary of the relator is

`1-h^q`,                                              `(LBS20)`

which evaluates to `1-zeta_p^q=1-zeta_p=pi`.  Hence

`H_1(B,O_chi) ~= O/pi O`,                             `(LBS21)`

generated by `[c]`.  Equation `(LBS17)` is its nonzero generator.

Consequently both maps out of the one-dimensional space `(LBS18)` are
injective, and

`H_2(B,F_p)/(ker(beta_1)+ker(beta_chi)) ~= F_p[Z_p]`. `(LBS22)`

## 5. Classification of polynomial stabilizers

Let `P=E/Gamma`.  For `d in E`, the stabilizer of `d Gamma` under `B` is

`S_d=B intersect d Gamma d^(-1)`.                     `(LBS23)`

Every element of `B` has the upper-triangular matrix form coming from
`(LBS6)`.  If its `c`-exponent is `m`, then its trace is

`q^m+q^(-m)+r-2`.                                     `(LBS24)`

Trace is invariant under conjugation.  Every element of `Gamma` has trace
in `Z[x]`.  For `m!=0`, the constant in `(LBS24)` is not an integer:

`q^|m|+q^(-|m|)=(q^(2|m|)+1)/q^|m|`,                 `(LBS25)`

and the numerator is `1 mod q`.  Therefore an element of `B` which is
conjugate into `Gamma` must have `m=0`.  It follows that

`S_d <= U:=ker(nu)=Z[1/q]`                            `(LBS26)`

for every double coset.

## 6. Stabilizer homology maps to zero

The inclusion of the translation subgroup induces the zero map

`H_1(U,O_chi)->H_1(B,O_chi)`.                         `(LBS27)`

One way to see this is the homology spectral sequence for

`1->U->B->Z->1`.                                      `(LBS28)`

The group `H_1(B,O_chi)` in `(LBS21)` is the base-direction term generated
by `c`; every fiber class from `U` maps to zero.  Equivalently, in the Fox
complex calculation `(LBS19)--(LBS21)`, the target is measured by the
`c`-coordinate, while chains supported in `U` have zero `c`-coordinate.

By `(LBS26)--(LBS27)`, naturality gives

`beta_chi(im(H_2(S_d,F_p)->H_2(B,F_p)))=0`.           `(LBS29)`

But `beta_chi` is injective on `H_2(B,F_p)` by `(LBS17)--(LBS22)`.  Hence

`im(H_2(S_d,F_p)->H_2(B,F_p))=0`                     `(LBS30)`

for every `d`.

Combining `(LBS22)` and `(LBS30)` proves:

**Theorem 1 (stabilizer-surviving paired class).**

`H_2(B,F_p) /`
` (ker(beta_1)+ker(beta_chi)`
`  +sum_d im H_2(S_d,F_p)) ~= F_p`,                  `(LBS31)`

generated by the explicit cycle `[Z_p]` in `(LBS12)`.  Its ordinary
Bockstein is the marked Laurent root `[h]`.

## 7. Nonzero augmentation transgression

Let

`I_P=Aug_(F_p)(E/Gamma)`.                             `(LBS32)`

Shapiro's lemma and

`0->I_P->F_p[P]->F_p->0`                              `(LBS33)`

give the exact segment

`directSum_(B d Gamma) H_2(S_d,F_p)`
` ->H_2(B,F_p) --delta_P--> H_1(B,I_P)`.              `(LBS34)`

Equation `(LBS30)` says that the first arrow is zero.  Therefore

`delta_P([Z_p])!=0`.                                  `(LBS35)`

This is a genuine derived augmentation-module class in the same identity
fiber which contains the fold radical vector

`e_h=delta_(h Gamma)-delta_Gamma`.                    `(LBS36)`

The equality `beta_1([Z_p])=[h]` is the precise marked feature absent from
the earlier unmarked congruence classes.

## 8. Global scope and the remaining gate

The theorem above is unconditional for the inclusion `(LBS1)`.  Turning it
into FALSE would require a global pair to which the Kun--Thom radical
theorem applies and a finite-cover amplification of `(LBS35)`.

The same-coefficient integral pair uses

`E_r(Z[x]) < E_r(Z[x^(+-1)])`.                        `(LBS37)`

Its infranormality follows because nonnegative exponent substitutions are
compressors and their group closure generates every Laurent root.  In
`(LBS1)`, exponent substitutions do not generate the localized scalar
coefficients `q^(-n)`.  Although the normal closure of `Gamma` in `E`
contains them by conjugating with `c`, this does not by itself prove that
the **compression semigroup** generates the ambient group.  Normal
generation is weaker than infranormality.

In fact, `FALSE_MATRIX_ORDER_COMPRESSOR_NO_GO.md` proves that `(LBS3)` is
not infranormal: every element which compresses the full polynomial
elementary group necessarily normalizes it.  Thus `(LBS31)--(LBS35)`
cannot be inserted into the existing Kun--Thom double using this exact
ambient pair.  The remaining valid targets are:

1. construct a Kazhdan infranormal enlargement in which the localized
   scaling direction is generated by genuine compressors while `c` stays
   outside every conjugate of the amalgamated subgroup; or
2. transplant the bar cycle `(LBS12)` to a known infranormal pair through
   a subgroup whose stable-letter exponent remains visible in every
   polynomial stabilizer.

Once one of these algebraic gates is met, `(LBS35)` supplies the exact
local marked obstruction; the remaining analytic task is to amplify it
along finite quotients without losing normalized Hilbert--Schmidt mass.
