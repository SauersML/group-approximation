## Setup

- `F = <a, b | R_1, R_2>`, with `R_1 = [ab^-1, a^-1ba]` and `R_2 = [ab^-1, a^-2ba^2]`, and `c = [a,b]`. These are
  cyclic conjugates of the words used in `thompson-f-metabelian-pairs-obey-sharp-linear-commutator-bound`.
- `||M||_2 = (tr_n M*M)^(1/2)` is the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm, and `e_U(g) = ||g(U) - I||_2^2 = 2 - 2 Re tr_n g(U)`.
- `delta_R(U,W) = max_i ||R_i(U,W) - I||_2`.
- `C_n(F)` is the least constant with `||UW - WU||_2 <= C_n(F) delta_R(U,W)` on `U(n)^2`.
- In the repository, nonhyperlinearity of `F` is equivalent to dimension-uniform HS stability of the
  commutator (statement 3 of `thompson-f-hyperlinear-iff-relator-system-hs-unstable`).
  `fixed-hs-word-gap-is-linear-domination` turns a uniform gap into a uniform linear bound.
- Amenable groups are sofic, hence hyperlinear. So the root would give nonamenability of `F`, which is open.
- Earlier fixed-dimension work:
  - `notes/TRUE_FIXED_DIMENSIONAL_ARC_BOUNDARY.md` proves projective fixed-dimension finiteness (FDA20) by curve
    selection and jets.
  - `notes/DOMINATION_DIMENSION_ESCAPE_CLAIM_MAP.md` lists the "analytic curve-selection/jet bridge" as not yet proved.
  - The Lean `PaperStatements` derive dimension escape from fixed-dimension domination hypotheses without proving them.

### 1. General theorem (direct proof)

Claim `profinite-kernel-words-have-fixed-dimension-hs-bounds`, route
`profinite-kernel-fixed-dimension-hs-bound-proof` (`requires: []`). Let `P = <S | R>` be a finite presentation and
`w` a word that is trivial in every finite quotient of `P`.

1. **Ideal membership.** In `B_0 = Z[x_(s,ij), y_s]`, every entry of `w(X) - I` lies in the ideal `J_n(R)`
   generated by `y_s det X_s - 1` and the entries of `r(X) - I`.
   - Proof: this is Malcev's argument run on the universal representation over `A = B_0/J_n(R)`.
   - `A` is Noetherian. If an entry `e != 0`, pick a maximal `m ⊇ Ann(e)`. Krull intersection in `A_m` gives
     `e notin m^k`.
   - `A/m` is finite (Zariski, Artin--Tate), so `A/m^k` is finite, and `P -> GL_n(A/m^k)` is a finite quotient
     not killing `w`.
2. **Linear bound.** Substituting unitaries into the integer certificate gives
   `||w(U) - I||_2 <= |R| n^2 L_n delta_R(U)` globally on `U(n)^S`, with exponent one and phases kept.
3. **Converse.** `C_n < infinity` for all `n` if and only if `w` is profinitely trivial. Use regular representations.
4. **Monotone.** `C_n <= C_(n+1)`, by padding with `1`.
5. **Mass form.** `e_U(w) <= |R| C_D^2 delta_R^2 + 4 (1 - mu_D(U))`, where `mu_D` is the share of the dimension
   in irreducible blocks of degree `<= D`.
6. **Projective form.** `1 - |tr w(U)|^2 <= C_(n^2)^2 max_r (1 - |tr r(U)|^2)`, via `U ⊗ conj U`. This recovers FDA20
   without curve selection or jets.

### 2. Consequences for F

Claim `thompson-f-commutator-bound-holds-in-each-fixed-dimension`, route
`thompson-f-fixed-dimension-commutator-bound-proof`. It requires the general claim,
`thompson-f-hyperlinear-iff-relator-system-hs-unstable` and `fixed-hs-word-gap-is-linear-domination`. Cannon--Floyd--Parry
(every proper quotient of `F` is abelian) is imported as a quotation, as in `thompson-f-is-lea-only-if-amenable-proof`.

1. The finite residual of `F` is `F'`, so `c` is profinitely trivial. Hence `C_n(F) < infinity` for every `n`, and
   `C_1(F) = 0`.
2. **Schemes.** `J_n(R_1, R_2) = J_n(c)`, so `Rep_n(F) = Rep_n(Z^2)` as `Z`-schemes. Every `F -> GL_n(A)` over a
   commutative ring has abelian image, and cocycle spaces and jets agree.
3. **Reformulation.** `F` is not hyperlinear if and only if `sup_n C_n(F) < infinity`. Each `C_n(F)` is algebraic
   (quantifier elimination). If statement 3 of the equivalence node holds at `epsilon = 1` with `delta`, then
   `sup_n C_n(F) <= 2/delta`.
4. **Degree escape.** `||UW - WU||_2^2 <= 2 C_D(F)^2 delta_R^2 + 4 (1 - mu_D)`. Hyperlinearity witnesses with
   defect `>= epsilon` eventually put `>= epsilon/4` of the dimension in irreducible degree `> D`, for every `D`.
5. **Lower bounds.**
   - `C_2(F) >= 1 + sqrt 2`, exactly, with `U = diag(e^(3 pi i/8), e^(-3 pi i/8))` and `W = [[0,1],[-1,0]]`. Here
     `c = U^2`, `R_1 = U^6` and `R_2 = U^10`. The pair generates a binary dihedral group.
   - `C_n(F) >= 3.2286` for `n >= 4`. A four-dimensional pair with Gaussian-rational Cayley parameters was
     verified in exact rational arithmetic (`replay_verify.py` prints `ALL_EXACT_CHECKS_HOLD`).
   - Remark 4.2 of the artifact: since `3.2286 > 3`, the metabelian identity of
     `thompson-f-metabelian-pairs-obey-sharp-linear-commutator-bound` forces this witness to generate a
     non-metabelian group.

| `n` | float optimum | exactly verified lower bound | structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2.4142135624 | `1 + sqrt 2` (symbolic) | binary dihedral, metabelian |
| 3 | 2.4142135422 | 2.4141 | `n = 2` optimum plus a trivial block |
| 4 | 3.2286951004 | 3.2286 | numerically irreducible (commutant dimension 1), non-metabelian |
| 6 | 3.2221768329 | 3.2221 | non-metabelian; search failed to reach the monotone bound 3.2286 |

`metabelian_word_check.py` (with `.out`) rechecks the identity behind Remark 4.2 and prints `||u_2(U,W) - I||_2` on
the stored witnesses.

These are lower bounds only. A failed search says nothing about the growth of `C_n(F)`.

## Approaches and where each dies

1. **Upper-bounding `C_n(F)` uniformly through the certificate of Theorem 1.1.**
   - The certificate exists in each dimension, but `L_n` is ineffective: the proof goes by contradiction through a
     maximal ideal and a Krull exponent. The explicit shape carries a factor `n^2`.
   - A dimension-free exact certificate in the plain group algebra is impossible. From
     `c - 1 = sum_q A_q (R_(j_q) - 1) B_q` in `C[F_2]`, mapping to `C[F]` kills every `R_j - 1` and gives `c = 1` in `F`.
   - So any dimension-free certificate of the kind in `fixed-coefficient-ideal-certificate-controls-hs-defect` needs
     extra structure (charts, compiled corners), not the bare relators.
   - The commutative entrywise certificates of Theorem 1.1 exist only because the matrix size is fixed. That is
     exactly what degrades as `n -> infinity`.
   - Dies at: no control of `L_n` in `n`.
2. **Jets, deformations, tangent spaces and curve selection in bounded dimension.**
   - Theorem 3.3 gives `Rep_n(F) = Rep_n(Z^2)` as schemes, so every such invariant of `F` coincides with that of
     `Z^2`. A local non-rigidity argument near commuting pairs cannot separate `F` from `Z^2` in any fixed dimension.
   - The "analytic curve-selection/jet bridge" of the claim map is not needed for the fixed-dimension statement, since
     item 6 proves it algebraically. It says nothing about uniformity in `n`, which is where the root lives.
   - Dies at: the scheme equality.
3. **Witnesses or obstructions from representations of bounded irreducible degree.**
   - Mass form (FD8): a hyperlinearity witness must keep at least `epsilon/4` of the dimension in irreducible degree
     tending to infinity.
   - Ultraproducts or direct sums of bounded-degree pieces, such as dihedral, binary polyhedral or `SU(2)` pairs,
     cannot be witnesses. Conversely, bounded degree is already controlled by `C_D(F)`, so a nonhyperlinearity proof
     must control exactly the pairs whose irreducible degree is unbounded.
   - Dies at: Corollary 3.5.
4. **Metabelian and bounded-derived-length pairs.**
   - The free-group identities of `thompson-f-bounded-derived-length-pairs-obey-linear-bounds` item 1 and
     `thompson-f-metabelian-pairs-obey-sharp-linear-commutator-bound` item 2 transfer verbatim to `||.||_2` by
     bi-invariance. Metabelian unitary pairs satisfy `||c - I||_2 <= 3 delta_R`.
   - The exact two-dimensional optimum is binary dihedral, with ratio `1 + sqrt 2 < 3`. The four-dimensional witness
     beats 3 and so is non-metabelian (Remark 4.2).
   - Witnesses must escape every variety `V` with `V(F) != 1`, and separately must have unbounded irreducible degree.
     Neither constraint implies the other.
   - Dies at: only restricts where witnesses live.
5. **Numerical growth of `C_n(F)`.**
   - L-BFGS-B, then an `l^16` surrogate, then Nelder--Mead, over Cayley coordinates.
   - `n = 2, 3` stall at `1 + sqrt 2`, and `n = 4` jumps to `3.2286`. At `n = 6` (40 restarts, budget 500) the search
     only reached `3.2221`, below the monotone bound.
   - The heuristic degrades quickly with `n`. It gives no evidence either way on boundedness.
   - Dies at: search dimension `2n^2` and landscape quality.
6. **Permutation witnesses as unitary witnesses.**
   - `||P - I||_2^2 = 2 x (moved fraction)`, so the repository's 27-point pair gives only `C_27(F) >= 2.2913`, far
     below the four-dimensional unitary pair.
   - Dies at: permutation pairs are a thin slice of `U(n)^2`.
7. **The root itself.**
   - Nonhyperlinearity implies nonamenability, so a proof would settle the amenability problem for `F` negatively.
     That problem is open (Guba's survey, arXiv:2305.07113).
   - Hyperlinearity of `F` is also open. By Theorem 3.4 it is exactly `C_n(F) -> infinity`, and by the constraints
     above the witnesses must have large irreducible degree and escape all varieties that `F` escapes.
   - BCJM (arXiv:2311.09202): if `F` were amenable, hyperlinear approximations would come from sofic ones.
   - Dies at: out of reach. Nothing here moves the amenability problem.

## Open directions worth a future session

- An effective certificate for `n = 2`: a Groebner basis over `Z` of `J_2(R_1, R_2)` expressing the entries of
  `c(X) - I`. It would give an explicit upper bound for `C_2(F)` and test whether `C_2(F) = 1 + sqrt 2`.
- Test whether `C_3(F) = C_2(F)`, since the float search found nothing better at `n = 3`. This is a finite real-algebraic
  problem, decidable but expensive.
- Lower bounds along structured families of unbounded irreducible degree, which (FD8) requires. The quasi-regular
  representations on `F/H` for finite-index `H` are useless, since `H ⊇ F'` makes them abelian. So any family with
  `C_n(F)` growing cannot factor through a finite quotient of `F`, and must satisfy the relators only approximately.
  A naive compression of the Koopman representation on `L^2[0,1]` to dyadic step functions is not a candidate as
  it stands: `x_0` has slopes `2` and `1/2` on a positive fraction of `[0,1]`, so the compressed operators are far
  from unitary in normalized HS. This was not pursued further.
