## 1. Strongest fixed-packet witness

For the raw Pauli packet

```text
K=<raw,z_11,z_001>,        |K|=16,
A_n=Alg(rho_n(K)),         F_n=the comb-transported carrier,
```

the exact intersection certificate gives

```text
dist_2(F_n,A_n)^2 -> 3/16.
```

Haar averaging over `U(A_n')` therefore gives a coordinate unitary `V_n`
such that

```text
[V_n,A_n]=0,
||[V_n,F_n]||_2^2 >= 3/8-o(1).
```

This is stronger than the binary-cut formulation: the witness centralizes
the entire raw finite packet.  The remaining atlas theorem is correspondingly
small—cross-root multiplication must make every such coordinate commutant
unitary asymptotically commute with `F_n`.

## 2. Why this does not automatically create a radical collision

The one-number Kun--Thom criterion uses a different commutant.  Its lamp must
centralize an exhausting family from the infinite Kazhdan subgroup `Gamma`,
not one fixed finite packet.  If `A_n` is contained in the algebra `B_n`
generated by a larger subgroup window, orthogonal-projection monotonicity
only gives

```text
dist_2(F_n,B_n) <= dist_2(F_n,A_n).
```

The fixed `3/16` distance from `A_n` therefore supplies no positive lower
bound at the `Gamma` scale.  The direction of monotonicity is exactly wrong
for the proposed splice.

There is also a purely algebraic obstruction to replacing `Gamma` by `K`.
For a subgroup `K<G`, write

```text
P_K={g in G : g K g^(-1) <= K}.
```

If `K` is finite and `g in P_K`, conjugation preserves cardinality, so the
displayed inclusion is equality.  Hence

```text
P_K=N_G(K).
```

If `K` were infranormal in the Kun--Thom sense, `P_K` would generate `G`.
But its normalizer is already a subgroup, so this would force
`N_G(K)=G`; equivalently, `K` would be normal.  Therefore:

> A finite subgroup cannot be both nonnormal and infranormal in the
> Kun--Thom sense.

The nonnormal infranormal hypothesis is what turns centralizer information
into a nontrivial sofic-radical word.  The sixteen-element Pauli packet can
organize finite multiplicities, but it cannot itself serve as that subgroup.
