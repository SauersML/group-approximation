# Property `(T)` gives the uniform real-linear degree-one repair gap

Date: 2026-08-11

## 1. Purpose

The exact finite `SL_3(Z)` charts in
`experiments/sl3_degree_one_spectrum_results.md` exhibit a stable-looking
bottom for the real degree-one Hodge operator.  No character-block
diagonalization is needed to prove that qualitative phenomenon.  It follows
abstractly from property `(T)`, finite presentability, and a Hilbert
ultraproduct argument.

This resolves only the **real-linear, exact-action** part of the phase repair
problem.  It does not prove the circle-valued stability condition `(CS1)`:
circle cocycles can carry stabilizer holonomy and integral winding data, and
the sofic charts needed in the general problem are only approximately
multiplicative.

## 2. Presentation cochains

Fix a finite presentation

`G=<S | R>`                                                `(PTG1)`

and fixed positive weights on its finitely many vertices, generator edges,
and relator cells.  For a unitary representation `pi:G->U(H)`, let

`C^0(pi) --d0_pi--> C^1(pi) --d1_pi--> C^2(pi)`            `(PTG2)`

be the cellular cochain complex of the presentation with coefficients in
`pi`.  Thus `C^1(pi)` is one copy of `H` for every oriented generator and
`d1_pi` is the finite Fox-derivative matrix of the relators.  The identity

`d1_pi d0_pi=0`                                           `(PTG3)`

holds for every exact representation.

The kernel of `d1_pi` is the space `Z^1(G,pi)` of unitary `1`-cocycles.  A
choice of values on `S` extends uniquely to a cocycle of the free group; the
finite equations `d1_pi b=0` say that it vanishes on the defining relators,
and hence on their normal closure.

## 3. Uniform coexact gap

**Theorem 1 (property-`(T)` presentation gap).**  Suppose `G` has property
`(T)`.  There exists a constant `c=c(G,S,R)>0` such that for every Hilbert
space `H`, every unitary representation `pi:G->U(H)`, and every
`b in C^1(pi)`,

`dist(b,Z^1(G,pi)) <= c^(-1)||d1_pi b||`.                `(PTG4)`

Equivalently, for every `b` perpendicular to `ker(d1_pi)`,

`||d1_pi b|| >= c||b||`.                                `(PTG5)`

**Proof.**  If no uniform `c` existed, there would be unitary
representations `pi_n:G->U(H_n)` and unit cochains

`b_n in ker(d1_(pi_n))^perp`, `||d1_(pi_n)b_n||->0`.     `(PTG6)`

Fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter and form the Hilbert ultraproduct

`H_omega=product_omega H_n`.                             `(PTG7)`

The coordinatewise unitaries define an exact unitary representation
`pi_omega` of `G`.  Because the matrices in `(PTG2)` have fixed finite size
and fixed group-ring entries, they commute with passage to the Hilbert
ultraproduct.  Hence `b=[b_n]` has norm one and

`d1_(pi_omega)b=0`.                                     `(PTG8)`

Property `(T)` is equivalent to the Delorme--Guichardet vanishing theorem

`H^1(G,pi)=0`                                           `(PTG9)`

for every unitary representation `pi`.  Apply it to the separable invariant
subspace generated by `b` if `H_omega` is nonseparable.  There is a
`v in C^0(pi_omega)` such that

`b=d0_(pi_omega)v`.                                    `(PTG10)`

Represent `v` by a bounded sequence `(v_n)`.  Then

`d0_(pi_omega)v=[d0_(pi_n)v_n]`,                       `(PTG11)`

and every `d0_(pi_n)v_n` belongs to `ker(d1_(pi_n))` by `(PTG3)`.  The
orthogonality in `(PTG6)` therefore gives

`||b_n-d0_(pi_n)v_n||^2`
` =||b_n||^2+||d0_(pi_n)v_n||^2 >=1`                   `(PTG12)`

for every `n`.  But `(PTG10)--(PTG11)` say that the left side tends to zero
along `omega`, a contradiction.  This proves `(PTG5)`.  Orthogonal
projection onto the kernel gives `(PTG4)`.  End proof.

The argument is qualitative and does not produce a numerical value of `c`.
It proves exactly what the numerical spectra were testing: the least
positive singular value cannot tend to zero along any family of exact
unitary representations.

## 4. Uniform degree-one Hodge gap

Property `(T)` also supplies a constant `c_0>0` such that `d0_pi` has least
positive singular value at least `c_0`, uniformly over `pi`.  Since
`H^1(G,pi)=0`,

`ker(d1_pi)=im(d0_pi)`.                                 `(PTG13)`

The orthogonal decomposition

`C^1(pi)=im(d0_pi) directSum ker(d1_pi)^perp`           `(PTG14)`

reduces the Hodge operator

`Delta_(1,pi)=d1_pi^*d1_pi+d0_pi d0_pi^*`.             `(PTG15)`

On the first summand the second term has gap `c_0^2`; on the second summand
the first term has gap `c^2` by Theorem 1.  Therefore:

**Corollary 2.**  For the fixed presentation `(PTG1)`,

`Delta_(1,pi) >= min(c_0^2,c^2) 1`                     `(PTG16)`

for every unitary representation `pi`.

In particular, apply this to the permutation representations of `SL_3(Z)`
on

`X_p=F_p^3-{0}`.                                       `(PTG17)`

The coexact degree-one bottom measured in
`sl3_degree_one_spectrum_results.md` has a positive lower bound independent
of `p`.  The scalar-character decomposition remains useful for explicit
constants, but it is not needed for existence of the gap.

## 5. Exact scope: why this does not prove `(CS1)`

Three distinctions are load-bearing.

1. **Real linear versus circle valued.**  Theorem 1 repairs an almost real
   cocycle to a real cocycle.  A circle-valued cocycle may carry nontrivial
   stabilizer characters, and choosing logarithms introduces integral
   `2`-carries.  Delorme--Guichardet vanishing does not remove those sectors.
2. **Exact versus approximate actions.**  The proof uses exact identities
   `d1_(pi_n)d0_(pi_n)=0` and an exact representation on the Hilbert
   ultraproduct.  Normalized-HS approximate permutation charts need not
   define a representation on the full Hilbert ultraproduct: their
   multiplication-error operators can have norm two on small subspaces.
3. **Existential gap versus explicit certificate.**  The ultraproduct proof
   gives no computable lower bound.  Character blocks or interval arithmetic
   can still certify a numerical constant for a particular finite family,
   but that is no longer the logical gate for qualitative linear repair.

Thus the proof-producing successor to the `SL_3` experiment is not a larger
spectral table.  The unresolved work is to control the nonlinear integral
carry while retaining the prescribed relatively-Haar joining, or to pass
from approximate charts to selected exact-action pieces without an
amenability boundary.  Those are precisely the two gaps left in
`POPA_FACTOR_MODEL_LIFTING.md` and
`PHASE_UNFOLDING_AMENABILITY_BARRIER.md`.

## Reference

The only external input is the Delorme--Guichardet characterization of
property `(T)`: a countable discrete property-`(T)` group has vanishing
first cohomology for every unitary representation.  The uniform presentation
gap above is proved directly from that input by ultraproduct compactness.
See Bekka--de la Harpe--Valette, *Kazhdan's Property (T)*, Theorem 2.12.4,
for the Delorme--Guichardet theorem and its property-`(T)` formulation.
