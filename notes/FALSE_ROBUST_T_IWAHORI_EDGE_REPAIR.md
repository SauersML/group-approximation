# Robust spectral gap does not repair the Iwahori edge

Date: 2026-08-13

## Outcome

The robust-property-`(T)` theorem of Dogon--Vigdorovich does not turn an
almost-compatible Iwahori pair into a compatible pair. There are two
independent obstructions.

1. Their effective Theorem 7.10 is an **operator-norm** assertion for a
   property-`(T)` group. The Iwahori amalgam

   ```text
   Gamma = SL_2(Z[1/2])
         = SL_2(Z) *_(B_+ ~= B_-) SL_2(Z)
   ```

   is used through property `(T;FD)`, not property `(T)`, and its matrix
   almost representations are controlled only in normalized
   Hilbert--Schmidt norm.

2. The Hilbert--Schmidt robust statement controls the trace of the
   *middle* spectral projection of the almost Laplacian. It neither makes
   the small spectrum equal to zero nor produces an exact invariant vector.
   Replacing a spectral interval `[0,o(1)]` by an actual kernel is a
   stability conclusion, and is precisely what Question 1.4 asks one to
   prove.

The most tempting adjoint application is actually vacuous: its distinguished
scalar vector is fixed before any edge correction is made.

## 1. The edge defect as an approximate intertwiner

Let

```text
alpha,beta : K=SL_2(Z) -> U(d)
```

be exact representations and suppose

```text
max_(s in S_+) ||alpha(s)-beta(sigma(s))||_(2,d) <= delta.  (RIE1)
```

On the normalized Hilbert space `HS_d=M_d(C)`, the formula

```text
rho_B(s)(T)=alpha(s) T beta(sigma(s))^*                  (RIE2)
```

is an **exact** unitary representation of `B_+`. The normalized identity
vector `1_d` satisfies

```text
||rho_B(s)(1_d)-1_d||_(2,d)
  = ||alpha(s)-beta(sigma(s))||_(2,d).                  (RIE3)
```

Thus edge repair would follow if `(RIE3)` forced `1_d` to lie close to a
unitary element of the exact intertwiner space

```text
Inv(rho_B)
 = {T : alpha(s)T=T beta(sigma(s)) for every s in B_+}. (RIE4)
```

But `B_+` is virtually free and has no property `(T)`. Indeed, in either
central sector its representation theory is that of

```text
Z * C_2.                                                (RIE5)
```

Consequently there is no uniform spectral gap for `(RIE2)`. Almost
invariant identity vectors need not be close to `(RIE4)` by an argument
internal to the edge group.

## 2. Why the ambient robust theorem cannot be substituted

The two vertex representations do define a normalized-HS asymptotic
representation of `Gamma`. Applying the adjoint functor is legitimate:

```text
||Ad(U)-Ad(V)||_(2,d^2) <= sqrt(2)||U-V||_(2,d).         (RIE6)
```

However, on each vertex the adjoint action is

```text
Ad(alpha(g))(T)=alpha(g)T alpha(g)^*,
Ad(beta(g))(T)=beta(g)T beta(g)^*.                       (RIE7)
```

Both sides of `(RIE7)` fix `1_d` **exactly**, independently of `(RIE1)`.
The scalar line therefore gives an exact zero eigenvector for the glued
almost Laplacian even when the two edge restrictions have no nonzero
intertwiner. Robust spectral gap around this already-present scalar line
contains no information about `(RIE4)`.

One might instead try to use the mixed action `(RIE2)` on the whole
amalgam. This would require an isomorphism of the two copies of `K`
extending

```text
sigma : B_+ -> B_-.                                     (RIE8)
```

No such extension exists: `(RIE8)` is the strict Iwahori commensuration
implemented by `diag(sqrt(2),1/sqrt(2))`, not an automorphism of
`SL_2(Z)`. Hence the mixed action is an exact representation only of the
edge and cannot be fed into an ambient spectral-gap theorem. Constructing
the missing ambient action is algebraically equivalent to repairing the
edge.

## 3. What the cited robust conclusions actually give

For an asymptotic representation `pi_n` into finite tracial algebras,
property `(T;FD)_rob` gives constants `lambda>0` such that, for each fixed
`a>0`,

```text
tr(1_[a,lambda-a](pi_n(Delta))) -> 0.                   (RIE9)
```

This permits an arbitrarily large spectral projection in `[0,a_n]`, where
`a_n->0`, with no kernel at all. Turning that projection into the kernel of
an exact representation is not a consequence of `(RIE9)`. The
operator-norm Theorem 7.10 similarly places the small spectrum in
`[0,C epsilon]`; it does not make it zero.

Therefore the implication

```text
robust spectral gap + small edge defect
    => exact near-unitary edge intertwiner
```

would be a new stability theorem, not an application of the existing
spectral-gap result.

## 4. Explicit central-sector coordinates

The failure can be seen without the adjoint language. Modulo the central
sign, write

```text
PSL_2(Z)=<a,b | a^2=b^3=1>.                              (RIE10)
```

With `u=ab` and `l=ab^2`, the upper Iwahori subgroup is generated by

```text
u=ab,
e=u l^(-2)=(ab^2)a(ab^2)^(-1),                           (RIE11)
```

and the lower image is generated by

```text
u^2=(ab)^2,
e_-=u^2 l^(-1)=(ab)a(ab)^(-1).                           (RIE12)
```

Thus a vertex representation is an arbitrary pair `(A,B)` consisting of an
involution and an order-three unitary, and the compatibility problem asks
for simultaneous perturbation so that the two pairs of edge coordinates
in `(RIE11)--(RIE12)` agree. High-dimensional irreducible mass is exactly
the regime in which the relative position of the spectral flags of `A` and
`B` carries unbounded information. Neither `(RIE6)` nor `(RIE9)` controls
that relative position.

## 5. Consequence for the research program

The remaining positive theorem must act directly on the mixed edge
representation `(RIE2)` or on the flag-coordinate map
`(RIE11)--(RIE12)`. A valid completion would have to prove one of:

1. a dimension-free kernel-opening theorem for mixed Iwahori intertwiners;
2. a dimension-free metric-regularity theorem for the two restriction maps
   in `(RIE11)--(RIE12)`; or
3. a different nonhyperlinearity mechanism not passing through Iwahori
   matching.

Ambient adjoint spectral gap alone proves none of these.
