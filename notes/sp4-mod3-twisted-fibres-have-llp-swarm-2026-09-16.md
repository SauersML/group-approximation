## What was proved (all in the artifact, complete)

1. **Theorem A (untwisting).**  Let `H` be one of:
   - `S = SL_2(Z) x SL_2(Z)` (block diagonal);
   - `P_S(Z) = Sym_2(Z) x| GL_2(Z)`;
   - `P_K(Z) = N(Z) x| ({+-1} x SL_2(Z))`.

   Then some character `phi : p^{-1}(H) -> T` has `phi(z) = omega`.  The
   `Z/3`-extension splits over `P_S(Z)`, and over `F x F'` for free `F, F'`
   in the two blocks.  Ingredients:
   - covering-space lemmas (sections 2.1--2.3): central kernel, homomorphic
     sections over connected subgroups with trivial `pi_1` map, and commuting
     lifts;
   - Lemma 3.2P: a full proof of `SL_2(Z) = <A,B | A^4, A^2 B^{-3}>`, by
     the Euclidean algorithm and ping-pong on the irrationals;
   - Lemma 3.2: every central `Z/3`-extension of `SL_2(Z)` carries a
     character with `z -> omega`;
   - Siegel: `pi_1(P_S(R)_0) = Z` is generated by `m(R_theta)`, which dies
     in `Sp_4(R)` through `U(2) -> SU(2) = S^3`;
   - Klingen: the Levi decomposition, and equivariance of the section over
     `N(R) = R^3`.
2. **Theorem B (calibration).**  For every subgroup `K` of such an `H`,
   `C*(K)` embeds unitally in `A_chi` as the range of a ucp conditional
   expectation.  This holds for both faithful `chi`.  So (TF3) implies LLP
   of `C*(K)`.  Ingredients:
   - the corner identification (Lemma 7.1);
   - the induced-representation expectation (Lemma 7.2);
   - untwisting with `phi` or `phi^2` (Lemma 7.3);
   - LLP descent along ucp left inverses (Lemma 7.4).

   No permanence theorem is imported.
3. **Instances.**  `C*(F_2 x F_2)`, which needs only Sanov and the free-group
   splitting, not Lemma 3.2P.  Also `C*(SL_2(Z)^2)`, `C*(P_S(Z))`,
   `C*(P_K(Z))`, and `C*(E)` for the Klingen extension `E = N(Z) x| S_2(Z)`
   (remark 9.5).
4. **Remark 9.3.**  By (DTC2), no finite-index subgroup untwists `alpha`.
   Inducing a character would give a finite-dimensional representation with
   `rho(z) = omega`.

## Approaches tried and where each dies

1. **Positive proof of (TF3).**  Dies on Theorem B: it must decide the LLP
   half of FFW Q1.6.  I found no 2024--2026 source doing so (literature
   below).
2. **Refutation through the subgroup fence with an untwisted failure.**
   Theorem B turns any LLP failure of a subgroup algebra of `S`, `P_S(Z)` or
   `P_K(Z)` into a refutation.  The known failures cannot be used:
   - ISW `SL_n(Z)`, `n >= 3`: there is no such subgroup at genus two.  The
     untwisted node already records that the claim lives at `g = 2` only.
   - ISW `Z^2 x| F` with the standard action (F finite-index free):
     `Sp_4(Z)` has no such subgroup at all.  **Sketch, not landed as a
     node.**
     - Let `A` be the Zariski closure of the image of `Z^2`, with
       `A = A_s x A_u`.
     - A finite-index subgroup of `F` centralizes `A_s`, by rigidity of
       diagonalizable groups.  So a finite-index `F`-stable lattice
       `Lambda` maps into `A_u`.
     - `log` makes `Lie(A_u) = C (x) Lambda`, the standard 2-dimensional
       module.
     - By Borel--Tits, `N(A_u)` lies in a parabolic `P` with
       `A_u <= R_u(P)`.
       - Borel: the Levi is a torus, so the image in `GL(Lie A_u)` is
         solvable, which is impossible.
       - Klingen: a 2-dimensional abelian subalgebra of the Heisenberg
         algebra contains the centre, which is an invariant line.
       - Siegel: the Levi image would contain `SL_2`, acting irreducibly on
         `Sym^2`.

     This depends on Borel--Tits, which was not fetched.  So that fence is
     empty in `Sp_4(Z)`, independently of Theorem B.
   - The Klingen rational fibres `A_t` (`klingen-rational-twisted-fibres-fail-llp`,
     another agent's node, not landed when I checked) are quotients of
     `C*(E)`, not subalgebras.  Quotients do not inherit
     the LLP.
   - Conclusion: refutation along this lane needs a new LLP failure for an
     untwisted group algebra, e.g. `F_2 x F_2`.
3. **Twisted ISW cohomological obstruction.**  On the parabolic radicals it
   is already dead (`isw-scalar-cocycle-obstruction-dies-on-sp4-parabolic-radicals`,
   `klingen-center-kills-isw-condition-one`).  Theorem A shows the multiplier
   itself untwists there, so twisting adds nothing new on these subgroups.
4. **Max-tensor diagonal trick.**
   - `g -> v_g (x) v'_g` untwists `alpha (x) alpha^2`, so it relates
     `C*(Sp_4(Z))` to `A_chi1 (x)_max A_chi2`.
   - Fell absorption `v_g -> u_g (x) v_g` relates `A_chi` to
     `C*(Sp_4(Z)) (x)_max A_chi`.
   - Transferring LLP along these needs LLP of max tensor products, which is
     not available.  Dies there.
5. **Ext reformulation.**  Enders--Shulman (abstract): for a class including
   `C*(F_n x F_n)`, LLP is equivalent to Ext being a group.  With Theorem B,
   (TF3) would make `Ext(C*(F_2 x F_2))` a group.  This is a reformulation
   with low leverage, not used.
6. **Finite-index untwisting.**  Impossible (remark 9.3 and DTC2).  Every
   untwisting subgroup has infinite index.
7. **Transfer to kill the 3-part of `H^2(SL_2(Z); T)`.**
   - The plan was restriction to a finite-index subgroup of index prime to
     three, followed by corestriction.
   - Torsion-free finite-index subgroups of `SL_2(Z)` have index divisible
     by 12, hence by 3, and the free ones are where the class dies
     trivially.
   - Dies there.  Replaced by the direct comparison-group argument
     (Lemma 3.2), which uses the presentation.
8. **(TF3) implies empty opnorm sectors.**  Already follows from existing
   nodes, so no new node.

## Side remarks (not landed)

- **Non-exactness of `A_chi`.**  `A_chi` contains `C*(F_2)` with a
  conditional expectation, so it is not exact.  This uses the literature
  facts that full `C*(F_2)` is not exact and that exactness passes to
  subalgebras; neither was re-verified.
- **Labelling slip in two nodes.**
  `isw-scalar-cocycle-obstruction-dies-on-sp4-parabolic-radicals` and
  `klingen-center-kills-isw-condition-one` call the integral Klingen radical
  "the integer Heisenberg group".
  - With the law `n(x,y,u) n(x',y',u') = n(x+x', y+y', u+u'+xy'-x'y)`, the
    commutator of the generators is `n(0,0,2)`.
  - So `N(Z)^ab = Z^2 x Z/2`, and `N(Z)` is isomorphic to the index-two
    subgroup `{a even}` of `H_3(Z)`, via `n(x,y,u) -> (2x, y, u + xy)`.  It
    is not `H_3(Z)` itself.
  - Rationally nothing changes, and `H_2 = Z^2` in both cases.  Their
    conclusions are probably unaffected, but a referee may want the wording
    fixed.
- **Attribution slip.**  `klingen-rational-twisted-fibres-fail-llp` (unlanded)
  credits
  "Farah--Fritz--Winter arXiv:2603.18456v2".  Everywhere else in the graph
  that arXiv number is Fournier-Facio--Willett.  This looks like a wrong
  expansion of "FFW".
- **Remark 9.5.**  (TF3) implies LLP of `C*(E)`.  Granting Corollary 2.2 of
  `research/artifacts/klingen-central-quotient-2026-09-16.md` (unlanded,
  another agent's in-flight work; the artifact no longer cites it by path or
  id, and the claim node's `distinct_from` entry for it was dropped because
  check.sh rejects unlanded ids), (TF3) alone
  then forces its constraints: every rational central quotient
  `C*(E) -> A_t` is min-inexact against `B(l^2)`.  There is no clash with
  `A_t` failing the LLP.
- **Metaplectic half shift.**  Possibly the calibration transfers to the
  `theta + 1/2` fibre by `metaplectic-half-shift-preserves-twisted-fibre-llp`.
  That needs `alpha` to be cohomologous to `exp(2 pi i b/3)` for the
  Meyer--Maslov cocycle `b`, which I did not check.
