# The one-orbit edge shear is spatial in a Connes-embeddable algebra

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

The one-orbit clutching automorphism from
`FALSE_COINDUCED_PARITY_MODEL.md` has a much simpler operator-algebraic
form after passing to the negative central corner.  It is exactly the dual
action of an index-two grading.  Its crossed product is not mysterious:
it is a two-by-two matrix algebra over the even corner.

More precisely, let `K` be a countable group with a nontrivial central
involution `z`, let

```text
epsilon:K->F_2                                             (EDS1)
```

be a surjective homomorphism satisfying `epsilon(z)=0`, and define

```text
theta(k)=z^(epsilon(k)) k.                                (EDS2)
```

Assume that `(EDS2)` is the edge shear under consideration.  Put

```text
p=(1-u_z)/2 in L(K),
P=pL(K),
P_0=pL(ker(epsilon)).                                    (EDS3)
```

The projection `p` is the identity of the negative corner.  The group
automorphism `theta` preserves `p` and induces

```text
alpha(pu_k)=(-1)^(epsilon(k))pu_k.                       (EDS4)
```

Then

```text
boxed:  P crossed_alpha C_2  isomorphic  M_2(P_0).        (EDS5)
```

Consequently, if `ker(epsilon)` is hyperlinear, there are a
trace-preserving embedding

```text
pi:P->R^omega                                             (EDS6)
```

and a self-adjoint unitary `V in R^omega` such that

```text
V pi(x) V=pi(alpha(x)),       x in P.                    (EDS7)
```

For the explicit coinduced-parity edge, the kernel of `epsilon` is a
subgroup of a residually finite vertex group.  It is therefore residually
finite and hyperlinear.  Thus the two negative edge embeddings are exactly
unitarily conjugate inside a Connes-embeddable tracial algebra.

This closes the **edge-only** compatibility problem.  It does not by itself
prove FALSE: the particular embedding `(EDS6)` must still be extended to
one adjacent vertex von Neumann algebra.  A second vertex is unnecessary.
Indeed, `FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_SPATIALIZATION_CERTIFICATE.md` proves that any
Connes-embeddable finite algebra containing one faithful negative vertex
corner and an edge implementer already contains a hyperlinear nonsofic
group.  Equivalently, it is enough to prove Connes embeddability of the
one-sided reduced amalgam generated by a vertex and the edge implementer.
No further projective or Schur obstruction lives on the edge itself; all
remaining difficulty is this one-sided relative-extension obstruction.

## 2. General index-two duality theorem

**Theorem 1 (negative central shear duality).**  Let `K,z,epsilon,theta`
satisfy `(EDS1)--(EDS2)`, with `z` central of order two, `epsilon`
surjective, and `epsilon(z)=0`.  Let `alpha` be the automorphism of the negative corner
`P` induced by `theta`.  Then `(EDS4)--(EDS5)` hold.  Under `(EDS5)`, the
copy of `P` is the canonical graded algebra and the canonical crossed-product
unitary implements `alpha`.

**Proof.**  Centrality of `z` and the homomorphism identity for `epsilon`
show that `theta` is a homomorphism.  Moreover
`epsilon(theta(k))=epsilon(k)` because `epsilon(z)=0`, so `theta^2=id`.
Thus it is an automorphism.  Since `z` is central, `p` is a central
projection.  In its
corner one has

```text
pu_z=-p.                                                 (EDS8)
```

Therefore

```text
p u_(theta(k))
 =p u_z^(epsilon(k))u_k
 =(-1)^(epsilon(k))p u_k,                               (EDS9)
```

which proves `(EDS4)`.  Put `N=ker(epsilon)`.  The Fourier basis of `P`
splits orthogonally as

```text
P=P_0 directSum P_1,
P_1=closure(span{pu_k:epsilon(k)=1}).                  (EDS10)
```

The automorphism `alpha` is `+1` on `P_0` and `-1` on `P_1`; in
particular

```text
P^alpha=P_0.                                            (EDS11)
```

Let

```text
B=P crossed_alpha C_2                                  (EDS12)
```

and let `v=v^*=v^(-1)` be its canonical implementing unitary.  Define

```text
e=(1+v)/2.                                              (EDS13)
```

For `x=x_0+x_1` with `x_i in P_i`, the relations

```text
v x_0=x_0 v,       v x_1=-x_1 v                       (EDS14)
```

give

```text
e x e=x_0 e.                                           (EDS15)
```

The same calculation for `xv` shows

```text
eBe=P_0e,                                               (EDS16)
```

and `x_0 |-> x_0e` is a trace-preserving isomorphism after normalizing
the corner trace.

Choose `t in K` with `epsilon(t)=1` and put `q=pu_t in P`.  This is a
unitary in the unital algebra `P`, and `alpha(q)=-q`.  Hence

```text
qvq^*=-v,
qeq^*=1-e.                                              (EDS17)
```

Thus `e` and `1-e` are Murray--von Neumann equivalent in `B`.  They sum to
one, so `e` is full and the usual two-equivalent-corners matrix
decomposition gives

```text
B isomorphic M_2(eBe) isomorphic M_2(P_0).              (EDS18)
```

This proves `(EDS5)`.  The canonical unitary `v` gives the asserted
implementation.  End proof.

The argument is the finite, order-two form of crossed-product duality, but
the corner calculation above makes all hypotheses and the exact negative
central sector explicit.

## 3. Connes-embeddable spatialization

**Corollary 2.**  If `N=ker(epsilon)` is hyperlinear, then `(EDS6)--(EDS7)`
hold.

**Proof.**  Hyperlinearity of `N` is equivalent to Connes embeddability of
`L(N)`.  The algebra `P_0=pL(N)` is a central corner of `L(N)`, so it is
Connes embeddable.  Equation `(EDS5)` makes `B` Connes embeddable as well.
Choose a trace-preserving embedding `B->R^omega`, restrict it to `P`, and
take `V` to be the image of the canonical crossed-product unitary `v`.
Then `(EDS7)` is the crossed-product relation.  End proof.

There is no amenability assumption here.  In particular this result is not
an application of uniqueness of embeddings of amenable algebras: the edge
may contain a diffuse property-`(T)` group algebra.  The implementer is
obtained by adjoining the finite dual action, whose whole crossed product is
identified by `(EDS5)`.

## 4. Application to the one-orbit clutching edge

Use `(CPM37)--(CPM45)` from
`FALSE_COINDUCED_PARITY_MODEL.md`.  Thus

```text
K=A_s semidirect Gamma                                  (EDS19)
```

is the common edge group, `z` is its central involution, and after choosing
the one-orbit clutching form there is a `Gamma`-invariant homomorphism

```text
epsilon:K->F_2,
epsilon(c,g)=sum_(y in O_0)c_y.                        (EDS20)
```

The two edge embeddings differ by

```text
theta(c,g)=z^(epsilon(c,g))(c,g).                      (EDS21)
```

The invariance of the orbit `O_0` makes `epsilon` a group homomorphism,
and `epsilon(z)=0`.  Hence Theorem 1 applies.

Each vertex preimage `J_i` is residually finite by `(CPM34)--(CPM35)`.
The even edge group

```text
N=ker(epsilon)<=K<=J_i                                 (EDS22)
```

is therefore residually finite.  In particular it is hyperlinear, so
Corollary 2 gives the exact Connes-embeddable spatialization of the edge
shear.

It is worth separating three levels which had previously been conflated.

1. **Finite equivariant quotients.**  These kill `z`; Proposition 3 of
   `FALSE_MACKEY_SPLIT_COCYCLE.md` rules them out.
2. **The negative edge von Neumann algebra.**  The shear is exactly spatial
   in a Connes-embeddable over-algebra by `(EDS5)--(EDS7)`.
3. **One external vertex extension.**  It remains unknown whether the edge
   embedding from item 2 extends to one faithful negative vertex corner in
   a Connes-embeddable finite algebra.  This already proves FALSE by
   `FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_SPATIALIZATION_CERTIFICATE.md`.  For the canonical
   reduced amalgam, however, the edge implementer regenerates the second
   vertex: `FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_GROUP_CORNER_AND_COVER.md` identifies it with a
   finite crossed product of the canonical twisted double.  Thus the two
   canonical CE questions are equivalent rather than independent.

Thus finite-quotient invisibility does not imply an intrinsic tracial
obstruction on the edge.  The missing theorem is an extension theorem for
the inclusions

```text
P subset pL(J_i),                                       (EDS23)
```

with the prescribed unitary `(EDS7)`.  Proving one such relative extension
would finish the coinduced-parity FALSE route; disproving it would require
an invariant of the inclusions, not merely of `P` and `alpha`.

## 5. The even edge is hyperlinear but not co-hyperlinear in a vertex

The spatialization above might suggest applying the group-theoretic
relative-embeddability theorem of Gao--Junge--Gao to the inclusion
`N<J_i`.  The strict compressor rules this out.

**Theorem 3 (compressor obstruction for intermediate overgroups).**  Let
`Gamma<=N<=J`, assume `Gamma` has property `(T)`, and suppose there are
`t in J` and `gamma in Gamma` such that

```text
t Gamma t^(-1)<=Gamma,
h=t^(-1)gamma t notin N.                               (EDS24)
```

Then `N` is not co-hyperlinear in `J` in the sense of
Gao--Junge--Gao.

**Proof.**  Suppose decreasing pairs `(J_i,H_i)` witness
co-hyperlinearity:

```text
intersection_i J_i=N,
H_i<=J_i,       H_i normal J,
J/H_i hyperlinear,       J_i/H_i amenable.            (EDS25)
```

Since `Gamma<=N<=J_i`, the image

```text
Gamma H_i/H_i <= J_i/H_i                              (EDS26)
```

is amenable.  It is also a quotient of the property-`(T)` group `Gamma`,
so it is finite.  In `J/H_i`, the first relation in `(EDS24)` gives

```text
q_i(t)q_i(Gamma)q_i(t)^(-1)<=q_i(Gamma).              (EDS27)
```

Conjugation is injective and the group on the right is finite, so the
inclusion is equality.  Conjugating back gives

```text
q_i(h) in q_i(Gamma),
h in Gamma H_i<=J_i.                                  (EDS28)
```

This holds for every `i`, whence `h in intersection_i J_i=N`, contrary
to `(EDS24)`.  End proof.

For the Kun--Thom vertex, take the original strict compressor `t` and
choose `gamma` with `h=t^(-1)gamma t notin Gamma`.  Embed these as zero-lamp
elements of `J_i`.  The even edge `N=ker(epsilon)` lies inside the common
edge, whose acting-group coordinate is `Gamma`; hence the zero-lamp element
`h` is not in `N`.  Theorem 3 applies.

We therefore have the sharp combination

```text
N is residually finite and hyperlinear,
P crossed_alpha C_2 is Connes embeddable,
N is not co-hyperlinear in J_i.                       (EDS29)
```

Thus the finite dual-action construction solves spatialization but not
relative extension.  Gao--Junge--Gao's group-theoretic sufficient package
cannot extend `(EDS6)` across either vertex; a positive proof must use a
genuinely operator-algebraic relative embedding.

## 6. A reusable finite-cyclic variant

The same argument extends to a central element `z` of order `m` and a
homomorphism `epsilon:K->Z/m`, after selecting a faithful central character
corner.  The induced shear is the dual `Z/m`-action, and finite-group
crossed-product duality gives

```text
P crossed Z/m  isomorphic M_m(P_0),                   (EDS30)
```

where `P_0` is the degree-zero corner.  The order-two case is the exact
one needed by the present construction and avoids all cocycle notation.
