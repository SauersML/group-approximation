# Finite stabilizers cannot groupify a diffuse relation algebra

Date: 2026-08-14

## 1. Outcome

Let `R` be an ergodic p.m.p. equivalence relation on a nonatomic standard
probability space `(X,mu)`, put

```text
A=L_infinity(X),                 M=L(R),               (FSG1)
```

and let `Lambda <= [R]` be a countable subgroup whose orbit relation is `R`.
Write `u_g in M` for its canonical full-group unitaries. The essentially-free
case was closed in `FALSE_FULL_GROUP_DENSITY_GROUPIFICATION.md`: then the
Cartan expectation of `W*(u_Lambda)` is scalar.

Allowing finite point stabilizers does not help.

**Theorem 1 (finite-stabilizer no-go).** If

```text
Stab_Lambda(x) is finite for almost every x,            (FSG2)
```

then there is a finite-dimensional subalgebra `B <= A` such that

```text
E_A(W*(u_g:g in Lambda)) <= B.                          (FSG3)
```

In particular

```text
W*(u_g:g in Lambda) != L(R).                            (FSG4)
```

Thus a canonical full-group groupification of a diffuse non-CE relation
cannot use trivial, uniformly finite, or arbitrarily large finite
stabilizers. Any surviving stabilizer-based route must use infinite
stabilizers, and weak containment from amenability alone does not provide the
normal trace-preserving embedding needed for groupification.

## 2. An ergodic finite-subgroup IRS has finite support

The stabilizer map

```text
s:X -> Sub(Lambda),       s(x)=Stab_Lambda(x),          (FSG5)
```

is equivariant for the action on `X` and conjugation on the subgroup space.
Its pushforward `nu=s_*mu` is therefore an ergodic invariant random subgroup.

The set

```text
Sub_fin(Lambda)={H<=Lambda: H is finite}                (FSG6)
```

is countable: it is a subset of the finite subsets of the countable group
`Lambda`. By `(FSG2)`, `nu` is supported on this countable invariant set.
Every probability measure on a countable set has an atom. Choose an atom
`H`. Invariance makes every conjugate of `H` an atom of the same mass. The
conjugacy orbit of `H` is an invariant set of positive measure, hence has
measure one by ergodicity. Equal positive atom masses force that orbit to be
finite.

Consequently there are finite subgroups

```text
H_1,...,H_r <= Lambda                                  (FSG7)
```

forming one finite conjugacy orbit such that

```text
Stab_Lambda(x) in {H_1,...,H_r} almost everywhere.     (FSG8)
```

Let `B` be the finite-dimensional algebra generated by the fibers of the
map in `(FSG8)`.

## 3. The Cartan expectation cannot escape `B`

For every `g in Lambda`, the standard groupoid formula gives

```text
E_A(u_g)=1_(Fix(g))
        =1_({x:g in Stab_Lambda(x)}).                   (FSG9)
```

Equation `(FSG8)` shows that every function in `(FSG9)` lies in `B`.
Therefore

```text
E_A(C[Lambda]) <= B.                                   (FSG10)
```

The conditional expectation is normal, and a finite-dimensional subspace is
closed in every relevant operator topology. Taking the von Neumann closure
in `(FSG10)` proves `(FSG3)`.

If `W*(u_Lambda)=M`, then for every `a in A` we would have

```text
a=E_A(a) in B.                                         (FSG11)
```

This would put the diffuse algebra `A` inside the finite-dimensional algebra
`B`, a contradiction. This proves `(FSG4)`.

## 4. Why amenable infinite stabilizers are a different gate

For an amenable subgroup `H<=Lambda`, the quasi-regular representation

```text
lambda_(Lambda/H) weakly_contained lambda_Lambda.       (FSG12)
```

Thus an action with amenable stabilizers suggests weak containment of its
natural groupoid representation in the regular representation. But weak
containment only yields a representation of the reduced group C-star
algebra. It does not produce a normal embedding of the resulting GNS von
Neumann algebra into `L(Lambda)`.

That distinction is load bearing. A hyperlinear group may carry non-CE
finite-factor characters; the Chifan--Drimbe--Ioana embedding-universality
theorem supplies exactly such characters on familiar hyperlinear groups.
Promoting `(FSG12)` to normal group-factor containment would be another form
of the regular-to-natural trace-selection theorem.

The full-group lane is therefore narrowed to one of two genuinely new
possibilities:

1. construct a W*-generating subgroup with infinite stabilizers and prove a
   normal, not merely C-star weak, regular-containment theorem; or
2. embed the non-CE relation algebra into a group factor by operators other
   than the canonical full-group unitaries.

Finite stabilizers provide neither possibility.

## Relation to the active endpoint

This no-go does not prove a nonhyperlinear group. It removes the only obvious
intermediate case between the essentially-free obstruction and the
infinite-stabilizer trace-selection problem. The Pauli/HNN
coordinate-commutant gate and the perfect-completeness LCS gate remain the
two direct constructive endpoints.
