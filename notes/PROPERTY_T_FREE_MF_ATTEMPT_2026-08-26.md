## Exact role of Property `(T)`

Finite-dimensional commutant rigidity itself does not use Property `(T)`.
The manuscript uses it when passing to a norm-matrix corona: the Kazhdan
projection turns the Hilbert--Schmidt asymptotic commutant into a projection
represented inside a stably finite corona, so a one-sided inclusion becomes
equality.  The abstract replacement is false without rigidity; see
`norm-corona-locally-finite-compression-countermodel`.

The separate macroscopic-support use is already Property-`(T)`-free for a
finite-order normal generator.  The exact abstract endpoint is
`torsion-normal-generator-mf-character-criterion`: full
MF radical is equivalent to triviality of every pointwise trace limit of
operator-norm asymptotic representations.  For the literal Steinberg target,
the unresolved instance is
`binary-leavitt-steinberg-mf-characters-are-trivial`.  This formulation uses
normal generation of `z`; it does not assume that the Steinberg central cover
is simple.

## Row-module Fourier reduction

Let the row-one abelian root subgroup be

```text
N={x_(1 bullet)(v):v in R^(19)}
```

and let `H=St_19(R)` be the parabolic actor.  On
`M=hat N=(R^*)^(19)`, a shear `x_jk(b)` sends

```text
lambda_j |-> lambda_j+b.lambda_k
```

and fixes the other coordinates.  Restriction of a character of `Delta` to
`N` gives an `H`-invariant probability measure on `M`.  The established node
`surviving-leavitt-corona-character-has-infinite-parabolic-orbit` is stated
for the last-column convention; its left/right-dual proof applies verbatim
to this row convention and says that the only finite `H`-orbit is `{0}`.  It
does not exclude diffuse invariant measures.  In particular, Haar measure
supplies the regular root profile and satisfies all bounded additive-rank
windows.

The remaining measure route has two logically separate parts:

1. classify ergodic `H`-invariant probability measures on `M`;
2. exclude the Haar/regular profile from restrictions of MF characters of
   the full Steinberg group.

The first statement alone cannot prove MF collapse.

## Rank-integrality audit

For `P_a=(1-rho(x_13(a)))/2`, exact additivity and the conjugacy of the two
depth-one cylinders give

```text
r(1)=2r(q)-2r(P_p P_q),       r(1)<=2r(q).
```

An earlier shortcut asserting `x_13(1)` conjugate to `x_13(q)` is false.
The available four-conjugate saturation gives rank inequalities, not that
equality.  Iterated parity and dyadic divisibility constraints are compatible
with sparse Haar-like rank germs; see
`sparse-dyadic-rank-germ-carries-haar-cyclic-holonomy`.  Hence a bounded
window of integer ranks cannot close the endpoint.

The literal stable Steinberg target also has no available Exel--Loring
shortcut in the audited route: the intended stable `K_2` pairing is empty.
Any central-extension variant must be treated as a distinct target.

## Two concrete firewalls

`norm-corona-locally-finite-compression-countermodel` constructs the Pauli
union in `prod M_(2^n)/directSum_norm` and implements its one-sided shift by
cyclic tensor permutations.  The compressor is strict, its relative
commutant is not normalized, and the scalar defect `-1` survives inside an
amenable corona group.  Therefore amenability or local finiteness cannot
replace Property `(T)` in the generic compression criterion.

`leavitt-diagonal-compression-is-elementary-amenable` locates the same
firewall inside the literal coefficient geometry.  The diagonal subgroup

```text
Lambda=union_k GL_3(F_2)^(2^k)
```

is locally finite, contains `z`, and either single manuscript compressor
produces an elementary amenable group.  Thus diagonal roots plus one
compressor admit an MF model separating `z`.  A load-bearing relation must
use a non-diagonal coefficient occurrence or both complementary compressors.

## Two-compressor test resolved negatively

The proposed diagonal two-compressor test does not carry the obstruction.
The exact elementary core has the split normal form

```text
C_lc({0,1}^N,GL_5(F_2)) semidirect (C_2*C_3).
```

It is LEF by a direct finite-amalgam argument: each prescribed finite
multiplication table uses only finite subgroups of the two locally finite
vertex groups `K semidirect C_2` and `K semidirect C_3`; amalgamating those
over the finite subgroup generated by the used seam elements gives a
virtually free group, and one finite quotient realizes the table.  Hence the
elementary core is operator-MF with the mark visible.  Pullback through the
Steinberg projection also gives a corona homomorphism of the chosen lifted
core retaining `z`.

Therefore a Property-`(T)`-free proof for the full Steinberg group must use
off-diagonal Leavitt roots.  Neither one diagonal compressor nor the full
pair of diagonal compressors can be load-bearing.

## Honest surviving targets

The surviving target is row-dual measure rigidity followed by exclusion of
the Haar MF profile, using a relation with genuinely non-diagonal
coefficients.  The relative-ICC/Bessel argument proves that a Haar root
character is regular on the parabolic away from its action kernel, but it
does not exclude that profile.  Neither a generic compression lemma,
diagonal compressor self-similarity, bounded-window rank integrality, the
finite-orbit lemma, nor the full diagonal two-compressor group resolves the
remaining endpoint.

## Scope firewall

A proof using only normalized Hilbert--Schmidt multiplicativity and excluding
the regular character would prove nonhyperlinearity, not merely non-MF.  A
genuinely MF-specific proof must consume operator-norm/corona structure in a
way unavailable to the regular representation.  Until such a step is proved,
`property-t-free-manuscript-results` and the requested Lean endpoint remain
blocked.

## Off-diagonal and finite-matrix update

The first raw inverse-prefix packet is now understood more sharply.  The
group generated by the diagonal core and `x_ij(s_0),x_ij(t_0)` retracts,
with the mark retained, onto the elementary group over

```text
J=F_2<S,T | TS=1>,             Q=1-ST.
```

Its finitely presented Steinberg cover is marked non-LEF: every finite-group
homomorphism kills `x_13(Q)`, while the ring embedding `J->R` carries that
root to `z`.  This is still not a non-MF theorem.  Operator-norm microstates
can retain the one-sided inverse equations as rectangular Morita arrows
unless the two coefficient occurrences are decoded on one square reservoir.
The exact remaining subproblem is recorded as
`binary-jacobson-steinberg-head-root-is-mf-invisible`; together with normal
generation it implies the full target through
`property-t-free-leavitt-via-jacobson-head-root`.

The terminal finite-matrix analysis is complete once a carrier exists.
`weighted-unitary-hall-deficit-gap`, the rectangular rank-deficiency and
operator-norm return lemmas, supercritical projection branching, the Atomic
Leavitt Gap, and the coarse-to-fine leakage theorem all turn an authenticated
dimension mismatch into a uniform defect.  What none of them proves is the
selector theorem.  A successful argument must choose source and target
projections from the individual finite matrix coordinate, authenticate the
native unequal-scale group-word occurrence on them, and obtain either a
positive weighted Hall deficit or a nonzero same-reservoir return.  The
selector cannot be a fixed group-algebra construction: such a construction
would also exist in the regular representation.  This finite-matrix-only
selection/authentication step is the remaining load-bearing seam, not the
terminal rank estimate.

The one-branch terminal estimate has since been sharpened in operator norm.
For contractions `X,Y` on one square finite-dimensional carrier, if

```text
delta=||YX-1||_op<1,
```

then `X` is invertible with `||X^(-1)||<=1/(1-delta)` and

```text
||XY-1||_op<=delta/(1-delta).
```

Thus a nonzero projection `P` satisfying
`||P-(1-XY)||_op=eta` forces
`1<=eta+delta/(1-delta)`.  This kills even a rank-one head after square
decoding; no positive-density hypothesis is needed.  Cairn records the open
semantic step as `binary-jacobson-head-root-has-opnorm-square-decoder` and
the terminal route as `binary-jacobson-opnorm-square-decoder-proof`.

The literal finitary head plus its obvious Whitehead translation does not
supply that decoder.  `binary-jacobson-finitary-whitehead-shift-is-marked-lef`
closes every inspected bilateral shift interval into a long finite cycle,
retaining the head transvection.  Consequently the first possible square
authentication must use a relation from the remaining Laurent-symbol
elementary quotient rather than only finitary matrix units and one shift.

## Additional route-level failures

Several abstract transfers remain unavailable for exact quantifier reasons.

* Tensor powers do not prove `operator-MF => hyperlinear` from bare MF data.
  The tensor exponent must grow slowly relative to the multiplicative defect
  but fast relative to the unknown rank of the spectrally separated part;
  MF supplies no rate relating those two quantities to matrix dimension.
* A non-MF C-star algebra generated by unitaries does not automatically make
  the abstract group generated by those unitaries non-MF.  Group-word
  multiplication does not recover the norm of every noncommutative
  polynomial; a norm-faithful groupification theorem is a separate input.
* A finite-central nonhyperlinear transfer requires an actual discrete group
  extension with the specified kernel and hyperlinear quotient.  A non-CE
  game algebra or central phase by itself does not provide that interface.
* Exact finite-dimensional gaps in linear-system solution groups are not
  operator-MF gaps without a dimension-independent point-norm stability
  theorem at the marked central involution.
* The abstract route “point-norm stable plus nontrivial finite-dimensional
  radical implies non-MF” is valid, but no independently Property-`(T)`-free
  group satisfying both hypotheses is supplied here.

These are interface failures, not weakened terminal estimates.  They agree
with the provenance and occurrence checklist in
`research/artifacts/property-t-free-provenance-delta-2026-08-26.md`.
