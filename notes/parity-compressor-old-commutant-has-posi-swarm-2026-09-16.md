## 0. The target

`(PCD1)` asks for a modification of the Toeplitz parity compressor by one
Schur--Clifford packet and one coefficient-covariance cell. On every marked
microstate with forbidden Hilbert mass `q>0`, a spectral subspace should exist
with

```text
W <= low-commutator space of Lambda,      tr_ad(W) >= c q^2 - C sqrt(E).
```

The Attempts list is long, and every entry is a *countermodel* built from:

- residual finite quotients (regular, semidirect, induced, telescoped or
  Følner-windowed); or
- congruence finite-field representations in which the packet survives and
  the old commutant density is diluted like `1/|Q_n|` or `1/d^2`.

The last bullet asks for a two-cell with "genuinely nonamenable joint
holonomy".

## 1. Idea

The packet embedding `iota` of `schur-child-crosses-toeplitz-compressor`
scales the centre-and-`Q` coordinates by the matrix unit `e_11`. So the
packet sign `J` becomes a **finitary** transvection: it lies in

```text
L_inf = GL_fin(N x [r], F_2) = ker(EL_r(T) -> GL_r(T/F)) <= EL_r(T) <= Lambda.
```

`L_inf` is locally finite. It is exhausted by `F_m = GL_(rm)(F_2)`, whose
minimal nontrivial degrees `delta(F_m)` tend to infinity. This follows from
the simplicity of `SL_n(F_2)` for `n>=3` together with Jordan's theorem on
finite linear groups (classical, not re-fetched).

A Kazhdan low-energy adjoint direction is almost invariant under
`Ad(phi_n(Lambda))`, and so under `Ad(phi_n(F_m))`. Almost-invariance under a
finite group whose nontrivial irreducibles are huge forces the direction to
be carried by the `F_m`-invariant vectors, up to `delta(F_m)^-2`. On the
sector `J=-1` there are no such vectors, because `J in F_m`. So the sector
carries no low-commutator density at all, **in every microstate sequence**.
No trace hypothesis is used, so this is stronger than the canonical-trace
statement `canonical-marked-corner-kazhdan-density-vanishes`.

Once the density is controlled by the `L_inf`-invariant mass `tau(e_inf)`, a
second step removes that mass from any character. When all letters are
comparable on `L_inf` (true for `H(alpha,chi)`), `e_inf` is central. Cutting
it off gives a hyperlinear character with the same forbidden mass, up to
normalization, and zero low-commutator density.

## 2. Results (FSS1--FSS7)

Setting: `G` is a countable group containing a property-(T) subgroup `Lambda`
with Kazhdan pair `(S,kappa)`.
`phi_n` are normalized-HS microstates with `tr o phi_n -> tau`. On
`M_(d_n)`, `A_n` is the adjoint Laplacian and `P_n=1_[0,b](A_n)` with a fixed
`0<b<kappa`. `P_F` is the group average and `e_inf=lim P_(F_m)`.

- **FSS1.** `limsup tr_ad(P_n) <= tau(P_(F_m))^2 + delta(F_m)^-2`, hence
  `<= tau(e_inf)^2`.
- **FSS2.** For projections `Z_n` with `tr(Z_n phi_n(.)) -> omega`:
  `limsup ||R_(Z_n)P_n||_2^2 <= tau(P_(F_m))omega(P_(F_m)) +
  omega(1-P_(F_m))/delta(F_m)^2`, hence `<= tau(e_inf)omega(e_inf)`. The same
  holds for `L_(Z_n)`.
- **FSS3.** The subspace and energetic forms of FSS2: any `W_n<=P_n` or
  low-energy `R_n` that is approximately supported on the sector has
  vanishing density whenever FSS2's bound vanishes.
- **FSS4.** If `J in L_inf` and `||Z_n(1+phi_n(J))||_2 -> 0`, then
  `omega(P_(F_m))=0` for `J in F_m`. The sector bound tends to zero. Spectral
  projections of `phi_n(J)` qualify when `J^2=1`.
- **FSS5 (Toeplitz instance).** `L_inf` for `EL_r(T)`, `r>=3`, satisfies the
  hypotheses. It is infinite, simple and normal, and it is killed by every
  finite-dimensional unitary representation and every finite quotient. Also
  `iota(<Q,Z>) <= F_2`. So the ARG1 Reynolds range (in `End(H_-)`), and every
  sector-supported low-commutator subspace, has adjoint density tending to
  zero in every microstate sequence of every `G >= Lambda`.
- **FSS6 (central compression).** If `G` is generated by letters `u` with
  `u L_inf u^-1 <= L_inf` or `u^-1 L_inf u <= L_inf`, then `e_inf` is central
  in `pi_tau(G)''`. For hyperlinear `tau` with `theta=tau(e_inf)<1`,
  `tau'=tau(.(1-e_inf))/(1-theta)` is a hyperlinear character with
  `tau'(e_inf)=0` and `tau'(y)=tau(y)/(1-theta)` for every `y=y(1-J)/2`,
  `J in L_inf`. `H(alpha,chi)` satisfies the hypothesis: `Lambda` normalizes
  `L_inf`, `J_H` is central, `chi(L_inf)=0`, and `beta(L_inf) <= L_inf`
  because `sigma^2(e_ij)=e_(i+2,j+2)`.
- **FSS7 (vacuity).** For such `G` and a nonzero projection
  `y=y(1-iota(J))/2 in C[Lambda]`, the following are equivalent:
  - (1) every positive-mass microstate sequence has low-commutator density
    at least `c q^2`;
  - (2) no hyperlinear character has `tau(y)>0`.

  Every quantitative `(PCD1)` implies (1). Statement (2) implies that `G` is
  non-hyperlinear. So `(PCD1)` in the `iota` placement is true only
  vacuously, when forbidden-mass microstates do not exist at all.

## 3. Proof sketch (full proof in the route node)

1. **Displacement.** `sum_s ||(Ad(phi_n(s))-I)X||_2^2 = 2|S|<X,A_n X>`.
   Split `P_n` at `kappa/2`. By `(PTB1)` of
   `property-t-hs-adjoint-low-spectrum-budget`, the part below `kappa/2` has
   total energy `O(delta_n)`. By `(PTB2)`, the part in `(kappa/2,b]` has
   trace `O(delta_n^2)`. So `||(Ad(phi_n(s))-I)P_n||_2 -> 0` for every
   `s in S`.
   Telescoping along a word, with `||Ad U-Ad V||_2 <= 2||U-V||_2`, gives
   `||(Ad(phi_n(k))-I)P_n||_2 = o(1)` for every fixed `k`. This is where
   `b<kappa` is used.
2. **Averaging.** Put `T_n = |F_m|^-1 sum_k Ad(phi_n(k))`. Then
   `||(I-T_n)P_n||_2 = o(1)`, so
   `limsup ||R_Z P_n||_2 <= limsup ||T_n R_Z||_2`, uniformly in `Z`.
3. **Trace formula.** Since `T_n^*T_n=T_n+o(1)` and
   `tr_ad(X |-> AXB)=tr(A)tr(B)`,
   `||T_n R_Z||_2^2 -> |F_m|^-1 sum_h tau(h) omega(h^-1)`.
4. **Schur orthogonality.** Decompose the finite-group functions `tau|F_m`
   and `omega|F_m` into irreducible characters. The trivial isotypic part
   gives `tau(P_F)omega(P_F)`. Every other part is at most
   `omega(p_sigma)/n_sigma^2 <= omega(p_sigma)/delta(F_m)^2`. Summing gives
   FSS2, and `omega=tau`, `Z=1` gives FSS1. Let `m -> infinity`.
5. **Sector vanishing.** If `J in F_m`, then `P_F J = P_F`. So
   `omega(P_F) = lim tr(Z_n phi_n(P_F))` equals
   `-lim tr(Z_n phi_n(J)phi_n(P_F)) + o(1)`, which is `-omega(P_F)`. Hence
   `omega(P_F)=0`.
6. **Toeplitz instance.**
   - `L_inf` is generated by finitary transvections, and elementary
     commutators place them in `EL_r(T)`.
   - It is the kernel of `EL_r(T) -> GL_r(T/F)`, hence normal.
   - As an increasing union of the simple groups `SL_(rm)(F_2)`, it is
     simple.
   - The kernel of a finite-dimensional unitary representation meets
     `L_inf` trivially or entirely. Trivially is impossible, because Jordan's
     theorem bounds the order of a simple finite subgroup of `U(d)`, while
     `|F_m| -> infinity`. So `L_inf` is killed.
   - `iota` scales every `q`- and `z`-coordinate by `e_11`. So
     `iota(<Q,Z>) - I` has entries supported on head index `1`, and
     `iota(<Q,Z>)` lies in the finite group `F_2 = GL(N_(<2) x [r], F_2)`.
7. **Central compression.**
   - The projection onto `L_inf`-invariant vectors commutes with `Lambda`
     (normalizer) and with `q` (centralizer).
   - For a letter `u` with `u L_inf u^-1 <= L_inf`, every `L_inf`-invariant
     vector is `u L_inf u^-1`-invariant. So `e_inf <= u e_inf u^-1`.
   - Both projections have trace `tau(e_inf)`, and `tau` is faithful on `M`.
     So they are equal. The case `u^-1 L_inf u <= L_inf` is symmetric.
   - `e_inf` lies in `M` as a strong limit of `P_(F_m)`, and it commutes
     with a generating set. So it is central.
   - Hyperlinearity of the corner: the microstates give a trace-preserving
     embedding `M -> prod_omega M_(d_n)`. The image of `1-e_inf` lifts to
     projections `p_n` with `tr(p_n) -> 1-theta`, and each corner unitary
     `pi(g)(1-e_inf)` lifts to a unitary in `p_n M_(d_n) p_n`.

   Weak point: this ultraproduct bookkeeping is standard but was written
   here, not fetched. See section 5.
8. **Vacuity.**
   - (1) implies (2): compress any putative hyperlinear `tau` with
     `tau(y)>0`, take microstates of `tau'`, and apply FSS1 with
     `tau'(e_inf)=0`.
   - (2) implies (1): every limit point of `tr o phi_n` is a hyperlinear
     character.

## 4. Why the Attempts fences do not transfer, and what replaces them

The residual countermodels in the target's Attempts, from
`canonical-packet-traces-do-not-place-old-commutant-density` through
`rf-amenable-two-cell-retains-coefficient`, are finite quotients,
finite-dimensional representations, or telescopes of such. The packet
survives in each with positive trace. In the `iota` placement the packet sign
lies in `L_inf`, which every finite quotient and every finite-dimensional
representation kills. So all these models have **zero** forbidden mass for
this packet, and they say nothing here.

The congruence exits of
`toeplitz-self-embedding-fixes-unbounded-congruence-types` kill the head
defect `1+xy`, and hence `L_inf`, too. The tensor-decoupled model of
`packet-kazhdan-commutant-placement-decouples` is unavailable, because
`iota(J)` already lies in `Lambda`.

This is not a gain for `(PCD1)`. FSS6 is a replacement countermodel that
works for any `G` with comparable letters, whatever two-cell is added, as
long as the added letters are comparable. Nonamenable joint holonomy, the
last live suggestion in the Attempts, is irrelevant to it: property (T) is
already present, and the obstruction uses nothing beyond `L_inf <= Lambda`.

What the obstruction does *not* say: it does not produce a hyperlinear
character of `H(alpha,chi)` with positive forbidden mass. Whether one exists
is the original non-hyperlinearity question. FSS7 only says that positive
old low-commutator density can never be the certificate.

## 5. Caveats, escapes and weakest steps

- **Placement.** FSS5 uses the `iota` embedding with `e_11`-scaled centre
  coordinates. If the intended packet sign is `J_H`, the external `C_2`, or a
  `Z` coordinate scaled by a non-finitary coefficient such as `xy`, then
  `J notin L_inf`. FSS4 and the orthogonality `y e_inf=0` can then fail.
  For a sign inside `EL_r(T)`, lying outside `L_inf` means having a
  nontrivial image in the congruence head `GL_r(T/F)`. That is exactly where
  the Attempts' congruence exits
  (`toeplitz-self-embedding-fixes-unbounded-congruence-types`) live. So this
  escape trades the present obstruction for those.
- **Cutoff.** Fixed `0<b<kappa`. For `b>=kappa` the displacement bound in
  step 1 is useless. The target only uses the low-commutator space below the
  Kazhdan gap, as in `property-t-hs-adjoint-low-spectrum-budget`.
- **Incomparable letters.** Adding a letter `u` with `u L_inf u^-1`
  incomparable to `L_inf` keeps FSS1--FSS5 but can make `e_inf` non-central.
  Then FSS6--FSS7 are not available. This is a second, narrow escape: such a
  cell must move finitary matrices to non-finitary ones and back.
- **Weakest steps.**
  - Whether the target's intended packet is the `iota` placement at all. The
    target says "one fixed Schur--Clifford packet" and does not name `iota`.
    The committed crossing node is the only packet embedding in the graph
    that I found.
  - The central-compression step 7. Comparability gives
    `e_inf <= u e_inf u^-1` or the reverse, and equality comes from
    faithfulness of `tau` together with trace invariance. The lifting of the
    corner is standard ultraproduct bookkeeping, written here but not
    fetched.
- **Marked semantics.** FSS7 treats marked microstates by cutting to
  `J_H=-1`, a central projection that commutes with `e_inf`. If the target's
  "marked microstate" means something non-central, that clause needs
  rechecking.
