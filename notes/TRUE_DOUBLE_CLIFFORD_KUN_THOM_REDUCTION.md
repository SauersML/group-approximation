# Double Clifford cancellation for the Kun--Thom wreath product

Date: 2026-08-13

## 1. Outcome

There is an exact fermionic enlargement of every binary permutational wreath
product in which the two Clifford signs cancel.  Applied to the explicit
Kun--Thom pair, this gives a new single-algebra endpoint for FALSE:

```text
If the permutation-Clifford crossed product is Connes embeddable, then the
explicit Kun--Thom nonsofic wreath product is hyperlinear.
```

The reduction is exact and retains the canonical group trace.  It is stronger
than a one-word phase construction: the whole independent Bernoulli lamp
algebra occurs inside the tensor square of the Clifford algebra.

The reduction does not by itself prove Connes embeddability.  In fact the
diagonal tensor-square crossed product already contains the original
Bernoulli crossed product.  Thus sign cancellation removes the algebraic
commutativity obstruction but not the coherent finite-dimensional covariance
obstruction.

## 2. Clifford algebra and its permutation action

Let a countable group `G` act on a countable set `I`.  Let `Cl(I)` be the
tracial von Neumann Clifford algebra generated by self-adjoint unitaries

```text
c_x^2=1,             c_x c_y=-c_y c_x  (x!=y).        (DCK1)
```

Its canonical trace satisfies

```text
tau_Cl(c_(x_1)...c_(x_r))=0                           (DCK2)
```

whenever the `x_j` are distinct and `r>0`.  Permuting the generators defines
a trace-preserving action

```text
beta_g(c_x)=c_(g x).                                  (DCK3)
```

Put

```text
C=Cl(I) tensorBar Cl(I),
a_x=c_x tensor c_x.                                   (DCK4)
```

## 3. The two signs cancel exactly

**Theorem 1 (double-Clifford Bernoulli embedding).**  The elements `(a_x)`
are commuting self-adjoint involutions and have the joint moments of
independent Haar signs.  Consequently there is a trace-preserving,
`G`-equivariant embedding

```text
L(directSum_I C_2) -> C.                              (DCK5)
```

### Proof

Self-adjointness and `a_x^2=1` are immediate.  If `x!=y`, then

```text
a_y a_x
 =(c_y c_x) tensor (c_y c_x)
 =(-c_x c_y) tensor (-c_x c_y)
 =a_x a_y.                                            (DCK6)
```

For every nonempty finite set `F subset I`, equation `(DCK2)` gives

```text
(tau_Cl tensor tau_Cl)(product_(x in F) a_x)
 =tau_Cl(product_(x in F)c_x)^2
 =0.                                                   (DCK7)
```

These are exactly the Fourier moments of the canonical trace on
`L(directSum_I C_2)`.  Hence the homomorphism determined by the standard
lamp at `x` mapping to `a_x` is trace preserving and therefore injective.
Equation `(DCK3)` makes it equivariant for the diagonal action `beta tensor
beta`.  End proof.

Taking reduced tracial crossed products in `(DCK5)` gives a
trace-preserving inclusion

```text
L((directSum_I C_2) semidirect G)
  -> (Cl(I) tensorBar Cl(I)) crossed_(beta tensor beta) G.  (DCK8)
```

No approximation or limiting choice is used in this inclusion.

## 4. The explicit Kun--Thom consequence

Take the explicit residually finite Kazhdan pair `Gamma<G` of Kun--Thom and

```text
I=G/Gamma,
W=(directSum_(G/Gamma) C_2) semidirect G.              (DCK9)
```

Kun--Thom prove that `W` is nonsofic.  Since Connes embeddability passes to
tracial von Neumann subalgebras, `(DCK8)` proves:

**Corollary 2.**  If

```text
(Cl(G/Gamma) tensorBar Cl(G/Gamma))
  crossed_(beta tensor beta) G                        (DCK10)
```

is Connes embeddable, then the fixed explicit group `W` is hyperlinear and
nonsofic.

There is also a one-copy sufficient condition.  If

```text
N_Cl=Cl(G/Gamma) crossed_beta G                        (DCK11)
```

is Connes embeddable, then `N_Cl tensorBar N_Cl` is Connes embeddable.  The
von Neumann algebra generated there by

```text
c_x tensor c_x,       u_g tensor u_g                  (DCK12)
```

is the algebra on the left side of `(DCK8)`.  Therefore Connes embeddability
of `(DCK11)` already makes `W` hyperlinear.

## 5. Exact limitation: the tensor square is not an escape hatch

The algebra generated by the commuting `(a_x)` is the Bernoulli algebra

```text
A isomorphic L_infinity({-1,1}^I).                    (DCK13)
```

It is normalized by the diagonal implementers.  The subalgebra generated by
`A` and those implementers is exactly

```text
A crossed G isomorphic L(W).                          (DCK14)
```

Thus proving `(DCK10)` Connes embeddable necessarily proves the desired
result; it cannot follow merely from hyperfiniteness of the coefficient
algebra.  Arbitrary crossed products of a hyperfinite algebra by a
hyperlinear group need not be Connes embeddable.

At the microstate level the remaining condition can be stated cleanly.
Every permutation automorphism of `Cl(I)` is approximately inner, so for
each fixed `g` one can find matrix unitaries implementing `(DCK3)` on any
prescribed finite family of Clifford generators.  What is not automatic is
choosing these implementers coherently so that

```text
||V_(g,n)V_(h,n)-V_(gh,n)||_2 -> 0                    (DCK15)
```

while the local covariance domains exhaust `I`.  Extending finite partial
permutations arbitrarily does not prove `(DCK15)`: a multiplication defect
on even one Clifford mode has order-one normalized `2`-norm after spin
implementation.  Tensoring two copies cancels the scalar spin cocycle but
does not dilute a mode-permutation defect.

Therefore the new endpoint isolates a **coherent Bogoliubov-innerness**
problem for one quasi-regular permutation representation.  It is a useful
alternative formulation of the Kun--Thom CE gate, not a completed proof.

## 6. Quotient formulation

There is a second exact description of the same boundary.  Let

```text
S=(directSum_G C_2) semidirect G                       (DCK16)
```

be the ordinary restricted wreath product.  Summing lamp coefficients on
the right `Gamma`-cosets gives a surjection

```text
S -> W.                                                (DCK17)
```

Its kernel is a locally finite abelian normal subgroup: on every coset it
consists of the finitely supported even-parity configurations.  The group
`S` is sofic because `G` is residually finite, whereas `W` is nonsofic by
Kun--Thom.  Thus their example also gives an explicit failure of closure of
soficity under quotients by locally finite normal subgroups.

For hyperlinearity, `(DCK17)` is not a permanence theorem.  Proving that this
particular locally finite quotient preserves hyperlinearity is exactly
another route to the desired hyperlinear nonsofic group.  Compressing finite
models of `S` to vectors fixed by larger and larger finite parts of the
kernel loses the canonical trace after renormalization unless those
fixed-vector corners are relatively invariant.  That relative invariance is
again the coherent covariance condition in `(DCK15)`.
