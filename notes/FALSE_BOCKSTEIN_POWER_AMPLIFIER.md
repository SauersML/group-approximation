# The Bockstein converts growing clock order into the exact FALSE ratio

## The elementary cyclic mechanism

Let

\[
C_k=\langle\kappa_k\rangle
\cong\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z.
\]

Its unique subgroup of order \(p\) is generated by

\[
b_k:=p^{k-1}\kappa_k.
\]

Let

\[
\chi_k:C_k\to\mathbb T,
\qquad
\chi_k(\kappa_k)=e^{2\pi i/p^k}.
\]

Then

\[
|\chi_k(\kappa_k)-1|
=O(p^{-k}),
\]

whereas

\[
\boxed{
|\chi_k(b_k)-1|
=|e^{2\pi i/p}-1|>0.
}
\]

Thus a unit clock increment is microscopic while its order-\(p\) Bockstein
shadow is macroscopically visible.

## Why the fold label is the Bockstein shadow

In the local integral coefficient complex, consider the coefficient sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow I
\xrightarrow{\;p\;}I
\longrightarrow I/pI
\longrightarrow0.
\]

On a cyclic \(p^k\)-summand of integral homology, the connecting homomorphism
associated to mod-\(p\) reduction lands in the subgroup killed by \(p\).  If
it is nonzero, its value is therefore

\[
u_kp^{k-1}\kappa_k,
\qquad p\nmid u_k.
\]

The already-established local Baumslag--Solitar calculation identifies this
ordinary Bockstein class with the fold label \([h]\).  The integral clock
class itself has exact order \(p^k\).  Therefore, on the local cyclic
summand,

\[
\boxed{
[h]=u_kp^{k-1}\kappa_k,
\qquad p\nmid u_k.
}
\]

The displayed equality is to be understood under the proved transgression
identification between the local coefficient homology and the fold-label
module.  Replacing \(\kappa_k\) by a unit multiple normalizes \(u_k=1\), but
keeping the unit visible makes the functorial statement invariant.

## Bounded-coordinate completion theorem

Let \(P=\langle S\mid r_1,\ldots ,r_\ell\rangle\) be the fixed folded-double
presentation, and let \(w\) be its radical fold word.

### Theorem 1 (Bockstein ratio certificate)

Suppose that for every \(k\) there is a finite central quotient of the
relation module containing a cyclic summand

\[
C_k=\langle\kappa_k\rangle\cong\mathbb Z/p^k
\]

and a character \(\chi_k\) as above, such that:

1. the marked class satisfies, for some \(p\)-adic unit \(u_k\),
   \[
   [w]=u_kp^{k-1}\kappa_k;
   \]
2. for each fixed presentation relator there is an integer \(m_{j,k}\) with
   \[
   \chi_k([r_j])
   =e^{2\pi i m_{j,k}/p^k};
   \]
3. the total coordinate size obeys
   \[
   \sum_{j=1}^{\ell}m_{j,k}^2=o(p^{2k}).
   \]

Then there exists a hyperlinear nonsofic group.

In particular, the stronger uniform condition

\[
\sup_k\sum_jm_{j,k}^2<\infty
\]

is sufficient.

#### Proof

The chordal estimate \(|e^{it}-1|\leq |t|\) gives

\[
\sum_j|\chi_k([r_j])-1|^2
\leq
\frac{4\pi^2}{p^{2k}}
\sum_jm_{j,k}^2
\longrightarrow0.
\]

On the other hand,

\[
|\chi_k([w])-1|
=|e^{2\pi iu_k/p}-1|
\geq
\min_{1\leq u<p}|e^{2\pi iu/p}-1|>0.
\]

The canonical finite central quotient/projective-regular construction now
gives Hilbert--Schmidt asymptotic representations of \(P\) retaining \(w\).
Their tracial-ultraproduct image is hyperlinear and not sofic. \(\square\)

## Functoriality target

The local Bockstein identity is not enough by itself.  It must survive three
maps:

\[
H_1(B_k,J_k)
\longrightarrow
H_1(A_k,N_k)
\longrightarrow
H_2(N_k\rtimes A_k)
\longrightarrow
\text{relative relation module of the double}.
\]

The first map is Shapiro/finite-module induction, the second is the mixed
edge of the split LHS spectral sequence, and the third is the fold
transgression.  All are natural with respect to coefficient short exact
sequences.

### Proposition 2 (formal Bockstein transport)

Once the group and module maps in the displayed chain are defined over a
commutative diagram of coefficient short exact sequences, every square with
the ordinary Bockstein commutes.  Hence the local equality

\[
[h]=u_kp^{k-1}\kappa_k
\]

is preserved by finite induction, by the mixed LHS edge, and by the fold
transgression, provided the image of \(\kappa_k\) is not killed.

#### Proof

Shapiro's isomorphism is the natural derived-functor isomorphism for induction
and restriction, so it commutes with connecting homomorphisms of coefficient
sequences.  The Lyndon--Hochschild--Serre spectral sequence is natural in
maps of extensions and in coefficient sequences; its exact couples therefore
commute with the coefficient connecting maps on every page and on the
associated filtration.  Finally, the fold transgression is itself a
connecting morphism of the relative bar-chain sequence, and connecting
morphisms are natural.  Composing these natural squares proves the claim.
\(\square\)

Therefore the identity

\[
[w]=u_kp^{k-1}\kappa_k.
\]

does not require a new analytic construction.  The substantive hypotheses
are existence of the induced/unit-split module map and survival of the class
in the relative quotient.

The remaining quantitative requirement is then precisely the coordinate
bound in Theorem 1: after choosing the finite central extension and its gauge,
the fixed presentation relators must pair with the clock character by
integers \(m_{j,k}=o(p^k)\) in aggregate.

This is a substantially sharper target than constructing arbitrary matrices.
The witness amplification is already exact; only functorial Bockstein
transport and bounded relator coordinates remain.

## Relation to the exact clock block

The projective-regular representation associated to all powers of
\(\chi_k\) packages the scalar sectors into the familiar clock operator.  A
single scalar sector sees the unit increment \(e^{2\pi i/p^k}\); the orbit of
sectors under the metacyclic shift distributes the marked spectrum and gives
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt displacement \(\sqrt2\).  The scalar Bockstein
calculation above is the irreducible central shadow of that clock/shift
amplification.
