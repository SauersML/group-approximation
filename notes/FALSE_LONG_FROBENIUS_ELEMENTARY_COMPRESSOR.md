# A long Frobenius elementary model for one strict compressor

Date: 2026-08-11

## 1. Outcome

The long paired chain from
`FALSE_LONG_PAIRED_FOURIER_Q_CHAIN.md` extends from the bare coefficient
module to the entire finite square-zero elementary kernel and its base
elementary action.

Let `B` be a finite commutative Frobenius algebra and choose `r>=4` with
`p` not dividing `r` (the radical construction may take `r=4`).  Let

`E=sl_r(B)`, `H=E_r(B)`,                               `(LFE1)`

and put

`W=E tensor V_n`.                                     `(LFE2)`

The group `H` acts on `E` by conjugation and `Q_n` acts on `V_n` in the
standard way.  The trace--Frobenius form on `E`, tensored with the standard
pairing on `V_n`, is perfect and invariant under `H`.

Suppose a ring automorphism `alpha` of `B` has finite order dividing `M`
and preserves the Frobenius functional.  It acts on `E`, `H`, and `W`.
There are two finite-dimensional compressor choices `S_0,S_1` such that,
for every fixed `0<=j<M`,

1. their first `j` forward conjugates agree on the full tangent group `W`;
2. both implement `alpha^j` on the tangent labels;
3. they implement the same `alpha^j` action on the base group `H`; and
4. their inverse conjugates of the marked tangent element differ by the
   coefficient scalar `q=1+p` and have fold energy exactly two.

For the actual Laurent construction one may take

`B=A_n[(Z/mZ)^d]`                                     `(LFE3)`

with Frobenius functional equal to the coefficient of the identity
monomial.  Every monomial substitution in `SL_d(Z/mZ)` preserves this
functional.  Taking `alpha` to be the reduction of the strict transvection
and choosing `M` divisible by its order gives the required finite model.

Consequently the selected-compressor gate is no longer merely additive:
all square-zero tangent additions, all base/tangent semidirect relations,
all base elementary relations, and arbitrarily many positive monomial
iterates are exact.  The remaining problem is simultaneous external
coherence: put these one-compressor models over one finite principal bundle
so the other generators of `SL_d(Z)` act on the same tangent/base model
without destroying common-positive agreement.

## 2. The finite square-zero elementary kernel

Let `tau_B:B->A_n` be a Frobenius functional, so

`(X,Y)_E=tau_B(tr(XY))`                                `(LFE4)`

is a perfect pairing on `E=sl_r(B)`: the trace pairing on `M_r(B)` is
perfect, and `p` not dividing `r` splits off the scalar line orthogonally
from the trace-zero summand.

Let

`J=B tensor_(A_n) V_n`, `J^2=0`,                      `(LFE5)`

and form the split square-zero ring `B directSum J`.  In the split special
linear group, the kernel of reduction to `SL_r(B)` is

`K={1+X:X in M_r(J), tr(X)=0}`.                       `(LFE6)`

Since `J^2=0`, multiplication in `K` is addition of the `X` coordinates.
Thus `K` is the additive group underlying `W=E tensor V_n`; the base group
acts by conjugation on the `E` factor.  Restricting to the subgroup
generated by elementary matrices gives the corresponding finite quotient
of the square-zero elementary group used in the radical construction.

The pairing on `W` is

`<(X tensor v),(Y tensor w)>`
` =tau_B(tr(XY)) (v^T w)/p^n mod Z`.                  `(LFE7)`

It is perfect.  For `h in H`, cyclicity of matrix trace gives

`<hzh^(-1),h z' h^(-1)>=<z,z'>`.                     `(LFE8)`

This orthogonality is what lets the same base elementary operator act in
every Fourier polarization below.

## 3. The skew cyclic polarization

Work on

`calH=ell^2(Q_n times W^M)`.                          `(LFE9)`

For `z in W`, define `B_0(z)` as the character multiplier in coordinate
zero and `B_j(z)`, `1<=j<M`, as translation by

`(1_E tensor g^(-1))z`                                `(LFE10)`

in coordinate `j`.  These are the direct analogues of
`(LPQ8)--(LPQ9)`.  The left `Q_n` action changes only `g`, and the diagonal
base action applies `Ad(h)` to the `E` component of every coordinate.
Equations `(LFE7)--(LFE8)` prove exact covariance under both groups.

Let `F_0` be Fourier transform in coordinate zero.  Write `X_j(z)` for
translation in coordinate `j`, so

`B_j(z)=F_0X_j(z)F_0^(-1)`.                           `(LFE11)`

Let `P_(alpha,M)` be the permutation of `W^M` induced by the linear map

`(x_0,x_1,...,x_(M-1))`
` |->(alpha(x_(M-1)),alpha(x_0),...,alpha(x_(M-2)))`. `(LFE12)`

Then

`P_(alpha,M)X_j(z)P_(alpha,M)^(-1)`
` =X_(j+1 mod M)(alpha z)`.                           `(LFE13)`

Because `alpha^M=1`, the skew cyclic permutation has finite order.  Set

`S_0=F_0P_(alpha,M)F_0^(-1)`.                        `(LFE14)`

It follows that

`S_0B_j(z)S_0^(-1)=B_(j+1 mod M)(alpha z)`.          `(LFE15)`

The same operator implements the ring automorphism on the base group.
Indeed, let `R_H(h)` be the diagonal permutation induced by `Ad(h)` on
all `W` coordinates.  Frobenius invariance makes `F_0` commute with this
orthogonal action, while `(LFE12)` gives

`P_(alpha,M)R_H(h)P_(alpha,M)^(-1)=R_H(alpha h)`.     `(LFE16)`

Hence

`S_0R_H(h)S_0^(-1)=R_H(alpha h)`.                    `(LFE17)`

Equations `(LFE15)--(LFE17)` are the complete tangent/base covariance for
the selected external compressor.

## 4. The relative gauge

Let `c_0` and `K_n=q^(-1)c_0^T` be as in `(LPQ2)` and `(LPQ17)`.  Define
`T` by right multiplication `g|->g c_0`, by

`x_0 |->(1_E tensor K_n)x_0`,                         `(LFE18)`

and, for `j>=1`, by

`x_j |->(1_E tensor c_0^(-1))x_j`.                   `(LFE19)`

It acts trivially on the `E` factor.  Therefore it commutes with the whole
base representation `R_H(H)`.  The calculation `(LPQ19)--(LPQ20)` on the
`V_n` factor gives

`[T,B_j(z)]=1` for `1<=j<M`,                          `(LFE20)`

`TB_0(z)T^(-1)=B_0(z)^q`.                            `(LFE21)`

Put

`S_1=S_0T^(-1)`.                                      `(LFE22)`

Starting with any `B_1(z)`, induction using `(LFE15)` and `(LFE20)` gives

`Ad(S_1^j)(B_1(z))=Ad(S_0^j)(B_1(z))`
` =B_(j+1)(alpha^j z)`                                `(LFE23)`

for `0<=j<M`, with indices understood before the next wrap.  Since `T`
commutes with `R_H(H)`, the same induction and `(LFE17)` give equality on
the first `M` forward base iterates as well.

In the inverse direction,

`S_0^(-1)B_1(z)S_0=B_0(alpha^(-1)z)`,                `(LFE24)`

`S_1^(-1)B_1(z)S_1=B_0(alpha^(-1)z)^q`.              `(LFE25)`

For the marked element

`z_*=P x^(e_2) tensor a`                              `(LFE26)`

or its aligned inverse-compressor label, the multiplier in `(LFE24)` is a
nontrivial primitive character in the `V_n` direction.  Averaging its
`p`-th power over coordinate zero gives zero exactly, hence the fold in
`(LFE24)--(LFE25)` has squared normalized HS norm two.

The left coefficient action of `c_0` commutes with `S_0,S_1` and scales the
marked `V_n` factor by `q`, exactly as in `(LPQ25)--(LPQ26)`.

## 5. The Laurent Frobenius specialization

Take `B` as in `(LFE3)`.  The coefficient-of-identity functional is
Frobenius because the group monomials form a basis and

`tau_B(x^u x^v)=1 iff u+v=0`.                         `(LFE27)`

Every `C in SL_d(Z/mZ)` permutes the monomial basis by `u|->Cu`, fixes the
identity exponent, and therefore preserves `tau_B`.  Its action on `H` and
`E` satisfies every hypothesis above.

Reduction of Laurent coefficients modulo `(p^n,x_i^m-1)` gives a ring map
from the original coefficient ring to the split square-zero finite ring
`B directSum(B tensor V_n)`.  Consequently the base/tangent representation
in Sections 2--4 is a genuine finite quotient representation before the
second compressor gauge is inserted.  The gauge changes only the inverse
polarization and preserves every tested positive iterate before the depth
wrap.

Choose `M` divisible by the order of the reduced transvection on `B` and
then let `M->infinity` through congruence levels.  For every fixed positive
word, the depth wrap eventually lies beyond its compressor depth, so
`(LFE23)` gives exact agreement of the two vertex models on that word.

## 6. Remaining simultaneous-coherence theorem

The construction proves the complete selected-compressor covariance, but
it does not yet prescribe the other external generators on the same depth
coordinates.  The exact principal-bundle representations from
`FALSE_PAIRED_FOURIER_PRINCIPAL_BUNDLE_SPLICE.md` satisfy all external
relations, while `(LFE12)` supplies the natural action on the tangent/base
fiber.  These two actions must now be combined.

In cocycle terms, if

`zeta_i(k,x)=varphi_i(x)^(-1)varphi_i(kx)`             `(LFE28)`

is the exact finite principal-bundle cocycle, the required fiber functor
must satisfy

`Pi_(i,kx)(alpha_k g)`
` =Ad(zeta_i(k,x)) Pi_(i,x)(g)`                       `(LFE29)`

for every tested base/tangent generator `g`.  Along the selected subgroup
`<t,s>`, Sections 3--4 solve `(LFE29)` for arbitrarily long depth windows.
The remaining statement is to solve it simultaneously for a generating
set of the finite congruence image of `SL_d`, with the two functors agreeing
on the fixed positive window.

This is now a finite equivariant Frobenius-module problem, not an arbitrary
matrix optimization.
