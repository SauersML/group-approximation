# No one-sided growth: the Kazhdan--Clifford proof architecture

## What kind of object this is

Kazhdan transport is **not** a fourth approximation class alongside MF,
sofic, and hyperlinear groups.  Those three names describe classes defined by
the existence of particular finite models.  Kazhdan transport is instead a
rigidity theorem about what operator-norm matrix models are forced to do in
the presence of a one-sided compression of a property-`(T)` subgroup.

Its novelty is structural: it produces an operator-norm obstruction and, via
the Clifford detector below, separates MF visibility from sofic and
hyperlinear visibility.  One could package arbitrary target families into a
new formal approximation class, but that packaging is not the mathematical
phenomenon proved here.

## The reusable theorem

Let `iota : Gamma -> H`, with `Gamma` Kazhdan, and let

```text
s iota(Gamma) s^{-1} <= iota(Gamma).
```

For an operator-norm almost representation `U_n : H -> U(d_n)`, consider
every uniformly operator-norm bounded matrix sequence `x_n` satisfying

```text
||[x_n,U_n(iota(gamma))]||_2 -> 0.
```

Kazhdan transport proves that both coordinate conjugates by `U_n(s)` and by
`U_n(s)^*` satisfy the same asymptotic commutation relations.  In the corona
of adjoint representations, if `P` is the Kazhdan fixed-space projection and
`V = Ad U(s)`, then

```text
P <= V P V*,      P ~ V P V*.
```

The norm-matrix corona is finite, so `P = V P V*`.  A one-sided
group-theoretic compression therefore becomes a two-sided symmetry of the
entire bounded asymptotic commutant.

## The intrinsic radical

For `L <= H`, let `compressionGroup L` be generated by every
`s` with `s L s^{-1} <= L`, and define the normal subgroup generated by

```text
[g z g^{-1}, ell]
```

for `g` in the compression group, `z` centralizing `L`, and `ell` in `L`.
All-compressors transport kills every such generator in the tracial
ultraproduct attached to every operator-norm almost representation.  The
Reynolds complementary corner then proves that every finite normal subgroup
inside this intrinsic defect lies in the norm-MF residual.

## The Kazhdan--Clifford contradiction

For a proper injective self-embedding `alpha : Gamma -> Gamma`, choose
`a` outside its range.  In the relative presentation put

```text
d = t c t^{-1},
u = [d,a],
w = u^2 = [d,a d a^{-1}],
```

with `c^2 = 1`, `c` centralizing the base, and `w` central.  Centrality and
`d^2 = 1` already imply `w^2 = 1`; the order-two relation is redundant.

The same element `a` supplies both halves:

```text
a in Gamma                 =>  Kazhdan transport gives u ->_2 1;
a notin t Gamma t^{-1}     =>  two Clifford sites differ, so w = -1.
```

If a corona representation detects `w`, cut to its negative central corner.
There `w -> -1`, while transport and `w = u^2` give `w ->_2 1`, contradicting
the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt distance `||1-(-1)||_2 = 2`.

## The three finite worlds

The exact and asymptotic arguments are instances of the same principle:

```text
finite sets:                 X <= Y and |X| = |Y|       => X = Y;
finite-dimensional spaces:  V <= W and dim V = dim W   => V = W;
finite C*-algebras:          p <= q and p ~ q           => p = q.
```

The finite-dimensional obstruction uses the middle line.  Operator-norm
Kazhdan transport uses the last.

## The sofic detector

The Clifford witness has finite coset floors
`Gamma/alpha^n(Gamma)`.  Finite Clifford lamps over finitely many floors give
split finite extensions of the residually finite affine base.  Directed union,
automorphic dilation, and the sofic-by-amenable extension theorem show that
the full witness is sofic.  Its central sign survives, but functoriality sends
the MF-invisible sign into its MF radical.  Hence it is a finitely generated
sofic non-MF group, and the original sign is MF-invisible but sofic- and
hyperlinear-visible.

`RadicalSeparation.lean` formalizes the abstract detector consequences.  The
concrete coset tower and the required sofic permanence construction are an
unconditional manuscript proof.  It is kept outside the numbered exact-claim
manifest until the corresponding sofic permanence infrastructure is
mechanized, so no conditional or paper-only declaration is presented as an
exact Lean endpoint.
