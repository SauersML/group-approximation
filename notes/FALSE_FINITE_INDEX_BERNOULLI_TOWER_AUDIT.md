# The finite-index Bernoulli tower is an exact local wall, not yet a radical wall

## 1. The affine tower gives an exact wall

Let

\[
\Gamma=\mathbb Z^3\rtimes \mathrm{SL}_3(\mathbb Z),
\qquad
\alpha(z,A)=(2z,A).
\]

Then \(\alpha\) is injective and

\[
[\Gamma:\alpha^n(\Gamma)]=8^n.
\]

The finite coset spaces

\[
X_n=\Gamma/\alpha^n(\Gamma)
\]

therefore form an exact algebraic tower.  The map

\[
F_n:X_n\longrightarrow X_{n+1},
\qquad
F_n(g\alpha^n(\Gamma))=\alpha(g)\alpha^{n+1}(\Gamma),
\]

is injective and intertwines the \(\Gamma\)-action at level \(n\) with the
action precomposed by \(\alpha\) at level \(n+1\).  Equivalently, after
passing to the direct limit \(\Gamma_\infty=\varinjlim(\Gamma,\alpha)\), the
ascending HNN group

\[
P=\Gamma_\infty\rtimes\langle t\rangle,
\qquad
t\gamma t^{-1}=\alpha(\gamma),
\]

shifts the levels exactly.

Put a two-point Bernoulli lamp at each point of the resulting locally finite
tower.  The root lamp is fixed by \(\Gamma\).  Its inverse shift has
stabilizer \(\alpha(\Gamma)\), so any
\(\gamma_0\in\Gamma\setminus\alpha(\Gamma)\) moves it.  Thus the associated
reflection is an exact relative-commutant wall: it commutes with the root
copy of \(\Gamma\) and does not commute with
\(h=t^{-1}\gamma_0t\).

The lamp group is locally finite and the tower group is amenable-by-linear;
the resulting discrete construction is sofic, hence its group von Neumann
algebra is Connes embeddable.  This is a genuine finite-tracial realization
of the quasi-regular rank-one prototype.  It is, however, only a local wall.

## 2. Why the finite-index pair cannot be the radical pair

The pair \(\alpha(\Gamma)<\Gamma\) already has a finite-dimensional wall.
Indeed, in the permutation representation on
\(\ell^2(\Gamma/\alpha(\Gamma))\), let \(p\) project onto the distinguished
coset and put \(V=1-2p\).  Then \(V\) commutes with
\(\alpha(\Gamma)\) and fails to commute with every element outside
\(\alpha(\Gamma)\).

Consequently, the corresponding double word survives in a finitely generated
linear group.  Such a group is residually finite and hence sofic.  The word
therefore cannot lie in the sofic radical of that double.

The same warning applies to the ascending construction: its exact wall lies
in a sofic group.  It cannot itself retain a previously certified
sofic-radical word.  The locally finite lamp may be an ingredient in a
Connes-embeddable algebra, but the final separating homomorphism must not
factor through this sofic tower group.

## 3. A modular obstruction to a purely finite-index Kazhdan completion

There is a structural reason the tower cannot simply be enlarged by more
finite-index compressors until it becomes the Kun--Thom property-(T) pair.

Let \(\Lambda<K\) be commensurated.  Its modular homomorphism is

\[
\Delta_\Lambda(g)=
\frac{[\Lambda:\Lambda\cap g\Lambda g^{-1}]}
     {[g\Lambda g^{-1}:\Lambda\cap g\Lambda g^{-1}]}
\in\mathbb Q_{>0}.
\]

This is a homomorphism.  If \(g\Lambda g^{-1}\leq\Lambda\) has finite index,
then

\[
\Delta_\Lambda(g)=[\Lambda:g\Lambda g^{-1}]>1
\]

unless the inclusion is equality.  Hence:

**Proposition.** If a group \(K\) is generated by elements which compress a
commensurated subgroup \(\Lambda\) into finite-index subgroups, and \(K\)
has property (T), then every such compression is equality.

**Proof.** Property (T) passes to the abelianization, and a discrete
Kazhdan abelian group is finite.  The image of \(\Delta_\Lambda\) is a
torsion-free subgroup of \(\mathbb Q_{>0}\), so it must be trivial.  The
display above then forces every asserted finite-index inclusion to have
index one. \(\square\)

In particular, the ascending group \(P\) visibly maps onto
\(\mathbb Z\) by the exponent of \(t\), so it is not Kazhdan.  Any genuine
Kazhdan infranormal completion of a strict finite-index tower must introduce
directions which are not finite-index commensurating compressors.  In the
known constructions these are precisely the infinite-index substitution
directions where the finite lamp model stops being automatic.

## 4. The exact remaining extension theorem

For an already certified pair \(\Gamma_0<G_0\), double
\(D=G_0*_{\Gamma_0}G_0\), and marked word

\[
1\neq w\in\operatorname{Rad}_{\mathrm{sof}}(D),
\]

it remains to construct a Connes-embeddable finite tracial algebra
\((M,\tau)\), a representation \(\pi:G_0\to U(M)\), and a **non-group**
unitary

\[
v\in\pi(\Gamma_0)'\cap M
\quad\text{with}\quad
[v,\pi(h)]\neq1.
\]

Then \(\pi\) and \(\operatorname{Ad}(v)\pi\) agree on \(\Gamma_0\), define
a homomorphism \(D\to U(M)\), and retain \(w\).  Its image is hyperlinear
because \(M\) is Connes embeddable, and it is nonsofic because a sofic image
would kill \(w\).

The finite-index Bernoulli tower supplies the desired wall locally.  The
remaining mixed-dilation problem is to extend the representation across the
mandatory infinite-index directions while preserving the wall and Connes
embeddability.  Solving that extension, not the finite-index tower itself,
is the complete FALSE proof.
