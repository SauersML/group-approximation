# One vertex spatialization already proves FALSE

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

The edge calculation in
`FALSE_EDGE_DUAL_SHEAR_SPATIALIZATION.md` left a relative extension problem:
extend the parity implementer from the negative edge corner to adjacent
vertices.  It is not necessary to extend it to both vertices.  An extension
to **one** vertex in a Connes-embeddable finite algebra already produces a
hyperlinear nonsofic group.

The reason is algebraic and quantitative-free.  The implementer centralizes
the Kazhdan subgroup `Gamma`.  Together with the ambient vertex group `G`,
it therefore defines an image of the Kun--Thom free-lamp amalgam

```text
H_2=G *_Gamma (Gamma times C_2).                       (OVS1)
```

The parity action on the vertex lamp algebra forces one standard
Kun--Thom radical word to remain nontrivial in this image.  Thus any
Connes-embeddable realization of the one-sided extension is itself a
complete FALSE certificate.

Equivalently, let `P` be the negative edge algebra, `M` the negative vertex
algebra, and `alpha` the parity automorphism of `P`.  Both components of
the reduced tracial amalgam

```text
N_one=M *_P (P crossed_alpha C_2)                     (OVS2)
```

are Connes embeddable.  If this one explicit amalgam is Connes embeddable,
then a hyperlinear nonsofic group exists.  The common algebra `P` is the
nonamenable edge; automatic Connes-embedding permanence over an amenable
amalgam does not apply.

This strictly sharpens the *abstract certificate*: an arbitrary ambient
algebra need only contain one faithful vertex and one edge spatializer.  It
does not create a second independent canonical CE gate.  The exact
group-corner calculation in
`FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_GROUP_CORNER_AND_COVER.md` shows that the canonical
one-sided amalgam is a finite crossed product of the canonical twisted
double, so the two canonical targets are CE simultaneously.

## 2. Abstract one-sided certificate

Let `Gamma<G` be a Kun--Thom pair.  Fix a strict compressor `t in G` and
choose `gamma in Gamma` such that

```text
t Gamma t^(-1)<=Gamma,
t^(-1) gamma t notin Gamma.                            (OVS3)
```

Let `Y` be a free `G`-set containing a chosen orbit and choose `y_0` in
that orbit.  Put

```text
O=Gamma y_0,
A=directSum_Y C_2,
J=C_2 z times (A semidirect G),                        (OVS4)
K=C_2 z times (A semidirect Gamma).                    (OVS5)
```

The displayed direct-product notation is the vertex splitting used in
`(CPM30)--(CPM35)`.  Define

```text
epsilon(a,g)=sum_(y in O)a_y in F_2,
chi(a,g)=(-1)^(epsilon(a,g)).                          (OVS6)
```

The orbit `O` is `Gamma`-invariant, so `(OVS6)` is a character of `K`.
It is trivial on `Gamma` and on `z`.

Put

```text
p=(1-u_z)/2,
M=pL(J),
P=pL(K),
w_j=pu_j.                                             (OVS7)
```

Let `alpha` be the dual grading action

```text
alpha(w_k)=chi(k)w_k,       k in K.                   (OVS8)
```

**Theorem 1 (one-vertex spatialization certificate).**  Suppose a finite
tracial von Neumann algebra `R` contains an injective unital
star-homomorphism

```text
iota:M->R                                               (OVS9)
```

and a unitary `V in R` such that

```text
V iota(x)V^*=iota(alpha(x)),       x in P.            (OVS10)
```

If `R` is Connes embeddable, then there exists a hyperlinear nonsofic group.

### Proof

First make the implementer involutive.  Equation `(OVS10)` and
`alpha^2=id` imply

```text
V^2 in iota(P)'.                                      (OVS11)
```

The unitary `V` commutes with `V^2`.  Choose by Borel functional calculus a
unitary square root `C=(V^2)^(-1/2)` inside the abelian von Neumann algebra
generated by `V`.  Then `C in iota(P)'`, and

```text
V_0=VC,
V_0^2=1,
V_0=V_0^*,                                             (OVS12)
```

while `V_0` still satisfies `(OVS10)`.  Replace `V` by `V_0`.

Because `chi|Gamma=1`, the unitary `V` commutes with every
`iota(w_delta)`, `delta in Gamma`.  Hence the assignments

```text
G -> U(R),       g |-> iota(w_g),
C_2 -> U(R),     generator k |-> V                     (OVS13)
```

agree on the commuting relations with `Gamma`.  The universal property
gives a homomorphism

```text
Phi:H_2=G *_Gamma (Gamma times C_2)->U(R).             (OVS14)
```

We show that `Phi` retains the standard radical witness.  Put

```text
X=iota(w_t)V iota(w_t)^*,
W=[X,iota(w_gamma)].                                   (OVS15)
```

For `g in G`, conjugation by

```text
X_g=iota(w_g)V iota(w_g)^*                            (OVS16)
```

acts on the vertex lamp at `y` by the character of the translated orbit:

```text
Ad(X_g)(iota(w_(a_y)))
 =(-1)^(1_(y in gO)) iota(w_(a_y)).                   (OVS17)
```

Indeed conjugate the lamp first by `g^(-1)`, apply `(OVS8)`, and conjugate
back.  Since `V` is an involution,

```text
W=X_t X_(gamma t).                                    (OVS18)
```

The two sets `tO` and `gamma tO` are distinct.  To see this, freeness of
the `G`-action identifies them with the left cosets `tGamma` and
`gamma tGamma`; equality would give `t^(-1)gamma t in Gamma`, contrary to
`(OVS3)`.  Distinct cosets are disjoint.  Choose

```text
y in tO symmetricDifference gamma tO.                 (OVS19)
```

Equations `(OVS17)--(OVS18)` give

```text
W iota(w_(a_y))W^*=-iota(w_(a_y)).                    (OVS20)
```

Therefore `W!=1`.

Let

```text
omega=[t k t^(-1),gamma] in H_2.                      (OVS21)
```

The Kun--Thom free-lamp theorem says that `omega` belongs to
`Rad_sof(H_2)`; its nontriviality in the abstract amalgam is also immediate
from Bass--Serre normal form.  By `(OVS15)`,

```text
Phi(omega)=W!=1.                                      (OVS22)
```

Let `L=Phi(H_2)`.  Since `R` is Connes embeddable, its unitary group embeds
in a tracial matrix ultraproduct, and so does the countable subgroup `L`.
Thus `L` is hyperlinear.  If `L` were sofic, the quotient homomorphism
`Phi:H_2->L` would kill every element of `Rad_sof(H_2)`, contradicting
`(OVS22)`.  Hence `L` is nonsofic.  End proof.

The proof does not require `Phi` to be faithful on all of `H_2`.  The lamp
anticommutation `(OVS20)` is exactly the certificate needed to keep one
radical word alive in its image.

## 3. The canonical one-sided amalgam

There are trace-preserving conditional expectations

```text
E_P:M->P,
E_P':P crossed_alpha C_2->P.                           (OVS23)
```

The first is the Fourier projection associated to the subgroup `K<J`; the
second is the canonical crossed-product expectation.  Form the reduced
finite von Neumann amalgam `(OVS2)` using these expectations.

The canonical inclusions of both factors are faithful.  If `v` is the
crossed-product generator in the second factor, then

```text
v x v^*=alpha(x),       x in P.                       (OVS24)
```

Thus Theorem 1 applies with `R=N_one` and `V=v`.

**Corollary 2 (single von Neumann algebra target).**  If the algebra
`N_one` in `(OVS2)` is Connes embeddable, then a hyperlinear nonsofic group
exists.

Both factors of `(OVS2)` are individually Connes embeddable:

1. `J` is residually finite by `(CPM34)--(CPM35)`, so `M=pL(J)` is Connes
   embeddable;
2. `P crossed_alpha C_2` is isomorphic to `M_2(P_0)` by `(EDS5)`, where
   `P_0=pL(ker(epsilon))`; the even group is residually finite, so this
   factor is Connes embeddable.

Therefore the remaining closure statement is one explicit instance of

```text
CE factor *_over a common CE algebra CE factor.       (OVS25)
```

The common algebra `P` contains the Kazhdan group algebra of `Gamma` and is
not amenable.  Known automatic closure over hyperfinite/amenable amalgams
does not cover `(OVS25)`.

## 4. Relation to the twisted vertex gap

`FALSE_TWISTED_VERTEX_INNERNESS_GAP.md` proves that no unitary in any finite
matrix amplification of the canonical vertex factor `M` can satisfy even
an asymptotic form of `(OVS10)`.  Corollary 2 explains the strength of that
obstruction:

* an internal spatializer is impossible;
* an external Connes-embeddable spatializer would already solve FALSE;
* a second vertex need not be supplied independently.

The edge crossed product is therefore not merely a local compatibility
device.  Its one-sided amalgamation with one residually finite vertex is a
minimal abstract certificate for the complete problem.  Canonically, the
crossed-product generator conjugates the first vertex into a second one;
the resulting algebra is exactly the negative central group corner and the
index-two extension described in
`FALSE_ONE_VERTEX_GROUP_CORNER_AND_COVER.md`.

This also identifies the precise role of the external correspondence.  In
`L^2(N_one)`, the vector `v` centralizes `Gamma` but has odd lamp parity.
The regular correspondence `L^2(M)` has no such vector by the twisted-gap
theorem.  Connes embeddability of `(OVS2)` asks whether this one new relative
correspondence can coexist with matrix microstates for the vertex.  If it
can, the radical word `(OVS21)` survives automatically; no subsequent
rank-amplification or trace-selection argument is required.
