# The Kun--Thom radical has an exact Clifford phase detector

Date: 2026-08-13

## 1. Setup

Let `Gamma<G` be an infranormal, nonnormal Kazhdan pair to which the
Kun--Thom centralizer-normalization theorem applies.  Put

```text
H=G *_Gamma (Gamma times C_2)
 =<G,k | k^2=1, [k,Gamma]=1>.                         (KCP1)
```

Choose `t in G` and `gamma in Gamma` such that

```text
h=t^(-1) gamma t notin Gamma.                         (KCP2)
```

With the commutator convention `[a,b]=aba^(-1)b^(-1)`, set

```text
r=[k,h],                 x=[k,r].                     (KCP3)
```

Kun--Thom's theorem puts the conjugate

```text
t r t^(-1)=[tkt^(-1),gamma]
```

in every sofic kernel.  Hence `r`, and therefore its commutator `x`, belong
to `Rad_sof(H)`.

## 2. Clifford crossed-product representation

Let `X=G/Gamma`, let `Cl(X)` be the tracial CAR/Clifford von Neumann
algebra generated by self-adjoint unitaries `(c_y)_(y in X)` satisfying

```text
c_y c_y'= -c_y' c_y,          y!=y',                  (KCP4)
```

and let `G` act by `g(c_y)=c_(gy)`.  Form the finite tracial crossed product

```text
N_Cl=Cl(X) crossed_product G.                          (KCP5)
```

Write `u_g` for its canonical group unitaries and `o=Gamma in X`.  Since
`Gamma` fixes `o`, the assignment

```text
g |-> u_g,                   k |-> c_o                (KCP6)
```

respects `(KCP1)` and defines a homomorphism

```text
Pi:H -> U(N_Cl).                                      (KCP7)
```

Put `y=hGamma`.  Condition `(KCP2)` says `y!=o`.  Direct calculation gives

```text
Pi(r)=[c_o,u_h]=c_o c_y.                              (KCP8)
```

The two unitaries `c_o` and `c_o c_y` anticommute.  Therefore

```text
Pi(x)=[c_o,c_o c_y]=-1.                               (KCP9)
```

The presentation-level identity behind `(KCP9)` is formally verified as
`GroupApproximation.CliffordLamp.commutator_lamp_lamp_mul_lamp` in
`GroupApproximation/Sofic/CliffordLampGroup.lean`.

In particular `x!=1`, and the same explicit word is simultaneously

```text
x in Rad_sof(H),             Pi(x)=-1.                (KCP10)
```

This is the desired algebraic phase lock: no additional shear group is
needed to turn the Kun--Thom defect into the Clifford sign.

## 3. Exact remaining analytic gate

If the tracial algebra `(KCP5)` is Connes embeddable, then the image
`Pi(H)` is hyperlinear and is not sofic.  Indeed `(KCP9)` retains the
sofic-radical word `x`, so a sofic image would contradict `(KCP10)`.

Thus the hyperlinear-versus-sofic problem is reduced to the single explicit
statement

```text
Cl(G/Gamma) crossed_product G is Connes embeddable.    (KCP11)
```

for the concrete Laurent-polynomial pair in Kun--Thom Theorem E.

There is a weaker finite-matrix endpoint which is sufficient and does not
ask for microstates of the whole crossed product.  Let `rho_n:G->U(d_n)` be
normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt asymptotic representations and let `C_n` be
self-adjoint involutions.  It is enough that, for every fixed
`gamma in Gamma`, one has

```text
||[C_n,rho_n(gamma)]||_2 ->0,                          (KCP12)
```

and, on putting `C_(h,n)=rho_n(h)C_n rho_n(h)^*`,

```text
||C_n C_(h,n)+C_(h,n) C_n||_2 ->0.                    (KCP13)
```

Indeed `(KCP12)` extends `rho_n` to asymptotic representations of the
presentation `(KCP1)` by sending `k` to `C_n`.  The same calculation as
`(KCP8)--(KCP9)`, now modulo normalized Hilbert--Schmidt null sequences,
gives

```text
x |-> -1.                                              (KCP14)
```

There is no hidden quantitative loss in this last implication.  If
`D_n=C_(h,n)`, the coordinate matrix for the second commutator is

```text
X_n=D_n C_n D_n C_n,
```

and involutivity gives the exact factorization

```text
D_n(C_nD_n+D_nC_n)C_n=X_n+1.
```

Left and right multiplication by unitaries preserve normalized
Hilbert--Schmidt norm, so

```text
||X_n+1||_2^2=||C_nD_n+D_nC_n||_2^2.                 (KCP14a)
```

Thus `(KCP13)` is exactly, with constant one, convergence of the radical
word to `-1`.  The factorization and norm identity are Lean-checked in
`GroupApproximation/Sofic/CliffordPhaseExtraction.lean`.

The passage from this scalar convergence to the final group-theoretic
conclusion is also formalized.  The theorem
`AsymptoticUnitaryRepresentation.negOnePhase_detects` proves that a word
converging to `-1` is nontrivial in the induced tracial matrix ultraproduct,
and
`exists_hyperlinear_not_isSofic_of_negOnePhase_soficInvisible` combines this
with membership in the full sofic residual.  Both are in
`GroupApproximation/Sofic/AntipodalRadicalCollision.lean`.

The resulting tracial-ultraproduct image is hyperlinear and cannot be
sofic.  Thus `(KCP12)--(KCP13)` is already a complete finite-coordinate
target for the open problem.

For an exact finite-dimensional representation of `G`, the inclusion
`t Gamma t^(-1)<=Gamma` makes conjugation by `rho(t)` send
`rho(Gamma)'` into itself.  Finite-dimensional equality of algebra
dimensions upgrades this to normalization.  Nevertheless no exact pair
`(rho,C)` can satisfy the detector and retain `x`: its finitely generated
linear image would be residually finite and hence sofic, contradicting
`x in Rad_sof(H)`.  Any solution of `(KCP12)--(KCP13)` must therefore use
genuine Hilbert--Schmidt instability, not a hidden exact representation.

The base `Cl(G/Gamma)` is hyperfinite.  This alone does **not** prove
`(KCP11)`: Connes embeddability is not known to be preserved under an
arbitrary action of a residually finite group.  The coset action here is
specifically nonsofic, so profinite-action crossed-product theorems do not
apply.  Likewise, approximate innerness of the individual permutation
automorphisms does not provide coherent implementing unitaries satisfying
the group law in a tracial ultraproduct.

There is a useful group interpretation.  The unitaries `c_y` generate a
locally finite central extension of `direct_sum_X C_2`; adjoining `G` gives
an explicit amenable-by-residually-finite group.  Its negative central
corner is `(KCP5)`.  Proving this one corner Connes embeddable is exactly
the matrix-coordinate problem, not an amenable-extension permanence fact.

## 4. Comparison with the finite-index tower

For the finite-index compressor group in
`TRUE_LITERAL_NONMF_MARK_IS_CE_VISIBLE.md`, the relevant coset levels are
finite `G`-sets and exhaust the Clifford algebra.  Their crossed products
have finite-dimensional approximants, so the analogue of `(KCP11)` is
Connes embeddable.

For the Kun--Thom pair, `G/Gamma` has no such finite invariant exhaustion.
This is precisely where the literal MF-radical detector and the sofic-radical
detector diverge analytically, even though `(KCP9)` shows that their local
Clifford phase calculation is identical.
