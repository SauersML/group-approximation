# A Kazhdan positive core is sofic and Connes embeddable

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

There is a particularly regular Kun--Thom pair for which the nonamenable
positive core from `FALSE_FULL_COMPRESSION_SEMIGROUP_DILATION.md` is not
merely conjecturally Connes embeddable: it is the group von Neumann algebra
of a sofic group.

Let (k=\mathbf F_q), let (d,r\geq3), and put

\[
 R_+=k[x_1,\ldots,x_d],\qquad
 R=k[x_1^{\pm1},\ldots,x_d^{\pm1}],
 \tag{PCS1}
\]

\[
 \Gamma=E_r(R_+),\qquad
 G=E_r(R)\rtimes \operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbf Z).
 \tag{PCS2}
\]

Let

\[
 L_+=\operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbf Z)\cap M_d(\mathbf N),
 \qquad T=\Gamma L_+\subseteq G,
 \tag{PCS3}
\]

and define

\[
 X_0=T\Gamma/\Gamma\subseteq G/\Gamma.
 \tag{PCS4}
\]

Then:

1. (Gamma<G) is a residually finite Kazhdan nonnormal infranormal pair;
2. (T) is a compression subsemigroup which generates (G) as a group;
3. the action (Gamma\curvearrowright X_0) is sofic; and
4. the generalized wreath product

\[
 W_0=\left(\bigoplus_{X_0}C_2\right)\rtimes\Gamma
 \tag{PCS5}
\]

is sofic.  In particular

\[
 N_0=L(W_0)
 =L^\infty(\{\mathord\pm1\}^{X_0})\rtimes\Gamma
 \tag{PCS6}
\]

is Connes embeddable.

Thus step 1 of the finite target in
`FALSE_FULL_COMPRESSION_SEMIGROUP_DILATION.md`--matrix microstates for the
common nonamenable positive core--is solved for this pair.  The remaining
problem is exclusively the simultaneous dilation of the positive external
compressors, with their non-Ore branching and group relations.

## 2. The regular finite-field Kun--Thom pair

Suslin stability gives

\[
 E_r(R_+)=\operatorname{SL}_r(R_+)
 \tag{PCS7}
\]

for (r\geq3).  Both rings in `(PCS1)` are finitely generated, so the
universal-lattice theorem gives property ((T)) to their elementary groups.
The external group (operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbf Z)) is Kazhdan, and property
((T)) is closed under extensions.  Hence both groups in `(PCS2)` are
Kazhdan.

They are residually finite by the familiar explicit congruence models.
Reduce exponent vectors modulo (m\mathbf Z^d), obtaining the finite group
algebra

\[
 k[(\mathbf Z/m\mathbf Z)^d],
 \tag{PCS8}
\]

and reduce the external group modulo (m).  Given a finite Laurent
polynomial, (m) can be chosen so that its finitely many exponent vectors
remain distinct.  These finite equivariant quotients separate every
nontrivial element of (G).

Every matrix in (L_+) preserves (mathbf N^d), hence sends (R_+) into
itself and compresses (Gamma).  Positive elementary transvections belong
to (L_+) and generate (operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbf Z)) as a group.  Once
the full external group is generated, its conjugates of (E_r(R_+))
generate (E_r(R)): every Laurent monomial is the image of a positive
monomial under a suitable element of (operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbf Z)).
Therefore (T) generates (G) as a group, proving infranormality.
Nonnormality follows from any determinant-one sign change which sends a
positive exponent to a negative one.

Because every (ell\in L_+) compresses (Gamma), the product rule

\[
 (\gamma_1\ell_1)(\gamma_2\ell_2)
 =\gamma_1(\ell_1\gamma_2\ell_1^{-1})\ell_1\ell_2
 \tag{PCS9}
\]

shows that (T=\Gamma L_+) is a semigroup.

## 3. Every positive stabilizer is congruence separable

Fix (ell\in L_+).  The stabilizer in (Gamma) of the point
(ell\Gamma\in X_0) is

\[
 H_\ell=\Gamma\cap\ell\Gamma\ell^{-1}
       =\ell\Gamma\ell^{-1}.
 \tag{PCS10}
\]

Let

\[
 R_\ell=k[\ell\mathbf N^d]\subseteq k[\mathbf N^d]=R_+.
 \tag{PCS11}
\]

The monoid (ell\mathbf N^d) is freely generated by the columns of
(ell), so (R_\ell) is again a polynomial ring over (k).  Conjugating
coefficients and applying `(PCS7)` to both polynomial rings gives

\[
 H_\ell=E_r(R_\ell)=\operatorname{SL}_r(R_\ell).
 \tag{PCS12}
\]

We claim that (H_\ell) is separable in (Gamma), in fact by finite ring
congruence quotients.  Take (g\in\Gamma\setminus H_\ell).  Equations
`(PCS7)` and `(PCS12)` imply that at least one matrix entry (f) of (g)
lies outside (R_\ell).

For (N\geq0), let (I_N\triangleleft R_+) be the monomial ideal spanned
by all monomials of total degree greater than (N), and put

\[
 Q_N=R_+/I_N.
 \tag{PCS13}
\]

This is a finite ring.  Choose (N) at least the degree of every monomial
appearing in (f).  Monomials of degree at most (N) remain linearly
independent in (Q_N), while the image of (R_\ell) is spanned by those
surviving monomials whose exponent belongs to (ell\mathbf N^d).
Since (f\notin R_\ell), at least one exponent in its support lies outside
(ell\mathbf N^d), and therefore

\[
 \bar f\notin\overline{R_\ell}\subseteq Q_N.
 \tag{PCS14}
\]

The finite quotient

\[
 \Gamma\longrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_r(Q_N)
 \tag{PCS15}
\]

maps (H_\ell) to matrices all of whose entries lie in
(overline{R_\ell}), whereas the selected entry of (g) does not.  Thus
the image of (g) is outside the image of (H_\ell).  This proves
separability.

Stabilizers of arbitrary points of the orbit (Gamma\ell\Gamma/\Gamma)
are conjugates of (H_\ell), so they are separable as well.

## 4. Soficity of the positive action and core

A transitive action of a residually finite group on (Gamma/H), with
(H) separable, has exact finite Schreier approximations.  Indeed, for a
fixed finite labeled ball, separability supplies a finite quotient which
distinguishes all of the finitely many required non-(H) cosets; its action
on the cosets of the image of (H) reproduces that ball exactly.

The set (X_0) is a countable union of the orbits above.  Any finite test
window meets only finitely many of them, so taking a common finite quotient
and a finite disjoint union of the resulting coset actions proves that

\[
 \Gamma\curvearrowright X_0
 \quad\text{is a sofic action.}
 \tag{PCS16}
\]

The acting group (Gamma) is residually finite and hence sofic.  The base
group (C_2) is finite.  The generalized-wreath-product theorem for sofic
actions now gives that `(PCS5)` is sofic.  Its left regular representation
therefore embeds into a tracial matrix ultraproduct, proving `(PCS6)`.

## 5. The sharpened endpoint

Let

\[
 W=\left(\bigoplus_{G/\Gamma}C_2\right)\rtimes G.
 \tag{PCS17}
\]

Kun--Thom's theorem makes (W) nonsofic, while the positive core (W_0)
is sofic by the theorem above.  Moreover (W) is generated by (W_0) and
the positive external matrices (L_+).  The root lamp is central in
(N_0), and a strict inverse predecessor has commutator energy (2) after
dilation, exactly as in `FALSE_FULL_COMPRESSION_SEMIGROUP_DILATION.md`.

Consequently the live problem has been reduced to the following extension
question:

> Starting from the sofic microstates of (W_0), can the endomorphisms
> induced by (L_+) be implemented by unitary dilators satisfying the
> finite relations of (operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbf Z))?

The answer cannot come from an Ore construction by
`FALSE_COMPRESSION_SEMIGROUP_ORE_NO_GO.md`.  A positive answer by a genuinely
non-Ore finite-matrix construction would make `(PCS17)` hyperlinear and
therefore give a hyperlinear nonsofic group.
