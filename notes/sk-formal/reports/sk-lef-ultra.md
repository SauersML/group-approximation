> Conversely, let Γ be LEF and generated by a finite set S, with finite models φ_n : B_n → Q_n on word balls.
> Then γ ↦ (φ_n(γ))_n embeds Γ in the algebraic ultraproduct ∏_ω Q_n, and the doubled regular actions embed this
> group in P = ∏_ω Sym(Q_n × {1,2}) by even permutations. Every even permutation of a finite set is a commutator
> of two permutations [Ore, Theorem 1], so each s ∈ S is a commutator of two elements of P. Let Δ₀ ≤ P be generated
> by S and these elements, and put Δ = Δ₀ × ℤ. A countable subgroup of an algebraic ultraproduct of finite groups
> is LEF, so Δ is an infinite finitely generated LEF group with Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ].
