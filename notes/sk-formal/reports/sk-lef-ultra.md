> Conversely, let Γ = {γ_1, γ_2, …} be a countable LEF group, replaced by Γ × ℤ so that it is infinite. There are finite groups

> μ_k(γ_j). On Q_k × ℤ/m_k × ℤ/4^{k+1} let b_k add 1 to the last coordinate, and let a_k act on the level i by f_jk if
> i = 2^{2j−1} and by t_k if i = 4^j, for j ≤ k, and trivially on the other levels. Then b_k^{−n} a_k b_k^n acts on the level
> i as a_k acts on the level i+n. As distinct pairs of powers of 2 have distinct differences, all below a quarter of the
> modulus, the word w_j = [b^{−2^{2j−1}} a b^{2^{2j−1}}, b^{−4^j} a b^{4^j}] evaluated at (a_k, b_k) acts by [f_jk, t_k] on the
> level 0 and trivially on the other levels, for j ≤ k. In the algebraic ultraproduct over ω of the symmetric groups of these
> sets, sending γ to the class of the permutations multiplying the first coordinate on the level 0 by μ_k(γ) and acting
> trivially elsewhere is a homomorphism, injective as μ_k(γ) ≠ 1 for γ ≠ 1 and large k. It sends γ_j to w_j(a,b), where
> a = (a_k)_k and b = (b_k)_k, so Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ] for the group Δ generated by a and b. Finitely many products and inequalities in the
> ultraproduct hold in some coordinate, so Δ is infinite, finitely generated and LEF.

## Route notes

- "well defined as |Q_k| divides m_k": `f_jk` uses `x ^ i.val`, and `pow_val_add_one` is the independence of the representative.
- "all below a quarter of the modulus": the levels are at most `4^k`. So `2^s + 2^{2j−1}` and `2^{2j} + 2^r` lie below
  `4^{k+1}`, and the congruence is an equality. This is the printed no-wraparound step, unwound through sums.
- ω is any ultrafilter on ℕ with `ω ≤ cofinite`; the endpoint uses `Filter.hyperfilter ℕ`.
- "Γ ≤ [Δ,Δ]" is an injective homomorphism into `Δ` whose image lies in `commutator Δ`. Each `γ_j` maps to the commutator
  `w_j(a,b)` of two elements of `Δ`.
