## Spelling
- R is oa-hyperfinite's Pauli model L_β(Γ), with Γ = ℕ →₀ (ZMod 2 × ZMod 2), proved to be a hyperfinite II₁ factor. Uniqueness of R is not used.
- M ⊗̄ R for M ⊆ B(H): on ℓ²(Γ; H) ≅ H ⊗ ℓ²(Γ), the double commutant of {x ⊗ 1 : x ∈ M} ∪ {1 ⊗ λ_β(γ)}. That is the von Neumann algebra generated by M ⊗ 1 and 1 ⊗ R. No completed Hilbert tensor product is needed.
- McDuff factor: a II₁ factor M with M ≅ M ⊗̄ R, as a unital ⋆-isomorphism. McDuff's central-sequence characterization is not used.
- "embeds in R^ω": a unital, trace-preserving, injective ⋆-homomorphism into the tracial ultrapower.
- "whose unitary group contains G": an injective group homomorphism G →* unitary(L(G) ⊗̄ R), g ↦ λ(g) ⊗ 1.

## Route for "hyperlinear ⇒ L(G) ↪ R^ω" (absorbed from oa-ozawa71, to be generalized to twisted group algebras so that it also embeds L_{σ∘pr₂}(G×Γ) = L(G)⊗̄R)
1. Trace vanishing: amplify the separated unitary models, V = (1 ⊕ u)^{⊗a} ⊗ (1 ⊕ ū)^{⊗a}, so that tr V_g ≤ ((2+ε)/4)^a for g ≠ 1.
2. A unital ⋆-hom π : ℂ[G] → ∏_ω M_k with ultratrace ∘ π equal to the coefficient at 1.
3. Extension to L(G) without Kaplansky density: the resolvent map f(t) = 2t/(1+t²) is 2-norm Lipschitz, and the unit ball of the ultraproduct is 2-norm complete. Moment matching, and injectivity from faithfulness of τ.
4. Compose with ∏_ω M_k → R^ω (oa-hyperfinite).
