## Open obligation

Exactly one, and it is the compression calculation owned by lane `ring-b-alg`:

```lean
def FullDefectAtFixedRing : Prop :=
  ∀ (R : Type) [Ring R] [Countable R] (s t : R), t * s = 1 →
    (∃ (m : ℕ) (a b : Fin m → R), ∑ k, a k * (1 - s * t) * b k = 1) →
    ∀ (n : ℕ), 4 ≤ n →
      HasKazhdanPropertyT.{0, 0} (elementaryGroup (Fin 3) R) →
      HasKazhdanPropertyT.{0, 0} (elementaryGroup (Fin n) R) →
      ∀ (M : Type) [Group M], IsOperatorMF M →
        ∀ (f : elementaryGroup (Fin n) R →* M) (x : elementaryGroup (Fin n) R),
          f x = 1
```

It is discharged by `manuscriptSaturatedPrintedDefectRadical` applied at
`G = elementaryGroup (Fin n) R` and `L = core n`, once `ring-b-alg` lands
`core n`, its surjection from `elementaryGroup (Fin 3) R`, and
`printedDefect (core n) = ⊤` under fullness.  Property `(T)` for `core n`
comes from `HasKazhdanPropertyT.of_surjective` along that surjection.

The second open obligation is upstream and belongs to lane `ejz-integral`:
`FinitelyGeneratedRingGeneralRankElementaryPropertyT`.  Every statement above
appears twice for that reason, once with it as a hypothesis and once over
prime characteristic where it is proved.
