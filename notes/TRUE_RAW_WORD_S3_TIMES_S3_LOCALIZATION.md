Date: 2026-08-13

## 1. Exact finite geometry

After the certified classical alignment, let `K` be the subgroup generated
by the two distinct second-chart letters in the compiled 25-syllable raw
word.  The exact matrix audit gives

\[
 |K|=6,
 \qquad K\cong GL_2(2)\cong S_3.
 \tag{RSS1}
\]

There are four distinct first-chart letters in the same word, with
multiplicities `2,4,6,1`.  Every one centralizes `K`.  If `C` is the subgroup
they generate, then

\[
 |C|=6,
 \qquad C\cong S_3,
 \qquad K\cap C=1,
 \qquad [K,C]=1.
 \tag{RSS2}
\]

Moreover

\[
 \langle K,C\rangle=K\times C,
 \qquad |K\times C|=36.
 \tag{RSS3}
\]

The script verifies `(RSS2)--(RSS3)` by enumerating the exact subgroup of
`GL_4(2)`: the centralizer of `K` in the generated order-36 group has order
six and element-order histogram

```text
order 1: 1 element
order 2: 3 elements
order 3: 2 elements.
```

Every first-chart letter has trivial `K` factor in its unique `K*C`
factorization.  Thus this is not merely a normalizer statement: the first
and second alphabets of the raw word occupy the two commuting `S3` factors
at the classical fold.

## 2. Exact microstate reduction

In a canonical two-chart matrix model, keep the first chart fixed and write
the second as `U rho(.) U*`.  The raw word uses only

```text
rho(C)             from chart 1,
U rho(K) U*        from chart 2.                      (RSS4)
```

Therefore its entire evaluation belongs to the algebra generated by two
exact six-element subgroup representations.  At `U=1`, `(RSS3)` makes the
alphabets commute and the word collapses to the identity.  Away from the
fold, the word is a closed transport holonomy measuring the failure of
`U rho(K) U*` to commute with `rho(C)`.

This explains why the raw-word telescope localized all compulsory Reynolds
energy to `K`: no other part of `H`, and no other first-chart element, occurs
in this word.

For a regular `A8` chart with external multiplicity `k`, restriction to the
order-36 subgroup is

\[
 \lambda_{A_8}|_{K\times C}
 \cong 560k\,\lambda_{K\times C}.
 \tag{RSS5}
\]

Restriction to `K` alone is `3360k` copies of its regular representation.
Consequently the fixed representation-type vertices have dimensions

```text
K-type             multiplicity
trivial             3360 k
sign                3360 k
standard (dim 2)    6720 k.                           (RSS6)
```

The relative implementer still has arbitrary matrices on the multiplicity
spaces; `(RSS6)` does not assume their recovery.  It does show that the raw
word's first nonlinear block problem has only three fixed `K`-types and a
fixed commuting `C` action.


Each single extension is an `S4` subgroup containing `K=S3`; the two

\[

\]

This identifies the finite quiver which the hard pair must traverse:


Recovering that first `S4` extension is part of the nonlinear coefficient

\[

 \tag{RSS10}
