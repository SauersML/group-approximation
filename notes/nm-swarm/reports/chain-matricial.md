Route note (keys `1d0f9a56866c`, `fd026a61f84f`, `ff376e50f433`).  The printed proof builds levels from a
separating partition.  Lean builds the exponent classes first:
- `D_r(x)` is the set of exponents reachable from `x` in at most `r` generator steps inside `C`.
- It is increasing in `r`, has at most `m` elements (freeness), and so is stable from `r = m − 1`.  This is the
  loop-erasing bound.
- The level partition is then a separating finite clopen partition.  Ordering its atoms by exponent, the first
  occupied atom is the minimal exponent, which gives `baseSet`.

Every printed claim is proved as stated; only the order of construction differs.

Why the generator set `E` matters: the full-orbit return set `{d | T^d x ∈ C}` is not locally constant on a
compact clopen `C ⊂ U`.  In the orbit closure of `z_k = a^∞ b 0^k c 2^∞`, with `C = {w_0 ∈ {b,c}}`, return
times are unbounded.  So the classes are those of the relation generated by the finitely many exponents in the
prescribed elements, as printed.
