## The characteristic zero case (`Kazhdan/GHWCharZero`)

`hasHaagerupProperty_closure_of_charZero`: over a field `F` of characteristic zero, the subgroup
generated by a finite `s ⊆ GL_2(F)` has the Haagerup property.

1. Build the finite extension.
   - The entries generate a finitely generated `ℚ`-algebra.
   - Noether normalization gives `ℚ[t_1..t_d]` under it.
   - Clearing denominators gives an integer `M > 0` such that `M^e x` is integral over
     `B = ℤ[t_1..t_d]` for every entry `x`.
   - Take `L = Frac(B)` and `K = L(entries)`, which is finite over `L`.
2. Non-archimedean places: ghw-charp2's `exists_places_minpoly_coeff` (0379bac08).
   - Suppose `a` is `M`-integral with valuations at most `exp N`.
   - Then the coefficients of the minimal polynomial of `M^N a` are integer polynomials of total
     degree at most `N [K:L]`.
3. Archimedean places: `GridPlace d K`.
   - These are the embeddings `K → ℂ` above the evaluations `L → ℂ, t_i ↦ z_i(k_i)`, for `k` on
     the grid `ℕ^d`.
   - There are finitely many embeddings over each point, so the family is countable.
4. Finiteness (`finite_of_minpoly_coeff`).
   - Suppose `a` is bounded at every grid place.
   - Then the integer polynomials above are bounded on a grid.
   - `IntegerGridFinite.finite_setOf_totalDegree_le_norm_eval_le` leaves finitely many, so there
     are finitely many minimal polynomials and roots.
   - Scaling by `M^N` is injective, which pulls the finiteness back from `M^N a` to `a`.
5. `hasHaagerupProperty_of_countable_places` (sec2-sentences' `GHWCountablePlaces`) concludes.
