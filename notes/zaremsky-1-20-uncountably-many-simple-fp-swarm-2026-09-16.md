## 1. Setup

  `simple-fp2-uncountability-via-perfect-simple-quotients`). Part (i) holds iff
  some finitely presented `H` has uncountably many perfect normal subgroups `N`
  with `H/N` simple.
- **The route being fed:** `uncountably-many-simple-fp2-via-v-times-shift-restrictions`.
  - `G = G_V × (Z ⋉ Σ)` on `C × Σ`, with `A(G)` finitely generated.
  - The restrictions `A(G) -> A(G|_{C×Y})` to Sturmian subshifts `Y` are simple,
    with pairwise distinct kernels `K_Y` (established).
  - Two prerequisites were open: the kernels `K_Y` are perfect, and `A(G)` is of
    type `FP_2`. If `A(G) = Γ/N` with `Γ` finitely presented and `N` perfect, then
    each `A(G)/K_Y = Γ/Ñ_Y` with `Ñ_Y` an extension of perfect by perfect, hence
    perfect. So each simple quotient is of type `FP_2`.

## 3. What was proved

1. **`v-times-ample-full-groups-are-generated-by-transpositions`**, direct proof
   `...-proof`. For `T = G_V × H`, with `H` any ample Hausdorff groupoid on a compact
   totally disconnected space: `F(T) = S(T) = A(T)`, and this group is perfect.
   - **Key identity.** Take a normal form `h = ⊔ B_{u_i→v_i} × b_i`. The elements
     `ρ = ⊔ B_{u_i a→a u_i} × Q_i` and `ρ' = ⊔ B_{v_i a→a v_i} × R_i` have trivial
     `H`-part, and `ρ' h ρ^{-1} = h^(0) h^(1)`.
   - **Why that suffices.** Elements with trivial `H`-part are locally constant
     maps `Q -> V`. `V` is generated by transpositions (Higman simplicity; Matui
     Lemma 6.10), so these elements lie in `S(T)`. Copies `h^(w)` are conjugate by
     `V × id`. So in `F/S`, `x = [h^(0)] = [h^(00)][h^(10)] = x²`, which forces
     `x = 1` and then `[h] = x² = 1`. Halving gives `S ⊆ A` and perfectness.
   - **Checks.** A worked example: the shift on half the space is a product of
     transpositions, with the explicit relation
     `r y r^{-1} = (g_0 y g_0^{-1})(g_1 y g_1^{-1})`. A finite-model check of the
     identity: `experiments/v-times-ample-full-group-swindle-2026-09-16/`, 300
     random elements with 200 points each, OK.
2. **`v-times-shift-restriction-kernels-are-perfect-proof`**, requiring 1. A
   kernel element has unit germs on `C × Y`, so it contains the units over a tube
   `C × U` with `U ⊇ Y` clopen. The rest lies in `F(G|_{C×P}) = A(G|_{C×P})`,
   `P = Σ ∖ U`, which is perfect and sits inside `K_Y`. So `K_Y` is a union of
   perfect subgroups of itself.
   - **By-products.** This works for every closed invariant `Y`, and
     `ker(ρ_Y on F(G)) = K_Y`. With `Y = ∅`: `F(G) = A(G)` is perfect.
   - **Consequence.** The route
     `uncountably-many-simple-fp2-via-v-times-shift-restrictions` now waits only on
     `v-times-shift-alternating-group-is-of-type-fp2`, which is equivalent to
     `F(G_V × (Z ⋉ Σ))` being of type `FP_2`.

## 4. Approaches tried and exactly where each died

- **Import an AH-type theorem** (Matui, Li). This dies at the hypotheses: the
  restrictions `G|_{C×P}` with `P` not shift-invariant are not minimal, and Li's
  theorem assumes minimality and comparison. `H_*(G_V × H) = 0` by Künneth, but a
  homology-to-full-group transfer for non-minimal groupoids was not available.
  Replaced by the direct swindle.
- **Support reduction by local transpositions** (`k ↦ k τ_{k|_U}` with
  `U ∩ k(U) = ∅`). It shrinks the support away from fixed points, but it dies at
  fixed points with non-trivial germs, such as attracting fixed points of
  prefix maps, where no such `U` exists. Abandoned.
- **Parking** (move pieces of `h^(0)` into `[1] × Q` by transpositions). It only
  reproduces conjugation invariance `k ≡ k^θ mod S` (Lemma Y in the artifact) and
  gives no relation between `h` and two copies of itself. The missing relation is
  exactly the rotation identity.
- **Direct presentation of `A(G)/N_Y`**, with `N_Y` generated by far-away
  3-cycles, to build an inverse of `ρ̄_Y`. Well-definedness of lifts reduced to
  the same question, whether elements supported off `C × Y` lie in `N_Y`. Circular
  until the lemma was available.
- **Multisection splitting** (from the claim's Attempts): proved but not needed.
  The tube argument of Step 1 handles every kernel element at once.
