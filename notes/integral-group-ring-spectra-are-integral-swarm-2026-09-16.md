# Swarm attack: integral-group-ring-spectra-are-integral-limits (2026-09-16)

Agent: swarm-integral-group-ring-spectra-ar. Lock taken on the target.

## Setup

- `SIR`: weak limits of normalized eigenvalue counting measures of symmetric
  integer matrices of uniformly bounded norm.
- `IR`: weak limits of root distributions of monic real-rooted integer
  polynomials with roots in a fixed interval
  (`research/artifacts/determinant-integer-root-limits-2026-09-12.md`).
- `SIR ⊆ IR` trivially. The target is `mu_A ∈ SIR` for all group-ring `A`.
  The open "converse" gap recorded in the graph is whether `IR ⊆ SIR`
  (totally real orbits realized by symmetric integer matrices with
  negligible extra spectrum).

## Choice of hole

The target implies the determinant conjecture for all groups, so a direct
attack is out of reach. The highest-leverage tractable question is `IR ⊆ SIR`:
if true, the target is equivalent to `integral-group-ring-spectra-are-integer-root-limits`;
if false, the symmetric form is a strictly stronger measure-theoretic
condition and no argument through `IR` alone can reach the target.
