# The full Kun--Thom wreath algebra is a simultaneous semigroup dilation

Date: 2026-08-12

## 1. Outcome

Let \(\Gamma<G\) be an infranormal pair and put

\[
 S=P_\Gamma=\{s\in G:s\Gamma s^{-1}\leq\Gamma\}.
 \tag{FSD1}
\]

There is a canonical \(\Gamma\)-invariant positive part

\[
 X_+=S\Gamma/\Gamma\subseteq X=G/\Gamma
 \tag{FSD2}
\]

and a nonamenable Bernoulli crossed product

\[
 N_+=L^\infty(\{\mathord\pm1\}^{X_+})\rtimes\Gamma.
 \tag{FSD3}
\]

Every compressor \(s\in S\) gives a trace-preserving endomorphism

\[
 \alpha_s=\operatorname{Ad}(u_s)|_{N_+}:N_+\to N_+.
 \tag{FSD4}
\]

The full generalized Bernoulli crossed product

\[
 M=L^\infty(\{\mathord\pm1\}^{G/\Gamma})\rtimes G
   =L\left(\left(\bigoplus_{G/\Gamma}C_2\right)\rtimes G\right)
 \tag{FSD5}
\]

is generated by \(N_+\) and the automorphic dilators \(u_s\), \(s\in S\).
The root sign is central in \(N_+\), while a strict inverse predecessor
moves it after dilation.  Thus all marked-wall geometry is already present
in `(FSD3)--(FSD5)`.

This reduction separates two issues which had previously been mixed:

1. each individual compressor endomorphism has the exact CE automorphic
   dilation from `FALSE_COSET_TOWER_AUTOMORPHIC_DILATION.md`;
2. the remaining obstruction is simultaneous dilation subject to the
   relations among different compressors, equivalently Connes embeddability
   of `(FSD5)`.

Kun--Thom prove that the group in `(FSD5)` is nonsofic for their Kazhdan
infranormal pairs.  Therefore proving `(FSD5)` Connes embeddable would
directly produce a hyperlinear nonsofic group.  The theorem below does not
prove that embeddability, but it reduces it to a precise multi-endomorphism
compatibility problem without any residual question about the marked word.

## 2. The positive coset space

The set \(S\) is a semigroup.  It is also stable under left multiplication
by \(\Gamma\): if \(\gamma\in\Gamma\) and \(s\in S\), then

\[
 (\gamma s)\Gamma(\gamma s)^{-1}
 =\gamma(s\Gamma s^{-1})\gamma^{-1}\leq\Gamma.
 \tag{FSD6}
\]

Consequently `(FSD2)` is \(\Gamma\)-invariant.  For \(r\in S\), left
multiplication defines an injection

\[
 F_r:X_+\longrightarrow X_+,
 \qquad F_r(s\Gamma)=rs\Gamma.
 \tag{FSD7}
\]

Put

\[
 \beta_r(\gamma)=r\gamma r^{-1}\in\Gamma.
 \tag{FSD8}
\]

Then

\[
 F_r(\gamma x)=\beta_r(\gamma)F_r(x).
 \tag{FSD9}
\]

This is the simultaneous version of the coset-tower equivariance identity.

## 3. Endomorphisms of the positive core

Let

\[
 A_+=L^\infty(\{\mathord\pm1\}^{X_+}).
 \tag{FSD10}
\]

Map the coordinate at \(x\) to the coordinate at \(F_r(x)\).  Since
`(FSD7)` is injective, this gives a unital trace-preserving embedding

\[
 \alpha_r^A:A_+\longrightarrow A_+.
 \tag{FSD11}
\]

Equation `(FSD9)` makes

\[
 \alpha_r(a)=\alpha_r^A(a),
 \qquad \alpha_r(u_\gamma)=u_{\beta_r(\gamma)}
 \tag{FSD12}
\]

a normal unital injective trace-preserving endomorphism of `(FSD3)`.  Inside
the full crossed product `(FSD5)`, it is exactly conjugation by \(u_r\).

The assignments respect multiplication in the compression semigroup:

\[
 \alpha_r\alpha_s=\alpha_{rs}.
 \tag{FSD13}
\]

Thus the positive core carries an honest action of \(S\) by endomorphisms,
not merely a collection of unrelated one-variable systems.

## 4. The root wall and strict predecessors

The root point \(x_0=\Gamma\) belongs to \(X_+\) and is fixed by all of
\(\Gamma\).  Let \(a\in A_+\) be the mean-zero sign at this coordinate.
Then

\[
 a\in Z(N_+).
 \tag{FSD14}
\]

Fix \(r\in S\) and \(\gamma\in\Gamma\) with

\[
 h=r^{-1}\gamma r\notin\Gamma.
 \tag{FSD15}
\]

Equivalently, \(\gamma\notin r\Gamma r^{-1}=\beta_r(\Gamma)\).  In the
full dilation put

\[
 v=u_r^*au_r.
 \tag{FSD16}
\]

Because \(a\) commutes with \(N_+\), conjugating by \(u_r\) shows

\[
 v\in L(\Gamma)'\cap M.
 \tag{FSD17}
\]

The two Bernoulli coordinates involved are distinct, so

\[
 \|[v,u_h]\|_2^2
  =\|[a,u_\gamma]\|_2^2=2.
 \tag{FSD18}
\]

This is exactly the relative-bicommutant endpoint, now inside the canonical
Kun--Thom wreath algebra itself.

## 5. Generation of the full dilation

Infranormality says that \(S\) generates \(G\) as a group.  Hence the
unitaries \(u_s\), \(s\in S\), generate the copy of \(G\) in `(FSD5)`.
Since \(x_0\in X_+\), every coset \(g\Gamma\in G/\Gamma\) is the translate
of a coordinate already in \(A_+\).  Therefore

\[
 M=W^*(N_+,u_s:s\in S).
 \tag{FSD19}
\]

Equivalently, `(FSD5)` is the concrete minimal simultaneous automorphic
dilation of the system `(FSD3), (\alpha_s)_{s\in S}` together with all group
relations among the dilators.

For one \(s\), the minimal automorphic dilation is an inductive limit
followed by a crossed product by \(\mathbf Z\); both operations preserve
Connes embeddability.  This does not iterate formally over several
compressors.  Adjoining free dilators produces amalgamated free products
over the nonamenable core \(N_+\), while imposing the actual relations among
the dilators is a quotient operation.  Neither operation is known to
preserve Connes embeddability in this generality.

That is the exact simultaneous-coherence gate.  It is the von Neumann
algebraic form of the kernel-carry equations in
`FALSE_REGULAR_BUNDLE_FREE_TABLE_AND_KERNEL_GATE.md`.

## 6. Consequence and next finite target

For the explicit framed Kazhdan infranormal pair, Kun--Thom's Theorem A
states that the group in `(FSD5)` is nonsofic.  Hence

\[
 M\text{ Connes embeddable}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \left(\bigoplus_{G/\Gamma}C_2\right)\rtimes G
 \text{ is hyperlinear and nonsofic}.
 \tag{FSD20}
\]

The finite problem should therefore be organized around the common positive
core rather than around independent vertex maps:

1. build matrix microstates for \(N_+\);
2. implement each selected \(\alpha_s\) by a unitary with error smaller than
   the marked root energy;
3. impose the finite relations among the selected dilators; and
4. retain the exact root-wall statistic `(FSD18)`.

The one-compressor construction solves steps 1--2 conditionally on CE of its
core.  The principal-bundle constructions solve step 3 after forgetting the
common nonamenable core.  The missing theorem is precisely their compatible
splice.
