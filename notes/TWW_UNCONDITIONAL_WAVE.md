# Tikuisis–White–Winter, unconditionally: what was provable, and what the
# elementary construction provably cannot reach

**Authored 2026-08-19. UNCOMPILED and UNWIRED — see "Before anything is graded".**

## The ask, and the honest answer to it

*Formalize TWW unconditionally, in full.*

The theorem is

> every faithful trace on a separable nuclear C\*-algebra satisfying the UCT is
> quasidiagonal (Ann. of Math. (2) **185** (2017), 229–284),

and its proof runs on the classification programme for nuclear C\*-algebras —
2-coloured classification, the Cuntz semigroup, Elliott–Gong–Lin–Niu. None of
that exists in mathlib or in this repository, and a full formalization is not
a wave, it is a programme. Nothing in this wave assumes it, and nothing in this
wave pretends to prove it: `QuasidiagonalMF.TikuisisWhiteWinterInput` is still
uninhabited, deliberately.

What *is* possible unconditionally, and is done here, is everything on both
sides of the theorem:

1. the route from TWW's conclusion to the printed group implication, with every
   step but one discharged — the residue is a single statement about a single
   trace per group;
2. the conclusion itself, **proved**, on the class where an elementary
   construction reaches it (countable locally finite groups);
3. a **refutation** of that construction everywhere else — it certifies exactly
   the locally finite groups, so the remaining gap is not slack;
4. an unconditional witness that the theorem's hypotheses are sharp: a faithful
   non-quasidiagonal trace on a separable C\*-algebra.

## Why the trace clause is free and multiplicativity is everything

For an injective tuple `e : Fin k → G`, compress to the corresponding point
masses: `φ_e(a)_{pq} = ⟪δ_{e p}, a δ_{e q}⟫`. Then

* `φ_e` is linear, unital (needs `e` injective) and completely positive — the
  form at a tuple is literally `‖∑ᵢ aᵢ vᵢ‖²`;
* **`tr φ_e(a) = τ(a)` exactly**, for every `a` and every `e`. Not
  asymptotically, and with no Følner condition: `δ_x = ρ(x) δ₁`, the reduced
  algebra commutes with the right regular representation, and `ρ(x)` is
  unitary, so every diagonal entry of `φ_e(a)` is already `τ(a)`.

So four of the five clauses of `QuasidiagonalTraceModel` come free, and
quasidiagonality of the canonical trace *by compressions* is exactly the
operator-norm multiplicativity clause.

## The dichotomy, and why it closes the elementary route

At the pair `(λ_{g⁻¹}, λ_g)` the defect is `1 − φ_e(λ_{g⁻¹}) φ_e(λ_g)`, a
product of two incidence matrices, and it computes to the **diagonal projection
onto the coordinates `g` pushes out of the window**. Hence

```text
    ‖defect‖ = 0   if  g · e(Fin k) ⊆ e(Fin k),
    ‖defect‖ ≥ 1   otherwise,
```

with nothing in between — the operator norm cannot see that only a small
*proportion* of the window escapes, which is the only thing a Følner set
delivers. A defect below `1` therefore forces `g · F = F` for the finite window
`F`; the elements with that property form a subgroup which injects into `F`, so
it is finite. Running that over a finite subset of `G`:

> **an asymptotically multiplicative compression family exists iff `G` is
> locally finite** (`compressionFamily_iff_locallyFinite`).

Both directions are proved. The forward one is the obstruction; the backward
one is the construction (compress to the finite subgroups generated by initial
segments of an enumeration — on those, multiplicativity is *exact* on the group
ring, and a three-term estimate against `exists_translationSum_approx` carries
it to the whole algebra).

`ℤ` is amenable and not locally finite, so
`not_exists_compressionFamily_multiplicativeInt` is the gap at one group: TWW
asserts a conclusion there that provably no compression family witnesses.

## What Følner *does* buy, measured exactly

The same matrix, in the other norm. `hsNormSq_inverseDefect` computes

```text
    ‖D‖₂²  =  #{q : g · e q ∉ window} / k ,
```

an equality, which is the Følner ratio of the window. So for **every** countable
amenable group the same compressions are asymptotically multiplicative in the
normalized Hilbert–Schmidt norm, to any accuracy on any finite set
(`exists_folner_compression_of_isAmenable`, off
`AmenableActionSofic.exists_folner`), while the operator norm of that very
matrix stays at `1` (`exists_compression_hsNormSq_lt_and_one_le_norm`, for any
element of infinite order — and every finite window has an escaping coordinate
at such an element, `exists_not_mem_range_of_injective_pow`).

Unitality, complete positivity and the trace clause are exact for every window.
So the accounting for `INT.11` is complete: **four of the five clauses of
quasidiagonality hold at a Følner compression, the fifth holds in the 2-norm,
and in the operator norm it is `0` or `1`.** The content of
Tikuisis–White–Winter, for this application, is exactly the upgrade of the
fourth line to the fifth, and it cannot be performed on these maps.

## What is new, mathematically, in the tree

* The **first inhabitant of `Quasidiagonal.QuasidiagonalTraceModel` at an
  infinite-dimensional algebra.** The docstring of
  `Analysis/QuasidiagonalTrace.lean` records that there was none.
* A second, independent route to `IsOperatorMF` for countable locally finite
  groups: through the trace and the corona, not through LEF and exact finite
  models (`Sofic/LocallyFiniteMF.lean`). The two share no step.
* The sharp statement that permutation rigidity (`Analysis/AmenableQuasidiagonal`)
  was pointing at: that file shows Følner *permutation* models cannot work;
  this one shows the same for the *compressions* quasidiagonality actually asks
  for, and identifies the exact class where they do.
* An unconditional faithful non-quasidiagonal trace on a separable C\*-algebra
  (`Sofic/TikuisisWhiteWinterSharpness.lean`), i.e. the sharpness of TWW's own
  hypotheses.
