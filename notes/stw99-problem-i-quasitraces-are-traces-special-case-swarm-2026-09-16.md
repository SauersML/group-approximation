## Angle

Special case / strengthening.  The best dated general result is Haagerup's
theorem: quasitraces on exact unital C\*-algebras are traces.  The goal was a
natural class strictly beyond it, or a permanence theorem the graph does not have.

The result is a **central fiber principle**.  A unital C(X)-algebra has
bounded-quasitrace linearity (QL) iff every point fiber `A/J_x` does.  It glues
over the points of a central spectrum, not over ideals.  It strengthens Haagerup
to fiberwise exact C(X)-algebras, which need not be exact.  It also reduces
products to their norm ultraproducts.

## What was proven

All details are in `research/artifacts/stw01-central-fiber-qt-linearity-2026-09-16.md`.

1. **Central fiber principle**, `stw01-central-cx-fibers-preserve-qt-linearity`.
   Direct route `stw01-central-cx-fibers-preserve-qt-linearity-proof`.
   - Setup: `theta: C(X) -> Z(A)` is unital and `J_x` is generated by
     `theta(C_0(X \ {x}))`.
   - Statement: `QL(A) <=> QL(A/J_x)` for all `x`.
   - Mechanism, part 1 (central cuts): for `0 <= z <= 1` central, `tau(z .)` is a
     bounded 2-quasitrace and `tau = tau(z .) + tau((1-z) .)`.  This works with
     `w = z^(1/2) x`.
   - Mechanism, part 2 (extreme characters): an extreme `tau` is therefore
     multiplicative on the center, so `tau o theta = ev_p`.  Then `J_p subset N_tau`,
     and Haagerup's descent applies.  Krein--Milman finishes.
   - New relative to the graph: cuts by positive central *elements* rather than
     projections, and localization of extreme quasitraces at a single point of an
     arbitrary central spectrum, including connected bases with no central
     projections.
   - Also proven in the artifact (Proposition 4.2): `ext QT(A)` is the disjoint
     union of the pullbacks of `ext QT(A_x)`.
2. **Products versus ultraproducts**, `stw01-products-qt-linear-iff-ultraproducts-are`.
   Route `stw01-products-qt-linear-iff-ultraproducts-are-proof`, which requires
   item 1.
   - Statement: `QL(prod_i A_i) <=> QL(A_i)` for all `i` and `QL(prod_omega A_i)`
     for all free `omega`.
   - Sequence algebra: `QL(A_infinity) <=> QL(A_omega)` for all free `omega`.
   - Key step: `a` with `lim_omega ||a_i|| = 0` factors as `theta(f) b` with
     `f_i = ||a_i||^(1/2)`, so the free fibers are exactly the ultraproducts.
   - New: the ideal-gluing theorems only return smaller products and never reach
     ultraproducts.
3. **Fiberwise exact C(X)-algebras**, `stw01-fiberwise-exact-cx-algebras-are-qt-linear`.
   Route `stw01-fiberwise-exact-cx-algebras-are-qt-linear-proof`, which requires
   item 1 and cites Haagerup Theorem 5.11.
   - This strictly contains the unital exact case.  Exactness passes to quotients,
     and fiberwise exact algebras need not be exact.
4. **Matrix products**.  The route `stw01-matrix-product-balancing-center-trace-proof`
   has `requires: []` and targets the previously OPEN
   `stw01-matrix-product-algebras-have-qt-linearity`.
   - It is a complete write-up of the balancing and center-valued-trace argument:
     greedy ordering, an explicit shift/permutation formula `diag(c) = w - v w v*`,
     `tau = tau o T` on `W_sa`, complex linearity, and quotients.
   - Small strengthening: on `prod M_(n_i)`, every function satisfying axioms
     (i)-(iii) is a trace.  No bound and no `M_2` witness are needed.
   - The mathematical idea is from the 2026-09-11 certificates artifact.  What is new
     here is the full proof and the route.
5. **Corollary 6.3 in the artifact (no node).**  `C(X, W/J)` has QL for every
   quotient `W/J` of a product of matrix algebras; for example
   `C([0,1], prod_n M_n)` does.  More generally, it suffices that each fiber is exact
   or a quotient of a matrix product.

**Remark tying to Milhøj--Rørdam** (`milhoj-rordam-traceless-family-quasitracial-ultraproduct`,
no node written).  The `P_n` there are traceless and nuclear, being Morita
equivalent to a nuclear algebra, so `QT(P_n)` is empty by Haagerup.  By item 2 and
Proposition 4.2, `ext QT(prod_n P_n)` consists exactly of pullbacks of extreme
quasitraces of the ultraproducts `prod_omega P_n`.  So `prod_n P_n` carries a
nontracial bounded quasitrace iff some ultraproduct `prod_omega P_n` does.

- If some `prod_omega P_n` has no tracial state, the quasitrace that Milhøj--Rørdam
  construct on it is nontracial, and it pulls back to `prod_n P_n`.
- The converse does not follow: an ultraproduct could carry both a trace and a
  nontracial quasitrace.

## Approaches tried and where each dies

- **Non-central `theta`.**  It dies at the cut axiom (i).  In `M_2` with the diagonal
  `C^2`, `tr o theta = (ev_1 + ev_2)/2` is not a character.  Only the radical-central
  weakening, `[theta(C(X)), A] subset` the quasitrace radical, survives (artifact,
  Remark 5.5).
- **Reducing Problem I further.**  For trivial center the principle is empty, and
  the graph already reduces to separable simple algebras.  So this is a permanence
  theorem, not a smaller minimal-counterexample class.
- **Subalgebra direction.**  Take `prod_i C(X_i, M_(n_i))` with unbounded `dim X_i`.
  Its remainder fibers embed into ultraproducts of matrix algebras but are not
  quotients of matrix products.  QL is not known to pass to subalgebras, so
  Proposition 6.2 does not transfer.
- **Continuous balancing over `C(X)`.**  Blockwise diagonalization is not continuous
  in `t in X`.  The fiber principle replaces continuity by evaluation, but only at
  extreme points.
- **Certified new examples.**  The standard nonexact fiberwise exact example over
  `N ∪ {infinity}`, with `C*_r(F_2)` at infinity, is also an extension of an exact
  algebra by a nuclear ideal, so it is already covered.  An example outside every
  combined established class needs a connected base with no exact ideal `C_0(U)A`.
  None was constructed or certified.
- **Non-unital algebras.**  Not treated.  The proof uses `tau(1)`, compactness of
  `QT(A)` and Krein--Milman.
- **Rejected earlier in this run.**
  - Tensor products and finite crossed products: no permanence lemma.
  - Real rank zero, one-relator groups, full group algebras: no mechanism beyond
    exactness.
  - A `sqrt 2` additivity constant and Dixmier averaging: one-sided inequalities
    are restatements (certificates artifact §4).
  - Directed unions of exact groups: already exact.
