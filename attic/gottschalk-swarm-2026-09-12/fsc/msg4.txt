Record the linear shadow of finite-stabilizer coset shift surjunctivity (w6-free-pos-d)

Artifact Section 4. Linear automata on (K^n)^(G/H) are transposes of
endomorphisms of the permutation module K[G/H]^n: injective when the
endomorphism is surjective, and the converse (Lemma 6). If char K does not
divide |H|, the module is projective and stable finiteness of K[G] gives
linear surjunctivity (Proposition 7). At a shared prime the module is not
projective (Lemma 8). Question 9 asks the modular Hecke version. It reduces to
Sylow subgroups, and it is positive for normal p-subgroups by
finite-normal-p-subgroups-preserve-modular-stable-finiteness (Proposition 10).
