Prove that characteristic-uniform rank arguments cannot force V's rank gate

whose identities already hold over a subring of C. So a proof of V's rank gate in characteristic p must use a
p-sensitive identity such as (1 - [s])^p = 0 for s of order p, and none of the established abstract tools
can be the decisive step. Combined with the sofic-configuration firewall, a proof must use a configuration not
known to be sofic together with p-torsion. Opens the order-char cycle law as the first p-sensitive structure
question, with its proved parts. Artifact Sections 3-5.
