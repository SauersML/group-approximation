Rule out bounded-nilpotence and commutative-matrix hosts for Leavitt unit groups

Construction side of the weak-finiteness counterexample routes (lane w6-wf-construct).

Theorem A: in characteristic p, permutation units of p-power order map to unipotents. So a host of
L_(F_p)(1,2)^x whose nilpotents have bounded index kills an element of order p, and the representation is
trivial (for G_3, no representation sends z to -1). This rules out division rings, M_n(D), semisimple
Artinian and Goldie rings, reduced rings and finite-dimensional algebras at once. Any host must contain
nilpotents of unbounded index.

Theorem B: by Malcev's residual finiteness (statement level), no nontrivial host sits inside GL_n(C) over a
commutative ring C, including non-reduced C.
