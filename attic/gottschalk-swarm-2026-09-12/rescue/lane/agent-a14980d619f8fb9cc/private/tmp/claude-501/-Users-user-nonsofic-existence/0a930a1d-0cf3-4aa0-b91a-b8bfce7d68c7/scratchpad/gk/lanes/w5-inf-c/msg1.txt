Localize Rokhlin deficit windows to their difference subgroup and bound site information by reads

A deficit configuration (k,E,F,psi) is carried by the subgroup <E E^-1>: dropping codewords whose
reads are not connected to the special site and conjugating by one window element moves it into
that subgroup unchanged. So no window of size at most 2, and no window inside a coset of a
Rokhlin-maximal subgroup, witnesses a deficit, whatever the codeword set is. Separately, the chain
rule bounds the information about one site by the entropy of the codewords reading it, giving
Phi >= log q/|F ∩ E^-1|; that is Shannon subadditivity only, not a uniform bound.
