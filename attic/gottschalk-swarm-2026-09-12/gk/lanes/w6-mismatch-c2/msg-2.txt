Prove the depth-mismatched transvection pair is inert in graph-of-groups rank models

A countable graph of groups whose vertex groups are locally finite subgroups of
L_(F_2)(1,2)^x carries a weakly finite char-2 rank model. The model has the free
profile on every finite vertex subgroup and is fixed-point-free, and disjoint
cylinder defects in one vertex group have rank 3/8 and multiply exactly.

The pair T_(0,1), T_(1,00) has a product of infinite order, yet it sits in the
tree R_0^x - H - g^-1 R_0^x g, where H is a locally finite block-triangular group.
So using the pair as a factor forces nothing. The single-letter HNN extension
breaks [T_(01,00), T_(1,00)] = 1, and H repairs it. A relation-only proof must
close a cycle of Steinberg relations that no locally finite subgroup absorbs,
use K_2(3,R), or use a global input. Records Attempts on the strict-deficit and
weakly-finite gate nodes.
