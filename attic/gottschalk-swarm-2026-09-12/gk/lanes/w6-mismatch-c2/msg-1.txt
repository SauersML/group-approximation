Prove graphs of locally finite unit subgroups carry weakly finite rank models

Every countable graph of groups whose vertex groups are locally finite subgroups
of L_(F_2)(1,2)^x, with amalgamation along intersections and partial conjugations
by units, has a characteristic-two rank model with the free profile on every
finite vertex subgroup. Free actions are transported along a maximal forest and
intertwined on the other edges. The model is fixed-point-free and weakly finite,
and disjoint cylinder defects in one vertex group have rank 3/8 and multiply
exactly. So relations routed through such a graph cannot force strict
submultiplicativity, the Sylvester gate, or the weakly finite gate. Artifact
Section 1.
