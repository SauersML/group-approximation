Assemble the binary and ternary weakly finite chains end to end (w6-vf-chain, Sections 2-4)

Corner fullness iff no nontrivial weakly finite char-p representation: PASS end to end for
binary (given simplicity of G) and ternary (given simplicity of PG as recorded). No-rep implies
non-surjunctivity elementarily, through the regular representation. The converse from a
Gottschalk counterexample is not established: Rep_p is J_omega = augmentation ideal, while the
counterexample needs only J_1 != 0.
