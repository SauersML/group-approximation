Prove amenable extensions of uniformly quantitatively surjunctive groups are surjunctive

Transplanting an automaton to a normal subgroup over a Følner set of the amenable
quotient gives an injective automaton over the kernel into products of proper
subshift tracks, one per disjoint Garden of Eden tile, plus a full track larger by
exactly the decoder boundary. A deficit uniform over tracks with different missing
patterns (UQS) pays that boundary, so UQS-by-amenable groups are surjunctive; over a
two-ended quotient the boundary is bounded and an asymptotic deficit suffices. Both
properties pass to subgroups and directed unions, so ascending HNN extensions of such
groups are surjunctive. The open input is recorded as
surjunctive-groups-are-uniformly-quantitatively-surjunctive, with the conditional
route into surjunctive-by-amenable-extensions-are-surjunctive. No nonsofic kernel is
known to have either property.
