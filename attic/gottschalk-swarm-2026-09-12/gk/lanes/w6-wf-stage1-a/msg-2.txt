Prove finite-dimensional chart representations kill the one-transvection lift of s0 (w6-wf-stage1-a)

Let H contain z, d1 and h = [[0,1],[-1,s0]], and suppose H has a finite-dimensional char-3 representation
with z -> -1 in which h carries the +1-eigenspace of d1 onto the -1-eigenspace. Then P1[h]P1 has no left
inverse supported in H. Compress h on V ⊗ V ⊗ V*: on the odd part of d1 its image misses the block
V+ ⊗ V+ ⊗ V-*. This covers GL_2(A), and GL_C(A) for any first-letter chart, whenever the chart
subalgebra A has a nonzero finite-dimensional representation. That includes the Jacobson algebra
F_3<s0,t0> (the partner k and the finitary involution included), F_3<s0,t0,t1>, and the polynomial and
free charts, with no soficity input. The chart entries of any surviving left inverse generate an algebra
with no finite-dimensional representation, like the Thompson unit g0. The artifact gains Section 8, and
the open claim gains the Attempts entry.
