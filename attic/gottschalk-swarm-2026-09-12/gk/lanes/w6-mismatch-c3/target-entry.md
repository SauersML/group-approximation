- 2026-09-12 `w6-mismatch-c3`: the mismatched pair is not an escaping configuration
  (`research/artifacts/depth-monotone-leavitt-firewall-2026-09-12.md`).
  - **Stably finite side algebras.** For every letter weight, the span of the `s_μ t_ν` with
    `ω(μ) <= ω(ν)` is stably finite, and its units over `F_3` are locally residually finite
    (`depth-monotone-leavitt-subalgebras-are-stably-finite`).
  - **Firewall.** `T_(0,1)`, `T_(1,00)`, their product, all level roots at every depth, `E`, level `V` and the
    depth-decreasing transvections lie in one such unit group, which has a weakly finite model with `z -> -1`
    (`depth-monotone-configurations-cannot-force-ternary-minus-one`). The relations in `W` hold in that model,
    so they give neither a one-sided pair nor a contradiction.
  - **Where it must go.** A certificate needs elements that change weight in both directions for every weight
    and every conjugate: non-level `V`, or sets like `{T_(0,1), T_(1,0), T_(1,00), T_(00,1)}`. The models kill
    `D`, so the defect form is not firewalled.
