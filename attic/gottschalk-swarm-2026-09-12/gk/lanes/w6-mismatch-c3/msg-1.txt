Prove the depth-monotone parts of the binary Leavitt algebra are stably finite (w6-mismatch-c3)

For letter weights a, b > 0, the span of s_mu t_nu with weight(mu) <= weight(nu) is a unital subalgebra
of L_K(1,2). Restricting the faithful action on locally constant functions to the finite-dimensional
stopping-partition subspaces F_k embeds it in prod End(F_k) / sum End(F_k), which is stably finite.
Over a finite field its finitely generated unit subgroups embed in prod GL(F_k), so they are residually
finite. Lands OPEN for w4-vf-gate. Artifact Sections 0-2.
