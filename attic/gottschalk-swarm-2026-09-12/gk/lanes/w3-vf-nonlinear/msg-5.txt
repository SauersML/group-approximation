Verify that Radu's coordinate subgroups are sofic

Lane w3-vf-nonlinear. Independent re-derivation of w3-lattice's coordinate subgroup
theorem, artifact Section 5.4. Pushing horizontal letters fixes y and preserves {x,z}.
P_y is the semidirect product of <a,b,c> by <y>, hence virtually free. In P_xz the
letters a, b fix the path of {x,z}-words and c reflects it, so the kernel is the even
c-parity subgroup and the quotient embeds in the infinite dihedral group. Both
subgroups are sofic, and the generation cases hold. The open linear target and its
route are correct as scoped.
