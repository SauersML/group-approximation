Record that the baker subgroup of 2V is an ascending HNN extension of V

From lane w6-free-neg-d. In the Brin-Thompson group 2V, V x 1 together with the
ternary baker unit is the ascending HNN extension of V along the halving D. A
ternary shift index in the second coordinate detects the stable letter. That
subgroup is sofic iff V is. So a fullness proof that uses only V x 1 and the
baker unit would already show V is not sofic. The same holds for V x V and for
1 x V with the baker unit, so a proof specific to 2V must leave all three.
