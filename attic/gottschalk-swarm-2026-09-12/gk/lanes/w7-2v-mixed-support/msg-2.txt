Count order-three subgroups to bound partial-support torsion classes for 2V

Let P be an elementary abelian 3-subgroup of V and s a state. Splitting F_2[P] into its simple modules
gives: the sum of s[e_K] over the order-3 subgroups K equals (3^(n-1) - 1)/2 + 3^(n-1) s[e_P]. Order-3
elements of V form two conjugacy classes, fixed-point-free or not.

On the tower groups every line is fixed-point-free. This re-derives the tower formula and s[e] >= 1/3
without the halving maps. On partition groups most lines have a fixed point, which gives
1/3 <= y <= (1 + 2t)/3 for the partial class y. For a 2V obstruction state, the class of a partial element
times 1 (x) e takes the value y/3, the product of the two values.

The regular values satisfy every such identity, so partial-support torsion does not exclude the profile.
Both 2V claims stay OPEN.
