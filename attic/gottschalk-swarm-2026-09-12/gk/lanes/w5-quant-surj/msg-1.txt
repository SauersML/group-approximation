Prove quotient restrictions settle invariant patterns and quantitative deficits, and isolate the residue

Restricting an injective automaton to configurations invariant under a normal subgroup gives an
injective automaton over the quotient. Over G x K with N normal in G and (G/N) x K surjunctive, every
N x 1 invariant configuration is in the image. So the single-fibre Garden of Eden theorem needs no
hypothesis on G, only surjunctivity of K.

For quantitative surjunctivity, a subshift missing an N-invariant configuration inherits the constant of
its restriction to G/N. Constant configurations give log(|B|/c_Y) over every group, and periodic points
make residually finite groups quantitatively surjunctive with no sofic models. The residue is the proper
subshifts containing Fix_N for every normal N with sofic quotient. For groups with no nontrivial sofic
quotient these are the subshifts containing all constants. Theorem Q meets only that residue. The target
stays OPEN.
