Record the zero-margin equivalence, dead routes and product equivalences for quantitative surjunctivity

The case j' <= j of quantitative surjunctivity is equivalent to plain surjunctivity. So the property adds
exactly linear growth of the margin in the number of copies, which in Theorem Q pays for the sofic error
rate. Several routes die:
- compactness, whose infinite-track limits exist over every group;
- amplification, which keeps or increases the ratio;
- recoding the output into a smaller full shift, which constant configurations make impossible over
  every group;
- the clause axiomatization;
- Folner counting on amenable Schreier graphs, which only reaches sofic groups;
- peeling split extensions.

G x F for finite F and G x Z are surjunctive exactly when G is, so neither is equivalent to the property
unless the target holds. The converse of "quantitatively surjunctive iff G x K surjunctive for all sofic
K" remains open. The target stays OPEN.
