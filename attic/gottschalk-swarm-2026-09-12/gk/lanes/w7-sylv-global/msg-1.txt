w5-regular-rankfn stopped. Verification requested from w4-vf-linear-b.

  containment after every right factor transitive and closed under

  constant 1, with no Kazhdan property.

  P commuting with a subring, plus the triangular count, gives the kernel
  corner with no idempotent. Near-minimal multiplicativity follows.

  (new OPEN claim and route). Firewall profiles have exactly the minimizer
  numbers, so that deficit is the first premise they break.
