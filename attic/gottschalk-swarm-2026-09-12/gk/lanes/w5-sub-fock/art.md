# The Fock Toeplitz input and independent defects on sofic subgroups

Lane `w5-sub-fock`, 2026-09-12. Family SUB. Verifier `w4-vf-gate`.

## 0. Task and verdict

**Task.** Decide whether exact Toeplitz commutators force a strict deficit for disjoint cylinder defects, or build a
`theta = 1` model. The relation is `[x_12(t0), x_23(s0)] = x_13(1)`, the one that second-quantized truncated
compressors miss by normalized rank `1/4` (`natural-fock-quantized-compressors-miss-toeplitz-by-a-quarter`). The
deficit targets are `rk(D_A D_B) <= theta rk(D_A) rk(D_B)` with `theta < 1`
(`leavitt-disjoint-cylinder-defects-strictly-submultiplicative`) and, in characteristic three,
`ternary-anti-central-disjoint-defects-have-a-strict-deficit`.

**Verdict: no deficit; a firewall.**
1. The Toeplitz pair and its commutator generate a dihedral group of order 8 inside the locally finite `UT_3(R)`.
   Exactness of the Toeplitz commutator is finite-subgroup data, realized exactly by regular models with
   `theta = 1`. The quarter measures the truncated-shift realization, not a constraint on rank models (Section 1).
2. Every sofic subgroup of `R^x` containing two disjoint frames carries a rank model that is free on all of its
   finite subgroups, with exactly multiplicative disjoint defects and no fixed vector
   (`sofic-subgroups-carry-independent-cylinder-defects`, Section 2).
3. The Toeplitz relation is load-bearing only together with elements outside finite subgroups. With the constant
   Weyl group it generates the Jacobson elementary group `EL_3(J)`; with the constant root groups it generates all
   of `R^x` (Section 3). So a relation-only `theta < 1` from Toeplitz data at two disjoint cylinders would prove
   `EL_3(J)` nonsofic.

## 1. The Toeplitz pair is finite-subgroup data

**Notation.** `R = L_(F_2)(1,2)`, and `R^x = EL_3(R)` through the three-leaf prefix code
(`leavitt-gl-equals-el-and-perfect-unit-group`), with `x_ij(r) = 1 + r e_ij`. The Steinberg relations hold over any
ring: `x_ij(a) x_ij(b) = x_ij(a+b)`; `[x_ij(a), x_jk(b)] = x_ik(ab)` for distinct `i, j, k`; and
`[x_ij(a), x_kl(b)] = 1` when `j != k` and `i != l`. For a cylinder `A`, `iota_A(g) = s_A g t_A + (1 - s_A t_A)`.

**Proposition 1.1.** Put `X = x_12(t0)`, `Y = x_23(s0)`, `Z = x_13(1)`, `x = x_12(1)`, `y = x_23(1)`.
- (a) `X^2 = Y^2 = Z^2 = 1`, `[X, Y] = Z`, and `Z` commutes with `X` and `Y`. So `<X, Y>` is dihedral of order 8.
- (b) `H_T = <X, Y, x, y>` is the group of unitriangular matrices with `(1,2)` entry in `span(1, t0)`, `(2,3)` entry
  in `span(1, s0)` and `(1,3)` entry in `span(1, s0, t0)`. It has order `4 · 4 · 8 = 128`.
- (c) The left regular module of `F_2[H_T]` realizes the Toeplitz commutator exactly, and
  `rk_reg((y-1)(x-1)) = rk_reg((Y-1)(X-1)) = 3/8`.

*Proof.*
- (a) In characteristic two `x_12(t0)^2 = x_12(2 t0) = 1`, and likewise for `Y` and `Z`. Steinberg gives
  `[X, Y] = x_13(t0 s0) = x_13(1)`, and `x_13` commutes with `x_12` and `x_23`. For involutions,
  `[X, Y] = (XY)^2`, so `XY` has order 4.
- (b) Products `ab` with `a in span(1, t0)` and `b in span(1, s0)` span `span(1, s0, t0, t0 s0) = span(1, s0, t0)`.
  This space is 3-dimensional, because `1`, `s0`, `t0` sit in degrees `0`, `+1`, `-1` of the grading of `R`. So those
  matrices form a group. It is generated by `X, Y, x, y`, since `x_13(s0) = [x, Y]`, `x_13(t0) = [X, y]` and
  `x_13(1) = [x, y]`.
- (c) A regular module is a genuine representation, so every relation of `H_T` holds exactly. `<x, y>` and `<X, Y>`
  are both dihedral of order 8, with the defect in the same position. `F_2[H_T]` is free over each of their group
  algebras. The free-module count `dim((y-1)(x-1) F_2[D_8]) = 3`
  (`finite-subgroup-data-cannot-force-defect-submultiplicativity`, route step 4) gives `3/8` for both. ∎

**Consequence 1.2.** For disjoint cylinders `A_1, ..., A_k`, the frames `iota_(A_i)(H_T)` commute pairwise and meet
trivially.
- **Why:** `s_A t_A s_B t_B = 0` for disjoint `A` and `B`.
- **So:** `prod_i iota_(A_i)(H_T)` is finite of order `128^k`. Its regular module realizes every Toeplitz commutator at
  every `A_i` exactly and gives `rk(D_(A_1) ... D_(A_k)) = (3/8)^k`.
- **Already covered:** this is a special case of `finite-subgroup-data-cannot-force-defect-submultiplicativity`, which
  lists the isometry-coefficient root elements among its covered inputs.

**Remark 1.3 (what the quarter measures).**
- **Where the quarter comes from:** `natural-fock-quantized-compressors-miss-toeplitz-by-a-quarter` starts from the
  forced solution `X^ = 1 + T_N E_12`, where `T_N` is a truncated shift. The natural level assignment forces that
  solution, and it leaves a one-particle transvection defect. Second quantization turns the defect into normalized rank
  `1/4`.
- **The regular model is different:** in the regular model of `H_T`, the compressor root elements are not shifts. `X` and
  `Y` are involutions acting freely, and the Toeplitz commutator holds exactly.
- **What this means:** the quarter is a property of realizations through level representations with the natural
  assignment. `transvection-displacement-forces-exact-local-embedding` already excludes those realizations for
  nontrivial rank models of `R^x`. It is not a defect that a genuine model must repair on the Toeplitz data.

## 2. Independent defects on sofic subgroups

**Theorem 2.1** (`sofic-subgroups-carry-independent-cylinder-defects`). Let `H <= R^x` be sofic. Let `A_1, ..., A_k`
be disjoint nonempty proper cylinders with `iota_(A_i)(D_8) <= H`, where `D_8 = <x_12(1), x_23(1)>`. Then `H` has a
characteristic-two rank model `sigma: H -> M^x` into a rank ultraproduct `M` of matrix algebras over `F_2`, with:
- (i) `rk sigma(alpha) = dim(alpha F_2[K]) / |K|` for every finite `K <= H` and `alpha in F_2[K]`;
- (ii) `rk(D_(A_1) ... D_(A_k)) = (3/8)^k`;
- (iii) no nonzero fixed vector, whenever `H` contains an element of infinite order.

*Proof.*
1. **The model.** Take a sofic approximation `phi_i: H -> Sym(n_i)`, and `sigma(g) = [P_i(g)]` from its permutation
   matrices. This is a homomorphism `H -> M^x`, as in `sofic-subgroups-carry-rank-models-violating-two-root-identity`.
2. **Freeness on a finite subgroup `K`.**
   - For `g != 1` in `K`, the proportion of fixed points of `phi_i(g)` tends to `0`. For `g, h in K`,
     `phi_i(g) phi_i(h) = phi_i(gh)` off `o(n_i)` points.
   - Let `W_i` be the union of the `phi_i(K)`-orbits on which all these identities hold and no nontrivial element of
     `K` has a fixed point. Then `W_i` is invariant, `K` acts on it honestly and freely, and `|W_i| = n_i - o(n_i)`.
   - So `F_2^(W_i)` is a free `F_2[K]`-module of rank `|W_i| / |K|`, and its complement has dimension `o(n_i)`.
   - On a free module of rank `m`, left multiplication by `alpha` has rank `m · dim(alpha F_2[K])`. That gives (i).
3. **Defects.**
   - The groups `K_i = iota_(A_i)(D_8)` commute pairwise and meet trivially, so `K = K_1 ... K_k ≅ D_8^k` is finite.
   - `F_2[K] = F_2[K_1] ⊗ ... ⊗ F_2[K_k]`, and `D_(A_1) ... D_(A_k)` is the tensor product of the `D_(A_i)`.
   - The image of a tensor product of left multiplications is the tensor product of the images. So (i) and
     `dim(D F_2[D_8]) = 3` give `(3/8)^k`.
4. **Fixed vectors.**
   - Let `g in H` have infinite order, and fix `N`. For `l <= N`, `phi_i(g)^l` agrees with `phi_i(g^l)` off `o(n_i)`
     points, and `phi_i(g^l)` has `o(n_i)` fixed points. So only `o(n_i)` points lie on cycles of length at most `N`.
   - Over `F_2`, the fixed space of a permutation has dimension equal to its number of cycles. That number is at most
     `n_i / N + o(n_i)`.
   - So `rk Fix sigma(g) <= 1/N` for every `N`, which is `0`. ∎

**Consequence 2.2 (scope of relation-only arguments).**
- **What cannot force a deficit.** No inequality `rk(D_A D_B) <= theta rk(D_A) rk(D_B)` with `theta < 1` follows from
  these inputs together:
  - group relations holding in one sofic subgroup of `R^x` that contains both frames;
  - rank calculus;
  - nontriviality and fixed-point-freeness.
- **So a relation-only proof must** use `sigma` jointly on a nonsofic subgroup containing `iota_A(D_8)` and
  `iota_B(D_8)`, and it proves that subgroup nonsofic.
- **Related firewalls:**
  - it extends `finite-subgroup-data-cannot-force-defect-submultiplicativity` (locally finite data) to every sofic
    subgroup, including infinite-order elements used as factors;
  - the characteristic-three counterpart is `anti-central-sofic-data-cannot-force-defect-submultiplicativity`.
- **Not covered.** The target's route also consumes the defect gap and near-minimal multiplicativity. Those are
  statements about all rank models of `R^x`, they don't restrict to subgroups, and this consequence says nothing
  about arguments that use them.
