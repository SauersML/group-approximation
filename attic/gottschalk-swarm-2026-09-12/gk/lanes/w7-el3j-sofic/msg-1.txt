Record containment and Kazhdan firewalls for soficity of EL_3(J)

Lane w7-el3j-sofic. Step 1 of the soficity question has no reduction.

finite by linear. So EL_3(J) contains no V, T, R^x, EL_3(C_2), F,
BS(2,3), Higman group, or locally finite extension of a finitely
generated infinite simple group.

EL_n(J) is Kazhdan. So EL_3(J) is LEA iff it is LEF, and EL_n(J) is not
LEA for n >= 4.
