
## 3. The one-sided halves are LEF and retain the head

Here `F` is any finite field and `E_F = EL_3(J_F)`. We write `V^3 = V ⊗ F^3` with basis `e_(k,i)` (`k >= 0`,
`i = 1, 2, 3`), and `L_3 = ker π` inside `E_F`. The polynomial subrings `F[S], F[T] <= J_F` are mapped isomorphically by
`π` onto `F[z]` and `F[z^(-1)]`.

**Definition.** `H_+ = π^(-1) SL_3(F[z])`, `H_- = π^(-1) SL_3(F[z^(-1)])`, and
`H_0 = H_+ ∩ H_- = π^(-1) SL_3(F)`.

**Lemma 3.1 (structure).**
1. `H_+ = L_3 x| EL_3(F[S])`, `H_- = L_3 x| EL_3(F[T])` and `H_0 = L_3 x| SL_3(F)`.
2. `E_F = <H_+, H_->`.

*Proof.*
1. **Elementary equals special.** `F[z]`, `F[z^(-1)]`, `F[z,z^(-1)]` and `F` are Euclidean, so `SL_3 = E_3` over each.
   In particular `π(E_F) = SL_3(F[z,z^(-1)])`.
2. **Splitting.** `F[S] -> F[z]` is a ring isomorphism. So `π` maps `EL_3(F[S]) <= E_F` isomorphically onto
   `E_3(F[z]) = SL_3(F[z])`. Hence `H_+ = L_3 EL_3(F[S])`, and `L_3 ∩ EL_3(F[S]) = 1`.
3. **The other halves.** The same argument applies to `F[T]`, and `SL_3(F[z]) ∩ SL_3(F[z^(-1)]) = SL_3(F)`.
4. **Generation.** `E_F` is generated by the `x_ij(c)` (`c in F`), `x_ij(S)` and `x_ij(T)`, since
   `x_ik(ab) = [x_ij(a), x_jk(b)]` and `x_ij` is additive. ∎

**Lemma 3.2 (the transpose-inverse symmetry).**
1. **The anti-involution.** The anti-automorphism of `F<S,T>` exchanging `S` and `T` fixes `TS - 1`, since
   `σ(TS) = σ(S)σ(T) = TS`. So it descends to an anti-involution `σ` of `J_F`.
2. **The automorphism.** `θ(g) = (σ(g)^t)^(-1)` is an automorphism of `GL_3(J_F)`, with
   `θ(x_ij(a)) = x_ji(-σ(a))`. So `θ(E_F) = E_F`.
3. **Compatibility with the symbol.** `π θ = θ̄ π`, where `θ̄(g) = (ḡ^t)^(-1)` and `ḡ` substitutes `z -> z^(-1)`. So
   `θ(L_3) = L_3` and `θ(H_+) = H_-`. ∎

**Proposition 3.3.** Every finitely generated subgroup of `H_+` or of `H_-` is residually finite. So `H_+` and `H_-`
are LEF, and hence sofic.

*Proof.* By Lemma 3.2 it suffices to treat `H_+`.
1. **Truncation.** Put `W_N = span{e_(k,i) : k < N}` and `U_N = span{e_(k,i) : k >= N}`.
   - `L_3` is the union of `L_(3,N) = {g in L_3 : (g - 1)(U_N) = 0, g(W_N) = W_N}`, since `g - 1` is finitary
     (Lemma 1.1).
   - Every `d in EL_3(F[S])` preserves `U_N`, because `S` raises the level. Also `d` commutes with `S^N`.
   - So with respect to `V^3 = W_N ⊕ U_N`, both kinds of element are lower block triangular:
     `g = [[A, 0], [B, D]]`, where `A in GL(W_N)`, `B in Hom_F(W_N, U_N)` and `D` is the restriction to `U_N`.
2. **The model group.** Identify `W_N = F^(3N)` and `U_N = F[S]^3` by `e_(N+k,i) -> S^k ε_i`. Then:
   - `D` is `d` itself for `d in EL_3(F[S])`, and `D = 1` for `g in L_(3,N)`;
   - `Hom_F(W_N, U_N) = M_(3 x 3N)(F[S])`.
3. **A congruence-separable group.** Let `G_N` be the set of block matrices `[[A, 0], [B, D]]` with
   `A in GL_(3N)(F)`, `B in M_(3 x 3N)(F[S])` and `D in GL_3(F[S])`.
   - Composition of operators on `W_N ⊕ U_N` is
     `[[A, 0], [B, D]] [[A', 0], [B', D']] = [[AA', 0], [BA' + DB', DD']]`.
   - This is matrix multiplication in `GL_(3N+3)(F[S])`, so `G_N` is a subgroup of `GL_(3N+3)(F[S])`.
   - It contains `L_(3,N)` and `EL_3(F[S])`. The operators on `V^3` determine the elements, since the action is
     faithful (`jacobson-shift-representation-is-faithful`).
4. **Residual finiteness.** `GL_m(F[S])` is residually finite. If `g != 1`, choose `k` above the degree of every entry
   of `g - 1`. Then `g` survives in the finite group `GL_m(F[S]/(S^k))`.
5. **Conclusion.**
   - A finitely generated `K <= H_+` has generators `l_j d_j` with `l_j in L_3` and `d_j in EL_3(F[S])`.
   - If `N` is large enough that every `l_j in L_(3,N)`, then `K <= G_N`, so `K` is residually finite.
   - Finitely generated residually finite groups are LEF. A group all of whose finitely generated subgroups are LEF
     is LEF, and LEF groups are sofic. ∎

**Corollary 3.4 (the head survives in each half).** `x_13(Q) in L_3 <= H_0`. For every finite subset `Φ` of `H_+`, or
of `H_-`, containing the head, the relations of `E_F` among the elements of `Φ` hold in a finite group in which the
head survives. In contrast, every amenable quotient of `E = E_(F_2)` kills `L`, and so kills the head
(Proposition 1.4).

**Proposition 3.5 (mixed relations are needed).** `E_F` is a proper quotient of the amalgam
`P = H_+ *_(H_0) H_-`.

*Proof.*
1. **Surjection.** The inclusions induce `φ : P -> E_F`, which is surjective by Lemma 3.1.
2. **A reduced word.** Put `a = x_12(S)` and `b = x_23(T)`. Their symbols are not constant, so `a in H_+ \ H_0` and
   `b in H_- \ H_0`. The word `a b a^(-1) b^(-1)` alternates between the two complements. By the normal form theorem
   for amalgams it does not lie in `H_0`.
3. **It lands in the edge group.** `φ(a b a^(-1) b^(-1)) = x_13(ST) = x_13(1 - Q)`, which lies in `H_0`.
4. **Not injective.** `φ` is injective on `H_0`. So `φ` is not injective. ∎

**Remark.** The literal Toeplitz commutator behaves the same way. `x_12(T) in H_- \ H_0` and `x_23(S) in H_+ \ H_0`,
while `[x_12(T), x_23(S)] = x_13(TS) = x_13(1) in H_0`. So the relation `[x_12(t0), x_23(s0)] = x_13(1)` of
`toeplitz-pair-and-weyl-elements-generate-jacobson-el3` is not a consequence of the amalgam.

**Consequence (firewall).**
* **Mixed relations are required.** A proof that sofic approximations, finite models or rank models of `E` must kill
  the head cannot use only relations that hold inside `H_+`, or only relations that hold inside `H_-`. Each half is
  LEF, and its finite models retain the head.
  - In particular, the constants, one Toeplitz root element `x_12(T)` and the head generate a subgroup of `H_-`. Their
    symbols generate the nonamenable group `SL_3(F_2[z^(-1)])`, yet this subgroup has finite quotients in which the
    head survives. This extends the symbol firewall recorded on
    `binary-jacobson-el3-rank-radical-is-the-finitary-kernel` beyond amenable symbol subgroups.
* **Where the obstruction must live.** The load has to be carried by relations that are not consequences of the
  amalgam. The simplest are the Toeplitz commutators `[x_12(T), x_23(S)] = x_13(1)` and
  `[x_12(S), x_23(T)] = x_13(1 - Q)`. This agrees with the location of the exact Toeplitz commutators in the defect
  deficit (commit 0d600e4ef on main).
* **Soficity attempts.** In the other direction, a soficity proof for `E` cannot simply glue approximations of
  `H_+` and `H_-`. The glued object is the amalgam, and `E` is a proper quotient of it.
