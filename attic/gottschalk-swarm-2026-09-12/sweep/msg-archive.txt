Archive the Gottschalk swarm's scratch files under attic

The Gottschalk swarm kept its coordination files, lane working files and
sweep tooling in a session scratchpad outside the repository. A restart
once wiped that scratchpad, so this commit copies every scratch file whose
content is not already somewhere on main into
attic/gottschalk-swarm-2026-09-12/, at its scratchpad-relative path:

- gk/RULES.md (roster, directives, verifier queue), gk/gkland.sh (the
  guarded lander with its node lint) and gk/gkshow.sh.
- gk/lanes/<lane>/: each lane's drafts, reports, small scripts and
  outputs. Most artifacts already landed under research/; the drafts and
  outputs that never did are here.
- rescue/: lane files recovered after the coordinator restart, and
  replay.py, which re-created them.
- sweep/: classify.py, merge.py, plan.py and archive.py, with the plans,
  logs and commit messages of the shared-checkout sweeps (8f5bd6faa1,
  9a95dafb50).
- fsc/, w5-rgr-p/, vfw.md, chg.txt: older coordinator drafts and messages.

Left out: files whose exact content is already on main, private lander
indexes (gk/state/), tree listings and FRONTIER copies derived from main,
Python bytecode, and staging copies. Nothing here is wired into Cairn,
builds or CI. attic/ is not a research/ path, so no node, route or status
changes.
