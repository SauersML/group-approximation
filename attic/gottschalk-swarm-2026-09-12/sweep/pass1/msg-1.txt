Sweep uncommitted work from the shared checkout onto main

The user asked for every uncommitted, staged or untracked file in the shared
checkout to reach main, whoever wrote it, without clobbering anyone. This
commit lands the files older than 30 minutes. Fresher files belong to
sessions that are still editing them, and a later sweep will take them.

Each path was classified against the current tip:
- Content already on main, or an older copy of a blob in main's history:
  skipped.
- Local deletions: never landed.
- New files and edits to paths main has not changed: landed at their own
  paths under research/, experiments/ and notes/.
- Research files that main changed since the local copy was taken: 3-way
  merged against the closest historical base. Clean merges land at the real
  path onto the exact tip blob (GK_ACK). Conflicting merges land the local
  copy under attic/sweep-2026-09-12/ and leave main's version untouched.
- Lean modules, CI and tool files, nm-swarm lane lists, census rows and
  README edits: copied under attic/sweep-2026-09-12/ only, with .lean
  renamed to .lean.txt, so no build, orphan ratchet, gate or wiring job
  sees them.
- One Cairn route, research/ugc-from-finite-moment-pairing-hardness.md,
  targets unique-games-conjecture, and every claim it requires would be on
  main. Landing it at its real path could mark the conjecture established
  on another session's unverified work, so it goes under attic/ as well.
  Its owner can promote it.

No path is deleted and no tip blob is replaced except by a clean merge onto
that blob.
