Why sufficient

commuting-lamp-defect-survives gives a nontrivial image of u in W/<zeta>, and W/<zeta> is a quotient of E/<w>; hence u is not in <w> = {1,w}. If E/<w> is MF, compose E -> E/<w> with a faithful norm-matrix-corona representation of the quotient: the result is a corona representation of E that does not kill u.

This makes literal-mark-quotient-mf a second route to unsquared-defect-mf-visible alongside commuting-lamp-mf-detects-defect, so the two open quotient-MF claims are jointly refuted by a single negative answer to corona-commutant-no-growth.
