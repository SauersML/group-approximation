Statement

For every corona representation Theta of E/<w>, the induced tracial representation into the tracial ultraproduct kills the level-collapse kernel R; and every element outside R is detected in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm by some corona representation. Hence the corona Hilbert--Schmidt radical of E/<w> equals its finite residual R.

Consequently Rad_MF(E/<w>) is a normal subgroup of R, and a faithful MF model of E/<w> must separate elements of R on corners of vanishing normalized rank. The same statement with the same proof holds for W/<zeta> (corona-hs-radical-is-finite-residual), so both open quotient-MF claims have the same shape: separate a trace-invisible level collapse in operator norm.
