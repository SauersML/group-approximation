Statement

The literal forty-one-relator group E has a nontrivial central involution w that every homomorphism from E to every norm matrix corona kills. Consequently E is finitely presented, generated by six elements after Tietze elimination, and is not MF; neither its maximal nor its reduced group C-star algebra is MF.
