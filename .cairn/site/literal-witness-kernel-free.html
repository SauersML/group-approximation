Statement

The kernel of the canonical surjection E -> W = ClLamp(X) x| V from the literal finitely presented group to its Clifford witness is a free group of countably infinite rank. It is therefore not amenable, and literal-witness-kernel-amenable is FALSE.

The map is an isomorphism on the telescope quotients, so the kernel equals ker(N_E -> ClLamp(X)), the kernel of the collapse of the block amalgam onto the group in which all distinct sites anticommute. Every block and the centre inject, so the kernel acts freely on the Bass-Serre tree of the amalgam; it is nontrivial and non-finitely-generated because [c_x, c_y] w lies in it for sites x, y in different blocks. Equivalently: E is free-by-sofic.

The refuted route also used extension permanence in the direction "amenable kernel and sofic quotient imply sofic". Elek--Szabo prove the opposite direction (sofic kernel, amenable quotient); the direction used there is an instance of the open extension problem for sofic groups, so the route was unsound independently of this computation.
