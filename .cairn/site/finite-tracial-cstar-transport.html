Statement

Formulate a natural class of finite tracial C-star algebras for which one-sided Kazhdan transport, finite-normal cancellation, and the central-sign obstruction remain valid in their norm ultraproducts, and prove the resulting target-general theorem.

Ideas

The proposed class is C_ftr: unital C-star algebras with a faithful tracial state (faithfully-traced-unital-coordinates). It is the minimal natural class, in the sense that each axiom is consumed by exactly one step:

tracial state on each coordinate  -> the ||.||_2 statements and transport
faithful                          -> nonzero corners carry states
finite (implied by faithful)      -> corona unitaries lift to unitaries
corner-closed (implied)           -> the sign / isotypic compression

Nothing else about matrices is used. In particular real rank zero, nuclearity, simplicity, strict comparison, cancellation and classification hypotheses are not needed if one argues in the ultraproduct rather than at finite stage; they reappear only if one wants the quantitative finite-stage form (spectral projections at an arbitrary threshold, a uniform comparison modulus), which is what the formalized matrix proof provides.

Two structural findings from the decomposition are worth keeping separate from the target theorem:

  • transport is norm-agnostic (finite-traced-kazhdan-transport accepts the

    • asymptotic-commutant hypothesis in ||.||_2), so property (T) transport is not where the operator-norm/tracial dividing line lies;

  • the dividing line is the corner: free in operator norm, cost

    • tau(p)^(-1/2) tracially (corner-renormalization-cost), and the class cannot be widened past it (tracial-coordinate-transport-barrier).

Property (T) enters at exactly one point of the general proof: it puts the fixed-space projection inside the algebra generated by the representation, so that Murray--von Neumann comparison happens in a finite algebra. Without it the projection is only in B(L^2), which is not finite --- the abstract counterpart of cyclic-base-sharpness.

Uniqueness is not among the axioms, and is not available anyway. A natural guess is that transport needs the embeddings into the ultraproduct to be unique up to unitary conjugacy. It does not: the proof compares two projections inside one finite algebra and never compares two embeddings. That is fortunate, because the available uniqueness theorem does not reach this setting. Hua--White, Uniqueness for embeddings of nuclear C-star algebras into type II_1 factors, arXiv:2601.08779 (verified from the abstract), proves that unital, full, nuclear maps from a separable unital exact C-star algebra satisfying the UCT into an ultraproduct of finite von Neumann factors, agreeing on traces and total K-theory, are unitarily equivalent, with a quasidiagonal-approximation corollary for matrix sequences. Every one of those hypotheses fails for the objects here: the group C-star algebra of a property-(T) group carrying the compression data is not nuclear, the quasi-representations that arise are not nuclear maps, and no UCT input is available. So the theorem is a landmark for the classifiable world and is inapplicable to this architecture; nothing in this graph should be built on it.
