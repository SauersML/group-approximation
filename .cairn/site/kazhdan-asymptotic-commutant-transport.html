Statement

Let a property-(T) group map into a group H, and suppose conjugation by s in H sends its image into itself. In every operator-norm asymptotic unitary representation of H, conjugation by the matrices representing s sends every uniformly bounded Hilbert--Schmidt asymptotic commutant of the Kazhdan image to another such asymptotic commutant.
