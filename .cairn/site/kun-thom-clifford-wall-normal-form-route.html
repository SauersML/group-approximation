Statement

This route is one direction of an EQUIVALENCE, not a reduction. It renames the problem; it does not shrink it. Read with compressed-commutant-majorana-from-wall-route, which is the other direction. Flagged explicitly after the team's "restatement dressed as reduction" catch: given the established relative-wall-commutant-growth, the prerequisite compressed-commutant-majorana is logically equivalent to the target, so nothing here is closer to a construction than the target was.

What the equivalence buys is not progress on the target but a better coordinate system for attacking it: the marked element h disappears, the demand moves to the compressed subgroup t Gamma t^(-1), and the anticommutation becomes a single Gamma-conjugation. That reformulation is what makes the growth index of compressor-growth-index-homomorphism applicable, and hence what yields commutator-compressor-carries-no-wall -- which is new information. The value is downstream of the restatement, not in it.

Mechanically: the wall normal form turns the two normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt conditions into one exact algebraic demand; pulling the involution back by Ad pi(t)^(-1) produces a Clifford wall in the original coordinates, and matrix representatives of a Connes-embeddable model give the finite endpoint (KCP12)--(KCP13) with constant-one error. Passing from an almost-representation family to an exact homomorphism into a tracial matrix ultraproduct, and back, loses nothing.
