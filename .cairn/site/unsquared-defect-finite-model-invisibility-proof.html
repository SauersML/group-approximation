Statement

Direct proof

Let C be the commutant of the image of the literal property-(T) base in a finite-dimensional representation and let conjugation by the image of t be Phi. The one-sided compression relation gives C <= Phi(C). Since Phi is a linear isomorphism and C is finite-dimensional, equality holds. The image of c lies in C, hence so does the image of t c t^-1; it therefore commutes with the image of v_1, killing u. A finite group embeds in a finite-dimensional permutation representation, giving the final assertion.
