Statement

Find an explicit countable group H, a nonidentity element x in Rad_sof(H), and one homomorphism from H to a hyperlinear group which does not kill x.

The Kun--Thom compression constructions provide the sofic-radical side. The open task is to make the same defect survive a tracial matrix model. The literal Clifford sign cannot be used unchanged: literal-clifford-ce-model also supplies a sofic detector of that sign.

Ideas

The supply side is not a separate problem. By sofic-radical-soficization, Rad_sof(H)=1 if and only if H is sofic. So "find a nonidentity element of a sofic radical" and "find a nonsofic group" are the same search, with the same two known instances and the same single (T)-compression mechanism behind both (docs/OBSTRUCTIONS.md §5). There is no separate technology for producing radical elements to be swept for, and a genuinely different source would in particular be a second nonsoficity mechanism. Note the degenerate end of the range: a simple nonsofic group has total sofic radical, and there the criterion says nothing, since a nontrivial homomorphism out of it is injective. The criterion has content only where the radical is a proper nontrivial subgroup.

The quotient lattice is fully constrained. By radical-collision-reformulation, any quotient of H retaining x is automatically nonsofic — which is what makes the criterion work, and equally what forbids every cheap certificate. No LEF, LEA, residually amenable, linear, or sofic-by-amenable quotient retaining x exists, and the criterion as a whole is equivalent to Question 3.4 rather than a reduction of it. The one asymmetry worth keeping is that the model may be non-faithful.

Which collapses of the base are even allowed. For any homomorphism q on H = G *_Gamma (Gamma x C_2), if q(h) in q(Gamma) then q(r)=1 because q(k) commutes with q(Gamma); and q(h)=q(t)^(-1)q(gamma)q(t). Hence

q(x)!=1  ==>  q(t) q(Gamma) q(t)^(-1) is a STRICT subgroup of q(Gamma),

witnessed by q(gamma). Every collapse of G retaining x must therefore carry the strict compression intact, so by the formalized no_strict_compression_of_invariantSize no image with a conjugation-invariant strictly monotone size — finite, compact, or exhausted by finite fixed sets — is reachable. There is no "simplify the base pair first" move.

Where the remaining freedom actually is. invariant-graph-clifford-phase maps out the lamp side instead: the quotients of H retaining x that come from central lamp extensions are classified by G-invariant graphs on G/Gamma containing the marked edge, the minimal ones are exactly those with nondegenerate adjacency form, and those are exactly the ones whose model algebra is a factor rather than a base with an invariant abelian (Cartan) direction. That is the structural reason the Clifford route escapes the permutation-normalization obstruction that kills the commuting wreath model, and it says the escape is not special to the complete graph. It also closes the obvious variation: at a symmetric marked double coset no lamp of order >2 yields a finer phase, because a G-invariant alternating pairing whose marked pair is reversible takes a value of order two there. The sign is all there is.

One trap, recorded. Kun--Thom's own group W=(directSum_(G/Gamma) Z/2) semidirect G has L(W)=L^infty(prod_(G/Gamma) Z/2) crossed_product G, the crossed product of a measure preserving action of a residually finite, hence sofic, group; and W is hyperlinear iff that algebra is Connes embeddable. It is tempting to finish by Elek--Lippner. Their theorem, as transcribed in Păunescu arXiv:1002.0605 Theorem 2.1 (numbering read off the arXiv HTML, re-verify before any manuscript use), covers equivalence relations generated by Bernoulli shifts of sofic groups — the regular action — and it is open whether every measure preserving action of a sofic group is sofic (loc. cit. Theorem 3.1 only reduces the general case to free actions). The generalized Bernoulli action over G/Gamma is not covered, and Kun--Thom show the underlying action on the set G/Gamma is not sofic in the Gao--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Patchell sense, which is what a proof of the Bernoulli case would need. The gap between an abstract trace-preserving embedding and a Cartan-preserving one is the whole problem (docs/NOTEPAD.md, "Abstract versus Cartan-preserving embeddings").

The lane that overtook this one (2026-08-14). With weakly-sofic-not-sofic settled, the sharpest question is no longer about detectors at all: hilbert-embeddable-length-hyperlinearity shows that a weak-soficity witness upgrades to a hyperlinear one exactly when its invariant lengths are conditionally negative definite, a finite character-expansion test. The Kun--Thom wreath already has weak-soficity witnesses, so Question 3.4 now has a route that needs no detector, no algebra and no control of the coset action — see hilbert-embeddable-witness-for-kun-thom-wreath. Anyone working this claim should check that route first; a survivor is only needed if the character test fails.

Supply, settled. The literal cluster is closed as a source: E is sofic (literal-group-sofic), so by sofic-radical-soficization Rad_sof(E)=1 and no element of E — including the infinite-rank free kernel of E -> W — is a radical survivor. The Kun--Thom groups remain the only supply, with four explicit words recorded in the corpus and two complete radical computations (docs/COMMUTING_WREATH_SOFIC_RADICAL.md, docs/CYCLIC_AUGMENTATION_SOFIC_RADICAL.md). The cheapest concrete form of this hole is the first of those: Rad_sof(W)=<<w>>_W for the single involution w=a_(h Gamma) a_Gamma, so a detector is a single projection (1-rho(w))/2 of positive trace.

A next unit that was tried and closed. Looking for a nondegenerate G-invariant graph of finite degree — a symmetric double coset Gamma h Gamma with [Gamma : Gamma cap h Gamma h^(-1)] finite — is futile: marked-double-coset-infinite-degree proves that index is infinite for the recorded marked element, so every invariant graph keeping the word alive has infinite degree. The localization hope for the finite-mode defects of docs/FALSE_FERMIONIC_KUN_THOM_ENDPOINT.md §5 is not available in this family.
