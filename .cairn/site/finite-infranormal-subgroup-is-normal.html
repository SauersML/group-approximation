Statement

Let K<G be finite and put P_K={g in G : gKg^(-1) <= K}. Then P_K=N_G(K), because conjugation preserves the finite cardinality of K and turns the inclusion into equality. Consequently, if P_K generates G (infranormality in the Kun--Thom sense), then N_G(K)=G and K is normal.

In particular no finite subgroup can be both nonnormal and infranormal, so a fixed finite Pauli packet cannot replace the infinite nonnormal Kazhdan subgroup in the Kun--Thom sofic-radical theorem.
