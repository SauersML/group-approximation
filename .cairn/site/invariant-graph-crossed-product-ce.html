Statement

For the explicit residually finite nonnormal infranormal Kazhdan pair Gamma<G, with X=G/Gamma, o=Gamma and y=h Gamma the marked pair, prove that there is a G-invariant graph S on X with

{o,y} in S,        b_S nondegenerate on directSum_X F_2,

for which the tracial crossed product

M_S = A_S crossed_product G

is Connes embeddable, where A_S is the twisted group von Neumann algebra of (directSum_X F_2, b_S) at the central character z |-> -1.

Every such A_S is the hyperfinite II_1 factor, so the difficulty is the action, not the base — as for the complete graph. The point of the existential form is that the anticommutation pattern is a free parameter and only one member of the family has to work. Degenerate graphs are excluded because they reintroduce a G-invariant abelian direction in the centre of the base, which is the coordinate the Kun--Thom permutation normalization argument acts on.

Few orbits yes, finite degree no — the two are not the same thing. The minimal member of the family is the single symmetrized marked double coset Gamma h Gamma, and it is available: the corpus already builds it, as the orbital target E_orb = K_(X,E_*) semidirect G at docs/NOTEPAD.md near line 9969, where the z=-1 lamp algebra is identified as the AF twisted group algebra C*_r(directSum_X F_2, (-1)^(B_*)) rather than the full CAR algebra — "a strictly weaker analytic target than the complete Clifford system". That is the concrete witness for this claim's existential, and it is where the attack should start.

What is not available is finite degree. It was natural to hope that the minimal member would also be locally finite, giving a Bogoliubov action touching finitely many Majorana modes per generator — the regime where the finite-mode permutation defects of docs/FALSE_FERMIONIC_KUN_THOM_ENDPOINT.md §5 could be localized. marked-double-coset-infinite-degree refutes that: the marked double coset of the Laurent pair is an infinite union of left cosets, so every invariant graph containing the marked edge, the minimal one included, has infinite degree at every vertex. The remaining freedom is in the shape of an infinite-degree nondegenerate graph, not in its sparsity.

The open sub-question this leaves. Is the minimal member nondegenerate? Its radical is the G-submodule ker(A) of F_2[X], where A is the F_2 adjacency operator of the orbital graph of Gamma h Gamma; by invariant-graph-clifford-phase item 4 this vanishing is equivalent to the quotient being monolithic and to the lamp algebra being a factor. A nonempty finite F in the radical would need every vertex to have an even number of S-neighbours in F; for |F|=2 this says the two vertices are twins, g Gamma h Gamma = Gamma h Gamma. Settling this decides whether the cheapest member of the family carries a factorial model or reintroduces the Cartan direction.
