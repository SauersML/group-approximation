Statement

Secondary import, deliberately. The primary — Bekka--Valette, Math. Z. 212 (1993) 293--299 — is paywalled and was not obtained; four routes to it were tried on 2026-08-14 (Springer, EUDML, Pestov arXiv:1512.01572, and the Math. Scand. article, the last returning 404).

What was read, with pdftotext: Li--Ng, arXiv:1304.7051, whose proof of Theorem 4.1 quotes Bekka--Valette Theorem 1 in use, and whose Remark 4.2 uses Bekka's Invent. Math. Theorem 5.1 for the amenability equivalence; and Kahl--Schneider, arXiv:2504.10988v2, for Definition 6.2 (mu-near representation) and Theorem B, both read directly.

The claim separates what is sourced from what is inferred: the quoted implication is Li--Ng's words, the non-applicability to near representations follows from Kahl--Schneider's Definition 6.2 being non-multiplicative, and the "load-bearing, not convenience" reading is flagged as inference in the body.
