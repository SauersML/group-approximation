Statement

Distinct also from the route sofic-action-permanence, which records the three permanence theorems and the refutation of their shared hypothesis: this claim proves the converse direction, which blocks the natural repair of relabelling the lamps. (distinct_from only accepts claims, so that disambiguation lives here rather than in the frontmatter.)

Let G act on X and let V be any group generated by elements a_x indexed by X and permuted by G — the commuting lamp group directSum_X C_2, the Clifford lamp group C(X), or any invariant-graph lamp group Vtilde_S. If the automorphism action G on V is a sofic C-action in the sense of Alekseev--Bradford Definition 4.23, for any class C whatsoever, then the set action G on X is sofic.

The proof uses only that the induced automorphism sends a_z to a_(gz), that the partial homomorphisms are injective on the finite window, and that the finitely many labels pi_s(a_z) form a finite set: the covariance equation is then literally the orbit-approximation equation. Commutativity, the order of a_z, and the presence of a central element play no role.

With coordinate-action-not-sofic (Kun--Thom Corollary D) this closes the halo/chart permanence route on the Clifford side exactly as on the Bernoulli side, and with the locally-sofic collapse theorem of docs/FALSE_HALO_ACTION_AUDIT.md it closes every repair that relabels the coset coordinates by finite, Clifford, Pauli, or finite-dimensional unitary data — all of those target classes are locally sofic. A chart-based proof would need a target that is already a finitely generated hyperlinear nonsofic group.
