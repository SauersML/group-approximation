Statement

Writing x=tau(EF), the squared distance is

1/4 - 8x^2 - (8/7)(1/4-x)^2,

with 0 <= x <= 1/8. Exact minimization gives 3/28. The canonical overlap x=1/32 makes the conditional expectation scalar and gives the stronger 3/16 value. These inequalities are kernel-checked in the cited Lean module.
