Statement

The finite residual of W/<zeta> = F_2[X] semidirect V is exactly

L_0 = { lamp configurations with an even number of lamps on each level },

the kernel of the level-parity map F_2[X] -> sum_{n in Z} F_2. The quotient (W/<zeta>)/L_0 = (sum_{n in Z} F_2) semidirect V, with V acting through its level homomorphism, is residually finite, hence LEF, hence MF.

So the entire failure of residual finiteness is a locally finite elementary abelian 2-group inside the lamp kernel, and the surviving defect ubar lies in it.
