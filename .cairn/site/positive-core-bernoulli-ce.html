Statement

For the regular finite-field Kun–Thom pair — k = 𝔽_q, d, r ≥ 3, R₊ = k[x₁,…,x_d], R = k[x₁^{±1},…,x_d^{±1}], Γ = E_r(R₊), G = E_r(R) ⋊ SL_d(ℤ) — put

L₊ = SL_d(ℤ) ∩ M_d(ℕ), T = Γ L₊ ⊆ G, X₀ = TΓ/Γ ⊆ G/Γ.

Then Γ ↷ X₀ is a sofic action, the generalized wreath product W₀ = (⊕_{X₀} C₂) ⋊ Γ is sofic, and therefore

N₀ = L(W₀) = L^∞({±1}^{X₀}) ⋊ Γ

is Connes embeddable.

This is the flexible side's only positive foothold, and it has never been in the graph. Every other node on this branch either demands a model or refutes a way of getting one; this one exhibits genuine matrix microstates for a genuine piece of the target algebra.

Why the positive part escapes what the full action cannot

coordinate-action-not-sofic establishes that G ↷ G/Γ is not a sofic action. The mechanism separating the two is stabilizer separability, and it is worth stating precisely because it is the sharpest structural fact known about this pair.

For ℓ ∈ L₊ the stabilizer of ℓΓ ∈ X₀ in Γ is H_ℓ = Γ ∩ ℓΓℓ⁻¹ = ℓΓℓ⁻¹. Since ℓ preserves ℕ^d, the monoid ℓℕ^d is freely generated by the columns of ℓ, so R_ℓ = k[ℓℕ^d] is again a polynomial ring and Suslin stability gives H_ℓ = E_r(R_ℓ) = SL_r(R_ℓ). Truncating R₊ by the monomial ideal of elements of degree > N gives finite rings Q_N, and for g ∈ Γ \ H_ℓ some entry of g has a monomial whose exponent is outside ℓℕ^d, so g survives in SL_r(Q_N) outside the image of H_ℓ. Every positive stabilizer is therefore separable in Γ by finite ring congruences.

A transitive action of a residually finite group on Γ/H with H separable has exact finite Schreier approximations, X₀ is a countable union of such orbits, and any finite window meets finitely many — so Γ ↷ X₀ is sofic. Soficity of W₀ then follows from Gao–Kunnawalkam Elayavalli–Patchell Theorem A (Γ sofic since residually finite, C₂ finite, action sofic), the same theorem that coordinate-action-not-sofic runs in contrapositive against the full action. One theorem, both directions, and the input that flips the sign is separability.

This also fixes what the LERF observation does and does not buy. Since all actions of a LERF group on sets are sofic (arXiv:2402.17150), G is not LERF and Γ is not separable in G. But LERF is far more than needed: only the positive stabilizers have to be separable, and they are.

What is not proved

W₀ is a proper subgroup of W, generated together with the positive external matrices L₊. Nothing here dilates those. The gap is simultaneous-dilation-splice, and the route that would use this foothold is positive-core-dilation-splice.
