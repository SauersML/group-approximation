Statement

For a nonnormal infranormal Kazhdan pair Gamma<G, put

H = G *_Gamma (Gamma x C2) = <G,k | k^2=1, [k,Gamma]=1>.

Choose h=t^(-1) gamma t outside Gamma, set r=[k,h], and x=[k,r]. Kun--Thom centralizer normalization gives x in Rad_sof(H). In the Clifford crossed product Cl(G/Gamma) crossed_product G, the representation k |-> c_Gamma satisfies r |-> c_Gamma c_(hGamma) and therefore x |-> -1.
