Statement

For the doubling family (and any Kazhdan compression pair with MF abelianized quotient), Rad_MF(K^(G/Gamma) x| G) is exactly the kernel of the fiberwise-abelianized product map onto K_ab^(G/Gamma-up) x| G. For perfect finite K the radical is the entire lamp base; for K = C2 it is even parity on every height level. Upgrades the non-MF criterion to a computation of the universal MF quotient.
