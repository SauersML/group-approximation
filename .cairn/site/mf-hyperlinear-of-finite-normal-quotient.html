Statement

Let G be a countable MF group with a finite normal subgroup N such that G/N is hyperlinear. Then G is hyperlinear.

This is the norm-to-trace converter of Bachner--Dogon--Lubotzky, On L^1-approximation of groups, arXiv:2508.17392v3, Proposition 1.6 (verified against the source: "Let Gamma be a finitely generated group with a finite normal subgroup N such that Gamma/N is hyperlinear. If Gamma is MF, then it is hyperlinear."); the route below is an independent in-repo proof which also drops finite generation.
