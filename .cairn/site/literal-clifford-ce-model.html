Statement

For the literal finitely presented group E and its central involution w, there is a homomorphism into the unitary group of a Connes-embeddable finite tracial von Neumann algebra which sends w to -1. Equivalently, w does not belong to the hyperlinear residual of E.

The model is the finite-coset Clifford tower, followed by its automorphic dilation and crossing with Z. It also realizes the exact Cl_8(C) = M_16(C) packet in the negative sector. Therefore the fixed spin block is valid structural information but cannot support a universal tracial theorem killing w.
