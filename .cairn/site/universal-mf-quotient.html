Statement

For every countable group G, let Rad_MF(G) be the intersection of the kernels of all homomorphisms to unitary groups of positive natural-dimension norm-matrix C-star coronas. This radical is the kernel of one such representation, the quotient G/Rad_MF(G) is MF, and every homomorphism from G to a countable MF group factors through that quotient. Consequently G is MF exactly when Rad_MF(G) is trivial.
