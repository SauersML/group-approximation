Statement

Let Theta be a corona representation of W/<zeta> or of E/<w> that is injective, and put C = Theta(Gbar)' cap Q. Then

C  strictly contained in  Theta(tau) C Theta(tau)^*  =  Theta(abar(Gbar))' cap Q ,

witnessed by the single lamp Theta(c_{tau o}).

So corona-commutant-no-growth is not merely sufficient to refute the two open quotient claims: its negation is necessary for either of them. Any proof that either quotient is MF must exhibit a corona representation whose relative commutant grows under the compression, and the growth is realized by a lamp, not by an exotic operator.
