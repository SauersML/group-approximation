Statement

The literal forty-one-relator group E is finitely presented, sofic, and hyperlinear, and is not MF in any of the formalized operator-norm matrix-corona conventions.

This strengthens Theorem E of the manuscript from the finitely generated witness W = ClLamp(X) semidirect V to the finitely presented source, and it separates the two approximation radicals on one finitely presented group:

Rad_sof(E) = 1 while {1,w} <= Rad_MF(E), w != 1.
