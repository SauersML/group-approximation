Citation import. Theorem 3.13, Definition 3.7, Lemma 3.6 and the proof sketch were read from pdftotext output of arXiv:2403.09582v2 on 2026-08-14; the Chapman--Dikstein--Lubotzky quotations were read the same way from arXiv:2410.02913v2 (their Theorem 1.5, Definition 1.4, and the Section 1 discussion of the lamplighter counterexample).

The elementary principle underneath — used here only through Gohla--Thom's own phrasing — appears verbatim in their introduction: "any group which is both sofic and stable must be residually finite", and in Section 3.2 in the sharper form "any sofic group which is stable in finite actions must be residually finite". They attribute it to Glebsky--Rivera, Almost solutions of equations in permutations, Taiwanese J. Math. 13 (2009), no. 2A, 493--500, arXiv:0709.1134. That elementary implication was not read in the Glebsky--Rivera original; it is imported on Gohla--Thom's statement of it, which is a primary source for the phrasing used here.
