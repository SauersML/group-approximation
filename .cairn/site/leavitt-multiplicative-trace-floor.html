Statement

For contractions T_0,S_0,T_1,S_1 in any nonzero finite matrix algebra,

max(||T_0 S_0-1||_2, ||T_1 S_1-1||_2, ||T_0 S_1||_2)
  >= 2-sqrt(3) > 1/4.

The bound is independent of matrix size. On a corner of fixed trace alpha, the corresponding ambient normalized-HS floor is sqrt(alpha)(2-sqrt(3)). Hence the terminal contradiction is complete once the three products are decoded on one positive-density coordinate corner.
