Established by citation. A. Thom, Examples of hyperlinear groups without factorization property, arXiv:0810.2180, Groups Geom. Dyn. 4 (2010), Lemma 3.3 and Remark 3.4. Both statements are transcribed verbatim in the artifact, from a pdftotext extraction of the PDF fetched from export.arxiv.org on 2026-08-14 — not from an HTML render. Published and refereed; used at statement level only.

The instantiation at the Clifford lamp group is the elementary observation that hat(Z/2) has two points and that the two twisted group algebras of directSum_X Z/2 at those characters are L^infinity({-1,1}^X) and Cl(X); it agrees with the independent direct computation in clifford-bernoulli-central-summands.
