Statement

The action Ad pi|_Gamma on L^2(M) is trace preserving with fixed space L^2(N), and ||alpha_s(p)-p||_2=||[pi(s),p]||_2, so property (T) bounds the L^2-distance from p to L^2(N) -- realized by E_N(p) since E_N is the orthogonal projection -- by kappa^(-1) times the commutator energy. Writing a=E_N(p), the bimodule and trace-preservation properties give tau(pa)=tau(a^2) and tau(a)=tau(p), so ||p-a||_2^2=tau(p)-2tau(pa)+tau(a^2)=tau(a(1-a)) exactly. Cutting a at 1/2 inside N produces a projection q with ||a-q||_2^2 <= 2 tau(a(1-a)), because (t-chi(t))^2 <= |t-chi(t)| <= 2t(1-t) on [0,1]. Adding the two estimates gives the stated bound. Complete argument in the cited note, Proposition 11.
