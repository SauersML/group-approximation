Statement

Direct proof

1. Finiteness. If v^v=1 in A then tau(1-vv^)=tau(v^v)-tau(vv^)=0 and 1-vv^>=0, so faithfulness gives vv^=1. For the ultraproduct, let a=(a_k) satisfy a^*a=1, i.e. ||a_k^*a_k-1||->0 (along the ultrafilter, respectively in the corona sense). Eventually a_k^*a_k is invertible, so u_k=a_k(a_k^*a_k)^(-1/2) is an isometry of A_k, hence unitary by the previous sentence, whence

||a_k a_k^*-1|| = ||u_k(|a_k|^2-1)u_k^*|| = |||a_k|^2-1|| -> 0,

so aa^*=1. Finiteness passes to the corona because the estimate is uniform in the same sense as the hypothesis.

2. Corners. For a nonzero projection p, tau(p)=tau(p^*p)>0 by faithfulness. pAp is unital with unit p; tau_p=tau(p . p)/tau(p) is a state, is tracial on pAp because tau is tracial on A, and is faithful because tau is: tau_p(x^*x)=0 with x=pxp forces x=0.

3. Lifting. Let P be a projection in prod A_k/directSum A_k and x=(x_k) a self-adjoint lift with ||x||<=1. Then ||x_k^2-x_k||->0, so spec(x_k) is eventually contained in [-gamma_k,gamma_k] union [1-gamma_k,1+gamma_k] with gamma_k->0; the function equal to 0 on the first interval and 1 on the second is continuous on the spectrum, so p_k=f(x_k) is a projection in A_k with ||p_k-x_k||->0. Thus (p_k) lifts P. The map prod_k p_kA_kp_k -> P(prod A_k/directSum A_k)P is surjective (compress any lift) with kernel directSum_k p_kA_kp_k, which is the second assertion.

4. Tracial ultraproduct. M = prod_k A_k / {x : lim_omega ||x_k||_(2,tau_k)=0} is the von Neumann ultraproduct of the tracial von Neumann algebras generated by the GNS representations of tau_k; it is a von Neumann algebra with faithful normal tracial state tau_omega, hence finite. On L^2(M,tau_omega) the modular conjugation J gives the commuting copy JMJ, and m tensor n^(op) |-> m JnJ^* is a normal surjection of the finite von Neumann algebra M tensorbar M^(op) (trace tau_omega tensor tau_omega^(op)) onto M or JMJ. A normal quotient of a finite von Neumann algebra is finite. End proof.

Remark on the boundary

Faithfulness of tau is what makes item 2 true, and item 2 is what the central-sign and finite-normal arguments consume. Dropping faithfulness leaves a class in which a nonzero projection can be trace-null and the corner carries no state; that is precisely the tracial-ultraproduct situation recorded in tracial-coordinate-transport-barrier.
