Statement

Let pi:G->U(M) with M Connes-embeddable finite tracial, and let v in M intersect pi(Gamma)' be a unitary with [v,pi(h)]!=1. Then the diagonal tensor powers (M^(tensor k),pi^(tensor k),v^(tensor k)) are again walls in Connes-embeddable algebras, self-adjoint if v is, and

sup_k ||v^(tensor k)-pi_k(h)v^(tensor k)pi_k(h)^*||_2^2 >= 2.

Hence the maximal-energy hypotheses (CHI8) and (WFI4) follow from the existence of any wall, and the flexible-Hilbert--Schmidt-instability conclusions drawn from them hold as soon as a wall exists at all.

Scope: amplification does not produce the Clifford (anticommuting) wall of the Clifford normal form, since v_k w_k=(vw)^(tensor k). Anticommutation is a genuinely stronger demand; energy is not.
