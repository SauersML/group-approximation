Statement

ClLamp(X) semidirect T and F_2[X] semidirect T are locally embeddable into finite groups, hence sofic and — by lef-implies-operator-mf — operator MF. Each is the directed union of the residually finite groups L' semidirect Gbar_k with L' a finite Gbar_k-invariant lamp subgroup.

Therefore both the witness and its commuting-lamp quotient are Z-extensions of LEF groups:

W        = (ClLamp(X) semidirect T) semidirect Z,
W/<zeta> = (F_2[X]    semidirect T) semidirect Z.
