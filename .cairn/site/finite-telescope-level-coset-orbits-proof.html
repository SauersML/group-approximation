Why sufficient

Successive telescope levels have relative index equal to the index of alpha.range; hence every level is commensurable with level zero. After embedding the telescope in the vertical semidirect product, the stable letter conjugates the base to the compressed range, which is again commensurable with the base. The level-zero copy and stable letter generate the vertical group, so the entire vertical group commensurates the base.

For a level L acting on V/B, the stabilizer of the coset represented by g is the pullback to L of gBg^-1. Commensurability gives this stabilizer finite index in L; orbit--stabilizer therefore makes the orbit finite. MappingTelescopeFiniteOrbits.lean checks every step, including the stabilizer identity and the relative-index calculation.
