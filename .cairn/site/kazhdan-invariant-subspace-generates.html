Statement

Let the transport data be as in coordinate-ultraproduct-transport, let Theta be a corona representation of H with models U_n which detects the central mark, Theta(z) != 1, let H_omega = prod_omega C^{d_n}, and let P be the projection onto the iota(Gamma)-invariant vectors. Then the closed tilde U(H)-invariant subspace generated by ran P is all of H_omega.

The mark-detection hypothesis is not decoration: without it the statement is false, by finite-dimensional-models-break-coordinate-generation. It is also harmless to the route, since a model that already kills the mark needs no argument.

Granting this, the central mark dies with no spectral input whatever: eps is central, so tilde U(eps) commutes with tilde U(H); by coordinate-ultraproduct-transport clause 3 it acts as the identity on ran P; hence it acts as the identity on every tilde U(h) ran P, hence on their closed span, which is H_omega. So tilde U(eps) = 1, and since the coordinate ultraproduct action carries the corona operator norm, Theta(eps) = 1.

Ideas

Why this replaces the earlier formulation. The node this supersedes, coordinate-invariant-vector-detects-mark, asked for a Kazhdan-invariant vector that the mark moves. That is not a reduction: clause 3 of coordinate-ultraproduct-transport says no Kazhdan-invariant vector is moved by the mark, so the earlier claim was, given clause 3, logically equivalent to the target rather than a step toward it. A route built on it renamed the problem. The present formulation has content because it can fail — see the next paragraph — and because it does not mention the mark at all.

How it fails, and what that costs. ran P can be zero. A property-(T) group has no almost-invariant vectors in a representation bounded away from the trivial one, and the finite-quotient models of a residually finite group typically give P = 0; inflating a single finite-dimensional representation with no invariant vectors makes this explicit (finite-dimensional-models-break-coordinate-generation). So this claim is a demand on the architecture: mark-detecting models of H must be forced to contain the trivial representation of iota(Gamma) asymptotically. The consequence for any future proof is sharp — it must use Theta(z) != 1 from the start and manufacture the Kazhdan-invariant vectors from the survival of the mark, since no argument treating all corona models alike can work. That is the honest price of the coordinate route. Contrast the adjoint side, where the same demand is free: the unitary U_n(c) is itself a nonzero iota(Gamma)-invariant Hilbert--Schmidt vector, which is exactly why the manuscript's transport is unconditional and exactly why it concludes only in the 2-norm.

A weaker sufficient form. Generation is more than needed. It suffices that the tilde U(H)-invariant subspace generated by ran P contain a vector on which tilde U(eps) acts nontrivially — or, since eps is central and its spectral projections exist in B(H_omega) and commute with tilde U(H), that ran P meet every nonzero spectral subspace of tilde U(eps). Any of these forms would do; generation is the cleanest to state.
