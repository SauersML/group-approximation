Statement

If a countable group H contains an element

x in Rad_sof(H) minus Rad_hyp(H),

then a hyperlinear nonsofic group exists. Indeed, choose a hyperlinear detector of x and restrict its codomain to the image of H. That image is hyperlinear. It cannot be sofic, because the quotient map from H would then have to kill x.
