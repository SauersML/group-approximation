Statement

Every countable LEF group is operator MF, in the norm-matrix-corona sense of countable-group-mf-conventions — hence in every formulation equivalent to it there (weak, actual-corona, unitary-sequence, local, increasing-dimension, ultraproduct, finite-set, group-theoretic, ambient-unitary).

This is the manuscript's lem:rfmf/lem:lfmf argument with its hypothesis weakened to exactly what that argument uses. At coordinate n, take a local embedding of the n-th window into a finite group Q_n and use the left regular representation of Q_n. Multiplicativity is exact for every pair in the window, because the local embedding is a partial homomorphism there; separation is the same sqrt 3 bound, because left translation by a nonidentity element of a finite group is a fixed-point-free permutation. An exhausting sequence of windows then makes the induced corona map injective.

Reusable corollary chain: locally residually finite implies LEF (each finite window sits in a finitely generated subgroup, which is residually finite, so a finite quotient of it embeds the window), hence sofic and MF. That is the form used by literal-telescope-core-lef and available to any other lamp-by-telescope argument, including lamp-telescope-base-is-lef.
