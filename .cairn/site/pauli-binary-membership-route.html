Statement

The decoder would make the transported trace-1/4 carrier asymptotically belong to the binary algebra of the trace-1/8 raw carrier. The established 3/28 squared-distance floor forbids this in every finite matrix algebra. Therefore a hyperlinear microstate sequence for the fixed atlas group cannot exist.
