Statement

If P_Gamma={g : g Gamma g^(-1)<=Gamma} generates G, then for every homomorphism rho:G->K to a compact group, the closure of rho(Gamma) is normal in the closure of rho(G). Compact recurrence upgrades each one-sided inclusion to equality. Consequently the Gamma-fixed space of every finite-dimensional unitary representation is G-invariant.
