Statement

Under the hypothesis, the trace-preserving expectation of the ultraproduct inclusion is the ultraproduct of the coordinate trace-preserving expectations, so the coordinate Pimsner--Popa inequalities pass to the limit and bound the index of the limit inclusion by the ultralimit of the coordinate indices. The wall normal form makes the limit index exceed one, whence so does the ultralimit. Nothing else is used, and nothing is claimed about the hypothesis itself. Complete argument in the cited note, Section 6.1.
