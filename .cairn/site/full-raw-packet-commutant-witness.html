Statement

Let K=<raw,z_11,z_001> be the sixteen-element raw Pauli packet, let A_n=Alg(rho_n(K)) in a trace-preserving finite matrix microstate, and let F_n be the transported trace-1/4 carrier. Then

dist_2(F_n,A_n)^2 -> 3/16.

Haar averaging over U(A_n') therefore supplies a coordinate unitary V_n with

[V_n,A_n]=0,
||[V_n,F_n]||_2^2 >= 3/8-o(1).

After doubling, V_n may be chosen as a self-adjoint involution without changing the energy.
