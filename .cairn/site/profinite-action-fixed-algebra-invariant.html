Statement

Let Gamma<G with G generated by elements t satisfying t Gamma t^(-1)<=Gamma. If G acts trace preservingly and profinitely on a finite von Neumann algebra A — profinite meaning A is the weak closure of an increasing union of finite-dimensional G-invariant subalgebras — then A^Gamma is G-invariant.

Finite-dimensional case: Aut(B) is a compact Lie group, the closure K of the image of Gamma satisfies alpha(t) K alpha(t)^(-1) <= K with the left side conjugate to K, hence of equal dimension and equal component count, so the inclusion is an equality; and B^Gamma=B^K. General case: the trace-preserving conditional expectations E_n:A->B_n are G-equivariant, being orthogonal projections onto G-invariant subspaces of L^2(A), and E_n(x)->x.

Consequence for this lane: no profinite action contains a unitary q in A^Gamma whose translate alpha_g(q) fails to commute with Gamma. So exact G-invariant AF or profinite filtrations cannot carry a relative wall, independently of property (T) and of soficity, and the Capraro--Paunescu profinite-action embeddability theorem cannot be applied to retain the Kun--Thom witness. A positive construction must use finite-dimensional algebras that are only approximately G-invariant, with a coherent normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt defect.
