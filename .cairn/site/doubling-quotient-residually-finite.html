Statement

For G = (Z[1/2]^3 x| SL3(Z)) x| Z with doubling stable letter and K finite, the quotient K_ab^(Z) x| G is residually finite: G embeds in GL4(Z[1/2]) (odd-prime congruence separation), and the quotient embeds in G x (K_ab wr Z) via the height homomorphism.
