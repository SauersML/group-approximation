Statement

Why sufficient

For every m >= 2, diag(m,m,m,1) scales the translation subgroup by m and fixes the linear generators. Translation by e_1/m is not integral, so the chosen element remains outside the self-embedding image and the two Clifford sites remain distinct. The Kazhdan base and sign argument are independent of m; the closed endpoint verifies the whole quantified family.
