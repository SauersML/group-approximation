Why sufficient

The locally finite lamp kernel is amenable and exact. The quotient V <= GL_4(Q) is exact by the exactness theorem for linear groups. Exact groups are closed under extensions, and group exactness is equivalent to exactness of the reduced group algebra. The canonical reduced trace is faithful and forces stable finiteness in all finite matrix amplifications. An MF embedding of C*_r(W) would restrict to a faithful corona model of W, contradicting sofic-non-mf-witness. Exactness is literature-backed; the remaining assertions have the cited closed Lean endpoint.
