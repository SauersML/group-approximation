Statement

Let H carry the transport data and suppose H has a finite-dimensional unitary representation sigma with no nonzero iota(Gamma)-invariant vector. Then the corona model V_n = sigma^{oplus n} — dimensions increasing, multiplicative defect zero — has coordinate ultraproduct equal to the sigma-space, on which iota(Gamma) has no invariant vector at all. So its Kazhdan projection P is zero and the subspace generated by ran P is zero, not everything.

Consequently kazhdan-invariant-subspace-generates cannot be asked of every corona model. It must be restricted to models that detect the mark, and even then it requires that no finite-dimensional unitary representation of H simultaneously detect the mark and kill the Kazhdan-invariant vectors.

Ideas

The existing architecture supplies the restricted hypothesis, and for a strong reason. By unsquared-defect-finite-model-invisible, every finite-dimensional linear representation of the literal group kills the compression defect u, hence kills everything in its normal closure, hence the mark. So no finite-dimensional representation detects the mark at all, and the counterexample above cannot be instantiated against a mark-detecting model. This is the same finite-dimensional shadow that Theorem B of the manuscript records, appearing here as the thing that keeps the coordinate route coherent rather than as a limitation.

What it costs. The restriction is not free: it means the coordinate route can never be established by an argument that treats all corona models alike. Any proof must use the hypothesis Theta(z) != 1 from the start, which is exactly the hypothesis one is trying to refute — so the argument has to be genuinely by contradiction, and the Kazhdan-invariant vectors have to be produced from the survival of the mark. That is a much more specific demand than "the models happen to contain the trivial representation", and it is the honest shape of the remaining difficulty.

Necessary condition on the source. Combining with bounded-rank-corners-excluded, both open analytic routes on the central-mark branch want the same unusual property of the Kazhdan source: absence of low-dimensional representations, minimal almost periodicity being the clean form. The repository has such a source, St_5(L), whose every finite-dimensional unitary representation is trivial. That the same hypothesis is forced twice, by two unrelated arguments, is mild evidence that it is the right architecture to build the torsion-free mark on.
