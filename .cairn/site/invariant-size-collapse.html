Statement

Criterion/FiniteQuotientBlindness.no_strict_compression_of_invariantSize (formalized). If subgroups carry a size that conjugation preserves and that distinguishes proper inclusions, then a compression is an equality. Two hypotheses, three lines. Every collapse in the development is an instance: finite sets (cardinality), f.d. spaces (finrank), finite groups (Nat.card), compact groups (Haar). The II₁ trace fails the OTHER hypothesis — it is refinement-blind — and that single failure is the whole open branch.

Diagnostic use: before building a model, check its category for an invariant size. Four architectures died this way; the check is minutes, the constructions were sessions.
