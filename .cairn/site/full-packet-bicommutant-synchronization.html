Statement

For every trace-preserving finite matrix microstate of the fixed atlas presentation, after exactifying the raw finite packet K, prove uniformly for coordinate unitaries V_n in Alg(rho_n(K))' that

||[V_n,F_n]||_2 -> 0,

where F_n is the comb-transported Pauli carrier. The proof must use the cross-root multiplication and compressor synchronization relations; the regular representation shows that no abstract finite-von-Neumann-algebra bicommutant theorem can suffice.
