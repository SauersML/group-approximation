Why sufficient

literal-lamp-kernel-clifford-block-amalgam presents the target as E/<w> = V _{B_1} ((Z/2)^8 semidirect B_1), and Shulman's Section 2.1 identifies the group amalgam with the C-amalgam, C*(G_1 _H G_2) = C(G_1) _{C(H)} C*(G_2). With the edge-compatible embeddings of amalgam-edge-compatible-corona-embeddings, Theorem 20 makes C*(E/<w>) MF, and a discrete group embeds in its full group C*-algebra, so E/<w> is MF in the group sense used here.

This is the first route to this claim that is not an exact-finite-model route, and it is strictly stronger than needed: it concludes MF of the full group C*-algebra. Its cost is that both vertex algebras must be MF, and both contain a property-(T) group.
