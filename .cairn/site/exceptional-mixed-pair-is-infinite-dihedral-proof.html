Statement

The free product C2*C2 maps onto <g,r>. Every reduced word is either an alternating word of odd length or a nonzero power of gr up to multiplication by one of the generators. An odd alternating word cannot be the identity, since that would make one involution a power of gr and force gr to have finite order. A nonzero power of gr cannot vanish because (gr)^2=h has infinite order. Thus the quotient map is injective. The conjugation identities are the generic two-involution calculation checked in the cited Lean file.
