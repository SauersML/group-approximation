The ring $A$ is called $H$ -unital if for all abelian groups $V$ , the complex $C^{\mathrm{bar}}(A)\otimes V$ is acyclic. If $A$ is flat as a $\mathbb{Z}$ -module, then $C^{\mathrm{bar}}(A)$ is a complex of flat $\mathbb{Z}$ -modules and $H_{*}(C^{\mathrm{bar}}(A))=\mathrm{Tor}_{*}^{\tilde{A}}(\mathbb{Z},A)$ .
