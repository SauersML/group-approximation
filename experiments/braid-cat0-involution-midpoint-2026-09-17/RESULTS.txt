Involution midpoint test for CAT(1) of the diagonal link X_n of |NCP_n|  (sw-098, 2026-09-17)

Lemma M (see midpoint_test.py docstring). If X is CAT(1), g is an isometry, g^2 z = z and d_X(z, gz) < pi, then the
midpoint of [z, gz] is g-fixed and lies within pi/2 of z. Since d_B <= d_X, a point z with d_B(z, Fix g) >= pi/2 and a
path from z to gz of length < pi refutes CAT(1). Here g ranges over <rho, r, Phi> (order 4n, Phi = Kreweras isometry)
and z over the vertices with g^2 z = z != gz. Fixed-set test: if g preserves order, cos d_B(z, v) <= 0 for every
g-fixed vertex v; if g reverses order, cos d_B(z, v) + cos d_B(z, gv) <= 0 whenever v < gv.
The upper bounds for d_X use vertex paths whose steps share a noncrossing spanning tree (HKS Prop. 4.4).
