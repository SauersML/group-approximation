  with Proposition B. No commuting element outside the predicted subgroup was
  found.
- The direct-product check (z^k in the B_n ball only for k = 0) printed no
  failure.

  delta = gamma^-1 z needs gamma^-1, of length 12, and the B_4 ball has radius 8.
  For n = 3, delta is found with k = 1 and

  gamma^-1, so delta = z . gamma^-1 as predicted.
- Scope: the script checks finite balls only. It is a sanity check of
  Proposition B, not a proof. It uses the faithful Artin action on the free group,
  and it is independent of the configuration-space argument in the artifact.
