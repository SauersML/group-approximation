      python3 rungs.py G 12 800 48 thin (a in 2..4, N <= 48, random charge pairs).
Every case: construction of the proof, all assertions (band simple+closed, seam charges adjacent to band,
rung closing value exists, final pair commutes, sites <= case bound, dist <= |F| sites, dist <= 36 rho_lower) pass.
rho_lower = max over 1 <= s,t <= M of rank([b^s,c^t]-I)/(s+t), M = min(40, max(lam1,|g|_1)+4) (a lower bound for rho).
